Ep 660 - Greed

Word count: 1119

[AMBIENT: EERIE CAVE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The mummy’s eyes snapped open, and a jolt went through the entire group.

[SFX: SHARP GASP]

It hit Malcolm, who was standing right beside Yolande, the hardest. He scrambled back like he’d been zapped by a live wire, staring at the corpse with wide, cautious eyes. This mummy, after all, had once been a world lord—a being with the power to command the very planet he walked on. He might just be a corpse now, but the moment those eyes opened, a primal fear sank its teeth into every one of them.

Yolande, however, just glanced at Malcolm. “Well, that solves one problem,” she said coolly. “I wasn’t sure how we were getting out of this place. I am now.”

Everyone else was still frozen, watching the mummy, terrified it might lurch to its feet. They couldn’t even begin to fathom the power of a world lord; a sliver of that strength would be more than enough to obliterate them all.

But beneath the terror, a different kind of light glinted in their eyes, the same avarice that shone in Malcolm’s: greed.

No matter who this man had once been, his corpse now represented an opportunity. The storage bracelet Yolande had just snatched from its wrist likely held treasures beyond their wildest imaginings. Still, even as they all eyed the prize, they knew they had to be smart. Yolande was the expert here, their only ticket out of the Dark Nether Cave. Treasure was useless if you were dead.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS PIANO]

Zane, however, was focused on a different puzzle. He lowered his voice and turned to Lavender. “If Yolande knew her brother died in here, why did she wait so long to come looking for him?”

He knew for a fact she’d been in the Tower of Sin for some time. If all she wanted were her brother’s effects, she could have come any time. The Dark Nether Cave opened every single year.

Lavender shrugged. “I’m not sure. Maybe it has something to do with you.”

“Me?” Zane frowned.

“It’s just a guess,” she said, “but she’s been on your tail since we got here. I don’t think finding her brother’s things is the only reason she came. She has another motive. And that guy Vesper—the one who gave you the Crystal Butterfly? He’s got his own agenda. If you actually find the inheritance, you’re going to have to play it by ear. I’ve got a bad feeling about him.”

Meanwhile, Yolande had gathered the mummy and placed it carefully into her Storage Ring.

[SFX: BLOOD DROP]

Then she produced the bracelet again. She bit her finger, squeezed out a single drop of blood, and let it fall onto the golden skylark at the clasp. The drop soaked in immediately.

Every eye in the group was fixed on her hands. They all knew what she was doing — refining it, binding it to herself so she could access whatever was stored inside. Malcolm, Timothy, and Garrick watched with barely concealed hunger.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

After a moment, Yolande touched the skylark and reached inside. She drew out a small object: something shaped like a crab, hanging from a long metal chain — a pendant.

She climbed out of the pit without a word and walked directly to Zane.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

"Don't move," she said. She lifted the chain and placed it over his head, letting the crab pendant rest against his chest. Then she leaned in slightly, as if catching a scent, turned her head, and walked away.

"I'll give it to you," she said over her shoulder.

Nobody spoke. Nobody had any idea what had just happened.

They didn't ask. Since Yolande had given them a glimmer of hope, following her obediently was the only sensible choice.

Yolande led the way. Zane and the others fell in behind her, with Malcolm bringing up the rear.

The landscape grew stranger the closer they got to the red dot. The desolate black earth gave way to scattered objects — remnants of those who had come before and never left.

Then, rising out of the fog ahead, was a sword.

Not the kind of sword any of them carried. This one was embedded upright in the ground and stood hundreds of feet high — taller than some mountains, its hilt swallowed entirely by the fog above. Whatever person or creature had once wielded it was beyond imagining.

Esther and Fabian moved first, both reaching for their Storage Rings simultaneously, trying to claim them. The sword didn't move.

[SFX: POWERFUL STRAIN]

Malcolm didn't bother with Storage Rings. He flew straight up, grabbed the hilt with both hands, and pulled. The fruit was still active in his blood — strong enough to lift a small mountain. The sword didn't move.

[SFX: FRUSTRATED GRUNT]

After a grunt of frustration, Malcolm finally hopped down from the giant sword embedded in the ground.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

“I can’t move it!” he announced, clasping his hands behind his back as he walked away. The bitter sting of finding a mountain of treasure and having to leave empty-handed was written all over his face.

Just then, Zane stepped forward, approaching the blade.

Malcolm sneered. “Hah! And you think you can pull it out?”

“Zane, the weight of this sword is impossible,” Fabian warned him. “Even if you’re stronger than you look, you’re not going to budge that thing.”

Zane just smiled. “Who said anything about pulling it out?”

The moment the words left his mouth, Yolande’s expression shifted in understanding. Lavender caught on, too. “The sword’s unclaimed,” she said. “If we can’t take it with us, smelting it down isn’t a bad idea.”

“Smelt it?” Garrick narrowed his eyes and let out a short, mocking laugh. “It’s not that easy.”

“This sword is from the upper world,” Jarrod added, shaking his head. “It’s been here for ages. The power contained in that blade is beyond anything we can imagine. You could bring every master refiner in the world here, and they still wouldn’t be able to melt it down.”

Zane didn’t say a word. He walked right up to the massive sword, sat down cross-legged, and closed his eyes. He’d already revealed his black fire to the others, so there was no reason to hide it now.

[SFX: WHOOSH OF FLAME]

A split second later, a flicker of black flame shot from between his brows and latched onto the steel.

“The black fire.” Esther and Fabian had seen it before, but Malcolm hadn’t.

[SFX: SIZZLING METAL]

His jaw went slack as the instant the dark flame touched the sword, a small hole sizzled and melted directly into the ancient blade.
