Ep 870 - Activation

Word count: 1166

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Every one of the saint-level geniuses stared at Zane, the greed in their eyes so thick you could cut it with a knife. But the image of the bodies left smoking in the crater was burned into their minds, a stark reminder of what happened to the last guys who tried something.

Stealing the pickax from Zane’s hand? That wasn’t a risk. It was a suicide mission.

So, they all just hung back, keeping their distance and watching from afar.

Zane quietly studied the Black Star in his hand. It was short, more like a small hammer than a pickax. He hefted it, trying to get a feel for the weight, and scanned its surface for any markings. No matter how he looked at it, he couldn’t make heads or tails of what made this thing a divine-class weapon. He was drawing a total blank.

Does this thing need Vitality to work? he wondered.

[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]

The thought had barely crossed his mind when he summoned a shimmering thread of Vitality and eagerly poured it into the pickax. Normally, a weapon would drink it right up, but he could feel the Black Star resisting, pushing back against the flow.

Okay, plan B. Maybe I can use my perception to check it out.

[SFX: MAGICAL REPULSION]

He focused his spiritual perception on the weapon, trying to probe its secrets. But each time he reached out, an invisible force field threw him back.

Do I need to bind it with blood?

Zane blinked. It was worth a shot.

[SFX: BLOOD DROPLET]

[SFX: ENERGY REPEL]

He flicked his finger, coaxing a drop of blood to the surface of his skin. He aimed it at the pickax, but the droplet veered away at the last second, spattering on the ground beside it as if repelled by some unseen energy.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TONE]

“What the hell does it want?” he muttered, completely baffled.

He glared at the Black Star, a fresh wave of frustration washing over him. It was like finding a mountain of treasure and having no way to carry it home. There was nothing he could do.

Sighing, he glanced over at the so-called geniuses watching from a distance. None of them dared to get close, and he knew they wouldn’t have any answers for him anyway.

Just as he was about to give up, a faint, familiar voice echoed in his mind.

[SFX: MYSTICAL ECHO]

Heh. The Black Star. A divine-class weapon. I knew it was buried in the Earth Tree, but I never thought you’d actually pull it out. It was the Cobalt Dragon.

“You’re awake!” Zane’s face lit up. He’d figured the Cobalt Dragon would be out for a lot longer.

I sensed the weapon, so I woke up, the dragon said, his voice laced with exhaustion.

Zane’s frustration melted away, replaced by a jolt of hope. It sounded like the dragon knew exactly what this thing was. A huge grin spread across his face. “I can’t figure out how to use it.”

The Cobalt Dragon chuckled. It’d be a miracle if you could just pick up a divine-class weapon like the Black Star and use it. The secret isn’t about complexity. If you draw the one-star divine textures in the reverse order, you’re essentially drawing the lines of a ten-star texture.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

Zane had always assumed a ten-star texture would be impossibly intricate. The idea that it could be so simple was a revelation. The greatest truths really were the simplest. Different paths, same destination.

But there was something else pressing on his mind. “I need to bind it fast,” he said. “When the War Emperors come to collect the Stone-Eater Bugs, they’ll take it from me if I haven’t bound it yet.”

Then don’t waste time, the Cobalt Dragon said.

Zane looked around. Every eye in the clearing was on him. He wasn’t doing this with an audience.

[SFX: PICKAX STRIKING GROUND]

Without a word, he swung the Black Star into the earth at his feet. Even without being bound, its hardness was extraordinary — it punched a deep hole into the ground in a single strike. Zane dropped into it and flooded the space around him with Vitality, sealing off the opening. Anyone watching from outside would see nothing.

[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND — QUIET]

[MUSIC: FOCUSED CONCENTRATION]

In the dim space below, he reached into his Storage Ring and pulled out his rune materials. The three ten-star textures on the pickax’s head were True Dragon Lineage inheritances — nothing to do with human rune traditions. The four kingdoms had their own divine rune lineages, and he didn’t want anyone piecing together what he was doing.

[SFX: PAPER RUSTLE]

He spread out a sheet of rune paper, extended a wisp of his soul from between his brows, and connected it to his rune brush. Three one-star divine textures. He’d inscribed them hundreds of times before — they were among the first patterns he’d ever learned.

[SFX: BRUSH ON PAPER]

Within a few dozen breaths, all three were done. He held them up next to the pickax and compared them to the ten-star patterns etched into the head.

Reversed. That was all. Draw a one-star texture backward, and it became a ten-star texture exactly. The most guarded secret of a divine-class weapon, and the answer was this simple.

“So what now?” Zane asked.

Use your Vitality to activate the one-star divine textures, then imprint them onto the pickax, the Cobalt Dragon instructed.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WORKSHOP]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL TENSION]

Zane did as he was told, carefully tracing the first one-star divine texture and pressing it into the Black Star. The moment it connected, one of the three ten-star textures etched on the pickax began to glow.

He didn’t ask any more questions, just watched the soft light with a sense of awe. He continued, imprinting the second divine texture, and then the third.

[SFX: MAGICAL HUM BUILDING]

A low hum started, growing steadily into a resonant thrum.

[SFX: POWER ACTIVATION]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT CRESCENDO]

The Black Star was finally active.

A stunning, iridescent sheen shimmered across its surface, like oil on water.

“Beautiful,” Zane breathed.

Don’t celebrate just yet, the Cobalt Dragon warned. Put a drop of your blood on it now. But remember, this is a divine-class weapon. It has a will of its own, and it will judge its master. Not everyone can earn its recognition.

“Right. Got it,” Zane nodded.

After all that work, if he still couldn’t become its master, then he’d just have to accept it. At least he’d tried.

[SFX: BLOOD DROP]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Just like before, he flicked his finger, and a single drop of blood arced toward the Black Star.

It was out of his hands now. If it wasn’t meant to be, it wasn’t meant to be. 

Still, he couldn’t help but hold his breath as he watched the droplet fall, his face a perfect picture of nerves and hope.
