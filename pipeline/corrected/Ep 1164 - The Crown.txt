Ep 1164 - The Crown

Word count: 1145

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL WONDER]
[AMBIENT: COSMIC SWIRLING STARS]
Walking on a road paved with actual stars was something else entirely. 
Zane and Lavender couldn’t stop themselves from staring at the constellations swirling all around them, packed so tightly it was hard to believe Livingston had managed to capture them from a past cosmic age and forge them into this massive Star Plate. 
Just like the stars Zane had connected with before, they came in every size imaginable. Some were dozens, even hundreds of times bigger than a person, while most were just tiny, glittering grains of sand.
[SFX: GENTLE STAR DRIFT]
As they walked, a small, blue star no bigger than Zane’s fingernail drifted near him. It was stunning—it looked like a tiny, churning sea was trapped on its surface. It was so small he couldn’t quite make out the details, but right next to it floated a massive fireball, countless times larger.
“It’s so beautiful,” Lavender breathed.
Zane found himself reaching for the tiny blue star, but Livingston stopped him. “The Star Plate is made of countless stars, and every last one is an important part of this galaxy. You can’t take any of them.”
Zane shrugged and let the star drift away, continuing down the path. The road didn’t look that long, but it turned out to be a serious trek. 
[SFX: METALLIC FOOTSTEPS]
With Livingston’s metallic body clattering along in the lead, they were moving at a snail’s pace. An hour later, it felt like they hadn’t gotten any closer to the center.
“How long is this going to take?” Zane finally asked.
“Well,” Livingston began, “at this rate, it’ll take about two days and two nights to cross this half of the plate. You see, when I spent hundreds of years building this, every single star was meticulously placed—”
Zane rolled his eyes. He wasn’t waiting for the history lesson. He just grabbed Livingston and took off, sprinting down the starry road. He had to make every second count; he’d be crazy to waste two days walking. In what felt like a few moments, he’d crossed the rest of the plate and reached the heart of the galaxy.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS PULSATING]
[AMBIENT: DEEP HUMMING SPHERE]
A massive sphere of light pulsed at the center, casting out blinding scarlet rays. It had to be at least ten thousand feet across, making the sand-like stars nearby look like nothing.
“Is this the sun of the last cosmic age?” Zane asked.
Livingston reached out and plucked another star from the plate.
[SFX: STAR CHIME]
Somewhere inside the galaxy, something shifted. A palace emerged from the swirling stars — hidden until now, its outline resolving out of the light like a shape that had always been there, waiting to be found.
“That,” Livingston said, “is the core of the Wheel of Reincarnation.”
Zane and Lavender exchanged a look.
“The Wheel of Reincarnation?” Zane said. “Is that… the Endless Abyss?”
Livingston smiled. “Yes.”
[MUSIC: SLOW REVELATION]
It landed like a stone dropped into still water. The entire underground structure — everything they’d fought through from top to bottom — was a single spatial artifact. Livingston had built it. Gideon had used it.
“The Wheel of Reincarnation is mine,” Livingston added, not without pride. “I built it for Gideon. He cultivated here. And it can do considerably more than hold an abyss.”
Lavender’s eyes sharpened. She said nothing, but filed that away.
They followed Livingston through the surface of the great sphere of light and into the palace within.
[AMBIENT: EMPTY PALACE STILLNESS]
It was empty.
No Gideon. No remains. The palace was clean stone and silence — a space that had once held someone and now held only the memory of them. Too much time had passed. Even a Supreme Lord’s body, left here across an entire cosmic era, would have returned to dust long ago.
[SFX: SLOW METALLIC FOOTSTEPS ECHOING]
Livingston walked the perimeter of the empty hall slowly, his metal joints marking each step. He shook his head.
“Gideon,” he said quietly. “You entered the Six Paths of Reincarnation before me.”
[MUSIC: QUIET GRIEF]
He stood there a moment. Then moved on.
Lavender had drifted toward a far corner of the palace, where a cluster of runes was carved into the stone wall. She studied them.
“These runes — they control the Wheel of Reincarnation?”
“Yes,” Livingston said.
“And the Wheel of Reincarnation,” Lavender said carefully, “can it move? Can it leave this place?”
Livingston drew himself up. The metal head spun.
“Move?” He let out a short laugh. “You’re asking if it can fly away. Of course it can fly away. It can do far more than fly away. You’re talking about something built by the number one weaponsmith in the universe.”
Lavender’s eyes lit up.
At that moment, Zane noticed something else. On the far side of the palace, a large dragon throne sat against the wall — and above it, floating in the air, was an object unlike anything he’d seen before. It looked like a headband, or perhaps a crown. It was made of neither gold nor iron; he couldn’t tell what it was made of. Its surface gave off a faint, steady golden glow, and it floated there as if it had been waiting.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS PULSE]
“What’s this?” Zane asked. He reached out toward it.
“Zane — don’t touch that yet—”
“Strange,” Livingston said, turning. “Why is that still here?”
But they were both a breath too slow.
[SFX: CROWN VIBRATING]
Zane’s finger made contact with the crown. The moment it did, the crown shook — a single, sharp tremor that seemed to pass through the air itself.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS COSMIC WONDER]
[AMBIENT: VAST COSMIC SPACE]
Across the universe, the other Supreme Lords were all staring up at the sky, completely baffled. What was happening was beyond anything they understood.
[MUSIC: EPIC ANCIENT HISTORY]
[AMBIENT: PRIMORDIAL COSMIC VOID]
When this grand new era began, there were no Supreme Lords. The title didn’t exist, and no one could carry what was known as the Heavenly Destiny. But the universe doesn’t stay empty for long. 
After a mass extinction and hundreds of millions of years of evolution, new races emerged. And then the competition began.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ASCENSION THEME]
The rules were simple: there was a hard limit to how many Supreme Lords could carry the Heavenly Destiny. The early bird got the worm, and in the beginning, anyone who managed to surpass the power of a World Lord was granted the title of Supreme Lord and the right to carry that destiny.
In this new Cosmic Age, which they called Cosmos, humanity had gotten a head start. Because of that, they snagged the most spots. In fact, the three great human factions alone had more Supreme Lords than all the other races combined.
But that was the problem. 
[SFX: HEAVY DOORS CLOSING]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RESTRICTION]
[AMBIENT: SEALED COSMIC BARRIER]
The system was supposed to be closed. With one hundred and twenty-eight Supreme Lords already in place, no one else could ascend — no matter how strong they became. To take that title now, you had to wait for one of the existing ones to die and seize their Heavenly Destiny.
Somewhere out there, one hundred and twenty-eight Supreme Lords were asking the same question. And none of them were going to like the answer.
