Ep 788 - How Did He Do It?

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

 The competition was getting brutal. One by one, warriors were being thrown from the circle, and the crowd was thinning fast.

Through it all, Zane moved like a phantom. He was cautious, quick on his feet, and impossible to pin down. Whenever someone lunged for him, he’d zip out of the way in a flash, staying just out of reach but always inside the boundary of the red circle.

It was pure chaos in there, with no clear alliances or targets. Everyone was just trying to knock out as many people as possible until only three remained. In a frenzy like that, nobody had the energy to waste on stubbornly chasing a ghost like Zane.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

 Almost nobody. Lord Randy’s venomous gaze was locked on Zane the entire time, and the constant pressure was really starting to get on his nerves.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]

 And Randy wasn’t the only one watching. From a distance, three powerful masters who had reached the Soul Sea Realm also had their eyes fixed on him.

“He’s got a solid handle on Spatial Law,” one of them murmured, “Looks like he’s even mastered the second level. The kid has potential.”

“Based on the strength he’s showing, I’d call him a saint-level genius,” another added. “He could definitely make it to the Third Tier in the Harlen Divine Kingdom. Hell, I wouldn’t be surprised if he hit the top of the Third Tier.”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

 “A guy like that deserves a Harlen Token,” the third master said, a troubled look on his face. “But something’s bothering me.”

With their keen insight, they could all see that Lord Randy was gunning for Zane.

[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT ARENA]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

 “Randy already declared he’s the only one getting a token this round,” the first master said, sighing. “And with his power, he can probably back that up. I’m worried he’s going to go after Zane.”

“It’s a shame. You rarely see this kind of talent in an unaffiliated warrior. I hope he makes it through.”

“It’ll be tough,” the third master concluded grimly. “Only three people in the entire Harlen Divine Kingdom are in the First Tier, and the power gap between them and the Second Tier is massive. Unless this Zane kid is strong enough to top the Second Tier, he doesn't stand a chance against Lord Randy.”

Of course, Zane had no idea he was the subject of so much concern.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE THEME]

 He just knew Randy was watching him with a little too much interest. Randy had come into this assessment with a goal beyond just securing his own token — he'd been scouting for promising young fighters to recruit. He'd originally had his eye on Edwin. But Edwin was the Crown Prince's man. There was no flipping him. This unknown independent, though — no allegiances, no master, no one who'd gotten to him first.

Randy had a knack for reading people, a skill inherited from his father. From the moment he laid eyes on Zane, he knew the kid's power ran deeper than what he was showing. Those masters in the Soul Sea Realm could only judge Zane on the surface, pegging his strength at the top of the Third Tier. But Randy saw something more. 

He was convinced Zane was hiding it — that his real strength put him somewhere in the Second Tier. A Second Tier warrior with no existing loyalties was exactly what Randy was looking for. The only problem was he still hadn't seen enough to be sure. Zane had done nothing but dodge. Randy needed to see him actually fight.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

 Meanwhile, a tough-looking warrior had finally locked onto Zane, his eyes burning with determination. This test was a crucial step, a chance that could change his entire life, and he had to pass no matter what. If he could just get rid of Zane and let the other fights play out, only three people would be left. He’d be one of them.

He had to take Zane down. Now.

Down to five. The circle had started to shrink. Randy watched with a faint smile as the remaining warriors unleashed their trump cards — the chaos finally forcing everyone's hand. None of them went near Randy. One turned his full attention to Zane. Nowhere left to run.

He went all out, pouring every ounce of his strength into a single strike meant to end it. To him, Zane was just a coward who only knew how to run.

 But in the final second, the impossible happened.

Zane retreated, step by step, letting his opponent press forward — deliberately backing all the way to the very edge of the red circle. One more step and he'd be eliminated. His opponent saw it and grinned.

Then Zane's aura changed.

The warrior saw Zane backing away, a clear retreat. 

[SFX: LIGHTNING FLASH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

Then, in the blink of an eye, like a flash of lightning, Zane was right in front of him. How?

It was impossible.

He was so stunned that he just stared blankly, his mind wiped clean.

Zane ignored the dumbfounded look on his face. He shot his hands out, grabbing his opponent’s wrists. With a precise squeeze, his fingers locked onto the man’s pulse points, cutting off the flow of Vitality to his arms and making it impossible for him to fight back. Not that it mattered; the guy was so dazed he’d forgotten how to channel his energy anyway.

[SFX: POWERFUL THROW]

[SFX: BODY IMPACT]

 While the warrior was still reeling, Zane let go of his wrists, grabbed him with one hand, and with a clean, powerful twist, tossed him straight out of the red circle.

It was so smooth it was almost casual.

For fighters at lower levels, getting physically thrown was common. But once you reached the Inner-Core Realm, the force of your Vitality was so terrifying that no one dared get that close. Every battle in the circle had ended with someone being blasted out by a powerful attack, not manhandled.

So when the other warriors saw Zane literally throw a man out of the ring, they were too shocked to speak. 

The same question echoed in all their minds: How in the world did he just do that?
