In truth, the easiest way for Zane to get out of this was to just open the door and leave.
Warriors got picked off in the dark all the time, even inside a safe zone, but no race would dare pull a stunt like that out in the open.
If Zane walked out of this practice room, the Ogres wouldn't lift a finger to protect this assassin. The mission was supposed to be over by now. Whether he’d killed Zane or not, the assassin was a dead man walking.
"You have to know you can't kill me," Zane replied, a faint smile playing on his lips. "Especially not in a tight space like this. You're outmatched. You will lose."
The assassin’s face fell, despair flickering in his small eyes. "So, if you're right… if I'm just one of their death soldiers… then I'm dead either way. Success or failure, it doesn't matter."
Zane turned to face him with a knowing smile. "You mean the Ogres?"
[SFX: BLADE HUMMING]
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION RISING]
The assassin didn't answer. In a situation like this, admitting who hired him was pointless. But he wasn't giving up. Instead, he prepared his strongest attack, the bone blade in his hand starting to pulse with an eerie red light.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Any counterattack fueled by life-essence was a desperate, all-or-nothing move—and incredibly powerful. The assassin had already blown his one perfect shot; this next one would be a gamble for his very life.
Seeing the assassin remain silent, Zane pressed on. "I think we can make a deal."
"What kind of deal?" the assassin asked, his voice sharp with curiosity.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FLUTE]
"Teach me your Technique of Folded Light," Zane offered, "and I'll let you walk out of here alive." The Twilight Group's method of concealment was one of a kind. It wasn't just about masking a presence; it was true, temporary invisibility. Zane was fascinated by it. He wanted it.
For a split second, the assassin’s eyes lit up.
He was a death soldier, after all. From the day he’d joined the Twilight Group, he’d been conditioned to sacrifice himself for them. But the will to live is a primal instinct, and some creatures will do anything to survive. Even death soldiers flinched when their own end was staring them in the face, though that hesitation usually came too late to matter.
It would’ve been one thing if Zane had just killed him after the botched attempt. But he was being offered a way out. It was a lifeline to a drowning man.
The assassin descended slowly from the wall. For a moment, something unreadable crossed his face. Then he spoke, his voice almost calm. "I want to agree." He took a step toward Zane. "Unfortunately… only those born of my people can master this technique. It cannot be taught."
That was all the warning Zane got.
[SFX: SMOKE BURST]
Black smoke erupted from the assassin's body. Ten shadows tore free of him at once, each one hurling itself at Zane from a different angle — a last, desperate gamble.
[MUSIC: CHAOTIC SURGE]
But Zane's eyes flashed green.
He stepped forward — a single, precise movement — and was beside the assassin before the man could react.
"You had one chance," Zane said quietly. "You didn't take it."
[SFX: HEAVY PUNCH]
[SFX: BODY IMPACT]
His fist drove through the assassin's chest. A spray of crimson hit the wall. The man slid down it slowly, eyes wide and full of something that wasn't quite anger — more like regret.
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
Zane stood over him for a moment. He couldn't know for certain, but he had a feeling the assassin hadn't really had a choice. Death soldiers didn't serve willingly. The Ogres would have had leverage — family, clansmen, someone the man couldn't abandon. Walk away from the mission, and everyone he cared about died with him.
He shook his head slowly.
The Tower would take care of the body. Within hours, there'd be nothing left — no trace, no evidence. Just another soul swallowed by the walls.
[AMBIENT: TOWER CORRIDOR]
[MUSIC: QUIET TENSION]
Zane stepped out of the practice room and immediately clocked the Ogre standing a short distance down the corridor, eyes fixed on the door.
Watching.
Zane walked straight up to him.
The Ogre opened his mouth, then closed it. "I… I don't know you."
Zane smiled pleasantly. "Of course you don't. But do me a favor — pass something along for me." He held the Ogre's gaze. "Tell your people: if they want me dead, the arena's right there. Come find me like warriors. This kind of thing?" He gestured back toward the practice room. "It's beneath you."
The Ogre sputtered, his pride flaring."We Ogres are never cowards!"
Zane just waved a hand and walked away.

In the Tower, the rules were simple and ran around the clock. The strong gathered Lucky Light, and their cultivation skyrocketed. The weak either got stuck on a floor, their progress stalling out, or they became stepping stones for everyone else.
The fourth and fifth floors passed without incident. One reckless giant decided to test his luck in the arena — and paid for it. Everyone else stood aside and let Zane through.

Even though Zane's unusual strength was becoming known, most War Generals were skeptical when they saw his cultivation level and his race. A human War Fighter on the sixth floor? It didn't add up.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[MUSIC: AGGRESSIVE DRUMS]

He was challenged so often that winning by default was off the table. On the sixth floor alone, he fought seven matches, killing six of his opponents and letting one human War Fighter walk away. After a week of that, the new War Generals finally got the message: Zane was unstoppable. Over the next three days, he picked up the three default wins he needed and moved on to the seventh floor without a hitch.
