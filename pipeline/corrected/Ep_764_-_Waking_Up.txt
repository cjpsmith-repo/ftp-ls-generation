Ep 764 - Waking Up

Word count: 1078

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL WIND]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

 The two siblings were destined for short, unlucky lives. It felt like their fate was etched into the stars, pushing them through one hardship after another just so they could fulfill some grander purpose.

Growing up, they were rarely ever together. Every time it seemed like they might finally have a moment, something would tear them apart again. A warrior could grow stronger through skill and luck, but escaping destiny? That was a different game entirely.

Just as everyone had told Zane, Elara was his true north. Every step he took, every choice he made, was somehow connected to her. Their lives were hopelessly, beautifully intertwined.

Back when he was with House Lowell, his only goal had been to find his sister at the Skyreach Sect. Once he found her, his mission became saving her. He’d thrown himself into his training, becoming a top disciple until he finally earned the right to pull her from the depths of Hell Mountain. But after a reunion that felt like it was over before it began, they were separated again, and years had passed without a word.

 Elara had been trapped in a void. With all six of her senses blocked, she had no idea what was happening around her—she couldn't even feel the passage of time. Two years ago, her mind had simply stopped recording new memories. She had all but given up hope, clinging to the one thought that kept her sane: the impossible chance that her brother could actually save her from Maddox.

There was no doubt Zane was a prodigy, but he was stuck in the Eastern Territories. He’d faced his share of monsters there, but the Heartlands were a whole other beast, home to fighters who were just as brutal, if not more so.

She never expected that the instant she opened her eyes, he would be standing right in front of her. Zane walked toward her and wrapped his arms around her. She buried her head in his chest, still unable to believe she was really back at his side. 

She wondered if he'd somehow made a deal with Maddox just to see her — and couldn't understand how he'd appeared here at all. She didn't dare ask. She was afraid of the answer. But she was a warrior. She knew there were questions she had to face. "Brother," she finally said. "Where is Maddox?"

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL PIANO]

 Seeing the shock on Elara’s face, Zane offered a gentle smile. “I would never lie to you,” he said, his voice earnest and low. “Maddox is dead. Gone for good. I’m the one who killed him.”

“I believe you,” Elara said, her voice firm. If Zane said it was true, it was true. He said he wasn’t lying, and he never would. Still, a knot of worry tightened in her chest. “Then… you’ll get me out of here?”

 If Maddox was really dead, she was finally free. She could go back to her brother, safe and sound, with nothing standing in their way. Even with her senses severed, her mind had kept turning, dreaming of this exact moment—the day her brother would come and rescue her.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

 But Zane just shook his head. “No.”

The light in Elara’s eyes flickered out. Her voice hitched, rising in disbelief. “Why?”

Just then, Zane turned his head, looking past her. “Can you give me a little more time?”

The young man with the feathered crown seemed to understand patience on a cosmic scale. He simply smiled. “Time is meaningless to me. Do what you need to do.” 

“Thank you,” Zane said, giving the man a grateful nod before taking Elara’s hand. He led her out of the Ethereal Spirit Hall.

[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL GARDEN]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

Though the Ethereal Spirit Sect had been invaded by the Corrupted Divine Kingdom, it now served as their temporary headquarters, so the grounds were pristine. The lush, green scenery was a stark contrast to the wreckage of Ethereal City, which had been looted and torn apart by rogue fighters.

They walked side by side down a shady, tree-lined path. Zane seemed calm, but Elara’s face was a storm of confusion. She was stuck on one question, turning it over and over in her mind. Why couldn’t he take her with him?

 After a few moments of silence, she suddenly stopped. “Why can’t you just take me with you?” she demanded, her expression souring like a child throwing a tantrum.

Zane thought for a moment before answering her honestly. “Because I’m not the one who will be taking you. He is.” He nodded back toward the hall they’d just left.

“I don’t want to go with him,” Elara shot back, shaking her head. They’d been apart for so long. Now that Maddox was gone, she couldn’t understand why they couldn’t just leave together.

Zane smiled bitterly. "I can't beat him. He's far too powerful."

"So what?" Elara said. "Is he more powerful than Maddox?"

"Yes," Zane said. "Much more powerful. Much, much more."

Elara's eyes narrowed. "Then we run. He hasn't tied our hands. Let's go now."

Zane shook his head. Running wasn't an option. Even if they tried — where would they go? The young man hadn't come from the Heartlands. Someone that powerful could track them anywhere in the blink of an eye. That was why he'd let them walk out without a second glance. He wasn't afraid they'd run. He knew they couldn't.

Zane paused. “Why don’t I tell you everything that’s happened since we were separated? We can walk while I do.”

Elara nodded eagerly. As upset as she was, she was desperate to know what her brother had been through all these years—especially how he’d managed to defeat Maddox so quickly.

“I saw you, actually,” Zane began. “At the Martial Tournament two years ago. But all your senses were blocked then.”

“Really?” she breathed. She had no memory of it at all.

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

 As they continued down the winding green path, Zane told her everything. 

He held nothing back, sharing every trial and triumph: the Cloud Road Trial, enrolling in the Cloud Hall, the Martial Tournament, the fight for the Dragon Soar Arena, his battle with Marilyn, activating the enchanted lamp, and his journey to the main continent. 

They had been apart for far too long, and the truth was the only gift he had left to give her.
