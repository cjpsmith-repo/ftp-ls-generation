Ep 956 - Appointment For A Fight
Word count: 1037
[AMBIENT: TENSE HALL ATMOSPHERE]
Tia sat quietly in her seat, seeming completely detached from everything going on.
But Elder Imogen was relentlessly laying into Cora, and the argument had gotten ugly.
Around Zane, Cora was usually quiet and agreeable, but the insults being hurled at her were too much. The leader of Cloud Hall might be a master of the Life-and-Death Realm, but Cora wasn’t scared. After all, her great-grandfather was right there with her.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
“Elder Imogen, you say I’m not worthy of Zane, so what?” she said stubbornly. “We may not be officially married, but we’re a couple in every way that counts!”
[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]
Her words plunged the entire hall into a stunned silence.
A cup of top-grade, century-old vintage sat untouched in front of Tia. But the moment Cora spoke, she reached out with her slender fingers and downed the drink in one gulp. Thanks to her training, she’d never get drunk, but the harsh bite of the liquor still sent a flush across her cheeks.
[MUSIC: COLD TENSION]
“Shameless,” she muttered, her voice cold as ice.
Intimacy wasn’t exactly a taboo subject in the martial world, but for Cora to announce it so bluntly in the middle of Cloud Hall was just… classless.
Tia’s comment made Xander furious. “How can you talk to her like that?”
Of course, Elder Imogen wasn’t about to let an outsider scold Tia on their home turf.
[SFX: ERUPTING ARGUMENTS]
[MUSIC: CHAOTIC CONFRONTATION]
A moment later, the silence was shattered again, giving way to a storm of sharp words and nasty arguments.
Not even Kieran, a natural peacemaker, could settle things down. The confrontation dragged on until, finally, it ended with Cora challenging Tia to a fight.
[AMBIENT: GUILD HALL]
[MUSIC: URGENT PERCUSSION]
“The fight is in Cloudhaven?” Zane asked after Kieran laid out the whole story.
Kieran nodded grimly. “Yes, it’s in Cloudhaven!”
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]
The second Kieran confirmed it, Zane was already moving, rushing for the portal.
From the World Merchants’ Guild’s point of view, this whole thing could have been solved peacefully, without a formal challenge. They believed it was always better for people to work out their differences.
But it wasn’t their place to interfere. Besides, Zane was back now. Maybe he was the key to fixing this mess for good.
[SFX: PORTAL WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: DIMENSIONAL TRAVEL]
Once he entered the portal in Oracle City, Zane shot through the channel, heading for Cloudhaven. With his incredible speed, it took him only a moment to reach the other side.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION FLASH]
[AMBIENT: CLOUDHAVEN PLAZA]
With a flash of white light, Zane materialized from the teleportation array in Cloudhaven.
A pair of Soul Realm guards stepped forward on instinct, asking for identification. Zane was already past them before the words left their mouths. One guard moved to sound the alarm — the other caught his arm. “That’s Zane. From Cloud Hall.” The first guard turned to look, but there was nothing left to see.
Above the plaza, Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors had gathered to watch — drawn by the clash of names, if nothing else. Alongside them, Life-and-Death Realm masters: Elder Imogen, Elder Xander. For the Heartlands, this was an unprecedented gathering, and all of it was over one man.
He had a gentle smile on his face as his gaze moved from Cora to Tia.
The two women faced each other across the open sky. Tia’s Ice Phoenix feathers — several feet long — swept in the breeze behind her. Her Icy Blue Halberd was already formed, its pale blue light sharp and cold. She stared at Cora without blinking.
Fighting over a man wasn’t something Tia would ever have chosen. But the man was Zane. When she thought of his kiss, something fierce and quiet rose in her chest. How could she share that with anyone? She couldn’t. So here she was.
[MUSIC: QUIET RESOLVE — BOTH SIDES]
Cora felt the full weight of what she was facing. Standing before a Life-and-Death Realm warrior was a pressure most Inner-Core cultivators couldn’t endure. But there was only one thought in her mind: stay by Zane’s side, no matter what. She didn’t need to win everything. She didn’t pursue perfection the way Tia did, and she had no desire to push Tia out entirely. She just wanted her place. That was all. And she wasn’t going to give it up.
She stretched out her hand. The sky rippled. Black shadows gathered above her — massive swords pulling through from the Violet Core Realm, dozens of them, then hundreds, their edges humming with restrained force.
[SFX: LOW DIMENSIONAL RUMBLE]
Tia’s grip tightened on the halberd.
[MUSIC: COLLISION IMMINENT]
“What are you two doing?” he asked, playing dumb.
“Zane!”
Relief washed over Cora the moment she saw him. She had never really wanted to fight Tia in the first place. The massive swords shimmering in the sky above her vanished as she ran toward him and took hold of his arm.
Meanwhile, Tia remained on guard, her Icy Blue Halberd still aimed at Cora as she stared at Zane.
At the first level of the Life-and-Death Realm, Tia could hold her own against warriors two or even three levels above her. Cora knew that. Everyone there knew that.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
She would never be so bold as to throw herself into a man’s arms the way Cora just had, but a flicker of hurt in her eyes was unmistakable as she watched them.
Xander and Brent both broke into wide smiles when they saw Zane and greeted him warmly.
Brent, the Ethereal Spirit Sect’s top talent, had always wanted to challenge Zane but never got the chance. Still, he called him “brother-in-law.”
[SFX: SHARP EXHALE]
Hearing that, Elder Imogen’s face twisted with anger. She let out a loud, cold snort, making her displeasure known.
Zane quickly turned to her and gave a respectful nod. “Elder Imogen, I heard you recently got back from your travels overseas. It’s wonderful to see you.”
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Elder Imogen scoffed. “Are you really? I bet you were hoping I’d die out there!” She had always thought highly of Zane, but his relationship with Cora made her furious with him.
Zane, however, kept the smile plastered on his face. He knew that stopping the fight between the two women was his top priority. Since Elder Imogen was clearly on Tia’s side, of course she was going to be hostile.
[AMBIENT: ICY WIND]
[MUSIC: COLD TENSION]
As for Tia, she stood as still as a flawless ice sculpture, but the moment her gaze landed on Cora, her expression turned glacial.
