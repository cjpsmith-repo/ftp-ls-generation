Ep 862 - Clarity

Word count: 1066

[AMBIENT: QUIET CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL HEALING]

Fiona knelt beside Zane, her hands cupped as she channeled the Purification Fire, slowly coaxing the flame into his body.

[SFX: MAGICAL RESONANCE]

Through the haze of the curse, Zane could see the serious set of her jaw, the intense focus in her eyes. As the flame worked its way through him, a strange thing happened. 

The fog of aggression and raw instinct began to clear, and with it, any trace of desire for the woman in front of him. His mind settled into a strange, quiet place, leaving him free to simply admire the princess of the Wind Divine Kingdom for what she was.

Her eyelashes fluttered with concentration, and her hair spilled over her shoulders like a perfect, dark waterfall. But when her gaze flickered up and met his, a sudden shyness broke her focus. A blush crept up her pale cheeks, and her fingers trembled, causing the Purification Fire to pulse in time with her racing heart.

[MUSIC: TENSE CONCENTRATION]

Zane had absorbed a hell of a lot of curse power from the weapons he’d melted down—it was an unavoidable side effect of absorbing all that Celestial Essence. Now, that power was a toxic sludge built up inside him. 

Fiona’s Purification Fire was a miracle, but it demanded incredible control. Even as a master of the third layer of Fire Law, all the Vitality she could muster only produced a stream of fire about as thick as her thumb. Cleansing him completely was going to take a while.

[SFX: CRACKLING PURIFICATION FIRE]

Still, under the steady flow of her flame, the curse began to fade. An hour crawled by. Tiny beads of sweat dotted Fiona’s forehead, but the dark energy radiating from Zane’s body had grown weaker and weaker.

[SFX: FLAME EXTINGUISHING]

“Whew! It’s done!” Fiona let out a long sigh, a relieved smile finally breaking across her face as she extinguished the flame.

[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION SPACE]

Even after the fire was gone, Zane kept looking at her. For that entire hour, he’d been in a state of absolute clarity, a truly amazing feeling. He realized her Purification Fire could be used for so much more than just curses. Any warrior struggling to calm their mind during training, flooded with distracting thoughts—her flame could just wipe the slate clean.

And now, after staring at her for an hour without a single stray thought, her face was etched into his memory.

Fiona noticed he was still watching her. Her expression turned unnatural. “You still haven’t told me how you got the curse energy into your body,” she said.

Zane thought for a moment, then smiled. “Just think of it as me eating the weapons.”

Fiona stared at him. She clearly didn’t believe it. Her eyes drifted past him to the copper boxes lining the walls. Every single one was empty. Not a weapon left. She looked back at Zane.

She’d been quietly observing him since they arrived at the Sacred Feather Sea. She’d thought she was learning more about him. Instead, he just kept getting more mysterious — as if every answer peeled back a layer to reveal three more secrets underneath. Exploring the Secret Land had felt like a vacation. Unraveling Zane was turning into its own kind of game.

Zane was already thinking ahead. He’d absorbed enough Celestial Essence to light up fourteen thousand Dragon Scales — but even that might not be enough to crack the golden leaf and get the Black Star Pickax out. The Ancient Heartstones were still his best path forward. He’d take what he could secure this year, and if he had to, he’d come back next year.

He was ready to move. Together, he and Fiona left the armory and made their way back to the pyramid.

[AMBIENT: MINING SITE — ACTIVE]

[MUSIC: BUSTLING ACTIVITY]

Most of the warriors who’d been scavenging the armory had already returned. With the Stone-Eater Bug threat cleared, the miners had gone back to the tree, and the trading area in front of the pyramid was alive again — voices overlapping, ores changing hands, deals being struck in every direction.

Camilla fell into step beside Zane. “Thirty-six more,” she said quietly. “That’s everything we’re going to get.”

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS RUNNING]

Just then, a voice shattered the quiet. “Fiona, look what I dug up!”

Callan, Fiona’s brother, came running over from a massive pile of ore, his face alight with excitement.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]

He held up a strange, black stone about the size of his fist, its surface covered in what looked like clouds of fire.

“What is it?” Fiona asked, blinking at the stone with open curiosity. The royal siblings didn’t know much about ores, and her brother’s sheer delight made her lean in closer.

Zane stared at it, too, just as confused. He couldn’t see anything special about it, other than its odd shape. 

The fiery patterns swirling across it were so uniform they almost looked like they’d been carved by hand. While he wasn’t an expert, his instincts were usually sharp enough to tell treasure from trash. He’d given Camilla a pile of ores earlier, and he knew that while some were common, at least seventy percent of them had been quite rare. This one, though… this one set off no alarms.

“This is a Black Heart Fire Stone!” Callan announced with a grin. “It’s incredibly useful for any warrior who practices Fire Law. Fiona, it’s for you!”

Zane’s expression only grew more puzzled. Camilla, who had been watching quietly, blinked a few times. “Black Heart Fire Stone? I’ve never heard of an ore like that.”

There were over a thousand types of ore in the mine, and Camilla could identify almost all of them on sight. Black Heart Fire Stone was not one of them. She studied the stone in the prince’s hand, her mind racing. The Crown Prince was a simple, honest guy. If he’d found it himself, that was one thing.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

But if he’d traded for it… he could have been scammed. Badly.

Worry tightening in her chest, Camilla focused her Vitality, sending a silent warning to Zane.

Zane was already suspicious, and her mental nudge was all the confirmation he needed. He looked at the prince. 

“Your Highness, did you dig this Black Heart Fire Stone up yourself, or did you trade for it?”
