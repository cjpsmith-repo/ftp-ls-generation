Ep 563 - All Eyes on Zane
Word count: 1118
[SFX: ARROW WHOOSH AND THUD]
The arrow thudded into the dirt, inches from Zane’s boot. Not a miss. A warning.
If he and his followers took one more step, the next one wouldn't be aimed at the ground. It would be aimed at his heart.
Zane paused, weighing his options.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS FOREST]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Just then, a massive black tree nearby began to glow with an eerie green light.
[SFX: DEEP TREE GROAN AND ROOTS TEARING]
With a deep groan, it tore its thick roots from the earth, shaking violently as it started to lumber toward them.
[SFX: CROWD PANIC AND SCRAMBLING]
The bizarre sight sent a wave of panic through the crowd. They scrambled, tripping over each other to hide behind Zane.
But Zane was unnervingly calm. A walking tree was nothing new to him. He’d faced one on the Cloud Road, though it was a sapling compared to this behemoth.
The tree shuffled closer, its thick branches waving like massive arms.
[SFX: DEEP GRINDING VOICE]
"Human," it boomed, its voice like grinding stone. "Turn back. You cannot pass."
The message was clear. He needed another route. His eyes scanned the terrain. Alone, he could slip through anywhere. But with hundreds of refugees depending on him, he couldn't afford to be reckless.
[SFX: MENACING GROWL]
Suddenly, a low growl rumbled from behind him. "I wouldn't move if I were you, kid."
Zane spun around.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]
[AMBIENT: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
A wall of Ogres, hundreds of them, blocked their path.
Most were on par with the Ogre Governor he’d fought before, but two of them stood out, practically radiating power. The one who’d spoken was one of them.
Zane eyed the two leaders, a faint smile playing on his lips. "And why's that?" he asked.
"Because you can either stay put, or you can die," the Ogre sneered. "A little Level Seven War Fighter like you? You're not even a threat."
Zane's curiosity was piqued. He'd been wondering about the martial hierarchy on this continent anyway. "If I'm a Level Seven War Fighter, what does that make you?"
"I'm no mere Fighter," the Ogre boomed, puffing out his massive chest. "I'm a Level Five War General."
[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]
"Is that so?" The smile on Zane's lips sharpened. In a heartbeat, the power coiled inside him exploded outward.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
He shot forward, a blur of motion. It was the advantage of a body honed to perfection—the ability to unleash his full strength in a split second.
He crossed the several-hundred-foot gap in a flash, appearing right in front of the Ogre, his body poised to slam into the creature like a cannonball.
The Level Five War General wasn't slow, but his kind relied on brute force. A human charging head-on was unheard of—suicidal.
A wicked grin split the Ogre's face. He raised his shoulder and charged to meet Zane. No dodge. No retreat.
[SFX: MAGIC ENERGY SURGE]
But in the instant before impact, Zane's eyes narrowed. Five hundred dragon scales erupted across his body. His forceful energy surged to a level that defied belief.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS COLLISION]
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT]
The collision produced a sound like a thunderclap. The Ogre War General—ten feet tall, his bones harder than any human's—was obliterated. Shattered into a spray of blood and armor fragments that scattered in every direction.
Zane hung in the air, a faint layer of dark energy on his skin—not for protection, but to keep the blood off his clothes.
He looked down at the remaining Ogres.
"What?" he said quietly. "Do you still want me to stay?"
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PAUSE]
It took a long, silent moment for the other Ogres to process what they had just seen.
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
Their dazed confusion slowly curdled into shock, then disbelief, and finally, a deep, burning humiliation.
Ogres were a proud, fearless race. To die in the heat of battle was the greatest honor. But to be killed—instantly—by a lone human? It was the ultimate disgrace. An absolute embarrassment.
They didn't waste a second trying to understand how Zane had done it. All that mattered was tearing him limb from limb.
[SFX: DEAFENING ROAR]
[AMBIENT: CHARGING ARMY]
The second Ogre War General let out a deafening roar, echoed by the hundreds of War Fighters behind him as they charged.
"Tear the human to pieces!"
"I'll flay him alive!"
"Grind his bones to dust!"
[SFX: GROUND SHAKING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
Their battle cries shook the ground as they stormed toward Zane.
[AMBIENT: TENSE WILDERNESS]
Twenty miles away, more than twenty humans were hidden in the dense forest. The bearded general from the fortress wall stared, his face drained of color.
"This kid... are we sure he's human?"
"Obviously, he's human," someone snapped.
The silver-haired young man's green eyes flickered. "Human, yes. But whatever cultivation method he's using, it's nothing like ours. A Level Seven War Fighter just obliterated a Level Five War General—an Ogre. Could he be from one of the Holy Lands?"
"Impossible. If the Holy Lands sent someone, we'd know. And if he's this talented, would they really let him wander alone?" another voice cut in. "If someone like this were properly nurtured and sent into the Tower of Sin, he could win untold advantages for the human race. He cannot be allowed to die."
"Agreed," the silver-haired man said, his eyes blazing green. "We protect him. At all costs."
The bearded general ran his thumb along the edge of his scimitar and chuckled. "Let's hold and see how this plays out first. I don't think these Ogres can touch him."
[AMBIENT: FOREST CANOPY]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Ten miles behind the ancient tree, three figures leaned against its trunk.
One of the women carried a bone bow across her back, longer than she was tall, with a quiver of five bone arrows.
"This human is very unique," she said quietly.
"Unique has nothing to do with us," the male beside her replied. "As long as he stays out of our territory, we're fine."
"I know." With the silent grace of a leopard, she leaped from one branch to another, her tall, lithe frame scaling the tree until she reached the top.
[SFX: GROUND TREMBLING]
[MUSIC: DRIVING BATTLE DRUMS]
The wave of Ogres crashed toward Zane, but he stood his ground, perfectly calm.
Killing humans might weigh on his conscience, but these Ogres? It didn't give him a moment's pause. Despite their intelligence, taking them down felt no different than slaughtering livestock.
[MUSIC: DARK EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
"Let me clear a path to your graves," Zane said, his voice cold as ice. After everything he had survived, his heart had hardened beyond breaking.
