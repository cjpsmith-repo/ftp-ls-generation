Ep 101 - It is him
Word Count: 1176
[MUSIC: SINISTER TENSION]
[AMBIENT: AUCTION HOUSE MURMURS]
"Zane Lowell? So it is him."
The name definitely rang a bell. Felix remembered this guy knocking his brother, Connor, out of The Gauntlet. After being eliminated, Connor had begged him to get rid of Zane Lowell. Felix wasn't about to waste his own time on some nobody at the Core Strength Level, so he’d ordered one of his loyalists, Miles, to handle it.
He never expected Miles to screw it up. But Miles got his ass handed to him by Zane and came back crying about it. Back then, Felix had just written Zane off as some clown not worth his time. He couldn't be bothered.
It wasn't until Liam from Misty Peak came asking for a favor that Felix finally sent Graves and Roman Stryker down to The Sunbelt to take Zane out for good. He never imagined that Liam, Graves, and Roman Stryker would all end up dead down there.
And now, just a few days later, here was Zane, right in the same auction house, making trouble for him.
As he pieced it all together, the smile on Felix's face stretched, becoming wider, more sinister. He strolled down the aisle of the auction house, heading straight for the private suite. As heads turned to follow his every move, a nervous hush fell over the crowd.
Felix had a reputation in The Capital. He was wild, arrogant, and completely fearless. If he started something here, it was going to be a massive headache for House Blackwood.
[SFX: HURRIED FOOTSTEPS]
Just then, Dustin and his men were heading for Zane's room. The House Blackwood staff had already filled him in on the beef between Zane and Felix, and Dustin knew Zane had already clashed with one of Felix's lackeys up at Misty Peak. That’s why he’d rounded up his crew and was rushing over now, moving quickly to block Felix’s path.
"Felix!" Dustin greeted him with a broad smile.
Felix didn't slow down. Instead he stepped forward and wrapped his arms around Dustin in an unexpected embrace, pulling him close. "I'll respect your family's house," he murmured into Dustin's ear, his tone pleasant but final. "I just want a word with the boy. Step aside."
Dustin's forehead was damp. He knew exactly what Felix was capable of. He nodded stiffly and moved.
Felix’s favorite weapon was psychological pressure; he loved to mercilessly torture an opponent's mind. From what he’d dug up, Zane was just some low-life commoner with no connections. Felix had already come up with a thousand different ways to kill him.
But physical pain was boring. Felix much preferred to break a man's spirit. That was his signature.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Felix stopped in front of Zane and smiled. "You beat up my family's man. Killed my assassins. And now you've made trouble for me here." He tilted his head. "People like you don't tend to last very long."
"Oh, really?" Zane said, a sneer spreading across his lips. "You might think you're some big shot, throwing threats around like that, but I've heard it all before. Seriously, can't you come up with something original? It’s a cliché."
Hearing that, the smile on Felix's face froze.
Zane added, "If you want to take me out, then come and get me. You and your family mean nothing to me, so save the empty threats. By the way, I have the Principles of Forging now.
I'm going to memorize every single word, and then I'm going to burn it to ash. If you think you're getting it from me, you'll have to crack my skull open and try to read it off my brain!"
[SFX: HEARTBEAT RISING]
Felix never expected such defiance. The guy had the nerve of a lion, acting completely superior.
House Sterling—one of the Seven Great Houses of the Empire, a power since the Empire began. And Zane didn't even blink at the name. If his family name couldn't make this guy flinch, what the hell would?
Felix had always prided himself on being wild and unpredictable, but that entire attitude was built on the power of his family name. Compared to Zane, it was night and day. This guy was unbelievably arrogant.
[MUSIC: DARK RAGE]
Felix was utterly humiliated. Deep down, he wanted to tear Zane limb from limb. And he could have. But killing someone inside The Gilded Gavel would probably piss off House Blackwood. His family could handle the fallout, though.
Everyone knew there was no such thing as a permanent enemy between the Great Houses. If a problem did pop up between House Sterling and House Blackwood, his family could just smooth things over by offering them a piece of the pie.
He’d screwed up. He’d shown respect for House Blackwood, promising not to hurt Zane here.
Damn it. I shouldn't have said that, Felix thought, seething with regret.
He had been too cocky, thinking he could easily intimidate some no-name like Zane. It wouldn't look good to kill him now, not after he'd just said he wouldn't.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
The nasty smile vanished from Felix's face. He stared at Zane, his expression a pale mask of disbelief.
"You're just an Outer Disciple! Who do you think you are, offending House Sterling? How dare you!" Nessa, one of Felix's entourage standing behind him, couldn't stay quiet any longer.
She had never seen anyone so bold. Zane simply ignored her and gave Corbin Blackwood, Dustin’s older brother, a slight bow.
"You're the first person I've ever seen embarrass Felix like that," Corbin said with a grin. "Dustin, you've got yourself one hell of a friend here."
"Thanks, Corbin. I appreciate it," Zane said, shaking his head. "Believe me, if there was any other way, I wouldn't have picked a fight with Felix."
"Don't mention it. And I'm only a few years older than you, not some old man. No need to be so formal," Corbin replied with a smile. "Zane, the items you won haven't been delivered yet. Come with me, and I'll get you the Crystal Cubes and the *Principles of Forging*."
Corbin seemed honest and direct, but he was also physically powerful and clearly sharp. Even though he admired what Zane did to Felix, he didn't say much more about it.
Zane was Dustin's friend, but it would be unrealistic for Corbin to openly back him against Felix. He had to think of his family's interests first. House Blackwood was running a business, not a charity, and it would be a huge loss for them if Corbin also took Zane’s side.
[MUSIC: STRATEGIC INTRIGUE]
As shrewd members of House Blackwood, Dustin and Corbin recognized potential when they saw it. They were strategically investing in Zane's trajectory—a calculated gamble on his remarkable promise.
If this audacious young man truly possessed the capability to challenge someone of Felix's stature, his rise would be nothing short of meteoric—a spectacle the Blackwood brothers were eager to witness, perhaps even influence, as the balance of power throughout the realm began its inevitable shift.
