Ep 1313 - The Final Offering

Word count: 1072

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: OPEN BATTLEFIELD]
[SFX: DOUBLE SWORD SLASH]
Two sharp sword strikes cut through the air, so sudden and precise they stunned everyone watching.
Janet and the others had assumed Zane was just testing Slade’s strength. But even if he wasn’t going all out, the attack had caught Slade’s companions completely off guard, leaving them scrambling to stay upright in midair. 
Back in the hall, Zane had only used his raw, inhuman strength to pass the tests; he’d never once revealed a technique like the Gods-Intimidating Strike. And yet, even then, they had all considered him the strongest of their group.
“I guess he can handle this on his own,” Janet whispered through her tears. She hated the thought of Zane fighting by himself, but a small, stubborn part of her was sure he would win.
[AMBIENT: HIDDEN CROWD MURMURS]
Meanwhile, a crowd of onlookers had gathered in secret, risking their lives to watch the battle unfold. At first, they’d had other ideas. They’d seen the massive hoard of Illusion Points these young fighters had racked up, and killing them would be a huge score. Even half of those points would be enough to skyrocket their own rankings and put them at the top of this world. They were just waiting for the right moment to pounce.
It was a gamble, of course. Success meant a life-changing haul of points. Failure meant being killed on the Illusion Battlefield and sent straight back to the city of ogres.
But after seeing the terrifying power Zane had just unleashed, they knew their plan was a bust. There was no point wasting their energy on a fight they were destined to lose.
[SFX: SPEAR TREMBLING]
Slade stood trembling, his face pale as he clutched the Saint-Killing Spear. He just looked dazed.
[SFX: COLD INTIMIDATING VOICE]
Zane stared him down. “We done here?” he asked, his voice cold.
He had no intention of killing Slade just yet. The plan was to shatter his confidence, to make him lose his focus. On the Illusion Battlefield, Zane couldn’t permanently break Slade’s spirit for martial arts, but he could sure as hell attack his confidence.
[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSH]
Slade lunged again with the Saint-Killing Spear.
[SFX: METAL CLANG]
And just like before, he failed. Zane batted the spear aside with his sword, then let his icy voice cut through the air once more.
“I’m disappointed. I didn’t expect an Ace Talent of the Demon Night to be this weak.”
Slade couldn’t even muster the strength to thrust the spear again. He was cornered, with nowhere to run, and could only stare at Zane with pure despair in his eyes. But even under that crushing pressure, a desperate, final need took root: he had to get rid of this monster.
“Yes,” Slade said dully, the fight gone out of his voice. “I rely too much on the spear.”
But the look in his eyes shifted before he’d even finished speaking—despair curdling into something colder. A thin smile crept across his face.
“But you’ve made a mistake too.”
“What mistake?” Zane asked, watching him carefully.
“Your mistake,” Slade said, “is thinking you’ve already won.”
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS DRONE]
[AMBIENT: SUPERNATURAL WHISPERS]
Before Zane could react, two thin, wiry vines—one black, one pale white—split open along the Saint-Killing Spear’s shaft and drove straight into Slade’s arm.
This was the last resort of every Queen of Slaughter who had ever wielded this weapon: when faced with an enemy they couldn’t beat, they could offer themselves to the spear completely, letting it burn through their body and remake them into something closer to unstoppable, at least for a little while. There was no true death on the Illusion Battlefield, only a trip back to the main city to be revived—so for Slade, giving himself over cost him nothing but the fight itself.
By surrendering his will to the Saint-Killing Spear, Slade became its slave. His body was now just a lifeless vessel for the weapon to control.
The change in Slade’s appearance and aura was so alarming that Zane’s blood ran cold.
Sensing the danger, Zane moved on pure instinct—twisting his body and driving his sword straight through Slade’s chest. But when he pulled the blade free, there wasn’t a drop of blood on it. Every ounce had already been drained into the spear itself.
“Kill!” Zane snarled, and the Thundergust tore into Slade again and again, opening one gash after another. But no blood came, and the wounds wouldn’t even close the body down—something had taken root inside Slade, threading through him in place of his own flesh, and whatever it was, it wouldn’t break.
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Without a second’s hesitation, he scrambled back toward the city walls, putting as much distance as he could between himself and whatever Slade had become.
Just then, Slade began to move. Or rather, the spear began to move him. His motions were as stiff and jerky as a zombie’s, but the speed was absolutely insane.
[MUSIC: FRANTIC COMBAT STRINGS]
[SFX: SUPERNATURAL MOVEMENT WHOOSH]
He moved so fast Zane could barely track him.
In the blink of an eye, the spear was in front of him.
[SFX: LIGHTNING FAST SPEAR THRUST]
It shot forward faster than a lightning strike, aimed straight for his heart.
[SFX: SPEAR PIERCING FLESH]
It tore right through his body.
The attack was brutally simple, but its speed was so incredible that Zane couldn’t even process it, let alone react. In theory, every martial art has a weakness. But when you add enough speed, even the most basic move becomes unstoppable. At a certain velocity, every strike is a killing blow.
Even with his Eight Smoky Melodies technique at full tilt, Zane found it impossible to dodge completely.
[SFX: FLESH TEARING]
[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]
The spearhead punched through his right shoulder and straight out his back, tearing a brutal, bloody wound.
[SFX: PULSING DARK ENERGY] 
The first strike was a success, and the spear’s killing intent seemed to pulse, growing even stronger. Before surrendering himself, Slade had poured all his desire to kill Zane into the weapon. Now the spear was acting on its own, and it wouldn’t stop until Zane was dead.
Slade’s arm pulled back, the second strike already beginning.
[SFX: DESPERATE RUNNING]
But before the spear could land another fatal blow, Zane had already spun around and charged headlong into the city of ogres.
