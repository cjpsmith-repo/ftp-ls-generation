Eliot watched Zane practice the Sword Step, stunned by his friend's rapid progress.
The Sword Step was a dizzying flurry of intricate moves, so complex that mastering it on the fly was nearly impossible. No matter how many times Finn demonstrated it, Eliot knew he'd probably never be able to remember the whole sequence himself.
So it was jarring to see how fast Zane picked it up just by watching. He moved with increasing speed, as if he'd been born knowing the Sword Step.
Watching the battle ring, Eliot saw Zane's footwork was almost identical to Finn's. He had to wonder if Zane was even human.
Eliot was just floored by Zane's abilities. He wasn't shocked that Zane could beat the other opponents---the kid had scored over fifty-five thousand points on his assessment, after all. Eliot already knew Zane was a better fighter than he was.
But seeing him learn the Sword Step---the one skill Finn was most proud of mastering---that fast? That was what really blew Eliot's mind. Zane had this unnatural, almost freaky talent for absorbing techniques on the spot.
[SFX: LOUD ROAR]
Just then, a loud roar from the ring snapped Eliot's head around.
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]
The clash between Finn and Nicholas was now in full swing, captivating the audience with its intensity. Finn, executing his Sword Step with masterful precision, had completely immobilized his opponent, moving with a grace that belied the technique's deadly efficiency.
Nicholas struggled visibly against Finn's dominance. His normally impassive face now contorted with frustration, veins pulsing at his temples as he fought to maintain control.
For years, competitors had whispered about Nicholas' legendary patience---how he would absorb pressure like stone absorbs rain---until that critical moment when his restraint shattered. Those who had witnessed his transformation before watched with bated breath, recognizing the telltale signs of his impending eruption. His eyes, once calm pools of concentration, now blazed with volcanic fury as his carefully cultivated discipline began to crumble beneath Finn's relentless assault.
When Nicholas finally unleashed his pent-up rage, it would sweep across the arena like a cataclysmic force of nature, leaving his adversaries to weather a storm few had survived intact.
[SFX: POWERFUL ATTACK]
"Dragon Shadow Kill!"
Nicholas poured everything into one thrust, all his Vitality blazing at the spear tip. Finn accelerated. A dozen afterimages cycled the arena. The spear found nothing.
Finn materialized behind him, two fingers pressed lightly to his back.
"I concede," Nicholas said quietly. He knew the gap.
"Finn wins."

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
A roar thundered through the arena. As the sound echoed, the faint images of dragons on Nicholas' spear flickered to life, coiling around the shaft and converging at the tip.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY CHARGING]
He poured all of his Vitality into the weapon, compressing it into a foot-long blade of pure energy.
So far, the competition hadn't held many surprises when it came to the members of the Soul Realm. Their disciples were just on a completely different level than anyone from the Skyreach Sect's Adept Realm.
Aside from Finn's upset victory, every other mismatched fight had ended with a win for the Soul Realm. But Finn's talent was the stuff of legends, so his performance wasn't a total shock.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
Master Young's lips twisted into a smirk when the next match was announced: Zane versus Cecil Sterling of the Soul Realm. He despised Zane for nearly killing Mack, a disciple from Blackrock Peak. "Hmph. Let's see how that little second-tier rat from the Adept Realm holds up against a real master."
A little rat from the second grade of the Adept Realm.
Even though Zane had won all his matches so far in the All Peaks Competition, the general feeling was that he'd just gotten lucky.
Basically, because Zane was only at the second level, most people wrote him off as an opportunist. They figured the second he faced a true master, like a disciple from the Soul Realm, he'd be hopelessly outmatched and get crushed.
[MUSIC: CONFIDENT DETERMINATION]
Instead of worrying, Zane found himself intrigued by the challenge ahead. This match presented a perfect opportunity to test out the Sword Step technique against Cecil Sterling in actual combat conditions.
The situation stirred memories of his earlier triumph as an Initiate Adept, when he had managed to get the upper hand on a master from the Soul Realm. Now, having shattered his former limitations and reached the second level, confronting another one seemed far less daunting.
What Zane didn't know was that Cecil Sterling hadn't come to the arena just to fight. House Sterling had sent word: if he encountered Zane, he was to finish it — permanently. The family would bear responsibility. Cecil had no reason to hold back.
He wasn't the only threat. Somewhere in the competition, another disciple had been quietly tasked with the same objective by a senior official with his own score to settle with Zane. That one hadn't shown himself yet.

In the brief time since witnessing Finn's demonstration, Zane had not merely learned but thoroughly internalized the enigmatic and unpredictable Sword Step. The prospect of implementing this newly acquired skill against a worthy adversary sent a thrill of anticipation coursing through his veins.

[MUSIC: TENSE SHOWDOWN]
Standing proudly in the ring was Cecil Sterling---tall and handsome, with delicate features that couldn't hide his arrogance. He held a fan decorated with The Runic Cipher, but it quickly became clear the symbols weren't just painted on.
[SFX: MAGICAL RUNES SPINNING]
They were slowly spinning, as if they were breathing.
"Zane Lowell. This isn't the first time I've heard your name," Cecil said, shaking his head in disgust. "It seems quite a few members of my family hate your guts. If you weren't a disciple here at the Skyreach Sect, you'd have been dead a hundred times over by now. But it looks like your luck has finally run out. You're my opponent, and I'm going to destroy you. Now, listen up---"
Zane cut him off, his voice laced with impatience. "Gotta hand it to you, Sterling. You all talk way too much. First Felix, then Preston, Josef, and now you."
As Finn closed his eyes again, he heard someone shout from the crowd, "What's going on? He knows the Sword Step too?"
"Yeah, that's definitely the Sword Step! Zane mastered it!"
[SFX: SWIFT FOOTWORK]
The crowd watched as Zane moved around the ring, using the exact same footwork Finn had used earlier to dodge Cecil’s chaotic cyclone of attacks.
At first glance, some of the sharper-eyed disciples recognized the technique and started scoffing at Zane's performance. "That's the Sword Step, all right. He's copying Finn move for move, but Zane's version is just a shell. It doesn't have any of the sword intent Finn uses. How pathetic. It's just a cheap imitation!"
"Ha-ha! Finn uses the real Sword Step. This guy just copied the moves without understanding the power behind them. What's the point?"
The endless chatter from the disciples buzzed in Finn's ears. Annoyed but also a little curious, he opened his eyes and watched Zane weave around the ring. Suddenly, one of Finn's eyebrows shot up.
[SFX: GASP OF REALIZATION]
A jolt of shock went through him.
"That's not true!" he blurted out. "Zane isn't just copying the steps!"
