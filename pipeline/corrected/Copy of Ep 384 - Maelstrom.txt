Ep 384 - Maelstrom

[AMBIENT: HIGH STAKES BATTLE]

[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]

Frank had essentially told Zane to piss off. Under normal circumstances, such blatant disrespect would spark a confrontation, but Zane recognized there were far bigger issues to deal with. 

He offered a small smile, choosing silence over retaliation as Frank's hostile words hung in the air. The insult meant nothing—not when survival was on the line.

Finn, on the other hand, looked completely pissed off. His jaw clenched, and his hands curled into fists at his sides. "God, this guy is so full of himself!" he muttered, loud enough for everyone to hear.

Lois' voice cut through the tension like a blade."If Frank says you're in his way, then you're in his way." Frank's fiancée fixed Finn with a withering glare, her expression dripping with contempt. "If you want to make it out of Death Road alive, you'd better listen to him!"

Finn's face darkened, a retort already forming on his lips.

Zane placed a hand on Finn's shoulder. "Let it go, Finn," he said quietly, his tone carrying the weight of strategic thinking rather than submission. "Since Frank's so confident in his abilities, we get to kick back and watch him take on the Sky-Reaver all by himself."

The implication was clear—let Frank exhaust himself playing the hero. They would conserve their strength and learn from observing his techniques.

Both Zane and Finn were in the Adept Realm, and since they hadn't revealed their true power yet, they probably came off as weaker than your average disciple from Cloud Hall.

But then again, being underestimated wasn't always a bad thing, especially in a place as dangerous as Death Road. If Frank wanted to play the hero and block the golden Sky-Reaver's attacks on his own, it was a win-win for Zane and Finn. They wouldn't have to lift a finger.

Lewis, standing beside Zane, was quiet as usual. For all his strength, Lewis wasn't exactly brave. If he were, he never would have given up that treasure box on the second tier so easily.

[AMBIENT: DEEP CRATER]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON GRAVEL]

Zane remembered their friend. "We should check on Patrick. He took three hits from that Sky-Reaver. I'm worried he's hurt." He hurried over to the edge of the massive pit, looking for Patrick.

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

[AMBIENT: DUSTY WIND]

By the time they reached the edge, Patrick had already pulled himself out. The golden light that had been shielding him was slowly fading. He didn't look seriously injured, but he was a complete mess—covered in dirt, his breathing ragged.

"Patrick, you okay?" Zane asked, his voice laced with genuine concern.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL REUNION]

Patrick's face lit up when he saw his friends."Zane! Finn! You guys made it!" 
"I'm fine," he said, though his voice carried an edge of frustration. "But I used up my ability on that thing. It'll be days before I can use it again."

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

Meanwhile, a smirk played on Frank's handsome face.
The transformation had changed things. Frank's earlier thrusts had shredded the beast's hands easily enough, but now his spear skidded off the golden scales without leaving more than a scratch. He needed a different approach.

[SFX: ROCK IMPACT]

[AMBIENT: BATTLE TENSION]

If he kept backing up, Frank was about to be backed into a twenty-foot-tall boulder. But then, the onlookers watched Frank sneer. He took a step toward the boulder and drove his heel into it, hard enough to leave a deep crater in the solid rock.

Using the recoil, Frank's body snapped into perfect alignment with his spear, and he began to spin at an incredible speed.

[SFX: ENERGY VORTEX SWIRLING]

As he spun, a swirling vortex erupted from the tip of his white spear, sucking in raw Spear Intent and compressing it into a violently condensed ball of energy.

This wasn't Vitality. This was pure Apex of Spear Intent, and the power it contained was unbelievable.

"I had no idea you could use Spear Intent like that," Zane said, his voice filled with surprise. He and Finn exchanged a look. The expressions on their faces said it all—they'd just witnessed something huge.

Even though Zane and Finn had already mastered the Apex of Sword Intent, they'd never even considered condensing it to such an extreme. There were a few reasons for that, one being that their foundational knowledge of sword intent was still pretty limited.

Plus, in recent memory at Skyreach Sect, they were the only two to ever reach that level, so they had no one to guide them.

Frank's situation was the complete opposite. As a disciple of the Cloud Hall in The Heartlands, he had access to countless masters who could offer pointers. With that kind of guidance, combined with his own hard work, mastering something like condensation after reaching the Apex of Spear Intent would have been a piece of cake.

In that sense, disciples from Cloud Hall had a massive advantage.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE ENERGY RELEASE]

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE CLIMAX]

"Maelstrom Spear!" Frank roared, unleashing one of his ultimate moves.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]

As he spun toward the Sky-Reaver at an insane speed, Frank became one with his long white spear. He and the weapon were a single, deadly entity.

The Sky-Reaver felt the crushing power of Frank's attack. But it all happened so fast that the creature had no time to react. All it could do was throw its arms across its chest, bracing for the impact with its own hardened body.

[SFX: SHARP CRACKING]

A series of sharp cracks split the air.

[SFX: SHATTERING SCALES]

[SFX: PIERCING IMPACT]

The hard, golden scales on the Sky-Reaver's arms exploded into fragments. Frank's spear, blazing with the Apex of Spear Intent, tore through the creature's defenses. Seizing the opening, Frank drove the spear deep into its flesh.

After punching through the Sky-Reaver's arms, the spear didn't slow down at all, continuing straight through its chest. The creature's swift defeat was shocking. It was left impaled on the spear, a grotesque trophy. As the weapon ripped through its body, a Beast Core tumbled from the gaping wound in its chest.

[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WIND]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]

A fierce beast's Core was incredibly valuable, and Frank wasn't about to let it go to waste. After dispatching the Sky-Reaver's corpse, he simply continued down Death Road, with Lois and Dermot following right behind him.

Finn's pride screamed that they could've handled Death Road just fine on their own. He was sure Frank wasn't the only one who could pass these trials. But since Zane had insisted they just stomach Frank's attitude and follow his lead, Finn begrudgingly tagged along.

As for Lewis, he had his own reasons for being there, so he didn't give Frank's attitude a second thought and simply fell in line with the others.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

After everyone had left, the lone Cloud Hall disciple who'd been standing off to the side suddenly looked up. A strange smile touched his lips as he glanced in a specific direction, then reached out and clenched his fist, as if crushing something invisible between his fingers. Then, dusting his hands together, he followed the group.

[SFX: MAGICAL DISTORTION]

That single, small gesture caused the image broadcast on the Scrying Slate at the entrance of Cloud Road to vanish completely.
