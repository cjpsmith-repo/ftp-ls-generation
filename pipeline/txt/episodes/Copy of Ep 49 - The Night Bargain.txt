 eEp 49 - The Night Bargain
Word Count: 1408
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
Zane joined the crowd of disciples who'd made it through the test.
[SFX: CRYSTALS FILLING BASKETS]
One by one, more of them stumbled out from Bloody Mountain, taking turns tossing their crystal fragments into the collection baskets.
[SFX: SKYCRUISER LANDING]
Just then, Zane spotted Ethan Grant hopping out of a Skycruiser. Ethan shot him a cheerful wave before rushing over to the baskets.
Zane watched as Ethan dumped a handful of crystal fragments inside.
Zane was genuinely surprised. "Ethan had passed the test, too?" But he was only at the Bone Refining Level. How could he possibly have collected ten purple fragments?
Zane had planned to give Ethan some of his spare fragments once he reached the third level, but then he'd run into the Ogre and never got the chance.
He had no idea how Ethan had managed to pass The Gauntlet on his own. There was no way someone at the Bone Refining Level could kill the unreal beasts on the third level.
How did he manage it? Could he be downplaying his abilities? The thought struck Zane.
[MUSIC: UPBEAT VICTORY]
A huge grin on his face, Ethan walked over, looking incredibly pleased with himself.
"Ha! I did it, Zane! I passed!"
"How? How did you get enough fragments?" Zane asked, still trying to wrap his head around it.
[AMBIENT: NIGHTTIME CAMP]
[MUSIC: CELEBRATORY BACKGROUND]
"Well, it's a long story. The short version is, I got really lucky." He then explained what happened after he entered the third level. Turns out, he'd stumbled upon two disciples from the Great Houses fighting over a fragment. By the time they were done, both were so critically wounded they were forced out of the test. Ethan just swooped in and collected their fragments.
Across the crowd, the Legacy House disciples who'd been eliminated were already being collected by their families. Connor caught Zane's eye as he left — a long, hard stare, the kind that didn't need words. He'd never lost before. He wasn't done.
Caleb was harder to read. He stood to one side, composed as ever, until Evan and Cole shuffled up to him. They'd failed — eliminated during the checkpoint chaos before they'd even had a chance to use their Finishing Moves on Zane. 
Caleb looked at them for a long moment, then said something low and sharp that made both of them flinch. Then he turned and walked away. Perrin followed behind him, face grey, saying nothing.
And that's how he passed. The atmosphere in the camp was way more relaxed now. After everything they'd been through, the disciples had finally reached their first goal. Some were so wired they couldn't even think about sleeping, spending most of the night buzzing about which Peaks they were going to choose.
[AMBIENT: CROWDED HALL]
[MUSIC: EXCITED CHATTER]
Jasper Finch and Eagle Peak were the main topics of conversation among the newcomers. Stories about Eagle Peak were everywhere. Towering over the other thirty-two peaks, it was famous for skimming the cream of the crop—taking the best of the best from the sect.
Zane and Ethan sat in a corner, just listening to everyone talk. Ethan was completely absorbed, his eyes practically shining with anticipation. Noticing his expression, Zane asked, "So, which peak are you hoping for? Eagle Peak?"
Ethan scratched his head and laughed. "I wish. But let's be real, I'm not good enough to get in."
Ethan knew his own limits. Eagle Peak only took the disciples who performed the best in the test. He figured that big guy with the greatsword was a shoo-in for Eagle Peak.
"You know, Zane, I've decided I'm sticking with you," Ethan said. "Good things happen when I'm around you. You're like my good luck charm."
Ethan wasn't wrong. If it weren't for Zane, he might not have even passed the Entrance Exam. And without Zane's help during The Gauntlet, he would've been eliminated for sure.
"What if I join Misty Peak?" Zane asked with a slight smile.
The question made Ethan stop short. After a beat, he blurted out, "Are you kidding me?"
After Zane's incredible performance in the test, he could get into any peak he wanted. And now he was thinking about joining Misty Peak?
Ethan had to remind him, "Bro, Misty Peak is the lowest-ranked of all the peaks. You know that, right?"
Zane nodded, an image of Isla Novak flashing in his mind. Without her, Wyatt would've gotten him kicked out of the test for sure.
Still, if Misty Peak was ranked last, the resources it offered wouldn't be anything like the other peaks. Its members would be at a huge disadvantage when it came to their share of Energy Capsules, Refinement Process techniques, and other monthly supplies.
Zane hesitated.
[MUSIC: TENSE DETERMINATION]
As the night wore on, Zane pushed everything else from his mind. He had to focus on what was really important: getting his knife back. He'd tear Bloody Mountain apart if he had to.
[AMBIENT: NIGHT WILDERNESS]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON MOUNTAIN PATH]
Once Ethan was sound asleep, Zane slipped out of the room. He made his way to the base of the mountain, cloaked by the darkness. He vaguely recognized the trial path from earlier and started to climb. The Magic Circle was inactive at this hour, so there were far fewer unreal beasts to worry about.
He searched for an hour. Six or seven spots that looked familiar — none of them right. The mountain looked completely different without the illusion running. He was starting to go in circles, and the knife felt further away with every wrong turn. He'd used it once and lost it. That thought sat heavy.
Then the branches ahead shifted. A woman in a green dress stepped out of the forest into the moonlight — her face luminous in it, her figure unhurried. For just a moment, Zane forgot what he was doing.
He caught himself. Isla Novak.
"It's the middle of the night," she said, a smile playing at the corner of her mouth. "Shouldn't you be resting?"
"Couldn't sleep," Zane said. "Just out for a walk."
"Hm." She took a few steps toward him, eyes bright with amusement. "I think you're looking for something."
Zane kept his face neutral. "That's a private matter."
"Oh, of course." She turned as if to leave — and raised her hand. The Shattered Dagger gleamed in her fingers. "I'll leave you to it, then."
"On one condition," Isla Novak added, her voice carrying a hint of triumph in the night air.
"Name it," Zane replied instantly, desperation evident in his tense posture.
"You join Misty Peak," she declared, a sly smile playing on her lips as moonlight caught the gleam in her eyes.
Zane exhaled slowly. Deep down, he'd anticipated this stipulation. For hours, he'd wrestled with the decision of which peak to choose, weighing prestige against practicality, resources against relationships. Now, the choice had been made for him by circumstances beyond his control—or perhaps by Isla's careful maneuvering.
It seemed Isla Novak had orchestrated his decision from the start, laying her trap with the precision of a master strategist. The realization should have angered him, yet he felt a reluctant admiration for her foresight.
Her tactics certainly bordered on manipulation, the kind of power play he'd grown accustomed to in House politics. Yet she'd recovered his treasured knife—a weapon irreplaceable to him and a feat he would have likely failed to accomplish alone in the vastness of Bloody Mountain. The scales of this bargain, though uneven, still offered him what he needed most.
[MUSIC: SOLEMN PROMISE]
"I'll pledge myself to Misty Peak tomorrow," Zane declared with solemn conviction, meeting her gaze directly. In his voice rang the unmistakable tone of a young man who understood the weight of binding promises—a lesson learned through bitter experience.
[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION]
A honeyed smile spread across Isla's face as she tapped the delicate ring adorning her finger. Within seconds, two small, mysterious rings materialized in her outstretched palm.
"Your knife bears a fracture," she explained. "And controlling it with your Life Energy remains impossible until you ascend to Nature Level—explaining why it never returns to your hand. Consider these a welcoming tribute to your future at Misty Peak. Take them."
Zane's gaze fixed on the twin rings resting in her palm, his mind racing with questions. What power did these innocuous circles hold? And what unseen price might come with such a gift?
