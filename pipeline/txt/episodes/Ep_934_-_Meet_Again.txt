Ep 934 - Meet Again

Word count: 1141

[MUSIC: GENTLE HARP MELODY]

The person playing the harp was none other than Cora.

She’d been missing Zane something fierce ever since he left. She didn't care about becoming his wife; all she wanted was to be by his side.

Even when Brent, who had also been gone for a long time, showed up in Oracle City after the Ethereal Spirit Sect was rebuilt and offered to take her back, Cora turned him down flat.

She was determined to wait for Zane here. That way, she'd be the first to see him the moment he returned.

Cora hadn’t gone back to the Ethereal Spirit Sect. But on her account, the World Merchants' Guild had poured a ton of resources into helping rebuild it.

The relationship between the World Merchants' Guild and the Ethereal Spirit Sect was anything but strained. And while Cora looked delicate, her power had skyrocketed after she activated her Violet-Core Physique.

Even though she was only at the Inner-Core Realm, she was so strong that she could be counted as one of the top three warriors in the Heartlands, all thanks to the power she drew from the Violet Core Realm.

[SFX: SWORD STORM]

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

The memory of that day—when Cora had summoned a storm of massive swords to annihilate endless warriors from the Corrupted Divine Kingdom—was still fresh in everyone's mind. If Zane was the key to fighting the Corrupted Divine Kingdom, then Cora was easily the second most important person in the war.

But Cora wasn't nearly as famous as Zane, and most warriors had no idea where those massive swords had even come from. Besides a handful of powerful fighters, most people didn't even know a woman that strong existed in the Heartlands.

That was because Cora spent most of her time in the Heavenly Stellar Palace. She got through the days, but the ache of missing Zane just kept growing.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC HARP]

Sometimes, on her better days, she'd have her maid bring the unconscious Tia out to get some sun. She would lose herself in her harp, the music a small comfort for her longing.

When Cora heard footsteps behind her, she assumed it was her maid, Faye. She’d sent her for the mulled cider she’d made, but Faye was taking her sweet time. A harp string twitched under her finger, and she frowned. 

Cora was still the same arrogant young woman she’d always been; she just hid that side from Zane. The delay was starting to grate on her.

[SFX: HARP STRING VIBRATION]

“Faye?” Cora’s voice had a dangerous edge. She twisted the harp string in her hand, making it vibrate with her irritation. Missing Zane always left her on a knife’s edge.

Still no answer.

Cora's frown deepened. She pushed the harp aside and turned around.

The long lashes above her eyes began to tremble.

Zane stood there with a smile on his face, watching her.

[MUSIC: WARM REUNION THEME]

Cora pressed her lips together. Then she took a step toward him — slow at first, then faster — and threw herself into his arms.

Half a year. For a cultivator, it was nothing. But the longing had been with her every single day.

She didn't close her eyes. Over Zane's shoulder, her gaze had already found Dorothea.

Cora was sharp. She'd decided long ago that no matter how many women surrounded Zane, she wouldn't show dissatisfaction. But that didn't mean she felt nothing. 

She studied Dorothea carefully. Even at Inner-Core Realm, she could sense something radiating off this woman — not a soul aura, but something deeper. The aura of someone with power beyond anything she had a framework for.

Was she from the Divine Kingdoms? Had she followed Zane back?

Dorothea was looking at her too. But the way she was looking — it made Cora's skin prickle. It wasn't the look of a rival. It was something else entirely, something she couldn't quite name, and it made her deeply uncomfortable.

Cora shot Dorothea a sharp glare. Then she turned back to Zane, brushed a strand of hair over her shoulder, and pressed closer.

Dorothea laughed — a clear, bright sound.

Zane glanced at her. “What?”

“Nothing,” Dorothea said, still smiling. “I just think your woman is very interesting.”

Zane gently stroked Cora's hair. Then he eased her back and said, “I need to treat Tia first.”

Cora nodded and stepped aside.

Dorothea followed him over to where Tia lay. She looked down at her for a moment. “So this is the one you mentioned?”

Zane nodded.

Dorothea studied Tia's face carefully. “Her life force has nearly dried up completely.” She tilted her head. “Strange. That only happens when someone is close to the natural end of their lifespan. But this woman is young.” She paused. “Very strange. What happened to her?”

“The Life and Death Wind,” Zane said. “From the River of the Netherworld.”

Dorothea blinked. Then she laughed in disbelief. “You're serious? The Life and Death Wind carries the Law of Causality — it erodes life force directly. It's almost certain death. How did she survive it?” She shook her head. “And how did you even get your hands on the Life and Death Wind in a world like this?”

“It's a secret,” Zane said with a sly smile. In reality, Tia's survival was thanks to the Celestial Fragment, which had restrained the Law of Causality within the Life and Death Wind. Of course, Zane wasn't about to explain all that to Dorothea. He just focused on the task at hand and pulled a life crystal from his Storage Ring.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL WONDER]

Dorothea's eyes lit up when she saw it. “That's incredible!”

It wasn't just Dorothea. Everyone, including the three leaders of the World Merchants' Guild outside the Heavenly Stellar Palace, felt a powerful surge of Raw Vitality the moment Zane took out the crystal.

The life crystal was a rarity even in the upper world. Dorothea wanted to say that using it like this was a waste, but since Zane was the one who’d acquired it, she held her tongue.

It was as if Zane wasn't holding a piece of ore, but a living creature, radiating potent Raw Vitality in every direction.

[SFX: CRYSTAL CRACKING]

He placed the life crystal on Tia's face and squeezed it gently. The surface cracked, and Zane snatched his hand back. The Raw Vitality was like a living thing that would rush into the nearest life force, and he couldn't risk it surging into his hand.

[SFX: ENERGY WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: MAGICAL TENSION]

A thick stream of Raw Vitality poured out, hovering above Tia and twisting into all sorts of strange shapes.

After a few moments, the dense cloud of Raw Vitality sank, seeping between Tia's parted lips.

Zane prayed that he wasn't too late.
