Ep 477 - Refining the Body

Word count: 1191

[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION SPACE]

Zane stood frozen as the energy took over his whole body.

The "warm current," as Zane had taken to calling it, felt like the gentle embrace of a summer ocean. He had no clue what it was, only that it healed him, soothing every ache and pain. The energy that coursed through him was a complete mystery.

A mystery,* Zane thought, a faint smile touching his lips. *And one I'm going to solve.

That thirst for knowledge pushed him to train his body relentlessly. His dedication left him covered in cuts and bruises, but they never seemed to last. The moment the "warm current" washed over him, they simply vanished. It’s a different kind of energy, he'd thought earlier, his brow furrowed in frustration. Something I've never even heard of.

"Primordial Energy?"

Zane murmured the words, the pieces finally clicking into place after what the seventh brother had told him.

The seventh brother had guided him, teaching him how to summon and circulate the Primordial Energy within his own body. It began as a tiny flicker, a flame so fragile a sudden gust of wind could have snuffed it out.

[SFX: INTENSE FIRE WHOOSH]

But then it spread. It happened so fast, Zane didn't even have time to register the change. The flame didn't just flicker; it exploded, tearing through him like a wildfire in a dry forest. It showed no mercy, scorching every inch of his body until he was completely engulfed.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION BUILDING]

He thought he could handle it. He pictured the summer seas, the crashing waves, the salty tang on his tongue—that warm caress that had always healed him. At first, he felt that same tender touch. But then the heat intensified, growing stronger and stronger until he was choking, the temperature rising to devastating levels. It burned him from the inside out. Every breath was like swallowing acid. He was being burned alive, his muscles convulsing in a hopeless battle against the fire that relentlessly licked at his flesh.

"No! Stop!"

A raw scream of despair tore from his throat as he thrashed wildly. He hated this.

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR COURTYARD]

From her hiding spot, Alice peeked over the wall. Her initial curiosity curdled into shock. The more she watched, the more horrified she became, seeing the warrior endure such agony just for the sake of training.

Zane’s throat was raw, his voice shredded from screaming. The pain was beyond anything he'd expected; he was totally unprepared. Time dissolved into a meaningless blur of pure torture. He had no idea if minutes or hours had passed inside his personal prison of searing fire.

He convulsed, slamming himself violently against the ground like a fish flopping on dry land, desperate for a single moment of relief as his mind fought to escape the unbearable agony.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL COURTYARD]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

"He's tougher than I thought," the seventh brother said, surprise clear in his voice.

"His soul has entered the Fighting Soul Realm," the Cobalt Dragon replied. "He can withstand it for a while before he passes out."

"That's just it. Passing out would be a mercy. This kind of torture is unbearable," the seventh brother said, shaking his head in pity. "But it's true you have to go through fire to be forged. Zane needs to understand the stakes of the future he's about to face."

[SFX: LOUD CRACK]

The dragons whipped their heads toward the loud boom.

[SFX: SPLINTERING CRASH]

It was followed by another splintering crash. The sound of something breaking.

[SFX: STONE SHATTERING]

In his delirium, Zane had slammed into a brown stone floor tile, shattering it into pieces. He didn't seem to notice as he staggered blindly across the courtyard.

[SFX: PILLAR CRUMBLING]

[SFX: WALLS COLLAPSING]

He was making a huge mess. His body seemed to have the strength of a hundred men. Every time he stumbled into one of the stone pillars, it shattered. Still lost in agony, he was completely unaware that he’d already run straight through the walls of three different rooms, causing them to collapse.

[AMBIENT: FRIGHTENED HOUSEHOLD]

[MUSIC: ANXIOUS UNDERTONE]

The chaos at the manor sent Zane’s neighbors cowering in fear. The owner gathered his family in their shop, his voice trembling as he tried to console them. "Don't worry, the warrior is just practicing. There's no need to be afraid. Just remember—no matter what happens, don't go into his courtyard and disturb him."

The family all nodded, but they were shaking so badly the owner doubted a single word had registered.

It's okay, he reassured himself. My wife is here, and so are my children. His eyes swept over the huddled group before widening in horror.

[MUSIC: SUDDEN HORROR STING]

"Alice! Where's Alice? She's not here!"

[SFX: FRANTIC FOOTSTEPS]

A dreadful silence fell over the room. Then, a panicked scramble as they searched the shop, but there was no sign of the missing girl.

Oblivious to her family's panic, Alice remained frozen in her spot, mesmerized by the unbelievable sight on the other side of the wall. Maybe it was her childish curiosity that kept her from feeling afraid. With a look of pure wonder, she whispered to herself, "Is that guy made of iron or something?"

"What's he doing? He's running into walls and breaking them, but he's not even hurt? How is that possible?" She pursed her lips, concentrating as she scanned his body for injuries. "Not even a scratch! Are all warriors, like, invincible?"

[SFX:  CRASH]

A thunderous crash cut off her thoughts.

Zane raged like a blind bull, wrecking anything and everything in his path.

"It all comes back to Elara’s Violet-Core Physique," the Cobalt Dragon continued his thought. "That’s why Maddox took her. Zane's defeat forced him to overcome one obstacle after another just to chase her, and all of it made him stronger by leaps and bounds. That's why I think Elara is his key."

"That makes sense," the seventh brother countered. "But I think it's too early to say for sure. If you were right, shouldn't Zane have been led to a much bigger world by now, not this small corner of The Heartlands?"

The Cobalt Dragon nodded in agreement. "You're right, it’s not a final conclusion. If she really is who I think she is, she might just be taken away by someone else again."

Both dragons fell into a heavy silence before sighing. "If our eldest brother were here, he'd know the answers," the seventh brother grumbled.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATION]

Fate was a slippery thing, like trying to navigate a desert with no map. Even beings as powerful as dragons couldn't see the whole picture. Kenneth had been the first to notice Zane's incredible potential and his unusual path, but even he knew nothing of a destiny that could lead the entire world out of chaos. It was only an intuition—a gut feeling that Elara was the one meant to guide Zane forward.

Unfortunately for Zane, he was left completely out of the conversation about his own future. He was still unconscious.

[MUSIC: TRANQUIL MORNING]

When Zane finally woke up, it was after a long, deep rest his body desperately needed. A dull ache radiated through him, and his head spun with disorientation. As he blinked his eyes open, the first thing he registered was the sight of a young girl leaning over him.

With a dry throat, he croaked, "Where am I? And who are you?"
