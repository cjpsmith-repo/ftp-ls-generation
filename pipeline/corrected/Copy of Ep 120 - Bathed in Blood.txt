Ep 120 - Bathed in Blood
Word count: 1132
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
[SFX: FRENZIED ENERGY] 
 Zane's body spun like a top, faster and faster. Connected to his thumb by a single ring, the Shattered Dagger became a blur, whipped into a frenzy by centrifugal force until it was spinning even faster than he was.
The Shattered Dagger could tear through soft soil and hard stone alike. Any tree roots or soft-bodied creatures like earthworms hiding underground would be instantly shredded. But the ground remained undisturbed; the demons hadn't noticed a thing.
The Demon Commanders closed in. Two hundred feet. One hundred. Fifty. Thirty.
As the five of them drew near, Zane was engulfed by their malice. But standing in the eye of the storm, he seemed completely unfazed. His body kept spinning furiously, as if he'd forgotten the danger closing in around him.
"Now!" Zane roared.
[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]
 When the Demon Commanders were just ten feet away, Zane abruptly threw his hands up, and his body shot into the air. Deep underground, the Shattered Dagger mirrored his every move. As Zane rose, so did the dagger. It carved a deadly crescent through the earth before rocketing up toward the five approaching Demon Commanders.
[SFX: BLADE SLICING]
 This time, the Shattered Dagger attacked from below. The assault was so unexpected that the Demon Commanders had no time to react. The dagger ripped right through them.
One demon's arm was severed at the shoulder. The Shattered Dagger tore a gaping hole through another's chest, while a third howled, clutching the gash it carved across his abdomen.
[SFX: DEMONIC SCREAMS]
 "Argh!" the Demon Commanders shrieked in agony.
Just a moment ago, they’d thought their prey was nothing more than a cornered rabbit.
[SFX: BONE SPURS WHISTLING]
 The Demon Commanders simultaneously unleashed a barrage of Bone Spurs from their bodies. The projectiles whistled through the air with frightening velocity, forcing Zane to abandon his offensive and scramble desperately for safety. His eyes darted frantically as he twisted and pivoted, managing to evade the first few deadly spikes by mere inches.
"Too many," he gasped, his breath coming in ragged bursts as the onslaught continued without mercy.
Initially, Zane had sufficient space to maneuver, his body flowing like water between the deadly projectiles. But the air soon bristled with Bone Spurs, their pale lengths converging from all directions. The space around him shrank with each passing second until escape became impossible, his options dwindling to nothing.
[SFX: BONE SPUR IMPACTS]
 A sickening thud broke the rhythm of his evasion as a particularly thick Bone Spur slammed into his thigh. The calcified weapon punched clean through muscle and sinew, emerging bloody from the other side. Zane bit back a scream, his face contorting with agony as warm blood cascaded down his leg.
Another long spur shot through the same leg from behind, pinning him in place. More spurs found their mark, sinking deep into his arms and legs.
Even with his body fortified as a Soul Armament, the pain was excruciating. Usually, a punch would just send a rush of warmth through him, dulling the impact and preventing serious injury. But now, seven or eight Bone Spurs skewered him from every angle.
[SFX: BODY COLLAPSE]
 His legs gave out. Unable to stand or even walk, Zane collapsed to the ground, weak and off-balance.
Across the clearing, Ingrid's eyes opened.
She took in the scene in pieces — the bone spurs skewering Zane from every angle, the blood soaking through his robes, the six Demon Generals surrounding him. She understood immediately what had happened. He had come after her. He had fought six of them alone.
"Zane—" she said, her voice barely audible.
He glanced at her. Just once. A faint smile. Then he looked away and turned his focus inward.
[SFX: DEMONIC LAUGHTER]
 The demon general who had kidnapped Ingrid let out a guttural laugh at the sight of Zane's broken state.
Just then, some of the other Demon Commanders—the ones he’d wounded with the Shattered Dagger—staggered to their feet. 
They muttered to each other in their harsh tongue, likely debating whether to kill this formidable human.
After all, Zane had reached the Near-High Level. 
It would be a massive gain for their research if they could capture him and take him back to their camp. 
Maybe they could unlock the secrets that made him so powerful. They knew it was vital for any race to learn its enemy's strengths and weaknesses; it was the only way to evolve.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]
 A long time ago, demons didn't have a culture to speak of. But through endless battles with humankind, they had absorbed what they could, eventually developing their own language. They studied human Training methods step by step, turning everything they learned back on their creators.
Eventually, both humankind and the Ogres had realized the same truth: they needed to learn from their enemies' strengths to cover their own weaknesses. 
[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL AFTERMATH]
 They had torn down the walls between them. Now, humans and Ogres had brokered peace through marriage alliances, sharing knowledge to strengthen each other.
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD]
[SFX: DRIPPING BLOOD]
 Drops of blood from Zane’s wounds dripped down, staining the white Bone Spurs red. Most men would have died instantly from so many devastating injuries.
But Zane didn't give up.
Forcing himself to one knee, he stared silently at the Demon Commanders and secretly began gathering the Corrupt Vitality in his core.
He planned to use the Corrupt Vitality to destroy the Bone Spurs pinning his body. No matter how hopeless things seemed, he had to stay calm. It was his only path to victory.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]
[SFX: MAGIC ENERGY SURGE]
 As he focused the energy, Zane remembered just how brutally it could ravage a body. He’d seen firsthand the gruesome fate that awaited anyone flooded with it.
He remembered striking another demon general, the Corrupt Vitality pouring into the creature like a black flood. It had dissolved tissue and flesh from the inside out. 
[SFX: DEMON WAILS]
[SFX: DISSOLVING FLESH]
[SFX: SKELETON CLATTER]
 Within moments, wisps of dark energy had bled from the demon's eyes, mouth, and ears, until nothing was left but a skeleton that clattered to the ground as everyone watched in horror. The creature’s body—and the Corrupt Vitality with it—had vanished completely.
The memory of their companion's horrific demise hardened the expressions of the remaining Demon Commanders. Astonishment flickered across their faces—never had they encountered a mere Near-High Level human who could retaliate in such dire circumstances, much less slaughter one of their elite without a trace of trepidation.
Blood saturated Zane's form from neck to feet. His once-pristine white robe, the proud emblem of a Skyreach Sect Outer Disciple, hung in shreds—a crimson banner of defiance against impossible odds.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BUILDING]
 Yet beneath the mantle of his catastrophic injuries, something terrifying stirred. The inferno blazing in his eyes transcended mere determination—it promised retribution, vengeance, and a reckoning that would haunt their nightmares for eternity. And as the Demon Commanders stared into those unblinking depths, a chilling realization dawned: they hadn't captured prey at all.
 They had cornered a predator.
