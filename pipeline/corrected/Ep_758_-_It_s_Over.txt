Ep 758 - It's Over

Word count: 1159

[AMBIENT: CRUMBLING STONE]

[SFX: WALL COLLAPSE]

 The soldiers garrisoned outside watched in disbelief as the city wall—a fortification stretching more than ten miles—began to crumble like a line of dominoes. On top of the collapsing ramparts, the men who had been spectating the fight scrambled for their lives, trying to escape the rain of falling debris.

[SFX: SWORD SLASH]

[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]

 In the chaos of the chase, Zane thrust his sword forward again. Maddox couldn't dodge in time. The blade sliced a long, deep gash across his chest, and blood arced through the air.

If this had happened earlier in the fight, right after Maddox had activated his Fiend Bloodline and a special layer of scales had formed over his skin, Zane probably couldn’t have even scratched him. Back then, even Zane’s most powerful attacks had failed to break through his defenses.

But things were different now.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

 Zane’s Double Stars Brilliance had completely burned away Maddox’s outer layer of flesh, leaving him raw and defenseless. It would take a long time to recover that kind of protection, and because of it, he was completely vulnerable to the top-grade Relic-Class weapon Zane was wielding.

If Maddox had known Zane was still alive and coming to kill him, he would have fled long ago. He’d have found some remote corner of the world to hide in, waiting until his defenses were back at full strength before showing his face again.

That was just wishful thinking, though. Now, he was facing Zane with his body exposed and his strength almost gone.

[SFX: CRASHING STONE]

[SFX: METAL CLASH]

 The sounds of crashing stone and clashing steel echoed as Zane hounded him from one section of the crumbling wall to the next. Every so often, the two fighters would emerge from the wreckage, and the sight left everyone speechless.

[AMBIENT: CROWD CHEERING]

 “Zane is a total beast! He’s not afraid of Maddox at all,” one soldier yelled. “I thought Maddox had him for sure, but then he just shows up again and starts hunting him down. That guy’s a legend!”

“His power is on another level,” another chimed in. “You can’t measure him by his rank. He’s a lower level than Maddox, but Maddox is getting torn apart!”

“We’re going to win! I can’t believe he might actually kill him!”

The incredible scene unfolding before them sent a surge of morale through the soldiers of Oracle City. It looked like the World Merchants’ Guild and the Cloud Hall were being saved, single-handedly, by Zane.

[SFX: MASSIVE CRASH]

[AMBIENT: OPEN BATTLEFIELD]

 With a final crash, Maddox cleared the city wall and stumbled into the open field outside.

 The second he was free, he sprinted toward Simon, screaming, “Simon, help!”

The three Guild leaders moved the instant they heard it. Kieran, Hobbes, and Burns broke from their positions and closed around Simon before he could take a step.

Simon looked at them. A strange smile crossed his face. "Do you really think you can stop me?"

What none of them knew — what even Maddox didn't know — was that Simon had never been truly fighting. The entire battle, he had been holding back, watching, preserving his strength. If the Corrupted Divine Kingdom won, it would be Maddox's kingdom, Maddox's glory. Simon had no interest in handing the Heartlands to someone else. He had his own plans — wait for the right moment, then turn on Maddox and take everything for himself.

But Maddox couldn't die yet. Not like this.

Maddox, the number one fighter in The Heartlands, had been run down by a twenty-year-old kid with a lower rank. His reputation was finished. This story would spread like wildfire through every tavern in the region, becoming the talk of the continent in days.

The corner of Simon's mouth twitched.

[SFX: SPECTRAL HISSING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]

Traces of pitch-black Vitality swirled from Simon's body and transformed into hundreds of small black snakes that shot toward the three Guild leaders — coiling in the air, fangs bared. The danger wasn't the fangs. It was the poison.

The three leaders scrambled to deal with the snakes. Simon walked through them and moved toward Maddox — his body radiating color, brighter than anything Zane had seen from him before. The brighter the color, the stronger the poison.

"Careful!" Kieran shouted toward Zane. "Don't let him touch you!"

[SFX: NET WHOOSH]

Zane glanced at Simon once. Then looked back at Maddox.

Simon's hand came up, and a massive, colorful net flew toward Zane's head. It reeked before it even landed — eighty-one types of poison, potent enough to kill a Soul Sea Realm warrior. Even Maddox would not have let that net touch him.

Zane walked straight through it.

He didn't slow down. His eyes stayed on Maddox the whole time. A ragged piece of the net caught on his shoulder and hung there as he pushed through.

*[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]*

The corner of Simon's mouth twitched. His Venom Net was, in his own estimation, every bit as absolute as Maddox's Life and Death Wind. The fact that it had done nothing — that this boy had walked through it like it was smoke — hit him somewhere deep. He understood now, in a way he hadn't before, what Maddox must have felt when the wind failed.

What kind of monster is this kid?

Up on the wall, the three Guild leaders were still fending off the black snakes. But their eyes were on Zane. They looked at each other — and couldn't help smiling.

[SFX: SWORD THRUST]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

Zane plunged his sword into Maddox's back once more.

Maddox was at the end of his rope. Zane felt like he'd just been reborn. The two of them were in completely different worlds, both physically and mentally.

[SFX: BLADE PIERCING FLESH]

[SFX: BODY HITTING GROUND]

As the sword tore through Maddox's body, a burst of raw energy from Zane drove the blade down, punching through his chest and pinning him to the ground. The sword hilt was still shaking.

But Maddox wasn't about to give up. The moment he was pinned, he tried to spring back to his feet — and Zane was already there. He stomped hard on Maddox's chest, slamming him back into the mud.

"It's over," Zane said, his hand resting on the hilt. He stared down at Maddox with absolute finality.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

Maddox lifted his head. A flicker of genuine panic finally appeared in his eyes. This war was supposed to be a sure thing. He had been so close to building his Divine Kingdom. But because of this kid, it had all gone south. Over a hundred thousand of his soldiers were dead. And now, he was about to die too.

How could he possibly accept this?

Just then, a strange, unnerving smile crept across Maddox's lips.

[MUSIC: SINISTER REVELATION]

"Over? Ha! It's far from over."
