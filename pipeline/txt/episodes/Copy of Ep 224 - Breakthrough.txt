Ep 224 - Breakthrough

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
It all happened in a flash.
One moment, the stream of Arcane Aurora was there; the next, it was gone. Zane had neutralized it with such swift precision that Zachary barely registered the movement---a silver flash cutting through reality itself, leaving nothing but empty air where deadly energy had surged.
Zachary's mouth fell open, his eyes widening in disbelief at the display of effortless mastery. His mind went completely blank as his fingers trembled around the hilt of Wyrmfire, the weapon suddenly feeling heavier in his grip.
Zane's voice sliced through Zachary's stupor, calm and measured despite the imminent danger. "Heads up. More coming."
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
Sure enough, four more blasts of Arcane Aurora were already hurtling toward them.
After seeing Zane's effortless display, Zachary's confidence took a serious hit. A shiver ran down his spine, and his face hardened with focus. For a swordsman, your sword heart---your conviction---was everything. It was the wellspring of your sword intent, the force that allowed you to conquer any distraction, any inner demon.
Seeing Zane's power had shaken him to his core. A wavering heart was a cardinal sin for a swordsman. It weakened your intent, draining the power from your blade until it was just a piece of sharpened steel.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]
Yet as quick as doubt had crept into his mind, Zachary reclaimed his composure, steadying his breath and centering himself.
A subtle, appreciative smile touched Zane's lips as he observed the transformation. Impressive indeed; Zachary embodied the true essence of a swordsman. The ability to quell inner turmoil and restore one's equilibrium with such speed revealed a rare and valuable talent---one that many cultivators spent decades trying to master.
Zane recognized in Zachary's determined eyes the same unwavering resolve his father had once praised in the finest blade-wielders: that perfect balance between acknowledging one's limitations and refusing to be defined by them.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Without another word, Zane's expression grew serious once more as he faced the next wave.
The blasts of Arcane Aurora were relentless, each new wave adding another streak of energy to the assault. The first wave had been a single blast, the second had two, the third three, and so on. To protect the crystal ball, they had to intercept every last one.
The Arcane Aurora was pure energy, drawn from glacial heartstones and refined to a point of extreme stability. It couldn't be brute-forced. Zachary's dragon-shaped sword energy, devastating against any monster, was useless here; it would simply dissipate. To cut through the Aurora, you had to rely on pure sword intent---your raw, fundamental understanding of the blade.
That's what made this trial the perfect way to sharpen a swordsman's skill.
[SFX: ENERGY BATTLE]
Zane and Zachary battled through the relentless waves of Arcane Aurora, their blades slicing the air with precision and determination. They conquered the first wave with ease, surpassed the tenth with growing confidence, pushed through the fifteenth with gritted teeth, and advanced all the way to the twenty-first wave, their resolve never wavering despite the mounting difficulty.
[SFX: ENERGY STREAKS ZOOMING]
A chaotic storm of brilliant energy streaks suddenly filled the air, illuminating their sweat-drenched faces as dozens of Aurora bands hurtled toward them at once. With a quick glance at each other, Zane and Zachary instinctively divided the onslaught, each warrior taking his own side in the deadly dance.
"Breakthrough!" Zachary bellowed, his voice cracking with emotion. The war cry echoed across the platform, not merely to steel his own faltering nerves but to acknowledge the incredible threshold they had crossed together. His knuckles whitened around Wyrmfire's hilt as he prepared to face his greatest challenge yet.
[SFX: SWORD SLASHING RAPIDLY]
Zane's blade became a blur of silver light, flashing like ripples across a dark lake. The bands of light from his sword blurred into a solid wall, erasing every streak of Arcane Aurora from existence. The blasts came at different speeds, but none could outpace his blade. In seconds, his side of the platform was clear.
[MUSIC: RISING DANGER]
Unlike Zane, Zachary fought against mounting exhaustion, his movements increasingly frantic as he pushed his body beyond its natural limits. Sweat poured down his temples while his lungs burned for air.
With a final, desperate lunge that drew upon his last reserves of strength, he sent his dragon-shaped sword energy tearing through the final streak of Aurora just before it could overwhelm him.
It worked, but it shattered his carefully maintained form. His shoulders slumped, his knees buckled, and his technique---practiced thousands of times in less demanding circumstances---abandoned him completely.
[SFX: BODY FALLING]
His momentum sent him stumbling, and he fell flat on his face. He scrambled back to his feet, one side of his face already starting to bruise.
Seeing the mark on Zachary's cheek, Zane smirked. "Nice one. You really laid it all on the line."
"It was... awesome!" Zachary gasped, struggling to catch his breath. "That's a new record for me. I actually made it through the twenty-first wave! I can't believe it!"
He'd spent countless hours on The Seven-Star Platform, and his personal best had always been stalling out on the twentieth wave. The one time he'd even glimpsed the twenty-first, it was only because someone had bailed him out on the last blast.
Today, he had beaten the twenty-first wave all by himself. It was unbelievable.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
But the break between waves was always short. Before Zachary could even recover, the twenty-second was already rushing toward them.
[SFX: CRYSTAL SHATTERING]
He gave it everything he had, but it wasn't enough. A final, merciless streak of Arcane Aurora slipped past his guard and shattered his crystal ball. Zachary was drenched in sweat, looking like he'd just been pulled out of a river.
A swordsman was usually more agile than a fighter wielding a spear or broadsword; they rarely ended up looking so messy. But Zachary had just pushed himself far beyond his limits, and he was a complete wreck. The feeling was new to him.
He collapsed to the ground, utterly exhausted. As he fought for breath, he watched Zane still calmly dealing with his own blasts of Arcane Aurora. All he could think was that the guy was an absolute monster. He was only at the eighth level of the platform, yet he was handling this trial without breaking a sweat. Just how powerful was he?
