Ep 813 - Fiona

Word count: 1148

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT THRONE ROOM]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

The rulers of the great divine kingdoms—from War King York to the heads of Harlen and Wind—all craved the same thing: a way to break through to the next level of their power. But with the limited resources in the Lower World, that barrier was nearly impossible for even the strongest warriors of the Soul Sea Realm to overcome.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

This was what made the Little Monster so valuable. With a talent that defied all limits, he was a priceless treasure, and every kingdom was watching him. His protected status gave him a free pass to be an absolute menace, and no one dared to get in his way. 

Not long ago, in the York Divine Kingdom, he became obsessed with a famous courtesan and ended up fighting a gifted prince over her. In a fit of rage, he simply killed him. When War King York heard the news, he was livid, but he still didn’t lay a finger on the monster.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL WIND]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

After a moment of consideration, the Holy Maiden looked up at the strange figure floating in the air before her. Biting her lip, she finally spoke. “Yes,” she said, her voice strained. “I need you to fight for me.”

[SFX: SINISTER LAUGH]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

“No problem.” The Little Monster bared his teeth in a predatory grin that, combined with his unnatural black eyes, made him look less human and more… well, monster. He rubbed his hands together with a greedy mischief, drifting closer to the Holy Maiden and sniffing the air around her. “But I'm going to need something in return.”

A wave of nausea rolled through the Holy Maiden. She had a sick feeling she knew exactly where this was going. “What do you want?” she asked, her voice low.

[SFX: TELEPATHIC WHISPER]

Still rubbing his hands together, the monster spoke, sending his words directly into her mind with his Vitality so that not another soul could hear.

[SFX: SHARP GASP]

Suddenly, the Holy Maiden’s face flushed a deep, furious red. “Are you trying to get yourself killed?” she snapped.

It wasn't surprising he'd make such a vile request; at least he had the sense to keep the taboo proposal a secret. If anyone else had heard him, it would have created a massive scandal for the secret order. 

His demand was as simple as it was shocking: he wanted to spend one night with her. How could she possibly agree to something so degrading?

Seeing the genuine fury in her eyes, the Little Monster backed off slightly. “Fine, if you won't agree to that. But the next time the Reverse Spirit Channel opens, you have to let me have first pick of whatever comes down from the upper world.”

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHOIR]

He was talking about the source of the secret order's power — the very reason the union of seven divine kingdoms supported them, and why even emperors had to show the Holy Maiden respect. 

Only the secret order could communicate with the humans in the Upper World, who sent down priceless treasures every few years through the Reverse Spirit Channel. The first to choose had always been the rulers of the seven divine kingdoms — but the distribution was handled by the Holy Maiden.

It was an excessive demand. But it was one she could live with. She bit her lip and nodded. “Fine.”

“Good.” The Little Monster didn't look at her again. He didn't look at Zane either. He simply found an empty patch of ground, lay down, and went straight back to sleep.

Up in the sky, Zane had watched the whole exchange. He couldn't hear what the Little Monster had said, but he had seen the Holy Maiden's face. And he had seen how quickly she agreed. Arrogance at that level rarely came without something real behind it. He filed it away and kept his expression neutral.

The Holy Maiden looked up at him. A cold smile crossed her lips.

“Zane Lowell,” she said quietly, too quiet for most to hear. “I told you I would kill you. Three years and you still haven't escaped.”

[AMBIENT: OPEN SQUARE]

[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]

With Logan defeated, the square went quiet. Neither the Wind nor the Star Field kingdoms moved to send a challenger. No one spoke. Logan had been a peak first-tier warrior — one of the strongest young fighters in the union — and Zane had handled him without breaking a sweat. The gap in strength was too obvious. No one wanted to be next.

“Does no one wish to challenge the Harlen Divine Kingdom?” the Emperor asked, his voice carrying easily across the square.

Silence.

Then, the Wind Divine Kingdom's emperor, who had been quiet from the beginning, finally spoke. “Fiona. If you feel like it, go and exchange a few pointers. But don't go too far.”

A soft voice answered from behind him. “Yes, Father.”

A princess stepped forward — and the sight of her drew murmurs from the crowd. She looked almost too delicate to be standing on a battlefield. 

As she turned, circles of flaming lilies bloomed beneath her feet, and she stepped onto them, floating gracefully across the grounds.

[CROWD MURMURS]

“A member of the imperial family — actually stepping forward to fight.”

“It's never happened before. Not in years.”

“That's Fiona of the Wind Divine Kingdom. They say she's the most talented warrior the imperial family has produced in a generation. The Crown Prince was crippled because of her.”

“If she reaches the Soul Sea Realm — she could be the first empress in the history of the divine kingdoms.”

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

After passing through the enchanted barrier, she offered a sweet smile, a faint light glowing between her eyebrows. “I'm Fiona.”

Zane’s eyebrows shot up. He hadn’t expected a princess like Fiona to be so direct and polite. “Zane,” he replied, matching her tone.

“My father believes it's shameful for royalty to fight their own battles,” Fiona explained, choosing her words with care. “But I think he rules because of his strength, not his title. If he wasn't powerful enough to command respect, worrying about his image would be pointless.”

Her voice was like a cool spring, soft and incredibly soothing.

“I couldn't agree more,” Zane said with a nod. He knew better than anyone that reputation was just a house of cards without real strength to back it up.

“I'd like to spar with you,” Fiona said, her graceful smile never wavering.

“It would be an honor,” Zane replied without hesitation, assuming the fight would begin as soon as they were done talking.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]

But to his surprise, Fiona’s gaze drifted past him toward the main square. A flicker of fear crossed her face when she saw the Little Monster, now sleeping in a heap on the ground. 

She lowered her voice, leaning in closer to Zane. “Listen to me,” she whispered, her voice tight. “After our match… if you lose, you lose. But if you win, you have to pretend you didn't. You have to let me be the winner.”
