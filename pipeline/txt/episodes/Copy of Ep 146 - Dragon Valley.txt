Ep 146 - Dragon Valley
Word Count: 1055
[MUSIC: SOMBER ROMANTIC]
Zane stood in the hallway long after she'd disappeared behind that door, his expression caught somewhere between bewilderment and something he couldn't name. He'd meant it as a joke — a little payback for the Charms she'd trapped him with. He never expected her to actually do it.
The soldiers nearby exchanged glances, shaking their heads as if trying to figure out what had gotten into her.
He didn't see Yolande again after that.
[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]
Yolande, on the other hand, was used to a solitary life. When she wasn't on the front lines with the imperial army, she was in her room, focused on her Advancement.
[AMBIENT: CITY STREETS]
[SFX: BAG RUSTLING]
A little while later, it was time for Zane to leave the city. He packed a bag, slung it over his shoulder, and headed for the gates. On his way out, he paused in front of Yolande’s house, staring at it for a long moment before finally turning and walking away.
With the other disciples from the Skyreach Sect already gone, there was no Skycruiser coming for him. Zane was on his own. Fortunately, the demon had been defeated and two of the Archfiends were dead. The demon camps had shrunk to a fraction of their size. On top of that, the imperial army in Silverspire had wiped out the demons in the surrounding areas, including the sky forest.
The next phase was a counterattack to retake the Sky Spire and the Stone Spire.
But none of that really had anything to do with Zane. He was just a disciple of the Skyreach Sect, not a soldier.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR WILDERNESS]
Once he reached the edge of Silverspire, Zane started toward the Skyreach Sect. He needed to get a move on.
"If only I could get my hands on a Skycruiser in Greene County," Zane thought, but he knew the odds were slim. Still, in a county this size, he could probably find a fast horse for sale, like a Pegasus or a Thunder Stallion. They weren't nearly as fast as a Skycruiser, but a horse would definitely cut down his travel time and save some energy.
"The waiters at the inn might know where to buy one," he mused. "I'll ask later."
[AMBIENT: RESTAURANT CHATTER]
[SFX: LOUD FOOTSTEPS]
As Zane was finishing his meal, a group of guys swaggered into the restaurant and stomped upstairs. They were a loud, arrogant bunch, walking in like they owned the place. One of them reached into his pocket, pulled out two fist-sized gold bullions, and slammed them on the table before glancing around the room.
[SFX: GOLD SLAMMING ON TABLE]
"Waiter! Bring us your best food and wine!" he bellowed.
The one who seemed to be their leader plopped into a chair, stretched out lazily, and started barking orders at the waiters. The waiters’ eyes nearly popped out of their heads at the sight of the gold, and they immediately scrambled to serve the group, bowing and scraping.
[AMBIENT: LOUD VOICES AND SILVERWARE CLATTER]
Zane watched them for a second before going back to his food. They were so loud that their booming voices echoed through the restaurant, punctuated by the clatter of their silverware.
"Boss, are we really not going back to Dragon Valley?" one of the men asked.
[MUSIC: TENSE UNDERTONE]
The leader shot him a look. "Go back? Are you kidding me? Do you have any idea who those people are? They're from The Seven Ruling Families! Piss off just one of them, and they could wipe out The Sequoia Circle without breaking a sweat!" he said, his eyes wide.
The others just shook their heads. They knew it was true.
"Damn it! It’s bullshit," the man next to the leader grumbled. "Our Circle’s been in Dragon Valley for years. We have to pack up and leave just because some big shots waltz in and take it? What a joke!"
The others nodded in agreement.
"We don't have a choice," their leader said. "You know that saying about how even a mighty dragon can’t handle a snake in its own turf? Well, to them, we're not even a snake. And they're a hell of a lot bigger than any dragon."
He kept on eating as he complained, his buddies nodding along.
"Lucky for us, they were generous," another one piped up. "With these Crystal Cubes they gave us, our futures are set. Let's find some girls and have a good time tonight!"
The moment the conversation turned to girls, Zane tuned them out. But his ears had perked up at the mention of Dragon Valley. The name was familiar; he was sure he’d seen it in the Principles of Forging.
The group leader glanced nervously at the Crystal Cubes Zane had placed on their table. He knew he had no choice but to take the payment and give Zane the directions he wanted.
Tiny beads of sweat broke out on his temples as he spoke carefully. "Misty Peak isn't that big of a range. Pretty small, actually. Take the north gate out of Greene County and walk about twenty miles down the main road. Dragon Valley is nestled into the side of Misty Peak. You’ll see a Redwood Forest as you start to climb. Go past the forest, and you'll find a deep pool. Dragon Valley is right behind it," he finished.
The directions to their old base came tumbling out of the leader, his nervousness making him talk fast. After he finished, Zane clapped him on the shoulder. "Good," he said with a slight smile. "The Crystal Cube is yours."
Zane paid for his meal and headed downstairs. He had to check out Dragon Valley.
The second Zane was out the door, the group's leader deflated, slumping into his chair like all the air had been let out of him. "Those bastards!" he cursed, thinking of the people who'd stolen their home.
"But that kid..." one of his men said, nodding toward the door. "Damn, he's powerful, but generous, too. I can't believe we just scored another Crystal Cube that easily!"
