[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD BUZZING]
The crowd was still buzzing over the wild twists and turns of Zane and Adrian’s fight. At this point, nobody was paying much attention to the other matches happening in the arena.
[SFX: HEAVY STONES BEING MOVED]
Since Zane and Adrian had completely torn up the stage, a crew from the Skyreach Sect had to haul in massive stones to repair the battleground. Afterward, the elders restored the protective arrays and magical barriers so the next round could begin.
Two personal disciples took the stage for the next round, but their opponents were only at the peak of the Adept Realm. These challengers were strong, no doubt—they'd fought hard to make it to the round-robin tournament. Still, they weren’t on the same level as someone like Finn, so nobody was expecting an upset. Just as everyone predicted, the two personal disciples made short work of their opponents and claimed their victories.
After that, the tournament continued as expected. Every personal disciple won their match, with Adrian being the only exception.
The disciples from the other peaks were having a hell of a year, too. Jill, Finn, and Wynn were still on winning streaks — but Wes of Skytop Peak had already fallen, taken down by a personal disciple in a match that lasted barely a breath.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
The fights between the top disciples and personal disciples were the centerpiece of the All Peaks Competition—it was what everyone was waiting for.
Zane and Adrian’s battle had gone beyond simple combat and become a true spectacle, a magnificent display of skill and determination that captivated the audience completely. The raw intensity of their exchange, and the heart-stopping moments where it looked like one of them was near death, had seared into the memory of everyone present.
Even veteran observers of the tournament found themselves discussing the nuances of that match with animated gestures and wide eyes, reliving each dramatic moment as though it had just unfolded before them.
[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION AREA]
In a corner of the area, Saint Viana sat on a meditation cushion, his eyes fixed on the arena below. "A while ago, I wouldn’t have dared ask Johanson to handle this, not with him being your apprentice. I would've had to find someone among the disciples of the thirty-three peaks. But things are different now. Isla Novak is always sticking her nose into the royal family’s drama. Rumor has it she’s under house arrest. She’s got her own serious problems to deal with. You really think she has the time to worry about Zane?"
Despite the logic, Saint Augustus still looked hesitant. "But Zane’s performance was incredible. He's already caught the leader’s eye. If Johanson makes a move and kills him, won’t that piss the leader off?"
[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]
Saint Viana sneered."Favor is for the living. A dead prodigy isn’t a prodigy anymore—just a corpse."
He turned to Saint Augustus, who still looked reluctant. Viana's voice dripped with contempt as he continued, "Don’t forget you had a hand in sending Elara to Infernal Peak. If Zane isn’t dealt with, he’s talented enough to make it into the Cloud Hall, maybe even become a core disciple. No matter how high he climbs, he could use his position there to crush the Skyreach Sect without breaking a sweat. Do you really think he’ll just let what we did slide?"
The magnificent Cloud Hall wasn't just a step above the Skyreach Sect—it was in another league entirely, towering over maybe even the entire Eastern Territory.
Even a single core disciple visiting from the Cloud Hall would be greeted personally by the leader. That alone showed the massive power gap between the two sects.
Saint Augustus was speechless. He knew Saint Viana was right.
Johanson was Saint Augustus's greatest pride — a personal disciple capable of finishing in the top five among all personal disciples in the sect. Only a handful of people in the entire Skyreach Sect could genuinely challenge him. For Johanson, disposing of Zane would be trivial.
Saint Augustus said nothing further. He reached out with a thread of Vitality, a silent transmission, and quietly alerted Johanson to prepare himself. Meanwhile, Viana was already working to arrange the match pairing.
If Zane really did enter the Cloud Road and then the Cloud Hall, they would live the rest of their lives looking over their shoulders. If Zane actually became a core disciple or earned a high-ranking position, getting his revenge would be a walk in the park.
The best way to secure their future was probably to deal with Zane now.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
"Damn it!" Saint Viana hissed, his voice tight with frustration. "I never thought Isla would get tangled up in the Empire’s internal bullshit so quickly. If I’d known this was going to happen, I would’ve just killed Zane at his damn trial!"
On the day of the trial, Saint Augustus hadn’t made a move because Isla held the Emperor’s Token. The emperor of the Empire was supposedly on par with Sir Kenneth in strength, and word was he even outranked him within the Cloud Hall.
If Isla had decided to go all out and use the Emperor’s Token, Saint Viana would have been in serious trouble.
Viana never expected the Empire to erupt into internal conflict in just two months. Of all the rumors flying around, the most shocking was that Kane was already dead. Since the news came directly from the royal palace, it had to be true.
