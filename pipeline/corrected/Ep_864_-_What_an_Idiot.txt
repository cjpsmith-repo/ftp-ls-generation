Ep 864 - What an Idiot

Word count: 1084

[AMBIENT: SACRED FEATHER SEA]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Ever since they’d entered the Sacred Feather Sea, Fredrick and Zane had been at each other’s throats. 

The York Divine Kingdom’s feud with Zane was no secret—both the Holy Maiden and War King York wanted him dead. Fredrick knew the power gap between them was massive, so he’d swallowed his pride during their minor clashes. Once they left this secret land, his father would kill Zane.

But Fredrick had his limits. Zane’s arrogance had pushed him too far.

White-hot rage flooded his vision. If it had been anyone else, even the so-called Little Monster, Fredrick would have already lunged. But the thought of Zane’s almost monstrous strength kept him rooted to the spot, his jaw tight as he fought the urge to attack.

“And what if I don’t?” Fredrick snarled, his voice low and dangerous. “What are you going to do about it? You want a fight?”

“No. I wouldn’t dare attack the crown prince,” Zane said, a smile touching his lips but not his eyes. “And there’s no need to.”

[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]

As he spoke, an invisible pressure began to radiate from him, a slow, crushing weight that settled over the clearing.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

Zane’s soul had merged with the will of the world itself. The sheer force of it was enough to make the other saint-level geniuses, Fredrick included, feel a primal sense of dread.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STOPPING]

As the tension between them stretched thin, the warriors who had been trickling out of the mine stopped in their tracks. Instead of heading back to the pyramid, they hung back, ready for the show. 

Most of them knew better than to get involved if a fight broke out. The logic was simple: Zane had survived an attack from War King York without a scratch. That meant a master of the Soul Sea Realm couldn’t touch him without activating their domain—and no one below that level could even hope to hurt him.

At the same time, Zane wouldn’t dare make a move on Fredrick, either. He had already pissed off the York Divine Kingdom enough. If he actually hurt or, worse, killed their crown prince, War King York would hunt him to the ends of the earth. So, everyone settled in to watch the drama unfold.

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY WAVE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

But as Zane’s soul pressure washed over them, the expressions on the faces of the other geniuses twisted in shock. The aura was impossibly powerful, carrying a sense of absolute command that buckled the knees.

One by one, Fredrick’s followers faltered. They tried to hold their ground — none of them wanted to be seen retreating from someone they’d just called a commoner — but Zane’s soul pressure kept building, and their legs began to tremble. 

One. Two. Three. 

They stumbled back, unable to stop themselves, until only Fredrick was left standing.

[SFX: TENSE SILENCE]

Zane raised an eyebrow. Fredrick’s soul was nowhere near the Fighting Soul Realm — and yet he was still on his feet. Interesting.

What Zane couldn’t see was the necklace hidden beneath Fredrick’s collar. It was a top-grade Mythic defensive artifact with a purple gem at its center, designed to eliminate sixty percent of any soul attack directed at the wearer. It had been made for female warriors; the style was unmistakably feminine, which was why Fredrick kept it hidden. But its value rivaled a semi-divine weapon, and right now it was the only thing keeping him upright.

[MUSIC: SLOW DREAD]

“Huh.” Zane’s smile widened. “Your soul is nowhere near the Fighting Soul Realm, but you’re still standing. Let’s see how far you can go.”

He took a step forward.

Fredrick’s face flushed red.

Another step.

The red drained away, replaced by a sickly pallor. Fredrick’s jaw was clenched so hard his teeth ached.

Another step. And another.

[SFX: STRAINED BREATH]

By the sixth step, Fredrick’s face was white as bone. He was shaking. But he didn’t move.

“Zane.” His voice came out through gritted teeth. “Don’t push your luck.”

Zane stopped. He looked at Fredrick for a moment, then spoke — his voice unhurried, almost conversational.

“That halberd in your hand is worth three to four hundred thousand supreme Aether crystals. All I’m asking for is nine thousand back to Callan. You’re a crown prince, and you can’t settle a debt that small?” He tilted his head. “I know saving face matters to you. But even a crown prince needs to know how to read a situation. A divine kingdom is just a divine kingdom. In the wider world, it’s nothing. In the Upper Realm, it’s less than a speck of dust. If you can understand that, you’ll know what to do.”

[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE STRINGS]

When Fiona heard Zane’s words, her eyes lit up. Part of her was grateful he’d stood up for her and her brother, but his words sparked something deeper. She had always been spoiled by her father, War King Wind, which had left her innocent and a bit headstrong. But she was realizing that even a king couldn’t protect her forever. Her future wasn’t guaranteed. Maybe it was time to rethink her place in the world.

Zane didn’t actually care about the nine thousand supreme Aether crystals; he’d already made a fortune selling ore. And, of course, Fredrick didn’t care about them either. For him, this was about pride. Handing them over would be the same as admitting he was scared of Zane, and that was the one thing he would never do.

So, even though Zane’s demand was perfectly logical, Fredrick refused.

[SFX: CRACKLING ELECTRICITY]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

The Vitality in his hand flashed, and arcs of purple lightning crackled to life, wrapping around his Heavenly Thunder Halberd. The ancient runes etched into the weapon flared as he poured his power into them.

The onlookers stared, their faces draining of color. Many had seen the halberd’s power firsthand when it had wiped out a horde of ghosts in a single flash—a strike that rivaled an attack from a Soul Sea Realm master.

[AMBIENT: PANICKED CROWD]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS RETREATING]

A wave of panic rippled through the crowd. Warriors scrambled back, and even Camilla, Fiona, and the other crown prince put distance between themselves and the confrontation. Getting caught in that blast would mean serious injury, if not death.

[MUSIC: CALM CONFIDENT THEME]

“Ugh. What an idiot,” Zane muttered, completely unfazed as he took another calm step toward Fredrick.
