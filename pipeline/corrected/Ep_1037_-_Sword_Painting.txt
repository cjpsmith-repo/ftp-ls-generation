Ep 1037 - Sword Painting

Word count: 1089

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

Zane had first learned about the power of Divine Weapons back in the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms.

The Upper World, however, had a much clearer grading system for them. Fiona’s red jade bracelet, for example, was a Low Grade-Three Divine Weapon.

Even the Emperors of the divine kingdoms wielded Grade-Three weapons. Fenton’s, on the other hand, was a Low Grade-One Divine Weapon—a full two grades higher than anything Zane had ever faced.

This Regis-Bane was on another level entirely.

[SFX: WEAPON ACTIVATION PULSE]

[MUSIC: DARK IMPERIAL THEME]

The moment the weapon came alive, its wielder seemed to transform into a powerful, aggressive monarch, ruling his kingdom with an iron fist.

A crushing pressure descended on Zane, trying to overwhelm him, to force him to his knees before this new authority. But he refused to back down.

His eyes glinted with scorn. “You think you can suppress me?”

Boom!

A powerful, equally oppressive force suddenly erupted from Zane’s soul.

A warrior’s training focused on three areas: their body, their Vitality, and their soul.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]

Zane’s body refining and Vitality cultivation were unlike anyone else’s.

His soul training, however, had been more conventional.

During his last stay in The Heartlands, the Cobalt Dragon had planted a soul seed in him, which allowed his soul to finally ascend to the Nightjade Fighting Soul.

Even though he’d worked hard to get there, Zane had never held much of an advantage in soul training and had been stuck in the Fighting Soul Realm for a long time.

The Saint Lord of the Soulbound Sect was already aware of Zane’s soul strength. But reaching the Nightjade Fighting Soul as a fifth-level Life-and-Death Realm warrior, while impressive in the Lower World, was nothing special in the Upper World.

Still, the soul seed planted by the Cobalt Dragon had granted Zane extraordinary power.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC]

As Zane unleashed his own counter-pressure, the seed flared to life, instantly tripling his soul pressure.

Not only that, but his soul was fused with the will of this world. The imposing aura of the Chaos World was now inseparably mixed with Zane’s own soul pressure.

Regis-Bane’s aura was formidable — but it was built on comprehension. A monarch’s understanding of power, forged into steel. Zane’s soul pressure was something else entirely: the genuine will of the Chaos World, an accumulation of countless years. There was no contest between the constructed and the real.

The weapon’s aura buckled. Zane’s pressure crashed over it and drove it back.

Across the arena, Fenton’s expression grew very still.

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]

The Regis-Bane that Fenton wielded was a Low Grade-One Divine Weapon, forged long ago from the very lifeblood of an Emperor from a tenth-grade divine kingdom.

For a long moment, neither fighter moved.

Zane tilted his sword slightly and smiled. “So — the fourth sword?”

Fenton shook his head. “The seventh.”

He had been counting in silence the entire time — every slash used to disperse Zane’s blade included.

Zane let out a quiet laugh. “You really don’t miss anything, do you.”

“If I can’t defeat you with this sword,” Fenton said, his voice level, “I’ll admit defeat.”

Zane met his eyes and nodded. “Then make your move.”

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

The Saint Lord’s eyes drifted back to Zane. A new thought had surfaced.

“This boy has already faced five tribulations,” he said quietly. “Has he opened his lotus flower? How many leaves?”

The elder beside him blinked, then slapped the side of his own head. “Of course — with this kind of talent, he must have. Seven leaves, perhaps?”

The Saint Lord said nothing for a moment. “Seven,” he agreed.

The elder gave him a look. “My lord… I think it’s more likely nine.”

The Saint Lord knew it too. He just didn’t want to say it. It was easier, somehow, to imagine a little less gold in the bag he’d let slip through his fingers. He exhaled. “Perhaps.”

Elder Roland let out a loud laugh. “How were we supposed to know he’d be so talented! Besides, if the Thirteen Palaces hadn’t found him, he’d still be down in the mines digging for Sovereign Stones!”

Elder Roland’s words only seemed to make the Saint Lord angrier. “Check the files on all the ascenders. Assess each person’s strength and talent and report back to me. You have one month!” the Saint Lord ordered, his voice like ice.

Elder Roland went pale. He realized the Saint Lord was just taking his anger out on him. The data would be useful for the Soulbound Sect, sure, but a search on this scale was a fool’s errand. They weren’t going to find another Zane anytime soon.

But you didn’t argue with an order from the Saint Lord. Elder Roland knew a long, pointless task lay ahead of him, but what choice did he have?

[AMBIENT: CRUCIBLE MURMUR]

Out in The Crucible, the mood had shifted. The watching warriors had gone quiet — not with shock this time, but with understanding. Zane was the real thing. There was nothing left to debate.

Somewhere in the crowd, Mervin Thorne watched in silence. He had come in ranked second in the Myriad Spirit House. He understood now, with cold clarity, that he was not in the same conversation as the two fighters in that arena. If he had stepped in instead of Fenton, he wouldn’t have lasted a single round.

The Holy City warriors who had harassed Zane in the weeks before — the ones who’d picked fights and looked for angles — sat very still. They were Soul Sea Realm cultivators. Zane was Fifth-Level Life-and-Death. And they had been lucky that he had a good temper.

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

During all this, Fenton was preparing his final move—the one that would decide this fight.

[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY]

“Sword Painting! Let the entire state appear before me, a single wisp of wind creating everything!” he chanted, swinging the Regis-Bane through the air.

[SFX: MAGICAL MANIFESTATION]

A magnificent picture materialized from the blade’s movements, as if it were a giant brush instead of a sharp weapon. The painting depicted a view of the entire state.

Flares of gold within the picture signified the power of Metal Law, while streaks of green represented Wind Law.

The very act of painting sliced across Fenton’s arms, letting his red Life-Essence swirl out to add its own vivid color to the magnificent picture.

The picture was almost complete.
