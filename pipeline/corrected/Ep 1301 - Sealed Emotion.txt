Ep 1301 - Sealed Emotion

Word count: 1041

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL MYSTICAL]
[AMBIENT: OTHERWORLDLY REALM]
In a place where everyone was just a soul, you didn’t see a True God’s power in the usual sense. Instead, the God projected his very essence into the world, allowing a warrior to understand his divine path simply by being there. It was like standing inside the concept of his power itself.
A single warrior, an ogre, guarded the isolated city. With one saber, he had just fought an army of tens of thousands and beaten them all back, his domineering aura radiating out in waves that made his enemies tremble. 
After his final attack, not a single soldier from the opposing army was left standing.
Of course, Zane was the only one who had managed to dodge the strike, and only because he’d thrown himself flat on the ground to avoid the deadly swing.
But the moment Zane climbed back to his feet, the ogre’s gaze fell on him. 
[SFX: SOUL PRESSURE BUILDING]
[MUSIC: TENSE OMINOUS]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
“Why aren’t you on the ground?” the ogre’s thunderous roar sent a tremor through Zane’s spirit.
“I still have both my legs. Why would I be lying down?”
“If you won’t lie down, you’ll kneel.”
[SFX: AIR TEARING]
[SFX: BLADE SWING]
With a sound like tearing air, the ogre swung the long blade in his hand and pointed it at Zane. Then, the saber swept downward. 
[SFX: CRUSHING WEIGHT DESCENDING]
It never touched Zane’s body, but he instantly felt a heavy pressure building on his shoulders, trying to force him to the ground. His knees felt like they were about to give out. A fierce, overwhelming aura pulsed from the unseen force, as if a mountain was pressing down on him.
This was his soul form, so the pressure wasn’t physical. He couldn’t use the power of his Dragon Scales to resist it.
“Kneel and submit to me!” the ogre shouted.
The saber continued its slow descent, and the pressure grew stronger, forcing Zane to use more and more of his will just to stay upright. But even against that incredible strength, he stood his ground, his feet planted so firmly they began to sink into the imaginary dirt. 
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS SINKING INTO GROUND]
His eyes were locked on the ogre, his face showing no hint of compromise. There was no surrender in him.
“I don’t kneel to the heavens or the earth. I’ve done nothing in my life to feel guilty about, so why would I ever kneel to you?” Zane said coldly, gritting his teeth as he held his ground.
The ogre sneered. “If you won’t kneel, then you’ll die.”
[SFX: MASSIVE BLADE WHOOSH]
[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY SURGE]
He swung the massive saber again. An incredibly intense blade energy, thick with the formidable aura of the weapon itself, came hacking down toward Zane.
[SFX: METAL CLASH IMPACT]
“Hmph!” Zane fought back, meeting force with force.
The huge sword he’d taken from an ogre soldier was too big to swing with his physical body, but in this battle of souls, what mattered was the power of one’s spirit.
[SFX: SWORD INTENT SHATTERING]
His first strike met the ogre’s blade energy head-on—and was annihilated instantly. Zane’s eyes narrowed, and he fell back several feet to steady himself.
The moment he retreated, something in his gaze changed. Every trace of feeling drained out of his eyes, replaced by an absolute cold.
[SFX: BLADE HUM INTENSIFYING]
He entered Sealed Emotion for the first time, the technique unfolding as pure sword intent, unburdened by the weight of a physical body.
[SFX: SWORD INTENT CUTTING]
The strike was thin, but it was unstoppable—it split a crack straight through the ogre’s blade energy, and Zane drove himself through the gap it left behind.
The ogre’s eyes went wide. He was a True God’s mark—his blade energy shouldn’t have broken so easily against some junior.
[SFX: BLADE SWING]
He struck again, another wave of overbearing blade energy crashing forward. Zane’s face stayed blank. He raised his sword and cut through it a second time.
[SFX: METAL CLASH IMPACT]
Having had his blade energy broken twice in a row, the ogre let out a booming laugh, shouldered his blade, and closed the distance in a single stride, bearing down on Zane directly.
[SFX: SWORD SHATTERING]
Under the weight of his next blow, the ogre soldier’s sword in Zane’s hand shattered outright—it had never been more than an ordinary blade, seized in a moment of desperation, and it hadn’t stood a chance.
[SFX: WHOOSH OF STEEL]
But the ogre wasn’t there to kill him. With a flick of his longsword, he sent another blade lying nearby sailing toward Zane. “I don’t believe you can break this one too. Hold onto it.”
[SFX: RAPID SWORD SLASHES]
[SFX: CLOUDS TEARING APART]
[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]
With each cut, the ogre’s blade shredded the thick, dark clouds above.
[SFX: FINAL SLASH]
After the final slash, the ogre spoke, his voice cold. 
“The saber is dominance. My entire life has been a pursuit of that single, overwhelming aura. I used my saber to achieve the Domineering Divine Path. And with it, I earned the Unrivaled Godly Sigil. My name is Reggie.”
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL ASCENDING]
[SFX: CLOUDS SPLITTING]
[SFX: BLADE ENERGY DESCENDING]
As he finished speaking, the clouds he had gathered were sliced completely apart, and a single ray of sunlight pierced through the gloom. At the same time, an invisible blade energy descended from the sky, gathering all around Zane.
[SFX: LOW HUMMING VIBRATION]
A low hum filled the air.
Zane, however, just pointed his sword toward the sky, completely calm. The blade energy was incredibly powerful, seeming to blend with heaven and earth, but even under its crushing weight, he remained unmoved.
He entered a state of Sealed Emotion again, cutting off all feelings until he was nothing. 
[SFX: BLADE ENERGY TORRENTS]
[AMBIENT: RAGING ENERGY STORM]
Countless streaks of blade energy rained down, surging wildly around Zane like a raging river. But he was a boulder in the middle of the current, standing tall and unmoving. The raging torrents couldn’t make him budge an inch; they were forced to split around him and flow off in two different directions.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: QUIET MOUNTAIN PEAK]
After a long time, the thick clouds in the sky began to close up again, and the blade energy stopped falling.
Zane stared at the True God named Reggie. He had finally come out of the state of Sealed Emotion, and his eyes looked a little brighter.
When Reggie noticed the change in him, he let out a loud laugh, though it was tinged with regret. “My path isn’t for you. Besides, you already have your own. But the path you’re trying to master… it’s doomed to be a waste of your time.”
“Why?” Zane asked, the simple word loaded with confusion after everything he had just endured.
