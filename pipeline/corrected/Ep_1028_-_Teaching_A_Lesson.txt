Ep 1028 - Teaching A Lesson

Word count: 966

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING SKY CITY]

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

Countless warriors streaked across the sky, their flight paths weaving together like a massive web.

These warriors were a different breed from the pedestrians on the streets below—bolder, more powerful. The argument brewing between Zane and a small crowd had already caught their eye, and several slowed, hovering in place to watch the show.

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

When they heard Zane call the Soul Sea Realm warriors scum and dare the whole lot of them to fight him at once, a buzz shot through the onlookers. They were always eager for a good show, but what really had them curious was why some nobody from the Life-and-Death Realm was acting so damn cocky.

But everyone knew you needed some serious power to back up that kind of talk. If he couldn’t defend himself, he was just asking to get himself killed.

[SFX: SWORD DRAWN]

“What’s your problem? Think you’re some kind of big shot?” a long-haired young man sneered, stepping out from the crowd. “Telling all of us to take you on at once? You’re way too full of yourself. I can handle a punk like you all by myself!”

Zane just grinned, raising the longsword in his hand. He was ready.

But the two hall leaders weren’t about to let a brawl break out. Their mission was to get Zane to The Holy City, which meant they had to shut this down—even if they knew Zane could more than handle himself.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Master Hill stepped forward. “Apologies,” he said, addressing the young warriors. “We have urgent business. There’s no time for this. I suggest you all stand down.”

“Back off? And who do you think you are, old man? You think you can make us?”

Master Hill’s expression darkened. “Kid, no matter who your elders are, you’d better learn to watch your mouth. If you won’t stop, I’ll have to teach you a lesson on their behalf.”

The long-haired youth laughed. “My grandfather is Elder Crane Heart of the Soulbound Sect. Teach me a lesson? I’m afraid you don’t have the qualifications.”

The two masters exchanged a glance. As expected, anyone this brazen in the Holy City had to have serious backing. Elder Crane Heart had a vicious temper and was fiercely protective of his own. Neither of them wanted that trouble.

“And what if I don’t?” the long-haired youth shot back with a snort. He could clearly feel the immense power coming off the two hall leaders, but he didn’t seem to give a damn.

The youth took a step forward. His crew surged with him.

“Tell us — how exactly are you going to teach anyone a lesson?”

“You might run a hall somewhere,” the long-haired youth said, pressing closer with each word, “but in the Holy City?” Another step. “You’re nothing.”

Each step forward pushed Master Hill back. One of the top figures in the entire Soulbound Sect, being walked backward through the sky by a boy in his twenties — in front of a crowd. The humiliation was plain on his face. He took another step back and said nothing.

Just then, Zane moved. Wind Law surging through his body, he burst forward and slapped the long-haired youth across the face. The crack echoed across the sky. The youth spun like a top, arms flailing. By the time he steadied himself, half his face was swollen red — five finger marks rising on his cheek like a relief carved in flesh.

The watching crowd stared.

“I told you you were trash,” Zane said. “You didn’t believe me. And in the end, you still had to drag out your family’s elders.”

“Kill him!” The youth’s humiliation erupted into fury. “Kill this brat!”

The whole group surged forward. Zane set his feet, ready.

Master Sloan raised a hand.

[SFX: TIME DISTORTION HUM]

A force laced with Time Law swept outward and locked the young warriors in place. Master Sloan nodded to Master Hill. Master Hill grabbed Zane, and the three of them vanished in a spiral of white light.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[SFX: TIME DISTORTION FADE]

After the three of them vanished, the effect of the Time Law slowly faded, and the young warriors could move again. Their faces were grim, each one struggling to process the humiliating defeat.

“As long as you’re in The Holy City, you can’t run from me!” the long-haired youth growled, his face twisted with malice.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS THEME]

He popped a pill into his mouth. An expensive, eighth-grade pill.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Most warriors wouldn’t dream of wasting such a costly pill on anything less than a mortal wound. Zane’s slap had been humiliating, sure, but it was hardly life-threatening. Still, the bright red handprint blooming on his cheek was a badge of dishonor he refused to wear. He swallowed the precious pill, needing every trace of his embarrassment erased—and fast.

The pill worked with astonishing speed. Not even a minute after he swallowed it, the angry red handprint on his cheek began to fade.

But while the mark on his face vanished, the rage in his heart only burned hotter.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: BUSY CITY STREETS]

In a flash of light, the Grand Teleportation Technique deposited Zane and the two hall leaders in a different corner of The Holy City.

The Grand Teleportation Technique was a high-level Space Law ability — capable of covering hundreds of miles in a single jump. At its peak, it could cross entire worlds.

Master Hill sighed. “Forget it. Let’s just keep moving.”

Zane noticed the worried look on Master Hill’s face. “Master Hill, why are you so worked up over a bunch of punks? They had it coming!”

Master Hill didn’t answer. Instead, Master Sloan cut in. “Zane, you don’t get it. The Thirty-six Halls might seem powerful to an outsider, but in The Holy City, we’re nothing compared to the major factions. Those kids… they come from powerful families. I have a feeling they’re not going to let this go.”
