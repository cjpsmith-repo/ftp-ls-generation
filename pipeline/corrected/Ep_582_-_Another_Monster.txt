Ep 582 - Another Monster

[AMBIENT: BUSY TAVERN]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]
Elder Kingsley’s smile stretched wide as he looked at Zane.
He felt a surge of hope for the Violet Heart Sanctuary’s future. Maybe, just maybe, things were finally looking up.
The Hundred Clans were tearing each other apart for control of the main continent, but this was a real chance for humans to reclaim their rightful place. It was humanity’s greatest advantage—the ability to cultivate Vitality. They had even produced two saint-level geniuses, one three hundred years ago and another a century back. Both had been their best hope for reaching the legendary Soul Sea Realm.
But tragically, neither had made it. The first was ambushed by a rival clan the second he stepped outside the Sanctuary’s walls. The other grew into a force to be reckoned with, only to perish in the Dark Nether Cave—a place of both untold opportunity and lethal danger, where even War Lords could be wiped out in the blink of an eye.
[MUSIC: SWELLING HOPE]
But Zane… Zane was different. Kingsley hardly dared to admit it, even to himself. His strength was already leagues beyond any normal War Fighter—hell, he could probably take on a tenth-rank general. And his soul… it felt strong enough to buckle the knees of a War Lord.
This young man might just be the one. If he could reach the Soul Sea Realm, he wouldn't just be unstoppable. He’d be a force no one on the entire continent could hope to contain.
Seeing that kind of potential, Kingsley knew he couldn't hold back. He offered Zane the full backing and resources of the Violet Heart Sanctuary.
He’d already gotten everything he'd hoped for from the meeting. Still, a part of him wanted to push it, to invite the kid to the Sanctuary right then and there. They had a teleportation array in the Cursed Land; it was a direct trip. And if, by some miracle, the powers-that-be in the Soul Sea Realm would lend Zane the Violet Heart Plate… well, that would change everything.
But he knew it was a fantasy. Even saint-level geniuses didn’t get that kind of special treatment. Besides, he didn’t have the authority to make an offer like that anyway. So, he kept his mouth shut.
As he, Olivia, and the others were leaving, she glanced back at Zane over her shoulder. "You still don't know my name!"
Zane offered a warm smile. "What's your name?"
Olivia’s own smile brightened. "Olivia," she said, then turned and followed Elder Kingsley.
[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION ROOM]
[MUSIC: DETERMINED TRAINING THEME]
Back in his quarters at the Tower of Sin, Zane didn't waste a second. He rented a new practice room and got right back to refining the Lucky Light he'd won from Teresa. He could feel it boosting his power at a staggering rate.
To have any chance of fighting Maddox and winning, Zane knew he had to become a War Lord. And he was going to do it right here, in the Tower of Sin.
But the Lucky Light had its limits. What he'd taken from Teresa had already pushed him from seventh to eighth rank—though truthfully, he'd been on the verge of that breakthrough anyway. The Lucky Light had simply been the spark. Now, to push further, he'd need just as much again. And the lower floors of the Tower didn't have enough to offer. The fighters down here had clawed their way up one step at a time, and their reserves of Lucky Light were thin.
The answer was clear: he had to climb. The higher the floor, the richer the reward. Every fighter who had made it to the fifth layer or beyond was carrying far more Lucky Light than anyone down here. That was where Zane needed to be.
The only problem was the Tower's rules. You couldn't skip floors. Even if every arena stood empty, it would still take time. There was nothing to do but move.
[AMBIENT: TOWER ARENA — MORNING]
[MUSIC: QUIET ANTICIPATION]
After a full night of cultivation, Zane stepped out of the practice room and made his way to the arena.
[SFX: CROWD SILENCE]
The place was never truly quiet — fighters competed day and night — but the moment Zane walked in, the noise died.
Word had already spread across the entire second floor, and well beyond it: a human War Fighter had used nothing but soul pressure to drive two Ogre War Lords out of a tavern. Everyone knew his name now. Everyone knew his face.
Two fighters had been in the middle of a match when Zane arrived. When the winner looked up and saw him standing outside the arena, the blood drained from his face. He'd just strung together sixteen consecutive victories. He was the undisputed overlord of the second floor.
It didn't matter.
[SFX: HURRIED FOOTSTEPS]
He scrambled out of the arena like he'd been burned.
No one laughed. Every creature watching understood perfectly. Dying at Zane's hands wouldn't be a defeat — it would just be a waste. You'd be nothing but a stepping stone, and your sixteen wins would mean less than nothing.
[AMBIENT: LARGE ARENA CROWD MURMUR]
Zane stepped into the empty arena and let his gaze sweep the crowd.
"Does anyone dare to challenge me?"
Silence. Nobody moved. Every fighter on the second floor wanted exactly one thing: for Zane to move on as fast as possible. Each day he stayed was another day of dread. If the arena stayed empty, he could advance. Everyone would be relieved to see the back of him.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]
Teresa’s sudden appearance silenced the crowd. In their fear of Zane, they’d forgotten there was another monster in their midst.
Zane was terrifying, but Teresa was just as dangerous. They were the two untouchables among the War Fighters, and now one of them was pissed off after a loss. If she decided to blow off some steam on them, it was going to be a bloodbath.
It was always the same story: the weak were at the mercy of the strong.
For any War Fighter on the first five layers, the choice was grim: climb, or become a stepping stone for someone else. The recent rule change in the Tower had been a small mercy. Before that, things were even worse. There was no such thing as a default victory, so War Generals would just farm points off the lower layers to get promoted. Weaker fighters were nothing but cannon fodder for the War Generals, who were, in turn, prey for the War Lords.
Back then, the Tower of Sin was pure chaos. No safe zones. A skilled fighter could be cut down at any moment, and the most ruthless killers had free rein to hunt anyone, anytime.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION RISING]
Seeing Teresa left a sour taste in Zane's mouth. So she thinks she can just show up and start trouble, a day after I shattered her precious winning streak? Does she really think I won't kill her?
His eyes narrowed, a flash of pure rage burning in them.
The sheer killing intent in his gaze made Teresa’s palms begin to sweat, but she didn’t back down.
"I want to challenge you," she declared. "But not on the second layer. I'll let you move up. When I catch up to you, we'll fight again!"
[SFX: CROWD RELIEF EXHALE]
A collective sigh of relief rippled through the crowd. So, Teresa wasn't a complete idiot after all.
Zane had let her go once. Challenging him again today would have been a suicide mission, and every single person there knew it.
