Ep 869 - Back Off

Word count: 1163

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD]

The other saint-level fighters completely lost their composure at the sight of the Black Star Pickax. 

They knew, logically, that they were no match for Zane, but logic didn’t stand a chance against this kind of temptation. An ordinary Divine-Class weapon was enough to make most of them risk their lives. But this? This was a top-tier Divine-Class weapon. The thought of possessing it was so intoxicating that dying for the chance seemed like a reasonable trade.

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

In a sudden rush, seven or eight of them threw themselves from the leafy platforms of the thirty-sixth layer. The moment they were airborne, an invisible force yanked them downward, but they had a precious split second to angle their descent. Every single one of them was aiming for the Black Star. 

They weren’t the only ones. Fighters from the thirty-seventh layer dove into the chaos, and even those on the thirty-fourth and thirty-third were crouched at the edge, their eyes locked on the prize. The second the Black Star began its descent, they were ready to jump.

The explosion that had shattered the golden leaf had blasted Zane, the pickax, and a shower of golden fragments into the air. For a brief moment, they hung there, weightless, before gravity—or whatever unseen force governed this place—seized them and dragged them down.

“Zane, watch out!” Randy yelled from a distant platform. He was too far away to do anything but shout, a frustration shared by Camilla and Fiona, who could only watch helplessly.

[MUSIC: DARK BATTLE THEME]

A dark look crossed Zane’s face as he saw the swarm of fighters angling toward him. He’d fought too damn hard for this pickax to let a bunch of opportunistic vultures try to snatch it from his grasp. They were about to find out he wasn’t an easy target.

“You want to rob me?” Zane scoffed, his voice dripping with contempt. “You must have a death wish.”

[SFX: ENERGY EXPLOSION]

With that thought, the soul pressure coiled in his mind exploded outward. He had used it before, but always with restraint. If he hadn’t been holding back, his soul—fused as it was with the World’s Will—would have been enough to keep these Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors from ever getting close. 

But the Black Star was too important. He wasn’t losing it. This time, he let it all loose.

[SFX: SOUL PRESSURE WAVE]

Even from more than ten floors away, Randy, Fiona, and Camilla felt it — a wave that turned their faces pale. Randy exhaled slowly. “He’s really angry this time.”

Camilla’s expression didn’t change. “They brought it on themselves,” she said flatly. “He risked his life to open that leaf. Anyone who jumps for it deserves what they get.”

Fiona said nothing. In the past, she would have called out, tried to stop it, urged restraint. Now, watching the figures dropping toward Zane, she found she had no such impulse. She thought: he did the right thing.

The warriors who had stayed on their leaves felt a wave of relief wash over them. They’d wanted to jump. They hadn’t. Feeling even a fraction of that soul pressure from this distance, they were very glad they’d held back.

The effect was immediate. As the full force of his soul pressure washed over them, the attacking fighters froze, their bodies suddenly rigid and unresponsive.

[SFX: BODIES IN FREEFALL]

They didn’t just stop moving. Blood began to seep from their eyes, their ears, their noses, their mouths — thin dark lines running down their faces as they fell. By the time they hit the bottom, there were no signs of life left in any of them.

Zane, meanwhile, was plummeting. He dropped past layer after layer in a freefall, going from the thirty-fifth level down to the twentieth in the blink of an eye. The Black Star was clutched in his hand, and the golden fragments rained down around him, a glittering storm in his wake.

[SFX: WIND RUSHING]

[SFX: IMPACT — GROUND]

He landed hard in the large pit at the base of the pyramid, driving the Black Star Pickax into the ground beside him. A breath later, the portals above flickered — and the bodies began to fall.

[SFX: BODIES HITTING GROUND]

One after another, they dropped around him like rain. Ten or more of them, landing in a rough circle with Zane at the center, hand still on the pickax. He stood in the middle of the carnage without moving. He didn’t look at them.

[AMBIENT: PANICKED CROWD]

The warriors who had stayed outside the pyramid stared. They hadn’t seen what happened inside. All they saw was Zane standing in a pit surrounded by corpses. Panic rippled through the crowd.

“He’s killing people — why is he killing people?!”

“What is that in his hand?”

“Is that — is that the Black Star Pickax? From the golden leaf on the thirty-fifth floor?”

“How did he get it out?”

The questions tumbled over each other. The corpses and the Pickax together were almost too much to process at once.

As the last of the glowing rings inside the mine faded, Randy grinned. “Let’s go get a closer look!”

[SFX: MULTIPLE BODIES FALLING]

He took a running leap off the platform, and the other warriors who had held back followed his lead, jumping into the open air to fall to the bottom.

Even the Crown Prince of the Harlen Divine Kingdom was smiling. Zane had shattered his expectations again and again. 

For so long, the Prince had been insecure about his brother Randy’s strength, always maneuvering to keep him down, to maintain his own fragile upper hand. But now, thanks to Zane, Randy was sure to pull ahead, and there wasn’t a thing he could do to stop it. And as he stood on the edge of the platform, a strange thought occurred to him: maybe that was okay. They were brothers, after all. Maybe it was time to let the rivalry go. A look of genuine relief softened his features as he stepped off the edge and into the fall.

Everyone regrouped outside the pyramid. Zane stood a short distance away, a slight frown on his face as he stared down at the pickax in his hands. He turned it over and over, trying to figure out what it was made of, but there was nothing remarkable about it. It was just… a pickax.

Is this really a so-called Divine-Class weapon? he thought, his confusion mounting. 

He thought of the Semi-Divine Weapon on Fiona’s arm, of Fredrick’s Heavenly Thunder Halberd. The sheer power and vibrant Aura they radiated put them in a different universe from common Relic-Class weapons. This thing, the Black Star, felt… dead in comparison. 

Other than being impossibly durable, Zane couldn’t find a single thing about it that screamed “Divine-Class,” let alone one of the most powerful in existence.

What was so special about this thing?
