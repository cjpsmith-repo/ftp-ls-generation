Ep 90 - The Soul Realm

Perrin lay sprawled on the black gravel, his eyes hollow with despair. "This is... wrong," he choked out, the words barely a whisper. None of it made sense. He was completely lost.
Back with House Lowell, his power had skyrocketed after he consumed one of their only two Aether Crystals.
Then, after joining the Skyreach Sect, he'd shrewdly aligned himself with House Warrick, even using Elara to get closer to Adrian. It was all a calculated climb, the reason he'd become a Nature Creature so fast.
Before him, only Zane's father, Milo, had ever become a Nature Creature in all of House Lowell's history. When his own father and uncle took charge, Perrin had foolishly believed his future was guaranteed—a smooth ride to the top. He'd strong-armed the elders into giving him an Aether Crystal, his head filled with dreams of restoring House Lowell's glory, of making them the most powerful family in Castermere County.
But today, Zane had dismantled him in a handful of moves. That dream was now nothing but dust.
"I'm a Nature Creature," Perrin muttered, staring blankly up at Zane. "Why am I this weak? How could I lose to someone who's only at the Marrow Refining Level?"
"Everything you have is an illusion," Zane said, his voice flat. A cold smile touched his lips for a second, then vanished, leaving only a trace of pity. "You only got this far because of Energy Capsules."
It was a common shortcut among the Great Houses. People like Perrin used Energy Capsules to inflate their power level, but they had absolutely no real combat experience. They always folded the moment they faced a true fighter like Zane—someone forged in the fires of real battle.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS WALKING AWAY]
With that, Zane turned and walked away, blinking hard against a sudden sting in his eyes.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE SOUNDS]
[SFX: BRILLIANT FLASH]
Meanwhile, on Infernal Peak, a brilliant light suddenly erupted, forcing everyone to turn. Even fighters locked in combat couldn't help but stare at the radiant glow.
"Who in the world is burning their Vitality like that?" one man gasped. "That kind of power is insane!"
"That's a Soul Realm fighter!" another exclaimed. "Their skill is off the charts. What are they even doing on Infernal Peak?"
"At the Skyreach Sect, the disciples in purple are the elite," a more experienced man explained to those around him. "They have dedicated masters. But that kid in the white robe... he's just an Outer Disciple."
"That's impossible! Why would a Soul Realm fighter be so reckless? They could kill an Outer Disciple without breaking a sweat. It's unheard of to burn your own Vitality just to fight some nobody."
A ripple of unease and confusion spread through the crowd on Infernal Peak.
The rumor was that a Soul Realm cultivator's power surpassed even that of a Nature Creature. Out of the hundreds of thousands of disciples in the Skyreach Sect, only a select few ever reached that level, and every single one was a member of the Honor Guard. No one could understand why one of them would need to make such a show of force against a far weaker opponent. A warrior from the Soul Realm could typically eliminate an Outer Disciple with a casual flick of their finger.
"That poor kid must be scared out of his mind," one of the onlookers murmured.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN ESTATE]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
After Zane had left her, Isla Novak had shut herself in a pavilion on Misty Peak, tracking his every move on the Engraved Slab. Through it, she could see everything unfolding around him.
"You stubborn brat," Isla muttered, her eyes fixed on the image. "Always finding new ways to cause trouble."
She looked northwest, her brow furrowing at the brilliant pillar of light she could see even from this distance—Adrian.
"I don't care if you live or die," she fumed to herself. "You think you're so tough? Fine. Get yourself out of this one." She huffed, trying to convince herself. "He is such a pain."
The truth was, masters at the Skyreach Sect had little real authority. It wasn't a true mentorship, not unless you were one of the chosen few who received personal training. Zane didn't have to listen to her, and her anger was mostly just her own fiery personality clashing with his stubbornness.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STORMING AWAY]
Isla gritted her teeth, her gaze snapping back to Zane's determined face on the Engraved Slab. After a few tense seconds, she stormed out of the pavilion and boarded a Skycruiser bound for Infernal Peak.
On the summit, Elara saw the pillar of light erupt from the mountain's waist. She looked down.
Her eyes found Adrian first. Then the white-robed figure he was facing.
She went very still.
"Brother?"
She was at the edge of the platform before she'd finished the thought — and leapt.
[SFX: ENERGY BARRIER ACTIVATING]
The Six Sides Star Formation caught her. Hexagonal barriers snapped into place below her feet, blocking her path. She hit them and bounced back.
She attacked. One barrier shattered — three more took its place. She attacked again. The formation's energy was endless.
[SFX: REPEATED IMPACTS]
Below, Zane was facing a Soul Realm cultivator with nothing but his body and his will. Elara struck the barrier again and again, her eyes fixed on her brother's face.
Outside, Zane froze at Adrian calling his name, “Zane. I don't appreciate your lack of respect.” 
Zane turned to see Adrian. Fist clenched. Ready to fight. “We don’t need to do this right now.”
Adrian tightened his fist. “Unfortunately, we do.” Adrian lunged at Zane.
[AMBIENT: WINDY MOUNTAIN PEAK]
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC MELODY]
On another of Infernal Peak's summits, Elara sat perched on a rock, fiddling with her slingshot. A sweet, wistful smile played on her lips as she lost herself in happier memories. Being imprisoned up here didn't leave her much else to do.
No matter how many times she tested it, the energy flowing around her didn't weaken. It seemed to have a limitless supply of power. The situation felt hopeless; she had no way to break free and get to Zane.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGES]
All the while, Zane stood his ground. He calmly faced the immense pressure rolling off Adrian. Any normal disciple would have been crushed into the ground by now, but Zane stood tall, refusing to show any sign of weakness.
He realized this pressure was far beyond simple Life Energy. It felt even more potent than the Vitality of a Nature Spirit.
"He's only releasing a fraction of his power, and I can barely withstand it," Zane thought. "He might be even stronger than the Nature Spirits."
Until this moment, Zane had no idea that Adrian was a cultivator from the Soul Realm.
[SFX: ENERGY PRESSURE INTENSIFYING]
"Do you have any idea how vast the gap between us is?" Adrian taunted, his voice dripping with condescension. "I could snuff you out with a flick of my finger. Rescuing Elara from Infernal Peak is a monumental challenge even for me. What hope could you possibly have? You wouldn't even make it to the summit. The fact that you're so self-righteous about it all is just... laughable."
[SFX: MAGICAL FLOWERS BLOOMING AND WITHERING]
As he spoke, several Ghost Orchids bloomed and withered at his feet in a silent, endless cycle.
