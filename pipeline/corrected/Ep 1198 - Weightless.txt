Ep 1198 - Weightless

Word count: 998

[MUSIC: WONDER AND DISCOVERY]
Livingston’s eyes locked onto the sword blank in the furnace, and he couldn’t look away. It was a piece of Star Essence Iron, not yet fully refined.
[SFX: SHIMMERING METALLIC RESONANCE]
After years of absorbing the furnace’s heat, it shimmered with a constant, rainbow-colored light.
Weaponsmithing was a ridiculously complex art. The higher the grade of weapon you were trying to make, the more intricate every single step became, but no step was more critical than smelting the metal itself.
You find all sorts of unbelievable materials out in the universe, and Star Essence Iron was one of the craziest. It came from a star called the Dark Star—a tiny, lightless thing that was almost impossible to spot. The thing about anything from the Dark Star was that it was absurdly heavy. A piece no bigger than your fingertip could weigh as much as a mountain range.
And it was practically indestructible.
Livingston did the math out loud. A fingertip-sized piece weighed as much as a mountain range. This blank was four feet long. He stared at it. “Tens of millions of mountains,” he said. “Maybe more. Whoever could actually lift that thing would have the strength to pick up the universe itself.”
The real problem wasn’t finding it, but figuring out what to do with it once you had it. It was just too heavy. Legend has it some master once managed to pry a piece off the Dark Star’s surface, only to have it slip from his grasp.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[SFX: EARTH CRACKING]
It didn’t just hit the ground; it plunged straight through, leaving a tiny, bottomless hole in the world.
[SFX: DIMENSION SHATTERING]
People said trying to stash it in a spatial sanctum was just asking for trouble—its weight would instantly shatter the pocket dimension.
But people are stubborn. An impossible problem just makes them work harder. In this case, someone came up with a wild solution: they built a bracket out of a weaker material to hold the Star Essence Iron, then set that bracket on a massive chassis constructed from three hundred thousand giant logs. The whole thing was reinforced with eight million divine textures.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
[SFX: GROUND CRACKING]
And even then, the moment they placed the Star Essence Iron on it, the entire chassis sank a thousand feet into the ground under the sheer weight, carving out a huge crater.
[SFX: POWERFUL HUMMING ENERGY]
[SFX: HEAVY OBJECT LIFTING]
It was only when they activated all eight million textures at once, creating a powerful floating force, that the whole contraption was finally lifted off the ground.
And even then, just keeping it stable consumed ten thousand Sovereign Stones every single day.
Zane, Freya, and Lavender were all stunned into silence. Only Saul’s expression remained unchanged — he’d seen enough of the universe to know that things like this existed.
Zane looked back at the furnace. The Star Essence Iron was just sitting there, inside it, like an ordinary piece of metal.
“No wonder,” he said quietly.
The entire Keep hadn’t shifted so much as an inch when a group of Supreme Lords bombarded it from outside for half a day. He’d always wondered about that. Now he understood — any structure that could hold Star Essence Iron like it was nothing was built on a scale he couldn’t even begin to imagine.
[MUSIC: WEIGHTY REALIZATION]
Livingston walked a slow circle around the furnace, still taking it all in.
Then his expression shifted. The excitement faded, replaced by something more complicated.
“The problem,” he said, “is that I can’t do anything with it.”
He gestured at the blank. “It’s been sitting here for countless years. Something this heavy — I can’t process it, I can’t work it, I can’t even move it.” He looked at Zane. “And I’d bet you can’t lift it either.”
Zane didn’t disagree.
Saul crossed the workshop to a corner and came back with a pair of light green pincers. He placed them into the furnace and clamped them around the sword blank.
“Try,” he said to Livingston.
[SFX: METALLIC SCRAPING]
Livingston grabbed the ends of the pincers with his claws and pulled. Nothing. The blank didn’t shift a fraction of an inch — it might as well have been part of the furnace itself.
He let go and stepped back.
[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]
Saul handed the pincers to Zane. Zane took them, clamped them back around the blank, and pulled.
[SFX: STRAINING EFFORT]
He activated every Dragon Scale in his body, driving all of it into his arms. His muscles strained. His bones groaned under the effort.
The blank didn’t move.
Freya watched from nearby and shook her head slowly. It didn’t matter how strong Zane was. Nobody could lift something that weighed like this.
Zane released the pincers and handed them back to Saul with a helpless smile.
Saul returned them to the corner. When he came back, he was carrying something else.
“Made out of wood?” Zane asked, his curiosity obvious.
He knew that fire and wood didn’t mix. A pair of standard metal pincers would melt in those rainbow-colored flames, so wouldn’t these just burn up instantly?
“Why don’t you give them a try, Master?” Saul said with a smile.
Zane nodded, reaching for the pincers.
The moment they were in his hand, he froze. “These… they’re weightless.”
And they were. Everything has weight—air, a feather, a single sheet of paper. But this wasn’t like holding something light. It was like holding nothing. The pincers had absolutely zero weight in his grasp.
Saul just laughed. “Try lifting the sword blank with them, Master.”
Zane’s curiosity got the better of him.
[MUSIC: MAGICAL REVEAL]
[SFX: METAL CLAMPING]
He looked down at the pincers, then plunged them into the furnace and clamped them around the glowing sword blank. A strange sensation shot through his hands.
Suddenly, it was as if the sword blank itself had become weightless.
[SFX: METAL LIFTING FROM FLAMES]
With just a gentle lift, he raised it from the flames as easily as if it were a feather, pulling it free from the furnace.
[SFX: SHIMMERING RESONANCE]
The unrefined blank still shimmered with that rainbow light, its surface coarse and unremarkable. But there was no question—this was the raw material for an incredible, Divine-Class weapon.
