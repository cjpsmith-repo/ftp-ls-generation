Ep 1111 - The Hall of Blades

Word count: 1090

[SFX: CELESTIAL WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]
For tens of thousands of years, Landry and Damian had clung to the hope that some miracle would come and free them from their suffering. It wasn’t an easy wait. That kind of time would break anyone’s spirit, no matter how determined they were at the start. After so many millennia, they had even forgotten what kind of miracle they were waiting for.
Then the sword clouds arrived.
[SFX: SWORD ENERGY PULSE]
It was that miracle, combined with the sheer agony of waiting for so long, that finally unlocked their understanding of the Gods-Intimidating Strike—about thirty percent of it, anyway. But that alone wasn’t enough to guarantee them a brilliant future. 
Their age and lack of natural talent had put a hard ceiling on what they could achieve. They’d wasted thousands of years meditating by a giant rock, completely neglecting to build up their Inner Worlds or strengthen their foundations.
Still, what they’d learned was enough to make a name for themselves in some lesser sacred place. Their futures weren’t half bad, all things considered. The younger of the two, Damian, would go on to become an elder in a major sect. The other, Landry, would become a master swordsman, eventually passing on his understanding of the Gods-Intimidating Strike to the next generation.
[AMBIENT: QUIET INDOOR SPACE]
After Passing the Torch, most of the gathered warriors scattered — some heading out to train, others returning home to settle unfinished business. 
Layla stayed. Lincoln had won his freedom, but crossing worlds took time; it would be a while before he found his way back to the Mist Palace. In the meantime, she lived across the hall from Zane, quietly happy, waiting. If his performance at Passing the Torch still stung a little, she had one consolation: he had lost to her brother.
Layla tilted her head, a sly smile playing on her lips as she looked at Zane. “So, how much did you pick up?”
Zane just shook his head. “Honestly? Not much.”
Layla sighed. “I think I got about fifteen percent of it. Unfortunately, this kind of technique doesn’t really fit my style, which means it’s going to be a pain for me to master.” She said it humbly, but you could hear the pride in her voice. Most warriors only grasp five or ten percent of the skill; Layla had probably learned more than anyone else there.
Even so, she was no match for Zane.
Because despite what he’d just said, Zane had almost completely mastered it.
He’d already absorbed the white sword shadows and honed his own sword intent long before the clouds had even appeared. The Gods-Intimidating Strike felt like it had been made just for him. 
But mastering a technique and wielding it at full power were two different things. Supreme Lord Sword had spent countless ages refining this strike. The gap between their cultivations was simply too vast to put into words. Zane could use it — just not perfectly. Not yet. Still, another trump card was another trump card.
[AMBIENT: DISTANT MURMURING CROWDS]
News of the Gods-Intimidating Strike was already spreading across the Alliance. Warriors who had once waited at the boulder — some for years, some for decades — and eventually given up were hearing the story now for the first time. Some had left on their own. Others had been driven away. All of them wept.
[AMBIENT: QUIET HALL]
But it wasn’t just about absorbing those shadows. A few years ago, back when he was still an Adept, Zane had learned the most valuable lesson of his life: the greatest truths are always the simplest. It was a shame there was no one in the Mist Palace to test this new skill on.
That night, Zane went looking for somewhere he could. After asking around, he found his way to a large hall deep within the Mist Palace. A plaque hung at the entrance: Hall of Blades.
Inside, a dreamlike training space waited — not a place for combat, but for practice. He stepped in, and the hall dissolved around him.
[SFX: SPATIAL SHIFT HUM]
[AMBIENT: VAST OPEN SKY WITH WIND]
What replaced it was staggering. A boundless mountain range stretched in every direction, its peaks lost in the clouds above, its roots invisible below. Gold and silver light pulsed across the stone. It couldn’t have been real — something this vast could only exist inside a constructed space, built from divine runes rather than earth and rock.
But it felt real enough.
[SFX: WIND ACROSS STONE]
And it was marked. Sword marks. Saber marks. Spear marks. Arrow marks. Fist marks. Generations of Mist Palace warriors had come here to test their limits, and the mountain remembered every one of them.
Zane smiled. He’d come to the right place.
Wasting no time, Zane unsheathed his sword, Thundergust, and pointed its tip toward the enormous mountain range.
That simple movement changed everything. His entire aura shifted, becoming so sharp it felt like his focus alone could shatter stone and split the mountains in two.
[SFX: BLADE HUMMING]
*The Gods-Intimidating Strike has no buildup,* he thought. *The instant my hand moves, the sword has to swing. No hesitation. No emotion. This mindset… It’s already touching the divine.*
[MUSIC_END: RISING TENSION]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CONTEMPLATIVE]
According to legend, the only way to become a true god was to forget all emotion. Supreme Lord Sword had done just that, casting aside all his feelings to create this single, devastating strike.
The concept was incredible. 
[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]
For a split second, Zane felt like he had it—and then, just as quickly, it was gone.
That was normal, though. Epiphanies were like that, coming and going in a flash. Insights are slippery, and warriors have to work to recapture them again and again. It was fine. For now, he just planned to practice the motion. He was sure the insight would come back to him.
Zane cast aside all hesitation.
[MUSIC: BUILDING POWER]
[SFX: SWORD FLARE IGNITING]
His sword intent flared, and a faint light shot out from Thundergust. The intent itself was uncontrollable, wild, but that was the point. It wasn’t supposed to be controlled, and it didn’t need to be. 
[SFX: POWERFUL THRUST WHOOSH]
All Zane could do was trust his own understanding and thrust the sword toward the mountains.
He immediately felt the intent change. 
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
It was like it had a mind of its own, forging its own path forward with the force of his attack.
