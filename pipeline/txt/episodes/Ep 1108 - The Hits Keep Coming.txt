Ep 1108 - The Hits Keep Coming

Word count: 1148

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
A voidstorm appearing at the exact moment Yolande attacked? That couldn’t have been a coincidence. The problem was, no one could explain how a force like that had formed out of thin air, much less why it had deflected her strike.
The other warriors were so fixated on Zane that they hadn’t even noticed. It was just one more puzzle on top of a growing pile. First, how was Zane dodging the Heavenly Tribulation? Not a single bolt of lightning had so much as grazed him. Then, the leader of the Mist Palace had attacked, and a freak voidstorm had materialized to protect him. They were completely stumped, trying to piece it all together.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
While the warriors muttered in confusion, Layla stood off to the side, quiet and thoughtful. She was working through a puzzle of her own. Her foot tapped the ground, sending tiny cracks spiderwebbing across the dirt. With a subtle flick of her finger, a small stone levitated from the ground and into her hand. She then hurled it straight at Zane.
The stone wasn’t much, but it flew with incredible speed, closing the distance in seconds.
[SFX: STONE WHISTLING THROUGH AIR]
[SFX: THUNDER CRASH]
[SFX: STONE SHATTERING]
Just as it was about to hit him, another bolt of lightning crashed down from the sky, shattering the stone into dust.
[MUSIC: LIGHT MYSTICAL THEME]
From where she stood, a faint smile touched Yolande’s lips. “It seems Zane’s luck is off the charts. He’s practically invincible,” she remarked.
The Lord of the Sunset World managed a helpless smile of his own. All these unexplainable coincidences… Zane must have absorbed the power of those Luck-Devouring Snakes. It was the only explanation that made any sense.
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL ATMOSPHERE]
In the Upper World, there were ways to temporarily alter one’s luck, but the effects were usually minuscule—more like good-luck charms than anything real. Secret sects had their “prayers for fortune,” and certain Divine-Class weapons supposedly increased the wielder’s luck, but he and Yolande had always seen them as psychological crutches. Any real change was too subtle to even measure.
But this defied all logic. Zane’s luck had fundamentally changed after absorbing those snakes. With this much fortune on his side, it was as if God himself had decided to intervene.
The proof was undeniable. The chance of a voidstorm appearing on Willow Island was nearly zero—maybe one in a hundred trillion. The odds of it happening at the exact moment Yolande attacked were even smaller. And yet, the Lord of the Sunset World had seen both happen with his own eyes.
“It’s a shame he’s just using it for this,” he said, shaking his head with a touch of regret. If only Zane had saved one or two of those Luck-Devouring Snakes, they’d be far more useful to him down the line.
Whether the Heavenly Tribulation was destined to appear or Zane’s incredible luck was just calling it down, one thing was certain: it wasn’t over.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS THUNDER]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
An hour later, the sixth tribulation finally broke apart and faded. The lotus above Zane’s head spun lazily, its shadow thinning—and then, just as it was about to vanish entirely, it stopped fading.
*Could my luck push it the rest of the way?* Zane wondered, watching the last unopened petal. For a second, hope flickered.
But the lotus held steady, with one petal stubbornly closed. Whatever his luck could do, it apparently couldn’t touch this. He let the thought go.
The man in the cotton robes, who’d been about to dispel the seal yet again, froze with his hand half-raised. He hadn’t expected this either.
“Transcending tribulations back-to-back?” someone in the crowd murmured.
“Nothing strange about it, really,” another said. “They don’t exactly wait politely in line.”
“I saw someone do this once,” a third voice added, lower. “Guy had suppressed his cultivation for way too long—broke through to Life-and-Death Realm and got hit with two tribulations stacked on top of each other. Wasn’t ready for it. The lightning got him. Didn’t survive.”
A short silence followed.
“...This Zane won’t die, will he?”
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
As the shadow of the lotus flower reappeared in the sky, the seventh Small Heavenly Tribulation began to descend.
Zane sighed, a wry smile on his face. He couldn’t believe this was happening. At this point, he wasn’t sure if his good fortune was a blessing or a curse.
[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]
Many of the warriors could only stare at the floral shadow, completely speechless. Seeing someone face two tribulations back-to-back was rare but not unheard of. Three in a row, though, was something else entirely.
The seventh tribulation came down hard—genuinely hard, the kind of power that would’ve tested a warrior on their ninth or tenth tribulation. But with Zane’s luck running this wild, even attacks this strong seemed to lose their bearings around him, glancing off without leaving a mark.
And that, strangely, didn’t sit right with Zane.
A heavenly tribulation wasn’t just punishment. It was supposed to be a forge—pressure that reshaped a warrior layer by layer as they climbed through the Life-and-Death Realm. If the tribulation couldn’t even land, was it doing anything at all? Was he even getting the part of this that was supposed to matter?
[SFX: LIGHTNING STRIKE]
He didn’t have long to sit with the question. A whip of purple lightning—the kind that looked like it belonged to something from hell itself—came down and caught him flat-footed, dropping him to the ground before he even registered it.
Zane braced himself. 
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
He flipped back up immediately, already steady again. If these tribulations kept stacking up, he’d take them as they came—luck or no luck, he’d never doubted he could handle a seventh-tribulation hit on raw strength alone. 
Another hour passed.
[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]
“Four Lesser Celestial Tribulations in a row?”
“After this one, he’ll be at the ninth level of the Life-and-Death Realm!”
“Is he going to break straight through to the Soul Sea Realm at this rate?”
“Wait, didn’t he take the Tenth Tribulation Introducing Pill? Could that be what’s causing all this?”
“It just never stops!”
[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ENERGY]
[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]
Zane looked up at the terrifying power gathering in the sky, not knowing what to feel. They were right—it felt endless. A huge part of him was eager to get stronger, to see what he’d be capable of after forging a Soul Sea with his Chaos Energy. But he never, ever expected it to happen this fast.
[SFX: MYSTICAL CHIME]
Another hour later, Zane was just staring blankly at the lotus shadow above him. The flower had bloomed thirty-five leaves—nine on the bottom layer, twenty-six on the top. Only one last leaf remained closed.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
[SFX: DISTANT THUNDER]
The Tenth Heavenly Tribulation was on its way.
