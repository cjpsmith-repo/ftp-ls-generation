Ep 175 - No Laughing Matter
Word count: 1165
[AMBIENT: TENSE COURTROOM]
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Zane’s gaze swept over Saint Viana and the assembled members of House Sterling before settling on Saint Augustus, his eyes unflinching despite the tension crackling through the chamber.
“Saint, let me ask you something,” he said, voice carrying clearly across the hushed hall. “If someone tried to kill you, right here, right now\... what would you do?”
Saint Augustus didn’t hesitate, his weathered face hardening with conviction. “If they came at me without cause? I’d kill them first,” he replied, fingers unconsciously tightening around the hilt of his ceremonial blade.
“Exactly,” Zane nodded slowly. “You get them before they get you. First law of survival.” He paused, letting the weight of his words sink in before continuing. “So tell me... do you know where the sword I used to kill Felix came from?”
Saint Augustus shook his head, brow furrowing. “I don’t.”
Of course, he didn’t. The realization dawned on Zane that the Saint hadn’t witnessed any of it---he was judging based solely on aftermath and hearsay. Even Saint Viana had arrived too late to see what really went down. Only Zane and Murry knew the whole story.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
“Then I’ll tell you the truth, Saint,” Zane said, his voice ringing with conviction. “The sword I used to kill Felix? It was his Vitality Greatsword. He came at me with it, so I took it from him and defended myself. So I ask you, when he was trying to run me through, should I have just stood there and let him?”
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
Doubt flickered across Saint Augustus’s face. The crowd behind them stirred, whispers rippling through the gathered onlookers. Some leaned forward with widened eyes, while others exchanged skeptical glances. The guards at the doorway shifted uncomfortably, watching their leader’s reaction with apprehension.
[MUSIC: ACCUSATORY THEME]
“Saint Augustus, if I may interject,” Angelo stepped forward, his voice dripping with disdain. “Felix was employing House Sterling’s Tier Four Cultivation Method: the ’Sevenfold Phantom Blade’---a technique that allows its wielder to manifest their Vitality as seven distinct blades.” He cast a contemptuous glance at Zane, nostrils flaring. “This\... boy\... claims he simply grabbed one of these manifestations. It’s utterly impossible. No cultivator in the history of our empire has ever snatched another person’s Vitality Avatar out of thin air. He’s fabricating this absurd tale to escape justice!”
“Grab a Vitality Avatar?” Saint Augustus repeated, dumbfounded. The other Saints exchanged bewildered glances. Centuries of collective knowledge among them, yet none had ever heard or even thought of such a possibility.
[MUSIC: REVELATION THEME]
A disappointed shrug rippled through Zane. He almost couldn’t believe it. The ten great Saints of the Skyreach Sect\... and they were this clueless? They didn’t even recognize a technique from their own Celestial Library---the Titan Strike.
“Saint Augustus, this has gone on long enough,” Saint Viana cut in, his voice like ice. “The boy is clearly lying. He murdered Felix. The sentence is execution.”
“Words won’t convince you, Saint Viana,” Zane said, his calm a stark contrast to his fury. “So how about I give you a demonstration?”
Viana's eyes narrowed. Then, slowly, he turned to Murry.
"Murry. Release a Vitality construct. Let him try."
[SFX: TENSE SILENCE]
Murry's smile didn't waver, but something behind his eyes went very still. He knew exactly what was about to happen. He'd watched Zane do this to seven of Felix's swords at once.
He had no choice. He uncapped his brush pen, summoned his Vitality, and wrote a single character in the air. It hung there, glowing, drifting toward Zane.
[SFX: BRUSH PEN WRITING]
Zane raised one finger. A thread of Corrupt Vitality shot out and touched the character.
[SFX: VITALITY BEING ABSORBED]
The Saints watched as every trace of Murry's Vitality drained out of the construct in seconds. Then Zane flicked his wrist, and the empty shell of the character began to orbit slowly around him like an obedient ghost.
[SFX: SHOCKED GASPS]
The hall went silent.
"This kid is telling the truth."
"What cultivation method does he practice? We have to know."
Viana could see the boy was serious, but the claim was absurd. Absorb another’s Vitality? He had never seen or heard of such a technique. Of course, they all knew of the Titan Strike; they'd been present when the single, legendary scroll was sealed in the Celestial Library. But it was a Tier Five Cultivation Method. As far as anyone knew, no one in history had ever mastered it.
“You’ve got to be kidding me,” one of the Saints muttered.
[SFX: ECHOING LAUGHTER]
Zane’s sudden, thunderous laughter reverberated through the hall. It rose from deep within him, full of a strange, wild confidence. To Saint Augustus’ ears, the sound grated like metal on stone. The fuming members of House Sterling exchanged glances of disbelief, their faces contorting with indignation at this final insult. What sane person laughs in the face of imminent execution?
Viana’s fingers tightened around his staff as he watched the boy, wondering if fear had finally broken his mind.
[SFX: VIOLENT SHOCKWAVE]
“Shut UP!” Augustus roared, his voice cracking with rage. The shout wasn’t just air; it was pure Vitality, a violent, purple shockwave that slammed into Zane.
[SFX: CHOKING GASP]
The laughter choked in his throat. Zane staggered, clutching his chest as pain seared through him.
He looked up, a bloody sneer twisting his lips. “So much for the great reputation of the Skyreach Sect. Is this how its Saints conduct themselves? I may be weaker than all of you, but at least I’m not shameless. You all stand there looking so dignified, and you can’t even tell right from wrong.”
[SFX: FURIOUS BELLOW]
“One more disrespectful word out of you, and I will kill you where you stand!” Augustus bellowed, his fury shaking the very air. How dare this whelp speak to him like that?
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
But Zane just laughed again, the sound harsh and ragged this time. “Go ahead. Now or later\... it makes no difference. Your threats are empty.” As he spoke, tendrils of dark purple aura---his Corrupt Vitality---began to bleed from him, gradually enveloping his body in a sinister glow.
The energy pulsed with his heartbeat, reflecting the strange calm that had settled over him. Death had been stalking him since his father’s murder, and he’d outrun it countless times.
If he was going to die today, they could be damn sure that he’d go down swinging.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
“Then I’ll grant your wish!” Saint Augustus roared, slamming a fist on the table.
[AMBIENT: CRUSHING PRESSURE]
Vitality exploded from his body, a crushing, limitless pressure that filled the hall. Against that kind of power, Zane was nothing. There was no defense, no escape.
“Kill him! Kill the arrogant fool!”
“He’s a menace! Leaving him alive is a mistake!”
The members of House Sterling were on their feet, screaming for blood. Angelo was practically vibrating with the desire to strike the blow himself, but he settled for a front-row seat to Zane’s execution. Saint Augustus would handle it.
[SFX: DRAMATIC PAUSE]
But just as Saint Augustus raised his hand to unleash the final, fatal blow, a lazy voice drifted in from outside the Hall of Judgment.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ENTRANCE]
“Well now... holding a major trial and you didn’t think to invite me?”
