Ep 1023 - Questions and Answers

Word count: 1010

[AMBIENT: TENSE MINE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL]

If Rylan had known Zane was going to act like a total moron, he never would have bothered showing up tonight.

His plan was simple: slap a slave seal on Zane and make him a subordinate. If Zane refused, Rylan would kill him. He never wasted useful people, which was one of the reasons he’d grown so powerful in the mine.

But to everyone’s shock, Zane was completely ignoring the danger, even talking to Rylan like he didn’t have a clue who he was.

“This is your last warning. Get out of my way,” Rylan snarled, his gaze turning to ice. Getting played by some low-level warrior in front of this crowd was humiliating.

Zane showed no fear. He just stared back and asked, “Tell me. What’s the point of becoming powerful?”

[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]

[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]

A flash of killing intent crossed Rylan’s eyes. Since Zane wasn’t backing down, Rylan figured he’d teach him a lesson—to hell with the rules and the onlookers.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

His hand clamped down on Zane’s shoulder like a hawk’s talon as the Vitality in his body surged. It might have looked like a casual grab, but he was actually channeling almost eighty percent of his strength.

Of course, no one else knew that. To them, it looked like Rylan was putting everything he had into it. They figured Zane’s bones would turn to dust in about three seconds.

But to their amazement, Zane stood perfectly still and repeated his question.

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY RELEASE]

Furious, Rylan unleashed his power. But Zane’s shoulder just swayed slightly, releasing a counter-force so strong that Rylan couldn’t even keep his footing.

[SFX: BODY IMPACT]

[SFX: GROUND SCRAPING]

Slammed by Zane’s power, a shocked Rylan was sent flying backward. His feet dug two deep grooves in the ground as he skidded for a hundred feet before his body flipped over. He had to smash his fist into the ground behind him just to stop himself.

The onlookers had watched the whole thing. Zane barely moved his shoulder. Yet the force had passed through Rylan’s body three times — first dissipating on contact, then again as Rylan skidded back, and finally into the ground when Rylan’s own fist couldn’t contain it. Even weakened three times over, it had buckled the earth for a thousand feet.

Rylan’s eyes went wide. He finally understood why the three slave seals had vanished at the same moment. This kid’s power was immeasurable.

Rylan had put a terrifying amount of force into that punch. If he hadn’t, the sheer pressure from Zane’s counter would have shattered his bones, one by one.

[SFX: MASSIVE GROUND IMPACT]

[SFX: EARTH RUMBLING]

[AMBIENT: DUST AND DEBRIS]

When Rylan’s fist hit the ground, the earth buckled. A wave of dirt rolled out, forming a thousand-foot crater. Several warriors meditating nearby were sucked in and thrown into the air.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Like a ghost, Zane passed through the space between them and reappeared directly in front of Rylan. Rylan’s Vitality surged instantly — full alert, ready to strike. No amount of face was worth his life.

Then Zane opened his mouth. “Tell me. What’s the point of becoming powerful?”

The expression on Rylan’s face was indescribable.

It seemed like if he didn’t answer, he wasn’t going anywhere. Rylan, the most feared man in the mine, ground his teeth and gave in.

“Becoming stronger means you live longer,” he said flatly.

“And what’s the point of living longer?” Zane asked.

“To become stronger,” Rylan said.

“And what’s the point of that?”

Rylan’s mouth fell open. Around them, the watching ascenders were turning red in the face, shoulders shaking, desperately holding it together. This was Rylan. If it had been anyone else, they’d have been on the ground laughing.

Finally, Rylan snapped. “Becoming stronger is for enjoyment!” he roared. “To dominate this world as you please! To dictate everything, to hold the life and death of all living things in your hands!”

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE AMBIENT]

Rylan’s roar hung in the air. Zane went still.

“Dictate everything…” he murmured.

He wasn’t tired of power. He was tired of this — the killing, the hierarchy, the same law grinding on forever. But if he could truly dictate everything, he wouldn’t have to accept it. He could change it. Change the law itself. Change the universe.

The light in his eyes came back, blazing.

“With my current strength, I don’t have the qualifications to be tired. I still have too many things to accomplish.”

He’d used Rylan — of all people — to find his way back. The answer Rylan had roared out in frustration wasn’t even the right one. It didn’t matter. It had pointed him in the right direction.

To a warrior, a crisis of faith was one of the most dangerous things there was. Some never found their way out. Some became hermits. Some went mad. A few, the moment they found their answer, ended their own lives.

Zane had walked out of it in a single night.

[MUSIC: GENTLE PIANO]

But just then, with a new light in his eyes, Zane smiled at Rylan and said the last thing he expected to hear: “Thank you.”

[SFX: CAPE SWOOSH]

[AMBIENT: GATHERING PLACE MURMURS]

Rylan frowned. He was done talking to this guy. With a flick of his cape, he stormed off to the other side of the gathering place.

He decided Zane wasn’t insane after all—he was just toying with him, like a cat with a mouse. The thought made him even angrier.

[AMBIENT: QUIET QUARTERS]

[SFX: ARMOR CLANKING]

When he got back to his quarters, he found two armored warriors waiting at his door.

The moment Rylan saw the two warriors from the Soulbound Sect, a smile spread across his face. The only reason he could act so high and mighty in this mine was because of their support.

“Hawthorne and Clark, what brings you two here? Running low on Sovereign Stones?” Rylan chuckled, his tone becoming instantly polite.

After taking control of the mines, he had given these two a steady supply of Sovereign Stones. The three of them knew the score. It was a simple deal: they looked the other way, and he paid them for the privilege.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Clark spoke, his voice as cold as iron. “We don’t want your stones today, Rylan. But I’m warning you: stay away from Zane.”
