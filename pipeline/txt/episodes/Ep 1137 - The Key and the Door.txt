Ep 1137 - The Key and the Door

Word count: 1193

To the Saint Lords from the most powerful sacred places, Lincoln and his team were nothing more than a key. And once the door was unlocked, a key was useless. Crushing them would be as easy as stepping on a bug.
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Abner could feel the terrifying power radiating from the crimson light coalescing in the ogre’s hand. He knew, with a certainty that turned his blood to ice, that if a single wisp of it touched him, he’d be turned to dust. The color drained from his face. Still reeling from the deaths of his six companions, the reality of his situation finally crashed down on him: he had picked a fight with people he should have never, ever crossed.
Worse, they were ogres. To their kind, people like him were insects, meant to be crushed on a whim.
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST WHOOSH]
As the bloody light shot toward him, Abner didn’t even have the will to dodge. What was the point? A World Lord was two entire realms of power above him. He stood no chance.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIELD MATERIALIZING]
But just as the light was about to hit, the Saint Lord from the Magnolia Sanctum reached out, and a pale blue screen of light materialized in front of Abner. “Yosef,” the Saint Lord said, his voice flat and cold. “We had an agreement. The ones who opened the Underground Palace were not to be harmed, regardless of their race.”
The crimson glow in the ogre Saint Lord’s hand slowly faded. He grunted, a noise of pure contempt. Killing an insect wasn’t worth the trouble anyway. 
[SFX: ENERGY DISSIPATING]
The human Saint Lord—the one from Magnolia Sanctum—glanced at Abner. Seeing a fellow human, he offered a piece of advice, his tone devoid of warmth. 
“You chose to enter this palace; you should have been prepared to pay the price. Life-or-death trials are part of being a warrior. Worry about staying alive, and nothing else.”
The message was brutally simple: Abner had led his friends here, and he had to be ready for them to die. Blaming someone else for not swooping in to save him was childish. It was his own weakness that was the problem.
Abner knew he was right, but the truth was a bitter pill he couldn’t swallow.
Then the Saint Lord turned, addressing the fighters his sanctum had sent through. His voice was measured but carried weight. “This opportunity comes once in a hundred thousand years. The last time these doors opened, I was still a child. Don’t waste it.”
A chorus of acknowledgment rose from the assembled warriors. They were ready.
[SFX: HEAVY LANDING]
Abner looked up just in time to see Lincoln, Zane, and Freya leaping down from the Hydra statue. A fresh wave of indignation washed over him. He knew Lincoln was strong, but how had Zane and Freya gotten out completely unscathed? They’d been swarmed by Cannibal Moths just a moment ago. It made no sense.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
As Zane landed, the human Saint Lord’s gaze fell on him, and a flicker of something like approval crossed his stoic features. He had seen it all clearly. The young man possessed some kind of special power, one that let him see through the illusions of the Dark Law. Surrounded by the moths, he’d managed to dodge and weave, holding out until the very end.
The Saint Lord studied him a moment longer. An independent warrior, by the look of him. If he was willing to come under the Magnolia Sanctum’s roof, with proper guidance, this one could become something formidable. He was still turning the thought over when one of his Empyrean Realm fighters stepped forward to report, and the moment passed.
Zane met the Saint Lord’s gaze briefly and gave a small, indifferent nod. The old man across from him wasn’t anything he hadn’t seen before.
Behind him, Freya quietly moved closer to his back. She’d sensed the Saint Lord’s cultivation the moment he arrived, and it was like standing at the edge of an abyss. Back in the Psychean Realm, even the Soulbound Sect’s Saint Lord had been a figure she could barely look up to. Whatever stood before her now was something else entirely — something she couldn’t even find the edges of.
Lincoln, meanwhile, stood to one side with the expression of a man watching his life’s plan dissolve. He’d worked it all out — thirty percent for Adrian, thirty percent for Zane, forty percent for himself. Clean, fair, settled. And now the entrance was flooded with three factions’ worth of fighters who hadn’t done a single thing to earn it. He’d opened the door. He couldn’t very well close it again.
Zane stepped over and put a hand briefly on his shoulder. “There’s still a chance.”
Lincoln looked at him.
“They can’t go in,” Zane said quietly. “None of the Saint Lords can enter — the palace won’t allow it. So it comes down to us against whatever geniuses they’ve brought. That’s a different contest.”
Lincoln held his gaze for a moment, then gave a slow nod. He was still unhappy. But Zane wasn’t wrong.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS CHAMBER ATMOSPHERE]
If the ogre Saint Lord hadn’t made a pact with the other two, he would have killed them all by now. To him, these warriors were just the key. With the palace open, their lives were meaningless. Seeing the ogre now flex his power, the human Saint Lord didn’t say a word to stop him. As long as Yosef didn’t actually kill anyone, he wasn’t breaking their agreement.
An invisible force slammed down on Zane, pinning him in place. His jaw tightened as the pressure mounted. 
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE THROUGH BODY]
In an instant, the power of the Dragon Scales surged through his veins, and he glared defiantly at the unseen force binding him.
The ogre Saint Lord wielded the power of a World Lord—or something close to it. Due to the Dark World’s unique history, true World Lords couldn’t exist here. So, he was what you’d call an “expectant” World Lord, possessing the raw strength but not the official title or the full scope of its abilities. The Saint Lord from the Soulbound Sect in the Psychean Realm was in the same position.
Even though the invisible pressure didn’t seem like a direct attack, it was backed by the strength of one of the three most powerful warriors in this entire supreme world. It was immense, suffocating. 
[SFX: VIOLENT STRUGGLING]
Next to Zane, Lincoln was struggling wildly, throwing his entire strength against the invisible grip, but it was no use. He couldn’t break free.
But Zane didn’t struggle. He just focused, activating the Dragon Scales one by one. 
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT RISING POWER]
As more and more power flooded his body, he fought back against the crushing force. 
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEP ON STONE]
Then, with a grunt of pure effort, he lifted his foot and took a single, heavy step toward the entrance of the Underground Palace.
The ogre Saint Lord’s condescending smirk vanished, replaced by sheer disbelief. He stared at Zane, completely baffled.
