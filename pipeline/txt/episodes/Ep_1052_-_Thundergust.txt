Ep 1052 - Thundergust

Word count: 1143

[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Zane had barely a moment to admire the Grade-One Divine Weapon in his hand before the crowd of fighters erupted, their shouts directed at the white-eyebrowed elder. They were furious.

Despite being a highly respected senior, the elder now found himself surrounded by a younger generation calling him out. He knew he was in the wrong, and he was cornered. An apology was his only way out.

“What’s all the commotion?” Zane asked, walking over with a slight smile on his face.

The white-eyebrowed elder knew that if he didn’t give them an explanation, the younger generation wouldn’t let this go. He sighed, turning grudgingly to Zane. “My apologies for my actions. It wasn’t that I wanted to keep the sword from you. I was just… curious to see how far you could go, so I was controlling the spot of light.”

“So you admit it! You were manipulating the light!”

“What a disgrace! They should rename the Hall of Arms the Hall of Deceit!”

The crowd of fighters roared their disapproval. “How can we trust you anymore!”

The elder had admitted he was wrong, but it did little to calm the furious fighters. He had no easy way out of this.

[MUSIC: CALM RESOLUTION]

Of course, Zane knew the elder had been messing with the little blue light. But in the end, he’d gotten the sword, so he decided to let it slide. “It’s fine,” he said with a dismissive wave. “Consider it a little extra workout.”

If the elder had truly wanted to stop him, Zane knew he never would have reached the sword.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

With the elder’s apology and Zane’s easy forgiveness, the fighters’ righteous anger began to cool. Their fury quickly melted into raw envy as their eyes fixed on the Grade-One Divine Weapon in Zane’s hand. It was an incredible prize, something any one of them would have killed to possess.

For most of the fighters here, even a basic Divine Weapon was the stuff of dreams.

A typical longsword ran about three feet, three inches, but the Thundergust was a full five feet long. That extra length gave its wielder a massive advantage in reach, enough to make them a terrifying force in a fight.

The blade was also wider than usual, bordering on a heavy sword, yet it was so impossibly thin that it felt surprisingly light. In a long, drawn-out battle, that would be a game-changer.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL WONDER]

At first glance, the sword looked as if it were forged in two layers. The core was a pale, ethereal blue, while the cutting edge was a brilliant golden-red. Intricate carvings covered the hilt, making the weapon look more like a priceless work of art than a tool of war.

Zane hadn’t realized he was being tested, but he appreciated that these fighters had stood up for him. He figured he owed them a good look at his prize, letting them admire it for a while before putting it away.

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Ryder watched Zane put the sword away with cold, flat eyes. The corners of his mouth pulled into a slow sneer.

“Strong weapon,” he said quietly. “Shame about the hand holding it. That sword’s going to outlast him.”

What Ryder didn’t say — what everyone in the hall already knew — was that those three weapons had been sitting in the dome for years. Warriors from both Groundstone Hall and Cloudswallow Hall had been eyeing them the whole time. Noble families had offered to buy them outright. The answer had always been no.

Now one of them was gone. And the man who’d taken it had arrived two days ago.

Whether he knew it or not, Zane had just painted a target on his back.

[AMBIENT: DISTANT HALL CHATTER]

Within two hours, word had spread through every corridor of Groundstone Hall. By the time the story reached Cloudswallow Hall, it had taken on a life of its own.

“A newcomer took the Thundergust?”

“I’ve been needing a good sword. I wonder if he’s reasonable.”

“Make him an offer. Enough Sovereign Stones, and anyone talks.”

Zane knew none of this. He’d already left the Hall of Arms.

[AMBIENT: PALACE COURTYARD]

Finding Yolande turned out to be harder than expected. The Mist Palace was vast, and she hadn’t told him where she’d be. He wandered through the buildings for a while before thinking to open the jade map she’d given him. He studied it, picked a direction, and headed toward the center of the palace.

He found a large hall. It looked right. He walked up the steps and reached for the door.

[SFX: SOFT THUD]

Something pushed him back. Not violently — just firmly, absolutely. He hadn’t even crossed the threshold.

“Huh.” He looked at the entrance with mild curiosity.

[SFX: METALLIC RING]

Zane offered a small smile, then pulled the five-foot-long Thundergust from his Storage Ring. “I got this one,” he said.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

The power that pulsed from the Thundergust was staggering. Harvey had already turned his back, about to enter the hall, but he felt the sword’s aura prickle his skin like a thousand sharp needles. The sheer force radiating from the weapon was enough to fill anyone nearby with a deep, primal awe.

Harvey was nearly as powerful as a World Lord. Only the most potent Divine Weapons could even make him flinch. A regular sword wouldn’t even register.

[SFX: FOOTSTEP HALT]

He’d already taken one step over the threshold of the Hall of True Mysteries, but he froze mid-stride. He looked back over his shoulder, his eyes locking onto the weapon in Zane’s hand.

He knew the Thundergust all too well.

Years ago, he had even wielded it himself for a time. But as second-in-command of The Mist Palace, he was only ever permitted to use it. He could never truly claim it as his own.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

He never, in a million years, imagined Zane would be the one to finally claim it. Disbelief washed over his face, stark and absolute.

Harvey wasn’t the only one left speechless. With Zane’s level of training, he should have gotten a Grade-Three Divine Weapon at best. Maybe a Low Grade-Two if he was incredibly lucky. But Harvey wasn’t thinking about any of that.

Yolande’s normally calm eyes were wide with astonishment. When the white-eyebrowed elder had contacted her through the Message Rune, she’d been focused on restoring Lavender’s soul and hadn’t pressed for details. She had assumed Zane had picked up a Low Grade-One Divine Weapon, or a standard Grade-One at best.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]

She never would have dreamed that Zane could claim the legendary Thundergust.

She could only shake her head. Then again, she thought, this was Zane. He was always full of surprises.
