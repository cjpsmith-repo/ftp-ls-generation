Ep 405 - A Hasty Exit

Word count - 1054

[AMBIENT: TENSE COURTYARD]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Emanuel planted his feet, letting the weight of his authority settle over the courtyard.  "Zane, tell us what happened on the Cloud Road. Everything. Don't leave out a single detail."

The threat remained unspoken, yet it hung in the air like smoke.

As far as Emanuel was concerned, he was being perfectly civil—generous, even. His approach was vastly different from Elder Toby's gentle methods. Toby had grown old, content to handle mundane responsibilities like scouting fresh talent despite his elder status. Emanuel, however, stood in his prime, his power at its absolute zenith. As one of Cloud Hall's pillars, it was only natural he'd look down on a mere disciple like Zane. Had the boy not managed to claim first place, Emanuel wouldn't have bothered learning his name.

He understood perfectly well that everyone craved a piece of the Cloud Road's treasures, especially relics from a sixth-grade power. Moments ago, his son Frank had breathlessly reported witnessing a Drake puppet on the road, followed by countless streaks of light converging on a single location like moths to flame.

Emanuel's jaw tightened at the memory. While he himself couldn't set foot on the Cloud Road—the restrictions were absolute—his son still could. The arched gateway opened only once every three years, and reactivating it demanded a fortune: two hundred supreme Aether crystals. An insane price, certainly, but one Emanuel was already plotting to pay. First, however, he needed intelligence. Those treasures would represent a massive boon for his own cultivation, perhaps even the breakthrough he'd been seeking for years.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]

Frank shot Zane a nasty sneer, savoring every moment of his rival's impending humiliation. "I asked you nicely, and you refused. Now my father's here." He paused, letting the threat hang in the air. "He doesn't play around."

Sir Kenneth and Toby's faces tightened with barely concealed concern, but they held their tongues. It wasn't their place to intervene—not when Emanuel Drew stood before them radiating barely restrained power. Provoking a man of his cultivation level was the last thing they wanted, especially over a matter that didn't directly concern Cloud Hall's interests. The other sect leaders remained equally silent, their expressions carefully neutral. Zane wasn't one of their own disciples, after all. Besides, they were just as desperate to know what treasures he'd discovered on the Cloud Road.

[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]

Finn's shoulders slumped as he exchanged a look of despair with Patrick. Both young warriors sighed, the sound carrying the weight of defeat. They figured it was over. Against someone like Emanuel Drew, Zane had no choice but to spill everything he knew.

But to everyone's shock, Zane simply blinked. He met Emanuel's hard gaze without flinching, his posture relaxed yet somehow defiant. When he spoke, his voice carried neither fear nor deference. "Master Drew, I was lucky to make it back alive from the Cloud Road." He paused, tilting his head slightly. "Why exactly do I owe you an explanation? Give me one good reason."

The square fell into stunned silence.

Emanuel froze. He'd expected immediate compliance—perhaps some nervous stammering or a desperate attempt at negotiation. He never, ever expected to be challenged so directly, so calmly, by a mere Adept Realm cultivator barely out of his teens.

[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]

Emanuel stepped toward Zane, his aura flaring with menace, when a small, glowing rune materialized beside his ear. The delicate symbol pulsed once with faint light, its intricate lines shimmering briefly before sparking into ash.

Every sect leader present recognized the Vitality-based messaging rune—a technique reserved for urgent communications that couldn't wait.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRONE]

Emanuel's expression shifted dramatically. His eyes went distant, as if listening to a voice only he could hear. His brow furrowed deeper with each passing heartbeat, his face darkening like storm clouds gathering on the horizon.

The crowd watched in tense silence, sensing something significant was unfolding. Even Frank leaned forward slightly, trying to read his father's reaction.

When Emanuel's focus finally returned to the present, he stared at Zane with a complicated, unreadable expression. Anger still simmered there, yes—but now it mingled with something else. Frustration? Calculation? Perhaps even a grudging wariness that hadn't been present moments before.

Whatever message he'd received had clearly changed everything.

[MUSIC: DARK RESOLUTION]

The silence stretched on, thick with anticipation. Finally, Emanuel’s lip curled into a sneer. "Count your lucky stars, boy," he spat. "Frank, we're leaving."

[SFX: FLYING SOUNDS]

With that, Emanuel shot into the sky, hovered for a heartbeat, and then vanished—a blur of gray against the clouds.

Frank remained rooted to the spot, completely baffled. Anticipation had been coursing through his veins just moments ago as he'd fantasized about his father ripping Zane's soul to shreds, leaving him a drooling vegetable. The thought alone had sent shivers of exhilaration down his spine.

He'd witnessed Zane's power firsthand on the Cloud Road—the devastating light arrows, the impossible mastery of Sword Intent at such a young age. The boy represented a clear and present threat to his own future in the Cloud Hall. Nothing would have satisfied Frank more than watching this upstart be permanently removed from the picture, his potential snuffed out before it could truly bloom.

[MUSIC: RISING SUSPICION]

But for his father to simply... back down? To abandon a soul-search without explanation? What happened? Who sent that message? Who could possibly wield enough power to make his father reverse course so swiftly?

A thousand questions screamed in Frank's mind, each more urgent than the last. Yet one glance at the grim set of his father's jaw—that dangerous tightness around his mouth that Frank had learned to fear as a child—told him to keep silent. He shot Zane one final glare, pouring every ounce of his hatred and frustration into that look, before launching into the air to follow.

[SFX: FLYING SOUNDS]

[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]

In the sudden quiet that followed, only the sect leaders, Toby, and Zane remained. They stood in stunned silence, still reeling from the abrupt reversal. The tension that had gripped the square moments ago now transformed into bewildered confusion. 

Like Frank before them, they all wrestled with the same burning question: who in the world had sent that message, and what kind of authority did they hold to make Emanuel back down so easily?
