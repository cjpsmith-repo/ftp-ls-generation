Ep 1211 - Wolves and Pigs

Word count: 1143

[MUSIC: TENSE PULSATING]
[AMBIENT: COMBAT ARENA]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
Thane’s bloodshot eyes flared as he activated his Bronze Blood.
The effect was instantaneous.Veins bulged across his arms, thick as snakes and pulsing with power. 
He felt strong enough to move mountains—back in the Lower World, anyway. But not here. Here, it wasn’t nearly enough to wrench the ogre blood axes from Zane’s hands.
*Just a little more,* Thane thought, straining against the deadlock. *He’s gotta be at his limit!*
When pulling didn’t work, he had to change tactics.
[SFX: GUTTURAL ROAR]
With a guttural roar, Thane threw his entire weight forward, shoving the axes back toward Zane. The sudden shift should have caught the guy off guard, maybe even made him drop the weapons.
It didn’t. Whether he pushed or pulled, nothing he did could budge Zane.
“Hey, dude,” Zane said, a lazy smirk spreading across his face.
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
Thane scowled, caught completely off guard as a terrifying amount of force suddenly surged from Zane’s hands into the axes.
[SFX: WEAPON CRACK]
The weapons started to split right down the middle. 
[SFX: WEAPONS SHATTERING]
[SFX: SPLINTERS SCATTERING]
The force was so overwhelming that the blades didn’t just break; they disintegrated, shattering into a cloud of splinters right in front of him. All Thane could do was stare in stunned despair at the two useless, splintered handles left in his grip. Those ogre blood axes were his clan’s greatest treasure.
Of course, this was the Illusion Battlefield, a place where no one really died, and no weapon was truly destroyed. Everything and everyone was just a projection. Any weapon damaged here, from a cheap stick to a priceless clan heirloom, was just a copy. The real one was perfectly safe back in the outside world. It was the only reason warriors from the Soul Sea Realm could even dream of wielding Divine-Class weapons, which were normally reserved for World Lords and Supreme Lords.
So the axes that had just shattered were fine. They were safe. So why did this feel like such a punch to the gut?
Because Zane had just crushed a Low Grade-One Divine Weapon with his bare hands.
Thane simply couldn’t wrap his head around how terrifyingly strong his opponent was. The guy in front of him couldn’t be this powerful, could he? It didn’t make any sense. 
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DREAD]
But as he stared at the splintered wood in his hands, his disbelief slowly curdled into a cold, deep-seated fear.
He looked up at Zane. *This guy is a monster,* he thought.
Zane wasn’t interested in dragging it out. Compared to the Celestial Mice he’d just fought through, Thane barely registered as a threat—and he’d never once lost to an opponent at the same level. Strength alone, even a true Spirit Supreme Realm warrior couldn’t match him.
“It’s over,” Zane said.
He reached out and touched a single finger to Thane’s forehead.
[SFX: GRISLY DISSOLVE]
Thane’s head dissolved into a fine red mist. His body folded backward and crumpled to the ground, already starting to dissolve.
[SFX: BODY DISINTEGRATING]
[SFX: RED LIGHT STREAKING AWAY]
A red streak of light shot off the battlefield, back toward the city to revive.
[SFX: POINTS RUSHING]
A flood of yellow motes broke free from where Thane’s body had been and poured straight into Zane.
On the wall, a few of the watching Ogres couldn’t hide the envy on their faces. Thane had spent hours grinding out seventy, eighty thousand points—and half of all that effort had just been handed straight to the man who killed him.
“Thirty-seven thousand four hundred,” Zane murmured, doing the math. “Add what I had before, and that puts me past forty thousand. Five hundred twenty thousandth in this world... twenty-four million in the Illusion Battlefield overall.”
It was a solid ranking by most standards. But set against the sheer number of competitors pouring in from every corner of the hundred thousand worlds, Zane only managed a tired half-smile. He still had more than twenty million Soul Sea Realm warriors ahead of him.
As Zane turned back toward the city of ogres, three more warriors charged him. He wasn’t interested. Judging by their weak auras, they had barely any Illusion Points worth taking.
[SFX: SPATIAL DISTORTION WHOOSH]
Before he could vanish, though, three or four more Ogre warriors broke from the wall and rushed him from a different direction—early Soul Sea Realm, nothing special about them. They weren’t here for rank or points. They just wanted to know how far they stood from a real fighter, to test themselves against death and walk away sharper for it.
They never even saw him move.
[SFX: RAPID STRIKES]
One after another, they were cut down and sent streaking back to the city before they could register what had happened.
Zane lingered a moment afterward, almost surprised. He’d known these warriors wouldn’t be carrying much, but he hadn’t expected so little—four of them combined had handed over a grand total of two Illusion Points. Two had given up one each. The other two had nothing at all.
He hadn’t been back long enough to recognize the pattern yet: warriors who didn’t care about points or rank, who died and revived and threw themselves back into the fight over and over without rest, chasing nothing but the fight itself—the rawest possible experience the Illusion Battlefield could offer. The stronger the opponent, the better, as far as they were concerned.
Zane didn’t have time for small fries, so he simply took a step through space and vanished before they even knew what was happening.
With him gone, the warriors on the city walls breathed a collective sigh of relief, while those on the battlefield below cautiously started moving again.
[MUSIC: PREDATORY STALKING]
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD OUTSKIRTS]
Meanwhile, Zane had already found a new target: an Ogre, nearly as strong as Thane, who was busy farming Illusion Points from regular warriors outside the city. The Ogre was cocky, moving with an unearned swagger. It was just his bad luck to run into Zane, who was very interested in that growing stash of points.
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
Just like everyone else Zane had targeted, the Ogre was quickly reduced to a red dot of light and shot back toward the city.
[SFX: DIGITAL COUNTER TICKING UP]
With that, Zane had taken down two “prize pigs,” and absorbed another thirty thousand Illusion Points, bringing his total to around seventy thousand. He was now ranked 410,000 in the Upper World and had broken into the top nineteen million overall.
His rank was climbing fast, but he was still a nobody with a long way to go.
Zane was just getting started. While most warriors wasted their time squabbling over a target for a measly fifty points, he was bagging tens of thousands with every opponent he took down.
It’s a simple system, really. The pigs get fat, and the wolves get the pigs.
Zane was a wolf, feeding on prize pigs who had collected a ton of Illusion Points just for him.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ENCOUNTER]
[SFX: DISTANT FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
He just wasn’t the only wolf on the prowl. And he was about to meet another one of his kind.
