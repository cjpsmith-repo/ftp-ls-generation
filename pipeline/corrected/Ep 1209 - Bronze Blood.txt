Ep 1209 - Bronze Blood

Word count: 1062

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS AMBIENT]
[AMBIENT: FANTASY WILDERNESS]
Zane didn’t even bother to check his ranking.
It wasn’t that he didn’t want a high rank in the Illusion Battlefield—he was definitely aiming for the top spot—but the competition had just started. Checking the leaderboard this early felt pointless.
After absorbing the Illusion Points from the fight, his gaze fell on a cave guarded by three Celestial Mice. He had no idea what was hidden inside, but his curiosity was piqued. 
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER SHIMMER]
As he started toward the entrance, however, a beam of light shimmered into existence, blocking his path.
A message flashed through his mind at the same time.
[SFX: MYSTICAL NOTIFICATION CHIME]
*To enter the Evil Wind Cave, you must pay 300,000 Illusion Points.*
Three hundred thousand. Zane let out a dry laugh. He’d collected less than four thousand. Reaching that total would take a long time.
He couldn’t enter after fighting off the mice, but he memorized the location. Whatever lay inside - guarded by three legendary beasts and requiring a fortune in points - had to be extraordinary.
With his mind made up, Zane didn’t linger. 
[SFX: WIND RUSH]
He turned and took to the air, flying back the way he came.
After about thirty minutes, the main city came back into view.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC BATTLEFIELD]
Near the gates, an Ogre in gleaming silver armor stood surrounded by a faint, bloody aura. 
[SFX: HEAVY AXES SWINGING]
[SFX: BRUTAL IMPACTS]
He was swinging a pair of massive, gore-soaked axes like a madman, carving through anyone who got close.
Zane recognized him. It was Thane, the same Ogre warrior he’d seen in the city earlier.
Thane came from a powerful and privileged background. Though not a core disciple of a top-tier sacred place, he was born into a clan so wealthy and renowned it might as well have been one. When the Illusion Battlefield opened, they had armed him with their most valuable treasures. He wasn’t just wearing the Sacred Ogre Armor; he was also wielding a pair of Low Grade-One Divine-Class axes.
He’d spent the last few hours leading a pack of Ogre warriors to hunt down fighters from other races. In that time, he alone had killed six hundred human warriors and another four hundred from other races. He’d already earned over fifty thousand Illusion Points in the first round alone, shooting him straight to the top of the leaderboard.
Now, he was slaughtering the same warriors as they revived for the second round. 
Since a warrior’s points were only cut in half on a second death, this round only netted him a little over twenty thousand more—bringing his running total to seventy thousand Illusion Points across both rounds.
Thane sought a third round of slaughter, but the warriors had learned. Staying inside the city walls cost nothing; dying again only enriched him. Even his Ogre allies abandoned him - everyone fought for their own ranking, and shared blood meant nothing here. With no willing victims, Thane was forced to hunt alone.
He didn’t have to look far. A handful of Ogre warriors—the same ones who’d fought alongside him minutes earlier—turned on him the moment he stepped outside the city, drawn by the thirty or forty thousand points a kill on him would be worth. He was strong, and his treasures made him stronger still, but three against one was three against one.
[SFX: GROWLING ROAR]
Thane’s bloodline surged—Bronze Blood, a cousin of the Ogre race’s Berserk Blood. It dulled pain and roughly doubled his strength for a short stretch, hitting harder than Berserk Blood ever could while costing his body far less. Under its pull, he turned the fight around fast.
[SFX: AXE CLEAVING IMPACT]
One axe came down in a careless instant and split an Ogre warrior clean in half, head to toe. The body dissolved into a streak of red light and shot back toward the city to revive—and every point he’d been carrying went straight to Thane.
The remaining two broke and ran, one of them peeling off for the city gates.
[SFX: METALLIC RINGING]
[SFX: AXE SPLITTING AND MULTIPLYING]
A strange mark flared to life across Thane’s blood axe. With a roar, he hurled it after them—and the axe split in two mid-flight, then split again, and again, until the sky behind the two fleeing warriors was choked with blades.
“I’ll cut you to pieces!”
The axes came down on both of them at once, shredding them where they stood.
[SFX: AXES MERGING]
A moment later, the countless blades folded back together, flowing home into Thane’s waiting hands as two axes once more.
A warrior’s Illusion Points were cut in half every time they died, and as Thane continued his massacre, the onlookers huddled on the city walls fell into a stunned silence.
[SFX: SHOCKED MURMUR]
“Those axes… I thought they were just part of the illusion,” one person muttered in disbelief.
“Please,” another scoffed. “Thane’s not even that strong on his own. He’s just relying on his clan’s Bronze Blood and those two Divine Weapons.”
“With those axes, his attacks are unstoppable,” someone else whispered. “And it looks like nothing can even scratch his armor.”
Thane’s display of power was so terrifying that not a single warrior dared to step outside the city’s protective walls.
[SFX: AXES SLAMMING METAL CHESTPLATE]
He slammed the flats of his bloody axes against his chestplate, the metal ringing loud and clear.
Fueled by the Bronze Blood, he roared at the crowd. “You idiots! Come on out so I can kill you! What good are you except for handing over your points?”
Thane was, admittedly, one of the smarter Ogres. He’d managed to convince other warriors of his race to help him gather points right when the battlefield opened. But even after racking up a fortune, his arrogance hadn’t faded. He kept taunting everyone—and not just the other races. Now, he was even turning on his own kind.
“Boone! Get out here and challenge him!” one of the Ogres yelled, shoving his friend.
“Damn it! I helped him kill all those humans, and now he’s coming after *me*?” Boone shot back, horrified.
“I guess this is how it works in the Illusion Battlefield,” a third Ogre grumbled. “Everyone’s an enemy. We were idiots for helping him in the first place.”
[SFX: AXES SLAMMING METAL CHESTPLATE]
Thane just kept smashing the axes against his chest, the deafening clang echoing across the field. His eyes were a dark, furious red, but a faint gleam flickered within them—a side effect of the Bronze Blood.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
He was still reveling in his own taunts when Zane appeared directly behind him.
