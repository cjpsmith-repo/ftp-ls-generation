Ep 887 - A World of Chaos

Word count: 1100

[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

One moment, two snow drakes were resting in their lair halfway up a mountain. 

The next, the mountain was gone, and they were plummeting through open air.

[SFX: CREATURE SHRIEK]

[SFX: WINGS BEATING]

They managed to catch themselves, wings beating frantically to gain altitude. 

As intelligent creatures, they tried to make sense of what just happened, but their minds came up blank. How could a massive mountain just… vanish?

[SFX: RUMBLING COLLAPSE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

But what they saw next was something else entirely. Across the horizon, the towering peaks in front of them were disappearing, one after another. A mountain would start to crumble, and in the next instant, it would just be gone.

The western part of the Heartlands wasn't exactly bustling with people, but it was packed tight with fifty or sixty thousand mountains. Even a Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior would need a lifetime to cross such a vast range on foot.

And right now, those mountains were being wiped from existence in a matter of seconds.

[MUSIC: APOCALYPTIC CHOIR]

This bizarre, terrifying scene wasn't just happening in the West; it was happening all across the Heartlands. Every single mountain was being annihilated.

[AMBIENT: MEETING ROOM]

[MUSIC: CALM BUSINESS ATMOSPHERE]

Miles away, on a small, thousand-foot-high mountain south of Oracle City, the three leaders of the World Merchants’ Guild were in a meeting with their investors. 

In the current landscape, the Guild was the most prominent power in the Heartlands. Not that Kieran, their leader, had any ambition to turn it into some sacred kingdom. They were a powerful organization, sure, but at the end of the day, they were merchants.

[SFX: GROUND CRACKING]

[MUSIC: SUDDEN DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Then, right in the middle of their discussion, the ground beneath them started to crumble.

Everyone froze. Before they could even process what was happening, the floor simply ceased to exist. The entire mountain was gone, wiped clean from the face of the earth.

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

[SFX: DISTANT RUMBLE]

“Are we under attack?” a Core-Forged Realm warrior shouted, his voice laced with panic. They’d all survived the last assault by the Corrupted Divine Kingdom, but the memory had left a deep, ugly scar.

“Attack? Damn it!” Hobbes, now hovering in midair, swore loudly.

Look, plenty of warriors could destroy a mountain. Hobbes and the other leaders could do it themselves. But not like this. Not instantly. This was an incredible, terrifying power, something beyond anything they could possibly imagine.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

An even louder, deeper rumble shook the very air. White Mountain, a fifteen-thousand-foot peak that dominated the Oracle City skyline, suddenly vanished.

For as long as anyone could remember, people in Oracle City could look south and see the dark, familiar silhouette of White Mountain. It stood like a giant, silent guardian.

Now, there was just empty sky.

“The… the White Mountain is gone, too!” someone stammered in disbelief.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: FEATHER EMPEROR'S ISLAND]

Back on Feather Emperor's Island, Zane stood motionless, his body still swallowed in black. But his mind was moving fast — tracking through the world's memory, counting what remained.

Five million mountains. Four million. Three million.

The black light wasn't stopping.

Two million. One million.

He had ruled out the ocean early. During the Thunder Tribulation, routing force into the Sacred Feather Sea had set off tsunamis hundreds of feet high — and that had taken three Soul Sea masters working continuously to suppress. 

The ocean of the entire world was on a different scale entirely. A tsunami of tens of thousands of feet. Every continent submerged. Every coastline gone. Ninety-nine percent of all life, wiped out. He couldn't do it.

He couldn't use the continents directly either. Any of them would shatter the moment he tried.

Five hundred thousand mountains.

He wasn't a sentimental person. He knew the world ran on strength. But there was a line between that and becoming something that fed on hundreds of millions of lives without a second thought. He didn't want to cross it. Not because he was kind. Because he didn't want to lose the last piece of himself that was still human.

Three hundred thousand. Two hundred thousand.

His jaw tightened.

One hundred thousand. Fifty thousand. Ten thousand.

He was running out of room. If the black light kept going, he would have to use the Eastern Region — the thirty-three peaks, the sect where he had trained, where disciples who couldn't even fly were still cultivating on those slopes. He thought of them. He thought of what would happen.

And then, finally, he felt it. The black light began to weaken.

[MUSIC: SOMBER PIANO]

[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND]

He had survived. But while he was still in one piece, the entire world of chaos had paid a terrible price.

Five million mountains, gone. Countless living beings—humans included—had likely been sacrificed in the process.

The black light drained from his skin, his eyes, his clothes. His body returned to normal. But his face carried no relief. Only a quiet, hollow sadness.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

“Zane survived the black light… it couldn’t kill him,” the Holy Maiden whispered, so shocked she couldn’t say anything else. 

The Soul Sea Realm masters stood in heavy silence. They all knew Zane could redirect energy—the ‘Little Monster’ from the Nine Cauldron Martial House had pulled stunts like this before. But no one knew the limits of that power. Was it possible he didn’t have any?

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

They didn’t understand the black light, not really. But they knew one thing for sure: it would have easily vaporized all of them. Even if there were a hundred times more Soul Sea Realm masters gathered here, they wouldn’t have stood a chance.

But Zane had withstood that incredible attack. What did he have to sacrifice to do it? Where did he send all that power?

Since there were no mountains on The Feather Emperor’s Island, most of them had no idea that every peak on the entire Divine Kingdom Continent had just disappeared.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

Finally, the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom couldn't stand the suspense any longer. “Zane,” he said, his voice tight. “I know you transferred that power. Where did you send it?”

Zane blinked, as if waking from a trance. “The mountains,” he replied.

“Mountains?” the Emperor pressed, leaning in. “How many?”

[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

Zane’s voice was hollow, filled with a sadness that seemed to swallow the air around him. “All of them,” he answered. “Every mountain in the entire world.”
