Ep 1205 - Eight Smoky Melodies

Word count: 1130

[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]
[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND WIND]
Compared to the other Ogres, the three encroaching on Zane were shorter, and their armor was definitely unique.
At first glance, it looked like simple black plate, but the moment it caught the light, it shimmered with an oily iridescence.
The armor was forged from what was known as Light Accumulation Iron. When activated, it could shift colors to match its surroundings. Woven with a few divine textures, it could make the wearer vanish completely—most warriors wouldn’t even sense them standing right there.
That kind of stealth might work on an ordinary Soul Sea Realm fighter, but Zane wasn’t ordinary. His perception was on another level, and he could easily pinpoint their locations no matter how well they tried to hide.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS HUNTING DRUMS]
These three had marked Zane for death the moment they saw him at the city gates. His display of power had been too much to ignore, and the second they saw him leave the city alone, they’d started their hunt. They weren’t pushovers, either. As a team, they were lethal, having once taken down a master of the Spirit Supreme Realm. Now, they were stalking Zane in silence, waiting for their moment.
[MUSIC: RISING ANTICIPATION STRINGS]
A flicker of boredom crossed Zane’s face. He had been quiet for years, but something in him had shifted. It was like a butterfly finally breaking free of its cocoon; he was ready for a fight, a baptism by fire that was long overdue.
He glanced down at the three Ogres hiding below and muttered, “Well, if you’re that eager, come on. Let’s do this.”
The three warriors shared a look, a sneer passing between them. 
[SFX: MAGICAL VANISHING WHOOSH]
A moment later, they simply vanished. Their method of concealment was incredible; it didn’t just mask their sound and aura, it rendered them completely invisible.
Zane just smiled as he watched them disappear. His original plan had been to lay low, maybe relax in the wilderness for a bit before getting into any real trouble. But if these guys were looking for a fight, who was he to deny them a little fun?
He hadn’t left the Elysian Keep in five years, and in all that time, he hadn’t had a single opponent worth his time. Lavender was a sword spirit, so sparring with her was out. And the power gap between him and Freya was just too massive; even with all the progress she’d made, she was still no match for him. He’d spent most of his time dabbling in the three attunement handbooks Gus had recommended.
[MUSIC: QUIET FOCUS]
Zane closed his eyes, and his perception spread outward, mapping the wasteland around him in his mind. Three blank spots moved steadily closer, converging on his position.
“Not slow,” he murmured. “But it’s a shame.”
[SFX: ENERGY STIRRING]
Deep in his Inner World, the World Tree sapling swayed gently, and a thread of Chaos Energy rose along it, drawn quietly into his body. A faint wisp of smoke gathered above his hands.
“Eight Smoky Melodies,” he said softly.
He didn’t particularly care for Gus, but his big brother’s eye for talent was undeniable. None of the three handbooks he’d recommended stood out on the shelves of the archive, but each had turned out to suit Zane uncannily well. It made a strange kind of sense—Gus had surpassed the Cosmic Order itself. To him, Zane might as well have been transparent.
Eight Smoky Melodies was unlike any escape technique Zane had encountered. Nearly every movement art in the universe drew its power from some Law, different paths, but always built the same way. Eight Smoky Melodies used none of that. The smoke curling above his hands was pure visualization, with no Law woven into it at all. By every rule he knew, that should have made it weak. Lesser, even. A child’s technique dressed up as something more.
But the pattern at its core—a sphere ringed with eight openings, threaded through by a single winding line—stirred something he couldn’t name. He’d felt this exact sensation only once before: during his first tribulation on the main continent, when a black light capable of unmaking heaven and earth had unspooled a thin, chaotic line through the sky, weaving and falling toward him in a pattern he could sense but never explain. The line in this technique wasn’t nearly as complex. But the feeling was the same.
Law or no Law, classification or no classification, Zane kept cultivating it anyway.
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT PERCUSSION]
Once hidden, the three Ogres held their breath and charged, striking at Zane from different directions. 
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS CHARGING]
[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]
The force of their combined techniques sent ripples through the air. 
[SFX: BLADE WHISTLING]
The warrior with the machete was especially fierce, his blade whistling as it sliced toward Zane.
But Zane was like smoke, just drifting.
His body went completely still inside—no killing intent, no thought of striking back, nothing but total surrender to the pattern he’d memorized. Even his mind had to cooperate with the technique for it to work.
[SFX: MACHETE CUTTING AIR]
The machete cut through the air, aimed directly for his back. It moved like lightning, so fast that a normal person wouldn’t have even registered the blur of steel. And yet, just as the blade came within three inches of his spine, Zane’s body seemed to flow with it.
It wasn’t that he was dodging. It was as if the force of the slash itself was pushing him forward, keeping him just out of reach.
The attack missed. 
[SFX: MACHETE SWING]
Without hesitating, the machete-wielding Ogre swung again.
[SFX: BLADE WHOOSH]
And again, the blade failed to close the gap. Zane remained exactly three inches away.
[SFX: BLADE WHOOSH]
A third slash. Still three inches.
The Ogre martial artist was stunned. But his shock didn’t slow his assault. What kind of movement technique *was* this? He’d never seen anything so bizarre. Zane’s body seemed to have no weight at all. Every time he swung, the very force of his own blade seemed to push Zane away. 
[SFX: RAPID BLADE SLASHES]
He kept slashing, but the machete just kept pushing his target farther from his grasp.
At this rate, he’d never land a hit. The distance between his blade and Zane’s back was always, *exactly*, three inches. Those three inches might as well have been an impassable chasm.
He was using the Imperial Slash, a legendary technique consisting of seven of the most powerful strikes ever developed by an Ogre Emperor, each one imbued with a devastating blade intent.
And yet, here was Zane, not even fighting back, while the legendary Imperial Slash couldn’t so much as graze the fabric of his clothes.
