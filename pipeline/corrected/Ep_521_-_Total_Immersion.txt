Ep 521 - Total Immersion
Word count: 1136
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Crafting a Sect-protecting array was a whole different league from the enchanted barrier Zane had thrown up in the arena.
For starters, a Sect-protecting array had to shield the entire Sect, and even a small sect's headquarters was way bigger than some arena.
Take the Skyreach Sect, for example—their territory was nearly the size of the Empire itself.
Cloud Hall, on the other hand, was an oddity.
For one thing, it was literally floating in the clouds.
And even though it was built on a giant floating rock, it was surprisingly compact for a fourth-grade sect.
Besides a protective shield, the array also needed some serious firepower. You couldn't just build a defensive wall with no way to punch back.
[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]
After leaving the ice palace, Cedric gave Zane the exact dimensions of Cloud Hall. Zane took to the air, getting a bird's-eye view of the whole layout before floating carefully back down.
"Any idea how many divine textures a project like this is going to eat up?" he asked the Cobalt Dragon.
"If you're planning on using a nine-star divine texture, one should do the trick," the Cobalt Dragon replied instantly.
Zane just rolled his eyes. A single nine-star divine texture could probably crack the Heartlands in half.
Besides, the Cobalt Dragon couldn't even draw a nine-star texture at his current level. And even if he could, there was no way Zane could get his hands on the required materials that fast.
[MUSIC: FOCUSED CONTEMPLATION]
"That said," the Cobalt Dragon continued, "Cloud Hall isn't exactly sprawling. By my estimate, a mix of three-star and four-star textures should do it — around four thousand nine hundred in total, with three five-star textures for the critical nodes. The real challenge is sourcing the materials."
Zane nodded and got to work. He found a table, sat down, and started listing everything out, brush moving steadily across the page.
[SFX: BRUSH ON PAPER]
The list kept growing. Standing at Zane's shoulder, Cedric watched the columns of materials multiply and felt his scalp go numb.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Each item on its own was expensive. Together, they were staggering. Getting all of it in a short window would mean paying double — maybe triple — the going rate.
When Zane finally set the brush down, Cedric picked up the list and stared at it for a long moment without moving.
"Something wrong?" Zane asked.
"This list..." Cedric muttered. "Do you have any idea what this is going to cost?" He did a rough mental calculation and looked up. "If we buy everything here, we're talking about nearly half of Cloud Hall's entire reserves."
Zane smiled. "Half the reserves, or the destruction of the sect. Which would you prefer?"
Cedric opened his mouth, then closed it again. "...Understood."
"Good. Get moving — sooner the better. High-price auction, private purchase, whatever it takes. If it comes to it, we'll strong-arm someone."
[MUSIC: EXCITED PREPARATION]
[SFX: HURRIED FOOTSTEPS]
Cedric distributed the list among Cloud Hall's deacons, and the operation kicked into gear. It turned out that roughly a third of the materials were already sitting in Cloud Hall's own stores. Another third were scattered across Cloud Sea City and the five major sects under Cloud Hall's banner — once Cedric passed the order down, shipments began arriving in a steady stream.
The final third had to be sourced directly from the World Merchants' Guild, which Cedric handled personally.
With each new delivery, Zane would check another item off the list with a thick brushstroke. Slowly but surely, the long list of materials shrank.
By the third day, the final item was crossed off. They had everything.
[AMBIENT: WIND THROUGH CLOUDS]
[MUSIC: FOCUSED CONTEMPLATION]
With all the materials gathered, Zane took over Cedric's private workshop. Like Nate and his Runic Tower, runic masters needed a specific environment to work their craft. Cedric's study was perched on the very edge of Cloud Hall. From the wide-open hall, you could look out on a rolling sea of clouds—the perfect vista for drawing runes.
A small crowd had gathered in the hall. A few were Cedric's own people, tasked with managing the materials and mixing the ink. Worried he might be shorthanded, Cedric had even called in a couple of other stewards to help.
[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]
The moment the first batch of ink was mixed to Zane's exact specifications, he unrolled a sheet of paper and gripped his brush.
Someone handed him the first container.
[SFX: BRUSH ON PAPER]
[MUSIC: INTENSE CONCENTRATION]
He dipped the brush in, his focus sharpening, and began to draw.
According to the Cobalt Dragon's blueprint, the array would require a staggering four thousand, nine hundred and one divine textures. The three-star texture Zane was drawing now was just one tiny piece of a massive puzzle.
[SFX: MAGICAL IGNITION]
[AMBIENT: WORKSHOP ATMOSPHERE]
The instant his runic quill touched the paper, his soul force ignited the ingredients in the ink.
[SFX: FIRE WHOOSH]
A blaze of red light flared to life, the fierce energy of a fire-aspected, offensive rune.
The tip of the quill danced across the paper like a stream of liquid fire, its movements steady and sure as it painted the design. The marks bloomed across the page like blossoming flames.
Watching Zane wield his Perfect Brushwork with such casual mastery, Cedric was utterly spellbound.
[MUSIC: QUIET WONDER]
Among Cloud Hall's disciples, Cedric felt the most ecstatic. Though considered a Celestial-level martial talent, his lifelong obsession with runes had stalled him in the Inner-Core Realm. If forced to choose between Cloud Hall and the runic arts, he'd choose runes without hesitation.
His skill ranked among the Heartlands' finest. He'd trained at the Runic Tower, leaving with soul energy in the top ten. Yet five consecutive runes left him dizzy for days.
That's why he'd estimated this project would require a minimum of a year. Rune-crafting demanded precision and focus. But Zane made it look effortless—instinctive, natural as breathing.
What Cedric couldn't fathom was Zane's soul strength. He couldn't imagine a Soul Realm warrior possessing a Battle-hardened master's soul.
Twelve hours passed. Zane never stopped. When he finally set down his brush, over two hundred three-star textures lined the hall's wall.
Cedric stared in awe. This kid must have a soul forged from iron.
[AMBIENT: BUSY WORKSHOP]
[SFX: GRINDING STONES]
After a full night's rest, Zane was back at it, refreshed and ready to continue drawing. The hall was a hum of activity, helpers busy breaking down materials, hanging them to dry, and grinding them into fine powders. The steps sounded simple, but each was a meticulous, complex process.
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL STRINGS]
[SFX: MAGICAL ENTRANCE]
Suddenly, the air in the hall grew cooler. Tia floated in. The stewards and assistants, who had been bustling about, all stopped and bowed. Tia just gave a small wave, signaling them not to disturb Zane.
Her eyes, warm as sunlight, were shining. She watched Zane with a quiet, powerful focus, her gaze as serene as a hidden valley.
[MUSIC: FOCUSED CONCENTRATION]
Zane was oblivious to it all. The only thing that existed for him was the tip of his quill and the world of divine textures unfolding before him.
