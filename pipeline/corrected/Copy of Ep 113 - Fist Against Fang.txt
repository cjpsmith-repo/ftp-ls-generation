Ep 113 - Fist Against Fang
Word Count: 1227

[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
 In the eyes of Cliff and his fellow Varsity members, the pledges represented nothing more than potential casualties waiting to happen. Their courage might be commendable, but bravery alone couldn't slay demons that had claimed the lives of seasoned warriors. The pledges were burdens to protect, obstacles to maneuver around during combat—dead weight that could jeopardize the entire mission.
Nevertheless, Skyreach Sect traditions remained inflexible: five Varsity members accompanied by five pledges. Before departing Misty Peak, they had meticulously crafted a strategy for managing—or rather, babysitting—these novices throughout the dangerous expedition. Cliff had personally insisted on keeping the pledges at the rear, far from any real danger.
Yet here he knelt, his life preserved by an Outer Disciple whom he had dismissed as insignificant mere hours ago. The realization stung his pride like salt in an open wound. Cliff's mind raced with confusion as he struggled to comprehend what had transpired. Why had the demon general recoiled from Zane's simple Shattered Dagger while completely disregarding his own Lightspeed Slash—a technique he had spent years perfecting? The question burned in his thoughts, challenging everything he believed about power and hierarchy.
[SFX: DAGGER BEING PICKED UP]
 Zane snatched his Shattered Dagger from the ground. "So, I’m special," he said, eyeing the demon general. "Does that mean I get a pass?"
"Oh, no," the demon general rasped, its forked tongue darting out to wet its lips. "Special just means you'll taste better."
A wet, gurgling laugh rumbled in the demon general's chest, full of disgusting satisfaction. It was already savoring the taste of Zane's flesh. 
[SFX: DEMONIC GROWL]
 Ignoring Cliff completely, it lunged for Zane.
[SFX: BLADE HUMMING WITH ENERGY]
 Seeing his chance, Cliff poured a stream of bright silver Vitality into his Lightspeed Slash. The blade hummed and shot toward the demon general, seemingly with a will of its own. ‘We have to work together,’ Cliff thought, 'or we’re all dead.'
But the demon general was on another level. It sidestepped the streaking blade with contemptuous ease, then sprouted several Bone Spikes from its back that swatted the sword away. This wasn't just some regular demon; they were completely outmatched.
Zane’s eyes tracked the demon general’s every twitch, his focus absolute. One mistake, one missed detail, and he was dead. 
[SFX: KNIFE SPINNING THROUGH AIR]
 The instant the demon lunged, Zane flicked his wrist, sending his Shattered Dagger spinning through the air. 
[SFX: AIR WHOOSH]
But the creature was impossibly fast. It blurred to the side, the knife slicing through empty air where its throat had been a millisecond before.
Gritting his teeth, Zane abandoned ranged attacks and charged. He reached inward, calling on the Cobalt Dragon in his mind, its hundred-plus scales shimmering with power. 
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
An incredible force flooded his limbs, converging in his right fist as he met the demon general head-on. His body, already forged into a Soul Armament, was the only reason he didn't tear himself apart from the strain. Anyone else would have disintegrated.
[SFX:  CRACK]
 The two fists collided with a sickening, bone-shattering crack that echoed through the forest.
[SFX: POWERFUL SHOCKWAVE]
 The shockwave of their impact blasted outward, pulverizing the stones at their feet. Zane was flung back dozens of yards, his eyes wide with shock. He dug his heels in, desperately trying to slow himself, carving deep trenches in the earth.
[SFX: BODY SKIDDING ON GROUND]
But even as he skidded to a halt, he felt something new. Fighting ordinary people hadn't done anything for him since his body became a Soul Armament, but the demon general's raw power had just awakened the Vitality Current within him. Zane closed his eyes for a second, feeling the fresh energy surge through his veins. The other disciples stared, stunned that he was still standing, a flicker of hope igniting among them.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
 That hope died the moment the demon general recovered.
The Demon General feinted with the two arm spurs — Zane dodged. Then, without warning, more than a dozen spurs fired simultaneously from its chest, back, and shoulders.
[SFX: BONE SPURS FIRING]
Zane twisted, saving his chest — but one spur drove clean through his shoulder. His feet left the ground. He hung there, impaled, suspended in midair.
The Demon General wrapped a bark-thick hand around his legs. Its laugh was slow and wet. "Special flesh tears the best."
It began to pull.
[MUSIC: DESPERATE TENSION]
A cunning smile spread across Zane's face, his eyes glinting with newfound resolve. "Stronger, perhaps," he contemplated while flexing his fingers, "but intelligence trumps brute force every time."
The demon general hesitated, its grotesque features contorting with uncertainty at the youth's unexpected display of confidence. Something about the boy's demeanor triggered a primal warning in its ancient mind. Dismissing the unsettling sensation with a shake of its massive head, the creature refocused on its primal objective: dismembering the human before it and feasting on his remains.
[SFX: BLADE WHISTLING THROUGH AIR]
 In that critical moment, the Shattered Dagger, which had lain forgotten amid the debris and carnage, suddenly launched through the air with deadly precision. With an almost imperceptible twist of his wrist, Zane had summoned the weapon back to his command, the blade gleaming hungrily in the filtered forest light.
[MUSIC: HIGH-STAKES FIGHT]
Caught off guard, the demon emitted a guttural sound of surprise as the dagger hurtled toward its face. Its reflexes proved formidable nonetheless, as a dense forest of Bone Spikes erupted from its back, arcing forward to intercept the incoming blade with mechanical efficiency.
The dagger, however, served merely as a calculated diversion in Zane's intricate mental chess game—a sacrificial piece in his greater strategy.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
As the demon focused on the spinning steel, Zane closed his eyes. A faint yellow light bloomed between his eyebrows, coalescing in an instant into a glowing, ethereal spike. He unleashed it.
[SFX: PSYCHE SPIKE]
[SFX: DEMON ROAR]
The Psyche Spike slammed into the demon general’s mind, and the creature roared in pure agony. Blinded by the psychic assault, it never even saw the real threat. The Shattered Dagger, its path now clear, plunged deep into the demon’s chest.
Even as the dagger found its mark, the demon general reflexively lashed out, driving one of its own Bone Spikes toward Zane’s heart. Zane reacted instantly, planting his feet on the demon's chest and gripping the spike in his shoulder with both hands. More than a hundred of his dragon scales lit up at once, flooding him with immense power that he channeled straight into his arms.
[SFX: BONE CRACK]
With a sharp crack, the sheer force of his grip snapped the thick bone spike in two.
Before the demon could even process the psychic pain, Zane wrenched the jagged half of the spike free and plunged it deep into the creature's skull.
[SFX: STAB]
[SFX: DEMON SHRIEK]
A final, agonized shriek erupted from the demon's throat as it collapsed to its knees. The creature clawed frantically at its head, its massive form convulsing violently against the blood-soaked earth.
With the Shattered Dagger gripped firmly in his hand, Zane loomed over the writhing body. His eyes narrowed with cold determination as he began to methodically dismember the demon, each precise slash releasing fountains of black, steaming blood that hissed when it struck the forest floor.
