Competition and gambling were inseparable—one always followed the other like shadow follows light.

The second and third-level sects throughout the Heartlands held their own tournaments, of course, drawing crowds and generating excitement. But seasoned gamblers knew better than to trust those events.

Corruption ran deep. Powerful sects manipulated outcomes with ruthless efficiency, arranging fixed matches that lined their coffers while draining the purses of unsuspecting bettors. A fighter destined to dominate would suddenly stumble in the final round, his inexplicable defeat costing gamblers fortunes in Aether crystals. The stench of betrayal hung over those arenas like smoke.

The Martial Tournament stood apart from such deception.

Hosted by the World Merchants' Guild, it operated under different rules entirely. The Dragon Soar Arena represented a coveted prize—a symbol of prestige that every major sect desired. The Guild had built its reputation on neutrality and fairness over centuries. They wouldn't compromise that legacy by conspiring with powerful factions to swindle spectators out of a few Aether crystals, no matter how large the sum. Their word was their bond, and that bond was worth more than any temporary profit.

This integrity transformed the Martial Tournament into a gambler's paradise. Here, skill determined victory. Here, the odds reflected genuine ability rather than backroom deals. Warriors could trust their instincts, study the fighters, and place their bets with confidence that the outcome would be honest.
