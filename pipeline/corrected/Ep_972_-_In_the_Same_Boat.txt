Ep 972 - In the Same Boat
Word count: 1006
[AMBIENT: PALACE HALL]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
With Kane gone, Sir Kenneth was the last powerhouse from Cloud Hall left in the Eastern Territories. Who in the entire Palace could possibly challenge him?
The Empire was just a kingdom of mortals, as helpless as infants before true warriors. It didn’t matter who wore the crown; the real power always belonged to the warriors.
The court officials were shell-shocked, but not one of them dared to utter a word. Even the handful of Inner-Core Realm warriors residing in the Palace kept their distance.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]
Kenneth had laid out two paths for Isla. She could overthrow Ray and seize the throne for herself, or she could journey to the Heartlands to be with Zane.
[MUSIC: GENTLE FLUTE]
But Isla’s choice had stunned him. She didn’t make a play for the crown, and she didn’t leave the Eastern Territories. Instead, she simply went back to Misty Peak to continue her life as a master at the Skyreach Sect.
Kenneth brought Zane up to speed on everything Isla had been doing while he was away.
Zane nodded, a faint smile playing on his lips. He had to hand it to Kenneth—the man had handled things well. Still, he was surprised Isla was willing to remain on Misty Peak for good.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
They were speaking through their Vitality. “What are you two whispering about?” Tia asked, her curiosity getting the better of her.
“Uh…” Kenneth flinched, suddenly flustered. Zane was using his Vitality for a reason, obviously trying to keep the conversation private. Kenneth couldn’t just spill the beans, but he was at a total loss for what to say.
Zane just smiled. “Nothing important,” he said. “Just catching up on things at the Skyreach Sect. Tia, Cora, you’re here now—you should let Sir Kenneth give you the grand tour. I have something I need to see to.”
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Cora said nothing, her gaze fixed on Zane. The look in his eyes told her all she needed to know.
Tia stepped in front of Cora, cutting off her path. “Is this about Zane?”
Cora met her eyes. “You could say that.” She paused. “Though I think we should probably forget about it.”
“What do you mean?” Tia’s voice dropped.
Cora tilted her head, as if choosing her words carefully. “Zane grew up here, in a Tier-Two Sect in the Eastern Territories. He’s not a promiscuous man — but he is merciful.” She let that sit for a moment. “I’m just wondering who that person might be.”
Tia went very still. She tried to control her expression. She didn’t entirely succeed.
Cora’s voice softened. “Isn’t it normal for a man to have multiple wives?”
“Master Tia, please, allow me,” Kenneth jumped in. “I can show you the different peaks of the Skyreach Sect.”
Zane used the opening to slip away.
[SFX: SHARP FOOTSTEPS]
“Preposterous!” Tia turned and swept into the hall.
[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
The moment Kenneth stepped into the main hall, he called for the maids to bring refreshments. Tia dropped onto the dragon throne at the center of the hall, arms crossed. Kenneth hovered nearby, too nervous to speak.
For a moment, neither woman said anything. They weren’t close — not like sisters — but they’d spent enough time together that the silence between them wasn’t entirely uncomfortable. Right now, Tia had the distinct feeling they were in the same boat.
Cora sat beside her. After a moment, she asked quietly, “Don’t you want to go and take a look?”
“No,” Tia said.
Cora shifted a little closer. “You really don’t want to?”
“I don’t want to...” Tia’s certainty wavered. She caught Cora’s expression — that patient, faintly mocking look — and felt her resolve crumble entirely. “Fine. Let’s go. Quietly.”
She turned to Kenneth. “Where did Zane go?”
Sir Kenneth’s face tightened. He weighed his options quickly. He’d rather offend Zane than the Hall Master. Zane was a man — he’d be magnanimous. Tia was another matter entirely.
“Misty Peak,” he said. “I’ll take you there now.”
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN PATH — MISTY PEAK]
[MUSIC: NOSTALGIC STRINGS]
Zane had already made it to the mountain’s waist — the stretch of Misty Peak where the outer disciples trained. He’d retracted his aura, moving quietly, the way he used to when he didn’t want to be noticed. Old habit. Below him, a crowd of disciples had gathered around something. He dropped lower to get a better look.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUND]
[SFX: COMBAT SPARRING]
Two disciples in black robes were sparring on the battle ring.
Why are inner disciples fighting out here? Zane wondered, but then a smile broke across his face as he recognized a familiar figure. “Ethan,” he murmured.
Ethan had been Zane’s first real friend at the Skyreach Sect. He’d changed—his entire bearing was different. And somehow, he was now an inner disciple.
Ethan had always had average talent and even less willpower; it was usually Zane’s own encouragement that kept him going. He’d even gone toe-to-toe with the master of House Moon for him back in the day.
It looked like his friend had been training his ass off since he’d left. Right now, Ethan was in the ring, his Vitality flaring. But his opponent was no slouch—maybe even a cut above him. Even so, Ethan’s tenacity was unbelievable. No matter how hard his opponent struck, Ethan absorbed the blow and refused to give an inch.
Ethan really had changed.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Zane sighed, impressed, but as he kept watching, his expression darkened.
The other warrior was clearly toying with him. He wasn’t even breaking a sweat, a cruel, mocking smirk on his face as he slowly wore Ethan down. Even over the clash of blows, Zane could hear his opponent’s taunts.
“You people are delusional,” he sneered. “Misty Peak, number one among the Thirty-Three Peaks? What a joke! Zane was the only one with any real talent here. The second he left, this place went right back to being nothing.”
