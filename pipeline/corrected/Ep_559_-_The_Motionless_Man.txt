Ep 559 - The Motionless Man
Word count: 1006
[AMBIENT: DARK OMINOUS ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
To the Ogres, humans were just livestock. Sure, human meat was a little more tender than beef or mutton, but they were a pain to raise—they took too long to breed and were a lot more work than a simple herd of cattle.
So, Ogres didn’t bother with them for everyday meals. Instead, they saved humans for special occasions, butchering a few for their big festivals or sacrificing them in bloody rituals. [AMBIENT: DISTANT SCREAMS AND CHAINS]
For the humans penned up in the pastures, life was a gut-wrenching nightmare. They lived in constant terror, waiting for the day they’d be slaughtered for the Ogres' amusement. Every time a festival or ritual rolled around, a dozen or so of them were dragged away to their deaths.
Every now and then, a few brave souls would try to fight back against the towering Ogres, but it was always pointless. No matter how hard they fought or how desperate they were to live, their defiance only ended in despair. There was no changing their fate. In fact, those who resisted the most usually died the worst, their rebellion only fueling the Ogres' rage.
[AMBIENT: QUIET PRISON TENT]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]
Lying on the cot, Zane’s body was relaxed, his eyes as calm as a still lake.
To the Ogres, he was just a sheep that might try to bite—a minor annoyance, nothing more.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
What they didn't know was that, to Zane, they were the ants, no matter how immense or imposing they seemed.
At his side, Craig gripped his sharpened wooden stick until his knuckles blanched white. Standing before the advancing column of Ogres, he had already made his peace with death. If the end came, he would meet it with a weapon in hand rather than roasting over their fires. His legs trembled violently, yet he held his ground, refusing to retreat even a single step.
As the Ogres closed the distance, Zane's eyes flickered with an otherworldly light.
His soul slipped free from his body.
[SFX: MAGICAL SURGE]
The invisible essence swept outward like a curved blade of pure will, silent and unseen, passing through the minds of the advancing Ogres one after another. The entire assault lasted less than a heartbeat.
[SFX: BODIES DROPPING — MULTIPLE]
Without warning or sound, every last creature collapsed. Their massive bodies shuddered once in violent spasm, muscles going rigid, before they crashed to the ground in a cascade of falling flesh.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]
Craig stared, his mouth falling open. The wooden stick hung loose and forgotten in his trembling hand.
The man beside him hadn't moved from his cot. Hadn't spoken a word. Hadn't even raised a finger in their direction. He had simply looked—and a dozen Ogres lay dead at their feet.
Craig's mind struggled to comprehend what his eyes had witnessed. This wasn't the power of a typical Soul Realm cultivator. This was something else entirely. Something that killed without motion, without sound, without mercy. The casual ease of it terrified him more than the Ogres themselves had.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: DARK ATMOSPHERIC]
Zane ignored the boy’s worshipping gaze. With a frown, he focused all his energy on expelling the disruptive Spatial Laws from his body. He had to be able to move again, at the very least.
Vesper had told him this continent was a massive landmass, dwarfing the entire Heartlands and rivaling the size of the continent that housed the union of seven divine kingdoms. Zane figured that meant there had to be powerful Ogre masters here, too. A new determination hardened within him. He had to get back to full strength before one of them found him.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The Ogre leader who’d ordered the attack stood frozen, staring at the motionless man. He couldn’t wrap his head around what Zane had just done. He’d never felt threatened by a human before—why would a man fear a sheep? But a sheep that could slaughter a dozen men with a single glance? That would terrify anyone. The Ogre leader felt that same primal terror now, his legs refusing to move.
[SFX: FEARFUL GASP]
The Ogre stumbled back, stammering, “D-Devil…”
[SFX: HORSE GALLOPING]
[SFX: WHIP CRACK]
He scrambled onto his courser, cracked his whip, and fled into the darkness.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL]
Zane had wanted to kill the leader with the others, but the Ogre was already too far for his Soul Power to reach. And right now, Zane couldn't even move a muscle. Too bad, he thought. Couldn't finish the job.
With a mental sigh, Zane let him go. The priority was getting mobile again, and fast. He had no idea what other threats were lurking out there.
After clearing the Spatial Laws from his spine, he focused on his legs. Paralysis was a vulnerability he couldn't afford, and he had to fix it. Now.
[SFX: KNEES HITTING GROUND]
[MUSIC: REVERENT STRINGS]
Just as Zane was concentrating on forcing his limbs to respond, the boy beside him suddenly dropped to his knees.
Craig's face was wet. His voice cracked as he pressed his forehead to the ground. "You're a god," he whispered. "You have to be."
He wasn't wrong to think it. To someone who had grown up in a pasture, who had watched people dragged away every festival season and seen every act of resistance crushed without mercy — what Zane had just done was beyond anything their world had room for.
[AMBIENT: RUSTLING MOVEMENT]
[MUSIC: SOLEMN CHOIR]
Craig's prostration triggered a ripple effect through the camp. Tent flaps rustled as figures emerged—first hesitantly, then with growing conviction. One by one, they stepped into the dim light and sank to their knees before Zane. Even the children, who had never known anything but fear, knelt in silent reverence.
These people had never chosen this existence. They had simply survived, day after day, because no alternative had ever presented itself. Because hope was a luxury they couldn't afford.
Because believing in change meant inviting unbearable disappointment.
Until Zane.
[MUSIC: EPIC HEROIC THEME]
It was like witnessing a miracle. One man had slaughtered an entire Ogre patrol in the blink of an eye. For the humans, who’d been penned up like livestock for so long, it was an act only a god could perform. To them, Zane was the god they had been praying for.
