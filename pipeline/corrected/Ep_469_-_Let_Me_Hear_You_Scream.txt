Zane dismissed Stoney, the pale-faced warrior, with barely a glance and shifted his focus to the hulking Men of Basalt advancing toward him with ponderous, grinding steps.

His fingers wrapped around the hilt of his sword as he drew the blade free.

[SFX: SWORD ENERGY WHOOSH]

"Flick the Sword!"

He channeled Streamer's devastating power through the weapon, projecting a concentrated wave of energy directly at the nearest stone man's head. The technique—deceptively simple in name—required precise control to penetrate defenses without dispersing the force.

The sword energy bored through the creature's exterior and pulsed through its core, fracturing it from within. For a heartbeat, nothing seemed to happen. Then a delicate spiderweb of cracks spread across the basalt surface—the only visible evidence of catastrophic internal damage—before the massive figure toppled forward and disintegrated into scattered rubble.

Zane's jaw tightened with grim satisfaction. One down.

"Flick the Sword!"

[SFX: STONE SHATTERING]

"Flick the Sword!"

[SFX: STONE SHATTERING]

[SFX: RAPID DESTRUCTION]

He didn't stop after the first kill. His movements became a blur of deadly intent as three more Men of Basalt shattered under his relentless assault, each one crumbling before it could close the distance.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS REALIZATION]

Stoney could only stare, stunned. By the time Zane destroyed the third basalt man, a cold dread washed over him: he was going to lose.

Of course, the warrior knew the rules of the magic array. Killing beasts awarded points for upgrading your towers. The last round, for instance, killing the crow zombies had awarded ten thousand points—the exact amount needed to upgrade a tower to the fourth level. It had to be precise; any more or any less, and the upgrade would fail.

This round, there were two hundred Men of Basalt, and killing one was worth one hundred seventy-five points. If someone managed to kill all of them, they would get fifteen thousand points, which would become the new requirement for the next tower upgrade.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Zane had already killed three of them. That was over five hundred points stolen right out from under him. Stoney's chest tightened as the realization struck: after this fifth killing competition, he wouldn't have enough points to upgrade his tower.

Fury flooded through him, hot and bitter. He hadn't done a thing to this guy, and here he was, brazenly poaching his kills. The warrior's hands trembled as he gripped his weapon tighter.

”I don't care who you are, you're a dead man," he snarled, his face turning beet-red as rage consumed him. "If I can't get you in this array, I'll hunt you down in Oracle City and kill you myself."

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

[SFX: SWORD CLASH]

After taking down the third basalt man, Zane brought his sword into a ready stance. He finally noticed the pale-faced warrior glaring at him with murderous intent. Recognition dawned, and Zane's expression brightened. He gave the man a cheerful wave. "Hey! It's you! Awesome!"

To be honest, Zane had felt a pang of guilt before he recognized the guy. He understood that poaching kills in someone else's territory was a terrible move—it meant the other warrior couldn't accumulate enough points to upgrade his tower.

But this particular warrior? The memory of their previous encounter surfaced, and Zane's guilt evaporated. This was the same arrogant fool who'd dismissed him earlier, who'd looked down on him with such contempt. Now, watching the man's face contort with impotent rage, Zane felt a flicker of satisfaction.

[AMBIENT: OBSERVATION CHAMBER]

The examiner stared at the Scrying Slate in disbelief. "He's refining his swordsmanship and his soul simultaneously. Is such talent even possible?"

Michael Nash chuckled, though his eyes never left the slate. "Have you forgotten? Zane is a rune master with flawless technique. His skill with runes rivals Arnold's."

[MUSIC: DEEP DISCOVERY]

The examiner fell silent at those words. Every cultivator knew that a rune master's soul possessed exceptional strength, growing more formidable with each inscription they completed. The mental discipline required to channel divine texture left an indelible mark on one's spiritual foundation.

He stroked his beard thoughtfully. After witnessing Zane's devastating sword technique, he'd completely overlooked the young man's expertise in runes. The two disciplines rarely coexisted in one person—the dedication required for either path typically consumed a cultivator's entire focus.

"Even so," the old man added, his face creasing with concern, "I doubt his soul has developed enough to resist Stoney's Discipline of Vile Malice. That technique is terrifying." He'd seen it shatter the minds of Soul Realm masters twice Zane's age.

Michael Nash's expression darkened. He nodded slowly, fingers drumming against the armrest of his chair. “I agree. Zane can only draw one- and two-star runes, which means his soul hasn't matured enough to withstand the Discipline of Vile Malice. I just hope the boy has another trick up his sleeve. Otherwise, this could end very badly.”

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]\

Back in the magic array, Stoney's face disappeared beneath a shroud of black light. A spectral hand materialized from the crown of his head, rising like smoke from a funeral pyre.

The ghostly black hand was formed from Stoney's very soul—a manifestation of his deepest malice. Among the techniques within the Discipline of Vile Malice, this wasn't the most devastating in raw power, but it was by far the cruelest. The Skull Ghost Hand allowed him to reach directly into a victim's consciousness and shatter their soul from within.

[SFX: GHOSTLY WAIL]

"I'll crush your soul!" Stoney's voice rang out with savage glee. "Skull Ghost Hand!"

[SFX: HIGH-PITCHED SPIRIT WHINE]

[MUSIC: SUPERNATURAL TERROR]

The spectral hand released a high-pitched whine—the sound of a thousand tortured spirits compressed into a single wail. It shot toward Zane's head with terrifying speed, crossing the distance in a heartbeat. Within seconds, it hovered directly above him, its five long, spindly fingers poised like daggers ready to plunge straight through his skull and into the vulnerable essence beneath.

Stoney's lips twisted into a dark grin when he noticed Zane standing perfectly still, making no attempt to defend himself. The young man's calm demeanor only fueled Stoney's sadistic anticipation. Did the fool not understand what was about to happen? Or had fear paralyzed him completely?

"Scream!" Stoney snarled, his voice dripping with malicious satisfaction. "Let me hear you shriek in agony! I want to savor every moment of your suffering!"
