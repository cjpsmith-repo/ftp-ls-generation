Ep 819 - Thank You and I'm Sorry

Word count: 1032

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]

Greater Legendary Beasts were ranked by level—ninth-level matched Spirit-Tribulation warriors, tenth-level equaled Life-and-Death cultivators, and eleventh-level rivaled Soul Sea masters. At the eleventh level, any beast's demon core liquefied, forging an internal world.

Yet facing a Greater Legendary Beast differed vastly from confronting ordinary creatures. True Dragons and True Phoenixes possessed innate talents and accelerated growth that enabled them to defeat opponents far beyond their realm—much like the most gifted humans.

“By spirit and soul, we are bound,” Fiona’s voice suddenly rang with an unnerving authority. “True Phoenix, Fiery Shine, hear my command and follow me!”

The True Phoenix, named Fiery Shine, was her bonded beast. A cultivator’s strength determined the strength of their bonded creature, and Fiona was only in the late stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. 

Being able to command a beast like this was almost unheard of, even in the upper world, where the True Phoenix population was vanishingly small. Much like the dragons, their numbers were dwindling; there were only forty-nine fully grown True Dragons in the entire upper world, and even counting the young, their total was less than a thousand.

[MUSIC: TENSE REVELATION]

War King York's eyes moved to War King Wind. “Old Wind,” he said, his voice cold and flat. “You hid this well.”

War King Wind smiled calmly. “If I'd wanted to hide it, I wouldn't have let her fight at all.”

The other kings' expressions eased. He had a point. From the beginning, he'd given Fiona no guidance, no signals. Whatever she'd revealed today, she'd revealed on her own.

But the implications of what they'd just seen weren't lost on any of them. A True Phoenix had almost no ceiling on its growth. 

If Fiery Shine reached level eleven — if it ever matched a Soul Sea Realm warrior — no one in the Divine Kingdoms could stop it. And beyond that: a True Phoenix of sufficient power might be able to do what no Soul Sea warrior had managed since the ascension path was destroyed. It might be able to rebuild it.

The thought settled over the watching masters like a weight.

[AMBIENT: ROARING FLAMES]

[SFX: PHOENIX CRY]

The phoenix stared directly at Zane, its eyes blazing with primordial power. Its feathers, each a hundred feet long, fanned out like a radiant cape behind Fiona. A scorching flame danced on the tip of every single one, each flicker radiating the pure essence of Fire Law. It was a breathtaking, terrifying sight.

The third layer of the Fire Law, Zane guessed, feeling the sheer intensity wash over him. The heat rolling off the phoenix was endless, holding both the power to destroy everything and an impossibly potent life force. Even though this True Phoenix was only at the ninth level, it made him feel completely outmatched. 

The pressure was suffocating, far stronger than anything he’d felt from Maddox during their fight. A Greater Legendary Beast was no joke.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL ORCHESTRAL]

If Fiona’s bonded beast had been a True Dragon or an Ice Phoenix, the pressure would have been even worse. As it was, this creature was probably on par with a master at the fourth or fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm. And Fiona herself was not to be underestimated. Even with everything he had, Zane wasn’t sure he could win this.

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FLAMES]

Because Fiona had focused her training on the laws of Fire and Time, the beast summoned through her bloodline was, naturally, a fire phoenix. Fortunately for Zane, his body was a Mythic Weapon, making him completely immune to fire. In fact, he’d been worried he wouldn’t get another chance to strengthen his physical body. The stronger his body became, the more power he could draw from the Dragon Scales. 

Gathering weapons to refine Celestial Essence was the easy part; the hard part was that his own body couldn’t handle that much force. If he drew on too much of the Dragon Scales’ power, he would simply fall apart.

[SFX: PHOENIX SCREECH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC BATTLE THEME]

Fiona leaned back, settling into the fiery plumage of the great phoenix. She pointed a finger at Zane. “Fiery Shine, Sun Flame Attack!”

[SFX: PIERCING PHOENIX CRY]

A piercing cry ripped from the phoenix’s throat.

[SFX: ENERGY GATHERING]

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FLAMES]

Fiery Shine threw its head back and opened its beak. The very energy of heaven and earth surged into its body, gathering in a blinding light in its mouth.

Zane was floored. It can absorb the energy of heaven and earth directly?

[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

When it had absorbed enough, it fixed its gaze on him. But then — Fiona moved.

She appeared at the phoenix's head in an instant and placed her hand gently against its neck. She whispered something to it, their minds linked in a silent exchange. Then she looked at Zane directly.

“Zane. I've shown you what Fiery Shine can do. If she releases that attack, you'll be turned to ash. I'm asking you one more time — think carefully. It's not too late.”

[AMBIENT: ANXIOUS CROWD MURMURS]

Below, the crowd had already made up their minds.

“Just admit defeat. He's already forced the imperial family to summon a Greater Legendary Beast — that's more than enough glory for one day.”

“He won't. He hasn't this whole fight.”

“Then he's courting death. The princess already gave him every chance she could.”

Zane looked at Fiona for a moment.

“Thank you,” he said. “And I'm sorry.”

Thank you for the warning. Sorry for what came next.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

Fiona's expression filled with regret. Summoning Fiery Shine hadn't been an easy choice, but as a warrior, she could never run from a duel. It was a fundamental rule, as essential as breathing. Since she couldn't avoid the fight, she had to see it through. 

With a deep breath, Fiona resolved to fight Zane with her true strength. This battle was forcing her to confront her mission, to understand what her father, the king, truly expected of her. Before this, she would have surrendered rather than summon Fiery Shine and risk killing Zane.

But not this time. She refused to disappoint her father again.

Seeing her unspoken warning had done nothing to deter Zane, Fiona's eyes narrowed. She turned back to her phoenix and gave its head a firm, knowing pat.

[SFX: MASSIVE FIRE BLAST]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

Fiery Shine had been waiting. On its master's command, it opened its mouth, and a ten-foot-wide beam of pure fire shot toward Zane.
