[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STRINGS]

Light bled through Zane’s eyelids, forcing him to blink. He had merged with the will of this chaotic world, and in doing so, inherited its memories, a flickering cascade of moments too vast to process all at once. He could only let them wash over him.

Navigating the torrent was surprisingly simple. All he had to do was focus his intent—to think of what he was looking for. He sifted through the endless flow of information, and in a flash, he found it.

“It’s real,” Zane breathed, the words escaping in a rush of disbelief. “The Soulcradle Wood - it’s on the Divine Kingdom Continent!” A wild hope surged through him, so strong it felt like his heart was trying to beat its way out of his chest.

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TEMPLE]

As he dug deeper, the story unfolded. 

The Holy Maiden of the York Divine Kingdom was part of a secret order that maintained a passage called the Reverse Spirit Channel. Through this channel, their counterparts in the upper world constantly sent down resources. The riches of the upper world were legendary; what they considered trash often became priceless treasures in the lower realms.

Zane’s focus sharpened as he realized most of the treasures sent through the channel had been funneled directly to the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms. And then he saw it: three blocks of Soulcradle Wood had been sent down.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TONES]

Two were already gone, their purpose served. They now housed the souls of two legendary warriors from the Soul Sea Realm. When these masters had begun to face the Five Aging Processes—the inevitable decay of a mortal life—they had no choice but to abandon their failing bodies. The Soulcradle Wood had become their last refuge.

But one piece remained. It was in the hands of War King Wind of the Wind Divine Kingdom.

The wood worked much like Vesper’s crystal ball, which had preserved his soul for so long. Zane knew that War King Wind had lived for more than ten thousand years; he was undoubtedly keeping the last piece for himself, a final lifeline for when his own body began to fail. A single piece of Soulcradle Wood could grant a soul another thousand years of existence.

Zane pulled himself from the river of memories, his expression hardening with resolve. He had to get that wood. No matter what it took.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

His attention snapped back to the present, his gaze falling on Lavender. She was ghostly pale and looked utterly drained. The thick cracks spiderwebbing across her soul seemed to have deepened. 

Lavender blinked slowly. Her red pupils, once sharp and fierce, had gone dull.

“Zane,” she murmured. “I'm… a little sleepy.”

He floated to her side and gently touched her face. “Then rest,” he said softly. “I'll get the Soulcradle Wood. I promise.”

[AMBIENT: DISTANT SACRED HALL]

[SFX: ANXIOUS CHATTER]

Far away, in the Demon Night's sacred grounds at the Sacred Feather Sea, a commotion had broken out.

“My Queen — something is wrong with the statue!”

“It's dimming. The Queen statue is almost extinguished!”

“Send word to Amber — now!”

The dimming of a queen's statue was no small tremor for the Demon Night. The alarm rippled outward — not just through the Sacred Feather Sea, but through every sacred ground the Demon Night held across the countless worlds. Every member who saw it felt the same cold dread.

[MUSIC: COLD DISTANT STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: VAST DARK]

A woman in a pale blue feather robe stood before three towering statues. Her gaze was fixed on one — the statue of the Queen of Slaughter.

Its light was nearly gone.

A slow smile crossed her face.

“Stubborn to the last,” she said. “I'd planned to send someone down through the Reverse Spirit Channel to finish the job. Seems I won't need to bother.”

She was the Queen of Punishment — another sovereign of the Demon Night, and Lavender's greatest enemy. The last time their statues had clashed, Lavender's champion had shattered hers. She had not forgotten. She had not forgiven.

But now Lavender's soul was nearly gone, and she had done nothing at all.

She laughed.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMURS]

Back at the West Dragon River, the Little Monster still stood where Zane had left him, motionless as a post. Zane walked over slowly, raised one hand, and pressed two fingers gently against the Little Monster's forehead.

The Little Monster toppled.

[SFX: HEAVY THUD]

He hit the ground with a crash and lay still. His soul had been unmade in the furnace. No matter how indestructible his body was, without a soul to drive it, he would not last long.

[MUSIC: DARK CRESCENDO]

[SFX: CROWD CHEERS]

The crowd erupted.

“Zane won! He actually won!”

“That Little Monster used a soul attack on him — did Zane come out of it intact?”

“If the Little Monster's gone hollow, will the Nine Cauldron Martial House just let this go?”

Fiona had never killed anything in her life, but she felt no pity now — only a quiet, deep relief as she watched the Little Monster fall.

The masters from the Soul Sea Realm were a different story. They were practically radiating anxiety. All of them had been waiting for the Little Monster to finally break through to the Life-and-Death Realm, believing his unique talent was the key to their own advancement beyond the Soul Sea Realm—the one goal that consumed every warrior who reached their level.

But Zane had just killed him.

Even the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom, who had remained silent until now, wore a dark and stormy expression.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]

“Zane!” a voice roared, shaking the very air with its fury. “You dare kill the Little Monster’s soul? You have a death wish!”

War Emperor York was trembling, his entire body radiating a rage so profound it was terrifying.

The expressions of the saint-level geniuses soured as they watched him, and even the other Soul Sea masters eyed him nervously. It was clear that War Emperor York was about to challenge Zane himself.

The Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom sighed inwardly, a bitter regret settling in his stomach. He should have told Zane to spare the Little Monster. The creature was technically still alive, but without its soul, it was nothing more than an empty shell.

Still, a burning curiosity tempered the anger of the Harlen Emperor and the other masters. How had Zane done it? They had studied the Little Monster for years without ever cracking its secrets. For all intents and purposes, the creature was immortal. Not only was its body indestructible, but its soul was a unique marvel, glowing with a strange light that outshone even the spirit of a Soul Sea master.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS SUSPENSEFUL THEME]

So how in the world did Zane manage to kill it?
