Ep 673 - True Power

Word count: 1008

[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]

[SFX: BLADE WHOOSHING]

[SFX: CREATURE GRUNTS]

The Shattered Dagger circled Zane like a silver hornet, and with each pass, another green-skinned creature lost its head. From a distance, Lavender drove her spear through one of them, the impact echoing Zane’s own deadly efficiency.

The creatures reeled back, their crude confidence shattering. This wasn't how it was supposed to go.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

Their shock quickly curdled into arrogant rage. Turning their attention to Zane, they shot into the sky, spears held high, their bodies pulsing with raw power. Their commander, his face a long mask of fury, pointed his cane at Zane’s group and ordered the attack.

A mocking smile spread across Zane’s face.

[SFX: GROUND TREMOR]

[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]

He channeled the power of the Dragon Scales to his feet and stomped, sending a tremor through the ground. The Shattered Dagger spun in his hand, radiating a brilliant light as it shot toward the sky. He was already a step ahead. Before the creatures could even think to throw their spears, they were cut down, dropping to the earth with heavy, lifeless thuds.

Zane sized them up. Their fighting style was crude, nothing like the warriors he knew. Their power was probably on par with the War Fighters from the main continent, but the laws of this chaotic world were far stricter. That meant that while his group’s strength was immense here, these creatures seemed pathetically weak by comparison. The only reason they’d been captured in the first place was because Yolande had forbidden them from fighting back. If they had gone all out from the start, this would have been over in minutes.

[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]

In a flash, the Shattered Dagger had taken down more than ten of them. Then Zane himself became a blur, shooting directly toward their commander.

The commander stared, dumbfounded. Impossible! He’d noticed they couldn't fly and had planned to use that very weakness against them. Waving his cane, he shot higher into the air, trying to create distance.

[SFX: CHANTING]

In a panic, the commander raised his staff and chanted something. He swung it hard, and sheets of fire cloud came rolling down toward Zane.

[SFX: FIRE ROAR]

Zane didn't slow down. He punched straight through the fire, burst out the other side unscathed, and kept climbing — though the effort had bled off some of his momentum. There was still a gap between him and the commander.

The commander allowed himself one breath of relief. He raised his staff again to press the advantage.

[SFX: BLADE FLASH]

Zane's right hand snapped up. A white light flickered once — and the Shattered Dagger drilled clean through the commander's chest.

[SFX: BODY FALLING]

They both dropped from the sky. Zane landed clean. The commander hit the ground and didn't move.

Yolande walked over without hurrying and crouched beside him. She spoke in his language. The commander's face was set with defiance; he clutched his wound and roared back at her.

"He won't tell us where the sacred object is," Yolande said, rising. "Even now."

"Then kill him," Zane said.

The Shattered Dagger flashed. It was done.

[SFX: WAR HORNS]

[AMBIENT: FORTRESS ALARM]

Suddenly, the piercing blast of horns echoed through the fortress.

[AMBIENT: ARMY MOBILIZATION]

[SFX: MARCHING TROOPS]

This wasn't just an outpost; it was a full-blown garrison. At the signal, swarms of green-skinned troops poured out, ready for war.

Some of the officers at the front were clearly shouting demands — even without Yolande's translation, the meaning was obvious. Release their leader. Surrender.

Their leader was already dead.

The front line registered it a moment later. Spears came up. They charged.

[MUSIC: BATTLE FURY]

The Shattered Dagger became a wheel of white light, cutting through the ranks in long, sweeping arcs. Row after row of green-skinned soldiers went down like grain before a scythe. Lavender's spear was just as merciless — each thrust punched through a line of them, skewering several at once. The two of them were untouchable.

Esther, Fabian, and Malcolm fought hard, but the difference in output was plain. Against Zane and Lavender, the green-skinned army was being dismantled. Even the ones who took to the air couldn't escape — the Shattered Dagger picked them off as they rose, dropping them back to earth like stones.

[SFX: WAILING RETREAT]

The army held for as long as it could. Then the fear set in. The roaring stopped. The charges stopped. What replaced them was wailing and the sound of running — hundreds of green-skinned soldiers breaking in every direction, flooding out of the stronghold.

Yolande watched them go, expressionless. "Follow them," she said.

[MUSIC: STEADY MARCH]

They followed. For roughly a hundred miles, they kept pace behind the routed army as it streamed deeper into the wilderness.

Then a city appeared on the horizon — far larger and more formidable than the crude walled outpost they'd left behind. It wasn't much by the standards of the main continent's great sanctuaries, but it was a different order of magnitude from what they'd seen before.

[SFX: MASSIVE STONE IMPACT]

[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]

A moment later, a giant stone slammed down with a deafening boom, completely blocking the wall's entrance.

Zane and his companions weren’t worried. This was child’s play. The wall might be a hundred feet tall, but Zane could clear it in a single jump.

"Looks like we aren't their only enemies," he noted.

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

"That’s the least of our worries," Lavender replied, gripping her spear. She was the only one who could fly, and she prepared to take to the air. "We get what we came for and get out."

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC SHIFT]

"Wait," Zane said, stopping her. "Let me handle this."

Esther and the others watched as he started walking slowly toward the barricade. Fabian glanced over his shoulder, a suspicious look on his face. "What the hell is he planning to do?"

[SFX: ARROWS BOUNCING OFF ARMOR]

No one answered. A few creatures on the wall fired arrows and threw spears at Zane, but the projectiles bounced harmlessly off him. He didn’t even slow down.

When he reached the gate, he looked up at the giant stone. It was like a small mountain wedged in the entrance, easily two hundred feet high.

[MUSIC: EPIC BUILDUP]

[SFX: POWER GATHERING]

Zane took a deep breath, gathered the power of the Dragon Scales into his fist, and struck. He had to fight the urge to laugh. It was time to show these creatures what true power looked like.
