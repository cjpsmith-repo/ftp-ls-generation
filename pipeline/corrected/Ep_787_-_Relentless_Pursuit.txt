Ep 787 - Relentless Pursuit

Word count: 987

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]

Zane’s plan was simple: keep a low profile. Hiding his true strength was the best way to snag one of the three spots in the red circle. As long as he played it smart, his current skill level was more than enough. He could easily dodge a whole group of fighters, even in the limited space, without revealing his hand. Besides, he wasn't powerful enough to draw much attention, so he shouldn't have been anyone’s main target.

At least, that’s what he thought.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

 To his surprise, a fighter came at him out of nowhere, clearly trying to knock him out of the ring. The sudden attack left Zane completely baffled. The air rushed past his ear as he seemed to float, circling the very edge of the red circle to avoid the blow.

This same guy had been on his tail from the moment the round started. Zane almost found it amusing, a faint smile touching his lips as he maintained his calm. After Zane had dodged the first attack, the guy should have gotten the hint and moved on. Zane wasn't looking for a fight, so he hadn't even bothered to counter.

But for some reason, the man just wouldn't let up. 

[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]

 He kept charging, a bright light flashing from his fist as he threw another wild punch.

“Get out!” the man sneered.

[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]

[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT]

 Zane moved like a blur, his breath coming in short bursts as he sidestepped one attack after another. Something was definitely wrong with this guy. He’d completely abandoned his defense, pouring every ounce of energy into an all-out assault. It was a reckless strategy, but an effective one for forcing someone out of the ring. There was no history between them, which made the relentless assault feel personal. It was like this fighter didn't even care about passing the test anymore—he just wanted Zane gone, no matter the cost.

Someone else is behind this, Zane thought. Who’s pulling the strings?

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 Then, out of the corner of his eye, he saw him. Randy.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

[SFX: ENERGY SWIRL]

 Randy stood confidently in the middle of the red circle, his hands clasped behind his back as faint particles of energy began to swirl around him. The battle still raged, but none of the other fighters dared to challenge him. Randy’s eyes, however, were locked directly on Zane.

“I knew it. It’s him,” Zane murmured, his expression darkening. Randy had been targeting him ever since he’d joined the martial house, and Zane still couldn’t figure out why. What was this guy’s problem? Was this just some test to gauge the limits of his strength?

[SFX: CROWD CHATTER]

From the sidelines, the watching warriors had plenty to say.

"Look at Randy just standing there. Nobody's even going near him."

"Speaking of which — look at that nobody he's been watching. The middle-stage kid. He's lapped the circle eight times and hasn't been touched once. How is that even possible?"

A young man nearby who had been watching in silence turned at that. "Embarrassing?" His voice was quiet but carried. "A late-stage Spirit-Tribulation warrior has been chasing him since the round started and hasn't laid a finger on him. If you think that's embarrassing, I don't know what isn't."

That was Edwin — the Crystal Martial House's strongest young warrior. When he spoke, people listened. 

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

 Suddenly, every eye in the watching crowd was on Zane.

The three Soul Sea masters had already been watching him. They had no interest in Randy's performance — his token was as good as guaranteed regardless of what happened here. It was the unknowns they were looking for. And this one, who had appeared from nowhere, was giving them something to think about.

Zane could have easily finished off the fighter hounding him, but he hesitated. The whole point of being here was to get a token quietly — use it to enter the Sacred Feather Sea, get the ancient heartstones, and go home. Every instinct told him to keep his head down and let the round play out. He kept moving in circles, hoping someone else would deal with his pursuer. But no one touched the man — too busy with their own fights, or perhaps assuming Randy had claimed him as his business. This couldn't go on.

Just as his attacker glowed with an even stronger aura, preparing to back him into a corner, Randy finally made his move. 

[SFX: ENERGY CLAWS WHOOSH]

 Three claws of pure energy shot from his position, tearing through the air straight toward Zane.

[SFX: TELEPORT FLASH]

 But in a flash, Zane’s figure flickered and vanished, reappearing in an instant on the other side of the red circle.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 No one, least of all Randy, could have predicted he could teleport. Randy’s face was a mask of pure shock as his attack missed Zane completely and flew straight toward the fighter who had been chasing him. The man could only gape, running right into the path of the energy claws.

[SFX: SLASHING BLADES]

[SFX: ARMOR BREAKING]

 There was a series of sickening slices as the claws tore through the man’s robes. A sharp crack followed, his protective armor shattering into pieces before dark red blood sprayed from his wounds.

Seeing that he’d accidentally hit his own man, Randy just snorted coldly, clearly not caring whether the fighter lived or died. His gaze immediately snapped back to Zane, though he didn't move an inch, his expression unreadable.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

 With no one chasing him anymore, the tension finally drained from Zane’s shoulders. He let out a long sigh that he didn’t realize he’d been holding.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

 Only about a dozen people were left in the circle, all still fighting fiercely. The intense drama had barely registered with them; they all had the same goal, and nothing was going to stop them from reaching it. This round was far more intense than the last. 

With Randy in the mix, things were tougher—he’d essentially already claimed one of the three spots without a fight.

That left everyone else to fight tooth and nail for the last two.
