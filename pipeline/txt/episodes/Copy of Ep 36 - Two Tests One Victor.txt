[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]
Thirty miles away, the four assailants finally collapsed to the ground, their lungs burning as they gasped desperately for air. Sweat drenched their clothes, muscles quivering with exhaustion beyond anything they had experienced before.
They lay sprawled across the dirt path, utterly spent. Though they had registered for the Entrance Exam legitimately, eliminating the boy had merely been a lucrative side arrangement—one that had backfired spectacularly.
Now, with their bodies betrayed by fatigue, even the thought of completing the exam seemed laughable, let alone pursuing their target further.
The Stone Tablet marking their destination still loomed five or six miles in the distance, but in their current debilitated state, that short remaining journey might as well have been across the entire empire. The eldest among them tried to rise, only to have his trembling arms buckle beneath him.
[MUSIC: CONFIDENT THEME]
Meanwhile, Zane swung his arms vigorously, embracing the pleasant burn of exertion coursing through his muscles. Unlike his pursuers, the physical challenge had only invigorated him, awakening something primal and powerful within his core. His lips curled into the faintest smile as he contemplated his next move.
He'd run them into the ground so completely they could barely stand, let alone flee. What terrified them more, though, was the chilling realization that they didn't have the strength to fight back, either.
Zane gazed down at the muscular man sprawled before him, his eyes cold with quiet authority. "Have I made myself perfectly clear? Or shall I reiterate my instructions?"
"No, no," the man stammered, his broad shoulders now hunched with submission. A forced smile quivered across his face, transparent in its attempt to mask his profound terror. "I heard you loud and clear. I'll deliver your message without fail."
Unlike his scholarly companion who had misjudged the situation fatally, this burly individual possessed a keener instinct for survival. The calculating look in his eyes revealed his rapid assessment—compliance meant life, resistance meant joining his friend's pitiful fate. He recognized when circumstances demanded surrender, a wisdom his companion had lacked.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON GRAVEL]
Satisfied with the man's capitulation, Zane offered a sharp, decisive nod. Without wasting another word or casting a backward glance, he pivoted and strode purposefully down the dusty road, the rhythmic crunch of gravel beneath his feet the only sound breaking the tense silence.
[AMBIENT: AFTERNOON COUNTRYSIDE]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE JOURNEY]
The path ahead stretched empty and inviting, bathed in afternoon light. Though he had technically already secured passage through the Entrance Exam, a burning curiosity gnawed at Zane's core.
His achievement thus far felt incomplete, merely a stepping stone.
"How far can I truly go?" he whispered to himself, the question both challenge and promise. His muscles ached pleasantly, still humming with power from his earlier exertions. The knowledge that the Marrow Refining Level milestone lay ahead ignited a fierce determination in his chest. He wasn't content with merely passing—he needed to discover his absolute limits, to push against the boundaries others accepted as immutable.
Even under four thousand pounds of pressure, which had already reduced his pace to a slow jog, it only took him another half hour to reach the next Stone Tablet.
This was the finish line for cultivators at the Marrow Refining Level—the marker that signified they'd passed the exam.
But the road stretched on, the true end still out of sight. Zane had no idea how much the pressure would ramp up if he crossed the line, but after a moment's thought, he decided there was only one way to find out.
He stepped over without hesitation.
[SFX: SUDDEN PRESSURE SHIFT]
[SFX: CRUSHING IMPACT]
The moment he did, an unimaginable pressure crushed down on him, slamming him to the ground as if a mountain had been dropped on his back.
"The pressure is insane!" Zane groaned.
His face, stomach, and limbs were pinned to the ground as if he were stuck in thick tar. He couldn't budge an inch. He tried to lift his head, but it felt like it weighed ten thousand pounds all on its own. No matter how hard he strained, he couldn't even lift it an inch off the ground.
Disappointment hit him like a punch to the gut. He mentally retraced his steps: the first ten miles were a thousand pounds, the second were two thousand, and the third were four thousand. Following that pattern, this section should have been eight thousand pounds.
But the force crushing him now felt like tens of thousands of pounds. It was like the pressure had jumped tenfold.
"I can beat this," Zane thought, gritting his teeth as he continued to fight against the crushing weight.
[MUSIC: INTENSE DETERMINATION]
The intense pressure forced him to tap into his reserves. He focused inward, calling on the two dragon scales inside him and channeling their power through his body. With a roar, Zane drew on every last ounce of his strength.
[SFX: POWERFUL ROAR]
"Get up!"
This kind of pressure would stop a master at the Marrow Refining Level cold and would even make someone who'd just reached the Nature Level think twice.
Only a true master of the Nature Level could hope to withstand it. Regular trainees at the Bone Refining Level and the Core Strength Level would be crushed instantly just by setting foot in this area.
That kid might have been tough enough to shrug off hits from four Marrow Refining fighters, but fifty thousand pounds of pressure should have flattened him instantly.
[AMBIENT: LOCATION SHIFT]
[SFX: REALIZATION GASP]
[MUSIC: URGENT TENSION]
The gravity of the situation hit them. The chief examiner and Isla leaped into their Skycruisers and raced toward the scene.
[SFX: SKYCRUISER ENGINES]
The Skycruisers streaked across the thirty miles in mere moments, their sleek forms cutting through the air with supernatural speed.
[SFX: SKYCRUISER LANDING]
[AMBIENT: HEAVY PRESSURE FIELD]
Upon dismounting their crafts, the officials froze in collective astonishment. Zane hadn't merely survived—he had defied all physical laws by rising to his feet, his stance unwavering despite the crushing pressure bearing down upon him.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WONDER]
"I've overseen this exam for years," the chief examiner whispered, his weathered face etched with wonder as decades of certainty crumbled before his eyes, "and I've never seen anything like this. Never." His fingers trembled slightly as he gripped his evaluation scroll, the parchment crinkling under his tightened grasp.
Isla clapped a hand over her mouth, her eyes widening until the whites showed all around her irises.
The composed instructor, known for her unflappable demeanor even in the face of exceptional talent, found herself utterly speechless.
A chill ran down her spine as she struggled to process the impossible scene before her.
"He's only at the Core Strength Level," she thought, her carefully constructed understanding of cultivation limits shattering like glass. "How is he resisting fifty thousand pounds of pressure? How is he even standing?"
Her breathing quickened as she realized the implications of what she was witnessing—something that would upend everything the Skyreach Sect thought they knew about cultivation boundaries.
