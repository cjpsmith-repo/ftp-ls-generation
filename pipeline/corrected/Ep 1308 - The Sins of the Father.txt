Ep 1308 - The Sins of the Father

Word count: 1187

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT HALL]
The Demon Night was built on the foundation of six ancient orders, the oldest and most powerful factions within its hierarchy. They controlled the ancient bloodline, which was why the three Queens were always chosen from their ranks.
Some time ago, Gunter had ridden the Queen of Punishment’s support to become the Demon Night’s fourth Supreme Lord, and his own order’s power had skyrocketed as a result. But even with three Queens meant to rule in unison, the Demon Night was a hornet’s nest of internal conflict. A Board of Elders, answering to the queens, was supposed to manage the six orders and keep a fragile peace.
Then Lavender vanished, and that fragile balance shattered completely.
The two orders loyal to the Queen of Life fell silent, following their queen’s lead as she simply ignored the Queen of Punishment’s aggressive power grabs. Meanwhile, the two orders under the Queen of Slaughter were torn apart. One sided with the Queen of Punishment, while the other remained loyal to Lavender. 
The Queen of Punishment’s retribution was swift and brutal, and she immediately began persecuting the loyalists.
This loyalist order was the keeper of the Saint-Killing Spear of the Demon Night. Every Queen of Slaughter in history had wielded the spear forged by this order, which was how they’d become known as the Order of the Saint Spear.
But now, they were broken. Many of their members had gone into hiding, while the rest had been captured and enslaved by rival orders. The men were forced into hard labor, and the women were taken as playthings for their captors.
Hailey was the daughter of the leader of the Order of the Saint Spear. If the queens had never turned on each other, she might have become one of the Demon Night’s elite Ace Talents. Instead, she was now just another powerless captive, a trophy in Slade’s collection.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
When Janet had seen Zane’s full name appear in the Corridor of the Emperor, she’d been intrigued.
But when Hailey saw it, she was overcome with a desperate, dizzying hope.
News of Lavender’s return had already ripped through the Demon Night. Hailey had never dared to dream that Zane would be the one to help her reclaim the throne. His support was the first glimmer of hope the Order of the Saint Spear had seen in a long time—and the only way Hailey could ever be free.
When the news first spread through the Roaring Token, the surviving members of her order had dismissed it. From their perspective, Lavender stood no chance against the Queen of Punishment. Just as the queen herself had declared, Lavender wasn’t even qualified to be her opponent.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
Then everything changed. 
Zane had actually killed Gunter.
And not just Gunter.
He’d taken down three other Supreme Lords with him.
Slade was Gunter’s son. 
[AMBIENT: CITY SQUARE TENSION]
Slade’s attention swept over the group. “The four of you—kneel, and let me kill you three times each. Do that, and I might let you live.”
“Five,” Ansel said, arms crossed, grinning. “There’s five of us. Can’t you count?” For all his usual timidity, he wasn’t budging an inch from Hailey’s side.
Slade’s eyes slid past him and landed on Janet. “I don’t collect anything but beautiful women,” he said, smiling. “This one’s got a flavor all her own. Keep me company here in the Illusion Battlefield, and I might spare you the trouble.”
Janet tilted her head, unbothered. “Gunter was a fairly unremarkable Supreme Lord as it is. And this—” she gestured vaguely at the air around them, “—is just a projection. Wouldn’t be nearly as satisfying as the real thing.”
“Then tell me where to find you in the flesh,” Slade said, eyes on her mouth. “I’ll travel however far it takes.”
“My family,” Janet said simply, “would never allow it.” Her smile turned razor-sharp. “Not even your useless father could’ve walked in and taken me. Not even the Queen of Punishment herself would dare send somone after one of us. So—still feeling brave?”
Slade’s expression froze solid.
“Doesn’t matter where you’re from,” he said, recovering, jaw tight. “This is the Illusion Battlefield. Strength decides everything here.”
Janet laughed. “Should’ve led with that.”
She turned, reaching over to pat Zane’s arm. “The rest is up to you.”
There was no walking this back now.
Both teams began filing out of the main city, and a crowd of onlookers trailed after them—cultivators with no hope of competing themselves, but every right to watch.
“Did you hear that? She’s from Janet’s family!”
“So what if she is—doubt she’s anyone important within it.”
“She’s not even planning to fight herself. Looks like she’s leaving it to that guy. Wonder what his deal is.”
“Whatever it is, he’s in trouble. Slade’s a real prodigy of the Demon Night, son of a Supreme Lord. No telling how strong he really is.”
Standing on the city wall and watching the scene unfold below, Janet couldn’t help but wonder. *Did he just get lucky, using the hall’s rules to kill that Ace Talent? Or is he really strong enough to beat someone like that on his own?*
[AMBIENT: CITY WALL WIND]
She, Hailey, Coleman, and Ansel watched as Slade became the first to leave the city.
He walked a dozen paces past the wall, then turned to face Zane.
Seeing that Zane hadn’t moved, he sneered. “Having second thoughts? Go ahead, be a coward. As long as you stay inside the main city, my hands are tied.”
Slade tried to sound magnanimous, but he was secretly worried Zane wouldn’t take the bait. If he stayed put, Slade would have to find another way to provoke him.
A small smile touched Zane’s lips. “No regrets. I’m just waiting for something. If they don’t show up, it might even be for the best.”
“Waiting for what?” Slade asked, his curiosity getting the better of him.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING]
[AMBIENT: SWARM CHITTERING]
Just then, a swarm of small black dots materialized in the air, floating in from both sides of the city wall, accompanied by a dry, chattering sound that filled the air.
It was the hundreds of tiny skulls. They couldn’t enter the city, so they’d been circling the walls, waiting. Now that Zane had appeared on the west side, they swarmed toward him.
Zane sighed. Shaking these things was going to be a real pain. They’d followed him all the way from the hall, and he still had no idea how.
“What the hell are those?” Slade stared, stunned, at the cloud of chattering, bobbing skulls.
As Zane stepped past the city wall, the skulls rushed him, but he didn’t even flinch. 
[SFX: SKULL SNATCHED FROM AIR]
He snatched one right out of the air and pointed it at Slade, a wicked grin spreading across his face. 
“You don’t need to know what they are. 
[MUSIC: DARK CONFRONTATION]
All you need to know is this: I killed your father, and I’ll make damn sure you suffer a hell of a lot more than he did.”
