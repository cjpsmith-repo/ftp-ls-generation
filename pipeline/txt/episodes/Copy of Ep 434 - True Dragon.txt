As the light enveloped him, Zane heard footsteps pounding closer from behind—rapid, desperate, closing the distance with alarming speed.

The young master of House Kendrick burst into view. A late-stage Inner-Core warrior, he'd somehow blazed through to the sixth floor with terrifying efficiency, leaving behind competitors who should have matched his strength.

Zane's pulse quickened. The timing couldn't be worse, or perhaps it was perfectly calculated.

The young master's face twisted with triumph and rage. His hand shot toward his weapon, but the light was already pulling Zane deeper into its embrace.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL HUMMING]

The blinding light felt like fire, igniting every cell in Zane's body. He could feel himself turning to cinders, his physical form dissolving into nothing.

"You?!" The young master of House Kendrick had planned this for so long, had gone to such lengths to catch Zane. But he was seconds too late. All he could do was watch as Zane’s smiling face faded into the light.

In the final moment before Zane vanished, the Cobalt Dragon transferred a fragment of its soul into him, subtly altering the teleportation's divine signature—just enough to go unnoticed.
[SFX: STONE GRINDING]
On the sixth floor, the young master pressed his Dragon Crystal Pendant against the circular glyph. Nothing — the pattern had shifted, subtly, just enough to reject the key.
Cursing, he yanked the pendant free. The moment it left the wall, the glyph blazed back to life. He drove it in again and felt the light take hold.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

[SFX: MASSIVE ENERGY BLAST]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL CRESCENDO]

The moment Zane disappeared, a pillar of white light erupted from the top of the Dragon Soar Arena and shot into the sky. It pierced clouds, wind, and strange fissures in space before vanishing beyond the horizon.

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR CROWD MURMURS]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL WONDER]

"Whoa... Incredible. The young master of House Kendrick actually pulled it off! Looks like he's been sent off to another world." Lady Darkmere let out a soft sigh, her eyes glistening with amusement as she stared up at the Dragon Soar Arena, her face alight with excitement.

It had long been rumored that the Dragon Soar Arena was a gateway to the vast world built by the True Dragon—a direct portal to the upper world. This was the proof. The legends were true. The gateway was real.

[MUSIC: SOMBER REFLECTION]

For the people of Zane's world, reaching the upper world by their own power was impossible.

While the crowd mistakenly celebrated the first beam, believing it was the young master, his three retainers from the Inner-Core Realm knew the truth. They watched with bated breath. So when a second beam flared to life moments later, they were the only ones who looked truly thrilled. He did it! Our young master is going through, too, they thought, relief washing over them. Their initial worry from the first beam vanished.

They might have failed to secure the Dragon Soar Arena itself, but at least their young master was on his way to gain experience in the upper world. If he could somehow break into the Spirit-Tribulation Realm there, House Kendrick could finally rise from a Third-Tier Sect order to a coveted Fourth-Tier.

[SFX: MAGICAL DISRUPTION]

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

But then, something went wrong. The light sputtered, and a figure materialized within it, looking unsteady. 

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

The young master of House Kendrick tumbled through the air before slamming into the ground with bone-jarring force. When his sight cleared, he found himself staring at the same familiar courtyard stones he'd been standing on moments before.

"Where the hell did I get sent?" he spat, his voice cracking with confusion.

"Y-Young Master?" one of the Kendrick warriors stammered, his face pale with shock.

The young master's gaze snapped to his retainers, and the humiliating truth crashed down on him like a physical blow. If they were still standing here—still in the same positions—his teleportation had failed completely. Heat flooded his cheeks as the realization settled in his chest like a stone. After everything he'd gone through, after all his boasting and preparation, that upstart had made a complete fool of him. In front of everyone.

His hands clenched into fists, knuckles whitening as rage and shame warred within him.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

Losing his temper completely, the young master roared, "I wasn't sent to the upper world! What the hell happened? Where did I go wrong?!"
"The glyph rejected my pendant," he muttered, still bewildered. "I had to pull it out and try again — and even then it barely worked."

"Young Master," one of his warriors whispered urgently, "everyone is watching."

The warning snapped the young master back to his senses. He looked up, and the blood drained from his face. 

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: WIND WHOOSH]

A group of Spirit-Tribulation warriors hovered in the sky directly above him.

[AMBIENT: TENSE OUTDOOR ATMOSPHERE]

Raven peered down at the scene. "Little man," he called out to the young master, his voice carrying easily, "who was it that got sent through just now?"

The young master of House Kendrick might be a powerhouse to Adepts and Soul Realm warriors, but before the strongest fighters from The Heartlands, he was nothing but a lamb for the slaughter.

He knew it, too. He had no choice but to answer Raven truthfully. "The one who got through… he was an Adept. I don't know who he was, but he was so fast I couldn't keep up with him." As the words left his mouth, a look of pure resentment twisted his features. He still couldn't fathom how some mere Adept had succeeded where he had failed.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]

Tia's brow furrowed in confusion. "An Adept? This Adept must have been incredibly bold."

Even Soul Realm cultivators like her needed thorough preparation before attempting teleportation.

She hadn't expected an Adept to be teleported away today.

Tia's mind raced through possibilities, but without knowing Zane's plans, she found it impossible to guess his identity. The world was full of warriors; even with her sharp instincts, she couldn't imagine such a coincidence. 

The strangeness of the situation lingered in the air, yet the Sect leaders remained fixated on the Dragon Soar Arena's ownership. Power and territory always took precedence over mystery.

Lady Darkmere's voice cut through the tension. "We need to discuss this matter properly," she said, her tone measured but firm. "But before that, shouldn't we eliminate that Winged Dragon first?"

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

The Winged Dragon possessed intelligence—ancient and keen. As the guardian beast of the Dragon Soar Arena, it had served faithfully for generations, and now it immediately sensed the shift in the air. Danger radiated from these mighty warriors like heat from a forge. Against opponents of this caliber, escape would prove nearly impossible. 

Its golden eyes darted between the Sect Lords, calculating odds that grew slimmer with each passing breath.

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL VOID]

Zane's body disintegrated into particles racing through the void before violently reassembling at his destination.

"What... is this place?"

A city bathed in ethereal blue light stretched before him. Behind him, transmission arrays extended to the horizon in perfect formation.

Zane stared at the transmission arrays in shock. "How can there be so many of them?"

In the Eastern Territories, constructing even one transmission array required vast quantities of top-grade Crystal Cubes. Yet over a hundred thousand stretched before him.

"Heh, one transmission array corresponds to one Dragon Soar Arena," the Cobalt Dragon said dismissively. "This represents only a fraction of the True Dragon Clan's work."

The dragon continued, its voice carrying an unexpected note of pride. "This city was built for the Dragon-blooded, like you! This is just a tiny piece of your true existence..."

The True Dragon's lineage extended beyond dragons to include the Dragon-blooded—mortals carrying True Dragon blood who could master draconic techniques, with rare individuals surpassing even True Dragons. Countless Dragon-blooded scattered across the Lower World explained why so many Dragon Soar Arenas existed: they served as gateways to the upper world.

This explained why the Heartlands' greatest fighters desperately sought access to the Dragon Soar Arena—it was the express path upward, designed by the True Dragon for its descendants.

Zane took a moment to get his bearings, then started walking toward Cerulean City. 

[SFX: WINGS FLAPPING]

He hadn't gotten far when a handful of guards on strange, bird-like mounts descended, surrounding him.
