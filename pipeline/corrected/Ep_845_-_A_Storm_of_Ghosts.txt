Ep 845 - A Storm of Ghosts

Word count: 954

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

A frown creased Zane’s brow at the Cobalt Dragon’s urgent warning. “What is it?” he asked cautiously. “What happened?”

“The Ghost Beast—it's speaking the dragon tongue,” the Cobalt Dragon explained, its voice tight with haste.

The dragon tongue? Zane knew the dragons had their own language, of course, but he’d had no trouble communicating with the Dragon-blooded on his last trip to the upper world. It had never even occurred to him that their ancient language was something he might encounter here.

The dragon confirmed. “That's right - It’s calling for more beasts from The Spectral Plains. If you don't get out of here now, you might not get another chance!”

[AMBIENT: THUNDER AND LIGHTNING]

[MUSIC: URGENT DRUMS]

The moment the warning ended, Zane snapped his head back toward the battle. The Ghost Beast was still being hammered by lightning, but Zane’s expression hardened. “Let's go!” he yelled to Randy and the others. “Everyone, out of here! Now!”

Randy stared, baffled. A minute ago, Zane had been perfectly calm. What the hell happened?

“Hurry!” Zane shouted over the storm. “That thing is calling for backup.”

Randy still hesitated, but one look at the sheer gravity on Zane's face was enough. He didn't need any more convincing. As he turned to go, a flash of movement caught his eye: the Crown Prince of the Harlen Divine Kingdom.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS HORNS]

He was leading Edwin and the rest of his retainers straight toward the Ghost Beast, clearly hoping to score some Ghost Beads for themselves.

Despite their issues, the man was still his half-brother. Randy sent a quick, silent warning with a pulse of his Vitality.

The Crown Prince shot him a surprised, frowning glance but seemed to trust the message, immediately motioning for his group to pull back.

[SFX: DEAFENING ROAR]

Just as they turned to retreat, however, the Ghost Beast let out a deafening roar that tore across the sky.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

“It's dying! Come on, everyone!” Fredrick shouted, his voice ringing with excitement.

“Whatever Ghost Beads we get, we'll split them evenly!” another fighter yelled.

“Split them equally? Are you kidding? The strongest gets the biggest share!” someone else shot back.

[SFX: MASSIVE COLLAPSE]

[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]

But the killing blow never came. Instead of dying, the ghost beast let out one final roar and collapsed to the ground. The moment it hit, the Writhing Weeds within a hundred feet erupted.

[SFX: VIOLENT GROWTH]

[SFX: RAPID WHOOSHING]

In the span of a single breath, the weeds shot up ten feet high, wrapping around every warrior still in the area. Thick coils of grass locked their arms, their legs, their torsos — layer after layer until they couldn't move.

[AMBIENT: PANICKED SCREAMS]

“Get me out of here!”

“It's coming — someone help!”

Warriors who cultivated fire laws ignited their Vitality and tried to burn through, but the weeds were everywhere.

“Stop! You're burning me!”

“If I don't burn it, we're both dead!”

[SFX: HORRIFIED SCREAMS]

[MUSIC: DARK HORROR]

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

The ghost beast rose slowly from the ground and began to move through the field of bound warriors. It raised one massive palm and brought it down. A warrior split in two. It moved to the next.

[AMBIENT: DARK SHRIEKING]

Then everyone heard it — a sound like the sky itself tearing open.

[AMBIENT: GHOSTLY WAILING HORDE]

From above, a black mass descended. Tens of thousands of vengeful spirits, pouring down toward the grassland.

The faces of the trapped warriors turned to despair.

[MUSIC: URGENT ESCAPE]

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

Zane was already moving. As he ran, he reached back and touched Fiona's shoulder. She had gone rigid, staring at the chaos behind them.

“Don't look,” he said. “Keep moving.”

She turned away and ran.

[SFX: THUNDEROUS CRACK]

They had covered six or seven miles when the sky behind them exploded.

A bolt of lightning so thick it looked like a pillar descended from the heavens and detonated across The Spectral Plains. The shockwave rolled through the ground beneath their feet. When the light faded, the swarm of vengeful spirits was gone — every last one of them, wiped out in a single strike.

Randy stared back at the fading light, a helpless smile on his face. “Fredrick.”

Zane looked at him. “He's that powerful?”

“It's not his strength,” Randy said. “He used the Heavenly Thunder Halberd — a one-use semi-divine weapon. Something like that could wound a Soul Sea Realm warrior. Once it's gone, it's gone.” He paused. “The secret order gives York first pick of everything that comes through the Reverse Spirit Channel. Fredrick came in here carrying treasures most people never even see.”

Zane said nothing. He filed it away.

Everyone maintained a steady pace as they moved through the remaining grasses of the Spectral Plains. Before long, Fiona looked up. “So once we get through here, we can enter the secret land?”

Randy nodded. “Technically, yeah. After the Spectral Plains, we'll reach the center of the island. The entrance to the Feather Emperor's Secret Land should be there.”

The rest of their journey was surprisingly easy. Before long, an incredible sight unfolded before them.

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CHOIR]

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

It was a paradise of intricate jade lines and shimmering light. Every spire glowed with a rainbow of colors. Normally, such a riot of color would feel gaudy, almost cheap, but the place held a strange power that soothed the eye, every hue perfectly balanced.

“It's so beautiful,” Fiona breathed, the first to run forward. Her eyes lit up, a wave of relief washing over the group as the peaceful sight replaced the tension of their journey.

Zane walked up slowly, studying the buildings. The architecture of the Dragon-blooded was stunning, the materials mixed with different colors of luminous sand. The sand absorbed sunlight during the day and glowed at night; even after thousands of years, it still looked dazzling.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

“The entrance is over here.” This wasn't Randy's first time, and the scenery was already familiar to him. He motioned for everyone to follow, leading them toward the entrance to the Feather Emperor's Secret Land.
