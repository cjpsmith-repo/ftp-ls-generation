Ep 381 - Pull it Together

Word count: 1056

[AMBIENT: OCEAN WAVES]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Zane stared out at the Sea of Raging Fire, his stomach twisting into a knot. Heat shimmered across the molten expanse, distorting the air like a mirage. The Cloud Road Trial had turned out to be a whole lot tougher than he'd anticipated.

Finally, it dawned on him - he knew the reason the last Hidden Test had been so brutal.

The Death Road was another hidden trial, concealed within the competition itself, and way more dangerous than the Life Road. It was so hard, it was basically a dead end. So few warriors survived the crossing that the path had earned its grim name through countless deaths. A trial that difficult required more than just skill and cultivation power. Luck played an equally important role. But Zane was betting that the true secret of the Cloud Road—the prize that made all this suffering worthwhile—lay waiting at the end of that treacherous path.

He knew what he had to do.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC SWELL]

He turned to Finn, his voice cutting through the ambient roar of flames. “We're taking the Death Road.”

The stone platform wasn't very big, and crowded with warriors who waited nervously for their turn to choose. When Zane's words reached their ears, heads snapped toward him. Expressions shifted from concentration to pure disbelief. Others simply stared at him, mouths slightly open, as if he'd just declared his intention to walk barefoot across the lava itself.

[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]

"Is he serious?" someone muttered from the crowd. "Frank and Lois are powerhouses, and even they only have a slim chance on the Death Road. What makes a Level Three Adept think he can handle it?"

The question hung in the air, unanswered. Everyone was thinking it, but no one bothered to say a word to Zane directly. What would be the point? They simply watched, letting him seal his own fate. Some looked away, already dismissing him as a corpse. Others kept their eyes fixed on him with morbid curiosity, wondering how long he'd last once the Death Road claimed him.

If he wanted to throw his life away, that wasn't their problem. They had their own survival to worry about.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]

Despite the hundred-plus stones that had already crumbled away on the Life Road, the other warriors were banking on teamwork. They'd seen it work already: just a little while ago, that disciple from the Luminous Sect had used her abilities to temporarily solidify a patch of lava, creating a cool, stable bridge for a few precious seconds. If they could coordinate skills like that—timing everything just right, pooling their resources—they were convinced they could make it across. They were huddled up now, already hashing out a plan.

Finn considered it for a moment. But before he could respond, Lewis, who’d been listening in on the crowd’s chatter, sidled up to Zane. “Great idea!” he shouted. “I’ll take the Death Road, too!”

Zane rolled his eyes. Of course. Getting rid of Lewis was next to impossible. Since the guy had just invited himself, Zane could only give a stiff nod.

“Then count me in,” Finn said, his voice firm. He'd turned Patrick down earlier, but Zane's conviction must have swayed him. Or maybe he just figured his chances were better with Zane than with anyone else.

[SFX: RISING FLAMES]

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

The moment they stepped onto the path, the fiery tide began to rise with ominous speed.

[SFX: PANICKED FOOTSTEPS]

Lewis shrieked, his voice cracking as he scrambled from one stone to the next. ”I don't want to die!" His movements were frantic, uncoordinated—each leap more desperate than the last. "Oh, this is bad! This is really bad!" Sweat poured down his face, though whether from fear or the rising heat, Zane couldn't tell. "I have such a bright future ahead of me! I can't drown in the Sea of Raging Fire! Not like this!"

His wailing grew more hysterical with each word. "Mom, I'm so sorry!" Tears streaked down his cheeks now, mingling with the sweat. "I promised I'd take care of you forever, and now I can't! I'll never see you again!"

Zane felt a flicker of sympathy despite the absurdity of the situation. Lewis's terror was genuine, raw—the kind that stripped away all pretense and left only the vulnerable core of a person exposed. Still, if the guy didn't get himself together soon, his panic would kill them all faster than any lava could.

[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

While Lewis was having his meltdown, Finn had already landed on the next stone. In one fluid motion, he spun around, grabbed Lewis by the collar, and whipped a sword from his back, pressing the cold steel against Lewis’s throat.

[AMBIENT: BUBBLING LAVA]

[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

“Shut up,” he growled, his voice dangerously low. “Or I’ll shut you up for good.”

Zane watched, not surprised. Finn wasn’t a talker like Lewis, and he definitely didn’t have Zane’s patience. The pressure of the Death Road, with the lava swelling around them, was clearly getting to him. Zane knew Lewis’s screaming wasn't just cowardice; it was his bizarre way of coping with stress, like how some people bite their nails or pace. But instead of calming anyone down, his hysterics were just fraying Finn’s nerves—and his own.

“No, don't kill me! I’ll be quiet, I promise! Okay?” Lewis pleaded, his eyes wide.

Zane knew that if Lewis hadn’t been caught so completely off guard, Finn wouldn’t have gotten a hand on him so easily. For all his whining, Lewis was still a skilled warrior.

Finn obviously wasn't really going to kill him. With a low grunt, he shoved Lewis forward onto the stone and leaped ahead without another word.

Zane gave Lewis a small, wry smirk and shook his head before leaping after Finn. Lewis looked absolutely miserable, but he finally shut up. He knew he had no other choice.

[SFX: RISING LAVA SURGE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

As they pressed on, the lava tide crept steadily higher, getting dangerously close. Their hearts hammered against their ribs with every jump. Any higher, and the path—and they—would be swallowed whole. 

They held their breath, their eyes fixed on the stones ahead as the fire surged.

Zane just hoped they could make it in time.
