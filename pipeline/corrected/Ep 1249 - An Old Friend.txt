Ep 1249 - An Old Friend

Word count: 1146

[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: DEADWOOD GATE FOREST]
A shadow fell over Aurora’s usually clear eyes. 
He and Rowan were sizing up Zane for the exact same reason. Any fighter knows to be cautious when facing a powerful opponent—sometimes, that caution even extends to your own damn allies.
 Aurora didn’t know Zane from Adam, and it looked like Rowan was the one who’d brought him into the fold. But if he was as strong as he seemed, why on earth would he agree to help her?
The Deadwood Gate was hallowed ground, a place where fighters came to rack up Illusion Points—millions of them, if you were good. If Zane took control of this place, the Demon Night would be left completely powerless. So, yeah, Aurora had every right to be worried.
“Kill this guy first!” one of the Ogres roared, a giant shield slung over his shoulder. He didn’t wait for a response, just charged.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS CHARGING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
The others followed a half-second later, a thundering wave of muscle and desperation, their burly frames like a moving mountain range rumbling toward Zane.
Rowan, however, didn’t move. She stayed on her side of the branch, making no move to lead her Demon Night members forward. Her lips were moving slightly, as if she were murmuring something to Aurora across the distance, but neither of them made a move.
Amber, on the other hand, was somewhere else entirely, just staring blankly at the young man at the other end of the branch. 
The last time she’d seen him, back when he’d first arrived on the main continent, he was just a kid—lanky, a little immature. Now, he radiated a confidence so powerful it felt like he could wipe out every Ogre in front of him with a single wave of his hand. For a fighter who’d taken down Supreme Lords, what were a few grunts? 
She knew him, and she trusted him completely.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: SHIELD SPLINTERING]
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]
The lead Ogre’s giant shield exploded into splinters. Zane moved like a ghost, weaving between the charging fighters with an eerie grace. He slipped through the smallest gaps in their formation, as fluid and untouchable as a wisp of smoke from the Falling Leaves Array itself.
[SFX: DULL IMPACT]
A single, casual slap to the back of the lead Ogre’s neck sent him crashing to the ground, his body bursting into a shower of golden light — Illusion Points, released and drifting loose in the air.
Before the light could even settle, Zane was already moving again, riding the edge of a second Ogre’s massive saber like he had no fear of the blade at all, spiraling up its length before driving his knee into the warrior’s face. Another burst of golden light exploded outward.
[MUSIC: RISING INTENSITY]
The Illusion Points chased after him in a shimmering trail, never quite catching up, always trailing three inches behind — and somehow, that trailing swarm seemed to be pushing him faster, his Eight Smoky Melodies skill turning their pull into momentum. 
One by one, the remaining Ogres dropped — a muffled thud here, a sharp crack there — each fall marked by another burst of light joining the swarm at his back.
[SFX: REPEATED DULL IMPACTS]
These weren’t weak fighters, either. Even the least of them ranked as a core warrior from a tenth-tier Holy Land, armed with Grade-Two Divine Weapons — a few even carried Grade-One. 
None of it mattered. Not one of them could land a hit on him, and the Demon Night onlookers watching from the sidelines felt a creeping sense of futility, running the math on their own chances against him and coming up empty every time.
With the last Ogre down, Zane’s floating form finally settled, and the trailing swarm of golden light poured into him all at once.
“Nearly a million,” he murmured, eyes flashing with quiet satisfaction as the points settled into him. 
Zane’s gaze slid past the still-rustling Falling Leaves Array and settled on Aurora, taking in the wary look on his face.
So that’s how it is, he thought. You didn’t hold back because you don’t trust me.
It didn’t bother him. He’d already found who he came for.
The moment he took a step forward, the fallen leaves rolled and shifted as one, rearranging themselves into a single, bristling wall — every blade of gold-edged leaf turning to face him.
“Don’t come any closer,” Aurora warned, his pale blue eyes locked on him, fingers trembling on the edge of an attack.
Zane kept walking anyway, that same faint smile never leaving his face, as if his warning hadn’t reached him at all.
Aurora’s jaw tightened, murderous intent flashing in his eyes. Zane was too strong. If Zane broke through the array, there’d be nothing Aurora could do to stop him. He was a breath away from committing to the attack, when Amber stepped into the array first.
“Amber?” Aurora asked, startled, but then he saw the hint of a smile on Amber’s face.
Amber just nodded at him. “Don’t worry,” she said, her voice calm. “He’s an old friend of mine.”
“An *old friend*?” Aurora was floored.
Amber kept nodding, her smile growing wider. “Remember what I told you before? There’s only one person in the entire universe who can change my fate. That’s him.”
The words hit Aurora like a physical blow. During their travels, Amber had told him bits and pieces of her past—how she’d crossed the Queen of Punishment, how she’d been imprisoned and tortured for it. 
She’d said that only one person in the universe could ever save her, a man known as the storyteller of the universe. A man who held a special Roaring Token, a man who could stand against dozens of Supreme Lords and come out on top.
[MUSIC: EPIC REVELATION]
If what Amber was saying was true… that man was Zane.
Even with his detractors, Zane was already a living legend. His accomplishments were the stuff of stories, but his actual strength was a subject of endless debate. 
Some said that as the inheritor of The Elysian Keep, his power was terrifying, putting him on par with the prodigies and saint-level geniuses of the great races. Others argued he was nothing special, just an ordinary guy who got lucky, using powerful items to take down those Supreme Lords. They said he wasn’t a true powerhouse at all.
But watching him now, seeing the effortless power in his movements, Aurora knew. He could absolutely hold his own against the best of them. 
[SFX: HEARTBEAT POUNDING]
His heart hammered against his ribs. The legend was standing right in front of him.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
His voice was barely a whisper when she finally spoke. “Are you the one she was talking about? Are you *that* Zane?”
