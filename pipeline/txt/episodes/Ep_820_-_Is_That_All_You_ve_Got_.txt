Ep 820 - Is That All You've Got?

Word count: 1111

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FLAMES]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

A regular fire spreads its heat out in every direction, burning wild and unpredictable, which is exactly what makes it weak.

The True Phoenix’s Sun Flame was a whole different animal. Its blazing white fire focused every ounce of heat into a tight, searing core, making it hotter, more powerful, and dangerously precise.

[SFX: WHOOSHING FIREBALL]

As the white fireball shot toward him, Zane had only one thought. Well, so much for these clothes.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE FLAMES]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

The idea had barely even formed before the flames swallowed him whole, turning him into a human torch.

[SFX: ROARING INFERNO]

A low, guttural roar echoed from within the inferno, but despite the horrifying sight, Zane was perfectly calm. He didn’t have to worry about his own safety—he was immune to most fire, with the notable exception of the black flames back in the soulforge. The Phoenix’s Sun Flames looked terrifying, sure, but they only channeled the first three layers of the Fire Law. That kind of power couldn’t even scratch him.

Now, if this had been a mature True Phoenix, he’d be in a world of hurt. An adult was a fourteenth or fifteenth-level Greater Legendary Beast, on par with a world lord or even a supreme lord. Forget the flames; a single swipe of its claw would have ground Zane into dust.

[SFX: SIZZLING HEAT]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BUILD]

The temperature around him climbed until it was almost unbearable, and his skin started to glow cherry-red, like a poker pulled from a forge. He looked down at his body, buzzing with anticipation, expecting the runes to appear any second. 

This was the whole point of letting himself get torched—he was hoping the intense heat would finally trigger his body’s transformation into a Relic-Class weapon. With the combined power of several thousand Dragon Scales and a surge of Forceful Energy, his strength would skyrocket. He figured that once he reached the late stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, he’d be able to take down warriors from the Life-and-Death Realm without breaking a sweat.

But that’s not what happened.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

After a full minute of the Sun Flame attack, nothing appeared.

“Huh? Why aren’t they showing up?”

Just getting burned by Phoenix flames wasn’t enough. Golden runes were supposed to materialize, allowing him to absorb the fire completely into his body.

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

He realized it in a flash: the flames weren’t powerful enough. It seemed that even the first three layers of the Fire Law weren’t going to cut it.

The Phoenix has to know more than just three layers, he thought. This couldn’t be its strongest attack. Ever since it appeared, he’d sensed a deeper, more mysterious Fire Law power coiled inside the beast, and he needed it.

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FLAMES]

[SFX: BARRIER RIPPLE]

The Sun Flame kept coming, slamming against the far edge of the barrier. The barrier rippled and buckled under the force — and the Soul Sea master who'd erected it shot a flash of blue light into it to keep it from collapsing. Getting his barrier broken by a Spirit-Tribulation-level fight would have been an embarrassment he couldn't afford.

Up on the steps, the Wind Divine Kingdom warriors had gone quiet. In their minds, Zane was already ash.

“That's what stubbornness gets you. He made our princess feel terrible, and now he's gone.”

“Good riddance. Dying was the least he deserved for what he put her through.”

Fiona sat on Fiery Shine's shoulder, one hand gripping a fistful of feathers. Her mood had sunk to the floor. She knew the cultivator's world was cruel. She knew this was the right choice. But knowing that didn't make it feel any less wrong. Zane Jones had a real future ahead of him. Watching it end like this made her chest ache.

She told herself she'd made the correct call. She told herself her father would be proud.

She didn't entirely believe either.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]

[SFX: FLAMES PARTING]

Then a head emerged from the top of the flames.

“Stupid bird!” Zane called out. “Put more effort!”

“Ah!” Fiona cried out, whipping her head away.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

She had barely registered that he was alive — that he was fine, standing in the middle of a Sun Flame attack without a scratch — before the embarrassment hit her. He had walked straight through the attack toward her, and now there was a very naked man standing much too close.

The crowd stared.

“They're… still fighting?”

“You ask me, who should I ask?”

Fiona, for her part, was not as upset as she should have been that her attack had failed. She was relieved. Genuinely, obviously relieved. And everyone could see it.

By now, his clothes had been completely vaporized. A few of the other female warriors in the square averted their eyes, but some shamelessly checked him out, clearly enjoying the show. Zane felt a flush of embarrassment, but he forced himself to push past it. He had bigger problems to worry about.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]

Fiery Shine had heard the taunt. A Super Divine Beast's intelligence was not to be underestimated, and Fiery Shine was no slave — its relationship with Fiona was something closer to friendship. It tolerated her commands most of the time. But when its dignity was insulted, even Fiona couldn't hold it back.

[SFX: INTENSE HEAT WAVE]

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FLAMES]

Every feather on its body stood on end. The temperature spiked so violently that Fiona herself couldn't stay on it — she dropped away from the phoenix's back, landing at a distance, her face still turned pointedly to the side.

“What were you thinking?” she scolded, her face still turned away. “Why did you have to make it mad? It’s absolutely furious now!”

He didn’t answer. He was past arguing with her. A much more important question was on his mind: this heat was strong, but would it be enough?

[SFX: MAGICAL FIRE RING IGNITION]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Suddenly, a shimmering ring of fire materialized around the Phoenix. In that instant, Zane felt it—a distinct, special kind of Law Power radiating from the attack. His heart leaped. That was it. That was the attack he needed. This was the mysterious power he’d sensed from the very beginning, something on a completely different level from the Sun Flame attack.

[SFX: BARRIER SHATTERING]

[SFX: WHOOSH OF FLIGHT]

The sight of that fire ring sent Fiona into a panic. Her father, War King Wind, was so alarmed that he shattered the arena’s enchanted barrier without a second thought, flew in, and yanked his daughter to safety.
