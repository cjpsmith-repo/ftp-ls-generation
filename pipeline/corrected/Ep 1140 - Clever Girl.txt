Ep 1140 - Clever Girl

Word count: 1092

[MUSIC: TENSE DESCENT]
After the vine section ended, the abyss opened up again — nothing but dark air and the whistle of wind. Then, below them, purple dots of light appeared, floating in the darkness like suspended sparks.
[SFX: SOFT GLOWING HUM]
A warrior snatched one as it passed. A moment’s silence. “It’s a rune. Crush it, and it sends you back to the entrance.”
That was all it took. Purple lights flared one after another as runes were crushed, and the figures wrapped in them shot upward and vanished. In under a minute, the group had been cut in half.
Zane caught one of the floating dots. Its function registered the instant it touched his palm. He looked at Freya.
“What do you want to do?”
Freya closed her fingers around the dot and tucked it away. “I want to hold on a while longer.”
“Then we hold on.”
Lincoln didn’t deliberate. The moment he understood what the rune did, he crushed it. Not to escape — to remove the option. He carried a debt that had no patience for retreat. The Illusion Battlefield was coming, and he intended to be ready. He pinched the rune to dust and kept falling.
The walls of the abyss were perfectly smooth, offering nothing to grab onto. Warriors in the past had tried to smash through them, hoping to punch a hole to safety, but they’d quickly learned it was pointless. A faint, invisible barrier of magic coated the stone, and this group wasn’t nearly strong enough to break it.
Below them, a thin white line appeared across the center of the abyss. As they drew closer it resolved into an enormous blade, edge-up, nearly invisible against the dark. A single saber. Everyone angled past it without difficulty.
Then a second. Then a third.
[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSE]
[SFX: SHARP METALLIC SCRAPING]
The numbers climbed. Four, five, six. Still manageable — until one warrior misjudged and a blade took his arm off clean.
[SFX: AGONIZED CRY]
Blood scattered through the air in a long red trail. Freya watched it and felt her throat tighten. The vines had been painful. This was different.
She tried to adjust her angle for the next blade. Her body wouldn’t cooperate — freefall left almost nothing to push against. She stared at the abyss walls, thinking hard.
Then she had it.
Freya didn’t even try to cling to the wall. Instead, she angled her body to fall about a foot away from it. 
[SFX: GIANT BLADES RISING]
[SFX: SHARP METALLIC SCRAPING]
The moment several giant blades shot up from the darkness below, she slapped her palm flat against the stone. 
[SFX: PALM SLAP AGAINST STONE]
[SFX: RUSHING WIND FREEFALL]
Zane’s eyes lit up. It was genius. In freefall, with no way to fly, dodging the blades was nearly impossible. One wrong move and you’d be sliced in half. Freya had just found a lifeline.
“That girl is clever,” Lavender said quietly. “She found the trick faster than most would.”
The warriors who’d chosen to brave the abyss were a stubborn bunch. The cold glint of the rising blades was terrifying, but they all gritted their teeth and held on. Seeing Freya’s trick, they copied her, adjusting their own descents to get closer to the walls.
The scene descended into controlled chaos. 
[SFX: MULTIPLE BODIES IMPACTING STONE]
[SFX: BLADES WHOOSHING UPWARD]
Bodies hugged the sheer rock face, and every time a new set of blades surged upward, they’d kick or slap the wall, using the impact to shove themselves out of the way. It was their only shot at surviving the gauntlet.
But the deeper they fell, the worse it got. 
[SFX: METAL BLADES SCRAPING]
At first, it was only two or three blades at a time. Soon, it was seven, then eight, then ten, all tangled together in a rising thicket of steel that left them with less and less room to maneuver.
Zane reached out, tapping the wall to send himself drifting twenty feet sideways, aiming for a five-foot gap between two massive blades. If he timed it right, he could slip through clean.
The other warriors watched his trajectory and felt a flicker of pity. In position above that gap was a massive Ogre warrior — one of the top geniuses of the Demon Energy Holy Land, built like a small fortress, with a golden skull mounted on his shoulder. He’d seen Zane angling for the same gap and was already moving to claim it.
Outside the abyss, the Ogre Saint Lord watched through his surveillance array and cursed under his breath. He knew exactly how that collision would end. “Idiot,” he muttered.
But just as Zane adjusted his trajectory, a sickening crunch echoed from beside him. 
[SFX: BODY IMPACT CRUNCH]
An Ogre had slammed too hard into the wall, and the force sent him careening right into Argus. The impact knocked them both away from the gap, and Argus flailed, trying to find another opening. It was too late. 
[SFX: RUSHING METAL BLADES]
Staring at the gleaming blades rushing up to meet him, a look of pure despair washed over his face. 
[SFX: MAGICAL TELEPORT ACTIVATION]
An instant before his doom, he crushed the purple dot of light on his hand, praying the enchantment would teleport him back to the palace entrance before he was shredded.
“Zane!” Freya’s voice cut through the noise from below. “Behind you!”
The massive Ogre had locked onto Zane and was driving his shoulder — and the golden skull — straight at him.
“Get out of my way!” the Ogre roared.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS COLLISION]
The impact rang through the abyss like a bell. The Ogre’s golden skull — a second-grade Divine Weapon, something he’d used to ram a twelfth-level beast to death — shattered on contact with Zane’s body. The Ogre barely had time to register what had happened before the rebound sent them both flying in opposite directions.
Zane, meanwhile, had already calculated the force of the collision. They were like two marbles striking each other. The impact sent him flying in the opposite direction, straight toward another Ogre warrior.
This Ogre was already in position, thinking he was clear to drop safely through an opening below. When he saw Zane hurtling toward him, his eyes went wide. 
[SFX: ANGRY OGRE ROAR]
“Hey! What do you think you’re doing?” he roared.
It wasn’t a matter of what Zane was doing; he couldn’t control it. 
[SFX: HEAVY BODY COLLISION]
[SFX: ARMOR IMPACT]
He crashed into the Ogre, sending the bigger warrior tumbling backward. The collision, however, killed Zane’s momentum perfectly, leaving him hovering directly over the gap he’d been aiming for.
[SFX: METAL BLADES CLOSING]
Suddenly, the giant blades formed a deadly net, closing in on them all.
[SFX: BODIES DROPPING THROUGH GAPS]
Everyone except for Argus and the Ogre that Zane had knocked away dropped through the openings, slipping between the blades to safety.
Argus’s body fell straight toward the steel. 
[SFX: MAGICAL TELEPORT WHOOSH]
In the split second before he was impaled, a shimmering purple light engulfed him and shot back up into the darkness.
