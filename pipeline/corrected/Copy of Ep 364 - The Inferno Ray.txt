They never would’ve risked the landing if they weren’t so desperate. Everyone found a spot to rest, meditating to recover their energy. This was nothing like their practice runs. They’d never faced a real life-or-death situation, never pushed themselves this hard. With muscles screaming and bodies aching, they took their time, hoping to avoid any permanent damage.

The Sea of Raging Fire was deceptively vast. Before the flames pulled back, the surface had looked almost calm. 

[SFX: BUBBLING LAVA]

[AMBIENT: INTENSE HEAT WAVES]

But as the heat rose to meet the cooler air, the lava churned into violent, swirling waves of fire. In the distance, they could see yellow-white flames licking the air where the fire burned hottest. And in those waves, they noticed strange little creatures, tilting their heads and staring back at them curiously. Every so often, one would spit a tiny flame before diving back into the molten waves, swimming like a fish.

These creatures were unlike anything known. They didn't just survive in the scalding lava—it was their home. Taking them from it for too long would probably kill them.

Watching them, Zane was struck by a sense of wonder at how the fire itself sustained them. His curiosity sparked as he thought about just how huge the Sea of Raging Fire was, and what other unknown life might be thriving in this impossible place.

If he were just a bit stronger, he might have risked it all for a chance to explore.

[MUSIC: SOMBER DEFEAT]

Meanwhile, Adler and his injured companion could only shake their heads in defeat. They knew the two women had only been sticking with them for protection, and they couldn't blame them for it. At this point, staying with them would only put the women in more danger. With their minds made up, the two women decided to press on, leaving Adler and the other disciple behind.

Zane pushed himself to his feet, casting one last glance at the mysterious creatures dancing through the flames below. As fascinating as this realm was, he couldn't afford to linger—the trial demanded forward progress, and the others were already moving ahead. He turned toward the next pillar, gathering his strength for the leap that would carry him deeper into the Sea of Raging Fire.

[MUSIC: VENGEFUL RAGE]

[AMBIENT: GRINDING TEETH]

Being abandoned stoked the flames of Adler's hatred for Zane. Grinding his teeth, he muttered, "This is all because of that jerk from the Skyreach Sect. That Level Three Adept. I swear, if I get my hands on him, I'll tear him apart and make him kneel."

[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WIND]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Zane was so far ahead now that he was completely out of sight. Catching up felt impossible. Adler blamed him for his partner’s injury, for them falling so far behind in a competition this important. The helpless waiting just made the hatred fester.

Of course, none of it was Zane’s fault. When they’d first entered the Sea of Raging Fire, he’d even helped them kill the fiery evil spirits and guided them safely to the high platform. But some people always need someone else to blame and can never admit their own mistakes.

Zane had met plenty of guys like Adler. He’d learned it was better to just ignore them than let their negativity screw up his focus during a trial like this.

Roughly eight people were still in this round. But this wasn't a normal competition—here, they needed each other to get past the obstacles. Trying to go it alone was a death sentence, and everyone knew it. So, they teamed up without a second thought, all except for Adler and his companion, who stayed behind.

[SFX: CREATURE ROAR]

[MUSIC: BATTLE DRUMS]

As they leaped from one stone pillar to the next, they kept running into terrifying, strange creatures. Each time, they’d instantly form a defensive circle, attacking from all sides.

It was obvious everyone here was the best their order had to offer; no one weak would have made it this far. Even the two women from the Luminous Sect were incredibly fast and agile, dodging attacks while striking back with deadly precision. Working together, the group reached the next high platform with little trouble.

This is how you do it, Zane thought as they pushed forward. Those two guys from the Duskfall Sect were idiots. It was crazy to ever think of them as teammates. 

In reality, no one here thought of the others as teammates. Any truce was temporary, born from necessity. It wouldn't be a surprise to anyone when the final competition started, and they all drew swords on each other.

[SFX: DRAMATIC STING]

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS RUMBLE]

[AMBIENT: BUBBLING LAVA]

[SFX: CREATURE MOVEMENT THROUGH LAVA]

The creature broke the surface in a spray of molten rock—an inferno ray, its body a writhing mass of crimson scales that pulsed with inner fire. 

[SFX: SWORD STRIKE]

One of the competitors raised his sword and struck at the approaching monster, his blade flashing in the firelight. The man's weapon connected with the creature's tail in a shower of sparks, but the impact barely left a scratch.

As the others watched, mesmerized, while the man’s second attack failed, the inferno ray closed the distance, its tail lashing out to strike him.

Getting dragged into the burning lava would be an instant death sentence.

While the others stood dumbstruck, Zane sensed the danger. 

[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]

[MUSIC: HEROIC ORCHESTRAL]

[SFX: MONSTER SHRIEK]

[SFX: LAVA SPLASH]

Only then did the man realize what had happened. Zane had just saved his life. He would be dead if it weren't for him. Utterly awestruck, all he could do was stare.
