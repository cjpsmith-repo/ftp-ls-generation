Ep 532 - Suppressive Force
Word count: 1111
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORCHESTRAL]
Nine orbs of light pulsed around the Celestial Tomb of Vesper. With each pulse, their light intensified, and from eight of them, warriors began to appear.
Just as the rumors had predicted, most of them were from the Heartlands, all but confirming that the Enchanted Lamps would fall into the hands of the major factions.
The smaller clans and factions didn't put up a fight. They knew the lamps were useless without incredible strength anyway. Trying to challenge the top masters from the Heartlands for the tomb’s treasures wasn't just a long shot—it was a death wish. The smarter play was to trade whatever they found to the major factions for something they could actually use.
This time, nine major powers were descending upon the Celestial Tomb of Vesper: the Cloud Hall, the World Merchants' Guild, Bloodwood Cliff, the Mysterious Luna Hall, and the Black Mountain Order, along with a few reclusive, independent warriors. Some of these masters had even reached the legendary Life-and-Death Realm.
Most warriors hailed from the Heartlands. All except one unusual team.
They arrived through a shimmering spatial rift after the others had already descended into the tomb.
[SFX: SPATIAL RIFT OPENING]
A woman in black robes stumbled out. "Is this the Celestial Tomb of Vesper?"
A man emerged behind her, surveying the area with confidence. "The one and only."
"But didn't you say we could only enter once all nine lamps were lit? Where is everyone?"
He smiled dismissively. "Nine lamps, nine entrances. The other eight ended up in the Heartlands—ants, every last one of them. We only need to find what we came for."
Her expression hardened. "I don't care about the treasures or the Flower of Life and Death. I only want that one thing. And I'll kill anyone who tries to take it from me."
Without hesitation, she walked straight into the passage. The man and his guards followed.
They were from the Divine Kingdoms—a civilization so vast it dwarfed the Heartlands entirely. The Heartlands, together with the surrounding domains stretching in every direction, was itself an almost unimaginably large expanse. And yet to the Divine Kingdoms, it was barren. A backwater. Barely worth acknowledging.
One of the man's ancestors had obtained an Enchanted Lamp two thousand years ago, and the family had guarded that secret ever since, terrified the Divine Kingdoms' powers would seize it.
But the tomb had other ideas.
[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
Suddenly, the air grew thick, and an immense pressure crashed down on them, suffocating their power.
"What's happening?" The woman stopped short.
The man's smile faltered. "This suppressive force—our cultivation is being suppressed!"
A Life-and-Death guard spoke grimly. "A powerful formation. There is no way to resist it."
Every warrior who entered the passage felt it—Life-and-Death masters, Spirit-Tribulation warriors, Inner-Core cultivators. None were spared.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
Back in the Cloud Hall's passage, the same force had already taken hold. No one was immune.  No one was immune. A slow, creeping panic began to set in as they were all caught in its grip.
It didn't matter if they were masters of the Life-and-Death, Spirit-Tribulation, or Inner-Core Realms—the dominating aura forced them all into submission.
[SFX: ICE CRACKLING AND FIZZLING]
Ice crystals sputtered between Tia’s slender fingers before fizzling out. A frustrated scowl twisted her features as she struggled against the force. "My power… it's been suppressed by a full realm," she said, her voice tight with disbelief. "I can only muster the strength of a mid-stage Inner-Core warrior."
It was a crushing blow. She had been at the middle stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, and now she was knocked all the way back down to the Inner-Core Realm. She clenched her fist, a shiver of fear running through her. Her eyes darted to the others, silently asking if she was alone in this.
Judging by the grim expressions on everyone's faces, she wasn't. The elders of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, like Toby, were now no stronger than Inner-Core Realm warriors.
Even Elder Imogen, a master of the Life-and-Death Realm, could now only use the power of a beginner-stage Spirit-Tribulation warrior.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
A chill cut through the group. This massive drop in power was a fatal weakness, leaving them all terrifyingly vulnerable. For a warrior, strength was everything. Without their full cultivation, they wouldn't survive the dangers lurking in the Celestial Tomb of Vesper.
Lost in their own frustration and dread, no one noticed Zane. He should have stuck out—his cultivation was the lowest of them all. He had only just broken through to the seventh level of the Soul Realm. If the suppression had hit him like everyone else, his power would have plummeted to the seventh grade of the Adept Realm.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
But miraculously, or maybe by pure luck, the suppressive force hadn't touched him.
Zane stared, baffled, as he felt his own power thrumming inside him, completely normal. He was stunned by the fear on his companions' faces; he didn’t feel a thing.
[SFX: MAGICAL HEAT PULSE]
He glanced around, trying to see if anyone else was unaffected, but his attention was snagged by a sudden, intense heat coming from his Storage Ring. He handled it carefully. It was the one Tia had given him—designed for a woman, so he’d never worn it.
One Storage Ring couldn't be placed inside another; the backlash would create an ethereal tempest, destroying everything inside. He had no idea what was causing the reaction, but he wasn't about to question the good news: whatever was in that ring, it was making him immune.
Zane was in the Soul Realm, but he had the strength to take on Inner-Core Realm warriors. That meant, right now, he could probably crush the Spirit-Tribulation masters who’d been knocked down to his level.
Here, in the Celestial Tomb of Vesper, he could actually fight them.
A faint smirk touched his lips. For now, he’d keep that little secret to himself, especially while traveling with the Cloud Hall.
[AMBIENT: ECHOING FOOTSTEPS IN CORRIDOR]
[MUSIC: BUILDING SUSPENSE]
After the initial shock wore off, the team pulled themselves together and pushed onward. There was nothing to be done about their diminished power. All they could do was hope their combat experience would see them through whatever lay ahead. They moved cautiously down the long corridor, finally reaching an entrance.
[SFX: POWERFUL WIND BLAST]
Just as a flicker of relief passed through the group, a frigid blast of wind erupted from the entrance, forcing them back a step.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC DANGER CRESCENDO]
"Careful!" Elder Imogen shouted, her voice sharp with alarm. "Something's coming!"
