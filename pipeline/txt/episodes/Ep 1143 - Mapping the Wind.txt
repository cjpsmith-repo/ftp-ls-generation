Ep 1143 - Mapping the Wind

Word count: 1170
  
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ANTICIPATION]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING CAVE WINDS]
Lavender claimed she had a plan to get the seed, so Zane figured his best move was to trust her. He kept his mouth shut and watched from the sidelines.
Most of the other warriors seemed to know their chances of snagging the Seed of the World Tree were pretty much zero, so they started aiming for the lesser treasures instead. 
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
One by one, they’d pick a prize, find a stone platform, and take a leap of faith, letting the currents of wind carry them off. From there, it was all up to luck.
After a moment, a striking blonde woman from the Demon Night strode onto one of the platforms. 
[SFX: SACHET TAP]
[SFX: MIST RELEASE]
But instead of jumping, she pulled a small, scented sachet from her Storage Ring and gave it a gentle tap. A faint pink fog immediately drifted out.
Even with the wind roaring through the cave, the sweet, refreshing scent reached everyone, cutting right through the stale air. 
[SFX: CROWD MURMURING]
A confused murmur went through the crowd. What was she doing?
“Hey, I used to do the same thing,” Lavender murmured beside him. “Someone must have taught her that old trick.”
Zane kept his eyes glued to the woman. “Is she mapping the wind currents?”
“Exactly,” Lavender said with a nod.
The pink fog poured down into the wind tunnel below the platform, leaving a clear trail. 
[SFX: SWIRLING WIND]
It swirled through the massive cavern, snaking around a few stalagmites before abruptly changing direction in midair and drifting toward the far side of the cave.
“She’s showing the exact path of the wind!” someone whispered nearby.
“That’s brilliant,” another added. “If she tests enough platforms, she’s bound to find the right one.”
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS CREEPING]
Impressed, several warriors started creeping closer to her platform, ready to jump the second she found the right path. After all, there was only one Seed of the World Tree, and nobody wanted to miss their shot.
She worked quickly and systematically. Each time she brushed her fingers across the sachet, a different colored mist drifted down — pink, then gold, then pale green — each one tracing a new trajectory through the cave, curling around stalagmites, splitting at junctions, fading as it reached the far wall. 
Every warrior in the cave watched and memorized. Platform after platform. The trails multiplied, filling the air with faint colored threads like a map drawn in smoke.
And then she finished. Over a hundred platforms tested. Every wind path charted.
“Not a single one leads to the top,” someone said.
It was true. Every trail ended at the far wall or the cave floor. Not one reached the ceiling where the seed hung. 
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Anxiety started to bubble in Zane’s chest. “What’s the plan?” he asked Lavender. 
But Lavender just smiled, completely unbothered. “No wind tunnel leads directly to the top. That’s not how it works. You have to change paths mid-flight — read the currents, push off the stalagmites, redirect yourself. The seed isn’t at the end of any one path. You have to build your own route to reach it.”
Zane wasn’t the only one working it out; the realization spread through the cave at the same time. The warriors who’d been waiting for the golden-haired woman to find the answer now understood there was no answer to find. You had to go in and figure it out yourself.
[MUSIC: RISING DETERMINATION]
Lincoln was already moving. He’d spent the entire time the golden-haired woman was testing memorizing every trajectory — not to find a direct path, but to plan a sequence. He knew which stalagmites were where, which currents ran which directions, and what force he’d need to push off each one. He chose his platform, glanced at Zane, and said, “Brother Zane — I’ll go first.”
Without waiting for a reply, he jumped.
[SFX: RUSHING WIND SURGE]
The wind caught him immediately, sweeping him along the first path. As he passed a stone pillar, he shot out his hand and snatched a pale yellow flower from its face — a rare heavenly herb, something he’d spotted and noted from the start — then used the impact of his grip to slam himself sideways into a new current.
[SFX: STONE IMPACT CRACK]
He was already on his second wind path. A crack split the air between his brows, and a pale green stone flared to life as he activated his Wrath of the Ocean. Riding the technique’s power, he launched himself upward off a stalagmite like a fish jumping upstream, arcing toward the cave ceiling.
[MUSIC: EPIC SURGE]
“He’s close!” someone shouted. “He’s almost at the World Tree!”
“Damn it, he’s going to get there first!”
[SFX: BODY IMPACT]
[SFX: VIOLENT GUST]
But then an invisible gust shot out from the side, slamming into him and knocking him violently off course. 
[SFX: HEAVY THUD]
A second later, he landed in a heap on the far side of the cave. All he could do was glare back at the prize he’d missed, his jaw tight with frustration.
A collective sigh went through the remaining warriors. Part of them was relieved—with Lincoln out, they still had a chance. But seeing him fail so spectacularly was a brutal reminder of just how slim their own odds were.
Now everyone understood. You couldn’t just ride a current to the top — you had to change paths, use the stalagmites, and build momentum. Lincoln had nearly done it. Which meant it was possible. Which meant none of them could justify staying put.
One after another, the warriors who’d been hesitating made their choice and jumped. Some had clearly been watching and thinking — they changed directions at the right moments, redirected themselves across two or three wind paths. But even the best of them fell short, an invisible gust catching them before they could reach the ceiling.
[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND TUNNELS]
Fueled by a mix of desperation and a sliver of hope, the rest of the warriors started taking the plunge, leaping into the wind tunnels and giving it everything they had. A few got lucky, picking the right current at the perfect angle, but even they couldn’t get close to the seed.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
Before long, most of them had ended up on the far side of the cave, empty-handed. Only Zane, Freya, Adrian, and a handful of others were left on the starting platforms.
Adrian’s shoulders slumped, his face a mask of defeat. He’d planned this trip to the Underground Palace for so long, but he’d never imagined this. The place was full of opportunities, but you had to be strong enough to actually seize them—and he clearly wasn’t. 
Freya glanced over at Zane. 
“I’m going, too,” she said, her voice resigned. She wasn’t aiming for anything specific anymore. At this point, she’d be happy with whatever treasure she could get her hands on.
