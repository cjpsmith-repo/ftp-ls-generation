Ep 1219 - The Happy Sentinels

Word count: 979

[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT PERCUSSION]
[AMBIENT: STONE CHAMBER ECHOES]
The four stone sentinels moved in perfect, deadly sync, their long staffs carving through the air—a thrust, a chop, a stab, a sweep.
They weren’t blindingly fast or overwhelmingly strong, but as Zane watched, he saw their true threat: their technique was flawless. The overlapping shadows of their staffs wove a net around him, cutting off every possible escape.
*Incredible staff work,* he thought. But flawless technique wasn’t everything.
He took a single, deliberate step and vanished, reappearing an instant later directly behind one of the statues.
[SFX: RAPID STAFF WHOOSHES]
He may have teleported, but three of the staffs changed course in a blur, their shadows streaking toward his new position. Zane didn’t even flinch. His right hand shot out and slammed against the back of the sentinel before him.
[SFX: STONE SHATTERING]
With a sound like splitting rock, the statue shattered. As it fell, Zane was already in motion. 
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
He used his Sword Step to vault over the three incoming staffs, then teleported mid-air, materializing behind the next target.
He struck it with the same brutal efficiency.
[SFX: STONE SHATTERING]
Their teamwork was perfect, their technique undeniable. But they were still just stone. Zane knew that even if a few of those staffs connected, his own durability meant they wouldn’t do much more than sting.
He brought his palm down again. And again.
[SFX: STONE CRACKING]
[SFX: RUBBLE FALLING]
In moments, all four statues were nothing but piles of rubble.
Zane’s gaze shifted to the back of the chamber. 
[SFX: HEAVY STONE DOOR OPENING]
“Another sanctum inside this one?” he muttered. From the outside, the sanctuary hadn’t looked that big. He was starting to realize this place was a lot bigger on the inside.
[AMBIENT: DEEP SANCTUM REVERB]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL TONES]
Despite his unease, Zane stepped through the doorway.
Inside, he found another group of four stone sentinels waiting.
But these four were different. The first set had been severe, their stone eyes filled with a grim, demon-slaying purpose. These sentinels, however, looked… happy. 
[MUSIC: WARM UPLIFTING STRINGS]
Their arms were raised in celebration, their faces carved into expressions of pure joy. As Zane studied them, a strange sense of ease washed over him, bleeding off the statues like warmth from a fire.
*The Happy Sentinels,* he realized, a name he’d only read about. He never expected to see them outside of The Hallowed Concord. 
[SFX: STONE CRACKING]
Cracks spread across all four statues at once. They leapt from their pedestals, sleeves fluttering as though caught in a wind that wasn’t there, smiles never wavering as they closed in.
[SFX: WHISTLING FINGER STRIKES]
A storm of finger strikes lanced out at him, each one aimed with unsettling precision at a vital point. Zane didn’t bother wasting time dodging every one — with sixteen more of these chambers likely waiting ahead of him, if the pattern held, he wasn’t about to burn effort here. He let his own durability absorb what he could, only shifting aside when a strike came in dangerous enough to matter.
“Break for me!”
[SFX: DOUBLE PALM STRIKE]
[SFX: STONE SHATTERING]
He drove both palms forward at once. The four Happy Sentinels shattered on impact, rubble scattering across the chamber floor.
[AMBIENT: STONE DOOR GRINDING OPEN]
The door ahead opened the moment the last statue fell. Zane pressed on without slowing down.
Zane blazed through chamber after chamber - with each chamber, the guardians grew stronger and stranger. By the eighth, Zane understood the scale of what he’d entered - not a shrine, but a test designed to push warriors through escalating trials. Eight chambers down, with no idea how many more waited below.
The count, it turned out, ran all the way to eighteen.
By the time Zane cleared the sixteenth chamber and stepped into the seventeenth, he could feel real pressure building in his chest for the first time since entering the temple. He’d come this far without a single misstep, but he knew better than to get comfortable — plenty of warriors fell right at the final stretch. Thundergust was already in his hand as he sized up what stood waiting for him.
[SFX: WHOOSHING SHADOW PALMS]
[SFX: BODY SHOVED BACK]
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
But as he stepped closer, a dozen shadowy palms materialized out of thin air and shoved him back.
“Come on!” A glint flashed in Zane’s eyes.
He unleashed Thundergust. 
[SFX: WIND BLADES WHOOSHING]
[SFX: ETHEREAL RUMBLE]
A storm of wind blades erupted around him, each one spinning with the deadly grace of a thrown knife, yet floating on the air as lightly as a flower petal. Behind them all, the faint shadow of a Nether God began to form.
[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACKLING]
Bolts of lightning followed, slower than the blades but arcing directly toward their targets and crashing against the four Tiger-Taming Sentinels.
[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE ORCHESTRAL]
The room exploded with power. Zane stood at the center of it all, the eye of the hurricane, his expression calm, the sleeves of his robes not even fluttering in the chaos.
[SFX: NETHER GOD SHADOWS DISPERSING]
A wave of Nether God Shadows shot ahead of the storm — and were smashed apart before they even reached the four statues, the Tiger-Taming Sentinels’ defense too fast and too absolute to get past.
The four stone sentinels returned fire with everything they had. 
[SFX: STONE GRINDING]
[SFX: TIGER ROARS ECHOING]
[SFX: PALM STRIKES WHOOSHING]
Each of the Tiger-Taming Sentinels channeled power into their palms, and with every strike, the faint image of a fierce tiger leaped out. In an instant, hundreds of palm strikes filled the air. As they attacked in unison, it was like being surrounded by a thousand roaring tigers, their spectral forms clawing at his storm of blades.
The two forces met in the middle of the room, grinding against each other in a perfect, violent stalemate.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DEEP DRONE]
It was then that deep within the ancient sanctuary, something ancient stirred.
[SFX: HEAVY STONE EYES OPENING]
[AMBIENT: VAST CHAMBER ECHO]
A massive figure, the Gilded Warden, opened its eyes. In one hand, it held a sword; in the other, a single lotus flower. Its expression was serene, utterly untroubled by the raw power of Zane’s aura washing through the halls.
The Warden’s thoughts echoed in the deep quiet of its chamber. *Another one. A practitioner of the Divine Path of Sealed Emotion. That path has been lost for a hundred ages. Why have so many appeared, one after another, all of a sudden?*
