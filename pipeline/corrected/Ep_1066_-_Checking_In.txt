Ep 1066 - Checking In

Word count: 1172

[AMBIENT: CROWDED HALL]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Everyone held their breath as Yolande faced off against Harvey.

Yolande wasn’t one to argue. She respected other people’s opinions, as long as they returned the favor. And even though she was the leader of the Mist Palace, she spent most of her time training in silence and didn’t let the title go to her head.

The reason Harvey was always so aggressive, always stirring up trouble, was simple: it was a power grab for control of the palace.

This time, though, Yolande didn’t stay quiet. Her voice was firm as she cut through the noise. “Who’s in charge here?”

After all, she was the leader of the Mist Palace.

The other vice leaders didn’t really disagree. They all knew the Red Cloud Ring had probably just malfunctioned—it likely couldn’t get an accurate read on Zane’s ability. But they also knew Zane had more than enough skill and power for Passing the Torch. If he wasn’t qualified, the ring would have rejected him outright. Really, they were just curious to know how many lotus petals he’d actually activated.

Harvey, on the other hand, was just trying to start something, as usual.

With Yolande taking a firm stance, a flash of malice crossed his eyes, but he knew better than to say anything more.

As for the martial artists who hadn’t made the cut, they wouldn’t dare open their mouths in front of Yolande. There was no point in crying over spilled milk.

“The Red Cloud Ring’s selection is over,” Yolande announced. “The three hundred and fifty-six of you who passed, follow me.”

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]

With that, she turned and flew off in a particular direction. The martial artists from Jasper’s Cloudswallow Hall exchanged curious glances before following close behind. The other vice leaders led their own people to do the same.

Zane and Layla were the last to go.

The two of them were something of an oddity in the Mist Palace. Officially, they were warriors of the Groundstone Hall, but in reality, their strength rivaled that of Cloudswallow Hall warriors. More surprising still, both were in the Life-and-Death Realm—and what was almost impossible to believe was that Zane was only at the fifth level.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL PALACE]

[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]

Following Yolande, the group left the Mist Palace and stepped into a teleportation tunnel.

For the next sixty hours, Yolande led the massive group of several hundred through the channel. Even moving this fast, crossing the Upper World would normally take years; the distance they covered was just a drop in the bucket compared to the sheer vastness of it all.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING ALIEN CITY]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORIENTAL THEME]

When they finally emerged from the tunnel, a sprawling city filled with strange, spiraling towers rose up before them. It wasn’t their final destination, just a layover. But while it was just a stop for the martial artists, tens of millions of people called the foreign city home.

[SFX: SHIP BOARDING]

[AMBIENT: HARBOR SOUNDS]

After landing, everyone boarded a large, foreign-looking ship. Once again, they entered a supreme world channel to continue their journey.

[AMBIENT: SHIP INTERIOR]

There was no need to spend Vitality flying on the ship, so Zane found a quiet spot, closed his eyes, and cultivated. Between cycles, his thoughts drifted to the lower realm, and he reached into the memory thread still woven through the World’s Will— the one connection he’d kept after ascending. He couldn’t use it to act on anything down there, but he could still watch.

He checked on Amber first. She had already passed her tribulation and ascended. It had likely gone the same way his had — the world pushing her out, whether she was ready or not. With her talent, she’d find her footing in the Demon Night.

He found Tia next.

For a moment, he just sat there, not moving. Then, slowly, a smile crossed his face — stunned and disbelieving, but real. Tia was pregnant. She had known before he left. He traced back through the memories carefully and saw it clearly: she’d known for days and said nothing. He’d ascended before she had the chance — or maybe before she’d found the words.

He was going to be a father.

[MUSIC: BITTERSWEET STRINGS]

The joy came first, warm and immediate. Then the weight of it settled in. He was up here. She was down there. There was no going back, not anytime soon. She would face all of it alone — the pregnancy, whatever came after. He stayed with the feeling for a long moment before letting it go. A warrior’s path was full of these losses. You couldn’t hold every good thing at once.

He checked one more time, scanning for any sign of Cora in the lower realm. Nothing. She’d already made her way up. He’d find out soon enough where she’d landed.

[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Lately, she’d been pushing back harder and harder against her training, and her constant whining was starting to get on the nerves of the other disciples in the Apeiron Tower. She was acting like a spoiled brat.

Cora hadn’t been in the Apeiron Tower for long. Compared to the thousands of years many warriors spent training, her few months there were just the blink of an eye. But in that short time, she had already shattered several records, and her raw talent made the other disciples sick with jealousy.

Her success was largely thanks to her special Violet-Core Physique. The bodies of ordinary martial artists just couldn’t compare, and the training methods of the Violet Core Realm were practically tailor-made for it, giving her a massive advantage.

What really floored everyone, though, was that Cora wanted to quit after all that. If she gave up now, all that incredible talent would just go to waste.

Of course, Elder Apeiron wasn’t about to let that happen. Even though she’d been away for a few days, she’d left a few other elders in charge of looking after Cora. She treated the girl like a precious treasure, so it was no wonder Cora was the target of so much jealousy.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE FLUTE]

“Cora, the path to the pinnacle of martial arts is paved with perseverance and willpower,” an elder said, trying to reason with her. “If you give up this easily, how will you ever achieve anything?”

[SFX: FRUSTRATED SIGH]

“I don’t want to achieve anything else!” Cora pouted. “I have a husband! I’m just a normal girl!”

Another elder shook her head. “I know your husband is no ordinary man. But the world of martial arts is cruel. Even a husband and wife have to be equals. You need to be strong enough to keep up with his journey. If you want to stay by his side, you have to put in the work now!”

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL CRESCENDO]

“Zane isn’t like that!” Cora cried, cutting her off. She just couldn’t stand the monotonous grind anymore.

She had no idea he already knew.
