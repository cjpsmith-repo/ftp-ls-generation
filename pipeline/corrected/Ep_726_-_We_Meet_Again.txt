Ep 726 - We Meet Again
Word count: 1047
Cora had made it to Cloudhaven, but Cloud Hall's barrier stood between her and Zane — and the guard at the gate had just told her, for the last time, that Zane wasn't there.
Cora just floated, staring at the barrier, lost in a world of her own. No one had a clue what was going on in that head of hers.
[AMBIENT: GATE AREA]
Back at the gate, the guard who’d dealt with Cora rejoined his partner.
“Dude, what were you thinking?” the other guard said, nudging him. “That girl is gorgeous. Why didn’t you talk to her more?”
The first guard just grunted. “Give me a break. She’s here for Zane. The thing is, she looks like she’s from some high-class family, so I don’t get why she’s here all by herself. Makes me wonder what Zane did to have her show up at Cloud Hall with no backup.”
“Hmm, you think we should tell Tia?” his partner asked.
A third guard chimed in.“Nah, let’s leave it for now. She's been swamped lately. Our job is just to watch the door and make sure no one gets in.”
As they whispered, none of them could take their eyes off Cora. Guard duty was a painfully boring job, and getting to stare at a woman as elegant and stunning as she was felt like a blessing.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY BUILDING]
After a long moment with her head bowed, Cora looked up and flicked her wrist. A crack tore across the sky, and from it, a massive sword descended, slashing down toward the Cloud Hall’s barrier.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: ENERGY RIPPLES]
With a deafening boom, the sword slammed into the shimmering shield. Ripples of energy flared across the surface of the Light of Water barrier, a sight that made the guards’ jaws drop.
“Holy crap!”
They were completely stunned. They never imagined a beautiful woman like her would be bold enough to straight-up attack Cloud Hall.
“What’s going on?” one of them yelled, staring at the spectral sword still pressed against their defenses. “Doesn’t she know Cloud Hall is a fifth-grade sect?” She was basically asking for a death wish.
“Hey, you! Stop that right now!” another guard shouted, drawing his weapon. “You don’t want to cause trouble here. Trust me. You keep this up, and you’re not leaving here alive!”
[SFX: MASSIVE SWORD BARRAGE]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
But it was like she couldn’t even hear him. She just kept going. This time, ten cracks split the sky, and ten swords shot out, each one screaming down toward the barrier. They hammered against the shield like a torrential downpour, the impact ten times stronger than before. The noise didn’t just rattle the guards; it sent a shockwave through the entire sect.
Within seconds, two stewards from the Inner-Core Realm rushed out of the main entrance, their eyes narrowing as they took in the scene. “Who are you,” one demanded, his voice sharp with authority, “and why are you causing trouble at Cloud Hall?”
[SFX: HUNDRED SWORDS MATERIALIZING]
Cora didn’t say a word. She just looked at them. Then, with another glance at the stewards and the terrified guards, she summoned over a hundred swords that filled the sky above her. And then she let them fall.
[SFX: THOUSAND SWORDS ROARING]
Then a thousand.
[AMBIENT: CLOUD HALL INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Inside Cloud Hall, Zane felt it mid-conversation with Finn — a shockwave through the barrier strong enough to mean only one thing.
He was moving before the thought finished. Tia, Saint Morphen, and a cluster of elders converged from different directions, all arriving at the entrance at the same moment.
"Only a Life-and-Death warrior shakes the barrier like that," Zane said.
Neither of them said Maddox's name.
[SFX: TEN THOUSAND SWORDS MATERIALIZING]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
Outside, ten thousand swords materialized.
Tia's expression went flat. "Who dares attack Cloud Hall?" She had already decided — whoever it was, they would be dead before those swords landed.
Then Zane recognized them.
[SFX: POWERFUL SHOCKWAVE]
Just as the world was about to erupt in chaos, a massive shockwave blasted through the area from behind the gate, and Zane suddenly appeared.
"Stop!" The word cracked out before he'd even thought it.
Cora's face lit up the instant she heard his voice — just for a moment. Then she saw his expression. The light went out. Every sword dissolved.
[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]
[MUSIC: TENSE EMOTIONAL]
He stared at Cora, his face revealing nothing. Then he whirled around, his glare landing on the guards.
Terrified, they stumbled over their words trying to explain. “Sir, we didn’t know you were back! We told her you were still far away, that’s why—” They were beyond scared. If the barrier had been destroyed on their watch, the punishment would have been severe.
Zane just snorted, dismissing them. He turned his attention back to Cora. “What are you doing here?”
Everyone else was wondering the same thing. Why was this woman so desperate to find him?
Cora seemed to consider the question for a moment, then simply shook her head. “I don’t know.”
Nearby, Saint Morphen and the other elders exchanged amused glances. They’d been around long enough to know when a personal drama was unfolding. It was pretty clear Zane had gotten into some trouble with a girl on the outside, and now she’d followed him home. It wasn’t long ago that Saint Morphen had seen Zane embracing Tia, so he settled in, curious to see how this was all going to play out.
Of course, it wasn’t their problem to solve. It was Zane’s.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Tia, however, was studying Cora with a complicated mix of emotions. “Are you,” she asked slowly, “the young lady from House Kensington?”
The way Cora had summoned so many swords was just like how Marilyn had confronted Zane years ago. It made Tia suspect she had a Violet-Core Physique. But Cora was only at the primary level of the core-forged realm, and she could already summon this many blades. She was clearly much stronger than Marilyn. There were only three known people with a Violet-Core Physique in the Heartlands, and two of them were with Maddox. Since the third was Elder Xander’s great-granddaughter, Tia’s guess seemed to be the only logical conclusion.
Seeing that she’d been found out, Cora gave a slight nod. “Yes. I am Cora.”
She seemed to expect that announcing her name would earn her some respect. Instead, Tia just gave her a strange look. “So you came all the way to Cloud Hall to find Zane… but you don’t know why?”
