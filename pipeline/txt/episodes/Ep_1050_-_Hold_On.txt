Ep 1050 - Hold On

Word count: 1113

[AMBIENT: HALL OF ARMS]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The other warriors in the Hall of Arms couldn’t believe what they were seeing. From their perspective, Zane was making it look effortless, climbing higher and higher.

Ryder, along with the white-eyebrowed elder, also sensed something was strange about the display.

Earlier, when Ryder had made his own ascent, he’d also intentionally held back, saving his strength for the upper reaches. But his reasons had been entirely different from Zane’s.

And even while holding back, Ryder had felt the immense pull of gravity once he reached his target. Unlike Zane, he hadn’t been able to stay composed or maintain a steady pace.

Which meant Zane wasn’t even close to his limit.

Ryder had reached one hundred ninety feet during his own attempt. But he hadn’t been able to stay there — he simply didn’t have the power to hold that height and chase the red orbs at the same time. The two things together were beyond him.

Zane was already past that.

[SFX: MAGICAL HUMMING]

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL ATMOSPHERE]

Small white orbs of light flitted all around Zane now, each one containing a Grade-Two Divine Weapon.

A few orbs even drifted across his face. He could have reached out and snatched one without a second thought.

But his gaze remained fixed upward, and the chaos energy roiling inside him only spun faster.

The Grade-Two weapons hovered at one hundred sixty feet. At one hundred eighty feet, Grade-One Divine Weapons. At one hundred ninety feet, only four red dots of light remained—the very Grade-One Divine Weapons Ryder had paid four hundred Sovereign Stones for.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

But the one Zane had chosen, a single blue dot of light, floated at a staggering two hundred feet, dancing just shy of the dome’s ceiling.

[SFX: POWER SURGE]

“Rise.”

[AMBIENT: INTENSE GRAVITATIONAL PRESSURE]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

The gravitational pull was becoming immense.

Every upward inch was a struggle. He maintained a delicate balance, forcing his chaos energy to spin at a dizzying speed while drawing on the power of his Dragon Scales to fight back. Deep within his body, the scales began to light up, one by one, like a chain of stars.

[SFX: MAGICAL RESONANCE]

Five hundred Dragon Scales.

A thousand.

Two thousand.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT CRESCENDO]

By the time the four-thousandth scale flared to life, he’d already reached one hundred ninety feet.

If he shifted his course now, he could easily claim one of the four Grade-One Divine Weapons.

But he didn’t.

He ran the numbers quickly. In the final thirty feet approaching the ceiling, the gravity doubled every ten feet. To keep pace, he’d need to keep doubling his Dragon Scales. At two hundred feet, if the gravity doubled again, he might not survive it. His body could withstand six thousand scales. He could push to eight thousand, but his body couldn’t carry that load.

He’d have to try anyway.

He hadn’t come this far to take the safer prize. He’d set a goal. A warrior only grows by pushing past what should be possible.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]

“That guy’s real target is Thundergust.”

“Seriously? If he just grabs a Grade-One weapon, he’d be set! But he doesn’t seem to want them.”

“Are you kidding me? If he’s got the power to get a better one, why would he settle for less?”

The warriors below were finally catching on. Zane had the strength to actually do this.

The hall had gone very quiet. Some warriors had stopped breathing. A few had their fists clenched without realizing it.

Taking a Grade-One Divine Weapon from this dome had happened before — rarely, but it had happened. Taking a former Palace Master’s weapon had never happened. Not once, in all the years the Hall of Arms had stood.

If they were about to watch it happen, that was worth remembering.

[SFX: DRAGON SCALES IGNITING]

He lit six thousand Dragon Scales.

The power surged through him like a dam breaking — flooding his limbs, his bones, every channel in his body. Something cracked audibly from deep within him.

[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]

“Some kind of secret technique—”

“Whatever it is, the dome’s going to match it. The stronger he gets, the harder it pulls.”

“Just watch.”

The gravity surged — but Zane’s power outpaced it, just for a moment. At the same time, he pushed Wind Law through his Chaos Energy, and his ascent speed multiplied.

The space up here was wrong. Not air — something thicker. Every breath pressed down on him like wet clay, the gravity so dense it had texture. He wasn’t moving through the air anymore. He was moving through something that didn’t want him there.

He didn’t rush straight up. He angled — diagonal bursts, twisting his body, spiraling upward in short arcs rather than fighting the gravity head-on. A few twists. A few more feet. Until finally, the ceiling was within reach.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

Zane steadied himself at the two-hundred-foot mark. He knew that if he relaxed for even a second, all his effort would be for nothing. He’d plummet to the floor, just like Ryder. So, once he reached his target, he focused all his energy on simply fighting the gravity, not yet making a move for the blue dot.

The blue dot fluttered around him, almost carelessly. At times, it would drift tantalizingly close, as if to taunt him, only to zip away just as his fingers could almost brush against it.

Three seconds.

Six seconds.

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

Zane’s breath came in ragged, strained pants. His body was at its absolute limit. If he pushed his power even a fraction more, his form would shatter, and he’d come crashing down.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]

“He’s just… hanging there at two hundred feet. What’s that guy doing?”

“He has to be focused on the blue dot. Floating at two hundred feet is terrifying—it’s like walking a tightrope. As long as he holds on, he can move slowly, but one mistake and it’s over!”

“But there’s a limit to how long he can stay up there, even if he’s slow. And he’s not moving at all!”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

At that moment, the white-eyebrowed elder was on his feet, his gaze locked on Zane.

A thought sparked in the elder’s mind. He decided Zane shouldn’t get Thundergust that easily. He wanted to see the true limit of the boy’s strength.

The elder had set up these orbs himself; he could control them with a mere thought. It wasn’t that he wanted to make it harder for Zane, not really. He was just genuinely curious.

He wanted to see how long the kid could hold on.
