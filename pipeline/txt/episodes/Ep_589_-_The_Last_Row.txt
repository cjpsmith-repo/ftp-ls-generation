Ep 589 - The Last Row
Word count: 1061
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORCHESTRAL]
The Tower of Sin was fifteen floors high, with a Talent Tablet waiting on the tenth.
Zane’s hunch was spot on. The tower functioned just like the Dragon Soar Arena—some kind of training ground for an ancient race. It was like a version of the Arena, only a hundred times bigger.
What he didn't know was if this one could also send people to the upper world.
He and Olivia took the lead, with a handful of human War Generals and a few creatures from other races trailing behind.
Pretty much everyone was dying to know about Zane's talent, so there was no way they were going to miss this.
[AMBIENT: VAST EMPTY HALL]
Not far from the arena, the floor opened into a huge, empty space. On any other floor, this would have been a living area, but this one was different. The most striking feature was a massive wall.
[SFX: ECHOING FOOTSTEPS]
Countless names were carved into it, ranked from top to bottom.
His eyes scanned the wall, quickly landing on "Olivia" in the sixth row.
He glanced back at Olivia. "What are all these names?"
She smiled. "The names of everyone who's been tested by the Talent Tablet."
Zane’s eyebrows shot up as he studied the wall again.
[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Olivia was from the Violet Heart Sanctuary. While the sanctuary itself wasn't a major power, it boasted a warrior who'd reached the Soul Sea Realm—which alone made it leagues stronger than anything in the Heartlands. Back home, there weren't any sacred places to speak of; even the Ethereal Spirit Sect was just a fifth-grade sect.
By that logic, Olivia's talent should have been a cut above Brent's.
So how could she possibly be ranked only in the sixth row?
[MUSIC: TENSE PIANO]
A look of pure disbelief crossed Zane's face.
The sixth row was packed tight with names. His gaze drifted upward, over the endless columns ranked above hers. Every single one of them represented someone with more talent than Olivia.
[SFX: CROWD GASP]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
"Boris! He's in the fourth row!" someone in the crowd behind him gasped. And sure enough, there he was.
Olivia leaned in. "In the entire history of the main continent, only six people have ever made it to the fourth row," she explained. "Two of them are right here on this floor with us: Boris the Ogre, and Cheryl of the Demon Night."
Zane nodded, impressed. It seemed Boris’s strength was truly exceptional.
His gaze kept climbing. The third row. The second. There, at the top of the second row, was a single name: Amber — a Demon Night genius from two hundred years ago. One person, in all that time, had climbed that high.
And the first row? Five names. Just five, in the entire history of the Tower.
Zane read them quietly. Some sounded human. Others didn't. Whatever race they'd come from, their talent had been in a class entirely its own.
He looked back at the wall for a moment, then turned to Olivia. "Why isn't Yolande's name up there?"
Olivia smiled. "Simple. She's never gone in."
Zane blinked. "She's been here six years and never used it?"
"She doesn't listen to anyone," Olivia said with a shrug. "Plenty of people have tried to convince her — every warrior who reaches the tenth floor should use the Tablet, even if just for the small boost it gives. But Yolande does what she wants. The human leadership doesn't dare push her. And it's not just us — the Ogres have sent assassins after her three times. All three failed."
Rumor had it the Tablet could give your talent a small boost, which was why nearly every warrior who made it to the tenth floor felt compelled to try it.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]
But Yolande, of course, wasn't like other warriors. She didn't listen to anyone—not her own kind, and especially not humans. The human leaders were too afraid to cross her, so they just let her do as she pleased.
So what had made her suddenly change her mind and agree to use the Talent Tablet?
A curious glint appeared in Olivia's gentle eyes. She had to be wondering the same thing. Why would Yolande suddenly do this? Was it because of Zane? There had to be a connection.
She’d never heard of Yolande commenting on anyone’s scent before. Besides, the girl rarely showed any expression, moving with the blank stillness of a puppet. If it weren't for the fact that she had to eat, you’d swear she was one of the walking dead.
Zane gestured toward the door with a smile. "After you."
Yolande didn't hesitate. But before she stepped through, she leaned in close, taking a quick, deliberate sniff of Zane’s collar.
[SFX: SNIFF]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
A flicker of satisfaction crossed her otherwise blank features before she turned and walked through the door.
[SFX: STONE RUMBLING]
[SFX: HEAVY DOOR CLOSING]
The moment she stepped inside, the stone wall began to tremble. The heavy door swung shut of its own accord, sealing her within.
"How long does the test usually take?" Zane asked, unable to mask the tension in his voice.
Olivia shrugged, though her eyes remained fixed on the sealed entrance. "It depends on the individual. Could be five minutes for someone with exceptional talent. Could stretch to a couple of hours for those who struggle."
Zane nodded, feeling his pulse quicken despite himself. He had no personal stake in Yolande's success—they barely knew each other—yet something about her quiet determination had struck a chord within him.
[SFX: MAGICAL GLOW]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BUILDUP]
The wall began to glow, its surface shimmering with ethereal light. The thousands of names carved into the stone flashed in rapid succession, each one representing a soul who had stood where Yolande now stood. The gathered disciples leaned forward, breath held, waiting for the verdict that would determine her fate.
A moment later, the result materialized.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]
Zane's expression froze. His chest tightened as disbelief washed over him.
Yolande's name had appeared, but instead of claiming a position among the middle ranks—or even the lower-middle—it had settled on the very bottom row. The worst possible placement. A ranking that marked her as barely worthy of acknowledgment, let alone respect.
