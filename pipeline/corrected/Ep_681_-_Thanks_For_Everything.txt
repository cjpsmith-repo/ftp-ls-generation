Ep 681 - Thanks For Everything

Word count: 1150

[AMBIENT: PASTORAL SETTLEMENT]

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]

Three months after the Tower of Sin's fall, Zane stood on Heavenly Eagle Prairie, preparing to leave the main continent—while behind him, a young man's heart swelled with excitement.

Zane had rescued Craig and his tribe from an ogre's pasture, placing them with a nearby settlement where they'd adapted well. Craig had become a warrior, reaching the Bone Refining Level.

The tribe never forgot Zane. Integration with others had taught them about martial arts and the continent's power struggles. They now understood Zane was a warrior, not a god—but to them, he remained a heaven-sent hero. Without him, they'd still be livestock awaiting slaughter.

[SFX: URGENT FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

"Where is he? Quick, tell me!" Craig grabbed the young girl by the shoulders, his voice tight with urgency as the commotion drew the other herdsmen from their tents.

But in his excitement, Craig forgot his own strength. As a warrior at the Bone Refining Level, his grip was like iron, and the girl’s face flushed as he shook her.

"Easy, Craig!" one of the other herdsmen scolded. "She can't talk if you keep shaking her like that."

The words snapped Craig back to his senses. He’d gotten carried away. He immediately let go, and the girl rubbed her shoulder, shooting him a glare before huffing and pointing a finger toward the horizon. "Over there! I saw him go that way!"

She was pointing toward the very spot where Zane had first appeared—not far from the pasture that had once been their prison. Even though the entire Heavenly Eagle Prairie now belonged to humanity, the tribe still gave that place a wide berth. The memories were just too raw.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

The instant her finger was raised, Craig was gone, shooting off like an arrow loosed from a bow. His new power gave him total mastery over his body, letting him cross the prairie with impossible speed.

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]

The rest of the herdsmen’s faces lit up with hope as they scrambled to follow, finally getting the chance to properly thank the man who had saved them.

The pastures were part of the Violet Heart Sanctuary's domain now, and on the main continent, everyone knew Zane's name. A man like Zane walked in legendary towers that they could only dream of.

[AMBIENT: OPEN GRASSLAND]

[MUSIC: SOLEMN STRINGS]

Amber descended from the sky in a sweep of purple robes, her eyes scanning the grassland below. Soul Sea Realm senses left nothing hidden. She extended a hand and pointed. "The teleportation array is here."

Zane's gaze drifted to a large pit nearby — the crater from his own impact, now overgrown with weeds, though the memory of the fall was still vivid.

Dorian Sinclair landed beside him, brocade belt fluttering in the breeze. Without a word, he raised both palms and drove them into the earth.

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

[SFX: GRASS PEELING BACK]

The ground shuddered. A thousand feet of grassland rolled back in every direction, exposing the great teleportation array hidden beneath.

Soul Sea Realm warriors from the Violet Heart and Heavenly Feather Sanctuaries descended, followed by Fabian, Esther Sterling, Olivia, and others who formed a wide circle around the array. Today, Zane would leave the main continent.

No one else would ever receive an escort from two great sanctuary leaders.

The Tower of Sin was settled. Both the Demon Night and humans now had two peak War Sages each—Fabian and Elder May—and both sides were planning their next push into the Dark Nether Cave.

Fabian had pressed Zane to stay. Zane refused. He'd broken through to War Sage during his months of cultivation, and given time, reaching peak War Sage wouldn't have been impossible. But time was what he lacked. He didn't know when the next Violet Core Physique cultivator would emerge, and the thought of Maddox finding her first sat like a stone in his chest.

Dorian cupped his hands toward Zane. "Even if we could walk with you a thousand miles, we'd still have to part. Young hero — this is as far as we go."

Dorian knew better than anyone what Zane had done. The Violet Heart Sanctuary had benefited more than any other faction. Dorian couldn't talk him into staying, so he'd done the next best thing — traveled here himself to see him off. For a Soul Sea Realm warrior, that was no small gesture.

Amber and the Demon Night warriors sank to one knee in unison before Lavender.

"My Queen," Amber said, "we pray for your swift return to the throne. Whatever dangers await you beyond this array — if you call, the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary will answer. Every last one of us."

It wasn't an empty promise. The Heavenly Feather Sanctuary had already prepared a sound-transmitting talisman — one end bound to Lavender's queen statue, the other placed in Zane's hand. If Lavender ever needed them, they could abandon everything and step into this array without hesitation.

Lavender’s expression was calm as she nodded. "I see," she replied simply.

[SFX: WIND GUST]

The wind caught at Yolande’s colorful skirt. "I hope you made the right choice," she said softly to Zane. "We will meet again one day!"

Yolande was still conflicted about giving Zane the Celestial Fragment. She knew it was the right move—entrusting it to the one destined to lead the world of chaos—but her conviction wavered. Unlike Vesper, Yolande had to contend with the politics of the upper world, where several lords fiercely disagreed with her decision.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

She couldn't shake the fear that one day, she and Zane would find themselves on opposite sides of a battlefield. Thanks to her Soul Split Spell, fragments of her soul were scattered across the continent while her true self resided in the highest echelons of the upper world. As long as Zane kept walking the path of a warrior, their meeting again was not a question of if, but when.

Zane simply nodded at her words.

With that, the two powerful War Lords began feeding supreme Aether crystals into the massive teleportation array. It consumed an immense amount of energy, but the Violet Heart Sanctuary could easily afford the cost.

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

[AMBIENT: OPEN PRAIRIE WIND]

Just then, a figure appeared on the horizon, sprinting across the prairie toward them at an incredible speed. It was a young man, and behind him trailed a crowd of more than a hundred herdsmen.

Esther Sterling watched them approach, a calm "Hmm?" on her lips. She visibly relaxed when she sensed the lead warrior was only at the Bone Refining Level.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

"Stop them," Dorian ordered his subordinates. They couldn't afford an interruption, not at such a critical moment.

Several warriors moved to intercept the group, but Zane held up a hand. "No," he said, his voice calm but firm. "Let them through."
