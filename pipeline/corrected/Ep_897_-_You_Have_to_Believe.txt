Ep 897 - You Have to Believe

Word count: 1111

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMURS]

Everyone stared at Zane as he inhabited the corpse of Victor Vayne. They were used to Zane’s wiry frame, so seeing him inside the corpse’s broad, muscular build was just plain weird. 

A few of the onlookers, like Randy, Fiona, and Callan, couldn’t help but crack a smile at the sight.

A faint smile played on his new lips as Zane took a few casual steps, quickly getting the hang of his new limbs.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

But just as he started to feel comfortable, a crushing pressure descended on him from all sides. It wasn’t coming from the outside world. A ripple of pure red suddenly bled through the corpse’s empty mind, flooding his entire mental landscape with its crimson light. The pressure was coming from the body itself.

Zane had used the Soul Core to hijack this body, but now he was just a passenger.

[SFX: HEAVY DRAGGING FOOTSTEPS]

The corpse shuffled toward a nearby stone pillar, dragging Zane’s consciousness along with it and completely overpowering his attempts to resist.

Here we go, Zane thought, his guard flying up.

He’d seen this happen to War King Wind and Scott. They’d been helpless against the commands of the young girl in the pillar, forced to walk toward her against their will. But that wasn’t what really had him on edge. The real problem was the very real possibility that he wouldn’t be able to state the corpse’s name when asked.

Victor Vayne, he repeated silently, trying to burn it into his mind.

[SFX: BODY COLLAPSE]

[MUSIC: DISORIENTING DRONE]

He felt the body stumble forward and collapse to its knees with a heavy thud, right in front of the pillar. At the same time, the blood-red ripple in his head pulsed stronger and stronger, severing his connection to the corpse. Everything grew distant. Someone seemed to be talking to him, the sound drifting down from the sky, somehow both soft and grating, but he couldn’t make out a single word. His thoughts were a tangled mess.

[AMBIENT: ECHOING STONE CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

“Answer me! What is your name!” the young girl’s voice demanded, echoing from the stone.

“My name? My name is…” In the chaos swirling through his head, the name was just… gone.

This is insane. I can’t remember it at all, he thought, a wave of frustration rising. I swear I knew it a second ago! No matter how hard he scrambled for it, he couldn’t recall a single thing about the body he was in.

Then his gaze pushed through the red haze and landed on the pillar. The strokes were there — exactly as he’d carved them.

Horizontal. Horizontal. Vertical. Hook.

He traced them in his mind one by one, rebuilding the name from its components the way he’d planned. It worked. The strokes assembled into characters. He had the name.

And then he looked at it — and couldn’t read it.

The two characters sat in his mind like something from a foreign language. He knew he’d just constructed them. He knew they meant something. But recognition refused to come. The words were completely, utterly alien to him.

[SFX: OMINOUS WHOOSH]

“Tell me your name, or I will…” The girl’s voice cut through the noise, every word crystal clear.

The red ripples surged. His soul was forced out.

Victor Vayne’s corpse rose, walked back to its coffin, and lay down.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

The Emperor looked at Zane. “Well?”

Zane shook his head slowly. “The stroke method works — I reconstructed the name. But when I looked at it, I couldn’t recognize it. The girl’s will doesn’t stop you from spelling it out. It stops you from reading it.”

He paused. “There’s only one way this ever works. Instinct. Your own name is the one thing you can always say because you don’t have to think about it — it’s just there. To say someone else’s name under that assault, you’d have to genuinely believe it was your own. Don’t just memorize it. Actually believe it, down to the bone.”

“War King Wind got lucky,” Zane continued. “One moment of pure instinct. That’s all it was.”

The Emperor’s expression darkened. “Then it’s impossible. No one can trick themselves that completely.”

“Without a thirteenth-floor corpse, the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land is out of reach,” one of the masters said quietly. “The Ancestral Dragon Egg — we can’t get to it.”

The Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom’s face fell. “So we fail again.”

Zane smiled. “I said I can’t occupy the corpse in the coffin. I didn’t say I was out of options.”

“The twelfth floor?” War King Wind asked.

Zane didn’t answer. His gaze drifted slowly to the stone pillar — and the Crystal Coffin suspended within it.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRONE]

As the others watched Zane kneel motionless, one of the powerful masters muttered, “That girl is of the Spirit Supreme Realm! She’s even stronger than the Master Priest. How could anyone possibly take over her body? We don’t even know if she’s dead or alive!”

[SFX: DRAMATIC REVELATION IMPACT]

Spirit Supreme Realm!

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

The words lit a fire in Zane’s mind. In the upper world, the Life-and-Death Realm was just the entry point, and the Soul Sea Realm was only the beginning. The true powerhouses, the backbone of this world, were the warriors of the Spirit Supreme Realm. The white-haired Master Priest was one, and Vesper probably was, too. Then there was Yolande… her own cultivation wasn’t that high, but with a million copies of herself, her total strength was likely far beyond even that.

Anyone more powerful was simply beyond his comprehension. Even a master of the Spirit Supreme Realm was a being who commanded absolute respect. Hearing what the man said, Zane found himself more intrigued than ever by the girl in the pillar.

“Forget whether she’s dead or not,” the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom said dismissively. “There’s no way to get those chains off or break open that Crystal Coffin. Taking her body is a lost cause.”

But Zane just smirked.

[SFX: METALLIC RING]

He turned and walked back to Victor’s open coffin, then reached out and produced a small Storage Ring.

When the masters of the Soul Sea Realm saw it, their eyes widened in surprise as they realized what he was about to do. Fiona and Randy figured it out a second later. Even Callan, who was usually a bit behind, suddenly gasped.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

“That’s it! The black star pickax! You can use it to open the Crystal Coffin!”
