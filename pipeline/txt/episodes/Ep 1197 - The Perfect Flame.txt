Ep 1197 - The Perfect Flame

Word count: 1046

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]
Zane was sprinting down the true path of the Elysian Keep.
Gus had secretly altered the path’s third section, and he was the only one who’d ever faced the new layout. But with Gus defeated, the conflict between Dark and Light energies had vanished along with the path’s resistance. Zane breezed right through.
By the time he reached the end, Freya, Livingston, and the others were already there waiting for him.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL SHIMMER]
The moment he cleared the final stretch, the thick fog overhead parted with a flash of white light. 
[SFX: DESCENDING MAGICAL OBJECT]
Something began to drift down from the sky—a token from the Elysian Keep.
Everyone, Zane included, just stared as it descended.
“A token!” Livingston shouted. “Is that the one for opening the Armory?” Of everyone who’d made the journey, he was always the most focused on the rewards.
Saul’s voice was its usual monotone. “Setting aside the tokens of heaven and earth, it could be any of the other six. Weaponsmithing requires fire, so the token of fire would be the one linked to the Armory. It’s a one-in-six chance.”
[SFX: METALLIC CLANG]
“A one-in-six chance!” Livingston yelled, shaking his metallic head. The odds weren’t great, but they weren’t terrible, either—and the possibility alone was enough to get him hyped.
Zane just smirked. He didn’t really care which one it was, as long as it wasn’t a repeat.
As the token floated down, Zane reached out, snatched it from the air, and flipped it over.
“What is it?” Livingston demanded, waving his sharp claws, practically vibrating with excitement.
[MUSIC: VICTORIOUS FANFARE]
Zane just smiled, turning his hand over to show them. Carved into the front in big, impressive letters was the word: FIRE.
Livingston threw his head back and let out a wild laugh. “It really is the token of fire! Luck is on my side! The heavens themselves have given me exactly what I wanted!”
Freya, Lavender, and even Saul couldn’t help but roll their eyes.
The Elysian Keep was a place beyond the heavens; its rewards had nothing to do with Livingston’s luck. If anyone’s luck was at play, it was Zane’s.
[MUSIC: ANTICIPATORY STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT STONE CHAMBER]
Token in hand, the group returned to the courtyard and made their way to the spire. 
[SFX: HEAVY STONE DOORS OPENING]
Zane held it up, and as he activated it, the doors to the Armory swung open.
Zane was the first one in, with an ecstatic Livingston right on his heels.
The moment he stepped inside, Zane stopped, stunned. The place had expanded to more than ten times its original size.
[AMBIENT: VAST ECHOING WAREHOUSE]
All around them, materials were stacked and piled in every direction — more kinds than Zane could count, filling the space from floor to ceiling.
His eyes landed on a pile of wood nearby. It had been cut into inch-long blocks, and each one was marked with growth rings that looked unsettlingly like ghost faces. 
Neither Freya nor Lavender could identify it — and Lavender’s knowledge of such things far exceeded either of theirs.
Zane crouched and reached for one.
“Stop!”
Livingston shoved past everyone and planted himself between Zane and the wood, his claws raised. His voice was shaking.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS LOW DRONE]
“This is… this is Primeval Deathspirit Wood.”
He stared at it for a long moment, as if he still couldn’t believe it was real.
“What is it?” Zane asked.
“It has no bark. No leaves. Just a bare trunk,” Livingston said, his voice dropping to something almost reverent. 
“How does it survive without bark or leaves?” Freya asked.
“It grows by absorbing the souls of the dead — bodies buried beneath it, feeding it. Trillions of souls, over hundreds of millions of years, just to bring it to this size.” He picked up one of the blocks with trembling hands. “The growth rings you’re looking at — those are faces. Every ring is a soul.”
Freya took a step back.
“I read about it in the archives,” Livingston continued, half to himself. “I didn’t think it actually existed. Something this fragile, over a timeframe this long — any mishap at all and it dies. Even a Supreme Lord couldn’t guarantee it survived that long without incident.” He looked up. “And yet here it is. A whole pile of it.”
“What’s it used for?” Zane asked.
“Burn it,” Livingston said. “It produces one of the three most perfect flames for weaponsmithing. Besides the mixed-element flame, there are two others: the Sun Spring Essence Flame and the black fire. Those two are incredibly hard to get. People say the Sun Spring Essence Flame is only found in the Sun Spring, and as for the black fire… they say it’s born inside a soulforge.”
Zane had mostly been tuning him out, but the word ‘soulforge’ snapped him to attention.
The black fire in his *own* soulforge… it was one of the three perfect flames.
He’d had it for years without knowing what it was. Every weaponsmith in existence would have given anything for access to it — and it had been sitting inside him the whole time, waiting.
Livingston, completely oblivious to Zane’s reaction, was already moving on. As he talked, his eyes landed on a refining furnace nearby.
He casually tossed the Primeval Deathspirit Wood he was holding, letting it clatter to the ground, and rushed toward a furnace tucked away in a corner of the Armory. It was an odd-looking thing, thin at the bottom and thick at the top, like a giant wine jug without a spout.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]
[SFX: METAL DOOR WRENCHING OPEN]
Livingston lunged for it and wrenched open a door on its side. 
[AMBIENT: FURNACE CRACKLING]
[SFX: BRILLIANT LIGHT BURST]
A brilliant, rainbow-colored light immediately shot out. To their surprise, the furnace was still lit, and a red-hot sword blank was lying inside.
It was obvious in an instant: that sword blank had been burning in there, untouched, for hundreds of millions of years.
Livingston didn’t move. He just stared into the open furnace, the rainbow light playing across his metallic face.
“What kind of weapon,” he said quietly, almost to himself, “takes hundreds of millions of years to forge?”
