Ep 601 - Celestial Rank
Word count: 1152
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
Phoebe of the Demon Night had taught Zane the basics: the Tower of Sin was essential to every race's survival on the Main Continent, a shared training ground too vital for any single faction to control.
The Flaming Place and Dread Place constantly schemed for dominance, but allowing either to succeed would doom the others. Instead, multiple races governed it jointly, maintaining access for their warriors.
The Dark Nether Cave at the Tower's base was equally important.
Now Zane possessed the authority to kick out anyone, from War Fighters on the lowest floors to seat owners at the summit. Their fates rested in his hands.
It was too much power to comprehend.
A thrill of power, sharp and electric, shot through him.
He spoke out loud, more to himself than anything. "Well, I passed the final test. Time to head out."
"Of course," the tower spirit replied. "Just remember, as the spirit, I am everywhere within the Tower of Sin. When you wish to exercise your authority, simply call for me."
Zane nodded and made for the entrance.
[AMBIENT: ANXIOUS CROWD MURMUR]
Outside the Talent Tablet, the crowd waited, collectively holding its breath for Zane’s return.
He’d been inside for what felt like an eternity, far longer than average, and the people waiting were getting restless.
Yolande had also reached the first row, just like Zane, but her test had taken a fraction of the time.
"Why isn't he out yet?"
"Maybe he kicked the bucket in there. Saves us the trouble."
"That's impossible. The Talent Tablet's test isn't dangerous. It just ranks your potential."
The whispers made Olivia’s stomach twist into knots. The Humans were already under enough pressure; if Zane had actually died in there, it would be a catastrophe.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Suddenly, the buzz of chatter died as the wall began to tremble.
Olivia's eyebrows shot up. "Is he coming out?"
She wasn't alone. Elder Kingsley, Fabian, and Jarrod all stiffened, their faces etched with tension.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Then someone cried out. Zane's name—which had been sitting at the top of the first row—was gone.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
"Disappeared? How is that possible?"
"Did he die in there? Is that why it vanished?"
"Don't talk nonsense. There was once a genius who died in the arena after coming out—his name stayed on the wall just fine. Names don't disappear like that."
"Then how do you explain it?"
[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD ARGUING]
The crowd erupted into an argument. It was unheard of. Names didn't vanish from the Talent Tablet—they only got pushed down. And the first row had so few names to begin with. Before yesterday, there had been only five, none of them from the Main Continent. Now there had been two more. You couldn't push a name off the first row. It simply didn't happen.
Olivia clenched her fists, the worry etched deeper into her face with every passing second. But she kept praying. Zane will be fine. He has to be.
[SFX: STONE CRACKING]
As the wall shook harder, a single speck of golden light bloomed on the first row. Then another.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL RISE]
Cracks spider-webbed across the stone, and the golden light flooded through them, blazing until it swallowed the entire wall.
[SFX: GASPS FROM THE CROWD]
[SFX: STONE CARVING]
Out of the cracks, two words carved themselves into the stone: Celestial Rank.
For a moment, no one spoke.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE LIGHT BURST]
Then the words detonated with a blinding cascade of golden light.
"Celestial Rank? What does that mean?"
"I have no idea—but that light—"
[SFX: MAGICAL INSCRIPTION]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT FANFARE]
More cracks split the wall behind the first two words, and letter by letter, a name took shape. Zane. The letters blazed with the same boundless golden light, ten times the size of every other name on the wall.
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD GASPS]
"Celestial Rank—Zane!"
"That's obscene. His name jumped straight to the top of everything."
"Does it have to be that big? That's ten times the size of the names below it—"
"I'll say one thing. This Zane is absolutely monstrous."
[MUSIC: OMINOUS CONTEMPLATIVE THEME]
Madeline watched with a sly smile. Let fate run its course, she thought. Someone with his talent possessed extraordinary luck. The Ogres would struggle to kill him.
But Boris's challenge remained.
If Zane won in the arena, the Ogres would ignore the rules and execute him immediately. Against tenth-level War Lord Malcolm, he stood no chance.
Even with Fabian and Jarrod's protection, the Ogres controlled the Tower's seats.
Four humans against over twenty Ogres? Impossible odds.
The human boy was doomed.
Fabian and Jarrod stared at each other, the disbelief plain on their faces.
"Jarrod," Fabian said quietly, "isn't there a Book of Celestial Rank somewhere at the top of the tower?"
"Yes. According to what's recorded in it, this Tower of Sin seems to have once belonged to a race called the Celestial Rank—but the records are vague." Jarrod shook his head. "I have no idea what that boy encountered inside the Talent Tablet."
"Whatever he encountered," Fabian said with a slow exhale, "to have his name engraved above every genius who ever lived…" He trailed off. "I don't even know what to say."
The golden light blazed in the eyes of the Demon Night Clan members, their expressions unreadable—proud, wary, complicated all at once.
The Human warriors in the crowd were a different story entirely. For too long, they had kept their heads down in the Tower of Sin, swallowed their pride, and suffered in silence. But now, looking up at those two enormous glowing words, every last one of them stood a little straighter.
[SFX: HEAVY DOOR CREAKING OPEN]
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
Just as all eyes were fixed on the golden words etched into the wall, the door finally creaked open.
When Zane had first entered, a handful of people had seen him off. Now, that handful had swelled into a massive, diverse crowd.
He was floored by the sight. "What the hell…?" He scanned the sea of faces, and a wave of relief washed over him when he spotted Olivia. He immediately started toward her.
Olivia’s eyes were glistening, like she’d been fighting back tears. "You're f-finally out," she stammered, her voice trembling.
Zane frowned. "What's wrong?"
Shaking her head, Olivia managed a smile and pointed behind him. "Look."
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
Zane turned, and his jaw practically hit the floor. He stared at the massive golden words carved into the stone wall. He knew the spirit had written his name, but did it have to be that damn big? It felt ridiculously arrogant.
He stood there, speechless. If he'd known this would happen, he would’ve told the spirit to tone it down. A lot.
Now he had a huge target on his back.
