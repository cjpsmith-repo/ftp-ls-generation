Ep 1005 - Soulbound

Word count: 1112

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL STRINGS]

The Cobalt Dragon explained that not all supreme worlds were created equal. In fact, neither this Upper World nor the Violet Core Realm was complete. The raw essence of heaven and earth in both places was barely any richer than in the Lower World.

Zane nodded, finally understanding.

Just then, one of the armored warriors noticed the state of the other Soul Sea Realm warriors and let out a sharp laugh. “Get a load of these guys! Ha! Every time we open a well, it’s the same thing. Never gets old!”

The warriors still in the well were fuming with indignation, but they were powerless. The sensation of their own Vitality churning violently within them was just too much to bear. None of them dared to float up and out of the well.

“Looks like we don’t have any standouts in this batch. Alright, then. You all can take your time getting used to it,” the armored warrior said, backing away from the edge.

With the enchanted barrier gone, there was no reason for Zane to stay in the water. The Vitality Pressure had no effect on him anyway.

[SFX: WATER SPLASH]

[AMBIENT: OPEN AIR]

In a single, fluid motion, he launched himself out of the well and landed softly beside it.

He took in his surroundings, his eyes widening in surprise. He hadn’t expected to see so many wells.

Right next to his were dozens of others, packed tightly together. A short distance away, more than ten armored warriors were moving between them, checking on each one.

So many Ascending Wells, Zane thought. Does that mean every one holds more warriors from the Soul Sea Realm? There must be hundreds of them, all just from the Lower World.

The thought made him sigh.

Meanwhile, his sudden appearance had sent a ripple of shock through the nearby armored warriors.

[AMBIENT: CROWD CHATTER]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Zane, standing calmly by the well, was impossible to miss. From beneath their helmets, they all stared at him in utter disbelief.

“What the—? Am I seeing things?”

“We just dropped the barrier on that well! How the hell did he adapt to the Vitality Pressure that fast?”

“What a freak!”

A few of the warriors just stared, speechless.

“You’re all missing the most important part,” another armored warrior pointed out. “This kid isn’t just a freak; he’s an impossible one. Can’t you see? He’s only at the Life-and-Death Realm!”

That realization left many of them completely stunned.

The armored warriors were all Soul Sea Realm cultivators themselves, tasked with guiding the ascendants from the Lower World. Year after year, they had seen countless warriors arrive.

And they knew exactly how rare fast adaptation was. Most ascenders needed ten days to half a month in the purification well before they could tolerate the Vitality Pressure outside. The fastest ever recorded had managed it in three days — a feat that had been talked about for years. There was one legend, a warrior who had gone on to make a name for himself across the Upper World, who had adapted in four hours. That story was still passed around as something almost mythological.

Zane had stepped out in under half an hour.

Several of the armored warriors exchanged looks. None of them had words for it.

[MUSIC: TENSE SHIFT]

The lead warrior — the one who had called them bumpkins not long ago — had started toward Zane with his jaw set. But somewhere between the well’s edge and where Zane was standing, something shifted in his expression. He slowed. His mind was working.

A warrior who broke the rules of ascension. A warrior who adapted to Vitality Pressure faster than anyone he’d ever seen or heard of. If this person rose — and someone like this would rise — making an enemy of him now would be a decision he’d regret for the rest of his life. He’d already insulted him once. He could only hope it hadn’t landed.

By the time he reached Zane, his expression had rearranged itself into something warm and open. He clasped his hands in a respectful greeting. “My apologies for the earlier remarks. Welcome to the Upper World, friend. You’ve just ascended into the Soulbound Sect.”

Zane noted the sudden change with quiet amusement. He’d seen it before — the same calculation, the same pivot. Some things were the same in every world.

“The Soulbound Sect,” the warrior continued, with a trace of pride he couldn’t quite suppress, “is one of three tenth-tier sacred sects in the Spiritual Realm. The pinnacle of what a human sect can be.”

Zane said nothing, but he filed it away. The four Divine Kingdoms together barely qualified as an eighth-tier sacred sect. Two full tiers separated where he’d come from and where he’d arrived. The gap was significant.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Zane knew he had to get stronger before he could even think about finding his sister, Elara. Once he had enough power, he could help Lavender return to the Demon Night people—and, ideally, help her defeat her enemy. After that, he still had to help the Cobalt Dragon forge an Astral Body.

Of course, each goal was a mountain higher than the last.

Lavender’s strength, for instance, was on par with a World Lord’s—and so was her opponent’s. At Zane’s current level, facing a World Lord was a complete fantasy. It would take an eternity to reach that kind of power.

Helping the Cobalt Dragon forge an Astral Body would be no small feat, either. But finding Elara was the most daunting task of all. It was also the most important. Zane could still feel the incredible, suffocating power of the young man who had taken her. His own strength wasn’t even a drop in the ocean compared to that guy’s. The gulf between them was simply unimaginable.

Zane’s timeline was tight. He didn’t have a moment to relax.

He had just arrived in the Upper World, and now he was about to be locked up like a common criminal. It wasn’t exactly a situation where he could be happy about being turned into a glorified slave.

Zane’s dark expression seemed to grate on the armored warriors. To them, even if he was the most promising of the new arrivals, he was still just some kid from the Lower World. A kid whose cultivation was only at the Life-and-Death Realm, no less.

“It’s the rule,” one of the warriors said, his voice holding a thin, mocking smile. “Whether you like it or not, you serve in the Soulbound Sect. You don’t get a choice. Don’t make this difficult for us.”

[SFX: HANDCUFFS]

Zane considered his options for a moment, then gave a tight nod of surrender. He held out his hands, allowing the armored warrior to cuff him.
