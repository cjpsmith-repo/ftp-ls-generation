Emanuel wielded serious influence over the hierarchy of Cloud Hall.

He had the title of Elder, the same as Toby, but the comparison between them ended there. Age had diminished Toby's power, relegating him to the lower echelons of Cloud Hall's leadership. Emanuel, by contrast, stood at the absolute zenith of his abilities. His fearsome strength made him one of the most formidable elders in the entire sect—a man whose mere presence commanded respect and instilled caution.

The Message Rune that had whispered urgently in Emanuel's ear contained only a handful of words, but it was clearly enough to get the message across. Only a select few had the authority to summon him away with such a short command, representing power that even Emanuel couldn't ignore.

But who could it have been? Who among them would bother to intervene on Zane's behalf?

This was Zane's first time on the Cloud Road. He'd never set foot in The Heartlands before today, and had certainly never crossed paths with any of Cloud Hall's senior leadership. The boy was an outsider, a newcomer from a distant sect. So who would risk their reputation and political capital to protect him now? What connection could exist between this young Adept and Cloud Hall's inner circle?

Several sect leaders exchanged puzzled glances, their minds racing through the same calculations. The implications troubled them. If Zane had attracted a powerful patron within Cloud Hall itself, the political landscape had just shifted in ways they hadn't anticipated.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Sir Kenneth stared at Zane, his expression twisted with confusion. In that moment, the young disciple from his own sect felt like a complete stranger. He’d always prided himself on knowing Zane, on being one of the few who understood his secrets. But it was finally dawning on him just how much he didn’t know.

Zane was supposedly just an Adept, a fact that did nothing to lessen Kenneth’s shock at how he’d just faced down Emanuel.

[MUSIC: WORRIED VIOLIN]

Even though the immediate danger had passed, Kenneth was still rattled. “That was reckless, Zane,” he said, his voice tight with worry.

Toby nodded grimly. “I agree. I don’t know who you’re counting on, but provoking Emanuel was not smart. He let you go today, but you’ll be running into him all the time in Cloud Hall. If he decides to come after you, you’ll be in a world of trouble.”

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Toby already had a pretty good guess who’d stepped in for Zane—there weren’t many possibilities. As far as he knew, only the current or former leaders of Cloud Hall could order Emanuel around like that. The last leader hadn't been seen in public for over a century.

That left the current leader of Cloud Hall as the most likely candidate. But that conclusion just raised more questions for Toby. Did Zane really have that kind of connection? How would they even know each other? Where could they possibly have met?

From what Toby had heard, the Cloud Hall leader had been in Secluded Cultivation for the last two years. If that was true, there was no way their paths could have crossed.

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

“Elder Toby, Sir Kenneth,” Zane said, bowing with his hands clasped respectfully. “Thank you for your concern. I know I pushed it, but I didn’t have a choice. What happened on the Cloud Road -  it’s personal. It’s not something I can just share with anyone, not even Master Drew. Besides, do you really think he would have just let me walk away, even if I’d told him everything?”

His words carried a weight that silenced them both. They knew the young man had a point.
One by one, the remaining disciples emerged from the vortex. Last of all came Lennon — soaked, shaken, but alive.

Then the Drake puppet had returned, sweeping low and extending its tail toward him. He'd grabbed on without thinking. It had carried him over the broken path and deposited him at the exit without a word.
He walked out blinking, quietly grateful to be breathing.


There she is! 

[SFX: TELEPATHIC WHOOSH]

The Cobalt Dragon's voice echoed in Zane's mind. Third from the left, in that group of disciples!

[AMBIENT: DISTANT CROWD MURMUR]

Zane squinted, his gaze sweeping across the distant figures clustered near the Cloud Road exit. He followed the Dragon's directions carefully, his eyes landing on a plain-looking girl who blended seamlessly into the group. The moment he focused on her, he found her staring right back at him.

[MUSIC: DISCOVERY THEME]

It was Tia.

When their eyes met, hers flickered away with practiced casualness, as if she'd merely been scanning the crowd. She was testing him, trying to gauge whether he had truly seen through her disguise.

Zane kept his gaze locked on her, unwavering, as if he could see past every layer of illusion she'd wrapped around herself. 

The intensity of his stare sent a ripple of annoyance through Tia. Had he recognized me? Impossible! She was utterly confident in her disguise. Zane wasn't even an Inner-Core cultivator. There was no conceivable way he could have penetrated her carefully crafted façade.

What she didn't know was that her disguise meant nothing against the Cobalt Dragon. The ancient creature could pierce through any mortal illusion, seeing straight to the essence of her soul with eyes that had witnessed millennia.

Tia attempted to maintain her casual act, though his unblinking stare was beginning to make her skin prickle with discomfort. 

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

A heartbeat later, she realized the pretense was pointless. A faint smile spread across Zane's face—knowing, almost playful—a look that practically screamed, The game's up. I see exactly who you are.

Heat flashed through Tia's chest. That insufferable little pest! He actually figured me out! Her jaw tightened as she fought to keep her expression neutral.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

She pouted, her lips thinning as she glared at him. “Stop staring at me,” she sent via Vitality, “or I’ll gouge your eyes out.”

[MUSIC: PLAYFUL STRINGS]

Zane just laughed, completely unfazed. He knew Tia’s rank was far higher than Emanuel’s. She was the one who had sent the elder packing with a single sentence. Even if Toby somehow figured out her real identity, he would never dare expose the truth.

“Hey,” Zane sent back, a playful tone in his own Vitality message. “What did you say to Master Drew in that Message Rune earlier?”

A sly smile touched Tia’s lips. "I told him to get lost.”

Zane was speechless.

He knew she was powerful, but he never expected her to be that blunt with someone like Emanuel. Suddenly, the utterly defeated look on the elder’s face as he’d stormed off made perfect sense.
