Ep 148 - The Tower
Word Count: 1110
[AMBIENT: FOREST SOUNDS]
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSE]
When the griffin emerged from the woods outside the Pink Force Field, it saw Zane. Its huge yellow eyes locked onto him like a predator that had just found its prey, and it began to creep forward.
[SFX: DEAFENING MONSTER ROAR]
[SFX: BIRDS SCATTERING]
[SFX: CREATURES SCRAMBLING]
The sound was loud enough to wake the dead, shattering the forest's silence. Strange creatures scrambled in the underbrush, a flock of birds exploded into the sky, and monsters scattered in every direction.
[SFX: VIOLENT WIND GUST]
The roar kicked up a wind as fierce as a tornado—that was no exaggeration. It tore right through the Pink Force Field as if it wasn't there, lifting Zane into the air and slamming him hard against a nearby tree.
[SFX: BODY SLAMMING INTO TREE]
[SFX: WOOD SPLINTERING]
The impact crashed through Zane like a thunderbolt, comparable to taking a direct blow from a Soul Realm master at full strength. His body slammed backward, embedding deep within the tree trunk's flesh, the wood splintering around him as he became pinned like an insect in a collection. Warm blood filled his mouth, metallic and bitter, before he coughed it out in a crimson spray. A profound wave of helplessness washed over him, his limbs temporarily unresponsive as shock coursed through his system. The realization struck him—had he collided with solid rock instead of the relatively pliable trunk, his journey would have ended right there, his body shattered beyond repair.
"What in the Underworld is this place?" Zane gasped, his voice ragged as he struggled to draw breath into his battered lungs. His eyes darted frantically across the bizarre landscape, fear mingling with desperate curiosity. "Where are all these things coming from? It's like walking into a nightmare."
It took him a moment to catch his breath before he could move. He tried to pull himself out of the trunk, the pain sharp and searing. Luckily, his body was as tough as a Spirit Weapon, built to withstand a huge impact. When he finally wrenched himself free from the hole in the tree, he saw the griffin still waiting for him outside the Magic Ward, poised to pounce.
[MUSIC: INTENSE DANGER]
At the slight movement, the griffin’s head snapped up, instantly on high alert. Zane quickly ducked back into the tree hole. But this time, the griffin didn't roar. It just charged straight for him.
The beast dashed toward the Pink Force Field. Zane held his breath, bracing himself. If that ward didn't stop it, this was the end.
[SFX: ELECTRICAL SPARKS]
[SFX: LIGHTNING STRIKE]
The moment the griffin made contact, sparks flew from the field's surface. A split second later, a bolt of lightning engulfed the beast. Its body began to disintegrate, piece by piece—first fur, then flesh and blood, and finally its skeleton, until absolutely nothing was left.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT THUD]
When the colossal beast vanished, a massive Demon Core crashed to the earth with a bone-jarring thud that reverberated through Zane's entire body. His teeth rattled from the impact as he stared in awe at the unexpected aftermath.
[AMBIENT: PULSING ENERGY]
The core radiated a brilliant, hypnotic glow—a swirling torrent of compressed energy trapped within its crystalline structure. It pulsed rhythmically, almost like a heartbeat, emanating waves of power that made the air around it shimmer and distort. Zane felt a primal fear rise in his chest as he gazed upon energy so vast he couldn't begin to comprehend its magnitude.
"If the energy contained in this thing is ever released," Zane thought, his mouth dry with dread, "it could obliterate the entire Eastern Territory in an instant." The realization sent a cold shiver down his spine as he contemplated the devastating potential resting before him—a weapon of unimaginable destruction disguised as a beautiful, glowing artifact.
"I wonder how much I could get for this on the East Side Market," he sighed, staring at the Demon Core resting just outside the Pink Force Field. 
He crouched at the edge of the ward, admiring it. The core was right there, so close, but he couldn't reach it. Besides, it was way too big for his Storage Ring.
[AMBIENT: ALIEN FOREST]
[MUSIC: AWE AND UNEASE]
He turned away from the core and pushed deeper into the forest. Every tree dwarfed anything he'd ever seen — hundreds of feet tall, their roots alone the size of buildings. A wide road cut through the center, built for something far larger than any human.
He ran it. Less than an hour later, a massive structure rose from the forest ahead.
It was a palace — enormous, ornate, radiating a faint seven-colored light. From its center, a beam of pink light shot skyward and fanned out into the barrier overhead. This was the source.
[SFX: MASSIVE GATE CREAKING]
The gate stood hundreds of feet tall, wide open. Zane scaled the threshold — three stories high on its own — and dropped inside.
Dozens of people were already there, all in the uniforms of the Seven Ruling Families, clustered near the entrance. They turned at the sound of his landing.
"A straggler," someone muttered.
A long-haired woman near the front looked him over without expression. "Walsh," she said quietly. "Kill him. Hang his head at the valley entrance."
Walsh's hand went to the dagger at his waist. Black fog began to curl from his feet.
[AMBIENT: DARK ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: TENSE MEMORY]
Walsh—a fighter from one of the Great Houses—was using a swirling black fog, a familiar tactic. Zane had faced similar attacks before. His first encounter was during his battle with Roman Stryker and Graves, but their black mist had been useless against his soul-force; he’d overpowered and killed Stryker in an instant.
The second time was against Aldrich’s Sacred Flame, which had been far more potent. That Hellfire could sever a person's connection to their spirit, blind them, and corrode anything in its path.
Zane could tell just by looking at him that Walsh was an assassin, and he figured this black fog would be even deadlier. But it didn't matter. Zane was determined to end this fight.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
He stood still, his back against the high doorway, watching Walsh close in. The black fog spread out, enveloping him. The moment it swallowed his vision, Walsh made his move.
[AMBIENT: SWIRLING FOG]
Walsh manipulated the fog, making it churn and roil like a weightless storm. Zane remained motionless, arms crossed, the Shattered Dagger held firmly in his hand. His gaze was fixed on the swirling darkness, waiting for the attack.
A soft voice drifted out from within the fog. "What's your name?"
Walsh was a man, but his voice was surprisingly high-pitched and thin.
Zane's brow furrowed. "Let's dispense with the pleasantries. Isn't elimination of targets what you Great House servants specialize in? What significance could my name possibly hold for you?"
"Oh, don't misinterpret my intentions," Walsh responded, his voice piercing through the dense fog with unsettling clarity. "This bears no connection to the house I represent. It's a personal principle I've maintained throughout my career. I commit to memory the name of each soul I dispatch. A ceremonial practice, if you will. I've extinguished the lives of two thousand, four hundred and sixty-one individuals since entering House Pauling's service, and I can recite every single one of their identities without hesitation."
"My name remains irrelevant to you," Zane stated with icy detachment.
"And what logic supports that assertion?" Walsh inquired, his words saturated with arrogant self-assurance.
[MUSIC: DEADLY DETERMINATION]
"Because," Zane replied, his voice dropping to a deadly whisper as his grip tightened on the Shattered Dagger, "when this fog clears, you won't be adding another name to your collection—you'll be nothing but a memory yourself." His eyes narrowed, reflecting not fear but the cold calculation of a predator who had already envisioned his opponent's demise. "Today marks your end."
