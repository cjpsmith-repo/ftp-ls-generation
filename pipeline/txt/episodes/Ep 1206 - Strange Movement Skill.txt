Ep 1206 - Strange Movement Skill

Word count: 1060

[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]
[AMBIENT: EERIE WIND]
The ogre and Zane were at a standstill. 
The monster had finally stopped its assault, and Zane was just hanging there in the air, a silent, unmoving target.
He’d known from the moment he started practicing it that the Eight Smoky Melodies technique was weird. He’d even tested it once in a spar with Freya. 
[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]
She’d come at him with that long sword of hers, all fury and steel, but she hadn’t been able to land a single hit.
The whole principle was based on a simple, universal truth: nothing in this world is ever truly still.
Trees and grass might look stationary, but they sway with every breath of wind. The stars aren’t just twinkling in place; they’re hurtling through the cosmos at unimaginable speeds. Even the hundred thousand supreme worlds were constantly adrift, sometimes colliding in cosmic events that took millions of years to unfold. Everything in the universe was in motion.
The Eight Smoky Melodies harnessed those infinitesimal ripples—the tiny, imperceptible movements in everything—and weaponized them into the ultimate shield. The smallest shift in the air was enough to move Zane’s body just out of harm’s way, which was why the ogre’s wild attacks had kept missing him by a hair.
Still, Freya was nowhere near as strong as these three ogres. Zane wasn’t impressed by them, though. To him, they were just guinea pigs, a perfect chance to see what this bizarre technique could really do.
[SFX: CHARGING FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: SPEAR THRUST]
The machete-wielding ogre was panting, trying to catch its breath, but the second one was already charging, spear leveled at Zane’s chest.
“Snake Spear!” he roared.
[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSHING THROUGH AIR]
The weapon was unique, its tip forged into the shape of a serpent’s head, fangs bared. It was designed to terrify, but as it hurtled toward him, Zane remained perfectly calm. Instead of dodging, he simply stretched out and flipped upside down in midair, his eyes locked on the black spear as it closed the distance to his head.
Three inches. From three inches away, he could see the cold, dark glow shimmering around the weapon’s tip. He could make out the faint, swirling lines etched along the spearhead and found himself appreciating the decent craftsmanship.
Three inches was all the distance it needed to bury itself in his skull.
But those three inches might as well have been an eternity. It was as if an invisible wall had sprung into existence, stopping the spear dead in its tracks.
[SFX: METALLIC VIBRATION]
The weapon quivered, its momentum completely gone, just shy of his forehead.
[MUSIC: GRIM DETERMINATION]
Their faces went gloomy. Even if they couldn’t explain what Zane was doing, it didn’t take a genius to realize he didn’t see them as a threat—and to ogres, being humiliated in a fight was unforgivable.
“Second Brother, Third Brother—cut off his retreat!” the lead Ogre roared, swinging the black broadsword again.
[SFX: WEAPONS CONVERGING]
The three of them closed in from different directions—saber, spear, and sword—boxing Zane in at the center. Wherever he tried to retreat, another blade was waiting. They’d finally trapped him.
Or so they thought.
[SFX: BODY TWISTING]
Zane’s body folded and stretched like something boneless, squeezing straight through a gap between their weapons that couldn’t have been more than half a foot wide—and even as he slipped through it, he never closed that maddening three-inch distance.
The three Ogres stared, momentarily stunned. Cultivators could twist and bend their bodies through training, sure—but not like that. Not flattened to nothing and threaded through a half-foot gap while still somehow keeping an opponent at arm’s length the whole time.
Zane straightened back to normal on the other side, a faint smile on his face. “Continue?”
Two of them went pale. Whatever confidence they’d walked in with was draining out fast—they were starting to realize they’d picked the wrong target.
“If you’re done,” Zane said, a thread of cold amusement in his voice, “I’ll just go ahead and collect what I came for.” A hundred and fifty Illusion Points, give or take.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[SFX: BOWSTRING RELEASE]
The third Ogre—the one who’d hung back this whole time—let an arrow fly.
Zane’s eyes narrowed. Something about this felt wrong.
“Eight Smoky Melodies—withdraw!”
He didn’t have time to disperse the smoke. The arrow struck home, locking onto him at the same impossible three-inch distance, and the force behind it sent him hurtling backward.
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
Ten thousand feet. He didn’t stop until the arrow’s momentum finally bled out and its tip began to dip toward the ground—and even then, his body refused to close that three-inch gap, as if some part of him still wanted to be struck. Only when the arrowhead hit dirt did his body twist free of the space between the arrow and the ground.
Even Zane had no idea what was happening.
“What the hell is this Eight Smoky Melodies?” he muttered, a genuine thread of disbelief in his own voice. “This is insane.” He had a fleeting, paranoid thought that Gus, that useless clown, must have tricked him somehow.
[SFX: BODY RISING THROUGH AIR]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS SCATTERING]
Shaking the last wisps of smoke from his body, Zane slowly rose into the air just as his opponents broke, scattering in opposite directions. The moment they realized they couldn’t hit him, their confidence shattered. They’d been stalking him for a while, but this fight had broken them.
Too bad for them, Zane wasn’t about to let them get away.
In seconds, they were ten thousand feet away, but with his power, catching them wouldn’t be a problem. Running was a coward’s move, but after seeing what Zane could do, they clearly didn’t care about honor anymore. They were lucky this was just the Illusion Battlefield. Death here just meant being revived back in the city after losing half their Illusion Points.
[SFX: MAGICAL ACTIVATION]
Realizing Zane was closing in fast, the ogres activated their camouflage armor, their forms blurring as they tried to blend into the landscape.
It was no use.
[SFX: ENERGY BEAMS ERUPTING]
[SFX: SOULS WHOOSHING]
There was no escaping their fate. 
Three streams of brilliant red light erupted, wrapping around the ogres’ souls before shooting back toward the main city, where they would have to wait to be revived.
