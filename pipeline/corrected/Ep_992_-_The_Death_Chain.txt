Ep 992 - The Death Chain
Word count: 1024
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Zane stared at the Eternal Scribe, completely baffled. “What the hell is a Roaring Token?”
The Eternal Scribe puffed out his chest, clearly ready to show off. But Saul cut in before he could. “It’s an old trinket the Master had,” he explained. “When you shout into it, your voice can be heard across the entire universe. Roaring Tokens are pretty common out there, but the Master’s is special. It can be used an unlimited number of times.”
“Unlimited uses!” the Cobalt Dragon roared again.
“Something like that shouldn’t even exist!” the Red Dragon bellowed, finally breaking his silence.
The two dragons were getting way too worked up, and all the roaring was making Zane’s head spin. Saul had just said the thing was basically a universal megaphone, and he’d been so casual about it, which made Zane assume it wasn’t a big deal. So why were these two freaking out?
“What’s the big deal?” Zane asked the Cobalt Dragon, feeling totally lost.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]
The Cobalt Dragon let out a long sigh, his voice thick with disbelief. “I knew the owner of The Elysian Keep was a True God who could defy the heavens, but I never imagined he’d possess a treasure like this.”
“What are you talking about? Can you spell it out for me?” The dragon wasn’t making any sense. It was just a tool for shouting—why was he so blown away? He hadn’t even been this worked up when Zane got the Black Star pickax. Was this Roaring Token really better than a divine-class weapon?
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]
The Cobalt Dragon said, “The one hundred thousand supreme worlds are enormous. It’s almost impossible to communicate with anyone across that kind of distance. But a single Roaring Token can send your voice to every last corner of existence!”
“Oh.” Saul had already covered that part. The dragon was just repeating him. Was broadcasting your voice across the universe really that big of a deal?
Seeing Zane’s skeptical look, the Cobalt Dragon actually smiled. “Once you ascend to the upper world, a voice that can reach the entire universe gives you the right to be heard by everyone. It’s why every race and major power is desperate to get their hands on a Roaring Token with unlimited uses!”
The dragon was trying his best to get through to him, but Zane was having trouble wrapping his head around the scale of it all. He knew the upper world was made up of one hundred thousand supreme worlds, but he couldn’t truly grasp how massive that was.
A single supreme world was so vast that gathering and commanding forces across it was next to impossible. And with the three great human powers controlling nearly ten thousand worlds spread far and wide, communication had to be a total nightmare.
The Cobalt Dragon went on. “The token gets its name from a divine beast — one with a single innate ability like no other: a voice rooted in the Law of Causality that can carry across the entire universe in an instant.”
Zane raised an eyebrow. “And someone turned that into a tool?”
“The Supreme Lords of old figured it out. They killed the beasts, harvested their throats, and had their craftsmen forge them into tokens. One beast, one token — two at most, if the craftsman was exceptional.”
“So they hunted them to extinction.”
“Nearly. For a while, Roaring Tokens were everywhere. Realm Lords had stacks of them. The universe was never quiet.” The Cobalt Dragon’s tone turned dry. “Two rival clans once used them to hurl insults at each other across hundreds of worlds for months. Countless beings suffered through it. Eventually the Supreme Lords had to step in and threaten them both into silence.”
Zane snorted.
“It wasn’t funny at the time. And it didn’t last — the beasts ran out. Now even powerful warriors think twice before using a single token. The unlimited version you’re holding…” The Cobalt Dragon trailed off. “There shouldn’t be one. That’s why we’re worked up.”
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Zane turned the token over in his hands, and something clicked.
It wasn’t just a communication tool. In a war spanning thousands of worlds, whoever controlled the flow of information controlled the war. You could coordinate your own forces across impossible distances. You could plant false intelligence in enemy ranks. You could erode morale before a single blow was struck — and the other side couldn’t answer back.
He laughed to himself. This was a good thing.
Zane hadn’t forgotten his real goal: finding his sister, Elara. He had a gut feeling she was safe, but he couldn’t stand the thought of her being out there with some stranger.
If he ever made it to the upper world, maybe he could use this Roaring Token to call out for her. Maybe she’d hear him.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WONDER]
“I just can’t wrap my head around how the owner of The Elysian Keep made a Roaring Token with unlimited uses,” the Cobalt Dragon said, still shaking his head in shock.
“I don’t care how he did it,” Zane muttered. As long as it worked, that’s all that mattered.
After stashing the Roaring Token in his Storage Ring, Zane crushed the second fragment. As the pieces crumbled, a shining chain appeared within the dust.
[SFX: CRYSTAL SHATTERING]
[SFX: CHAIN RATTLING]
He pulled it out, his voice heavy with disappointment. “What the hell is this?”
He’d been hoping for divine-class weapons, or at least one. Instead, after crushing two fragments, all he had was a pile of weird junk.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
When Saul saw the chain, however, he just smiled. “That’s the Death Chain.”
“The Death Chain? What’s it for?” Zane asked.
“Remember when you first entered The Elysian Keep?” Saul asked. “And you triggered the Desperate Melee in the spire?”
Zane nodded. He remembered it all too well.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS ECHO]
[MUSIC: DARK MEMORY]
Everyone had been linked together by some strange energy, forced to fight to the death until only one person was left.
“Exactly,” Saul said. “The Death Chain locks you to an opponent. The chain only breaks when the fight is over, and one of you is dead. Even if you lock yourself to a Supreme Lord, he can’t get away unless he kills you first.”
