Ep 649 - We Shouldn't Be Here
Word count: 1121
[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE]
[SFX: MONSTER GROWLS]
The Dark Nether Cave was crawling with monsters, mostly Shadow-maws and Frost Wolves. But worse things were skittering in the dark, like the Shadow Gnawers—rodent-sized beasts that could chew a warrior to ribbons in seconds. These were just a few of the demonic creatures that called the lowest level of the cave home.
Once, the Ogres had controlled the entire Tower of Sin. They’d harvested the cave’s rich resources, building their power on the main continent while forbidding any other race from setting foot inside. If not for Amber’s incredible talent, no one would have ever been able to challenge their reign. But the Ogres paid a heavy price for their dominance, losing countless warriors exploring the cave’s depths. To survive, they developed specialized tools, and against the fire-fearing Shadow-maws and Frost Wolves, they created the Scorchplate.
It was only a mid-tier enchanted weapon, offering little in the way of defense, but it was forged for a single, terrifying purpose.
Timothy held it in his palm and channeled a sliver of energy into it.
[SFX: FLAMES IGNITING]
A heartbeat later, pale blue flames erupted from the other side, igniting the cavern roof until it looked like half the sky was on fire.
[SFX: MONSTER PANIC SCREAMS]
The Shadow-maws’ wild squawks turned to panicked shrieks, and the Frost Wolves’ guttural howls twisted into frightened yelps. Faced with the roaring blue light, the beasts faltered, their assault losing its edge.
“Now, Timothy! Go!” Malcolm commanded.
[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]
[SFX: WHOOSHING FLAMES]
Clutching the Scorchplate, Timothy shot forward like a streak of light, blasting toward the demonic horde. The pale blue flames trailed behind him like the tail of a comet as he hurtled straight for the monsters. The Shadow-maws shrieked, scattering in chaos as if they’d seen their worst nightmare made real. At the same time, the Frost Wolves surrounding the three members of the Twilight Group whined in fear and scrambled back. With a single enchanted tool, Timothy had broken their siege.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL]
His eyes fell on three columns of smoke rising nearby, and a cruel smile twisted his lips. “You can come out now, Garrick!”
As the smoke cleared, Garrick and two other high-level warriors appeared, looking battered but alive.
“Timothy, thank you—”
[SFX: PUNCH IMPACT]
Garrick never finished the thought. Timothy’s fist connected with his jaw in a sickening crack of bone. The Twilight Group was known for many things, but hand-to-hand combat wasn’t one of them.
Garrick flew backwards like a stone from a sling and hit the ground hard.
By the time he got up, his face was slick with blood. He wiped his mouth with the back of his hand and looked at Timothy without flinching. "There was no other way. Sometimes that's the only way to survive."
Malcolm drifted forward and fixed Garrick with a cold stare. Neither of them liked being used. But they'd had no choice — Garrick was the only one who knew where Zane was. "Where is he now?"
The unspoken threat in Malcolm's voice was plain. If Garrick couldn't answer, he wouldn't be leaving this cave.
Garrick nodded. Before any of this began, he'd quietly planted a tracking technique on Zane — a secret method unique to the Twilight Group, one that left no fluctuations and evaded even the sharpest senses. "They've entered the inner circle," he said.
Malcolm's frown deepened. Even as a War Sage, he had no appetite for the inner circle, especially not with the Tide of Chaos raging. But his mission was clear. The Ogres had been crushed inside the Tower of Sin. He was their only chance to turn it around.
Even if Zane pushed all the way to the core, Malcolm would follow him in.
"Then take me there," he said.
Garrick opened his mouth to protest. After everything they'd just survived, the last thing any of them wanted was to go deeper. But Malcolm's eyes found his — flat, enormous, and utterly without mercy. Garrick had no doubt that a refusal would be his last act.
He swallowed. "I'll… lead the way."
Malcolm retracted his aura without a word.
[SFX: WHOOSHING FLAMES]
Timothy raised the Scorchplate and led the group toward the Bloody Pass. The path they carved was brutish and direct — pale blue fire blazing ahead of them, Shadow-maws and Frost Wolves pressing into the walls on either side. Not one of them dared to charge.
[AMBIENT: EERIE CAVERN SILENCE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
Meanwhile, Zane didn't know he had become prey.
He had no map — and even if he did, it wouldn't have helped. The maps Fabian and Esther carried were already useless here. Every race knew too little about the inner circle, and most who'd ventured in to fill the gaps had died before they could mark anything down.
What Zane had instead was the Crystal Butterfly.
He'd figured it out quickly. When he moved in the right direction, it burned hot in his ring. When he veered off course, the burning stilled. It was the only compass he had, and it was enough.
With the Tide of Chaos raging, the monsters of the inner circle should have been more aggressive than ever, tearing apart anyone who dared trespass on their territory. Instead, the cavern was dead silent. It was deeply, unnervingly wrong.
“This creepy silence is really getting on my nerves,” Fabian gritted his teeth, fighting the urge to scream. He knew making a sound in this place was a death wish, but the quiet felt suffocating, like the air before a storm.
Just as the group followed Zane deeper into the gloom, he suddenly stopped and tilted his head. “Did anyone hear that?”
“Hear what?” one of the Demon Night warriors asked, his voice tight with nerves.
[SFX: DISTANT WOMAN SOBBING]
“Sobbing,” Zane said calmly. “It sounds like a woman crying.”
[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]
“It’s not!” Fabian felt the hair on his neck stand on end. “Zane, don’t be so jumpy!”
Jarrod shook his head. “Come on, man, stop trying to scare us! I’d rather fight a horde of monsters than wander around in this place.”
But Yolande, her pale eyes wide, slowly nodded. “I heard it, too.”
Fabian stared. “You did?”
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
Esther Sterling’s expression hardened. She raised her hand, and a stream of Vitality flowed from her slender fingers, channeling into her ears. A moment later, her face went pale. “I hear it,” she whispered.
“What is it?” Fabian breathed, his voice laced with dread.
“I don’t know.” Esther had been to the inner circle three years ago, but she’d never encountered anything like this.
“It doesn’t matter what it is,” Jarrod said, his voice trembling. “We shouldn’t be here. Let’s go!”
[SFX: WOMAN CRYING ECHO]
[MUSIC: HORROR CRESCENDO]
Just then, the crying grew impossibly louder, and this time, everyone heard it.
The sound of a grieving woman weeping echoed from the darkness behind them, a lament so full of sorrow and unspeakable wrongs that it chilled them all to the bone.
