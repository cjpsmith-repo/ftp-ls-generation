Ep 999 - Someone is Watching
Word count: 1020
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL MOUNTAIN]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CHOIR]
The words “Rise up!” had barely left Zane’s lips when the mountain shuddered.
At the same time, the divine textures began to pulse with a faint halo of light, as if some ancient power was stirring from its sleep. The halo circled the Sacred Mount Lodestone.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING EARTHQUAKE]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL CRESCENDO]
Then, the impossible happened. The entire mountain began to rise, breaking free from the sea.
The Sacred Mount Lodestone didn’t look all that different from an ordinary mountain, standing about ten thousand feet tall with a proportional width. But its weight was another story. While a normal mountain was just rock, this one was made of pure, solid lodestone.
This lodestone was incredibly dense and contained the power of the five elements, making it immune to any single law. That was why it was impervious to any blade. Sure, there were other mountains in the world, some rich with pure iron ore that might have been even heavier, but they were useless to Zane. He had no interest in refining them.
[SFX: MASSIVE WATER CASCADING]
[AMBIENT: OCEAN WAVES]
As Zane ascended, more of the mountain emerged from the depths, water cascading down its sides. Soon, the entire peak cleared the surface and hovered in the air.
Startled by the violent upheaval, creatures nesting on the mountain scrambled from their homes and plunged into the sea below.
[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY PULSE]
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
At that moment, Zane flicked a finger. Dozens of crimson beads of blood sprang from his skin.
“Blood Refinement Technique.”
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL MOUNTAIN WIND]
[MUSIC: ANCIENT RITUAL THEME]
Claiming the Sacred Mount Lodestone required more than just the six-star divine textures. The Myriad Manifestations of Nature detailed a far more crucial step: the Blood Refinement Technique.
Preparing the thirty-three divine textures had been harder than anything he’d attempted before. He’d had to consult the Cobalt Dragon more than once — talking through the purpose of each rune, checking his reasoning, occasionally asking for direct help.
The Cobalt Dragon had worked it out: the thirty-three textures weren’t just a cage for the mountain. They were designed to strip the Five Elements Power from the stone entirely, converting it into pure Law of Earth. Only once the five elements were gone could the Blood Refinement Rune sink fully into the mountain and bind with it.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SWIRLING]
Guided by the technique, the drops of blood spun in the air, merging into a single, palm-sized rune that pulsed with power.
The rune drifted down, a speck of red against the colossal mountain below.
The Sacred Mount Lodestone’s power of the five elements made it notoriously difficult to claim; no single law could hope to control it. But for this exact purpose, the Blood Refinement Technique was perfect.
It made Zane admire his Master all the more. Had the man created all the secret techniques in the Myriad Manifestations of Nature himself, or had he simply collected them on his travels?
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING]
[MUSIC: EPIC CRESCENDO]
As the blood rune touched the mountain, the tremors intensified. Then, the entire massive peak began to rotate, matching the spin of the rune perfectly.
[SFX: MASSIVE STONE ROTATION]
At that moment, all thirty-three divine textures blazed to life simultaneously — each one erupting with its own distinct power. The multicolored flow of the five elements that had always moved slowly beneath the mountain’s surface simply stopped. The mountain, which had always glimmered with shifting colors, turned pale yellow. All that remained inside it was the Law of Earth.
And in that weakened state, wind blades that had previously dissolved harmlessly against the mountain’s surface began cutting into it. Second-layer Wind Law blades — the kind that had barely registered before — left deep dents in the stone.
“Extraordinary,” the Cobalt Dragon murmured in Zane’s mind, watching the transformation. “In the upper world, entire holy grounds use Five Elements Power for their defensive arrays — precisely because it has no absolute weakness. These thirty-three textures could dismantle those arrays just as easily as they just dismantled this mountain. The person who conceived of this method…” He trailed off. “Whoever it was, they were in a different class entirely.”
The weakness was temporary. Once the elemental energy rebuilt, the mountain would be impregnable again. But right now, a single well-placed strike from a Life-and-Death Realm warrior could shatter it.
Zane didn’t intend to give anyone the chance.
[SFX: SPATIAL TEARING]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHOIR]
Six of the thirty-three textures flared brighter than the others — the ones carrying Law of Space. The space around the mountain bent and stretched, forming a contained geometric boundary that enveloped it completely. The mountain was drawn inside.
Then it began to shrink.
Slowly at first, then faster — the mountain compressing as the spatial boundary tightened around it, rotating faster and faster as it did. Ten thousand feet became a thousand. A thousand became a hundred. Then less. The great pale-yellow peak, ancient and immovable, spun itself down until it fit in Zane’s palm.
He closed his fingers around it. It was still rotating, faintly warm, its surface smooth and yellow.
A satisfied smile crossed his face.
The mountain’s true strength would return once it recovered its elemental power. Then, it would be a true fortress.
For now, though, it was vulnerable. He needed to get it somewhere safe before its elemental power recovered and it became impossible to move again.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane had claimed the Sacred Mount Lodestone. He had achieved his goal.
But just then, his heart seized. A deep, chilling premonition washed over him—his sixth sense screaming a warning.
Zane’s brow furrowed. Someone’s watching me. He swept his gaze across the churning sea, his senses flaring out to probe the horizon.
The wind howled, and the waves crashed, but there was nothing. Not a soul in sight. As for the creatures of the Chaotic Ocean of Stars, they’d learned their lesson from him and were cowering in the depths, too terrified to surface.
Zane couldn’t find the source of the spying, and yet the feeling of being watched only intensified with every passing second.
[AMBIENT: VOID RUMBLE]
[SFX: TEARING FABRIC]
Just then, a rift tore open in the space just behind him, a clean slice through the fabric of reality. Inside was absolute darkness, a void like the Umbral Plane itself where no light could ever escape.
[SFX: METAL WHOOSH]
In the same instant, a long, black hook shot from the emptiness, aimed straight for Zane’s back.
