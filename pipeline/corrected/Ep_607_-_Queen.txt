Ep 607 - Queen
Word count: 997
[SFX: SWORD MATERIALIZING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC]
Zane had nowhere left to go—no escape, no technique left to deploy, no defense that could withstand what was coming. But the instant he summoned his sword spirit, everything shifted.
Lily's ghostly form flickered into existence beside him, her translucent features sharp with determination. Without hesitation, her spectral hand shot out and seized the hilt of the Blood Fiend Emperor's wicked sword.
Zane felt a surge of relief mixed with awe—even in spirit form, Lily moved with the confidence of a master swordsman who had faced death countless times before.
[SFX: BLADE WHOOSH]
With a single, fluid motion, she ripped the massive blade free from the Storage Ring’s dimensional space and swung it to intercept the black light.
[SFX: METAL CLASH]
Cheryl didn’t even flinch. She saw the gigantic sword materialize but barreled forward anyway, so arrogant in her power, so certain that nothing could stop the black light screaming from her fingertips.
[SFX: METAL SCRAPING]
Five deep gashes instantly tore across the black sword’s surface. Cheryl’s eyes lifted past the scarred metal, catching a glimpse of Lily just as the blade swung down toward her neck.
Cheryl just blinked, completely stunned.
[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Then, to everyone's disbelief, the blade froze. It stopped dead, inches from Cheryl’s throat, its deadly arc cut short.
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMURS]
The crowd stared, a sea of shocked and confused faces. What the hell was happening? The fight had just hit its peak—why did it just…stop? It was the question on everyone's mind.
Zane let out a shaky breath, relief washing over him at Lily's perfect timing. He still had a few aces up his sleeve, last-resort skills he could’ve used, but he hated the idea of revealing his hand unless it was life or death. He’d never pegged the seemingly gentle Cheryl as someone this ferocious. And he definitely hadn't expected her to throw her life away in a final, desperate lunge.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
But as his heart hammered against his ribs, Zane realized something was wrong. Something was deeply, fundamentally wrong.
Why did my sword spirit disobey me? The question echoed in his mind, Lily’s actions leaving him completely baffled.
Until now, Lily had been an extension of his will. If he commanded her to fight, she’d pursue his opponent to the ends of the earth. If he needed her to defend, she would become an unbreakable shield, no matter the cost.
He’d told her to block Cheryl’s attack, to stop her. But she had appeared, swung the sword, and then… stopped herself. He chewed on his lip, his mind racing. What is going on?
This had never happened.
Why did she stop? And how could she speak? Normally, sword spirits were the culmination of a lifetime of training, a swordsman’s intent given form over decades. But Zane had taken a shortcut—climbing the mountain, absorbing the sword spirits of others.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Logically, even after absorbing so many, his own shouldn't have been able to manifest a human form so easily. But then he'd absorbed the sword spirit of that woman, and everything changed. His own spirit took on her face, her form.
He tried to shake the thought away. It didn’t matter what she looked like; she was his sword spirit. A tool. An extension of his will, not a living thing.
But she had just spoken. And in that single moment, the implications crashed down on him, leaving him stunned.
It all clicked into place. That’s why she stopped the blade just shy of Cheryl’s neck. She had her own thoughts. Her own will. She was unwilling to kill her own kind, even on his command.
Cheryl was still frozen, staring at Lily. The fight, the claws, the fury — all of it had drained from her face. She looked at Lily the way you'd look at something you'd stopped believing in. Then, barely above a whisper:
"Queen…"
She dropped to one knee.
[SFX: CROWD MOVEMENT]
The movement spread through the stands like a wave. Every member of the Demon Night — warriors, generals, seat holders — rose and knelt, one after another, all facing the center of the arena. Even Madeline. The seat holder of the Demon Night, on one knee.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CHOIR]
A group entered from the far side of the arena. At their head was another seat holder of the Demon Night — Elder May, the one Phoebe had sent word to while she'd been tracking Zane through the lower floors. He hadn't been in the Tower when her message arrived. He'd come the moment he could. Phoebe herself was still one floor below — as a Champion, she couldn't access the tenth.
Elder May took one look at Lily and knelt without hesitation. The War Lords behind him followed.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Elder May stopped before Lily, his voice tight with something between reverence and disbelief.
"Queen… is that you?"
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL POWER]
In a flash, Lily turned, her cold eyes locking onto Zane’s. She radiated the power of a queen, an innate authority that demanded the world kneel at her feet.
She looked at Elder May. Two words.
"It's me."
The moment they landed, the air changed. Something vast and ancient pressed down across the entire arena. Warriors dropped to their knees without meaning to. Others gripped the stands and held on. Malcolm, Fabian, Jarrod — all of them bracing, white-knuckled, against an instinct they couldn't name.
Even Zane felt it. He locked his knees and stayed upright through sheer will.
Then Lily turned. Her cold eyes found his.
[MUSIC: TENSE PIANO]
"You..." Zane started, the word catching in his throat before he forced the rest out. "Who are you?"
Honestly, Zane had stared down massive, terrifying beasts without breaking a sweat. He’d even faced the soul echo of the master of the Tower of Sin without an ounce of fear. But now, waiting for Lily to answer, he was a complete wreck.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REALIZATION]
His mind was a chaotic storm. His own sword spirit—a being that shouldn't even be sentient—was about to speak to him.
The sheer impossibility of it all left him utterly speechless. What great power had he been keeping all this time?
