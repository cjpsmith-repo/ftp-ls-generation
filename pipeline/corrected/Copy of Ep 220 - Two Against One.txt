Ep 220 - Two Against One
Word Count: 1208
[AMBIENT: TENSE COURTYARD]
[SFX: MAGICAL BATTLE]
Jerry and Mark shot each other a look. Their fire attacks were completely useless against Zane. They hadn't been scared before, but now, fear flickered in their eyes.
Maybe they'd really screwed up. They shouldn't have treated him like just another outer disciple. They should've looked past his cultivation level.
Even though he was only at the first tier of the Adept Realm, the guy could summon a Mythic Weapon and had an incredible reserve of Vitality.
Clearly, Zane wasn't just some nobody. They'd seriously miscalculated.
Honestly, Jerry and Mark had been blinded by greed. They hadn't considered all the variables, which was probably why Zane had shut them down so easily. It was only now, thinking it over, that they started to realize just how wrong they'd been.
He wasn't some easy mark, and they'd been fools to underestimate him.
Well, what was done was done. Too late for regrets. Since they'd already picked this fight, feeling sorry for themselves was useless. Besides, they hadn't even gone all out yet. They figured if they really gave it their all, they could still defeat this arrogant kid.
Also, aside from getting their hands on a Mythic Weapon, they could probably beat the secret of how he forged it out of him.
No guts, no glory, right? To get to the top, you had to take big risks. And Jerry and Mark decided this one was worth it.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
"Mark, no more holding back!" Jerry warned, his eyes fixed on Zane. He yanked a spear from his Storage Ring. A bloody light glinted from its tip, two Spirit Glyphs etched into the shaft.
[SFX: WEAPON CLASH]
It was a medium tier Spirit Weapon: the Beacon Spear. Jerry lunged, but Zane was a blur of motion. In one fluid movement, he batted the Beacon Spear aside and flicked his own sword toward Jerry.
[SFX: WEAPON SHATTERING]
It all happened so fast Jerry didn't even have time to react. Before he knew it, his Beacon Spear lay on the ground, shattered by Zane's sword. By the time he looked up, Zane had the point of his Streamer leveled inches from his throat.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
Jerry's face drained of color. Time seemed to slow to a crawl as the certainty of his own death washed over him. Staring at the bright silver glow of the Streamer, he knew this was the end. "I'm done for!" he yelled.
Knowing he couldn't dodge, that this was it, he threw his arms up to shield his chest---a desperate, reflexive act.
[SFX: SWORD WHOOSHING THROUGH AIR]
[SFX: SWORD PIERCING FLESH]
[SFX: GROAN OF PAIN]
The Streamer pierced straight through Jerry's forearm and out the other side. Then, just as quickly, Zane ripped it free and drove it toward his chest for the finishing blow. But it stopped short, the tip just breaking through his clothes to prick his skin.
Jerry froze, a cold chill spreading from the point of the blade through his chest. He was sure he was dead. So why had Zane stopped? Utter confusion washed over him.
Terrified, he didn't dare move. He slowly raised his eyes to Zane's, finding his expression ice-cold. Zane snorted, then pulled the sword back. Other than the new hole in his arm, he was unhurt. The Streamer was so sharp that when Zane pulled it away, not a single drop of blood fell from his chest. Even the wound on his arm was shockingly clean; it took a long moment for the first few drops of blood to well up and trickle down.
For an Adept, it was a minor injury. But Jerry had been positive Zane was going to kill him. Who spares an enemy in a fight to the death? He never expected Zane to pull his punch. Jerry couldn't fathom why he'd been let off the hook.
Withdrawing the Streamer, Zane spun and swung the sword in a rising arc.
Just as he did, Mark's machete began to glow with a terrifying, gloomy light.
[SFX: METAL WEAPONS CLASHING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
The silver arc of Zane's Streamer shot across the space between them like a meteor, colliding with the sickly light from Mark's machete. The moment the two lights met, an unearthly wail grew louder. A shadowy black skull formed around the machete's glow, its fanged mouth agape, glaring at Zane as if it wanted to tear him to pieces.
But the light from the Streamer was overwhelming. They clashed for barely a second before the ghostly light of Mark's machete shattered into a million fragments, scattering like confetti.
[SFX: GROUND ERUPTING]
As the fragments of light rained down, the perfectly flat ground around them erupted into a pockmarked mess of craters and gouges.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Swinging the Streamer casually in his hand, Zane walked toward Mark, one step at a time.
Panic was written all over Mark's face now.
He'd just used his ultimate technique. The 'Spectral Howl' was designed to terrify his opponents, to make them drop their guard. On top of that, he had forged the ghostly machete himself. Combining his refinement methods with that weapon allowed him to challenge fighters far beyond his own cultivation level.
And while Mark might not have been Jerry's equal as a refiner, his combat prowess was well-known in their circle.
[SFX: WEAPON IMPACT]
And yet, Zane had just shattered it all with a single, simple swing. The power was unbelievable.
Zane lowered Streamer and looked at them both. "If we were outside the Sect, you'd both be dead right now."
Neither man spoke.
"I'm letting you live because you're Skyreach Sect instructors. That's the only reason." His voice was flat, matter-of-fact. "So here's what happens next: you keep your mouths shut. Tell your disciples the same."
Jerry nodded immediately. "We won't say a word."
"My students won't dare breathe a syllable," Mark added.
Zane almost smiled. Hundreds of disciples had already seen what happened in the Seventeenth Court. The news was probably halfway across Quenching Peak already. But that wasn't really the point.
He turned and walked away without looking back.
"He... he's a monster," Mark stammered, retrieving his ghostly machete from the ground. His voice trembled, his hands still shaking from the impact.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]
"You're wrong," Jerry replied, shaking his head. He might have been the weaker fighter, but he was more perceptive; he could see the bigger picture. "The young are always ruthless. But now, another kind of monster has just joined the Skyreach Sect."
Zane had the strength to take on both of them---seventh-rank Adepts---and he'd beaten them without breaking a sweat. On top of that, he could forge Mythic Weapons. There had probably never been anyone like Zane in the entire history of the Sect.
"So what if he's a monster? He's still just an outer disciple. I get another shot, I'll make him pay!" Mark swore.
"Make him pay?" Jerry sneered, shaking his head. "You? Mark, I'm not trying to kick you while you're down, but he just dismantled you. Zane was toying with us. You go after him again, you're not just asking for trouble---you're asking for a grave."
Mark didn't want to admit it, but his voice lost its edge. Deep down, he knew it too: a guy like Zane was destined for greatness. He should just be grateful Zane hadn't gotten serious.
"I was just blowing off steam," he muttered, clearing his throat. "Besides, no matter how sharp his senses are, he can't hear us from all the way over here... can he?"
