Ep 884 - Full Bloom

Word count: 976

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TEMPLE]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL TENSION]

With every shudder of the lotus flower, a new petal had unfurled. Now, as it trembled once more, they all held their breath, waiting for another to bloom.

Just moments ago, the white-haired priest’s words had crushed the hopes of the powerful warriors gathered there. They’d written off any chance of Zane pulling off some miracle to impress the visitors from the upper world.

[MUSIC: RISING HOPE]

But now, as the flower quivered again, every eye was locked on it, hope flaring back to life. 

The priest himself had said that even in a supreme world, you might never find a single person who could make the nine-petaled lotus blossom completely. Anyone who managed it would command the attention of the heavens. And they needed that attention—the ascending passage had to be repaired, no matter the cost.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]

Suddenly, the priest’s expression hardened. His eyes, sharp as lightning, were fixed on the lotus. 

He understood the significance of this flower and its nine petals far better than any of the other masters present. Most fighters practiced martial arts that followed the celestial laws, absorbing its rules step-by-step, from the lowest rungs up to the Original Laws and finally to the superior ones, like the Law of Causality. But that path had a ceiling. Those who followed the heavens could never escape the cycle of reincarnation, forever bound by its shackles.

Only those who defied the celestial laws could ever hope to surpass the heavens and achieve true greatness.

Of course, not every warrior had it in them. That potential—the true definition of talent in the upper world—belonged only to someone who could make a nine-petaled lotus bloom. Down here, “talent” just meant having a special ability or a unique gift. But up there, it meant you were a being capable of transcending the heavens themselves.

[MUSIC: EPIC REALIZATION]

A fighter who could awaken all nine petals was someone any faction, any race, would move mountains to claim.

Compared to this, the Baptism by Laws was nothing. The priest had been intrigued when Zane grasped Fiona’s Time Law so quickly, but it was a flicker of interest, not awe. It was nothing like the bone-deep shock he felt now, watching this perfect, nine-petaled flower. 

The other masters, recalling the priest’s earlier explanation, now understood they were witnessing something extraordinary. The problem was that none of them had ever actually been to the upper world to see such a thing for themselves.

Up on the platform, Zane watched the lotus in silence. He wanted all the petals to open — not just for the passage, not just for the warriors below. 

The Cobalt Dragon had told him that the number of petals was a preview of how high he could climb. And if he wanted to see Elara again — taken by that young man with a tap of one finger, carried off somewhere beyond his reach — he could only imagine the height he would need to reach. Even nine petals might not be enough. But it was a start.

[SFX: MAGICAL TREMBLING]

Under his gaze, the lotus shuddered — and the eighth petal fell.

[SFX: SHOCKED GASPS, THEN LAUGHTER, THEN TEARS]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]

“It’s open — all of them are open — after all these years —”

“The eighth leaf — we don’t have to seek those unorthodox methods anymore. We can ascend openly!”

One of the Soul Sea masters was openly weeping. He wasn’t alone. These were warriors who had lived for tens of thousands of years, who had crossed dangerous seas and hunted creatures in the deep dark, looking for any method that might carry them upward. Many of their peers had died in the attempt, cut down far from home, their ambitions unfulfilled. 

And now, standing here, they were watching a boy from the lower world hand them everything they had spent lifetimes chasing.

Some of them had even heard whispers of a Dragon Soar Arena — a Dragon Bloodline Clan relic deep in the Heartlands that could carry a warrior to the upper realm. 

But the price was a thousand years of imprisonment on arrival. A thousand years. Even for warriors with their lifespans, it was a bitter trade. They had never dared.

This was different.

Then the ninth petal fell.

[MUSIC: MAGICAL REVELATION]

Nine leaves. A perfect bloom. The lotus rotated slowly, complete at last.

Zane’s mouth curved into a quiet smile. The process had been complicated. But the result was good.

[SFX: CROWD REJOICING]

A collective exhale moved through the crowd like a wave. Relief, joy, disbelief — all of it at once. They had endured. It was done.

[SFX: MAGICAL TREMBLING]

Just then, the fully bloomed lotus began to tremble again.

“What’s happening?” Zane asked, staring at the flower in confusion. If all nine petals were out, why was it still shaking?

“Master Priest, what’s going on?” one of the warriors called out.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

For once, the priest had no answer. The nine petals had formed a perfect, complete bloom. Yet it continued to vibrate, as if something inside was fighting to break free. He was completely stumped, staring blankly at the impossible sight before him.

Nearby, the Holy Maiden gently bit her lip, her mind racing, sifting through secrets gleaned from ancient texts. 

Her secret order had a history that stretched back to the last Cosmic Age, a legacy passed down long before most beings in this era even existed. While the end of an age meant destruction, some powerful heritages could transcend time itself. Because of this, she had access to knowledge that even the priest did not.

A nine-petaled lotus, when fully opened, granted its master a great fortune. But that was only the first level: the Lesser Lotus Flower. If someone could shatter the celestial barrier… they could reach the second. The Greater Lotus Flower.

[SFX: HEARTBEAT]

At the thought, her heart hammered against her ribs.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

“Master Priest,” she blurted out, her voice filled with a new, breathtaking possibility. “Is he… is he trying to open the second level? The Greater Lotus Flower?”
