Ep 1260 - In The Waiting Room

Word count: 1035

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS AMBIENT]
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
Zane was actually gathering Illusion Points pretty quickly, all things considered. He didn’t have to wear himself out traveling between locations; he could just pull out the Teleportation Token and jump to his next spot. 
Even so, he was still leagues behind the top warriors. He couldn’t figure out why. Was he missing something? Did the other top-tier guys know a trick he didn’t?He pulled out the iron ticket, and a voice immediately spoke in his mind.
*“Entry to the Hall of Causality costs five million Illusion Points. Ten warriors are required to open the hall. We are currently waiting for six more. Do you wish to pay?”*
[MUSIC: TENSE REALIZATION]
Six more? His stomach dropped. The last time he’d checked, they only needed three. Why would the number go up?
Then it hit him. Of course. A group of ten must have already gone in. The four people waiting now were the start of a *new* group.
He whistled softly. Five million Illusion Points was staggering, yet others were clearly willing to gamble. Earning points remained brutally difficult - cutting down dozens of ogres yielded less than a million. Few warriors on the battlefield matched Sloane’s strength or point value.
It seemed he had no choice. As ridiculous as the price was, he had to get into the Hall of Causality.
Zane steeled himself and sent his intent into the iron ticket. “I’ll pay.”
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY FLOW]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
The moment he gave the command, he felt a strange, draining pull. 
[SFX: FLOWING LIGHT STREAM]
Golden streams of light, the very Illusion Points he’d fought so hard for, began to pour from his body, siphoned away by the ticket in his hand. It was such a massive amount that the process seemed to stretch on, the river of light flowing endlessly from him. 
As he watched it go, Zane couldn’t help but wonder what it was all for. Were the points really just for show? For a spot on the rankings? It couldn’t be that simple. There had to be more to the Illusion Battlefield and its entire system.
Whatever the real answer was, he doubted he’d get it until the whole thing closed.
[SFX: DOOR OF LIGHT OPENING]
As the last of the points drained from him, the ticket flared bright yellow, and the light split open into a doorway. Zane stepped through without hesitation.
[AMBIENT: SMALL ENCLOSED CHAMBER]
On the other side was a small, square room. Four people already sat scattered around its corners — one Ogre cultivator, and three humans: two men and a woman.
One of the seated men cracked an eye open at Zane’s arrival. “Another one’s here. Five more to go,” he said, then closed his eyes again without another word.
The Ogre cultivator’s gaze crawled over Zane, sizing him up, a look of open challenge in his eyes. He’d clearly been the first one through the door, and just as clearly, he’d already tried needling the others and gotten nothing for it.
Zane glanced around the room. “This is the Hall of Causality? Doesn’t look like there’s a way out.”
He reached back over his shoulder out of habit — the skulls had been gnawing at him for what felt like forever, and their sudden absence left him almost unsettled by how light he felt.
“This is just the waiting room,” the woman in white said, flat and expressionless. “The Hall itself doesn’t open until there are ten of us. Until then, we wait.”
She said it like someone who’d done this more than once before.
Aside from Zane and the Ogre, the other three had entered before. Their irritation was obvious - two newcomers meant two liabilities. But fighting was forbidden here, which explained why the skulls couldn’t follow him through the door.
Zane didn’t take the woman’s coldness personally. He just filed it away.
Waiting grated on him. Had he known, he’d have entered last and skipped the wait entirely.
With nothing else to do, he sat in an empty corner. The woman in white meditated cross-legged, eyes shut. The two men circulated their Qi. Only the Ogre cultivator kept glancing around, searching for an excuse.
[SFX: TABLE TAP]
Zane tapped the table lightly, and a miniature mountain spun free into existence — the Sacred Mount Lodestone he’d refined.
The Ogre cultivator spared it a glance and curled his lip in open disdain. Turning a mountain into a weapon struck him as a waste of effort, plain and simple.
The two human men reacted more quietly. Their eyes flickered toward it for a moment, clearly aware it was no ordinary trinket — but neither one gave it more than that single glance before closing their eyes again.
The woman in white, who had been staring intently at the nearby mountain, finally spoke. Her eyes were wide with a flicker of disbelief that her cold, stiff tone couldn’t quite hide.
[MUSIC: TENSE REVELATION]
“Is that a refined Sacred Mount Lodestone?”
She knew just how difficult—bordering on impossible—it was to refine one. The fact that this guy seemed to have actually pulled it off was astonishing.
Zane simply nodded. “Yeah.”
[SFX: RUNESTONE SEAL BREAKING]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]
With that, he broke the seal on the runestone he was holding and began absorbing the secret cultivation technique etched within it.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE DISCOVERY]
The first piece of information from the runestone flooded Zane’s mind. The magnetic power was a fusion of the Five Elements. To use it, the power of the five Original Laws had to be perfectly balanced and circulated at the same time. The more balanced they were, the easier they’d be to control. But that was the catch—the five Original Laws weren’t naturally in sync.
[SFX: ENERGY CHANNELING]
[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ELEMENTAL POWER]
Zane focused, drawing five distinct streams of Chaos Energy from his core. 
[SFX: FIVE DISTINCT ENERGY RAYS MATERIALIZING]
The energy flowed through his channels and into his fingertips. 
Five distinct rays of light appeared, each representing a different Law Power.
None of them were in balance.
Five powers, five fingers, and not one of them willing to listen to the others.
