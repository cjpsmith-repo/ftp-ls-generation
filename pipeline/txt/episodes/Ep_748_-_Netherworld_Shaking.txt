Ep 748 - Netherworld Shaking

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Back in the Heartlands, you heard stories all the time about the Netherworld, about fiends and ghosts. Whether any of it was real was anyone’s guess.
But according to the Cobalt Dragon, the fiend blood running through Maddox’s veins was very real. The man wasn't just part fiend; he was one, living and breathing right here. Zane had no idea how Maddox had gotten that kind of lineage, but if his beat-up relic of a weapon could summon a monster from the Netherworld, it was probably tied to that Nameless Saber.
Either way, Zane knew one thing for sure: the man standing before him was a whole new level of dangerous.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
A few months ago, a revelation like this would have shaken him. Not anymore. Now, he just didn't care. It didn't matter if Maddox was a fiend from the depths of hell or a god from on high. If this was a fight to the death, Zane was ready to give him one.
"Not enough?" Maddox taunted. "Fine, I'll go all out!"
[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE ACTION]
Zane didn't waste another second. He lunged, his fist crackling with the furious energy of his Dragon Scales.
Maddox’s hand shot out like a viper, catching Zane’s fist just inches from his face.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
The blow could have obliterated a War Sage, yet Maddox caught it effortlessly. A strange rune flared in his eyes—the mark of the Fiend race. Chaotic energy surged through his body. Those bizarre, glowing eyes sent a chill through every observer. Even Zane felt the immense pressure, though his expression remained unchanged.
"Hah!" Maddox sneered, his grip tightening around Zane's fist.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[SFX: CRUMBLING STONE]
He used Zane's own momentum against him, hoisting him into the air, swinging him in a brutal arc, and hurling him straight into the city wall.
His goal wasn't just to hurt Zane. He wanted to use him as a battering ram — drive him through the wall and breach Oracle City's defenses in a single move.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
The moment Maddox surged toward the wall, every rune the Runic Tower had spent years laying across Oracle City blazed to life at once. Defensive runes, speed runes, strength runes, Vitality recovery runes — the entire grid lit up simultaneously, a blinding cascade of glowing lines flooding the stonework.
[MUSIC: EPIC WAR DRUMS]
[AMBIENT: MASSIVE ARMY]
On the ground, Simon raised his hand. Hundreds of thousands of independent warriors advanced in square formations—War General-level fighters low, Inner-Core masters mid-height, Spirit-Tribulation warriors high above. Each formation channeled tens of thousands into a single Life-and-Death-level strike.
Dozens of squares struck Oracle City simultaneously. The full battle had begun.
Maddox's mountain-like form pursued Zane through the air.
Tia shot from the wall to catch him, but his momentum was too great—she'd be carried into the stone with him. Mid-flight, Zane pulled her behind him, shielding her with his body.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
He hit the wall hard enough to punch a hole through it. A section of runes went dark, destroyed on impact. Dust and debris rained down around them.
[AMBIENT: CRUMBLING STONE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
Maddox didn't slow down. He walked through the gap and into Oracle City, his transformed body filling the breach, contemptuous of everything around him. He hadn't come here to fight at the walls. He'd come to end this.
[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]
"Die, Zane!" he roared, drawing back his fist. "Supreme Emperor Fist!"
The golden construct that materialized above him was bigger than before — bigger than the one that had cratered the ground outside the gate, more solid, dense enough to feel like it could crack the sky. It came down toward Zane and Tia both.
Zane moved to intercept it. Tia's hand closed on his shoulder. "Let me."
[SFX: FREEZING WAVE]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL STRINGS]
She raised both hands. A spreading cold rolled outward from her body, and everything inside it slowed — the air, the debris, the cascading light of the golden fist. The massive construct ground to a halt in midair, frozen in place, blazing but motionless.
Tia stepped forward and swung the halberd.
[SFX: SHATTERING IMPACT]
The fist came apart.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
Maddox laughed. "Impressive, Tia. You've grown." His eyes moved over her with open calculation. "My son has always had good taste. Surrender now, and I'll make you the Crown Princess of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom. He never forgot you."
Zane's voice was flat. "Your kingdom doesn't exist yet. And your son — Eden, wasn't it? — you'd really saddle Tia with that waste?"
Maddox's grin didn't waver. "My son has his flaws. You, on the other hand, are about to be a corpse."
Tia smiled at Maddox — a sweet, composed smile with nothing warm in it. "I'm afraid I don't have the fortune to enjoy that title. You'll have to find someone else."
[SFX: ICE ERUPTION]
She thrust out her hand, and a forest of ice spikes erupted from the ground, each one hundreds of feet tall, driving toward him.
[SFX: SHATTERING ICE]
Maddox put his fist through them. They exploded into glittering shards that rained across the street like broken jade.
"Such a shame," Maddox said, his Vitality surging around him like a tidal wave. "I'll have to kill someone so beautiful today. And I promise you, I won't be gentle." He raised his Nameless Saber again, its tip aimed straight at Tia.
[SFX: SUDDEN WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]
A jolt of panic shot through Zane. "Get out of there!" he yelled, scrambling to his feet and grabbing her hand to pull her away.
An evil grin spread across Maddox's face. The Nameless Saber seemed to lock onto Tia, pinning her in place. "There's no escape, Tia! You die today!" he roared. "And don't worry, Zane will be joining you soon enough."
[SFX: DEEP RESONATING PULSE]
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
The blade trembled in his grip as something otherworldly flooded into it. Whatever it was, the power was immense. It was only there for three seconds, but it was enough to stun the entirety of Oracle City into absolute silence. Warriors locked in combat froze mid-swing. Regular folks cowering in their homes held their breath. Everyone, from the most powerful masters to the terrified citizens, felt a primal certainty wash over them: if they moved, they would die.
Zane and Tia felt it too, like an invisible claw had reached into their chests and squeezed their hearts. What in the world was hiding inside that saber? How terrifying was this attack going to be?
There was no time to think. Maddox was aiming for Tia, and Zane’s only thought was getting her to safety.
[SFX: MASSIVE SWORD SLASH]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
"Netherworld Shaking!" Maddox screamed, swinging the Nameless Saber down in a devastating arc right at her.
