Ep 1246 - Sacrifice

Word count: 1087

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
[AMBIENT: MASSIVE WHIRLPOOL CHURNING]
They didn’t hesitate, diving straight into the massive whirlpool that churned between the two dead tree spirits. It was their only shot.
Rowan took the unconscious woman from Zane’s arms. 
[SFX: ARROW PLUNGED INTO FLESH]
She pulled a strange, green-tipped arrow from her quiver, and without a word of explanation, plunged it straight into the woman’s forehead.
The move was so sudden it startled even Zane, but he realized a second later that the arrow wasn’t a weapon. 
[SFX: PULSING ENERGY]
It pulsed with a powerful, life-giving energy—Raw Vitality. He understood then: this wasn’t an attack. It was a cure.
As Rowan worked to heal her, the other warriors watched Zane, their eyes narrowed. They were sizing him up, wondering just how powerful someone had to be to escape those two colossal tree spirits. 
They eyed him cautiously, a chill going through them as they remembered the dismissive way they’d treated him earlier. If they’d actually managed to piss him off, they had a sinking feeling none of them would be leaving this place alive.
A moment later, the woman’s eyes fluttered open. She was completely bewildered. The last thing she remembered was the crushing weight of a branch, the certainty of death. She’d expected to wake up back in the city, safe. She never imagined she’d open her eyes to find herself at the Deadwood Gate, with Rowan standing right beside her. 
It felt like some impossible dream.
Rowan saw the confusion on her face. “He saved you,” she said, gesturing to Zane.
“The human warrior…” The woman’s gaze shifted to him, her eyes wide with gratitude.
Zane just gave her a curt nod, his attention already fixed on the world that lay beyond the gate.
[MUSIC: AWE-INSPIRING ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL FOREST WITH WIND]
Before him stood a tree of impossible scale. Its trunk was so wide it might have been a mountain range, stretching for what looked like hundreds of miles. It soared upward until its highest branches disappeared into the very atmosphere, lost to sight.
“This is the ancient World Tree,” Rowan explained, seeing where he was looking. “The holy tree of the ancient Sorcerer Race. It has thirteen main branches, and our Demon Night warriors are trapped on the ninth.”
“The ancient World Tree? The ancient Sorcerer Race?” The names meant nothing to Zane. 
His mind was on a more practical problem. With his immunity to poison, he could easily draw the bugs away himself and get out unscathed. 
But he felt no urge to volunteer. He was human. Why should he be the one to risk his neck for a bunch of Demon Night warriors he didn’t know? The only one he cared about was Amber—she’d helped him out more than once back on the main continent. Honestly, the rest of them could rot for all he cared.
[MUSIC: SLOW BUILDING TENSION]
“Let’s go up, then,” Zane said. He hadn’t come this far to watch Amber get sent back to the main city before he even found her.
Rowan nodded and took the lead, climbing into the sky. Zane followed close behind, the rest of the Demon Night warriors trailing after them.
[AMBIENT: WIND HOWLING AT ALTITUDE]
An hour of flying later, they still hadn’t passed a single branch — nothing but bare, endless trunk beneath a thickening blanket of cloud. Even the trunk itself began to vanish into the haze the higher they climbed.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS WARNING]
Rowan slowed, calling back a warning. “Careful from here. This is where the World Tree Venom Bugs start showing up. Whatever you do, don’t let the venom touch you.”
The words alone put everyone on edge.
[SFX: CRACKING BARK]
Not long after, a swollen growth split open on the bare trunk beside them.
A black, horned insect burst free, wings buzzing, and shot straight for the group. The others hadn’t thought much of it at first — against a trunk that size, it had looked small. Up close, it was ten feet long.
“World Tree Venom Bug! Move — now!” Rowan snapped, pushing her speed higher.
[SFX: MULTIPLE CRACKS]
More growths split open beneath them, one after another, each releasing another of the massive insects into pursuit.
“We can’t outrun all of these!” one of the Demon Night warriors called out.
Rowan’s voice stayed level. “We were never going to outrun them. This gate’s been opened three times, and even Aurora and Amber couldn’t clear these bugs.”
“Then how did they get past?” Zane asked.
Rowan’s eyes settled on Harley. “Someone makes a sacrifice. Every climb, someone draws them off.”
Harley’s expression changed instantly. “What — Rowan, are you saying —”
Rowan nodded. “These bugs are easy to lure. Harley. Go down.”
“I don’t want to!” Harley shook his head. “I came here to help Aurora! Why should I —”
“Your death would help Aurora more than you standing here arguing,” Rowan said coldly.
“Why don’t you send him down instead!” Harley jabbed a finger at Zane. “He’s the outsider!”
Zane’s eyes flashed at being singled out, but he said nothing.
Rowan’s expression darkened at Harley’s outburst. “Go down and draw them off, and I’ll report it to Aurora myself to cover the Illusion Points you lose, and you’ll be compensated with double that amount on top. Otherwise…”
Fights were rare in the Demon Night’s city, but the punishment for disobeying a direct order was severe. 
[SFX: RUSHING WIND DESCENT]
Harley visibly hesitated, his jaw tight. For a long moment, it seemed he might refuse, but then he grit his teeth, spun on his heel, and plunged downward.
[SFX: ENERGY SPEARS LAUNCHING]
[AMBIENT: INSECT SWARM CHITTERING]
A volley of energy spears, glowing with orange light, erupted from him as he shot toward the ground. The attack immediately drew the attention of the hundreds of World Tree Venom Bugs that had been trailing them. The swarm converged, a chittering mass of black chitin, and hurtled straight for him.
“Go to hell!” Harley screamed, his voice raw with fury. He was being forced on a suicide mission, and he unleashed every ounce of that rage on the approaching swarm.
But for all his fury, the orange spears weren’t strong enough. 
[SFX: SPEARS SHATTERING ON CHITIN]
[SFX: VENOM LAUNCHING]
They shattered against the bugs’ tough shells with sharp cracks, doing no damage at all. In response, a black, viscous venom began to drip from the creatures’ horns before launching from their bodies in glistening, deadly streams aimed straight at Harley.
