[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORCHESTRAL]

 The Sacred Feather Sea was a lot like the Cursed Land - it was a no-go zone for most warriors, located smack in the middle of the continent. But where the Cursed Land was forbidden to the strong, the Sacred Feather Sea was off-limits to the weak.

Every year, the union of the seven divine kingdoms mounted a joint expedition to explore its depths. For them, this sacred place, left behind by the dragon-blooded people, was the lifeblood of their power. Beyond the resources in the vast sea itself, their real growth came from the secret lands hidden within it. To the rulers of the seven kingdoms, the Sacred Feather Sea was the most valuable treasure they possessed.

[SFX: MASSIVE SHIP CREAKING]

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

 All the chosen warriors from the Crystal Martial House boarded a colossal ark that was less a ship and more a palace adrift in the sky, with hundreds of floors stacked high enough to hold tens of thousands of people. Zane followed the other initiates onto the vessel, and once it lifted into the air, it carved a path northwest through the clouds.

[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD MURMUR]

 A buzz of anticipation filled the air. The other Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors were practically vibrating with excitement, and it was no wonder why. To be chosen by the seven kingdoms for training in the Sacred Feather Sea was to be marked for greatness. It was a well-known fact that ninety-nine percent of the Soul Sea Realm masters across the kingdoms had distinguished themselves on their first trip here. The secret lands were the ultimate measuring stick for a warrior’s potential, and every single person here was ready to give it their all.

"Whoa, it's beautiful," someone breathed. "I'd always heard the Sea was golden, but I've never seen it myself."

"They say the light is the residual power from a blood sacrifice," another warrior added quietly. "Billions of dragon-blooded people."

[MUSIC: EPIC DRAMATIC STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL GOLDEN SHIMMER]

 A crowd of initiates rushed to the ark’s deck, craning their necks to get a better view of the shimmering sea below. 

Zane joined them, standing at the rail and gazing down at the golden waters. He could feel it, too—the raw power of that sacrifice thrumming in the light. The fall of the dragon-blooded must have left a scar on the world, a lingering echo of anger and injustice.

He strained his eyes, trying to pierce the distance, but the secret lands remained hidden somewhere beneath the waves. If his guess was right, they were four islands, but even from this height, he couldn't spot a single one on the vast inland sea.

Patience, he told himself. He’d been on edge lately, a coiled spring of anxiety, but worrying wouldn't get him anywhere. He might have no rivals left in the Heartlands, but he was nowhere near strong enough to take on the entire union of seven kingdoms alone.

The ark moved at an incredible speed, yet the journey still took two days and three nights. 

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CROWD]

[SFX: DISTANT RUMBLE]

On the morning of the third day, they finally arrived. The West Dragon River came into view first — one end a deep azure, the other end suddenly and sharply golden where it met the Sacred Feather Sea. Despite being connected, the golden water didn't flow into the river. The two colors sat side by side like oil and water, an abrupt border between two worlds.

More than twenty giant arks were already docked along the riverbank. A sharp voice rang out as the Crystal Martial House's ark descended: "Harlen Divine Kingdom, Crystal Martial House has arrived!"

A large square below was already filling up with more and more Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors.

Zane's eyes moved to the palace steps above the square, where the Soul Sea masters had gathered. He counted more than thirty of them. The Crystal Martial House's three masters occupied a position in the lower half of the steps. As more arks arrived, he began to understand the hierarchy. The stronger the house, the higher their masters stood.

The Harlen Divine Kingdom wasn't the strongest of the four — not by a long way. In the last hundred years, the York Divine Kingdom had led the rankings, followed by the Star Field Divine Kingdom, then the Heavenly Wind Divine Kingdom, and finally Harlen at the bottom. The ranking was based on the performance of each kingdom's warriors and younger generation, not the individual strength of their monarchs — Soul Sea rulers lived for tens of thousands of years and rarely fought each other directly.

The square kept filling. Zane kept his head down. Somewhere in this crowd were warriors from the York Divine Kingdom — the same kingdom whose Holy Maiden had sworn to kill him after what happened in the Celestial Tomb of Vesper. He'd killed two of their Soul Sea masters there. With thirty-plus Soul Sea masters now gathering on those steps, being recognized was not something he could afford.

[SFX: WHOOSHING LIGHT]

[MUSIC: MAJESTIC HORNS]

Just then, a flash of light caught the corner of Zane’s eye. He looked up to see a brilliant glow blooming in the west, like a small sun rising fast into the morning air. As it drew closer, Zane could make out its shape: a golden palace, gliding through the sky with impossible speed. In the blink of an eye, it was hovering near the West Dragon River. A deep, booming laugh rolled out from within.

"Ha! Looks like I'm a bit early. The other old men aren't here yet."

Immediately after, hundreds of figures streamed out of the golden palace, led by a middle-aged man with a reddish, weathered face. One look was all it took for Zane to feel the man’s overwhelming presence. It wasn't just soul pressure; it was the kind of crushing authority that only came from holding immense power for a very long time.

Not even Amber, the head of the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, had an aura like that, but she’d only been in charge for a little over two hundred years. The only person Zane had ever felt something similar from was Lavender. Of course, Lavender’s power was on another level entirely—this man’s felt directed at human warriors, while Lavender’s could suffocate nearly any living thing.

[AMBIENT: LARGE CROWD MURMURS]

 A voice cut through the murmurs of the crowd, sharp and clear. "It's Scott, ruler of the Star Field Divine Kingdom!"

So this was one of the continent's top dogs, Zane realized. As a ruler in the union of seven divine kingdoms, the man’s strength had to be among the four greatest on the continent.

[MUSIC: MAJESTIC ORCHESTRAL]

 Scott just laughed. Instead of joining the other dignitaries, he hovered over the square for a moment, his gaze sweeping over the thousands of young warriors below.

"Where are the disciples from my Star Field Divine Kingdom?" he called out, his voice carrying effortlessly over the crowd.

Hearing their king, the warriors from the Wisteria Martial House and the other schools of his kingdom roared back as one, a sound like rolling thunder.

"Greetings, my King! We are here!"

[SFX: GROUND RUMBLE]

 The unified shout of hundreds of Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors was so powerful that the very ground seemed to shake.
