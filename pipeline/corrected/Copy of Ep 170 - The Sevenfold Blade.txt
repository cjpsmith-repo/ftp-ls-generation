Ep 170 - The Sevenfold Blade
Word Count: 1007
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
“How dare you!” Felix’s voice trembled with indignation, his features contorting into a mask of rage. The casual display of superiority from this upstart ignited something primal within him.
Zane’s arrogance sent a surge of white-hot fury through Felix’s body, causing his fingers to twitch instinctively toward the remaining weapons at his disposal. Never before had anyone spoken so boldly to a scion of House Sterling.
[SFX: WHISTLING PROJECTILE]
Just then, something whistled through the air at an incredible speed.
[SFX: DART FLYING]
A thin willow leaf dart shot from the doorway, aimed right at Zane.
He barely seemed to notice it, calmly reaching out and flicking it aside.
[SFX: DOOR OPENING]
A moment later, a stunning woman strode through the door and shouted, “Felix, kill him!”
Zane recognized her---Vivian. They’d met at the Blackwood Auction House.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY FLARE]
Vivian's attack was all the signal Felix needed. An orange light flared around his hand, coalescing into a large sword. It was the Sevenfold Phantom Blade, a Tier Four Cultivation Method from House Sterling. With a flick of his wrist, the orange-red sword shot forward and hung in the air, its tip pointed at Zane as if held by an invisible man.
Then, the sword multiplied until there were seven identical blades, each more than capable of killing on its own. The orange light in Felix's hand flickered again and again, and with each flash, another blade appeared. Soon, seven swords hovered in the air, each gripped by an unseen swordsman.
“Kill him, now!” Felix commanded.
[SFX: SWORDS SLICING AIR]
At once, the seven swords rushed towards Zane. They moved in perfect concert, a whirlwind of steel closing in from all sides—some lunging with horizontal stabs, others chopping down from above.
[SFX: MULTIPLE DARTS FLYING]
At the same time, Vivian unleashed another volley of willow leaf darts, flicking them at Zane with blinding speed.
Behind Zane, Murry raised his Pen of Judgment. Using his Vitality as ink, he swept a single massive character into the air.
"Seal!"
[SFX: ENERGY COMPRESSION]
The character shot forward — faster than the swords, faster than the darts — and slammed into Zane. The Corrupt Vitality in his hands vanished instantly, suppressed.
Murry allowed himself a smile. "My Seal cuts off your Vitality entirely. Let's see what you do now."
Zane didn't bother answering. He drove the Emerald Crystal in his arm into the character and swung his fist.
[SFX: SEAL SHATTERING]
A surge of blood-red energy cracked the character like porcelain, and Zane's fist punched straight through it. The Seal exploded into fragments.
Murry stared.

[SFX: SWORD SWISH]
With lightning reflexes, Zane pivoted on his heel, easily sidestepping one gleaming blade before snatching it right out of the air.
Felix’s eyes widened in shock. He quickly composed himself, forcing his features into a contemptuous sneer that didn’t quite reach his eyes. “Nice catch. Too bad you only have two hands. I’d like to see you try and catch the other six.”
[SFX: SWORDS FLYING THROUGH AIR]
At his command, the other six swords transformed into a deadly constellation, swarming toward Zane from every direction. But Zane was unfazed.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
“Don’t worry. Just watch,” Zane replied with a smirk. Corrupt Vitality surged from his hand, seeping into the sword he held. In a flash, the blade's color shifted from orange to a deep, swirling purple-black. A satisfied sigh escaped Zane as he admired the change.
Confident in the familiar color, he let go of the sword. This time, however, he was the one in control. With just a thought, the purple-black blade obeyed his silent command, hovering defensively in front of him.
[SFX: METAL SWORDS CLASHING]
The six orange swords rained down attacks, but Zane expertly wielded his stolen blade, blocking every fierce strike.
The color drained from Felix's face as he watched Zane’s performance. He never dreamed that someone---especially this kid---could actually steal one of his swords. And now, the guy had complete control over it.
What kind of bizarre cultivation method is he using? Felix wondered.
But this wasn’t the time for questions. He had to kill Zane, and fast. No more wasting time. He'd lost one sword, but he still had six.
Felix waved his hand again. The six remaining phantoms raised their swords, ready to strike on his command.
[SFX: MAGICAL CORRUPTION SPREADING]
Suddenly, purple-black spots began to bloom on the weapons. Felix watched, his blood running cold, as the corruption spread across the blades like a disease.
“What the hell is going on?” Felix exclaimed.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
His eyes wide with disbelief, Felix felt his connection to the swords vanish. This was insane! He could only watch, helpless, as all six of his blades turned from orange to that same corrupt, purple-black.
“I told you,” Zane said, his voice low and serious. “You’re not strong enough to kill me anymore. With your current power, you should run the moment you see me. It’s a shame you weren’t smart enough to listen.”
Zane's Corrupt Vitality had evolved. Its ability to absorb and convert energy was terrifyingly fast now. Every time his corrupted sword blocked one of Felix's, it created an opening. In those brief moments of contact, a sliver of Corrupt Vitality had seeped from blade to blade. It was just a small amount, but it was enough. The corruption took root, rapidly converting Felix's own Vitality and hijacking his weapons.
[SFX: SWORD TRANSFORMATION]
As a result, one of the seven corrupted swords suddenly swelled, growing into a massive blade nearly ten feet long and several feet wide. It shot directly toward Felix.
“Oh no! Stop!” Murry Cook shouted. “If you kill him, you’ll be signing your own death warrant!”
He couldn’t believe Zane would actually dare to kill Felix. Did the guy have any fear at all? Killing Felix here, in the Skyreach sect... who could possibly protect him after that?
“Don’t run, Felix! I’ll kill him!” Vivian declared fiercely.
[SFX: DARK ENERGY TRANSFORMATION]
A dark energy seemed to possess her. Vivian's eyes turned blood-red, and crimson spots blossomed on her pale skin. Even the willow leaf darts she threw now seemed to drip with blood. Zane watched the transformation, figuring she must be tapping into some strange and powerful attunement method.
“You’re still not giving up?” Zane asked, looking at her in disbelief.
The massive sword swept in front of him, not just blocking the bloody darts but absorbing their dark energy. Then, with a flick of Zane’s will, the door-wide flat of the blade slammed into Vivian.
[SFX: IMPACT THUD]
[SFX: BODY HITTING WALL]
The impact sent her flying into the wall. She hit with a sickening thud and slumped to the floor, unconscious.
