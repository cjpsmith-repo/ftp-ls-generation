Ep 812 - The Little Monster

Word count: 1087

[AMBIENT: VOID DIMENSION]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

The moment Logan broke free from the void, Zane was already there, emerging right behind him. Logan’s counterattack was a wasted motion; Zane’s Formless Sword technique batted aside the Spatial Blade as if it were nothing.

Zane threw a punch.

[SFX: HARD IMPACT]

His follow-up was a relentless storm that completely overwhelmed Logan, giving him no room to even think about retaliating. He was growing weaker by the second. The latest blow sent a fresh web of fractures across his armor, which now looked like it might shatter from a stiff breeze.

“Again!” Logan roared, because he would rather die than admit defeat.

[SFX: SHATTERING GLASS]

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

Another punch landed with the sickening sound of shattering glass. Logan’s Spatial Armor finally gave out, exploding into a thousand pieces just as Zane’s fist slammed directly into his unprotected chest.

The force of the blow ripped through him, and his body shot downward like a falling meteor.

[AMBIENT: OPEN SKY]

[SFX: METEOR FALLING]

[SFX: MASSIVE CRASH]

Their fight had carried them from the void to the skies just outside the enchanted barrier. With nothing to stop his fall, Logan slammed into the grand square below. The surrounding warriors scattered as he crashed into the ground, carving a massive crater out of the stone.

[SFX: WIND GUST]

[AMBIENT: DUSTY BATTLEFIELD]

A strong gust of wind immediately swept the dust and smoke clear, revealing Logan lying motionless at the bottom of the pit.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

No one could tell if he was dead or alive.

The saint-level geniuses from the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms could only stare in disbelief. It was hard to process what they were seeing, especially after Logan’s overwhelming display of strength right before this fight. He had torn through several of the top geniuses from the Harlen and Star Field Divine Kingdoms, and his words had inspired half the warriors present. He was impossibly talented yet humble—the very picture of a hero everyone could look up to.

Most of them would have agreed that among those in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, he was unbeatable.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

But now, his legendary armor had been shattered by sheer, raw power. He was lying in a crater like a downed animal, having suffered a total and crushing defeat.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]

“Excellent! Absolutely marvelous!” the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom exclaimed, his voice ringing with triumph.

His kingdom had suffered a deep humiliation under Logan’s dominance. While they hadn’t held a major advantage in recent years, their geniuses were always competitive. They’d never been beaten this badly. But Zane had just saved their reputation. It didn’t matter if he was from their kingdom or not—he had won them back their pride.

Randy was beaming. He didn't need to say a word. His father had always been fair — and by backing Zane, Randy's standing in the Emperor's eyes had just risen considerably.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

The Holy Maiden moved the moment Logan hit the ground. She crossed the square in a green blur and dropped into the crater beside him. She pulled a jade-white pill from her pouch and pressed it between his lips.

War Emperor York's expression was dark. “Holy Maiden. Is he alright?”

“His internal organs are shattered.” She stood and looked up at Zane in the sky, her eyes cold. “Nothing that won't heal.” Then, without missing a beat: “Little Monster. Fight on my behalf. Kill him.”

A sharp voice cut in from somewhere in the crowd. “By the rules, York's turn to send a fighter has passed.”

The Holy Maiden's jaw tightened. “Then I'll wait for the next round,” she said through her teeth.

[AMBIENT: SQUARE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Somewhere in the square, flat on his back and snoring through the entire fight, lay the Nine Cauldron Martial House's most valuable asset: the Little Monster. And to them, he was more important than anyone knew.

His value wasn't just about his current strength. He was only in the late stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm; he couldn’t possibly go toe-to-toe with a true powerhouse from the Soul Sea Realm.

The Nine Cauldron Martial House had plenty of experts in both the Life-and-Death and Soul Sea Realms. They still needed the Little Monster. Thanks to him, the York Divine Kingdom had flourished over the last thirty years, its younger generation of warriors advancing at a pace that was nothing short of incredible.

He possessed a very unique, almost unbelievable talent.

[MUSIC: ENLIGHTENING CHIMES]

Fighting him was a transformative experience. Any warrior who faced him in combat would often find themselves in a sudden state of enlightenment. If a saint-level genius from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm was stuck at a plateau, a single fight with the Little Monster was practically guaranteed to trigger a breakthrough.

And his talent wasn’t just limited to them. It could also work for experts from the Life-and-Death and even Soul Sea Realms, though the effect diminished the stronger the warrior was. For someone at the level of War Emperor York, for instance, the benefit was almost nonexistent.

[MUSIC: REVERENT CHOIR]

Still, nearly every warrior in the land was drawn to his incredible ability. And even though he was, by nature, a foul and unpleasant creature, everyone at the Nine Cauldron Martial House treated him like a living god.

When the Holy Maiden spoke his name, he stirred. His eyes opened — and there was no white in them. His irises were pure black, like holes cut through to somewhere else entirely.

He crossed to the Holy Maiden's side without appearing to move his feet.

“Hi Sweetheart - you want me to fight on your behalf?”

It was a serious taboo. Even War Emperor York addressed her as Holy Maiden. This Little Monster was being way too casual.

She glared at him. If she'd had any other option, she wouldn't have called on him at all. He had publicly declared, more than once, that he intended to take her into his harem. She had been furious. She had been powerless. The Nine Cauldron Martial House was the strongest martial house in the union, and he was too valuable to touch.

[MUSIC: DARK CONTEMPLATIVE THEME]

Of course, many of the Soul Sea Realm experts across the Union had their own plans for him. At his current level, his talent didn’t have a major impact on them. But they couldn’t help but wonder… what if he could be raised all the way to the Soul Sea Realm?
