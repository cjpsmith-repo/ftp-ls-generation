Ep 37 - The Vitality Pill
Word Count: 1267
[AMBIENT: BUSY INTERIOR HALL]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CULTIVATION THEME]
Throughout its storied history, the Skyreach Sect had welcomed countless gifted students into its hallowed halls. Some disciples possessed such natural aptitude that they ascended through the ranks with breathtaking velocity.
These rare talents could transform themselves within a mere twelve months, vaulting from the rudimentary Bone Refining Level directly to the prestigious Nature Level.
Indeed, certain exceptional students still technically classified at the Bone Refining Level demonstrated abilities that rivaled practitioners halfway to Nature Level, effortlessly manipulating colossal, mountain-sized objects as though they were pebbles.
Their names echoed through the sect's corridors, inspiring awe and envy in equal measure.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Yet standing against the brilliant backdrop of such legendary prodigies, Zane Lowell appeared remarkably ordinary—another face in the crowd of hopeful cultivators seeking enlightenment beneath Skyreach's towering peaks.
Sure, for someone at the Core Strength Level, Zane's performance was flawless. Not only had he held his own against four guys from the Marrow Refining Level, but he still had the stamina to blow past Mile Marker Thirty afterward.
But in the eyes of the silver-eyebrowed elder and Isla Novak, that just made Zane an average candidate, not an exceptional one.
Take Isla's own Misty Peak, for example. Even though it was ranked last among the thirty-three peaks of the Skyreach Sect, she had at least three disciples who could have handled that same attack to pass their Entrance Exam.
So, no, neither of them was particularly impressed.
To be fair, though, it was supposed to be impossible for someone at the Core Strength Level to withstand fifty thousand pounds of pressure.
Yet Zane had stood his ground the entire time, showing no sign of giving up. The fact that he'd overcome every challenge so far was a miracle in itself.
You'd be hard-pressed to find another disciple at his level, on Misty Peak or any of the other thirty-two peaks, with that kind of raw power.
[AMBIENT: ATMOSPHERIC PRESSURE]
Feeling the tension spike, Isla felt an urge to step in, to save Zane from the crushing force.
But just as she made a move to intervene, the silver-eyebrowed elder put a hand out to stop her.
[MUSIC: TENSE MYSTICAL]
"Hold on," the chief elder said, his silver eyes locked on Zane with unwavering intensity. "Just watch."
[SFX: LIQUID TRICKLING]
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
Following his gaze, Isla's jaw dropped as she witnessed streaks of thick, crimson liquid seeping through the pores of Zane's skin, trickling down his arms and neck in rivulets.
"He's refining his core," she gasped, her voice trembling with astonishment. "This is absolutely extraordinary! I haven't seen anything like this in decades!"
Unlike the four brutish attackers who had confronted Zane earlier, both Isla and the chief elder possessed the refined perception that came only with centuries of cultivation experience. They immediately recognized that the red substance wasn't blood at all. It represented toxins being systematically purged from the very essence of Zane's internal organs—a phenomenon typically witnessed only in the most advanced practitioners.
For ordinary cultivators, even those with considerable talent, cleansing their organs was an agonizingly slow and methodical process, often taking months or years of dedicated meditation. To their utter amazement, Zane was expelling an unprecedented volume of impurities in mere minutes, his body glowing with an almost ethereal quality as the toxins departed.
Zane's frame shuddered. A final wave of crimson flooded from his pores — so dense he looked carved from blood. His legs were giving out, knees dipping under the relentless pressure. 
He'd hit the peak of the Core Strength Level; one step from the Marrow Refining Level — but his body had nothing left to give. 
The chief elder stepped forward, gripped him by the collar, and hauled him clear.

[MUSIC: SHIFT TO SUSPICIOUS]
"That kid's future," the chief elder began, a note of wonder in his voice. He was about to declare that Zane had a bright future ahead of him, but Isla cut him off, her voice suddenly sharp. "His future looks pretty bleak, doesn't it?"
"Isla, what are you talking about?" the elder asked, confused.
"I just don't think he's very skilled," Isla said with a dismissive smile. "He got lucky, that's all. Barely scraped through the exam. As for what we just saw... we should probably just forget it ever happened."
The chief elder faltered. Sect rules required reporting a once-in-a-thousand-years talent. What he'd just witnessed clearly qualified.
"Do I need to use the token?" Isla asked quietly.
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
The elder coughed. He knew exactly what she meant — an imperial artifact of terrifying authority, one that could compel action from a power no ordinary request could reach. Clans had been erased by its word. He had no doubt she'd use it.

Of course, the chief elder was no fool.
"Since you've already decided," he said at last, "there is nothing to report."
"Good." Isla's tone warmed by precisely one degree. "And the recording — the letter tablet. Delete it entirely. I want no third person seeing what happened here."
The elder nodded without another word.
"Take this Crystal Talisman. It's your entrance card. Now go wait for the next phase of the exam," the chief elder commanded.
Not wanting to push his luck, Zane gave a polite nod and started to walk away.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
After a few steps, he paused and looked back. "Excuse me, sir. If I'm lucky enough to be accepted into the Skyreach Sect, can I... can I come back here?"
Zane had just discovered that training under that kind of extreme pressure pushed his body past its limits, accelerating his progress. And honestly, he was already getting hooked on the feeling.
At Zane's request, the elder offered a cold smile. "This area is for practitioners who've reached the Nature Level. If you want to train here, you have to spend sect points. We'll let your trespassing slide this one time. But this conversation never happened. Got it? Not a word to anyone."
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS HURRYING AWAY]
Zane nodded meekly and quickly got out of there.
Once Zane was gone, the elder turned to Isla. "What do you think?"
"Well," Isla answered casually, her eyes flicking to the old man. "This is for you."
[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]
On her finger was a stunning Prismatic Gem Ring, set with five different colored jewels. As the chief elder watched, she lightly tapped the ring, and a bright silver pill popped out. She caught it and casually tossed it to him.
The chief elder fumbled to catch the silver pill, his hands trembling as recognition dawned in his eyes.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
"This is... this is a Vitality Pill," he whispered, voice cracking. "An entire year of life..."
For an old man like him, it was a treasure beyond measure. Even the wealthiest cultivators in The Empire couldn't purchase more time. To someone in the twilight of their years, an extra twelve months meant everything.
The elder clutched the pill to his chest, eyes glistening with emotion. "Why would you—"
"Consider it a small token between friends," Isla interrupted, her smile never reaching her eyes. She glanced in the direction Zane had departed. "I have a feeling that boy will require... special handling."
[SFX: RING GLINTING]
Her finger traced the remaining jewels on her Prismatic Gem Ring thoughtfully. "And I prefer to handle such matters personally."
The elder nodded slowly, he understood the unspoken agreement—his silence had been purchased at an extraordinary price.
Whatever Isla Novak wanted with that boy, it was worth far more than a single year of life.
