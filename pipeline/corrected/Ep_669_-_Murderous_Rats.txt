Ep 669 - Murderous Rats

Word count: 977

[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

They'd survived the fall, survived the darkness, and survived each other. Now they had to survive whatever lived down here.

Malcolm said nothing. He figured his own strength was more than enough to keep him safe inside the Dark Nether Cave, but with his inner power completely sealed, he couldn’t access any of it. All he could do now was stand tall and face whatever was coming.

[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND]

The land ahead was barren, broken only by a few withered shrubs. That didn’t mean they could relax. Every shadow felt like a threat, and they were all terrified that some demonic beast would leap out and attack. The group followed Yolande closely, while Lavender, in her ghostly form, floated right beside Zane. As his sword spirit, she was weightless, and whatever power in this place suppressed everyone else’s energy barely seemed to affect her.

As they walked, Yolande suddenly slowed to a stop.

“What is it?” Esther Sterling asked, her hand instantly going to the dagger at her hip. Without their powers, the warriors were like tigers without teeth. If a fierce beast caught them off guard, they’d be torn apart.

[SFX: RUSTLING GRASS]

Yolande’s eyes were fixed on something just ahead. A fat, almost innocent-looking rat poked its head out from a clump of grass. Its beady eyes cautiously sized up the group, its whiskers twitching.

“It’s just a rat,” Malcolm sneered, trying to shoulder past them.

“Careful,” Yolande warned, throwing an arm out to stop him. “Those are Murderous Rats.”

[SFX: MURMURS]

“What?!”

After everything they’d been through, everyone was on edge. They’d also learned that Yolande wasn’t the type to cry wolf.

[SFX: SWORDS DRAWN]

They all scanned their surroundings, suddenly tense. Fabian and Jarrod even drew their swords, ready for a fight.

[SFX: MULTIPLE SQUEAKS]

“There are more of them,” Fabian said, spotting three or four more rats hiding behind every patch of grass up ahead.

“They’re still just rats,” Jarrod said with a dismissive shake of his head. “Nothing to worry about.”

“They’re coming,” Yolande warned again.

[SFX: SHARP SQUEAKING SIGNAL]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Just then, a sharp squeaking sound cut through the air. It had a strange rhythm, like a signal. At the sound, the seemingly harmless rats grew agitated. Their black, beady eyes began to gleam with a vicious light, and their fur bristled, standing on end like needles.

[SFX: RUSHING WAVE OF RATS]

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC SQUEAKING]

Like a floodgate opening, the rats poured out from their hiding spots, forming a gray wave that swept toward the group.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

"Get ready!” Yolande shouted. “These things are fierce. They can tear a person to pieces!”

Malcolm wasn't listening. He took two steps forward — one arm still strapped to his side from Yolande's earlier lesson — and brought his iron-booted foot down hard on the nearest cluster of rats.

[SFX: STOMP]

[SFX: RAT ATTACK]

He missed. The rats scattered instantly, impossibly agile, and in the next second five or six of them were swarming up his body. Some found gaps in his armor and drilled inside. He let out a string of curses, grabbing at his own chest plate, digging them out one by one.

Timothy was having the same problem. Their heavy armor, worth a fortune and built to withstand war, was useless here — worse than useless. It was full of gaps, and the rats knew exactly where to find them.

[SFX: SWORD SLASHES]

[SFX: RAT SCREECHES]

"Standing still is just waiting to die," Esther said flatly. A flywheel appeared in her hand, spinning nimbly as she stepped forward — every rat that came within reach was shredded on contact. "Move. All of you. Forward."

Fabian and Jarrod were already moving, swords flashing, cutting a path through the swarm. Yolande moved with them, silent and precise.

Zane drew his sword and slashed — a clean arc that split dozens of rats in half. But the wave kept coming. Hundreds of them turned toward him at once, a black tide rolling in from every direction.

Yolande frowned. She didn't move to help.

Malcolm watched from the corner of his eye as the rats buried Zane completely. A thin, ugly smile crossed his swollen face. He wanted Zane dead — had wanted it since before the Tower. He couldn't do it himself, not here, not now. But if the rats finished the job for him, that was just fine.

[SFX: SWARMING RATS]

[AMBIENT: OVERWHELMING SQUEAKING]

Soon, Zane was completely covered. The pile of rats on top of him grew and grew until it formed a small, writhing mountain.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

“We have to get to Zane!” Fabian shouted, his duty to protect him overriding everything else. After cutting down a few more rats, he started to rush toward the squirming pile, with Jarrod right behind him.

Before he could take two steps, Lavender’s long spear shot out, blocking his path.

“What are you doing?” Fabian yelled, his face twisting in confusion. “He’ll be eaten alive if we don’t help him!” He knew Lavender was Zane’s sword spirit; if Zane died, she died too. Yet she looked completely relaxed, almost bored, as if Zane’s safety wasn’t even a concern.

Lavender answered coolly, “Don’t worry. He’s fine.”

Just as the words left her mouth, the rats chewing on Zane’s body suddenly scattered.

[SFX: MUFFLED BOOM]

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

A muffled boom came from under the pile, followed an instant later by a deafening explosion.

[SFX: BODIES RAINING DOWN]

Thousands of rats were blasted into the air at once, their crushed bodies raining down in every direction.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]

When the dust settled, Zane was standing there, completely naked. His clothes had been torn to shreds by the rats’ teeth, but his body was barely even bruised.

Yolande blinked, staring at him, wondering how he could have possibly survived that swarm. Esther and Lavender, however, just stared at Zane boldly. Among the Demon Night race, females held a higher status, so his nakedness didn't make them blush in the slightest.

Zane’s face, on the other hand, turned bright red. Those damn rats had humiliated him. He quickly pulled a fresh set of clothes from his Storage Ring and threw them on, trying his best to ignore everyone staring at him.
