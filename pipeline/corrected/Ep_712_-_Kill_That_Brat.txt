Ep 712 - Kill That Brat
Word count: 1036
[AMBIENT: TENSE PALACE HALL]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The warrior from House Calder, a master of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, felt the color drain from his face. Everyone knew that when Finn drew his sword, someone died. Fighting him wasn't a risk; it was a death wish, and every warrior who had reached the Spirit-Tribulation Realm prized their own life far too much for that.
Sure, they were all technically on Emanuel’s side, supposedly united to take down Tia. But really, they were in it for themselves. If Emanuel seized control of the Cloud Hall, they’d all be rewarded handsomely.
It just wasn’t worth dying for.
[SFX: SWORD SCRAPE]
“I’m not doing it,” the warrior from House Calder said, shaking his head. “You go if you want.”
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Emanuel’s jaw tightened. “If you’re all too scared to die,” he said, his voice cold and sharp, “then how the hell are we supposed to storm the ice palace?”
The other Spirit-Tribulation masters just glanced at each other, a tense silence hanging in the air. Finally, someone muttered, “You’re not afraid? Then you go first.”
“Yeah, Emanuel, you lead the way!” another called out. “If you’re the first one through the door, we’ll be right behind you!”
“Just take the lead,” a third chimed in. “We promise, we’ll follow.”
[MUSIC: DARK CONTEMPLATION]
The more they shouted, the hotter Emanuel’s anger burned. If I go first, Finn will cut me down in a second, he thought, glaring at the suddenly brave-sounding warriors. And then the leadership of the Cloud Hall won’t go to my family. It’ll go to House Calder, or Dillon, or Ward. I’m not an idiot.
[SFX: FOOTSTEP FORWARD]
“Dad, I’ll go!”
The voice belonged to Marcus, Emanuel’s oldest son and Frank’s brother.
“You?” Emanuel asked, stunned for a beat before a dismissive smirk crossed his face. He shook his head. “You’re no match for Finn.”
Marcus was only at the Inner-Core Realm. If he charged in, Finn wouldn't even need to bother; Saint Morphen could kill him with a single blow. There was no point in sending his son to a meaningless death.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
“If none of you are willing to move,” Emanuel said, his voice dropping to a low, dangerous growl, “that witch will reach the Life-and-Death Realm. Do you have any idea what happens to us then?”
The question landed like a physical blow, and the crowd went dead quiet. History was not kind to failed rebels. It wasn't just they who would suffer; their entire families would face crippling punishment. The Cloud Hall probably wouldn't wipe out their houses completely, but the retribution would be more than any of them could bear.
“Then what if we all attack at once?” someone finally suggested.
[SFX: CROWD ROAR]
A roar of agreement went through the warriors, their faces twisting into grim masks of determination. They were past the point of no return. They had to go all in. But even with their newfound resolve, the thought of facing Finn hung over them like a shroud.
[MUSIC: BATTLE DRUMS]
[AMBIENT: WEAPONS DRAWN]
On the platform, Saint Morphen and the other defenders had already made their preparations. Weapons rasped from their sheaths in a single ragged chorus.
"Draw your weapons!"
Everyone who stood on that platform had pledged their lives to follow Tia. They knew what was coming. They were ready.
[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]
The two sides closed the distance. On the platform, Finn appeared to be sitting perfectly still — but he was two inches off the ground, hovering, slowly sliding left and right as he tracked the approaching warriors, adjusting his angle with quiet, precise movements. Picking his target.
The Spirit-Tribulation warriors saw him move and their expressions changed. Whoever Finn chose was dead. The question was who.
"Charge! Don't let him thrust!"
[SFX: BATTLE CHARGE]
They surged forward. Saint Morphen and the defenders stepped up to meet them.
Finn's eyes — still bleeding — snapped open.
In his pupils, two faint sword shadows shimmered. Anyone who met his gaze in that moment felt it instantly — a stabbing pain, as if needles were driving straight through their eyes. Several warriors flinched away instinctively.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS CRESCENDO]
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]
Then the aura hit. A vast, crushing presence radiated outward from Finn — the accumulated Force of the Heavens, finally released. Everyone in range felt it: the sudden, vertiginous sensation that the sky itself was pressing down, that Finn was not a person but a fixed point around which everything else was insignificant. They knew it was an effect of the technique. Their minds were still affected.
[SFX: BLADE FLASH]
A blinding arc of sword-light erupted from the palace gates.
It moved so fast that no one had time to react, let alone dodge. All they saw was a searing flash, followed instantly by three sharp, wet sounds.
[SFX: BLADE CUTTING FLESH]
It was the sound of a blade tearing through flesh.
[AMBIENT: DEAD SILENCE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRONE]
For a heartbeat, time seemed to stop. Everyone—from Saint Morphen to Emanuel—stood frozen, stunned into a silence so deep no one even dared to draw a breath.
A moment later, three Spirit-Tribulation warriors swayed on their feet. Their eyes were wide with disbelief and a final, sputtering rage.
“Why… me?” one choked out.
Then, one by one, they collapsed.
With a single strike, Finn had killed three masters of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. He was only at the middle stage of the Inner-Core Realm, but the Celestial Blade technique he practiced was just that devastating. A wave of shock rolled through the crowd. Not even Zane could have pulled off something like that when he first became a War Lord.
“Lucas!”
“Wayne!”
[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD]
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Roars of fury and grief erupted from Emanuel’s men.
Seeing the carnage, Emanuel let out a long, shaky breath. He was lucky. So damn lucky. If that attack had been aimed at him, he’d be one of the corpses cooling on the ground right now.
He seized the moment. “Charge!” Emanuel bellowed, his voice raw with fury and adrenaline. “Kill that brat!”
The remaining warriors were incensed. They were all from third-grade houses, and the three men who’d just been cut down were their brothers in arms. Their shock morphed into pure, unadulterated rage.
The crucial thing was that the technique was so powerful that it took a long time to recharge. Now that Finn had used it, he was just an ordinary Inner-Core warrior—no real threat to a Spirit-Tribulation master.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BUILD]
This was their chance. This was the perfect time to strike.
