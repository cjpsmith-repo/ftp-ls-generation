Ep 92 - Titan Strike

[SFX: HEARTBEAT]
Thump-thump, thump-thump.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
Adrian's heart thundered against his ribs like a war drum. Fresh blood trickled from the jagged gash on his face, staining his pale cheek with rivulets of crimson that caught the light as they descended.
"I can't believe it..." he whispered, his voice trembling with a mixture of awe and indignation. "This is a total shock. No one warned me about this."
With deliberate slowness, Adrian lifted a trembling hand to his wound, wincing slightly as his fingers made contact with torn flesh before wiping the blood on his sleeve, leaving a dark smear across the expensive fabric. He turned to face Zane, eyes narrowing with newfound wariness.
[SFX: HAUNTING SCREAM ECHO]
"You hit Perrin, and he screamed like a cornered rat," Adrian observed, recalling the haunting sound that still echoed in his mind. "That had to be a soul attack—nothing else explains that kind of reaction. No wonder he went down so fast."
He stared at Zane intently, his expression cycling through disbelief, fury, and a hint of reluctant respect. "A kid at the Marrow Refining Level who's mastered the Mind-breaking Art..." Adrian shook his head, the implications sinking in with terrifying clarity. "It's just unbelievable! Who in the seven hells taught you such forbidden techniques?"
Adrian had seen his share of fights, and having just sidestepped death himself, he realized how stupid he'd been to think Zane was an easy mark.
"Alright, I'll admit it. I messed up," Adrian said, his voice dangerously calm as he wiped away the fresh blood seeping from the cut.
"You're a killer hiding in plain sight. If it weren't for my family's Guardian Locket and my own soul being strong enough to take the hit, that Shattered Dagger of yours would've finished me."
He touched the wound on his cheek again. "This gash is the price I pay for underestimating you. You got me good, and you should be proud of that. But you're still going to pay for what you've done, even if you "are" Elara's brother!"
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
As the words left his mouth, a swarm of Starlight Lilies, blazing with a piercing pink glow, bloomed from his hands. But these weren't beautiful flowers; they were weapons of death.
"I'm going to grind you into dust!"
[SFX: IMPACT BARRAGE]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
The Starlight Lilies shot toward Zane, too fast for him to even think of escaping. In an instant, his body was buried beneath them, each ethereal flower carrying the weight of ten thousand pounds. Over a hundred of them piled on, pinning him to the summit of Infernal Peak, each one heavier and more powerful than the last.
[MUSIC: TENSE MAGICAL ATMOSPHERE]
The Runic Power Circle was an impossibly complex web, a creation so intricate that even Jude, the top-ranked Magic Circle master, had struggled to assemble it. No one else stood a chance of dismantling it.
Elara bit her lip, her eyes locked on the spot where Zane had disappeared under the mountain of Starlight Lilies.
"Okay!" she pleaded, her voice cracking. "I won't destroy the Energy Matrix, but you have to save my brother!"
The old man smiled. "I don't need to. It seems help has already arrived."
"Who is it?" Elara asked, her voice tight with anxiety.
[SFX: SKYCRUISER ENGINE APPROACHING]
The old man pointed toward a Skycruiser gliding toward Infernal Peak.
"Her? Master Isla Novak?" Elara's face was a mask of doubt. "Why would Isla Novak come to save my brother?"
"Zane is a student of Master Isla," the old man explained. "Now that she's here, he'll be fine."
"I hope so."
Hearing that, Elara's shoulders relaxed slightly. She turned her gaze back to the mountainside, watching silently. If anything went wrong, she was ready to shatter the Runic Power Circle herself.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[SFX: CRUSHING WEIGHT]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DANGER]
If Zane had been just an ordinary student at the Marrow Refining Level, the crushing weight—well over a hundred thousand pounds—would have flattened him into a bloody pulp.
Even Nature Spirits would be crushed under this kind of pressure.
Zane was fighting back with everything he had, but the force was suffocating him, bearing down like a mountain on his back. Just trying to draw a breath felt impossible.
"It's insane that someone in the Soul Realm is this strong," he thought, his own power feeling fragile as glass. "I don't stand a chance!"
He was completely paralyzed, but he refused to give up. His mind, detached and unnaturally calm, was already searching for a way out. It was a strange state of being, one that allowed him to stay focused on the edge of death, to ignore the searing pain, as if his cracking body belonged to someone else.
[SFX: CRACKING SKIN]
Cracks spiderwebbed across his skin. Blood poured from the fissures, and he knew he was running out of time.
"What other options do I have?" Zane thought frantically. "Shattered Dagger, dragon scales, the Psyche Spike... I've tried them all, and none of them were enough."
The only technique he hadn't used was Titan Strike.
It was a Level-Five Technique, the only one of its kind known to the Skyreach Sect. Its power was said to be immense, but it had always been beyond his grasp. He'd spent ages practicing, trying to summon just a whisper of Corrupt Vitality, but no matter how hard he tried, he'd come up with nothing.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC POWER SURGE]
Just as he was about to give up, something shifted. He didn't have to force it anymore. The Corrupt Vitality roared to life on its own, a hungry void swallowing his regular Energy.
[SFX: ENERGY EXPLOSION]
As the energy in his core drained away, the Corrupt Vitality exploded in its place, flooding his entire being in an instant.
"Titan Strike is a Level-Five Technique... this Corrupt Vitality has to be way more powerful than my regular energy. Let's see what it can do!"
Zane clenched his fists. He couldn't relax for a second while channeling this new power. He knew he was still too weak, that he had no real shot at winning against a Soul Realm master like Adrian.
But Zane was fueled by a defiant courage. He would fight with everything he had, even if there was only a sliver of a chance he'd make it out alive.
[SFX: LOW HISSING]
[AMBIENT: SIZZLING DISSOLUTION]
A low hissing filled the air. As Zane forced the Corrupt Vitality through his body, something strange began to happen. Threads of the dark energy slowly seeped out through the cracks in his skin. The moment they touched the Starlight Lilies, the ethereal flowers began to sizzle and dissolve.
