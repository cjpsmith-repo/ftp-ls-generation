Like Evan, the other hundred thousand disciples of the Skyreach Sect were totally floored by what they were seeing.
Everyone knew Zane was a monster when it came to basic swordsmanship.
But Evan had run his mouth in front of the entire Sect, bragging that he wouldn’t even need to draw his sword to take Zane down. What was he thinking?
The pathetic part was that most of the disciples had thought the exact same thing.
But after getting a taste of Zane's foundational swordsmanship, some disciples had been completely blown away. They hyped him up so much that everyone else was starting to see Evan's boast for what it was: absolutely ridiculous.
So now, they were all on the edge of their seats, just waiting to see Evan eat his words. They'd get a real kick out of seeing him forced to forfeit.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
At that moment, a single thought echoed through the minds of a hundred thousand disciples gathered around the arena. You personal disciples are always so damn arrogant, thinking you’re untouchable. Let’s see how you handle Zane’s sword skills now! You’re gonna have to tap out and call him Master!' The anticipation hung thick in the air as the crowd leaned forward, their faces alight with vindictive pleasure at seeing the mighty brought low—a rare satisfaction that united them all in this breathless moment of potential humiliation for one who had looked down on them for too long.
[SFX: SWORD BATTLE]
Evan was mortified. He'd talked a big game, and now he couldn’t back it up. The regret hit him like a punch to the gut.
How the hell was Zane’s basic swordsmanship this good?
The move Zane was using was as basic as it gets. A horizontal stab—honestly, the simplest, most fundamental move in the book.
Normally, countering a move like that was child’s play; it was the very first technique most swordsmen learned. To build a solid foundation, a kid would practice it thousands, even tens of thousands of times.
It was just a simple horizontal stab, so why couldn’t he dodge it? No matter which way he moved, he was trapped.
It looked like basic swordsmanship, so how were his moves so damn effective? Evan felt like he was losing his mind.
Of course, Evan had no idea that Zane had been crushing it in the All Peaks Competition using only these techniques. While Zane’s moves were simple, the crowd knew it was no surprise that Evan was on the ropes.
It seemed like Evan was the only one living under a rock, completely clueless about Zane’s incredible performance in the tournament so far.
After all, for the last three years, Evan had been the most talented disciple in the Skyreach Sect, outclassing everyone with his raw power.
And his strength had only grown since then.
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
Seeing the razor-sharp blade slicing toward him, Evan's hand instinctively shot to his own sword. But then he remembered his promise and froze. Instead, he twisted his body, contorting himself to just barely dodge Zane's attack.
Evan had managed to avoid the first strike, but it left him completely off-balance. Then came Zane's second strike. Then a third. A fourth.
[MUSIC: TENSE REALIZATION]
Clarity cut through Evan's panic. He was completely boxed in. He had no other choice.
"Master!"
[SFX: TRIUMPHANT SHOUT]
"Ha!"

[SFX: LAUGHTER]
Not far away, Elara couldn’t hold back a laugh when she heard Evan call Zane his master. She was practically doubled over, gasping for air.
Evan had lost plenty of bets to Elara before and had been forced to call her ’Queen.’ Now, he was keeping his word and calling Zane ’Master.’ He’d dug his own grave, and no one was about to throw him a rope.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD LAUGHTER]
Elara's laugh broke the silence, and then the entire crowd exploded. The sound of their laughter crashed over the arena, ringing in Evan's ears.
He was a total laughingstock.
Evan’s face went beet red. He got even angrier when he saw Zane just give him a small, knowing nod.
'I’m your senior disciple,' he thought furiously. 'I just called you ’Master,’ and you have the balls to just accept it? Fine. I’ll make you regret that.'
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
When the laughter finally died down, Evan's voice cut through the silence. "Yes, I underestimated you. Now, I want a rematch—this time, with swords. The loser has to call the winner ’Master’ whenever we meet from now on. Zane, you in?"
Zane was stunned into silence. He just stood there, staring at Evan like he’d grown a second head.
The entire crowd of a hundred thousand disciples stared back at Evan, their jaws on the floor. Was this guy serious? Zane had already agreed to a sword fight to decide the champion of the All Peaks Competition.
And now Evan was tacking on another insane demand?
Evan seemed to realize he'd overplayed his hand. He clenched his jaw and amended his challenge. "Well, I can’t force you, but let’s have a proper fight with real sword techniques!"
[SFX: SWORD HUM]
The words were barely out of his mouth when his longsword hummed, the tip leveling at Zane. Suddenly, Evan's entire expression hardened into stone.
Even though it was only a Mysterious-grade weapon, the blade in Evan’s grasp commanded the authority and presence of a legendary Mythic weapon.
His stance shifted subtly, his fingers curled around the hilt with practiced precision, revealing years of disciplined training beneath his previously frivolous exterior.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
Zane felt a shift in Evan’s aura—gone was the buffoon, replaced by a deadly swordsman whose focused intensity reminded him of the formidable Iceni warriors he had encountered on Killing Sword Mountain.
The air between them crackled with tension as Zane reassessed his opponent. As a senior disciple of the Skyreach Sect, Evan’s reputation clearly hadn’t been built on empty boasts. His eyes now reflected a cold calculation that sent a chill down Zane’s spine, warning him that he was definitely not going to be a pushover.
