Ep 1055 - Unlimited

[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

The moment Zane said he had a Roaring Token, Lavender’s expression shifted.

Zane had given her the short version of what happened after she passed out, but he’d barely had time to get into the details, so he’d left out the part about the Roaring Token.

But Zane trusted Lavender completely, so there was no reason to keep anything from her.

[SFX: DRAMATIC REVEAL]

Of course, Lavender was stunned to hear Zane even had a Roaring Token. While it wasn’t a big deal to her, a single Roaring Token was a fortune to an ordinary warrior—and especially to Zane.

“Did Yolande give this to you?” Lavender asked after a moment’s thought.

But Zane just shook his head.

“No, it’s mine,” he said, handing her the token. “You use it. It’s better if I don’t speak.”

[MUSIC: CURIOUS TENSION]

“What? Why not?” Lavender asked, her curiosity piqued.

“You’ll see,” Zane said with a small, knowing smile.

Lavender didn’t know that Zane had already used a Roaring Token to curse out Supreme Lords. He’d even used one to count numbers just to kill time. His voice was probably recognizable by now.

Then again, with trillions of creatures across the universe, it wasn’t unheard of for two people to sound alike.

Still, if he used the token now, the Queen of Punishment might put two and two together. So, he was letting Lavender do the talking.

As Lavender watched him with a puzzled expression, Zane helped her activate the Roaring Token.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

Her voice boomed from it, cold as ice.

[SFX: MAGICAL RESONANCE]

“Queen of Punishment, who said I don’t have a Roaring Token? I’m curious how long you can keep up that arrogant act, you witch! I am Lavender, the Queen of Slaughter of the Demon Night, and I will always be queen. As long as the Demon Night exists, I will not die. And I swear on it, I will tear you from that throne!”

[AMBIENT: COSMIC ECHO]

Her voice thundered from the token, echoing across every supreme world.

[SFX: SUDDEN VOICE INTERRUPTION]

“Lavender, you’re really back! Where are you?” Supreme Lord Leroy’s voice cut in an instant later.

“Well, well. I didn’t expect you to get a Roaring Token,” came the Queen of Punishment’s cold reply. “I underestimated you. But since you’ve come to the Upper World — prepare to die.”

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The moment Leroy’s voice cut in, a bitter smirk touched the Queen of Punishment’s lips. She couldn’t help but shake her head; Zane truly was vicious.

But as Lavender finished speaking, she glanced down at the token. It was still whole.

She stared at it. A Roaring Token disintegrated the moment it was used — that was the law. Every warrior in the universe knew it.

“What’s happening?” she murmured, turning it over in her hands.

[SFX: ROARING TOKEN ACTIVATION]

Before she could ask, Zane reached over and activated it again. “Keep going,” he said. “Say everything you want to say.”

[SFX: MAGICAL RESONANCE]

Lavender’s sharp inhale of shock echoed out across every supreme world.

She looked at him. He just shrugged. “It works unlimited times.”

“What—”

Her stunned cry rang through the universe a second time.

Zane smiled at that. Lavender had always seemed untouchable — someone nothing in the world could rattle. It was quietly satisfying to see her looking like this.

Lavender stared at the token for a long moment, then turned it over again, as if she still didn’t quite believe it. She had always believed Zane was destined for great things. But she had never imagined he would arrive in the Upper World and immediately possess something like this — something that, in the right hands, could reach every living creature in the universe at once.

She set her jaw. “Help me activate it.”

As for the Supreme Lords from the other races, they could only remain silent.

The feud between the two Demon Night queens was none of their business, but that didn’t stop the other races from enjoying the show.

[AMBIENT: TENSE THRONE ROOM]

The Demon Night race wasn’t large, nor did they control many supreme worlds, but their power was undeniable. The more intense their infighting became, the happier the other races were.

Still, the other races couldn’t help but find it strange. Wasn’t the Queen of Slaughter supposed to be on the brink of death? Where on earth did she get so many Roaring Tokens? And why did it suddenly feel like the tokens were being devalued?

First, there was that mysterious singer, then some unknown young man, and now the Queen of Slaughter was tossing Roaring Tokens around like they were pebbles. How did they all get their hands on so many?

[AMBIENT: MIST PALACE]

Far away, in the depths of the Mist Palace, Yolande floated in her bath, eyes half-closed. Lavender’s voice drifted into her mind — sharp, relentless, unwavering.

The corner of her mouth curved upward. She shook her head slowly.

Zane really was something else.

[MUSIC: GRINDING TENSION]

Two hours passed. Then four. Then six.

Lavender’s throat had gone dry long before, but she did not stop. Every time the token should have crumbled to dust, it held. Every time the Queen of Punishment fell silent, Lavender’s voice filled the void.

In her palace, the Queen of Punishment sat with her hands clamped over her ears — useless as ever against a sound that poured directly into the mind.

She looked at the Roaring Tokens she had left. The number had dropped sharply. She had burned through over a hundred trying to fight back. Now she stared at the small pile remaining and felt something she had not felt in a very long time.

Helplessness.

[SFX: ROARING TOKEN ACTIVATION]

“Enough!” she roared, activating a Roaring Token of her own.

[MUSIC: DARK MENACING STRINGS]

“Enough?” Lavender’s voice dripped with ice. “Beg me. Beg me, and maybe I’ll stop.”

“You’re nothing but a fading spirit on its last legs. You think you’re worthy of my begging? You must be dreaming!” the Queen of Punishment shot back. How could she possibly beg for mercy in front of the entire Demon Night race?

“Then I’ll just keep going,” Lavender’s voice echoed back. “I could do this forever. Could you?”
