Ep 796 - Marital Troubles

Word count: 1154

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: DARK MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

 The Feather Emperor’s and Divine Emperor’s Secret Lands were both notoriously dangerous, but they were nothing compared to the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. 

In the Feather Emperor’s land, you were mostly safe as long as you followed the rules and knew what you were doing. The Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, on the other hand, was a place of pure corruption. Only the Dragon-blooded were allowed inside; no other living thing could survive its lethal atmosphere.

 The union of the seven divine kingdoms had debated the problem for years before finally landing on a grisly workaround. The corpses of the long-dead Dragon-blooded were perfectly preserved there, many treated with secret techniques that kept them from decaying. The idea was to possess these bodies, using them as vessels to bypass the blood rites that guarded the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land.

But even with this plan, their exploration had barely begun. Acquiring the Dragon-blooded corpses was difficult, and using them came with a whole other set of restrictions.

Randy had given Zane the rundown on all this. The four lands were originally meant to work in harmony, with the Sacred Feather Sea serving as the ancestral home of the Dragon-blooded, but something had gone wrong, and now the whole region was locked down. Zane knew he couldn't just charge into the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. If the combined power of seven kingdoms was struggling to make headway after years of trying, what chance did he have? 

Getting the Ancient Heartstones and the Ancestral Dragon Egg was going to be a monumental task.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

 Even without powerful warriors from the Soul Sea Realm or the Life-and-Death Realm standing in his way, getting in there alone would be next to impossible. Working with Randy seemed unavoidable.

Randy understood this perfectly. He couldn't match his brother's resources or his network — the Crown Prince had spent years building alliances, locking up every talented young warrior in the kingdom before Randy even had a chance. 

The only card Randy held that the Crown Prince didn't have was honesty. No staged performances, no guards demanding people kneel, no golden invitations delivered with calculated warmth. Just straight talk. He'd been that way with Zane from the first moment, and he wasn't about to change now. It was the only thing that had gotten Zane through his door.

“After I get the Harlen Token, I’m putting a team together to head into The Sacred Feather Sea,” Randy said, his smile not quite reaching his eyes. He was clearly worried Zane might back out. “Since you’re so set on those Ancient Heartstones, you’ll have to pull your weight.”

"No problem," Zane said.

"Good!" Randy clapped his hands. "Bring the wine."

[SFX: WINE POURING]

 A servant appeared moments later with a pair of jade cups and a flask of something deep red — almost blood-colored. The smell reached Zane before the cup did: sweet, rich, nothing like ordinary liquor.

"Bloodrose wine," Randy said. "The finest thing the Harlen Divine Kingdom produces. Even at our level, it'll get you drunk." He picked up his cup, drank it in one clean motion, then turned the cup upside down with a grin. "See? Nothing to worry about."

Zane raised an eyebrow, then raised his cup. With his constitution, poison was the least of his concerns. He was just curious.

[MUSIC: WARM STRINGS]

 It was remarkable — warm going down, sweet on the back of the throat, and entirely unlike anything Zane had tasted on the main continent or in the Heartlands. The Bloodrose wine was something the Divine Kingdoms had developed specifically for cultivators, brewed with materials that could actually reach past their constitution and do what wine was supposed to do. 

By the second cup Zane felt the edges of things soften slightly. 

By the third, both of them were, for the first time in a long while, just a little bit drunk. It was a strange feeling — not unpleasant. They talked easily, the earlier tension of the assessment and the Crown Prince's visit finally bleeding out of the room.

After a while, Randy turned to one of the maidservants. "Go and fetch the Princess Consort."

The maidservant hesitated. "Your Highness, I believe she has already retired for the evening—"

"Then wake her up," Randy said.

The maidservant nodded and left. Zane watched this with mild confusion. What was Randy doing?

Randy refilled his cup, swirled it once, and smiled at Zane. "There's a saying in the Harlen Divine Kingdom — if you can see Hester dance, you'd give up a hundred years of your life. She's considered the most beautiful woman in the kingdom." He drank. "I'll let you be the judge."

Zane didn't say anything, but he felt a flicker of unease. Hester was Randy's wife — the Princess Consort. Summoning her to perform for a guest in the middle of the night felt like something else entirely.

After a few minutes, the woman entered. She was Spirit-Tribulation Realm, clearly from a prestigious family, and her bearing showed it — composed, refined, the kind of person accustomed to being treated with a certain deference. She was also visibly furious.

Hester had been placed in this household by the Crown Prince. Married to Randy under the Crown Prince's arrangement, she reported his movements at regular intervals. Randy knew it. She knew he knew. The two of them maintained the surface of a marriage while regarding each other with wary, mutual suspicion. It was a miserable situation for both of them, and none of it was her fault.

"Dance for us," Randy said.

Her expression hardened. "I will not."

[SFX: SHARP WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

 The temperature in the room dropped. A murderous aura erupted from Randy — sharp, cold, absolute.

"Dance," he said quietly, "or I will kill you."

Hester's composure cracked. She knew with absolute certainty that he meant it — and that if he followed through, no one in this household would say a word in her defense. Every person here answered to the Crown Prince, not to her.

She gritted her teeth. And she danced.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC DANCE MELODY]

Zane watched with a hollow feeling in his chest. The hatred in her eyes was unmistakable. He understood what Randy was doing — venting his rage at the Crown Prince through the only person in this gilded cage he could actually touch. He was also, in his clumsy way, trying to build trust with Zane, showing him the full picture of how completely his brother had boxed him in.

But the Lord was not yet twenty, and he didn't think about the cost of things. Forcing his own wife to perform for a stranger was a disgrace to himself, whatever the provocation. It was pathetic. 

And sitting through it made Zane feel like he was perched on the edge of someone else's very ugly wound.
