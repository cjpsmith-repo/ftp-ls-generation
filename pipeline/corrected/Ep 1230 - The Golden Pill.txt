Ep 1230 - The Golden Pill

Word count: 1217

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUMS]
The golden hammer-shadows materializing before Zane were part of a mysterious technique, thousands of years old. 
[SFX: FLICKERING MATERIALIZATIONS]
More and more of them flickered into existence, making it impossible to tell which were solid and which were just illusions. It was clearly a difficult puzzle to solve.
But Zane had no intention of solving it. As hopeless as it looked, his plan was simpler: just hit back, harder. It felt more efficient than trying to find some clever way out.
Everyone knew Norton’s strength was off the charts. It was a given that no warrior at his level could match him in a contest of raw power. Going head-to-head with Norton on strength alone was supposed to be suicide.
Zane, however, wasn’t at a disadvantage. He had the power of the Dragon Scales and an enhanced body. Against him, even a much stronger warrior was vulnerable, let alone someone at the same level. Knowing this, Zane didn’t retreat a single step. 
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS CHARGING]
He charged straight at Norton.
“You’ve got some nerve,” Norton growled, watching Zane rush toward him instead of running away. He braced himself, pouring every ounce of strength into his arms as the Soul Sea inside him began to churn. 
[SFX: ENERGY SURGING]
All his immense power surged, coiling for the kill. 
Zane opened up the power of the Dragon Scales to a full ten thousand pieces. Strength flooded through him like a tide. He wasn’t interested in defending against the storm of hammer shadows raining down — Norton himself was the only target that mattered.
[SFX: SPEED BURST]
Zane’s speed spiked by thirty percent. He drove one hand into a fist — an ordinary-looking Forward Punch carrying far more power than it had any right to.
Watching him come, Norton laughed, certain Zane’s move was some kind of desperate last resort. But Norton wasn’t reckless underneath the bravado. He’d already been smashed through twice by this man, and he wasn’t about to risk finding out what a third hit could do. 
With a flick of his wrist, he pulled one golden hammer back in front of his chest as a shield, keeping the rest of the storm bearing down on Zane’s head. If they killed each other here, half of both their points would be left lying in the open for any opportunist to scoop up — an outcome he had no intention of allowing.
It was the right call. It just wasn’t enough.
[SFX: LOUD CRACK]
Zane’s punch hit the shielding hammer dead center — and the force behind it was nothing like what its size suggested. The hammer split down the middle, and Zane’s fist drove straight through the gap into Norton’s chest.
Zane’s eyes flickered at the sound. *A defensive treasure,* he thought, a flicker of annoyance running through him.
Zane was more worried about Norton having a golden pill. If Norton crushed it to kill himself, all of this would be for nothing.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[SFX: BODY THROWN]
Norton was thrown backward by the overwhelming force, and he knew he was seriously hurt. 
[SFX: ENERGY COLLAPSE]
He could feel the immense pressure still crushing his internal organs, and the shockwave was so powerful it even shook his inner world, causing his Soul Sea to collapse.
But Norton wasn’t thinking about his own life. He was desperate to protect the master’s Illusion Points. He could be brought back to life if he died, but if Zane took those points, he would have to face the young master’s punishment—a fate far worse than death.
[SFX: OBJECT MATERIALIZES]
Because of this, the moment he was sent flying, a golden pill appeared in his hand. But just as his palm started to close around it, he heard a cold sneer.
Zane had already seen this coming. 
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: WHOOSHING MOVEMENT]
With a burst of speed, he closed the distance between them, rendering Norton’s desperate move useless. 
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
A sudden streak of blue light flashed.
Shock washed over Norton as a dull, numbing ache bloomed in his right wrist. 
Zane’s sword, Thundergust, had sliced his hand clean off. 
In a single, fluid motion, Zane shot into the air, snatched the golden pill from the severed hand, and then dropped, pressing his knee into Norton’s chest and aiming the edge of his blade at his throat.
“My young master won’t—”
Zane didn’t let him finish. 
[SFX: SWIFT BLADE STRIKE]
[SFX: BODY FALLING]
With a swift, clean motion, he ended it.
In another setting, Zane might have heard Norton out. But this mysterious city made him wary - the Illusion Points could vanish at any moment. Better to kill Norton immediately and claim the prize. He still couldn’t understand why the points had scattered when that blue-clad warrior died.
With Norton gone, Zane examined the golden pill. He figured it had to be some kind of powerful, one-time-use treasure. Its energy was terrifying; if a warrior managed to release it in an enemy’s face, the effect would be devastating. But since it had to be crushed to be activated, did that mean it was only good for suicide?
[AMBIENT: HIGH WALL]
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
On the wall above, the watching warriors reacted with a mix of envy and pity. Envy, because Zane had matched Norton head-on and won outright, walking away with a fortune in points. Pity, because they were certain they knew how this ended.
“Norton’s dead. The City Lord’s going to come for him herself now,” one murmured.
“This guy hasn’t even left yet. Better slip away while he still can,” said another.
“Must be from another city. He doesn’t know what she’s capable of.”
They’d seen it before — warriors far stronger than Norton had beaten down the City Lord’s people again and again, only to be hunted down and crushed by her personally in the end. No Soul Sea Realm warrior could outrun her once she came looking. As far as they were concerned, Zane’s fate wasn’t going to be any different.
What none of them realized was that Zane had no plans to run. 
His target had never been the subordinates — it was the City Lord herself. If her people were already sitting on fortunes like this, hers had to be staggering. Killing two of her subordinates in a row all but guaranteed she’d come looking for him eventually. That was exactly what he wanted.
While he was studying it, the pill suddenly grew hot in his palm. 
[SFX: OBJECT THROWN THROUGH AIR]
He could feel a formidable power surging inside and, sensing it was about to explode, he instinctively hurled it as far as he could.
[SFX: SHARP CRACK]
[SFX: BLOOD-RED LIGHTNING ERUPTION]
With a sharp crack, the golden pill shot into the sky. It hovered about a hundred feet up, and then a dense storm of blood-red lightning erupted from it. Zane was stunned. 
If he hadn’t reacted so quickly, he would have been caught right in the middle of that.
A chill ran down his spine. 
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: FALLING SNOW]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON SNOW]
He turned his head and saw a woman dressed in purple walking slowly toward him as snow began to fall in the silent air around her.
