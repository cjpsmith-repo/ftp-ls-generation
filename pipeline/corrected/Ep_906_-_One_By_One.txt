Ep 906 - One By One

Word count: 1043

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The Dragon-Blooded Archer had just killed four Soul Sea masters with a single arrow — and it was just getting started.

They were pinned down, trapped between moving forward and retreating. The emperors’ faces were so grim they might as well have been carved from stone.

“What in the world is that?” Zane asked, his eyes narrowed to slits.

The Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom just shook his head. “I’ve never seen anything like it.”

War King Wind frowned. “That statue is smart,” he growled. “It knows we’re inhabiting these corpses, so it’s aiming for their heads to take us out for good.”

[SFX: ARROW WHOOSH]

He was right. They were all possessing corpses, making them something like zombies, but zombies with souls. A shot to the body would just punch a hole through dead flesh—no real damage, no blood. 

But if one of those arrows pierced the head, the soul itself could shatter. The damn things could punch clean through a shield of Lodestone; even the powerful Dragon-Blooded corpses the four rulers were using couldn’t block their force.

“Your Majesty, what should we do? We have to go back!”

“Your Majesty! You have to get us out of this.”

A few of the Soul Sea Realm warriors were starting to panic. The crown prince and the others, though terrified, kept their mouths shut. Fiona and the younger warriors knew they were powerless here; all they could do was listen to their fathers and hope they had a plan.

But what plan could there be?

War King York, the Harlen Emperor, and Scott all looked deeply frustrated. They had fought so hard to claim these bodies from the thirteenth floor, all for their expedition into the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. To be stopped before they even got inside was infuriating.

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

Just then, War King Wind’s voice cut through the tension. “We charge. It’s our only option. That statue probably can’t move. Everyone, together, behind the giant shields!”

War King Wind didn’t know if he was right—more people might die because of his command—but any decision was better than just standing there getting picked off.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE FORMATION]

[SFX: METAL SHIELDS CLANGING]

Hearing his orders, the Soul Sea Realm masters didn't hesitate. Carrying the shields around them, they began to jog toward the Path of Heroes.

Then one of the Soul Sea masters pointed at the sea.

“Look — it's moving!”

[SFX: SLOW FOOTSTEPS ON WATER]

[MUSIC: DREAD STRINGS]

The Dragon-Blooded Archer had stepped off its base and onto the surface of the golden sea. It moved with an easy, unhurried stride, tracking them.

“It can move,” someone said, voice hollow. “It can move and it can walk on the sea.”

Panic crept back in. The jog faltered. Inside the box, everyone was already shoulder-to-shoulder — now they began pressing toward the center, away from the edges, and the formation grew dangerously crowded.

“Calm down!” War King Wind roared. “Spread out — all of you, spread out now! If you bunch together, one arrow kills six. If you spread out, it kills one!”

The command had some effect. The warriors forced themselves apart, creating what little space the box allowed.

The Archer had already nocked a second arrow.

[SFX: BOWSTRING TENSION]

[SFX: PIERCING STRIKE]

The black shaft left the bow and accelerated in three distinct bursts — each explosion doubling its speed, the final stage reaching something beyond what the eye could properly track. It punched through both sides of the shield box and vanished.

A single Soul Sea master dropped.

“Only one this time,” Scott said quietly.

But the silence that followed was worse than the screaming. There was still most of the Path of Heroes ahead of them. One arrow per volley, one death at a time — the math was brutal.

“We can't keep going like this,” someone said. “And we can't go back.”

“Then what do we do?”

War King Wind's voice cut through the despair. “Someone has to go out there and destroy that statue.”

A Soul Sea master shook his head immediately. “The Path of Heroes — we can't use Vitality out there. We can't fly. Whoever goes out faces the blue arrows with nothing.”

The box went quiet. Then, slowly, every eye in the formation turned to Zane.

He was the only one among them with a physical body. He had used Vitality to fly earlier. Everyone in that box had watched him since the West Dragon River tournament — a young fighter for the Crystal Martial House who had somehow become the only thread any of them had left to hold onto.

War King Wind looked at him directly. “Zane. If you can get us through this — the four kingdoms owe you a debt that can't be repaid. Whatever you need from us, now or later—“

Zane raised a hand. “We're all trying to get to the same place. Saving you is saving myself.” He glanced at the shield wall. “Open it up.”

Two of the masters unfastened their giant shields just enough to create a small opening. Brianna's body was slight enough that a tiny crack was all he needed. 

[SFX: METAL CLICK]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

With a sharp click, the two warriors locked the shields back together. The black light from the path was deadly, but so were the arrows.

The moment Zane was exposed, seven or eight blue arrows screamed toward him, closing the distance in seconds.

“Let’s go!”

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Zane was already moving, his Vitality flaring to life. The speed of a Spirit Supreme Realm warrior was incredible. In the blink of an eye, he shot into the sky, leaving a blur where he’d just been standing. Maybe he didn’t have perfect control of his power, because a single leap launched him nearly five thousand feet into the air.

“Be careful!” Brianna warned from inside his mind. “If an arrow hits you, dealing with that Dragon Arsenic will be a real pain!”

Zane just chuckled and nodded. 

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

His gaze swept across the golden ocean below and locked onto the Dragon-Blooded Archer. As if sensing his stare, the archer raised its head, its scarlet eyes finding his.
