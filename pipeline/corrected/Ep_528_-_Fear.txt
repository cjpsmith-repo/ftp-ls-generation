Ep 528 - Fear

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
Zane was self-aware. He knew his strengths, and he definitely knew his weaknesses.
When it came to runes, no one in the Heartlands could touch him. The divine script, a gift from the upper world, was on a whole other level than the crude markings they used here.
But that was only for runes. In a real fight? He was an ant. He was staring down elders from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. And then there was the leader of Cloud Hall and Maddox himself—both masters of the Life-and-Death Realm. Meanwhile, Zane was stuck in the Soul Realm.
If those two Life-and-Death masters actually threw down, the Spirit-Tribulation elders would get wiped out in the crossfire, just lambs to the slaughter. Tia might be able to help the Cloud Hall leader, but Zane? He wouldn't even make good cannon fodder.
Bottom line: it was pointless for Zane to even think about fighting back.
[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]
[SFX: ENERGY GATHERING]
Energy from Heaven and Earth poured into Maddox's huge, powerful frame, making him look less like a man and more like a mountain. The mountain's eyes glinted, and he threw a punch straight at the sky.
"Firmament Shattering!"
[SFX: ELECTRICAL CRACKLING]
[SFX: AIR TEARING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL CRESCENDO]
From where Zane stood, the attack was pure insanity. The air itself seemed to shred and tear under the force of that one punch.
[SFX: BLAZING ENERGY WHOOSH]
As his fist rocketed toward the barrier, it blazed with a light so blinding it pulsed with raw, destructive power.
"So this is the power that can shatter worlds," Zane thought, his eyes wide as he tracked the brilliant light hurtling toward him.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
The fist light hit the barrier less than a meter from Zane's face.
The pressure was immediate and absolute. For a moment, he felt like he couldn't breathe — like the air itself had been crushed out of existence.
[SFX: DEAFENING BOOM]
It was like a million firecrackers detonating at once. The shockwave tore outward in every direction, shredding the air into countless wind blades that scattered across the sky. Some of them sliced back at Maddox himself, cutting clean through his robes. He didn't even flinch. The blades left no mark on him.
Even the residual force of that single punch would have been enough to kill Zane ten times over.
But it didn't touch him.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]
The Light of Water surged into massive waves at the point of impact, dispersing the force across its entire surface until nothing remained. The waves settled. The barrier returned to its quiet shimmer, unmarked and unbroken.
Tia exhaled. She hadn't just placed a safe bet on Zane—she'd hit the jackpot.
The elders understood: a Sect-protecting array capable of stopping the Heartlands' strongest warrior. Its value defied calculation. Warriors had finite lifespans—a thousand years, two thousand, then they fell. An array like this, properly maintained, could endure forever. Tia had secured the best deal in Cloud Hall's history.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Maddox was stunned. Never had he imagined his full-strength punch couldn't shatter the kid's enchanted barrier.
In all his years fighting across the Heartlands, nothing had shocked him this much. He alone knew the world-breaking power behind that strike.
Yet this thin, bubble-like film had stopped his fist cold. The barrier's true strength lay completely beyond his comprehension.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
What made it worse was the kid's confidence. He stood just feet behind the barrier, face blank, as if to say, You really think you can touch me?
How long since anyone had dared mock him like that? Maddox couldn't remember.
Since he broke through to the Life-and-Death Realm, every Order in the Heartlands scrambled to show him respect. Even the Fifth-Grade Ethereal Spirit Sect wouldn't dare cross him.
For this kid to act so high and mighty was bizarre.
Still, one punch wasn't enough to make him quit. He'd come to wipe out Cloud Hall, and no protective array would stop him.
Behind the barrier, Zane's lips barely moved. "Ready? Let's go."
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
For his second try, Maddox drew the Nameless Saber from the scabbard at his waist.
His son, Eden, had failed to unlock the saber's true potential, a failure punctuated by his defeat at Zane's hands. Maddox had learned a lesson from that. A man has to carve his own path. He has to rely on his own power. Even a saint-level genius like his son had to grow on his own terms. Strength borrowed is no strength at all.
[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL BUILD]
[SFX: METALLIC RESONANCE]
Maddox gripped the Nameless Saber, and the very air around him shifted. His aura, once as immense and immovable as a mountain, now sharpened, honed to a razor's edge. He radiated a light that seemed to fuse him with the blade, man and saber becoming one.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
A ripple of unease went through the Spirit-Tribulation elders as they felt Maddox's aura transform.
[SFX: ENERGY CONDENSING]
[MUSIC: BUILDING DREAD]
Maddox put distance between himself and the barrier, then held the saber horizontally. The earthy yellow energy in his arm turned to silk threads and poured into the blade. A massive shadow began to grow from it — expanding, swelling, until a blade of condensed energy stretched three hundred feet long and four feet wide, hung in the air above Cloud Hall.
Even without activating the blade intent locked inside it, that shadow alone could cleave a small mountain in half. With the intent released, it could flatten one entirely.
"Furious Void Slash!"
[SFX: BLADE TEARING THROUGH AIR]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]
The blade came down in a single horizontal sweep. It looked like it could cut the sky itself in two.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
A long, narrow scar burned itself across the surface of the Light of Water, stretching all the way to the point directly in front of where Zane was standing. The barrier shuddered. Waves dozens of feet high crashed across its surface.
But it held.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
Maddox lowered the saber. For just a moment — barely perceptible — his breathing wasn't quite steady. That strike had cost him, and it hadn't been enough.
His expression darkened.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
He really had no way through. That was the truth, and no matter how much he didn't want to admit it, he couldn't deny it.
Just as he was turning it over in his mind, searching for another approach, something changed.
A heavy, sudden sense of danger dropped into his chest like a stone.
It was rare for anyone in the Heartlands to pose a genuine threat to him, but in that split second, an ominous wave of danger washed over him.
He dodged on pure instinct. Only then did he see it: a strange, colorful mist rolling in to where he'd just been standing. He'd been just fast enough to escape before it could swallow him whole.
[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]
In that same instant, a bolt of lightning as thick as a dragon slammed down, aimed right for him.
[SFX: MASSIVE LIGHTNING STRIKE]
The dragon-sized bolt of lightning seared past him, only grazing his side as it missed its mark.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Even the glancing blow was enough for Maddox to feel the raw power of the attack. A direct hit… he might not have died, but he'd have been crippled. That, combined with the strange mist… a horrifying realization struck him.
Cloud Hall's defensive array could actually kill him.
[MUSIC: DARK CRESCENDO]
For the first time in longer than he could remember, the most powerful man in the Heartlands stood completely still — not out of calm, but out of something he had almost forgotten the feeling of.
Fear.
