Ep 916 - The Secret Of Visualization

Word count: 1143

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

In this world, a warrior could transform their Vitality into something tangible, forging it into reality through sheer meditation. 

If a warrior wanted to manifest a saber, for instance, they’d have to spend countless hours studying the weapon, picturing every curve of the blade, every detail of the hilt. The more intensely they focused, the more solid the resulting Vitality saber would become. 

But this kind of visualization was fueled by Vitality; the more vividly a warrior imagined an object, the more real it became, and without that raw energy, a saber could never take form, no matter how hard they tried.

Brianna had just turned that entire concept on its head. She claimed that if a hundred trillion beings all visualized the same thing at the same time, it could spring into existence from their collective willpower alone—no Vitality required. 

The idea was so immense it left Zane reeling. “You mean we could create a legendary weapon just by thinking about it? Without any Vitality at all?” Zane asked, his voice laced with disbelief.

“It’s not that complicated,” the girl huffed. “The Ancestral Dragon is the core belief of the True Dragons. That shared faith is what brought it into existence, and its presence allows our race to thrive.”

“Okay, I get seeing the Ancestral Dragon as a symbol of your faith,” he said, trying to follow her logic. “But why let an Evil Dragon be born? Are you telling me a bunch of True Dragons actually worship that thing?”

The girl shook her head. “Of course not. Who’d be dumb enough to do that? Every creature in this world has both a light and a dark side. The Evil Dragon is the manifestation of the darkness within our race—a kind of living Hell. It sounds like you have no idea that all of Hell was created from the combined negative thoughts of every creature in the universe.”

Her words struck Zane with the force of a physical blow.

He tried to wrap his mind around it. An entire dimension of suffering, born from nothing more than imagination. It meant that if you pictured something hard enough, it could actually claw its way into existence. Thoughts weren't just thoughts—they were seeds. And suddenly, the universe felt a whole lot more dangerous.

Zane turned back to the crater. The seven Demi-Immortals had the Evil Dragon surrounded, and from where he stood, it didn't look like much of a contest. “It doesn't seem that strong,” he said. “Seven Spirit Supreme Realm warriors against one dragon — it doesn't look like it can hold out.”

Brianna's reply was measured. “It needs to undergo seven metamorphoses before it reaches its true form. This one has only completed three. But if it reaches its fourth, none of these seven will be able to stop it.”

“And if they don't stop it?”

“Then it eats.” Her voice was flat. “After its fourth metamorphosis, it will consume this entire world as fuel for its fifth. Every living thing in it.”

Zane went still. He had merged with the world's will — this plane ran through him now, and he through it. Whatever that meant for his future, it meant this: he wasn't going to stand by and watch the world get eaten.

“Then the Demi-Immortals are actually doing us a favor,” he said.

“Don't thank them yet,” Brianna said. “They have no interest in killing you or the others — you're too insignificant to bother with. You were only useful as bait. But once the dragon is dealt with, they'll clean up what's left. Resetters don't leave loose ends.”

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

[SFX: FLAMES ERUPTING]

A furious roar ripped through the air as flames erupted from the Evil Dragon's massive head. The man's attack had been little more than a scratch, but it had been enough to enrage the beast.

“It's about to breathe fire! Wade, bind it!” the man in the silver mask ordered.

[SFX: MAGICAL HISSING]

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY]

With a series of hisses, countless green threads began to spiral out from the ground, weaving a magical net.

“Can I actually escape controlling your body?” Zane asked. “All seven of them are Spirit Supreme Realm.”

“Apart from Vorn, the others are manageable,” Brianna said. “You can only draw on thirty percent of my strength in this body — but I'm going to teach you a movement technique now. Use my Vitality with it, and you'll have a chance.”

She transmitted it directly into his mind — the Cloud-Chaser Skill, comprehended from the Cloud Chasing Lion, one of the fastest divine beasts in existence, second only to the Heavenly Rat. A thousand miles in a single day. Above the nine heavens and below the nine underworlds.

The girl knew her physical body was compatible with the skill she was about to share. She just hoped it would be enough to give him a chance to escape.

[SFX: GROUND SHAKING]

[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]

Loud crashes echoed as the enraged Evil Dragon thrashed wildly, its massive body shaking the very ground. Its battle with the seven Demi-Immortals was now in full swing.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Five minutes later, Zane’s eyes snapped open.

“How much of the skill did you get?” the girl asked. She didn’t think he could have absorbed much in just five minutes. If he could just tap into the Wind Law from her Vitality, that would be enough.

“I think I’ve got it,” Zane said, flashing her a bright, confident smile. In a way, the skill felt like it was made for him. He was already sensitive to the Cloud-Chaser Lion’s unique talents, and this skill was based on that very same Legendary Beast. Having the girl share it directly gave him a whole new level of understanding.

[SFX: BEAST HOWL]

[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]

The Evil Dragon let out a deafening howl as it shattered Wade's restraints, its colossal body soaring out of the crater and into the sky.

“Vincent! Grab it and drag it back down!”

“Don’t let it get away! Dorothea, bind that thing!”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The girl’s eyes flashed. “Now! This is our chance. Are you ready?”

“Yes!” Zane nodded.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY BURST]

Just then, her inner world began to tremble. An invisible power washed over her physical body, instantly shattering Dorothea’s binding field.

[SFX: SOUL CONNECTION WHOOSH]

The moment the restraint was gone, Zane felt his soul connect to the girl’s body. He didn’t waste a second, immediately drawing on her vast well of Vitality.

Dorothea gasped, the abrupt loss of connection to her power sending a jolt through her. 

[SFX: ETHEREAL DISAPPEARANCE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Tearing her gaze from the rampaging Evil Dragon, she looked down just in time to see Zane’s figure drift away like smoke on the wind, disappearing in a flash.
