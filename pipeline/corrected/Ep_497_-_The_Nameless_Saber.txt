Ep 497 - The Nameless Saber

Word count: 1157

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC DISCOVERY]

Like everyone else in the arena, Eden couldn't believe his eyes.

Even as a Divine-level talent—someone who stood at the pinnacle of his generation—Eden had only managed to grasp two types of Law Power. And even then, he'd only mastered about eighty percent of each, a feat that had taken him years of grueling cultivation and meditation.

But Zane… Zane had mastered three. And what was truly shocking? He'd comprehended them fully.

Eden's hands trembled slightly as the realization settled over him like ice water. This wasn't just talent. This was something else entirely.

It was a given that every cultivator had their strengths and weaknesses. Someone gifted with Wind Law might be terrible with Fire Law, which was why most martial artists only ever comprehended a single type of Law Power. The human soul could only stretch so far, only contain so much understanding of the fundamental forces that governed reality.

Across all of The Heartlands, fewer than twenty geniuses had ever managed to learn two.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

That was why the crowd had been so stunned when Eden revealed his command of both the Wind and Metal Laws. They were in awe of his talent. Whispers had spread through the stands like wildfire—here was a prodigy who might one day rival the legendary masters of old.

But now, to their utter shock, Zane revealed he'd mastered those same two Laws on top of the Fire Law he'd shown at the start of the fight.

The crowd could barely process it. Silence descended over the arena like a suffocating blanket, broken only by scattered gasps and the sound of someone's weapon clattering to the ground in disbelief. Even the most experienced cultivators sat frozen, their minds struggling to reconcile what their eyes had witnessed.

Eden felt something unfamiliar stirring in his chest—not quite fear, but close. For the first time in years, he questioned whether victory was truly within his grasp.

[AMBIENT: OBSERVATION PLATFORM]

"He must've experienced a major breakthrough in the Dragon Soar Arena," Kieran said, his eyes narrowing as he studied the raw Law Power radiating from Zane's form. The intensity of it made the air shimmer.

Burns, who had remained silent throughout the entire match, finally spoke. "I agree. The boy stumbled upon a complete Law Power in there. After all, you won't find treasures like that anywhere in The Heartlands. Still, traveling to the upper world and fully comprehending three different types in such a short time..." His voice trailed off with grudging respect. "That's no easy feat. This kid possesses serious talent—the kind we haven't seen in decades."

[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]

Among the three leaders, each brought something different to their alliance. Kieran was shrewd and calculating, always three moves ahead in any political game. Hobbes burned hot with passion and rage, quick to action and quicker to anger. Burns, however, was a man of few words—so few that disciples called him The Silent Man. Rumor held he'd once gone ten months without uttering a single syllable. His silence wasn't weakness but strength, each word weighted with meaning.

For him to speak now, and at such length, meant something significant had shifted.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

When Burns finished, all three leaders turned their gazes toward the Dragon Soar Arena, which loomed in the distance like a sleeping giant. The ancient structure held secrets none of them fully understood, mysteries that had persisted for generations. They were dying to know what Zane had experienced within those walls—what transformations had occurred to produce such impossible results.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]

The truth was, the Cobalt Dragon had orchestrated Zane's journey to the upper world. There, he had undergone the Baptism by Laws—a transformative process that rewrote the very fabric of his cultivation potential, rendering him compatible with every form of law refinement.

Without such a baptism, even cultivators blessed with Divine-level talents in the Heartlands could only aspire to master two or three Laws throughout their entire lives. Most would consider themselves fortunate to grasp even one. This fundamental limitation separated the merely exceptional from the truly transcendent. That was precisely why the Dragonfall Palace valued Zane so highly—his potential defied the natural order itself.

The old man continued to stare at Zane, his features betraying a mixture of disbelief and wonder. After decades of witnessing prodigies rise and fall, he'd thought himself immune to surprise. Yet here stood this young man, shattering every assumption he'd held about the limits of human potential.

"This kid's mastery…" The elder's voice emerged barely above a whisper, tinged with an emotion he couldn't quite name—perhaps awe, perhaps envy, perhaps something else entirely. "It's just incredible. I've lived three hundred years, and I've never seen anything like it."

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Eden's hand moved to the weapon at his side. The Nameless Saber—whispered about throughout the Heartlands—rested in its scabbard, awaiting its master's blood.

"Talent means nothing if you don't live long enough to use it," Eden said coldly.

[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]

He drew the blade in one fluid motion. Metal so dark it devoured light emerged with a sound like tearing silk. Ancient runes lay dormant along its length.

Eden dragged the edge across his palm. Crimson drops fell upon the surface, and the weapon transformed. Dark tendrils coiled around the metal. The runes blazed to life, flickering with malevolent hunger.

Zane's eyes narrowed as he studied the awakening blade. Even from across the chamber, he could feel the oppressive weight of its aura—a suffocating presence that made the air itself feel heavy. This wasn't merely a weapon; it was something far more dangerous, something that had claimed countless lives before this moment.

Maddox’s brow furrowed, but he slowly sat back down. He didn't think his son needed to go this far—to activate the power of the Nameless Saber. Zane shouldn't have been a match for Eden.

But Eden saw it differently. He was going to use everything in his arsenal to end Zane's life, right here and now.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS HUM]

A low hum filled the air. As Eden’s blood seeped into the etched patterns of the blade, the saber began to radiate a palpable, suffocating Malice.

The blood pulsed through the steel of the Nameless Saber as if it were a living vein.

After coursing through the blade, the blood dripped onto the stone floor. But strangely, it didn't soak into the cracks of the arena.

The crimson drops skittered across the ground as if they were alive, swarming together to form a single, glowing rune.

[SFX: SUPERNATURAL EMERGENCE]

[MUSIC: HORROR CRESCENDO]

A moment later, a hand pushed its way out of the rune. It was followed by shoulders, a head, a torso, and then legs.

A monster made of blood had materialized before their very eyes. It was hunched over, with two jagged horns on its head and a tail as sharp as a saber. It stood perfectly still, radiating an endless, malevolent aura.

[SFX: CROWD PANIC]

[AMBIENT: SCREAMING CROWD]

"It's a Netherworld Fiend!" someone screamed from the stands. "Straight from the rivers of Hell!"
