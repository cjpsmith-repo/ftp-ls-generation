Ep 798 - Training

Word count: 1234

[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUND]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

 "Don't worry," Camilla said with a smile. "I won't hurt him."

[SFX: RAPID WHOOSHING STRIKES]

 The words were barely out of her mouth before the blur of her palms around Zane intensified, each strike looking deadlier than the last. If Zane couldn't find a way to counter her, he was going to get seriously hurt.

A spark ignited in Zane’s eyes. He stood his ground, focusing, trying to understand the Time Law she was weaving into her attack.

"It won't help you to just stand there," Camilla said, her voice laced with contempt as she watched him stare blankly. 

She put her full force behind a single, decisive strike aimed right for him.

[SFX: SUDDEN GRIP]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PAUSE]

 Zane had been studying her movements, but in the end, he just shook his head. He reached out and effortlessly closed his hand around her slender wrists. "Your use of the Time Law… it's too faint," he said softly. "I can't even get a read on it."

For a moment, Camilla just froze, stunned. She tried to wrench her hands back, but it was like they were locked in a steel vise.

Renzo and Barry had been settling in for a good show, eager to see what method Zane would use to defend himself. Camilla’s Thousand-Handed Maiden technique was a dazzling sight, like countless flower petals falling at once, but it was infused with a trace of the Time Law that could alter the flow of time in a small area. It was nearly impossible to break; you needed a more powerful technique to even have a chance.

[MUSIC: SHOCKED REVELATION]

 They never expected Zane to shatter it with such a simple, dismissive move. They exchanged a bewildered look, their shock growing with every passing second.

Camilla finally yanked her wrists free. "What do you mean the Time Law is too faint?" she snapped.

"I was trying to comprehend it mid-fight," Zane said. "There wasn't enough to work with."

The room went quiet.

"Even I have only mastered ten percent of the first level," Camilla said, her voice sharp. "You think that's something you can just pick up in a sparring match?"

Zane tilted his head. "Ten percent of the first level?"

Camilla's expression turned murderous.

What she'd achieved was no small thing. The Time Law was harder to cultivate than even Space Law, and rarer still to find a tablet for. Ten percent of the first level was something to be proud of. The look on Zane's face suggesting it wasn't quite enough was not something she was going to take quietly.

A faint, almost pitying smile touched Zane’s lips, and the sight of it sent a fresh wave of fury through Camilla.

[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]

 Randy walked over, grinning. "Alright, that's enough. His strength is at least on par with mine. He'll be a huge help to our team."

"Hmph!" Camilla scoffed, pulling her hands free as Zane released her, though she was far from convinced.

But she couldn't deny what had happened. He had casually broken her signature attack just by reaching out and grabbing her. And with Randy so obviously backing him, she had to face a startling possibility: The kid's strength was on the same level as Gregory’s. He was an extraordinary talent who should have been ranked in the First Tier. There were countless martial artists in the Harlen Divine Kingdom, but only four people had ever made it that far. Zane’s power was beyond anything she could have imagined.

"I can tell you've always been a loner," Randy said to Zane, his tone shifting. "You don't seem to understand what it means to be on a team."

The words reminded Zane of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom that Maddox had established. They had a special method for combining the Vitality of tens of thousands of Soul Realm warriors, allowing a low-level army to unleash terrifying power. He nodded, taking Randy’s words to heart.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

 Randy waved to the others, then walked to the center of the hall and extended a hand toward Zane. "Try to attack me."

Renzo and Camilla flanked him, while Barry stood just behind.

Is he going to fight me with the others? Zane wondered, studying the formation curiously. Since he’d already agreed to join, he might as well learn how they fought.

[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]

 With that thought, he blurred into motion, charging straight for Randy.

[SFX: ENERGY SHIELD ACTIVATION]

 Before he could even get close, Renzo thrust out a hand. Faintly purple waves of Vitality surged from his body, forming a swift, rotating shield around Randy. At the same time, Camilla smiled and waved a slender hand, creating a thin curtain of light. As Zane rushed into its field, he felt everything—including his own speed—grind to a crawl.

The Time Law, Zane realized. And all three of them are supporting Randy.

[SFX: BLADE SWOOSH]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

 In that slowed moment, Randy’s figure flashed and reappeared directly behind Zane. With a flip of his wrist, he placed the cold, sharp edge of a dagger against Zane’s neck. It was a perfectly executed team maneuver.

But Zane was quick. Camilla’s understanding of the Time Law wasn’t deep enough to truly bind him. With a slight twist, he slipped out of Randy's hold like an eel. A confident smirk spread across his face as he reached back, ready to snatch the dagger from Randy’s hand.

[SFX: PSYCHIC BLAST]

 Just as he moved, a powerful tremor shot through his mind, feeling like a mountain-splitting axe had just cleaved through his skull. Barry had launched a soul attack.

Still, he didn't counter. He let out a faint groan, staggered slightly, and let Randy place the dagger back against his neck. Randy smiled, satisfied that the demonstration had worked.

"Kill him!" Camilla called out, still clearly holding a grudge.

Randy just laughed and lowered the dagger.

"There's not much time," Randy said. "After we enter the Sacred Feather Sea, we'll face serious competition. Practice with the others when you can. The five of us need to get the best possible result from this expedition." His eyes held a quiet intensity. He'd been under his brother's thumb for too long.

Zane nodded. Randy's goal didn't conflict with his own. If he could get what he needed and help Randy at the same time, he wasn't going to hold back.

He learned their personalities as much as their techniques. Renzo was easygoing and steady, never raising his voice. Barry was rigid and quiet, efficient and sparing with words. Camilla was exactly as she'd seemed from the first moment: lively, restless, incapable of sitting still.

As time went on, Randy's royal duties called him away more and more, and he rarely showed up at the martial house.

[AMBIENT: LARGE GATHERING HALL]

[MUSIC: ANTICIPATORY DRUMS]

Three months later, the Crystal Martial House gathered all the martial artists who had passed the test, Zane included. He guessed it was because the Sacred Feather Sea was about to open — a treasury for the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms, much like the Dark Nether Cave on the main continent. He just wasn't sure how they planned to send everyone in.

Camilla, Barry, and Renzo weren't there. As part of Randy's personal force, they hadn't needed to take the test. 

They would likely be leaving with Randy himself.
