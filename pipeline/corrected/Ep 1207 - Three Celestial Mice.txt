Ep 1207 - Three Celestial Mice

Word count: 1115

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD WITH DISTANT EXPLOSIONS]
[SFX: WHOOSHING LIGHT STREAKS]
Red dots of light streaked back toward the Ogre city, one after another.
Warriors fell repeatedly, losing half their Illusion Points with each death, but the city resurrected them moments later. The system created the ultimate training ground - far superior to the Illusionary Arenas that only the most powerful sacred orders could build for their elite disciples.
This Illusion Battlefield differed fundamentally. Open to one hundred thousand supreme worlds, any Soul Sea Realm warrior could participate regardless of origin.
Most recognized their slim chances of climbing the ranks. Competition was brutal - heirs from minor sacred orders, city-backed prodigies, top prospects from major powers, Saint Lords’ children, descendants of World Lords and Supreme Lords, and the era’s true stars destined to lead the world of chaos.
How could an ordinary warrior ever hope to stand out against that?
So, once they gave up on a high rank, a lot of them stopped worrying about losing points. They just threw themselves into battle, fighting, dying, and reviving in a constant cycle. They used the life-and-death stakes to hone their skills and forge their warrior’s will.
[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION SHIMMER]
High above the Mist Palace, a massive, shimmering pyramid of names materialized in the sky. It was the leaderboard, and warriors all over the battlefield kept glancing up to check the rankings.
“I still don’t see Zane’s name,” the old man noted, standing respectfully beside Yolande. “There are so many people at the bottom of the pyramid, it’s impossible to find him. But Layla Rhodes is already on there. She’s ranked one hundred thirty thousandth in her supreme world, and four million, six hundred thousandth overall.”
Even with millions of names, their sharp senses could easily pick out a specific target. The problem was, Zane had barely earned any points since leaving the city.
“That’s strange,” Yolande said, frowning. “What is that boy doing out there?” With Zane’s strength, cracking the top five million inside a single city should have been simple, even if dominating the whole Illusion Battlefield was out of reach. Something wasn’t adding up.
“Keep watching him,” she said. “And report to me the moment you spot any Mist Palace cultivators.”
The Mist Palace was one of the core factions of the Human Alliance, and its cultivators were elites in their own right. But the universe was vast, and brilliant talents were hiding in every corner of it. Yolande wasn’t foolish enough to claim her people would take first place outright.
As for Layla, she’d already cracked the top one hundred thirty thousand in her own supreme world. With her talent, climbing into the top five thousand shouldn’t be out of reach—how much further she went from there would come down to performance.
[MUSIC: TIME-PASSAGE TRANSITION]
Twelve hours came and went. There was no true day or night inside the Illusion Battlefield, only the unbroken rhythm of battle.
[SFX: PUNCH IMPACT]
[SFX: SHELL CRACKING]
A vicious tortoise’s shell shattered under one of Zane’s punches. A faint light flashed, and the creature hiding beneath the shell died before it even understood what had hit it.
[SFX: POINTS MATERIALIZING CHIME]
As the tortoise’s corpse dissolved, ten Illusion Points shot free and streaked straight toward Zane.
Hunting alone in the wilderness yielded points slowly, but Zane didn’t mind. Many experts employed the same strategy - leaving the main cities to let the competition burn out before returning. Some even dug holes and slept through the chaos. Zane chose to explore instead.
With the tortoise dead, he surveyed the endless hills stretching before him. The Illusion Battlefield was enormous - easily fifty supreme worlds combined. Even with countless main cities scattered across it, the distances between them were vast.
“Guess it’s about time to head back,” he murmured, smiling faintly as he turned to make his way to the nearest city. It was almost time to cash in.
But just as he turned, a faint energy fluctuation brushed past him from the front left.
His eyes flickered. A heartbeat later, he was gone—and reappeared on the ridge above, the basin spread out below him.
Down in the basin between two peaks, a ring of light spun steadily in front of a cave mouth.
“What is this?” Zane felt a flicker of surprise. Maybe the Illusion Battlefield wasn’t as straightforward as he’d assumed—maybe points didn’t only come from killing other cultivators.
Circling that rotating light were three mice, their fur a deep gold, each one the size of a small pig. Even lying still, they radiated a heavy, commanding aura—the kind that came from bloodline alone.
Celestial Beasts. Celestial Mice.
Zane’s brow furrowed slightly. A fully grown Celestial Beast was nothing to take lightly under any circumstances, and these three were Level Eleven—power on par with the Soul Sea Realm itself. And whatever was inside that cave, it was important enough that three of them were standing guard over it.
That alone was enough to pull Zane in.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WILDERNESS]
He was making his way slowly down the side of the mountain when the three mice lifted their heads, alert to him now. 
[SFX: MICE CHITTERING]
Their beady black eyes locked onto him as they chittered to each other.
Zane kept his cool, but a new layer of caution settled over him. You didn’t mess with Celestial Mice.
He continued his slow approach.
Two hundred feet. One hundred and fifty.
One hundred.
[SFX: RAPID WHOOSHING ATTACKS]
The instant he crossed that invisible line, the three mice vanished. 
[SFX: SHARP STABBING IMPACT]
Blurry shadows flashed in front of him, and a sharp, stabbing pain lanced through his body.
A moment later, the mice reappeared a short distance away, forming a triangle around him. They bared their tiny front teeth, which seemed to glow with a faint, rainbow light. That was the source of their power. He knew their teeth were infused with the Law of Causality, turning them into weapons that could kill their prey in an instant.
[SFX: FLESH TEARING]
[SFX: BLOOD SPLATTER]
Three deep cuts suddenly bloomed on his left shoulder, chest, and leg, blood welling up immediately. The attack had been so fast, it was over before he even registered it.
Zane ignored the pain. The speed of those little things was beyond anything he could have imagined; he wasn’t stupid enough to give chase. He fixed his eyes on the shimmering entrance to the evil wind cave—his objective—and started moving toward it.
[SFX: RAPID DISPLACEMENT WHOOSH]
The second he moved, the three mice vanished again.
[SFX: SWORD DRAWN WITH METALLIC RING]
[SFX: ETHEREAL SPIRITS MATERIALIZING]
And the second they did, Zane drew his sword, Thundergust. 
The blade cast a defensive arc of shadow around him as dozens of phantom Nether Gods erupted from its tip, fanning out to form a protective screen.
The three mice went still, eyes locked on the spirit screen, and the standoff held—neither side willing to make the first move toward the cave.
