Ep 866 - The Serene Void

Word count: 1052

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

As the crowd of nervous, saint-level geniuses watched from below, Zane scaled the pyramid, finally stopping on the thirty-fifth floor.

“The thirty-fifth?” someone muttered. “What’s he doing up there?”

“I don’t know, but he’s heading for that narrow doorway.”

“Wait, the one that leads to the golden leaf? Is he going after the Black Star pickax?” A scoff cut through the murmurs. “What a waste of time. Does he really think he can just pull it out?”

Everyone knew the legend. Anyone who passed through that particular door on the thirty-fifth floor found themselves in the heart of a giant tree, right before a single, shimmering golden leaf. And sealed within that leaf was a divine-class weapon. This was common knowledge, passed down from the Sacred Feather Sea ages ago, a story told across the seven divine kingdoms.

They all knew about the pickax. They also knew that no one had ever managed to claim it.

For years, countless warriors from the Soul Sea Realm—beings of immense power—had thrown everything they had at the golden leaf. Not a single one had ever managed to break it.

And now Zane was just strolling onto the thirty-fifth floor like he owned the place. He had a way of making the impossible seem plausible. 

For a split second, a wave of anticipation swept through the crowd. Maybe he could do it.

But the thought was quickly crushed by reality, and they all started shaking their heads.

“No way,” one warrior insisted. “If the Black Star pickax was that easy to get, someone from the Soul Sea Realm would’ve snatched it up long ago.”

“You’re right,” another agreed. “He’s probably just trying his luck. But a divine-class weapon isn’t some toy you can just walk away with.”

“The guy’s got some serious muscle, I’ll give him that. But all he’s done is rough up War King York. He’d need to be ten times stronger than he is now to even have a prayer of getting that pickax.”

While the debate raged, curiosity got the better of some of the bolder warriors. The thirty-fifth floor carried some risk, but as long as they avoided the purple Stone-Eater Bugs — which rarely came below the fortieth floor — they figured they could get out in time if things went wrong. One by one, they began to climb.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

They didn’t just stop at the thirty-fifth floor. Warriors poured through narrow doors above Zane’s leaf and below it, crowding onto adjacent leaves on every side — above his head, beneath his feet, to his left and right. They draped themselves over the edges of their leaves and stared down at him.

Zane looked around at the faces peering at him from every direction and felt a flash of irritation. It was just smashing a leaf. What was there to see? He briefly considered luring a purple Stone-Eater Bug down from the upper floors to clear them out. Then he let it go. If they wanted to watch, let them watch.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

He walked to the center of the golden leaf and stood before the Black Star Pickax. He took a deep breath and closed his eyes.

Since refining his body to its current state, he hadn’t had a chance to test his true limits — Fiona had interrupted him before he could finish. Now he would find out.

He began drawing on the Dragon Scales.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

One thousand. The power moved through him like condensed liquid, slow and heavy. His body held.

Two thousand. Every cell expanded under the pressure. Still holding.

Three thousand. The expansion intensified, pushing outward against his skin. He could feel it in his bones. Still holding.

[SFX: CRACKING BONES]

Four thousand. His bones began to crack — not breaking, but straining at the joints, forced open by the sheer volume of power flooding through him. His body visibly swelled.

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD GASPS]

“Look — he’s getting bigger,” someone whispered from a leaf above.

“His whole body just expanded. How is that even possible?”

“He’s packed with power. I can feel it from here.”

Zane frowned. Four thousand Dragon Scales and he was already at his limit. He couldn’t channel more without risking his body tearing itself apart. 

The problem was, four thousand wouldn’t be enough. Soul Sea masters had tried everything — multiple warriors attacking the same leaf simultaneously, techniques, brute force — and none of it had worked. Four thousand Dragon Scales from a single warrior wasn’t going to be what finally broke it.

He needed more. But his body wasn’t ready. Since advancing to his current physical state, he hadn’t been able to refine it further — the Red Dragon was still asleep, and without that fire source, the next stage of body refinement wasn’t possible.

Five thousand.

The decision was instant.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC BUILD]

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

Without hesitating, he began summoning power from even more scales.

But as the energy of five thousand Dragon Scales erupted inside him, his body was wracked with a pain so profound it felt like every cell was screaming. It was an outrageous, all-consuming agony, far too much to handle.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHIMES]

An idea surfaced through the haze of pain. The Serene Void. 

Zane fell back on a familiar technique, a mental state where he could temporarily sever the connection to his own physical suffering. In the Void, it felt like the agony belonged to someone else, a distant echo he could observe but not feel.

Of course, The Serene Void had its price. Pain was a signal, a warning that the body was overloaded or tearing itself apart. Ignoring it was like ignoring a fire alarm while the house burned down around you.

But right now, he had no other choice. He had made his decision, and he would see it through.

The power of five thousand Dragon Scales surged into his body, swelling every muscle and bone to its absolute limit.

[SFX: FLESH TEARING]

[MUSIC: HORROR STRINGS]

The creaking sounds from his frame stopped, replaced by a sudden, sickening tear as a wound split open across his chest.

[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]

[SFX: FABRIC RIPPING]

A line of blood shot out with the force of an arrow, slicing clean through his clothes. The immense pressure had ripped him open, and blood began to pour out, shredding his robes. A moment later, more jets of blood burst from his back, his legs, and his shoulders.

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD GASPS]

Down below, the crowd could only stare, their faces a mask of utter shock and astonishment.
