Ep 112 - Demon General
Word Count: 1243
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]
[AMBIENT: DARK ENERGY]
 At that moment, a wave of shock rippled through the apprentices, their faces frozen in disbelief. As a foul, evil presence rolled off him, they all scrambled backward, creating a wide circle around the Core Apprentice who had just spoken.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
 With a sharp rasp of steel, Cliff drew his sword, its tip leveled at the apprentice. "I know you're not Merrick," he yelled. "Who the hell are you?"
[SFX: EERIE CACKLE]
[SFX: FLESH TEARING]
 An eerie cackle clawed its way out of "Merrick's" throat. Just as the sound died, sharp thorns burst from his body. To everyone’s horror, not a single drop of blood followed, even as the thorns tore through his skin.
"I've been hiding in this sky forest for a long, long time," the thing rasped. "Haven't seen a human in ages. And now, I’ve finally found some. I must say, your flesh smells just as delicious as I imagined!"
[SFX: FLESH MELTING]
 After that horrifying declaration, its grotesque, long tongue snaked out to lick its lips. Then, its head began to collapse inward, the flesh and bone melting away to reveal the monstrous creature underneath. The apprentices realized with a jolt of terror that this wasn't Merrick at all, but a demon wearing his skin like a suit.
"When did you devour him and take his body?" Cliff demanded, inching his gleaming sword closer to the demon.
The demon flashed a grin full of needle-sharp teeth. "You’re just a pathetic little human," it sneered. "Why would I waste my breath on you?"
[SFX: EAR-SPLITTING SHRIEK]
[AMBIENT: DEMON SWARM]
[MUSIC: IMMINENT DANGER]
 The words had barely left its mouth when it unleashed an ear-splitting shriek. Suddenly, dozens of demons swarmed from the side of the valley. They were not only hideous to look at, but their evil aura was so potent that it was like a physical force, making the apprentices tremble uncontrollably.
[SFX: SCREAM — CUT SHORT]
One of the pledges never had a chance. A Demonic Grunt was on him before he could move, and in an instant he was gone. The demon raised what remained above its head — a deliberate display, aimed squarely at the survivors.
The effect was immediate. 
"Kill them!" Cliff’s voice cut through the rising panic, firm and clear. He knew they couldn't show a shred of fear. As a Core Apprentice, his composure was a beacon, steadying the others in the face of the ambush. The apprentices from Misty Peak were in for the fight of their lives. Demons scouting the sky forest was one thing, but running into this many at once was a death sentence.
Henry and Casey were scared stiff, frozen solid by a fear so pure it felt like ice in their veins.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
 Zane shook his head in disgust. He was too far away to do much for the main group, but not for everyone. With a frustrated grunt, he broke into a sprint. When he was about thirty feet away, he threw the Shattered Dagger.
[SFX: DAGGER CUTTING THROUGH AIR]
[SFX: DEMON HOWLS]
 In a silent, deadly arc, the dagger sliced cleanly through the necks of two Demonic Grunts.
Zane walked over to Henry and Casey. "You two are pathetic," he said, his voice laced with contempt. "Are you sure you were ready to come to the sky forest and fight demons?"
Relief washed over Henry, quickly followed by a hot flush of shame at Zane’s condescending tone. It wasn’t that they didn’t want to fight; they were simply stunned, their bodies refusing to obey. It was as if their feet were fused to the forest floor, trapped by a kind of terror they'd never known.
"I never thought the top Outer Disciple of Misty Peak could be so useless," Zane sneered.
A dark blush crept up Henry’s neck, burning his cheeks. His life had been a string of praise and easy victories. He was the genius, the prodigy. No one had ever dared to call him out like this.
"Don't believe me?" Zane pressed, his voice dripping with scorn. "You’re frozen stiff in front of a couple of Demonic Grunts. If that’s not useless, what is?"
Henry couldn't take it anymore. "I'm not useless!" he screamed.
"Fine," Zane nodded. "Then prove it. Those grunts are about as strong as someone at the Nature Level—weaker than you, actually. So fight them. Kill them. Better to die on your feet than just let them take you."
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
 Goaded by Zane, something inside Henry snapped. With a primal roar, he drew his Azure Blade, his eyes bloodshot as he charged headlong at the Demonic Grunts. As an apprentice on the cusp of Core status, Henry’s raw talent was undeniable. But now, fueled by Zane's taunt, he unleashed a ferocity he never knew he possessed.
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
 Zane watched, a flicker of surprise on his face, as Henry moved like a thunderstorm. His Azure Blade became a blur of motion, a whirlwind of steel. He swung with the fury of a cornered animal, and with a final, desperate cry, he cleaved a demon clean in two.
[SFX: DEMON DEATH CRY]
 Afterward, Henry stared at the bisected demon, his chest heaving and his eyes wide with disbelief. *I did it. I actually killed one.* In that instant, the icy fear that had paralyzed him shattered.
He stood there, dazed, then grunted. "I get it," he said, his eyes finding Zane’s. "Their energy… it doesn’t have a hold on me anymore. I know why."
By then, Zane was already moving on to the next Demonic Grunt. He gave Henry a single, indifferent glance before his Shattered Dagger flashed out again. He’d already killed four of them.
A sigh of awe escaped Henry. The casual power, the sheer speed—his earlier jealousy was melting into pure admiration. He had to admit it: he was nowhere near Zane's level. Zane, not him, was the true top Outer Disciple of Misty Peak.
But the battle was too intense for introspection.
[SFX: SWORD CLATTERING]
 Cliff cried out as his sword was ripped from his grasp, arcing through the air to land far out of reach. Before he could even react, the demon general swiped at his chest, its claws poised to tear him open. A sickening grin twisted the demon general’s face as it closed in for the kill.
[SFX: DEMON SHRIEK]
[SFX: WHISTLING DAGGER]
 But just as its claws were about to make contact, the demon general shrieked and leaped back, yanking its hand away as if it had been struck by lightning. An instant later, the Shattered Dagger whistled through the exact spot where the demon’s hand had been.
If the demon general hadn't pulled back, the dagger would have sliced its claw right off.
Cliff, heart pounding from his near-death experience, stared in disbelief. His own enchanted sword hadn’t fazed the demon general in the slightest, yet it had recoiled in terror from that simple dagger. *What is that thing?* he wondered.
His eyes followed the dagger’s trajectory back to Zane, who stood nearby, his arm still extended from the throw.
A flicker of regret crossed Zane’s face.
"Damn thing’s fast," Zane sighed, lowering his arm. "Too cautious. My dagger didn’t even connect."
The demon general fixed its monstrous, hate-filled eyes on Zane. A flicker of genuine fear entered its gaze as it shrieked, its voice high and unnerving. "You… you’re different."
