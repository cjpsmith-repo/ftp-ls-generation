Ep 297 - Consummation
Word Count: 1174
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
While Tracy was powerful, her strength came from raw talent and her heritage, not from hard work. When it came to knowledge and experience, she was leagues behind the Guardians and elders of the Skyreach Sect, or even Finn and Patrick.
Of course, they were all surprised, but for very different reasons.
Patrick's shock came from understanding exactly what had just happened in the combat ring.
[SFX: MAGICAL TINGLING]
Tracy, on the other hand, was shocked not because she understood what happened, but because the Blue Phoenix's Blood was tingling in her veins. Even though she didn’t get what had technically occurred, the sensation sent a primal wave of fear through her, a natural defense mechanism kicking in. It was enough to tell her that something extraordinary had just taken place.
The Blue Phoenix was a legendary creature with senses so sharp they bordered on precognition. It could sense things others couldn’t. If danger was near, its blood would tingle, warning its host. That's why Tracy was so shaken she nearly screamed.
Patrick didn’t have the blood of a Blue Phoenix, so he couldn’t feel anything like that. But he'd already noticed that the skills Zane and Finn were using were far beyond their current cultivation levels. Whatever was happening here, it was anything but common.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
"If my eyes aren’t playing tricks on me," Patrick muttered, "their swordsmanship just hit the highest possible level. Both Zane and Finn... they just used this fight to reach the consummation of sword intent." A look of anticipation, almost ecstasy, flashed across his face. He thought he might have finally found a worthy match.
Up until now, there was only one disciple in the entire Skyreach Sect who was stronger.
It’ll still be a struggle for him to defeat me, though, Patrick thought. He’ll most likely be held back by his low cultivation level. What really gets me is how someone with his current strength is only at the Second Level of the Adept Realm. It’s bizarre.
[SFX: SWORD CLASH RESONANCE]
What was truly unbelievable, though, was that Finn and Zane had both reached the consummation of sword intent at the exact same moment, right in the middle of a battle.
Sword intent was an abstract concept, incredibly difficult to grasp and even harder to master.
[SFX: SILENT SWORD CLASH]
Why was there no sound when their swords clashed? The audience was stunned. Zane’s Streamer and Finn’s Blood-Corroding Sword, two first-class weapons, had collided with full force, yet they hadn’t made a sound. It was as if they were made of paper.
The reason for the silent impact was that the moment they achieved the consummation of their sword intent, they entered a state of pure epiphany that instantly suppressed all sound.
Sir Kenneth understood it differently. The sound hadn't been suppressed — it had been swallowed. Two fighters reaching the consummation of sword intent simultaneously had slightly dislocated the space around them. That spatial gap absorbed the sound and the aura both. It was the space itself that went silent, not the strike — though to anyone without Kenneth's knowledge, the effect looked the same: all Vitality and aura simply gone. 
[SFX: SPATIAL DISTORTION HUM]
It wasn't a lack of power. On the contrary, the moment they reached that peak, all their Vitality and physical strength vanished. In that instant, all that remained was the sword intent itself. Everything else faded away to make room for it, and that's why the clash was silent.
The moment their swords parted, both Zane and Finn dropped to the ground and sat. No signal, no discussion — just two fighters hitting the same threshold at the same instant and doing the only thing that made sense: staying there.
The crowd didn't know what to make of it. 
[AMBIENT: CONFUSED MURMUR]
"Are they... sitting down? In the middle of a fight?"
"Did someone win? Is this over?"
[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Patrick watched Zane and Finn, then closed his eyes. His mind, usually a calm sea, now had ripples of disturbance. He needed to quiet it; such feelings were a major taboo for practitioners of the Monastic Path.
[SFX: WHISPERED MANTRA]
"Without disturbance, there is calm. Without change, there is constancy," Patrick murmured, reciting the mantra. "Sooner or later, I’ll have to fight them both. And I will defeat them. Calm down... just calm down."
"Did they both just achieve the consummation of sword intent at the same time?" Sir Kenneth wondered aloud. He couldn’t help but rub his rough hands together, a look of pure joy spreading across his face.
There weren’t many people in the world who could even comprehend sword intent. Anyone who could cultivate it was considered a genius, destined to become a powerful warrior in the Eastern Territory. But to achieve the consummation of sword intent? That ranked a person among the absolute best in the Skyreach Sect. All the Guardians and elders would be scrambling to take such a student as their own, to make them one of the Sect’s Chosen.
[MUSIC: HUMBLE ORIGINS THEME]
Finn had come from a humble background. After he joined the Sect, his outstanding performance had many elders wanting to take him on as a personal disciple. But Finn's loyalty to Skytop Peak ran deep, and he wasn’t willing to leave. So, he had remained at Skytop Peak ever since.
Zane, however, had only been cultivating his swordsmanship for a short time, and he'd secretly learned the Sword Step just by watching Finn. Before this competition, most people in the Skyreach Sect didn’t even know his name. No elder had been knocking down his door to recruit him.
The Skyreach Sect, and its head, Kenneth, prized disciples who showed a mastery of sword intent above all else.
At a sharp warning from Master Jasper, the disciples fell silent, not daring to even breathe too loudly.
The Skyreach Sect placed immense value on nurturing talent and wouldn’t tolerate anyone disturbing gifted individuals in a moment of breakthrough. They paid special attention to the geniuses who stood out from the masses.
You could say that the value of a few geniuses like Zane, Finn, Patrick, and Tracy was almost equal to that of hundreds of thousands of ordinary disciples combined. To be a genius was to stand out from the crowd; to be average was to be forgotten.
The Skyreach Sect was tolerant. Every disciple received a variety of benefits and support just for joining. But it was also cruel. The resources showered on the gifted were a world away from what was given to the ordinary members. That was just the way the system worked.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TRANSITION]
Zane felt like he was in a strange state. With his eyes closed, he should have been seeing nothing but darkness.
But right now, he could see a mountain towering in front of him.
[SFX: METALLIC SHIMMER]
It was a mountain, its slopes covered in blades that jutted out like jagged rocks.
A mountain made out of swords.
