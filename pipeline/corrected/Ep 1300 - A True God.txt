Ep 1300 - A True God

Word count: 1158

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: CELESTIAL WIND]
The rules of the Heavens were, for all intents and purposes, unbreakable.
Of course, that hadn’t stopped people from trying. A small group of Supreme Lords once hatched a desperate plan to get around them. First, they created a secret, incredibly expensive elixir of rebirth. 
[SFX: DRINKING ELIXIR]
[SFX: DEATH GASP]
They drank it, died, and were reincarnated with all their memories intact. They had to start their entire lives over from scratch, climbing the ladder of power all over again. But to their horror, it was all for nothing. 
[SFX: LOTUS FLOWER ACTIVATION]
Even upon reaching the Life-and-Death Realm and activating the Lotus Flower during the Lesser Celestial Tribulation, they remained trapped by the same limitations.
This failure explained why all races obsessed over the Illusion Battlefield and its Ace Talents, hoping one would become a True God and shatter the barrier of the Heavens.
Most believed a new True God would emerge from the Soul Sea Realm. The current era had produced an explosion of prodigies - warriors between twenty and forty who had already reached the Soul Sea Realm with no signs of slowing. So many had risen that people called them the “Golden Generation.”
*The Godly Way of Snow.*
*Hayden.*
Zane’s eyes lit up. He’d never even heard her name before, which probably meant she wasn’t from this era. This was his chance. 
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
He started walking toward her, trying to look casual as he wracked his brain for an opening. He was dying to know what it took to be compatible with The Godly Way of Snow. The woman—Hayden, a True God—had mentioned a Godly Sigil, but he had no idea what that was. Man, if he could just get back to The Elysian Keep, The Eternal Scribe would have the answer in a heartbeat.
This was a big deal. If he ever wanted to break through the Heavens and find the Divine Land, he needed to understand this. 
“After you surpass the Heavens and become a True God,” Zane asked, “where do you go?” His thoughts had already jumped to Elara — the trail he still hadn’t finished chasing.
“The Divine Realm,” Hayden said. “The endless Divine Realm.”
Zane fixed the words in his mind.
Around them, the last of the ice-rivers stilled. Hayden touched the bell once more, and the blizzard died away completely, leaving only white silence. Then an invisible cold slid down Zane’s spine — absolute cold, the kind where even energy and mass seemed to disappear. He had no strength left to resist it.
When the chill finally eased, Hayden let out a soft sigh. “You’re not compatible with the Godly Sigil.”
A flame ignited behind Zane’s eyes, and the ice world vanished around him.
After a moment’s thought, Zane stepped out of the warm lamplight and continued down the hall.
He’d only taken two steps when something on the walls of the Corridor of the Emperor caught his eye. 
Murals.
[MUSIC: HAUNTING STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: HEAVY SNOWFALL]
The first carving showed the birth of a baby girl. It must have been snowing hard that day, because the sky in the mural was white with it, and a solar eclipse hung in the sky like a black hole.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Zane moved on to the second mural. The girl was a little older now, just starting her martial arts training. It looked like she was learning to control her power.
By the time he reached the third mural, a long sigh escaped him.
*I think I get it,* he thought. *This is her life story, carved right into the walls.*
So this was what happened when you became a True God—the Heavens themselves etched your story into stone for all time.
He walked deeper into the Corridor, studying the murals one by one. They showed her rising as a warrior, adored by everyone. They showed her activating the Lotus Flower and a dozen other moments from her life.
*Whoa. When she hit the Life-and-Death Realm, she only activated one petal of the Lesser Lotus Flower!* 
The thought hit him with such force he almost stopped in his tracks. In the Upper World, the number of lotus petals you activated was directly tied to your potential to break through the Heavens. It was the ultimate measure of talent.
Anyone who could only manage a single petal was considered to have the most basic, bottom-of-the-barrel talent. Even a standard tenth-grade sacred place wouldn’t waste a core disciple spot on a warrior like that. Their core disciples were expected to activate at least five to seven petals.
[MUSIC: RISING INSPIRATIONAL STRINGS]
But as he continued down the corridor, Zane started to see the pattern. He began to understand how this woman had become a True God against all odds.
Every single time she faced a Heavenly Tribulation, she activated another petal. It meant she had endured nine Lesser Celestial Tribulations to finally activate all nine petals of the Lesser Lotus Flower. She did the same in the Soul Sea Realm, the Spirit Supreme Realm, and the Spirit Transformation Realm, activating a new petal with every trial she overcame. In the end, she had activated every last petal of both the Greater and Lesser Lotus Flowers.
Zane’s thoughts snagged there. After his own first Lesser Celestial Tribulation, he’d already opened thirty-five petals of his own Greater Lotus Flower — missing only the final one. Every tribulation since had made him stronger, but that last petal still refused to bloom.
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL TEMPLE ECHO]
Each lamp in this corridor held the mark of a True God, all of them from this universe. This place, the Corridor of the Emperor, was how their memories were saved from being erased by the Heavens.
A short distance on, another torch waited — its glow harsher, more domineering, a will that looked down on the world. Another True God’s mark, Zane thought, and stepped toward it.
[SFX: DEEP EXPLOSION]
The flame detonated around him again. When his vision cleared, he stood on an empty battlefield: a lone city ahead of him, an army of Demon Race warriors at his back, and at the city gates, a single towering Demon Race warrior holding the entire horde off alone with nothing but a broadsword.
An internal war within the Demon Race, Zane realized. And this one’s the second lamp’s True God.
“SLAY!”
“SLAY!”
“SLAY!”
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC BATTLEFIELD]
The sudden roars shattered the silence. 
[SFX: CHARGING FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: WEAPONS BRANDISHED]
Soldiers, screaming and brandishing weapons, were storming toward him.
The first of the ogres charged right at him. As the bulky figure lunged, Zane flicked his wrist, snatching the giant sword from his enemy’s grasp.
[SFX: HEAVY BLADE SWING]
[SFX: MULTIPLE BODIES FALLING]
[SFX: SWORD SNATCHED FROM GRIP]
But almost immediately, he felt a surge of deadly power closing in from behind. Without a second thought, he dropped flat to the ground.
[SFX: ENERGY BLADE WHOOSH]
A blade of pure energy sliced through the air right where his head had been, staying about three feet off the ground. It skimmed so close he felt the wind of its passage on the tip of his nose.
[SFX: THOUSANDS SCREAMING]
At the same time, the entire battlefield erupted in the screams of thousands of ogre warriors as the scene dissolved into bloody chaos.
