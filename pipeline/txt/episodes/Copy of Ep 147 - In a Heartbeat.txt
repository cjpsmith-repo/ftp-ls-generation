Ep 147 - In a Heartbeat
Word Count: 1144

[AMBIENT: RURAL ROAD]
[MUSIC: ADVENTURE THEME]
 Zane did exactly as the big man had told him: twenty miles down the main road, straight out of the north gate of Greene County.
 At his pace, he covered the distance in no time. Just ahead, he spotted a path winding up a small mountain. This had to be Misty Peaks.
The big man was right—Misty Peaks was small and unassuming. It was just one of a dozen identical peaks, the kind most people in Greene County wouldn't even know by name. When Zane reached the summit, he saw a redwood forest stretching across one side of the mountain.
[AMBIENT: FOREST]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: TRICKLING WATER]
 Zane followed the directions and stepped into the forest. Almost immediately, the gurgle of running water reached him. Glancing around, he saw several small streams trickling down the mountainside, converging in a clear pool below.
“Dragon Valley has to be just past this pool,” he thought. “I wonder what kind of secret is hiding in there.”
[SFX: SUDDEN VOICES]
 As Zane skirted the edge of the pool, two voices suddenly barked from up ahead.
"Who goes there?"
[SFX: WHIZZING AXES]
 Before Zane could answer, a sharp whizz cut through the air. An instant later, two Whirlwind Axes were spinning straight for him.
[SFX: DAGGER IMPACT]
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
 He hadn't expected an attack so soon, but his reflexes were pure instinct. In a flash, he drew his Shattered Dagger and sent it flying. The dagger carved a sharp arc through the air, shattering both axes mid-flight. A small, dangerous smile touched Zane's lips as he peered through the dense trees, his eyes searching for his attackers.
[SFX: WEAPONS CLASHING]
 He spotted them a moment later—two men who, seeing him charge, were already raising another pair of axes, aiming for his head. Zane gave them no time to throw. He blurred past them, his Shattered Dagger mirroring his every move.
One of the guards froze, his expression locked in shock as a thin red line bloomed across his neck. In the same motion, Zane spun, and the dagger followed suit. The second guard's neck met the dagger's path. 
[SFX: BLADE WHISTLING THROUGH AIR]
 The Shattered Dagger whistled through the air with lethal precision, and as it passed the first guard, an identical crimson line appeared across his throat. The man's eyes widened in shock, his fingers instinctively reaching up as his life drained away. Before the second guard could even process what had happened to his companion, the dagger had already found its mark, slicing through flesh with ruthless efficiency. Blood sprayed in a fine mist, catching the dappled sunlight filtering through the trees.
[SFX: BODIES COLLAPSING]
[SFX: WEAPONS CLATTERING]
 In a heartbeat, both House Sutton guards collapsed to the ground, their weapons clattering uselessly beside them. Zane observed their deaths with cold detachment, noting how quickly the light faded from their eyes. These men had made their choice when they aligned with those who stood against him, and he felt no remorse for the swift justice he'd delivered.
[AMBIENT: FOREST CLEARING]
 Zane recalled his Shattered Dagger and strode past the two bodies without a second glance. The big man had said Dragon Valley was the base camp for the Sequoia Circle. Sure enough, the valley was littered with weapons, whiskey barrels, and a few crudely built wooden cabins for shelter.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
 Zane moved through Dragon Valley, but he didn't see another soul. It was strange. Eerily quiet. Why were those two guards the only ones here?
“This is wrong,” Zane thought, frowning. *If the Seven Ruling Families are here, they wouldn't leave the valley with just two low-level guards from House Sutton. So where is everyone? Is there another way in or out of this place?*
He scanned his surroundings as he pushed deeper into the valley. That's when he noticed them: countless footprints packed into the dirt. He followed the tracks with his eyes, but the trail didn't lead to a passage. It stopped dead at a sheer rock wall, where three massive redwood trees grew. The footprints led straight to the trees and then… vanished.
Zane’s frown deepened as he studied the three redwoods. The base of each trunk was as wide as a serving platter. They were ancient, judging by their size. But the one in the middle had been sawed clean through, leaving only a massive stump. Looking closer, he could see the cut was fresh.
[SFX: HAND PASSING THROUGH ILLUSION]
 After a moment's thought, Zane reached out to touch the stump. His brow furrowed as his hand passed right through it. It was a shimmering illusion.
A Magic Circle, he realized. A powerful one. It all clicked into place. The Sequoia Circle had lived here for years without finding this. Why? Because the entrance was sealed, hidden until someone cut down that specific tree. And the Seven Ruling Families must have been the ones to do it.
Zane prepared to step through the portal, but a flicker of caution made him pause. He pulled a simple mask from his tunic and secured it over his face before stepping through the shimmering illusion.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY FIELD]
[SFX: CRACKLING LIGHTNING]
 The sight that greeted him on the other side stole the air from his lungs. He wasn't in a tunnel, but at the edge of a vast, shimmering field of energy—a magical ward. Arcs of raw lightning, thick as tree trunks, ripped across its surface. A cold dread washed over Zane. He knew with absolute certainty that if one of those bolts touched him, he wouldn't just die. He’d be vaporized. Nothing left but ash.
He swallowed hard. Until this moment, he’d never faced anything that could utterly annihilate him.
And this… this ward wasn't even a living thing. It was just a spell, an enchantment stretched across the landscape, humming with unimaginable power. Zane could only wonder if some long-dead archangel had woven this barrier.
His first instinct was to test its strength with his Nether Essence, but he immediately dismissed the thought. The ward seemed passive, content to exist unless provoked. He had no intention of provoking it. He wasn't about to find out what that kind of end felt like.
[SFX: DISTANT MOVEMENT]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
[SFX: WINGS UNFURLING]
 As Zane retreated cautiously from the ward's edge, something stirred beyond the barrier. A massive shape emerged from the distant trees—initially resembling a tiger until it unfurled magnificent wings. A Griffin.
Ordinary predators, even in great numbers, would have posed little threat to someone of Zane's abilities. This creature, however, defied comprehension. The Griffin towered hundreds of feet tall, its shadow darkening the landscape like an eclipse. Before this ancient colossus, Zane stood diminished—not merely vulnerable, but insignificant. A mere speck against majesty that could snuff out his existence with a single talon.
