Ep 661 - The True Dragon Body

Word count: 1155

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL ORCHESTRAL]

It wasn't just Malcolm who was shocked. Fabian and Esther were right there with him, their jaws practically on the floor.

Zane’s black fire had seemed almost unstoppable when he’d unleashed it, scorching the very tongue of the Goliath Bull-Toad. But the toad, for all its power, was still a living thing. It made a certain kind of sense that the fire could burn it.

This sword, however, was something else entirely. It was clearly a cut above any Relic-Class weapon they’d ever seen, and they all had the same thought: this might be a Divine-Class weapon.

[SFX: CRACKLING FLAMES]

And Zane’s black fire was literally eating it.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

[SFX: METAL MELTING]

As it did, a river of Celestial Essence began to pour toward him.

“That’s so much Celestial Essence,” Esther breathed.

“Whoa! You could forge weapons with that!” Fabian exclaimed.

A fine idea, except there wasn’t a single weaponsmith among them. The essence was priceless, but to them, it was useless.

[SFX: METAL CRACKING]

[AMBIENT: INTENSE FORGE HEAT]

As Zane intensified the heat, a small hole appeared in the center of the blade. It widened, growing until it was as broad as the sword itself, leaving the bottom half a shimmering, fiery pool of molten liquid.

[SFX: MASSIVE METAL CRASH]

A moment later, the entire top half of the blade gave way, plunging into the earth with a deafening crash that sent a spiderweb of cracks across the ground.

Zane didn't even glance at it. His eyes were fixed on the rippling pool of metal as he continued to melt what was left.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL POWER SURGE]

The Celestial Essence surging from this one sword was equal to that of millions of Mythic weapons combined. It flowed toward him in a torrent, and he gathered every last drop.

This new power would also boost his Dragon Scales, making them more critical than ever. Used at the right moment, they could work miracles. Now that he’d established the Confluence Platform within him and refined his body, his physical limits had skyrocketed. He could handle far greater amounts of power.

As he focused all his will on melting the sword, a question formed in his mind, directed at the Cobalt Dragon. What would happen if I lit up all the Dragon Scales?

He’d only ever ignited a few at a time. The thought of activating all of them at once had never even crossed his mind; he couldn’t begin to fathom the amount of Celestial Essence it would take.

But now, looking at the torrent flowing into him from a single ancient blade, the question felt less impossible than it once had.

The Cobalt Dragon was quiet for a moment. Then he said, simply: "If all the Dragon Scales are lit… I will be able to reconstruct the True Dragon Body."

"The True Dragon Body?" Zane repeated.

The Cobalt Dragon let out a long, slow breath — something almost like a sigh. "When you first lit the scales, I never dared imagine you'd grow this far. The essence of the nine of us brothers has been suppressed under this furnace for so long. Our physical bodies… completely destroyed. But if we could reconstruct it—" He paused. "We might actually see the sun again."

The words carried a weight Zane didn't fully know how to answer. Reconstructing a True Dragon's body wasn't something the Cobalt Dragon had ever let himself hope for. But hope had a way of growing on its own.

The Cobalt Dragon's voice was quieter now. "Zane. Thank you."

Zane smiled slightly. "Don't thank me. I need the Dragon Scales as much as you need them lit. And honestly, I don't know when I'll manage to light all of them."

"That may come sooner than you think," the Cobalt Dragon said.

Before Zane could answer, another voice broke in — louder, and considerably less solemn.

"Hey. You'd better help me, too."

It was the Red Dragon, the Cobalt Dragon's seventh brother.

Zane chuckled. "There's no hurry. I'll get to you once your brother's fully lit."

[SFX: FINAL HISS OF COOLING METAL]

About an hour later, the last of the ancient sword dissolved into light. Zane rose to his feet, satisfied, and turned to the group.

"All right," he said. "Let's move."

Yolande glanced at him without a word and turned to walk.

[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WIND]

[MUSIC: SOMBER LOW STRINGS]

They pressed deeper into the inner circle. The ground ahead was a wasteland — pits and craters stretching in every direction, the earth scorched and split as though something cataclysmic had torn through it ages ago. Skeletons lay scattered across the terrain, some crumbling to ash the moment a boot brushed near them. Whatever battle had been fought here, it had been beyond anything their world had seen in living memory. God only knew how many powerful warriors had died on this ground.

Malcolm, Timothy, and Shane picked their way through the debris with hungry eyes, scanning every shadow and hollow for something worth taking. They'd come this far — they might as well leave with something.

They didn't.

A few weapons turned up, half-buried in the dirt. But every single one was shattered, the spirituality long since drained out of them, the Celestial Essence gone. Broken relics with no power left. Scrap iron, essentially.

Malcolm kicked a fractured blade aside in disgust. They'd walked through a graveyard of weapons and come out with nothing.

The red dot in the sky had swelled to the size of a wagon wheel. Even through the thick layers of fog, the oppressive force of the Dark Nether Death Light pressed down on them, draining their vitality faster and faster. Everyone could handle it for now, but the cost was mounting. In just the last few minutes, Fabian had already pulled out two supreme Aether crystals, crushing them to absorb their energy.

Malcolm and Timothy were in even deeper trouble. As body refiners, they relied on their own Forceful Energy for protection—an energy that couldn't be instantly replenished with crystals. It had to be generated slowly, over time.

At first, the pressure from the Dark Nether Death Light had been manageable. They’d barely needed to use any Forceful Energy at all, relying on their sheer physical toughness. But as they drew closer to the red dot, the intensity had ramped up, and they’d been left with no choice but to shield themselves. They weren't worried initially; their bodies were still producing energy faster than it was being consumed.

Now, however, maintaining that balance was becoming a serious problem. The drain was outpacing their recovery, and their reserves were slowly but surely running out.

[MUSIC: RISING DANGER]

Once their Forceful Energy was gone, their bodies would be completely exposed to the increasingly lethal light. It would be like standing naked in a raging inferno or a frozen wasteland.

How could they possibly survive that?
