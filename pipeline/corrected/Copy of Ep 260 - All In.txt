The golden light that erupted from the Hymn of Justice was more terrifying than Zane had ever imagined.
It screamed past him, so close it nearly seared the back of his robes. A direct hit would have sliced him in two, and even the miss left him reeling from the raw power of the blast.
That kind of force was just plain terrifying.
Zane was confident in his own resilience—he'd only think twice about taking a direct hit from a veteran of the Soul Realm, someone like James Moon. And now, it seemed like Richard, channeling his Hymn of Justice, was tapping into that same level of power.
Still, it wasn’t a fair comparison. Richard was probably pushing himself to his absolute limit. James, on the other hand, hadn't even broken a sweat in their fight. Zane could still remember the crushing pressure James radiated. Both were masters of the Soul Realm, sure, but Richard had just arrived. James had been living there for years.
[SFX: ENERGY BEAM IMPACT]
The golden beam streaked past Zane and crashed into the shimmering curtain of the enchanted barrier with devastating force. For a split second, the magical shield held firm, its luminous surface rippling violently like disturbed water.
Spectators gasped collectively as the barrier strained against the onslaught, its protective runes flaring in desperate resistance. But the ancient safeguards were insufficient against Richard’s unleashed power. With a sound like shattering crystal, the radiant light punched through the supposedly impenetrable defense, leaving a jagged hole that sparked with residual energy.
[SFX: BARRIER BREAKING]
[SFX: LIGHT STREAKING]
[SFX: IMPACT ON GROUND]
Free of the barrier, the golden beam hurtled toward the unsuspecting disciples from Vulture Peak. It streaked past one young man’s head, close enough that he felt the scorching heat sear his earlobe, before boring a deep, dark hole into the ground.
The disciple remained frozen, his complexion draining to a ghostly white. If the beam had been a fraction of an inch closer, it would have taken his head clean off.
"By the ancestors," he whispered, trembling fingers touching his ear to make sure it was still there.
The All Peaks Competition was no joke; participants and spectators alike understood the grave risks. Getting maimed or even killed by a stray attack was a very real possibility.
The Vulture Peak master’s face tightened with concern. His normally composed demeanor cracked under the weight of responsibility for his charges. "Back up! Now!" he commanded his disciples, his voice sharp with urgency. If another one of those beams broke through, he knew they might not be so lucky.
[SFX: ENERGY BARRIER REINFORCEMENT]
Alarm flashed across the faces of the arena's Guardians. Vitality overflow injuring spectators would be a disaster. They immediately joined forces, pouring their own Vitality into the barrier's matrix and visibly thickening the shimmering wall of energy.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
Richard quickly sized up the situation.
He could see the Corrupt Vitality steadily chipping away at his Hymn of Justice. He had to take Zane down before it consumed the hymn completely. Otherwise, it was over.
[SFX: BURSTING GOLDEN LIGHT]
As beams of golden light burst from the Hymn of Justice, one after another, Zane moved like a cornered animal, jumping, rolling, and dodging with frantic grace.
Both fighters were desperately trying to land a finishing blow.
Richard wasn't firing blindly. Each beam came from a different angle — left, right, above — methodically cutting off Zane's options, funneling him backward until there was nowhere left to go.
[SFX: GOLDEN BEAMS — CLOSING IN FROM MULTIPLE ANGLES]
Zane's back hit the corner. Golden light blazed from his left, his right, above — a three-way trap with no gap.
"Damn," Zane muttered.
His only option was to charge straight at Richard. But Richard had already anticipated it — a final beam was already forming, aimed squarely at the path between them. A direct rush meant walking straight into it.
Then Zane's feet moved on their own.
[SFX: SHARP FOOTFALL — DIAGONAL]
Sword Step. Not a fighting technique — barely even a movement skill — but it was the one variable Richard hadn't accounted for. He'd seen Zane use it against Cecil and dismissed it as acrobatics. In the heat of the fight, he'd forgotten it entirely.
One diagonal step, a sharp twist of the body, and Zane slipped past the blocking beam. He came out of it already moving forward, closing the distance with Richard in an instant.
If Richard connected, Zane was done for. But as long as Zane kept dodging, his Corrupt Vitality would eventually devour the Hymn of Justice, and Richard would lose. 
At that same moment, the last trace of golden light on the hymn's surface went dark. The Corrupt Vitality had finished its work — the Hymn of Justice collapsed entirely, taking Richard's final option with it.
Just like that, Zane had flipped the entire fight on its head, so quickly that most of the disciples in the crowd were still trying to process what happened.
"You don’t get it," a knowing spectator mumbled to his friend.
"Richard’s sense of justice won’t let him push past his limits. If he gives up, it’s because he knows—he knows—he can’t win. That’s just who he is."
The crowd watched in stunned silence as Zane’s strategy unfolded brilliantly, his victory becoming increasingly inevitable with each passing moment. As Richard’s golden light dimmed against the relentless assault of Corrupt Vitality, the implications of Zane’s imminent triumph rippled through the arena, affecting fortunes and futures alike.
Suddenly, a wail of pure despair tore through the crowd.
"Aaaah! My points! I just lost over eight hundred points!" a disciple cried out, his voice cracking. Eight hundred points—the equivalent of sixteen hundred crystals. Even for a kid from a noble house, that was a crippling loss. He’d bet the farm on Richard and now he had nothing.
But the happiest people in the arena weren't Zane, Master Warren, or the disciples from Misty Peak. It was the ones who had bet on Zane.
[SFX: WILD ECHOING LAUGHTER]
Among them was Zachary from Ninety-Foot Peak, whose wild, echoing laughter was the only sound coming from his section. The other inner disciples shot him nervous glances, clearly thinking he'd finally snapped, but nobody dared say a word. Zachary was just that strong. He hadn’t even entered the competition, but outside of the fighters in the ring, he was probably the strongest person there.
Even Zachary knew he looked like a complete fool, just standing there and cackling like a maniac, but he was too damn happy to stop.
He had bet twelve hundred crystals on Zane and was about to get a nine-to-one payout.
With Zane’s victory, Zachary's personal stash of crystals would skyrocket to over ten thousand. It was a ridiculous amount of wealth, more than he'd ever dreamed of. It made him rich. Instantly.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT]
Every time he pictured those ten thousand crystals, Zachary felt his heart pound and his breath catch in his throat.
Should he keep betting on Zane? Now that Zane had beaten Richard, the odds were bound to drop for the next match. This had been a hard-fought victory; Zane had proved himself. No bookie in their right mind would offer such high odds on him again. They'd lose their shirts.
Even if the odds drop to one-to-two, my ten thousand will become twenty thousand, Zachary thought, his heart hammering.
Twenty. Thousand. Crystals.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED INTENSITY]
His expression hardened into a mask of pure determination. He was going all in. Zachary decided he would bet every last one of his ten thousand crystals on Zane.
