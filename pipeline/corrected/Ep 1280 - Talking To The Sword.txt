Ep 1280 - Talking To The Sword

Word count: 1043

[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]
[AMBIENT: DARK SHADOWY CORRIDOR]
Zane’s provocation worked.
[SFX: HOOK WHIPPING THROUGH AIR]
[SFX: HOOK PIERCING FLESH]
From the darkness, a long hook shot out and snagged him. The moment it bit into his flesh, a strange power flooded his body, locking his muscles and making it impossible to resist. 
[SFX: BODY DRAGGED ACROSS STONE]
He felt like he was wrapped in chains and was pulled into the shadows like a weightless feather.
“Now!” Zane shouted.
[SFX: BOWSTRING RELEASED]
[SFX: FLAMES WHOOSHING]
The instant he was dragged across the threshold, Hailey released her bowstring. Three swirling balls of flame had been hovering in the air before her Moonburst Flamebow, and now they shot forward.
[SFX: THREE FIREBALLS STREAKING]
The target of all three fiery orbs was Zane himself.
Truthfully, the woman had hesitated. The Moonburst Flamebow was a Divine Weapon, and while she couldn’t unleash its full potential, the arrows were still more than enough to obliterate an ordinary warrior. Even an Ace Talent would think twice before facing them. But Zane had insisted. He’d promised he’d be fine.
In the end, she could only trust him and do as he’d asked.
Larkin snorted as he watched one of his men haul Zane into their territory. 
His own thin figure blurred as he rushed forward to meet them. He still hadn’t figured out what game the kid was playing, but that didn’t mean he was afraid.
[SFX: SWORD MATERIALIZING]
“I don’t care what tricks you’re pulling,” Larkin said coldly, waving an arm as a long iron sword materialized in his hand. “Now that I have you, you’re going to die.”
Larkin had another, more famous name: the Otherworldly Swordsman. It was a title he’d earned. Among all the independent warriors, he was the only one to have ever reached the rank of Ace Talent. That was the kind of power that great clans spent fortunes to cultivate in their heirs, yet Larkin had clawed his way to the top all by himself, starting from nothing. 
His rise was a legend, a miracle for every warrior without a clan to back them. Now, within the Thoughtless Minds, he stood as an equal to men like Lawson, Nigel, and Sloane.
A thought, sharp and cold as the steel he carried, sliced through his own. 
[SFX: TELEPATHIC WHISPER]
It was the sword’s consciousness. *Against absolute strength, all schemes are useless.*
“I know,” Larkin muttered, his expression unreadable. “I shouldn’t have hesitated.” 
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS CHARGING]
He brandished the blade and charged toward Zane.
The sword had been with him for years, an artifact from a higher plane of existence that surpassed even the Heavens—a place it had called the Divine Land. 
When Larkin first found it, their energies had fought against each other, a constant drain. But over time, their bond had deepened. He learned its secrets, and in return, the sword’s power had helped him become a saint-level genius. When the Illusion Battlefield opened, the sword had been clear: this competition was crucial.
So far, he’d faced no real resistance. There were few people in the entire universe who could truly challenge him. This kid seemed clever, sure, but he wasn’t nearly strong enough to be a real threat.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[SFX: FLAMES ERUPTING]
Just as that thought crossed his mind, three powerful orbs of flame erupted from the darkness right behind Zane.
The warrior who had hooked Zane was impulsive, but he was also thorough. 
[SFX: SPEAR THRUST WHOOSH]
The moment his prey was in range, he’d lunged, a short spear in his hand shooting straight for Zane’s head. The spinning tip was just about to pierce his skull. He never expected three fiery arrows to appear out of nowhere, aimed at the exact same spot.
“Damn it!” the warrior cursed, instantly abandoning his attack. 
[SFX: SPATIAL BLINK]
His figure flashed as he tried to blink out of existence.
But a faint smile crossed Zane’s face. 
[SFX: IRON GRIP CLAMP]
His hands shot out like lightning and clamped down on the warrior’s arm with an iron grip. The man was trapped.
“Die!” 
[SFX: SWORD SLICE THROUGH AIR]
At the same time, Larkin’s sword sliced through the darkness, aimed straight for Zane.
He didn’t even have to think about his attack. The sword handled everything. It was his greatest weapon, an extension of his own will. It had seen ages pass and regarded the entire universe as child’s play. It had never considered even the Supreme Lords to be unbeatable, as if there was nothing left that could surprise it.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
But before the blade could land, the three fireballs detonated.
[MUSIC: ROARING INFERNO]
[AMBIENT: BLINDING FLAME AND HEAT]
The blast tore through the darkness, light and heat pouring out in a wave so fierce that even the Dark Law couldn’t hold against it—the flames seemed to be devouring the shadows outright. Across the hall, the warrior maintaining the Dark Law strained just to keep it standing.
The rest of Larkin’s warriors fled the instant the fire burst open. The one still trapped in Zane’s grip wasn’t so lucky—he was burned to ash in the blast.
Larkin’s eyes went wide. Every instinct in him screamed to retreat.
*Don’t be afraid, kid—I can shield you,* the sword sent, and a wave of golden light poured from its hilt, spreading down Larkin’s arm and across his chest and neck until he was cloaked head to toe in gold.
The flames broke against him and left him untouched.
[SFX: ETHEREAL ENERGY]
But as Larkin pushed through the blaze, the sword saw something that stopped it cold. Zane, too, was wrapped in the same golden light—except where Larkin’s shield simply held the fire back, the light around Zane was pulling the flames in, consuming them whole.
*What... is that?* the sword sent, edged with something it hadn’t felt in longer than it could remember: genuine shock.
“What’s wrong?” Larkin asked.
*Nothing. Kill him first,* the sword answered.
“Alright.” Larkin was already on top of him. Most of the techniques the sword had taught him were for movement; the blade itself was the real invincible force. As long as Larkin could get close to an opponent, the sword would do the rest. He was fairly certain that if he ever got within striking distance of a Supreme Lord, the sword could kill them in a single blow.
[SFX: WHOOSHING BLADE]
The black iron sword drew a silent arc in the air. It didn’t radiate any power, but Larkin knew just how devastating its silent passage was.
The young man before him was already dead.
Zane smiled at Larkin. 
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: GLOWING LIGHT PORTAL]
With impossible grace, he began to float backward, retreating into the light.
