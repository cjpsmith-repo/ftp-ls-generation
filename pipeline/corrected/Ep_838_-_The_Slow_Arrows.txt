Ep 838 - The Slow Arrows

Word count: 1032

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL OCEAN WAVES]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

The warriors pressed toward the Sacred Feather Sea—a vast, untapped mine. The Blood Sacrifice Power guarding it would weaken after the Sacrificial Ceremony for five days before surging back with such intensity that even Soul Sea Realm masters couldn't advance.

As the group’s pace naturally slackened, Zane, who was trailing Randy, eased up on his descent as well.

[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACKLE]

Just then, several figures shot toward them from the island’s shore. The one in the lead was literally crackling with lightning.

Randy glanced back at him. “That’s Fredrick,” he said, his voice low. “Crown prince of the York Divine Kingdom.”

Fredrick hadn’t fought anyone in the last tournament. The royal families usually stayed out of those kinds of brawls, but ever since Fiona had entered the fray, a lot of princes from the divine kingdoms were itching for a shot at Zane. That is, until Zane’s bout with the Little Monster, which had ended with War King York himself stepping in. 

When Zane landed a punch that made the emperor cough up blood right there in front of everyone, the crown princes had wisely backed off. If Zane could injure a Soul Sea Realm master, then he was on a whole other level.

But that didn’t mean they’d accepted defeat. The union of the seven divine kingdoms had produced countless brilliant talents over the years. Zane might be stronger now, but they were confident they could surpass him one day. Besides, armed with their semi-divine and Divine-Class weapons, the princes still looked down on him.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: COMPETITIVE TENSION]

With a whoosh, Fredrick and the six warriors flanking him shot past. Zane and Randy had left first, but they weren’t in any particular hurry, so it didn’t take long for Fredrick’s group to overtake them. As he flew by, Fredrick shot Zane a sharp, appraising look but didn’t pause for even a second before continuing onward.

“Fredrick’s got a hidden talent of his own, and it's no joke,” Randy warned Zane. “I haven’t seen him fight in seven or eight years, but I’d bet he’s at least as strong as Fiona.”

Still, Randy seemed confident Zane could take him. After all, he’d handled the Little Monster and had taken every punch War King York could throw at him. No matter how scary Fredrick’s talent was, going up against Zane would be a tall order.

Zane just nodded, his expression unreadable.

[MUSIC: GRADUAL TENSION BUILD]

As they pushed deeper into the Sacred Feather Sea, the Blood Sacrifice Power pressed down harder. Everyone's altitude dropped — slowly at first, then faster, until the whole group was skimming just above the surface of the pale golden water. The True Dragon's blood they'd swallowed was holding the worst of it at bay, but it was a losing battle for most of them.

Randy raised a hand. “That's enough. Everyone down.”

[SFX: WAVES LAPPING]

He reached into his Storage Ring and produced what looked like a small folded paper boat — covered edge to edge in densely packed runes. He tossed it onto the water.

[SFX: RAPID EXPANSION]

The boat unfolded instantly, swelling from palm-sized to dozens of feet long in a matter of seconds, its hull gleaming with shifting light.

“It's a high-grade Relic,” Randy said, catching Zane's eye. “Passed down from the upper world. Weapon refiners down here rarely bother with auxiliary items like this — not worth their time when they could be forging weapons. Only craftsmen from the upper realm would spend the effort.”

One by one, they stepped aboard. The vessel steadied beneath them and began to move, cutting through the golden waves at a smooth, fast pace.

[AMBIENT: OPEN SEA]

Hours passed. The sun dropped toward the horizon, painting the golden sea in shades of amber and red. By the time darkness settled over the water, a massive shape had appeared ahead — an island, dark against the sky.

Zane didn't ask. He reached into the world's memory instead.

The Feather Emperor's Secret Land, the Dream God's Secret Land, and the Divine Emperor's Secret Land — each on its own island. He cross-referenced what he saw against what he knew.

The island ahead was the Feather Emperor's Secret Land.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS APPROACH]

As the boat drew closer, the pressure from the remaining curse intensified. Even with the ceremony's effect, what was left was enough to bear down hard on a Spirit-Tribulation warrior.

[SFX: WIND WHOOSH]

The boat finally eased toward the shore. Randy jumped off first. The others followed — and as Camilla stepped onto the island, her legs gave way beneath her. She was about to go down.

Zane caught her.

“Thank you!” she said, offering him a sweet smile. Zane gave her an indifferent glance and a slight nod in return.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY HUM]

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Down below, a soft glow of Vitality appeared in Randy’s hand and shot toward their paper boat. The vessel spun for a moment, then rapidly shrank until it was small enough to fit in his palm before flying back to him. He scanned the dense forest blanketing the island, getting his bearings. “Let’s go,” he commanded.

[AMBIENT: RUSTLING LEAVES]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON FOREST FLOOR]

They weren’t the only ones. At the same time, dozens of other warriors were disappearing into the tree line.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Once inside the forest, Randy grew extremely cautious. As he walked, he began counting under his breath.

“What are you counting, Randy?” Camilla asked, looking confused.

He ignored her, his focus absolute. “Three hundred and thirty-six, three hundred and thirty-seven…”

[AMBIENT: MYSTERIOUS FOREST]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

It was the first time Zane, Renzo, Camilla, and Barry had ever explored the Sacred Feather Sea, so Randy’s strange behavior was a complete mystery.

“Three hundred and ninety… four hundred!”

[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]

He stopped abruptly. “We’ve entered the Slow Arrow Field.”

“The what?” Camilla was more confused than ever.

“The Slow Arrow Field,” Randy repeated, his eyes scanning the path ahead. “It’s a place with… well, extremely slow arrows. Everyone, be careful.”

The others were still trying to wrap their heads around what that could possibly mean when Randy took a single step forward.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

A soft whoosh filled the air as a wall of arrows materialized right in front of them.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

Every single arrowhead glowed with a faint blue light, almost certainly coated in a deadly poison. Poisoned arrows were common enough, but the truly bizarre thing was their speed. 

They were drifting through the air slower than a person taking a casual stroll.
