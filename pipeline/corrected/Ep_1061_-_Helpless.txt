Ep 1061 - Helpless

Word count: 1146

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

Every Divine Weapon came with an epic backstory, but a Grade-One weapon was a legend in itself.

And even though Thundergust hadn’t made it onto the Cosmic Spirit Index, it was still an absolute masterpiece.

Its previous owner, Elder Dunn, had only ever been able to summon three Nether God Shadows at once. According to the Nether God Mind Method, three was the absolute limit.

It wasn’t a matter of speed. With the old man’s level of mastery, his understanding of swordplay was on a level Zane couldn’t even fathom. The problem was that as soon as a fourth Nether God Shadow appeared, the first would simply vanish to maintain the balance. No matter how many he created, only three could ever exist at the same time.

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY SURGE]

But now, with a single, casual swing from Zane, ten Nether God Shadows flickered into existence, standing shoulder to shoulder.

Zane couldn’t explain how, but he figured it had to be his Chaos Energy. It was like it was supercharging everything—the attunement handbooks, the Laws he’d activated—and giving them all a massive boost. That had to be why the number of Nether God Shadows had multiplied. It was the only theory that made any sense, even if he had no way to prove it.

Besides, he was less concerned with the why and more with the shadows themselves. And he had to admit, the technique was pretty damn cool.

Zane was already brainstorming tons of new combat applications for the Nether God Shadows—coordinated strikes with a few of them, or just overwhelming an opponent with the whole crowd.

On top of all that, he had his sword spirit, Lavender. She could easily hide among the phantoms, making her the perfect assassin.

With most of her Soul Power restored and that argent fighting soul of hers, she could probably take down a high-level master with a well-timed sneak attack.

[AMBIENT: PALACE HALL MURMURS]

Word that Zane had claimed Thundergust right after entering the Mist Palace spread like wildfire, and the other warriors couldn’t believe he’d pulled it off.

As for that insane sword strike he’d unleashed, it was the talk of the entire Mist Palace.

The Groundstone Hall warriors knew better than to mess with Zane, but the ones from Cloudswallow Hall were getting restless. Some of their more talented members were even talking trash, threatening to challenge him, like they had no clue what they were getting themselves into.

The top-tier warriors of Cloudswallow Hall were a different matter. Going after a Life-and-Death Realm newcomer — even one who’d claimed the Thundergust — wasn’t worth their time.

The real reason the top-tier warriors had gone quiet was simpler than pride. Passing the Torch was almost upon them, and every serious contender was deep in preparation. The event offered more than bragging rights — there were rewards inside it that no one wanted to miss. Going after a Life-and-Death Realm newcomer now would be a waste of time they couldn’t afford.

So for the moment, Zane had peace. He was happy to take it.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

There was one other thing quietly nagging at him. Lavender.

She had been fighting alongside him long enough that her current weapon no longer suited her. A Sacred Weapon spear had served her well once, but her soul strength had come too far for that. What she needed now was a Grade-One Divine Weapon — something worthy of what she’d become. And the only place to get one was the Hall of Arms.

Three hundred Sovereign Stones just to get back through the door.

He thought about asking Yolande. She’d probably say yes without blinking. But that wasn’t how he did things. He’d earn it himself.

He could wait. Passing the Torch would come first — and if the rumors about the rewards inside were true, the Sovereign Stones problem might solve itself.

[SFX: RESONANT BOOM]

It had started the moment Elder Dunn left. Layla had pulled out a Tiger’s Roar Token — a sound-broadcasting token capable of transmitting voice across an entire realm — and used it to challenge every warrior in the Mist Palace. Groundstone Hall. Cloudswallow Hall. Anyone who thought they were worth a fight.

It wasn’t subtle. The token forced the sound directly into the mind of every warrior in range, whether they wanted to hear it or not. Deacons, stewards, even a Deputy Palace Master or two had been annoyed enough to consider intervening — until word got around that Elder Dunn himself had sent her. After that, everyone decided the Cloudswallow Hall could sort it out on their own.

The area around Zane’s door had actually gone quiet in the days after he claimed the Thundergust. Warriors from Groundstone Hall who passed his door gave it a wide berth. There was a wariness there that hadn’t existed before. Then Layla moved in across the entrance, and all of that evaporated overnight.

[AMBIENT: CROWDED STREET NOISE]

Thanks to all the chaos Layla was stirring up, the entrance to his residence was a noisy, crowded mess again.

It was early morning, and after pulling an all-nighter training, the constant noise was the last thing he needed.

[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]

[MUSIC: PEACEFUL MEDITATION]

Lavender quietly lit three sticks of incense, hoping to help Zane focus. Their faint fragrance was supposed to be refreshing, to bring a sense of peace.

[SFX: LOUD CROWD ERUPTION]

Suddenly, an explosion of noise erupted from outside.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

“I, Garrett Lund of Cloudswallow Hall, accept your challenge!”

[SFX: CROWD CHEERING]

A wave of cheers followed immediately. It seemed like everyone was dying to see Layla finally go down.

“Come on, Garrett, you got this!”

“That woman’s way too arrogant. Someone needs to teach her a lesson!”

“Garrett Lund is ranked seventy-ninth in Cloudswallow Hall. There’s no way she can beat him.”

Zane let out a wry, half-amused, half-annoyed smile. It seemed like this chaos was his new normal, whether he liked it or not.

[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUND CROWD]

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

Over the past few days, four or five different warriors had come to challenge Layla Rhodes. And they were all from Cloudswallow Hall.

Garrett Lund, ranked seventy-ninth, was the latest challenger.

[SFX: CROWD CHEERING]

Every single one of them had been crushed by Layla. And the more humiliating their losses, the more determined Cloudswallow Hall became to take her down.

If they couldn’t pull off a win this time, the challenges would just keep on coming, one after another, until Layla was finally beaten.

Which meant the racket outside his door wasn’t ending anytime soon. Zane really, truly hoped Garrett Lund could defeat her. If not, he was doomed to suffer through this noise every single day.

Lavender glanced toward the door, her expression unreadable. “This Garrett Lund is no match for her,” she stated flatly.

Her soul perception was way stronger than Zane’s, so he knew better than to doubt her. Even from this far away, she could accurately gauge a warrior’s strength.

Zane pressed his lips into a thin, frustrated line.

He just wished someone strong enough would show up and finally give him some peace.
