Ep 1179 - Return of the Queen

Word count: 1029

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WONDER]
A tenth-tier sacred place was so vast that it was almost impossible to wrap your head around. In a supreme world, you’d be lucky to find two or three World Lords, tops. Most warriors went their entire lives without ever meeting one.
As for Supreme Lords? They were myths. Even scattered across a hundred thousand supreme worlds, they were little more than legends whispered about across the universe.
The path of a warrior was all about breaking your own limits, one step at a time. Every time you reached a new level of power, you earned a new name for it—from the five realms of body refining, through realms like Soul and Inner-Core, all the way up to Soul Sea and Spirit Transformation. The final stop, in theory, was becoming a World Lord. For anyone relying on talent and hard work alone, that was the end of the line.
To go any further, you needed something more. You needed a Crown of Destiny. Only by bearing that Heavenly Destiny did a warrior have a shot at becoming one of the mythic Supreme Lords.
There were only one hundred and twenty-eight of them in the entire universe.
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY MANIFESTATION]
And four of them had just appeared right in front of Zane.
[MUSIC: TENSE RISING STRINGS]
He was, for a moment, completely stunned. He knew they were here for his Crown of Destiny, but he was quickly realizing that the crown was a much, *much* bigger deal than he’d thought.
To make matters worse, the number kept climbing.
[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]
Five.
[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]
Six.
Seven.
[SFX: ETHEREAL SHIMMER]
A low hum filled the air as the sword spirit inside him shimmered into existence. Lavender stood beside him, her gaze fixed on the sky.
[SFX: TENSE VOICE]
“Supreme Lord Gunter,” she said, her voice tight.
“Gunter?” Zane’s eyes locked onto the seventh arrival. The man was clearly a member of the Demon Night race, but Zane had never heard the name.
“If Gunter is here,” Lavender stated, her tone flat and certain, “then the Queen of Punishment won’t be far behind.”
“The Queen of Punishment.”
[MUSIC: DARK VENGEFUL STRINGS]
The name hit Zane like a physical blow, and a cold, sharp hatred flooded him. He had never wanted to kill anyone as badly as he wanted to kill her—not even his old nemesis, Maddox. He would have spared Maddox, no matter how much he hated him, if the man had just been willing to let Elara go.
But this woman was different.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING GLOW]
As Lavender watched the sky, a deep blue light began to glow in the distance. At its head was a figure in Saint Sky Armor, a Saint Thunder Meteor Hammer gripped in her hand. 
[SFX: HEAVY ARMORED FOOTSTEPS]
She advanced slowly, deliberately.
Trailing behind her, like a second shadow, was a ribbon of deep blue light — her own Pool of Faith, flowing with her, feeding her strength with every step. This was the battle form of the three queens. With the Pool of Faith replenishing her, the Queen of Punishment’s power was no less than any Supreme Lord gathered here. The assembled lords knew it. As she came closer, one by one, they took a step back.
[SFX: RETREATING FOOTSTEPS]
She was one of the three queens of the Demon Night. She had once killed a Supreme Lord just to help Leroy Flynn claim a Crown of Destiny.
The death of a Supreme Lord was a universe-shaking event. It was an incredibly difficult feat with massive consequences. Even a top-tier Supreme Lord couldn’t be one hundred percent certain they could take out a lower-ranked one.
But Lavender had done it. She had been the first World Lord to ever kill a Supreme Lord, even if she’d had to use the Faith Energy from the Pool of Faith to pull it off.
No one ever forgot. 
From that day on, every Supreme Lord in the universe knew her name: the Queen of Slaughter, the woman who had slain one of their own without bearing any Heavenly Destiny herself.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Then, several hundred years ago, she had vanished.
The Demon Night did their best to keep it quiet, but with statues of their queens in every sacred place, hiding her absence was impossible. For the Supreme Lords of other races, it was great news, and they eventually pieced together the truth: the Demon Night’s two most powerful queens were fighting each other.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD OF LORDS]
And now, watching her emerge from that tiny Elysian Keep, it seemed the rumors were true. The most powerful of the Demon Night queens was hiding right here. The gathered lords watched, suspicious. Why hadn’t she tried to bear the Heavenly Destiny herself? She already had a Crown.
Addison stepped forward. “Hold on.” His eyes were on the Queen of Punishment, not on the Keep. “You’re walking into a Crown of Destiny dispute between all of us. This isn’t Demon Night business.”
A figure drifted to the Queen’s side like smoke — Gunter, appearing without a sound, wild smile fixed on his face. He looked at Addison. “The Queen of Slaughter of the Demon Night is hiding in that Keep,” he said pleasantly. “Tell me, old monster — does that not make it an internal affair of our clan?”
Addison’s eyes narrowed. “The Queen of Slaughter.” He turned the name over, something shifting in his expression.
Around them, the other assembled Supreme Lords stirred. So that was it. The most feared warrior in the Demon Night — the woman who had killed one of their kind without bearing Heavenly Destiny — was inside that tiny Keep. That was why the Crown hadn’t moved. That was why the Demon Night was treating this like their private matter.
The suspicion on their faces deepened. If Lavender had the Crown, why hadn’t she borne the Heavenly Destiny herself?
The Queen of Punishment ignored their silent speculation. She stopped a short distance from the keep, a small, cruel smile curving her lips. 
“Lavender. Don’t you think it’s time to stop hiding?”
Lavender’s gaze was locked on her face. Even she, normally so calm and composed, couldn’t hide the fury in her eyes. 
[MUSIC: DARK CONFRONTATION]
She started to move, to speak, but Zane suddenly reached out and put a hand on her arm, stopping her. A slight, almost unnerving smile touched his own face.
“I’ll handle her.”
