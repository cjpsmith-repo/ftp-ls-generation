Through rigorous training in Martial Refinement, every martial artist honed their physical fitness and endurance to superhuman levels, pushing past the limits of a normal person and even extending their lifespans far beyond the norm.

While most people were lucky to see a hundred, martial artists could live for much, much longer. Those who reached the level of Madame Vermillion, the head of the Dragonfall Palace, for example, had an average lifespan of ten thousand years.

Madame Vermillion looked like she was sixteen, but her true age was a number so vast it was almost meaningless. It had been lost to the ages, but one thing was certain: back when she actually was sixteen, Zane’s ancestors hadn't even been born yet. The empires that existed before the current one hadn't even been founded.

And yet Zane, completely clueless, actually thought she was sixteen.

[MUSIC: SOFT CONTEMPLATIVE MELODY]

It should have sounded like an insult, but she didn't mind one bit. In fact, she was flattered. Despite her age, she still enjoyed that kind of attention now and then. But ever since she’d become the head of Dragonfall Palace, no one had dared to speak to her so casually, which meant compliments like that were few and far between.

Madame Vermillion studied Zane with those ancient eyes that held millennia of secrets. A faint smile played at her lips as she reached into her storage ring.

[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]

"Young Master Zane," she said, her voice carrying the weight of ages, "I have watched countless prodigies rise and fall throughout the eons. Few have held the potential I sense in you."

She held up a crystal vial. "This is True Dragon's Heartblood. Not the diluted essence you claimed in the Cloud Road trial, but the genuine article—drawn from an ancient Dragon who perished defending the Celestial Realm itself."

Zane's breath caught as he recognized the astronomical value of what she held.

She extended the vial toward him. "I offer you one drop—a treasure beyond measure. Use it wisely, for no gift of this magnitude will ever cross your path again."

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]

Zane was stunned speechless by the gift she was offering him. Alan, the city lord, Elder Howard, and Elder Jovan were all floored by her generosity. They had never imagined she could be so magnanimous with anyone.

[AMBIENT: GRAND PALACE HALL]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The city lord, his voice thick with envy, couldn't help but ask, "Do you... truly intend to give that to him? The True Dragon's Heartblood... isn't that a bit much for a boy like him?"

Madame Vermillion just arched an eyebrow at him. "Of course I do. Did you hear me stutter? Since when do I go back on my word?"

The city lord quickly held up his hands. "No, no, I'm not questioning you. It's just... the True Dragon's Heartblood is priceless. It’s too valuable to just be given away."

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS UNDERTONE]

He couldn't shake the feeling that something else was going on. The True Dragon's Heartblood was one of the Dragonfall Palace's most sacred treasures. The idea that Madame Vermillion would just hand it over to a kid from the Lower World like Zane—someone who had no right to even be in their presence—seemed too good to be true. He had a nagging suspicion it was all connected to that dragon, Josh. Or maybe the Head had discovered something else entirely.

Still, the city lord knew better than to ask. Besides, he’d already received a message from the Cobalt Dragon and knew not to interfere with Zane. Whatever he thought about it, there was nothing he could do. And whatever Madame Vermillion was planning, it was far above his station to question it.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]

While the city lord had pieced together that this was all about the Cobalt Dragon, Elder Howard, Alan, and Elder Jovan were still completely in the dark. They couldn't wrap their heads around why the head of the Dragonfall Palace would offer Zane the True Dragon's Heartblood.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]

For Elder Howard, though, the shock curdled into a bitter resentment. He was one of the lucky few who had ever received a single drop of the True Dragon's Heartblood, and only as a reward for his incredible contributions to the palace. It hadn't come easily; he had nearly died earning that reward.

But this kid, Zane, had just waltzed into the Dragonfall Palace, done nothing to prove himself, and was now getting a drop as a casual gift. What was so damn special about him that the Head favored him this much?

Despite the jealousy gnawing at his insides, Elder Howard could only attribute this stroke of fortune to Zane's extraordinary luck. The young man seemed to attract opportunities that others spent lifetimes pursuing.

Madame Vermillion turned to Zane, her expression softening into a brilliant smile. "It's a special gift, so it's only right that it goes to someone truly worthy." She paused, her eyes searching his face as if measuring his character one final time. "Wouldn't you agree, Zane?"

Zane felt the weight of every gaze in the chamber pressing down on him. Elder Howard's barely concealed resentment, the city lord's calculating assessment, the confusion radiating from Alan and Elder Jovan—all of it crystallized in this single moment. Yet beneath the scrutiny, he sensed something else from Madame Vermillion: genuine hope that he might prove worthy of such a precious treasure.

"Yeah! You're absolutely right," he replied, unable to suppress the grin spreading across his face. The sheer magnitude of what he was receiving hadn't fully registered yet, but gratitude surged through him nonetheless. He stepped forward and accepted the generous gift from her outstretched hand, the vial warm against his palm.

[SFX: SUPERNATURAL WHOOSH]

The moment he did, the ghost of the Owl Beast was unleashed, overwhelming him instantly. His soul, even after all its recent growth, couldn't handle the sheer force of the impact. Inside the sixth circle of the black ball, the pressure was already thirty-two times what it had been in the first. But when the Owl Beast's ghost broke free, that pressure became utterly unbearable.

The combined force from the sixth circle and the ghost's influence nearly tore Zane's soul to shreds. 

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

But just in the nick of time, the Cobalt Dragon activated The Soulforge, and its colorful glow flooded his being, stitching his soul back together. Bathed in that mysterious light, Zane finally broke through to the level of Fighting Soul.
