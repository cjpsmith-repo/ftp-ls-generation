Ep 683 - A Rare Treasure

Word count: 1100

[AMBIENT: OCEAN WAVES CRASHING]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Waves crashed against the shoreline reefs, a constant, violent roar that was the only song the Chaotic Ocean of Stars had ever known. For centuries, it had raged like this, a tempest of untamed water. Only the strongest and bravest dared to venture into its abyss. After all, the ocean hid just as many treasures as it did dangers, and every year it became a watery grave for the warriors who gambled and lost.

The Sea Wyrm Sect, a Third-Grade Sect, was perched on the far eastern edge of the main continent, right on the ocean’s doorstep. They understood its moods better than anyone. They knew that while the Chaotic Ocean of Stars was deadly, powerful beasts rarely strayed into these marked territories. Every spring, the Sea Wyrm Sect sent its disciples into these waters for a grueling trial to forge their strength.

Their mission was to hunt a specific level-four water beast: the Duskshell Clam. The clam itself wasn't much of a fighter, but its shell was legendarily tough, making it no easy kill. Slaying one might yield a treasure called a Shadow Pearl, a priceless ingredient for crafting pills.

These pearls were so popular in the markets of the Heartlands that the sect could never keep up with demand, making them a massive source of income. As a bonus, the clam’s meat was a delicacy. Once caught, it was dried and sold in the Heartlands for a fortune, prized for its rarity.

The Sea Wyrm Sect was weak as third-circle sects went, its remote location having stunted its growth for generations. But the shifting situation in the Heartlands had driven scores of independent cultivators to its door, all of them looking for shelter. The sect had taken them in — not out of generosity, but calculation. These were experienced fighters who required no training, and they could generate wealth immediately. The sea trial wasn't really a trial at all. It was a protection fee, dressed up in the language of discipline.

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

Today, the trial began. More than ten thousand disciples, all from the Soul Realm, took to the air, skimming just above the churning surface of the water.

“Bernard! We need to find at least ten Shadow Pearls. If we don’t, there’ll be hell to pay.”

[AMBIENT: OCEAN WAVES]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The man who spoke was dressed head-to-toe in black, a scorpion tattoo etched onto his right cheek, lending him a grim, menacing air. He was addressing his second, a sharp-eyed warrior named Bernard. The surrounding seas were treacherous, even for warriors of their level, which was why they always moved in small groups. This man was part of a five-person crew, and as they broke off from the main host to begin their hunt, they carried a darker, more sinister energy than the other disciples.

These five hadn't always been sect disciples. Back in the Heartlands, they'd been pirates, working the eastern coastline, and more than a hundred warriors had died at their hands. When the sects began closing ranks against independent cultivators, the open water became unsafe. They'd had no choice but to wash their hands of the old life — or at least pretend to.

Under the five of them working in tight formation, the first Duskshell Clam didn't stand a chance. Its legendary shell cracked, and inside they found exactly what they were after.

"Lucky start," one of them said, holding up the Shadow Pearl. "First one and we're already up."

Bernard barely glanced at it. He just nodded and pushed deeper into the water. Eventually, his eyes lit up. “Well, look at that. Looks like my luck’s finally turning."

[SFX: WATER SPLASH]

Without another word, he plunged into the water below.

The other four men exchanged a glance before pulling small aqua-breath pills from their pockets and swallowing them. One of them, clearly more experienced, did a quick, cautious scan of the area to make sure no other teams were nearby.

[AMBIENT: UNDERWATER BUBBLES]

Seeing the coast was clear, he flew over to the spot, popped his own pill, and followed Bernard into the deep.

But before any of them hit the water, the sky cracked open.

[SFX: THUNDERCLAP]

[MUSIC: SHARP STING]

A bolt of lightning split the clouds, and something came screaming down from above at impossible speed, trailing a column of displaced air. It hit the ocean surface like a hammer, throwing up waves several feet high in every direction.

The five of them pulled up short, staring.

"What was that?" one of them breathed.

Bernard's eyes narrowed. He'd heard that rare treasures sometimes announced themselves this way — thunder, strange lights, objects falling from the sky. He scanned the surrounding water. No other teams in sight.

"Could be something valuable," he said. "Let's go look."

They were all skilled divers, and the pills let them cut through the water effortlessly. In no time, they reached the seafloor.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

[SFX: HEARTBEAT]

But what they found waiting for them wasn't treasure. It was a person. They all froze, staring in disbelief.

"How the hell did he just appear out of nowhere?” one of the warriors asked, his voice carried through the water by his Vitality.

[AMBIENT: UNDERWATER BUBBLES]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Bernard just shook his head, baffled. Then his gaze fell on the Storage Ring hanging on the man's chest, and a flicker of raw greed flashed across his face.

“He looks hurt,” Bernard said, his eyes meeting the others’. “And he’s trying to heal himself underwater. Weird. You think we should…?”

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TONES]

This was their old trade, after all—robbing and killing in these waters. It wouldn't be the first time they’d decided to mug someone who’d let their guard down. Even though they were disciples of the Sea Wyrm Sect now, old habits died hard, especially when no one was watching. Besides, even the so-called righteous sects in the Heartlands had their own dirty secrets. It was a simple, brutal truth of their world.

“But his power level…” one of the others began, his eyes fixed on the man meditating on the ocean floor. He trailed off, hesitating.

“Bernard, this guy… he’s a Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior!”

[MUSIC: FEARFUL TREMOLO]

Doubt washed over Bernard’s face. A warrior at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm was so far out of their league it wasn’t even funny. Even if the man was badly injured, he could probably wipe out their entire group of Soul Realm warriors without breaking a sweat.
