Ep 366 - The Blinding Arrogance
Word count: 1088

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

Some of the others were enraged by the Cloud Hall disciple's refusal to let them into the Hidden Test. They’d been planning to try their luck at the Hidden Test, but now they found themselves barred from entry, forced to swallow their pride while these arrogant guards mocked their very existence.

Since none of them dared to go up against disciples from a Cloud Hall disciple, they remained frozen in place, caught between shame and self-preservation. Their nervous glances darted between each other, each silently hoping someone else would speak up.

One of the Cloud Hall disciples scoffed, his lip curling with disdain. "Don't you know your place? You're not qualified to breathe the same air as us. You might strut around as a big shot in some Second-Tier Sect, but here? You're nothing." He paused, letting his contempt sink in. "I'd bet you wouldn't even crack the top hundred fighters in Cloud Hall!"

His words struck like physical blows. Fury rippled through every disciple from the other sects; even disciples who had shown no interest in entering the island now glared at the Cloud Hall guards with barely contained rage. The insult wasn't just personal—it was an attack on their Sects, their masters, everything they had sacrificed to reach this point.

[SFX: SUDDEN WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]

In a flash, Zane materialised directly before the disciple and seized him by the throat. The sudden movement caught everyone off guard—one moment, he stood among the crowd, the next, his fingers wrapped around the arrogant cultivator's neck.

"Really?" Zane's voice carried a dangerous edge as he dragged the struggling disciple toward the platform's edge. The man's feet scraped against stone, leaving marks in their wake

[AMBIENT: ROARING FLAMES]

Below them stretched an endless expanse of raging fire. Waves of searing heat rose from the inferno, distorting the air itself. If Zane released his grip, the disciple would plummet straight into those merciless flames—no status or technique could save him from that fall.

"Let go of me this instant!" the disciple bellowed, his face flushing with indignation. Despite dangling over certain death, arrogance still colored his tone, as though his mind refused to accept that someone had so thoroughly overpowered him. His fingers clawed at Zane's wrist, but the iron grip didn't budge. The reality of his situation hadn't penetrated his pride—not yet.

Behind them, the other disciples watched in stunned silence. Some felt a surge of satisfaction at seeing Cloud Hall's arrogance finally challenged. Others held their breath, wondering how far Zane would push this confrontation.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL RISING TENSION]

Zane's smile widened as he deliberately loosened his grip. The disciple's body shifted downward, inch by terrifying inch. "Let you go?" Zane's voice carried a dangerous edge. "As you wish. But don't blame me for what happens next."

The disciple's eyes went wide as he slid lower, the searing heat from below washing over his back like dragon's breath. In that moment of clarity, he realized: if Zane released him, he was dead.

"You bastard, pull me up!" The scream tore from his throat, raw with desperation.

Zane tilted his head, studying the man with detached curiosity. "Still cursing at me? I almost admire your guts." A pause, then his fingers opened.

The disciple plummeted.

His stomach lurched into his throat. The world became a blur of heat and terror. He tried to scream, but fear strangled the sound in his chest. His mind fractured—this was death, this was the end, all because he'd underestimated some nobody from a backwater Sect—

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

But then he stopped falling.

The disciple's breath came in ragged gasps as his brain struggled to process what had happened. He wasn't dead. He hung suspended in the scorching air, swaying gently.

[AMBIENT: WIND HOWLING OVER ABYSS]

Looking up with trembling eyes, he saw the impossible truth: Zane held him by nothing more than his collar, pinched delicately between two fingers. His entire life—years of cultivation, decades of potential—balanced on a scrap of fabric and Zane's whim.

The message was unmistakable. Zane could end him with less effort than swatting a fly.

The two Cloud Hall disciples had figured it would be easy to keep the other fighters out of the Hidden Test. Cloud Hall had a massive reputation, and its members were known for their incredible skills, so they assumed no one would dare challenge them.

It was a nice little power trip.

The guy now dangling over the edge had assumed that being from Cloud Hall meant he could push these nobodies around and make them back down. After all, Cloud Hall was a Fourth-Tier Sect. The leaders of those Second-Tier Sects would barely qualify to be assistants there. That's how powerful Cloud Hall really was.

It was why the other fighters didn't dare cross the Cloud Hall disciples, even though some of them were probably stronger.

[SFX: SWORD SLASH]

[SFX: SCREAM OF PAIN]

Suddenly, the second Cloud Hall disciple stared in shock as his own arm was lopped off at the shoulder. He clutched the bleeding stump, shouting through gritted teeth, "How dare you cut off my arm! I'll—"

[SFX: BLADE SWOOSH]

Before he could finish, Zane thrust his sword, Streamer, forward, nicking the skin between the disciple's eyebrows. A few drops of blood trickled from the small cut.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

"I'm not in the mood for your trash talk," Zane said flatly. "I've heard enough. Say one more word, and you're dead."

Hearing that, the Cloud Hall disciple started to tremble. He'd made it to the Cloud Road; he was supposed to be a top-tier disciple, miles above the average members of Cloud Hall, and certainly leagues above someone like Zane. But right now, he couldn't stop his body from shaking.

The second Cloud Hall disciple said nothing. It wouldn't take long to reattach his arm and heal the wound, but he knew it would be a very long time before he could use that arm for his techniques again.

[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]

[SFX: SWORD SHEATHING]

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

After sheathing his sword and putting it away in his Storage Ring, Zane walked toward the path beside the platform. All the other fighters watched him go, their eyes filled with awe.

Zane was the only one who had dared to stand up to the disciples from Cloud Hall. They'd heard rumors about him, but they had no idea that he wasn't just gifted; he had nothing to lose. And that made him dangerous.
