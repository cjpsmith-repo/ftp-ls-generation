Ep 1010 - Don’t Wake the Beast

Word count: 1005

[AMBIENT: DEEP CAVE ECHOES]

Zane couldn’t have cared less about the few hundred supreme Aether crystals, but Freya insisted on carefully dividing them, splitting the pile right down the middle.

Once they’d stashed the crystals, they picked up the pace, heading deeper until the passage dead-ended.

Freya scanned the area but couldn’t spot any more supreme Aether crystals.

“So, what now?” she asked, turning to Zane. “Try another cave? Or keep digging?”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

Zane just pointed at the floor. “We go down.”

“Down?” Freya’s eyes went wide. “You mean to the very bottom?”

Digging sideways would be a pain, sure, but they’d probably still find plenty more crystals. And with their combined strength, they could tunnel anywhere on this level. A cave-in would barely be an inconvenience.

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

But going deeper meant going after Sovereign Stones. And she’d heard the stories—level-eleven and level-twelve beasts roamed the depths. She could probably handle a level-eleven, but level-twelve? Those things were on par with Spirit Supreme Realm masters. If she ran into one of those, she was dead. Simple as that.

It hit her then just how dangerously ambitious this guy from the Life-and-Death Realm really was.

Zane just watched her, a faint smile on his face. He didn’t need her to come with him; he wasn’t about to force her. Honestly, he’d have more freedom digging alone anyway. Having a partner just felt like carrying extra weight.

Freya looked at him for a long moment. Then she made up her mind. Her instinct had brought her this far. She wasn’t turning back now.

[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]

He took two steps forward, picked a spot, and made a fist. He rotated his wrist slowly as the power of two thousand Dragon Scales coalesced in his right hand. Then he slammed it into the ground.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

[SFX: STONE CRACKING]

With a single, devastating punch, cracks spiderwebbed across the stone floor, spreading out from under his feet.

Freya’s jaw dropped. The guy’s strength was unreal. And from the look of it, he hadn’t even put his full power into that punch. His physical strength was probably way beyond her own, and she was in the Soul Sea Realm. Is he really just Life-and-Death Realm? she thought, stunned.

Zane threw a second punch.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

[SFX: GROUND COLLAPSE]

The fractured ground exploded, collapsing into a massive crater seventy or eighty feet deep right where he stood.

He kept going. Punch after punch, driving straight down — a hundred feet, two hundred, five hundred. As the shaft deepened, Freya didn’t just watch. She worked the exposed walls as he went, chiseling out dozens of top-grade stones from the freshly broken rock. A thousand feet. Two thousand.

[SFX: BREAKTHROUGH RUMBLE]

Then his fist punched through entirely. The rock gave way and he dropped into open space — debris raining around him. In the same instant, the Chaos Energy in his body circulated and his figure steadied, floating silently in the air of the cave below.

[MUSIC: TENSE DESCENT]

Above, Freya stood at the edge of the shaft and looked down. She could stay here. Keep mining top-grade stones, stay safe, spend fifty years getting out the slow way. Or she could follow him.

She jumped.

[AMBIENT: DEEP CAVE WINDS]

The caves down here were nothing like the tunnels above. Those had been carved by human hands over generations. These were natural, formed by the earth itself, tens of thousands of feet below the surface, never touched by sunlight. Cold winds moved through the passages in irregular gusts, carrying a heaviness that pressed against them. They moved carefully, voices low. A wrong step here meant waking something that couldn’t be outfought.

[SFX: MYSTICAL ROAR]

This time, a faint roar from the token caught Zane’s attention. He made a mental note—the token was an interesting little item. But it was Freya who reacted first.

“That’s a Roaring Token,” she said quietly, her voice careful. “Incredibly rare — made from a divine beast whose voice carries across the entire universe. The Law of Causality in its cry means the sound reaches every living being simultaneously, no matter where they are. Ancient Supreme Lords hunted the beasts nearly to extinction. A single token is worth tens of millions of Sovereign Stones.” She paused. “Whoever just used one is broadcasting their message to the entire universe.”

“To put it in terms you’d understand,” Freya added, “the Soulbound Sect controls roughly one-twentieth of this entire Upper World. Ten years of everything the Sect harvests — every stone, every resource, every drop of revenue — wouldn’t buy half of one of these tokens. That’s what just got used to shout at someone.”

Zane said nothing. But inwardly, something clicked. The Roaring Token in his own possession — the unlimited one the Master had given him — suddenly felt a great deal more interesting. If a token could reach every corner of the universe at once…

It could find Elara.

He filed it away. Sovereign Stones first.

[AMBIENT: DEEP CAVE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

They squeezed through a narrow passage that opened up into a vast cavern.

[SFX: ECHOING DRIPS]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

And there, lying in the center, was a massive creature. It had nine heads, each one resting on the ground, all of them sound asleep.

[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]

“A Hydra,” Freya breathed, her voice tight with fear. “A fully grown one.”

“Shh!” she hissed in Zane’s ear. “That’s a level-twelve beast—as strong as a Spirit Supreme Realm master! We have to go back, now!” Her body went rigid, every instinct screaming at her to flee.

But Zane didn’t move. He just pointed deeper into the cavern. “Look.”

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL HUM]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CHOIR]

Nestled near the sleeping Hydra was a pile of milky-white gems, at least thirty of them. Even from four hundred feet away, they could feel an incredibly pure energy radiating from them.

[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]

“Are those... Sovereign Stones?” Freya whispered, her terror suddenly warring with a jolt of greed. The level-twelve beast was terrifying, but a whole pile of Sovereign Stones was impossible to ignore.

Getting her hands on them would complete a third of her task. Even splitting them with Zane, they’d each get more than ten.

The prize was almost worth the risk. Almost.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION BUILD]

But that Hydra was a death sentence waiting to happen, and she couldn’t afford to take that lightly.
