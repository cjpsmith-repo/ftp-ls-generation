Ep 1223 - Teleportation Token

Word count: 1099

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
[AMBIENT: VAST ETHEREAL CHAMBER]
The giant Gilded Warden could have just let Zane go. But after what he’d just witnessed? Not a chance. He was far too curious.
This young man had gotten past all eighteen sentinels using nothing but his own strength. He was definitely someone to watch.
The Warden ran the math in his head again, but it just didn’t add up. He knew how it was supposed to work for warriors in the Soul Sea Realm. When they opened their Soul Seas, their Vitality liquefied, turning into a spiritual seawater stored inside their Inner Worlds. It allowed them to hold a massive amount of power, but it was still a finite resource. A Soul Sea had its limits.
Based on his calculations, Zane should have managed two Gods-Intimidating Strikes at most. Each one drained over half his Vitality. Even a prodigy with a second Soul Sea could manage three strikes total. Four made you one of the universe’s most powerful figures.
But Zane Lowell, defying all expectations, hadn’t stopped at two, or three, or even four. 
He had just stood there and calmly unleashed seventeen sword strikes.
To say the Gilded Warden was shocked was putting it mildly. The kid had done the impossible. Did he have some kind of treasure that could instantly replenish his Vitality? Or maybe he was using a magical skill to siphon energy from somewhere else? No, the Warden would have noticed.
[SFX: GOLDEN LIGHT ACTIVATION HUM]
Driven by a need to understand, the Gilded Warden activated the golden light hidden within the four sentinels, determined to get a closer look.
He wasn’t the first to be so utterly baffled by Zane. Gus, a man Zane had met years earlier, had been just as stumped, but his faith in his own Master—and the strict standards his Master had for taking disciples—had led him to accept the impossible. Gus had quickly realized that Zane didn’t have a Sea of Vitality at all, but a Chaos Sea.
The Gilded Warden, of course, was perfectly aware of Chaos Energy. It was the primordial source of all life, but it was also the very essence of death. To any living thing, it was a poison so potent that a single touch meant annihilation. Only a great avatar could possibly survive contact with it. The thought that Zane might be wielding it was so absurd it never even crossed the Warden’s mind.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED UNDERSCORE]
Watching the sentinels knit themselves back together yet again, Zane felt a flicker of real frustration — at this rate, when would it ever end? But quitting wasn’t in him, not after coming this far.
[SFX: SWORD INTENT FLARING]
Thundergust flared once more. “Break!” Ten rapid strikes reduced the four sentinels to fragments again, the golden light between the pieces dimming fast — and for a breath, Zane let himself hope. Then it surged right back to full strength, same as before.
His expression sobered. If this kept looping, brute force alone wasn’t going to end it. His gaze drifted, unfocused, toward the inner doorway — some part of him sensing he wasn’t as alone in here as he seemed. Deep in the sanctuary, the Gilded Warden felt that gaze land on him and, for just a moment, his composure wavered. One more try, he decided. If I can’t find this kid’s limit now, I’ll let it go.
But Zane didn’t cooperate. Instead of striking to kill, he shifted tactics entirely.
“Eight Smoky Melodies!”
[SFX: MIST SWIRLING]
Wisps of white mist curled off him as he slipped fluidly between all four reformed sentinels, always just out of reach. No matter how their palms roared or their Dragon Scales flared, every strike landed on empty air. Zane grew looser by the second, weaving through them like he was floating in water, glancing toward the door now and then with the ghost of a smirk. If the sentinels wouldn’t stay broken, he’d just waste his hidden opponent’s patience instead.
It worked. Worn down, the Warden reached out and pinched shut the power animating all four statues, freezing them where they stood.
[SFX: STONE DOOR CREAKING OPEN]
The door to the eighteenth chamber creaked open. Zane stepped past the motionless sentinels without a backward glance, mildly satisfied his gamble had paid off — whoever was back there clearly hadn’t wanted to drag this out any further than he had.
Inside, there were no more statues waiting for him. Just a bare offering table, and on it, a single token of white jade. 
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE MYSTERIOUS]
Zane was completely unaware of the Warden’s scrutiny. 
He was too busy admiring the Teleportation Token he’d just acquired from the sanctuary—the same one the Supreme Lords had once used to reach the Elysian Keep. He’d spent a staggering three hundred thousand Illusion Points just to enter this cave, so he’d been hoping for a decent payout.
Fortunately, this token was more than just a fair trade; it was the perfect solution to one of his biggest problems.
The Illusion Battlefield he was in was divided into fifty supreme worlds. Without using one of the hidden test passageways, crossing from one to another was an exhausting, time-consuming ordeal. But this Teleportation Token changed everything. 
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
With it, he could travel back and forth between the supreme worlds with ease. The thought of it made a grin spread across his face.
He clutched the token, then took one last look into the depths of the ancient sanctuary. He could feel the presence of several incredibly powerful individuals within the inner court, but he knew better than to pry. 
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ECHOING]
After placing the token securely in his Spatial Sanctum, Zane turned and left.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
The moment he stepped out, he was instantly transported back to the outer courtyard. 
[MUSIC: LIGHT MYSTERIOUS]
[SFX: INSECT WINGS BUZZING]
The small, six-winged insect reappeared before him, as if it had been waiting. This time, however, its vicious glare was gone, replaced by a strange stillness that almost felt like a nod of approval.
Zane followed it along the path toward the entrance. The spinning lights were still there, but he walked with an easy confidence now, a world away from the tension that had gripped him on the way in.
[MUSIC: TENSE OMINOUS]
[AMBIENT: CAVE ENTRANCE WIND]
Just as he reached the entrance, he heard a scuffle. 
[SFX: BODIES THUDDING]
From where he stood, he could see a scattered pile of Illusion Points at the mouth of the cave, lying among the corpses of several warriors.
