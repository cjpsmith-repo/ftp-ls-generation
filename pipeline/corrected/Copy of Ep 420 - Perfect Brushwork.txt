At first, it was a disaster. 

The Cobalt Dragon had chosen meditation runes as Zane's starting point—divine textures specifically designed to deepen a practitioner's focus and calm the restless mind. Typically inscribed on practice room walls or cultivation furnaces, these patterns radiated subtle energy that steadied concentration and banished distractions.

Zane understood why the Dragon had selected this particular texture. Cultivation demanded unwavering focus, a mental discipline as rigorous as any physical training. Without it, even the most talented practitioner would stumble. The meditation rune served as both foundation and tool, teaching precision while offering practical benefits.

"One of the simplest one-star divine textures," the Cobalt Dragon had explained, his ancient voice carrying a note of encouragement. "The lines are clear and straightforward—perfect for a beginner."

Zane had nodded, though he'd learned to recognize the Dragon's peculiar sense of scale. Simple was a relative term when your teacher had witnessed the rise and fall of entire civilizations. 

Still, Zane felt a flicker of determination warming his chest. He'd survived so much already. Surely he could master a few brush strokes.

His fingers tightened around the unfamiliar brush, its weight both lighter and stranger than his sword. The prepared ink gleamed wetly in the lamplight, waiting.

[SFX: BRUSH SCRAPING PAPER]

A dull, gray line bled across the smooth paper, its messy edges mocking the fine brush's potential. The failure was undeniable. Zane's fingers still hadn't adjusted to the foreign weight of the brush, so different from his sword's familiar heft. The ink felt slick and unpredictable beneath the bristles, resisting his control.

[SFX: DEEP DRAGON CHUCKLE]

"The beginning is always the hardest part," the Cobalt Dragon rumbled, amusement warming his ancient voice.

Zane just shrugged. The teasing didn't get to him; he hadn't actually expected to nail it on his first try. This was going to be hard work, and he knew it.
He studied the ruined paper, analyzing where his control had wavered. The Cobalt Dragon's earlier lesson surfaced in his thoughts: divine texture creation transcended mere painting. The artist had to become one with the brush, extending his consciousness through the bristles themselves. Only by channeling spiritual energy into the ink could he perceive its individual components—the ground Azurewood, the Frost-Leaf essence, the cloud ash—and guide each particle into perfect alignment before his hand ever moved to create that sleek, flawless line.
"Watch," the Cobalt Dragon said. A sliver of soul force — a miniature version of the dragon himself — slid down through Zane's fingers and into the quill. "Draw."
Zane moved his hand. A dazzling green line appeared on the paper, smooth and full, every particle of ink perfectly deployed.
Zane stared at it. "...So strong."
"Obviously," the Cobalt Dragon said, withdrawing. "Now you know what you're aiming for."

[SFX: PAPER SLIDING]

[MUSIC: FOCUSED MEDITATION]

He slid a fresh sheet of paper into place, closing his eyes for a moment to center himself before trying again.

He actually gets it, the Cobalt Dragon thought, surprised despite himself. The laughter from moments before faded. Even seasoned runic experts can take months to grasp this. For him, it was… natural.

"Months? It takes people months? Is it really that hard?" Zane’s shoulders slumped, the vast gulf between his own skill and the dragon's suddenly feeling immense. But hearing he was already ahead of the curve sent a jolt of motivation through him. The thought that he might actually have a knack for this reignited his determination.

A confident grin spread across his face. He grabbed another sheet of paper and picked up the brush, his brow furrowed in concentration. "Okay," he muttered to himself, "feel the ingredients... connect with my soul..."

[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION ROOM]

[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]

Closing his eyes, he pushed his energy into the brush, sensing the microscopic particles suspended in the ink. Four ingredients, he thought, visualizing the perfect strokes. Potent Azurewood Root is the main one, Frost-Leaf Herb the least. I need to push the Frost-Leaf to the bottom... that's the only way to let the Azurewood dominate. He remembered the green light from the dragon's own work. It has to be the key ingredient. He continued to break it all down in his head, analyzing every component.

"Now!"

Focusing every ounce of his concentration, Zane pressed the brush tip to the paper. The connection was instantaneous. 

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

A brilliant green light—the very same glow he’d seen from the dragon’s hand—erupted from the point of contact.

What? How did he… 

[MUSIC: AWE AND WONDER]

The Cobalt Dragon stared, momentarily stunned by the perfect brushwork. As the shock receded, a complex mix of emotions washed over him. It seems I've completely underestimated this boy.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL WONDER]

On just his third try, Zane had achieved what runic experts called "perfect brushwork"—flawlessly manipulating every component in the ink. For the Cobalt Dragon, executing this on a simple one-star texture was trivial. But for a novice to do it on his third attempt? It was unheard of.
There was another thing worth noting. Because Zane had achieved perfect brushwork — fully utilizing every material in the ink — his one-star texture was effectively equivalent to a two-star. "Even if I drew this myself," the Cobalt Dragon admitted, "the result would only be marginally better than yours."

There’s only one explanation, the Cobalt Dragon mused, watching Zane with new eyes. The boy was born for this. Even an ancient being like him couldn't help but be impressed by such raw genius. If the so-called experts in The Heartlands witnessed this, they'd probably faint on the spot.

The dragon, processing the boy's incredible feat, decided to press on with the lesson. "Normally, runes like these are painted on an array flag and activated with a control plate. The array masters in the Eastern Territories power them with Crystal Cubes. Here in The Heartlands, we use Aether crystals."

Zane nodded, absorbing the information. He carefully placed the finished paper against a wall and channeled a small amount of his Vitality into it.

[SFX: MAGICAL ACTIVATION]

[AMBIENT: SOFT MYSTICAL HUM]

The moment his energy touched it, the texture came alive. 

[MUSIC: GENTLE ETHEREAL CHIMES]

A soft green light radiated from the symbol, washing over Zane in a warm wave. He felt an instant sense of refreshment, like waking from a deep, perfect sleep. The knots in his shoulders loosened, and the day's exhaustion simply melted away.

Damn, this thing really works, he thought, a grin tugging at his lips. I wonder how much I could get for one of these?

He'd arrived in The Heartlands thinking his one hundred thousand Crystal Cubes made him rich, but he'd quickly learned that was pocket change here. 

He made a mental note to ask about the going rate for divine textures tomorrow. Maybe this was his ticket.
