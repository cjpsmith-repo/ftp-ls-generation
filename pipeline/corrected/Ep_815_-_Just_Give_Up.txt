Ep 815 - Just Give Up

Word count: 1094

[AMBIENT: ROYAL TRAINING GROUND]

As a princess of the Wind Divine Kingdom, Fiona rarely got the chance to truly let loose. All the royal families in the seven kingdoms had their own secret training grounds, some even built with techniques from the upper world, but it was nothing like a real fight.

Because she’d never competed in public, everyone judged her strength by her title alone. No one knew what cultivation method she used or which Laws she had mastered. They all knew she was strong, of course. They just never imagined she was this strong. Facing her, Zane was completely out of his league.

[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

Even holding back, using only seventy percent of her power, the strike was devastating. The bracelet on her wrist hummed before vibrating violently, releasing a massive wave of force.

[SFX: BONE CRUSHING IMPACT]

The impact sounded like a watermelon cracking open.

[SFX: BODY SLAM]

Zane’s body was blasted backward, slamming straight into the enchanted barrier.

[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER CRACKLING]

Luckily, the barrier had been erected by a powerful warrior from the Soul Sea Realm. It twisted and buckled under the force of the collision, but a flash in the warrior's eyes was all it took to snap it back into place.

A hit like that would have knocked any other fighter out cold, but Zane’s body was a spiritual weapon. As the immense force surged through him, his body automatically began converting it into Primordial Energy to heal the damage. The problem was, his refinement wasn't complete. Against a powerhouse like Fiona, he could only convert about seventy percent of the impact; the rest was just raw injury.

Still, he withstood it. The damage was minor, and in his current state, it only took a few seconds to recover.

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ARENA]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Fiona’s white and blue gown swayed as she looked down at him, her heart tight with worry. Even though she’d held back, she was afraid the blow had been too much. But her concern vanished in the blink of an eye, replaced by a relieved smile as she watched Zane push himself up from the shimmering floor of the barrier.

Zane sighed inwardly. This girl was a total saint.

Lavender’s voice teased in his mind. Hmm? Falling for her already? 

Zane said nothing. He flew back up and looked at Fiona.

“Done!” Fiona said.

Zane blinked. “Done?”

Fiona tilted her head, genuinely puzzled. “Well — isn't there already a winner?”

[AMBIENT: CROWD REACTION]

[MUSIC: LIGHT COMIC STRINGS]

A beat of silence. Then the warriors from the Star Field, York, and Harlen kingdoms all burst out laughing. The Wind Divine Kingdom warriors stayed quiet — she was their princess, their goddess; they weren't about to laugh at her. The Soul Sea masters smiled too. Even they weren't immune.

War King Wind closed his eyes. He had no one to blame but himself. The moment he'd recognized Fiona's talent, he'd sheltered her completely, never letting her go out alone, never letting the world roughen her edges. She'd poured everything into cultivation and almost nothing into living. Her strength was terrifying. Her understanding of the world was that of a child of seven or eight.

“We don't have a winner yet,” Zane said.

Fiona's expression shifted. She thought about it carefully. “But… you clearly lost just now.”

A trace of slyness crossed Zane's face. He matched her tone exactly. “But… I haven't admitted defeat.”

Fiona blinked. Then her eyes lit up. “Oh! So I have to make you admit defeat. I understand now!”

She wasn't slow — she just hadn't encountered enough of the world to know these things without being told. Now that she understood the rule, she had exactly one goal: to make him say it.

[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE THEME]

Her white and blue dress fluttered as she leaned forward and floated toward him, light as a fairy.

The bracelet trembled. Time Law spread outward from her wrist.

Zane felt it immediately and reached for a node — he'd already identified one not far away and began to connect to it through Spatial Law. But the Time Law hit him before he could complete the step. 

The teleport froze halfway. And then his thoughts slowed. 

Everything slowed. 

He could see her approaching, her speed multiplied many times over from his perspective, but his body wouldn't respond. His mind was moving through deep water.

There was nothing he could do.

Fiona approached. Her brow furrowed slightly. She didn't like this. But she was representing the Wind Divine Kingdom, representing the imperial family. She couldn't afford to lose. With no better option, she pushed her strength higher.

Ninety percent. No more than that.

Her lily-white hand came down in what looked, from the outside, like the gentlest of pats on his forehead.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

There was a sickening thud as Zane’s body crashed into the enchanted barrier. He had been suspended in the air by Fiona's Time Law, falling with impossible slowness, but the moment she released it, his speed increased dramatically, and he smashed hard into the barrier below.

“Will you give up this time?” Fiona asked, frowning deeply.

“No, I won’t.”

[MUSIC: DETERMINED HEROIC THEME]

Wiping a trickle of blood from the corner of his mouth, he shouted, “I’ll never give up!”

“Then face me again!”

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[SFX: POWERFUL STRIKE]

In a flash, Fiona appeared right in front of him. The bracelet on her wrist trembled as her pale hand seemed to lightly brush past his chest, but the impact sent him flying toward the top of the enchanted barrier.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

This time, she’d used one hundred percent of her strength.

“Do you give up?” she asked again, a desperate edge to her voice. It was obvious that hurting him was making her incredibly uncomfortable.

[SFX: BRUTAL IMPACT]

[SFX: BLOOD SPLATTER]

Another thud. This time, Zane was sent flying to the left, spitting up more blood.

“Do you give up?” Fiona asked again, her voice pleading.

“No,” he replied flatly, looking up at her.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

She’d never encountered a fighter like this. His stubbornness was making her genuinely anxious. He was clearly no match for her, but he endured through sheer willpower. After a moment, she finally broke. “I’m begging you, just give up!”

She didn’t want to kill Zane, but he refused to surrender, no matter how hard she hit him. For any other warrior, the solution would be simple: just kill him.

But for Fiona, it was an impossible choice.
