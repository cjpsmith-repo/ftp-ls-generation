Ep 1041 - Speculation

Word count: 1077

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

The assembled lords and elders were all sharp. They could piece together the whole story from a single clue, connecting dots most people didn’t even know existed.

Zane had shouted for Yolande, and his voice had boomed across the entire universe. It didn’t take a genius to connect the dots.

Across the whole universe, only a Roaring Token could pull something like that off. Nothing else even came close.

And then it clicked. They all remembered the rumors—some mysterious young man had used over ten thousand Roaring Tokens at once. Could Zane be that guy?

The thought hit them like a physical blow, and they all stared at him, trying to wrap their heads around the possibility.

Zane, however, didn’t seem fazed in the slightest, completely unbothered by the sideways glances and hushed whispers. His calm only confirmed their suspicions. Their throats went dry. They stared at him, absolutely floored.

This guy had Roaring Tokens? And not just a few, but a ton of them?

The atmosphere in the massive hall grew thick with tension.

But the more they thought about it, the more the doubt crept in. One by one, they started to second-guess themselves.

It just seemed impossible.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

How could a warrior from the Lower World possibly get his hands on that many Roaring Tokens? They couldn’t even begin to imagine what he’d had to do. What kind of extreme measures had he taken? What vital part of himself had he traded away for them?

What Zane didn’t know was that ever since he’d first used a Roaring Token to broadcast his voice, the rumors about his identity had spun completely out of control. People had been trying to unmask him for ages, and they weren’t giving up now.

First, there was that mysterious singer whose voice had triggered beast attacks across the universe—they still hadn’t figured out who she was. Now there was this mysterious young man, just as elusive. The only real difference was that he hadn’t actually hurt anyone. At least he was less destructive.

But proving any of it was another story. The universe was vast. Trying to pinpoint one guy using Roaring Tokens across a hundred thousand supreme worlds? It was damn near impossible. As long as he wanted to stay hidden, they’d never know for sure.

The lord of the Psychean Realm cleared his throat and turned to Yolande. “When you say he was ‘shouting so loudly’... what exactly do you mean?”

Yolande’s eyes flickered with amusement. “Oh, that. When we were in the Lower World, I gave him a Message Rune. He’s been pestering me every day since he ascended — begging me to come collect him. Apparently, the mine was miserable.”

Every elder in the hall rolled their eyes in unison. The story was obvious nonsense — a lower-world talisman couldn’t reach across thousands of worlds. But nobody dared say so out loud.

Zane said nothing. Internally, he was furious — she’d just waved his Roaring Tokens in front of an entire hall of powerful figures. But arguing would only make it worse. He steadied himself. No one actually believed it. Suspicion without proof, he could live with.

The lord of the Psychean Realm and the Saint Lord of the Soulbound Sect thought it was absurd. They figured even a Supreme Lord couldn’t get their hands on that many Roaring Tokens, so how could some kid from the Lower World?

[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]

With three licenses up for grabs and Fenton having already claimed one, the last two were still on the table.

The Saint Lord turned to Yolande. “Palace Lord — Zane ascended through the Soulbound Sect. Now that the Mist Palace is taking him, shouldn’t we receive some... consideration?”

“No.” Yolande didn’t even let him finish.

The Saint Lord nearly choked. He gritted his teeth and tried again. “I’m not asking for much. I simply have a disciple in the Myriad Spirit House. Could he be granted entry to the Thirteen Palaces?”

Yolande laughed. “Just say it plainly. Why dress it up with compensation first and then slip someone in through the back? I’ll send two or three people into the Thirteen Palaces — that’s fine.”

The Saint Lord smiled awkwardly. She’d seen through the whole maneuver. Still — Fenton’s spot in the Thirteen Palaces was now guaranteed. Zane’s arrival at the Soulbound Sect had not been without its benefits.

The World Lord’s eyes lit up at the mention of two or three spots. He was about to speak when Yolande held up three fingers. “Three total. Since Zane ascended through the Soulbound Sect, I’ll give you three quotas. How you distribute them is your business.”

Even though the Soulbound Sect already had a license, their Saint Lord argued they’d earned another one thanks to Zane. He felt it was only fair. The lord of the psychean realm shot that down immediately, arguing that the Soulbound Sect just got lucky. It was completely unfair for one sacred place to snag two licenses.

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

[SFX: HEATED VOICES]

After Yolande left with Zane, a full-blown argument broke out between the two sacred places over the remaining spots. It got so heated that another tenth-grade sacred place from the Psychean Realm jumped in, its Saint Lord insisting the only fair thing was to split the three licenses evenly—one for each of them.

[AMBIENT: WIND AT HIGH ALTITUDE]

[MUSIC: MAJESTIC ORCHESTRAL]

A carriage floated high above the Soulbound Sect.

The purple carriage was a masterpiece. Vines of strange, shimmering flora formed from pure energy snaked around its frame.

But the most incredible part was what was pulling it: two coursers that were actual legendary beasts.

Legendary beasts were notoriously proud and arrogant. They might form a pact with a warrior, becoming a partner, but they could never be truly enslaved or tamed.

This “Heavenly Phoenix Horse,” however, was one of the rare exceptions—a legendary beast that could be tamed, though it took an insane amount of time and effort. Because of that, owning a Heavenly Phoenix Horse wasn’t just about having a great ride; it was the ultimate status symbol.

[AMBIENT: REVERENT CROWD MURMURS]

Everyone watched in reverent silence as Yolande led Zane into the carriage.

From the outside, it looked no different than any other, but when the doors swung open, they revealed a different world.

[SFX: MAGICAL DOOR OPENING]

[AMBIENT: GRAND PALACE HALL]

The inside was as vast and magnificent as a palace.

“So, you gonna tell me?” Yolande asked, a playful smile on her face. “Where did you get so many Roaring Tokens?”

“Uh…” Zane trailed off, not even sure how to begin to explain.
