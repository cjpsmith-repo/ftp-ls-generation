Ep 888 - Every Mountain in the World

Word count: 1089

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

An eerie silence settled over the world, and even the Soul Sea masters held their tongues.

Finally, a sharp voice cut through the stillness. It was the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom. “Zane, are you saying that every last mountain in the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms has been wiped out?” 

“It’s not just the Union,” Zane answered honestly. “The mountains in the Heartlands and the other regions were hit, too. Almost every mountain in the entire world of chaos is gone.”

Most of the masters had already pieced that much together. The moment Zane said he’d transferred the power into the mountains, they knew. If a powerhouse like War King York attacked him, the redirected energy would be enough to flatten an entire mountain range.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The sheer force radiating from that black eye had been terrifying, the power contained in its dark light beyond imagination. If Zane had truly channeled all of it into the earth, just how many mountains would it take?

The Divine Kingdom Continent was covered in countless peaks and ranges. No one had ever kept an accurate count, but a rough estimate put the number at several hundred thousand.

But would even that be enough to absorb the force of that blast? How many would it take? A thousand? Ten thousand? A hundred thousand?

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

The real number was closer to five million.

Every mountain in the world was gone, save for a few stray peaks in the Eastern Territories.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The masters fell silent again before the shock truly hit them. They were only just beginning to grasp the sheer scale of the impact this would have on the entire world. 

What, exactly, was the limit of Zane’s power? He could channel energy across the whole world of chaos. Did that mean he was the world?

Someone had finally landed on the answer, though they couldn’t have possibly guessed the truth: that Zane had merged with the World’s Will itself. In a way, he was the world of chaos now.

“What in the hell was that black eye?”War King Wind muttered, sighing when no answer came to him. A single attack had wiped out nearly every mountain in existence. What kind of being was capable of that?

Zane shook his head. “I don’t know.” Even the Cobalt Dragon had no answer — so neither did he.

The White-Haired Priest spoke carefully. “Whatever it is, to wield that kind of power so casually — it would have to be among the most powerful beings in existence.”

In his mind, the Cobalt Dragon pushed back. Even the strongest warriors of the upper world couldn’t do this so effortlessly. I cannot determine what that eye was.

More importantly, the eye’s owner must have completely underestimated Zane — assumed he was too insignificant to survive the attack. That was why it had simply left after firing. One casual strike, already forgotten.

That thought settled uneasily in Zane’s chest. He had no known enemies in the upper world. 

Something about the lotus blooming must have drawn that eye to him — or there was another reason entirely. He didn’t know. But he filed it away as a warning: he would have to be far more careful going forward.

Zane checked in with himself. The breakthrough was complete. The Vitality in his core had solidified — thick and viscous, his core able to hold more than before, every drop more concentrated. He was stronger. Meaningfully stronger.

His next goal was already set: the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. He had promised the Cobalt Dragon he would retrieve the Ancestral Dragon Egg, and he intended to keep that promise. But first, there were ores to deal with.

He approached the pyramid — and immediately felt something wrong. The Soul Sea masters were looking at him differently. Not the guarded suspicion he’d grown used to, not the hostility York had never bothered to hide. Something warmer. Almost familial.

They had done the math. Zane was their key to the ascending passage. And a warrior who had opened the Greater Lotus Flower — his future in the upper world was beyond imagining. Making an enemy of him now would be foolish. Making a friend of him might be the most important thing any of them ever did.

Being stared at by dozens of ancient masters with that particular look in their eyes, Zane felt a chill run down his spine. He suspected they’d all taken the wrong medicine.

One of them finally broke the silence. “Are you really just going to give those treasures away?”

The Harlen Emperor smiled. “Exactly. That stuff is far too valuable to just hand out. But there are a few things we need. Zane, you could set a price and sell them to us instead.”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane was stunned. Something felt off. Why were these masters, who had been so hostile just moments ago, suddenly being so accommodating? Were they planning something?

Nearby, the White-Haired Priest and the Holy Maiden watched him in silence. News of this would definitely have to be sent through the Reverse Spirit Channel, but now wasn’t the time. They would wait until he was finished with the ores.

Zane turned their behavior over in his mind, but couldn’t make sense of it. He mentally checked in with the Cobalt Dragon, but the dragon was just as confused. The dragon didn’t know about the ascending passage — so it had no way of understanding why these warriors had suddenly shifted.

Since he wasn’t getting anywhere, Zane decided to push it aside for now. He wasn’t a blacksmith. Most of what he’d pulled from those upper floors had limited use for him directly — he’d keep whatever the Cobalt Dragon identified as useful for high-level divine runes, and let the Emperor handle the rest.

[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]

He reached into his Storage Ring and began pulling out the ores one by one.

The Soul Sea masters watched eagerly, their curiosity piqued. There weren’t many ores from the forty-fifth to the forty-seventh layers, but each one was utterly unique.

[SFX: LIQUID SPLASH]

[AMBIENT: CURIOUS MURMURS]

The first crystal Zane pulled from his Storage Ring flowed like water, slipping right through his fingers and splattering onto the ground. A moment later, the blue liquid instantly reformed into a small, round ball that bounced playfully at his feet.

Zane smiled. “So, how much do you think this Water Crystal is worth?”
