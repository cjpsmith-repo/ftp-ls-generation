Ep 1086 - Absorbing the Flame

Word count: 1129

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMURS]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

“He actually jumped? Into the flame pool?”

“Anyone who touches the Immortal Profound Flame gets burned to a crisp! Does that kid have a death wish?”

Zane had been standing at the edge of the pool for so long that all the leaders assumed he’d eventually lose his nerve and back off. So when he just went for it, leaping straight into the inferno, they could hardly believe their eyes. The Immortal Profound Flame was a fire that never went out, a constant, searing glow. You stayed far, far away from it if you valued your life. Only a fool would press on. These were men who had seen just about everything, with power that rivaled World Lords, but this completely caught them off guard.

“Sir, shouldn’t we go get him?” one of them asked the Lord of the Sunset World, his voice tight with alarm.

The World Lord paused, considering. It would be a real shame for a warrior as incredible as Zane to die in the Enchanted Barrier Tower. Layla was already a shoo-in for the Silverwood Pavilion, but Zane… he was special. He was all but guaranteed a spot in the Gossamer Pavilion. No, the Lord of the Sunset World wasn’t about to let that kind of talent just burn away.

He shot to his feet, ready to dive in after him. A World Lord could cross tens of thousands of miles in the blink of an eye.

[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

But a gentle voice stopped him cold. “Sir, please wait.”

Hearing Yolande, the Lord of the Sunset World turned, a flicker of surprise on his face. “Oh? What is it?”

“There’s no need to pull him out,” she said, her voice shaking just a little before finding its resolve. “He… he won’t die.”

At first, her heart had seized right along with everyone else’s. The leaders’ panic was logical; she knew exactly what should happen to anyone who stepped into that pool. But the more she thought about it, the more she realized that Zane wasn’t the type to do something so reckless. If he chose to jump, he must have a plan. He’d proven time and again that he played by his own rules. There was no need to worry.

The Lord of the Sunset World seemed to agree. He took a step back, his eyes fixed on the spot where the young man had vanished. He, too, wanted to see if Zane could pull this off. As if on cue, the leaders’ frantic worrying began to fade, replaced by stunned silence at what they saw next.

[AMBIENT: ROARING FLAMES]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Deep within the inferno, the Immortal Profound Flame wasn’t burning Zane at all. Instead, it was being drawn into him, a swirling vortex of fire. He knew he had to absorb it all, just as Lavender had warned. She’d told him that if he wasn’t careful, he’d walk out carrying a ball of the stuff, and he figured the others wouldn’t exactly be thrilled about that.

[SFX: DRAGON BURP]

After the Ancestral Dragon swallowed the last wisp of the flame, it let out a loud, satisfied burp. Zane held out his hand, and with a little snort, the dragon floated over obediently. He tucked it back into his bestiary bag, where it thrashed around in protest for a moment before finally settling down.

[AMBIENT: COOLING ENERGY]

With the fire gone, the searing red glow of Zane’s skin finally began to cool. He could feel the change immediately. His body had been tempered to a whole new level, though he wasn’t sure what rank he’d reached. A quick glance at his chest showed no new divine textures, but he’d have to figure that out later. There was no doubt, though—his strength had absolutely skyrocketed.

[AMBIENT: PALACE SQUARE]

[MUSIC: AWE-STRUCK STRINGS]

Outside the tower, one of the palace masters spoke in a daze. “He went over. He actually crossed it.”

“He’s broken thirty thousand miles. Thirty thousand, five hundred—”

The announcement hung in the air. Then silence fell — complete, total silence — over the palace masters, the Lord of the Sunset World, and every warrior in the square.

Layla said nothing. Her master had told her she would be the main character of this era, the one this age was made for. But standing here now, watching the display, she felt that certainty quietly come apart.

She reached up and unclipped the small ornament above her right ear — a blue butterfly hair clip, a Divine artifact she’d carried for years. She turned it over in her palm and began to rub it slowly between her fingers.

[SFX: CRACKING]

As she rubbed, hairline cracks spread across the wings. The tiny divine patterns etched into the surface began to fracture one by one. The butterfly split down the middle, then kept breaking, the pieces growing smaller and smaller until they crumbled to powder in her hand. She had pushed her own energy into it deliberately.

She let the dust fall from her fingers. Then she raised her head, and her eyes were clear and fierce.

“I’ve lost this round,” she said quietly. “But the Passing the Torch trial isn’t over. In the next segment — I won’t lose to you.”

[MUSIC: DETERMINED STRINGS]

While the square was still silent, Zane kept going.

Thirty-one thousand miles.

Thirty-two thousand.

Thirty-three thousand.

No one in the square tried to predict where he would stop anymore. Every limit they’d set had already been shattered. They just watched.

He pushed all the way to thirty-six thousand miles before he finally came to a halt.

[SFX: CRACKLING ELECTRICITY]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS LOW DRONE]

Blocking his path was a waterfall of thunder and lightning — vast columns of crackling energy pouring downward from somewhere above, wrapped in the power of the Water Law. The light it threw off was blinding, and the force it radiated was more intense than the flame pool had been. Another Supreme Lord’s work, sealed here for an eternity.

Zane studied it carefully. He’d dared the flame pool because he had absolute confidence in his body’s resistance to fire. He had no such confidence here. Lightning was a different matter entirely — he had no reliable defense against it, no assurance he could walk through and come out the other side. And unlike the flame pool, there was nothing to gain by forcing it.

He knew when to stop.

To test the tempering, he began to channel the power of his Dragon Scales.

[SFX: POWER SURGE]

[MUSIC: EPIC CRESCENDO]

A wave of energy surged through him.

One thousand Dragon Scales.

Three thousand.

Five thousand.

Ten thousand.

Fifteen thousand.

He activated every last one of them, and his body took the strain without so much as a tremor. All he felt was raw power thrumming under his skin. He clenched his fist, amazed as the simple movement unleashed enough force to visibly warp the space around his knuckles.

Well, he thought. Looks like I’m going to need to find more Celestial Essence.
