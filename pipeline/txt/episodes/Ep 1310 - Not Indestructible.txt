Ep 1310 - Not Indestructible

Word count: 1079

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ETHEREAL STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL VOID RESONANCE]
The moment Janet laid eyes on the Saint-Killing Spear, a cold dread washed over her.
[SFX: DEEP RESONANT HUM]
The Lofty Wood inside her was screaming a warning. There was something deeply, fundamentally wrong with that weapon.
[SFX: VIBRATING HARMONIC PULSE]
It was forged from two materials, one black and one white, twisted together in a spiral that seemed to vibrate in a terrible harmony with the Lofty Wood in her own body. 
A flash of horrified understanding hit her. She suddenly knew why. The black and white shafts weren’t just metal—they were made from two *other* kinds of divine wood.
The legends spoke of four, all existing beyond the heavens. There was the Ray Wood in the east, the Sunrest Wood in the west, the Aetherwood in the south, and her own Lofty Wood from the north. Of the four, the Ray Wood had never been seen in the universe, and the Lofty Wood had only appeared when Janet herself was born. But the other two—the Aetherwood and the Sunrest Wood—had been here since the beginning.
The Sunrest Tree, black as the void, was the sun’s resting place—the very key used to ignite it and bring light to creation. The Aetherwood, pure white, grew with impossible speed, its invisible roots spreading to support the hundred thousand supreme worlds and a million great worlds, connecting everything without ever being seen.
[MUSIC: TENSE PULSATING DRUMS]
And the Saint-Killing Spear had been forged from them both.
Feeling the power of the two divine woods resonating with her own, Janet knew she had to warn Zane. 
But he was already moving. 
He knew better than to try and block that spear’s shadow head-on, so he twisted his body and shot to the side.
“Eight Smoky Melodies!”
[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSH]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
The phantom spear shot straight for him. On pure instinct, Zane activated the technique, a surge of power allowing him to dodge just in time. 
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
The spear flew past his body and slammed into the distant city wall.
[SFX: DEEP GRINDING RUMBLE]
[SFX: STONE CRACKING]
A deep, grinding rumble echoed out. Something was wrong. The wall, said to be indestructible, was being torn apart. 
And it should have been. The city walls carried Celestial Law itself woven into their stone—a protection so absolute that not even a Soul Sea Realm warrior or a World Lord could so much as chip a single brick from them. 
Only a True God who’d inherited a piece of the Heavens themselves could hope to touch what Celestial Law protected.
[SFX: DRILLING THROUGH STONE]
The wild, violent spin of the spear’s shadow drilled a small, clean hole right through it.
Zane stared, his eyes wide. “What kind of power is this?”
Even though the spear had only carved a fist-sized hole, the implications were enough to shock everyone watching. 
[SFX: SPEAR SHADOW WHISTLING]
Because it wasn’t just the wall the phantom spears threatened. One stray shadow, wild and unaimed, tore straight through the shimmering membrane that was supposed to make the city an absolute safe zone—no fighter was ever meant to bring violence past that boundary.
[SFX: WET IMPACT]
It punched clean through the chest of a cultivator pressed up against the wall, someone who’d been leaning in close, trying to catch the details of Zane’s strange footwork. He never even saw it coming.
Not every shadow found a target, though — the ones that sailed past the wall and out over open ground simply drilled into the earth and kept going, swallowed up without a trace. A low, constant rumble rolled out from the ground the whole time, like something enormous chewing through rock far below.
For a moment, he just stood there. The hole through his chest didn’t seem to register.
“Hey... you,” the man beside him said, and pointed.
Only then did he look down and see the wound. He crumpled, his body dissolving into scattered light, his soul already streaking back toward the city to be revived.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURING]
The realization spread through the crowd like ice water. The city wasn’t safe after all—whatever this spear was, it was strong enough to answer even Celestial Law.
“Retreat, retreat!” someone shouted.
“Let’s watch from further back,” another voice called.
“I was standing right next to him,” one warrior muttered, pale. “If that thing hadn’t lost its edge going through the barrier, that would’ve been me.”
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS RETREATING]
The other warriors, not wanting to miss the battle but wanting to live, found safer corners to watch from. The top of the city wall was suddenly much less crowded.
Janet, Hailey, Jeffrey, Coleman, and Ansel didn’t budge. They might not have been on Zane and Slade’s level, but they could handle the pressure waves from the fight. The spear’s shadow had lost some of its speed after piercing the light barrier, giving them just enough time to dodge if they had to. 
Slade’s followers didn’t leave either—a mix of the bold and the ones who just wanted to show off. They all stood their ground on the wall.
[SFX: RAPID SPEAR WHOOSHES]
The spear’s shadow was impossibly fast. Zane was forced into a breakneck dance, constantly dodging as Slade pressed his attack. In less than two minutes, Zane had evaded over a hundred of the deadly phantoms. 
“Dodging fast, aren’t you?” Slade called out, grinning. “Let’s see how long you can keep it up!”
[SFX: SPEAR THRUSTING RAPIDLY]
This was the Shadowy Spin—the Saint-Killing Spear’s most basic technique. It barely cost Slade anything to use, but there was nothing basic about what it could do.
[SFX: RAPID FOOTWORK]
He was relying on nothing but his own speed and the Eight Smoky Melodies, and he was starting to get tired.
*I can’t keep this up,* Zane thought, his lungs burning. *Sooner or later, one of these is going to hit me.* He was confident in his body’s resilience, but he knew a direct hit from that spear would be catastrophic.
Suddenly, as another spear shadow flashed past, Zane’s bow appeared in his hands.
[SFX: ARROWS WHOOSHING]
The instant the longbow materialized, three streaks of black light shot from it.
“Grade-three Divine Weapons?” Slade’s face twisted in contempt. “How childish.” 
[SFX: SPEAR SPINNING]
He spun his long spear twice like a windmill.
[SFX: METAL CLASHING]
With three sharp, metallic rings, the Saint-Killing Spear batted the arrows out of the air like they were nothing.
