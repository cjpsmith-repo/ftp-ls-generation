Ep 740 - Say Something

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Maddox was certain he knew where Lord Wallace came from.
Beyond the Chaotic Ocean of Stars lay a continent so vast it made The Heartlands look like a glorified island. That land was once home to a union of seven divine kingdoms, but a long-ago war with the Dragon-blooded had whittled their number down to four. Lord Wallace was from one of them—the York Divine Kingdom. It was the same kingdom as the Holy Maiden they’d encountered in the Celestial Tomb of Vesper.
After that encounter, Elder Roland, a warrior from York who had reached the Life-and-Death Realm, had kept his word. He’d given Simon the internal version of the Scripture of Five Poisons, and Simon had spent the better part of the last two years engrossed in its study.
As for the Holy Maiden, she had sworn an oath in that tomb to lead her kingdom’s forces and wipe The Heartlands off the map. So far, she hadn’t shown her face.
It was all because crossing the Chaotic Ocean of Stars was a monumental undertaking.
[SFX: THUNDER AND STORM]
[AMBIENT: RAGING OCEAN WAVES]
A furious Star Storm had been raging across it for years, effectively cutting off The Heartlands from the four kingdoms. Lord Wallace claimed the storm was still active but weakening with each passing year. Once it subsided enough, the Holy Maiden would finally make her move.
This meant that Maddox and the York Divine Kingdom were hardly allies. Given his nature, Maddox would never be content as anyone’s subordinate.
[MUSIC: DARK AMBITION THEME]
His ambitions stretched far beyond The Heartlands; in truth, he saw the four divine kingdoms as little more than stepping stones on his path to something greater. For now, though, he would continue to work with them, using them for his own ends. He’d already gained a great deal from their arrangement, and he wasn't about to turn away a useful tool.
What gnawed at him more than anything was Zane. He was convinced the young man was hiding something monumental — something in his body that went beyond normal talent. And Zane kept slipping away.
Then the Soul Harvesting Ritual had collapsed because Zane had taken Cora's maidenhood - something Maddox had never anticipated. He had no idea when the next Violet Core Physique would surface. It could be decades. It could be longer.
He had to move faster. If Wallace's warriors arrived before he'd claimed Zane's secret for himself, the opportunity would be gone. He made a mental note: Simon needed to come out of seclusion.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]
The last time Maddox had him cornered in The Dreaming Marshes, Zane had not only escaped but had taken Cora with him. In the aftermath, Maddox and Simon joined forces to crush the Ethereal Spirit Sect. They then sent a message to the Cloud Hall, offering to trade the sect’s leader, Xander, for Cora. Maddox had assumed it would be an easy deal. After all, what was one woman compared to the head of an entire sect?
[AMBIENT: MELODY CITY]
Back in Melody City, Finn turned to Zane. "There was no array here. The whole thing was bait — a trap to draw Spirit-Tribulation warriors into the Spirit Container." He looked at Zane carefully. "We should report back."
"Wait," Zane said.
He had already taken out the signaling token — the white one Tia had given him during the Cloud Road trial, the one that let them communicate directly within a hundred miles. He poured a thread of Vitality into it.
[SFX: VITALITY SURGE]
Nothing.
He tried again. Nothing. She wasn't within range.
"Let's go," he said. "We'll take another mission on the way."
The others exchanged glances but followed without question.
In the air, Zane kept his hand on the token. Every few minutes he tried again. The token stayed silent. He didn't stop.
[SFX: TOKEN TREMOR]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Then, halfway back, he felt it — a faint tremor in the token. A fluctuation. Barely there, but unmistakable.
He slowed. "Tia," he said quietly into it. "Is that you?"
Silence.
She had heard him. He was certain of it. She was within range — somewhere — and she was saying nothing.
“Is there a city anywhere around here?” Zane asked, his voice cutting through the quiet.
Finn approached, his expression uneasy. “To the southeast, you’ll find Wavecrest City, but that’s a major stronghold for the Corrupted Divine Kingdom. Their forces are concentrated there. The city is a natural fortress—easy to defend, a nightmare to attack. The Guild has no plans to move on it anytime soon.”
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The territories held by the Guild and the Corrupted Divine Kingdom were a tangled mess, but Wavecrest City was one of their most critical outposts, garrisoned by their strongest fighters. Rumor had it that Darius himself, the Corrupted Divine Kingdom’s military advisor, was stationed there.
At Finn’s words, Zane’s eyes flashed with sudden, sharp understanding. “She’s there.”
[SFX: WHOOSH]
Before anyone could react, he shot into the air like a bolt of light, heading straight for Wavecrest City.
“Boss, what’s going on?” Lewis asked, shrugging in confusion.
[SFX: MULTIPLE WHOOSHES]
“Follow him!” Finn snapped, taking off with Patrick right behind him. At this point, their faith in Zane was absolute.
[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
As Zane tore through the sky, he frantically tried to reach Tia with the signaling token, but all he got was silence.
“Hello?”
“Tia!”
“Tia, answer me!”
“Will you just say something?”
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
No matter what he said, the token remained dead. The silence only stoked his growing panic. There was no telling what kind of madness she might throw herself into in a blind rage. So what if she was at the Life-and-Death Realm? Maddox had the backing of the divine kingdoms, an enemy the Cloud Hall couldn't possibly hope to match. Against powers like that, even a warrior of her caliber was nothing.
[SFX: DISTANT CITY SOUNDS]
[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL BUILD]
After flying for nearly seventy miles, the silhouette of Wavecrest City finally cut across the horizon.
As he stared at it, a cold, blazing fury ignited in Zane’s eyes.
