[AMBIENT: RUGGED COUNTRYSIDE]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The Cloud Hall disciple quickly discovered that redemption would not come easily—or at all.

Lennon shook his head, his expression grim. "Sorry, that's not how it works. We each get one shot. If you fall, you can't get near the cliff again."

He spoke the truth. When the disciple scrambled to his feet and rushed back toward the cliff face, he'd slammed into an invisible, enchanted barrier with bone-jarring force. The impact sent him staggering backward, but desperation drove him forward again. He charged once more, this time smashing his head against the unyielding wall so hard that blood began trickling down his forehead. His fists pounded uselessly against the barrier. Not even the Scythe of the River-Winds from the Formation of the Twelve Signs could pierce through.

The disciple sank to his knees, his shoulders trembling with frustration and shame. His one chance—gone in seconds.

"Everyone gets just one try," Lennon repeated, his voice firm but tinged with sympathy. "You fall, the barrier locks you out permanently. So let's take our time. We'll go one by one."

The lesson hit hard. After witnessing their companion's humiliating failure, the other disciples felt their earlier confidence evaporate. They had been reckless moments before, driven by greed and the conviction that the first person up would claim the best treasures. But now, watching their fellow disciple bleeding and defeated at the barrier's edge, they wondered what cruel trials waited for them on that cliff face.

The mad dash was over. Suddenly, no one felt any urgency at all.

The unspoken plan shifted: let someone else go first, watch their attempt, figure out how hard it was, and come up with a better strategy. But as they exchanged nervous glances, nobody seemed eager to volunteer as the next test subject.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Just then, a man in a black cloak shot out of the jungle, moving toward the cliff like a wisp of dark smoke.

"Who is that?"

[SFX: WEAPON DRAW]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Lennon's face hardened the moment he saw him. He whipped out a Green Caltrop Pike, aimed it at the man, and lunged.

[SFX: SWIFT MOVEMENT]

Lennon was fast, but to everyone's astonishment, he missed. The figure in black was already leaping toward the cliff. Just as Lennon attacked, the man seemed to kick off the air itself, abruptly changing his direction.

[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Everyone knew flying was forbidden on the Cloud Road. They figured the man in black knew it too, since he'd jumped to change course instead of just taking flight. It was a seriously impressive skill.

The strange maneuver made it impossible for Lennon to land his attack.

[SFX: WEAPONS DRAWN]

Realizing the threat, the other disciples drew their weapons. But the figure in black kept leaping in unpredictable directions. As they attacked, he tucked and rolled to dodge, continuing his break for the enchanted barrier at the cliff face.

Once he was through it, he'd be safe. The barrier would shield him from any outside attacks.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FLUTE]

Zane gasped. Hidden in the shadows, his eyes widened in awe, fixed on the mysterious man. That was an amazing skill. He didn't look like a disciple from Cloud Hall; he had to be from one of the other sects.

He watched the cloaked figure disappear through the barrier. The disciples from Cloud Hall stood frozen on their platforms, their formation scattered, their opportunity lost.

"Did you see that?" one of them muttered, his voice choked with frustration.

Lennon Anderson's jaw clenched. "That technique... I've never seen anything like it. Who the hell was that guy?"

[AMBIENT: INDOOR HALL]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Meanwhile, far from the cliff, the sect leaders observed through their Scrying Slates, their attention fixed on the mysterious cloaked figure's display of skill. Sir Kenneth leaned forward, his fingers drumming against the armrest of his chair as his eyes narrowed with interest. "Remarkable agility," he murmured, almost to himself. "That disciple moved through the wind gusts as if they were nothing more than a gentle breeze."

Toby shifted uncomfortably in his seat, unable to tear his gaze from the Scrying Slate. "It looks like Lewis, from the Providence Sect.  Look at that pure skill. No formation, no external aid—just perfect control."

Kenneth glanced at him with a knowing smile. "You sound jealous, old friend."

Toby's face flushed slightly, but he straightened his shoulders and cleared his throat. "There's no need for jealousy," he said, his voice regaining its usual confidence. "Kenneth's right about the skill being impressive, but let's not forget—the Head of our Cloud Hall obtained the silver box from that very cliff."

[SFX: SURPRISED GASP]

"Wait, what are you talking about? Our leader is the one who got the silver box?" one of the other sect leaders asked, shocked. It was the first any of them had heard of it.

Toby nodded. "Yes, but no one's managed to pull it off since. And that's not even mentioning the gold and black boxes. They've been sitting up there for years, completely untouched."

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The leaders watched the boxes on the cliff through the Scrying Slate, all of them desperate to get their hands on one. With their current power, taking them would be easy. But the Cloud Road wouldn't let them enter a second time. What a damn waste.

"Look! Lewis just made it to the first platform," Toby said slowly. "According to the rules of the cliff, you can only grab one box of each color. If Lewis gets a red one, he'll have to go for a blue one next."

"Lewis can get that red box! I know he can!" The Leader of the Providence Sect was completely confident. Lewis Bailey was his star pupil, the most outstanding talent the Providence Sect had seen in a thousand years.

The platform Lewis was standing on was the same one the first disciple from Cloud Hall had tried—and failed—to reach.
