Ep 1017 - Look Who’s Back

Word count: 1177

[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATION]

Zane had used the Roaring Token twice and heard nothing — only silence where Elara’s voice should have been.

Zane’s brow furrowed. The other possibility was unthinkable: Elara wasn’t in the universe at all. If that was true… well, it would be more than just terrible news.

The universe was impossibly vast—maybe infinite. How could he even begin to find his sister in all that? And if Elara was somehow outside the universe, finding her would be even harder.

[SFX: MAGICAL ACTIVATION]

A flicker of frustration crossed his face. He stretched out his hand and activated the Roaring Token once more.

“Elara Lowell, where are you?”

[SFX: BOOMING VOICE ECHO]

[AMBIENT: COSMIC VASTNESS]

For the second time, Zane’s voice boomed across the entire universe as the token vibrated gently in his palm. Countless races and living beings heard the now-familiar call.

“Damn. Who the hell is Elara Lowell? Must be someone special if this guy is burning through Roaring Tokens to find her without a second thought.”

“Talk about reckless.”

“But it’s a weird way to do it. You don’t just yell a name, right? Shouldn’t he at least say where he is? Who looks for someone like that?”

What they didn’t get was that Zane didn’t have a powerful family or a Supreme Lord backing him up. Given his current strength and status, he had to be careful. He couldn’t risk giving away his name or location. If he revealed too much, he’d just be painting a target on his back.

After his second shout faded, he waited another five minutes. Still nothing. With every second that ticked by, Zane’s patience wore thinner.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

The plan had been so simple. Use the Roaring Token to find his sister. After that, get enough Sovereign Stones to become an independent warrior. Then, find a way to travel to the supreme worlds and finally reunite with Elara.

But he was stuck on step one. Elara wasn’t answering.

Frustrated, Zane reluctantly activated the Roaring Token again. Just like before, he shouted, “Elara Lowell, where are you?”

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHISPER]

The last word had barely left his lips when a voice echoed in his mind. A response.

“Hmph. So what if you’ve mastered The Aetheric Incarnation? I’ll be waiting for you in the Autumn Mountain World. Let’s see if you have the guts to show up.”

[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]

For a split second, Zane’s heart leaped—but the hope soured the instant he recognized the voice. Supreme Lord Armond.

Barely a breath later, another voice crackled through — Magnus. “Hehe. Just wait patiently. Wait for me to take your life.”

Already on edge, Zane’s impatience boiled over into pure fury. He was already terrified his call wasn’t reaching Elara. Now, this interruption just fanned the flames of his anger.

He couldn’t be bothered to provoke either of them. But the two of them were going back and forth on his channel, drowning out any chance Elara had of hearing him—

He activated the token a fourth time.

“Would you two shut up?”

[SFX: UNIVERSE-WIDE SILENCE]

Across the universe, every living being who had just been following the Supreme Lords’ quarrel went completely still. Zane’s first three calls had been calm, searching. This one was pure irritation, raw and unfiltered, blasted across a hundred thousand worlds.

No one said anything for a long moment.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]

In a distant reach of the Upper World, in a vast palace suspended in brilliant clouds, ascending passageways lined the walls — dozens of them, flashing with milky white light in steady succession.

These were not passageways for ordinary warriors to ascend. The woman who had created them sat at the center of the palace, submerged to her shoulders in a pool of emerald liquid.

She was striking — composed, unhurried, her wet hair pinned loosely above her neck. The only imperfection was her back, crosshatched with old scars that the pool’s glow couldn’t quite hide.

Clone after clone stepped out of the ascending passageways — each one identical to the woman in the pool, each one shedding her clothes as she walked, stepping into the light, and dissolving into a red point of light that was absorbed into the woman’s body. Clothes accumulated around the edges of the palace in great piles.

This was the final stage of the Million Clones Technique. A million pieces of her soul, scattered across the Lower World to cultivate independently, now returning home one by one. She was drawing on everything they had learned, everything they had lived, to push herself to the next level.

This was Yolande.

Most people would be lucky to have one or two Roaring Tokens. Yolande, given her special position, had five—an almost unheard-of number. She only used them for the most urgent matters.

And in just a short time, she’d already heard Zane shout three times.

What a waste, Yolande thought, practically speechless. Using Roaring Tokens just to find his sister.

She had recognized his voice the moment it rang out. Two of her returning clones carried memories of him — one from when he was barely past the Adept Realm, one from when he broke through at the Evil Tower. She had sifted through those memories carefully.

His ascension speed was remarkable. That alone made her pause. But what really caught her attention was something else entirely.

Two destinies. In the same person. They contradicted each other at a fundamental level. They could not coexist. And yet somehow, in this one young man, they did.

She had no explanation for it. She filed it away.

“Where did he even get those tokens?” she murmured. Even she only had five.

She’d learned from her clone in Alba Imperia that Zane had a sister named Elara Lowell, who was apparently connected to a Tier-Two Sect in the Lower World.

The girl had been kidnapped and taken to The Heartlands. But how she’d ended up in the Upper World was a mystery; her clone’s memories offered no clues.

Yolande simply scattered her clones like seeds, never controlling their paths. Besides, controlling a million clones at once was impossible anyway.

[SFX: DISTANT ECHOING VOICES]

Then, she heard the two Supreme Lords start using their own Roaring Tokens to curse each other out. It was common knowledge that Armond and Magnus were old rivals. Their bickering had gone on for years, and they had wasted who knows how many Roaring Tokens on it.

They were Supreme Lords; they had plenty of tokens to burn. But Zane wasn’t. How could he be so wasteful?

[AMBIENT: WATER RIPPLES]

[MUSIC: SERENE MEDITATION]

Yolande scanned her surroundings. No sign of the ascending passageways opening again. Assuming her clones were still traveling, she sank her entire body into the rejuvenation pool. The absorption process had left her drained, forcing her to submerge herself completely in the restorative liquid.

Just as the liquid covered her ears, she heard Zane’s angry curse echo through the universe.

[SFX: SUDDEN SPLASH]

A strangled cough burst from her lips. She was so blindsided by his outburst that she swallowed a mouthful of the pool, sending her into a violent coughing fit.

The coughing fit subsided, and Yolande lay still in the pool, staring at the ceiling and trying to make sense of this miraculous young man.
