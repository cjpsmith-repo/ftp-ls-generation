Ep 1182 - The Star of Golden Sand

Word count: 1029

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL TENSION]
Confusion rippled through the Supreme Lords. 
[SFX: WEAPONS CLASH HALT]
Even the Queen of Punishment, Addison, and her opponent, Gunter, paused their battle mid-strike.
Nothing could escape the senses of warriors this powerful, which was exactly why they’d been fighting in the first place. They were sure the miniature Elysian Keep couldn’t possibly get away. But now, it had vanished without a trace. Escaping from under the noses of so many Supreme Lords should have been impossible.
They all analyzed the situation in a split second and came to the same conclusion: the Elysian Keep hadn’t left the Star of Golden Sand. It was just hiding somewhere on it.
Faced with this new problem, the squabbling stopped. They had all come for the Crown of Destiny—that was the only goal. No one was actually interested in sparring.
The Queen of Punishment’s cold gaze swept across the star’s surface again and again, but she couldn’t find a single clue. Her hostile eyes finally landed on the other Supreme Lords. *If it weren’t for these bastards,* she thought, *the Keep never would have gotten away from me.*
[SFX: MULTIPLE HEAVY LANDINGS]
A few more figures dropped from the sky as other Supreme Lords rushed to the scene.
[SFX: SHOCKED EXCLAMATION]
“Magnus!” someone exclaimed.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING STRINGS]
Supreme Lord Magnus was a mid-rank Lord, but his presence was enough to make even the Queen of Punishment frown.
[SFX: GOLDEN LIGHT SPREADING]
Magnus tilted his head back slightly and pressed his palms together. Two rays of golden light poured from his eyes, spreading outward and sweeping methodically across the surface of the Star of Golden Sand.
“It’s been a while,” one of the other Lords observed. “Magnus’s Eyes of Rebirth can see through any illusion. He’ll find it.”
Magnus said nothing. His expression stayed flat as the twin beams of gold combed through every inch of the star. The Eyes of Rebirth drew on inherited Celestial Law — not cultivated, but passed down directly. The supreme laws of Cosmic Order. Everything from the smallest grain of sand to the largest star was governed by them, without exception.
In a few moments, the scan was complete.
Magnus frowned.
“Strange,” he muttered. “It’s clearly still on the star. But I can’t see it.”
[SFX: UNEASY MURMUR]
The gathered Lords stirred. One spoke carefully. “The Eyes of Rebirth can’t find it?”
The thought that followed, no one wanted to say out loud: the Elysian Keep was an object that existed beyond the reach of Cosmic Order itself.
The Queen of Punishment’s expression darkened further. She had sent Cassian and the others ahead, and it had cost her precious time. More Lords had gathered while she was delayed — and now even Magnus couldn’t locate the Keep. She had nothing.
“And Armond!”
As Supreme Lord Armond landed, his eyes immediately locked onto Magnus. The two were a famous pair of rivals, a constant source of amusement across the universe. They’d been challenging and quarreling for countless years but had never actually fought—not once. Plenty of other Lords had asked them why, but they always ignored the question.
Seeing them appear together, everyone on the Star of Golden Sand expected them to start bickering. They were shocked when the two men greeted each other like old friends who hadn’t seen each other in years.
This was why Supreme Lords rarely fought to the death. If one Lord killed another, the Crown of Destiny would simply dissipate and reappear in some random corner of the universe, letting the prize fall into someone else’s hands. Unless they were sworn enemies, they wouldn’t go all out. Even when the Queen of Punishment had fought four of them earlier, no one had been using their full power.
[MUSIC: EPIC GATHERING THEME]
Before long, more Supreme Lords arrived, bringing the total number circling the Star of Golden Sand to twenty. It was incredibly rare for so many of them to gather in one place.
[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]
[AMBIENT: INTENSE HEAT CRACKLING]
Armond spoke up, a small flame already turning in his hand. “Since no one can see where it’s hiding, I’ll keep things simple.” The flame stretched into a line, then curled, shaping itself into a small fire dragon coiling lazily across his palm. He smiled. “I’ll just burn the whole star down.”
[SFX: RUSHING FLAME — RAPID EXPANSION]
He released it. The moment the dragon left his hand, it exploded outward, doubling, tripling in size as it hurtled toward the Star of Golden Sand, slowly coiling around the star and beginning to melt its surface.
[SFX: CONTEMPLATIVE MURMUR]
“He’s going to burn the entire star down,” Zane murmured from inside the Keep, an eyebrow twitching. “Armond is a decisive man.”
As the fire began to eat away at the Star of Golden Sand, Zane felt a faint trace of sorrow. The feeling was so clear he knew instantly where it came from: the will of the stars themselves.
Stars had their own consciousness. Zane had learned this long ago while practicing the Stellar Body, and he’d already activated his connection to more than twenty of them. The sadness he felt was coming from the very stars he was now linked to. But it was a damn pity he couldn’t do anything to stop it. Shaking his head slightly, he could only watch as Armond’s dragon continued to incinerate the star.
[SFX: MASSIVE STAR CRACKING]
[SFX: INTENSE FLAMES ROARING]
A huge crack split the Star of Golden Sand down the middle as the fire dragon tightened its embrace. The entire celestial body was starting to fall apart. But Armond’s flames didn’t die down; if anything, they burned even hotter, melting away all the easily incinerated materials.
[SFX: STAR COLLAPSING]
Within five minutes, the star’s diameter shrank from eighty thousand miles to forty thousand. An hour later, it had been reduced to just ten thousand miles across.
And just like that, a massive star was slowly being burned down to nothing. But somewhere inside, the Elysian Keep lay quiet, still hidden.
Armond scoffed, a cruel smile on his face. *Let’s see how long the Keep can take this heat. Let’s see how long you can hide, you little brat. You had the nerve to curse me out back then. At this point, I don’t even care about the Crown of Destiny. I’m taking your Roaring Token.* 
He wasn’t one to let go of a grudge, and he still remembered every word Zane had thrown at him.
