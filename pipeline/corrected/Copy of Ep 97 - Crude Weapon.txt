Ep 97 - Crude Weapon
Word Count: 1283
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FANTASY]
Dustin couldn't remember the last time an Elder Phoenix Gem had hit the market. Then again, he was barely twenty.
In his entire life at The Gilded Gavel, for as long as he could remember, they'd never auctioned one.
So, Dustin was dying to know where Zane had gotten his hands on this gem. The fact that it was an Elder gem was even more astonishing.
Still, he managed to bite back his questions. As a proper heir of the House Blackwood, he knew the house rules. And rule number one was to never, ever ask about a client's sources. It was the biggest taboo in the business. He quickly composed himself, his professional mask sliding into place.
"Zane, an Elder Phoenix Gem... this could fetch an incredible price," Dustin said, his tone measured. "It's beyond rare. Do you want to sell it in this auction, or wait for the next one? If you wait, we can put the word out and attract buyers who are specifically looking for a Phoenix Gem. We could probably land a much higher price than we would today."
A gem this valuable wasn't something your average buyer could afford. With a little more time, The Gilded Gavel could draw in bidders who had not only the interest but also the deep pockets to match. The final price would likely be astronomical.
"How about today?" Zane said without a moment's hesitation. "I don't mind selling it now." The possibility of a higher price didn't faze him.
Seeing Zane was set on it, Dustin didn't push. He just nodded. "That's fine. We're already auctioning off some valuable Crafting Components and Alchemical Reagents today, which has drawn in a lot of powerful, old-guard crafters from The Capital. I'm sure the Phoenix Gem will still command a very handsome price."
[AMBIENT: GRAND AUCTION HALL]
[SFX: DOOR OPENING]
Once they'd settled on it, Dustin led Zane into the back hall of the auction house. The moment they stepped inside, Zane felt a jolt of awe.
He remembered Alec's description of the Scorpiomane and couldn't help but be impressed. Alec had been right on the money, pegging the beast at about thirty feet tall. Being able to tell that just by looking at its crystal core was a testament to the man's experience and keen eye.
"What kind of starting price are we thinking for this crystal core?" Dustin asked.
Alec let out a hearty laugh. "For a treasure this exceptional, there's no telling who'll come crawling out of the woodwork for it. Especially with The Crucible about to reopen, the clans are always hungry for better weapons. And a Phoenix Gem is a top-tier material for weapon crafting. Based on that, the final price is anyone's guess. But if we have to set a starting bid, I'd say thirty thousand Crystal Cubes is a fair place to start."
"Thirty thousand Crystal Cubes?!" Zane was so stunned by the figure that he couldn't stop the words from bursting out.
His reaction took Alec and Dustin by surprise. They exchanged a glance before Dustin turned to him. "Zane, you think the starting price is too low? Don't worry. Once the bidding gets going, the final price will climb much higher than that."
Zane shook his head. "No, Dustin, you've got it wrong. The price is perfect!"
He didn't think the price was low at all. In fact, it was astronomically higher than he'd ever imagined. He knew the Phoenix Gem was valuable, but he had no idea it was this valuable. The Matriarch's Core from the Razorwing Queen had only netted him one thousand Points, which was equivalent to two thousand Crystal Cubes.
He'd figured the Phoenix Gem would be in the same ballpark. But a starting price fifteen times higher than the Matriarch's Core? It completely shattered his expectations.
Zane's ignorance of market dynamics was a clear sign of his inexperience. The Razorwing, after all, was famous for its rapid breeding. Dozens, sometimes hundreds, of Razorwing Queens were slain at Dragon's Keep every year. That kind of supply naturally drove the price of a Matriarch's Core way down.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
A Scorpiomane, however, was a different beast entirely. The creatures had been hunted to the brink of extinction across the entire Eastern Territory.
It was a rare event to see a Scorpiomane at all, let alone a fully grown one. And its crystal core possessed unique properties for crafting weapons and potions.
The two cores weren't even in the same league; the difference in value was massive.
Hearing Zane had no objections to the starting price, Alec went on, "But if we add this to today's lineup, we'll have two major headliners for this auction. I still think it would be better to save the Scorpiomane crystal core for the next one. It would fetch a much higher price."
Alec was thinking along the same lines as Dustin. An item like this deserved its own special auction.
"No need to wait. My friend is fine with adding it to today's auction," Dustin instructed calmly. "Alec, please secure this Scorpiomane crystal core and get it ready."
"Right away, sir." Alec got the message; the timing wasn't up for discussion. With a quick shake of his head, he had an assistant bring over a lavish, velvet-lined box. He then carefully placed the crystal core inside and sealed it.
With the arrangements for the crystal core settled, Dustin walked Zane back to the auction room. The event was in full swing, so he only had time for a quick nod to Rhys.

[SFX: AUCTIONEER'S GAVEL]
The first item up was a trio of flowers so rare they were said to bloom only once in a lifetime. Starting price: three hundred Crystal Cubes.
"Ornamental only," Rhys said flatly. "Can't even use them in medicine."
"Someone will pay for them," Ethan said. "Rich nobles love that kind of thing."
He was right. A Great House descendant walked them up to eight hundred Crystal Cubes before the gavel fell.
Zane watched without interest. Pretty flowers weren't going to get Elara off Infernal Peak.
The next item was a weapon.
"...its power is comparable to a mid-tier Soul Armament. The starting bid is two thousand Crystal Cubes. Bidding may now begin."
'Two thousand Crystal Cubes? That's not too bad,' Zane thought.
[MUSIC: EXCITED CONTEMPLATION]
He was suddenly interested in this Soul Armament. He couldn't care less about the power of a low-tier weapon like the Sky-sunder Axe. What he was interested in was the Celestial Essence he could get from dismantling it. He knew a legendary arcane weapon typically yielded five or ten drops. Since a Soul Armament was a tier higher, maybe it would yield fifty drops of Celestial Essence... or even a hundred.
The thought alone sent a thrill through him, and he seriously considered bidding. As long as he could light up another Dragon Scale, his strength would grow. If he could light ten at once, his power would temporarily double. A boost like that would be a game-changer.
If he could, Zane was ready to bid. But the price shot up so fast he had to abandon the idea almost immediately.
"Two thousand five hundred Crystal Cubes!" a craftsman shouted excitedly.
"Two thousand seven hundred!" another called out.
"Three thousand Crystal Cubes!"
[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD]
Despite its imposing name and authentic Soul Armament status, the Sky-sunder Axe appeared unwieldy and unrefined. Zane had anticipated modest interest, yet to his astonishment, craftsmen swarmed like vultures over the artifact. The bidding price exploded upward, each call eclipsing the last with alarming speed—revealing a hidden value in the crude weapon that Zane had completely overlooked.
