Ep 286 - Crash Into You

[MUSIC: EPIC INTRODUCTION]
Jacob exploded onto the scene at the previous All Peaks Competition, emerging as an unexpected dark horse who captivated everyone with his meteoric rise. As one of the Sect’s Chosen, achieving such prominence while only at Level Two of the Adept Realm astonished the entire Skyreach Sect. His unprecedented breakthrough into the Top One Hundred defied all expectations.
With unwavering determination, Jacob held his own in brutal confrontations against significantly more powerful disciples. The crowds gasped as he withstood blows that should have shattered lesser competitors. For months afterward, his remarkable performance dominated every conversation in training halls and dining chambers throughout the Sect.
Most people dismissed Jacob’s achievement as a fluke—a perfect alignment of circumstances that could never be replicated. The resigned sighs of masters emphasized the fundamental truth: challenging opponents from higher levels remained virtually impossible.
No one anticipated his record would fall merely three years later. Then a young man named Zane arrived, his quiet confidence masking an unprecedented talent. With each battle, Zane didn’t merely break Jacob’s record—he shattered it, displaying raw power and a ferocity that left spectators speechless, their faces frozen in expressions of disbelief as they witnessed history being rewritten before their eyes.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
At the Second Level of the Adept Realm, Jacob had set an incredible record by cracking the Top One Hundred. But in this competition, Zane didn’t just break the record—he demolished it, blasting his way into the Top One Hundred, then the Top Fifty, and finally, impossibly, the Top Five. It was the stuff of legends, the kind of feat people had never seen before and would likely never see again.
Anyone who heard the stories later could barely believe them. For years, people who hadn’t seen it with their own eyes would doubt it ever really happened.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
Jacob was arrogant. For him, making the Top One Hundred at the Second Level of the Adept Realm hadn’t felt like a big deal. He’d been ready for every grueling fight in the competition three years ago. The record itself meant nothing to him, but the fact that Zane was the one who broke it grated on him.
Because of that, everyone started comparing him to Zane.
Jacob wanted to shut that talk down, and fast.
It was common knowledge that certain cultivation methods were just flat-out stronger than others in the same tier.
Take a sinister method like Bleached Bone Distilled Soul. It was technically a Tier Four cultivation method, but its power dwarfed any other method in that tier. Even the best of the standard methods couldn’t hold a candle to it.
The White Bone Armor it produced had an incredible, almost malevolent, defensive power. Not even Zane's body, which was said to be like a Spirit Weapon itself, could compare.
[SFX: BONE ARMOR MATERIALIZING]
[SFX: SHADOW PHANTOMS FORMING]
Just as Jacob summoned the White Bone Armor, Zane unleashed his Shadow Phantoms.
The sight of the six phantoms gave Jacob pause. "No sword?" he asked, surprised.
Zane’s ridiculously skilled basic swordsmanship had left a deep impression on Jacob. It was one of the main reasons he didn’t dare lower his own power level to fight him.
Before, he wouldn’t have given a damn what Zane thought. Jacob would have willingly dropped his power to fight him, even unprompted. After all, he'd made his name challenging those in higher power levels. The idea of some kid from a lower level challenging him was a joke. He was too proud to even entertain it.
But after seeing Zane's swordsmanship, he knew. If he dropped his power down to the Adept Realm, he'd never win. He had to scrap that idea. He was forced to admit, if only to himself, that Zane might actually have more raw talent.
"Not yet," Zane said, his six Shadow Phantoms swirling around him like smoke.
[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT]
"If you don’t use it now, you’re not getting another chance!" Jacob was already moving before the words left Zane's mouth, closing the distance between them in a flash.
His White Bone Armor had unstoppable defensive power, even tougher than armor forged from a Mythic Weapon. And since it was formed from his own Vitality, it was weightless, linked to his mind so he could move as if it were his own skin.
[SFX: SHADOW PHANTOMS CHARGING]
Jacob stood before him, radiating pure menace, but Zane was in no hurry. With a single thought, he sent all six Shadow Phantoms charging at Jacob.
[SFX: LOUD IMPACT CRASH]
Bang!
Encased in his White Bone Armor, Jacob was a human tank, slamming head-on into the six phantoms.
But while his armor was tough, the phantoms were strong. On that very first impact, they brought Jacob to a dead stop.
[SFX: BONE CRACKING AND GROWING]
Jacob took a slow, deep breath and sneered as puffs of blood-red mist erupted around him. His near-perfect White Bone Armor began to morph. Two horns sprouted from the helmet, reinforcing its defensive power even further.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
"Didn’t expect your summons to have that much bite," Jacob growled, his confidence soaring. "Let’s see you stop me this time!" He charged at Zane again, a blur of white bone and red mist.
This time the phantoms shattered on impact, dissolving into dark wisps. Jacob grinned — then watched them pull back together and charge again.
"You probably didn't watch my previous battles very carefully," Zane said. "My phantoms don't stay down."
The pattern repeated — Jacob smashing, the phantoms shattering, the phantoms reforming. Again and again. The crowd began to murmur. Jacob was burning through his energy while Zane stood at the center of it all, almost relaxed, simply redirecting the phantoms each time they reassembled.
Finally, Jacob changed tactics. He doubled his speed, cut around the phantoms entirely, and drove straight at Zane.
"Finally done playing?" Zane said — and instead of dodging, he accelerated toward Jacob.
[SFX: CROWD CHATTER]
"Why is he trying to take on that armor with just his body?"
"Jacob is wearing the White Bone Armor! It's basically an unbreakable shield," someone shouted from the crowd. "Nothing short of a Mythic Weapon can get through that. Zane isn't using his sword... looks like he wants to fight him head-on. That's why he's charging!"
"Are you kidding me? Jacob is a human tank in that thing! He could crush someone with the armor alone. I know Zane has superhuman strength, but how can he possibly survive a direct hit?"
For a split second, Jacob looked surprised, even curious, before a cruel grin spread across his face.
He'd been worried Zane would play cat-and-mouse, dodging and weaving, making it impossible to land a solid hit. But this idiot wasn’t running. He was charging right at him. Is he out of his mind? Jacob thought. Fine by me. I'll cripple you, and then no one will ever dare compare you to me again!
[SFX: MAGICAL ACTIVATION SOUND]
Just as they were about to collide, the Dragon Scales in Zane's mind lit up, changing everything.
