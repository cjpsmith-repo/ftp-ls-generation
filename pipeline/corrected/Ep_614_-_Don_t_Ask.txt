Ep 614 - Don't Ask
Word count: 1187
[AMBIENT: TENSE TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
With the Ogres retreating and both War Sages dead, the crisis had passed—but Esther Sterling sensed something far larger had just begun.
Zane's mysterious abilities made the Demon Night's help unnecessary. The Ogres never stood a chance. She felt relieved Zane was close to Lavender—that was the only reason the Demon Night wasn't facing him as an enemy.
But even if the Demon Night and Ogres had allied, they couldn't have touched Zane inside the Tower of Sin.
Her gaze settled on Maurice. "So, Maurice, you still want to try and kill him?"
The ogre's face twisted. After a tense moment, he spat, "We're leaving."
Without their War Sages, the Ogres had no advantage. Worse, they still had Zane to deal with. If even the War Sages couldn't handle him, sending War Lords would be suicide. The remaining Ogre War Lords turned to follow Maurice out.
Malcolm, however, couldn't tear his eyes from Zane. A level-ten War Fighter who'd just disposed of two War Sages like nothing. If they didn't kill him now, he'd become an existential threat.
But how could they possibly kill him?
Malcolm could only grit his teeth and trail after Maurice.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS SHIFT]
You could feel it in the air—the sharpest warriors in the room knew the power dynamics in the Tower of Sin were about to change forever. The one hundred twenty-eight seats on the top floor were about to be completely reshuffled.
The Ogres were built like mountains, and after they lumbered out, the tenth floor felt strangely empty.
Only then did Esther finally ask the question on everyone's mind. "Zane, how did you do that?"
He'd tossed two War Sages out of the Tower without lifting a finger, and they hadn't been able to put up the slightest resistance. She just couldn't wrap her head around it.
"Don't ask," Lavender said flatly from within him.
The words sobered Esther instantly. She nodded once. "Understood."
[MUSIC: QUIET INTERLUDE]
[AMBIENT: SETTLING CROWD]
At Esther's signal, the Demon Night War Lords departed, leaving only scattered warriors murmuring about what they'd witnessed.
Lavender crossed to Yolande, who stood expressionless at the arena's edge.
"I know you," Lavender said quietly, a statement of fact.
"I know you too," Yolande replied with the same stillness.
"I hope you succeed."
"If I do, I'll help you."
Lavender nodded. "Thank you."
It was the closest anyone had seen Lavender come to smiling.
[SFX: DISPERSING LIGHT]
Lavender dissolved into a scatter of red specks that drifted through the air and sank back into Zane's body.
Zane had overheard everything. Lavender descended from above—everyone close to the Demon Night knew this. If she and Yolande knew each other, Yolande had descended too. That explained the two versions he'd encountered: one in the Eastern Territories, another here. He filed the mystery away.
Lavender turned back to him. "After you've refined your Lucky Light, we'll go to the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary."
Zane nodded and crossed the floor toward Fabian and Jarrod. "Thank you. Both of you. You stepped forward when it counted; that matters. I promise I'll go to the Violet Heart Sanctuary. But the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary comes first. Wait for me."
The gratitude on their faces was almost uncomfortable to look at. Two of the most powerful human War Lords in the Tower of Sin, looking at a level-ten War Fighter like he was the only solid ground in a flood.
Zane turned and left, heading for the living area to rent a practice room. He needed to refine the Lucky Light he’d won.
Watching him walk away, Olivia opened her mouth to say something, then thought better of it and held her tongue.
[AMBIENT: SEALED CHAMBER SILENCE]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]
Sitting quietly in the sealed chamber, Zane smiled faintly. His body had become crowded—nine True Dragons in his mind, The Soulforge, and a conscious sword spirit who happened to be a queen of the Demon Night.
He pulled the Lucky Light from his signaling token and began refining it.
[SFX: ENERGY MANIFESTATION]
When Zane drew it out, it manifested as a blazing white light so dense it was almost solid.
With this much, I should be able to make a breakthrough.
As the thought crossed his mind, the Vitality within him began to circulate, streams of it coiling around the Lucky Light, refining it and pouring the raw energy into his body.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]
[MUSIC: ANTICIPATION]
Three days later, Zane stepped out of the training chamber. Cheryl was waiting for him, a wide smile on her face. The other members of the Demon Night were gathered nearby, though Zane knew they weren't really waiting for him. They were waiting for their queen, Lavender.
Zane nodded at Cheryl. "We can head out now."
His schedule was tight, but he’d promised to take Lavender to the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, and he always kept his word. Besides, there was still plenty of time before the Dark Nether Cave opened. He couldn't return to The Heartlands until he entered that cave and claimed the inheritance inside anyway, so he had more than enough time for a detour.
[AMBIENT: TOWER INTERIOR]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
Cheryl led him back to the first floor of the Tower of Sin. After a short wait near the entrance, Esther Sterling, the strongest of the seat owners, arrived with two of her top War Lords. Together, they all stepped out of the Tower and headed south, toward the Cursed Land.
[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Flying was impossible here; anyone who tried was immediately slammed back to the ground by a crushing, invisible force. So, they traveled on foot, and after a few hours, a large teleportation array appeared on the horizon.
[SFX: CRYSTAL CHIME]
[SFX: MAGICAL ACTIVATION]
Cheryl stepped forward, pulling several Aether Crystals from her pocket. She placed them into slots on the array, and the entire structure flared to life with a brilliant light.
"Go on," Esther said, nodding at Zane.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL HARP]
He stepped into the shimmering portal. As light engulfed him, his thoughts turned to Yolande—a million versions scattered across lower realms, each accumulating experience that flowed back to the original. He'd thought he understood extraordinary talent. Now he realized he'd barely glimpsed it.
The upper world was full of people whose methods of growing stronger bore no resemblance to ordinary cultivation.
[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]
[SFX: TELEPORTATION FLASH]
When the light faded, they were airborne. Their travel speed was insane now that they could fly. The holy tree that had looked so distant was suddenly right in front of them, a journey that took only five minutes.
[AMBIENT: GRAND TREE SANCTUARY]
[MUSIC: MAJESTIC CHOIR]
The Heavenly Feather Sanctuary had clearly been notified of their queen’s arrival. The top of the enormous tree was crowded with people. A crimson carpet was rolled out along a thick, trunk-like branch, and at the far end of it stood a woman dressed in regal purple.
Even from tens of thousands of feet away, he could feel her aura wash over him. It wasn't aggressive, but it was heavy, a pressure far more profound than that of the Ogre War Sages or even Maddox back at the Life-and-Death Realm.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
Staring at that distant figure, Zane already knew who she was. He'd seen her name on the Talent Tablet.
The leader of the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary. One of the rare warriors who had reached the Soul Sea Realm.
It was Amber.
