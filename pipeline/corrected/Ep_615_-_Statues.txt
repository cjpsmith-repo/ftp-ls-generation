Ep 615 - Statues
Word count: 1088
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Warriors who reached the Soul Sea Realm were the most powerful beings in existence.
Below that level, those who ascended to the Life-and-Death Realm or became War Sages grew so powerful the world itself tried to crush them with relentless crises—cosmic punishment for their strength.
Once a warrior broke through to the Soul Sea Realm, however, the world's punishments couldn't touch them. Their power forced exile to the upper world. Amber's staggering talent had carried her from Adept to Soul Sea Realm in just two hundred years, leaving her ample time before forced ascension.
[SFX: MASSIVE TREE TREMOR]
[MUSIC: REVERENT CHOIR]
The holy tree shuddered the moment Zane and Lavender landed on its massive trunk. It wasn't just a tree; it was a fortress, as colossal as a castle, and when it shook, it felt like the world was ending. With a reverence that bordered on worship, Amber dropped to one knee before Lavender. “Welcome, My Queen,” she said, her voice filled with awe.
Lavender just gave a small, almost indifferent nod. The sight of a Soul Sea Realm warrior bowing to her didn’t even make her blink. For her, it seemed, this was just another Tuesday.
[SFX: MASS MOVEMENT]
Then the rest of them followed—the other powerful warriors from the Soul Sea Realm, the War Sages of the Demon Night—all sinking to their knees in perfect, chilling unison. A wave of motion rippled across the tree until more than one hundred thousand members of the Demon Night were kneeling, every single one of them staring up at Lavender with absolute, unwavering devotion.
Their three queens were gods to them. Across millions of lower worlds, countless sacred places were dedicated to their worship, but the queens themselves almost never appeared in person. For Lavender to grace the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary with her presence was an event beyond their wildest dreams.
[SFX: RUSTLING LEAVES]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Then the tree began to do something Zane had never seen before.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
Green veins lit up across every branch, pulsing with light that flowed downward along the bark, converging on the main trunk, then climbing back upward toward the branch where they stood. When it reached Lavender, the light surged and enveloped her completely.
"What is that?" Zane asked.
"The life force of the Sacred Tree," Cheryl said quietly.
[SFX: RAPID DECAY]
As the light poured out of it, the holy tree began to wither. The lush green leaves yellowed and fell, scattering across the ground below. It wasn't just the tree—the plants surrounding it were fading too, the color draining out of everything within reach.
Zane stared. "If they keep pulling from it like this, won't the tree—"
"Ninety percent of its life force," Cheryl said. "That's what they're taking. Our queen's soul was badly damaged. She needs it."
Zane watched the leaves fall.
"The tree will recover," Cheryl added. "Two or three years, and it will be as it was. The Demon Night do not do this lightly—normally, they draw only a small amount each year, for the newborns. But for their queen, there is no question."
Lavender absorbed the last of the green light. In an instant, it was gone.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING]
The holy tree began to tremble more violently, its thick branches swaying back and forth like arms in a storm. Zane felt a powerful force pulling him down, gluing his feet to the bark. No matter how hard the tree shook, he knew he wasn’t going anywhere.
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD BELOW]
[MUSIC: HUSHED AWE]
Below them, the hundreds of thousands of Demon Night members who had been kneeling finally rose. A wave of hushed excitement moved through the crowd.
"I saw her. It really is the queen. She looks exactly like the statue."
"Why has she come here like this? Something must have happened in the upper world. Why else would our queen be living inside a human's body?"
"Keep your voice down. Do you want to be punished?"
The rumors had been circulating since word first reached the Sanctuary. For years, Lavender's statue had been dimming—not just here, but in every Demon Night holy place across every lower realm. It was an omen that something had gone terribly wrong. Lavender's arrival had confirmed their fears, even if none of them dared ask what had happened.
Lavender turned to Amber. "Take me to the statues."
"Yes, My Queen." Amber rose without hesitation and led her along the crimson carpet toward the heart of the trunk.
[AMBIENT: SACRED TEMPLE]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL STRINGS]
Inside the main trunk, hollowed out like a cathedral, stood three enormous statues. Each was a Demon Night queen rendered in perfect detail, each radiating its own light.
The first held a great scepter and glowed with a deep, steady green. Just standing near it, Zane could feel something alive in the air around it. "She's the Queen of Life," Cheryl said quietly.
Another depicted a woman wielding a brutal meteor hammer, glowing with a soft blue light. It seemed like a strangely savage weapon for the Demon Night to revere.
“She’s the Queen of Punishment,” Cheryl explained from beside him.
"That makes sense," Zane said, turning to the last statue.
The last one was also bathed in a red glow, but it was far dimmer than the other two, barely a flicker in comparison.
[SFX: SOFT MAGICAL HUM]
The statue itself, however, was a perfect likeness of Lavender.
No wonder they recognized her right away, Zane thought, his eyes fixed on the stone face. The resemblance is uncanny. But something was off. Why was the statue holding a spear?
As far as he knew, Lavender used a sword.
He thought back to Killing Sword Mountain, the one place they’d ever truly connected, where he’d accidentally acquired a sword spirit. Lavender had been there, and she’d mastered the Apex of Sword Intent—she had to be a sword user. The statue holding a spear made no sense at all.
Zane was still trying to work it out when Lavender stopped directly in front of her own effigy. She turned, her gaze sweeping over the kneeling crowd. “All of you may leave,” she commanded, her voice cutting through the air. “Zane, you stay. No one is to enter the palace without my permission. That is an order.”
Of course, he had to stay. As his sword spirit, she couldn't stray too far from him. It was just a fact of their strange bond.
“Yes, My Queen.” Amber nodded, not hesitating for a second. “No one will approach the palace without your command.”
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS FADING]
[AMBIENT: EMPTY PALACE HALL]
She rose and quickly led Esther Sterling and the other Soul Sea Realm warriors away, leaving Zane alone with Lavender in the heart of the sacred palace.
