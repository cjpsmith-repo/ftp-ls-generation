[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT ARCHIVE ECHOES]
Livingston still felt like he was dreaming, even as he followed Zane down into the archives of the Elysian Keep.
Now that they were inside, Zane’s first order of business was getting Freya and Livingston settled. He suggested Freya take a look around and find a book on attunement that might help her, since the method she’d learned in the Lower World was pretty basic.
Livingston, on the other hand, was completely spellbound by the sheer number of books. He blew right past the sections on attunement—useless for his current puppet body—and made a beeline for the texts on weaponsmithing. He got so lost in a book that when Zane came to check on him, he flat-out refused to leave.
Once the two of them were situated, Zane didn’t waste any time. 
[SFX: MAGICAL SHRINKING WHOOSH]
He stepped outside, and with a gentle tap of his hand, the majestic Elysian Keep shrank back down into a palm-sized token.
The Wheel of Reincarnation was in tatters, but it was far from destroyed. Zane was confident that even if the Queen of Punishment’s lackeys came after him, he could just duck back inside the Keep. Besides, they wouldn’t be able to catch him anytime soon. Not before they dealt with the crumbling Wheel of Reincarnation, anyway.
[SFX: COSMIC FLIGHT RUSH]
Feeling secure, Zane took control of the Wheel once more and shot off into the cosmos to see what he could find.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
[AMBIENT: DEMONIC REALM ATMOSPHERE]
Meanwhile, in the Punishment World—the core supreme world of the Demon Night—things were anything but quiet.
The world itself was unique, structured not as a planet but as a single, gigantic mountain that stretched from the edge of its reality all the way to the center. It was aptly named Punishment Mountain. Of course, it was so massive that the people living on it had no idea they were on a mountain at all. You could only see its true form from the void of space.
[AMBIENT: LARGE CROWD MURMURING]
At its peak, the Palace of Heavenly Punishment stood like a beacon. Today, countless Demon Night warriors had gathered at its gates.
These were the elders of the Demon Night, ancient men and women who had lived for millions of years. Some had already endured the Five Aging Processes and were now just withering away, waiting for death. As mere World Lords, they could never become immortal, and there was nothing they could do to extend their lives.
This had been building for seven hundred years.
Seven hundred years ago, the Queen of Punishment had made her move against Lavender — and the Board of Elders had stayed silent. Killing a queen was an unimaginable crime, but when the killer was another queen, what could they do? They had believed Lavender would die. They had waited for a new queen to be born.
She hadn’t died. After seven hundred years, her soul had recovered enough to return to the universe — and some of the older members of the Board had begun to stir. If Lavender had the strength to come back, then let her come back. They watched and waited.
Then the Queen of Punishment brazenly dispatched Cassian and the other three to hunt Lavender down, and the whole universe watched how it ended.
That was the final straw. More than twenty elders had descended on the Palace of Heavenly Punishment that morning, and the doors had not opened once.
After twelve to fourteen hours of silence, they had stopped waiting politely.
“Bring the queen out! We demand an explanation!” one of them roared.
“She will answer to the Board of Elders for what she’s done!” shouted another.
“We can’t let her ruin everything!”
For all their blustering, though, none of them dared to actually charge the Palace of Heavenly Punishment.
[SFX: DOOR OPENING]
Then the doors parted — just enough for a single figure to step through.
Kaelan stood at the threshold, silver hair falling past his pointed ears, a cold smile on his face. He said nothing. He didn’t need to. The Queen was resting, he told them pleasantly. The elders should return.
The elders erupted. Lavender was no traitor of the Demon Night. The Board of Elders was not decoration. If the Queen would not come out, they would impeach her.
Kaelan’s smile didn’t waver. “Impeach?” He let the word sit in the air for a moment.
In theory, the Board of Elders held that right. In the entire history of the Demon Night, it had never once been exercised. The lower ranks of the clan knew their queens, not the Board. The elders represented seventy-two great clans and carried real weight in internal affairs, but against a queen’s hold on the hearts of the clan, their authority had limits. The threat was real enough to cause trouble. It was not real enough to succeed.
Kaelan knew it. The elders knew it. The cold smile stayed.
[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ENTRANCE]
In the middle of the protest, a figure materialized quietly in the center of the throne room. He was a tall man in a long, plain robe, cinched at the waist by a belt carved to look like a serpent.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
“My Queen,” he said, addressing the woman on the throne. He didn’t bow.
The Queen of Punishment offered him a warm smile. “Gunter. What’s wrong?”
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
There weren’t many Supreme Lords in their race—only four, spread across the hundreds of supreme worlds and countless sacred places the Demon Night occupied. It wasn’t that the Demon Night was weak; it was just that only one hundred and twenty-eight souls could bear the Heavenly Destiny. No matter how gifted a newcomer was, they couldn’t ascend if all the positions were filled. And without the Crown of Destiny, a warrior could never break through to the next level.
Tens of thousands of years ago, the Demon Night had only three Supreme Lords. The fourth Crown of Destiny had been won by Lavender herself — with the help of the Dream God Arrow. That was how Gunter had his position.
Supreme Lord Gunter was one of her staunchest supporters, which was precisely why his sudden appearance made her nervous. Had he come for the same reason the elders were screaming at her gates? Was he here to talk her out of it?
That would be a problem. A “suggestion” from Gunter was something she couldn’t just ignore.
But his next words made her let out a silent sigh of relief.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RUMBLING BASS]
[SFX: DISTANT THUNDER]
“The heavens trembled,” he said simply.
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
“The heavens trembled?” the queen repeated, a flicker of ruthlessness crossing her beautiful face. She wasn’t a Supreme Lord and didn’t bear the Heavenly Destiny, so she hadn’t felt the vibration herself.
