Ep 844 - Writhing Weeds

Word count: 987

[AMBIENT: MYSTERIOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Before anyone set foot in the Sacred Feather Sea, they needed to get the lay of the land. It was a good thing, too, because some saint-level genius had just asked a question so dumb it made you wonder how he’d survived this long, naively assuming the next area would be a walk in the park.

The truth was, the union of seven divine kingdoms had been plumbing the depths of the Sacred Feather Sea for years and still hadn't mapped the whole thing. The biggest roadblock was simply that the place was trying to kill you at every turn.

That said, it was a cakewalk compared to the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. The Feather Emperor’s, Dream God's, and Divine Emperor's Secret Lands were all significantly easier to navigate, which was why the seven kingdoms usually sent their younger generation to tackle them while the real masters—the powerhouses from the Soul Sea and Life-and-Death Realms—threw themselves at the most treacherous one of all. The second the Sacred Feather Sea opened, they would find a way into the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, consequences be damned.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

Randy paused the group at the edge of the new terrain. “Listen up,” he said, his voice low and serious. “A long time ago, the dragon-blooded spread a curse here with a blood sacrifice. A lot of the spirit beasts that used to live in what is now The Spectral Plains were caught in it. Their ghosts are still here, so watch your backs.”

He didn't mention that he'd already felt something off back in the Slow Arrow Field, a subtle shift that made him suspect The Spectral Plains would be even deadlier than he remembered. Even if he was wrong, a little extra caution couldn't hurt.

Everyone nodded, their faces grim, and they moved forward as one.

[AMBIENT: EERIE GRASSLAND]

[SFX: RUSTLING WEEDS]

The Slow Arrow Field had been a chaotic jungle, but the Spectral Plains was a vast, pitch-black grassland. As they drew closer, they saw that the ground itself was alive, the dark weeds writhing in the dirt like a carpet of insects.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

“Alright, pick up the pace. Don't stop for anything,” Randy commanded, his eyes fixed on the undulating ground.

“These are called Writhing Weeds,” he added. “Extremely tough. They're not hard to deal with on their own — but the vengeful spirits are another matter. Keep moving and don't give them a chance to grab you.”

“Why?” Of course, it was Renzo. It seemed the guy hadn't learned a thing. Without waiting for an answer, he strode onto the grassland.

[SFX: VIOLENT RUSTLING]

[SFX: WHIPPING TENDRILS]

The instant his boot touched the ground, the weeds erupted in a violent frenzy, twisting into thick, rope-like tendrils that shot out to ensnare his ankles.

“Is this what you were so worried about?” Renzo scoffed, a look of pure disdain on his face. A few pathetic weeds trying to trip him, a warrior of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm? This was a man who could shatter mountains. He wasn't about to be taken down by grass. With a contemptuous stomp, he crushed the knotted weeds into pulp.

He hadn't even finished the thought when the grass came back.

[SFX: TENDRILS TIGHTENING]

More tendrils shot out and seized his wrists, yanking his arms flat against the ground. Then his legs. Then his torso. The Writhing Weeds moved with terrifying speed, weaving over and around him in layer after layer until he vanished entirely beneath a writhing mound of earth. The mound heaved and shook as he struggled, but the weeds held.

[SFX: BLADE UNSHEATHING]

[MUSIC: HEROIC ACTION THEME]

Randy was already moving. A crimson-gold blade appeared in his hand and he crossed the grass in swift, light steps, cutting low across the mound in a single sweeping arc.

[SFX: SHARP CUTTING]

The weeds split apart. Renzo burst free, gasping, and spent a long moment brushing himself off. When he finally looked up at the Writhing Weeds again, the disdain was gone.

[SFX: THUNDER CRASH]

[SFX: LIGHTNING STRIKES]

Just then, the scene ahead exploded with light. Purple, blue, and white bolts of lightning crashed down one after another, slamming into the shimmering form of a ghost beast.

The others had cornered it. 

The ghost beast was humanoid, arms stretched wide, mouth open and uttering strange, wordless syllables. It moved with agonizing slowness — it couldn't catch a single warrior no matter how it reached for them.

[SFX: CRACKLING ELECTRICITY]

Fredrick was at the front, purple lightning crackling from his body as he drove bolt after bolt into the creature. The thought of stopping never even crossed their minds. A ghost beast's body could yield a fortune in Ghost Beads — priceless treasures that could strengthen a warrior's soul. Why would they pass that up just because Fiona was giving them a dirty look?

[SFX: DEAFENING THUNDER]

The thunder was deafening.

Renzo shot Randy a smug look. “This Spectral Plains doesn't seem nearly as terrifying as you made it sound,” he remarked.

After the beating they’d taken in the Slow Arrow Field, Randy's warning had put everyone on edge. But watching this ghost beast get pummeled into submission, completely unable to fight back, made his caution seem overblown.

Randy just managed a helpless smile.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

“Last time, the Slow Arrow Field was a breeze, and the Spectral Plains nearly killed us,” he explained. “Looks like this time, it's the other way around.”

Seeing Fiona was still fuming over the group ganging up on the creature, Callan gently tried to guide her away. “Fiona, you can't stop them. Let's just keep moving.”

But while Callan was trying to leave, Renzo, Barry, and a few others were dragging their feet. Barry, in particular, whose training relied on a powerful spirit-based technique, was practically drooling over the Ghost Beads. The prize was so close he could taste it, and for a moment, he was torn.

Suddenly, a voice, frantic and terrified, screamed inside Zane's mind. It was the Cobalt Dragon. His soul was faint, but unlike Lavender, he hadn’t fallen into a deep slumber.

[MUSIC: SUDDEN DANGER]

”Zane! Run!” the dragon shrieked. ”Get out of The Spectral Plains! Get out now!”
