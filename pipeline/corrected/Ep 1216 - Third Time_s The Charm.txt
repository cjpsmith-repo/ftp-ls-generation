Ep 1216 - Third Time’s The Charm

Word count: 990

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: CITY WALL BATTLEFIELD]
Other powerful fighters lined the city wall, some even stronger than Lance, but far more composed.
When Zane first killed Lance and earned all those Illusion Points, several had considered testing him. A few Ogre warriors even plotted to attack together.
Their instincts held them back. Something about Zane felt different - powerful in ways they couldn’t define.
When Zane killed Lance a second time, they breathed collective relief. His coiled posture practically dared another challenge. If he’d handled Lance so easily, his true strength likely exceeded what he’d revealed. People fear the unknown, and Zane remained a complete mystery.
He floated in the air, wearing a faint smile that now seemed unnervingly calm and impossible to read.
[SFX: ENRAGED HOWL]
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
A moment later, a figure came screaming out of the city, howling with rage. 
It was Lance. 
For him, dying twice to the same man was a humiliation he couldn’t stomach. He was consumed by a blind fury, his mind completely gone.
“Scram!”
Lance’s roar tore across the city wall, and the warriors gathered there scrambled to open a path. He leaped, using the wall itself as a springboard, and blasted out over the battlefield once again— so consumed by rage that he never even bothered to lock onto Zane’s position.
[AMBIENT: HUSHED CROWD]
A few of the hidden powerhouses watching from the crowd let out quiet sighs. If Lance could have reined in his anger, he might have actually stood a chance. But his martial heart was in chaos now, and charging out blind like this would only hand Zane more points.
Sure enough, the moment Lance cleared the wall, he realized Zane was nowhere in sight. In his fury, all he’d wanted was to get out and kill him — but he hadn’t tracked where Zane actually was.
While Lance hung there in the air, dazed, a quiet voice spoke from directly above him. It was Zane.
He was floating just over Lance’s head, his eyes cold and unreadable, Thundergust flickering with a faint blue light in his hand.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
“You said you’re a wolf,” Zane said, a faint smile crossing his face as he raised the sword high. “I’ll give you that one. But you’re a weak wolf, at best.”
[SFX: BLADE WHOOSH]
The sword came down — but at the last instant, it turned flat and slapped the back of Lance’s neck instead of cutting.
[SFX: CRACK]
A sharp crack rang out, followed by Lance’s muffled groan as he plummeted toward the ground.
[SFX: SPACE-STEP WHOOSH]
Zane took a single step through open air and vanished, reappearing instantly below Lance, sword raised to meet him as he fell. 
“As for you calling me a sheep,” Zane said, “I’m going to have to disagree.”
[SFX: BLADE WHOOSH]
[SFX: CRACK]
The flat of the blade caught Lance again, sending him tumbling back up into the sky like a struck ball. Two hits in, and Lance was too rattled to even speak — though he’d clearly heard every word.
[SFX: SPACE-STEP WHOOSH]
Zane flashed forward again, reappearing above Lance once more, smiling. “I’m not a sheep. Maybe I’m not even a wolf.” His eyes sharpened. “I’m the lion that eats wolves.”
[MUSIC: SHARP ORCHESTRAL]
This time, Zane didn’t hold back. A decisive killing intent burst outward as he became something colder, more merciless — a blade of pure sword intent tearing straight toward Lance, who had nothing left to fight back with.
[SFX: SWORD IMPACT — FINAL]
With that strike, Zane killed Lance for the third time.
It should have been unthinkable. Every warrior in the Illusion Battlefield had a home city to retreat to — losing a fight didn’t have to mean dying, not when running was always an option. Only a fool would keep charging back out to die again and again.
But that was exactly what Lance had done.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Between the three kills, Zane had earned one hundred forty thousand Illusion Points for the first, seventy thousand for the second, and thirty thousand for the third — bringing his total past three hundred ten thousand points.
He checked his ranking again. With Lance’s points added in, he’d rocketed up to roughly eighty thousandth in this world — and three million, nine hundred thirty thousandth overall. It was the first time he’d broken into the top fifteen million.
Outside, in the wider world, most eyes on the ranking pyramid only paid attention to the top five million. Cracking that threshold, out of a trillion warriors across a hundred thousand great worlds, was no small thing.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: LARGE GATHERING MURMUR]
Meanwhile, all the warriors of The Mist Palace were gathered in front of the ranking pyramid, their eyes fixed on the countless names scrolling across its surface.
“Nigel from the Thoughtless Minds is still holding first place!”
“Of course he is. He’s their prodigy, right? They say he used the Supreme Lord of Original Sin’s technique to absorb a Celestial Fragment and survive his own core flux backlash. He’s definitely got the strength to be number one.”
“Ugh, our guys are getting crushed. Not a single one has made it into the top ten thousand! Layla’s barely hanging on around one hundred and ten thousand. I can’t believe Jasper is actually doing better than her—he’s in the top eighty thousand.”
“Hey, don’t get so worked up. This is just the beginning. Zane hasn’t even shown up yet, right?”
“Yeah, I can’t find his name anywhere!”
“I found him!” a warrior from The Mist Palace suddenly shouted. 
[MUSIC: RISING HOPEFUL STRINGS]
“Zane! He’s at three million, nine hundred thirty thousandth! He just broke into the top five million!”
[AMBIENT: QUIET FOCUSED CROWD]
Yolande, who had been standing silently this whole time, suddenly looked up, her eyes bright. Since the Illusion Battlefield opened, Zane had completely vanished. She’d started to wonder if he’d even made it inside at all. Hearing his name was a wave of pure relief. She trusted him. As long as he was in the fight, she knew he’d claw his way to a good ranking.
“He’s only ranked at three million, nine hundred thirty thousandth?” someone else grumbled in disbelief. “After that performance during the Passing the Torch? What on earth is he doing?”
