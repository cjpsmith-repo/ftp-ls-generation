Ep 975 - A World Built for Giants
Word count: 1107
[SFX: MAGICAL FLASH]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
The flash of light that marked Zane’s disappearance left Isla staring, her eyes wide with curiosity. “Is that a teleportation array?” she asked, her voice full of awe.
The Eastern Territories weren’t exactly swimming in teleportation arrays. The only one ever built in the Skyreach Sect was the one leading to the Cloud Road, and even that thing was practically falling apart from neglect.
Using one to move a single person cost a supreme Aether crystal. Even someone as loaded as Sir Kenneth would think twice about spending that kind of cash.
So for Isla, seeing one pop up out of nowhere was a real shock.
Tia and Cora, on the other hand, didn’t even blink. Back in the Heartlands, teleportation arrays were as common as dirt. Some merchants with enough coin even used them to move goods. It was pricey, sure, but it was also safe and fast.
Zane hadn’t told them where he was taking them, but Tia figured whatever secrets this little backwater held couldn’t be that impressive. A place this small couldn’t possibly be hiding some lost world.
Still, no matter what, she would follow her husband to the ends of the earth.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: OTHERWORLDLY SPACE]
The moment Tia stepped between the two bamboo stalks, a white light engulfed her. When it faded, she was somewhere else entirely. A look of genuine surprise finally broke through her composure.
“There’s no teleportation tunnel... and I can’t sense any Spatial Laws at work,” she murmured. “That’s strange.”
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL FOREST]
[MUSIC: WONDER AND AWE]
Behind her, Cora and Isla passed through the bamboo gate, appearing beside her. The scenery was breathtaking—not for its beauty, but for its sheer, impossible scale.
“These trees are massive! Are we in the land of giants?” Isla craned her neck, staring up at the trees that towered over them like mountains.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]
At first glance, the landscape looked normal. But after a moment, you realized everything had been magnified a thousand times over. Even the weeds at the base of the trees were as big as a house. It was a world built for giants.
Zane chuckled at their stunned faces. The first time he’d come here, he was only at the Adept Realm. The scale of the place had terrified him, too—not to mention the impossibly large griffin he’d spotted on the other side of an enchanted barrier. Luckily for him, that barrier had saved his life.
“What is this place?” Cora asked, her curiosity officially piqued. In truth, she didn’t really care where they were. But seeing how much Zane wanted to surprise them, she decided to play along. The man had her heart, after all.
Tia and Isla, however, were genuinely curious and turned to Zane, waiting for an answer.
Zane gave them a sly smile. “Come with me. I’ll show you.”
[SFX: VITALITY SURGE]
[MUSIC: RISING DANGER]
Before he could take a step, Isla gasped. Cora spun around. Tia’s Vitality flared instantly, her body dropping into a fighting stance.
Outside the barrier, dozens of enormous leopards had appeared. They were like nothing any of them had ever seen — each one the size of a small building, their fangs alone as tall as a grown person. They moved in slow, prowling circles, fixing the group with cold, unblinking eyes.
[SFX: LOW BESTIAL GROWLING]
“Don’t panic,” Zane said, his voice steady. “Anything that touches the barrier dies. They know it.”
The women steadied themselves, though Tia’s eyes didn’t leave the leopards. She could feel their power pressing against her senses — and what she felt made her skin prickle. These creatures were strong. Impossibly strong.
Zane was thinking the same thing. He reached out with his perception, trying to gauge their cultivation level the way he could with any Spirit Supreme Realm warrior — or beyond. He found nothing. A blank wall. Even at the Life-and-Death Realm, he couldn’t read them.
He kept his expression neutral, but the realization settled quietly in his chest. The Elysian Keep was still operating at a level he couldn’t fathom.
[SFX: DEEP ROARING — DISTANT]
The leopards paced and snarled, but none crossed the line. Eventually, one by one, they withdrew into the shadows of the giant trees. Smarter than the Griffin, at least — but not smart enough to simply walk away.
Zane stepped forward and took Tia’s hand. She didn’t pull back. Cora immediately jogged up and grabbed his other hand with a grin. Isla fell into step quietly behind them, saying nothing.
They walked on. The path widened, and then the Keep itself came into view.
[MUSIC: SWEEPING ORCHESTRAL]
The gate was enormous — thousands of feet high, its archway broad enough that a mountain-sized giant could have walked through without ducking. Tia stared up at it, speechless for a moment.
“The person who built this place,” Cora said, tilting her head back, “was very tall.”
Zane thought back to what Saul had told him the last time he’d left The Elysian Keep. The token he now held was the key, allowing him to control the outer courtyard and even enter the keep itself. Zane might have been the owner on paper, but the token was what gave him any real power here.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
He pulled the signaling token from his Storage Ring just as two enormous golems slowed their lumbering approach. They were still striding forward, but it was clear they no longer saw the group as a threat.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: ARMOR CLANKING]
They stomped right up to Zane and dropped to one knee.
Zane gave a slight nod, then waved the three women forward. Though still hesitant, they followed him into the inner courtyard.
To be fair, at Zane’s current strength, these golems couldn’t have hurt him anyway. But since he had a token to control them, he wasn’t about to waste his energy on a fight.
[AMBIENT: WATER CHURNING]
[SFX: SPLASHING]
After entering the inner courtyard, they reached a pond where the water churned with countless piranhas.
[AMBIENT: ECHOING CHAMBER]
[SFX: STONE GRINDING]
Still holding the token, Zane led the three of them straight into the central spire. Inside, he began rearranging a set of runic statues into a specific pattern, which allowed them to pass through the spire with ease.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Soon, a voice drifted over to them. “Zane, my friend. It’s been a long time. I’ve been waiting for you for four years.”
[SFX: MECHANICAL WHIRRING]
Not far away stood a middle-aged man in a silk robe. It was Saul, the one who had given him the token all those years ago. He had the body of an intricate puppet, but a living soul was hidden inside.
Zane smiled and nodded at his friend. “You’re right. It’s been a while. How has everything been?”
“You tell me!” Saul replied. “In four years, you’ve jumped from the Adept Realm all the way to the Life-and-Death Realm. Your training speed is incredible. But it’s still not enough!”
