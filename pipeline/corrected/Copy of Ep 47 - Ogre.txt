Meanwhile, Zane was completely oblivious. He had no idea he was the talk of the town, that a group of powerful masters was dissecting his every move, or that he'd caused such an uproar outside.
After taking out Joe, Zane had inherited all his crystal fragments. The pile he had now was more than enough to pass The Gauntlet.
"So, what now?" he wondered, letting out a long sigh of relief.
All he had to do was find a quiet corner, lay low until the test was over, and maybe catch his breath. After that, he'd officially be an Outer Disciple of the Skyreach Sect.
He finally took a moment to check the damage. His clothes were shredded and he was covered in blood. Luckily, the wounds looked way worse than they felt. The thread-thin cuts crisscrossing his hands and feet were bloody and raw, but they were just surface wounds. They hadn't touched his Life Energy, which meant they'd be easy to heal.
[SFX: ENERGY HEALING]
Clenching his fists, Zane focused his energy. A deep red, blood-like fluid oozed from the cuts, purging them as it flowed down his arms and legs. He flexed, forcing the muscles underneath to knit back together. It was a basic healing skill for anyone who'd reached the peak Strength Level.
In just a few moments, he felt almost as good as new.
Once the wounds were sealed, Zane paced back and forth, testing his weight on his feet and making sure no pain lingered. Then, with a final glance around, he started walking.
But a question still nagged at him. The weapon he'd taken from Joe was a Spirit Weapon. "I wonder how many dragon scales the Black Fire in my mind will light up if it swallows this thing," Zane thought. "Three? Four? Maybe even five?"
He also had more crystals than he could use. If he ran into Ethan out here, he could offload some. But the third layer was vast. Ethan could be anywhere.
Outside, the situation was less calm.
The masters had lost Zane's feed the moment Joe was eliminated — Joe's Student ID had been their only window in. Now the feed was blank again, and several of them rounded on Wyatt, demanding answers. 
He stood there and took it, face dark as a storm cloud, saying nothing. He couldn't explain. He couldn't defend himself. Not with Isla watching.
[SFX: OMINOUS PRESENCE]
He had no idea what to expect. Suddenly, a cold dread washed over him.
[AMBIENT: DANGEROUS WILDERNESS]
[MUSIC: TENSE DANGER]
A Level 5 Beast. At least. Zane didn't have time to wonder why a monster this powerful was on the third layer. His only thought was to run.
Back on the mountain, Zane had no idea any of this was happening. He was still walking, still thinking about Joe's dagger, when a cold dread settled over him without warning.
The stone forest ahead of him erupted. Three trial disciples and two Great House students came sprinting out of the trees, faces white with terror, not even looking where they were going.
Behind them, the stone forest collapsed.
The Ogre burst through. Six meters tall, horned, eyes blazing. 
It caught one of the Great House disciples mid-stride, scooped him up, and crammed him into its mouth. A flash of light — and the disciple was gone, eliminated on the spot.
The other fleeing students scattered in every direction.
A Level-5 Beast. At least. Zane didn't have time to wonder why a monster this powerful was on the third layer. His only thought was to run.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
Hoping to slip away unnoticed, Zane took off, sprinting away from the Ogre. But the monster immediately dropped its pursuit of the other trainees and came thundering right for him.
[SFX: HEAVY THUNDERING FOOTSTEPS]
The ground shook with each heavy, crashing footstep behind him. "Oh, you've got to be kidding me!" he thought, his heart sinking. "If I'd known it was going to follow me, I never would have run!"
He figured he should find a place to hide until the Ogre lost interest. But a frantic glance around confirmed it: there was nowhere to hide. He had no choice but to keep running.
The Ogre looked slow and clumsy, but each of its lumbering strides ate up the ground, covering the distance of eight of his own frantic steps. No matter how hard he pushed himself, the Ogre was just plain faster.
Zane focused on his breathing, trying to coax more speed out of his exhausted legs. He already had more than enough fragments. Getting beaten to a pulp and knocked out of the competition now would be a total, mortifying joke.
But even with Zane running flat out, the gap between them was closing fast. They were as mismatched in speed as they were in strength.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT CRASH]
When the Ogre was just a few feet away, it lunged, swinging a massive palm down to swat him like a fly. At the last possible second, Zane dove forward. The Ogre's attack smashed into the ground right where he'd been, and the impact sent a shockwave through the earth that made his ears ring.
He risked a look back and saw a giant, five-fingered crater gouged into the dirt. If he'd been a split-second slower, he would've been a red smear on the ground.
[SFX: SCRAMBLING FOOTSTEPS]
Not wasting a second, Zane rolled, scrambled to his feet, and kept running.
[SFX: MONSTROUS GROWL]
The phantom creature, an Ogre born from illusion, growled something garbled in its strange tongue and charged after him again.
Zane had no choice but to keep running, a surge of angry frustration rising in his chest. Why was this thing only chasing him?
Unfortunately, he couldn't exactly stop and ask. And even though the Ogre was just an illusion, getting killed by it would be unbelievably embarrassing. Besides, it was a Phantom Beast; it was insane to think it could be reasoned with.
[AMBIENT: TENSE MEETING ROOM]
[MUSIC: URGENT DRAMATIC]
While Zane was running for his life, things outside Bloody Mountain were descending into chaos. Several instructors, watching the Engraved Slabs, had realized the Ogre had escaped the fifth level and was now running rampant on the third.
"Wyatt, what the hell is going on?"
"That Ogre is supposed to be locked on the fifth level! How did it break through the Runic Circles and get to the third? This has never happened before!"
Facing their accusations, Wyatt stammered, "I—I don't know. Maybe there's a flaw in the Runic Circles on Bloody Mountain. I have no other explanation for how this could have happened."
"I demand we stop The Gauntlet immediately," one of the instructors declared.
"I agree. We have to stop it. If that thing gets to the other participants, they'll all be unfairly knocked out of the running."
[AMBIENT: FOREST CHASE]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
Zane desperately watched the Ogre's every move, dodging and weaving between its attacks. As he ran, he wracked his brain, trying to remember everything he knew about Ogres. Their biology was completely different from a human's. From the moment they were born, Ogres were immensely stronger. It was why, back in the ancient wars, humanity had had such a hard time fighting them.
But the Creator was fair. Ogres had a fatal weakness. Their massive, powerful bodies couldn't be sustained by a single heart, so they had a second one.
Having two hearts wasn't the flaw itself; in fact, that's what gave them their incredible strength. The problem was the location of the second heart: it was on their back. Only a thin layer of flesh and muscle protected it. If you could break through that, destroying the second heart and killing the Ogre was easy.
Due to this fatal flaw, Ogres invariably donned substantial battle armor. The protective gear featured a prominent, elevated plate across their backs—specifically designed to shield that secondary heart—a distinctive element humans derisively dubbed a "tortoiseshell."
[SFX: SUDDEN STOP]
[MUSIC: REVELATION MOMENT]
As Zane darted between trees, his lungs burning with each breath, a sudden realization struck him. He skidded to a halt, nearly stumbling as his eyes widened in disbelief.
[SFX: DRAMATIC HEARTBEAT]
This Ogre bore no armor whatsoever on its exposed back.
