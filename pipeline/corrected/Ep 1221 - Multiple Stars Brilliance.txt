Ep 1221 - Multiple Stars Brilliance

Word count: 1124

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT STONE CHAMBER]
Zane knew better than to underestimate these four Dragon-Taming Sentinels. The power rolling off them was on another level entirely; a normal warrior in the Soul Sea Realm would have been torn to shreds by a single one of their palm strikes.
Even with his back against the wall, a competitive fire lit in Zane’s gut. They both wielded the power of the Dragon Scales, and he was determined to find out just who was stronger.
The stone dragon coiled on the sentinel’s shoulder was undeniably impressive, but it was also a bitter reminder of what he lacked. 
Here, in this Illusion Battlefield, he was completely alone. He could draw on the power of his own Dragon Scales, but he couldn’t project the Cobalt Dragon’s soul or even speak to him. He could summon the sword spirit, but Lavender’s consciousness wouldn’t be there. The two allies he’d come to depend on for guidance were out of reach, leaving him to fight this battle on his own.
There was no time to hesitate. 
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
[SFX: HANDS CLAPPING TOGETHER]
The nearest sentinel was already charging, its hands clapping together. 
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
A green light flared from the stone dragon on its shoulder, flooding the statue with raw power.
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
Zane mirrored the motion, Dragon Scale energy surging through his own body. He had no intention of dodging. He just braced himself, stretching out both palms to meet the attack head-on.
The sentinel thrust its palms forward, the attack carrying the force of a dragon’s roar. 
[SFX: STONE DRAGON ROAR]
The stone dragon on its shoulder threw its head back and roared along with it, adding its own power to the assault.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
[SFX: SHOCKWAVE]
In the blink of an eye, their palms slammed together.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: BODY THROWN THROUGH AIR]
A deafening crash sent Zane flying backward. His blood churned, and a wave of nausea washed over him as he sailed through the air. The sentinel’s power was no joke.
But as he was thrown back, so was the Dragon-Taming Sentinel.
If it had been one of the ordinary statues, Zane’s strike would have blasted it to dust. But these sentinels were different. They had to be, built tough enough to contain the immense power channeled from the stone dragons. Any lesser vessel would have crumbled to pieces before the attack was even unleashed. It reminded Zane of how his own body used to be, too weak to handle more than a fraction of his own Dragon Scale power without collapsing.
[SFX: STONE SLAMMING INTO WALL]
Two crashes echoed through the inner courtyard at once — Zane hit the far wall, and across the chamber, so did the sentinel he’d just traded blows with. Deep cracks split across the statue’s body, thick enough that it looked ready to collapse entirely.
[SFX: GOLDEN LIGHT HUM]
Then golden light bled out from the cracks, and in the space of a breath, they sealed themselves shut. The glow faded as if it had never been there.
Zane wiped a thin line of blood from the corner of his mouth, frowning as he pushed himself upright. “It can still heal?”
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
He didn’t get long to think about it. The other three sentinels were already closing in, palms raised, the dragons on their shoulders lit and ready. A three-palm hit like that would put him down hard, maybe for good. Zane’s jaw tightened, and his figure tore through space, flashing past all three and reappearing in the opposite corner of the courtyard.
“This is a bit troublesome,” he muttered.
[AMBIENT: DISTANT STARLIGHT HUM]
He wasn’t out of options, though. He was confident he could clear all four sentinels with the Gods-Intimidating Strike alone if he had to — but this was as good a time as any to test something else instead. For years now, he’d poured serious effort into cultivating his Stellar Battle Body, and if it didn’t work here, all that time would have been wasted.
As starlight began gathering around him, he already had his answer. The Illusion Battlefield wasn’t some hollow illusion at all — it was a real, sealed-off world, because he could feel actual stars answering his call from across the sky. That confirmation alone made the risk worth it.
He also wasn’t about to retreat. The Illusion Battlefield had only just opened, and every genius in the universe would eventually measure themselves against him here. If he wanted an honest read on where he actually stood, this was the moment to find out.
[SFX: STARLIGHT BEAMS CRASHING]
Two more loud cracks echoed through the chamber as beams of starlight slammed into the ground. They looked weightless, but their power was anything but.
[SFX: ENERGY SWIRLING AND CRACKLING]
Three hundred beams of starlight swirled together, a chaotic fusion of crimson, orange, yellow, and every other color imaginable. The beautiful cascade quickly warped into an unstable, churning rainbow of energy.
[SFX: ENERGY SPHERE LAUNCHED]
The second the multicolored sphere formed, Zane shot it forward. If he’d held on to it for even three more seconds, he would have been the first one devoured by its power.
“Multiple Stars Brilliance!” he yelled.
[SFX: DEAFENING HIGH-PITCHED BUZZ]
[SFX: ENERGY SPHERE HURTLING]
A deafening, high-pitched buzz tore through the air as the sphere of starlight hurtled toward the four Dragon-Taming Sentinels.
“Explode!”
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[AMBIENT: BLINDING WHITE LIGHT]
The condensed power of three hundred stars, all their different energies pushing against each other, finally fused and then detonated. The resulting blast was absolutely blinding. The entire inner sanctum was flooded with a white-hot light, making it impossible to see a thing.
But deep within the ancient sanctuary, the massive Gilded Warden opened its eyes. Its huge, round face was blank as it stared intently at the wash of light consuming the inner chamber.
[SFX: STONE SHATTERING REPEATEDLY]
[SFX: GOLDEN LIGHT CRACKLING]
Through the glare, it watched as the four Dragon-Taming Sentinels shattered, again and again. The stone dragons on their shoulders broke apart, and though strands of golden light tried to knit their bodies back together, the swirling starlight snuffed them out instantly.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT SANCTUARY ECHO]
“The Dragon-Taming Sentinels cannot be destroyed,” the Warden’s voice echoed, speaking to no one. “Help me test him. I want to see how much of The Divine Path of Sealed Emotion he has truly mastered.”
With that, the Gilded Warden waved the lotus in its left hand. 
[SFX: GOLDEN PULSE FLASH]
[SFX: BODIES REFORMING]
A flash of gold pulsed through the sanctuary, and the sentinels, which should have been annihilated, were instantly made whole again.
