Ep 1075 - Stop Bothering Me

Word count: 1166

[AMBIENT: UNDERWATER CAVERN]

[MUSIC: EPIC CEREMONIAL DRUMS]

The Purification Pool was the first true test in the Passing the Torch ceremony, a place for the warriors of the Thirteen Palaces to prove their strength. For hours, thousands of cultivators had plunged into its depths, each one trying to outdo the last as their palace leaders watched on, having ordered them all to hold nothing back.

So far, Layla from the Mist Palace had set the bar. She’d slipped into the water and sunk twenty feet in seconds, the pool’s immense pressure seemingly not touching her. It was an effortless display of power.

Zane, who was also from the Mist Palace, had shown up late, and his performance was… less than impressive. He just floated near the surface, barely sinking at all. Whether he was holding back or not, the result was the same: he looked like one of the weakest warriors there, a total disappointment.

[AMBIENT: ECHOING VOICES]

The murmurs from the palace leaders above filtered down through the clear water.

“And I thought he was supposed to be some kind of prodigy. So much for that.”

“Guess he’s not too bright if he actually bought into that old legend from the Supreme Lord Sword.”

The grumbling reached Zane’s ears, but he didn’t seem to notice or care. He just caught Yolande’s eye and gave her a small, conspiratorial grin. She smiled back, a silent, shared joke passing between them that neither one fully understood.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE FLUTE]

[AMBIENT: GENTLE WATER FLOW]

Then he turned his focus inward, letting the water of the pool wash over him, feeling his mind grow sharp and clear.

[SFX: WATER SPLASH]

[AMBIENT: POOLSIDE]

The moment Zane climbed out of the pool, a man dressed in simple cotton clothes clapped his hands once.

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STRINGS]

A massive, shimmering character for the word “Seal” blazed to life in the sky, instantly cutting them off from the Orange Sea Realm outside. It felt as if they’d been cordoned off into one of the one hundred twenty-eight pocket dimensions rumored to exist within the boundary.

Zane stared, impressed. The man’s technique was incredible. He has to be halfway to becoming a World Lord, he thought, or at least have the strength of one. The sheer power on display was staggering.

[AMBIENT: LARGE CROWD MURMUR]

With the path sealed, Zane followed the crowd into a massive square. A few two-foot-long stone tablets, covered in script as small as insects, were laid out for study. At the far end of the square, thousands of warriors were already seated, each with their own similar stone tablet in hand.

The old man in cotton clothes fixed Zane with a cold look. “You — pick a tablet and sit down.” His tone made it clear Zane had not improved his standing.

Yolande leaned close and said quietly, “The easier tablets were taken hours ago. Whatever’s left will be difficult.”

Zane nodded and moved into the square, letting his gaze sweep across the remaining tablets. He picked one up and turned it over in his hands.

[SFX: STONE SCRAPE]

At first glance it looked like a metal law inscription — fourth level, from what he could tell. He hadn’t gotten to the fourth level yet, not for lack of ability but lack of time. Then he looked closer.

“Wait — this isn’t pure Metal Law.”

The tablet had six sides. Each face held the imprint of a different Law. Six Laws in total — and at the points where they intersected, the powers didn’t just meet. They merged.

Zane turned the tablet slowly, his eyes narrowing. Was the whole point of this to comprehend six Laws at once? He wasn’t sure what the inheritance trial ahead actually tested, but something told him this was the key to it.

He tucked the tablet under his arm, moved to a quiet corner of the square, and sat down.

[MUSIC: FOCUSED CONCENTRATION]

[AMBIENT: QUIET STUDY HALL]

[SFX: CROWD MURMUR DYING DOWN]

He had barely settled when a voice rang out across the square — loud enough to turn heads.

“Zane! Over here!”

It was Layla. She was on the far side of the square, waving at him, a space beside her conspicuously empty. Around her, warriors who had been deep in concentration looked up, frowning. The sudden noise in the middle of a study session was not welcome.

But no one said a word. Layla’s performance in the Purification Pool had been the best of anyone there. Half the warriors in the square had already been asking around about her. She could get away with it.

What they couldn’t understand was why she was waving at him. A sea of eyes turned to Zane — taking in his cultivation level, doing the math, and arriving at the same conclusion: disdain. A fifth-level Life-and-Death Realm warrior. Why was she saving a spot for someone like that?

Zane felt every one of those looks. He thought, a little resentfully, that none of this was his idea.

He ignored her and kept his eyes on his tablet.

“Zane!” Louder this time. “Come here! I have things to ask you!”

A few warriors actually winced. Zane put his head down and refused to look up.

That was when he heard the sound of fabric moving — then footsteps — and then Layla was standing over him, having crossed half the square with her tablet tucked under her arm. She knelt gracefully beside him and set her own stone tablet down. “Hey,” she said, her voice sharp. “I’m talking to you. Didn’t you hear me?”

“I heard you,” Zane said, his eyes still glued to the intricate writing on the stone.

[MUSIC: TENSE PIANO]

“Then why didn’t you answer me?” she pressed.

He just shrugged. “Didn’t feel like it.”

“What’s that supposed to mean? I didn’t do anything to piss you off,” she said, refusing to be brushed aside.

A headache was already starting to form behind Zane’s eyes. He really hoped she wasn’t about to make a scene, but he also wasn’t the type to be a total jerk to her. He finally sighed, tearing his gaze from the stone to look at her. “Fine. What do you want?”

Layla rolled her eyes, but a playful smile immediately took over. “So, what did you figure out from the Supreme Lord Sword’s message?”

Zane shook his head. “People have studied those words for ten thousand years and come up with nothing. What makes you think I’d find some secret?”

“But you were in there for so long,” Layla said, baffled. “And you got nothing?”

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

She just wouldn’t let it go. Zane’s patience finally snapped. “Look, can you please just be quiet? I need to study this tablet.”

“Oh.” Her smile vanished, and she looked genuinely hurt. “Fine. It’s just... if you want to understand the six types of Law Power on these tablets, you need to put in the time now. The Law Storm is a fusion of a bunch of different powers. The more you grasp here, the farther you’ll get in there.”

[SFX: SUDDEN REALIZATION]

“Law Storm?” Zane’s head snapped up.

A slow, triumphant smile spread across Layla’s face. She had him. “I thought you wanted to study the tablet on your own,” she said, savoring the moment. “Why are you asking me?”
