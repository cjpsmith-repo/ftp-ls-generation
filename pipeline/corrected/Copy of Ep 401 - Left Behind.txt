Zane couldn't see what made the lamp so special, but the sight of it left Tia completely stunned. Her breath caught. Her fingers trembled. Suddenly, everything else the Blood Fiend Emperor had left behind seemed trivial in comparison.

The Heavenly Fruit Tree and the Drake blood were incredible finds—the kind of inheritance you'd expect from a Sixth-Tier sect. Either treasure alone would have elevated Cloud Hall to the next level, transforming it from merely powerful to a force to be reckoned with.

But Cloud Hall didn't need them right now. As a Fourth-Tier sect, it was already growing at a breakneck pace, leaving similar sects in the dust. And if Tia herself grew stronger—which she would—they were already on track to become a Fifth-Tier sect anyway.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

This Enchanted Lamp, though… this was something else entirely.
Zane looked at the lamp, then at Tia. "So who gets it?"
"It's mine." She didn't look up.
"You haven't proven you're Cloud Hall. You haven't even shown your face."
"Do I look like a bad person?"
"Someone hiding their identity, sneaking into the Cloud Road, scheming for a dead emperor's inheritance?" Zane smiled. "Little bit."
[SFX: BRACELET CLASP]
She produced a token — snow-white, Cloud Hall clouds on the back, the character "Tia" at its center — and tossed it to him. A voice transmission token, direct line to Cloud Hall.
Zane turned it over. Not conclusive proof. But he'd already worked it out: only Cloud Hall had authority to open the Cloud Road.
"When the time comes," Tia said, "you're Cloud Hall. You go in with me."
He pocketed the token.

Tia's eyes gleamed with an intensity Zane had never seen before. Her usual calculating composure cracked, revealing raw astonishment beneath. Whatever this lamp represented, it transcended mere power or wealth. It touched something deeper—something that made even a cultivator of her caliber forget to breathe.

[AMBIENT: COSMIC WINDS]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CHOIR]

Thousands of miles from the Heartlands, buried deep in the Chaotic Ocean of Stars, lay a celestial tomb.

And just like the name suggested, it was the tomb of a celestial being.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS BELLS]

Turns out, even celestial beings weren't truly immortal. Their supposedly endless lives eventually came to an end, victims of the unstoppable march of time. After ten thousand, or even tens of thousands of years, their bodies would begin to fail in a series of irreversible stages: Filthy Clothing, Falling Hair, Sweating Armpits, a Stinky Body, and finally, a Restless Mind. Once they hit that final stage, their immortality would shatter, and they'd fade away just like any mortal.

These five stages were known as the Five Aging Processes.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC FLUTE]

Legend said the tomb belonged to Vesper, a celestial being who had ascended from the mortal world. After an impossibly long life—maybe tens of thousands of years—he sensed the final stage approaching: the Restless Mind, that terrible unraveling of consciousness that marked the end even for immortals. Rather than fade away in some distant realm, he chose to return to his homeland, to the world where his journey had begun, to face his end.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL UNDERWATER CAVERN]

[MUSIC: ANCIENT MYSTERIOUS THEME]

A century after his return, Vesper constructed a grand mausoleum for himself at the bottom of the Chaotic Ocean of Stars. He named it the Celestial Tomb of Vesper, ensuring his legacy would endure even after his consciousness scattered to the void.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

The tomb became a deadly siren's call for countless cultivators, drawing them in with promises of celestial treasure. Most died before they even drew close. The Chaotic Ocean of Stars had always been a forbidden zone for humans—a place where even Soul masters hesitated to tread. With celestial beings now guarding Vesper's final resting place, it transformed into an absolute death trap. Simply put, no one could get in.

For tens of thousands of years, not a single soul managed to retrieve anything from the tomb. Every ambitious cultivator who attempted the journey met their end in those dark waters. Eventually, even the most desperate treasure hunters learned their lesson and stayed away, leaving the Celestial Tomb of Vesper undisturbed in its watery grave.

[SFX: MYSTICAL CHIME]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]

Then, two thousand years ago, everything changed. Several Enchanted Lamps surfaced in The Heartlands, appearing in the hands of merchants, buried in ancient ruins, and discovered in forgotten storage vaults. They looked like ordinary oil lamps—unremarkable bronze vessels that any common household might possess. Yet carved into the bottom of each one was a name that sent shivers through the cultivation world: 'Vesper.'

Naturally, the rumors started flying.

Some scholars believed the lamps were keys to the celestial tomb's defenses—that Vesper himself had scattered them across The Heartlands, perhaps hoping a worthy successor might one day claim his legacy. Others dismissed them as elaborate fakes, clever forgeries meant to deceive the gullible and desperate.

[SFX: MECHANICAL DRAGON ROAR]

[MUSIC: EPIC TRIUMPHANT]

The Drake puppet was another priceless piece of the Blood Fiend Emperor's inheritance. The Cobalt Dragon hadn't bothered with it—a puppet with no life force or blood held no interest for the beast—but for Cloud Hall, it was a game-changer. In a war, it would be a terrifying war machine, capable of wiping out enemies with a single roar.

[SFX: WHOOSHING FLIGHT]

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

Zane and Tia climbed aboard the puppet, which then shot into the sky, soaring right over the Cloud Road. Down below, the disciples, still struggling through the trials, could only stare up in envy.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Frank saw it, too. A sick feeling twisted in his gut—the thought that Zane and that mysterious disciple might have somehow survived. Was the Drake part of the test? Some kind of shortcut? It had appeared right after they fell, which felt like too much of a coincidence.

But Frank wasn't one for coincidences. He forced the thought from his mind. There was no way they survived. Still, seeing the treasures he'd coveted fly away was a crushing blow. All the fight went out of him, leaving him feeling hollowed out and exhausted.

Like a gambler who’d just lost his entire fortune, he suddenly didn’t care what happened to anyone else.

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

Before long, the Drake puppet came to a halt at the exit of the Cloud Road. Its massive jaws opened, and Zane hopped out. He turned back. "Aren't you coming with me?"

Tia offered a small smile, but then her expression hardened. With a final pat on its snout, the dragon's mouth snapped shut. It shot back into the sky, leaving Zane standing there, completely bewildered.

He never could figure women out. 

[AMBIENT: PORTAL HUM]
