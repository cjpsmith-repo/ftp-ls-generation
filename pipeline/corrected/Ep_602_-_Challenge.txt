Ep 602 - Challenge
Word count: 995
[AMBIENT: WIND HOWLING]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
They say the mighty oak is the first to fall in a storm, while the reed just bends. It’s better to bend than to break. The strongest are always the first to go.
Olivia knew this. So did Zane.
Before he touched the Talent Tablet, Zane had no idea what he was really capable of. Sure, he had skills, but did he really need the ‘Celestial Rank’ title? What the hell did that even mean?
Still, a title like that had to be a big deal.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]
Suddenly, Zane felt a crushing presence descend on him. He turned to find an Ogre War Lord staring him down. Zane had seen them before, but their raw power was always staggering. And friendly was the last word he’d use to describe them.
Zane's brow furrowed. "A tenth-level War Lord?"
"That's not just any Ogre," Olivia whispered, her eyes locked on the creature. "Malcolm holds a seat of power."
[SFX: BOOMING VOICE]
"You little brat," Malcolm's voice boomed, rattling the eardrums of everyone in town. "Think you're some kind of hotshot, carving your name up there so big?"
Olivia's heart hammered against her ribs. This was exactly what she’d been afraid of. If Malcolm decided to kill Zane right here, right now, the humans could do nothing but stand by and watch.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]
"So what if I did?" Zane shot back, a muscle twitching in his jaw. Unlike everyone else, he wasn't about to cower before Malcolm.
[SFX: CROWD GASPING]
A collective gasp went through the crowd. You could see the shock on every face. Where did this kid get the nerve to talk back to Malcolm? Was he trying to get himself killed?
Everyone knew how this story went. Talents like Zane were destined for a tragic end. The Ogres always came for them, looking to snuff out the flame before it became a fire.
The war between humans and Ogres was their own mess. The other races just watched from a safe distance, content to stay out of it.
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
Malcolm's expression darkened. Purple-black energy crackled and coiled around his fist as he took a slow step toward Zane.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC BATTLE]
Two figures materialized instantly at Zane's sides—one in black, one in white—moving like ghosts. Fabian and Jarrod. They said nothing, but their bodies were rigid with readiness.
Malcolm's eyes swept over them with contempt. "Have you two thought about the price of getting involved?"
The threat landed like a stone dropped into still water. Everyone understood it. Even if Fabian and Jarrod could match Malcolm together, what came after would be a massacre. Every Human warrior, general, and seat owner would be purged from the Tower. The Humans would lose their foothold entirely.
That was the sorrow of the weak. Without strength, you endured. The alternative was a bloodbath.
Zane took it all in without speaking. He could read the situation well enough. He wanted to see if there was any room to turn it around.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS FORWARD]
Boris stepped out from the Ogre crowd. "Zane," he called. "I challenge you."
Zane smiled. "Sure."
He didn't hesitate for a second.
Malcolm glanced at Boris and said nothing. If Boris could kill the kid in the arena, cleanly and by the rules, there would be nothing for the other races to criticize. And if Boris failed—well, it wouldn't be too late to act then. He'd wanted to give this brat some pressure, but the brat hadn't even flinched. That alone made Malcolm's blood boil.
With the challenge accepted, the confrontation dissolved. Zane turned and walked, Olivia falling in beside him. Their destination was the arena. The Ogres followed behind Boris. And behind all of them, the massive crowd surged forward in a hustle.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
The tenth floor of the Tower of Sin was the domain of the War Generals, a gathering place for the most talented warriors of every race.
The tenth floor was a world unto itself. To even think of climbing higher, War Generals pushed themselves to the limit in secret attunement rooms, their training far surpassing anything on the floors below. The competition was brutal.
But the showdown between Boris and Zane was too big to miss. Even the most dedicated War Generals had snuck out of their attunement rooms to see it for themselves.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FLUTE]
In the stands, a young woman in a long, green robe watched intently. Her hair seemed to float on the thick air, and her eyes were as sharp as daggers. This was Cheryl of the Demon Night—a saint-level genius and a legend in her own right.
On the tenth floor, Cheryl and Boris were constants in the same conversation—the two most powerful young talents on the continent.
Cheryl normally kept to herself, locked away in a secret attunement room enchanted with a Time Law spell. Ten days of training inside was only a single day in the Heartlands.
She and Boris had fought twice, and both matches ended in a draw. But to Cheryl, they felt like losses.
Arena rules dictated that a stalemate meant an exchange of Lucky Light. And in both fights, Cheryl had wagered—and lost—far more than Boris. The memory still stung.
Pride was in her blood as a member of the Demon Night. Her one goal was to surpass Boris and claim the top spot. It was why she’d poured all her points into a time-altering practice room.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Being pulled from her training for this should have annoyed her, but the news that someone had actually taken Boris’s challenge was too good to pass up.
For a second, she felt a pang of envy. She'd wanted to be the one to challenge Zane herself.
The kid had earned the Celestial Rank title and was already a tenth-level War Fighter. He had to be something special.
Cheryl settled back into her seat, her sharp eyes fixed on the arena floor. She'd seen Boris fight. She knew exactly what he was capable of. Whatever this human boy had, it had better be enough.
