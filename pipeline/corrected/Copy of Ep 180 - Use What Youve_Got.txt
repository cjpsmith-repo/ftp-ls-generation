Use What You’ve Got
Word count: 998
[AMBIENT: UNDERWATER BATTLE]
[SFX: CHALLENGING FIGHT]
Zane twisted away from the Gillwretch’s razor-sharp claws, barely evading another deadly strike in their underwater battle.
The creature moved with natural grace while he could barely maneuver in the Heavy Water. It wasn’t just the crushing pressure, but the unpredictable turbulence and whirlpools that made every motion a struggle.
Whenever he tried to swim, he wasn’t just fighting the currents---his own movements stirred the water up even more, turning his immediate surroundings into a chaotic mess.
“The current\...” he gasped, bubbles escaping his lips as the Gillwretch circled for another attack.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
Zane channeled his Corrupt Vitality outward, allowing it to seep from his body in tendrils of dark energy. As it dispersed through the water, it illuminated the chaotic currents, rendering the invisible flows suddenly visible to the naked eye.
"Fighting these currents is pointless---they’re just tossing me around ," he thought, his heart racing. "Maybe instead of resisting, I should just go with it."
With that, Zane stopped resisting and let the newly visible currents guide him through the oppressive Heavy Water.
[SFX: WATER RUSH]
A violent surge yanked the tendrils of Corrupt Vitality away from him. In that same moment, the Gillwretch’s gleaming black spear sliced through the water toward his chest. Acting on instinct, Zane twisted his body, deliberately letting a powerful undercurrent grab him.
[SFX: UNDERWATER CURRENT]
The swift flow of the Heavy Water shot him forward with startling velocity. In a heartbeat, he found himself twenty-five feet from his previous position, the spear harmlessly piercing the space where he had been.
The Gillwretch was incredibly fast, but Zane’s quick thinking had saved him from serious injury.
[SFX: UNDERWATER CREATURE MOVEMENT]
But his relief was short-lived. The Gillwretch knew these waters, and knew the game that Zane was playing. When its attack missed, it immediately dove into the same current, barreling after him.
Despite Zane’s clever maneuver, Gillwretches had a natural agility in the water that humans could never match. Now that they shared the same current, the predator rapidly closed the distance between them, its eyes gleaming with murderous intent.
“Crap!” Zane thought, panic rising in his chest as the creature gained on him. “If we’re both riding the current, it’s still gonna be faster than me!”
[AMBIENT: UNDERWATER CURRENTS]
[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
Zane ducked his head and swam straight down. A glance over his shoulder confirmed his fear: the yellow Gillwretch was closing in at twice his speed, its black spear aimed right at him.
Caught in the turbulence, he had nowhere to go. A cold knot of dread tightened in his gut as he watched the spear shoot toward him.
[SFX: WHIRLPOOL RUSHING]
Just then, a whirlpool tore through the water right behind him, severing the current he was in and snagging the Gillwretch.
Caught in the vortex, the creature was powerless, dragged further and further away from its target.
Zane let out a breath he didn’t realize he’d been holding. "That was way too close. Thank God for that whirlpool."
But a lucky break wasn’t a solution. To actually train in Emberfin Lake, he had to be able to move freely through the Heavy Water. Otherwise, even the strongest fighter was just a sitting duck.
Everyone else must have faced this. I wonder how they deal with it? He kicked himself for not asking Leo about it earlier. Zane knew he was worlds behind the nobles when it came to information. They had their networks and were always one step ahead.
[SFX: FISH SCHOOL SWARMING]
Suddenly, a school of fish swarmed around him. Emberfins. They materialized from the murky depths, surrounding him in a mesmerizing, shimmering cloud.
“Visualizing the fishtail from memory was a bust,” Zane thought as his eyes tracked their fluid movements, “but what if I used a real one as a model? It’s worth a shot.”
He carefully reached out his arm toward the school, his movements deliberately slow and gentle. A flicker of hope sparked within him as the fish didn’t immediately flee.
Fish are incredibly sensitive, but they didn’t dart away. They seemed almost curious about his presence. Many of them simply swam past his outstretched hand, their bodies occasionally brushing against his skin.
[SFX: SUDDEN LUNGE]
This was his chance. Zane lunged forward and snatched an Emberfin, his fingers closing around the slippery creature before it could react.
[SFX: FISH THRASHING]
The fish thrashed its tail violently, desperately trying to wriggle free. But it was no match for Zane’s determined grip.
[SFX: SCHOOL OF FISH SCATTERING]
The moment he caught it, the rest of the school scattered in alarm, the dense swarm vanishing instantly into the depths. All that remained was the single Emberfin trembling in his hand, its gills flaring frantically.
Zane stared intently at his captive, studying every detail of its anatomy. He steadied his breathing, cleared his racing thoughts, and focused his mind once more, determined to succeed where he had previously failed.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
He released the Corrupt Vitality from his body, once more trying to shape it into a fishtail. With a living, breathing model right in front of him, the visualization finally clicked. In just a few moments, the Corrupt Vitality coalesced behind him.
The tail was a murky, purple-black color and still looked a bit pathetic, but it was a massive improvement over his last attempt.
Zane gave it a little flick and felt a powerful push from behind.
[SFX: UNDERWATER SWIMMING]
The faster he swished his shadow tail, the greater momentum he generated. Soon, Zane found himself zipping through the Heavy Water just like an Emberfin, slicing through the turbulence and whirlpools without any trouble.
A triumphant smile crept across his face. Darting between rock formations and swirling eddies, Zane felt a rush of exhilaration he hadn’t experienced in a long time.
After approximately half an hour of high-speed swimming, Zane noticed his muscles had loosened up remarkably. Though he couldn’t match the natural speed of a genuine Emberfin, he was definitely faster than the aggressive Gillwretch that had threatened him earlier.
With newfound confidence surging through him, Zane swished his new tail even faster and plunged deeper into the mysterious depths of Emberfin Lake, curious what other secrets might lurk in its heavy waters.
