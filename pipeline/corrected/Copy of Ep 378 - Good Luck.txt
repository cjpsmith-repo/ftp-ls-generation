To most cultivators, destiny was nothing more than an illusion. But that didn't stop plenty of them from believing in it anyway. 

Over centuries, an entire profession had emerged from this desperate hope. These individuals called themselves Destiny Masters, boldly claiming they possessed the ability to help cultivators alter their fate, to bend the invisible threads of fortune to their advantage.

The majority of cultivators scoffed at such grandiose promises, dismissing these so-called masters as nothing more than skilled con artists preying on fear and desperation. Countless cultivators had emptied their coffers hiring a Destiny Master before heading into some dangerous, mysterious realm, only to meet gruesome ends despite the supposed protections. Their gold bought them nothing but false confidence.

The debate continued to rage on: was destiny real, or was it just a fool's hope?

Men of Kenneth and Toby's elevated standing tended to cautiously believe in destiny. Their centuries of experience had shown them patterns too precise to dismiss as mere coincidence. The real challenge lay in finding real Destiny Masters, and the fakes knew better than to try their schemes on such powerful figures.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

Destiny, as it happened, was the only reason Zane had managed to escape the automaton's relentless attacks.

As it turns out, people blessed with good fortune are remarkably hard to kill. They had an uncanny ability to survive even when death's cold hand reached for their throat, when all logic suggested their end had arrived.

The sect heads and Toby were floored by Zane's extraordinary luck. Kenneth had been shocked when the young cultivator not only survived but actually claimed the coveted silver treasure box. 

Toby had fallen silent, his usual commentary abandoned as he processed what they'd witnessed. Whatever force guided Zane's path, it clearly had no intention of releasing him to an early grave.

Their world was full of wonders and terrors in equal measure. Scattered across the realm lay mysterious places shrouded in danger, ominous locations where cultivators could seize extraordinary opportunities or meet swift deaths. The question wasn't whether such treasures existed, but whether you had the courage to stake your life on claiming them.

Some disciples approached these trials with calculated caution, weighing every risk against potential gain. Others, driven by desperation or ambition, threw themselves forward without hesitation. Zane seemed to occupy a space between these extremes—bold enough to seize chances others would flee from, but guided by an instinct that kept him alive when all logic suggested otherwise.

Kenneth had witnessed countless disciples perish chasing glory. Yet watching Zane navigate impossibilities with that peculiar blend of recklessness and precision stirred something in the sect head's chest—perhaps hope, perhaps envy. The young cultivator didn't merely survive; he thrived where others crumbled.

Hearing that, Kenneth's heart soared. If Zane built a name for himself, it was good for him, too. And if Zane's performance caught the attention of the Head of Cloud Hall? Kenneth stood to gain even more.
