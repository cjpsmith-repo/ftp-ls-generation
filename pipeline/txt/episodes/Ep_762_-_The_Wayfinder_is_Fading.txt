Ep 762 - The Wayfinder is Fading

Word count: 1103

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[SFX: BLOOD DRIPPING]

 Zane’s world narrowed to the horrifying sight of warm, crimson blood soaking the collars of the girls’ clothes. It gushed from Elara’s neck, and with every weakening pulse, her life was slipping away right in front of him. His mind first went blank, then flooded with a million useless thoughts at once, none of them capable of stopping the nightmare unfolding before his eyes.

Eden was savoring every second of it.

"Don't worry," he sneered, his eyes fixed on Zane's face with open delight. "These two women will definitely die. It's just a matter of time." 

He didn't need anything else. He didn't need escape or victory. He just needed Zane's pain. That was enough.

[AMBIENT: VAST EMPTY SKY]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL MYSTERIOUS]

 Millions of miles from the Heartlands, under an endless and empty sky, a flock of strange birds circled a desolate mountain. Their wingspans were so vast they might as well have been small universes unto themselves. If you could somehow get close enough, you’d see entire civilizations teeming on their feathered bodies—warriors trained on those massive wings, all of them dreaming of one day ascending to the world above.

But even these universe-sized birds were little more than specks of dust against the mountain they orbited. The peak was a titan of stone that defied all logic, so colossal that no single vantage point could ever capture its entirety.

At the very top, a handful of figures sat in silence, their gaze fixed on the chaos unfolding worlds away. They could have been there for a day or a thousand years; it was impossible to tell. For beings like them, time was a flimsy concept—a river that could freeze solid or flood its banks in an instant. An entire millennium could slip by in the time it took to blink.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

 “We have a problem,” a young man with a feathered crest finally said. “The wayfinder is fading.”

“I feel it,” a white-haired elder beside him agreed with a slight nod. “If she dies, we’re at a dead end.”

“Then do something,” the young man shot back.

 Before either could respond, a gruff voice cut in. “I won’t.”

A heavyset man standing at the precipice shook his head. “The kid can’t even protect his own sister. Why did you choose him? He’s not the only one capable of leading the world of chaos. Just find someone else.”

 “You don’t get it,” the young man muttered. “He’s the one. He already has the Celestial Fragment.”

“Then you go help him. I’m not getting involved,” the chubby man scoffed.

“Fine,” the young man snapped. “I’ll go myself!”

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

 And just like that, he was gone, vanishing from the mountaintop without a trace.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL HALL]

[MUSIC: DARK EMOTIONAL PIANO]

 Back in the Ethereal Spirit Hall, time itself felt like an enemy. Every passing second was a fresh stab of agony in Zane’s chest as he stared at the blood still pooling around the girls’ necks. The sight of it was enough to make him feel like he was cracking apart from the inside out.

Then the light came.

[SFX: SOFT ENERGY BLOOM]

[MUSIC: UNEARTHLY STILLNESS]

A gentle radiance bloomed in the air above the hall and spread silently downward, enveloping everything beneath it. The power contained within it was unlike anything in the Heartlands — just a trace of it could unmake the entire main continent. 

The moment it touched them, everyone in the hall stopped. Not by choice. Their feet locked in place. Their eyes stopped moving. Their lungs stopped drawing breath. Even the blood seeping from the women's necks hung suspended in the air, frozen mid-drip.

Every person in the hall — Zane, Owen, Eden, the Guild leaders — stood like statues, unable to blink, unable to speak, unable to do anything at all.

Then a figure appeared in the stillness.

[SFX: SOFT FOOTSTEPS]

He looked no older than his early twenties, with an exquisite feathered crown on his head, but his eyes held the weight of thousands of years. He walked calmly across the frozen hall toward Eden, raised one finger, and tapped him lightly on the forehead.

[SFX: SOFT IMPACT]

It looked effortless. It looked like nothing. Eden turned to ash where he stood, the threads of Vitality unraveling with him, and in an instant, he was simply gone.

The young man turned and smiled warmly at Zane. The light released its hold.

[SFX: SHARP COLLECTIVE EXHALE]

Zane, Owen, and the Guild leaders all lurched back into motion at once, lungs gasping, blood rushing.

"Who — who are you?" one of the Guild leaders managed, his face white with terror.

The young man didn't look at him. His eyes stayed on Zane. "I'm going to take Elara."

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

 The young man with the feathered crest smiled, his eyes landing on Lavender. “You’re the girl from Demon Night. The one they call the Queen of Slaughter.”

Even though Lavender had earned that title over many long, bloody years, this man spoke to her as if she were a child. Zane stared at him, unable to even begin to guess how ancient he truly was.

Lavender met his gaze, her own voice pleading. “Elara is Zane's wayfinder. He needs her. You can’t just take her like this.”

 “It’s kinder to end her suffering now,” the man replied calmly. “If Zane proves he has the strength, she will guide him up the mountain when the time is right.”

Lavender fell silent. She knew there was nothing she could say. Despite her rank in Demon Night, she was utterly powerless against him.

But Zane wasn’t about to give up on his sister. He tuned them out and started walking toward Elara, his heart clenching at the sight of her. Her senses had been sealed—he could feel it. He had to break that seal.

“Don’t take another step.”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

 The young man appeared in front of Zane, blocking his path with a single raised finger. “I’d rather not scar your warrior’s spirit, but if you insist on coming closer, I’ll have to let you feel a taste of my power.”

“Your power?” Zane asked, his eyes narrowing.

The man’s smile widened. “Yes. About one ten-millionth of it.”

[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]

 He held his finger out, and concentric circles of pure light began to radiate from the tip. Zane’s face went rigid. He stared at the blinding display, a single, terrifying thought echoing in his mind.

Could that really be just one ten-millionth of what he can do?
