Ep 1048 - A Constant Speed

Word count: 1067

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT HALL]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

The second page of the scroll wasn’t exactly a secret in the Hall of Arms. It was a tradition passed down through generations of the Mist Palace: whenever a leader stepped down, they’d leave one of their treasures behind in the Hall of Arms.

These three weapons belonged to the last three leaders of the Mist Palace. But even among Grade-One Divine Weapons, there were different tiers of quality. The weapons of former leaders were naturally far more powerful than your ordinary Grade-One Divine.

The rules were simple enough. To try for a weapon on the first page, you paid one hundred Sovereign Stones and could choose any four weapons to take into the dome. But if you wanted to go for one of the weapons on the second page, it still cost one hundred Sovereign Stones, but you could only choose a single weapon.

Zane studied the three weapons. Two were sabers—the Aetherlight Saber and the Flame Shadow Saber. He’d never trained with a saber, so they were out. His eyes just skimmed over their images before moving on.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

And then Zane’s gaze fell on the last weapon. A sword.

Thundergust.

The sword was five feet long. Its blade was said to move with the force of the wind, while its strikes landed like thunder.

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

“For two hundred thousand years, this sword was my companion across the universe. When I left my post, I placed it here in the Hall of Arms. To those who have the good fortune to find it, may your own strength be enough to claim it. I hope whoever obtains this sword achieves even greater things with its help.”

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT ARMORY]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Zane read the inscription beneath the sword, words likely left by a former leader of the Mist Palace.

When he finished, his eyes were alight with determination. Without a second thought, Zane pulled one hundred Sovereign Stones from his Storage Ring and handed them to the white-eyebrowed elder.

The elder smiled. “Have you decided on a weapon?”

Zane nodded. “I have.”

“Which one? I’ll release it for you,” the elder said, his smile unwavering.

Zane pointed. “I want Thundergust.”

The elder’s smile vanished. “That sword was left by the leader before last,” he said flatly.

“Did that leader undergo the Five Aging Processes?” Zane asked.

He was just a young man, and the time scales of the Upper World were something he could barely imagine. It seemed likely the previous leader would have entered the Five Aging Processes by now.

The elder sighed and shook his head. “No. He hasn’t entered the Five Aging Processes — if anything, he’s likely attempting to break through to the Empyrean Realm.”

Zane took a sharp breath.

The elder turned back to the dome and flicked a finger. A single blue orb of light rose into the air — distinct from the white and red ones — and drifted all the way to the very top, floating above everything else.

[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]

The hall went still.

[AMBIENT: BUZZING CROWD]

“Is that... blue? I’ve never seen a blue orb before.”

“Nobody attempts the second page. Nobody. At least a red orb shows up occasionally — someone’s tried for a Grade-One before. But blue?”

“He must have just wanted to see what would happen. A hundred Sovereign Stones to satisfy his curiosity.”

“Either that or he has no idea what he’s getting into.”

Ryder’s cold smile returned, sharper now. “I’ve been in the Mist Palace for years and I’ve never once seen anyone put a second-page weapon in the dome. The ignorant truly are fearless.”

Matthew snorted. “If he’s got Sovereign Stones to throw away, he should just hand them out. At least someone would get some use out of them.”

Zane smiled faintly at that and said nothing. He didn’t have Sovereign Stones to throw away — these hundred had come back to him through the Soulbound Sect, and his entire net worth was barely above that. He’d put nearly everything he had on this attempt. His goal was simple: he was walking out of here with the Thundergust.

He turned and walked toward the center of the dome.

[SFX: CROWD CHATTER]

“He’s just putting on a brave face. I bet he’s barely holding on. I’ve seen a hundred guys just like him!”

Matthew snorted. “Yeah, right. He’ll be lucky to walk away with a Grade-Three weapon.”

As the surrounding warriors heard this, someone else whispered back, “He’s stronger than you, Matthew. A Grade-Three would be easy for him.”

Before he stepped in, one thought gave him pause. Matthew hadn’t been worth a second glance. But Ryder was different — a Soul Sea Realm warrior who had still struggled to reach the red orbs. The blue one sat above even those. Zane took a quiet breath, let his Chaos Energy circulate through him, and gently left the ground.

The gravity hit him the moment he rose.

Strong. Noticeably strong. But not absolute — not like the mineral tree space on the Divine Kingdom Continent, where the gravity had been inescapable, where a single falling leaf could drag a warrior to the ground. This he could work with. He didn’t rush. He rose slowly, steadily, letting his altitude build one measured increment at a time.

The white-eyebrowed elder, however, just watched Zane, his friendly gaze now sharpened with surprise. He had guarded the Hall of Arms for years and had seen countless peerless, over-confident talents try their luck here. But he could always sense the doubt flickering beneath the surface.

Even Ryder, who was also climbing slowly, had to fight against the gravity. He moved in jolts—sometimes fast, sometimes slow—like someone pushing a heavy car in rhythmic, straining bursts. Only someone with immense, overwhelming strength could push that same car at a perfectly constant speed.

But Zane… Zane was moving at a perfectly even pace. Not too fast, not too slow, without a hint of deceleration. It meant the gravity wasn’t even a challenge for him; his strength was more than enough to overcome it.

[MUSIC: TENSION BUILDING]

“He’s kept a perfectly steady speed from the moment he started,” the elder thought, narrowing his eyes. “Is he just showing off? Or does he really have that much power in reserve? Just how high can he go at this pace?”
