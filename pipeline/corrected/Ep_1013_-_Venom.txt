Ep 1013 - Venom

Word count: 1010

[AMBIENT: DEEP CAVE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Freya’s Snowfall Domain bought them three seconds, tops. But three seconds was all they needed to get out of there alive.

This place was a deathtrap, but high risk meant high reward: a damn good chance of finding Sovereign Stones.

Still, it was a stupid move for a newly ascended warrior to gamble their life so early in the game. Most people weren’t reckless enough to risk it all this deep in the mine. They stuck to searching for supreme Aether crystals instead of risking their necks for the Jades at the bottom.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

In the three-second window Freya gave them, they dove into the vertical shaft Zane had created, launching themselves up and away from the beast.

But the Hydra wasn’t about to let its prey get away that easily.

[SFX: MONSTROUS HISS]

The hydra’s heads all whipped toward them as the beast launched its entire body into the air. But in its blind rage, the Hydra forgot one crucial detail: its own colossal size. The hole was only twenty, maybe thirty feet wide.

[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]

It smashed violently into the opening but just kept pushing, relentless.

[SFX: ROCK CRACKING]

Realizing it couldn’t fit, the beast resorted to brute force.

[SFX: CAVE COLLAPSE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

The Hydra rammed its massive body into the opening over and over, causing the entire cave system to start collapsing around them. But even with debris raining down, the creature’s pursuit didn’t slow.

[SFX: WHOOSHING ICE PROJECTILES]

[MUSIC: INTENSE CHASE]

As they raced upward, Freya kept firing icicles down on their pursuer.

Coming from a Soul Sea Realm warrior, Freya’s icicles were powerful, but the Hydra’s defenses were on another level. Her attacks couldn’t even scratch the thick scales protecting it. The few that did pierce through healed over in an instant — the wounds closing before her eyes like they had never existed.

When it came to raw attack power, the Hydra was considered the weakest of all Legendary Beasts. It just had one unique trait that made it a nightmare to fight.

Regeneration. Even if you cut off one of its heads, it would just grow back. Zane knew all about that kind of power—after absorbing the lifeblood of the nine-headed dragon on Cloud Road, he’d gained a similar healing ability.

Then the smallest of the nine heads opened its mouth.

“Venom!”

[SFX: SHRIEKING HISS]

[MUSIC: HIGH DANGER]

All nine mouths condensed at once. Then nine scarlet pillars of Hydra venom erupted upward through the shaft.

The smell hit them first — thick, metallic, laced with something that made the eyes water. Where the venom struck the surrounding rock, it didn’t just splatter. It ate through it. Milky white foam and black smoke rose from every impact point. Holes opened in the stone walls like the rock itself was dissolving.

There was nowhere to go. The shaft was narrow, the venom was rising fast, and they were still hundreds of feet from the top.

Freya’s face went pale. “What do we do?”

[SFX: MOVEMENT IN SHAFT]

Zane grabbed her.

She pulled back instinctively. He was below her, positioning himself between her and the rising venom. For one terrible second, she thought he was trying to use her as a shield — push her down, climb over her. She fought him, flipping him under her instead.

Then she understood.

He hadn’t resisted at all. He’d let her flip him. He was lying below her now, arms spread wide, his back to the poison, and he was still ascending — still moving, still calm.

He was blocking it for her.

[SFX: VENOM IMPACT]

The venom crashed into his back.

The rock around them continued to dissolve. Black smoke curled up from the walls. But Zane didn’t flinch. The poison ran off him. His clothes burned away at the back, eaten through in seconds — but the skin beneath was unmarked. The impact of the venom hitting him actually pushed him faster up the shaft.

Freya stared at him. He was completely immune to the poison.

She had thought she understood what she was dealing with — a Life-and-Death Realm warrior, one realm below her. She had been wrong. How many secrets did this person have?

[SFX: RUMBLING EXPANSION]

Behind them, the Hydra forced itself wider. The passage groaned and cracked as the beast expanded the shaft to ten times its width, shaking the rock like an earthquake. It was still coming.

They were only alive because of the Hydra’s clumsy pursuit and relatively weak attacks. If it had been any other level-twelve beast, they would have been torn to shreds ages ago.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT RELIEF]

As the shaft widened into the middle layer of the mine, the Hydra slowed.

It stopped.

Its smallest head craned upward, eyes burning with fury, and let out a sharp shriek. “Next time — next time you dare come back down here, I will make sure you don’t leave.” The words echoed up the shaft long after the beast had turned away.

Then it retreated into the dark.

Weakest or not, it was still a Legendary Beast. Shaking it was a huge weight off their shoulders. The adrenaline from escaping with their lives was unreal. Best of all, they’d managed to collect thirty-nine Sovereign Stones.

[AMBIENT: ECHOING MINE SHAFT]

“Let’s call it a day,” Zane said, his voice calm despite everything. “We’ll split these fifty-fifty when we get back.”

Freya nodded, relief washing over her. “Of course!”

[MUSIC: CALM CONTEMPLATIVE]

They flew the rest of the way up the mine shaft together. Zane took the opportunity to examine one of the Sovereign Stones in his hand.

At first glance, Sovereign Stones and Aether Crystals looked similar, but Zane knew the difference. Aether Crystals held natural Vitality - Supreme variants simply contained more energy in purer form.
Sovereign Stones, however, condensed the world’s Vitality like fruit on a branch, offering far more than mere energy.

The stone felt warm and smooth in his palm, and it hummed with a pure Vitality that anyone holding it could absorb.

Too bad it was useless to him. Zane ran on Chaos Energy, not Vitality.

Just as he was thinking that, six or seven warriors appeared at the top of the shaft.

They were all Soul Sea Realm.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

“Hold it right there!” one of them shouted.

“Huh? This one’s just a Life-and-Death Realm guy!” another one jeered.
