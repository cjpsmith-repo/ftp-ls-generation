Ep 275 - No Mercy
Word count: 1072
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
Johanson launched his attack with lethal intent. Unlike a common fighter who might test his opponent’s defenses, an assassin strikes only once—with absolute finality. The cold calculation in his eyes betrayed his purpose.
Against any other opponent, he might have exercised restraint, perhaps allowing them the dignity of surrender or merely inflicting a disabling wound. But Zane represented something that couldn’t be allowed to exist. The young cultivator’s unprecedented talent had transformed him from mere competitor to existential threat. Mercy wasn’t an option—Zane had to be eliminated. Every technique, every ounce of power at his disposal would be unleashed without reservation.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY FLARE]
As Johanson unleashed the soul attack, a bright light flared from his hand, twisting and solidifying into a short sword—a blade shaped like a coiled snake.
The sword was a family heirloom, a Sacred weapon passed down for hundreds of years. Three centuries ago, when the Empire crushed his family, they’d hidden their treasures in a secret realm of the Shadow Group. That was how the snake-shaped sword survived, eventually finding its way into Johanson’s hands.
His preferred method was simple but devastating: first, disorient his opponent’s mind with the soul attack, then slice their throat with the serpent blade. It was nearly impossible to defend against.
If an opponent’s soul couldn’t withstand the initial assault, there was no escaping the blade that followed. And no matter how tough Zane’s body was, it couldn’t block a Sacred weapon.
In Johanson’s mind, this two-step attack meant one thing: Zane was already a dead man.
I’m destined to be a world-famous assassin, Johanson thought, a cruel smile touching his lips as he savored the imminent victory. His eyes gleamed with calculated malice, reflecting years of meticulous training from Saint Augustus. You’re just going to be another pile of bones on my path.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
The arena crowd roared with anticipation, their collective voice swelling into a deafening cacophony. Spectators leaned forward on their seats, hungry for bloodshed, their faces illuminated by the magical lights hovering above the fighting grounds.
Fighters in the arena always fought to win, and in a bout this intense, serious injuries—even death—weren’t unheard of. The Sect’s competitions had always walked a fine line between controlled combat and lethal confrontation.
But this wasn’t a battlefield where survival justified any means, and Zane usually let his conscience be his guide, preferring to defeat rather than destroy his opponents. His father’s teachings about honor in combat had remained with him despite everything he’d endured.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
This time, though, Zane knew he had to use lethal force. He’d sensed Johanson’s killing intent from the start. From the moment the fight began, it was obvious Johanson wasn’t just trying to win—he wanted Zane dead.
Zane had a simple rule: treat people how they treat you. If someone tried to kill him, he was more than happy to return the favor.
Kindness to an enemy is cruelty to yourself.
His last opponent, Adrian, had been a real jerk, but Zane had never sensed any true killing intent from him. So Zane had shown him mercy, even helping him when he lost control during their fight.
Johanson, on the other hand, was out for blood. So Zane wasn’t holding back.
In fact, Zane had been wary of Johanson’s killing intent from the very beginning. He had a gut feeling Saint Augustus and Saint Viana were pulling the strings, but he couldn’t prove it. And without proof, he wasn’t about to start throwing accusations around in front of every disciple of the Skyreach Sect.
While keeping an eye on Johanson, Zane had been trying to figure out his strategy. If Johanson wanted to drag the fight out, Zane didn’t have many options.
But Johanson’s fury over a single comment told Zane everything he needed to know: for all his power, the man wasn’t a disciplined assassin. That’s why Zane had flopped to the ground and baited him with insults—to get the disciples from the Skyreach Sect to needle Johanson even more.
[SFX: DRAMATIC IMPACT]
The moment Johanson realized Zane had blocked his soul attack, his face fell. He knew he’d made a huge mistake.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL REVELATION]
That soul attack Adrian described... that must be one of his trump cards! But how? How is that possible? His body is already insanely tough for someone at the Second Level of the Adept Realm, but his soul is this strong, too? My attack didn’t even faze him! Is he even human?
[SFX: WEAPON CLASH]
As Johanson reeled, trying to process his opponent’s impossible strength, Zane launched his counterattack: the Psyche Spike.
[SFX: PSYCHIC ATTACK]
Instantly, the Psyche Spike pierced Johanson’s mind. He felt a crushing force slam into his soul, a spike of pain so intense it nearly sent him to his knees.
But Johanson’s reaction was impressive compared to Zane’s previous opponents. He’d been strengthening his soul since childhood, and it was far tougher than most.
[SFX: DAGGERS COLLIDING — SHARP CRACK]
In the same instant, the Shattered Dagger appeared in Zane's hand — and a crack opened along Johanson's snake sword as the two blades met. Johanson threw himself back into the shadows, a line of blood across his forehead.
"Whoa, that was close! I didn’t even know Zane had a weapon! It looks like a shattered dagger!"
"That blade is no ordinary weapon. Johanson’s serpent sword is a Sacred weapon, but Zane’s broken blade just dented it without getting a scratch. That means Zane’s weapon is a much higher tier!"
"Zane is definitely holding back. I wonder what else he’s got up his sleeve."
"Ha! You’re showing your ignorance," scoffed a knowledgeable observer, their voice cutting through the murmurs. "What you witnessed was more than mere physical combat—they were fighting on a spiritual level as well. Judging by Johanson’s expression, he lost. Look at him - the guy took a serious psychic blow."
[AMBIENT: COMPETITION ARENA]
The All Peaks Competition was full of extraordinary talent from across the realm, but this entire exchange had taken less than a second. Among the hundreds watching, only a select few spectators truly understood the multi-dimensional battle that had unfolded before their eyes. Their faces registered both awe and concern as they exchanged knowing glances.
"What?" someone stammered, breaking the momentary silence.
[SFX: CROWD GASPS]
A collective gasp rippled through the crowd of disciples, the sound swelling like a wave as understanding gradually dawned on them. The atmosphere in the arena shifted palpably from casual interest to tense anticipation as the implications of Zane’s mysterious abilities began to sink in.
