[AMBIENT: QUIET TOWN]
[SFX: WHOOSHING COMET]
A comet of violet fire streaked across the sky, and for a terrifying moment, it was all anyone in Pyreton could see.
[AMBIENT: PANICKED CROWD]
Shouts erupted from the streets below.
“It’s a fireball!” someone screamed. “Gods, it’s falling!”
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: CRASHING DEBRIS]
Then came the crash—a deafening explosion of splintering wood and shattering tile as the object slammed straight through the roof of a house.
[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]
Through the settling dust, onlookers could just make out a figure wreathed in purple flames.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
“Is that… a warrior?”
Most of Pyreton’s powerful warriors had already marched off to follow their sects, leaving only ordinary folks behind. To them, the great wars between factions were just distant noise; their lives went on just the same no matter who sat on the throne. But this was different.
This was a warrior—or something like one—unconscious in a crater of purple fire that had once been a living room.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
The flames burned with an unnatural intensity, a violet wall of heat that kept the gawking crowd at bay.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
Before long, the town’s remaining muscle arrived—three warriors of the Adept Realm, shouldering their way through the crowd. They took one look at the violet inferno and froze, their confident swagger vanishing.
[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]
[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]
The sheer power radiating from the blaze was suffocating, a force so palpable they didn’t dare take another step. All they could do was watch helplessly as the fire consumed the house, reducing it to a pile of smoldering ash.
The flames didn't stop there. They kept burning.
Hours passed. More warriors arrived — stronger ones, drawn by rumors spreading through the city. None of them could get close either. The purple flames were indifferent to cultivation level.
By nightfall, the strongest warriors left in Pyreton had gathered at a cautious distance, watching the violet light pulse steadily in the dark. By morning, they were still watching.
The flames burned for a full day and night — something that only the highest grade of the Violet Core Physique could produce. Lower grades didn't generate flames at all on awakening.
The fire itself, before Cora even opened her eyes, was proof of what she was.
[MUSIC: EPIC AWAKENING THEME]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
Inside the fire, Cora's consciousness returned slowly, then all at once. She wasn't just waking up — she was being reborn. A torrent of power, raw and untamed, surged through her as her Violet Core Physique awakened fully. She found herself in the Violet Core Realm — not dreaming of it, but inside it, training there, drawing strength directly from its essence. Knowledge came without being sought. Secrets the Realm had held for centuries flooded into her, including things she had no other way of knowing.
New knowledge flooded her mind. The Violet Core Physique was rare but not unique — countless beings across the universe possessed it, with six distinct ranks classified by gradient. The grade wasn't just a measure of starting power. It was a ceiling. The gap between grades was permanent and absolute. There was no working around it, no secret method to bridge it. The grade you were born with was the grade you would die with.
[SFX: MYSTICAL FLAMES CRACKLING]
As the purple flames danced harmlessly over her skin, she felt an unshakeable certainty settle in her bones. Hers was obsidian-violet. The strongest there was.
[AMBIENT: FLAMES SUBSIDING]
As the flames finally subsided, revealing her unharmed, one of the warriors let out a low whistle. A smug grin spread across his face. "Forget rude, I like a girl with some fire," he sneered to his companions, his voice dripping with false bravado. "A pretty face just makes it more fun."
Something shifted in the air.
A muscle in Cora's jaw twitched. For a heartbeat, she remained perfectly still, the coiled tension of a predator about to strike. The violet flames had awakened something primal within her, a certainty that thrummed through her veins like molten steel.
Her eyes snapped open, glowing with an eerie violet light that seemed to pierce straight through the warriors' souls.
[SFX: POWERFUL WIND GUST]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CLIMAX]
A sudden, powerful gust of wind tore through the street, carrying with it the weight of ancient power. The three men stumbled backward, their cocky grins faltering. They looked up, and their blood turned to ice.
The sky above them had transformed into a nightmare.
Hundreds of massive, ancient swords materialized from nothing, each one dozens of feet long, their edges gleaming with deadly promise. They hung suspended in the air, arranged in perfect formation like an army awaiting command.
The smirking warrior's face drained of color. His companions stood frozen, their earlier bravado evaporating like morning mist.
Cora rose to her feet, dust and ash falling from her clothes. She cleared her throat, a small, sharp sound that echoed with terrifying finality.
[SFX: MASSIVE SWORD DESCENT]
And as one, the swords plunged downward.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACTS]
[SFX: GROUND SHATTERING]
The sound was like a series of explosions as the colossal blades slammed into the earth, shattering the ground and sending deep cracks spiderwebbing through the street. Pyreton was a small city, and the impact sent tremors through its foundations, making distant residents think it was an earthquake. The three warriors scrambled to dodge, but there was nowhere to run. They were pinned, crushed, and silenced before a single scream could escape their lips.
A strange, triumphant smile touched Cora’s lips as she rose into the air, levitating effortlessly over the carnage she had wrought. It was the first time she had ever killed anyone. The feeling was terrifying, exhilarating, and absolute.
[AMBIENT: HIGH ALTITUDE WIND]
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
High above the rooftops, the confidence wavered for a moment.
Ethereal City, or Cloudhaven?
Her gaze drifted toward the horizon, in the direction Zane had gone, and a familiar ache tightened her chest. But then she thought of her time in the Violet Core Realm, of the incredible strength now humming in her veins. For the first time, she had faith in her own power. With a final, sad glance behind her, she turned and flew toward Cloudhaven.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE THEME]
She had the power to protect herself, the power to destroy her enemies. But as the wind whipped past her, she realized she still had no idea what she was fighting for.
