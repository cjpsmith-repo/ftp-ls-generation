Ep 892 - Don't Go

Word count: 1006

Zane had no idea what kind of corpse he’d end up with, or how powerful it might be. All he knew for sure was that the best ones were on the higher floors.

[AMBIENT: ECHOING STONE STAIRCASE]

[SFX: INTENSE LIGHT BURST]

The searing Soul Purifying Light that flooded the staircase lasted for a full thirty seconds before finally sputtering out. 

War King Wind didn’t waste a single one of them. The instant the glow vanished, his command echoed in the minds of every warrior. “Move!”

[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

The fighters flanking the stairs surged forward, a wave of bodies scrambling up to the next level in the precious half-minute before the light returned. As they rushed up the steps, a familiar glow began to build from above—the light was returning.

“Back!” War King Wind commanded. “To the platforms!”

Dozens of burial huts lined the platforms of the third floor, but the corpses inside the coffins were only from the late Inner-Core Realm. The Dragon-blooded were leagues beyond the warriors from the united kingdoms, who counted Core-Forged fighters by the thousands. So where were all the bodies? 

Looking around, Zane counted maybe six or seven hundred huts in total. It meant the warriors buried here must have been important—perhaps they’d all died young, earning this necropolis as their final resting place.

[AMBIENT: EERIE CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

Zane didn't bother giving the coffins a second glance. They were all far too weak for his purposes.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

The entire necropolis seemed to hold its breath. Every warrior stood silently, waiting for War King Wind’s next command. The Soul Purifying Light was lethal to the soul, meaning the movements of over a hundred people had to be perfectly synchronized. A single misstep, even a split-second of hesitation, would strip them of their souls and leave them as mindless husks.

[SFX: COMMANDING SHOUT]

“Stay sharp!” War King Wind’s voice cut through the silence. “The light spreads from the top down. The higher we climb, the less time we’ll have.” He watched the glow dim, and the second it vanished, he gave the order: “Go!”

As one, the warriors surged up the stairs again, a flawless, unified tide of motion.

[MUSIC: SUDDEN SUSPENSE]

[SFX: STUMBLING]

But this time, someone faltered.

Fiona had slowed without realizing it, distracted by the Dragon Lineage inscriptions carved into one of the coffin lids. By the time War King Wind's order reached her, the gap had already opened.

Fiona found herself sitting on the steps, completely dazed, with Zane’s body shielding her. She’d hesitated. That split-second freeze had nearly cost her everything. Zane’s soul was resilient, but hers was a different story. Anyone who hadn't reached the Fighting Soul Realm had a soul as fragile as a newborn’s, and while Fiona’s was trained, it was still dangerously delicate. In the face of certain death, she had simply frozen. 

Looking back, she realized she could have just ducked onto a platform on the third floor to wait out the light. But it was too late for that now.

Callan lunged toward the stairs the moment he saw her fall behind. A Soul Sea master cut directly into his path and held him back — if he went down, the light would take them both.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

The other warriors watched in silence. There was no hiding the regret on their faces. A True Phoenix bloodline. A warrior who had spent seven years mastering Time Law, who could compress a hundred seconds into one, who rode a ninth-level bonded beast and stood a real chance of becoming the first empress in the history of the divine kingdoms. The thought of losing her here, on a staircase, because of a moment's distraction — it was difficult to accept.

War King Wind was frozen. His soul was the strongest among all the masters present. It didn't matter. Even he couldn't touch the Soul Purifying Light. There simply wasn't enough time.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Then Zane moved. 

Dozens of Soul Sea masters' consciousnesses flooded him simultaneously — a wall of voices all carrying the same message. Don't go. You are our only hope. The ascension passage, the lotus bloom, everything we've waited for — it all depends on you. If you die here, it ends. Don't go. 

He felt every one of them. He ignored all of them.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE MOVEMENT]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]

He reached Fiona and grabbed her — not pulling her back up, but sideways, diving for the third-floor platform. The light swept under his feet as they cleared the edge.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

The impact sent them skipping straight off the third floor and tumbling down to the second. In the last fraction of a second, Zane twisted and put himself beneath her.

[AMBIENT: ECHOING CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: RELIEF STRINGS]

The Soul Sea masters let out a collective sigh of relief, seeing that the two of them were safe.

War King Wind was flooded with gratitude. Since reaching the Soul Sea Realm, he’d had few worldly worries, which meant most of his focus was on his daughter. Whatever they found in the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land would be meaningless if he lost Fiona.

The other masters were relieved as well, though their concern was for Zane, not Fiona. He was their only ticket to the upper world. If he died here, all their efforts would be for nothing. In their eyes, Fiona’s life was a small price to pay to ensure Zane’s survival.

 In truth, if they could have stopped him from going, they would have — Fiona's life was a small price against that. They were grateful it hadn't come to that choice.

[SFX: PULSING ENERGY WAVE]

As the deadly light pulsed across the staircase again, Fiona pushed herself off Zane. Thank you, she sent, the thought a quiet, shaken whisper in his mind.

Zane just smiled. It’s nothing. Come on, let’s keep moving.

Ahead, War King Wind had paused his ascent, waiting for Zane and Fiona on the fourth-floor platform.
