Ep 59 - Defying the Great Houses

[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
"Look, Zane, you're new. I get it. You're good—I'll give you that. You took down Tristan Hayes, and that's respectable. That's why I tried to give you some friendly advice. I was showing you the smart play here. Don't make this worse for yourself."
When Liam saw his words weren't getting through, his tone hardened. He couldn't believe how stubborn Zane was being.
"Oh, really? The 'smart play'?" Zane shot back. "And what if I do make it worse for myself? I'm not interested in talking. If you've got a problem with me, we can settle it in the battle ring."
"Hey, the battle ring isn't the only way to solve things," another pledge cut in. "You guys should just take a step back. No need to let this blow up."
Zane turned to see a large guy with a round, friendly face smiling at him.
"Dustin, stay out of this. It's none of your business," Liam said, his face tightening with irritation.
The big man, Dustin, was ranked seventh among the pledges at Misty Peak. Out of ten thousand pledges, he was undeniably one of the best. A wide grin spread across his face, showing off two prominent front teeth.
"Your business? I don't think this has anything to do with you, Liam. I know you're tight with the Sterlings, but you shouldn't be defending their little lackey. The guy's a spoiled brat. He acts all high and mighty just because he serves them, like he's forgotten his place. A servant for the Sovereign family is still just a servant! Look, killing him might be going too far, but we can't afford to lose a guy this talented over something like this. It's a damn shame, but I've seen it happen before. No point dwelling on it. I've got my own path to walk."
Turning to Zane, he added, "That's what I'm talking about! You've got guts. How about we grab a drink?"
[AMBIENT: WALKING AWAY]
[MUSIC: FRIENDSHIP THEME]
Zane didn't say much, just grabbed Ethan and Rhys and followed Dustin. On the way, they were already laughing and talking like old friends.
[AMBIENT: QUIET OBSERVATION POINT]
From a small building nearby, Isla Novak watched and let out a quiet sigh of relief. The tension below had her on edge, but Zane wasn't one to be pushed around. She'd always known there was more to him than met the eye. He hadn't just beaten Tristan; now he'd managed to piss off the Sterlings, a powerful Great House. "Good", she thought. Pressure makes people grow, and the Sterlings would definitely strike back.
Isla's eyes narrowed in thought. "I'll have his back. Seraphina from the House Sterling owes me a favor." Just like that, a plan started to form.
[AMBIENT: TAVERN ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: RELAXED SOCIAL GATHERING]
As the seventh-ranked pledge, Dustin Blackwood's experience and influence naturally earned him the respect of Ethan, Rhys, and Zane. As the four of them drank together, the mood grew relaxed and friendly. Ethan and Rhys chatted easily with the humble senior.
"I saw what you did in the battle ring, Zane," Dustin said, tipping his cup back and finishing his drink. "That Core enhancement..."
Zane glanced at him. "You've got a sharp eye, Dustin."
"What? Zane, you enhanced your Core?" Ethan exclaimed, unable to hide his shock.
Zane nodded. "I would've lost to Tristan if I hadn't broken through to the Marrow Refining Level right then and there."
Dustin shook his head, impressed. "The way I see it, you keep honing that skill, you're going to be a pillar of our group, Zane. You've got a real talent for it."
"You're giving me too much credit, Dustin. I'm just a rookie compared to the real masters on this peak," Zane replied, staying humble.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STORYTELLING]
"You're being too modest. Have you ever heard the legend of Maiden's Peak?" Dustin asked, looking around with a mysterious glint in his eye. "It's ranked seventeenth among the Peaks at the Skyreach Sect, and it's all women."
The wine was loosening Rhys' tongue, but his training kept him from getting sloppy. The four of them were feeling good, but they were far from wasted, and the mood was still light.
Smiling, Dustin went on, "Maiden's Peak is seventeenth this year, but a few years back, it was second only to Eagle Peak."
Rhys had been at the Skyreach Sect a year longer than Zane and Ethan, but he didn't know much about the other peaks. He blurted out, "What? A peak with only women ranked higher than the men? No way. Come on, how could they outshine us?" To Rhys, it was obvious that men were better at Close Quarters Combat.
"There's something you don't know, Rhys," Dustin explained with a smile. "Maiden's Peak owed its rank and influence to one female disciple."
"And let me guess," Zane added, "she was so beautiful she put every other Peak except Eagle in the shade?"
"Bingo!" Dustin boomed, clapping his hands. "You hit the nail on the head, Zane! Stunningly beautiful, incredible figure. She made a name for herself within two years of joining the Skyreach Sect. So don't sell yourself short. I believe you can do just as much as she did. Unfortunately, she was framed. Now she's rotting away in Infernal Peak."
"What's her name?" Ethan asked, drawn in by the story.
"Her last name is Lowell," Dustin said, his voice dropping as he looked straight at Zane. "Just like yours."
Zane's mind instantly flashed to Elara, and his hatred for the Skyreach Sect burned white-hot.
Sensing Zane's fury, Dustin saw the anger flash in his eyes at the injustice and quickly tried to smooth things over. "The Seven Great Houses run the Skyreach Sect. The people at the top call all the shots. Still, a lot of people felt for Elara. Two of the instructors from Maiden's Peak fought tooth and nail for her, tried to appeal the ruling. In the end, it cost them everything. They were forced out."
Hearing this, Zane said it made his blood boil whenever evil people got away with their crimes, and he thanked Dustin for the reminder.
With the lighthearted mood gone and the hour growing late, the dinner party broke up, and the four of them went their separate ways.
[AMBIENT: EXERCISE ROOM]
To burn off his frustration, Zane headed straight for the exercise room.
"I'm too weak," he realized, the bitter truth gnawing at his core. "Far too weak to save Elara."
Gaining entrance to the Skyreach Sect had merely been the first tentative step. Dustin's tale served as a brutal reminder—he faced adversaries so formidable they could obliterate him without hesitation.
[SFX: VIOLENT CRASH]
Statues and dumbbells crumbled beneath his savage strikes, shattering into fragments until nothing remained but debris and his dust-covered hands. The exercise room lay in devastation around him.
[MUSIC: DARK DETERMINATION]
Hours later, Zane finally departed, his fury exhausted, his mind crystalline. Something had shifted within him—a dangerous resolve taking root.
[AMBIENT: MISTY MORNING]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS JOURNEY]
As dawn broke the following morning, he ascended the mist-shrouded ridge toward the masters' residences. The restricted tasks—those perilous missions others feared—were his only path forward. He needed to speak with the masters directly.
What awaited him at the summit, however, would change everything.
