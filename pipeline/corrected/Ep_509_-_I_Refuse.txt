The Grade-Six Elixir spread warmth through Owen's ravaged body, easing his pain. The medicine flowed through his meridians, repairing shattered vessels and knitting torn flesh. Color returned to his ashen cheeks.
Yet Owen's body was nearly destroyed. Even with full recovery, regaining his former strength would take years—if he ever reached that level again.
As the elixir numbed his pain, Owen attempted a grateful smile that emerged as a grimace. He lifted his head to look at Zane, hope suddenly rekindled.
His plan to take down Marilyn had failed, meaning he'd never reclaim his girlfriend unless Maddox released her—something that would never happen.
[MUSIC: RISING HOPE]
Discovering that someone else was also gunning for Marilyn was a lifeline. Even though he was still swimming in hopelessness and despair, Zane was a spark of light in the crushing darkness.
Zane leaned over him, quietly checking his condition. He met Owen's eyes and gave him a reassuring nod.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
"Just focus on resting," Zane said, his voice low, before walking back to the platform. He muttered under his breath, "Leave the rest to me."
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT DRUMS]
Marilyn watched the exchange, a faint sneer touching her lips. She scoffed quietly and turned away, her gaze landing on the ongoing fight.
[SFX: CLASHING WEAPONS]
In the battle ring, Brent from Ethereal Spirit fought Joel from Mysterious Luna Hall. Though Joel was more advanced than Brent, Brent had vowed to challenge the strongest fighters.
Originally targeting Eden, then Zane, Brent's plans shifted when Zane crushed Eden instantly. Now Brent unleashed devastating strikes, sending Joel crashing down. He strode to the treasure chest and drew a Challenge Card, lips curving into a predatory smile.
"I challenge Zane!" His voice rang sharp across the hall.
Zane met his gaze with cool indifference, not bothering to stand. "I decline."
Disappointed, Brent crossed his arms and slouched.
[MUSIC: TAUNTING TENSION]
"Guess you don't have the guts to fight me," the boy said glumly. He raised an eyebrow, trying to get a rise out of him. "What, scared you'll lose?"
[MUSIC: DARK DETERMINATION]
Shaking his head, Zane gave the kid an unimpressed look. He had no intention of rising to such a childish taunt. If fate allowed, he'd deal with this kid and put him in his place right after he took care of Marilyn.
[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT ARENA]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Lips pursed, Brent stomped back to the platform where his sect's members were muttering angrily. Uncrossing his arms, he gripped his challenge card, thinking miserably, I just wanted to give them a good show.
One of them sighed. "I was really looking forward to seeing Zane and Brent fight!"
"One's a fifteen-year-old at the ultimate level of the Soul Realm," a voice in the crowd whispered loudly.
"The other is seventeen and at the fourth grade of the Soul Realm," another muttered. Judging by the disappointed faces, it wasn't just Brent who was let down. The two of them were unbelievably powerful, and everyone knew it.
"Maybe Zane has his reasons for not fighting… this far into the tournament, I can't believe he's a coward."
[MUSIC: RISING ANTICIPATION]
Most of the fighters in the arena were disappointed that Zane had refused Brent's challenge. But just when they thought the match was off the table, a new wave of anticipation swept through them.
[SFX: DRAMATIC GONG]
The old man officiating the tournament drew the next lot, and sure enough, Zane's name appeared on the board again.
"Zane Lowell of Cloud Hall will now face Davis Brandt of the Black Mountain Order!" the old man announced.
[SFX: SUDDEN HALT]
A satisfied grin spread across Davis Brandt's face. But just as he was about to leap from the platform, Zane's clear voice rang out. "Wait!"
[SFX: CROWD GASP]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]
Without taking a step, Zane raised his voice so everyone could hear. "I refuse!" Reaching into his pocket, he held up another exemption card, drawing shocked gasps from the crowd.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]
"Why did he refuse again?" some fighters murmured, scratching their heads.
"Is his whole plan just to turn down any fight he doesn't like?" one of them wondered, glancing at the unconcerned man. "He must have a pile of those exemption cards."
Another fighter tilted his head, completely puzzled by Zane's strategy."Davis Brandt is at the primary level of the Core-Forged Realm. He's not that tough—definitely weaker than Eden or Brent."
[MUSIC: FIGHTING MONTAGE]
What happened next only left the crowd more baffled. Over the next thirteen fights, Zane's name was called twice more. Both times, he simply waved an exemption card, even though he was guaranteed an easy win against weak, independent warriors.
In the meantime, Marilyn fought in two matches herself, soundly defeating her opponents. After each victory, she’d chosen both an exemption card and a challenge card.
She weighed her options and decided to hold on to her challenge card for now, returning to her platform with a plan forming.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Finally,Marilyn saw that Zane was out of exemption cards. It was time for him to face the music.
She stared at Zane, a fascinated look on her face. There was something she wanted to clear up before they fought. "You know, I really don't get you."
"What do you mean?" Zane countered, frowning.
A teasing smile played on her lips. "Did you burn through all your exemption cards on purpose, just waiting for me to challenge you?"
Zane had to admit, she was sharp. Almost admirably so. He let out a short chuckle. "I couldn't get a challenge card to use on you myself." His eyes narrowed, the corner of his mouth twitching. "I had to wait for you to challenge me."
[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSE]
"Aren't you afraid of dying? Or are you just here for the same reason as that pathetic weakling, Owen? Now that I think about it, you have the same last name as that charming little Elara," she teased, testing how far she could push him. "That's not a coincidence, is it? You two must be related."
Maddox watched them face off, intrigued. He had never bothered to dig into Elara’s family background. After all, she was from the Eastern Territories, a world away from the Heartlands. He’d never had a reason to visit the Skyreach Sect to learn about her family.
More importantly, Maddox had never imagined anyone could ever pose a threat to his position. His power was so far-reaching that he hadn't paid any attention to Elara's history. Besides, the only thing that had ever mattered to him was that she possessed a Violet-Core Physique.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]
Looking back, that was why Zane had seemed familiar. He couldn't quite place him. After all, Zane had just been a nobody back then—another face in the crowd who never tried to climb the ranks.
But now, he might just be their worst nightmare.
