Any treasure capable of strengthening the soul was incredibly rare—practically priceless.
On paper, the Soul-Nurturing Heartstone was just another Mythic Weapon. But while the Heartlands were flooded with Mythic Weapons, only a handful could actually boost a person's soul. Most Mythic Weapons enhanced physical strength, channeled elemental power, or amplified spiritual energy. True soul cultivation treasures, however, remained vanishingly scarce even among the wealthiest sects.
Even for the World Merchants' Guild, the Soul-Nurturing Heartstone represented a significant investment—the kind of treasure they'd normally reserve for their most promising disciples or use as leverage in critical negotiations.
When Nate heard Kieran was giving it to Zane, shock rippled through him so intensely he had to clench his jaw to maintain composure. He kept his mouth shut, though. Saying anything would just get him laughed out of the room. The mathematics were brutally simple: compared to Zane, the Guild's own runic experts looked like a bunch of useless hacks fumbling with children's toys.
They weren't amateurs—far from it. Their rune techniques represented the pinnacle of what the Heartlands could produce. Nate himself was the only one present who could even draw a five-star rune, a feat that had taken him thirty years of dedicated study to achieve. For any other organization, a craftsman of his caliber would be a top asset, a crown jewel to be protected and celebrated.
But Zane was on a different plane entirely. He wasn't just better—he existed in a realm so far beyond them that comparison became meaningless. They weren't in the same league. They weren't even playing the same game.
Nate felt a strange mixture of humiliation and awe settle in his chest as he watched the young man who had rendered his life's work obsolete.
[MUSIC: DIPLOMATIC THEME]
Kieran’s offer was a clear peace offering, an attempt to get back on Zane’s good side.
The World Merchants' Guild had moved fast. Its three leaders had recognized Zane's potential the moment Michael Nash sent that first piece of rune work to their headquarters.
They just never expected things to get this messy. First, Zane had barely stepped out of the Dragon Soar Arena before Kieran ticked him off by grilling him about its secrets. Then, they’d run into each other again during the Martial Tournament.
Of course, the World Merchants' Guild wasn't afraid of Zane. But they were smart. They valued talent, and their entire empire was built on going all out to recruit gifted people.
The Soul-Nurturing Heartstone was an apology.
It wasn't too late for Kieran to make things right.
Zane accepted it graciously. He knew exactly how valuable the Heartstone was. It wouldn't do much for him—his soul had already reached the Fighting Soul Realm—but he could always give it to someone else. Or sell it and make an absolute killing.
After accepting the Heartstone, Zane turned to the managers. "I just finished drawing these," he said, "but I'm not a hundred percent sure they'll work."
The Guild managers carefully set the last three stones bearing Zane's four-star divine texture into place. Then, the old man took out an Array Plate to test the enchanted barrier.
Nate and the others were secretly praying it would fail.
[SFX: ENERGY FIELD ACTIVATION]
But to their bitter disappointment, the moment the old man activated the Array Plate, a powerful energy field shimmered to life.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]
After the first barrier materialized, five more followed—six layers blooming with faint radiance.
These barriers appeared far more solid than before, the result of Zane's perfect brushwork drawing out the materials' vitality to its fullest extent.
"Excellent! The repair was successful!" The old man examined the barrier with satisfaction. "Young Master Lowell, here is your payment—fifty thousand premium Crystal Cubes."
An attendant approached bearing a purple crystal nameplate. "You may withdraw fifty thousand premium Crystal Cubes from any World Merchants' Guild vault."
"[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD MURMURS]
Finally! The matches can resume!"
"Fifty thousand premium Crystal Cubes? My third-grade Order doesn't generate that much revenue in two years..."
"Someone should rob him!"
"First, you couldn't defeat him. Second, your Order stands no chance against Cloud Hall. You're courting death!"
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TRANSITION]
After the hour's delay, the old man resumed drawing lots.
The tournament matches began anew.
Order disciples maintained their advantage over independent cultivators—except for one dark horse: Owen from the Fleecy Downs.
[AMBIENT: ROARING CROWD]
[MUSIC: VICTORIOUS FANFARE]
Owen won all three of his matches, driving the arena's excitement to its peak.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]
When Owen drew a challenger card, he stood in the ring and circled slowly. His gaze swept the platforms and locked with Zane's.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
"You want to challenge me?" Zane asked.
Owen smiled faintly, then moved his gaze to Maddox's elevated platform. Two women stood there - Zane's sister, Elara, and another. When Owen's eyes fell upon the second woman, endless sorrow filled their depths, and he clenched his scimitar tightly.
"I challenge the independent warrior, Marilyn!"
[MUSIC: TENSE REALIZATION]
Zane’s eyes flashed. He glanced toward Maddox’s section, his mind reeling. Why would Owen pick Marilyn?
Owen had fought in the pre-qualifiers on the same day as Zane, who had shaken him down pretty hard with his defensive tower. But Owen, who hailed from The Fleecy Downs, was clearly loaded; he’d forked over a ton of supreme Aether crystals without even blinking.
His power was at the peak of the Soul Realm, easily strong enough to take on a Core-Forged Realm warrior. The guy's real strength was no joke. Still, Zane never figured he would challenge Marilyn.
Zane's heart stuttered. Could he have the same goal as me? Did Owen come here for her? It was more than possible.
What her relationship with Owen was didn't matter at this point.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]
"This is getting complicated," Zane muttered, his brow furrowing.
Marilyn was his target. He had to be the one to kill her, and he couldn't afford any screw-ups before he got his shot. If Owen actually managed to kill her, that would be perfect. But if he failed, Marilyn would be on high alert, and that was the last thing Zane wanted.
There was nothing he could do to stop the challenge now. And Owen, he figured, was probably just as determined.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Marilyn strode forward, her movements fluid and graceful, a charming smile playing on her lips. "Challenge me? Ha ha ha. You want to kill me, don't you?"
Her words hit home. Zane’s own heart clenched, and out of the corner of his eye, he saw a flush creep up Owen’s neck.
They’d both been called out.
