Ep 218 - Devious Allies

[AMBIENT: BLACKSMITH FORGE]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ANTICIPATION]
No one is more attuned to a weapon than the smith who forges it.
Harrison stood transfixed, his disbelief gradually yielding to awe as the sword in Zane's hand challenged everything he thought possible. The weapon pulsed with an otherworldly energy that filled the chamber, drawing every eye in the vicinity.
Masters and disciples alike had fallen silent, their expressions ranging from shock to envy as they witnessed something most had only heard of in ancient tales. The air itself seemed charged with possibility as Zane looked at his creation.
And even though Harrison was still struggling to accept that Zane had actually crafted a Mythic Weapon, the sword humming in the air before him was all the proof he needed.
Zane clasped the hilt firmly, swinging it gently through the air. A satisfied smile crept across his face as the weapon's perfect balance resonated through his arm.
[MUSIC: MAGICAL DISCOVERY]
His eyes caught a mesmerizing pattern near the base of the blade---an intricate design far more complex and beautiful than the Spirit Glyphs etched onto his own body. The closer he looked, the more certain he became that this wasn't a Spirit Glyph at all, but something far rarer---a Mythic Sigil.
A Mythic Sigil. The realization sent a thrill of excitement through his chest. There could be no mistake about what this meant: he held in his hands a genuine Mythic Weapon, something most smiths would sacrifice decades of their lives merely to glimpse.
After testing its perfect weight and balance with a few experimental movements, Zane felt an inexplicable connection forming between himself and the blade---as though it were an extension of his soul rather than mere metal. Suddenly overcome with an urge to witness its true power, he pivoted sharply on his heel and sliced the sword diagonally upward through the air.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
[SFX: ENERGY BEAM PIERCING METAL]
A brilliant arc of silver light shot from the sword's tip, slicing through the air with devastating precision. The radiant beam penetrated the steel eaves overhead without resistance. Though Quenching Peak's roof had been forged from reinforced metal designed to withstand the intense heat of a thousand forges, the luminous ray punctured through it like a hot knife through butter, leaving a perfect hole in its wake.
The blade had done this entirely on its own. Zane stared in awe, his fingers tingling where they gripped the hilt, realizing he hadn't had to draw on his Corrupt Vitality at all.
"It didn't have a name when I started," Zane thought. His eyes traced the path of destruction above, where stars now peeked through the newly created opening. A smile tugged at his lips as inspiration struck. "But now that it's complete, it deserves a name worthy of its nature. That silver light, cutting through darkness and steel alike\..."
He raised the blade before him, watching how it caught and transformed the lamplight. "I'll call it Streamer."
[SFX: MAGICAL TREMBLING]
The moment the name formed in his mind, the sword in his hand trembled.
"Huh?" Zane eyed Streamer, intrigued. They say when a magic weapon grows powerful enough, a sliver of its consciousness awakens. It can't speak, but it can forge a bond---a link between the sword's intent and the wielder's mind.
Once that intent is fully awake, it can manifest as a sword beast, and the weapon truly becomes an extension of its master.
But whether this blade would ever form a sword beast was a question for another time. Zane pushed the thought aside.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]
[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDUP]
The commotion had caused a hell of a stir. Disciples in their black and white robes were now swarming from all over Quenching Peak, crowding the gate of The Seventeenth Court and craning their necks to see what was going on. They were all dying to know which weaponsmith had caused such a racket.
In no time, the entrance was completely mobbed.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
From a short distance away, two figures sprinted into view, skidding to a halt at the gate. The men, Jerry and Mark, had come running from the Fire Pool. Seeing the crowd bottlenecked at The Seventeenth Court, they knew they'd found the source of the disturbance, which saved them the trouble of searching.
[SFX: CROWD DISPERSING]
When the disciples at the gate saw their masters arrive, they quickly scattered to make way.
"Who was forging the weapon?" Mark demanded as he strode through the gate.
But Jerry's eyes were sharper. The second he stepped into the courtyard, his gaze swept the area and locked onto Zane---and the sword in his hand.
Jerry stepped forward, eyes fixed on the blade. "Did you refine this sword?"
"That's right," Zane said easily.
Jerry took a breath. "How did you do it?"
Zane smiled. "I can't say."
The answer hung in the air. Jerry's expression didn't change, but something shifted behind his eyes.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS THREAT]
Zane saw it instantly: the raw greed in their eyes. They coveted his sword.
Jerry and Mark weren't even trying to hide it. As far as they were concerned, they were the masters of Quenching Peak. The whole mountain was their domain. Some outer disciple from another peak like Zane wouldn't dare say no to them.
If it weren't for all the disciples watching, they would've dropped the pretense and just taken it by force.
A weapon this special, and the secret to forging one---it was enough to drive any weaponsmith mad.
Jerry and Mark thought they had Zane completely fooled, but they had no idea he wasn't taking them seriously at all.
Both men were at the seventh grade of the Adept Realm. But at the same level, weaponsmiths were always weaker than pure combatants. The two of them were no match for him.
If they tried anything, Zane wouldn't mind teaching them a painful lesson. It would be the perfect chance to test Streamer's power.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]
"Get out of the way! Move it!" Jerry barked, waving the disciples away from the doorway of The Seventeenth Court. "Show's over! Stop gawking!"
Once the crowd had cleared, he turned to Zane. "Zane. Come with me."
Jerry led the way, with Zane following and Mark bringing up the rear. They had deliberately sandwiched him between them, just in case their little "fool" decided to bolt.
Not that they thought it was necessary. How could a disciple at the first grade of the Adept Realm possibly escape from the two of them? They'd already made up their minds: if Zane didn't hand over his Weapon, and the secret to how he made it, he was never leaving Quenching Peak alive.
[AMBIENT: QUIET COURTYARD]
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSE]
Jerry led Zane into The Masters' Courtyard, with Mark right on his heels. They were sure they knew how this would end.
They were very, very wrong.
