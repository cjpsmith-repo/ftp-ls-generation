Ep 380 - The Death Road

Word count: 1041

[AMBIENT: LAVA BUBBLING]

[SFX: BOOTS ON STONE]

Lava sloshed over the bare rocks as the Sea of Raging Fire receded. A group of warriors leaped down from a high platform, their boots hitting the steaming stones. They were moving fast, jumping from one rock to the next in a desperate rush forward.

Zane and Lewis were right on their heels, both picking up the pace. They kept their guard up, scanning the treacherous path as they followed the others.

Zane tried to shake Lewis, deliberately speeding up, but he hadn't counted on the guy being so quick. When Zane pushed forward, Lewis stayed right with him. When he slowed, Lewis did the same. To make matters worse, Lewis just would not shut up.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS RUNNING]

"Zane, seriously, what was in that silver treasure box on the third layer?" Lewis asked, his voice a little too eager.

Zane just rolled his eyes. Did this guy really think he was that naive?

Lewis caught the flash of annoyance in Zane’s eyes and had the decency to look a little embarrassed, but it didn't stop him. He just plastered on a smile and changed the subject. "That Hidden Test was brutal, wasn't it? What'd you think, Zane?"

Brutal? Zane thought. Yeah, that was one word for it.

Ever since he’d started on the Cloud Road, none of the trials had felt particularly difficult—not until the Hidden Test. Sure, a few top fighters had died in the earlier stages, but that was either just bad luck or they weren't as good as they were cracked up to be.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

But the Hidden Test, he had to admit, was on another level.

When he’d stumbled upon it, he’d had his sights set on the black treasure chest at the very top. Turns out, even going all out, he’d only managed to snag the silver treasure box on the third layer and the ones below it.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS DUNGEON]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The Shadow Wolf King that guarded the fourth layer was way more powerful than he’d anticipated. He wasn't about to bet his life on a fight he knew he couldn't win, so he’d backed off.

It was obvious how insanely difficult the test was. Top fighters from every sect were struggling just to claim the red treasure box on the first layer. Even the disciples of Cloud Hall—guys skilled enough to enter the Cloud Road and on par with the best from any Second-Tier sect—couldn't even get past the first layer.

The difficulty of each layer corresponded to the value of the treasure inside. You couldn't find loot like this even in Cloud Hall.

And that was the thing. If Cloud Hall had set up all these trials, would they really offer up something like the "Malevolent Cycle of Life and Death," a Tier Five Cultivation Method, as a prize? No way.

A top-tier method like that was probably out of reach even for Sir Kenneth, let alone something they’d just hand out to disciples on the Cloud Road.

So, how did it end up there? The answer probably had something to do with the Blood Fiend Emperor. Some powerful figure who called himself an emperor once ruled this place. It was a safe bet these ancient ruins belonged to a forgotten Sixth-Tier sect.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STOPPING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRONE]

Zane's thoughts were cut short when the entire group ground to a halt. The path ahead simply ended—a sheer drop into nothingness. They were trapped.

The situation was ridiculous. After everything they'd endured to reach this point, were they really going to have to turn back? Zane took a deep breath, frustration building in his chest. 

The other warriors muttered curses under their breath, their faces reflecting the same bitter disappointment. With no alternative presenting itself, most of them trudged back toward the high platform, their footsteps heavy with defeat.

Zane scanned the group, his eyes searching for a familiar face. Patrick was nowhere to be seen. "Where's Patrick?" he asked aloud, unable to keep the concern from his voice.

The platform wasn't large enough to hide anyone. Patrick should have been immediately visible. Zane felt confusion tighten around his thoughts like a vice. Patrick had gotten a head start, but Zane hadn't passed him on the path, and he certainly wasn't among those gathered here now. Something didn't add up.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Where the hell had Patrick gone? A cold knot of worry formed in Zane's chest. Could he already be dead?

He dismissed the thought almost immediately, shaking his head. No, Patrick had too much strength and skill to die on the Cloud Road. 

"He took another path," Finn said, his casual smile seeming oddly out of place given the circumstances.

Zane's eyebrows shot up. "Another path?" He stepped closer, studying Finn's expression for any sign of deception. "What other path?"

"Yeah, there is one." Finn gestured vaguely toward the distance, his tone almost dismissive. "Frank, Lois, Patrick, and a couple of other disciples took it. It's just a trail of broken rocks over there—scattered stepping stones across the lava, really. Doesn't look that difficult to cross, at least not from here."

Zane's mind raced. If the path existed and appeared manageable, why would anyone choose to turn back? He narrowed his eyes at Finn, sensing something unspoken lurking beneath the surface.

"Then why didn't you take it?" Zane pressed, his voice hardening with suspicion. "If it's so easy, why are you standing here instead of moving forward?"

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

"Because that path is called the Death Road," a disciple from Cloud Hall cut in, his voice cold and sharp.

Finn nodded grimly. "According to the Cloud Hall disciples, there's a fork in the road up ahead. One path is the Life Road, the other is the Death Road," he added. The Cloud Road was just full of surprises.

[AMBIENT: EERIE WIND]

Almost everyone chose the Life Road when they reached the fork. In the past, a few disciples had tried their luck on the Death Road, but none of them ever came back.

Over time, people pretty much forgot it even existed, assuming the Life Road was the only way forward.

[MUSIC: DARK FOREBODING]

The so-called "Death Road" wasn't really a path at all—it was a trap. A one-way ticket to a dead end that no one had ever survived.

So why the hell had Patrick taken it?
