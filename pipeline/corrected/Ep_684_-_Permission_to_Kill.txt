Ep 684 - Permission to Kill

Word count: 1071

The teleportation array had deposited Zane at the bottom of the Chaotic Ocean of Stars, and he was already drawing the wrong kind of attention.

[SFX: WATER RUSH]

Bernard hesitated for a second before giving the signal to fall back. In a flash, the five warriors shot toward the surface and vanished from sight.

Even with his eyes closed, Zane knew. He could feel the lingering hostility of the five Soul Realm warriors who had just targeted him. It was just his luck. His first trip to the main continent had ended with a violent voidstorm slamming him into the Heavenly Eagle Prairie. Now, on his way back, he’d managed to run right into another one.

This new storm had been far more intense, but he was stronger now. With his improved cultivation and mastery of the Spatial Law, he’d avoided serious injury. The real problem was the raw spatial energy the storm had forced deep into his body—a volatile power that would take a massive effort to purge.

They knew their limits, at least, Zane thought, his eyes twitching beneath their lids.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Just then, he sensed three more people approaching and frowned. So much for the Chaotic Ocean of Stars being deserted. It was getting awfully crowded down here.

These three newcomers were also from the Soul Realm. As they drew near, they dove straight for the reef where he was resting.

"Tessa, I found one! A Duskshell Clam, right at the bottom of that reef!"

The voice, muffled by the water, was surprisingly female. Three of them, it turned out. Zane realized they weren't independent warriors, but inner disciples of the Sea Wyrm Sect, likely completing a trial. After swallowing their aqua-breath pills, the three women swam deeper, their shimmering silks trailing behind them like the fins of graceful mermaids.

[SFX: SUDDEN GASP]

"You're right, there's a Duskshell Clam hiding under the—Aah!" One of the girls, mid-sentence, let out a choked scream. Her eyes were wide with panic, fixed on the shadows beside the reef.

Confused, the other two followed her gaze and finally saw him—a man, sitting cross-legged on the ocean floor, meditating.

[AMBIENT: UNDERWATER CAVE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

"What is someone doing all the way down here?" one whispered through the water.

"You think he's… cultivating?"

"This is bizarre."

"Wait a minute," Tessa said, leaning closer. "Look at him. He’s not just meditating. I think he's hurt."

She reached out and pressed a finger gently to his arm, checking for warmth. Alive. The three of them put their heads together, then — with considerable effort — combined their strength and hauled him up onto a nearby reef.

"He doesn't look that heavy," one of them panted.

"Well, he is," another said.

"His eyes are still closed. He looks like a statue."

Zane sat on the reef and said nothing. There were all kinds of people in the world. These three were so naive it was frightening — the kind of naivety that got people eaten alive out here.

They were clearly talented, but their sheltered lives in a second-rate sect had left them hopelessly naive.

[AMBIENT: UNDERWATER TENSION]

Zane's frown returned. He could sense them now — the same five Soul Realm warriors from before, and with them, three others. Soul Sea Realm warriors. So that was their move. Too afraid to act alone, they'd gone to find someone stronger and cut them in on the score.

Just then, Elder Holt appeared with two of his men. "Kill him! I don't care how badly he's hurt!"

[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

The warriors immediately focused their Vitality, preparing to strike Zane. Zane’s eyes narrowed, and he tensed, ready to counter. But just as the attack was about to launch, a surprised cry cut through the water. The three young women had returned, positioning themselves between Zane and his attackers.

[SFX: WATER RUSH]

"Elder Holt, what are you trying to do?" Tessa demanded.

Elder Holt's expression didn't change. "Leave," he said flatly. "This man is mine to deal with."

"We're not leaving," Tessa said. "We'll wait until he wakes up."

"I said leave!" Holt snapped. He was a sect elder. That still meant something. The three women exchanged looks, then flew off with resentment written plainly across their faces.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]

The old man just snorted and nodded toward Bernard and his two cronies, who had also returned. "They have a death wish," Holt said dismissively. "They're your problem now."

Bernard's face lit up. "What do you mean?" he asked, barely containing his excitement.

Elder Holt just shook his head, his voice cold and flat. "Do I have to spell it out for you? Get rid of them."

A sick energy buzzed through Bernard and his men. Since joining the Sect, they'd been forced to play by its rules, their darker impulses held in check by strict codes of conduct. Permission to kill three powerful women from the Soul Realm was a twisted fantasy they never thought they'd get to live out—a chance to indulge every violent urge they'd suppressed for years.

[MUSIC: AGGRESSIVE TENSION]

The five of them charged, their faces twisted with anticipation, barely trying to conceal their murderous intent.

The three girls might have been naive, but they weren't stupid. One glance at the predatory gleam in their pursuers' eyes told them everything. They knew exactly what would happen if they were caught—knew it with a certainty that turned their blood to ice.

"We have to go!" Tessa yelled, a grim fire blazing in her eyes despite the terror clawing at her chest.

[SFX: WATER RUSHING]

[MUSIC: FRANTIC CHASE]

They spun around and kicked for the surface in a desperate bolt for freedom, their movements frantic but coordinated. Water churned violently around them as they pushed their cultivation to its limits, every muscle screaming with effort.

Their pursuers laughed, a nasty, jeering sound that carried through the water like poison. "Go back?" Bernard taunted, his voice dripping with cruel amusement. "You really think you're getting out of here?"

At the same time, Elder Holt finally made his move on his original target, his patience exhausted.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

[SFX: WEAPON SWOOSH]

He lunged, thrusting his spear straight for Zane's heart with lethal precision. The weapon cut through the water like a silver streak, aimed to kill.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]

In that instant, Zane's eyes snapped open. He met the gaze of the elder and his two men with a cold smile, as if he were looking at three corpses—men already dead, who simply hadn't realized it yet.

There was no fear in his expression, only a chilling certainty that sent an involuntary shiver down Holt's spine.
