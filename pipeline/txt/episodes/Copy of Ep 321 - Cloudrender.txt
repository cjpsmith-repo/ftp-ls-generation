The tournament had Elara on edge, her knuckles white with tension. But the moment she caught sight of her brother Zane’s confident smile—that familiar glint in his eyes—her anxiety melted away. He wasn’t improvising; he had orchestrated this entire scenario.
Following his subtle lead, she gracefully presented the Cloudrender to Evan, noting how the crowd hushed in anticipation.
Evan seized the sword with barely concealed eagerness, his fingers tracing the length of its peculiar snow-white blade with familiar reverence. The weapon defied conventional understanding—crafted from some strange, wood-like material rather than traditional metal. Yet when he tapped it experimentally against his palm, it sang with the unmistakable crystalline resonance of the finest steel, sending shivers down his spine.
[SFX: STEEL PING]
"Well, what choice did I have?" he declared with a theatrical shrug, masking his relief behind bravado. "I couldn’t use my Vitality, and my Mythic weapon was moments from shattering into a million worthless pieces. Things are going to be very different now that I have the Cloudrender !" His eyes narrowed with renewed determination. "This magnificent blade won’t break on me. Let’s see you block this, Zane!"
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
With a practiced flick of his wrist, Evan transformed the snow-white sword into a dazzling blur of motion, its afterimages painting ephemeral circles in the air like ghostly calligraphy. The audience gasped collectively at the display of masterful swordsmanship.
In response, Zane simply gave his Streamer a slight, almost dismissive shake, his posture relaxed yet vigilant. The corner of his mouth lifted in the barest hint of a smile—the calm demeanor of a chess master who had foreseen his opponent’s moves many steps in advance.
"Go ahead," he said, his voice casual yet carrying an undercurrent of absolute certainty that echoed throughout the arena.
[SFX: MAGICAL SWORD ACTIVATION]
Evan raised the Cloudrender and gave it a shake, unleashing a torrent of pure white light from the blade.
"Try and stop this!" he yelled.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY WAVES]
Billowing waves of light rolled out like clouds, rushing toward Zane with lethal intent hidden beneath their ethereal beauty. The radiance danced through the air, deceptively elegant yet carrying enough force to cleave through stone.
The tournament regulations were strict: competitors could only use swords. No Vitality manipulation, no additional attunements. These restrictions had forced Evan to restrain himself with his earlier Mythic weapon, knowing its fragile construction would crumble against the superior craftsmanship of Zane’s Mystic-grade Streamer. Fear of destruction had tempered his strikes, leaving his true potential leashed.
But now, with Cloudrender’s reassuring weight in his palm, Evan let go of his hesitation. A newfound confidence gleamed in his eyes as his attacks accelerated, each strike flowing into the next with increased vigor and precision. The white wooden blade hummed through the air, trailing luminous arcs that captivated the audience.
Despite this renewed onslaught, Zane kept up his composed defense. His expression was unreadable as he executed the same circular parry with meticulous timing, his movements economical yet graceful. The Streamer traced perfect arcs through the air, meeting each of Evan’s attacks with calculated precision rather than brute force.
[SFX: SWORD IMPACT]
[SFX: SWORD IMPACT]
A hollow resonance echoed through the arena each time Cloudrender met Streamer, sounding almost like a persistent woodpecker drilling into a tree. The distinctive sound punctuated their deadly dance, drawing murmurs from the captivated crowd.
"You looked so confident, I genuinely believed you’d figured out some brilliant strategy," Evan said, his brow furrowing as he adjusted his grip on the luminous blade. "But here you are, just defending yourself like a frightened novice. Honestly, I’m disappointed. I expected more from someone who’s caused such a stir."
He let out a soft chuckle. "You're just delaying the inevitable. Once I back you to the edge of the arena again, it's over. Don’t waste my time. Just give up. It’ll save you what’s left of your dignity."
[SFX: MAGICAL SWORD ENERGY]
Once again, waves of radiance spread from his sword like clouds. He swung the blade effortlessly, as if it weighed nothing.
Watching from the sidelines, Elara felt a knot of confusion. Zane had seemed so sure of himself—she was positive he'd found a way to counter the Sword of a Thousand Blossoms. But Evan was the top-ranked fighter in the Skyreach Sect for a reason. His swordsmanship was his greatest pride. People could laugh at how goofy he acted, but no one ever questioned his skill.
But what could Evan do? He was getting more and more frustrated. Zane was completely shutting down his offense, leaving him zero openings.
If he could just use his Vitality, he could blast Zane’s sword aside and cut him to ribbons. But the rules were clear: the second he channeled his Vitality, he’d forfeit the match.
Am I really worse than him when it comes to pure sword skills? Evan thought, a flicker of doubt crossing his mind.
[AMBIENT: CROWD CHATTER]
A buzz rippled through the crowd.
"The tables have turned! Zane is actually schooling Evan in pure swordplay!"
"Zane’s basic technique is so weird! If I could learn that, maybe I could take on Evan too, just sword against sword!"
"You’re all talk. Why don’t you get up there and try it?" someone shot back. "Everyone thinks Evan’s a clown, but you’re the real fool if you underestimate him. He’s been number one for a reason."
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Zane had completely reversed the momentum, shocking the spectators who watched with bated breath. Mere moments earlier, Evan’s ferocious onslaught had driven Zane across every corner of the arena, yet throughout the barrage, Zane maintained an impossible serenity. His patience bordered on supernatural—each movement deliberate, each step calculated—striking only when retreat was no longer an option.
From her position in the crowd, Elara watched the transformation with growing admiration. A slow, knowing smile crept across her face as she watched her brother’s strategy unfold. She knew he had a plan, but even she hadn’t anticipated such a masterful shutdown of Evan’s techniques.
[SFX: MAGICAL SWORD ENERGY]
Now, with the rhythm of battle dramatically shifted, Zane advanced with measured aggression. His blade sang through the air with newfound authority as he methodically forced Evan backward. Each thrust and parry drove his opponent closer to the arena’s edge, the renowned prodigy’s confidence visibly crumbling with each retreating step.
The corner that waited for Evan seemed not just physical but psychological—a trap that his reputation might never come back from.
