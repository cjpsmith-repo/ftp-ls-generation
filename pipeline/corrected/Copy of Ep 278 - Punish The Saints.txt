Ep 278 - Punish The Saints
Word count: 1179
[SFX: POWERFUL GROWL]
A thunderous growl tore through the air, striking everyone’s ears with jarring clarity. The sound traveled impossibly far, reaching not only the disciples enduring punishment on Infernal Peak but also members of all Thirty-Three Peaks—hundreds of thousands of individuals experiencing the same bone-chilling roar at the exact same instant.
"Who was that? What kind of creature could have made such an awful sound?" whispered one disciple, clutching his trembling hands against his chest.
"It’s coming from the base of the mountain!" replied another, her voice quavering, "How is it still so loud from that far away? Is a human even capable of a sound like that?"
The outer disciples’ faces drained of color, their eyes widening with fear. Many recalled ancient texts describing legendary beings whose mere voices could shatter stone and bend steel—beings they had dismissed as myths until this moment.
Throughout the Thirty-Three Peaks, even the most confident disciples fell into stunned silence. Those surrounding the arena froze in collective dread, their bodies tensing instinctively against whatever might follow. Some of the younger ones clutched at their seniors’ robes, seeking reassurance that none could honestly provide.
[SFX: INTENSE ROAR]
But the sound continued to swell, reaching an incredible peak. The force of the roar alone knocked many disciples, and even some of the masters, flat on their backs.
Only a handful managed to stay on their feet, like Finn, Wynn, and a few personal disciples. But even their faces were pale, and it was clear that just resisting the powerful sound wave was draining their Vitality.
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
This wasn’t just sound—it was highly condensed energy. Ninety-nine percent of it focused into a brilliant yellow ripple that shot directly into the arena, shattering the purple light.
At the same time, Johanson stumbled out from the shadows where he'd been hiding. The bright yellow ripple had torn past him, and the force of the attack was devastating. His face was ashen as he hit the ground, coughing up a mouthful of blood.
The sound wave was perfectly aimed. It struck its target with pinpoint accuracy, then vanished the instant it destroyed the purple light in front of Zane.
Zane felt the shockwave too, but he wasn’t seriously injured.
He realized the shout was meant to stop the match. But who did that? he wondered. That kind of power is unbelievable. He was floored, but also grateful the attack hadn’t been aimed at him. A direct hit would have torn him to pieces.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION]
A moment later, a middle-aged man materialized out of thin air, his form shimmering as he transitioned from transparent to solid matter. He hovered elegantly above the ground, his robes barely stirring despite the residual energy crackling through the arena.
Sir Kenneth, the leader of the Skyreach Sect, surveyed the scene with piercing eyes that reflected centuries of accumulated wisdom. His face, though composed, betrayed a flicker of controlled rage at the violation of sacred traditions under his watch.
Master Elwood appeared right behind him.
"It must have been the leader who made that roar."
"That’s incredible... The leader’s roar just saved Zane! But why would he help him?"
"Is that the leader’s true power? It’s staggering. If he hadn’t focused that sound wave, it would have killed every single one of us!"
The referees and several elders bowed respectfully to Kenneth. "Sir!" they all said in unison.
What the watching disciples didn't know was that several of those elders had seen through the attack the moment it appeared. The purple light wasn't Johanson's — it was far beyond his level, and anyone with eyes and experience knew it. But they had said nothing. Johanson was a personal disciple. Zane was a boy from a humble background with no patron and no faction. The calculation wasn't complicated.
Sir Kenneth was the only one who hadn't made that calculation.
Zane lifted his head and looked at Kenneth. He stood there in silence, a storm of emotions churning inside him.
Zane could only stare, dumbfounded. The highest levels of martial arts were more powerful than he could have ever imagined. When would he ever be strong enough to reach the leader’s level?
[SFX: POWERFUL COMMANDING VOICE]
Sir Kenneth gave Zane a casual glance, then his expression hardened into stone. "Viana, Augustus, you old bastards, get out here now!" he boomed.
The disciples were completely lost.
Viana and Augustus? Was he talking about Saint Viana and Saint Augustus, two of the Ten Great Saints of the Skyreach Sect? Had the leader roared just because of them? 
The two saints staggered forward, their sage-like composure completely gone.
Sir Kenneth looked at them with cold eyes. "I don't know who gave you the courage," he said, his voice low and steady and somehow more frightening for it. "Whether you're as stupid as dogs, or whether you've simply forgotten that I exist — to dare to kill a disciple in the All Peaks Competition, in front of everyone, so brazenly."
Most disciples had already seen what happened on the Scrying Slate. All of them, from the newest recruits to the most experienced members, were seething with rage.
"What is going on? Why the hell would Saints try to kill someone during the All Peaks Competition?"
"They’re always going on about how the Skyreach Sect has the strictest rules. Looks like those rules only apply to us regular disciples. The Saints can just do whatever they want!"
"Punish them! The same way you’d punish any of us! They can’t get away with this, even if they are Saints! They should be executed! Executed! Executed!"
The disciples of the Thirty-Three Peaks were all furious. But the competition grounds were too far away; none of them had the power to project their voices the way Sir Kenneth had.
"Sir, please, let me explain," Saint Augustus pleaded. "It’s true, I interfered. But I wasn’t thinking straight. It was Saint Viana, he pushed me into it."
Saint Augustus wasn’t a cruel man by nature. He had hesitated before acting, but Saint Viana had pressured him.
"Augustus, don’t you dare try to pin this on me," Saint Viana shot back. "Johanson is your disciple. You were the one who used the purple light to attack Zane. Why are you trying to drag me into this?"
Saint Viana had to distance himself from the situation. But as he spoke, he saw the murderous intent in Sir Kenneth’s eyes.
[MUSIC: TENSE REVELATION]
It hit him then: Kenneth was actually going to kill them.
They had tried to assassinate a disciple during the All Peaks Competition, with every member of the Skyreach Sect watching. If they weren’t punished, the Sect’s reputation would be ruined.
And the price for breaking rules that sacred? The answer was obvious.
[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]
Realizing this, Saint Viana shot into the air and fled into the distance.
Unlike Saint Augustus, who had hesitated until the last moment, Saint Viana was decisive and ruthless. He knew staying meant certain death. His only chance was to run.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CONFRONTATION]
Seeing Saint Viana flee, Sir Kenneth sneered. He raised a powerful arm and thrust his hand through the air, aiming directly at the escaping Saint.
