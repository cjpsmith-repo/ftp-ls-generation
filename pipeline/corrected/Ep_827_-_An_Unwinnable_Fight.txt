Ep 827 - An Unwinnable Fight

Word count: 1059

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The Little Monster didn't seem too worried about Zane absorbing its innate gift. As far as it was concerned, Zane was no greedier than the powerful warriors from the Soul Sea Realm who were still stuck on the stairs. 

Besides, it had promised the Holy Maiden it would win this fight, and it had no intention of letting Zane leave this place alive.

[SFX: WHOOSHING SPEARS]

[SFX: BARRIER IMPACT]

Hundreds of golden spears materialized and shot forward, riddling the enchanted barrier with holes. The mighty warrior from the Soul Sea Realm could only scramble to repair the damage, but new holes kept appearing as fast as he could seal them. 

The Little Monster’s assault was relentless, and it looked like Zane was trapped for good. In reality, the creature was just blowing off steam; the barrage wasn't causing Zane any real trouble.

[MUSIC: DARK CRESCENDO]

After a few moments, a strange, cruel smile crept across the Little Monster's face. “That's enough,” it hissed. “Now, it's time to send you to Hell.”

[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT WHOOSH]

In a flash, it shot forward, closing the distance until it was less than two feet from Zane.

[SFX: PUNCH IMPACT]

[SFX: ENERGY DISSIPATION]

Zane’s eyes narrowed as he threw a punch, but the creature simply raised a hand and blocked it. Just like that, all the force behind Zane’s attack vanished into thin air.

Now it was Zane’s turn to be shocked. How is that possible?

Wary of the monster's flexibility, Zane had unleashed eight hundred Dragon Scales—seventy to eighty percent of his full power. Even a top-tier Soul Sea Realm warrior couldn't have blocked such force, yet the creature absorbed it effortlessly, as though Zane's strike carried no more weight than a child's tap.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

[AMBIENT: RUMBLING MOUNTAIN]

Suddenly, a deafening explosion ripped through a mountain near the West Dragon River.

The massive mountain range, towering over ten thousand feet high, stood beside the river. Paired with a mountain on the opposite bank, it formed a canyon known as Dragon’s Maw Gorge, a famous landmark on the Divine Kingdom Continent. The peak erupted with a powerful force, and moments after the explosion, the entire range began to crack and collapse from its center.

[AMBIENT: RUMBLING MOUNTAIN COLLAPSE]

[SFX: CRASHING ROCKS AND WATER]

Huge boulders tumbled down into the West Dragon River, sending massive waves crashing through the churning water until the entire mountain had come down.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

Back in the arena, Zane gritted his teeth. He pulled his fist back and this time channeled everything — every Dragon Scale, every drop of Vitality — into a single strike. A punch that could kill a Soul Sea Realm warrior outright.

The Little Monster didn't dodge. It raised its palm.

[SFX: MASSIVE PUNCH IMPACT]

[SFX: ENERGY DISSIPATION]

The force vanished. Again.

[SFX: SECOND MOUNTAIN COLLAPSE]

On the opposite bank of the West Dragon River, the second mountain of Dragon's Maw Gorge exploded from within and began to crumble, boulders crashing into the churning water below.

“What's going on?” someone wondered aloud from afar. “There isn't a soul on the West Dragon River. How did that mountain just collapse on its own?”

“I don't know — but did you see what Jones just did in there? That punch would have leveled a Soul Sea Realm warrior. And the Little Monster just swatted it away.”

The Little Monster grinned at Zane. “Interesting, isn't it?”

“The power is being transferred,” Zane said, frowning.

The Little Monster chuckled. Then, without warning, it turned its back on Zane entirely and faced the watching kingdoms.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

“You call yourselves geniuses?” it called out, black eyes sweeping the stairs. “It takes you thousands of years to reach the Soul Sea Realm. Thousands of years. You're not geniuses. You're a pile of garbage.” It cackled. “And this one — “ it jerked a thumb at Zane — “this one just came from nowhere and made all of you look like fools. If he's garbage, what does that make you?”

The laughter echoed through the arena. Not one Soul Sea master spoke. There was nothing to do but endure it.

“What's happening?” Zane asked quietly.

Lavender's voice came back measured and grim. “He's the Son of the Plane. The plane itself is absorbing the damage for him — the same way a Realm Lord uses their domain. When two Realm Lords fight, the stars in their worlds explode. He's not a Realm Lord, but he's the plane's incarnation. Your power hits him and gets transferred into the world itself. You can't destroy the entire world.”

“So he really is unkillable,” Zane said.

“Not exactly. If you ever reach Soul Sea Realm and your domain is strong enough, you can envelop him in it — cut him off from the plane's will. Then he's just a body.” She paused. “But that's a long way off.”

The Little Monster scanned the watching kingdoms, disappointed. It had hoped its display would provoke someone—anyone—into challenging it, providing an excuse for a brutal beatdown. But everyone remained silent.

No one took the bait.

[MUSIC: MENACING CRESCENDO]

The Little Monster turned to stare at Zane, a mad glint in its black eyes. “I forgot,” it purred. “Our fight isn't over yet.”

Hearing this, Zane instinctively backed away.

“Scared? It's too late,” the Little Monster said with a menacing smile. “Your talent is so much higher than those pigs. I bet you taste a lot better than they do.”

With that, it lunged, baring a set of razor-sharp teeth.

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

A cold dread seized Zane, paralyzing him. If this thing could truly nullify his full power, then he had no way to win.

His mind raced, putting the pieces together. With his current strength, he could topple mountains and split rivers. But the monster’s body was a portal to the world of chaos, an immense, endless void. 

When Zane’s power poured into the creature, it wasn't being blocked; it was being transferred, diluted into an entire universe. And there was no way Zane could destroy the entire universe. Even the masters of the Soul Sea Realm couldn't do that, let alone him.

Maybe the person who took Elara from the Ethereal Spirit Sect had that kind of power, he thought grimly.

[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: DESPERATE CHASE]

There was no point in attacking again. Acting on pure survival instinct, Zane retreated, trying to put as much distance as he could between himself and the monster. But in the tight confines of the enchanted barrier, how could he possibly dodge its attack?

[SFX: ECHOING SHOUT]

Just as the creature was about to close in, the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom shouted, his voice echoing with desperation, “Zane! The Harlen Divine Kingdom admits defeat!”
