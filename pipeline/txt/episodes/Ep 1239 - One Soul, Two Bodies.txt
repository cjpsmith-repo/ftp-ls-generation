Ep 1239 - One Soul, Two Bodies

Word count: 1043

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ARENA]
A grin spread across Zane’s face the moment he saw Sloane’s Five-Element Palm Print. He immediately unleashed the power of thirty thousand Dragon Scales and met her attack with a palm strike of his own. It looked like a simple, ordinary counter, but it pulsed with an incredible amount of raw power.
The truth was, Sloane’s attack was a desperate, last-ditch effort. Just as the onlookers had guessed, she wasn’t fighting at her full strength. Her power was literally split in two. 
Somewhere out in another of the upper worlds within the Illusion Battlefield, a second version of Sloane, named Laney, was fighting her own battles. They were one soul in two bodies.
While such techniques were rare, they weren’t unheard of. It was similar to the attunement Yolande had used to split her soul into a million pieces, though Sloane’s was far more practical. After Yolande split her soul, all the fragments could do was seek enlightenment. 
Sloane’s two bodies, however, could train and grow their powers separately. When they finally merged back into one, she would become whole, revealing her true power as the top talent of House Kincaid.
But right now, separated from Laney, Sloane could only access her Earth and Metal Law powers. Her other three Original Laws were pitifully weak, which meant her Five-Element Palm Print was incomplete and fundamentally flawed. 
If Laney had been here, with her completely different fighting style, the outcome of this fight might have been different. When they merged, their strengths and weaknesses would balance out, giving Sloane perfect mastery over all five elements. But she wasn’t here. 
 And under the crushing force of Zane’s assault, Sloane’s attack began to crumble.
“It’s over,” Zane said, a faint smile on his lips. After shattering her palm print, he gently placed his hand on her forehead. The gesture was so soft, it looked as if he were simply brushing a bead of sweat from her brow.
[SFX: MASSIVE ENERGY RELEASE]
But with a quiet flick of his wrist, a massive, invisible force erupted. 
Before everyone’s eyes, Sloane’s body simply dissolved into nothing.
[SFX: SOUL WHOOSH]
[SFX: EXPLOSION OF ENERGY]
A red light, her soul, shot back toward the main city as a cloud of Illusion Points exploded from where she had stood, swelling to twenty or thirty feet wide. The warriors watching from the city walls stared, their eyes practically popping out of their heads. As the “lord” of her city, Sloane had eight “butchers” gathering Illusion Points for her day and night. The amount she’d amassed was staggering.
[SFX: RUSHING WIND STREAKS]
The points didn’t linger. They streaked toward Zane in a rush and sank straight into his body. He felt his total surge upward, and in the same instant, his position on the pyramid began climbing at a dizzying rate.
This… Zane thought, momentarily stunned by the number now sitting in his mind: four million six hundred thousand Illusion Points.
Between what he’d taken off Norton earlier — a few hundred thousand — and what he’d just claimed here, the math was staggering. Sloane alone had been sitting on more than eight million points. He’d walked away with more than half her entire fortune. Her accumulation had gone far beyond anything he’d imagined.
Before this fight, he’d been sitting at three hundred ninth in his home world, and fourteen thousandth across the entire Illusion Battlefield. Now, in a single moment, that ranking had rocketed upward more times over than he could easily count. Among the trillions of warriors competing, the top one hundred thousand were considered dazzling, exceptional existences — and he’d just been thrown well past that line.
[AMBIENT: EXCITED PALACE]
Back at the Mist Palace, the mood had turned electric. Zane hailed from their halls, and expectations for him ran high — his ranking had been lukewarm up until now, and plenty had quietly worried about him. 
Yolande’s expression stayed composed on the surface, but underneath it, she’d never doubted him for a second. A small, knowing smile crossed her lips as his numbers kept climbing. “Fourteen thousandth,” she murmured to herself. “I wonder how long before he cracks the top one thousand.”
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Every great clan possessed star players, but saint-level geniuses stood apart - prodigies so gifted they could single-handedly alter their clan’s destiny. Clans poured all resources into nurturing them. A universal saying warned: “To destroy a powerful clan, first kill its saint-level genius.” As long as that genius survived, their extraordinary talent would enable an eventual comeback. Even if a clan fell, its saint-level genius had to be protected at all costs - a seed capable of regrowing the entire family tree.
Of course, there was a bit of myth to it. Some of the oldest clans were so deeply rooted that they could produce a new genius even if the current one fell. Still, the saying proved just how vital these individuals were.
Making it into the top ten of the entire Illusion Battlefield was an incredible achievement, but Sloane had an advantage. While everyone else fought as an individual, she had two extremely powerful bodies working in tandem. Her Illusion Point count was naturally double that of other top talents, and sometimes even higher than some of the saint-level geniuses.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
But for House Kincaid, her rank wasn’t good enough. They wanted Sloane to be number one. It was first place or nothing. After investing so many years into her training, anything less than the top spot would be a humiliation.
And House Kincaid was no small family to carry that weight for. Over a million warriors bore the Kincaid name, a number that rivaled a nation in its own right, and the family patriarch himself — a man who rarely surfaced these days — had come out of seclusion just to watch this Illusion Battlefield unfold. 
She was the one the family had staked everything on.
[SFX: DRAMATIC WHOOSH]
And then, on the massive ranking pyramid displayed before the heads of House Kincaid, Sloane’s name suddenly began to plummet.
[SFX: DESCENDING PITCH DROP]
From seventh place, it dropped. And dropped. All the way down to the seven-hundredth rank.
