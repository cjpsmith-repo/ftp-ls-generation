Ep 599 - The Final Man
Word count: 1159
[AMBIENT: TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
The tower was quiet as everyone considered Boris's impossible-sounding offer.
Malcolm huffed, "You got a solution? Spit it out!"
"Since the humans are so obsessed with their rules, let's play their game," Boris said. "We don't need to hand over ten Relic-Class weapons. When Zane comes out of that Talent Tablet, I'll challenge him and kill him in the arena." He spoke with a chilling certainty, as if the fight was already won.
Malcolm, Fabian, and Jarrod exchanged glances.
Fabian and Jarrod understood their only hope was getting Zane into the arena. A victory would cement the rules and rally other races to their side.
Malcolm saw different possibilities. Boris—a prodigy whose cultivation exceeded Zane's by an entire realm—would crush him. The Ogres could eliminate Zane legally without paying the humans anything.
If Boris somehow failed, they could simply break the rules and kill Zane anyway.
[SFX: BOOMING LAUGHTER]
A booming laugh erupted from Malcolm. "Yes! I like it! What do you say, humans?"
Fabian and Jarrod remained silent. Getting the Ogres to agree to an arena fight was a concession in itself, the most they could hope for. So, though they were sick with worry, they could only nod.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]
Olivia let out a soft sigh, her eyes fixed on Zane’s glowing name on the wall. She wasn’t stupid. She knew exactly what the Ogres were up to.
Even if Zane somehow beat Boris, she doubted the Ogres would honor the outcome. There was nothing but bad blood between their two races.
He never should have come to the Tower of Sin, she thought, a wave of regret washing over her. He should have stayed at the Violet Heart Sanctuary, focused on getting stronger. Waited until he was powerful enough to face these Ogre War Lords. With his talent, he doesn't even need this Tower. A breakthrough was probably just around the corner for him if he'd just kept training. Ugh, how did everything get so messed up?
Of course, she couldn't know that Zane didn't have the luxury of patience. His sister was waiting for him, and every day that passed was another twist of the knife in his gut. Time was running out.
The Tower of Sin was his only shot at getting the power he needed, fast. He had no other choice.
[MUSIC: PASSAGE OF TIME]
Four hours passed. The crowd that had been watching the wall shifted restlessly. No one had ever taken this long.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
"What is he doing in there?"
"Could he be dead?"
"If he were dead, his name would have disappeared from the wall."
They looked back at the first row. Zane's name still burned there, bright and steady. Whatever was happening inside, it wasn't over.
[AMBIENT: TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Inside the chamber, the ninth Soul Echo crumbled into white ash at Zane's feet.
He stood over it, breathing hard. Nine down.
"How many total?" he asked.
"Ten," the Tower Spirit answered. "Defeat ten, and you pass the final test."
"Then this is the last one."
Around him, the remaining patterns were fading one by one, dissolving into the dark until only a single rune remained, hanging in the air directly ahead. It began to move toward him.
[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION]
A flash of brilliant light. A figure stepped out of it.
Zane's eyes narrowed.
The previous echoes had all carried clear markers — pointed ears, unusual proportions, features that placed them firmly outside the human race. This one was different. He looked at the figure carefully, searching for the tell.
There was nothing.
Ears, brows, eyes, build — every proportion exactly right. Not just human-looking. Perfect. More human than any human Zane had ever seen, and yet that perfection itself was the problem. No person looked like this. No person was this flawless.
"Is he actually human?" Zane murmured.
"No," the Tower Spirit said. "He is the master of this Tower. He belongs to the Demi-Immortal Race."
"The Demi-Immortal Race." Zane studied the figure. "Why does he look so much like us?"
"That question I cannot answer."
"Is he dead? Is that why his echo is here?"
"Yes. This is the final test. Defeat him, and you pass."
Zane straightened. Whatever the Demi-Immortal Race was, whatever this man had been in life — none of it mattered right now. He was one step from the end.
Before he could move, the figure charged.
No warning. No stance. Just pure forward momentum.
Zane felt it immediately — no Vitality, nothing. Just a body, moving with terrifying speed and force.
A body refiner, Zane realized. He suppressed everything and came at me with pure physical strength.
He put his sword away. Fine. Let's do this properly.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT EXPLOSION]
They met in the middle. The collision was savage — no technique, no law, just two bodies hitting each other at full force.
Zane flew back.
So did the figure — but only by a few feet.
Zane landed hard, staring.
He hadn't used Dragon Scales. He never needed to, not against any of the others. A straight collision without any power boost should have sent an opponent flying. Instead, the figure had barely moved.
"His body," Zane thought, genuinely stunned. "It's as strong as mine."
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL CRESCENDO]
"AGAIN!" Zane roared, channeling the Dragon Scales' power and activating a hundred of them.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT EXPLOSION]
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT]
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
The air cracked as they collided again. This time, Zane was thrown back a hundred feet—but his opponent was sent hurtling a hundred and fifty.
"AGAIN!"
[SFX: THUNDEROUS COLLISION]
Zane kept releasing the power of the Dragon Scales, just a little more each time. With every clash, he flew back a little less, and his opponent flew back a little more. After the next impact, a trickle of blood dribbled from the corner of the perfect man’s mouth.
"Golden blood?" Zane thought, the sight confirming his suspicion. This guy isn't human. No human bleeds gold.
The perfect man wiped the blood from his lip, his expression hardening. He couldn't win a battle of pure physical strength. If this went on, he would be the first to fall.
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
He shifted his stance, clenching one hand into a fist. Faint streams of light began to swirl around his knuckles, coalescing into a vortex of crackling energy.
Zane recognized it. 'Forceful energy! So, a member of the Demi-Immortal Race who focused on Body Refining. Interesting. In that case... let's see how he likes my technique!'
Suddenly, victory wasn't the point. Zane saw a different prize on the line: a breakthrough.
"Stellar Body!"
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Zane had never used Stellar Body in a real fight before. Now was his chance.
It was time to see just how powerful the Transcendent Divine Might truly was.
