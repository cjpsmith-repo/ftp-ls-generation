Ep 174 - Winner Takes All
Word Count: 1083
[AMBIENT: TENSE PRISON YARD]
Even though the big guy had beaten every inmate skilled with Vitality, nobody was actually impressed. They all knew that without the Vitality-suppressing runes, they could've easily mopped the floor with him.
This new kid, however, seemed to have that same kind of resilience. The big guy’s punch hadn’t even made him flinch. He was at least as tough as their so-called “champ.”
“Hmph. Should’ve just stayed on the floor and played dead. You asked for this, so don’t blame me for what happens next.” The words were barely out of his mouth before the big guy charged at Zane.
But with his Dragon Scales and Spiritual Weapon Body, a fistfight was the last thing Zane was afraid of.
[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDUP]
Watching the hulking figure barrel toward him with the force of an enraged bull, Zane narrowed his eyes and deliberately lowered his shoulder. Rather than retreat, he launched himself forward.
Conventional wisdom dictated that in raw physical confrontations, the bigger man usually had the upper hand. But Zane’s lips curled into the faintest smile. "Not this time."
Just before impact, he summoned the energy in his body, channeling the power of over a hundred Dragon Scales.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
Their bodies collided with tremendous force, the impact reverberating through the stone-walled chamber.
Among the gathered disciples from the Skyreach Sect, hushed conversations broke out as they exchanged knowing glances.
“Look at that skinny fool,” muttered one disciple, shaking his head in disbelief. “The big guy’s going to crush him like dried parchment.”
“Pure madness,” agreed another, her eyes widening as she leaned forward. “What possessed him to charge straight into that mountain of muscle? Either he’s extraordinarily brave or has some peculiar desire for self-destruction.”
The onlookers fell silent, collectively holding their breath as they awaited the inevitable conclusion to this mismatched confrontation.
[SFX: LOUD COLLISION]
Another dull thud reverberated through the prison chamber as their bodies collided.
The burly inmate staggered backward, his face contorting with disbelief. He’d expected to bulldoze through this skinny kid, but he felt like he'd just run into a solid mountain. The force coming off Zane felt like the ocean tide---constant, overwhelming, and completely unstoppable.
[SFX: THUD AND GASPS]
The outcome left everyone speechless.
Zane was practically a splinter next to the big guy's massive frame.
But surprisingly, Zane didn’t move an inch, standing his ground like a rock. The big guy, on the other hand, stumbled back several steps, lost his footing, and crashed to the floor, his belly jiggling like a dropped water balloon.
Zane narrowed his eyes and slowly walked over to the man on the ground. He glanced down, then squatted and asked, “Wanna go for round two?”
The big guy felt like his bones were broken, his face pale with pain. He finally realized just how powerful Zane was---stronger and tougher, in a whole different league.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
A hush fell over the muster station, the air thick with tension as every prisoner held their breath. No one dared utter a word, their wide eyes fixed on the scene before them.
The unwritten law here was simple yet brutally enforced: the winner was king.
Before Zane’s arrival, the big guy had reigned supreme over this corner of the prison, his physical dominance unchallenged. Today, Zane had stripped away that authority; the crown had changed hands, and no one else was dumb enough to mess with him.
They all knew that challenging this newcomer would result in nothing but pain and public humiliation. Even the most aggressive among them shrank back.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS WALKING AWAY]
Zane rose to his feet, his face betraying no emotion as he surveyed the room. He walked to a corner and sat down, completely ignoring the big guy still sprawled on the floor.
The sound of his footsteps echoed in the cavernous space as he settled against the wall, folding his arms across his chest.
“Can’t practice in here anyway,” Zane thought, allowing his eyelids to grow heavy as he leaned his head back against the cold stone. A faint smile played at the corners of his mouth as he added to himself, “Might as well get some rest.”
[AMBIENT: MYSTERIOUS ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TONES]
The story of Steven Hill was a dark chapter in the history of the Skyreach Sect, a cautionary tale that served as a stark reminder of their principles. Nine years after joining the Sect, he broke through to the Soul Realm and mastered the most powerful arts in the world. Within that realm, he was untouchable.
In the end, however, Steven betrayed the Sect and became its sworn enemy. He roamed the Eastern Territory, slaughtering the Sect’s disciples wherever he found them.
The Skyreach Sect was forced to ally with the Empire just to deal with him, sending dozens of their best disciples on the hunt.
Ultimately, the Sect managed to kill Steven, but it cost them the lives of three of their own Saints.
After that incident, the Skyreach Sect established a new rule: a disciple’s character was more important than anything, even talent. They refused to train gifted people with dark hearts, as it went against the very principles the Sect stood for.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REFLECTIVE]
[AMBIENT: QUIET MEETING ROOM]
[SFX: WOOD SLAPPING ON TABLE]
Saint Augustus, wearing a distinctive purple hat, slapped a square piece of wood on the table. A formless curtain of purple light radiated from it.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY WASHING OVER]
As the light washed over everyone, a sense of calm settled in the room. This was the unique effect of Saint Augustus’ Vitality.
“Zane,” Saint Augustus said, his voice calm but carrying an undeniable weight. “Tell me. Did you kill Frey with your sword in the Hall of Judgment?”
Even though he was being interrogated, Zane felt calm. Saint Augustus was asking questions, not just declaring him guilty. It meant he had a chance to explain himself.
Zane nodded. “Yes.”
[AMBIENT: SURPRISED CROWD]
At his direct confession, several of the Saints exchanged looks of disbelief. Why would he admit to it so openly? Didn’t he know the Sect’s rules? If found guilty of murder, no one in the world could save him.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
A smirk touched Saint Viana’s lips. “Well, since he’s confessed, there's no need to continue this. A life for a life!”
“Yes, Saint Viana is right!” the members of House Sterling chimed in. “He has to pay for his crime! That’s the law!”
