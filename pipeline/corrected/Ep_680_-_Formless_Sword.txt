Ep 680 - Formless Sword

[AMBIENT: TENSE CAVE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

Watching Maurice’s face twist into a mask of pure savagery, the crowd had the exact same thought: This guy is insane.

Esther and the others scrambled back from the frenzied Ogre, and Zane fell back right alongside them, a calculated retreat designed to keep Maurice trapped in the deadly glow of the Dark Nether Death Light. Even on the fringes of the cave, far from the Mad Scorpion itself, the light was more than a top-tier War Lord could ever hope to withstand.

But Maurice, lost to his rage, didn’t even bother shielding himself with his energy. His only goal was to drag Zane into a fight to the death. With the three spiritual altars in his body already burning away, he’d thrown away any thought of self-preservation. He had no intention of surviving this.

[SFX: SIZZLING FLESH]

Zane moved like lightning, and everyone else hurried to keep pace. The moment Maurice broke from his hiding spot and charged, the Nether Light went to work. His skin sizzled, blackening and curling away from the muscle beneath. As he sprinted, his very flesh seemed to melt off his frame, his once-tall body shrinking as his blood and tissue vaporized. His brutal face dissolved, revealing the stark white skull underneath until he was little more than a living skeleton barreling forward.

[SFX: AGONIZED SCREAM]

Maurice’s howl of agony only seemed to fuel his attack as he finally tore through the orange barrier of light.

[SFX: SWORD SWING]

In that instant, Zane’s focus sharpened. He swung his long sword.

“Formless Sword!”

[SFX: SWORD ENERGY BLAST]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

The name was a misnomer. The attack was anything but formless; it simply meant Zane had such complete mastery over the technique that he could shape its power at will. A terrifying wave of sword energy roared from his blade, crashing head-on into the light gathering around Maurice’s fist.

[SFX: SHARP CRACK]

A sharp, cracking sound echoed through the cave.

The Nether Light had already incinerated most of Maurice’s body. The flesh on his hands was long gone, leaving only blackened bones. When his fist collided with Zane’s attack, those bones began to splinter and crack apart.

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY EXPLOSION]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

“Die!” Zane yelled, pouring more power into his strike. The might of the Dragon Scales erupted from within him, the force of it blasting Maurice back out of the protective barrier.

[SFX: BONES CLATTERING]

Thrown backward, Maurice tilted his skeletal head to the sky, his eyes filled with a final, helpless understanding. He had lost. His body dissolved completely, and a pile of scorched bones clattered into the shadowy depths of the Dark Nether Cave.

Standing inside the orange light, Zane looked down at the chasm, a little surprised. He had to admit, the Ogres were a true warrior race. Maurice’s plan had been a good one—desperate, but clever. Unfortunately for him, he’d underestimated both the dangers of the cave and Zane’s own strength. As long as he gave it his all, Zane knew he’d have no regrets, even if he never became the strongest warrior in the world.

[MUSIC: QUIET REALIZATION]

"So that's it," Esther said, staring at the side of the Tower. "The Ogres found a secret passage. That's how they've been hiding here to cultivate."

Fabian nodded slowly. "Colin and Neil. Malcolm and Timothy. They were all using it. That's how they became War Sages without anyone knowing."

Zane glanced at the passage, then back at the others. "We can use it too. Arrange for people to hide there — humans and Demon Night both."

"There's a cost," Yolande said. "Anyone who breaks through in this cave can never fully leave it. They can move through the Tower for short stretches, but they can't return to the outside world. It's permanent."

A silence fell over the group.

"Someone will have to make that sacrifice," Zane said. "For the race."

[MUSIC: RESOLVED TENSION]

They returned to the top floor. The Twilight Group War Lords were already there, rounded up and held at spearpoint by the Demon Night and the human War Lords who had survived.

Zane looked them over without expression. "Clean them up."

"The Twilight Group?" Fabian asked.

"They fed Malcolm my location. They stood with the Ogres when they came back." Zane's voice was flat. "They don't leave."

The Twilight Group War Lords were wiped out.

[MUSIC: SWEEPING AFTERMATH]

The news spread fast. Every Ogre War Lord was killed. The story raced across the main continent like a brushfire — shock in every corner, silence from the Ogres' Sacred Land. They didn't retaliate. They didn't dare. They had already lost the Tower of Sin; a full war against the Demon Night and the humans combined would end them permanently. So they swallowed it. The decline of the Ogres was no longer a question of if, only when.

The Twilight Group, without their protectors, faced the same verdict. A few scattered assassinations followed in the coming weeks — meaningless gestures from a faction that had already lost.

In the Tower, Zane returned to cultivating. When the mood took him, he walked out into the Cursed Land and stood under the open sky, reaching upward through the void, trying to coax another star into the Stellar Body. The technique was as unforgiving as it had always been. He failed more than he succeeded. He didn't let it bother him — he'd known from the first day he stepped onto this road that it was going to be hard. That was the road. You walked it anyway.

[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL PRAIRIE WIND]

[MUSIC: GENTLE PASTORAL MELODY]

Three months later, a few fluffy clouds drifted across the clear blue sky above the Heavenly Eagle Prairie. A gentle breeze stirred the air, and the sun was just warm enough to make you want to lie down in the grass.

[AMBIENT: OPEN PLAINS WITH WIND]

[MUSIC: PEACEFUL PASTORAL]

Hundreds of tents now dotted the plains. A few bare-shouldered men sat high on their coursers, their skin tanned by the sun as they herded sheep across the vast grasslands. Ever since the Ogres’ surrender, their presence had dwindled. The prairie, once divided between the Ogres, the Demon Night, and the Humans, now belonged entirely to humanity.

The local herdsmen had slowly gathered, forming a small nomadic clan of their own, complete with a handful of warriors—enough to be considered a grade-two Order, if you were ranking such things.

[SFX: WHOOSHING LIGHT BEAMS]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

Suddenly, dozens of brilliant beams of light streaked across the sky.

[SFX: SHEEP BLEATING]

[SFX: HORSES NEIGHING]

The sheep, sensing the immense pressure from above, bleated and scattered in a panic. The herdsmen shouted, trying to wheel their horses around and round them up.

But in the pasture, a young girl lifted her head, her wide, dark eyes fixed on the sky, completely unfazed by the dazzling light. She stared for a long moment before her face broke into a grin. Spinning around, she sprinted toward a nearby tent.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT FANFARE]

“Father! Craig! It’s the True God! The True God is here!”

At her shouts, a young man burst out of the tent, his face alight with the same excitement. In just two years, the little boy had shot up, growing into a tall, handsome young man.

“The True God?” Craig yelled, his voice cracking with excitement. “Where?”
