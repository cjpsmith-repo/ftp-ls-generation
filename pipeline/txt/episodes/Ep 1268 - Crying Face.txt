Ep 1268 - Crying Face

Word count: 1218

[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]
[AMBIENT: STONE TEMPLE RUMBLING]
As a massive stone slab ground down toward him, Zane remained perfectly calm, a white mist rising from his body.
“Eight Smoky Melodies,” he murmured.
Suddenly, his body felt as light as a feather. The stone’s immense size made it perfect for Zane’s technique.
Eight Smoky Melodies was a unique movement style - the faster and stronger the attack, the more fluidly he could move. Only Sloane’s Crescent Blade had ever breached his three-inch defensive perimeter.
[SFX: MASSIVE STONE WHOOSHING]
Just as the slab was about to crush him, he twisted in a way that seemed to defy physics. He took a single step sideways and was simply... gone, squeezed out from the path of destruction like a leaf pressed between two hands.
“What the hell was that?” one of the other warriors blurted out, his voice thick with confusion.
Once clear, Zane landed softly on the stone floor. He didn’t have time to answer. 
[SFX: MULTIPLE STONES ROLLING]
Two more square stones were already bearing down on him. After the group had scrambled from the hall’s exit, they’d found themselves right back in the thick of it, dodging the rolling behemoths all over again.
“You saved my life,” the warrior he’d just helped said, rushing over to him.
Zane just offered a small smile and shook his head. “We’re all trying to get through this together. We have to cooperate when we can. Helping you is helping myself.”
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL CONTEMPLATIVE]
With that, the suspicion that had been simmering among the ten of them finally began to dissolve. At first, some of them had only been thinking for themselves. But now, it was sinking in: teamwork was the only way they were getting out of here alive.
“But with that glowing barrier blocking the way forward, I don’t see how we’ll get through,” someone said, his voice edged with defeat.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]
“Everyone, be careful,” Zane said. “There has to be a mechanism.” As they all backed away from the onslaught, they scanned their surroundings, desperate for a plan.
Just then, the short human warrior from The Hallowed Concord spoke up. “Maybe the mechanism is on the stones themselves.”
Before anyone could react, he spun to dodge an approaching stone. 
[SFX: BLADE DRAWN]
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]
A short sword appeared in his hand—a Divine Weapon, the Blade of Hallowed Censure. With a grunt, he zipped around the massive block. Though he couldn’t fly in the hall, his speed skyrocketed as he used the stone’s own momentum to propel himself.
[SFX: BLADE SLICING STONE]
He brought the blade around and sliced it lightly across the stone’s surface.
A faint mark bloomed where the blade touched. He circled again, and again, each pass leaving the same strange mark behind until it was etched deep into the boulder’s face. Then he pulled back, held the blade high, and called out, “One word to break ten directions—shatter the maxim!”
[SFX: HUMMING VIBRATION]
The mark began to spin, faster and faster, until it blurred into a ring of light. A burst of radiance tore free from the boulder’s surface—what looked like countless blade-shadows raining down against the stone itself.
[SFX: STONE MECHANISM ACTIVATING]
The stone reacted instantly.
It slowed, then stopped rolling altogether. For a moment it looked as though the attack had worked. But when the light faded, the boulder sat there, untouched and whole—and the ghastly face on its surface was back, its wicked grin twisting slowly into something else. A crying face.
“Everyone, be careful!” Janet called out, her eyes locked on the change.
No one knew exactly what was coming, but the shift alone was enough to put ice in their veins—and it wasn’t just this one boulder. 
Every stone in the hall now wore the same crying face, all at once.
“Don’t stay in line with them!” Zane snapped, already stepping to the side.
[SFX: ENERGY BEAMS CHARGING]
The instant he moved, every boulder in the hall fired a beam of light outward in all four directions—each one three feet wide, three feet tall, tearing straight down its row all the way to the far wall. With so many beams crossing the hall at once, there was barely a gap left to stand in.
Zane found himself boxed in, front and back lit up with light, able to shift only left or right. He didn’t dare move recklessly. He’d already called the warning once—there was no way to know if it had reached everyone in time. Touch one of those beams, and there’d be nothing left to save.
The beams held for ten full breaths before finally dimming and winking out. When the light cleared, two small points of light hung in the air where two warriors had been standing.
“Two of us didn’t make it,” Zane said quietly.
The two had been standing in a spot with no way to dodge — eight boulders converging with no gap between them. Each point was worth close to three million Illusion Points, more than enough to be worth grabbing under any other circumstance. But no one moved for them. No one was in the mood.
[SFX: STONE RUMBLING]
Before long, the boulders began rolling again. This time, though, they weren’t chasing anyone — they were sliding into position, forming a single straight line across the hall.
“There’s a gap — one boulder short over there!” someone shouted.
[AMBIENT: HUMMING ENERGY GRID]
Seeing this, Zane yelled, “Everyone, get to me! Now!”
The moment the last warrior scrambled into the square where Zane stood, the rows of giant stones lit up, unleashing beams of light all at once. The stones formed channels, blocking the beams from shooting left or right, and instead funneling them forward and backward until the entire hall was a grid of deadly energy. There were no blind spots.
Lined up edge to edge, the boulders numbered forty-three in total — and the hall’s floor had room for forty-four. That single missing stone was the only gap in the entire grid.
They were standing in the one empty space on the grid—the one spot where a stone was missing. It was their only chance to survive. A trap like this had to be beatable; it couldn’t just be an execution chamber.
[SFX: ELECTRICAL BUZZING INTENSIFIES]
The group crowded together, their faces pale as they stared at the dense walls of light humming on either side. Every other inch of the hall was now a death zone. 
[MUSIC: BRIEF RELIEF]
[AMBIENT: QUIET BREATHING]
Safe for the moment, they finally had a chance to catch their breath.
“He shouldn’t have attacked that stone,” someone complained, glaring at the short warrior.
“No,” Zane cut in. “He did what we had to do. His attack forced the trap to change.”
If the short warrior hadn’t acted, they would still be running for their lives with no end in sight.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]
[AMBIENT: HUMMING ENERGY GRID]
“So what now?” another warrior asked, his voice shaking. “How are we supposed to get past this? We can’t dodge in time, and we can’t just stand here forever!”
Zane took a deep breath, his eyes scanning the far end of the hall. 
“The key,” he said, his voice low but clear, “must be those ghastly faces.”
