Ep 376 - Man vs. Machine

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

Zane poured every ounce of strength into the strike, channeling his Core Strength through his arms as he swung Streamer in a silver blur, aiming to cleave the automaton clean in half at the waist.

[SFX: METALLIC CLANG]

But the blade merely skittered across the automaton's body, failing to leave even the faintest scratch. A violent vibration shot up Zane's arms, the shock so intense his hands went numb and tingling. His fingers nearly lost their grip on Streamer's hilt.

Zane's chest tightened with frustration. The construct felt as though it had been forged from some impossibly hard metal—harder than anything he'd encountered before.

"Damn," he muttered through gritted teeth, his breath coming hard. "This thing's tougher than I thought. Not even a Mystic Weapon can dent it."

[SFX: CRUMBLING STONE]

His mind raced. If Streamer couldn't cut through, what chance did he have? The platform continued crumbling beneath his feet, stone fragments tumbling into the abyss below. He had seconds at most to formulate a new strategy—or this trial would end with his death.

[SFX: MECHANICAL WHIRRING]

Before Zane could even try for a second swing, the automaton jolted into motion. It tilted forward with mechanical precision, sending its whirring blades rushing straight for him.

The blades spun so fast they blurred into twin discs of death, silver plates glinting in the light. Zane's heart hammered against his ribs as he calculated the distance—too close, far too close.

[SFX: BLADE WHOOSH

[SFX: BODY HITTING GROUND]

He threw himself backward, his body hitting the crumbling stone hard enough to knock the wind from his lungs. Pain shot through his shoulder as he rolled, the deadly blades passing mere inches above his head. 

His pulse thundered in his ears. That had been too close—another fraction of a second and those blades would have torn through him like paper.

[SFX: BLADES GRINDING STONE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]

He thought he was in the clear, but then the automaton did something unexpected: it drove its blades straight into the ground. It was trying to slice the platform in two and send him plummeting!

This thing was smart—way too smart. It was almost like it was thinking for itself.

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Most automatons were mindless, only capable of following the simplest commands. But this one was different. It had forced him back, and now it was trying to destroy his footing. It could strategize.

Zane couldn't afford to dwell on it. His only goal right now was staying on the platform. It wasn't about the Cloud Hall students waiting for him outside the barrier; it was about losing his shot at the silver box. Besides, he still wanted a crack at the gold boxes higher up. The black box felt like a long shot, but he had to try. He wasn't the type to back down from even the slimmest of chances.

Zane wasn't a fool; he had a good head on his shoulders. He was the type to give everything he had for an opportunity. But if, after all that, it still didn’t pan out, he could accept it—chalk it up to not being strong enough, or just plain unlucky. Zane knew how to take a loss.

Take the Elysian Keep in the Eastern Territories, for example. That was a massive opportunity, but he was only ever its temporary owner. Becoming its true master would have been life-changing, but he knew his limits and didn't push it.

Pushing yourself was important, but knowing your limits was crucial. Today, though… today felt different. He had a real shot here, and there was no way he was letting it go without a fight.


[SFX: GRINDING METAL ON STONE]

The automaton was still grinding into the stone. A harsh, grating scream filled the air as rock chips sprayed from the platform. At this rate, the overhang was already halfway gone. Any longer, and the whole thing would snap off under its own weight.

[AMBIENT: OBSERVATION AREA]

Toby watched the Scrying Slate and sighed, shaking his head. "Always chasing long shots. It's like he has no idea what his limits are, especially at only the Third Level of the Adept Realm."

He glanced at Kenneth. "From that purplish sheen, I can tell it's made of Darksteel. It's used to forge top-grade Sacred Weapons—some of the hardest in existence. There's no possible way Zane is breaking through that."

Seeing the color drain from Kenneth's face, Toby clamped his mouth shut. He'd said enough. All he could do now was hope Zane had another trick up his sleeve to get away from that thing and off the platform.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

At this point, nobody cared about the silver box. All eyes were on Zane, everyone wondering the same thing: could he get away? What good was a treasure box if you were dead?

Could he escape? Or was this it?

Meanwhile, Zane's mind raced. After everything he'd been through, there was no way he was letting some machine be the end of him.

[SFX: ENERGY EXPLOSION]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

Suddenly, Zane let out a guttural roar as his energy exploded outward in a shockwave. The Dragon Scales covering his body flashed, and a furious power surged through him.

Ignoring the screaming pain, he heaved, trying to wrench his body free. He had to get out, no matter the cost. It was all or nothing.

[SFX: HARD IMPACT]

But the automaton didn't give an inch. The sheer, immovable force of it slammed into him.

The true gap in their power was immense; he felt like a child trying to kick down an ancient oak.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TONES]

Hopelessness washed over him. For a split second, he was sure it was over.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL RISING NOTES]

His gaze dropped to the ground, and then his eyes lit up. He saw it: the automaton had anchored itself by driving its blades deep into the stone platform. He had no idea if the plan forming in his mind would work, but it was the only one he had. And at this point, what did he have to lose?

[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]

[SFX: HEARTBEAT]

For the first time, he stopped struggling and went completely still.
