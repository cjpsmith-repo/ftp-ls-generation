With his weapon shattered, desperation flashed in Emanuel’s eyes. "Zane, I'm an elder of Cloud Hall!" he yelled. "Even if I committed treason, my fate is for Tia to decide. I surrender! You can't kill me!"
His words gave Zane pause.
Technically, the man had a point. As a key elder, Emanuel’s rank far outstripped Zane’s. Zane, after all, was just a regular disciple with no official standing.
Emanuel knew that if he could just survive this moment, he could leverage House Drew's influence to make Tia spare his life. As long as he was breathing, there was always a chance for a comeback. He was a patient man. He'd spent decades building toward this moment, outmaneuvering rivals, cultivating alliances, waiting for the right opening. One reversal didn't erase all of that. As long as he drew breath, the game wasn't over.
But Zane's hesitation lasted only a moment. Keeping Emanuel alive was keeping a tiger. The man had targeted him repeatedly since the day he joined Cloud Hall — and more than that, his goal today had been to kill Tia. Anyone who reached for Tia reached for something Zane would not allow anyone to touch. Emanuel had made his choice the moment he launched this attack.
"You're right," Zane said. "I can't kill you." He drew his sword. "I'll do it on her behalf."

Across Cloud Hall, disciples gathered at windows and on rooftops, their eyes glued to the drama unfolding in the sky. Most of them had stayed neutral during the rebellion; a power struggle between the higher-ups had little to do with the rank-and-file fighters. For them, it didn't matter who was in charge. Right now, they were just here for the show.

"Oh my god, Zane's back! He's chasing Master Drew with his bare hands!"
"That kid is a monster. He vanishes for two years and comes back fighting one of the elders!"
"Are you blind? Drew can't even touch him. This isn't a fight, it's a beatdown!"

But those from the younger generation from the powerful families weren't enjoying the spectacle, especially those from House Drew. Seeing Emanuel relentlessly beaten back by Zane made their faces go grim. They were too weak to even think about joining the fight.
[MUSIC: DARK DRAMATIC STRINGS]
Over at the Elite House, Frank stood frozen, watching his father get hunted across the sky, forced to beg for his life. A storm was brewing behind his eyes, his expression hardening into a mask of disbelief and rage.
He remembered the Cloud Road Trial, how full of himself he’d been. Back then, he saw himself as the only one destined for greatness. Who among the younger disciples could possibly compare? He’d never even considered Zane a rival.
Now, in just two years, Zane had become someone his own father couldn't handle. Frank was still stuck at the Soul Realm — his cultivation stunted, his martial heart damaged ever since Zane had broken his hand on the Cloud Road. He couldn't separate his hatred from the wreckage Zane had made of his future.
The worst part was that his talent should have carried him past all of this. Heaven-grade. The elders had said it themselves. Under normal circumstances, he'd have left his peers far behind by now. But a broken hand had become a broken spirit, and two years of depression had eaten through both. His cultivation had crawled while everyone around him moved. Even Finn — a nobody from nowhere — had outpaced him. All of it traced back to one moment on the Cloud Road.
Someone grabbed his arm before he could move. A shake of the head. Don't.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
"Who is Tia to you?" Emanuel demanded, still dodging. "Whatever she can give you, I can give you more. Just let me go."
Zane slashed — a crescent of sword intent that spread wide. Emanuel blocked it with a spare spear from his Storage Ring.
"You're nothing but her kept man!" Emanuel roared, attacking as he shouted. "Everyone in Cloud Hall knows it — I made sure of it!"
Zane's eyes showed a flicker of recognition. "So it was you," he said. "Spreading those rumors since the day I arrived."
"Yes! Am I wrong?" Emanuel thrust again. "You're her pet, and everyone knows it!"
Zane blocked the spear and cut it apart in three slashes. "You're wrong," he said.
Emanuel reached for another weapon. "You'll do it, but you won't admit it." It was his last gambit — to goad Zane into losing focus. But Zane's mind was still water.
[SFX: BODY FALLING THROUGH AIR]
Emanuel's body suddenly went limp, falling from the sky like a kite with a snapped string.
Zane pulled his sword free. "I'm not Tia's kept man," he said quietly. "Tia is my woman."
[SFX: ANGUISHED SCREAM]
A heart-wrenching scream tore through the air from below. "Father!" Frank cried out, his eyes wide with horror.
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED SILENCE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
But Zane only shot Frank an indifferent glance before banking toward the Ice Palace, leaving everyone in Cloud Hall stunned into silence. The shock wasn't just that he had killed an elder. It was his parting words.
Tia is my woman?
Was he telling the truth? To say something so bold in front of everyone… maybe it was real. It was hard to wrap their heads around. Tia had a spotless reputation, not just in Cloud Hall but across the entire Heartlands. She was beautiful, impossibly talented, and a leader of immense status. In their minds, she was the perfect woman—noble, untouchable. How could she suddenly be Zane's woman?
It felt impossible.
Just then, Zane heard Lavender call out from behind him. "Hey, since when is Tia your woman?"
He answered with a wry smile. "Just running my mouth."

The Ice Palace stood at the highest point of Cloud Hall. The elders of the Inner-Core Realm who hadn't fled had watched the battle's conclusion from afar. They saw Zane decisively defeat Emanuel, and for those who had joined the rebellion, the will to fight completely drained away. They were all weaker than Emanuel. If they tried to resist now, they’d just be asking for the same fate.
All they could do was surrender and await judgment. With the backing of their powerful families, it was unlikely they’d be executed, but they definitely weren't getting off scot-free.
