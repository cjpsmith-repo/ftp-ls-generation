[AMBIENT: ROYAL BANQUET HALL]
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
Everyone at the prince’s banquet was there to network and build alliances; the fight was just supposed to be a little entertainment to liven things up. But Zane’s arrogance was rubbing a lot of people the wrong way, and they were getting pissed. So when Doug Drew stepped up to challenge him, the crowd roared.
One spectator turned to his friend, his eyes gleaming with anticipation."Doug created his own movement technique—hell, he even named it after himself. He moves so unpredictably that opponents totally lose track of him. Just watch, he’s about to give Zane a lesson in what real martial arts look like!"
His friend nodded eagerly. "I hope he draws blood. That’ll teach him that nobody who works for the Third Prince is a pushover!"
"Damn right!" a third voice chimed in, wine sloshing in his cup as he gestured emphatically. "Zane’s only at the Third Level of the Adept Realm. He won’t even see Doug’s attacks coming. The boy is completely out of his league."
[SFX: CROWD ROARING]
[MUSIC: HIGH STAKES BATTLE]
Doug had earned his reputation for blinding speed. Nimble as a hummingbird, he darted from one spot to the next, a blur of motion that made it impossible to predict his next move.
Zane, however, maintained his position, calm as a mountain weathering a storm. His eyes narrowed in concentration, methodically scanning the space while his muscles tensed imperceptibly beneath his robes. The clamor of the crowd faded into distant noise as he focused his senses, straining to detect where Doug might materialize next.
[SFX: SWIFT MOVEMENT]
Seeing his opponent was fighting barehanded, Zane refrained from drawing his sword, preferring to match the combat style. Through the disorienting blur, he caught a fleeting glimpse of Doug’s form and launched a calculated punch, his knuckles cutting through the air with devastating precision.
[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]
But in that critical moment, Doug’s wings trembled. He twisted his body in an impossible arc, his muscles flowing like liquid as he dodged Zane’s fist. A triumphant gleam flashed in Doug’s eyes as he pivoted, his razor-sharp claws slicing through the air before connecting with Zane’s exposed right flank.
[SFX: CROWD GASPS]
"He got hit!" gasped a spectator, their voice rising above the collective intake of breath from the astonished crowd.
[SFX: FABRIC TEARING]
The claws tore three small gashes in Zane’s clothes, leaving his skin untouched beneath the torn fabric.
[AMBIENT: MOCKING CROWD]
"Nice!" exclaimed an onlooker. "Doug’s move was incredible—so smooth it was like watching a ghost!"
"That punk can’t even land a proper hit," sneered another. " I wasn’t sure what to expect, but if this is the top disciple from the Skyreach Sect, what a joke!"
"The Skyreach Sect? That so-called hallowed sanctuary for martial arts?
What a total embarrassment. And Doug hasn’t even unleashed his full potential!"
But through all the mockery, a solitary voice rose in Zane’s defense: Gale. Like Zane, Gale was a top-ranked disciple from the Skyreach Sect; he understood what others couldn’t, although their backgrounds were quite different.
Born into poverty and recruited by the Third Prince, Gale’s allegiance had been purchased through necessity rather than conviction. Despite his strength, Gale was desperate for the training resources only royal patronage could provide, forcing him to choose his political alignment carefully. Still, his pride as a Skyreach disciple wouldn’t allow him to silently stand by and let everyone trash Zane.
Gale, who had participated in the All Peaks Competition with distinction, shook his head firmly. "You’re partially right—Doug isn’t going all out. Disciples of the Skyreach Sect are stronger than you can comprehend. Mark my words: There’s no way Doug is winning this fight."
"Why champion an outsider, Gale?" someone fired back with undisguised hostility. "Is he really that good?"
Ignoring the surrounding chatter, Doug’s spiraling attack finally closed in on Zane’s arm.
[SFX: DULL THUDS]
A series of dull thuds echoed through the suddenly silent hall, the sound hollow and anticlimactic where screams of pain should have been.
But to everyone’s absolute shock, Zane’s arm remained intact, without a single scratch.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
"Impossible," someone whispered from the crowd. Doug’s claws were stopped dead against Zane’s arm, unable to even break the skin. The young man hadn’t even flinched, his expression unchanged as he regarded his attacker with calm indifference.
The smirks on the onlookers’ faces moments before now melted away, replaced by wide eyes and slack jaws. Several nobles instinctively stepped backward, their earlier mockery forgotten as the implications of what they were witnessing began to sink in. Gale’s words hung in the air, suddenly prophetic rather than merely loyal.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
The same thought flashed through Doug’s mind and the mind of every single guest at the banquet: Is Zane some kind of monster? Everyone knew the natural limitations of human physicality. No matter how strong, the human form was still just flesh and bone, wrapped in a delicate layer of skin. The animal kingdom had beasts with thick hides and hard shells; humans just weren’t built that way.
But today, Zane proved them wrong. He showed them that a human body could be so impossibly tough that it was practically immune to harm, standing perfectly still after taking a hit that should have torn him apart Zane’s calm demeanor only heightened the unsettling display of power.
His eyes betrayed no pain, not even the slightest discomfort, as Doug’s claws remained uselessly pressed against his unblemished skin.
This wasn’t a fight anymore. It was a taunt. And you could only pull off a taunt like that if you were overwhelmingly, undeniably stronger than your opponent.
Doug’s face flushed with humiliation as he realized he had become nothing more than a prop in Zane’s demonstration.
Observing the subtle, knowing smile on Zane’s face, many guests finally understood the truth. The whispers, the sidelong glances, the judgments—Zane had dismissed them all because, to him, their opinions meant nothing to him. Like insects buzzing around a dragon, they had never registered as worthy of his concern.
[MUSIC: AMBITIOUS ROYAL]
Meanwhile, Prince Ray’s eyes gleamed. He now saw that Zane wasn’t just a big name—he was a rare talent. And that made Ray even more determined to recruit him.
At first, Ray hadn’t cared about Zane’s actual strength, just his reputation. Landing the top disciple of the Skyreach Sect would create a domino effect, giving him an invaluable inroad with the entire Sect.
Zane was supposed to be the key.
But now, Ray saw that Zane was more than a key. He was a weapon. A razor-sharp sword. At only seventeen, with the power he already possessed, his future was limitless.
And Ray had to have him on his side.
