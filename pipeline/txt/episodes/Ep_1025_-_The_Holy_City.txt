Ep 1025 - The Holy City

Word count: 1081

[AMBIENT: MINING CAMP]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Master Sloan and Master Hill had come all the way from the Soulbound Sect’s inner halls for one reason — and they were prepared to wait as long as it took.

The armored men offered to go into the mine to find Zane, but Master Sloan and Master Hill shut that idea down immediately.

Why were the two masters being so damn patient?

Their refusal got the armored men talking, their whispers echoing through the camp. They wondered what Zane’s deal was and what possible reason the two masters had for being so set on waiting for him.

[AMBIENT: EVENING WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FLUTE]

They waited from noon until the sky bled into dusk, only for Zane to finally emerge from the mine.

Freya, however, was long gone. After snagging her one hundred Sovereign Stones, she’d gone straight to her uncle. The two of them had exchanged communication tokens before parting ways.

But up here, in the supreme world, a communication token was a joke.

The token was designed for the Lower World, its range topping out at sixty or seventy thousand miles. The Soulbound Sect’s territory was over ten thousand times bigger than that, making the token useless most of the time. It would only work if, by some miracle, Zane happened to be within that tiny radius of Freya.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]

Now that the dust had settled, Zane’s new goals were crystal clear. He’d just landed in the Upper World and didn’t know the first thing about it. He was on his own and would have to figure it out as he went.

While he got his bearings, his top priority was waking up Lavender. She’d been asleep for far too long, with no sign of stirring. Zane planned to gather two hundred Sovereign Stones, then find a treasure that could heal her soul.

He’d gotten lucky today, running into the Hydra again. Not only had he scored seven Sovereign Stones from the beast, but he’d also had a breakthrough. If he wanted to find more Sovereign Stones, he needed to follow the fierce beasts that liked to lurk in the mine, soaking up the stones’ energy.

If the beasts weren’t so ridiculously powerful, Zane would’ve seriously considered finding a way to tag them. It would save him the trouble of hunting them down every single time.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

But the moment Zane set foot back in the collection point, a bad feeling washed over him.

His eyes scanned the area, and his brow furrowed.

The armored warriors were all standing in a neat row, rigid and silent. No one moved to take his Sovereign Stones. Zane looked around, unsure who he was even supposed to hand them to.

His gaze landed on the two figures in blue robes standing apart from the rest, and he went still. “These two are the real deal,” he thought.

By now, Zane had seen his fair share of impossibly powerful people.

Take the Master he met in The Elysian Keep. That was just a shadow his Master had left behind, so it was impossible to guess his true strength.

Then there was the young man who’d taken his sister, Elara, from the Ethereal Spirit Sect. That guy’s power was also completely off the charts.

Then there was the elder from the Demi-Immortal clan he’d encountered during the dual cultivation ceremony. Based on everything Zane had seen, that one was likely a World Overlord.

The two masters in front of him were powerful — genuinely so — but they fell short of that level. Still, Zane had no interest in provoking them.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

The weight of the situation suddenly slammed into the armored men, Clark and Hawthorne, and a chill of pure terror ran down their spines.

Thank God they’d warned Rylan in time not to mess with Zane. If they hadn’t stopped him, and something had happened to Zane on their watch, they’d probably be dead right now.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

Still shaken, they both silently agreed they couldn’t let Rylan run his mouth anymore. Even though they’d gotten some sweet perks from him, they’d be the ones paying the price if he pissed off some ridiculously strong newcomer.

While the other armored men looked on with envy, Master Hill stepped forward and smiled faintly. “Congratulations. You’ve completed your service. Welcome to the Soulbound Sect.”

Zane frowned. “I think there’s been a mistake. I don’t intend to join the Soulbound Sect.”

The armored warriors glanced at each other. The lead warrior had already told the masters exactly this.

Master Sloan spoke from the side. “That’s not your decision. These are orders from the Holy City. We’re taking you there directly.”

The armored warriors’ jaws nearly hit the floor. They’d assumed Zane had caught the eye of the hall masters and might be invited to one of the thirty-six halls — already an unimaginable honor for most. The Holy City itself was another thing entirely.

Zane asked, a hint of doubt in his voice, “The Holy City? What’s that?”

His question took the men by surprise. But then they remembered he was from the Lower World, so of course, he wouldn’t have heard of The Holy City.

Master Hill explained, “It’s the heart of our Soulbound Sect. Think of the Sect as a holy kingdom. The Holy City is its capital—its most sacred ground.”

The analogy clicked, and Zane got it right away. He asked, “And if I say no?”

Hearing Zane even consider refusing, the armored men were floored.

They would have surrendered everything short of their lives for a chance to enter the thirty-six halls. The Holy City was so far beyond that it didn’t even register as something to want — it was simply impossible. And here was this ascender, fresh out of the mine, casually asking what it was.

The two masters, however, didn’t look surprised. They’d seen it all before. They knew there were plenty of top-tier sacred places out there, and some exceptional talents were picky about where they pledged their loyalty.

Master Hill countered, “At your current level, you don’t really have a choice. To be clear, we were ordered to bring you to The Holy City.”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The way he said it, Zane knew the conversation was over. They were all smart enough to understand the unspoken truth: with his current strength, he couldn’t fight his way out of this.
