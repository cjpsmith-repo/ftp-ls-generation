Ep 261 - Never Back Down
Word count: 1190
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Taking down Richard in the tournament hadn’t been easy. Zane had held back, only using skills from the Titan Strike, which made the win feel like more of a lucky break than anything else.
The demonic phantom technique, one of those skills, was far more powerful than he'd anticipated, catching even him off guard. He'd planned to save it for the next round, but he knew he couldn’t count on beating someone like Richard with brute force alone.
As for the disciples from Misty Peak, they were shocked, but at this point, they were almost getting used to it. Zane just kept winning, kept proving everyone wrong, and the shock was starting to wear off.
[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT CROWD]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT]
So when Zane stepped down from the ring, Master Warren seemed to be struggling for the right words. Instead of some hollow praise, he just laughed, grabbing Zane by the shoulders. "You’re Misty Peak’s lucky charm, you know that?"
Maybe, just maybe, Zane’s efforts alone could completely change Misty Peak’s fate. They could climb from last place among The Thirty-Three Peaks all the way to the top—just like Elara had once single-handedly dragged Maiden’s Peak up the ranks to second place.
With this latest victory, any lingering doubts the Misty Peak disciples had about Zane’s strength had vanished.
Richard was on a whole other level compared to Cecil, the guy Zane had fought earlier.
Sure, Cecil was a warrior in the Soul Realm, but he was on the lower end of the spectrum.
So what was the difference?
Richard was the top disciple of Blackrock Peak.
[SFX: DRAMATIC IMPACT]
And Blackrock Peak was ranked second among The Thirty-Three Peaks. Beating Richard meant Zane wasn't just good; he was strong enough to be considered one of the top inner disciples in the entire Skyreach Sect.
With the group stage over, fewer than two hundred disciples remained. The next phase was the rotation round — and this was where things changed. The personal disciples were entering the competition for the first time. They were the sect's elite by any measure; in past All Peaks Competitions, personal disciples had swept the top rankings so completely that the inner disciples barely got a look in.
[AMBIENT: TENSION BUILDING IN CROWD]

But despite his achievements, some people just refused to believe it.
"Maybe Richard just let him win," one disciple muttered, unwilling to accept the outcome.
The conversation immediately pivoted. "Speaking of prodigies, what about Jacob?" another asked.
"Ha! So what? If Zane hadn’t come along, Jacob would still be a legend for reaching Level Two of the Adept Realm and cracking the Top One Hundred. That’s rare, yeah," a senior disciple offered, breaking it down. "But Zane, at the same level, just beat Richard and blasted his way into the top fifty. You gotta understand, the gap between the top hundred and the top fifty is massive. It’s easy to climb a few spots in the hundreds. But in the top fifty, moving up even one spot is a brutal fight. If you ask me, Jacob’s strength three years ago doesn’t even compare to what Zane is doing right now."
"You’re right about three years ago," another senior disciple shot back. "But that was three years ago. Jacob has been training nonstop and just broke through to the Soul Realm. He was already punching above his weight class back then, so can you imagine how terrifying he is now? How is Zane supposed to compete with that?"
The comparison was natural; both Zane and Jacob had first made waves in the tournament while only at Level Two of the Adept Realm.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Hearing the chatter, Jacob slowly walked to the edge of the battle ring. His sharp eyes locked onto Zane, and his voice boomed across the arena. "Lowell! I’m challenging you to a fight!"
[AMBIENT: CROWD FALLS SILENT]
A hush fell over the crowd. No one expected him to be so intense, so direct. The disciples had just been talking; they never thought Jacob would actually call Zane out.
To many, it felt less like a fair challenge and more like a top dog trying to put a newcomer in his place. Zane was only at Level Two of the Adept Realm, while Jacob had already reached the Soul Realm. He was probably a monster even among others at his level.
"Zane, don’t agree to it," Master Warren whispered.
He didn’t want to get on Jacob’s bad side. Even as a master of Misty Peak, he knew Jacob’s mentor was a saint or an elder—someone way above his pay grade.
But Zane met Jacob's gaze without a hint of fear. "Whether we fight or not depends on the rules of the Sect," he said calmly. "If the All Peaks Competition matches us up, I won’t back down."
[SFX: JAW CLENCHING]
Jacob’s jaw tightened, clearly annoyed by the response. "What, are you scared? Don’t worry. I won’t let anyone say I’m bullying you. I’ll power down to the Adept Realm. We’ll fight on your level, fair and square!"
He stared Zane down, his eyes burning with pride.
To his surprise, Zane just laughed. Jacob’s words lit a fire in him. He shot back, his voice sharp as a blade, "Me, scared? Jacob, do you even know what ’fair’ means? I’ll stick to what I said. If we get matched up, you better not hold anything back. Because I sure as hell won’t."
Jacob held his gaze for a moment, then something shifted in his expression — not quite a smile. "Fine," he said. "But if you get eliminated before we meet, that's your answer. It means you were never qualified to face me in the first place."
His tone made it clear: he wasn't worried about being eliminated himself.
A collective gasp went through the disciples from every peak. The entire arena seemed to hold its breath.
At the same time, tens of thousands of disciples watching on the Scrying Slate took a sharp breath. Even though the slate only showed Jacob, Zane’s voice was enough to send a shockwave through the viewing audience.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE RING]
Meanwhile, in another match, Stanley snapped his fan open, whipping it through the air.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
The sharp sound of the fan slicing through the air silenced the crowd. As Stanley's hand flipped the golden fan, streaks of Vitality shot out from the Spirit Weapon.
[SFX: MAGICAL MOVEMENT]
Stanley’s Vitality was... different. He focused his mind on the concept of sharpness itself, so the Vitality drawn out by The Gold Flower Fan didn’t form a shape. Instead, it spun into golden threads as fine as hair. They were nearly invisible until they caught the light.
Zane, like everyone else, was glued to the fight unfolding in the ring. He'd faced a similar attack before. During the trial on Bloody Mountain, Joe Fane had used his Spirit Weapon to create some kind of unbreakable energy wire. Zane had come out of that test covered in a web of cuts.
Watching the golden threads shimmer in the air, Zane could tell they were infinitely more dangerous than Joe’s wires. The whole audience waited, wondering how Jacob would possibly handle this.
And just as they wondered, Jacob simply started walking forward, straight into the storm of deadly golden threads.
