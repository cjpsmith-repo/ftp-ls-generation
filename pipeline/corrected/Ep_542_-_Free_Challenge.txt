Ep 542 - Free Challenge
Word count: 1148
[AMBIENT: MISTY TOMB]
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
A shadow fell over Maddox's face as murderous intent surged through his veins. The Fiery Star Flame—his most devastating technique—had failed. Zane had survived what should have been a killing blow. The kid possessed a resilience that defied expectations, a toughness that made Maddox vibrate with frustration.
If an opportunity presented itself within this celestial tomb's swirling mists, he would strike without mercy. Maddox prided himself on his strategic thinking, on his ability to recognize threats before they matured. He'd watched too many rivals make the fatal mistake of underestimating young prodigies, allowing them time to cultivate their strength. That pattern ended here.
Even if this tomb denied him the chance, alternative paths remained. He would find another method to shatter Cloud Hall's Sect-Protecting Array, another angle to exploit. The outcome was inevitable. One way or another, through poison, blade, or calculated ambush, Zane Lowell would not leave this place breathing.
[SFX: SPINNING LIGHT BEAM]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BUILD]
The beam of light spun, a blur of motion before it slowed, finally stopping on the short man they called Poison King Simon.
[SFX: DICE ROLL]
[SFX: OMINOUS GLOW]
Simon rolled a four. The fourth disc glowed an ominous red.
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD]
A quiet, collective sigh of relief rippled through the crowd. Leaving a man like that alive was a disaster waiting to happen for any warrior from the Heartlands. Even standing near him felt dangerous, as though the very air around his diminutive frame carried traces of death.
Still, he was a master of the Life-and-Death Realm. Raw cultivation at that level transcended ordinary concerns. There was no telling if this would be enough to stop him.
Zane watched the Poison King's expression carefully. The man's face remained impassive, revealing nothing. If the red glow concerned him, he concealed it with the same mastery he applied to his lethal craft.
[SFX: ETHEREAL VOICE ECHO]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Everyone figured the red disc spelled trouble for Simon. But as he stepped onto it, a voice echoed in their minds—not out loud, but inside their own heads. "Free Challenge."
"What?"
"A Free Challenge?"
"Does that mean… he can challenge anyone he wants?"
"This is bad. Way too many people from the Heartlands have crossed him."
The faces of the Heartland's warriors soured. They thought Simon was about to get what he deserved, but instead, he’d just been handed a weapon.
The ancient tycoon of the World Merchants' Guild remained calm, unconcerned that Simon would choose him. The three heavyset leaders flanking him, however, looked tense. Together, the three of them could hold their own against a Life-and-Death Realm warrior. Alone, none of them stood a chance against Simon.
[SFX: SINISTER CHUCKLE]
[MUSIC: MENACING CRESCENDO]
Simon let out a low, menacing chuckle. "A Free Challenge. Now… who to choose?" His gaze swept past the leader of Cloud Hall, Tia, and the others, not even pausing. But when his eyes locked on Maddox, he lingered for a moment.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Maddox sneered. "You want to challenge me?"
"Oh, I'll take your life eventually," Simon shot back. "Just not today." His gaze drifted over the people from the divine realm and settled on the group from Bloodwood Cliff. Lady Darkmere’s face went white, her eyes wide as if she were staring down her worst nightmare.
"What's the matter, Lady Darkmere?" Simon sneered. "You look terrified."
"No, it's… it’s nothing," Lady Darkmere stammered, trying and failing to sound calm.
"Afraid I'm going to pick you?" he pressed.
"You're a master of the Life-and-Death Realm, and I am merely in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm," Lady Darkmere said with a bitter smile. "Of course I'd be no match for you."
The man from the Divine Kingdom laughed coldly. "Is bullying women all you're good for? If you’ve got any guts, try me."
Everyone knew the man from the Divine Kingdom was on another level. Simon had zero chance of winning. After a beat, Simon let out a chilling laugh that scraped the air. "Well, since you asked so nicely," he hissed, "I'll take that challenge!"
As the words left his mouth, Simon took a step toward the center of the platform. But before anything else could happen, the beam of light spun away, landing on Tia.
[SFX: MAGICAL BEAM WHOOSH]
The beam settled on Tia, and her turn arrived.
She knelt gracefully beside the dice, her fingers hovering over them for just a moment. When she released them, they tumbled across the platform.
[SFX: DICE ROLL]
"Six!"
A subtle smile curved Tia's lips as she rose and moved forward six steps.
[SFX: MAGICAL GLOW]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT CHIME]
The sixth disc beneath her feet erupted with a brilliant yellow halo, its light reflecting in her dark eyes.
"Roll again!"
She didn't hesitate. The dice flew from her hand with ease.
[SFX: DICE ROLL]
"Six!"
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
Murmurs rippled through the watching crowd. Two sixes in succession—impressive, but perhaps a coincidence.
"Roll again!"
[SFX: DICE ROLL]
Tia's expression remained serene, almost meditative, as she cast the dice a third time. They clattered across the stone, spinning in perfect synchronization before settling.
"Six!""And again!"
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The crowd stared, speechless. Eyes wide, jaws slack.
It was unbelievable. Tia's luck was off the charts!
[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]
All told, Tia had moved sixty steps, counting the twelve from her previous turns!
Standing on the transparent disc, she was now closing in on the center of the abyss. Everyone else was still practically at the starting line. Zane was probably the most frustrated of them all, stuck just three steps from where he'd started.
But Tia was already far, far ahead of them. Standing alone out in the middle of the chasm, she became the sole focus of every eye.
[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD CHATTER]
"Her luck is unbelievable!"
"It's more than luck. She's the chosen of Cloud Hall. I heard she came from a normal family, no connections at all. To climb from nothing to become one of the strongest women in the Heartlands… who else has that kind of fortune?"
"My god, sixty steps! The end looks like it's at one hundred. A few more rolls like that and she's won the whole thing, right?"
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TONE]
As the crowd buzzed, the Holy Maiden of the Divine Kingdom’s face grew storm-dark.
She was a woman who thrived on being the center of attention, the object of all envy. Just moments ago, she had been in the lead. She couldn't believe that this… this ant… had been blessed with such insane luck. She was the Holy Maiden of a powerful House. How could she be upstaged by a complete nobody?
[SFX: MAGICAL BEAM OF LIGHT]
A vicious glint sparked in her eyes. And in that exact moment, the beam of light stopped, its glow falling directly on her.
