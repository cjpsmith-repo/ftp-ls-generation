Ep 1243 - The Ogre City

Word count: 1143

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
The two cities Zane had just left couldn’t have been more different, and it all came down to the people living in them.
The Ogre city had been a free-for-all. Ogres were naturally aggressive, and once they entered the Illusion Battlefield, their brutish nature took over completely. It was a constant, chaotic brawl where everyone fought everyone.
The human city, on the other hand, was centralized. The fighting was just as frequent, but the spoils—all those precious Illusion Points—were funneled up to one person: Sloane. Her eight top lieutenants collected the “taxes” from every skirmish. She was the undisputed ruler of that city, the only thing keeping it from descending into the same kind of anarchy as the Ogre’s territory. You could kill each other all day long, but at the end of it, everyone paid Sloane.
Strip away Sloane’s grip on the place, and it would look exactly like the Ogre city within a day.
[MUSIC: DISCIPLINED MILITARY MARCH]
[AMBIENT: ORDERLY DEMON CITY]
But this place, the main city of the Demon Night, was something else entirely. It was… orderly.
The Demon Night were a race that lived and died by the chain of command. Fights between them were rare, and they always presented a united front to outsiders. There weren’t even that many warriors around. 
[SFX: MARCHING FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: HEAVY GATE OPENING]
Zane watched as a steady stream of them headed out the city gates, presumably into the wilderness to hunt beasts for points.
They still challenged each other, of course, but they did it with a bizarre sense of decorum, arranging duels outside the city walls where no one would interfere. 
Seeing how disciplined everything was, Zane knew that just jumping someone for their points was off the table. The warriors here weren’t even wary of him; in a city where everyone followed the rules, they just assumed he would, too. He even saw a few Ogres and humans wandering around, moving through the city just as peacefully as everyone else.
“Looks like I came to the wrong place,” Zane muttered to himself, his face falling.
The whole point of the Teleportation Token was to let him jump between cities, picking off high-value targets and racking up a massive score. But this was a bust. Most of the warriors were out in the field, their points were minimal, and what little they had was scattered across the population instead of consolidated under one leader like Sloane. 
[SFX: SOFT MECHANICAL WHIR]
He pulled the Teleportation Token out to check it anyway. A thin layer of black aura still clung to the surface. “Right,” he muttered, tucking it away again. “Can’t use this back to back — has to clear first.” 
With his token on cooldown, his plan was shot. Still, he remembered two more cities sitting to the north and northwest, close enough to reach on foot if he gave it some time. He’d been planning to head north anyway, so maybe tagging along with the group heading for The Deadwood Gate was his best bet.
[AMBIENT: CROWD GATHERING]
As he made his way toward the city gate, he noticed a crowd had already formed there — mostly Demon Night, with a couple of human cultivators mixed in, all watching the horizon like they were waiting on something. Zane sized them up and kept walking. They weren’t worth his time; killing every last one of them wouldn’t net him more than a few tens of thousands of points, and he’d likely get swarmed trying.
Before he could pass them by, three streaks of light dropped out of the sky — Demon Night cultivators, returning from outside the walls.
“There’s a forest eight thousand miles north of the gate,” the first announced. “Iron-scaled spiders nest there — eighty points each. Recommend parties of twenty.”
A good chunk of the crowd peeled off immediately to form a group and head out.
[MUSIC: SUBTLE INTRIGUE]
“Is this... still the Illusion Battlefield?” Zane murmured, watching them organize with the same calm efficiency as everything else in this city.
He’d been thinking about the Battlefield too narrowly — assuming it was just cultivator against cultivator. But the Battlefield spanned fifty great worlds, and beyond the main cities there was an entire wilderness to explore. 
He’d already farmed a good number of points off the Celestial Mice outside the Demon Night city earlier — why wouldn’t there be other ways to profit out here?
There were, evidently. Somewhere on the leaderboard, a cultivator sat at rank ninety-seven, having never killed a single person — strength on par with any clan’s top genius, all of it built on hunting Vicious Beasts in the wild, including a handful of true Divine Beasts.
The second Demon Night cultivator spoke. “Northwest, thirteen thousand miles out, there’s an abandoned tower. Killed the Shining Shadow Evil Spirit on the bottom floor myself — three hundred points. Seven floors total, need at least nine people.”
More cultivators called out and grouped up, the two human warriors among them. Zane felt a flicker of interest — with the token still cooling down, tagging along toward the tower wasn’t a bad way to pass the time. He was about to speak up when the third cultivator cut in ahead of him.
As he was weighing his options, a Demon Night warrior stepped forward and made an announcement. 
“The Deadwood Gate is twenty-four thousand miles north. We’re putting together an urgent rescue mission. Anyone who joins and helps save Amber and Aurora will get five hundred thousand Illusion Points!”
A murmur shot through the crowd. Five hundred thousand points was a staggering reward, enough to land most of them in the top ten million on the leaderboards. 
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
But The Deadwood Gate had a nasty reputation. An emergency rescue there meant something had gone very, very wrong.
“Wait, Amber and Aurora led the city’s elites out there, right?” one of the warriors called out. “Did they fail their expedition?”
The Demon Night warrior shook her head grimly. 
“They didn’t fail. They were ambushed. A group made up mostly of Ogres has them trapped inside The Deadwood Gate. They’re in serious danger.”
[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD MURMURS]
A wave of outrage rippled through the onlookers.
“The Ogres again! It’s always them.”
“Those disgusting scavengers.”
“I’m in. I’ll go to The Deadwood Gate!”
It was the classic Demon Night paradox. They had more than enough power to take whatever they wanted, but they just weren’t wired for conquest. As a result, one of the most powerful races in the Upper World held a laughably small territory of just over three hundred supreme worlds.
[MUSIC: SUBTLE INTRIGUE]
Zane had tuned most of it out—the politics, the ambush, the reward. None of it mattered. But one word cut through the noise and grabbed his full attention.
Amber.
