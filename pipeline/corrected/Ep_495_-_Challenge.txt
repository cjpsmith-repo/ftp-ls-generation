Ep 495 - Challenge

Word count: 1101

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Eden had all but mastered the Wind Law, which paired perfectly with his wind-based attunement. But on top of that, he'd also mastered a second Law, making him a rare dual practitioner.

He wasn't interested in showing off, though. All he wanted right now was to break something. Or someone.

Benson’s expression tightened as he watched Eden charge him like an uncaged animal. He tapped the ground with his longsword, then flicked his wrist upward.

[SFX: WATER RUSHING]

"Water Tornado!"

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

[SFX: WATER EXPLOSION]

With a deafening roar, a dragon made of pure water erupted from the battle stage and lunged for Eden.

The Water Tornado screamed through the air, carrying enough force to blast through a city wall in an instant. But Eden didn't flinch, and he sure as hell wasn't dodging. He just charged straight into it.

[SFX: WATER SPLASH]

[SFX: TEARING IMPACT]

The moment he hit the swirling torrent, he crossed his arms and simply tore it apart. The water dragon, for all its immense power, disintegrated against his raw strength. Eden burst through the spray, his eyes still locked on his real target.

[SFX: BATTLE CRY]

"Die!" he roared, lunging for Benson.

Just then, Benson stomped his foot, and a slick vein of water shot across the stone floor. The sudden surge knocked Eden off balance, making him stumble just past his target. But Eden ignored the cheap trick, spinning around to grab for him again.

This time, Benson rolled clear, dodging Eden's grasp.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHANTING]

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY]

Scrambling to his feet, Benson held his sword horizontally, one hand bracing the flat of the blade, the other gripping the hilt. He closed his eyes, his voice low. "Bestow upon me the power of the Sea God. Fill me with the will of pure water."

"You don't stand a chance." Eden's expression was unnervingly calm. A gust of wind carried him forward, and in a blink, he was floating right in front of Benson. He reached out again. "Die."

He wasn't taking this seriously at all. All he felt was the rage boiling in his blood, and the only thing that would cool it was killing the man in front of him.

But Benson was one of Cloud Hall's best, and he wasn't nearly as weak as Eden seemed to think.

As Eden’s hand shot out, Benson swung his longsword in a vicious arc.

Eden was strong, but he wasn’t Zane. He didn't have that freakishly tough body. There was no way he could catch a sword with his bare hands.

He had no choice but to pull back.

[SFX: WIND WHOOSH]

[SFX: BODY DISSOLVING]

But retreating didn’t mean he was giving up. After dodging the blade, a powerful force enveloped Eden, and his body dissolved into the air.

[SFX: WIND BARRIER ACTIVATION]

"Wind Barrier!"

Only those who understood wind's laws could manifest this technique, concealing themselves within the currents.

[AMBIENT: ARENA]

[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE THEME]

Benson traced his sword in a circle on the ground. Ripples spread across the stone, revealing disturbances in the air to his right.

"There. Break!"

He swung, but Eden's voice whispered in his ear. "Wrong guess. I'm not interested in hide and seek."

[SFX: WIND BLADE SLASH]

Benson leapt right. Too late. Eden's wind blades shredded half his arm, leaving it useless.

Enduring the pain, Benson retreated and raised his sword with his other hand.

Before he could lift it, Eden materialized and struck again.

[SFX: SWORD CLATTER]

The sword clattered down. Both of Benson's arms hung at his side, completely useless.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

[SFX: WIND WHOOSH]

"Time to die," Eden said, raising his hands. Green wind blades shot from his wrists as he slapped toward Benson.

Eden had moved carefully to prevent Benson from spotting the lethal intent. If Benson saw it, he could surrender and ruin everything.

Benson bit his tongue, burning blood essence. He circulated True Energy and formed dark blue water curtains around himself.

"Break!"

[SFX: WATER SHATTER]

Eden shattered them with one swipe, but they slowed the attack enough for Benson to retreat to the arena's edge. "I surrender!"

[MUSIC: TENSION RELEASE]

Kieran removed the barrier and stepped in front of Benson.

Eden rushed forward with a grim smile. "In the Martial Tournament, life and death are up to fate. How can he surrender?"

"Enough," Kieran said, his face dark.

Eden had no choice but to give up, fury burning in his stomach.

"Both sides leave the arena. Eden, draw a card!" Kieran's mustache twitched.

Eden's eyes lit up. If he drew a challenge card, he could face Zane again.

[SFX: CARD FLIP]

[MUSIC: IRONIC TWIST]

He drew a card and turned it over: "Avoid Battle."

[SFX: CARD SHATTERING]

The card shattered in his grip.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

Eden returned to the platform without looking back.

Maddox observed his son's expression and smiled. "You still can't control your anger."

"Father, didn't you tell me not to control myself!" Eden said gloomily.

Maddox shook his head. "Anger can be a fire that forges you, but you're just letting it burn you alive. That won't do you any good. You have to learn how to be angry, son. How to use it."

"You're right, Father," Eden nodded, his jaw tight.

He'd already made up his mind. He didn't care that his own level was leagues above Zane's. The second he got his hands on a challenge card, he was calling him out.

[SFX: DRAMATIC PAUSE]

[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]

Suddenly, just as the old man was about to announce the next battle, Zane’s voice cut through the arena. "Wait!"

Tia's gaze shifted toward him, her brow furrowing. Though Zane stood on the Cloud Hall platform once more, his eyes held a distant quality, as if his thoughts wandered across continents.

His injuries had already vanished—the torn flesh knitting itself together, bruises fading to nothing. His body's extraordinary regenerative abilities worked with quiet efficiency, erasing all evidence of the brutal confrontation.
The old man turned. "What is it?"

"I want to use my challenge card," Zane declared.

"Who do you want to challenge?" the old man and Tia asked in unison.

[AMBIENT: SILENT CROWD ANTICIPATION]

Every eye in the stadium locked onto Zane. He was the first person to use a challenge card in the entire tournament. No one had a clue who he'd pick.

Tia frowned, her mind racing. Could it be Marilyn?

Brent’s eyes were wide with desperate hope. Choose me. Please, let it be me! The waiting was starting to kill him.

"I want to challenge an independent warrior," Zane announced, his voice ringing with conviction. He pulled out his challenge card and pointed across the arena to the platform where Maddox stood.

"Eden Caldwell!"

[MUSIC: INTENSE CONFRONTATION]

[AMBIENT: CROWD GASPS]

"Challenge me?" Eden was stunned. He'd just been plotting how to get a card to take Zane out himself. Who the hell did Zane think he was, challenging him first?

Does he think I'm some kind of easy target? The nerve.

The challenge was exactly what Eden wanted, but the sheer audacity of it still pissed him off.
