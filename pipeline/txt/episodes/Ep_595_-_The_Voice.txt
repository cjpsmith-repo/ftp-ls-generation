Ep 595 - The Voice
Word count: 1216
[AMBIENT: MYSTERIOUS CHAMBER]
Zane was starting to get a handle on how the Talent Tablet worked.
He’d figured out the first trial was a test of a warrior’s understanding of the fundamental Laws. After that, it tested the strength of their body, crushing them with immense pressure to gauge their physical limits.
That was as far as his knowledge went. He had no clue what other mysterious trials the Talent Tablet had in store. But one thing was for sure: when it came to raw physical talent, Zane knew he could take on anyone.
His body was anything but ordinary. A body refiner couldn't hold a candle to him, let alone some warrior who cultivated Vitality. Once he broke through to Relic-Class—or even higher—his power would be terrifying. And with his potential, he was on the fast track to reaching that incredible level.
After the physical trial came the test of the soul. He’d barely scraped by on that one, too.
Now that he’d passed all three, Zane figured it had to be over.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
He stared at the sealed door set into the wall ahead. The dark, eerie space gave him a gut feeling that this wasn't the end. He just knew something else was waiting for him.
"What else could they possibly test? Don't tell me they're going to hand me an cultivation method," Zane thought, a dry chuckle escaping him.
The idea was a long shot, but not impossible. Gifting him a new method could be a test in itself. He knew any method from the Tower of Sin would be anything but simple, but he’d still be pretty happy to get one. Still, it was just a passing thought.
[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
As he stood there, wondering what came next, a pair of eyes suddenly materialized in the darkness before him.
In this vast and diverse world, creatures came in all shapes and sizes. But no matter how different they were, they all had one thing in common: eyes. True Dragons had them, Blue Phoenixes had them, and humans had them. It was a universal sign of life.
So when pinpricks of light coalesced into a pair of eyes right in front of him, he froze. "Who are you?" Zane asked, his gaze locked on them.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TOWER INTERIOR]
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
Another point of light appeared, dancing in the air before forming the shape of a mouth. Then, a distant, ancient voice echoed, "Human, I am the spirit of this Tower of Sin!"
"The spirit of the tower!" Zane's eyes widened, and he shook his head in disbelief.
[MUSIC: ANCIENT ETHEREAL]
"Yes," the melodic voice continued. "As the spirit of the tower, I have existed here for a million years."
In the Heartlands, the oldest known figures were ancient overlords—freakish by any measure. Even the most venerable among them had lifespans measured in tens of thousands of years, not millions. The idea that anything could persist that long made Zane's skin crawl.
"There is no need for surprise," the spirit said, unbothered. "My kind exists in a different form from yours. We have no physical body—we are something closer to pure awareness. We do not age. As long as this Tower stands, I cannot die."
Zane turned this over in his mind. It made a strange kind of sense. He thought of his own spirit companion—bound to him, tied to his survival. This was the same principle, just on an incomprehensible scale. Bound not to a person, but to the Tower itself.
"What do you want with me?" Zane asked.
"Your talent is the finest I have witnessed in a million years. You have passed all three tests. None of them found your limit. Because of this, I wish to ask you something. Are you willing to take a fourth test?"
Zane's eyes sharpened. "There's another one?"
"Yes. This test has never been administered before. You are the first person to ever be offered it."
Zane raised an eyebrow. "What's in it for me?"
"This Tower was built as a trial ground—a means for its master's race to grow stronger. Every warrior who passes the Talent Tablet receives something in return. If you pass this final test, what you receive will be far greater than what any other warrior has ever been given."
Zane had heard Olivia mention it once — that the Talent Tablet didn't just measure talent. It enhanced it. The idea had lodged itself in the back of his mind ever since. Now, hearing this, his interest sharpened into something closer to hunger.
"I'll do it," he said, without hesitation. "But I have a question first."
"Ask it."
"Who built this Tower? What race did they come from?"
The spirit was quiet for a long moment. "I can tell you this much: the master of this Tower belonged to the Demi-Immortal Race. They cannot be classified as human."
Zane filed it away. It wasn't much, but it was a thread. When he reached the bottom of the Tower, maybe the rest of the answers would be waiting for him.
"Your question has been answered," the spirit said. "The final test begins now. I hope you are ready."
[AMBIENT: COSMIC VOID]
[SFX: WHOOSH FALLING]
As the spirit's voice faded, the light around Zane began to shift and warp. Suddenly, he was falling, sinking into what felt like an endless, starry sky.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]
Mysterious patterns swirled into existence around him, seeming to swallow him whole. They were abstract, each one unique, and the raw energy they radiated filled Zane with a profound sense of awe. All he could do was stare. The feeling seemed to bypass thought and hit him on a purely instinctual level.
Zane stared at them, his mind racing. "Is this the final test? What exactly is it supposed to be testing?"
As he mulled over the questions, the nearest pattern shot toward him. A flicker of strange familiarity hit him. It was just like when his consciousness brushed against a star during his Stellar Body cultivation.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
As the pattern drew closer, Zane felt impossibly small, like a single grain of sand against a tidal wave.
Zane's brow furrowed. "This feels wrong."
The energy inside him exploded outward, crashing against the encroaching pattern.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ANCIENT THEME]
A flash of brilliant white light erupted, and a person materialized in front of Zane. The figure wore simple, ancient robes and held an emerald shortsword.
[SFX: ETHEREAL VOICE ECHO]
Right on cue, the spirit's voice rang out. "This is a Soul Echo. The one before you is the echo left behind when this master died."
Zane shot back, raising an eyebrow. "And?"
"Defeat him," the spirit replied simply.
A knot of anxiety tightened in Zane's stomach. "How strong is he?"
"When he was alive," the spirit explained, "his strength was equal to a master in the Soul Sea Realm."
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REALIZATION]
Zane was speechless. Was this some kind of joke?
No matter how powerful he was, there was no way he could stand against a master from the Soul Sea Realm.
A warrior that strong could crush him with one hand. Was that about to happen to him now?
