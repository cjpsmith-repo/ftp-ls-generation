Ep 50 - The Return of the Shattered Dagger
Word Count: 1218
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN TWILIGHT]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Noticing the confused look on Zane's face, Isla Novak attached a tiny ring to the hilt of the Shattered Dagger and slipped its twin onto her thumb. Then, she hurled the knife down the mountainside. Zane gasped. The blade was sharp enough to bury itself anywhere, and in the deepening twilight, finding it would be a nightmare.
[SFX: KNIFE ZIPPING THROUGH AIR]
But a moment later, Isla flicked her wrist, and the Shattered Dagger zipped right back into her hand. Zane stared in amazement. The two tiny rings were so discreet, it was as if an invisible string connected them.
After the throw, a simple tug was all it took to recall it. Even though it was razor-sharp, Isla caught it barehanded without so much as a flinch.
Zane was blown away by her skill.
Zane took the Shattered Dagger and the pair of small rings from her, bowing respectfully. "Thank you for this generous gift."
Isla chuckled and ruffled his hair. "None of that formal stuff. Soon enough, you'll be one of my own on Misty Peak. Now, go on and get some rest," she said warmly. "Tomorrow, after the peak selections are done, you'll choose your upgrade skills."
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN PATH]
Zane nodded and headed back down the mountain path.
[SFX: KNIFE THROW]
On the way, he slipped one ring onto his thumb and the other onto the hilt of his Shattered Dagger. He threw the blade just as he'd seen Isla do, but when he tugged, the knife shot straight back "at him".
[SFX: KNIFE WHOOSHING]
[MUSIC: SUDDEN DANGER]
Breaking out in a cold sweat, Zane dove for the dirt, the knife slicing through the air where his head had just been. Zane was tough, but not tough enough to survive a direct hit from that blade. How did she make catching it barehanded look so effortless? Why couldn't he do it?
Judging by Isla's confidence and Zane's unwavering choice, it was obvious the two had struck some kind of deal. The other masters stayed quiet, but you could tell they were silently judging her. If Isla were being unreasonable, they might have spoken up. But she wasn't, and they all knew crossing her was just asking for trouble.
[MUSIC: CEREMONIAL TRANSITION]
With that settled, the competition for the top disciple was over. The masters moved on to the second-ranked Outer Disciple.
Following Zane's lead, Ethan Grant chose Misty Peak as well.
It took the better part of the day to assign every disciple from the trials to a peak. But the day's work wasn't over. Next came the crucial task of choosing their upgrade paths.
[AMBIENT: GRAND LIBRARY HALL]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL DISCOVERY]
Within the Skyreach Sect stood a hall known as the Celestial Library.
This library housed manuals for nearly every upgrade path known in the Empire.
But a Skyreach Sect pledge couldn't just walk in and pick one. Each selection cost a hundred Points—which had a market value of about two hundred Crystal Cubes.
For pledges from the Great Houses, a hundred Points might as well have been pocket change, but for most commoners, it was a fortune. What frustrated everyone, rich or poor, was the sheer number of upgrade paths in the Celestial Library. With countless options ranging from high to low level, making the right choice was nearly impossible.
Some pledges would finally save up a hundred Points, only to walk away with something like the Savage Fist Style—a cheap manual you could buy for a dozen gold pieces in the Capital.
Spending a hundred Points on that was a complete rip-off.
There was a real art to making a choice in the Celestial Library.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN FOREST]
All pledges were granted a single trip to the Library to choose their path. Guided by their masters, they set out. The deeper one ventured into the grounds of the Skyreach Sect, the more its sheer scale became apparent. They passed through mountain forests, skirted serene lakes, and navigated between incredible buildings of all shapes and sizes before finally arriving at the gates of a grand hall.
[SFX: PULSATING LIGHT]
A violet haze enveloped the entire structure, with pulsating streaks of amethyst light emanating from within its walls. As Zane fixed his gaze upon the ethereal glow, a sharp pain pierced his eyes, causing him to wince slightly. This discomfort struck a chord of recognition within him—a diluted version of the sensation he had experienced from the Cobalt Dragon Effigy nestled in his consciousness. His heart quickened at this unexpected connection.
[MUSIC: APPROACHING DANGER]
"Pledges, avert your eyes from the light immediately," cautioned one of the masters, his voice tense with genuine concern. "Those radiant beams can inflict damage upon your soul, penetrating directly through your vision." The master's weathered face reflected years of witnessing such dangers, his furrowed brow emphasizing the gravity of his warning.
At the warning, the pledges immediately tore their eyes away.
A person's soul was everything, and any damage to it was called a "soul injury." Even a minor soul injury could leave a person dizzy and weak for days. A more serious one could cause paralysis or even permanent brain damage, turning them into a mindless husk for years.
"What is that light, Master?" one of the pledges asked.

"That is Will," the master said. "Everything in the world carries a Will of its own. Humans have it. The heavens have it. A sword carries Sword Intent." He gestured toward the pulsating violet beams. "What you see there is the Will of a Vengeful Beast — one our sect hunted down long ago and buried beneath this hall. Its body is gone, but its Will endures."
[AMBIENT: SUPERNATURAL PRESSURE]
The explanation landed differently on Zane than on the others. If the light was a slain beast's Will… then what had he been living with inside his own mind all this time? The thought sent a quiet tremor through him. He didn't ask the question aloud. Not yet.
The pressure in this hall was stronger, and some of the pledges already looked pale and nauseous.
A person's ability to resist the will of the Vengeful Beast—the source of this pressure—came down to the strength of their soul.
Most pledges had never cultivated their soul strength—an innate quality distinct from physical power, though some naturally possessed greater spiritual fortitude than others.
[AMBIENT: SUPERNATURAL PRESSURE]
Several determined disciples stood rooted in place, eyes clenched shut, battling against the Vengeful Beast's overwhelming presence. Their resistance proved futile as their faces drained of color before dizziness overtook them.
[SFX: PERSON COLLAPSING]
"I can't..." gasped one pledge as he staggered backward toward the exit. Another collapsed mid-retreat, requiring others to drag his limp form away.
The remaining candidates recognized their limitations. They retreated to the outer hall, wisely choosing to browse for upgrade paths there rather than risk irreparable soul damage.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL LIBRARY]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Only a select few dared venture deeper into the library's purple haze.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT INTENSIFYING]
Zane took a step forward, then another. The pressure crushed against his consciousness like an invisible vise, yet something within him resonated with the ancient power.
As others fell away, he pressed onward—alone now, moving toward a destiny that called to him from the shadows beyond.
