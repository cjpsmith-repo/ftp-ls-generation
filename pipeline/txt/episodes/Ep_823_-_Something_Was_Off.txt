Ep 823 - Something Was Off

Word count: 1121

[AMBIENT: CROWD LAUGHTER]

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

The laughter was still echoing through the arena when the Little Monster's expression curdled into something dangerous.

The Little Monster had just made a big show of saying he wouldn’t use his Vitality against Zane—only his raw physical strength. But without Vitality, he couldn’t fly. Zane was already perched at the very top of the enchanted barrier, so how exactly was the creature supposed to even get to him?

He'd already made his boast, though, and he couldn't back down now.

[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]

So he broke into a dead sprint toward the edge of the barrier. The creature looked bizarre, all long, gangly arms and legs, but the moment he started moving, it was like every part of his body snapped into perfect, terrifying sync. He became a streamlined weapon, a warrior with a single purpose.

He crossed the entire arena in a few heartbeats and hit the enchanted barrier without breaking stride, scrambling up its shimmering surface.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Halfway to the top, he did something impossible: he flipped backward, planted his feet against the barrier, and used the momentum to launch himself through the air like a cannonball, straight at Zane.

A mocking grin touched Zane's lips as he watched the creature somehow manage to reach him without an ounce of Vitality.

[SFX: TELEPORT WHOOSH]

Zane vanished, reappearing a split second later directly behind the mid-air monster, his arms already outstretched. He threw a punch — three hundred Dragon Scales behind it — and the Little Monster hit the bottom of the barrier like a rubber ball.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

But something was off. The punch had landed clean, and yet it had felt wrong — soft, almost boneless, like hitting a sack of water. The Little Monster didn't seem injured at all.

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

The Little Monster peeled himself off the barrier floor and slowly stood. Those black eyes fixed on Zane with a feral gleam.

“Don't you plan to use your Vitality?” Zane asked, hands behind his back.

“No need,” the Little Monster sneered. “You're not worth it.”

“If you want to keep getting hit,” Zane said, “I'm happy to oblige.”

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

Zane gritted his teeth. The thing could take a punch just as well as he could.

But they were complete opposites. Zane had tempered his body in the Flames of Rebirth, transforming it into a living weapon as hard and invincible as a Relic. 

The Little Monster, though? His body was soft, almost boneless. Every attack felt like punching a sponge. Zane realized with a surge of frustration that actually hurting this thing was going to be next to impossible.

“Shall we continue?” Zane called down, forcing a casual tone.

[SFX: CREATURE SNARL]

The Little Monster scrambled back to his feet, spat on the ground, and shot Zane a wicked, twisted smile. He didn't bother with words. He just launched himself back into the air.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

Zane met him the same way — blinked behind him, five hundred Dragon Scales this time. The punch landed. The Little Monster hit the floor again. And again, nothing.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Again, Zane blinked behind him, this time ready to unleash the power of eight hundred Dragon Scales. If five hundred couldn't do the trick, maybe this would.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

But just as he was about to strike, the monster’s arm—impossibly—shot out toward him. For a confusing second, Zane couldn't process what he was seeing. The creature's arm had twisted completely around, reaching backward to grab him.

It was a move no human could ever make. It was like trying to twist your neck a full three hundred and sixty degrees; a normal person would have snapped their own bones. The creature’s flexibility was off the charts. 

Zane was still trying to wrap his head around it when the arm caught him, its grip instantly tightening around his own.

[SFX: TIGHT GRIP]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS BASS]

Like a living rope, the arm coiled around Zane’s, binding him completely.

In the stands, the noise died. Even the Soul Sea masters had gone still.

“Ha!” the Little Monster cackled, a joyful, predatory look on his face. “See? Told you. I don't need Vitality for someone like you.”

[SFX: POWERFUL THROW]

His vicious grin widened. A colossal surge of strength flowed through his arm, and with a grunt, he flung Zane toward the ground.

Caught completely by surprise, Zane had no time to prepare—all he could do was brace for impact.

[SFX: CRASH INTO BARRIER FLOOR]

[SFX: STRETCHING LIMB]

[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]

But the Little Monster wasn't done. The arm was still coiled around him, and now it began to stretch — seven feet, eight feet — the Little Monster hanging in the air above him, using the tension of his own extended limb like a slingshot. His free fist came down like a meteor aimed at Zane's head.

Zane tilted his head. The fist hit the barrier floor instead.

[SFX: STRUCTURE SHAKING]

The entire structure shook.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: ARENA OBSERVATION AREA]

The Holy Maiden watched from the York Divine Kingdom's corner, her expression complicated. When she'd first gone to find the Little Monster, she'd been certain Zane was finished. Now she wasn't so sure.

The senior leaders of the Nine Cauldron Martial House had been shaken by the discovery of this creature. They'd dispatched over a thousand Inner-Core Realm warriors into Sevenwaters Forest just to investigate, and even then, it took a ten-day-long chase before they finally managed to capture him.

At the time, he was only seven years old, with strength barely at the Marrow Refining level. He'd never had a single lesson in combat; everything he knew, he'd taught himself.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

When the Nine Cauldron Martial House's trial party entered Sevenwaters Forest — tens of thousands of warriors — they never came back out. 

Every last one of them was dead. The bodies were found scattered across the forest floor, killed in different ways — but with one thing in common. Every single head had been smashed open. Every single brain had been sucked clean. The scale of it stunned the upper ranks of the Nine Cauldron Martial House. Whatever had done this wasn't a beast. It was something worse.

The Nine Cauldron Martial House spared the Little Monster's life. Dangerous as he was, the upper ranks wanted to unravel his secrets. They ended up learning more than they ever expected. For one, his bones were unnervingly soft and flexible.

[SFX: BLADE CUTTING]

In one experiment, they severed an arm.

[MUSIC: EERIE REVELATION]

[SFX: REGENERATION SOUND]

A few days later, a brand-new one had grown in its place.
