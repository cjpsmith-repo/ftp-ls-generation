 Zane crept to the edge of the shimmering shell, putting him just feet away from Netherlord Aldrich.f
They say you can't get distracted during a battle of Vitality, but Aldrich scoffed at such mortal rules. As the Netherlord, he considered himself above human limitations; a minor distraction meant nothing to him.
Zane knew rushing in blindly was a death sentence—he'd end up skewered on one of the Netherlord's spikes in a heartbeat. For now, caution was his only play. His safest bet, he figured, was to strike with his Shattered Dagger. He wasn't naive enough to think he could kill the Netherlord in one hit, but even a small injury might be enough to throw him off his game.
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
[SFX: MAGICAL WEAPON FLIGHT]
 With that, Zane pressed his Storage Ring, sending the Shattered Dagger rocketing toward Netherlord Aldrich’s back.
Meanwhile, Aldrich mentally berated himself for his careless oversight. He had summarily dismissed the youth when unleashing his Hellfire, regarding him as nothing more than an insignificant speck beneath his notice. 
"What a foolish lapse in judgment," he thought bitterly, his ancient pride wounded. Masters of the Soul Realm barely warranted his consideration, so why would he have spared a thought for some fragile human stripling? He had been utterly convinced the boy would simply disintegrate into ash and memory.
Never in his centuries of existence had Aldrich envisioned a mortal not merely withstanding his Hellfire but actually consuming it as though it were a pleasant draught. The very notion sent an unfamiliar chill through his otherwise burning core. This unexpected development altered the entire battlefield calculus. His crimson eyes narrowed as he studied the boy with newfound wariness, his clawed fingers flexing with suppressed rage.
[SFX: AIR DARKENING]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BUILDUP]
 "A mistake," he murmured to himself, the words tasting like poison on his tongue. "One I shall not repeat." The air around him darkened as his focus sharpened, his previous dismissive attitude replaced by deadly intent.
Zane, on the other hand, still had faith in his Titan Strike. He'd used it to defeat Hugh, a master of the Soul Realm, once before, though Zane knew he’d only won because Hugh hadn't been giving it his all.
[SFX: IMPACT THUD]
Just like the Shattered Dagger, the dark purple fist of energy slammed into an invisible barrier before it could even reach Netherlord Aldrich.
[MUSIC: TENSE DEFEAT]
 Zane had failed again. His face fell with frustration.
“Damn it,” he thought, a wave of hopelessness washing over him. “Neither the knife nor the Titan Strike worked. Looks like I have no choice but to get in close. Crap. But he's so strong... way out of my league. What if he just swats me out of the air and kills me in a split second?”
 "Listen to me, boy!" Netherlord Aldrich thundered, his commanding voice echoing across the battlefield like rolling thunder. "Cease this pointless resistance, and I shall bestow upon you the mercy of serving in my ranks when Silverspire falls to my dominion."
Zane's lips curled into a contemptuous smile, his eyes flashing with defiance despite the exhaustion gnawing at his limbs. "Sorry, but I've never been particularly skilled at taking orders. Not interested in being anyone's slave."
"Then what would you aspire to be?" Netherlord Aldrich inquired, genuine curiosity mingling with the malice in his tone as he studied the audacious youth before him.
With unwavering resolve hardening his features, Zane locked eyes with the powerful being and sneered. "How about your master instead? That arrangement sounds far more appealing to me."
[MUSIC: DARK THREATENING]
 Netherlord Aldrich’s voice dropped, laced with fury. "You dare? You dare insult a Netherlord? Do you have any idea what the consequences are for that?"
"Not interested in finding out," Zane said coldly. "All I know is humans and demons are enemies. Don't waste your breath trying to get me to surrender."
[SFX: EVIL ENERGY SURGE]
 "You have spirit, boy. It's a shame you have to die. I hope you don't regret this choice." With that, Netherlord Aldrich intensified his attack, increasing the flow of evil energy. The pressure on Yolande Shaw grew heavier.
In that moment, her face paled, and she was clearly struggling to hang on. Zane watched her, noting how she kept that same blank expression, even now. Her attempt to look composed almost seemed pathetic. But as much as he disliked her, he had to respect her determination.
*Guess it's now or never,* Zane thought grimly. He had to do something. If Yolande fell, he'd be in an even worse spot than anyone in Silverspire.
As Yolande and Aldrich remained locked in their standoff, Zane paced anxiously inside the barrier, his mind racing for a plan. Then, his eyes landed on Hugh in the distance, and an idea clicked. He immediately started waving his hands, trying to get Hugh's attention.
Of all the masters from the Soul Realm, Hugh was the only one Zane had ever beaten, but that didn't mean he was weak. Anyone who reached the Soul Realm was either exceptionally gifted or from a privileged background.
Hugh saw Zane's frantic signals and instantly understood. During their last fight, Zane had managed to absorb Hugh’s Apex Dragon, twisting it with his Corrupt Vitality. He knew Zane could absorb another person's Vitality and make it his own. Hugh knew exactly what the boy was planning.
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
 Without a second's hesitation, Hugh threw his arms out, unleashing his Apex Dragon and sending it hurtling straight toward Zane.
*This ends now!*
[SFX: POWERFUL LEAP]
 With his mind made up, Zane launched himself from the barrier, closing the distance to Netherlord Aldrich in a single leap.
The moment Zane moved, Yolande Shaw seized the opportunity. As Aldrich's focus shifted, she began to pour more Vitality into her own attack, preparing a final, decisive blow. Whatever the boy was doing, it didn't matter as long as it kept the Netherlord distracted.
[SFX: ENERGY SPIKE]
 As he hurtled toward the Netherlord, a sharp, pale-yellow thorn shot from Zane's forehead, aimed right at Aldrich. "Psyche Spike!" He knew it wouldn't do much real damage—its only purpose was to create a diversion.
The gambit worked. All eyes were on Zane's suicidal charge. No one, not even Zane himself—who was completely focused on his attack—noticed the subtle, dangerous shift in Yolande's expression.
