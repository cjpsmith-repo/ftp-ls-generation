Ep 1092 - Looks Can Be Deceiving

Word count: 1144

[MUSIC: MOUNTING DANGER]

[AMBIENT: LARGE CROWD MURMUR]

On the other side of Willow Island, tens of thousands of warriors were packed into a large square, their voices a low roar. The journey into the Orange Sea Realm was a massive opportunity, a chance to test their mettle against the top talents from the Thirteen Palaces. More than that, injuring or killing one of those prodigies came with rewards that had them all practically drooling.

But this season of Passing the Torch was almost over. If they missed their shot now, they’d have to wait for the next one.

[SFX: CONFUSED CHATTER]

“Wait, there are only a hundred and sixty-one opponents left,” one warrior said, confused. “Shouldn’t the test be over by now?”

“Yeah, it’s not two hundred, so this should have been the last group,” another agreed. “But now they’ve set up a special fight with just two of them? What’s that about?”

“If they’re getting a private match, they must be something special,” a third chimed in, eyes glued to the massive Scrying Slate that showed the arena. “A test with just two fighters? They have to be exceptional.”

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

A grizzled veteran scoffed. “Exceptional? They’re just a couple of kids from the Life-and-Death Realm. I bet that one at the fifth level wouldn’t have even made it past our first round.”

What he called their “first round” was actually the eleventh round of the test. These warriors only joined the Illusionary Arena after the initial ten rounds, so they never saw the fighters who’d been eliminated early on. As they all chattered, they watched Zane and Layla’s images on the slate, their own fates tied to the outcome. These hired fighters were brought into the arena in waves, sorted by strength, with the strongest saved for the final rounds.

[AMBIENT: ARENA BATTLE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

Back inside the Illusionary Arena, Zane and Layla were swarmed.

These weren’t mindless puppets you could just hack your way through. They were thinking, breathing opponents who used actual strategies. Still, their training was rudimentary. Their only real advantage was their numbers, and even then, they were all just at the seventh or eighth level of the Life-and-Death Realm—only a few levels higher than Zane, which meant they were no real threat at all.

[SFX: LIGHTNING STRIKE]

[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]

Zane’s sword, Thundergust, erupted in a series of blinding flashes. Beams of pure light shot out, and four of his opponents vanished on the spot. The other thirteen scrambled back, their agility techniques barely saving them from the deadly assault.

[SFX: SPECTRAL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]

Zane’s figure flickered, and in an instant, he was standing behind all thirteen of them. He raised Thundergust overhead and brought it down in a single, unhurried arc.

[SFX: SPIRIT HOWL]

Thirteen ghostly figures tore free from the blade — Nether God Shadows, each one a near-perfect copy of Zane himself, moving with his exact intent. They tore through the remaining warriors in an instant, shredding them and flinging what was left clean out of the Illusionary Arena.

[AMBIENT: PALACE OBSERVATION DECK]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Up on the observation platform, the Palace Lords stared, baffled. “How did he do that?” one of them asked. “Elder Dunn’s Nether God Shadow could only ever split into three.”

“Thirteen, all at once,” another murmured. “Is it the sword, or is it him?”

Yolande gave a small laugh. “It’s him. The Nether God Mind Method was Elder Dunn’s own technique — he taught it to Zane personally. Even Elder Dunn never managed more than three. I have no idea how this kid managed thirteen.”

The Palace Lords fell silent, clearly struggling to recalibrate their sense of what Zane was.

[AMBIENT: DISTANT CROWD MURMUR]

Out in the great square, the tens of thousands of sparring-partner warriors had gone quiet too. A few minutes ago, some of them had been confident they could at least land a hit. Now, watching the replay on the Scrying Slate, none of them said a word.

“...So that’s what the top of the Thirteen Palaces actually looks like,” one finally managed.

“And they’re still just the reserves,” someone else added grimly. “If the real prodigies of Gossamer Pavilion are even sharper than this—”

“Enough,” a third cut in, jaw tight. “Whoever goes up against him next round — make it our best. We’re not losing face twice.”

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

When the warriors assigned to Layla’s platform got their first look at their opponent — a young woman, striking even through the haze of the Illusory Space — more than a few of them relaxed. Whatever happened in here, it was just a projection. No real consequences. A couple of them traded grins and started talking about exactly what they planned to do to her once they got close.

They had no idea what they were asking for.

The fastest of them got within twenty feet before his heart suddenly began to pound erratically. A moment later, all of them found it hard to breathe, their bodies refusing to obey their commands as a sharp pain exploded from their cores.

[SFX: HEARTBEAT PULSE]

[MUSIC: HORROR STRINGS]

Normally, pain that intense would make a warrior pass out. But somehow, they all stayed conscious, trapped and awake, every nerve screaming from the vibrations emanating from Layla’s sword. It was like being a prisoner tortured over and over, shocked back to consciousness with a splash of cold water every time the blackness of fainting promised relief. The pain was so unbearable that they found themselves begging to die just to escape the illusion.

It was only then that they realized how badly they had underestimated her. Layla wasn’t even focused on competing with Zane anymore. Instead, her entire focus was on precisely controlling the agony her opponents felt, calibrating it for maximum effect.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]

It lasted for only a few minutes, but for the warriors caught in her power, it felt like centuries.

[MUSIC: CALM TENSION]

Just then, a calm voice cut through the arena. “Layla, stop.” It was the man in the simple cotton robe. His tone was mild, but it carried an undeniable authority. After all, the Thirteen Palaces had invited these fighters; it wouldn’t look good if one of their own was seen torturing them for sport.

[SFX: SWORD SCRAPING METAL]

Layla scoffed, a flicker of annoyance in her eyes. With a fluid motion, she drew a circle in the air with the tip of her sword and then plunged it into the platform. Her ten-foot-long black hair swirled around her, fanning out as she flicked the steel surface of the blade with a single finger.

[SFX: METALLIC RING]

A clear, metallic ring echoed across the arena.

[SFX: VIOLENT EXPLOSION]

[MUSIC: HORROR STRINGS]

The suffering warriors instantly exploded into a fine mist of blood.
