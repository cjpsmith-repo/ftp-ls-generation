Ep 903 - Playing the Part

Word count: 1117

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Heads snapped around as a figure dove from above, moving at an incredible speed. Everyone recognized the body—it was the woman from the Spirit Supreme Realm.

[AMBIENT: PANICKED CROWD]

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

A mad rush erupted. War King Wind grabbed Fiona and the Crown Prince, physically dragging them toward the front of the pack. The rule was simple and unspoken: stay ahead to live, fall behind to die.

“Holy crap, they're fast,” Zane muttered, watching the Soul Sea Realm masters ahead of him scatter at a mind-boggling pace. He couldn't stop the power coursing through him—a warm, pulsing Vitality that spread from his stomach. 

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

The Vitality was a nonstop stream of power flowing from the girl's Inner World. Zane channeled it and, with a single thought, launched himself into the sky.

Here on The Emperor's Isle, flying was supposed to be impossible. The blood sacrifice that powered the necropolis had nullified it, and the corpses everyone inhabited were drained of Vitality. That's why the Soul Sea Realm masters—men who could normally soar through the heavens—were stuck scrambling on their own two feet.

[SFX: FAST WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]

But their frantic sprinting was no match. Zane shot past them in a blur.

This speed is insane, Zane thought, stunned by the force of his own acceleration. Is this the power of a Spirit Supreme Realm master?

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[SFX: LOUD CRASH]

He poured more of the borrowed Vitality into his flight and blasted forward, moving faster than he’d ever imagined possible. Even a combination of his own energy and Vitality couldn’t hold a candle to this. In an instant, he’d not only caught up to the frontrunners but blown right past them. 

The problem was, he wasn't used to this body and had no idea how to pump the brakes. He overshot his landing completely and slammed into the ground ahead of the group, carving a massive crater out of the earth as he tumbled.

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHISPER]

A sharp, indignant thought, not his own, echoed in his mind. ”Hey, that’s my body! Don’t be so reckless!”

The Soul Sealing Spell kept the girl from physically controlling her limbs, but it didn't stop him from feeling her outrage. The crash hadn't caused any real damage, but that didn't mean she was okay with him treating her body like a cannonball.

Zane pushed himself to his feet, dusting himself off with an embarrassed grin. 

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

He walked toward the group of Soul Sea masters with a smile — and was met with a wall of cold, hard stares.

Every one of them looked like a man preparing to die. 

Scott stepped forward from the group, his expression the calmest of the lot.

“Senior,” he said carefully, “we stumbled into this place by accident. If we've caused any offense, we ask your forgiveness.”

Zane looked at him. Then, slowly, he let the smile drop from his face.

“Forgiveness?” he said, his voice going cold. “Stumbled in by accident? That's a generous way to put it.”

The mood among the Soul Sea masters turned bleak. If this woman wanted a fight, they were finished — occupying borrowed corpses, cut off from their full strength, facing a Spirit Supreme Realm warrior on her home ground.

Then Fiona pushed through the crowd.

“Senior.” Her voice was steady despite everything. “What did you do to Zane? Is he dead?”

Even now, she was still asking about him. Zane felt something shift in his chest.

He looked at her — and smiled. “Of course I'm fine.”

[MUSIC: DAWNING RECOGNITION]

The words landed strangely. Fiona's brow furrowed. She stared at the woman's face, at that smile, trying to place why it felt so familiar. A person could change their appearance. They could change their body. But some things didn't change — small habits, the way a smile reached the eyes.

The realization hit her like a thunderclap.

“Zane,” she said. “You're Zane. You actually did it.”

Zane nodded.

The tension shattered. The Soul Sea masters looked at each other, then back at the woman — at Zane — and the relief that swept through the group was almost physical. Scott laughed first. Then the others followed.

Once the shock wore off, curiosity took over. They crowded around him, sizing up Brianna's body with undisguised fascination. Randy grinned.

“So,” he said, “how does it feel?”

Zane gave him a look. There was no good answer to that question. But this was a Spirit Supreme Realm body. With Brianna's power behind him, his odds of getting through the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land — and getting to the Ancestral Dragon's Egg — had just multiplied several times over. He could put up with a little strangeness for that.

“It's different,” he said flatly.

Fiona noticed the state of his borrowed clothes — torn to shreds from the crash landing, her legs almost completely exposed — and clucked her tongue in disapproval before tossing him a bundle of fabric.

Zane caught it, his momentary gratitude dissolving into speechlessness when he saw what it was: an emerald green dress. He knew she meant well, but there was no way he was putting that on. Shaking his head, he pulled a simple shirt and pants from his own Storage Ring and wrapped it around his new form.

[AMBIENT: EERIE NECROPOLIS]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

With Zane now occupying the powerful body, the path was clear for the four Emperors to claim their own prizes on the thirteenth floor. They returned a short while later, each having successfully taken over a new corpse. Even inside their new shells, none of them could hide their elation.

Only one of them had ever managed to claim a body from the thirteenth floor before. Now, all four had succeeded. It suddenly seemed entirely possible that they could explore the entire Heavenly Stellar Secret Land in one go—and maybe even claim the Ancestral Dragon's egg.

“We have you to thank for this,” the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom said, his voice ringing with genuine gratitude.

War King Wind nodded in agreement. “He's right. Without you, we never would have gathered enough Soul Cores to even attempt this.” 

Now inhabiting Victor's body, he stood like an iron tower in the middle of the crowd, radiating a powerful and commanding presence.

Zane smiled. “I have a feeling I'm going to need all of your help before this is over.” 

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: DISTANT WIND]

His gaze shifted toward the far end of the sprawling necropolis. The Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, the very core of the Dragon-Blooded legacy, was located somewhere high above it all.
