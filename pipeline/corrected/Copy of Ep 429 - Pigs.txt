Ep 429 - Pigs

Word count: 1220

[AMBIENT: BUSY TAVERN]

[MUSIC: LIGHT FOLK MUSIC]

Man, this food was incredible. The smell of roasted garlic and onions saturated the tavern, and juice practically exploded from the venison as Zane carved into it. 

He tore into it like a man who hadn't seen a real meal in weeks, each bite a warm, savory reminder of home. He immediately flagged a server for another helping of the house special.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

All around him, the tavern’s staff was staring. The servers had sharp, predatory smiles on their faces, watching him with an unnerving hunger. It was the look a pack of wolves gives its cornered prey.

After his time on Killing Sword Mountain, Zane’s senses were honed to a razor's edge; he could practically taste the hostility hanging heavy in the air. But his gnawing hunger was a far more immediate threat. He saw right through their little act, of course.

The meal was almost suspiciously good, but no vague threat was going to stand between him and a full stomach. As far as he was concerned, the fight could wait. He just kept eating, motioning for more and more.

Zane absolutely devoured everything they brought him. As he polished off one dish after another, the servers' confident smiles slowly melted away, replaced by open confusion.

When he’d finally cleared every plate on the table, he leaned back and grinned, a shred of venison caught between his teeth. "What's with the long faces?"

The men, all wearing their hair in long braids, looked mortified and quickly lowered their heads. "N-nothing," one of them stammered.

"You guys wondering why the poison isn't working?" Zane asked, his smirk widening.

[MUSIC: SUDDEN BATTLE]

His bluntness made their faces drain of all color. The manager, standing nearby, just sneered and barked, "Now!"

[SFX: CROSSBOW MECHANISMS CLICKING]

The men instantly drew small, mechanical crossbows from their belts, each loaded with a coiled Wraithgold rope. Without a moment's hesitation, they fired them straight at Zane.

[SFX: ROPES WHIPPING THROUGH AIR]

Seven or eight Wraithgold ropes shot through the air, wrapping around his limbs and torso, instantly tightening. Before he could even react, they dragged him from his seat and into a grimy backyard with the rest of their catch.
Zane could have dodged. He didn't. He let the ropes take him, curious where this was going.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
The shopkeeper strolled over, grinning. "Funny — you knew the food was poisoned and you still ate it. But these ropes?" He tapped one with a finger. "They'll shut down your Vitality completely. Not so calm now, are you?" He waited for the panic to set in.
Zane just looked at him.
The shopkeeper's grin faltered. He jerked his head at the staff. "Take him out back."

[AMBIENT: TENSE BACKYARD]

[SFX: BITTER LAUGHTER]

The other captives couldn't help but let out a few bitter chuckles at the delusional newcomer. He’d apparently been boasting that he was from the Cloud Hall—a sect so elite it was practically legendary. 

“Don't be absurd, boy,” grumbled one of the captives. “You're a little piggy, just like the rest of us.”

Zane narrowed his eyes.“What does that mean?”

One of the warriors snapped, his voice thick with despair."It means we're pigs being served up for slaughter,"  "Now shut up and let us die in peace." The others just looked away, defeated. What was the point of talking? Their fate was sealed.

Zane remained calm. "Tell me everything, and I'll get you out of here."

A nearby warrior, a Level Seven Adept, let out a hollow laugh. "You? You're just a Fourth-Level. You're weaker than I am. Save us? Don't make me laugh. Looks like they caught a clown along with the rest of us pigs." 

This whole tavern was a trap designed to capture Adepts. The ropes binding them weren't ordinary. Woven with Wraithgold Threads, they were designed to suppress a warrior's Vitality, making them impossible to break. Even the Soul Realm cultivator among them was helpless.

[SFX: ROPE SNAPPING]

Just as the warrior's laughter died, a sharp crack echoed through the yard.

"What was that?" he muttered, his eyes widening. He stared at Zane, his jaw hanging open. One of the Wraithgold ropes at Zane’s feet lay severed on the ground. How?

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC RISING TENSION]

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

Those special ropes were notoriously tough, but they were no match for the raw power of Zane’s Dragon Scales. Drawing on that latent force, a faint golden light began to trace the contours of his muscles, even making his eyes gleam. He brought his bound hands up and pulled them apart. The rope, said to be unbreakable, groaned under the strain before snapping with a sound like shattering rock.

The other captives stared, their mockery turning to slack-jawed astonishment. Physical strength like that… without a shred of Vitality? It was impossible. A sliver of hope cut through their despair. This was their one chance. Realizing a guard could see them at any moment, they kept their voices to a hushed, desperate whisper.

"Help us! Untie our ropes!"

Zane answered slowly, "First, you need to tell me what's going on. And what's this about being pigs?"

[AMBIENT: HUSHED WHISPERS]

At that, a half-dozen voices erupted at once, a chaotic mess of fear and desperation. One warrior finally spoke over the others. "Have you heard the rumors? About the Amethyst Thunderoak on Twilight Peak?"

Zane nodded. That was why he’d come.

"The mountain is teeming with powerful beasts," the warrior explained, his words tumbling out in a rush. "To get to the Thunderoak, some warriors hired these thugs to capture people. They use us as… as bait. Human shields to clear a path."

Zane shook his head in disgust."To use such dirty tricks… and they call themselves warriors." 
[SFX: ROPES SNAPPING IN RAPID SUCCESSION]
Zane spread his arms. Every rope binding him snapped at once. He pulled a broken flying knife from his coat and moved quickly through the yard, cutting the others free one by one.
The moment the last rope hit the ground, the freed captives didn't wait. The Soul Realm warrior's Vitality surged back instantly — he launched a volley of razor-edged energy at the inn. The others followed. Within moments, the building came down in a crash of timber and dust.
[SFX: STRUCTURE COLLAPSING]

[SFX: BOOMING VOICE FROM ABOVE]

Suddenly, a voice boomed from above, dripping with contempt. "What is this? How did the pigs get loose? Myron, is this the best your men can do?"

Another voice from the sky just snorted. "Don't worry. They aren't going anywhere."

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

[AMBIENT: WIND WHOOSH]

Terror seized the captives as they looked up. Hovering in the air above them were three Inner-Core Realm masters.

Everyone was paralyzed with fear.

[MUSIC: FRANTIC CHASE]

The Soul Realm cultivator was the first to react. "Run!" he screamed, launching himself into the air in a desperate bid for freedom. He didn't even look back. In a situation like this, it was every man for himself.

But one of the masters merely sighed and lifted a hand.

[SFX: ENERGY WHOOSH]

A colossal palm of pure energy materialized and swatted him out of the sky. The cultivator froze mid-air, arms and legs splayed, a fly caught in an invisible web.

[SFX: BONE CRUNCH]

[SFX: BODY EXPLOSION]

With a sickening crunch that seemed to vibrate through the very ground, his body went rigid. A second later, he exploded in a shower of red mist.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[SFX: BLOOD DRIPPING]

Blood rained down, coating the upturned, horrified faces of the captives below. They just stood there, stunned, as the fine crimson spray settled over them. Just like that, the most powerful warrior among them was gone—reduced to nothing more than a stain on the air.
