Frank had always believed Patrick was his only true rival—the sole warrior who could match him blow for blow. The thought that someone else, someone he'd dismissed as beneath his notice, might surpass them both sent a chill down his spine. 

He remembered back when they were battling those three Sky-Reavers. Patrick had thrown everything he had at taking down just one of them—the strongest of the trio—channeling every last ounce of his strength into a flurry of attacks and dodges. Patrick had even unleashed his Shadow of the Immovable Guardian, but the Sky-Reaver's defenses were just too strong, and he'd gone down anyway.

He’d landed blow after blow, hammering away at the creature’s thick hide, but he'd focused so much on offense that he’d left himself wide open.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

The battle ended with the Sky-Reaver finishing Patrick off, landing three brutal blows that left him crumpled on the ground. But even in defeat, Frank knew Patrick was a hell of a fighter. After all, no one could deny the power of his Black Lotus Karmafire.

Frank was sure he could shatter Patrick’s Form of the Adamant Soul with his spear, but he also knew he’d never be able to withstand the Black Lotus Karmafire if Patrick really let it loose.

It was always tough to compare warriors at the same level, and a real fight might not even settle the score. Still, Frank had been overconfident. He’d thought he had this one in the bag, figuring Patrick was just another opponent to crush.

As for Finn and Zane, Frank hadn't given them a second thought. Their power levels were miles below his, and from what he’d seen, they hadn’t shown an ounce of their true strength.

[MUSIC: REALIZATION THEME]

But seeing the two Adepts now, Frank suddenly remembered what Patrick had tried to tell him. "On Death Road, with their help…" he'd started to say.

Frank had cut him off right then, convinced that a couple of Adepts were just as weak and useless as any regular disciple from the Soul Realm. What help could they possibly be? He had even put on a show of respecting them while secretly thinking they were completely worthless.

If he’d had any idea what was coming, he would have asked a hell of a lot more questions. Then maybe he wouldn't be standing here feeling like such an idiot. [SFX: HEARTBEAT]

[MUSIC: SHAMEFUL STRINGS]

The shame hit him like a punch to the gut, and heat flooded his face.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

That was his problem, wasn't it? Pride. It had been part of him since he was a kid. And now, he was finally realizing that those two might have actually been the key when he needed it most.

Frank’s face burned.

Up ahead, Finn's Extreme Thunder Sword Intent tore through the swarming Nether Phantoms. His blade carved brilliant arcs through the darkness, each strike destroying another tar-like creature, but the effort proved futile—for every phantom he took down, two more oozed from the shadows to take their place.

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

Finn's movements grew sluggish, his arms trembling with exhaustion. Sweat poured down his face as he forced himself to continue, driven by sheer willpower. His vision blurred at the edges, and his lungs burned with each ragged breath.

[SFX: SWORD CLATTERING ON GROUND]

[SFX: BODY COLLAPSE]

The sword slipped from his fingers. Finn crumpled to his knees, then collapsed completely, his chest heaving as he gasped for air. He tried to lift his weapon one more time, but his muscles refused to obey. The endless tide had finally broken him.
Zane felt a thought take shape. They weren't creatures. They were energy — a mass of it, dark and hungry. And Corrupt Vitality devoured energy.
He sent six Ogre Horde phantoms forward into six of the black masses. Frank glanced over and shook his head — he'd already proven physical attacks were useless.
The phantoms collided. Nothing seemed to happen.
Then the first Black mass split open. Dark energy poured out of it in a rush, absorbed instantly into Zane's Corrupt Vitality. One by one, the other five followed.
"It works," Zane said.


[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

What happened next unfolded so quickly that only a  few people—Patrick and Finn among them—even registered it. They’d been too busy fighting for their lives to give it much thought before. But now, with the fighting paused and their senses heightened by fear, the truth struck them with devastating clarity: Zane's Vitality wasn't just powerful—it was overwhelming, a presence that dwarfed everything they'd encountered.

What followed was even more shocking.

[SFX: WHOOSHING ARROWS]

[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]

At Zane’s command, thousands of tiny arrows of dark energy shot toward the Nether Phantoms. As the bolts found their targets, the creatures froze solid, as if they’d been struck by lightning.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

An eerie silence fell over the area as every single Nether Phantom stopped dead in its tracks. Frank and the others held their breath, their eyes wide.

What came next did not disappoint.

Zane swept his hand through the air with deliberate precision. "Go!" 

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY BLAST]

[SFX: CASCADING EXPLOSIONS]

A heartbeat later, a rolling cascade of soft, popping explosions rippled outward from all directions. The sound built upon itself—gentle at first, then swelling into a symphony of destruction that made the ground beneath their feet vibrate. Frank felt his chest tighten as he realized he was witnessing something no Soul Realm cultivator should be capable of achieving.

[AMBIENT: MASSIVE ENERGY RELEASE]

[MUSIC: MAGICAL DISCOVERY]

Hundreds of Nether Phantoms burst at once, unleashing a tidal wave of purple Vitality.

The Vitality from a single Nether Phantom was enough to replenish Zane’s energy completely. Now, his Corrupt Vitality was absorbing the power from hundreds of them at once. The sheer volume was staggering.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL WIND]

Vast clouds of purple Corrupt Vitality drifted across the dark sky, swirling in hypnotic patterns that seemed to pulse with their own malevolent life. 

There was so much of it that if Zane tried to absorb it all, his body would probably explode on the spot.

Everyone stared upward, trembling in awe at the cloud-like mass of Vitality. Their faces reflected the eerie purple glow, eyes wide with a mixture of fear and wonder. They had never witnessed such a massive concentration of power—not in any demonstration, not in any battle, not even in the legendary tales passed down through their sects. 

No one they knew—not even the Head of Cloud Hall himself—could handle that much Vitality inside their body without being reduced to ash.
The mystery disciple was the last to look away. For the first time since any of them had met him, something had shaken his composure. He stared at Zane a moment longer than the others, then said very quietly, almost to himself: "So it's that technique. Maybe he's the one."

Then, cutting through the stunned silence, came Zane's voice, carrying a mix of amusement and annoyance that seemed almost absurd given the apocalyptic display above them.

"It's a real shame to waste so much Vitality."
