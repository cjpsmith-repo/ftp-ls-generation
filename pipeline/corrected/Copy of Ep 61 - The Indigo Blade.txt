Ep 61 - The Indigo Blade
Word count: 1195
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL DISCOVERY]
Later that night, Zane pulled out Julian's Indigo Blade to get a better look. It was a thing of beauty, masterfully crafted.
The scabbard was inlaid with vibrant gems and fine gold thread. It was so well-preserved, Zane could tell Julian had cherished it.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
A sharp whoosh cut through the silence of his room as Zane drew the blade. Once free, it pulsed with an ethereal purple and blue light.
"What an incredible blade."
It was a Legendary Weapon, on par with Evil Jace's Hellfire Blade. But Zane could feel the quality was a cut above even that. He hesitated. It felt like a crime to melt down a sword this magnificent.
Julian had sent his friends to plead for its return, but Zane had flat-out refused. To get stronger, he needed to offer the sword to the Cobalt Dragon.
Zane's brow furrowed as he stared at the blade. After a moment, his mind was made up. Without another second of hesitation, he reached out to the Furnace in his mind.
[SFX: MAGICAL FLAME BURST]
A jet of black flame shot from his brow, lancing straight for the Indigo Blade. It moved so fast that it left a bizarre arc hanging in the air. The sword went up like kindling, consumed from tip to hilt in an instant.
When the black flame finished devouring the blade, only a few drops of Celestial Essence remained. The drops of liquid metal glowed brilliantly in the dim room. Zane counted them, and his eyes lit up.
"The Indigo Blade really was on the same level..." he murmured.
[SFX: DEEP RESONANT PULSE]
The drops of Celestial Essence drifted toward the Cobalt Dragon statue in his mind, drawn into its open mouth. Two dark scales on the dragon's body suddenly blazed with green light — twin rays that flared and faded, leaving two more scales fully lit. Zane counted. Four scales now burned on the Cobalt Dragon's body.
He clenched his fist. At Marrow Refining Level, with four scales behind him, he might actually be able to hold his own against a Nature Level expert. He had pored over the refining texts in Evil Jace's storage ring looking for answers about the Celestial Essence, but had found nothing. Whatever its origin, only a true refining master would know. For now, it remained a mystery.
[AMBIENT: QUIET BEFORE DAWN]
Zane rose before first light. He moved through the corridors of Misty Peak without making a sound. He didn't want Ethan or the others to know he'd accepted the mission — they would only try to talk him out of it, and there was nothing to talk about. This was something he had to do.
[AMBIENT: BUSY OUTDOOR AREA]
[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS THEME]
The insect offensive would likely be over by the time the other disciples even got there. That's why most of them headed for the bluesky parking ground to get there faster.
The Skyreach Sect had Skycruisers, and the bluesky parking ground was their port. The Skycruisers were fast enough to reach The Sunbelt in a single day.
Zane stopping at the bluesky parking ground could only mean one thing: he was headed to The Sunbelt to fight the Razor swarm. Liam couldn't figure out why Zane was in such a rush to take on a mission that was so clearly out of his league.
"If you're in that much of a hurry to get yourself killed, I'll be happy to help," Liam thought.
A cold smile touched Liam's lips as he backed away slowly. First, he'd report this to Felix.
The bluesky parking ground was a man-made, square landing platform, raised about ten feet off the ground.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STAIRS]
As Zane walked up the steps to the platform, he saw a crowd of Skyreach Sect disciples already waiting. They were all there for the same reason he was: they'd accepted the mission to deal with the insect invasion. Most wore the Shadow Cloak of a Core Apprentice, with only a few in the White Robes of Outer Disciples. Every single one of them looked battle-hardened, their power sharpened by surviving countless life-or-death fights.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
But whether they were an Outer Disciple or a Core Apprentice, they all had the same reaction when they saw Zane: wide-eyed disbelief. It wasn't his appearance that caught their attention—it was his rank.
He was only at the Marrow Refining Level, far too low for a mission this dangerous.
The mission to stop the insect invasion was no cakewalk. For one, the Razor swarm attacked in overwhelming numbers. On top of that, each individual Razorwing was deceptively strong.
Worse still, they were fearless. They'd hurl themselves at even the most powerful fighters, swarming their enemies without any sense of self-preservation. Even a High-tier candidate could be overwhelmed and killed if they weren't careful.
That's why the mission's minimum requirement was High-tier. So to see someone at the Marrow Refining Level cleared for the job... no wonder everyone was staring.
[SFX: PERSON SITTING DOWN]
Zane ignored the stares. He found an empty corner of the platform, sat down, and closed his eyes to wait.
[SFX: HUSHED WHISPERING]
Across the platform, a group of four or five Core Apprentices were whispering among themselves, shooting glances his way. They were just loud enough for him to hear, and it was obvious they wanted him to.
"Since when does the Sect let some Marrow Refining kid sign up for a suicide run?" one of them sneered.
Another one chuckled. "Guess he's got a death wish."
Zane gave no reaction.
"Hey, kid," the burly one said, stepping forward. "A little free advice. You wanna hunt the Razor swarm, you need a team. Go in alone, you're just bug food. Why not tag along with us?"
Zane just shook his head. "Thanks, but I work better alone."
Without another word, he stood up and moved to a quieter corner of the platform before sitting and closing his eyes again. The exchange didn't go unnoticed by the others on the platform.
His confident refusal and fearless demeanor gave several onlookers pause. Their initial contempt dissolved, replaced by contemplative expressions as they reassessed the young cultivator before them.
[SFX: CROWD WHISPERS]
Whispers circulated through the crowd—tales of his lightning-fast reflexes, of a blade that materialized from nowhere. The cultivators exchanged uneasy glances, each privately wondering if they could have evaded such an attack. Most acknowledged the uncomfortable truth: they would have failed. Zane's movements defied normal perception, his weapon appearing as if conjured from the very air itself.
Oblivious to their shifting opinions, Zane settled into deep meditation, focusing his energy for the challenges ahead. The Points that would save Elara remained his sole concern, not the judgments of these strangers.
[SFX: GROUND TREMBLING]
The ground beneath them suddenly trembled. A shadow fell across the assembly, growing larger by the second. Zane's eyes snapped open as a deafening rumble shattered the silence.
[SFX: DEAFENING RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Above them, descending through parting clouds, loomed the colossal silhouette of a Skycruiser—its battle-scarred hull gleaming ominously in the sunlight.
As the massive vessel approached, something unexpected caught Zane's attention. Along the carrier's underside, barely visible to the untrained eye, ran a peculiar pattern of markings that made his blood run cold. They were identical to the ancient runes he'd discovered in his father's Codex—the very same symbols that had changed his life forever.
