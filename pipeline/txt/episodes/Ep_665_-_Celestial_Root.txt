Ep 665 - Celestial Root

Word count: 1133

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS TOWER ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]

With Huey in the picture, no other race ever stood a chance of getting a seat in the Tower of Sin. Even the Demon Night, for all their natural power, had always been kept in check by the Ogres. At least, that was true until Amber showed up.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

But the Ogres’ real downfall began the day Huey vanished.

No one knew if he was dead or alive. He was just… gone. One moment, he was a fixture in the Tower, the next, he’d disappeared into thin air without a trace.

At first, the Ogres managed to hold their ground. But decades ago, when the Demon Night began their ascent, the Ogres finally lost their top spot. Ever since, Esther Sterling has held the number one seat, leaving Maurice with number two.

“Huey’s disappearance was a huge blow to us,” Malcolm said, his voice laced with old frustration. “If he hadn’t gone missing, the Demon Night would’ve never had the guts to push us around.”

“Don’t you think it’s weird, though?” Yolande asked. “That he just vanished like that?”

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

“Weird? Of course, it’s weird!” Malcolm shot back with a frown. He stared at her, and a sudden, terrible realization dawned in his eyes. “It was you. You killed him, didn’t you?”

Yolande just nodded, her gaze turning toward the impossibly huge monster in the distance. “I did. I turned him into nourishment for the Tower of Sin. It absorbed his body completely.”

[MUSIC: DARK HORROR]

The truth hit Malcolm like a physical blow. He suddenly remembered that Yolande had appeared out of nowhere right around the same time Huey had vanished. He’d never connected the two events before.

The talent Yolande had displayed when she first arrived on the first floor was terrifying. It was enough for the Ogres to throw everything they could at trying to kill her. But just like Amber, her power was far beyond what they’d anticipated. No matter what they threw at her, she always came out on top.

She’d climbed to the tenth floor with ease.

Just as the Ogres were about to commit all their resources to finally getting rid of her, she made a strange announcement: she had no intention of entering the Talent Tablet, nor did she seem interested in ascending to the eleventh floor. She was just… staying. Her decision to settle on the tenth floor baffled the Humans and every other race in the Tower. After the initial surprise wore off, the Ogres decided to just keep watching her.

"Why?" Malcolm asked. The question came out before he could stop it. "Why did you kill Huey?"

Yolande finally shifted the conversation to where it needed to go. "Because Huey was Vesper's puppet. He was waiting in the Tower of Sin for Zane."

The pieces fell into place. Vesper, far away in his celestial tomb, had never known Huey was dead. As far as he was concerned, the moment Zane entered the Tower of Sin, Huey would find him, protect his climb, and bring him here — to this abyss — to complete the inheritance. That was why Vesper had told Zane nothing. He didn't need to. Huey was supposed to handle everything.

But Yolande had gotten there first. She'd climbed quietly to the tenth floor, drawn no attention, and killed the most powerful seat controller the Tower had ever seen — a peak War Lord who'd held that rank for a century. Then she'd waited.

"If Huey had still been alive," Yolande said, glancing at Zane, "he would have brought you here himself. And he would have told you exactly what I'm about to tell you now: throw the Crystal Butterfly in. It's the only way to open the passage."

Zane was quiet for a moment. He had no way of knowing which side was right — Vesper's or Yolande's. Maybe neither. Maybe both. It didn't matter. He'd walk his own path. Every step forward left a mark, and the only way to go further was to keep moving.

He opened his hand.

[SFX: CRYSTAL HITTING STONE]

The Crystal Butterfly tumbled from his fingers, struck the edge of the abyss in a shower of sparks, and dropped into the darkness. For a moment, they could see it — a small red glow falling fast — and then it was gone.

"What happens now?" Zane asked.

"We wait," Yolande said.

"How long?"

Yolande shook her head. "This abyss has no bottom. I don't know where it needs to fall before it takes root."

"No bottom?" Zane frowned. "Everything has a bottom."

Lavender spoke up quietly from beside him. "If I'm right, this is an endless abyss. Someone once explored it — fell for fourteen hundred years without hitting the bottom. Flew back out after two thousand."

Everyone went still. Fourteen hundred years of falling. The darkness at the rim suddenly felt different — not just deep, but boundless. There were things in this world that simply could not be measured, and standing at the edge of one of them was its own kind of terror.

Three hours passed.

Then the rumbling began.

[AMBIENT: DEEP CHASM RUMBLE]

Everyone spun around and followed Zane, moving to a safer distance.

“What is it?” Esther Sterling asked, her eyes fixed on the abyss.

Whatever was down there had to be almost as big as the Mad Scorpion to be making that much noise.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]

“Actually,” Yolande replied, “you’ve all seen it before.”

“We’ve seen it before?” The question rippled through the group, laced with shock.

Yolande nodded. “It’s the plant that chased us here. We only saw its roots. Its main body is down there.”

Lavender sneered. “It’s just a Celestial Root!”

“Why is it coming up?” Zane asked.

Yolande offered only a cryptic smile. “You’ll understand soon enough.”

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING ROAR]

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS CHASM]

The roar from below grew louder and louder until it was a deafening, physical force. Zane and the others had to channel their Vitality just to block their hearing. As the thing in the chasm climbed closer, a current of fear and worry passed through the group. Yet, through it all, the Mad Scorpion remained sound asleep.

“It’s here,” Yolande said, her gaze locked on the chasm’s opening.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL HORROR]

[SFX: CREATURE EMERGING]

A moment later, it crawled into view—a giant, grotesque plant pulling itself out of the abyss. At its very top was a bizarre, hideous face.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC STING]

The scariest part? That face was staring right at them.

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING CREATURE]

It was almost as large as the Mad Scorpion’s head, easily the size of a small mountain, and its two enormous eyes were fixed directly on Zane and his companions.
