Ep 1083 - Broken Record

Word count: 1143

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TOWER INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The truth was, Zane wasn’t actually absorbing the Law Storm. Not directly, anyway. The storm’s energy was so intensely concentrated that it had started to mimic a basic form of Chaos Energy—almost like it was trying to restore itself to some primordial state right there inside the Enchanted Barrier Tower.

But it wasn’t true Chaos Energy, and Zane knew better than to pull something so volatile into his core. Still, that didn’t mean he couldn’t use it. He realized with a jolt of exhilaration that the Law Storm couldn’t hold him back anymore. The resistance was gone. It felt as easy as flying through the open skies of The Heartlands.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

With that thought, he picked up speed and shot forward like a bat out of hell.

[AMBIENT: PALACE SQUARE]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

The palace leaders watching from the square could only stare, their surprise mounting with every passing moment. In what felt like an instant, Zane blew past the nineteen-thousand-mile mark.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

Then twenty thousand.

Twenty-one.

Twenty-two.

He didn’t slow down, didn’t hesitate, just kept pushing onward at full speed.

“This is incredible,” one of them muttered. “It’s like he’s just taking a leisurely flight back home.”

“What kind of trick is this?” another demanded. “He has to be cheating!”

[AMBIENT: QUIET SQUARE]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

Huddled in a corner of the square, Jasper was still tending to his injuries. A bitter smile touched his lips as he watched Zane’s impossible ascent. Man, I was so naive, he thought, shaking his head. So desperate to prove myself and take first place. Why didn’t I realize it was impossible from the start? With Layla and Zane in the running, I’m not even in the same league.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Meanwhile, Layla’s composure had completely shattered. Her heart hammered against her ribs, and her hands were clenched into fists so tight her knuckles were bone-white.

“He passed twenty-three thousand miles,” she muttered, her eyes fixed on Zane’s distant figure.

[SFX: HEARTBEAT]

“Is he going to keep going?” She wanted to scream at him, to tell him to stop. Stop moving! she pleaded silently, desperately. Just stop!

But Zane kept going.

And Layla’s mind was spinning. She’d activated the Wrath of the Ocean. She’d poured everything into this run. And Zane hadn’t even used his Vitality to shield himself — he was doing all of this on pure physical strength alone. How was that possible? How was any of this possible?

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT CRESCENDO]

Twenty-three thousand miles fell behind him. Then twenty-four, and twenty-five.

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

“He’s already past twenty-five thousand,” the Lord of the Sunset World said, clapping his hands with a booming laugh. “Amazing! Truly amazing. I missed his earlier performance, so I had no idea the kid was capable of something like this.”

“Well,” Yolande teased with a grin, “it looks like you’re about to take a big hit on your bet.”

The World Lord just laughed again. “I’ve always been generous with the younger generation. Since I made a promise, I won’t go back on my word.”

A playful thought sparked in Yolande’s mind. “You know,” she said, her smile widening, “there’s a reward for passing twenty thousand miles. I wonder… is there another one for passing thirty thousand?”

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

Surprise flashed across the World Lord’s face. The other palace leaders just stared, dumbfounded.

“Thirty thousand miles?” one of them scoffed. “That’s impossible.”

“Yolande,” the Lord of the Sunset World said, still smiling, “you know why Layla stopped at twenty-eight thousand miles.”

Yolande nodded. “Twenty-eight thousand miles is the inner circle of the Law Storm. The power there hasn’t been fully dispersed. For a warrior at Zane’s level, it’s a forbidden zone.”

“Then how can you possibly think he’ll break thirty thousand?” another palace leader asked.

Yolande just smiled and said nothing. She didn’t have the confidence to say he would. But Zane had a way of exceeding every expectation she’d ever set for him. That was the only thing she was betting on.

“I don’t have the confidence,” she admitted at last. “But what if?”

“There’s no ‘what if,’” the palace leader said flatly. “It’s impossible.”

Yolande shot him a look. “You don’t have to be so sure about that.”

The Lord of the Sunset World laughed. “Since Yolande has spoken — if this boy really does break thirty thousand miles, I’ll give him a reward. And it will be a great one.”

Yolande smiled.

Nearby, Layla had heard every word. She kept her face carefully blank, but inside, something cold was twisting in her chest. Thirty thousand miles. They were talking about Zane reaching thirty thousand miles — as if it were already decided, as if surpassing her record were just a formality.

It was one thing for Layla to set such an intimidating, suffocating record. It was another thing entirely for Zane to come along and shatter it just a few hours later, and so effortlessly at that.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

“Twenty-six thousand!” someone in the crowd shouted.

“Twenty-seven!”

“Twenty-eight thousand — he’s only two hundred miles from breaking Layla’s record!”

At the two-hundred-mile mark, the color drained from Layla’s face. Her nails were digging into her palms so hard that they broke the skin. Two hundred miles. At Zane’s speed, that was nothing. A few breaths. There was no question anymore.

He was going to surpass her.

A sharp pang of regret cut through her. She’d been so certain he couldn’t reach her. So certain she’d left a comfortable margin. If she’d known — if she’d had any idea — she would have pushed harder, taken the injuries, bought herself another thousand miles at least.

She hadn’t. And now it was too late.

[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]

Zane crossed twenty-eight thousand, two hundred miles. Layla’s record — the record that had felt like a ceiling no one would ever touch again — was gone, broken within hours of being set.

The consensus in the crowd was that Zane would leave the tower now. He’d beaten everyone. He’d secured first place. There was no point in pushing himself any further.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

But they were wrong.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED HEROIC THEME]

Zane wasn’t trying to beat anyone. From the very beginning, his goal had nothing to do with competition. He was following his own path, pushing forward on his own terms. The only person he was trying to challenge was himself.

So, he didn’t stop. He just kept charging forward.

[AMBIENT: DARK TEMPEST WINDS]

“We won’t be able to see him once he enters the inner circle,” a palace leader noted, staring into the dark gray tempest.

“But we can still sense his position,” the World Lord replied with a helpless smile. He finally understood. “He’s already reached twenty-nine thousand miles.” Zane wasn’t just breaking a record; he was aiming for thirty thousand.

Just then, another leader spoke up.

[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]

[MUSIC: FOREBODING DRUMS]

“Around the twenty-nine-thousand-mile mark… I remember there’s a flame pool. It contains the Immortal Profound Flame left behind by the Supreme Lord of Profound Flame himself. Even after all these years, the Law Storm hasn’t completely consumed it. I don’t think Zane will be able to cross that.”
