Ep 1121 - The Feast

Word count: 1060

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
[AMBIENT: QUIET HALL INTERIOR]
You had to be smart to be a hall leader in a tenth-tier sacred place, and Master Sloan was no fool. He knew exactly what it meant when Yolande, the leader of the Mist Palace, showed up out of the blue to personally take Zane away.
He wasn’t a detective, so he couldn’t connect all the dots, but he didn’t need to. It was enough to know that the woman who’d collected Zane was not only the head of the Mist Palace but also the daughter of a Supreme Lord—the kind of person the Lord of the Psychean Realm himself came out to greet. 
Hall leaders like Sloan weren’t even allowed in the same room.
Sloan had suspected from the beginning that Zane had powerful connections. After all, the Saint Lord himself had ordered them to bring the kid to the Holy City. Still, he never imagined Yolande herself would travel all this way just to escort Zane to the Mist Palace. 
[MUSIC: TENSE REALIZATION]
That level of backing sent a genuine chill down his spine. He found himself anxiously replaying their entire journey, praying they had treated Zane well enough.
And now, here Zane was, back at the Soulbound Sect.
The journey he’d just made crossed over a thousand supreme worlds. For someone like Sloan, covering that kind of distance was a monumental task, something that would likely cost his entire fortune and take an eternity. 
Yet Zane had gone to the Mist Palace and returned as if he’d just taken a short trip.
Zane himself didn’t seem to grasp what a staggering accomplishment this was. Very few Soul Sea Realm warriors could cross so many supreme worlds; only the absolute top talents had the resources for it.
Sloan’s eyes subtly flickered around, immediately recognizing the Heavenly Phoenix Horse standing behind Zane. He couldn’t place the origin of Thundergust, the sword in Zane’s hand, but he could tell at a glance that it was a Grade-One Divine Weapon. 
[AMBIENT: PULSING ENERGY HUM]
[SFX: SUBTLE MAGICAL SHIMMER]
If it had been any other warrior with a Heavenly Phoenix Horse and a sword like that, Sloan might have seriously considered killing them for their treasures. A chance to get his hands on something so extraordinary was a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity. But this was Zane. He didn’t have the guts to even entertain the thought.
[SFX: BLOOD ESSENCE DISSOLVING]
[SFX: ENERGY REFINING]
He quickly raised his hand and destroyed the remnants of Julius’s blood essence, refining it into a compressed piece of Lifeblood.
He held it out to Zane with both hands, his expression appropriately contrite. “Julius cultivated the dark blood arts,” he said. “He was building toward a second body, but his main body fell before he could complete it. This Lifeblood is all that remains of him. Please accept it as my apology, and do with it as you see fit.”
Zane glanced at it, then held it out to Freya. “He was still your family,” he said simply. “You should keep it.”
[MUSIC: BITTERSWEET STRINGS]
Freya’s hand trembled slightly as she took it. Julius had imprisoned her, tried to sell her — and yet. She didn’t absorb it. She wasn’t sure she ever would. But she closed her fingers around it and put it away.
With that settled, Sloan extended a formal invitation. He oversaw a hall in another city not far from Clearhaven — nothing compared to the Sect’s Holy City, but substantial enough — and he hoped Zane would do him the honor of visiting. 
Zane had time to spare before his appointment in the Dark World. He accepted.
Freya rode in a daze the entire way.
[AMBIENT: GRAND FEAST HALL]
Meanwhile, Freya was devouring everything on her plate like it was her last meal. The spread in front of her was staggering. In the Lower World, ingredients like these would have needed to be refined into pills just to access a fraction of their power. Here, they were just dishes on a table.
Despite the way she was wolfing down her food, none of the Soul Sea Realm maids in the hall dared to laugh. They didn’t even have the nerve to shoot her a dirty look. Zane and Freya were only at the early stage of the Soul Sea Realm—a lower level than even the dancers and singers performing for them—but they were their leader’s honored guests. Only a fool would show them the slightest disrespect, and that was a death wish.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Zane wasn’t eating. His time in the Thirteen Palaces had taught him that for a warrior at his level, every step forward depended not just on talent, but on a staggering amount of resources. The food at this feast, as lavish as it was, wasn’t the kind of vital energy he needed. Even several tenth-tier sacred places combined couldn’t supply what he required to advance.
Freya, between bites, kept stealing glances at him.
A year ago, she’d ascended with some sense of advantage — she had an uncle waiting for her, a foothold others didn’t have. Zane had nothing. No connections, no backing, no plan that she could see. And yet here she was, sitting at the table of a hall master who controlled a thousand cities, being served treasures she’d only read about — and all of it was because of him. The man she’d met in a mine.
She still couldn’t figure out what had happened to him in a year. She wasn’t sure she ever would.
[SFX: PROBING VOICE]
“Zane,” Master Sloan said, trying to sound casual as he probed for information. “You went to the Thirteen Palaces. What brings you all the way back to our supreme world? If I remember right, the Mist Palace is… what, one thousand three hundred and twenty supreme worlds away?”
Zane had a feeling Sloan wouldn’t let that go. He just smiled. “Can’t stay in the palace and train forever. Even warriors from the Palaces have to go out and get experience. I’m just passing through to meet a friend in the Dark World.” He saw no reason to hide it.
[SFX: SUDDEN REALIZATION BREATH]
Beside him, Freya froze, her eyelashes fluttering. She looked up from her plate, stunned. 
*More than a thousand supreme worlds?* she thought. *Is that really how far we traveled?*
