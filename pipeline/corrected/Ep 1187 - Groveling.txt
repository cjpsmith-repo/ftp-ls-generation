Ep 1187 - Groveling

Word count: 1001

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
This wasn’t the kind of fight an ordinary warrior could just jump into. Yolande knew she wasn’t cut out for it, and neither was the old man. But just because they were outmatched didn’t mean they weren’t paying attention.
They weren’t the only ones. The entire universe had its eyes fixed on this single spot, and on this day, they all learned a new name: Zane, a warrior from the lowly Soul Sea Realm.
The Supreme Lords trapped in Zane’s snare felt that universal gaze, and their faces slowly darkened with frustration. 
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
The ones on the outside, however, just stared at the shimmering tether connecting the five of them, their own faces turning deathly pale with relief that they weren’t the ones caught in it.
The situation had spun completely out of control. 
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Of the five Supreme Lords trapped together, only one could survive.
The problem was that none of them were alone. They all had ties to the powerful figures watching from the sidelines. Addison and Ranell, for instance, were from the same faction; if Addison made a move, Ranell would have his back. And if Supreme Lord Gunter acted, the Queen of the Demon Night wouldn’t just stand by and watch. A fight to the death here wouldn’t just kill five lords—it could throw the entire universe into chaos.
And for what? Thanks to some kid’s cheap trick, even if they paid that price, they’d get nothing in return. The thought of being so thoroughly outsmarted by a nobody from the Soul Sea Realm was a humiliation they could barely stomach.
For a long moment, the Supreme Lords on the outside just weighed their options and did nothing. 
Instead, their collective gaze fell upon a single grain of sand. The tether had come from there.
A faint smile touched Addison’s lips. In a single leap, he was standing right in front of it. He opened his mouth to speak, then hesitated, the words catching in his throat. Begging a kid was deeply humiliating for a Supreme Lord, but after a moment’s thought, he forced himself to speak.
“Your name is Zane, right?”
[SFX: ECHOING VOICE]
A detached voice echoed across the universe. “Supreme Lord Addison. What do you want?”
Addison looked up at the sky, cursing the kid in his head. *He’s really going to make every creature in a hundred thousand worlds watch me grovel.*
“This Desperate Melee — can you take it down? Call it off?” Addison kept his tone even, almost conversational. As if he were asking a favor between equals.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
Inside the grain of sand, Zane glanced at Saul.
Saul’s expression didn’t change. “No. This was set by my master. Only he could undo it — and he isn’t here. Once the tether links living beings, the rule holds. One survives. That’s all.”
Zane relayed the answer, his voice spreading across the stars. “It can’t be removed. That’s not how it works. The Desperate Melee was set by the Keep’s master — not me. I don’t have the power to undo it even if I wanted to. Only one of you walks away. There is no other path.”
The faces of all five Lords went very still.
Addison held his position in front of the grain. His voice stayed level. “And if the five of us simply stayed? Waited it out? What then?”
He wasn’t the only one wondering. The other four Lords had quietly held back, watching the negotiation, each one hoping Addison might find something — some loophole, some angle — that would make a one-in-five gamble unnecessary.
Zane looked at Saul again.
[SFX: LOW RESONANT HUM]
“Tell them to look at the tether,” Saul said.
“Look at the tether,” Zane said. “Look carefully at the color.”
The five Lords looked. The tether was black and white — two colors, each occupying roughly half the band. But if you watched it long enough, you could see it moving. The white was being consumed. Slowly, steadily, the black was spreading.
When Zane had first triggered the Melee, the colors had been split evenly. Now, the black had already taken sixty percent. The white was down to forty.
“The white is time,” Zane said. “When it’s gone — when the tether turns fully black — all five of you die. Not one survivor. All five.”
“How long?” Addison asked.
“Three hours.”
No one spoke. The five Lords looked at one another, and the last trace of patience in the space between them dissolved.
But someone had to make the first move. And that someone was Magnus.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]
A faint pattern of the Water Law shimmered across his skin, and a glowing ring of water materialized behind him, radiating a chilling aura.
[SFX: WATER WHOOSH]
His first target wasn’t one of the weaker lords. It was Addison.
Magnus had already run the numbers. As a mid-rank Supreme Lord, he was stronger than the other four trapped with him. If it were just the five of them, his odds would be good. But it wasn’t. Ranell was out there, another mid-rank lord just as strong as him, ready to back Addison up. He’d have to face that duo eventually. Better to take Addison out now, while he wasn’t expecting it. Ranell would be furious, sure, but with his ally dead, he might just cut his losses and stay out of it.
That was the logic. 
[SFX: SUDDEN ATTACK WHOOSH]
So he struck.
“You—!” Addison had feared Magnus the most from the start. His own plan was simple: wait for the others to wear each other down, then team up with Ranell to pick off the lone survivor. He never imagined Magnus would come for him first. The shock and rage were plain on his face.
[AMBIENT: REALITY WARPING DISTORTION]
The moment Magnus moved, Gunter attacked as well. The other two Supreme Lords didn’t waste a second, immediately lunging for their own targets. The power unleashed by a single Supreme Lord was terrifying enough to shatter a world. Now, four of them were fighting for their lives, trapped inside a space barely a hundred miles wide.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE PERCUSSION]
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC ENERGY STORM]
With a deafening, reality-shaking *BOOM*, the battle began.
