Ep 695 - I'll Be Waiting

Word count: 1085

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

A storm of emotions crossed Cora’s face as she stared at Zane, her teeth gritted. “Tell me your name,” she demanded.

Cora rarely left her home, but she was surprisingly well-informed about the young elites of the Heartlands. She hadn’t been at the Martial Tournament in Oracle City two years ago, but she’d spent weeks helping her brother, Brent, study his potential opponents. This man was young, yet he’d already reached the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. He was no ordinary warrior.

Robin had summoned her, but he hadn’t mentioned anything about this guy. They’d clashed the second they’d laid eyes on each other, leaving no time for introductions.

A slight smile played on Zane's lips. "Why, so you can hunt me down for revenge later? I hope that day comes." He seemed completely unfazed by her fury. "My name is Zane. Zane Lowell."

Cora's brow furrowed. Then her expression shifted entirely. She turned to Brent. "Brent — is he the Zane you never stop talking about?"

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATION]

She'd heard that name more times than she could count. Every time Brent came home from the Ethereal Spirit Sect, it was Zane this, Zane that — the one opponent he'd never gotten to face, the one he measured himself against. And the legends had only grown.

Brent was considered a god-level genius; the idea that someone had outpaced even him had struck Cora as almost impossible. And then there was the story everyone told — how Zane had stood up to Maddox directly, how his sect-protecting formation had turned Maddox away. That story had been turned into songs. She'd heard it sung in teahouses.

She'd always been curious. She'd thought, one day, she'd like to meet this person. Then he'd gone into the celestial tomb and never come out, and Brent had quietly concluded he was probably dead.

She'd thought it was a shame.

Now she looked at the man in front of her and tried to reconcile the legend with the person who had just threatened to kill her in her own home.

Brent let out a soft sigh beside her. "Yeah, sis. That's him."

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL REVELATION]

In a flash, it all clicked into place. She finally understood why he’d cornered her with two impossible choices. Everyone in the Heartlands knew what had happened to his sister, Elara—that Maddox held her captive. A wave of quiet respect washed over Cora, extinguishing some of her anger. This man would stop at nothing to save his sister. He would burn the world down if he had to.

And, of course, she realized that if she fell into Maddox’s hands, Zane’s sister would be in even greater danger.

[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

Understanding, however, didn't erase the insult. He had humiliated her—worse, he'd done it in front of her family. Cora was far too proud to let such an offense slide, no matter how noble his motivations might be.

She drew herself up, channeling all her frustration into a stony glare that could have frozen fire. Her voice came out cold and measured. "Fine. Even if you are that Zane, I'll be paying you back for this one day. You can count on it."

For a moment, she thought she saw something flicker in his eyes—amusement, perhaps, or maybe respect. Then his expression settled into something maddeningly calm.

"I'll be waiting," he replied, his tone carrying neither mockery nor concern, just quiet certainty.

The simplicity of his response only irritated her further. He wasn't taking her threat seriously, treating it like some distant appointment he might or might not keep. But Cora had made a promise, and she always kept her word. One day, when she'd grown strong enough, she would remind him exactly what it cost to corner a daughter of the Ethereal Spirit Sect.

[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL MANOR]

[MUSIC: LIGHT RELIEF]

Robin let out a shaky breath, visibly relieved that a real fight hadn’t broken out. “Well, if you’ve made your decision, that’s that,” he huffed. “The leader of Cloud Hall already invited you to stay there to keep you safe, but you’re so stubborn you kept refusing.” He clapped his hands together. “It’s noon, and lunch is ready. You can leave after you’ve eaten.”

A teleportation array connected Ethereal City and Cloudhaven, so getting to Cloud Hall would be simple.

Zane gave a slight bow. “Thank you for your kindness.”

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS WALKING AWAY]

As everyone else started moving toward the dining room, Cora stalked off in the opposite direction. “I’m not eating with him,” she called over her shoulder. “I’ll be ready to go after lunch.”

A wry smile touched Robin’s face. He turned to Zane, his expression apologetic. “Please, forgive her. My daughter… she’s always been like this.”

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

The teleportation arrays sat on the west side of the city — one array bound for Cloudhaven, one for Oracle City. Today, the lines were longer than usual. Word had spread that Maddox was preparing to move on Ethereal City, and people were getting out while they still could. The cost of passage had doubled; no one complained.

Most of the cultivators in line were ordinary Adepts. They'd been waiting half a day.

When two Inner-Core warriors pushed to the front, the grumbling started immediately.

[SFX: CROWD GRUMBLES]

"Hey — there's a line!"

"First come, first served. That applies to everyone."

"Some people have no manners."

One of the warriors sneered, glaring at the others in line. “Some of us have more important things to do. You got a problem with that? Wanna fight?”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

The crowd fell silent. Most of them were Adepts, with only a handful having reached the Soul Realm. The two men pushing their way to the front were both at the Inner-Core Realm. The power gap was just too wide. Saying anything else would be suicide.

Their silence only fueled the men’s arrogance. “That’s what I thought,” one of them jeered. “Learn your place and think before you open your mouths.”

A quiet grumbling rippled through the line as it slowly shuffled forward, the teleportation arrays flashing white with each departure. As the two line-cutters reached one of the arrays, one said lazily, “Alright, let’s get this over with.”

His companion threw a haughty glance over his shoulder, as if daring anyone to object.

[SFX: OBJECT TOSS]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BUILDUP]

Then, he pulled a purple-black bead from his tunic, tossed it toward the teleportation array, and both men leaped back.
