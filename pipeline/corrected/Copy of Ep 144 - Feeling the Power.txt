[MUSIC: INTENSE MAGICAL]
 Zane gritted his teeth, ready to bet everything on a single shot. He held out his right arm, motioning for the Black Fire to flow into the Glyphs etched into his skin. 
[SFX: BURNING FLESH]
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
Thanks to his intense training, the fire recognized the markings and obeyed instantly.
The moment the Glyphs absorbed the Black Fire, a tremor ran up Zane’s arm. An immense heat radiated from the markings, but instead of pain, Zane felt a strange wave of relief. 
In moments, the Glyphs began to spin. The inner and outer rings moved in opposite directions, and a bright light flared from their center. 
As the ritual began, Zane snatched the Phoenix Crystal with his other hand and pressed it into the glowing core.
[SFX: HUM]
[SFX: LIGHT FLARE]
 A low hum filled the air. The instant the crystal made contact, a blinding light flared, and the Glyphs went still. The Phoenix Crystal was gone. 
Stunned, Zane touched the spot where it had been. He pressed down, feeling a hard object just beneath the surface. It was the Phoenix Crystal, now fused with his own flesh. Once the Black Fire receded, Zane pushed himself to his feet and flexed his arm.
"Alright, Phoenix Crystal," he muttered. "Time to see if you're as fast as they say."
The room had all the training equipment he could want, and he quickly found a Stone Golem to use as a sparring partner. Squaring his shoulders in front of it, Zane took a deep breath and unleashed a flurry of punches. 
[SFX: RAPID PUNCHING]
 A rapid series of thuds echoed through the room. In the blink of an eye, he’d thrown twenty punches.
[AMBIENT: TENSE NIGHT ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: MILITARY TENSION]
 Every soldier in Silverspire recognized Yolande Shaw's name, which carried the weight of both respect and fear throughout the ranks. Her reputation preceded her wherever she went.
 "Officer Shaw," the lead soldier stammered, his voice cracking slightly as he addressed the formidable woman. "Apologies for the disturbance at this hour. We're conducting a city-wide inspection under direct orders. We... we know who you are, of course, but protocol is protocol." He swallowed hard, avoiding her penetrating gaze. "If you'd just cooperate with us, we'd greatly appreciate it."
Yolande stood firmly at her gate, cutting an unusual figure in her elegant Iridescent Gown paired incongruously with mud-caked Work Boots. Her posture remained perfectly straight, betraying neither concern nor compliance. "What precisely is this inspection for?" she asked, her voice deceptively calm yet edged with authority. "And why, may I ask, am I being subjected to inspection again when I've already been cleared?"
"We're searching for a Demon Commander who may have infiltrated the city during the chaos, ma'am," the soldier explained, struggling to maintain his composure as beads of sweat formed on his brow. "High Command has authorized us to use the Mirror of Truth to identify any potential threats." His fingers tightened nervously around the ornate mirror in his hands as he awaited her response.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]
 They called her the "Killer Girl" for a reason. Yolande Shaw wasn't someone you crossed. Even with a completely blank expression, her presence alone was enough to send a chill down their spines.
"I'm not possessed by a demon general," she said flatly. "I don't need your ridiculous inspection. Now get out." She turned to go back inside, but another soldier spoke up.
"I'm afraid you can't refuse, Officer Shaw," he said, his voice trembling slightly. "It's a direct order from High Command. Everyone is to be inspected. No exceptions. Not even for the commander. Please… cooperate."
The soldiers were terrified, but they held their ground. Orders were orders. Yolande just stared back, her silence a wall of defiance. She had no intention of submitting, and she couldn't care less about their military orders.
[SFX: DOOR BEGINNING TO CLOSE]
 Just as the door was about to swing shut, Zane stepped forward. "They're just doing their jobs, Yolande," he said. "Loyal soldiers following orders. As a high-ranking member of the Silverspire Guard, you should be setting an example, not making this harder on everyone."
The soldiers tensed, but a wave of relief washed over them as Zane stepped in. None of them would have dared to speak to her like that.
[SFX: FABRIC SWISHING]
 Yolande spun around at the sound of his voice, the hem of her gown flaring to reveal a glimpse of her lean legs. She fixed Zane with a blank stare. "This doesn't concern you," she said, her voice like ice. "Back off."
A smirk played on Zane's lips. "Maybe I'm crossing a line, but it's the right thing to do. Just let them do the scan. It'll be easier for everyone, you included. It'll take what, two seconds?"
[SFX: MAGIC WARD MATERIALIZING]
 Yolande's cold, gray eyes didn't leave his. Then, with a flick of her wrist, a shimmering Magic Ward materialized in the air between them.
"Damn, short fuse," Zane muttered, taking a quick step back. He knew Yolande was stubborn, but he hadn't pegged her as this unreasonable. She went from zero to a hundred at the slightest provocation.
Zane was just trying to help, and the soldiers knew it, but they were powerless to intervene. They could only stand aside, silently praying he'd survive her wrath.
[SFX: FIST IMPACT]
[SFX: MAGICAL GLOWING]
 Zane didn't hesitate. Crack! He slammed his fist into the first Magic Ward. Drawing on the Emerald Crystal on his left arm, he tagged each ward one by one, leaving a glowing red sigil on their surface. 
He already knew the Bio-Energy from the crystal destabilized magical shields; even the slightest touch was enough to compromise them.
[SFX: MAGIC SHIELDS SHATTERING]
 Once the wards were marked, he switched tactics. Now, drawing power from the Phoenix Crystal, he unleashed a flurry of punches, shattering them completely. Yolande summoned new wards with incredible speed, but Zane smashed through them just as fast.
[AMBIENT: CRYSTALLINE CHIMES]
 Fragments of shattered magic cascaded around them, each crystalline piece chiming like delicate bells as they fell, creating a haunting melody that accompanied Zane's relentless assault.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TONES]
 When her defenses proved futile, Yolande's demeanor transformed. A sinister energy pulsed from her core as a solitary ward materialized before her, creeping toward Zane with deliberate menace.
[SFX: MALEVOLENT ENERGY PULSING]
 This ward bore no resemblance to its predecessors. Its crimson depths glowed with malevolent promise—unmistakable in its deadly intent. The Crimson Cage had returned—the very same lethal enchantment she had unleashed upon the Netherlord himself.
