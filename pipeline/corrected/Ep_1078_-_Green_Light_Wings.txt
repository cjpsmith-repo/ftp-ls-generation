Ep 1078 - Green Light Wings

Word count: 1015

[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

The four thousand warriors had been split into three groups, their long line stretching into the distance.

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

The lead group, only about twenty strong, was already eight hundred miles deep into the Law Storm. The storm was a maelstrom of raw power here, but for these top-tier talents, it was nothing. They were all racing, competing to be the fastest. The moment they’d entered the Enchanted Barrier Tower, they’d bolted for the thousand-mile mark.

Leading the pack was a man from the Shadow Palace named Lee. He was shrouded in a black aura that simply absorbed any fragments of the storm’s laws that strayed too close, allowing him to push forward without losing any speed at all. Right behind him was Jasper, one of the most exceptional fighters to have ever ascended to the Upper World. He wasn’t using any tricks, just relying on pure power, his eyes scanning the churning chaos as he pressed forward.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

“Nine hundred miles down,” Layla mumbled to herself, a small smile playing on her lips. “Just another hundred to go.” And with that, she came to a dead stop.

The fighters in the lead group were only a mile or so apart, each pushing themselves to their absolute limit. So when Layla suddenly stopped, the others blew right past her, shooting her confused looks. In the blink of an eye, she went from third place to dead last in the lead pack.

[SFX: POWERFUL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]

She only paused for a moment. Then, with a light flick of her wrist, two wings of brilliant green light erupted from her back. She gave them a gentle flap and shot forward with unbelievable speed, soaring past the others like a comet.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

She became a streak of green light, leaving the others in her wake.

Lee was at the nine-hundred-and-fifty-mile mark, fifty miles from glory, when something flashed past the corner of his eye. It was gone before he could focus on it — just a blur in the storm, he thought. Probably a Law fragment. He couldn’t care less about the main prize, some Tribulation Exterminating Pill that everyone would get. No, the Shadow Palace had made him a secret offer: be the fastest to finish the first thousand miles, and a Grade-One Divine-Class weapon was his. He was determined to secure this victory for them.

A few breaths later, the old man’s voice rang out across the tower.

“The first cultivator to cross the thousand-mile mark — Layla Rhodes of the Mist Palace!”

Lee stopped dead. His face went blank. “What… what just happened?”

He hadn’t even seen her pass him.

[MUSIC: INTENSE RACING THEME]

Outside the tower, the palace leaders were on their feet.

“Those wings—” one of them said.

“Green Light Wings,” the old man confirmed, his voice carrying a note of genuine surprise. “I haven’t seen a pair of those in a very long time.”

“Green Light Wings belong to the Wind Spirit — that’s not a human ability. How does she have them?”

No one had an answer. They stared at the faint green trail Layla had left behind in the storm, each of them quietly recalculating what they thought they knew about Elder Dunn’s disciple.

[MUSIC: QUIET RESOLVE]

Layla had already crossed the line and stopped. She turned and looked back. The rest of the lead group was still a long way behind her, scattered across the storm. Her expression was cool, unhurried.

No challenge at all.

She let herself wonder, briefly, where Zane was. He wouldn’t be competing for first place — that much she knew. But the real contest between them wasn’t over. It hadn’t even started yet.

Meanwhile, the chaotic energies of the storm seemed to swirl in an orderly pattern around Zane. Even as the tempest intensified, it didn’t seem to faze him in the slightest.

He was still at the back. The third group had long since pulled ahead of him, and even their tail was out of reach. He was last — again.

He shook his head, pushing the lingering sense of familiarity out of his mind, and picked up his pace.

When he finally crossed the thousand-mile mark, the old man didn’t bother announcing it. Every other warrior, including the entire third group, had already passed.

[SFX: WIND SHIFT]

The moment he crossed the line, he felt it — the storm changed. The pressure didn’t just increase; it reorganized. The Laws were condensing, pulling toward a center. It reminded him of the Chaotic Ocean of Stars, that vast windstorm he’d crossed before — except that had been pure Wind Law. This was everything at once, and it was getting heavier.

He filed it away and kept walking.

[AMBIENT: TOWER EXTERIOR]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS OBSERVATION]

Outside the Enchanted Barrier Tower, a group of powerful figures watched the proceedings.

“The three-thousand-mile mark is where it gets really tough,” the man in the simple cotton clothes said. “A lot of them will wash out before they even get close.”

One of the palace heads nodded in agreement. “The power of the storm condenses after the first thousand miles. It gets a lot stronger. The real test is how these fighters will handle that pressure.”

“And what about that slowpoke? Still bringing up the rear?” another palace head laughed.

“He is,” a third one replied with a chuckle. “But that kid’s a real weirdo. He’s already past the thousand-mile mark, yet he still hasn’t used any of his Vitality. Is he planning on going the whole way without it?”

They had all noticed Zane, but not in a good way. They just thought he was strange. Their attention was on their own champions, all wondering who had the stamina to go the farthest now that the initial sprint was ending.

The relentless storm was taking its toll. While those in the lead pack had more or less maintained their blistering pace, the second-tier fighters had slowed to a crawl. The dangers were constant and deadly. Every so often, a blazing fire spirit would erupt from the swirling chaos and lunge at a warrior, or a blade of black wind would materialize out of thin air.

In this place, you had to stay on high alert, or you’d end up dead.
