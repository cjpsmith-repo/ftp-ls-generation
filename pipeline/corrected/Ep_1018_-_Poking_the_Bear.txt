Ep 1018 - Poking the Bear

Word count: 1114

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

Roaring Tokens were so insanely valuable that only the wealthiest and most powerful warriors could even dream of using one.

Armond and Magnus, two of the strongest warriors in the entire universe, were famous for using them just to argue with each other.

It was a pain in the ass for anyone listening, but nobody was dumb enough to get on their bad side—not even the other Supreme Lords.

It wasn’t just that they were terrifyingly strong. It was because Armond and Magnus were both independent warriors, the kind who would go on a reckless rampage if they got pissed off, without a single thought for the consequences.

So, even though the way they wasted their Roaring Tokens annoyed a lot of people, everyone just had to suck it up.

They settled for cursing them out behind their backs.

[SFX: ROARING TOKEN ACTIVATION]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

Zane, on the other hand, wasn’t feeling subtle. He called out the two Supreme Lords using his own Roaring Token.

It was a dumb move, but also kind of badass.

He hadn’t meant to start a fight with two Supreme Lords. He was frustrated and scared and running out of ideas, and the two of them squabbling across his channel was the last straw. It was pure impulse. He regretted it the moment the token stopped vibrating.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL POOL]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL STRINGS]

But Yolande was impressed, stunned by Zane’s sheer nerve. ‘Interesting kid,’ she thought. ‘I wonder where he is now, after making it up from the Lower World.’

She had to agree with her clone from the Tower of Sin: anyone unbound by destiny was crucial to the current era, and it wasn’t right for anyone to try to force a new fate on them.

“He has no set destiny… but if he ascended all the way to my world, then fate must be pulling the strings to bring us together again,” she murmured, rising from the pool.

[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]

The moment she stepped from the mist, a faint green light swirled around her, coalescing into an elegant gown.

[SFX: PORTAL OPENING]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHOIR]

Just then, five or six confluence portals shimmered into existence before her, each holding one of her clones.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]

[SFX: RUSTLING CLOTHES]

All the clones began to undress, preparing to merge back into her main body.

But Yolande held up a hand. “Wait here for me. I need to go deal with something first.”

The clones froze obediently, like puppets waiting for their master to pull the strings.

The “something” she had to deal with? Finding the names of every recent ascendant from the Lower World. She ordered a complete list, determined to check every single name herself.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Meanwhile, Magnus had recovered from the shock of being called out by a nobody.

[SFX: ROARING TOKEN ACTIVATION]

His voice crackled across the universe. “Brat. Do you know who I am?”

Every living being within earshot winced. This was not going to end well for whoever that kid was.

Zane activated the token. “I know. Aren’t you Magnus?”

“You know who I am, and you still dare? Do you want to die?”

“I don’t want to die,” Zane fired back. “But what exactly are you going to do about it?”

[SFX: UNIVERSE-WIDE MURMUR]

The logic landed on every listener at once. Finding one anonymous warrior across a hundred thousand worlds was impossible. Magnus knew it. Everyone knew it.

Armond, despite being Magnus’s oldest enemy, found himself in the unusual position of agreeing with him. They had both been insulted. He activated his own token.

“We can’t do anything to you,” Armond said, his voice tight. “So tell me — how many Roaring Tokens do you have to throw away like this?”

It was meant as a taunt. It became an invitation.

In the past, when Echo Beasts — divine creatures whose roar carried the Law of Causality across the entire universe, producing Roaring Tokens as a natural byproduct — were still plentiful, tokens were cheap enough to abuse. Realm Lords would hand thousands to subordinates to hurl insults at rivals day and night, filling the universe with a constant stream of curses that drove every living thing half-mad.

That era ended only when the Echo Beasts were hunted nearly to extinction. Now, even Supreme Lords thought twice before spending a single token.

Which was why what happened next left the universe speechless.

Zane activated the token.

“You want to know how many I have?”

He activated it again.

“Then count.”

[AMBIENT: COSMIC MURMURS]

[MUSIC: CURIOUS TENSION]

“Who is this guy, just showing off his wealth like that?”

“Probably some spoiled brat of a Supreme Lord. I’ve never seen anyone burn through tokens like this!”

Similar comments echoed from creatures all across the universe.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC BUILD]

But Zane just kept counting.

“Twenty.”

“Twenty-one.”

“One hundred.”

“Ten tokens alone could buy an ordinary tenth-tier sect,” someone muttered. “He’s already past that.”

The crowd had gone from curious to stunned before the count even hit fifty.

[SFX: SHOCKED GASP]

[MUSIC: ASTONISHMENT THEME]

Magnus and Armond were speechless.

Sure, they had more Roaring Tokens than just about anyone, but their stashes were a joke compared to this kid’s. Magnus, who farmed tokens from his stash of Echo Beasts — rare divine creatures capable of producing them — only had a little over a hundred on hand. The next batch wouldn’t be ready for a while.

Armond had even fewer—maybe eighty, tops.

And even with collections that dwarfed any other Supreme Lord’s, they could never afford to just waste them the way Zane was.

They couldn’t wrap their heads around it. How did some no-name kid from the back end of nowhere get his hands on so many Roaring Tokens?

Trading just a few of those could have netted him a mountain of resources for his training! Why was he just throwing them away like this?

[AMBIENT: COSMIC VOID]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

While they fumed, Zane’s voice kept echoing across the universe.

“One hundred and ten.”

“One hundred and twenty.”

“One hundred and thirty.”

The numbers just kept climbing.

[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDUP]

The counting went on for four straight hours. Across the universe, listeners went from shocked to disbelieving, and finally, to just… numb. Zane had just hit the ten thousandth mark when another voice finally cut through the silence.

[SFX: ETHEREAL VOICE ECHO]

“Hey, what are you doing? Is counting really that much fun?”

It was a woman’s voice, light, airy, and incredibly pleasant.

But the World Lords and Supreme Lords who heard it?

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRAMATIC CHORD]

Their faces went slack with terror.
