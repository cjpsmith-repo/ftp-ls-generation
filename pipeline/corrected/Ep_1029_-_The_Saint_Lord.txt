Ep 1029 - The Saint Lord

Word count: 1056

[AMBIENT: SACRED TEMPLE]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STRINGS]

Master Sloan and Master Hill had gotten Zane away from the long-haired youth’s crew, but their relief was short-lived — they still had to deliver him to the Saint Lord.

The Soulbound Sect was a sacred place of the tenth grade—one of the three major powers in this supreme world—and its very heart was here in the Holy City.

Here, an elder was a high-ranking official. You just didn’t mess with them.

Zane had seen this song and dance before, so he was used to the protocol. Still, he managed a smile. “Thanks for your concern, but I’ll be fine.”

“I certainly hope so,” Master Sloan said with a solemn nod.

The three of them continued on their way.

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

A few hours later, a massive structure, nearly the size of Oracle City, loomed before them.

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

[MUSIC: WONDER AND AWE]

But what truly stunned Zane was that the building wasn’t on the ground, nor was it floating in the air like the Cloud Hall. Instead, it rose directly from the back of a giant tortoise.

“That gigantic tortoise...” Zane breathed, his voice trailing off.

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT CREATURE BREATHING]

The creature was clearly alive, yet it lay perfectly motionless, radiating an aura that felt like nothing from his world.

“That’s no tortoise,” Master Sloan corrected gently. “It’s a colossal snake with a shell.”

“A snake with a shell? And it just lets people build a city on its back?” Zane asked, his eyes wide with disbelief.

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Master Sloan chuckled. “Of course. It’s the contractual beast of our sect’s leader. It also serves as the guardian of the entire Holy City.”

“And it just… doesn’t move?” Zane pressed, his curiosity getting the better of him. “With a whole building on its back?”

“It hasn’t moved in three thousand years,” Master Sloan said. “I remember, when I was young, it used to be positioned over there. It’s only shifted its position once in all that time.”

The Upper World was filled with so many incredible things it was hard to keep track. If a single sect held this many wonders, Zane could only imagine what miracles the other supreme worlds contained.

He stared in awe for a few more minutes before tearing his eyes away to follow Master Sloan and Master Hill toward the massive building.

[SFX: ARMORED FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

As they drew closer to the strange creature, a squad of armored warriors emerged from the structure, stepping out to block their path. The warriors wore brown armor forged from what looked like dragon scales, and every last one of them had reached the Spirit Supreme Realm.

“Who goes there?” the lead warrior demanded, his voice cold. “State your business at the Spirit Hall.”

Master Sloan simply smiled and held up the token in his hand.

The lead warrior’s eyes widened the moment he saw it. “Master Sloan! My apologies. What brings you here today?”

“We were summoned by the Saint Lord,” Master Sloan said. “Please pass on the message.”

The lead warrior’s expression shifted. He turned and disappeared inside.

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Eventually, they were brought before a middle-aged man. He sat alone at a long table covered in strangely shaped fruits, their fragrance filling the hall. A purple-gold crown rested on his head. He was eating unhurriedly.

Master Sloan and Master Hill stepped forward and bowed. “Greetings, Saint Lord.”

The Saint Lord gave a faint nod. His gaze moved to Zane — and in that single look, Zane felt completely seen through. It wasn’t a comfortable feeling.

The Saint Lord frowned. “Good seedling,” he murmured, almost to himself. “But at this level, he shouldn’t qualify…” He looked again. “Strange. We have warriors like this. It shouldn’t be him.”

Zane had no idea what he was talking about. Neither did the two masters.

He didn’t have the patience for riddles. “Why did you summon me?” he asked.

Master Sloan went rigid. The Saint Lord was strict — addressing him like that, without any ceremony, was the kind of thing that got people punished.

But the Saint Lord only smiled faintly. “Nothing serious. The mines weren’t suitable for you.”

“Then can I leave the Soulbound Sect?” Zane asked immediately.

“No.” The smile didn’t waver. “You can’t leave the Sect. You can’t even leave the Holy City.”

Zane’s frown deepened. “You’re imprisoning me?”

Master Sloan couldn’t take it any longer. “Zane,” he said quietly, “the Saint Lord is keeping you in the Holy City. He won’t treat you badly. That’s all you need to know.”

The Saint Lord waved him off. “It’s fine. I’m not imprisoning you. Someone is coming here — it will take some time. Wait here.”

“Who?” Zane asked.

“You’ll know when they arrive.” The Saint Lord looked at Master Sloan. “Take him to register for a nameplate to the Myriad Spirit House. He can use the cultivation area while he waits.”

“Yes, Saint Lord.” Master Sloan bowed immediately.

The Saint Lord glanced back at Zane. “If you need anything, come find me directly.” With that, the audience was over.

That brief, quiet encounter left the two masters far more rattled than before.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL ORCHESTRAL]

Their Saint Lord was on par with the World Lords, one of the most powerful masters in the entire Upper World. Even without the official title, his strength was every bit their equal. And yet, today, that same Saint Lord had been so… friendly. He’d personally arranged for Zane to get a token and had even told the boy to come directly to him for help.

Master Sloan and Master Hill had figured Zane was connected to an elder, but this changed everything.

[AMBIENT: SACRED TEMPLE]

[MUSIC: REVERENT CHOIR]

It was common knowledge that the Myriad Spirit House was the very core of the Soulbound Sect. Only the most exceptional disciples were qualified to train there. Master Sloan and Master Hill were both from the Myriad Spirit House, and while they may not have been the most brilliant warriors of their time, they were still skilled enough to lead their own halls.

A place like that was reserved for the most talented core disciples. Zane wasn’t even a member of the sect. Normally, an outsider like him would never be allowed inside. But since the Saint Lord himself had invited him to train there, no one would dare object.

And so Zane, a young man at the Life-and-Death Realm, became an even greater enigma to the two hall leaders.

Just who in the hell was backing this kid?
