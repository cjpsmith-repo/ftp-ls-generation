[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CHOIR]

[AMBIENT: COSMIC RESONANCE]

So, the heavens have feelings.

It wasn't just a metaphor; it was a truth that washed over Zane with the force of a tidal wave, a flood of emotions from the Celestial Fragment that now resided within him. In that instant, he understood the crushing helplessness the fragment must have felt as it shattered, and he finally grasped what Lavender had told him: the current Cosmic Age was the sky itself.

When the old age was destroyed, a new one had to rise and take its place. This was why warriors who broke through to the Life-and-Death Realm had to face the Heavenly Tribulations—all of it was channeled through the sky. But how did the new replace the old? The question was a vast, unsolvable mystery, but with the fragment inside him, Zane had a feeling the answers would come in time.

[SFX: MYSTICAL CHIME]

[AMBIENT: CELESTIAL HUM]

 Beyond the raw emotions, something else was taking shape in his mind. Rows of tightly packed characters were mapping themselves across his consciousness. They were arranged in pairs: a line of brilliant gold script followed by a line of deeper, darker gold, like a question and its answer. It was the Law of Causality, recorded for eternity, though the script was all gibberish to him for now.

The first row was the cause; the second was the effect.

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

 It was the fundamental principle of the universe, a dizzying revelation that hit him all at once. If a warrior wanted to master cause and effect, they had to understand this law, which governed everything. Plant a seed, and it grows. Work hard, and you see results. Cultivate a technique, and you break through. From the smallest blade of grass to the most powerful warrior, everything was bound by this chain of causality, making it the supreme rule of the cosmos. 

Laws of Fire, Water, Time, and Space were nothing next to it—they were all lesser rules, existing side-by-side but forever subordinate.

And the rules recorded in the fragment were from three point six billion years ago. The sheer age of it was staggering. 

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

Then one row lit up.

A single pair of characters blazed with golden light, and Zane's consciousness locked onto them as if drawn by gravity. The meaning of them arrived whole, without translation.

Because of the eternal cycle of reincarnation, it will never perish.

Eternal reincarnation. Eternal immortality. He turned the words over in his mind and sank into them completely, unaware of anything else.

[AMBIENT: QUIET CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL PIANO]

Cora sat beside him in silence, watching his face. He had created too many miracles for her to give up on him now. But sitting still while he lay there, cold and motionless — that was its own kind of suffering.

Regret had been building in her chest since the moment she pulled him out of the battlefield. She had the ability to summon infant true spirits from the Violet Power Realm — forces that, even in their earliest state, should have been enough to deal with Maddox. She had held back because Zane had told her more than once not to interfere in his fight. He had to face Maddox himself. She had respected that.

Watching him now, she wasn't sure she'd make the same choice again.

"Cora."

She looked up. Lavender was staring at Zane with an odd expression.

"Something's happening to him."

[SFX: SHARP GASP]

Cora looked down. Zane's face had changed. The hard lines were softening. The weight behind his eyes — the years of killing and loss that had settled into his features — was fading. He looked younger. Not a little younger. Noticeably younger.

"His body," Cora said slowly. "It's getting smaller."

"What nonsense—" Lavender looked. She went still. "It has. It actually has."

[MUSIC: UNSETTLING STRINGS]

The change kept coming. He dropped from his early twenties to something like fourteen or fifteen, the sharpness of his face smoothing into something more open, more boyish. His clothes — already loose from the battle — became comically oversized, hanging off him like a child trying on an adult's robes.

Then eleven or twelve.

Then seven or eight.

Cora watched with wide eyes, unable to speak.

Three or four years old. Then younger still. Then Zane was an infant, lying in his own clothes, his face round and soft, his lashes long against his cheeks.

"Is that…" Cora's voice came out barely above a whisper. "Did he look like that? When he was small?"

Lavender didn't answer. She was thinking.

[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Then the reversal began. The infant form stretched and expanded, aging in seconds — baby to boy, boy to child, child toward the young man he had been.

 Lavender’s eyes shot wide. “What’s happening? Is he… is that a Physical Reforging? Or is it reincarnation?”

Even with all their knowledge, Lavender and the Cobalt Dragon knew next to nothing about the Law of Causality. It was the purest law of the heavens, something even Supreme Lords struggled to grasp. Anyone who could master it was considered the best of the best. 

From what Lavender could recall, both reforging and reincarnation could produce an effect like this, but seeing it in person was a first. There was no way to be sure. 

“Does it matter which one it is?” Cora asked, her voice tight with worry.

“No,” Lavender admitted. “If his body is being reforged, it will become purer. If he’s being reincarnated, it won’t hurt him. Either way, I think… I think he’s going to wake up.”

But Zane’s body was already an anomaly, having been purified by Primordial Energy. A Physical Reforging was a rare gift for any other warrior, but for Zane, it might not be an improvement. A reforged body might not be as strong as the one he already had.

“I see,” Cora said, nodding slowly.

She didn't understand a word of it, and she didn't care. 

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL PIANO]

 She just wanted Zane to open his eyes.
