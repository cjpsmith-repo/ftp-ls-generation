Ep 732 - We Don't Need Him
Word count: 1108
[AMBIENT: TENSE MEETING HALL]
[SFX: COLD WIND GUST]
Most of the elders were gathered in the meeting hall, waiting. Just before Tia was scheduled to arrive, a bitter wind swept through the chamber, plunging the temperature until it felt like a walk-in freezer. A palpable wave of Malice washed over them, so thick and potent that a few of the elders couldn’t stop themselves from flinching.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Then she walked in, her face a thundercloud. The elders exchanged uneasy glances, completely baffled as to what could have set their leader off.
"What happened to her?" one whispered.
"Who in the hell pissed her off?" another muttered back.
"Quiet! She’ll hear you!"
The air was so heavy with her foul mood that no one dared speak above a whisper. The last thing any of them wanted was to be the target of that fury.
[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]
Saint Morphen stepped forward and bowed low. "Leader Tia, everyone is assembled. We can depart whenever you're ready."
"Fine," Tia snapped, her mind clearly a thousand miles away.
Noting her dark expression and distracted tone, Morphen felt compelled to press the issue. "You should know," he said, his voice cautious, "Zane is not here yet."
At the mention of his name, every elder turned to watch Tia, holding their breath for her reaction.
Since Zane’s return, they had all been forced to re-evaluate him. Though he was only in the early stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, he’d killed Emanuel with terrifying ease. He was likely stronger than even Saint Morphen now, making him one of Cloud Hall’s most indispensable fighters. A war between sects might involve billions of warriors, but it was always the masters at the very top—the ones with power that could shatter mountains—who decided the final outcome.
After a month of tense negotiations, Cloud Hall and Oracle City had finally struck a deal. As the two most powerful sects in the Heartlands, they couldn't just sit back and wait for the Corrupted Divine Kingdom to swallow them whole. They would fight back, together.
Saint Morphen had originally planned to inform Zane himself, but Tia had insisted on taking care of it. Now, she was back, looking ready to kill someone, and Zane was nowhere in sight. The situation made no sense, and Morphen, along with every other elder in the room, was completely lost.
[SFX: ENERGY BURST]
"We don't need him," Tia roared, her aura exploding from her in a violent shockwave. Her power had always been formidable, but since reaching the Life-and-Death Realm, it had become something else entirely. As that energy surged through the hall, a cold dread snaked down the spines of the elders who felt it. "Gather all warriors at or above the Soul Realm. We're leaving. Now!"
[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
High above Cloud Hall, countless disciples formed ranks in the sky before descending like a living storm toward the city of Cloudhaven below. From the ground, it looked as if the heavens were raining black specks, each one a warrior plunging toward the earth. For the ordinary people of the city, it was a sight both terrifying and awe-inspiring.
[AMBIENT: BUSY CITY CROWD]
"I heard Cloud Hall is marching to war!"
"About time! The Corrupted Divine Kingdom has been expanding too fast. I bet this is a counterattack with the World Merchants' Guild."
At the teleportation array, Saint Morphen pulled Tia aside and spoke quietly. "We should bring Zane. Whatever else is happening, this is too important. His grudge with Maddox runs deep, and his strength could decide this war. Personal matters shouldn't determine the fate of Cloud Hall."
Tia looked at him. Then she smiled — a cold, thin smile. "He seems very happy where he is. A beauty waiting at his door every day. I wouldn't want to interrupt that."
Saint Morphen stared at her.
She turned and stepped through the array.
The other elders looked at each other, smiled bitterly, and followed.
[AMBIENT: QUIET TRAINING ROOM]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
While an army marched to war, one of its most critical warriors remained completely oblivious, his world contained within the four walls of a practice room.
The Cobalt Dragon and Red Dragon had gone quiet — during a breakthrough push, they rarely interfered. Even Lavender seemed to have sunk into a deep sleep and had barely appeared in days.
Outside his door, the small offering of a few flowers had multiplied. Cora had been placing one pot a day — colorful, carefully arranged, each one trimmed and positioned with the kind of attention she'd once given to her wardrobe. There were thirty-five now, one for each day that had passed.
Cloud Hall itself had grown nearly empty. The practice rooms that once hummed with activity were almost deserted. Even the ordinary people of Cloudhaven knew about the expedition. Cora, absorbed entirely in her small world at this door, had no idea.
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY EXPLOSION]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
Suddenly, a powerful aura burst from within the room. It was one of Cloud Hall's top-tier training chambers, with walls forged from a special alloy, but even they couldn’t fully contain the sheer force that erupted from it.
A smile bloomed on Cora’s face. "Did he break through?" she whispered to herself. "I wonder if he’ll come out today." Her cheeks flushed as she stared at the door, nervously rehearsing the words she wanted to say to him.
[SFX: DOOR OPENING]
With a soft click, the door swung open. Zane stepped out, a faint, weary smile on his face. He had finally reached the middle stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.
[MUSIC: UPLIFTING ORCHESTRAL]
Cora jumped to her feet and rushed toward him."You did it! You really broke through!"
"Sorry to keep you waiting," Zane said, his voice raspy. He had lost all track of time in his meditation. "How long was I in there?"
Cora’s gaze softened. "Count the flowers," she said, her voice barely a whisper. "That's how many days it's been."
Zane’s eyes swept over the small garden that had sprung up at his door, and his expression shifted. "Thirty-five days," he murmured, stunned. "I was really in seclusion that long."
[MUSIC: INTIMATE PIANO]
"It's okay," Cora reassured him. Tucking a stray strand of dark hair behind her ear, she looked down and spoke so softly he almost missed it. "Zane… can I stay with you tonight?" As the words left her lips, her cheeks turned bright red.
Surprised, Zane hesitated for only a moment before the tension left his shoulders and he gave a small nod. They were both so young, and it had been a first for them both. It was only natural they would long for the closeness they’d shared.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
The only thing that gave him pause was the part of his heart that still belonged to Tia—unaware, of course, that she had already marched out of his life and gone to war.
