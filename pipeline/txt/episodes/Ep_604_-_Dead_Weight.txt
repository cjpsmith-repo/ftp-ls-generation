Ep 604 - Dead Weight
Word count: 1124
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]
Right now, Boris looked like some primal beast torn from a forgotten age.
Any warrior who made it to the tenth floor of the Tower of Sin was at least a tenth-level War General. But it didn't matter what their rank was; anyone caught in Boris's oppressive aura would feel their heart hammer against their ribs.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
"Damn, Boris is the real deal. I bet even some of the War Lords on eleven don't have an aura this heavy."
"No kidding. They don't call him the Unbeatable War General for nothing."
"But if you ask me, Zane's the one to watch. He's just a tenth-level War Fighter, and he's going toe-to-toe with Boris. Think about it—if a new War Lord can't beat Boris, doesn't that mean Zane could probably handle some of the weaker ones right now?"
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[SFX: GROUND CRACKING]
As Boris’s aura and power surged, a wave of raw energy erupted from him. He stomped his feet, and the arena floor cracked, leaving two deep footprints in his wake.
Zane’s eyes narrowed. The iron sword in his hand seemed to move on its own, snapping up to block the attack.
Zane’s sword was just a sliver of steel, its blade barely three fingers wide. In contrast, Boris's broad-bladed saber was a massive, brutal weapon.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
When Boris saw Zane raise that little blade to block him, a vicious grin split his face. He charged, swinging his saber down on Zane’s head with the force of a thunderclap.
"Thunderous Cleave!"
Boris didn't care if Zane was blocking with an iron sword or an entire mountain. It made no difference. He was sure one hit would shatter it to pieces.
[SFX: CRACKLING ELECTRICITY]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Raw power pulsed off Boris in waves. Countless streaks of scarlet lightning—his blade intent made manifest—crackled along the spine of his saber, infused with the raw power of thunder.
The technique was surprisingly similar to Finn's Extreme Thunder Sword Intent.
[AMBIENT: CONFUSED CROWD MURMUR]
A confused murmur rippled through the crowd, no one understanding what they were seeing.
"Why isn't Zane dodging?"
"Is he seriously going to take that head-on?"
"That's suicide! His little sword is no match for that monster. Boris will just chop right through it!"
"He's too confident!" Olivia couldn't help but shout.
Zane had other options, better ways to handle this, but he chose to meet the attack directly. The only reason, Olivia realized, had to be sheer, unfiltered confidence.
But it wasn't just confidence. Zane understood that he and the Ogres had already passed the point of no return. The War Lords watching from the stands would never let him walk away. Since the Talent Tablet had already set off a wave in the Tower, he intended to make it a storm. He wanted to crush the Ogres' arrogance completely. And this sword was where it started.
[SFX: MAGICAL ACTIVATION]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL RISE]
"Dragon Scales, activate!"
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
One by one, the Dragon Scales in Zane’s mind began to glow with a jade-green light. As immense power flooded his body, the faintest hint of a smile touched his lips.
[SFX: MASSIVE CLASH]
Zane's sword met Boris's saber. Not with skill, not with deflection—just a light, almost casual block, like he was holding a door shut against a breeze.
In everyone's eyes, Boris had thrown everything into that strike. It was his most powerful attack. War Lords from the upper floors wouldn't dare take it head-on. And yet Zane stood there like a wall, perfectly still, not dispersing the force, not using any technique. Just holding.
And Boris stopped dead.
The arena went silent.
Boris stared at the blade pinning his saber in place. The hand gripping his weapon felt suddenly, terribly weak.
He already knew. He'd known the moment the swords met.
"I lost."
He said it plainly, without drama. It wasn't surrender—his warrior's spirit was too hard for that. But Boris's most powerful strike, the one he'd spent years forging, had just broken against a human boy holding his sword like a feather. Every title, every reputation, every year of training—it all rang hollow now.
Boris's saber slowly pulled back. He said nothing more.
Zane lowered his sword and gave a slight nod. "Thanks for the fight."
In the Tower's arena, the winner had every right to take the loser's life. Zane didn't. Not out of fear of the Ogres—he'd already shown them what he was. He spared Boris because Boris was the only Ogre he'd respected. And because killing him wouldn't prove anything that hadn't already been proven.
[SFX: MAGICAL LIGHT]
A thick beam of Lucky Light shot out from Boris’s chest and poured into the token on Zane’s.
It was just as he'd predicted. The tenth floor of the Tower of Sin was a goldmine for Lucky Light. That was one of the main reasons he’d rushed to get here; the rate of accumulation was just so much faster.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
Defeated, Boris staggered off the arena floor, carrying his saber like dead weight. He looked like a walking corpse.
His warrior's spirit was as tough as forged steel, but Zane’s blow had left it cracked and on the verge of shattering. He would have to retreat, to train and reforge his will. If he found a breakthrough, he would return stronger, more terrifying than ever. But if he failed… this single defeat could cripple his path as a warrior for life.
Malcolm watched from the stands, his expression unreadable. He'd been shocked when Zane seemed to toy with Boris earlier. Now that shock had curdled into something colder. This kid couldn't be allowed to keep rising. Not a week from now, not even a day. If Zane was given time, the Ogres wouldn't have a foothold left in the Tower.
Malcolm leapt down from the stands and stalked toward the arena. "Zane, since our War General couldn't get the job done, how about you fight me instead? Do you accept?"
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMUR]
A shockwave of disbelief went through the crowd.
According to the rules of the Tower of Sin, warriors from the upper floors could visit the lower ones, but they were strictly forbidden from fighting in the arenas there.
The rule existed for balance. If fighters could just drop down to easier floors to farm Lucky Light from weaker opponents, the entire system would collapse.
There was no way Malcolm didn't know the rules.
To challenge Zane anyway wasn't just arrogant—it was shameless.
The rules of the Tower existed for a reason. But rules, Zane was beginning to understand, only mattered to the people who couldn't afford to break them.
