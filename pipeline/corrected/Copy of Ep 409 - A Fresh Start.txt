Ep 409 - A Fresh Start

Word count: 1172

[AMBIENT: HOWLING MOUNTAIN WIND]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

In the far eastern Heartlands, a massive mountain range clawed at the sky. Soaring high enough to pierce the clouds, they served as the continent's natural shield against tsunamis from the Chaotic Ocean of Stars. Their staggering height kept every peak buried under a thick blanket of snow, in some places a hundred feet deep. The range was considered a dead zone—fall into that snow, and you weren't climbing out.

Even Soul Realm warriors, who could fly, wouldn't dare venture too deep. The snowstorms that raged at the peaks were brutal, the wind cutting like razor blades. Not to mention, the range was home to untold monsters and terrifying beasts. Anything that could survive an environment that harsh was bound to be one hell of a fight.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

But today, on the very highest peak, the snow had vanished. After thousands of years of gathering snow, one peak now stood bare, a stark black fang against its white-capped neighbors.

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FLAMES]

A powerful, bare-chested man stood panting on the summit, a gray flame flickering at his back. At his feet lay a massive Snow Drake, five hundred feet long and scorched from head to tail. Snow Drakes were native to these frozen lands, born fierce and impossibly strong, but this one lay defeated, its enormous body broken.

[MUSIC: PLAYFUL FLUTE]

A woman in a fox-fur robe called out, floating in the air behind him. "Little Maddox! I thought the plan was to see the snow and eat a Snow Drake’s heart.”  She was posed languidly, as if lounging on an invisible bench. “But you went and melted the whole mountain. How am I supposed to see the snow now?”

[MUSIC: SEDUCTIVE STRINGS]

Her voice dripped like pure honey, sweet enough to enchant anyone who heard it. Yet even more captivating than her words was the robe itself, which she wore carelessly open, revealing flawless skin even paler than the surrounding snow. She seemed utterly unbothered by the freezing wind that would kill an ordinary person in minutes.

"Your charms and your complaints are wasted on me," the man grunted, wiping blood from his knuckles with an air of practiced indifference. There was no heat in his voice, only the weary patience of someone who'd had this conversation a thousand times before. "You wanted to see the snow, so I brought you. You wanted to eat a Snow Drake's heart…"

[SFX: TEARING FLESH]

[SFX: DYING CREATURE BREATHING]

He bent down and tore the heart straight from the dying drake's chest, his movements efficient and brutal. Creatures like these possessed impossible resilience; even mortally wounded, their hearts still beat with furious power. The organ pulsed in his grip, hot blood steaming in the frigid air.

The woman recoiled, her expression shifting from playful seduction to genuine revulsion. Her nose wrinkled delicately. "Oh, Little Maddox, that's disgusting!" She waved one pale hand dismissively, as if she could banish the sight. "How could I possibly eat that?" Despite her protest, there was a glimmer of amusement in her eyes—she was testing him, as always, pushing to see how far his patience would stretch.
Then Maddox went still.
"Little Maddox?" The woman's amusement faded.
"Someone wants me dead." His voice was flat, almost bored. He had a sense for it — a perception honed by years of enemies and assassination attempts. Thousands of people had pointed their killing intent at him over the years. He'd stopped paying attention long ago.
But this one was different. Not because the person was strong — they were barely worth noticing. What made him pause was the intensity. Pure, focused, almost irrational in its depth.
He couldn't locate them. Couldn't even tell where they were. Just the faint pressure of an obsession pointed squarely at him.
"An ant," he said finally, and let it go.
But the unease didn't quite leave his eyes.

[AMBIENT: QUIET HALL]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Back in Cloud Hall, Zane retreated to the solitude he needed. Time to train. Time to process everything the Cloud Road had revealed to him.

His Warrior's Vow pulsed through his veins, stronger than ever before. When Finn and Patrick saw the renewed determination burning in his eyes, their relief was palpable—they'd witnessed his despair at the map, and now they saw him rise from it.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SWIRLING]

[AMBIENT: MEDITATION ROOM]

Zane settled into a cross-legged position on the practice room floor, his breathing slow and deliberate. He closed his eyes for a moment, centering himself, then began rotating his hands in a fluid, circular motion. The movement felt natural, almost instinctive, as if the technique had been waiting inside him all along.

The Vitality around him responded immediately, swirling inward like water spiraling down a drain. His hands spun faster, building momentum, and the wild energy coalesced into something tangible—a perfect, shimmering disk that hummed with barely contained power.

“The Malevolent Cycle of Life and Death… The Blood Fiend Emperor’s most powerful technique,” he murmured. “I’m surprised it isn’t more complicated.”

A small smile touched his lips as he watched the spinning wheel of Vitality. He was still a long way from mastering its first level, the Exterminating Wheel, but this was a start. He’d created the basic form.

Attuning with Malice has its perks, he thought. It’s a shortcut—easier to master and far more powerful than other techniques. That's why so many are drawn to it. Of course, there was the small problem of being consumed and controlled by the Malice itself. But for Zane, who had already survived Killing Sword Mountain, that wasn’t much of a risk.

“I wonder what the Exterminating Wheel can really do?”

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

With that thought, Zane drew out a sliver of Malice.

[SFX: VIOLENT ENERGY PULSE]

[SFX: SHATTERING STONE]

The moment the blood-red sliver touched the wheel, violent energy pulsed through the room. The Exterminating Wheel began to spin with terrifying speed, far beyond anything Zane had anticipated. His breath caught in his throat as he realized his mistake—he'd underestimated the technique's raw power.

The blast of energy was unbelievable. It tore through the practice room floor beneath his feet, shattering layers of reinforced wood as if they were paper. The destruction didn't stop there. The energy ripped through a thick slab of rock meant to withstand Core Strength attacks, reducing it to fragments in an instant. 

Zane's eyes widened in horror as the devastating force kept going, punching through four hundred feet of floating stone that formed Cloud Hall's foundation. The wheel's energy finally slammed into the ground over half a mile below with a thunderous impact that shook the air.

Zane stood frozen, staring at the gaping hole where the solid floor had been moments before. His hands trembled slightly—not from fear, but from the realization of what he'd just unleashed. This technique was far more dangerous than he'd imagined.

[SFX: ALARM BLARING]

An alarm started blaring outside, its shrill cry cutting through the settling dust and debris.

Zane's heart sank. “So much for keeping a low profile.”
