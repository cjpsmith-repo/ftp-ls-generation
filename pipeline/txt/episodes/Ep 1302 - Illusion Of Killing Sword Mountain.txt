Ep 1302 - Illusion Of Killing Sword Mountain

Word count: 1070

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: COSMIC VOID]
There’s a ceiling to everything in the universe, even power.
Take Fire Law, for instance. Its power caps out at the tenth level, but not just anyone can get there. Soul Sea warriors could only hit the fifth level, Spirit Supremes the sixth, and Spirit Transformation warriors the seventh. Even World Lords stalled out at the eighth, and the mighty Supreme Lords at the ninth. It was a universal law, a hard limit that was nearly impossible to break.
The only exceptions were the rare few Supreme Lords who had accepted the Heavenly Destiny of Fire Law. They were the ones who could cultivate it to its absolute peak—the tenth level.
That tenth level was considered perfection within the current cycle of the heavens, but it wasn’t the *real* end of the line. There were levels beyond the tenth, heights so far out of reach that most beings couldn’t even fathom them, let alone dream of getting there. 
To get there, you had to transcend the heavens themselves and become a True God.
And that wasn’t something just anyone could do.
[SFX: SHIMMERING CHIME]
That lotus flower you saw floating over the heads of so many warriors? That was the mark of their potential to cross that very barrier.
Everyone trained in their own Godly Way—the Way of the Five Elements, the Way of Snow, you name it.
“If I’m not mistaken… you’ve trained in the Divine Path of Sealed Emotion,” Reggie said, studying him.
“Sealed Emotion…” Zane repeated, surprised. “That’s a Godly Way?”
Zane understood its core principles—it was how he’d pulled off that Gods-Intimidating Strike. But he’d never realized it was a full-fledged Godly Way. He’d just assumed it was a technique he’d picked up from mastering the strike itself.
“Yes,” Reggie confirmed. “It’s an incredibly powerful Godly Way. But it’s also forbidden.” He paused, his expression turning serious. 
“Succeed in cultivating it, and you’ll bring the wrath of the gods down on your head. Trust me, you don’t want that.”
Reggie radiated a fierce power, the kind that came from fearing no one. It was that untamed energy, that commanding presence, that had allowed him to master the Domineering Divine Path in the first place. And yet, even he seemed genuinely wary when he spoke of the Path of Sealed Emotion.
“Is that right?” Zane murmured, his eyes flickering.
[MUSIC: QUIET DETERMINATION]
Unlike Reggie’s brash confidence, Zane’s strength was quieter, but his spirit was forged from something far tougher. He’d listen to the advice from a True God, of course—he wasn’t a fool. But no amount of warning was going to change the path he’d already set for himself.
Reggie seemed to read the thought in Zane’s eyes anyway. He laughed and lowered his saber. “That’s all I have to say. Go, then.”
[SFX: DEEP BOOM]
The isolated city, the battlefield, and the demon army scattered across the ground all dissolved at once. Zane found himself back in the Corridor of the Emperor, torches lining the corridor on either side.
[MUSIC: SOMBER WALKING THEME]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING CORRIDOR]
He walked on, passing torch after torch, each one holding the soul imprint of a different True God. 
Torch after torch, the auras grew heavier the deeper he went, each one harder to stand against than the last.
Whatever these True Gods had pursued, however different their paths and their natures, Zane could feel the same thing radiating from every one of them: a will to reach the peak, and then to keep climbing past it.
“I won’t give up,” he murmured. Sealed Emotion hadn’t been his choice—it had simply been the path his sword found for him. But he didn’t regret it. Not once.
Then, ahead of him, one flame caught his attention—flickering fast, faster than any of the others, and carrying a strange, familiar feeling from the soul housed within it.
“This flame…” Zane murmured, and stepped forward without hesitation.
[SFX: FLAME EXPLOSION]
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
The flame burst open in front of him. When Zane opened his eyes, surprise flickered across his face—he recognized this place. Countless swords stood driven into the mountainside before him: the Killing Sword Mountain. He’d stood at the foot of this very mountain once before, the day he first grasped his perfect sword intent. He’d never expected to find himself back here, even if only inside another True God’s vision.
The Supreme Lord he was thinking of was said to be missing. If he were dead, his Heavenly Destiny would have already scattered and reformed into a new Crown of Destiny. The fact that it hadn’t meant Supreme Lord Sword was still alive.
So, seeing the Killing Sword Mountain here… did that mean Supreme Lord Sword had broken through and become a True God? And if so, had he done it by following the Path of Sealed Emotion?
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE MYSTERY]
Of course, it was all just a guess. The mountain could belong to another True God from the same lineage, not necessarily him. Still, Zane had a gut feeling.
Standing at the foot of the mountain, Zane looked up and saw a faint figure standing at the very peak of the Killing Sword Mountain.
“Alright,” Zane murmured, steadying himself with a deep breath. “Time to climb up there and get some answers.”
Whoever that was, Zane was going to pass this test.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]
[SFX: SWORD SHOOTING FROM GROUND]
A rush of air was his only warning before a longsword shot up from the ground, aiming straight for him. Just a Mysterious Weapon.
Zane didn’t even flinch. 
[SFX: BLADE CAUGHT]
[SFX: SPIRITUAL ENERGY DISSIPATING]
He just held out two fingers, catching the blade by its very tip and stopping it cold. With a casual flick of his wrist, the sword’s spiritual energy fizzled out, leaving it inert.
He held the now-ordinary blade for a moment before starting his slow walk up the mountain.
The first time he’d climbed this mountain, every single step had been a life-or-death struggle. Now, it felt like a walk in the park. 
[SFX: MULTIPLE SWORDS RISING]
Swords kept rising from the ground, swarming him one after another, but he just waved them away like annoying flies.
[MUSIC: NOSTALGIC REFLECTION]
Even though this was just an illusion, not the real Killing Sword Mountain, a deep sense of nostalgia washed over him. 
It was good to be back.
