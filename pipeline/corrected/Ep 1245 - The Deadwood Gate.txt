Ep 1245 - The Deadwood Gate

Word count: 1081

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[SFX: MAGICAL PORTAL HUMMING]
Between two massive, skeletal trees, a whirlpool of shimmering silver light churned in the air.
Rowan took the lead. “We have to go through that,” she warned, her voice tight. “But be ready. The Deadwood Gate will attack.”
[MUSIC: INTENSE RISING TENSION]
[SFX: MASSIVE TREES GROANING]
The words were barely out of her mouth before the two colossal trees started to shake, groaning as if waking from a long slumber. 
The group watched in horror as the supposedly dead wood came alive, their branches whipping violently in their direction. 
Rowan hadn’t been kidding.
There was no time to hesitate. 
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS ON FOREST FLOOR]
With Rowan at the front, the group charged forward. 
[SFX: BRANCHES LASHING]
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY CRACKLING]
The moment they got close, the branches lashed out, glowing with a strange, deep green light that hummed with the power of life itself. The force behind them was so immense that the air itself seemed to crackle with each swing.
“Watch out!” Rowan shouted. They were like flies next to these things; a single clean hit would be enough to crush any one of them.
[SFX: BRANCH WHOOSHING]
A thick branch snapped through the air, aimed straight for Zane. 
He didn’t flinch, simply ducking under the whistling limb as it flew past his head. But just as he straightened, three more came swinging at him from different angles. 
He moved with an easy grace, his Cloud-Chaser Skill letting him weave through the gaps between the attacks. He didn’t even need to use his Eight Smoky Melodies. This was barely a warm-up.
The others weren’t so lucky. 
[MUSIC: CHAOTIC BATTLE DRUMS]
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[SFX: BODY FALLING]
A sharp cry cut through the chaos as a branch slammed into the Demon Night archer. The sickening crack of the impact knocked her out cold. 
[SFX: BRANCHES WRAPPING]
Before her body could even hit the ground, three more branches shot out from one of the trees and wrapped around her limp form.
Rowan’s face went white. 
“Save her!” she screamed, watching in horror as the branches began to drag their unconscious teammate toward the tree’s trunk, which seemed to be opening like a grotesque mouth.
Two other Demon Night warriors rushed to help, but it was useless. 
[SFX: MULTIPLE IMPACTS]
[SFX: BODIES TUMBLING]
A dozen smaller branches swatted them away like insects, sending them tumbling back several feet. The trees were just too strong. The situation was grim. They’d come here to rescue their trapped friends, and now it looked like they were about to lose someone before they even got inside.
“They’ve done this before,” one Demon Night cultivator muttered darkly. “Last time we tried this gate, it dragged one of ours in alive — didn’t kill her outright. Just held her there. When the rest of us tried to save her, it picked us off one by one. We lost the whole party and half our Illusion Points before we ever got inside.”
The Dead Tree Spirit wasn’t just brute strength — it was patient, and it was smart. It knew exactly what a rescue attempt would cost them.
Dying in the Illusion Battlefield wasn’t permanent, but it was still a huge setback. In the midst of the chaos, Zane, the man they’d all but dismissed as dead weight, moved. 
“He’s an idiot,” one of the Demon Night cultivators said flatly, watching him go. “Not a pity if he dies. He’s probably not even carrying enough points to matter.”
“Zane, it’s useless! Come back!” Rowan shouted after him, her warning cutting through the chaos — she knew exactly how this played out, and she didn’t want him dragged in too.
He didn’t listen. He kept moving.
[MUSIC: HEROIC TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]
One moment the archer was being pulled into the tree; the next, she was cradled in his arms, safe.
Or she should have been. Before he could turn back, dozens of branches — fifty, maybe sixty — shot out from every direction at once and wrapped around both of them in an instant, sealing them into a tight, woven cocoon and hauling them back toward the tree’s main trunk.
[SFX: WOOD CREAKING AND TIGHTENING]
Rowan’s stomach dropped. These weren’t ordinary branches — dense with the Laws of Life, they could shrug off blades, fire, even most Divine Weapons without a scratch. 
Aurora himself had been caught in the same trap once and hadn’t been able to break free. If Zane couldn’t get out, there’d be nothing left to do but watch.
A faint cracking sound built from inside the cocoon, growing louder.
[SFX: RISING CRACKING SOUND]
Then, with a deafening crack, the cocoon exploded outward.
The trees weren’t done. 
[SFX: BRANCHES WHOOSHING]
More branches came flying at him, but Zane didn’t even bother to dodge this time. He just punched.
[SFX: WOOD SHATTERING]
[SFX: REPEATED IMPACTS]
The sound of snapping wood echoed through the air as each blow shattered the limbs flying toward him. He knew, with a detached certainty, that he could tear these two ancient tree spirits apart with his bare hands if he wanted to. The only thing stopping him was that they formed the gate itself, and he had no interest in destroying the entrance over a nuisance.
Rowan stared, her breath catching. He’d broken free of that cocoon in what felt like two or three heartbeats — something even Aurora hadn’t managed. A moment ago they’d been mocking him. Now nobody said a word.
“Forward!” Rowan yelled, her voice cutting through the mayhem. In that single moment, she knew she’d made the right choice. Zane’s strength was beyond anything she could have imagined. For the first time, she felt a real sliver of hope that they could actually save Aurora and Amber.
[MUSIC: TENSE UNCERTAIN STRINGS]
But a cold doubt still coiled in the back of her mind. Zane wasn’t one of them. He wasn’t Demon Night. What if, after all this, he decided to take the Deadwood Gate for himself and the humans? It was a major strategic point for earning Illusion Points—Aurora and Amber had used it to climb into the top three thousand ranks.
It was too late for second thoughts, though. She had brought him here. She had to trust him. And maybe, just maybe, she could. He had risked himself to save one of her people. 
Rowan took a deep breath and pushed forward, praying he wouldn’t turn on them in the end.
