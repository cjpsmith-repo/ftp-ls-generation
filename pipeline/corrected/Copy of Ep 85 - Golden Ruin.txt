Suspended in the air, Boyd radiated a divine golden light that transformed him into a celestial being. His normally handsome features now glowed with an otherworldly radiance, making him appear less human and more like an ancient deity descended from the heavens.
The immense power emanating from his form created waves of spiritual pressure that crashed against everyone present. Such overwhelming force drove the weaker pledges to their knees, their bodies trembling as they struggled to remain conscious under the spiritual onslaught. Even the stronger cultivators found themselves gripping their seats, eyes widening in astonishment.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
The magnificent golden light coalesced and expanded, morphing into a massive Light Disc that gracefully descended to encompass the entire battle ring.
At its perimeter stood Boyd, his arms extended wide, fingers tracing intricate patterns through the air. His eyes, now pools of molten gold, narrowed in concentration as sweat beaded on his brow.
After completing a series of precise hand gestures that left trails of shimmering energy, he drew a deep breath and bellowed, "Ruin Ring!"
Those who achieved the Nature Level had transcended ordinary human limitations, ascending to a realm where the boundaries between flesh and spirit blurred. Such cultivators typically possessed not only profound wisdom accumulated through their journey but also a devastating ultimate technique—a final gambit reserved for dire circumstances.
For Boyd, whose pride had never before been so thoroughly challenged, the Ruin Ring represented his masterpiece, a technique he had perfected through years of solitary training and sacrifice. But the move devoured his Vitality. Even after the grueling battle with Zane, Boyd had more than fifty percent of his energy left.
But this was the ninth time Boyd had been sent out of the ring. Each time Zane's punch launched him clear of the arena, Boyd had snapped a Tectonic Ring against the wall and ridden the rebound back in. Each time he came back, Zane was already waiting at the edge.
Out. Back. Out. Back.
The crowd had stopped cheering after the third time. By the fifth, they'd gone completely silent. By the ninth, nobody knew what to say.
Up on the roof, Chloe had stopped talking too. She watched Boyd bounce in and get sent back out like a ball that couldn't land.
"He's running out," she finally said.
Isla said nothing. She was trying very hard not to smile.
Boyd knew it too. Every rebound cost him Vitality. Every exchange burned through what little he had left. He couldn't keep this up — and so, on his ninth return, he channeled everything he had left into one final move.
[SFX: ENERGY DRAINING]
The moment he summoned the Ruin Ring, the rest of it was completely drained. Standing at the edge of the disc, Boyd's face went pale, and he almost collapsed.
He had been hesitant to use the Ruin Ring. He'd developed the technique to give him an edge in The Crucible.
If he revealed his secret weapon now, his future opponents would be expecting it.
But given how the fight was going, Boyd was out of options. He wasn't sure any other attack could guarantee a win against Zane.
What pissed Boyd off even more was being forced to use his ultimate move on some kid at the Marrow Refining Level. Boyd had been so sure he could beat Zane easily. For a Nature Spirit, taking down a mere refiner should have been simple.
[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]
Suddenly, Zane dashed to the edge of the battle ring and threw a punch.
With his Vitality completely gone, Boyd could barely stand. Focused on just keeping his balance, he never saw it coming.
[SFX: IMPACT THUD]
The surprise punch caught him completely off guard, sending him flying out of the battle ring. The moment he hit the ground, he passed out cold.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]
Zane stood at the edge of the arena, his chest heaving as he gasped for air.
His Adamant Body had allowed him to survive the Ruin Ring, but he was still shaken by the power of Boyd's final attack.
He knew how terrifying Nature Spirits were, but this fight had been even harder than facing the Diabolic Duo at Dragon's Keep. He'd taken them out with the Shattered Dagger, all strategy and precision, before they even had a chance to draw their weapons.
Looking back, Zane was relieved he'd killed the two Demons so quickly. After seeing what a Nature Spirit could do, he wasn't so sure he would have won if they'd had the chance to fight back.
[SFX: BODY FALLING]
Finally, Boyd, the top-ranking pledge from Eagle Peak, had been defeated.
Having vanquished many pledges from Eagle Peak single-handedly, Zane had secured a decisive advantage for Misty Peak in the competition.
Among the Eagle Peak group, someone muttered to the person beside them — something about Misty Peak producing monsters. Two years ago it had been Elara, who'd dismantled a top-ranked peak's disciples one by one. Now this.
Yet despite his remarkable triumph, the Misty Peak pledges remained immobilized, as if transformed into marble figures. None had dared imagine he could withstand the devastating Ruin Ring.
There he stood nonetheless, battered but unbowed at the arena's edge, his victory defying all expectations.
The spectacle left them collectively speechless, their minds struggling to process what their eyes had witnessed.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
"Zane won! Misty Peak won!" Ethan finally exclaimed, breaking the spell of silence that had fallen over the crowd. His voice cracked with disbelief and exhilaration as he was the first to recover from the collective shock.
[SFX: GROWING CROWD CHEERS]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT VICTORY]
As the reality of their unexpected victory penetrated the stunned audience, Ethan released a jubilant cry that echoed through the arena. Within heartbeats, a chorus of voices swelled to join his celebration. The initial hesitant cheers quickly transformed into a thunderous ovation as the Misty Peak pledges found their voices.
"Zane! Zane!" they chanted in unison, their faces alight with newfound pride.
"Zane! Zane!" The rhythmic acclaim reverberated across the grounds, growing louder with each repetition.
They roared until their throats grew hoarse, whistled until their lips went dry, and flung their arms skyward in triumphant abandon. Even those who had previously dismissed Zane now celebrated him as their champion, their earlier doubts washed away by the tide of his impossible victory.
Zane's face turned bright red. He'd been so focused on surviving that he hadn't realized he was standing at the edge of the arena, completely naked. Even though they were all guys, he still felt pretty damn embarrassed.
Noticing Zane's awkwardness, Rhys quickly took off his own outer robe and tossed it to him. Zane wrapped the white robe around himself and stepped out of the battle ring.
With Ethan and Rhys by his side, he walked over to Dustin, who, despite his injuries, had stayed to watch the fight.
"Dustin, how are you feeling?" Zane asked.
The fat on Dustin's face wobbled as he chuckled. His small eyes glinted, all traces of his earlier discouragement gone.
On the contrary, people who failed but kept pushing were often the ones who ended up succeeding. But Isla Novak's comment just sounded like bragging.
Seeing that Chloe was still upset, Isla comforted her with a smile. "Hey, Chloe. I'll make it up to you, I promise!"
Chloe pouted. Considering Isla Novak's influence, she felt she had no choice but to accept. Besides, she figured, having Isla Novak owe her a favor was a pretty good trade-off.
That night, all of Misty Peak was buzzing. Everyone was talking about Zane's performance in the battle ring.
[MUSIC: FIERCE DETERMINATION]
Unlike the others, Zane remained detached from the festivities. His mind dwelled elsewhere, consumed by a singular purpose. Alone in his quarters, he emptied his Storage Ring, revealing a glittering mound of crystal cores that caught the lamplight in eerie reflections. By dawn, he would seek out Isla to convert these precious spoils into Points—the currency that might finally grant him passage to the dreaded Infernal Peak, where his sister's fate hung in the balance.
