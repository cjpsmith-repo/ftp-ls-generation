Ep 1305 - The Illusion Became Real

Word count: 1109

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT SANCTUARY ECHO]
Guidance from The First Beacon had been a game-changer for Coleman. It was the key that had finally unlocked the secrets to an indestructible body, a feat he’d failed to achieve on his own when he’d first tried to become an Ace Talent. 
[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY PULSE]
He now understood the principle behind the sentinels Zane had fought in the ancient sanctuary—they kept regenerating because they could draw power from the Tome of Abiding Past. It was the same concept, and Coleman had finally grasped it.
The whole group was thrilled. Everything they’d found in the Corridor of the Emperor was rare and unbelievably valuable, but the real prize was the torches. 
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT DISCOVERY]
They contained the soul marks of True Gods. Finding an actual True God in this universe was practically impossible, but this was the next best thing. It felt like the gods themselves were guiding them.
A few minutes after Coleman emerged, a booming laugh echoed from the corridor’s entrance.
“Ha-ha! Did you all pass?” 
Ansel’s voice preceded him, and then he came strolling through the ten-foot-high archway, a huge, goofy grin plastered on his face. “I can’t believe how easy the Fifth Hall was. Those illusions from the torches were scary, but totally useless.”
His smug declaration was met with stunned silence.
The Fifth Hall of Causality wasn’t a test you could pass or fail. Everyone was meant to get through. The entire point was to use the journey to try and comprehend the auras radiating from the True Gods—it was an incredible training opportunity, not an obstacle course.
“How did you get through?” Janet finally asked, breaking the silence.
Ansel just shrugged. “When the illusion grabbed me, I just ran. I kept running and managed to get away from the flames. I thought I was gonna die a few times, but I always broke out of it just in the nick of time.”
Zane, Coleman, Hailey, and Janet could only stare, speechless. If the soul marks of the True Gods could have been shocked, they would’ve been.
Coleman patiently explained just how valuable the corridor had been, and what Ansel had squandered. Ansel was silent for a moment, the grin slowly sliding off his face as the reality of his mistake sank in.
Then he let out a guttural roar of frustration and sprinted back toward the corridor.
It was no use. No matter how hard he tried, he couldn’t get back in. The Corridor of the Emperor was a one-way path.
Everyone else had come away from the Corridor of the Emperor with real gains, and their spirits were high. Only Ansel trudged along with a face full of gloom, still chewing on everything he’d thrown away.
[AMBIENT: VAST GOLDEN HALL]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ANTICIPATION]
Not long after, the group moved on and found the corridor’s only exit, which led them into a second circular hall. At its center, a ring of golden light flickered, and around it drifted five small, pinkish points of light, hovering like tiny will-o’-the-wisps.
[SFX: CHIME]
The moment the group entered, all five dots floated forward and settled in front of each of their foreheads.
[SFX: SOFT PULSE]
A single thought dropped into each of their minds at once. As it settled in, understanding spread across every face—then pure delight.
“This can turn illusions into reality!” Coleman said, stunned. “This is the real reward!”
The dot, they now understood, was a way to carry something out of the Illusion Battlefield for good. Everyone who entered had done so as a projection—a perfect duplicate of themselves, right down to their gear. 
Zane’s Thundergust and Black Star Pickax, Coleman’s borrowed Blade of Hallowed Censure—all of it existed in two places at once, one inside the Illusion Battlefield and one outside it. Normally, when a warrior left, the projection and everything it carried simply dissolved, leaving nothing behind but the memory of the experience. This dot broke that rule. Whatever it touched, a person got to keep.
Janet let out a breath, eyes bright. She’d gambled five million Illusion Points just to get into this Hall the first time—and failed, sent straight back to the main city for it. Rather than let that loss stand, she’d gambled five million more to come back. For someone without points to spare, it had been an enormous risk. Now, she finally understood it had paid off.
Coleman didn’t hesitate. His standing in The Hallowed Concord wasn’t low, but it wasn’t enough to be personally granted a Divine Weapon of his own—the Blade of Hallowed Censure was only his to carry for the Illusion Battlefield, and he’d have had to hand it back the moment he left. Now, he wouldn’t have to give up anything.
[SFX: BLADE UNSHEATHING]
He drew the blade in one motion and pressed the pink dot against it.
[SFX: CRUSHING CRYSTAL]
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
The dot dissolved into fine powder that settled across the blade, and a faint mark bloomed on its surface where the dust had touched.
Zane, meanwhile, was still trying to decide what to copy. He already had a decent collection of treasures, and he wasn’t in a rush to duplicate any of them. Besides, his greatest weapon was himself, and the pink dot had been clear: items only.
[MUSIC: THOUGHTFUL CONTEMPLATIVE]
*An item,* he thought, his mind racing. 
After a few seconds, his eyes lit up.
He was being so dumb. Why was he only thinking about the gear he’d brought *with* him? There was no point in duplicating Thundergust or the Black Star; he only needed one of each.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL RISING]
But there was one treasure from the Illusion Battlefield that would be a complete game-changer on the outside: the Teleportation Token. With that, he could go anywhere in the universe in an instant. The six years he’d been trapped in The Elysian Keep wouldn’t have been a problem. With that token, not even a Supreme Lord could hold him captive.
His only hesitation was whether it would even work. The token seemed designed to record the main sanctuaries within the Illusion Battlefield’s supreme worlds. Would it function out here?
[MUSIC: TENSE UNCERTAIN]
*This is a long shot,* he thought, pulling out the token anyway. *It’s an item made for the Illusion Battlefield, after all.* But it was a risk he had to take.
[SFX: CRUSHING CRYSTAL]
He reached up and crushed the pink dot between his fingers.
The process was just like before. 
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]
The dot shattered, dissolving into a cloud of shimmering dust that drifted down and enveloped the token in his hand.
