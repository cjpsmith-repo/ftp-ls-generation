"But he's a High-tier candidate! You're at a huge disadvantage!" Rex said anxiously.

Hearing that, Shane was already moving to step in. He knew just how strong Troy was—one of the best soldiers among the High-tier candidates. Zane would be walking into a slaughter.
[SFX: HAND ON SHOULDER]
But before he could get to his feet, Leo's hand landed on his shoulder. He shook his head. "Let him make his own choice," Leo said. "We need to trust him. He came here to fight the Razor swarm alongside these soldiers, right? He has to prove he belongs."
"But—" Shane started, still not convinced.
Leo cut him off before he could argue, murmuring, "I have a feeling there's more to Zane than just his Marrow Refining Level."
As one of the top disciples at the Nature Level, Leo Brandt had a knack for seeing things others missed. With Leo so confident, Shane didn't have much of a leg to stand on. He backed down, but the doubt was still etched on his face.
[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDUP]
Troy couldn't have cared less about Zane's reputation.
"Ha! The kid's weak, but at least he's got guts!" Troy bellowed. "Alright, you heard him! Let's clear this floor! Move the damn tables!"
[AMBIENT: BUSY TAVERN]
[SFX: FURNITURE MOVING]
A chorus of shuffling boots and scraping chairs filled the bar as soldiers scrambled for a better view. Brawls were common enough at Dragon's Keep that the bartenders, seeing what was about to go down, efficiently cleared the remaining furniture out of the way.
In moments, a wide-open space was cleared in the center of the room. Troy planted himself in the middle of it, a cocky grin spreading across his face as he beckoned to Zane. "Alright, kid. Let's dance!"
[MUSIC: SLOW BUILD]
Zane's expression shifted. The easy calm in his eyes hardened into something else — sharp, focused, cutting straight through Troy's bravado.
He took his first step forward. Then his second.
With each step, something changed. The air around him seemed to thicken. By the fourth step, the soldiers nearest to him had unconsciously leaned back. By the seventh, the entire tavern had gone quiet. The laughing had stopped. The muttering had stopped. Even Troy had stopped grinning.
The pressure Zane was giving off had nothing to do with his cultivation level. It came from somewhere deeper — from a soul that had been tempered and refined far beyond what his rank suggested. It pressed down on the room like a physical weight.
Even Leo's eyes had widened slightly.
Troy gritted his teeth. He told himself it was a trick. A Marrow Refining Level kid couldn't actually be stronger than him. 
Troy loaded everything into his fist and swung.
[MUSIC: TENSE FIGHTING]
[SFX: STRUGGLE SOUNDS]
Troy's face and neck flushed beet red. He was trapped—couldn't push forward, couldn't pull back.
He was being completely overpowered by a supposedly weaker fighter.
A wave of stunned silence rolled through the bar. As his army buddies, they all knew how strong Troy was. They'd never imagined that Zane, a kid at the Marrow Refining Level, could catch Troy's fist with one hand and just... stop him cold.
That was no small feat. Even Shane, who'd reached the Nature Level, wasn't sure he could pull that off. It meant Zane was, impossibly, stronger than Troy.
What none of them knew was that Zane had awakened four dragon scales, giving him power that rivaled a strong fighter at the Nature Level.
[SFX: POWERFUL SHOVE]
Seeing that Troy couldn't break his grip, Zane took a deep breath, then threw his entire body into a shove that sent Troy flying.
Troy's eyes widened in a mix of shock and fear as he felt Zane's power surge through his arm. Before he could even process what was happening, he was launched backward.
[SFX: FURNITURE SMASHING]
He smashed through a row of tables and chairs, the furniture exploding into splinters around him, before crashing into a heavy wooden cabinet. The cabinet splintered against the wall, and Troy crumpled to the floor in a motionless heap.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
You could have heard a pin drop in the tavern.
Fights between the Imperial Army and the Skyreach Sect were common. And while the Sect disciples were highly skilled, the Imperial soldiers were hardened veterans who had learned brutal, practical skills on the battlefield. It usually evened the odds.
But now, one of their own had just been utterly humiliated by a young trainee barely at the Marrow Refining Level.
A wave of secondhand embarrassment washed over the soldiers.
[MUSIC: SOLEMN AFTERMATH]
Normally, a fight like this would have erupted into a full-on tavern brawl. But tonight, everyone knew Troy had deliberately picked this fight. What's more, he was a seasoned soldier who had provoked a junior trainee. It made them hesitate.
The Imperial Army soldiers might not be the most polished guys, but they weren't stupid. Troy had picked this fight, and he'd made a complete ass of himself. He couldn't blame some trainee for his own bad luck.
At that moment, even Shane felt a prickle of embarrassment. Zane was here on his invitation, which put Shane in a tough spot. On one hand, he couldn't exactly chew Zane out when Troy was the one who started it all just to show off. On the other, Zane's display of strength had been unreal. He'd completely owned him.
Looking at Troy's motionless form, Shane felt a flash of resentment. They were both Imperial Army. Troy's foolishness was a black eye on them all.
"Troy got cocky after a few drinks," Shane thought. "The Skyreach Sect is the holy grail of combat. Their disciples are the real deal. Troy decided to make a fool of himself, and he got exactly what he deserved."
Shane's only move was to smooth things over. "Alright, fight's over," he announced, his voice cutting through the silence. "We've all got work in the morning. Let's call it a night."
It was a weak excuse, but it gave the soldiers—and Troy—an out.
[AMBIENT: OUTSIDE THE TAVERN]
Outside, Shane pulled Zane, Leo, Max and Rex aside and told them what was behind it.
Some time back, Troy had led his squad on a mission alongside a group of Skyreach Sect disciples. They got hit by a Razorwing swarm mid-operation. The disciples, strong enough to cut their own way out, did exactly that — and left Troy's squad behind. By the time the squad fought their way free on their own, dozens of Troy's men were gone. Eaten.
Shane shook his head. "Brothers who went through fire and water with you, dying like that in front of your eyes. That kind of thing stays with you."
Zane was quiet for a moment. "I understand. He's not a bad person."
Shane nodded. "Glad you see it that way. Get some rest — there'll be a mission in the morning."

Though incredibly powerful, they were also famously wild and short-tempered, liable to kill someone on a whim.
Remembering his promise to Felix, Liam swallowed his irritation. He explained patiently, "Dragon's Keep is Imperial Army territory. Do you really think you can just kill someone in their house and walk away?"
