Ep 622 - Absolute Madness
Word count: 1035
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
This was the first time Zane had faced a second-level War Lord since becoming a War General. Even so, nobody on his side seemed all that worried. Cheryl and the rest of the Demon Night members, along with Olivia and the other humans, were watching with an air of relaxed confidence.
After all, they’d all seen him take down Boris back when he was just a level-ten War Fighter. Boris hadn’t just been some run-of-the-mill opponent; he was a level-ten War General, the most gifted prodigy to come out of the Ogre sacred lands in a generation. Most people agreed that even an early-stage War Lord wouldn’t have stood a chance against him.
[SFX: METAL SCRAPING]
[MUSIC: BATTLE DRUMS]
The Orc War Lord across from him, however, was clearly out of patience. His words died in a grunt as he yanked two massive battle-axes from his back and barreled straight toward Zane.
Orcs were a stout race, and this one barely came up to Zane’s chest, but they compensated for their size in other ways. They were built like brick walls, and their Blood Energy was far more potent than that of other races, granting them a healing factor that made even fatal wounds a temporary inconvenience.
As the squat warrior charged, Zane’s expression didn’t so much as flicker.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
He simply reached into his Storage Ring and drew a longsword.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]
A ripple of murmurs went through the crowd as soon as the blade appeared.
Watching from the sidelines, Amber leaned toward Lavender. "That sword and the one I carry are the only two top-grade relics left in the Demon Night," she said quietly.
Lavender's eyes went wide.
Amber didn't sugarcoat the rest. "At this point, the only ones in this Tower who can probably keep Zane in check are the absolute top-tier War Lords."
[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]
It was true. Zane had earned a reputation among the creatures in the Tower of Sin, especially for a first-level War General. A big part of why he could even consider standing up to a second-level War Lord was because this particular opponent was an independent, not a warrior from the Orcs' sacred place.
[AMBIENT: DISTANT BATTLE SOUNDS]
In the Heartlands, independents often surpassed sect-raised cultivators who relied on inherited techniques. But here on the main continent, the sacred places' legacies proved far more valuable. Every warrior had climbed the Tower by battling countless opponents from every race, giving even sacred place geniuses superior combat experience that placed them above independents.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
That's why this second-level War Lord proved easier to fight than Boris.
Still, Zane remained realistic. Against a sacred place War Lord, he could handle second-level, perhaps third-level at a stretch. Fourth-level or higher? He'd have to flee.
This was precisely why he'd rejected the eleventh floor. Better points, yes, but crawling with unbeatable War Lords. The marginal increase in Lucky Light wasn't worth the risk. Smarter to stay on the tenth floor and gather light steadily.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA CROWD]
As the battle began, the Orc War Lord caught snippets of the crowd’s chatter, and his disbelief curdled into rage. They were all convinced that he, a War Lord, couldn’t handle a piddly first-level War General. Orcs were notoriously hot-headed, and being underestimated was a fatal insult.
[SFX: AXETHROW]
He hurled one of his axes in a wide arc. It spun through the air and curved around behind Zane, slicing in from his blind side. Simultaneously, the War Lord charged head-on with the second axe raised.
Zane didn't panic. Without turning, he launched into a clean somersault, and the spinning axe shrieked past beneath him. As he came down, he snapped a hard kick into the flat of the blade, sending it rocketing back toward the Orc at twice the speed.
The War Lord just grinned, snatching his own axe out of the air with a casual flip of his wrist. Before Zane's feet touched the ground, the Orc erupted with a second surge of Blood Energy and closed the distance in an instant.
"Savage Slash!"
[SFX: RAPID AXE STRIKES]
Golden light blazed from the battle-axe as he unleashed a relentless flurry — dozens of strikes, each one fast enough to blur. When the War Lord finally stopped, chest heaving, he was certain nothing could have survived that.
Then he saw Zane standing a few meters away, completely unharmed, watching him with a faint smile.
[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]
"That's the Spatial Law," someone in the crowd breathed. "He's moving through space itself. No movement technique can touch that."
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]
The insult hit its mark. In a flash, the Orc’s eyes flared a venomous red. A brutal, crimson energy began to bleed from his fists and wrap around his battle-axes.
A warrior in the crowd gasped and pointed at the Orc.“He’s going into Absolute Madness!”
"Once the Orcs go Absolute Madness, they won't stop until their target is dead — or they are," someone added. "And when their brains cool down, if they ever do — they'd be as dangerous as the Ogres."
Watching the War Lord transform, Olivia wondered what Zane would do. She'd defeated three tenth-level War Generals—not through strength, but by dodging and exhausting them. Even in Absolute Madness, they never touched her.
But facing one head-on? She couldn't beat even a seventh-level opponent, much less a tenth.
Zane's expression turned serious. However weak this particular War Lord was by War Lord standards, the gap between a War General and a War Lord was real. He couldn't afford to ignore it.
[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]
[SFX: SPINNING ENERGY]
The War Lord came again — not charging this time but spinning, torquing his body into a rapid rotation. The two red lights on his arms blurred into a single blazing wheel, the axe-light weaving through it in brilliant arcs.
"Universe Rotation!"
Anything caught in that spin would be torn apart.
[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]
Zane, however, wasn’t dodging. Despite the odds, his expression was locked in determination.
He was planting his feet, ready to meet this berserker head-on with nothing but his top-grade sword.
