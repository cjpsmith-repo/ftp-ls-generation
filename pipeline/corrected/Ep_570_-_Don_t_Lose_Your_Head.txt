Ep 570 - Don't Lose Your Head
Word count: 1075
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Zane stared at the arena floor - just seconds ago, it had been covered in blood. And now the blood was gone. Confusion was written all over his face.
Phoebe could tell exactly what he was thinking and offered a smile. "The Tower of Sin is a... strange place," she began. "It's almost alive. It absorbs everything inside it."
Zane's brow furrowed even deeper. "Absorbs everything?"
Phoebe nodded. "Blood, discarded junk, anything that isn't alive gets absorbed by the tower. If you leave a weapon on the floor for too long, it'll take that, too."
"Wait, it can even absorb weapons?" Zane asked, his shock obvious.
“That's right,” Phoebe said. "Any lifeless object will be absorbed if it's left on the floor for a while. For example, if someone died and no one claimed the body, the tower would eventually absorb it."
[SFX: ENERGY BEAM]
Zane was floored. But before he could respond, a milky white light suddenly coalesced in the arena, shooting straight into the token hanging from the Ogre's chest.
Even though Zane didn't know what the light was, he could feel the incredibly pure energy radiating from it. A primal instinct flared inside him, a desperate urge to grab that energy and make it his own.
"What is that?" he asked.
"A reward from the Tower of Sin. It's called Lucky Light," Phoebe explained patiently. "You get some whenever you beat an opponent in the arena. The stronger they are, the more Lucky Light you get. The running theory on the main continent is that the tower turns everything it absorbs into that stuff."
"Lucky Light!"
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
Zane had seen Lucky Light before. But the Lucky Light here was different. This light didn't flow into your body directly—it flowed into your token.
The Cobalt Dragon chuckled inside Zane's mind. Looks the same as the light from the Dragon Soar Arena, doesn't it? But that light boosted your power directly. This stuff has to be refined before you can use it.
It all clicked for Zane now. He finally understood why the creatures of the main continent valued the Tower of Sin so much.
Training in the Dragon Soar Arena was one thing, but it took ages for the arena to build up its Lucky Light. Zane, being the first to enter, had taken nearly all of it, leaving little for anyone who came after. But the Tower of Sin was a different story entirely. Defeat an opponent, get Lucky Light. As long as you kept winning, you kept growing.
And that was why no single race had ever managed to take control of it. The curse kept War Sages out, which meant no race could ever send its heaviest hitters in to tip the balance. The Tower stayed contested. The continent stayed chaotic. And the cycle kept spinning.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
In the arena, Borgan the Ogre hadn't finished. He paced the platform, still holding the Orc's severed head, scanning the crowd.
"Six wins today!" he bellowed. "Who else? Anyone? Or have you all decided to just watch?"
Then Borgan's eyes landed on Zane. "A human War Fighter? Since when do humans send War Fighters into the Tower? Don't you cowards usually wait until you're at least a War General before you dare show your faces?"
The crowd shifted. Every set of eyes in the arena found Zane.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]
Zane hadn't planned to fight today. He'd only just arrived — he wanted to read the room, understand the Tower before throwing himself into it. But here he was, already targeted.
Phoebe glanced at him, still wearing her calm smile. Her job was to watch him until Elder May arrived. She'd just watched this same human outrun three Ogre War Lords for over a thousand miles. She wasn't worried.
"Looks like he's got his eye on you," she said.
"So it seems." Zane loosened his wrists and stepped into the arena.
Borgan licked his lips. "I'm surprised you had the guts. Let me guess — you thought one win would make you brave?"
Zane didn't say a word.
Borgan didn't like that. Something about that gaze — like a man who'd spent a long time looking down from a great height — got under his skin. He didn't know why. He just knew he wanted to rip it off Zane's face.
"Die, human! Let me hear you scream!" Borgan roared, his forceful energy detonating as he charged.
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD TENSION]
As Borgan charged, Zane's hand shot out like a viper, his finger tapping against the Ogre's forehead.
[SFX: CRACK]
A sickening crack echoed through the arena. Zane sidestepped, a single, fluid motion that easily evaded Borgan's collapsing frame.
In front of the silent crowd, Borgan's head exploded in a spray of red mist.
[SFX: BODY IMPACT]
The headless body stumbled forward a few more steps before crashing to the floor.
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED SILENCE]
The chatter died. A stunned silence fell over the arena.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]
Brutality like that was nothing new in the Tower of Sin. It was common in duels where the power gap was just too wide.
The creatures weren't surprised by how Zane had killed Borgan. They were shocked that he was human.
Human War Fighters were notoriously weak compared to the other races on the main continent. For one to even enter the Tower of Sin was basically a death sentence. It was why the Violet Heart Sanctuary almost exclusively sent War Generals.
And yet, this level-seven human War Fighter had just obliterated an Ogre with a single finger.
[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]
The dead silence finally broke as hushed whispers rippled through the crowd.
[SFX: MAGICAL LIGHT WHOOSH]
The Ogre's blood slowly soaked into the arena floor. Another milky white light appeared, this one shooting directly into Zane's token.
As the Lucky Light flowed into it, the simple metal token now felt heavy in his hand.
Vesper was right, Zane thought, standing alone in the center of the arena. This place is a gold mine for getting stronger fast. But getting to the bottom of this tower… that's going to be a whole other story.
[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
Just as he turned to step out of the arena, another burly figure climbed in.
"Hey, kid. You think you can just take your win and walk away?"
