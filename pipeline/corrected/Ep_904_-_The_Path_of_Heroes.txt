Ep 904 - The Path of Heroes

Word count: 1054

[AMBIENT: EERIE WIND]

[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]

The Emperor’s Isle was less a home for the living and more a massive cemetery for the dead. 

Tombstones lined both sides of the road, a testament to the legendary warriors of the Dragon-Blooded—a clan so powerful and exclusive that only its core members, those who bore the ‘Shaw’ surname, were granted burial on the revered platforms above. The rest, even those who had achieved the Soul Sea Realm, were buried here in the dirt. Warriors who didn’t share the family name were considered lesser, their legacies reduced to a simple grave, their bodies left to decay. The only things setting them apart were the golden dragon-head tombstones that you could spot from a distance.

A somber mood settled over the Soul Sea Realm warriors as they passed the graves. This was their future, too. 

Without a path to the upper world, they could never ascend beyond their current limits. Time would eventually claim them, and for all the grand funerals and ornate coffins, they would be just as forgotten as the names on these stones. In the end, the life of a warrior in this universe was a desperate race against that final, unavoidable fate.

[AMBIENT: CRASHING WAVES]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Beyond the cemetery, a dark forest gave way to the edge of the isle, which narrowed until it met a long, thin stretch of sea. A road, maybe twenty feet across, sliced right through the water, a brown ribbon dividing the churning golden expanse.

While a priest’s ceremony had weakened the blood sacrifice’s power in the outer Sacred Feather Sea, here, at the approach to the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, it was raw and overwhelming. 

[SFX: VIOLENT WATER CRASH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

The sea itself shimmered like molten gold, its waves crashing violently against the narrow road. The sacrifice’s energy was so potent that it would incinerate any of the Soul Sea Realm warriors who dared to pass through it. It had no effect on the Dragon-Blooded, however, which was precisely why these warriors had gone to the trouble of possessing their corpses. It was their only ticket inside.

The towering War King Wind stopped at the foot of the causeway. Callan fell in beside him.

“Father,” Callan asked, “what exactly are we walking into out there?”

War King Wind glanced at his son. “You remember the Slow Arrow Area in the Feather Emperor's Secret Land?”

The juniors exchanged uneasy looks. They remembered it well enough — the slow-drifting blue shafts, the Dragon Arsenic, the near-misses. Zane's Lodestone had been the only reason any of them made it through.

“This is the same principle,” War King Wind said. “Except the arrows here don't move slowly.”

Callan paused to take in this new information.

“The four kingdoms didn't figure out how to cross this path cheaply,” War King Wind continued. “It cost more than ten Soul Sea masters to learn what we know now. Every step forward was paid for in blood.”

[AMBIENT: WAVES]

“This is the Path of Heroes,” he announced, his voice booming over the waves.

“The Heavenly Stellar Secret Land is at the other end.”

Scott spoke up. “Bring them out.”

At his word, four Soul Sea masters from his kingdom reached into their Storage Rings and hauled out a set of enormous shields — each one a full six meters across. They drove them into the ground one by one. Zane counted more than ten in total.

He studied them. The surface of each shield gave off a faint seven-colored sheen, and he could feel the Five Elements radiating from within.

“Are those made from Lodestone?” he asked.

Scott's eyes flickered with a trace of pride. “You can't process Lodestone — not directly. But when the mountain fell, it shook the entire world on its way down. Fragments broke off. The Black Iron Divine Kingdom collected most of them.” He nodded at the shields. “That's what you're looking at.”

Zane looked at them again with new eyes. The kingdoms had spent generations finding ways into this place.

War King Wind turned to the younger warriors. “Stay behind us. The Fast Arrow Zone is far worse than anything you faced in the Feather Emperor's Secret Land. Keep close and don't move out of formation.”

The juniors nodded, their nerves plain on their faces.

The Soul Sea masters fitted the shields together. Delicate buckles ran along each edge — the moment two shields touched, they locked. Within minutes, the group was enclosed in a long, airtight box of Lodestone-reinforced steel, eight shield-bearers holding the formation in lockstep.

[AMBIENT: OCEAN WAVES]

[SFX: STONE GRINDING]

They were barely a hundred feet down the path when the roar of the waves intensified. Dragon statues began to rise from the golden sea, each with a blue arrow clenched in its stone jaws.

War King Wind didn't even flinch. “They're coming,” he said, his voice a low rumble. “Stay sharp. Shields ready.”

The eight mighty warriors from the Soul Sea Realm tensed, gripping their huge shields even tighter.

[SFX: SHARP CRACK]

They had barely taken another step on the path when a sharp crack split the air.

[SFX: ARROW WHOOSH]

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: OCEAN WAVES]

A flash of blue light shot across the sea’s surface, leaving a shimmering ripple as it sped toward their defensive formation.

The sheer speed of the arrow stunned Zane. This area was clearly far more dangerous than the last. An arrow like that would tear a hole straight through a Soul Sea Realm warrior, and he was sure they were tipped with Dragon Arsenic. Worse, now that he was stuck in this girl's body, he'd never survive a direct hit. His own body was as durable as a Relic-Class weapon; this one felt as fragile as glass.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT]

The arrow crossed a thousand feet in the blink of an eye, slamming into the giant shield on their left with an explosive boom. The force was incredible. The entire shield formation tilted precariously to the right, nearly sending them all tumbling into the churning gold below.

“Hold steady!” War King Wind roared. 

[SFX: METAL SCRAPING]

He slammed a massive hand against the shield beside him, single-handedly shoving their entire formation back onto the center of the path.

And that was only the first arrow.
