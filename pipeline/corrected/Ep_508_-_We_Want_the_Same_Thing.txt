[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: DARK RISING FURY]
If anyone in the crowd understood Owen's pain, it was Zane. After all, they were hunting the same prey: Marilyn.
Watching Owen's brutal defeat churned a storm inside him—a tempest of fury and recognition. But it didn't shake his resolve to kill Marilyn. If anything, the sight of Owen's broken body cemented his determination.
He squeezed his eyes shut, forcing himself to look away from her, afraid the murderous rage burning through his veins blazed too bright to hide. Every fiber of his being screamed to act now and end her where she stood. But that path led only to his own destruction.
Not yet, he told himself. Not here. Not like this. He couldn't afford to let rage dictate his actions, no matter how righteous that rage might be. He would kill Marilyn on his terms, not hers.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]
Tia sat in silence behind him. She thought about reaching out with her Vitality, but let out a quiet sigh instead. She knew there was no talking Zane out of this. Besides, he’d already proven he could hold his own against a warrior from the Inner-Core Realm. Zane always had another trick up his sleeve; you never knew what he’d pull to get the win.
But the second Zane made a move on Marilyn, Maddox would step in. He wouldn’t just stand by and watch.
The three leaders of the World Merchants' Guild were completely in the dark about Zane’s plans, so there was no stopping what was coming. The three of them wouldn’t stand a chance against an enraged Maddox.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Zane was walking into an impossible fight.
As the realization settled, Tia bit her lip and stared at his back. A flicker of determination hardened her gaze; her mind was made up.
[MUSIC: SINISTER STRINGS]
Down on the arena floor, Marilyn looked down at Owen, who was pinned and powerless. “I’m not going to kill you,” she said, her voice a soft poison. “But I will make your life a living hell. In fact, the Soul Harvesting Ritual is perfect for that girlfriend of yours. Once her six senses are sealed away, she’ll ascend to the Inner-Core Realm. And when she offers her Pristine Essence to the ritual, she’ll absorb Maddox’s Vital Essence and rise all the way to the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. It’s an exquisite way to awaken desire… to let someone taste every pleasure this world has to offer.”
[SFX: DARK ENERGY RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
At her words, Owen began to tremble, the black thorns and crimson flowers quivering with the force of his rage.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL]
He stared up at Marilyn, his eyes hollow. His bloody lips parted as he rasped out a reply.
“What was that?” Marilyn asked, taking a step closer.
[SFX: BLOOD COUGH]
Owen coughed, blood spattering from his lips as he fought to get the words out. “I said… you’re nothing but a dried-up old hag.”
Fury twisted Marilyn’s face.
[SFX: ENERGY BLADE WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC]
She raised her hand, her fingers sharpening into a blade of pure energy. She swung it down toward Owen’s head, only to freeze with the edge hovering inches from his skin. From the corner of his eye, he could see it shimmering. “You’re trying to bait me into killing you.”
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACH]
A second figure knelt beside him. Zane.
Owen's eyes widened in disbelief. He didn't know this man—not really. He'd traveled thousands of miles from the Fleecy Downs for this tournament, and besides his girlfriend, he didn't know a soul in Oracle City.
The only memory he had of Zane was bitter: the qualifiers, where this stranger had blackmailed him out of a fortune in supreme Aether crystals. Owen had replayed that humiliation countless times, the sting of it still fresh. So what the hell was Zane doing here now, kneeling in the dirt beside him?
"This is a Grade-six Elixir," Zane whispered. His voice carried an unexpected gentleness. "It'll save your life."
Owen stared at the precious elixir, its luminous contents swirling with concentrated power. A lifeline from the last person he'd expected.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]
Zane had gotten the elixir from Tia. He had no real connection to Owen, and the man’s death meant nothing to him. But seeing that same desperate fire in Owen’s eyes, that same willingness to die for his goal, Zane knew he had to help.
Tia didn’t have many Grade-six Elixirs. They were priceless healing remedies from the Cloud Hall.
"Why?" Owen rasped, suspicion thickening his voice.
[SFX: AETHER SURGE]
[SFX: HEALING ENERGY]
Zane enveloped the elixir in his Vitality. With a gentle squeeze, the golden sphere cracked. He flicked his finger, and the pill dissolved into a fine powder that drifted into Owen’s mouth.
The question hung between them. Other than Tia and her master, no one knew the real answer. Zane hesitated, weighing his words carefully. Finally, he chose to reply with his Vitality, sending a silent thought directly into Owen's mind.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WHISPER]
"Because we want the same thing."
Owen's eyes had been dull and lifeless moments before, resigned to the certainty that his fight had ended. But as Zane's words echoed through his consciousness, something shifted. A spark ignited in their depths—fragile at first, then blazing brighter. His mouth fell open, breath catching in his throat. "You… you're…"
Unlike Zane, Owen had prepared meticulously before setting foot in Oracle City. He hadn't merely researched Marilyn; he'd delved deep into the history of the other woman blessed with the Violet-Core Physique. The name surfaced now from the depths of his memory like a stone breaking through water: Elara Lowell.
Zane's surname was Lowell.
The pieces fell into place with devastating clarity. Owen's chest tightened as understanding washed over him. Here was another soul trapped in the same desperate struggle, fighting against impossible odds for someone precious to them.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REALIZATION]
He finally understood exactly who Zane was, and why they stood together in this moment.
