Ep 131 - A Criminal
Word count: 1023
[AMBIENT: BATHROOM INTERIOR]
[SFX: WATER SPLASHING]
Yolande discarded her skirt atop the box and eased herself into the barrel, leaving only her slender shoulders and back visible to Zane. As his gaze involuntarily traced the elegant curve of her spine, he felt a sudden jolt of astonishment. The reality before him shattered his preconceptions entirely.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REVELATION]
Her skin, far from the flawless expanse he had unconsciously anticipated, bore a tapestry of violence. Countless scars crisscrossed her back—some thin and precise as if carved by blades, others broad and ragged, telling tales of whips and worse. The marks overlapped and intertwined, creating a chaotic testament to suffering that made Zane's breath catch in his throat.
What struck him even more than the scars themselves was their deliberate preservation. With her formidable power and elevated position, erasing such blemishes would have been trivial. A single Beauty Potion—coveted luxuries among the women of The Capital—would have restored her skin to pristine condition. While such elixirs remained beyond the reach of common folk due to their exorbitant cost, for someone of Yolande's standing, such an expense would have been utterly insignificant.
Zane pondered whether she deliberately preserved those scars. What trauma had she endured that made her so indifferent toward her physical appearance? Perhaps the marks served as reminders of battles won or lessons learned—testimonies etched into flesh that beauty potions could never replace.
[SFX: WATER SPLASH]
As these questions swirled through his mind, the tranquil silence was shattered with a sudden splash of water. Yolande bolted upright, her head pivoting sharply in his direction. Her keen eyes located him almost instantly. With a deep frown creasing her otherwise composed features, she flicked her wrist with practiced precision. Her vibrant, multi-colored dress soared through the air like an obedient servant, draping itself completely over the box and plunging Zane into abrupt darkness.
[SFX: FABRIC RUSTLING]
Through the fabric barrier, he detected the soft rustle of clothing as she dressed herself with swift efficiency. The sounds of movement ceased, followed by a moment of unnerving stillness. Then, without warning, the makeshift curtain was yanked away, revealing Yolande standing before him, fully clothed and radiating silent fury.
She was now in a loose white robe, its long sleeves swallowing her arms and leaving just the tips of her slender fingers visible. The garment highlighted her slim figure, making her look surprisingly… cute. But the illusion shattered the second he saw her cold expression. Cute wasn't the word. Not even close.
[SFX: BOX DRAGGING THUDS]
She dragged the box out of the bathroom with a series of jarring thuds.
From what Zane could gather, Yolande had no interests outside of combat.
[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]
Once they reached her room, she shoved the box into a corner and promptly forgot he existed. She sat on a cushion, closed her eyes, and began to meditate.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSE]
Zane's mind raced frantically as panic seized him. If she genuinely intended to abandon him in this box until the military office reopened, his situation was dire. He needed to devise an escape plan immediately before his window of opportunity vanished completely.
After mentally cycling through his limited options, Zane inhaled deeply to steady himself and shouted, "Hey!" His voice reverberated through the silent chamber with startling intensity, bouncing off the walls and amplifying his desperation.
Yet Yolande remained eerily motionless, her disciplined body betraying not even the slightest acknowledgment of his outburst. Her meditative trance seemed impenetrable, her face a mask of serene concentration despite the disruption.
"Hey!" he attempted once more, injecting greater urgency into his voice while studying her face for any flicker of response.
But Yolande Shaw had transformed herself into a living sculpture, completely immersed in her meditative practice. She had evidently mastered the art of mental isolation, rendering herself deaf to external disturbances. The world beyond her consciousness might as well have ceased to exist, leaving Zane to shout futilely into a void of her making.
"If you keep ignoring me, I'm leaving!" Zane threatened, trying to sound convincing.
When Yolande didn't even flinch, he repeated himself and started dragging his feet noisily toward the door. As he passed her, he saw her eyes were still closed, but he caught the barest flicker of a flare in her nostrils.
[AMBIENT: BUSY CONFERENCE]
[SFX: DOOR OPENING]
"Yolande, what's with the guy?" Axel, the imperial army's field commander, asked, his surprise obvious. The man was in the white robes of a Skyreach Sect Outer Disciple. Yolande pulled some weird stunts, but bringing a High-level candidate to a critical military conference was a new level of bizarre.
"He's a criminal," Yolande replied coldly, walking to her seat. "He committed a serious crime. I'm taking him to the military office as soon as it reopens."
"A criminal? What'd he do?" Axel pressed, his curiosity piqued.
At that, Hugh—one of the top warriors present—started to panic. The humiliation of everyone finding out that he, a master from the Soul Realm, had nearly been killed by a mere candidate would be unbearable. He shot Yolande a frantic look, winking desperately to signal her to keep her mouth shut.
But Yolande was completely straightforward, with zero grasp of subtlety. She looked right at him and asked flatly, "Hugh, why are you winking at me?"
The other top warriors froze, their gazes flicking between a mortified Hugh and a clueless Yolande.
Lennie knew exactly what Hugh was trying to do, and he knew there wasn't a thing he could do to help. Nobody, not even Axel, could stop Yolande once she set her mind to something. Seeing the sheer terror on Hugh's face, Lennie had to physically choke back a laugh. He was howling on the inside.
"It's nothing," Hugh muttered, flicking his hand dismissively while inwardly cursing his own foolishness. Of course Yolande wouldn't grasp his subtle cues—her literal-mindedness was legendary among the Soul Realm elite.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
Yolande's perplexed gaze lingered on him momentarily. "Okay," she said, abruptly pivoting toward Axel. "This man murdered Josef today. And he nearly defeated Hugh in combat."
[SFX: SHOCKED GASPS]
"What?"
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
The single word reverberated through the chamber, hanging in the air like a thunderclap as every face turned first to Zane, then to Hugh, their expressions transforming from disbelief to shock to horrified understanding.
