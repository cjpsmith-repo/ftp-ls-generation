The grandstand fell silent, then erupted in shocked whispers when Zane challenged Eden.

Zane's first two battles had been nothing short of extraordinary. His Body Refining technique, his mastery of the Fire Law—he'd left everyone astounded with skills that defied conventional understanding. Spectators leaned forward in their seats, exchanging glances of disbelief.

It seemed impossible to accept that someone from the fourth level of the Soul Realm could wield such devastating power. The natural order of strength, the hierarchy everyone took for granted, appeared to crumble before their eyes.

But the evidence was undeniable. He hadn't just defeated Alex from House Gresham—a fighter whose reputation had been built over years of victories. He'd also taken down Brody from Bloodwood Cliff, a cultivator known for his vicious techniques and merciless fighting style. Zane hadn't lost a single exchange. The crowd understood this wasn't mere fortune or a fortunate streak. Through raw skill and unwavering determination, Zane had proven himself capable of standing toe-to-toe with fighters from the Inner-Core Realm.

Even the skeptics, those who'd initially dismissed Zane as an upstart from the Eastern Territories, found themselves forced to acknowledge what their own eyes had shown them.

Whether they liked it or not, the truth stared them in the face, undeniable and absolute.

Zane was the real deal.

When Zane's challenge rang out, the eyes of the Cloud Hall disciples blazed with renewed hope.

Benson had been their strongest Inner-Core disciple, but Eden had torn through his defenses like parchment, inflicting devastating injuries within the first exchange. The brutality of it still haunted them—how Benson's desperate surrender had been the only thing standing between him and death. Every Cloud Hall disciple who witnessed that humiliation felt their blood simmer with impotent rage.

But fury alone changed nothing. The martial world operated on an ancient, merciless principle: strength commanded respect, while weakness invited destruction. Those who couldn't accept this truth rarely survived long enough to learn it.

Still, something about Zane's defiant challenge stirred a dangerous ember of possibility in their hearts. Perhaps this upstart Soul Realm cultivator possessed something Benson lacked—some hidden edge that could restore their honor.

Can Zane actually go up against Eden and win?

Tia glanced over at Zane. After a beat, she asked, her voice hesitant, "Are you… sure you can beat him?"

Zane turned to her, a confident smile spreading across his face. "Why wouldn't I be?"

That smile sent a warmth through Tia's chest, and her pulse quickened. The confidence radiating off him made him even more handsome. Even facing an opponent as powerful as Eden, Zane didn't show a hint of fear.

As Zane started to leave the high platform, Tia added quietly, "Maybe you can take out Marilyn. If Maddox makes a move, I'll do my best to help you."

Zane offered a faint smile, ignoring the crowd's chatter. A wind began to whip up around him.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

Golden light began to swirl around Zane, merging with the rising wind. Each blade that formed fused seamlessly with a streak of radiance, the two elements intertwining like threads in a tapestry.

Within moments, golden wind blades danced in a lethal orbit around him. They appeared denser, sharper, more devastating than Eden's had been. The golden light radiating from them blazed far brighter than Eden's silver glow, casting shifting shadows across the arena floor.

Just like you said,* Zane thought, his consciousness reaching toward the Cobalt Dragon. *I can do it too. This isn't a true fusion of Laws—not yet. It's more like embedding one power into another, layering them together. But the combination…* He felt the energy thrumming through his body, wild and potent. *It feels so much stronger.

The Cobalt Dragon's presence stirred in his mind, a note of satisfaction coloring its ancient voice. Zane allowed himself a moment of quiet pride before refocusing on the battle ahead.

Most warriors in the stands stared in bewilderment, their faces twisted in confusion.

"Doesn't Zane use fire techniques?" someone called out, voice cracking with disbelief.

"Can someone please explain what's happening?" another demanded. "His wind blades look just like Eden's. Is he just copying him?"

Then a trembling voice cut through the confusion—an older cultivator, his face pale with shock. "I can hardly believe what I'm seeing… but that's… that's the complete power of the Wind Law. And the full power of the Metal Law!"

The words dropped like stones into still water, and silence rippled through the entire crowd.

The complete Fire Law. The complete Wind Law. And now the complete Metal Law.

Three fundamental forces, each mastered to perfection. It was impossible. And yet, it was happening.

"Can someone please tell me how he mastered so much Law Power?" The question came out half-strangled, desperate. "Not just mastered—they're all complete! That should take decades—lifetimes even!"

No one answered. No one could. They simply stared at the seventeen-year-old standing in the center of the arena, surrounded by his impossible power, and felt the foundations of their understanding crack beneath them.
