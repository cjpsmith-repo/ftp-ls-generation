Ep 699 - A Mid-Air Trap

Word count: 1067

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

As Maddox closed in, Lavender was already moving — spear leveled, crimson light trailing behind her as she drove straight for him.

[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSH]

She spun on him, spear in hand, and lunged in a silver flash.

Maddox’s breath caught. A mid-grade Relic-Class weapon!

He felt a burn of pure envy. There were only a handful of low-grade Relics in the entire Heartlands, and here was Zane’s sword spirit wielding a mid-grade one. But the shock quickly gave way to confusion. Wasn’t Zane’s spirit supposed to use a giant sword? Where did a spear come from?

The kid’s luck was just unbelievable. First, he breaks through to the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, and now he pulls a weapon like this out of thin air.

Maddox sneered, his voice dripping with avarice. “Nice spear. Too bad it’s going to be mine soon enough. All your luck, all your little secrets—they’ll belong to me.”

He was certain Zane was hiding something huge, a key that could elevate him to the status of an emperor, or maybe even something greater. His ambition had never been limited to the Heartlands. Beyond the Chaotic Ocean of Stars lay a union of seven divine kingdoms, and Maddox planned to conquer them all. He’d spent his entire life clawing his way up, and his goal was to stand at the very pinnacle of existence.

Defeating Zane was simply the next step.

Lavender’s spear tip was inches from his face, but Maddox didn’t even flinch.

[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]

He threw a punch, and a beam of brownish-yellow light shot from his fist, aimed straight for Zane.

But Lavender just scoffed. “Break.”

[SFX: ENERGY SHOCKWAVE]

[SFX: SHATTERING]

The spear in her hands hummed, releasing an invisible shockwave of power that erupted outward, shattering Maddox’s attack into dust.

Maddox just stared, his mind reeling. How had Zane’s sword spirit gotten so strong? He knew he hadn’t used his full strength, but for a mere spirit to so easily block his attack… it was impossible.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

A wicked grin spread across Lavender’s face. “Surprised?”

The words hit him harder than the attack. “You can talk?” he blurted out, his jaw slack. A sword spirit that could speak? He’d seen her before, and while her sword dance had been impressive, he’d written her off as nothing special. Now, the spear techniques she displayed were flawless, and her power was astounding.

His expression hardened into something more serious. "Zane — you came back too early. Give it two or three more years, and you might actually have been a problem. As it is…" He smiled. "It's a shame."

[SFX: DEEP RESONANT PULSE]

A crushing aura poured off him. It wasn't directed at anyone in particular — it simply existed, the natural weight of a man who had dominated the Heartlands for decades. Inside the city, warriors felt it in their chests. Mortals crumpled where they stood, vision going dark, and wouldn't stir for hours.

Zane heard the words and felt the truth of them. Maddox wasn't wrong. If he'd waited — returned to the main continent at peak cultivation — this confrontation would look very different. But if he'd waited, Maddox would already have Cora. The formation would be complete. And by the time Zane was ready, Maddox might have broken through to an even higher realm entirely. There was no perfect timing. There was only now.

Lavender's eyes moved to Maddox. "The strongest in the Heartlands," she said, her voice carrying a quiet contempt. "And you're only at the War Sage realm. What a title."

It wasn't a deliberate display of power — just the natural bearing of someone who had ruled for a very long time. And somehow, for just a moment, even Maddox felt it.

His jaw tightened. He threw another punch — full strength this time. The fist light was a deep, earthy yellow, dense with Earth Law, and it hit the air like a falling mountain.

Lavender didn't try to meet it. She dissolved — red specks scattering — and streamed back toward Zane. She had done what she came to do.

Maddox snapped out of his shock, his expression hardening. He realized this wasn't going to be the easy fight he'd expected. He couldn't treat Zane like some rookie who'd just scraped his way into the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. Just like back when he was in the Soul Realm, Zane was once again pulling off the impossible.

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

Lavender reformed beside Zane, spear in hand, and turned her gaze on Cora. The look on her face hadn't changed. She'd disliked this girl from the moment she laid eyes on her — sheltered, stubborn, no sense of the world she was actually living in. Left to her own judgment, she'd have solved the problem much more simply. But she knew Zane, and she knew he wouldn't allow it.

“I thought you weren’t afraid to die,” Lavender’s voice echoed in Cora’s ear. “So why are you clinging to Zane so tightly?”

At first, Cora had been mortified that Zane was carrying her, but as he’d shot into the sky at a terrifying speed, she’d instinctively grabbed his shoulder for dear life. Now, her face burned with shame.

“I…” Cora trailed off, unable to answer the taunt. Then, a spark of her usual stubbornness returned. “Zane, put me down.”

Lavender shot back. “Put you down? So Maddox can snatch you up for his Soul Harvesting Ritual? Don’t get the wrong idea. Elara is a hundred times more important than you are.”

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

“Then just kill me!” Cora cried, the reality of her situation finally crashing down on her. It was either this humiliation or death. There was no other way out.

“Lavender, that’s enough,” Zane said, his voice calm but firm. He could still make his own choices, and he refused to let this innocent girl die.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL ORCHESTRAL]

He circled high above Ethereal City, Cora in his arms, while Maddox closed the distance behind them. Just as he leaped toward a grand, palace-like building, Zane came to an abrupt halt in midair. He turned to face his pursuer, a strange, knowing smile playing on his lips.

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

Maddox immediately went on high alert. The kid wasn’t just going to give up and let himself be captured.

Something felt very wrong.
