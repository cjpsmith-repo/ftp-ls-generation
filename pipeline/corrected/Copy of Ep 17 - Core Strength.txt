His only salvation lay in fighting his way to freedom.

Though Zane moved with impressive speed, Owen glided across the ground like a predatory shadow wreathed in purple light. The seasoned expert, who had mastered the Core Strength Level years ago, intercepted Zane's path with effortless grace, his movements fluid and precise.
"You're in over your head, kid," Owen taunted, his lips curling into a cruel smile that never reached his eyes. "There's no escaping your fate. I'll extend one final offer: surrender your Crafting Formula!" His voice dropped to a menacing whisper. "Refuse, and I'll ensure you experience agony beyond imagination. Your suffering will become legendary before I tear you apart and reduce what remains to ashes."

"Go to hell!" Zane spat, his eyes blazing with the accumulated rage of two years of humiliation. He channeled his fury into a powerful punch aimed at Owen's smug face.
[SFX: RAPID COMBAT MOVEMENTS]
As Zane's fist sliced through the air, Owen sidestepped with contemptuous ease, twisting inside Zane's guard with the practiced efficiency of a veteran fighter. His hands became blurs as he delivered a devastating series of blows to Zane's torso, each impact precise and calculated.
The difference in their abilities was staggering.
A fighter at the Core Strength Level commanded three times the power of even the most accomplished Bone Refiner. Owen's fists carried the weight of mountains as they hammered into Zane's body, flooding his system with overwhelming force.
[SFX: ENERGY STRAIN]
Within seconds, the pain transformed into that familiar rush of warmth. Even though his body was converting the energy at a furious rate, the sheer number of powerful punches overwhelmed his limit.
The excess force that wasn't transformed began tearing him apart.
[SFX: VIOLENT IMPACT]
"Aah!" Zane screamed as he was sent flying. He rolled with the impact and staggered back to his feet, spitting a mouthful of blood.
Seeing Zane get back up after that kind of beating made Owen's eyes widen. "Incredible. That defensive technique of yours is something else. Tell us how you do it, and I might just let you live."
"Why would I believe you? You lie as easily as you breathe. I wouldn't trust a single word out of your mouth," Zane countered, fighting to steady his ragged breath.
[MUSIC: TRANSFORMATIVE REVELATION]
A wave of relief washed over him. It was as if the beatings from Andrew and now Owen had finally flushed the last of the Toxins from his bones.
After months of taking hits, Zane had reached the peak of the Bone Refining Level, but lately, his progress had stalled. He was getting stronger every day, which meant his body demanded more of that warm energy. He knew the beatings from those kids wouldn't be enough for much longer.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
Now, this beating transformed into a torrent of warmth, cascading through his system and purging the final remnants of toxins. The pain dissolved into power, feeding his transformation rather than breaking him.
Every single bone underwent refinement, strengthening, and hardening with each impact. What should have shattered him instead forged him anew, like iron hammered into steel.
He savored the novel sensation—an exhilarating surge of power that connected him intimately to every bone in his body. It felt as though he could command each one at will, directing them with mere thought rather than muscle alone.
As his skeletal structure grew denser and more resilient, his breathing pattern shifted dramatically, becoming deeper and more controlled. He discovered he could draw a single, impossibly long breath that filled him with vitality, holding it while others would have long since gasped for air.
With each measured inhalation, his body purified itself from within, shedding impurities and weakness like autumn trees releasing their withered leaves to the wind. The transformation felt both natural and profound, as though his body had always been destined for this evolution.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT EVOLUTION]
This unmistakable metamorphosis confirmed what he had strived for through countless beatings and endless pain—he had finally transcended the Bone Refining Level and reached the coveted Core Strength Level, a milestone his tormentors never believed he could achieve.
[MUSIC: INTENSE TRANSFORMATION]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
As energy surged through his veins like liquid fire, his heart soared with triumph, yet his face remained an impassive mask, betraying nothing of the transformation occurring within.
"Well, you're quite the resilient specimen, aren't you?" Owen sneered, his voice dripping with condescension. "Let's discover how that stubborn spirit holds up after a few hours of my special questioning techniques."
He advanced toward Zane with predatory confidence, lunging to seize him with outstretched hands.
[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCH]
In that critical moment, Zane's fist launched forward with lightning precision, connecting squarely with Owen's smug face. Having completely misjudged Zane's newfound power, Owen found himself utterly unprepared for the devastating impact.
[SFX: BODY CRASHING AND TUMBLING]
The blow catapulted Owen backward thirty feet through the air, his body crashing against the hard floor and tumbling several times before he managed to scramble upright, his dignity in tatters.
"You... You've reached the Core Strength Level!" Owen gasped between ragged breaths, clutching his chest as crimson rivulets streamed from his mouth.
His face transformed into a portrait of disbelief and dawning horror.
Zane merely curled his lips into a contemptuous sneer, savoring this moment of long-awaited retribution. His gaze, cold and calculating, swept across the other men like a winter wind. "Who else wishes to try their luck against me?" he challenged, standing taller now, his power radiating visibly around him.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
A deathly silence blanketed the yard.
No one moved a muscle. No one dared draw breath.
The unspoken question hung in the air—if Owen couldn't subdue him, who among them could?
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHALLENGER]
From the shadowed corner of the yard, a voice sliced through the silence like a blade. "I dare," it declared with quiet confidence. "And I will."
