Ep 185 - Death Drops
Word Count: 1183
[AMBIENT: TENSE OUTDOOR WIND]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STANDOFF]
A sharp gust of wind sliced through the electric tension hanging between the Adepts. The question in everyone’s minds was the same: did Zane have any idea who he’d just insulted? Did he have a death wish? Or had he failed to grasp exactly how powerful the Seven Great Houses were?
But Zane’s relaxed demeanor suggested he wasn’t taking Bob seriously at all. His casual stance betrayed no anxiety whatsoever, as though calling someone of such an elevated station a loser was something that happened every day.
Regret knotted itself in Steward Carter’s stomach, twisting uncomfortably. He cursed himself for not making Bob’s identity crystal clear to Zane. He should have emphasized the cardinal rule that you never provoke a member of a Great House, especially when you’re a nobody. This rule went double when the member of a Great House has a spectacularly bad temper. There was no possible way that this would end well.
But then a second, colder realization hit him: it wouldn’t have mattered. Zane already knew Bob’s status. He just didn’t give a damn.
When Leo heard what Zane said in his defense, his eyes went wide with shock. A warmth spread through his chest---he felt genuinely moved that Zane would stand with him against such a formidable foe. Zane clearly recognized that Leo had only tried to help, and now he was returning the favor, even when facing a cultivator at the peak of the Adept Realm.
But this was insanity, standing up to a loose cannon with a cruel streak like Bob. Zane was being headstrong, and dangerous. This arrogance felt familiar to Leo.
[MUSIC: REMINISCENT FLASHBACK]
It reminded Leo of Zane’s reckless streak back in the south. Even then, as a mere Initiate Adept---barely established in his cultivation---Zane had the audacity to launch a soul attack against Randall, a Fourth-level Adept, before verbally chewing the man out without a hint of hesitation.
The Adept Realm was what warriors called those who had broken through the Nature Level and transformed their bodies into something beyond ordinary human limits. Most never made it. Those who did were divided into nine levels, with Consummation — the full circle — sitting just below the threshold of the Soul Realm. Zane had only just crossed the first threshold. Bob stood at the ninth.
This confrontation, however, was in a whole different league. Bob towered above Randall in every conceivable measure.
For one thing, the power gap between Bob and Zane was way bigger than it had been with Randall. More critically, their backgrounds were nothing alike. Randall was a nobody, a commoner from humble origins who had fought for every scrap of power he’d gained. Bob, on the other hand, carried the privileged bloodline of House Pauling, and all the resources and connections that came with it.
In the earlier encounter, Randall had ultimately backed down, intimidated by the weight of Leo’s family name and pressure from Jon. He’d swallowed his pride and accepted defeat.
Bob, with his aristocratic arrogance, would never back down so easily. Leo’s stomach tightened as he contemplated their predicament. What possible advantage could they gain by pissing off Bob? The consequences would be severe, perhaps fatal. Everybody knew he was a cruel bastard.
Finally, the situation seemed to have caught up with Bob. He snapped into focus, his eyes flashing fire and a deep furious red creeping over his face. His hands squeezed into fists, and his lips began to part in a snarl.
Everyone took a deep breath and braced themselves for the inevitable explosion of his fury.
[SFX: MAGICAL MIST]
But then something strange happened. Just as quickly as the color flooded Bob’s face, it drained away. The fire left his eyes, and his fists unclenched.
A faint red mist started to rise from the crown of his head. A moment later, his expression was placid again.
None of this looked natural; it was clear that Bob was using some kind of magic or technique to reign in his anger.
But getting his temper under control didn’t mean he was about to forgive Zane. Powerful Adepts had their own ways of handling rage. Some laughed maniacally. Others, like Bob, just smiled. It was a cold, thin smile that promised the arrogant kid in front of him was a dead man.
"Who are you?" Bob's eyes narrowed, scanning Zane with sudden calculation. Before attacking, he wanted to know what he was dealing with. Even someone as arrogant as Bob knew there were people you didn't touch — people with the right names behind them.
Leo stepped forward, moving to cut Zane off. He'd seen where this was going.
But Zane spoke first.
"Misty Peak. Outer disciple. Zane Lowell."
[SFX: TENSE SILENCE]
The last of Bob's hesitation evaporated. No family. No backing. Nobody. He'd been holding back for nothing.
That smile returned — colder this time.
Without a word, he sprang into motion.
[SFX: SWORD SWING]
[AMBIENT: WATER DROPLETS]
Bob’s sword sliced through the air with deadly precision, scattering countless water droplets that sparkled like diamonds in the sunlight. These weren’t ordinary drops, however---they instantly formed into a massive, tightly woven net of pure Vitality that surged toward Zane and Leo with frightening speed.
Leo’s eyes widened in horror as he recognized the technique. Each glistening droplet, though innocent in appearance, contained enough concentrated Vitality to pierce through flesh and bone with ease. A single drop would mean certain death.
“Zane, get back!” he shouted, his voice cracking with urgency. The young man’s stubborn courage would be his undoing against such a merciless attack.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
In one fluid motion, Leo unsheathed his sword, the metal singing as it cut through the air. His face hardened with concentration, beads of sweat forming on his brow as he channeled his power.
Dark red energy, thick as a man’s thumb and pulsing with ancient power, emanated from his blade. It lashed out with precision, targeting the deadly droplets one by one in a desperate race against time. Leo’s eyes narrowed, his entire being focused on the lethal constellation bearing down upon them.
[SFX: FREEZING EXPLOSION]
Every time the red energy struck a droplet, a freezing power exploded outward. The water flash-froze into a solid ball of ice that crashed to the ground, shattering the hard stone pavers into dust on impact. The power behind each one was incredible.
Leo swung his sword in a frenzy, the red energy from his blade knocking down one frozen drop after another.
[MUSIC: OVERWHELMING TENSION]
But Bob had unleashed too many. For every dozen Leo destroyed, a hundred more swarmed in.
He was running out of options. There was no time to think---only to swing faster, to desperately try and destroy every last one.
[SFX: FORCEFUL GRAB]
Just as Leo lost himself in the desperate defense, a hand grabbed the back of his tunic. An immense, unstoppable force yanked him backward.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STANDOFF]
And then, Zane was standing in front of him, alone, facing the swarm of deadly water drops that hurtled toward them.
