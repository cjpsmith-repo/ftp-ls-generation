Ep 1162 - Beyond the Abyss

Word count: 1133

[MUSIC: DARK MYSTERIOUS]
[SFX: LIGHTNING BOLT]
The dark abyss was lit up by a single, massive bolt of lightning, a thick braid woven from countless smaller strands that hung suspended in the void.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS ROAR]
[SFX: ELECTRICAL EXPLOSIONS]
Every time Zane grazed the bolt on his way down, a thunderous roar echoed from the depths, followed by explosions of thirty- to forty-foot-wide orbs of pure electricity. The power crackling within them was terrifying; the Thunder Law they contained had surpassed the fifth level, reaching an intensity Zane couldn’t even begin to comprehend. The force was so immense that even his spiritual weapon body could barely take the abuse.
But he just gritted his teeth and kept plunging downward. He was moving at top speed, and stopping was impossible even if he wanted to.
[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACK]
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: SHOCKWAVE IMPACT]
An orb of lightning exploded right beneath his feet. The blast of blinding white light was followed by a massive shockwave that slammed into him, halting his fall and flinging him upward in a wild, uncontrolled spin. Zane managed to right himself mid-air, a trickle of blood running from the corner of his mouth.
“This is way too dangerous,” he mumbled, his brow furrowed. But his expression was still tight with determination. Using the upward momentum, he angled his body and charged back down into the storm.
Lavender, who had been floating beside him and watching it all, let a faint smile soften her usually cold face. She drifted behind Zane, closed her eyes, and wrapped her arms around his chest from behind. 
[MUSIC: GENTLE ETHEREAL]
[SFX: WINGS UNFURLING]
Her sharp, elegant wings, formed from pure Faith Energy, began to flex and bend, wrapping around not just her, but Zane as well, cocooning them both.
“Go,” she commanded.
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ELECTRICITY]
With a sudden *whoosh*, their descent accelerated, becoming several times faster than before. The increased speed triggered an even greater storm of lightning. 
[SFX: MULTIPLE EXPLOSIONS]
[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLES]
The number of exploding orbs in the abyss multiplied, and violent rumbles shook the darkness around them. 
But it wasn’t just the orbs. The thunder spirits had minds of their own — and they used them. As Zane and Lavender plunged deeper, the spirits gathered, clustering in their path and launching wave after wave of attacks. Thunder surged up from below in crashing bursts.
[SFX: RAPID IMPACTS]
No matter how powerful the blasts were, they hammered uselessly against Lavender’s wings, unable to break through. Any spirit that couldn’t get out of the way in time was smashed apart by the wings themselves — there and then gone.
Zane’s understanding shifted. He’d seen Lavender draw on the Pools of Faith before, but never like this. Even at sixty percent recovery, she cut through the fifth circle’s attacks without effort. World overlords wouldn’t survive a breath against her. She might even give a Supreme Lord pause.
He thought of the Ancestral Dragon, curled in his bestiary bag — how it had quietly gorged itself on her Faith Energy back in Auspice City without either of them noticing. At the time, it had seemed like mischief. Now, watching the wings shed attackers like water off stone, he understood what it had actually eaten.
They reached the bottom moments later, completely unscathed.
[AMBIENT: DEEP STILLNESS]
They landed in a quiet valley. After the chaos of the abyss, the silence hit like a physical thing. The valley floor was dense with unknown plants — broad-leafed and dark, growing thick and luxuriant despite the dim light that reached this far down.
There was no cave visible. No stone house. Just the plants, wall to wall.
Lavender’s eyes moved across the valley. Then, without a word, the Faith Energy shifted. The spiked wings retracted, reshaping in an instant into twelve narrow blades that slotted neatly into her back — two compact rows, smaller and more controlled than the wings had been.
[SFX: BLADES UNSHEATHING]
The blades lifted and began to move, sweeping through the plants at high speed, cutting everything down to the roots. It took moments. When it was done, the valley floor was cleared, and there on the far wall — partly hidden by what had been growing over it — was a small cave entrance.
Smaller than any of the previous four. Noticeably so.
Lavender had never been this deep. She was at the Empyrean Realm when the fifth circle turned her back on her prior visits. Whatever was inside, she had no information to give him.
Zane didn’t ask. He just walked toward it.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Lavender followed, a trace of genuine curiosity in her eyes — a rare thing on that face. Who had built this place? Why? What was left inside after so many hundreds of millions of years?
In the narrow passage, the blade-wings collapsed further, condensing into a small pattern against her back to fit through the tunnel.
They entered a stone house. The walls had been cut clean — straight lines, precise angles, the work of someone who’d had time and intention. There was a stone bed against one wall.
Nothing remained on it but a thick pile of dust.
No matter how he looked at it, it seemed perfectly, bizarrely ordinary.
Lavender’s gaze settled on one corner of the structure. “There’s a back door,” she said.
Zane nodded and moved to open it.
[SFX: DOOR CREAKING OPEN]
[SFX: POWERFUL SUCTION]
The moment he pushed the door inward, a powerful, irresistible gravitational force yanked him forward.
Lavender felt it too. She tried to plant her feet and resist, but was shocked when the force effortlessly dragged her right through the small doorway behind him. 
Her Faith Energy, which had shielded them from the lightning, was completely useless against this strange new pull.
[SFX: SUDDEN WHOOSH]
“Whoa!” Zane yelled.
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL WONDER]
[AMBIENT: COSMIC VOID WITH DISTANT CHIMES]
They landed side-by-side, finding themselves in a strange, unknown space filled with countless stars of all sizes.
[SFX: AWE-STRUCK BREATH]
“So many stars,” Zane said, marveling at the magnificent sight. They twinkled like distant lamps. “Is this an Illusionary Arena?”
Lavender shook her head. “No. Our physical bodies were pulled in. An Illusionary Arena is just a projection.”
“These stars… they’re different from the ones in our universe,” Zane murmured, his eyes scanning the celestial display.
His training in the Stellar Body technique required him to observe the stars daily, so he knew them well. While he couldn’t possibly recognize every star in existence, he was certain that none of the constellations he knew were arranged like this. 
[SFX: DEEP RESONANT HUM]
Before them, the stars were organized in a perfect, orderly pattern, forming a massive whirlpool that spun slowly, majestically, around a single, central point.
