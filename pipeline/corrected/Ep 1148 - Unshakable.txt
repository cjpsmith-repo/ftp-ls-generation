Ep 1148 - Unshakable

Word count: 1003

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
Lavender wasn’t just counting on Zane; she was betting everything on him.
It wasn’t like he was her only option. Back in the Upper World, she could have called on her old followers, or even gone to Supreme Lord Leroy for help. 
But she had a gut feeling about Zane. A deep, unshakable certainty that he was the one who would help her reclaim her throne. Maybe finding the entrance to the Underground Palace was a coincidence, or maybe it was just another case of his ridiculous luck.
She thought back to her own journey into the abyss of that palace. She’d always regretted not making it past the fourth round herself. The palace held secrets she’d never been able to reach — secrets that weren’t just remarkable by the standards of the Dark World. They were extraordinary by the standards of the entire universe. And now, he was right back there. That’s why she’d said what she had. And when he’d simply nodded in response, a quiet resolve in his eyes, she knew she’d been worrying for nothing.
She should have known better. Zane wasn’t the type to give up. She remembered him on Killing Sword Mountain, how every single step had been pure torture, demanding everything he had and more. Even pushed to his absolute limit, he had never backed down. He was the type to give it his all and then some. He didn’t need reminding.
She was the sharpest sword of the Demon Night, a conqueror who had led her people across countless worlds, every step forward marked by the blood of her enemies. Warmth wasn’t in her nature. She was cold, ruthless, a hardened killer. 
[MUSIC: SOFT EMOTIONAL STRINGS]
But tucked away inside Zane’s body, a strange sense of relief always washed over her. With him, she felt… safe.
The Endless Abyss was a storm of unpredictable dangers where a single distraction meant death. And yet, Lavender felt no fear. Not with Zane.
Zane had no idea what was running through Lavender’s mind. He was too busy focusing on the fall. The Blade Mountain Trap had been surprisingly easy, which only meant something much worse was coming.
As he fell, his eyes moved to Freya. She was holding her own, expression steady. If the next trial pushed past what she could handle, he’d tell her to use her escape rune. She’d chosen to follow him into this place — that made her safety his problem.
He’d been through some extreme environments, but this was a whole other level. Those who couldn’t handle the pressure had already used their escape runes to bail. The allure of treasure was strong, but not strong enough to die for. Most people weren’t willing to trade their lives for a few shiny trinkets.
Those who remained were just hoping to get through this part quickly. 
[MUSIC: CREEPING DREAD]
Then the temperature dropped.
It wasn’t sudden — it crept in, degree by degree, until Zane noticed the walls of the abyss had changed. A thin layer of white frost clung to the stone. Lower down, it thickened into ice spikes jutting from the rock, pale and sharp in the dim light.
The air itself was beginning to freeze.
[SFX: ICE CRACKING AND SETTLING]
Zane could feel it — the cold seeping through his skin, slowing his blood. He pulled Chaos Energy through his body in a steady circuit, trying to generate heat. It helped, but only barely. Around him, the other warriors were doing the same, some of them pushing their Law of Fire to conjure flames. The flames lasted three or four breaths at best before the cold swallowed them whole.
[SFX: SHIELD SHATTERING]
A few warriors tried condensing their Vitality into protective barriers. That was worse. The moment a barrier formed, it froze — and then shattered, breaking apart into fragments of different colors that drifted away and embedded themselves in the icy abyss walls like shards of stained glass.
[AMBIENT: DEEP GROANING COLD]
There was nothing left to do but endure.
The fall slowed. The frozen air had thickened to something almost viscous, generating enough resistance to drag at their descent. In another situation, it might have been a relief. Here, it just meant spending longer in the cold.
An hour passed. Maybe more. The Spirit Supreme Realm warriors — some of the strongest people in the abyss — had gone grey-faced, their lips dark purple, barely keeping their Vitality circulating. Even Zane’s teeth were clenched.
Then he noticed Freya.
[MUSIC: QUIET SURPRISE]
She was calm. Not performing calm — actually calm. Her breathing was even, her expression composed. While everyone around her was burning through their reserves just to stay conscious, the extreme cold seemed to barely register for her. Her ice law cultivation ran so deep that this temperature, brutal as it was, felt almost natural. Of everyone falling through the abyss, she looked the most at ease.
Zane watched her for a moment. Then, despite everything, he almost smiled.
[SFX: ICE CRYSTAL WHOOSHING UPWARD]
But just when they thought the worst might be over, something appeared below them: a diamond-shaped crystal of ice, the size of a man, shooting upward.
[SFX: INTENSE COLD BLAST]
The crystal shot past, and a wave of cold so intense blasted Zane that his thoughts felt like they were turning to slush. His reflexes slowed to a crawl. The crystal was only fifty feet away when his brain finally caught up.
[SFX: BODY WRENCHING MOTION]
He wrenched his body sideways, barely dodging the projectile.
He was lucky. He saw Lincoln and Freya manage to avoid it, too.
[SFX: ICE CRYSTAL WHOOSH]
But one of the other warriors wasn’t fast enough.
Normally, an ice crystal—even one the size of a mountain—wouldn’t pose much of a threat to a seasoned warrior. But this was something else entirely. 
[SFX: BODY FREEZING SOLID]
The instant the crystal made contact, the unlucky man froze solid. A split second later, the force of the impact shattered him.
[SFX: SHATTERING ICE AND BONE]
The sound was sharp and sickening, like a porcelain doll dropped on a stone floor.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REALIZATION]
[AMBIENT: WIND HOWLING DESCENT]
A wave of horror washed over the survivors. As they continued their descent, Zane and the other warriors exchanged grim looks. They were all finally beginning to understand just how much danger they were really in.
