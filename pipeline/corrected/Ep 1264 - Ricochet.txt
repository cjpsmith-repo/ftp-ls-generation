Ep 1264 - Ricochet

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]
The Alabaster Staircase was a good twenty feet wide, its hundreds of thousands of steps carved straight out of the majestic mountain, leading from the base all the way to the summit.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Even though flying was forbidden, the ten of them were making pretty good time.
 After the warning from the woman in white, they’d all relaxed a bit. After all, they were ranked in the top thousand of this supreme world—hell, in the top hundred thousand of the entire Illusion Battlefield. None of them felt like picking a fight they couldn’t win, and besides, their guide had promised them a ten-million-point reward in the Hall of Causality. It was supposed to be a smooth ride.
The woman in white led the way with Zane right on her heels. The other warriors kept a respectful distance, climbing at a steady pace, while a short human warrior brought up the rear.
After a solid five-minute climb, the group came to a stop before a massive hall built halfway into the mountainside. The staircase ran straight through it, connecting to another flight of stairs at the back. To keep going, they had to pass through.
The hall was made of some unknown, brilliant white material that seemed to glow. There were no signs, no warnings on its outer walls—nothing.
Of the ten, only the woman in white had ever been inside the Hall of Causality. It was her second time. The rest of them were new to this, so they all watched as she walked toward the entrance, completely unfazed.
She glanced back with a small smile. “This is the First Hall of Causality. Getting through it isn’t too hard. Let’s go.”
Zane gave a sharp nod. He’d already spent five million Illusion Points just to get to the front door; there was no turning back now. 
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING HALL]
With that, the group followed her inside.
The hall was empty. Just a vast, silent space with nothing in it.
Every warrior there had explored their share of strange places in the Illusion Battlefield, but the sheer emptiness of the hall set everyone, including Zane, on edge. It reminded him of the top of the World Tree—another place that had been eerily quiet right before it tried to kill him. If he’d been just a little careless, he would’ve died instantly. He still had no idea how to get rid of the swarm of tiny skulls that had been relentlessly chasing him ever since.
Sensing the tension, the woman in white smiled again. “Don’t be so nervous. This is just the first hall. It’s not that bad.”
“So what’s in it?” someone asked.
“Just what the name says,” she replied, her voice echoing slightly in the big room. “The hall attacks using the laws of the heavens. Every strike is infused with the Law of Causality.”
A ripple of unease passed through the group. Even Zane’s eyes flickered — invoking the Law of Causality meant they were dealing with one of the mid-tier laws of the universe, maybe higher. That wasn’t something to take lightly.
“It seems to change every time,” the woman in white admitted, her smile easing just slightly. “I can’t say for certain what we’ll run into. But I do know the first hall isn’t too difficult. Stay careful, and everyone gets through.”
[SFX: RUNE IGNITING]
A strange, glowing rune bloomed to life on the wall to their left, a carved figure etched at its center. Its mouth opened, and a white ray of light tore free, streaking straight toward the woman in white.
“Be careful!” Zane shouted.
But she barely reacted, stepping back at an unhurried pace and letting the light pass within inches of her. It crossed the hall and vanished into the open mouth of a second rune on the far wall.
“I told you,” she said, glancing over her shoulder. “The first hall isn’t so bad — as long as you’re paying attention.”
[SFX: ENERGY BEAM WHOOSH]
Then another beam of white light shot across the hall, hanging in mid-air for a second before zipping off in another direction.
[SFX: RAPID ENERGY FLASH]
Another beam flashed from one side to the other so fast they barely saw it, paused, and then repeated the pattern.
The beams moved in predictable rhythms. For warriors of their caliber, it was a simple trick. 
The deeper into the hall they went, the faster the beams came — sometimes hanging dead in the air for a beat before rocketing off, sometimes crossing so fast the eye couldn’t track them, then repeating a few breaths later. 
None of it was truly a threat. At full speed, warriors of their caliber could have cleared the entire hall in less than a single breath.
Even so, there was something unnerving about them — the same way a person could walk a perfectly wide, flat path without a second thought, but hesitate over every step if that same path were suspended a hundred feet in the air. The danger was mostly an illusion. That didn’t stop it from getting under people’s skin.
Once they got the timing down, they relaxed and started moving faster, easily weaving between the paths of light.
“We’re getting close,” the woman in white announced, having clearly taken charge.
“What’s the reward for clearing the first hall?” the ogre martial artist called out from the back.
“You’ll find out when we get there,” she replied with a mysterious little smile.
Just then, Zane stopped dead in his tracks. 
[SFX: LIGHT BEAM WHOOSH]
He watched a white beam flash past, and an idea sparked in his mind. *I wonder if that light could destroy these skulls.*
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]
He reached behind him, pried one of the small skulls off his back, and held it carefully by its base. Taking a deep breath, he placed it directly in the path of the next oncoming beam.
[SFX: POWERFUL BEAM IMPACT]
The beam shot out and slammed right into the skull.
[SFX: MASSIVE ENERGY SURGE]
The instant it made contact, a massive, unbelievable force shot up Zane’s arm—a kickback that felt stronger than the full power of all his Dragon Scales combined. He instinctively let go, his hand going numb.
[SFX: BEAM DEFLECTION RICOCHET]
The small skull deflected the beam, sending it ricocheting straight toward the ceiling.
[MUSIC: CHAOTIC FRANTIC]
[AMBIENT: WILD RICOCHETING CHAOS]
Suddenly, the hall was pure chaos. The white beam wasn’t just bouncing wildly around the room anymore—the tiny black skull was right there with it, pinballing off the walls at an insane speed.
