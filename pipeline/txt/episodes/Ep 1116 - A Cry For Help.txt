Ep 1116 - A Cry For Help

Word count: 1141

[MUSIC: MAGICAL FLIGHT]
Zane spurred his Heavenly Phoenix Horse onward. He’d made the appointment with Lincoln a month ago, which meant he still had five months left to take care of his own affairs.
His thoughts drifted to Freya, the girl he’d met by chance in the Sovereign Stone mines. He wondered what kind of trouble she’d gotten into since they parted ways.
[SFX: HORSE HOOVES GALLOPING]
Lost in thought, he urged his horse on, leaving the inter-realm channel behind them. 
[SFX: ARMOR SHIFTING]
As he approached, the soldiers guarding the passage snapped to attention. They didn’t even ask for the usual toll; one look at the legendary beast he was riding was enough to earn their respect. Zane had his Gossamer Token ready—which would have gotten him through for free anyway—but to his surprise, they just waved him past.
[AMBIENT: MURMURING VOICES]
After Zane disappeared into the common teleportation channel, the soldiers watched him go, murmuring among themselves.
[SFX: SURPRISED EXCLAMATION]
“Holy crap, was that a Heavenly Phoenix Horse? You don’t see one of those in a backwater place like this,” one of them said.
“I bet our own Saint Lord couldn’t get his hands on a mount like that,” another added. “Who was that guy?”
“Son of a World Lord, maybe? Has to be. But he didn’t have that stuck-up attitude they usually have. Seemed pretty chill, actually.”
They had no idea that just six months ago, Zane had been a brand-new arrival, having just ascended from the Lower World.
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY STREETS]
[SFX: HORSE LANDING]
When he reached Clearhaven, Zane guided the Heavenly Phoenix Horse down for a landing. The city had a strict No-Fly Ban, so riding through the streets wasn’t an option. Once they were on the ground, he pulled out his bestiary bag, planning to guide the horse inside.
The bag had plenty of space, but to Zane’s surprise, the horse wanted nothing to do with it. 
[SFX: HORSE NEIGHING]
[SFX: HOOVES STAMPING]
It reared up on its hind legs, backing away and refusing to go in.
“What’s wrong?”
The horse had been perfectly calm the entire journey. Zane was stumped.
“You think it’s because of the Ancestral Dragon?” Lavender asked.
Of course. The Ancestral Dragon was a special case. Even though it was completely submissive to Zane, its powerful presence was undeniable. 
A legendary beast like the Heavenly Phoenix Horse would have an instinctual fear of it. 
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON COBBLESTONE]
In the end, Zane had no choice but to take the reins and lead the horse into the city on foot.
As they walked, he reached out to Freya through his Message Rune. 
The Upper World was vast — the Message Rune had a limited range, and he had no way of knowing exactly where she was. He’d circled the area on horseback, covering hundreds of thousands of miles, burning the rune again and again. On the sixth attempt, he finally got a response.
[SFX: FAINT VOICE THROUGH RUNE]
She told him she was in serious trouble but was cagey with the details, refusing to explain what was actually going on.
With no other choice, he followed her directions to a meeting spot. 
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: QUIET RESIDENTIAL STREET]
The address led him to a nice-looking residence, but his stomach dropped when he saw the four Soul Sea Realm warriors standing guard out front. He could also sense several divine textures woven around the house, not to mention the hidden presence of other powerful fighters inside.
[SFX: TENSE REALIZATION BREATH]
It all clicked into place. Freya wasn’t just in trouble. She was a prisoner.
“What do you want?” one of the guards shouted, spotting Zane and his unusual mount.
These guys weren’t nearly as sharp as the soldiers at the inter-realm channel, who saw warriors from all over the world day in and day out. They were just local muscle, following the city lord’s orders. They could tell Zane’s horse was no ordinary animal, but they clearly weren’t intimidated.
Zane frowned at their rotten attitude. “I’m looking for Freya,” he said, his voice even.
The guards exchanged a look. They knew Freya had only recently ascended from the Lower World. They couldn’t figure out how this guy knew her.
“Never heard of her,” the same guard snapped impatiently. “Now get lost.”
But Zane just pointed toward the house. “I know she’s in there. I just got a message from her.”
The guard’s expressions hardened. “You want some friendly advice? Walk away. There are things here you don’t want to get mixed up in.” He leaned in, dropping his voice like he was doing Zane a favor. “The woman inside was selected by the city lord himself — she’s being offered to the higher-ups in the Soulbound Sect. She didn’t exactly go along with it willingly, which is why she’s here. So ask yourself — is the city lord really someone you want to cross?”
[SFX: DOOR OPENING]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON BALCONY]
At that moment, as if summoned by his words, Freya was escorted onto a balcony by another guard. 
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL STRINGS]
Her eyes widened in shock when she saw Zane standing below.
She had used the Message Rune as a last-ditch effort, a desperate plea for help, even though she knew saving her was nearly impossible. Her own uncle, the city lord—a master at the late stage of the Spirit Supreme Realm—was the one holding her captive. Zane, on the other hand, was just another warrior who had ascended from the Lower World. What could he possibly do?
Zane offered her a small, tight smile. “So your own uncle is holding you prisoner. No wonder you needed help.”
“I used the Message Rune because I had no other choice,” Freya said, shaking her head. Just seeing him had calmed her a little, and she seemed resigned to her awful fate. “You should go, Zane. I don’t want you to get in trouble because of me.”
Finding her uncle had been her decision, and Zane was just a friend she’d met in a mine. There was no reason for him to risk his life for her. Besides, even if he wanted to save her, he probably couldn’t.
She hadn’t expected to end up here. When she’d left the mine, she’d thought her uncle was her way out — a foothold in the Upper World, someone to rely on. She hadn’t imagined he’d turn around and offer her up like a bargaining chip. If she’d known, she would have stayed. Kept mining. Earned her independence the hard way. At least then she’d have been free.
But to her surprise, he reached a hand out toward her, his voice firm and unwavering. 
“Come with me. It’s going to be okay.”
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Hearing his words, the guards’ faces immediately turned to stone.
