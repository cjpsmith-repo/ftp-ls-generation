Having to admit something like that in public sent a hot flush crawling up Cora’s neck. The Cloud Hall elders, whose expressions were already strained, exchanged another round of awkward glances before their eyes all drifted to Zane.
Pierce and his men were just stunned. If Cora was telling the truth, then she was completely useless to Maddox.
[SFX: CONTEMPTUOUS LAUGH]
“Ha! Miss Cora, you put on such a pure act back at the Kensington estate,” Pierce sneered, his voice dripping with contempt. With Cora no longer a virgin, his entire plan was shot.
Fury coiled in his gut. For years, he’d boasted he was better than any other man, but this woman right here wouldn't even give him the time of day. “I can’t believe you’ve been fooling around with some nobody.”
“He is not a nobody!” Cora shot back, glaring at him.
“Then who is he?” Tia asked, her curiosity suddenly getting the best of her.
The Ethereal Spirit Sect elders glanced at her, confused, clearly wondering why this woman was sticking her nose where it didn’t belong.
But Saint Morphen and the other Cloud Hall elders already had a good idea who Cora was talking about. They watched Tia’s sudden investment in the topic with knowing, troubled eyes.
Just then, Zane’s voice cut through the tension. “It was me.”
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
The entire hall fell silent.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
“Cora is with me now,” he continued, his tone firm. “And because of that, I can’t let you take her to Maddox.”
He hadn’t planned on hiding it, but admitting it in front of Tia still sent a wave of embarrassment through him. But things had gone too far. He couldn’t deny it now; he had to take responsibility for his actions.
At his confession, Tia's gaze dropped to the floor. She had been on the verge of a breakdown just last night, her face pale then. But now, it was as if every last drop of color had been siphoned from her, leaving her as white as bone.
“Gentlemen,” Zane said, his voice leaving no room for argument. “I think this meeting is over. We aren't finding a way to save your sect leader today.”
Pierce gritted his teeth, his glare a physical force against Zane. He desperately wanted to just grab Cora and drag her out of here, but he couldn’t. Not on someone else’s turf, not with the head of Cloud Hall present, and certainly not with Zane’s power being a terrifying unknown. Trying to take Cora against his will would be a suicide mission.
Besides, what was the point now? Even if he could take her, she was useless to him.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS DEPARTING]
[AMBIENT: EMPTY HALL]
The warriors from the Ethereal Spirit Sect wasted no time, filing out of the hall quickly.
Once they were gone, Zane looked at Tia. "Are you feeling alright?"
"I'm fine," she said, without looking at him. Her eyes moved to the elders. "Is there anything else to report today?"
The elders glanced at each other. There was plenty to report — the Corrupted Divine Kingdom's movements, the war response, and a dozen decisions that needed making. But one look at Tia's face settled it. Not today.
"Nothing urgent," Saint Morphen said.
"Then we'll continue another time." Tia turned and walked out without looking back. She moved as if Zane and Cora weren't in the room at all.
After she was gone, Cora tugged gently at Zane's sleeve. "What's wrong with her?"
Zane reached over and smoothed a strand of hair from her face. "Nothing," he said. His expression said otherwise.
After that day, urgency gripped Cloud Hall. With the Ethereal Spirit Sect destroyed, everyone from the Hall Master to the lowest disciple understood what was approaching.
Cultivation rooms filled. Warriors who had coasted now pushed desperately toward their next breakthrough, knowing that when the Corrupted Divine Kingdom arrived, only personal strength stood between survival and annihilation.
There was another kind of tension too, quieter and stranger. The elders noticed it first. Whenever Zane was in a room, Tia either said nothing or left. She didn't argue with him, didn't reprimand him, didn't acknowledge him. She simply looked through him as if he weren't there.
"It's a waste," one elder muttered to another, in what he thought was a private corner. "Zane has done more for this hall than any of us. He should be made an elder. The Hall Master can't let personal feelings get in the way of—"
He stopped. Tia had drifted around the corner as silently as smoke, her eyes pale and cold. She looked at them for a long moment. Then she drifted away again without a word.
The two elders stared at each other. "Someone needs to talk to her," one finally said. "Before this gets worse."
[MUSIC: GENTLE HOPEFUL MELODY]
For Cora, however, life had never been better. The lingering sadness from her time in Ethereal City was still there, a dull ache in the back of her mind, but here, she had finally found something like a home. She knew, deep down, that Zane’s feelings might not run as deep as hers, but that didn’t matter. In her heart, she had already decided he was her husband.
That simple conviction changed everything.
She stopped dwelling on the past and threw herself into her new life, even venturing into the local market to buy rare ingredients. She took over Cloud Hall’s kitchen, eager to finally put her skills to use.
Cora may have been arrogant, but as a daughter of a noble house, her education went far beyond combat. She was skilled with the harp, flower arrangement, hosting, and painting. But her true, hidden talent was cooking. Back home, as the prized mistress of House Kensington, servants had always handled such things. Now, she had a purpose.
[SFX: COOKING SOUNDS]
[AMBIENT: KITCHEN]
Every day, she would prepare exquisite dishes and bring them to the practice rooms, waiting for Zane to emerge.
But Zane didn't train for a day or two at a time. He would often go into seclusion for five or six days straight. Cora would eat a little of the food she’d made, but she saved the rest for him, leaving it until it eventually spoiled. She never complained.
[MUSIC: PATIENT WAITING THEME]
Day after day, she waited patiently outside the attunement rooms, a container of cooling food in her hands.
The other disciples of Cloud Hall used those rooms, too, and soon, everyone saw her. It didn’t take long before every junior warrior knew the story.
They all knew that Miss Cora was waiting for Zane, every single day. But when would he give her the time of day?
