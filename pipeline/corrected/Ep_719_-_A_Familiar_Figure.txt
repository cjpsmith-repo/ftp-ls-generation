Tia had merged with the Ice Phoenix and launched herself into the sky to meet the third wave of the Celestial Tribulation head-on. The petals looked harmless to the naked eye— but every one that touched her sent a nightmare crashing through her soul.
Training the soul was the hardest part for any warrior. Zane had the rare ability to hone his body, his core energy, and his spirit all at once, but few others could manage that kind of multitasking. Tia’s soul, while stronger than an ordinary warrior’s, was still a far cry from his.
A few dozen nightmares, she could have handled. But now, thousands of them swarmed her, an overwhelming tide that threatened to pull her under. How could she possibly survive?
Within minutes, her face was as white as a sheet. She started to stumble, her body swaying as she struggled to hold on.
A knot of worry tightened in Zane's gut. An injury to the soul wasn't like a broken bone; it could be permanent. I need to step in.
“Wait,” Lavender said, holding out a hand to stop him. “This looks like an attack, but it’s also a form of training. It could be good for her.”
Zane knew she was right. Warriors had to endure hardships to grow stronger, and Lavender had the experience to know exactly how far someone could be pushed before they broke. But knowing that and watching it happen were two different things. He cared too much about Tia, and he couldn’t stand to see her get hurt.
Just as the storm of flower petals was about to swallow her whole, Tia fumbled for a small, bronze-colored pill and forced it down.

She had cleared those first two waves almost too smoothly. Warriors who faced three waves already exceeded the ordinary — and she hadn't expected a third. She certainly hadn't expected a soul attack. Physical pain, she knew how to endure; the body had ways of protecting itself, could go numb, could even lose consciousness when it had no other choice.
Soul pain was different. There was no numbness. No escape. It was subtle and completely real, and it bored into her from the inside with every petal that made contact.
