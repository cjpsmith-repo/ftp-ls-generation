Ep 302 - Malice
Word count: 1136

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS THEME]
Malice was just another name for a killing aura.
The very presence of soldiers and bandits, men who had taken countless lives, practically dripped with it. Their every move and expression gave off a dark energy. Once you'd killed that much, you could never wash the stain of Malice away.
Sometimes, the Malice was so thick it could cloud your mind and trip you up on the battlefield.
If you let it corrupt you, it would poison your path as a warrior. At a critical moment, you could be consumed by your own inner demons, trapped in a spiral of violent thoughts.
[MUSIC: STORYTELLING TRANSITION]
There was a story about a young man named Trent Sutton. He was from House Sutton, one of the Seven Great Houses of the Eastern Territories.
Trent walked this deadly path. As his skill grew, so did his cruelty, and his methods became more and more vicious. Before long, he'd massacred countless people without a second thought.
At first, House Sutton protected him. In a world where power was everything, this path of killing offered a shortcut to immense power.
Its destructive potential was terrifying. Based on Trent’s rapid progress, he was on track to become a pillar of their House, so they looked the other way.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
But as time went on, Trent's body count just kept rising. One day, he even killed two men from his own house. That's when the members of House Sutton finally realized he'd gone mad. The Malice had consumed him, turning him into little more than a mindless killing machine.
[SFX: BATTLE SOUNDS]
By the time they understood the truth, it was too late. House Sutton was no longer strong enough to stop him. They had no choice but to team up with the other six Great Houses to finally put him down.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WINDS]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL EASTERN]
Killing Sword Mountain was kind of like a bank in the Eastern Territories, a place where you could deposit and withdraw Malice.
According to the mountain's rules, the higher you drove your sword into the rock, the more Malice you could store and access.
Make no mistake, Malice was an incredibly valuable asset. When used to fuel certain cultivation methods, it could dramatically boost a warrior’s strength. This was especially true for unique methods that specifically required Malice; the effect was exceptionally powerful.
Think of it this way: if one sliver of Malice could boost an attunement method's power by a certain amount, two slivers would double it, and so on.
However, there was always a limit to how much Malice a warrior who walked the path of killing could handle. They always had to be on guard against it, or risk being overwhelmed and consumed by their inner demons.
That's why this path was considered a double-edged sword. It was incredibly powerful, but it could turn on its wielder at any moment.
But a swordsman who entered Killing Sword Mountain could store their Malice there. This let them escape its corrupting influence—the mountain’s true purpose. It was also why swordsmen considered Killing Sword Mountain a sacred place.
A swordsman lucky enough to place their sword high up the mountain could deposit and withdraw even more Malice.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WINDS]
This was Zane’s first time on Killing Sword Mountain. Just making it here had been a stroke of incredible luck. He had no idea if he'd ever get another chance to come back. This was probably a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity. Even if he made huge breakthroughs in his training down the line, he might never get another shot.
Take Val, for example. He was incredibly strong now, and by all rights, he should’ve been able to place his Sacred Weapon way higher than the edge of the ridge. But he never got another chance to enter Killing Sword Mountain, so his weapon was stuck right where he left it.
That’s why this time, Zane was determined to drive his sword as high as it would possibly go.
[SFX: SWORD CLASH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
When the spirit of the Sacred Weapon attacked, Zane didn’t hold back. He unleashed his strongest techniques with his best weapon.
Wielders of Sacred Weapons were powerful enough to rule entire regions. As far as Zane could tell, Val's strength was probably on par with someone like the head of Cloud Hall.
Even if the sword only held its spirit and a tiny fraction of Val’s own power, it was going to be a tough fight for Zane, even with everything he had.
[SFX: ICE MAGIC ATTACK]
"Absolute Freeze!" Val’s spirit roared arrogantly. "Watch me freeze you solid!"
Then, he commanded the sword spirit to attack.
[SFX: ICE CRYSTALS FORMING]
The air turned razor-sharp. Ice crystals materialized around Zane, and the biting cold slowed his movements — his body simply had no resistance to it.
But Lily did.
Zane channeled her killing intent through the Streamer. It shot out in threads, shredding the ice crystals on contact, and drove straight through Val's attack.
"Impossible," Val's spirit breathed. "Your own killing intent is nothing — so where is this coming from?"
He didn't understand. The killing intent wasn't Zane's. It was Lily's, and it was extraordinary.
[SFX: SWORD SPIRIT SHATTERING]
Zane drove the blade home. Val's sword spirit cracked layer by layer and dissolved.
The Sacred Weapon re-planted itself in the ground. A rush of blue light poured out of the blade and flooded into Zane's body.
"You’ve defeated the spirits of two Sacred Weapons. That’s an outstanding achievement," the Cobalt Dragon said slowly in his mind.
"But I’m afraid you won’t be able to defeat the next one, no matter how hard you try."
It clicked. The reason Lily's sword — a low-grade Mysterious Weapon — had been planted so high on the mountainside wasn't luck. It was her killing intent. It had been extraordinary all along.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS ENERGY]
[SFX: MAGICAL WEAPON ACTIVATION]
Not far from Zane, another Sacred Weapon began to flash with a golden light. It was a greatsword, wider than Zane’s entire body and easily seven feet long. The master of such a sword must have been a giant. What worried Zane even more was the hint of reddish energy shimmering on the greatsword’s surface. If he was right, that was Malice, compressed to an unbelievable degree. Just looking at the sword was terrifying.
Zane knew his own limits. He knew that with his current strength and the Malice from his own sword spirit, he’d done well just to defeat Val’s spirit and make it this far up the ridge. It was a solid result—maybe the best he could hope for right now.
But Zane wasn’t ready to stop.
[SFX: DEEP BREATH]
[MUSIC: DETERMINED BATTLE THEME]
He took two steps back and gripped Streamer firmly. Then, with a deep breath, he sprinted forward.
"Oh, Zane..." the Cobalt Dragon sighed helplessly in his mind. He knew exactly what Zane was about to do.
[SFX: SWORD BEING THROWN]
He couldn’t beat the next challenge, and he knew it.
So Zane took a deep breath and hurled his sword with every last ounce of his strength.
