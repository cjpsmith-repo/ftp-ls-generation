Ep 1163 - A New Age

Word count: 1029

[MUSIC: MAGICAL WONDER]
The stars spread before them were a perfect, miniature replica of the universe. It was an absolutely incredible sight.
But Zane hadn’t jumped into the abyss and endured all those trials just to look at the scenery. 
[SFX: RUSTLING FABRIC]
He reached into his bestiary bag and pulled out Livingston.
[SFX: WOODEN JOINTS CREAKING]
The puppet had been shoved in there earlier, and the moment he was out, his head swiveled around, taking everything in.
“Huh? You’ve broken into Gideon’s home!” Livingston exclaimed.
The puppet was a relic from a bygone era, and at his words, both Zane’s and Lavender’s eyes lit up. Zane hadn’t had a chance to bring Livingston out while they were navigating the abyss, but now, at the bottom, he figured the puppet’s ancient knowledge might be exactly what they needed to get past this level.
“Oops, my mistake,” Livingston said with a laugh. “The entire Endless Abyss is technically Gideon’s home. But this little Star Plate? I built this myself. It’s a perfect copy of our universe, right down to the last star. Everything you see here is synced with the real sky outside. Gideon used it to practice his Transcendent Divine Might—the Star Plucking Technique!”
Zane shot Lavender a look. The Star Plate had been impressive enough on its own, but Livingston’s revelation left them completely stunned.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]
“The Star Plucking Technique?” Lavender repeated, her voice full of awe. “A Transcendent Divine Might?”
Only a Supreme Lord could create something like that. There were plenty of Supreme Lords in the universe, and Lavender knew practically all of them, but she had never heard of the Star Plucking Technique. She could only think of two Divine Mights that drew on the power of the stars: Zane’s own incomplete Stellar Body and the Dual Forest of Stars Theurgy, which was practiced by an ogre Supreme Lord. A third one was unheard of.
“Even I’ve never heard of it,” the Cobalt Dragon chimed in from within her.
[AMBIENT: COSMIC VOID]
Lavender’s gaze drifted over the swirling cosmos. “The universe considers the heavens a supreme power. What’s the name of the current Cosmic Age?” she asked, thinking aloud. Each Age had its own will, its own name.
“Was Gideon a Supreme Lord from the last Cosmic Age?” Zane asked, cutting to the heart of it.
Livingston’s spinning metal head slowed, then stopped spinning entirely.
For a long moment, the puppet stood completely motionless. Then, quietly:
“The Cosmic Age… has changed?”
“It has,” Lavender answered.
The cosmic age Gideon had lived in — that Livingston had been built in, had forged weapons in, had gone to sleep in — had run its full course, collapsed, and been replaced. A new age had risen in its place. The universe had been reborn around him while he slept.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC WEIGHT]
“No wonder,” he said finally, and something in his voice had gone flat. “No wonder the weapons were all dead. I kept thinking — even if the Celestial Essence had faded, it shouldn’t have faded that completely. There shouldn’t have been anything left.” 
[SFX: CLANG]
He raised one metal hand and brought it down against his own head with a hollow clang. “Of course, the Celestial Essence became useless. It was attuned to the old laws. When the age ended, everything sealed inside those weapons became worthless. That’s why there was nothing left to find.”
The Celestial Essence was a refiner’s art — the practice of observing a trace of the age’s fundamental law and sealing that understanding into a weapon. It was what separated a master’s work from everything else. And every last piece of it, in every weapon Livingston had ever made, had died with the old age.
Then Livingston straightened. The metal head began to spin again. When he looked at Zane, the arrogance was back, sharp as ever.
“No wonder you juniors have never heard of me.” He let out a laugh — loud, unbothered, entirely himself. “I’m the number one weaponsmith in the universe! The previous universe, granted — but still! Hahaha!”
Zane said nothing, but his thoughts had shifted. He’d assumed Livingston was boasting. Now he wasn’t so sure. If Livingston had been the greatest refiner of the previous cosmic age — before the current era’s records began — then the claim wasn’t arrogance. It was simply a fact that no one alive would be able to verify or dispute. And the forge at the heart of his inner world was still empty.
He filed that thought away for later.
Livingston nodded and walked toward the miniature universe. He reached out and casually plucked a star from its place. 
[SFX: GALAXY SPINNING FASTER]
[SFX: LIGHT PATH FORMING]
The entire galaxy immediately began to spin faster and faster, a path of light forming right through the middle of the Star Plate as Zane and Lavender watched, mesmerized.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC REFLECTION]
“See, I wanted to live a long time,” Livingston explained, his voice softening. “So I begged Gideon to find the rarest materials in the universe to make this puppet body for me. The price was that I had to stay here and forge weapons for him for four hundred thousand years. This body is powered by Vitality, but I can’t absorb it from the world around me. Gideon used to send me huge shipments of Sovereign Stones to keep me going, but then… the deliveries just stopped.”
Once the last of the Sovereign Stones were gone, Livingston had fallen into a deep sleep, only to be woken by Zane.
“I created this Star Plate,” he said, waving them over. “Gideon should be long dead by now. I wonder what other treasures he left behind. Come on.”
[AMBIENT: DEEP MELANCHOLIC SILENCE]
Zane couldn’t miss the profound sadness that had crept into the puppet’s voice. An eternity had passed in what felt like a dream. His world was gone, his friends were dead. He was still here, but everything he’d ever known wasn’t.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS INTO STARLIGHT BEAM]
[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS ANTICIPATION]
[AMBIENT: STELLAR ENERGY HUM]
Zane and Lavender followed him into the beam of starlight that cut through the rotating galaxy.
