Ep 629 - Powder Keg
Word count: 1069
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
The whole continent was a powder keg, and the war declared between the humans and the ogres was the spark. Things were so bad that even the Tower of Sin, usually a self-contained world of its own, was descending into chaos, and the warriors inside were starting to panic.
The Cursed Land, where the Tower was located, was a melting pot for every race imaginable. That made the Tower a perfect miniature of the entire continent, and the intel you could get there was second to none. Some guys even made a living just gathering information from inside and selling it on the outside. Now, with this hurricane of a war slamming the continent, the Cursed Land was caught in the middle of the storm, and the Tower of Sin was its very eye.
[MUSIC: FOCUSED DETERMINATION]
And right there, in the eye of the storm, was Zane. He was completely unfazed by the chaos brewing outside, his focus entirely on his own path.
He did have one small problem, though: he couldn’t stay on the tenth floor anymore.
The Lucky Light from countless battles elevated him to second-level War General, amplifying his already formidable strength. The Tower of Sin's mystical properties accelerated advancement several times faster than anywhere else—precisely why every race valued it. Combat experience here was priceless.
At his current pace, breaking through to War Lord was imminent. Since he'd dismantled Bradley, the ogres had been issued an ultimatum: back down, or every ogre—including their top-tier War Lords—would abandon the Tower of Sin.
Inside the Tower, the mood among the Ogres had changed completely. They'd gone quiet — avoiding the arenas, staying out of public areas, keeping their voices low. For a race known for arrogance and noise, the silence was almost eerie. The other races noticed. And most of them were quietly delighted. The Ogres had ruled this place with an iron fist for as long as anyone could remember. Watching them shrink into the corners, suddenly careful and subdued, was a sight many had waited a long time to see.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The moment Zane’s foot hit the stone of the eleventh floor, two third-level ogre War Lords converged on him.
[AMBIENT: TOWER CORRIDOR]
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
"You must be Zane," one of them sneered. "Finally made it to the eleventh floor. How about a match in the arena? Right now."
"A second-level War General with the nerve to come up here?" the other one added, his voice a low growl. "You've got a death wish."
They were obviously trying to bait him into a fight. The truth was, the ogres already knew they couldn't touch Zane in a straight-up fight inside the Tower. He’d instantly defeated Bradley, their fourth-strongest War Lord. What chance did anyone else have?
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
That left them with only one option: kill him in the arena.
That was just the Ogre way. Even if they knew they were outmatched, they’d still fight to the death. Besides Boris, there were plenty of other tenth-level ogre War Generals on the floor below. After Zane’s little negotiation with Maurice, many of them had volunteered to take him on in the arena. But then, suddenly, they’d all stopped.
Zane had been a little confused at first. It just wasn't like the ogres to back down. Since they couldn't kill him in the safe zones, it was a safe bet the hot-headed race would try to eliminate him by the book. Instead, he’d won by default for days on end, which forced him to advance to the next floor whether he wanted to or not.
[MUSIC: REVELATION STRINGS]
But now, seeing these two ogre War Lords blocking his path, it all clicked. So that was their game. Everyone on the tenth floor knew it would be a suicide mission to fight him, so they just… stopped. They’d let him advance, pushing him up to a higher floor where stronger fighters could deal with him.
"I'll take your challenge," Zane said, his eyes narrowing into a faint smile. "But you'd better be prepared to lose your lives."
"The Demon race doesn't have anyone who's afraid of dying!" one of them shot back. "Besides, you're just a second-level War General."
"That's right," the other laughed. "As long as you don't use that Tower power of yours — hahaha!"
Zane raised one hand slightly. "You mean this?"
[SFX: INVISIBLE FORCE]
An invisible force wrapped around the first War Lord instantly. The laughter died. The War Lord's face went pale as the pressure closed around him — he knew exactly what had happened to Bradley, even if the details hadn't been made public. One twitch of Zane's finger and he'd be in pieces.
"You — the rules forbid fighting in the safe zone!" he choked out.
"The safe zone rules were set by the seat holders," Zane said, his voice flat. "But I don't think that rule will be around much longer."
"Every race agreed to those rules—"
[SFX: BODY DROPPING TO FLOOR]
Zane's finger moved. Not to kill — just enough. The War Lord crumpled to the ground. Zane stepped past him without a glance and walked toward the arena.
"The new rules," he said to no one in particular, "will be set by me."
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
"This guy doesn't even seem human."
"He's not wrong, though. Set aside who he is — he's the controller of the Tower of Sin. Who's going to stop him in here? Esther? Maurice? He killed Bradley in the blink of an eye. If he wants to set the rules, that's his right."
"The rules have always belonged to whoever has the biggest fist. That's how the Ogres ran this place for years, and nobody said a word. Same principle."
As a crowd gathered, Zane walked into the arena. He turned and pointed at the two Ogres. "Fifteen days," he announced, his voice ringing with absolute confidence. "In those fifteen days, I will give you every chance you want to challenge me."
[MUSIC: BATTLE DRUMS]
"I'm going to kill him!" the first ogre roared, his face twisted in fury. He lunged toward the arena entrance without a second thought.
For the ogres, it had come down to two options. They could either attack and destroy the Violet Heart Sanctuary within the next fifteen days, or they could kill Zane right here, inside the Tower of Sin.
If they failed at both, every last ogre would be kicked out of the Tower. It would be the greatest humiliation their people had ever faced.
They would do anything and kill anyone to prevent it.
