Ep 442 - Forging The Soul

Word count: 1101

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TRAINING GROUNDS]

[MUSIC: WONDROUS DISCOVERY]

Elder Howard finished explaining the Fighting Soul Realm. Zane stood motionless, absorbing the revelation. He'd never encountered this concept—not in scrolls, from masters, or even in whispered rumors. The Eastern Territories knew nothing of it.

Excitement and frustration churned in his chest. Another fundamental truth his homeland had never discovered. How many other secrets lie beyond the mountains, waiting to reshape his understanding of power?

Elder Howard had anticipated Zane's ignorance. No one in the Eastern Territories had ever awakened a Fighting Soul. Even Sir Kenneth—the region's strongest warrior and leader of the Skyreach Sect—lacked one, much less a Nightjade Fighting Soul.

At the peak of the Adept Realm, Zane couldn't have known. Perhaps at Inner-Core Realm, he might have caught whispers.

Any other student would have earned a scoff. But Zane's talent had earned Howard's respect, so he explained.

Ordinary souls were fragile. Minor damage could weaken you for days; serious injury could cause permanent mental harm. Complete shattering meant instant death—the body crumbling to dust.

But a trained soul, strengthened to a certain threshold, became a weapon. You could fight other souls directly.

Soul training was exponentially harder than body cultivation, but the payoff was immense. It was the only path to the Fighting Soul level.

[MUSIC: INSPIRATIONAL STRINGS]

"That's… incredible," Zane said, hooked. "But how do I reach it? The Fighting Soul level?" He'd once dreamed of nothing more than reaching the Inner-Core Realm, but now, that felt like just another step on the path. A new, much higher summit had just appeared before him.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]

A smug, knowing smile crept across Elder Howard's face. He had him. Not only was Zane captivated, but he would need a mentor. "Let's be blunt. Here in the Lower World, you have absolutely zero chance of obtaining a Fighting Soul, let alone a Nightjade Fighting Soul. The souls of people from this world are just naturally weak. Yours is strong, I'll grant you—comparable to many practitioners in the Soul Realm—but you still have a long, long way to go to reach the Fighting Soul level."

Zane had always thought his soul was as strong as it could possibly be, tempered as it was by the soulforge in his mind. But hearing Howard’s blunt assessment, he realized how much further he had to climb.

Instead of feeling discouraged, a fresh wave of motivation washed over him. He would just have to get stronger.

"Elder Howard," Zane asked, his voice full of respect. "Please, tell me what I need to do."

Elder Howard's smile widened, pleased by the deference. He chuckled and gestured toward the black sphere. "Extend your soul into it, and you'll understand."

"Extend my soul into it?" Zane blinked, then shook his head reflexively. "Elder Howard, you can't be serious."

Though Zane sensed the Nightjade Fighting Soul's overwhelming power, the sphere's pressure would kill him instantly. His soul hadn't reached the Fighting Soul Realm.

Elder Howard reassured him. "We wouldn't establish this training to send you to your death. Other warriors cultivate here as well."

He gestured toward the motionless bodies surrounding the sphere, their souls already inside. "This opportunity is reserved for the clan's elite who ascended from the lower realm. Seize it." 

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

Zane nodded and approached. Settling cross-legged, he extended his soul into the darkness.

[SFX: SHARP STABBING]

[AMBIENT: DANGEROUS VOID]

Pain lanced through him immediately—countless daggers stabbing, piercing, riddling his soul with holes. Frost and flames assaulted him simultaneously.

His teeth chattered. He trembled uncontrollably.

Without a physical body, souls remained fragile. Though stronger than most at his level, Zane's soul hadn't transformed completely. He couldn't bear this agony.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]

Through the pain, he spotted a small yellow flame flickering ahead. He rushed toward it.

The closer he drew, the less pressure crushed him. The flame's warmth felt like a bonfire in ice, an oasis in a desert.

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL RADIANCE]

When he reached it, gentle radiance began repairing his damaged soul.

Many martial artists' souls huddled around this oil lamp. Similar lamps scattered throughout the space protected warriors from destruction.

[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]

[AMBIENT: OPPRESSIVE ENERGY]

At the center, a giant Moonkin Spirit stood chained by lightning. The terrifying pressure emanated from the creature itself.

Warriors periodically left their lamps to endure the evil Spirit's scouring pressure, then returned for repair.

This method resembled Zane's first tempering in the Soulforge, though that pain had been a hundred times more intense; the repair was countless times faster. That experience had made his soul incomparably tough.

Without these lamps in the lower realms, such tempering would leave one an idiot.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED DRUMS]

For Zane, this was a rare opportunity. He would seize it.

After his soul nearly recovered, he rushed beyond the lamp's boundary. The terrifying pressure swept toward him again.

Most warriors couldn't withstand it for more than six seconds before scrambling back.

[MUSIC: INTENSE RISING TENSION]

But Zane stood his ground, pushing himself to endure the crushing force for just a little longer. He gritted his teeth, his jaw clenched tight. Just a little longer.

[SFX: HEARTBEAT]

Three seconds.

Six seconds.

Thirty seconds.

[AMBIENT: EERIE VOID]

[SFX: SOUL TEARING]

After half a minute, a terrifying sensation washed over him—his soul felt like it was being torn apart, like a heavy cloak being ripped to shreds by an unseen force.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

"Now!"

[SFX: MAGICAL HEALING GLOW]

Just as he felt his consciousness begin to fray, Zane stumbled back toward the small Warding Lantern. The moment he crossed into its protective light, the tearing stopped, and he could feel his soul slowly begin to mend.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

"Pushing it a little hard there, aren't you, new guy?" a man lounging near the Warding Lantern called out with a smirk.

These warriors had been in the black sphere far longer than Zane. They were veterans of its brutal soul pressure and knew their limits down to the second. None of them could take it for more than fifteen seconds. To see this newcomer hold out for thirty was stunning, and frankly, a little insulting. The kid had shown them all up without even trying.

Zane just offered the man a faint smile, saying nothing as he focused on his recovery. As soon as his soul felt whole again, he stepped back out of the light.

[MUSIC: TRAINING MONTAGE]

Over and over, he repeated the process. After enduring the torment from the Owl Beast's ghost a few times, Zane found the pain becoming more bearable, his endurance stretching.

His first attempt was thirty seconds. Then it grew to thirty-six, then a full minute, then one hundred and twenty seconds.

[MUSIC: EPIC CRESCENDO]

Before long, Zane was lasting five whole minutes. It was the longest anyone had ever withstood the phantom torment.

[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]

[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]

The other warriors could only watch, their jaws slack, completely stunned by the impossible rate of his progress.
