Ep 238 - Crushing Fist
Word count: 1300
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
With his offensive strategy crumbling, Langdon finally shifted to defensive tactics, summoning a thick layer of earthen armor that encased his skin like a second hide.
"Heh. My Earthen Armor can withstand a direct strike from a Soul master," he sneered, confidence returning to his voice despite the beads of sweat forming on his brow. "You're strong, I'll give you that, but what are you going to do now? Nothing penetrates this defense without Vitality---something a low-level cultivator like you couldn't possibly wield."
Langdon hadn't intended to reveal this technique so early. The All Peaks Competition attracted countless talented fighters; common sense dictated saving one's trump cards for truly desperate situations. But the reality stared him in the face---without this armor, defeat was inevitable. His pride wouldn't allow such humiliation in the first round.
For an initial opponent, Langdon wasn't particularly formidable, yet Zane had to acknowledge the fighter possessed an unexpectedly diverse arsenal of techniques. Each time Zane thought he'd gained the upper hand, Langdon produced another surprise.
"It can truly withstand a full-strength assault from a Soul master?" Zane inquired, his voice deceptively casual as he studied the earthen shell surrounding his opponent.
A dangerous smile played across Zane's lips, his eyes gleaming with anticipation as they traced the contours of the Earthen Armor. The crowd fell silent, sensing a shift in the atmosphere of the arena.
"Alright then," he taunted, flexing his fingers as energy began to gather around his fists. "Let's see what happens when I actually get serious."
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
"Crushing Fist!"
In a fluid motion, Zane slipped sideways, his form blurring as raw power surged through his veins. He channeled half the strength of his Dragon Scales, feeling the ancient energy cascade throughout his body. The air around his clenched fist shimmered with barely contained force.
His physical frame, already honed beyond ordinary human limits through countless hours of training, hummed with potential. Even without enhancement, Zane's strength far exceeded that of his peers. Now, as he tapped into merely half the draconic power residing within him, his capabilities multiplied exponentially.
The sensation was intoxicating---muscles coiling with energy, bones reinforced by cultivation techniques passed down through generations. His eyes narrowed in concentration, a predator's focus settling over his features as he calculated the precise point of impact that would demonstrate his superiority without revealing all his secrets.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
The sound of Zane's fist on the Earthen Armor was a dead, heavy sound, like a sledgehammer hitting granite. Langdon's earth-based Vitality was perfect for defense. His armor was meant to mimic the properties of the land itself---capable of bearing any weight. The design was supposed to disperse the force of any blow instantly across its entire surface, weakening the impact to almost nothing.
[SFX: REPEATED IMPACTS]
As Zane's fist connected, Langdon stumbled back two steps, his face flushing crimson. He'd made a fatal miscalculation: assuming Zane was already fighting at his peak. Under the crushing force, the Vitality in his body roiled violently, making it a struggle just to stay on his feet. He could feel the Earthen Armor groaning under the strain.
"How is this possible?" Langdon, who'd been born with immense strength, knew how impossible it was to squeeze out more power once you'd hit your limit. "How did Zane's strength just leap like that? Wasn't he already going all out? Or... was his power limitless? Had he been holding back this whole time?" Langdon's mind reeled.
Without giving him a moment to recover, Zane channeled the same power from his Dragon Scales and punched the Earthen Armor again.
[SFX: LOUD CRACKING IMPACT]
Fissures spiderwebbed across the thick plating with a sickening crunch, the armor appearing ready to shatter at any moment. The once-proud protection now looked like fragile pottery on the verge of collapse.
Blood sprayed from Langdon's lips as his face transformed into a choked, purplish-red mask of pain. He had desperately tried to contain it, but the internal damage was too severe, forcing the crimson evidence of his defeat to emerge.
In that moment of clarity, Langdon recognized the insurmountable gulf between their abilities. His pride, cultivated through years of privilege at Azure Peak, crumbled faster than his vaunted armor.
"Referee! Referee, I surrender!" Langdon cried out, throwing his trembling hands skyward as he watched Zane's fist rise again. Terror flashed across his eyes---not merely fear of another blow, but the dawning realization that he had grievously underestimated his opponent from the beginning.
[SFX: REFEREE WHISTLE]
"Zane won, Langdon lost!" the Referee announced, his expression unchanged.
At Langdon's concession, Zane stopped.
Master Warren let out a breath. "Zane, good job!" 
The other Misty Peak masters exchanged glances. If Zane kept performing, Misty Peak's ranking would climb, and every master on the peak stood to benefit.
Nearby, Master Silas of Sea Peak leaned toward his disciple and kept his voice low. "Born with great strength — it's a novelty, nothing more. History is full of disciples like him. None of them amounted to much." He nodded at the arena. "York - if you meet anyone from Misty Peak in the next rounds, don't hold back."
York nodded. He was unremarkable to look at, easy to overlook in a crowd — but when his eyes moved, there was something sharp and dangerous in them. "If I meet that one, I'll finish it in ten breaths," he said, without any particular heat. He just meant it.
Zane heard every word. He said nothing, and smiled.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMURS]
[MUSIC: TRANSITIONAL THEME]
The crowd's murmurs faded as attention shifted to the adjacent arena, where Eliot faced his opponent Mack in what promised to be an equally compelling match.
Mack was no ordinary opponent. He'd reached peak-tier Adept at his age while simultaneously developing his skills as a Magic Array Master — a combination that required exceptional soul aptitude. Either ability alone would have made him dangerous.
If Mack managed to set up his battle formation, his power would spike, putting Eliot at a serious disadvantage.
[SFX: MAGICAL WEAPON MATERIALIZATION]
Knowing this, Eliot decided to strike the second Mack revealed the array flag. A pristine white spear materialized in his hands.
[SFX: SPEAR THRUST]
He immediately lunged, jabbing at Mack in a rapid-fire assault.
"Huh? He uses a spear?" Master Warren asked, surprised.
He'd never seen Eliot wield one before and was caught off guard.
The disciples from Misty Peak exchanged puzzled glances. As far as they knew, Eliot had never used a weapon when taking on challengers back at their peak. In every fight they'd seen, he'd only ever used his fists.
But here, at the start of his first match, he'd immediately drawn a weapon. And his white spear was no cheap practice tool; it was a middle-grade Spirit Weapon.
"Ha! Looks like Eliot's been holding out on us," Master Warren chuckled. It was obvious he'd kept his spear skills a secret for the All Peaks Competition. Just as The Commander's Axioms said, "Know yourself and know your enemy, and you'll never lose." On the other hand, the less your opponent knew about you, the better your odds.
But another master from Misty Peak shook his head. "Don't celebrate too soon, Warren. The fact that he brought out the spear immediately shows how wary he is of Mack's magic array. Otherwise, he would've saved it for later."
"You have a point," Warren replied. "But the match just started. It all comes down to how he performs."
[SFX: SPEAR SLICING AIR]
Down in the arena, Eliot's spear sliced through the air. But Mack wasn't just a master of arrays; his reflexes were incredible. He evaded Eliot's thrusts like a fish weaving through a current.
Unable to land a clean hit, Eliot stopped trying to chase Mack's shadow. He realized the real threat wasn't Mack himself---it was the array flag. Once that magic array was active, his odds of winning would plummet.
[SFX: FORCEFUL SPEAR THRUST]
With that thought, Eliot pivoted and stabbed at the array flag planted in the ground, intending to destroy it.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
But the moment his spearpoint made contact, everything changed.
