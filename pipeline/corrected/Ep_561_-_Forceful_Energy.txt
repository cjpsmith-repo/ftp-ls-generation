Ep 561 - Forceful Energy
Word count: 1056
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Zane had already cut down half the Ogre Governor's army—and now the Governor himself was out for blood.
The Ogre Governor was the size of a small mountain, an impression only magnified by the massive courser he sat upon.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS CHARGE]
[SFX: WHOOSHING SPEAR]
With a deep growl, the Ogre charged straight for Zane. He and his mount moved with terrifying speed, a torrent of red, forceful energy pouring from his body. As he closed the distance, the Ogre leaped from his courser, soaring through the air as he brought his long spear down in a vicious arc aimed right at Zane.
Throughout it all, Zane stood frozen. Even as the spear tip grew dangerously close, he didn't move an inch.
But that didn't mean the Ogre's power was a joke. A strike like that could flatten a small mountain in an instant. Still, Zane held his ground.
[SFX: FURIOUS ROAR]
A furious roar tore from the Ogre's throat."Go to hell!"
His spear reached Zane, backed by a power that could crush anyone in its path. But instead of Zane’s body being obliterated, something bizarre happened.
[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY PULSE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
A flicker of light shot out from between Zane's eyebrows—his very soul—and streaked toward the Ogre Governor's mind.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER ACTIVATION]
But before Zane's soul could reach its target, a faint yellow light flared to life, wrapping the Ogre Governor in a protective barrier.
Zane scoffed, a faint smile playing on his lips. "A treasure to defend against soul attacks? Cute. Too bad it's so low-grade."
He pegged it as a Top-Grade Spirit Weapon. If it had been a high-level Mythic or Relic-Class weapon, it might have stood a chance. But a simple Spirit Weapon was nothing against a soul that had reached the Fighting Soul Realm.
[SFX: CRACK]
A spiderweb of fractures raced across the yellow light.
[SFX: SHATTER]
The protective barrier shattered, useless against Zane's soul attack.
Zane's consciousness plunged into the Ogre Governor's mind, obliterating it.
[SFX: SPEAR STRIKE]
[SFX: BODY IMPACT]
The Ogre's spear struck simultaneously, hurling Zane backward across the pasture. His body carved a trench ten feet deep and a thousand feet long.
Zane rose, brushing dirt from his clothes. "Not bad. You've actually got some strength."
The Spatial Laws hadn't been fully purged from his body yet, leaving his limbs feeling awkward and disconnected.
The Ogre Governor stood frozen, spear outstretched—a grotesque statue.
[SFX: HEAVY BODY FALLING]
A gust of wind swept across the pasture. Then, with a resounding crash, the Ogre Governor's massive body toppled to the ground.
[MUSIC: TENSE SILENCE]
The Ogre soldiers stared. Their commander, promoted just this year—dead at the hands of a human boy. And that boy hadn't even flinched.
[SFX: CROWD WHISPERS]
Whispers rippled through the ranks.
"He's not human. He's a demon."
"The commander's strongest blow would have shattered a Rumbling Thunder Rhino's bones... and this boy is completely unharmed."
One by one, the remaining soldiers wheeled their horses around.
[SFX: RETREATING HOOVES]
The army that had numbered over a thousand broke apart and scattered across the pasture, leaving nothing behind but corpses—none of which bore a single visible wound.
[MUSIC: REVERENT SWELL]
Behind Zane, every man and woman who had been watching dropped to their knees.
"He stood alone against the Ogre army..."
"He is our True God."
Every human in the pasture pressed their foreheads to the earth—every one except two. A little girl and a little boy lingered at the edge of the crowd, watching Zane with wide, curious eyes.
Zane turned to face them. "Those soldiers are finished. They won't be back anytime soon." He paused. "You should use this chance to escape."
Silence. Then—
"We're not going anywhere."
"We follow only the True God."
"Only you can guide us."
Zane studied them. These people had been born and raised in a pasture controlled by the Ogre Clan. They didn't know the outside world. They didn't even know where they were. It was that very ignorance that had made them so quick to kneel.
It was, to put it plainly, a troublesome situation.
His goal on this continent was the Tower of Sin. He had stepped in out of kinship—they were his people, human like him—but he had no plan to play shepherd. And even if he'd wanted to protect them, there was no guarantee he could.
Still. He wasn't cold-blooded.
"Fine," Zane said. "We leave together."
[SFX: BUSTLING CAMP ACTIVITY]
His word was apparently law. The crowd erupted into motion, dismantling tents and bundling belongings with the efficiency of a people long accustomed to moving. Within the hour, they were ready to go.
Before they set out, a man approached with two others, pushing a cart toward Zane.
Zane didn't refuse.
[AMBIENT: ROLLING PLAINS, CART WHEELS TURNING]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Zane's thoughts drifted to Vesper's Celestial Tomb—the testing ground that had led him here. He'd won the Wheel of Fortune, but instead of receiving an immortal's bone, he'd claimed the bone of a Cloud-Chaser Lion, a Legendary Beast and close relative of the Greater Legendary Beast, the Celestial Chimera.
He faced two urgent tasks: purge the remaining Spatial Laws from his body, then unlock the Pathforged Realm—eight platforms that would elevate his Body Refining technique beyond generating forceful energy and finally allow him to learn the Transcendent Divine Might from the Cobalt Dragon's seventh brother.
Sitting cross-legged on the rolling cart, Zane turned his focus inward. After half a day's work, the last traces of Spatial Laws dissolved from his body.
He drew the Cloud-Chaser Lion's bone from his Storage Ring.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]
[MUSIC: BUILDING SUSPENSE]
Zane held the bone gently, then willed the violent, forceful energy to surge from his body.
Vitality and forceful energy were two completely different things.
Vitality was like a fine mist, growing denser and more refined as one's power increased. Forceful energy, on the other hand, was thick and heavy, flowing slowly through the body. A warrior generated it from within; it couldn't be drawn from the Vitality in the world, which meant it wasn't a limitless resource.
[SFX: POWERFUL LION ROAR]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
The forceful energy flowed into the bone. As it did, a sudden, violent roar echoed in Zane's ears, shocking him to his core.
