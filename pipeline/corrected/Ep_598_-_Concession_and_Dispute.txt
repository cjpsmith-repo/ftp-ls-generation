Ep 598 - Concession and Dispute
Word count: 1006

[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]
The Demon Night had arrived, and everyone on the tenth floor, regardless of race, was holding their breath.
A tall, slender figure glided toward them, her Demon Night War Generals trailing in her wake. The Demon Night, a race comprised almost entirely of women, always drew stares from the males of every species. Greed and lust burned in their eyes when they looked at these stunning creatures.
But that was a mistake. The moment any impure gaze fell upon the woman leading the procession, their eyes seared as if touched by a hot flame.
[SFX: BURNING SIZZLE]
"AHH!"
"My eyes! My eyes!"
"It hurts! I can't see!"
"Am I going blind?!"
[SFX: CROWD PANIC]
Screams of agony tore through the crowd.
The ones who hadn't looked away fast enough doubled over, clutching their faces. The ones who had stood well back watched them with a mixture of pity and contempt.
[AMBIENT: CROWDED HALL]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
"What were you thinking, looking at her like that?" a man hissed to his friend. "That's Madeline, a seat holder for the Demon Night in the Tower of Sin! Anyone who looks at her with impure thoughts gets burned. They say she's only a tenth-level War Lord, but her soul has already reached the Fighting Soul Realm."
"The Fighting Soul Realm? My god… Some War Sages don't even reach that level!"
"And a soul that powerful is said to be completely immune to attacks from an ordinary one."
"So those idiots just burned their own eyes out staring at someone who could have killed them without blinking. Serves them right."
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHOIR]
Madeline’s gaze swept over the wall of names, her expression tightening almost imperceptibly. She'd heard the rumors—that a mere tenth-level human War Fighter had claimed a spot in the first row—but seeing the proof was something else entirely.
A human? she thought. How could he be so gifted? He’s even stronger than our Amber.
She studied the name for a long moment. Around her, the crowd kept its distance. Even Malcolm, who had been radiating contempt at everything in his path, had gone slightly quieter since she arrived.
Her eyes shifted to Fabian and Jarrod, who had moved to the front when she arrived.
Fabian — one of the four human seat holders, a level-ten War Lord in white — stepped forward. Across from him, Malcolm, the Ogre seat holder who had just declared Zane's death sentence, watched him with open contempt.
"Madeline," Fabian began, his voice firm. "The Fourteen Races' Agreement established these rules. Are we really going to stand by while the Ogres just ignore them?" He then turned to the crowd, his voice booming. "If any of you has the skill to kill Zane in the arena, we humans won't say a word. But if anyone tries to harm him outside of it, you'll have to answer to us!"
He was appealing to her directly, and everyone present knew it. The humans had four seats to the Ogres' twenty-plus. Without the Demon Night, they had no leverage. Fabian's argument was sound, but sound arguments meant nothing without power to back them.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
Everyone waited.
Madeline smiled faintly. It was the kind of smile that seemed to reach right into you.
"The Demon Night will not be getting involved in this matter," she said.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
The words hit the humans like a physical blow. Fabian's expression didn't change, but Jarrod's jaw tightened. Elder Kingsley and Olivia exchanged a look.
Olivia felt the bottom drop out of her stomach. She'd known it was a possibility. She'd told herself to prepare for it. It didn't help.
Madeline's reasons were her own, but they weren't hard to guess. The Demon Night and the Ogres were rivals, yes — but on this particular question, their interests aligned. Neither race wanted the humans to rise. The Demon Night had already watched Amber lift them once. Their own numbers were slow to grow, their foundations not as deep as the Ogres'. They couldn't afford another human prodigy reaching his potential.
It was easier to let the Ogres act. Cleaner.
Malcolm looked satisfied.
But then Madeline spoke again.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
"That said," she continued, her tone unhurried, "the rules of the Tower were established by all fourteen races together. If the Ogres intend to break them, there must be a price. Otherwise, what is the point of having rules at all? What is the point of holding a seat?"
She let that sit for a moment.
"Break the rules freely, and the Tower returns to what it was at the beginning — no order, no safety, every race hunting every other race's geniuses the moment they walked through the door. That serves no one."
It wasn't mercy. It wasn't sympathy for the humans. It was the logic of someone who understood that order, even imperfect order, was worth preserving — because without it, the Demon Night would eventually suffer too.
Malcolm understood that. His satisfaction flickered.
Malcolm's expression soured. "Fine. Compensation." He waved a hand. "The Flaming Place and the Dread Place will offer ten low-grade Relic-Class weapons. Zane dies, and we fulfill that promise."
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Jarrod’s face flushed with anger. "Ten low-grade Relic-Class weapons? What do you take us for? Beggars?"
A Relic-Class weapon was valuable, no question, but they weren't unheard of in the sanctuary—the Violet Heart Sanctuary alone possessed at least twenty of them. The human race wasn't about to sell out one of their own for ten weapons, not even for a hundred. A talent like Zane was priceless and had to be protected. Besides, a weapon is only as good as the warrior who wields it.
Malcolm's sneer deepened. "Ten weapons. That's the offer. This is not a negotiation, human. Understand?"
Fabian just scoffed and shook his head. "So you're dead set on ignoring the rules, then," he said, his tone dripping with contempt. "And you really think that pathetic offer is enough to make us hand over a talent like him?"
Malcolm opened his mouth to retort, but Boris calmly held up a hand, cutting him off. "There's no need to argue with them. I have another solution."
