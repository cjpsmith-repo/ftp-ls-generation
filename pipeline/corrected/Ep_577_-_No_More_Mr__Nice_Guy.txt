Ep 577 - No More Mr. Nice Guy
Word count: 950
[AMBIENT: TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Teresa’s goal was to reach the tenth floor of the Tower of Sin in a month and a half.
According to the Tower's rules, the absolute fastest anyone could reach the tenth floor was one month. But you had to be so powerful that nobody would even dare to challenge you from the moment you stepped inside.
In the past, the few who’d made it to the tenth floor in just a month were all legendary War Lords.
Some gifted individuals from different races would train in seclusion in their sacred lands until they reached the War Lord level. Only then would they enter the Tower of Sin for practical combat experience.
[SFX: DISTANT BATTLE SOUNDS]
War Fighters were confined to the first five floors, while War Generals battled it out on floors six through ten.
Unless they ran into another War Lord on their way up, no one would ever challenge these titans. With no opponents for a solid month, they could ascend to the tenth floor at the fastest possible pace.
But for a War General like Teresa, making it to the tenth floor in a month was a long shot. Other fighters on her level were clawing their way up, too. And every fight would slow her down, costing her precious days.
Teresa wasn't bothered by the thought of facing other War Generals; it was just part of the game. So far, it had been a piece of cake. Every War General she’d faced was a total pushover.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
But this guy was different. He was an eighth-level War Fighter. And what really threw Teresa for a loop was that he was human.
[SFX: DRAMATIC STING]
Since when did some cowardly human have the guts to challenge the noble Demon Night?
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
A murderous glint entered Teresa’s eyes. The contrast between her beautiful face and that deadly look was chilling enough to make a man’s heart stop.
"The stories don't do you justice. The women of the Demon Night truly are breathtaking," Zane said, slowly drawing his sword.
Teresa asked, raising a delicate eyebrow. "Is that a Relic-Class weapon? Since when did the Violet Heart Sanctuary get so generous, handing out relics to a lowly War Fighter?"
[SFX: SWORD DRAWN]
With a whish of steel, Zane brandished his sword, a faint smile playing on his lips. "I'm not from the Violet Heart Sanctuary."
Teresa let out a cold laugh. "Doesn't matter who you are. All I know is you're about to die."
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT DRUMS]
She rose to the balls of her feet and then shot forward. The powerful energy coursing through her glowed faintly, outlining her graceful figure against the arena floor.
[SFX: BATTLE SOUNDS]
Zane didn't retreat. He met her charge head-on, raising his iron sword and blocking her dagger with a single, unhurried movement.
Teresa recovered fast. Pulling back, she launched into her next technique. "Wild Storm Stab!"
[SFX: RAPID BLADE STRIKES]
Her dagger became a blur — stabbing out dozens of times in an instant, fast as a raging storm, aimed at every inch of him.
The dagger and sword collided again and again, a rapid-fire chorus of steel. The crowd went silent.
Then Teresa's burst ran dry. Her vital energy and physical strength had limits, and she'd pushed both to the edge.
Zane's sword pressed down, and suddenly they were face to face — close enough that the tips of their noses almost touched.
"How is this possible?" she whispered, horror flickering in her eyes.
"It's indeed quite ruthless," Zane said mildly.
"You're mocking me!"
[SFX: SCRAPING STEEL]
Teresa gritted her teeth and dragged her dagger along the edge of his sword, sparks flying. Then, quick as a snake, she slipped around to his side. In close quarters, her short blade had the advantage — she wasn't going to give him room to swing.
She stabbed at his neck.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
Zane dipped his head just enough. Her arm caught nothing but air.
"You're fast," he said with a casual smile, holding his iron sword at a slight angle. He was still using nothing but basic swordsmanship.
But the more Zane perfected his basic swordsmanship, the more his movements evolved along with it.
[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]
Teresa’s strike hit nothing but air. Zane ducked under her lunge, pivoted, and swung his iron sword in a flat, horizontal arc—aiming the blade right for her rear.
Watching from the side, Phoebe let out a tiny shriek, her hand flying to her mouth so she wouldn’t distract Zane.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]
Meanwhile, in the stands, a figure shot to his feet. He was a War Lord of the Demon Night.
[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
As a saint-level genius from the Demon Night's sacred lands, Teresa hadn't entered the Tower of Sin alone. Elders from her race were watching over her every step of the way.
The War Lord had been confident that Teresa’s current strength was more than enough to clear the first nine floors without breaking a sweat.
But this human kid challenging her put him on edge.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
[SFX: BATTLE SOUNDS]
To his utter shock, Zane was actually countering her. As the boy’s sword sliced through the air toward Teresa's backside, the War Lord's mind went blank. With the sharpness of a Relic-Class weapon, that boy could cleave her in two. He couldn't let her die. The clan elders would have his head.
The faces of the other creatures in the stands mirrored his disbelief.
Most of them couldn't process what they were seeing.
How could a talent like this exist among the weak humans of the main continent? They blinked, wondering if their eyes were playing tricks on them, but there was no doubt—Zane was a human War Fighter.
[SFX: SWORD SWOOSH]
[SFX: BLADE SLAP]
At the last second, Zane had twisted his wrist, smacking Teresa on the rear with the flat of the blade instead of the edge.
He had gone easy on her. But that was over now.
