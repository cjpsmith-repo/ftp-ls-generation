Ep 371 - Zig Zag

Word count: 1086

[AMBIENT: DEEP CAVE WIND]

[SFX: ECHOING HOWL]

A low howl echoed from deep within the cave, growing louder with each passing heartbeat. A moment later, a violent gust of wind blasted out of its mouth, whistling like the breath of some ancient behemoth.

[SFX: WHIPPING WIND]

The wind whipped at Lewis's black cloak, snapping the fabric with such force that it sounded like thunder cracks. His hair streamed behind him, and the pressure threatened to tear him from the platform entirely. But his feet remained planted on the stone as if they'd grown roots deep into the mountain itself. No matter how fiercely the gale screamed around him, he stood his ground, unmovable as a mountain.

"Look at that guy," one of the Cloud Hall disciples muttered, his voice barely audible over the roaring wind. His eyes had gone wide with disbelief.

The other disciples stood frozen, their earlier confidence shaken. They exchanged nervous glances, suddenly aware of how severely they'd underestimated this cultivator from the Providence Sect. None of them had expected him to possess the raw power needed to face the full fury of that wind head-on—let alone withstand it without even activating a defensive technique.

Zane watched from his position, a flicker of respect crossing his features. Lewis wasn't just enduring the trial. He was mastering it.

[SFX: DEEP WHOOSH]

Another deep whoosh echoed from the cave.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[SFX: WIND BLADES MATERIALIZING]

Just as Lewis braced himself, the wind suddenly intensified, howling with even greater fury. This time, it wasn't just a gale—hundreds of sharp wind blades materialized within the gust, shooting straight for him.

“Wind blades!” a Cloud Hall disciple shouted with a vicious grin. “Good! They'll tear him to shreds!”

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

Lewis’s face was hidden in the shadows of his hood, his expression a mystery. But as the wind blades hurtled toward him, he began to move in a way that defied logic.

A gale that strong should have knocked him flat, especially if he tried to fight it. But Lewis didn't budge.

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

Instead of being pushed back, he started moving forward, zigzagging across the platform. With this bizarre footwork, he weaved between the slicing wind blades. He wasn't just surviving—he was advancing, step by step, toward the red treasure box!

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane’s eyes were glued to Lewis’s movements. What is that footwork? he thought, his brow furrowed in concentration. He’s moving like a sailor… tacking against the wind! Suddenly, Zane’s eyes lit up with understanding.

An experienced sailor doesn't head straight into a strong current; they sail sideways, zigzagging to use the wind's own pressure to push them forward. It’s the same principle sailors have used for centuries. The boy in the cloak was using the force of the gale to propel himself, and the zigzagging motion was the perfect way to dodge the wind blades.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]

“Incredible,” Zane breathed, a slow smile spreading across his face. “That kid is brilliant.” He couldn't hide his admiration for how cleverly Lewis was navigating the gale.

[SFX: WIND DYING]
Lewis reached the red treasure box and seized it. The moment he did, the gale died. In the sudden silence, a black shape materialized above the open box—shifting, formless—then shot straight into Lewis's forehead. He went still, standing motionless on the platform for a long moment. Then, without a word, he turned and began climbing the cliff face toward the second stone platform.

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMURS]

Meanwhile, the color drained from the faces of the Cloud Hall disciples. Their smug smirks had vanished, replaced by slack-jawed disbelief. It was one thing to dodge the wind blades, but to actually advance against the gale?

The disciple who’d been blown off the platform earlier looked the most shaken. He’d been kicking himself for not being prepared, but now a cold dread washed over him. He realized that even if he’d withstood the first gust, the onslaught of wind blades would have torn him apart.

Claiming that the red treasure box was a prize most considered next to impossible.

[SFX: DRAMATIC STING]

“He'll be melted in seconds up there!” another disciple cried out.

[AMBIENT: CAVE ECHO]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Everyone watched as Lewis moved forward again, one careful step at a time. Every eye—Zane’s, the Cloud Hall disciples’, even the sect leaders’ watching from outside the Cloud Road—was locked on him.

They realized with a collective jolt that he was going for the legendary blue treasure box. The crowd was floored.

Years ago, the Head of Cloud Hall himself had only managed to claim the silver treasure box—just one level above this one. And now, some kid in a cloak, barely a man grown, was about to surpass him. No wonder the leaders of the other sects were watching with unconcealed envy.

[SFX: FLAMES EXTINGUISHING]

However, just as Lewis closed in, only a few feet from the blue box, the flames around it suddenly died.

This was his chance. Lewis was a quick study; he’d already figured out the cave's rhythm. Opening a treasure box shut down the traps on its floor—he’d learned that with the red box on the level below. He also knew there would be two waves of attack. Down there, it was a gale followed by wind blades.

Up here, the first attack had been a massive blast of fire. He knew he couldn't possibly withstand whatever came next. 
[SFX: STONE SCRAPING]
Rather than waiting out the fire, Lewis had already been moving. He pressed himself flat against the platform, a shimmering barrier of condensed Vitality coating his body—just enough to hold back the searing heat. Inch by inch, he crawled forward through the inferno, the tongues of flame roaring inches above him.

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

The moment the flames vanished, he bolted for the blue treasure box. Every muscle in his body screamed with urgency—he had to grab it before the second assault began.

Time seemed to slow. In the blink of an eye, Lewis lunged forward, closing the final distance with desperate speed. His heart hammered against his ribs as his hand shot out, fingers stretching toward the cool metal of the blue treasure box. The legendary prize was within his grasp. Glory, recognition, power—all of it hung in the balance of this single moment.

Just as his fingertips hovered inches away from the box's surface, a deafening sound shattered the silence.

[SFX: DEAFENING BOOM]

The color drained from Lewis's face. His survival instincts overrode his ambition in an instant. He recoiled like a startled rabbit, throwing himself backward in a desperate retreat, his earlier confidence evaporating. The blue treasure box, so tantalizingly close moments ago, now seemed like a death trap he'd narrowly escaped.

Suddenly, a massive fireball erupted from the mouth of the cave.

[SFX: FIREBALL ERUPTION]
