Ep 947 - Wipe Them Out

Word count: 1157

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The absolute calm of the two Spirit Supreme masters was unnerving. The Ogres tensed, feeling the crushing weight of a mountain settle on their shoulders.

“Alright, enough small talk. Let’s get down to it,” Dorothea said sharply, which seemed to annoy the young man in black. He quickly changed the subject, his gaze landing on the tall, burly Ogres. 

[SFX: BLADE SWING]

Giving the Black Nether Saber a casual swing, he said, “Good thing the race we’re supposed to wipe out is the Ogres. Never been a fan of them, anyway.”

Dorothea smiled. “What a coincidence. Me neither.”

In the upper world, no one would call the Ogres weak, but they were known for being aggressive and way too pushy.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHOIR]

The Demi-Immortal Race had always been a neutral power in the upper world, with goals that set them apart. While races like the Humans, Demon Night, and Ogres were focused on expanding their own territories and influence, the Demi-Immortal Race was a mosaic, made up of the most talented individuals from countless races. They were a unique force in the universe, with a mission all their own.

Because the Demi-Immortal Race was both neutral and powerful, they got along with most races. They had solid relationships with the Humans, the Demon Night, and other major powers across the universe.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

The Ogres, however, were a serious problem. They were fiercely loyal to their own bloodline and refused to let any of their kin join the Demi-Immortal Race. If they ever found out an Ogre had defected, they’d brand them a traitor and hunt them to the ends of the earth.

The Ogres were stubborn and violent, too. They’d clashed with the Demi-Immortal Race several times, with the conflicts occasionally escalating to the point where even Supreme Lords got involved.

Now that a couple of their warriors had come down to the Lower World and stumbled upon these Ogres, there was no way they were just going to let them walk.

They were on a tight schedule. Even if they weren't here for Zane, they'd still jump at the chance to destroy the Ogres' sacred place.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Lamar's anxiety spiked as he listened to Dorothea and the young man in black. He had no idea where these masters came from, but he wasn't about to stand by and let his Flaming Place be destroyed.

Lacey and Chantel exchanged a tense glance. The outcome still wasn't certain. What if these two could be persuaded?

“Honored masters! Might I have a word?” Lamar asked, his rough voice trembling.

“Hm? What is it?” the young man in black asked, glancing at Lamar without a hint of interest.

“This is just a grudge between our two sacred places and him.” Lamar pressed on, knowing how weak his argument was but having no other card to play. “The conflict between our clans is ours to resolve. Honored masters, we ask only that you stay out of it. The Flaming Place will offer everything we have — all our clan's treasures — as a gesture of respect.”

The young man in black yawned. He raised his broadsword and let a slow, cold smile cross his face. “I have no interest in the treasures of a small holy land.” His eyes settled on Lamar. “I'm more interested in slaughtering Ogres.”

“Do it,” Dorothea said simply. The flower in her eye began to spin.

[MUSIC: DESPERATE SCRAMBLE]

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

Seeing that begging had failed, Lamar, Aldous, and Reuben made their decision in the same instant. Vitality burst from all three of them simultaneously, and they broke in three different directions, fleeing at full speed.

“Escape?” The young man in black's voice was cold. His body became a black shadow — circled once — and vanished.

[SFX: BLADE FLASH]

In the next moment, he reappeared beside Reuben. 

No one saw the strike. All they saw was a flash of black light, and then Reuben's massive body was tumbling toward the ground, trailing clouds of blood mist.

He was already gone before he hit the earth.

The young man in black turned into light again and shot toward Aldous.

[MUSIC: EERIE SLOW PURSUIT]

Dorothea didn't hurry. She followed Lamar at a leisurely pace, her hands clasped behind her back, the flower in her eye glowing faint green.

The light washed over Lamar. His speed began to drop. Then his head turned — slowly, then frantically — and he started swinging his ghost staff at empty air, roaring at things no one else could see.

[SFX: FRANTIC ROARING]

[SFX: CRACK, BLOOD BURST]

The roars came faster and faster. Then — a single wet crack — and an arrow of blood burst from Lamar's chest. His heart had exploded on its own. The jet of blood shot dozens of feet before falling.

He dropped from the sky, eyes still wide open. Whatever he had seen in those last moments, he'd taken it with him.

[MUSIC: STUNNED SILENCE]

“It was an illusion,” Lacey whispered.

“He was so frightened his heart gave out,” Chantel said quietly. Even as enemies, they both felt the chill of it.

[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD]

[SFX: FLEEING FOOTSTEPS]

With all three Soul Sea masters dead in seconds, the remaining Ogres didn't wait. They scattered in every direction, breaking formation and fleeing. The Demon Night gave chase, morale surging.

Lacey and Chantel held back from the slaughter. The battle was already decided, and they both knew what came next.

Before Zane could even say anything, Lacey gave the young man a respectful bow. “Master, the Demon Night will never forget this. We don't know what materials you need, but we'll do everything in our power to help you find them.”

The young man in black chuckled. “Ah, they’re nothing too rare,” he said, his tone surprisingly polite. “You should deal with those little rats first.”

Zane eyed him suspiciously. The guy was usually a total ass who never had a nice thing to say. Why was he being so polite to the Demon Night?

The confusion must have been written all over his face, because Dorothea chuckled and whispered, “The woman he fell for is from the Demon Night.”

It all clicked into place for Zane. So that's why the guy was acting so weird. The Demi-Immortal Race was full of prodigies from every race, so it made sense there would be Demon Night members among them. 

He was in love with a Demon Night girl, so of course he was going to be nice to her people. And no wonder he was so eager to kill the Ogres.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Just then, Chantel spoke up, her face etched with worry. “Lacey, I don’t know what’s going on with Amber. If the Dread Place really went after her… I’m afraid she could be in real danger.”

A flash went through Zane’s eyes as he sifted through the memories of the world. A moment later, he let out a sigh of relief. “Amber’s fine,” he said with a smile. “She’s already on her way back.”

He lifted his gaze to the horizon. 

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Sure enough, a few minutes later, a streak of light shot across the sky, heading right for the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary.
