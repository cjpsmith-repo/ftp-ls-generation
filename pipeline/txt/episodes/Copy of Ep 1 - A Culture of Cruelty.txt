Ep 1 - A Culture of Cruelty
Word Count: 1251
[AMBIENT: DAMP CELLAR]
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
[SFX: LONG SCRATCH]
Zane Lowell etched another tally into the damp cellar wall—seven hundred and thirty days since his father's assassination. Seven hundred and thirty days since his transformation from heir to slave within these once-familiar walls.
Vengeance now consumed his every thought. He had just turned seventeen.
The oil lamp cast dancing shadows as he extracted his worn journal, its pages brimming with schemes that would chill his uncle's veins.
"Just one month to complete the Codex of Ascension," he murmured, his unnaturally luminous eyes transfixed by the wavering flame. "Repaying cruelty with kindness..." A harsh laugh escaped his throat. "Such nobility delivered my father to his grave."
[SFX: DOOR RATTLE]
The unexpected rattle of the cellar door cut his words short. Their early arrival signaled something unusual.
[SFX: LAMP EXTINGUISHING]
He quickly blew out the lamp and pulled the shabby cotton quilt over himself.
[SFX: DOOR FLYING OPEN]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
The door flew open, and heavy footsteps stomped toward him.
The man in charge kicked the side of Zane's bed. "Still sleeping?" he shrieked. "Dreaming of being the Young Master again? Get your lazy ass out of bed!"
He was a steward for House Lowell, a sour-faced man with a wart on his forehead so large it was hard to look away.
[SFX: RUSTY BEDSPRINGS]
Zane sat up, feigning sleep as he rubbed his eyes. He threw off the quilt, swung his legs over the side of the bed, and planted his feet on the cold floor.
He dressed in silence, his movements methodical. His clothes were old and threadbare, but he kept them neatly folded—a small act of order in the chaos.
[SFX: CHAINS RATTLING]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
The steward signaled, and men quickly forced Zane into leather armor and shackles before leading him to the Lowell Training Hall.
House Lowell controlled vast resources in Castermere County, yet remained insignificant within the Eastern Territory's hierarchy.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR ESTATE]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Guards escorted Zane through luxurious grounds—a daily journey reminding him of wealth forever beyond his reach.
The Training Hall stood as an imposing open-air arena flanked by marble lion statues, its black basalt floor emanating strange energy.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]
[SFX: COMBAT SHOUTS]
In the center of the hall, dozens of Lowell kids in identical gray robes practiced their forms under a teacher's watchful eye. Their shouts echoed as their fists sliced through the air. To earn a place in the family, every child had to train relentlessly.
[AMBIENT: COLD AUTUMN WIND]
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
Ten-year-old students sweated in the cold autumn wind, creating a misty haze.
Across the hall stood men like Zane—shackled, bruised, and dressed in leather armor. Guards pushed him into their line.
[SFX: CLANKING SHACKLES]
These men, mostly criminals purchased from prisons, served as practice targets for Lowell children. Many had been maimed or killed over time.
Unlike them, Zane was House Lowell's eldest son—once respected as Young Master until his father was poisoned by his brother two years ago. Framed as a traitor's son, he became a slave, beaten by children who once bowed to him.
Then, two years ago, his world was shattered. His father, the head of House Lowell, was poisoned by his own brother. In the chaos that followed, the other three branches of the family framed his father as a rebel. His own family, unable to fight the accusations, was stripped of all power.
Zane Lowell, the former Young Master, was branded a traitor's son and became a slave. A human punching bag for the very kids who once groveled at his feet.
He'd been living this nightmare for two years. He'd lost count of the blows, the insults, the humiliations.
[SFX: BOOMING VOICE]
"Alright, that's enough striking practice for today!" the instructor boomed. "Go ahead and pick your targets! Hitting a live person is the only way to understand real combat. You'll learn the weak points of the human body, and how it feels to break them!"
[SFX: THUDS AND WHIMPERS]
After the instructor finished, the kids descended on the line of slaves. The hall quickly filled with the sickening thud of fists on flesh and the pathetic whimpers of men begging for mercy. To the Lowell kids, these slaves weren't people; they were objects.
More than a few made a beeline for Zane. There was a special kind of sick satisfaction in beating the former Young Master.
As it became his turn to be a punching bag, Zane calmly moved to protect his vital organs. After two years of this, the motions were second nature.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
Before long, a group strode through the entrance, led by a young man in expensive, tailored clothes.
"The Young Master is here!"
"Young Master! You've returned from your seclusion! You look refreshed, your progress must have been immense!"
"Our Young Master is a true genius, the future of House Lowell! You must be so much stronger now. Surely you've reached the Bone Refining Level!"
The kids who saw the young man enter stopped their practice and swarmed him, showering him with compliments. It was obvious they were all desperate to get on his good side.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Zane's gaze fell on the young man, and a cold, quiet rage began to coil in his gut.
Perrin Lowell. His cousin. The same age as him. And now, with Zane a slave, the new Young Master of House Lowell.
Zane heard Perrin had been training in seclusion for some time. It looked like it paid off.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
Perrin, sharp as ever, felt Zane's hateful glare and turned, his eyes locking with Zane's. A smirk played on his lips as he walked straight over.
"Zane Lowell," his voice dripped with condescension. "I've been gone a long time. I have to say, I didn't expect you to be alive when I got back."
"Sorry to disappoint you," Zane mumbled back, his voice low.
"Who do you think you're talking to? What was that tone?"
"You don't get to speak to the Young Master like that! Get on your knees and apologize right now, or you'll regret the day you were born!" Several of the Lowell kids started shouting, acting as if Zane had personally offended them.
Zane's eyes swept over them, his expression a blank mask. He recognized them all—the same kids who used to trail after him like lapdogs, too afraid to even breathe in his presence. The moment he fell, they had scrambled to become Perrin's new lackeys.
Perrin held up a hand, silencing his fawning defenders. He turned back to Zane, a smug grin spreading across his face.
"Zane, do you have any idea why I went away to train?" Perrin's voice dropped to a whisper only Zane could hear. "It was because of you."
A flicker of emotion disrupted Zane's carefully maintained façade.
"The elders harbor suspicions," Perrin elaborated, his fingers caressing the jeweled hilt of an ornate dagger sheathed at his belt. "They believe you remain a threat. That you may have... inherited certain formidable talents from your disgraced father."
Perrin leaned forward, his breath ghosting against Zane's ear as a glacial smile crept across his features. "And they've granted me authority to verify those suspicions today. With everyone bearing witness."
[SFX: MASSIVE DOORS SLAMMING SHUT]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS CRESCENDO]
The massive training hall doors crashed shut behind them, the ancient wooden bar sliding into place with a resonant thud that echoed like a death knell throughout the chamber.
