Ep 1068 - Supreme Lord Sword

Word count: 1006

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATION]

Zane’s eyes widened at her words. “Sword Intent with no flaws? That’s actually possible??”

Even though Zane had mastered the Apex of Sword Intent and created his formless sword, he wouldn’t dare claim his own was flawless. Still, the formless sword had no set style. Anyone who wanted to break it would practically have to move heaven and earth. Not that it was impossible, but so far, Zane had never met anyone who could.

“Of course. You think the sword intent of the Master of Killing Sword Mountain would have flaws?” Layla said, puffing up with pride.

“The Master of Killing Sword Mountain?” Zane’s eyes glinted with curiosity.

Layla pursed her lips. He really is from the Lower World, she thought. He doesn’t know a thing.

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHISPER]

Suddenly, Lavender’s voice echoed in Zane’s mind. She’s talking about Supreme Lord Sword. He’s one of the most powerful Supreme Lords in the entire universe. His sword is embedded at the very peak of Killing Sword Mountain. If you have questions, ask me. Don’t ask that woman again!

Needless to say, Lavender couldn’t stand the sight of Layla.

But Lavender didn’t know much about the dealings of mortals, things like the splintered Orange Sea World or the event they called “Passing the Torch.” Those were internal Alliance affairs, after all. But Supreme Lord Sword and Killing Sword Mountain? How could she not know about them?

[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Zane gave a slight nod, but his eyes were already drawn back to the poem carved into the massive rock.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Each character was hundreds of feet tall and wide. As he drew closer, he could feel a razor-sharp sword intent radiating from the stone. The warriors closest to the rock had already been forced to look away, unable to bear the pressure from the giant words.

But Zane’s gaze only intensified.

[SFX: WHOOSH ENERGY WAVE]

Under his focused stare, the massive characters seemed to ripple. A split second later, a wave of pure sword intent shot straight for him.

The wave of intent was like a faint shadow. Zane stared hard, but he could only make out a vague outline.

[SFX: SHARP PIERCING SOUND]

He didn’t fight back. He simply let the sword intent wash over him. The moment it pierced his body, a sharp jolt ran through him—it felt exactly like being stabbed with a real sword.

So that’s why everyone was so uneasy looking at these words, he realized. The invisible sword intent.

[MUSIC: INSPIRATIONAL RISING TONE]

A spark lit up in Zane’s eyes.

Up ahead, Yolande was already moving, leading the group around the boulder. “Our destination is just behind this rock,” she called back — and then she noticed Zane had stopped.

“Hey.” Layla turned. “What are you doing? Are you just going to stand there?”

Zane was intrigued. Could he use this invisible sword intent to temper his own?

He didn’t keep it to himself. He reached back and drew Thundergust, leveling it toward the carved words. “I can see faint sword intent in the poem,” he said. “I want to try using it to temper my own.”

Yolande and Layla both stared at him.

[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]

“Impossible,” Layla said softly. “Is the legend true?”

Yolande’s expression shifted — complicated, unreadable. When she’d first brought Zane into the Mist Palace, she’d told herself to expect anything from him. She’d meant it. But she still wasn’t quite prepared.

Because of their reaction, Zane’s concentration broke. The pale sword intent shadows vanished instantly. He could only see them under intense focus — the moment his mind slipped, they were gone.

He looked at them, puzzled. “What?”

Yolande steadied herself. “Can you actually see them? What do they look like?”

Layla said nothing. Yolande had already asked exactly what she wanted to ask.

“Pale white swords,” Zane said. “About two feet long. Quite short.”

Yolande exhaled slowly. “Two feet. White.” She nodded. “That’s the Bright God Sword — Supreme Lord Sword’s blade. Two and a half feet, white as light. The sword of one who could play chess with the gods.”

[MUSIC: REVERENT STRINGS]

Lavender’s voice came quietly into his mind before Yolande could continue. Supreme Lord Sword had no successor. His sword technique died with him — or so everyone believed. The legend said he’d carved it into this poem. That if anyone could see the sword intent hidden in the words, they could comprehend it. Inherit it. A technique that could play chess with the gods, sealed inside stone, waiting for someone worthy enough to read it.

It had driven swordsmen across the universe to ruin. Some had spent every Sovereign Stone they had just for the chance to stand here.

[AMBIENT: ECHOING TEMPLE]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE FLUTE]

“There were warriors with incredible perseverance,” Yolande said softly. “Ones who were willing to spend their entire lives here, no matter how grueling it was, just to comprehend Supreme Lord Sword’s intent.”

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT MEDITATION GROUND]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CONTEMPLATIVE]

Some warriors from the Life-and-Death Realm spent their entire existence in front of this giant rock. They just sat and meditated, trying to visualize and understand, hoping that one day they might unravel the poem’s mysteries. Sitting in one spot for tens of thousands of years would wear the very ground down, forming a pit.

Zane sucked in a sharp breath. “Did anyone ever actually succeed?” he asked.

A small, sad smile touched Yolande’s lips as she shook her head. “No. Not a single one.”

“What? They spent their entire lives here and still failed? That’s… such a waste,” Zane sighed.

“Think of them like gamblers,” Yolande said quietly. “The more they lost, the more stubborn they got. In the end, they couldn’t walk away from the table and just… wasted their whole lives here. They were betting everything on learning some ultimate swordsmanship. But they lost, and they had to live with that bitter truth.”

“Still, that’s kind of admirable,” Zane said.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]

But Layla frowned. “Not everyone accepted it so gracefully. Some of them couldn’t. Right before they died, after going through the Five Aging Processes, a lot of them blamed Supreme Lord Sword. They say they carved their own words here somewhere, but I’ve never seen them.”

Yolande shook her head and pointed. “They’re right over there.”
