Ep 690 - A New Contender

Word count: 1130

[AMBIENT: TAVERN INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

The warriors stared, completely speechless, at the supreme Aether crystal glinting in front of them. A treasure like that was a fortune, even for fighters at the Soul Realm.

But for Zane, making money was second nature. Back in the Eastern Territories, he’d used Celestial Essence to rack up Crystal Cubes like it was nothing. After arriving in the Heartlands, he’d mastered the art of drawing divine textures—a skill so rare that any sect would pay a king’s ransom for him to design their protective arrays. A Third-Grade sect like the Sea Wyrms didn't even have the standing to make such a request.

[SFX: NERVOUS GULP]

One of the warriors swallowed hard. "But… this is way too much," he stammered.

"Pay the tab," Zane said. "The rest is yours."

[SFX: EXCITED GASP]

Their eyes lit up. "For real?!"

Zane nodded, confident the offer was more than enough. To his surprise, though, they all shook their heads.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Their excitement died as quickly as it had flared. This had to be a trick. No one just gives away a supreme Aether crystal. Either Zane was a fool or he was setting a trap—and he definitely didn't look like a fool.

Seeing their hesitation, Zane sighed. "I don't have any special requests. I just want to hear the story about the Kensington girl," he said. "Tell me, and the crystal is yours."

One of the men blinked, still doubtful. "You want to know about Miss Cora? Is that really all?"

Zane gave a firm nod. "Yes."

A strange look passed between the warriors before one of them leaned in. "You just got to Ethereal City, right?"

"Yeah, just arrived today."

"No wonder you're asking," the warrior said with a short laugh. "It’s not exactly a secret. The whole city is talking about it."

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane’s expression hardened. "Don't tell me," he said, his voice dropping low. "This Cora… she has the Violet-Core Physique, doesn't she?"

The warrior’s eyes widened. "Whoa, how did you know?"

[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]

"Just a guess," Zane murmured, his gaze distant. The words sent a jolt through him. Elara. His sister was his one true weakness, the only thing that could rattle him. He’d killed Marilyn and thrown a wrench in Maddox's plans  he was still at the Soul Realm, just to keep her safe. Who would have thought another person with a Violet-Core Physique would pop up here, in Ethereal City of all places?

"Miss Cora has it, all right," the warrior confirmed, his voice now grim. "Maddox has his eye on her, and everyone in the city has been on edge. We all figure it's only a matter of time before he leads the Corrupted Divine Kingdom here to take her."

A headache was already starting to throb behind Zane’s eyes. "Then someone needs to marry her, and fast."

The warrior gave a wry smile. "You don't know the half of it. Do you know who she is?"

Zane frowned. "What does it matter who she is?"

"The Ethereal Spirit Sect's Elder Xander — he was born a Kensington. Has a wife and children in the family. The girl is his great-granddaughter."

"Ah." Zane saw the shape of the problem immediately. Even so, he said, "This is too important to let sentiment get in the way. Great-granddaughter or not, she has to be married off. Surely everyone understands that."

"Everyone does," the warrior said, his expression helpless. "Except her. The family has arranged introductions — good men, talented disciples from the sect itself. She turns them all down flat. Says she hasn't found anyone she actually wants." He shook his head. "The Kensington family spoils her something terrible. No one can make her do anything."

Zane fell quiet. This was Maddox's doing, at the root of it — and now the problem had followed him all the way to Ethereal City. He couldn't handle it the way he'd handled Marilyn. The Kensington girl wasn't Maddox's subordinate. She was an innocent, and the great-granddaughter of a sect leader, besides.

"Two days ago," another warrior added, leaning in, "we caught a spy. One of Maddox's men, poking around the city. The way we see it, he's already making his move. It could be any day now."

That settled it. What had started as an overheard conversation was now something Zane couldn't walk away from.

[SFX: CHAIR SCRAPING]

Zane stood. "The crystal is yours," he said and walked out without looking back.

Behind him, the sound of scuffling broke out almost immediately — the warriors fighting over it before he'd even cleared the door.

[AMBIENT: CITY STREET]

[MUSIC: PENSIVE STRINGS]

Outside, a faint light condensed beside him, and Lavender appeared at his side, drifting along at his pace. Passersby stopped and stared. She paid them no attention.

She looked at him with a small smile. "The answer is obvious, you know."

"Is it?" Zane said.

"Find a man for her." A pause. Then her finger swung toward him, unhurried and certain.

"Me?!" Zane shook his head, holding up his hands. "No. No way. I can't."

Lavender just blinked her big eyes and pouted. "So, what's the plan?"

"We'll wait and see for now," he said. The Kensington family's position made everything complicated. He found himself wondering what Elder Xander was actually planning to do — whether he intended to keep letting his great-granddaughter dig her heels in until Maddox came to collect her.

He had no idea that Elder Xander was, in fact, just as troubled.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

News that his great-granddaughter possessed a Violet-Core Physique had spread like wildfire, and there was no way to contain it. In the past, such a discovery would have been a cause for celebration. But these were dangerous times, and it was practically a death sentence. Maddox, the monster himself, just so happened to be looking for someone with that exact physique to complete his Soul Harvesting Ritual.

The leaders of both Cloud Hall and the World Merchants' Guild shared his dread and had already pushed Elder Xander several times to marry off his precious great-granddaughter.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

ElderXander was trying his best. He’d even suggested the clan’s master use house discipline to force her into obedience, but it was hopeless. No amount of coaxing or threatening could sway her. His great-granddaughter was impossibly stubborn, swearing she’d rather die than marry someone she didn’t love. It was probably all those romance novels she read, filling her head with foolish ideas about "true love."

Elder Xander was an old man; he couldn't possibly understand what went on inside a teenage girl's head.

[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]

Regardless, she had to be married off, and soon. Their very survival depended on it.
