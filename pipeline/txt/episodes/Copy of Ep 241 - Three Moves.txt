Ep 241 - Three Moves
Word Count: 1179
[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT ARENA]
Master Silas' lips curled with disdain as he surveyed the arena. "Ha! The Misty Peak disciples are still competing? I would have guessed they were eliminated before meeting any worthy opponents. How sad for them," he remarked, his voice laden with mockery that echoed across the tournament grounds.
He had the confidence of someone whose favorite protégé was about to compete.
York appeared from behind his master, rolling his shoulders as he stepped forward. His muscles rippled beneath his training garments while he performed a series of calculated stretches. A cold intensity radiated from his eyes as they locked onto Zane, dissecting his opponent's posture and bearing with the focus of a predator sizing up its prey.
"Three moves," he assured Silas with unwavering conviction. "I'll take care of that newbie in three moves, nothing more." He smiled, already savoring the admiration that would follow his swift victory.
To York, confronting a Level Two Adept like Zane seemed laughable. His abilities far surpassed Zane's modest power. True, Zane had demonstrated remarkable raw strength earlier, but what advantage would that provide against York's refined techniques? Brute force became meaningless when faced with superior Vitality manipulation and lightning speed. "This kid would be a fool to try and fight back," York thought to himself.
Overhearing their exchange, Master Warren rubbed his face, frustration written in every line. He yearned to defend his disciples, but words failed him in the face of such arrogance.
Initially, he had harbored genuine optimism about Zane and Eliot's prospects. Then Eliot lost big in his first match, sustaining injuries that eliminated him from the next round. This disappointment twisted painfully in Warren's gut, leaving him no choice but to clench his teeth and endure York's taunts silently.
Such was the warrior's path. Strength commanded respect while weakness offered only two alternatives: survive or perish. At least within the Skyreach Sect, survival remained an option. Beyond these walls, even that mercy vanished.
[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]
Zane had overheard the conversation as well, and he couldn't resist commenting on York's plan. "Three moves, huh?" Zane inquired, arching an eyebrow. His expression remained perfectly composed, as if nothing in the world could disturb his tranquility.
But it didn't mean he was ready to accept his fate. Plenty of warriors had earned Zane's respect---Sir Kenneth, the Skyreach Sect's Head seated on the platform, was one of them. York, despite his skill, hadn't demonstrated anything so far that made Zane worry.
"Exactly. That's all I need to take you down. With luck, I might need only two," York replied dismissively, his laughter echoing across the arena.
Zane smiled. "Does that mean if you aren't able to take me down in three moves, you'll concede?"
York snorted. "I see what you're doing here, and your little moves don't scare me. Sure, challenge accepted. If you're still standing after three moves, I'll acknowledge defeat!"
"Excellent! York, remember your position as Sea Peak's premier inner disciple. You can dominate anyone here," Master Silas declared, unconcerned by his student's impulsive wager. He had full confidence York's power would be no match for a Level Two Adept. As far as he was concerned, York could vanquish Zane with a single strike, let alone three.
Zane nodded and strode toward the arena with measured steps, maintaining a dignified silence that conveyed more authority than any boastful declaration could have achieved.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
The crowd's murmurs swelled into a chorus of excitement as the next competitors entered one by one. Anticipation hung thick in the air, almost palpable. Soon, Zane stood facing York across the battle platform, their gazes locked in silent challenge while tension crackled between them.
"Let's see what the famous Misty disciple can do against real talent," York sneered, his fingers dancing through the air.
[SFX: SWIRLING SHADOWS]
Darkness gathered at his command, forming vicious, knife-edged shadows that encircled Zane with predatory intent. The slicing shadows whirled faster, their edges gleaming with malevolent purpose as they tightened their orbit around him.
Zane assessed the technique with practiced calm. He could have simply braced himself and taken their impact---these shadows, impressive as they appeared, wouldn't have done any real damage. But such passivity would only create unnecessary spectacle and prolong the inevitable.
[SFX: ENERGY SHATTERING]
Instead, Zane stepped forward decisively, his fists blurring with extraordinary speed as he shattered the shadows with bare hands. The dark constructs dissipated and evaporated into wisps of nothingness that scattered across the arena floor. A collective gasp rippled through the audience.
[SFX: AUDIENCE GASP]
"Impossible!" York snarled, his composure fracturing. He quickly regained his stance, channeling more Vitality into his hands. "That was just a test. Here comes true power!"
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
With practiced precision, York traced complex patterns in the air, his fingers leaving trails of blue-black energy. The energy coalesced into five writhing shadow snakes that shot toward Zane from different angles, their fangs dripping ethereal venom.
Zane didn't retreat. Instead, he pivoted gracefully, his hands glowing with subtle power. As each serpent struck, he caught them mid-lunge, crushing their spectral forms one after another. The final two serpents dissolved into mist between his fingers, their essence dispersing like smoke in the wind.
"That's two moves spent," Zane announced. His smile remained unwavering, touched with just enough confidence to unnerve his opponent. "You have one move left."
[MUSIC: RISING STAKES]
York's face contorted with disbelief and mounting fury as the implications of his predicament dawned on him.
The sheer force behind his strikes was undeniable. He knew that no one in the crowd would forget this display of prowess.
While Zane's extraordinary strength wasn't entirely unexpected---they had witnessed it previously---his ability to destroy Vitality-infused slicing shadows with nothing but his hands commanded universal attention. The masters from rival peaks watched most keenly, recognizing the need to warn any disciples who might later have to go up against Zane and his formidable fists.
"Yes, Zane!" Master Warren roared enthusiastically from the sidelines, finally exhaling the breath he'd been holding.
Since arriving with the Misty Peak disciples, he had endured constant derision. Now, at last, he could stand proud. Yet his relief remained tempered by caution. "One move remains! Stay vigilant, Zane!"
"Finish him, Zane! Show him what Misty Peak can do!"
"He's nothing but empty boasts! If his final move fails, victory is yours!"
[AMBIENT: CHEERING CROWD]
The Misty Peak disciples cheered for their champion, their voices rising in unified encouragement.
Master Silas' expression darkened considerably. He couldn't fathom how Zane had conditioned his fists to such remarkable resilience, but witnessing it firsthand, he recognized that York faced a genuine challenge.
York's eyes narrowed to slits. Though he had gravely underestimated Zane, his swordsman's discipline prevented him from succumbing to agitation. Years of training allowed him to set aside distracting emotions and focus solely on the task at hand.
[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDUP]
After a moment of concentrated silence, York deliberately raised his blue-black antique machete, his voice resonating with cold, unwavering determination as he announced his final move.
"Mad-Monster Hacking! Break!"
