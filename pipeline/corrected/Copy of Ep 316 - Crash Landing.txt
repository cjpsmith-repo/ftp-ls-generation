Ep 316 - Crash Landing
Word count: 1070
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC]
Since embarking on the Monastic Path, Patrick had valued his emotional control even more than his physical strength. The discipline had become the cornerstone of his identity.
As the reincarnation of The Immovable Guardian, his composure was second to none throughout the realm. He confronted every challenge, regardless of its gravity, with a tranquility that others found both admirable and unnerving.
The ancient monastic wisdom taught that the Guardian lives in your heart, not out there in the world—meaning that a calm, centered mind was the essential foundation of their method. Patrick had internalized this teaching so completely that it flowed through his veins. As a master, his heart usually resembled a still, placid lake—reflecting everything around it while remaining unchanged by external forces.
[SFX: RISING TENSION]
Today, however, his tranquility was completely shattered. The placid lake of his heart was gone, replaced first by gentle ripples that quickly escalated into violent, crashing waves. Now, as the temperature around them skyrocketed, his composure transformed into an active volcano of emotion—spewing forth confusion, disbelief, and raw, unfiltered anger.
Patrick’s throat constricted painfully as he fought to control his ragged breathing. A metallic taste filled his mouth as he bit the inside of his cheek, drawing blood. His carefully cultivated serenity—the foundation of his identity—crumbled like sand beneath a tidal wave.
"How in the hell is Zane doing that?" he whispered, his voice barely audible above the roar of flames.
[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]
The Black Lotus Karmafire was the definition of hell on earth—impossibly powerful and indefensible. Patrick didn’t need anyone to explain just how lethal it was.
He would have felt better if Zane had used some secret weapon or a new technique to block the Karmafire. But that wasn't the problem. The problem was, Zane wasn't afraid of it. At all. It was like the flames were nothing to him. And the worst part? Whirlpools of energy were forming around Zane, actively sucking the Karmafire right into his body.
That was just impossible. It was a complete violation of everything he knew.
When he fought Finn, Patrick hadn’t shown his full strength. He'd been holding his best moves back because, in his mind...
[SFX: FIRE EXTINGUISHING]
[AMBIENT: QUIET ARENA]
Finally, the last wisp of fire vanished. The arena was clear again.
[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION]
A look of pure pleasure spread across Zane's face as a new Spirit Rune materialized on his chest, a gift from Patrick’s Black Lotus Karmafire.
That made three of them. His body had now been refined into a Top-Grade Spirit Weapon.
One more symbol, and his body might just become a Mythic weapon. He wondered what that would feel like.
After watching it all unfold, Patrick just shook his head. "That’s enough," he said, his voice clipped. He was throwing in the towel. Even with other moves left in his arsenal, if Zane could just absorb the Black Lotus Karmafire, there was no point in continuing.
He had to be the unluckiest guy in the world to draw Zane as a rival.
Zane had beaten Finn earlier in the tournament, too, but Finn hadn’t lost this badly—and Patrick was supposed to be the superior fighter.
A huge part of it was that Zane had countered, absorbed, or just plain ignored every single one of Patrick's deadliest attacks without even breaking a sweat.
First, there was the golden ward defense he'd used at the start, which had been shattered by Zane's insane power. After that, he'd used his Adamant Soul form, but even that couldn't scratch Zane’s Spirit Weapon body.
Then came the Shadow of the Immovable Guardian, which should have been a real game-changer—something Zane couldn’t just power through.
But right at that moment, Zane’s sword spirit, Lily, had appeared out of nowhere, unleashing her Malice in a devastating sword dance. Not even the Sword of Celestial Judgment was supposed to stand up to the Shadow of the Immovable Guardian, but Lily’s dance had shattered it with ease.
It had even caught Zane by surprise; he had no idea how or why Lily was suddenly so powerful.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE CLIMAX]
And then, Patrick played his ace in the hole: the Black Lotus Karmafire.
Anyone else who saw it would have surrendered on the spot. How could a human body possibly withstand that inferno?
[SFX: DRAMATIC IMPACT]
Patrick never could have imagined that his ultimate move would be the very thing that forced him to quit.
It just so happened that Zane wasn’t afraid of fire. In fact, his body craved it to refine and strengthen itself. The Karmafire was hell for anyone else, but for Zane, it was a trip to an amusement park.
He couldn’t have been happier. To Patrick's disgust, Zane soaked up every last drop of it like a guy chugging his favorite beer.
If it wasn’t for that one thing, Patrick wouldn’t have lost.
Zane started to say, "Patrick, don’t get discouraged. It’s not that I’m stronger. It’s just that your best moves..."
A streak of white light appeared in the southern sky, moving fast — too fast. It was closing in on the arena without slowing at all.
"Hey—" Zane started.
Too late.
[SFX: LOUD CRASH]
He was cut off by a sudden crash. A man had fallen from the sky, landing headfirst. His neck was bent at an angle that looked instantly fatal.
But the man just calmly grabbed his own head with both hands and twisted.
[SFX: BONE CRACKING]
With a sharp crack, it snapped back into place. He looked totally relaxed, almost comfortable.
The young man, who looked to be in his twenties, was handsome in a strange way. There was something off about his features—his eyes, nose, and mouth just didn’t seem to belong on the same face. The result was almost comical.
Crashing in front of everyone like that should have been humiliating.
But no one was laughing or questioning his power.
Anyone else hitting the ground headfirst from that height would have died on impact. The fact that this guy survived without a scratch was all the proof they needed that he was no one to mess with.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ENTRANCE]
Patrick sighed. The guy always had to make a chaotic, over-the-top entrance. Still, he greeted him politely. "Good to see you, Evan."
So that was him. The young man was Evan, the true number one disciple in the Skyreach Sect Rankings—and supposedly the most powerful disciple in the entire Sect.
Things were already interesting, but they were about to head in a whole new direction.
