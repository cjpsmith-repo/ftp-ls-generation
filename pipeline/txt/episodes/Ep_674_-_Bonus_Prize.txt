Ep 674 - Bonus Prize

Word count: 1164

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[SFX: DEEP STONE THUMP]

Zane’s fist slammed into the giant stone with a deep, muffled pulse that seemed to beat from the very heart of the rock. It wasn’t loud, but in the dead silence, everyone heard it.

[SFX: STONE CRACKING]

[SFX: RUMBLING COLLAPSE]

A moment later, a network of fractures raced across the massive boulder, making it look like a cracked eggshell. The lines spiderwebbed over its surface until, with a low rumble, the entire thing just gave up. The stone imploded, collapsing into a cloud of dust that billowed outward and swallowed Zane whole.

When the grit finally settled, the huge boulder that had stood before him was simply gone. All that remained was a fine powder coating the ground, as if it had never been there at all.

The green-skinned creatures watching from the city wall just stared, their mouths hanging open. It was at that exact moment that they seemed to realize this warrior wasn't someone to be trifled with.

[AMBIENT: FRANTIC ALIEN SHOUTS]

A wave of frantic shouts erupted along the ramparts in a language Zane couldn’t begin to understand.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Even Malcolm’s jaw went slack. To turn a stone that size into dust so effortlessly, he would have had to pour his Forceful Energy into the blow. But the rules of this strange little world were different. No one could use their Forceful Energy or Vitality, period.

Which meant Zane had done that with nothing but raw, physical strength. Malcolm felt a bead of sweat trickle down his temple. What kind of monster was hiding inside that man?

Yolande, however, seemed to know exactly what Zane was going for. She turned and called out to one of the creatures on the wall. After a brief exchange, she faced Zane again.

“Let’s go inside,” she said.

[AMBIENT: ALIEN CITY]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

They entered the city, its architecture alien to anything human. Green-skinned guards holding long spears lined the streets, surrounding them, but none of them dared to make a move. Zane’s little demonstration had clearly left an impression. After a few minutes, the creatures silently parted, clearing a path.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]

Three figures walked out from between the parted ranks. The one in front wore a reddish-gold crown and carried a brocade box held out before him at chest height. The emperor — that was clearly what he was — stopped in front of Zane and spoke.

Yolande translated. "He says they're willing to offer the sacred relic. But he has one condition."

"What condition?" Zane asked.

"He wants you to help them destroy a rival race. Something to the north."

Zane shook his head. "Not interested."

Yolande relayed the answer. A flicker of disappointment crossed the emperor's face. Then, slowly, he extended the brocade box toward Zane. Perhaps he understood that this object was never truly theirs to keep — that if it wasn't Zane standing here today, it would have been someone else eventually.

Zane took the box and opened it.

[SFX: SOFT MAGICAL HUM]

Inside, resting on a bed of cloth, was a long, narrow crystal — incomplete, like a shard broken from something larger. It gave off a faint but immense aura, as if something vast and ancient was coiled inside it.

Zane reached in and picked it up. It was cold.

"This is the Celestial Fragment?" he said. "Looks like an ordinary crystal."

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

[MUSIC: COSMIC SURGE]

The moment the words left his mouth, a violent roar tore through his mind. An overwhelming torrent of sensation poured in through his palm — the birth and death of stars, the turning of ages, billions of years of the universe unfolding all at once behind his eyes.

[SFX: SHARP CRACK]

Yolande's hand snapped out and knocked the fragment from his grip. It hit the ground.

"Your soul is strong," she said quietly. "But not strong enough. Not yet."

Zane steadied himself. "What is it actually for?"

Yolande looked at him for a moment, something unreadable in her expression. "You'll understand when the time comes. I hope you choose the right path."

Lavender's eyes narrowed. "Why don't you just say it plainly? You want him standing beside Lumen."

The room went still. Yolande served Lumen — everyone understood that now.

Yolande didn't deny it. "Zane has the destiny to fight for the world," she said. "Whatever path he walks will be the right one. That much I believe."

Lavender said nothing more. She turned her head away.

[AMBIENT: MYSTERIOUS CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: DISCOVERY THEME]

The brocade box didn't just contain the Celestial Fragment; nestled beside it was a small, intricately carved Array Plate.

“What is this?” Zane asked.

[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]

“It’s how we leave the Fog Array,” Yolande replied.

At her words, a wave of palpable relief washed over Esther Sterling and the others. They couldn't have cared less about some Celestial Fragment, but that small plate was everything. It was their only ticket out of this place.

[AMBIENT: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

[MUSIC: JOURNEY THEME]

With their prize in hand, there was no reason to stick around. They packed up their things, left the city of the green-skinned creatures, and got back on the trail.

“So how do we get out of the Mad Scorpion’s body?” Esther asked, her voice a mix of curiosity and exhaustion.

“The same way we came in,” Yolande answered flatly, taking the lead with unshakable confidence.

“If we can get out of this internal world, then the creatures from here should be able to leave too, right?” Zane asked suddenly. The thought had been nagging at him.

Yolande just laughed and shook her head. “The beings born inside the Mad Scorpion are part of the system. They have to play by its rules, so they’ll never even see the exit. But us? We’re outsiders. The governing force of this world is actively trying to push us out, which is what gives us a chance to leave.”

Time bled away in the fog. Before they knew it, a month had passed.

[AMBIENT: TOWER HALL]

[MUSIC: WORRIED TENSION]

Back at the Tower of Sin, the top-level War Lords from both the Demon Night and the human factions were gathered, their faces etched with worry. Elder May had been standing vigil by the tower’s exit every single day, but there was still no sign of Esther Sterling or her team.

“It’s been a month,” one of the Demon Night War Lords said, his voice tense. “If they don’t come back soon, the Dark Nether Death Light will flood the cave again.”

[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]

Once the Death Light reached its peak, any warrior left inside the cave would die. It had been that way since the Tower of Sin first opened its doors, and there had never been an exception.

In truth, they weren’t really worried about Esther Sterling and the others. The person who truly mattered to both the Demon Night and the humans was Zane.
