The question hung in the air, wrapping the arena in a tense silence. All eyes were locked on Zane.
Maybe it was because his background seemed even more mysterious than Yolande's, but the crowd was far more interested in what he had to say. They waited, breathless.
Zane didn't answer her directly. But her question told him one crucial thing: she knew about another Yolande.
"I think we've met before," he answered vaguely.
"Where?" Yolande pressed, a playful glint in her eyes.
Zane sidestepped the question. "We should talk sometime, if we get the chance," he said with an easy smile.
"Sure," Yolande replied, leaning closer. [MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FLUTE]
She took a small, deliberate sniff of the air near him. "I like your scent. You can find me anytime."
Her words confused the crowd. They sounded flirtatious, but she’d delivered them with a dead-straight face, a combination that struck everyone as deeply strange.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]
A faint smile touched Zane’s lips. Yolande had definitely made an impression, and he was curious about her mysterious past. Back in Alba Imperia, he’d heard she was from House Walker, one of the most powerful families in the Eastern Territories. But was that the truth? He knew nothing about her—not her history, not her background, nothing. If a chance to find out presented itself, he wasn't about to pass it up.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]
Just then, a few Ogres emerged from one side of the arena, led by Boris, the most formidable talent from the Flaming Ogre Sacred Place.
Boris had been on edge ever since the Ogres sent an assassin after Zane.
For a saint-level genius, the strongest of his kind, to be worried about a human… it was strange, to say the least. But Boris was a stubborn warrior; once he made up his mind, he never wavered. The moment Zane entered the Tower of Sin, Boris marked him as his rival.
He was determined to find an opportunity to challenge Zane. The thought had taken root, and he wouldn't let it go.
He should have advanced to the eleventh floor a month ago. He had the strength for it. But he hadn't moved. He'd stayed right here, on the tenth floor, and waited.
[MUSIC: WARRIOR THEME]
This was his warrior's spirit. In his mind, an opponent he'd chosen was his to defeat—or kill. No one else. It was precisely this fighting spirit that had made Boris the strongest talent his people had.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
That’s why he was so disgusted by the Ogres’ underhanded tactics, especially sending the Twilight Group assassin. As far as he was concerned, such a dishonorable method was a stain on the Ogre name.
Unfortunately, Boris had no say in the decision. Even with his father being the most influential member of the sacred Flaming Place, he was powerless to change a thing.
Boris was shorter than most Ogres — almost slight by their standards. The warriors behind him loomed like small mountains, all of them tenth-level War Generals. But their auras were nothing compared to his. Most Ogres gave off something wild and ancient, like the weight of an old mountain. Boris's aura was different. It was sharp. Like a blade pressed against skin — not yet cutting, but promising that it would.
Zane, by contrast, looked completely at ease. A faint smile, a relaxed posture, nothing to suggest the edge he carried. He'd learned, after everything he'd been through, how to keep that hidden.
Everyone in the arena knew who Boris was. He was considered one of the two strongest War Generals on the tenth floor — a fighter so powerful that even early-stage War Lords thought twice before crossing him. And everyone knew why Boris was here.
He was the Ogres' answer to Zane.
Boris stopped a few paces away. "I've been waiting for you," he said.
Zane's brow lifted slightly, and a smile crossed his face. "I've been looking forward to this too."
Boris held his gaze for a long moment. Then he said, "You haven't been to the Talent Tablet yet. Go. I'll be here when you get back."
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]
Zane turned to Olivia with a questioning look. She smiled. "The Talent Tablet is here in the Tower — every warrior who reaches the tenth floor can use it. It doesn't just measure your talent. It can actually raise it." She paused. "How much depends on luck."
It was clear the other creatures were just as curious. Zane was only a level-ten War Fighter, yet his strength was off the charts. He was already confirmed as a saint-level genius, but there were tiers even within that. Everyone was dying to see just how impressive his talent really was—and how high it could go.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Talent and luck, however, were much harder to change.
Luck was a mystery. Untouchable. No one could control it or even tell if someone was truly lucky. Maybe only God knew for sure. People said Zane had good luck on his side, but no one could say how much, or if it would hold out today. All they could do was guess based on what he'd accomplished so far.
Talent, on the other hand, was easier to understand.
A talented warrior trained faster. They could grasp difficult concepts at a glance and master new skills with little practice. These were the signs of incredible talent, ranked across different levels: human, earth, Celestial, saint, and divine.
But even that was just a rough guide. Zane remembered what Olivia had told him: the Talent Tablet could not only test and measure a warrior's exact talent, but it could even increase it.
The thought was still unbelievable to him.
He looked at Olivia, still trying to take it all in. “You're telling me this thing can change my talent?”
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
Olivia laughed. "It's true! The Talent Tablet in the Tower of Sin can actually change a warrior's talent. I can take you there right now!"
