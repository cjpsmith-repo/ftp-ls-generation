Ep 1126 - Heading North

Word count: 1196

[MUSIC: BOLD ETHEREAL]
For years, over a million people had filled the square, praying day and night. All they had to show for it was a large, spherical container, now only a third of the way full with a shimmering green liquid: their Faith Energy.
[SFX: DRAGON SCALES SCRAPING STONE]
Hidden behind a stone pillar, an Ancestral Dragon scaled the structure until it reached the top. The container was some kind of unique, Divine Weapon designed specifically to hold the Faith Energy, but that meant less than nothing to the dragon. It just opened its massive jaws and bit down hard.
[SFX: MASSIVE CONTAINER CRACKING]
The sharp sound echoed across the square.
[SFX: LIQUID GUSHING]
The dragon’s jaws had punched a hole right through the bottom of the vessel, and the green Faith Energy immediately began pouring into its open mouth.
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD SILENCE]
Every person on the square looked up, stunned into silence. The sacred vessel holding their faith was broken. The believers were frozen, their god—their everything—violated before their very eyes. How could this be happening?
[SFX: LOUD GULPING]
A moment later, the silence was shattered by loud, gulping sounds as they watched the Faith Energy inside the container visibly drain away.
[SFX: CROWD CHAOS]
The entire square erupted into chaos. To these believers, the outside world meant nothing. Only their faith mattered, and the green liquid in that sphere was the physical proof of it.
It wasn’t just the believers, either. The powerful warriors inside the shrine were just as stunned. 
[SFX: WHOOSHING ROBES IN FLIGHT]
Several figures in white robes flew out from the shrine’s inner sanctum, their faces filled with horror and disbelief as they stared at the rapidly emptying container. The Faith Energy they had painstakingly collected for years was gone.
Some of them attacked. Law Power rumbled through the square as several Divine Realm experts launched strike after strike at the small creature clinging to the pillar.
[SFX: LAW POWER CRASHING]
Nothing connected. Every attack dissolved the moment it reached the Ancestral Dragon, as though the laws themselves refused to touch it. The dragon didn’t even flinch — it just kept drinking, its small body radiating a kind of lazy satisfaction.
The Radiant Light Sect didn’t guard the shrine that heavily. While the Faith Energy was precious, no warrior would ever try to steal it — it was completely useless to them. They simply had no defense for this.
[SFX: CRACK SPLITTING STONE]
When the last drop vanished, a thin crack split open in the stone floor at the edge of the square. The Ancestral Dragon squeezed out through it and launched itself into Zane’s arms with a series of happy gurgling sounds.
[SFX: Ancestral Dragon GURGLES]
Zane caught it. The little creature nuzzled against him, its eyes half-lidded, unmistakably pleased with itself.
Freya watched the chaos still rippling through the square. “You planned all of this,” she said. It wasn’t a question.
Zane just shrugged. “Some people have gone a bit mad. I don’t imagine their god will be much comfort to them right now.”
[MUSIC: QUIET RESOLVE]
The news spread fast. But the Radiant Light Sect wasn’t the only secret sect collecting Faith Energy in Auspice City — the human race had several, each gathering believers and accumulating their own reserves. When word reached the others that the Radiant Light Sect had been robbed, most of them quietly celebrated their rival’s misfortune.
Their celebration didn’t last long.
In the days that followed, every store of Faith Energy in Auspice City disappeared. 
By the time it was done, the dragon had eaten its fill. It crawled into Zane’s arms, curled up, and went completely still — as unresponsive as a hibernating snake, no matter how many times Zane tried to rouse it.
Lavender’s voice reached him quietly. “It has enough now. It’s beginning its first transformation. When it wakes, it won’t look like a seahorse anymore.”
Zane looked down at the small, motionless creature. A dragon that actually looked like a dragon. He filed that away and turned his attention to other things.
[MUSIC: SETTLED PURPOSE]
He spent some Sovereign Stones on a residence and settled in to wait. Freya used the time well. Over the next several days, while the dragon slept, she quietly gathered everything she could find about the Demon Night presence in Auspice City.
She came back with a clear picture. The Demon Night’s local sacred land was called the Violetwood Sanctuary — a tenth-tier holy land with fewer than a hundred thousand members in the city— a small number for a race of their size, but the Demon Night had never been comfortable dealing with outsiders. What trading they needed to do, they handled through a dedicated group of warriors assigned specifically to outside contact.
Most of them were clustered in the city’s northern district.
Zane had made Lavender a promise: he would help her reclaim her throne. Her plan was to gather resources for him while quietly pulling together her former followers. She had her Roaring Token — in better days, a single broadcast would have summoned hundreds of loyal Demon Night to her side. Things were more complicated now. An open summons was too risky. They would have to find another way.
Freya stayed at the residence. Zane went north alone.
[MUSIC: DARK MYSTERIOUS]
[AMBIENT: DISTANT CRACKLING FLAMES]
The city’s architecture was brutal, built from slabs of blood-red iron-flame rock. Faint red flames seemed to flicker deep inside the dark stone, giving everything a feeling of raw, untamed power. It was a stark contrast to the neat, orderly lines of human cities.
But in the northern district, where many of the Demon Night lived, the buildings gave way to a massive forest. 
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON TANGLED ROOTS]
The roads became narrow trails winding through the trees, with tangled roots. Anyone else would have found it impossible to navigate, but for the Demon Night, who had lived on their holy tree since birth, it was as easy as walking down a city street.
Still, other races didn’t just wander into their territory, so Zane’s sudden appearance drew more than a few surprised looks.
It was a good thing he had Lavender’s directions.
[MUSIC: AWE-INSPIRING STRINGS]
Following them, he soon found the center of the settlement: a colossal tree that soared a thousand feet into the air. This wasn’t a sacred place for the Demon Night, so there was no reason for a holy tree to be here, which only made its presence in the middle of Auspice City all the more mysterious.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
[SFX: SPEAR THRUST]
As Zane got closer, two Demon Night members stepped out from the shadows to block his path. One of them leveled a long spear at his chest.
[SFX: THREATENING VOICE]
“That’s far enough, human.”
[SFX: SUSPICIOUS VOICE]
“What brings you here?” the other one asked, her eyes narrowed. “Are you looking for someone?”
Only a handful of humans ever visited the Demon Night settlement, and it was always on business handled by a special group of warriors tasked with dealing with outsiders. 
A human walking in alone was not part of the plan.
