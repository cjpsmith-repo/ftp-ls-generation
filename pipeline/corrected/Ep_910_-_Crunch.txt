Ep 910 - Crunch

Word count: 1128

[AMBIENT: EERIE WIND]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Fiona was confused. She’d believed her father when he said the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land was probably just an empty city, a ghost town with nothing inside. But the moment she’d stepped foot in this place, her heart had started hammering against her ribs, as if some invisible weight was pressing down on her.

“What’s wrong, Fiona?” the War King asked, his voice laced with concern. He’d been trying to calm his daughter, but she didn’t look relieved in the slightest. If anything, she seemed even more wound up.

Fiona’s eyes were wide. “I think I heard something,” she whispered.

“Easy, Fiona. You’re just on edge,” Callan added, trying to sound reassuring.

She bit her lip. A knot of fear was tightening in her stomach, but she figured with her father and the other Soul Sea masters here, it couldn’t be that dangerous. She gave a small nod and kept following them, though the uneasy feeling clung to her like a shadow.

Zane drifted closer to her, frowning at the genuine anxiety on her face. He knew some people in this world were born with instincts that went beyond the normal, and with her True Phoenix bloodline, she might have been sensing something they couldn't.

They had only gone about eighty feet farther when the body Fiona was controlling went completely rigid.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS CRUNCHING]

[SFX: BONE CRUNCHING]

The crunching sound of bones being chewed echoed through the silence, just as she’d heard before.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Fiona whipped her head around, her gaze locking onto a nearby street. She froze, as if her feet had been fused to the stone pavement.

“Fiona!” the War King snapped, his brow furrowing in disapproval. His daughter had always been sharp and composed. A little naive, sure, but she was no coward. A coward would never have dared to challenge Zane, the man who had already taken down Logan. Several of the other masters shot her suspicious looks, clearly wondering what she was doing.

[SFX: BONE CRUNCHING]

The sound was sharp and clear in the dead quiet of the sacred land. It was the distinct noise of a predator gnawing on a fresh kill—a sound that belonged in a forest, not here. Everyone knew there were no living creatures in the Sacred Feather Sea. While a few had argued that something might be hiding in the ruins, most had dismissed the idea. The entire sea was sealed by the immense power of a blood sacrifice, after all.

“I can feel it,” Fiona said, her voice dropping to barely a whisper. Her dark red lips had gone pale. “Its presence. It's… it's my nemesis.”

A ripple of unease ran through the group.

Nemesis? What nemesis could she mean? The human race had enemies in the upper world — the Demon Night, countless other races — but no single race had ever stopped humanity's expansion. What could make a True Phoenix bloodline recoil like this?

Brianna's voice came quietly into Zane's mind. “That woman has True Phoenix blood, doesn't she?”

“Yes,” Zane replied. “What do you know?”

[MUSIC: ANCIENT MYSTICAL THEME]

“Since ancient times, dragons and phoenixes have fought. As the two supreme divine beasts of the universe, True Dragons and True Phoenixes have been natural enemies across countless eons. If she's sensing a nemesis — it's a True Dragon.”

Zane's frown deepened. The nine True Dragons lived in his mind, but he had never seen a real one in the flesh. And how could a True Dragon exist here? An adult True Dragon's body stretched for thousands of miles. It made no sense.

“Maybe the Ancestral Dragon Egg hatched,” Brianna said. Then a pause. “But… that shouldn't be possible either.” Even she didn't sound certain.

War King Wind turned to his daughter. “Your nemesis? What nemesis, Fiona?”

She shook her head, her whole body trembling now as if she stood in a blizzard. “I don't know. I just — it's very scary. Whatever it is.”

Zane stepped in front of her. He looked directly into her eyes. “Is it a dragon?”

The word landed like a match on dry wood. Whatever had been vague and formless in Fiona's mind suddenly snapped into focus. The color drained completely from her face. [MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

“Yes,” she breathed. “A dragon. And it's no ordinary one.”

The reaction that swept through the Soul Sea masters was immediate.

“The Ancestral Dragon Egg — it hatched?”

“We have to go back. If the Ancestral Dragon is loose in here—“

“This entire world could collapse. We need to leave now.”

Their goal had always been to retrieve the egg. A dormant egg was one thing. A living Ancestral Dragon was something else entirely — something even Soul Sea masters had no business touching. The plan was unraveling fast.

Just as the group began edging backward—

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

[SFX: CREATURE GNAWING]

Then they saw it. 

The creature wasn't very big. It had the head of a dragon set on a short, stocky body, like a miniature drake. It was crouched over a snow-white bone, gnawing away as a pair of long, ivory fangs jutted from the sides of its mouth.

“Is that a dragon?” one of the masters muttered.

“Doesn’t look like any dragon I’ve ever seen,” another replied.

“Even if it is, it’s nothing to be afraid of.”

The sound had startled them, but the sight of the small, almost comical creature made the tension drain from the group. Even though they were all piloting corpses, their senses were still their own, channeled through their souls. And one look was all it took to tell them that this little thing posed no threat.

“Fiona, you’re not scared of this little guy, are you? I’ll go grab it for you!” a master from the Wind Kingdom declared with a laugh. He urged the corpse he was piloting forward, rushing toward the tiny drake.

“Don’t go near it!” Zane yelled.

But the man was already moving, closing the distance in a flash. He reached out a hand, ready to snatch the creature up.

[SFX: DEAFENING ROAR]

The little drake suddenly lifted its head. It opened its mouth—a mouth that unhinged to a size far too large for its body—and let out a deafening roar.

[SFX: DISINTEGRATION BLAST]

The sound was immense, a physical shockwave utterly impossible for something so small. The blast was so powerful that everyone instinctively covered their ears as they watched in horror. The top half of the Soul Sea master’s corpse simply disintegrated, vanishing into a cloud of fine dust.

Crazier still, half of the massive stone house behind where he’d stood crumbled and dissolved, turning to powder in the dead, silent air.
