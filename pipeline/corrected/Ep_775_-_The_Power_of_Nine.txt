Ep 775 - The Power of Nine

Word count: 1093

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The sky above Oracle City had just become the most dangerous place in the Heartlands — and Lord Wallace, who had arrived expecting to crush insects, was only now beginning to understand what he'd walked into.

Once a warrior reached the Soul Sea Realm, they moved at speeds that were just unthinkable. Trying to catch one if you were at a lower level? Impossible. Even another Soul Sea warrior would have their work cut out for them trying to run down an equal.

Knowing this, the forces from the main continent had laid a trap, determined to keep the powerhouses from the York Divine Kingdom from getting away. Over a hundred elite warriors from the Demon Night were lying in wait nearby, hidden by one of their secret treasures: the Emerald Tree.

It was a neat trick. You scattered the seeds, and in just three days, they’d sprout into a small forest of person-high trees. The real magic, though, was that they completely masked the aura of anyone standing beneath their leaves. They were perfect for setting an ambush, but pretty useless for a quick getaway. After all, if you were running for your life, you didn’t have three days to wait for your cover to grow.

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY BURST]

 From among those very trees, more than a hundred powerful auras suddenly erupted, boxing in Lord Wallace, Master Abel, and the rest of their group.

[SFX: DOMAIN ACTIVATION]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC BATTLE THEME]

 Six Soul Sea masters from the Demon Night, along with Dorian and a few others from the Violet Heart Sanctuary, all unleashed their domains at once. A domain was a special ability you gained upon reaching the Soul Sea Realm, a unique pocket of power usually built from a warrior’s strongest techniques. The combined force of their domains slammed down, trapping both Wallace and Abel like flies in a jar.

The two masters from the York Divine Kingdom were completely blindsided. They never saw it coming. 

Zane had actually managed to gather nine Soul Sea warriors. 

In the entire union of seven divine kingdoms, you could count the number of Soul Sea masters on your fingers. Wallace and Abel were two of them, holding key positions in the York Divine Kingdom with prestige that outstripped even the Holy Maiden’s. Reaching that realm was like a mortal becoming a god. If it weren't for some recent cosmic anomaly, they would have already ascended to the upper world.

In the Heartlands, a single Life-and-Death warrior could turn the whole continent on its head. Wallace had known that. He'd been so certain this land was his playground that the sight of nine Soul Sea masters closing in around him felt like something out of a fever dream. One of the Life-and-Death warriors behind him actually rubbed his eyes. How was this possible?

[AMBIENT: DEADLY SILENCE]

 The nine Soul Sea masters said nothing. They simply held the encirclement. It was Zane who spoke, his voice quiet and unhurried, the heartstones raised in his hand one more time. "Last chance. Where do these come from?"

Wallace and Abel weren't listening. They were already calculating — angles, distances, which direction offered the best chance of breaking through.

But one of the Life-and-Death Realm warriors behind them was thinking about something else entirely. Soul Sea masters fought Soul Sea masters. In a battle at that level, he was cannon fodder. 

He didn't hesitate. "I know. Those are Ancient Heartstones. They come from the Feather Emperor's Secret Land."

Wallace and Abel spun around and fixed him with a stare that could have curdled iron. Wallace's lip curled. "You think he'll actually let you live for telling him that?"

The warrior flinched — but held his ground. "The Feather Emperor's Secret Land is in the center of the four Divine Kingdoms," he pressed on, his eyes on Zane. "In the Sacred Feather Sea."

Zane's mind was already moving. Ancient Heartstones. A named location. Not some obscure secret — something that could be found. That was enough.

He smiled at Wallace. "You were right. He answered first." He raised his voice. "Everyone — move. Leave that one alive."

The other Life-and-Death Realm warriors felt it in the same instant — a cold flash of regret. The Origin Stone of Life wasn't even a closely guarded secret. Any one of them could have said it. If they'd only spoken first.

There was no time to dwell on it.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

Both Wallace and Abel released their domains simultaneously. Wallace drove straight for Zane — threatening him directly was his only real path to survival. Abel's domain swept outward toward the Demon Night warriors.

Zane had no choice but to fall back. At his current strength, he was roughly the equal of a fourth or fifth-stage Life-and-Death warrior — formidable by any standard in the Heartlands, but nowhere near enough to stand against a Soul Sea Realm master head-on.

[SFX: DRAMATIC WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: HEROIC THEME]

 The moment Wallace closed in, a figure appeared between them. It was Dorian, and he looked almost divine himself.

He gave Wallace a faint, knowing smirk. "You should be fighting me."

[SFX: GROUND IMPACT]

 Wallace hesitated, taken aback by this new opponent. But the raw frustration of failing to kill Zane overrode his caution. He skidded to a stop, slammed his palm on the ground, and a flash of brown light shot across the battlefield. 

The clash between Soul Sea warriors had begun.

The Life-and-Death Realm warriors could only watch, frozen in a mixture of terror and awe. They knew better than to get involved if they wanted to live.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

[SFX: PULSING ENERGY WAVE]

 The light around Lord Wallace pulsed outward. "Bewitching Domain!" he roared, and the brown haze expanded at an incredible speed. Anyone not fast enough to escape was swallowed by it. 

 A fog instantly clouded their minds, filling them with an overwhelming, animalistic urge to attack anyone and everyone nearby. Caught in that light, they’d lose all reason and slaughter their own allies without a second thought.

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 Given enough time, Wallace could have covered all of Oracle City in his domain, turning it into a blood-soaked slaughterhouse in less than an hour.

But he couldn't do that today. Not while facing nine other Soul Sea warriors. They were on a completely different level, and his bewitching haze wouldn't touch them.

But Zane was a different story — and Wallace was already turning his attention back to him.
