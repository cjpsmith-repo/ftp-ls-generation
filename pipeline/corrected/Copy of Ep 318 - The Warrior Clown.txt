Ep 318 - The Warrior Clown
Word count: 1023

Evan dreaded running into Elara Lowell with the primal fear of a field mouse spotting a hawk’s shadow overhead, his confident demeanor crumbling whenever she appeared.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL POWERFUL]
Back in the day, Elara reigned supreme as Maiden’s Peak’s most exceptional disciple. Her talent was off the charts, and strong enough that she stood shoulder to shoulder with the Sect’s own Chosen, those rare individuals handpicked for greatness.
The contrast between Zane and Elara’s ascent to prominence couldn’t have been more stark—like comparing a sudden lightning strike to the gradual rising of the sun.
Zane had erupted into the collective consciousness virtually overnight, a phenomenon that bewildered the entire Sect. He emerged as a powerhouse seemingly conjured from thin air, his abilities fully formed and devastating. Before his dramatic appearance on the stage of power, only a select few—Leo Brandt with his analytical mind, the perceptive Zachary Anderson, and certain observant disciples from Misty Peak—had any idea just how talented and strong he really was.
Elara's rise, on the other hand, was a different story. She'd been granted special admission to the Skyreach Sect for her raw talent and was basically groomed for greatness from day one. The entire Sect watched over and protected her. In fact, she'd hardly faced a single real challenge her entire time there; Elara's only real trouble was the constant stream of admirers who sought her attention.
She wasn’t just talented; she was stunning, bordering on the ethereal.
Her genuine kindness toward everyone she encountered only magnified her appeal, turning admiration into devotion across the entire Sect. The Skyreach Sect’s leadership treasured her as one of their most valuable assets, recognizing how rare such a combination of ability, beauty, and character truly was. Like a celestial body with a strong gravitational pull, she unconsciously drew others into her orbit, leaving young men throughout the Sect captivated by her radiance.
The average disciples—those without exceptional strength, remarkable talent, or an impressive family name—simply admired her from afar, acutely aware of the unbridgeable gap between their stations. Many nursed private infatuations but understood the futility of pursuing someone so far beyond their reach.
Because of this, only disciples from The Seven Great Houses dared approach her with romantic intentions, their confidence bolstered by privilege and pedigree.
[SFX: VIOLENT IMPACT]
Knox Walker, the young master of House Walker, was her most relentless admirer. In the end, he paid a steep price for pushing his luck—Elara brutally maimed him. That was the official reason the Skyreach Sect gave for locking her away on Infernal Peak.
[MUSIC: NOSTALGIC REMINISCENCE]
Evan himself, one of the Sect’s own brilliant Chosen, had carried a torch for Elara back in the day. But Evan had a reputation for being a class clown, and the other disciples were always daring him to make a move, constantly giving him crap about it.
At the time, Evan was number one on the Skyreach Sect List—the strongest disciple in the entire Sect. Even Elara had a hard time dealing with him.
But Elara was smart. She played him, tricking him into a bet.
Evan stood apart as a true prodigy among his peers. His innate abilities went far beyond those of fellow disciples, creating a talent gap that few could hope to bridge. In the hierarchy of the Sect, such exceptional skill propelled one to prominence, and Evan’s rise seemed destined from the moment he first demonstrated his gifts.
When he triumphed in the final confrontation of the previous All Peaks Competition, the victory carried more than mere prestige. His eyes had gleamed with uncharacteristic seriousness as he claimed the coveted prize—an opportunity to traverse the legendary Cloud Road and gain entrance to the hallowed Cloud Hall. The spectators had fallen silent that day, watching with a mixture of envy and admiration as the usually carefree youth accepted an honor many would sacrifice everything to attain.
[SFX: SUDDEN HALT]
According to legend, Evan actually made it to the Cloud Road, that sacred path coveted by all disciples. Yet remarkably, midway through this once-in-a-lifetime trial, he simply got tired and just... fell asleep.
Walking the Cloud Road was a dream for every single disciple in the Sect. Elara herself would have sacrificed anything for such an opportunity—a chance that countless practitioners would readily spill blood to obtain. The masters all looked at Evan with expressions of utter disbelief.
They exchanged knowing glances and sighed deeply, their collective wisdom reduced to a single, damning assessment: Evan really is an idiot.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: ANTICIPATION]
Evan had been training in isolation for a long time, so he'd missed the All Peaks Competition. But most of the other disciples had seen it, either live in the arena or on the Scrying Slate.
Zane had blown everyone’s minds with his mysterious and powerful sword techniques—everyone’s, except Evan’s, apparently.
Sure, Evan was ranked high on the disciple Roster, but nobody here thought Zane would lose a straight-up sword fight. Best case scenario for Evan? A draw.
And now he was going to fight Zane without even drawing his sword? Had he forgotten what would happen if Zane forced his hand? The guy was unbelievable.
He’d already gone through the humiliation of calling Elara "My Queen."
If Zane beat him now, he wouldn't just look like a fool; he’d look like a fool twice over.
Since Evan was so dead set on making an idiot of himself, no one bothered to remind him of what happened last time. He'd dug his own grave, and now he was about to lie in it.
[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDUP]
"Alright," Zane said, done wasting his breath. "If you insist." The words were barely out of his mouth before he lunged.
[SFX: SWORD THRUST]
Zane led with a classic: a straight, level thrust, all his power focused into the very tip of his sword. A simple horizontal stab.
At first, Evan looked bored, so cocky he probably figured he could dodge it in his sleep. But the second he moved to sidestep, the blood drained from his face.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
"Whoa... what is this?" In that instant, Evan’s whole expression shattered.
