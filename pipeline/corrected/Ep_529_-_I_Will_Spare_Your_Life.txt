Ep 529 - I Will Spare Your Life

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
While Maddox came to terms with his vulnerabilities, Zane sent a message to Cedric through his Vitality. "What a shame; you were too slow."
Cedric was just as pissed. He'd been lying in wait inside Cloud Hall for Zane's signal to spring the Madness Trap. The plan was simple: surround Maddox, then hit him with Thunder's Wrath.
The Madness Trap itself was a nasty combination of a magic array and an inferno array. If Maddox had been caught, he would’ve been swallowed by endless illusions, leaving him wide open for the lightning strikes from Thunder's Wrath.
[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACK]
But Maddox was too damn sharp. He sensed the killing intent just in time, bolting out of the Madness Trap's range and narrowly dodging the lightning from Thunder's Wrath.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
A runic expert from the Inner-Core Realm and a Soul Realm warrior had just tried to assassinate the most powerful man in the Heartlands. If word of this got out, it would throw the entire region into chaos.
And the crazy part? They almost pulled it off.
Even if the Madness Trap failed to kill Maddox and Thunder's Wrath only wounded him, the Cloud Hall warriors were not to be underestimated. They might have seemed weak to a Life-and-Death Realm master, but a combined assault from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors would have been enough to cripple Maddox.
And that wasn't even counting Cloud Hall's heavy hitters: the formidable Tia and its leader, a Life-and-Death Realm master in her own right.
[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]
The same elders who’d been scared stiff by Maddox moments ago now watched him flee with something like pity in their eyes.
Even the leader of Cloud Hall, her face an unreadable mask, couldn't hide the flicker of regret.
Nearby, Tia sat perched on her icy blue halberd, her long, pale legs swinging slightly. She pouted, keeping her own disappointment to herself.
But then, Maddox had absorbed the good fortune of countless people over the years. Taking him down was never going to be easy.
 [AMBIENT: WIND HOWLING]
[MUSIC: DARK ATMOSPHERIC]
Long after he’d put some distance between himself and Cloud Hall, Maddox could still feel his heart hammering against his ribs. His power was undeniable, but it wasn't until he was clear of Cloud Hall's Order-protecting array that the cold grip of fear finally tightened around him.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT]
 [AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
If he’d actually been caught in that trap, with the entire Hall swarming him… he would have died. It was a possibility that had never even crossed his mind.
[MUSIC: DARK REFLECTIVE STRINGS]
He was a killer who had clawed his way up from one bloodbath to the next. He'd stared death in the face more times than he could count, but not since reaching the pinnacle of his power had anyone pushed him that close to the edge.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
 [MUSIC: IRRITATED TENSION]
And the whole time, that infuriating kid, Zane, had just stood at the edge of the enchanted barrier, watching the whole thing with a completely unbothered look on his face.
That kid was a real piece of work.
He was only in the Soul Realm, yet possessed so many tricks. If he reached the Inner-Core Realm—or worse, the Spirit-Tribulation Realm—he'd become a genuine threat to everything Maddox was building.
This wasn't about revenge anymore. Zane had become an obstacle that required removal.
Maddox approached the barrier and stopped.
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]
[MUSIC: STRANGE TENSE STRINGS]
"Zane. You've earned the right to call yourself my opponent," he said.
The Spirit-Tribulation elders stared. A warrior who had lived for centuries was telling a teenager he was worthy of his time? Any one of them could have ended Zane in seconds. Yet here stood the most powerful man in the Heartlands, paying him a compliment.
Zane's expression remained flat. "Release my sister. And I'll consider letting you live."
The elders cycled through confusion, disbelief, and existential crisis. Had the stress broken him?
Even Elder Imogen's eyebrows shot up.
Only Tia laughed quietly behind her hand. He was always like this.
[MUSIC: IRRITATED TENSION]
Maddox's face shifted between fury and reluctant amusement. "Bold words, kid. Don't worry about your sister—worry about yourself. You can't hide behind that barrier forever."
He was right. If he found another warrior with the right constitution to complete his ritual, no barrier could contain him.
Zane held his gaze for one more second, then turned away and sent a quiet message through his Vitality. "Cedric. Drive him out."
[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]
Under Cedric's control, Thunder's Wrath answered. Bolt after bolt hammered down toward Maddox in a relentless sequence.
Maddox couldn't break through. He couldn't retreat without eating lightning. Zane had already walked away.
Nothing remained but to leave.
[AMBIENT: DESTROYED FORTRESS]
He left—but not quietly.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
[SFX: CRUMBLING STONE]
Below Cloud Hall, Maddox tore through Cloudhaven's outer walls in a single pass, stripping them clean across a hundred miles. Hundreds died or were injured.
To Maddox, they were ants. He could have leveled the entire city, but crushing ants held no interest.
[AMBIENT: DESTROYED FORTRESS]
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
Maddox knew that killing too many of them would become a problem. It would infect him with a heavy Malice, forcing him to waste time cleansing it from his system. So he’d settled for destroying their walls and left, satisfied.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
[AMBIENT: QUIET HALL]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TRANSFORMATION]
Once Maddox was truly gone, wrinkles slowly crept back across Elder Imogen’s youthful face. Her hair bled back to white, and her shoulders stooped, returning her to the frail, elderly woman she normally was.
Tia floated over, taking her master’s arm with a giggle. "Master, you were so beautiful just now."
"Oh, stop it, you," Elder Imogen, rolling her eyes. Her mastery of the Law of Life allowed her to use Vitality to reclaim her youth, a form she could have easily maintained. But with the threat gone, there was no point.
[AMBIENT: RELIEVED CROWD MURMURS]
[MUSIC: GENTLE HOPEFUL MELODY]
The tension in Cloud Hall finally began to break. With Maddox gone, it felt like everyone could breathe again. The younger disciples, who had been watching from a safe distance, couldn't help but sigh in admiration. They'd seen Zane stand his ground behind the barrier, not even flinching as Maddox unleashed his power.
 [AMBIENT: DISCIPLES CHATTERING]
"What's the big deal?" one unimpressed disciple grumbled. "That barrier's tough as nails. Zane was just hiding. It's not like he was in any real danger."
"Are you kidding me?" another disciple shot back. "First of all, Zane is the one who drew the runes that make that barrier so tough. And second, I bet you wouldn't have the guts to stand there and face down Maddox, barrier or no barrier."
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
The grumbling disciple went quiet. He knew the other guy was right. Even if you know you're safe, it takes a special kind of nerve to stare down someone like Maddox.
It’s like being afraid of heights—you know the railing on the skyscraper is secure, but your legs still turn to jelly when you look over the edge.
