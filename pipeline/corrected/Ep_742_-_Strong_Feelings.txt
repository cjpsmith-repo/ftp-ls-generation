Ep 742 - Strong Feelings
Word count: 1003
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
The electric net had tightened to its absolute limit — and then detonated, consuming Zane in a blinding column of white light. As it condensed, its raw force rivaled the full-strength strike of a Life-and-Death Realm master.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Tia’s heart hammered against her ribs, a sharp pang of regret twisting in her gut.
“Zane!”
She knew that even with her own defenses, an attack like that would have torn her apart. And while Zane’s strength was incredible—he’d already reached the Spirit-Tribulation Realm—she couldn’t imagine anyone withstanding a direct hit of that magnitude.
“Are you really going to die over a few words? Don’t be a fool!” Tia cried, her eyes fixed on the blinding light, her face a mask of pure terror.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
A moment later, the blazing white energy slowly faded. Every warrior on the battlefield held their breath, their eyes glued to the epicenter of the blast. A cold smile played on Darius’s lips; he was certain the man had been reduced to ash.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT REVEAL]
But that smile vanished the instant the light cleared, revealing the warrior he’d tried to obliterate still floating in the air, completely unharmed.
“So,” Zane said with a grin, glancing back at Tia. “Am I a fool?”
Seeing him safe, Tia felt her heart finally settle back into her chest. The relief was so powerful it almost buckled her knees, but it was immediately swamped by a wave of furious anger. Her expression hardened. “You can go to hell!”
She wasn’t wrong to be angry. The combined Vitality of over ten thousand Soul Realm warriors was an unbelievably destructive force. Even with a body as tough as a high-grade Mythic Weapon, Zane couldn’t have survived that head-on. A direct hit would have left him gravely injured, if not killed him outright.
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]
But just before the blast connected, he had used the power of the Spatial Law to slip outside of normal space entirely. He had reached the second level of the Spatial Law, and at that level he moved through space like a fish through water — no matter how stormy the waves, he simply flowed with them. Fluid, frictionless, untouchable. No attack could find him unless the attacker was equally versed in the same law. That was the ace up his sleeve, the thing that made the Spatial Law so unbeatable. Along with the Law of Time, it was one of the two most mysterious and powerful principles in existence.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
Still, Zane knew he was in real danger.
Darius knew it too — though he hadn't seen it coming. For months, Wavecrest City had been his fortress and his hunting ground. The Birdcage made him nearly untouchable. He would occasionally lead thousands of warriors out of the city — not just to raid, but to season them, let them gain experience against the Spirit-Tribulation cultivators foolish enough to wander within reach. Ordinary warriors at that level were no match for him; a wave of his banner was enough to finish them. He had grown confident. Comfortable.
Then Tia had appeared.
She hadn't announced herself. She hadn't issued a challenge. She had simply attacked, and before Darius could even raise the formation, thousands of his men were dead. He had nearly joined them. He'd run, regrouped, and thrown everything he had into assembling the Birdcage in time — and it had worked. He had caged a Life-and-Death Realm warrior. He had told himself that was a victory.
He had even allowed himself to look forward to what came next. The Hall Master of Cloud Hall — young, extraordinarily powerful, the most formidable woman in the Heartlands, and by all accounts unmarried. Once her Vitality was exhausted, he had methods that went well beyond simple defeat. Methods that could strip a warrior's will entirely, bend them past the point of resistance. He had been savoring the thought.
And then this man had appeared. A Spirit-Tribulation warrior — in Darius's estimation, nothing more than a reckless mid-stage cultivator with a death wish. He had sent half the formation against him without a second thought. That, he now realized, had been a catastrophic mistake. If he had known who this man was, he would never have been so careless.
But he hadn't known. And now he stood with no explanation for what he had just witnessed. He couldn't identify the method. He couldn't even guess at it. Whatever Zane had done, it was completely outside Darius's experience, and that blank incomprehension was more unsettling than the failure itself. The satisfaction he'd felt moments ago had curdled into something cold and uncertain.
He couldn't run. Wavecrest City was a fortress, not an escape route, and he couldn't outpace a Life-and-Death Realm warrior even if he abandoned everything. His only path was forward — exhaust them both, outlast them, hold the line.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
But even now, at the most critical moment, he wouldn’t retreat. He damn sure wouldn't run.
His only option was to fight for his life.
[SFX: WHIP CRACK]
[AMBIENT: BATTLE TENSION]
With a sharp crack, Darius whipped his banner through the air. The six Spirit-Tribulation Realm elders instantly shifted their positions, channeling their Vitality into the rune hanging in the sky. Another Great Rune flared to life and shot directly toward Zane.
Zane wasn’t about to let Darius have his way.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
Without a second of hesitation, he dove, not away from the fight, but straight for the Soul Realm warriors on the ground. He had seen the problem at a glance. The Vitality being channeled upward from tens of thousands of warriors was inherently uneven — heterogeneous, impossible to unify, no matter how skilled the masters controlling it. Even six Spirit-Tribulation elders couldn't smooth out every inconsistency. There was a flaw in there. There always was. And as long as those warriors kept feeding power to the elders, the formation would hold — but cut the supply, and the whole structure collapses.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
A sneer touched his lips as he faced the sea of freelance fighters below.
They had chosen to side with the Corrupted Divine Kingdom. Now, they had to be prepared to die for it.
