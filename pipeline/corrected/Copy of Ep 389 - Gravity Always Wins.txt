Ep 389 - Gravity Always Wins

Word Count: 1198

If Zane and Finn took seventy percent of the Nether Phantom essences, and the guy from Cloud Hall didn't want any, that left just thirty percent to be split between the other four.

To no one's surprise, their offer didn't go over well.

[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]

"Seventy percent?" Lois asked, her voice ice-cold. "What, you think Frank and I are just your sidekicks?" She was pissed. As far as she was concerned, the essences belonged to her and Frank, period.

Frank made his displeasure clear as well. "Zane, I'll give you thirty percent since you helped. But I wasn't expecting this. You want to take half the essences for yourself? I'm not a pushover."

Zane’s eyes narrowed. "I don't care how tough you think you are. Finn and I are taking seventy percent. End of discussion."

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

Frank sighed, and a long blood dragon materialized on the hilt of his white spear. With a slight flourish, he planted its tip on the ground. "I don't like fighting my own," he said solemnly. "But if anyone in this group wants to stir up trouble, I have no problem teaching them a lesson about the rules first."

[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]

Seeing Frank ready to throw down, Zane drew his Blood Drinker. He shook his head. "Frank, you're strong, but if you think that alone makes you the leader, you're just arrogant."

[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]

As he finished speaking, the Apex of Sword Intent began to coalesce around his Blood Drinker, a wisp of energy dancing like a nimble spirit on the blade's tip.

A moment later, Finn was at Zane's side, his own blade crackling with the same Apex of Sword Intent.

Frank's eyebrows shot up. They both have the Apex of Sword Intent? He might be arrogant, but he wasn't an idiot. Finn alone was a serious threat. And now here was Zane, who was clearly even stronger than Patrick.

The unsettling realization that Zane not only had that eerie purple-black Vitality but could also channel the Apex of Sword Intent gave Frank pause.

[SFX: RUMBLING EARTHQUAKE]

Just then, the small world they were in began to shake violently. Zane looked up at the sky. Cracks spiderwebbed across it, just like the ones he’d seen on Cloud Road—a sure sign this pocket dimension was about to collapse.

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

[AMBIENT: COLLAPSING DIMENSION]

"Quick, in!" Zane commanded, leaping into the red vortex without a second thought.

Finn, Patrick, Frank, and Lois all plunged in right after him.

[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane shot out of the vortex, tumbling headfirst into open air. It was a dizzying, stomach-lurching sensation—no ground below, just an endless fall.

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

The wind screamed past his ears. He caught a glimpse of the Cloud Hall disciple plummeting nearby. He realized the vortex had spit them out high above a massive abyss.

[AMBIENT: DEEP ECHOING VOID]

Below them, the chasm was a bottomless pit of darkness. No one knew how deep it went, but a fall like this was a death sentence.

Soon, however, Zane spotted several stones floating in the void. They were all different shapes; some were as big as a table, others no larger than a fist. Some unknown force was holding them in place. 

An idea popped into his head. I can use these to break my fall! 

[SFX: STONE LANDING]

Just as the idea crossed his mind, he saw the Cloud Hall disciple suddenly twist in mid-air, landing perfectly on a floating stone.

His agility was incredible, his landing flawless. The movement was more fluid than anyone Zane had ever seen.

Zane did the same, tucking into a backflip to correct his own fall. He aimed for a floating stone and reached for it. Unfortunately, his landing wasn't nearly as graceful. 

[SFX: SPINNING STONE]

[SFX: SCRAMBLING]

He put too much weight on one side, and the stone started to spin wildly. He scrambled, just barely managing to hook his fingers around an edge, spinning with it until he could finally pull himself on top and find his balance.

Looking up, Zane saw Frank and Patrick both clambering onto stones of their own.

Meanwhile, Lewis, having made it through the vortex, was already back to his usual showboating. He leaped between the floating stones with the grace of a dragonfly skimming a pond, all while letting out a stream of theatrical yelps as if he were in mortal danger.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

Once everyone had found a stone and steadied themselves, the Cloud Hall disciple suddenly pointed up. "Look! Up there! It's the exit!"

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL LIGHT HUM]

A wave of relief washed over the group. They all looked where he was pointing. The abyss was littered with floating stones, and at the very top, a bright light shone down. They could even see shattered debris floating near it.

All they had to do was use the stones to climb their way out, and they would be free of the Death Road.

Finally seeing the light at the end of the tunnel was a rush, but it was tinged with disappointment. After risking life and limb, they’d all been hoping for a bigger prize. A fraction of the Nether Phantom essences just didn't feel like enough.

But for now, survival was all that mattered. Making it out alive would make them all legends.

[AMBIENT: ECHOING CAVERN]

[SFX: STONE SHIFTING]

Lewis giggled. "This isn't as hard as it looks." He hopped onto a small, fist-sized stone and wiggled his body like some kind of acrobat, showing off his incredible balance.

[MUSIC: CREEPING DANGER]

While Lewis was busy performing, the disciple from Cloud Hall decided it was time to eliminate his competition.

Zane’s feint—a basic swordsmanship move—seemed to throw the Cloud Hall disciple off for a second. But then a strange smile spread across his face, and without hesitation, he reached out and grabbed the flat of Zane's Blood Drinker with his bare hands.

It was an insane move. This guy was only at the first level of the Soul Realm, yet here he was, grabbing the sword of a warrior like Zane—someone whose raw strength made him nearly unbeatable. Zane might not have been able to stop Frank’s spear head-on, but he had countless other ways to win a fight, which made the disciple's sheer confidence all the more shocking.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]

The disciple from Cloud Hall gave Zane an eerie smile and whispered, "You're coming down with me."

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

[SFX: FABRIC TEARING]

Before Zane could react, the disciple yanked him forward by the front of his tunic, pulling them both off the floating stone and into the yawning darkness below.

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

To make matters worse, a Drake materialized directly below them, surging up from the depths with impossible speed. The enormous flood dragon's scales gleamed as it rose through the darkness, its ancient eyes fixed on them with predatory intelligence. 

Zane's heart hammered against his ribs—not from fear of the fall, but from the calculated precision of this ambush. 

This was no random attack. The Cloud Hall disciple knew exactly what he was doing.
