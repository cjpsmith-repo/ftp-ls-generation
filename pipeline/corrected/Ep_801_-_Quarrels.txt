Ep 801 - Quarrels

Word count: 1150

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

No one saw it coming. Logan could actually bend space itself, wrapping it around his body like a personal suit of armor. The only way to beat that was to literally tear through the fabric of space, and most warriors at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm didn’t have that kind of power. 

Watching from the sidelines, Zane couldn’t help but be impressed. The techniques coming out of the divine kingdoms were on another level. With legacies like these, their fighters should be leagues ahead of anyone from The Heartlands.

[SFX: CRACKLING FIRE]

Titus's attacks were incredibly sharp, but Logan’s spatial power was smothering him, preventing him from unleashing his full strength. If Titus could rip a hole in space or had some training in Spatial Law himself, he might’ve stood a chance. As it was, it was becoming painfully obvious that his ferocious strikes and mastery of Fire Law weren't going to be enough.

To Zane, at least, the outcome was already a foregone conclusion.

“I think Titus is going to lose,” he murmured, mostly to himself.

Of course, the warriors next to him heard every word. Titus was a legend in the Wisteria Martial House—a top-tier fighter for the entire Star Field Divine Kingdom. The guys from his house were all on the edge of their seats, praying for him to win. So when they heard some random fighter from the Harlen Divine Kingdom call the match early, they weren't about to stand for it.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

“The hell did you just say?” one of them shouted, getting right in Zane’s face. “Are you looking for trouble, kid?”

Zane was a little taken aback by the sudden hostility, but didn’t react.

The Crystal Martial House warriors weren't having it either. Someone shot back. Then someone else. The Wisteria warriors brought up the Crystal Martial House's ranking — dead last, four years running. The Crystal Martial House hit back hard enough that both sides stopped talking and started reaching for their weapons.

These were all top-tier geniuses, hot-blooded and spoiling for a fight. Vitality crackled off them in waves. Nobody was watching the arena anymore.

[SFX: SURGE OF VITALITY]

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Suddenly, a crushing presence washed over the area, silencing the disturbance. The aura pressed down on the warriors from both houses like a physical weight. It felt like a mountain was sitting on their foreheads, pinning them in place.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]

[MUSIC: POWERFUL ORCHESTRAL]

This was the power of a warrior from the Soul Sea Realm.

Zane, however, was the only one who remained completely unfazed. He just looked up at the powerful figure in the distance and offered a faint, calm smile.

This caught the Soul Sea warrior by surprise. He could feel that one person in the crowd hadn't been affected by his aura at all.

[SFX: PRESSURE RELEASE]

He gave Zane a curious look before finally pulling back the pressure from everyone else.

Since when did the Crystal Martial House have someone like that? My aura couldn't even touch him, he thought, intrigued.

A warrior from the Soul Sea Realm had a soul trained to the level of a Fighting Soul. Against his aura, any Spirit-Tribulation fighter should have been completely immobilized. Zane hadn’t moved, but the warrior just knew—the kid wasn't being held down. He was simply standing still.

A Spirit-Tribulation warrior with a soul at the Fighting Soul Realm? Impossible. Maybe he has some kind of artifact that resists soul pressure? But not even the man from the Divine Kingdom would have something like that, let alone some random kid.

His curiosity lingered, but he didn’t say anything. His only job was to maintain order during the competition. This little mystery wasn’t his problem to solve. He gave Zane one last look and moved on.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

By the time the commotion died down, the fight was over.

Just as Zane had predicted, Titus’s attacks were fierce—probably the strongest of any Spirit-Tribulation warrior there. But brute force wasn’t enough to win here. These fighters were all talented prodigies with their own unique skills, and Logan’s Spatial Armor was the perfect counter to Titus’s raw power.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

Titus slammed into the ground. He hissed in pain before forcing himself into a kneel. “Your Majesty! Forgive me. I have failed you!”

Scott smiled kindly. “Please, get up. Winning and losing are part of a soldier’s life. There’s nothing to forgive. Don’t worry about it.”

The Emperor didn’t blame him, but Titus still looked crushed. He was supposed to be representing both the Wisteria Martial House and the Star Field Divine Kingdom, and he felt like he had let them all down.

It was no surprise that the fighters from the Star Field Divine Kingdom looked completely dejected. 

With nowhere to direct their frustration, several of them turned it on the Crystal Martial House.

“Think you're something? Why don't you send someone up and prove it?” one of them called out. “Let's see how long your best lasts against Logan.”

The taunting rippled through the crowd. Nearby, Randy watched in silence. He had the right to send a fighter — that was the whole point of this competition, to determine entry order into the Sacred Feather Sea without the kings tearing each other apart directly. He glanced sideways at Zane. The only fighter on his side he genuinely trusted for this was standing right there. But would Zane agree to go up? Randy wasn't sure. He hesitated.

[SFX: BLADE UNSHEATHING]

[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE THEME]

Before Randy could make up his mind, the Crown Prince acted first. From across the square, he caught Edwin's eye and offered a faint smile. Edwin needed no further instruction. He flew forward, pulling a thick, wolf-fang-like saber from his back. The blade pulsed with a faint blue light.

A mid-grade Relic-Class weapon for a Spirit-Tribulation warrior. I wonder if the divine kingdoms have any Divine-Class weapons, Zane thought idly.

He wasn’t particularly worried about who won. He wasn’t really from the Crystal Martial House or a citizen of the Harlen Divine Kingdom, so he didn’t have the same skin in the game as the other fighters. The only reason he’d even come was for Tia. Still, he needed to watch these fights closely to get a read on the competition.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

One nagging thought did surface, though. Since the Crown Prince had sent Edwin up without hesitation, was it possible Lord Randy might ask him to fight, too?

It wasn’t that Zane was afraid. But if he stepped onto that stage, he would definitely attract the attention of the Holy Maiden from the York Divine Kingdom. And what was he supposed to do then? 

He still remembered that the last time they met, she’d sworn she was going to kill him.
