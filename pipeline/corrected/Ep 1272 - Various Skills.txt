Ep 1272 - Various Skills

Word count: 1084

[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
[AMBIENT: CAVERNOUS HALL]
[SFX: BOWSTRING RELEASE]
When the woman from the Demon Night released the bowstring, a low thrum vibrated through the air, the string blurring on the bow.
[SFX: FIRE VORTEX SPINNING]
But instead of shooting forward, the vortex of fire simply hovered in front of her, spinning slowly.
She flashed a shy smile at the crowd. “We should probably back up a little.”
One look at the raw power churning in that fiery vortex and everyone knew she was right. The explosion would be bigger than any of them could imagine, and they needed to move.
She put away her Moonburst Flamebow and drifted back toward the hall with the others, leaving the fiery vortex hanging in the air. Zane, Janet, and the rest of the group retreated hundreds of feet before stopping.
“That’s not far enough,” the Demon Night woman said with another smile. “Let’s go back more. I don’t want anyone to get hurt.”
So the group followed her to the middle of the hall.
“Okay, this should be far enough.” 
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: ROOM SHAKING]
The vortex of fire shot toward the huge, square rock. 
[SFX: MASSIVE FIRE EXPLOSION]
On impact, the fiery energy detonated outward, a blazing inferno that easily swallowed nearly half the hall.
She had judged the blast radius perfectly.
“It’s not just the five layers of Fire Law,” Janet whispered, her eyes wide. “There’s some strange Law of Causality mixed in. That must be what lets it condense the fire’s power so much. Is that the secret of the Moonburst Flamebow?”
In the middle of the raging fire, the surface of the huge rock began to glow with countless Illusion Points. 
[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FLAMES]
[SFX: MAGICAL CHIMES STREAMING]
They danced in the searing air before streaming straight toward the woman from the Demon Night. There were several times more points than the Ogre martial artist had managed to earn earlier.
When the fire finally died down, and the last of the Illusion Points had been absorbed, the Ogre anxiously demanded, “How many? How many Illusion Points did you get?”
“Two point eight million,” she replied with a faint smile.
“What?” the Ogre roared, his face turning green with envy. It was a bitter pill to swallow. He never should have been the one to go first.
Her score sent a surge of excitement through the other warriors.
Zane turned the number over in his head. Relying on plundering points off other warriors alone wouldn’t be enough to hold his ranking for long—not against numbers like that. If he wanted to actually climb, he needed to keep pushing through halls like this one.
[SFX: STRIKES ECHOING]
One by one, the rest of the group stepped up to take their shot. Another human warrior went next, throwing everything he had into a single strike—no Divine Weapon behind it, but the boulder still gave up just over one million points. The Ogre let out another wail every time someone claimed their share. 
By the time the group had cycled through, nearly everyone had walked away with more than a million points apiece.
[SFX: SOFT ENERGY HUM]
Then Janet stepped up, a confident smile already in place. She didn’t draw a weapon. Instead, she pressed her palms together and rotated them slowly in opposite directions, a strange aura gathering between them.
She laid her palm against the boulder—a light, almost lazy touch.
“You don’t even want points?” the Ogre asked, baffled by how little force she’d used.
[SFX: POINTS TRICKLING]
A single point drifted free from the boulder’s surface. Then another. The Ogre barked out a laugh. “That’s worth what, one hundred points?”
[SFX: POINTS SURGING]
But the trickle kept building, faster and faster, until it became a flood.
“Three point one million. Not bad,” Janet said with a faint smile, shooting Zane a look before stepping back. The Ogre just stood there, stunned into silence.
Of the eight who remained, six had already taken their turn. Only Zane and the short martial artist were left.
They exchanged a glance, and the short martial artist stepped forward first. “This one’s mine.”
Just as Janet had predicted, earning Illusion Points was easy if you knew the trick. 
The short martial artist was completely silent.
[MUSIC: MEDITATIVE STILLNESS]
[AMBIENT: TEMPLE SILENCE]
He had a Divine Weapon of his own—the Blade of Hallowed Censure—but he made no move to draw it. Instead, he pressed his hands together, closed his eyes, and went still.
“What’s he doing?” the Ogre muttered. No one answered.
The boulder rewarded strength, and everyone here understood the deeper game—they were all temporary allies now, but out in the world, warriors of this caliber would cross paths again. Seeing what the others could really do, and what they were holding back, was worth more than the points themselves.
Time seemed to stretch. The short martial artist stood like a monk in meditation, his aura gone perfectly tranquil, as if the rest of the hall had ceased to exist. Long minutes passed before a sigh broke the silence—low, weary, almost mournful, like it belonged to something that had already seen how this would end.
The moment his palm touched the rock, he pulled back, as quick and light as a dragonfly dipping into water.
[SFX: MASSIVE ENERGY FLOOD]
But even with that soft touch, a massive flood of Illusion Points poured into him.
*What a strange power,* Zane thought, giving the martial artist a serious look. *The methods of The Hallowed Concord are truly deep and powerful.*
The three great human powers in the universe were all formidable. The Thoughtless Minds was a sprawling collective of clans, and the alliance was made up of countless sacred orders. But The Hallowed Concord was the oldest of them all, their path of cultivation entirely different from the others, as they followed the direct teachings of The Ascended.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
“Three point nine million Illusion Points,” the short man announced casually.
A collective gasp went through the crowd.
It wasn’t the number itself that shocked them; most of them had over four million Illusion Points stored up already. But for him to get nearly four million in a single hit… did that mean his palm strike was several times stronger than their own attacks?
The woman from the Demon Night had used a Divine Weapon and only earned two point eight million. This guy, who came out of nowhere, got three point nine million with just his bare hands.
Janet’s mouth twitched in annoyance. The record she’d just set had been instantly shattered. Not one to back down, she turned to Zane. “Your turn.”
Frustrated, she found herself hoping Zane could somehow score even more than the quiet martial artist who had just shown them all up.
