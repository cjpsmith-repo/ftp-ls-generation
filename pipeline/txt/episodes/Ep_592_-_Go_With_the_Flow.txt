Ep 592 - Go With the Flow
Word count: 1059
[AMBIENT: TOWER COURTYARD]
[MUSIC: COMPETITIVE TENSION]
Zane's name continued to climb. It didn't take him long to hit the sixth row, easily blowing past Dylan.
How far would he actually climb in the end? Dylan didn't want to find out.
Everyone at the Tower of Sin knew Dylan was the most talented disciple from the Violet Heart Sanctuary—second only to Olivia. He knew he wasn't on her level, and he’d made his peace with that.
Ever since they were kids, Olivia was the only one who could go toe-to-toe with him. On top of that, he’d been nursing a massive crush on her for just as long.
But then this jerk Zane popped up out of nowhere, yanking the rug right out from under him and stealing the spotlight Dylan felt he’d earned. It felt like Zane was replacing him, and he hated it.
At the Violet Heart Sanctuary, Olivia was practically worshipped. It was almost laughable, really. She always kept Dylan at arm's length, but with Zane, it was the complete opposite. Her patience and enthusiasm for the guy were infuriating. How could she not see how cocky he was? Seeing Zane soak up the attention he’d always craved made Dylan's chest burn with jealousy. So, every time Zane’s name climbed another rank on the stone tablet, Dylan’s jaw clenched, his whole body trembling with frustration.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]
Inside the chamber, the second level of the Spatial Law was called 'Tearing.' If fully comprehended, a warrior could rip space apart at will—the resulting fault not only blocking all attacks, but forming a blade of pure space. The crack itself became the weapon.
Zane had only just gotten his hands on this knowledge when the stone tablet began to fade.
He stared at it.
In the time he'd had, he'd absorbed maybe a tenth of it. Ten percent. He would have given anything to stay longer, to let the rest of it sink in. But the Tower of Sin wasn't offering that.
"Is the test over?" he murmured.
[SFX: SPATIAL RIFTS TEARING]
As soon as the words left his mouth, the darkness around him shifted. Brilliant colors flickered at the edges of his vision—and then a crack split the air directly in front of him.
A spatial rift. And it was moving fast.
It shot toward him like a living thing, a jagged mouth yawning open, hungry.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane's figure flashed. He blinked sideways using the first level—Travel—and the rift snapped shut on empty air.
He landed in a crouch, breathing hard.
"So this is the test," he muttered, eyes scanning the darkness.
Not studying the law. Applying it.
He'd assumed the Talent Tablet had some secret method for measuring raw talent directly. That was naïve. Talent, he realized now, was just a warrior's ability to comprehend—and comprehension could only be proven by use.
The tablet identified each person's natural affinity, fed them the corresponding law, then threw that same law back at them as a weapon. Efficiency of comprehension. That's what it was measuring.
"But this test isn't fully accurate," Zane said, shaking his head.
He'd just used the first level to dodge. That was skill, not talent. The product of training, not innate ability.
But Zane had underestimated the power of the Talent Tablet.
[SFX: SPATIAL RIFTS TEARING]
A moment later, more rifts began to tear open the space all around him. Zane braced himself. He knew he’d have a split second to move once they fully formed, before they snapped shut and crushed him.
[MUSIC: INTENSE DISCOVERY]
It looked like hundreds of monstrous mouths yawning open in the darkness.
God, there are so many of them.
[SFX: RAPID TELEPORTATION WHOOSHES]
Zane became a blur, blinking high and low to evade the aggressive rifts closing in on him.
But the pressure was mounting. With every new rift, the space he had to work with shrank. The chamber wasn't that big to begin with, and as the rifts multiplied, his safe zones were vanishing. It was only a matter of time before he was overwhelmed.
He thought back to his fight with Marilyn and the giant swords she’d summoned. He’d used Spatial Law to dodge her attacks back then, too. But even with a hundred wicked blades flying at him, there had always been a safe pocket of space to jump to. This was different. Here, the safe spaces themselves were disappearing. He frowned, his focus sharpening. If he couldn't find a way around these rifts, the test was over.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION BUILD]
Zane’s eyes narrowed, his mind racing for a solution.
He'd glimpsed level two and grasped the basic theory.
Level one meant pulling two spatial points together. Bending reality to take a shortcut.
Level two worked differently.
Space possessed strength. Smaller worlds were weaker, easier to tear. In this unstable chamber, tearing was effortless. But in the greater world outside, even powerful warriors couldn't rip space by brute force. They could only find weak spots, disturb the fabric, and create a crack wide enough to slip through.
That gap separated level one from level two.
Zane had seconds, not days, to bridge it.
Like a fish reading currents, he wouldn't fight the spatial flow—he'd become part of it.
[SFX: DEEP BREATH]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
He gritted his teeth and balled his fists. For a moment, he stopped trying to travel. Instead, he took a quick breath and stood perfectly still.
[SFX: SPATIAL DISTORTION WHOOSH]
The moment Zane went still, the hungry maws of the rifts converged, ready to swallow him from all sides. As the assault bore down on him, he squeezed his eyes shut.
If anyone had been watching, they would have thought Zane had given up. They would have been wrong. He had a plan.
He whispered into the darkness, a new confidence in his voice. "To tear space, you first have to become one with space itself. Level one touches on it, but just barely. It doesn't go deep enough. But if I combine that with what I just figured out… I know I can do this!"
He was betting everything on this idea. There was no time to second-guess.
[SFX: VIOLENT TEAR]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
An instant later, the first rift lunged for him—a jagged tear in reality aimed right for his head, ready to swallow him whole.
