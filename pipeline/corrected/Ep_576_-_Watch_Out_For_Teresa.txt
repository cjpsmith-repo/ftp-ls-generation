Ep 576 - Watch Out For Teresa

[AMBIENT: NIGHT SKY]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]
After a long, tiring journey, Zane slowly drew his consciousness back from the vastness of the universe, ending his meditation.
As his awareness settled back into his body, a shudder ran through him. He took a deep breath and opened his eyes, his gaze landing on the star-dusted sky.
[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOFTOP]
Phoebe kept her distance. Her eyes were fixed on the remarkable young man before her, a quiet curiosity in her gaze. She could feel something was different about him now, but she couldn't quite put her finger on it.
Enjoy your trip through the cosmos? the Red Dragon chuckled, sensing Zane’s return.
Zane’s mind was still reeling from the things he’d seen out in the universe, and even now, he could feel his mind tethered to a faint, purple star. He tilted his head, his eyes scanning a specific corner of the sky.
His vision was so sharp that he could pick it out in a single glance.
I’ve connected with a star, Zane spoke in his mind. How do I draw on its power?
The Red Dragon sputtered, his voice cracking with disbelief. “What? You connected on your first try?”
It had taken him five full years to connect with a star for the first time!
Taming a star was supposed to be nearly impossible, and forging a connection with one was even harder. The Red Dragon had wandered the universe for five whole years just to find a star willing to accept him.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL WONDER]
But on Zane's very first journey, he’d already earned a star’s acceptance. The realization left the Red Dragon speechless.
The Cobalt Dragon laughed softly. Brother, don’t forget, this is Zane’s destiny at play.
The dragon race was powerful, and the Red Dragon's own strength had once been immeasurable, but even his destiny couldn't necessarily outshine Zane's.
It was said that warriors who possessed a fighting destiny were the world's favored children. This meant very few beings could claim better fortune than Zane.
Now that he had the star's approval, he could finally draw on its power.
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL COSMIC HUM]
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
Following the methods described in the Stellar Body, Zane reached out with his mind. At the edge of the sky, the distant star began to twinkle, and a faint, ethereal light shimmered across Zane’s skin.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Watching from a distance, Phoebe’s eyes widened. She had no idea why a faint light was suddenly emanating from Zane's body.
The Demon Night clan had always been acutely sensitive to energy, and whatever that light was, it sent a chill through her. Just what kind of cultivation method was this man using?
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
Zane felt the starlight humming through him and wanted badly to push further — to start training in earnest right now. But he held himself back.
He'd left the Tower to draw on the starlight. If he wanted to cultivate, a practice room was a better place. He had no intention of revealing anything about himself out in the open.
He stood and, without a word, headed back inside.
[AMBIENT: ARENA SILENCE]
Back on the first floor, he stepped onto the arena stage. No one came.
In a single day, word had spread: Zane wasn't a fighter anyone on the first floor could beat. Boris had already warned the Ogres to stand down. The other races had done their own calculations and reached the same conclusion.
He returned to his practice room and trained. The next day, he stepped onto the stage again. Same result — not one challenger.
On the third day, the Tower recorded it. Three consecutive empty stages. Zane had earned the right to advance without points.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ASCENDING]
He didn't stop to rest. He moved straight to the second floor.
[AMBIENT: TOWER SECOND FLOOR]
[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]
The second floor looked almost identical to the first — same layout, same facilities. The fighters here were a step stronger, but not dramatically so.
Phoebe had told him how the Tower worked on the way up. Floors one through five were the domain of War Fighters. From the fifth floor on, it was War Generals. The tenth floor and above belonged almost exclusively to War Lords.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]
As soon as Zane stepped onto the second floor, he saw her.
A woman from the Demon Night stood alone in the center of the arena stage. No one else was on it. The crowd watched from a distance, none of them moving.
Phoebe sucked in a breath behind him. "That's Teresa. She's actually here."
Teresa was a god-tier genius from the Demon Night clan's sacred land — a fifth-level War Fighter who had spent years in seclusion before entering the Tower. By Demon Night standards, even a War Fighter of her caliber outclassed most of what the second floor had to offer.
"She's strong," Phoebe said carefully. "My cultivation is higher than hers, but even I'm not sure I could beat her. I'd recommend waiting for her empty stage before you go up."
Zane looked at the stage. A faint smile crossed his face. "Is that so?"
[AMBIENT: TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Teresa was fuming. Three straight days of default wins just to advance one floor in the Tower of Sin? What a colossal waste of time.
She needed to reach the tenth floor as fast as possible. Her strength meant she'd absorb the most Lucky Light there, which would skyrocket her training speed.
What really pissed her off was that she'd already clocked two default wins on the second floor. But on the third day, some idiot had to step into the arena and challenge her. Furious, Teresa had killed him instantly. But since that wasn't a default win, her countdown reset. Three more days. Wasted.
Thankfully, the insta-kill was brutal enough to scare off any other morons. She just had to wait it out to finally earn her way to the third floor.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]
But just then, a figure stepped into the arena. It was none other than Zane.
She sized him up from head to toe, her eyes icy with rage. “So, another dumbass War Fighter thinks they can challenge me. Guess I wasn't brutal enough last time. You people just don't learn. This time, I'll show you what ruthless really looks like.”
Zane gave her a small smile. “I'm curious to see just how ruthless that is.”
[SFX: BLADE DRAWN]
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]
Teresa pulled a short dagger from her storage ring, her voice dripping with ice. “Ruthless enough to plunge you into the deepest pits of despair.”
