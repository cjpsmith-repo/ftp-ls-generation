Ep 736 - You're My Subject
Word count: 1062
[AMBIENT: TENSE MEETING ROOM]
All eyes in the room were pinned on Zane, especially those of the three leaders: Kieran, Hobbes, and Burns.
Zane had just broken through to the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. While that alone was a massive boost to Cloud Hall’s combat strength, it was nothing compared to his skill with runes. His real value wasn’t in his fighting prowess but in his mastery of the ancient art. So, naturally, everyone assumed he would join the Runic Tower.
It just made sense. The World Merchants' Guild needed his unique talent to defend against the Corrupted Divine Kingdom, and Zane needed their resources to finally take on Maddox.
But Zane simply shook his head. “Mister Kieran, I’m sorry, but I can’t join the Runic Tower right now.”
[SFX: SURPRISED MURMURS]
“But why?” Kieran asked, his brow furrowed in confusion.
“I don’t have enough time to set up an array,” Zane explained, shaking his head again. He didn’t just draw simple runes; he wove divine textures, an infinitely more complex process. The Trinity Killing Array he’d built for Cloud Hall had taken him nearly two months. That place was a fraction of the size of Cloudhaven, and Oracle City was at least three times bigger than that. There was simply no way he could construct a protective array around a metropolis that vast in the time they had.
“But don’t worry,” he added quickly. “I’ll still join the fight.”
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
It was time to finally settle the score with Maddox. But before that, another thought nagged at him. He hadn’t heard a single word from Elara since the last Martial Tournament. Maddox had been gunning for Cora, so Zane prayed Elara was still safe, but he had no idea where she was or where Maddox could be hiding her.
Kieran studied Zane, realizing he couldn’t be swayed. He remembered how this kid, back when he was only in the Soul Realm, could fight warriors from the Inner-Core Realm—punching a full realm above his weight.
Now, Zane was at the middle stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. Could he do it again? Could he actually take on a master from the Life-and-Death Realm? If so, that would make him far more powerful than Kieran or any of the other leaders present.
“Zane, since your mind is made up, I won’t press the issue,” Kieran said with a diplomatic smile. “You can form a team with the others and follow the army’s orders.”
Zane nodded and turned to go sign up.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
[SFX: IMPACT]
Just as he was leaving the meeting hall, a flash of white shot down from the sky like a meteor, landing in his path.
The figure that hit the ground was Tia — but not the Tia anyone in Oracle City was used to seeing. Her hair, usually immaculate, was singed and half-burnt, loose strands scattered across her shoulders. Her clothes were torn in several places, the fabric scorched. There were bruises on her face.
Behind Zane, Cora ducked instinctively, pressing herself against his back as debris from the shattered roof skittered across the floor.
Tia noticed. Her eyes flicked to Cora for exactly one second. Pretentious, she thought.
Saint Morphen and the other Cloud Hall elders swarmed her immediately.
Kieran stepped forward with concern. "Hall Master, are you hurt? Were you attacked?"
"I'm fine," Tia said, her voice easy, her expression composed despite the state of her. "I walked into a trap. Minor injuries."
She'd gone after a Corrupted Divine Kingdom camp a thousand miles out — over four thousand War General-level warriors, one Spirit-Tribulation warrior. She'd planned to take down the Spirit-Tribulation warrior first to remove the threat, then work through the rest at her leisure. What she hadn't known was that the Spirit-Tribulation warrior was bait. The whole camp had been arranged by Darius, the Corrupted Divine Kingdom's military adviser, specifically to catch her.
The moment she moved on him, she stepped into one layer of the trap after another. Four thousand warriors coordinating in formation — each one an ant on its own, but together something she couldn't simply ignore. She'd torn her way out by force, but it had cost her.
"Hall Master, please — no more solo raids," one elder said.
"Next time they won't send Darius alone," another added. "Next time it will be Maddox himself."
Tia didn't respond. She'd heard it all before.
She turned to go.
Zane stepped into her path. He didn't say anything — he didn't know what to say. He just stood there.
Tia looked up, her beautiful eyes shooting daggers at him. "What are you doing?”
She’d done a lot of killing recently, and a heavy aura of malice clung to her like a shroud. But beneath all that cold fury, Zane saw it—a flicker of panic.
Kieran, Hobbes, Burns, and the contingent from Cloud Hall were all watching, holding their breath. If Zane could convince Tia not to leave Oracle City again, it would be a huge victory. They all wondered how he was going to pull it off.
[MUSIC: INTIMATE TENSION]
“Tia, don’t be so stubborn,” Zane said softly, looking her right in the eyes.
His words landed like a physical blow, leaving the Spirit-Tribulation warriors in the hall completely speechless. He wasn’t talking to her like an outsider, and he definitely wasn’t acting like she was his superior.
For a split second, the tension in Tia’s shoulders seemed to ease.
[MUSIC: DARK EMOTIONAL SHIFT]
But then her gaze drifted past him and landed on Cora, who was standing just behind him. The instant she saw the subtle expression on Cora’s face, any flicker of warmth in her heart was extinguished, replaced by a glacial rage.
“What did you just call me?” she snapped, her voice cutting through the silence. “How dare you call me Tia? I am the leader of Cloud Hall. You are a member. You answer to me! Zane, you’d better learn your place.”
Watching the drama unfold, Kieran could tell things were about to go south fast. As an outsider, he had no idea what the history was between Tia and Zane. The Cloud Hall elders, of course, knew everything, but they could hardly broadcast their personal drama. Saint Morphen couldn't just announce that the leader of Cloud Hall was acting out because she was jealous.
So, Kieran quickly stepped forward, forcing a smile — but everyone in the room was watching Zane, not him, wondering if he was actually going to pull this off.
