Ep 440 - Baptism by Laws

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TEMPLE]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]

The obelisks were ancient, their surfaces covered in words engraved by master cultivators from the time of the ancient dragons—beings who understood the Laws on a level mortals could never hope to achieve.

Cultivators from the Lower World couldn't even dream of reaching that level of understanding. That's why these obelisks had been placed here: to help the Dragon-blooded deepen their own connection to the Laws.

The fundamental Laws were things like Fire, Wind, Time, and Space. From these basics, countless others had evolved. The Essence of the Mountain, for example, was an offshoot of the Earth Law. The Celestial Law was an epiphany born from the intersection of nature and the Metal Law, discovered by master weaponsmiths.

Different cultivators had a natural pull toward different Laws. Zane had chosen an obelisk that held the secrets of the Spatial Law, since Elder Howard believed he had a strong enough affinity for it to learn something.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

But as the scene unfolded before him, Elder Howard realized how profoundly wrong he had been. Every eye was fixed on the summit of Zane's obelisk. Even cultivators deep in meditation with their own monoliths broke their concentration, drawn by an inexplicable force. The pull was irresistible—no one could tear their gaze away.

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

No one present had witnessed anything remotely similar—not in their lifetimes, not in the records they'd studied. Confusion rippled through the assembled cultivators like wind across water. Even Elder Howard stood frozen; his centuries of experience could not explain what unfolded before them.

Then, after several heartbeats of stunned silence, understanding flickered across his face. His eyes widened with something approaching awe.

The legendary Baptism by Laws.

The phrase existed only in the most ancient texts, relegated to myth by generations who had never seen it manifest. Howard's hands trembled slightly—whether from excitement or trepidation, he couldn't say. Around him, disciples exchanged bewildered glances, their ignorance written plainly on their faces. They had no frame of reference for this phenomenon, no stories passed down from masters who had witnessed such a thing.

How could they? This was so much more than ordinary cultivation, something far more profound—something that belonged to an age when the world itself was younger and wilder.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]

All they saw was the obelisk trembling violently as a torrent of Law Power poured into Zane. Every cultivator watched, their faces etched with envy.

Worse, the vibrations became so intense that some cultivators couldn't keep their souls anchored to the stone. Most had struggled just to find a foothold in the first place, and now, as the tremors worsened, more and more souls were thrown off. 

[SFX: INTENSE RUMBLING]

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC CROWD PANIC]

It was a complete disaster.

The other elders began to figure it out."A Baptism by Laws," they murmured. "It hasn't happened in ages."

The baptism didn't actually make a cultivator stronger; Zane’s power and cultivation level wouldn’t increase. Instead, it did something far more profound: it strengthened a cultivator’s connection to all the Laws.

Most cultivators were born with a natural affinity for a specific Law, whether it was Thunder or Wind. Zane was a perfect example—his strong bond with the Spatial Law was what allowed him to teleport.

But after undergoing the baptism, his connection to every Law would be amplified.

Zane descended from the monolith platform, his mind still reeling from the profound insights etched into ancient stone. The crowd parted before him, whispers following in his wake.

[SFX: CROWD WHISPERS]

An elderly man emerged from the gathered disciples,  his eyes gleaming with predatory interest.

"I am Elder Jovan," he announced, his gaze fixed on Zane. "Your performance at the monoliths has not gone unnoticed. Cloud Hall is vast, and not all houses offer equal opportunities."

Elder Howard materialized beside Zane, his expression darkening.

"Elder Jovan," Howard said, his tone carrying a warning. "This disciple is already under my guidance."

Jovan waved dismissively. "Guidance? What guidance?" He turned to Zane, his voice dropping to a conspiratorial whisper. "Join me, young cultivator. I will personally provide you with five drops of True Dragon's blood—a treasure that will accelerate your breakthrough to the Soul Realm."

The offer hung in the air like a blade.

Zane's pulse quickened. True Dragon's blood was legendary, worth more than most cultivators earned in a lifetime. Five drops could shave years off his cultivation journey.

Elder Howard's jaw tightened, his hands clenching at his sides.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

Elder Howard stepped in between Zane and Elder Jovan. "Your pathetic offer isn't even worth discussing! Join my house, young man, and you will receive ten drops of True Dragon's blood!"

[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]

Zane’s mind went blank.

He hadn't planned on joining any faction when he came to the upper world. His goal was simple: complete the trials and return to The Heartlands. He still had so much to do back home—especially saving Elara, the most important person in his life.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

He never expected things to get so complicated. Now, two elders were practically fighting over him.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

Ten drops of True Dragon's blood. He remembered how a single drop had caused a dramatic change in his veins, his very bloodline. With ten drops, the transformation would be beyond anything he could imagine.

[SFX: FOOTSTEP]

Finally, Zane nodded at Elder Howard. "Sir, my name is Zane Lowell. I apologize for not introducing myself sooner—things have been a little hectic."

[MUSIC: WARM ORCHESTRAL]

Hearing this, a warm smile spread across Elder Howard's face. "Of course, my friend. Zane is a fine name! Now that you've completed your obelisk's challenge and ranked as an elite, you're qualified for the next trial. Of course, since you just received the Baptism by Laws, you could also choose to cultivate the Laws on the other obelisks. The choice is yours."

"I'd like to cultivate the other Laws first," Zane said instantly. So that was what the strange phenomenon was called—a Baptism by Laws.

While it was happening, he’d felt a powerful urge to try the other obelisks, but the rules had stopped him. Now, there was no reason to waste this opportunity.

Zane and Elder Howard continued their pleasant conversation, completely ignoring Elder Jovan. 

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TONE]

His face darkened, his expression turning sour as he was left standing alone.

Howard would regret this, he'd make sure of that.
