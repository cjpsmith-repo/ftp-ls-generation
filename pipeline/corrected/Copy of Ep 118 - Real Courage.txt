 Zane, Dalton, and Cliff sprang into action first, yet their reflexes proved woefully inadequate against the demon general's supernatural velocity. With a movement that barely registered to human eyes, the creature darted across the clearing and seized Ingrid, tucking her effortlessly under one muscular arm before vanishing into the forest.
[SFX: BODY BEING SQUEEZED]
 Never had Ingrid imagined her Concussive Blast would mark her as the demon's prime quarry. Waves of primal fear washed over her as she thrashed against its iron grip, her cultivator's strength rendered pathetically insignificant against the monster's raw power. A single, almost casual compression of its massive arm sent darkness flooding into her consciousness, her body going limp in surrender.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
[MUSIC: SOMBER TENSION]
 The remaining disciples stood paralyzed, witnessing in stunned silence as the demon general disappeared with their comrade. The young women from Maiden's Peak turned ashen, their faces drained of all color. Ingrid had been their cornerstone, their leader in all but name—the one who had kept their spirits high through countless trials.
 "What in the heavens are we going to do?" cried one of the girls, her voice cracking with desperation.
"Ingrid's been taken!" another shouted, clutching at her robes as if seeking something to ground her against the rising tide of panic.
A third disciple pressed her trembling hands against her face. "Who's going to save her now?"
 "She's not going to make it!" wailed the youngest of them, tears already streaming down her cheeks. "No one survives being taken by demons!"
Cliff, Dalton, and the other Varsity members were just as rattled. They'd actually believed that with Zane around, they stood a chance against a demon general. But watching it snatch one of them so easily changed everything.
Give chase? That was a suicide mission. Dalton could hold his own against a demon general, sure, but killing one—or even catching one that was trying to escape—was a whole other ball game.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL REVELATION]
 Zane stood frozen, his face unreadable as he watched the demon disappear into the trees. Normally, he would’ve stayed out of it. He wouldn't risk his neck for a woman he barely knew. But the sheer helplessness on Ingrid’s face as she was ripped away… it reminded him of his sister, Elara
 He hadn't seen Elara's face that day on Infernal Peak, but he imagined she must have worn that same look of raw terror.
"I'm going after it," Zane declared, his voice a low, determined growl as the muscles in his jaw clenched tight with resolve.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
 Corrupt Vitality surged through his veins like liquid lightning, coiling with primal power in his legs. The ancient energy transformed him into something more than human—a predator born to hunt. Then he vanished, leaving only a whisper of displaced air as his form blurred into the forest, racing after the demon general with unnatural speed.
A fragile spark of hope pierced through the apprentices' collective dread. They exchanged glances, their expressions shifting from despair to cautious optimism. Each had witnessed Zane's extraordinary strength firsthand—how he'd torn through enemies that should have been beyond his cultivation level. If anyone could rescue Ingrid from the clutches of that monster, it was the enigmatic outsider who seemed to defy all natural laws.
[AMBIENT: FOREST AFTERMATH]
[MUSIC: SOMBER DETERMINATION]
 Once Zane disappeared from view, the remaining disciples gathered their wits and began tending to the battlefield. Cliff organized the wounded into groups while Dalton secured the perimeter, both working with the grim efficiency of those who had narrowly escaped death.
Miraculously, despite the ferocity of the attack, only a handful of apprentices had sustained injuries, and not a single life had been lost in the brutal clash against the Demonic Grunts and their two generals. The fact hung in the air between them, unspoken but universally acknowledged.
They all recognized the uncomfortable truth as they bandaged wounds and collected scattered weapons: without Zane's intervention, the forest floor would now be carpeted with their broken bodies. 
His absence left a palpable void—a reminder that their survival thus far had hinged upon the abilities of one extraordinary individual whom many had previously scorned or feared.
[AMBIENT: FOREST MOVEMENT]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
 Not long after, a group of ten apprentices from the Skyreach Sect emerged from the woods. They were from Stormcrown Peak, and compared to the others, they'd gotten lucky. Their demon hunt had been going smoothly, with nothing but a few Demonic Grunts to deal with.
Their Core Apprentice, Preston Sterling, was leading them. Preston was ranked second among the apprentices at Stormcrown Peak, but since everyone from House Sterling was a cut above the rest, his position was basically set in stone. None of the others, not even Pim or Felix, would dare challenge him.
Preston's eyes swept the group. "Where's Zane Lowell?" he asked. "Don't tell me he's already dead. I wanted that kill for myself."
Cliff's sword cleared its scabbard. "Zane just saved every life on this field." He shook his head. "You're not qualified to talk about killing him."
Every Misty Peak disciple drew a weapon.
Preston looked at them and smiled.
[MUSIC: TENSE REVELATION]
 Dalton stepped forward to fill them in. "Things have changed. We're dealing with Demon Commanders now."
"Huh? Demon Commanders?" Preston shot back, his confident expression dissolving. The rest of the Stormcrown apprentices looked terrified. They’d been relaxed, thinking they were only up against Demonic Grunts. As long as the Varsity members did their job, that was a piece of cake.
But the mention of Demon Commanders sent a wave of fear through them. They knew none of them could handle a demon general. Running into one would be a death sentence.
A faint smile played on Dalton’s lips. "Remember how I told you not to mess with Zane?" he asked, adding, "He just killed a demon general."
Cliff cut in, "No! Two! He killed two Demon Commanders! He took one out before today!"
 Listening to them, Preston's face paled, his expression twisting in disbelief. "I don't believe it!" he finally declared.
Dalton idly twirled one of his Twin Blades, not even bothering to argue. "Believe what you want. But if you ever decide to go up against Zane, feel free. I’m sure he’ll wipe the floor with you. For now, I'd advise you not to cause any trouble. If you’re scared, stick with us. If not, you can take off. But if you keep running your mouth, I doubt all ten of you combined could take me."
[AMBIENT: UNEASY SILENCE]
 Dalton's revelation silenced the Stormcrown disciples. The humiliation of being lectured paled against the stark reality: Demon Commanders prowled the Sky Forest. What fate awaited them should they encounter such a formidable foe alone?
After an unbearably tense silence, they reluctantly abandoned their pride and remained with the group. Sacrificing dignity seemed trivial compared to sacrificing their lives to demonic claws.
Besides, the bitter feud between Zane and House Sterling belonged to Preston alone—a personal vendetta they had no desire to die for.
