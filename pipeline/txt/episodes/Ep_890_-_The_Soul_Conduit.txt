Ep 890 - The Soul Conduit

Word count: 1104

[AMBIENT: EERIE WIND]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The entire Emperor’s Isle was one massive, black cemetery.

On the shore, the other masters of the Soul Sea Realm had been waiting for some time. They watched with open curiosity as the rulers of the seven divine kingdoms arrived, trailed by Fiona, the Crown Prince, and a handful of other young warriors.

A master from the Harlen Divine Kingdom couldn’t hold back his question. “Your Majesty, why did you bring them to the Divine Emperor’s Secret Land?”

Since these masters had remained on the shore instead of venturing to The Feather Emperor’s Island, they had no idea that Zane had found the Black Star Pickax and a whole cache of Soul Cores. 

Soul Cores were so scarce that the Soul Sea masters competed fiercely each year for entry to the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. Some had even suggested fighting for them, but quickly abandoned the idea—a true battle between Soul Sea Realm masters would obliterate the entire Emperor’s Isle within moments.

So, with that problem still hanging over their heads, why were the rulers bringing along their heirs? And Zane, too? Were they really planning to give some of the precious Soul Cores to these kids?

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BUILD]

Finally, the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom offered a faint smile. “Martin,” he said, “bring out all the spare soul conduits.”

[SFX: SURPRISED GASP]

Martin, one of the masters, looked startled. “Your Majesty, just how many Soul Cores did you get? Do we really need the spares?”

[AMBIENT: QUIET CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

The soul conduit, an item used to activate a Soul Core, was an invention of the Harlen Divine Kingdom’s own blacksmiths. Before they’d set out this year, Martin had personally counted twenty-nine Soul Cores, meaning they’d need the same number of conduits. 

Normally, they considered themselves lucky to find even a single Soul Core after clearing out all the Stone-Eater Bugs on The Feather Emperor’s Island. Finding two was unheard of; it hadn’t happened in decades.

Had the rulers gotten so incredibly lucky that they’d found three or more?

The conduits themselves weren’t particularly valuable, so Martin had brought two hundred of them just in case. But it didn’t matter how many conduits you had if there were no Soul Cores to use them with.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]

A thin smile touched the emperor’s lips. “Bring them all out. We have more than a hundred Soul Cores this time.”

[SFX: STUNNED SILENCE, THEN ERUPTION OF VOICES]

“More than a hundred? That means everyone can enter the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land —”

“How is that possible? There shouldn’t be that many in the mine —”

“The Stone-Eater Bugs digest the cores if you wait too long. You can’t just pull that many out at once —”

One of the masters leaned forward. “Your Majesty — did someone find a way to open the top leaves?”

The Emperor nodded. “We have Zane to thank. He retrieved every one of those cores himself.”

The masters who had come over from Feather Emperor’s Island were already smiling. They’d been there. They’d seen it. But the tribulation — the lotus — that still overshadowed everything else, even the Black Star Pickax.

The Emperor recounted it all: the tribulation cloud covering the entire continent, the lotus blooming, both layers, thirty-five petals. The ascending passage. The black eye. The mountains.

The waiting masters listened in silence. When he finished, they looked at Zane differently. The Heavenly Stellar Secret Land suddenly felt like a footnote. Repairing the ascending passage — that was something none of them had dared to hope for in their lifetimes.

Martin brought out the soul conduits without another word.

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL HUM]

Zane studied the conduits. They were small boxes — wider at the top, narrowing toward the base, each resting on four small legs. He reached out with his soul sense to examine them and was immediately repelled. Whatever they were made of, it blocked spiritual perception entirely.

A moment later, three shadowy figures appeared beside the conduits. Even as silhouettes, their forms were recognizable. Fiona’s soul seemed delicate, while Callan’s stood tall and strong.

War King Wind spoke before anyone entered. “This year’s exploration will likely be the largest in recent years. The corpses you choose to occupy will determine what you can do inside. Choose well — the strongest bodies go to those with the strongest souls.”

One warrior was set aside; his soul had been injured, and it wasn’t safe for him to enter. He would wait on the shore.

Seeing this, Zane didn’t hesitate. He tossed the Soul Core from his hand straight into an empty conduit.

[SFX: MAGICAL PULL]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Instantly, he felt a powerful magnetic force pulling from it, aimed directly at his soul. Instead of fighting it, he let it draw him in.

[SFX: SOUL SEPARATION]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CHOIR]

In a flash, Zane felt his soul merge with the Core. It passed through the conduit and emerged on the other side, now untethered from his body and free to wander.

One of the Soul Sea masters drifted alongside him. “The conduit extracts your soul and fuses it with the core,” he said. “Once you’re inside a corpse, the core lets you control it temporarily. The stronger your soul, the more powerful a body you can take.”

As his soul floated free, Fiona, Callan, Randy, and the others all turned to look.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL SOUL REALM]

Randy’s soul, like the Crown Prince’s and Fiona’s, was a grayish-green—the color of instability. The souls of the Soul Sea Realm masters were solid, pitch-black, signifying their stability in the Fighting Soul Realm.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

But Zane’s soul was different. It glowed with a vibrant green light—the faint but unmistakable shimmer of the world’s will.

Fiona’s thought reached out to him, laced with awe. Zane, your soul! 

Before, Zane’s soul had always been hidden away in his mind, invisible to anyone who couldn’t peer directly into his thoughts. Now, out in the open, there was no hiding it.

A faint smile touched his spectral face as he called out to the others. “Come on. Let’s go.”

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT CEMETERY]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

One by one, the souls that had merged with the Soul Cores began to move, drifting deeper into the Divine Emperor’s Secret Land—a perfectly preserved cemetery. After flying for some time, they stopped before an entrance where a single lamp hung beside the gate.

[SFX: ECHOING VOICE]

“Everyone, wait a moment,” a master’s voice echoed in their minds, his consciousness wrapping around Zane and the younger warriors.
