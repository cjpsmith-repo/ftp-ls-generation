Ep 573 - Advance Freely
Word count: 1157
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
Zane rolled his eyes. A pack of Ogres, each the size of a small mountain, was trying their best to pick a fight with him. He just shook his head at their shamelessness.
They were just a bunch of War Fighters. Why were they so eager to die?
He gave the fierce-looking Ogres a dismissive glance. Well, if they were that determined to hand over their Lucky Light, who was he to refuse? He was just about to take them up on it.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
But then, a blue-black Ogre emerged from behind the others.
This one was smaller than his clansmen, but he carried himself with a commanding presence. His eyes held a cold, sharp glint that was unnervingly focused. When his gaze swept over the crowd, it felt like a blade scraping across their skin.
He was only a War General, but the intense pressure rolling off him was enough to intimidate anyone. Even Zane felt it, a flicker of surprise touching his face.
[SFX: SHARP BREATH]
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
"Get out of the way," the War General said, his voice ice.
When the Ogres blocking the path saw who was behind them, their faces flooded with a mixture of fear and respect.
[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]
[AMBIENT: FEARFUL WHISPERS]
"It's Elder Boris! Move it!"
"Don't push! Our apologies, Elder Boris."
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
The Ogres scrambled to clear a path. In moments, Zane found himself face-to-face with the slender War General. At least he’d caught the name—Boris—from the others.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
Boris stepped forward, fixing his cold gaze on Zane. "You're human, but no ordinary War Fighter."
"What's it to you?" Zane shot back, meeting his stare without a hint of fear.
[MUSIC: DARK THREATENING STRINGS]
"It’s not just the Ogres who want you dead. The Giants, the Orcs, and…" Boris’s gaze shifted to Phoebe, who stood just behind Zane. A flicker of surprise entered his eyes. "And the Demon Night. We have dozens of ways to kill you, but I think the arena is most fitting. I'll be waiting for you on the tenth floor of the Tower of Sin. I hope you don't run, or decide to camp out down here forever."
[MUSIC: DEFIANT HEROIC THEME]
Zane let out a short laugh. When the smile faded from his face, he replied coolly, "The only reason I came to the Tower of Sin is to climb. What floor you're waiting on means nothing to me. And you don't need to make some big announcement. You're not nearly important enough to be on my radar."
[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]
Zane’s words made every Ogre’s jaw hit the floor.
It was true Boris was only a War General, but his status among the Ogres was higher than even many War Lords.
The Ogres had two sacred lands on the main continent: the Flaming Place and the Dread Place. Boris was the most powerful prodigy to ever come from the Flaming Place.
Many of the Ogres were furious. A few looked ready to explode.
But Boris raised a hand, cutting them off before they could start. He looked at Zane with something that might have been satisfaction. "Not bad. There are very few humans with that kind of nerve. I'm looking forward to this more than ever."
He turned to go. Then, as if remembering something, he paused and addressed the Ogres around him. "Don't throw your lives away challenging him. Let him advance freely."
[SFX: ANGRY MURMUR]
"But, Elder Boris—" one of the Ogres started, unable to hide his frustration.
Boris's eyes flicked toward the speaker, and the Ogre's defiance evaporated instantly.
Boris said nothing more. He turned and walked away.
The remaining Ogres were still glaring at Zane, but with Boris's order hanging over them, not one of them moved to block his path.
[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Phoebe was sharp. One look from Zane was all she needed. Offering an elegant smile, she gave him some space. He needed to practice.
She had been a huge help, but she couldn't trail him forever just for the sake of his sword spirit, Lily. Phoebe wasn't trying to pry, but the information she needed about her queen was too critical to ignore. It was the only reason she was sticking so close to him.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS - WALKING]
As they passed through the Tower on their way out, Zane glanced at the arena. Battles were still raging — they never stopped. There was no day or night in the Tower of Sin. For most of the creatures here, life came down to three things: fighting, cultivating, and dying.
"What did Boris mean by 'advance freely'?" Zane asked.
Phoebe smiled, two small dimples appearing on her face. "To move up a floor, you need points. But sometimes a fighter is so strong that no one will challenge them. No challenges means no points, and no points means they're stuck — they can never reach a hundred no matter how long they wait."
"So they just stay on the first floor forever?"
"That's what the free advancement rule is for," Phoebe said. "If a fighter goes three days without a single challenger — three empty stages in a row — it gets recorded. After that, they can move up without needing the points."
Zane nodded slowly. It made sense. Even a system built by rival races had to be fair enough to function.
[AMBIENT: NIGHT WILDERNESS]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]
After putting some distance between them, Zane found a secluded spot and sat cross-legged. He tilted his head back, looking to the sky where the stars were so dense they seemed to melt together, forming a shimmering pillar of silver light.
"Can we start now?" Zane asked.
The Red Dragon's voice echoed in his mind. Not bad. This place is perfect for cultivating the Stellar Body.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
As the voice faded, a flood of complex characters and information rushed into Zane's mind.
The second half of the Stellar Body technique is lost, but the first half contains all the core principles. It says the first step is to activate your Star Power. And like they say, the first step is always the hardest. You don't have to get it on your first try, but feel free to give it a shot.
Mastering a Transcendent Divine Might wasn't supposed to be easy.
When the Red Dragon himself had mastered the Stellar Body, his own power had been at a level Zane couldn't even comprehend. Even then, it had taken him five full years to master the technique.
Zane said nothing, sitting quietly on the ground as he processed the flood of information.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
Two hours later, the stars had shifted silently across the sky. Zane’s eyes snapped open, a sharp light glinting within them as he stared up at the heavens.
