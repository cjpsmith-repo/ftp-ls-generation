Ep 108 - Predator to Prey
Word Count: 1236
[AMBIENT: DEMONIC PRESENCE]
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
"Is Zane smiling? Is he actually laughing in the face of a demon?"
"It's not that big a deal. He's only passed the first two rune circles."
"You wanna try it? Look around! Who else would dare to laugh like that right now? Even Henry isn't cracking a smile!"
Henry's face turned white at their words. If he had to face that demon, he’d force a smile, too. After all, smiling was a hell of a lot less embarrassing than crying. Deep down, he vowed to find the right time to put Zane in his place for constantly showing him up. As he plotted his revenge, Henry’s mood began to lift.
The murmurs reached Zane, making him feel a little self-conscious. To avoid any more misunderstandings, he wiped the smile off his face as he entered the second rune circle, his expression hardening into a scowl.
Inside the second circle, the threat felt more real, but he could still withstand the demon's aura. Zane pressed on, and soon enough, he’d crossed the third, fourth, and then the fifth rune circle.
[SFX: DEMONIC ROAR]
The moment he stepped into the fifth circle, the massive demon shuddered. It lifted its grotesque head and let out a roar aimed right at Zane. The roar of an ordinary monster wouldn't have fazed him, but this was different. There was something deeply menacing in this demon's cry.
[MUSIC: INTENSE HEARTBEAT]
[SFX: DARK ENERGY SWIRL]
The sound hit him like a physical blow. His heart hammered against his ribs, and a tremor ran through him.
Zane’s heart pounded even faster as the malevolent energy swirled around him. The demon's gaze was fixed on him as if he were the only living thing in existence. Fear was a natural reaction; anyone would be terrified, trapped by a monster like this. His heart hammered, and an involuntary shiver wracked his body.
[AMBIENT: DARK OPPRESSIVE ATMOSPHERE]
Primal instincts screamed through Zane's veins, urging him to flee, to seek shelter from this abomination. Every fiber of rational thought demanded retreat from the looming threat. He was not immune—the overwhelming compulsion to flinch backward clawed at his resolve, while the demon's suffocating aura of negativity pressed against his consciousness like a physical weight.
"This demon's aura is strange," Zane thought, analyzing the sensation even as it threatened to overwhelm him, "but it's not enough to stop me."
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
He squared his shoulders and faced the demon directly, an unbreakable determination gleaming in his eyes. The creature's gaze locked with his, a silent battle of wills unfolding in the charged space between them. Where others had faltered, Zane found strength in the depths of his purpose.
His resolve crystallized into something harder than steel, and he took a deliberate, defiant step toward the monstrous entity.
[SFX: DEMONIC GROWL AND HOWL]
The demon's response reverberated through the chamber, its fury palpable at this human's unprecedented audacity.
Demons, much like humans, were smart enough to recognize a mortal threat. But this one reveled in the mental torment it inflicted on weaker prey. Until Zane, it had been confident its aura alone could scare any human half to death.
[SFX: DEAFENING DEMONIC ROAR]
But this human… he seemed completely unfazed. Enraged, the demon let out another deafening roar. A vicious wave of malevolent energy surged toward Zane, crashing over him and wrapping around him like a tide.
The energy shackled him like invisible chains, pulling and tearing at his resolve. His steps slowed, his courage wavering as he fought to reach the next circle.
[AMBIENT: CRUSHING PRESSURE]
When he finally stumbled into the seventh rune circle, he stopped, forced to close his eyes. The pressure was immense, a crushing weight on his mind. He was struggling.
"He reached the seventh circle! Remarkable... but why has he halted?" whispered one pledge, fingers digging anxiously into their palms.
"His eyes are shut tight. What in blazes does he think he's doing?" muttered another, voice trembling with a mixture of awe and disbelief.
From nearby, Isla's voice cut through the noise. "When facing a demon, you must have an absolutely calm heart. This demon's strength is only equivalent to a Nature Level cultivator's. Ask yourself — would a Nature Level cultivator make you this afraid?"
A Nature Level cultivator. Zane almost laughed. Felix Sterling was Soul Realm and hadn't broken him. Why was this thing doing what Felix couldn't?
Then it came to him. The State of Selflessness.
He pulled his consciousness out of his body entirely, looking down at himself from outside. His body was trembling with fear — but he was not his body. From this detached place, the demonic aura found nothing to grip. The fear dissolved.
When he opened his eyes, there was nothing in them — no courage, no terror. Just clarity.
Zane stood motionless, merely a foot away from the eighth circle. The demon observed this moment of vulnerability, its yellowed eyes narrowing with predatory calculation.
[SFX: GUTTURAL GROWL]
Slowly, it tilted its massive head, unhinging its cavernous jaw to reveal row upon row of gleaming, razor-sharp teeth that dripped with viscous saliva. A low, guttural growl rumbled from deep within its chest.
[MUSIC: SUDDEN ATTACK]
Then, with frightening speed, it lunged forward, its massive form cutting through the air as it aimed to clamp its lethal jaws around Zane's unprotected head.
[SFX: SCREAMS]
A wave of horrified screams swept through the plaza.
Just as its jaws were about to close around his skull, Zane's fist shot out. In that same instant, a Dragon Scale flared to life inside his mind.
A single Dragon Scale Shard granted its user incredible power, far beyond any fighter of the same rank. So what kind of power would a hundred of them, igniting all at once, unleash?
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
Slam!
[SFX: SICKENING CRUNCH]
Before its fangs could even touch him, a sickening crunch echoed across the plaza as Zane's fist connected with the demon's skull.
Zane hadn't even drawn on his Life Energy. That was pure, raw physical strength.
[SFX: DEMON DISINTEGRATION]
Still, the impact hit with the force of a tidal wave. In the blink of an eye, the demon dissolved into a fine, bloody mist. The overwhelming power of the blow had shot straight through its body, incinerating its flesh to ash and shattering its bones into dust.
The other pledges just stood there, mouths agape, stunned by the horrifying spectacle. In theory, since the demon was sealed, many of them could have killed it… as long as they kept their distance.
But their failure wasn't one of strength; it was a failure to overcome their own inner demons. A few had done exceptionally well, reaching the seventh or even eighth circle. They were braver than most, able to suppress their fear long enough to get close. That was their strategy: suppression.
Unlike his peers, Zane employed a radically different approach. Rather than merely suppressing his fear, he had eliminated it entirely through trance combat, rendering the demon's malevolent aura utterly powerless against him. This extraordinary mental state didn't just shield him—it transformed him into something the creature had never encountered before: a predator immune to its psychological weapons. Standing before the beast, Zane faced it with the cold, calculating presence of something far more dangerous than any demon. The creature, for the first time in its existence, had unknowingly become the prey.
