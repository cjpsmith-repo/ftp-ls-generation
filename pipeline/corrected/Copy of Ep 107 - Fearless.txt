Ep 107 - Fearless

[MUSIC: HEROIC THEME]
 There was no doubt Johnson was a master fighter. He’d been a High-level candidate for years, just one step away from reaching the Nature Level. All he had to do was clear the final hurdle: converting his Life Energy into Vitality.
But for all those years, he'd been stuck right there. Even his disciplined training at Misty Peak hadn't been enough to get him over that last hump. Every fighter knew the deal: the only way to get stronger was through constant battle, to overcome challenges with grit and patience.
If Johnson just kept training at Misty Peak, it might take him another ten years to break through. 
That’s why he wanted to seize this opportunity—to push his limits and hopefully fast-track his progress.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS PRESENCE]
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
 He didn't flinch as he confronted the demon. Its corrupting aura washed over him, stirring a primal fear deep in his gut. A voice screamed in his head: Run.
But with every pledge watching, Johnson forced himself forward, one step at a time.
He quickly crossed the outermost rune circle Isla had drawn. The demon's hideous eyes locked onto him as he approached. Though the creature was physically weak, unable to land a real blow, its aura of pure evil was as potent as ever.
[SFX: DEMONIC GURGLE]
 The demon gurgled wetly, its throat producing a sound that slithered through the air like something viscous and tainted. 
Though none of the pledges could decipher the creature's language, the noise resonated as a deliberate, mocking laugh that seemed to penetrate their very souls. The sound carried an ancient malice that transcended mere communication.
[MUSIC: INTENSE HORROR]
 In an instant, Johnson's complexion transformed from determined to ghostly white. An overwhelming, primal terror seized his body, flooding every nerve with the instinct to flee. Despite being the fourth-ranked fighter at Misty Peak, a warrior who had faced countless dangers, he scrambled backward with desperate, ungraceful movements, as though invisible claws were reaching for his throat. His eyes, wide with horror, reflected something the others couldn't see—something that shattered his courage like brittle glass.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
 The pledges watched in stunned silence as Johnson retreated, his reputation crumbling with each backward step. A collective realization dawned across the gathering—a profound understanding that shifted their perception of strength.
True bravery, they now understood, had little to do with raw power or cultivation level. It manifested in the ability to stand unwavering when confronted by overwhelming odds. Like a tiny insect defiantly positioning itself before an oncoming freight train—an objectively foolish decision, perhaps, but one that commanded a grudging respect. There was something noble, something fundamentally admirable about the courage to face certain defeat yet refuse to yield to fear.
[SFX: WRITING ON PAPER]
Isla recorded the name of each pledge and how far they got. Based on their performance, she'd pick the top five to receive special training in demon slaying.
Seeing the pledge at the Marrow Refining Level make it to the sixth circle gave a lot of people their confidence back. Now that they understood this was a test of courage, not just raw power, they felt more prepared to face the demon themselves.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
 One after another, hundreds of pledges tested their nerve against the demon's corrupting influence, each with a different result. The worst showing was a pledge who completely lost it the second he stepped into the first circle. The best performance, by far, belonged to Henry.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
 As the number-one-ranked pledge, Henry set a high bar. Facing the demon, he walked steadily forward, a blue steel longsword in hand. The other pledges held their breath as he stepped confidently into the seventh rune circle. To everyone’s amazement, he didn't even slow down until he reached the eighth.
[SFX: DEMONIC ROAR]
 Only after the demon let out a horrifying roar did Henry finally turn back. His face was ashen. Still, the pledges were relieved to see that his only sign of fear was the loss of color in his face. All in all, Henry had proven he was the bravest one there.
Once he was clear of Isla’s rune circles, Henry stretched his arms and took a few deep breaths to steady himself. After a few moments, his usual relaxed smile returned.
He scanned the crowd of pledges, and his gaze landed on Zane. The shift in his attention didn't go unnoticed.
[MUSIC: RIVALRY THEME]
 Henry should have fought in the competition against Eagle Peak, but he'd been away on a solo training mission and missed his chance. When he got back, all anyone could talk about was the pledge who had single-handedly won the whole thing for Misty Peak.
[MUSIC: TENSE REVELATION]
 It was Zane.
Zane already knew about Henry’s reputation. Henry, on the other hand, hadn’t paid Zane much mind until now. He'd first heard the kid's name when Zane, still a newcomer, had fought Tristram. Beating Tristram, one of Misty Peak's top thirty fighters, was a huge upset and had definitely turned some heads.
Henry hadn't thought much of it at the time.
Zane met his gaze with a simple nod before turning his attention to the demon in the distance. Then, he started walking.
The demon's aura was a peculiar kind of pressure, different from any other. Most Auras could be used offensively, but this one was designed purely to amplify a person's deepest fears. You couldn't judge a demon's power by the terror it induced; that fear was just a natural talent, not a true measure of its strength.
Possessing extraordinary strength and an unyielding spirit, Zane barely registered the impact of a standard Aura. Though not an attack, the demon's presence broadcast an unmistakable message: I embody fear itself.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER CROSSED]
[AMBIENT: DEMONIC PRESENCE]
 As he crossed the threshold into the first rune circle, the aura engulfed him. A sudden pang of dread pierced his heart.
Yet it failed to consume him.
[MUSIC: CONFIDENT HEROIC THEME]
 A smile spread across Zane's face as tension melted from his frame. Without hesitation, he strode confidently into the second circle.
Among all who had faced the creature, he alone had managed to smile in its terrifying presence—and his eyes now gleamed with something that made even the demon pause.
