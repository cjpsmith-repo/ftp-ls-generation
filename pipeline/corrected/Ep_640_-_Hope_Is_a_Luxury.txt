Ep 640 - Hope Is a Luxury
Word count: 1021
[AMBIENT: CAVE ECHOES]
[SFX: BIRD SHRIEKS]
The Shadow-maws might not have been smart, but instinct screamed at them that a powerful monster was just ahead. They swarmed to block the path, a living wall of feathers and claws trying to keep us from getting any closer, their shrieks echoing off the cave walls.
[SFX: MASSIVE WING FLAPPING]
The cloud of birds thickened, circling the small group before swooping down into a giant, feathered tornado.
“We have to fight!” someone yelled, but the words were drowned in the flapping of a thousand wings.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Fabian’s face was grim. He was toast. It was written all over him. The other top-tier War Lords looked just as hopeless. A couple managed to keep their composure, but the rest were already defeated. They knew that even if they fought to the last man, it wouldn’t make a difference. Hope was a luxury they no longer had.
[SFX: CROWD GRUMBLES]
They collapsed into a chorus of complaints, regret plastered on their faces. Even Zane’s expression had darkened. He knew that getting away from this many Shadow-maws was going to be a nightmare, even for him.
But he’d never been the type to give up. He fought, always, even when staring down certain death.
Now, though, the War Lords’ will to fight had completely evaporated. It was clear they’d already surrendered.
[MUSIC: DARK DETERMINATION]
“They’re just a flock of birds!” Zane roared, his voice cutting through the noise. “You call yourselves elite War Lords and you’re acting this pathetic?” He glared at them, his eyes burning with contempt. “So what if we die? Are you going to go down fighting, or are you going to die like cowards? Which is it? Since we’re going to die anyway, why not go down swinging?”
As his shouts echoed through the cavern, a murderous energy rolled off him in waves. Tapping into the power he’d gained from the Killing Sword Mountain, he unleashed a torrent of pure Malice.
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY BLAST]
With a final yell, he threw his fists toward the swirling vortex of birds, sending a shockwave of force crashing into them.
Lavender and Yolande had been silent until now, but they didn’t look ready to give up, either. They were both from the upper world, after all, with the strength of truly elite warriors. They could stay calm even in the face of death. It was the ideal mindset for any fighter—not being fearless, but understanding how to face the possibility of dying and using that clarity to sharpen your focus.
The War Lords looked at the three of them — Zane, Lavender, Yolande — and something shifted. If these people weren't giving up, how could they?
[SFX: MASSIVE ELEMENTAL ASSAULT]
One by one, they turned and unleashed everything they had. Flame, frost, lightning, fist energy, sword light — it all tore into the swarm at once. The combined assault staggered the Shadow-maws, punching holes in the flock and dropping dozens from the sky. For one brief moment, the tornado hesitated.
Then the survivors regrouped and pressed forward again.
"It's really over—"
"Clench your teeth!" someone else snapped back. "If we die, we die fighting!"
[SFX: DEEP POOL RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: SUDDEN SHIFT]
Then the tongue came.
[SFX: MASSIVE TONGUE WHIP]
It shot out from a deep black pool in the darkness — over a hundred feet wide, stretching three to four miles in an instant, sweeping across the entire swarm in a single pass. Compared to it, the Shadow-maws were mosquitoes.
"Watch the mucus — it's poison!" Fabian shouted.
He was too late for one of them. A peak War Lord caught a single drip on his shoulder.
[SFX: BODY DISSOLVING IN ACID]
There was no scream — no time for one. His body dissolved, piece by piece, weapon and all, until there was nothing left. Even the low-grade relic in his hand was gone. Zane watched it happen and felt a cold knot form in his chest. His own body was said to be impervious to poison — but looking at what that mucus had just done to a relic, he wasn't so sure it would hold.
Everyone else dodged. The tongue rolled and swept, and wherever it passed, Shadow-maws stuck to it like flies to paper. Most of the swarm was gone in seconds, dissolved by the mucus before the tongue even pulled back. The remaining dozens shrieked and scattered into the dark.
Zane just kept staring straight ahead, frozen in place.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DISCOVERY]
His eyes flashed green, cutting through the darkness. In the distance, a massive black pool. And in that pool — a toad the size of a mountain, most of its body submerged, only two house-sized eyes breaking the surface. They glowed dark red, scanning the cave with slow, ancient patience.
The tongue rolled back into its mouth. The Bull-Toad sat still for a moment. Then, unsatisfied, it cried out again.
[SFX: DEEP BULL-TOAD CROAK]
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS RUMBLING]
[SFX: HEAVY STONE IMPACT]
He watched as the Goliath Bull-Toad heaved its two massive forelimbs onto the stone ledge.
It’s coming over, he thought, his heart pounding against his ribs.
At that same moment, the other War Lords were celebrating their ridiculously narrow escape. They couldn’t believe a giant tongue had just shot out of nowhere and saved them from the Shadow-maws. Sure, its mucus had dissolved one of their own, but at least most of them had made it.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
But Zane just kept staring straight ahead, frozen in place.
“Alright, everyone,” Fabian said cautiously, his voice low. “Let’s get out of here. Now. We’re not technically in the Goliath Bull-Toad’s territory, but we’re way too close. If it—”
Before he could finish, Zane screamed, “Run! Everyone, scatter!”
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY BURST]
[SFX: WHOOSH]
In that same instant, the power of the Dragon Scales erupted from his body, and he launched himself into the sky with a powerful leap.
When the others heard Zane’s warning and saw him take off, a wave of pure dread washed over them. The group of War Lords broke apart, scrambling in every direction.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
[SFX: GROUND SHAKING]
Just as they did, the massive tongue slammed into the exact spot where they had all been standing a second before.
The impact was deafening, leaving an eighty-foot-wide crater in the ground.
[SFX: SIZZLING ACID]
Mucus dripped from the tongue, sizzling as it ate into the scorched earth.
Fear flashed across their faces. For a heartbeat, everyone held their breath.
[SFX: FAST WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]
Then the tongue moved again, shooting up from the crater and rocketing straight for Zane.
