Ep 426 - Cedric the Converted

Word count: 1054

[AMBIENT: ICE PALACE ECHO]

[SFX: BOOTS SKIDDING ON ICE]

Cedric burst into the ice palace, his boots skidding across the slick floor as he fought to keep his balance. His arms windmilled briefly before he caught himself against a pillar. It was a clumsy entrance—something unheard of for a practitioner of the Inner-Core Realm, especially one of Cedric's standing.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Tia's voice sliced through the cavernous hall before he could even offer a greeting.  "Cedric, what is it?" 

She rose from her meditation cushion, her expression darkening as she took in his disheveled appearance. The interruption alone would have annoyed her, but the wild look in his eyes sent a thread of unease through her chest. In all their years working together, she had never seen him like this—not even when facing dangerous formations or volatile arrays.

"Why the panic?" she demanded, stepping forward. "Is something wrong with the protective arrays?"

The look on his face was one of pure terror, and a sliver of confusion pierced her composure. His hands trembled slightly at his sides, and a thin sheen of sweat glistened on his brow despite the palace's frigid temperature. It couldn't be about Zane, could it? The thought seemed absurd. What could a mere beginner have possibly done to reduce Cedric—the Heartlands' foremost rune expert—to this state?

"No, the arrays are fine," Cedric managed, catching his breath in ragged gasps. His chest heaved as though he'd sprinted the entire distance. "It's… it's about Zane." 

He immediately dipped into a bow, perhaps hoping the formal gesture might soften whatever storm was about to break. "My apologies for the intrusion, Lady Tia."

Tia acknowledged his bow with a slight nod, her brow furrowing. "Zane? What happened? Is that why you came bursting in here?" It seemed like a ridiculous reason to interrupt her. Cedric’s job was simple: talk Zane out of his little quest to learn runes and get him back to his proper training. It was what they did with all the beginners who got distracted. This whole thing felt… off.

"Yes, exactly. I need to talk to you about him," Cedric replied, his voice still tight with urgency.

A small smile played on Tia's lips. "What is it? Did you do as I asked? Has he lost his confidence?" As Cloud Hall's top runic expert, Cedric's knowledge was unmatched in all the Heartlands. A few choice words from him should have been more than enough to crush a novice's ambition.

Cedric had thought so, too. His plan had been simple: find Zane, look over whatever rudimentary runes he’d managed to scratch out, and tear them apart with criticism. Beginners always made mistakes, and Cedric could spot flaws in the work of even master rune makers. A kid like Zane should have been an open book.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL REVELATION]

But the rune Zane had created was unlike anything he had ever seen. The brushwork was perfect, the texture already activated and humming with power. How could he criticize that? A hot flush of shame had washed over him. He couldn't even manage that technique. How was he supposed to crush Zane’s confidence when the kid’s skills already surpassed his own?

"No," Cedric finally answered, shaking his head. "He hasn't."

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

"What?" Tia's smile vanished. She studied Cedric's face, noting the pallor beneath his usual composure. "You look terrible. What exactly happened?"

Instead of answering, Cedric bowed his head and held out a piece of parchment, his hand trembling slightly.

As a runic expert herself, Tia took it and examined the work with practiced eyes. She didn't recognize the specific rune or its function, but the craftsmanship stopped her breath. The lines of the divine texture flowed with fluid grace, each stroke imbued with utter perfection. It was the kind of work she'd only seen in ancient texts, preserved from masters long dead.

Her fingers tightened on the parchment's edges.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

"Is this real?" she demanded, her gaze snapping up to meet his. A frostiness crept into her eyes, and her voice turned so icy it made Cedric's heart clench. "Did Zane draw this?"

Cedric answered with a weary smile that held no triumph, only resignation. "Yes, Zane drew it. I watched him activate it myself. I have no idea how he learned the perfect brushwork technique, or who could have possibly taught him."

Tia looked down at the parchment again, as if hoping she'd misread it the first time. But the truth remained unchanged, mocking her carefully laid plans.

"I see," she said at last, nodding slowly. Each word emerged measured and cold. "So this is why you interrupted me."

Cedric nodded. "Yes. This is why. I'm afraid I can't complete the task you gave me. In fact… I think Zane might be the key to helping us with the order’s protective arrays. The ancient arrays of the Ethereal Spirit Sect were all drawn with perfect brushwork. If we can combine that skill with our own sixth-Tier designs, Zane could make Cloud Hall's defenses the strongest in the Heartlands!"

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Tia didn't reply. She had never expected this. Not only had Cedric failed to crush the boy’s spirit, but he’d returned a true believer.

Could that young man, Zane, really be that much of a prodigy?

She was lost in thought for a long moment. Finally noticing Cedric still lingering anxiously in the hall, she dismissed him with a wave of her hand. "I understand. You may go. I need to think about this."

Cedric let out a visible sigh of relief and quickly departed.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL MIST]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL MEDITATION]

After a long while alone, Tia returned to her seat on the dais. She settled into the familiar position, feeling the cool stone beneath her as she crossed her legs. A thick, pearlescent mist began to swirl around her once more, responding to the subtle shift in her spiritual energy.

The meditation rune had changed everything. Her initial plan to crush his confidence now seemed shortsighted. If he truly possessed knowledge of such advanced techniques, forcing him to abandon that path would be wasteful. Besides, she needed him to reach the Soul Realm, and perhaps this strange pursuit would forge his spiritual power faster than conventional training.

Let him continue, she decided as she sank back into her meditation, her consciousness beginning to drift into the deeper layers of the Goddess Path. Let's see just how far he can go.
