Ep 707 - Conflicting Feelings
Word count: 1017
Cora never expected things to go this far.
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL CITY]
Ever since she was a girl, she had mastered everything she touched. The lyre, the chessboard, the painter’s brush—all bent to her will. Her natural talent for the martial arts was enough to make seasoned warriors burn with envy. Add to that her stunning beauty, and it was no wonder men were constantly falling at her feet.
Back home in Ethereal City and at the Ethereal Spirit Sect, she was treated like an untouchable goddess, a divine presence that ordinary people could only dream of approaching. Countless gifted young men had tried everything to win her over.
[MUSIC: SOMBER PIANO]
Yes, Cora had been overflowing with arrogance. She never imagined a day like this would ever come. But this decision had come to her cleanly, without the panic she might have expected.
She'd watched Zane in the marsh — not the fighting, though that had been something — but the moments in between. The way he'd taken the Black Feathered Snake's bite without flinching. The way he'd pulled her down into the mud and breathed a warning against her ear instead of just leaving her there, which he easily could have done.
And when he'd talked about his sister, his voice had gone flat in a way she recognized — not detachment, but the kind of control a person uses when something hurts too much to approach directly. He'd been carrying that for a long time.
She'd seen what Maddox did to Ethereal City; she had no illusions about what would happen if he got his hands on her. She was a component in it. A virgin with the Violet Core Physique — that was all she was to him.
The Soul Harvesting Ritual wasn't some vague threat; it could be the end of life as they knew it.
The thought made something harden in her chest.
She was Elder Xander's great-granddaughter. She had spent her entire life being exceptional, being chosen, being set apart. And now, when it actually mattered, she was supposed to wait quietly and hope the man in front of her could hold off a Life-and-Death Realm master long enough for her to hide in the dark?
No.
If she did this, Maddox lost his third component. The ritual couldn't start. Zane didn't have to go die. His sister stayed alive a little longer while he figured out how to get to her. One decision, and the whole equation shifted.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
As they came together, the darkness around them changed. A faint glow began seeping from Cora's skin — soft and purple, threads of light curling outward like silk unspooling from a cocoon. The light spread and condensed, wrapping around both of them until they were enclosed in a quiet, luminous shell. It was the Violet Core Physique releasing something it had held in reserve — a deep, concentrated energy that had nowhere to go but out.
[AMBIENT: DARK PALACE CORRIDOR]
Lavender, stationed at the corridor entrance with her spear across her knees, kept her eyes on the dark passage ahead. "Zane," she said, her voice flat. "There are hundreds of forks in this palace. Maddox won't find this spot quickly. But quickly is still relative."
No response. Lavender curled her lip and turned back to the dark.
Slowly, the cocoon of light began to dissolve, the purple energy seeping inward — into both of them. The Violet Core Physique was, among other things, the ideal body for dual cultivation. The energy it released in this way didn't dissipate. It absorbed. Whatever Maddox had intended to harvest with his ritual, a portion of it was now flowing through Zane instead, settling into his body like something that had been waiting to find a home.
Neither of them fully understood what had just passed between them. But both of them felt it.
[AMBIENT: COLD STONE CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC CELLO]
When it was over, Cora lay on the cold stone floor, her chest rising and falling, a fine sheen of sweat across her skin. She stared up at the dark ceiling. The rage she'd carried into this place was still there — she could feel it, banked and ready. She still intended to make him pay for all of it, someday, when they were out of this place, and she had her Vitality back, and circumstances were less insane. That hadn't changed.
What had changed was harder to name. He wasn't what she'd expected. She wasn't sure what she'd expected — cruelty, indifference, something to confirm every terrible thing she'd assumed about him. Instead, she just felt confused, and confusion was somehow worse.
[SFX: FABRIC RUSTLING]
Zane was already on his feet. He pulled a loose set of clothes from his Storage Ring, tossed them in her direction, and immediately turned his back, refusing to meet her eyes.
[MUSIC: BITTER PIANO]
A fresh spike of anger shot through her. You took my virginity, she thought bitterly, and now you can’t even bear to look at me?
The worst part was that she was right.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REFLECTION]
Zane had been a virgin, too. What had just happened was as new to him as it was to her, and now that the desperate act was over, a hot wave of awkwardness washed over him. He wasn't dismissing her. He just had absolutely no idea what to do with himself.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
To Cora, though, his shame just looked like denial—as if he was trying to pretend it had never happened.
"Get dressed," Zane said, his voice flat, his back still to her. "We need to find a way out of here."
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
For some reason she couldn't explain, she refused. "No!" she snapped, the word flying out before she could stop it. "I don’t want to!"
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL PIANO]
A hot blush immediately flooded her cheeks. She couldn’t believe the words had come out of her own mouth.
Never in her life had she imagined she could be so shameless, so utterly raw, in front of a man. And look where it had gotten her.
