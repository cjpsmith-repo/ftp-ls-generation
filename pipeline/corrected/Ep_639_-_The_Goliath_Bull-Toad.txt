Ep 639 - The Goliath Bull-Toad
Word count: 963
[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT DRUMS]
Nobody slowed down. The Bull-Toad's croak was still echoing off the cave walls, and the thousand-strong swarm was right on their heels — there was no stopping now, whatever was waiting ahead.
A grin Fabian could barely contain broke across his face. The others couldn’t believe it either. People say that when you’re pushed into a corner, you find a strength you never knew you had.
He’d told them all to run just moments before, terrified of waking the real horrors of the Dark Nether Cave and not even sure they could handle the Shadow-maws. But with no way out, they’d fought for their lives—and somehow, they’d actually won.
[SFX: CREATURE SHRIEKS]
A few of the winged creatures still circled high above, restless. They were smart enough to figure out the warriors below weren't an easy meal, but this was the Dark Nether Cave; nothing in here was easy to kill. As far as the local food chain went, these humans were still the path of least resistance. So the Shadow-maws stayed, their eerie calls echoing through the cavern in a clear summons for reinforcements.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Fabian's face darkened at the sound. "They're calling for backup," he said, his voice grim. He stared up at the circling shapes for a moment, then made his call. "Kill them. We can't let them keep calling."
Several War Lords didn't need to be told twice. They shot upward like arrows loosed from a bow. But the Shadow-maws were fast — far more agile in the air than any human warrior. They peeled away the moment anyone came close, circling back the instant the War Lords landed.
Fabian watched, his jaw tight. He'd have preferred they came at him straight. At least then he could fight back. He couldn't catch them, couldn't chase them off, and every second they circled was another second their calls carried deeper into the cave.
Zane kept to the middle of the pack, his expression grim. This place was every bit as dangerous as Dorian had described. Even with his insane luck, getting the inheritance wasn't going to be a walk in the park.
[SFX: MASSIVE WING BEATS]
More and more of the creatures swarmed the sky above, their screeching so loud it felt like it was drilling into your skull. Soon, there were so many of them that they blotted out the faint, ghostly glow of the Nether Death Light.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PERCUSSION]
"W-what do we do?" one of the War Lords stammered, his voice trembling.
"Scatter!" Fabian yelled, his own voice tight with fear. "Everyone run in different directions!"
"That won't work," Zane cut in sharply. "There are too many of them. We'll never outrun them all." His eyes flashed with a hard, determined glint as he scanned the flock. "Where's the most dangerous thing in this part of the cave?"
Fabian stared at him, confused. "What are you talking about?"
"We lead them to it," Zane said, his jaw set.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
It was a crazy gamble, but it was their only one. Fighting this swarm was suicide. Staying put was suicide. Their only shot at survival was to pit one monster against another—to jump from the frying pan into the fire and hope they didn't get burned.
Fabian's mind raced. He had no time to argue; Zane’s plan was insane, but it was all they had. Then it hit him—the map. The explorers from the Tower of Sin all contributed to a shared map of the cave. While people were cagey about marking lucky finds, they were damn good about marking threats. It gave everyone a better chance to survive. The problem was that the map was unreliable; monsters moved. But one entry had been consistent for years: a forbidden zone to the west.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
Rumor was, it was home to a Goliath Bull-Toad.
The Bull-Toad was a legend. It had been there for ages, and countless warriors who'd strayed too close had been snatched up by its massive tongue, never to be seen again. After a while, no one who entered the Dark Nether Cave dared go anywhere near the place. Not even Soul Sea masters would test it, let alone a War Sage or a group of War Lords.
On any other day, Fabian wouldn’t have gone near it if you held a blade to his throat. A fresh wave of dread washed over the group. A true monster. If its cry alone was terrifying, what was the beast itself like? Were they really running toward it just to escape the birds? It felt like trading one death sentence for another.
But good idea or not, they had no other choice. Everyone turned and ran.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: FRANTIC CHASE]
The swarm followed. The Shadow-maws were faster than most of them on open ground, diving low every few seconds to pick at the edges of the group.
Then they heard it.
[SFX: DEEP BULL-TOAD CROAK]
[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]
The sound hit like a physical force. Trees snapped and bent flat. The wave rolled through them before anyone had time to react, and several War Lords — even a few peak ones — staggered, blood running from their ears.
"Cover your ears!" Fabian shouted. "Use your Vitality — don't let it damage you!"
"Why didn't you warn us sooner?!" someone snapped back.
Everyone flooded their ears with Vitality. When the second wave came — another bone-deep, thunderous croak — they stayed upright. But they could still feel it in their teeth, their chests, the backs of their eyes.
The sound washed over them in deep, vibrating waves, and the birds in the sky actually faltered, their flight hesitating for just a second before they pressed on. The swarm was nearly on top of them now, angling ahead, moving to cut off their path forward.
[SFX: BIRD WINGS FLAPPING]
The flock was right on top of them now, close enough to dive at any moment and cut off their escape for good.
