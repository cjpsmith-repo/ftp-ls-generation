Ep 959 - The Mountain
Word count: 1106
The ice Tia formed was as hard as iron, but Zane’s power erupted from within, his muscles trembling with the strain. Cracks spiderwebbed across the frozen surface, turning the once-flawless coffin a milky white.
[SFX: CRACK]
Then, with a final, sharp crack, it exploded into a thousand pieces.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGES]
Tia was fuming. The moment the ice shattered, she unleashed another surge of Vitality, and a new coffin formed around him instantly. But just as fast, Zane flexed his power, and it too burst apart.
[AMBIENT: RUMBLING ICE]
[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]
This cycle repeated—ice forming and shattering around them, the glittering shards piling up beneath their feet. The pile grew into a small mountain, lifting them higher and higher.
The dome of the ice palace had been rebuilt with an opening at its peak ever since Tia’s last tribulation nearly brought the whole thing down.
[SFX: BREAKING THROUGH ROOF]
Now, as their ice mountain continued to grow, it pushed them straight through that opening and out into the open sky.
Tia’s palace was situated at the highest point of Cloud Hall, and the guards patrolling below had incredible vision. Anyone who happened to look up would see everything unfolding at the top.
[AMBIENT: OPEN SKY WIND]
Zane still held Tia tight in his arms as the ice mountain carried them through the roof.
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
“What the hell? Is that an ice mountain growing out of the palace?”
“Is the Leader cultivating a new technique? A breakthrough, maybe?”
“You idiot! Can’t you see there are two people on it?”
Even from so far below, the whispers reached Tia. A hot blush crept up her neck. This was completely humiliating.
“Let go of me!” she hissed, her voice a furious whisper.
“I’m not letting go,” Zane said, his voice firm.
“Please!” Tia had never begged for anything in her life, but she was desperate.
“It’s no use, even if you beg.” A small, infuriating smile played on Zane’s lips.
More and more eyes found them from windows and corners below. Tia could feel every single one.
She had held herself together through everything — the argument, the icicles, the ice coffin, the crowd. But something about being seen like this, held against her will in front of all of Cloud Hall, broke through the last of her composure.
[MUSIC: BREAKING POINT]
“You’re bullying me,” she said. Her voice cracked on the last word.
“Yes,” Zane said simply. “I am.”
Tia buried her face and cried. Not the elegant, restrained kind — the helpless kind, the kind she had no control over. She couldn’t beat him, couldn’t outmaneuver him, couldn’t even get free. There was nothing left to do but cry.
Zane’s hand moved through her hair. He turned her face toward his.
[MUSIC: TENDER STRINGS]
[SFX: GENTLE KISS]
He kissed her. At first, she made a small sound of protest, her lips still trembling. Then, slowly, she stopped fighting. Her eyes closed. After a moment, she kissed him back.
The dignified Hall Master of Cloud Hall, kissing a man on top of her own Ice Palace in full view of her sect. She would never have believed it possible. But she had stopped thinking entirely.
[AMBIENT: DISTANT COTTAGE DOORWAY]
At the far edge of Cloud Hall, at the door of a small thatched cottage, Elder Imogen stood watching. A faint smile crossed her face. After a long moment, she turned and went back inside, muttering to herself: “Young people… perfectly normal.”
[MUSIC: QUIET RESOLUTION]
When Tia’s head had cooled enough to think again, she reached one hand behind Zane’s back and pressed her palm toward the ice mountain below. Every shard dissolved at once, turning back to Vitality and dispersing into the air.
Without the ice to support them, they dropped fast.
[SFX: HEAVY LANDING — THUD]
Zane hit the ground hard. Tia used him as a cushion, the momentum bouncing her lightly back to her feet. She stood barefoot on the cold stone, smoothed her hair with both hands, and looked down at him with a smile that held just a trace of satisfaction.
[SFX: WIND RUSH]
[MUSIC: PLAYFUL STRINGS]
Zane was back on his feet in an instant. The look in his eyes made Tia take a step back, her hands coming up instinctively. “No,” she said.
“Why not?”
“You promised.” Her chin lifted. “After the ceremony. Not before.”
She was traditional in this, even if she was traditional in nothing else. Zane looked at her for a long moment. Then he leaned in close and said quietly, “Then we start preparing for the ceremony tomorrow.”
Tia said nothing. But she didn’t say no.
[MUSIC: WARM RESOLUTION]
Within three days, word had spread across the entire Heartlands: Cloud Hall was holding a ceremony. The World Merchants’ Guild carried the news to every corner of the region, and the response was immediate. The Cora and Tia dispute, which had been the talk of the Heartlands for weeks, went quiet overnight. Tia had made her choice. She and Cora would never be without friction — that much was certain — but for now, the rivalry had settled into an uneasy peace.
Preparations took more than a month.
[AMBIENT: FESTIVE CROWD MURMUR]
[MUSIC: CELEBRATORY STRINGS]
When the day arrived, Cloud Hall was unrecognizable. The pile of rare gifts sent for the occasion had already grown into a hill twenty or thirty feet high.
The rest of Cloud Hall had been polished to a gleam, looking brighter and newer than ever.
The three divine textures Zane had created for the sect’s defense left Cloud Hall’s own rune expert, Cedric, feeling completely outclassed. He knew that even if he poured every ounce of his energy into the task, he could never forge protective arrays as powerful as Zane’s.
Still, studying those divine textures had deepened his own understanding of runes immensely, making him second only to Zane in all of The Heartlands. Cedric had even lent his own skills to the celebration, creating several decorative runes.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
They weren’t particularly powerful, but when supplied with Aether Crystals, they glowed with shifting colors. Some of the light projected onto the enchanted barrier known as the Light of Water, creating dazzling images of flying dragons and phoenixes that danced across its surface.
[AMBIENT: LARGE GATHERING HALL]
[MUSIC: GRAND CEREMONIAL DRUMS]
Inside Cloud Hall’s main training hall, a massive crowd of warriors from across the Heartlands had gathered. These were all heavy hitters—the leaders of the most powerful clans and sects.
Naturally, the heads of the Fourth-Tier Sects were there, including those from Mysterious Luna Hall, Bloodwood Cliff, and the Black Mountain Order.
They all remembered when Zane was just a junior in the Soul Realm. Now, as they came forward to offer their congratulations, they couldn’t help but feel a sense of awe. Before their very eyes, his power had skyrocketed to a point where no one in The Heartlands could challenge him.
The man they had once looked down on was now someone they could only look up to.
