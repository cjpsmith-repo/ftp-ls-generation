Ep 45 - Time to Fight
Word count: 1255
[AMBIENT: WILDERNESS SOUNDS]
Zane's assessment was spot-on.
For The Gauntlet, the third layer was where most people stayed. Only a handful of the braver—or more foolish—newbies dared to venture into the fourth or fifth.
The reason was simple: they just weren't strong enough. The creatures in the higher levels were a whole different breed of tough, and most rookies would get torn apart before they could snag a single fragment.
It was a suicide run, plain and simple.
With his mind made up, Zane spent the next six hours prowling the third layer, racking up a decent haul of fragments.
[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS THEME]
[SFX: CROW SCREECHING]
His first opponent was a Hell-Crow that dive-bombed him from the sky, its aerial attacks making it nearly impossible to dodge. Every time Zane lunged, the creature would glide just out of reach. It was a frustrating game of cat and mouse that earned a wry shrug from Zane.
As he wracked his brain for a strategy, an idea sparked.
Zane abruptly dropped to the ground, going completely still. He was betting on the Hell-Crow's nature—a scavenger wouldn't be able to resist a free meal.
Sure enough, the crow saw Zane's motionless form and fell for it, swooping down for the kill.
[SFX: IMPACT PUNCH]
The instant it was within arm's reach, Zane's fist shot out like a viper. The impact sent the Hell-Crow tumbling to the ground, dead from the single, perfectly timed blow.
[SFX: ICY WINDS]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
Later, he stumbled upon a massive Glacial Mammoth.
The beast's sheer size was intimidating enough, but its raw power was the real threat.
[SFX: ANIMAL ROARS]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
For most, it would have spelled disaster, but Zane felt no fear. Trusting his instincts, he dispatched it with deadly efficiency. After that, he took on a pack of three black wolves, a Storm Dragon, and even a colossal gorilla.
Overall, the creatures on this level were a significant step up in size and strength from the second.
It took Zane a bit more time to wear them down, but while the beasts were powerful, they weren't smart. A little creative thinking was all it took to turn their strength against them.
After six hours, he'd bagged a total of eight purple fragments. Just one more, and he'd clear The Gauntlet.
[SFX: PORCUPINE SNARL]
As if on cue, a Bloodthorn Porcupine materialized before him. Its eyes burned with primal rage, and saliva dripped from the corners of its mouth, trailing down its ugly, jutting tusks. With a guttural snarl, it charged.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSE]
But Zane's attention was instantly ripped away from the beast. Someone else was here. His mind raced as he sized up the newcomer, his eyes locking onto the dagger in the man's hand. Treasure Grade, Zane guessed.
After all the fights he'd won, he was confident in his abilities, but he wasn't an idiot.
A smirk played on Joe's lips. "Sorry, but I don't think you've got a choice in the matter. You know, I'm not surprised those three idiots washed out, but I almost feel bad for them. So, to make things right, I figure you should pay the price for getting members of House Fane booted from the trial. Sound fair to you?"
Joe reached into his coat and let a fistful of purple shards pour through his fingers onto the ground. Dozens of them, chiming against the rocks — enough to pass the trial ten times over. "Win, and they're all yours," he said.
Zane went still, his body tensing, already calculating an escape route. Just as he'd thought. Joe was spoiling for a fight.
"Not interested," Zane said. He only needed one more shard. There was nothing in this for him. He turned to leave.
[SFX: ENERGY MATERIALIZATION]
But before he could make a move, a glowing blue thread materialized in the air before him. It quickly multiplied, weaving itself into a small square that expanded at an explosive speed, forming The Grid.
[SFX: ENERGY NET EXPANSION]
In seconds, a massive energy net stretched for hundreds of feet, sealing both him and Joe inside.
Joe let out a wild laugh. "Don't you think The Gauntlet is a total bore? Seriously, fighting these mindless beasts? It's an insult to our talent. Time to spice things up. A real fight might be the only thing that puts me in a good mood. And if you're lucky, I might even leave you in one piece!"
Zane's eyes narrowed into slits, his gaze fixed on Joe a short distance away. It was painfully obvious there was no getting out of this. He let out a quiet sigh, a grim acceptance settling over him.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN EXTERIOR]
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
Meanwhile, the masters waited outside Bloody Mountain, each of them focused on Joe through their Engraved Slabs. Joe had shown the most potential in The Gauntlet, but most of them had already lost interest. While they used the trial to scout apprentices, they had their reasons for shifting their focus.
For one, while Joe was arguably the strongest candidate, he was all but guaranteed to join Dragon's Tooth Peak, where his relative, Master Fane, presided. None of the other masters were going to waste their breath trying to recruit a kid who'd already made up his mind.
Zane was the other reason. The masters had been watching the candidates on the second and third layers, and Zane's skills had definitely caught their attention. They'd kept a close eye on him ever since he'd saved Ethan Grant, risking his own neck against a group of disciples from The Great Houses.
Since the two seemed to be friends, the masters assumed they'd stick together. To their surprise, Zane had vanished. He didn't seek out Ethan in the next layer, leading them to believe he was a lone wolf—the type of guy who wasn't a fan of people and preferred to come and go as he pleased.
[SFX: MAGICAL SLAB ACTIVATION]
After a long stretch of not being able to find Zane on their Engraved Slabs, one of the masters switched his view back to Joe, just in time to see the fight begin.
Once he alerted the others, a buzz of excitement shot through the group. They all immediately shifted their slabs to Joe's location. Given the potential of both young men, they knew they were in for one hell of a fight.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]
Zane knew if he'd been a split-second slower, the filament would've taken his head clean off. Joe gave him no time to recover. With a flick of his wrist, he spun the dagger, and a chaotic web of Cobalt Fibers exploded from the blade. He then surged toward Zane, aiming to engulf him in the shimmering, deadly threads.
[SFX: RAZOR FIBERS SLICING AIR]
One wrong move and the razor-sharp fibers would tear him to ribbons.
Right now, all Zane could do was retreat. He couldn't get near Joe with that storm of Cobalt Fibers swirling around him; charging in was suicide. He needed a new plan, and fast.
This wasn't how things were supposed to go. The trial had been going perfectly. He had more than enough fragments to pass. And now, this. He was so close to the finish line, only to get blindsided. What the hell was he supposed to do?
A sardonic grin crept across Zane's lips as he assessed his predicament. The lethal mesh of fibers stretched endlessly in every direction, imprisoning them within its shimmering boundaries and leaving scarcely any space to evade. Joe's onslaught continued without mercy, driving Zane backward with each calculated attack.
[SFX: ENERGY WALL CRACKLING]
[MUSIC: DESPERATE SITUATION]
With a final desperate step, Zane's spine pressed against the crackling energy wall of The Grid. Trapped. Cornered like prey. The cold realization washed over him—he had reached the end of his retreat.
