Ep 149 - An Interesting Boy

[AMBIENT: EERIE FOG]
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
 Walsh stood frozen in the smog for a beat, then a sharp, mocking laugh tore from his throat.
"Hahahaha! Wow. It's been a long time since anyone had the nerve to talk to me like that. A kid—a candidate—thinks he can take that tone with me? That's hilarious. Now, you're going to tell me your name. Or I'm going to start carving. A thousand little pieces. Bet you'll be singing by then, won't you?"
[SFX: SURGING FOG]
 The black fog surged, swallowing Zane whole. Instantly, a lethal killing intent flared to life behind him, aimed squarely at his heart. But Zane's soul was on another level—stronger than even some warriors from the Soul Realm. A mere nature creature like Walsh couldn't hope to hide from him.
He pinpointed Walsh's true position with ease. Playing along, Zane kept his eyes locked forward, his body tensed as if expecting an attack from the front. Let Walsh think he'd fallen for the oldest trick in the book. He'd be ready the moment the real attack came from behind.
"Die!"
The deadly aura at his back intensified. Zane felt Walsh right on top of him and whipped the shattered dagger backward, leading with the back of his hand.
[SFX: BLADE IMPACT]
[MUSIC: SURPRISE REVELATION]
 The blade met flesh. Zane felt the impact, assuming he’d just skewered Walsh. But in that same instant, another figure flickered into existence right in front of him. It was Walsh!
[SFX: EVIL LAUGHTER]
 "Hahahaha!" Grinning, Walsh lifted a Serpentine Dagger to slash at Zane's chest before melting back into the fog.
It all clicked into place. They knew he was just a High-level candidate—hardly worth a full team. There was only one person here: Walsh. Which meant the two figures that appeared just now…
"Don't get ahead of yourself," Zane sneered. "You really think you'll get the chance to cut me a thousand times?"
[AMBIENT: ECHOING FOG]
 "Why not?" Walsh's voice echoed from the fog. "I've already tagged you twice. Only nine hundred and ninety-eight to go. I've got all the time in the world to play."
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
 As Walsh finished speaking, the black fog churned and rolled in again, signaling a third attack.
This time, Zane didn't move. He just stood there, waiting. Walsh’s illusory attacks might have a high success rate, but this time, he was facing Zane.
Zane’s intuition was a razor's edge. He felt the killing intent bloom from three places at once: his left, his back, and directly in front of him.
"Just as I figured," Zane muttered. "Coming from the front this time."
[SFX: BLADE STRIKE]
 He shot forward, his broken blade striking the figure before him for the third time. 
[SFX: ENERGY RIPPLE]
As it made contact, Zane made a circular motion with his finger, and a surge of dark purple Void Energy rippled through the air.
The Void Energy was faint, its color nearly indistinguishable from Walsh's black fog. An untrained eye would have missed it completely.
The Corrupt Vitality billowed out, breaking free like ripples spreading across water. The moment the energy touched the black fog, Zane slapped his forehead in theatrical frustration.
He yelled, "Dammit, I guessed wrong! He's not in front—he must be behind me!"
Energy flared from the Phoenix Crystal on his right arm, and Zane's speed exploded. 
[SFX: RAPID BLADE SLASHES]
 In the blink of an eye, he was on the figure *behind* him, his blade a silver blur. He landed over thirty slashes before the sound even registered. Only when he pulled back did Walsh’s screams finally catch up.
[SFX: AGONIZED SCREAM]
Zane deliberately avoided a fatal blow. Instead, with surgical precision, he launched into another flurry of strikes, methodically severing Walsh's limbs one by one. Though his blade could have reduced the man to ribbons in seconds, he exercised remarkable restraint, leaving his opponent alive but utterly helpless.
[SFX: FOG DISSIPATING]
[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]
 With a casual flick of his wrist, Zane banished the oppressive black fog that had engulfed them. He approached the mutilated figure on the ground, his footsteps echoing in the sudden silence. Looking down at Walsh's trembling form, a cold smile played across his lips.
"Still curious about my name?" he asked, his voice as brittle as winter ice. The contempt in his eyes spoke volumes about what he thought of his defeated opponent.
"How... how did you know?" Walsh shrieked, his face contorted with both physical agony and the deeper torment of humiliation. Blood pooled beneath him as he struggled to comprehend his failure.
"You really want to know?" Zane asked with a sardonic smirk, savoring the moment of complete dominance. He twirled his blade idly between his fingers, the metal catching the light as it spun.
Walsh's face, now a grotesque mask of suffering, twisted further as desperation overtook him. "Tell me!" he begged through gritted teeth, his pride abandoned in the face of his devastating defeat.
[SFX: BLADE SLASHING]
 "You can ask them in the Underworld." The shattered dagger in his hand flashed.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]
 "Who I am is none of your business," Zane said, turning to face the others. "And don't try to impress me with your titles. It won't work. I want nothing to do with the Great Houses. Let's all just go our separate ways."
The long-haired woman let out a short, sharp laugh. "Oh, you sweet, naive boy."
"Maybe I am," Zane said, holding the flying blade level across his chest. "But anyone who crosses me ends up just like him." He nudged Walsh's writhing form with the toe of his boot.
He showed no fear of the Seven Great Houses, and they stared at him, stunned. A dangerous energy began to radiate from several of them. If they all decided to attack at once, he knew he'd have to run for his life. But he had read the Principles of Forging; he wasn't a fool. 
In a world like this, especially within the Court, the impossible was always on the table. He wasn't about to back down now.
[SFX: ERUPTION OF LAUGHTER]
 Silence hung in the air for a heartbeat—then shattered. A single chuckle escaped one representative's lips, followed by another's snicker. Within moments, thunderous laughter erupted from the gathered members of the Seven Great Houses. They convulsed with mirth, tears streaming down some faces, as if Zane's deadly threat ranked among history's most hilarious jests. Their unified derision echoed through the space, transforming his moment of defiance into something far more ominous.
