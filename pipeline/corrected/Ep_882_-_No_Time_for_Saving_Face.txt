Ep 882 - No Time for Saving Face

Word count: 1128

[AMBIENT: SACRED TEMPLE]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]

The second petal had barely stilled before every eye in the crowd fixed on the lotus, waiting.

If the lotus flower produced three petals, Zane would qualify as a core member of a top-tier sacred institution.

“I knew this kid was something special,” the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom chuckled to himself. 

But then, War Emperor York’s sour voice cut in. “Hmph. So what? No matter how talented the kid is, he’s still trapped in the Lower World, just like the rest of us.”

His words made the Emperor frown, but what the white-haired priest said next sent a shockwave through the powerful warriors of the Soul Sea Realm, making their hearts pound in their chests.

“If the boy can make the lotus produce enough petals, it will grab the attention of the humans in the upper world,” the priest said with quiet confidence. “It’s very likely they’d be willing to pay a steep price to help us restore the ascending passage in this realm.”

[SFX: SHOCKED GASPS]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

“What? Restore the ascending passage?!”

“Is that even possible? Do the people in the upper world really have that kind of power?”

“If the passage could be restored… we could finally ascend!”

[AMBIENT: TENSE GATHERING]

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]

The warriors trembled with barely contained excitement. They had spent their entire lives reaching the Soul Sea Realm, only to become trapped in the Lower World—unable to advance, unable to ascend. Repairing the passage lay far beyond any Soul Sea master’s abilities, and they had abandoned hope long ago.

But if someone restored it, they could finally ascend, pursue higher martial arts, and achieve true power. Yes, they would face deadlier challenges, but every warrior present would rather burn brightly than fade slowly. Higher cultivation also meant longer life.

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL REVELATION]

Among them were the three masters from the Crystal Martial House. They stared at Zane with a complex mix of emotions, their dawning excitement impossible to hide. They had hoped for an outstanding performance from him, but they’d never imagined he might hold the key to changing their very destiny.

Even War Emperor York was torn. The grudge he held against Zane was nothing compared to the chance to ascend.

“M-Master Priest,” one of the warriors asked, his voice shaking, “how many petals would it take? Enough for them to help us repair the ascending passage?”

The White-Haired Priest was quiet for a moment. “Seven, at minimum. Seven petals might be enough to draw their attention. Nine would guarantee it — nine petals means the first layer of the lotus has fully bloomed. That is the legendary Nine-Leaf Lotus.” 

He paused. “In all the countless Holy Lands across a single great realm, not one may ever produce a Nine-Leaf warrior. Even warriors with seven leaves number only a few hundred per great realm. The weaker realms have fewer still.”

The silence that followed was heavy.

A few hundred, across an entire great realm; the probability was almost too small to hold onto.

“Then even seven —” someone began.

“Is not guaranteed,” the White-Haired Priest said. “But it is possible. And it is enough that they might act.” He let that settle. “When I crossed my own tribulation at Soul Sea Realm, I bloomed three petals. At my second tribulation — five.”

That drew stares—five petals from the White-Haired Priest himself. And still, the ascending passage remained broken.

They all knew the outcome was set in stone; it couldn’t be changed by sheer willpower. 

And that’s what made the waiting so agonizing.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[SFX: MAGICAL SPINNING]

As everyone watched with bated breath, the lotus continued to spin. But no more petals appeared.

“One more,” one of the masters cried out, tears streaming down his face. He looked utterly broken, all the dignified air of a powerful warrior gone, replaced by the desperation of a beggar. It wasn’t clear who he was pleading with—Zane, the heavens, or the lotus itself. “Just one more petal! Please!”

No one mocked him. If they thought begging would work, they’d have thrown their pride to the wind and joined him. This was no time for saving face.

Then Fiona’s voice rang out from the side. “The third petal! Open! Open!”

Her brother Callan looked at her. “Fiona, that’s not how it —”

The third petal trembled. And opened.

[SFX: GENTLE MAGICAL CHIME]

The Holy Maiden watched it fall, something shifting in her expression. She thought, without meaning to, of the Wheel of Fortune — of the bad luck this man had flung at her so casually, as though it cost him nothing. “The third petal,” she said, almost to herself.

“Not bad,” the White-Haired Priest said, nodding slowly. “Three leaves. The boy should be proud. And since the third has opened —” he glanced at the lotus — “the fourth will follow. He has already surpassed me.”

As if in answer, the fourth petal slowly unfurled.

[SFX: SOFT WHOOSH]

Then the fifth.

[MUSIC: SWELLING STRINGS]

The breathing of every Soul Sea master in the crowd quickened at once. Five petals. One more after this, and they would reach the threshold. Just one more petal.

“One petal,” the Emperor murmured. “Just one more.”

“Who knew this boy would become our only hope?” War King Wind said quietly, shaking his head. “I hope he succeeds.”

Across the gathering, warriors began to pray under their breath, voices barely audible, as though speaking too loudly might disturb whatever force governed the flower’s turning.

Up on the platform, Zane blinked, staring at the slowly spinning lotus in the sky. He had no idea he was causing such a commotion below. He didn’t realize that every single person there was pinning their lifelong hopes on him.

[MUSIC: CALM MEDITATION]

After waiting a moment longer, he simply sat down cross-legged and closed his eyes. The Cobalt Dragon had told him the number of petals was determined by fate, not by will. There was no point in worrying about it.

Zane understood this, but the powerful masters didn’t. They knew nothing about how the lotus worked; they only believed that intense focus could influence destiny. So, when they saw Zane close his eyes and start meditating at this critical moment, they were stunned into silence.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

War Emperor York was the first to snap. 

He turned on the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom, his voice a frantic, strangled hiss. “Go talk to him! Tell him to take this seriously! He can meditate any other time, but right now—at this crucial moment—he needs to be focused on that flower!”

Every soul in the crowd was pinning their fate on him — and not all of them would take disappointment quietly.
