Ep 716 - Life and Death

[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDICAL ROOM]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
With Patrick and Lewis busy patching themselves up, Zane figured they didn't need him hovering. He left them to it and walked over to Saint Morphen. "How is Tia?" he asked, his voice low.
Saint Morphen gave him a reassuring nod. "She's fine."
"Don't lie to me," Zane shot back, his eyes narrowing. "If she was fine, Emanuel never would have had the guts to pull a stunt like this." He knew for a fact that if Tia were at her full strength, no one, especially not Emanuel, would dare to challenge her authority. The thought that something had gone terribly wrong tightened a knot in his stomach.
Seeing the raw fear on Zane's face, Saint Morphen realized his mistake. "No, you don't get it," he explained, his voice urgent. "She's in the middle of her Life-and-Death Tribulation! That's why they saw an opening and made their move."
Zane was floored. "The Life-and-Death Tribulation? But… where's Imogen?" He knew that even with Tia attempting a breakthrough, Emanuel never would have tried anything if the leader of Cloud Hall had been there to keep the peace.
"She's away, seeking her own path," Saint Morphen replied calmly. "Emanuel actually warned Tia against this. He said that with both her and the Hall Leader gone, Cloud Hall would be vulnerable."
But Tia had been determined.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL BUILD]
The Corrupted Divine Kingdom was growing stronger by the day, and she believed that if she could just break through to the Life-and-Death Realm, she might finally have a real shot at fighting Maddox. She just never counted on Emanuel betraying her. If Zane hadn't shown up, his men would have already stormed the ice palace and threatened everything.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
"I have to see her," Zane said, his jaw set. He'd never faced a Life-and-Death Tribulation himself, but he knew the legends. It was a brutal ordeal with a razor-thin margin of survival. Warriors who weren't ready for it were all but guaranteed to die.
"But Zane," Saint Morphen said, his expression serious, "Tia gave specific orders. No one is to enter the ice palace while she's inside."
Zane just held his gaze for a long moment before slowly shaking his head. Without another word, he turned and walked straight for the palace. Saint Morphen started to reach out to stop him, but let his hand fall. He watched Zane's steady stride and thought, He has a point. Without Zane, Emanuel's rebellion would have succeeded. Maybe he’d earned the right to check on her.
[AMBIENT: EERIE ICE PALACE]
[SFX: CREAKING DOORS]
A dense, icy mist clung to every surface inside the palace. He pushed open the doors to the inner chambers, Lavender appearing right behind him, and found he could barely see more than a few feet ahead, relying on his magical sight to cut through the fog.
Tia sat cross-legged at the center of the chamber, encased from the neck down in a solid column of ice. Her eyes were shut, her expression completely still. Two years — and she looked exactly the same. Not a day older. Zane circled the column slowly, something quiet and difficult moving through him.
"She's already at the final stage," Lavender said. "Once she breaks through, the tribulation descends."
"Will she be able to handle it?" Zane asked.
Lavender was quiet for a moment. "The technique she cultivates is extraordinary — far stronger than most at the same level. But techniques like that draw heavier tribulations. It's why so few warriors choose them." She glanced at him. "Her tribulation will not be ordinary."
Zane's eyes stayed on Tia. "And if she can't survive it?"
The Cobalt Dragon's voice rumbled quietly from within him. "There is a way. But—"
"But we're not there yet," Lavender said simply. "If it comes to that, I'll tell you. For now, we wait."
Zane said nothing. He settled in beside the column and watched.
More than twenty hours passed. The mist never thinned. Tia never moved. Then, slowly, hairline cracks began to spread across the ice — branching and multiplying until the entire column fractured at once, dissolving into a glittering fall of shards that vanished before they reached the ground. Tia sat alone in the open air of the chamber, still and silent.
[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]
[AMBIENT: VIOLENT STORM]
Just then, a deafening crack of thunder shook the palace, and the sky outside roiled with black clouds.
"So the Lesser Celestial Tribulation is starting?" Zane asked, startled by the sudden violence of the storm.
Lavender nodded grimly. "Yes. This is one of the signs."
[AMBIENT: DISTANT THUNDER]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
"If this is just the Lesser Tribulation," Zane murmured, intimidated, "what's the big one like?"
A faint smile touched Lavender’s lips. "A powerful warrior will face nine of these, each one worse than the last. Only after that can they face the Greater Celestial Tribulations in the Soul Sea Realm." She looked out at the storm, her voice dropping to a whisper. "And their power… is unimaginable."
The Greater Tribulations were a distant problem, but a lesser one felt terrifyingly close. He knew that as soon as he perfected his skills and reached the peak of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, he’d have to face one himself.
[SFX: MASSIVE THUNDER CRACK]
[SFX: CRASHING DEBRIS]
[SFX: SHATTERING ICE]
A bolt of lightning shaped like a writhing dragon slammed into the roof. The ice palace was built like a fortress, its roof reinforced with thick spikes of ice, but the blast was so powerful it tore a gaping hole right through it. A shower of stone, ice, and splintered beams rained down into the chamber.
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL ICE AURA]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHOIR]
Miraculously, none of the debris touched Tia. A frigid aura pulsed from her, her eyes still shut tight. She was completely focused on the ordeal to come, so lost in her preparation that she didn't even seem to notice Zane was there.
Staring up through the jagged hole, Zane watched the lightning dragons swirl in the dark sky.
He took a steadying breath. "The Light of Water is supposed to protect the entire Hall," he said, half to himself. "How are they getting through?"
