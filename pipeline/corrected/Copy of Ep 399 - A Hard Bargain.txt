Ep 399 - A Hard Bargain

Word count: 1277

[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION SPACE]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Zane had leveled up fast, but he knew it still wasn't enough.

He was still way behind the members of the Great Houses in the Eastern Territories. Take his friend Leo, for example. Zane had first met him on a mission for the Skyreach Sect when Leo was only at level one of the Adept Realm. But when Zane saw him again a few months later, Leo had shot up to level five. Zane was blown away by how much he’d improved in such a short time.

Of course, it was common knowledge that some martial artists used pills to fast-track their progress. The problem was that it usually left them with an unstable foundation. It was like trying to rush a cake—it might look perfect on the outside, but the inside would still be raw. Some fighters were willing to pay that price just to get ahead faster, but a weak foundation would almost always come back to bite them when they tried to advance further.

But for those fighters, it was all about pride and reputation. Even with an unstable foundation, once a martial artist hit the Soul Realm, they were instantly a respected figure in the Eastern Territories. For a lot of them, that was enough. Without a solid base, advancing from the Soul Realm to the Inner-Core Realm and beyond was like trying to thread a needle in the dark, so most of them just gave up on ever reaching those higher levels.

Zane wasn't like them, though. His foundation was rock solid. He’d even hold himself back from leveling up sometimes, waiting for his body to make the jump on its own. It was like letting a dam fill to the brim until the pressure itself caused it to burst.

[AMBIENT: TRANQUIL WATER]

[SFX: GENTLE RIPPLES]

There in the pond, Zane finally stabilized his breakthrough. But instead of kicking back, he stayed in a lotus position, continuing his breathing exercises. He had a feeling that staying in this environment a little longer could only help.

[SFX: ETHEREAL ECHO]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DRONE]

"I should warn you," a voice echoed in his mind. "The Cultivation Methods of a Dragon aren't for you. Your body is still too weak. Even if I taught you some of our techniques, you wouldn't be able to use them with your current strength."

Zane hid a smirk. He knew the Cobalt Dragon had sensed how desperate he was for a new method, but he had to play it cool.

[MUSIC: PLAYFUL TENSION]

"Hey, I never asked you for anything," Zane shot back mentally. "And I definitely never agreed to give you Tia's share. But if you're offering… who am I to say no? I'll gladly take it."

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ORIENTAL THEME]

"You're a cunning kid, I'll give you that. But a smart one," the Cobalt Dragon said, his voice brimming with pride. "Fine. I'll teach you a set of rune-making methods. It's one of the greatest skills we Dragons perfected in our runic studies."

Truthfully, he’d been planning to teach Zane something useful for a while. Sharing even a fraction of his vast knowledge would be a massive boost for the kid. But he’d held off, knowing Zane’s low level would have made it impossible for him to grasp, let alone use, the Cobalt Dragon's power.

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL WORKSHOP]

But now, seeing Zane's increased strength and his stable foundation, he’d finally decided to teach him the art of writing runes. After all, runes were used for everything in this world and had countless applications. If Zane became a rune expert, he could handle plenty of tough situations without the Cobalt Dragon having to step in.

Runes came in all shapes and sizes, with endless uses. Magic array experts, for example, were a special kind of rune master in the Eastern Territories who built massive arrays out of hundreds, sometimes thousands, of individual runes.

Runes were also critical in weaponsmithing. If a smith could incorporate special runes while forging a weapon, they could dramatically increase their chance of success and even improve the weapon’s quality. The same went for making pills.

[SFX: METALLIC CHIME]

Hearing the Cobalt Dragon's offer, Zane couldn't help but grin. He'd made most of his money selling his Celestial Essence and had already thought about becoming a weaponsmith because of it, but he'd only learned the absolute basics of smelting. He hadn’t even touched the most crucial part of forging a weapon.

But if the Cobalt Dragon could teach him how to use runes, mastering weaponsmithing would be a hell of a lot easier.

Without a second thought, he answered, "I love it! That's exactly what I was thinking. If you can really teach me, you can have as much of that Drake blood as you want. I've already absorbed what I need, so the rest is useless to me now."

[SFX: DRAGON GROWL]

"You tricky bastard!" The Cobalt Dragon felt annoyed but had to admit Zane had played him perfectly.

[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL GARDEN]

[MUSIC: GENTLE MYSTICAL FLUTE]

Meanwhile, in the herbal garden, Tia was patiently counting and cataloging all the herbs. Each one required a specific touch; otherwise, it would wither the moment it was handled. For instance, the root herb called Nurturing Primrose would let out a sound like a crying baby if touched carelessly, then wither and die in a single breath, losing all its value. The Fiery Dragon Saliva, on the other hand, was packed with a fiery poison that would infect anyone who touched the plant.

So, Tia was handling all of them with extreme care.

[SFX: SOFT FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

After finishing her careful harvest, Tia secured the last of the precious herbs and turned her attention back to the pool where she'd left Zane. 
She'd set aside a few herbs to add to the blood pool. They would help purify his Vitality, settle his core, stabilize what he'd broken through to. A small thing — but his talent warranted it.
Her eyes narrowed when she found it empty—not a single drop of the valuable Drake blood remained. The corners of her mouth twitched in annoyance as she approached him, her expression shifting from surprise to calculated indignation.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

"Didn't we agree to share everything?" she spat, glancing at the chests on the ground. There was no way to get the Drake's blood back, so she figured she might as well mess with Zane a little. "Since you drank all the blood yourself, I don't see why I should share these with you now!"

"Whoa, hold on, that's not fair at all!" Zane shot back. "You took every single herb from that garden! The Lunar Anesthesia Herb alone is worth more than that entire pond of blood. You really think you can pull one over on me?"

[SFX: DRAMATIC STING]

"God, you drive a hard bargain! Fine, then maybe I should get half of that giant Sword of the Blood Fiend Emperor. How about that?" Tia retorted. She loved getting a rise out of him.

They went back and forth, bickering over the rest of the incredible treasures from the Sixth-Tier Sect. And while Tia was mostly just messing around, she liked the idea of getting even after he'd hogged all the Drake blood. Zane, for his part, wasn't giving an inch.

[MUSIC: CURIOUS WOODWINDS]

Getting nowhere, they finally agreed to just open the treasure chests first. Once they saw what was inside each box and knew what the items did, they could figure out a fair way to split the loot.

If, of course, they could keep from killing each other.
