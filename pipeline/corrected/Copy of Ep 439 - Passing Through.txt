Ep 439 - Passing Through

Word count: 1054

[AMBIENT: WIND HOWLING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE TRIAL]

Christopher had planned to stop and try to get a handle on the law etched into the obelisk before climbing higher, but the spatial repulsion intensified with every foot he climbed. Any higher, and he felt certain the pressure would simply tear him from the monument's surface.

Then he spotted Zane, still climbing steadily.

Christopher had always taken pride in his talent, considering himself a natural—someone born with gifts that set him apart from ordinary cultivators. That confidence had carried him through countless challenges. But watching Zane ascend while he remained frozen here, muscles trembling against the spatial force, shattered something inside him.

It made his blood boil. He couldn't let this happen.

The thought of losing was unbearable. Losing to Zane was unthinkable.

He was only eighty or ninety feet from the top, and they were both moving fast.

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

But the difference between them was stark. Christopher was desperate, his movements strained and anxious, while Zane was cool and collected. He’d already cracked the Spatial Law on the lower half of the obelisk, figuring out how to suppress the repulsive force. For him, the pull felt much gentler now, allowing him to climb faster, almost without resistance.

Christopher, on the other hand, was a mess. Before the climb, he’d been so sure his grasp of the Spatial Law was light-years ahead of Zane's. Now, so close to the top, he’d hit a wall. He couldn't stop and decipher the law now, not at this critical point.

He had nothing left in the tank, but he refused to stop. Losing wasn't an option.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

[SFX: HEARTBEAT]

He gritted his teeth and poured every ounce of will and soul-strength into fighting the spatial repulsion. Each inch upward demanded more than the last. 

But Christopher refused to yield. Pride, ambition, and raw stubbornness drove him forward when logic demanded retreat. The higher he climbed, the more ferocious the resistance became. What had started as gentle pressure now felt like iron bands constricting around his soul, squeezing tighter with each passing moment.

Every movement became an agonizing struggle against forces he couldn't fully comprehend, much less master. The gap between understanding the Spatial Law intellectually and wielding it practically yawned before him like an abyss.

Still, he climbed. Losing to Zane—to that upstart who'd only recently grasped the basics—would be unbearable.

[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION SPACE]

While their souls climbed the obelisk, their physical bodies sat motionless on the ground. Christopher’s body was beet-red and shaking uncontrollably. 

Zane’s, however, was the picture of peace—calm and still, his breathing slow and steady.

[SFX: IMPACT THUD]

[AMBIENT: HIGH ALTITUDE WIND]

Finally, their ascent came to a dead stop. Thirty feet from the summit, they both slammed into an invisible wall at the same time.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

Neither of them could move an inch. A spatial barrier blocked their path.

"Guess this is our stop for a bit," Zane said, blinking as he focused. He still needed some time to figure out the Spatial Law enough to get through the barrier, but since he already had the basics down, it wasn't a huge deal. Even at this height, the repulsion field barely bothered him.

Christopher, however, was not doing so well. He had been banking on raw power, trying to brute-force his way to the top without stopping. This sudden barrier caught him completely off guard, bringing his desperate rush to a grinding halt.

The seemingly thin spatial barrier might as well have been a canyon. With the strength he had left, there was no way he could force his way through.

And he definitely couldn't stop to meditate on the Spatial Law while fighting off this intense repulsion.

He watched Zane effortlessly adapt to the new obstacle. Just then, Zane glanced over at him, gave a slight, almost pitying shake of his head, and turned back to studying the barrier with a faint smile.

That smile sent Christopher over the edge.
But Christopher wasn't an idiot. Seething as he was, he could feel his soul straining against the repulsion — one more push and he'd be flung off entirely. Falling from the obelisk in front of everyone was worse than retreating. Slowly, jaw clenched, he climbed back down to comprehend properly. There would be other chances to beat this kid.

[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT REVELATION]

As Christopher seethed Suddenly, it all clicked for Zane. The fractured theories of the Spatial Law swirling in his mind suddenly snapped together, forming a perfect, coherent picture. The key. He finally got it. He looked at the spatial barrier again, this time with a confident grin.

A spatial barrier, he thought. I can get through this.

And more than that — if he could truly master this, he wouldn't need to destabilize space the way Saint Morphen did just to slip through it. He could move through space directly. Short distances, clean teleportation. The thought lit him up inside.

With a surge of confidence, he let go of the obelisk. Instead of falling, his form simply floated, drifting right through the barrier as if it were a heat shimmer.

This barrier couldn't be broken by physical force or raw soul power.

Zane’s goal was never to break the barrier, but to pass through it. And that’s exactly what he did. 

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]

His soul moved like a fish slipping through water, gliding past the obstacle with ease until he was standing at the very summit.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

Watching Zane reach the top, Elder Howard beamed. "Less than six hours," he breathed, his voice tight with disbelief. "He went from knowing nothing about the Spatial Law to fully grasping its first level! 'Elite' doesn't even begin to cover that kind of talent!"

He glanced around at the other stunned faces and let out a booming, joyous laugh.

Less than six hours? Elder Jovan thought, completely floored. The idea was terrifying. The obelisk was designed to accelerate learning, saving refiners from the slow work of discovering the law bit by bit, but six hours was unheard of. Julian had certainly never witnessed such a miracle. If that's true, this kid isn't just a genius. He's a genius among geniuses! What backwater Lower World did he crawl out of?

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

Before the two elders could even process it, all the Law Obelisks around them began to shake violently, sending a deep rumble through the ground and sparking chaos among the onlookers.

The monuments were trembling as if in an earthquake, but that was impossible.

Confused, Elder Howard looked up. 

[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]

He saw streams of Law Power arcing from the other obelisks, shooting through the air and converging on Zane.

"It can't be…" he whispered, his eyes wide with awe. "Is this… the Baptism by Laws?"
