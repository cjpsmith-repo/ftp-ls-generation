Ep 568 - The Tower of Sin
Word count: 1089
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane’s body was at its breaking point. He forced himself to back away from the three Ogre War Lords, his eyes never leaving them.
[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]
But something was wrong. The ogres weren’t charging. Instead of rage, their faces had twisted into expressions of shock and alarm.
“What is that doing here?” one of them bellowed, its cold eyes locked on Zane.
The three powerful War Lords glared, their muscles coiled, but not one of them made a move to attack.
Zane tensed, bracing for a fight to the death. But it never came.
He was confused. Why weren’t they attacking?
[SFX: MAGICAL TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS THEME]
Just then, the teleportation array blazed back to life. A figure emerged from the blinding light: Phoebe, the girl from the Demon Night. Her sharp eyes swept the area before she walked right past the three hulking War Lords as if they were nothing but statues, her focus entirely on Zane.
Zane’s confusion deepened. She was only a War General, yet she carried herself with a casual confidence that defied the presence of three War Lords.
[MUSIC: INTRIGUING MELODY]
She strolled up to him, a knowing smirk on her face. “Wondering why they haven’t torn you limb from limb yet?”
Zane could only nod.
[AMBIENT: TENSE STANDOFF]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Phoebe held up a hand, spreading her fingers dramatically. “This is the heart of the main continent. We call it the Cursed Land. Anyone who initiates an attack here is struck down by the curse. Instantly.” She turned and gave the fuming ogres a brilliant smile. “And that is why our friends here don't dare to make a move.”
“The Cursed Land?” Zane’s eyebrows shot up.
Phoebe laughed. “That’s right. No one can attack here. On top of that, if a warrior stronger than a War Lord sets foot in this place, they’re killed by the curse, too.”
“Stronger than a War Lord?” Zane repeated, his breath catching in his throat. The idea that a curse could kill someone that powerful made the Cursed Land far more terrifying than he could have possibly imagined.
Phoebe nodded. “The teleportation arrays are actually traps. They’re designed to lure War Sages here, where the curse can kill them for us. It’s just a shame these three are only War Lords. Otherwise, they’d already be dead.”
Zane was floored.
One of the War Lords bared his teeth. "You got lucky, human. But don't think you're safe. If you dare set foot in the Tower of Sin, I guarantee you'll die screaming." He held Zane's gaze for a long, cold moment, then turned away.
The other two followed. They stepped back onto the array, placed their Spirit Stones, and disappeared in a flash of white light.
Zane stared at the empty platform.
"The Tower of Sin," he said quietly. "Is it here? In the Cursed Land?"
Phoebe raised an eyebrow. "You know about the Tower of Sin?"
"It's why I came to this continent."
She studied him for a moment. "I can take you there. But first — tell me about your sword spirit."
Zane had expected this.
[MUSIC: SOFT MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
[SFX: SOFT RED GLOW]
He summoned Lily without another word. A flash of red light, and she stood before them.
Phoebe's composure cracked instantly. She dropped to one knee, head bowed.
"Greetings, Queen."
Zane blinked. "Queen?"
Phoebe rose slowly, her eyes moving between Zane and Lily with an expression caught between reverence and bewilderment. "She is the Queen of the Demon Night. Why does your sword spirit look exactly like her?"
Zane opened his mouth and closed it again. He'd found Lily by accident on Killing Sword Mountain. He hadn't chosen her form — she'd simply transformed into this after absorbing enough sword spirits. Even the Cobalt Dragon couldn't explain it.
"I genuinely don't know," he said. "After she transformed, she just... looked like this."
Phoebe's brow furrowed.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
The Demon Night had three monarchs, revered across every world their people had touched. Three statues stood in their Holy Land: the King of Eternal Night, the King of Myriad Demons, and the Queen of Slaughter. Phoebe had grown up before those statues.
And the Queen of Slaughter's statue had gone dim.
No one in the Demon Night knew why. It had troubled their people for as long as anyone could remember. And now here was a human boy, carrying a sword spirit that was the Queen of Slaughter's living image.
Phoebe had no answer. She would have to wait for Elder May. She let it go — for now.
"Alright," she said. "I've heard your answer. My name is Phoebe."
"Zane."
She turned and pointed ahead. "Then let's walk. I'll tell you about this continent on the way."
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE FLUTE]
This chaotic continent—the main continent, as she’d called it—was a truly bizarre place. As an outsider, Zane knew nothing of its history.
Apparently, ever since the Cursed Land was established, the various races had built countless teleportation arrays all over, leaving behind special marks to trap enemy War Sages.
Humans were one of the fourteen great races and had established their own sacred place, the Violet Heart Sanctuary, in the continent’s far north. But among the fourteen races, the human’s sanctuary was ranked almost dead last.
The Ogre War Lords had sneered that the humans on this continent were weak, but Phoebe’s words painted an even bleaker picture.
Back in the Heartlands, the Eastern Territories, and the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms, humans were the undisputed powerhouses. Other races barely had a foothold. But here, they weren't the dominant species, a fact that truly surprised Zane.
He was about to find out why.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
It was all because of the Tower of Sin. The strongest warriors from every race on the main continent were all inside it.
The Tower of Sin held cultivation resources beyond imagination, resources that were essential for every race to thrive. Because of this, each race’s top priority was to send its people into the Tower to train and fight for control. There was just one catch: War Sages were forbidden from entering.
“You’ll understand more once you get inside the Tower of Sin,” Phoebe said, watching him with a slight smile. Her orders were just to follow Zane until Elder May arrived, and none of this was exactly top-secret information.
[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]
Flying was impossible here, so they ran. As warriors, they covered the ground at an incredible pace. After a few hours, a colossal structure began to dominate the horizon.
Phoebe pointed toward the massive structure. “That’s the Tower of Sin.”
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
[SFX: DEEP RESONANT HUM]
Zane stared, his eyes widening as he processed what he was seeing.
The Tower of Sin was built upside down.
