[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Ailsa kept her head bowed, her face hidden.

[SFX: SUPERNATURAL GLOW]

[MUSIC: EERIE CHOIR]

But at the sound of Amber's voice, she looked up. Everyone saw her eyes glowing with an eerie blue light, like otherworldly fireflies on a dark summer night.

[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

In a flash, she lunged at Zane, her hands shooting out to snatch the Soulcradle Wood.

The sudden attack caught him off guard, and his defenses shot up instantly. He couldn't believe a member of the Demon Night would endanger Lavender like this.

The queen's soul was impossibly fragile. Even the slightest damage to the Soulcradle Wood could shatter it completely.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

With a flick of his wrist, Zane tossed the wood into the air.

Ailsa's eyes were glued to the brown branch. She leaped for it, but Zane was faster. He snagged her by the ankle and yanked her back down.

“Get down here!”

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

He slammed the blue-eyed girl onto the ground, snatching the Soulcradle Wood safely out of the air.

[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]

[SFX: STRUGGLE]

In a heartbeat, the two guards beside Amber were on top of Ailsa, pinning her to the floor.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

“Ailsa!” Amber shrieked. “What the hell do you think you're doing?”

Ailsa ignored Amber, her gaze fixed on Zane. “You're Zane, aren't you?” she sneered.

Zane's brow furrowed. That voice, he thought. He'd heard it before—back in the Holy Tree Hall. It was the voice from the statue of the Queen of Punishment, the one he’d shattered.

[SFX: MULTIPLE FOOTSTEPS]

[AMBIENT: REVERENT SILENCE]

The moment Ailsa spoke, every member of the Demon Night dropped to one knee.

[SFX: UNIFIED VOICES]

“Queen!”

Their people had three queens. Lavender was the Queen of Slaughter; the one before them now was the Queen of Punishment. It seemed this queen had used some forbidden technique to possess Ailsa’s body, all to harm—or maybe even kill—Lavender.

Amber had a sinking feeling she knew why the Queen of Punishment was after Lavender, but this was a fight she couldn't afford to get involved in. For now, she had to play neutral and see how things played out.

Zane, on the other hand, wasn't Demon Night. He couldn't care less about some power struggle between their queens. Anyone who tried to hurt Lavender was his enemy, and he'd do whatever it took to stop them.

[MUSIC: DARK CONFRONTATION]

Zane let out a cold smirk. “Yeah, I'm Zane. And you're the Demon Night's Queen of Punishment, I take it? Got a name?”

[SFX: MENACING VOICE DISTORTION]

“You have no right to know my name,” the possessed Ailsa snarled. “I suggest you hand over that Soulcradle Wood. Let Lavender's soul fade from existence. Do that, and I just might let you live.”

For a moment, Zane said nothing. Then he threw his head back and laughed.

“No wonder,” he said, walking toward her slowly. “No wonder Lavender called you a witch. Her soul is barely holding together, and here you are trying to finish the job.” He shook his head. “She was right about you.”

The Queen of Punishment's eyes narrowed. “You're destined to fight in the Battle of the Worlds. Many will die in that war. You will be one of them.”

“Witch.”

“If you hand over the Soulcradle Wood, I can offer you the full power of the Demon Night Clan—“

“Witch.”

“My rank is higher than Lavender's. My abilities are beyond anything you can imagine—“

“Witch.”

She exploded. “Amber! Kill him!”

Amber stayed kneeling. Not a single Demon Night warrior moved.

Left with only Ailsa's body to work with — a third-level War Sage, and a possessed one at that — the Queen of Punishment's strength was a fraction of what it should have been. A soul controlling a borrowed body couldn't draw on its full power. Zane knew that better than anyone. It took three moves. Then she was on the ground, and he was walking away.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON WOOD]

He let her go, then walked alone into the Holy Tree hall.

The Queen of Punishment's statue stood where it always had. He crossed to it and tapped it twice with two fingers.

“Anything else to say?”

The statue's eyes opened. She drew breath—

“Witch.”

[SFX: STATUE SHATTERING]

His fist connected, and the statue burst apart. He shook out his hand and walked back out.

[AMBIENT: SANCTUARY GROUNDS]

[MUSIC: CALM RESOLUTION]

The Demon Night said nothing. Dorian stood to one side with an expression of complete bafflement.

Zane kept his face relaxed. But privately, he made himself a promise: once Lavender had a body again, he would help her take back everything that belonged to her. The Queen of Punishment would answer for every bit of it.

[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL NATURE]

[MUSIC: GENTLE RECOVERY]

With the immediate crisis handled, everyone went their separate ways, leaving the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary to heal.

The land was already recovering at an incredible rate. The holy tree's trunk was scorched black, but thankfully, its root system was unharmed. Its roots stretched for thousands of feet underground, some even reaching a hundred miles out, constantly drawing nutrients from the earth. This massive network was vital for the tree's accelerated recovery.

And as the tree healed, it nourished the sanctuary itself. Tiny green seedlings were already pushing up through the ash, a sure sign the land would be lush again soon.

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

Meanwhile, Dorian and the Violet Heart Sanctuary had joined forces with Lacey and the other Soul Sea Realm masters to launch an attack on the Ogres' two sacred lands.

It was obvious the Ogres' power would wane after they were kicked out of the Tower of Sin, but no one expected them to be wiped out this fast. 

In less than a year, their two great sacred lands would be nothing but ruins, destroyed by the combined might of the Humans and the Demon Night.

The fall of the Ogres was a relief to the other races. The once-mighty Ogres had been brutally cruel, a stark contrast to the more reasonable Humans and Demon Night. Even after taking control of the Tower of Sin, the Humans didn't abuse their power by breaking established rules or trying to eradicate other factions.

Within two days, the Demon Night had gathered all the materials Wade had asked for.

The Tower of Sin was finally within reach.
