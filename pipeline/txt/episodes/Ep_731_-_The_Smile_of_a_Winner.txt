Ep 731 - The Smile of a Winner
Word count: 1298
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING SECT HALL]
[MUSIC: GOSSIP TENSION]
Gossip was a currency in Cloud Hall, and lately, everyone was spending it on Zane. The disciples were green with envy, their whispers following Cora wherever she went. They’d steal glances at her as she walked by, huddled together, talking.
“Zane’s one lucky bastard, huh?” one of them muttered. “His power just explodes in two years, and he comes back with a girl like that, totally wrapped around his finger.”
“Oh, stop whining,” another shot back. “If you were half as sharp as Zane, you’d have figured out how to set up these sect-protecting arrays yourself. Then you’d have your own girl.”
“Forget that. Have you heard the other rumor? The one about Zane and our leader, Tia? They say they used to be a thing.”
“You’re dreaming. You really think a woman as proud as our leader would ever fall for a guy like him?”
“Hey, I heard it straight from an elder. But things have been tense between them, apparently. Because of Cora.”
News like this had a way of getting around. For fighters who knew the right people, it wasn't hard to get the inside scoop. And if the rumors were true, it begged the question: how was one man supposed to keep two women like that happy?
[SFX: DOORS OPENING]
The door to the practice rooms swung open, and Zane stepped out. His eyes swept over the crowd before landing on Cora, who was waiting nearby, a smile already on her face.
She was in a different outfit again — she appeared in front of him every day in something new, and even in plain everyday clothes, something about the way she put herself together made it work. The disciples watching from a distance exchanged another round of envious looks.
Unlike Zane, Cora wasn't known for her dedication to training. She’d reached the Inner-Core Realm mostly through pills and other resources, not by pushing her limits. A life of luxury had made her soft; she was no fighter. It was an unsettling thought because Cloud Hall couldn’t possibly stand against the Corrupted Divine Kingdom on its own.
"Didn't I tell you not to wait out here?" Zane said.
Cora nodded agreeably. "I know."
He knew she'd be back in two days. Her stubbornness was bone-deep — but at least she didn't show the same arrogance in front of him as she had when they first met.
She took his hand as they walked through the long corridor, then laid out the dishes one by one. The food was still warm. After the meal, Zane spent a short while gathering what news he could about the situation in the Heartlands, then returned to cultivation.
He could feel a breakthrough coming — mid-stage Spirit-Tribulation was close. When it arrived, his odds against Maddox would be considerably better. Until then, every hour counted.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]
[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]
The enemy was slowly creeping into the Heartlands, swallowing up the weaker, lesser sects one by one. Those smaller sects had no way to resist. Without losing a single soldier, the Corrupted Divine Kingdom simply absorbed them, forcing them to pledge loyalty and become subjects.
[AMBIENT: COUNCIL CHAMBER]
As the situation grew more dire, the pressure on the Cloud Hall elders mounted. But Tia, their leader, seemed completely oblivious. She hadn't even emerged from her ice palace.
“Our leader can’t just hide away like this,” an elder said, his voice strained. “Oracle City has sent envoys over and over, but she won’t even grant them a meeting.”
“Right now, only Cloud Hall and the World Merchants’ Guild can stand against the Corrupted Divine Kingdom, but she’s doing nothing. What are we supposed to do?”
“Seriously. Our leader is incredibly powerful, but she’s letting her personal feelings get in the way. This is a disaster. Saint Morphen, maybe you should try talking to her?”
A few days ago, Tia had announced she was entering seclusion and refusing all visitors. Under normal circumstances, it wouldn’t have been a problem—a ten-year training session was practically routine. But this was the worst possible time. War was on their doorstep, and Cloud Hall couldn’t afford to be without its leader.
Saint Morphen was closer to Tia than anyone, and he understood her better than most. He still saw her as the child he’d watched grow up, and he knew her stubbornness was as much a part of her as her talent. It was that very trait that had made her the leader of Cloud Hall. He doubted he could persuade her.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Still, at the urging of the other elders, he finally decided to make his way to the ice palace.
[AMBIENT: ICY WIND]
[SFX: CRACKLING ICE]
Inside, a delicate blue frost coated every surface, formed from the sheer Vitality Tia gave off during her training. When she was still at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, the chill had been manageable for him. But now that she’d reached the Life-and-Death Realm, the frost was a genuine threat. Morphen moved carefully, channeling his own Vitality to fight off the intense cold as he navigated the swirling energy.
When he finally passed through the last layer of frost and found her, he didn’t know whether to laugh or cry.
[AMBIENT: QUIET ICE PALACE]
[MUSIC: SOFT CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
She was asleep. A handful of small Ice Phoenixes were curled up beside her. When Saint Morphen stepped closer, they spread their wings and fixed him with alert, suspicious eyes.
Fighters at her level rarely slept from exhaustion; they simply quieted their minds and bodies. But here she was, lost to the world. A month had passed since she’d gone into seclusion—the longest month anyone in Cloud Hall could remember. Though they felt a world away, the teleportation array meant the war was only a single jump from their gates.
Then Tia's eyes opened. She looked at him coldly. "What's the matter?"
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
"The World Merchants' Guild has sent envoys multiple times," Saint Morphen said carefully. "They say they have urgent matters to discuss. They can't just sit and wait for the Corrupted Divine Kingdom to move first."
Tia was quiet for a moment. Then she nodded. "I know. The Guild is more exposed than we are — they have no sect-protecting array, and Oracle City is a wealthier target. They're right to be anxious." She uncrossed her legs and stood. "Tell them we'll meet. Cloud Hall and the Guild will coordinate a joint response against the Corrupted Divine Kingdom."
Saint Morphen let out a slow breath. "I'll notify them."
The teleportation array between Cloudhaven and Oracle City was still intact. Distance, at least, was not the problem.
When Tia finally floated out of her ice palace, the first person she saw was Cora. Her eyes narrowed. The younger woman was sitting by the door to the practice rooms, her chin resting in her hand as she stared at the closed door in a daze.
“Cora is with me now.” The words echoed in Tia’s mind, sharp and unbelievable. A part of her still refused to accept Zane had said them.
Her expression soured, a flash of raw envy in her eyes. If only she could forget she was the leader of Cloud Hall, if only she could just wait for Zane like that, how happy she would be.
[MUSIC: SUBTLE TENSION]
At that moment, Cora must have felt her gaze. She looked up slowly, and when her eyes met Tia’s, she blinked before breaking into a bright, innocent smile.
Tia scoffed under her breath. Annoyed, she turned and flew away.
So pathetic.
[MUSIC: DARK TRIUMPHANT THEME]
Meanwhile, Cora turned her head back toward the practice rooms. A faint, knowing smile spread across her face—the smile of a winner.
