Zane and Yates stared at each other as the crowd watched breathlessly. How could Zane disarm Yates so completely? No one could understand it. The display of power was unmistakable. In a fluid motion, Zane snatched up the spear and swung it toward Yates's head with lethal precision. The weapon cut through the air with a menacing whistle.
[SFX: WEAPON WHIZZING THROUGH AIR]
Yates stood paralyzed, his earlier tough guy bravado vanishing in an instant. His eyes widened in terror, pupils dilating as the reality of his situation crashed upon him. Not a muscle twitched in defense; his body betrayed him when survival mattered most.
The spear's tip approached its target with inevitable momentum, promising swift retribution. But just as the spear was about to connect, a brilliant light erupted between weapon and victim.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
[SFX: CROWD GASPS]
Blue Vitality materialized and coalesced into a dense, shimmering shield before Yates, its sudden appearance drawing gasps from the onlookers who had been holding their collective breath.
[SFX: METAL IMPACT]
The spear slammed into the barrier, producing a resounding clang that echoed throughout the hall. Not a single fracture appeared on the shield's surface; instead, the tremendous force of impact sent the weapon careening back toward Zane with doubled velocity.
With fluid grace, Zane pivoted sideways, his body moving with calculated precision. He snatched the incoming spear from midair and immediately launched it back at the shield.
[SFX: REPEATED METAL IMPACTS]
Seven, eight times he struck the shield, each impact more forceful than the last. The metallic clangs filled the room, yet the shield's blue surface remained flawless.
[SFX: METAL CLATTER]
Finally, recognizing the futility of his efforts, Zane casually tossed the spear aside. It clattered against the floor as a cool, knowing smirk touched his lips. His eyes, sharp and calculating, looked past the seething Yates to the true source of his interest---the imposing figure standing behind the young man.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ENTRANCE]
The man who had blocked the attack stepped forward. It was James Moon, Yates's father, his posture radiating authority.
"You've already humiliated my son, kid." His voice was ice cold, each word precisely measured. "What purpose does it serve for you to keep pressing him?"
Privately, James couldn't help but be impressed by the youth's capabilities. Zane's power vastly exceeded what one would expect from a first-level Adept. At this rate, the boy would no doubt evolve into an unstoppable force within the cultivation world. But his alliance with Ethan marked him as an adversary---a situation James could not let go.
"Why should I stop?" Zane launched back with a cocky smile on his face. "Everything he threw at me was meant to kill. Why can't I return the favor? You're his father. You let him run wild until he became a menace, and now you have the nerve to blame me? That's insane."
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
The assembled crowd shifted uncomfortably, their whispers rippling through the chamber.
James's face flushed, a mix of fury and humiliation. How dare this kid insult House Moon in front of all their guests? They might not be major players, and most weren't even cultivators, but they were all prominent figures with close ties to the family.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ESCALATION]
James clenched his jaw. He was a master of the Soul Realm. He wouldn't be humiliated by some first-level Adept.
"How I raise my son is none of your business!" James retorted, trying to sound like a reasonable elder, but his voice was tight with rage. "How dare you cause a scene in my home! My son hasn't done a thing to you." Deep down, he was itching to put the cocky young man in his place.
"'He hasn't done anything?' I'm making trouble? Are you kidding me?" Zane fired back. "I came here as a guest of my friend, Ethan. How is that causing a scene? Your son is the one who started picking fights with Ethan the second he walked in. And you---you're holding Ethan's heirloom hostage. As the head of this house, aren't you the one who's completely out of line here?"
Ethan stared, his mouth hanging open. Hearing Zane stand up for him like that... his heart swelled. He couldn't believe he was lucky enough to have a friend like this. In front of everyone, he felt his eyes begin to well up.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL CONFRONTATION]
Emboldened by Zane's words, Ethan stepped forward. His voice shook, but he didn't stop.
"Uncle. You told me my father died under a barrage of arrows. You said you weren't there when he died." He pointed at James's wrist. "So why does his blood jade bracelet have your name on it? If you weren't there — how did it end up in your hands? Did you kill him?"
[SFX: SHOCKED MURMURS FROM THE CROWD]
James's expression didn't crack. A cold smile settled on his face. "Go dig up your father's grave and ask him yourself. That's not a question I'm able to answer."
"Fine. Then answer me this — why do you have the bracelet?"
James said nothing. He turned his gaze from Ethan as if the question were beneath him.
"You don't know what's good for you." James's voice dropped to something quiet and dangerous. "I've looked after you your whole life. Is this how you speak to your elder?"
"Looked after me?" Ethan let out a short, bitter laugh. "Every servant in this house outranks me. You've been trying to push me out for years and we both know it."
James's patience snapped. He raised his hand, and blue Vitality coalesced into a longsword. Its tip swung toward Ethan before it had even fully formed.
[SFX: VITALITY BLADE IGNITING]
[SFX: CROWD PULLING BACK]
"I'll teach you some manners — on your father's behalf."
Zane was already moving.
[SFX: FIST IMPACT — EXPLOSIVE CRACK]
His fist met the longsword mid-thrust. Dark purple Vitality detonated on contact and shredded James's blade on the spot.
"He doesn't need that kind of lesson," Zane said. "And you don't have the right to give it."
James stared at the dissipating remnants of his sword. For the first time, something shifted behind his eyes — not rage, but calculation. Whatever he'd expected from a first-level Adept, it wasn't this.
[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDUP]
Suddenly, Dustin's brow furrowed. He knew Zane was outmatched. Beating a Soul Realm master as an Adept was nearly impossible; Zane couldn't afford a single mistake. Slipping a hand under his shirt, Dustin placed a finger on the signaling token against his chest. As he traced its surface, an invisible wave began to ripple outward.
He wasn't the only one. Across the room, Ingrid was doing the exact same thing.
"This guy," she thought, a wry smile playing on her lips. "He can't go anywhere without diving headfirst into trouble. He comes over for dinner and ends up fighting the head of the house." Still, a surge of pride washed over her. This was her man.
How many Adepts, after all, would dare to go head-to-head with a Soul Realm master?
As far as she knew, only Zane had the guts.
And he was just a first-level Adept.
With more training, once he reached the ultimate level, he'd be able to take down any of them. Ingrid had no doubt he could do it, but that didn't stop the knot of worry tightening in her stomach.
James was no pushover; he was far more powerful than Hugh.
Just to be safe, she activated the Signal Talisman in her hand. The Capital City of the Empire was vast, but for a Soul Realm master, crossing it would take only an instant.

But James was different.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
