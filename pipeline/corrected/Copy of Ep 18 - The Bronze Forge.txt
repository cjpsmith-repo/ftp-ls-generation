Ep 18 - The Bronze Forge
Word Count: 1122
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
The crowd parted, revealing a middle-aged man dressed in brilliant white. He moved with the effortless grace of an Eternal, his face bearing a deceptively charming smile that never reached his calculating eyes.
"Bryson," Zane spat, his face contorting into a venomous scowl. His heart hammered against his ribs as cold rage washed through him. Of all the people in this wretched world, his oldest uncle was the absolute last person he wanted to confront.
Bryson—the true architect behind his father's murder—had manipulated his younger brother Owen like a puppet master, making him nothing more than a willing accomplice in their treachery.
[AMBIENT: SUFFOCATING SILENCE]
With Bryson's arrival, a suffocating silence descended upon the yard. The air itself seemed to grow heavy, charged with unspoken malice. Zane dropped into a low defensive crouch, every muscle in his body coiling tight like a spring ready to release.
Bryson existed in an entirely different realm of power—far beyond mere Marrow Refining.
He stood merely half a step away from achieving the legendary Nature Level itself.
And Zane? He had just barely reached Core Strength. An entire level separated them—not merely a gap but an unbridgeable chasm. They weren't even playing the same game.
Yet surrender remained unthinkable. If Zane lacked the will to fight, he would have broken long ago under the weight of his torment. Fate had finally granted him this second chance, this singular opportunity to reclaim his shattered life, and he refused to perish here like a cornered animal. "I have to take it," he thought, a desperate fire igniting in his chest, spreading warmth to his trembling limbs. "I have to live—not just for myself, but for my father's memory."
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC FIGHTING]
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE MOVEMENT]
Zane took one deep, steadying breath, then exploded into a dead sprint. He didn't make it twenty feet before a sword, humming with purple light, plunged from the sky and slammed into the ground directly in his path.
[SFX: SWORDS PLUNGING INTO GROUND]
Zane twisted, changing direction on a dime, but another sword appeared, then another. They rained from the sky, a cage of steel and light, forcing him back step by step until he was right where he started, his face a grim mask of frustration.
That was the power of someone approaching the Nature Level—the ability to command Life Energy itself.
Bryson wasn't quite there, but the sliver of control he possessed was already terrifying.
Bryson clasped his hands casually behind his back. "I let you live to carry on your family's line, Zane. A kindness, it seems, you failed to appreciate. You wound your uncle, you try to flee your own clan... Since you're so eager to die, I'll be happy to grant your wish."
[SFX: SWORDS FLYING THROUGH AIR]
At his command, the swords ripped from the ground, swirled into a vortex of steel, and shot toward Zane. His eyes widened. His body might be tough as forged iron, but against Bryson's power, he might as well be made of paper. A wave of helpless fury washed over him. "It can't end here. Not like this."
Just then, Owen cut in. "Bryson, wait! Don't kill him. Not yet."
The swords froze, inches from Zane's skin. Bryson turned, his expression annoyed. "And why not?"
"There's something different about him," Owen said quickly. "He's had some kind of specialized training. His body... It's unnaturally strong. Perrin and Andrew were using Aether Crystals to boost their refinement, and this kid still left them in the dust."
One of the swords drifted until its tip hovered an inch from between Zane's eyes. "So," Bryson said, his voice devoid of emotion. "What's your secret?"
Zane just sneered.
If he was going to die anyway, he'd be damned if he'd give them the satisfaction. He wouldn't say a word.
[SFX: WOOD EXPLODING]
Suddenly, the heavy plaque above the main doors of House Lowell exploded into splinters.
[SFX: HEAVY METAL CRASH]
[AMBIENT: PEOPLE SCREAMING]
A massive Bronze Forge dropped from the sky, hitting the ground with a deafening crash. The younger Lowells clapped their hands over their ears, crying out at the noise as the forge began to roll, tumbling down the stone steps and sending them scattering and shrieking.
[SFX: SWORDS WHISTLING THROUGH AIR]
"Who dares?!" Bryson snarled, his voice cutting through the air like a blade. Instantly, all the swords redirected, whistling as they flew toward the rolling forge with deadly precision.
[SFX: METAL STRIKING METAL]
They struck the bronze with a series of percussive booms and high-pitched shrieks of metal on metal, gouging deep furrows in its surface. Yet the forge continued its relentless advance, undeterred by the assault. It hit the bottom of the steps, spun once with an unnatural wobble that defied physics, and then rolled directly toward Zane, as if drawn by some invisible force.
Cursing under his breath, Bryson abandoned his sword control and lunged for his nephew, his fingers outstretched with desperate urgency.
[SFX: MECHANICAL MOVEMENT]
But the forge moved with surprising speed.
[SFX: STARTLED CRY CUT SHORT]
Just as it reached Zane, a scrawny, skeletal hand shot out from an opening and yanked him inside the metal shell, his startled cry cut short as he disappeared into darkness.
"You're not going anywhere!" Bryson roared, his face contorted with rage. As Zane vanished inside, Bryson sprang forward and seized the forge by its rim, his formidable strength halting its movement instantly. The veins in his forearms bulged with effort as he held the massive object in place.
A moment later, the same scrawny hand emerged again, now transformed into a talon-like weapon aimed directly at Bryson's face, its bony fingers gleaming with an otherworldly energy.
Bryson reacted with lightning reflexes, swatting the attack aside with his palm.
[SFX: VIOLENT SHOCKWAVE]
The impact of their collision sent a violent shockwave through the air, and Bryson staggered back several steps, his eyes widening with genuine surprise and a flicker of fear.
Bryson's face darkened with recognition, old memories surfacing behind his eyes. "Evil Jace?" he uttered, the name carrying weight and history.
[SFX: OMINOUS MAGIC]
As if responding to his cue, an elderly man poked his head out of the forge. He was extraordinarily emaciated, his bones pressing against skin so thin it resembled ancient parchment, his sunken eyes gleaming with manic intelligence.
[SFX: WHEEZING CACKLE]
A wheezing laugh escaped his withered lips, echoing across the courtyard. "Hah! What luck! What incredible luck!" he cackled, his gaze fixed on where Zane had disappeared. "This boy... he has a particular scent. Smells like a finely forged weapon." His tongue darted across cracked lips in anticipation. "He'll make perfect material for my next masterpiece. I'm taking him!"
