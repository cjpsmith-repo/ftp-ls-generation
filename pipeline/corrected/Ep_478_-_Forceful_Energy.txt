Ep 478 - Forceful Energy

Word count: 1100

[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

Zane had braced himself for the pain of Body Refining, but he never imagined it would be this bad. Every nerve screamed in protest as the technique tore through his body, reshaping muscle and bone with merciless precision. It was unbearable.

Even with his soul in the Fighting Soul Realm and his willpower honed to a razor's edge, it wasn't enough. He'd faced death before, but this was different. This pain didn't just assault his body; it invaded every corner of his consciousness, leaving no refuge.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

[SFX: HEARTBEAT FADING]

The pain simply overwhelmed him, and the world went black.

[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL ROOM]

[SFX: DOOR OPENING]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]

A girl in a green silk dress came in, carrying a steaming pot of spiced tea. She had kind, pure eyes, Zane noticed, and was surprisingly pretty.

"Oh, you're awake," she said softly.

Seeing his eyes flutter open, she quickly poured a cup of tea and held it out to him with both hands, a gesture of genuine care.

Zane eyed her warily at first, instincts honed by countless betrayals making him suspicious of any kindness. But his guard dropped when he realized she was just a normal person, completely outside the world of martial arts. No cultivation aura. No hidden intent. Just simple human compassion—something he'd almost forgotten existed.

[SFX: LIQUID POURING]

[SFX: DRINKING]

He relaxed, giving her a small, polite nod before carefully taking the cup and downing a large gulp of the hot, refreshing tea.

"You looked like you were in agony," she said, her voice laced with concern. "Should I get a doctor?"

Alice Bishop knew warriors were built differently than regular people, but she'd never seen anyone in as much torment as Zane. Whatever he was going through, it seemed bad enough to need a doctor.

Zane managed a faint smile and shook his head. "No, thank you. I'll be fine. A doctor wouldn't be able to help with this, anyway."

"How can you be so sure?" she pressed. "I know an amazing doctor, Dr. Warrick. His clinic is right around the corner, and he's been practicing for decades."

When it was clear Zane wasn't going to budge, she dropped it. On her way out, she promised to bring him dinner later that evening.

The second Alice was gone, Zane shot up from the bed—and slammed his head right into the wooden ceiling.

"Oww!" he yelped, rubbing the sore spot.

The noise brought Alice rushing back. She poked her head through the doorway, her expression a mix of concern and curiosity.

Zane rubbed his head, embarrassed. He let out an awkward laugh. "Sorry about that. If I broke anything, I'll pay for it."

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]

Zane had always believed the warm current flowing through his body served a single purpose—purging impurities. That cleansing process was merely the first step, preparing his body to become pure enough to reach the Adept Realm. But ever since achieving that breakthrough, the current had felt strangely dormant, like a sleeping beast that no longer stirred. He'd dismissed it as useless, its purpose fulfilled.

How wrong he'd been.

The realization struck him with the force of revelation. This wasn't some residual energy left over from his advancement. It was Primordial Energy—the fundamental force that would unlock his Body Refining training. All this time, the key to his next stage of cultivation had been flowing through his body, waiting for him to understand its true nature.

[MUSIC: EPIC RISING POWER]

After the seventh brother had guided him in using the Primordial Energy for his refinement, Zane felt completely transformed. The instant he'd jumped from the bed, a new power had surged through him—something raw and distinct from Vitality. He could feel it humming under his skin, but he had no idea how to control it.

That lack of control was exactly why he'd just slammed his head into the ceiling.

"Ha-ha, looks like you formed your first Forceful Energy on the first try," the seventh brother's voice echoed in his mind. "That Primordial Energy packs a punch, doesn't it?"

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL ENERGY HUM]

"Forceful Energy?" Zane repeated, his eyes lighting up with excitement.

"Yep," the seventh brother confirmed. "It's the signature martial art of the Body Refining path."

The Body Refining system and the Vitality cultivation system were two totally separate martial arts disciplines. While both began with physical conditioning through the Five Realms of Body Refining, their paths diverged sharply after that. Vitality cultivators would progress through the Adept, Soul, and Inner-Core Realms, striving for the Spirit-Tribulation Realm and beyond.

Body Refiners, however, embarked on a different journey. After mastering the five foundational realms, they had to refine each of their one hundred and eight Nexus Points to build their internal Forceful Energy.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BUILDING TENSION]

Within his consciousness, the flame and the lightning bolt intertwined, then shot like an arrow toward the point between his eyebrows.

This was a major Nexus Point in the human body—the twentieth point of the Crown Conduit.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TRIAL]

Zane’s whole body began to tremble as the merged energy pierced his first Nexus Point. Cold sweat beaded on his forehead and trickled down his temples.

Refining Nexus Points was just as searing as the full Body Refining, but horrifyingly concentrated. Instead of his entire body screaming in agony, all of that torment was focused on a single, blinding point.

[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]

Pure flame and lightning energy radiated outward from the spot between his eyebrows, and his skull felt like it might split apart from the mounting pressure.

This was the ultimate test of his willpower—perhaps the greatest he had ever faced. The flame and lightning were born from his own comprehension of the Laws of Fire and Thunder, but now he had to contain and refine that raw, volatile power within a single, impossibly fragile point in his own body.

The irony wasn't lost on him. He had mastered these forces, bent them to his will in combat, and wielded them against opponents who had cultivated for decades longer than he had. But mastery in battle meant nothing here.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

One slip, one heartbeat of lost focus, and that energy would surge directly into his brain, burning through everything that made him who he was. His memories of Elara, his promise to rescue her, his father's legacy, Finn's unwavering friendship—all of it would turn to ash in an instant.

He couldn't let that happen.

He wouldn't let that happen.

Gritting his teeth until his jaw ached, Zane forced his trembling hands to remain steady on his knees. Every instinct screamed at him to release the pressure, to let the energy dissipate before it destroyed him. But he had come too far, sacrificed too much, to surrender now.

He had to make it through.
