Ep 53 - Two Paths to Prison
Word count: 1025
[MUSIC: TENSE DISCUSSION]
"Two ways," Rhys Bolton said, holding up two fingers. "First, you can spend a hundred Points on a pass to Infernal Peak."
"Points?" The word seemed to follow Zane everywhere since he'd arrived at the Skyreach Sect. The old man at the Entrance Exam had mentioned them for getting into the high-pressure training room. Then, after that brawl with House Warrick before The Gauntlet, the Core Apprentice who'd started it all got hit with a thousand-Point penalty.
"So, what's the deal with these Points?" Zane leaned in. "How do you even get them?"
"You can't do a single thing in the Skyreach Sect without Points," Rhys explained. "You guys have to get this. Pledges need them to become Core Apprentices. You need them for cultivation elixirs, for food, and for your room. Everything runs on Points, and it's all tracked on your Access Card."
"So how do you get a hundred Points fast?" Zane cut to the chase. He didn't care about the system; he just needed a way to get to Elara at Infernal Peak.
"The Sect masters assign daily tasks," Rhys said with a tight smile. "You complete them, you get Points. The problem is, pledges don't earn much. It could take you half a year to make a hundred. Hell, I've been here a while and I still don't have a hundred Points to my name."
"Half a year?" The words felt like a punch to the gut. No. There had to be a faster way.
"Well," Rhys added, "you could always trade two hundred Crystal Cubes for a hundred Points."
A spark of hope lit up in Zane's chest; he had plenty of Crystal Cubes in his Storage Ring. But his hope fizzled when he did the math. The exchange rate was insane. At that rate, any pledge from the Sect could go back to the normal world and live like a king.
[MUSIC: ANXIOUS UNDERTONE]
"Even if I trade a hundred Cubes for fifty Points, how do I get the other fifty?" Anxiety washed over him. That was still three months of grinding out tasks. Three months until he could see Elara. Too long.
Desperation etched itself across Zane's face. "You mentioned there were two ways to reach Infernal Peak."
Rhys's tight smile transformed into a knowing smirk, his eyes glinting with mischief. "The second way? Simply break a major Sect rule. They'll exile you straight to Infernal Peak without a moment's hesitation."
[MUSIC: SOMBER CONTEMPLATION]
Zane fell silent, absorbing the implications before releasing a dry, humorless laugh that echoed in the empty space between them. "Well, that's certainly one direct route," he thought, running his fingers through his disheveled hair. But deliberately getting himself expelled hardly constituted a viable plan—especially after everything he'd sacrificed to reach this point.
His shoulders slumped as reality settled upon them like a heavy cloak. With no Great House connections or powerful sponsor to advocate for him, Zane recognized that patience remained his only true ally in this unfamiliar world of cultivation politics. The bitter taste of this realization lingered on his tongue as he contemplated the long, arduous path that stretched before him.
[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION ROOM]
The instructions in the book appeared deceptively straightforward. Zane had mastered the theoretical framework of transforming his soul into a piercing thorn, yet each attempt birthed an overwhelming sense of wrongness that blossomed deep within his chest. This visceral discomfort sabotaged his spiritual practice before completion, leaving him frustrated and increasingly doubtful.
Ten grueling days had passed. Confined to his sparse quarters, Zane had relentlessly drilled the same technique countless times without glimpsing even a flicker of success.
The invisible barrier he faced felt more impenetrable than any physical wall—a maddening obstacle that tested his resolve and patience. "Practice makes perfect," he muttered to himself, a mantra that rang increasingly hollow as hours bled into days. His determination wavered between fierce resolve and crushing disappointment with each failed attempt.
[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL MOUNTAIN ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL UNDERTONES]
Exhaustion finally conquered his stubborn pride. Zane acknowledged that his mind needed respite from this fruitless cycle. A stroll around Misty Peak's grounds might clear the fog of failure that clouded his thoughts.
The fresh air called to him after his self-imposed isolation—a prison of concentration and mounting frustration. His absence from common areas and single-minded dedication had sparked curious whispers among fellow pledges, who wondered what mysterious technique consumed the newcomer so completely.
As he walked out of his tiny room, he caught his reflection in a mirror and did a double-take. His hair was a mess, and he'd grown a full-on beard.
Just then, Ethan Grant walked by. He wrinkled his nose. "Dude, you need a shower."
Turns out, Misty Peak had everything—bathrooms, dining halls, gardens, even coffee lounges.
[AMBIENT: BUSY DINING HALL]
After Zane was finally clean, he and Ethan headed to the dining hall. The place was stunning, with tables and chairs arranged in perfect symmetry and decorations that looked like they belonged in a palace. The moment they sat down, an array of mouth-watering dishes appeared on the table before them.
Among the culinary offerings was Power-grain, a prized delicacy from the Eastern Territory typically accessible only to society's upper echelons, yet remarkably available to every Skyreach pledge. The grain melted on the tongue with its tender sweetness, releasing an intoxicating aroma that wafted through the dining hall.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Zane had just lifted his spoon, anticipating that first delectable bite, when the dining hall's atmosphere shifted. Conversations hushed. Heads turned. A group of pledges strode purposefully across the room, their gazes fixed with predatory intensity on where Zane and Ethan sat.
The leader—a broad-shouldered youth with a jagged scar running along his jawline—planted his hands on their table, leaning forward until his face hovered inches from Zane's. His lips curled into a sneer as he jabbed a thick finger at Zane's chest.
[SFX: TABLE THUMP]
"This seat's taken," he growled, voice dropping to a dangerous whisper that somehow carried more threat than a shout. "Move. Now."
Behind him, his companions shifted into position, effectively blocking any easy exit. The dining hall fell completely silent, dozens of eyes watching, waiting to see how the new pledge from nowhere would respond to this very public challenge.
