Ep 285 - Desperate Measures
Word count: 1023
[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT ARENA]
[SFX: SWORD SLICING AIR]
Sunlight flashed off the Streamer as Zane sliced it through the air, light dancing along its polished steel.
[SFX: BLADE STOPPING]
With a flick of his wrist, he brought the blade to Tracy's throat. The dancing points of light slid down the sword, stopping just an inch from her pale neck. An inch from death.
He was so close that the slightest pressure would be enough to end it.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
"Stay down," Zane said, his face an emotionless mask. "I don’t know the first thing about ’Burning the Life-Essence,’ and honestly, I don’t care if you want to throw your life away. All I know is it’ll give you a power-up, and I’m not giving you that shot. So. Stay. Down. Or die."
[SFX: BLADE CUTTING SKIN]
His voice was ice, holding no warmth for the beautiful woman before him. To prove his point, he pressed the Streamer forward, just a fraction. With absolute control, the tip broke her delicate skin. A single, thin trickle of blood traced a line down her neck.
He was being this harsh for a reason. He might not have known exactly what Burning the Life-Essence did, but any technique that fed on a person’s lifespan had to be terrifyingly powerful. He finally had the upper hand, and he wasn’t about to give her an opening.
Tracy felt the cold intent behind the blade at her throat, and the fight seemed to drain out of her. Her tense shoulders slumped. She was frozen, a puppet with its strings cut.
She had never been this close to death.
Then, without warning, she turned her head.
Zane yanked the sword back on instinct.
"I admit defeat," Tracy said, her voice barely above a whisper.
[AMBIENT: SPECTATOR AREA]
Watching from outside the arena, Patrick let out a long, quiet sigh of relief as he saw her finally give in.
[SFX: ANGRY FOOTSTEPS]
But once she was out of the ring, Tracy stomped over to a corner and sat down, her cheeks puffed out in a furious pout.
He knew that look. Having been like a big brother to her for years, Patrick knew her temper inside and out. Well, he thought, maybe a little failure will do her some good. Might knock some of that attitude out of her in the long run.
The important thing was, Zane had done the right thing. He'd stopped Tracy from Burning the Life-Essence and sacrificing her own lifespan.
Burning the Life-Essence was a desperate, last-ditch move. Only someone as reckless as Tracy would even think of using it just to win a tournament match.
Patrick turned his gaze back to the arena and gave Zane a grateful nod.
Zane returned it with a small smile before stepping out of the ring.
They both knew their own fight was coming soon enough.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL REFLECTION]
Watching Zane leave the arena victorious, Master Warren was overcome by a storm of emotions.
He'd led Misty Peak for a long time, and ever since his disciples started competing in the All Peaks Competition, his greatest dream had been to see one of them crack the top one hundred.
He’d pinned all his hopes on this year, but when Eliot was eliminated early, the spirits of everyone on Misty Peak had hit rock bottom. Then came Zane. To everyone’s shock, he’d powered through every match, taking down opponent after opponent, no matter how strong. He just kept winning. And now, all of Master Warren’s hopes rested on his shoulders.
He was so overwhelmed, all he could do was let out a quiet, awestruck breath.
If only Isla were here to see this, Master Warren thought. I wonder what she’d think, seeing her favorite disciple come so far.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
But she wasn’t. Rumors had been swirling throughout the Empire—the palace was in turmoil. A power struggle between the princes, and somehow, Isla was caught right in the middle of the mess.
Master Warren was from the same peak as Isla; they went way back. He’d been trying to keep tabs on the situation, but his position wasn’t high enough to get good information. He wasn’t as well-informed as, say, Saint Viana. He had no idea Isla had already been placed under house arrest.
With no word from her at all, he could only assume the worst—that she was trapped, or even imprisoned.
And there was nothing he could do to help her.
Then, an idea struck him. His eyes lit up.
Zane!
Maybe Zane can help her! He just beat Tracy. If he keeps this up, he could absolutely make it to the Top Three! That means he’d be qualified to attempt the Cloud Road!
If he passes the trial and gets into the Cloud Hall, he’d be in a position to help Isla.
Even if Zane joins the Cloud Hall, his strength won’t match Master Elwood’s or Sir Kenneth’s right away... but he’d still be a huge help. Once you’re in the Cloud Hall, your future is limitless.
Of course, the final rankings for the very top seats of the Skyreach Sect—and by extension, the All Peaks Competition—would ultimately be decided by the higher-ups. For now, what the roaring crowd cared about were the showdowns between the top contenders.
That didn’t mean the other matches weren’t important. The battle for the top one hundred spots would directly determine the ranking of the Thirty-Three Peaks, a matter of immense pride for all the disciples.
[MUSIC: ANTICIPATION BUILDING]
As the crowd buzzed with anticipation, wondering who would be up next, the Referee finally announced a match everyone had been waiting for.
[SFX: CROWD ROAR]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ANNOUNCEMENT]
"Personal disciple Jacob versus Zane of Misty Peak!"
Earlier, when Jacob had first stepped into the arena, he’d publicly challenged Zane. He was so confident that he’d even promised to restrain his Soul Realm power and fight Zane at the Adept Realm level. But Zane had shot back with even more arrogance, telling Jacob not to bother holding back—they’d have a real fight when the time came.
The two had become rivals before they’d even traded a single blow. Now, the round-robin had finally pitted them against each other.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Every single person in the audience knew they were in for one hell of a show.
