Ep 721 - Jealousy
Word count: 1074
[AMBIENT: QUIET MONASTERY]
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
Tia had arrived at the prestigious Cloud School years ago, carrying the hatred of her family’s annihilation like a stone in her chest—a weight so heavy she’d never once shed a tear. From that day forward, she’d thrown herself into martial arts, driven by the single, burning hope that she would one day avenge them.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS]
The competition at Cloud School was a world away from the Skyreach Sect. But that was to be expected. The Skyreach Sect was a backwater, and Zane’s old rivals there were little more than scrappy fighters from local families—so-called noble houses that wouldn’t even register as a footnote in The Heartlands.
Cloud School’s students, on the other hand, were mostly heirs to powerful third-grade houses. Tia managed to stand out among them not just because of her incredible talent, but because of a determination that bordered on obsession.
When she first walked through its gates, she was an orphaned girl, younger than most and a complete outsider from another region. The other students shunned her for it. But that isolation only fueled her determination. With nothing but grit to her name, she fought her way up, step by agonizing step, eventually clawing her way past millions of other students to take first place in the Cloud Road Trial.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHOIR]
Her victory earned her a place in Cloud Hall, where she became a rising star under the tutelage of Elder Imogen. Imogen taught her the Goddess Path, a grueling discipline that required Tia to disperse her cultivation eight separate times before she finally ascended to become the leader of Cloud Hall.
But the iron-willed leader wasn’t who she really was. Deep down, she was still that lost little girl, and she kept all her fears and sorrows locked away where no one could ever see them. She could face down death without flinching, a pillar of calm in any storm. But all that self-control shattered the moment she saw Zane standing before her. The feelings she’d suppressed for so long finally broke free, and she just burst into tears.
[MUSIC: ROMANTIC STRINGS]
She’d fallen for him back during the fight for the Dragon Soar Arena. Somehow, the young man had slipped past her defenses and found a place in her heart. But back then, she’d been too afraid, too burdened by her own doubts, to face what she felt. Then came the celestial tomb. She’d lit the Enchanted Lamp and ventured inside, finding priceless treasures, but when she emerged, Zane was gone.
And it was in his absence that she finally understood just how much he meant to her.
For two years, there was nothing. Not a single word. She'd dispatched Cloud Hall's own people to search the entire Heartlands, and when that wasn't enough, she'd issued high-reward missions that spread to the major sects — warriors from across the region taking up the search.
The Cloud Hall elders had criticized her for it. She silenced them in one breath: the rewards came from her own pocket, and Zane's value to Cloud Hall was self-evident. A warrior who could build a sect-protecting array like he had would be worshipped like an ancestor anywhere. Cloud Hall had no reason not to look for him. But even with that many eyes searching, nothing turned up. A year later, she'd had no choice but to cancel the missions.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL PIANO]
She had watched him arrive at Cloud Hall, had seen him rise through the ranks. In her mind, she was his leader, his mentor. And she had fallen in love with him. So, seeing a strange woman suddenly at his side—even one claiming to be just his sword spirit—put every single one of her defenses on high alert.
When the tears finally slowed, Zane looked at her carefully. "Are you done crying?"
Tia blinked. The question landed like a splash of cold water. She became suddenly, acutely aware that she was still in his arms — that she had completely lost her composure in front of him. Her face flushed. She pulled back, straightened herself, and looked away.
"Where have you been?" she asked, her voice sliding back into the measured tone of a Hall Master. "Two years, Zane. You left Cloud Hall without permission. According to our rules, that's a punishable offense."
Zane's mouth curved into a grin. "I went somewhere you've never heard of."
Tia's eyes narrowed. "Be serious."
"I am serious." The grin didn't budge. "What's my punishment?"
She pressed her lips together. "I haven't decided yet. When I think of something suitable, I'll let you know."
Lavender watched from a few steps away, resisting the urge to roll her eyes. Just hug him, she thought. You almost died. He's standing right there. But Tia had already rebuilt every wall in the span of thirty seconds. Lavender didn't understand it — if she felt something, she said it. But she hadn't grown up the way Tia had, and whatever had shaped that iron self-control ran deep.
When Tia finally turned to her with that guarded look, Lavender simply met it with a knowing smile. "Yes, I come from the Demon Night."
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
While Ogres and Demons were not unheard of in The Heartlands, a race like the Demon Night was a complete mystery. Tia had certainly never heard of them, and Lavender’s smile only sharpened her irritation.
"Why are you with Zane?" Tia asked bluntly.
A mischievous glint appeared in Lavender’s eyes. Oh, this is fun, she thought. If you like bottling things up, I’ll give you something worth bottling.
She let out a small giggle. "I'm Zane's sword spirit," she said, her tone light. "We were originally one."
The phrase—we were originally one—hung in the air, deliberately ambiguous. It was a perfect double entendre, easily mistaken for the words of a lover. And in a way, it was true. Before Zane had found her a new body, Lavender had been a spirit dwelling within his sword and, by extension, within him.
Tia's frown deepened as she turned her gaze on Zane, her expression demanding an explanation.
[MUSIC: SERIOUS DRAMATIC]
"Lavender, stop messing around," Zane cut in. He turned to Tia, his expression serious. "It's a long story. I promise I'll explain everything later. But right now, we have to deal with Cloud Hall."
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
A rebellion had just torn through Cloud Hall. Tia didn't know it yet, but Zane had arrived just in time to stop it.
Now, the fate of the traitors was in her hands.
