Ep 1229 - Norton

Word count: 1031

[MUSIC: GENTLE CONTEMPLATIVE]
[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM WITH DISTANT WIND]
The woman in purple was leaned way back in her chair, feet propped up on a stool, completely lost in an old leather-bound book. It was the latest in a popular series she was obsessed with.
And calling her a “fan” was a serious understatement. As a warrior from the Soul Sea Realm, she could have just used her perception to burn the entire book into her memory in a single instant. But where’s the fun in that? That’s no way to enjoy a good story. To really get the plot, to fall for the characters, you had to read every single word. There was a special kind of thrill in watching their fates unfold, page by page.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
Just as she was getting to the good part, a shimmering cloud of yellow Illusion Points zipped into her room and swirled right toward her.
As the points flowed into her, she frowned. 
“What happened?” she muttered, her voice tight with annoyance. She hated being interrupted when a story was getting interesting. “Who killed my guy? Oh, they are going to regret this.”
She carefully dog-eared the page, then tucked the book away safely in her Storage Ring. With a sigh, she shot up from her chair and headed outside. 
[AMBIENT: WINTER EXTERIOR WITH WIND]
The air was cold, and she left a trail of small, neat footprints in the fresh snow.
[MUSIC: TENSE PULSATING]
[AMBIENT: CITY WALLS AT NIGHT]
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]
Meanwhile, Zane was moving fast along the city walls. He’d been right—the City Lord had more than one person working for her.
Turns out, she had eight of them, one posted at every entrance to the city, collecting Illusion Points on her behalf. 
They’d all been her personal bodyguards back in The Heartlands, so it was only natural they’d stick with her here. They were more than happy to hand over every point they collected.
These guards didn’t have the best reputation. People called them the “butchers,” both for their cruelty and for the shady ways they shook down other warriors for their points.
At his pace, it only took Zane a few minutes to cross the ogre city.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
A mountain of a man named Norton stood guard at the far gate, a huge golden hammer in each fist. Everything about him screamed danger. 
His voice boomed across the entrance. “Line up! Cooperate, and I’ll make this quick. One swing. You won’t feel a thing before you’re resurrected back in the city.”
A line of dejected warriors was already forming, resigned to handing over half their accumulated points.
Truth was, Norton didn’t even want to be here. He loved a real fight, and there hadn’t been one since this whole thing started. Every warrior who’d tried him early on had been beaten down so many times they’d gone soft, obedient as sheep. Standing guard for his mistress, cutting down warriors who wouldn’t even raise a hand to defend themselves — it bored him out of his mind. But her orders came first, so here he stood.
Just then, Norton spotted Zane. 
[MUSIC: AGGRESSIVE BATTLE DRUMS]
His face twisted in a furious grin. “Finally! Someone who’ll fight back!”
[SFX: METAL HAMMER CLASH]
[SFX: HAMMERS SMASHING TOGETHER]
[SFX: SHOCKWAVE RIPPLE]
He smashed his hammers together, the impact sending a shockwave rippling through the air.
Zane’s face broke into a grin of his own. He pulled a thread of Chaos Energy through his body, and a gust of wind kicked up around him as his speed surged. He wanted this fight over fast — the last thing he needed was for Norton to bolt for the city, or crush some hidden trick like the warrior with the golden orb had.
[SFX: SPACE RIPPLING]
Zane shot forward and cut through space itself, planning to close the gap and end things with one clean, thunderous strike before Norton had a chance to react.
[SFX: HAMMER SMASHING VOID]
But Norton just snorted, swung both hammers, and smashed them straight into the empty air where Zane had vanished.
[SFX: BOOM]
Zane was thrown staggering out of the void, caught completely off guard. Norton’s hammers hadn’t just tracked his teleport — they’d struck him inside it.
Norton looked just as stunned. His hammers could flatten mountains. A Soul Sea Realm warrior caught by them should have been paste. Instead, Zane had simply stumbled out, qi and blood churning but otherwise unhurt.
“What the hell are you?” Norton breathed. But the shock didn’t send him running like it had the blue-robed warrior. If anything, it lit something up in him. An opponent who could take a full hit and still stand — that was rare enough to be worth savoring, and he wasn’t about to let it go. Whatever points he still owed his mistress could wait.
“Your body’s something else,” Norton said, a wild grin spreading across his face. “But don’t think that means I can’t break you. Don’t run.”
[SFX: HAMMERS CLASHING]
He slammed his hammers together again, ripples spreading through the air, and dropped into a strange, powerful stance.
[SFX: HEAVY CHARGING FOOTSTEPS]
Then he charged, his powerful legs carrying him forward in a strange, lurching gait. 
[SFX: HAMMERS WHOOSHING WILDLY]
As he came, he swung his arms wildly, creating a whirlwind of golden hammers that was both an attack and a shield. His movements were so chaotic that even Zane couldn’t track his next move. He was like a charging bull, focused on nothing but destruction, ready to plow through anything in his path.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]
The wild display definitely got the attention of the warriors waiting in line. Norton’s hammers were terrifying, but a lot less so when they weren’t aimed at you.
“Oh, that dude is toast. You can’t dodge Norton’s attacks.”
“Norton’s going to smash him into pulp. Those hammers are no joke.”
“I heard a story that he once got so mad at the City Lord that he hit the city walls and shook the whole fortress.”
Of course, that was a huge exaggeration. All the sanctuaries in the Illusion Battlefield were created by the Heavens, physical manifestations of their rules. It was impossible to shake their walls. Still, it made for a good story about just how strong Norton was.
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT BUILDUP]
But Zane remained perfectly calm. To him, this was just another opponent. 
[SFX: POWER SURGING]
He stood his ground, letting power surge from his core into his arms. He looked up, his eyes locking onto the storm of golden hammers hurtling toward him, and got ready for a fight.
