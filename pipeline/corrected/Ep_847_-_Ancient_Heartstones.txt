Ep 847 - Ancient Heartstones

Word count: 1028

[SFX: PICKAX STRIKE]

[SFX: SHATTERING CRYSTAL]

Zane gripped the Relic-Class pickax and swung, cracking the giant leaf with a sharp thud. He kept at it, widening the fissure until the leaf shattered, spilling a handful of different-sized ores into his palm.

They weren't exactly a treasure trove. Rare, maybe—even in The Heartlands—but not top-tier. Warriors like Randy, guys from the Alliance of Seven Kingdoms, would probably just toss them aside without a second thought.

But Zane wasn't them. He carefully gathered the ores and tucked them into his Storage Ring. Now what? He’d mined the leaf, which meant there had to be a way out, but he was completely lost, floating in a void with no sky or ground in sight.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[SFX: MAGICAL RIPPLE]

Just then, he glanced up and saw another warrior, someone four or five levels higher, rapidly carving ores from a nearby leaf. Once he was finished, the man simply… jumped, plunging into the emptiness below. A moment later, a ripple of light appeared out of thin air, and the warrior vanished right through it.

So that’s how it worked. A teleportation array, probably hidden somewhere beneath the tree.

Zane was about to leap when he spotted something glinting among the tree's scattered roots. The giant tree was drawing ore up through its main trunk, funneling it into the branches, and finally depositing it in the leaves. A thought struck him: why not just cut off the whole branch?

[SFX: SUDDEN PULL]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]

He lunged toward a thick branch, his hand already reaching for his sword—a top-grade Relic-Class weapon—but before he could even draw it, a powerful force yanked him down. The world blurred.

[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]

When his vision cleared, he was standing outside the pyramid again, sword and pickax still in hand.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL FOREST]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Randy was standing right in front of him, a smirk on his face. He nodded at Zane’s sword. “Trying to take a shortcut, huh? Saw you going for a branch. Plenty of people have had that idea. No one’s ever pulled it off.”

Of course. It made perfect sense. Warriors from the Alliance had been exploring the Sacred Feather Sea for years; they would have tried everything. Hacking off a branch was obviously more efficient than mining a single leaf. But as he now knew firsthand, it didn't matter if you were a warrior from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm or even the Life-and-Death Realm—the tree wouldn't allow it.

“How does the pyramid work?” Zane asked. “The floors — what's on each one?”

Randy gestured toward the structure. “Forty-seven floors, forty-seven leaves. The higher you go, the better the ores, but the harder the leaf and the more dangerous the conditions. The first floor has thousands of doors. By the time you reach the twenty-fifth, there are fewer than a hundred.” He paused. “Ancient Heartstones won't appear below floor twenty-five.”

Zane's eyes sharpened. “Then I'm going to twenty-five.”

Just then, Fredrick arrived at the base of the pyramid with a group of warriors in tow. Without breaking stride, he led them straight up to the twentieth floor, and they filed through the doors one by one.

Randy watched them go. “Smart. The twentieth floor is a good starting point for most.”

Zane was already climbing.

He moved fast, jumping from ledge to ledge up the face of the pyramid. The higher he got, the fewer doors there were — wide rows of entrances near the base tapering to a sparse handful near the top. The air felt different up here, heavier somehow, like the pyramid itself was pushing back.

[SFX: PORTAL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

He'd landed on the twenty-fifth floor, scanned the leaf, and jumped straight back out. No Ancient Heartstones. He hadn't even raised the pickax.

The ores on this leaf were remarkable by any normal standard — dense, richly colored, some of them pulsing faintly with the shimmer of Law Power on their surfaces. On any other trip, he would have filled his Storage Ring. Today, they might as well have been gravel. He hadn't even bothered to mine them.

Below the pyramid, warriors who had been watching began to talk.

“He went straight to the twenty-eighth floor?”

“With the strength he showed, he can probably handle it. Below thirty isn't too dangerous. Above thirty is another story.”

He’d just been ejected from the twenty-eighth floor, tumbling out of the portal with a familiar wave of frustration. He’d failed again. There were no Ancient Heartstones there, either.

Frustration was starting to boil over. He didn't just need one or two Ancient Heartstones; he needed a mountain of them. To fully restore Tia's vital energy, he’d need at least a thousand. At this rate, he’d be lucky to find a couple hundred.

He ran the numbers again, and they kept coming out wrong. Sixteen Heartstones on one leaf, if he was lucky. Forty-seven floors, a fraction of which would even have them. Other warriors competing for the same leaves. A thousand was the minimum Tia needed — not a rough estimate, a floor. Two hundred wouldn't save her. Three hundred wouldn't save her. He needed to find a way to do this faster.

He dusted himself off, gripped his pickax tighter, and headed for the entrance to the twenty-ninth floor.

“What's he trying now?” someone in the crowd muttered.

“Still looking for some rare ore, I guess.”

By now, a small crowd had gathered at the base of the pyramid, watching. Word had apparently spread about the warrior who kept climbing higher and coming back empty-handed.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT DISCOVERY]

This time, when Zane’s feet touched down on a leaf, his eyes lit up. A slow smile spread across his face.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

He'd found them. Ancient Heartstones.

The light-blue leaf beneath his feet was massive, at least ten feet wide and five feet thick, shimmering like a giant crystal. Sealed inside were several egg-shaped Ancient Heartstones—exactly what he’d been searching for.

Zane counted sixteen of them. It was nowhere near enough, but it was a start. He could gather these, then keep searching the other leaves on this floor. As he prepared to start mining, his eyes caught something else. In the center of the cluster of Heartstones was a shiny object he didn't recognize.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

“What is that?” he murmured, leaning in for a closer look.
