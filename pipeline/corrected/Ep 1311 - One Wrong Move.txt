Ep 1311 - One Wrong Move

Word count: 958

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL BATTLE]
[AMBIENT: WINDSWEPT BATTLEFIELD]
Zane loosed three arrows from his longbow—a Semi-Divine Weapon from the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms—but he knew they wouldn’t do any real damage to Slade. They were just a distraction. 
[SFX: ARROWS RELEASED]
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]
Sword already in hand, he shot forward the moment the arrows left the string, closing the distance between them in a blur.
[SFX: ARROWS DEFLECTED]
Slade swatted the arrows aside like they were nothing, his face a mask of calm. But inside, he was on edge. He might be an Ace Talent, but his opponent was *Zane*. The guy who killed Supreme Lords. The great clans hadn’t let up their siege on The Elysian Keep for six years, their World Lords camped outside like vultures. They weren’t after Zane himself—they wanted the Keep he had inherited. That inheritance was the real source of his power.
But then Slade’s eyes fell on the sword in Zane’s hand—a low-grade Divine Weapon, barely grade one. A wave of relief washed over him. *That’s* the best The Elysian Keep could give him? The rumors about its power must have been seriously overblown.
Slade, of course, had no idea that The Elysian Keep didn’t have any ready-made weapons, only a stash of incredibly rare materials. All he knew was that with his own Saint-Killing Spear in hand, he was unbeatable.
[SFX: SPEAR SWING]
[SFX: PULSING ENERGY]
“Arrogant kid!” Slade snarled, swinging his long spear. 
[SFX: ENERGY WAVE EXPANDING]
A blood-red light pulsed from the weapon, coiling in the air like a serpent before fanning out into a wave of pure destructive energy. “Twin Killing Intent!”
This was the second skill of the Saint-Killing Spear. While some Divine Weapons on the Cosmic Spirit Index were known for their bizarre, reality-bending powers, the Saint-Killing Spear was all about raw destructive force. When it came to pure, unadulterated power, it was easily in the top three.
[SFX: IMPACT FORCE]
[SFX: SLIDING FOOTSTEPS]
The wave of blood-red light crashed into Zane, forcing him back dozens of feet. A sneer spread across Slade’s face. “I didn’t think the famous Zane Lowell would be such a coward,” he taunted. “All you do is run. How... interesting.”
Ever since the fight started, Zane had been giving ground, making it look like he couldn’t even get close. Murmurs rippled through the onlookers. Of course, this was how it would go. The Saint-Killing Spear was the deadliest weapon in all of Demon Night. With something like that, even a regular warrior could challenge an Ace Talent. Slade wasn’t winning on his own merits; he was just riding the power of his spear.
[SFX: BLADE DRAWN SLOWLY ALONG STEEL]
Then Zane did something strange. He raised the Thundergust high, ran his fingers slowly down the flat of the blade to its tip, then clamped the point between two fingers and turned the hilt out toward Slade.
Confused murmurs spread through the crowd. Janet, Coleman, and Hailey traded uncertain glances—what was he doing?
But Zane’s mind was elsewhere, back on the peak of Killing Sword Mountain, trading blows with Supreme Lord Sword. Their duel hadn’t really been a contest of strength—it had been closer to a game of chess, each move a question and an answer, testing how well each of them understood himself and the other. 
The technique Supreme Lord Sword had passed to him wasn’t one fixed move at all. It could become any move, whatever the moment demanded—the trick wasn’t raw strength, but reading an opponent clearly enough to know exactly which move to make, and when.
By offering his hilt, Zane was trying to do the same thing to Slade now—read him, and answer him before he could even finish the question.
[SFX: RAPID SPEAR STRIKES]
Slade tried to press his advantage, unleashing a flurry of attacks. 
“Shadowy Spin!”
[SFX: METAL DEFLECTING STRIKES]
Spear-shadows blurred through the air, but Zane’s movements were flawless. 
A smile flickered across his face—then vanished. Something in him went still. All emotion sealed away, leaving no fear, no excitement, nothing left to disrupt him.
[SFX: SWORD DEFLECTING RAPID STRIKES]
He didn’t meet the spear-shadows head-on. Instead, he laid his blade flat along each shaft as it came, finding the same soft point every time—like striking a snake just behind the head—and let his sword turn each strike aside before it could land.
Gasps rippled through the crowd. “He hasn’t dodged once!” someone shouted. “How is he still standing?!”
Slade felt a surge of panic. *How is he doing this?*
Zane gave him no time to think. He was a whirlwind of motion, the essence of his Gods-Intimidating Strike an overwhelming pressure that gave Slade no room to breathe.
[SFX: METAL CLASHING REPEATEDLY]
Zane battered aside the spear-shadows, relentlessly closing the distance until he was right on top of Slade. 
“Time to die, Slade,” he said, his voice low and cold.
[SFX: SPEAR GLOWING WITH ENERGY]
The Saint-Killing Spear began to glow again as Slade tried to unleash his ultimate attack. 
“Twin Killing Intent!”
But this time, Zane was ready. 
[SFX: METAL CLANG]
[SFX: SWORD STRIKING SPEAR SHAFT]
He drove his sword, the Thundergust, down onto the upper shaft of Slade’s spear. The unstoppable attack was choked off before it could even form.
As an Ace Talent, Slade’s reflexes were lightning-fast. 
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS RETREATING]
The instant his spear was pinned, he stumbled back, retreating in a heartbeat. 
[SFX: SPEAR SPINNING]
As Slade moved, the long spear in his grip seemed to shorten, spinning around for a new attack. The moment it was free, he lunged from an unexpected angle.
“Cycle Stab!”
It was a desperate move, a basic spear technique, not one of the Saint-Killing Spear’s special abilities. But Zane had seen it coming. He’d already shifted his body half a foot to the left, anticipating the exact move. 
[SFX: SPEAR GRAZING ARMOR]
Slade’s spearpoint grazed his shoulder, missing its mark.
In a fight between masters, one wrong move is all it takes.
And Zane was always three steps ahead. 
Now that he’d forced an opening, he stuck to Slade like a shadow, giving him no chance to recover.
