Ep 880 - Thunder and Metal

Word count: 1130

[AMBIENT: THUNDER RUMBLING]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

The Celestial Tribulation was a disaster sent from the heavens, its lightning dragon a creature that served no master. 

But Zane, having just mastered the fourth level of the Thunder Law, was attempting to do the impossible: refine it.

For any warrior who reached the Life-and-Death Realm, these trials were a necessary evil—a series of life-or-death crucibles that forged them into something stronger. Each one was a Lesser Celestial Tribulation, pushing them past their limits.

[SFX: ELECTRIC CRACKLING]

Lightning still crackled in Zane’s eyes. He had absorbed the entire lightning dragon, and raw electricity surged through his body. It was only his precise control over the Law Power that allowed him to process the overwhelming energy without being torn apart from the inside.

The fourth layer of the Thunder Law wasn’t just destruction. Every Law contained its opposite — Fire held rebirth and nirvana. Thunder held life. Spring thunder shook the earth, waking hibernating things, setting the world moving again. The fourth layer was called Shape — the beginning of life through destruction.

[SFX: SKIN CRACKING]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CHOIR]

The power of thunder spread through Zane’s body, differentiating, pouring into each of his cells. The charred skin on his surface began to crack. Then, slowly, it peeled away — layer by layer, falling off in pieces, revealing bright, new flesh beneath. By the time it was done, his body looked as though it had just been born.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

The masters from the Soul Sea Realm muttered among themselves, a mix of awe and envy on their faces.

“He actually absorbed the whole Thunder Tribulation. What kind of monster is he?”

“It’s not unheard of. Lester did the same thing when he broke through to the Soul Sea Realm.”

“That was different. The tribulation Lester faced was only at the second level. That lightning dragon contained the power of the fourth-level Thunder Law.”

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

The tribulation hadn’t just failed to harm Zane; it had made him stronger.

“The Thunder Tribulation was just the first wave, and it was that terrifying,” War King York complained. “What in the hell is coming next?”

[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]

[AMBIENT: WIND WHOOSH]

As if in answer, the churning black clouds above began to tear apart, shredded layer by layer until sunlight finally broke through the enormous rift. In minutes, the storm that had blanketed the entire continent vanished, leaving the sky bright and clear.

Several of the Soul Sea masters who had been battling the resulting tsunami flew back toward The Feather Emperor’s Island.

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: CELESTIAL CHOIR]

Just then, a golden ray of light blazed across the horizon, carrying more than ten figures within it. At their lead was a white-haired priest in elegant robes—the very same man who had overseen the competition by the West Dragon River. He was a human warrior from the upper world.

“Master Priest!” the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom called out, his greeting laced with respect.

“Master Priest,” War King Wind said, flying up to meet them as the other Soul Sea warriors bowed. “The tribulation… did it draw you here?”

The White-Haired Priest landed on the pyramid with his followers, his gaze sweeping over the crowd. “Who is undergoing this trial?” he demanded, his voice sharp. “The power is staggering.”

Scott stepped forward. “Master Priest — it is Zane, a saint-level genius of the Harlen Divine Kingdom.”

The White-Haired Priest went still. His gaze found Zane — sitting cross-legged, skin just beginning to peel away, lightning burns across every inch of him. A Spirit-Tribulation warrior.

“He’s in the middle stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm,” the White-Haired Priest said. The disbelief was plain on his face. “How has he triggered a tribulation? How has he triggered one this powerful?”

None of the Soul Sea masters could answer him.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

The White-Haired Priest had noticed Zane before — at the West Dragon River. By his assessment, Zane was talented enough to be a key training target in a second-class Holy Land. Impressive for the lower world. Unremarkable in the context of the upper world’s tens of thousands of realms and tens of millions of Holy Lands. 

Unlike Vesper, who could calculate Zane’s fate directly, the White-Haired Priest could only judge by what he observed. And what he had observed before today had not prepared him for this.

His expression shifted. He would stay and watch. If the tribulation was this powerful, there was a chance — however small — that lotus flowers would bloom when it was over. That was the only thing that truly measured a warrior’s potential. He wanted to see how many Zane could produce.

Behind him, the Holy Maiden of the York Divine Kingdom stayed quiet. Her role included communicating with the upper world — she knew more about what lay beyond than any of the Soul Sea masters present. She had not expected it to be Zane undergoing this tribulation. Her expression was difficult to read.

Zane, however, wasn’t paying attention to the newcomers. Will there be a second wave? he wondered, tilting his head toward the sky.

He knew that for ordinary warriors, the Thunder Tribulation was the end of it. But the truly gifted were tested with multiple waves, each one magnifying their power. Even saint-level geniuses often faced more than one, so for him, another seemed almost certain.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Sure enough, a faint golden shimmer appeared in the clear sky. The light intensified, coalescing into the sharp, distinct forms of golden swords.

[SFX: METALLIC RESONANCE]

“That’s the Metal Tribulation!” one of the Soul Sea warriors cried out, his voice tight with fear. He’d faced one before. “Right after the thunder… this one will be even worse.”

The White-Haired Priest, however, shot the man a look of utter disgust. He couldn’t hide his contempt for the ignorance of these divine kingdom masters. “It’s just a Metal Tribulation,” he said flatly. “What’s so surprising about it?”

To him, the trials of thunder, metal, wind, or water meant little. Only the manifestation of the lotus flower could determine a warrior’s true potential. Zane’s tribulation might be terrifying, but without that lotus flower, it was nothing more than a flashy sideshow.

These were the Five Elements Tribulations — thunder, metal, wind, water, and their kin. Fearsome to those who had never left the lower world. Meaningless as a measure of true potential.

[AMBIENT: RUMBLING SKY]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL CRESCENDO]

Up above, the golden swords multiplied. They grew from a thousand to ten thousand, then a hundred thousand, until a million blades filled the heavens. 

They swarmed together until they blotted out the sun, forming a single, shimmering cloud of death that hung over the world.
