Ep 29 - Ethan Grant

[AMBIENT: BUSY CITY]
Zane kept his eyes closed and nodded.
When he opened them a few seconds later, a young man with a kind face stood before him, looking about eighteen or nineteen.
The moment their eyes met, the young man offered a slight smile. "My second time," he said.
"What's the Entrance Exam?" Zane inquired, drawn to the stranger's approachable demeanor. Something about the young man's open expression invited confidence.
Though Zane had heard countless tales of the Skyreach Sect's notorious selectivity, the specifics of their admission process remained a mystery to him. Coming from outside The Capital left him at a disadvantage; his knowledge was limited to rumors of rigorous testing that eliminated all but the most exceptional candidates.
[MUSIC: INSPIRING WONDER]
The young man's face brightened with enthusiasm. "The Skyreach Sect stands unrivaled—their crafting instructors are simply unmatched anywhere in the world. Everyone dreams of studying there," he explained, gesturing animatedly with his hands. "Given the overwhelming number of applicants, they've established a multi-tiered selection process. Unless you bear the privilege of Great House status, you must first conquer the Entrance Exam before even attempting the Academy Trials."
"I appreciate the information," Zane replied, curiosity piquing his interest further. "If you wouldn't mind sharing, what does the exam entail?" The legendary difficulty of gaining admission to Skyreach had always intrigued him. Now, hearing about these elaborate hurdles, Zane couldn't suppress the bitter thought of how Perrin had circumvented them all. His former acquaintance hadn't merely gained entry—he'd secured the coveted position of Core Apprentice immediately, a fact that still gnawed at Zane's conscience.
A light laugh escaped the young man's lips. "It's essentially an extensive endurance challenge," he revealed, leaning closer as if sharing a secret. "Participation requires at least Bone Refining Level cultivation. Those at Core Strength or Marrow Refining Levels may participate as well, but face proportionally greater challenges. For instance, while Bone Refining cultivators must complete a specific distance, Core Strength participants must cover significantly more ground."
[MUSIC: MOMENT OF REALIZATION]
Just a race? The simplicity stunned Zane. He blinked in momentary disbelief, the corners of his mouth twitching upward. Perhaps fortune had finally smiled upon him.
After all, anyone could run. The surge of power from reaching Core Strength Level had transformed Zane's endurance, enabling him to sustain a pace for hours that would have exhausted his former self. His muscles now possessed a resilience that made long distances seem trivial rather than daunting.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL THEME]
Relief washed over Zane upon learning the exam's nature. He relaxed his tense shoulders and turned toward his newfound acquaintance, engaging in conversation to whittle away the tedious wait.
The young man's animated expressions and genuine enthusiasm proved surprisingly refreshing after months of isolation.
"I'm Ethan Grant," the young man offered with an easy smile that reached his eyes. "My family holds minor standing here in The Capital—nothing impressive, but we've been here for generations."
Ethan explained how the Sect conducted these examinations monthly, providing regular opportunities for aspiring cultivators.
His first attempt had ended in disappointment, leaving him with valuable experience but wounded pride. Now he approached his second chance with tempered expectations and improved preparation.
[AMBIENT: BUSY COURTYARD]
[MUSIC: GENTLE CONVERSATION]
The queue inched forward steadily as they conversed, the afternoon sun casting long shadows across the courtyard. Before either realized how much time had passed, Ethan reached the registration table, with Zane merely a step behind him. Their conversation had transformed the monotonous wait into something almost pleasant—a rarity in Zane's recent experience.
After Zane signed in, the receptionist handed him a Crystal Talisman etched with his ID.
As he carefully tucked it away, Ethan spoke up.
"Hey, Zane, make sure you get some serious sleep tonight," Ethan advised, his expression betraying genuine concern. "Running sounds deceptively simple, but the Sect's course challenges even the most prepared candidates. They've designed it to break spirits as much as test bodies. We'll receive the complete details the day after tomorrow."
Ethan hesitated momentarily, studying Zane's face. "You're not from The Capital, are you? Something about your mannerisms gives it away. If you need accommodation, my family's home has plenty of space—you could stay with us rather than waste money on overpriced inns."
The offer resonated with sincerity; Ethan's open demeanor suggested a person unaccustomed to deception. Nevertheless, Zane declined with a polite smile that didn't quite reach his eyes.
His two years in servitude had carved harsh lessons into his soul about humanity's capacity for cruelty—that generosity invariably carried hidden costs. While not completely cynical toward everyone he encountered, Zane guarded his trust like a precious resource, dispensing it only after careful consideration.
[SFX: IMPOSING GATE OPENING]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: CEREMONIAL THEME]
Just as their conversation concluded, a distinguished group emerged through the Skyreach Sect's imposing main gate. Several figures glided forward in elegant Shadow Cloaks that seemed to absorb light, while the majority wore pristine White Robes that gleamed in the afternoon sun.
Noticing Zane's attentive gaze, Ethan leaned closer, lowering his voice conspiratorially. "The ones draped in White Robes are Outer Disciples," he explained, excitement coloring his words. "Should we successfully navigate both the exam and subsequent Academy Trials, we'll earn the privilege of donning those garments ourselves."
"What significance do the Shadow Cloaks hold?" Zane inquired, studying the commanding presence of those particular disciples.
"Shadow Cloaks?" Ethan's eyes widened with reverence. "Those mark the Core Apprentices! Everything about their position—the status, the monthly allocation of Energy Capsules—vastly surpasses what Outer Disciples receive." His voice softened with yearning as he added, "My father's most cherished ambition is for me to ascend to Core Apprentice rank and wear one of those magnificent cloaks someday."
Zane inclined his head, memories cascading through his mind of that fateful day when Owen had triumphantly announced Perrin's acceptance as a Core Apprentice. The news had stung like salt in an open wound, a bitter reminder of how quickly fortunes could shift.
How did he manage such a feat? Zane wondered, his brow furrowing slightly as he contemplated the possibilities. Was it the Aether Crystal that had given Perrin the advantage?
The rare artifact had been coveted by many, its properties legendary among crafters. Could it truly have accelerated his Crafting Skills to such extraordinary levels in so short a time? The thought left a hollow feeling in Zane's chest, a mixture of suspicion and reluctant admiration.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Absorbed in his thoughts, Zane failed to notice the approaching contingent of Shadow-Cloaked disciples until one halted abruptly, his gaze fixed directly on Zane with unmistakable recognition.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
"Zane! I'll be damned," the figure called out, his voice dripping with mockery. "So the rumors of your demise were greatly exaggerated. You actually believe you can join the Skyreach Sect? The sheer audacity of you appearing here is absolutely priceless!"
Zane's eyes narrowed at the familiar voice calling his name. As the speaker's features came into focus through the crowd, a cold wave of revulsion washed over him, settling like a stone in his stomach. His jaw clenched involuntarily, and his fingers curled into a tight fist at his side.
Speak of the devil. The very person who had occupied his thoughts moments ago now stood before him in the flesh, wearing that same smug expression Zane remembered all too well.
