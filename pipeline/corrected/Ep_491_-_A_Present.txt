[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS DRUMS]

The Soul-Killing Poisoned Blood was a forbidden technique from Bloodwood Cliff—a terrifying art that could unlock a fighter's full potential, but at a brutal cost: it inflicted devastating injuries on its user.

No sane person would ever dream of using it unless they were already staring death in the face.

But it was a risk Brody had to take. Lady Darkmere had ordered him to bring Zane down, and failure was not an option. He could only hope his leader would reward him with something that could help him break through to a higher refining level.

[SFX: CRACKLING FLAMES]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

Once the blood runes marked his skin, Brody’s eyes burned with bloodlust as he stared up at Zane. The moment the curtain of light flickered and died, hot, scarlet flames erupted from his hand.

"I've mastered a third of the Fire Law," Brody snarled. "If you know what's good for you, you'll just stand there and let my blood flames burn you to ash." As he spoke, the flames in his hand swelled, hungry for the kill.

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMURS]

"Brody mastered the Fire Law!" someone shouted. "What's with this tournament? Law Power is supposed to be incredibly difficult."

"Brody's a top Bloodwood Cliff disciple—he's qualified," another replied. "Still, this year's competition is shocking. In the past, only the top three achieved this."

"Back in my day, only Spirit-Tribulation Realm fighters could master Law Power. The talent now is insane."

[SFX: WHOOSH OF PURE FIRE] 

The scarlet blaze burned bright, but Zane didn't even flinch. "Blood flames? You want to burn me?" A mocking smile touched his lips as he waved a hand, a wisp of pure, unadulterated fire appearing over his palm.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]

When Tia saw Zane conjure fire, her eyes widened. She realized she knew nothing about her student.

She'd been amazed by his talent during the Cloud Road Trial and invested heavily in his training, certain he'd become a pillar of Cloud Hall. Then came his rune mastery—even the chief expert admitted Zane surpassed him. After the Dragon Soar Arena, she discovered he'd mastered Spatial Law.

Zane had just revealed his mastery of the Fire Law's first layer.

Tia's mind raced as understanding dawned. *The upper world; He must have inherited the Fire Law there. Her pulse quickened. How many more secrets did this young man have?

She was right about the inheritance—but she couldn't possibly guess the full truth. During his Baptism by Laws, Zane had actually mastered all nine original laws, a feat so extraordinary that even mentioning it would invite disbelief.

Tia studied him with fresh eyes. The casual way he summoned that flame, the absolute confidence in his stance—this was the bearing of someone who had touched powers beyond her comprehension.

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLE ARENA]

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]

Even though Brody was half-mad with power, he could sense the terrifying purity of the flame on Zane's palm. Still, he had no time to hesitate. Lady Darkmere had marked him with the blood runes; he had no choice but to eliminate Zane.

His face twisted with rage as he bellowed, "Go to Hell!"

[SFX: WEAPON MATERIALIZING]

[SFX: FLAMES WHOOSHING]

Brody shot forward, a brown scimitar materializing in his hand. In a split second, scarlet blood flames engulfed the entire blade.

He moved with shocking speed. In the blink of an eye, he was already above Zane, bringing the scimitar down in a deadly arc.

[SFX: POWERFUL SLASH]

[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]

"Blood Flame Hacking!"

Blazing flames licked the scimitar's blade, amplifying the force of his attack. Boosted by the blood rune, Brody's every move was incredibly powerful.

But just as the blade swung down, Zane was gone, having nimbly dodged the strike. Before Brody could even react, Zane shot his own flame forward, striking him square in the chest with his palm.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT]

Brody stumbled back several feet but managed to keep his balance. Scrambling for his footing, he swung his flaming scimitar at the circle of fire now surrounding Zane. As soon as his blood flames collided with Zane's pure fire, they began to consume each other.

Since Zane had never practiced any fire-based attunement techniques, he couldn't fully unleash the power of the flame in his hand.

But an understanding of Law Power was more important than the method of channeling it. The flame Brody had unleashed looked immense and powerful, but it soon became clear that he was at a disadvantage.

[SFX: FLAMES CRACKLING]

Zane didn't need mastery over fire to win this exchange. He simply poured more energy into his flame, feeding it until his fire overwhelmed Brody's blood flames entirely. The crimson fire sputtered and died, leaving Brody's scimitar bare.

[SFX: RUNNING IMPACT]

[SFX: CRASH]

Before Brody could react, Zane closed the distance and struck him squarely in the chest with his free hand. The impact sent Brody crashing to the ground, his scimitar clattering away. He tried to rise but collapsed, unconscious.

[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT GROUNDS]

[MUSIC: CALM TRANSITION]

With the fight over, Zane pushed the thought aside and headed back to the area for the independent fighters.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]

[MUSIC: ANTICIPATION BUILDING]

After several rounds, the gap between independent fighters and sect disciples became obvious. Two independents, however, captured attention: Owen, at the ultimate level of the Soul Realm, and Trevor Beaumont.

Both had defeated talented disciples from Mysterious Luna Hall and Black Mountain Order, respectively. While most independents couldn't qualify for even lower-ranked sects, these two had clawed their way to the Inner-Core Realm through sheer grit. The crowd recognized them as blessed by Destiny.

The Third-level sect disciples were the weakest remaining, unable to challenge top independents or fourth-level sect elites.

After the thirty-second round, the old man stepped forward to announce the next match.

[MUSIC: ANTICIPATION BUILDING]

"Benson of the Cloud Hall will battle Eden Caldwell, an independent fighter."

[SFX: CROWD CHEERING]

"Eden Caldwell! The son of the greatest fighter in The Heartlands! Now this is going to be interesting!" someone in the audience yelled.

"It's a safe bet that Eden Caldwell and Brent from the Ethereal Spirit sect are the best fighters in this tournament. I bet one of them takes the championship, and the other gets second place!"

"I don't know. Benson is an amazing fighter, too. He's from the Cloud Hall, which is ranked first among the fourth-level sects. I think he'll be a tough opponent."

"Eden Caldwell?" Zane murmured, his gaze shifting to Maddox in the audience. He watched the young man beside him stand and walk toward the arena.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

As the fighter took center stage, Zane narrowed his eyes. So that's Maddox's son, he thought, already losing interest.

Meanwhile, on the stage, Eden looked down at a block of glowing, mysterious ice that had been presented to him. It was clearly very valuable, but he looked at it coolly. "I have no interest in receiving such a cheap present."

He reached out with his index finger, pointed at the ice, and released a whisper of Vitality.

[SFX: ICE CRACKING]

[SFX: GASPS OF SHOCK]

Gasps erupted as spiderweb cracks spread across the Mysterious Ice before it crumbled to dust.

"He destroyed it!"

"What a waste—even for the son of The Heartlands' strongest cultivator."

"I would've given anything for that treasure!"

Dismay rippled through the crowd, but Eden ignored their protests. Once the ice had disintegrated completely, he swept the fragments aside, revealing a bright, glowing core.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

[SFX: MAGICAL GLOW]

The icy debris scattered around Eden obscured the crowd's view, leaving them straining to glimpse what lay at the center of the destruction.

Zane leaned forward, his pulse quickening. The glow pulsed with an otherworldly radiance, casting dancing shadows across Eden's impassive face.

What could that glowing core possibly be? And why would Eden sacrifice something so valuable to reach it?
