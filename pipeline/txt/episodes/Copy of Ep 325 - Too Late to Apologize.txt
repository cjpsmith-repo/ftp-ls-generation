Ep 325 - Too Late to Apologize
Word count: 1181

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
Tristan’s eyes widened in sudden recognition. That face—those unmistakable features—belonged to Zane Lowell, a ghost returned from the shadows.
[MUSIC: SOMBER FLASHBACK]
Two catastrophes had rocked House Lowell the previous year.
The first struck like lightning when Zane was violently kidnapped by the notorious master weaponsmith known throughout the Eastern Territories as "Evil Jace." The second calamity followed when Perrin—the family’s golden boy and brightest hope—was mysteriously crippled and unceremoniously kicked out of the prestigious Skyreach Sect, his promising future extinguished.
No one knew if Zane was alive or dead, or where Evil Jace had taken him.
Equally perplexing was the identity of whoever had destroyed Perrin’s cultivation abilities, leaving him a hollow shell of his former self.
House Lowell, ever conscious of its reputation, suppressed both scandals with ruthless efficiency.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]
At first, everyone assumed Zane was dead. Evil Jace’s notorious cruelty left little doubt; a kid like Zane wouldn’t have stood a chance with him.
But as time wore on, whispers began to ripple throughout House Lowell.
While many still believed he was dead, others spoke of an unbelievable alternative. They claimed that Zane had not only survived but flourished, securing a coveted position as a disciple at the Skyreach Sect, that hallowed institution every cultivator aspired to join.
Even though the stories were packed with surprisingly specific details, most of the Lowells didn't buy it. After all, they'd seen Zane get dragged off by a man with a reputation for pure, sadistic evil. The family elders shook their heads, their faces firm with dismissive certainty. Besides, getting into the Skyreach Sect was an almost insurmountable challenge, not some walk in the park.
Perrin, after years of relentless, punishing training, was the only Lowell offspring who had ever reached a high enough level to be accepted. The family collectively scoffed at the notion that Zane—whom they had always regarded as mediocre at best—could possibly reach such heights. They wrote off these persistent rumors as fantasy, a comforting fiction crafted by those unwilling to accept the harsh reality of his demise.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
Just as the Lowell family reluctantly began to accept Zane’s disappearance, a second catastrophe struck with merciless timing.
Perrin—their sole hope for family glory—returned home in utter disgrace. His cultivation powers had been forcibly stripped away, leaving him a hollow shell of his former self.
The Skyreach Sect had not merely dismissed him; they had systematically dismantled everything that defined his identity as a cultivator. His eyes, once bright with ambition, now carried the vacant stare of someone who had glimpsed greatness only to have it violently torn away. The family elders watched in horrified silence as their carefully constructed dreams crumbled before them, unable to comprehend how their perfect heir could fall so precipitously from grace.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
The hit to House Lowell's prestige was devastating.
Every family in Castermere County poured resources into training their kids for a shot at the Skyreach Sect. They weren't aiming for the stars; they just hoped their kids could become an inner disciple and reach the Adept Realm. Even that was a massive achievement.
Years ago, Milo Lowell had only reached the Adept Realm, and it was still a huge deal. An Adept was powerful enough to be the backbone of a family. Having even one outer disciple in the Sect meant other families wouldn’t dare mess with you, terrified that the disciple might one day come back powerful enough to settle old scores.
Perrin hadn’t just reached the Adept Realm; he'd started cozying up to a powerful family, House Warrick. Everyone thought he had a limitless future, and the Lowells had pinned all their hopes on him.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
Grey sized Zane up, his scrutinizing gaze traveling from Zane’s unfamiliar attire to his confident stance. *How does he know my name? And what’s this about me forgetting him? Who is this guy?*
"Stop them!" Grey snapped.
His men charged down the steps.
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]
They never reached him. Small black points materialized in the air ahead of them — and in the same instant, drove through each man's chest. No warning. No sound from Zane or Elara. The thugs stumbled, looked down at the holes in their bodies with expressions of pure confusion, and dropped.
[SFX: BODIES HITTING GROUND]
Grey stared at the corpses. He couldn't process what he'd just seen. Those two hadn't moved.
Wisps of dark energy rose from the fallen men, drifting toward Elara. Zane stepped slightly ahead of her and drew them in first.
[SFX: DARK ENERGY ABSORPTION]
This was what the path of Malice demanded — and he had chosen it. He let it settle into him without resistance.
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
Gradually, the blood drained from Grey’s face, leaving him ashen and trembling. Though the young man before him exuded a presence entirely transformed—colder, harder, with an aura of lethal capability—that face remained unmistakable, etched into Grey’s memory from years of serving the Lowell household.
[MUSIC: REVELATION THEME]
"You... you’re Zane!" Grey’s voice emerged as a choked whisper, his eyes widening with disbelief and dawning horror. His hands began to tremble at his sides, and sweat beaded on his forehead. How is he back? And how has he become this powerful? The weak boy I remember couldn’t even look me in the eye. "Zane?" The name hung in the air, charged with tension and unspoken history.
The name made the servant sweeping the floor freeze mid-stroke. He stared, just as stunned as Grey, at the man's sudden appearance. He'd worked at the manor for decades; he knew the Lowell family's history inside and out. Looking closer, he saw it was true. It was really him.
And the young woman behind him... she looked familiar, too. She had the eyes of Milo Lowell, who'd passed years ago. Wait a second. That must be Elara! The old servant never thought he'd see the two siblings again. He was so overcome with emotion that his hands began to shake. If he wasn’t gripping his broom so tightly, it would have clattered to the floor.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
"Since you remember who I am," Zane said, his voice flat and cold, "then you know why you're going to die today."
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
With that, he slowly drew a sword from his Storage Ring.
"I— Please! Don't kill me!" Grey stammered, stumbling backward.
"Please, Zane, I know I was wrong! I'm sorry! Just give me a chance! I can—"
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
Before Grey could finish his plea, a flash of icy light split the air.
[SFX: BLOOD DRIPPING]
A thin red line blossomed on his neck as blood began to well.
"I... I..." Grey clutched at his throat, his hands instantly slick with his own blood. He staggered, then collapsed, a choked gurgle escaping his lips.
His windpipe was severed. All he could do was stare up at Zane, his eyes pleading for mercy.
Zane stepped closer, looking down at Grey's pudgy, terrified face.
"Sorry is useless," he said calmly. "If it wasn't, what would be the point of a sword?"
