Ep 157 - The Puppetmaster
Word Count: 1332
[AMBIENT: BATTLE AFTERMATH]
[MUSIC: SOMBER VICTORY]
Normally, defeating a Soul Realm master would have been impossible. Zane was only a half-step into the Adept Stage---nowhere near that level. In a fight to the death, he would have lost, plain and simple, if Seymour hadn’t already been badly wounded.
Even with that advantage, it was a brutal win. No matter how bad it got, he'd managed to hang on and see it through.
[SFX: LOW GRINDING STONE]
Just then, a low grinding echoed from The Runic Cipher. Zane looked up to see the runic statues slowly sliding away from the characters on the floor, returning to their original positions.
The statues seemed inert, so he slumped to the ground, catching his breath. After a few minutes, he pushed himself back to his feet.
His eyes swept over the bodies of the Soul Realm masters, and he couldn’t help but sigh. If they'd just believed him, none of this would have happened.
Ah, what’s done is done, he thought with another sigh. In a high-stakes game like this, they were never going to trust me anyway. He knelt and began prying the weapons from their lifeless hands. They were valuable, powerful items: a handful of low-level Spiritual Weapons, a few mid-level ones, and even a single top-grade Spiritual Weapon.
They were dead, so the spoils were his.
He carefully gathered all the weapons and valuables, stashing them away in his Storage Ring. Before turning to the runic statues, he paused, observing a moment of silence over the bodies of his rivals.
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]
After a few moments, he moved to the runic statues and began shifting them according to the method in Chase’s Record.
“Three for Heaven,” Zane murmured, pushing three statues to the corresponding symbol.
“Six for Earth...” he continued, sliding another six into place.
“For the Water is...”
“For the Fire is...”
“The last one goes...”
One by one, he slid each statue to its proper place, his voice a low whisper reciting the instructions.
[SFX: MECHANICAL CLICK]
[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]
When he finally pushed the last statue into position, the faint hum from The Runic Cipher carved into the ground died out, and the whole mechanism went still.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
A figure stepped forward from the shadows beyond the statues---a tall, slender puppet with jointed wooden limbs and glass eyes that shimmered with an unsettling human warmth. This was Saul, the ancient guardian of the Elysian Keep, a puppet given life by a human soul and bound to this place for thousands of years.
Saul just smiled sadly and sighed. “If you were trapped in one place, day after day, with no one to talk to, you’d stop talking eventually. Maybe even forget how. I've been alone for thousands of years, and I’ll be alone for thousands more. After all that time, would you still want to live forever? Or would death start to look like a welcome relief, an end to the loneliness?”
Zane fell silent. Saul had a point. An eternity of solitude... it would feel more like a curse than a gift. Death might actually be a mercy. Still, Zane was human; he understood loneliness. But Saul was a puppet.
He asked, “Saul, you’re a puppet. How can you feel lonely?”
Saul just shook his head. “My body may be a puppet, but my soul was taken from a human,” he answered. “So, in a way, I am human. I have human feelings because I have a human soul.”
Using a human soul to create a puppet...
The concept was so alien Zane could barely wrap his head around it. A technique that incredible was hard to believe.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
Seeing Zane’s shocked expression, Saul seemed unfazed, as if he was used to it. “Anyway,” he continued, “after three thousand years, I finally have company again. You’re the fourth person to make it this far.”
“The fourth?” Zane asked. Despite his shock, he’d latched onto the key part of Saul’s sentence. “So, others have been here before? In this Elysian Keep?”
Saul nodded. “Of course. Others have come and solved The Runic Cipher. But they all failed the test and died. So, I hope you succeed. I don’t want to wait any longer. I’ve had enough.”
“All failed? They all died? What does that mean?” Zane asked, his brow furrowing. A knot of dread tightened in his stomach.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
“If you want to become the Master of the Elysian Keep, you have to pass the test. And the test, of course, is dangerous. If you fail, you die,” Saul explained slowly.
Zane’s eyes widened, his throat suddenly dry.
“I’ll die if I fail? Can I just... skip the test and leave?” he asked, staring at Saul with a desperate sliver of hope.
[SFX: DARK CHUCKLE]
At this, Saul actually chuckled.
“According to the rules, yes, you can give up. My Master was powerful, but he wouldn’t force his legacy on anyone unwilling. Only someone truly worthy and content could inherit it. However... I don’t want to wait any longer.”
"Great," Zane thought. "So the rules say I can walk, but he won’t let me. He’s so sick of being alone he’s going to force me to take the test, Master’s rules be damned."
Saul noticed the hesitation on Zane’s face and went on, “Don’t worry! To make sure you pass, I’ll do my best to help you. I’ll give you guidance wherever I’m allowed. I’ve waited thousands of years for you to get here. The test is dangerous, yes, but I’ll do everything in my power to help you succeed!”
Realizing he was trapped, Zane sighed.
“All right. I understand. So how do I pass this test? When do I start? Where is it?” Zane asked.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
With a wave of his hand, Saul said, “Come with me!”
They left the pavilion and stepped onto another jade path leading deeper into the mansion. The moment they did, the water churned.
[SFX: WATER EXPLOSION]
An Abyssal Pike launched itself at them. Zane shouted a warning — but Saul had already raised one hand. His fingers stretched, impossibly fast, seventy feet in an instant, and punched straight through the creature. He squeezed once and let the pieces drop into the water.
The other Pikes dove and didn't surface again.
Zane said nothing. Any thought of running died right there.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Saul turned and walked toward the edge of the courtyard, where three other puppets stood motionless. They were crude by comparison, nowhere near as finely crafted as Saul.
When Saul saw Zane staring at them, he explained, “You can pick any one you like. They’re for you.”
Zane raised an eyebrow. “What do you mean?”
[MUSIC: EERIE REVELATION]
“If you fail the test, I’ll save you,” Saul said, gesturing to the puppets. “I’ll take your soul and seal it inside one of them. You’ll get to stay here with me for a long, long time. It’ll be like you never died at all.”
“The souls of the other challengers... they stayed here, too,” he added. “My craftsmanship isn’t as good as my Master’s, though. I can only keep a soul bound to a puppet for about a thousand years. I can’t make them immortal like he made me.”
Now, Zane finally understood. To escape his own loneliness, Saul had created companions. He’d turned the challengers who broke into the Elysian Keep and failed the test into his puppets.
And even though Saul said his work wasn’t as good as his Master’s, the three puppets looked to be in decent shape... because the souls that once inhabited them had faded away centuries ago.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT INTENSIFIES]
The thought of being sealed inside one of those things if he failed sent an icy chill down Zane's spine. He swallowed hard.
A thousand years trapped in a wooden body, slowly going mad from loneliness... it was a fate worse than death.
