Ep 1224 - Guess I Got Lucky

Word count: 1028

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[AMBIENT: CAVE ECHOES WITH COMBAT]
[SFX: METAL CLASHING]
The cave shook with the clang of metal on metal. Seven Ogre fighters were locked in a desperate battle with three Celestial Mice. Zane had already killed three of the creatures when he first got here, but another three had skittered out of the shadows to take their place.
He’d found the cave a little while ago, but he clearly wasn’t the first. Looked like some other group had stumbled on it, and one of them must have gone to get backup after realizing he couldn’t handle the Celestial Mice alone.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
Now, only seven of them were left, and they could only watch as glowing orbs of Illusion Points condensed where their comrades had fallen. The bodies of the dead fighters were already fading away, their points just hanging there in the air. 
Zane counted the orbs—more than ten of them. The Celestial Mice must have killed at least ten fighters, sending them all back to their sanctuaries to revive.
Everyone knew you could absorb another fighter’s points when you defeated them, but the mice didn’t seem to care. That meant all those points—half of what each dead fighter had—were just floating there, free for the taking. He could get them without lifting a finger. 
No way was he letting an opportunity like this slip by.
He shot toward the nearest orb. 
[SFX: ENERGY ABSORPTION]
He just had to touch it, and the glowing yellow points flowed right into him.
The seven Ogre fighters saw what he was doing.
[SFX: ANGRY SHOUTS]
“Hey! What the hell are you doing?”
“You got a death wish, kid?”
“Those are our brothers’ points! We’ll hunt you down for this!”
Their angry shouts echoed through the cave, but they were too busy fending off the Celestial Mice to do anything about it. 
[SFX: ARMOR TEARING]
The mice were unnaturally fast, darting in to bite with razor-sharp front teeth that might as well have been Divine-Class weapons. They chewed right through armor, and even the Ogres’ thick skin wasn’t enough to stop them from getting wounds so deep you could see bone.
The Ogres knew there was treasure deeper in the cave, which was why they’d teamed up in the first place, but these mice were just too damn strong. 
[SFX: ENERGY ABSORPTION WHOOSH]
And to make matters worse, the points from their fallen comrades were now flying straight into Zane’s body.
Six of the seven had had enough. All six abandoned the mice entirely and turned to converge on Zane — and as if drawn by the sudden shift in violence, two of the remaining Celestial Mice broke off from their assault and came at him too, leaving only one still occupied elsewhere.
Zane’s expression didn’t so much as flicker. As far as he was concerned, there was no such thing as “too far” inside the Illusion Battlefield. This was elimination, plain and simple; the weak got cut and the strong stood at the center of the stage. 
[SFX: MIST SWIRLING]
“Eight Smoky Melodies!”
White mist curled off him as the technique took hold, and no matter how vicious the attacks coming at him were, not one of them so much as brushed his sleeve.
“This guy’s movement technique is insane!” one of the Ogres roared, frustration cracking his voice.
“Surround him!”
“You circle around behind him!”
But what happened next only baffled them further. The Celestial Mice were fast — far faster than Zane on his own — and yet the instant one lunged for him, Eight Smoky Melodies turned his whole body into something like drifting smoke. 
To the Ogres watching, it looked for all the world like Zane’s own speed had somehow caught up to the mice’s — he moved too fast for their eyes to properly track, and a target they couldn’t see was a target they couldn’t surround.
[SFX: ENERGY STRIKING GROUND]
Even while weaving through the chaos, Zane didn’t forget to drive a thread of Chaos Energy down into the stone beneath his feet, tracing out a faint, mysterious white halo that widened to take in the whole group.
Zane had used this combination twice before against the Celestial Mice - each creature worth three thousand Illusion Points made the setup worthwhile. This time, however, he’d trapped not only the mice but also the six furious Ogres inside the circle.
[SFX: DISTANT RUMBLE BUILDING]
Before long, a terrifying pressure rolled down out of the sky, and wild, untamed lightning came crashing after it. Everything caught inside that white halo was baptized in the full force of a Heavenly Tribulation.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS EXPLOSION]
[SFX: ANGUISHED ROARS]
A chorus of furious, unwilling roars rang out as all six Ogres dissolved into red points of light, streaking away toward the main city to be revived. In the same instant, the two Celestial Mice caught in the blast burst apart as well, shattering into countless tiny motes of golden light.
The last six Ogres to fall must have been much stronger than the ones who had died earlier, because they dropped a ton more points—about ten thousand each, on average. Adding the points from the three Celestial Mice he’d already killed, Zane’s total shot back up to one hundred thirty thousand.
“Guess I got lucky,” Zane said with a small smile, hovering gently as he looked out at the horizon.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED ADVENTUROUS]
This “supreme world” was just one of fifty that made up the Illusion Battlefield, each one filled with countless sanctuaries. With the Teleportation Token, he didn’t have to go back to that specific one anymore. It was time to go on a rampage and rack up as many points as he could.
But first, he had to make sure the token actually worked.
He pulled out the white alabaster token and drew a tiny bit of Chaos Energy from the Chaos Sea in his core, channeling it inside. 
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY CHANNELING]
[SFX: FAINT GLOW FLICKERING OUT]
The token gave off a faint white glow… and then immediately died.
Zane frowned. “Wait, is this thing useless?”
A moment later, the token started shaking violently in his hand. 
[SFX: TOKEN VIBRATING VIOLENTLY]
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY ERUPTION]
A powerful force erupted from it, and Zane was instantly swallowed by a blazing white light, so bright it looked like a small sun had just ignited in the middle of the wilderness.
It felt like an eternity before the blinding light finally faded. 
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
When it vanished, Zane was gone with it.
