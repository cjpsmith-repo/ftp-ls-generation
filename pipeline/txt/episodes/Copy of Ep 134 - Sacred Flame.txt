Ep 134 - Sacred Flame
Word Count: 1040
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
 Immobilized within the Glass Ward, Zane found himself utterly vulnerable, unable to shift even his head in the constricted space. Like prey awaiting slaughter, he stood frozen as events unfolded beyond his control.
[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]
 The Netherlord's attack came with terrifying swiftness. Aldrich lunged forward, his predatory eyes gleaming with malice as he launched his assault, clearly intending to eviscerate the helpless youth before him.
Panic flooded Zane's veins as cold realization settled in his gut. The bone spur—deadly and precise—hurtled toward him with unnatural speed, its jagged edge promising certain death. He tracked its approach with widening eyes, his muscles straining uselessly against the invisible prison. A scream built in his throat as the projectile closed the distance, his mind racing through memories of his sister, his father, and all the promises he had yet to fulfill.
“I'm not strong enough to break this Glass Ward,” Zane thought bitterly. “But for Aldrich, it's probably like cracking an egg. This is it. I'm dead.”
[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]
[SFX: CUTTING FLESH]
 Just as the bone spur was about to impale him, a force yanked him up and sideways. The tip of the spur grazed his face, leaving a thin, bloody line.
"Too close," Zane breathed, his heart hammering in his chest. He was grateful, but also completely baffled. Why would Yolande Shaw save him? He was sure she couldn't care less if he lived or died. But if she did care, then why did she drag him into this mess in the first place? He couldn't make any sense of her.
The truth was, Yolande Shaw wasn't that complicated. She didn't overthink things; she had one simple goal. In her eyes, Zane was guilty, plain and simple. He should be handed over to the military office for trial. But she was worried they might let him off the hook, so she’d decided to handle it herself.
Her logic was a little skewed, but her voice was firm with determination. "You're right. Even if you run, I can catch you anytime. Get ready, I'm letting you out!"
[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]
 But before Yolande could free him, Aldrich launched another deadly attack. The Netherlord spread his arms, and a black ball of energy—his Demon Core—began to form in front of him.
[AMBIENT: SUPERNATURAL ENERGY]
[MUSIC: DARK MYSTICAL]
 The moment the Demon Core appeared, it started releasing Phantoms. Instead of scattering, they were pulled by some invisible force, flowing like water into a mold until they took the shape of Glyphs.
The Glyphs looked nothing like human writing, each one seeming to pulse with its own dark meaning. Within moments, hundreds of them were slowly spinning around the Demon Core.
 "Yolande, watch out!" Axel shouted from nearby. "That's the Sacred Flame of the Netherlord Aldrich!"
Axel identified the Glyphs immediately, his face paling with recognition. Having confronted Aldrich before—and suffered the horror of possession by him—a wave of cold dread washed over his being. He lunged forward, desperate to alert her to the impending danger.
"Yolande, get away!" he shouted, voice cracking with urgency. "It's Hellfire—Aldrich's signature technique!"
The warning caught in his throat as he realized what would happen. Within the Hellfire's influence, the Netherlord's abilities would amplify dramatically, his strength and speed escalating to unfathomable levels.
Axel's desperate cry echoed uselessly across the battlefield. The warning had come too late.
[SFX: VIOLENT ERUPTION]
[AMBIENT: DARK SUPERNATURAL] 
 The Demon Core cradled in the Netherlord's skeletal hands shuddered violently before erupting, unleashing a torrent of black mist that flooded the area with terrifying speed. The darkness billowed outward like a living entity, hungry and relentless.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS HUM]
 A low, ominous hum permeated the air as Yolande attempted evasion, her movements suddenly appearing sluggish against the supernatural speed of the encroaching darkness. The mist, voracious and predatory, engulfed her completely within seconds, her silhouette vanishing into its murky depths.
[SFX: FALLING BODY]
Zane plummeted through the air, suddenly released from Yolande's ward with no warning. His stomach lurched with the free fall as he braced for impact with the ground below. Before he could crash against the earth, tendrils of the black mist snaked around his limbs, enveloping him in their suffocating embrace.
"Wait, what is this stuff?" he gasped, his eyes widening with alarm as the darkness closed in around him.
[SFX: SUFFOCATING DARKNESS]
 He could feel the mist's thickness, heavy and suffocating, like he’d sunk deep into a swamp. When he opened his eyes, all he saw was darkness; the mist had swallowed the light. Before he could adjust, a searing pain erupted across his skin.
*What is this mist? It's eating me alive!*
[AMBIENT: DARK SUPERNATURAL PRESENCE]
 His body was a Soul Armament. It was impervious to any normal weapon, even the deadliest poisons from the Eastern Territory.
[SFX: CORROSIVE HISSING]
 But this black mist was completely different. It felt like millions of invisible ants gnawing at him, chewing through his skin bit by bit, despite the durability of his Soul Armament.
What could he do? If he couldn't find a way to stop it, the mist would devour him completely, until nothing was left. He was already at his limit. The Netherlord's power was insane.
But then, he felt it—an invisible barrier. Hope surged through him. At least this wasn't like Yolande Shaw's enchantment, where his punches did nothing. He felt a slight give. Maybe, just maybe, he could break this one.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACTS]
 He hammered his fists against it, again and again.
After three solid punches, the barrier rippled like the surface of a pond. The waves spread out, shook violently for a moment... and then went still. It was completely unharmed.
“This is not good,” Zane thought, his hope draining away. “My regular strength isn't enough to break through this thing. Maybe I should try using the Nether Essence.”
[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]
 Zane clenched his fist and raised it over his head. The Nether Essence began to pool in his fist, and he focused all of it, intending to shatter the barrier.
But to his utter shock, the Nether Essence barely even scratched it. That was impossible. He'd used the Nether Essence countless times, and it had never failed him before.
So why was it failing now? It just didn't make any sense.
