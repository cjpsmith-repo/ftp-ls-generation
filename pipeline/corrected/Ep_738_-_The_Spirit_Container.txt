Ep 738 - The Spirit Container
Word count: 1146
[AMBIENT: WAR CAMP]
[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
The war against the Corrupted Divine Kingdom had become a brutal numbers game. The elite warriors of Oracle City, masters of the Inner-Core and Spirit-Tribulation Realms, were up against an endless tide of low-level fighters. The strategy, cooked up by the Cloud Hall and the World Merchants’ Guild, was simple: small, elite teams would run guerrilla-style raids to slow the invasion.
And for a while, it worked beautifully. The Corrupted Divine Kingdom was still consolidating its forces and couldn’t spare its own top-tier fighters to counter the raids. But lately, the casualty reports had grown grim. Oracle City was losing people—powerful warriors, some in the middle or even late stages of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.
[MUSIC: UPBEAT ADVENTURE]
Zane was on his way to pick up a new mission when he ran right into Finn and his crew. Patrick and Lewis were with him, fresh off a half-month assignment and looking to get back out there. When Zane mentioned he was going it alone, teaming up just felt right.
Lewis, especially, was ecstatic. He hadn't forgotten how Zane handled himself—the kid was a force of nature. They’d all agreed that Zane’s strength was easily on par with a Life-and-Death Realm master. Having that kind of talent on their side wasn’t just a bonus; it meant their odds of making it back alive had just skyrocketed. You could almost see the relief wash over him.
"Alright, with Zane on board, we need a new name!" Lewis declared with a grin. "I'm thinking... the Best Friend Team!"
Finn just gave him a dead-eyed stare. "That's awful."
"Awful? What's wrong with it? Fine," Lewis shot back, his grin widening, "the Lewis Team it is!"
Finn shook his head, looking genuinely disappointed. "That's somehow even worse."
[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]
[SFX: FLYING WHOOSH]
As they flew low over the landscape, Zane slowed and cut in. "How about the Vanguard Team?" he offered.
"Vanguard," Patrick mused, nodding. "I like it."
Lewis, however, looked personally offended. "That's just weird. Nobody even knows what that means, and it's definitely not as cool as my names—"
[SFX: SWORD DRAWN]
He was cut short by the sharp shing of a sword being drawn right behind him. Finn had his blade out. Lewis wisely shut his mouth.
[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Three days later, the newly minted Vanguard Team found themselves fourteen hundred miles east of Oracle City. Their mission: destroy a teleportation array that the Corrupted Divine Kingdom was secretly building in a small city called Melody City.
The location was strategically important, and the array — if completed — would dramatically shorten the enemy's supply lines. The Corrupted Divine Kingdom couldn't move warriors in openly without alerting Oracle City, so the work was being done in secret, with a covert guard.
Melody City was small — fewer than four hundred thousand people. By Heartlands standards, that barely registered. Oracle City counted its population in the hundreds of millions. But size wasn't the point. The point was the location.
They entered without incident. Locals tried to sell them things. They ignored them and fanned out through the streets, Zane extending his perception across the whole city, sweeping for spatial fluctuations that would betray the array's location.
He found nothing.
He swept again. Still nothing. The entire city, and not a trace of the array that was supposed to be there.
"Something's wrong," Zane said.
"I feel it too," Lewis said immediately. He'd been twitchy since they walked through the gate — his instinct for danger was almost embarrassingly reliable.
Finn looked around. The streets had been busy when they entered. Now they were empty.
Then the city walls lit up. A pale blue barrier spread across them, enclosing the entire city in a dome of light. And then the doors began to open — one after another, all down the street — and the people came back out. Their eyes were blank. They moved without purpose, drifting like they'd forgotten how to be alive.
"What is this?" Lewis said quietly.
Zane didn't answer. He looked at Finn. Finn looked back, read his expression, and sat cross-legged on the spot, drew his sword across his knees, and closed his eyes. He began to gather power. Patrick and Lewis took up positions on either side, watching the blank-eyed mortals with their hands on their weapons.
Zane shot into the sky.
[SFX: EERIE CHILD VOICE ECHO]
A sharp, high-pitched voice suddenly echoed around them, clear as a bell. "I trapped another one! A late-stage Spirit-Tribulation warrior, right in my little cage in Melody City!" It sounded like a child, no older than three or four.
Zane's head snapped up.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER MATERIALIZING]
A shimmering blue barrier had materialized out of thin air, trapping them. "Who's there?" he demanded.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]
A moment later, a faint, human-shaped figure began to coalesce atop the barrier. It was translucent, like heat haze, and Zane knew instantly he wasn't looking at a person, but a soul—or some kind of projection of one. He’d never seen a skill like it on the main continent. The only thing that came close was when Lavender had projected her own soul to speak with the Queen of Fate in the upper world, but that was a completely different animal. This felt… predatory.
"Who are you?" Zane repeated, his voice sharp. "Answer me!"
[AMBIENT: EERIE SUPERNATURAL ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
The hazy figure solidified, its form resolving into that of a small boy. He had the soft skin and cherubic face of a toddler, but the innocence stopped there. His eyes held a chilling, bottomless cruelty.
[SFX: CHILLING CHILD VOICE ECHO]
"You don't need to know my name," the boy chirped, his voice a horrifying mismatch for the viciousness on his face. "Inside my Spirit Container, you're all just souls waiting to be harvested."
"Spirit Container?" Zane asked, keeping his voice level.
The boy nodded, a cold little smile playing on his lips. "I don't mind telling you. If I gather the soul power of a few hundred thousand mortals from this city, I can project my soul! And I have the Fighting Soul!"
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL REVELATION]
The pieces clicked into place with horrifying speed. All those high-level masters who had been dropping like flies… it was him. This child was killing them. His Fighting Soul had to be so powerful that even Life-and-Death experts were defenseless against it.
"You’re not from the Heartlands," Zane stated, a sudden certainty in his voice.
The boy just snorted. "Does it matter where I'm from? Just die. Maybe I'll tell you after."
[SFX: PSYCHIC ATTACK WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
He gave Zane one last, cold smile, and then his presence slammed into Zane's mind like a tidal wave—a direct assault from his Fighting Soul, aimed at shattering Zane's very consciousness.
