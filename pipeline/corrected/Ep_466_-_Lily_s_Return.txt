Ep 466 - Lily's Return

Word count: 1100

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: MAGICAL DISCOVERY]

Zane closed his eyes and reached inward, calling forth Lily. His sword spirit materialized beside him—a luminous presence that pulsed with quiet power. She had been with him for some time now, but he still knew so little about her true nature.

Most cultivators spent years coaxing their sword spirits into existence, painstakingly molding them from formless energy. Lily had arrived different—whole, aware, and bearing secrets he had yet to uncover.

Zane hadn't summoned his sword spirit since conquering the Cloud Road challenge.

A fully formed sword spirit was a rare sight—even among fighters at the Inner-Core Realm, only a select few possessed one. And yet, Zane's was already complete.

The spirit was still a mystery to him.

Typically, a warrior who passed the trials of Killing Sword Mountain received a raw, unformed mass of sword spirit that they then had to shape themselves.

The fact that Zane's had formed instantaneously was unheard of, a phenomenon born from his battle with the Demon Night. The spirit had taken the woman's form the moment he defeated her, before he even had a chance to meditate on it.

Zane had no idea why, so he’d asked the Cobalt Dragon. But all the dragon could tell him was that the Demon Night was a race powerful enough to command a divine realm, putting them on par with the True Dragons themselves. Beyond that, the secrets of the formidable Demon Night were a closed book.

For now, it was a puzzle far beyond his power to solve.

Zane still hadn't witnessed the full extent of Lily's power. But ever since their encounter with the Blood Fiend Emperor's giant sword, her strength had been skyrocketing.

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

As the black tortoises lumbered toward them, Zane sent a command with his mind and simply watched, curious to see how far Lily had truly come.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Lily became a streak of crimson silk, the Blood Fiend Emperor's massive sword slung effortlessly over her shoulder as she shot toward the beasts. Her movements carried an elegance that seemed almost playful, as though she relished the opportunity to demonstrate her newfound strength.

Each tortoise stretched a hundred feet in length, protected by a shell four feet thick. Breaking through even one would have posed a serious challenge for Zane—he'd need to channel significant power into a concentrated strike. Their slow, lumbering pace was deceptive; their sheer durability made them a monumental threat that had to be stopped before they reached the tower. A single breach in their defenses could spell disaster.

But for Lily, it was nothing. She moved with fluid grace, her slender form a stark contrast to the colossal blade she wielded. The sword—which should have been impossibly heavy for someone her size—seemed to weigh no more than a feather in her hands.

Zane felt a surge of satisfaction watching her. Whatever transformation the Blood Fiend Emperor's weapon had triggered in Lily, it had elevated her to a level he hadn't thought possible. Pride mingled with relief—having such a powerful ally might prove essential in the trials ahead.

[SFX: BLADE SLICING]

Her blade carved effortlessly through the lumbering beasts, cleaving their thick shells as though they were parchment. One by one, the tortoises fell, their massive bodies collapsing in clouds of dust until the battlefield lay silent.

[AMBIENT: QUIET BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

When the last creature toppled, Lily stood motionless among the carnage, her face serene and empty. She resembled an exquisitely carved doll—beautiful, perfect, and utterly devoid of life. The sight stirred something uncomfortable in Zane's chest. This wasn't the spirited companion he'd once known. Whatever power she'd gained had come at a cost he didn't fully understand yet.

From his perch atop the tower, he sent another mental command through their bond.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

In a crimson blur, Lily materialized beside him, her movements so swift they left streaks in the air. She stood at attention, awaiting his next instruction with the same blank expression.

[AMBIENT: OBSERVATION CHAMBER]  

In the observation chamber, the examiner's jaw had gone slack. "That sword spirit of Zane's..." His voice emerged as barely more than a whisper. "It's incredible."

"Incredible? How so?" Michael Nash leaned forward, genuine curiosity coloring his tone. Though he and the old man had both reached the Spirit-Tribulation Realm—placing them far above the examiner in raw power—they found themselves at a disadvantage here. They could identify a sword spirit when they saw one, but as cultivators who'd never walked the path of the blade, the subtleties escaped them entirely.

The examiner struggled to articulate what he'd witnessed. Michael Nash was, after all, the most formidable figure in The Heartlands. Even institutions as mighty as the World Merchants' Guild, the Ethereal Spirit Sect, and Cloud Hall treated him with extreme deference. He ruled the entire region as its undisputed master, and few dared to question his authority.

Rumor had it that Maddox once demanded Cloud Hall’s leader hand over a female fighter with a Violet-Core Physique, and Cloud Hall had no choice but to obey.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]

It’s rare for someone that powerful to lack ambition. People said Maddox’s ultimate goal was to unite all of The Heartlands and forge The Eternal Kingdom.

If he were to succeed, the World Merchants' Guild and the Ethereal Spirit Sect would be the first to be crushed.

With their long lifespans, powerful men like the old man and Michael Nash knew they would likely live to see the storm Maddox was brewing. What that meant for the future of the World Merchants' Guild, no one could say for sure.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

The mere mention of Maddox’s name was enough to make everyone fall silent, casting a dark cloud over the tower. In truth, Maddox was just as much a threat to the Ethereal Spirit Sect, and they were the only fifth-grade sect in the land.

[SFX: DEEP BREATH]

Michael Nash finally broke the silence. "Are you saying Zane could have a rise like Maddox's?"

The old man shook his head, his expression thoughtful. "I can't say for sure. Hundreds of thousands of fighters have that kind of potential. Zane is powerful and exceptionally talented for his age, but reaching Maddox's level is nearly impossible. Still… he has a much better shot at it than anyone else in his generation."

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Michael Nash gave a grim laugh. "Even if that's true, it's not like the World Merchants' Guild can claim him. He's here for the pre-qualifiers, but he's a disciple of Cloud Hall, not one of ours. Still, he's definitely someone we need to stay on good terms with."

The stronger Zane got, the clearer it became: they couldn't afford to piss the kid off.
