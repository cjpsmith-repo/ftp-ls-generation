Ep 610 - War Sages

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]
Rage burned through Maurice as Zane kept up the fight, a desperate dance of retreat and attack. No one had ever gotten under his skin like this, had ever dared to defy him. But in Maurice’s eyes, this was nothing more than the last twitches of a dying animal.
"Pathetic," he snarled. "You're just a cornered rat, snapping at the air when the fight's already over. You think you're some great beast? You're an ant I can grind under my heel. This is useless."
Maurice was dead set on killing Zane. He was the root of all this chaos. Kill Zane, he thought, and the humans lose their link to the Demon Night. They’d never dare challenge the ogres alone. The thought of crushing Zane for good solidified his resolve. As for the Demon Night seeking revenge for their queen? Let them try. The ogres feared no one.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
A flare of pure starlight erupted from Zane's fist.
Though it looked like a simple jab, the punch was packed with far more power than it seemed. The faint glow pulsed with a hidden force that went far beyond any ordinary technique.
Maurice sneered, confident his bone-crushing strength would shatter the starlight. He was wrong.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
A dazzling light exploded from Maurice’s own right hand, far brighter than he’d anticipated. He thought his energy would overwhelm the starlight, but when their fists collided, the impact was immense.
[SFX: BONE CRACK]
A sharp, searing pain shot through Maurice's right hand.
He raised his hand and stared at it — covered in hairline fractures, blood seeping through the cracks. Then he laughed.
"Ha! A warrior actually managed to injure me. Interesting. Very interesting!"
But by the time the laughter faded, Zane had already retreated to the edge of the arena. Lavender, Madeline, and Elder May had closed ranks around him. Then two more figures flashed forward — Fabian and Jarrod, stepping up shoulder to shoulder.
Malcolm's eyes swept over them with contempt. "You two human clowns dare to join in? Looks like the humans don't want their seats in the Tower of Sin anymore."
Maurice strode onto the arena floor, sixty Ogre War Lords at his back, encircling Zane's group.
Fabian and Jarrod remained silent. They'd calculated the risk before stepping forward—the Ogres were formidable, but the Demon Night's presence shifted the odds.
Zane looked across at Maurice, a slow smile spreading across his face. "Did the Ogres hand out our seats? Or do you just think this Tower belongs to you?"
Maurice waved a hand dismissively. He was done talking.
Despite his bluster, he ranked only second. The Demon Night dominated the Tower of Sin, claiming a dozen seats from one hundred twenty-eight—including the coveted first position.
And the one holding that top seat, Esther Sterling, was the woman who had just arrived.
"The Tower of Sin doesn't belong to a single race." Esther Sterling's voice rang out, clean and effortless, stopping him cold. "And it seems the Ogres forgot to ask the other seat holders before making their move."
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY WAVE]
[MUSIC: MAJESTIC ORCHESTRAL]
Esther Sterling led the War Lords of the Demon Night forward, carrying herself with an effortless grace and authority. A moment ago, Maurice’s aura had felt like drowning in lava to the creatures on the tenth floor. Now, Esther’s presence washed over them like the first thaw of spring, a rush of life and energy that left everyone feeling instantly revived.
Esther Sterling strode toward Lavender, her gaze sweeping over the tense group. She dropped to one knee.
In perfect unison, the War Lords behind her knelt as well. "Your Majesty!"
Lavender glanced at the sea of kneeling figures before speaking, her voice cutting through the silence.
[SFX: COLLECTIVE MOVEMENT]
"Stand."
Esther Sterling rose, her gaze locking onto Maurice. "Long live the queen. We live to serve. You should really think twice about this, Maurice."
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
In recent years, the Demon Night’s power had grown, already surpassing the Flaming Place and the Dread Place. But they weren't a violent or bloodthirsty people. They’d always tolerated the overbearing ogres, choosing not to escalate things as long as a line wasn't crossed.
Today, that line had been stomped on.
Maurice looked at her for a long moment. Then he made his offer.
"Esther Sterling. The Ogres will give up five seats. All we want in return is the boy."
Five seats. The crowd absorbed that in silence.
The Ogres held twenty-six seats. The Demon Night held sixteen. Five seats would make them equal at twenty-one each. Every seat granted five passes to the Dark Nether Cave, one of the Tower's most coveted resources. The humans had clawed their way to four seats over the years. Now the Ogres offered five just to watch one human die.
Fabian and Jarrod stiffened. The offer was almost too good to refuse—and that terrified them. If the Demon Night accepted, Zane would die, and the Ogres would immediately turn on the humans, reclaiming four seats in an afternoon. Net loss: one. Net gain: everything.
Maurice had already done the math.
Esther Sterling was quiet for a moment. Then a faint, almost helpless smile crossed her face.
"Let me put this simply," she said. "If our queen ordered every member of the Demon Night to lay down their lives — all one million of them — they would do it without hesitation. Do you understand what I'm telling you?"
"So you won't give in," Maurice said.
"You can say that," Esther replied.
"Then the Ogres and the Demon Night fight."
"You can say that."
Maurice threw his head back and laughed. It was a deep, rolling sound that filled the entire floor. "It seems the Demon Night have finally grown bold enough to move against us!"
Esther Sterling's brows drew together. Beside her, Madeline and Elder May frowned. They'd suspected it. Maurice's confidence had never wavered, not even when the odds tilted against him. He had something. Some card he hadn't played yet.
"Crane! Stone!" Maurice's voice rang out. "It's time."
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS DESCENDING]
Two figures walked down from the eleventh floor.
The moment they appeared, the air changed. Their aura was ten times heavier than Maurice's — ten times heavier than anything on the tenth floor.
Esther Sterling went pale. "Crane… Stone… They were dead. They went into the Dark Nether Cave and never came back. How are they—"
Someone in the crowd shrieked, their voice laced with terror."War Sages! There are War Sages in the Tower of Sin!"
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Seeing the two ogres, Zane’s heart plummeted. The ogres had War Sages—and the Demon Night had been completely in the dark. How? How on earth could a War Sage step into the Cursed Land, let alone the Tower of Sin?
"Impossible," Esther Sterling breathed, her eyes wide with shock.
On the main continent, the pecking order was clear: at the very top were the Soul Sea Realm masters, with the War Sages just below them, forming the true backbone of any real fighting force.
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TEMPLE ECHO]
But the Tower of Sin had rules, ancient and absolute. The most important one—the one that defined the very balance of power here—was that no Soul Sea Realm master or War Sage could ever set foot in the Cursed Land. Any who tried would be utterly destroyed.
That rule had stood since the dawn of the Tower, without a single exception. It was why all the races had built their teleportation arrays to the Cursed Land, designed to look ordinary to any warrior below the rank of War Sage, effectively filtering out the overpowered.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
And yet, here they were.
