Ep 1099 - Exchanging Punches

Word count: 1029

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MUFFLED]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

The roar of the crowd outside was a distant, muffled thing, completely shut out by the Illusionary Arena.

Layla desperately wanted to watch her brother, Zane, in his fight, but she was a little busy with an opponent of her own.

“Focus on me, little girl!” her opponent taunted, a mocking laugh echoing in his voice.

[SFX: GHOSTLY WHOOSH]

A strange, ghostly aura seeped from his body, coalescing into a swarm of fist-sized skulls that shot straight for her.

The thought of Zane fighting for his freedom weighed on her, a heavy stone in her gut even as she faced her own battle. She didn’t bother to reply to the warrior. Instead, she twisted gracefully in the air, dodging the spectral attack as her long hair swirled around her. But even as she moved, her eyes were locked on the far platform, on Zane.

“Careful, little girl,” the ghost cultivator pressed, his voice dropping low. “Slip up once, and this ends fast.”

She didn’t answer. Winning this fight meant nothing to her anymore—only Zane’s did. But her distraction wasn’t lost on him, and he closed in, pressing his advantage while her attention stayed elsewhere.

[SFX: SWORD SWING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

There, Zane swung his sword, and Lincoln let out a wild laugh. A brilliant golden light flared from the man’s body as he caught the blade of pure wind with his bare hand.

[SFX: BLADE SHATTER]

The blade shattered on impact.

[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACKLE]

The lightning that arced across the burly man’s body didn’t even make him flinch. Zane immediately summoned a Nether God Shadow, but his opponent smashed it to pieces just as easily. Having dismantled Zane’s opening attacks without breaking a sweat, Lincoln charged, closing the distance between them in a heartbeat.

But Zane was calm, his expression unreadable even as the man systematically shut down every move he made. The battle was his entire world; nothing else existed.

[SFX: WHOOSHING ENERGY]

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

In the blink of an eye, ten more Nether God Shadows erupted from his hands, surging toward his enemy.

Seeing the wave of phantoms, Lincoln let out a ferocious roar. The golden light around him intensified, hardening like a second skin until he was a gleaming, solid-gold figure, the glow almost blinding. Zane’s shadows were fierce, but they were no match for him.

[SFX: SHATTERING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

The moment they got close, they shattered into dust.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

“That guy has a deep understanding of the Metal Law,” one of the palace leaders commented from the viewing stands. “This could be trouble, even for Zane.”

Another leader shook his head. “Don’t be so sure. Zane made it across the flame pool. He must have learned something no one else knows. All we’ve seen him use is that Thundergust sword. We have no idea what he’s really hiding.”

[SFX: ROARING CROWD]

Meanwhile, the warriors from the Thirteen Palaces of the Alliance were shamelessly cheering for Lincoln, roaring their approval at his every move.

[SFX: TEMPORAL DISTORTION]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

After destroying the last of the shadows, Lincoln took a heavy step forward, a ripple of Time Law erupting from his body and washing over the entire platform. Zane was forced onto the defensive, dodging streaks of golden light while fighting the strange temporal force that threatened to rip his sword, Thundergust, right out of his hand.

[SFX: METALLIC RESONANCE]

The Time Law faded as quickly as it came—Zane unwound it with barely a thought, the same way he’d have used it himself. It had only ever been a test, and Lincoln knew it. Stepping back, he crossed his hands in front of him, and a ring of solid gold light bloomed into existence between them.

[SFX: ROARING SUCTION]

The pull hit Thundergust instantly—a force trying to wrench the sword clean out of Zane’s grip. For a heartbeat, it nearly worked.

Lincoln’s grin faltered. That shouldn’t have happened. His Everlasting Golden Ring could strip the weapon from warriors twice this kid’s level. He bared his teeth and slammed his hands together again—the ring tripled in size, and the suction surged.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Zane’s expression didn’t change. The hidden power of the Dragon Scale flowed into his grip, and Thundergust held rock-steady—didn’t so much as tremble.

“...Huh?” For the first time, real surprise broke through Lincoln’s confidence. Was raw strength this kid’s trump card after all?

He expanded the ring again, and streaks of golden light lanced toward Zane—not to finish him, but to keep him busy, keep that sword pinned in place. Zane wove through the barrage, dodging the golden light while holding firm against the pull on his blade. Every Law, pushed far enough, could shape the world. There was no strongest Law—only the strongest warrior wielding it. For the moment, neither of them was budging.

The Nether God Shadows hadn’t done a thing. The man’s Metal Law technique was simply overpowering. But that didn’t mean Zane was out of options. He could use the shadows to teleport Lavender to the man’s side. With her current strength and the Nightshard Spear, she could end this in a single strike.

But he couldn’t. Lavender’s identity was too dangerous to reveal, and besides Yolande, he didn’t trust anyone here.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]

More than that, this was his fight. He didn’t want to rely on her for it.

Decision made. Zane sealed Thundergust back into his Storage Ring. Then, in a flash of movement, he charged straight at Lincoln.

This was exactly what his opponent had been waiting for, trying to force Zane into a close-quarters brawl from the start. A faint, confident smile touched Lincoln’s lips. He had absolute faith in his physical strength; he’d perfected the Form of the Steel Spirit, a technique that made his body as tough as any Divine-Class weapon. In a hand-to-hand fight, he was unbeatable.

“Finally!” he boomed, his grin widening. “Decided to stop wasting my time?”

Zane didn’t say a word, his face a mask of calm. But beneath the surface, he could feel his body humming with all the power he’d been holding back since the fight began.

[SFX: BOOM]

They met head-on, Lincoln throwing a punch fueled by pure, raw power. For just a moment, it seemed the entire fight would come down to whose body was stronger.

A deafening crack echoed between them, and the confident smirk on the burly man’s face dissolved into pure shock.
