Ep 1257 - Cracking Skulls

Word count: 1132

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: WIND THROUGH MASSIVE TREE BRANCHES]
Zane had worked his ass off to get to the very top of The World Tree, and all he had to show for it was a single iron ticket and a swarm of annoying, biting skulls. 
He scanned the treetop, but there was nothing else. Not a single thing.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY PULSE]
He even activated his magical eyes, focusing his gaze on the thirteen Giant Eyeballs suspended in the sky above. A greedy thought flickered through his mind.
He licked his lips. “I wonder what I’d get for shattering one of those things.”
[SFX: WHOOSH UPWARD FLIGHT]
Without a second thought, he shot upward, breaking through the fog and soaring straight for them. 
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT FORCE]
But just as he closed in, a massive, invisible force stopped him dead in his tracks and slapped him right out of the sky.
The impact was brutally strong, a wave of pure pain that sent him recoiling. He had no control as he plummeted from the tree. 
[SFX: BODY FALLING THROUGH AIR]
His first instinct was to fight it, to try and stop his descent, but he quickly realized he was powerless against the strange energy binding him. So, he just gave up and let gravity take over.
[MUSIC: CALM WAITING]
[AMBIENT: FOREST BASE ATMOSPHERE]
Down at the base of The World Tree, Amber, Aurora, and the rest of the team were waiting. A few of them were starting to wonder what was taking him so long.
“You think he got sent back to the city of ogres?” Aurora asked, tilting his head up. The thick fog blanketing the tree made it impossible to see a thing. If he *had* been sent back, it meant he’d died up there.
“That’d be a real shame,” Rowan said. “All those Illusion Points he racked up, just left at the top of the tree.”
Amber, however, just shook her head. “I don’t think he died.”
“But it’s been forever!” Just as Aurora spoke, a dark shape burst through the fog and slammed into the ground nearby.
 [SFX: HEAVY IMPACT EXPLOSION]
[SFX: GROUND TREMOR]
[MUSIC: SUDDEN DRAMATIC TENSION]
The impact sent a tremor through the earth. Amber didn’t hesitate, rushing toward the crater that had just formed.
“Zane!” she yelled, her voice tight with worry as she saw him climbing out of the hole. “Are you okay?”
Zane managed a weak smile at her concern. “Don’t worry. I’m fine.”
Aurora drifted over, but before he could say a word, he noticed a dark mass hovering just above his head. He looked up, and his jaw dropped. 
A cloud of small, palm-sized skulls was floating right there. It wasn’t just him—Amber and the rest of the Demon Night team stared, completely stunned.
“Be careful!” Amber yelled.
[SFX: SWORD DRAWN]
Amber’s blade flashed free of its sheath in an instant, a sharp arc of sword light cutting straight through the hovering swarm.
[SFX: SWORD STRIKE THROUGH AIR]
[SFX: SKULLS SCATTERING]
Dozens of the little skulls were sent tumbling away by the strike. But in the blink of an eye, they whipped back around and resumed biting at Zane’s back, teeth grinding like something out of a nightmare.
Amber’s eyes widened. She knew exactly how much force she’d put behind that strike — and there wasn’t a single mark on any of them.
“They’re useless to fight,” Zane said, a little helplessly. “These things can’t be broken.”
Aurora couldn’t quite hide a smile despite himself. Scary as the skulls looked, Zane hauling that whole biting cluster around on his back made him look like some kind of overgrown, furious tortoise. It was almost funny.
“It’s some kind of curse from the Sorcerer Race,” Zane said, peeling one of the skulls off his shoulder and flinging it away to demonstrate. It spun through the air and came right back at him. He caught it out of the air a second later, turning it over in his hand.
Something clicked. He reached into his Storage Ring.
[SFX: ITEM MATERIALIZING]
A dark, heavy pickaxe dropped into his palm.
Aurora’s breath caught. “Is that—a Black Star Pickax? A Supreme Artifact?”
He’d inherited every fine treasure a World Lord’s line could offer, and even he had never once laid eyes on a true Supreme Artifact. Across a hundred thousand Great Worlds, there were maybe three thousand of them in existence — one born, if that, every million years.
Zane didn’t care where it came from. He just wanted to know if it worked.
[SFX: METAL IMPACT]
He brought it down on the little skull with everything he had.
Nothing. Not even a scratch.
Zane stared at the unmarked skull, genuinely shaken. A weapon built on the Law of Causality — something that should have cut through anything — couldn’t touch it. Whatever was bound in that skull exceeded the artifact itself.
That made no sense. The ragged, cloaked things that summoned them had barely put up a fight. How could something so weak connect to something this unbreakable? If the race behind it possessed such power, why hide at the edge of the world instead of ruling it?
He had no answers. He put the pickaxe away.
“Hang on,” Aurora said, an idea sparking. “The main city runs on the law of the land — no fighting allowed inside the walls, not by anyone. Maybe that rule holds against these things too.”
Zane’s eyes lit up. He hadn’t thought of that.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE THOUGHTFUL]
[AMBIENT: QUIET CITY STREETS]
Fighting within city walls was forbidden by heaven’s own decree - a law even these pests couldn’t violate. His only option.
He’d traveled twenty thousand miles north from the demon night race’s main city to reach The Deadwood Gate. Two sanctuaries lay nearby, one north and one west. At this point, he’d do anything to escape these skulls.
“So, besides the swarm of floating skulls, what else did you find up there?” Aurora asked. He knew he’d never make it to the top himself, but his curiosity was killing him. “Did you get, like, a hundred fruits?”
Zane’s eyes widened. A hundred fruits? That would be worth ten million points.
[SFX: METAL TICKET PULLED OUT]
He sighed, pulling out the iron ticket in defeat. “No. Not a single fruit. I didn’t even get one lousy point. All I got was this thing and these damn skulls. And apparently, to even use it, I have to pay five million Illusion Points.”
Aurora gasped. “Five million?!” He’d been working his tail off and had only managed to gather about two million himself.
What could possibly be so special about that ticket to justify a price that high? You had to pay five million points just to get into some hall?
