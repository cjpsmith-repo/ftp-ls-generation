Ep 66 - Beyond the Walls
Word count: 1220
[SFX: RHYTHMIC WAR DRUMS]
[AMBIENT: MILITARY FORT MORNING]
The deep, pounding rhythm of war drums yanked Zane from his fitful slumber. His heart raced as he threw open his door, immediately engulfed by the raw, electric energy of the morning muster. Below, a hundred disciplined soldiers marched out of the fort in tight formation, their boots stamping against the hard earth in perfect synchronicity with the relentless drums.
Shane, his face etched with the grim determination of a veteran, found him amid the chaos and swiftly pulled their team together. His voice carried an edge of urgency as he outlined their mission: hunt the Razor swarm beyond the fort's imposing walls.
[AMBIENT: DESOLATE BATTLEFIELD]
The main battlefield stretched for desolate miles from the gates of Dragon's Keep, a grim landscape carved out by the unforgiving war. Mounds of dead Razorwings lined the path like grotesque monuments to the ongoing conflict.
Zane's stomach turned as he observed clusters of haggard people picking through the carcasses with desperate efficiency.
Every so often, someone would cautiously jab a carcass with a long stick, jumping back when the creature twitched. With a jolt of realization, Zane understood they were all merely civilians—ordinary people with no cultivation abilities. Not a single Refiner moved among them, leaving them utterly defenseless against any real threat.
"What are they doing out here?" he wondered, a chill running down his spine despite the morning heat.
[MUSIC: GRIM POST-BATTLE]
A single Razorwing could effortlessly massacre an entire group of these vulnerable scavengers, tearing through flesh and bone with terrifying speed. This barren wasteland, littered with the remnants of battle, offered no sanctuary for ordinary people whose only defenses were their numbers and primitive tools fashioned from desperation rather than skill.
Zane observed them sifting through the decaying bodies with practiced hands, noting the mechanical efficiency born from necessity. Their faces, weathered and grim, told stories of hardship he could scarcely imagine. The question that had been festering in his mind finally escaped his lips.
"Why are these civilians risking their lives outside the fort?" he asked, his voice betraying both bewilderment and genuine concern. "Don't they fear death lurking in every shadow?"
Shane's response came with a bitter laugh that never reached his eyes. "Afraid of dying? Of course they are," he replied, running a hand through his dust-caked hair. "We lose civilians out here every season. Just earlier this week, a pair of Razorwings breached our defenses and slaughtered a dozen innocent souls before we could intervene."
"Then what drives them to venture into such peril?" Zane pressed, his brow furrowing as he struggled to comprehend such willingness to face mortality.
Shane just smiled. "It's because of your sect."
Seeing Zane's blank look, Leo stepped in to explain. "Every Razorwing has a crystal core in its chest. The sect uses them to track our kills. We're supposed to collect them on the spot, but when you're fighting a swarm... you miss a few. These people are hoping to get lucky. The cores fetch a high price inside the fort."
It clicked for Zane. He did the math in his head: twenty cores for one point, one point for two Crystal Cubes. Refiners had little use for gold or silver, so they'd gladly trade for cores. For a civilian, a single lucky find could mean a comfortable life—maybe even a fortune. No wonder they were willing to risk it all.
[AMBIENT: QUIET RAINFOREST]
Up ahead, the lush, green rainforest was eerily quiet. The swarm scared off everything else. Every year, the native creatures either fled or went deep underground, just as helpless against the horde as any human. The silence was absolute, broken only by the whisper of wind through the leaves.
[SFX: RUSTLING LEAVES]
They moved into the forest like ghosts. A Razorwing's grass-green body was perfect camouflage; they could be anywhere, lying in wait. The thought had barely crossed Zane's mind when he brushed past a shrub and two scythe-like blades shot out at him.
[SFX: SUDDEN ATTACK]
Purple Vitality ignited across Zane's skin. He caught one blade bare-handed, snapped it off, and caved in the creature's skull. The Razorwing dropped — and the forest came alive.
[SFX: RISING INSECT DRONE]
A seething green tide poured from the foliage. Zane had one thought: points. Each one a step closer to Elara.
[MUSIC: BATTLE FRENZY]
He threw himself in. Vitality threaded from his body in tight coils, driving each blow. Two Razorwings hit him in a pincer — he punched outward with both fists and caved their abdomens. Cores tumbled free. When one lunged from behind, he ducked and drove a short punch into its gut.
[SFX: IMPACT]
The force detonated inside the carapace. Seven cracks. It fell. He found a rhythm — one blow, one kill — and the bodies piled up.
[SFX: CRACKING CARAPACE, REPEATED]
Nearby, Leo strolled through the chaos, loosing black-red sword beams that punched clean through anything that came near. Rex and Max fought with the brutal efficiency of veterans who knew exactly where to strike. Behind them, Shane's Emerald Task Force held formation — shields, spears, arrows.
When it was over, Zane counted sixty-odd kills. Three points. One hundred points meant twenty thousand kills — half a month of this. Half a month to see Elara. He could live with that.
He worked faster.
[AMBIENT: FOREST ADVANCE]
Wave after wave came as they pushed deeper in. Zane learned the sounds — the rustle before a strike, the pitch of wings — until he could read the ambush before it sprang.
In the distance, more branches rustled, and a swarm of them materialized from the foliage.
[MUSIC: TENSE DISCOVERY]
But this time was different. Mixed in with the swarm were dozens of larger, darker figures.
"Razorwing Alphas," Zane breathed, his eyes narrowing.
These Alphas commanded far greater value than their common counterparts. A single Alpha yielded a full point—equivalent to twenty regular specimens. Naturally, they presented a substantially more formidable challenge to overcome.
"Attention, everyone! Beware of the Alphas' poisonous stingers!" Shane called out, his voice cutting through the humid air.
The caution was primarily directed at Zane; the veterans among them had encountered these creatures before. A Razorwing Alpha, classified as a level-four beast, possessed strength comparable to a High-class fighter. Its dark purple carapace bristled with venomous spikes that could fell even experienced warriors with a single puncture. They were cunning, vicious adversaries that had claimed countless lives throughout the years.
Shane's urgent warning fell on deaf ears as Zane, driven by determination and perhaps a touch of recklessness, surged forward without hesitation, his eyes fixed on the deadly creatures before him.
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: SHARP MANDIBLE CLICKS]
As he rushed them, the Alphas clicked their mandibles in a series of sharp, percussive sounds—a language of their own. Unlike the mindless regulars, these things were smart. They could communicate.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
After a flurry of clicks, half a dozen Alphas broke from the pack, fanning out to intercept him. In a heartbeat, they had him surrounded.
"Dammit, Zane, that was reckless!" Shane yelled from behind. "They've got you boxed in!"
"We gotta help him," Rex said, already moving forward with Max at his side. They had become a tight unit on the battlefield and weren't about to let a friend get overwhelmed.
Only Leo held his ground. It looked reckless, but he knew Zane better. For all his bravado, Zane was a strategist. He wouldn't have charged in without a plan.
Leo watched, his curiosity overriding his concern. "What are you up to, Zane?" he wondered. "How was he going to handle six Alphas at once? "
