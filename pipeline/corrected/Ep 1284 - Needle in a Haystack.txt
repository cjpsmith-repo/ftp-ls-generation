Ep 1284 - Needle in a Haystack

Word count: 1106

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL REVERB]
In the Trinity Realm, if you wanted to be the best, you trained at the Jadeheart Sanctuary’s Lucent Hall. It was reserved for only the most talented disciples, but with all the top-tier Soul Sea Realm warriors off competing in the Illusion Battlefield, the place was usually empty.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
[AMBIENT: CROWDED HALL MURMURS]
Not today, though. 
[SFX: ELECTRIC BUZZ]
Today, the main hall was electric, packed with warriors from the Life-and-Death and Spirit Supreme Realms. The air was thick with anticipation as everyone waited for the results to come in from the competition.
[SFX: MAGICAL GLOW HUM]
All eyes were on the glowing pyramid floating near the ceiling, where names shifted up and down the rankings. The stakes were high, and as the gathering place for the sanctuary’s strongest, everyone had high hopes for their homegrown talent.
“Josiah’s ranking just went up! He’s at two hundred twenty thousand!”
“He might actually break into the top hundred thousand.”
“He’s really living up to the hype.”
There were, on average, a hundred thousand supreme worlds, and each one had about three major sacred places like this one. Josiah Sloan was the most outstanding warrior in the entire Jadeheart Sanctuary, and they were all rooting for him to crack that top hundred thousand.
It wasn’t just disciples, either. Even the Saint Lord of the Jadeheart Sanctuary himself was there, observing the rankings from a high balcony.
The pyramid floated in the grand hall’s center, housing the sanctuary’s only Illusionary Arena. The arena demanded constant Vitality to operate.
Two methods could supply this power in the Upper World. The first - burning Aether crystals or Sovereign Stones - was prohibitively expensive. The second, more economical approach relied on warriors supplying Vitality directly from their bodies. Gathered in sufficient numbers, they could continuously draw ambient Vitality from the world and convert it. This energy powered not only arenas but also large-scale divine arrays.
[MUSIC: SOMBER CONTEMPLATIVE]
[AMBIENT: CHAMBER ENERGY HUM]
Thousands of people were gathered in a chamber behind the grand hall, constantly pouring their Vitality into the divine patterns etched into the floor. 
[SFX: ENERGY FLOWING THROUGH CONDUITS]
The glowing lines acted as conduits, channeling the collected energy forward.
Tia hadn’t earned her place among the sanctuary’s true disciples, and she knew it. 
When she’d first ascended, a half-step World Lord had summoned her as a potential disciple. He offered a male-female dual cultivation technique rivaling a Transcendent Divine Might - but only for untouched women. Already married, Tia was immediately disqualified.
Passed over and unable to simply return to the mines after being singled out, she’d been sent here instead, pouring her Vitality into the runes day after day.
“Sigh. At least we’re spared the mines, but I don’t know how much longer I can stand pouring Vitality all day,” the woman beside her muttered, a disciple in red robes.
“Our strength isn’t enough to earn a place in the Lucent Hall,” Tia said evenly, her hands steady against the glowing rune beneath them. “This is better than the mines.”
“That hall only takes the sanctuary’s core disciples. Might as well dream of flying,” the woman scoffed. “You should’ve taken the World Lord’s offer when you had the chance—dual cultivation or not, it’s a shortcut to the higher realms most of us would kill for.”
“I wonder if my husband could ever earn a place in that hall,” Tia said quietly.
“Your husband?” The woman let out a short laugh. “Dream on. Every Soul Sea Realm warrior in a Tier-Ten sanctuary like this one is already the best of the best. What are the odds he’s one of them?”
[SFX: WHIP CRACK]
A whip lashed out of nowhere, snapping through the air just beside them both.
“Keep chattering, and I’ll send you both to the mines myself,” a heavyset overseer barked from a few paces off.
Neither of them had been hit, but the force of the strike still stung against their skin like a blade.
The overseer sauntered closer, sneering down at Tia. “What’s that look for?”
“Nothing,” Tia said, turning her face away.
“I heard you ascended looking for a husband. Also an ascender, is he?” the woman pressed, clearly delighted by whatever gossip had reached her.
Tia said nothing.
“How naive. The universe is enormous—who knows where he ended up? For all you know, he’s still down some sanctuary’s mine shaft, same as the rest of the nobodies.” The overseer’s smile widened at her own cruelty.
It wasn’t an idle jab. Even a capable Soul Sea Realm warrior could spend decades, sometimes centuries, working off a debt that size. Tia’s expression didn’t change, but her heart sank all the same—finding Zane might be a thousand times harder than she’d let herself believe.
The overseer walked off with a satisfied smile, leaving Tia to mechanically feed her Vitality back into the rune, sorrow settling deeper behind her eyes.
The work was tedious, and it gave Tia far too much time to think. Her thoughts kept drifting back to Zane. The universe felt impossibly vast, and the odds of finding him felt like searching for a needle in an endless haystack. A wave of terror washed over her—what if she never saw him again?
Tia forced herself back to the task, channeling her energy into the glowing lines on the floor, her eyes now clouded with a deep sadness.
Suddenly, a voice boomed through the air, seeming to echo directly in her ears.
“More than sixteen warriors from the Thoughtless Minds have ranked in the Vanguard One Hundred! This is a cause for celebration!”
[MUSIC: CURIOUS INTRIGUE]
The announcement startled her out of her reverie. Everyone in the universe was so glued to the Illusion Battlefield that this was the first time she’d heard anyone use a Roaring Token.
“Wh-what was that? Where did that voice come from?” she stammered, looking around in a panic.
“It’s a Roaring Token,” a warrior next to her explained calmly. He wasn’t an Ascender like her; he was stuck on this boring detail for other reasons, so of course he knew. “It’s a device that can broadcast sound across the entire universe.”
“Roaring Token?” The name sounded familiar, but she couldn’t quite place it. 
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL REALIZATION]
Then it hit her, and her eyes lit up. A Roaring Token. If she could get a Roaring Token, she could contact Zane. She could tell him where she was.
If he heard her call, he would absolutely come for her.
“A Roaring Token sounds amazing!” she said, turning to the man. “How many Aether crystals would one cost?”
She wasn’t stupid. She’d planned ahead before ascending, bringing about two hundred thousand Aether crystals with her.
But the warrior just laughed. “How many Aether crystals? Ha!”
Tia frowned, not understanding what was so funny.
