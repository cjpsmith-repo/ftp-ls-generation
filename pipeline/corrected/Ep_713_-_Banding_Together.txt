Ep 713 - Banding Together
Word count: 962
[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]
[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]
Finn’s sword was meant for Emanuel, a killing blow that should have been easy. As the leader who had united all the Third-Grade Houses in Cloudhaven, Emanuel’s death should have mattered. But at the last second, Finn pulled back. What was the point? Even with the rebel leader gone, they were still hopelessly outnumbered. The rebels would just crown a new leader and keep fighting.
Instead, he’d redirected his attack, taking out three Spirit-Tribulation warriors from a blind spot. It was a good move, but it barely made a dent in the chaos. The battle raged on. Saint Morphen and Emanuel were evenly matched, their duel a blur of fierce, lightning-fast strikes, but Emanuel’s forces still vastly outnumbered their own.
Meanwhile, Patrick, Lewis, and the other Inner-Core Realm warriors were completely outclassed. Facing off against fighters from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, they were cut down almost instantly.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]
Suddenly, a warrior broke through their crumbling defenses and charged straight for Finn, his hands wrapped around a massive, emerald-green saber.
“You killed my brother,” he roared. “Now you die!”
He didn't even finish the sentence before his saber came whistling down, aimed right for Finn’s neck. Finn slid low across the ground, his own sword swinging up to meet the attack.
[SFX: SWORD CLASH]
The blades met with a sharp ding, but his opponent barely flinched, easily deflecting the blow.
Finn’s most powerful technique, the Celestial Blade, required time to build power—time he didn’t have. Without it, he was no match for a Spirit-Tribulation warrior in a head-on fight.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
As soon as the man parried his sword, he spun and drove a fist straight into Finn’s chest.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[AMBIENT: BATTLE GROUND]
The impact was crushing. The blow slammed into him, sending him flying backward, skidding and rolling across the ground before he finally came to a stop. Gasping for air, Finn lay there, his eyes burning with defiance.
[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE STRINGS]
He remembered the conversation with Patrick and Lewis, the moment they’d decided to side with Tia. The main reason, of course, was Zane. They all knew about his connection to her. The three of them had been hesitant at first, but then even the normally timid Lewis had stepped up, ready to protect her. After that, there was no way Finn or Patrick could back down.
He still didn’t know if it was the right choice. But as he struggled to breathe, he thought that maybe a warrior’s decisions weren’t always about what he stood to gain. Maybe some things were just worth fighting for.
[AMBIENT: INTENSE BATTLE]
[MUSIC: FAST-PACED ACTION]
Lewis rushed in next, his movement technique spinning out dozens of afterimages that surrounded the Spirit-Tribulation warrior in a blurring ring. The warrior found the real Lewis among all the shadows with a single punch and sent him flying without breaking stride.
Patrick's palm strike came next — a massive imprint of force slamming down from above. The warrior glanced up and shattered it with one slash. He didn't break stride.
[SFX: HEAVY BOOT STOMP]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
He reached Finn. He put his boot on Finn's sword hand and pressed the blade to his throat.
"A pity," he said. "An Inner-Core disciple who can kill Spirit-Tribulation warriors. But you chose the wrong side."
Finn closed his eyes.
The blade didn't fall.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
[SFX: BLADE CAUGHT]
A hand had closed around the saber — bare-handed, stopping it inches from Finn's throat. The warrior stared. The blade wasn't moving. He pushed. It bent.
Finn opened his eyes.
Zane stood there, the high-grade Relic crumpling in his grip like paper.
"You're back," Finn said quietly.
"I'm back," Zane said.
Lewis's face broke into an ecstatic smile. Patrick let out a long, slow breath. In his heart, one thought settled cleanly: we made the right choice.
[MUSIC: EPIC ARRIVAL]
[SFX: VITALITY SURGE]
Zane straightened and let his voice carry — powered by Vitality, cut through the entire battlefield.
"I'm Zane. I'm back."
[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]
[SFX: COLLECTIVE MOVEMENT]
The fighting stopped. One by one, then all at once, every warrior on the square turned.
[MUSIC: VICTORIOUS REVELATION]
Saint Morphen was the first to speak. His face had broken into a full smile — something no one had seen from him in days. "Zane. You've broken through to the Spirit-Tribulation Realm."
[MUSIC: DARK TRIUMPHANT THEME]
Emanuel was already tasting victory. He was certain he’d be the new master of Cloud Hall. All he had to do was keep Saint Morphen tied up while his men mopped up the rest. From there, he’d storm the ice palace and kill Tia. In a few days, the entire fortress would be his. Sheltered by the Trinity Killing Array, he wouldn’t even have to fear the Corrupted Divine Kingdom. With Cloud Hall’s resources at his command, he could ascend to the next level of power. His future would be limitless.
[MUSIC: SUDDEN TENSION]
But right at this critical moment, that blasted Zane had to come back.
Wasn’t he supposed to be dead?
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
Most of Cloud Hall believed Zane was still out there somewhere, but Emanuel had convinced himself it was impossible. The man was gone, lost for good. So his shock was absolute when he saw him standing there, not only alive but having already reached the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
A shadow of unease fell over the faces of the warriors from the Third-Grade Houses. How were they supposed to deal with Zane?
“Zane,” Emanuel sneered, forcing his confidence to return. “So what if you’re back? You’re just in the early stages of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. That’s nothing compared to my men. You really think you can turn this around by yourself? What a joke! Men, go kill him!”
His words were a clever rally. Tia’s forces were tiny, mostly Inner-Core warriors who stood no chance. Even with Zane, the odds were impossible.
“Yeah! He’s just one Spirit-Tribulation warrior! He’s no match for all of us!”
“Let’s take him down together!”
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
Bolstered by their leader’s command, four Spirit-Tribulation warriors activated their Vitality. In a flash, they surrounded Zane, cutting off any hope of escape.
