Ep 885 - A Good Catch

Word count: 1141

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

The White-Haired Priest was completely lost. 

The priest recognized the Greater Lotus Flower, but what stunned him was the young man who’d activated it.

He’d never imagined someone so young could accomplish this feat. The Lesser Lotus Flower maxed out at nine petals—rare but achievable over three and a half billion years. 

The Greater Lotus Flower, with its potential for twenty-seven petals, belonged to another league entirely.

Perhaps someone in a supreme world had manifested it recently—the upper world was too vast for him to know everything. But in all his years and records, he’d never heard of a single contemporary achieving it.

[MUSIC: EPIC REVELATION]

The only legend he knew was Supreme Lord Lloyd, who had activated the Greater Lotus Flower two and a half million years ago after surviving the Reincarnation Tribulation. If other Supreme Lords had achieved it, the entire realm would know. Only a Supreme Lord could accomplish such a feat—no wonder the priest stared at Zane as though he’d grown a second head.

A heavy feeling settled in his stomach. He could have accepted a nine-petal flower, but this exceeded anything he could comprehend. If he reported this to the upper world, it would trigger a shockwave spreading like wildfire.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]

His throat went dry. Years ago, the upper world had sent him to oversee the seven divine kingdoms and find the Ancestral Dragon Egg—his only ticket home. 

But if he could confirm Zane capable of activating the Greater Lotus Flower, everything would change. The rewards would be unimaginable.

Could Zane truly do it?

The odds were nearly zero. A false report would bring punishment far worse than any reward. Yet as he watched Zane, a dangerous spark of belief flickered within him.

[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY PULSE]

Up on the platform, Zane had no idea what was happening. He reached out to the Cobalt Dragon. “Hasn’t the nine-petal lotus finished blooming? Is it growing a second flower?”

The dragon laughed. Ha. Your ideas are always strange. It’s not a second flower. It looks like you’re about to open the Greater Lotus Flower. You have the fate of the Battle of the Worlds. Just watch.

[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]

[MUSIC: WONDER AND AWE]

As if on cue, a stamen began to rise slowly from the center of the nine-petaled bloom. At its tip sat a small bud. As everyone watched, the stamen climbed higher and higher, the bud swelling visibly until it had already surpassed the first lotus entirely — layers of new petals wrapped tight around it, waiting.

[SFX: SOFT MAGICAL BLOOMING]

[MUSIC: SWELLING STRINGS]

The second lotus opened. Petal by petal, it unfurled above the first.

Nearby, Callan had started counting under his breath. “One, two, three, four —”

He kept going. The crowd went utterly silent.

The second lotus opened to twenty-six petals. And stopped.

One short.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

The two layers of lotus floated together in the air, releasing a sweet fragrance, dazzling streamers escaping from the petals in slow, looping arcs.

“This person…” the Holy Maiden said softly, almost to herself. She was thinking of the Wheel of Fortune in the Celestial Tomb — how Zane’s luck had been wretched at the start, bumpy and cruel, and then how he had been the first to rise and clear it entirely. She had wondered about that ever since. 

Now, watching the double lotus turn above his head, she stopped wondering. It all made sense.

The White-Haired Priest straightened. “He has at minimum the destiny of a Battle of the Worlds. In this Great Era, he may not be the absolute protagonist — but he will play an important role. That much is certain.”

“A Battle of the Worlds,” the Holy Maiden echoed. She had known a Great Era had descended — how else to explain the explosion of god-grade geniuses across the four kingdoms, something that had never happened before in their history? But she had never imagined a warrior carrying that specific destiny would appear here, in this world.

[MUSIC: LIGHTHEARTED STRINGS]

Meanwhile, War King Wind was on a completely different wavelength. He was thinking about how he’d been singing Zane’s praises back in the Northern Region. 

The kid’s future was blindingly bright, and for the first time, the War King had found someone he thought was actually worthy of his daughter, Fiona.

He was particular about this. Countless Soul Sea masters had approached him over the years with proposals, and he had turned every one of them away. None of the younger generation in the four kingdoms had met his standard. But Zane’s origins — a small place, unclear background — none of that mattered now. 

A character the upper world would move mountains for couldn’t be passed over. And Fiona needed a dual cultivation partner.

So, without a second thought, he blurted out, right in front of everyone, “Fiona, what do you think of Zane?”

[MUSIC: ROMANTIC FLUTE]

Even though Fiona was innocent, she was old enough to know exactly what her father was getting at. Her eyes, which had been glued to Zane, snapped away as a furious blush crept up her neck. She looked down and pouted. “Dad, what are you talking about?”

The War King scratched the back of his head, a little sheepish. He knew his daughter was shy, but he felt a sense of urgency. If he didn’t move fast, the other old foxes would catch on and start parading their own daughters in front of Zane. He wanted to lock this down, and fast.

[MUSIC: SCHEMING TENSION]

His instincts were dead on. The other Soul Sea Realm masters who overheard the exchange all shared a look. That sneaky bastard, they thought in unison. With Zane’s potential, his future is limitless! No wonder he’s trying to marry his daughter off to him.

The War King wasn’t the only one with a plan. All the other masters with young, pretty daughters of their own suddenly started scheming.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]

High above, the colossal lotus flower slowly rotated one last time. Its vibrant color dimmed, and then, petal by petal, it faded from sight.

Zane let out a quiet sigh. “Man,” he said to the Cobalt Dragon in his mind, “I really thought I was going to activate that last petal.”

The dragon just sneered back. “In your dreams, kid. Just activating a single petal of the Greater Lotus is a miracle. You should be thrilled. Besides, now that it’s appeared once, it’ll show up every time you face a tribulation from now on.”

Zane nodded, accepting the reality check, and started walking toward the pyramid. He still had a mountain of ores to deal with.

[SFX: OMINOUS CRACK]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

But just as he took his first step, a thin black line suddenly sliced through the clear sky above.
