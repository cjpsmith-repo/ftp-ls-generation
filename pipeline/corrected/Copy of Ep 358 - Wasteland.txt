Ep 358 - Wasteland
Word count: 1108

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ADVENTURE]

The Cloud Road was more than just a trial for new disciples to test their Weaponsmithing skills—it was a path of opportunity.

The road was riddled with challenges designed to push the disciples to their limits, laying bare their strengths and weaknesses. It also revealed who had luck on their side. Some might have a natural talent for the craft but not the fortune to stumble upon the treasures hidden along the road. Others might be blessed with luck but lack the skill to seize the chances that appear before them.

Zane immediately noticed this whirlpool was nothing like a teleportation array. It wasn't some magical construct like the Skyreach Sect's Bloody Mountain, either.

[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSITION]

 As Zane stepped into the whirlpool, the transition felt incredibly simple, like stepping through an ordinary doorway. But in that deceptively simple moment, reality transformed completely around him, unveiling an entirely foreign landscape.

"Another world," Zane murmured, his voice barely above a whisper. His eyes, bright with curiosity, methodically scanned the strange new horizon. 

[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND]

He saw a world unlike anything he'd ever seen or even heard of—a barren wasteland of decay and ruin stretching out in all directions. 

Before their departure, Sir Kenneth had explained the nature of the Cloud Road in great detail.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WORLDBUILDING]

"Scattered throughout our world lie hidden realms that are invisible to common folk and inaccessible through ordinary means. They don't exist in the physical sense; they reveal themselves only to those who've achieved a certain level of mental enlightenment."

It reminded Zane, of his own journey to Killing Sword Mountain—a passage he'd been able to access only after he'd grasped the Apex of Sword Intent. These mysterious places weren't part of the visible world; they existed in dimensions that were accessible only to the truly awakened mind.

Kenneth had warned that the entrance would seal itself once the Supreme Aether crystals exhausted their power. Zane shivered slightly, not from cold but from the profound emptiness permeating this realm. 

His fingers unconsciously traced the hilt of Streamer as his eyes swept across the devastation surrounding him. 
Finn and Patrick had already moved on, disappearing down separate paths into the ruins. Zane hadn't moved.
It was the sky that held him. This was a broken world, and its sky showed it — great jagged cracks splitting the space above, edges like shattered glass catching the light. To anyone else they were just damaged. To Zane, with his new sensitivity to the Spatial Laws, each crack was a visible seam in the fabric of space itself. He couldn't look away.

What horrible force, he wondered, could have wrought such complete devastation? The ruins whispered ancient secrets, but their voices were too faint, too distant for him to comprehend fully. A sense of both awe and unease settled in his chest as he contemplated the fallen grandeur before him.

[MUSIC: TENSE OBSERVATION]

Meanwhile, as Zane stood lost in thought, Sir Kenneth watched his every move from outside the entrance, a knot of worry tightening in his gut.

It wasn't hard for the sect leaders to observe their disciples through the Scrying Slate. While everyone else had already pushed forward on the Cloud Road, Zane hadn't moved an inch. He just stood by the entrance, staring blankly at the sky. His bizarre behavior certainly hadn't gone unnoticed.

"What is Zane doing?" Kenneth muttered to himself. He knew Zane. He knew the destiny the boy carried. Unlike Evan, Zane was cautious; he never did anything reckless. So why was he just… standing there? Did something go wrong? What happened in there? The questions echoed in his mind, and he couldn't shake the growing worry.

[SFX: SUDDEN LAUGHTER]

Suddenly, Eddy erupted in laughter. "Well, well, Kenneth. History repeating itself? I seem to remember Evan falling asleep on the Cloud Road three years back! He was one of yours, wasn't he?"

His words sparked a memory, and the other sect leaders chuckled, recalling Evan's embarrassing display.

[MUSIC: REMINISCENT MELODY]

The truth was, Evan was a gifted weaponsmith. He would have easily made it into Cloud Hall—hell, he probably would have outperformed everyone in the contest—if he hadn't fallen asleep on the Cloud Road. But his carelessness cost him first place. No one could believe he'd just dozed off mid-trial. They could still picture how furious Sir Kenneth had been, so enraged they thought he might actually kill Evan right then and there as punishment.

Would history repeat itself?

"Don't worry, Kenneth," Eddy said, his tone dripping with false sympathy. "You brought your best, and they're all sharp. Even your third-best disciple managed to grasp the Apex of Sword Intent. Maybe this one just found something and is meditating on it. Wouldn't you agree?" 

For some reason, Eddy held a bit of a grudge against Zane. It wasn't hate, really—more like pure envy for the Skyreach Sect's incredible luck. He truly hadn't expected one of their disciples to have already understood the Apex of Sword Intent.

Toby turned to him with a look of astonishment. "What was that, Eddy? Did you say a disciple from the Skyreach Sect has already grasped the Apex of Sword Intent?"

"That's right, Elder Toby," Eddy said with a grin. "And he's just their third-best. Makes you wonder how powerful their top two must be, doesn't it?"

Toby nodded, his interest clearly piqued. "Interesting… The Apex of Sword Intent…" He couldn't believe Kenneth had trained a disciple to that level.

All the other sect leaders wore expressions of shock or envy. Even Toby looked skeptical.

Toby gave Sir Kenneth a serious look. "Zane is only at Level Three of the Adept Realm. How could he have been granted such a profound opportunity? According to the ancient tomes, the Sword Tempering Pool is a lesser hallowed ground. You said the opportunity he found was far greater. Did he visit a true hallowed ground of the blade?"

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

Kenneth simply nodded. "Zane didn't tell me where he went, but I sensed a great deal of Malice clinging to him. So, my guess is he went to Killing Sword Mountain, one of the three hallowed grounds of the sword."

At Kenneth's words, Toby's composed expression finally cracked. 
The hallowed grounds were sacred places all weaponsmiths dreamed of visiting, separated into greater and lesser sanctums. Killing Sword Mountain was one of the most revered hallowed grounds of the blade—the pinnacle of them all. 

Toby couldn't believe that a young man who had only just reached Level Three of the Adept Realm had already been to that legendary mountain. 

A bitter wave of envy washed over him.

He hated Zane the minute he laid eyes on the kid, and now he knew why.
