Ep 1150 - Snow Demons

Word count: 1030

[MUSIC: EERIE SUSPENSEFUL]
[AMBIENT: COLD MOUNTAIN WIND]
[SFX: WOMAN’S LAUGHTER]
A woman’s laughter suddenly echoed up from the chilling abyss, thin and sharp, and every fighter on the ledge went rigid.
“What was that sound?” a warrior asked, his voice tight with dread. “Where did it come from?”
Everyone had heard it, a faint, chilling giggle, but no one could pinpoint the source.
[SFX: SOFT EERIE LAUGH]
Then they heard it again, a soft laugh that seemed to drift right beside their ears.
“What the hell is that?” an Ogre fighter roared, his voice laced with a frustration that bordered on panic.
Silence was the only answer he got. It wasn’t that they were ignoring him; it was that nobody knew what to say.
Zane’s expression hardened. *What is that?* he asked in his mind.
Lavender’s voice was a calm presence within him. *If I remember correctly, that would be a Snow Demon.*
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
*A Snow Demon?* Zane’s eyes widened.
A faint smile seemed to color her tone. *Don’t worry. A Snow Demon won’t be much of a threat to you.*
[SFX: WHOOSHING BLUR]
As Zane tried to process that, a white shadow flashed past him, a blur of motion that vanished back into the depths. 
[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]
He wasn’t the only one who saw it; a collective gasp went through the group.
“What is that? That white thing—it’s circling us!” a warrior shouted.
“It’s a Snow Demon! I know it!” another fighter yelled, his voice cracking. “Those damn things only exist in frozen lands! They’re evil spirits from the Snow World!”
[MUSIC: RISING DREAD STRINGS]
No sooner had the words left his mouth than more white shadows began to peel away from the walls of the abyss. 
[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSHING]
They were formless, like smoke, and drifted through the air with an unnatural ease. They moved as fast as the warriors, circling the group and tightening their ring.
There were exactly as many of them as there were warriors in the abyss. One each. And since Zane was still at the bottom — the point of the falling pyramid — his Snow Demon reached him first.
“Everyone, be careful!” another man warned. “Snow Demons can possess your soul! They’ll make you lose your mind!”
“Ha! That damned thing is going to make me lose my mind? What a joke!” the Ogre fighter boomed. 
[SFX: WEAPON WHOOSH]
The weapon whirred toward one of the Snow Demons—and passed straight through its misty body without leaving a mark.
The legends were true. Snow Demons were infamous creatures from the Snow World, known for taking the shape of the person most important to a warrior and using that illusion to trick them. 
Zane watched his Snow Demon carefully as it circled him. At first, it was shapeless — half-formed things, strange outlines that dissolved before they could settle. But as it circled, he felt something odd: a faint pressure, like fingers brushing the edges of his mind. It was reading him. Feeling for what mattered most.
Then it began to change in earnest.
Pale arms emerged from the mist first, smooth and unhurried. Then a waist. Slender legs. A tall, composed frame that solidified with each pass. The last thing to appear was the face — and when it did, it was unmistakable.
Inside him, Lavender had been watching with quiet curiosity to see whose form it would take. When the face finally appeared, a faint flicker of disappointment crossed her expression. It wasn’t hers.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL STRINGS]
For Zane, that person was Elara. 
[SFX: GHOSTLY COALESCING]
And as one of the swirling forms in front of him began to coalesce, he found himself staring at a perfect image of his sister. 
He recognized immediately that it wasn’t quite right. The Snow Demon hadn’t shaped itself after the real Elara — it had shaped itself after the Elara that lived in his memory, the one that years of longing had quietly made more perfect than the original. He knew that. He saw the difference.
[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACKLE]
He drew Thundergust anyway. Lightning crept along the blade — the kind that warded off evil. He raised it.
But his arm stopped.
He’d used the Roaring Token to search for her after arriving in the Upper World. She never responded. Either she was beyond its reach, or she didn’t have one. Either way, he had no idea where she was or when he’d find her. He was so certain he would — he just didn’t know when.
The image in front of him didn’t move, didn’t speak. It just looked at him with an expression he’d spent years trying to remember exactly. Faced with this phantom, he couldn’t bring himself to look away.
His heart ached. Just for a second, his resolve fractured.
That was all the opening the Snow Demon needed. 
[SFX: EERIE HYPNOTIC HUM]
Its eyes began to glow with a hazy, dream-like light.
As the light flashed, its color reflected in Zane’s own eyes. 
[SFX: BODY COLLAPSING]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL DREAMSCAPE]
[AMBIENT: MUFFLED VOID]
He was lost, his body going limp as he began to fall.
Inside him, Lavender remained curled up, doing nothing to stop it.
[SFX: DEEP DRAGON RUMBLE]
But the Cobalt Dragon finally spoke, his voice a low rumble. *Why didn’t you warn him? He should have been able to fight this off.*
Snow Demons could be deadly to fighters with weak spirits, but for someone like Zane, they should have been a minor problem at best—especially with the power of Lavender and the other dragons inside him. The Cobalt Dragon had stayed quiet, assuming Lavender had it handled, since she was the expert on the Endless Abyss. It was her place to advise Zane, and he hadn’t wanted to overstep.
But she’d just let Zane fall into the trap. The Cobalt Dragon didn’t understand.
[MUSIC: CALM MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Lavender’s voice was serene. *That was his choice. He misses his sister. Let him stay in the illusion a little longer. I have a feeling he can find his own way out.*
The Cobalt Dragon just rolled his eyes, which glowed with a deep, powerful energy.
