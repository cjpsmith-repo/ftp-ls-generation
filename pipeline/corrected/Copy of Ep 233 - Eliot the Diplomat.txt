Ep 233 - Eliot the Diplomat
Word count: 996
[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]
[MUSIC: UPBEAT WELCOME]
Zane sat the head of the table in Misty Peak's inner sanctum, where a dozen disciples sat in a semicircle around Eliot. The polished wooden floor gleamed beneath his feet as he scanned the room, noting the various expressions that greeted him---some curious, others dismissive.
Sunlight streamed through tall windows, illuminating ancient weapons displayed on the walls and casting long shadows across training mats worn smooth by countless duels.
As conversation hushed at his arrival, Zane straightened his shoulders and approached the gathering, conscious of his newfound status yet uncertain of his place among these elite cultivators who had earned their positions through years of dedicated training.
"Alright, let's give a warm welcome to the newest member of the inner disciples!" Eliot said with a smile, clapping his hands. "Zane passed the assessment, so he's been promoted to an inner disciple of Misty Peak. Congratulations and welcome to the team, Zane!"
Zane gave a small wave. "Thanks, Eliot. Glad to be here. Cliff mentioned you guys were talking about the All Peaks Competition?"
Eliot nodded. "Yeah, that's right. You know what they say, 'know your enemy, know yourself.' Since you're new to the inner circle, you might not be up to speed on everything. I was asked to go over our strategy for the competition with you."
[SFX: SCORNFUL SCOFF]
"What strategy?" an inner disciple scoffed. "The other peaks are way stronger than us. How are we supposed to get an edge? Best we can hope for is not getting wiped out on day one." The dig was clearly aimed at Zane, a jab at the idea that one new disciple could make any real difference.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
Hearing that, Eliot's smile faded. "It all comes down to effort," he said firmly. "I know we're not the strongest peak, but we'll never know what we're capable of if we don't give it our all. I'd rather go down fighting than not show up at all."
Zane nodded, agreeing completely.
Eliot was right. Strength wasn't everything; determination was the key to any victory. You couldn't get far without believing in yourself. A true fighter would rather lose than quit before the match even began.
At two years older than Zane, Eliot had already reached the Peak of the Adept Realm. His natural talent was a huge part of his success, but he also had an incredible will to win.
Even though the inner disciple's comment was aimed at Zane, he didn't dare talk back after Eliot shut him down. After all, Eliot was highly respected and the unofficial leader of their group.
"So, tell me about the competition," Zane asked directly. "How strong are the other fighters?"
It was a rookie question, but what else could you expect from someone who'd just joined their ranks?
The All Peaks Competition in the Skyreach Sect was held every three years. All the participant data was on record for any disciple to review. Most everyone was already familiar with it.
Another disciple started to snicker at Zane's question, but Eliot shot him a look that shut him up. "Zane's new to the Skyreach Sect," Eliot said, "so of course he doesn't know the specifics of the competition. That's why I asked him to be here, so we could go over the rules and our opponents."
[MUSIC: INSTRUCTIONAL THEME]
Eliot then began explaining the details of the competition to Zane.
All Thirty-Three Peaks in the Skyreach Sect would participate in the competition. Each peak sends a set number of fighters. Skytop Peak always sent the most; Misty Peak, the fewest.
"So, the Draught of Clarity and access to the Cloud Road are the prizes for first place?" Zane asked.
"The Draught of Clarity is only for first place," Eliot answered. "First place also receives a Sacred Weapon from the Sect's treasury. The Cloud Road, on the other hand, is a reward for the top three finishers. They get to enter for the trial." He patiently explained, though he couldn't help but notice Zane seemed more focused on the prizes than the fight itself. \*Was he really only focused on placing in the top three?\*
[MUSIC: SOMBER REALIZATION]
Traditionally, personal disciples monopolized the top three positions. Disciples from the Thirty-Three Peaks rarely entertained hopes of cracking the top ten.
This effectively meant that disciples from the Thirty-Three Peaks had no shot at the Cloud Road, a fact Eliot, ever considerate, hesitated to mention. His kindness prevented him from extinguishing Zane's evident enthusiasm with such disheartening truth.
One of the black-robed disciples, however, lacked such restraint. He leaned forward with a condescending smile playing across his lips. "You genuinely believe you can land a place in the Cloud Road, Zane?"
Zane locked eyes with him, his voice dropping to an icy timbre. "Yeah, I do. Do you have a problem with that?"
"Easy there," the disciple replied with a forced grin. Had Zane not already established himself as the premier talent among the inner disciples, the man would have laughed right in his face. Instead, he just offered a sarcastic smile, his eyes betraying his skepticism.
Sensing the mounting tension, Eliot intervened diplomatically. "With enough dedication and training," he remarked softly, "someone might accomplish what the rest of us thought was impossible."
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
Zane now had the lay of the land for the All Peaks Competition.
To win first prize, he would have to defeat not just every disciple from the Thirty-Three Peaks, but all the personal disciples, too. It was a hell of a mission.
[AMBIENT: NIGHT SKY]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE]
Later, Zane stood in the doorway of his room, looking up at the stars. His sister, Elara, had defeated so many powerful disciples on her own and led Maiden's Peak to a second-place finish. As her brother, he had to live up to that.
"The Draught of Clarity will be mine, and I will get into the Cloud Road."
[SFX: FIRE IGNITION]
His eyes blazed. A sacred fire of sheer determination burned inside him.
He had to do this. He didn't want to think about what would happen if he failed.
