Ep 290 - Aftershock
Word count: 1151
[MUSIC: EPIC WARRIOR THEME]
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
Every warrior knew the chilling power of Aura. It wasn’t just strength; it was the crushing weight of a fighter’s presence, an overwhelming force that could dominate an opponent before a single blow was even thrown. The true masters could project their Aura to terrifying distances, using it as a weapon all its own.
But power was all relative. The Aura that had just erupted from the Drake bone was immense, yes, but it shouldn’t have been enough to stagger someone like Master Elwood. After all, Elwood was leagues more powerful than Jacob. It was safe to assume Jacob himself could never have channeled an Aura that potent.
Who did, then? That question would have to wait. The immediate problem was that Master Elwood—the number one elder of the Skyreach Sect, second only to the head and vice head—wasn’t the only one rattled.
And if even he felt threatened, the less powerful warriors were in serious trouble.
The referee for the match had the good sense to flee long before Jacob even brandished the bone spear. He’d hastily retreated to what he believed was a secure distance, his heart pounding with each step that carried him from the arena’s center. Unfortunately, his calculation was woefully inadequate.
Though the ref was far from weak—having risen through the ranks with considerable skill—this particular fight had spiraled well beyond his capabilities. A cold sweat broke across his brow as he watched the confrontation unfold. He’d seen his fair share of danger, but he also knew that this match had transcended ordinary combat. He was no fool; he knew getting caught in the crossfire would be disastrous, and he wanted no part of it.
Something about this encounter stirred a primal fear he hadn’t felt since his earliest days as a disciple. Despite his veteran foresight and swift retreat, the referee couldn’t escape the devastating aftershock that followed.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS ROAR]
The thunderous roar hit him like a physical blow, a sledgehammer to the skull. A deafening hum flooded his mind, and his world went black as he crumpled to the ground.
Standing closest to the ring, he'd taken the brunt of it.
Master Elwood was already moving. His Vitality surged out in a cascade, and the moment it reached his arms, something strange happened — the deep lines of his weathered hands smoothed away, replaced by the hands of a young man. His cultivation ran toward life force, and under enough strain, it showed.
"Rise!" 
[SFX: ENERGY CURTAIN EXPANDING]
A curtain of green light snapped into place around the arena's perimeter. But the roar wasn't a physical force — it was soul pressure. The curtain could blunt it, not stop it. Even halved, it was still devastating.
The disciples watching from the stands weren’t faring much better.
Even those who'd recently reached the Soul Realm found themselves on the verge of collapse. They instinctively reached for their swords, their hands trembling with the effort to hold back, knowing their defensive shields would barely be enough to block the force of that roar.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
Sir Kenneth, head of the Skyreach Sect, descended from the high platform once more.
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
Despite his burly frame, he moved with an unnerving grace, stepping down as if walking on air, a grim general surveying a battlefield. His expression was more serious than ever, his mind churning.
"What was that? How could a single roar be so potent?" Sir Kenneth demanded, his usual calm indifference gone, replaced by a deep, furrowed concern.
Master Elwood shook his head, his voice tight with worry. "Jacob... he’s strong enough to wield the Drake Bone Spear to this degree. I’m afraid for Zane…" He trailed off, unable to finish the thought.
"No," Kenneth cut in, shaking his head. His mind was already racing past Elwood's conclusion. "No Drake is capable of releasing an Aura like that."
To him, Master Elwood’s analysis was too simple, and fundamentally flawed. He was assuming the roar came from the Drake bone, but Kenneth didn’t believe that for a second.
[SFX: DISTANT ROAR]
His disbelief wasn’t a slight against the power of Drakes. They were formidable. The Aura that had just rocked the arena was undeniably strong, terrifying to the younger disciples. But for warriors like Kenneth and the other elders, its raw power was hardly worth mentioning. A true Drake’s roar could have leveled the entire hilltop.
It wasn’t the strength of the roar that unsettled Kenneth. It was the feeling it carried.
There was something else layered within it—an ancient, profound presence. It felt like a higher being looking down on all of creation as if it were dust. A Drake could never project an Aura so otherworldly.
Drakes were powerful aquatic beasts, capable of summoning storms and floods, but they weren’t true Legendary Beasts. They were just distant relatives, creatures with a faint trace of dragon lineage.
What worried Sir Kenneth was that only one thing could create a presence this profound.
[MUSIC: EPIC REVELATION]
A TRUE DRAGON.
The idea was insane, but the feeling was unmistakable. It came from a Greater Legendary Beast.
"Not from a Drake?" Master Elwood’s eyes went wide with astonishment. "Are you suggesting... it came from a True Dragon?"
Kenneth nodded grimly. "That’s exactly what I’m thinking."
"Impossible!" Master Elwood blurted, forgetting his station for a moment. "True Dragons don’t exist! They’re legends, bedtime stories!"
The world was a vast place, filled with those who trained to reach the peak of martial arts and those who spun tales of mythical heroes and creatures. Years ago, a book called "A General Guide to Legendary Beasts" had become wildly popular, cementing such creatures as pure fantasy in the minds of many.
[AMBIENT: THICK CLOUD OF CORRUPT VITALITY]
For now, a thick cloud of Corrupt Vitality shrouded the fighting ring, obscuring everything from view. Not even Kenneth’s powerful spiritual sense could penetrate it. All he could do was wait for it to dissipate.
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC TOURNAMENT GROUNDS]
[SFX: PEOPLE SCRAMBLING]
The grounds of the All Peaks Competition, meanwhile, had devolved into chaos. Stewards scrambled through the crowd, carrying small bottles of soul-nourishing pills. They found disciples pale-faced and disoriented from the Aura’s impact and pressed pills into their hands. For those who had passed out completely, a single pill wasn’t enough. The stewards were forced to perform emergency first aid on the spot, turning the festive tournament into a frantic triage scene.
Master Warren had been hit by the roar, too. His head was still buzzing, a pounding headache making him unsteady on his feet.
But in that moment, he wasn’t worried about himself. He was terrified for Zane. A tiny sliver of Aura had leaked from the ring, been weakened by half by Master Elwood's green light curtain, and still managed to injure him this badly.
If he was this hurt all the way out here, what was happening to Zane, standing at ground zero?
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REALIZATION]
Master Warren tried not to think about it, but an answer forced its way into his mind, and his face went deathly pale.
