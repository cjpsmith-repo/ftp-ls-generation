The more he thought about it, the clearer it became: his only option was to use his secret weapon to take Zane down. Andrew's hand shot to his pocket, pulling out a small box.
[SFX: SMALL BOX BEING PULLED OUT]
It fit neatly in his palm, its surface etched with a trail of glittering symbols. He aimed the box at Zane, his thumb slamming the trigger on the back as he roared, "Go to hell!"
Zane's eyes widened the moment Andrew revealed the box. As the former heir of House Lowell, he knew exactly what it was: the Stormbreaker Halberd, one of the family's best defensive weapons.
A secret weapon, crafted from a Talisman. Inside, a powerful bolt of lightning was sealed, designed for an instant kill in a surprise attack. But it was a one-shot deal—and incredibly expensive. That's why they were only ever given to the younger members of House Lowell for personal protection.
"Andrew, you twisted, vicious bastard," Zane thought. "To use something like that just because you're cornered."
[SFX: ELECTRICAL CHARGING]
No matter how tough he was, a direct hit from the Stormbreaker Halberd was a death sentence. Just as the thought hit him, a spear of pure lightning—a Stormbreaker Halberd—erupted from the device, screaming toward him.
[SFX: LIGHTNING BOLT]
At the last possible second, Zane threw himself to the ground, rolling hard.
[SFX: BODY HITTING THE GROUND]
[SFX: DEAFENING CRACK]
The lightning bolt shot over his head with a deafening CRACK, singeing the ends of his hair. A scream ripped through the air, but it wasn't his.
[SFX: HUMAN SCREAM]
[SFX: ELECTRICAL EXPLOSION]
The Stormbreaker Halberd had struck a slave standing just outside the arena. He was instantly engulfed in blinding white fire, turning to ash in the blink of an eye. A wave of shock and horror washed over the crowd. Andrew had gone too far this time. That blast could have hit anyone. What if it had been a Lowell child instead of a slave?
It was terrifyingly clear: Andrew didn't give a damn about anyone in House Lowell but himself.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]
Zane rose to his feet, his face an unreadable mask of cold fury. He slowly clenched his fist. "You've had your fun, Andrew. Are you satisfied? Because now... It's my turn."
[SFX: DESPERATE SCREAM]
"No!" Owen Lowell screamed from the high stands, seeing the sudden shift of power in the arena. He launched himself over the railing, a sudden, chilling realization dawning on him: there was something wrong with Zane. Something impossibly wrong. It had to be a conspiracy; it was the only way Zane could still be alive. But for all his speed, Owen was too far from the arena floor.
[SFX: SWIFT MOVEMENT]
The time it would take Owen to reach them was more than enough. Zane was on Andrew in a flash.
"Shadow Fist!"
[SFX: SERIES OF IMPACT SOUNDS]
Before Andrew could even register the attack, Zane's fist struck home, landing on seven key points across his body in a single, fluid motion. A series of sickening, muffled pops echoed from within him.
Immediately, blood began to pour from seven spots on Andrew's body. Zane had targeted the joints where his arms and legs met his torso, along with other vital points. The injuries weren't fatal, but they would cripple him, guaranteeing he could never cultivate his power again.
Just one punch. With a single attack, the arrogant second son of House Lowell was utterly defeated.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
A stunned silence fell over the arena.
Who would have thought that the slave they'd been kicking around for two years had that kind of power? A cold shiver ran down the spines of the Lowell kids who had tormented Zane. They'd always seen him as weak, as someone they could bully without a second thought. They never imagined he was hiding this much strength.
"He beat Andrew with just one punch," they thought, a cold sweat breaking out. "What if he comes after me next?"
Andrew's face was a mask of pure disbelief. He'd seen Zane as a toddler in comparison to his own strength at the Bone Refining Level. He'd been so sure crushing him would be child's play. How could he have been so wrong? He had the Aether Crystal; his training speed was supposed to be leagues beyond Zane's. Andrew's legs gave out, and he crumpled to the ground.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
Owen rushed to his side and scooped him into his arms. "Andrew, my son! Are you alright?"
"I didn't hit any vital organs. He'll live," Zane mumbled.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Owen looked up, his normally refined face twisted with fury. He pointed a trembling finger at Zane. "You worthless bastard!" he snarled. "Why would you do this to my son?"
"Uncle Owen," Zane's voice was steady, cutting through the tension. "Everyone here knows the risks of a Fatal Duel. We were meant to fight to the death. You, of all people, should remember how many slaves have injured Lowell children in this very arena before!" He took a breath, his voice ringing out. "Now that I've defeated Andrew Lowell, according to the House Code, I am no longer a slave. I am free to leave House Lowell!"
"Ha, ha, ha. I was kind enough to let you live all these years, and I never expected you to get this strong." Owen paused, helping a dazed Andrew back to his feet before letting out a cold, humorless laugh. "You think I'm just going to let you walk away? Don't even think about it!"
Zane's face fell. "Uncle Owen, the House Code is clear. If I win this fight, I earn my freedom."
[SFX: COLD LAUGHTER]
"The Code?" Owen's anger melted into a cold, mocking sneer. "Only your worthless father cared about the House Code. Let me make something clear to you, boy. I make the rules in House Lowell now. Me!"
Zane's face contorted with rage at his uncle's words. For years, he had dreamed of killing the man standing before him for what he'd done to his family. But he had always forced himself to be patient, to wait for the right moment. He knew he wasn't strong enough to avenge his father. To do that, Zane needed to leave House Lowell, to train, to have time to get stronger.
He had pinned all his hopes on Family Training Day, on winning the Fatal Duel, and finally claiming his freedom.
It had never even occurred to him that Owen Lowell would cheat him like this, at the one moment that mattered most. After everything he had endured, his uncle's cruel words shattered his last sliver of hope.
The last two years of suffering, of biting his tongue and enduring every humiliation, flashed before his eyes.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
A laugh bubbled up in his chest—a bitter, broken sound. "I guess I'm the fool. It's ridiculous... I actually believed a piece of trash like you would follow the rules. I was a fool to think you'd show me any mercy, that you'd remember I'm family. You murdered your own brother, seized control of the House, and stole the Miracle Cure for yourself. You've committed every crime imaginable. You're not even human! You're a monster! And I swear to you, one day, I will cut off your head with my own two hands and get justice for my father!"
