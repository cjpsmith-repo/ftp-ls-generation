Ep 789 - Show Me What You've Got

Word count: 1125

[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUND]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

 A heavy thud echoed as the warrior was thrown clear of the red circle, hitting the ground hard.

Physically, he was fine. For a fighter of his caliber, a fall like that was nothing. The real damage was to his pride. Being carried off on a stretcher would have been less embarrassing. This? Getting tossed out like a piece of trash? This was pure humiliation.

Still, he managed to pull himself together as he got to his feet. Warriors from the divine kingdoms knew a thing or two more than those from the Heartlands, and he recognized Zane’s speed for what it was: nothing short of incredible. The distance, the angle, the timing—it was all flawless. He’d been gathering his own strength for an attack, which was exactly why he couldn’t react in time to stop Zane from closing the gap.

There was only one way to explain a move like that. Zane’s reflexes and judgment were razor-sharp, the kind of instinct forged in countless battles. A guy like that was genuinely terrifying.

With that thought, the warrior took a deep breath and watched Zane, the anger in his eyes replaced by a mix of awe and fear. Anyone who had faced Zane’s power firsthand knew just how frightening he was.

The rest of the crowd was just as shocked.

Even though the warrior had been so unceremoniously dumped from the circle, he was still a powerful Third Tier member. No one was about to start laughing at him. Most were just stunned that he was out at all, because none of them had even seen what Zane did. The man was simply too fast.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

 Meanwhile, the three powerful warriors from the Soul Sea Realm watched with grim expressions.

[AMBIENT: CROWD CHATTER]

 "I underestimated that kid," one of them said. "With that kind of strength, there's no way he's just a Third Tier member."

"I'm sure of it," another agreed. "He has the power to reach the Second Tier."

"Unless I'm mistaken," the third mused, "he just used raw physical force. Is it possible he's trained in both Body Refining and Vitality?"

It was rare to find warriors who trained in both disciplines, whether on the main continent, in the Heartlands, or even in the divine kingdoms.

"And he's an independent warrior," the first master added. "Think about what that means. The two sixth-rank houses funnel every resource the kingdom has into their geniuses — pills, techniques, cultivation chambers, decades of careful training. Those geniuses are the product of everything the kingdom can offer. This kid had none of that. No house, no backing, no resources beyond whatever he scraped together on his own. And he's standing at the top of the second tier anyway."

They'd seen flashes of brilliance from independents before. But this was different. In recent decades, something had shifted, a tide that had caused an explosion of talent across all four kingdoms. A kingdom that once produced ten or fifteen geniuses in a generation was now producing over a hundred. 

The first tier had kept pace — four warriors who could crush every other genius without effort. Everyone else was fighting over what remained. For an unknown independent to claw his way to the top of the second tier in the middle of all that was genuinely remarkable.

 Zane, for his part, had just wanted to end the fight quickly. If it dragged on, he’d be forced to reveal more of his abilities, and he wanted to keep his cards close to his chest for as long as possible.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

From across the circle, Randy's eyes were still on him. He was certain of it now — Zane had been holding back the entire round. The throw, the spatial step, the instincts — all of it was real, but none of it was his ceiling. A housed genius at the top of the second tier would have been impressive enough. An independent warrior at that level, still concealing his true strength, was something else entirely. Randy wanted to see the rest of it. A slow smile crossed his face. "Not enough," he said quietly. "That's not your full strength. Show me what you've really got."

Zane could only sigh inwardly. He'd been naïve. The warriors here had far exceeded what he'd expected. Slipping through unnoticed was no longer an option. And he hadn't forgotten what Randy had said before the round started: only one person was walking out with a token.

On the other side of the circle, the other two fighters had finished. One blasted the other clean out of the shrinking ring. By rights, the round was over — three survivors, results to be announced.

Randy glanced at the three Soul Sea masters. A small look, nothing more. They understood. They said nothing. They wanted to see it too.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

 The last remaining warrior fumed to himself. This is bullshit! Lord Randy is taking this way too far. I gave everything I had to pass this test, and now he’s just going to break the rules and kick me out?

[SFX: TICKING CLOCK]

There wasn’t much time left. From the moment the red circle began to shrink, the clock had been set for only three hundred seconds. The initial confrontation and Randy’s little speech had already eaten up a hundred and thirty-five of them.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 "I already passed the test! Why are you—" the warrior protested, desperate to reason with him. The last thing he wanted was to actually fight Randy.

"Shut up," Randy snapped. "I'm not repeating myself."

He stood in the center of the shrinking circle, with Zane and the other warrior on either side. As the ring of light closed in, it forced them all closer to him.

Sweat beaded on the warrior’s forehead as he watched the distance between him and Randy disappear. He gritted his teeth, the injustice of it all churning in his gut. But one look at the Lord standing just a few feet away told him everything he needed to know. He was no match for him. If he fought, he’d just get humiliated and badly hurt, not to mention the trouble it would cause him later.

[SFX: GRINDING TEETH]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

 Finally, with another grinding of his teeth, he just stood there. The red circle kept shrinking, its glowing edge moving silently past his feet.

[SFX: DEFEAT SOUND]

 He was out.

Many of the onlookers watched with pity. This was a brutal test with a ninety-percent failure rate. This warrior had made it through, only to have Randy force him to quit anyway. 

It was a total shame.
