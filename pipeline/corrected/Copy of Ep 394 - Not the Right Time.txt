[AMBIENT: DEEP CAVERN]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

Zane had come all this way for two things: the crystal key and the Drake spirit. Deep within him, the Cobalt Dragon stirred, its hunger a hot, impatient coil in his gut.

Either this Drake spirit is brave, or it’s just stupid, the Cobalt Dragon chuckled, its voice rumbling through Zane’s mind. It’s so eager to make a home in your body… might as well let it. I’ll be sure to give it a warm welcome.

The arrogant voice echoed with a low rumble in his head. Zane went perfectly still, opening himself up. 

[SFX: WHOOSH OF ENERGY]

The Drake spirit shot through him like a bolt of white-hot lightning.

[MUSIC: SINISTER STRINGS]

"Ha! Way too easy! What kind of fool faces me with no defenses?" The Drake spirit gloated inside his head. "Your body is mine now, and I won’t leave a single scrap of your soul behind! It's a fine vessel—strong, solid. I'll savor tearing it apart, muscle by muscle, until nothing of you remains. Then I’ll claim the ancient refining arts, the treasures of the Sixth-Tier Sect. No one can stop me. I will be Emperor! The one true master of this world!"

Sealed away for ages, its first taste of freedom had driven the spirit mad. When it found no resistance in Zane’s mind, its arrogance swelled.

[SFX: MANIACAL LAUGHTER ECHO]

A wild, piercing laugh echoed, threatening to shatter Zane’s consciousness.

This was the real danger. The Drake's soul was immensely powerful, far stronger than any human's. One wrong move, and Zane's own soul would be ripped from his body, leaving it an empty shell for the dragon to claim. It was a mismatch of power, an invasion a weaker spirit couldn't possibly hope to fend off. The Drake howled in triumph as it settled into its new home.

[MUSIC: SUDDEN SILENCE]

[SFX: HEARTBEAT]

But its victory cry died in its throat.

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

[MUSIC: EPIC CHOIR]

The first thing the spirit saw inside Zane's mind was his own Soulforge, its embers smoldering with latent power. And coiled around it were nine other dragons, so vivid and lifelike they felt terrifyingly real.

[SFX: GULP / SWALLOW]
The Drake spirit's laughter stopped. Then it was gone.
Zane looked up. The crystal key hung above the chamber, snagged on nothing, suspended in the dark where the Drake spirit had left it. Tia was already moving — she leaped, snatched it clean out of the air, and landed without breaking stride.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

The Fiery Dragon Spirit was a monster of pure power. Tia had barely held her own against it when it was smaller; now, it was enormous. Its gaze locked onto the crystal key in Tia’s hand. 

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

With a savage roar, it lunged, its entire focus on the key.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

[AMBIENT: ARCTIC WIND]

"Stay back, Zane!" Tia warned, her voice sharp. Her expression hardened as she braced for the attack. Spreading her arms, she wove the air between her hands into a column of ice as tall as a person. Trapped at its core, a single, tiny feather from an Ice Phoenix gleamed.

A real one.

Unlike the fleeting projections most warriors could summon, the Ice Phoenix Tia had conjured earlier was different. Through sheer force of will, she had meditated the spiritual creature into true, physical existence—a rare feat that created a living familiar far more powerful than any illusion. The Ice Phoenix itself was a pale shadow of a true legendary beast, but its feather… a single, real feather held catastrophic power.

[SFX: ICE BLAST]

[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND]

Tia slammed a hand onto the ice column. An arctic blast shot from it, weaving curtains of frost through the air. In the blink of an eye, a tidal wave of solid ice surged toward the Fiery Dragon Spirit.

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

[SFX: FLAMES ROARING]

As guardian of the crystal key, the Fiery Dragon Spirit was relentless. It ignored the stinging cold, held its ground, and unleashed a torrent of fire from its massive maw. The ice wall hissed and melted under the red-hot flames. 

[SFX: ICE SHATTERING]

The barrier thinned with every passing second until the dragon charged, shattering it into a million glittering shards.

[SFX: FIRE BLAST]

[SFX: ICE CRACKLING]

The battle was a brutal stalemate. The dragon breathed torrents of flame, and Tia worked feverishly, pulling endless curtains of frost from the ice column to meet them. Neither was willing to give an inch.

"Damn, it's freezing," Zane shivered, goosebumps prickling his skin. He wrapped his arms around his chest, uselessly trying to stay warm. The temperature had plummeted, turning the world into a blur of white. His breath plumed in front of him like a frozen cloud.

[MUSIC: ROMANTIC TENSION]

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FLAMES]

But when his eyes landed on Tia, a strange heat washed over him. The world seemed to blur at the edges, until all he could see, all he could hear, was her. 

The dragon’s flames flickered across her face, casting dancing shadows that bathed her in an otherworldly glow. She was a vision. Strands of sweat-damp hair clung to her cheeks. A suit of shimmering ice armor now encased her, fitting her like a second skin, the rigid plates clinging perfectly to the graceful lines of her body. The sight made Zane’s own breath catch in his throat.

His heart started to pound in his chest. He could hear his own ragged breathing, and despite the arctic air, his skin felt like it was on fire. A hot flush of confusion and embarrassment washed over him. What is wrong with me? This was the absolute worst time for... whatever this was. He tried to shake it off, to focus, but his gaze was helplessly drawn back to her.
His hand moved before his mind caught up. He reached out and took hers.
Tia's palm was warm — startlingly so in the freezing air, damp with sweat. Her fingers curled around his and didn't pull away.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Tia felt his eyes on her, a stare that burned hotter than the dragon’s flames.

[SFX: MYSTICAL ECHO]

The Cobalt Dragon’s voice dripped with amusement. I did warn you, Drakes are creatures of desire. This space you're in is the Drake's Essence Chamber. That yellow smog you saw? A potent, all-natural aphrodisiac.

The Chamber naturally stirs desire in any living thing, the dragon continued. Some even consider it a sacred substance. There are martial arts practices where intimacy is the key to unlocking greater power. For them, this stuff is the ultimate ingredient.

Even as a puppet, the chamber remained intact. The spirit retreated into it, and Tia sliced it right open, releasing that powder into the air. A tiny pinch of that mist would sell for a king's ransom back in The Heartlands, you know.

[SFX: SHOCKED GASP]

Zane sputtered, his eyes wide. "An aphrodisiac? Wait, I'm supposed to be immune to all poisons!"

It's an aphrodisiac, not a poison, genius. Of course you can't resist it, the Cobalt Dragon replied dryly.

Zane let out a shaky breath. He was lucky the Cobalt Dragon had snapped him out of it. He never would have been able to fight that feeling on his own.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

He looked up and saw Tia walking toward him, dazed. Her arms were outstretched, her eyes glazed over with a strange, unfocused light.

A cold dread washed over him as he realized what was happening. He wasn't the only one. Tia had been right in the thick of it when that mist exploded. 

From the look on her face, she’d inhaled a lot more of it than he had.
