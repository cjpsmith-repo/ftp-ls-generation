Ep 704 - Hunter and Hunted
Word count: 1053
[AMBIENT: SWAMP ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Gray-scaled crocodiles lay in wait just beneath the murky water. They were called Orange-Tooth Crocodiles, and while their hides were valuable enough to craft Spirit Weapons, few warriors were brave enough to hunt them. The things were just too fierce, their hides ridiculously tough. Even warriors at the Inner-Core Realm struggled to take one down, and since the hide was the only real prize, the risk just wasn’t worth the reward.
Right now, over a hundred of them were closing in on Zane, and he had no idea.
To stay hidden from the hunter tracking them, he’d suppressed his Vitality, which meant he couldn't extend his perception. Unfortunately, that also meant he couldn't sense the crocodiles creeping up on him and Cora as they moved through the swamp. All he could see were faint ripples in the water, drawing closer by the second.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE RUSTLING]
All of a sudden, an Orange-Tooth Crocodile exploded from the surface, its massive jaws snapping straight for Cora.
The creatures were masters of camouflage; one moment they looked like a piece of rotting wood, the next they were launching at you with lightning speed.
Hearing the splash behind her, Cora spun around and came face-to-face with an enormous mouth lined with two rows of sharp, orange teeth that looked like they could chew through solid iron. She froze. Warriors from her realm weren’t typically scared of these beasts, but Cora wasn’t a warrior. She had no combat training, no idea how to fight something like this.
[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION DRUMS]
[SFX: RUSHING WATER]
Suddenly, Zane was there. He skimmed across the water, launched himself into the air, and stomped down hard on the crocodile’s lower jaw just as its upper jaw snapped shut, inches from his leg. Without missing a beat, he slammed his fist into the creature’s snout.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
The punch ripped the crocodile’s mouth apart, and bright red blood gushed into the water. As the beast thrashed in agony, Cora screamed.
Six more crocodiles had just surfaced around her, their eyes locked on what they clearly thought was an easy meal.
A flicker of annoyance crossed Zane’s face. The crocodiles themselves weren't a threat; their jaws weren't even big enough to swallow him whole. But killing all six at once would be tricky. He could unleash his Vitality and wipe them out in an instant, but the resulting energy surge would be a flare in the dark, leading the warrior from Maddox’s divine kingdom right to them.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
He made his decision. He let just enough Vitality flow to grab Cora and move — low and fast, skimming the surface. The crocodiles churned after them, but they couldn't fly. By the time the water settled behind him, they'd cleared the pool entirely. He cut his Vitality the moment they hit solid ground.
Too late.
Far across the marsh, Simon's eyes snapped open. A slow, predatory smile spread across his face as he sensed the telltale surge of energy. His years of hunting had honed his instincts to a razor's edge—he knew the signature of desperate power when he felt it.
"Found you," he murmured, savoring the moment. "Southeast—near the crocodile pool."
He raised his voice just enough for his warriors to hear. "Move. Watch for the Orange-Tooth Crocodiles in the water."
[SFX: WHOOSH]
Several streaks of light peeled off through the fog, converging on the southeast at speed. The warriors moved with practiced efficiency, their eagerness palpable in the air.
Simon followed at a more measured pace, deliberately unhurried. Let the others rush ahead like eager hounds. He had his own reasons for wanting to arrive before Maddox.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SWAMP]
[MUSIC: TENSE DRONE]
Zane and Cora pushed on through the tall grass. Then Zane felt it — a flicker of Vitality behind them. He grabbed Cora and pulled her flat into the undergrowth just as a streak of light blazed overhead and vanished into the fog. A Spirit-Tribulation warrior from Maddox's divine kingdom. The man had already passed them. Zane waited, watching the fog close back around the trail.
He was about to move when Cora went rigid beside him. A small spider was making its way up her arm — a Ghost-Face Spider, named for the unsettling pattern on its back. Harmless. But Cora didn't know that. Her mouth opened. Zane's hand was already moving — a half-second too late.
[SFX: STIFLED SCREAM]
The cry was brief and sharp. Zane flicked the spider away and pressed them both deeper into the grass.
Cora’s scream, though brief, echoed faintly across the swamp. It was just enough to make the hunter pause.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SWAMP]
[MUSIC: TENSE DRONE]
He couldn’t be sure if he’d heard a woman’s cry or just the shriek of a strange bird, but it was enough to make him suspicious. He decided to do one last, careful sweep of the area.
Hovering in the air, the Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior scanned the ground below, his perception combing through the swamp and the tall grass inch by inch. He was getting closer.
Watching him drift nearer, Zane pulled Cora tighter against him. His eyes were calm, but every muscle in his body was coiled tight. If the man kept being this thorough, he was going to find them. A cold thought settled in Zane’s mind: the hunter had better hope he was lucky enough to miss them. If he didn't, he was in for a world of hurt.
If the warrior found him, Zane had only one option left: kill him.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The man had no idea he was flying toward his own doom. From his perspective, he was hunting a fledgling Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior traveling with a weak Inner-Core Realm girl. Taking them down would be easy, and capturing Zane would be a huge feather in his cap. Just like in any other order, competition among the warriors of the divine kingdom was fierce. Standing out meant more rewards, especially as Maddox’s power and influence grew.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
Five hundred feet.
Three hundred feet.
Two hundred feet.
One hundred feet.
[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]
At this distance, Zane wouldn't even need his Vitality. He knew with absolute certainty he could kill the man in a single strike with his bare hands.
