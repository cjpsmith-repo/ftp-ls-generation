Ep 800 - Rival Factions

Word count: 1176

[AMBIENT: LARGE CROWD GATHERING]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

Scott’s voice boomed across the square. “Hah! Warriors of the Star Field Divine Kingdom, let’s see what you’re made of!”
As emperor, he led his contingent to the highest platform, his princes and princesses—all formidable warriors in the Life-and-Death and Spirit-Tribulation Realms—trailing in his wake.

[SFX: WHOOSHING COMET]

Just as Scott’s group settled, a brilliant blue streak tore through the sky, shooting down like a comet and leaving a long, shimmering trail. As the light drew closer, Zane saw its true form: a colossal sword, magnified to an impossible size.

“The emperor of the York Divine Kingdom is here!” someone in the crowd shouted, recognizing the signature weapon.

[SFX: SWORD LANDING]

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

 Sure enough, the massive blade halted over the riverbank, and from it emerged a host of powerful warriors from the Soul Sea Realm, followed by hundreds more.

The same sharp voice that had announced Scott now declared, “War King York of the York Divine Kingdom has arrived!”

Where Scott was boisterous, War King York was a figure of grim silence. He flew across the square without a word, leading his people directly to their designated platform.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

 As Zane watched them pass, his eyes caught on a woman just behind the War King, her face obscured by a veil. A jolt went through him, and he instinctively shrank back into the anonymity of the crowd.

It was the Holy Maiden of the York Divine Kingdom.

 Zane had no idea what her standing was now, but if she spotted him, he knew the powerful Soul Sea Realm warriors at her emperor’s side would be on him in an instant. The thought was terrifying; both Scott and War King York were masters of the Soul Sea Realm, a level of power that was the stuff of legends.

Two more arrivals followed in quick succession. The Heavenly Wind War King came next, then the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom — a man whose face Zane hadn't seen until now. Behind the Emperor, among his entourage, Zane spotted Randy. And beside Randy, in his wheelchair, the Crown Prince.

Once all four rulers had taken their places, the sharp voice rang out again: "All four Divine Kingdoms are present. Per established practice, we will now determine the order of entry."

The square fell quiet. Zane pieced together what he hadn't known before. Historically, the kingdoms' rulers had nearly come to blows over who entered the Sacred Feather Sea first — Soul Sea masters fighting over a treasure trove was a disaster waiting to happen. So they'd devised this instead: let the young generation settle it. Whoever's warriors performed best determined their kingdom's entry order. 

Over time, it had grown into something far larger than a formality. Observers tracked the results year after year, using them to predict each kingdom's future trajectory.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

[AMBIENT: EXPECTANT CROWD SILENCE]

 A young man broke from the crowd and stepped forward, bowing toward the platform. “Your Majesty, I am Titus, representing the Wisteria Martial House.”

Scott, the emperor of the Star Field Divine Kingdom, clearly knew of the young warrior’s reputation and smiled with pride. “Good! You will fight today. Now, who wishes to challenge this young man?”

[SFX: WHOOSH OF LIGHT BEAM]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

 His words had barely faded when a beam of light flashed, and another warrior shot toward the foot of the platform. He was tall and powerfully built, clearly from the York Divine Kingdom.

“I, Logan from the Shadow Martial House, accept the challenge.”

A faint, hard smile touched War King York’s lips. Finally, one of his own had stepped up. “Logan,” he said, his voice a low rumble, “they say you are invincible among those in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. Do not let me down.”

“My invincibility is just a rumor, Your Majesty,” Logan replied, his tone humble but his eyes blazing with a confidence that bordered on arrogance. He scanned the square as if the other Spirit-Tribulation warriors were nothing more than insects.

[AMBIENT: LARGE CROWD ROAR]

[SFX: ENERGY BARRIER FORMING]

 With the fighters chosen, the battle was set to begin. A roar went up from the crowd as two warriors from the Soul Sea Realm descended from the platform. With a wave of their hands, they wove thin lines of energy into a shimmering, enchanted barrier that encapsulated the square, sealing the arena. A barrier like that, forged by Soul Sea masters, wasn't something a Spirit-Tribulation warrior could ever hope to break.

The square immediately erupted.

[SFX: LOUD CROWD CHATTER]

"That guy claims to be invincible below the Spirit-Tribulation Realm? Sounds like the York Divine Kingdom has gotten good at making things up!"

"Brother Titus will put him in his place — just watch."

"Brother Logan will knock his teeth out! Every last one!"

Warriors from the Star Field and York kingdoms were nearly at each other's throats already, faces flushed, voices rising. 

Zane just shook his head. These were Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors — exceptional by any standard — and they were bickering like children. Back in the Heartlands, a Spirit-Tribulation warrior was typically over a hundred years old, hardened by decades of grinding cultivation. The ones who'd come through the Tower of Sin had been forged in genuine life-and-death conditions. These warriors had grown up in Martial Houses, pampered and pushed but never truly broken. They were young in every sense of the word.

The Soul Sea masters on the platform didn't even glance at them. They'd seen this every year.

Zane found his attention drifting. His only goal was getting into The Sacred Feather Sea, but for now, all he could do was wait for a winner to be declared.

He figured it wouldn’t last long, anyway. The Harlen Divine Kingdom only had four First Tier warriors—the crown prince, Lord Randy, Edwin, and Gregory. Even if the Star Field and York kingdoms were a bit stronger, they couldn’t have many more. Six or seven, at most. This whole event was an annual formality, and the Soul Sea emperors, hardened by countless Life-and-Death Tribulations, weren’t about to be drawn into petty squabbles. Their focus was entirely on the fight unfolding in the air.

[SFX: FLAMES WHOOSHING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

Titus moved first, his body wreathed in a refined, destructive flame—he’d mastered the third layer of the Fire Law, and the annihilating power it contained was no joke. Zane could tell the man’s strength was considerable.

[SFX: SPATIAL DISTORTION]

 But his opponent, Logan, was on another level. Space itself seemed to twist and warp around his body, forming an invisible shield that deflected every lick of flame. Zane watched, fascinated. He’s using Spatial Power to create layers of space as armor.

The realization hit him like a physical blow. That was the third layer of the Spatial Law—a concept so advanced that even Zane hadn’t begun to grasp it. It was true, then. 

The Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms ran deeper and held more power than he could have imagined.
