Ep 785 - The Red Circle

Word count: 993

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]

 The second the Spirit-Tribulation warriors stepped into the red circle, they made a mad dash for the center. The circle itself was only a few hundred feet across—an easy leap for any of them—so from the moment they entered, it was a scramble to claim the prime spot in the middle and shove everyone else out.

“Get back! This spot is mine!” a warrior built like a gorilla roared, planting himself right in the bullseye. He was the first to reach it, and as the other fighters glared at him with open jealousy, he just stood his ground, chin held high. 

He was at the peak of the late Spirit-Tribulation Realm, his temples bulging and radiating a heavy aura that screamed of some seriously aggressive training. Because of that, nobody dared to challenge him for the center.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

 Just as the thirty of them settled into their spots, a master from the Soul Sea Realm appeared in the air above them. “The first round begins now!”

[SFX: ENERGY EXPLOSION]

 The instant he finished speaking, a wave of raw Vitality exploded from every warrior at once.

Zane's brow furrowed, a flicker of concern on his face. He'd noticed something strange: the immense power erupting from the fighters wasn't dissipating. It was trapped inside the red circle. But what really confused him was that none of the other several hundred warriors in the audience seemed surprised. 

He thought about it for a moment, and it clicked — it had to be the floor. The patterns, the metal, the circle itself. Without that containment, thirty Spirit-Tribulation warriors going all out could level the entire Martial House in seconds. The Divine Kingdoms had been doing this for thousands of years. Of course, they'd figured out how to build an arena that could hold it.

"That guy in the center is out of luck," Zane said quietly.

The warriors nearest him turned with looks of open contempt. Clearly, this newcomer had no idea who he was looking at. That independent cultivator had a formidable reputation — trained both energy and body, technique more overbearing than most. As far as anyone watching was concerned, his token was already secured.

SFX: POWERFUL BLAST]

 In the sudden chaos, the gorilla-like warrior who had so boldly claimed the center was immediately blasted out of the fighting area.

[SFX: COUGHING]

[SFX: BLOOD SPLATTER]

 He stumbled, spitting a mouthful of blood onto the ground as he glared back at the men who had just attacked him.

Not even a few seconds into the round, the most promising contender was already out. 

[SFX: SUDDEN IMPACT]

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMUR]

 A ripple of shock went through the spectators who’d pegged him as a shoo-in. The three powerful masters from the Soul Sea Realm, however, just exchanged faint, knowing smiles. This was exactly what they’d expected.

The warriors who had scoffed at Zane’s earlier prediction glanced back at him, their faces burning with shame. Their rock-solid confidence from a moment ago was gone, replaced by the dawning thought: This guy really knew what he was talking about.

Among everyone present, only the three masters and Zane had seen it coming. They understood that, besides raw strength, wisdom was the real key to winning this thing. It was an unspoken rule, but it was the most important one.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRATEGIC THEME]

 Out of thirty fighters in the circle, only three would be left standing. With those odds, nobody wanted to be the first one eliminated, and a lot of them had the same idea: take out the strongest guy first. The moment they’d stepped into the ring, everyone had sized up their opponents. The thinking was simple: You look weaker than me? You’re not a threat. Someone else will knock you out, or I’ll deal with you later when it’s just a few of us left. But that guy? The arrogant one in the middle? He’s a problem.

From the very beginning, their goal wasn’t to weed out the weak but to eliminate the strong. And who was the first to go? The one who looked the strongest, of course. 

The arrogant moron who dared to grab the center sealed his own fate the moment he stood there. The warriors ganged up on him out of pure survival instinct. This test wasn't about finding the single strongest fighter; it was about finding the smartest decision-makers, the ones who could make the most of what they had.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

No one drew weapons — the no-kill rule held — but that didn't make it gentle. The battle that followed was chaos. Warriors blasted each other across the circle, some knocked unconscious and dragged past the red line, others retreating voluntarily rather than absorbing a combined strike. 

The opening minutes were brutal — eliminations coming one after another, some fighters launched clean out in a single hit, others were worn down by two or three opponents working in tandem and then shoved over the line while they were still catching their breath. The circle was so small that there was nowhere to hide. Every exchange was immediate, every mistake punished instantly.

Then the pace changed. Ten or so warriors remained, circling carefully, each one watching for an opening and guarding against being caught off balance. Fast as shadows, but striking rarely. Every move calculated.

When it came down to six, the fighting nearly stopped altogether. Three spots. Six fighters. Even odds. No one wanted to overcommit.

But the Crystal Martial House had a way to force their hand.

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

 Suddenly, the red circle trembled and began to shrink, slowly closing in on the center. It was a gradual squeeze, giving the warriors inside just enough time to panic. The fighting area was getting smaller and smaller, and they knew they had to make a move.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACTS]

The first fighter to lose his nerve and rush in was the first to be tossed out. Right after him, two others were knocked out of the ring in quick succession.

[MUSIC: VICTORIOUS HORNS]

 Just like that, the battle was over. The last three warriors standing were the winners of the first round.
