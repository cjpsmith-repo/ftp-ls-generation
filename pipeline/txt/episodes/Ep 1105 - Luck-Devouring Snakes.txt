Ep 1105 - Luck-Devouring Snakes

Word count: 1132

[AMBIENT: PALACE HALL]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The palace leaders all turned to the Lord of the Sunset World, their faces a mask of confusion. One of them finally broke the silence. “What is going on?”
[SFX: OMINOUS HISS]
The Lord of the Sunset World’s eyes were glued to the golden serpents. “Luck-Devouring Snakes,” he hissed, the name itself a curse.
A wave of disbelief rippled through the leaders.
“Luck-Devouring Snakes? I thought they were just a myth.”
“That’s a trial from the age of gods. How could a warrior in the Life-and-Death Realm possibly trigger something like that?”
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
The Lord of the Sunset World’s words triggered recognition. Warriors from the hundred thousand supreme worlds understood fate better than anyone from the lower realms. Their entire purpose was to defy natural law, rewrite destiny, and achieve immortality.
Normal people lived and died in decades. A warrior’s lifespan shattered that limit, and for this defiance, the heavens demanded a price.
Beyond longevity, warriors struggled against cosmic design itself. Where ordinary men faced heavy buckets or poisoned wine, warriors confronted unbreakable mountains, unbeatable opponents, and tribulations that could annihilate them instantly. Breaking free from the cosmic cycle meant true freedom. Failure meant reincarnation - a lord in one life, dust in the next.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHIMES]
And that’s why the lotus flower was so important. It was a measure of a warrior’s luck, their potential to shake off the heavens’ control. The more petals, the stronger their fortune. It wasn’t a symbol of talent, but in the Upper World, it was considered far more valuable.
[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]
[SFX: SNAKE HISSING]
Seeing Zane, a man of clearly immense luck, now swarmed by snakes that devoured that very same luck… a knot of worry tightened in the leaders’ chests. A warrior had to face their tribulation alone. No one could interfere.
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL WHISPERS]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
The Lord of the Sunset World’s gaze settled on the lotus floating above Zane’s head—twenty-six petals, almost perfect—and a question slipped out before he could stop it. “What kind of fate does this one carry, that the heavens would send something like this down on him?”
Yolande answered without hesitation. “Zane has two Life Providences. His Battle of the Era providence—strictly speaking, that one’s a fake. His fate was never going to be something anyone else gets to control.”
The Lord of the Sunset World’s eyes sharpened with something between surprise and unease. “Then I’ll need to send word to Gossamer Pavilion.”
“Let’s wait,” Yolande said, a faint smile touching her lips. “At least until he’s through this.” Gossamer Pavilion would be a fine home for Zane—eventually. But he’d already inherited far too much to simply be handed off before he’d even survived the day.
“But can he survive it?” the Lord of the Sunset World asked, the worry plain on his face now. “The Luck-Devouring Snakes aren’t like other tribulations. They don’t test strength. They take providence itself—and providence is everything. A warrior with strong luck can turn disaster into fortune, walk through danger untouched. A warrior with weak luck finds killing intent in every direction, and every step becomes a fight. If these snakes strip Zane’s providence away...”
[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]
Up above, Zane watched the golden snakes drift through the air—slow, almost lazy—and felt a chill settle into his chest. They didn’t carry anything like the raw power of the lightning, fire, or nightmare-tribulations he’d already faced. But something about them set off every alarm he had. Whatever these things were after, his instincts screamed that it mattered more than any of that.
Then the snakes opened their mouths—and seemed to be eating something. Something he couldn’t see.
“It’s your providence.” Lavender’s voice slipped into his mind, calm and certain. “These are Luck-Devouring Snakes. They feed on it directly.”
Devouring his providence?
Zane went still. Cultivation methods, treasures, raw power—none of that was really what had carried him this far. It was his will, his talent, and his providence. Take any one of those away, and everything else stopped mattering. Every warrior who’d ever climbed high knew the same thing.
And his providence had always run strong—stronger than just his own life. People close to him tended to do well. Tia’s fortunes had turned the moment their paths crossed; so had others’. It wasn’t something he controlled; it just happened, like a tide that lifted everything nearby.
He thought, briefly, of the Wheel of Providence—the time his luck had seemed to curdle, every roll coming up worse than the last. That hadn’t been his providence failing. That had been someone else’s hand on the wheel, rigging the outcome no matter how strong his luck actually was.
This was different. This wasn’t someone cheating the system. Something was just... eating it. Bite by bite. And once it was gone, there’d be no getting it back.
That settled it. Zane moved—fast, without thinking twice. No one who realized something was stealing the very thing that had carried them this far would just sit there and watch it happen.
[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]
He drove straight at the nearest snake—and passed clean through it. The thing wasn’t even there. An illusion. A phantom with teeth.
To the warriors watching below, including Layla, the golden snakes looked almost gentle—nothing close to the tribulations she’d just fought through. They couldn’t understand what Zane was even doing.
The Lord of the Sunset World’s expression went grim. He’d assumed—hoped—Zane had some answer for this. Watching him swing at empty air and come up with nothing, that hope died.
The snakes didn’t react to Zane at all. But once he moved, they shifted, twisting lazily through the air to realign—and went right on feeding, as though he’d never been there.
“A Luck-Devouring Snake isn’t really a creature,” the Lord of the Sunset World said, his gaze fixed on Zane. “It’s something beyond life. I read about this once, in the old records. Ages ago, another warrior with incredible fortune attracted this same tribulation. He could do nothing but watch as the snakes drained his luck dry, completely unable to fight back.”
[MUSIC: SOMBER PIANO]
One of the palace leaders shook his head, a grim finality in his voice. “So, his struggle is pointless.”
“What a damn shame,” another sighed, his voice laced with genuine disappointment. “I was looking forward to seeing what a kid like that could become.” Even though Zane wasn’t from his palace, he had a soft spot for prodigies, and it stung to see one about to be extinguished.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]
Yolande, however, was a picture of calm. In her eyes, Zane was the man who always turned impossible situations into miracles. She’d seen him do it time and time again, and she still believed he could do it one more time.
