Ep 1000 - A Mad Rush To Ascend
Word count: 1174
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]
The black hook came for Zane before he’d even registered the rift. A spike of pain shot through his head — and then he saw it.
[SFX: WHOOSHING HOOK]
Carved from what looked like animal bone, a black hook shot out of the rift with impossible speed, aiming to drag him in.
[SFX: WIND GUST]
But Zane was faster. Riding a sudden gust of wind, he shifted a few feet to the side, dodging the hook by a hair’s breadth.
The hook missed, hovering in the air for a tense moment before whipping around to strike at him again.
“Cloud-Chaser Skill!”
Zane knew he couldn’t stop.
Even the seven masters of the Celestial Paragon—all at the Spirit Supreme Realm—had fled from a Shadow Creature. If he got dragged into that rift, it was all over.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]
[MUSIC: EPIC CHASE THEME]
The storm crackled with the Wind Law. Pushing his Cloud-Chaser Skill to the limit, the energy inside him exploded, propelling him to top speed.
[MUSIC: INTENSE CHASE]
[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]
But the black hook sped up too, matching him pace for pace. It was hell-bent on catching him.
“Damn it, I can’t shake it!” Zane’s blood ran cold as the hook gained on him.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
With the hook just a dozen feet away, Zane whipped around in mid-air, his power surging. If he couldn’t run, he would fight. It was his only option.
[SFX: CHAIN SNAP]
But just as he braced for impact, the chain attached to the hook snapped taut. Though its tip still gleamed with a cold light, the hook froze, unable to move another inch.
A slow grin spread across Zane’s face. Relief flooded him. The chain was too short.
The hook had been sent from the Umbral Plane, but the Shadow Creature itself couldn’t leave its own dimension.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
A moment later, the black hook shot back into the rift with a whoosh. The Shadow Creature had apparently given up.
But Zane didn’t dare let his guard down. The Shadow Creatures were clearly active near the Chaotic Ocean of Stars. He didn’t know what they wanted with him, but he knew it wasn’t smart to stick around and find out. He immediately bolted toward The Heartlands.
[SFX: SPATIAL RIFTS TEARING]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL]
Sure enough, just as he’d suspected, spatial rifts kept tearing open on his way back, hooks shooting out to snag him.
But Zane was ready each time, dodging every attack in his mad dash for safety.
The high-stakes chase didn’t let up until he finally reached the edge of the Chaotic Ocean of Stars.
[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]
And then — nothing. The rifts stopped. The hooks stopped. The Forsaken simply gave up.
Zane kept moving, but his mind didn’t. He turned the problem over as he flew. The Forsaken’s range wasn’t supposed to have limits — they moved through the reverse side of reality itself. There was no reason they couldn’t have followed him all the way to Cloud Hall and hunted him indefinitely. So why had they stopped at the Heartlands’ border?
There was only one answer that made sense: something on the reverse side of the Heartlands frightened them. Something powerful enough that they wouldn’t cross into that territory.
The thought stopped him cold. Whatever it was, it had been there a long time — quietly, without ever appearing in the Heartlands’ recorded history. That meant it wasn’t hostile to the world above. But it was hostile enough to the Forsaken that they wouldn’t dare cross its threshold.
Zane considered it for a moment. He had the ability to tear open space and enter the reverse side himself. He could look.
He decided against it. Whatever was down there, he wasn’t strong enough to provoke it. Some doors were better left closed.
[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE STRINGS]
Back at Cloud Hall, he spent two months with the mountain.
The simplest use was obvious: it was a weapon. Hurl it at an opponent, and even a Soul Sea Realm master couldn’t stop it — the five elements combined meant no single Law could block it. But the Myriad Manifestations of Nature described something more refined. Over countless years of sitting at the bottom of the Chaotic Ocean of Stars, the Five Elements Power inside the mountain had slowly evolved into something purer. That light could suppress all Five Elements Power in the world. What that meant in practice, Zane was only beginning to understand.
He was still working it out when his cultivation session two months later was interrupted by something he’d never felt before.
A tremor. Deep in his soul — not pain, not danger. Joy. The World’s Will itself, radiating pure, uncomplicated joy.
Zane opened his eyes.
“The ascension passage,” he murmured. “It’s been repaired.”
He didn’t know who had done it — the Celestial Paragon, the humans, some arrangement between them. He hadn’t expected it to happen this fast. But the World’s Will didn’t lie.
[SFX: DISTANT RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]
The repulsive force — the plane’s way of expelling those whose power had outgrown it — only acted on warriors who had reached the Soul Sea Realm. Life-and-Death Realm warriors like Zane weren’t strong enough to be rejected. The plane had no interest in pushing him out. Not yet.
But for the Soul Sea Realm masters across the four divine kingdoms, it was a different story entirely.
[AMBIENT: COUNTRYSIDE]
Five minutes after the repelling force first appeared, a brilliant silver light erupted from the Harlen Divine Kingdom, shooting straight up toward the sky.
“Damn it! Who the hell is that? What’s the big rush?” the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom cursed, watching the streak of light from his palace.
The masters of the Soul Sea Realm, across all seven divine kingdoms, had waited centuries for this. For them, the arrival of this new power was a dream come true. But as much as they all craved it, a reckless ascension was out of the question!
[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]
The divine kingdoms had a fragile peace, maintained by their relative strengths. If all the Soul Sea Realm masters in the Harlen Divine Kingdom ascended, leaving only the Emperor behind, how could his kingdom possibly survive?
It was a full-blown crisis. The Emperor immediately summoned every Soul Sea Realm master in the kingdom to the palace.
The problem was, they all wanted to ascend. Most of them could have done so right after reaching the Soul Sea Realm, but in reality, they had been stuck at that level for countless years, unable to advance.
Now, they were all in a mad rush for the upper world, and no one, not even the Emperor, could stop them.
The thought running through most of their minds was the same: Sure, you’re the Emperor. And maybe I’ll still call you ‘Your Majesty’ in the upper world, for old times’ sake. But if you think you can stop me from ascending, you’ve got another thing coming.
