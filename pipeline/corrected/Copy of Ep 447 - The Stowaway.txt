As vice leader of the Dragonblaze Hall, Alan Nolan’s power was second only to the leader himself. His skill was legendary. For an organization of their stature to send the vice leader to greet a newcomer meant one thing: they were taking Zane seriously.

Elder Jovan was practically buzzing with excitement. If he could land Zane for the Dragonblaze Hall, it would be a massive feather in his cap.

"So, where is he? This young man who survived the Baptism by Laws," Alan asked as he greeted Elder Jovan, his gaze sweeping the area. The kid was all anyone seemed to be talking about.

"Sir, he's in elite training, refining his soul," Julian answered, his eagerness practically spilling over.

"Then take me to him," Alan said with a sharp nod. His voice was calm, but his eyes burned with an intensity that betrayed his casual tone.

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR TRAINING GROUND]

They made their way to the training ground. Standing at the edge, Alan scanned the trainees. "Which one is he? Zane, was it?"

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Elder Jovan pointed to a slim, handsome young man. Alan’s sharp eyes narrowed, sizing him up. Just a kid in his Formative Age who’d barely reached the Consummation of the Adept Realm. Nothing special. A flicker of skepticism crossed Alan's face.

"He really activated the obelisks of souls and went through the Baptism by Laws?" Alan pressed.

"Yes, sir. I wouldn't dare joke about something like that!"

"Hmm." Alan nodded, a faint, confident smile playing on his lips. "I'll have a word with him when he's done." 

After all, countless hopefuls clamored to join the Dragonblaze Hall each year. Convincing some kid from the Lower World? It would be child's play.

[SFX: CRACKING GLASS]

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

The thought had barely crossed his mind when a black sphere floating nearby began to vibrate violently. A sharp crack split the air, followed by a cascade of splintering sounds. The noise echoed for a beat before the Soul Shackle etched across the sphere’s surface shattered completely.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

"What in the hell is going on?" Alan demanded, his eyes wide, the casual fire in them now a raging inferno. "That shackle is meant to imprison the evil spirit of the Owl Beast! How could it just… break?"

Elder Jovan was frozen, too shocked to form a word as he stared in horror at the chaos erupting before them.

The splintering intensified as the trainees within the sphere frantically recalled their souls.

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

[AMBIENT: PANIC CHAOS]

"Get out! Run!"

A moment later, they came scrambling out of the training ground, faces pale with terror. Elder Jovan lunged forward, grabbing one of the fleeing trainees by the arm. "What's happening? What happened to the sphere?"

"I—I don't know!" the boy stammered, eyes wide with fear. "That guy… he just walked right up to the Owl Beast's spirit! The Soul Shackle broke, and now it's out!" Though he didn't know Zane's name, he clearly recognized the kid everyone had been gossiping about.

Alan's expression shifted. "No matter how strong his soul was, nothing survives a Nightjade Fighting Soul unleashed from its shackle." He shook his head. "I came all this way for nothing."

Elder Jovan's face fell. "What a waste," he muttered. "What a terrible waste."

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]

Meanwhile, the city lord felt a cold knot of dread tighten in his stomach.  The memory of the message surfaced, as sharp and unwelcome as the day it arrived.

At first, he'd been ecstatic. A direct message from someone as powerful as Josh! What extraordinary fortune. But his excitement had curdled into fear the moment he'd read the Dragon's scrawled words in the margin.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Josh had written that he'd met a young man named Zane by chance during his travels. Though not of their blood, if this Zane ascended through the Dragon Soar Arena, the city lord was to welcome him as one of the Dragon-blooded race. Then came the warning, cold and absolute: tell no one, or your life is forfeit.

The city lord's hands trembled slightly as the memory washed over him. He thought of how the nine dragons had simply vanished into thin air—no trace, no explanation. It reeked of a conspiracy far too big for someone of his station. Getting involved meant signing his own death warrant, becoming a corpse without ever knowing who held the knife.

So he'd kept his mouth shut. He hadn't breathed a word to anyone, hadn't even dared ask the stowaway's name when he'd pardoned him. It wasn't cowardice—you don't rise to his position without both courage and considerable skill. But he'd learned long ago to recognize when a situation exceeded his capabilities. Even the masters of the Dragon-blooded race, with all their legendary power, would want nothing to do with a fight like this.

His throat went dry as another thought struck him: what if Josh himself was merely a pawn in something larger? The possibility made his blood run cold. He'd built his career on knowing when to advance and when to retreat, and every instinct screamed that this matter belonged to forces operating on a level he could scarcely comprehend.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

And now, to his horror, he realized the person at the center of all this chaos was the very same stowaway he'd been warned about.

At that moment, the city lord wasn't alone. Elder Howard stood just behind him, having arrived with the same group. At the sound of Zane’s name, Howard's head snapped up.

"Zane?" He stepped forward. "That's the stowaway the city lord personally approved — and he's only peak Adept Realm. How could he have made it to the sixth circle?"

The city lord said nothing, but his expression tightened.

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

His thoughts were shattered by a new voice, clear and commanding. "Elder Howard, is that Zane? The practitioner from the Lower World who underwent the Baptism by Laws?"

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

From behind the elder, a woman in an elegant red gown emerged. Her beauty was radiant, almost otherworldly, but it was the single, blood-red mole between her brows that truly held the eye, giving her an air of captivating danger.

A flicker of something—interest, perhaps irritation—flashed in Alan's eyes at the sight of her. He offered a slight, polished smile and a shallow bow. "Madame Vermillion. I didn't expect to see you here."

Madame Vermillion was, after all, the head of the Dragonfall Palace.

[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

She paid him no mind, her gaze fixed on the shattered black sphere as she began to descend. Her silence was a clearer rebuke than any words; the Dragonfall Palace and the Dragonblaze Hall were bitter rivals, and her presence here was a challenge.

Things were about to get ugly.
