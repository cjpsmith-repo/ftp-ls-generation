Ep 677 - Kill or Be Killed

Word count: 1101

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS TOWER INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

Peace was the last thing you’d find in the Tower of Sin that year.

The creatures inside were buzzing, debating what to do. The more timid ones had already bailed, following the human warriors out of the tower and into the Cursed Land, hoping to avoid getting caught in the crossfire if things went sideways.

[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE]

[SFX: ENERGY BARRIER HUM]

Deep inside the Dark Nether Cave, Zane and his group pushed forward, enveloped by a protective orange barrier.

[SFX: VIOLENT SHOVE]

[MUSIC: SUDDEN BETRAYAL]

But the moment they were clear of the Mad Scorpion’s enormous corpse, Yolande activated a pendant on Zane’s chest and shoved Malcolm and Timothy straight out of the shield.

The two Ogres were left completely exposed.

[SFX: ENERGY BARRIER SHATTERING]

[SFX: DEATH LIGHT BLAST]

[SFX: OGRE CRIES]

They threw up a wall of energy to defend against the Dark Nether Death Light, but it held for barely three seconds before shattering. At this range, not even a warrior from the Soul Sea Realm could withstand the lethal light pouring from the scorpion’s head. The two War Sages didn't stand a chance.

Seeing the shock on Zane’s face, Yolande asked coolly, “So, when were you planning on dealing with them?”

“I was going to find their hideout first,” Zane answered, still reeling.

“Malcolm never would’ve given you the chance,” she shot back. “He would’ve attacked the second we were out of that fog. If he’d gotten his hands on your pendant, we’d be the ones left to die.”

For once, Lavender found herself agreeing with Yolande.

Zane just wasn’t as experienced with this kind of betrayal, or maybe he wasn’t as ruthless. Compared to him, Yolande and Lavender were veterans of these deadly games. They knew that when explorers entered a place like this, only the most cold-hearted survived to claim the prize. Everyone else was just another body on the pile.

[AMBIENT: CAVE INTERIOR]

[SFX: ECHOING DRIPS]

By the time they emerged from the scorpion’s body, the fog in the cave’s core was already starting to thin.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

“The fog is clearing!” Zane said, his voice tight with confusion as he took in how much it had dissipated.

Yolande’s eyes widened in realization. “It must be the time flow. Time moves way faster inside the scorpion than it does out here. We might have only been in there for a few days, but months could have passed in our world.”

“Holy crap!” Zane’s expression darkened. “Does that mean the Dark Nether Cave is closed? The humans back in the tower… they must be in serious trouble.”

Before he could take another step, he noticed something on the ground — thin golden lines pulsing faintly beneath the mist.

"That's what's left of the Fog Array," Yolande said. She drew out the Array Plate and fed a stream of Vitality into it. The plate began to spin.

[SFX: GOLDEN LINES SHIFTING]

As it turned, the golden lines on the ground began to separate, the connected web breaking apart into gaps. One by one, the pathways opened. When the last line split, the array was gone.

They walked out.

[MUSIC: EERIE CALM]

It was the first time in the cave's history that anyone had moved freely under the Death Light at full strength. The light poured down around them, and the pendant held. The monsters — the Frost Wolves, the Shadow-maws, everything — were dormant. When the Death Light was at its peak, even the creatures that lived here went still.

They passed through the Blood Gate without a single encounter. The Shadow-maws and the Frost Wolves were curled in their nests on either side, motionless.

Beside him, Esther Sterling's face was pale with worry. But behind the worry, something else was working. She glanced at the pendant on Zane's chest, then at the Array Plate in Yolande's hand, and her expression shifted.

With those two things, Zane could walk in and out of this cave freely — any time, at full Death Light, with the monsters asleep. He could even break through to War Sage in here without interference from the cursed land. All it took was a steady supply of Vitality Stones to keep the pendant running.

Fabian and Jarrod had reached the same conclusion. Their thinking went one step further: if Zane ever left the main continent, leaving the pendant and the Array Plate behind would keep the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary tied to the Violet Heart Sanctuary. The alliance wouldn't die with his departure.

Zane caught their looks. He understood immediately.

"If I leave," he said, "I'll give both of these to Fabian. The humans share them with the Demon Night."

When Fabian, Jarrod, and Esther heard that, their faces lit up. A promise from Zane was as good as gold.

[AMBIENT: TOWER INTERIOR]

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

With no monsters to slow them down, the group made it back to the Tower of Sin in record time. At the base of the tower, they activated their Vitality and soared upward, sticking close to Zane. But when they landed on the top floor, their hearts sank.

“The exit is sealed,” Esther said, staring at the massive door.

It was the only way out. The door was designed to seal itself every time the Dark Nether Death Light flooded the cave, and it was rarely left open.

[SFX: HEAVY STONE POUNDING]

Zane walked up to it and began pounding his fist against the stone, the heavy thuds echoing through the silent chamber.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Meanwhile, on the other side of that door, Maurice had just killed two of the humans’ top-level War Lords. The remaining human and Ogre commanders were locked in a tense standoff, ready for the final clash.

[SFX: ECHOING KNOCK]

Suddenly, the knocking echoed through the room. Everyone froze, stunned into silence.

Who could possibly be knocking on the door now, with the Death Light at its absolute peak?

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL RISING TENSION]

One of the human War Lords shouted with joy, “Open the door! It has to be Fabian and the others! No—it must be Zane!”

Another human commander rushed for the stairs to unseal the exit, but he didn't get a single step.

[SFX: CRUSHING IMPACT]

[SFX: BODY HITTING GROUND]

Maurice’s fist crashed into the shoulder, sending him flying. The man hit the ground in a spray of blood, unconscious before he even landed.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DREAD]

The Ogre War Lords began to look nervous, a cold dread seeping into their bones.

Could it really be that kid again?
