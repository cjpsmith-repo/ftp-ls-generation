Ep 766 - The Beggar's Offering

[AMBIENT: QUIET HOSPITAL ROOM]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

 Even so, Zane knew the chances were slim.

The famous doctor, Walter Reed, had already examined Tia, and his verdict was grim. She wasn't injured or sick; her Raw Vitality was simply… gone. It left her in a deep coma, like a flower that had irrevocably wilted. You couldn't bring a dead flower back to life, but Zane wasn't about to give up. Tia was still fighting somewhere in the darkness, and he had no right to abandon her.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

 In the aftermath of the war, most of Cloud Hall’s disciples had already returned to Cloudhaven through the teleportation array. With Tia unable to lead, the elders unanimously agreed to appoint Zane as the interim leader. It made sense. After his victory against Maddox, Zane was undisputedly the most powerful warrior in The Heartlands, and having someone of his stature at the helm would be a massive advantage.

But Zane refused.

He had no interest in being in charge. Now that the Corrupted Divine Kingdom was defeated, he knew both Cloud Hall and the World Merchants' Guild would govern The Heartlands just fine without him. Besides, he had more important things to do.

Still, a leader was needed. In the end, Zane chose Saint Morphen. The man had taken a nasty punch from Maddox during the battle and needed a few days to recover, but he was as good as new. More importantly, he’d served as Tia’s trusted right-hand, and Zane felt comfortable leaving Cloud Hall in his care.

[AMBIENT: SOFT BREATHING]

[MUSIC: TENDER EMOTIONAL THEME]

 That left Zane free to remain by Tia’s side. She looked like a sleeping beauty, her face peaceful but showing no sign of ever waking. He spent most of his days cultivating at her bedside, and when he wasn't, he would just sit and watch her, finding a strange, hollow comfort in the quiet rise and fall of her chest.

By now, tens of thousands of visitors had passed through — every eccentric, alchemist, and self-proclaimed miracle worker in The Heartlands, drawn by the reward. The World Merchants' Guild screened out most of them, but enough slipped through to keep Zane's days full of false hope and deeper disappointment. He saw every one of them anyway. He couldn't afford not to. 

"Sorry to keep disturbing you," he told Tia once, looking at her still face with a rueful smile. If noise could actually wake her, he'd have filled the room with it long ago. But this was just his own wishful thinking.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

 Meanwhile, the World Merchants' Guild spread the news of Tia’s condition throughout the Heartlands. Their influence, built on their control of the Aether Crystal trade, reached every remote corner of the region, and soon, everyone was talking.

[AMBIENT: TAVERN CHATTER]

"I heard the reward is a Relic-Class weapon,” someone whispered in a tavern, “plus a thousand supreme Aether crystals.”

“That’s insane,” their friend replied. “Most third-grade guilds don’t have that kind of wealth.”

The promise of such a reward brought out every charlatan and con artist imaginable, all of them flocking to the Heavenly Stellar Palace with their supposed miracle cures. The guards were getting sick of it.

Half a month after the search began, a beggar in rags entered Oracle City. He had a cultivator's bearing beneath the filth — Heavenly Stellar Realm, by the feel of him, though no one would have guessed it looking at him. He made his way to the Guild's headquarters first, but they turned him away without a second glance. He didn't leave. Instead, he quietly asked around until he'd located the palace where Tia was being kept.

The gate guards wouldn't let him through either. So he scaled the wall.

[SFX: SOFT THUD]

The moment he landed inside, the one-star runes embedded in the courtyard screamed to life — not offensive, but designed to alert. Guards came running from every direction and had him surrounded in moments.

The beggar held up his hands. "I'm not here to cause trouble," he said quickly. "I have a treasure — something that can restore the Hall Master's life force. Please, just let me present it. That's all I ask."

“Treasure? From you?” one of them said coldly. “That’s a joke. Get him out of here.”

[SFX: RUSTLING CLOTH]

[SFX: MAGICAL GLOW]

Realizing they’d never believe his words alone, the beggar fumbled with a tattered cloth sack and held out a fragment of ancient, glowing crystal. “This was left by an immortal,” he pleaded. “It can restore a person’s Raw Vitality. I heard what happened to your leader. This will work, I know it!”

[SFX: SHOVE]

"Get lost,” the guard spat, shoving him back. Why would he listen to some rambling madman? Oracle City was crawling with scammers these days, and this was just another one for the pile. Most of them were smart enough to hang around the palace grounds, but this one was bold enough to try and force his way in.

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR BALCONY]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

 Zane, watching from a nearby balcony, hadn’t planned on getting involved. But then he saw the crystal, and everything changed.

The beggar wasn't lying. 

[SFX: MAGICAL HUMMING]

 From this distance, Zane could feel the pulsing Raw Vitality radiating from the fragment. He focused his perception on it and felt a shock—the crystal was practically humming, almost as if it were alive.

Down below, the guards had lost their patience. “Get him out of here! Lock him up for a while; that’ll teach him a lesson.” 

[SFX: STRUGGLE AND FOOTSTEPS]

 Two of them grabbed the beggar by the shoulders and started dragging him away.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC INTERVENTION]

 That’s when Zane stepped in. “Wait.”

[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]

 His voice cut through the air. The head guard’s face went pale when he saw who had spoken. He immediately dropped into a low bow. “Master Lowell, we—”

“Quiet,” Zane said, dismissing the apology with a wave. He fixed his gaze on the beggar. “You. Come with me.”

At his words, the guards instantly let go. 

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL RELIEF]

 A look of pure, unadulterated joy washed over the beggar’s face as he scrambled to his feet.
