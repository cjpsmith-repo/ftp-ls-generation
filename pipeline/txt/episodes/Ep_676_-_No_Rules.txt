Ep 676 - No Rules

Word count: 1071

[AMBIENT: TENSE HALL]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

The laughter from the Ogre War Lords finally died down. Maurice stepped forward, his presence swelling with each step until it was suffocating.

[SFX: STONE CRACKING]

The stone floor cracked under his feet, the small craters sealing themselves shut the moment he passed.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

“What do we want?” Maurice stared at Elder May, his voice dripping with ice, and let out a chilling laugh. “We want the Demon Night to give up half of its seats. And we want every single human in the Tower of Sin, from the War Lords to the War Generals, to kill themselves. Right here, right now.”

[AMBIENT: DEAD SILENCE]

A grim silence fell over the humans and the members of the Demon Night. Then the whispers started.

[AMBIENT: PANICKED CROWD MURMURS]

“What? He wants us all to just… kill ourselves? That’s insane!”

“The Ogres are drunk on their own power! This is completely out of line!”

“They’ve always been tyrants. The humans and the Demon Night are in real trouble this time.”

Maurice ignored the frantic murmurs, his gaze locked on Elder May. “So,” he said. “Do you accept?”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

In the past, the Demon Night would have never entertained such a ridiculous demand. Even now, though they had only recently gained a few more seats, they weren't about to give up half of them. While they hadn’t suffered major losses on the trip to the Dark Nether Cave, they had lost two of their most important members: Lavender and Esther Sterling hadn't made it back.

Elder May sneered. “Maurice, do you really think Esther Sterling was the only talent we had?”

With the exception of a few humans, the one hundred and twenty-eight seat holders at the top of the Tower were all powerful War Lords. Their strength varied, but the gaps weren't insurmountable. The Demon Night was, on the whole, much stronger than the Ogres, so they weren't afraid of a straight fight. But Maurice himself was another story entirely.

[SFX: MENACING LAUGHTER]

Maurice burst out laughing. “Oh? And what else do you have? The Heavenly Feather Sanctuary? They’re strong, sure, but they wouldn’t dare start a war with our sanctuaries. Besides, you know damn well we have War Sages in the Tower of Sin.”

The truth was, Maurice had no idea if Malcolm and Timothy were alive or dead. He hadn't been able to check the secret passage, and his Message Rune to Malcolm had gone unanswered.

But it didn’t really matter. Even if they were dead, the Ogres could always train more War Sages. After all, they were still the only ones who knew about the passage. After the two Ogre War Sages, Colin and Neil, had appeared, the other races had started to suspect that the Ogres had found something inside the Dark Nether Cave. Their suspicions weren't baseless; ages ago, several races had tested a theory that warriors could become War Sages within the cave.

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ENERGY]

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Behind Maurice, the Ogre War Lords began to radiate a menacing energy. The air crackled, thick with the promise of a bloodbath.

Elder May said nothing. The silence stretched.

Maurice's expression shifted. "Let me be clear," he said. "That wasn't a suggestion. It was an order."

One of the human War Lords stepped forward, jaw set. "Our people will withdraw. Every human, out of the Tower of Sin, voluntarily. That's as far back as we can go."

It was an enormous concession. Everyone in the room knew what the Tower meant — centuries of accelerated cultivation, irreplaceable. To leave was to fall behind every other race indefinitely. To offer it was to offer everything.

Maurice looked at him for a moment. Then he laughed. "What qualifications do you humans have to negotiate with me?"

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Before the War Lord could respond, Maurice crossed the room. Fifty feet in a single step. His hand shot out — palm-leaf wide — and closed around the man's head.

[SFX: SICKENING CRACK]

The head imploded. Maurice let the body drop and kicked it to the side without looking at it.

[MUSIC: BRUTAL TENSION]

The remaining human War Lords went rigid.

"You're breaking the rules of the Tower," one of them said through his teeth.

Maurice turned to face him. "Rules?" He took a step forward. "There are no rules in the Tower of Sin. The strong devour the weak — that's the only law here. Right now, I am the law."

[SFX: BODY HITTING STONE]

His fist connected. The War Lord crashed into the wall behind him and slid down it, leaving a dark smear of blood on the stone.

[MUSIC: BATTLE CHARGE]

That was enough. The remaining human War Lords ignited — Vitality crackling across their skin, faces set with grim resolve. They knew they couldn't win. They fought anyway.

Behind Maurice, the Ogre War Lords powered up in answer, their auras slamming into the room like a wall.

“Since you refuse to do the job yourselves, my men will do it for you!” A bloodthirsty grin twisted Maurice’s face. He was dead set on wiping out every last human in the Tower today.

[SFX: TELEPATHIC PULSE]

Hearing his threat, Elder May sent a message to a nearby member with a silent pulse of his Vitality. “Get all the humans from the first to the fourteenth floors to the Cursed Land.”

They would be safe there. It was the only thing he could do for them now.

[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]

The warriors in the Tower of Sin were all talented prospects from their respective sanctuaries; even the weakest was considered gifted. The humans here represented the future of the Violet Heart Sanctuary. To kill them would be to sever the sanctuary's lifeline. Elder May couldn't save the War Lords, but he could at least get the other War Generals and fighters out. The Demon Night and the humans were still allies, and even with Zane gone, he still had a duty to help Lavender’s people.

[AMBIENT: EVACUATION CHAOS]

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

But the humans in the Tower were smarter than Elder May gave them credit for. When Maurice and his clan had stormed the top floor with such shocking force, most of the human warriors below had seen the writing on the wall.

Aside from a few who were training in seclusion, many had already started evacuating on their own.
