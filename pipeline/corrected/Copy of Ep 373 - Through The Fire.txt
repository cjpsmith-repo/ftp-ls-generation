Ep 373 - Through The Fire

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The disciples were stunned, completely speechless. These were the top dogs from Cloud Hall, guys who could go toe-to-toe with the best from any second-rate sect out there.

But next to Zane? They felt like a bunch of nobodies.

Zane couldn't have cared less what they thought. He was already focused, conjuring the Shadow Phantoms as he charged forward. In the blink of an eye, he was standing right over the red treasure chest.

[SFX: MECHANICAL GRINDING HALT]

[AMBIENT: EERIE CAVE SILENCE]

The moment Zane’s boots hit the ground beside the chest, a low whirring echoed through the cave. The traps inside ground to a halt, and the howling wind died as if it had never been there.

Finally, Zane thought, a wave of relief washing over him.

[SFX: CHEST LOCK CLICK]

He ran his hands over the chest's surface until his fingers found the mechanism. With a soft click, the lid popped open.

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHOIR]

But before he could even peer inside, a shapeless mass of Umbral Essence floated out. It seemed almost alive, pulsing softly as it drifted in the air.

Zane reached out instinctively, but his fingers passed right through it. Confused, he leaned in to get a better look at the strange, intangible substance. The moment he did, the essence wriggled and shot straight for the space between his eyebrows.

[MUSIC: SERENE BELLS]

[AMBIENT: GENTLE WIND CHIMES]

A cool, refreshing energy washed over him. It felt like standing in the middle of a gentle snowfall, the chill spreading through his mind—but it wasn't unpleasant. It was like a cold drink on a scorching day, washing away all his fatigue and stress.

He could feel his soul getting stronger, purified. His spirits soared.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]

[SFX: ENERGY ABSORPTION]

Zane had no idea how long he’d been in that trance. When he finally opened his eyes, the umbral mass was gone. He’d absorbed it completely.

Because Zane's soul already dwarfed those of most disciples in strength, he absorbed the mystical energy much faster than Lewis could have.

Under normal circumstances, such a rare substance would catapult someone's Soul Power to extraordinary heights. The energy did strengthen Zane—he could feel the warmth spreading through his consciousness, the subtle sharpening of his mental acuity—but the overall effect remained disappointingly modest.

Zane's Soul Power, already cultivated to tremendous depths through the Godforge Technique and his unique experiences, absorbed the essence like an ocean accepting a single raindrop. The boost, while tangible, amounted to almost nothing in the grand scheme of his cultivation. He felt a flicker of frustration—not at the treasure itself, but at the reminder of how far he still had to climb. At best, his Soul Power had improved by a fraction of a percent.

Still, Zane reminded himself, even small gains mattered. His father had taught him that cultivation was a marathon, not a sprint. Every advantage, no matter how slight, could prove decisive in the battles ahead.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED ORCHESTRAL]

[SFX: CLIMBING EFFORT]

With the red box cleared, Zane didn't waste a second. He started climbing again, his eyes locked on the next target: the blue treasure box.

[AMBIENT: ECHOING CHAMBER]

Lewis, who had failed to even reach the blue box, sneered as he watched Zane keep climbing. "What an idiot," he muttered. "He should've quit while he was ahead. Does he have any clue how much harder it gets? He really thinks he has what it takes for the blue box? The guy probably doesn't even have a plan."

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

[MUSIC: CEREMONIAL]

The Leader of the Providence Sect gave Sir Kenneth a bittersweet smile."Congratulations, Kenneth! Your disciple made it to the red treasure chest!" 

Kenneth just laughed. "Thank you. But your own disciple, Lewis, got there too. Let's just say we share the honor."

[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Meanwhile, Zane came face-to-face with a solid wall of fire. He paused, trying to absorb the power from the flames, but it was no use.

"Just my luck," Zane grumbled under his breath. 

[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Shaking off the thought, he braced himself and charged straight through the inferno.

He had no idea that back on the ground, his actions were causing an uproar. 

[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD CHEERING]

The crowd was going absolutely wild.

[AMBIENT: FOREST AMBIENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Far away, Lewis was perched in a tree, using a special technique to slow his breathing and stay hidden from the other disciples—and the Ghost-Faced Spiders. He'd been sitting there, idly chewing on a stalk of grass out of sheer boredom. But when he saw Zane just… walk right into the fire and pass through it like it was nothing, his jaw went slack. The piece of grass dropped from his lips.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REALIZATION]

"Holy crap…" Lewis whispered, his mind reeling. "What the hell just happened?" He couldn't believe what he'd just seen.

He was supposed to be the ace of Providence Sect, a once-in-a-millennium talent. He was so far ahead of everyone else back home that he'd won their Proving without even breaking a sweat. The guy who came in second wasn't even on his radar, let alone the rest of them.

Going into this Cloud Road Trial, only two other disciples had even caught his eye: Lennon Anderson and Frank, the top two from Cloud Hall. His own sect's Leader had warned him repeatedly not to mess with either of them.

That's why he’d kept his face hidden under a cloak during the Hidden Trial—he didn't want to draw any unwanted attention, especially from Lennon.

But he was quickly realizing this trial was packed with skilled fighters. It wasn't just the Cloud Hall disciples; there were talented people from all over. At first, he hadn't paid anyone else much mind. He was so confident, he figured no one here could touch him. He’d mentally written off the other strong fighters as just "not bad."

And then this Zane guy—who was supposedly only at Level Three of the Adept Realm—came out of nowhere. His skill, his control… it had been one shock after another. But seeing him walk through a wall of fire like it was a warm breeze?

Lewis had been eager for some true competition, but he had to admit it. 

He was no match for Zane. Not even close.
