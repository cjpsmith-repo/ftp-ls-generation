Ep 596 - The Soul Echo
Word count: 1078
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
The Soul Echo stood motionless across from Zane, its shortsword of pure emerald raised and waiting.
Warriors powerful enough to reach the Soul Sea Realm only ever came from a sacred place or a divine kingdom.
If a sect had a member break through to the Soul Sea Realm, they could establish a sacred place. If an independent warrior managed it, they could found their own divine kingdom. The Heartlands had never produced a warrior of that caliber.
Creating either a divine kingdom or a sacred place took an insane amount of time and resources, and only a Soul Sea Realm warrior was qualified to rule one.
Above War Fighters were War Generals, then War Lords, and even higher, War Sages. But warriors from the Soul Sea Realm were on a whole other level, far beyond even the Sages. And Zane was just a level ten War Fighter. He wouldn’t stand a chance against one of them.
Those warriors could practically split heaven and earth and even had the power to ascend to the upper world. One of them could kill someone like Zane just by lifting a finger.
Was this final test really supposed to be this hard?
"Don't worry," the spirit of the tower said. "These Soul Echoes are just memories of the dead. Their power will be scaled down to match yours."
Zane nodded.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
Still, pitting a War Fighter against a Soul Sea Realm warrior to test their potential was insane. If this were a real fight, Zane knew he wouldn't even be able to touch his opponent's sleeve before being turned to dust.
Even with the warrior's power suppressed, his chances of winning were razor-thin.
When it came to combat experience, understanding of the Laws, or mastery of Vitality, a warrior from the Soul Sea Realm was in a completely different league.
Even with their power scaled down to a level ten War Fighter, they could still easily take down an ordinary War Lord.
Zane’s chances were slim to none. Still, he was determined to give it everything he had.
Shoving aside any thought of failure, Zane drew his iron sword from his Storage Ring.
[SFX: SWORD DRAWN FROM SHEATH]
Gripping it tight, he charged his opponent.
[SFX: METAL CLANG]
With a sharp clang, the Soul Echo raised his Emerald Sword and effortlessly blocked the attack.
The block didn't faze Zane. He immediately raised his sword to deflect the Soul Echo's blade.
But the Emerald Sword seemed to have a mind of its own, swaying with Zane’s weapon and mirroring its every move.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
[SFX: SWORD MELEE]
Zane pressed harder. Swift and fierce, his sword carved strike after strike, a relentless barrage from every angle. Within a few breaths, he'd attacked over a hundred times.
The Soul Echo had used one sword move.
Back and forth. The same move, over and over. And it was enough.
Zane's brow furrowed. That mocking expression on the Soul Echo's face hadn't moved. Not once had he retreated a single step.
Something was wrong. Not with the Soul Echo — with him.
His Formless Sword was powerful precisely because it stripped everything back to the essential. But at the far end of simplicity, he was starting to see the flaw: no flexibility. Every move simplified to its core, but the core itself had become rigid. Predictable.
Moves were made by people. Even the most fundamental sword art had room to bend.
He stepped back, putting distance between them. The Soul Echo watched him with that same faint smirk.
And then, hovering just at the edge of his mind, something shifted. A barrier he hadn't known was there — thin as paper, solid as iron — and then it wasn't.
"The extreme of simplification," Zane murmured, "is nothingness."
No move was better than a move.
He exhaled.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
"Formless."
His sword moved. Crooked, loose, almost careless — like a child swinging a stick. There were no rules in it. No structure. Nothing to read.
The mockery vanished from the Soul Echo's face.
[SFX: METAL CLANG]
The Soul Echo blocked. But this time, Zane's blade followed up with a casual stab that slipped past the guard entirely and drove straight for the vital point.
For the first time, the Soul Echo stepped back.
Zane pressed the advantage instantly. Another formless arc, from an angle that shouldn't have worked. The Soul Echo deflected, but Zane's next move broke his sword hand's rhythm, and the jade blade twisted wide.
Then the Soul Echo's sword flared.
[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION BUILD]
A soul-stirring burst of Thunder Law erupted from the blade — third level, Zane was almost certain. Lightning crackled toward his head with a force that made his heart slam against his ribs.
At full strength, it would have killed him before he blinked. But suppressed, it was survivable.
He gritted his teeth and held his ground.
Break it again.
Zane was back in the zone.
[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]
His iron sword danced through the air, carving a strange, unpredictable arc. It looked chaotic, almost random, but it slipped right past the Soul Echo's defense and pierced him through the heart.
[SFX: SWORD PIERCING FLESH]
[SFX: BODY DISINTEGRATING]
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
As the iron sword ran him through, the Soul Echo's form cracked and shattered, dissolving completely into a cloud of fine white ash.
Zane took a shuddering breath. "Did I win?"
Just then, another pattern settled over his head.
"The test isn't over yet."
Beating the Soul Echo gave him a huge confidence boost. He wasn't nearly as nervous about facing another suppressed warrior from the Soul Sea Realm anymore.
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
While Zane was taking his ultimate test inside the Talent Tablet, the creatures waiting outside were getting more and more confused.
The test was taking far too long.
The longest anyone had ever taken before was two hours, and Yolande's name had shot to first place in less than an hour.
But Zane had been in there for three hours.
[SFX: ANXIOUS WHISPERS]
[MUSIC: WORRIED PIANO]
Olivia was a nervous wreck. He hasn't run into some kind of trouble, has he?
Meanwhile, Elder Kingsley remained calm, completely unfazed.
He did, however, notice that more and more Ogres were gathering on the tenth floor of the Tower of Sin.
He didn't know what that meant, but it couldn't be good.
