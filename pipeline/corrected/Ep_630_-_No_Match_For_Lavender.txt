Ep 630 - No Match For Lavender
Word count: 1075
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
The Ogre War Lord leaped into the air and brought his foot crashing down onto the arena floor. The impact sent a shudder through the stone, making it buckle for a split second before snapping back into place.
[SFX: MAGICAL FLASH]
Suddenly, a flash of red light pulsed around Zane, and Lavender materialized beside him. She held out her hand, her expression unreadable. "Give it to me."
"Give you what?" Zane asked, thrown off. Ever since Lavender had regained consciousness, she’d developed a habit of just… jumping into things. "What are you talking about?"
"The spear," she answered, her tone flat.
"Why?"
A wry smile touched her lips. "Let me fight for you this time."
Maybe all that time spent inside his body had given her the itch for a fight. Zane couldn’t help but smile back.
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
With a flick of his wrist, he tapped his Storage Ring, and a long spear shot out, flying straight toward her.
Lavender caught it effortlessly with one hand.
[SFX: DARK ENERGY SURGE]
The moment her fingers closed around the shaft, her entire presence shifted. A powerful, dark energy flared in her eyes.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
Whispers rippled through the stands.
"What? Why the sudden swap?"
"She's the Demon Night Queen! You should feel honored just to see her fight!"
"That’s Zane's sword spirit—it can act as a substitute. That part’s not so strange. What's weird is a substitute with its own damn mind."
[SFX: ANGRY MURMURS]
Among the Demon Night members in the stands, the mood had darkened. The Ogre had blown past their Queen like she wasn't worth a glance. The insult wasn't lost on them. If it weren't for the rules of the arena, more than a few would have already jumped onto the floor.
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]
On the arena floor, the Ogre War Lord’s eyes gleamed. He didn’t care about the woman, the spear, or the crowd. The only thing that mattered was Zane. Ogre culture worshiped heroism above all else. If he took Zane down, he would be a legend. That was all the motivation he needed.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
Taking a deep breath, the ogre channeled immense energy into his legs. Power exploded outward, wrapping him in an aura that made him look like a cornered beast. He broke into a full sprint, a blur of muscle and fury charging straight for Zane, blowing right past Lavender as if she were a statue.
But before he could get any closer, Lavender’s voice cut through the air, cold and sharp. "I’m your opponent."
Silent and graceful, she lifted her spear and pointed it at the charging warrior.
[SFX: METAL CLASH]
"Get out of my way!" the ogre roared, his eyes still locked on Zane. He parried her initial thrust with his saber without even looking, clearing a path. He pushed forward again, his eyes burning with murderous rage.
Zane stood calmly in the corner of the arena, arms loose at his sides. He had no intention of defending himself.
[MUSIC: DARK BUILD]
The Ogre's Vitality surged into his saber — everything he had, poured into one strike. He roared. He was going to cut this human in half and die a hero.
What he'd underestimated was the spear at his back.
After her first thrust was deflected, Lavender's spear drew a long arc through the air. Then, with a single subtle shake of her arm, the shaft bent like a snake raising its head — and she threw it.
[SFX: SPEAR PIERCING FLESH]
The tip tore through the Ogre's back at the exact moment his saber came down toward Zane. A churning vortex of dark energy erupted from the wound and spread outward in all directions, shredding him from the inside. The saber dropped from his fingers.
[SFX: METAL CLANGING]
It hit the arena floor with a crisp ring. From start to finish, Zane hadn't moved a single step.
Lavender looked down at her fallen opponent and curled her lip. "He wouldn't fight me."
Zane chuckled. "Someone will."
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
Just as his voice fell, the second Ogre War Lord jumped onto the floor.
Zane spread his hands toward Lavender with a faint smile. "Why don't you do it?"
Her second opponent seemed smarter. He was just as willing to die to kill Zane and save his people, but he clearly had no intention of throwing his life away for nothing. After watching his comrade fall, he knew he’d be run through by Lavender’s spear before he could even get close to Zane. There would be no chance for a desperate, final stand. He had to kill this woman first.
Unfortunately for him, he had completely underestimated Lavender's strength.
[SFX: ENERGY WAVE]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STRINGS]
With every thrust of her spear, Zane could feel a unique wave of energy rippling from the weapon—a special kind of 'Force' that even he couldn't comprehend. It was a formless power that sent a chill down his spine, and he saw it unnerved Amber, the top prodigy from the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, as well.
Sometimes, Zane thought, that strange wave felt a lot like the aura from the spirit inside the Tower of Sin—a power inherited from the tower’s previous owner. Could Lavender somehow be connected to them? He filed the question away, deciding he would have to ask her when he got the chance.
It was obvious to everyone watching that this Ogre War Lord was no match for Lavender either. He had no answer for her skill.
[SFX: WEAPON SHATTERING]
After just ten strikes, his weapon shattered into fragments no bigger than grains of rice.
[SFX: SPEAR THRUST]
A moment later, Lavender’s spear pierced his throat.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY FLOW]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT CHIMES]
As the ogre’s body crumpled to the ground, a stream of white light shot from his corpse and flowed into Zane’s chest. The Lucky Light from those two Ogre War Lords was equal to that of ten War Generals. His gamble to reach the eleventh floor had been the right call.
In less than ten minutes, Lavender had taken down two Ogre War Lords.
[AMBIENT: FEARFUL CROWD]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS LOW STRINGS]
The other ogres who had been lining up to enter the arena were now frozen in place, their faces a mixture of desperation and despair. If they couldn't even get past his spirit, what hope did they have against Zane himself?
