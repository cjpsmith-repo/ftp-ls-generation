Ep 922 - No More Discussion

Word count: 1104

[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Dorothea knew how to pick her battles.

The Ancestral Dragon was a once-in-a-generation prize, no doubt. But it was nothing compared to a warrior on the verge of becoming a Golden Celestial Paragon. 

Even if it was all a bluff, she could always deal with the dragon later. Breaking an imprint was a nightmare, especially one on a creature of that magnitude. The price would be steep, even for her people. But it was just a problem, and problems could be solved. Losing a potential celestial warrior, on the other hand? That was a mistake you couldn’t fix.

The math was simple. She had to see for herself.

Zane’s golden eyes were still narrowed in suspicion, and Dorothea knew he didn’t trust her an inch. She offered him a small, disarming smile. “I’m not here to capture you,” she said softly. “Will you come with me?”

He gave a single, cautious nod.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS DESCENDING STAIRS]

Dorothea led the way down the stairs, and Zane followed, cradling the tiny dragon in his arms. They moved quickly, leaving the spire and stepping out of the bubble of warped time. They’d spent a full hour inside, but thanks to the thousand-fold acceleration, only a flash had passed for the outside world.

[SFX: TEMPORAL DISTORTION WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: RAPID PLANT GROWTH AND DECAY]

 The moment Zane crossed the threshold, the normal flow of time snapped back into place. All around the base of the spire, the plant life, caught in the temporal whiplash, erupted into a frantic cycle of blooming, withering, and dying, over and over again.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 A black-clad figure shot from the sky like an arrow, halting abruptly in midair. It was a young man, his expression tense.

“Dorothea, what’s going on?” he demanded. “Delroy’s barrier just shattered!”

Before he could finish, his gaze snapped past her to Zane, then down to the creature in his arms. The pieces clicked into place, and his eyes ignited with a raw, possessive fury.

 “What is this?” he snarled. “The Ancestral Dragon… It’s hatched?”

He’d entered the spire countless times himself, but the dragon had always been shielded by a refracted space, making it impossible to grab. All he could do was use the accelerated time flow to speed up its hatching. That was why they hadn't panicked when Delroy's barrier broke; he never imagined someone could actually get to it.

Without another word, the man in black reached for the broadsword at his back and drew it in a single motion.

To the young man in black, the golden-eyed figure standing behind Dorothea was simply a girl — he had no idea it was Zane inside Brianna's body.

[SFX: BLADE UNSHEATHING]

[SFX: RUSHING BLADE]

Zane poured Vitality into his legs and launched into the Cloud-Chaser Skill, twisting clear of the strike.

Dorothea's right eye flared. The flower pattern in its iris spun, and a burst of pink light shot from the stamen directly onto the young man's blade.

[SFX: SHARP CRACK]

The broadsword froze in midair, locked solid. The young man's momentum carried his hand clean off the grip. He grabbed nothing but air.

He landed and rounded on Dorothea, his voice low and dangerous. “Little Big Sister — are you betraying the Demi-Immortal Race?”

Dorothea shook her head. “No. There's something more important.”

“More important?” his voice rose. “More important than the Ancestral Dragon?”

She considered for a moment. “I've found the Tower of Crystal.”

The words hit him like a physical blow. He went still.

As a Demi-Immortal himself, he knew exactly what the three training towers meant to their people — and he knew one had been lost. His own name sat in the first row of the Talent Tablet alongside Dorothea's. He understood the Tower of Crystal's significance.

“But—“ He struggled. “Even so — we can't just let her walk away with the Ancestral Dragon.”

“No more discussion,” Dorothea said flatly. “We go to Max. You'll understand when we get there.”

The young man in black clenched his jaw. He knew her well enough to read the shift in her — she was rarely serious, quick to laugh, hard to rattle. Whatever she'd seen in that spire had rattled her. He said nothing more.

The little dragon, sensing the hostility, buried its head in Zane's chest. It had already imprinted, and as far as it was concerned, Zane was its mother, its protector — its entire world.

[AMBIENT: OPEN SKY]

The three of them took to the air — Dorothea at the front, Zane behind her with the dragon in his arms, the young man in black trailing at the rear, his eyes fixed on Brianna/Zane's back. He wasn't going to let this girl slip away.

The little dragon seemed unbothered by any of it. It pressed its furry head against Zane's cheek and made soft, burbling sounds — the sounds of something very small and very content, like a human infant certain it was safe.

[SFX: DISTANT DRAGON ROAR]

A distant roar tore through the air, drawing their attention.

The little dragon flinched at the sound. It burrowed deeper into Zane's chest and went very still, trusting him to stand between it and whatever had made that noise.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Over the crater, Max and the four others were still locked in a desperate struggle with the young Evil Dragon.

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

“Watch out! It’s about to seal itself for its fourth transformation!”

“Stop it, or we’re all dead!”

“Aim for its neck! I already opened a wound there!”

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSIONS]

Explosions of power slammed into the Evil Dragon one after another, erupting in blinding flashes against its dark scales.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

At the edge of the crater, the warriors from the seven kingdoms could only stare, paralyzed. The power wielded by the Spirit Supreme masters was beyond anything they could comprehend, but the Evil Dragon was just as terrifying. It was still a juvenile, yet it fought with the strength of a level-eleven beast—or a human warrior of the Soul Sea Realm, like them. And here it was, holding its own against several Spirit Supremes at once. The thought of what it would become when fully grown was bone-chilling.

Of course, the Soul Sea masters were terrified. They could barely protect themselves as it was. Most were just counting the seconds until they were wiped out. Back home, they were respected masters. 

Here, trapped in these walking corpses, they were ants caught in a battle between gods, without even the strength to fight back.
