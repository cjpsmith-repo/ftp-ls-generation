Ep 116 - A Hollow Impact

[AMBIENT: DAWN FOREST]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
 The sun rose, promising another day of demon hunting, but the relief of surviving the night was already souring into fear.
For as long as anyone could remember, the Sky Forest had only been home to Demonic Grunts. That’s why the Skyreach Sect used it as a hunting ground for their apprentices—the low-level demons were simple-minded and weak enough for even the Varsity members to handle easily.
But a Demon General was a whole different beast. If Demon Commanders were really infesting the Sky Forest, then the apprentices of the Skyreach Sect weren't the hunters anymore. They were the prey.
Zane's team hadn't been the only ones to run into a Demon General yesterday. Apprentices from Maiden’s Peak had seen one too. Two sightings in one day wasn't just a coincidence; it was a terrifying sign that the number of Demon Commanders in the forest was way higher than anyone had estimated.
Everyone knew it was supposed to be almost impossible for a General to slip into the Sky Forest, let alone stay hidden—that security was the whole reason the Sect used it for training. So what was driving so many of them here? How did they break through Silverspire, the demons' main line of defense in The Crucible? Had something gone wrong there?
The questions swirled, unanswered, fueling the anxiety that kept them tossing and turning all night. 
[AMBIENT: BATTLE SOUNDS]
[SFX: COMBAT CLASH]
 Dawn broke to reveal a harrowing sight: a lone apprentice from Eagle Peak standing defiantly against a Demon General. The young cultivator, muscles taut with strain and face etched with determination, embodied the renowned strength of his peak, yet even Eagle Peak's finest found themselves overwhelmed. They battled not one but two Demon Commanders simultaneously, while dozens of snarling Grunts circled like vultures, waiting for weakness.
Dalton, his robes torn and bloodied, faced one General alone. His movements, though precise, had begun to slow as fatigue crept into his limbs. 
[SFX: IMPACT TREMORS]
 Each clash sent reverberations through the morning air, the impact of their exchanges shaking nearby trees.
Several yards away, three other Varsity members formed a desperate triangle against the second General—a hulking monstrosity with obsidian skin and eyes that glowed like molten copper. Despite their coordinated attacks and defensive formations, they barely maintained their position. Sweat poured down their faces as they gasped for breath, their Vitality flickering dangerously low. The General toyed with them, a predator savoring the hunt before the kill.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]
 "They won't last another minute," someone whispered, voicing what everyone feared.
The delicate balance of the conflict hung by the thinnest thread. One misstep, one moment of hesitation, and blood would soak the forest floor. The spectators watched with mounting horror, knowing they witnessed the prelude to a massacre.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
[AMBIENT: BATTLE SOUNDS]
 "We have to help them!" Cliff yelled, then charged into the fray, his sword aimed at a Demonic Grunt. 
[SFX: ENERGY POWERING UP]
 He summoned his Vitality, and the energy wrapped around his blade, making it gleam in the morning light. The other apprentices from Maiden's and Misty Peak followed his lead, rushing in to take on the swarm of Grunts.
In any other trial, apprentices wouldn't dare interfere in another Peak’s fight unless things were truly desperate. But this time was different. After their own run-ins with Demon Commanders and the heavy losses they’d already suffered, they were all worried about the apprentices from Blackrock Peak. 
As slim as the chances were, none of them wanted to admit they were probably already dead. In a situation like this, they had to stick together.
But their help didn't tip the scales much, since they were only fighting the Grunts, not the Generals. 
The Demon Commanders were so powerful that any interruption could shatter the fragile balance between Dalton and his opponent, or the three other apprentices and theirs. A single misstep could get them all killed.
With that in mind, the apprentices focused on clearing out the Demonic Grunts. Only one of them broke from the pack and charged straight for a Demon General.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
 It was Zane.
His target was the General being held at bay by the three Eagle Peak members. 
Zane sized them up instantly—three Varsity members, probably Nature Level Four. Their strategy was smart: a triangular formation that was both a solid defense and an effective attack pattern. Working in perfect sync, they managed to deflect the General's relentless onslaught.
Judging by their movements, the three must have had the same teacher. 
No matter how powerful the General's attack, they always found the right angle to block its hits. In theory, their flawless teamwork should make them three times stronger than if they fought alone, putting them on par with the Demon General.
Watching his desperate run, Ingrid had no idea what he was thinking. She couldn't believe that Zane, who was just a high-level candidate, was actually charging a Demon General. Still, she couldn't help but be impressed by his sheer guts. “That kid has potential,” she thought, a silent prayer forming on her lips. "I just hope he lives to see it."
As if sensing her thoughts, the Demon General, which had been focused on the three Varsity members, finally turned its attention to Zane. A cruel, mocking sneer twisted its features when it saw what was happening. This human wasn't even a nature creature! The sheer audacity of this kid, charging it like this... was he suicidal?
At that moment, every eye widened in horror as the General and all watching apprentices believed Zane was charging toward certain doom.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BUILDUP]
 Yet when his diminutive frame collided with the demon's colossal, granite-like body, what followed stunned them into absolute silence.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
 A nauseating, hollow impact echoed through the forest.
[SFX: TREES SPLINTERING]
[AMBIENT: FOREST DESTRUCTION]
[SFX: DEMON HOWL]
 Then the Demon General—a towering monstrosity thrice the height of an ordinary man—hurtled backward as though struck by an unstoppable force of nature. The creature tore violently through a dozen ancient spruce trees, splintering them like twigs before finally grinding to a halt in the distance.
Zane, meanwhile, stood perfectly untouched. He rose to his full height and nonchalantly dusted off his attire, his expression betraying no hint of exertion—as if he'd merely brushed against a stranger on a crowded street rather than demolished a being of nightmarish power.
