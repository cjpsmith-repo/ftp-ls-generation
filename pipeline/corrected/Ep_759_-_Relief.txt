Ep 759 - Relief

Word count: 1040

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 Zane watched Maddox with a cold, steady calm. He’d known from the start this fight wouldn’t be easy, and that a man like Maddox wouldn’t go down without a final, desperate move.

[SFX: MANIACAL LAUGHTER]

 “Sooner or later, the Heartlands will be wiped out by the York Divine Kingdom!” Maddox’s laugh was a ragged, maniacal sound, his voice thick with malice. The charred ruins of his face made him look monstrous. 

[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]

With trembling hands, he fumbled inside his Storage Ring and pulled out the Octogram Mirror. He summoned one last burst of strength and hurled it at Zane.

Zane batted it aside with a casual flick of his wrist. 

[SFX: METAL CLATTER]

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]

 The mirror clattered against the ground, rolling a few times before it settled. As everyone watched, a human figure began to coalesce on its cracked surface, shimmering into existence. All eyes had been on the duel, on Zane and Maddox, but now the crowd held its breath, wondering what last trick the cornered man had up his sleeve.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

 A boy—one Zane had met before—slowly materialized from the mirror’s depths.

Zane glanced at the apparition, then shot a sneer at Maddox. “Is this your last resort? This is all you’ve got? He’s a nobody. I already beat him once without breaking a sweat.”

The boy’s voice echoed from the mirror. “Your name is Zane, right?”

“What of it?” Zane shot back.

“You’re right, I lost our last fight,” the boy admitted, his voice taking on a chilling edge. “We fought in Melody City, and all I lost that day was a tiny fragment of my soul. But now that the Star Storm has died down, you’re going to get what’s coming to you. This is your end!” 

[SFX: EVIL CACKLE]

 He cackled, the sound high and cruel. “Soon, the entire Heartlands will be mine! And the secret hidden in the depths of your mind will belong to me, too!”

Zane's face darkened. The secret of the Cobalt Dragon and what lay within his mind had never been known to any outsider. The last time he had faced Lord Wallace's soul, the Cobalt Dragon had swallowed it whole. He hadn't expected him to know anything.

Maddox saw Zane's expression shift and mistook it for fear. He sneered. "Have you thought it through? Give me everything, and I'll consider sparing your life."

[SFX: SWORD DRAW]

Zane pulled his sword. A single slash of light cut the Octogram Mirror into pieces. Lord Wallace's image blurred, then dissolved — his whole figure fading until nothing remained but the ghost of a strange smile.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]

The moment the sword came out, Maddox surged up from the ground. Even now, even like this, he wouldn't quit.

[SFX: SWIFT DECAPITATION]

Zane didn't give him the chance. The sword light came out like a silver snake, wrapping around Maddox's body. In an instant, it was done. His head rolled across the blood-soaked ground. His mouth opened and closed weakly, once, twice — whatever last words he had died with him. His Vitality dissipated completely.

[MUSIC: QUIET RESOLUTION]

Zane stood still for a moment. Since the earliest days of his path — since the Skyreach Sect — Maddox's shadow had sat like a brand on his martial heart. Now it was gone. The weight of it lifted so suddenly that he almost didn't recognize the feeling.

Relief.

[SFX: RETREAT — RUSHING WIND]

Up in the air, Simon saw it happen. His expression changed. His Vitality surged, and he turned and ran — a streak of light vanishing into the distance. He had seen Zane walk through his Venom Net without blinking. 

Maddox was dead. There was nothing left here for him.

The remaining Corrupted Divine Kingdom warriors scattered with him, fleeing in every direction. A handful were run down by Oracle City's fighters. The rest disappeared into the world.

[AMBIENT: QUIET BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

The setting sun had turned the sky red. Its light fell across the broken tiles of Oracle City, across the churned-up ground, across the mixture of mud and blood that stretched for miles in every direction. Zane walked through it slowly, leaving footprints behind him.

Cora’s smile was gentle and sweet, meant to bring a sense of ease to the tense atmosphere. She reached out to touch Zane’s arm, but he took a slow step away, his gaze fixed on Tia as he continued walking.

Cora’s hand was left hanging in the empty air, and a sharp ache bloomed in her chest. She quickly composed herself, dropping her hand and remembering who she was. With a quiet sigh, she fell into step behind him. She could tell he was in a dark mood; now wasn't the time to push.

[SFX: WHOOSH OF FLIGHT]

[AMBIENT: CELEBRATING CROWD BELOW]

 Zane walked right up to Tia, gathered her into his arms, and launched himself into the sky. Floating high above the battlefield, he looked down at the warriors celebrating below.

The army of Oracle City hadn’t suffered nearly as much as their enemies. Of the several hundred thousand fighters who had defended the city, about a third had fallen. But the army of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom was almost entirely gone, wiped out by Cora’s massive, sweeping blades.

[AMBIENT: CITY SQUARE CELEBRATION]

[SFX: CROWD CHEERING]

 The surviving fighters had gathered in the city square, their relief and joy turning into a massive celebration. Suddenly, a single voice cut through the noise.

[SFX: SINGLE VOICE SHOUT]

 “Zane! The Mighty!”

The cry was loud and clear. A moment later, another voice joined, then a dozen more.

“Zane! The Mighty!”

[SFX: MASSIVE CROWD CHANTING]

[MUSIC: EPIC TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

 Hundreds, then thousands, then tens of thousands of voices took up the chant. The roar grew, a tidal wave of sound from the throats of every warrior, their faces turned up toward the sky.

Today, Zane had become the savior of Oracle City. No—he was the savior of the entire Heartlands.

[SFX: THUNDEROUS ROAR]

 The fighters began to pour their Vitality into the chant, amplifying their voices until the sound could be heard across the whole region. Their people had suffered enough; this was a roar of survival, a thunderous declaration that the nightmare was over. The sound was almost frightening in its intensity, a promise that from now on, everyone would fear and respect Zane the Mighty.

[AMBIENT: STREET CELEBRATION]

[SFX: JOYFUL DANCING CROWD]

 As the roar drifted through Oracle City, the common folk poured into the streets, cheering and dancing in pure, unbridled joy.

[MUSIC: GENTLE EMOTIONAL STRINGS]

 High above it all, Zane lowered his head. He gently stroked Tia’s dark hair and whispered in her ear, “Tia, can you hear that?”
