Ep 230 - Triumph of the Rookie

[MUSIC: MAGICAL BATTLE]
[SFX: SPINNING WHIRLPOOLS]
Countless tiny whirlpools of sword intent materialized in front of Zane, spinning chaotically in various directions and at different speeds. These deadly whirlpools shredded any unfortunate phantom warriors caught in their relentless grasp, leaving nothing but dissipating energy in their wake.
Though he was still using fundamental sword techniques, Zane had transformed them into something extraordinary. He'd meticulously refined each movement, stripped them down to their essential core---thereby unleashing the full, devastating potential of his sword intent. The simplicity had become his greatest weapon.
With unwavering focus, Zane advanced his assault. The Streamer danced through the air, a mere silver flash as it wove between warriors, all of them sixth-level Adepts. His newly refined techniques flowed with such unpredictable grace and overwhelming power that the phantom warriors found themselves incapable of mounting any kind of defense.
Still, these were sixth-level Adepts---formidable opponents by any standard. Zane couldn't cut them down as effortlessly as the previous wave. Each required him to identify a precise vulnerability, adapt his swordplay accordingly, and strike with impeccable timing to eliminate them with a single, devastating blow.
Even so, he dispatched all twenty of them in short order.
"I've barely scratched the surface of my capabilities," he reflected, wiping a light sheen of sweat from his brow. A flicker of concern crossed his face. "But if this pattern keeps up, the next wave is going to be a lot tougher."
Twenty level six warriors suggested the next wave would likely feature level eight opponents. Zane tightened his grip on Streamer, a momentary doubt clouding his confidence. He wasn't sure he could handle twenty such powerful entities at the same time. The sword hummed softly in his hand, as if responding to his uncertainty with reassurance.
[AMBIENT: SWIRLING MIST]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Just as the thought crossed his mind, a swirling mist flooded the area. A moment later, dim figures began to materialize in the white fog.
A bitter smile touched Zane's lips. He'd been too optimistic. His new opponents weren't eighth-tier---they were ten warriors who had reached the peak of the Adept Realm.
But Zane wasn't about to back down. He couldn't actually get hurt inside the magic array, so he saw this as the perfect chance to test his true strength. Gripping the Streamer, Zane held his ground against the onslaught of ten peak-level Adepts.
[SFX: MAGICAL BATTLE SOUNDS]
His power was undeniable. Zane was a true prodigy.
[AMBIENT: CAPTIVATED CROWD]
Isla's eyes widened in shock, her breath catching in her throat. She couldn't believe what she was seeing. Based on this display, she figured he was strong enough to take on someone who had just entered the Soul Realm. It was incredible.
What Isla didn't know was that Zane had already killed a master at the Soul Realm. In fact, Hugh, another expert at that level, had been utterly crushed in his fight against Zane.
Originally, Isla had only pushed Zane to join the All Peaks Competition for the experience. After all, at only seventeen and just a second-tier Adept, his cultivation level was low.
If he kept improving at this rate, she'd figured he might place well at the next competition in three years. But with so many gifted disciples in the Skyreach Sect, she'd worried she was overestimating his potential just by encouraging him to join.
She never, ever expected him to show this kind of strength in the trial.
Even though the phantoms in the array were only at the baseline strength for their level---not as tough as real fighters---Isla herself could only handle two or three at a time. During her own trial, she'd merely survived; she couldn't kill them. Zane was doing more than that. He wasn't just defending himself---he was creating openings to strike back.
[SFX: PHANTOM WARRIOR VANISHING]
A few moments later, a red dot on the magic array plate vanished.
Each red dot on the plate represented a phantom warrior. One disappearing meant Zane had managed to take one down.
He was fighting back while under assault from ten peak-level Adepts. "How is he even doing that?" Isla asked herself. She and the other masters wondered if he was just using some rare movement technique to dodge. But the fact that warriors were dying proved he wasn't just running. He was on the offensive.
The gap between inner and outer disciples at Misty Peak was massive.
The outer disciples were a talent pool, and only the absolute best could become inner disciples. Even at Misty Peak, which ranked last among The Thirty-Three Peaks of the Skyreach Sect, they had an inner disciple who had reached the Adept Realm's peak.
At the top-ranking peaks, like Skytop Peak, some inner disciples had even reached the Soul Realm.
But Zane's performance proved that even as an outer disciple, he was among the Sect's absolute elite.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT REVEAL]
An hour later, the crowd outside the magic array watched as the white fog finally began to clear, revealing Zane standing within.
The instructor checked the Array Plate and went quiet for a moment.
"Zane Lowell — fifty-five thousand points."
[SFX: CROWD ERUPTING]
The number hit the crowd like a thunderclap. Fifty-five thousand. More than anyone had ever seen from an outer disciple.
Across the crowd, Ben's forced smile said everything. He'd told himself that even if Zane outscored him, he'd still issue a challenge. But fifty-five thousand points — higher than even Misty Peak's top-ranked inner disciple — had put that thought to rest for good. There was nothing left to prove. Nothing left to argue.
On the Array Plate, Zane's score sat above every inner disciple on Misty Peak. The moment he stepped through the gate, he was already number one.
"It's finally over. The kid's a prodigy!"
"I wonder how many points he got."
Many outer disciples had been waiting for nearly two hours to see the results. As a new inner disciple, Zane would get more pills and money, not to mention access to a few secret cultivation sanctums reserved just for them.
While these sanctums weren't as good as Emberfin Lake or The Seven-Star Platform, they were free to use---no points required. That made them an excellent option for inner disciples from ordinary families. Even some disciples from noble houses used them, since not every kid from a wealthy family got the full backing of their clan.
The inner disciples lived at the summit of Misty Peak.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: TRANSITION THEME]
So, after passing the trial, Zane was assigned a new house. He packed his few belongings into his Storage Ring and headed to his new home.
As he climbed from the hillside to the peak, he passed several inner disciples in black robes, all of whom gave him curious looks.
Usually, the established inner disciples had a strong sense of superiority. They tended to look down on newcomers, like veterans sizing up a fresh-faced rookie.
After all, the newbies could rarely match them in strength.
Zane, however, was an exception.
