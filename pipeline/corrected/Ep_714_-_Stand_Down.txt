Ep 714 - Stand Down
Word count: 1015
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]
Four Spirit-Tribulation warriors swarmed Zane at once, two of them mid-stage experts, a clear sign of just how wary they were of him.
Back when he was only in the Soul Realm, Zane had a reputation for taking down saint-level geniuses who had already reached the Inner-Core Realm. Now that he’d broken through to the Spirit-Tribulation Realm himself, no one had a clue what his true limits were.
Still, they figured that four-on-one ought to be impossible odds.
Seeing the swarm of enemies, Saint Morphen’s face tightened. He took a step, his body shimmering as he prepared to use his Mystical Pace to jump into the fray, but Emanuel’s spear shot out, blocking his path and forcing him back.
Everyone’s eyes were glued to Zane. Finn, Patrick, and the others looked sick with worry. It didn’t matter how much stronger Zane had gotten over the past two years; this was four Spirit-Tribulation experts. How could he possibly take them all on by himself?
[SFX: MYSTICAL SHIMMER]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
But as the four masters closed in, Zane’s eyes glittered with a confident, almost excited light. His form grew hazy, blurring like a heat mirage, his image rippling and distorting like a reflection on disturbed water.
An illusion? one of the warriors thought, frustrated.
Some kind of movement technique? another wondered.
But their attacks were already in motion, and Zane hadn’t so much as flinched. They weren’t about to waste an opportunity like this.
[SFX: WHOOSH OF WEAPONS]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
A storm of attacks rained down on Zane in an instant. But then something bizarre happened.
[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]
The weapons seemed to connect—swords and sabers striking and stabbing right where he stood—but they passed straight through him as if he were nothing but air. He was completely unharmed.
[SFX: SWORD CLASH]
Even as he parried Emanuel’s strikes, Saint Morphen glanced over at Zane, his concern melting into pure astonishment. “He’s using Spatial Law!” he exclaimed, stunned. “A higher form of it!” Where has he been for two years? Morphen wondered, his mind racing. How did he master this?
Emanuel was just as distracted, his face a mask of disbelief as he watched the impossible unfold. “How is that even possible?” he muttered, a cold dread washing over him.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
It was the same terrible feeling he’d had the first time he met Zane—the sickening realization that he had once again completely underestimated him.
He remembered that first meeting all too well. Back then, he’d dismissed Zane as some lucky kid who’d ascended from the Skyreach Sect. He’d almost killed him at the end of the Cloud Road, but Zane’s sudden appearance had ruined his plans. As a member of the Cloud Hall, Emanuel was now painfully aware of just how extraordinary Zane’s talent was.
It wouldn't be long before Zane stood at the pinnacle of the martial world. Emanuel had known that for years — had felt it in his gut the first time he laid eyes on him — and had done nothing decisive about it. Every opportunity he'd had to remove Zane early, he'd let slip. He'd told himself there would be a better moment. There never was.
Emanuel wasn’t a fortune-teller. He never could have dreamed that Zane would return at that exact moment.
[SFX: FOUR HEAVY IMPACTS]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]
Four punches. One for each of them. The Spirit-Tribulation warriors went down hard, skidding across the stone, faces white. None of them got up.
Only a Life-and-Death Realm warrior should have been able to do that. Everyone on the platform knew it. Two years ago, Zane had been a War General. Now he was here, and the gap between him and the Spirit-Tribulation Realm looked the same as the gap between him and everyone else had always looked — vast, and growing.
The platform went silent.
Finn, still on the ground, let out a faint laugh. "Still normal," he said quietly.
No one else laughed. They were all doing the same math — Spirit-Tribulation Realm, four opponents down simultaneously, Law of Space. The conclusion landed like a stone. Zane could fight Life-and-Death Realm warriors. And Tia hadn't even broken through yet.
Zane looked at Emanuel's remaining forces. "Stand down," he said. "You'll be given a lighter punishment."
The hesitation was immediate. Several warriors loosened their grips.
"Don't listen to him!" Emanuel snapped. "You plotted rebellion. You think he'll spare any of you? You're already dead if you surrender. Fight!"
The grips tightened again. It didn't matter. Zane had already turned toward Emanuel.
"Zane, spare me! Just this once!" Emanuel begged, desperately dodging Zane’s attacks. "I'll go into hiding! I swear, I'll never set foot in the Heartlands again!"
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
"Sorry, not happening," Zane said coldly, closing the distance between them with each leap. "I was already planning to kill you, but I hesitated because you were a Cloud Hall elder. You forced my hand. It’s too late to beg now."
"If you kill me, my family, House Drew, will become an enemy of the Cloud Hall!" Emanuel threatened. "All the noble houses of Cloudhaven will move against you!"
"You really think the great houses will rally behind a measly Third-Grade House like yours?" Zane shot back, now only ten feet away. "Dream on."
[SFX: WEAPON DRAWN]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]
"Then go to hell!" Realizing there was no escape, Emanuel spun, pulling a black spear from his Storage Ring. "Sun-Ender Spear!" he roared, thrusting it at Zane.
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
The tip of the spear erupted in a blinding light, so bright it seemed to eclipse the sun. For all his panic, Emanuel was a Cloud Hall elder, and his power was undeniable.
For a moment, Emanuel believed it might be enough. The spear had never failed him. The light at its tip was blinding even to him, and he'd poured every last drop of his strength into the thrust. If anything in his arsenal could stop Zane, it was this.
But Zane didn’t even flinch. He just sneered, catching the spearhead with his bare hands.
[SFX: METAL SHATTERING]
With a simple, almost casual push, the top-grade Mythic weapon buckled, cracked, and shattered into pieces.
This was the last straw for Emanuel. His heart plunged into a bottomless abyss. The difference in their strengths was far greater than he had ever imagined.
