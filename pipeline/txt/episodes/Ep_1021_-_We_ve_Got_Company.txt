Ep 1021 - We’ve Got Company

Word count: 1009

[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND MINE]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Rylan was a little baffled that Zane was only at the Life-and-Death Realm.

He’d already heard from Ernest’s crew about what happened yesterday—how Zane had sent Ernest flying into the mine with a single slap.

That kind of strength gave Rylan pause. It was why he’d sent three of his men to tail Zane into the mine.

All three were his top guys. Even though they’d also come from the Lower World, they were a cut above the rest, far stronger than your average warrior.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS DRONE]

Rylan had given them a simple order: make Zane disappear quietly.

The Soulbound Sect mostly turned a blind eye to the fighting and killing in the mine, but Rylan still had to be smart about it. If he killed off too many workers, the Sovereign Stone haul would drop. And if that pissed off the higher-ups, the armored guards who ran this place would come looking for him.

So, Rylan usually avoided killing. As long as the workers coughed up their Sovereign Stones without a fuss, he left them be.

But when he had to make a move, he didn’t hesitate. Zane had dared to kill one of his men, and for that, he had to die. Otherwise, how could Rylan hold his position as leader of the strongest crew? How would he keep the respect of his men?

[AMBIENT: QUIET CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS PIANO]

Freya’s mind was still racing. Thinking it over, she realized Zane had deliberately avoided her yesterday. She had no idea where he’d been during that time, which meant he’d had plenty of opportunity to be doing… something else.

[SFX: MENTAL TRANSMISSION]

Just as her imagination was running wild with possibilities, a voice echoed in her head.

“We’ve got company,” Zane said.

[AMBIENT: TENSE FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Even though the three men behind them were trying to stay hidden, there was no fooling Zane’s senses. He’d locked onto them the moment they left the collection point.

“What should we do?” Freya asked through their mental link.

“Let’s talk after we’re inside,” Zane replied.

He hadn’t been sure of their intentions at first. After all, they could have just been heading to the mine. But after Zane intentionally changed directions a few times, it became obvious they were following him.

To be fair, the warriors weren’t being all that sneaky. Rylan had told them to make Zane “disappear” in the mine, and in their eyes, a warrior from the Life-and-Death Realm was hardly a threat.

[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]

The two of them picked up the pace and ducked into the nearest tunnel. The three men followed without hesitation.

Inside, they found Zane waiting alone, sword in hand. He smiled. “Hello.”

The leader looked him over. “You knew we were coming, and you didn’t run. Brave.”

“Brave without strength is just a faster death,” one of the others said.

“I didn’t even provoke Rylan,” Zane said mildly.

“Doesn’t matter. He said you die, you die.” The leader’s eyes slid to Freya. “Leave the woman. Don’t damage her.”

The three of them moved at once, attacking from three directions.

[SFX: ICY BLAST]

Freya’s Snowfall Field unfurled, a wave of biting cold sweeping outward and locking around all three men.

[SFX: SWORD FLASH]

Zane’s blade moved once. It was over.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

As the last man dropped, Zane stood quietly for a moment. The Upper World, the Lower World — it made no difference. The strong preyed on the weak. He felt a sudden weariness settle over him. If the peak of martial arts led only to more of this, what was the point of chasing it?

The question lingered. His grip on the sword tightened anyway.

[AMBIENT: LUXURIOUS CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: SEDUCTIVE MELODY]

The three women in front of Rylan swayed their hips, dancing seductively for him.

Women were a rarity here. Only a select few men got to enjoy their company, and Rylan was, of course, one of them.

These three had once been powerhouses in the Lower World—maybe revered priestesses or even empresses of their own kingdoms. It was a cruel twist of fate that after ascending, they were reduced to depending on men like Rylan just to survive.

As the women continued their dance, the color suddenly drained from Rylan’s face.

[SFX: METAL CRUSHING]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

The metal cup in his hand crumpled into a twisted ball of scrap.

“The seals… they’re gone.”

The marks he had placed on his own men had vanished in an instant.

[SFX: DARK REALIZATION]

That could only mean one thing: all three of them were dead.

Rylan’s jaw tightened. In the Lower World, he’d been untouchable — a peerless hero who’d nearly unified an entire realm. He’d expected the Upper World to be a new stage for his ambitions. Instead, he was here, managing a work crew in a mine, living in a stone house he’d cut himself because the guards wouldn’t allow him to build a palace.

He had tens of thousands of Sovereign Stones saved, enough to bribe his way out and buy the resources to make his cultivation surge. He just needed to endure a little longer.

These were his most capable fighters; he knew exactly how strong they were. What’s more, the seals had disappeared at the exact same moment, which meant someone had killed all three of them in the blink of an eye.

Could that kid from the Life-and-Death Realm and the woman from the Soul Sea Realm have done this?

[MUSIC: TENSE PAUSE]

Impossible.

Rylan immediately dismissed the thought.

It was more likely Zane had tricked them, luring them deep into the mine where they ran into some powerful beast. But no, his men were too careful for that. They never would have let Zane lead them into the deep tunnels.

[MUSIC: SEDUCTIVE MELODY]

“Rylan! What is it? You look pale,” one of the women purred, settling onto his lap with a sway of her slender waist.

Rylan snorted. “It’s nothing. Just… something interesting happened.”
