Ep 763 - Welcome Back

Word count: 1095

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

Zane stared at the concentric rings of light radiating from the young man's fingertip. He had no idea what this power was made of — he couldn't sense a single ripple of Vitality in it, not a trace of the energy he understood. Whatever this young man was using, Zane knew he had to give this fight everything he had.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

 The light rushed toward him. Focusing with all his might, Zane unleashed every last shred of starlight he could summon. At the same instant, the Dragon Scale power that had been simmering just beneath his skin erupted with a deafening boom. It was a combined force that could have shattered a mountain range or carved a new channel for a river.

 But the moment the circles of light reached him, Zane finally understood the true, terrifying gap between them.

[SFX: ENERGY HUM]

 The rings of light seemed almost peaceful, harmless. You couldn't feel the energy they held until they made contact. The instant they touched Zane’s defenses, an overwhelming, absolute force radiated out, pinning him in place. He knew, with sickening certainty, that even with ten times his strength, he wouldn't be able to break free.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

[SFX: ENERGY IMPLOSION]

[MUSIC: CATASTROPHIC IMPACT]

The first circle of light exploded, triggering a chain reaction as the others followed suit. The energy packed inside them should have been enough to wipe the entire Ethereal Spirit Sect off the map. Instead of blasting outward, it did something impossible—it focused, collapsing all of its destructive power inward, directly onto Zane.

[SFX: THUNDEROUS CRASH]

Another deafening crash tore through the air.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

 Zane was shot backward like a cannonball. He rocketed past the Ethereal Spirit Hall, over the entire sect, across the plains, and soared clear over Ethereal City. Propelled by that incredible force, he was moving twenty times faster than his normal bursts of Vitality could ever carry him, covering several hundred miles in a matter of seconds.

[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]

[AMBIENT: FOREST]

 He finally slammed into a lush forest, the impact splintering hundreds of trees before his body skidded to a halt, carving a deep trench in the earth. The forest was now a disaster zone: a narrow path of destruction carved straight through the woods, littered with the shattered trunks of ancient trees.

[AMBIENT: QUIET FOREST]

[SFX: RUSTLING LEAVES]

 A small killer weasel, a fierce third-grade beast, scurried stealthily through the undergrowth. Its eyes locked onto Zane for a moment before it recoiled, vanishing back into the shadows. The raw aura bleeding from Zane’s broken body was a clear warning: this was not something to be trifled with.

Lying face down on the forest floor, Zane struggled to process what had happened, but it was useless. Was that really just one ten-millionth of the man's total strength? If he used his full power, could he shatter this entire boundless world with a single wave of his hand?

He couldn't figure it out. And he couldn't figure out why someone like that would appear here — or why he had to take Elara.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]

[AMBIENT: SECT HALL]

Back in the Ethereal Spirit Hall, no one spoke. The Guild leaders stood frozen, too awed to breathe. 

Every person in the hall watched him with wide, reverent eyes and didn't dare make a sound.

The young man looked around at them, then smiled pleasantly and shook his head. "I accidentally used a little too much. That was about three-ten-millionths of my power, not one. It's surprisingly difficult to control at such a small scale."

A collective shudder ran through the hall. Three-ten-millionths.

"Now then," the young man said, glancing toward the entrance of the hall. "I've pushed that boy rather far." He raised one hand and closed it gently around nothing.

[SFX: INVISIBLE FORCE SURGE]

Hundreds of miles away, Zane had barely gotten to his feet when something enveloped him — an invisible force, calm and absolute, lifting him off the ground. Before he could react, he was moving, pulled through the air at impossible speed. Within moments, he was standing back in the Ethereal Spirit Hall, exactly where he had been before.

Zane stared at the young man. The young man smiled back. "How do you feel?"

"Fine," Zane said.

"Good." The young man turned toward Elara. "Then I'll take her now."

"Wait." Zane's voice was quieter this time. Not a challenge — a request.

The young man looked back.

Zane held his gaze. He had run the calculation; there was no fight to be had here. For now, there was only one thing he wanted.

"I've been separated from her for a long time," Zane said. "Break the seal. Give me thirty minutes with her before you take her."

The young man considered this, then nodded. He stepped to Elara and lightly tapped the space between her brows with one finger and reversed the seal in the blink of an eye. 

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY RELEASE]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]

The act defied belief.

Reversing a six-sense seal was extraordinarily complex. Had they brought Elara to the World Merchants' Guild, specialists would have needed months—perhaps years—to undo it. The technique was connected to the same esoteric power the Guild used for secret transportation, so resource-intensive that they rarely used it. 

Using it merely to transport Inner-Core warriors was wasteful. But for the Soul Harvesting Ritual, Maddox had deemed it essential.

This man had reversed it with a flick of his wrist.

[SFX: SOFT GASP]

[MUSIC: GENTLE HOPEFUL STRINGS]

 Elara’s eyelids fluttered, then opened. Her eyes, finally seeing the world again, were filled with a deep, unsettling confusion.

Her senses had been sealed for over two years. Though she had continued her cultivation, her body and mind had been completely locked away from the world. 

For warriors over a hundred years old, who might enter secluded cultivation for a decade or even a century at a time, such a period would have been a minor inconvenience. But Elara was still so young. She had spent a tenth of her entire life trapped in a silent, senseless void. The experience was bound to leave scars on her mind and spirit.

[AMBIENT: ECHOING VOID]

 She was completely lost, her mind a swirl of doubt as she stared at the unfamiliar surroundings and the strange faces gathered around her.

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL PIANO]

Then, a familiar figure swam into view. A look of shock, then recognition, spread across her face. Her lips parted, and a single, hopeful word escaped.

"Brother?"
