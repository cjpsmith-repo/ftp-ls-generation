Ep 484 - A Bloody Victory

Word count: 1208

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

Independent fighters relied on diverse cultivation methods, each cultivator forging their own unique path. Although their ranks rarely matched those of prestigious sect disciples, they made up for it with cunning, adaptability, and techniques born from necessity rather than tradition.

Dawson was a perfect example. The man had wandered the Heartlands alone for years, surviving without the protection or resources of any sect. He'd even ventured into the legendary Chaotic Ocean of Stars—a realm where most cultivators perished within hours—and returned to tell the tale. True, he hadn't penetrated as deeply as the Spirit-Tribulation Realm masters who could withstand the spatial storms at the Ocean's heart, but for an Inner-Core fighter operating without backup, the achievement spoke volumes about his resourcefulness and will to survive.

Zane watched intently, noting how Dawson moved with the calm confidence of someone who'd learned every lesson the hard way.

[SFX: ENERGY SHIELD MATERIALIZING]

The instant Dawson lunged forward, a surge of earthy yellow Vitality erupted from his core, solidifying into a massive shield that encased his entire body. The defensive technique manifested so quickly it seemed almost instinctive—the kind of split-second reaction that only came from countless life-or-death encounters where hesitation meant annihilation.

The shield's surface rippled with dense, concentrated energy, its golden-brown hue reminiscent of ancient stone that had weathered millennia of storms. Zane could sense the technique's stability, the way it anchored Dawson's movements while maintaining flexibility. This was pure survival instinct refined into martial art.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

They called him the Divine Walker for a reason. His signature technique, the Traceless Snowstep, allowed him to move at blinding speed without leaving so much as a footprint in fresh snow. His entire combat philosophy revolved around explosive acceleration—closing the distance on an opponent in a single breath before unleashing a relentless barrage of strikes designed to overwhelm their defenses and grind them down to exhaustion.

The technique had become his trademark, earning him victories in more duels than he bothered to remember.

Segar might not have possessed widespread fame beyond the Mysterious Luna Hall's inner circles, but he represented one of the Heartlands' most enigmatic Grade-Three Orders. Dawson was confident, but years of surviving as an independent cultivator had taught him that overconfidence killed more warriors than lack of skill ever did. He committed fully from the opening exchange, channeling his Vitality without reservation.

You didn't reach the Inner-Core Realm without backing or resources unless you were both exceptionally talented and ruthlessly practical.

Across the arena, Segar stood motionless, his face slightly pale beneath the harsh illumination of the fighting ring. He simply watched as Dawson's massive form barreled toward him, the earth-aspected shield rippling with defensive power.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]

"My master always said the first battle needs a little blood," Segar remarked, his voice carrying an unsettling calm that contrasted sharply with the cruel smile twisting his otherwise plain features. "It's the only way to keep the crowd entertained. Sorry, but it looks like I'll have to borrow some of yours."

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: GROWING DANGER]

He crossed his fingers, gave a slight tug, and a few strands of black silk materialized between them. A heartbeat later, they shot toward Dawson like vipers striking from the shadows.

Dawson's entire fighting style relied on his earth-aspected cultivation and impenetrable defense. Years of training had forged that Vitality shield into something formidable—capable of withstanding techniques that would obliterate lesser cultivators.

[SFX: FABRIC TEARING]

Yet Segar's black silk sliced through the barrier as though it were tissue paper, wrapping tight around Dawson's body before he could even register the breach.

The threads coiled with lethal precision—around his arms, constricting his stomach, encircling his head, binding his legs. Each strand tightened incrementally, cutting into flesh with surgical cruelty.

Dawson's eyes widened in shock, then narrowed in pain. He struggled against the bindings, muscles straining, but the silk only dug deeper with each movement. A thin line of blood appeared where the threads pressed against his neck.

From his vantage point in the crowd, Zane leaned forward, studying the technique with grim fascination. The silk wasn't merely strong—it contained some quality that allowed it to bypass defensive barriers entirely. A Soul Realm technique, certainly, but one he'd never encountered before.

Segar's smile widened into something predatory. Satisfaction gleamed in his eyes as he surveyed his handiwork. His voice boomed across the arena, carrying to every corner of the hushed crowd.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

"Bloom!"

He crossed his hands and yanked.

[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD GASPS]

With a series of sickening, wet rips, the black silk bit deep. Bright red blood erupted from Dawson's body, showering the ground in a chaotic spray. From a distance, the gruesome display looked like a single, blossoming red rose.

In seconds, Dawson's dismembered body lay in scattered pieces across the ground.

The hundreds of thousands of warriors fell silent. They had expected death, but not such swift, visceral brutality. Even hardened fighters felt their stomachs turn.

Without deep enmity, most fighters wouldn't slaughter opponents so mercilessly. Many now eyed the unremarkable Segar with wariness—this Mysterious Luna Hall disciple possessed a ruthless streak.

"Segar of the Mysterious Luna Hall wins!" A referee waved his hand, summoning attendants to clear the arena.

The opening bout had ended in a single exchange. Only the bloody spectacle would be remembered.

Segar returned to the platform. Elder Luna nodded. "Well done, Segar."

Segar's features brightened with surprised delight. "Thank you, Master!"

Observing this, Zane's eyes gleamed. It was brutal, to be sure, but it suited his purpose. Only here can I kill Marilyn.

Kieran pulled two more nameplates. "Cloud Hall, Zane Lowell versus independent warrior, Alex Gresham!"

Zane showed no fear as he strode toward the arena.

[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

When the announcer called the next challenger from Cloud Hall, every head swiveled toward their members on the high platform. They had five competitors this time. But as the name was called, none of the five moved. The crowd murmured in confusion as, instead, two fighters emerged from the section where the independents were seated.

[SFX: CONFUSED CROWD MURMUR]

"What’s going on? Why's a guy from Cloud Hall coming from over there?"

"Whoa, are my eyes playing tricks on me? I didn't even see him before. Is that kid at the Soul Realm?"

[SFX: SHOCKED GASPS]

"He is at the Soul Realm! And he’s Fourth Grade Soul-Illuminated! The Fourth Grade! How the hell did someone at that level get through the pre-qualifiers?"

Fighters who cleared the pre-qualifiers usually had some buzz around them, becoming minor celebrities in their own right—like Alex Gresham, the eldest son of a Third-Grade House in Oracle City with close ties to the World Merchants' Guild.

But Zane was a nobody. He was new to The Heartlands and had spent every waking moment training since joining Cloud Hall. Outside of Michael Nash and a few others in the World Merchants' Guild, his name meant nothing.

Zane had been standing right there in the crowd, but with all eyes glued to the major sects, a Soul Realm fighter standing among a sea of Inner-Core cultivators had gone completely unnoticed.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]

Now, as he stepped forward, he was the center of attention. It was all the more surprising since, to a casual glance, the energy he gave off seemed far weaker than that of the other fighters.

But that would make surprising them all that much more fun.
