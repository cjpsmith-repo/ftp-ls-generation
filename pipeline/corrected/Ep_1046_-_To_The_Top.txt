Ep 1046 - To The Top

Word count: 1012

[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

For Ryder Golding, watching his brother struggle so hard only to end up with a Grade-Three Divine-Class weapon was pretty discouraging.

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

Matthew shoved himself off the floor, his jaw tight with frustration.

For someone of their status, a Grade-Three weapon was practically an insult. Standing to the side, Matthew said through gritted teeth, “I completely underestimated the dome’s pressure. Ryder, I’m going again!”

Another attempt would cost three hundred Sovereign Stones—the equivalent of three million supreme Aether crystals. For most warriors, that was a staggering fortune.

Of course, even for warriors from The Mist Palace, it wasn’t exactly pocket change. But Ryder didn’t hesitate.

[SFX: STONES CLINKING]

He reached into his pocket, pulled out a heavy pouch, and dumped three hundred shimmering Sovereign Stones onto the white-eyebrowed elder’s table.

The elder offered a faint smile.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

The moment he accepted the payment, Matthew shot forward like a bullet, flying back toward the dome.

He’d already wasted one chance; this time, he was determined to get something better, no matter what.

He was aiming for a Grade-One Divine-Class weapon. With his strength, a Grade-Two should have been within reach on his first attempt. Settling for a Grade-Three had stung his pride. He wasn’t here to play it safe — he was here to get it back with interest.

But that kind of ambition came with a price.

[SFX: HEAVY THUD]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

His sights were set so high that the crushing pressure slammed him back to the ground before he even got close. He came back empty-handed.

Watching from the side, Ryder’s frown deepened.

[AMBIENT: HALL OF ARMS]

[SFX: COLLECTIVE SIGH]

A collective sigh went through the other warriors in the Hall of Arms. Matthew had just burned through three hundred Sovereign Stones for nothing. What a waste.

This time, Matthew was too embarrassed to ask for another go. He just retreated to the sidelines in silence.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED TENSION]

Suddenly, Ryder stepped forward, placing four hundred Sovereign Stones before the elder. “Four hundred,” he said calmly. “Let me give it a try.”

Four hundred Sovereign Stones? Zane thought, his eyes widening. They just said it was three hundred. Why is he paying an extra hundred?

The chatter from the warriors around him quickly answered his question.

[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]

“Whoa. Ryder’s not playing around. That extra hundred means he’s going for the Grade-One weapons!”

“The man’s a beast.”

Zane said nothing, but his mind was already turning. Back in the Lower World — in the Heartlands, on the main continent — he’d always had top-grade weapons at hand. But they fell behind the moment he reached the Divine Kingdom Continent. Part of the reason was simple: he relied on his own body more than any weapon he carried, because his body was itself a weapon. But standing here now, watching the dome, he understood it differently. In the Upper World, a truly top-grade Divine Weapon wasn’t a luxury. It was a necessity.

Observe a while longer, he told himself.

The white-eyebrowed elder produced a picture scroll, its pages filled with illustrated Divine Weapons. He held it out to Ryder. “One Grade-One weapon is currently on loan to a deacon,” he said. “Sixteen others are available. Choose any four — they’ll be placed in the dome. That’s what the extra hundred covers.”

Ryder took the scroll and scanned it.

He was a sword cultivator. He picked four swords from the sixteen options without much deliberation and handed the scroll back. The elder nodded, stretched out a hand, and flicked his fingers.

[SFX: FOUR CHIMES]

Four red orbs of light rose into the dome, drifting up through the white ones until they reached the very top — faint and distant, but unmistakable.

The watching warriors fell quiet for a moment. Grade-One Divine Weapons sat at the top of the dome for a reason — they were incentives, but also the hall’s own reserve. Elders and deacons borrowed them when they needed a weapon and returned them afterward.

In all the years the dome had stood, warriors from Groundstone Hall and Cloudswallow Hall combined had almost never successfully claimed one.

Ryder didn’t move immediately. He reached into his Storage Ring and swallowed a pill — something that would push his Vitality by at least twenty percent. It was worth thirty or forty Sovereign Stones on its own. He’d already committed four hundred. The pill was just the final piece.

He took a slow breath.

[AMBIENT: ENERGY DOME HUM]

[MUSIC: EPIC RISING]

With that, Ryder began to rise.

He rose deliberately — almost lazily. The dome’s gravity was tied to the cultivator’s power; the moment he surged, it would surge to match. The delay before it adjusted was barely a breath long, but it was the only edge available. So he climbed slowly, letting his power build in increments, buying himself every inch he could.

White orbs of light, each holding a Grade-Three weapon, drifted all around him. He didn’t even glance their way, his gaze fixed upward.

[SFX: MAGICAL BREAKTHROUGH]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

Soon, he broke through to the next level, where the orbs glowed with the light of Grade-Two Divine-Class weapons.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

[SFX: WHISPERS ECHO]

The whispers in The Hall of Arms started up again.

“He’s already at the Grade-Twos. He should just grab one of those!”

“Yeah, a Grade-Two is nothing to sneeze at. But he paid for a shot at a Grade-One. He’s going all in.”

“That’s a tough gamble. If he fails, he could walk away with nothing.”

“I heard from someone in Groundstone Hall that the last time a Grade-One Divine Weapon was actually taken from this dome was about a hundred years ago,” another warrior muttered.

[MUSIC: INTENSE STRUGGLE]

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

By now, Ryder’s face was flushed crimson. Under the immense pressure, every inch upward was a grueling fight. He gritted his teeth and pushed, his muscles screaming in protest as he completely ignored the tempting white lights swirling just beyond his reach.

His entire focus was lasered in on the faint red glimmers far above—the Grade-One Divine-Class weapons.

[SFX: MAGICAL ASCENSION]

[MUSIC: EPIC RISING]

He clawed his way past the last of the Grade-Twos, finally entering the rarified space where the Grade-One weapons hovered.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CLIMAX]

[SFX: PULSATING ENERGY]

He was less than ten feet from the four pulsating red orbs.

As he stared up at them, a fierce, unyielding light burned in his eyes.

Ten feet had never felt so far.
