Ep 1316 - Chaos In The Cities

Word count: 1023

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL BATTLEFIELD]
Slade’s threat was still hanging in the air, Hailey’s fate seemingly sealed—when the voice cut through everything.
That was her voice—the mysterious announcer who’d explained the rules of the Illusion Battlefield all those times before. Only now, the rules had suddenly changed.
Up until this point, the sanctuaries had been a safe haven, protected by the battlefield’s Celestial Law. As long as you were inside one, you were completely safe, even from top-tier talents and saint-level geniuses. But as the battle wore on and the competition grew more intense, a small handful of people had managed to hoard most of the Illusion Points. 
Slade, for example, had racked up so many that he was ranked fourth in this entire supreme world. His plan was simple: just stay inside a sanctuary, and it would be almost impossible for anyone to catch up.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
But now, that was all over. The sanctuaries were no longer safe. They were about to become battlegrounds.
[SFX: WAR DRUMS]
With almost every warrior already gathered inside a city, the new rules meant one thing: open war.
[SFX: RUSHING SKULLS]
[SFX: BONE RATTLING]
Instantly, the small skulls that had been hovering just outside the city walls swarmed in, latching onto Zane’s back. They cascaded down from his shoulders, forming a gruesome cape of bone.
Slade had been laughing, a wild, manic sound that echoed through the square, but the announcer’s voice stopped him cold. He just froze, completely stunned.
A slow smile spread across Zane’s face. “Why aren’t you laughing anymore?”
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REALIZATION]
Before, Slade hadn’t been worried about dying. He had three Exemption Medallions; even if Zane killed him three times, he wouldn’t lose a single Illusion Point. Besides, after Zane took his Saint-Killing Spear, Slade never would have left the sanctuary. Inside the walls, Zane couldn’t touch him. But who could have possibly predicted this? That the one rule keeping him safe would be lifted at the worst possible moment?
A chill shot down his spine. As long as he held his fourth-place rank, he was guaranteed to advance to the next stage. Now, that was no longer a sure thing. Realizing just how much trouble he was in, Slade instinctively started backing away.
The chance to claim the spoils of war was now open to everyone, which meant anyone could be plundered.
[MUSIC: APOCALYPTIC CRESCENDO]
[AMBIENT: CITYWIDE MAGICAL EXPLOSIONS]
Across the city, flashes of brilliant light erupted as waves of raw power rolled through the air. It felt like the start of a total war between the races—something people had only whispered about for years, a coming cataclysm that would plunge the universe into chaos. This battlefield was starting to feel like a dress rehearsal for the end of the world.
It wasn’t only happening here. Across every supreme world in the Illusion Battlefield, the same hell was breaking loose—warriors turning on each other inside cities that had been safe only moments before, others bolting for the wilderness rather than risk it.
[SFX: MASSIVE SWORD STORM DESCENDING]
Somewhere in another world entirely, a woman moved her fingers like she was conducting an orchestra, and a storm of huge swords descended on a main city below her, wiping out most of the warriors sheltering inside it in a single stroke. She tilted her head, almost bored. “Lucky timing,” she murmured. “They all hid instead of running. I was about to give up and leave.”
Out beyond the battlefield, on the great ranking pyramid everyone could see, names surged and collapsed by the thousands—the fluctuations were wildest near the bottom, barely a ripple near the top. The strongest had the least to fear and the most to gain; where they’d once had to ration how much they could safely take from any one target, they could now simply take everything, from everyone, with nothing stopping them.
A similar reversal was already playing out elsewhere in the battlefield, though not in Zane’s own city—here, with the Halls of Causality exit packed full of strong warriors, no one could sweep the place clean that easily. Not even Zane.
“Protect yourselves,” Zane called back to the others before he moved.
Janet, Jeffrey, Coleman, Hailey, and Ansel pulled together at once, forming a tight knot in the chaos. Around them, the onlookers who’d been debating whether to fight or flee finally made their choices—the fastest vanished into the wilderness, while the slower ones were caught and forced to hand over half their Illusion Points before crawling back to life somewhere in the city. But the five of them had nothing to fear here. Together, they were strong enough that no one in this city could touch them—they could only take from others.
Zane didn’t let Slade get far.
[SFX: RAPID AERIAL PURSUIT]
Slade broke for the sky, rocketing upward the moment he cleared the square, putting several hundred feet between them in a heartbeat. But Zane was faster. He was already climbing after him, closing the gap before Slade had gone even a fraction of the distance he needed.
[AMBIENT: OPEN SKY]
By the time Slade leveled off thousands of feet above the city, Zane was already there, blocking his path.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
 Zane casually held the Saint-Killing Spear in one hand and his sword in the other.
He gave Slade a small, predatory smile. “So, should I kill you three times? Or five? Your choice.”
If Slade died once, he’d lose half his points. Twice, and he’d lose three-quarters. After three deaths, he’d be left with barely an eighth of his current total. His face darkened. If Zane killed him three times, he’d drop out of the top one hundred thousand.
That was a loss he couldn’t afford.
[SFX: WEAPON MATERIALIZATION]
[SFX: SPEAR SPINNING]
With a flick of his wrist, a long purple spear appeared in his hand. It was a Grade-One Divine Weapon, his own signature spear, and an impressive piece of equipment. As the son of a Supreme Lord, he had access to treasures an ordinary warrior could only dream of.
Still, it was nothing compared to the Saint-Killing Spear.
