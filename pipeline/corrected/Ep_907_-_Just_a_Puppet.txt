Ep 907 - Just a Puppet

Word count: 1139

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The Dragon-Blooded Archer might have been just a puppet, but it was designed to be the greatest archer of all time. 

The moment its eyes locked onto him, Zane felt a shiver slither down his spine, the primal fear of a sheep being stalked by a wolf.

“So, if he shoots you, are you gonna die?” Zane asked, his voice a low murmur in their shared mind.

It was a known fact that a Spirit Supreme Realm warrior’s body was leagues stronger than someone from the Soul Sea Realm, but Zane was curious just how much tougher they really were.

“Are you insane?” Brianna shot back, her thoughts laced with fury. “His bow is a semi-divine weapon, and his arrows are actual Divine-Class artifacts. You think I can just block those with my body?”

Divine-tier arrows. The words landed with the weight of a physical blow, and Zane felt the earlier shiver morph into a full-body chill. An eighth-grade sacred place, he thought. They had to be, if even a puppet guard was armed with weapons of that caliber. No wonder the light from that black arrow had been so blinding. A knot of pure dread tightened in his gut.

[SFX: BOW CREAKING]

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Out on the surface of the golden sea, the Dragon-Blooded Archer had already notched one of those black arrows onto its semi-divine longbow and was drawing a bead right on them.

Zane’s eyes narrowed, every muscle tensing to charge. But just as he was about to move, the girl’s voice echoed in his head again. “Wait, are you really just going to rush in? You're planning to use my life force—my body—to kill him? Just like that?”

“What other choice do we have?” he retorted.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

The raw power and physical might of a Spirit Supreme Realm warrior were more than enough to overwhelm anyone from the Soul Sea Realm. The archer might be a monster, but it wouldn't be able to match his speed.

“But to even activate my—Hey!”

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Before she could finish her protest, Zane was already gone, ignoring her completely as he shot straight for the archer.

[AMBIENT: CROWD GASPS]

From below, the other warriors gasped.

“Zane's attacking! He's charging the statue!”

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

Every single one of them held their breath, watching as the agile figure of the young girl soared through the air and dove down with terrifying speed.

The wind screamed past Zane's ears. His golden eyes were fixed on the Archer below — and he saw it move. The black arrow shifted. 

Then the bow released.

[SFX: THREE-STAGE EXPLOSION]

The arrow accelerated in three consecutive bursts, each explosion amplifying its speed until it became something barely visible. Zane twisted in the air, his body spiraling into a sharp, diagonal shape — and the arrow grazed his ear. The turbulence from its passage dragged across his cheek like a blade.

“Be careful!” Brianna snapped. “That's my face!”

“Sorry,” Zane said, grinning despite himself. “Your skin is just very… delicate.”

He was already close. Below him, dozens of blue arrows traced the path of his dive — but they were nowhere near fast enough to catch him.

The Archer was intelligent enough to know what was coming. The moment Zane closed in, it turned and retreated — leaping sideways across the surface of the sea with startling agility. 

Zane blinked. It wasn't walking on water. Now that he was this close, he could see the tops of stone pillars just beneath the surface, spaced perfectly for the Archer to hop between them. From a distance, it looked like it was skimming the sea. In reality, it had never left solid ground.

An archer being approached was an archer in trouble, and the Archer knew it. But Zane wasn't letting it open distance.

[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: CHASE THEME]

They bounced across the pillars in a blur. Zane gathered Vitality into his hand and condensed it — not into anything elaborate, just a thin blade of force. It was temporary, makeshift. But Vitality at the Spirit Supreme Realm carried the laws of Brianna's inner world inside it. Even a crude edge like this was something no Soul Sea warrior could have formed.

The Archer couldn't shake him. So it stopped.

It planted its foot on a submerged pillar and stamped down hard.

[SFX: EXPLOSION OF WATER]

A golden wave erupted from the surface, spraying upward and flooding Zane's vision for a split second. In that gap, the Archer had already drawn and nocked. Three feet away. Point blank. Its scarlet eyes stared directly into his.

Then it opened its mouth.

“Quack quack… go… die…”

The words were in the human tongue, grinding and mechanical, like language being chewed through stone. Zane's eyes widened slightly.

The bowstring released. Zane tilted his head — just barely — and the black light burned past his ear for the second time.

He smiled. “Not that easy.”

At this range, the arrow hadn't reached its full acceleration. It had left the bow with three explosions still ahead of it. Before those detonations could compound its power, it was already past him.

The Archer swung the bow itself like a weapon, whipping the bowstring at his neck.

Zane's hand shot out. Brianna's fingers closed around the bowstring — and caught it.

[SFX: SHARP CUT]

Blood bloomed immediately. The string sliced into her fingertips.

“Hey!” Brianna's voice cracked with outrage. “Can you please remember that this is my body?!”

Zane winced internally. His own body was practically impervious — swords, spears, raw force, none of it left a mark. He kept forgetting that flesh and blood worked differently. Even at the Spirit Supreme Realm.

He didn't apologize this time. Instead, he drew the bowstring back and released it in one sharp motion.

[SFX: WHIP CRACK]

A thin, deadly line—like a snapped bowstring—hissed through the air, sending shimmering ripples across the surface of the sea below.

The Dragon-Blooded Archer froze on the spot, its scarlet eyes fixed on him. 

[SFX: BLADE SLICE]

[SFX: HEAVY THUD]

A moment later, a perfect, thin line appeared on its neck, and its entire head slid clean off its body. The cut was so smooth it revealed the puppet’s insides, which were made of a strange, soft jade. As the head tumbled onto a stone pillar, Zane lunged, reaching for the bow and quiver still clutched in the puppet's hands.

There was no way he was letting a semi-divine longbow and a quiver of divine-tier arrows just sink into the sea. That would be insane.

But just as his fingers brushed against the prize, the headless body twitched. Apparently, losing its head didn't mean it was done fighting. It snatched a black arrow from the quiver and tried to stab him in the face.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE BOOM]

Just as it raised the arrow, a loud BOOM echoed across the water. The headless puppet’s arm suddenly went limp, its attack cut short.

Zane, on the other hand, just smiled. 

[SFX: CRUSHING IMPACT]

He lifted his foot and brought his heel down hard, crushing the Dragon-Blooded Archer's jade head into dust.
