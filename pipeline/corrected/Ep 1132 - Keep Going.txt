Ep 1132 - Keep Going

Word count: 1066

[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND PALACE]
[MUSIC: DARK MYSTERIOUS]
The Dark Underground Palace wasn’t exactly a secret in the Dark World. Plenty of people knew about it, but for the last hundred thousand years, no one had managed to get into its mysterious depths.
Legend told of a Supreme Lord from a forgotten era who died here hundreds of millions of years ago. Even Supreme Lords faced mortality - unable to ascend to True Godhood, they eventually succumbed to the Five Aging Processes. Before death, this ancient Supreme Lord channeled incredible power to construct a massive underground palace, sealing his complete knowledge and legacy within - an incomparable prize for any warrior.
The catch was that the palace opened at random times, and the method for getting in was different every single time. Over the countless years since it was built, many warriors had found their way inside and come out with huge rewards. Still, getting in wasn’t something just any schmuck could pull off.
Lavender had been inside once before, ten thousand years ago, the last time the palace had opened. She’d found a ton of resources, and it had changed everything for her. So, when she heard that Lincoln had found a new way in, she immediately convinced Zane to go with him. She could still remember the spoils.
The entrance to the Underground Palace was no secret either. Every soul in the Dark World knew roughly where it was. What had changed was that over the years — more than ten years of ordinary life building up around it — civilians had settled right on top of it. Homes, yards, the quiet business of people who had no idea what lay beneath their feet.
Lincoln led the group through without slowing down. No one moved to stop them.
“This is it,” Lincoln said, stopping at the threshold. He and Zane stood at the front.
Adrian, just behind them, smiled. “Everyone already knows where the door is. It’s just the road inside that tends to be the problem.” He gestured ahead with easy deference. “After you, Lincoln.”
The seven brothers filed in behind them in silence. They hadn’t said much since Adrian had smoothed things over back at the manor. They didn’t need to. As far as they were concerned, the agreement held until Lincoln opened the palace. After that, there was no reason to keep anyone around — except Adrian, who still had his uses. The others could be dealt with once the hard part was done. None of this showed on their faces.
[MUSIC: GRAND ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: PALACE INTERIOR]
In the Magnolia Sanctum, a tenth-tier power established by the human race, a massive palace reached into the clouds. Two warriors in black robes hurried inside.
“Saint Lord, a few more warriors entered the Underground Palace today,” one of the warriors reported, bowing his head.
From his throne, their leader’s face was unreadable. “Who are they? What race? Their cultivation levels?”
“Human warriors, sir. Eleven of them. Seven are at the Spirit Supreme Realm, and the other four are at the Soul Sea Realm,” the warrior answered.
[SFX: DISMISSIVE CHUCKLE]
The Saint Lord shook his head. “Every year, some overconfident fools try to force the palace open, hoping to find some treasure.”
“But, Saint Lord,” the warrior ventured, “there’s no set pattern for when it opens. Maybe they’ll actually succeed.”
A faint smile touched the Saint Lord’s lips. “It doesn’t matter. I’ve already set up a monitoring array in the palace. If it opens, I’ll know immediately. And if they really do manage it, that just saves us the trouble. I’ll send our own disciples in after them.”
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND PASSAGE]
As Zane and the others made their way deeper, all three of the biggest powers in the Dark World had somehow gotten word of their attempt. For now, they were just watching. Warriors tried to get into the Underground Palace all the time, but no one had succeeded since the last official opening a hundred thousand years ago.
That didn’t mean the three great powers didn’t care. The palace was a place of immense strategic importance. Thanks to a series of political protocols, the three powers coexisted in this region with a surprising lack of friction—they even had trade agreements. But that was a local anomaly. Elsewhere, their races were still locked in endless war. 
It wasn’t just the three great powers watching, either. Every seventh- and eighth-tier sect with any foothold in the Dark World had placed their own eyes at the entrance — or somewhere inside the passage. The moment Zane’s group had stepped through, word had begun moving through a dozen different channels at once.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
Whichever group of warriors finally claimed the palace’s core treasures would tip the balance of power for good. After all, this was the tomb of a Supreme Lord, and though it had been opened many times, its greatest secrets were still locked away.
[SFX: ECHOING FOOTSTEPS SLOWING]
Up ahead, they saw a solid wall. It looked like they’d finally reached the end of the passage. 
[SFX: DISAPPOINTED SIGHS]
But as they got closer, their hope curdled into disappointment. It was just a sharp bend in the tunnel. Around the corner, another endless passage stretched out before them.
“This damned passage,” one of the brothers muttered, loud enough for everyone to hear. “How long can it possibly be?”
“Show some patience,” Abner said, his voice measured. “This palace was built by a Supreme Lord. You expected it to be small?”
“I expected there to be an end to it,” the brother shot back. “We’ve been moving for hours. If this keeps up, it’ll take two or three months just to reach whatever’s inside.”
A few of the others said nothing, but the restlessness was visible.
“Keep going,” Abner said, his voice laced with frustration. 
“Wait,” Lincoln said suddenly.
Every head snapped toward him. “What is it?” Abner demanded, his impatience making his voice sharp.
Lincoln didn’t even bother to look at him. He couldn’t stand the brutes. The only reason the seven brothers were even here was because of some deal with Adrian. 
[SFX: Storage Ring ACTIVATION]
[SFX: OBJECT PRESSED AGAINST STONE]
Ignoring them completely, Lincoln pulled a strange, patterned object—a divine texture—from his Storage Ring and pressed it flat against the stone wall.
He closed his eyes and focused, pouring his Vitality into the object. 
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT CHAMBER]
[SFX: PULSING GREEN LIGHT HUMMING]
A faint green light began to pulse from it, casting an eerie glow on the stone. 
[SFX: STONE WALL GRINDING]
[SFX: DUST FALLING]
With a grinding scrape, the wall slid sideways in a shower of dust and ancient cobwebs, revealing a new, hidden passage.
