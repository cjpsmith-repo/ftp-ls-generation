Ep 190 - Finding Dustin
Word Count: 1038
[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION ROOM]
While Saint Augustus and Saint Viana plotted his downfall, Zane focused on calming his thoughts, contemplating his future course of action. 
Perched cross-legged on the floor of his chamber, he gazed intently at the Tapestry of Sun, Moon, and Stars that hung on the wall before him. The ancient cloth seemed to shimmer with celestial energy, drawing his attention into its intricate patterns as if they held answers to his mounting questions.
He'd avoided Emberfin Lake for several days, mainly due to his dwindling point reserves. The substantial wealth he had accumulated after slaughtering the blade locusts and extracting the crystal core from their queen had made him the envy of every junior disciple throughout the Skyreach Sect. 
To his peers, a few thousand points represented an unimaginable fortune—enough to fund months of comfortable cultivation. Zane had exercised remarkable restraint, spending only modest amounts on healing potions and a single vitality elixir to enhance his rigorous training regimen. A faint smile crossed his lips as he recalled the shocked expressions when word of his earnings had spread through Misty Peak.
His trip to the Celestial Library had merely cost him a hundred points—a trivial sum compared to his reserves. Yet this perspective shifted dramatically for those in higher cultivation tiers. For a senior disciple engaged in advanced practices, that same amount of points would barely last ten days of intensive training—maybe half a month if they were lucky. The realization of how quickly wealth evaporated at higher levels filled Zane with a creeping anxiety about his future path.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
 The Sect's point system was brutally practical. If you wanted access to the special training grounds to get stronger, and fast, you had to run missions for the Sect. Then you could sink the points you earned right back into your training.
He'd once dreamed of earning millions of points in no time. Looking back, he realized how naive he'd been. The truth was, it wasn't just about earning points—he had to spend them, too. At this rate, collecting millions felt like a pipe dream.
Besides running missions, there was one other way: buying points with crystals. The going rate was two crystals for one point, but Zane knew he’d have to be careful to avoid drawing the wrong kind of attention.
Outside of Dustin, Zane couldn't think of anyone else he could trust with something so sensitive.
“We're good friends,” he thought. “And he's already helping me sell the Celestial Essence. His family's loaded, so it's not like he needs to scam me. Still… trust but verify.” It couldn't hurt to be cautious.
He left for Dustin's place, a plan forming in his head.
[AMBIENT: EMPTY ROOM]
 But when he got there, the room was empty.
After asking around, he learned Dustin wasn't home; he'd gone to train on one of Misty's smaller peaks. Without a second thought, Zane set off to find him.
Several of the lesser peaks surrounding Misty Peak were used for intensive training. They were nothing compared to the Sect’s guarded Attunement Sanctums, but at least these spots were free for any disciple from Misty Peak.
Falcon's Perch, it turned out, was one of them.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WINDS]
[SFX: DISTANT ECHOES]
[SFX: CREAKING WOODEN BRIDGE]
Reaching Misty's main peak required crossing a treacherous path carved directly into a vertical cliff face, rendering it virtually inaccessible to all but the most daring. Masters of the Soul Realm had driven wooden stakes into the rock, fashioning a crude bridge of steps across the yawning chasm. The weathered wood creaked in the mountain winds, each stake seemingly ready to surrender its grip on the ancient stone at any moment.
Most junior disciples would take one look at the bottomless drop and be too terrified to even try it. The path represented not just a physical challenge but a mental barrier—a test that separated the truly determined from those who merely claimed ambition. Even seasoned Adepts often chose longer, safer routes rather than trust their lives to these precarious wooden footholds suspended between existence and oblivion.
[SFX: WIND GUSTING]
 Standing at the edge, Zane glanced over at Falcon's Perch, just a short distance away. Then, without hesitating, he leaped onto the first stake.
The path looked insane, but for someone with enough nerve, the footing was solid.
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS ON WOODEN STAKES]
Sprinting from stake to stake, Zane reached the top of Falcon's Perch in minutes. He’d heard Dustin had been training here a lot lately, so he was pretty sure he'd find him.
Dustin's massive frame shuddered with each ragged breath he took. Sweat streamed down his face, completely soaking his white robes.
While most guys his size might be lazy, Dustin was the exact opposite. He was relentless, and few could match the sheer effort he poured into his training.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WINDS]
 Falcon's Perch was a round, flat-topped peak, with the main peak of Misty rising like a titan to its right. The side of the main peak facing Falcon's Perch was a sheer cliff, smooth as if sliced by a giant blade, offering almost no handholds.
Even wearing a heavy, weighted vest, Dustin was stalled out, trying to scale that impossible wall. He was chasing Flynn’s record of a hundred fifty feet, a feat most junior disciples from Misty Peak couldn't even dream of attempting.
Honestly, as a son of a rich and powerful family, Dustin could just coast. He could take over the family business one day and, even as a basic warrior still refining his body, order around masters in the Adept Realm.
Like they say, money talks. All he'd have to do is pay them enough, and for the House Blackwood, that was pocket change.
The Blackwood heirs were born with a knack for business, but when it came to cultivation, they were leagues behind the descendants of the Seven Great Houses.
But Dustin was different. Ever since joining the Skyreach Sect, his only goal was to break through to the Adept Realm on his own terms.
[SFX: SUDDEN APPEARANCE]
Just as Dustin gathered himself to charge the wall again, Zane appeared on Falcon's Perch, materializing right in his path and shattering his focus.
