Ep 668 - Inside The Mad Scorpion

Word count: 1094

[AMBIENT: DARK CAVERNOUS RUMBLING]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Without their powers, Malcolm and Esther were just as fragile as anyone else. They’d always relied on their Vitality and raw energy to keep them from harm, but now, tumbling through the darkness, they were getting battered and dizzy just like the rest of them.

The crab ornament on Zane’s chest, normally fed by his own power, had gone dark, its protective orange shield completely gone.

[SFX: MASSIVE ENERGY BLAST]

By some miracle, the beam of Dark Nether Death Light that had erupted from the Mad Scorpion’s head had missed them. If that red blast had connected, they’d all have been vaporized on the spot.

[AMBIENT: DEEP ECHOING VOID]

[SFX: WIND RUSHING]

As they continued to fall, everyone lost their grip on Zane and scattered. The inside of this colossal beast was far bigger than any of them could have imagined. The Mad Scorpion had been perched right on the edge of an abyss, so there was no telling how deep its body went. The part they had seen was already monstrously huge, like the tip of some terrifying, living iceberg stretching down into the endless dark.

Zane, at least, was doing fine. He’d bumped into a few things on the way down, sure, but his body was tougher than solid steel. When a giant rock that had been sucked into the beast’s gullet slammed into him, he didn’t even flinch.

[SFX: ROCK SHATTERING]

The rock, however, shattered into a thousand pieces.

Everyone else was in real trouble. Without their Vitality, Esther, Fabian, and the others had no protection. In this state, even a small, stray rock could cut them wide open. Thankfully, the inner walls of the scorpion were soft and fleshy. If they hadn’t been, just bouncing around inside its body would have been enough to kill them.

They finally skidded to a stop on a slick surface and spent the next ten minutes crawling around in the pitch-black, trying to get their bearings.

[SFX: GROUND COLLAPSING]

Suddenly, Esther let out a muffled yelp as the ground gave way beneath them, sending them tumbling once more until they finally hit the real bottom.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL GLOW]

After sliding through the slimy passage, they were stunned to find a magical, glowing light illuminating the cavernous stomach.

But Zane was the only one in any shape to really take a look around.

Besides him and Yolande, everyone was hurt. Everyone who could move reached for their healing medicines. Esther had it the worst; her clothes were stained red with blood from several deep cuts. Even Malcolm and Timothy, both built like tanks, were injured. Timothy limped badly as he struggled to his feet, and Malcolm’s face was so swollen it was almost comical. Fabian and Jarrod, on the other hand, looked completely unscathed.

And what Zane was looking at made no sense.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WONDER]

This wasn't a stomach cavity. Or rather, it was — but it was something else entirely. The space stretched out in every direction, boundlessly vast. Above them was a sky. Blue, open, real. Mountains rose in the distance. A river wound between them. Plants grew along its banks. If he hadn't known where he was, he would have sworn he was standing somewhere in the open world.

"There's a self-contained world inside its stomach?" he asked.

Yolande nodded. "Yes. This is a self-contained world the Mad Scorpion carries within itself."

Esther, her wounds bound, walked over and looked up at the sky. "After reaching the Soul Sea Realm," she said quietly, "a warrior's Vitality transforms into an ocean — a haze that forms a world of its own within the body. That's why it's called the Soul Sea Realm. But a Soul Sea warrior's body still can't nurture living things. Only at a higher level does an internal world begin to truly form."

Yolande nodded once. "In the bodies of the strongest in the upper world, their internal worlds contain entire countries. Tens of millions of creatures. Wars."

The group went quiet.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]

Zane stared at the sky. The thought arrived without warning: the universe itself, with all its countless worlds — could that also be inside some creature's body?

He said it aloud.

Lavender had already slipped back out. She smiled slightly. "No one can answer that."

"Why not?"

"Because people have been searching for billions of years," she said. "The greatest minds in the upper world have looked for the edge of the universe — an exit, a boundary, anything. No one has ever found one."

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Malcolm wasn’t in the mood to wait. He reached for Yolande with his massive hands, but just as he lunged, she shifted her weight. Even in her clogs, she was impossibly quick, leaping onto his bulky arm. She took two light steps up his bicep, wrapped her own arms around his forearm, and threw her entire body weight down.

[SFX: BONE CRACK]

[SFX: PAINFUL SCREAM]

The sickening sound of bone snapping echoed in the chamber. Malcolm let out a guttural scream, his thick arm going limp and hanging uselessly at his side.

Timothy stared, stunned, and started to charge forward, but Yolande had already dropped back to the ground. Her small frame was a stark, dangerous contrast to Malcolm’s crumpled form. She shot Timothy an icy look.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

“If you want to get out of here and make it back to the Tower alive,” she warned, her voice cutting through the tension, “you’ll shut up and do exactly as I say.”

A wave of shock rippled through the group. She had just broken Malcolm’s arm with a single, simple twist. She might have been a powerful War General, but this was different. This was raw skill. With no powers to draw on, she’d used pure technique to disable a man twice her size.

Ignoring their stunned faces, Yolande turned and started walking ahead. Malcolm, clutching his shattered arm, pulled a pill from his pocket and swallowed it. Even with a potent healing medicine, it would take days for the bone to fully mend.

As he watched her go, a thought suddenly struck Zane, and his eyes lit up with understanding.

[AMBIENT: CAVE ECHO]

Down here, in the belly of this beast, no one could use their Vitality. That meant only the most basic, physical fighting styles actually worked. What Yolande had just used was Serpent’s Coil—a fundamental martial arts technique.

Even though she was a fraction of Malcolm’s strength, her perfect form had made all the difference.
