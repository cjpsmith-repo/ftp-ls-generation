Ep 1022 - Zane’s Predicament

Word count: 1064

[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND]

[MUSIC: DARK BROODING STRINGS]

Even after killing the three warriors, Zane couldn’t shake the thought nagging at the back of his mind.

Standing right beside him, Freya could tell something was off. He was in a dark mood, and when she tried to talk to him, he just tuned her out.

What Zane was wrestling with wouldn’t have even registered as an issue for most warriors. They all fought and scraped for power, but few had a true endgame.

Early on, Elara had been his guide. Maybe it was fate. Even now, she was the force that kept him going, his main reason for pushing forward.

But finding Elara was a necessity, not his ultimate goal. No, his true purpose was rooted in the deepest, most stubborn part of his being.

In fact, this wasn’t the first time Zane had faced something like this. When Elara was taken, that crisis came from outside — from an enemy he could name and target. Defeating that enemy was enough to break free.

This time was different. This dilemma came from within himself, a doubt about the very goal he’d been chasing his whole life. That made it far more dangerous. The stronger his obsession had been, the harder this fall.

[MUSIC: BROODING ORCHESTRAL]

His real obsession was mastering the martial arts. He had to know the absolute limits of his own strength. Could he one day bend this world to his will? Or maybe even create one of his own?

But after reaching the Upper World, he’d discovered the rules of the game were exactly the same as in the Lower World.

A wave of disgust washed over him, quickly souring into confusion. If reaching higher levels and ascending to this world just meant more of the same—the same struggles, the same hierarchies—then what was the point? He couldn’t find any reason to care.

The day’s mining reflected his state of mind. He stayed out of the deep levels, went through the motions, and came back with nothing but a handful of top-grade Sovereign Stones. It was a wasted day, and he knew it.

[AMBIENT: MINING CAVERN]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

Newcomers might not have known who Rylan was, but the old-timers knew damn well not to cross him, even if he was just another transplant from the Lower World. The guy had a reputation for being more dangerous than the armored warriors of the Soulbound Sect—and that was saying something.

The armored warriors, at least, were bound by the Sect’s rules. But Rylan wasn’t bound by anything. He did whatever the hell he wanted. Despite coming from the Lower World, he held the power of life and death over everyone in this mine.

[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION SPACE]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Zane was still sitting quietly, eyes shut tight, wrestling with the question that plagued him.

Even the Cobalt Dragon sensed the shift in Zane’s spirit.

But it said nothing, only watching Zane’s every move.

The dragon had already guessed Zane was caught in a crisis of faith—a dangerous place for any warrior. What made it worse was that the more driven and confident Zane had been, the harder this fall would be.

No one could help him now. Not even the Cobalt Dragon’s wisdom would do any good. Zane had to get through this on his own—win the war inside his own head. It was a battle of perspective, a fundamental struggle no one could interfere with. To change his mind would be to change his very soul, wiping away who he was and leaving a stranger in his place.

Freya sat nearby, saying nothing. After what she’d seen today, she understood something clearly: without Zane, she’d have been like the other female ascenders here — dependent on whoever was strongest, with no real choice in the matter. She was lucky, and she knew it.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

Just then, Rylan strode up to Zane.

Zane’s eyes snapped open, sensing the presence of a Soul Sea Realm warrior. He looked up, his face a complete blank. He’d never met the guy, but it didn’t take a genius to put two and two together. This had to be the one who’d sent those three warriors after him.

“Where are they?” Rylan asked, his voice like ice.

“Who?” Zane asked.

Rylan let out a cold laugh. “Don’t play dumb. My three warriors. You’re going to pretend you haven’t seen them?”

“Oh, right. Them,” Zane said with a casual shrug. “I killed them.”

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

“You killed them? All by yourself?” Rylan’s voice dripped with disbelief.

Zane sighed, a long, weary sound. “That’s right. Just me.”

[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]

Nearby warriors, whether they’d been training or resting, froze, their attention snapping to the confrontation.

A Life-and-Death Realm warrior was staring down someone from the Soul Sea Realm. And on top of that, the new guy was claiming he killed three of Rylan’s men.

Was this guy for real? Or just suicidal?

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Rylan’s face flushed, his fists clenching at his sides as he fought to control his temper. He couldn’t stand this guy. His plan had been simple: intimidate the new blood, make him another one of his lackeys. He never expected to be not only ignored but to have this kind of bullshit thrown in his face.

Every instinct screamed at him to attack Zane right then and there, but something held him back. Grinding his teeth, he swallowed his rage. “You’ve got two choices. Accept a slave imprint and work for me — or go into the deep mine tomorrow and don’t come back.” He turned to leave.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

But Zane wasn’t done.

He was a blur of motion, reappearing in an instant to block Rylan’s path. He slapped his own head and laughed — a pure, unguarded sound that caught everyone off guard. “I know why I’m tired,” he said. “I’ve heard words like yours my whole life.”

Rylan stared at him. He genuinely couldn’t tell if this kid was brave, stupid, or broken.

Zane’s smile faded. He looked Rylan dead in the eye. “But knowing why I’m disgusted doesn’t give me the answer. What’s the point of all this power? Do you even know?”

Rylan tried to step around him, but Zane mirrored his move, blocking him again. Now Rylan was visibly shaking with rage.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

The onlookers stared, wide-eyed. One of them muttered under his breath, “He’s lost his damn mind. Must’ve been scared senseless by Rylan. There’s no other way he’d have the balls to block his path.”

But they didn’t know Zane.
