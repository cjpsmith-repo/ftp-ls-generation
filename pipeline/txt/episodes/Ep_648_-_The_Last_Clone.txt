Ep 648 - The Last Clone

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
Zane looked on helplessly as Fabian blocked his path. It was pointless trying to explain; Fabian couldn’t possibly understand what was driving him, and Zane had no intention of sharing his secret with anyone.
To Fabian and Esther, charging in there now wasn’t just reckless—it was a death sentence. But with the eruption of the Tide of Chaos, everything in the inner circle had changed.
“Move,” Zane said, his voice calm but firm. “I have something I have to do.”
Jarrod stepped up beside Fabian, forming a solid wall. “Zane, you’re too important to the Violet Heart Sanctuary. We can’t let you go any further. You’ll die in there!”
A faint, almost bitter smile touched Zane’s lips. “Really? And what if this is more important? Should I give up on it just because I matter to your Sanctuary?” He held their gazes until, one by one, they looked away in shame.
“But why would you have to give up on yourself?” Jarrod asked, his confusion genuine.
Zane just shook his head. There was no way to make them understand. “You don’t have to come with me. I’ll go alone.”
[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]
Meanwhile, Lavender looked down at Esther Sterling, who was kneeling on the ground. “What are you doing?” she asked, her brow furrowed.
“My Queen,” Esther said, her head bowed. “The inner circle isn’t safe. You should stay here. Once the demonic beasts retreat, we can leave.”
But Esther's calm words masked a deeper fear. She knew something the others didn't. Back in the upper realm, Lavender's statue had already gone dim, which meant the sword spirit standing before them was Lavender's last divine clone. If Zane died here, this clone would vanish with him. And that meant Lavender herself would fall.
It was not something Esther could allow.
Lavender's expression didn't waver. "My affairs are not yours to decide," she said, her voice cold and final.
Esther clenched her jaw. She had no standing to argue — not with Lavender, not even close. Neither did Amber. No one in the Demon Night did. But she could make a choice of her own.
"Then I will follow at my Queen's side," Esther said, lifting her head with quiet resolve.
Behind her, the remaining Demon Night War Lords looked at one another. Then, one by one, they knelt. Their faces were set. They understood what entering the inner circle likely meant. They followed anyway.
[MUSIC: SOLEMN ORCHESTRAL]
When Fabian saw it — the Demon Night kneeling, committing, ready to march into the inner circle without hesitation — something shifted in him. He pressed a hand to his forehead and let out a long breath.
"Fine," he said at last. "I suppose we're going to the inner circle."
Sometimes, there was no choice. Circumstances made it for you.
And the demonic beasts in the outer layer showed no sign of retreating anyway. Waiting here was its own kind of death. In the end, they turned and followed Zane deeper in.
[AMBIENT: DEMONIC BEAST SWARM]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Not long after Zane and the others pressed forward, three streaks of light shot in the opposite direction near the Bloody Pass. The surviving Twilight Group War Lords were still alive — barely. Their concealment technique kept them ghostlike and invisible, drifting as smoke through the swarming beasts. But maintaining it was bleeding them dry.
Every time their reserves dropped to thirty percent, the smoke would thin, and they'd flicker back into reality — right into the teeth and claws of the beasts surrounding them. They'd sprint a short distance, slam down Vitality crystals to replenish, then dissolve back into smoke and repeat. Over and over, burning through their supply at a ruinous rate. Half their crystals were already gone. The math wasn't good.
"What do we do, brother?" one of them asked, his voice a frantic thread of Vitality.
“How would I know?” came the reply.
“Someone has to lure these damn things away!” another chimed in.
The three of them huddled in the thick, smoky air, their panicked thoughts the only thing connecting them.
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY SURGE]
Just as despair began to set in, two lights ignited the path ahead—one black, one red. The lights grew, resolving into two figures as massive as mountains.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
For a moment, the three War Lords were stunned into silence. Then, one of them breathed, “I think… I think we might be saved.”
“It’s Malcolm!” the other exclaimed. “They’re coming!”
A third voice cut in, awed and bewildered. "Malcolm's cultivation — is that… War Sage?"
The other two fell silent, staring. It was true. The pressure radiating off Malcolm had changed. He hadn't just survived the wait in that passage — he'd broken through.
"The Ogres must have other secrets in this cave," one of them murmured. "First Colin and Neil, now Malcolm advancing to War Sage… how many cards do they have hidden?"
Whatever the answer, it didn't matter right now—the higher Malcolm's cultivation, the better their odds. Hope surged in all three of them.
[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
“Wait, weren’t the ogres kicked out of the Tower of Sin? How did they get into the Dark Nether Cave?”
The ogres had never told the Twilight Group about the secret passage beneath the Tower. It was unthinkable that they would share such a vital secret with another race, even one they controlled. In fact, to advance their own agenda, the ogres had given Shane only one order: the moment he found Zane in the Dark Nether Cave, he was to report it to Malcolm with a Message Rune.
The command had baffled Shane at the time. He’d expected an assassination order, not a simple request to track Zane’s movements. What good would that do? Did Malcolm have some other way into the cave? Or had he been hiding here this whole time?
Those questions had plagued him for days. Now, he hoped Malcolm was their salvation. But as he watched, Malcolm and the other ogre stopped short, making no move to help. In that instant, Shane knew they had no intention of intervening.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
He quickly sent a message to Malcolm, a thread of pure Vitality. “They’ve rushed into the inner circle! But I left markers on them. We can track them!”
Of course, Shane wasn’t just going to hand over Zane’s location. If he did that, he’d lose the only bargaining chip he had to get Malcolm to save him.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BASS]
“That old fox,” Timothy laughed, his voice a low, sinister rumble. “He doesn’t want to tell us.”
Malcolm shook his head. “It’s just survival. He thinks we’re his only way out, so he won’t give up his leverage easily. If we want to know where Zane is, we have to save those three. Take out the Scorchplate the Flaming Place gave us.”
[SFX: MYSTICAL SHIMMER]
Timothy nodded, pulling out a small, golden scale that shimmered with contained power. The ogres had controlled the Tower of Sin for ages; they knew the Dark Nether Cave better than anyone. And the Scorchplate was a weapon that might just be strong enough to turn the tide against the swarming demonic beasts.
