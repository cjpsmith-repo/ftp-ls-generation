Ep 164 - Serpent's Coil

[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
The Armored Warrior clamped down on Zane's arm with crushing force, his grip like an iron vise. Pain shot through Zane's nerves, yet a strange exhilaration coursed through him at the same time.
This was precisely what he'd hoped for. With the distance between them eliminated, the tactical advantage shifted in his favor.
[SFX: DRAGON POWER SUMMONING] 
A smile crept across Zane's face as the familiar feeling of Dragon Power surged through him. With newfound strength, he hoisted the Armored Warrior into the air, ready to slam him into the ground. If the warrior was barely at the Adept Realm, a blow like that would finish him for good.
But the Armored Warrior defied expectations. Before Zane could complete his punishing slam, his opponent executed a masterful twist, his body contorting with impossible flexibility.
Zane was ready to crush him with his bare hands, but the warrior was clinging so tightly they both lost their balance and crashed to the ground.
[SFX: BODIES CRASHING TO GROUND]
During their descent, Zane thrust his hand toward the warrior's exposed neck, fingers curved like talons, but his grip slipped across the polished surface of his opponent's armor. 
Frustration flashed in his eyes as the warrior twisted free from his grasp. In one seamless, practiced movement, the armored figure rolled across the ground and reversed their positions, pressing Zane firmly against the cold floor. 
[SFX: COMBAT MOVEMENT]
The weight of his opponent crushed against his chest, making each breath a struggle as Zane found himself suddenly trapped beneath the Warrior's calculated maneuver.
Just then, Zane was struck with a thought that hit him like a bolt of lightning.  "Serpent's Coil!"
 The warrior's movements… they were just like Serpent's Coil, a body-conditioning art from the Eastern Territory.
Most fighters practiced a handful of different styles before hitting the Flesh-Honing Stage, and Serpent's Coil was a common one. But it was just a stepping stone. Once you reached the Flesh-Honing Stage, you focused on true mastery and left Serpent's Coil behind. It was considered a basic conditioning exercise, useless for a serious fighter's career.
Zane had learned Serpent's Coil as a kid, but after reaching the Flesh-Honing Stage, he'd moved on to Violet Fist, his family's ancestral technique.
He couldn't believe it. An ordinary armored warrior, using Serpent's Coil like this? Zane had always written it off as a basic fitness routine. Now, his own techniques felt useless against it.
*A single fragment of the Immortal's soul can have this much power?* Zane thought, gritting his teeth. Frustration burned through him as the Armored Warrior held him down. But he wasn't about to give up. That fifth Contemplation Picture was his.
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLE]
 The Armored Warrior locked his foot behind Zane's and then went perfectly still. There was an arrogance in his stillness, a silent challenge. That fragment of the Immortal's soul wasn't just powerful, it was smart.
"Damn it!" Zane cursed.
He bucked, a raw cry of anger tearing from his throat as he struggled to break free. But no matter how hard he fought, the Armored Warrior held him pinned, a knee digging into his ribs.
[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]
Feigning surrender, Zane relaxed his muscles completely, his breath held in anticipation as he  waited for the perfect moment to strike. Then, with explosive precision, he executed a lightning-fast twist and flip, springing back to his feet with newfound determination etched across his face.
Undeterred, the Armored Warrior lunged with calculated aggression, his movements betraying a stubborn resolve to dominate his opponent once more. The fragment of immortal soul within him radiated cold confidence with each step.
Zane's lips curled into a vicious smirk, his eyes glinting with dangerous intensity as blood pounded in his ears. "Go to hell," he snarled, his words a both a threat and a promise.  
[SFX: FLESH TEARING]
[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]
In a violent eruption, Zane's chest burst open, spraying blood. A broken knife flew out from the gushing wound, hurtling with deadly precision toward the Armored Warrior's exposed head.
[SFX: BODY FALLING]
The Warrior crumpled.
Saul laughed. "Cunning. I'll give you that."
Zane pressed a hand to his chest. "A remnant soul only carries battle memory. No intuition. It couldn't predict something that made no sense — so I did something that made no sense."
"Apart from the first master of this place," Saul said, quieter now, "no one has ever claimed that picture. You're the first."
[SFX: LIGHT DESCENDING]
A crack split the air and a beam of light pulled Zane upward. When it released him, he was back in the hall. The Tapestry of Sun, Moon, and Stars drifted from the wall and settled into his hands.
He couldn't look away. The sun, moon, and stars on the parchment were moving — slowly, endlessly. He tried to roll it up. His body wouldn't cooperate.
"First time with a Contemplation Picture," Saul said. "Sit down and visualize. It'll release you."
Zane sat.
The Warrior had devoted half a year to contemplating the Nine-Day Cycle Picture with its intricate, densely packed levels. By comparison, the Tapestry of Sun, Moon, and Starscontained merely three elements—sun, moon, and stars—but that just made it seem all the more difficult to master.
And yet... Zane had begun contemplating this ancient art mere minutes ago and already channeled power from the stars. His unprecedented affinity stunned even the immortal fragment residing within the Warrior. Zane wasn't just talented—he was extraordinary, a prodigy whose natural connection to the cosmos defied all conventional understanding of cultivation techniques.
[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL MEDITATION]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TRANSFORMATION]
 Three days passed. Zane remained on the ground, lost to the world.
After three thousand cycles of cultivation, his eyes finally closed. He meditated for a long moment before opening them again. Above, the stars still burned brilliantly.
“The Sun Moon Stars Picture is incredible,” he thought. “Absorbing the starlight… my spirit feels lighter, my very soul purified.” A deep sigh of awe escaped him.
It was more than just a gut feeling. As one of the Immortal's greatest works, the Contemplation Picture was capable of so much more.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY]
 Zane focused, channeling his energy into his core and forcing the Corrupt Vitality out through his palms. He stared as specks of Starlight began to glitter within the dark purple haze. It was strangely beautiful.
"Corrupt Vitality usually swallows any other Raw Vitality it touches… but it's coexisting with this Starlight. Wait a minute—"
[SFX: ENERGY DISSIPATION]
 Just as the thought formed, the Corrupt Vitality vanished from his hands.
"What's going on?"
[SFX: BURNING ENERGY]
 At the same time, a slow burn started deep in his core, where the rest of his Corrupt Vitality was circulating. It felt like someone had lit a fire inside him. The flow quickened, the heat intensifying with every passing second.
[MUSIC: INTENSE TRANSFORMATION]
 The Corrupt Vitality radiated out from his core, rushing through all his Channels.
The Corrupt Vitality itself had changed. It felt thicker now, smoother.
Zane was thrilled by the change. "Am I… breaking through?"
