Ep 14 - My Cousin
Word Count: 1170
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]
Andrew's eyes found Zane almost immediately. When their gazes locked, a small smile touched Zane's lips.
A month ago, Zane had beaten Grey. He'd expected Andrew to come after him right away, but Andrew had been biding his time. Now Zane knew why.
Andrew had been waiting for Family Training Day—waiting for the Fatal Duel, where he could pick Zane as his punching bag and kill him without anyone batting an eye.
But to Zane's surprise, Andrew didn't call his name.
Instead, he pointed to a powerfully built man standing right behind him. A frown creased Zane's brow. The man was a recent acquisition of House Lowell, a death-row prisoner. But for a condemned man, he looked strong and healthy, with no obvious internal injuries. "Strength Level, at least," Zane guessed.
[AMBIENT: ARENA ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
Once chosen, the man didn't hesitate. He launched himself into the arena, his eyes blazing with a raw, cornered-animal fierceness. In a Fatal Duel, there was no running. Hesitation was a death sentence. Fighting back with everything you had was your only shot at survival.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
Etiquette had no place in this lethal confrontation. The condemned man launched himself at Andrew, charging like an enraged bull across the arena. The strange stone floor trembled and fractured beneath his powerful strides, sending small fissures racing outward with each thunderous footfall.
Andrew merely chuckled, his casual confidence betraying his absolute certainty in the outcome. Purple energy coalesced in his cupped palm, swirling with deadly intent as he patiently waited. When the prisoner closed the distance, Andrew gracefully sidestepped and released the concentrated power.
[SFX: IMPACT THUD]
The Shadow Fist connected with a deceptively gentle thud against the charging man's torso. Though the impact appeared minimal, its effect proved devastating.
[SFX: STUMBLING]
The prisoner's momentum instantly vanished as if he'd struck an invisible wall. He stumbled awkwardly past Andrew, then pivoted to renew his assault, utterly unaware that his life had already been claimed. As he attempted one more step forward, determination still burning in his eyes, his fate was sealed.
[SFX: ORGAN RUPTURE SOUNDS]
Six distinct pops resonated from within his body, each marking the rupture of a vital organ. Blood erupted from half a dozen points across his chest and abdomen, staining his prison garb crimson. His eyes widened with horrified comprehension as realization dawned. His mouth gaped in a desperate attempt to speak, but produced only a wet, gurgling rattle.
[SFX: BODY COLLAPSE]
The vitality drained from his gaze like water through sand, and he crumpled to the arena floor, a marionette with severed strings.
Andrew had extinguished a formidable life with just a single strike.
[AMBIENT: CROWD CHEERING]
The younger family members in the crowd erupted into cheers. Even some of the adults clapped in approval. But amid the roar, no one seemed to notice the few Lowell kids who remained stone-faced, completely unfazed.
As the oldest son of the third branch, Andrew had a reputation.
Everyone knew he'd been fortifying his body with powerful supplements since childhood, which they assumed was the secret to his rapid progress. But this was too fast. There was only one explanation: Andrew had taken the second Aether Crystal. Perrin had already claimed the first one; this was the last.
Zane shook his head, a bitter smile on his face. His uncles had done the unthinkable. For centuries, no one in House Lowell had dared to touch the two Aether Crystals. But after seizing control, his uncles had simply claimed them for their own sons. Zane had been furious when he heard about Perrin, but now, learning Andrew had taken the last one, all he felt was a surprising, cold calm.
The Aether Crystal was said to be incredibly powerful. House Lowell had only possessed two, and now they were both gone.
Still, Zane didn't feel at a disadvantage.
The crystal had its benefits, but his own refinement process was already purifying his body. It wasn't as critical for him as it would be for others. That didn't stop the deep disappointment he felt in his uncle's greed.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS ANNOUNCEMENT]
Grey's voice thundered across the arena from the high platform. "The victor of the first Fatal Duel is Master Andrew!"
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
Flexing his wrist with casual arrogance, Andrew called back, "I've barely broken a sweat! I wish to challenge one more opponent." His eyes glinted with predatory anticipation, scanning the line of potential victims.
Grey's face lit up with malicious delight. This was the moment he'd been waiting for—Andrew would finally exact revenge on his behalf. A broad, triumphant smile spread across his features as he gestured expansively. "By all means, Master Andrew. The arena remains yours to select another challenger."
[SFX: FEARFUL MURMURS]
Andrew turned deliberately toward the assembled slaves, his extended finger tracing a languid path through the air like a conductor before an orchestra of fear. Each time his finger paused on someone, their complexion drained to ashen gray, eyes widening with terror.
They had all witnessed his lethal efficiency moments ago—selection meant certain death. His pointing finger carried the finality of an executioner's axe, each gesture a potential death sentence.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
After prolonging the torturous selection, Andrew's gaze finally settled on Zane, his eyes hardening with vindictive pleasure. He beckoned with a sharp, commanding motion, his laughter cutting through the tense silence. "You. Get up here now!" The words hung in the air, heavy with two years of accumulated hatred.
[SFX: CROWD GASPS]
Every head in the crowd turned. A collective gasp rippled through them as they saw who Andrew was pointing at. For a slave in a Fatal Duel, the outcome was simple: win, or die trying.
Even after the rebellion, Zane was technically still a Lowell.
In the two years since he'd been made a slave, none of the other kids had ever seriously harmed him—a restraint they never showed the other human punching bags.
But now, Andrew had picked him. The shock was palpable. Some of the spectators shook their heads in pity, certain that Zane was about to die.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON ARENA FLOOR]
Without a word, without a moment's hesitation, Zane stepped out from the line of slaves. His face was a complete blank as he walked into the arena.
He approached Andrew and gave a formal, almost dismissive nod. "Master Andrew."
"Cousin Zane, why so polite?" Andrew sneered, his eyes narrowing. "We both know only one of us is walking out of this arena."
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
The purple energy that had obliterated his previous opponent began to coalesce around Andrew's fists again, pulsing with deadly intent. The Aether Crystal's power radiated from him in waves that distorted the very air.
Zane rolled his shoulders, feeling the Godforge Technique humming through his bones—his secret weapon, his only chance at survival.
[AMBIENT: ARENA SILENCE]
[MUSIC: SHOWDOWN THEME]
"You're right," Zane replied, his voice carrying across the suddenly silent arena. "Only one of us is walking out."
