Ep 793 - Dragon Brothers, Royal Brothers

Word count: 1183

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]

The Red Dragon's voice rolled on, unhurried, as if recounting something that had happened yesterday — and Zane could barely keep up, each revelation landing before he'd finished processing the last.

 *Whoever controls the Ancestral Dragon has the power to control the entire dragon race, the Red Dragon said, his voice a low rumble. As the most powerful of the legendary beasts, it is an absolute. There’s no way we’re ever handing that egg over to humans.*

Zane listened, not daring to interrupt.

 The Red Dragon pressed on. The great war on the Divine Kingdom Continent… it was really a war between humans and dragons in the Lower World. The humans summoned my oldest brother through a blood sacrifice, but they never expected what he would do. He obliterated three of their divine kingdoms. Then, in the instant before the summoning space collapsed, he used a secret method to shatter the path of ascension for this entire world. Now, no one can leave. No one can ascend to the upper world.

[MUSIC: SHOCKING STRINGS]

 “What?” The words hit Zane with the force of a physical blow. His eyes went wide.

The fact that no one could ascend had always been this world’s greatest mystery, its most profound problem. Once a warrior reached the Soul Sea Realm, they were meant to walk the path of the immortals. But they were all trapped. People like Dorian, Amber, and all the masters from the union of seven divine kingdoms were stuck in this boundless world with no way out. Aside from the dragon soar arenas, there was no other known way to ascend.

Everyone knew something was fundamentally broken, but no one had ever found the cause. The question just hung there, unanswered. All those powerful Soul Sea Realm masters who should have ascended were left to wither, trapped until the Five Aging Processes consumed them and they died in despair.

 And Zane never would have guessed the reason. It was because the Red and Cobalt Dragons' brother had destroyed their only way out.

“But why?” Zane finally asked. “Why would your brother destroy the path in the first place?”

The Cobalt Dragon let out a heavy sigh. “It was because of the Ancestral Dragon, of course. The egg appeared in the chaos of this world, and my clan took it into our care, letting it hatch slowly under our protection. But the humans found out. That’s what started the war.”

[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATION]

 “So that’s what happened,” Zane said after a long moment of silence. He looked from one dragon to the other, putting the pieces together. “In other words, you want me to go to the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, get the egg, and hand it over to the dragon race?”

[SFX: ANGRY DRAGON SNORT]

 *The dragon race? Hmph!* The Red Dragon snorted, his voice thick with anger and disdain. 

That’s right, Zane, the Cobalt Dragon cut in, stopping his brother before he could say more. I want you to get the egg. But it must not be returned to the dragon race.

Zane frowned. "Even if the egg is really in there, Soul Sea Realm masters have been entering that sea for generations. If they couldn't get it, what chance do I have at my current strength?"

The Cobalt Dragon smiled faintly. The Heavenly Stellar Secret Land is different from the other three. They cannot obtain it. But you — there is a chance.

Zane was quiet for a moment. Then he nodded. "Then I'll do my best." He had no idea whether he could pull it off, but the Cobalt Dragon had asked. That was enough. He'd enter the Feather Emperor's Secret Land first. Everything else came after.

[AMBIENT: QUIET CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

For three days, Zane didn't leave his room. He cultivated in silence, and Francis stood guard at the door without being told twice.

On the third day, he heard sharp footsteps outside, followed by a shrill voice. "The Crown Prince has arrived. All unrelated persons, clear the way!"

Zane rose and went outside.

The sight that greeted him was striking. Dozens of Life-and-Death Realm guards lined the path in matching golden robes, identical silver sabers at their hips. Zane glanced at the weapons. Low-grade Relics, every single one of them — and all exactly the same. The kingdom could mass-produce Relics. He filed that away.

[MUSIC: COURTLY ELEGANCE]

At the head of the procession sat a young man in a wheelchair. He looked sickly — pale, slight, the kind of person you'd overlook in a crowd. But his eyes were another matter entirely. They flickered with a sharp, unsettling light.

Before Zane could speak, a guard at the Crown Prince's side stepped forward. "Why haven't you knelt to greet the Crown Prince?"

Zane looked at him coldly and said nothing.

"That's not necessary," the Crown Prince said, his voice mild but firm. He smiled at Zane. "Forgive him. A man of your talent shouldn't kneel to anyone. Mr. Jones, I apologize."

Zane kept his expression neutral. He'd caught it immediately — the guard's demand, the Crown Prince's rebuke, the deliberate use of Zane's alias on a first meeting. It was a performance, carefully staged to make him feel seen and valued. 

The Crown Prince was smoother than Randy, and that made him more dangerous.

“Your Highness,” Zane said, trying to keep his voice casual. “What brings you here?”

 The Crown Prince offered a polished smile. “I heard you passed the Crystal Martial House examination as an independent warrior. I had to meet the man in person. Your reputation is impressive, and your talent clearly lives up to it.”

“You’re too kind,” Zane replied, keeping his expression neutral. “I’m really just a simple guy.”

[AMBIENT: PALACE COURTYARD]

[MUSIC: REGAL STRINGS]

 “Come on, let’s skip the formalities,” the Crown Prince said, his smile never wavering. “There’s a banquet at my residence today. I’d be honored if you’d attend.” As he spoke, he shot a pointed look at his attendant.

The man stepped forward, mirroring the prince’s smile, and held out a shimmering golden invitation.

Zane stared at the card, a knot tightening in his stomach. Did he really want to get involved in this? He knew exactly what this was. The Crown Prince was trying to recruit him, just like Randy had, though with a great deal more grace. But that was just how this game was played. Politeness didn’t mean a thing.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]

 Just as he was trying to figure out how to refuse, another voice cut through the air. “Zane! You made it to the city and didn’t even come say hello?”

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]

 It was Randy.

Surprisingly, he was alone, with no attendants in sight. His green robes swayed as he strolled toward them, his eyes flicking to the Crown Prince before landing on Zane with a grin. “How have you been?”

Zane just gave a slight nod, his head spinning. 

First the Crown Prince, now his brother. What in the world did these two want from him?
