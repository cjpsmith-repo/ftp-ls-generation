  Ep 44 - The Third Layer

[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
[SFX: FIGHTING SOUNDS]
As the leader of this pack of Legacy kids, the guy in blue had to be pretty strong. He just smirked when he saw Zane darting toward him, his clenched fist shifting into the stance for Talon Fighting. He lunged, shouting, "Ha! Think you can get away?"
[SFX: IMPACT THUD]
The attack was incredibly powerful; his hands curved into dragon claws as they shot down toward Zane's head. But Zane stayed cool, twisting sideways to slip right past the grab. He then rammed his shoulder into the guy's chest, knocking him off balance.
[SFX: QUICK MOVEMENT]
As the kid stumbled backward, Zane moved in a flash. In the blink of an eye, he was right behind him, wrapping an arm around his neck just before he could fall.
[SFX: CHOKING SOUND]
Zane tightened his grip and yelled, "Anyone takes another step, and I'll drop him. He fails the test!"
[AMBIENT: HUSHED TENSION]
When the other students from the Great Houses saw Zane holding the guy in blue by the throat, they all froze. The guy in blue, Connor, was the sharp-witted youngest son of an influential member of House Sterling. Setting up this roadblock had been his idea; he was clearly the ringleader.
But right now, Connor was in Zane's grip and could be knocked out of the test in an instant.
"Hey, look," Connor said, his voice surprisingly calm for someone at Zane's mercy. "If you let me go, I can make sure you get out of here safely."
"Yeah, right," Zane shot back. "You really think I'd fall for that?"
Zane probably could have just bulldozed his way through their little blockade. It would've been tougher, but doable. But every single one of these Legacy kids was holding a mysterious weapon, and some of them looked high-end.
Even though Zane's body was as tough as a mysterious weapon itself, getting stabbed still wasn't on his to-do list.
"Tell your buddies to back off!" Zane ordered.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
"Do you have any idea who I am?" Connor said, his voice dropping to an icy tone. "I'm a Sterling."
There was a clear pecking order among the Great Houses. More than thirty Houses held the Legacy title throughout the Capital, but only seven of them were at the very top.

Connor figured that was enough. Nobody touched a Sterling. He'd just stand here, let the name do the work, and this commoner would fold.
He moved to break free. It was the wrong call. 
The moment Connor shifted, Zane's grip snapped tight. One twist —
[SFX: SHARP CRACK]
— and Connor went limp. The light took him almost immediately, dissolving him into motes that drifted upward.
Before the surrounding disciples could process what had just happened, Zane grabbed Connor's fading form and hurled it straight into the crowd.
[SFX: BODIES STUMBLING]
The nobles scrambled back in shock. In the half-second of chaos, Zane was already gone — sprinting hard for the third layer.
Meanwhile, outside Bloody Mountain, the mentors had been watching.
Wait a second, a thought hit Wyatt. Before the kid left, Isla Novak had checked his Student ID. Wyatt thought he'd figured it out—she must have done something to the guy's Student ID.
The moment Wyatt opened his mouth, an icy chill shot up his spine, freezing him in place.
He snapped his head around and saw Isla staring coldly at him. If looks could kill, her glare would have dropped him dead on the spot for even thinking about saying a word.
Several other mentors were already shouting at him. "Wyatt! Why can't we pull up this student's feed? What's wrong with the system?" "Fix it — we need to see this kid!" The commotion was getting louder.
Wyatt swallowed hard, forcing the words back down his throat.
Isla's expression was frosty. She wasn't worried about Wyatt ratting her out for what she did to Zane's Student ID. She just couldn't believe Zane had actually gone back to save someone. He'd shown incredible power breaking through that barrier, and now every examiner had seen it.
Isla knew they would all be fighting her for Zane now, regardless of her social status. Watching Zane sprint across the Engraved Slab, she shook her head, annoyed. She had wanted to recruit the boy quietly, but his amazing performance was making things complicated.
She clenched her teeth and whispered, "Hmph, you little brat. I'll make sure you have a hell of a time when you get to Misty Peak."
[SFX: BURST OF SPEED]
In a single burst of speed, Zane covered a few hundred feet before he saw a colorful strip of light hovering in the air ahead.
He figured it must be the entrance to the third layer.
[SFX: MAGICAL PORTAL SOUND]
He plunged headfirst into the light and disappeared.
[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Passing through the light was like stepping into another world. The scene before him was a stark contrast to the second layer. The lush trees were gone, replaced by bare hillsides and a forest of jagged rocks. Caves, at least ten feet deep, dotted the slopes, with black, toxic smoke billowing from their mouths. The whole place looked desolate and dead.
The other students who had passed through earlier were nowhere to be seen. He tried to find a sign of Ethan, but he seemed to have lost his trail completely.
"Where on earth did Ethan get to? Maybe he found a safe place to hide after he got here. I hope you're safe, man," Zane thought, sending a silent prayer for his friend's safety as he started to navigate the third layer.
[AMBIENT: ROCKY TERRAIN]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
After making his way up a hillside, Zane stepped into the jagged, rocky forest. The crystal fragments he had were nowhere near enough to pass the Gauntlet, so he had to keep moving.
The unreal beasts on the third layer were all higher-level and much more of a challenge. Generally, the level three beasts were about as strong as someone at the Marrow Refining Level.
But since the unreal beasts were transformed from evil creatures, they were even stronger.
[AMBIENT: FOREST AMBIENCE]
For that reason alone, Zane knew he needed to stay on high alert. He scanned his surroundings warily as he walked through the forest. He'd taken only two steps when a sound sliced through the silence.
[SFX: SNAKE HISS]
A snake's hiss. The hairs on his arms stood on end, a primal instinct telling him something was stalking him.
Zane whipped his head toward the sound, his eyes scanning every inch of the rocky landscape, but found nothing but jagged rocks. Everything was perfectly still.
Frowning, Zane wondered, "Was I just hearing things? Or am I just getting jumpy?"
[SFX: CREATURE ATTACK]
Once a boa constrictor has its prey in its coils, escape is impossible. A meal usually follows. But the prey it had chosen this time unleashed a sudden, overwhelming strength the boa couldn't handle. With no time to react, it was instantly torn into several pieces.
[SFX: SNAKE BEING TORN APART]
The five pieces writhed on the ground before dissolving into motes of light and vanishing completely. Zane had slaughtered the boa constrictor.
[SFX: SHARP PING]
As the snake faded, a sharp ping echoed off the rocks.
[SFX: FRAGMENT CLINKING]
Following the sound, Zane saw a purple fragment break loose from a jagged rock and clink to the ground. After the fight he'd just survived, that sound was music to his ears.
Now, he only needed nine more purple fragments to pass the Gauntlet. He realized the third layer was a much better hunting ground.
Zane picked up the purple fragment, wiped it clean on his sleeve, and tucked it safely into his pocket with the others. He glanced to his right and jogged ahead, eager to find more.
He estimated the fourth layer's denizens would match a High-tier candidate in strength—perhaps even surpass them, given their bestial nature. The rational part of his mind firmly rejected any notion of ascending further.
Yet temptation whispered through his thoughts. Just three kills on that fourth level would secure his passage through the Gauntlet.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Zane's gaze drifted upward to where the path continued its winding ascent into mist-shrouded heights. Something pulled at him—a curiosity, a challenge, or perhaps something deeper. His hand unconsciously tightened around his weapon as he took a single step toward the ascending trail.
