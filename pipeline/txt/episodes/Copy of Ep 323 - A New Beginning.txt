Ep 323 - A New Beginning
Word count: 1205

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL DISCOVERY]
Zane stood transfixed, his mind struggling to process the reality before him.
A Sacred Weapon. The thought sent shivers down his spine.
Then it hit him.
A second Sacred Weapon.
He'd been carrying one this whole time — he just hadn't let himself believe it. The resonance he'd felt from Streamer since the day he'd forged it, the way it had performed far beyond what any Mythic weapon should be capable of — he'd filed it all away, told himself he was wrong, that it wasn't possible. But standing here, about to receive a Sacred Weapon as a prize, the pieces finally locked into place.
Streamer was Sacred. It always had been.
Two Sacred Weapons. His mind struggled to process the scale of what that meant.
[MUSIC: THOUGHTFUL CONTEMPLATION]
Sir Kenneth noticed the flicker of hope on Zane's face and just shook his head with a small smile. He clapped his hands, signaling for the stewards to bring out the weapons.
The Skyreach Sect was ancient and wealthy, with a massive arsenal of Sacred weapons. Kenneth didn't just trot out the most powerful one every time; the grade of the weapon depended on the disciple’s potential.
He remembered the Cloudrender he’d given to that fool Evan last time. A magnificent Sacred Weapon, utterly squandered in unappreciative hands.
Evan had shown no reverence for the extraordinary blade—and had even lost it to Elara through a careless wager. Sir Kenneth’s heart still ached for the sword itself, as if the weapon possessed a spirit that could feel the sting of such disrespect.
This time, however, struck him as fundamentally different. He had genuine confidence that Zane would treasure any Sacred Weapon placed in his care. The young man’s eyes revealed a depth of understanding that Evan had lacked—a recognition of true value. In fact, Sir Kenneth nurtured high hopes for this promising young man.
His wish was that Zane wouldn’t merely survive the Cloud Road trial, but would thoroughly blow his fellow disciples out of the water with unprecedented brilliance. Maybe then, those insufferable representatives from Cloud Hall would give up their constant scrutiny and criticism of his judgment. Their persistent doubts about his ability to identify genuine talent had grown tiresome over the years, and he longed for vindication through Zane’s success.
[SFX: CEREMONIAL FOOTSTEPS]
A moment later, twelve stewards emerged from the back of the hall, each carrying a long, narrow jade box.
They formed a line, presenting the open boxes to Zane one by one.
[MUSIC: MAGICAL DISCOVERY]
Inside lay an array of swords. Three of them were middle-grade Sacred weapons, their blades shimmering with an otherworldly light; the remaining nine were low-grade, yet each radiated power that would make any cultivator’s heart race.
Zane’s breath caught in his throat as he surveyed the treasures before him. Most of the Seven Great Houses possessed only a handful of low-grade Sacred weapons, perhaps a single middle-grade weapon if they were lucky. Two of the Great Houses couldn’t claim ownership of even one middle-grade Sacred weapon.
While the collections of the Great Houses were a mix of spears, knives, and swords, Sir Kenneth had arranged nothing but exquisite swords for Zane’s selection. The significance wasn’t lost on him: three middle-grade weapons presented as casually as common steel would be anywhere else.
The sheer abundance of Sacred swords was stunning. A thought crept into his mind as he gazed at the gleaming blades: if the Skyreach Sect had such an impressive collection of swords alone, how many Sacred knives did they have stashed away? How many spears?
Their wealth was beyond comprehension, so gigantic that even the legendary Palace, with all its storied riches, probably couldn’t claim half of what the Sect had in its possession. Zane, stood there, taking in the true scale of power in front of him.
Patrick and Finn stood breathless, their eyes wide with envy as they stared at the swords. Finn, especially, looked like he was ready to devour them with his eyes. But he knew they were beyond his reach. He hadn’t placed first, after all.
Zane’s eyes landed on a blade. It was slimmer than most swords, with an elegant profile that belied its lethal edge. The moment Zane's fingers closed around the hilt, a faint pulse of energy thrummed against his palm 
"This one," Zane lifted the sword.
He drew it from the box in a single, fluid motion. The crimson sheen rippled along the blade like a living thing, and for just a moment, a flash of red light flickered at its edge.
"Blood Drinker," he murmured, the name forming in his mind as naturally as breathing. A sword with a thirst. It was perfect. 
Sir Kenneth was watching him. "I knew you'd choose that one." His tone shifted, quieter now. "The path of Malice is formidable — but it carries a price. Push it too far without control and the killing intent turns inward. Best case, you lose your mind. Worst case, you don't survive it." He paused. "Do you intend to continue down this path?"
[SFX: MOMENT OF RESOLVE]
Zane met his gaze without hesitation. "Yes."
Sir Kenneth studied him for a long moment, then nodded. A person with genuine resolve didn't abandon their path because someone questioned it. If anything, Zane's answer only confirmed his judgment.
"Then it's yours."
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
Zane stored Blood Drinker away in his Storage Ring before moving to stand beside Finn and Patrick. It was clear that awarding him a Sacred Weapon wasn’t the only reason Kenneth had called them all here.
[SFX: SMALL JADE BOX BEING TOSSED]
After the last steward had left, Kenneth pulled a small, square jade box from his waist and tossed it to Zane.
"This is your second reward," he said. "The Draught of Clarity."
The potion was essential for disciples after they reached the Soul Realm, so Zane couldn’t use it yet.
When a disciple hit that realm, they inevitably faced a training wall that was incredibly difficult to break through. The Draught of Clarity was the key to that breakthrough.
Needless to say, the potion was priceless. The Skyreach Sect only produced a few draughts each year, and even the Guardians and elders were desperate to get their hands on one.
After Zane put the potion away, Sir Kenneth spoke again, his tone shifting.
"The real reason I called you three here is to talk about the Cloud Road."
[SFX: DRAMATIC PAUSE]
At the mention of the name, Zane, Finn, and Patrick all sobered instantly.
Many warriors in the Eastern Territories trained their entire lives only to top out at the Adept Realm.
Sure, Adepts lived far longer and were much stronger than ordinary people, and they could live comfortably just about anywhere in the Eastern Territories.
But for the top disciples of the Skyreach Sect, that was never going to be enough. They dreamed of making a name for themselves on a much bigger stage.
[MUSIC: ASPIRATIONAL THEME]
Passing the Cloud Road trial and earning a place in Cloud Hall... that was their first real step into that bigger world.
This was their future on the line, and it was an incredibly big deal.
