Ep 188 - New Opportunities

[AMBIENT: BATTLE AFTERMATH]
[SFX: CROWD WHISPERS]
The battle's aftermath hung in the air like a lingering storm cloud. Students and instructors alike stood in stunned silence, their eyes fixed on the shattered remnants of Bob's ice technique scattered across the stone floor. 
The defeat of House Pauling's prodigy had created ripples that would soon become waves throughout the Sect. 
As the onlookers gradually dispersed, whispering among themselves about what they'd witnessed, Leo stared at Zane. He'd been serious about the offer that could reshape the young cultivator's future. “Well, what do you say? Care to join me?”
Sunspire Peak was the fourth-ranked of the Sect's Thirty-Three Peaks, making it prime real estate for the powerful families. In fact, most members of House Brandt chose to train on that very mountain. If Zane moved there, no one would dare mess with him. Not to mention all the perks that came with it.
But Zane just shook his head. “Thanks for the offer, but I’m fine at Misty Peak. Besides, arranging a transfer sounds like a huge hassle for you. I really don't think it's necessary.”
For a regular disciple, getting transferred to Sunspire Peak was next to impossible. But for Leo, it was a piece of cake. All it would take was calling in a favor with his family. But Leo could tell Zane wasn't biting. And since Zane had turned him down so politely, Leo let it go.
He released a weary sigh, his shoulders slumping slightly. "But since you haven't been promoted to inner disciple status yet." He ran a hand through his hair. "I'm sorry to tell you that participation in the All Peaks Competition remains beyond your reach."
"The All Peaks Competition?" Zane's voice caught in his throat as his heart skipped a beat, his eyes widening with unmistakable interest.
Leo's eyebrow arched as curiosity flickered across his features. "You seem surprised. Didn't you know it was coming up soon?"
Zane shook his head, a faint flush of embarrassment coloring his cheeks. While he'd certainly heard whispers about the All Peaks Competition—the prestigious event the Skyreach Sect hosted every three years—he'd never delved into its specifics. This monumental contest determined the rankings for all Thirty-Three Peaks, reshaping the hierarchy that governed their world.
Every disciple throughout the Sect anticipated this event with a mixture of dread and excitement, knowing the peak rankings directly influenced their personal evaluations. The stakes were immense—better rankings translated to coveted privileges and resources that could accelerate cultivation breakthroughs for both inner and outer disciples alike. Zane felt a sudden hollowness in his stomach at the thought of missing such an opportunity.
[MUSIC: ANTICIPATION]
 Most importantly, the competition only happened once every three years. And almost every time, a few disciples would make a name for themselves, becoming the Sect’s newest rising stars.
“The All Peaks Competition is scheduled for early next year. With your kind of strength, I'm sure you'll do great. But you can't compete until you're an inner disciple of Misty Peak.” After a moment's thought, Leo added, “I don't get why Misty Peak hasn't promoted you yet. Normally, outer disciples have to pass the annual Inner Disciple Trial to move up. But your strength and skill are way beyond the average outer disciple. They should just make an exception and promote you. If someone at Misty Peak is deliberately giving you a hard time, you need to tell me.”
Leo knew he might be overthinking it, but his offer to help was genuine. It was definitely weird that someone with Zane's power hadn't been made an inner disciple yet.
But what Leo didn't know was that no one at Misty Peak was trying to sabotage him. Zane nodded, grateful for Leo's kindness. He just told Leo he'd do everything he could to become an inner disciple as soon as possible and qualify for the competition.
Leo's expression grew serious. "One more thing. Bob isn't going to let this go. Getting knocked out in public by a first-level Adept — that's not something someone like him forgets. You've made an enemy today."
Zane just shrugged. "He was already one."
Leo was quiet for a moment. Then: "If he comes after you, he'll have to come through House Brandt first."
It wasn't a casual offer. Zane knew what it meant — the Brandt name between him and House Pauling. He felt the weight of it.
"I appreciate that," he said. And he meant it.
Zane wasn't about to let an opportunity like this slip by.
The All Peaks Competition wasn't just a chance to test his own strength—it was a chance to prove himself.
[MUSIC: TRANSITIONAL THEME]
[AMBIENT: ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICE]
 Meanwhile, Steward Carter was mulling over Zane’s recent performance. If Zane kept diving deeper into the lake of Heavy Water, to one hundred fifty or even one hundred eighty meters, he would shatter the records of every other disciple at the Skyreach Sect.
The stewards of the Sect, especially those in charge of the training grounds, had an unofficial duty: to record and report any exceptional activity in their areas.
Zane's performance was more than good enough to be reported to the higher-ups. If they saw it, the top leaders might notice him, maybe even grant him more training resources as a reward.
With that in mind, Steward Carter recorded Zane's performance and sent the file up the chain of command. If the report caught their eye and they decided to invest in Zane's training, Carter would get a bonus for discovering new talent.
Carter figured an incredible talent like Zane would definitely get the leaders' attention. Sure, the Skyreach Sect was packed with gifted disciples, and there was no shortage of prodigies. But the Sect was massive, and it was always on the lookout for more.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
 But to Carter's surprise, his report was met with total silence. It was as if no one at the top was paying Zane any mind at all.
As far as Carter knew, that was impossible. He suspected his report had gotten lost in the shuffle, that the leaders had never even seen it.
So, he submitted another one.
The result was the same: absolute silence from the top.
It wasn't easy to become a steward at the Skyreach Sect. Carter was a sharp man, and he quickly put two and two together.
[SFX: REALIZATION MOMENT]
 “I sent that report up twice and got nothing,” Carter thought to himself. “That can only mean one thing: the leadership is ignoring Zane on purpose.”
It wasn't Carter's place to dig into it. He was just a low-level steward and couldn't afford to get on the wrong side of anyone at the Skyreach Sect. So, he dropped the whole idea of getting credit for discovering new talent.
But who would be trying to sabotage a once in a lifetime talent like Zane?
