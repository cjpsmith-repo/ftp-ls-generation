Ep 1204 - Not Interested

Word count: 1106

[MUSIC: DECISIVE FLOURISH]
Zane stood his ground in the open wilderness beyond the city, sizing up the three Ogres who’d just dropped their cover to confront him, their hulking frames blotting out the light behind them.
He thought about it for a second, then figured, *Nah.*
He launched himself into the air and was gone.
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
[AMBIENT: CROWD SURGE]
The second Zane left, the crowd of warriors who’d been waiting inside the city flooded out of the gates. But the moment they crossed the threshold, they scattered, each person scrambling to put some distance between themselves and the next. That was just the rule of the Illusion Battlefield: the city was safe, but the moment you stepped outside, you were on your own. It didn’t matter how skilled you were—out here, anyone could become prey.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BUILD]
One of them, an ogre who looked completely unremarkable, rushed out with the rest. But once he was clear of the gates, a strange, predatory smile twisted his lips.
[SFX: FLAME IGNITING]
“Purple Flame Explosion!” he roared.
In an instant, violet flames erupted from his body, the raw power of Fire Law sending out searing ripples in every direction. More than a hundred warriors had already made it outside. The other ogres among them sensed the danger first, throwing up shields of pure energy to protect themselves, but it was useless.
[SFX: SHIELDS SHATTERING]
The purple flames burned through their defenses like they were tissue paper, drawing screams of agony.
[MUSIC: CHAOTIC CARNAGE]
“Aaaargh!”
“Damn it, these things burn like hell!” someone shouted, but it was already too late.
In the blink of an eye, the fire had engulfed them all. Some tried to fly, to escape into the air, but the faster they moved, the hotter the flames burned. They’d only get a few yards before turning to ash that scattered on the wind. Others desperately summoned shields of ice, but the crystalline walls melted the second the flames touched them, leaving the casters to be burned to a crisp.
[SFX: ASHES SCATTERING]
[AMBIENT: DISTANT SCREAMS FADING]
As each warrior died, a thin beam of red light shot from their body and flew back toward the city.
[MUSIC: COLD TRIUMPH]
The ogre, meanwhile, simply stood there, absorbing half the Illusion Points from every person he’d killed. With a cold smile, he watched the warriors still cowering inside the city, a ball of purple flame pulsing in his open palm. His attack had covered a massive area, but any flames that licked toward the city walls had simply vanished. The city was a true safe zone—no attacks could get in.
[AMBIENT: ANGRY MURMURING]
“I’ll get more from hunting stronger beasts, anyway,” he murmured to himself.
One of the demons in the crowd finally found his voice. “We’re all demons,” he snarled. “With your methods, I never thought—”
“I don’t want to hear it.” The Ogre cut him off flat. “There’s no such thing as race on this Battlefield. There are only winners and losers. Every single one of you is a stepping stone—for me to become the supreme ruler of this world.”
Anger flashed across the surrounding faces, but understanding dawned on even more of them. Whatever they thought they’d prepared for when the Battlefield opened, it hadn’t been enough.
The Ogre strolled toward the city gate, the purple flame still pulsing in his palm. The instant his hand crossed the boundary, the flame guttered out, swallowed by the city’s protection.
“My name,” he said, not bothering to turn around, “is Lance.”
[SFX: WHOOSH OF DEPARTURE]
He vanished into the distance without another word.
“Lance,” someone whispered. “He’s a direct descendant of the Ogre Flame World Lord.” 
“Just our luck, getting dropped in the same city as him.”
“Luck has nothing to do with it,” another voice cut in. “Every city’s got a monster or two when you’re pulling from a hundred thousand worlds.” A pause. “Makes you wonder if he’s stronger than those two humans from earlier. Wouldn’t mind seeing that fight.”
[AMBIENT: DISTANT CITY NOISE]
Lance was far from the only one. All across the Illusion Battlefield, the same standoff kept repeating itself at city gates—someone too strong, too eager, or too cautious bringing the flow of warriors to a halt. Meanwhile, above every Illusory Space in the hundred thousand worlds, the pyramids kept shifting, names climbing and falling as every race and power watched closely, hunting for the ones who’d rise to the top.
[MUSIC: QUIET TRANSITION]
[AMBIENT: WILDERNESS WIND]
Meanwhile, Zane touched down on a hillside and continued on foot. 
[SFX: WIND BLADE FIRING]
A wind blade shot from his fingertip and punched clean through the chest of a gray-horned deer creeping low in the grass ahead—docile-looking, but a Level-Ten beast all the same.
[SFX: BEAST DYING]
It let out one final, unwilling cry before its body began to fade. A handful of pale yellow motes drifted toward Zane in its place.
“One Illusion Point? For that?” He shook his head.He’d cut down any beasts he happened to cross, but the payout was pathetic. A creature on par with that gray-horned deer would only net him one or two Illusion Points—barely worth the effort. The real game in the Illusion Battlefield wasn’t about grinding low-level mobs. The smart play was to let the other, stronger warriors do the hard work of collecting points, and then just hunt them down and take it all.
Honestly, Zane could have just found a quiet spot to meditate, or even take a nap. But after being locked away in The Elysian Keep for so many years, he was itching to see what this vast battlefield had to offer.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CALM]
Just as he was thinking, he slowed, a small smile touching his lips. He stopped dead in mid-air and glanced down over his shoulder. “So, how long are you guys gonna keep tailing me?”
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
The wilderness was silent.
“Look,” Zane said casually, turning back to face forward. “Why don’t you go collect some more Illusion Points, and then come find me? You’re not really worth my time right now.”
He’d clocked three people on his tail, which meant they hadn’t had much time to hunt. Killing all three would probably only net him a hundred and fifty points total. He really couldn’t be bothered. 
[SFX: ROUGH LAUGHTER]
[SFX: FIGURES MATERIALIZING]
A rough laugh boomed through the quiet air. Three hulking figures shimmered into existence—a trio of ogres, their muscles bulging as they stared down at Zane.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
“Ha! Maybe not,” a voice growled, “but we’re definitely interested in you!”
