Ep 691 - Cora's Dilemma

Word count: 1121

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL CITY ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: MAJESTIC ORCHESTRAL]

House Kensington was one of the great noble houses of Ethereal City, but it hadn’t always been that way. For most of the city's thousands of years of history, they were considered a minor family, just another second-tier house lost in a sea of names. But ever since one of their own became the leader of the Ethereal Spirit Sect, their status had skyrocketed, finally earning them a place among the city’s most powerful families.

[AMBIENT: EVENING CRICKETS]

[SFX: LANTERNS FLICKERING]

The Kensington estate was practically a town in itself, a sprawling property on the east side of the city that housed nearly ten thousand family members. At dusk, the lanterns at the main gate flickered to life, bathing the entire area in a warm glow. Powered by Vitality from the low-grade Aether Crystals inside them, they’d been burning for years. An Aether Crystal, even a low-grade one, wasn’t something an ordinary family could ever dream of affording, and the light from the Kensington lanterns tracing the estate walls was a spectacular sight.

[AMBIENT: BUSY CROWD CHATTER]

[SFX: VENDOR CALLS]

Every night, crowds gathered in the square out front to admire the view. Some leaned against the bridge railings, chatting idly, while others just stared, mesmerized by the sheer wealth on display. It was the brightest spot in all of Ethereal City, and because of the nightly crowds, vendors had set up stalls selling everything from needlework to small trinkets.

"Are those lanterns really burning Aether Crystals?" a man nearby murmured, craning his neck to count them. "That must be a fortune."

His companion laughed. "They've been burning for decades. Who knows what it's cost them." He shook his head. "Still — if I'd taken up the martial path when I was young instead of books, maybe I'd have lanterns like that."

"The martial world is soaked in blood," the other man said. "That fortune didn't come easy."

A third man cut in with a grin. "Speaking of the Kensingtons — I hear the young lady is looking for a husband. A thousand suitors and not one of them good enough for her. Maybe you should try your luck."

“I’m afraid someone like her is completely out of my league,” his friend said, slowly shaking his head.

The third man scoffed. “At least you know where you stand. A thousand young men have already gone to the Kensington estate to try their luck, some of them heaven-level talents in their twenties and thirties with incredible cultivation levels. You know what happened? Every single one of them was rejected. The young lady is apparently very picky, and not one of them has managed to catch her eye.”

When a noble house as influential as Kensington started looking for a husband for their daughter—a girl with a higher status than any princess in the mortal world—it became the talk of the town.

[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL GARDEN]

[MUSIC: SERENE MEDITATION]

In a quiet courtyard on the estate, a sixteen-year-old girl sat perfectly still between two alabaster screens, deep in her cultivation practice. Dressed in white, with light green ribbons draped over her shoulders, she was stunning.

[SFX: KNOCKING ON DOOR]

Just then, a series of knocks echoed from the courtyard door.

Hearing it, the girl’s brow furrowed. She rolled her eyes, determined to ignore it, but the knocking was relentless, as if the person on the other side had no intention of stopping until she answered. Sighing in defeat, she finally called out, “Come in!”

[SFX: GATE OPENING]

The gate swung open, and three people walked in. The one in the lead, a young man with bright, lively eyes and a faint smile, moved quickly toward her, slipping past the screens. “Cora,” he said sweetly, his eyes twinkling as he reached her.

Cora opened her eyes, her irritation plain to see. She glared at him. “Brent, can you please walk like a normal person?”

This was Brent, a saint-level genius from the Ethereal Spirit Sect who had been adopted into House Kensington. Having grown up on the estate, he was close with everyone in the family, especially the girl in front of him.

He just smiled and placed a stack of scrolls in front of her. “I got so many scrolls today!” he said, giggling like a little kid.

The sight of the scrolls only seemed to make Cora angrier. The Vitality in her palm pulsed, and a blazing flame shot from her hand.

[SFX: FIRE WHOOSH]

For a split second, it looked like the entire stack was about to go up in smoke.

But Brent reacted instantly. He flicked a finger, and the air in front of the scrolls seemed to warp.

[SFX: MAGICAL DEFLECTION]

The fire vanished harmlessly, though the sudden attack left him rattled. "These are from the family — at least let me finish," he said.

Cora closed her eyes. "Then get on with it."

Brent unrolled the first scroll. A painted portrait, a young man with a poem beside his likeness. He read out the man's name, his cultivation, his family standing. "Next," Cora said, before he'd finished. He reached for another. Same result. Portrait after portrait, poem after poem, each one dismissed in a single word. This had been happening three times a day for the past month.

When the last scroll was rolled back up, Brent smiled. "Anyone catch your eye today?"

"No."

"Then we'll try again tomorrow." He tilted his head. "I wonder when sister will finally get married."

Cora opened her eyes at that. A reluctant smile crossed her face. "You little pest," she said. "Can't you ever be serious?"

[AMBIENT: TENSE CITY MURMURS]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The entire city was on edge. Word had gotten out that a spy from the Corrupted Divine Kingdom had slipped past the walls, and the whispers said they were after Cora.  Maddox had waited decades for his formation — Marilyn's death had forced him to start over, and now Cora had appeared. Rumors were flying that the Corrupted Divine Kingdom was planning to attack Ethereal City just to get to her.

The other major houses had immediately descended on the Kensington estate, politely but firmly suggesting they find Cora a husband as soon as possible. It was only because of their respect for Elder Xander that they hadn’t just taken her away by force for the city’s protection.

[MUSIC: DARK DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

To put it bluntly, Cora had two options: marry someone immediately or be killed. Under no circumstances could she be allowed to fall into the hands of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom.

Faced with that grim reality, the entire House Kensington was beginning to feel the crushing weight of the pressure.
