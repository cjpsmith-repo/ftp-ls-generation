Ep 379 - Taking a Break

Word count: 1177

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CAVE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

High on the fourth-level platform, two chests glowed with a dark golden light. Every warrior stared at them, their eyes glittering with the same raw desire.

So far in the Cloud Road Trial, no one had managed to get their hands on a gold treasure box. Not even the Head of Cloud Hall had ever earned one.

The other warriors were practically drooling over the gold chests, and Zane couldn't deny he was tempted, too.

He’d already snagged rewards from three chests, and each one had been rarer and more powerful than the last. He, more than anyone, knew what these chests were worth. The first layer gave him a boost to his spiritual strength. The second… the Lifeblood of the Nine-Headed Dragon. And the third? A secret manuscript from the Blood Fiend Emperor himself, detailing a Tier Five Cultivation Method! 

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

What kind of reward could be waiting on the fourth layer? And what about the fifth? The rewards had to be legendary.

But Zane wasn't a fool. He'd watched too many talented cultivators throw their lives away chasing power they weren't ready for. Their names were forgotten now, reduced to cautionary tales whispered in training halls.

He had already risked his neck for the silver treasure box, and that gamble had nearly cost him everything. The memory of that Darksteel automaton's blade whistling past his throat still made his pulse quicken. At his current level, reaching for a gold chest would be suicide—plain and simple.
Treasures, weapons, Cultivation Methods—they were all important. But no prize was worth dying for. A dead cultivator, no matter how talented, would never reach the heights he was meant to climb.
Zane exhaled slowly, feeling the weight of temptation lift from his shoulders. The decision settled in his chest with quiet certainty.

Besides, he was still at a pretty low level. He figured there would be other chances to score rewards like these once he got stronger. Kenneth had said disciples below the Third Level of the Soul Realm could enter the Cloud Road Trial. If he managed to become an elite at Cloud Hall, they'd probably let him back in. After all, Cloud Hall ran the trial. As long as they made the rules, he figured he’d have other shots at it.

Still, even though he'd decided to back off for now, a part of him was dying to know what the fourth layer's trial was actually like.

With that thought, he started inching his way along the cliff face before leaping onto the edge of the fourth rock platform. 

[SFX: ENERGY BEAM]

[SFX: BEAST GROWL]

The instant he landed, two crimson beams of light lanced out from the cave's depths. A low, guttural growl reverberated from the darkness inside.

[SFX: BEAST ROAR]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

Ice-cold terror shot through Zane's veins like poison. For a split second, it felt as though an invisible hand had clamped around his heart, squeezing until his chest constricted. His muscles locked, freezing him in place despite every rational thought screaming at him to move.

What was that thing? How could any creature possess such overwhelming, suffocating power? Zane had faced down Demons before, but this was different. This fear was primal, ancient, the kind that lived in the deepest recesses of human instinct. The kind that reminded him he was prey.

[SFX: RAPID HEARTBEAT]

His heart hammered against his ribs like a war drum, each beat thundering in his ears until it drowned out everything else.

His mind screamed at him. Run NOW! Every fiber of his being demanded he bolt—to throw himself off the platform, scramble down the cliff face with bleeding fingers if necessary, and just get away from whatever nightmare lurked in that cave.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

But just as he coiled his legs to jump, the beast shot out of the cave. It moved with such terrifying speed that Zane's eyes couldn't track it—all he saw was a black blur hurtling toward him like death incarnate.

[SFX: BODY LANDING ON STONE]

Zane hit the ground and steadied himself. Above him, the beast padded to the platform's edge and threw its head back.

[SFX: WOLF HOWL]

The howl that followed split the air. Below, Cloud Hall disciples stumbled backward — some sat down where they stood, legs gone. Even Lennon's face had drained of color.

The creature was enormous, pitch-black, shaped like a wolf but wrong in every proportion. A Shadow Wolf King — a legendary beast so far removed from ordinary life it could barely be called living at all. It fixed its blood-red eyes on Zane for a long moment. Then it turned and walked back into the cave.

[AMBIENT: QUIET GATEWAY]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Back at the Scrying Slate, Toby had been worried sick that Lennon would use the Formation of the Twelve Signs to eliminate Zane. The thought had gnawed at him for hours, a constant anxiety he couldn't shake. Unfortunately, he hadn't been able to get a message to the top disciple to warn him against doing anything rash, anything that would destroy both their futures.

Not even his family's considerable influence could protect Lennon if he actually killed Zane. Not with everything that had happened.

Luckily, Lennon had proven himself a smart young man—sharp enough to read the political currents swirling around them and decide against aiming the formation at Zane. 

A wave of relief washed over Toby as he watched Zane walk away from the gateway, whole and unharmed. 

[AMBIENT: JUNGLE ATMOSPHERE]

[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]

Zane headed back the way he came, toward the lush jungle. Along the way, he pulled a fresh robe from his Storage Ring and changed out of the tattered rags of his old clothes. Just as he was finishing up, he frowned. The hairs on the back of his neck stood up. Someone was watching him.

"Show yourself!" he called out.

[SFX: WHOOSH AND LANDING]

Instantly, a shadow dropped from the sky, landing without a sound. It was the boy in the cloak—Lewis.

"You're incredible!" Lewis said, his voice brimming with awe. "You actually got the treasure box from the third layer. That’s insane! Can I join you? Let me be on your team!"

Zane gave the cloaked boy a once-over and shook his head before turning away. "I don’t team up with people who hide their faces." 

[SFX: FABRIC RUSTLING]

A moment later, Zane heard a rustle of fabric and glanced back. The boy had thrown back his hood, revealing a handsome, slightly mischievous face. "My name is Lewis Bailey," he said with a grin.

"I'm Zane Lowell," Zane replied simply.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]

With the introductions out of the way, they started walking together. The Cloud Road Trial was only half over, and Zane knew allies would become more critical as the tests got tougher. It was a shame Finn and Patrick had bailed early, but Lewis was strong. He seemed like he could be a decent partner.

Lewis, however, was a talker. He immediately started bombarding Zane with questions, and Zane was already starting to regret his decision. Luckily, a new distraction appeared.

[AMBIENT: ROARING FLAMES]

[SFX: FIRE CRACKLING]

As they emerged from the jungle, Zane saw the fiery waves of the Sea of Raging Fire churning ahead.

He turned to Lewis. "The Sea of Raging Fire is starting to recede. It's time to move."
