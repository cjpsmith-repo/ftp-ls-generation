Ep 81 - One Punch
Word Count: 1274
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMURS]
The pledges from Misty Peak were crushed. After three brutal losses, Dustin had finally clawed back a win for them, but the relief was short-lived.
A new challenger stepped into the ring — short, with a shrill voice and a smirk that said he'd been waiting for this.
"Who's next?"
Dustin squared up. He wasn't done.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
[SFX: RUSHING MOTION]
What followed was fast — too fast for most of the crowd to follow. The short man's speed was something else entirely, and Dustin, for all his agility, couldn't match it. They blurred around each other in a tight, furious exchange until the short man slipped behind Dustin, clasped his hands, and drove his thumbs into Dustin's back with a concentrated burst of Vitality.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
Dustin flew off the ring like a kite with a cut string.
[SFX: RUSHING MOVEMENT]
Zane was already moving. He flashed to the edge of the ring and caught Dustin before he hit the ground — hundreds of pounds of dead weight, held easily in one hand.
[SFX: COUGHING — WET]
Dustin spat blood. His face had gone the color of chalk. "Two ribs," he managed. "Definitely two ribs." Then he looked up and his eyes went wide. "Zane. You're back."
"Don't talk," Zane said. He reached into his Storage Ring and pressed a medicinal pill into Dustin's hand. "Take this. We'll catch up later."
He lowered Dustin to the ground and turned to face the ring.
When the short fighter who'd beaten Dustin called out the rest of Misty Peak, no one stepped forward. The silence was pathetic. Not a single pledge was willing to take on the challenge.
[SFX: DRAMATIC SILENCE]
Then, a voice cut through the noise. It wasn't loud, but everyone heard it. "I'll fight."
It was Zane.
"What? Zane wants to fight for us?"
"No way. He beat Tristram, sure, but he was ranked thirtieth on our peak. What makes him think he can handle a pledge from Eagle Peak?"
"God, we're screwed. Another loss, guaranteed. There goes my elixir for next month. How am I supposed to train without it?"
The grumbling from the Misty Peak crowd was a testament to their shattered morale.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
In the battle ring, the short fighter stared at Zane. Hearing the crowd's doubts, his smirk stretched into a condescending grin.
"You? A guy at the Marrow Refining Level? Ha! Fine, I accept. But don't expect any mercy."
Inter-peak fights were common, but to keep things fair, the fighters were typically chosen from the top thirty ranked pledges of each peak—the best of the best. It was unheard of for a pledge ranked in the top thirty to not even be a High-tier candidate.
So when a kid at the Marrow Refining Level stepped up to represent Misty Peak, it caught the Eagle Peak pledges completely off guard.
[SFX: CROWD EXCITEMENT]
[MUSIC: BATTLE PREPARATION]
"Alright, kid! Let's see the real difference between the Marrow Refining Level and a High-tier candidate!"
[SFX: RUSHING MOTION]
The instant the words left his mouth, he blurred into motion, lunging at Zane like a gust of wind.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
As his opponent closed the distance, Zane took a single step forward, intercepting the charge with a punch that landed square on the guy's face.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
In that split second, the Dragon Scale Shards in his mind flared with green light, surging a colossal wave of energy through his body. All of it—that power, and his own—channeled into a single, devastating strike.
[SFX: BODY SLAMMING INTO WALL]
[SFX: BRICKS CRUMBLING]
The short man shot backward over a hundred feet, slamming into the wall of the battle ring so hard that a shower of shattered brick rained down over his crumpled body. It was impossible to tell if he was even breathing.
Zane flexed his wrist, his expression unreadable. "I was also curious about the difference between the Marrow Refining Level and a High-tier candidate," he said, his voice flat. "And for the record, I'm not interested in your name."
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
A stunned silence crashed over the arena.
The once-rowdy crowd was dead quiet, their mouths hanging open.
Ethan's eyes lit up. He'd been eager to see what Zane would unleash and had been confident he'd win, but he never imagined it would be with a single punch.
One hit. That's all it took for Zane to drop a High-tier candidate. Everyone stared in horror. Even the Varsity members from Misty Peak, cloaked in their signature Shadow Cloaks, looked completely stunned by the display.
"What's your assessment of his power, Glenn?" one of the cloaked figures inquired, leaning forward with evident curiosity.
Glenn analyzed the situation with measured caution, his features concealed beneath the Shadow Cloak's dark fabric. "To demonstrate such overwhelming strength while merely at the Marrow Refining Level..." he mused, stroking his chin thoughtfully. "At this pace, his promotion to Core Apprentice seems inevitable."
He fell silent for a moment, his eyes narrowing as he reconsidered his evaluation. "No, I'm understating it. Should he evolve into a Nature Creature, he might challenge Ronald's position as our premier talent. The raw potential emanating from him is... unsettling."
"Truly?" gasped the other member, eyes widening with astonishment. A hint of disbelief colored his voice as he glanced back toward the arena where the dust still settled from that singular, devastating blow.
Glenn shook his head. "I can't gauge his full strength from one punch. But my gut tells me that was just the tip of the iceberg. I'm not even sure you or I would be a match for him."
"Not a match?" the other member repeated, baffled. "But we're Nature Spirits. We can shape our vitality into energy weapons. Our attacks are on another level entirely. How could he—"
"I don't know," Glenn interrupted, shaking his head. "It's just a feeling."
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
"Wow." After a long moment, the Misty Peak pledges finally snapped out of their shock, only to see the next challenger step into the ring. An aura of explosive power rolled off his massive frame. Seeing him, a new wave of worry washed over the Misty Peak crowd.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
Eagle Peak really was on another level. Zane had just taken down one of their fighters, and now they'd sent in someone who looked even more formidable. This was Keith, ranked ninth at Eagle Peak. It made the Misty Peak pledges wonder, with a sinking feeling, just how powerful their number one really was.
"Look, my only real skill is brute strength," Keith said, his expression completely serious. "If I lose control, I could easily smash you to a pulp. You should reconsider this. I'll accept your surrender right now, no judgment."
Zane scrutinized his opponent's countenance, detecting not the expected smugness but genuine concern. The muscular fighter was actually attempting to spare him from harm.
A soft chuckle escaped Zane's lips. "No, but I appreciate the courtesy."
"Then brace yourself!" Keith drew a profound breath, his chest expanding like a bellows before he launched forward, thundering toward Zane with the momentum of an unleashed juggernaut.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS FOOTSTEPS]
[AMBIENT: TREMBLING PLATFORM]
The platform trembled beneath each thunderous footfall, reverberating through the arena like war drums heralding destruction. Keith adhered to no subtle strategy or deceptive maneuvers; he was a devoted disciple of the ancient principle that overwhelming might could obliterate even the most sophisticated defense.
His intention was brutally simple—to pulverize Zane with nothing but unadulterated, devastating power. The air itself seemed to compress before him, creating a visible distortion that raced toward Zane with terrifying finality.
