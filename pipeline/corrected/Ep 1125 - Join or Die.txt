Ep 1125 - Join or Die

Word count: 1173

[MUSIC: DARK MYSTERIOUS]
Zane knew one thing for sure about the Demon Night: they were devout. From the moment they were born, they were baptized before statues of their three queens, and that loyalty was etched into their very bones. The only way the Radiant Light Sect could have recruited any of them was if they’d found some way to force their obedience.
The smile on the white-robed man’s face slowly faded as he took in Zane’s serious expression. “Do I really have to spell it out for you?”
A sudden grin spread across Zane’s face. “Looks like I don’t have much of a choice, do I?”
The man seemed pleased. “You’re a smart kid. I’m sure you’ll make a great believer. The gods will surely protect you—”
Zane cut him off. “No, I’m not joining.” 
The man’s expression froze, his gaze turning cold as ice. “Do you have any idea what it means to refuse?”
“Not really,” Zane said, a faint smile playing on his lips. “But I’m definitely not interested in wasting my time forging a ‘sword embryo’ for some ‘Supreme Lord.’”
The Sect’s plan to create a sword embryo wasn’t exactly a public secret, but it was known among the highest-ranking figures in the Upper World. Invoking the name of God, the Sect had managed to gather an incredible number of followers, and in their eyes, a Supreme Lord was no different from a god.
Zane’s blunt dismissal was pure blasphemy. The recruiter’s face darkened, his mouth twisting into a cruel smile. He waved a hand, and several beams of light shot down from the shrine’s upper floor.
[SFX: ARMOR MATERIALIZING]
A warrior in gleaming golden armor materialized inside each one.
[SFX: COMMANDING VOICE]
“Seize the man who insulted our God,” the recruiter commanded. “We will burn him to death in hellfire.”
Zane felt a flicker of annoyance. All they’d done was step inside to look around, and now getting out was going to be a whole ordeal. What kind of messed-up place was this?
[MUSIC: DARK BATTLE TENSION]
A flash of green light, and a five-foot-long sword appeared in his hand.
[SFX: SWORD MATERIALIZING]
[SFX: SHADOWY FIGURE ERUPTING]
A shadowy figure — a Nether God — erupted from the blade and rushed the golden warriors.
But nothing happened.
The Nether God slammed into the nearest golden warrior — and stopped cold. A faint green light rippled across the armor, absorbing the strike entirely. The warrior didn’t even flinch.
Zane’s eyes narrowed. He sent another shadow, then another. Same result every time — that green light swallowed everything, leaving the warriors completely unharmed.
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
He switched approaches, sweeping a broad arc of sword light across the group. The blast sent a dozen warriors skidding back across the stone floor. But they were already climbing to their feet before the echo faded, green light still glowing, expressions blank and unmoved.
Whatever that light was, it didn’t just block — it negated.
“Let’s go,” Zane said, grabbing Freya’s arm.
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: MASS ARMORED MOVEMENT]
They didn’t make it far. A thunderous crash rang out as a second wave of golden warriors poured in from every direction — a hundred of them at least, flooding the shrine floor in a wall of gleaming armor. The first group fell in behind them. There was no gap in the line.
Freya’s face went pale. She remembered these golden-armored warriors all too well. They fought like puppets, attacking with everything they had, with no regard for their own safety. From what she’d seen, they either had slave seals on them or were being controlled by some other foul magic. Either way, she had no intention of joining some secret sect just to end up like one of them.
[SFX: BODIES IMPACTING STONE]
Zane didn’t slow down. He lowered his shoulder and drove forward, a wave of force bursting outward with each step, sending warriors spinning away on both sides. The green light held — none of them were badly hurt — but it didn’t matter. He wasn’t trying to hurt them anymore. He just needed a path.
Four guards broke through the chaos and reached him at once. Two clamped onto his arms; the other two grabbed his legs.
[SFX: STRUGGLE GRUNT]
For a moment, he actually stopped.
Then his patience ran out.
[SFX: POWER SURGE BOOM]
The Dragon Scale power surged through him in a single violent pulse. His body shook, and all four warriors were hurled away simultaneously — this time, the green light couldn’t hold. The force passed straight through it, and the guards hit the ground hard, coughing blood.
That got the rest of them moving. The remaining warriors charged in a mass, expressions wild with fanaticism, completely indifferent to what had just happened to their comrades.
Zane stopped holding back. Power flooded through him like a breaking wave, and he drove straight through the crowd — warriors flying left and right, the whole golden sea parting whether it wanted to or not. Freya stayed tight on his heels.
[SFX: DOORS BURSTING OPEN]
They hit open air.
[SFX: ABRUPT SILENCE]
And behind them — nothing. Zane turned. Every single golden warrior had stopped at the threshold, standing motionless, staring out at him with blank eyes. Not one of them stepped through the door.
They couldn’t leave the shrine.
The white-robed man appeared at the entrance a moment later, unhurried, the pleasant smile already back on his face. “It’s not too late to reconsider. You won’t regret joining us.”
Zane looked at him. “I won’t regret it,” he said. “But you will.”
“What does that mean?” the man asked, his smile flickering for just a moment.
Zane shrugged and turned away.
The Sect clearly didn’t dare push too far inside Auspice City. Once they were clear of the shrine, no one followed.
But Zane had no intention of letting it end there.
[MUSIC: QUIET RESOLVE]
Evening settled over Auspice City. Zane brought Freya back to the edge of the shrine.
“Why are we back here?” Freya asked, eyeing the entrance. The golden warriors had left a mark on her. She had no desire to go anywhere near that threshold again.
“Someone’s hungry,” was all Zane said.
[SFX: BESTIARY BAG OPENING]
Freya looked at him, confused, until she saw him pull a small Ancestral Dragon from his bestiary bag.
[SFX: DRAGON GURGLING AND STRUGGLING]
The little dragon let out a few gurgling sounds, its head cocked toward the shrine. It had clearly smelled something it considered food and immediately began to struggle, trying to squirm out of Zane’s grip.
“What… what is that?” Freya asked.
It was no surprise she didn’t recognize it. The dragon was still just a baby and looked nothing like the True Dragons of legend.
Instead of answering, Zane just looked at the dragon. “Go on. But be careful.”
[SFX: CREATURE SINKING INTO STONE]
He let go, and the tiny creature instantly sank into the ground, disappearing from sight.
Since Zane didn’t explain, Freya didn’t press him. He must have had his reasons.
[AMBIENT: MASSIVE SQUARE WITH MURMURING CROWD]
Meanwhile, out in the massive square, the believers were all lying flat on the ground, mumbling to themselves as they offered up their Faith Energy. None of them noticed the tiny Ancestral Dragon moving rapidly just beneath the stone, heading straight for the center of the square.
[SFX: MOVEMENT THROUGH STONE]
Sensing its “food” directly above, it changed course, slipping soundlessly into the massive stone pillar that held up the glowing sphere.
