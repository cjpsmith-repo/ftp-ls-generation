Ep 1094 - Space Erasing Hand

Word count: 1069

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

The warriors’ attacks were useless against his overwhelming power. The best they could hope for was to slow him down a little, but even that felt like a long shot.

But Zane didn’t look worried. He’d blown through the eleventh round, so the twelfth wasn’t about to give him much trouble. Even with a dozen warriors closing in and vines coiling around his legs, there wasn’t a hint of panic on his face.

[SFX: SWORD CRACKLING WITH ENERGY]

Just as they swarmed him, a sharp crackle erupted from his sword, Thundergust.

The thing is, the difference between these guys and the fighters from the Alliance wasn’t just talent or raw strength—it was gear. Warriors from the Alliance could walk into the Hall of Arms and pick up a Grade-Three, maybe even a Grade-Two Divine-Class weapon, without much fuss.

But Zane’s Thundergust was a Grade-One, the best of the best. And when he channeled the power of chaos through it, the sword became something these warriors had no answer for. That, combined with his unstoppable Nether God Shadow technique, put him in a league of his own.

[SFX: MASSIVE THUNDER EXPLOSION]

Suddenly, a wave of thunderous energy exploded from Zane. Bright blue arcs of lightning, each no thicker than a pinky finger, shot out and linked together, forming a crackling net that blanketed the entire platform with him at its center. There was nowhere to run.

The warriors caught in the blast were instantly engulfed.

[SFX: BODIES CRASHING]

They froze in place for a few seconds, their bodies blackened and smoking, before crashing to the platform in a heap. The smell of ozone and burnt flesh filled the air.

[SFX: RAPID WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Just as the lightning net faded, a lone figure zipped through the air—the only survivor. He was a spatial manipulator, and he’d managed to dodge the worst of the blast. But as he reappeared, ready to strike, seven identical figures flickered into existence right in front of him. Zane’s Nether God Shadows. Before the man could even react, they tore him to pieces.

And just like that, Zane had passed the twelfth round. It had been tougher than the eleventh, sure, but not by much.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

What he didn’t know was that on the other side of Willow Island, tens of thousands of spectators were watching with growing frustration. They’d all been waiting for him to finally reveal his trump card, but twelve rounds in, all he’d used was that one sword.

Was that sword really all he had?

Off to the side, a warrior named Adrian just kept chewing on his wooden stick, his expression unreadable.

Whatever Zane’s real limit was, it wasn’t something Adrian could provoke out of him — not with swordsmanship alone. He’d half-expected the kid to crack under the pressure of a coordinated assault and reach for something else. Instead, Zane had just... used the sword. Again. Either that sword really was everything, or this kid’s composure ran deeper than anyone gave him credit for.

Adrian’s gaze drifted upward, toward the highest point of the square — toward the cages.

A handful of them sat up there, holding burly, ragged-clothed prisoners. Death-row convicts, banished to what everyone called the Villain World — the trial’s true final test, reserved for the rare genius who made it far enough to need it. So far, no one had.

Adrian’s eyes lingered there a moment longer before sliding back down to the platforms below.

Layla’s plan had been simple. The Sword Cry Technique alone should have carried her clean through to round nineteen — nothing below that level could touch it.

[SFX: WATER RUSHING]

But the moment round twelve began, water curtains rose around the platform’s edge, and a wall of conjured waves crashed down over her before she could react.

[AMBIENT: DRIPPING WATER]

She stood there a moment, soaked from head to toe, her clothes clinging to her. Sound didn’t carry the same way through water — her Sword Cry Technique would barely register against opponents like these. Someone had built this specifically to counter her.

The Water Law cultivators didn’t close in. They just kept at it — more waves, spears of water lancing toward her — while Layla calmly turned them aside with her sword, almost bored.

A small smile touched her lips. “You all think I only know the Sword Cry Technique...”

From across Willow Island, Adrian’s voice carried back, flat and satisfied. “Of course I don’t. I just want to see how many trump cards you have.”

It was exactly the reaction he’d been hoping for.

[SFX: SPACE DISTORTION]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

With a wave of her hand, the very space around her sword-shadows collapsed, erasing everything in a wide circle.

“The old man’s Space Erasing Hand,” the Lord of the Sunset World noted, his eyes gleaming. “He must think highly of Layla to have taught her that.”

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLE ATMOSPHERE]

“That’s not how you use it,” Yolande commented flatly. “She’s wasting the technique.”

The Space Erasing Hand was one of the old master’s signature moves. To pull it off, you needed a deep understanding of Spatial Law. The user would literally reach out, touch space itself, and wipe a whole section of it out of existence. Of course, the old man could erase a much larger area than Layla. She could only manage the space just outside her spinning sword-shadows, a circle about a hundred feet across.

[SFX: SPATIAL DISTORTION WHOOSH]

The warriors who specialized in Water Law had been keeping their distance at the edge of the platform. But the moment that circle of space vanished, they were all yanked forward against their will.

It was a fundamental principle of spatial manipulation. Even those who couldn’t perform the Space Erasing Hand knew the theory: if you delete a chunk of space from the middle of something, the space on either side has to snap together to fill the void.

And that’s exactly what happened. Layla had erased a ring of space in the middle of the platform, and now everything on the outside—including those poor warriors—was being dragged violently toward the center.

[SFX: VIOLENT BLADE STRIKES]

[MUSIC: DARK DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

And waiting for them in the center was the spinning vortex of her sword-shadows. The helpless warriors were pulled right into the blades and torn to shreds.
