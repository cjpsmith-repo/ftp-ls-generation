Five streams of deadly energy---plus the gleaming Ruin Sword itself---now hunted their prey with singular purpose.

But getting rid of James wasn't going to be that simple. The first energy stream had already locked onto him, tracking him with unerring precision. The other four ribbons of deadly power simply followed its lead, closing the gap with frightening speed. Zane could feel their heat against his back, the air crackling with imminent death just inches behind him.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Before, James had been holding back, his control of the energy almost lazy. Not anymore. Now, blind with fury, he poured every ounce of his focus into the attack. The five streams of energy accelerated, blurring as they tore through the air after Zane.

Drawing deeply on the Phoenix Crystal's power, Zane commanded his body to become weightless, his movements fluid and swift as mercury. The ancient energy surged through his veins, transforming leaden fatigue into desperate agility. His eyes darted frantically across the buildings until they locked onto a shadowy opening carved into a weathered stone wall.
Without hesitation, he launched himself toward it, his heart hammering against his ribs as the deadly energy streams closed in behind him. The narrow gap swallowed him whole, plunging him into a dim alleyway where the scent of moss and decay hung heavy in the air. Shadows danced across the crumbling walls as Zane pressed forward, each footfall echoing his mounting dread.

It was a dead end. Not a hundred feet later, he was trapped. 

He'd run out of room to dodge. An energy stream blocked his path forward, another cut off his retreat, and two more boxed him in from the sides. The fifth one was screaming down from above.
"What now?" A dozen desperate scenarios flashed through Zane's mind. None of them ended well.
He had to risk it.
The five streams of energy hadn't formed a perfect cage; there was a tiny gap, a sliver of hope he could try to squeeze through.
Of course, one wrong move and he'd be swatted like a fly---or lose an arm or a leg, at best. Still, it was better than certain death. He could find an elixir to mend bone or reattach a limb. If he died, that was it. Game over.
But even if he survived this, what about the next attack? He was at full strength and still couldn't outrun them. Injured, he wouldn't stand a snowball's chance in hell.

No time to overthink it. Survive the next few seconds. That's all that mattered. Consequences be damned. Just as he gathered himself to dart through the gap, a voice echoed in his mind.
"Kid, stand still! I'm here to help you!"
"Who's there?" Zane thought, his eyes darting around the empty alley.
[SFX: ICE CRYSTALLIZING]
Instead of an answer, the air around him flash-froze. Countless ice crystals materialized from nothing, instantly fusing into a small, solid wall of ice. The crystals swirled around him, and in the blink of an eye, five separate ice walls had slammed into place, boxing him in and blocking the five incoming attacks.

Now enclosed in his crystalline shield, Zane could see deep gashes carved into the ice where the attacks had hit.
He let out a shaky breath, his heart still hammering against his ribs as tremors ran through his body.
That was too close. If that powerful stranger hadn't stepped in, he would have been sliced in two.
Back in the guest hall, something else was happening.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
A broad figure had stepped directly in front of James, one thick hand pressing down on the tip of his sword. The man was built like a woodcutter — ruddy-faced, heavily built, covered in coarse hair. In his other hand he held a black hatchet, its blade flickering with red light.
"Lord Moon." His voice was unhurried. "I wouldn't make another move if I were you."
James stared at the hatchet. The color drained from his face.
"You're... the Woodcutter of West Mountain."
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC RECOGNITION]

He lowered his sword.
Back in the alley, Zane caught his breath.
Masters of the Soul Realm were on a whole other level. It made him realize just how big of a risk he'd taken back at The Elysian Keep.
"Alright, kid. Get yourself to the guest hall at Moon Mansion," his mysterious savior told him, the voice now casual in his mind. "Don't worry. As long as I'm here, James won't dare to kill you."

As soon as the voice faded, the ice walls shattered, collapsing into a pile of frozen chunks at his feet.
