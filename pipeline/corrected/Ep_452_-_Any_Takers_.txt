Ep 452 - Any Takers?

Word count: 1141

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]

The power gap between cultivation levels was massive. A warrior at the peak of the Soul Realm was in a whole different league than someone at the third grade.

That’s why every top-tier Soul Realm warrior swarmed Zane, cocky as hell. They figured taking down a third-grade fighter like him would be a walk in the park—an easy way to score some points. So, naturally, everyone wanted a piece of him.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE SOUNDS]

While Dominic was busy confronting Gareth from the Mysterious Luna Hall, the others saw their opening and rushed Zane all at once.

[SFX: AGGRESSIVE WHOOSH]

Mason was the first to lunge, his attack quicker and more brutal than anyone else's. He didn't give a damn if he crippled Zane—as long as the kid was breathing, it was good enough for him. He'd sworn he would drag Zane back, no matter what.

The other warriors could only shake their heads, a flicker of pity in their eyes. They knew Mason’s reputation: once he set his mind on something, he'd stop at nothing. And right now, he was dead set on capturing Zane. It looked like the kid's luck had finally run out. If that attack landed, he'd be crippled for life, easy.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PAUSE]

But in the blink of an eye, it was over.

[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]

[SFX: CRASHING DEBRIS]

Zane moved, a blur of motion, and a single, devastating blow sent Mason flying. He smashed clean through several houses in a shower of splintered wood and stone before finally slamming into the dirt. The impact was so brutal, he'd be lucky to fully recover in half a year.

‘What the hell happened to him in the last three months? How did he get this strong, this fast?’ Dominic thought, stunned. ‘This is unreal. He’s on another level now.’ He replayed the moment in his head. Either Zane’s power has skyrocketed, or the best of the Soul Realm have gone soft. Either way, Zane just made one hell of a statement. Dominic knew he could beat Mason, sure, but not like that. Not with one clean, effortless hit.

Back in the Elite House, Dominic had always sensed something special about Zane. He saw the kid's raw potential, knew he was no ordinary student. He’d even predicted Zane would surpass him in three, maybe five years.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]

Turns out, he was right about the potential but way off on the timeline. It didn't take five years. It took three months. Zane had become everything Dominic had imagined—and then some.

Dominic was a firm believer in the indomitable spirit of a warrior. But watching Zane take down Mason so effortlessly… it was enough to make him question everything. A bitter pill of frustration and wounded pride went down hard.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[SFX: RUBBLE SETTLING]

With Mason out cold in the rubble, Zane’s gaze swept over the remaining warriors. He could see the fear plain on their faces; none of them would dare make a move now. He knew it. They knew it.

Still, he couldn't resist twisting the knife. "Any other volunteers? I'm just warming up!"

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

Gareth, from the Mysterious Luna Hall, stood frozen, a cold sweat breaking out across his skin. He couldn't find the words.

He'd just barely escaped Mason's fate. If Dominic hadn't intercepted him—hadn't blocked his own attack on Zane—that would be him lying broken in the dirt. There was no way he could have dodged that hit.

‘What in the world was that? What kind of technique did he use on Mason? How could it be so damn powerful?’

[AMBIENT: EERIE WIND]

The questions echoed in Gareth’s mind, but he had no answers.

Even for a smart guy like Gareth, figuring out Zane's technique was impossible. After all, even masters of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm—warriors like Saint Owen—could only warp space to use the Mystical Pace. They couldn't even begin to grasp the first level of Spatial Law. The stone carvings, both on the Cloud Road and in Cloud Hall, were incomplete. The full law was lost, which was why no one had ever truly mastered its first level.

But Zane had. He had completely mastered the first level of Spatial Law back in the upper world, where he’d had access to training resources far beyond anything imaginable in The Heartlands. That was the whole point of the Dragon Soar Arena, wasn't it? The reason so many of The Heartlands’ best fighters clawed their way in: a shot at the upper world.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

Dominic stepped forward, his expression carved from granite. "I held back because I didn't know Zane had entered the Arena," he announced, each word deliberate and weighted. His gaze swept across the assembled warriors, measuring their resolve. "That ignorance ends now. We've crossed paths again, and I refuse to stand idle while others move against him." He paused, letting the silence stretch taut before delivering his ultimatum. "Anyone who raises a hand against Zane raises it against Cloud Hall itself."

The declaration hung in the air like a blade suspended above their heads. A fresh wave of terror rippled through the warriors. Dominic's reputation preceded him; he wasn't one to make empty threats.

But despite the fear that gripped them, despite the cold sweat beading on their brows, not a single warrior retreated. Pride, desperation, or perhaps simple stubbornness rooted them in place. They had come too far, risked too much, to turn back now.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

"So you've all chosen death! Fine. I'll make it quick!" Zane roared. He was done wasting time. He’d gone through hell and back to return to the Heartlands, and he was so close to finally leaving the Dragon Soar Arena. He wouldn't let these idiots stop him now. They were pushing his last nerve, leaving him no choice but to end this.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[AMBIENT: POWER HUM]

The air itself seemed to hum with power as he lifted his leg, energy crackling along his limbs like living lightning. Then, like a ghost, Zane floated forward, his movements defying the natural laws that bound ordinary cultivators.

His heart pounded with a mixture of fury and anticipation. These fools had no idea what they'd provoked. After everything he'd been through, he refused to be delayed by common thugs seeking glory.

He crossed the threshold, finally clear of the Dragon Soar Arena's confines. Freedom lay just beyond this mob of would-be heroes. His muscles tensed, power gathering in his core as he prepared to unleash devastation on the warriors blocking his path.

Then a voice cut through the tension, stopping him cold.

[MUSIC: TENSE SURPRISE]

"Don't get too arrogant! You just might be the one dying today!"

The voice was impossibly sweet, almost melodic, and it was enough to make Zane freeze mid-motion.
