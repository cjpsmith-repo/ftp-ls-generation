Ep 711 - Sacrifice

[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL MONASTERY]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Cloud Hall had come a long way in the last few years. It wasn't just Tia who had grown stronger; every fighter under their banner had been steadily improving. After establishing the Trinity Killing Array, the sect was, for all intents and purposes, a fifth-grade power. In the eyes of everyone in The Heartlands, they were already on par with the Ethereal Spirit Sect, lacking only its long history and legacy.
Once Tia broke through to the Life-and-Death Realm, that status would be set in stone. With their influence soaring, she might even be able to elevate Cloud Hall to a sixth-grade sect—the first sacred place in The Heartlands.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
[SFX: DISTANT THUNDER]
But just as Tia reached the most critical point in her training, a massive internal conflict finally erupted.
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT HALL]
It wasn’t exactly a surprise. The seeds of this rebellion had been sown decades ago. Cloud Hall had grown out of the old Cloud School, which had expanded by absorbing several third-grade houses from Cloudhaven—powerful families like the Drews, Shaws, Dillons, and Wards. Over the years, it had separated from its origins and slowly become powerful in its own right. But no matter how much Cloud Hall grew, its leadership had always passed to a descendant of one of these founding houses.
That was the old way, at least. Elder Imogen had been the first exception. She didn't come from a noble line; she was just an incredibly gifted student from the Luminous Sect, an affiliate of Cloud Hall. She was powerful, decisive, and exceptionally talented. While some of the major houses grumbled about her origins, none could deny her strength.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Tia, however, was a different story. Though she’d grown up in the Cloud School, her background marked her as a complete outsider. Worse, there was no shortage of talent among the great houses. Emanuel Drew, for instance, had both the strength and the standing to challenge for the leadership.
[AMBIENT: TENSE COUNCIL CHAMBER]
When Elder Imogen passed the title to Tia instead, it caused an uproar, especially within House Drew. They might have conceded that Tia was superior to Emanuel in some respects, but her leadership brought changes that grated on the old guard. Her lenient treatment of Zane, for example, had been a major point of contention. The arguments only died down after Zane constructed an indestructible, sect-protecting array.
But it was never enough. The old noble houses didn't share Elder Imogen's vision. Even with all the benefits Tia brought to Cloud Hall, they could not stomach an outsider holding the reins of power. And Emanuel was the one leading the charge.
Emanuel had been looking for his moment for years. Now he had it. Elder Imogen had left Cloud Hall to seek an opportunity for her own Life-and-Death breakthrough, traveling with an independent warrior into the outer reaches. With her gone, there was no one left with the authority or the strength to stand behind Tia. And Tia herself was at her most vulnerable — deep in the Life-and-Death Crises, unable to defend herself or even respond.
The conflict had split Cloud Hall into three factions. Emanuel's group was the largest: third-rank families who'd fully thrown in their lot, knowing that if Tia survived and broke through, they would all be punished for what they'd done. There was no turning back for them.
The second faction was a neutral group caught between family loyalty and personal conscience, unwilling to attack Tia but unable to openly defy the houses they belonged to. The third faction — Tia's supporters — was led by Saint Morphen, backed by Cloud Hall's younger disciples who had no family allegiances pulling them the other way.
For days, Emanuel's forces had launched wave after wave at Tia's Ice Palace. Wave after wave had been turned back.
The reason was Finn.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE SQUARE]
Finn sat cross-legged on the square in front of the Ice Palace, a dark green longsword across his knees. Blood ran steadily from both his eyes, tracing long red lines down his face and dripping onto the stone beneath him. Beside him, Patrick and Lewis stood guard, and Saint Morphen and the remaining loyal warriors had formed a perimeter around them all.
The bleeding was the cost. Finn had unlocked the Sword Eye — a higher level of the Heaven Sword technique that allowed him to draw on Heaven's Force directly, channeling it into his blade and storing it as pure sword intent. In theory, given enough time to charge, a single strike could kill even a Life-and-Death Realm warrior. In practice, the strain of holding that force in his body was destroying him from the inside out.
He could only strike once. After that, he was spent — and everyone knew it. But no one was willing to be the one to absorb the blow.
Three days ago, Larry Dillon — known throughout Cloudhaven as a master of defense — had decided to try. His Stolid Earth Technique was a perfect application of Earth Law, allowing him to disperse an enemy's attack across his body and channel the force harmlessly into the ground. He'd used it to withstand strikes from Saint Morphen himself. He stepped onto the square, confident.
[SFX: SWORD DRAWN]
Finn rose to meet him.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
[SFX: GROUND SHATTERING]
The energy was so immense that the ground for a hundred feet around him instantly pulverized into fine dust, yet it still wasn't enough to stop the attack. Finn’s blow was simply too powerful. It tore through Larry's defense and killed him in a single, brutal motion.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
That one display of impossible strength was enough to halt the advance. The Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors froze, their charge dissolving into terrified hesitation. Rushing the palace now was a death sentence.
Stymied, Emanuel could only resort to launching futile, long-range attacks at the ice palace, none of which did any real damage.
[SFX: FRUSTRATED ROAR]
"We can't keep this up!" Emanuel roared, his voice laced with frustration. He had planned to capture Tia swiftly, but now his warriors were too terrified of Finn to storm the palace. The delay was costing him everything. If Tia managed to complete her breakthrough, all his efforts would be for nothing.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
"You!" he yelled, pointing a shaking finger at a Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior from House Shaw. "Get up there and engage him!"
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD SILENCE]
If they could just force Finn to focus on a single target, the rest of his forces could swarm in and eliminate Tia's remaining supporters.
To put it simply, they needed a sacrifice.
