Ep 8 - The Only Rule That Counts
Word Count: 1245
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
[SFX: GUARDS SCUFFLE]
Even with all their strength, the servants couldn't budge Zane.
Now that he had reached the Bone Refining Level, his feet were planted like roots, as if they'd been hammered straight into the ground. The men pulled with everything they had, but they couldn't move him an inch.
Soon, they were all drenched in sweat and gasping for breath.
Seeing this, Darren sneered, "Zane, I'm giving you a way out. Serve Grey. It's better than being everyone's punching bag. Don't make this harder than it has to be!"
[SFX: DEEP BREATH]
[SFX: ENERGY CHANNELLING]
Fire flashed in Zane's eyes. He took a deep, steadying breath, his chest tight. After reaching the Bone Refining Level, he could finally channel his energy, finally fight back.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS SHOCKWAVE]
He planted his feet and unleashed a wave of raw power that burst from him in a thunderous shockwave. The ground buckled as if a bomb had just gone off. Every servant, including Grey and Darren, slapped their hands over their ears as a high-pitched whine rang out in the aftershock.
"Grey Hawn! Darren Fane!" Zane roared. "Even my uncle, Bryson, wouldn't dare ask me to be his servant. You two are nothing but servants in House Lowell. Who the hell do you think you are, acting with such arrogance? I don't know where you get off thinking you could even suggest something so ridiculous."
The raw power rolling off Zane made Darren stumble back, but Grey Hawn stood his ground. This was exactly what Darren wanted—to sic Grey on Zane.
Seeing the fury on Zane's face, Darren knew he was close. The one thing Grey couldn't stomach was being disrespected. Zane was in for it now.
Grey Hawn remained motionless, the picture of calm. With narrowed eyes, he said, "Zane, learn your place. You're a servant now, not a young master. Perrin is the young master. It should be an honor for you to serve me."
[SFX: VIOLENT SHOVING]
[SFX: PEOPLE FALLING]
"Shut up!" Zane shouted, violently shoving the servants off him and sending them sprawling.
Grey watched the outburst with a patient smirk. "Are you trying to rebel? According to House Lowell rules, rebellion is a death sentence."
A harsh laugh escaped Zane's lips as he advanced on him. He sneered, "Family rules? How dare an outsider like you lecture me about my own family's rules? Let me teach you what the rules of my house really are!"
As Zane moved to strike, he drew on all his newfound strength.
"Zane, don't you dare!" Grey said calmly. "If you touch me, Master Andrew will have your ass. You're nothing but a punching bag in this house. Are you really that eager to be beaten to death?"
[SFX: SHARP SLAP]
A sharp crack echoed through the yard. Zane had slapped Grey clean across the face.
There was no stopping him now.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CONFRONTATION]
[SFX: REPEATED SLAPS]
He stepped forward again, grabbed Grey by the collar, and slapped him again, and again, and again.
Color drained from Grey Hawn's wrinkled face under the brutal assault. The bright red print of Zane's hand was already blooming on his swelling cheek. A fine mist of blood sprayed from his lips with every impact. Zane smirked. Grey's face was already swelling into a bruised, crimson mess.
Watching from the side, Darren trembled from head to toe.
"According to House Lowell rules, those who spread lies and falsely accuse others shall be punished."
[SFX: SHARP SLAP]
A sharp slap landed across Grey's other cheek.
"According to House Lowell rules, those who are arrogant and bully others shall be punished."
[SFX: SLAP]
Another slap followed.
"According to House Lowell rules, anyone who stirs up trouble and twists the facts will be punished."
[SFX: PAINFUL GRUNT]
"Ouch!"
"According to House Lowell rules..."
[SFX: PAINFUL GRUNT]
"Ouch!"
[SFX: SPITTING BLOOD]
Each slap was hard enough to make Grey spit blood. Darren was shaking, horrified.
Both he and Grey were stunned. Zane had been a slave for so long, always obedient, silently enduring every insult thrown his way. How could he possibly dare to strike the steward?
What they didn't realize was that Zane's quiet compliance wasn't obedience—it was patience.
[AMBIENT: GATHERING CROWD]
[SFX: MURMURING]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC, HISTORICAL]
Hearing the commotion, several of the younger Lowell family members had gathered in the yard.
They watched, every single one of them just as shocked as the servants.
Grey had been acting outrageously for years, protected by his connection to Andrew Lowell. He was so deluded that he carried himself like one of the masters.
Some of these kids had been bullied by him before, but they never fought back because of his position. Grey had no idea how much his own servants despised him, let alone the young family members he tormented. He was just as clueless about the hatred they all felt for him.
And today, someone was finally giving him what he deserved.
But these kids, young as they were, understood that arrogance was born from powerful support. Grey was arrogant because of Andrew Lowell. For all his posturing, Grey was disgustingly humble around the Second and Third Branches of House Lowell.
Regardless, it was completely out of line for a slave to beat a steward like this. Zane was in deep trouble. Everyone knew that what he had just done was a death sentence.
[SFX: SLAPPING SOUNDS]
Zane slapped Grey a few more times before he finally stopped. By now, Grey Hawn's face was a bloody, mangled pulp. His neatly tied hair was a matted disaster of blood and torn flesh.
After the brutal beating, Grey weakly opened his mouth to threaten him.
"You... you just wait..."
[SFX: HARDEST SLAP]
Before Grey could finish, Zane slapped him across the face one last time.
[SFX: BODY FALLING]
Grey crumpled to the ground.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Zane's cold gaze landed on Darren.
A tremor of pure fear ran through Darren. "Zane Lowell, don't you forget you're just a slave!" he yelled, backing away.
"So what if I'm a slave?" Zane said, slowly stalking toward him.
"Don't you dare cross me! My position is far higher than yours!" Darren shouted, trying to sound intimidating.
"Position? High, low... what does any of that matter?" Zane growled, his voice low and threatening as he closed the distance. "My name is Lowell. Even as a slave, I am a Lowell. And you seem to have forgotten the only rule that counts. My fist is stronger than yours, so I'm above you. My strength is greater, so I'm above you. My martial arts are better, so I'm above you!"
He lashed out and grabbed Darren. His newfound power filled him with a surging confidence. Zane raised his hand.
[SFX: SHARP SLAP]
The crack of the slap was so sharp that the onlookers flinched.
[SFX: BODIES COLLIDING]
After striking Darren, Zane threw him on top of Grey. The two men screamed as their bodies collided in a painful heap.
Zane looked down at them, his chin high. "Let that be a lesson to both of you," he said, his voice cold. "Respect the person, not the position."
[MUSIC: POWERFUL THEME]
Then he turned and walked away.
The crowd of Lowell family and servants parted for him like the sea, a path clearing instantly as he headed for the door.
A bystander shook their head. "Did you feel that power? That wasn't normal. That was something else."
