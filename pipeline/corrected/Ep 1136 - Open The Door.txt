Ep 1136 - Open The Door

Word count: 1103

[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
[SFX: MOTH SWARM BUZZING]
The moths were closing in from every direction, and the space left to maneuver had shrunk to almost nothing.
Zane’s expression tightened. *Are these things unkillable?* he thought, his frustration mounting. 
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
He’d just seen Deke and Fergus charge out, their protective Vitality flaring into a swirling storm around them. But even they were struggling, and while they weren’t on his level, they were still masters of the Spirit Supreme Realm.
Still, every creature had a weakness, even these nightmare insects. Usually, things like this were vulnerable to thunder and lightning. 
[SFX: MOTH WINGS FLUTTERING]
As he yanked Freya out of the way of another pass, a lone moth drifted toward them out of the gloom, a ghostly speck in the shadows.
An idea sparked.
[SFX: ENERGY GATHERING HUM]
Zane began to gather his Chaos Energy, ready to summon a bolt of lightning.
“Don’t,” Lavender’s voice cut through his thoughts. “It’s useless.”
The Cobalt Dragon echoed the warning instantly. “Thunder won’t work on them. In fact, the lightning will only attract more.”
[SFX: ENERGY DISSIPATING]
Zane immediately reined in the power he was gathering. So that’s why Abner and his crew hadn’t tried it.
“Then what do we do?” Zane asked, his frown deepening as the ghostly moths floated closer.
“We wait,” Lavender said. “Just wait for the door to the palace to open, then you run for it.” She’d been here before, a hundred thousand years ago, but there hadn’t been any Cannibal Moths back then. If she’d known, she would have warned him, and they wouldn’t be trapped like this.
Zane gave a grim nod. All they could do was keep moving, keep pulling Freya out of the path of the swarm.
[SFX: TINKLING MOTH HATCHING]
But more of those faint, tinkling sounds echoed as more moths hatched, filling the air. Soon, there was almost no room left to move.
Zane’s eyes swept the darkness. The moths were thinner near the Hydra statue — not gone, but thinner. It wasn’t much, but it was something.
He went still, watching the swarm churn. Half a breath. Then he saw it — a gap.
“Hold on,” he said, and pulled Freya with him.
[SFX: RUSHING MOVEMENT]
They shot upward. Freya felt the ground leave her feet, the world spinning in the absolute dark as Zane hauled her up onto one of the statue’s heads. She couldn’t see her own hand in front of her face. She had no idea where she was — only that Zane’s grip hadn’t let go.
Below them, the swarm churned on. The statue head was a small island above the worst of it.
[SFX: PIERCING SCREAM]
[SFX: BONE CRACKING]
A piercing scream ripped through the chaos, cut short by the wet, sickening crack of bone. Someone else had been overwhelmed.
[MUSIC: TENSE PULSATING STRINGS]
Freya’s heart hammered against her ribs. She didn’t know how much longer they could last. At any second, she could be the next one devoured, leaving nothing but a skeleton behind.
Zane’s jaw clenched. The moths were packed so tightly now that they were completely boxed in. There was nowhere left to run.
[SFX: CLICK]
Then — a sound. A sharp mechanical click, like a lock disengaging deep inside stone. The entire Hydra statue shuddered beneath them and let out a low, grinding roar.
[SFX: STONE MECHANISM ENGAGING]
[SFX: STATUE RUMBLING]
“I’ve got it!” Lincoln’s voice rang out from inside the seventh head, tight with relief. “The door is open!”
[AMBIENT: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS AND BATTLE FORMATION]
Just as their last bit of open space vanished, three blazing white lights tore through the darkness.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION CRACK]
The Grand Teleportation. Someone — three groups of someone — had been waiting just outside, watching for exactly this moment.
The light from the teleportation was blinding after so long in the dark. And in that sudden flash of illumination, Freya saw what she hadn’t been able to see before — the entire space, floor to ceiling, was thick with black moths. A seething, impenetrable mass of them in every direction.
Zane had guided her through all of that, which meant he’d been able to see them the whole time.
She didn’t have time to process it.
[SFX: SMOKE HISSING]
[SFX: MOTHS DROPPING]
The warriors who emerged from the light each carried a thin length of pale yellow bamboo. From the cut end, wisps of yellow smoke curled into the air. The Cannibal Moths reacted instantly — swarming toward the light — and died the moment they touched the smoke, dropping from the air in cascades, one after another, dead before they hit the ground.
One group was led by a woman from the Violetwood Sanctuary, who served a queen named Lavender. Beside her, a man from the Magnolia Sanctum let out a low, satisfied laugh. “A hundred thousand years,” he said. “Last time the palace opened, I was still a child.”
The Violetwood Sanctuary’s leader glanced at the seventh head. “A hundred thousand years ago, our Queen entered through the sixth head,” she said, her voice cool and even. “Let’s see what the seventh has to offer.”
The other was a contingent of Ogres, led by their Saint Lord. They’d clearly been waiting nearby, watching the entrance to the palace, not caring in the slightest whether Zane or the brothers lived or died. To them, lives like theirs were cheap.
[SFX: HEAVY DOOR CREAKING OPEN]
The moment the palace door opened, they’d planned to rush in with their best young fighters. It was well-known that the palace rejected warriors as powerful as these leaders, who were all on the verge of becoming World Lords. If they tried to force their way in, the whole structure would collapse. But that didn’t stop them from sending in their own armies of Spirit Supreme Realm warriors.
Abner was drowning in despair. Trapped in the dark, he had been forced to watch as his brothers were devoured one by one, reduced to nothing but piles of bone. He wasn’t sure how much longer he could have held on by himself. 
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY BLASTS]
But just after the last of his six brothers had fallen, these newcomers had appeared, wiping out the swarming moths with terrifying ease. He just stared, reeling from the shock.
Then, a raw, burning rage cut through the numbness. 
“You,” he choked out, his voice cracking. “Why didn’t you come sooner?”
Normally, Abner would never have dared to speak to masters of this level with anything but the utmost respect. But as his gaze fell on the scattered piles of bones that were all that was left of his brothers, his grief was too much to contain.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
[SFX: CRIMSON ENERGY CHARGING]
The Saint Lord of the Ogres just shot him a cold look and sneered, not even bothering with a reply. A mere Spirit Supreme Realm warrior had no right to question him. The massive ogre shifted his weight, and a crimson light, pulsing with immense power, began to form at his fingertip. 
He was going to kill Abner for the insult.
