Basic swordsmanship?
[SFX: CROWD GASPS]
A stunned silence fell over the crowd. No one had ever seen such a basic technique used against an opponent this powerful.
But in the sea of shocked faces, one person seemed almost glad—Zachary, from Ninety-Foot Peak.
He'd already witnessed what Zane's incredible basic swordsmanship could do, so he got why everyone was so floored.
Maybe Zane's skill is just blowing their minds, he thought. The first time Zachary saw Zane use basic swordsmanship on the Seven-Star Platform, his opponent hadn’t been anywhere near this strong.
But now, facing a much tougher fighter, he was still using it. Zachary figured the crowd had to be even more shocked and unsettled than he'd been back then.
Basic swordsmanship was the first discipline most warriors learned. There were other Foundational Disciplines, too, like Basic Spearsmanship and Basic Fist Forms.
Typically, a young warrior would master the basics in childhood and then move on to more advanced styles, using what they'd learned as a foundation.
But the style itself was so simple it was practically useless against powerful enemies. It had no advanced tactics, no real power. No one in their right mind would ever use it in a serious fight like this.
It was like trying to solve a calculus problem using only basic addition.
[SFX: MURMURING CROWD]
"Has Zane lost his mind?"
"How can he use basic swordsmanship with a Mythic Weapon? Does he think this fight with Tracy is some kind of joke?"
"Fighting with basic moves in the All Peaks Competition? That's a massive insult to his opponent!"
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
In the arena, though, Tracy was dead serious. The moment Zane raised his sword, she felt a powerful, undeniable threat.
She knew it was just a simple downward chop, but she also knew its power was anything but simple. There were hidden depths to his version of the move that she couldn't quite grasp.In the stands, Finn's eyes narrowed. Something was off. It was Basic Swordsmanship — unmistakably — and yet it wasn't. He couldn't put his finger on what was wrong with it. He just knew, instinctively, that it wasn't simple.

"Impressive swordsmanship," Tracy declared, "but you’ll need more than just a sword to beat me!"
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
The shadow of the Blue Phoenix behind her blazed with brilliant light as the Vitality gathering in her hands intensified.
[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]
"The Claw of the Blue Phoenix!" she screamed.
She curled her fingers, forging sharp, blue talons from the swirling light and Vitality before lunging at Zane.
[SFX: ENERGY SLASHING]
Five shimmering blue claw marks, each one pulsing with the ferocious power of the Blue Phoenix, tore through the air toward him.
The Blue Phoenix was a legendary beast. Even with just a fraction of its bloodline, Tracy was a terrifying force.
Most of the disciples had thought Zane might win with his strange chop, but feeling the immense power of the Blue Phoenix flowing from Tracy, they weren’t so sure anymore.
It was impossible to call a winner. Both Zane’s and Tracy’s abilities were far beyond the level of the other disciples in the competition. Even their past fights were no longer a good measure. The only way to know was to see who was left standing.

[SFX: SCREECHING COLLISION]

"Oh my god!"
"What is that noise?!"
"My head is spinning! I think I’m gonna throw up!"
The screech was unbearable for many of the less-experienced disciples. Even those who had reached the top of the Adept Realm turned pale, clearly uncomfortable but managing to tough it out.
Only the disciples who had reached the Soul Realm just frowned slightly, barely reacting.
A fierce sword intent radiated from the simple three-foot blade, methodically dissolving the aggressive claw marks. But the Claw of the Blue Phoenix was powerful. A closer look would have revealed countless tiny wind shears swirling within the claws, constantly trying to tear the Streamer apart.
Luckily, Zane’s Streamer was a Mythic Weapon, strong enough to take the abuse. If it had been a lesser magical weapon, or even a Spirit-Forged one, it would've been shredded to pieces by now.
After just a second, Zane's chop finally won out, shattering the blue claws completely.
"The claw marks are broken!"
"Looks like Zane’s sword is stronger than her attack!"
[MUSIC: RAISING THE STAKES]
"Zane’s only got the upper hand because of his weapon!" one disciple in the crowd yelled. "I mean, he’s wielding a Mythic Weapon, and Tracy’s just using her own power."
Actually, Tracy wasn’t fighting empty-handed. She had two short Stiletto Hairpins hidden in her palms.
The pair of daggers was incredibly sharp and perfect for sneak attacks.
When Tracy fought, she usually saved the Stiletto Hairpins as her trump card, meant to leave deep, bleeding holes in her opponent’s body.
And she'd already used them. She'd managed to pierce Zane's shoulder with two of them the moment she'd activated her Blue Phoenix power.
But the second Zane raised his sword, he'd taken the upper hand. Tracy felt like she couldn’t get her footing anymore.
Zane is only Second Level of the Adept Realm. He might be special, but how is he even competing with me?
He’s a nobody! Just one face among hundreds of thousands of disciples in the Skyreach Sect! A single grain of sand!
How is he beating me with this stupid basic swordsmanship he picked up from who-knows-where! Tracy thought, her frustration boiling into fury.
"I will never lose to you!" she declared, gritting her teeth.
[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDUP]
Her eyes began to glow with a fiery red light. That could only mean one thing: she was going to burn the blood of the Blue Phoenix.
