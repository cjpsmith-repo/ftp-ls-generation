The Lucky Light had barely settled in Olivia's token before Zane was crossing the floor toward her.
"Damn, nice work," he said, his voice ringing with genuine praise.
Olivia smiled and shook her head. "Don't even start, I'm still a mile behind you."
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]
On paper, she had him beat. She was a tenth-level War General, while Zane had only been a tenth-level War Fighter before absorbing the Lucky Light. Even now, he was just a first-level War General. But Olivia knew, just like everyone else now standing around them on the tenth floor, that she wouldn’t stand a chance against him. The whole crowd of human War Generals had gathered, and even Dylan—who’d been practically consumed by jealousy before—was now looking at Zane with something like awe.
[MUSIC: EPIC CINEMATIC]
Zane’s victory hadn't just rattled the Tower of Sin; the shockwaves had reached all the way back to the Violet Heart Sanctuary. When he first entered the Tower, the hope was that he might get strong enough to help humanity win a single, extra seat. They were a minor power here, and they'd be thrilled with just one more; the four they currently held had been won with blood and sacrifice.
But as Zane climbed higher, revealing more and more of his true strength, the people watching—both in the Tower and the Sanctuary—started paying closer attention. After meeting him, Elder Kingsley had revised his expectations. He no longer thought Zane would just get them a seat. He predicted Zane would earn a high-ranking one, maybe even cracking the top thirty. It was a hell of a forecast, especially since no human had ever broken into the top fifty.
From the start, the four human seat owners at the top of the Tower had been tracking his progress. But no one, not even Kingsley, could have predicted the firestorm he’d unleash after leaving the Talent Tablet.
He’d gone head-to-head with the Ogres and the Demon Night, and somehow, single-handedly turned the tables. Kicking out the two War Sages the Ogres had been hiding in the Tower was a move that went beyond anyone’s wildest expectations.
Zane had stunned every race on the continent. The Tower of Sin was always the center of attention, and in just a few days, his name was on everyone's lips.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHIMES]
On the main continent, anyone with a shot at reaching the Soul Sea Realm was called a "saint-level genius." But after the Talent Tablet displayed two new words right before his name—Celestial Rank—that's what everyone started calling him. Celestial Rank.
Only Zane knew the truth behind those words.
"Elder Kingsley asked me to pass something along," Olivia said. "Come on—let's find somewhere quieter."
Zane followed her to one of the residential rooms on the tenth floor. Outside the door, several human War Generals stood guard.
[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]
[MUSIC: SUBTLE TENSION]
Inside, under a faint amber light, Olivia's expression was carefully composed. She was doing her best to seem casual, but Zane could see the nerves underneath it.
She wasn't alone in that. Kingsley, the four seat-controllers, every peak War Lord the Violet Heart Sanctuary had in the Tower—they were all watching this moment with their breath held. Because Zane hadn't come from the Violet Heart Sanctuary. He hadn't come from the main continent at all.
The Sanctuary had done its homework. They'd traced him back to where he'd first appeared without warning, rescuing a group of prisoners from an Ogre pasture. The ancient teleportation array buried in those plains had swallowed every person sent into it and returned none of them. Which meant Zane had come from somewhere else entirely—another continent, another world.
He was a passerby. Human, yes—but that was no guarantee he'd help them. And then there was Lavender, a queen of the Demon Night living inside his body. The Demon Night weren't enemies of the humans, but they weren't allies either.
Everything hinged on Zane's intentions. That was why Kingsley had given this task to Olivia. He'd even hinted, carefully, that if she needed to make a personal sacrifice to secure Zane's loyalty, she shouldn't hesitate.
Olivia had understood immediately what he meant. She'd also known immediately that it wouldn't be necessary. Kingsley and the Sanctuary elders were overthinking it. Zane wasn't that kind of person.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
She met his eyes and spoke simply. "Elder Kingsley wants to know: how far are you willing to go for us?"
He could see the hesitation in Olivia’s eyes, feel the unspoken question hanging between them. He appreciated her directness, though; honesty was a rare thing, and he respected it.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
He let out a low chuckle. "If I have my way," he said, his voice dropping, "I'm going to wipe every last Ogre from this tower."
Olivia gasped. "Wipe them all out? Can you really do that?"
Zane just shrugged, his honesty unwavering. "Not sure," he admitted. "But I'm damn well going to try."
Hearing that, Olivia felt a wave of relief wash over her. She hadn't known Zane long, but she already knew he wasn't the type to make empty threats. He either kept his mouth shut or did exactly what he said he would.
"The Ogres are torn on what to do," she said, a flicker of worry in her eyes. "But I'm guessing they’ll still try to negotiate with you." She left the other half of her thought unsaid: if they offered him the world, would he be tempted to take it?
"Negotiate?" Zane raised an eyebrow, a slow, dangerous smile spreading across his face. "Fine. Let's see what they're willing to offer."
[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
And it was true. The Ogre race was tearing itself apart.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TOWER INTERIOR]
On the top floor of the Tower of Sin, there were one hundred and twenty-eight private spaces, each one its own separate dimension, similar to the inside of a Storage Ring but without the rule against holding living beings.
This was why there were always exactly one hundred and twenty-eight seats. Each space had a door marked with its rank. Esther Sterling of the Demon Night, ranked first, had the door to space number one. Maurice, the runner-up, had number two.
[SFX: ANGRY VOICES ARGUING]
Lately, the twenty-six Ogre seat owners had been gathering in one of those spaces every single day, their arguments over how to deal with Zane growing more furious each time.
