Ep 1142 - The World Tree

Word count: 1139

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
High in the vaulted ceiling of the cave, a single withered vine dangled from a crack in the rock, its leaves shrunken and clinging to a shriveled stem. The vine itself was nothing special, but at its very tip hung an incredible seed. It was the size of a thumb, a brilliant, smooth yellow. Who knew how many years it had hung there, perfectly balanced between the cave roof and the floor far below.
Down below, however, no one was looking up. The warriors were too busy gawking at the strange flowers and herbs filling the cavern floor. The seed was so high above that the tiny, gleaming treasure had gone completely unnoticed. 
That is, until a warrior’s excited shout ripped through the air, grabbing everyone’s attention.
The cavern was a garden of exotic plants, and greed glittered in every eye. Most of the warriors—everyone except Zane and Lincoln—hailed from the three great powers of the Dark World. Resources there were scarce, and they’d never seen the kind of rare treasures that warriors from the Thirteen Palaces took for granted. To them, every single herb in this cave was a chance to gain immense power. 
[MUSIC: TENSE RISING STRINGS]
And when they finally saw the seed, that greed turned into pure, burning desire.
The excited voices overlapped as warriors who recognized it called out what they knew. The World Tree had only appeared five times in the entire history of the universe. Two of those times, the warriors who obtained the seed had gone on to become Supreme Lords. 
It was classified as a “core treasure” — a rare category of items that didn’t just sit inside a warrior’s inner world but actively shaped it, growing alongside the warrior, accumulating world power, blooming and bearing fruit over time. Most treasures, even divine ones, couldn’t interact with a warrior’s inner world at all. A core treasure could. And it was thought to be a relic of the previous evolution cycle — meaning every seed that existed was irreplaceable. Once used, it was gone from the universe forever.
Meanwhile, at the entrance to the Underground Palace, the three Saint Lords weren’t impressed. They just frowned.
“Unless I’m mistaken, that’s a Seed of the World Tree,” the Ogre Saint Lord said, his eyes flashing with the same greed as the warriors below. He wanted it instantly.
The saint lord of the Magnolia Sanctum shook his head with a sigh of regret. “We’ve already broken through to the Spirit Supreme Realm. Our inner worlds are set. A treasure like that is useless to us now.”
The Saint Lord of the Demon Night, however, was calm. “They’re lucky to have found it,” he said. 
[MUSIC: OMINOUS FOREBODING]
“But whether they can get it is another story. That seed has been hanging there for countless years. This palace has been opened plenty of times, and no one has ever managed to claim it.”
He knew this better than anyone. Six openings, and the seed had never been claimed. The last time, his own queen had led the Demon Night’s finest inside and still walked away empty-handed. These kids had no chance.
So, as far as he was concerned, the World Tree’s seed was just a shiny, unreachable prize. The three of them fell silent, content to watch the show.
“That’s the legendary Seed of the World Tree!” someone shouted. “It can be the foundation of a warrior’s power, growing right along with them!”
Freya stared up at the seed for a long moment. Then she turned to Zane. “Can you get it?”
Before Zane could answer, movement caught everyone’s eye. One of the Ogre warriors — a decisive one — had stopped deliberating. He walked the narrow path connecting the stone platforms, studied the seed above, studied the platforms below, and made his choice. He didn’t pick a platform. He stepped off the edge directly into open air.
[SFX: POWERFUL WIND RUSH]
The moment he stepped off the edge, a powerful gust of wind ripped through the cave.
[SFX: HEAVY BODY SWEPT UP]
An Ogre with a body like a small elephant was tossed into the air like a leaf, swept up into the gale, and carried high into the cavern.
“The current is pushing upward!” the Ogre yelled, a thrill in his voice.
Panic rippled through the other warriors. There was only one seed. If he got to it first, their chance was gone.
[SFX: WIND REVERSAL]
[SFX: BODY SLAMMING DOWN]
But just as quickly, the wind reversed, slamming him violently downward. Caught in the storm, the Ogre was helpless. One moment, he was soaring; the next, he was plummeting into the depths. Those with sharp eyes could see the look of pure frustration twisting his face as he fell. He did get a little lucky, though. 
As he shot past a stone pillar, he managed to snatch a precious fruit from its side. At least he didn’t walk away empty-handed.
[AMBIENT: TENSE CAVERN ATMOSPHERE]
After watching that disaster, everyone else grew cautious. No one was making a move without thinking it through first. Instead, they all turned their attention to the air, trying to figure out the wind’s patterns.
Now that they understood the mechanics, the calculation was clear. Every stone platform had a wind tunnel beneath it — jump from one, and the wind would carry you somewhere. The stalagmites throughout the cave were covered in rare treasures. Any warrior who jumped would almost certainly land on something valuable. Under normal circumstances, that would have been enough. Everyone could have left satisfied.
But next to the World Tree seed, nothing else in the cave was worth a second glance. Every warrior was studying the platforms, trying to figure out which tunnel led to the top.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Zane leaned against the cavern wall, his eyes fixed on the distant seed. “Is it really that important?” he asked.
[SFX: TELEPATHIC VOICE ECHO]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
“Of course it is,” Lavender’s voice echoed in his mind, her tone leaving no room for argument. “Why else do you think I sent you in here with that brute? The World Tree seed is one of the reasons I came here. You will get it for me.”
“The odds are impossible,” Zane muttered, shaking his head.
His luck was good, sure, but not *that* good. Not anymore. Right after he’d absorbed the Luck-Devouring Snakes during his tribulation, he’d been on an incredible streak—it felt like anything he wanted just fell into his lap. But that feeling had faded. If he’d managed to absorb a few more of those snakes back then, maybe he’d be confident enough to try for it.
“Your chances might be small,” she replied, and he could practically hear the smile in her voice. “But I have a plan.”
