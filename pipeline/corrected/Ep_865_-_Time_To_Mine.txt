Ep 865 - Time To Mine

Word count: 1018

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The Heavenly Thunder Halberd was already in Fredrick’s hand, its runes flickering to life.

Fredrick’s nerves were shot. He knew exactly what Zane was talking about, and the math wasn’t pretty. In the entire union of the seven divine kingdoms, the value of a Heavenly Thunder Halberd was basically infinite. Sure, in the upper world, it might only fetch a few hundred thousand supreme Aether crystals, but down here? No one could afford that. Any semi-divine weapon was priceless.

It had torn him up inside to use one just to save his own skin before. Now, he was about to burn his last one on Zane.

[SFX: ELECTRICAL CRACKLING]

A sharp crackle ran across the surface of the short halberd as a circular red rune lit up, spitting arcs of stray lightning. All he had to do was press it, and the weapon would unleash hell.

His fingers trembled. The sheer pressure rolling off Zane was so immense that Fredrick felt like he might actually pass out. God, he wanted to just give up. A deep, primal fear had taken root in his gut, screaming at him that this was a man he couldn’t afford to cross.

[MUSIC: DARK WHISPER]

But another voice, cold and greedy, whispered in his head. Kill him. Even if you have to use the halberd, it’ll be worth it. Your father will reward you.

[SFX: HEARTBEAT POUNDING]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

His heart hammered against his ribs, his eyes turning bloodshot as his finger finally slammed down on the rune.

[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

A low rumble escalated into a shrill, awful whistle as lightning channeled through the weapon.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

Then, with a deafening boom, a gigantic bolt of energy erupted from the tip of the halberd.

“Go to hell, you bastard!” Fredrick roared, refusing to back down, not to Zane.

[SFX: THUNDER BLAST]

The thunderous blast swallowed his voice, but somehow, Zane heard him loud and clear.

Zane just smiled.

[SFX: LIGHTNING SURGE]

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ENERGY]

The massive river of lightning surged forward and, in a flash, swallowed him whole. A blinding crackle of light forced every other genius on the shore to flinch and look away. The thick bolt of energy tore through everything in its path, continuing far out over the vast Sacred Feather Sea where it made the water glitter with faint golden light. 

What none of them could see was what Zane had done the moment Fredrick’s finger hit the rune. He hadn’t braced himself. He hadn’t tried to absorb the blast with his body. He’d transferred it — redirected every last joule of that lightning force outward, into the Sacred Feather Sea itself. The sea was vast enough to swallow it without so much as a ripple.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC RESOLUTION]

[AMBIENT: QUIET AFTERMATH]

Zane was still standing in the exact same spot. Same expression. Same faint smile. Not a mark on him.

[SFX: STUNNED SILENCE]

The onlookers stared. They’d all known Zane was powerful — they’d watched him fight Fiona and the Little Monster. But this was something else. 

The gap between Zane and everyone else here wasn’t one realm. It was two. 

One genius whispered it aloud before he could stop himself: “Is he even human?”

Zane shook his head slowly and took another step forward. “That was a bad trade,” he said.

Fredrick’s eyes were wild. The necklace hidden beneath his collar was burning hot against his skin, the gem inside it straining under the pressure. “So what!” he snapped, his voice cracking. “My father will kill you! You can’t beat my father!”

“I don’t deny it,” Zane said, and took another step.

“Then kneel! Beg me to ask him to spare you! Hahahaha—” Fredrick was shouting now, his composure completely gone.

Zane looked at him. “You’re not qualified,” he said quietly, and took one more step.

[SFX: SOFT CRACK]

A faint sound came from Fredrick’s chest. The gem at the center of his necklace — the one that had been blocking sixty percent of Zane’s soul pressure this entire time — split apart. Hairline fractures spread across its surface in an instant, and then it shattered, fragments scattering inside his collar.

Without it, Fredrick’s soul had nothing between it and the full weight of Zane’s pressure.

[SFX: BODY HITTING GROUND]

When the light faded, Fredrick was unconscious on the ground, the now-inert halberd lying beside him.

“Looks like he fainted,” Zane said with a dismissive shrug. “We can wait for him to wake up.”

Fiona let out a soft, weary sigh. “Don’t bother. It’s only nine thousand Aether crystals.” It was a fortune, and she was still furious that Fredrick had scammed her brother, but it wasn’t worth this. In the grand scheme of things, the money was nothing. 

Fredrick had just paid a much, much heavier price anyway—that Heavenly Thunder Halberd was worth more than he’d ever see in his life.

Zane had no intention of killing Fredrick. He understood his own limits. Once a Soul Sea master activated their domain and cut him off from the world’s will, he couldn’t win. Pushing War Emperor York that far would be a mistake he couldn’t walk back from.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

A new worry began to creep into Fiona’s mind. Zane had already pushed War Emperor York far enough. If the War Emperor showed up here, in her family’s own territory, would he come after Zane? If it came to that, she decided, she would step in. Her father would back her. That was a promise.

“Time to get back to mining,” Zane said, shaking off the entire incident.

He still had work to do. After absorbing all that Celestial Essence and igniting over ten thousand Dragon Scales, his plan was simple: smash the golden leaf to pieces, get the Black Star pickax, and then head to the top of the giant tree. 

At least, that was the best-case scenario. 

More likely, he wouldn’t be able to break the leaf, which meant he wouldn’t be getting his hands on a divine-class weapon anytime soon.
