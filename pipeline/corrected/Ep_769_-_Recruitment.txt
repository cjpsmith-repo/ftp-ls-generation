[AMBIENT: TENSE MEETING HALL]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

All eyes were on Zane.

Kieran had just voiced the question on everyone’s mind: Zane had promised them powerful warriors from the Soul Sea Realm, but so far, no one had shown up. Where were they?

Sure, the Heartlands had its share of recluses who kept out of the public eye—even the World Merchants' Guild didn't know much about some of them. There were rumors of a couple of Life-and-Death Realm masters hiding out somewhere, but nobody knew for sure. The one thing everyone did know, however, was that there were no Soul Sea Realm warriors among them. So where exactly was Zane planning to find these allies?

"I'll have your answer in seven days, Kieran," Zane said, his voice steady as he clasped his hands. "I just ask for your patience."

Kieran nodded. He trusted Zane more than anyone, and besides, Zane had no reason to lie. It wouldn't do him any good, not when it would only make things worse with the masters of the York Divine Kingdom already after him.

The others weren’t so sure, but they were willing to wait. They’d find out soon enough if Zane was bluffing.

Hanson, who had followed Zane here hoping for a reward, felt his head start to spin at the mention of Soul Sea Realm warriors. This meeting hall was filled with fighters from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm; he was just an Adept, completely out of his depth. Any one of them could have crushed him without a thought, so he kept his mouth shut.

Finally, seeing a pause in the conversation, he managed to mutter, "So, about my reward…"

Kieran shot him a cold look before ordering someone to take him to collect the supreme Aether crystals.

Hanson didn't have the nerve to ask Zane about joining Cloud Hall right then and there, but he was smart enough to connect the dots. Zane had already given his word, and that was good enough for him. He figured he’d collect his thousand supreme Aether crystals and then head straight for Cloud Hall. With Zane’s promise as his ticket in, how could they possibly turn him away?

[AMBIENT: HEAVENLY PALACE INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STRINGS]

 Back at the Heavenly Stellar Palace, Zane summoned Lavender. She’d been hidden inside his body the entire time, so she already knew what he needed.

Before they’d left the main continent, a woman named Amber had linked a special Message Rune to Lavender’s statue. Lavender’s own soul was too weak to communicate with the Demon Night’s sacred land the way the other queens could, but this rune would bridge the gap.

[SFX: MAGICAL RUNE IGNITION]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CHOIR]

 As Zane ignited it, Lavender’s eyes fluttered shut, her lashes trembling as she sank into a deep trance.

[AMBIENT: SACRED SANCTUARY]

[MUSIC: CEREMONIAL CHANTING]

Tens of thousands of miles away, across the endless sea, the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary stood on the main continent. 

Amber led the Demon Night through their daily ritual — kneeling before the three statues of their supreme queens, faces full of devotion. 

Then, on the third statue — the one that governs slaughter — a Message Rune ignited. Lavender's voice rang out, low and calm.

Every Demon Night warrior in the hall froze. Then joy broke across their faces all at once.

Amber dropped to one knee, her voice trembling with excitement. "My Queen!" 

"I need warriors," Lavender said.

Amber didn't hesitate for even a breath. "Yes, My Queen. I will come today."

But Lavender's reply was cool. "If you leave, what becomes of the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary? And the Violet Heart Sanctuary?" The Ogres' Sacred Land had been quiet — but only because Amber was there. The moment she left, those old monsters would move. "Stay. Send me a few Soul Sea Realm warriors from each sanctuary. That is enough."

Disappointment flickered across Amber's face, but she bowed her head. She would not disobey.

When the connection closed, Amber rose and turned to face the assembled Demon Night. "Who is willing to go to the Heartlands and serve the Queen?"

Every Soul Sea Realm warrior stepped forward. The Heavenly Feather Sanctuary alone had seven, and Amber was one of the strongest. The Violet Heart Sanctuary had three. Beyond them, Life-and-Death Realm warriors pressed forward as well, unwilling to be left behind. Amber looked them over and made her selections.

The Ogres' Sacred Land received word of the movement within the day. But with Amber still at the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, they didn't dare act. They held their position and watched.

[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]

[AMBIENT: VIOLENT WINDSTORM]

 Just then, a massive voidstorm tore open the sky above The Heartlands, a swirling tempest with a teleportation array blazing at its heart. The ethereal winds shrieked, powerful enough to crush an ordinary martial artist to dust.

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC STORM]

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE DRUMS]

 But from within the chaos, a squad of over a hundred warriors charged out of the array, completely unfazed by the storm. The group was led by nine masters from the Soul Sea Realm, with the rest all powerful War Sages.

[AMBIENT: COASTAL WINDS]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

 "So this is The Heartlands? Zane's home?" Dorian murmured, gazing down at the distant coastline with a swirl of emotions. Before Zane’s arrival on the main continent, the humans of the Violet Heart Sanctuary had been like orphans, clinging to a tiny corner of the land just to survive. He never imagined the world Zane came from would be ruled entirely by humans.

"Let's go," one of the Soul Sea Realm masters from the Demon Night said curtly, her eyes sweeping the area. She didn't share Dorian’s sentiment; the only thing driving her and her kin was their duty to their queen.

With that, the group of over a hundred martial artists surged forward as one.

[AMBIENT: SECT COURTYARD]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]

A scream ripped through the air near the Sea Wyrm Sect, on the outer edge of The Heartlands. "Sir, something terrible has happened!"

Elder Marsh, the leader of the Sea Wyrm Sect, scowled at the disciple who had screamed.  "What is all this panic about? A warrior’s spirit requires calm above all else. Panicking won't get you anywhere!"

 The elder fumed, though a part of him felt smug. The Corrupted Divine Kingdom had torn through half of The Heartlands, wiping out countless Third-Grade Orders, but his Sea Wyrm Sect had survived practically untouched—a testament, he believed, to his own cunning.

Just outside the sect gates, the ground trembled beneath the footsteps of over a hundred warriors, radiating a pressure that made the air feel like lead.
