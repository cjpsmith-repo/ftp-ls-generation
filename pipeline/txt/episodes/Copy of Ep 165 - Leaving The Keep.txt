Ep 165 - Leaving The Keep
Word Count: 1182

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT REVELATION]
 A slow grin spread across Zane's face.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SWIRLING]
 As the Corrupt Vitality coursed through him, he stretched out his hand and willed a stream of it into his palm. It had definitely changed. The energy was still a deep, dark purple, but now it swirled like a dense, violet fog.
"I've made a breakthrough," he whispered, a hint of awe in his voice.
After being stuck just shy of the Adept Stage for so long, he’d finally crossed the threshold.
The Corrupt Vitality thrumming in his veins was refining itself, becoming true Vitality. The thick, dark mist in his hand was the proof.
Now that he’d reached the Adept Stage, his strength had leaped to an entirely new level. More importantly, he had shattered the barrier holding him back. From now on, his training would be much, much faster.
"Congratulations. You are now an Adept," Saul said with a faint smile.
In truth, from Saul’s perspective, an Adept was nothing special. Even a master at the Soul Realm was less than an insect to him. But Saul was merely a servant in The Elysian Keep, and Zane was its temporary master. As long as Zane kept proving himself, he was destined to become the keep’s true master. So, for now, respect was in order.
Zane acknowledged Saul with a slight nod, his lips curving into a satisfied smile. Attaining the Adept stage represented not merely advancement in power, but a crucial stepping stone toward his ultimate ambition.
He closed his eyes and surrendered himself to the sensation of newfound strength coursing through his veins. The dense, violet fog-like Vitality settled into his core, resonating with each heartbeat. As he breathed deeply, Zane savored this moment of transformation—the culmination of countless hours of relentless training and unwavering determination. 
His thoughts drifted briefly to his father, wondering if he would have been proud of this achievement. The familiar ache of loss mingled with his triumph, tempering his elation with purpose. This power wasn't merely for himself; it was another weapon in his arsenal for the justice he still sought.
[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL MEDITATION]
[MUSIC: TRANSCENDENT AWAKENING]
 Three days later, Zane opened his eyes again. As an Adept, he could feel the change in every fiber of his being. His breathing was deeper, more powerful; he could go five minutes between breaths without any effort.
[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCH]
 His power now stable, Zane jumped to his feet and threw a punch into the air—the first move of the Titan Strike, Ogres Shaking the World.
[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]
[AMBIENT: FAINT STARLIGHT SHIMMER]
 A shadowy fist, more solid and faster than ever, shot through the air. Since Zane had absorbed power from the Tapestry of Sun, Moon, and Stars, the spectral energy glittered with faint starlight.
Zane smirked, looking at the lingering traces of the shadow. It was a shame he didn't have an opponent to test the true power of his Titan Strike on. He glanced at Saul for a split second before shaking his head. He knew Saul’s strength was practically infinite. Testing his new abilities on him would be pointless; he probably wouldn't even register the attack.
Zane had plenty to celebrate. He'd become the temporary master of The Elysian Keep and even gotten his hands on the best Contemplation Picture. 
But his good mood soured as he remembered he couldn't touch anything else in the massive keep. If he could just get into The Alchemist's Laboratory and find a single elixir, his strength would skyrocket.
“Everything happens for a reason”, he thought. The Master of the Elysian Keep must have had his reasons for setting things up this way. Zane decided not to push it.
Saul had made it clear: he was confined to this single room. With those limitations, hanging around was just a waste of time.
Before Zane could move, Saul reached into his robes and produced a small medallion, heavy for its size, its face engraved with dense patterns. "For when you want to return," he said. "Show it at the entrance."
Zane took it and turned to go. Something made him pause — the look on Saul's face. For all his power, the ancient guardian looked unutterably alone.
"I'll be back," Zane said. "And when I come back, it won't be as temporary master."
Saul's expression didn't change much. But something shifted behind his eyes. "Seven others said something like that," he said quietly. "None of them ever came back. You're the first person — ever — to claim that fifth picture. Not even the second or third masters of this place managed it." He paused. "Do your best."
[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]
 Zane gave Saul a respectful nod. "I've been in The Elysian Keep for a while now. If there's nothing else, I should be going. Goodbye."
No sooner had Zane left than he was hit with a heavy dose of reality.
[SFX: SUDDEN CONFRONTATION]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
 "What happened to the elders from my family? Where are they?" the man demanded, blocking his path.
[AMBIENT: THREATENING ATMOSPHERE]
 Zane's expression darkened. "Like I said, I don't know you. And I'm in no mood to answer your questions. Get out of my way if you want to live."
The sheer power Zane had displayed earlier had already put them on edge. He had killed Yves and taken Leon's life, after all. And while some of these Adepts were stronger than Leon, the gap wasn't wide enough to make them feel safe. For now, they were all terrified of Zane.
Zane's threat was so potent that the Adepts instinctively took a step back. But then one of them shouted, "Everyone, together! He has to know what happened in The Elysian Keep! Don't let him get away!"
[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD]
There’s courage in numbers. Bolstered by the shout, the other men found their nerve and squared up to Zane. They were scared of him, sure, but they didn't believe he could take on so many Adepts all by himself.
Seeing them surround him, Zane’s eyes narrowed, and a powerful killing intent rolled off him in waves. He didn't want to kill anyone else, but these people from the Great Houses were insufferable.
[SFX: ENERGY SUMMONING]
"If you want to die, then come closer. I'll be happy to grant your wish. You'll all end up just like the masters from the Soul Realm inside the keep!" Zane summoned his dark purple Corrupt Vitality, which swirled around him as he prepared for a fight.
"What? All of them died?"
"What about my uncle?"
"No way! They're masters of the Soul Realm. They can't die that easily! You're lying!"
Zane's words implied that every single master of the Soul Realm was dead. The message was clear, but no one could bring themselves to believe it.
They all knew The Elysian Keep was a dangerous place, where even masters of the Soul Realm were as fragile as insects and could die at any moment.
But they refused to believe it. The truth was simply too much to accept.
And Zane was the perfect target for their frustrated rage.
