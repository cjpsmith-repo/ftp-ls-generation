Ep 58 - Stay Calm

[SFX: CRACKLING LIGHTNING]
The lightning crackling around Tristan Hayes faded, his life force draining away with it.
The effect on his body was immediate and startling. His skin withered, and his hair bleached to a dull gray. In a matter of moments,
Tristan, a man in his twenties, had aged into someone who looked to be in his forties.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC VICTORY]
The Divine Thunder was a devastating power, but its raw energy had just recoiled on its wielder. Even Zane was taken aback by what had become of Tristan.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED CROWD MURMURS]
Around the battle ring, the crowd stared at Zane, a mix of fear and awe on their faces.
This newcomer appeared almost supernatural in his abilities. In less than a month at Misty Peak, Zane had toppled Tristan Hayes—the thirtieth-ranked pledge whose reputation had once seemed unassailable. The crowd stood transfixed, their expressions a mixture of disbelief and reluctant admiration.
They had just witnessed an extraordinary battle, culminating in a defeat that shattered all expectations. Several disciples exchanged wide-eyed glances, struggling to process what their eyes had clearly seen but their minds refused to accept.
What struck the onlookers as particularly astounding was Zane's accomplishment while remaining at merely Core Strength Level. A murmur of speculation rippled through the gathered disciples like wind through tall grass. The unspoken question hung in the air, electric with possibility: if he achieved Marrow Refining Level, or perhaps even Near-High status, might he someday challenge masters at the Nature Level?
The very thought sent shivers down their spines.
His raw power transcended conventional understanding, defying the established hierarchy that had governed their world. Some watched with envy burning in their eyes, while others felt a strange hope kindling—perhaps the rigid structures they had always accepted were not as immutable as they had believed.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS POWER]
No one said a word. The silence was thick with awe. Whether he could one day challenge a master was up for debate, but one thing was for sure: Zane was in a league of his own among disciples at his level. That much was undeniable.
Seeing Tristan's humiliating defeat and the state he was in, Julian Croft's face twisted with fury. He felt he had to do something. He shot his right hand out, pointing, and roared at Ethan Grant in a sharp, commanding voice, "Return the Indigo Blade!"
Zane ignored him, stepping in front of Miles Sutton and blocking his path.
"This was your idea, wasn't it?" Zane asked, his voice calm as he closed the distance between them. He stopped right in front of Miles, his gaze ice-cold. "Putting Tristan up to challenging me."
Miles chuckled. "So what if it was? What are you gonna do about it? I'm with House Sterling. Who here can afford to mess with them? My master wants you dead, so you're going to die. I was just trying to speed things along. I suggest you start writing your will."
"House Sterling?" Zane asked, his tone dangerously level, ignoring the outburst. "And who exactly are you to them?"
"Me?" The fat on Miles's face jiggled as his sneer widened. "I don't mind telling you. I'm a trusted servant of the third son of House Sterling."
"The third son of House Sterling? He's involved in this?"
[AMBIENT: CROWD WHISPERS]
A whisper broke out in the crowd. "Zane pissed off someone from House Sterling? The third son is a total psycho!"
Murmurs quickly spread. It seemed plenty of people had heard of him. The third son was Felix Sterling, better known by a much less flattering nickname: "Felix the Freak."
The name fit his cruel, unpredictable nature and explosive temper. Everyone called him Felix the Freak for a reason.
[MUSIC: DARK INTENSITY]
"Oh?" A flicker of something new appeared in Zane's eyes, and his expression hardened. "So, you're Felix's man," he remarked, his voice dripping with condescension. "And a bold one at that, stirring up trouble between disciples on our own Peak."
Like any other peak, Misty Peak had its internal rivalries, but those were House matters. An outsider meddling in their business was another thing entirely. The thirty-two peaks of the Skyreach Sect all operated the same way: you could fight amongst yourselves, but when an outsider got involved, you stood together against the common enemy. It was an unspoken rule.
Miles pushing Tristan Hayes to attack Zane was a major taboo, a serious violation. Zane's accusation immediately drew the hostility of every Misty Peak disciple present.
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMURS]
Suddenly, every eye was on Miles. Faces in the crowd hardened, turning on him as one.
Even if Misty Peak was ranked last, they had their pride. Felix Sterling might be powerful, and House Sterling was certainly a giant, but did that mean their members could be bullied?
What made it even worse was that this troublemaker wasn't even a noble, just a servant.
Zane stared hard at an Outer Disciple who had stepped forward to intervene. The guy was clearly trying to give Zane "valuable advice" for his own good, but Zane saw right through it—he was just pressuring him to bow down to House Sterling. He was another suck-up, trying to curry favor with a Legacy House, and Zane hated that.
Before the disciple could interject, Miles Sutton, still pinned beneath Zane's intimidating presence, shrieked in desperation, "Liam, enough with these pointless games! Help me eliminate him! Slaughter Zane, and my master will shower you with rewards beyond imagination!"
Liam, who held the prestigious fifteenth rank among Misty Peak pledges, assessed the situation with calculating eyes. Though merely an High-tier candidate, his abilities far surpassed Tristan's. His humble origins had always barred him from the inner circles of Great House scions, yet he'd meticulously cultivated connections with several minor Sterling branches over the years.
[AMBIENT: SILENT TENSION]
Stepping forward now for Miles represented more than a simple intervention—it was a calculated gambit for advancement. If he navigated this confrontation skillfully, Liam could secure direct access to Felix himself, a prize worth any risk. His fingers twitched subtly toward the hilt of his blade as the crowd fell silent, sensing the shift in atmosphere.
"Perhaps," Liam said, taking another step forward, his voice carrying across the suddenly hushed hall, "it's time someone taught our new celebrity the true meaning of Misty Peak's hierarchy."

[MUSIC: OMINOUS BATTLE THEME]
