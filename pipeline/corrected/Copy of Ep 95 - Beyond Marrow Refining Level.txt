Ep 95 - Beyond Marrow Refining Level
Word Count: 1086
[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS]
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
Zane shifted into the stance pictured in the Titan Strike manual.
Since this was an upgrade path designed for ogres, it would take any human an insane amount of time and effort to master, even with dedicated practice. Luckily, Zane's physical strength was already well beyond average. For anyone else, even completing the first step would be impossible.
As the energy coursed through him, Zane's brow furrowed. He couldn't feel a trace of Corrupt Vitality inside him. "Why?"
Just yesterday, he'd managed to produce a small amount of it after practicing the upgrade. Afterward, that Corrupt Vitality had devoured his Violet Pulse Energy like a starving beast until every last drop had been converted.
But the moment he executed the first move of the Titan Strike—Earthshatter—every bit of Corrupt Vitality in his body was instantly depleted.
Usually, once you cracked the code to an upgrade path, your advancement was smooth sailing for a while. Breaking through one level was like opening a door to the next. It was almost unheard of for someone to actually regress like this.
He could create and use Corrupt Vitality yesterday, but not today. Had he forgotten a step?
After thinking it over, Zane still couldn't figure it out. As a Tier 5 Power Technique, Titan Strike's power should be incredible, but this upgrade path was far more difficult than he'd ever expected.
[MUSIC: INTENSE CONCENTRATION]
What was he doing differently? Zane sank deep into thought, his mind drifting back to the fight on Infernal Peak. He remembered that desperate moment against Adrian, knowing he was losing but still trying to use Titan Strike, clinging to that sliver of hope for survival.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
That's when it hit him. He'd been activating the Psionic Strike first. The more Violet Pulse Energy flooded his system, the more Corrupt Vitality it consumed, allowing it to finally build up inside him.
[SFX: ENERGY SWIRLING]
About two hours later, his gut was finally full of Corrupt Vitality. When Zane stretched out his arms, the energy spiraled up and swirled around them. To him, it looked like a tiny Void Demon fluttering around his hands. Seeing this, Zane flicked a finger at the creature.
[SFX: CREATURE LEAPING]
Instantly, the little Void Demon leaped from his palm and shot into a corner of the room, where it continued to hop around after hitting the ground.
"Whoa! The Life Energy can actually leave my body!" Zane was shocked. Even while practicing this upgrade path, he'd never heard of anything like this.
There were five levels of physical form, and at his level, Life Energy was only supposed to exist inside the body or on its surface. The energy should have dissipated the moment it left his body, like a plant torn from its roots.
Only refiners who'd reached the level of an Adept-tier candidate could separate Life Energy from their bodies.
Zane remembered seeing his uncle Nolan pour his Life Energy into his longsword. He could then manipulate that Life Energy from a distance, killing his enemies with the sword. That was a feat only possible for cultivators who were Adept-tier candidates.
Zane, however, was still at the Marrow Refining Level. He could pour Life Energy into a weapon, sure, but the energy would just vanish the moment the weapon left his hand. But now, this purple-black Primal Vitality hadn't disappeared after leaving his body.
"Have I... reached a level higher than Marrow Refining?" Zane wondered aloud.
[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE TENSION]
Before entering Infernal Peak, he had figured he was at the peak of the Marrow Refining Level. Yesterday, during the fight with Adrian, his body had been pummeled by those Starlight Lilies. The pressure from their attacks had been almost unbearable. He'd been fighting for his life, so he hadn't exactly been paying attention to the subtle changes in his body.
But looking back, Zane remembered a massive surge of Vitality Current flooding his system, refreshing his body just like before. Could the toxins have been completely cleared from his marrow in that moment?
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT REVELATION]
Since he'd been so focused on defending against Adrian, he hadn't noticed that all the toxins in his marrow had been refined and purified. Now, as a rush of warmth spread through him, Zane realized it was very likely that every last toxin had been removed.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
With this thought, Zane carefully examined his body. A slow smile spread across his face as he confirmed his theory. In the heat of battle, his marrow had been completely purified. He'd leveled up to an Adept-tier candidate.
The five levels of Physical Enhancement were the foundation. Only when a person's body was free of all toxins could they become a true creature of nature. Still, becoming an Adept-tier candidate wasn't the same as reaching the Nature Level; that came down to the difference between Life Energy and Vitality. A person couldn't become a creature of nature until their Life Energy was fully converted into Vitality.
Even though he hadn't succeeded in freeing Elara from Infernal Peak, Zane had unexpectedly broken through to a new level.

Someone in the Soul Realm could kill a Marrow Refining Level refiner just by burning their Vitality—the sheer power difference is overwhelming. The gap between them is like an ant trying to fight an elephant."
"You can't say that for sure! Zane's the exception to every rule. He beat a nature creature when he was only at the Marrow Refining Level." Apparently, Ethan, ever the optimist, was a firm believer in the underdog.
But Rhys, having been at Skyreach Sect longer, understood just how vast the gaps between realms truly were. He continued, "Ethan, I don't think you really get what the Soul Realm means. The Empire has a massive territory and tons of resources, right? Its population is in the hundreds of millions, but how many of them actually reach the Soul Realm? The gap between the Soul Realm and the Nature Level is way bigger than you think. I'm just saying, you can't take this rumor seriously."
"Unlike you, I actually believe in miracles."
"You mean an ant eating an elephant?"
