Ep 1042 - Faster Every Day

Word count: 1084

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATION]

Looking at Yolande, Zane had the distinct feeling he’d traded one impossible situation for another. She was one of the few people he could actually trust — which somehow made it worse.

She clearly assumed he had a bottomless supply of Roaring Tokens, and he couldn’t exactly explain how he’d gotten his hands on even one. The truth was tangled up with The Elysian Keep, his Master—a whole mess of secrets he had no intention of dragging her into. So, he kept his mouth shut.

Yolande seemed to understand he wouldn’t tell her, and she didn’t press. Since he refused to explain, she wasn’t about to force the secret out of him. For her, it was enough that he didn’t deny it.

[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]

Instead, she changed the subject. “Do you have any idea who that woman was? The singer from the other day?”

Zane, lost in thought, just shook his head.

He’d been wondering the same thing. Just like him, the mysterious singer seemed to have a never-ending supply of Roaring Tokens. It was a safe bet that hers, like his, could be used over and over again. But as for who she was, he was completely in the dark.

“Alright.” She nodded, accepting his answer, though her gaze lingered, sizing him up.

This was the first time she’d met him in her true form, not as a clone. It was strange to think that one of her other selves had decided to let Zane ascend on his own terms, a decision the Demi-Immortal Race had honored. She’d been glad they hadn’t forced his hand. And yet, by some twist of fate, he’d wound up in The Alliance anyway. It really did seem like his destiny.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

A Man Without Destiny had no set path, but wherever Zane’s led, she figured it wouldn’t hurt to lend a hand.

Helping him was a smart play, especially with that token of his. An infinitely reusable Roaring Token was an unbelievable asset, something countless races would kill for. She might need his help more than once, and since her own supply was limited, he could be the one to spread the word for her.

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

As Yolande’s mind raced, her gaze settled on him, and a familiar, unnerving feeling washed over Zane.

He knew she didn’t have some mind-reading power; it was simply the crushing weight of the gap between them. The difference in their power was so massive that it was almost a physical presence. He’d felt this same pressure once before, under the scrutinizing gaze of the Saint Lord of the Soulbound Sect.

There was still no response to Elara’s message. Maybe it was just as Lavender had warned him — the young man who had appeared at the Ethereal Spirit Sect was an existence fate itself couldn’t touch. If that was true, Elara was already beyond its reach. Zane was unwilling to accept that. But the possibility was undeniably high. Finding her would require strength he didn’t yet have.

He turned to Yolande. “Is there any way to awaken Lavender?”

Yolande paused. Her million clones had seen too many people and things — pinpointing one was no small task. But Zane was an exception. She found Lavender through his memories without much difficulty.

“If I remember correctly,” she said, “only a strand of her soul remains. Her disappearance isn’t a secret. If she falls completely, a new Queen of Slaughter will eventually be born to take her place.”

Zane nodded and said nothing.

“You want to help her reclaim the throne?” Yolande asked.

He nodded again.

She studied him. “Your current strength is nowhere near enough.”

“I will improve my cultivation,” Zane said flatly.

Yolande laughed at his expression. “I’m not your master. I won’t tell you what to think. Once we reach the Mist Palace, I’ll awaken Lavender — and repair her shattered soul.”

Zane’s eyes lit up. He’d hoped for awakening. He hadn’t expected repair.

Yolande smiled faintly. “Let’s go.”

[SFX: HORSES NEIGHING]

Outside, the Heavenly Phoenix Horses called out.

[SFX: SPATIAL WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: DIMENSIONAL TUNNEL]

The carriage shuddered — and launched into a spatial tunnel that hadn’t been there a moment before.

Zane blinked. “How—”

“Their innate ability,” Yolande said. “They can enter spatial tunnels freely.”

Zane’s power level was just too low. Back in the Lower World, he was a top-tier warrior. Here, in the Upper World, reaching the Life-and-Death Realm was just getting started.

[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]

[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS ORCHESTRAL]

On the first day, Zane had watched the scenery drift by and quietly tested himself — at full strength, he could almost keep pace with them. Almost. By the second day, they were twice as fast. By the third, twice that again. Each day they doubled, without apparent limit.

[SFX: INTENSE WHOOSHING]

But by the fourth day, that was impossible.

The coursers were moving so fast that the world outside dissolved into a blur of streaking lines, making it impossible to distinguish a person from a planet.

The supreme world channel was supposedly dotted with checkpoints, each demanding a steep toll—a cost that would bankrupt an ordinary warrior trying to cross a single supreme world. But no one dared flag down Yolande’s carriage. Even if they had the nerve, they didn’t have the speed.

On the sixth day, Yolande’s voice cut through the hum of their passage. “We’ve left The Psychean Realm.”

We just now left? Zane thought, stunned. At this speed, he’d assumed they’d crossed into another supreme world days ago.

He did a quick, staggering calculation. At their current pace, traveling a thousand supreme worlds would take nearly twenty years.

It put something into perspective. To most warriors, crossing worlds was an impossible luxury — ninety-nine percent would never leave their own world in their entire lives. The hundred thousand worlds were a concept, a legend. Everyone had heard the names of Empyreans and World Lords. Almost no one had ever seen one.

And somehow, the Heavenly Phoenix Horses just kept getting faster.

[AMBIENT: MASSIVE INTER-REALM CHANNEL]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

Leaving the Psychean Realm behind, they merged into an even wider passage: the inter-realm channel. The sheer scale of it was mind-boggling. To build these channels, to thread thousands of supreme worlds together like pearls on a string… it was a feat of engineering beyond imagination.

World Lords build the supreme world channels, he reasoned. So something like this… it has to be the work of Supreme Lords.
