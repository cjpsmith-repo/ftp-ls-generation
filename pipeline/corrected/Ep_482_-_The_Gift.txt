Ep 482 - The Gift

Word count: 1100

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane stared at his sister, horror flooding through him as he took in the transformation that had overtaken her.

The woman standing beside Elara mirrored her exactly: a puppet with closed eyes and a face wiped clean of all expression, all humanity. Both stood motionless, their features slack, as if the very essence of who they were had been stripped away.

What the hell did Maddox do to Elara? Zane's mind screamed, each thought a knife twisting deeper. How did she end up like this? His hands trembled at his sides, but he forced them still. Every instinct demanded he rush forward, shake her awake, break whatever dark spell held her captive. Instead, he fought to keep his expression neutral, to maintain the mask of composure even as his heart shattered.

The hard truth settled over him like a shroud. Maddox was the strongest warrior in The Heartlands—a force of nature Zane couldn't hope to challenge. Not yet. Perhaps not for years. If he lost his cool now, if he let rage override reason, not even Tia could save him. And if he fell here, Elara would remain trapped in this nightmare forever.

"Huh? Is that the Six Senses Blocking Method?" the Cobalt Dragon said, his voice cutting through Zane's spiraling thoughts. He might have been the weakest of the nine True Dragons, but he remained the most knowledgeable—a repository of ancient wisdom that spanned millennia.

"What's that?" Zane asked through gritted teeth, unable to tear his gaze from Elara's blank face.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]

Keeping his eyes locked on Elara and the woman, the Cobalt Dragon explained, “It’s an cultivation method. It completely shuts down all six of the user’s senses—sight, sound, smell, taste, and touch. It even robs them of their sense of space, cutting them off from the outside world entirely. The Six Senses Blocking Method is supposed to be for personal training. It’s a damn shame someone like Maddox got his hands on it.”

The dragon clearly knew more secrets Zane couldn't begin to guess at, but he didn't care. “Will it hurt them?” he cut in, his voice tight.

“Not directly,” the Cobalt Dragon replied. “It can actually give their power a huge boost in a short amount of time. Don’t forget, Maddox’s been trying to set up that Soul Harvesting Ritual, right?”

[SFX: DRAMATIC REALIZATION]

[MUSIC: TENSE CRESCENDO]

“I know. He’s using this to boost Elara’s power so she can reach the Inner-Core Realm… Wait.” Zane’s eyes shot up as a horrifying thought clicked into place. “Marilyn and Elara both have a Violet-Core Physique. That has to mean something.”

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

His gaze darted to the woman next to Elara. Just like his sister, her senses had been sealed away.

“Exactly,” the Cobalt Dragon said calmly. “She has a Violet-Core Physique, too. Maddox has found the three people he needs.As soon as your sister and that woman reach the Inner-Core Realm, the Soul Harvesting Ritual will be complete.”

“Kinda weird, though,” the seventh brother cut in with a giggle. “The Heartlands is a pretty small place. Finding three people with a Violet-Core Physique here seems like a crazy coincidence. You know, that physique is super adaptable. Makes for an amazing body refiner.”

Zane barely heard him, his own anxious thoughts drowning everything out.

Sir Kenneth had told him the Violet-Core Physique was incredibly rare—maybe one in a trillion.

Maddox needed only for Elara and the other girl to reach the Inner-Core Realm to complete his Soul Harvesting Ritual.

Zane stared at Marilyn as veins twitched violently across his fists.

Though he'd marked her for death, killing her in the arena would prove nearly impossible.

[SFX: BRIGHT LIGHT PIERCING]

[MUSIC: GRAND ENTRANCE]

Suddenly, three bright lights pierced the sky, breaking through the clouds.

Three chairs emerged from the lights, bearing the World Merchants' Guild's Alliance Chiefs. The three fat men wore expressions completely different from when Zane had arrived at the Dragon Soar Arena.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

"Today, the World Merchants' Guild is honored by your presence," Kieran, the charmingly naive fat man, announced. "We've prepared a grand gift—every warrior present will receive a share!"

[SFX: CHAINS RATTLING]

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

Hundreds of shackled warriors were driven onto a platform—Adept to Soul Realm cultivators with weakened auras.

"These prisoners committed unspeakable crimes," Kieran continued. "Today, we'll use their lifespan as our gift to you!"[SFX: MASSIVE DRUM MATERIALIZING]

A massive drum materialized above the prisoners' heads.

"It's the Vital Energy Drum!" someone shouted.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]

Someone in the crowd muttered in disbelief, and the sound rippled through the spectators.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION BUILD]

While the crowd murmured, Kieran stood atop the massive drum, hoisting the twenty-foot-long drumstick. It weighed fifty thousand pounds, but he lifted it like a twig, swinging it in one smooth arc.

[SFX: MASSIVE DRUM STRIKE]

[SFX: DEEP DRUM THUD]

[AMBIENT: ENERGY VORTEX]

The stick slammed into the drum with a deep, gut-wrenching thud. The moment it hit, an invisible wave shot out, swirling around the cultivators below and forming a vortex of energy.

[SFX: WITHERING BODIES]

Instantly, the cultivators’ bodies began to wither as the drum drained their blood and life force.

[SFX: GOLDEN LIGHT BURST]

Then, a ray of golden light burst from above the drum, fanning out in all directions.

The disciples from Cloud Hall, the Ethereal Spirit Sect, and the other major sects were closest to the drum. They greedily soaked up most of the golden light before it could reach anyone else.

The light then washed over the cultivators who had passed the pre-qualifying test, including Zane and Owen.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHOIR]

“This golden light… it can extend our lifespan?” someone in the crowd gasped. As the light hit Zane, he drank in a massive portion, a jolt of pure energy surging through him, leaving him feeling refreshed and powerful.

With so many people there, the light had grown thin by the time it reached the spectator stands. Still, no one wanted to miss the rare opportunity. Hundreds of thousands of spectators inhaled greedily, but the light vanished in a flash. The amount each of them received was a tiny fraction of what Zane had absorbed—maybe one-hundred- millionth of it.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]

Once the golden light faded, all eyes snapped back to the drum. The death row prisoners still stood in place, but now they were just shriveled husks. Their skin was a ghostly white, their empty forms looking like a row of standing zombies.

What had just happened to these wretched souls?
