Ep 685 - Fight Back

Word count: 950

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The moment Zane’s eyes opened, the expressions on the three Inner-Core Realm warriors soured.

Elder Holt roared. “Quick! Kill him!”

They lived by a simple code: if you make an enemy, you eliminate them. Showing mercy was just an invitation for a knife in the back later. They didn't hesitate for a second.

But as they lunged, ready to unleash a storm of attacks, Zane just stood there, perfectly still. He casually tapped the Storage Ring on his chest.

[SFX: MAGICAL FLASH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

In a flash of light, a long spear materialized in the air before him.

A tall, slender woman with lavender hair appeared alongside it, offering the three warriors a devastatingly captivating smile. She reached out, her fingers wrapping around the spear’s shaft.

[SFX: WHOOSHING SPEAR]

With a flick of her wrist, she sent the weapon whistling forward.

[SFX: SHATTERED METAL]

The men's low-grade Mythic weapons shattered like glass on impact.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

The instant he saw the mid-grade Relic-Class spear, a cold dread washed over Elder Holt. Even a low-grade relic was a treasure of almost mythical value in The Heartlands; there were only a handful in the entire region. Yet this woman held a mid-grade one like it was nothing.

And who the hell was she? A beautiful woman who’d appeared from thin air? A sword spirit given physical form? The idea was too much for them to process. All they knew for sure was that they were hopelessly outmatched.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Regret flooded their minds, but it was a luxury they couldn’t afford. They’d made a play for Zane’s treasures, and the price of failure was death. If they were going to die, they’d at least die running.

Having fought side-by-side for years, they had an unspoken understanding of situations like this. In perfect sync, they scattered, retreating in three different directions.

But Lavender gave them no chance to escape.

[SFX: ENERGY WAVE]

The spear in her hand trembled, sending out invisible waves of energy. An Inner-Core warrior from a place like this was no stronger than a mid-level War General on the main continent. These men, hailing from a minor sect like the Sea Wyrm Sect, were nothing compared to true prodigies like Olivia and Cheryl.

Three blossoms of blood bloomed on their backs before they could even turn to fight. Like kites with their strings cut, they plunged silently into the sea, their blood staining the water a deep, spreading red.

Lavender turned back to Zane. "Done."

Something about being back in the Heartlands had loosened something in her. She couldn't quite name it — a warmth, a quiet relief. As queen of the Demon Night, she had always been the one giving orders, never the one following them. And yet, doing things for Zane had come to feel natural. Easy, even. She didn't examine it too closely.

"Those three women," Zane said.

Lavender shrugged. "What about them? They're nothing to us."

Zane was already on his feet. "I spent enough effort getting out of that sea," he said. "I'm not leaving them to this." The last of the spatial energy had cleared from his body. He jumped off the reef and shot forward as a streak of light.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

Bernard was a natural-born fighter, and years of combat had honed his instincts to a razor’s edge—something Tessa could never hope to match. In a real fight, he was more than an opponent; he was a predator.

Behind her, the other two women were already down. Paralysis spears — thin, glowing blue — had caught them between one breath and the next. They'd gone limp mid-flight and been caught before they hit the water.

"Don't worry," one of the men had laughed, hauling a girl up by the collar. "It's just an anesthetic. You won't die for a while yet."

Tessa hadn't looked back. She couldn't. If she ran, she might survive to avenge them. If she stayed, all three of them died for nothing. A fierceness she hadn't known she possessed rose up behind her eyes.

Then a figure stepped into her path. The scorpion tattoo. Bernard.

"You can't run," he said simply.

"If the sect master finds out about this, you'll all be buried without a grave," she said through her teeth.

Bernard smiled. "They won't find out. That's rather the point."

[SFX: SWIFT MOVEMENT]

He sidestepped her flying sword with ease, closing the distance in a single fluid motion until he was right behind her. Resting his chin on her shoulder, he breathed softly into her ear and whispered, "You’ve got power, I'll give you that. It’s a shame you have zero real fighting experience. Keep flinging that sword around like a toy, and you’ll never even touch me."

[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]

Revulsion twisted in Tessa’s gut. She jabbed her elbow back hard as she called the flying sword back, thrusting it backward in a desperate attempt to finally stab him.

But Bernard anticipated the move perfectly. He took a small step to the side, dodging it effortlessly while sticking to her back like a ghost. "Tessa, I told you," he murmured, his voice thick with amusement. "You can't hurt me."

Tessa’s technique was sloppy, but her will was iron. She poured every ounce of it into her next move.

Bernard was enjoying this, toying with her like a cat with a cornered mouse.

[SFX: BLADE CUTTING AIR]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]

But this time, Tessa’s flying sword didn’t miss. Instead of arcing back toward him, it shot past her and straight into another warrior standing nearby.

The man, Wray, had been cackling as he fought one of the other girls, so caught up in his murderous intent assault that he never saw the blade coming.

"Wray, watch out!" Bernard shouted, shocked that Tessa would suddenly switch targets.

[SFX: SWORD IMPACT]

[SFX: BODY THUD]

His warning was too late. The flying sword had already pierced clean through the man’s chest.
