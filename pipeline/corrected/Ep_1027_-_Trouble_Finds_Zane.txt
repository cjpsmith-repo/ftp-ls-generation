Ep 1027 - Trouble Finds Zane

Word count: 1027

[AMBIENT: BUSY SKY TRAFFIC]

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

A pack of young fighters tore through the sky, shamelessly flying against the flow of traffic and breaking the flight rules over The Holy City. People on the ground scowled, but no one could do a thing to stop them.

Zane was new to the city and didn’t want any trouble, so he just angled his path to stay clear of them.

But it looked like trouble was determined to find him anyway.

Zane’s expression didn’t change. As the two fighters locked onto him and accelerated, he quietly began drawing power into his body.

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

Even though he’d tried to get out of their way, two of the young fighters broke from their group and barreled right toward him, wild, crazed grins on their faces. They were obviously spoiling for a fight.

Unfortunately for them, Zane wasn’t the type to back down from a bully. Back in the mines, he never thought twice about taking out anyone who crossed him. These entitled punks were nothing but spoiled troublemakers who had never been put in their place. He figured it was time someone taught them a lesson.

Few people ever dared to stand up to these kids, who came from rich families in The Holy City. With their high status and the backing of powerful elders in the Soulbound Sect, they were used to looking down on everyone and getting away with it. Basically, no one could touch them.

There was a reason they’d picked him specifically. A Life-and-Death Realm warrior who looked to be in his early twenties — not unimpressive in the Upper World, but not untouchable either. They’d expected him to dodge. That was the game: make someone scramble out of the way, laugh at them, move on.

But Zane was different. He didn’t just hover there and take it; he called them out.

[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL STRINGS]

“Get lost,” he said flatly, his voice cutting through the wind. “Or I’ll move you myself.”

His words enraged the fighters. For them, reputation was everything, more important than their own lives. Zane had just called them scum in public, and they weren’t about to let that slide.

Zane truly couldn’t care less about these young fighters.

After his crisis of faith in the mine, he’d come out the other side with something clarified: there were people in the world you couldn’t reason with. For those people, only one thing got through.

His words left the two hall leaders escorting him completely stunned. They had never met anyone so arrogant—a lone Life-and-Death Realm warrior facing off against a whole group of Soul Sea Realm masters.

When they saw what was happening, the color drained from their faces. Their mission was to get Zane to The Holy City safely. How would they ever explain it if something happened to him on their watch?

But there was no time for them to step in.

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

Spitting with rage, the two young fighters were already speeding toward Zane. The only thing the masters could do was shout, knowing a single hit from these Soul Sea Realm warriors would be enough to seriously injure him.

“Get out of the way! Hurry!”

To their horror, Zane just hovered there, staring down the two men charging him.

The pair let out high-pitched, taunting laughs as they closed in. But their laughter died in their throats when they saw the strange, cold smile that broke across Zane’s face. Why was a mere Life-and-Death Realm warrior smiling just moments before a collision he couldn’t possibly survive?

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

And then, they collided.

[SFX: HEAVY THUD]

A muffled thud echoed through the air.

Zane slid back several dozen feet before coming to a stop — not because he had to, but because it felt better that way. He couldn’t use Power Transfer here, so he’d taken the hit with his body alone. His blood and Vitality churned for a moment, then settled. His body had absorbed the worst of it without missing a beat. If he’d wanted to, he could have stood completely still.

The two fighters were not so fortunate.

[SFX: BONE CRUNCH]

The moment they hit him, it felt like striking a mountain — except the mountain hit back. Their internal organs shifted. Their Inner Worlds pitched and rolled. The Vitality seas inside them surged with waves they couldn’t contain. Blood burst from their mouths, and they dropped, falling like kites with cut strings, plummeting straight down toward the mortal market below.

[SFX: DISTANT CRASH]

[AMBIENT: PANICKED CROWD]

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

Mortals scattered in every direction, not stopping to look. Everyone in the Holy City knew better than to be nearby when warriors fell. The two young men lay crumpled on the ground as their companions dove down to collect them.

The two masters had squeezed their eyes shut, unable to watch the man they were supposed to be protecting get torn apart.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

But as the dust settled and they opened their eyes, Hall Master Hill turned to Hall Master Sloan and muttered quietly, “No wonder he was chosen. The kid has some skills.”

Zane glanced at the two masters and said simply, “Shall we continue?”

The masters nodded. But as the three of them turned to leave, the remaining fighters moved to block their path.

“Hold on,” one of them said coldly. “You’re just going to walk away after bumping into someone?”

“He flew into me,” Zane said.

“So what? You could have dodged. Instead, you just stood there and let it happen.” Another voice, from the back of the group.

Zane looked at them for a moment.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS BUILDUP]

Then the corner of his mouth curled.

[SFX: SWORD DRAWN]

A flash of blue light flared as Zane activated his Storage Ring. In an instant, a sharp sword was pointed at the nearest fighter’s throat.

“Don’t tell me who you are,” he said coldly, “and don’t tell me who your elders are. You’re scum, hiding behind family names.” He looked across the group. “Come at me all at once, or one by one. Your choice.”
