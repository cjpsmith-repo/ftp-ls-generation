Ep 362 - Send In the New Guy
Word count: 1264

[AMBIENT: VOLCANIC CAVERN]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

The other disciples watched from their precarious positions, eager to see how Zane would handle the Red Fire Snake. They exchanged knowing glances, silently congratulating themselves on their strategy. Using this arrogant newcomer as their sacrificial lamb would eliminate a dangerous competitor for the remainder of the Cloud Road Trial—a convenient solution to multiple problems.

A small part of them harbored hope that Zane might actually slay the creature. If the kid somehow managed it, they'd be spared the ordeal of confronting the massive monster themselves. But the chances of a mere Level Three Adept defeating such a beast were slim to none—laughable, even. It was far more likely the snake would just swallow him whole.

They'd watch his final moments, maybe feel a fleeting pang of guilt, then continue their journey with one less rival to worry about.

[SFX: BUBBLING LAVA]

While running, Zane had already spotted the Red Fire Snake emerging from the molten depths. The creature's sheer size dwarfed anything he'd encountered before—even those fiery spirits from earlier paled in comparison to this monstrosity.

[SFX: MASSIVE CREATURE EMERGING]

The beast's colossal bulk seemed to work against it, forcing its movements into an almost dreamlike slowness. Zane's enhanced vision tracked every detail as the serpent lifted its massive head from the lava, rivers of molten rock cascading off its crimson scales. The neck followed, uncoiling segment by segment, each movement deliberate and terrifying in its inevitability.

His heart hammered against his ribs, but his mind remained sharp, calculating. The snake's sluggishness might be his only advantage—if he could exploit the brief window before it fully emerged, he might have a chance. Behind him, he sensed the other disciples watching, waiting for him to fail, to become nothing more than a convenient distraction for their own survival.

That thought ignited something fierce within him. He wouldn't give them the satisfaction.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

Zane gritted his teeth and channeled the Phoenix Crystal’s energy into his right arm. He doubled his speed, blasting forward like an arrow and leaving a trail of afterimages across the stone pillars.

"Huh? Is that guy really trying to force his way through?" sneered Adler, one of the top disciples of the Duskfall Sect and a third-place finisher in The Crimson Crucible.

"You think he can get past it? He looks fast enough," another disciple from the Duskfall Sect said, frowning.

"No way," Adler said, his voice dripping with condescension. He'd clearly done his homework before entering the Cloud Road, and he wasn't about to let anyone forget it. "The Red Fire Snake only looks slow because it's huge. Trust me, it's not slow at all. That fool's about to learn the hard way."

[SFX: MONSTER ROAR]

[SFX: JAWS SNAPPING]

Just as Adler predicted, the moment the Red Fire Snake had its head and upper body out of the lava, it opened its enormous mouth and lunged right at Zane. The creature moved with terrifying speed, its massive jaws snapping through the air like a steel trap.

Seeing the snake charging at him, Zane felt his heart hammering against his ribs. He pushed himself harder, drawing deeper on the Phoenix Crystal's power, gaining more speed with every desperate step. His muscles burned with the effort, but he refused to slow down. I have to get past it!

"He's getting even faster," the disciple from the Duskfall Sect said in shock, his earlier smugness replaced by genuine surprise. "How is that possible for a Level Three Adept?"

Adler just laughed, though a flicker of uncertainty crossed his face. "Ha, he must be using some special technique. Burning through his reserves like an idiot. But even then, he's not fast enough! And now he's trapped. If he can't dodge that monster, he's a dead man." Despite his mocking words, Adler found himself leaning forward, unable to look away from the unfolding spectacle.

[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

An insane wave of heat washed over Zane as the Red Fire Snake closed in. The giant snake head loomed larger and larger until he could see the two burning fangs inside its maw.

I’m not fast enough! If I keep going, I’m going to run straight into its mouth!

The thought shot through his mind, and Zane activated every single Dragon Scale on his body. The scales flared with a brilliant light, funneling all their power into his legs.

[MUSIC: HEROIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

[SFX: STONE CRACKING]

[SFX: POWERFUL WHOOSH]

Zane roared as he planted a foot on a stone pillar. The pillar, though shaky, had stood in the Sea of Raging Fire for years. He launched himself with so much force that he left two deep footprints burned into the stone's surface.

Flying was forbidden on the Cloud Road. Jumping was his only option.

[SFX: WIND RUSHING]

Zane shot across the sky like a meteor, leaving a dark streak in his wake. He had dodged the Red Fire Snake's attack.
[SFX: FIRE BLAST]

The snake didn't give up. It whipped its head around and blasted a column of fire straight at him. Zane hit the next stone pillar without looking back and launched himself again, riding the arc of a second jump clear of the flames.

The disciple from the Duskfall Sect cried out in disbelief.  "How did he jump that high? Is he a grasshopper or something?" 

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FIRE SEA]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Zane landed safely on the high platform, unharmed. He walked to the edge and looked out over the area. The tide of the fire sea was rising slowly. He wondered what happened to the four disciples from the Duskfall and Luminous Sects.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

[AMBIENT: RUMBLING LAVA]

When he glanced down, a deafening explosion shattered the air. A massive plume of fire erupted toward the sky.

A moment later, four small figures came scrambling desperately across the stone pillars.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane smiled. Those four had wasted a lot of time tangling with the Red Fire Snake—time they couldn't afford to lose.
He wasn't the only one who'd made it this far. A handful of other disciples were already on the platform, nursing burns and catching their breath. One of them looked Zane over with surprise. "How did you get through with only one snake? There were three of them on the path."
"Three?" Zane hadn't seen the others. Someone ahead of him had already taken down two.

He shook his head at their shortsightedness. If they had simply worked with him from the start, they could have taken the snake down together, conserved their energy, and crossed safely as a team. Instead, they'd chosen arrogance over cooperation. Even though Zane hadn't killed the beast, he'd scouted the path ahead, identified the dangers, and cleared a route. Now, they were just following in his footsteps without contributing a thing. Did they really think he was that easy to use?

A flicker of satisfaction crossed his face. Although he had dodged the Red Fire Snake, he'd left behind a royally pissed-off creature for the other four to handle. That had been more than enough to slow them down.

They had already wasted a ton of time, and with the lava tide rising, they were about to run out of places to go.

The four of them were getting close to the platform, which meant they’d probably burned through their own special skills to boost their speed.

But even with that, they couldn't outrun the lava tide creeping up the stone pillars. With the pillars only being about three inches wide, the lava would swallow their footholds in seconds. Then, they’d have nowhere left to step, nothing left to save them from the inferno below.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

Zane wasn't running a charity. These guys had brought this on themselves, and he was perfectly happy to stand at the edge of the high platform and watch it all unfold.
