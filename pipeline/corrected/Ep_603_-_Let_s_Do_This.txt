Ep 603 - Let's Do This
Word count: 1085
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]
Malcolm crossed his massive arms, his gaze sweeping over the arena. The Talent Tablet had proved the kid had talent, sure, but talent wasn't everything. Boris’s training level was higher. From where Malcolm stood, the odds of Boris killing the kid were pretty damn high.
[MUSIC: DARK THREATENING]
"If Boris fails to kill you today," Malcolm said, licking his lips, "I'll do it myself."
The Ogres had already lost too much because of Amber. Now, she was strong enough to threaten both the Flaming Place and the Dread Place—an indignity the Ogres could not stomach. Amber was a threat, but this Zane… he was supposedly even stronger.
In Malcolm’s eyes, Zane was a dead man walking. It was just a matter of who got to land the killing blow: him or Boris.
[SFX: CROWD ROAR]
[AMBIENT: ARENA EXCITEMENT]
"Come on, Zane Lowell!" a War General roared from the stands.
His voice echoed across the arena, igniting the crowd. Soon, the air was thick with screams from all sides.
More Human War Generals from the tenth floor began to fill the stands, their own cheers adding to the roar.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED HEROIC]
Zane nodded toward the human section. Let's do this.
"I've been waiting for this," Boris sneered. "Your people don't sound confident, kid."
"If they really thought you could win, they wouldn't need to scream," Boris continued, arrogance saturating his words. "My people's belief is silent, absolute."
Zane's smirk never wavered. "We're done talking. I thought Ogres were reckless brawlers, but you're trying cheap psychology. If you're so sure you'll win, why bother?"
"You're right, kid." A vicious grin spread across Boris's face. "Don't hold back. The thought of you trying your damnedest to kill me makes this fun."
For the Ogres, this wasn't a competition. It was an execution. That's how deep their hatred ran.
[AMBIENT: ARENA TENSION]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]
Both fighters drew their weapons.
Boris's blade was enormous—a massive war saber, its edge suffused with a faint red light. A holy weapon. When Boris gripped it, the red light vanished from the blade and reappeared in his eyes instead, burning there like embers.
"This saber is called Blood Devour," Boris said, his calm expression slowly twisting into something wilder. "The warrior who holds it feels no pain until he falls. It gives me endless strength and courage." His voice had taken on a crazed edge.
Zane turned his own sword in his hand, a flicker of something like envy crossing his face. His longsword was a holy weapon too—standard issue from his training—but it had no special ability. Just the toughness and strength of its rank. Still, it would have to do.
"It's an embarrassment to have a War Fighter as my opponent," Boris said, the wildness settling back into cold contempt. "But you earned the Celestial Rank title, so I won't treat you like one. Show me everything you've got."
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Boris launched himself upward, vital energy erupting from his body. The saber came down in a massive arc of blade light, aimed straight at Zane's head.
Zane stepped to the side. The blade found nothing but air.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
The saber buried itself in the arena floor. Boris dragged it sideways in a grinding semicircle and swung again.
Zane drifted aside again. Effortless.
Two deep gouges split the arena floor, ragged as a devil's grin. The Tower's stone began to slowly knit itself back together.
"All you do is dodge," Boris snarled. "Let's see you dodge this."
He began to spin the saber. Faster and faster, until it was no longer a blade—just a roaring circle of red-tinged steel.
[SFX: SPINNING BLADE WHOOSH]
"Wheel of Destruction!"
The spinning arc came crashing down, wide enough to cut off every angle of retreat.
"Now," Zane said quietly.
He held his sword out—crooked, loose, like a child who hadn't quite learned to grip it properly. Then he stabbed forward. Aimlessly, it seemed.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]
The crowd didn't know what to make of it. But in the stands, Cheryl rose slowly to her feet, her sharp eyes fixed on Zane's blade.
She was a swordsman herself, trained in the Demon Night's own tradition. She knew fine technique when she saw it—and she knew a disaster when she saw one. What she was looking at was neither.
"It looks wrong," she murmured. "But I can't find the gap in it."
She didn't want to admit it. But the conclusion came anyway.
Across the stands, Malcolm's brow furrowed. Fabian and Jarrod were frowning too. Even Madeline.
Setting aside everything else—race, rivalry, the stakes of this fight—there was something in that crooked sword that none of them could cleanly break.
"Where did he learn that?" Fabian muttered, shaking his head.
Madeline said nothing, but her smirk had faded.
[SFX: SWORD CLASH]
Zane's sword caught Boris's spinning saber mid-revolution and stopped it dead. Then a surge of force traveled up through the blade—and Boris felt it like a wall hitting him.
Got him, Zane thought, the grin spreading across his face.
Boris stared at that grin and felt something cold move through him.
For an Ogre, whose pride was as massive as his frame, it was a devastating blow.
[MUSIC: GRIM AFTERMATH]
His face burned with shame. Boris let out a guttural roar, wrenching his saber free and bringing it around in a massive cleaving arc.
But Zane was unfazed. His Formless Sword technique had no moves—and because it had no moves, it had no flaws. He slipped past the attack like water, like wind, like something that couldn't be grabbed.
[SFX: METAL CLANG]
Again, Zane’s Relic-Class sword locked Boris's saber in place.
Got him again, Zane thought, a grin spreading across his face.
Seeing that smirk, a cold wave of despair washed over Boris. He felt completely outclassed. Among all the War Generals, he’d only ever considered Cheryl a true rival. He was confident he could even take on a low-level War Lord. But today? Today he was being toyed with by a mere War Fighter. A human. Zane was making an absolute fool of him.
For an Ogre, whose pride was as massive as his frame, it was a devastating blow.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
"Anyone who mocks me dies!" A dangerous red light flared in Boris’s eyes.
Boris had rarely resorted to it. The cost was permanent. But he had no choice now.
This human brat had to die. For his own pride and for the future of all Ogres, he had to be destroyed.
[SFX: POWER ERUPTION]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
As the raging energy in his veins flooded into the saber, the aura erupting from him swelled to ten times its previous strength.
And he was just getting started.
