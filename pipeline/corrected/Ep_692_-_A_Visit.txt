[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Once Brent finally got his laughter out of his system, his expression turned serious.

"I was at the Ethereal Spirit Sect today," he said. "My master told me he can't keep letting you do whatever you want anymore."

The master Brent was talking about was Elder Xander, leader of the Ethereal Spirit Sect—and Cora’s great-grandfather. If it weren't for Elder Xander, no one would have put up with Cora’s stubbornness for this long. But even he was reaching his limit. Though he’d always had a soft spot for his headstrong great-granddaughter, the elders of the Sect and the nobles of Ethereal City were coming down on him hard. With tensions rising, he had to start thinking about the bigger picture.

Sacrificing one girl from House Kensington was a small price to pay. Besides, she had to get married eventually, and there was no shortage of talented young men lining up for the chance.

A shadow of sadness fell over her face."Did Great-Grandpa really say that?"

Brent, though a year younger than Cora, carried himself with a maturity that came from his training. He tried to soften the blow. "Cora, he's under a lot of pressure right now. From the sound of it, I think Maddox is planning to make his move soon."

"How dare he!" Cora was about to fly into a rage, but a thought seemed to pull her back from the edge. "Wait. Our Ethereal Spirit Sect is the strongest order in the Heartlands. If Maddox really makes a move, he wouldn't be stupid enough to attack us first."

Brent just gave her a helpless smile. "What makes you so sure? The top three powers in the Heartlands are the Ethereal Spirit Sect, the World Merchants' Guild, and the Cloud Hall. The Guild has its ancient protector — Maddox won't touch them easily. Cloud Hall has that sect-protecting formation he's already come off worse against. But us? We're the strongest in name. That actually makes us the most tempting target. And if he brings us down, every independent cultivator in the Heartlands will think he's unstoppable."

Gritting her teeth, she made a grim decision.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TONES]

"If it comes to that, I'll find some poison. If Maddox really shows up at our gates, I'll drink it myself before I let him have me."

Brent looked at her steadily. "I'm afraid you won't be able to die even if you want to."

The words stopped her cold. For a moment, the composure cracked — real fear crossed her face. She knew exactly what it would mean to fall into Maddox's hands alive. Then the fear hardened back into resolve.

In the end, all Brent could do was sigh before turning to leave with the other two servants in tow.

[AMBIENT: EARLY MORNING CITY]

[MUSIC: LIGHT ADVENTUROUS THEME]

The next day, just as dawn was breaking, a young man in a simple green robe appeared in the public square before the Kensington estate. He had arrived in Ethereal City late the previous night and, after a good night's rest, had made his way halfway across the sprawling city as soon as the sun came up.

[AMBIENT: LARGE CROWD MURMURING]

When he reached the square, he found a massive crowd already gathered. A line of people snaked for hundreds of feet, packed with over a hundred would-be suitors. It was a motley crew, a mix of burly, powerful-looking men and elegant scholars with refined manners. Their skill levels were all over the map, from ordinary men with no training at all to powerful warriors from the Inner-Core Realm.

"So, all these guys are here to compete for Lady Cora's hand?" Zane thought, a wry smile touching his lips as he stood off to the side. It seemed House Kensington was getting desperate. He’d heard the contest had been going on for a while now. Just how high were her standards? With this many suitors, why in the world hadn't she found a single one she liked?

[SFX: ANGRY SHOUT]

A servant from the Kensington estate yelled, pointing accusingly at Zane."Hey, newbie! If you're here for the marriage contest, the back of the line is over there!"

"I'm not here for the contest," Zane said. "I'd like to visit the head of your family."

The servant sneered. "He doesn't have time for guests. Move along — or I'll have you removed."

[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]

An elderly steward appeared from nearby and cut the servant off sharply. "You blind fool, hold your tongue." He waved the servant away, then turned to Zane and bowed. "Forgive him. If you wish to see the Patriarch, I'll carry the message myself. May I have your name?"

Zane wasn't bothered. The servant was just a normal person and couldn't sense his power.

The other fighters in line, however, were a different story. The moment they laid eyes on Zane, their expressions shifted. They could feel the immense energy rolling off him—he had reached the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.

He’s that powerful at his age? they all wondered. What sect is he from?

On the main continent, it wasn't unheard of for a warrior Zane's age to become a War Lord—cultivators like Cheryl or Olivia could probably reach that level if they pushed themselves in the Tower of Sin, where training accelerated a person's growth far beyond what was possible in the Heartlands.

But here, reaching the Soul Realm at his age was almost unthinkable. Only the most talented disciples, backed by a major order pouring a fortune of resources into their training, could ever hope to achieve such a feat. The quiet realization sent a ripple of commotion through the crowd.

[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]

"Did you hear that? The kid said his name is Zane Lowell. You don't think he's the Zane Lowell from the Cloud Hall, do you?"

"But isn't he supposed to be dead? There's been no news of him for two years. It's like he just vanished off the face of the earth."

"I don't know, it's possible. Who else could reach the Spirit-Tribulation Realm in just two years? The only other one I can think of is Brent, that genius from the Ethereal Spirit Sect."

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]

The steward of House Kensington stared at Zane, momentarily dazed, before approaching him cautiously. "You… Are you really Zane Lowell?"

Zane offered a faint smile. "Yes, I am."

"Right. I'll pass the message along. Please, wait here!" With that, the steward took off toward the estate, moving so fast he practically flew. Even if he had his doubts about the young man’s identity, it wasn't his place to question it. That was a decision for the master of House Kensington to make.
