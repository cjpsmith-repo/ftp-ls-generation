Ep 1158 - Nine Suns

Word count: 1127

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL AMBIENT DRIFT]
Deep inside Zane, something was changing.
[AMBIENT: INNER WORLD STIRRING]
The Primordial Chaos Tactic had never been an ordinary cultivation method. When Zane had first forced it into practice — using his body as the vessel, letting chaos replace Vitality at the most fundamental level — the True Dragons themselves hadn’t been certain what it would produce. The technique had put him on a path none of them could fully map. They only knew that if he stayed on it, he would eventually go somewhere beyond their imagining.
What it had built inside him was unlike anything a cultivator’s inner world was supposed to be.
[SFX: DISTANT CREATURE SOUNDS]
Deep within that world, on a land that was still wild and unfinished, an ape was moving through the undergrowth. It was a small thing, quick and careful, filling its arms with sweet berries before the larger creatures could find it. It had learned that much — which paths were safe, which patches of forest to avoid, which beasts were too dangerous to provoke. It was smarter than the other animals around it. Not by much. But enough.
What drew it most, more than the berries or the safety of its cave, was the tree at the far end of the land. Enormous. Impossibly tall. Something about it pulled at the ape in a way it had no words for, only a feeling — a pull it couldn’t explain and couldn’t resist. But it was too small to climb that high. It always turned back before it even reached the roots.
Today, the ape gathered its harvest and started home. There were young ones in the cave, crying for food.
[SFX: DEEP RESONANT BOOM]
Then the sound hit.
[SFX: APE STARTLED CRY]
The ape froze. Its arms tightened around the berries. And then, for the first time in its short life, it looked up.
[MUSIC: SLOW SWELLING WONDER]
[AMBIENT: COSMIC STILLNESS]
The sky was vast. The ape had never truly seen it before — not like this, not as something to look at. It had always been just the ceiling above the trees, the place light came from. But now it looked at the depth of it, the dark space between the light, and something in its small chest went quiet with a feeling it had no name for.
Where did the sky end?
What was on the other side of it?
It didn’t know how to ask the question properly. It didn’t have the concepts yet. But the question was there — a tiny seed of it, forming for the first time.
[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]
Then a thin line of light appeared across the sky. Seven colors wound together, spiraling slowly, like a vast snake coiling and uncoiling above the clouds. The ape stared at it. The colors were too close to the seven-colored serpents on the ground — the ones that meant danger, meant death. Its chest tightened.
But the thing in the sky didn’t descend. It spiraled once more, then began to curl inward on itself, tightening into a sphere. A round, glowing thing, hanging in the sky like strange fruit.
[SFX: SOFT RESONANT PULSE]
The ape stretched one arm toward it. Then a second sphere appeared beside the first. Then a third. A fourth. A fifth.
The ape stretched out both hands. Counted. Bent a finger back. Nine. There were nine.
It already knew how to count that high. No one had taught it.
[SFX: SOFT RUSTLE]
Something moved in the brush beside it. The ape tucked its berries tight and vanished down a hidden path, back toward the cave.
[AMBIENT: OPEN LAND, WIND]
Across the whole of Zane’s inner world, every creature had stopped moving. Every head was turned skyward. Not with thought — most of them had none — but with the pull of something immense, something that reached even the most primitive of instincts. Only the ape had looked up with anything like wonder. The rest were simply drawn, the way plants are drawn toward light.
[MUSIC: DEEP BUILDING RESONANCE]
Outside, in the lake of source energy where Zane had been lying motionless for nearly an hour, the surface finally began to move.
[SFX: WATER CHURNING]
The warrior who had been absorbing beside him had long since hit his limit — a respectable amount by any measure — and had been watching ever since, certain Zane would surface at any moment. Ten thousand drops, he’d assumed. Maybe a little more. Then this would be over.
But Zane hadn’t surfaced.
[SFX: VORTEX FORMING, WATER RUSHING]
Eight channels opened around Zane’s still body, pulling source energy in from every direction. 
The warrior stared. He had no framework for what he was seeing. The lake’s surface buckled and surged, all of it pulling inward toward the man lying at the center.
[SFX: RUSHING WATER INTENSIFYING]
“That’s — that’s not possible,” he said, to no one in particular.
Even if every warrior present leapt into this lake and drank from it as hard as they could, they wouldn’t drain it. This lake represented the concentrated power of a Saint. 
And Zane was pulling it in by the barrel.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT UNDERTONE RISING]
[AMBIENT: VAST COSMIC HUM]
Inside his inner world, the nine spheres of light were condensing. They burned brighter as they tightened, the seven colors bleeding together until they were indistinguishable — until each one blazed with the clean white ferocity of a sun. 
Nine of them, hanging above the wild land. Their gravity pulled at the Chaos Sea far below, raising tides that rolled and crashed against shores that had never seen waves before.
The creatures on the land scattered. The ape, back in its cave with the young ones, pressed itself flat against the stone and covered its eyes.
And then — abruptly — it stopped.
[MUSIC: SUDDEN QUIET]
[SFX: SETTLING WATER]
Zane lay still in the shallowing lake. The vortex was gone. The nine suns burned quietly above his inner world, steady now, their light reaching every corner of the land. He had taken everything he could hold. Any more and the gravitational pull of the condensed energy would have torn the world inside him apart.
He opened his eyes.
The warrior beside him hadn’t moved. He was still staring, his expression caught somewhere between disbelief and something that looked almost like awe.
Zane sat up slowly, water sheeting off him, and looked at what remained of the lake.
[SFX: QUIET DRIPPING, STILLNESS]
He didn’t say anything. There wasn’t much to say. He had done what the method asked of him, and the method had delivered. The nine suns were his now — burning inside him, illuminating everything, pulling at the Chaos Sea like nine small moons.
