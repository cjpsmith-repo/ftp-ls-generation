Ep 1194 - Too Much Talk

Word count: 1092

[MUSIC: DARK BATTLE]
[SFX: COUGHING]
[SFX: BLOOD SPATTER]
Gus coughed, spitting a mouthful of golden blood onto the path. This body, a unique construct of Dark and Light energies, felt just like the real thing, down to the last detail. He wiped his mouth, a strange light in his eyes as he looked at Zane with something like respect.
“The Master’s instincts are always sharp. You really are a talented warrior,” Gus said, getting back to his feet and taking a quick stock of his condition. “But you have to be one in a million to be chosen as a disciple. The first guy the Master took on, my senior? He’s a prodigy. And Zane… I don’t think you can possibly beat me. I’ll give you one more chance. Just bow to me, and—”
But Zane was already done listening. 
[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]
He turned away, and with a flick of his will, sent his sword flying straight for Gus while he himself took off toward the end of the path. He had no time for this guy’s pointless speeches.
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]
Seeing Zane fly off, Gus gritted his teeth and shot after him. *Oh no you don’t.* Ever since he’d become the Master’s disciple, he’d been bullied by his senior—the first disciple, who’d joined a few hundred years before him. That gap had cost Gus centuries of torment, and even now, he hadn’t managed to turn the tables. If things kept going this way, he’d never get his payback.
But now, *finally*, he had a junior of his own. There was no way he was letting him go.
In his real body, this would just be bullying; with his true power, a single glance could have annihilated Zane. But here, in this form made of pure energy, they were on an even playing field.
[SFX: WHOOSHING LEAP]
“Don’t run, Zane!” Gus yelled, suddenly leaping into the air. He flickered through a series of bizarre movements, easily overtaking Zane and reappearing directly in his path.
“Get out of the way!” Zane shot back, vaulting around him. His sword flew to his side, and he grabbed the hilt, lunging at Gus with all his strength. Zane wasn’t like him. He had one goal: get through this trial. If Gus was determined to pick a fight, Zane would kill him without a second thought.
A calm smile spread across Gus’s face as he watched the sword come at him. Even though his power was capped at the early stage of the Soul Sea Realm—same as Zane—the gap in their experience was immense. 
Gus didn’t dodge. He raised his hands and caught the blade bare-handed — the most precise and dangerous move a martial artist could attempt. The sword had to be read perfectly: its angle, its force, the exact line of the edge. Any miscalculation and both hands would be shredded. The five-foot blade drove in — one inch, five inches, nine, twelve — before Gus’s grip arrested it completely, tip stopping just short of his chest.
[SFX: BLADE CAUGHT BY BARE HANDS]
“Not a bad strike!” he said. “The Master doesn’t use a sword, so you must have figured this out on your own. I’m impressed you have such a deep understanding of sword intent. But compared to our senior? It’s nothing.”
Gus clamped his hands down, about to taunt him again — but Zane didn’t give him the chance.
“Too much talk.”
He wrenched Thundergust free and drove it forward again.
[SFX: SWORD THRUST]
[SFX: BLADE CAUGHT BY BARE HANDS]
Gus caught it a second time — but this time his palms rubbed together in a quick, practiced motion, sending the hilt spinning in Zane’s grip. In the same instant, Gus’s shoulder dropped, and he drove it hard into Zane’s chest.
[SFX: HEAVY COLLISION]
[SFX: BODY SKIDDING BACK]
The impact sent Zane sliding back across the path. But Gus hadn’t come off clean either — he retreated several steps, reached up and pinched his shoulder, and a sharp crack rang out as he reset the broken bone himself.
[SFX: BONE CRACK]
He rolled the joint experimentally, then laughed and tossed Thundergust back. The blade stuck into the path in front of Zane, hilt vibrating.
“What are you made of?” Gus said, flexing his hand with a look of genuine surprise. “I’ve never had someone send me back like that in the same realm.”
Zane pulled the sword free without a word. In his entire life, he had never lost a straight physical collision. Not once. And this construct of Dark and Light energy had just done it.
He charged again.
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: HEAVY COLLISION]
[SFX: BODY SKIDDING BACK]
Same move. Same result. Zane hit the ground.
Gus watched him get back up, and his smile shifted into something almost paternal.
“Junior, you should accept your fate. You’re not even thirty years old — and I’ve lived for several epochs. If a kid who hasn’t seen his third decade actually beat me, I’d have to wonder what I’ve been doing with my life all this time. Honestly, even a dog that lived as long as I have would have become a spirit by now—”
He stopped.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
A cold, ruthless aura poured off of him, and in that same instant, the unique power of the Gods-Intimidating Strike flooded through Thundergust.
The Gods-Intimidating Strike was a technique of almost unimaginable power, capable of injuring a True God when wielded by a Supreme Lord. But its raw strength wasn’t even its most terrifying aspect. Throughout history, plenty of geniuses had created monstrously powerful martial skills. The problem was, they all came with a cost—a huge expenditure of energy, or worse, a long preparation time. In a real fight, that was a fatal flaw. You couldn’t block a sudden attack with something that took ten seconds to charge.
But the Gods-Intimidating Strike was different. It was instant. It required no preparation at all. And that made it a threat to more than just gods.
As all emotion drained from his face, Zane’s sword shot forward again. 
[SFX: SWORD THRUST]
“Think you can still catch it?”
Before the words were even out, the sword was already in front of Gus.
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLE ATMOSPHERE]
Gus could see this attack was on another level entirely, but he refused to back down. He reached out again, intending to catch the blade just as he had before.
And he did. His hands closed around the steel.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE ENERGY SURGE]
[SFX: CRACKLING SWORD INTENT]
But the instant he made contact, a devastating sword intent erupted from the blade and surged straight through him.
[MUSIC: CATASTROPHIC DIVINE POWER]
*Gods-Intimidating Strike.*
[SFX: REALITY SHATTERING]
[SFX: HEAVENS SPLITTING]
It was a single blow that could slice through the heavens, shatter ten thousand laws, and destroy a True God. It was absolute. It was unstoppable. And Zane, having mastered ninety-nine percent of the technique, could unleash its terrifying power at will.
How many people at his level could possibly stand against that?
