Ep 774 - Show Yourself

Word count: 1168

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT KINGDOM]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

 Throughout the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms, it was unheard of for a martial artist in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm to defeat a warrior from the Life-and-Death Realm. It was the stuff of legend, a feat so rare it was said to reshape the very foundations of power. Even Master Abel, in all his years, could have never pulled off something so impossible.

So when Lord Wallace spoke of Zane, Abel couldn’t help but feel a flicker of curiosity. For a boy so young to possess such incredible strength… Abel suspected it had to be a secret inheritance from his dragon-blooded lineage.

“Don’t worry, Abel, I’m not trying to trick you,” Wallace said, his voice laced with confidence. “When have I ever shown you such disrespect? You know you can trust me.”

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

[SFX: ENERGY SWIRL]

With that, Lord Wallace shot higher into the sky, taking a deep breath as he gazed down upon Oracle City. A tide of raw energy began to swirl around him, making the very air seem to ripple with power.

Then, with all the force he could muster, he roared.

“Zane Lowell! Zane Lowell! Zane Lowell! Get out here! Show yourself!”

[SFX: SONIC BOOM]

[AMBIENT: CITY IN CHAOS]

 His voice crashed down like thunder, echoing through every street and alley of Oracle City. For the fighters below, it was a deafening proclamation of might. But for the ordinary citizens, it was a physical blow. The force of the sound alone sent them crumpling to the ground, their eyes rolling back in their heads as they fainted. Even the weaker martial artists fared little better; the violent vibrations made their blood thrum painfully in their veins, and a few felt warm trickles run from their ears as their hearts hammered against their ribs.

There was no doubt about it. This was pure, unadulterated showing off.

[AMBIENT: WIND HOWLING]

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

 This was the kind of chaos a warrior from the Soul Sea Realm could unleash, the sheer dominance of his aura. For a moment, Lord Wallace just reveled in his own staggering strength, immensely proud of the fear he knew he was inspiring in every fighter below. He was like a high general from the capital, accustomed to being surrounded by his peers. Here, in a lesser kingdom, he was a god among insects, and the feeling was intoxicating. In the world of martial artists, however, a stunt like this was seen as nothing short of shameless.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

 Master Abel found the whole display deeply amusing. Laughing, he flew up to hover beside Wallace, gathering a surge of energy himself. "Lord Wallace, you seem to be in a good mood! I think I'll give it a try, too! Hey, Zane Lowell! Come out and show your face! What are you so scared of? Get out here!"

Behind them, the four Life-and-Death Realm warriors exchanged glances and smiled quietly among themselves. As followers, it wasn't their place to steal the spotlight. But they were looking forward to the show. Cutting down warriors of the Heartlands would be like cutting vegetables.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

Oracle City went completely silent.

Such a vast city, and not a single warrior appeared. Not one.

Wallace's childlike face twisted into a sneer. "Cowards."

Abel smiled faintly. "Weak and cowardly both, it seems. The rumors didn't exaggerate."

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

Then a streak of light shot out from the city and came straight for them.

Zane.

[MUSIC: RISING VICTORY]

Inside, Zane was smiling. He'd been waiting for this for a long time.

Wallace recognized him immediately — the same face from their last encounter. He watched Zane approach alone and felt a flicker of satisfaction. "Smart boy. You know you can't run. Come quietly, let me take what's in your mind, and I'll leave your body in one piece."

Zane's eyes flickered. "I don't plan on dying, Wallace."

"Whether you die isn't up to you," Abel said, his tone dismissive. A Spirit-Tribulation warrior, however impressive his record, was beneath his notice.

Zane just smiled. "I think it is. But I'm not here to argue about that." He reached into his Storage Ring and produced a handful of broken fragments — the ancient heartstones Hanson had given him, long since drained, but kept. "I'm here to ask a question."

[MUSIC: REVELATION STRINGS]

 The six warriors stared at the fragments. Something shifted in Wallace's expression — and Abel's. For just a moment, both of them looked genuinely surprised.

"Where did you get those?" Wallace asked, his eyes fixed on the shards.

Zane's heart jumped. That reaction was all the confirmation he needed. The stones were from the Divine Kingdoms. And there were more.

"That's my question," Zane said. "What are these? Where do they come from? Are there more in the four Divine Kingdoms?"

Wallace's surprise hardened back into contempt. "I might know the answer. But there's no point telling a dead man."

"No." Zane shook his finger. "There's every point."

Abel frowned. Something about the boy's composure was wrong. He was too calm. "What do you mean?" he asked.

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSE]

 “The first one of you six who tells me what I want to know gets to live.” A proud, almost childish smile spread across Zane’s face, but his eyes held a savage, chilling cold.

All six masters froze, their faces a mask of shared disbelief. They were completely stunned. Had they heard him correctly?

[SFX: SHARP HISS]

 Lord Wallace hissed, “What was that? Care to repeat yourself?”

A kid from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm was openly disrespecting masters from the Life-and-Death and Soul Sea Realms. For a split second, they all wondered if Zane had simply lost his mind. What kind of madman talked to them like that?

“Alright, then,” Zane repeated calmly. “I said, whoever tells me the secret of the ancient crystal gets to live. Think of it as a race.”

“This is a waste of time,” Wallace snapped. “Abel, your soul-searching technique can tear his mind wide open. I’ll hold him down while you pick his secrets apart!”

Wallace already knew Zane was harboring secrets—and that the dragon’s soul within him was nothing to be trifled with. The last time he’d sent his own soul into the boy’s mind, it had been annihilated, even after being fortified by the Spirit Container. That was why Wallace was hesitant to try again. If the dragon devoured his soul, the risk would far outweigh any reward.

But Master Abel practiced a rare secret technique, one that could shield a soul from being consumed. That was the real reason Wallace had brought him along. Otherwise, friendship or not, he never would have shared the prize.

 The moment Wallace tensed to attack, a look of profound regret crossed Zane’s face.

[SFX: DRAMATIC POWER SURGE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE CLIMAX]

“Looks like nobody’s interested in my offer. A shame. I guess none of you value your lives all that much. Well, then… come on out, all of you!”
