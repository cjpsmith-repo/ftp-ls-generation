Ep 817 - Compressing Time

Word count: 1109

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL REALM]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

The powerful masters of the Soul Sea Realm couldn't wrap their heads around the fact that Zane was actually learning the Time Law from Fiona.

In the union of seven divine kingdoms, the Time Law was inscribed on a single totem—a relic passed down from the upper world through a secret spiritual channel. But here’s the thing: that particular totem wasn’t very popular. Most warriors flocked to the totems carrying the third or fourth layers of the Fire Law, or the ones for Ice, Metal, and Thunder.

The reason was simple: the Time Law was just too damn hard to understand.

They had no idea what things were like in the upper world, but across all seven kingdoms, you could count the number of warriors who could even begin to grasp the Time Law on two hands. Take Camilla, for instance, the warrior who followed Randy around. She wasn't some kind of prodigy, but just by getting a ten-percent handle on the first layer of the Time Law, she’d managed to rocket to the top of the Second Tier.

Even someone as gifted as Fiona had spent seven long years just to fully master that first layer. She’d reached the late stages of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm in only nineteen years, yet understanding the Time Law had eaten up seven of them all by itself.

Because it was so difficult and time-consuming, most warriors simply gave up on it. Others would try their luck, realize they had zero talent for it, and quit in frustration. The Time Law was undeniably powerful, which only made it more infuriating for those who wasted years on it and got nowhere.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

So, on the Divine Kingdom Continent, it was an accepted fact: only two kinds of people ever bothered with the Time Law. The first were those with a powerful, natural affinity for it. Among the entire younger generation, Fiona was the only one.

The second were Soul Sea Realm warriors. With lifespans measured in tens of thousands of years, even warriors without natural affinity could grind their way through Time Law eventually. It just took centuries.

That was why the Soul Sea masters watching from the steps were so unsettled. They had spent lifetimes on Time Law and knew exactly what it cost. What Zane had just done in the middle of a fight shouldn't have been possible.

[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]

And the Little Monster had noticed too.

He was still lying on the ground, but his snake-like tongue — forked at the tip, long enough to reach his nose — slid out slowly as his black eyes fixed on Zane.

“Tsk tsk,” he murmured to himself. “That kind of talent… if I devoured him and took it for my own…”

He let the thought trail off. His tongue flicked again.

What the four remaining divine kingdoms' upper echelons knew — and kept tightly secret — was the true source of the Little Monster's gift. He devoured their brains and absorbed their talent. The “enlightenment” that warriors experienced sparring with him was talent leaking out of him from everyone he'd consumed. 

Over the years, the four kingdoms had quietly sacrificed dozens of second-tier geniuses to him. A genius would occasionally vanish from a martial house, and no one investigated too closely. The kingdoms were vast. People disappeared.

Zane's Time Law talent was extraordinary. The Little Monster filed it away and went back to sleep.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

Fiona's expression grew more serious by the second. She may have come across as an innocent and naive girl, but when it came to martial arts, any trace of that vanished. 

In a blur, she circled behind Zane again.

Zane was still slow — but noticeably faster than before.

[SFX: HARD IMPACT]

Fiona's palm came down on his back. Zane twisted, got his elbow up, and took the force on his arm instead of his spine. The blow sent him flying, but he landed in a defensive posture. No serious injury.

He steadied himself. Then he reached into his Storage Ring and tossed it gently into the air.

[SFX: SLOW DESCENT]

The ring rose three feet above his hand — and then slowed to almost nothing. Bit by bit, grain by grain, it descended through the air and landed softly on his outstretched finger.

Fiona watched, wide-eyed. “Your talent is incredible,” she said. “It took me three years to reach that level. You did it in the middle of a fight.”

Zane smiled. This was what he'd been waiting for. Months ago, before they'd left the Heartlands, he'd fought Camilla specifically to try to absorb Time Law through her. But she had too little of it — barely ten percent of the first layer — and it hadn't been enough to give him a foothold. He'd later learned the Time Law totem was kept in the Wind Divine Kingdom's imperial family. He hadn't had a way to access it.

Until now.

“Shall we continue?” he asked.

Fiona's delicate brow rose slightly. “Don't get smug. In absolute speed, I'm still ten times faster than you.”

She was right. But Zane had already done the calculation. Without Time Law, he was faster than Fiona, and his raw physical power pushed it further. If he ran that against her hundredfold compression, the gap between them narrowed to roughly fivefold.

Five times was manageable.

She shook the bracelet, and Time Law spread outward again. The battle resumed.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

Fiona’s palms were a relentless blur. She had the advantage in speed, no question, but Zane possessed a lifetime of combat experience she just couldn't touch. He seemed to know where she would be before she moved, his defenses already rising to meet her attacks.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

But what was truly astounding wasn't just that he was blocking her. It was that with every blow she landed, his grasp of the Time Law was accelerating.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION STRINGS]

By the time he blocked her thirteenth strike, he could already compress time by a factor of eleven. When she hit him for the twentieth time, he could compress it by twelve.

It was true what they said: the beginning is always the hardest part. Once Zane gained a foothold in the first layer of the Time Law, his understanding began to skyrocket.

[AMBIENT: QUIET OBSERVATION AREA]

Watching from the sidelines, one of the masters from the Soul Sea Realm finally pieced together what was happening. He shook his head slowly.

Well, he thought, a small smile playing on his lips. Fiona’s about to have her hands full.
