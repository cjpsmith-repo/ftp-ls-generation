Ep 646 - Run for your Life
Word count: 958
[AMBIENT: TENSE WILDERNESS]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The air in the Bloody Pass was so thick with tension you could cut it with a knife. Everyone moved in near silence, careful not to breathe too loudly. The pass served as a grim border between two different kinds of monsters, neither of which ever crossed into the other's territory. It was what made this place so dangerous—but normally, if you were smart and quiet, you could slip through without a problem.
Fabian and Esther had crossed this pass more times than they could count, always without a scratch. They weren't dumb enough to attract the monsters. This time was different, though. This time, they were dragging a bunch of idiots from the Twilight Group along for the ride, and that made everything a whole lot harder.
[SFX: ANGRY SHOUT]
“If we make it back to the Tower of Sin, I swear we're kicking out every last one of them,” Esther vowed through clenched teeth. “Move it!”
She wasn't wrong to be pissed. Zane could have easily expelled the Twilight Group from the Tower now that they'd lost the Ogres' protection, but he'd held back. The last thing he needed was to anger other factions and accidentally hand the Ogres more allies. Still, it was no secret the Twilight Group was on thin ice.
Behind them, the Twilight Group War Lord that Lavender speared lay trembling on the ground. His mind was still reeling. Why would she do that? Didn't she know what she'd just started?
What he hadn't said — what none of them had said — was that they'd been feeding Malcolm information from the moment they entered the cave. Zane's location, his group's size, and their route. If the Ogres could take him out here, the Twilight Group would have their seat at the table again. The Moon Shadow Begonia had just been an opportunity of convenience. He never imagined anyone would be reckless enough to start a fight in the Bloody Pass.
He certainly never imagined the woman with the spear would move without a second's hesitation.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
[SFX: WOLF HOWL]
From the forest to their left, shadows surged between the trees. Frost Wolves — dozens of them, then more, the undergrowth shaking as they came.
[SFX: SHRIEKING BIRD CRY]
Above, the sky darkened. Zane glanced back as he ran and felt his stomach drop. Four, maybe five thousand Shadow-maws had taken to the air, filling the space above the pass like a storm cloud.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
"Go!"
More than a dozen streaks of light shot toward the pass.
The rest of the group didn't have time for second thoughts as they sprinted across the pass. Esther wasn't done, though.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: COLD CALCULATION]
She turned mid-sprint. A strange light flashed from her hand, and bright petals bloomed in the air behind her — dozens of them, drifting lazily toward the three remaining Twilight Group War Lords. Each petal was a death sentence if it touched skin. She wasn't trying to kill them outright. She was slowing them down.
The three Twilight Group War Lords saw what she was doing. They scattered, dodging, weaving — their speed cut in half. The Frost Wolves and Shadow-maws were almost on them.
"Hmph," Esther said, eyes forward. "The concealment technique burns through Vitality fast. They won't last long. They brought it on themselves."
"We're the ones in trouble," Fabian shot back, his voice tight.
He was right. A large portion of the monsters had broken off from the Twilight Group entirely and swung back toward the main group.
[SFX: WINGS OVERHEAD]
"Why aren't those wolves and birds fighting each other?" Jarrod said through gritted teeth.
Nobody had an answer. Normally, different demon clans clashed constantly. These two seemed to have reached some unspoken arrangement.
Behind them, the three Twilight Group War Lords dissolved. Their bodies turned to black smoke, drifting slowly. The Frost Wolves circled the smoke. The Shadow-maws wheeled above it. They could smell them — but couldn't touch them. The concealment technique held. For now.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION BUILD]
In a matter of breaths, the main group burst through the Bloody Pass. Two forks opened up ahead.
Esther and Fabian both lurched toward the left — and stopped dead.
[SFX: SNAKE HISSING]
[AMBIENT: ROCKY TERRAIN]
Zane spotted movement nearby. Two small snakes, slithering over the rocks. They weren't very big—he wouldn't even have called them pythons. To him, they just looked like a couple of reptiles.
But Esther and Fabian saw something else entirely.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
“Oh, crap! We're screwed!” Esther yelled.
“What do we do now?” Fabian shouted at the same time.
Zane frowned. “What's the problem?”
[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]
“Purgatory Fire Snakes,” Fabian choked out.
Esther jumped in, her voice shaking. “They're the strongest monsters from the inner circle! What are they doing all the way out here?”
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Fabian added, “Two of those things wiped out seventeen of the Giants' top War Lords in seconds. Only one of them escaped, and only because he used a secret technique to save his soul!”
Even during the Tide of Chaos, monsters from the Dark Nether Cave's inner circle almost never ventured this far out. The two zones weren't that far apart, but it was like an unwritten rule: they stayed in their own territory. It was the only reason so many high-level War Lords dared to explore the outer circle at all, sometimes even bringing their lower-ranked War Generals along for experience.
[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]
But now, that rule was broken. The power gap between the monsters of the two circles was enormous. With two of the inner circle's deadliest creatures blocking their path, they were in serious trouble.
Zane’s expression darkened. He hadn't realized two small snakes could be that terrifying. “Is there another way out?” he asked, pointing toward a path branching off to their right.
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
“No way!” Esther and Fabian shot back in unison.
Zane raised an eyebrow. “And why not?”
[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]
“Because,” Esther said, her voice grim, “that's the path to the inner circle.”
