Ep 1321 - The Golden Door
Word count: 1304

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERIC]
[AMBIENT: DISTANT BATTLEFIELD MURMURS]
The new rules for the Illusion Battlefield threw everything into chaos, catching everyone completely off guard. It took three long days for the dust to settle and for the contestants to even begin to wrap their heads around the changes.
[SFX: MASS FOOTSTEPS RUNNING]
[SFX: PANICKED CROWD]
The second the announcement hit, fighters fled the cities in droves.
What had once been the safest zones in the entire battlefield were now the most dangerous places to be. With nowhere left to hide, most of the contestants retreated into the wilderness.
The most ruthless fighters, however, saw the chaos as an opportunity. 
[MUSIC: AGGRESSIVE HUNTING THEME]
[SFX: WEAPONS CLASHING]
They tore through the cities in a feeding frenzy, hunting down weaker opponents to steal their Illusion Points.
[AMBIENT: DENSE WILDERNESS WITH BEAST CALLS]
Once the urban centers were picked clean, they set their sights on a new target: the vast wilderness. It was the perfect hunting ground. Not only was it teeming with fierce beasts, but it was also where every other fighter was now hiding. A lucky hunter could score points from both monsters and the contestants they stumbled upon.
The great wilderness hunt was on. Across the battlefield, fighters scattered—some becoming predators, others prey.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRATEGIC]
While most of the top-tier combatants joined the chase, Zane had no plans to join them. He didn’t see the point in scrambling through the wilds for a few hundred thousand Illusion Points. He was currently ranked second in this Upper World—a huge accomplishment, sure, but he wasn’t satisfied. His real goal was to hunt down the other nine warriors in the top ten.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS UNEASE]
[SFX: WOOD CREAKING AND CRACKING]
Before deciding on his next move, Zane found a quiet corner in the wilderness and drew out the Saint-Killing Spear, letting the Chaos Energy flow into it. The moment the aura touched the weapon, the spear’s surface began to crack and pop, almost like beans roasting over a fire. 
His eyes narrowed. The spear was bent—only slightly, but unmistakably. The two divine woods forged together to make it, one black and one white, should have been unbreakable.
[SFX: SOFT MAGICAL HUM]
Looking closer, he caught a touch of green near the middle of the shaft. Two fresh buds had sprouted from the wood.
“Even divine wood shouldn’t do that once it’s been forged into a weapon,” he murmured. He turned the thought over in his mind—it had to be the Chaos Energy aura. He didn’t have a deep enough understanding of divine trees to be certain, and it was unlikely anyone else would either. But the buds were real, and they were growing.
More than that, he wanted to bring the Saint-Killing Spear out of the Illusion Battlefield with him. And to do that, he needed to make a copy of it.
Zane had already worked it out. The spear in his hands was just an illusion; the real one was still out there in the real world, in Slade’s possession. After all, Slade was the one who’d brought it into the battlefield in the first place. The genuine Saint-Killing Spear was probably hidden deep in demon territory by now, making it nearly impossible to retrieve.
But what if he could copy it here, in the Hall? If he took a perfect replica out of the Illusion Battlefield, would it function just like the real thing?
He had to try. 
[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION]
His mind made up, Zane summoned an iron ticket, which materialized in his hand with a flash of light.
At his current rank, the brawls happening across the wilderness were beneath him. He was more than willing to spend five million Illusion Points for another shot at the Hall, especially since the Hall itself offered a pretty good payout anyway.
Of course, entering the Hall was a massive risk. People like Hailey and Janet, who were clawing their way into the top fifty, would rather just steal points from other fighters than gamble their lives. If they died in there, they’d lose their shot at qualifying.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RISING]
But for Zane, it was different. Even if he died in the Hall and lost half his points, he was ranked so high he still wouldn’t drop out of the top fifty. And if he succeeded? He’d have a copy of the Saint-Killing Spear.
It was a risk worth taking.
*Is anyone else even willing to go into the Hall right now?* he wondered.
He glanced at the iron ticket in his hand and felt a surge of hope. It showed him that nine other fighters were already in the queue, waiting to enter. Only one spot remained. 
[SFX: TRANSACTION CONFIRMATION CHIME]
Not wanting to miss the last slot, Zane immediately paid the five million points. He couldn’t pass this up. 
And just like that, it began.
[MUSIC: SOARING TRIUMPHANT]
[SFX: PORTAL DISSIPATING]
Zane emerged from the Hall with a faint new imprint marking the Saint-Killing Spear — and forty million more Illusion Points to his name. He’d gone up against several True Gods this time and walked out on his own two feet, richer for it. 
Whoever held first place in this great world could keep the title a little longer; with a haul like that behind him, second place had never felt more like winning. 
He glanced down at the two small sprouts, entwined now near the spear’s center, and let himself smile, already picturing the look on Lavender’s face when she saw what her spear had grown into.
[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE CONTEMPLATIVE]
[AMBIENT: DISTANT BATTLE ECHOES FADING]
With the entire Illusion Battlefield descending into chaos, time seemed to fly. Six days passed in a blur of violence and survival.
During those six days, the fighting was relentless, but finally, the top fifty qualifiers from each Upper World were decided. Some had earned their spots through pure skill; others had just gotten lucky.
Among the qualified, Cora was probably the happiest, but also the most anxious. 
She was thrilled because she could finally leave this Upper World and find Zane. She was getting closer to him. But she was also terrified that he might not have made it into the top fifty of his own Upper World. If he failed, she might not even get the chance to see him again.
[SFX: MAGICAL PORTAL MATERIALIZING]
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL ASCENDING]
[AMBIENT: DIMENSIONAL PORTAL HUM]
As she wrestled with these conflicting emotions, a golden door of light materialized before her. Her eyes widened, and with a soft smile, she stepped through.
[SFX: MAGICAL PORTAL MATERIALIZING]
The same golden door appeared in front of Zane.
*This is it,* he thought. *The second stage is starting. I can’t wait to meet the people on the other side. They have to be the strongest fighters from all fifty upper worlds—the most talented people in the entire universe.*
[MUSIC: SOFT REFLECTIVE UNDERSCORE]
For just a moment, before stepping forward, he let himself look back at everything it had taken to get here. 
So much had changed since this had all begun—more than he could have imagined back when he’d started. And so much was still waiting for him on the other side of all of it. 
His sister Elara, still out there, waiting for him to come back and set her free. 
Cora and Tia, and Isla Novak—each of them holding a piece of a promise he intended to keep. 
Lavender, who’d stood by him through all of it.
Finn, and a drink they still owed each other.
He let out a slow breath. Whatever waited beyond this door, he’d find his way to all of it eventually. He always had. After all, he was Zane Lowell.
[MUSIC: WARM THEME EMERGING]
Somewhere behind him, two small sprouts kept growing, twined together on a spear that had no business changing at all — a strange little mystery he’d get to the bottom of someday, when there was time.
For now, there was only the light ahead of him. And somewhere just beyond it — closer than he knew — another golden door was letting someone else through too.
