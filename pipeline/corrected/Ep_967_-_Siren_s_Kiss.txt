Ep 967 - Siren’s Kiss
Word count: 1065
The Violet Ascendancy Sect might have been legendary across the universe, but Cora had never heard of it, and most of what the beautiful woman was saying was going right over her head.
Still, if joining this Sect was what it took to become the woman’s student, Cora was all for it. A wide grin broke across her face. “Yeah, totally! I’m in!”
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
Just then, Krista, the beautiful woman, glanced up. The giant swords had formed a poem in the sky, a lament that laid bare just how deeply Cora missed someone.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Krista’s brow tightened. “The path of our Sect is one of a pure heart, free from worldly desires,” she said, her tone suddenly stern. “If you have any attachments to the mortal world, they must be severed, one by one. Your mind must be completely clear to train here.”
Cora blinked. “What?” That wasn’t what The Old Man had said at all. She shook her head firmly. “No way. If that’s the rule, then I’m out. I won’t join your Sect, and I won’t be your student!”
Krista hadn’t expected such a swift reversal.
But Cora possessed the First-Rank Violet-Core Physique—a prize Krista had no intention of letting go.
[MUSIC: DARK THREATENING THEME]
A cold laugh escaped Krista. “You’re young and naive. I can’t just let you run off and do something reckless.”
Cora didn’t care how powerful she was. “I am not joining the Violet Ascendancy Sect!” she insisted. “You can’t keep me from Zane!”
The name had slipped out before Cora could stop herself. She clapped a hand over her mouth, her face falling.
Krista caught it. Zane. She turned the name over quietly, measuring it against every promising young warrior she had ever encountered across the universe — the geniuses, the prodigies, the ones worth remembering. It wasn’t there. A nobody, then. Nothing to worry about.
She would never know how wrong she was.
Seeing the thunderous look on her master’s face, the other woman, Bridget, quickly tried to intervene. “Cora, please. My master is only saying this for your own good. You should reconsider.”
Cora’s frustration only grew. She just wanted a teacher, not some bizarre, annoying rule. Without another word, she decided she was leaving.
[SFX: MAGICAL FREEZE]
But as she tried to turn, her body locked up, feeling as if it were sinking in quicksand. She couldn’t move an inch.
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
“Caw!” The Ancestral Dragon, however, was completely unfazed. It hopped onto Cora’s shoulder and let out a defiant roar at Krista.
“This little dragon… it’s not affected at all!” Krista exclaimed, a flicker of genuine surprise in her eyes.
The truth was, the Ancestral Dragon could shrug off the constraints of most Original Laws. Even high-level Spatial and Time Laws were useless against it.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
[SFX: SWORDS WHOOSHING]
“Let me go!” Cora just wanted out of here, back to her own world. Pinned in place, she threw her hand out in desperation, and the massive swords filling the sky came crashing down.
[SFX: TIME FREEZE]
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
Krista, however, just offered a faint smile. With a casual wave of her hand, she froze every single sword in mid-air.
[AMBIENT: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Krista surveyed the frozen blades with calm eyes. “You’re only at the Inner-Core Realm,” she said, “and yet you can command millions of swords. Your talent is genuinely remarkable.” The smile didn’t reach her voice. “But don’t mistake that for a threat to me. Now — stop this foolishness and come to the Violet Ascendancy Sect. I will personally oversee your training, and when the time comes, I will help you find the most outstanding man in the universe.”
Cora stared at her, frozen in place but not in spirit. “You’re not my master,” she said flatly. “Who asked you to choose anyone for me?”
Krista stepped closer until she stood directly in front of Cora. Her expression was pleasant, her voice kind. “If you truly refuse to join us,” she said, “then perhaps I should simply kill that man you’re pining for. That would clear your mind rather quickly, wouldn’t it?”
Cora’s heart lurched. She didn’t know how powerful this woman was — but she could guess. Someone like this could probably reach Zane anywhere. The thought sent a cold wave of panic through her.
It only lasted a moment.
Cora’s expression hardened. “Try it,” she said quietly. “If you lay a hand on him, I’ll kill myself on the spot. I would rather die than call you master.”
“You dare show your face here!” Krista seethed, her fury suddenly ignited by another woman who had just appeared, lounging provocatively on a floating bed.
[MUSIC: SEDUCTIVE MELODY]
The newcomer, a barely-dressed woman named Leah Jones, propped herself up on an elbow, her long legs coiled like a pair of slender snakes. She draped an arm over the muscular man beside her. Her eyes drifted to the poem in the sky, a sly smile playing on her lips. “Well now, little one. I can tell you’re no stranger to a man’s touch. Making sad poems in the sky with giant swords… You must miss him something fierce, huh? You should think about joining my Siren’s Kiss Sect. Not only can you enjoy the company of any man you desire, but you can also learn the secrets of our Coupled Cultivation methods.”
[SFX: SHARP HISS]
“You witch!” Krista hissed before Leah could finish.
Leah wasn’t bothered in the slightest. She just smirked. “There’s more than one path to power, Krista. We’ve had this argument a thousand times, and you’ve never won it.”
Cora just stood there, hugging the Ancestral Dragon tightly as her head spun. She was completely lost, with no idea who these people were or what was happening.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
She couldn’t have known that she’d stumbled into the middle of a bitter rivalry between two of the three great sects in the Violet Core Realm, all of them famous across the universe. Krista Donovan was the leader of the Violet Ascendancy Sect. The sultry newcomer, Leah Jones, led the Siren’s Kiss Sect.
The two sects were polar opposites. Krista’s Violet Ascendancy Sect was essentially a nunnery; its members were required to have few worldly desires and to sever all ties to their past lives.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL PARADISE]
The Siren’s Kiss Sect, on the other hand, was exactly what its name suggested: a tempting paradise for many male warriors. It was home to countless daring and free-spirited women, though few men were ever brave enough to actually step foot inside.
Did she really have a choice here?
