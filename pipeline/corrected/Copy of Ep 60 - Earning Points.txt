Ep 60 - Earning Points
Word count: 1038
[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]
[SFX: PEN SCRATCHING]
The only sound in Isla Novak's room was the frantic scratching of her pen.
Hunched over her desk, Isla didn't look nearly as tired as she felt. She looked more like a fairy in her rich purple gown embroidered with swirling cloud patterns.
She glanced at the mountain of paperwork on her desk, her jaw tightening as she rolled her eyes. With half the instructors at Misty Peak gone for the day, everything had been dumped on her.
She knew they'd done it on purpose. Did they really think a little extra work would make her quit? Not a chance.
[SFX: SHARP KNOCK]
Lost in her work, she didn't notice Zane until he appeared in her doorway. He knocked, and the sharp sound made her jump. Her head shot up. She wasn't expecting him, and a faint blush crept up her cheeks before she managed to compose herself.
"Zane," she said. "What can I do for you?"
Stepping into the room, he answered, "Hello, Master Novak. I'm here to pick up some assignments."
"Assignments?" she asked, blinking. A small smile touched her lips as she looked up at him. "You don't have to be in such a rush. All the pledges are getting Energy Shards soon—that should be more than enough for your training."
New pledges weren't usually in a hurry to take on assignments. Their power levels were still too low for anything but the simplest jobs, and the Points they offered were barely worth the effort.
"How do you know if I haven't even tried?" Zane pressed. "Can I at least see the list?"
His request was unusual, maybe even a little out of line, but Novak produced a second pamphlet and set it in front of him.
[MUSIC: ANTICIPATION]
[SFX: PAGES TURNING]
Zane's gaze skimmed past the verbose descriptions, fixating immediately on the Point values. The first assignment boasted a tantalizing two hundred Points—enough to make his heart quicken with anticipation.
When he shifted his attention to discover what such a lucrative task entailed, his eyes widened involuntarily. The assignment demanded the hunting of a Shadow crawler, requiring not just its death but the meticulous skinning and preservation of its hide in pristine condition.
"Two hundred Points," he murmured under his breath, weighing the reward against the risk.
Shadow crawlers were enigmatic and scarce predators indigenous to The Gloomwood's murky depths in the eastern territories. Their reputation for savagery preceded them; whispered tales circulated that cultivators below Synergy level who dared pursue these creatures rarely returned to share their experiences—those fortunate enough to survive at all bore scars that never truly healed.
Moving reluctantly to the next listing, Zane discovered another two-hundred-Point opportunity. This assignment called for the tracking and capture of a notorious fugitive who had eluded authorities for years—a criminal whose abilities had frustrated even seasoned hunters.
A knot formed in Zane's stomach as reality sank in. These assignments weren't merely challenging—they were potential death sentences for someone at his cultivation level. The disparity between his abilities and the requirements of these tasks stretched like an unbridgeable chasm before him, mocking his desperation to help Elara.
Still, feeling like it was a waste of time, Zane kept scanning the pages until another task caught his eye. This one had no set reward, offering a range from a single Point to thousands.
He read the description carefully. The mission was to help fight off an insect assault—a huge problem for the southern part of the Empire.
Every year, between April and May, a massive swarm of creatures called the Razor swarm descended on the south, leaving devastation in its wake.
The Razor swarm was no joke. Unlike normal insects, each one was the size of a grown man. The Empire sent huge numbers of troops south every year to fight the invasion, but they couldn't handle it alone. The Skyreach Sect also sent reinforcements, which was why the job was on the list.
"For every twenty members of the Razor swarm you eliminate, one Point shall be awarded. Should you manage to defeat a Razor swarm leader, an additional Point will be granted."
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
Zane calculated swiftly in his mind, seeing an opportunity where others might perceive only danger. "Master Novak," he declared, his voice unwavering with newfound determination, "I wish to undertake the insect invasion assignment."
A knowing smirk crept across Isla's refined features. She regarded him with twinkling eyes, her expression both amused and calculating. "A clever selection," she remarked, leaning slightly forward in her chair. "It's the sole task on that entire list within your capabilities, is it not?"
[MUSIC: TENSE DETERMINATION]
Zane responded with nothing more than a measured smile, offering no contradiction to her assessment. Deep within, a fire of desperation burned—the face of his imprisoned sister flashing momentarily in his mind. The source of his Points mattered little; their accumulation was everything. His sister's freedom hung in the balance, and he would endure whatever necessary to secure it.
Isla's laughter died in her throat, her expression changing in a flash. "No, don't go."
The smile on Zane's face froze. She knew he was stronger than he let on, but the insect invasions were still incredibly dangerous.
It wasn't just the regular Razor swarm—much deadlier monsters got mixed in with the horde. The chances of him getting killed were high.
If she'd just kept her cool, she might have been able to talk him out of it. But his reckless ambition, his sheer eagerness for a shortcut, got under her skin.
She couldn't hold back. Throwing the token at him was pure impulse, her frustration getting the better of her.
She immediately regretted snapping. He was the student she'd fought to recruit, after all. It was insane to let him march off to his death.
She turned to say something, but the words caught in her throat when she saw the massive grin spreading across his face.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STORMING AWAY]
He was thrilled about the token. The sight of his excitement only fueled her anger. Shooting him one last, furious glare, she stormed out of the courtyard.
Zane wasn't an idiot; he knew he'd pissed her off. But he couldn't worry about that right now.
[MUSIC: RESOLUTE DETERMINATION]
He had to earn those Points. He had to save Elara.
