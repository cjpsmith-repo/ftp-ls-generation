Ep 682 - The New Disciple

Word count: 1102

[MUSIC: DETERMINED STRINGS]

It was Craig, the kid from the pastures.

When Zane first showed up on the continent, none of the other herdsmen had dared to stand up to the Ogres. But Craig had stood right beside him, ready to fight.

Of course, at his level, the kid was hopelessly outmatched, but it took real guts to hold your ground when you knew it was going to end badly. He was the only one who’d stepped up while everyone else just stood there, trembling.

Now, looking at the assembled fighters, Craig couldn’t gauge their power. He’d heard that War Fighters could fly, so these people had to be on another level entirely, but that was just a wild guess. His own nomadic tribe had War Fighters, and their auras were nothing compared to the oppressive presence these people gave off. He figured even the weakest among them had to be a War General.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS RUNNING]

[SFX: KNEE DROP]

He rushed forward, his eyes shining as he stared at Zane, and dropped to one knee. "I—I'm a martial artist now! Please, let me be your student!"

Craig's earnest plea made the other powerful warriors, including Dorian and Amber, chuckle. There was something so pure and endearing about the boy.

Zane just shook his head. "I'm sorry, I don't take students. Besides, I'm leaving, and you aren't strong enough to survive the long-distance teleportation."

But Craig was more determined than Zane had expected.

Still kneeling, he declared, "I'll follow you even if it costs me my life."

"Wow," one of the War Lords remarked. "This kid doesn't give up."

Craig had begged Zane to train him when they first met, and Zane had refused him then, too. Given his current situation, taking on a student was the last thing on his mind. He had no choice but to turn the boy down again.

Still, Craig didn't look like he planned on getting up until he got a yes.

Just then, one of the human War Lords spoke up. "It's pretty obvious he doesn't want a student," he said to Craig. "How about you become mine instead?"

Craig had only just reached the Bone Refining Level, which wasn't nearly enough to get into the Violet Heart Sanctuary. He’d have trouble passing even their easiest entrance exam. For a War Lord to offer to take him as a student was an unbelievable honor.

The man clearly had his reasons — with Zane's influence in the Violet Heart Sanctuary, attaching yourself to anyone close to him was good politics. And if Zane ever returned to the main continent, the favor would pay itself back tenfold.

Craig raised his head, rolled his eyes, and shook his head firmly. "No. I want the true god."

Everyone blinked. They hadn't expected that. He wouldn't even take a peak War Lord.

Zane tried once more. "I told you — I'm leaving. Even if I were willing to take you in, I couldn't give you guidance or any benefit whatsoever."

Craig opened his mouth, searching for an argument. He found none. But he didn't get up.

It was Dorian Sinclair who broke the standoff. He looked at the kneeling boy with a trace of amusement and said, "Then how about me?"

[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]

The laughter died. Every warrior present felt the weight of what had just been said. Dorian almost never took disciples.

Olivia — the greatest genius the Violet Heart Sanctuary had produced — had once asked two elders to intercede on her behalf, begging for exactly this opportunity. She had been refused.

Dorian had clearly meant it as a joke. Craig couldn't possibly distinguish his power from a regular War Lord's — at his level, they were all equally incomprehensible. He'd assumed Craig would dig in and refuse, same as before.

He had underestimated Craig.

Craig's eyes flicked across the faces around him. When the War Lord had spoken, no one's expression had changed. When this man in the brocade belt spoke, every face in the circle shifted. Craig couldn't read cultivation. But he could read people.

A sly look crossed his face. He nodded quickly. "If the true god won't have me — then yes. I'll follow you."

Everyone laughed. Amber smiled and said, "Well, Dorian — it seems you can't take that back."

Dorian accepted it without fuss. He looked down at Craig and said, "Becoming my disciple will be very difficult. You need to prepare yourself for that."

[SFX: DISTANT RUSTLING]

The rest of the herdsmen had finally caught up. They weren't bold like Craig. They stopped well back from the circle of warriors, dropped to their knees, and pressed their foreheads to the grass, kowtowing toward Zane again and again. No one had told them to. It was just what their hearts demanded.

[SFX: LOW HUM BUILDING]

[MUSIC: SOLEMN SWELL]

The last Aether crystals clicked into place. A blazing white light split the array down the center, the pattern ignited, and a column of light punched straight into the sky. The array was open.

"It's time," Zane said. Lavender’s form dissolved into a spirit that flowed back into his body. He turned, said his goodbyes, and stepped into the teleportation array.

As Olivia watched him vanish into the blazing white light, her eyes began to burn, the world blurring through a sudden film of tears. There was so much she wanted to say to him, but just being here to see him off was more than she could have hoped for. When would she ever get the chance to talk to him alone? And even if she did, what would she say? That she wanted to follow him? That was impossible.

[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]

[AMBIENT: OPEN SKY]

The light from the array shot into the sky, arcing like a comet before finally disappearing over the horizon.

Dorian watched the light fade. "I wonder where that thing leads," he murmured.

Amber shrugged. "Maybe one day, we'll get to find out."

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WIND]

The main continent was actually quite barren. In terms of both wealth and size, it was nothing compared to the Heartlands. And yet, there were a dozen sacred sites on this continent, while the Heartlands had none.

The Tower of Sin was the reason. The existence of the Tower and the Dark Nether Cave was why so many holy grounds were gathered here.

[SFX: WARP TRAVEL]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

As Zane rocketed through the warp, his mind raced.

The Heartlands—that was the real battlefield. After almost two years of training on the main continent, he was finally going back.
