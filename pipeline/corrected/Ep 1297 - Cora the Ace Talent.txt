Ep 1297 - Cora the Ace Talent

Word count: 1152

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY ATMOSPHERE]
The city of ogres was full of heavy hitters, but after watching Cora dismantle that Ace Talent, they all burrowed even deeper into hiding. Her takedown put every other top fighter in the city on high alert. They did some digging and quickly found out she was from the Violet Core Realm, with an extremely rare First-Rank Violet-Core Physique.
The long-haired guy she’d killed had been ranked seventy-fourth in the Illusion Battlefield. By taking half of his massive stash of Illusion Points, Cora’s own rank skyrocketed from just outside the top one hundred all the way to sixty-fourth.
[SFX: CHEERING CROWD]
The Vanguard One Hundred always got a ton of attention across the universe, and the news of Cora’s new rank reached the Violet Core Realm in a flash, setting off a wave of cheers in the Apeiron Tower.
“Cora made it into the Vanguard One Hundred!”
“Every one of them is an Ace Talent from a different clan. She’s the real deal!”
“She has a First-Rank Violet-Core Physique, and she’s pretty much the face of the Violet Core Realm. It’s no surprise she made the top one hundred.”
While it was true that ninety-nine percent of the fighters who qualified for the Violet Core Realm had Violet-Core Physiques, the gap in power between them was colossal. Just a few years ago, the warriors in the Apeiron Tower had welcomed Cora as their newest junior.
As Elder Apeiron’s direct apprentice, Cora technically outranked most disciples by two or three levels. Yet her youth made everyone treat her like a junior. Before she left for the Illusion Battlefield, many worried. Her strength and talent were undeniable, but they considered her too naive, too sheltered. A fight demanded more than raw power - it required wits and strategy.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: QUIET TOWER INTERIOR]
“Cora’s always talking about that guy,” someone murmured.
“I wonder if she’ll get to see him in the Illusion Battlefield.”
“I doubt it. Cora’s brilliant, but he’s from the Lower World. He’ll probably wash out pretty quick. How could their paths possibly cross?”
Just as the debate picked up, a tall woman appeared. She was another one of Elder Apeiron’s disciples and Cora’s direct senior.
“Wash out?” she said, her voice cutting through the chatter. “Cora is an Ace Talent. The guy she chose isn’t getting eliminated.”
“Sister Margaret,” one of the younger disciples ventured, “Cora may be a genius, but she was born in the Lower World. How could someone raised out on the fringe of the universe ever measure up to an Upper World cultivator? Her man might not even crack the top million on the Illusion Battlefield. He’ll be eliminated sooner or later.”
“I think so too...” another murmured in agreement.
It wasn’t unheard of for a true genius to rise out of the Lower World. But for a realm as small and cut off as Cora’s to produce both a First-Rank Violet-Core Physique and catch the eye of someone equally exceptional seemed almost too much to hope for.
Margaret only smiled. “The man Cora’s been mooning over is named Zane.”
Cora had only ever shared that name with Margaret. No one else in the tower knew it.
“Zane?”
“That name sounds familiar...”
The Violet Core Realm sat at the very edge of the universe — a broken, drifting fragment that never picked up the Roaring Token’s broadcasts. But in recent years, disciples had been traveling across the greater realms more often, and word had started trickling back.
Zane’s name hadn’t cracked the top one hundred yet. But once disciples found it near the top of the rankings pyramid, murmurs broke out among the female disciples.
Cora had always been the one they envied - a prodigy, absurdly young, personally apprenticed to Elder Apeiron. Now she’d rocketed into the Vanguard One Hundred with an equally impressive match waiting. Disciples from powerful families who shared her rare physique suddenly found the gap between them harder to swallow.
[AMBIENT: WINDSWEPT MOUNTAIN PEAK]
[MUSIC: FOREBODING DRONE]
Zane led Janet and the others to the top of the mountain, where the ruins of the Hall of Causality waited. This was the last hall they’d have to face.
“Can this still be called a hall...?” Coleman said, staring up at the rows of broken stone pillars.
There had probably been a proper hall here once, but now all that remained in front of them was a row of round stone pillars, worn smooth by time.
“This is the last test. Don’t underestimate it. I’d wager the difficulty here is far greater than anything we’ve faced in the previous halls,” Zane said evenly.
By now, everyone trusted his judgment without question. No one hesitated — they simply fell in line behind him.
Though the roof of the hall had crumbled away long ago, two tall pillars still stood at the entrance, marking the threshold of the fifth Hall of Causality.
The moment Zane stepped between them, a strange feeling stirred somewhere deep inside him. Behind him, Janet and the others’ expressions shifted slightly as they crossed the threshold too.
[SFX: CREAKING HINGES]
Then came a sound like teeth grinding — the ear-splitting creak of some ancient, unseen door swinging on rusted axles.
[SFX: HEAVY DOOR SLAM]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING STONE HALL]
Suddenly, the heavy slam of a door echoed through the hall.
The warriors spun around, startled. There wasn’t a door between the two pillars—so where did that sound come from?
Hailey frowned, moving quickly toward the empty space and reaching out a hand.
“The door’s closed,” she said softly.
They all exchanged curious glances and walked over to see for themselves, reaching out just as Hailey had. Sure enough, their hands met the surface of an invisible barrier.
“Well, since the door is closed, we’ll just keep moving forward,” Zane said with a small smile. “I’m guessing none of you want to turn back, right?”
They had fought way too hard to get here; of course, no one wanted to give up now. The second, third, and fourth halls had flooded them with a crazy amount of Illusion Points. The fifth hall had to hold something even better.
[SFX: KITTEN MEOW]
“Meow,” the flame kitten in Janet’s arms whimpered.
Janet laughed. “See? Flame agrees. Let’s keep going.”
She was clearly the sentimental type. In the short time she’d held the flame kitten, she’d already named it Flame. Zane, on the other hand, had been followed by the Ancestral Dragon for ages and still hadn’t bothered to give it a name. The thought had never even crossed his mind.
Since Janet didn’t have a bonded beast, she had no bestiary bag. For now, she just had to carry the little kitten in her arms.
[MUSIC: MAGICAL REVELATION]
[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ENERGY]
[SFX: LIGHTNING STRIKE]
Just as she spoke, a thin bolt of lightning arced from the sky above the peak, snaking its way down to wrap around a stone pillar. The second the lightning made contact, countless shimmering fragments materialized at the top of the pillar. 
[SFX: MAGICAL FRAGMENTS SWIRLING]
They swirled in a vortex, reassembling themselves piece by piece until the edge of a rooftop began to take shape.
