Ep 883 - Desperate Times

Word count: 1047

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]

The lotus had held at five petals for what felt like an eternity, and Zane was just sitting up there meditating. 

People were starting to unravel, no one more than War King York.

The irony was so thick it was almost laughable. War King York, the man who had made Zane’s life a living hell—and even tried to kill him on more than one occasion—was now on the verge of getting on his knees and begging that same kid to make a flower grow.

As the most ambitious of the seven kings, York was the most desperate to ascend to the upper world, and that desperation had him coiled as tight as a spring. 

He’d watched the petals on Zane’s lotus unfurl one by one, a flicker of hope turning into a roaring fire when the fifth one bloomed. He’d actually started to believe. 

But now, it had stopped. 

The raw humiliation of pleading with his sworn enemy was the only thing keeping him from collapsing right there. He wanted to kneel, to weep, to scream for just a few more petals, but his pride held him captive.

With no other choice, York turned to the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom for help. They weren’t friends, but they maintained a fragile civility for the sake of harmony within the union.

Shaking his head, he descended from the pyramid and started toward Zane. He wasn’t doing it for York—he couldn’t care less about that man’s ambition—but for every Soul Sea warrior whose future was pinned on this single trial.

He stopped a few dozen feet away, hesitant to interrupt a trial in progress. “Zane, can you hear me?” the Emperor called out. “I don’t mean to be rude, but… don’t you think it’s a bit strange to be meditating while your lotus is blooming?”

[MUSIC: CURIOUS LIGHT STRINGS]

Zane opened his eyes and looked up, his expression clouded with confusion. “What do you mean, Your Majesty? Why is it weird?”

The Emperor gestured toward the flower spinning slowly in the air, “Maybe you could… you know, take this a little more seriously?”

“Take it more seriously?” Zane repeated, his gaze following the Emperor’s to the lotus. 

Oh, he got it. They thought he was slacking off.

The truth was, Zane hadn’t planned on taking this seriously at all. The Cobalt Dragon had been perfectly clear: the number of petals was already written by Destiny. There was nothing he could do to change it.

Still, the earnest, almost pleading look on the Emperor’s face was enough to make him feel a little embarrassed. With a quiet chuckle, Zane pushed himself to his feet.

He stared up at the lotus, genuinely baffled. What did it matter to any of them how many petals he opened?

[SFX: MAGICAL BLOOM]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHIMES]

Just as he did, a sixth petal bloomed, followed almost immediately by a seventh.

It was what they had been hoping for.

The lotus kept spinning. No new petals fell.

“Open already,” someone muttered.

“Half an hour feels like a year —”

“Can’t it just —”

Before anyone could react, one of the Soul Sea masters completely lost his composure. “OPEN! IS IT OPENING?! IT’S OPENING!” The shout hit like a thunderclap. Warriors around him staggered. Even Zane winced and covered his ears.

“Shut up!” another master snapped. “Do you want to ruin everything?!”

The master caught himself, face burning. “I — I’m sorry,” he stammered, dropping his voice to almost nothing. “I got carried away.”

Fiona, sitting on the steps of the pyramid, let out a slow breath. “This lotus flower is really beautiful,” she said softly.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]

“Man,” one of the Soul Sea masters sighed, his voice full of awe and longing. “If only it had two more petals.”

That comment made the white-haired priest snort with laughter. “Oh, sure,” he scoffed. “And if he manages to summon a lotus with nine petals, the upper world will have no choice but to take notice. They’d have to fix the passageway for us, no matter the cost!”

His words, meant as a joke, sparked an impossible glimmer of hope on the faces of the warriors. 

“The seventh leaf,” someone noted quietly. While they had been talking, it had opened — inevitable once the sixth had fallen. The lotus held at seven now.

The ascending passage was everything. A seven-petal lotus gave them a thirty percent chance, but a nine-petal one? That would be a guarantee. For a heartbeat, they let themselves dream.

“Sir,” War King York said to the priest, his voice uncharacteristically strained, “surely there’s something we could offer in return. Something that would make them act —”

The White-Haired Priest cut him off. “Offer what? What does this continent have that the upper world wants? You’ve been searching for the Ancestral Dragon Egg for years and still don’t have it. There is nothing here that commands their attention.” He paused. “That said, I will speak on your behalf. And the Holy Maiden will carry the message through the Reverse Spirit Channel.”

The Holy Maiden nodded. “I’ll do what I can.” She knew her role — she was the conduit. The one who could truly move the upper world was the man beside her.

But the priest was quick to douse the flame. “It’s a nice thought, but a nine-petal lotus is practically a myth. It’s something you rarely see even in a supreme world. I’m not saying warriors from the Lower World are inferior, but I have never once heard of it happening.”

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

His words brought them all crashing back to reality. Of course. Getting their hopes up would only lead to a steeper fall. It was better to accept their fate. How could they expect a kid from the Lower World to conjure a miracle that even the greatest masters only dreamed of? The idea was absurd.

With that, a grim calm settled over the crowd. They went back to being their collected selves, staring blankly at the seven-petaled lotus as it continued its slow, steady rotation.

Two hours passed in near-total silence.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[SFX: MYSTICAL RUMBLE]

And then, just as everyone had resigned themselves to the outcome, the flower began to tremble.
