Ep 703 - Hunt And Escape
Word count: 1058
[AMBIENT: EERIE MARSH]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
A thin mist clung to everything in the Dreaming Marshes, a stark contrast to the magical fog Zane encountered in the Dark Nether Cave. That had been the product of a magic array, and if you couldn’t find the plate that powered it, you were trapped for good.
This, however, was just nature’s handiwork—a nuisance for regular people, maybe, but for someone at Zane’s level, the real threats were the treacherous terrain and the vicious beasts hiding within it.
Cora’s eyes widened at the sight of a murky pond, her disbelief turning to horror. Zane might have a body resilient enough to fight off its poison, but she was certain that if a single drop touched her skin, her entire body would dissolve into dust.
“But I want to wash up,” she muttered, unable to stand the filth caked on her skin and clothes.
“Just deal with it,” Zane said, shaking his head as he pushed through a thicket of bushes and pressed on.
He was heading in the same direction he’d felt Maddox’s senses probe. They weren’t that deep into the marshes yet. If Maddox was already pushing further in, Zane figured he might be able to slip out the way they came and finally escape.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]
It was a gamble, a bet on his own cleverness. Zane suspected Maddox had intentionally let his presence be known to create a false trail, making Zane think he was already deep inside the marsh.
Then, once Zane was exposed and running for the exit, Maddox would ambush him. If he was wrong about this, he was in serious trouble.
[AMBIENT: DREAMING MARSHES]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
As he moved, his mind raced, trying to anticipate what was coming. Suddenly, his focus sharpened. He dropped into a crouch, yanking Cora down into the bushes with him just as a figure flashed through the air above.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
The shape blurred back into the mist, shooting toward the edge of the marshes like a lightning bolt.
“I was right,” Zane muttered, a flicker of excitement in his eyes. If he’d made a break for it, Maddox would’ve had him cold.
From his hiding spot, he watched Maddox fly out of the marshes at full speed.
The dense fog limited even Maddox's senses. Though the Dreaming Marshes appeared deserted, countless creatures lurked within. If Zane went to ground here, Maddox would never find him. That's why he'd feinted—making Zane believe he was heading deeper before doubling back to cut off the exit.
Maddox cleared the treeline and scanned the open ground. No sign of Zane. He landed and pressed a thin rod into the earth. Its purple crystal tip still flickered with Vitality—a detection marker he'd planted earlier. Anyone passing would leave a trace. He studied it.
Nothing.
A cold smile crossed his face. "That cunning little rat is still in there."
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
He didn't have to wait long. Streaks of light appeared on the horizon — dozens of them, cutting toward him fast. Simon was at the front, arriving first by a wide margin, the rest of his warriors fanning out behind him.
Simon landed and gave Maddox a single nod. The warriors behind him bowed low.
Maddox noted that Simon didn't bow. "Zane brought the Kensington girl into the marshes. Can you find him?"
Simon's smile was unhurried and confident. "I know every corner of this place. There's nowhere he can hide." He turned to his warriors. "Fan out on my grid. The marsh is vast, but it works in our favor — every zone has its own beasts. The moment that boy disturbs one of them, there'll be a fight. And the moment there's a fight—" he tapped his temple— "I'll know exactly where he is."
[SFX: HISSING MIST]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS SWELL]
As Simon's warriors dispersed into the fog, a slow curl of green vapor began seeping from Simon's body, spreading outward and dissolving into the mist. He closed his eyes, arms loose at his sides, as if listening to something far away.
On the surface, the two of them were perfectly aligned — the most powerful warriors in the Heartlands, working toward the same goal. But Maddox wasn't foolish enough to mistake convenience for loyalty.
Once the Soul Harvesting Ritual was complete and he'd broken through to the Soul Sea Realm, Simon's usefulness would have an expiration date.
What Maddox didn't consider — couldn't see from where he stood — was that Simon was thinking the same thing. He had no intention of letting Cora fall into Maddox's hands; it would make him far too powerful. If the girl had to die to prevent that, so be it. But she would not be handed over.
Their alliance held. It just held the way two blades hold when pressed against each other — stable only because neither had moved yet.
Once the danger had passed, they started moving again. Cora lifted the hem of her dress with both hands as she trudged through the muck, her lips curling in disgust with every squelching step.
[SFX: SQUELCHING FOOTSTEPS]
She watched her sleeves sink into the pitch-black sludge, and a wave of revulsion washed over her. Zane noticed her pained expressions, of course, but he couldn’t afford to worry about the proud young lady right now. Things were too urgent.
It was funny how different people could be. Lavender’s status was a thousand times higher than that of this girl from House Kensington. Cora didn’t seem to grasp how serious their situation really was.
[AMBIENT: WATER LAGOON]
After crossing a soggy patch of grass, a wide lagoon opened up before them. It wasn’t deep—the water would probably only come up to their waists—but since they couldn’t risk using their Vitality to fly, they had no choice but to wade through.
[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL LAGOON]
[SFX: WATER SPLASHING]
Cora turned to him hopefully.“This water isn’t poisonous, right?”
Zane stepped into the lagoon. “No,” he answered flatly.
A small smile finally touched her lips. At last, she could wash the filth off her body.
They moved slowly through the water. Cora took the opportunity to scrub the mud from her skin and clothes. As her white dress became soaked, it clung to her frame, revealing a slim waist and a graceful figure she clearly worked hard to maintain.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
[SFX: OMINOUS WATER RIPPLES]
But just as she was preoccupied with her washing, a few faint ripples disturbed the surface of the lagoon not far away.
They moved silently, cutting across the quiet water and heading straight for them.
