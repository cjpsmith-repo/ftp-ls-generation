Ep 722 - Cloud Hall is Back
Word count: 1041
[AMBIENT: ICE PALACE INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Zane had pulled Tia back from the edge. Now, with the tribulation behind them and Cloud Hall waiting, it was time to face what had happened in their absence.
"What happened to Cloud Hall?" Tia asked, her eyes widening.
"You'll know when we get out," Zane said, already turning toward the mouth of the ice palace.
Tia shot a look at Lavender, who met her gaze with a stare that said it all. An entire conversation passed between them in that silent, knowing glance.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
A moment later, Lavender dissolved back into her spirit form and flowed into Zane, curling up in the space of his mind like a disappointed child.
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL MINDSCAPE]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL HARP]
The Cobalt Dragon felt her unrest. Little one, why the long face? his voice echoed in her thoughts. What’s bothering you?
Don't talk to me! Lavender shot back.
[SFX: DEEP DRAGON LAUGHTER]
The Cobalt Dragon could only offer an awkward mental smile, but the Red Dragon let out a hearty laugh. I guess our little girl has something on her mind.
"Don't call me 'little girl,'" she snapped. "You two might be dragons, but you're not any older than I am."
She had every right to be prickly. In the upper world, she was a being on the verge of becoming a world lord. As the Demon Night Queen, she was the spiritual totem of her people. Her history was long and storied, and frankly, she felt she had little reason to respect the ancient dragons cohabiting Zane's mind. She knew their origins, and she knew that, just like her, they were entirely dependent on Zane for their existence. They all lived in the same space and usually kept to themselves, but Lavender's foul mood made her feel like picking a fight.
"Warriors don't measure age in years. That's for mortals," the Red Dragon countered with a smirk. "And right now, you're acting like a stubborn little girl."
At that, Lavender just closed her eyes and went silent. A storm of emotions was still raging inside her, and she knew another word would just make things worse.
Zane, for his part, was completely oblivious to the drama unfolding within him. It was getting a little crowded in there, what with the nine dragons, Lavender, and the sentient—if not particularly bright—black fire from the soulforge all calling him home.
[AMBIENT: ICE PALACE EXTERIOR]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Outside the ice palace, Saint Morphen and the other two Spirit-Tribulation Realm elders were still waiting, visibly shaken by what they had witnessed. Seeing Tia emerge unharmed was a profound relief, and as for whatever had happened between her and Zane, they knew it was best to pretend they hadn't seen a thing.
It was a good thing Tia was unaware they’d been inside earlier. If she knew, she probably would have died of embarrassment on the spot.
[SFX: URGENT FOOTSTEPS]
The moment she stepped out, her gaze found Saint Morphen. "Zane said something happened at Cloud Hall!" she said, her voice tight with urgency.
"How bad is it?"
Saint Morphen stepped forward and bowed. "A few days ago, Emanuel Drew led his House and others in an open rebellion against you."
Tia's expression changed. She had known Emanuel was resentful — had always known — but rebellion was something that had never happened in Cloud Hall's history. In hindsight, attempting the breakthrough at this moment had been naive. Any of the previous Hall Masters would have known better.
"The rebellion has been put down," Saint Morphen continued. "Emanuel was killed by Zane. The other rebels have been captured."
Tia took a slow breath. When she spoke again, her voice had gone cold. "Then it's time to make clear to those third-rank families who the Master of Cloud Hall truly is."
Cloud Hall was a sacred place, the crown jewel of Cloudhaven, and those who earned the right to enter it were hailed as living gods.
Down in Cloudhaven, the third-rank families had no idea what was coming. Emanuel had sealed the hall when he activated the barrier — no information had escaped. The deaths of Emanuel and Larry Dillon, the failure of the rebellion — none of it had reached the city. The families were still waiting, confident and complacent.
"Look — Cloud Hall is back!" a member of House Drew called out.
"Mister Emanuel's probably already settled things," someone from House Dillon said with a smirk. "That girl's probably done for."
A member of House Calder frowned. "Someone spotted Zane heading for the hall a few days ago. If he got inside—"
"So what?" another cut in. "What was one man going to do?"
The head of House Ward nodded along. But the head of House Calder just shook his head slowly. "I keep having a bad feeling about this."
No one listened.
"Well, let's go see what's going on!" a warrior from House Dillon declared with a confident laugh.
"Let's go!"
"But where's Emanuel? Don't tell me he's gotten too lazy to show his face now that he's master of the hall."
[AMBIENT: WIND HOWLING]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
With that, a dozen figures shot into the sky, streaking toward the floating sanctuary. But halfway there, a biting cold washed over them, so intense it felt like a physical blow. The chill carried a killing intent that made their hearts hammer against their ribs as a primal fear, sharp as claws, seized them.
Even ordinary people on the ground could feel the immense power radiating from above.
[SFX: ICE CRACKLING]
[MUSIC: EPIC DRAMATIC]
A lone platform jutted out from the edge of Cloud Hall, supported by a massive stone pillar. And on that platform stood Tia. She was clad in armor of shimmering, translucent ice, with thirty-foot-long Ice Phoenix feathers cascading down her back, swaying gently in the wind far below her. She stood with an unyielding grace, her cold gaze sweeping over Cloudhaven as an aura of immense power rolled off her in waves.
[SFX: GASPS OF CROWD]
"That's the aura of a Life-and-Death Realm warrior!" someone choked out.
"It's Tia! She broke through!"
"Oh no… Does that mean Emanuel failed? Or did he not even make a move?"
It was obvious to everyone that Emanuel had lost.
Tia was the one standing on top of Cloud Hall, after all. Still, they clung to a sliver of hope that perhaps he hadn't made his move yet—the kind of willful denial that springs from desperation.
