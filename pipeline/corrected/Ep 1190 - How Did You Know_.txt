Ep 1190 - How Did You Know?

Word count: 1069

[MUSIC: MAGICAL MYSTERIOUS]
“Well, hey there. Sounds like I missed something interesting.”
A young woman’s laughter chimed in, a voice Zane recognized. 
The first time he’d ever used his Roaring Token, she had been the one to answer, her reply a beautiful, haunting song. Then she had gone silent for over a year. But now, here she was, the mysterious singer’s voice echoing across the entire universe.
“Nothing much,” Zane replied through his token. “Just got jumped by a bunch of Supreme Lords.”
[MUSIC: TENSE BROODING STRINGS]
The casual arrogance in Zane’s tone left every listening creature in the universe completely speechless. It also sent a fresh wave of fury through the Supreme Lords currently retreating to their home factions. But what could they do? Zane was tucked away safely inside the Elysian Keep, and they were utterly powerless to get to him.
It was infuriating enough that they couldn’t touch him, but now he was rubbing their noses in it. The Supreme Lords had never felt so disrespected. All they could do was swallow their humiliation and stay quiet.
“Impressive,” the mysterious singer praised. “Let me think — without the Heavenly Destinies of Shadow, Darkness, Strength, and Earth… yes. Without those four gone, you really are something.”
[AMBIENT: UNIVERSE-WIDE STILLNESS]
The retreating Supreme Lords all stopped at exactly the same moment.
She knew. Without being anywhere near the battle — without a single witness to tell her — she had named the four Heavenly Destinies that had just gone unanchored. She knew which Lords had died and which powers had dissipated. And she had said it the way someone states the weather: offhand, certain, completely unbothered.
Every Supreme Lord in the universe heard those words. And every one of them felt, for just a moment, something close to fear.
The silence broke when one of them spoke through their own Roaring Token. “Little girl.” The voice was flat, carefully controlled. “You weren’t at the battle. How do you know which Heavenly Destinies dissipated?”
A beat of quiet. Then she giggled.
“Oh, do you want to know?”
“...Yes.”
“That’s too bad,” she said cheerfully. “I’m not going to tell you. You old fogies aren’t going to beg me, are you?”
Across the hundred thousand worlds, the Supreme Lords who heard this went rigid with anger. But none of them pushed further. Whatever she was — wherever she came from — she was not someone to provoke. They had known that since her first song had triggered the largest Beast Attack in living memory. They knew it more certainly now.
They said nothing. 
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATION]
Zane’s expression remained blank as he set the Roaring Token down. He wasn’t going to reply unless he had to. He had no real connection to this woman; they’d only spoken that one time. 
Zane’s token was a gift from his master, a relic from outside the current Cosmic Age. Both it and the Elysian Keep were creations from beyond the heavens, which led Zane to a simple conclusion: this girl was no ordinary person.
After all, mastering a Law of Causality was the ticket to becoming a top-tier Supreme Lord, one of the strongest beings in the universe. A power like that could change the destiny of an entire race.
The Demi-Immortals were the perfect example. They had practically built their empire on gaming the system.
Originally, their race had two Supreme Lords. One of them held the Heavenly Destiny of Revival, a Law of Causality that could bring the dead back to life. It wasn’t great for combat, but it had other uses.
Around that time, an incredibly powerful World Lord had emerged from their ranks, a man so talented he was granted the title of Golden Celestial Paragon. For nearly two hundred thousand years, this World Lord waited for the chance to claim a Crown of Destiny for himself. When one of the universe’s Supreme Lords finally died, he made his move. The other Demi-Immortals secured a Crown of Destiny for him, and he successfully claimed his Heavenly Destiny—but the result was a letdown.
He’d only acquired a low-rank Law of Causality, meaning he’d be stuck as a bottom-tier Supreme Lord for life.
So the Demi-Immortals killed him.
Then, their Supreme Lord with the power of Revival simply brought him back. When he died, the Crown of Destiny vanished, and once revived, he was back to being a World Lord. Shortly after, the Crown reappeared. The Demi-Immortals captured it again, and the man claimed his Heavenly Destiny a second time. It was another useless, low-rank power.
So they killed him again. 
And revived him again. And he claimed the Heavenly Destiny again.
They took full advantage of the loophole, collecting all sorts of bizarre Laws of Causality. He got one that let him see through anyone, and another that let him control the stars. He even got the Heavenly Destiny of Sound once, which basically worked like a Roaring Token—a rare treasure, sure, but a pretty lame power for a Supreme Lord.
This cycle of death and rebirth repeated thirteen times. 
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT CRESCENDO]
On the fourteenth try, they finally hit the jackpot: a top-tier Law of Causality, the strongest in the universe. At last, the World Lord became a top-rank Supreme Lord and, eventually, their leader.
The sheer absurdity of the story made Zane let out a short laugh.
“What are you laughing at?” the mysterious singer asked, her attention snapping back to him.
“Nothing,” Zane said. “Hey, can you do me a favor?”
He never expected her to beat him to the punch. “You’re looking for Elara Lowell, right?”
Zane’s heart hammered against his ribs. 
[MUSIC: TENSE REVELATION]
“Yes,” he said, his voice steady despite the excitement buzzing through him. “I’m looking for a woman named Elara Lowell. Do you know where she is?”
Her next words sent his hopes crashing back to earth. 
“I don’t know,” she said, her tone softening as if she could sense his crushing disappointment. “It’s just… you mentioned you were looking for her the last time we talked.”
At that point, it wasn’t just the Supreme Lords who were pissed off. Even Zane wanted to scream at her.
From beside him, Lavender snorted. She glanced up at the sky and muttered, “Ignore her. She’s just some dumb girl.”
Zane shrugged and reached for the Roaring Token to put it away. 
But then the singer’s voice came through again, and her words made him freeze.
“I can ask around for you. But I have one condition.”
