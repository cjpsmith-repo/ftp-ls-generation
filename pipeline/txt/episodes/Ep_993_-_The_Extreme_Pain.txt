Ep 993 - The Extreme Pain
Word count: 1171
[AMBIENT: QUIET CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Zane was smart enough to know you don’t lock yourself in a room with a Supreme Lord.
Still, after Saul’s explanation, Zane could see just how useful the Death Chain could be. It could trap an opponent of equal strength, making escape impossible.
Zane figured it was a hell of a lot more practical than the Roaring Token. Better yet, the choice to use it—if and when—was entirely his.
[SFX: METALLIC CLINK]
Zane stuffed the Death Chain into his Storage Ring, leaving the remaining pieces for later. Right now, he was more interested in the attunement method his Master had just passed down.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL TONES]
His Master had poured the knowledge for the method—one he’d created and named himself—directly into Zane’s mind. It was called the “Primordial Chaos Tactic.” A pretty straightforward name. Zane figured his Master was probably just too lazy to come up with anything fancier.
Then again, a warrior’s strength had nothing to do with their name, so why should an attunement method be any different?
Zane had his reservations. After all, his Master had only come up with the theory and had never actually tested it himself. But that was also part of the appeal. If a method was easy for anyone to get their hands on, it wouldn’t be that valuable—and Zane wouldn’t have been interested in the first place.
This was different. No one had ever practiced it before.
[MUSIC: BUILDING ANTICIPATION]
If Zane started down this path, he’d be walking into the unknown. Who knew what he would find at the end?
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
Zane followed the instructions, slowly condensing the Vitality in his gut. The wisp of chaos energy that had vanished before suddenly roared back to life, whirring inside him.
[SFX: CHAOTIC WHOOSH]
And in that same instant, his world dissolved into pure, absolute pain.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
The color drained from Zane’s face as the agony shot through him, freezing him to the spot. He couldn’t move. He couldn’t even scream.
“What’s wrong?” Tia asked, her voice laced with panic. The three women had been thrilled when it seemed Zane had received some kind of gift from the book. Now, seeing his face go ghost-white, they were terrified.
[SFX: GRINDING RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]
It started in his core—a warrior’s most sensitive and vital point—like something was grinding away at his insides, relentlessly. From there, the pain exploded outward, setting every nerve in his body on fire.
Cora rushed to his side. “Zane, are you okay?” she asked anxiously.
[SFX: BODY COLLAPSE]
He didn’t answer. His eyes rolled back, and he crumpled.
[MUSIC: DARK ATMOSPHERIC]
A single, overwhelming thought flooded Zane’s mind: regret. If he’d known it would be like this, he never would have tried the damn method, no matter the reward.
It wasn’t that his willpower was weak. He just wasn’t a god. His spirit was stronger than most, but this… this was a level of pain no living thing could endure. And he was no exception. Maybe a rock could take it, but he couldn’t.
The pain was too much for his body to hold. His own self-protection mechanism was doing this to him — forcing him under before he broke apart entirely.
[SFX: LABORED BREATHING]
He came back. Rolled his eyes. Went under again.
The second time he surfaced, he lasted one breath. The third, five or six. On the fourth, he managed twenty — long enough for several howls to tear out of him before the darkness took him again.
Each return was the same. The pain didn’t dull. It didn’t become familiar. Every wave hit with the same savage clarity as the first, as if his body refused to stop registering it.
On the tenth wake, his lips moved.
“We’ll… take our leave,” he managed. “Talk… when we’re back.” Then he was gone again.
[AMBIENT: ICE PALACE INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
Seeing the agony twisting Zane’s features, any trace of a smile vanished from the three women’s faces.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]
[SFX: DRAGON WINGS]
Tia produced the Drake puppet. Between one of Zane’s brief windows of consciousness, he gave a faint nod to Saul and the Eternal Scribe — the closest thing to a farewell he could manage — and then the group climbed aboard and the puppet carried them out of the Elysian Keep.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL WONDER]
They emerged through Misty Peak. As the Drake puppet cleared the peaks, people across the surrounding county looked up. The old legend, confirmed before their eyes. By the time the group had gone, the local clans had already issued a ban: no one was to approach the mountain.
Tia flew them back to the Heartlands. In the body of the puppet, Zane continued his cycle — unconscious for hours at a stretch, awake for minutes at a time. The journey passed around him.
[AMBIENT: CLOUD HALL]
Back at Cloud Hall, Tia arranged a separate residence for Isla before retreating to the ice palace with Zane.
[AMBIENT: WIND HOWLING]
Tia stood a fierce, unwavering guard outside the ice palace, leaving all other matters of Cloud Hall to Saint Morphens.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
As time dragged on, Zane’s periods of consciousness grew longer. The pain never lessened; there was no getting used to it. Each new wave that washed over him was just as agonizing as the first, making him feel like his head was about to explode. He found himself praying for oblivion, preferring it to the torment of waking up.
Three months passed.
One day, Zane slowly drifted back to consciousness. The moment he woke, he braced himself for the next several hours of torment.
[MUSIC: GENTLE HARP]
[AMBIENT: SOFT WIND]
Then, a delicate melody drifted in. Outside the ice palace, Cora was stroking the strings of her harp. The music was so achingly beautiful it managed to cut through the haze of his agony, putting him at ease.
While the music couldn’t stop the pain, it soothed him. Zane struggled to sit up, and Tia, who had been by his side for who knows how many days, instantly rushed to support him. “Easy,” she said softly.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL REVELATION]
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
But as he sat up, something felt… off. Zane froze. The pain in his core… it was gone.
The torment that had been his entire world for three months had simply vanished.
