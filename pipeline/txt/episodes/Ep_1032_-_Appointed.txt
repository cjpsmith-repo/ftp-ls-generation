Ep 1032 - Appointed

Word count: 1006

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

If you were to rank all the talent at the Myriad Spirit House, Fenton stood in a class all his own. Everyone else, from second place down to the hundredth, was just part of the pack.

And the crazy part was that Fenton hadn’t even reached the Soul Sea Realm yet. On top of that, he had already endured nine Small Heavenly Tribulations.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Zane remembered swallowing the Tribulation Flower. The Cobalt Dragon had told him it was a rare treasure found only in the Lower World—a unique edge for its warriors.

The flower forced a fighter to face nine life-or-death trials.

It sounded awful, but those trials actually forged a warrior into something more. Once someone entered the Life-and-Death Realm, the number of Small Heavenly Tribulations they survived would strengthen their core, turning them into a far more powerful Soul Sea Realm master down the line.

For Fenton to have gone through nine of those tribulations without some special flower was already mind-blowing.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

But the Soulbound Sect was aiming for something even bigger. They were praying he would trigger the legendary Tenth Small Heavenly Tribulation.

[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]

If a warrior endured ten of these trials in the Life-and-Death Realm and survived them all, they were practically destined to become a Supreme Lord one day.

To have a Supreme Lord whom they had trained themselves would be a massive honor for the Soulbound Sect. They had pinned every last one of their hopes on Fenton.

Their standing in the Psychean Realm wasn’t great. They were often overshadowed by the top two sacred places. But that could all change if Fenton became a Supreme Lord.

No one could deny that Fenton was their most prized disciple.

The moment he stepped up to speak, Mervin fell silent.

[AMBIENT: TENSE HALL ATMOSPHERE]

Since Fenton was considered the strongest and most promising warrior in the Myriad Spirit House, his words carried more weight than even their supervisors’.

If Fenton said Mervin wasn’t qualified, then that was it. He wasn’t. End of story.

He walked toward Zane. Calm. Unhurried. “I, Fenton Summers, would like to challenge you.”

[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]

The crowd went rigid. They’d accepted Fenton backing Zane — but this was something else entirely. Fenton challenging him personally? Even if he won, what was there to prove? If he lost — unthinkable as that was — it would be a humiliation the Soulbound Sect would never live down.

Zane looked at him for a moment. There was something different about this one. But his answer was the same.

“I came here to wait for someone,” he said. “Not to fight.”

[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]

“Refusing Fenton, too?”

“No guts. Just excuses.”

“Kneel and kowtow if you won’t fight — that’s the least you owe him!”

Fenton didn’t react to the crowd. “You can’t refuse,” he said. “It’s the Saint Lord’s order.”

Zane raised an eyebrow. “Why?”

“The Alliance has already appointed you,” Fenton continued, without softening it. “Defeating you is how I prove my strength to them. That’s the Saint Lord’s purpose — and he wanted you to know it.”

Zane sat with that for a moment. It assembled quickly. The Saint Lord wasn’t doing this for Zane’s benefit — he was using Zane as a measuring stick for Fenton. It was straightforward. Zane didn’t particularly mind.

[SFX: CROWD UPROAR]

“The Alliance appointed him? That’s why he doesn’t care about the Myriad Spirit House?”

“An ascender from some unknown Lower World — chosen over all of us?”

Mervin and the group who’d started all of this finally understood. They’d spent days trying to put this man in his place. It turned out he’d already been selected by a force none of them could touch.

The warriors around them couldn’t make sense of it. Fenton had survived nine Small Heavenly Tribulations. He was holding out for a tenth — and could step into the Soul Sea Realm any time he chose. A fifth-level Life-and-Death Realm fighter from some unknown Lower World had no business being in the same conversation. So why had the Alliance chosen him over everyone here?

Technically, the Soulbound Sect was already part of the Alliance. But supreme worlds like The Psychean Realm were just considered minor, outer worlds on the fringe of the Alliance’s territory.

When Fenton said he wanted to join the Alliance, he meant he wanted to join one of the Thirteen Palaces that formed its very core.

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

Each of the Thirteen Palaces occupied an entire supreme world and was led by a World Lord whose status dwarfed that of any other.

These Thirteen Palaces were the true heart of the Alliance, the center of power everyone aspired to.

Getting into the Thirteen Palaces meant gaining access to the best martial arts training grounds in the known universe. But admission was damn near impossible, since they were incredibly selective.

Only the most celebrated and powerful talents earned a spot. They were either the children of Supreme Lords and World Lords, or they had impossibly rare abilities that made them too valuable to ignore.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

So how could someone like Zane get chosen for the Thirteen Palaces? What was going on?

[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD MURMURS]

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

The disciples were instantly green with envy, a jealousy that quickly soured into outright rage. They hadn’t liked him before, but now they despised him.

It’s human nature to want what you can’t have, and to resent the person who gets it so easily.

How could some nobody from the Lower World just waltz in and get an invitation to the legendary Thirteen Palaces of the Alliance?

[MUSIC: CALM CONTEMPLATIVE]

Fenton, however, was built differently. There was no jealousy in him, no hatred. “I get why the Saint Lord did it,” he said calmly. “True talent will always shine through. Getting into the Thirteen Palaces is a huge opportunity, but it’s not the only path to the top.”
