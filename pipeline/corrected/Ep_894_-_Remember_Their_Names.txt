Ep 894 - Remember Their Names

Word count: 1006

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: EERIE TOMB]

By the time Zane and the other four rulers reached the thirteenth floor, they no longer had to worry about the Soul Purifying Light. This was it—the top.

It opened into a wide, circular platform with a view of the entire Divine Emperor’s Secret Land. Not that there was much to see. The whole landscape was a sprawling vista of graveyards and corpses, and no one really wants to spend their time staring at the dead.

Dozens of coffins were arranged in neat rows across the platform. Zane knew without a doubt that every body they had passed on their way up, from the first floor to this one, belonged to a key member of the long-dead dragon-blooded people. Every casket had been beautifully crafted, but they were nothing compared to these.

The coffins on the top floor were masterpieces, their surfaces carved with mysterious divine textures that glowed with active power. Zane’s curiosity flared. Those textures needed a constant supply of Vitality to stay lit. He wondered if there was a massive Aether crystal buried deep beneath the necropolis. It was the only explanation he could think of for how they were still running.

“How is this possible?” he muttered to himself. “How many Aether crystals would it take to keep these active?” Even a colossal crystal wouldn’t be enough to power these enchantments for thousands of years—especially not when the dragon-blooded people had been wiped out in a blood sacrifice over ten thousand years ago.

The Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom must have seen the confusion on Zane’s face, because he smiled. “Their power doesn’t come from Aether crystals. Just look at the ground. You’ll get it.”

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TOMB]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Zane followed his gaze and looked down. Sure enough, a massive divine texture, far larger than any on the coffins, was etched into the platform floor. It all clicked into place. He recognized the pattern instantly: a seven-star divine texture, a Vitality Conduit. The Cobalt Dragon had stressed just how important this particular texture was when he’d taught Zane about them. It could constantly pull Vitality from Heaven and Earth, converting it into a limitless power source for the enchantments on the caskets.

“But if all these coffins are carved with divine textures,” Zane asked, “isn’t that going to make it pretty hard to take over the bodies inside?”

“It is,” the Emperor confirmed. “The owners of these bodies were all top-tier masters in the Soul Sea Realm. When they were alive, they were probably stronger than we are now.”

“Then why have the four kingdoms never managed it?” Zane asked.

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TOMB]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

The Emperor’s expression shifted. “We have. Once. Thirty-six years ago, War King Wind succeeded.” He paused. “Since then, every attempt has failed. We’ve all climbed up here, year after year, and come back empty-handed. The twelfth floor is as far as any of us has gotten.”

“Why?” Zane asked.

The Emperor smiled faintly. “We can’t remember their names.”

Zane stared at him. “That’s the reason?”

“You’ll understand in a moment,” the Emperor said. “The warriors buried here were the most important figures the Dragon-blooded ever produced. They are not going to tolerate just anyone walking into their bodies. If you want to take one, you have to know who they were.”

[AMBIENT: ENERGY CRACKLING]

At that, some of the weaker masters backed away, but several of the more powerful Soul Sea Realm experts surged forward. Scott had already stopped beside one particular coffin. A few small trees grew around its base — ancient things, tens of thousands of years old, and still not quite the height of a man.

A Soul Sea master nearby supplied the details. “The warrior in that coffin led the Dragon-blooded’s Holy Land. He’s been dead forty thousand years.”

They surrounded one of the coffins and began hammering away at the defensive texture protecting it. Zane’s eyes sharpened as he recognized it — a Six-Star Dragon Seal. A five-star divine texture could hold off a Life-and-Death warrior. A six-star could hold off a Soul Sea master. Against this many Soul Sea masters at once, it had no chance.

[SFX: SHATTERING GLASS]

[AMBIENT: ENERGY CRACKLING]

Under their combined assault, the defensive ward shattered, and the seven or eight small dragons made of pure Vitality that had materialized to defend it slowly faded away. The masters had split their effort deliberately — several breaking away to draw the dragons off while the others attacked the rune directly. They’d clearly done this before.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]

Throughout the chaos, Scott just stood off to the side, his lips moving as he repeated the name “Alexander Shaw” over and over. He didn’t even seem to notice when the spectral dragons appeared and sent others scrambling for safety. He didn’t flinch. He didn’t move. The name was the only thing that mattered.

With the defensive texture broken, the Soul Sea Realm masters worked together, using their borrowed bodies to pry the heavy lid off the coffin.

[SFX: COFFIN LID OPENING]

Inside lay a silver-haired, middle-aged man: Alexander Shaw himself. Even as a corpse with his eyes peacefully closed, a commanding aura of raw power rolled off him, a testament to the incredible strength he’d wielded in life. The four rulers with Zane had a similar presence, but it was a flickering candle next to the bonfire radiating from Alexander’s body.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

This man had clearly been a master at the absolute peak of the Soul Sea Realm. In his day, he might have been the strongest warrior in the entire world of chaos.

The sheer force of the corpse’s aura made Scott tense, but he just shook it off. He turned, gave a sharp nod to the other three rulers, and then vaulted into the open coffin.

The moment he was inside, Scott’s hand shot out and pressed the spot between the corpse’s eyebrows. The body’s mouth fell open on reflex, and a stream of light—Scott’s soul—shot straight down its throat.

In an instant, the power of his Soul Core flooded through the corpse’s limbs and organs.

[SFX: BODY RISING]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

A moment later, the tall, powerful body of Alexander Shaw climbed out of the coffin.
