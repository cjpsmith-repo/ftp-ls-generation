Ep 398 - Take a Bath

Word count: 1156

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]

The gigantic sword stood upright, its blade plunged deep into the ground.

It towered a good ten feet high and was at least a foot across—a weapon forged not for mortals, but for legends. The massive blade gleamed with dark, forbidding metal, etched with intricate lines that pulsed like molten ruby veins. Even motionless, thrust into the earth like a monument to forgotten wars, the sword radiated menace—a predator lying in wait.

Zane's breath caught as he approached. Just the hilt rose as tall as he stood, wrapped in what appeared to be ancient leather that had somehow resisted decay. The crossguard extended like outstretched wings, each tip sharp enough to pierce armor. He reached out tentatively, his fingers stopping inches from the grip.

Zane's hand trembled slightly. Part of him wanted to grasp that ancient hilt, to test himself against the weight of history. But something deeper—instinct, perhaps, or wisdom born from too many close calls—held him back.

[SFX: METALLIC RESONANCE]

Tia, on the other hand, barely glanced at it."That's the Sword of the Blood Fiend Emperor." It wasn't the right weapon for her, she knew, but most male warriors would kill for a blade like that.

The sword was planted right at the exit of the Cloud Road. Tia had seen it plenty of times, just like every other student who'd passed the test.

Over the years, tons of students from Cloud Hall had tried to pull it free, but no one ever succeeded. Even those with superhuman strength couldn't get it to budge an inch.

Its weight was one thing, sure, but students training in the Soul Realm could lift far heavier objects. There had to be another reason. And there was: an enchanted barrier protecting the blade. No one was getting that sword unless they could break the barrier first.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

But now, with the sword’s resting place exposed in the middle of this square, the barrier was gone.

"I thought the Blood Fiend Emperor was human," Zane asked, unable to tear his eyes away from it. "How could anyone use a sword that huge?"

Even if the Emperor was strong enough to lift it, the sheer size made it seem… impractical. Too long, too clumsy for a normal person to fight with. Zane tried to picture it, but it just didn't make sense.

A thoughtful look crossed Tia's face. "It belonged to the Blood Fiend Emperor, sure," she said. "But who says he wielded it himself? What if his sword spirit used it for him? If his spirit was enormous, or if the Emperor merged with it, that'd be a huge advantage."

"Sword spirit?" Zane echoed, his interest piqued. Her theory was fascinating.

Ever since a puppet had ripped his Streamer to pieces, Zane had been using the Blood Drinker, which meant his sword spirit, Lily, was without a weapon.

Zane eyed the giant sword, wondering if he could give it to Lily.

But he quickly dismissed the thought. Even though Lily was slim and towered over Tia, she probably wasn't strong enough to handle it.

Tia caught the spark of interest in Zane's eyes and smirked. "Let me guess: you have a sword spirit, too?" Having sword intent was one thing; having a sword spirit was something else entirely. Tia had seen Zane’s sword intent, but she found it hard to believe someone in the Adept Realm could already have a sword spirit.
Specks of deep red light gathered and condensed into a tall, slender figure — sharp ears, near-perfect features, Demon Night clan through and through.

[SFX: SPIRIT MANIFESTATION]

Tia looked Lily up and down. "Of course your sword spirit looks like that." Then she studied her more carefully, and her expression shifted. "Her form is extremely condensed. Do you know the Sword Spirit Control Technique? A sword spirit can infuse into a blade and wield it independently."

"Something like this?" Zane tossed Lily the Blood Drinker.

[SFX: SWORD CAUGHT — ENERGY SHIFT]

The moment Lily's hand closed around the hilt, she moved — a sword dance, every step and swing laced with killing intent wrapped inside something almost beautiful. Flawless.

Tia watched in silence.

"Infuse part of her into that sword," she said finally, nodding at the giant blade. "She can pull it out."

Red light drifted from Lily into the sword. The markings glowed. Lily took the hilt and pulled — the ten-foot blade came free like it weighed nothing. She held it, ran through the dance once, then drove it back into the stone and returned to Zane.

"I'll take it," Zane said.

"I wasn't going to stop you," Tia said.


[SFX: RUMBLING THUNDER]

Before she could finish, The sky suddenly went dark, blotted out by something massive falling toward them.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL GARDEN]

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

Huge chunks of meteorite and dark forging metals rained from the sky.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]

Then, a lush green garden, guided by a beam of light, descended and settled in a corner of the square. Tia’s eyes went wide, and she immediately rushed toward it.

Powerful herbs took ages to grow, and with no one to tend them since the Blood Fiend Emperor's death, they had been left to grow wild for thousands of years. Any that survived the harsh, unpredictable conditions would be worth a fortune.

"Is that the herbal garden—" Zane began, but Tia cut him off. She spun around, grabbed his wrist, and dragged him toward the exit. With a grunt, she threw him.

"This garden isn't for you!" she snapped. "Go take a bath!"

[SFX: SPLASH]

Zane felt himself lifted off his feet and sent flying. He landed with a huge splash right in the pool of Drake's blood. 

[AMBIENT: BUBBLING LIQUID]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Seconds later, his skin began to burn.

He hadn't expected the Drake's blood to be so searingly hot.

Zane's body had been tempered like a weapon, so he could normally handle intense heat. But this was different. The blood of a Dragon burned far hotter than any normal fire. The sensation was… strange.

[MUSIC: CALM MEDITATION]

But the strange feeling didn't last. As Zane's body adjusted to the intense heat, comfort washed over him. The burning transformed into something almost pleasant, like sinking into a hot spring after days of hard travel.

[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]

His pores opened up, drinking in the potent nutrients dissolved in the blood. Zane closed his eyes and exhaled slowly, feeling his heartbeat steady. He let himself relax completely, cycling through his martial arts techniques in his mind—the familiar patterns grounding him, centering his focus.

Seeing that Zane had settled into meditation, Tia scowled and turned her attention back to the garden. She moved with purpose, her earlier irritation still simmering beneath the surface. She needed a few key herbs to fully recover her strength, and she hoped—no, she needed—to find them here. Every moment counted.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT CRESCENDO]

About an hour later, Zane's eyes shot open. A tremor rippled through his core—deep, powerful, unmistakable. 

Fresh Vitality surged through his meridians like a river breaking through a dam, flooding his system with raw energy. His breath caught. Soaking in the pool of Drake's blood had triggered an unexpected breakthrough, and he could feel his cultivation rising, transforming, reaching for the next realm.
