Ep 382 - The Enemy of My Enemy

Word count: 1112

[AMBIENT: RUMBLING LAVA]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Four hundred feet left to go.

Three hundred feet.

Two hundred feet.

Zane's gaze fixed on the wall of rock looming ahead, his heart hammering against his ribs. It sure as hell looked like a dead end. The realization struck him like a physical blow—after everything they'd survived, would it end here, consumed by molten stone?

[SFX: RAGGED BREATHING]

Behind them, the lava roared closer, its heat scorching the back of his neck. Lewis's ragged breathing filled his ears, each gasp edged with barely contained panic. The tension wrapped around them like a shroud, suffocating and absolute. If that wall turned out to be solid, they were finished.

[SFX: MAGICAL GLOW]

Just when he was ready to call it quits, Zane's eyes landed on the rows of runes etched into the stone pillars blocking their path. "Colors! There are colors on them!"  Hope surged through his chest—faint, desperate, but real.

"Go!" Finn yelled, his eyes flashing with sudden determination as he surged forward, teeth gritted. He had no idea what the runes meant, couldn't decipher their ancient patterns, but they represented a sliver of hope—and right now, their only way forward.

Zane's mind raced. The colored runes had to mean something. They had to. The alternative was unthinkable.

[SFX: BUBBLING LAVA SURGE]

The lava swelled again, creeping up the sides of the pillars until only the very tops peeked through.

Ten stone pillars stood at the dead end, each one covered in glowing runes. Zane leaped onto the first, and the symbols under his feet flared to life. He bounded from one to the next, a trail of light blazing in his wake.

[SFX: VORTEX OPENING]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]

As his feet hit the final pillar, a vortex spun into existence right in front of him.

It was the same dark, bluish-gray swirl they’d seen under the arched gateway of the Cloud Road.

"It's a path!" Zane yelled, a wide grin spreading across his face. His gamble had paid off.

Just then, Finn caught up. When he stepped onto the last pillar, a second vortex appeared right in front of him.

Did that mean everyone got their own exit?

[AMBIENT: INTENSE WIND]

[SFX: RUSHING WATER]

A moment later, Lewis staggered into view, looking like a half-drowned man who’d just found a life raft. 

But when Lewis hit the last pillar, nothing happened. No vortex.
He looked down. In his rush, he'd skipped the fourth pillar entirely.
"Go in without him," Finn said flatly.
The lava was already lapping over the stones. The fourth pillar was more than half submerged.
Lewis didn't wait to be told twice. He launched himself into the air, touched the tip of his foot to the fourth pillar just long enough to trigger the rune, and threw himself toward the final stone. His own vortex spun open. He dove through.
[SFX: HEAVY LANDING / PAINED GROAN]
He came out the other side in a heap, clutching his foot. The brief contact with the lava-soaked stone had been enough.

Seeing the vortexes, he pushed himself forward in a final, desperate burst of speed.

"Here," Finn said, holding out a small, white pellet. "Take this. It's a Frost Pill, for the burns."

Lewis gave him a grateful look, took the pill, and popped it into his mouth.

As Lewis choked down the medicine, Zane and Finn took in their new surroundings. They were on the Death Road, but that first test hadn't been nearly as bad as they'd braced for. Just a little nerve. Which probably meant the real challenge was still ahead.

[AMBIENT: EERIE DIMENSION]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

The vortexes had thrown them into some kind of separate dimension. The space was dim, lit by a gray sky that pulsed with a faint, sickly light. The air felt heavy, suffocating—like being trapped in a cave so deep the sun was just a memory.

[AMBIENT: DARK JUNGLE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

In the distance, a dark jungle clawed its way out of the bleak landscape. The shadows between the trees looked darker than night, and who knew what kind of powerful creatures were lurking in there.

"Let's go," Zane announced. Finn stuck close to him, while Lewis scrambled to his feet to hobble along behind them. Warriors like them usually healed fast, but even with the Frost Pill, Lewis's burns would take time to mend.

Lewis cursed himself. That blue treasure box… if he'd just pushed harder for it, he could've absorbed the Lifeblood of the Nine-Headed Dragon. An injury like this would've vanished in a heartbeat.

[AMBIENT: DENSE JUNGLE NIGHT]

[SFX: DISTANT CREATURE SOUNDS]

They reached the edge of the dark jungle, where the faint light from the sky was swallowed by the canopy. They could barely see a thing, forced to rely on instinct to move forward.

Lewis, for his part, kept his mouth shut. He was arrogant, sure, but he wasn't an idiot. He knew making a sound in a place like this was just asking to die.

[SFX: WHOOSHING PROJECTILE]

They’d only been walking through the silent jungle for a few minutes when a faint swish cut the air—something was coming right for them.

The sound was barely there, but Zane’s sharp senses caught it.

"Be careful!" Lewis shouted.

Zane was already moving. He yanked Finn back just as a small, dark shadow zipped past. It wasn't quite fast enough, though—it grazed Finn's arm as it flew by.

[SFX: FABRIC TEARING]

The fabric on Finn’s sleeve was torn open, revealing a shallow cut on his skin.

Finn asked,  gripped his Blood-Corroding Sword and scanned the darkness. "Ow! What was that?" 

The small, black shadow hadn't moved like a thrown dart. They all saw it swerve after hitting Finn and disappear back into the jungle. It had to be a creature.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

"I saw it," Lewis said, his eyes wide as he sniffed the air. "A tiny bat. Its wings… they're like razors. A Blade Bat. That's what nicked you."

"How did you see that?"
"Natural gift," Lewis said with a shrug. "To me, dark is just… not that dark."
Up ahead, another vortex shimmered into view between the trees.

With no other real choice, they stepped through the vortex.

[SFX: EXPLOSION]

[AMBIENT: ROARING FLAMES]

The moment they landed on the other side, an explosion rocked the ground and the world erupted in flames.

Zane recognized it right away. "That's the Black Lotus Karmafire!" It was Patrick's signature move.

But who was he fighting? Zane knew Patrick only used that technique as a last resort. It took days for the power to recharge after summoning his Shadow of the Immovable Guardian; he wouldn't blow it unless his life was on the line. Patrick was in deep trouble.

"Who is he fighting?" Zane muttered, staring toward the flames.

"I don't know," Finn said. "Let's go check!"

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

Zane took off toward the battle. They might have been rivals back at Skyreach Sect, but on the Death Road, they were allies. 

He had to help.
