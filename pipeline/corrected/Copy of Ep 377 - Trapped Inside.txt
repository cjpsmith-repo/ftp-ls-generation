Ep 377 - Trapped Inside

Word count: 988

[AMBIENT: TENSE PLATFORM]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]

As he was coming up, Zane hadn't bothered absorbing all the Celestial Essence from his Dragon Scale reserves. He'd figured selling the surplus would net him a comfortable stockpile of Crystal Cubes—a decision that seemed prudent at the time.

In the Eastern Territories, commanding a hundred-plus Dragon Scales had made him virtually unstoppable. That raw power had crushed anyone foolish enough to challenge him, whether they matched his cultivation level or even boasted mastery of the Soul Realm. He'd felt invincible then, untouchable.

Now, facing this relentless automaton, that same strength felt ridiculously inadequate. Zane wished he'd known what awaited him on this trial; he would've absorbed every last drop of Celestial Essence he could find. If he could just activate five hundred Dragon Scales, he could tear this mechanical monster apart with his bare hands, rip it limb from limb until nothing remained but scrap metal.

The fantasy was intoxicating—but dangerous.

Whenever Zane fixated on raw power, he overlooked the most critical detail: his own body's limits. The Dragon Scales took quite a toll. If he forced five hundred Dragon Scales through his body right now, the overwhelming energy would likely rip him apart from the inside; he'd destroy himself before ever touching his opponent

Even though his body was as tough as a Spirit Weapon, there was still a risk it would shatter if he pushed too much power through it.

Zane's breathing steadied as cold reality tempered his frustration. Brute force alone wouldn't save him here. He needed to think, to adapt, to find another way.

[SFX: METALLIC GRINDING]

Zane still couldn't break free from the automaton's iron grip, but his mind raced, searching for any advantage. Then he saw it—a detail so obvious he'd nearly missed it in his panic.

The machine balanced on two razor-sharp blades that served as its feet. No matter how perfectly engineered, that meant its center of gravity had to be precarious.

[SFX: METAL SCRAPING STONE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ESCALATION]

A sharp point inched toward his heart. Zane's pulse thundered in his ears. He had seconds, maybe less.

[SFX: HARD IMPACT]

He threw his whole body backward with desperate force, feeling the automaton lurch forward to compensate. Before it could recover, he rammed his weight in the opposite direction, using his trapped position as leverage. The sudden shift forced the construct to yank its bladed feet from the stone platform just to maintain its stance.

If he couldn't overpower it, maybe he could make it destroy itself.

[SFX: METAL SCRAPING ON STONE]

A harsh screech of metal on stone filled the air.

Still clutching Zane's hands, the automaton stumbled back, lurching wildly from side to side. With every awkward step, its bladed feet gouged fresh holes in the platform.

Balancing on those blades was clearly a challenge, and Zane's desperate thrashing was making it wobble even more.

As the machine stumbled back, Zane got closer and closer to the silver treasure box. Just a few more feet and the fight would be over. Victory was right there.

[SFX: METAL HOOK IMPACT]

But what happened next caught Zane completely off guard. Just as the automaton teetered on the edge of collapse, a long hook shot out from its ankle and slammed into the platform. Two spikes on its end dug deep into the stone, anchoring the machine in place.

"Damn it!" Zane cursed, his pulse hammering in his ears.

When brute force failed, he had to rely on his wits. But thinking clearly was nearly impossible when death was seconds away. He'd never expected the automaton to adapt this quickly.

This thing is smart, Zane realized, his grip tightening on the machine's wrists. Too smart for a simple construct. Is it thinking for itself? Or is there a human soul trapped in there?

The thought sent a chill through him. He'd read about soul-implanted automatons, immortal guardians created through forbidden techniques, their consciousness bound to metal shells for eternity. The idea of such an existence made his stomach turn.

He decided to gamble on it. "You're trapped in there, aren't you?" 

[SFX: METAL SCRAPING STOPS]

At his words, the scraping of blades stopped. The automaton froze mid-motion, its mechanical limbs locked in place.

Zane's heart raced. He'd struck a nerve—or whatever passed for one in this thing. "I know you're in there," he pressed, keeping his voice steady despite the fear coursing through him. "If there's a way to free you, I can help. Just tell me what to do."

[AMBIENT: CAVE ECHOES]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

After a few tense seconds, the automaton started moving again, turning back toward its cave.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS DRONE]

Implanting a human soul into an automaton made them indestructible, basically immortal. It sounded great on paper, but Zane knew it was a living nightmare. A fate worse than death—the horror of being forced to stay alive forever.

He immediately thought of Saul, how relieved he’d looked when Zane found him in The Elysian Keep. Saul had told him how spending tens of millions of years trapped in a machine was an agony beyond words. Zane knew exactly what the soul in this thing could be going through.

But this automaton seemed programmed only to kill, not to listen. If there really was a soul inside, it should have been able to communicate… unless it was sealed away, a prisoner in its own metal body.

[AMBIENT: CLOUD ROAD]

[SFX: ECHOING LAUGHTER]

"Ha ha ha!" Sir Kenneth's laugh boomed from under an archway at the Cloud Road entrance.

Toby was right there with him, shaking his head in disbelief. "Unbelievable. Just… unbelievable. The kid always finds a way out! He got into that space, spotted those Spatial Fractures instantly, and used them to save his own skin. It's amazing!"

Kenneth nodded in agreement. "I know! Escaping the automaton's grip like that? A second ago, I was sure he was a goner. I never would've guessed there'd be a Spatial Fracture right there for the taking. Good thing he understands the Spatial Law well enough to use it."

He watched Zane on the screen, shaking his head in admiration. "Dammit, that is one lucky kid."
