Ep 258 - Zane on the Rise
Word Count: 1000
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TRAINING GROUNDS]
[SFX: CROWD CHEERS]
Cheers echoed throughout the arena, the spectators’ faces full of curiosity and awe after witnessing Zane’s extraordinary display of power. The impossible had manifested before their eyes, challenging everything they believed about the limitations of human potential and the fundamental principles of Vitality manipulation.
Most of the disciples from the Skyreach Sect knew very little about Heaven and Earth, let alone the mysteries of sword intent and other advanced martial arts.
The questions were all over the place. What exactly was sword intent? How did it even work? Sword intent was on a whole other level, a concept so hard to grasp that mastering it was almost impossible.
The disciples didn’t understand Heaven and Earth.
But even so, most of them were Adepts, and a few had even reached the Soul Realm. So, they understood how Vitality worked pretty well.
After becoming an Adept, a person could gather Vitality in their core. Then, by focusing their mind, they could transform it into different elements.
If you could imagine it, your Vitality could become it.
For example, Richard turned his Vitality into dark ink for his brush. Others could change their Vitality into daggers or broadswords. Someone could even shape it into a house for people to live in.
[SFX: VITALITY MANIFESTATION]
When Zane’s six Shadow Phantoms materialized from his Vitality at the battle’s commencement, the spectators remained unimpressed. The crowd exchanged dismissive glances, considering these dark silhouettes trivial compared to Richard’s thirteen mighty words that loomed with menacing authority over the arena.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
An oppressive atmosphere descended upon the gathering as the deadly energy emanated from Richard’s thirteen killing words. Even seasoned warriors felt their skin prickle with unease simply looking at the ominous characters.
Whispers rippled through the audience—how could Zane, merely at the second level of the Adept Realm, possibly withstand such overwhelming force? His six Shadow Phantoms appeared hopelessly outmatched against the legendary Array of Thirteen Kills, their wispy forms seemingly fragile against Richard’s masterful creation.
Veterans of countless battles shook their heads in anticipation of Zane’s swift defeat, while his supporters clutched their hands in silent prayer.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
A single Shadow Phantom surged forward, intercepting the entire onslaught of the Array of Thirteen Kills with astonishing grace. The crowd fell into stunned silence as the phantom not only withstood the legendary technique but shattered three of the array’s Deathly Canticles with a single devastating strike.
It was inconceivable.
[MUSIC: GROWING MYSTERY]
Each of those Shadow Phantoms contained power comparable to a Mythic Weapon—artifacts that kingdoms would wage wars to possess. More astounding still, the phantom, after being reduced to fragments by the remaining forces of Richard’s array, simply coalesced back into its original form, wisps of purple-black energy knitting together as if the destruction had never occurred.
"His Vitality has turned into a solid being that reconstructs itself after being completely shattered," called out a veteran cultivator, his voice cracking with disbelief. "This is insane!"
"My God!" exclaimed a wide-eyed spectator, clutching the railing until her knuckles whitened. "Zane is way more powerful than he looks. How are those phantoms so strong? Is that guy really only at the second level of the Adept Realm?"
A smug-looking man nodded vigorously. "I’ve said all along that Zane was hiding his true capabilities. Nobody took him seriously because of his low cultivation level. If they had recognized his potential, like I did, they would have approached these battles with much greater caution!"
"This goes beyond all of that," argued a battle-scarred Adept. "His abilities are just plain weird! A Vitality construct that regenerates after destruction? In my decades as an Adept, I’ve witnessed countless techniques, and I’ve never seen anything remotely like it!"
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]
The crowd was buzzing.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE THEME]
In the Skytop Peak section, two tall men stood at the front. One of them, Finn, was eager to travel the world and make a name for himself. He might not be as powerful as Wynn right now, but if he kept aiming that high, he could definitely surpass Wynn’s own accomplishments one day.
The two of them stood silently, their eyes fixed on the battle as it grew more intense.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL BATTLE]
Facing Zane’s unstoppable Shadow Phantoms, Richard was starting to feel the pressure.
He waved his brush, and more Deathly Canticles shot from the tip, flying toward Zane to reinforce the Array of Thirteen Kills.
At first, it worked. The new Deathly Canticles smashed into the Shadow Phantoms, though some were destroyed in the process. But Richard had plenty of Vitality, and the words only cost him about ten percent of his total energy. That was nothing. He just needed to keep creating more Deathly Canticles to replace the ones that were lost.
[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]
But just a few moments later, Richard ran into a whole new problem.
One Shadow Phantom was busy holding off the main attack from the thirteen words, while the other five split into two groups. One group moved to intercept the new Deathly Canticles Richard was creating. Every few seconds, as he tried to send in reinforcements, two of the Shadow Phantoms would block their path.
Not a single new Deathly Canticle could get through to help the main array. They were completely outmatched and couldn’t even take down one Shadow Phantom on their own. In the end, the reinforcing Deathly Canticles were destroyed by the phantoms, which were left completely unharmed. It was obvious how powerful the Shadow Phantoms really were. They could only be destroyed by the combined, crushing force of the full thirteen-word array, and even then, they’d take a few of the Deathly Canticles down with them. One-on-one, a single Shadow Phantom could easily crush a single Deathly Canticle.
[SFX: HEART BEATING RAPIDLY]
Richard’s heart sank. He knew that if the Array of Thirteen Kills couldn’t get reinforcements, it would fall apart, and the battle would be over in seconds.
[SFX: WHOOSHING ATTACK]
The remaining phantoms split into two lines. As Richard watched in horror, three of them broke away and rushed straight toward him.
