Ep 472 - Fair Game

Word count: 995

[AMBIENT: TENSE EXAMINATION ROOM]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The examiner hesitated, his gaze shifting between the Scrying Slate and Tia's faint, unreadable smile. Her reaction sent warning bells ringing through his mind—that serene expression was never a good sign. Should he really pull Zane from the magic array? The decision weighed heavily on him; each second of indecision felt like an eternity.

After a long moment of deliberation, he steeled himself and turned to face her directly. His voice carried a forced authority that barely masked his uncertainty. "Zane's actions have completely thrown off the balance of the pre-qualifying matches. The entire structure of the test has been compromised. He has to be disqualified." He paused, searching her face for any hint of agreement. "What are your thoughts?"

Michael Nash and the old man exchanged a knowing look. Idiot, their expressions said in perfect unison. They both subtly backed away from the examiner, wanting no part of whatever fallout was about to occur. Michael even took an extra step toward the door, his survival instincts finely honed after decades in the Guild.

Meanwhile, Tia's eyes remained glued to the Scrying Slate, her focus absolute. She watched Zane methodically mow down wave after wave of purple-crystal centipedes. The corner of her mouth twitched—whether in amusement or irritation, no one could tell. Her fingers drummed once against her armrest, the only sign of emotion she permitted herself to show.

[SFX: SUDDEN TEMPERATURE DROP]

[MUSIC: MOUNTING DREAD]

As the examiner's words hung in the air, Tia's gentle smile didn't just fade—it twisted into something cold and sharp, like a blade emerging from silk. The temperature in the room plummeted. A deep, bone-penetrating cold spread through the chamber, having nothing to do with the weather outside. The entire palace transformed into an icebox. Even the guards stationed by the main doors began rubbing their arms vigorously, exchanging confused glances as they wondered where the sudden, unnatural draft originated.

"What did you just say?" Each word dropped from her lips like a shard of ice, slicing through the quiet room. She finally tore her gaze from the slate, fixing the examiner with a stare that could freeze blood in veins. The shift in her attention felt like a predator acknowledging prey. "Is there some rule in the World Merchants' Guild that I'm not aware of? Some obscure regulation you'd like to enlighten me about?"

The examiner's face paled. He shook his head hastily, though sweat had begun forming at his temples despite the frigid air. His hands trembled slightly at his sides. "No, but what he did was essentially cheating," he insisted, his voice wavering between conviction and fear. "It completely unbalances the competition. If this continues, once he passes the first stage, no other warrior will stand a chance against him in the second round." He swallowed hard, knowing he was digging himself deeper but unable to stop. The words tumbled out in a desperate rush of justification.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION BUILD]

Tia's sneer cut across the room like a blade, her eyes transformed into chips of ice. "Oh, really?" She took a single step forward, her presence suddenly overwhelming the space between them. "Have you ever heard the expression…?"

"What expression?" The examiner's demand came out sharper than he intended, his voice cracking slightly at the edges. He knew perfectly well he was outmatched—in status, in power, in every conceivable way. He'd just poked the bear, and now the bear was circling him with predatory intent. Yet something in him refused to yield. His jaw tightened, and he straightened his spine despite the tremor running through his limbs. He believed, with every fiber of his being, that he stood on the right side of this argument. That conviction was the only thing keeping him upright under the weight of her glacial stare.

[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

"If it isn't explicitly forbidden, then it's fair game," Tia said coldly. "You never made a rule against this, so all Zane did was use a loophole. Your loophole. You're the one who made the mistake here, not him. How can you justify disqualifying him now? You can talk about the ‘greater good’ all you want, but let's be real. This is about your oversight throwing off the balance, and now you want to make Zane the scapegoat. That isn't just unfair—it's selfish," she snapped.

Her outburst left the examiner speechless.

She was right. It was a classic case of 'if there's no rule against it, it's not a crime.' He'd simply overlooked this possibility when designing the parameters for the magic array; technically, Zane hadn't broken a single rule.

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY HUMMING]

Even Michael and the old man had to admit Tia had a point. But so did the examiner. The magic array was built around a careful power curve. A level-ten tower gave archers the punch of a mid-stage Inner-Core Realm warrior. Level eleven was equivalent to a late-stage Inner-Core warrior. And level twelve? That was on par with an early-stage Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior.

Normally, contestants would be lucky to hit level ten before facing the final wave. Zane had already built a level-twelve tower before the end was even in sight. The examiner’s worry was justified; Zane was already in a league of his own.

[SFX: HEAVY RHYTHMIC THUMPING]

[SFX: GROUND RUMBLING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL ORCHESTRA]

Suddenly, the very ground began to tremble. In the viewing room, everyone staggered to keep their balance as three colossal silhouettes appeared on the horizon of the Scrying Slate.

"Here they come!" Zane shouted, his expression hardening as he braced for the fight.

[AMBIENT: DEEP OCEAN RUMBLE]

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

His enemies were three giant obsidian crabs.
Deep-sea behemoths, each one stood two hundred feet tall, their shells like moving hills. Their strength was terrifying; every ponderous step covered a hundred feet of ground.
Zane knew the lore—in theory, they could grow indefinitely. With enough resources, they could become titans thousands of feet tall, capable of crushing mountains with a single step or draining entire seas. But he'd never heard of any actually existing on that scale in his world.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

And all three of them were heading straight for him.
