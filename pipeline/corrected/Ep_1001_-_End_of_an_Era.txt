Ep 1001 - End of an Era

[AMBIENT: IMPERIAL PALACE]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

The sky lit up with silver streaks of light right after the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom called his meeting. Only about half the Soul Sea Realm masters even bothered to stick around, and they were already streaking toward the imperial palace to answer his summons. Most of them were on good terms with the Emperor, with a few even coming from his own family.

[SFX: WHOOSHING ASCENSION]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHOIR]

Dozens of brilliant lights scattered across the sky as the Soul Sea Realm warriors ascended. It was a breathtaking sight, leaving citizens across the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms staring up in awe.

They’d already handed off their titles, settled their family affairs, and given their students final instructions. Now, the Soul Sea Realm masters were free to ascend to the Upper World without a thing to worry about.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

The Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom, War King Wind, Scott, and War King York watched with heavy hearts as their people ascended to the Upper World ahead of them. Even though they were happy for them, they couldn’t help but feel a pang of jealousy. They were being left behind, tied down by duty. They didn’t have that kind of freedom. They were Emperors who couldn’t just pass down a list of instructions; they had to make sure their kingdoms wouldn’t fall apart the second they were gone.

It would be a long, long time before they could ascend without a care in the world.

[MUSIC: CONFLICTED TENSION]

Not everyone’s conflict was about duty. On the main continent, Amber had watched the ascension passage open with feelings she couldn’t shake.

She wanted to ascend — her talent was undeniable, and she knew it. But ascending meant returning to the Demon Night, and returning meant facing the consequences of defying the Queen of Punishment’s direct order.

She had been commanded to kill Zane and Lavender. She had refused. There was no version of going home that ended well for her.

She was trapped — too ambitious to stay, too exposed to leave.

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

War King York was especially pissed off. Almost every single one of the York Divine Kingdom’s Soul Sea Realm masters had chosen to ascend. Only one stayed behind—his own brother.

It didn’t help that they’d all taken off the second the passageway was fixed, completely blowing off his summons. He was tempted to threaten them, to swear he’d wipe out their entire clans if they left without his permission. But he held his tongue. He knew he’d have to ascend eventually. What if the enemies he made today came looking for revenge against his clan after he was gone? Risking his entire clan over a moment of rage just wasn’t worth it.

[MUSIC: DARK BROODING]

Still, he couldn’t help but grit his teeth at the sight of the glowing sky. He’d never been a kind ruler and had always treated his kingdom’s Soul Sea Realm warriors harshly. Now that they had a way out from under his thumb, it was no surprise they weren’t about to stick around and take his orders. They’d chosen to escape his grip the first chance they got.

[AMBIENT: VAST KINGDOM]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

Far more Soul Sea Realm warriors chose to stay behind in the other three divine kingdoms, but the numbers were still shockingly low. The Divine Kingdom Continent was about to witness a major power shift among the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms. The age of Soul Sea Realm masters was over.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TRANSITION]

Now, it was time for the Life-and-Death Realm warriors to take their place.

From a distance — through the Memory of the World’s Will — Zane watched it all unfold. He cast his perception across the four Divine Kingdoms and confirmed what he’d suspected: nearly every Soul Sea Realm master had already gone. Only five or six of the younger ones remained, still bound by obligations they hadn’t yet settled. The age of the most powerful warriors the Divine Kingdoms had ever produced was over in the space of a single day.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL FOREST]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL STRINGS]

Meanwhile, Zane’s power continued its steady climb, and in the Soulcradle Wood, Lavender’s soul was slowly knitting itself back together. She was still deep in an unconscious sleep, recovering, but not yet returned.

As for the girl...

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

Zane had arranged for her to speak with the Cobalt Dragon a few times. According to the dragon, there was no way to break the Soul Sealing Spell here in the Lower World. She’d have to return to the Upper World for that.

[MUSIC: SOMBER PIANO]

The problem was that it would be impossible for Zane to bring her with him when he finally ascended.

There was an easy way to send her back—through the Dragon Soar Arena—but both the girl and the Cobalt Dragon shot that idea down fast.

Even though the girl was a dragon herself, she refused to go back, something about some bad blood with her own kind. Once Zane heard that, he dropped it.

[AMBIENT: SOLEMN HALL]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

He had no choice but to place her body deep within Cloud Hall, leaving it under heavy guard.

He’d just have to ascend to the Upper World on his own, find a solution, and then come back for her someday.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]

The old man’s pill was still working through Zane’s system, and it showed. His cultivation climbed at a pace that would have seemed impossible even a year ago.

He faced his fourth Life-and-Death Tribulation just three months after his third.

As his power grew, the time between these trials got shorter and shorter.

[MUSIC: PEACEFUL MELODY]

Honestly, Zane almost wanted to pump the brakes on his own progress. This was a peaceful time in his life, a rare chance to relax and spend time with his ladies. But there was no stopping his lightning-fast progress.

He told himself he’d try to hold off on ascending, even after he broke through to the Soul Sea Realm.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

But after his fifth Life-and-Death Tribulation, it felt like the world itself was starting to reject him.
