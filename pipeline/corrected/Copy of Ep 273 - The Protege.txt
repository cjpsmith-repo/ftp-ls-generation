Ep 273 - The Protegé
Word count: 1284
[MUSIC: EPIC FANTASY THEME]
Within the Skyreach Sect, Sir Kenneth reigned supreme, unchallenged in his authority and power. If the incredibly talented Kane Novak hadn’t seized control of the Palace in the Capital, the Sect’s formidable leader would have no doubt commanded the entire Eastern Territory with an iron fist. His mere presence inspired reverence and fear among disciples and masters alike.
The hierarchy beneath him flowed with rigid precision—the vice leader stood as his right hand, followed by the council of elders who deliberated on matters of importance, and then the saints who enforced the Sect’s will. Each position carried its own weight of responsibility and privilege.
In truth, a saint’s actual influence and capabilities varied with age and experience. Those whose talents failed to shine brightly enough found themselves stuck at the Skyreach Sect, their ambitions forever stunted. The prestigious Cloud Hall, with its ancient halls and whispered secrets, disdained mediocrity, opening its doors only to those whose brilliance could not be denied. Many saints harbored bitter resentment at being overlooked, their frustration simmering beneath polite veneers.
Zane’s history with the saints was etched in his memory like a fresh wound. Saint Augustus himself had overseen his trial, his piercing gaze evaluating his every move, his voice cool and calculating as he pronounced judgment. The memory of his scrutiny still sent an uncomfortable chill down his spine.
Naturally, this caused Zane to be on high alert, knowing that his new opponent was one of Saint Augustus’ protégés. This knowledge weighed heavily on him, sharpening his vigilance to knife-edge precision as he prepared to face one of Saint Augustus’ personal disciples.
His muscles tensed beneath his skin, while his grip tightened around his weapon until his knuckles blanched.
A cold certainty settled in his gut—this adversary would not merely fight as an individual, but as an extension of Saint Augustus himself, embodying his techniques, expectations, and ruthless ambition.
Such a disciple carried twice the threat: skilled in his own right, yet further empowered by the shadow of his formidable mentor looming behind every move. Zane drew a measured breath, steadying the rapid drumming of his heart against his ribs as he centered his focus on the challenge ahead.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
Johanson wasn’t wearing the purple robe of a personal disciple, but a simple, solid black coat. He was thin with dark skin, and as he stood across from Zane, he looked like a frail kid with no discernible aura.
If the crowd didn’t know who he was, they would’ve written him off as just some fragile student based on looks alone. The unassuming boy didn’t look at all like the top disciple of the Skyreach Sect.
Everyone gives off an aura. A child’s is playful, a general’s is murderous, a scholar’s is refined. It’s a natural projection tied to a person’s inner strength and life force. Through their training, warriors develop a much stronger life force than regular people—they can be as tough as a bull, and their auras project that power.
But Johanson could completely suppress his. He obviously knew a secret technique to conceal it.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
[AMBIENT: ARENA ATMOSPHERE]
Stepping into the arena, Zane summoned Vitality to his body. He wasn’t afraid of guys who were all bark and no bite, like Adrian. Zane had seen right through him. But this Johanson... he was a complete mystery.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE THEME]
The second the referee declared the battle open, Johanson let out a hollow, eerie laugh. Without another word, he started the fight.
[SFX: SWIRLING VITALITY]
Black Vitality swirled around his body, streaming behind him like tattered ribbons as he moved. Floating in mid-air, he looked like a ghost.
As Johanson flew around Zane, he left black shadows hanging in the air. Unlike Zane’s Shadow Phantoms, which were mere silhouettes, these were perfect copies of Johanson himself.
`His Vitality Avatar is mimicking his body,` Zane thought. `This has to be an illusion or assassination attunement method.` He knew what Johanson was up to. The shadows blended perfectly with Johanson’s black coat and could probably even act as a shield. He was trying to control the atmosphere of the battlefield.
Zane fixed his focus and pushed his Soul Power out, locking onto Johanson's position. He didn't dare let go. Johanson knew it too — and his response was to move faster, laying down shadow after shadow, filling the arena until the chaos made it nearly impossible to track him.
Then, after about ten breaths, Johanson simply vanished.
[SFX: PRESENCE DISAPPEARING]
One moment Zane had him. The next — nothing. The divine lock slipped, and Johanson was gone, somewhere inside the web of shadows. Zane's jaw tightened. He had no clean counter to this. All he could do was wait for Johanson to make the first move.
What Zane didn't know was that Johanson hadn't just come here to win. Saint Augustus had given him a quiet order before the match: kill Zane. Make it look like an accident. Fists and feet have no eyes — it happened in the arena sometimes. Johanson had accepted without hesitation. Not out of loyalty. Out of appetite. He wanted to watch Zane struggle and show fear before the end. That was the part he enjoyed most.
The only thing bothering him right now was that Zane wasn't afraid.
It was a classic assassin’s tactic to trap an opponent. When a group of them closes in on a target at night, some will let out ghostly cries to disorient their prey, making for an easy kill in seconds.
There was a reason for that. Johanson came from the Shadow Group — an ancient assassin bloodline that had once terrorized the Eastern Territory. Their branch here had been dismantled three hundred years ago, when the Burning Sky Dynasty joined forces with the Skyreach Sect and the Seven Great Houses to wipe them out. But the group’s headquarters still existed somewhere on the other side of the continent, and Johanson intended to return there one day. The Skyreach Sect was just a stepping stone.
He was the strongest assassin the Shadow Group had produced in three hundred years.
[SFX: GHOSTLY ECHOING LAUGHTER]
Johanson let out another cold laugh, and the dark shadows echoed it, making it impossible to pinpoint his location by sound alone.
"So you can laugh? I thought you were mute!" Zane called out.
At that, Johanson’s laughter died. Was he calling me an idiot? Trying to bait me? Fury flashed through him.
[SFX: RUSHING PHANTOMS]
Just then, Zane made his move. Six Shadow Phantoms appeared behind him, rushing straight for Johanson.
[SFX: DISSOLVING BODY]
Johanson took a sharp breath, his body dissolving into a black shadow just as one of Zane’s phantoms zipped past his ear.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
It was a dangerously close call.
Zane smiled. "Too bad. You’ve trained well enough to hide yourself, but the second you got pissed at me, you gave away your position!"
The kid’s senses were sharp. He’d pinpointed Johanson’s true location just from that flicker of emotion.
"Nice," all the black shadows of Johanson said in unison. "You’ve got some skills, but you’re no match for me."
"If you really think I’m no match for you, then stop floating around looking for an opening," Zane shot back with a grin. "Come out and fight me. Should be easy, right? But you won’t. You’re a coward. So why should I believe for a second that I can’t beat you?"
[SFX: HEARTBEAT INTENSIFIES]
Johanson’s anger flared again, but he wrestled it back down. What was happening to him? He was a master of staying calm. Why was this kid’s obvious bait getting under his skin so easily?
[AMBIENT: MENTAL FOCUS]
He forced himself to be still, mentally reciting the Assassin’s Ten Commandments.
A true assassin must guard against arrogance, carelessness, and anger.
The line between killing and being killed was a razor-thin mental game.
And he was here to kill.
