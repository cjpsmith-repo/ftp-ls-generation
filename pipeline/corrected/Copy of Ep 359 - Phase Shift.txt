Ep 359 - Phase Shift
Word count: 1101

[AMBIENT: QUIET DISCUSSION HALL]

Zane was too powerful, and Toby couldn't let it stand. But he'd have to play this carefully. 

He looked at the group of elders, a look of calculated composure on his face. “You know, Zane is a very lucky kid. With talent like his, he'll be up for a Nimbus Expanse Pill if he can get himself into the top five in the Cloud Road Trial. I will personally put his name in for one when this is all over.”  

His words reverberated through the chamber, leaving everyone—Sir Kenneth included—utterly dumbfounded, their expressions frozen in disbelief.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

A Nimbus Expanse Pill.

This coveted treasure was the ultimate secret of Cloud Hall. Even accomplished stewards like Kenneth and Eddy had rarely glimpsed such a prize; they were exclusively reserved for those who'd made a major contribution to the Hall.

The pill was legendary—it boosted a warrior's fundamental power without any side effects, stabilising their core and helping them shatter training plateaus that might otherwise halt their progress indefinitely.

[SFX: HEARTBEAT INTENSIFIES]

A sharp, bitter resentment stabbed at Sir Kenneth's heart as he struggled to maintain his dignified expression.

For countless seasons, he had dedicated himself tirelessly to finding talented disciples for Cloud Hall, scouring the provinces and enduring countless disappointments. And what did he have to show for it?  The rewards he deserved—the acknowledgment of his contributions—were always just beyond reach, dangled before him like phantom promises.

Now, before Kenneth could even claim credit for his most remarkable discovery, Toby was already offering Zane a treasure that Kenneth himself had coveted for years. The audacity stung like salt in an open wound.

Though Kenneth had felt genuine pride watching Zane's meteoric rise—had even developed a paternal affection for the boy he'd discovered and mentored—he couldn't suppress the intense pang of jealousy that twisted in his chest. As a warrior who had fought his way to the Inner-Core Realm through sheer determination, Kenneth craved that pill with an intensity that bordered on physical pain.

[SFX: SOFT CHUCKLE]

Toby chuckled, seeming to read his mind. "Kenneth, if Zane makes it into the top five, I'll make sure to report your contribution in finding such a brilliant disciple. Cloud Hall won't forget about you."

Relief and excitement washed over Kenneth. "Thank you!"

The other leaders present kept their faces perfectly straight, but inside, they were all scoffing. The top five? In his dreams. No disciple from a Tier Two Sect has ever placed that high in the Cloud Road Trial.

There were five Tier Two Sects under Cloud Hall's watch: the Skyreach Sect, the Providence Sect, the Luminous Sect, the Duskfall Sect, and the Crimson Sect. Each had sent several disciples to the trial this year, making for about forty or fifty contestants in total. Cloud Hall itself was sending in just as many to compete.

But the skill gap between the Cloud Hall disciples and the ones from the Tier Two Sects was a chasm.

It was nearly impossible for a disciple from a Tier Two Sect to even make the top ten, let alone the top five. In the last few hundred years, only two had even come close—one from the Luminous Sect and one from the Providence Sect—managing to snag eighth and ninth place, respectively. Aside from them, the top ten was always a clean sweep by Cloud Hall's own.

The last time there was a Cloud Road Trial, Kenneth had bragged that his guy, Evan, was a shoo-in for champion. After Evan bombed, Kenneth became the butt of every joke for the next three years.

Kenneth stared at his fellow elders and wondered how his miraculous protegé was faring on the other side of the whirlpool. Whatever you do, kid, don't let me down. I can't go through that kind of humiliation again.

"What is he doing?" Toby asked.

"The Cloud Road Trial is a fragmented space," Kenneth said. "There are shards of space floating everywhere inside it. That boy is comprehending spatial laws."

[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]

Even Toby went quiet at that.

Before the silence could hold, Lady Sigmund of Luminous Sect had heard enough. "Perfect sword intent, and now spatial laws?" Her voice was sharp. "Kenneth, I'm warning you — stop before you embarrass yourself the way you did with Evan."

Kenneth smiled pleasantly. "No one's forcing you to believe me. But when we came through the space storm together—"

"That's enough." Toby's voice cut across the room. "He's moving."

Every eye snapped back to Zane.

[AMBIENT: WASTELAND]

[MUSIC: GROWING MYSTERY]

For his part, Zane was busy trying to figure out this strange new world. What are these lines? he wondered. They can't be spatial rifts. If they were, this place would be unsurvivable.

He crept toward one of the lines, hesitantly reaching out a hand to touch it.

His fingers passed right through. Nothing. He could see it, plain as day, but there was nothing to feel.

[SFX: MAGICAL TINGLING]

But then, after holding his hand there for a few seconds, a strange sensation prickled at his skin.

[SFX: WHOOSHING TELEPORT]

Zane's vision blurred as his body was yanked along the length of the line.

[AMBIENT: SPACE-TIME DISTORTION]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WONDER]

The line itself was only about a foot long—roughly the length of a single stride. But in that tiny space, Zane felt like he was moving with impossible speed, free and untethered. He was moving so fast it looked like a dozen copies of him flickered in and out of existence within that one-foot space.

But they weren't afterimages, not the trick-of-the-light you get from high speed. These ghostly copies of him appeared and vanished all at once.

His feet hadn't moved an inch, yet his whole body had slid forward a foot, following the path of the line. It was a bizarre feeling, like he'd become a ghost and just performed a Phase-Shift through space.

So weird… but so cool, Zane thought. Stepping away from the line's influence, he immediately spotted another one just ahead. This one was about half a foot long. The moment his fingers grazed it, he zipped forward another half-foot, just like before.

The more he looked, the more lines he found. One by one, he touched them, letting them pull him through little pockets of space.

He wasn't covering much ground this way; each line was barely a foot long, and he had to actively hunt for the next one. But he couldn't stop. The whole thing was just too fascinating, his mind already racing with the possibilities.

[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]

And Zane had no idea that back outside the trial, his strange little dance had left the leaders of every Sect—as well as Elder Toby of Cloud Hall—absolutely speechless.
