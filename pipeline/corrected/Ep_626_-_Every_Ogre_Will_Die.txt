Ep 626 - Every Ogre Will Die

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Zane’s gaze had been locked on Maurice from the moment it all went down, and Maurice’s eyes hadn’t left him since.
[SFX: LIQUID ABSORPTION]
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
The Tower of Sin absorbed Bradley's dark blood instantly, leaving no trace. Within a day, it would consume his body too—sustaining itself by devouring everything left behind.
This process had been the Ogres' greatest advantage: kill opponents, absorb their Lucky Light, let the tower consume the remains to recharge. The perfect cycle that built their power.
But now, everything had changed. The tower's power was Zane's to command.
Maurice finally understood how Zane held the power of life and death over every single person inside. He saw with terrifying clarity that the Ogres had absolutely no leverage. No room to negotiate. Nothing.
Zane finally broke the long silence, his voice calm. "So, do you accept my terms?"
[MUSIC: BITTER REALIZATION]
A bitter smile found its way onto Maurice’s face. He’d always seen himself as the master of the Tower of Sin. Even after Esther Sterling had defeated him, he was still the leader of the ten strongest Ogre War Lords, completely unfazed by the Demon Night. He’d run his operations here flawlessly. Every time he returned to the Ogres’ two sacred strongholds, the masters from the Soul Sea Realm showered him with rewards.
But now? Standing in front of a kid who was just a level-one War General, he felt utterly powerless, like all the strength had been siphoned right out of him.
He knew exactly what would happen if the Ogres were kicked out. No matter how much the Soul Sea Realm masters valued him now, he’d have to face the wrath of his entire race. He wouldn’t just be a failure; he’d be a traitor, despised by both the Flaming Place and the Dread Place for all time.
But what could he do? How could he possibly fight back?
Sure, he might have been a little stronger than Bradley, but they were both top-tier War Lords. Maurice was ranked second out of one hundred and twenty-eight seats; Bradley had been fourth. The difference was negligible. He had nothing left.
Maurice was quiet for a long moment. Then he said, "I'm sorry. I cannot accept your conditions."
[MUSIC: MOUNTING DREAD]
"Then I'll kill every Ogre in this Tower," Zane said. His voice hadn't changed. No anger, no bluster — just a flat statement of fact.
The Ogre War Lords went very still. They believed him completely.
The Demon Night War Lords exchanged quiet glances. They were thinking the same thing — how close they'd come to being on the wrong side of this. When Zane had first appeared on the tenth floor, and the Ogres had moved against him, the Demon Night had held back, waiting to see which way the wind blew. If Lavender hadn't appeared when she did, they might have started targeting him themselves. The thought was sobering.
And the four human seat holders — Fabian, Jarrod, and the others — were still somewhere far away in their own minds. For hundreds of years, the humans had endured in this Tower, heads down, taking what scraps they were given. And now, because of one person, everything had been turned upside down. They hadn't processed it yet. They weren't sure they could.
Maurice played his last card, the bitterness still in his voice, but the edge sharpening beneath it. "You can expel us, and the Tower will be yours. We can't touch you here, Zane. But the Ogres' Sacred Land still stands outside these walls. And the Violet Heart Sanctuary — your people's only home on this continent — will be flattened the moment we leave."
[MUSIC: DARK THREATENING PULSE]
The four human War Lords went rigid. Maurice was right about that much. The Violet Heart Sanctuary had no answer for the combined strength of the Flaming Place and the Dread Place. Not even close.
Fabian's mouth opened. He shut it again. What right did he have to ask anything of Zane? Zane owed them nothing. The Sanctuary had never done a single thing for him — it had been the other way around from the start.
He swallowed his words and stared at the floor.
Zane's expression didn't flicker. He raised one finger. "I've heard enough. I'll give you one month. After that, any Ogre I find in this Tower dies."
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
Fabian and Jarrod's faces drained of color. The Violet Heart Sanctuary was in trouble.
A faint smile played on Zane's lips. "I'm sure you've done your research on me. Since I'm not from the main continent, what does the Violet Heart Sanctuary's fate matter to me? Besides—"
"Besides what?" Maurice pressed.
"Do you really think the Flaming Place and the Dread Place can actually make a move against the Violet Heart Sanctuary?" Zane asked, his smile growing wider.
Maurice's eyes flashed. He suddenly realized what he'd overlooked — the one force on the continent that outweighed both his sacred places combined.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
Just then, a flicker of red light pulsed in the air, and Lavender appeared at Zane's side. Instantly, every top-level War Lord from the Demon Night dropped to one knee.
[MUSIC: REVERENT ORCHESTRAL]
Even Esther Sterling knelt, her head bowed. "My Queen," she said, her voice full of reverence.
Zane gave Lavender a small smile before turning his attention back to Maurice. "You forgot about the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary."
[MUSIC: EPIC CONTEMPLATIVE]
The main continent was home to fourteen great races, and each had its own sacred place.
But not all of them were created equal. The humans’ Violet Heart Sanctuary, for example, was only considered a sixth-grade sacred place because it had just a few warriors who’d reached the Soul Sea Realm. Two thousand years ago, the humans didn’t even have a sanctuary, just the Violet Heart Order. Theirs was considered the weakest of all.
The Ogres’ Flaming Place and Dread Place, on the other hand, were at the very peak of the sixth-grade sanctuaries. Combined, their power was almost enough to qualify as a seventh-grade.
[MUSIC: POWERFUL DRAMATIC]
Almost. Because there was only one true seventh-grade sanctuary on the entire continent: the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary. And the sole reason it had been promoted was Amber.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]
And Amber had promised Zane her full support.
