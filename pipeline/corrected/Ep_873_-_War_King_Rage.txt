Ep 873 - War King Rage

Word count: 1087

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Every Soul Sea Realm master on the island had their eyes fixed on the pickax in Zane’s hand. 

None of them could be sure if it was the Black Star Pickax, the one that had been sealed inside the golden leaf. But it sure looked like it.

War King York was the first to react. One of his saint-level geniuses, seeing all the powerful Soul Sea Realm warriors fixated on Zane, had the presence of mind to quickly send York a private message with his Vitality. 

York already had his suspicions, but he couldn’t quite wrap his head around the idea that Zane had actually pulled it off. The message was all it took; York’s doubt evaporated, replaced by a surge of pure, unadulterated greed.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Before any of the other Soul Sea Realm warriors could even process what was happening, War King York vanished in a flash, reappearing in an instant right beside Zane.

He was so fast that by the time the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom and War King Wind registered his movement, the pickax was already in his hands. A look of pure, unadulterated joy lit up his face.

Zane, perched on the pyramid’s edge, just watched the emperor’s giddy display with a deadpan expression. He had to physically stop himself from rolling his eyes.

 Zane had no interest in provoking the ruler into a real fight. Besides, it didn’t matter. The divine weapon had already accepted him as its master. York could hold it all he wanted, but it was completely useless to him.

“The Black Star Pickax! It’s really the Black Star Pickax!” York laughed, his voice booming with triumph.

Countless treasures were hidden in the leaves of the top three layers, but the Soul Cores were absolutely vital to the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms. Until now, their only real hope had been the Stone-Eater Bugs, and those little critters rarely bothered to climb that high to gnaw on the ores. With this pickax, York could mine the very top leaf himself.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

“Is that really it? The Black Star Pickax?”

“How did he even get it out?”

“You’re telling me Zane dug that out of the thirty-fifth-floor leaf? How is that even possible?”

The murmurs rippled through the crowd of powerful warriors. So many of them had tried and failed to claim that legendary weapon; seeing it now, real and tangible, left them in stunned disbelief.

War King Wind turned to Fiona. “Was it really him? Did he actually smash the golden leaf on the thirty-fifth floor?”

Fiona nodded. “We saw it ourselves.”

War King Wind said nothing. He was starting to wish he’d skipped the Secret Land this year entirely. The pickax was extraordinary — but in York’s hands, after years of the Little Monster tipping the balance, this was the last thing any of them needed.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

York clutched the pickax, his initial excitement giving way to a hint of doubt. He stared at it for a long moment, tracing its lines. Then, a strange thought occurred to him. He tried to pour his Vitality into the weapon, but nothing happened. It was completely inert.

He dripped his blood onto the head. Nothing. He pressed his soul into the metal. Nothing. He tried every method he knew, one after another — and not one of them worked.

“What’s going on?” he frowned. Could it be a fake? But it looked and felt so real. It had to be the genuine article. So why couldn’t he use it?

His eyes snapped to Zane.

Fiona had watched York’s attempts with growing frustration. She opened her mouth — but Zane caught her eye.

“Don’t be hasty, Fiona.” His voice was calm. “Some things will belong to whoever they belong to. You can’t run away from it.”

Fiona blinked. Then a small smile crossed her face. The pickax had already chosen him. Of course it had.

“Zane, what’s the meaning of this?” York demanded.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PAUSE]

Zane just shrugged.

“Did you make the Black Star Pickax recognize you as its master?” York’s voice had gone very quiet.

“Yes,” Zane said. “So what?”

“Hand over the method. Or I’ll take your life.”

Zane laughed. “How interesting. I dug it out myself. It recognized me. You snatched it from me — and now you want me to hand over the binding method?” 

He tilted his head. “You’re the Emperor of a Divine Kingdom. It’s rare to see someone quite so shameless.”

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]

For a split second, York wanted nothing more than to strike Zane down where he stood. But he held back. He knew that if the pickax didn’t accept him as its master, it was just a useless hunk of metal, and Zane was the only one who knew its secrets. Attacking him was out; Wind and the Harlen Emperor would never allow it. Threats wouldn’t work on a kid like Zane, either.

After a tense moment, York’s tone shifted. “Tell me how to make this thing accept me as its master,” he said, trying to sound persuasive. “Do that, and the York Divine Kingdom will grant your every wish for the rest of your life! We’ll train you, sparing no expense. All our martial houses, all our secret training grounds—they’ll be open to you!”

Threats had failed. Bribery was all he had left.

Hearing this, the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom couldn’t help but roll his eyes. Did York really think Zane was born yesterday? The Harlen Divine Kingdom could already offer him everything York was promising; the difference in their resources was negligible. There was no way Zane would trade a divine-class weapon for such an empty promise.

“York, give it back to him,” the Harlen Emperor said calmly. “Since the Black Star Pickax has already chosen its owner, we can simply have Zane help us mine the Soul Cores.”

“Hmph. Why would I, a War King, need help from some kid?” York snorted, his grip on the pickax tightening. He clearly had no intention of returning it.

[SFX: FLAMES ERUPTING]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

Suddenly, a furious roar ripped from War King Wind as flames erupted at his feet. The fire twisted and morphed into swift firebirds that began circling him—his Firebird Domain, fully unleashed.

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FIRE]

 “You might not need his help, but we do! We’ve put up with you for years, and you’ve only gotten more unbearable! I’d rather see that pickax in the hands of an outsider than let you keep it! York, give it back to Zane right now, or we fight!”

This time, War King Wind was absolutely furious.
