Ep 1097 - Brother

Word count: 1088

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA ATMOSPHERE]

The burly man’s pride was a fortress. He just couldn’t stomach the idea that his opponent was some fifth-level warrior from the Life-and-Death Realm.

A kid, really. Not even a man. He almost scoffed out loud. But then he remembered—this kid was a prodigy from the Thirteen Palaces, and anyone who’d survived all twenty-four qualifying rounds just to get here was supposed to be the best of the best. Underestimating him would be a fatal mistake.

So how in the world was he only at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm?

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

“Don’t underestimate him,” Adrian advised. “I watched him through all twenty-four rounds. We haven’t seen half of what he’s capable of. If I had to guess, he’s only using about thirty percent of his real power.”

“Thirty percent? Against warriors from the Soul Sea Realm?” the burly man asked, his voice thick with shock.

“That’s why I’m telling you, pick the woman,” Adrian said flatly.

[SFX: CROWD CHEERING]

Both Zane and Layla had crushed their twenty-four matches, tearing through the competition like a pair of battering rams. Adrian’s last-minute tactical shifts had thrown them a few curveballs, but they’d both adapted and come out on top.

Zane was a quiet, low-key fighter, all business with his longsword. Layla, on the other hand, was all flash and flair, putting on a hell of a show for the crowd with her unique techniques. Their styles told a story. Adrian figured Zane’s understated approach meant he was hiding his true strength, which made him dangerously unpredictable. Layla, however, put all her cards on the table. That kind of predictability could get you killed in a real fight.

The Palace leaders and even the Lord of the Sunset World seemed to agree with his assessment. They were all stronger and wiser than Adrian, but his raw intuition still managed to impress them.

Too bad the burly man wasn’t listening.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]

“Nah, I think I’ll take the boy,” he snickered. “A warrior with that kind of strength, still stuck in the Life-and-Death Realm? He’s worth a closer look.”

Adrian’s expression darkened. Honestly, he couldn’t care less about these so-called prodigies. His plate was already full: beat Layla, free the big guy, and find the secret treasures of the Dark World. The whole point of this trial was to win, period. Zane was clearly a wild card, and Adrian wasn’t sure they could afford that kind of risk.

The burly man had been a top dog in his old life, before everything went sideways and he was banished to the Villain World. He’d spent his whole life chasing power, always fighting people stronger than him. This was the exact opposite. His own power hadn’t grown much in the resource-starved Villain World, but he sure as hell wasn’t afraid of some kid from the Life-and-Death Realm.

He looked Zane up and down, sizing him up. Zane returned the favor.

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

To Zane, the man’s power felt dense and solid, but layered over it was a thick, unyielding pride. No doubt about it, this was a hardened fighter with serious strength. Zane let out a quiet sigh. This is going to be a tough fight, he thought.

[AMBIENT: ADJACENT ARENA]

On the platform next door, Layla wasn’t in a much better mood. Round after round, Zane had stayed right on her heels—and somewhere past the twentieth round, he’d actually started pulling ahead, finishing some matches twice as fast as she did. If they both made it through this final round, the whole thing would end in a draw. Layla didn’t love that idea.

So when the light on her own platform began to gather, she found herself hoping—almost willing it—for someone strong. Someone who’d actually give her a fight.

[SFX: ENERGY CONDENSING]

A figure took shape across from her. His clothes hung in rags, and a jagged scar cut across his forehead. A cold, gloomy aura rolled off him in waves—the kind that made the hair on the back of your neck stand up, like being watched by something that was already dead.

“A Ghost cultivator?” Layla’s confident expression flickered for just a second. Cultivators who walked that path were unpredictable, and their methods were nasty.

The man grinned, all teeth. “Well, would you look at that. My luck’s turned around—my opponent’s just a little girl.”

[MUSIC: MOUNTING DANGER]

Seven death-row prisoners had been brought up from the Villain World for this final round, but only two of them would get a shot at freedom. The burly man had already claimed Zane, which left this one for Layla by default. He’d paid dearly just to be standing here—and if he won his freedom, Adrian would be owed a steep debt in return.

Layla’s composure settled back into place almost as quickly as it had slipped. If her opponent was this strong, then whoever Zane was about to face had to be strong too. That was exactly what she’d wanted.

She studied the Ghost cultivator a moment longer. Dangerous, yes. But not unbeatable.

Curious, Layla let her gaze drift sideways to Zane’s platform—to size up the man her brother’s fate might depend on, if it came to that.

Her eyes went wide.

[SFX: DISTANT SHOUT]

Just as their eyes locked, a shout tore through the air from the adjacent arena. “Brother!”

[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane and the entire audience froze, stunned by Layla’s scream. A shocked silence fell over the stands as every head turned to watch the scene unfold.

The burly man stared at Layla, completely baffled. He knew he had a sister, but he’d left her behind ten years ago when he was sent to the Villain World. She’d only been seven or eight back then. He didn’t recognize the powerful young woman standing there now.

“Brother! It’s me! Layla!” she yelled, her heart pounding in her chest as she waited for any sign of recognition.

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL PIANO]

The moment he heard her name, all doubt vanished. A light ignited in his eyes, something that hadn’t been there for a very, very long time.

The young woman Adrian had told him to fight… that was his sister.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

The realization hit him like a physical blow. To think she’d grown into such a powerful warrior. He couldn’t believe he’d almost raised his hand against his own sister.

Even the Lord of the Sunset World was speechless. No one could have predicted a twist like this in the final trial.
