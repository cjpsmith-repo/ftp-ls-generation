Ep 991 - The Roaring Token
Word count: 1108
[AMBIENT: QUIET TEMPLE HALL]
[MUSIC: SOLEMN STRINGS]
Zane didn’t hesitate anymore. He dropped to his knees and bowed low before the owner.
He’d hesitated at first, unsure about taking on a Master so suddenly. A man doesn’t just kneel for anyone, and Zane was no exception.
But now that he had chosen his path, he would show the proper respect.
[SFX: MYSTICAL WHOOSH]
The owner, however, seemed to be a detached man with little use for ceremony. As Zane bowed, an invisible force caught him, gently lifting him back to his feet.
“That’s enough,” the owner said with a nod. “We warriors have little time for formalities. Since you’re willing to be my student, I will pass this book’s attunement method on to you. But first, do you have any idea why I made the path to it so difficult?”
“I don’t,” Zane admitted, shaking his head.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DEEP DRUMS]
The owner continued, “I spent tens of thousands of years in countless calculations to forge this new path. Even I don’t know what lies at its end. The attunement begins by replacing your Vitality with chaos energy. To do that, you must shatter your core and welcome Chaos into your body. Only someone with incredible determination could withstand that pain. Your willpower got you through the path out there, but I’m warning you—the agony of this process is beyond anything you can imagine. Do you understand?”
Zane nodded cautiously. “I do.”
A faint smile touched the owner’s lips. “It’s easy to say yes now. You’ll understand the true meaning of pain soon enough.”
His eyes scanned Zane from head to toe. “Strange. There’s Primordial Energy inside you. You’ve used it for body refining before, haven’t you?”
Zane could only nod. It was as if the man could see every secret his body held.
“Your body is... unusual,” the owner repeated, though he didn’t elaborate. “Body refining is a painful process, but the agony of replacing your Vitality with chaos energy will be a thousand times worse.”
Pain was impossible to quantify. What one person found unbearable, another could endure. But the owner spoke of the difference as if it were a chasm.
He knows, Zane realized. He must have already sensed the nine dragons and just chose not to say anything. Which meant the Cobalt Dragon hadn’t just revealed itself on its own.
[SFX: MIST]
The owner raised a hand and pointed into the depths of the space. A wisp of gray mist drifted out from somewhere far away — slow, formless, barely visible. Chaos. Real Chaos energy.
It circled once in the air, then shot toward Zane’s abdomen and vanished into his Vitality core.
Zane waited. He felt almost nothing — just a faint needle-prick, deep inside, there and gone.
“That’s it?” he asked.
“The seed has been planted,” the owner said. “What you felt was nothing. When you begin to cultivate it — when the Chaos starts to move — you will understand what I meant.”
Zane said nothing. He believed him.
“In all my years,” the owner added, almost to himself, “I have taken only two disciples. You are the second.”
He didn’t say who the first was. He didn’t seem inclined to.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]
Then the space shifted. The owner’s presence pressed against Zane’s mind — not invasively, but deliberately — and a stream of knowledge poured in. The cultivation method, transmitted directly. Zane felt it settle into him like water finding its level.
During the transmission, the owner went quiet for just a moment. When he spoke again, his tone had changed — not alarmed, but genuinely surprised.
“The Soulforge…” he murmured. “Suppressing nine true dragons. Interesting.” He studied Zane for a breath longer, then seemed to decide something. “When you ascend to the Upper Realm — before you are strong enough to protect yourself — the Soulforge must remain your absolute secret. Tell no one. Show no one. Do you understand?”
“So he’s using me? That’s one way to look at it,” Zane mused, a smirk playing on his lips. The whole situation was getting more interesting by the second.
He’d walked into The Elysian Keep, scored a priceless attunement method, and was now being showered with gifts. From where he was standing, it felt like he was the one taking advantage of his new Master.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL VOID]
The Cobalt Dragon chuckled inside his mind. A shrewd move, taking you as a student. Especially with your Greater Lotus Flower. Clearly, the ancient dragon was playing a different game entirely.
Then the owner’s voice grew quieter, more distant. “I have been away too long. This projection cannot hold much longer.” A pause. “Cultivate diligently. Perhaps one day I will return from the Outer Realm — and when I do, I will see what my cheap disciple has made of himself.”
The figure faded.
Zane stared at the space where he had been. “Cheap disciple,” he repeated. “How is that cheap?”
The Cobalt Dragon’s voice floated up, dry and unhurried. “He took in a disciple who had cultivated a Greater Lotus Flower, for nothing. From where he’s standing, he’s the one who got the bargain.”
Zane looked at the gifts the owner had pressed into his hands before fading — several shiny fragments, each one small enough to fit between his fingers, each containing something sealed inside. He turned them over slowly.
“From where I’m standing,” he said, “I’m not sure he’s wrong.”
Zane let it go.
[SFX: MAGICAL PORTAL OPENING]
Just then, a door materialized behind him—the exit from the painting. He flew through the opening, his gaze still fixed in wonder on the shiny fragments cupped in his hands.
As he flew, he remembered his Master’s instructions and crushed one of the fragments.
[SFX: CRYSTAL SHATTERING]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHIMES]
It felt surprisingly tough, yet brittle, shattering with a gentle squeeze. In its place, a palm-sized sculpture of a roaring bear materialized in his hand.
“What is this?” Zane murmured, turning the small sculpture over, a mix of confusion and curiosity on his face.
Even the wise Cobalt Dragon went silent in his mind, its focus entirely on the small sculpture.
[SFX: PORTAL WHOOSH]
But Zane had already passed through the portal, emerging from the painting. The Eternal Scribe’s eyes landed on the sculpture in Zane’s hand, and he let out a hearty laugh. “The Master left a legacy in the book, it’s true, but I never imagined he’d leave the Roaring Token for you!”
“The Roaring Token!”
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
The Cobalt Dragon’s voice shrieked in Zane’s mind, laced with a terror Zane had never heard from it before.
