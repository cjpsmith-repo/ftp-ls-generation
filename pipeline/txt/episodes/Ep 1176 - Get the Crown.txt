Ep 1176 - Get the Crown

Word count: 1099

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: TENSE COUNCIL CHAMBER]
[SFX: BODY CRUMPLING TO FLOOR]
The members of the Board of Elders watched in horror as their leader crumpled to the ground, their own faces slack-jawed as if they’d all just been hit by a truck. They never would have dreamed that the Queen of Punishment would actually have the guts to attack them, let alone one of their own.
“If you insist on continuing this nonsense,” she said, her voice dangerously low, “I don’t give a rat’s ass about killing every single one of you.”
With the warning hanging in the air, the Queen of Punishment turned and left, Kaelan and Supreme Lord Gunter following in her wake. She’d planned on having a little fun with the cat-and-mouse game she was playing with Lavender, but she couldn’t afford to waste any more time on these over-the-hill elders. Not now. 
With the Crown of Destiny in play, the game was over.
“Kaelan, tell Maverick and Tanya to finish the job immediately,” the Queen ordered once they were clear. “And if they find the Crown of Destiny, they bring it straight to me.”
“The Crown of Destiny?” Kaelan asked, his shock obvious as his eyes darted to Supreme Lord Gunter.
Gunter just gave a grim nod. 
“A Crown of Destiny has appeared. There’s a good chance Lavender has it.”
“Understood.” Kaelan was quick, and he didn’t need to hear another word.
[MUSIC: COSMIC MYSTERY]
[AMBIENT: RAGING STORM WITH THUNDER]
Light-years away, on the surface of a forgotten star, a storm had scoured the landscape for a million years—a raging scar of thunder and fire visible for millions of miles. No living thing could possibly survive in such an extreme environment.
[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY CRACKLING]
And yet, in the eye of that very storm, a man sat cross-legged with his arms outstretched, his fingers dancing as they traced complex equations in the air.
[SFX: FRUSTRATED MUTTERING]
“Not here,” he muttered, his focus absolute. He shifted, his hand a blur as he began a new calculation. “Not here, either.”
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT REVELATION]
He worked, completely engrossed in his search, until suddenly, his eyes snapped open, glowing with the raw radiance of the stars themselves.
“I’ve done it!” A triumphant grin split his face. “It’s coming to me! This Crown of Destiny is mine!”
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: DYING STORM WINDS]
As the words left his lips, his figure vanished into the massive storm around him. At that same moment, the million-year tempest began, bit by bit, to die down.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS CONVERGENCE]
[AMBIENT: VAST COSMIC SPACE]
Across the vastness of the universe, other Supreme Lords were running their own calculations. Though their methods varied, they all began to pinpoint the same location. They had all locked onto Zane, and they were already making their move.
It had been a month since Zane had left the Dark World.
[AMBIENT: SPACECRAFT HUM]
At the Wheel of Reincarnation’s maximum speed, a month of travel had barely made a dent in the distance. Deep space had no spatial channels — the shortcuts that made travel between major worlds possible. 
Out here, the only warriors who could move freely were those who had mastered a specific movement technique requiring world-lord-level cultivation. Zane was nowhere near that threshold. The Northern Sky Region was still very far away.
For most of that month, he had sat cross-legged at the controls and cultivated.
[MUSIC: MAGICAL DISCOVERY]
Something had been shifting in his inner world. The seed from the World Tree he had planted in his Vitality core was still slowly absorbing his Chaos Energy, as it had been doing since he first took it in — but now a small whirlpool had begun to form deep within his inner world. The seed was converting the Chaos Energy into something else entirely. Not true essence. Not law energy. Something he had no name for.
He had asked Lavender about it. She didn’t know. He had asked Cobalt Dragon. Same answer. The Primordial Chaos Tactic offered no guidance either. Whatever the seed from the World Tree was doing, it was beyond any of their experience. Zane filed it away and kept cultivating.
It was a development Zane himself had never seen coming.
He’d put his faith in his Elysian Keep, but the defensive shield shattered like glass.
The Wheel lurched. Zane opened his eyes.
Two figures stood before the Wheel of Reincarnation — one man, one woman, both Demon Night. Zane took their measure in an instant. Their strength was close to Cassian’s. He couldn’t fight opponents like that, not yet. Lavender had been exposed too early. He hadn’t had time to grow.
There was nothing to do but run.
“The Queen said not to hold back,” Tanya said. “Destroy the Wheel. Kill the young man. Take the Crown of Destiny.”
Maverick smiled faintly, drawing his Infinite Crystal Sword. “It’s just an easy task. Why don’t you just watch?”
[SFX: SWORD MULTIPLYING]
He gave the sword a gentle shake. It split into two. Two became four. Four became eight. In moments, countless swords hung above the Wheel of Reincarnation, trembling in the void.
[SFX: CRYSTAL SWORD IMPACT]
[SFX: METAL TEARING]
A moment later, the first Crystal Sword punched clean through the hull of his Wheel of Reincarnation, leaving a gaping, bowl-sized hole. 
[SFX: GLASS SHATTERING]
[SFX: SWORDS EMBEDDING]
The swords tore straight through the Wheel, punched out the bottom, and then, impossibly, ricocheted back up, embedding themselves in the craft until it bristled with deadly crystal spikes.
[SFX: SWORD PIERCING FLOOR]
As Zane sat at the bottom of the Wheel, another sword shot through the floor right where he was sitting, forcing him to scramble aside. He watched it disappear into the ground below before dragging himself back to the controls, his expression a mask of cold concentration. The Wheel of Reincarnation was a high-grade spatial tool, but at his current strength, he couldn’t stop the damage. The best he could do was push it as far as it could possibly go.
[SFX: RAPID CRYSTAL BARRAGE]
While his focus remained locked on maneuvering the Wheel, another ten swords burst into the craft. Zane dodged again in a flash, his hands never leaving the controls as the Crystal Swords shot back and forth, tearing his craft to absolute shreds.
[MUSIC: COLD DETERMINATION]
“The time is now,” a woman said from a distance, her voice as cold as ice. “I’ll do it myself.”
[SFX: BLADE ENERGY CHARGING]
She held a ten-foot-long saber behind her, parallel to the ground, as a hungry light flared along its blade.
