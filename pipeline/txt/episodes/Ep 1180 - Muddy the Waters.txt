Ep 1180 - Muddy the Waters

Word count: 1121

[MUSIC: DARK MENACING STRINGS]
The Elysian Keep was the only thing standing between Zane, Lavender, and total annihilation. If it fell, he knew he’d have no other choice but to accept his fate.
He was surrounded, trapped between a swarm of Supreme Lords and the Queen of Punishment herself. It was a deathtrap, plain and simple. But if making things more complicated gave him even a sliver of a chance to get out of this, he was more than happy to light the fuse.
[SFX: MAGICAL ARTIFACT ACTIVATION]
With a grim resolve, he pulled out the Roaring Token.
A moment later, his voice boomed across the entire universe.
“Well, look who it is. We really have to stop meeting like this.”
A flicker of pure rage crossed the Queen of Punishment’s otherwise frozen features. How dare he, she thought, the words a silent snarl. This insect dares to speak to me like that? 
But her expression remained as cold as ice.
“In my eyes, you are nothing,” she said, her voice cutting through the void. “A warrior from the Soul Sea Realm isn’t even qualified to speak to me. Send Lavender out. Now.”
Zane’s voice rang out again, laced with scorn.
“Please. In my eyes, you’re not a queen, you’re just some monster. You want us to come out? Not happening. If you’re so powerful, why don’t you come in and get us? This Elysian Keep is full of treasures. And besides the Crown of Destiny, I’ve also got my Roaring Token. This thing is on a whole other level, isn’t it? Beyond the heavens. Its power is limitless.”
[AMBIENT: EXCITED MURMURING CROWD]
His broadcast sent a shockwave across the hundred thousand supreme worlds.
“A guy from the Soul Sea Realm has a Crown of Destiny?”
“The legends say a Crown of Destiny is what turns a World Lord into a Supreme Lord. And some kid has it?”
“If I had that Crown, I could become a Supreme Lord!”
Across the universe, low-level warriors chattered with excitement.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS FOREBODING DRONE]
The truth was, if a Crown of Destiny actually fell into their hands, it would be an instant death sentence. They’d get nothing out of it but a quick, violent end.
But for the World Lords, it was a different story. The news lit a fire of pure envy in their hearts.
Every supreme world, except for the most barren backwaters, had a World Lord. Some, like the Dark World and The Psychean Realm, even had two or three. 
When they heard Zane’s words, every single one of them wanted that Crown for themselves. They had already reached the absolute peak of their power, and the only path forward was to obtain a Crown of Destiny. They just couldn’t fathom how some kid from the Soul Sea Realm had managed to get his hands on one.
“Get me that Crown of Destiny!”
“I’ll have my grandfather seize the Crown for me.”
“Where is that brat right now?”
Zane turned from the viewscreen. “What star is this?” he asked Lavender.
She considered for a moment. “I believe it’s the twelfth star of the Middle Sky Region,” she said. “Though I’m not entirely certain.”
Zane nodded — and activated the token again.
[SFX: VOICE AMPLIFIED ACROSS UNIVERSE — SECOND BROADCAST]
“It’s a great honor to see so many Supreme Lords gathered here on the twelfth star of the Middle Sky Region. I wonder if there will be even more on their way. I, Zane, am truly honored by the company.”
The assembled Supreme Lords exchanged glances. Some wore looks of grudging appreciation. Others shook their heads with contempt — the location was superfluous information to them; any Supreme Lord could already calculate the Crown’s position. But that wasn’t the point. The point was that the Crown’s existence was no longer known only to the one hundred and twenty-eight. It was now known to every living being in the universe.
The waters were muddied beyond untangling.
Across the universe, two Supreme Lords in particular felt the broadcast differently. Magnus and Armond — old rivals, both humiliated by Zane’s Roaring Token long before this day — had been watching events unfold with more than passing interest. They hadn’t necessarily planned to move for the Crown. But the Roaring Token was another matter entirely. Now that Zane had confirmed it with his own voice — a token that surpassed the Heavens, infinitely reusable — their doubts dissolved. Both of them set out for the Middle Sky Region. Not for the Crown. For the token.
Of course, most World Lords were powerless to do anything.
[MUSIC: DARK CONSPIRATORIAL STRINGS]
But those with powerful backers were already starting to make their moves.
A bitter regret washed over the Queen of Punishment. She should have gone to the Dark World herself the moment she’d learned of Zane’s existence, instead of wasting time sending others to kill him. So much time, just thrown away. If she’d acted then, she would have already claimed the Crown and ended Lavender’s life long ago.
Now, thanks to his little announcement, every power-hungry lord in the universe would be on their way. He could cut a deal with any one of them, trading the Crown for his own safety. The boy was smart—he knew the more chaos he caused, the better his chances of survival.
Realizing this, the Queen of Punishment knew she couldn’t hesitate a second longer.
[MUSIC: EXPLOSIVE BATTLE ORCHESTRAL]
[SFX: THUNDER CRACKLING]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
Faith Energy surged through her, and the Saint Thunder Meteor Hammer in her hands erupted, crackling with a storm of lightning.
[SFX: LIGHTNING STRIKE]
Her attack was about to come crashing down on the Elysian Keep.
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC BATTLEFIELD]
[SFX: RUSHING SMOKE]
But just as she made her move, a tide of black smoke surged past her. It was Supreme Lord Addison. He’d been the first to arrive, and with the classic cruelty of an Ogre, he wasn’t about to let his prize slip through his fingers.
[SFX: GIANT HANDS FORMING]
Dozens of giant hands formed from the smoke, reaching for the Elysian Keep from every direction.
Before they could connect, countless tiny shadows burst from Supreme Lord Gunter’s body.
[SFX: SHADOWS BURSTING]
[SFX: DISSOLVING ENERGY]
Each shadow was only the size of a man’s palm, but they were infused with a strange Law of Causality that seemed to dissolve anything they touched. In the blink of an eye, Addison’s smoky hands simply melted away.
But even Gunter could only stop one of them.
[SFX: MULTIPLE POWERFUL RUSHES]
In that same instant, the other five Supreme Lords charged forward, none of them willing to stand by and let someone else claim the Crown of Destiny.
