Ep 654 - The Gate's True Gift

Word count: 1126

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: GROWING DANGER]

"A War Sage."

The words were a choked whisper, but they hung in the air with the weight of a death sentence. Esther Sterling’s breath caught in her throat. The sheer power rolling off Malcolm was unmistakable. He’d done it.

The color drained from Fabian’s and Jarrod’s faces. The sudden ambush felt chillingly familiar, reminding them of how Colin and Neil had just materialized out of nowhere inside the Tower. After controlling this place for so long, the Ogres must have found secrets no one else ever knew.

But even as the shock hit them, not one of the warriors flinched. Their breathing remained steady, their stances unshaken. Faced with a newly ascended War Sage, they were utterly, unnervingly composed.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

The irony was that Malcolm was the one who looked confused. This wasn't right. A flicker of doubt crossed his face, and his posture tightened with suspicion. Why weren't they panicking? They were about to die.

He could almost understand the casual indifference from those two freaks, Lavender and Yolande. They were from the upper world, after all. Their approach to martial arts was on a plane most Lower World warriors couldn't even fathom. Take Yolande—the Gate of Many Lives was child’s play to her. She’d already split her soul into a million fragments, allowing a million avatars of herself to train all at once. The combined experience made the Gate’s attunement method look like a parlor trick.

But Zane and Esther? Why were they so damn calm? Even Fabian and Jarrod, two of the most reliably cautious top-tier War Lords, were just… staring.

A wave of bitter disappointment washed over Malcolm. This was supposed to be his moment. He’d spent months crammed into that miserable secret passage, dreaming of this. He had pictured them scattering in terror or, better yet, dropping to their knees to beg for their lives. But this blank, unimpressed staring? It was an insult.

What Malcolm didn't know was that these warriors had just lived and died dozens of times over. The Gate of Many Lives had reshaped their understanding of mortality from the inside out. Not even death held its old terror anymore.

Fabian, who had nearly shattered during the entity chase — who had needed Zane's Psyche Spike just to keep moving — was now standing with the stillness of someone who had made peace with the worst.

That was the Gate's true gift. A spirit hardened enough to hold steady at the moment it mattered most.

After a moment, a sneer twisted Malcolm's lips. "What's the matter? You think you're still in the Tower of Sin? Think Zane can just snap his fingers and kill anyone he wants in here?"

Fabian met his taunt with a level gaze. "We're not that stupid. And now that you're here, what good would panicking do?"

Esther's eyes stayed fixed on Malcolm. "When you were a peak War Lord, you were nowhere near my level," she said evenly. "You've broken through — I'll give you that. But you're a first-level War Sage. Are you really so sure that changes things?"

Malcolm had had enough.

When he'd entered that passage under the Tower, he'd known exactly what kind of mission it was. A death warrior's mission. One objective: kill Zane. If Zane died, everything the Ogres had lost evaporated. They returned to the Tower, they reclaimed their dominance, and he — Malcolm — would be the hero who made it happen. That was worth any price.

Only then did Zane notice the second figure standing in the shadows behind Malcolm — Timothy, his massive frame still, watching. He hadn't said a word since they'd arrived.

"Go!" Malcolm launched himself directly at Zane, his massive frame crossing the distance in an instant. At the same moment, Timothy moved — and the three surviving Twilight Group War Lords fanned out beside him, cutting off the flanks.

[SFX: DEMONIC SCREAM]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

A snarling phantom of demonic energy screamed from Malcolm's blade, lunging straight for Zane.

"Protect Zane!" Fabian's voice was calm, not desperate. He and Jarrod moved as one, stepping into Malcolm's path. Their swords came up simultaneously, sword intent beginning to spiral and intertwine between them.

The two of them drove everything they had into Malcolm's strike. Layered sword intent surged forward in coordinated waves — the kind of technique that could stop an ordinary War Sage cold.

Malcolm's blade didn't slow.

[SFX: ENERGY SHATTER]

Every layer shattered on contact. Fabian and Jarrod's faces hardened. Together, they were supposed to be able to handle a standard War Sage. Malcolm was clearly something else.

"Change your move!" Fabian snapped, and they shifted formation without missing a beat, continuing to press, buying seconds.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE ENERGY BURST]

"Star Shakes the Earth!"

[SFX: SWIRLING VORTEX]

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ENERGY]

A vortex of pure starlight erupted from Zane's fist, opening its maw to swallow the demonic phantom whole.

"Shatter!" Zane roared. He knew the power of his Stellar Body—a single punch could kill a low-level War Sage. This should have been more than enough.

[SFX: ENERGY WHOOSH]

But then, the impossible happened. Right before being pulled into the vortex, the phantom shrieked and split apart. It morphed into three searing blades of energy that curved around the pull of the starlight, homing in on Zane from three different directions.

Shit.

Zane’s confidence evaporated. He’d completely underestimated the power of a true War Sage.  Or maybe, he'd just underestimated Malcolm. The Ogres hadn't left him and Timothy behind by accident — this was a calculated move. Malcolm hadn't just broken through. He'd mastered his new power.

[SFX: SPEAR SLASH]

"Hah! Shatter!"

[SFX: ENERGY SHATTER]

Suddenly, Lavender was there. Her spear became a silver blur, its tip puncturing one of the energy streams. With a sharp twist of the shaft, the attack was shredded into nothing. At the same instant, Yolande simply pointed a finger, and a second stream shattered like glass.

[SFX: METAL CLASH]

[SFX: SPARKS]

Zane threw up his Relic-Class sword just in time, blocking the final stream in a shower of sparks that sent him staggering back a step.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS THEME]

For a second, Malcolm just stared, genuinely stunned that they’d survived. Then, a slow, cruel smile spread across his face. "Not bad, Zane. Not bad at all," he said, shaking his head with something like admiration. "You've really improved these past few months. I have to admit, I'm impressed. Give it another year, and you might have actually been a threat."

He let out a low chuckle. "Too bad for you. You're nowhere near strong enough to beat me now, and you'll never get the chance to surpass me. You're going to die today."
