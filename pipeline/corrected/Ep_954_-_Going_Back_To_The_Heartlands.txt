Ep 954 - Going Back To The Heartlands
Word count: 1120
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMURS]
When the tremors finally subsided, a dazed confusion settled over the crowd of creatures.
“It just... stopped? Like that?”
“What the hell is going on? Weren’t the Demi-Immortal warriors supposed to be moving the Tower of Sin?”
“Did they stop? Weren’t they strong enough to move it?”
“Maybe...”
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
It took a few minutes of panicked speculation, but the truth eventually filtered through the crowd: the Tower of Sin hadn’t been moved. It stood right where it always had, whole and intact.
But they had learned something crucial in the process—the Tower of Sin would only remain on the main continent for three hundred years. After that, the Demi-Immortals would return to take it away for good.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL ORCHESTRAL]
The news brought a wave of mixed relief and anxiety across the main continent. The fact that the Demi-Immortal Race was giving them a three-hundred-year grace period felt like an enormous favor.
The word going around was that this buffer had been granted because Zane had agreed to some of the Demi-Immortal Race’s conditions.
On top of that, the Demi-Immortal warriors had installed two teleportation arrays inside the Tower of Sin. One led to the Violet Heart Sanctuary, the other to the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary. This effectively gave the Demon Night and the humans a greater hold on the Tower. After all, any War Lords from other races who ascended to War Sage would now have to use one of their arrays to travel between the tower and the Heartlands if they wanted to continue their training.
[MUSIC: QUIET DETERMINATION]
But before any of that could be settled, there was something Zane needed to see to himself.
Amber had been asking for weeks. When the Queen of Punishment had possessed Ailsa and attacked the Soulcradle Wood, the wood had taken real damage — and Amber had wanted to draw life force from it immediately to feed into Lavender’s soul. Zane had refused. The wood was already injured. Draining it further would only set back both recoveries.
But the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary had surprised him. In the days that followed, the sanctuary’s life force surged back with extraordinary speed — new growth pushing up through the ash, the wood’s surface visibly mending. Zane watched it carefully. When he was satisfied the wood had recovered enough, he agreed to stay.
[AMBIENT: TREETOP]
At the top of the Soulcradle Wood, with only Amber present, Zane stood still as the great tree began to sway gently. Life force gathered along its branches — less than the last time, far less, but enough.
[SFX: SOFT RUSTLING — LEAVES AND WIND]
Zane was careful. Even now, with the Queen of Punishment’s statue destroyed, the Demon Night had raised a new one almost overnight. He didn’t trust that she wouldn’t try again — use it to reach into the wood and strike at Lavender’s soul a second time. He kept his perception sharp throughout.
The life force flowed into the Soul Nourishing Tree. Zane let his perception follow it in.
[MUSIC: TENDER STRINGS]
Lavender’s soul had recovered. Not fully — not even close to what it had been — but the improvement was real and significant. She was still asleep. And if she woke tomorrow, she wouldn’t be able to summon the sword spirit to fight as she once had. But she was healing. It was only a matter of time.
Zane withdrew his perception slowly. That was enough for now.
[AMBIENT: HEAVENLY SANCTUARY]
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
Once the Compass Wheel flew back to the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, Zane withdrew his consciousness from the puppet. He smiled, looking down at the doll—a near-perfect replica of himself. He turned to Wade. “Can I keep this?”
“No problem,” Wade said with a chuckle.
Wade may not have been the strongest of the seven Demi-Immortals, but he was incredibly knowledgeable. And while he was a man of few words, his straightforward nature had earned Zane’s respect.
Zane placed the puppet, a perfect replica of a Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior, into his Storage Ring.
The Demi-Immortals had come to the main continent with one goal: take the Tower of Sin. But by confirming he’d earned the golden celestial rank, Zane had managed to leverage a massive favor from them for all the warriors of the main continent.
It wasn’t that Zane was against joining the Demi-Immortals. Brianna may have called them a bunch of crazies, but compared to the other major powers, they seemed refreshingly neutral. The seven of them would report back to their people about him, and others would take it from there. Then, they’d get back to their own war with the Evil Dragon.
[MUSIC: DEPARTURE]
Joining the Demi-Immortal Race would have to wait until he ascended, in any case. He had only just stepped into the Life-and-Death Realm — ascension was still a distant horizon.
Vorn had already told him: once they returned to the Heartlands, the seven of them would report his situation to their higher-ups. Someone else would follow up when the time came. After that, they had their own war to get back to.
True to his word, when they arrived at the Heavenly Stellar Palace via the Demi-Immortals’ transmission mark — faster and smoother than any array Zane had ever used — Vorn didn’t linger. A brief pause. Then the blood-red mark in the courtyard flickered to life again, and all seven of them were gone.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION FLASH]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: QUIET PALACE HALL]
As he watched the Demi-Immortals disappear, Zane thought, I guess the Demi-Immortal Race has some beef with Master Priest and the Holy Maiden. He shrugged. Not his problem.
[MUSIC: PEACEFUL RELIEF]
For the first time in what felt like forever, a wave of pure relief washed over him.
Ever since he’d started his training, time had been a constant pressure, a ticking clock always pushing him forward. He still had things he needed to do—help Lavender reclaim her status, find his sister.
But those goals were so far out of his league, he knew he couldn’t achieve them anytime soon. The young man who had taken Elara, for instance, was on a level of power Zane couldn’t even begin to fathom. Even now, with all he had accomplished, Zane knew that man could kill him a thousand times over with a single glance. This entire world of chaos probably couldn’t withstand one of his attacks.
So, for now, this was a rare moment to just breathe.
His gaze swept across the Heavenly Stellar Palace. The grand hall was empty. Tia must have returned to the Cloud Hall after she recovered. He wondered if Cora had gone back with her.
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: URGENT TENSION]
Just then, a burly figure came barreling through the air toward him. It was Kieran, one of the three leaders of the World Merchants’ Guild. The moment he spotted Zane, Kieran broke out in a frantic sweat and yelled, “Zane, you’re finally back! Something terrible has happened! They’re fighting!”
Zane’s brow furrowed. “Fighting? Who’s fighting?”
