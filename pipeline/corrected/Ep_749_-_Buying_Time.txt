Ep 749 - Buying Time
Word count: 1061
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL TENSION]
Just as Maddox raised his saber to strike, a wave of raw power surged over the city walls, followed by an ancient, booming voice that cut through the chaos.
[SFX: POWERFUL VOICE ECHO]
“Maddox! Your fight is with me!”
They say a man who lives a thousand years becomes a legend, and the speaker was just that—the ancient tycoon, the cornerstone of the World Merchants' Guild, and the only man in all the Heartlands to see a millennium. He had been holding back, waiting for the perfect moment to intervene, and this was it.
Everyone knew his power. Two years ago, when Maddox had tried to murder Zane after the tournament, it wasn’t Tia or the guild leaders who had stopped him. It was the tycoon, and him alone. His strength was, at the very least, a match for Maddox's.
The old man brought his hands together, the wrinkled skin of his knuckles tightening as he drew a withered palm back to strike.
But Maddox just laughed, a low, guttural sound. “I've been waiting for this, old man,” he snarled. “You're the only reason Zane is still breathing. You want a death wish? I’m more than happy to grant it!”
[SFX: BLADE WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
His original target, Tia, was forgotten. Maddox pivoted, redirecting all the power he’d gathered for his Netherworld Shaking technique. He swung the Nameless Saber, and a blade of shimmering yellow wind shot from its edge, spinning silently toward the tycoon.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
[SFX: WIND WHISTLE]
The instant that wind left the saber, a primal terror seized everyone in and around Oracle City. It was a cold, alien feeling that locked the breath in their lungs and froze them in place. Zane acted on pure instinct, yanking Tia back with him. As they retreated, he whispered, his voice tight with dread, “Cobalt Dragon, what is that? Why does it feel so… wrong?”
But before the Cobalt Dragon could answer, it was over.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
[SFX: WIND WHISPER]
The wind reached the tycoon, not with a crash, but with a whisper. It seemed no stronger than a summer breeze, barely stirring the hem of his robes. And then, in the space of a heartbeat, the light in the old man's eyes simply… went out. He crumpled to the ground without a sound, a lifeless puppet with its strings cut.
Horror contorted Zane’s face.
From somewhere across the battlefield, Kieran let out a raw, agonized roar: “Sir!”
Kieran's cry hit the other two Guild leaders like a blade. Their faces went dark. They had built everything alongside the ancient tycoon — he was the foundation the three of them stood on. They had never imagined, not once, that he could fall in a single exchange.
Maddox's lip curled. "Don't worry," he said, his voice almost pleasant. "I'll send you all to hell one by one." He turned back to Zane and Tia and raised the Nameless Saber again. "Now — it's your turn."
Zane and Tia's hearts sank. The old man hadn't even gotten an attack off. He was the one person in the Heartlands whose strength matched Maddox's — and he hadn't lasted a single exchange. What did that make them?
"Cobalt Dragon," Zane said quietly as they retreated through the city streets, putting distance between themselves and Maddox. "What was that technique?"
The Cobalt Dragon's voice was careful, measured. "That was the Life and Death Wind — a wind that only blows along the Yellow Spring River in the Netherworld. Even the fiends who live beyond the Yellow Spring avoid it. Anyone it touches has their life severed instantly. There is no defense against it."
"Can it be stopped?"
A pause. "It shouldn't be controllable at all. Even fiends treat it with extreme caution when crossing the Yellow Spring. I don't know how Maddox is wielding it." He didn't say the rest, but Zane heard it clearly: he had no answer.
Maddox followed at a leisurely pace, contemptuous of the battle raging across the city. He was right to be. Only two people in Oracle City posed any real threat to him, and he had them both in front of him. Kill Zane and Tia, and the walls didn't matter.
That heartbroken cry ignited something in Zane. His vision went red, his mind blanking in pure rage.
Years of frustration erupted—Maddox looming over him since Skyreach Sect, every forced retreat, every time the man stayed one step ahead. He'd come prepared to end it. Instead, he was running again.
Tia recognized the fury consuming him. A warrior's mind was his greatest weapon, but she knew Zane. If he could witness that and stay calm, he wouldn't be human.
[MUSIC: CALMING ETHEREAL]
[SFX: ICY ENERGY FLOW]
Tia leaned in, pressing her palm flat against Zane's chest. A wave of icy energy flowed from her hand, a stark contrast to the fire burning inside him. “Zane,” she murmured, her voice a steady anchor. “Breathe. Calm down.”
The chill spread through his chest, pulling him back from the brink. His heart, which had been hammering against his ribs, began to slow to a steady rhythm. The fury drained from his face. “You're right,” he breathed out, the words a surrender. “I have to calm down.”
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
He’d always prided himself on his control, the unflappable calm that had saved him more times than he could count. He never imagined that Maddox could get under his skin like that, to provoke a rage so absolute it threatened to consume him.
[SFX: MAGICAL SUMMONING]
With his composure regained, Zane spoke a single name, his voice low but clear. “Lavender!”
[SFX: WEAPON MATERIALIZATION]
[SFX: SPEAR SPINNING]
Instantly, a speck of crimson light appeared at his side, coalescing into the form of a woman. As she appeared, Zane reached out, and a beam of light shot from his Storage Ring. A mid-grade Relic spear materialized, spinning twice in the air before landing perfectly in Lavender's waiting grasp.
“Want me to kill him for you?” Lavender asked, a hint of a smile playing on her lips.
Zane shook his head, his expression grim. “No, it's too dangerous. You can't beat him.” He met her eyes. “But you can hold him off. Just buy us time.”
Lavender’s smile widened into a confident grin. “Consider it done.”
It was the perfect strategy. As a spirit, Lavender was a being of pure soul. She had no life force for Maddox’s terrifying wind to extinguish.
[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]
[SFX: LIGHTNING STRIKE]
At Zane’s nod, she gripped the spear, her form blurring as she launched herself toward Maddox like a crimson bolt of lightning.
