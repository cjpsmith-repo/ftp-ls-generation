Ep 177 - Saying Sorry

[AMBIENT: JUDGEMENT HALL CROWD]
No one could believe what Isla was asking Saint Viana to do.
Zane fought hard to keep a straight face and not break out laughing at this turn of events. Isla didn’t have to be so ruthless, but she'd turned the tables and saved Zane’s skin. And thankfully, he wasn’t hurt.
Her message was crystal clear: she usually kept a low profile, but if you crossed her, you'd pay the price. No exceptions.
Saint Viana felt as though he had to say something. This whole mess was his fault, after all, and he'd just let Saint Augustus take the heat for it. Viana spoke up, his voice tight. “My Princess, don’t you think you’ve gone a little too far?”
“Out of line?” Isla shot back with a shake of her head. Before Viana could even get a word in, she plowed ahead. “I’m not asking for the world here. Your screw-up almost got Zane killed. All I want is an apology. Is that really asking too much? Just admit you were wrong. It’s not that hard.”
“I understand your position, but consider who we are,” Saint Viana stammered. “Our station is far above his. We can’t just---”
But Isla wasn’t having it. “Don’t even start, Viana,” she cut him off. “I chose Zane. And you just wait. One day, he’ll be so powerful that every last one of you will be looking up to him. So don’t give me that crap about his status being too low for an apology.”
“Apologize to him?” Viana’s face contorted with indignation. “Do you really expect a Saint to humble himself before an Acolyte?”
Isla stepped forward, her royal presence filling the chamber. “I expect a Saint to acknowledge when he has erred, regardless of who stands before him.”
The hall fell silent as all eyes turned to Viana. His shoulders stiffened, pride warring with pragmatism across his features. Finally, he turned to Zane with thinly veiled contempt.
“Very well. I... apologize for my hasty judgment,” he muttered, each word extracted like a painful splinter. “The Sect’s procedures were not properly followed in your case.”
Zane nodded curtly, accepting the hollow apology without comment. The tension in the room gradually dissipated, though the Saint’s resentful gaze promised this matter was far from settled.

[MUSIC: TRANSITION THEME]
The whole ordeal ate up a good chunk of Zane’s time, and by the time he got back to the Skyreach Sect, he was way behind the other acolytes.
“Lady Novak, there’s one thing I still don’t get,” Zane said after a moment. “Why did the Scrying Slate record what happened?”
[SFX: MAGICAL RECORDING DEVICE]
Truth be told, Isla had recorded the whole thing by accident.
Zane had been missing for a while after the Demon Purge. Isla had heard through the grapevine---from people like Cliff---that he was okay, just detained by some master from the Imperial Army. From Cliff’s description, she’d figured it had to be Yolande.
[AMBIENT: QUIET CONTEMPLATION]
All Isla could do was wait for him to show up back at the Sect.
But as the days dragged on with no sign of him, Isla started to get worried. She was on the verge of sending a team to Alba Imperia to track him down when she happened to glance at her Scrying Slate and realized she could see his location.
She'd tinkered with his Acolyte's Sigil before---in fact, she'd used it to keep tabs on him the last time he went to Infernal Peak by himself. Since Zane always had the sigil on him, Isla could track his location with her Scrying Slate as long as he was inside the Sect's territory.
[SFX: SCRYING SLATE ACTIVATION]
And that's how her Scrying Slate ended up recording everything. When she watched the playback, she saw Saint Viana threatening Zane. The second she saw it, she contacted Caspar, and they both rushed to Mount Judgment.
So now Zane was asking how she’d recorded it all. Isla just chuckled, not about to tell him the whole truth.
[MUSIC: TENSE DISCUSSION]
“They apologized. For now, it’s over,” Isla said. “But the Saints are furious. I’m not worried about me, but I am worried they’ll try to take it out on you.”
Zane nodded. He got it.
“I know,” he said. “Even without this, they’d never leave me alone. But the feeling’s mutual. I’m not letting them off the hook, either. Especially not Viana and Aldric.”
He knew he'd made powerful enemies today by forcing a public apology. Hearing Zane's defiance, Isla sighed. For a second there, she'd forgotten just who she was dealing with.
Deep down, she knew Zane wouldn’t hold this kind of grudge if it was just about him. It was different when someone threatened his family---then he’d make them pay. The fact that he hadn’t taken things further this time showed he still had a good heart.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]
“We’ll worry about that later,” Isla said after a moment. “Right now, you’re not nearly strong enough to take on a Soul Realm master, let alone a Saint. If you want to save your sister, you have to get a lot stronger.”
Zane just nodded and walked out of the Hall of Judgment with her.

Later, Zane noticed Ethan seemed off. “You’ve got something on your mind,” he said, studying him. “What’s wrong?”
Ethan was from The Capital and was usually a total goofball; nothing ever seemed to get to him. But he looked genuinely miserable now, and Zane knew something was up.
Ethan's eyes widened, surprised he'd been called out.
“Oh, it’s nothing! Seriously, Zane, I’m just glad you’re back. C’mon, let’s drink to that!” Ethan said, raising his cup.
The harder Ethan tried to brush it off, the more certain Zane was that something was wrong. He didn’t raise his cup. “If we’re really friends, just tell me what’s going on,” he said.
As soon as Zane spoke, both Dustin and Rhys turned to look at Ethan. They'd noticed he was acting weird, too.
After a beat, Ethan forced a smile. “It’s not a big deal, really. My family’s throwing a party next month. See, House Moon is one of the Great Houses, but\... we’re pretty low on the totem pole.”
At that, Dustin chimed in. “Ethan, you mean House Moon from the north side of the city?”
Dustin family had business dealings all over the Capital, so he knew most of the big players. The second Ethan mentioned being from a Great House, Dustin had put it together.
What was their friend hiding?
