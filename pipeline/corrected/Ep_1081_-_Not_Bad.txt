Ep 1081 - Not Bad

Word count: 1217

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Layla tried to find Zane before she left the Enchanted Barrier Tower, but the endless Law Storm made it impossible. She had no idea where he was or if he’d already been knocked out of the test.

“So Zane made it to eighteen thousand miles, right?” Layla said with a smile. Even though she’d quit before he did, she felt pretty damn good about her performance. There was no way Zane was beating her record.

Still, twenty-eight thousand, two hundred miles was the best she could do.

[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]

[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND]

By that point, the pressure had become terrifying - a whirlwind of thunder and fire, razor-sharp wind blades, and instant-freezing frost. She could have pushed another thousand miles, but it wasn’t worth it.

She’d beaten everyone - ordinary fighters and gifted ones alike. The shocked faces of warriors from the Thirteen Palaces satisfied her completely as they stared with newfound respect and awe.

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]

Yolande’s voice called out. “Layla, please come upstairs.”

Layla gave a small nod, moving with a light, confident step until she stood before the assembled leaders.

Before today, these powerful figures—warriors qualified to be World Lords and held in the highest regard—had never given her a second glance. Compared to them, she was just a kid, leagues beneath them in age and power.

But now, after what they had just seen, they were looking at her like she was one of them.

Even standing before all that power, Layla wasn’t the least bit nervous. Instead, she turned to the old man in the simple cotton robe. “If there’s a reward for crossing ten thousand miles,” she asked, her voice clear, “shouldn’t there be one for crossing twenty thousand, too?”

The old man was completely blindsided. It had never even occurred to him that someone could reach twenty thousand miles, so he hadn’t prepared anything for such a feat.

[SFX: EXCITED MURMURS]

One of the other leaders spoke up with a grin. “She should absolutely get a reward! Twenty-eight thousand miles… that’s incredible! She deserves the best we can offer!”

“I agree!” another chimed in. “At the very least, she gets a prize for setting a new record!”

The old man hesitated, trying to think of something suitable.

[SFX: SPATIAL TEAR]

A voice cut through the air from somewhere above — calm, unhurried, and carrying a weight that made every person in the hall go still. Someone had broken through the seal the old man had set around the observation area and was descending slowly into the hall.

“Let me give her the reward.”

[AMBIENT: CROWD CHATTER]

The palace masters turned. The moment they saw who it was, they rose to their feet as one. Even the old man in the cotton robe straightened with unmistakable respect.

“The Lord of the Sunset World—”

“He came personally—”

The Lord of the Sunset World’s status in the Alliance was without equal among those present. He controlled the thirteen great worlds of the Thirteen Heavenly Palaces. Among all the Realm Lords, he was a core figure — his authority second only to the Heavenly Venerables themselves.

Layla didn’t move. She didn’t bow or step back. She just looked at him. “You’ll give me the reward?”

The Lord of the Sunset World smiled at her with an almost grandfatherly warmth. “Yes, little girl.”

“I’m not a little girl,” Layla said, correcting him without missing a beat. “I’m Layla.”

He laughed, shaking his head. “I came in a hurry and didn’t bring anything prepared. But I have three treasures with me — quite extraordinary ones. Choose whichever you like.”

[SFX: SHIMMER OF LIGHT]

He waved a hand, and three beams of light materialized before him, each one cradling a treasure. Two were Grade-One Divine Weapons — a snow-white sword and a pale yellow saber, both listed on the Cosmic Spirit Index. Low-ranked, perhaps, but still far beyond ordinary Grade-One Divine Weapons. The third was a feather robe, its surface shimmering with delicate rainbow-colored patterns. A Grade-One Sacred Weapon — and a defensive one at that, which made it extraordinarily rare. Its value was no lower than either of the Divine Weapons beside it.

“The sword and the saber are on the Cosmic Spirit Index,” the Lord of the Sunset World said. “The robe I came across by chance. It’s a very special defensive treasure.”

Layla didn’t hesitate. “The robe.”

“Are you certain?” he asked.

“Of course.” She smiled. “It looks better.”

[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]

The palace masters exchanged looks, speechless. Items listed on the Cosmic Spirit Index were among the most coveted treasures in existence — and she’d passed on two of them because she liked the look of the third. Not one of them knew what to say.

The Lord of the Sunset World handed it over, clearly trying not to laugh. Layla caught the robe as it drifted toward her, shook it out, and draped it over her shoulders in one smooth motion. She rose onto her toes and spun once, the rainbow patterns catching the light. The pride on her face was completely unguarded.

[MUSIC: WARM STRINGS]

The Lord of the Sunset World watched her with an amused expression. “After a performance like that, Silverwood Pavilion will be coming for her soon. What are her master’s intentions, do you think?”

Yolande considered. “He sent her here to take the Passing the Torch trial — he has a plan. I don’t think he’ll turn Silverwood Pavilion away.”

“Good.” The Lord of the Sunset World nodded, then turned his gaze toward the tower. A curious look crossed his face. “There’s still someone in there?”

Yolande nodded. “One of mine.”

“He’s reached the eighteen-thousand-mile mark,” he said, a note of surprise in his voice. “That’s not bad.”

In fact, eighteen thousand miles wasn’t just “not bad”—it was excellent. It was the same record set by Supreme Lord Illusion.

[SFX: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

And Zane was already at eighteen thousand, three hundred miles, meaning he’d just broken it. This was a lot more than just “not bad.”

But with Layla having just set the bar at twenty-eight thousand miles—a full ten thousand ahead—Zane’s achievement suddenly felt a lot less impressive.

Yolande pursed her lips. “Zane seems to be meditating,” she said quietly. “I think he’s going to go farther.” She couldn’t be sure, but she’d had a hunch Zane would beat the old record, and she’d been right. Still, she had no idea how much farther he could actually go.

Layla glanced at the tower, still smiling. “I agree! What Zane’s done is already great! He might go a little farther, maybe even hit the twenty-thousand-mile mark!”

Yolande shot her a sharp look, unable to stand the arrogance. It was obvious Layla didn’t think Zane stood a chance of touching her record. Yolande secretly prayed he would prove her wrong, but she knew how insane Layla’s new record was. For some reason, that thought really bothered her.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

The Lord of the Sunset World spoke up. “Let’s just wait and see if this boy has any surprises for us. If he can reach twenty thousand miles, I’ll give him a reward too. I just wonder how long he plans on meditating in there. It can’t be easy to do that inside the Law Storm.”

[AMBIENT: SILENT TENSION]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRONE]

A hush fell over the crowd. All eyes were fixed on the tower, where the last person standing hadn’t moved in a while.

Everyone was wondering just how long he was going to stay like that.
