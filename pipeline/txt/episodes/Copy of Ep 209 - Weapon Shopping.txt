Ep 209 - Weapon Shopping
Word Count: 1127
[AMBIENT: BUSY PAVILION]
Zane stood inside the Weapon Pavilion, the familiar scent of metal and polish greeting him. He needed a proper sword for the upcoming All Peaks Competition---something worthy of his father's legacy and capable of withstanding the demands of his newly acquired strength. There was no chance his current weapons would hold up against the caliber of opponents he'd face.
With his hands tucked into his pockets and eyes scanning the displayed merchandise, he stepped further into the establishment, mentally calculating how much he could afford to spend while waiting for Dustin's auction proceeds.
"Mr. Lowell! Always a pleasure. What can I get for you this time?" The shopkeeper, a middle-aged man who'd honed his craft over decades, beamed, his voice giddy with the memory of Zane's last visit.
Zane got straight to the point. "I'm looking to see what you've got in stock."
[SFX: METALLIC WEAPONS CLANKING]
The shopkeeper was dying to ask what Zane had done with the last batch of weapons. Over two hundred Mythic-grade pieces---enough to arm a small army. What in the world did one man need them for? But he knew better. Prying could cost him a valuable customer.
Instead, his smile widened. "Of course! What did you have in mind? We just received a new shipment of Mythic weapons."
The shopkeeper's eyes lit up, already picturing another bulk order.
But Zane shook his head. "Not today. Maybe later. Right now, I'm just in the market for a single sword."
[MUSIC: BUILDING ANTICIPATION]
Zane was in no rush to buy more Mythic weapons for crafting Celestial Essence. He'd wait and see how Dustin's auction of the first batch went before making his next move. Besides, he still had over six hundred drops on hand. Even if the auction was a runaway success, he wouldn't flood the market.
In the Eastern Territory, he was the only game in town when it came to a steady supply of Celestial Essence. Its value came from its rarity, and Zane knew it. Dumping a large amount at once would just crash the price. The smart play was to sell it in small, controlled batches.
"A single sword?" the shopkeeper asked, his curiosity piqued. "Any particular kind?"
"I'll know it when I see it," Zane said. "Just show me what you've got." He didn't have anything specific in mind; he'd have to browse. The right blade always took some searching.
"Well, if you're only looking for one, there's no point in showing you the ordinary pieces on the first floor," the shopkeeper said with a knowing smile. He'd seen the kind of money Zane was willing to spend. After buying two hundred Mythic weapons, a man like him wouldn't be satisfied with standard stock. "No, I'll take you straight to the second floor. That's where we keep the real treasures of the Weapon Pavilion!"
[AMBIENT: BUSY WEAPON SHOP]
Zane nodded and followed the shopkeeper through the bustling establishment, his eyes drawn to the gleaming array of weaponry hanging on the walls. As he approached the ornate staircase leading to the second floor, two stern-faced guards stepped forward, blocking his path with practiced efficiency.
"The second floor is reserved exclusively for members of the Weapon Pavilion," one declared flatly, his hand resting casually on his own sword hilt.
Disappointment flickered across Zane's face. The upper level housed the truly legendary weapons---artifacts of extraordinary power and craftsmanship that remained tantalizingly beyond his reach.
The shopkeeper had seen enough. He stepped forward, his voice sharpening. "Stand aside. This young master is my guest." The guards exchanged a glance but held their ground.
"Now." The shopkeeper's tone left no room for argument. Reluctantly, the guards stepped back.
"My apologies," the shopkeeper said to Zane, smoothing his jacket. "They follow the rules. Can't fault them for that." Zane just shrugged. They were only doing their jobs.
[SFX: DISPLAY CASE OPENING]
The shopkeeper, noticing Zane's crestfallen demeanor,  quickly guided him toward an elegant display case nestled in a quieter corner of the shop. With a flourish that spoke of years of salesmanship, he produced a small key and smoothly flipped open the intricately carved lid, revealing a collection of weapons that outshone everything else on the main floor.
"Not to worry, young master," the shopkeeper murmured with a conspiratorial smile. "I save my finest accessible pieces for discerning customers such as yourself."
With reverent hands, he unbuckled one of the swords inside and lifted it from its velvet resting place, his eyes gleaming with pride. "These swords," he announced with theatrical timing, his voice dropping to an enticing whisper that drew Zane closer, "have quite a remarkable story behind them."
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
The shopkeeper drew the white sword first, presenting it handle-first to Zane.
Zane took it, and the moment it settled into his grip, the sheer weight of it dragged his arm down. "Whoa," he breathed. "This thing is heavy."
Zane's strength was nothing to scoff at; even without tapping into the power of his Dragon Scales, he was far stronger than the average man. But just holding this sword one-handed was a struggle.
Most swords were crafted to be light, their blades thin and nimble. Lighter meant faster, more focused strikes. This sword, however, was clearly forged with the opposite philosophy. Zane couldn't begin to guess what it was made of to be this dense.
The shopkeeper, who clearly knew the answer, just chuckled. "Ah, but that's all part of the story, Mr. Lowell! Please, allow me." He turned to a nearby attendant. "Quickly, two chairs for us."
Zane was torn between amusement and annoyance. The shopkeeper was really laying it on thick---did they honestly need chairs for a story? But before he could say he was fine standing, the attendant had already set two chairs directly behind them.
The shopkeeper gestured for him to take a seat. Once they were both settled, he began. "This sword is called the Nine-Edge Sword. It weighs one hundred times more than an ordinary blade."
"The Nine-Edge Sword?" Zane repeated. He popped the clasp on the sheath and began to carefully draw the blade.
Unsheathing a sword usually produced a clean, sharp sound as steel slid against the scabbard's throat.
[SFX: HEAVY GRINDING SCRAPE]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC DISCOVERY]
But the Nine-Edge Sword was so heavy that all Zane heard was a low, grinding scrape as he pulled it free.
The blade was wider and thicker than most, with a powerful, handsome profile. A fine, cross-hatched pattern was forged into the metal itself, catching the light.
"It's called the Nine-Edge Sword," the shopkeeper explained with a proud smile, "because it was forged by folding the steel of nine different legendary blades into one. And forgive me for sounding dramatic, but it will absolutely change your life."
