Zane knew he could end this right now. One blast of his full power, one overwhelming show of force, and Slade would be dust. The Saint-Killing Spear was deadly, sure, but Zane had the raw strength to shatter it.
But there was another way. A smarter way. He could wear Slade down, dodging the spear’s sharp tip and using the explosive power of his Gods-Intimidating Strike to exhaust him. Besides, his fight with Supreme Lord Sword had taught him a few things, and Slade was the perfect sparring partner to put those new skills to the test.
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]
So Zane closed the distance, forcing them into a brutal, claustrophobic dance.
Slade tried to backpedal, desperate for enough room to swing his spear, but Zane was like a shadow, plastered to him, never more than three feet away. 
[SFX: SPEAR SWINGING THROUGH AIR]
Slade lunged and stabbed, swinging the Saint-Killing Spear in a wild arc, but it was useless.
No matter what he tried, Zane saw it coming. 
[SFX: BLADE DEFLECTING METAL]
Every time the spear was about halfway to its mark, Zane would flick his own blade out—a flash of his Thundergust technique—and the spearhead would be deflected, the attack neutralized before it even had a chance to build momentum. Slade’s moves were completely shut down.
He felt like he was fighting in chains. And he’d been so confident, too! He was an Ace Talent, for crying out loud, wielding the damn Saint-Killing Spear. Even a saint-level genius should have trouble with it, and this guy was making it look like child’s play. He couldn’t even get a clean shot.
Zane just stood there, his face utterly blank, which was somehow the most humiliating part. It felt like an open mockery, his every effort dismissed as a joke.
The warriors watching from the city wall were stunned. This didn’t look like a fight between two masters of the Soul Sea Realm at all.
“Zane is incredible,” one of them breathed. “He’s not even using any special skills. He’s just shutting Slade down with a plain longsword!”
“Slade’s the idiot here,” another scoffed. “He’s got the Saint-Killing Spear, and he’s letting himself get boxed in like that. If that were me, I’d risk it all on one desperate counter.”
“You’d be dead before you could try,” a third shot back. “Zane would cut you down the second you moved. You’re the idiot if you can’t see that.”
[SFX: SOFT PURR]
Janet, holding the little flame kitten in her arms, watched Zane’s movements with narrowed eyes. “He’s not fighting,” she murmured. “He’s practicing.”
“If he’s really using an Ace Talent for sword practice,” Coleman said with a faint smile, “then I’ve seriously underestimated his strength. And his nerve.”
Until now, everyone had pegged Zane as being on par with an Ace Talent, nothing more. And sure, the power levels among Ace Talents varied wildly—Sloane, for instance, was the strongest of them, her power already brushing up against that of a saint-level genius. All she needed was a deeper understanding of the Godly Way to become a candidate for the true gods.
Something ugly stirred in Slade’s chest. If this were a fair, head-on fight, he’d have already put a dozen holes through this arrogant kid’s body. But Zane wasn’t fighting him head-on—he was strangling him, inch by inch, and Slade couldn’t stand it a second longer.
“Get out of my way!” he snarled.
[SFX: SPEAR SPINNING RAPIDLY]
The Saint-Killing Spear whipped into a spinning blur, carving wide circles through the air as Slade tried to force Zane back by sheer momentum.
[SFX: RAPID FOOTWORK]
But Zane matched him step for step, staying glued to him no matter how fast the spear spun. Slade’s mind screamed a single question: what kind of movement technique was this? The guy was like a ghost he couldn’t shake loose.
[SFX: SPINNING SLOWS TO STOP]
When the spinning finally stopped, Zane was still there, sword held close, his face as blank as ever.
“Go to hell!” Slade roared, and the spear shot forward like a diving dragon—full power, full confidence, the same strike he’d planned to carry him into the Illusion Battlefield’s top ten.
[SFX: METAL CLANG]
But the strike only made it halfway. Zane laid the Thundergust flat along the spear’s shaft with a single easy motion, and the deadly weapon was turned harmlessly aside once again.
Zane didn’t press forward. He didn’t give an inch back either. He simply held his ground like a patient chess player, testing the exact limits of his own understanding of the Gods-Intimidating Strike.
As the fight drifted closer to the city wall, eight of Slade’s companions from the Halls of Causality exchanged grim looks. There were no rules at this stage of the Illusion Battlefield—nothing stopped them from lending a hand.
“We should take action,” one of them said, and together they dropped from the wall.
“Oh no—they’re joining in!” Janet’s eyes flashed.
“Then let’s go too,” Coleman said, already moving.
Hailey jumped down right behind them, unwilling to leave the man fighting on her behalf outnumbered.
Excited murmurs broke out along the wall. “There’s more of them on Slade’s side now,” someone called out. “Zane’s people might be in trouble.”
The eight warriors split cleanly: two moved to shield Slade and give him room to fight, three peeled off to attack Zane directly, and the rest moved to cut off Janet, Coleman, and Hailey before they could help.
It was a clean plan.
[MUSIC: RISING DRAMATIC STRINGS]
But they had all made a critical mistake. They had completely misjudged Zane’s true strength, assuming he was pouring every ounce of focus into just blocking Slade’s attacks.
[SFX: WARRIORS LANDING]
[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]
The moment those other warriors leaped from the city wall, however, a sword intent began to condense on Zane’s blade, sharp and impossibly fast. He brandished his sword and thrust it toward the new arrivals.
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY WHOOSH]
“Gods-Intimidating Strike!”
[SFX: SLICING BLADE]
[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]
[SFX: BODIES FALLING]
The condensed energy drew a razor-sharp arc through the air, sweeping along the top of the city wall right where the charging warriors were. The two who had just jumped were caught completely by surprise. They had no time to react, no time to even register the attack before it sliced clean through them. A spray of blood erupted, and their bodies tumbled from the wall, lifeless.
A dead silence fell over the entire area. Everyone—inside the ogre city, outside the walls, even Slade, who’d just been locked in that desperate struggle—was frozen, staring in absolute shock. Was *that* his true power?
But Zane wasn’t finished. After the first strike killed two, he launched a second without a moment’s hesitation. 
[MUSIC: COLD TENSION]
He had fully entered his state of Sealed Emotion, and his actions became brutally methodical. There wasn’t a shred of mercy in him.
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY WHOOSH]
“Gods-Intimidating Strike!”
[SFX: SLICING BLADE]
[SFX: BODIES FALLING]
His longsword slashed out again. Another wave of sword intent ripped through the air, and this time, three more warriors fell.
In the span of a few seconds, Zane had killed five of Slade’s followers. When the rest of his men saw that, they didn’t think twice.
