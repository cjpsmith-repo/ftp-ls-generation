Ep 782 - The Crystal Martial House

Word count: 1047

[AMBIENT: MYSTERIOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STRINGS]

To Zane, the Sacred Feather Sea was a complete mystery. He and Francis had barely stepped away from the wall when Francis's words were still ringing in his ears — one look at the Sacred Feather Sea could get you killed.

He just couldn't wrap his head around how a small inland sea could contain so many hidden lands. After hearing what Francis had to say, his curiosity only grew. 

What was this place? Everyone acted like an ordinary warrior couldn't even steal a glance at it. Could you really die just from looking? The whole idea sounded ridiculous.

"So these walls go all the way around the entire sea?" Zane asked, crossing his arms.

"Yep," Francis confirmed. "They form a perfect circle, sealing the whole thing off. Flying over is impossible — there's a strict No-Fly Ban. Anyone who tries it will have masters from the Life-and-Death Realm on them in an instant."

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL ORCHESTRAL]

Building city walls on that scale, just to enclose an inland sea, was a staggering thought. It seemed more than a little over the top. What kind of treasures could possibly be hiding in there to make it all worthwhile? The War Kings and War Kings of the divine kingdoms weren't fools; they had to have a reason for all this. The more Zane thought about it, the more he wanted to see it for himself.

"Don't even think about breaking the No-Fly Ban," Francis warned, reading his mind. "Let's say you somehow get past the Life-and-Death warriors. Masters from the Soul Sea Realm will show up next. They'll just keep coming, one after another. It's way too dangerous."

Zane just shook his head and laughed. He knew Francis was right; it would be a stupid move. Forcing his way in could attract the attention of a War King, and then he'd lose any chance of getting into the Feather Emperor's Secret Land.

[AMBIENT: LARGE CROWD MURMURING]

Leaving the imposing walls behind, Zane found the Crystal Martial House after a short walk.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL THEME]

It was a sixth-rank house, its entrance marked by a unique design. Sprawled out before it were thousands upon thousands of warriors, all kneeling on the ground.

Zane frowned, taking in the sheer number of them — easily more than ten thousand. Most were at the Soul and Inner-Core Realms. "What's going on here?" he asked.

"Years ago, a warrior failed the entrance exam and refused to leave," Francis said. "He knelt at that gate for ten years, through rain and drought, until an elder finally took pity and let him in despite his lack of talent. After that, word spread. Now every warrior who can't make the cut comes and kneels, hoping someone inside will be moved by their dedication."

"Does it work?"

Francis shrugged. "Rarely. Sincerity only goes so far. Talent still matters."

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Zane was floored. He couldn't believe they would go to such lengths. Back in the Heartlands, a warrior who couldn't get into one sect could always try another, or go independent. Here in the Harlen Divine Kingdom, there was no such option. The Martial Houses held an absolute monopoly. If you wanted to advance, there was no other path.

He walked up to the gate. The guards on either side took one look at him — a Spirit-Tribulation warrior with a Life-and-Death Realm man at his back — and their expressions shifted immediately. They assumed he had to be from a powerful noble family. Imperial relations, maybe. They stopped him politely.

"I want to sit the entrance exam," Zane said.

"The exam runs monthly," the guard replied. "The next one is in half a month. You'll need to register in advance." He walked Zane through the process.

It was exactly as Francis had described. No rejection, no suspicion — within the Harlen Divine Kingdom's borders, Zane was already considered one of its own. Joining a Martial House wasn't like joining a sect. Members were partners, not disciples. The relationship ran both ways, and the benefits were generous — because the four kingdoms competed fiercely with one another over the Secret Territories, each pouring manpower and resources into their houses to stay ahead.

There was one more thing worth knowing: there were only two sixth-rank Martial Houses in the entire Kingdom — one in the capital, one here. Both held quotas to access the Sacred Feather Sea. But earning a quota meant rising to the very top — and every warrior who entered the Sacred Feather Sea was at minimum a Life-and-Death Realm master. Even after passing the exam, Zane would still have to outcompete them.

He had expected as much, but he remained calm. Since his goal was rescuing Tia, he had to be careful and avoid any rash moves. He registered, then left.

[AMBIENT: QUIET TRAINING HALL]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

For the next two weeks, Zane stayed inside the house. He didn't just train; he also bought books, studying the local customs of the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms. The more he learned, the better his advantage. Opportunity favors the prepared, after all. This trip was nothing like his first arrival on the main continent — but he knew that powerful opponents still lay ahead, and he couldn't afford to face them unprepared.

Half a month later, he returned.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING MARTIAL HOUSE]

[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]

Zane registered as instructed and received a token, which granted him entry into the Crystal Martial House. But the moment he stepped inside, four warriors from the Life-and-Death Realm stormed past him from behind. As they moved to block his path, one of them barked, "Move!"

Zane's brow furrowed at the shout.

[SFX: WHIP CRACK]

He turned his head just in time to see the sharp crack of a whip slicing through the air toward his face.

[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]

With a flicker of annoyance, Zane watched as Francis's hand shot out and snatched the whip mid-air. His gaze then shifted past the four guards to the person behind them: a young man, no older than seventeen or eighteen, dressed in a fine brocade robe. He had already reached the late stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.

Zane had a bad feeling about this kid.
