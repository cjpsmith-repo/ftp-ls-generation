[MUSIC: MAGICAL ENERGY]
As Sir Kenneth descended, the other figures floating in the air gradually alighted around him. The entire council of elders from the Skyreach Sect had assembled—an unprecedented gathering—including the reclusive Master Elwood, whose appearances were as rare as summer snow.
Master Elwood, renowned throughout the empire for his unparalleled mastery of the healing arts, walked directly to Zane. His ancient eyes, bright despite his deeply lined face, assessed the young man with clinical precision. "Stop resisting," he advised, his voice carrying the gentle authority of one who had mended thousands of broken bodies and souls.
[SFX: MAGICAL HEALING WAVE]
Zane relaxed his mental barriers, surrendering to the healer’s expertise. A soothing wave of Green Vitality, cool and refreshing, washed through his consciousness, probing delicately for damage.
As he deepened his examination, Master Elwood’s bushy eyebrows rose in astonishment. He murmured to himself. "What? How can this be?"
A mental assault from two Inner-Core Realm warriors would normally shatter a person’s soul beyond repair, leaving them a hollow shell at best. But to the elder’s profound surprise, Zane’s soul remained almost completely intact—resilient where it should have been fragmented. The young man suffered no grave injuries—just exhaustion that clung to him like a thin veil. A brief period of rest, perhaps a day or two, and he’d be fully recovered.
Master Elwood had seen Zane’s remarkable performance and already knew his soul was tougher than most, but this defied all conventional understanding. His internal monologue raced to make sense of it. Those two princes are powerful enough that even I would have to be careful, he thought, genuine amazement flickering across his normally stoic features. That this young man made it through virtually unscathed feels like a miracle. I can’t explain it, but there’s no denying that the kid is damn impressive.
[SFX: SURPRISED EXCLAMATION]
Edmund recognized Sir Kenneth immediately, leaping to his feet with remarkable agility and bowing deeply. "Sir Kenneth!" His voice carried both respect and a hint of trepidation.
Ray, standing nearby, quickly clocked the situation before following Edmund’s example.
Even the emperor himself would be compelled to show obeisance before the formidable leader of the Skyreach Sect, so these two princes—regardless of their royal blood—were certainly not exempt from this tradition.
[MUSIC: REVERENT THEME]
The crowd fell into a hushed silence, gazing in undisguised awe at the imposing figure of Sir Kenneth. His presence seemed to alter the atmosphere of the room, as if the air itself recognized the authority of the most powerful man in the Eastern Territories.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
But to everyone’s shock, Sir Kenneth didn’t even glance at the bowing princes. He completely ignored them, walking straight over to Zane.
He clapped him on the shoulder and grinned. "Look at you, thinking big!
That’s my boy," he laughed. "You want to wipe out this whole country?
Sounds like fun. I’m in!"
Ray’s heart leaped into his throat. Edmund, meanwhile, managed a pained smile that looked more like a grimace.
Ray gathered himself. "Sect Leader, with respect — you're overstepping."
Sir Kenneth looked at him for the first time. "Overstepping? You can bully my disciples, but no one's allowed to push back?"
The guard behind Edmund spoke up, his voice sharp. "Sect Leader, you have an agreement with the Emperor — non-interference in dynastic affairs."
If Sir Kenneth decided to obliterate the Empire, not a single living soul possessed the power to stop him. In the current landscape of power, his might was uncontested.
When Emperor Kane still held the throne, he alone surpassed Kenneth in strength and influence. Under Kane’s vigilant eye, the empire had flourished, experiencing unprecedented prosperity and stability. The people had lived without fear, secure in the knowledge that their emperor’s power protected them from both internal and external threats.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]
But now, Kane was missing. And in the entire Empire, who was left to stand up to a man like Sir Kenneth?
Edmund was surrounded by his guards, and his strongest supporter among them, Bram, was a master at the primary level of the Inner-Core Realm.
But even Bram was no match for Sir Kenneth.
Ray’s man, Hendy, had also reached the primary level of the Inner-Core Realm.
But even if Hendy and Bram joined forces, they wouldn’t stand a chance against Sir Kenneth.
And it wasn’t just him. The vice leaders of the Sect were here as well, an unprecedented show of force.
Isla understood that nothing had fundamentally changed within the palace walls. Her brother, the Crown Prince, was still trapped by his fate. But now, with Sir Kenneth backing Zane, she saw a glimmer of hope—a real chance to turn things around.
If Sir Kenneth valued Zane this much, then maybe one day the young disciple really would have the power to bring the empire to its knees.
Of course, all this talk of destruction was just a threat. Even if Zane gained that kind of power, he’d never use it that way. Warriors of his caliber had no interest in being emperor.
For an ordinary person, becoming emperor was the ultimate ambition. But for a warrior, there were far greater heights to climb.
Even back when Kane held the throne, he'd barely cared about running the country. The Crown Prince had handled the day-to-day affairs of the empire while Kane was off taking missions from Cloud Hall. He was less an emperor and more a pure warrior at heart.
Edmund and Ray exchanged a glance, and it was decided. The brothers didn’t agree on much, but they were on the same page in this moment: Zane needed to get the hell out of the Palace and take Sir Kenneth with him. If letting their sister Isla go was the only way for it to happen, then it was a small price to pay to be left alone by the Skyreach Sect.

[SFX: CHEERFUL CROWD]
[MUSIC: NEW BEGINNINGS]
When Isla and Zane returned to Misty Peak, they were greeted by a hero’s welcome.
The peak was a completely different place. New attunement sanctums and magic arrays had been built, giving the disciples a much better environment for their training.
Misty Peak had made history, going from dead last among the Thirty-Three Peaks to first place. Every disciple reaped the benefits of this change, and they showered Zane with gratitude for making it happen, and Isla for bringing him there in the first place.
Their celebration was long overdue. But it ended abruptly, in a way that no one expected.
