Ep 436 - True Blood

Word count: 1170

[AMBIENT: PRISON CELL]

[SFX: METAL CHAINS]

Zane fought hard not to roll his eyes as he watched the guards grow agitated over the divine glyph he'd just drawn, their faces flushed with a mixture of awe and suspicion.

The main guard asked him again. “Where the hell did you get this?”

There's no way they'd believe that he had drawn it. What should he say?

[SFX: MYSTICAL WHISPER]

A voice suddenly resonated through his mind—the Cobalt Dragon. "Tell them you met a spiritual master named Josh in the Lower World. Say he gave the divine glyph to you."

Zane's pulse quickened. The lie felt dangerous, yet the Dragon's calm certainty steadied him. He drew a slow breath, composing his expression into one of earnest recollection.

"I met a spiritual master in the Lower World," Zane began, watching the guards' reactions carefully. "His name was Josh. He was the one who gave me this divine glyph."

His heart hammered against his ribs as he waited for their response, knowing that a single flicker of doubt in his eyes could unravel everything.

[SFX: IRON GATE UNLOCKING]

The guards exchanged grave looks. After a tense moment, one of them gave Zane a sharp nod, unlocked the iron gate, and let him out of the cell.

The other stowaways from the Lower World could only gawk as Zane walked free.

'What did that Adept-level creature do?' they all wondered. 'Why would they just let him go?'

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
"Wait here," the guard ordered Zane. "I need to report this to the lord of the city." With that, he hurried off, the divine glyph in hand.

"Cobalt Dragon, what message did you leave on that glyph?" Zane asked mentally.

He could feel the Cobalt Dragon's sly amusement. "I just left a trace of my soul on it," the dragon explained. "The guard couldn't recognize the soul signature itself, but he knew the dragon mark I left with it."

"I see," Zane murmured.

The guard brought him to a vast square on the far side of the city. Two old men were waiting. Their cultivation was unreadable — either concealed or simply beyond Zane's ability to gauge.

One of them looked him over and frowned. "He's not Dragon-blooded. Just an ordinary human. Why was he sent here?"

"He has a trace of Drake blood," the other said, studying Zane more carefully. "But not enough to qualify."

The guard stepped forward. "City lord's orders. He's been approved for training."

The first old man's expression shifted — not warmth, but acceptance. "City lord's order," he repeated, as if that settled it. 

[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

The Cobalt Dragon continued. "This place you see before you is not the capital of the Upper World, as you might assume. It's a specialized city, constructed for one sacred purpose: to forge elite Dragon-blooded warriors into weapons of unparalleled power."

A chill ran down Zane's spine—not from fear, but from the weight of realization. Everything he'd seen so far, from the towering spires to the energy-saturated air itself, existed solely to create beings far beyond anything he'd encountered in the Eastern Territories. Even the casual practitioners walking these streets likely possessed strength that would make Soul Realm masters back home seem like children playing with wooden swords.

"How many warriors train here?" Zane asked, his voice barely above a whisper.

The Cobalt Dragon's presence pulsed with ancient pride. "Thousands at any given time. And yet, most will never advance beyond these walls."

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TEMPLE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORIENTAL]

Even the Dragon-blooded who reached the Upper World had to pass a trial to truly enter this realm—it was how the dragons weeded out all but the most gifted. The message on the glyph? That was the Cobalt Dragon's request: for the city's lord to grant Zane a chance to train among them.

Zane nodded, the weight of the opportunity settling on him. If he wanted to reach the pinnacle of martial arts, he'd have to push himself harder than ever before. The Cobalt Dragon had helped him immensely, but Zane knew he couldn't rely on that power forever.

The Cobalt Dragon had saved him from a thousand-year prison sentence—a debt Zane could never repay. This chance to train in the Upper World was another incredible gift. If he could just pass the trial, the rewards would be immeasurable.

"Since you're back in your homeland, why not reveal yourself to them?" Zane asked. The soulforge, the black fire, the Cobalt Dragon and the eight others bound within it—it was all a massive mystery, one that had led him to this very moment.

[AMBIENT: DEEP SILENCE]

A long silence stretched in Zane's mind.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

“Even if I came back, what would be the point?" the dragon finally replied, his voice heavy with a sigh. "Some things just can't be changed." The deep sadness in his tone was unmistakable.

A pang of that same sadness echoed in Zane's own chest.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

[AMBIENT: STONE HALL]

Just then, the guard returned, followed by an old man with an authoritative air. This had to be the city lord. The old man wordlessly tossed Zane a signaling token and a small crystal vial.

"There are training chambers over there," he said, his voice raspy but firm. "Pick one. Absorb this drop of True Dragon's blood. Find me when you're done, and then your training can begin."

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY PULSE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

Zane caught the vial, feeling a frantic, living energy pulsing from within, as if something was desperate to escape.
"Generous of them," Zane muttered to the Cobalt Dragon.
The dragon snorted. "Generous? A full-grown True Dragon is thousands of miles long. Its blood could drown the entire Heartlands. One drop is nothing."

[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION CHAMBER]

He followed the old man's directions, choosing one of the chambers at random. Once inside, he sealed the door, knelt on a floor cushion, and carefully uncorked the vial.

[SFX: CORK POP]

A single, crimson droplet shot out, hovering in the air before him.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TENSION]

It was just one drop of True Dragon's blood, but Zane could feel the immense power radiating from it, a palpable heat against his skin.

"Well, if you're just going to stare at it, you might as well give it to me," the Cobalt Dragon's voice teased in his mind.

Zane knew he was kidding, of course. He played along with a mental pout. "There's only one drop. You want it? No way."

With a grin, he extended two fingers, sending a tendril of Vitality to coil around the droplet. He guided it swiftly into his mouth.

He remembered the thick drake's blood from the Cloud Road, how it would clump back together if it ever splashed. He'd thought that was incredible, but this True Dragon's blood was something else entirely. The single drop fought against his Vitality, writhing as if it had a will of its own.

But the strength of a single drop was no match for him. In the end, he swallowed it down.

[SFX: INTENSE ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

The moment the drop went down his throat, Zane's own blood began to boil. He could feel the True Dragon's blood surging through him, a fiery torrent merging with his very veins.

The pressure was so intense, he thought he might explode.
