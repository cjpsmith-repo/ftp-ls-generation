Ep 875 - The Tribulation Flower

Word count: 1170

[SFX: PICKAX STRIKE]

[SFX: CRYSTAL CHIME]

The Black Star pickax clanked against the giant leaf. Bright blue cracks spiderwebbed across its surface, and one by one, life crystals popped out and tumbled to the ground.

Zane allowed himself a small smile. With a casual wave of his hand, he gathered the crystals and tucked them away in his Storage Ring. 

He’d been a little underwhelmed when he first got the Black Star, since all it seemed to do was dig. But then again, it was a pickax. What else was it supposed to do? 

Now that he had the life crystals, he’d gotten what he came for. Still, after all the trouble he’d gone through to get the pickax, he figured he might as well dig up a few more ores while he was here.

[MUSIC: REGAL THEME]

Just as Zane started looking for his next target, a polite voice cut through the air. It was the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom. “Zane, now that you have the Black Star, I need to ask a favor of you.” 

Of all the Soul Sea Realm masters who probably wanted his help, the Emperor was the one person he couldn’t refuse. He had stepped in to help Zane a few times now, and for all intents and purposes, Zane was considered part of his kingdom. Turning him down wasn’t an option.

“Your Majesty, just tell me what you need,” Zane said, his voice full of respect. “I’ll do whatever I can to help.”

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

The Emperor nodded, a satisfied look on his face. The kid knew his place. “To enter the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms needs a special kind of ore from these leaves. It’s called…”

“A Soul Core, right?” Zane finished, cutting him off before he could even think about it. He remembered Lord Randy mentioning them.

A faint smile touched the Emperor’s lips. “Yes. And there happens to be one about ten feet to your left.”

[AMBIENT: DEEP UNDERGROUND CAVERN]

[MUSIC: DISCOVERY THEME]

As it turned out, the forty-fifth, forty-sixth, and forty-seventh floors were littered with Soul Cores. 

Soul Cores were essential for entering the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, yet the kingdoms never had enough. This year might be different. Every powerful warrior’s hopeful gaze fixed on Zane.

Following the Emperor’s directions, Zane moved over and quickly found an ore under his feet.

[SFX: MAGICAL PULSING HEARTBEAT]

It was perfectly round and throbbed with a soft, steady pulse, like a tiny, buried heart.

“So this is a Soul Core?” Zane’s eyes lit up with excitement. “If I get this, I can enter the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land?”

[SFX: STONE CRACKING]

He raised the Black Star and gave the leaf a light tap. The leaf cracked open, and the Soul Core dropped into his hand.

The Emperor turned to the assembled Soul Sea masters. “Everyone — look around. Mark the Soul Cores under your feet. We’ll need Zane’s help with each of them.”

The Soul Sea masters spread across the upper leaves at once, scanning the surfaces beneath them and calling out.

“Two here — forty-sixth floor.”

“One on this leaf — forty-fifth.”

“Three on the top floor.”

Zane looked at the scene and made a decision. He only needed one Soul Core for himself. The other ores he would give away — the Soul Sea masters clearly wanted them but hadn’t asked. Being generous here cost him little and bought him goodwill he couldn’t afford to waste.

He turned to the Emperor. “I have a request.”

“Go ahead,” the Emperor said.

“I’d like to join the trip to the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land.”

[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]

York’s voice came immediately, cold and flat. “The Heavenly Stellar Secret Land is the most important location in the seven divine kingdoms. You’re an outsider.”

Scott spoke before anyone else could. “Zane isn’t an outsider — he’s a member of the Harlen Divine Kingdom. And with his help, we’ll have over two hundred Soul Cores this year. The old rules don’t apply.”

The Emperor nodded in agreement. York snorted but said nothing. He needed Zane to dig.

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL MINING]

[SFX: RHYTHMIC TAPPING]

Zane got to work. He moved between leaves, tapping the Black Star against each one, pulling out Soul Cores and pocketing the other ores as he went. The Soul Sea masters watched him take the rare materials and held their tongues.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL ANTICIPATION]

This was it. This was his chance.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL STRINGS]

Anyone in the Lower World who reaches the Soul Sea Realm is qualified to ascend, the Cobalt Dragon’s voice explained in his mind. But in the upper world, the Soul Sea Realm is just the beginning—the very first step on a warrior’s path. The Lesser Celestial Tribulation isn’t just a trial; it’s a baptism by fire. Passing through it is a major event, and the more tribulations a warrior can endure, the greater their potential.

The dragon went on to explain that warriors who reached the Soul Sea Realm after passing only five or six tribulations were considered low-tier in the upper world. Those who passed through seven were seen as second-rate. But warriors who could endure eight Lesser Celestial Tribulations? They were considered top-tier talent, and the major powers would spare no expense to train them.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

But someone who can pass nine, the dragon continued, its voice heavy with significance, is a true one-of-a-kind genius. A person like that will undoubtedly shake the world and carve their name into history. Among the great races, you might only see five or six such individuals in ten million years.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]

Zane was floored. It seemed the upper world didn’t bother with rankings like “celestial-level” or “saint-level” geniuses. With the resources available up there, getting a warrior to the Soul Sea Realm was probably easy. What you couldn’t change was the number of tribulations they had faced to get there.

“If that’s the case,” Zane thought aloud, “then people in the upper world must be desperate for this Tribulation Flower, right?”

He had reached the top floor while they spoke. There it was — at the center of the forty-seventh floor’s three leaves, a delicate geometric flower made entirely of ore, its angles precise and unnaturally symmetrical. He’d noticed it from the forty-fifth floor. He couldn’t stop looking at it.

“Break it open,” the Cobalt Dragon said.

“This flower…” Zane hesitated.

“It’s the Tribulation Flower of the Tree of Earth,” the dragon said. “It increases the power of the Life and Death Tribulation — all the way to the maximum.”

Zane stared at it. “Why would anyone want that?”

[SFX: MYSTICAL CHIME]

The Cobalt Dragon chuckled. There are no Gravewood Trees in the upper world. The Tribulation Flower is a gift meant only for you, warriors of the Lower World.

What exactly would the Tribulation Flower do to the warrior who claimed it — and did Zane really want to find out?
