Ep 572 - Block The Way
Word count: 1159
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: DARK ATMOSPHERIC]
Danger was everywhere, but Zane wasn't fazed.
For a place called the Tower of Sin, it was surprisingly safe. It operated under its own set of rules, completely separate from the Cursed Land.
Here, fighting was strictly forbidden outside the arenas. Any beef—whether between sworn enemies or entire rival races—had to be settled in the ring. Anyone who broke that rule would be hunted down and dealt with by every other race in the tower.
[AMBIENT: LARGE ECHOING HALL]
Phoebe led him into a sprawling hall. "These are the living quarters. The practice rooms can be rented for a few points a day. This is just a basic room, but there are exclusive, secret ones that can run you up to a hundred points a day."
Zane arched an eyebrow. "A hundred points a day?" With only twenty points from two wins, a room like that felt a world away.
Phoebe shrugged. "You heard me. In those rooms, time moves at a ten-to-one ratio."
Zane's eyebrows shot up. "A time ratio? What does that even mean?"
Phoebe let out a small sigh, realizing she'd have to spell it out for him. The only way to manipulate the flow of time was with the Time Law.
After his baptism, Zane had gained a fundamental understanding of the nine basic laws of the upper world. But there was one that remained a complete mystery: the Time Law.
Think of it this way: Time is the king, Space is the queen. Together, they were the most powerful forces in the world. The Dragon-blooded didn't have an obelisk for the Time Law because none of them had ever truly mastered it.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WONDER]
The idea of a practice room that could bend time sent a thrill through Zane.
He was running out of it.
If he fought hard and earned enough points, he could rent one of those rooms. One day inside would be the equivalent of ten days back in The Heartlands. A single year would be like a decade. The possibilities were staggering.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Still, it was the Time Law itself that truly fascinated him.
"You said you can find all sorts of cultivation methods here in the Tower," he said to Phoebe. "Are there any for the Time Law?"
"I know of some, but…" Phoebe trailed off, her gaze flicking away from his.
"But what?" Zane pressed.
She just offered him a small, cryptic smile. "You won't find them by sticking to the first floor. You'll have to go deeper."
Zane scoffed. "So how do I get to the next floor?"
Phoebe shrugged again. "One hundred points."
The answer gave him pause. He hadn't run into any real trouble on the first floor, and the fighters so far were hardly a challenge. A hundred points… that shouldn't be too hard to scrape together.
Zane nodded, letting the subject drop. He had no plans to move up just yet.
[AMBIENT: PRACTICE ROOM - QUIET]
[MUSIC: MEDITATIVE CALM]
After renting a basic room, Zane sat cross-legged and drew out his Tower nameplate. It looked ordinary enough, but it had absorbed the Lucky Light from his fights. He activated it.
[SFX: ENERGY FLOW]
The Lucky Light stored inside began flowing into his body — steady, familiar. Different from what he'd used before, but the same in the ways that mattered. It gathered in his core, and his power began to circulate on its own.
He'd felt a breakthrough coming for a while now. This was enough to push him over.
Eighth level.
But it still wasn't fast enough.
He needed to reach War Lord. He needed the inheritance waiting at the bottom of the Tower. Only then would he have a real shot at Maddox — and Maddox wasn't standing still. At any moment, he could find a woman with the Violet-Core Physique and open the array. Time was the one thing Zane couldn't afford to waste.
The thought sharpened something in him. He had someone he could ask.
Seventh Brother, he thought in his mind.
[MUSIC: COSMIC WONDER]
The Cobalt Dragon's Seventh Brother answered quickly. You must stop calling me that. I am more than my brother's brother. Please call me Red Dragon.
Zane swallowed. Of course, sorry for not asking sooner. Red Dragon, I need your help with the Pathforged Realm.
A low chuckle echoed inside his mind. I knew you hadn't forgotten. What I'm giving you is incomplete — only the first half survives. But even a fragment of a transcendent divine might is worth more than any ordinary cultivation method."
The Red Dragon continued. "It's called the Stellar Body; it draws the power of the stars directly into your body. Not visualization — not the impression of starlight. The real thing. The more stars you can draw upon, the stronger your body becomes."
Zane went still. The idea that he could reach out and pull from the stars themselves was incredibly exciting.
The Red Dragon continued. The first stage is simple: draw upon a single star. That's all you need to begin.
Zane looked up at the stone ceiling of the practice room. There are no stars in here.
The Red Dragon chuckled. Ah, yes. I may have forgotten to mention — the Stellar Body requires an open night sky to cultivate.
Zane stared at the ceiling a moment longer, then stood up and walked out.
[AMBIENT: HALLWAY]
The second Zane stepped out of the practice room, Phoebe was on him. "Have a breakthrough?"
Zane just nodded.
"Back to the arena, then?" she asked.
"Not right now," he replied. He wanted to head outside the Tower and see if he could draw on the starlight there.
"Oh, is that right? Because there are a few groups already lined up for you."
[AMBIENT: TENSE CORRIDOR]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
Before he could answer, six or seven massive ogres stepped out from a side corridor, blocking their path.
[MUSIC: CONFRONTATION DRUMS]
"Hey, human," one of them sneered, sizing Zane up.
Zane cut him off with a groan. "I don't have time for this."
"No time?" the ogre scoffed. "You don't have a choice, human."
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING LAUGHTER]
The other ogres rumbled with laughter, but Zane wasn't impressed.
"You really want to make a scene in here?" he asked, his voice low and tight.
"They won't fight you in here," Phoebe murmured with a knowing smile. "But they can definitely block your way out."
The rule against fighting was strict, but that didn't mean there weren't loopholes.
[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]
One common tactic involved "death soldiers"—disposable assassins whose attempts remained untraceable, whether they succeeded or failed. Zane had already proven immune to such methods.
Another approach was provocation. When that failed, the ogres simply blocked the hallway.
Their massive frames filled the narrow corridors, heads nearly scraping the ceiling. They could form an impenetrable wall of muscle—infuriating, but technically within the rules.
