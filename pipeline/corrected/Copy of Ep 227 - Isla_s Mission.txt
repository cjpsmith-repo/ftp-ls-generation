The Seven-Star Platform was the perfect place to hone one's sword intent.
If a swordsman started training at six, he might master the basics by twelve and become a true practitioner by sixteen. But after that, unless you were a prodigy, getting any better meant facing life-or-death trials.
You only live once, right? Easy to say, but putting it into practice meant risking your neck just for a sliver of improvement. It was insanely dangerous.
It didn't matter if you used a sword, a blade, or just your fists---every martial artist eventually hit the same wall.
But the Skyreach Sect had countless magic arrays and secret sanctums designed to sharpen your skills. An outsider would kill for access to just one of them, which was exactly why the Sect was seen as the holy grail of martial arts in the Eastern Reaches. It drew in talented young fighters from all over, every one of them willing to do anything to get in.
Still, even the most gifted among them couldn't improve on the Seven-Star Platform as fast as Zane did. On his first run, he had two major breakthroughs, unlocking concepts most people couldn't grasp in a lifetime. It was a combination of incredible insight and a hell of a lot of luck.
Even after beating level eight, Zane felt like he hadn't hit his ceiling. Steward Song was already pushing him to go back and take a shot at level nine.
Zane's performance was just that mind-blowing. Steward Song was eager to cash in on discovering such a talent, but he had no idea that Steward Carter had pulled the same stunt back at Emberfin Lake.
Back at Misty Peak, a message was waiting for him. Isla wanted to see him.

[MUSIC: SOMBER MELODY]
The chambers were empty. The sheets and quilt on the bed were perfectly made, as if her father hadn't been there in a long, long time.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
Lately, a seed of doubt had taken root in Isla Novak's mind, a nagging thought that maybe her father had already left the Eastern Territory for good.
Apparently, she wasn't the only one. At the palace, the seven princes and three princesses whispered about it among themselves. But the truth was a secret buried so deep, it was impossible for anyone to unearth.
[AMBIENT: HUSHED WHISPERS]
Rumor had it that her father had left long ago to travel the world, with no plans to return. Some even whispered that he might have died somewhere out there.
It sounded insane, but Isla couldn't just dismiss it. After all, her father was the undisputed king of the Eastern Territory---the most talented emperor the Empire had ever seen when it came to martial arts.
In the past, the Head of the Skyreach Sect was always stronger than anyone at the Palace. The Sect called the shots on most things and even had the power to decide the fate of the Empire itself.
This was possible because the Sect didn't just have a ridiculously powerful leader; it also had solid backing from Cloud Hall.
Countless mighty fighters resided in Cloud Hall. To them, both the Skyreach Sect and the Empire were just territories they managed. Because of this, the Empire had no choice but to tolerate the Sect's dominance, terrified of offending Cloud Hall.
Luckily, since the Skyreach Sect was located within the Empire, their interests rarely clashed. More often than not, they had to work together. That's why the powerful Sect was still willing to train so many experts of the Soul Realm and then offer them to the Empire as a gesture of goodwill.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC SHIFT]
But everything changed when Kane Novak took the throne. Kane's power was as strong as, if not stronger than, the Head of the Skyreach Sect. On top of that, he had even joined Cloud Hall, which put him a cut above the Sect's leader in status.
[MUSIC: IMPERIAL POWER THEME]
This was fantastic news for the Empire. They could finally take charge on multiple fronts, making the Skyreach Sect feel more like a subordinate. For the last few decades, the word on the street was that the Sect's influence was fading while the Empire's power was skyrocketing.
As a member of Cloud Hall, Isla's father had been instrumental in this shift, getting pulled into all sorts of matters.
There was no question that Kane was incredibly powerful. To Isla, he stood on a peak so high that no one else could even see the summit. In all the years of the Empire, there had only ever been one Kane Novak.
Isla herself wasn't very powerful, but her status as a princess meant she knew enough of history's secrets to understand there was always a bigger fish. Kane might rule the East, but the moment he stepped outside of it...
As a royal princess, she had no way to control the Skyreach Sect, and neither did Farrell, the crown prince.
Two of her brothers were already moving against Farrell. The Seventh Prince had spent the past year quietly pulling House Walker and House Sutton into his corner. The Third Prince was doing the same with other noble houses. Both were waiting for the right moment — and both knew exactly when that moment would come.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
With no other options, Isla set out to find someone qualified to protect and guide Farrell. But this person couldn't be a disciple from one of the noble houses. So, her only choice was to look among the common-born, which is what led her to become a tutor at Misty Peak. Since Misty Peak was ranked last, most of its disciples were commoners.
Isla knew it was foolish to pin all her hopes on a single disciple from the Skyreach Sect. Throughout history, plenty of common-born disciples had achieved amazing things, but they were few and far between compared to those from the noble houses.
Left with no other choice, Isla had to give it a shot---a last-ditch effort to salvage a hopeless situation. If one of her disciples could, by some miracle, make an incredible leap---or even pass the Cloud Road and be chosen as a disciple by Cloud Hall---there was a chance the problems at the royal palace could be solved.
[MUSIC: SOMBER HOPELESSNESS]
But the odds of finding someone that worthy were laughably small. Practically zero. It's no wonder everyone thought her coming to Misty Peak was just a waste of time.
If only she knew of someone who was capable of beating the odds.
