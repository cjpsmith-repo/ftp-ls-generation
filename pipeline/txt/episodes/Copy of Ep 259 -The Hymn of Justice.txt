Ep 259 -The Hymn of Justice
Word Count: 1070
[SFX: LOUD BOOMING IMPACT]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
The Shadow Phantoms attacked with blinding speed. In a blur of motion, three of them struck twice, and one of Richard’s Hymns of Warding cracked and shattered. With only five Hymns left, he figured he could maybe survive another eight or nine hits.
At this rate, his defenses would be gone in seconds. If they all hit him at once, Richard knew he wouldn’t survive a single blow.
He was in deep trouble. He had to write more Deathly Canticles and more Hymns of Warding. He had his Runic Brush, and normally, his script was flawless—a master’s work. But right now, he was scribbling so frantically he couldn’t spare a thought for penmanship. His script was a crooked, messy scrawl, betraying none of the mastery he usually possessed.
His usually steady hand was shaking violently, all his focus poured into pushing his Vitality to its absolute limit.
But even pushing his Vitality to the brink, he couldn’t write fast enough to replace the hymns as they shattered. The number of Deathly Canticles in his Array of Thirteen Kills was dropping, and his protective Hymns of Warding were dwindling right along with them.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER FORMING]
"One final Hymn of Warding!" Richard gasped, his fingers cramping as he poured his remaining strength into the glowing script.
Each solitary Hymn withstood just two strikes from the relentless phantoms before shattering into golden fragments. At their current velocity, his protection would vanish in a single breath. Throughout the arena, spectators exhaled collectively, the inevitable conclusion looming before their eyes—Richard, despite his valiant effort and remarkable skill, faced certain defeat.
[SFX: ENERGY BATTLE]
Some already began to turn away, unable to witness the final moments of his downfall. Richard’s face, streaked with sweat and etched with determination, betrayed no resignation. His eyes, though weary, still burned with resolve.
Just as the phantoms descended upon his final protective barrier, their shadowy forms poised to shatter his last defense, Richard executed a maneuver that stunned everyone present.
Instead of reinforcing his failing protection or trying to escape, his brush moved with sudden clarity and purpose. His trembling fingers steadied as he began inscribing the intricate patterns of a Hymn of Justice—a technique far more complex and powerful than anything he had attempted thus far in the battle.
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMURS]
Zane concentrated on commanding his phantoms to smash Richard’s final defensive barrier when he sensed an overwhelming force emanating from his opponent. The sensation caught him off guard, disrupting his focus.
This power was different from anything he had encountered before—not piercing or aggressive, but rather a profound, crushing presence that pressed down upon him like the weight of the world. For a fleeting moment, his six Shadow Phantoms hesitated in their advance, their ethereal forms flickering as his dominion over them temporarily weakened.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
"The Hymn of Justice?" Zane’s pupils constricted, his expression turning grim.
He shouldn’t have been surprised. Anyone in the top hundred of the Skyreach Sect List was a force to be reckoned with. Alright then, Zane thought with a mental shrug. Let’s have a good, fair fight. Even facing this overwhelming force of justice, Zane showed no fear. Instead, a defiant light sparked in his eyes as a chaotic energy began to swirl around him.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL WRITING]
Richard dismissed the phantoms from his awareness, his focus absolute and singular. He wrote with fierce determination, each character forming beneath his brush growing increasingly powerful and vibrant with every stroke.
His final defensive barrier held firm against the onslaught. Sweat beaded on his brow as he poured his essence into completing the entire Hymn of Justice, each word now rendered with perfect clarity—a testament to his mastery even under extreme duress.
"Done!" Richard announced, a note of triumph in his voice that masked the exhaustion threatening to overwhelm him.
The Runic Brush in his hand moved with elegant fluidity as he executed the final stroke.
[SFX: MAGICAL HUM]
A low, resonant hum filled the arena, vibrating through the spectators’ bones and raising the fine hairs on their arms. The sound carried an ancient power that spoke to something primal in all who heard it, a reminder of forces that had existed long before any of them drew breath.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
The completed hymn hung in the air beside him, humming with power. Its black, magical ink began to glow with a radiant golden light.
Richard looked like a saint from some ancient legend, radiating an aura so powerful it made you want to bow without a second thought.
[SFX: BEAM OF LIGHT]
His gaze swept across the Shadow Phantoms. He pointed, and a beam of golden light shot from the Hymn of Justice, tearing straight through them.
[SFX: PHANTOMS DISSOLVING]
With a series of pops, the phantoms dissolved.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
But just as quickly, the wisps of shadow coalesced, reforming into new phantoms.
"Impossible," Richard said, his voice flat with disbelief. "Their Corrupt Vitality can’t be endless. They shouldn’t be able to reform after being destroyed so many times." He gestured again and again.
Under his control, beam after beam of golden light struck the Shadow Phantoms. But no matter how many times he shattered them, they stubbornly reformed.
Zane watched, his own mind racing.
The phantoms weren’t immortal, of course.
Everything in Heaven and Earth was bound by time; the phantoms had to have a limit. At the same time, he knew Richard's Hymn of Justice channeled the Spirit of Justice—a force immune to normal attacks, even from a Mythic Weapon.
[SFX: ENERGY DEVOURING]
But as Zane’s Corrupt Vitality wrapped around the hymn, it began to devour the golden light.
"It’s swallowing the light? No way!" Richard’s confidence crumbled into panic. He’d never imagined Zane’s Corrupt Vitality could actually consume the light of his Hymn. As the words on its surface began to dim, a terrifying thought hit him: it was being devoured.
Richard's eyes snapped to Zane. He'd made a huge mistake. He shouldn’t have been wasting his time on the phantoms. He should have gone for Zane from the start. Take Zane down, and the phantoms would disappear.
[SFX: MASSIVE ENERGY BEAM]
Finally, Richard knew what he had to do. He thrust out his hand, and a massive beam of golden light—six times thicker than before—erupted from the Hymn of Justice and shot straight for Zane.
