Ep 489 - Slow and Steady

Word count: 1115

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

As the shimmering curtain of light at the center of the arena dissolved, the match between Blake and Edgar began.

In a fight like this, everything mattered—your state of mind, your physical conditioning, and the very methods you trained with.

Blake and Edgar were prime examples. Both had reached the Inner-Core Realm and were top students in their respective sects, so their minds were sharp, their focus absolute. They’d both trained with a tier-four cultivation method and were at the primary level of the Core-Forged Realm.

Today was the ultimate test of their mastery over the Thunder Law.

[SFX: ELECTRIC CRACKLING]

The moment the match began, a shroud of purple lightning enveloped Blake, casting him in the image of a Thunder God—arcs of electricity coiled around him like thin snakes, crackling across his skin. Across the arena, Edgar looked every bit his equal, a ball of raw lightning crackling in each palm. The spheres pulsed with a violent, unstable rhythm, humming with terrifying power.

[SFX: LIGHTNING STRIKES]

The arena roared to life as they unleashed a volley of lightning strikes, weaving a blinding web of light through the air. They clashed again and again, each impact erupting in a searing white flash, followed by the sharp crack and deafening boom of thunder!

[AMBIENT: CHEERING CROWD]

The martial artists in the crowd were on the very edge of their seats, captivated by the fighters' blinding speed. Most were stunned into silence, eyes glued to the spectacle, while others erupted in cheers, roaring the name of their favored champion.

Those who specialized in thunder-based techniques were in a state of pure focus, their eyes tracking every move, trying to burn each step and gesture into their memory.

Mastering a Law Power was a grueling journey, no matter where you were in the universe. The inscribed stone tablets were accessible only to the fourth and fifth-grade sects, making it nearly impossible for independent warriors to develop Law Power independently. Anyone from the lower-grade sects had to teach themselves, a path with no guidance and no guarantee of success. Grasping even a sliver of Law Power could take a lifetime. For them, watching Blake and Edgar was more than a fight—it was a lesson. A rare chance to gain real insight. That was why they watched with such desperate intensity.

In the stands, the students from the major sects sat with deliberate calm. Their faces were blank masks, but behind them, their minds were racing, sizing up the two fighters, constantly measuring their techniques and strength against their own.

[SFX: POWERFUL ROAR]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

"Give up!”Blake roared, his voice cutting through the thunder.

[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

The purple lightning that had been coiling around him like a sleek snake suddenly swelled, thickening into a raging python of energy.

Edgar’s expression hardened. In response, the lightning balls in his hands compressed, shrinking to the size of his fists but glowing with even greater intensity.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

[SFX: BLINDING FLASH]

[AMBIENT: ARENA RUMBLE]

The python of lightning crashed into the condensed spheres. A massive explosion rocked the arena, and a searing white light bleached all color from the world, forcing everyone to squeeze their eyes shut.

Blake staggered back a few steps, his face pale. But Edgar was thrown more than ten feet, skidding across the stone before finding his footing. A trickle of blood seeped from the corner of his mouth.

They were both at the primary level of the Core-Forged Realm. They had both mastered the Thunder Law to a similar degree. The difference, now painfully clear, was in their cultivation methods. Blake's was just that much stronger.

[MUSIC: SOMBER RESOLUTION]

“You win,” Edgar said, wiping a smear of blood from his mouth. His gaze was a complicated mix of disappointment and respect. “Looks like I’ve still got a ways to go.”

Blake just gave a slight nod, saying nothing. He had to admit, if they weren't fighting on this stage, he wouldn't have beaten Edgar so easily.

[AMBIENT: QUIET GRANDSTAND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WHISPER]

From his seat in the grandstands, Zane watched the aftermath. Suddenly, the voice of the Cobalt Dragon's Seventh Brother echoed in his mind.

Did you see that? That’s the power of the Thunder God’s Form. A sign of extreme strength.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT DETERMINATION]

All my Nexus Points are refined,* Zane thought, his eyes lighting up. *I should be able to break through to the Pathforged Realm! I *will* learn the Transcendent Divine Might!

[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATIVE]

I gave you my word, and I don't go back on it,* the Seventh Brother cautioned. He paused for a beat. *But I'm warning you, it’s not as simple as you think. Reaching the Pathforged Realm is hard enough, but it's especially brutal here in the Heartlands. In fact, it's easier to break through to the Inner-Core or even the Spirit-Tribulation Realm than it is to reach Pathforged!

I get it, Zane shot back mentally, his excitement hardening into a cold, steely resolve.

He knew chasing advanced knowledge was a long, hard road. But slow and steady had always won the race. That was a truth as old as time itself.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]

While Zane and the Seventh Brother spoke in his mind, Blake was helped from the arena and led back toward the Cloud Hall group.

A collective sigh rippled through the stands. They all knew Blake was strong, but no one had realized he was this strong—even stronger than Edgar.

Watching the intense showdown, many warriors felt the supreme Aether crystals they'd spent had been well worth it. The trip to Oracle City was proving to be worth every bit of the price of admission.

[SFX: SHUFFLING NAMEPLATES]

[AMBIENT: EXPECTANT CROWD]

Once the arena floor was cleared, the old man mounted the platform again. He reached into the container, his hand swirling through the nameplates before pulling out two. His voice boomed across the stadium.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC DRUMS]

"Brody Booth of Bloodwood Cliff versus Zane Lowell of Cloud Hall!”

[SFX: AGGRESSIVE SNORT]

[MUSIC: MENACING STRINGS]

Hearing his name, Brody sniffled, wiping his mouth with the back of his hand as he stalked to the edge of the grandstand. His scowl twisted into a predatory grin. “Good, good,” he rumbled. “Finally. I get to fight him!”

Brody had just been complaining to a friend that he hadn't gotten a crack at Zane yet. He never figured his name would be drawn so soon.

“Looks like luck's on your side, Brody!” one of the other warriors called out, clearly trying to butter him up. “You're totally gonna kick that kid's ass!”
