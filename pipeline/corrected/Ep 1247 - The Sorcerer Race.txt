Ep 1247 - The Sorcerer Race

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TENSION]
[AMBIENT: EERIE CAVERN ECHOES]
The black venom spraying from the World Tree Venom Bugs was corrosive as hell, eating away at anything it touched.
[SFX: SIZZLING ACID]
[SFX: ENERGY SHIELD CRACKLING AND DISSOLVING]
In the blink of an eye, the orange shield of light protecting Harley fizzled out and dissolved into nothing.
[SFX: MONSTROUS ROAR FROM DEPTHS]
A moment later, a savage, hungry roar echoed up from the chasm below.
Even though you couldn’t actually die in the Illusion Battlefield, the pain was one hundred percent real. Being slowly eaten away by that venom? That was a special kind of agony no one wanted to experience—an unbearable, creeping death. The monstrous sound from the depths sent a chill down everyone’s spine, and they immediately started scrambling for an escape route.
“Let’s get out of here,” Rowan said, her voice tight but determined. “If we can just get past this section, we should hit the first branch!”
After what happened to Harley, no one dared to slow down. 
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS RUNNING]
They ran without stopping, terrified the World Tree Venom Bugs would swarm them again. A little while later, a faint green light flickered in Zane’s eyes. 
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL DISCOVERY]
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT FOREST ATMOSPHERE]
Through the thick white mist, Zane spotted a massive branch wide enough to hold a city.The World Tree - sacred home of the ancient Sorcerer Race. Their greatest mages rivaled the Supreme Lords. 
Legend claimed one possessed the universe’s most powerful Heavenly Destiny, a power so terrifying that even now, the mere thought made people tremble.
They said all the sorcerer needed was a name and a face.
With a single thought, he could imagine his enemy’s death, and it would happen instantly. A kill switch in his mind. If Zane had that kind of power, he could take someone out just by picturing it. The human imagination is endless; who could possibly stand against a power like that? It was weaponized thought.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
But then, for some unknown reason, the Sorcerer Race had vanished from the universe. At their peak, they controlled thousands of worlds and were one of the most brilliant civilizations of their time, but the entire race just disappeared. No traces, no bodies, not even bones left in their sacred places. Their vanishing act became one of the biggest mysteries of all time.
Zane hopped lightly onto the massive branch. “Why are we stopping? Can’t we just keep climbing?”
Rowan shook her head. “It’s not that simple. From the first branch up, there’s a deadly spell protecting the tree. Fly straight up without following the path, and you’ll explode. No exceptions. Aurora found that out the hard way, the first time this gate opened.”
[MUSIC: ANCIENT RITUAL DRONE]
[AMBIENT: HOLLOW WOODEN CREAKING]
She led them along the trunk until they reached its end, where an ancient wooden plate sat waiting. Arranged across its surface, fist-sized skulls were lined up in neat rows — too small to belong to any adult. Zane’s jaw tightened at the sight.
“Step on the plate. It breaks the curse. Old Sorcerer Race ritual,” Rowan said, and stepped onto it first.
[SFX: PLATE ROTATING]
[SFX: SKULLS CHATTERING]
As the others followed, the plate began to slowly spin beneath their feet. At the same time, the tiny skulls’ jaws clicked open and shut, letting out a dry, rasping cough with every motion. Even warriors of the Soul Sea Realm felt their skin crawl at the sound echoing through the empty space.
[SFX: PLATE STOPPING]
Eventually, the plate ground to a halt, and the skulls fell silent. A faint light rippled up from beneath their feet, resolving into a line of glowing script.
“There,” Rowan said. “We’re clear to keep going.”
They pushed on, and the pattern repeated — another trunk, another wooden plate, another line of unlocking script — seven times in total, each climb a little faster than the last since nothing stood in their way.
On the seventh trunk, Zane felt it before he saw it: a powerful aura bleeding down from somewhere above.
Rowan’s face lit with relief. “We caught up! The Ogres haven’t broken through Aurora’s array!”
Zane didn’t wait. He shot forward in a streak of light, arcing through the air and landing hard on the branch ahead of the others, his eyes already scanning the fighting below.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[AMBIENT: COMBAT CHAOS]
[SFX: CLASHING WEAPONS]
Meanwhile, on the seventh branch, Amber and Aurora were fighting for their lives. 
They’d been ambushed the moment they arrived, a whole squad of Ogre martial artists charging them out of nowhere. For hours, they’d been barely holding on, relying on Aurora’s Falling Leaves Array and Amber’s sharp sword skills.
None of Amber’s gear was truly hers — when the Illusion Battlefield pulled a cultivator’s state into itself, it mirrored what they’d had going in, and Amber had walked in with nothing. A prisoner didn’t get to keep a sword. 
Aurora had equipped her from scratch, and when Aurora learned exactly what Amber had been imprisoned for and by whom, he’d gone quiet, then promised her freedom once the Battlefield ended — a promise that meant everything, even coming from someone whose own mother sat on the Board of Elders, whose own clan feared crossing the Queen of Punishment as much as anyone else did.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
Honestly, if she died here, it wouldn’t be the worst thing. At least her time in the Illusion Battlefield with Aurora had been happy.
 Aurora was her only real friend in the Upper World—the kind of friend who had once, in a hushed and furious voice, talked about trying to free Amber from the Queen of Punishment’s grasp. No one dared to stand against the Queen, who had executed the head of the Board of Elders just a few years back, but the fact that Aurora had even considered it meant everything.
It was why, the three times they’d passed through the Deadwood Gate, Amber had willingly transferred all her Illusion Points to Aurora. It was the only way she knew how to help her friend.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[AMBIENT: COMBAT CHAOS]
Amber gripped her sword, her gaze drifting to Aurora, who stood calmly at the center of the swirling chaos, maintaining the array. A strange sense of peace washed over her.
[SFX: RAZOR LEAVES WHIRLING]
[AMBIENT: OGRE GRUNTS AND STOMPING]
Above them, countless thumb-sized leaves swirled in a complex, deadly pattern. Each leaf was razor-sharp, capable of slicing through protective energy and skin with equal ease. 
This whirlwind of mayhem was the only thing holding back more than ten Ogre warriors, who stomped their huge feet and grunted in frustration. If any of them dared to step into the Falling Leaves Array, they’d be sliced to ribbons while also having to deal with the blinding flash of Amber’s sword.
The standoff had finally worn their patience thin.
“That’s it! I’m going in!” one of the Ogres roared, his voice raw with desperation. “I’m going to tear those two Demon Night cultivators to pieces!”
