The other participants stood motionless, their faces frozen in disbelief. Their eyes took in the tower, drinking in every detail of Zane's creation. They gaped at the sheer destructive power of the thing

Even from a distance, his tower existed in a different category entirely—not just superior, but fundamentally transformed. Their minds churned frantically as they tried to figure it out. What technique had he employed? What hidden advantage had he discovered that eluded everyone else?

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

The power radiating from Zane's tower was something they couldn't even comprehend. Some of the younger fighters felt completely overwhelmed, while veterans who'd survived countless battles found themselves questioning their own expertise. This wasn't supposed to happen—not in the first round, not from someone at the third level of the Soul Realm.

The pre-qualifiers had already weeded out the weak, eliminating three-quarters of the original four hundred participants. The ones who remained represented the best of the best.

The round with the giant obsidian crabs had tested them mercilessly. Those hill-sized monstrosities, with their impenetrable shells and crushing pincers, had pushed every participant to their absolute limits. Only fighters with both overwhelming strength and iron patience had made it through the relentless onslaught.

Most of them had thrown everything they had at the giant crabs. Some had powerful secret skills, while others had burned through their precious Life-Essence. A few had even sustained injuries that would leave permanent scars. Yet they'd all succeeded, upgrading their towers to level ten through sheer determination. The path forward had seemed clear: challenge the remaining competitors, defeat them systematically, and claim one of the coveted spots in the main tournament.

But Zane's tower shattered that comfortable narrative. The sight of it drained the color from their faces, extinguished the fire in their eyes. Hope, so carefully cultivated through rounds of brutal combat, evaporated like water on hot stone. Their chances of advancement—once bright and tangible—now seemed impossibly remote, maybe even nonexistent.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION RISING]

The moment that realization hit, their shock curdled into bitter anger. It was just so unfair that Zane's tower was leagues ahead of everyone else's. The same thought ran through all their minds: 'He's only at the third level of the Soul Realm, so how the hell is his tower stronger than ours? It's impossible! He must have cheated!'

In the real world, they would have no choice but to swallow their pride and accept defeat. After all, picking a fight with someone like Zane would be suicide—a one-way ticket to an early grave. But this was the magic array, where death held no permanence. The knowledge emboldened them, stripping away the caution that normally governed their actions.

Without a second thought, they surrendered to their rage.

[SFX: ANGRY CROWD ROAR]

"Get the cheater!" someone screamed, voice cracking with fury.

"Rush him! There's no way he can take us all on at once!" another bellowed, fist raised high.

The crowd surged forward like a breaking wave, individual grievances merging into collective outrage.

"The World Merchants' Guild must have rigged this for him!" a woman shouted, her face flushed crimson. "He cheated, no doubt! This is complete bullshit!"

The accusation spread through the mob like wildfire. Of course—it was the only explanation that made sense. How else could someone at the third level of the Soul Realm possess such overwhelming power? The Guild must have stacked the deck in his favor, given him advantages the rest of them could only dream of.

[MUSIC: GROWING DANGER]

As the mob charged forward, archers positioned atop their level-ten towers drew their bows in perfect unison. Hands trembling with adrenaline, they nocked their arrows—each one shimmering with condensed ice energy. The temperature around the towers dropped noticeably as frost spread across the bowstrings.

Then, as one, they released.

[SFX: ARROW VOLLEY]

A volley of glowing ice arrows streaked through the air, all converging on a single target.

[SFX: ARROWS WHISTLING]

The air hissed with the sound of a hundred arrows slicing toward their target.

Each level-ten tower had ten archers. With over a hundred towers firing at once, Zane was facing a massive assault. Every arrow from a level-ten tower packed the punch of a full-on attack from a mid-level Inner-Core Realm fighter. That meant Zane was staring down the combined force of over a hundred of them.

The volley of arrows practically blotted out the sky, casting a chilling shadow and pressing down on him with immense power.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

Faced with such a brutal assault, Zane's expression hardened into something dead serious. He'd never looked more focused. He could have easily let his level-twelve tower take the hits, but he refused. He wasn't going to hide behind it.

'Avoid,' Zane commanded his tower telepathically, and the massive structure began to move.

They had noticed Zane when he'd first shown up at the test site, but they’d written him off. What could a fighter at the third level of the Soul Realm possibly do? Now, their arrogance had backed them into a corner they couldn't escape.

"What do we do? Go all out? If we all attack together, we might have a shot!" one of them said, trying to sound confident.

His companion just shook his head grimly. "Each one of his archers is as strong as a Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior. Even if we throw everything we have at him, our odds of winning are, what, twenty percent? It’s not worth it."

"The guy's ruthless. He didn't even blink when we brought up the Violet Lustre Order. Our name means nothing to him. So what's the plan?"

"Let's try to cut a deal for now. He's obviously a rising star. We can always crush him later if we cross paths in the main tournament."

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

They exchanged a look, a cruel glint in their eyes. One of them stepped forward, offering Zane a shallow bow. "Hey, hold on a second!"

Zane's patience was wearing thin, but he decided to play along. A smirk touched his lips as he turned to them. "So, you've made up your minds? Let's hear it. What's the offer?"

"We're from the Quarryheart Sect," the man said, gesturing to his friend. "Give us the other two spots, and we'll each pay you fifty supreme Aether crystals. How about it?"

[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]

Zane glanced around, completely unimpressed. Then, he raised his voice, shouting to the entire crowd, "Fifty supreme Aether crystals for a spot in the main tournament! Do I hear any higher bids?"

A single supreme Aether crystal was worth a million low-grade ones—fifty was an astronomical sum, even for an Inner-Core Realm fighter. The other participants were floored that Zane wasn't even batting an eye at the offer.
