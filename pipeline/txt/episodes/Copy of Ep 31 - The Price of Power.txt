Just then, a brilliant flash erupted from within the Skyreach Sect, and a Skycruiser shot out, gliding toward them. Two armored figures stood on its deck, their sharp eyes scanning the area. Patrol.
"It's the inspectors," one of the black-robed disciples muttered.
Perrin scowled at the sight of the Skycruiser. The Skyreach Sect had strict rules against its members fighting. But with so many disciples training in close quarters, brawls were inevitable. Everyone just knew to keep an eye out for the inspectors.
With the inspectors hovering overhead, Perrin and his crew couldn't make their move. Caleb shot Zane a sneer, clapped Perrin on the shoulder, and strutted away, hands clasped behind his back like he owned the place.
[MUSIC: THREATENING UNDERTONE]
Perrin spun on Zane. "You got lucky," he snarled. "Don't think just because you made it to the Capital, you're safe. It doesn't matter if you get into the Sect or not—you're a dead man. I won't touch you today, but I'll be watching. Waiting for the perfect moment to end you. If you're smart, you'll find a nice, quiet alley and do the job yourself. Trust me, it'll be a hell of a lot less painful than what I have in mind. The choice is yours."
Then Perrin and the other black-robed disciples turned and followed Caleb.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS MARCHING AWAY]
Once the black-robed disciples had left, Ethan Grant, who'd been watching from a distance, slowly approached. "Zane, are you crazy?" he said, his voice full of awe. "Even pledges don't dare to mess with Core Apprentices. And you went right after them before you've even passed the entrance exam?"
Ethan lowered his voice. "That one at the back — the one Perrin was deferring to. You need to watch out for him."
"Caleb?" Zane said.
"If I'm reading it right, he's from one of the Legacy Houses. The Warricks." Ethan's expression was grave. "That's how your cousin got his black cloak. He didn't earn it — he found the right patron."
Zane said nothing. He'd suspected as much. A stolen Aether Crystal and a powerful friend in the right place — that was Perrin's entire story. It always had been.
Somehow, the Furnace had emerged to swallow the Hellfire Blade. It had then ignited a Dragon Scale, granting Zane a massive surge of power.
If he could just light a second one, his strength would multiply.
Before his run-in with Perrin, Zane hadn't felt any urgency to light that second Dragon Scale. But the confrontation changed everything. Getting into the Skyreach Sect was going to be even harder than he'd imagined. He was on his own here in the Capital, and to survive, he had to get stronger. Fast.
[AMBIENT: MEDITATIVE CALM]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ENERGY]
Settling cross-legged on the ground, Zane closed his eyes and slipped into meditation, his consciousness reaching delicately toward the mysterious Furnace within his mindscape. The Black Fire had manifested unexpectedly during his confrontation with Jace, transforming the deadly Hellfire Blade into pure Celestial Essence—a memory that still sent shivers down his spine.
Would the enigmatic Black Fire appear again at his command? Zane harbored serious doubts, his uncertainty gnawing at him like a persistent hunger.
The imposing Furnace hovered motionless in his mental landscape, suspended in perfect stillness. Not a single spark or flicker disturbed its dormant surface.
Zane directed his spirit toward it, circling the ancient structure twice while attempting to awaken its power. His efforts yielded nothing but silence, frustration building with each passing moment.
How did one activate such a primordial artifact? The Ancient Smithing Manual he'd discovered provided no practical guidance—merely a collection of cryptic Magical Words that danced beyond his comprehension. The burden of discovery rested entirely on his shoulders, a weight that felt increasingly heavy as time slipped away.
Drawing a deep mental breath, Zane cleared his thoughts and focused his will with renewed determination.
[AMBIENT: DEEP MEDITATION]
His spirit halted before the Furnace, examining its intricate surface. Among the nine Dragon Engravings etched into its ancient metal, only a single one showed signs of life: the Cobalt Dragon. Its piercing gaze locked directly onto Zane's spirit, intelligent and predatory, as if evaluating his very essence.
Zane's heart sank.
[SFX: ATMOSPHERIC PRESSURE]
He remembered how the dragon's gaze had nearly shattered his soul before. Even though his soul had been tempered and strengthened by the Furnace, the Cobalt Dragon's stare was still immense. An overwhelming pressure settled over him, like the weight of a mountain, stealing the breath from his lungs.
His soul was strong enough to keep from being crushed, but that was all. He had to find a way to communicate with this thing. Before the raw power of the Cobalt Dragon, he felt as insignificant as a speck of dust.
[MUSIC: INTENSE DETERMINATION]
Zane forced his spirit toward the Cobalt Dragon engraving. With every inch he gained, the pressure intensified. He had to fight the primal urge to turn and flee. "Don't stop. Keep going!" his mind screamed.
The pressure mounted with each step, slowing him to a crawl. His spirit felt heavy, sluggish, as if wading through thick mud. The force radiating from the Cobalt Dragon was so intense his teeth began to chatter, and it took every ounce of his will to fight the instinct to run for his life.
The outcome of Zane's painstaking efforts fell dismally short of his expectations.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
A Mid-Tier Weapon dissolved completely into a full, glistening drop of Celestial Essence. Yet the essence extracted from a Basic Enchanted Weapon proved disappointingly minuscule—barely visible specks that forced him to narrow his eyes in concentration just to confirm their existence.
After methodically melting down every weapon stored in his Storage Ring, Zane's accumulated harvest amounted to a mere five drops of Celestial Essence. His shoulders slumped with disappointment as he watched a tendril of black flame gather these precious drops and dart back into the depths of his mindscape.
[SFX: FLAME]
Closing his eyes, Zane shifted his awareness inward, tracking the flame's journey with intense concentration. His breath caught as the scene unfolded within his mind's eye.
[SFX: DRAGON RUMBLE]
[SFX: VIBRATIONAL RUMBLES]
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY PULSE]
The black flame approached the imposing Cobalt Dragon and carefully deposited all five drops of Celestial Essence into its waiting maw. The dragon engraving shuddered in response, sending vibrations through Zane's consciousness. To his amazement, a second scale on the creature's body—the Green Scale—began to pulse with an emerald light, growing steadily brighter as if on the verge of ignition.
"Did it work?" Zane thought, his heart racing with anticipation. A hopeful smile crept across his face, warming his features that had been set in concentration for hours. He could almost taste the power that would surge through him if another Dragon Scale activated, multiplying his strength when he needed it most.
[SFX: FADING MAGICAL ENERGY]
But his elation proved premature. The Green Scale flickered uncertainly for several moments, its light wavering like a candle in the wind before gradually fading into darkness once more.
The smile melted from Zane's face, replaced by a bitter grimace of frustration. His fists clenched at his sides as the reality of his situation sank in.
"Six drops," he whispered to himself, the words tasting like ash in his mouth. "It takes at least six drops of Celestial Essence just to activate a single Dragon Scale?" The magnitude of resources required staggered him, each scale representing a mountain of wealth he didn't possess.
