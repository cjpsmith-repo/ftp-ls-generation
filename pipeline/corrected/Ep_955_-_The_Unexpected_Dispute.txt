Ep 955 - The Unexpected Dispute
Word count: 1245
[AMBIENT: HEAVENLY PALACE COURTYARD]
Kieran had been keeping tabs on the Heavenly Stellar Palace for a while. So, when he heard Zane was finally back, he dropped everything and raced over.
Zane, on the other hand, was just confused by the frantic look on Kieran’s face. What now? he wondered.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Panting, Kieran caught his breath, calmed himself, and laid it all out. It didn’t take long for Zane to put it all together—Cora and Tia were fighting again. What was it this time?
Anyone who knew them knew they weren’t even in the same league when it came to their training. Tia was a master in the Life-and-Death Realm; Cora had only reached the Inner-Core Realm. A whole Spirit-Tribulation Realm stood between their power levels. They weren’t even close.
But Cora’s strength was deceptive. She wasn’t a conventional warrior. She drew her power directly from the Violet Core Realm, not from her own cultivation.
Now, with Zane having already taken out both Maddox and Simon, the Heartlands were mostly clear. Aside from a few rogue masters who’d stayed in the shadows, Tia and Cora were arguably the most powerful figures left.
A real fight between them would be a clash of titans.
[MUSIC: QUIET REFLECTION]
If Zane was being honest, this was partly his own doing. He’d woken Tia and then left in a hurry — no proper conversation, no arrangements made. After he was gone, the atmosphere between the two women had shifted. It didn’t take long for Kieran and the others to notice that something had changed, even if nothing had been said out loud.
The truth was, both of them had reason to dig in.
Cora’s temperament had softened around Zane — she’d even looked after Tia while Tia was unconscious, which was no small thing. But that wasn’t the same as backing down. Her nature was to never admit defeat. She could give ground on almost anything. Not this.
Tia was different. She’d been a prodigy since childhood, running Cloud Hall before most people her age had even chosen a path. She was older than Zane, and her feelings — real as they were — showed themselves carefully, in small increments, never all at once. She pursued perfection in everything.
Neither of them was going to make this easy.
[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL COUNTRYSIDE]
[MUSIC: CALM REFLECTIVE STRINGS]
In the wake of Zane dismantling the Corrupted Divine Kingdom and eliminating Simon for good, a fragile peace had settled over the Heartlands. The last remnants of the old order were crushed, and organizations like the Ethereal Spirit Sect and Ethereal City began the slow, careful process of rebuilding. Other groups, like the Cloud Hall and the World Merchants’ Guild, were growing steadily. But all that peace meant the region’s fighters had less and less to talk about.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION RISING]
Then, out of nowhere, the feud between Cora and Tia became the talk of the Heartlands, spreading like wildfire.
Before this, rumors had been swirling that Tia and Zane were an item. Most people knew Zane was close to Cora, the young lady from House Kensington, but they had no idea she was the one who had summoned the storm of swords that annihilated the forces of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom. Now that their argument had blown up into an open challenge, news like that was bound to travel fast.
Overnight, Zane became the envy of half the men in the Heartlands.
Tia had always carried herself with a distant, almost untouchable air, a reputation reinforced by her high rank and incredible power. Most men could only admire her from afar.
And now, here was Zane, with both of these incredible women supposedly fighting over him.
It was already common knowledge that Zane was a powerhouse—he’d killed Maddox and recruited top-tier masters to take down divine kingdom leaders.
[MUSIC: BITTERSWEET STRINGS]
What most people didn’t know was that behind the gossip, something genuinely significant had just happened. Tia had received a message on her signaling token. Elder Imogen was alive.
Elder Imogen had gone overseas before Zane had even left the Heartlands. Her strength had been fading — she couldn’t survive a second Life-and-Death Tribulation, and going overseas to search for an opportunity was the only path left to her. Everyone understood what that meant. Warriors who left for the open sea in search of new chances almost never came back.
Tia had said her goodbyes. Elder Imogen was the last family she had — after everything had been taken from her, her master was the only one left. Losing her to the sea had been something Tia had quietly learned to accept.
[SFX: SOFT CHIME — SIGNALING TOKEN]
And then the token had lit up. Elder Imogen’s voice. She was alive. She had crossed the second Life-and-Death Tribulation and was already on her way back to Oracle City.
[MUSIC: OVERWHELMING RELIEF]
The World Merchants’ Guild organized a banquet to welcome Elder Imogen home. Leaders of the fourth-tier sects across the Heartlands were invited — Elder Xander among them, still deep in the work of rebuilding the Ethereal Spirit Sect.
It should have been a simple occasion. Elder Imogen had returned stronger than she’d left, the Heartlands were at peace, and there was plenty to celebrate. Kieran, Hobbes, and the others had organized it with good intentions.
Then Elder Xander, in the course of polite conversation, casually mentioned Cora; his great-granddaughter would one day marry Zane of the Cloud Hall. A matter of course, in his eyes. Something to be happy about.
[SFX: SHARP GASP]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
But when Elder Imogen heard Xander casually mention Cora’s name, she was stunned, and her face clouded over. She spun to look at Tia, who was staring blankly into space, and the master—a woman known for her fiery temper—finally snapped.
She knew Tia had feelings for Zane. And while she once thought Zane wasn’t good enough for her prize student, she’d since changed her mind. In her eyes, it was a perfect match: the master of the Cloud Hall, the most powerful sect in the Heartlands, and its strongest warrior. They were practically made for each other. So who was this Cora girl, coming out of nowhere to get in the way?
“A sparrow is a sparrow,” Elder Imogen said coldly. “How can it become a phoenix?”
No one in that room was a lightweight. Even with the Ethereal Spirit Sect still rebuilding, Xander was a force to be reckoned with. It was only a matter of time before his sect regained its former glory. When he heard the Cloud Hall leader’s insult, his expression darkened. He adored his granddaughter and wasn’t about to stand by while someone dragged her name through the mud.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ROOM]
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
The two leaders started arguing, and the air in the room crackled with tension. Kieran, Hobbes, and the others exchanged nervous glances, all of them starting to regret this little gathering. No one had expected a stray comment to ignite such a powder keg.
If it had just been Xander and the Cloud Hall leader, things might have eventually cooled down. They could have sorted it out once Zane returned. But when Brent saw his sister being insulted, he got furious and jumped to Cora’s defense.
[SFX: ANGRY VOICES]
[MUSIC: CHAOTIC DRUMS]
Soon enough, Tia and Cora were pulled into the argument themselves, and the whole situation spiraled completely out of control.
