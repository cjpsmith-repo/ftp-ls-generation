Ep 828 - One Final Move

Word count: 1003

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

The Emperor's voice cut through the barrier like a blade — and for a moment, everything stopped.

Zane had never admitted defeat, not once in his entire life. Even when things looked hopeless, the thought of giving up never even crossed his mind.

But watching the fight from the stands, the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom just couldn't take it anymore.

In a way, the Emperor was a lot like his son, Randy. They were both fiercely loyal to the people they cared about—a quality the Crown Prince sorely lacked. This fight wasn't for some grand cause; winning or losing was just a matter of bragging rights. The Emperor wouldn't lose his throne if Zane were defeated. If the stakes had been that high, he never would have put Zane in the ring in the first place.

Besides, even a loss wouldn't be a disgrace. Zane had already beaten Logan and Fiona, bringing more than enough honor and pride to the Harlen Divine Kingdom. Who else from the seven-kingdom union would even dare to challenge him, other than this Little Monster? 

The Emperor had seen enough. Zane was out of tricks, and if the fight went on any longer, that monster was going to tear his head from his shoulders. That was the last thing he wanted to witness.

Randy let out a long, shaky breath when he heard his father call to end the match. Zane’s performance had already shattered his expectations and achieved everything they’d hoped for. His only fear was that the Little Monster might actually kill him. With his father stepping in, Zane would be safe.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

But War King York and the Holy Maiden looked deeply troubled. “Your Majesty,” the Holy Maiden said, her voice laced with ice, “this is highly improper.”

The Emperor just smiled at her. “This is a friendly match before we open the passage to The Sacred Feather Sea. Over the years, it’s become a simple exhibition to gauge the strength of our younger generation. Since it’s just a competition, isn’t it perfectly normal for one side to concede?”

The Holy Maiden was still young and hopelessly naive next to a seasoned ruler like the Emperor. His logic left her speechless.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

War King York wasn't finished. He let the silence stretch, then spoke loudly enough for everyone to hear. “Your Majesty — Zane injured one of ours. Now that he can't handle our man, you want to walk away? That seems like a rather small price to pay.”

The Emperor's expression didn't change. “When warriors fight, someone loses. Surely you know that.”

War King York smiled. “In the spirit of fairness — and so that neither fighter leaves with regrets — let's say they settle it with one final move. I'm sure Zane himself would want that.”

“This—” the Emperor began.

“Since neither side objects,” York said, raising his voice to the arena, “let's begin.”

The Emperor's face darkened. He had been outmaneuvered in front of every kingdom present, and there was nothing left to say.

[MUSIC: SINISTER TENSION]

Inside the barrier, the Little Monster leaned against the wall and grinned at Zane. “Did you hear that? One move.” He clicked his tongue. “One move is more than enough to kill you.”

Zane's eyes were clear. He'd heard every word.

The Emperor had conceded on his behalf. Zane didn't care. He had no intention of running — he was already turning the problem over, looking for an angle. There had to be a way to beat this thing in a single move. He just hadn't found it yet.

The crowd assumed he would try to dodge, to survive. They were wrong.

[AMBIENT: CROWD CHATTER]

A ripple of unease spread through the other Soul Sea Realm masters. The Little Monster was far too dangerous. Many of them had opposed the Nine Cauldron Martial House’s proposal to raise him in the first place, terrified of nurturing a viper in their own home. He wasn't human. If he grew any stronger, he would become impossible to control. Seeing his power explode now only deepened their fears. Yes, his talent was a shortcut they could use to advance their own training, but what would happen when they inevitably lost their grip on him?

[MUSIC: WORLD-SHAKING THEME]

“Are you ready, Zane?” the Little Monster called out. That wretched grin spread wide. “I've never shown this move to anyone. But I'll let you see it — give you something to talk about on your way to the underworld.”

Then the laughter stopped.

The Little Monster's aura changed.

[SFX: DEEP RESONANT RUMBLE]

It wasn't the aura of a warrior anymore. It shifted — mountain, ocean, plain, swamp — rolling through one after another like the world itself was breathing through him. The arena went quiet. Not the silence of anticipation. The silence of something vast pressing down on everyone at once.

Even the Soul Sea masters felt it. Under that aura, towering and shapeless and endless, every person present felt small. Not outmatched. Small. Like standing at the foot of a mountain range that had no summit.

War King York's expression had gone rigid. He had kept the Little Monster suppressed to the Spirit-Tribulation Realm all these years and thought he understood its limits. 

He had been wrong.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE ENERGY BURST]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]

A flash of white light erupted in Zane’s mind as a multicolored will forced its way in.

He’s trying to kill me with the will of the world itself? Zane thought, horrified. 

Before he could react, the iridescent will slammed mercilessly into him.

[SFX: CRACKING GLASS]

Cracks spiderwebbed across the surface of his once-solid soul.

Such overwhelming power! Zane felt his spiritual presence begin to buckle and retreat.

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC ENERGY SWIRL]

“Ha! And here I thought your soul was supposed to be so powerful, Zane!” a voice echoed in his mind, the will of the world swirling around him, laughing wildly. 

“Strong as a master of the Soul Sea Realm, they said! Even stronger! So how does it feel to be crushed by my attack?” 

Though it was the will of a world of chaos, it hadn't truly awakened. The Little Monster was still the one pulling the strings.
