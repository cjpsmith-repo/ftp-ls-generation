Ep 814 - The Powerful Princess

Word count: 1001

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Fiona had just stepped onto the field — the first member of any royal family to enter the arena — and before the fight had even begun, she was already warning Zane to lose on purpose.

“Oh yeah? And why’s that?” Zane asked, a curious smile playing on his lips.

Fiona’s expression turned serious, the playful energy from moments ago gone. “Because if you beat me, the ‘Little Monster’ will challenge you next. And he’s… not right in the head. He’s a lunatic, Zane. He’ll kill you.”

He couldn't help but let out a soft chuckle. The concern in her voice was so genuine that it was almost disarming. She really was a princess, wasn't she? Powerful, sure, but with a purity that could only come from a life spent sheltered within palace walls.

“I appreciate the warning,” Zane said, his smile widening a fraction, “but I can handle myself.”

Fiona seemed to realize he wasn't going to be swayed. She just gave a single, resigned nod. “Alright then,” she said. “Get ready.”

Zane was still processing the fact that she’d actually given him a heads-up before attacking. She was all quiet confidence and genuine sweetness—right up until the moment she raised her arm.

[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]

A Red Jade Bracelet was clasped around her wrist, pulsing with a raw, terrifying power that made Zane’s heart hammer against his ribs.

His eyes locked onto it. “What is that thing?”

Seeing the look on his face, Fiona actually lowered her arm, the attack fizzling out before it even began. “It was a gift from my father,” she said, as if it were the most normal thing in the world.

Zane was floored. In the middle of a fight, she’d stopped to answer a question.

[AMBIENT: CROWD LAUGHTER]

Around the square, a ripple of disbelief and amusement went through the other fighters. It was absurd, but also… charming.

“She’s adorable!” one of them whispered.

“That’s the princess of the Wind Divine Kingdom, you idiot. Don’t even think about it.”

“Hah! Don’t let that fool you,” another scoffed. “She could list out all her weaknesses, and you’d still get crushed. The girl’s innocent, not weak.”

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

Even Fiona's father, War King Wind, looked a bit uncomfortable.

“Your daughter is… impressive,” the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom remarked, a slight smile on his lips. “And quite charming.”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]

The buzz of chatter continued, but Fiona’s next words cut through it like a knife, silencing the entire square. “I really like this bracelet,” she said, stroking the red jade. “It’s a Semi-Divine Weapon.”

A Semi-Divine Weapon?

[SFX: DRAMATIC IMPACT]

Every warrior in the square went still. They all knew the tiers: Spirit Weapon, Mythic-grade, Relic-Class — each a significant step above the last. Above all of those sat divine tools, weapons of a completely different order. Most warriors in the Divine Kingdoms knew divine tools existed. Very few had ever laid eyes on one. A semi-divine weapon was as close to that threshold as anything they were ever likely to see. The square stayed quiet for a long moment as every pair of eyes fixed on the bracelet. For most of them, this was the closest they would ever get.

Zane felt it too. He was holding a top-grade Relic. By comparison, it might as well be a practice sword.

Inside him, a voice cut through his thoughts.

“Zane. Be careful of that bracelet.” It was Lavender — quiet, clipped, certain.

“I know,” he said inwardly. He'd already felt it.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

[SFX: REALITY DISTORTION]

Before he could even finish the thought, the world warped. Suddenly, Fiona was right there, her arm wreathed in flames, arcing straight for his head.

[SFX: FLAMES CRACKLING]

There was no time to react. But an inch from his temple, her fist stopped, the flames licking at the air. The crowd gasped.

“This will sting a bit,” she said, her voice apologetic, “but it shouldn’t injure you too badly.”

[SFX: SPATIAL TEAR]

Her hesitation was all he needed. Zane tore through space, reappearing a safe distance away, his expression grim. He’d only gotten away because she’d literally paused her attack to warn him. He wasn’t dodging; he was just delaying the inevitable.

He got away? Fiona felt a flicker of disappointment. She’d only meant to be courteous, but he’d used it to escape. How sneaky. Still, it didn't really matter. No one could outrun time.

Zane put distance between them and forced himself to think clearly. He recognized Fire Law, third level at minimum; the flames coating her arm had that quality. He could work with fire. The other thing was the problem.

He ran through what he knew about his own Spatial Law. First level: connect two space nodes, step from one point to emerge from another. It was fast — fast enough that most opponents struggled to react. But they still had a window. A fraction of a second, a ripple in the air, something. He had proven that against Logan — Magic Vision could track a path through the void because movement, even spatial movement, left a trace.

What Fiona had just done left no trace.

She hadn't moved between two points. She had compressed time within a range and stepped through the moment she created. Inside that compression, time itself was under her control. There was no ripple to read. No path for Magic Vision to follow. No fraction of a second to perceive and respond to. By the time his senses registered her, she was already there. The bracelet amplified all of it.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

This was what made Time Law so terrifying.

The only counter was Time Law itself — or Spatial Law developed to a level far beyond where he currently stood, where the two Laws might begin to check each other. 

He had neither. He was in trouble.

[AMBIENT: HEAVY ATMOSPHERE]

[SFX: TIME DISTORTION]

She focused. The air for hundreds of feet around them grew thick, heavy. For Zane, it felt like moving through molasses; the world had slowed to a crawl. And just like that, she was in front of him again, her palm glowing with fire, ready to end it.

No warning this time, she thought.
