Ep 70 - Refined by Flame
Word count: 1333
[AMBIENT: MAGMA CAVE ]
For now, the two massive beasts were too caught up in their battle to notice Zane.
One of them, the Razorwing Queen, had a mangled limb from their earlier fight. Even at a disadvantage, her one remaining appendage was still a powerful, deadly weapon shaped like a sword.
The Razorwing Queen also had a slight size advantage, but the smaller Scorpiomane was quicker on its feet. And since the lava cave was its natural habitat, it had the home-field advantage.
[SFX: CAVE RUMBLING]
[SFX: FIRE ERUPTION]
The moment the beasts clashed, the entire cave shook. Rocks tumbled from the ceiling, littering the floor with rubble as boulders rained down from high on the walls and fire erupted from deep within the earth.
[SFX: DEAFENING CRASH]
With a deafening crash, the Scorpiomane slammed the Razorwing Queen to the ground. But the queen dug her hind legs in, shoving the Scorpiomane back a foot.
[SFX: SLASH]
[SFX: SNARL]
She lunged, swinging her massive blade at the lion-like creature. The Scorpiomane dodged and snarled, lashing out in return.
But the Razorwing, a creature that lived to kill, was far more ferocious than any ordinary beast.
[SFX: FLESH TEARING]
[SFX: BEAST SHRIEK]
A horrifying, tearing sound filled the air as the blade sliced deep into the Scorpiomane's flesh. Its heart and other organs spilled out onto the ground. Fatally wounded, the Scorpiomane let out a mournful shriek.
At the same time, the creature whipped its nimble tail up, slashing at the Razorwing Queen's head.
[SFX: SICKENING CRACK]
There was a sickening crack as the stinger connected. Unable to dodge in time, the queen's head split open and tumbled to the ground. Her headless body went limp, the blade trembling for a second before the rest of her collapsed.
[MUSIC: SOMBER AFTERMATH]
Having dealt the final blow, the Scorpiomane fell right beside her, its own life draining away.
[AMBIENT: SILENT CAVE]
Zane stood frozen, staring at the carnage. He couldn't wrap his head around what had just happened.
The magma around him had gone cold.
When he'd hit the surface, the burning had been immediate and total — the kind of heat that should have ended him. His skin had gone dark red, then something else had happened. Golden pieces had erupted across his body, spinning faster and faster until they became whirlpools — and the whirlpools had begun to drink.
[SFX: DEEP ABSORPTION HUM]
The Corefire had poured into him. Tendrils of it first, then streams, then the whole lake pulling inward as if he were a drain. The magma had stopped boiling. It had darkened. Solidified. By the time the last of it went black and still, the entire lake had turned to cold stone — and Zane had punched his way out through the crust.
[SFX: STONE CRACKING]

[SFX: GENTLE ENERGY PULSES]
He'd jumped into a lava lake to escape certain death, and somehow, his body had absorbed the Corefire from the molten rock. The flames must have refined him, boosting his physical strength.
He looked down at his body and noticed a new, wave-like pattern on his chest.
Zane stared at it, puzzled. Something in the back of his mind nagged at him—he'd seen that pattern somewhere before.
[SFX: FINGER SNAP]
Then it hit him. He snapped his fingers. "That's it! I remember now! It's a Spirit Rune!"
Zane had seen runes like it in the Weapon Forging Manuals left behind by Evil Jace. Weapons fell into a few classes: Mysterious Weapons, Spirit Weapons, Mythic Weapons, Sacred Weapon, and Divine Arms. While Mysterious Weapons didn't have any unique markings, special characters like this were only found on higher-tiered treasures like Spirit, Mystic, and Sacred Weapon. Spirit Runes appeared on Spirit Weapons, Mystic Runes on Mythic Weapons, and so on.
[MUSIC: REVELATION THEME]
A Spirit Rune on his chest... that meant his body had transformed, evolving from the level of a Mysterious Weapon to a Soul Armament!
Zane knew the difference all too well.
[SFX: COMBAT SOUNDS]
During The Gauntlet, he'd taken a brutal beating from Joe's dagger—a Soul Armament. If his own body hadn't been as strong as a Legendary Weapon, Joe would have killed him.
Now that his body had evolved into a Soul Armament, his strength would be massively enhanced. This brought him to the most important point.
"The biggest difference between Spirit Weapons and Mysterious Weapons isn't just toughness or sharpness," Zane murmured into the cavern's silence. "Every Soul Armament has its own unique skill. Like how Joe's could summon that Energy Thread."
"So if my body is a Soul Armament now... what kind of skill did "I" get?"
[MUSIC: EXCITING BUILDUP]
The possibilities sent a thrill of excitement through him.
"But how do I activate it?" he wondered.
[SFX: MAGICAL ATTEMPT]
Standing on the solidified lava, Zane stretched out a hand, took a deep breath, and shouted, "Hah!"
He took two quick steps forward and launched into a clumsy spinning kick. After a few more awkward attempts, he stood still, scratching his head and chuckling. He felt like an idiot. He didn't even know for sure if he "had" a new skill, let alone how to trigger it. This was pointless.
[AMBIENT: CAVE DARKNESS]
The moment Zane released his focus, the golden luminescence enveloping his body dissolved into nothingness, casting the cavernous space into impenetrable darkness. A fleeting sense of vulnerability washed over him as the comforting light vanished.
With practiced movements, Zane reached into his Storage Ring, fingers searching for the familiar contours of his fire starter. The cool metal against his fingertips provided a momentary reassurance.
[SFX: FIRE IGNITING]
A bright spark erupted from his hands, dancing briefly before catching hold, illuminating the cave with flickering amber light. Shadows leaped and receded across the rough stone walls as Zane surveyed his surroundings, his heart still racing from the earlier transformation.
His gaze fell on the two massive corpses — and then, belatedly, his brain caught up with him.
He'd nearly walked past a fortune.
[SFX: KNIFE CUTTING — WET]
He crossed to the Queen's chest and cut it open to reveal a fist-sized purple crystal core. He had no idea how many points it was worth, but he wasn't leaving it behind.
He moved to the Scorpiomane next. He took the beasts’s core as well as its tail, severing it at the joint.
The Queen's blade he left. It was too large for his Storage Ring, and carrying it out would paint him in the Queen's scent — a beacon for every Razorwing in the jungle.
He stowed everything and stood.
Nothing stirred within the vast chamber—only the massive, grotesque corpses of the Razorwing Queen and the Scorpiomane remained a silent testament to their savage battle. Their enormous bodies, once vessels of terrifying power, now lay broken and still. Zane shuddered, contemplating how close he had come to sharing their fate.
[AMBIENT: NIGHT WILDERNESS]
[MUSIC: PEACEFUL NIGHT]
Hours later, exhausted yet exhilarated, he finally emerged from the cave's mouth into the velvet embrace of night. The cool air caressed his face, a welcome relief after the stifling heat of the underground chambers.
A breathtaking tapestry of stars blanketed the heavens, countless pinpoints of light scattered across the inky darkness. Zane tilted his head back, marveling at their beauty while realizing with a jolt of concern just how late the hour had grown. Time had slipped away during his underground ordeal, and now the lateness might complicate his return to Dragon's Keep.
Zane retraced his steps, carefully making his way back across the canyon and through the jungle toward Dragon's Keep. Luckily, the Razorwing swarm wasn't nocturnal. They spent their nights tucked away in their lairs, only venturing out under extreme circumstances. And any other predators that might have been out hunting had been driven away by the insects' constant aggression.
So, Zane's journey through the dense jungle was surprisingly quiet; he didn't encounter a single creature.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL RETURN]
After what seemed an eternity, the welcoming lights of Dragon's Keep finally pierced the darkness ahead, beckoning like distant stars.
[AMBIENT: FORTRESS INTERIOR]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Within the fortress walls, Zane strode purposefully toward Shane's quarters nestled in the Emerald Task Force barracks. As he approached the imposing structure, two vigilant guards materialized from the shadows, their stern faces illuminated by flickering torchlight.
[SFX: WEAPONS RAISED]
The sentinels instantly raised their gleaming lances, the weapons catching the moonlight with deadly promise. "Halt! Who dares approach?" one barked, his voice slicing through the night silence.
