Ep 1104 - Zane’s Tribulation

Word count: 1064

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Zane couldn’t understand what was happening. Had the Tribulation Pill already worn off?
His was only the sixth heavenly tribulation, not the tenth—and at his current power, it should’ve come on its own, pill or not. Disappointed didn’t cover it. He was flat-out baffled. Why hadn’t it shown up yet?
Yolande glanced at Layla and frowned. She’d been looking forward to Zane’s trial too—curious how many petals his Lotus Flower would show, even at the sixth tier. But the look on Layla’s face soured it: a smug, condescending sneer. Layla had no idea Zane had thrown their match on purpose, let her brother win. Why hadn’t he ever told her?
Now Zane was the only one left to face his trial, and the others weren’t going to wait forever. The pill was supposed to trigger a tribulation within two hours—Layla’s case aside, nothing had ever come this late.
[AMBIENT: CROWD GRUMBLES]
Someone in the crowd finally suggested that the official in the cotton robes just end the whole thing and remove the seal. The official had a real soft spot for Zane, but rules were rules. If the tribulation hadn’t started by now, it wasn’t going to. With a heavy sigh, he gave a sad, reluctant nod.
“Alright,” he said, raising a hand to dispel the seal and officially end the Passing the Torch ceremony.
[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]
But just as he moved, the sky tore open with streaks of blinding golden light, freezing everyone in place. The light coiled and twisted, writhing like a nest of golden serpents across the heavens.
Every head snapped upward—the palace leaders, Yolande, the Lord of the Sunset World. Golden light reflected in their wide, uncertain eyes as they stared at the space above Zane.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
And in that exact moment, the faint image of a Lotus Flower materialized over his head.
[SFX: CROWD GASP]
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
“It’s here! It’s Zane’s sixth heavenly tribulation!” someone shouted.
“Big deal,” another scoffed. “It’s just the sixth. We’ve all been through that.”
It was true. Zane was only at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm, the lowest of anyone here. No one from the Thirteen Palaces was weaker than him. They’d all passed their sixth tribulation ages ago, so it was hardly anything to get excited about. But Layla had just cleared the tenth, and Zane—at his level—had only completed five. 
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Yet his Lotus Flower was already sprouting twenty-six petals.
As the lotus over Zane’s head solidified, it became clear this wasn’t the Lesser Lotus Flower. One more petal, and it would reach total perfection.
Layla’s fourteen petals had felt like a miracle minutes ago. Now, standing next to this, the gap suddenly looked less like a gap and more like an entirely different category of existence.
“It can’t be fake... can it?” someone in the crowd murmured.
“How could it be fake?” another voice shot back.
“But then how is he still only at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm?” a third cultivator asked, baffled. “Someone explain that to me.”
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]
A faint smile crossed Yolande’s face. When she’d tested Zane with the Red Cloud Ring back at the Mist Palace, the ring had overloaded and gone dark—and she’d quietly suspected then that he carried a Greater Lotus Flower of his own. She just hadn’t expected it to already be this close to perfection.
The Lord of the Sunset World’s gaze cut toward her, his expression unreadable. “Yolande. Did you know about this?”
“Know about what?” Yolande kept her face carefully blank.
“That Zane has a Greater Lotus Flower.”
“I suspected,” she admitted. “I didn’t expect it to have bloomed this far already.”
The Lord of the Sunset World’s expression turned complicated. “Then it seems,” he said quietly, “that whatever path this one walks—it isn’t something our people are going to have much say in.”
Yolande didn’t answer. Privately, though, she thought he was right—Zane’s fate was already tangled in things far beyond what any of them could steer. But that wasn’t something she could say out loud, not even to the Lord of the Sunset World. As for what came next for Zane, she’d let him find it himself, the same as she always had.
What Layla didn’t know, what would have probably made her faint on the spot, was that Zane had activated his Lotus Flower during his very first tribulation, and it had sprouted all twenty-six petals right from the start.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]
Even without that knowledge, the sight was a crushing blow. It was a monumental defeat that shattered her ego right then and there.
Her own Greater Lotus Flower had bloomed only five petals—and that had taken everything she had, on her tenth tribulation. Zane’s twenty-six petals had come on his very first. There was no way to spin that. The distance between them wasn’t a gap anymore. It was a different world entirely.
The Lotus Flower might just be an illusion, but it was also a direct measure of a warrior’s potential, a sign of their power to one day defy the heavens. And you could never predict when it would appear or how many petals it would have. 
No matter how Layla tried to spin it, the gap between them was a gaping chasm, and she was being forced to stare right into it—right at her own inadequacy.
“This is just too much,” she muttered, her voice a low growl.
In her mind, Zane had gone too far. She’d completely forgotten that he’d never wanted to compete with her in the first place. He’d only joined the ceremony for the prize, not for the status his power might bring. 
Layla was the one who had targeted him from the beginning, provoking him for no good reason. But now, stewing in her own disappointment and wronged pride, she convinced herself he’d done all this on purpose, just to spite her. The blame was all his.
[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]
[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]
As the Lotus Flower solidified, the golden snakes in the sky began to stretch, growing from a few feet long to dozens, then hundreds. They spiraled through the air like living spears before shooting straight down at Zane.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The moment the golden snakes had appeared, the Lord of the Sunset World’s expression had changed. He felt like he’d seen them somewhere before, a nagging familiarity he couldn’t place. Then, as they shot toward Zane, the memory clicked.
[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]
“Holy crap!” he yelled.
