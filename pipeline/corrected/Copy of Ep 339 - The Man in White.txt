Ep 339 - The Man in White

Word count: 1099

[AMBIENT: GRAND BANQUET HALL]
[MUSIC: ROYAL CELEBRATION]
As the Third Prince of the Palace, Ray was throwing a massive banquet, and everyone who was anyone was there.
[SFX: MURMURING CROWD]
The moment Zane stepped into the grand hall, he could feel the potent Vitality of several powerful figures. Masters of the Soul Realm, he guessed.
"Get Zane a seat!" the Third Prince commanded as soon as he saw him.
[SFX: SCURRYING FOOTSTEPS]
Instantly, half a dozen maids scurried over. They set up a square table right in front of Zane, piling it high with fresh fruit and all kinds of meats before placing a chair.
Since Ray had promised to get Isla to the banquet, Zane sat down to wait patiently. As for the prince, if he was really dead-set on getting his support, Zane figured he'd have to play this just right.
[SFX: CRYSTAL GOBLET RAISED]
He hadn’t expected Ray to move so fast. The second Zane was seated, Ray returned to his own place at the head of the hall, raised his crystal goblet with a smile, and announced, "Zane, you took first place in the Skyreach Sect’s All Peaks Competition, and I’ve wanted to meet you ever since. You’ve been away from the Sect these past few days, so I never got the chance. I’m deeply honored that you’ve graced my humble palace today. So, I’d like to propose a toast. To you! I’ll drink first, as a sign of my respect!"
Ray's respectful words echoed through the hall, but you could see the doubt on his guests’ faces. These people weren’t from the Skyreach Sect. Many were from the most powerful Great Houses and boasted incredible strength of their own, some even at the Soul Realm. And the prince had never rolled out the red carpet for anyone like this before.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
"Your Highness, being your guest doesn’t give him a pass to be arrogant!" one of them argued.
"Yeah, I agree!" another guest added. "If he’s really that good, we’ll eat our words. But I bet he’s all talk. Just another tough guy putting on a show."
[SFX: CHAIR MOVING]
The grumbling rippled through the room until a man in white rose from his table. He clasped his hands in a respectful gesture toward Ray and said, "Your Highness, since everyone’s in such high spirits, I’d like to challenge Zane to a friendly match. It would be fantastic entertainment for the guests, and I might just learn a thing or two from him!"
"Now that’s a great idea! After the opera and the dancers, just sitting around drinking was getting a little dull. A good fight would definitely liven things up!"
The guest who spoke was thrilled at the prospect of stirring up trouble.
Ray nodded. "Since everyone is so excited, I don’t want to be a wet blanket. So, Zane, what do you say? A little competition?"
A small frown creased Zane's forehead, and he didn't answer right away.
Honestly, he felt trapped.
Ray's game was painfully obvious.
Suggesting a fight was clearly a way to test Zane’s real strength. But he hadn’t even come for the prince's banquet; he was only here for Isla. Now, out of the blue, they were trying to push him into a fight.
He was in no mood for such a pointless spectacle.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Seeing Zane’s silence, the man in white sneered coldly. "What’s the matter, Zane? Scared? Worried you'll lose and make a fool of yourself?
Anyone who comes as a guest of the Third Prince has real skills. If you lose, you get the hell out of here!"
"That’s right! You have to earn your place here!" one of the guests roared.
"The Third Prince doesn’t keep losers around!" another shouted, giddy with excitement.
Zane could only sigh, looking at the eager, angry faces staring at him like he was the enemy.
By now, the Third Prince must have already won the support of several powerful families, especially a few of the top Great Houses represented by the guests here tonight.
But the prince only had so many resources to go around. If Zane joined his inner circle, they were afraid he'd take a piece of their pie. And so, in their eyes, he was the common enemy.
And the Third Prince was just letting them provoke and challenge him, all to test his true strength and decide if Zane was worth recruiting.
"If Zane really doesn’t want to fight, then let’s just drop it.
Everyone who comes to the Silversun Hall is a friend. Please, don’t let a small thing like this ruin the vibe," Ray said, pretending to play peacemaker. The problem was, he'd already lit the fuse. It would be almost impossible for Zane to back down now.
"So, what’s it going to be?" Doug, the man in white, asked as he strode to the center of the hall, his eyes locked on Zane.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
Zane looked at Doug, then glanced at the Third Prince. Finally, he nodded. "Alright. I accept your challenge."
Ray's smile widened. "Wonderful! And to make things interesting — a thousand Crystal Cubes to anyone who beats him today."
[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD MURMUR]
The mood in the hall shifted instantly. A thousand Crystal Cubes was a fortune for anyone not born into nobility. Zane caught the prince's game immediately — this wasn't one fight. It was an open invitation for everyone in the room to take a turn.
Doug stared at Zane with a condescending smirk. "You know, I can’t stand guys who put on this whole mysterious act, like they’re above it all. When I see someone like that, I just feel an overwhelming urge to knock ’em down a peg," he said gruffly.
Zane couldn’t help but let out a snort of laughter.
Dressed head to toe in white, Doug looked so refined and handsome, but his words were so coarse. The sheer contrast between his appearance and his crass mouth was enough to make Zane burst out laughing.
"Hmph. We’ll see if you’re still laughing in a minute," Doug snarled.
[SFX: ENERGY SWIRLING]
He threw his hands up, channeling a dark red Vitality. The energy swirled and solidified between his fingers, hardening into ten long claws. At the same time, two wings of condensed Vitality burst from his back.
Seeing the transformation, Zane's eyebrows shot up as his mind raced. As a guest of the prince, Doug obviously had a few tricks up his sleeve. He was mimicking a bird, shaping his Vitality into claws and wings. That meant his speed was probably off the charts.
[SFX: POWERFUL WING BEAT]
[MUSIC: FAST-PACED BATTLE]
Before Zane could finish the thought, Doug's wings beat once—hard.
He shot toward him so fast that to the rest of the room, he was just a blur of motion.
