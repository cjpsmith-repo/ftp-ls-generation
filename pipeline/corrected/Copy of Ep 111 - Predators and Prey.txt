Ep 111 - Predators and Prey
Word Count: 1155
[AMBIENT: DENSE FOREST]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Sky Forest earned its name from the spruce trees that shot up everywhere you looked. They were hundreds of yards tall, straight as spears, their peaks scraping the clouds. Standing at the forest's edge, the students felt a knot of dread tighten in their stomachs as they stared into the vast, dense woods.
[SFX: SKYCRUISER IDLING]
Once everyone disembarked from the massive Skycruiser, instructors divided the students into small groups before dispatching them into the depths of Sky Forest. Each unit comprised ten individuals—five seasoned Varsity members paired with five untested pledges.
[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL]
[SFX: APPROACHING FOOTSTEPS]
The moment Zane's feet touched the ground, the five Varsity members from Misty Peak encircled him like predators cornering prey. Their faces betrayed a mixture of contempt and wariness, having witnessed his earlier confrontation.
"Look, I don't give a damn what your beef with Preston is," declared a Core Apprentice in an immaculate black robe, stepping forward with shoulders squared. His voice dripped with arrogance, each syllable carefully crafted to establish dominance. A cold smile played across his lips as he continued, "But out here, you do exactly what I say if you want to make it out alive. Pull the same crap you did on the Skycruiser, and you're demon food. Got it?" His eyes narrowed, searching Zane's face for any hint of defiance or submission.
"Don't get ahead of yourself," another one added. "You've got nothing to back up that attitude. You know the saying: 'the nail that sticks out gets hammered down.' You're just an Outer Disciple—barely even a High-level candidate. You need *us* to get through this. So why'd you have to go and pick a fight with Preston and drag us into your mess? Let's get one thing straight: if he comes for you, you're on your own."
"I hear you've become quite the legend among the other pledges," a third member interjected, his voice laced with disdain. "Allow me to clarify your situation: pledges exist at the absolute bottom of our hierarchy. Whatever impressive feat you managed back at the academy amounts to absolutely nothing in these woods. Learn your position and remain there, unless you fancy becoming demon fodder."
Zane, however, paid no attention to their threats. His senses—refined through countless hours of training—had evolved beyond those of his peers. He could detect the gentlest rustle of leaves, the softest footfall on forest debris, even the subtle shift in air currents that signaled movement nearby. No sound, no matter how faint, escaped his notice as his mind cataloged every potential threat in their surroundings.
[AMBIENT: DEEP FOREST SOUNDS]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON PATH]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS JOURNEY]
The Misty Peak contingent pressed forward into the dense woodland, eventually leaving their fellow Skyreach Sect members far behind. A heavy silence fell over the group as they navigated a serpentine path that stretched roughly twenty miles into the wilderness. Every step was measured, every movement calculated with precision. The towering spruces cast long shadows across their path, creating an eerie atmosphere that kept even the most experienced Varsity members on edge, though none would admit to the unease that crept along their spines.
[SFX: BRANCH SNAP]
Suddenly, a sharp *crack* echoed through the trees. The Core Apprentice scouting ahead had snapped a dry branch under his boot.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL]
"What was that?" Henry yelped, his voice trembling. He'd never been in a situation like this before, and his nerves were shot.
"Easy, Henry. It was nothing. Just a branch," the scout called back, shooting him a mocking grin.
"Watch out!"
[SFX: RUSTLING BRANCHES]
[SFX: SNAKE HISS]
But as the scout turned to taunt Henry, a massive snake lunged from behind a thick spruce. Its huge, grayish-green body was the color of bark, its camouflage so perfect it had been hiding in plain sight.
The snake struck, its jaws unhinging as it went to swallow the scout whole.
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
In that same instant, a flash of silver light streaked past the snake's head.
[SFX: HEAVY THUD]
A second later, the creature fell to the ground, sliced clean in two.
"No demons yet, but the forest has other teeth. Watch yourself, Quentin," the apprentice who'd killed it said calmly, holding a gleaming sword.
Quentin, the scout, stared at his dead attacker, then gave his teammate a shaky but respectful nod. "Thanks, Cliff. You saved my skin."
Trailing behind the group, Zane watched the whole thing. He raised an eyebrow. Cliff was a Varsity member—one of the few elite fighters Misty Peak had to offer, and his speed was impressive.
It was only a Class Four monster, but Cliff's reaction time had been nearly as fast as his own. Zane’s hand was already moving toward his Storage Ring; if Cliff hadn’t acted, his shattered dagger would have.
Cliff gave a curt nod, sheathed his sword, and took the lead. As the apparent leader of the Misty Peak apprentices, he guided them onward. They killed plenty of monsters along the way, but still no demons. The five Varsity members fought as a seamless unit, taking down one beast after another and harvesting their crystal cores.
Meanwhile, Henry, Casey, Zane, and the other two pledges were left to do little more than watch from the sidelines.
[AMBIENT: FOREST VALLEY]
[MUSIC: PEACEFUL EXPLORATION]
Around noon, after a long, halting journey, they finally stopped to make camp at the edge of a valley.
[SFX: CAMPFIRE CRACKLING]
"You two, get some firewood. Start a fire," Quentin ordered two of the pledges. Soon, the whole group was gathered around the flames, eating their rations.
"Cliff, have you really killed a demon before?" Quentin asked as they ate.
"Yeah, four years ago," Cliff replied, a smug grin spreading across his face as he tore a chunk of meat from a roasted whitetail deer. "I was still just an Outer Disciple then. The Crucible was about to close, so I took a demon hunting mission. We managed to corner a Demonic Grunt, and I was the one who landed the killing blow."
Dispatching a Demonic Grunt as an Outer Disciple represented an extraordinary achievement, and Cliff knew it well. He attempted a modest demeanor, yet unmistakable pride resonated in his voice. "Fortune favored me that day, truthfully. My strength then paled compared to what I command now. Should I encounter a Demonic Grunt today? I'd vanquish it with merely three strikes."
As Cliff's boast hung in the air, one of the previously silent Core Apprentices—a figure who had been devouring his meal with unsettling intensity—interrupted with a voice that scraped like metal against stone. "Such confidence deserves examination. Let us put your prowess to the test. See if I can withstand your vaunted three moves. Draw your sword... now."
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
[MUSIC: TENSE REVELATION]
Without warning, the air around them fractured as a wave of malevolent energy erupted from the Apprentice's body, ancient and hungry—unmistakably demonic in its essence.
