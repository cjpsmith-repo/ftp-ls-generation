Ep 1303 - Supreme Lord Sword

Word count: 1183

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]
Zane reached the mountainside, his eyes already fixed on the spot just ahead. This was where he’d found Lavender’s longsword, but now, the ground was bare.
He walked a few hundred feet farther, scanning the area. He’d thrown his own sword away around here, too, but no matter where he looked, there was no sign of it.
*Of course,* he thought. *This is just an illusion created by a True God’s soul mark, not the real Killing Sword Mountain. This could be what it looked like a hundred thousand years ago, or a million. Hell, maybe even ten million.* He sighed. *I guess I’m not getting my sword back.*
[SFX: SHARP WHINE]
[SFX: SWORDS RAINING FROM SKY]
A sharp whine cut through the air, and suddenly, swords were raining from the sky.
[SFX: METAL CLASHING REPEATEDLY]
He expertly swung the strange weapon in his hand, deflecting blade after blade as he pushed relentlessly forward, eager to see who was waiting for him at the top.
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT RHYTHM]
The weapons kept coming, their quality improving with every foot he climbed. Each one was sharper than the last, attacking him with a dizzying array of basic swordsmanship styles.
*The Divine Path of Sealed Emotion is technically a type of swordsmanship,* he mused, his mind working even as his body fought. *I wonder if I could find my own Godly Way just by training in other sword styles.*
The question stuck with him. The other True Gods he’d met had all found their own unique paths. Did that mean there wasn’t some pre-written manual for it? If you mastered a technique so completely that it broke the rules of the heavens, could you create a new Godly Way? Could there really be that many paths to godhood?
If so, then why was his path—the Divine Path of Sealed Emotion—considered forbidden?
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN PEAK WIND]
As these thoughts raced through his mind, Zane continued his climb, and finally, he reached the summit.
A middle-aged man with a long, wispy beard sat perfectly still, a short sword floating gently beside him. 
[SFX: SWORD HUMMING]
The blade was about two and a half feet long and pure white—simple, plain, and yet radiating an incredible sharpness. It didn’t look like much, but Zane knew exactly what it was.
“The Gods-Intimidating Sword,” he breathed.
His eyebrows shot up in recognition. He’d already guessed the man at the peak would be Supreme Lord Sword, but seeing the legendary weapon Yolande had described confirmed it. Then again, since Supreme Lord Sword had also appeared in the Corridor of the Emperor, maybe there was a better name for him.
[SFX: SWORD HUMMING INTENSIFIES]
The white sword suddenly hummed louder, circled once in the air, and flew straight at him. Zane didn’t hesitate—he raised the strange weapon in his hand to block it.
[SFX: METAL SHATTERING]
It shattered instantly. There was no comparing an ordinary blade to a weapon like that.
Zane’s expression didn’t change. He raised his hand, and another sword appeared in his grip—a first-grade divine artifact he’d claimed on the way up.
[SFX: METAL CLASH IMPACT] 
He clashed with the floating sword three times in quick succession. Through all of it, Supreme Lord Sword never opened his eyes, as if the whole exchange barely registered.
Strange—every other True God Zane had met, he’d felt nothing much for. This one, he found himself genuinely fascinated by.
Every time Zane tried to press forward toward the peak, the sword drove him back. He fell into Formless Sword just to keep from being pushed off the mountain entirely, and even then, it was barely enough.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Supreme Lord Sword still hadn’t so much as glanced at him.
Zane’s frown deepened. His focus sharpened to a point, and a restless, unpredictable thread of sword intent shot out of him.
It was a move he saved only for real crisis. But against a True God, there was no room to hold anything back—he didn’t have the right to.
[SFX: SWORD IMPACT DEFLECTION]
The strike connected, and the floating white sword went spinning away, knocked clean off its course.
Supreme Lord Sword’s eyes snapped open. For just a moment, real astonishment showed on his face.
Zane pressed his palms together and bowed, then walked slowly forward.
[MUSIC: CALM CONTEMPLATIVE]
Before Zane could speak, Supreme Lord Sword got there first. “You know who I am?”
“Supreme Lord Sword.”
Supreme Lord Sword studied him a moment longer. “You... you’ve walked my same path of the sword?”
“Yes.”
A faint smile crossed Supreme Lord Sword’s face. Still seated, he raised a hand, and the white sword flew back into his grip. Then he thrust it out toward Zane.
A thread of green light lived inside that blade—sword intent, but purer than anything Zane had ever touched. The pinnacle of it.
Zane didn’t hesitate. He felt no killing intent behind the strike—only a test—and thrust his own sword out to meet it.
[MUSIC: SUSPENDED STILLNESS]
The two swords never touched. The Supreme Lord’s blade stabbed halfway forward, then withdrew—and the instant it did, it stabbed out again. Zane matched him thrust for thrust, withdrawal for withdrawal, neither sword ever quite meeting the other.
It went on and on, the strikes blurring together until time itself seemed to lose meaning.
[SFX: SWORD INTENT FADING]
Finally, Supreme Lord Sword drew his blade back and settled into stillness.
The man looked at Zane with a detached expression. “If I hadn’t seen it with my own eyes,” he said, his voice calm, “I never would have believed someone could understand the poem I left behind.”
“I figured it was just a coincidence,” Zane replied.
“No,” Supreme Lord Sword said with a faint smile. “Not exactly. Your luck isn’t fixed. It seems someone is pushing you in a different direction.”
Zane stared, completely lost. “Pushing me?”
“I don’t know who it is, or what they want,” the man continued, his gaze distant. “But I’m sure they’re making arrangements for you.”
Zane’s brow furrowed. He had no idea what the man was talking about, and the mystery of it all sent a chill down his spine. Zane didn’t believe in fate. He’d always carved his own path, even when things seemed hopeless. 
But this man’s words challenged everything. 
[MUSIC: OMINOUS QUESTIONING]
Was there really someone out there, planning his entire life?
Seeing the confusion on Zane’s face, Supreme Lord Sword offered another slight smile. 
“That’s not something we need to discuss. It doesn’t concern me.” He changed the subject as if closing a book. 
[MUSIC: CALM CONTEMPLATIVE]
“How many petals have bloomed on your lotus flower?”
“Nine on the Lesser Lotus, twenty-six on the Greater,” Zane answered automatically.
Supreme Lord Sword nodded in approval. “Not bad. Thirty-five petals at the Soul Sea Realm… you’ll be a saint-level genius before you know it. Not bad at all.”
He knew, then, that Zane had truly succeeded on the Divine Path of Sealed Emotion. 
With only one petal left to bloom, his lotus flower would soon be complete.
