Ep 1296 - I’ll Kill You First

Word count: 1093

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC BATTLEFIELD]
The Illusion Battlefield was a free-for-all, with everyone trying to steal Illusion Points from each other, and the competition was only getting fiercer. The best fighters all knew the fastest way to rack up points was to get into the hall, but that was easier said than done.
First, you had to hunt down an iron ticket, which was hidden in secret lands deep in the wilderness—places filled with unknown and deadly dangers. Second, even if you survived that, you had to pay an entrance fee of five million Illusion Points. For the average fighter, both requirements were basically impossible. Who in the sanctuaries could ever save up that kind of currency?
[SFX: SHIMMERING PORTAL OPENING]
Just then, in a sanctuary nestled in one of the upper worlds of the battlefield, a gate of blinding silver light shimmered into existence in the center of the city. 
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMURS]
Nine fighters strolled out, and the moment the onlookers saw them, a hush fell over the crowd. Everyone knew these nine were in a different league.
The Illusion Battlefield had a system for concentrating its talent. Fighters who survived the trial of the hall were all transported to this central sanctuary, a designated city in the heart of an Upper World. It was a deliberate design to find the best of the best. Anyone who walked out of that hall alive was already ranked in the top fifty of their entire world. 
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BUILDING TENSION]
The unspoken understanding was that with all the top players gathered in one place, an even bigger, more brutal showdown was inevitable.
Out of the ten who had entered the hall together, only one had died. The strength of these experts was staggering.
“How boring,” the leader of the group, a man with long, flowing hair, complained with an arrogant shake of his head. “I can’t believe the reward for the fifth hall was just *this*.”
“Ha! Speak for yourself,” another fighter, a bald man, said with a chuckle. “I’m more interested in the treasures you can find out in the field. Some seriously rare stuff pops up here.” 
He was thinking of the Revival Token he’d found in a hidden corner of the battlefield—a one-time-use item that could bring its holder back from the dead. He hadn’t needed it in the hall, but the entire Illusion Battlefield was a projection created by the heavens. If he didn’t use the token here, it would vanish when he left. He never would have guessed the fifth hall’s reward would be something he could actually take with him, so he’d naturally chosen to keep the token.
“Killing people is the best part of this,” the long-haired youth said with a dismissive smile. “This sanctuary is the gateway to the hall, so it must be packed with strong players. What do you say we head out of town and find someone to kill?”
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD WHISPERS]
His words spread through the city, drawing stares. Anyone who could pass through the hall was either a top-tier talent or came from an influential background, like a direct descendant of a World Lord. But this guy didn’t seem to care. This sanctuary, after all, was where the superstars gathered.
Among the nine stood a woman ranked fourth in the group, her expression touched with a lingering unhappiness. She was dressed simply, in a plain white robe with a pale embroidered wrap draped over her shoulders—an indifferent, elegant sort of beauty. 
This was Cora.
From the moment Cora entered the Illusion Battlefield, she’d ignored rankings and points entirely. Placed in an Ogre main city surrounded by six hostile neighbors, she’d wandered out and started killing, losing count of her victims. Only finding Zane mattered.
She hadn’t even understood the rules at first. Early on, she’d let herself be killed just to learn what happened - only to wake reborn in a main city, more frustrated than before.
Killing one warrior at a time proved inefficient - fighters could escape over the walls and scatter. 
[SFX: SWORDS MATERIALIZING]
So Cora laid out a hundred thousand swords in a ring-shaped array and encircled an entire city. No one inside could break through. Eventually, the trapped warriors struck a deal: each would let her kill them three times in exchange for freedom.
She repeated this across seven main cities. The Ogres realized this quiet, elegant woman was a walking nightmare. Without trying, she’d climbed into the top one hundred of the entire Illusion Battlefield. She hadn’t cared - she’d even let herself be killed once more, losing half her points, before slowly climbing back.
But after learning the Illusion Battlefield was split into fifty separate great realms, her mood had soured. The odds of Zane being in the same realm as her were only one in fifty. She’d held out hope of finding him in the Hall of Causality this time. She hadn’t.
“Cora, why are you unhappy?” the long-haired youth asked, watching her.
Cora glanced at him, indifferent. “Nothing,” she said.
He was, by reputation, a prodigy of the Human Alliance, and he’d led their group through the entire hall—deliberately shielding Cora from harm the whole way through, which was exactly why she’d never had cause to show any real strength inside it.
[MUSIC: COLD THREATENING STRINGS]
“Alright,” she said, her voice completely flat as she stared blankly at him. “But once we’re outside the city, I’ll kill you first.”
He just chuckled. 
“Is that a challenge? If you want to fight, I’m happy to oblige.”
Without another word, Cora turned and walked toward the city gate. 
Sooner or later, she figured, the fifty upper worlds would have to team up. Otherwise, the talent scattered across the realms would never be able to compete for the very top spots. For now, though, it was better to boost her own rank. If she was near the top, she might finally be able to find Zane. She just wondered what his rank was now.
[MUSIC: SUDDEN INTENSE PERCUSSION]
[SFX: SWORD SLASH CUTTING AIR]
Five minutes later, a sword flashed like lightning just outside the sanctuary.
[SFX: AGONIZED SCREAM]
The sound was followed by the agonized scream of the long-haired fighter as Cora killed him, sending him back to the sanctuary to be revived. As the curtain of light enveloped him, he thought bitterly about how he’d basically carried the quiet, bookish woman through the hall. He couldn’t understand it. 
[AMBIENT: SANCTUARY REVIVAL CHAMBER]
[SFX: MAGICAL LIGHT SHIMMER]
