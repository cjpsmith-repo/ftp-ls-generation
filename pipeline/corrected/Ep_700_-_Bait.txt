Ep 700 - Bait

Word count: 1054

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Maddox smiled with satisfaction.

It looked like Zane was finally leaving Ethereal City, and Maddox couldn’t have been happier. He immediately used a Message Rune to contact Simon, telling him they needed to move in and capture Zane now.

But Zane wasn’t leaving. He was just circling the city, a casual flight that immediately put Maddox on edge.

“What’s got you so worried?” Zane called out with a smirk, hovering just a thousand feet away.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]

Maddox’s expression hardened. He scanned their surroundings, but saw nothing out of the ordinary. Still, that smug grin on Zane’s face sent a chill down his spine. Something was wrong. The two of them hung in the air, locked in a tense standoff.

“Alright, if you’re just going to stare, I’m out of here,” Zane announced, making a show of turning to leave.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

Rage flared in Maddox’s chest. A double bluff. It had to be. He had no choice. Unleashing his Vitality, Maddox shot toward Zane like a cannonball.

The moment Maddox was airborne over the palace, Zane’s voice boomed. “Attack!”

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL RUNE ACTIVATION]

Instantly, beams of light erupted from the palace below. They shot outward, connecting into a vast web. Within a fifteen-hundred-foot radius, every street blazed with a brilliant red glow, as if the sun had crashed into the city. The glowing lines converged, forming a gigantic, intricate rune.

[SFX: TELEPORTATION FLASH]

At the same time, a pulse of Spatial Law power rippled from the palace. With a few blinding flashes, Brent, Robin, and the seven warriors known as the Septuplets of the Ethereal Spirit Sect appeared from nowhere.

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

From the massive rune on the streets, the red light twisted and solidified into countless small dragons. They swarmed into the sky, encircling Maddox in an instant. Some coiled around his arms and legs, others wrapped tight around his torso, pinning him completely.

“I’ve heard the stories about Arnold’s hidden relic,” Zane said, his voice filled with genuine admiration as Brent and Robin moved to flank him. “The one even more powerful than the sect’s protective arrays. So this is the Dragon-Snare. It’s incredible.”

And Zane had reason to be impressed. The Heartlands wasn’t the upper world; masters like Arnold didn’t have access to ancient inheritances like the divine texture Zane did. Arnold had to figure it all out on his own, and somehow, he’d managed to create a rune with the power of a three-star divine texture. The sheer genius of it was staggering.

[AMBIENT: TENSE STANDOFF]

The seven Septuplets floated into the air, forming a loose circle around the trapped Maddox, their longswords drawn and leveled at him.

Two more disciples appeared alongside them, escorting a young woman in green between them — Cora's personal maidservant.

Cora broke free of Zane the instant she saw her. "What are you doing? Let her go!"

Brent's expression was pained. "Cora. She was reporting on you. She's one of Maddox's people."

"That's impossible. She's been with me since we were children."

"Cora, stop." Even Brent's voice had gone cold.

"But she—"

[SFX: SPEAR WHISTLE]

An invisible spear shot grazed Cora's cheek — close enough to draw blood, close enough to leave a thin red line across her face.

"Shut your mouth," Lavender said quietly, "for your own good."

Cora fell silent. Robin studied his daughter's bloodied face but offered no words of comfort, no apology.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REALIZATION]

The truth crashed over her in that suffocating quiet. She replayed the lunch in her mind—everyone so composed, everyone laughing at just the right moments. Elder Xander had orchestrated everything from the beginning. The entire family must have known. They'd needed someone to lure Maddox to this exact location, someone he would actually pursue with single-minded determination. Zane's presence had merely been a fortunate accident, an unexpected bonus to their scheme.

The plan had existed long before today.

Her chest tightened as the realization settled like ice in her veins. They'd used her. Her own family had turned her into bait without a word of warning, without seeking her consent.

No one even glanced in her direction. Every eye remained fixed on Maddox, as if she'd already served her purpose and ceased to matter.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

But Maddox just sneered, a complete lack of fear in his eyes. “I’ll admit, that was clever. Smarter than I gave you credit for. But it’s not going to be enough to stop me.”

[SFX: DARK ENERGY ERUPTION]

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS TENSION]

As he spoke, a black mist erupted from the space between his eyebrows, instantly shrouding him and the glowing restraints of the Dragon-Snare.

[SFX: SIZZLING DISSOLVE]

The moment it touched the light, the tendrils began to sizzle and dissolve.

[MUSIC: RISING PANIC]

Panic flared among the sect members. “Wait, the Dragon-Snare is supposed to block his Vitality! How is he doing that?” one of them yelled.

“That’s not Vitality,” another shot back. “It must be a secret art that doesn’t rely on it!”

A third voice, desperate, cut through the noise. “Don’t let him break free! Kill him now!”

This was the Ethereal Spirit Sect’s big play, the culmination of all their planning. They were betting everything on Arnold’s Dragon-Snare. It was an all-or-nothing gamble, but if they could eliminate Maddox, the Corrupted Divine Kingdom would crumble, and the glory for their sect would be immeasurable. It was precisely this shared goal that had convinced Zane to work with them in the first place.

The Dragon-Snare was supposed to be inescapable, even for a warrior at the Life-and-Death Realm. But Maddox, with this unknown secret technique, was proving that legend wrong.

[SFX: FORMATION SHIFT]

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

A wave of anxiety washed over the sect members. Seeing their ultimate weapon failing, the Septuplets immediately shifted into their most powerful formation. Six of them fell back in perfect sync, while their leader surged forward. The leader became a conduit, drawing the Vitality and sword intent from his six brethren.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

His blade flared with an almost blinding light, and though he was only at the late stage of the Spirit Realm, the power crackling from his sword now rivaled that of a true master.
