Zane propelled himself forward with renewed vigor, racing uphill as his heightened senses detected every movement around him. A relentless pack of pursuers hounded his trail, their determined footfalls echoing behind him, each one fixated on claiming him as their prize.
One pursuer, more ambitious than his companions, veered off the main path. Taking a treacherous shortcut with remarkable agility, he maneuvered to intercept Zane, his movements betraying years of experience navigating Infernal Peak's harsh terrain.
"Ha! A little Marrow Refining runt from the Skyreach Sect?" the man taunted, his voice gravelly from years of breathing Infernal Peak's ash-laden air. "You'll be some fun for us before we're through!"
The interceptor stood imposingly before Zane—a burly figure, perhaps in his thirties, with a face weathered by hardship and violence. His dark skin bore numerous scars, testaments to countless battles, while his eyes gleamed with a predatory intelligence that had likely served him well in this desolate place.
"Get out of my way," Zane warned, his voice cutting through the air like a blade. Despite the danger, his pulse remained steady, his mind calculating the fastest way to eliminate this obstacle if diplomacy failed.
The man's laughter resonated with contempt as he circled Zane. "You're completely at my mercy, boy. I could snuff out your life right here on Infernal Peak without a second thought—and no one would ever know." His confidence suggested he'd done precisely that many times before.
His companions lingered at a distance, forming a loose semicircle as they watched with anticipation, apparently content to let their leader handle what they perceived as easy prey.
[MUSIC: THREATENING INTENSITY]
"Last warning," Zane declared, each word measured and dripping with lethal promise. "Move, or die right where you stand." His fingers twitched slightly, ready to summon his weapons at the slightest provocation.
The man merely scoffed, his expression revealing how thoroughly he underestimated the youth before him. He extended his large, calloused hands toward Zane—hands that had clearly broken bones and ended lives. If those fingers managed to grasp him, Zane recognized the crushing strength they would deliver.
[SFX: COMBAT MOVEMENT]
The attacker lunged forward with surprising speed. In that critical moment, Zane channeled his soul with practiced precision, focusing his energy to form a deadly Psyche spike.
[SFX: PSYCHIC POWER ACTIVATION]
"Psyche Spike!" he called out, unleashing his power.
[SFX: AGONIZING SCREAM]
The spike slammed into the man's mind, and he screamed, a raw, agonizing sound. While he was still reeling, Zane hurled the Shattered Dagger.
[SFX: BLADE CUTTING AIR]
[SFX: IMPACT AND BLOOD SPRAY]
The psychic blade sliced through the air with lethal precision, penetrating the man's chest as effortlessly as a hot knife through butter. It erupted from his back in a spray of crimson before embedding itself in the rock wall behind him, the impact sending tiny fragments of stone cascading to the ground.
[SFX: WEAPON RECALL]
With a mere flick of his wrist, Zane recalled the weapon. It returned to his waiting palm, immaculate and gleaming—not a single droplet of blood marred its surface. The man's body crumpled to the ground, his eyes still wide with disbelief at his swift demise.
"Anyone else?" Zane inquired, his voice eerily calm as he swept his glacial gaze across the remaining pursuers. Their faces paled with dawning horror. Without waiting for their response, he turned away and resumed his ascent, each step deliberate and unwavering despite the treacherous terrain.
Behind him lay the corpse of a Nature Level cultivator who had fatally misjudged his opponent. In his final moments, bewilderment had consumed him—how could someone of supposedly inferior cultivation have dispatched him so easily? The answer was simple yet profound: his arrogance had blinded him to the true threat standing before him, and that blindness had cost him everything.
The remaining attackers stood paralyzed, their earlier bravado evaporating like morning dew. They exchanged nervous glances, their breathing shallow and rapid. This youth was no ordinary prey—he was a predator disguised in sheep's clothing.
[SFX: RETREATING FOOTSTEPS]
Without a word, they scattered into the shadows, their footsteps fading rapidly as they fled from the monster they had unwittingly provoked.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN ATMOSPHERE]
At the summit of Infernal Peak, one visitor moved freely where others feared to tread.
Adrian Warrick---the only son and heir of House Warrick, one of the most powerful Legacy Families in the Empire. Handsome, polished, and draped in the deep hues of the Council Robes reserved for elite disciples personally trained by the masters of the Skyreach Sect. Where most young cultivators his age were still grinding through the Nature Level, Adrian had already broken through to the Soul Realm---a feat that had earned him a reputation as one of the most gifted prodigies of his generation.
But it wasn't ambition that brought him to the peak today. It was something far more dangerous.

[MUSIC: LIGHT PLAYFUL TUNE]
Elara fiddled with a slingshot, a slight smirk playing on her lips. "Since Perrin can't come up to Devil's Peak for now," she said, "we'll need you to run messages for us."
"You don't even have to ask," Adrian said immediately. "Of course I'll do it. That's what friends are for!" For Adrian, any excuse to be near Elara was a gift.
Elara's expression softened slightly. "Any news of my brother?" she asked. "Perrin mentioned he came to the sect but hasn't gone home."
"He's doing well," Adrian said. "Sending letters. That's all I know."
Elara nodded and said nothing more.
[SFX: SLINGSHOT]
Elara gave him a grateful nod. She picked up a smooth stone from the platform, settled it into her slingshot, and let it fly out over the chasm. The stone shot out like a dart, soaring toward some unseen target on the other side. But halfway across, it lost momentum and arced down into the depths.
Even though she missed, a genuine smile lit up her face—brighter than anything he'd seen from her in a long time. It was a stark contrast to her usual quiet demeanor. Adrian seized the opportunity, staying to chat with her while she was in such a rare, good mood. When he finally left, he felt like he was walking on air.
When Adrian Warrick returned from Infernal Peak, two anxious figures awaited his arrival.
"Adrian! Did you see Elara?" Caleb rushed forward, his voice tinged with worry as he clutched Adrian's sleeve. "How is she doing up there?"
Perrin hovered just behind, his usually confident demeanor replaced by thinly veiled concern, eyes searching Adrian's face for any hint of news.
Gently disengaging himself from Caleb's grip, Adrian strode directly to Perrin with purposeful steps. His face broke into an animated expression as he clasped Perrin's shoulder, excitement radiating from his entire being. "Perrin, you simply wouldn't believe what I witnessed today," he exclaimed, his voice dropping to an almost reverent tone. "She actually smiled—not one of those forced, hollow expressions, but a genuine, radiant smile that transformed her entire face!"
Perrin's tense frame visibly relaxed, his shoulders dropping as though a great weight had been lifted. "She must be aching for everyone terribly," he murmured, his voice cracking with emotion. "These visits of yours... they'll become her lifeline in that dreadful place."
"I'll keep going as often as possible," Adrian vowed, his eyes gleaming with newfound purpose. "Now, come along! Tonight calls for celebration!" He practically danced ahead of them, leading the way with buoyant steps.
