Ep 688 - Maddox's Plan

Word count: 1031

[AMBIENT: ELEGANT PAVILION]

[MUSIC: SOULFUL MELODY]

Inside the Skywater Pavilion, steam curled from a cup of mulled cider while a dozen maids danced gracefully to a soulful melody.

The head of the sect, Elder Marsh, was all smiles at Zane’s side. “Zane,” he began, leaning in, “the word in The Heartlands is that you died in the celestial tomb. Now that you’re here, safe and sound, I’m sure no one will believe that rumor anymore.”

Zane couldn’t care less what people were saying. He cut straight to the point. “You said there was a reason for the ambush. What was it?”

Marsh let out a heavy sigh. “It seems you’ve been away from The Heartlands for a couple of years. You’re completely out of the loop.”

“That’s exactly why I’m here,” Zane replied, his tone serious. He wasn’t wrong. If he weren’t so determined to find out what was happening, he never would have bothered with a Third-Grade sect like this.

Marsh nodded. “Things were peaceful for a while after the Fourth and Fifth-Grade sects returned from the celestial tomb. That lasted about six months. Then a new power rose up right here in The Heartlands—the Corrupted Divine Kingdom, formed by Maddox.”

The name landed like a stone, and Zane’s expression immediately hardened. Maddox and Cloud Hall were his two biggest priorities—Maddox held the key to Elara’s fate, and Cloud Hall was tied to Tia. But he kept his face a mask of calm, listening intently as Marsh continued.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]

“It’s Maddox’s self-proclaimed kingdom,” Marsh sneered. “None of the warriors in The Heartlands acknowledge it, but that doesn’t mean he isn’t causing trouble.”

Zane knew what that meant. When a sect reached the Sixth-Grade, it became a sacred place—prestigious, but still just a sect, like the Violet Heart Sanctuary he’d known on the main continent.

Establishing a divine kingdom, though, was something else entirely. It would belong to Maddox and Maddox alone. He could take the throne and rule like an emperor. It was clear Maddox had established his kingdom to consolidate power, trying to force every sect in The Heartlands, from the highest to the lowest, to bow down to him.

Of course, the warriors of The Heartlands weren’t about to let that happen. As soon as Maddox made his move, all the sects had issued a joint statement refusing to recognize his so-called kingdom.

[MUSIC: GRIM UNDERTONE]

But Maddox hadn't stopped there. He'd begun sweeping up independent cultivators from across the Heartlands — warriors with no sect affiliation who had always existed outside the system, relying on their own grit to grow. There were more of them than most people realized, and Maddox had moved fast, drawing them in with promises of future rewards and few restrictions. The World Merchants' Guild, the Ethereal Spirit Sect, and Cloud Hall had been forced to respond, instructing every sect in the region to do the same.

"The Sea Wyrm Sect wasn't eager to take them in," Marsh said, his smile gone. "Some of these independents were little better than bandits. But we had no choice." He spread his hands. "The problem is, a sect comes with rules. These men are used to answering to no one. Maddox offers them freedom — for now, at least. We can only offer discipline." He shook his head. "Still, it's join a sect or join the Kingdom. Anyone who refuses both finds trouble at their door."

"How can this be?" Zane said quietly. Two years ago, none of this had existed. Now the entire landscape of the Heartlands had been redrawn around a conflict that hadn't yet broken into open war.

Elder Marsh's expression was grim but unsurprised. "No one expected it to reach this point. But here we are." He paused. "There will be a war between the sects and Maddox. It's only a matter of time."

Zane nodded slowly, his mind churning.

"One more thing," he said, after a moment. He kept his voice level, though the question cost him something. "Maddox's formation — the one that requires the Violet Core Physique. Has it been completed?"

He hadn't wanted to ask. He was afraid of the answer. But there were things in this world that had to be faced.

Marsh was well aware of Zane’s history with Maddox and knew his sister, Elara, had fallen into the man’s hands. He saw the unspoken question in Zane’s eyes and answered it directly.

“Not yet. They say everyone in the Corrupted Divine Kingdom is looking for women with the Violet-Core Physique.”

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]

A knot Zane hadn’t realized he’d been carrying for two years finally loosened in his chest. That had been his greatest fear. It looked like luck was still on his side.

Seeing the relief wash over Zane’s face, Marsh offered a reassuring word. “Don’t worry. The Violet-Core Physique is so rare, they won’t find one easily.”

Zane nodded.

Now that he was back in The Heartlands, the feud between him and Maddox was going to be settled, once and for all.

[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION HALL]

“You mentioned you’re still healing,” Marsh added quickly. “I can offer you a place to stay. Our Sea Wyrm Sect has all kinds of remedies.”

Zane smiled and politely declined. “That’s all right. I’m just passing through. Now that I’m back, I need to get to my own territory. Thank you for the hospitality, but I have to hurry back to Cloud Hall.”

He had what he came for. The conversation with Marsh had confirmed two critical things: Cloud Hall was still safe, and the third woman with a Violet-Core Physique had not yet been found. That was enough for now.

“Since you have important matters to attend to, I won’t keep you,” Marsh said, knowing he couldn’t stop Zane even if he wanted to. He rose to see him off, even summoning the three Spirit-Tribulation Realm elders to escort him from the Sect’s grounds.

[SFX: WHOOSH OF DEPARTING FLIGHT]

After Zane vanished over the horizon in a streak of light, Master Croy turned to the sect leader, his voice sullen. “Elder Marsh, why were you so polite to that kid?” He knew who Zane was, but he couldn’t understand the show of respect for someone with no connection to their sect.

Marsh just snorted. “What do you know?”
