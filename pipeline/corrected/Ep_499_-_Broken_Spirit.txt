Ep 499 - Broken Spirit

Word count: 983

[AMBIENT: LARGE ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

For hundreds of years, the World Merchants' Guild had hosted the Martial Tournament once a decade. They'd run it dozens of times, and by now, they had it down to a science.

Take the arena's enchanted barrier, for example. They'd perfected it.

Seventy years back, during the tournament finals, two fighters went all out, burning their own Life-Essence to unleash their full power.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]

They shattered the enchanted barrier, and the blast tore through the audience. Over a thousand martial artists in the stands were injured; hundreds died.

It was a hard lesson, and after that, the Guild started taking the arena's safety features a lot more seriously.

[AMBIENT: CONSTRUCTION SOUNDS]

The arena floor itself was now built from the toughest materials imaginable. The heartwood of a super-strong Ironwood tree was woven into silk. The hardest basalt, ground into a fine powder, was mixed with that silk to create the bricks. Those bricks were laid one by one and then covered with a layer of steel plate. And this wasn't just any steel. It was forged by a master weaponsmith and infused with Celestial Essence. The floor itself was practically a Mythic Weapon.

But the shimmering curtain of the enchanted barrier was even more impressive. The top runic experts in the Heartlands had designed it, using over four hundred runes to weave six distinct enchanted barriers, each separated by just a finger's width. In the gaps between them, the runes created a dispersing effect that fused all six layers into one.

That was the level of protection the World Merchants' Guild provided. And for seventy years, that shimmering curtain had never been broken.

Earlier, when Zane's sword spirit, Lily, had slammed the Netherworld Fiend into the barrier with her sword, it had flickered, but the old man hadn't even flinched. So what if one layer broke? There were still five more behind it.

That confidence lasted all of thirty seconds.

[SFX: GLASS SHATTERING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

The moment Zane and Lily switched places, Zane threw a single punch—and the entire enchanted barrier shattered like glass.

[SFX: CHAIN EXPLOSIONS]

[SFX: DEBRIS FALLING]

The instant the barrier broke, a chain of explosions rocked the arena. Smoke and dust erupted into the air as the runes hidden in the ground, unable to withstand the backlash, blew themselves apart.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]

Just how powerful was Zane's body?

Breaking the barrier was no challenge for any of the elders at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.

But even Hobbes, who was famous for his explosive strength, couldn't shatter it with his body alone. It was out of the question for Tia, Lady Darkmere, or Raven. Maybe Maddox could pull it off, but that was a big "maybe."

[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]

And Zane was only at the Soul Realm. Not only that, he hadn't even aimed for the barrier. He'd punched the Fiend, and the force had just rippled through its body to hit the curtain.

Even with the Fiend's body absorbing most of the impact, it was still enough to completely destroy the barrier.

It might have looked like Zane was backed into a corner, using risky moves because he couldn't take down Eden, but that wasn't the plan at all.

[MUSIC: TAUNTING TENSION]

"You're supposedly at the Core-Forged Realm, and I'm just in the Soul Realm. And I'm still wiping the floor with you," Zane said, choosing to attack with words instead of his fists. "Are you sure you're a Divine-level talent? You fight more like an Earth-level. Actually, from where I'm standing, your talent isn't even worth ranking."

Zane only planned on killing one person in this entire tournament: Marilyn.

If he went all out right now—unleashed the Malevolent Cycle of Life and Death—he could probably kill Eden on the spot. But Eden wasn't the target. Killing him now would just screw up the whole plan.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS THEME]

But just because Zane didn't want to kill Eden didn't mean he was going to go easy on him. The son of his enemy was still his enemy. Besides, Eden had already tried to kill him. So Zane was going to do something worse: he was going to shatter his will to fight.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CONFRONTATION]

"You look like you're at least thirty, right?" Zane continued, his voice dripping with contempt. "Thirty years old and you've only just hit the Core-Forged Realm. And to top it all off, you can't even handle a guy from the Soul Realm."

Goaded by Zane's relentless taunts, Eden finally snapped. A primal yell tore from his throat as he abandoned all strategy and charged straight for Zane, completely disregarding the deadly arc of Lily's greatsword.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

[SFX: SWORD CLATTERING]

Lily would never allow him to reach Zane. Her greatsword crashed into Eden's ribs with devastating force, launching him across the arena floor. He skidded to a halt directly at Zane's feet, his carefully maintained hair now disheveled and matted with sweat. The Nameless Saber clattered away from his outstretched fingers, spinning uselessly across the stone.

Zane gazed down at the fallen warrior, his expression a mixture of disdain and cold satisfaction. "And a guy like you wants to propose to Tia? You really think you're worthy?"

The words struck deeper than any blade. Eden's fingers trembled against the cold arena floor.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

"Enough!" Maddox roared, veins bulging across his forehead as he shot to his feet.

As one of the Heartlands' top experts, he recognized Zane's true objective immediately—this wasn't just about winning the fight. Zane was systematically dismantling Eden's confidence, crushing the foundation of his cultivation path itself.

Zane merely glanced at Maddox, his voice carrying a note of finality. "Too late."

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

[AMBIENT: HUSHED CROWD]

Sprawled on the cold stone, Eden felt something fundamental shatter within him. No physical wound had been inflicted, yet a sharp, hollowing ache bloomed in his chest—a pain that reached far deeper than any technique or weapon could penetrate. His vision blurred at the edges.

The roar of the crowd faded to a distant murmur. In that terrible moment, Eden understood that he had lost far more than a simple duel.
