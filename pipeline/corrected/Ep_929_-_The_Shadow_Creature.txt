Ep 929 - The Shadow Creature

Word count: 1049

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

War King Wind could only watch, stunned, as his daughter bolted from his side. 

He was one of the most powerful warriors in the Lower World, but in the presence of the seven Spirit Supreme Realm masters, he was nothing. They had been volatile and unpredictable from the start, and he was terrified that Fiona’s impulsiveness would be the thing that finally set them off. 

He reached for her, but she was already gone, shooting through the crowd like an arrow loosed from a bow.

Her father knew her better than anyone. He’d seen the way she looked at Zane and had figured out her feelings long ago, but he’d never imagined this. Fiona had always been so gentle, so reserved. To see her act with such unthinking desperation was a shock.

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL PIANO]

She skidded to a stop directly in front of Zane, her captivating eyes locked on his, her expression dead serious. She bit her lip, her voice barely a whisper. “You'll really come back?”

Zane saw the raw hope in her eyes and offered a small, cautious smile. “Of course I'll be back.”

The words hung in the air, and Fiona suddenly felt the weight of everyone’s stares. A hot flush crept up her neck, but the hesitation lasted only a second. She’d already come this far. 

Steeling herself, she pulled the simple necklace from around her neck—not valuable, but a cherished keepsake—and stepped forward to fasten it around his.

“I'll be waiting for you,” she said, forcing the brightest smile she could manage before turning and walking away without a backward glance.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

Zane watched her go, the cool weight of the necklace a strange comfort against his skin. A silent nod was all he could offer before he turned and boarded The Compass Wheel. He caught the faint smirks on the faces of the young man in black and his hulking companion but chose to ignore them.

[AMBIENT: SPATIAL PORTAL HUMMING]

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

Once Zane and the seven Demi-Immortals were aboard, Brianna, the girl they’d brought with them, was ushered on as well. The Compass Wheel began to rotate, and the warriors inside were pulled into the shimmering spatial aperture. A moment later, the great vessel vanished from the sight of the onlookers below. 

[SFX: PORTAL CLOSING]

As Delroy lowered the enchanted barrier, the rift in the sky began to heal, closing like a wound on the fabric of chaos.

On the shore below, the pressure lifted the instant the Demi-Immortals were gone. The Soul Sea masters exhaled. They tidied up quickly and began moving out of the Sacred Feather Sea — the blood sacrifice power was slowly recovering, and none of them wanted to still be here when it did.

The White-Haired Priest didn't move. His face was dark.

The Holy Maiden stepped to his side. “Do we still report Zane's information through the Reverse Spirit Channel?”

“Report it.” His voice came out low and tight, then broke open. “Open the channel now. The Demi-Immortals' reach is too long.” He could barely contain it — the Ancestral Dragon, the Greater Lotus Flower, the Mark. All of it walking away on that wheel. “Whether it's Zane or the dragon — if the Demi-Immortals want them, they'll have to fight for them.”

[AMBIENT: SPATIAL VOID]

Inside the space crack, there was nothing. No light, no sound, no sense of direction — just pitch black in every direction. Wade guided The Compass Wheel forward at speed, following the coordinates Zane had given him.

Zane stared into the dark for a while, curious. Then he gave up and sat cross-legged.

“Is there really nothing out here?” he asked.

The young man in black didn't look up. “Why else would it be called the void?”

Zane rolled his eyes. Among the seven, only Vorn and Dorothea were tolerable. The rest were arrogant, or unpleasant, or both. He closed his eyes and began to meditate.

Then the cold hit. A gloomy, bone-deep chill swept across the wheel — not wind, not temperature, something else. Everyone felt it at the same moment. Vorn was on his feet before anyone spoke.

“Bad luck,” he said, his voice flat. “Shadow creature.”

“The hell is a shadow creature?” the young man in black demanded.

Delroy's expression had gone tight. “Creatures that live on the other side of the world. So I have to correct you — there is something in the void. Shadow creatures are rare, but they're powerful. We have a problem.”

The young man in black said nothing. He had just used the void's emptiness to mock Zane. The irony was not lost on him.

The wheel kept moving at speed. They could hear something now — a low presence in the dark — but they couldn't find it. Everyone scanned the void and found nothing.

Zane's eyes flashed green.

With Magic Vision active, the darkness peeled back into a green-tinted world. He swept his gaze around — then behind them.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS VOID]

[MUSIC: HORROR CRESCENDO]

[SFX: DISTANT RUMBLING]

Tens of thousands of feet away, a colossal head was hurtling toward them through the void. 

[SFX: SERPENTINE HISSING]

It was shaped like a man’s, but it was the size of a mountain, and in place of hair, its scalp writhed with a thick nest of living pythons.

Unlike the others, Zane’s Magic Vision cut through the distortion of the void, letting him see the approaching horror in perfect, sickening detail.

“What do you see?” Vorn asked, his voice tight with nerves. 

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The Shadow Creature had been bad enough. He didn't dare question how Zane could see so clearly; he just needed to know what they were facing.

“It's a head,” Zane said, his own voice strained as it grew closer. “A huge head!”

“A head?” Vorn’s eyes widened in dawning terror. “This is awful! How can something like that exist in the shadow of the world of chaos?”

Delroy looked absolutely horrified, his voice trembling. “Oh no. We have to get out of the void. Now!”

“Get out of the void?” Zane argued. “But the Chaotic Ocean of Stars is out there! That Star Storm is still—“

[MUSIC: INTENSE STRINGS]

“The Star Storm is nothing compared to that thing!” Vorn snarled. “Delroy, get ready!”

With that, he swung his cane out in front of him, channeling what felt like every last drop of his power into it. 

[SFX: REALITY TEARING]

[SFX: SCREECHING METAL]

The air filled with the sound of splintering space—an ear-splitting screech like ten thousand nails dragging across a chalkboard.
