Ep 211 - The Unfinished Blade
Word count: 1161
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL FANTASY]
[AMBIENT: WEAPON SHOP]
The late autumn sunlight filtered through the high windows of the Weapon Pavilion, casting long shadows across the polished wooden floor.
Zane had no idea how long he'd been in there. He stood before the display rack, his fingers tracing the intricate designs of various blades while the shopkeeper watched with keen interest. The weight of his recent discoveries pressed upon his mind, yet the prospect of finding the perfect weapon for the upcoming All Peaks Competition brought a focused determination to his eyes.
Around them, the soft clink of metal and murmured conversations of other customers created a soothing backdrop as Zane contemplated his options, aware that his choice today might well determine his fate in the battles to come.
The last time Celestial Essence was auctioned at The Blackwood Auction House, the elders from The Capital's noble families were still kicking themselves for missing out. If they'd been there, the price for fifty drops would have skyrocketed past a mere few hundred thousand crystals.
After asking about the price, Zane handed the "Green Wave" sword back to the shopkeeper and pointed to the third one on the rack. "What's the deal with that one?"
The shopkeeper lifted the third sword from the rack and set it before Zane.
It was a strange thing to look at. The scabbard was gray and dull, the hilt unremarkable — no presence, no pull. Just an empty blade.
[SFX: FAINT CUTTING RESONANCE]
But when Zane's eyes reached the edge, he paused. The blade itself was razor sharp, faintly radiating a cutting aura that didn't match the rest of the sword at all. As if whatever soul the weapon had retreated entirely to its edge and stopped there.
The shopkeeper gently returned the "Green Wave" to its place. He glanced at the third sword, shook his head, and sighed. "Based on what I know, I'd really advise against it. You should pick a different one."
"Oh? How come?" Zane asked, his curiosity piqued.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REVELATION]
"Well, to put it simply... it's an unfinished blade," the middle-aged shopkeeper said in a low voice. "When Chase was forging this, he worked for over two days straight. Right after he drew the refining fire from the steel, he sat down to meditate. And\... he never got back up. Chase passed away right there, just sitting at his forge."
Zane's eyebrows shot up. "So you're saying making this sword killed him?"
[SFX: DISTANT FORGE SOUNDS]
The shopkeeper nodded. "You could say that, but it's not the whole story. Chase knew his time was almost up; his natural lifespan was coming to an end. But for him, forging a Mythic Weapon was more important than squeezing out another year of life. It's a damn shame, really. He poured everything into one last shot at greatness, and the sword is still unfinished. If he hadn't died, it could have been a Mythic Weapon. Now, it's just a half-finished blade, so it's not nearly as valuable. A few weaponsmiths have looked at it over the years, but none of them are confident they can complete it."
Zane was struck with an idea. "The infusion process requires a weaponsmith to condense their understanding of Celestial Law into the weapon, right? What if someone just filled the sword with Celestial Essence?"
"Ha, ha, ha!" the shopkeeper laughed. "Celestial Essence is the essence of Celestial Law. Of course that would work---it'd be even better than the original method!"
"Then why doesn't somebody just buy the sword and do that?" Zane was still confused.
The shopkeeper considered this. "That's a good question. First off, Celestial Essence is ridiculously expensive. Most people can't get their hands on it, even with a mountain of crystals. Second, it's an unfinished weapon. Who knows how much Celestial Essence it would drink up? It's a total crapshoot. Even if it could become a Mythic Weapon, it might take dozens, maybe even hundreds of drops. A weaponsmith would be much better off just strengthening an existing high-level Weapon. It's a safer bet with a guaranteed payoff."
"Oh, okay. I get it," Zane said, finally piecing it together.
Celestial Essence was so rare that even people with fortunes in crystals couldn't buy it; there was just never any for sale. And if a weaponsmith did manage to get some, they'd use it to upgrade a Spirit Weapon they already knew and trusted. It made way more sense to turn their own weapon into a Mythic Weapon than to gamble on an unfinished sword that might be a bottomless pit.
[MUSIC: THOUGHTFUL CONTEMPLATION]
But he couldn't stop staring at it. "This sword... it feels right for me. I've got plenty of Celestial Essence. And even though I haven't mastered Weaponsmithing, the infusion process itself doesn't seem that complicated. It might seem crazy, but I want to give it a shot."
[SFX: CONFIDENT DECISION]
After a moment of thought, a slow smile spread across Zane's face. "I'll buy this sword," he told the shopkeeper.
"What?" The shopkeeper stared, unable to believe what he'd just heard. "Did you say you want the third sword?"
Zane nodded, his expression serious. "Yeah. I choose this one."
"But..." the shopkeeper hesitated. He hadn't told Zane the whole truth. The sword wasn't technically for sale. Chase had poured his dying hope into this blade, and the shopkeeper remembered what the Pavilion Boss had told him: if someone could actually finish the sword and turn it into a Mythic Weapon, it was to be given to them. Free of charge.
Zane sensed the man's hesitation and felt something was off. "Is there a problem? The price is ten thousand crystal cubes, right? Or are you asking for more?"
"No, that's not it," the shopkeeper said, realizing how suspicious he must seem.
Just then, the shop's owner came in and disrupted the energy of the entire place.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL SHOP INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ENTRANCE]
Some women spend their lives perfecting the art of glamour; others seem to be born with it. At high levels, it could even be used in a fight to disorient an enemy. Some could even command an opponent with a single look, forcing them to surrender without a struggle.
It didn't matter if this woman's glamour was a natural gift or a practiced skill. Zane honestly didn't care. But he knew, without a doubt, that he'd just been hit by it. No wonder the shopkeeper had immediately looked at the floor when she walked in. He must have known his boss possessed a powerful glamour.
In the end, glamour was a lot like an illusion---it worked by misleading the senses and reading a person's mind to control them.
But anyone with a strong enough spirit wouldn't be so easily enthralled.
[SFX: MAGICAL PULSE]
Zane blinked, deliberately emptying his mind. The strange pull on his consciousness vanished. The woman's glamour washed over him but found nothing to latch onto. In a single heartbeat, his mind was clear again. But could it happen again?
It was very strange. Why was she so powerful? What was it about this woman that was so special, and possibly dangerous?
