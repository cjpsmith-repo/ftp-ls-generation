Ep 587 - A Perfect Copy
Word count: 1016
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Zane stared at the girl from his past. But was it actually her?
The Heartlands and the main continent were worlds apart, separated by hundreds of thousands of miles. The only way Zane knew of to cross that distance was the giant teleportation array in Vesper’s celestial tomb.
But could this really be a coincidence?
The Yolande in front of him was a perfect copy—same face, same clothes, same personality. The only things that didn't line up were her power level and her fighting style.
The Yolande from Alba Imperia fought with enchanted barriers. This one used a technique called Point Strike.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS ROAR]
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
As the thought crossed his mind, the orc in the arena let out a ground-shaking roar.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: WHOOSHING AXE]
He charged, his heavy steps thundering across the stone as he swung a massive axe, the wild swings whipping up gusts of wind.
Against the orc's brute force, Yolande was all grace—a leaf on the wind, weaving just out of reach of the axe's deadly arc.
Olivia noticed the look on Zane's face. "You look worried," she said, her smile hidden behind her hand. "Don't be. Yolande is… strange."
Zane’s brow furrowed. "Strange? How so?"
"She's been in the Tower for six years," Olivia explained. "And for most of that time, she's been right here, on the tenth floor."
Zane’s eyes widened. "Six years? Stuck on the tenth floor?"
Olivia nodded. "She fights six matches a month and racks up a ton of Lucky Light. I bet she's collected enough to create twenty War Lords by now, but she never uses it. She just gives it all away."
Zane was speechless. "She gives her Lucky Light away?" he repeated, the words catching in his throat.
"Yep," Olivia confirmed. "Sometimes, when a human challenges her, she just forfeits the match. All that Lucky Light she's stockpiled goes straight to them."
The Tower's rules technically allowed it, but what was her endgame? Why stay here, on the tenth floor? Why remain a tenth-level War General? Why give away all her Lucky Light? What was she trying to do?
Most warriors came to the Tower of Sin to get more powerful, to climb the ranks with Lucky Light. Yolande was doing the exact opposite—just giving it all away.
Another question surfaced in Zane's mind. "If she's been here that long, why does anyone still challenge her?"
Olivia shrugged, a soft smile on her face. "There's always someone brave—or foolish—enough to chase a big payout. Yolande collects a huge amount of Lucky Light, and every so often, someone gets cocky and thinks they have a shot."
Five years ago, a human War Lord had taken Yolande's gifted Lucky Light and used it to break through repeatedly, eventually climbing all the way to the top of the Tower and claiming a seat for the human race. Yolande hadn't fought for that seat herself — but without her, it never would have happened.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: HIGH STAKES BATTLE]
In the arena, the situation flipped in an instant. Yolande's body blurred — she shot through a gap in the Orc's axe swing, her small wooden shoes connecting with his face in a clean kick that sent her spinning upward.
She flipped in the air. Something gathered in her hand — colorless, invisible, completely transparent. Not a trace of visible Vitality.
Zane's eyes narrowed. He'd seen that before. In Alba Imperia, the Yolande he knew had the same thing — Vitality so pure it was utterly clear. He'd never seen it in anyone else. Until now.
"Netherworld Finger!"
[SFX: SHARP CRACK]
Her finger drove forward. The Orc's head snapped back — and then he dropped. A hole the size of a fist had been punched clean through the arena floor beneath him, the stone writhing and sealing itself shut like a wound.
[AMBIENT: CROWD WHISPERS]
A ripple of hushed voices spread through the onlookers.
"We shouldn't have challenged her. She's been sitting on this floor for six years, and she's still at tenth-rank War General — not because she can't advance, but because she won't. She's the master of this floor."
"The reward is too tempting. She hasn't given away her Lucky Light in six months. If someone could beat her right now, they'd jump straight to War Lord."
"Hundreds of War Generals have died trying. And now there's another human monster just stepped onto this floor. The human race might actually be on the rise."
Yolande collected the Lucky Light from the Orc's nameplate without expression. She swept her gaze across the crowd. "Who else wants to challenge me?"
Silence.
She blinked once, then stepped down from the arena.
Zane watched her go, a thought forming at the back of his mind. What he really wanted to know — what he needed to know — was whether she had scars on her back. The Yolande from Alba Imperia had them. If this one did too, it would mean something. But that wasn't a question he could ask here, in front of everyone.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
Yolande took a deep breath, her gaze turning ice-cold as she locked eyes with Zane. "You have a nice scent. I like it."
Zane had no idea how to respond. Her behavior only solidified it for him: this couldn't be the same Yolande from Alba Imperia. But that just left him with more questions.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
"Fight me," Yolande said. "If you win, I'll give you my Lucky Light."
Zane wasn't sure he could beat her right now, though he might be able to pull off a narrow win if he activated his Stellar Body. But there was no point in going that far—they were both human.
Zane shook his head. "I won't fight you."
"Why?" Yolande asked, her stare intense.
"Where are you from?" he asked, partly out of genuine curiosity, partly to change the subject.
Yolande blinked, and for the first time, a flicker of genuine emotion crossed her face.
"You've seen me before?"
