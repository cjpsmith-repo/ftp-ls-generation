Ep 795 - Strange Bedfellows

Word count: 1003

[AMBIENT: ROYAL PALACE HALL]

Randy leaned forward, his earlier arrogance set aside, and asked the question plainly: Why did Zane want to enter the Sacred Feather Sea, and could the two of them help each other get there? 

After everything that had happened at the Crystal Martial House — the fight, the challenge, the political circus with the Crown Prince — it was the first honest conversation they'd had. 

Randy’s tone was surprisingly civil, which, coming from a high-ranking lord of the Harlen Divine Kingdom, was a statement in itself. He was the kind of royal who had never stooped to cooperating with anyone before. Zane was the first. The crown prince might have recruited saint-level geniuses like Gregory and Edwin, along with other talents from the major martial houses, but they were followers, not partners. Most powerful princes just wanted a crew they could command; Randy, it seemed, wanted a genuine ally.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

 The offer caught Zane off guard. He took a slow sip of his cider, his gaze drifting to the scenery outside the palace as his mind started turning. He hadn’t planned on aligning with any faction when he’d arrived in the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms, but he wasn’t about to turn down a good opportunity, either. If it was a win-win, why not? Randy was undeniably arrogant, but he also seemed like a straight-shooter, and he was a prince of the Harlen Divine Kingdom. 

Zane had no intention of being anyone’s lackey, so teaming up with a guy like Randy felt like a smart move. The only real complication was the bad blood between Randy and the crown prince, but that wasn’t Zane’s problem. He wasn’t planning on sticking around forever anyway.

Noticing the flicker of hesitation, Randy leaned forward. “Still afraid of my brother, are you?”

Zane looked at him for a long moment. He hadn't come here to get tangled up in royal politics. He had one goal — get the ancient heartstones, go home, save Tia. Everything else was noise. But refusing every hand extended to him wasn't going to get him into the Sacred Feather Sea any faster, and Randy, for all his arrogance, was offering something real. This was temporary. He could walk away the moment he had what he needed.

 Zane had to grin at how blunt the guy was. “Alright, I’m in,” he said. “Let’s work together. I’m here for the Ancient Heartstones in the Feather Emperor’s Secret Realm.” It wasn’t exactly a secret, so there was no harm in laying his cards on the table.

“Oh? The Ancient Heartstones?” A knowing smile spread across Randy’s face. “Why didn’t you just say so?”

A jolt went through Zane. He’d traveled all this way just to find them. If he could get them straight from Randy, it would save him a world of trouble.

[AMBIENT: QUIET LUXURIOUS ROOM]

[SFX: SINGLE HANDCLAP]

 Randy clapped his hands once, and a servant girl approached with quick, quiet steps.

“Bring me the Ancient Heartstones,” Randy murmured, his voice barely a whisper.

The servant bowed her head and slipped carefully from the room.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 A hot thrill of excitement shot through Zane’s chest, so intense he could barely keep his expression neutral. If Randy actually had the Ancient Heartstones, he’d pay anything to get them.

The undisguised hope on Zane's face made Randy's smile widen. He'd been watching Zane since the assessment — the way he moved, the way he calculated, the way he gave nothing away unless he chose to. But this? This was unguarded. Whatever those stones meant to him, it went beyond ambition or greed. "It seems those stones are extremely important to you. Trying to save someone, right?"

He leaned back. “Ancient Heartstones can replenish a person’s life force, sure. But the corpses they’re found near… those are the most precious treasures.”

“Corpses are treasures?” Zane asked, his confusion growing.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

 The Sacred Feather Sea, Randy began to explain, was the ancient gathering place of the Dragon-blooded. 

[SFX: DISTANT SCREAMS]

[MUSIC: DARK CHOIR]

 Many of them had been slaughtered there in a massive blood sacrifice, turning the entire region into an incredibly dangerous, forbidden zone. For a long, long time, the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms couldn’t get anywhere near it. 

In fact, the Sacred Feather Sea was once considered even more oppressive than the Cursed Land; at least beings below the rank of War Sage could still enter the Cursed Land. 

Zane had been in the Cursed Land. He knew what that felt like — the weight of it, the way the air itself seemed to push back. Whatever had been left behind in the Sacred Feather Sea had been worse than that. Far worse. The Sea was a different story entirely.

[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS THEME]

 But no matter how deadly a place is, there’s always someone willing to try to get in. The legacy left behind by the Dragon-blooded was a feast waiting to be claimed, and the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms couldn’t rest with such a prize right on their doorstep. 

[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER BREAKING]

 After countless bloody attempts, they finally found a way into three of the so-called secret lands: the Feather Emperor’s, the Divine Emperor’s, and the Mysterious Dream.

The Union profited immensely from what they found, which only made them hungry for the final prize: the Heavenly Stellar Secret Realm.

[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]

 This last one was the very heart of the Dragon-blooded civilization, and breaking into it proved to be a nightmare. It stumped the Union for ages. Countless warriors, including more than a few Soul Sea Realm masters, died just trying to map a path inside. In the end, they had to accept defeat. The sheer power of the blood sacrifice still radiating from the Heavenly Stellar Secret Realm was just too much to overcome.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SINISTER UNDERTONE]

 So, they turned their attention back to the other lands—and the corpses in the Divine Emperor’s Secret Realm.
