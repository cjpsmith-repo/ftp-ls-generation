Ep 641 - Hold Your Tongue

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
[SFX: WHOOSH]
The moment Zane spotted the colossal tongue lunging at him, the blood drained from his face. Did it know I saw it? he wondered, but there was no time to figure it out.
Thanks to his warning, the others had scrambled out of the way, but when Fabian and Jarrod saw the sheer scale of the attack, their expressions hardened with dread. Zane was the most important person in the Tower of Sin. Anyone else could die, but he had to be protected at all costs.
Fabian hadn't wanted to provoke the Goliath Bull-Toad, but now he had no choice. He had to attack."Zane, get back! Now!"
[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]
[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]
In the next instant, he and Jarrod moved as one, their years of partnership culminating in a single, fluid motion. Light flared from their swords, two beams spiraling around each other until they merged into a blinding blast of energy aimed straight for the massive tongue. The target was so huge and moving so fast that there was no way they could miss.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
Their combined strike, powerful enough to challenge a War Sage, barely even scratched the thing. The impact was so insignificant it might as well have been a tickle. It was a complete and utter failure.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TONES]
"Damn it!" Fabian roared in frustration, his heart—along with everyone else's—sinking into a pit of despair. No matter how strong Zane’s Destiny was, it felt like nothing could save him now.
[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE]
This was the Dark Nether Cave, deep beneath the Cursed Land. A Soul Sea Realm master wasn’t just going to pop out of nowhere to rescue him. And even if one did, Fabian doubted it would matter. He suspected that not even Dorian himself would be strong enough to take on this monster.
[SFX: WHOOSHING TONGUE]
As the tongue rocketed toward him, Zane’s mind raced, desperately searching for an escape. If its saliva could melt a Relic-Class weapon, what chance did his own body have? Even if he could somehow withstand the poison, he’d be dead the second that thing wrapped around him and dragged him into the toad's waiting mouth.
He fell back fast, pushing his speed to the limit. He even crossed space, flickering sideways to open distance. The tongue was faster.
There was only one option left.
"Titan Strike!"
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]
His fist drove forward, the star-vortex slamming into the tongue and caving the surface inward. The stench rolled over him in a wave. It was the strongest punch he had — and against this thing, it left a dent and nothing more. First time that technique had ever failed him.
Floating beside him, Lavender’s face was grim. She took a deep breath, raised her long spear, and with a sharp flick, sent an invisible wave of energy surging toward the tongue. At the same moment, Yolande pointed a finger, and a beam of light shot from its tip.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACTS]
Their attacks actually seemed to work, especially Lavender’s. The invisible force from her spear slammed relentlessly into the tongue’s underside, tearing open countless ragged cuts. But the wounds were nothing against something that size. The tongue kept coming.
Zane made his call.
"Cobalt Dragon. Black Fire."
[SFX: MYSTICAL IGNITION]
[MUSIC: EERIE SUPERNATURAL THEME]
A tiny flame emerged from between Zane's eyebrows — black, barely the size of a fist, dancing in the air as if orienting itself. Then it shot straight at the tongue with a mind entirely its own.
Across the cave, Yolande's eyes flashed the moment she saw it. Her expression stilled, and a frown crossed her face — the look of someone who had just confirmed something they had long suspected. Lavender caught it immediately, her gaze moving to Yolande and staying there.
The Black Fire was invisible against the tongue's massive surface. It drilled in and disappeared without a mark.
Then the tongue went berserk.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
It whipped and twisted without direction, slamming into the ground again and again. Each impact blasted a new crater into the cave floor. Everyone scrambled back. The noise carried for miles, but nothing in the outer layer would come — not with the Bull-Toad at the top of the food chain.
"What is that Black Fire?"
"Whatever it is, the toad can't stand it."
"Why doesn't it just pull the tongue back?"
"Because the fire is still inside it. Pull it back now, and it burns from the inside out."
[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]
Suddenly, the front of the tongue burst into flames. The black fire that had been clinging to its surface ignited, and a section nearly ten feet long, starting from the tip, was instantly burned to a crisp. The air filled with the sickening smell of overcooked meat.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
The massive appendage thrashed, slamming against the ground in agony.
Only then did the wisp of black fire finally detach itself. The moment it was free, the tongue snapped back into the darkness, writhing as if trying to escape the lingering heat.
The little wisp of fire seemed proud of itself. It danced in the air, chirping happily for a moment before zipping back to the spot between Zane’s eyebrows.
[MUSIC: URGENT TENSION]
Wasting no time, Zane's eyes flashed green as he scanned the darkness ahead. The Bull-Toad had left its pool. The creature was the size of a small mountain, lying flat in the darkness nearby. Even from this distance, it could probably close the gap in a single leap. If that happened, they were all dead. "Let's get out of here! Now!"
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS DARKNESS]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Everyone followed his gaze but saw nothing in the gloom. They didn’t need to ask what he saw, though. They could imagine it all too well. If the tongue alone was that terrifying, they didn't want to find out what the rest of the beast was capable of. They were completely and hopelessly outmatched.
Zane had just burned off a piece of its tongue. This was their only chance to escape.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: ESCAPE CHASE]
They turned and ran, leaving the giant toad to its pain.
They had no intention, and frankly no nerve, to ever face it again.
