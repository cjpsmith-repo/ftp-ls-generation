Ep 100 - A Great Fortune
Word Count: 1250
[AMBIENT: AUCTION HOUSE MURMURS]
[SFX: SHIMMERING PARTICLES]
Zane was still stumped by those mysterious silver particles.
He'd scoured the Advanced Forging texts Jace had left behind, but there wasn't a single mention of them. Zane figured a real Master Weaponsmith would have actually covered something like that in their guides.
Though considered a top-tier Master Weaponsmith back in Castermere County, Jace's reputation diminished to mere mediocrity when measured against the Capital's elite craftsmen. Zane watched with growing bewilderment as the auction house erupted into barely contained chaos over the Celestial Essence, the fervor in their eyes betraying an almost religious devotion to the substance. Their frantic bidding and desperate expressions left him increasingly perplexed about the nature and purpose of this coveted material.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATION]
"Are these silver droplets really that valuable?" he whispered to himself, running his fingers absently along the edge of his seat while recalling the similar particles his Black Fire had produced. The disconnect between his accidental discovery and the fortune being offered before him sent a chill down his spine. Something profound connected these worlds of craftsmanship, something that might hold the key to his sister's freedom—if only he could unravel its mystery.
"Twenty-three thousand Crystal Cubes! The Celestial Essence is mine!" an old man shouted. His hair was snow-white, but his face was flushed with a surprising amount of energy.
"Not so fast, Hawkins! You can't hog all the Celestial Essence for yourself. I'll bid twenty-five thousand Crystal Cubes!" a gruff voice called out from another man with a stern, imposing face.
The flushed-faced man's brow furrowed. "Parker Sutton, I'm taking the Celestial Essence home today. Don't even think about getting in my way!"
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
As the two men shot to their feet and glared daggers at each other, the tension in the auction house became thick enough to cut with a knife.
In their private room, Ethan leaned in. "Those two old guys? One's a Hawkins, the other is a Sutton. That means they're from two of the Seven Great Houses. Both families are heavy hitters here in the Capital. Since they're both dead set on getting the Celestial Essence, you can bet the final price is going to be insane."
Money was never an object for the Great Houses. Both men were clearly influential elders, and if they really wanted the Celestial Essence, they wouldn't hesitate to spend whatever it took.
But even as powerful as the Hawkins and Sutton families were, there were other giants in the room. It wasn't just the two old men; plenty of others weren't ready to give up their shot at the Celestial Essence.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
Zane just stared at the auction block, his eyes wide.
"What is Celestial Essence?" he asked. "Why is it so valuable? Why does everyone want it so badly?"
Ethan laughed. "I always figured you were some kind of genius who had all the answers. Guess not, if you don't even know what Celestial Essence is."
Ethan leaned in, happy to show off. "Every ranked weapon gets its power from a trace of Celestial Law fused into it during forging. The more powerful the weapon, the more it contains."
"So it's inside every weapon?" Zane asked.
"Technically, yes. Even a Basic Enchanted Weapon holds a trace. But here's the thing — no one has ever figured out how to get it out. Burn the weapon, smash it, destroy it completely — the Celestial Essence just vanishes. It's never been extracted. That's why whenever any appears in the open, everyone loses their minds over it."
Zane shook his head. He'd read plenty of books on weapon forging, but not one of them ever mentioned Celestial Essence. The first and only time he'd ever seen it was when the Black Fire inside his mind forged that weapon for him.
As the bidding war raged on, Zane's mind drifted to his own recent sale. "Seventy thousand Crystal Cubes... That's more than enough. After The Gilded Gavel takes their cut and I cover the cost of that 'Principles of Forging' book, I should still clear around forty thousand. But more importantly..."
Zane did the math quietly. One hundred Crystal Cubes bought a superior Mysterious Weapon. Smelt it down with the Black Fire and it yielded five drops of Celestial Essence. One thousand Crystal Cubes — ten weapons — produced fifty drops. Fifty drops just sold for one hundred and twenty thousand Crystal Cubes.
His heart hammered. He had a way to extract Celestial Essence that no one else in the world apparently possessed. If he played this right, freeing Elara wasn't just a dream anymore.
[MUSIC: MOMENT OF REALIZATION]
A revelation suddenly crystallized in Zane's mind. "I think I'm beginning to understand what this Celestial Essence actually does," he murmured, his eyes narrowing thoughtfully as pieces of the puzzle aligned in his consciousness.
[AMBIENT: AUCTION HALL CHATTER]
Lost in contemplation, the clamor of the concluding auction faded to background noise. Zane's fingers absently traced patterns on the armrest while his thoughts raced through possibilities and implications. Only when chairs scraped against the floor did he notice that attendees had begun dispersing, their animated chatter filling the hall as they filed toward the exits. Only those settling accounts and claiming their purchased treasures lingered behind.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
As Zane rose to depart, a flash of rich satin caught his eye. Approaching with calculated steps was a young man whose expensive attire matched his arrogant demeanor. A self-satisfied smile played across his refined features, exuding the confidence of someone born to privilege. Beside him glided the same enchanting woman who had visited Zane's quarters earlier, her presence somehow both decorative and dangerous. Zane's muscles tensed involuntarily as recognition struck him. Felix—the persistent troublemaker who had already orchestrated an attempt on his life. Their paths crossing here could hardly be a coincidence.
[SFX: MURMURING CROWD]
Felix gestured dismissively at Zane, his smile twisting into a wicked grin.
Without missing a beat, Zane pointed right back, a cool sneer on his lips. "Right back at you."
Onlookers who caught the exchange could only shake their heads. If the two were on the same level, the open hostility might have made sense. But this kid was just some high-tier nobody. What gave him the right to be so arrogant? Who did he think he was, challenging Felix? Was he crazy, or did he have some serious backing?
Felix faltered momentarily, caught off guard by the unexpected response. This marked their first genuine face-to-face encounter, and while he had deliberately provoked with his contemptuous gesture, he hadn't anticipated such immediate defiance. The audacity of this apparent nobody to return his disdain so directly left him perplexed. Between them yawned a chasm of power and status that should have commanded deference, yet this stranger showed none. Felix's brow furrowed as he struggled to reconcile the brazen behavior with what he understood about the natural order of things.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
A servant clad in the distinctive colors of House Sterling approached with measured steps, bowing slightly before addressing his master.
"I asked you to do some digging," Felix whispered, leaning closer to ensure privacy in the still-crowded auction hall. His eyes never left the insolent young man standing before him. "Find anything useful?"
"Young Master," the servant replied with practiced discretion, his voice barely audible above the ambient chatter, "the boy's name is Zane Lowell." He emphasized the family name with subtle significance, watching as recognition flickered across his master's features.
