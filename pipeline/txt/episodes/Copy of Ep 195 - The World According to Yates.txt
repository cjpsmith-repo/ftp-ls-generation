Ep 195 - The World According to Yates
Word Count: 1271
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Across the Eastern Territory, masters in the Soul Realm didn’t just take orders from anyone. You had to be a real force to be reckoned with.
Take the rich merchants in the Province of Galeria, for example. One might be wealthy enough to hire a few Adepts as bodyguards---a rare luxury in itself---but hiring a master from the Soul Realm? That was a complete pipe dream.
[SFX: DRAMATIC IMPACT]
But Blackwood House? They could do that and much more.
Blackwood House had several Soul Realm masters on its payroll.
These powerful masters willingly served the family, ready to step in when things got dicey, all for the connections and resources Blackwood House provided.
More importantly, though, Blackwood House had bankrolled these masters back when they were still coming up, earning their unwavering loyalty. That was the real reason they stuck with the family.
[AMBIENT: WEALTHY ESTATE]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE]
As the third son of Blackwood House, Dustin had grown up around this stuff, which gave him a keen sense for these kinds of power dynamics.
Dustin assessed that if Zane somehow beat the odds and ascended to the Soul Realm, his capabilities would surpass even the elite leadership at Blackwood House. The raw potential simmering beneath Zane’s composed exterior fascinated him.
This recognition underpinned Dustin’s desire to invest in Zane’s future. Yet his investment went beyond financial backing. Zane hardly struggled with finances---evidenced by his recent plan for Dustin to auction the rare Celestial Essence, an arrangement that promised Dustin substantial compensation as well.
What Zane truly required extended beyond wealth. Dustin chose instead to contribute something far more valuable: his genuine companionship and unwavering support, cultivating a bond that deepened with each interaction.
Dustin had observed how Zane prized loyalty, authentic friendship, and mutual respect above all other virtues. Despite Zane’s occasional fiery temperament---a trait Dustin found both concerning and admirable---his dedication to those he considered brothers clearly outweighed any personal ambition or material pursuit.
For this quality alone, this capacity for profound allegiance, Dustin willingly placed his confidence in Zane’s journey. As he watched his friend navigate the complex social dynamics around them, Dustin felt an unexpected surge of pride in their alliance, sensing they stood at the threshold of something momentous.
But just then, an unpleasant voice floated over to the group. It belonged to Yates Moon---Ethan’s cousin and the favored heir of House Moon. A Level Four Adept and inner disciple of Eagle Peak, Yates carried himself with the entitled swagger of someone who had never been told no.
“You think he's a top disciple in the Skyreach Sect? Seriously? The disciples from Misty Peak are the weakest in the entire Sect. Honestly, you’re better off not joining at all than ending up there. It’s nothing but a training ground for losers.”
Ethan shot to his feet, furious. “Yates, what the hell is that supposed to mean?”
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
[SFX: PERFORMANCE STOPS]
The feast hadn’t even started, and with only half the guests there, Ethan’s outburst drew a lot of attention. Heads turned to see what the commotion was about. Even the performers on stage paused to look over.
Yates rose slowly, a smirk playing on his lips as he struggled to hold back a laugh. Ethan was always such an easy target, but something was different today. It was weird. Where had he suddenly found a backbone? Was he really counting on these guys to have his back?
[MUSIC: SNEAKY SCHEMING]
When Yates first walked in, he had already sized up Ethan’s friends.
It was a skill all disciples from the Great Houses had---a sharp eye for sizing people up.
First, he'd clocked the simple white robes on Zane, Dustin, and Rhys and immediately wrote them off as commoners. No family, no noble blood.
But while Yates's read was quick, it was dead wrong. Dustin was from Blackwood House, which was far from some no-name family. Sure, it wasn’t one of the Seven Great Houses, but it was the only family that could actually keep pace with them. In terms of real power, Blackwood House was right up there.
Of course, the plain white robes were misleading, so you couldn’t really blame Yates for writing Dustin off as a nobody.
After his initial once-over, he’d figured the three of them weren’t much of a threat anyway.
While Zane was an Adept, he was only level-one. Dustin and Rhys were just Initiate Adepts.
Yates wasn’t worried. He was a level-four Adept, and his friends were level-four and level-five. With that kind of firepower, he hadn’t given Ethan’s companions a second thought, which was why he had no problem calling them losers right to their faces.
Yates sneered, his expression souring. “Ethan, when did you grow a spine? You really have the nerve to talk to me like that?” With so many people watching, he had to put Ethan back in his place and remind him who he was dealing with.
Zane set down his cup.
"Since he's Ethan's cousin," Zane said pleasantly, "that makes you all family. And family doesn't talk to each other like that. So how about we start over?"
He smiled. It was a perfectly reasonable offer.
Yates looked at him like he'd just said something stupid. "Family? Him?" He laughed. "Who in this house considers him family?"
"Good," Zane said. The smile stayed. "That's all I needed to hear."
Dustin and Rhys glared at Yates, fuming. They couldn’t believe Ethan’s own family would be so disrespectful---or that they’d get dragged into it and humiliated just for being his guests.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
But one person in their group remained perfectly calm, his expression unreadable: Zane.
[SFX: WATER POURING]
Cool and collected, Zane picked up a dark-red enameled kettle from the table and slowly poured a stream of water over his hands.
[SFX: WATER SIZZLING]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
As the water trickled down, Vitality began to rise from within him, enveloping his hands in a faint glow. Under its heat, the water sizzled and evaporated into steam before it could even hit the floor.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
Once his hands were dry, Zane stood up, the ghost of a smile on his lips. He glanced toward the door, just as a group of female disciples from the Skyreach Sect was walking in.
According to the Sect’s rules, female disciples were required to wear either black or white robes on sect grounds, depending on their rank.
But all the disciples thought the robes were hideous. So, whenever they had a chance to leave the sect, they’d quickly change into more beautiful clothes---long dresses patterned with flying birds, or embroidered blouses paired with floral skirts.
These were the disciples from Maiden’s Peak, and they’d all changed before arriving, looking absolutely gorgeous.
[MUSIC: UPBEAT ROMANTIC]
“Brenda, is that you?” Even though he’d been seconds from a brawl with Ethan’s group, Yates’s face instantly lit up at the sight of the women from Maiden’s Peak. Brenda was Yates’s cousin and a disciple of Maiden’s Peak, known for her sharp tongue and her close friendship with Ingrid. “And Nina! You’re here too! What an honor!” he called out, his voice practically bubbling with excitement.
The sight of the ladies completely derailed his interest in fighting Ethan and his friends. He might have come for the family reunion, but the real reason he’d shown up was that his cousin, Brenda, had mentioned she was bringing some friends home for a visit.
One of those friends was Ingrid. Yates had a massive crush on her and thought about her constantly. And here she was, in his family’s home.
