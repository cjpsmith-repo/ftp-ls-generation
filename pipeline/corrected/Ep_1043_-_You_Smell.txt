Ep 1043 - You Smell

Word count: 1133

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: EPIC JOURNEY THEME]

As they traveled, Yolande and Zane hopped from one supreme world to the next. Every last one was occupied by humans, and this was just one of their three great powers. Zane was starting to get a real sense of just how dominant humanity was across the universe.

Throughout the journey, Zane had been completely focused on his training.

[SFX: CARRIAGE STOPPING]

[AMBIENT: NIGHT ATMOSPHERE]

About a month later, he felt the gentle sway of the purple carriage come to a halt. He opened his eyes and glanced out the window, where an orange crescent moon hung in the sky.

“We’re here.”

[SFX: CARRIAGE DOOR OPENING]

Yolande pushed the carriage door open and stepped out.

Zane followed her, a thought suddenly striking him. “So, if Fenton wanted to come here, how long would that take him?”

Yolande paused for a moment. “Well, if the Saint Lord of his Soulbound Sect didn’t help, he’d have to make the trip on his own. It would probably take him about two or three hundred years.”

Zane was speechless. The number was staggering. But Fenton was the pride of the Soulbound Sect; there was no way their Saint Lord would let him waste centuries just traveling. Besides, crossing this many supreme worlds must have cost a fortune—way more than Fenton could ever afford on his own.

“Let’s go,” Yolande said, already walking ahead. “Once you’re strong enough, you’ll find the universe isn’t nearly as big as you think.”

Zane watched her go, the fabric of her colorful skirt flowing around her. A small smile touched his lips before he fell into step behind her.

Truthfully, this version of Yolande felt… off. The two clones he’d met before had completely different personalities, and even though he understood why, he couldn’t shake the feeling of talking to a stranger. But seeing her graceful figure, he knew it had to be the same person.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY]

[MUSIC: GRAND ORCHESTRAL]

After a few minutes of walking, a massive city came into view. To Zane, the cities of the Upper World were all behemoths, each one feeling as vast as the entire Heartlands back home. He didn’t even try to wrap his head around how big this one really was.

At the city’s heart stood a colossal stone pillar, so enormous that Zane could see it clearly from what had to be hundreds of miles away. It must have been tens of thousands of feet tall, with a towering palace perched at its very peak.

“This is the Mist Palace,” Yolande said. “One of the Thirteen Palaces. Follow me in.”

She raised her hand. An invisible force wrapped around Zane — and in an instant, the world shifted.

[SFX: SPATIAL WHOOSH]

They were no longer hundreds of miles away. They were at the entrance.

Zane had moved short distances himself, but Yolande had just crossed hundreds of miles in a single jump, as casually as taking a step. Upper-world warriors were said to master both Spatial Law and Time Law. Judging by what he’d just felt, her control of Spatial Law was profound.

[AMBIENT: SOLEMN MARCH]

Thousands of stairs stretched before them. Every three steps, a warrior stood at attention — hundreds of them lining the ascent to the top, every single one at the Soul Sea Realm. They were motionless, expressions blank, eyes forward.

With each step Yolande took, the nearest warriors lowered their heads in silent salute. She didn’t acknowledge a single one. She just kept walking.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Before they reached the top, streaks of light appeared on the horizon and closed the distance in an instant. A small group landed beside Yolande.

Zane’s eyes swept over them. At the front was a thin, middle-aged man — hawk nose, narrow eyes, gaze sharp enough to cut.

Zane tried to read his cultivation and found nothing. Blank wall. But the aura rolling off him was unmistakable. He’s also a World Lord?

The hawk-nosed man bowed to Yolande — a shallow, perfunctory thing. “Palace Lord, you’re back. I came to welcome you.”

There was no respect in his voice.

Yolande’s expression cooled. “No need. I have things to do. You can go.”

He didn’t move.

Instead, he gestured behind him. Two well-dressed young warriors stepped forward, chins raised.

“Allow me to introduce my new recruits,” he said. “The first — his father is a World Lord. At birth, his father used the power of his entire realm to purify the boy’s body.” He paused for effect. “The second has inherited a prominent bloodline. Seven lotus leaves have already sprouted. Once he breaks through to the Soul Sea Realm, he’ll be more than qualified for Cloudswallow Hall.”

The two young warriors looked at Yolande with reverence. Their eyes, when they drifted to Zane, were a different story.

Yolande glanced at them. “Not bad,” she said flatly. “The Mist Palace can cultivate two more geniuses.”

The hawk-nosed man stroked his chin and still didn’t step aside. His eyes slid to Zane. “I’m curious, Palace Lord — what exactly did this boy say to make you come collect him in person? He must have quite the background.”

He was smiling. It didn’t reach his eyes.

“Oh, I get it now. He’s from the Lower World!” one of the warriors sneered, cutting in.

“I can smell the disgusting stench on him from here!” the other added.

In fairness, they seemed respectful enough toward Yolande. But they’d obviously picked up that the man with the hawk-like nose wasn’t, and seeing him try to humiliate her, they decided to take their cues from him and turned on Zane instead.

A flash of anger crossed Yolande’s face, but she quickly suppressed it, her eyes darting nervously toward the hawk-nosed man. She bit her lip, clearly at a loss for what to say.

[MUSIC: DEFIANT GUITAR]

That’s when Zane let out a low chuckle. He sniffed the air dramatically. “Funny,” he said. “I don’t smell a thing.”

[AMBIENT: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

One of them scoffed. “You’ve gone nose-blind to your own stench. Of course you can’t smell it.”

“Ohhh.” Zane sniffed again, feigning understanding. “I get it now. So, I guess that means you two can’t smell yourselves, either?”

“What are you talking about? What smell?” one of the warriors demanded.

“You don’t know? You both reek of stupidity,” Zane said with a grin, pinching his nose. “It’s not exactly disgusting, but it’s pretty unpleasant. Not really the kind of air I want to be breathing.”

A small laugh escaped Yolande’s lips. She knew Zane wasn’t the type to back down, but she hadn’t expected his tongue to be quite so sharp.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

“How dare you!” one of the men sputtered, his face turning red.

“How dare you!” Zane echoed, his expression mocking.

“You bastard! Who do you...”

“Bastard! Who do you think you are? Do you have any idea who my father is?” Zane finished for him, his voice dripping with sarcasm.

He couldn’t help himself; these two were just too easy to bait.
