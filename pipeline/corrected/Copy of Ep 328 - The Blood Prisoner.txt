Ep 328 - The Blood Prisoner
Word count: 1055

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
The sight of Zane struck Miles like a physical blow, freezing him where he stood. His breath caught in his throat as a single thought reverberated through his mind: Why is he back now, of all times?
[SFX: VIOLENT IMPACT]
The initial shock quickly transformed into pure horror as Miles watched Zane kill Darcy with terrifying efficiency. Just like that—one moment alive, the next gone, snuffed out like a candle.
Miles whispered to himself, his fingers trembling at his sides. How could Zane do that, kill with such cold detachment? That wasn’t just any young noble—that was Darcy, the young master of House Hawthorne, killed on Lowell soil over nothing. The political implications crashed through Miles’s mind. House Hawthorne would demand justice. This was a death sentence for all of them.
Bile rose in Miles’s throat as cold sweat beaded on his forehead.
"Z-Zane," he stammered, his voice cracking with naked fear, "do you have any idea who you just killed? The consequences—" Zane cut him off with a dismissive shake of his head, his eyes reflecting nothing but cold indifference. "Already answered that, didn’t I? I don’t give a damn who he is."
[SFX: PANICKED SHOUTING]
Miles shrieked, losing what little composure he had left. "I don’t give a damn that you don’t give a damn! That was Darcy! Young Master of House Hawthorne! Do you even know what that means? His father is the Lord of House Hawthorne—the most powerful man in Castermere County, a fifth-level Adept! Darcy died here. They’re going to wipe out the entire Lowell family!"
A wave of fear washed over the onlookers. A fifth-level Adept was a force of nature in Castermere County. Was Zane really strong enough to stand against all of House Hawthorne?
[MUSIC: THREATENING UNDERTONE]
Zane's eyes, cold and flat, pinned Miles in place. "Shut up, or you'll end up just like him."
A shiver ran down Miles’s spine; it was like being stared down by a viper. Still, he gritted his teeth. He sputtered, his voice barely a whisper. "If you kill me, my dad... he won’t let you get away with it."
"And if I don’t kill you, you think Bryson’s going to let me go?" Zane sneered, a cruel smirk playing on his lips.
"That’s right! You came back here just to die!" Miles blustered, clinging to the thought of his father for courage. And his father had gotten stronger, breaking through the Marrow Refining Level to become a third-level Adept.
"Me? Here to die?" Zane let out a sharp laugh. He shot a glance at the pavilion beside the training grounds, then planted a foot on its wall and launched himself upward. With an effortless, fluid motion, he landed silently on the roof.
[AMBIENT: ROOF]
From his new vantage point, the entire Lowell estate spread out below him—a sprawling network of buildings, courtyards, and pavilions. It was a testament to generations of Lowell work, intricately arranged and painstakingly built.
Zane murmured, his voice low and intense, "The great houses in the Imperial City would probably laugh at this. But this is ours. My family built this, piece by piece. I’m not about to let Bryson burn it to the ground."
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
Taking a deep breath, Zane channeled his Vitality until it crackled in the air around him. Then he shouted, his voice magically amplified, booming across the entire estate. "Listen up, men of the Lowell family! This is Zane. I’m back! I want every member of this family, main line or side branch, young and old—if you bear the Lowell name, get to the training field now! And a special invitation to Bryson and Ken, you two bastards!
Come out and meet your end!"
[AMBIENT: DARK INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS]
The moment Ken pushed the door open on its rusted hinges, the thick, coppery stench of blood hit him like a physical blow.
A raw, hoarse voice rasped from the darkness within. "Who’s there?"
"It’s Ken!"
"Ah. You’re here about the boy making all that noise, aren’t you?" A dry, rattling laugh echoed from the room. The prisoner had clearly heard Zane’s challenge—and the part where he called Ken and Bryson bastards. It didn’t take a genius to figure out there was bad blood.
[AMBIENT: DARK CELLAR]
"Yes, Blood Prisoner," Ken said, the words tasting like ash in his mouth. "We need your help." It was humiliating, but he was out of options.
Another dry chuckle rumbled from the darkness. "That boy has power in his voice. You wouldn’t be down here groveling if you could handle him yourself. He must be strong. You think I’m a fool?" The Blood Prisoner’s cackle was sharp this time, full of mockery.
But Ken pushed on, his voice tight with desperation. "Blood Prisoner, we’ve kept you well-fed. You’ve had fresh blood and flesh for days... we even brought you ten living men. If he beats us, that all stops. No one will be left to feed you. And he will find you down here. When you’re exposed..." He let the threat hang in the air.
[SFX: THREATENING GROWL]
"Are you threatening me?" The voice was dangerously low now. A beat of silence, then, "...Fine. I’ll help you. This once. In return, you will bring me one hundred living people within a month. I want to drink their blood and feast on their flesh."
"One hundred living people..." Ken’s face went pale. Castermere County wasn’t empty, but making a hundred people vanish without a trace? Impossible. The kind of attention that would draw... it would bring ruin down on the Lowell family.
"Can’t do it? Then this conversation is over. I’ll find another benefactor," the voice said with chilling finality.
That jolted Ken from his stupor. "I’ll get them for you, Blood Prisoner!" he blurted out, nodding frantically. "I’ll do it. Just as you say." He bowed low and began to back away.
[SFX: BOOMING LAUGH]
A booming, triumphant laugh filled the cramped space. "Excellent! I’ll drain this upstart of his blood and chew on his bones!"
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
[AMBIENT: COPPERY REEK]
With that, the Blood Prisoner emerged from the cellar, a thick, coppery reek of old iron trailing behind him.
Ken gulped nervously. He hoped what came next would be worth a hundred lives.
