Ep 159 - Tug of War
Word Count: 1166

[AMBIENT: CLIFF OUTDOORS]
[SFX: THUNDEROUS ROAR]
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
The biggest difference in this deadly tug-of-war was simple: Zane was alone. His rivals had a hundred men on their side.
But that actually gave Zane an edge. It was nearly impossible for that many people to pull in perfect sync. There were bound to be variations in their timing and strength.
To get around that, they'd roar in unison, using the sound to time their pull.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS ROAR]
 “This must be one of those 'details' Saul mentioned!” Zane realized. “If he pointed it out, it has to be the key to passing this test!”
“Strength in unity,” Zane thought. “I just have to break their rhythm so they can't pull as one.”
He flashed the men a peculiar smile, then deliberately allowed the rope to slacken between his fingers.
[SFX: HEELS DRAGGING]
As Zane eased his grip by nearly a third, he permitted them to drag him gradually toward the precipice, his mind calculating every move in this dangerous game of strategy. The rough fibers bit into his palms, but he maintained his composure, waiting for precisely the right moment.
After they'd hauled him forward several feet, Zane suddenly planted his heels firmly into the ground and wrenched the rope backward with unexpected force. His muscles strained against the tension as he channeled everything he possessed into a single, explosive burst of strength that violently pulled the rope in his direction. Sweat beaded on his forehead as determination blazed in his eyes.
"Hey! Ha!" the men shouted in unison, their voices echoing across the ravine as they spotted Zane suddenly fighting back with ferocious intensity. They collectively heaved against his pull, their faces reddening with effort.
“Just as I thought,” Zane smirked to himself, a glimmer of satisfaction crossing his features. The instant the men exerted their full strength against him, he deliberately relaxed his grip again, feeling the subtle shift in momentum that would soon work to his advantage. The rope burned against his skin, but the pain meant nothing compared to the satisfaction of seeing his strategy unfold perfectly.
[SFX: POWERFUL ROPE PULL]
The instant the rope went slack, Zane yanked again with tremendous force, his muscles burning with exertion as he poured every ounce of strength into the sudden movement. The unexpected jolt traveled down the length of the coarse hemp, catching his opponents off guard.Acting alone gave Zane a crucial advantage—he could shift strategies instantly, following nothing but his own instincts and timing. The hundred men across the ravine, however, remained prisoners of their collective rhythms. Their unity, once their strength, now became their vulnerability.
Zane's lips curled into a grim smile as he repeated the tactic with calculated precision. Pull with savage intensity, then abruptly release, only to wrench back again when they least expected it. Each time he disrupted their pattern, confusion rippled through their ranks. Sweat dripped into his eyes as he worked, but a cold determination had settled in his chest.
[SFX: CHAOTIC SHOUTING]
 Soon, they were all shouting out of sync, their combined strength a chaotic mess.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
 That was Zane's chance. He put everything he had into one massive pull.
Even if their raw strength matched his, their timing was so shot that they were effectively much weaker.
[SFX: MEN STUMBLING]
 As a result, the men started to stumble, sliding one by one toward the cliff's edge.
With every man who went over, their team lost a fraction of its power. On Zane's next powerful yank, more than thirty men were pulled over the edge. The remaining seventy men now only had about seventy percent of his strength. At that point, he had nothing to worry about.
[SFX: FINAL TUMBLE]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT]
 Zane's confidence grew with each pull as the line of men on the other side dwindled. Finally, the last of the hundred men tumbled into the ravine below. And just like that, Zane had won the strength competition.
[SFX: REALITY SHIFT]
The moment he pulled the entire rope to his side, the world around him shifted. When Zane looked up, he found himself back in the hall.
A smile spread across Saul's face. If it wasn't for the fact that he didn't seem to breathe, the immortal's puppet looked completely human.
"Well done!"
"There are three tests in total," Saul continued. "Pass the first two and the Elysian Keep is yours. The third determines your reward — but I can't say more until you've passed the second."
"What kind of reward?" Zane asked.
Saul just smiled. "Pass the second test first."
Saul pointed to a rune on the floor. Zane stepped on it.
[SFX: PAINFUL SURGE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS]
 The praise barely registered. An agony unlike any other shot through Zane. If it weren't for the black fire raging within him, the pain from the Soul Thorn would have made him pass out instantly.
His soul was strong, but his face still twisted in agony, like he'd just bitten straight into a lemon.
[MUSIC: TENSE DETERMINATION]
 Zane lifted his head and stared at the source of his new torment: a flight of stairs stretching up to the horizon. "How much pain am I in for if I get stabbed on every single one of these steps? Looks like I don't have a choice."
[SFX: POWERFUL LEAP]
 Zane suddenly leaped forward. In just a few bounds, he cleared twenty-three steps at once.
Even though jumping was a smart move, he was still jabbed by a thorn every time his foot landed. Zane gritted his teeth and pushed on, figuring it was better than getting hit on every single one.
With every leap across dozens of steps, a searing pain shot through his soul each time he landed.
After a dozen leaps, his soul had been pierced a dozen times. Even though Zane's soul was far stronger than an average person's, the pain was making his head spin. He couldn't help but clutch his head in his hands.
He had to sit down and take a break.
Looking at the endless steps still ahead, Zane took a deep breath. Once the world stopped spinning, he started climbing again.
[SFX: EXHAUSTED BREATHING]
 After pushing through several hundred more steps in one go, Zane finally landed on the two-thousandth step.
[SFX: REALITY SHIFT]
 The moment his foot touched it, the world around him changed again.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL FLAMES]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS SUPERNATURAL]
 The Soul Thorns were gone, replaced by clusters of crimson flame.
Zane figured they weren't real fire, since he couldn't feel any heat. But when he stepped on them, he felt his very soul start to cook.
He almost wished they were real fire. After all, thanks to being tempered by the Hallowed Flame, the Fire of the Earth's Core, and the Demon's Holy Fire, he had built up a resistance to heat and could even absorb it.
But this flame was different. It left his body untouched, going straight for his soul.
