Ep 708 - Departure
Word count: 1147
[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND PALACE CORRIDOR]
Cora had said no to leaving — and then she said nothing at all.
[MUSIC: TENSE NEGOTIATIONS]
In a situation like this, lashing out would only make things worse. Zane didn't even turn around; he just kept walking. Lavender, however, did glance back at Cora, her spear held loosely in her hand.
The look was enough to make Cora’s face burn with shame. She curled in on herself, scrambling for any scrap of cloth to hide her body. A cold little laugh escaped Lavender's lips before she turned and hurried to catch up to Zane.
[SFX: COLD WIND GUST]
A sudden, chilly wind, out of place in the deep underground palace, snaked through the corridor. Cora shivered and quickly pulled on the spare clothes Zane had left for her. They were far too big for her slender frame, hanging loosely around her waist and legs, but stretching uncomfortably tight across her chest.
Frustration flared through her, and she suddenly remembered the spare set she had in her own Storage Ring. She’d completely forgotten in the panic. But as she reached for it, she saw Zane and Lavender were already disappearing down the corridor, leaving her behind. Gritting her teeth, Cora abandoned the idea of changing and ran to catch up.
She didn't say another word. She understood her position now with uncomfortable clarity.
Zane didn't need to take her anywhere. Her Violet Core Physique was no longer of use to Maddox, which meant she was no longer anyone's target, but she was also no longer anyone's priority. She was deep in a lethal underground labyrinth with no idea which way led out. If Zane walked away, she died. It was that simple. The stubbornness that had gotten her this far went quiet.
She stayed close and kept her mouth shut.
Zane moved through the labyrinthine paths without a backward glance. Lavender, walking beside him, paid Cora no mind, a mysterious smile playing on her lips.
"What?" Zane finally asked.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
"So," Lavender began, her smile widening. "How are you feeling?"
Zane hesitated for a moment. "My energy is flowing more smoothly," he admitted. "My Vitality… it feels purer."
Lavender’s strange smile returned. It wasn’t the answer she’d been expecting. After what just passed between them, Zane’s power should have skyrocketed. She wanted to press him for details, but she knew it would only make him uncomfortable, so she let it go.
She changed the subject. "There are actually some incredibly powerful — and secret — Coupled Cultivation methods in the upper world."
"Coupled Cultivation?" Zane frowned. "I was taught those were forbidden paths."
A faint, knowing smile touched Lavender's lips. "That's just how things are seen here. In the grand scheme of the universe, there is no such thing as forbidden and permitted — only what works and what doesn't."
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
She stopped. Zane had gone still.
[SFX: CRASHES]
Behind them, deep in the palace, something had changed. The distant sounds of pursuit — the occasional echo of movement, the faint pressure of Maddox's aura pressing through the stone — had shifted into something louder and more chaotic. A crash. Then another. Then a sound that wasn't any of Maddox's people.
Something else had woken up.
Maddox's temper had finally gotten the better of him. Frustrated by the endless forks and dead ends, he'd started smashing through walls — and had disturbed something that had been sleeping in the deep palace for a very long time. Even Simon had encountered this creature years ago and barely survived. Now the two of them and what remained of their warriors were running the other direction.
Zane didn't wait to find out what it was. He moved.
[MUSIC: URGENT PERCUSSION]
Ahead, a thin sliver of light appeared between two stone slabs. Zane fixed on it and didn't slow down.
[SFX: STONE GRINDING — EXIT]
[AMBIENT: OPEN AIR — SWAMP EDGE]
They burst out into grey daylight. After the dead silence of the palace, even the sound of the marsh felt loud.
Cora came through the exit a half-step behind Zane, squinting against the light. She pressed her lips together and said nothing.
Lavender looked back at her, something almost like amusement crossing her face. "Didn't you keep telling Zane to let go of you? Weren't you the one who wasn't afraid of death?"
Cora said nothing. Lavender smiled and turned away.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]
Zane didn't linger. He grabbed Cora and launched them both into the air, burning Vitality to put distance between them and the palace exit as fast as possible.
The marsh had more to say before it let them go. A predatory bird dropped out of the cloud cover above them — enormous, silent until it wasn't, its wingspan blotting out a section of sky. A three-hundred-foot swamp python surfaced from the murk below, its body thick as a building. Zane didn't stop for either of them. He adjusted his angle and kept moving, trusting speed over confrontation.
Two hours after leaving the palace, the marsh fell away beneath them.
[AMBIENT: CITY IN TURMOIL]
They came down above Pyreton — a mid-sized settlement, nothing like Ethereal City or Cloudhaven. The Corrupted Divine Kingdom's expansion had already cast a shadow over it. Warriors from local sects were moving south in groups. Nearly half the city's fighters were gone, and ordinary citizens were following, packing what they could carry and leaving the rest.
High above the now-emptying city, Zane released Cora's hand. "This is where we part ways," he said, his voice casual. "Head south. If you go around The Dreaming Marshes, you'll reach Ethereal City."
[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Cora had been resting against his back during the flight, the wind whipping past them. The sudden finality of his words left her stunned. The war with the Corrupted Divine Kingdom wasn't ending anytime soon. If Maddox failed to catch Zane, he would undoubtedly turn his forces back on the Ethereal Spirit Sect. With the Septuplets gone, even with Elder Xander and the other elders there, the sect's protective array wouldn't survive another assault. What good could she possibly do by going back?
"I…" Cora started, but the words caught in her throat.
Zane glanced at her, his expression unreadable. He said nothing. If Maddox already sensed he was out of The Dreaming Marshes, then he was likely already on their trail. The road to Cloudhaven was long and dangerous, and bringing Cora would only slow him down. He didn't say any of this.
[SFX: WHOOSH OF FLIGHT]
He simply turned and flew away, leaving her suspended in the air.
[MUSIC: DARK EMOTIONAL PIANO]
Cora floated alone, watching his silhouette shrink into the horizon. She bit her lip so hard she tasted blood, but the only thing she could feel was a bitter resentment at his coldness. Overwhelmed, an idea — raw and desperate — began to form in her mind.
She hung there for five long minutes, a fire igniting deep in her gut.
[SFX: FIRE IGNITION]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
Suddenly, it erupted.
[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]
Purple flames burst from her core, engulfing her completely.
They weren't born of her own Vitality — her power had no connection to fire. The flames were something else entirely.
They were from the Violet Core Physique.
