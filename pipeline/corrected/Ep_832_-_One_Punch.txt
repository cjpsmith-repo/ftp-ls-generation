Ep 832 - One Punch

Word count: 1023

[AMBIENT: TENSE THRONE ROOM]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

War King York's fury had not cooled since Zane pushed the Little Monster to the ground — and now, with the crowd still reeling, he was done waiting.

Feeling way out of his depth, the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom kept his mouth shut, not daring to argue with War King York. But his son, Randy, couldn't hold his tongue and stepped forward to defend Zane.

“War King York,” he said, his voice ringing with conviction, “injuries are part of any battle. Why are you blaming Zane for this?”

[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]

A ripple of shock went through the other warriors. Even Zane looked surprised. Just having the guts to stand up to War King York was impressive enough on its own.

“Shut up!” York roared, his face a mask of pure fury. “Who do you think you are, talking back to me? Say one more word, and I'll kill you where you stand!” He was so enraged he wasn't even thinking straight.

Randy might have been a favored son of the Harlen Emperor, but he was still just a prince, not the heir. With his own father staying silent, it wasn't his place to challenge a man like York. To the powerful warriors watching, Randy looked like an absolute fool.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION BUILD]

But the moment York threatened his son, the Harlen Emperor couldn't take it anymore. He spoke, his voice dangerously low. “York, what is the meaning of this? How dare you speak to my son that way in front of me?”

“What do I mean? You figure it out,” York sneered, not even sparing him a proper glance. He had no time to waste on the Harlen Emperor. All he wanted was to unleash his fury on Zane; everyone else was just noise.

Just then, a wild laugh erupted from Zane. “Ha! War King York, you're the ruler of a divine kingdom, an emperor to countless warriors, and you don't even know the rules? A moment ago, that 'Little Monster' of yours tried to destroy my soul. Was I supposed to just sit back and let it happen? That's ridiculous! If I hadn't fought back, I’d be the one with a shattered soul right now. It got exactly what it was asking for.”

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]

“Oh, you're right. Injuries are part of the fight,” York said, his voice chillingly calm but simmering with rage. “But this is an eye for an eye. You killed my son, and now you've done this to the Little Monster. It's time to settle the score. I've had enough of your arrogance!”

York took one step forward, passing straight through the barrier as though it wasn't there, and came to stand before Zane.

[SFX: PRESSURE WAVE]

The soul pressure hit like an avalanche — hundreds of mountains pressing down at once. The saint-level geniuses below staggered. Some grabbed their heads. Others simply dropped, eyes rolling back, consciousness snuffed out. York wasn't even directing it at them; he was venting, and the overflow alone was enough to flatten lesser warriors.

Zane didn't move. Didn't flinch. His soul had merged with the will of the world itself — York's pressure washed over him and found nothing to grip.

York's eyes narrowed. 

Zane's gaze was as still as water. He simply asked, “How do you want to settle this?”

York took a deep, steadying breath. The Little Monster was his kingdom's ace, the trump card that gave the York Divine Kingdom a massive advantage over the other six. With it gone, his kingdom would suffer immensely. For one, the creature helped the princes cultivate at an astonishing rate. For another, once it reached the Life-and-Death Realm, it could have even assisted warriors in the Soul Sea Realm. 

Everyone had been waiting for the Little Monster to mature, and now, thanks to Zane, that future was gone.

It was because of that creature that the other rulers had conceded so much to York, even letting his kingdom take the lion's share of resources from the Reverse Spirit Channel. Now that it had been reduced to a drooling simpleton, the loss was staggering—far too great to let Zane just walk away.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS PERCUSSION]

“How?” A sinister smile twisted York's face, his eyes blazing with a mad light. “Are you really asking such a stupid question? You'll pay with your life. But I'll give you a chance. If you can survive one punch from me — and I'll use only eighty percent of my strength — I'll let you go.” He let out a dark chuckle. “Prepare to die.”

At the top of the stairs, Fiona gasped, her hand flying to her mouth. The scene was too horrifying to watch. Zane, taking a punch from a warrior in the Soul Sea Realm? It was a death sentence. The thought sent a flicker of sadness through her eyes. She couldn't help but worry, knowing just how terrifyingly powerful War King York truly was.

Randy squeezed his eyes shut, his face a portrait of misery. Even a prince like him wouldn't dare face a single punch from that man.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The Soul Sea masters all shook their heads quietly. Eighty percent. That was no chance at all. Even thirty percent would kill Zane instantly.

But Zane was thinking about the Little Monster.

The creature had transferred every attack into the surrounding mountains and rivers — a borrowed strength, flowing through the plane itself. Now that Zane had merged with the world's will, he felt that same connection humming around him. Everything was familiar. Everything was his. If he could channel York's blow the same way, distribute it into the earth and the river and the stone…

It wasn't certain. But it was something.

Zane simply nodded. “Agreed.”

York blinked. He had expected argument, deflection, anything but that. He laughed. “You really think you can survive it?”

“I don't know,” Zane said.

[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

A cold, triumphant smile crept across York's face. He began to gather his Vitality, the very air around him crackling with power.

But just as he prepared to strike, a voice rang out from the stairs. “Wait!”

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Everyone who had been holding their breath for Zane's certain death snapped their heads toward the sound. 

Who would dare speak right now?
