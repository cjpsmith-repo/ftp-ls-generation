Ep 1019 - The Voice

Word count: 1102

[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION CHAMBER]

Zane had been counting for hours, all just to get under the skin of those two Supreme Lords.

[SFX: MAGICAL COMMUNICATION CHIME]

The last thing he expected was for a woman’s voice to cut through his counting.

Zane’s heart leaped at first, hoping it was Elara. But his hope crashed when he realized it wasn’t her voice. “This is getting old,” he grumbled back at the woman.

“Then why are you still doing it?” she asked.

“Honestly, it’s pretty boring. I’m not really in the mood to talk anymore. I think I’ll go cultivate,” he said, finally tucking away the Roaring Token he’d been fiddling with.

But the woman on the other end quickly cut in, “Wait! Don’t go. Want me to sing you a song?”

[SFX: MULTIPLE COMMUNICATION TOKENS ACTIVATING]

The offer sounded innocent enough, but her suggestion was met with an instant, panicked chorus of voices. Suddenly, nearly every reclusive warrior was chiming in on their Roaring Tokens.

“No! Please don’t sing!”

“Your voice is lovely, it really is, but please, for the love of all things sacred, don’t sing! Nobody wants to hear you sing!”

“Hey, hey! Didn’t you promise us you wouldn’t do this anymore?”

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC OVERLAPPING VOICES]

They were all talking over each other.

Just a little while ago, Zane’s squabble with Armond and Magnus had been met with dead silence from the other Supreme Lords. No one had considered their little fight important enough to chime in.

But this? A simple offer to sing had sparked a loud, unified freak-out.

Zane couldn’t make sense of their reaction. She just wanted to sing. Wouldn’t that be better than listening to him count all day?

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS MELODY]

“I just want to sing a song. Why is everyone so nervous?” the woman asked, her tone gentle. “Am I really that bad?”

[SFX: PANICKED VOICES]

“No! No one in the entire universe has a more beautiful voice than you, but you promised us you wouldn’t sing again!”

“We think your voice is wonderful, but please, just don’t sing.”

“Don’t sing. Please, just don’t do it!”

The Supreme Lords’ frantic pleas only made Zane more curious. “Why are they so afraid to hear you sing? Are you really that terrible?”

The woman giggled. “They don’t like it when I sing, but I really want to.”

A few years ago, this woman’s voice had appeared in the universe for the very first time. Her opening line had been so spectacularly useless that it was still remembered by every living being who heard it: “Is there anyone here?”

Of course, there were people. But no one — not even Armond or Magnus, who had plenty of tokens to burn — could be bothered to answer something so pointless. So after she asked several times and got nothing, she simply started singing.

[MUSIC: RISING HORROR]

Her voice was extraordinary. Under any other circumstances, it would have been a gift to hear it. But there was a problem: her voice carried something else inside it. A demonic quality, subtle and invasive, that crept into the minds of intelligent beings and set them on edge — and drove beasts with undeveloped minds into an absolute frenzy.

What followed was the largest Beast Tide in the history of the universe. Every manner of beast, from the weakest swarms to creatures of terrifying power, all going berserk at once. The weaker races had no warning and no defense. Countless Holy Lands and Divine Kingdoms were destroyed.

All because of one song.

After that, various Supreme Lords had attempted to find out who she was and where she came from. She refused to answer every time. Eventually, they gave up trying to identify her and settled for persuading her never to sing again. She agreed — and then went completely silent. For years, her voice hadn’t been heard in the universe.

Until today. Until Zane.

The Supreme Lords weren’t just terrified — they were furious at him. He was the one who’d drawn her out. They didn’t dare say so, but the resentment was there.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

“Hmm,” the woman hummed thoughtfully. “Okay. I won’t let the demonic sound out. Don’t worry.”

“Are you sure?” one of the Supreme Lords asked, his voice strained. “Last time it was the reverberation that set them off. You’re certain it won’t happen again?”

“It won’t,” she said simply. “I’ll keep it clean.”

Even with her promise, no one fully relaxed. A Beast Tide wasn’t just beasts going wild in one place — it was a simultaneous eruption across entire worlds. Even the strongest factions in the universe had no reliable way to stop something like that once it started.

[AMBIENT: MILITARY ALERT]

[SFX: ALARM SIRENS]

Across the universe, defenses were already snapping into place. Every entrance, main road, and city wall was suddenly reinforced and bristling with guards.

[MUSIC: EPIC WAR DRUMS]

It wasn’t just one race or one faction. Every living being with the sense to be afraid was moving. From the smallest Tier-Three sects to the great tenth-tier Holy Lands and Divine Kingdoms, orders went out simultaneously: gather your forces, deploy to the passes and city walls, prepare for the worst.

It was as if the entire universe was preparing for war.

No one even knew who she was, so there was no way to hold her accountable if she unleashed that kind of devastation on the universe a second time.

They had to stay vigilant against a threat that could shatter worlds.

The Soulbound Sect, where Zane was, was no exception. Its members were mobilizing for war, and powerful masters were already gathering to defend their home. The warriors who had recently arrived from the Lower World weren’t expected to do a thing. None of them had a clue how terrifying a Beast Attack actually was, so most just kept on digging for ore.

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL HAUNTING MELODY]

A moment later, a clear, melodious voice rippled across the universe.

“Reviving in the midst of the wind and leaves,

floating under the shadow of the trees.

The musical notes around my fingertips, for whom?

The whistling wheels.”

[AMBIENT: STARRY NIGHT]

[MUSIC: NIGHTINGALE SONG]

The melody was slow and elegant, almost haunting, and her lilting voice was a perfect match for it. It sounded like a nightingale singing on a cool, starry night.

But no one was in the mood to appreciate her singing—except for Zane, of course.

Everyone else was wound far too tight, the threat of a massive Beast Attack hanging over their heads, to pay any mind to her otherworldly voice.
