Ep 881 - Lotus Flower

Word count: 1192

[AMBIENT: TENSE WIND]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

Zane stared up at the constellation of golden longswords forming in the sky, drawing a slow, deep breath as every muscle in his body pulled taut.

They were forged from pure law, not Celestial Essence, which gave him no way to gauge their power. Still, the cold, silver glint off their edges was unmistakable. This was going to hurt.

[SFX: METALLIC RING]

A moment later, the cloud of blades churned without a breath of wind. With a high, metallic ring, the first sword shot down. The rest followed in a heartbeat, the golden cloud collapsing into a rushing torrent aimed straight for him.

Zane dug his heels in, bracing himself against the storm. His eyes narrowed, glinting like steel as he met the golden deluge head-on.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]

“I survived my Metal Tribulation only because I had the Demon Heart Armor, and I still burned through two hundred thousand supreme Aether crystals,” one of the masters from the Soul Sea Realm said, shaking his head. “Those swords look far sharper, and that kid has nothing.”

The War King just offered a helpless smile. “If the armor still existed, I’d gladly lend it to Zane. But it was destroyed decades ago.”

Just as they spoke, the golden torrent crashed over Zane.

[SFX: MASSIVE ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

Zane met the first sword head-on, snatching it out of the air and crushing it in his fist in a single, explosive motion. It wasn’t a real sword at all. He realized it was just a construct of pure Metal Law, weaker than even a standard Relic-Class weapon. So that was their real strength.

[SFX: METALLIC RAIN]

But in the next instant, their numbers multiplied, raining down from every direction. He couldn’t possibly catch them all. Suddenly, he was like a man who couldn’t swim tossed into a raging river, completely submerged by the golden flood.

His body was as durable as a Relic-Class weapon itself, and every sword that struck him shattered into golden dust. The particles dissolved into wisps of Metal Law and vanished into the air. But even if the blades couldn’t break him, their edges were still impossibly sharp, slicing at him with every impact.

The cuts multiplied, layering over one another. One scratch became ten, ten became a hundred — and then the accumulated edges finally found purchase. Blood welled up across Zane’s arms and shoulders in thin lines, running down in bright rivulets.

Zane exhaled slowly and closed his eyes.

[SFX: DEEP RESONANT PULSE]

[MUSIC: LOW RUMBLING STRINGS]

Thousands of miles away, on the western edge of the Divine Kingdom Continent where no one ever went, a mountain range stood silent and vast. 

Then, without warning, invisible sword energy began seeping through the rock.

The mountains — thousands of feet high — started to crumble. Not in great crashing avalanches, but quietly, steadily, as though something unseen was gnawing them away from the inside. Stone became dust. Dust drifted on the wind. Peak after peak dissolved until the entire range had simply ceased to exist.

[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]

The golden swords above Feather Emperor’s Island vanished.

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

The Wind Tribulation came next. Then the Water. The remaining Five Elements Tribulations arrived one after another, each wave cresting and breaking against him in turn.

An hour passed.

Zane looked up at a clear sky and blinked. “Is it finally over?”

He felt lighter than he had in years. Through every wave, the Vitality in his core had continued its slow, steady solidification. He was almost on his feet when the shadow of a lotus appeared above him — pure white, enormous, its bud still closed.

[SFX: ETHEREAL SHIMMER]

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]

“What is that?”

“Why is there a lotus in the sky?”

“Is this another tribulation? It doesn’t look like one.”

The Soul Sea masters exchanged uncertain glances. None of them had an answer.

The White-Haired Priest did.

His expression changed the moment he saw the bud. His voice, when it came, was barely steady. “It bloomed. A lotus — in a Lesser Celestial Tribulation. It actually bloomed.”

[SFX: SOFT MAGICAL CHIME]

Beside him, the Holy Maiden went very still, her thoughts moving quickly. Through restricted documents held by the secret order, she knew what the lotus represented. In the Upper Realm, there were no Heaven-grade or God-grade geniuses — potential was measured only by the tribulation. The lotus was said to be a mark that surpassed the Heavenly Way itself. 

It didn’t guarantee anything. Across a hundred thousand great realms, many warriors had bloomed a lotus over the ages, and in 3.6 billion years, how many had actually surpassed the Heavenly Way? Very few. But the lotus meant the possibility existed.

What stopped her was something else entirely. “Lotus flowers are supposed to appear in major tribulations,” she said quietly. “Not minor ones. Why did he — “

She trailed off, staring at Zane. They were enemies. That hadn’t changed. But her curiosity, against her will, was stronger than her animosity. “How many petals do you think he’ll open?” she asked the White-Haired Priest. “Three? Five?”

The White-Haired Priest shook his head. “A warrior who blooms three petals is fit to become the core of a nine-grade Holy Land. Five petals —” he paused — “five is enough to enter a tenth-grade Holy Land. To be the heart of a supreme first-rate force.” He turned to her. “This needs to go through the Reverse Spirit Channel. Now.”

The Holy Maiden nodded; she understood exactly what that meant. Whatever conflict stood between her and Zane — it was nothing next to this.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

A chill ran down the spines of the other Soul Sea warriors, their expressions turning grim. They were considered titans in the Divine Kingdom Continent, rulers of seventh-grade divine kingdoms. But after all these years, they still hadn’t conquered The Sacred Feather Sea, a mere eighth-grade sacred place. What would a ninth-grade place be like? Or a tenth? It was beyond anything they could imagine. Compared to the masters of the upper world, they were nothing.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

The pure white lotus spun slowly above Zane’s head.

[SFX: SOFT WHOOSH]

After a long moment, a single petal gently unfurled from the bud, releasing a sweet fragrance that instantly filled the air.

“It smells incredible,” Fiona breathed, taking a deep sniff. The scent was rich without being overpowering, and just a single breath of it felt like it was seeping straight into her soul.

“The first one,” the Holy Maiden murmured, biting her lip. She had only ever read about the lotus in ancient texts; to see a warrior actually make one bloom was something else entirely. She found herself hoping, almost against her will, to see more petals open.

Zane stood perfectly still, his eyes clear as he watched the lotus turn slowly in the sky above him. Thanks to the Cobalt Dragon, he knew exactly what this meant.

[SFX: GENTLE MAGICAL CHIME]

As the flower continued its graceful spin, a second petal unfurled.

“The second one,” the Holy Maiden whispered, keeping count.
