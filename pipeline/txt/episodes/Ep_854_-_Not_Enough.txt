Ep 854 - Not Enough

Word count: 1133

[SFX: DIGGING THROUGH DEBRIS]

Zane dug the ores from the Stone-Eater Bug’s carcass and handed them over to Camilla. He’d managed to get a good number of Ancient Heartstones, but it still wasn’t nearly enough.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

Tia’s life force was almost gone. This new haul might be enough to wake her, but it wouldn’t come close to restoring her to full strength. The guilt of letting her get into this state was a heavy weight on Zane’s shoulders, and the only way he could even begin to make things right was to find every last Ancient Heartstone he could get his hands on.

When it became clear there was no Soul Core inside the bug, the other saint-level geniuses let out a collective, disappointed sigh. It wasn’t like they’d expected to get the core for themselves, but seeing Zane catch the creature had given them a sliver of hope.

After dealing with the corpse, Zane turned to Randy. “So,” he asked, “what’s the big deal with these Soul Cores, anyway?”

It wasn’t some closely guarded secret—even most of the newcomers to the Sacred Feather Sea seemed to know about it. Randy simply said, “You remember that Heavenly Stellar Secret Land I told you about?”

Zane nodded.

Most of the masters from the Soul Sea Realm didn’t care much for the other secret lands; their one true objective was the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORCHESTRAL]

It was far more important than the other three combined, holding the secrets of the dragon-blooded and the key to the Ancestral Dragon Egg.

“So the Soul Core is the key to getting in?” Zane asked.

“Exactly,” Randy said with a smile. “Only with a Soul Core can you enter the Grand Graveyard inside the Divine Emperor’s Secret Land and control the corpses of the dragon-blooded.”

[SFX: MYSTICAL RUMBLE]

Zane’s eyes lit up. At the same time, he felt a faint tremor in his mind—the soul of the Cobalt Dragon, stirring. Zane had promised the dragon he’d get the Ancestral Dragon Egg, but only if the opportunity presented itself. Right now, even a powerful warrior from the Soul Sea Realm would struggle to get into the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. With his current strength, Zane knew he didn’t stand a chance.

Before the Cobalt Dragon could speak, Zane had already answered in his mind: “If I can get a Soul Core from this mine, I’ll go.”

The relief that crossed the Cobalt Dragon’s face was quiet but unmistakable. He closed his enormous eyes. Beside him, the Red Dragon did the same. They’d taken a heavy blow blocking the Little Monster’s soul attack — they needed time to recover, and they weren’t going to ask more of Zane than he could give.

Zane didn’t press Randy further on why the Soul Core was needed to control the corpses inside the Divine Emperor’s Secret Land. Knowing the answer was useless until he actually had one. Right now, Ancient Heartstones came first.

He bid farewell to Randy and headed back to the pyramid.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING MARKETPLACE]

Camilla had already put the new haul to work. The commotion around Zane had done her a favor — every warrior still working the mine had drifted past her stall at least once, and plenty of them had Ancient Heartstones buried in their Storage Rings. She wasn’t going to let a single one slip through.

He figured it was a good thing the Sacred Feather Sea opened every year. If he couldn’t pull it off this time, he’d just focus on gathering enough Ancient Heartstones for Tia. He could always head back to the Heartlands, help her recover, and return to this continent when he was stronger.

[AMBIENT]

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[SFX: PICKAXE STRIKES]

Back inside the mine, Zane had expected the higher floors to yield more Ancient Heartstones. Half a day of work above the thirtieth floor told him otherwise. The leaves were harder, but the Ancient Heartstones weren’t more frequent. If anything, they were slightly fewer. He understood now why Randy had pointed him toward floors twenty-five through twenty-nine. The problem was he’d already stripped most of those himself.

He kept climbing — thirtieth floor, thirty-first, thirty-second — driving his pickax into leaves that grew denser with every level.

[SFX: PICKAXE STRIKES]

He ran the numbers as he worked. The mine reset every year — new leaves, new ores. Every year, the kingdoms stripped it clean. Which meant this year’s total yield was fixed. His best estimate: five to six hundred Ancient Heartstones across the entire mine.

His goal was a thousand.

He let out a slow breath and kept swinging.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]

Six hours later, Zane emerged from the mine. The grumbling of the other geniuses echoed around him.

“Not even a Soul Sea Realm warrior could keep up with him, right?”

“I’m done. This year is a total loss. Next year, I’m convincing my father not to let him in. He’s going to strip the entire mine clean by himself!”

“Ugh, I really want that Saint Forging Stone, but that woman is asking for fifty Ancient Heartstones. Fifty! I don’t even have one!”

Many of them were seething with jealousy, but no one dared to try and rob Zane. The guy hadn’t even flinched when he stared down Fredrick, the crown prince of the York Divine Kingdom. They knew if they tried to jump him, it wouldn’t end well for them.

Zane’s mining speed was easily on par with the powerful warriors from the Soul Sea Realm. By channeling the power of the Dragon Scales, his raw strength could match and sometimes even surpass theirs.

But what’s more, those warriors had to rely on their own physical stamina, which eventually ran out. Zane, on the other hand, was running on a different system entirely. He was channeling the seemingly endless power of the Dragon Scales, and at his current level, he was using it far slower than it could regenerate. It gave him a practically bottomless well of energy. With an advantage like that, who could keep up?

Speed wasn’t the only issue, though. The real problem was that the number of Ancient Heartstones was finite.

Camilla had been busy. The ores piled around her had grown into a small mountain — easily as tall as a person, shimmering with every color imaginable. It was the result of Zane mining without stopping to sort. He didn’t know how to identify ores — he’d just taken everything. Most of it was valuable. Some of it wasn’t.

[SFX: HEAVY THUD]

“Camilla,” Zane asked as he walked over, tossing another brightly colored ore onto the mountain, “how many Ancient Heartstones have you managed to trade for so far?”

Whatever the number was, he already knew it wouldn’t be enough.
