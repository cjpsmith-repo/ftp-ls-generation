Ep 963 - Persuasion
Word count: 1160
[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
The old man had made his pitch — and Cora, still unconvinced, was halfway out of her seat.
“Hold on, young lady. Just hear me out,” the old man said quickly. He realized his mistake then—it had never crossed his mind that someone might see martial arts as a waste of time.
Still, everyone had their own motivations. He’d seen his fair share of eccentrics among the Demi-Immortals. And since this young woman was so fixated on Zane, he knew just how to bait the hook.
Seeing the look on the old man’s face, Cora forced herself to swallow her anger and sat back down. She shot him a cold look. “What else do you want?”
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS HARP]
He chuckled. “You may not be the bride today, but I can guarantee you’ll be the one by Zane’s side in the end.”
That got her attention. She blinked, her focus sharpening on him. “What do you mean by that?”
“The bride’s not bad,” he said, shaking his head and glancing over at Tia. “She might be a top-tier warrior in the Lower World, but that means absolutely nothing in the Upper World.”
“What does any of this have to do with me?” Cora asked, confused. The old man was talking about it all so casually, she had no idea how to respond.
He chuckled again. “Of course, it has something to do with you. Zane is a truly powerful warrior. Why would someone like him stay cooped up in a place like the Heartlands?”
“What do you mean, stuck in the Heartlands?” she retorted. “It’s a huge place with plenty of resources. Where else does he need to go?”
She thought the Heartlands was perfectly fine. It was great for Zane—no one there could ever be a real threat to him. A peaceful, comfortable life was more than enough of a blessing. It wasn’t that Cora was short-sighted; she just had zero interest in anything beyond her own little world.
After awakening her First-Rank Violet-Core Physique, Cora had a vague sense of what her special body was capable of. But she’d never once considered training it or getting stronger—it just didn’t seem that important. Besides, warriors with Violet-Core Physiques were always women, so it made sense for a woman to be her teacher, not this old man.
The old man privately rolled his eyes. Vast? Resource-rich? The entire Heartlands was a distance he could cross in a few steps — smaller than one of his courtyards. Smaller, even, than his Inner World. And resources? He’d watched every eye in the room light up at the sight of a single Divine Weapon, like starving ghosts who hadn’t eaten in a hundred years. He couldn’t even bring himself to call this place remote. It wasn’t worthy of the word.
But Cora had a strange temperament, and saying any of that out loud wouldn’t help him. So he kept his thoughts to himself and pressed on.
“Actually — before we get to that.” The old man leaned forward. “That green pill I gave Zane just now. You know what it does?”
Cora shook her head.
“With that pill and his talent, Zane’s cultivation will accelerate beyond anything you’ve seen. And when he breaks through the Soul Sea Realm —” the old man paused for effect, “— the Lower World will reject him. He’ll have no choice but to ascend to the Upper World.”
Cora’s expression darkened. “You gave him something that will take him away from here?” Her voice was flat, but her eyes were sharp. “That’s not doing him a good turn. That’s doing him harm.”
The old man stared at her. Of all the reactions he’d expected, this wasn’t one of them. “Even without the pill,” he said carefully, “he would still ascend eventually. That’s just what happens when someone reaches that level.”
“And then?” Cora asked.
“And then,” the old man continued, “his bride — talented as she is — won’t be able to follow him. Not quickly. Without Zane coming back for her, it would take her a hundred, maybe two hundred years to ascend on her own.”
Cora was very still. “And then?”
The old man almost rolled his eyes again. “Isn’t it obvious? You have a First-Rank Violet-Core Physique. With the right training, your cultivation speed won’t be slower than his. You could ascend with him. You could be at his side in the Upper World — while she sits alone waiting for him down here.”
He let that sit for a moment, then added with a pointed glance toward Tia: “Don’t think she’s better off than you right now. The one who laughs last laughs loudest.”
[MUSIC: DAWNING REALIZATION]
Cora’s eyes lit up for the first time.
She’d never cared about cultivation. But the old man had just drawn her a picture of the future — Zane in the Upper World, Tia left behind, and Cora already there waiting for him. For that, she could work. For that, she could endure anything — including sitting here today, smiling through a wedding that felt like a knife in her chest.
“I understand,” she said quietly. Then, after a moment, “So who’s this master? Don’t tell me it’s you.”
The old man shook his head firmly. “Of course not. It’s my wife.”
“So where is she?” Cora asked, her curiosity piqued.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
“Open your hand,” he ordered.
Cora obediently opened her pale, delicate palm.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
Suddenly, a scarlet cross materialized on the old man’s fingertip. The divine pattern inside it resembled a Demi-Immortal’s locator rune, only much, much smaller.
[SFX: MAGICAL PULSE]
The scarlet cross immediately sank into Cora’s palm, leaving a striking mark against her soft, pale skin.
Cora raised her hand, staring at the mark. “What is this?” she asked, her voice laced with worry.
“It’s just a locator rune,” the old man replied calmly. “The next time you enter the Violet Core Realm, my wife will be able to find you.”
“The next time I enter the Violet Core Realm?” Cora repeated. “‘But I don’t plan on ever going in there,” she confessed. The only reason she’d ever hide there was if someone was chasing her.
The old man’s eyes widened in disbelief. “My word, you really do hate training, don’t you? You have to find the time to enter the Violet Core Realm. As long as you’re in there, my wife can find you! You have to work for it—fight for your own happiness.” With that, he jutted his chin toward the happy scene nearby.
[AMBIENT: WEDDING CELEBRATION]
[MUSIC: JOYFUL STRINGS]
Guests were arriving in droves, chatting amongst themselves, while Zane and Tia stood at the center of it all, beaming.
Cora shot the couple a cold glare that no one else noticed.
[MUSIC: DARK DETERMINATION]
Her expression hardened with a new resolve. “Okay,” she said, her voice firm. “I get it.”
