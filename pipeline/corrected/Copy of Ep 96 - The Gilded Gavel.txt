Ep 96 - The Gilded Gavel
Word Count: 1040
[AMBIENT: SECLUDED MOUNTAIN HALL]
[MUSIC: SERENE CULTIVATION MELODY]
In a secluded side hall on Misty Peak, several Skyreach Sect officials distributed elixirs with methodical efficiency. The chamber remained relatively quiet, with only a sparse gathering of disciples patiently awaiting their allotments. Sunlight filtered through high windows, casting long shadows across the polished stone floor.
Zane, Ethan, and Rhys approached the distribution table, presenting their access cards with practiced motions.
[SFX: CRYSTALLINE TINKLING]
Each received a small collection of Grade-two Energy Capsules—crystalline capsules that gleamed with a subtle azure luminescence. These valuable elixirs were specifically formulated to accelerate the body's absorption of Life Energy, making them invaluable supplements for rigorous cultivation sessions. Though commonplace within the sect's hallowed grounds, these capsules commanded respectable prices in outside markets, a fact not lost on the three young men as they carefully secured their acquisitions.
"These should last us through the week's training," Ethan remarked, examining one capsule with appreciative eyes before tucking it away.
Rhys nodded in agreement, his expression brightening at their good fortune. "Perfect timing, too—I depleted my last reserves yesterday."
Zane remained silent, mentally calculating how these capsules might accelerate his ambitious training schedule. Every resource brought him one step closer to his ultimate goal of saving Elara.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
Just as they were stashing the capsules away, they spotted someone heading down the mountain path toward them. He was a big dude but surprisingly light on his feet. There was only one person like that on Misty Peak: Dustin Blackwood. He'd been laid up after a nasty fight in the battle ring a few days ago.
"Hey, Dustin!" the three of them called out.
Seeing them, Dustin broke into a wide grin and ambled over.
"Dustin, you all healed up?" Zane asked, a flicker of concern in his voice. He'd been cooped up training for three days straight since getting back from Infernal Peak and hadn't had a chance to check on him.
Dustin just waved a thick arm, brushing it off. "Just a scratch. All healed up now!"
Zane nodded. "Glad to hear it."
"What's the rush? It's not like they're gonna run out of capsules," Rhys asked, curious. "Got somewhere to be?"
"Yeah, I'm actually heading off-mountain for a bit," Dustin answered. "There's a big event at The Gilded Gavel today. My brother wants me to check it out."
"The Gilded Gavel?" Ethan and Rhys exchanged a look, their eyes widening in sudden realization.
"Wait a second," they asked in unison. "You're not from House Blackwood, are you?"
Zane just looked confused.
Dustin gave a casual nod.
[MUSIC: PRESTIGIOUS ORCHESTRAL]
House Blackwood was a legend in The Empire. The family wasn't known for producing powerful warriors, nor were they even a Great House. Instead, they wielded a different kind of power, one that put them on nearly equal footing with The Seven Great Houses.
Their secret? Every descendant of House Blackwood was a business genius. That was their edge. They controlled the majority of the economy in The Empire, overseeing deals in the largest trading posts and auction houses.
Even the trade of Crystal Cubes ran through them, giving them a unique and unshakable grip on the Empire's economy. And the renowned Gilded Gavel was just one of the many jewels in their crown, the venue for most of the realm's biggest and most legendary auctions.
"Whoa, hold on. You're a Blackwood?" Rhys exclaimed, shaking his head in disbelief. "Man, I never would've guessed! You're so chill about it!"
[SFX: EXCITED MURMURS]
"It's damn near impossible for a regular person to even get in the door at The Gilded Gavel," Ethan said, his voice buzzing with excitement. "Dustin, any chance you could get us in to have a look?" He was a total sucker for this kind of thing and wasn't about to miss an opportunity like this.
Dustin grinned. "No problem at all. Piece of cake."
Then he turned to Zane. "Zane, you wanna come with?"
Zane was about to say no, but Dustin was a step ahead of him. He figured an Outer Disciple wouldn't have anything worth auctioning, but the kid's potential was off the charts. It was always smart for House Blackwood to get on the good side of someone like Zane.
But Zane surprised him, a slow, sly smile spreading across his face. "Actually," he said, "I do have something to sell."
"Oh yeah? What is it?" Dustin asked, his curiosity piqued. He knew Zane wasn't one to bluff; if he said he had something valuable, it was the real deal.
"A Phoenix Gem," Zane replied, his voice low.
[MUSIC: TENSE REVELATION]
The words made Dustin's round face go rigid. Every member of House Blackwood had a nose for business, and Dustin was no exception. He'd grown up with a razor-sharp sense for the value of rare treasures.
When Zane said "Phoenix Gem," Dustin could hardly believe his ears.
The gem had incredible uses, highly sought after by alchemists and Master Weaponsmiths alike. Not only that, but the Scorpiomane—the beast it came from—was on the verge of extinction.
Dustin wracked his brain, recalling the last time one had been auctioned off. Three years ago. It had sold for nearly twenty thousand Crystal Cubes, and that one, if he remembered correctly, had come from a young Scorpiomane.
If Zane really had a Phoenix Gem to auction, Dustin would be bringing home a massive deal for his family.
"Zane," he said, his easy-going tone gone, "follow me."
Seeing how dead serious he was, Dustin pulled him away from the others toward a more secluded bend in the path. Once they were out of earshot, he turned back, his eyes intense. "Can I see it? The Phoenix Gem?"
Zane was almost amused by how intense Dustin had become, though he felt a little awkward, too. He knew the gem was a big deal in the empire, but Dustin's reaction was still a little over the top.
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
Zane tapped his Storage Ring. A moment later, the gem materialized in his hand, shimmering with a fiery inner glow. It was whole and about the size of a fist, its powerful aura leaving no doubt it had been taken from a fully-grown Scorpiomane.
