Despite his voracious appetite for literature chronicling the Empire's famed geographical wonders, these two locations remained complete enigmas to Zane. Not once had he encountered mention of them in any text he'd studied.
[SFX: PAGES FOLDING]
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
After contemplating the discovery, Zane meticulously refolded the delicate winter silk map and tucked it back within the pages of "Principles of Forging" before slipping the tome into his Storage Ring.
The mysterious treasure would need to wait its turn. His immediate focus demanded preparation for the impending demon-slaying expedition, a thought that sent a mixture of excitement and trepidation coursing through his veins.
Earlier that day, Zane had indeed pulverized a demon to dust, but circumstances had heavily favored him. The creature, bound in chains and neutralized by magical seals, posed no genuine threat—it couldn't retaliate. That test merely gauged courage rather than combat prowess.
A cold shiver ran down his spine as he considered what awaited beyond the mountain's protective boundaries: authentic demons, unrestrained and lethal. Their razor-sharp claws and insatiable bloodlust would show no mercy to the unprepared.
This sobering reality had driven Zane to dedicate every available moment to fortifying his physical capabilities. He pushed himself through grueling exercises until his muscles screamed in protest, honing his strength and building his endurance with relentless determination. Each drop of sweat represented another fraction of power that might mean the difference between survival and death in the coming confrontation.
[MUSIC: ANNOUNCEMENT FANFARE]
A few days later, Isla announced the five pledges chosen for the mission, and Zane’s name was on the list. He was joined by two other pledges, Henry and the third-ranked Casey. The remaining two spots went to students so advanced they’d already reached the High Class and were pledges in name only.
Isla Novak gave the chosen few a couple of days to get their affairs in order.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
When the day came, the five pledges and five members from Misty Peak gathered at the landing grounds. The entire area was a sea of people—a stark contrast of black and white, with Varsity members in their Shadow Cloaks on one side and pledges in their White Robes on the other.
Passing the demon test wasn't about being the strongest student from each peak. It was about being the bravest—the ones who could master their fear.
In the center of the landing grounds, one of the masters was laying out the rules of engagement. No one—pledge or Varsity—was to venture into The Crucible to face the greater demons alone. At their current level, it would be suicide.
Beyond The Crucible lay the Sky Forest, a woodland territory bordering the empire. It was a common entry point for demon scouts sent to gather intelligence. Their mission was simple: three hundred and thirty students from the Sentinel Peaks of the Skyreach Sect would enter the forest and eliminate those scouts.
[SFX: MASSIVE ENGINES HUMMING]
As the master concluded his instructions, an enormous Skycruiser descended from the clouds, casting its imposing shadow over the assembled crowd. The massive vessel's metal hull gleamed in the sunlight, its engines emitting a low, resonant hum that vibrated through the ground beneath their feet.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MOVEMENT]
[MUSIC: TENSE MILITARY MARCH]
"Time to move out," the master announced, stepping aside with a sweeping gesture. The disciples, a sea of black and white robes, began filing into the colossal Skycruiser with military precision, their faces masks of determination and barely concealed anxiety.
[SFX: COMMOTION IN CROWD]
Preston Sterling suddenly broke ranks, pushing through the orderly lines to thrust an accusatory finger directly at Zane. His eyes blazed with inherited fury as he snarled, "You have the nerve to insult my brother? Are you actively seeking your own demise?"
Zane met Preston's glare unflinchingly, a cold smile playing at the corners of his mouth. The temperature around him seemed to drop as he replied, "Seeking my demise? How amusing." He crossed his arms casually, though his muscles remained taut beneath his robes. "Let's be honest with each other. Would your family ever leave me in peace? Would your brother cease his relentless harassment? It appears I've been marked for death regardless of my actions, so why should I restrain my thoughts?" His voice carried an edge of bitter truth that caused several nearby disciples to exchange uncomfortable glances.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Preston's face flushed crimson, his mouth opening and closing as words abandoned him. The veins in his temples throbbed visibly as rage consumed his features, his body tensing like a coiled spring ready to launch himself at Zane right there on the Skycruiser's boarding ramp.
Zane took two deliberate steps forward, his previously neutral expression morphing into a contemptuous sneer. His eyes, cold as winter frost, locked onto Preston's. "You want a fight?" he challenged, voice dangerously soft yet carrying to everyone nearby. "I'm happy to oblige. Perhaps you'll fare better than your brother did."
[SFX: HANDS ON SHOULDERS]
Just then, two burly Varsity members materialized behind Preston, their muscled hands clamping down on his shoulders with surprising force. "Not in here," one of them hissed into Preston's ear, his voice low but firm. "Save it for the Sky Forest. Kill him there if you want, where no one will interfere."
With his companions restraining him, Preston's nostrils flared as he drew several ragged breaths. He shot Zane a look so venomous it might have poisoned a lesser man, his eyes promising retribution.
[SFX: ANGRY SNORT]
After a tense moment that seemed to stretch into eternity, he snorted derisively and wrenched himself free, stalking back to his seat with clenched fists and wounded pride.
Zane slowly reined in the Corrupt Vitality gathering in his core. He wasn't going to lie to himself—facing off against Preston, a powerful creature of nature, sent a jolt of pressure through him.
Though his exterior projected fearlessness, inwardly Zane remained poised on a knife's edge. At the faintest whisper of killing intent from Preston, he stood ready to strike back with devastating force, consequences to the Skycruiser be utterly damned.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
The power coursing through his body felt electric—over a hundred dragon scales now pulsed with activation beneath his skin, while the perfected Titan Strike technique hummed within his core. Never had such strength flowed through his veins, and a primal part of him hungered to discover the true extent of these newfound capabilities.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BUILDUP]
As he eased back into his seat, Zane's eyelids drifted shut, his senses absorbing these final precious moments of tranquility. The countdown to chaos had begun.
