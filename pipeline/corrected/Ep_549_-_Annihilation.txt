Zane knew that was the only reason he’d won.
But nobody else did.

[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Zane met her burning, hateful gaze. "We'll see about that," he said, his voice dangerously quiet.

Worse, it had happened on the combat platform, fair and square.
What in the hell was going on in the Heartlands?? How could a place like this produce someone like Zane? This shocking turn of events forced the guards to reconsider everything they thought they knew.
[MUSIC: DARK VENGEANCE]
For the Holy Maiden, only revenge mattered now.
Back home, her status ranked second only to the Crown Prince. In a divine kingdom where warrior-emperors lived for thousands of years and sired countless children, only the most exceptional princes could claim the crown. The rest vanished into obscurity.
But this prince had been different—he'd brought her here. Since childhood, the Holy Maiden had been worshipped, surrounded by reverence and solitude. She'd had no real friends except for him.
And Zane had killed him.

Six.

She stepped onto it without hesitation.
[SFX: DARK MAGICAL RESONANCE]
"You can annihilate anyone here."
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS CHOIR]
A ruthless smile spread across the Holy Maiden's face. Her eyes found Zane.
Tia's expression snapped into alarm.
Not far away, Maddox quietly exhaled. When Zane had beaten the Prince, he'd felt something he hadn't expected—a flicker of genuine fear. The kid's luck was stronger than he'd imagined. He needed to be dealt with while he was still young. And now, it seemed, someone else was going to do it for him.
The three leaders of the World Merchants' Guild exchanged a long, silent look. They shook their heads. It was a shame. Not only were they losing the sect-protecting array Zane had promised—they were losing the most valuable runemaster the Heartlands had ever produced. They'd been trying to bring him into their fold since the beginning.
What a waste.
Many other warriors shook their heads too.
Then Tia's eyes went cold.
[MUSIC: EERIE TENSION]

The Holy Maiden glanced at her with something like amusement. "You're the leader of a Fourth-Grade sect. What exactly are you going to use to hunt me down?"
"This."
Tia bit the tip of her tongue. She blew softly—and a hexagonal blood-colored snowflake formed in her palm.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
She patted it gently. It spun—and flew straight toward the Holy Maiden.
Everyone was separated by barriers in this abyss. No one could cross them. Attacks were blocked. That was the only reason the dice game hadn't dissolved into chaos long ago.
But the blood-colored snowflake passed through the barrier as if it wasn't there—and drilled silently into the Holy Maiden's body.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

That was a curse released at the cost of burning one's own life essence.
From this moment on, the Holy Maiden would be Tia's mark for the rest of her life. Tia would never stop hunting her. And both of them—hunter and hunted—would suffer. Every year, for a third of it, a cold poison would tear through their bodies. The pain was said to be unbearable.
[SFX: REALIZATION STING]

Zane didn't know the exact ritual, but he knew it must have cost Tia dearly.
