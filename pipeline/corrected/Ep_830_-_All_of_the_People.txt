Ep 830 - All of the People

Word count: 995

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

For thirty minutes, Zane and the Little Monster stood as still as statues, locked in a silent standoff. A battle of souls should’ve been over in a flash, not dragged on like this. The stillness felt wrong, thick with an unspoken question that grew heavier with every passing second.

The soulforge continued its steady spin. Finally, after what felt like an eternity, the lid lifted.

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

A stream of pure, multicolored energy flowed out, shimmering as it floated quietly within Zane’s mind.

“The will of this chaotic world…” Zane murmured, his eyes fixed on the brilliant light. “It’s so pure now.”

The Cobalt Dragon's voice echoed in his thoughts. “The Little Monster's soul has been refined. That's what's purified it — his nature had tainted the world's will. Now it's masterless. Zane, you can absorb it.”

Lavender gently pulled her hands from his, a weariness shadowing the smile she offered him. “Go on,” she urged.

Lost in the moment, Zane didn’t see the exhaustion weighing on her. He just nodded and moved toward the pulsating light.

[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]

He was still a few feet away when the will suddenly darted from him, as if trying to escape. Though not fully conscious, it sensed his intent and shied away, not wanting to be absorbed.

But it had nowhere to go. It was trapped inside his mind, a frantic, shimmering light with no escape.

Zane’s soul finally closed the distance. The moment he made contact, a tremor ran through him, and a tidal wave of information crashed into his consciousness, drowning him in a massive illusion.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

[SFX: REALITY SHIFT]

A whole world unfolded before him.

“Is this a projection of the entire world of chaos?”

[SFX: WHOOSHING VISIONS]

Images flooded his vision: countless mountains and rivers, boundless oceans teeming with trillions of creatures. Then came the faces, one after another, an endless stream of mortals, Adepts, and even powerful warriors from the Inner-Core, Spirit-Tribulation, and Soul Sea Realms.

“Are these all the people in this world?”

The faces came too fast to follow. Zane only recognized one. A brief flash — Olivia, from the Violet Heart Sanctuary. Then she was gone, swept away in the torrent. In a world of trillions, she was the only face he knew.

Then the humans gave way to everything else — every beast, every creature, every race across every forest, ocean, mountain, and secret realm in the world, passing before him in an instant and vanishing.

[SFX: DEEP RESONANCE]

When the last image faded, Zane became aware of something new sitting in the back of his mind. A vast, deep memory — not his own. The accumulated record of the world itself, from ancient times to the present.

I wonder if I can read it, he thought.

He tried. It opened like instinct.

[MUSIC: GENTLE RELIEF]

He searched for Tia. Found her immediately — lying quietly in Oracle City, still and pale but alive. The image was perfectly clear, close enough to touch. He let out a breath he hadn't known he was holding.

[SFX: SHIMMERING LIGHT]

Zane withdrew from the memory. As he did, he noticed his soul. The cracks were gone. Every one of them, sealed. And alongside the dark green of his soul, a new color had taken root — multicolored, shimmering faintly.

He had absorbed the will of the world. The process was complete.

His eyes moved to Lavender.

Her soul was still covered in cracks. She looked dim, her fiery red eyes fading at the edges. While absorbing the world's will had repaired Zane's soul, it had done nothing for hers.

“Don't worry,” she said, catching his look. She smiled faintly. “I just need to rest.”

“Lavender—”

“By the time you restore her soul through ordinary means,” the Cobalt Dragon said quietly, “it will already be gone.”

Zane's chest tightened. “What do you mean?”

“Her soul was a remnant before any of this happened. The Holy Tree gave it strength, but it can't self-repair. It's on the verge of disintegration. A day. Maybe two.”

Lavender didn't argue. She knew the state she was in. She still had enemies in the Demon Night — unfinished business, debts she hadn't collected. It was too early for her soul to disappear. But whether it disappeared wasn't up to her.

Her smile stayed, but it didn't quite reach her eyes.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]

The Cobalt Dragon's voice cut in. “You'll need Soulcradle Wood to create a new vessel for her soul, and then you'll have to slowly nurture it.”

Soulcradle Wood? Zane’s jaw tightened. The name meant nothing to him. How was he supposed to find something he’d never even heard of?

“It’s a treasure from the upper world,” Lavender said, her voice laced with discouragement. “It makes sense you haven’t heard of it.” She clearly didn’t hold out much hope of finding it in their Lower World.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]

The upper world.

A spark ignited in Zane’s eyes. “I can get to the upper world to search for it!” He knew a way. The Dragon Soar Arena in Oracle City—it was a gateway. After changing hands from the Ethereal Spirit Sect to the Corrupted Divine Kingdom and then the World Merchants’ Guild, it had finally ended up back under Oracle City’s control. He was sure he could find a way to ascend.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS CELLO]

“I’m afraid we don’t have that kind of time,” the Cobalt Dragon said bluntly. “Look at her. She probably won’t last more than a day or two.”

A day or two. The words hit him like a physical blow.

He was trapped. Even if he abandoned his search in the Sacred Feather Sea right now, he couldn’t make it back to the Heartlands in two days. But if he left, he’d miss his chance to get the Ancient Heartstones for a whole year—far longer than Tia could possibly wait.

A knot of pure panic tightened in his gut. What was he going to do?

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHIMES]

Then it hit him. The memories. The storm of information he’d just absorbed from the world’s will. Could the answer be in there? 

Could he find the Soulcradle Wood through them?
