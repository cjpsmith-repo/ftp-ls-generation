Ep 1073 - Building Sword Intent

Word count: 1170

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: INTENSE MEDITATION]

A single drop of sweat traced a path down Zane’s forehead. He was close.

After a long, grueling process, he’d just about perfected his sword intent. Cyclones of pure energy, a sight he knew well, spun around him in endless streams. It felt like he’d finally hit a ceiling, that there was nowhere left for his power to grow.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

But then, just as he felt on the verge of a breakthrough, it all went sideways. The intent went wild, suddenly and shockingly unstable.

[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC POWER]

Before, he could unleash it with a thought, as if it were a part of him. Now, it was fighting him, bucking against his will even as the aura around his sword flared with more power than ever before.

The neat whirlwind of energy dissolved, the intent coiling around his blade like a striking snake, jumping and lashing out. He was stronger, but his control had gone to hell.

Zane’s brow furrowed. “What is going on?” he muttered, the question swallowed by the hum of raw power. Sharpening his intent against the onslaught of white sword shadows had certainly made his sword spirit stronger, but now it was too wild to manage.

He hesitated, just for a second. Should he keep going?

[MUSIC: DETERMINED DRUMS]

[SFX: POWER SURGE]

His gaze fell on the dissipating forms of the white shadows he’d just cut down, and his jaw set.

Giving up wasn’t in his nature. With a grunt, he wrestled the impossibly strong intent back under his command, forcing it into submission.

[SFX: RAPID SWORD STRIKES]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT BATTLE]

As the energy grew even sharper under his forceful control, he found he could cut down the white shadows faster than ever.

It was progress. Where it once took seven strikes to shatter one, he could now do it in four. He faced down a torrent of hundreds of sword shadows and, just as he’d thought, tore through them in four clean, consecutive strikes.

[SFX: SWORD INTENT RELEASE]

Then, in a moment of carelessness, it got away from him. The intent on his blade shot out on its own — he hadn’t willed it, hadn’t aimed it — and tore through dozens of shadows before burying itself in the desert sand nearby, leaving a small crack in the ground.

Without intent on his blade, he couldn’t hold back the torrent. The shadows overwhelmed him before he could condense a new one.

He went down again. And got back up again.

[MUSIC: GRINDING REPETITION]

Two more hours. Three strikes now.

Another two after that. Two strikes.

Then six hours in, something shifted. The shadows came in their usual flood — and he didn’t faint. His blade was moving faster than thought, the sword flowers he formed filling every angle, and no matter how many came at him, he shattered them all. Every single one.

But the intent was completely beyond his control now. He could hold it for three breaths — no more. After three breaths, it exploded outward on its own, firing in whatever direction it pleased. He had to slash hundreds of times in those three breaths, then instantly recondense. Slash. Explode. Recondense. Over and over.

[SFX: RAPID CYCLING ENERGY]

The Chaos Energy in his body burned through at a staggering rate. If he’d still been running on ordinary Vitality, he’d have needed a thousand supreme Aether crystals to sustain this. But Chaos Energy was different — efficient, concentrated. A small amount went a long way. He had just enough.

[MUSIC: EERIE STILLNESS]

Then, the rock went quiet.

Zane held his blade ready, waiting for the next wave. It didn’t come. He stared at the carved characters. The shadows had stopped.

“Are there no more?” he murmured, looking at the poem. He had no idea how many he’d shattered — the number was impossible to calculate. What he didn’t know was that Supreme Lord Sword had originally sealed nine million, nine hundred and ninety thousand shadows into those words. Over millions of years, the occasional warrior had accidentally broken one or two without knowing it. Now, after hours of Zane’s relentless assault, the last one was gone.

[MUSIC: PHILOSOPHICAL PAUSE]

He turned the thought over. Supreme Lord Sword had clearly designed this — the poem, the shadows, all of it — to temper sword intent. But what kind of sword intent had he been trying to create? One this volatile, this impossible to control? Zane didn’t have an answer.

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR TRAINING GROUND]

To the side, Landry and Damian hadn’t moved in hours. They stood like statues, eyes fixed on Zane’s sword practice, expressions blank. They couldn’t see what was happening — but they could feel it. Something was building. Something was about to break open. They weren’t going anywhere.

Just as he was steeling himself for another round, a voice cut through his concentration.

[SFX: DISTANT VOICE CALLING]

“Zane, are you still staring at that rock?” Yolande called from a distance. “The Passing the Torch ceremony is about to start.”

He paused, the energy around his blade quieting. “Just a little longer?” he called back.

“Sure, but the first round of the test starts with the ceremony,” she warned. “You need to get over here, or you’ll be disqualified.”

The disciples of the Thirteen Palaces were all required to take the test together. Being late wasn’t an option.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]

As Zane considered this, he noticed a change in Lavender.

She had always been an illusion, a beautiful mirage that looked human but wasn’t. You could see the faint shimmer of a reflection in water, how light passed right through her.

Not anymore. If she hadn’t told you she was a sword spirit, you’d never know. She looked completely, utterly real. From the way her dark hair fell around her shoulders to the soft texture of her snow-white skin, she seemed solid.

She touched her own arm with a look of wonder. “I feel… solid,” she said, a faint smile on her lips. “Like my body is actually becoming real. And my power… it’s so much stronger now.”

Before, without a physical form, she’d always liked to rely on Zane. She’d never been all that motivated to find a body of her own. Now, seeing this change, she knew she’d made the right choice in coming with him.

Zane glanced over at her. He caught sight of the glossy sheen on her hair, the fair, smooth skin of her face, and for a second, it actually made his breath catch. But he quickly forced his attention back to the enormous stone.

Whatever secret Supreme Lord Sword had left behind, it wasn’t going to give itself up easily. He’d spent hours strengthening his sword spirit, which was a huge success on its own. Unlocking the final mystery would have to come down to luck, and he wasn’t about to push his.

The ceremony was starting. Zane didn’t waste another second.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

He launched himself into the air, flying away from the side of the massive stone.

[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Not long after he was gone, Landry and Damian, the two Spirit Supreme Realm warriors, were still staring, mesmerized, at the giant characters carved into the stone.

Then, a faint sound, like stone giving way under immense pressure, broke the silence.

[SFX: STONE CRACKING]

A crack spiderwebbed across the surface of one of the massive words.
