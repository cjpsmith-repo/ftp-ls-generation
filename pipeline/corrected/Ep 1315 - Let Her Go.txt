Ep 1315 - Let Her Go

Word count: 1052

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
After exploring the secret land, Slade had obtained three Exemption Medallions. Combined with the Saint-Killing Spear’s power, he should have been unstoppable. By sacrificing a piece of himself to the spear, he could eliminate a saint-level genius - let alone mere Ace Talents.
That strategy worked in the wilderness, far from the main city, where he could have killed Zane without spending any Illusion Points.
Instead, Zane had humiliated him, and Slade snapped.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
He should have lured Zane into the wild. But arrogance made him believe he could win with spear skills alone. When Zane forced him onto the defensive, that arrogance curdled into desperation - pure, unadulterated rage.
A stupid plan. He stood practically on the city’s doorstep. Even with the sacrifice’s massive power boost, killing Zane here was impossible.
Zane hadn’t just defeated him; he’d seized the Saint-Killing Spear itself.
Slade was radiating pure fury. He needed to make someone hurt, and his eyes landed on Hailey, standing a short distance away.
He barked her name.
A tremor ran through Hailey when she heard the command.
“Don’t go over there,” Janet whispered, her voice tight with fear.
But Hailey was frozen, trapped between a desperate hope and a lifetime of terror.
“You wench!” Slade yelled, his face twisting into a vicious sneer. “Don’t you forget where you are right now.”
She was still in his palace. He might not be able to touch her here in the Illusion Battlefield, but the moment she left, she was nothing more than his plaything again.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
Hailey pressed her lips together, her eyes filling with a deep, hopeless sadness. She didn’t have to say a word. Coleman and Janet could see it on her face: she had no other choice.
[SFX: SLOW FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
She began the long, slow walk toward him.
The wicked smile on Slade’s face grew wider. This whole disaster with Zane had started because of her. He’d lost, sure, but Zane couldn’t do anything else to him now. This, at least, he could control.
Just as Hailey reached the halfway point, Zane stepped between them again, his gaze calm as he faced Slade.
“What do you want?” Slade demanded, his voice dripping with ridicule.
“Let her go,” Zane said softly.
Slade let out a sly, mocking laugh. “No problem. First, give me back my spear.”
Zane hesitated. He’d actually been planning to make a copy of the Saint-Killing Spear before returning it.
Seeing the flicker of indecision, Slade couldn’t help but laugh again, this time with a note of triumph.
It only sharpened his cruelty. “Hahaha… Get over here,” he called to Hailey, “and give everyone a proper show. Dance for us.”
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
A low, ugly murmur of excitement rippled through the crowd. The women of the Demon Night were famously proud, untouchable—it was rare for anyone to see one of them humiliated like this.
“Slade!” Hailey’s voice cracked with fury.
“Remember what you are,” he cut her off, smiling. “Not my woman. My plaything. And it isn’t just you I hold—your family answers to me too. Every one of them. You don’t get to say no. Not here, not anywhere.”
[MUSIC: SORROWFUL PIANO]
Hailey’s hands curled into fists at her sides. She turned to Zane instead, her voice dropping soft and unsteady. “Will you take my Queen home? Back to the Demon Night?”
Zane went still. He’d pieced together fragments of the story from Coleman and Janet, but never the whole of it.
“I’m only twenty-eight,” Hailey went on, “in true years. I still have time to become something more than this, an Ace Talent in my own right. I don’t want to spend what’s left of it as his toy. My sect has waited years for her return, enduring things I can’t even say out loud. I always hoped it would come soon enough. I don’t think it will. Not soon enough for me.”
Tears slid freely down her face now.
Something in Zane’s chest ached at the sight of her. He reached out and wiped the tears from her cheek. “I will,” he said simply.
Those two words were enough. Hailey went quiet, rubbed her eyes clear, and turned back toward Slade with a small, resolute smile.
“Slade,” she said, her voice clear and carrying, “I’ve always wanted to tell you—if it weren’t for your father’s name, your skill with that spear would be nothing to speak of. And in bed? Even less.”
The other warriors in the crowd exchanged confused glances. Slade was an Ace Talent, a powerful warrior at the Soul Sea Realm. What possible reason could a woman have for rejecting him?
Coleman clasped his hands, his expression serene, but the corners of his mouth twitched into a small, satisfied smile. Ansel, on the other hand, wasn’t nearly as subtle.
A grating laugh rumbled up from his chest.
[MUSIC: TENSE THREATENING STRINGS]
Slade’s face went stiff. He stared at Hailey, his gaze so cold and sharp it felt like it could tear her apart.
But then, to everyone’s surprise, he laughed.
“Ha ha ha! I didn’t realize you’d gotten so bold since entering the Illusion Battlefield. Very good. Just outstanding. Do you have any idea what this little stunt is going to cost you?”
“Death,” she said. Her voice was steady, confident. Zane’s promise had given her all the assurance she needed. Besides, she was so tired of living as Slade’s slave.
“No,” Slade said, shaking his head slowly. “Why would I let you die that easily? You, of all people, should know about the worst punishment the Demon Night has to offer.”
Hailey’s face was a mask of indifference. “The Punishment of Eternal Night.”
“That’s right,” Slade roared, his excitement swelling as he finally found an outlet for his rage. “I’m going to throw you and your entire family into the Punishment of Eternal Night! I want you to suffer in a way you will never, ever forget!”
[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE MELANCHOLY]
Zane let out a quiet sigh. None of this would be happening if he were just strong enough. The faster he gained more power, the sooner he could get back to the Demon Night and bring Lavender home.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRAMATIC SWELL]
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL BATTLEFIELD RESONANCE]
Just then, a booming voice echoed across the battlefield, cutting through the tension. “The rules of the Illusion Battlefield have changed. Battle restrictions within the sanctuaries are now lifted. The restriction on flying is also lifted.”
