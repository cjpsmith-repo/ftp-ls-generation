Even in the higher realms, a Transcendent Divine Might was the stuff of legends. Only Supreme Lords could forge one.
Most attunement methods drew on the fundamental rules of the universe—visualization techniques born from observing the world, or entirely new methods built from a deep enough comprehension of the Laws. But those were all built on weaker laws. A Transcendent Divine Might was different. It tapped into the most powerful law of all: Causality.
[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]
Once Causality entered the picture, things got interesting. The black star pickax Zane had carried held only a sliver of that law—not even a form suited for combat—and it had still ranked in the top three thousand on the Cosmic Spirit Index.
But weapons had limits; a Transcendent Divine Might required a warrior to master Causality themselves. Living beings could grow without limit—mastering it was like getting an all-access pass to the highest rules of the universe. A Divine-Class weapon, by contrast, was fixed the moment it was forged.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]
By some pure, dumb luck, the Lord of the Sunset World had mastered a Transcendent Divine Might once. That single achievement had allowed him to take a massive leap forward and establish his own grand world. But he was an outlier. Even here today, among all the leaders and vice leaders of the Thirteen Palaces, only Yolande possessed one. As a descendant of a Supreme Lord, she’d been given a chance to learn it, and she’d been good enough to take it.
Of course, learning one was considered brutally difficult. Each was unique, harder to master than the last. The Soul Split Spell Yolande knew had simply paved the way for her to even begin.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STRINGS]
As for Zane, he’d poured all his time into the Stellar Body ever since he’d learned of it. His progress was on a trajectory to be incredible, but that was all in the future. Right now, he was still young, only at the third level of the technique. And the version he had was incomplete.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Even so, when he activated the Stellar Body and drew on the starlight, he left more than a few of the palace leaders stunned.
[AMBIENT: REVERENT WHISPERS]
“You all know the stories,” one of the leaders murmured, shaking his head in awe. “Supreme Lord Star spent his entire life just understanding the stars. The histories say that when he drew upon the starlight from every corner of the sky, he forged it into swords and crossed ten thousand worlds just to kill Supreme Lord Lyra. What a legacy to live up to.”
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Inside the crucible, starlight layered over Zane’s body, the gathered power of the stars growing denser by the second. The Stellar Body didn’t work like other techniques—the more stars it pulled in, the stronger it became, climbing without any clear ceiling.
Across from him, Lincoln’s expression hardened by degrees. He’d never imagined he’d end up locked in a dead-even fight with someone a full level below him. No more hesitation. Golden light surged out and wrapped around his entire body, and the pale green stone on his forehead pulsed with light in steady, urgent flashes.
[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]
The two of them rushed each other again, faster this time, closing the distance across the platform in a blur.
[SFX: BOOM]
The collision threw off a flash so bright it swallowed the whole platform—the golden light folding in on itself, the gathered starlight shuddering and breaking apart.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
A few low, muffled impacts sounded from within the light. Then a figure came flying out of it—Zane—and hit the surging clouds below the platform. 
Eliminated.
In the center of the platform, Lincoln stood with hairline cracks running across his arms and chest, his sword-straight brows drawn down in a thoughtful, faraway look.
[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]
Inside the crucible, Zane’s eyes opened. A flicker of something tired and helpless crossed his face before he pushed himself up and walked out on his own.
Passing the trial had never mattered much to him—it was just a challenge, one stage among many he’d have the chance to prove himself on later. What had actually moved him was the look on Layla’s face right before the end: that same sad, lost expression he’d once seen on Elara. He hadn’t planned it. Something in him had just shifted, and he’d let the match go.
Outside the crucible, the man in cotton clothes floating in the air had a strange look on his face. He wasn’t the only one. The leaders of the Thirteen Palaces, including Yolande and the Lord of the Sunset World, all looked puzzled. They had all felt it.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
They all came to the same, unbelievable conclusion: Zane had thrown the match. On purpose.
The only reason they could think of was that he’d done it to help Layla. And they were supposed to be competitors—rivals, even. She had provoked him time and time again.
[AMBIENT: COUNCIL CHAMBER]
“I never would have figured that kid Zane to be such a good guy,” one of the leaders remarked with a shake of his head. “You know, in our world, where it’s survival of the fittest, kindness isn’t exactly a virtue.”
[MUSIC: WISE CONTEMPLATION]
The Lord of the Sunset World just chuckled. “For a weak warrior, kindness is a fatal flaw. But for the truly powerful? It’s mercy, and you don’t take that lightly.”
In a chaotic universe where the strong prey on the weak, every race and every creature lives on the brink. For someone without enough power, being kind was just asking for trouble. 
But when a person was truly powerful, it didn’t matter if they compromised or gave in sometimes. Their foundation was so unshakeable that the worst that could happen was a temporary, self-imposed defeat.
Zane wasn’t at that level yet. 
[MUSIC: EPIC FORESHADOWING]
But in the eyes of the Lord of the Sunset World, he was going to get there a hell of a lot sooner than anyone thought.
