Ep 1283 - No Sense Wasting Points

Word count: 1016

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING STONE HALL]
[SFX: CHAIN HOOK WHIPPING]
Zane cast his long hook, snagging Coleman and yanking him to safety just as he charged into the darkness. The moment Coleman’s feet touched down, he turned and pulled the rest of their allies—Janet, Hailey, Ansel, and the others—to their side.
[SFX: DEEP THUD]
The last of them had barely cleared the edge when a dull *thump* echoed from the gloom. 
[SFX: MAGICAL ORB EXPLOSION]
[SFX: ENERGY WHOOSH]
A bright orb—pure Illusion Points—exploded from the shadows and shot straight for Zane.
The fighters who’d just made it into the hall had all collected a ton of points, and in that single burst, Zane absorbed over a hundred thousand more.
There was no way the others were going to let Zane get too far ahead, but for now, they had bigger problems.
Larkin was already dead, sent back to the main city to respawn. Losing their leader had shattered the morale of the remaining seven fighters. They weren’t weak, but their confidence was shot. They had no fight left in them, and Zane knew they were no longer a threat.
Janet, Hailey, and Coleman were no slouches, either.
Coleman’s Blade of Hallowed Censure lit up, and he cut down the fighter who’d been manipulating the Dark Law. The oppressive darkness vanished instantly.
[SFX: MAGICAL POINTS RAINING]
[SFX: BLADE IGNITING]
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
After Janet absorbed the points from the final kill, everyone’s eyes were fixed on Zane again.
For a second, it almost felt like the Third Hall of Causality wasn’t so tough after all. Janet and the others had barely lifted a finger; only Hailey had really needed to help Zane out. But they all knew better. The hall was deadly. If Zane hadn’t dragged Larkin down into that river of energy, their entire group would have been wiped out. They’d only made it through because of him.
“Why’s everyone staring at me?” Zane asked, his gaze drifting toward the hall’s entrance where Larkin’s group had first appeared.
Janet just pursed her lips, a small smile playing on her face. “That guy doesn’t seem like the type to take a loss lying down.”
“Who?” Zane asked, playing dumb.
[SFX: RUSHING ENERGY RIVER]
Janet just pointed toward the deep ravine and the river of energy flowing through it.
“I’ve already pissed off Sloane,” Zane said with a shrug. “I don’t mind adding one more to the list. Besides, those points are just sitting there, wasted,” he added, eyes fixed on the cluster of Illusion Points still floating above the river.
Janet, Coleman, and the others followed his gaze, the same regret creeping over their faces.
Then Hailey stepped up beside him, hook already in hand. “Go down. I’ll pull you back up.”
“Hm?” Zane looked at her.
“Trust me. I won’t let you fall in.”
[SFX: HOOK COILING]
The mechanics were simple enough to explain and brutal to execute: the points hovered just a foot above the river’s surface, ready for the taking, but the hook had to catch him at the exact right instant. Too early, and he’d miss the points entirely. Too late, and he’d be corroded before the hook ever closed around him. There was no room for error either way.
“I can hit a flying butterfly from ten miles out,” Hailey said. “There’s no mistake in me.”
“We could throw hooks too,” Janet offered.
Coleman shook his head. “Better if it’s just her. Two hooks colliding out there, and the Laws in them cancel each other out. We’d only get in the way.” No one wanted to risk Zane over pride—he’d become the one they were counting on to get them through whatever came next.
Zane glanced at the river, then back at Hailey. He was already sitting on more than ten million Illusion Points. Whatever waited in that current was worth the risk.
He nodded, walked to the edge, and didn’t hesitate. “I’m jumping.”
[SFX: BODY LEAPING]
He dropped straight toward the glittering cluster of points.
[SFX: POINTS ABSORBING]
They drilled into him the instant he passed through them—but the fall didn’t stop. He was less than a foot from the river when the hook finally caught him.
[SFX: HOOK CATCHING AND PULLING TAUT]
The sudden stop snapped his body forward, and for a heartbeat the tip of his nose hovered mere inches above the surface. Any closer, and the current would have eaten him alive—fire he could shrug off, but this, he couldn’t.
[SFX: RAPID ASCENT]
Hailey hauled him back up and onto solid ground in seconds. The others watching could only stare, more than a little envious of the fortune he’d just risked everything for.
“Thank you,” Zane said once his feet were steady.
Hailey just gave a small nod. “No need. You earned it.”
[SFX: DIGITAL RANKING UPDATE CHIME]
And after collecting Larkin’s Illusion Points, Zane’s ranking had shot up yet again.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
Warriors all across the battlefield were finding iron tickets in hidden mystic places, granting them entry into the hall. At this point in the game, the leaderboards were in complete chaos. 
The cost of entry alone—five million Illusion Points—was staggering. Even top-ranked fighters would see their standing plummet from a loss of a few tens of thousands, let alone millions. When Zane paid the fee, his name had dropped clean off the leaderboards.
[SFX: DIGITAL RANKING SURGE]
But clearing the Second Hall of Causality had sent his rank screaming back up the charts. Now, after absorbing another ten million points in here, he’d landed at twenty-third in this supreme world.
[SFX: LEADERBOARD CHIME]
Across the entire Illusion Battlefield, he’d leaped in rank all the way to one hundred thirty. The top one hundred spots were almost exclusively held by Ace Talents—the absolute peak of the pyramid, reserved for the most exceptional beings in the universe.
Zane was officially on the radar of the game’s upper echelon. They’d been watching him ever since his rank was still in the millions, but this was different.
[AMBIENT: SPECTATOR MURMURS]
“Zane is definitely not weak.”
“So much for him just relying on his Elysian Keep. His own strength is on par with an Ace Talent!”
“If this guy pushes a little harder, he could crack the Vanguard One Hundred!”
Not long ago, he’d just broken into the top ten thousand. Now, he was sitting at one hundred thirty. 
At this rate, cracking the top one hundred felt inevitable.
