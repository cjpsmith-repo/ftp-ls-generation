Ep 537 - A Six-Sided Die
Word count: 1246
[AMBIENT: DEEP CAVERN ECHO]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
The abyss opened up below like a colossal chimney, its walls dotted with stone platforms all set at the same level. Crowds were already gathering on them.
[SFX: BOOMING VOICE REVERB]
The powerful voice boomed from the platform right next to Zane's.
[MUSIC: TENSION RISING STRINGS]
Zane’s eyebrows shot up. He knew that voice. It was Maddox.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]
The Cloud Hall disciples tensed at the sight of him. It was one thing to be in a standoff with Maddox from the safety of their Order's Trinity Killing Array. But out here? If it came to a fight, they wouldn't stand a chance.
A cold laugh escaped Zane. "What an honor," he sneered, his voice dripping with sarcasm.
[SFX: WHOOSH OF ARRIVAL]
Elsewhere, the three heads of the World Merchants’ Guild appeared on another platform, along with their ancient tycoon and a host of elders. Zane spotted the old man among them.
He was a warrior who’d grown old before he could break through to the next realm. Men like him often chased miracles in their final years, hoping for one last breakthrough to extend their lives. A single gamble like this could add centuries to their lifespan. The worst that could happen was death, but staying home meant a slow, inevitable death anyway—a fate any true warrior would rather spit in the face of.
"Kieran, Hobbes, Burns," Tia said, nodding to the three leaders of the World Merchants' Guild.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL RELIEF]
Relief washed over the Cloud Hall disciples. The Guild was on their side, and with the tycoon present, their combined strength might be enough to keep Maddox in check.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL GATHERING]
[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]
Moments later, more factions arrived, the leaders and elders of Bloodwood Cliff and the Black Mountain Order appearing on their own platforms.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
But one platform stood out. It was occupied by a single, middle-aged warrior who also radiated the power of the Life-and-Death Realm.
There weren't many warriors of that caliber in the Heartlands, and no one recognized this man. Everyone stared, trying to gauge his strength.
Even Maddox was taken aback; he could sense the man's power was at least on par with his own.
[SFX: ECHOING VOICE]
"Friend," he called out, his voice echoing. "I don't recognize you. What corner of the Heartlands do you hail from?"
When the warrior had landed, he'd found himself trapped by an unbreakable barrier. He’d simply sat cross-legged and closed his eyes to rest.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Now, hearing Maddox, he opened them, a sneer twisting his lips. "Heh. So you're Maddox."
"Eight thousand miles I ran," he said coldly. "Four million warriors chasing me, day and night. I wonder if Maddox remembers. If the Ethereal Spirit Sect remembers. If Cloud Hall remembers. If the World Merchants' Guild remembers."
[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]
"The Poison King," Tia murmured. "Simon. Spirit-Tribulation level when it started—but he'd obtained the Five Poison Scripture. Every major faction wanted it. The World Merchants' Guild offered a million top-grade Spirit Stones just for his location. They chased him eight thousand miles across the Heartlands. He disappeared into the Dreamy Swampland decades ago."
"And you didn't participate?" Zane asked.
"I was young then."
Maddox smiled slowly. "I didn't expect you'd see through life and death. Even while hunted, you showed extraordinary resourcefulness. Impressive."
Simon's expression hardened. "Impressive? I was driven into the Dreamy Swampland. I spent decades living like an animal—not quite human, not quite ghost."
His voice dropped. "When I take what belongs to Vesper, I will return. I'll wash the Heartlands clean. Every Fourth and Fifth Grade Order—gone. I'll build something new on the ruins."
The platforms went quiet. Maddox chuckled and looked away—Simon's rage targeted all the major factions equally.
But the others weren't relaxed. Cloud Hall disciples, Guild elders, Ethereal Spirit Sect members—all wore tight, guarded expressions. Simon's appearance was not good news.
Then the final platform filled.
The woman in black and the handsome man from the Divine Kingdom stepped out and glanced around the abyss with mild, disinterested eyes. The woman's gaze swept across the assembled warriors and settled nowhere in particular.
"So these are the martial artists of the Heartlands," she said flatly.
Maddox frowned. "You came from the Divine Kingdom?"
His question drew every eye. A Divine Kingdom lamp. That meant one of the nine had been in the Divine Kingdom all along.
"Ant," the woman said, not looking at him, "you don't have the right to ask me questions."
The vein in Maddox's neck jumped. "Say that again."
The handsome man smiled pleasantly. "My little sister called you an ant. Personally, I think that's generous. You should feel honored."
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE AURA IMPACT]
Maddox's aura exploded outward — and slammed straight into the emerald barrier at the edge of his platform. The barrier didn't move. He was contained, and everyone knew it, and that made it worse.
He had endured too much. At the Martial Tournament, Zane had killed his ally right in front of him. Then Cloud Hall had hidden behind their array and laughed at him. And now two Spirit-Tribulation juniors from some distant kingdom were calling him an ant.
"Aren't you afraid of death?" he said quietly.
The woman in black looked at him for the first time. "Warriors from the Heartlands?" A thin smile crossed her lips. "I don't think you have the qualifications to threaten us."
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
The mood across every platform darkened. It wasn't just Maddox she was insulting — it was all of them. Every faction, every elder, every Life-and-Death master present. The Central Domain dismissed as a backwater by two young warriors who hadn't even reached the Life-and-Death Realm themselves.
Zane looked them over. Early Spirit-Tribulation for the woman. Mid Spirit-Tribulation for the man. Young bone age — prodigies, without question. But in terms of raw power, neither of them could match any of the Fourth-Grade Order leaders standing around this abyss.
Where did they get off?
[SFX: SUDDEN MATERIALIZATION]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
Just as the woman finished speaking, several warriors in black materialized behind them. Every single one of them had reached the Life-and-Death Realm.
Zane shook his head. "No wonder they're so confident," he muttered. "They've got a whole squad of Life-and-Death masters backing them up."
The warriors in black stood silent and still, making it clear that the young man and woman were in charge.
Even Maddox looked wary of this new force.
The eight factions stood on their platforms, ringing the abyss. All eyes eventually drifted to the last remaining area, which was still empty.
What no one knew was that the final platform was meant for House Ashford of Salt City—a house that no longer existed, already wiped out by the animated corpses they were all here to face.
Whispers passed between platforms, Vitality carrying hushed theories and questions. What was Vesper playing at? What was the purpose of this bizarre setup?
[AMBIENT: DEEP CAVERN ECHO]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Just as the tension reached its peak, a ball of pure light bloomed in the center of the abyss.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION WHOOSH]
It warped and pulsed like a drop of mercury before snapping into the shape of a perfect cube. As it stabilized, milky-white and blood-red spots materialized on its surface.
The light finally settled into the form of a die—a simple, six-sided die, the kind you’d find in any back-alley gambling den.
