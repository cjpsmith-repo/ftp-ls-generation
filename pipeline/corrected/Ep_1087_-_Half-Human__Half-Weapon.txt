Ep 1087 - Half-Human, Half-Weapon

Word count: 1162

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CONTEMPLATIVE]

Gathering the Celestial Essence was easy for Zane now.

It used to be a struggle, held back by the limitations of his own Spirit Weapon body, but that was no longer an issue. His physical body had become a Divine-Class Weapon in its own right. He had no idea what grade it was, but he knew for a fact it could hold the power of more than ten thousand Dragon Scales.

A thought struck him, and he tapped the side of his head. If his body kept getting stronger, could it eventually become a top-tier Divine-Class weapon?

I’ve heard the Cosmic Spirit Index is pretty special, he mused. The Law of Causality it contains is useless in a fight, but it can detect every magic weapon in the universe.

If his body became that powerful, would his name pop up on the Cosmic Spirit Index?

[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]

As cool as that sounded, Zane had a feeling it wouldn’t be a good thing. The last thing he wanted was for anyone to find out about his unique body, so he voiced his concern.

Both Lavender and the Cobalt Dragon agreed immediately. It was definitely possible.

His body might look like flesh and blood, but the soulforge had fundamentally changed its essence. In a lot of ways, it now had the characteristics of a weapon.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL REVELATION]

He was half-human, half-weapon—something unheard of in the entire universe. While they believed it was possible, neither Lavender nor the Cobalt Dragon could be one hundred percent certain his name would actually appear on the Spirit Index.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WATERFALL]

[SFX: CRACKLING LIGHTNING]

When Zane finally stopped in front of the lightning waterfall, the Lord of the Sunset World, Yolande, and the other palace leaders all braced themselves, assuming he was about to try to cross it.

Even Yolande was on edge. If Zane was foolish enough to actually go into that waterfall, she’d have to go in and pull him out herself.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

But after reaching a distance of exactly thirty-six thousand miles, Zane just… stopped. He paused for a moment, then vanished, reappearing at the entrance of the Enchanted Barrier Tower.

The Profound Flame had tempered his body and leveled him up again, but it had also charred his clothes to a crisp. He pulled out a spare set of white robes and changed right there.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]

Against the soot and grime of the tower, he looked revitalized, almost otherworldly.

Most of the warriors who’d returned from the Enchanted Barrier Tower were either nursing injuries or covered in filth. Zane had traveled thirty-six thousand miles—twice as far as Supreme Lord Illusion and eight thousand miles farther than Layla.

Yet here he was, looking perfectly fine, like he’d just come back from a casual vacation. The sight left most of them completely speechless.

“Zane,” Yolande’s voice carried down from above. “Come on up.”

Zane kicked off the ground and flew up to the observation platform. His eyes found Layla’s first.

She looked at him for a moment, then smiled — genuinely, without any trace of the tension from before. “Congratulations,” she said.

Zane gave a small nod.

She held his gaze a beat longer than necessary. Congratulations, she thought. But this isn’t over.

Yolande was next. She smiled faintly, something warm in her expression that she rarely let show. “I don’t think anyone will match that result for a very long time. You didn’t disappoint me.” A pause. “Congratulations.”

“Thank you,” Zane said.

[MUSIC: CEREMONIAL STRINGS]

Yolande then turned to introduce the Lord of the Sunset World. “This is the Lord of the Sunset World. He made two promises — a reward for reaching twenty thousand miles, and another for reaching thirty thousand.”

Zane blinked. He hadn’t known about any rewards beyond the ten-thousand-mile mark. He’d pushed to thirty-six thousand for his own reasons, not for prizes. But if a reward was on offer — especially from someone of the Lord’s standing — he wasn’t about to turn it down.

The Lord of the Sunset World looked him up and down with an amused expression. “Young man, the thirty-thousand-mile reward was essentially Yolande’s doing — she talked me into it. But I made the promise, and I keep my word. Let’s start with the twenty-thousand-mile reward. What do you want?”

Zane didn’t hesitate. “A spear.”

Layla’s head turned. “A spear?” she asked. “What do you need a spear for? Don’t you use a sword?”

Zane didn’t answer her directly. The spear was for Lavender. Her soul had recovered more than half, but she had no weapon, and the Demon Night’s warriors used spears. He’d tried to get her one at the Hall of Arms, but he hadn’t had enough Sovereign Stones.

Yolande understood without being told. She gave a quiet nod.

The Lord of the Sunset World laughed. “A spear! What a coincidence — that’s my weapon too.” He was still laughing as several beams of light shot out from behind him like dragons clearing the water, floating in a row before Zane.

“Choose from these four,” he said, crossing his arms with a proud expression.

The four spears were different colors and lengths, each radiating a distinct aura. None of them were ordinary. Yolande studied them and raised an eyebrow. “I suspect you have better ones than these, my lord.”

The Lord smiled. “You’re not wrong. I have two Grade-One Divine Weapons — both ranked in the top four thousand on the Cosmic Spirit Index, just below the Supreme Divine Weapons. But if Zane wants one of those, it’ll cost him both rewards.”

“Zane,” Lavender said flatly, “you don’t need to use both your rewards on the spear.”

He still hesitated. He knew how incredibly powerful Lavender was, and if he could get her an even better spear, he wouldn’t think twice about it. One reward, two—it didn’t matter.

Lavender saw the thought flicker across his face. “Don’t. I just want the black spear on the right.”

Her tone was final, leaving no room for argument. Zane nodded to the Lord of the Sunset World. “Alright, then. I want this spear,” he declared, pointing at the black spear on the far right.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

“Oh?” A flicker of surprise crossed the Lord’s face. “You seem to know your spears. Or did someone give you a little advice?” he asked, his gaze sliding over to Yolande.

All four spears were ranked somewhere around nine thousand on the Cosmic Spirit Index, but the Nightshard Spear Zane had picked was the best of the bunch, currently ranked at nine thousand and thirty-six. The Index refreshed every month, and a weapon’s ranking could fluctuate, especially for lower-grade items. But there was no question that this Nightshard Spear was more valuable than the other three.

Since Zane had chosen it so quickly, the Lord of the Sunset World figured Yolande must have tipped him off.

Yolande, of course, knew it was Lavender who had guided Zane’s choice. She just shook her head. “I didn’t say a word to him. Besides, you’d have felt the fluctuations in my Vitality if I had.”
