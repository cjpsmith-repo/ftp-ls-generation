Ep 941 - All Eyes on Zane

Word count: 1126

[AMBIENT: EERIE CURSED LAND]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The pink light Dorothea unleashed revealed the Cursed Land for what it truly was.

[SFX: GHOSTLY WHISPERS]

If Zane had known just how many ghosts were swarming this place, he never would have set foot inside.

Even the young man in black and Delroy were visibly shaken. With a faint smile, Dorothea extinguished the pink glow.

[SFX: MAGICAL FADE OUT]

The moment the light died, the ghosts vanished, and the Cursed Land was plunged back into the same empty darkness as before.

Zane sucked in a sharp breath, his lungs burning.

“Ugh… that was disgusting,” the young man in black wheezed, clutching his chest.

Vorn shook his head. “Well, so much for breaking in.”

Delroy nodded. “He's right. There's no fighting that horde. We'd be torn to shreds if we tried to push through. Looks like all we can do is report back to command.”

Vorn sighed. “This whole trip to the Lower World has been a total bust. We didn't catch the Evil Dragon. And the Ancestral Dragon…” His eyes flickered to Zane, the one the damn thing had imprinted on. “Now we can't even get into the Tower of Sin. Are we seriously going home with nothing?”

Delroy gave a helpless shrug. “What other choice do we have?”

Vorn’s calculating gaze landed on Zane. “I want to get inside that Tower. I need to see it for myself.”

Delroy rolled his eyes. Seriously? After what they’d just seen, did Vorn really think they could just waltz into the Cursed Land? The man had lost his mind.

But just as he was about to shut him down, an idea seemed to spark in the young man in black. He rounded on Zane. “Hey, you knew, didn't you? You knew we couldn't get in here the whole time. You were just bluffing about that title of yours, banking on the fact that we had no way to prove you wrong.”

Zane just scoffed and rolled his eyes, not even dignifying the accusation with a response.

Vorn ignored the squabble, turning to Wade. “Did you bring any of your puppets on this trip?”

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL REVELATION]

A sudden silence fell over the group, followed by a ripple of excitement.

“The puppets!”

“That's right! Wade's puppets might actually work!”

Wade wasn't just their resident expert on flying contraptions; he was also a master puppeteer. His puppets could potentially navigate the Cursed Land unharmed. But Wade's expression fell.

“Unfortunately… I didn't bring any on this trip.”

The excitement died as quickly as it had risen.

“Can you make some?” Vorn asked, his tone leaving no room for argument. “Using whatever materials are available here?”

Wade tilted his head. “I can. But lower-world materials will limit what I can produce. Don't expect anything near Spirit-Tribulation level.”

A brief silence. Then Vorn spread his hands. “We're not going in to fight. We just want to look.”

Wade gave a single nod. “Then it's doable.”

The hope of seeing the Tower of Sin surged back through the group, especially for the young man in black. He was itching for a chance to expose Zane as a fraud; if Zane's supposed title didn't show up on that Talent Tablet, he'd be screwed.

[MUSIC: QUIET REALIZATION]

Zane said nothing, but the exchange had settled something in his mind. A puppet master who could produce Spirit-Tribulation-level constructs from scratch — and even that was considered a downgrade. He'd known the gap between the Lower World and the upper world was vast. Hearing it laid out so casually made it feel bigger.

“I can speed things up,” Zane said. “I know several factions on this continent. They'll have material reserves — easier than foraging.”

Vorn looked at him. “Then lead the way.”

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: OPEN SKY]

They boarded the Compass Wheel, and Zane gave Wade the heading. The main continent wasn't large — especially with the Cursed Land occupying its center, leaving the surrounding territories compressed and close together. Within minutes, the eastern coast came into view, and the distinctive canopy of the Violet Heart Sanctuary's Holy Tree rose on the horizon.

Then Zane's eyes sharpened.

[MUSIC: SUDDEN TENSION]

[SFX: DISTANT EXPLOSION]

A column of smoke was rising from the direction of the Holy Tree. Not cooking smoke — thick, dark, wrong. And beneath it, he could feel it: the distant pulse of Vitality fluctuations, powerful and chaotic.

“That's a battle,” he said.

His first thought was: impossible. Amber was here. That was the entire reason the Violet Heart Sanctuary and the Demon Night had been able to send warriors to the Heartlands in the first place — Amber alone was enough to hold the Flaming Place and the Dread Place in check. As long as she was here, the Ogres wouldn't dare move.

But if there was a battle —

Zane's face darkened. He dropped off the Compass Wheel and broke into a sprint.

[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]

Hundreds of streaks of light cut across the sky — War Sages locked in combat, their clashing energies tearing the air apart.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Esther Sterling had fought hard to reach the rank of a second-level War Sage, but even with her newfound power, she was barely holding her own. Her opponent, a fourth-level Ogre War Sage, was simply overwhelming her.

[SFX: FIERY BLADE WHOOSH]

“Hahaha! Just give up, Sterling!” her opponent cackled, his voice a gravelly boom as he swung a fiery red saber. “You're a fine-looking Demon Night woman. It'd be a real shame to mess up that pretty face. I know we're from different stock, but you surrender to me, and I'll make you the happiest woman alive!” Bright red energy flared from the blade, lashing out toward Esther’s heart.

[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]

Zane wasn't about to let that happen. He launched himself forward, sprinting toward the fight.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

Esther's senses were honed to a razor's edge. Even as she dodged another of the Ogre's brutal swings, she felt a new presence rushing in from behind. Assuming it was another enemy joining the fray, she spun, unleashing a blinding spray of light-infused blossoms right at the newcomer.

[SFX: SWIFT MOVEMENT]

Zane twisted, a blur of motion as the deadly petals sliced past him. “Esther, it's me!”

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL STRINGS]

The voice—so familiar—cut through the chaos. She faltered, her head whipping around. When her eyes landed on him, the tension in her face melted into pure, unadulterated joy. She never would have dreamed he’d appear now, in her most desperate moment.

“Zane!” she cried, relief flooding her voice.

[SFX: SUDDEN STOP]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

Hearing the name, the Ogre War Sage froze mid-charge, his head snapping toward the newcomer. He was just as stunned by the sudden arrival.

[MUSIC: DARK DRAMATIC REVEAL]

Zane. The name was a curse on the lips of every Ogre, whispered in the Flaming Place and spat in the Dread Place. This was the man solely responsible for their entire race being cast out of the Tower of Sin. 

Every last one of them knew the name Zane, even if they’d never seen the face that went with it.
