Ep 212 - The Woman
Word count: 1072
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
[AMBIENT: WEAPON SHOP INTERIOR]
Crisp air swept through the Weapon Pavilion as Zane stood his ground, shoulders squared against the unexpected mental assault. The woman's glamour---a subtle yet powerful technique designed to bend wills---had washed over him like water against stone.
He'd emptied his mind completely, drawing upon meditation techniques learned during his secret training, leaving nothing for her influence to grasp. As their eyes locked in silent confrontation, the pavilion's other patrons continued browsing weapons, oblivious to the invisible battle of wills unfolding before them. The shopkeeper shifted uncomfortably nearby, clearly recognizing what had transpired but hesitant to intervene.
The woman was in shock. She'd never expected him to have the willpower to resist her glamour. What stunned her was his complete immunity. For a moment, she'd felt utterly powerless.
Everyone knew glamour worked best on younger men. The moment they hit puberty, they became easy prey. So for this kid---barely twenty and what should have been her easiest target---to shut her down so boldly? It didn't just make her curious. It made her determined.
That first encounter left a deep impression on the woman---who just so happened to own the entire Weapon Pavilion. A flicker of curiosity ignited in her, a sudden, burning need to know more about Zane.
"You're the one who wants to buy the sword, then?" she asked, her smile wide and inviting.
Her voice was still a little rough, but the tone was surprisingly soft, almost gentle. The kind of sound that could pull at your heartstrings.
Zane nodded. "I am."
"Well..." The woman sighed, her hand drifting to stroke the white tiger's fur around her shoulders. A polite, sharp-edged smile played on her lips as she closed the distance between them. Looking him straight in the eye, she said, "Didn't my manager tell you? This particular sword isn't for sale."
Zane returned her smile just as politely. "He did. But I feel a connection to it. It would be a shame to walk away. Is there any chance you might make an exception? Name your price."
His persistence earned a short, sharp laugh from her. She shook her head. "This isn't some common blade I can slap a price tag on. It's the culmination of Chase's final years. He poured his life's work, every bit of his knowledge and dedication, into that sword. He was a close friend of my father's, so I inherited it. But his will was very clear: I can't sell it. I can only give it to someone who can refine it into a Mythic Weapon."
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT VILLAGE]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STORYTELLING]
"Centuries ago, during a crippling drought, a clan elder invited a witch to drive away the bad omen they believed was causing their suffering. On her advice, they built a great furnace and lit a fire that was never to be extinguished. The clan prospered. They grew stronger, their territory expanded, and eventually, they became the most powerful force in The Eastern Territory. And that fire? It has never gone out. Not for a single day. It became their most sacred symbol, the heart of their hopes and dreams."
"After burning for a millennium, a living spirit finally emerged from the flames. A fire spirit forming from a flame---any flame---is incredibly rare, no matter how long it burns. There are only a handful in the entire world. And now, by a stroke of pure luck, they had stumbled upon one---a beautiful, delicate thing."
Zane thought of the Corefire he had absorbed. He was sure that fire had burned in its subterranean cave for well over a thousand years before he found it, yet even it hadn't produced a spirit.
[SFX: SHARP INTERRUPTION]
As Zane was lost in his thoughts, the woman's voice cut through the silence. "I don't like my time wasted. You'd better be as special as you seem to think you are. Let's see what you've got." Her tone was laced with mocking contempt.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ATMOSPHERE]
[SFX: FLUTTERING WINGS]
As they stood in silent opposition, the fire spirit took flight. Its incandescent golden body circled above, a living ribbon of light, impossibly beautiful. Sensing the tension, the fiery bird descended, landing weightlessly in the woman's palm. It tilted its head, its clear, golden eyes fixing on Zane.
[SFX: BIRD CHIRP]
It let out a sharp chirp, as if reading its master's thoughts and adding its own tiny note of intimidation.
But the test was the last thing on Zane's mind. He found himself more fascinated by the woman herself.
He never expected to meet someone so mysterious and powerful, let alone one with a fire spirit, here in the Capital. "Who is she?" he thought. "Hiding in plain sight at the Weapon Pavilion, her power completely masked. She looks like she's barely twenty, but the way that fire spirit obeys her... she's no ordinary girl. And why can't I sense her strength, when I can read it on almost anyone else? An incredible talent who owns a fire spirit... I doubt even the Skyreach Sect could produce someone this capable."
He couldn't tear his eyes away from the woman and her tiny, brilliant bird.
[MUSIC: TENSE NEGOTIATION]
Still, Zane remained confident in his unique fire abilities. He gave her a subtle smile, his eyes reflecting a hint of challenge. "So, if I demonstrate my fire to you, the sword becomes mine? No strings attached?"
The woman's enigmatic smile returned as she shook her head. "It's not that easy. This isn't about showing me just any old flame. You've heard of Chase's reputation. Surely you don't expect me to compromise his legacy so easily?"
She locked her penetrating gaze directly into his eyes, her voice dropping to a more solemn tone. "If you present me with an ordinary flame, you prove yourself unworthy of this sword. I simply cannot entrust it to undeserving hands. Who knows what you'd turn it into? Perhaps a top-grade Spirit Weapon at best? Or worse, your limited skills might only produce a medium-grade Spirit blade. Either outcome would betray Chase's vision. His life's work---his entire legacy---would be for nothing."
Her fingers traced the unfinished blade's edge as her expression hardened. "And that," she whispered, "is the one betrayal I will never permit." The golden fire bird suddenly flared brighter on her shoulder, its flames casting eerie shadows across the room as it fixed its molten eyes on Zane. For a fleeting moment, he felt something ancient and terrifying stir within the blade itself, as though Chase's unfinished creation was awakening to judge Zane worthy---or condemn him entirely.
