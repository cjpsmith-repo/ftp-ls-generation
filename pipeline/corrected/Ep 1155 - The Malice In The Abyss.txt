Ep 1155 - The Malice In The Abyss

Word count: 1058

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
[SFX: RUSTLING FABRIC]
Zane grabbed Livingston, shoved him into his bestiary bag, and cinched it shut. A quick glance inside confirmed the little guy was secure. The other warriors watched, their faces tight with envy.
They couldn’t be sure if Livingston’s skills were really as legendary as he claimed, but he *had* just taken credit for the entire trove of ancient treasures they’d discovered. And this stuff wasn’t junk. A smith who could forge weapons like these would be a priceless asset to any of the major powers.
Even if he was lying, the puppet had spent years in the Underground Palace; he had to know its secrets. But Zane had snatched him up without a second thought, and the message was clear: he wasn’t sharing. There was nothing anyone could do about it. To keep Livingston from spilling too many secrets to the wrong people, Zane had bagged him at the first opportunity.
It wasn’t lost on the others, either, that this was the second time Zane had walked away from a cave with something extraordinary. First, the World Tree seed. Now, a puppet that claimed to be the greatest weaponsmith alive. 
To the rest of them, it felt less like an expedition and more like watching one man collect prizes while everyone else tagged along to watch. A few muttered about it. None of them said it to his face.
And yet, none of them turned back either. Whatever resentment simmered under the surface, it didn’t outweigh the simple fact that Zane’s strength was the reason any of them still had a shot at going deeper. They followed him without a word of agreement between them, because some part of every one of them understood: wherever he was leading, it was further than they’d get alone.
The move didn’t just rankle the warriors in the palace.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR OBSERVATION POST]
Outside, the three great lords monitoring the situation were just as irritated.
The head of the Magnolia Sanctum shook his head.
“That kid is pushing it,” he muttered. “He can’t just hoard everything for himself.”
A smith capable of forging Divine-Class weapons was an incredible prize, a fact the sanctum lords knew better than anyone. Magnolia’s leader felt the shortage of such weapons like a constant weight on his shoulders. His sanctum might handle a portion of the arms trade for the Alliance, but they were just a middleman—they saw none of the real profit.
“Ha!” Yosef sneered, his expression twisting. “Let him take what he wants from the Endless Abyss for now. The kid thinks he’s hit the jackpot, but he still has to get out of the Underground Palace. I’d love to see him try to make it out of the Dark World in one piece.”
Yosef’s words drew a sharp frown from the human faction’s leader. “That boy is one of ours, from the Thirteen Palaces of the Alliance. If he gets out of there, I’ll personally see to it that he leaves this place safely. So don’t even think about it.”
Zane, of course, heard none of this. 
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND CAVE ECHOES]
Back in the Underground Palace, he called out to Freya.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
He turned his back on the glittering mountain of ancient treasures without a second glance and walked out of the cave.
[SFX: TREASURES CLINKING]
[SFX: QUICK FOOTSTEPS]
Hearing his call, Freya quickly scooped up a handful of treasures for herself before hurrying after him, a satisfied smile on her face as she mentally tallied up how many Sovereign Stones they would fetch.
“Let’s keep going,” Zane said, and tipped himself back into the abyss without waiting for a response.
Freya didn’t hesitate either. She trusted him completely. She leaned her body and dropped in after him. Behind her, Lincoln and the others followed, one by one, back into the fall.
“This abyss isn’t a straight drop,” Lavender said, her voice settling into Zane’s mind. “It’s a ring. You’re not falling — you’re circling. Every full loop brings you to a new cave.”
That explained it. The caves weren’t stops along a descent — they were waypoints on a loop, over and over.
“How many loops?” Zane asked.
“Five, as far as I know. The ancient Supreme Lord built five rounds into this ring. A hundred thousand years ago, I made it through four of them myself. Whatever the real secret of this place is, I’d bet everything it’s waiting past the fifth.”
Five rounds. They’d cleared two. The third had just begun.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]
Normally, a warrior could handle the Malice that built up inside them. It was the practitioners of the dark arts who struggled, needing to kill constantly just to gather more, risking madness as it festered into inner demons. So, even with the air in the abyss thick with the stuff, the warriors weren’t too worried. It wasn’t inside their bodies, after all. A little unsettling, sure, but not terrifying.
[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]
They were about to find out just how wrong they were.
“There!” The golden-haired woman from the Demon Night had been watching the depths below them the entire fall, and she was the first to spot it. “Everyone, be careful.”
Zane followed her gaze down into the abyss. A wash of blood-red had bloomed at the bottom, and the moment he saw it, something in his chest went tight.
“What is that?” he asked.
The deeper they went, the thicker the Malice became. 
[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]
At first, it was just a blood-red light tracing the cracks in the abyss walls. 
[SFX: FLOATING PARTICLES HUMMING]
But soon, it condensed into crimson specks of light that floated in the air around them. There was no avoiding them. Even huddled in the center of the chasm, the warriors felt the specks drift into them, bumping against their skin.
[SFX: SKIN ABSORPTION HISS]
Each time a speck touched them, it dissolved, the Malice seeping through their skin and into their bodies. The first few times, no one felt a thing. But as they absorbed more and more, a restless energy began to thrum under their skin. 
[SFX: ACCELERATING HEARTBEATS]
Hearts started pounding in chests. An ugly, violent urge began to build—a need to just break something. To tear everything around them to pieces.
