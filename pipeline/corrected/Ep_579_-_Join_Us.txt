Ep 579 - Join Us
Word count: 1073
[AMBIENT: TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
The woman looked ready to murder Zane. She also looked familiar. Zane was sure he’d seen her before, back when he first arrived at the Tower of Sin.
She’d been standing right next to that human War General, Dylan. She hadn’t said a word during Zane’s unpleasant run-in with the guy.
The famous Violet Heart Sanctuary might be a big deal, but it hadn’t exactly left a great impression on him. If the whole place was crawling with jerks like Dylan, he figured it was best to steer clear of them entirely.
So when she approached him now, he was instantly annoyed, and he didn't bother to hide it.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
The woman, Olivia, was furious. As a ninth-level War General of the Violet Heart Sanctuary, she was used to a certain level of deference. Yet this man was looking at her like she was for sale. A woman of her standing commanded respect, and she wouldn’t tolerate his blatant arrogance.
But she couldn’t afford to lose her composure. She forced the fury down, taking a deep, slow breath. The hard edge in her eyes softened, and then, to Zane's surprise, she laughed.
[SFX: SHARP LAUGH]
"Ha! Let me tell you something. I doubt someone like you could afford me. I'm expensive. Priceless, in fact."
Zane blinked, caught off guard. He'd expected another arrogant lecture, like the one he'd gotten from Dylan. But Olivia was… tolerant. Amused, even. He decided to drop the crude act.
"Alright," he said. "Who wants to see me?"
Olivia let out a quiet breath of relief. That idiot Dylan had poisoned the well last time — she'd been half-expecting Zane to walk away entirely.
She understood why the Sanctuary elders were so invested in him. On the Sea God Continent, humans were considered weak, barely clinging to their status as one of the fourteen great races. Every time a human genius entered the Tower of Sin, they got torn apart by the other races. That cycle of defeat had ground down the confidence of an entire generation.
But Zane had just beaten a god-tier Demon Night genius as an eighth-level War Fighter. That was unprecedented. Word had already reached the Sanctuary elders, and the order had come down fast: protect him, support him, nurture him.
If Zane became a War General, he could be a symbol, proof that humans could compete. If he reached War Lord, he might win the human race another seat entirely.
That was the only reason she was tolerating Zane's rudeness.
"Our human War Lord, Elder Kingsley, wants to see you," Olivia said, her tone all business.
Zane nodded. "Alright, lead the way. Let's get this over with."
[AMBIENT: BUSY TAVERN]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS IN CORRIDOR]
He followed Olivia as she led them deep into the living area. After a long walk through a maze of corridors, she finally stopped in front of a tavern.
Someone was waiting for them inside. He was an ordinary-looking man dressed in simple grey clothes, deliberately suppressing his energy to avoid drawing attention.
The man brought his hands together in a formal gesture of respect, a surprising warmth in his voice. "Mister Lowell! I've heard so much about you!"
[SFX: MURMURS OF SURPRISE]
The gesture didn't go unnoticed. Other martial artists in the tavern exchanged baffled looks. Why would a War Lord be greeting a mere War Fighter with such respect?
Zane inclined his head in return. "You must be Elder Kingsley."
"Please, have a seat so we can talk." Elder Kingsley gestured toward a nearby table.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]
Zane took a seat. Once they were all settled, Kingsley got right to the point. "Mister Lowell," he began, "it's safe to assume you're not from the Violet Heart Sanctuary, correct?" It was less a question and more a statement. They had already looked into Zane's background. While the Sanctuary had plenty of talent, someone with his remarkable skill wouldn't have gone unnoticed for so long.
"That's right," Zane confirmed. "I'm not."
"I see. Would you mind telling me where you're from?" Elder Kingsley asked.
Zane knew that on the main continent, besides the Violet Heart Sanctuary, there were only a few scattered sects. Most were affiliated with one of the sacred places; they had to be, otherwise they'd never survive the attacks from other races.
"I'd rather not answer that," Zane said with a slight smile.
"Fair enough." Kingsley nodded, unbothered. Then he leaned forward slightly.
"In that case, would you consider joining the Violet Heart Sanctuary?"
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]
Zane already knew what joining would mean. Resources. Protection. The full backing of the most powerful human organization on the continent. Under other circumstances, it might have been worth considering.
But he hadn't come to this continent to build a new life. He had a goal, and it was waiting at the bottom of the Tower of Sin. Joining the Sanctuary meant obligations, and obligations meant time — time he didn't have.
He thought of Elara.
"I'm already a member of another sect," Zane said. "I'm sorry."
Kingsley's face fell slightly, but he recovered quickly. "I won't press you. But I want you to know — nearly ninety percent of the human race on this continent is part of the Violet Heart Sanctuary. You carry the hopes of our people, whether you join us or not. We've seen what you can do." He paused. "If you're willing to help when it matters, that's enough for me."
Zane nodded. “If I can help, I won't refuse.”
Kingsley smiled. "Then I'm satisfied. We humans may only hold four seats in the Tower of Sin, but we are still one of the fourteen great races. If you ever need our help, please don't hesitate to ask."
"A seat? What's that?" Zane asked.
"A seat represents the right to enter the top of the Tower," Kingsley explained. "It's the only way up. But it comes with another responsibility: those who hold a seat help establish the rules and punishments for the entire Tower. There are one hundred twenty-eight seats in total, all on the fifteenth floor. Every so often, they're reassigned based on a cultivator's strength. Unfortunately… humanity holds only four of them."
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
"The right to enter the top of the Tower," Zane repeated, his eyes suddenly sharp.
That was it. That was the goal that had driven him across thousands of miles to this continent.
The right to enter the top of the Tower. The inheritance waiting at the bottom. He was closer than he'd ever been — and every race in the Tower wanted him dead.
