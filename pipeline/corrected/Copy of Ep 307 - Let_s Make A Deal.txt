[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE]
The spectral palm of the Immovable Guardian materialized above Patrick, pressing down with the same crushing force Tracy had once aimed at Zane.
But things were different. Patrick's power was on a whole other level.
His strength wasn't just in his bloodline; he was the reincarnation of the Immovable Guardian.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
Golden runes streamed across the spectral palm, pulsing with a brilliant, unwavering light.
The sheer power radiating from the golden palm made Finn's expression harden. He had finally torn himself free from Patrick's Eternal Cycle, but the escape had cost him dearly, leaving his body screaming in protest.
Is this Patrick’s real strength? he wondered. Gritting his teeth, Finn stared up at the massive palm descending on him. He wrenched more vitality from the heavens and the earth, even pulling in a sliver of raw Primal Vitality. He funneled every last drop into his Extreme Thunder Sword Intent, unleashing a razor-sharp blade of energy to meet the attack head-on.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
This was it. The final clash.
If I win, I earned it, Finn thought. If I lose... then that's my destiny.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: BLINDING FLASH]
A deafening explosion rocked the arena as the golden palm collided with the blue thunder. The resulting flash was so brilliant it felt like a star had just gone nova, forcing everyone to look away.
Everyone but Zane. Standing outside the ring, he didn’t even flinch.
The blinding light flashed across his brown eyes, but he never looked away, tracking every single move the two fighters made.
Zane slowly shook his head as he watched them go at it. Finn's going to lose. Earlier, Finn had claimed he had a fifty-fifty shot at beating Patrick, a chance that would jump to ninety percent once he reached the Soul Realm. He had completely underestimated his opponent.
With his current strength, Finn didn’t have a ten percent chance against Patrick.
To be fair, Finn’s recent breakthrough was incredible. He'd reached the Apex of Sword Intent and even tempered his blade in the sacred Sword Tempering Pool.
That kind of power should have been enough to bridge the gap in their cultivation levels. In his mind, reaching the Soul Realm would all but guarantee his victory. He was naive.
As the reincarnation of the Immovable Guardian, Patrick was just on another plane of existence.
And that was a terrifying thought.
If Finn got another chance, if he actually managed to break through to the Soul Realm, he might finally have the strength to stand a real chance against Patrick.
After the fight settled, Patrick walked over to Zane. "The Immovable Guardian can only be summoned once — it'll take a few days before I can call on it again. Do you want to fight today, or wait?"
Zane didn't hesitate. "Three days. I'm not interested in fighting you at half strength."
Patrick nodded. "Three days, then."
[AMBIENT: ARENA AFTERMATH]
[MUSIC: SOMBER DEFEAT]
The blinding light in the arena finally faded. Finn was leaning heavily on his Blood-Corroding Sword, his whole body trembling. The usual confidence on his lean face was gone, replaced by a mask of bitter sadness.
This was a young man who believed every fight made him stronger, and he had just been utterly crushed. The frustration was written all over him.
But frustration hits everyone differently.
For some arrogant types, their dedication to martial arts is only skin-deep. A single defeat is enough to break their spirit. They stop training, or worse, develop a flaw in their heart that they can never overcome.
But for others, that arrogance is rooted in a profound devotion to their craft. For them, a setback is just fuel for the fire, pushing them to train even harder. These are the people who seek.
[SFX: ABRUPT CONVERSATION SHIFT]
During the competition, Zane had sought out Sir Kenneth.
"I can definitely get enough crystal cubes to pay you," Zane said.
Sir Kenneth studied him. "Two million crystal cubes. Where would a Skyreach disciple get that kind of money?"
"That's not really the point," Zane said evenly. "The point is whether it'll work."
With the methods he'd developed, making money would be easy.
Fifty drops of Celestial Essence could fetch two hundred and fifty thousand crystal cubes at auction. He'd already gotten hundreds of drops from refining just a hundred Mythic weapons.
Even if a flood of Celestial Essence hit the market and tanked the price, getting his hands on two million crystal cubes wouldn’t be that hard.
Besides, Zane could always go to Dustin Blackwood for help. He figured, after his recent performance, he'd earned enough goodwill with House Blackwood. Borrowing two million crystal cubes from them shouldn’t be an issue. They were the wealthiest family in the Eastern Territories, after all; they made even the Seven Great Houses look poor.
Even though Sir Kenneth rarely showed his face at the Skyreach Sect, he knew the political landscape of the Eastern Territories like the back of his hand. If Zane was this confident about raising two million crystal cubes, Kenneth could easily guess it had something to do with the Blackwoods. There was no other way a regular disciple could come up with that kind of fortune.
His eyes narrowed as he thought it through. Considering his exceptional talent and power, this young man is more than a prospect - he’s a sure thing. House Blackwood would be foolish to overlook such a promising alliance. He studied Zane’s composed features, noting the fierce determination that belied his youth. The boy had already accomplished what many cultivators twice his age could only dream of achieving. A partnership with someone of his caliber would have payouts far beyond financial success—it would be a strategic masterstroke for a house’s future influence in the realm.
[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]
Finally, Kenneth let out a long sigh.
Zane was a picture of calm, even though he was a wreck on the inside.
"So, that’s a no?"
Since the moment he joined the Skyreach Sect, he'd been focused on one thing: saving his sister, Elara, and getting her off Infernal Peak as soon as possible.
If he could help it, Zane didn't want Elara to spend a single second longer on that mountain.
That was why he was always pushing, always clawing his way toward those one million Points. Even when he had to spend some on himself, he only did it when he knew he could earn them back and then some.
But...
[MUSIC: SOMBER REVELATION]
"No," Kenneth said, shaking his head. "Even if you pay the two million cubes—the full equivalent of one million points—you can’t take her away."
"Why?" Zane demanded.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT INTENSIFIES]
He gritted his teeth, his heart hammering against his ribs. He could feel his eyes starting to burn, turning red around the edges.
He’d always respected Sir Kenneth, but in this moment, he wanted to murder the old man.
