Ep 1069 - The Mystery In The Rock

Word count: 1214

[AMBIENT: CAVE ECHO]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Zane and Layla followed Yolande’s finger, their eyes drawn to the dense text carved into the rock face.

The words varied wildly in size and style. Some were massive, as tall as a person, while others were no bigger than the palm of a hand. They were scrawled in all sorts of different scripts.

[SFX: STONE SCRAPING]

“I threw away a brilliant future for this, spent my whole life staring at this rock, and for what? Nothing. Let it be known across the universe: this is nothing but a scam cooked up by Supreme Lord Sword!”

“Liar! Supreme Lord Sword is the biggest liar of them all! I wasted ten thousand years here just to grow old. If I get another life, I swear I’m coming back to shatter this damn stone.”

“Hey, newcomers, give it up. Stop dreaming and focus on your own training. Trying to decipher Supreme Lord Sword’s intent from these words? It’s a fool’s errand! Life’s too short to waste it here.”

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC FLUTE]

The wall of text was a monument to failure, filled with bitter warnings from those who came before, all telling newcomers to give up the hopeless vigil.

Zane sighed, shaking his head as he read the desperate inscriptions. He could feel their frustration bleeding from the stone. To spend an entire lifetime staring at this rock, only to end up with nothing… He couldn’t imagine accepting a fate like that either.

“So where is everyone now?” Zane asked curiously.

From the shallow pits worn into the ground where people had once sat, Zane could tell countless warriors had come to meditate before this rock. Their dedication had literally carved itself into the earth.

“Ever since the Orange Sea Realm was chosen to host the Alliance’s Passing the Torch ceremony, regular warriors can’t get in anymore,” Yolande explained, scanning the empty clearing. She shook her head. “Besides, no one has cracked its secrets in millions of years—at least, not in living memory. Most warriors have given up on the legend. Still, you’ll usually find a few diehards meditating here. I guess not today.”

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

Nearby, the vice leaders and top talents from the Mist Palace stood waiting, their gazes fixed on the ground to avoid the rock’s overwhelming aura.

“What are they doing over there?”

“I think that kid claimed he figured out the rock’s mystery!”

“That’s impossible!”

Harvey’s lip curled. “Claiming to see something in that rock is practically a tradition on Willow Island,” he said, loud enough for everyone to hear. “Scammers have been pulling this trick for years. One swore he could see the Heaven and Earth Chessboard. Another claimed he saw the Snake Shadow. Someone else insisted it was the Ten Thousand Swords Formation.” He glanced at Zane with open contempt. “There’s always another one.”

One of the bystanders muttered under his breath. “My uncle had a real shot at the peak realm. Threw it all away, standing in front of that rock. Watched him go through the Five Aging Processes and die with nothing.”

Harvey didn’t linger. “We came here for Passing the Torch, not to babysit the Palace Master.” He turned and walked, and his people followed. The other warriors drifted after them, one by one, until the area around the rock was nearly empty.

Layla turned to Zane, her expression serious. “Zane, are you absolutely sure you saw a white sword shadow?”

Zane was quiet for a moment. “I’m not sure,” he said carefully. He had seen the pale shadows, felt them pierce through him — but after everything Yolande and Layla had said, he couldn’t be confident. Millions of warriors had failed. How could he be certain what he’d seen was real?

A flicker of disappointment crossed Layla’s face.

Even though neither she nor Yolande thought he was a liar, the claim was just too much to swallow. For millions of years, countless warriors had studied this rock, and not a single one had unlocked its secrets. The idea that Zane could see the key to it all the moment he arrived was, frankly, unbelievable.

“Then let’s not waste time,” Layla said. “Let’s go.”

Yolande looked at Zane for a moment — she had her own suspicions about what he’d actually seen, but she kept them to herself. “The warriors of the Thirteen Palaces will already be there.” Then, quieter: “Can you find your way?”

“Can I stay a while?” Zane asked.

She nodded. “I’ll come find you when Passing the Torch begins.” She turned and headed around the boulder, Layla a step behind her.

[AMBIENT: DESERT WIND]

With everyone gone, Zane descended quietly and found a place to sit in the hard desert sand. He looked up at the poem again. He could still feel the piercing sensation moving through him — the shadows were there. He just couldn’t see them.

His mind was too unsettled. Yolande and Layla’s reactions had gotten to him more than he’d realized.

He closed his eyes briefly and began repeating a single word to himself. Calm. Calm. Calm. Dozens of times, until his breathing slowed and the noise in his head faded.

[SFX: SLOW EXHALE]

When he opened his eyes and looked back at the rock, something faint stirred in the stone — the barest outline of a white shadow, emerging from the carved words.

He had it again.

[SFX: MOCKING LAUGHTER]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Suddenly, a jeering voice cut through the air. “Haha! Well, look what we have here. Not many young warriors are still interested in Supreme Lord Sword’s little mystery these days.”

[SFX: MAGICAL DISSIPATION]

Zane’s fragile focus shattered. The white sword shadow vanished in an instant. He frowned, a flash of pure annoyance cutting through his concentration.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

He hated being interrupted like this. Any warrior did. It was the ultimate breach of etiquette—a simple cough at the wrong moment could send someone deep in cultivation into a state of psychosis. The anger was justified.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Zane’s face darkened as he looked up to find the source of the voice. It was a middle-aged man in dark, shabby clothes, carrying a bit of extra weight, and covered in filth. He looked like he hadn’t bathed in years, his beard a tangled, matted mess.

The man was practically buzzing with excitement to see Zane and called out to his friend, “Landry! We’ve got another one! Get over here!”

[SFX: WHOOSH]

With a blur of motion, another man shot through the air to join him. This one was thinner, but no less grimy.

Their skill is surprisingly high, Zane thought, sensing they were both at the Spirit Supreme Realm.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FLUTE]

The warrior named Landry sized Zane up, a wide smile spreading across his face as his dark eyes twinkled with manic energy. He asked, “Young man, are you trying to understand the mystery hidden in this giant rock?”

Zane nodded slowly. “That’s why I’m here.” His eyes drifted over their ragged appearance. “How long have you two been here?”

[MUSIC: EERIE REVELATION]

Landry and the portly man glanced at each other, sharing a wild, unhinged grin. Landry jabbed a thumb toward the heavier man. “This is Damian. He’s been here for over twenty thousand years.”
