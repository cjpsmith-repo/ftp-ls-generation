Ep 13 - Family Training Day
Word Count: 1104
[AMBIENT: HOWLING WINTER WIND]
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
This year's winter was colder than anyone could remember.
A bitter chill swept down from the vast frozen tundra to the north, blanketing most of the Empire in a sea of white.
Thick snow, soft as cotton, covered the ground.
Even the sprawling training grounds in front of House Lowell weren't spared.
[SFX: SNOW BEING CLEARED]
But today was Family Training Day, and servants had cleared the arena, piling the snow high along its edges.
Twelve teams of Lowell children stood in neat formations, awaiting inspection by the House elders.
There used to be thirteen branches of House Lowell. But ever since the allegations that destroyed Zane's family and shattered their branch, only twelve remained.
Family Training Day was an annual event designed to push the Lowell children to their limits.
This day was just as critical for them as it was for their "human punchbags." The performance of each branch determined its resource allocation for the coming year.
A strong showing meant a bigger share of valuable assets, like special pills to accelerate Physical Enhancement or Restorative Tonics that could knit wounds in moments.
But for the slaves, the stakes were far higher: life, death, or freedom.
[MUSIC: TENSE MILITARY MARCH]
[SFX: MARCHING FOOTSTEPS]
Zane marched with the other slaves toward the training grounds. A grim resolve was etched on every face. They all knew what was at stake. In a Fatal Duel, there were only two ways out: win your freedom, or die trying.
For the Lowell children, however, the Fatal Duel was merely a test. Victory brought rewards and the family's favor. The only consequence for losing was the sting of shame.
Most of the Lowell heirs were already formidable, their skills and power far beyond those of an ordinary person. But many of them still folded under pressure, lacking true combat experience. It wasn't unheard of for them to lose. That was the whole point of the Fatal Duel. The rules were designed to push the slaves to their absolute limits, forcing them to fight with the desperation of a cornered animal.
It was the only way for the Lowell children to get a taste of a real fight.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUND]
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
Moments after the slaves took their places, a middle-aged man in a green robe stepped onto the high platform.
Owen Lowell. Zane's uncle. He had a long beard and a face that was unnervingly smooth, as if carved from stone.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]
A flash of pure hatred burned in Zane's eyes at the sight of him. He could never forget the role Owen played in his father's death. The man carried himself like a respectable gentleman, but behind that mask, he'd used deceit and poison to murder his own brother.
To Zane, he was nothing but a damn hypocrite.
If Zane was sure he could win, he would have killed Owen right then and there.
But Owen was a master of the Core Strength Level, his internal organs honed and fortified. His raw power was said to equal the weight of three Ancient Cauldrons.
People whispered that he could rip a bear apart with his bare hands or shatter a fortress wall with a single punch.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
Owen stood on the high platform, took a deep breath, and announced, "Today, we have good news. My nephew, the young master of House Lowell, has been accepted into the Skyreach Sect. He is now one of their Core Apprentices."
He didn't raise his voice, but it carried across the entire training ground, a testament to his mastery. With his internal organs perfectly trained, even a whisper from him would have reached every ear.
Envy flickered across the faces of the other Lowell children. Everyone had expected Perrin to get into the Skyreach Sect, of course.
Disciples in the Skyreach Sect were ranked. Countless hopefuls passed the grueling entrance exams, only to be named Outer Disciples—a title that came with little prestige and even fewer resources.
But a Core Apprentice? How had Perrin improved so much, so fast? No one had seen that coming. The envy simmering among the other children was suddenly very understandable.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPICION]
Zane wondered the same thing, a cold knot forming in his stomach. The Aether Crystal. Could the one Perrin had stolen from him really be that powerful? Powerful enough to make him a Core Apprentice?
Zane's fists clenched, a wave of anxiety washing over him. His uncles had let him live because they saw him as a bug to be crushed at their leisure—no real threat to their power. But Elara... Elara was different. Her talent was undeniable. She was a threat.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]
Elara had been safe from the coup that destroyed their family only because she was already at the Skyreach Sect. But with Perrin there now... his presence could only mean trouble for her. The thought of his sister in danger lit a fire in Zane's gut. He had to get to the Skyreach Sect, and he had to get there now.
"Perrin is the pride of our house!" Owen's voice boomed, cutting through Zane's thoughts. "But his success must not distract us from our own duties. Today is Family Training Day! Each of you will choose a slave for the Fatal Duel. Victory will double your allotment of Hardening Shards for the next year. Your monthly allowance will also be doubled. And as a bonus: a Stamina Pill."
[SFX: EXCITED CROWD]
A low buzz of excitement spread through the Lowell children at the mention of such generous rewards. The air crackled with their ambition.
The slaves, however, only looked grimmer. Better rewards meant a harder fight. Fueled by greed, the Lowell children would be more ruthless than ever. Their already slim chances of survival had just gotten even thinner.
When Owen finished his speech, he returned to his ornate chair on the platform. The steward, Grey, took his place. Grey's eyes swept over the slaves before locking onto Zane. A vicious glint flashed in his gaze, and Zane could practically hear the man's thoughts: "You die today, boy."
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMURS]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BATTLE PREPARATION]
A cruel smirk touched Grey's lips. He raised his voice, shouting, "Let the selection begin! The Lowell children may now choose their punching bags! First to the arena is Andrew Lowell, eldest son of the third branch!"
Andrew leaped from the platform into the snowy arena below. He gave a respectful bow to his father, Owen, then turned, his eyes scanning the line of slaves, searching for his opponent.
