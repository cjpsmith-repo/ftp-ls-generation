Ep 562 - A Warning
Word count: 1120
[SFX: PRIMAL LION ROAR]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
The roar that erupted came from a primal ferocity locked deep within the Cloud-Chaser Lion's bone.
Even the bones of a Drake, a creature almost legendary, could hold their ferocity for ages. But the Cloud-Chaser Lion was a true Legendary Beast, a close relative of the even more powerful Greater Legendary Beast: the Celestial Chimera.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
The moment Zane’s forceful energy flowed into the bone, it awakened the spirit trapped inside.
[SFX: MYSTICAL WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL WIND]
As the lion’s roar echoed in his mind, Zane immediately wrapped the bone in his Vitality, smothering the primal power within.
Even so, though he managed to suppress ninety-nine percent of its force, a faint ripple still escaped.
[AMBIENT: FEARFUL CROWD MURMURS]
[SFX: BODIES COLLAPSING]
The few hundred people surrounding Zane exchanged terrified glances, their faces draining of color. The weaker ones trembled, their legs giving out as they collapsed to the ground.
Zane let out a shaky breath, relief washing over him. If he hadn't contained that roar, if that primal ferocity had truly been unleashed, no one around him would have survived.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL MEDITATION]
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL RESONANCE]
With the bone now soaked in his energy, Zane began to refine it, drawing out the essence held within.
To establish the Pathforged Realm within himself, he first needed to forge the Confluence Platform. He would use the bone's essence to build it.
[SFX: ETHEREAL SHIMMER]
[MUSIC: TRANSCENDENT STRINGS]
A milky-white mist began to form between his eyebrows as he absorbed the essence, bit by bit. Inside the swirling mist, a platform was slowly taking shape.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]
But even after he’d absorbed the entire bone, the Confluence Platform between his eyebrows remained incomplete.
Establishing the Pathforged Realm was a major hurdle for body refiners. It was never going to be easy, not even with the bone of a Legendary Beast.
One bone wasn't enough. Zane pulled another from his Storage Ring.
[SFX: MAGICAL WRAPPING]
This time, he was far more careful, wrapping it in a thick layer of Vitality before he began, preventing even a whisper of the Cloud-Chaser Lion’s power from leaking out.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TRANSFORMATION]
As Zane refined the second bone, the mist around his Confluence Platform gradually receded. If he were to close his eyes and look inward, he would see a solid white platform standing there.
[SFX: ENERGY CIRCULATION]
At that moment, the forceful energy that had been spread evenly throughout his body began to slowly gather on the platform, circulating at a steady, rhythmic pace.
The Confluence Platform was the very foundation of the Pathforged Realm. Once it was formed, a body refiner's ability to generate forceful energy would skyrocket—and that was just the first of eight platforms.
[AMBIENT: VAST WILDERNESS]
[MUSIC: SLOW JOURNEY]
While Zane's inner world was being forged anew, the group's progress across the land was painfully slow. It would take them days, if not weeks, to make any real headway.
The land beyond the pasture was unlike anything Zane had seen before. The soil, the mountains, the very plants—all of it was black, as though the color had been drained from the world entirely. It pressed down on the spirit.
And it was quiet. Too quiet. Not a single member of the Ogre Clan. Not even a living creature.
Zane understood what silence like that usually meant.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Since reaching the Fighting Soul Realm, his soul had grown far more sensitive. And now it was telling him, clearly, that they were being watched. Multiple presences, surrounding them from all directions, hidden in the dark.
"Why aren't they moving?" The question gnawed at him. Even if these were Ogre Clan scouts, why hold back? Zane had no answers. He was new to this continent—he didn't know its powers, its politics, its fault lines. All he could do was keep moving and take it one step at a time.
What Zane didn't know was that the watchers were just as puzzled as he was.
This was the Heavenly Eagle Prairie—the northeast corner of the continent. Ten thousand years ago, humans had built a civilization here. Five thousand years ago, the Ogre Clan had burned it to the ground. Since then, three powers had carved the prairie into thirds: the Ogre Clan, a human faction, and a third race known as the Demon Night Clan. For over ten years, an uneasy truce had held. No one wanted to be the one to break it.
And then Zane had walked straight into the middle of it.
A hundred miles away, two Ogre Clan generals stood atop a fortress wall, trading uneasy glances.
[AMBIENT: FORTRESS WALLS]
[MUSIC: MILITARY TENSION]
"Where did that human come from? Our scouts say he appeared out of nowhere—origin energy, spatial fluctuations. Someone opened a passageway."
"Taking a few captive humans is nothing. But if he's running a scheme..."
"If we move against him now, the humans will respond. And the Demon Night Clan won't stay out of it either."
The two generals fell silent. Finally, one of them said, "Report it up the chain. The boy is moving slowly—he has hundreds of ordinary people with him. We have time."
[AMBIENT: ARMY FORTRESS]
Two hundred miles away, three other men were just as puzzled by the sight.
They were clad in the armor of army generals. One of them, a middle-aged man with a full beard, sat atop a fortress wall and chuckled. "Where'd this kid come from? He's clearly just a Level Seven War Fighter, but he waltzed into the ogre's pasture and walked out with a few hundred people in tow. What's his game?"
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
"Who cares if he gets himself killed? We'll just play dumb and pretend we never saw a thing," another said coldly. "We've had ten years of peace. It didn't come cheap. If we show our faces, we risk starting another war."
The last one, a handsome young man with silver hair and eyes that flashed green, shot back. "No way. I'm reporting this to my father. Something about this is off. This isn't our call to make. And wherever the kid is from, he's still human."
The bearded man shook his head, his amusement fading. "You're right, it has to be reported. The bigger problem is that the kid is heading straight for the Demon Night's territory."
The three factions watched Zane’s group closely.
Their progress was a crawl. Forty miles the first day.
Forty-five the next.
By the third day, their pace had slowed to just thirty.
[AMBIENT: TENSE WILDERNESS]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
Then, on the fourth day, an arrow as thick as a man's arm came screaming out of the distance, launched from at least ten miles away.
It was carved from the bone of some massive beast, its surface etched with strange, flickering runes.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
The bone arrow wasn’t aimed at anyone. It slammed into the dirt right in front of Zane, burying itself halfway into the ground with a heavy thud.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane stared at the quivering shaft, his brow furrowing.
Clearly, it was a warning. But of what?
