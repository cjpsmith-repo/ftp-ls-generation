Ep 861 - Purification Fire

Word count: 1087

[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUND]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

If Zane unleashed his full strength, a single punch could probably shatter a Soul Sea master. But that didn’t mean he was invincible. 

Raw power wasn’t everything, and Zane knew it. The truly powerful warriors from the Soul Sea Realm were faster, their technique was flawless, and their understanding of the Laws was on a completely different level. In a real fight, they’d still wipe the floor with him.

For now, though, his focus was locked on the golden leaf. The plan was simple: smash it open with brute force and get the Black Star Pickax hidden inside. This was the perfect chance to test his physical limits.

[SFX: ENERGY BUILDING]

He began to gather the power of the Dragon Scales, ready to channel it through his body. Just as the energy started to build, Fiona’s voice cut through his concentration.

“Zane? Where are you?”

[SFX: DARK ENERGY HUM]

She had wandered into the arsenal with him and was now carefully inspecting the weapons scattered across the floor. The dark energy clinging to them was palpable, and she couldn’t help but wonder if there was a way to break the curses, to purify them.

She knew that certain Original Laws, once mastered, shared the ability to purify. Thunder Law, for instance, could scour something clean with lightning. Fire Law was more destructive—it didn’t just cleanse a curse, it burned it away completely.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHOIR]

But Fiona had mastered something unique: the Purification Fire.

It wasn’t a hot flame. In fact, it was so cool that it wouldn’t even singe a piece of wood. Its true power was its incredible effectiveness against negative energies like curses and its ability to harm evil creatures. 

Still, looking at the malevolent energy swirling around these weapons, she wasn’t sure if her fire was strong enough.

[AMBIENT: TENSE MAGICAL ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

There was only one way to find out. A milky-white flame flickered to life in her palm, and she cautiously brought it toward the curse enveloping a nearby spear. Her Purification Fire was the complete opposite of the destructive black flame that resided in Zane’s mind; where his could destroy almost anything, hers wouldn’t damage the weapon at all.

[SFX: MAGICAL HISSING]

As the pearly flame touched the spear, the coiling black smoke hissed and began to slowly fade.

It works! Fiona thought, a spark of excitement cutting through her focus.

She tossed the purified spear aside and went looking for Zane.

[MUSIC: TENSE LOW STRINGS]

“Zane?” she called softly. Then she saw him — and her hand flew to her mouth. “What happened to you?”

Zane stepped toward her, genuinely puzzled. “What are you talking about?”

No one should have been able to tell that he’d spent the last few hours absorbing Celestial Essence from the cursed weapons.

Fiona pointed at him. “You’re smoking.”

Zane grabbed the nearest weapon from an open copper box — a large saber with a blade polished to a mirror shine. He held it up and looked at his own reflection.

Black smoke curled off his skin in thin, steady wisps.

His stomach dropped.

Absorbing Celestial Essence from cursed weapons hadn’t destroyed the curse energy—it had followed the Essence directly into his body.

The curse energy had two effects on living beings. The first was slow erosion — seeping into the physical body over time, distorting the mind, warping the personality, until a warrior lost their reason entirely. 

The second was structural: cursed weapons were roughly half as strong as they would otherwise be, because the curse degraded the material itself. Zane’s body was a Relic-Class physique. The same logic applied. If the curse energy wasn’t dealt with, his physical strength could be seriously compromised.

The first effect didn’t worry him much — his body’s immunity had held against poisons and worse. But the second was a real problem.

And there was a third concern he hadn’t voiced. If any of the Soul Sea masters on the island saw him walking around with black smoke pouring off his skin, they’d ask questions. Questions he couldn’t answer without explaining the black flame, the Soulforge, and everything connected to it. 

The only person who knew any of that was Lavender — and her soul was currently resting in a piece of Soulcradle Wood.

Fiona was still staring at him, her expression caught somewhere between alarm and fascination. “Did you eat them?” 

Zane kept his face perfectly neutral. “What?”

She gestured at the copper boxes. “The weapons. Did you actually eat them?”

“No,” he said flatly.

Fiona blinked. She didn’t push further. But the curiosity in her eyes didn’t go anywhere.

Zane exhaled slowly. He didn’t have a solution. He couldn’t explain how the smoke had gotten there, and he couldn’t make it stop on his own.

Fiona seemed to read all of this in his expression. Her alarm softened into something calmer. “I don’t know exactly what curse energy does to a person, but it can’t be good.” She paused. “But I can purify it.”

“You can purify me?” Zane asked, shocked.

“Yes,” she replied, her serious expression making it clear she wasn’t joking.

A slow smile spread across Zane’s face. “Well, if you can purify me, then…”

[AMBIENT: QUIET CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

His mind was already racing. If he had an ability like that, he’d be cleansing these weapons one by one and selling them for a fortune. But then he remembered who he was talking to—the princess of a divine kingdom. She did not need money, and these Mythic and Relic-Class weapons were probably just junk to her.

Fiona seemed to read his mind. A knowing smile touched her lips. “Don’t move,” she commanded softly. “And don’t worry. I know what I’m doing.”

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

Another milky-white flame bloomed in her hand, and Zane realized he couldn’t feel a single trace of heat from it.

“What kind of flame is this?” he asked, staring at it, completely stunned.

“This is the Purification Fire,” Fiona explained as she slowly pushed the flame toward him. “It can purify anything.”

[SFX: SOFT MAGICAL HUM]

When it touched his skin, he felt no heat at all. Instead, a strange, pleasant chill spread from the point of contact, seeping into his body.

He thought it was a miracle, a flame that felt like ice. As the chilling sensation moved through him, his mind became incredibly clear, perfectly serene. 

It wasn’t that his thoughts were gone; it was more like the fire was temporarily silencing all the background noise—every distracting impulse, every negative feeling, every selfish intention. For a moment, his mind was utterly pure. 

Of course, once the flame was gone, the usual chaos would return, but for now, there was just… quiet.
