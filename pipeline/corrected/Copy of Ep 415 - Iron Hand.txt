Ep 415 - Iron Hand

Word count: 1153

[AMBIENT: TENSE HALL ATMOSPHERE]

Zane's words lit a fuse in the hall.

[SFX: ANGRY CROWD ERUPTION]

The room exploded into a chorus of angry shouts, the air crackling with hostility. Disciples who had been lounging against pillars straightened, their faces flushing crimson. Others leaped to their feet, fists clenched so tightly their knuckles turned white.

"Son of a bitch! He's every bit as arrogant as they say. Who the hell does he think he is?"

"Let's go! Let's tear him apart!"

"Yeah! Let's wreck him!"

The shouts intensified as the disciples surged forward with raw aggression, their voices overlapping in a cacophony of rage. If looks could kill, Zane would have been dead a dozen times over—each glare sharp enough to pierce steel, each scowl twisted with genuine murderous intent.

Even Dominic, a Soul Realm master, let his expression sour at Zane's declaration. His gaze raked over Zane with the intensity of a blade scraping bone, trying to peel back the layers and see what lay beneath. He'd known something was off the first time they met—a wrongness he couldn't articulate, like staring at a perfect painting and sensing the brushstrokes didn't quite match. But he still couldn't put his finger on it. The boy's movements were too precise, his timing too perfect, his words too calculated.

He couldn't have known the truth. Since reaching the Fusion Realm, Saint Morphen's power over his puppets had become absolute. His control was so subtle, so impossibly refined, that even a Soul Realm master like Dominic could only sense a vague wrongness—a shadow at the edge of perception that vanished the moment he tried to focus on it. Never the whole picture. Never the invisible strings dancing through the air like spider silk.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane's footsteps echoed through the chamber as he strode forward. For a split second, he felt the phantom strings pulling at his limbs go slack—like a marionette suddenly cut loose. The sensation was jarring, almost disorienting. The weight of his body, the rhythm of his heartbeat—all of it returned to him in a rush.

He was in command of his own body again. Right now, though, he almost wished he wasn't.

Freedom meant responsibility. It meant facing this pack of furious elite disciples all by himself, without Saint Morphen's invisible protection. They looked ready to tear him to pieces, their faces twisted with barely restrained violence. Some had already begun circulating their qi, the air around them shimmering with deadly intent. Zane could feel the pressure building—not just the spiritual pressure of their cultivation, but the raw weight of their collective hatred pressing down on him like a physical force.

This could get very ugly very quickly.

[SFX: ENERGY AURA FLARE]

A disciple surged forward from the crowd, his aura blazing with the unmistakable power of a sixth-stage Soul Realm cultivator. The spiritual pressure rolled off him in waves, making the air itself seem to thicken. "Zane, I challenge you to a duel!"

Zane's jaw tightened. His heart hammered against his ribs, but he forced himself to breathe slowly, evenly. A faint smile touched his lips—tight, controlled, betraying none of the anxiety churning in his gut. He needed to buy time, to establish some semblance of control over this volatile situation before it spiraled into complete chaos.

He shook his head deliberately. "I'll accept your challenge," he said, his voice carrying across the silent hall despite its measured tone. "But first, I'm setting a rule."

“You’ve got to be kidding me. After all that tough talk, you need rules?”

“Weren’t you just bragging about how you could take all of us on at once? A ‘master’ like you shouldn’t need any rules, should he?”

“Quiet down!” someone called out. “Let’s hear him out.”

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

The crowd’s jeering subsided. “I’ll only accept three challenges a day,” Zane announced coolly. “And I pick my opponents.”

It was a gambit he’d invented on the spot. He couldn’t afford to alienate the entire Elite House, but he knew Tia would never let him back down. This was the only middle ground he could think of. If they refused… well, he might as well just stand there and let them beat him into the ground.

“Are you serious?” a disciple complained. “You just declared you’d challenge everyone in the Elite House, and now you’re chickening out with only three picks?”

“So what? Let him,” another scoffed. “It’s not like there are any weaklings in the Elite House.”

A few disciples glanced around, as if trying to locate their weakest link and finding no one.

“Let him choose,” another urged. “We’ll all get to see just how powerful this Adept-level prodigy really is.” A roar of agreement went through the crowd. The consensus was clear: no matter who he picked, he was going down.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

Once the hall quieted again, Zane's gaze swept across the hostile faces before him. He took his time, letting the silence stretch as he studied each disciple with deliberate care. Some met his eyes with burning defiance. Others shifted their weight, eager for violence. Finally, his attention settled on one particular disciple, a man whose reputation preceded him.

[AMBIENT: AGGRESSIVE CROWD CHANTING]

Johnny, known as “Iron Hand,” stepped into the ring. The crowd erupted.

“Come on, Johnny! Pulverize him!”

“Punch his head clean off!”

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD ROAR]

“Tear him to shreds! That’ll teach him to be arrogant again!”

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE]

Johnny’s confidence swelled with the crowd’s cheers. But then, a brilliant light flared across Zane’s body as two hundred Dragon Scales materialized. His muscles swelled, bulging with so much power that thick veins pulsed against his skin, his entire frame seeming ready to explode with raw energy.

Simultaneously, fiery energy from the Phoenix Crystal flooded his right arm, supercharging his fist with devastating speed and force.

[SFX: POWER CHARGING]

“Titan Strike!” Zane roared, throwing his punch.

[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT EXPLOSION]

Technique became irrelevant. This was now a brutal, head-on collision of pure power—strength against strength, speed against speed.

Johnny and Zane struck each other in the same instant. As their fists connected, a flicker of fear crossed Johnny’s face. Zane just smirked.

Johnny was known as “Iron Hand,” his fist said to be as tough as a Spirit Weapon. But in the end, it was still flesh and bone. Zane was another story entirely. His body was a Top-Grade Spirit Weapon, reinforced by the three Spirit Glyphs etched across his chest. He was just one step away from becoming a living Mythic-grade weapon. Against that, Johnny’s “Iron Hand” stood no chance.

Johnny’s punch landed squarely, but it only sent Zane staggering back a few steps. Instead of pain, a strange warmth bloomed in his chest, absorbing the impact.

[SFX: BODY FLYING THROUGH AIR]

Zane’s punch, however, fueled by two hundred Dragon Scales, sent Johnny flying.

[SFX: HEAVY CRASH]

[SFX: WALL IMPACT]

His massive frame crashed through several elite disciples before slamming hard into the far wall. He slumped to the floor in a broken heap, out cold.

[AMBIENT: DEAD SILENCE]

The hall plunged into a dead, stunned silence, so total it was as if the room had been instantly emptied.
