Ep 33 - Nineteen Miles
Word count: 1120
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING MARKETPLACE]
[SFX: LABORED RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
Zane furrowed his brow, conflicted about what he was witnessing. The Entrance Exam had transformed the surrounding area into a bustling marketplace, with gear rental vendors reaping substantial profits from desperate applicants. Every aspiring cultivator in The Capital frantically sought any advantage they could secure.
"But doesn't utilizing equipment like this essentially undermine the purpose of the test?" Zane inquired, his voice tinged with genuine curiosity. The Skyreach Sect had designed their examination specifically to eliminate the unworthy, which made it peculiar that they would permit weapons during such a crucial evaluation.
Ethan Grant responded with a knowing smile that crinkled the corners of his eyes, betraying his experience with the system. "Here's what you haven't grasped yet," he explained, leaning closer as if sharing a valuable secret. "The Skyreach Sect houses thousands of members, and many of them are legitimate prodigies with extraordinary talents. This little examination barely registers in their concerns. At this stage, we're not even considered pledges."
He gestured broadly at the chaotic scene around them. "They won't squander their precious time monitoring trivial matters. As long as we refrain from slaughtering one another, they remain utterly indifferent to whatever methods we employ to complete the course."
Ethan's words clicked for Zane. The Skyreach Sect might have strict rules, but those were for its actual members. This exam was just a wide net meant to filter out anyone who couldn't cut it.
[AMBIENT: ATMOSPHERIC PRESSURE]
[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]
The second Ethan finished talking, he was gasping for air. The bracelet only blocked about half the pressure, and the rest was making it a struggle just to run.
"Stop talking. Focus on your breathing," Zane told him.
[SFX: LABORED FOOTSTEPS]
Ethan nodded, his face twisting as he grit his teeth and forced himself onward, pushing his body to its absolute limit.
Zane, on the other hand, was running almost comfortably. He'd acclimated to the pressure a while back, and now he could practically ignore it.
[SFX: BODY COLLISION]
As the miles wore on, another runner suddenly slammed into Ethan, sending him stumbling. A flash of hot anger shot through Zane. He glanced at Ethan, who was fighting to stay on his feet, then turned to the man who'd hit him. "We don't have a problem with you," Zane said, his voice dangerously low. "Why'd you do that? You looking for a fight?"
The man fumbled in his pocket for a Stamina Pill. As soon as his fingers found it, he popped it in his mouth and swallowed.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
"You're right, I don't have a problem with you," he sneered. "But someone else wants you to fail."
"Who?" Zane asked, his body tense.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
The man just grinned, turned, and took off running. "You'd better just quit now," he called back over his shoulder. "Otherwise, you're not just gonna fail—you're gonna get yourself killed."
Zane stared after him, his expression grim. The pieces clicked into place. He didn't know anyone in The Capital, so the only person with a motive was Perrin.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]
He was using dirty tricks to stop Zane from ever joining the Skyreach Sect. An icy resolve settled over him. Still, this was a Skyreach Sect trial. They wouldn't dare do anything too reckless, not here.
[AMBIENT: LABORED BREATHING]
With that thought, Zane went back to Ethan, who had collapsed on the ground, his face pale as he gasped for breath. Seeing Zane approach, he struggled to sit up, but his body wouldn't cooperate. He just turned his head.
"Zane... I'm spent," Ethan wheezed. "You go on without me. I can't even sit up, let alone finish this." He looked away, staring up at the empty sky. "Bone Refining Level guys only have to run ten miles. I've only got, what, a mile or two left? If I had anything left in the tank, I could make it. But I don't."
"I'm sorry, man. This is my fault," Zane said. The attack was meant for him, but Ethan was the one paying the price.
Ethan waved a weak hand. "Forget it. Story of my life," he said, his voice laced with bitter resignation. "I was born with a bad hand. I'm used to it."
It was clear from his tone that Ethan had been through his own share of hardships.
[MUSIC: MOTIVATIONAL SWELL]
"Get up, Ethan!" Zane said, his voice firm. He wasn't about to let his friend give up.
A pained smile touched Ethan's lips as he shook his head. "I can't," he mumbled, sounding hopeless.
"How do you know if you don't even try? We train to push our bodies past their limits. Nobody gets to the top without facing this kind of crap. Get up and give it everything you've got!" Zane urged him.
The words echoed in Ethan's mind. The path of a fighter wasn't about fame or fortune. It was about pushing yourself to your absolute potential, which meant facing down any and every obstacle. It was never supposed to be easy. The whole point was to break past your limits.
Zane's words sparked something in him, and a conflict played out across Ethan's face. His usual easygoing expression hardened into something serious. Finally, he nodded. "You're right. If I give up this easily now..."
[SFX: BODY STRUGGLING, COLLAPSING]
[SFX: EXHAUSTED GROAN]
He shifted, trying to push himself up, but his muscles screamed in protest and gave out. He fell back with a groan, defeated. Zane saw it then—the will was there, but the body was broken. There was nothing more he could do.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR TRAINING COURSE]
[MUSIC: DETERMINED SOLO JOURNEY]
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
Abandoning Ethan, Zane methodically stretched his limbs, centering his thoughts. The crushing two-thousand-pound pressure diminished his speed considerably, yet his robust physique would easily handle the remaining ten miles despite this handicap.
The path stretched eerily vacant before him—a consequence of his delay with Ethan. All Bone Refining Level candidates had already completed their required distance at the ten-mile marker, while Core Strength runners had made it even further along the course. Solitude enveloped him completely.
He settled into a measured cadence, like a lone wanderer traversing an endless journey. After three miles, his breathing quickened slightly, the first hint of exertion. Two miles later, his respiration grew more pronounced, each breath becoming deliberate. By the eighteen-mile mark, his lungs seared with each painful inhalation, the invisible weight pressing against his chest like a vise.
Narrowing his eyes against the distance, Zane glimpsed his destination ahead. Victory in this exam seemed assured, a certainty within his grasp.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]
Then, with merely one mile remaining, he spotted them.
[SFX: OMINOUS FOOTSTEPS]
Four imposing silhouettes materialized across his path, waiting with predatory patience. Their poised stances radiated controlled power—unmistakably cultivators at the Marrow Refining Level.
Three faces were unfamiliar, but the fourth sent a flash of recognition through Zane—the burly assailant who had deliberately knocked Ethan down earlier, his smug expression now twisted into anticipation.
