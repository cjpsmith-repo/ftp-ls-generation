Ep 1127 - Destroy The Statues

Word count: 963

[MUSIC: HIGH TENSION]
The spear tip pointing at Zane’s throat glinted coldly, but he didn’t even blink. 
[SFX: METAL SCRAPE]
He just reached out, pinched the razor-sharp point between his thumb and forefinger, and offered a small, disarming smile. “I’m not looking for anyone.”
The Demon Night women hesitated. Their job was usually just to scare off trespassers, not kill them. They were here on a trade mission, after all, and killing potential customers was bad for business.
“If you’re not looking for anyone, then leave,” the lead guard warned, her eyes narrowing. But something was off. The man wasn’t nervous in the slightest.
She expected him to back down. 
Instead, Zane casually pushed her spear aside. “I’m not looking for anyone,” he agreed. “But I’m not leaving, either.”
“Then you’re looking to die!” she snarled. This was the heart of their territory; they couldn’t just let some human waltz in and cause trouble. 
[SFX: SPEAR THRUST]
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT DRUMS]
A warrior of the Soul Sea Realm, she put her entire weight into the strike, a thrust that should have punched straight through him. 
[SFX: SHARP METAL CATCH]
But the spear traveled no more than three inches before it stopped dead, caught between two of Zane’s fingers. He was still smiling.
Her eyes went wide. She threw all her strength into wrenching the spear back, but it wouldn’t budge. It was like the weapon was fused to his hand, caught in an unbreakable vice.
Seeing their leader pinned, two other guards leaped from the branches above to join the fray. 
[SFX: WHOOSH MOVEMENT]
But just as they descended, Zane released the spear, took a single step, and shot toward the great tree at the center of the clearing.
“Stop him!” one of them screamed.
As their weapons swung at his back, Zane flicked his wrist without even looking, and the attacks were swatted away with a deafening clang. His face was calm, but his eyes held a deadly focus. Lavender’s words echoed in his mind: this was the first step, the most important one for her revenge.
[SFX: HEAVY WOODEN DOORS SMASHING]
[SFX: WOOD SPLINTERING]
With a sound like thunder, Zane crashed straight through the heavy wooden doors at the base of the Oaken Pavilion.
[SFX: SHOUTS FROM INSIDE]
“Someone’s broken into the Oaken Pavilion!”
“Stop him!”
The entire Violetwood Sanctuary settlement erupted. Demon Night cultivators came pouring in from every direction — but Zane was already moving.
[SFX: VINES SLICING AIR]
Three emerald vines whipped toward him from the left. He stepped sideways through the space between them, his body slipping through the gap as if the air itself had folded around him, and kept running.
A broadsword taller than a person came swinging from the right — he flipped over it without breaking stride.
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS ON WOOD]
The second floor. The third. Zane moved through the Oaken Pavilion like water, weaving between six or seven Empyrean Realm cultivators who came at him from every angle — faster, more agile than any of them expected from a human. Every attack landed a moment too late, cutting air where he’d just been.
[SFX: BREAKING WOOD]
He burst through to the top floor.
A soft voice cut through the chaos.
“Stop.”
[SFX: PINK ROBES IN FLIGHT]
A figure in pink robes descended from above, coming to rest between Zane and the far end of the room. Her feet barely touched the floor. Her expression was calm — not the hot fury of the guards below, but something colder and more considered. The pressure she carried was in a different category entirely.
[SFX: CHAIN CUTTING SPACE]
When Zane didn’t stop, she raised two fingers. A flying shuttle shot from her wrist, trailing a dark green chain that seemed to fold through space — and in the same instant it vanished, it reappeared coiled around Zane’s legs, binding him to the floor with invisible force.
He lurched to a halt.
But his sword was already out.
[SFX: NETHER GOD SHADOWS ERUPTING]
Six shadows burst from the blade simultaneously and launched themselves at the far wall — at the three statues standing there.
[SFX: SHUTTLE CUTTING AIR]
The pink-robed woman’s wrist snapped again. The shuttle tore back through space, shredding through shadow after shadow — one, two, three, four, five.
Two got through.
[SFX: STONE STATUES SHATTERING]
Inside, two sharp cracks rang out as the shadows struck home.
The likenesses of the Queen of Life and the Queen of Punishment lay in fragments on the floor.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
The pink-robed woman — Nyla, the Violetwood Sanctuary’s ranking warrior in Auspice City — stared at the wreckage. A moment ago, she had simply intended to punish a trespasser. Now her eyes were cold with killing intent.
She had spent a fraction of a second wondering what a human could possibly want in the Oaken Pavilion — no treasures, no secrets. Now she understood. And the understanding made it worse.

[SFX: FURIOUS SCREAMING VOICES]
“Kill him! Kill him!”
“How dare he destroy the queens’ statues!”
“He deserves to die!”
[AMBIENT: TENSE CHAMBER SILENCE]
The other warriors who had stormed in after him froze, their own shock turning to rage as they saw the sacred fragments scattered across the floor.
Nyla didn’t hesitate. 
[SFX: CRACKLING LIGHTNING SPHERES]
With a scream, she launched two deadly flying shuttles at Zane, each one trailing a crackling ball of blue lightning. The spheres of lightning were no bigger than his fist, but Zane felt a jolt of genuine alarm at the sheer power radiating from them.
[SFX: CALM VOICE]
“Fifth-layer Thunder Law,” Zane noted, his voice deceptively calm. “Alright, Lavender. Now would be a good time to step in, unless you want me to actually get into trouble.”
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
[SFX: SHADOW MATERIALIZING]
[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSH]
The words had barely left his lips when a blood-red shadow peeled away from his body, solidifying into a distinct figure. A moment later, the Nightshard Spear shot from Zane’s Storage Ring and slapped into the newcomer’s waiting hand.
[SFX: METAL CLANG DOUBLE STRIKE]
With two fast, metallic strikes, the red figure effortlessly swatted the lightning-charged shuttles aside.
Nyla, who was already rushing forward for another attack, felt her fury spike at the sight. But then her charge faltered.
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED STILLNESS]
Her expression dissolved from rage into pure, unadulterated shock as she finally got a clear look at the figure standing beside Zane.
