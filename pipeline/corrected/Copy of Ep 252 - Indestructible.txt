Ep 252 - Indestructible
Word count: 1179
[AMBIENT: MARTIAL ARTS ARENA]
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
[SFX: WIND BLADES]
Horror gripped the spectators as Cecil’s massive wind blades hurtled toward Zane with devastating force. The atmosphere pulsated with murderous energy. Throughout the stands, disciples from various peaks leaned forward in their seats, eyes widening in anticipation of what seemed inevitable—the brutal demise of Misty’s unexpected champion.
"Zane, just throw in the towel!" Master Warren shouted, his voice cracking with genuine concern. He clutched the railing before him, knuckles white with tension. Beside him, Misty’s disciples had abandoned all pretense of stoicism—some covered their eyes, others whispered desperate prayers. Victory in the competition had become meaningless; Zane’s survival was all that mattered now.
But Zane ignored their pleas. His eyes, focused and unnervingly calm, reflected an inner resolve that none could comprehend. Without hesitation, he charged directly into the howling maelstrom of wind blades.
The jagged, impossibly thick blades converged upon him from every angle, their edges gleaming with lethal promise. Time seemed to slow as they swarmed toward him, the air between them and their target vanishing in an instant of terrible certainty.
[SFX: VIOLENT WIND BLADES]
With a sickening shredding sound, the blades struck, sinking into his body. His brand-new robe instantly disintegrated, exploding outward like a violent firecracker and scattering a shower of tiny fabric scraps throughout the arena.
[SFX: FABRIC TEARING EXPLOSION]
"It’s too late. He must have been torn to shreds," whispered a disciple, watching the delicate fabric confetti drift and settle in the aftermath. But as the dust cleared and visibility returned, his jaw dropped involuntarily. A naked figure still hovered defiantly in midair.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]
The spectators could make out an almost perfect physique—smooth skin gleaming with a healthy, metallic sheen, powerfully built muscles flowing harmoniously across his frame, and radiating an unmistakable aura of strength.
Upon closer examination, however, the astonished onlookers could see faint white lines etched across his skin—ghostly traces where the deadly blades had attempted but failed to penetrate his extraordinary flesh.
[AMBIENT: AWESTRUCK CROWD]
"I don’t believe it! How did he survive that attack without a scratch?" a disciple exclaimed, his eyes wide with disbelief.
He wasn’t the only one. A ripple of shock spread through the crowd as everyone stared, dumbfounded.
Even Sir Kenneth, who never seemed to care about anything, shot to his feet, his eyes locked on Zane.
The female disciples from Maiden’s Peak had been staring silently at the naked Zane, but then their gazes drifted downward. Their faces instantly flushed bright red as they let out little shrieks and spun away. They covered their eyes with their hands, but still couldn’t resist peeking through their fingers.
One of them, however, just kept staring. A slow smile played on Tracy’s lips, her eyes filled with excitement. "Well, Patrick," she said, "looks like all your bragging was for nothing. I bet even your Titan’s Guard can’t hold a candle to his natural skin."
Patrick trained under the philosophy of Inner Peace, and his mental calm was his greatest asset. He lived by the creed "to not be swayed by gains or losses" and could always keep his composure. But right now, the surprise in his eyes was impossible to hide. Hearing Tracy's words, he let out a self-deprecating laugh. "You’re right. I guess I underestimated him. That guy’s body is way stronger than I ever imagined. I have no idea how he did it."
No one was more stunned than Cecil. He had watched Zane fly straight into the blades, certain it was over. House Sterling had promised him a reward — a precious pill, one even sect elders rarely obtained — if he killed Zane here. He had been so close.
But Zane had walked out without a scratch. Cecil's jaw tightened. There was one option left — a forbidden technique, a move that would damage his own essence and blood to break through that defense. He hesitated. He had never fully controlled it. One wrong execution and he'd hurt himself worse than his opponent.
He was still deciding when the eye of the storm collapsed.
The two cyclones had finally torn each other apart. Wind blades erupted in every direction — including inward. Cecil spun, tracking the currents, but there was no safe ground anymore. His own technique was hunting him.
[SFX: WIND BLADES CLOSING IN]
Dodge the blades — take Zane's fist. Dodge Zane — take the blades. His survival instinct made the choice for him.
A gust caught Zane and pushed him straight toward Cecil. He didn't resist it. Fists were enough.
Cecil stepped into the open to avoid a wind blade cutting from behind — and Zane's punch was already there.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
[SFX: BODY FLYING THROUGH AIR] 
Cecil’s body hurtled through the air like a discarded toy, spinning wildly from the force of Zane’s devastating blow. The audience gasped collectively as they witnessed the Soul Realm disciple reduced to a helpless ragdoll, his previous confidence shattered in an instant.
[SFX: BODY SLAMMING INTO BARRIER]
With a sickening thud, he crashed against the shimmering curtain of the enchanted barrier. The magical field rippled violently upon impact, absorbing some of the force yet still leaving Cecil broken. His limbs splayed awkwardly as he slid slowly to the ground, leaving a faint trail along the barrier’s surface.
Cecil quickly lost consciousness. His eyelids fluttered briefly, a final glimmer of disbelief crossing his features before darkness claimed him. The arena fell into stunned silence as the once-proud disciple from a prestigious peak lay defeated, his reputation shattered as thoroughly as his fighting spirit.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMURS]
[MUSIC: VICTORY THEME]
The referee rushed into the arena. With a simple wave of his hand, he suppressed the chaotic cyclone of wind. Then he announced, "The winner of this match is Zane from Misty Peak."
After being declared the winner, Zane calmly stepped off the field.
The moment he saw Zane leave, Master Warren ran over and handed him a robe. Zane quickly pulled it on.
"Oh, Zane, you’re a miracle! You beat Cecil! That was incredible!" Master Warren said, unable to contain his excitement.
Zane had beaten strong opponents before, but Cecil was on another level. None of his previous opponents were ranked anywhere near that high on the Skyreach Sect’s List. Cecil was in the Soul Realm and ranked eighty-third on the list.
And now that Zane had beaten him, his place among the top one hundred disciples was guaranteed.
Just as Eliot had predicted, Zane might actually turn things around for Misty Peak.
What made it even more amazing was that Zane was only seventeen and at Grade Two of the Adept Realm.
It was a true miracle for someone at Grade Two of the Adept Realm to make it into the Top One Hundred. Most fighters in The Eastern Territory, after all, only started seriously cultivating their Vitality after breaking into the Adept Realm.
If Zane could just reach the Soul Realm, Master Warren thought, he could climb to the very top of the list. He might even take first place in the next All Peaks Competition. Master Warren figured it wouldn’t be long now.
But he had no idea that Zane had no intention of waiting for the next competition. He was aiming to take first place this year, at his current level: Grade Two of the Adept Realm.
