Ep 835 - A Strange Request

Word count: 1074

[AMBIENT: GRAND THRONE ROOM]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Zane couldn't make sense of the gentle smile on War King Wind’s face. Why would a man that powerful be so polite to him? He was a nobody.

But he pushed the question aside. For now, he was just grateful. The Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom had stood by him time and again, so Zane bowed deeply. “Thank you, Your Majesty.”

The Emperor shook his head. “No, there's no need for that. I should be the one thanking you. You fought for us, and on behalf of everyone in the Harlen Divine Kingdom, I thank you.”

The fight with the Little Monster was over. Zane had won. But it was more than just a victory; it was a statement. 

He had absorbed a full-powered strike from a Soul Sea Realm master, an impossible feat that sent a clear message. After a display like that, no warrior from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm would dare challenge him—not unless they were looking for a spectacular humiliation. And since the whole thing had just been an evaluation with no real stakes, his new reputation was the only thing that truly mattered.

A smile played on Lord Randy’s lips. “Father, since Zane won, what's his reward?”

“A reward! Of course,” the Emperor chuckled, turning to Zane. “I was told you're in urgent need of Ancient Heartstones. Is that right? We have a few left in the treasury.”

“Thank you, Your Majesty,” Zane cut in before the Emperor could finish, “but I don't need them right now.”

The Emperor’s offer was generous, but Zane knew the kingdom's supply of Ancient Heartstones was low. Most were used as soon as they were found, and a handful of stones wouldn't be enough to save Tia. He’d have to search for more himself in the Sacred Feather Sea.

[MUSIC: URGENT TENSION]

But that was a problem for later. Right now, his mind was on Lavender. She had fallen into a deep sleep, her soul flickering like a candle in the wind, threatening to go out at any moment. He had to act fast, and for that, he needed Soulcradle Wood.

[AMBIENT: THRONE ROOM]

The Emperor looked surprised. “Are you sure? I was under the impression you were desperately seeking them.” Now Zane was turning them down without a second thought. He couldn’t figure it out. “If not the heartstones, then what can I offer you?”

Zane clasped his hands together. “Your Majesty, I need a piece of Soulcradle Wood.”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The Emperor's eyebrows rose. Soulcradle Wood was only useful for containing souls — a niche purpose that made it practically useless for most warriors. He didn't understand why Zane would want it. More to the point, he didn't have any.

“I'm sorry,” the Emperor said. “There is none in our kingdom.”

Zane had expected the answer. He turned.

His gaze moved to War King Wind.

“As far as I know,” Zane said carefully, “there should be a piece of Soulcradle Wood in the Wind Divine Kingdom.”

[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]

[MUSIC: TENSE PIANO]

War King Wind went very still. The wood had come through the Reverse Spirit Channel — a closely guarded secret. Ordinary people had no way of knowing it existed, let alone where it was kept. Even the Harlen Emperor looked surprised.

Wind's eyes settled on Zane, quiet and measuring. “Is that so? I'll give it to you. But first — answer me one thing.”

Zane's jaw tightened.

“How did you know I had it? Who told you?”

[MUSIC: UNEASY STRINGS]

Zane said nothing. He couldn't tell Wind the truth — that having merged with the world's will, he existed in a state of near-omniscience, that there were no secrets in this world he couldn't access in an instant. 

He reached into that vast river of memory now, searching frantically for a cover. Who else knew about the wood?

Almost no one. The Holy Maiden had passed it to Wind personally. Only two or three people knew of its existence.

He couldn't use the Holy Maiden's name. That lie would unravel immediately.

He was running out of options. He glanced over — and found Fiona already watching him, her expression unreadable.

“What?” Wind pressed, a faint smile on his face. “You can't answer a simple question?”

Zane clenched his teeth.

Then Fiona's voice cut through the silence. “Father, I'm the one who told him.”

[MUSIC: SHOCKED REVELATION]

“What?” War King Wind stared at his daughter, completely floored. “You told him? When?”

“Just a moment ago,” Fiona said, her smile calm and unwavering. “When he asked his king about it, I whispered the information to him with my Vitality.”

War King Wind’s gaze shifted to Zane, sharp and questioning. “Is that right?”

It was a flimsy excuse, but it was a lifeline. Zane wasn't about to let it go. He nodded immediately. “Yes. Your daughter told me you had a piece of Soulcradle Wood.”

The story was a stretch, but if they both stuck to it, it might just hold.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]

Of course, War King Wind didn't believe his daughter for a second. But he was more curious about Zane's secret than he was interested in exposing a lie. Since Fiona had stepped in, he wouldn't press the issue. 

After all, the wood was only useful to a soul without a body — something most living warriors preferred not to think about. Rare as it was, it wasn't the kind of treasure anyone fought over.

[SFX: MYSTICAL RESONANCE]

He reached into his Storage Ring. What emerged looked, at first glance, like nothing — a brown, withered branch, dry and unremarkable. But the veins running through it were wrong. Looking at them too long made something in the chest pull, as if the wood were calling softly to whatever lived inside.

With a small, unreadable expression, he passed the Soulcradle Wood to Fiona.

Fiona crossed the space between them and held the wood out to Zane.

“Thank you,” he breathed, his voice thick with gratitude.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]

He took it, his hands trembling slightly with relief. Lavender. He could save her now.

Fiona offered him a soft, fleeting smile before turning and rejoining her father.

War King Wind had watched the entire exchange, his eyes missing nothing.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FLUTE]

Now he tilted his head back, gazing up at the sky, his thoughts a complete mystery to everyone around him.
