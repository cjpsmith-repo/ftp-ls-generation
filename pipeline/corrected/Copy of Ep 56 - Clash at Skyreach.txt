Ep 56 - Clash at Skyreach
Word count: 1228
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Tristan's patience had worn thin.
[SFX: ANGRY FOOTSTEPS]
He stomped right up to Zane and snarled, "Zane, you promised me a fight in the battle ring today. What took you so long? If you're going to back out, I'll let you off the hook. All you have to do is kneel and admit I'm better than you. After that, you can get the hell out of the Skyreach Sect and go crawl back home like the coward you are!"
Zane just blinked, putting on a look of pure innocence. "Well, I did agree to fight you today," he said coolly. "But last I checked, 'today' lasts twenty-four hours. Did I ever give you a specific time?"
Zane's smart-ass reply only made Tristan see red. He shot back, "You think you're clever, spouting all that nonsense. But all your talk won't save you once the fight starts."
"Before we start, I have a request," Zane said, his tone flat.
"A request? What do you want?" Tristan scoffed. "And I'm telling you right now, we're sticking to Sect rules! The winner isn't held responsible if the other guy dies or gets crippled. That's how it is!"
He was already planning to kill Zane, so there was no point in promising to go easy on him.
[MUSIC: STRATEGIC INTRIGUE]
Zane shook his head, a slight frown on his face. "No, I get it. We're supposed to beat each other senseless. I know the rules. It's just... a straight fight is boring. I was thinking we could raise the stakes. Put something on the line. Winner takes all."
Zane reached into his Storage Ring and produced a pile of Crystal Cubes — one hundred of them, stacked in his palm, catching the light. 
Around the ring, the watching disciples went quiet. For most of the outer disciples at Misty Peak, one hundred Crystal Cubes was a fortune. 
"One hundred Crystal Cubes against a Spirit Weapon," Zane said. "You win, the Cubes are yours. I win, the weapon is mine."
The request caught Tristan completely off guard.
He never imagined Zane would ask for something like that. Once the shock faded, Tristan wondered what kind of game Zane was playing. The outcome was a sure thing, anyway. Zane was a dead man the second he stepped into that ring.
Tristan decided to humor him, if only to get the fight started. He couldn't believe Zane's nerve, making a bet.
Where was all this confidence coming from? Did the fool actually think he could win? Zane was only at the Core Strength Level, while Tristan was already halfway to becoming a High.
[AMBIENT: FLASHBACK]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC SHIFT]
Tristan's confidence flickered. He didn't have a Spirit Weapon. He scanned the crowd and his eyes landed on Julian Croft.
"Julian. Lend me your blade. Just let me borrow it for a bit. You'll get it right back," Tristan said, acting like borrowing a mysterious weapon from Julian Croft was no big deal.
To his surprise, Julian just shook his head. "Nope."
"What? You think this loser is actually going to beat me?" Tristan snapped, furious that anyone would dare refuse him.
Julian shook his head again. "Nah, I'm sure you won't lose. But what's in it for me?"
"You..." It finally clicked for Tristan.
He clamped his mouth shut, let out a heavy sigh, and rolled his eyes. "Fine. Half the Crystal Cubes are yours when I'm done with him. Deal? Now, are you gonna lend me that legendary arcane weapon or not?"
Only then, after Tristan offered him a cut, did Julian crack a satisfied smile. "Should've just led with that. Here, catch!"
With their negotiation over, Julian unhooked a saber from his waist and tossed it to Tristan like it was nothing—not some legendary arcane weapon.
[SFX: BLADE TOSSED, CAUGHT]
Tristan snatched the blade out of the air. Deep down, he knew that was Julian all over: always looking for his cut.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
A sharp, ringing sound echoed as Tristan drew the double-edged sword from its sheath.
[SFX: MAGICAL PULSING]
The three-foot blade pulsed with a purple light. "How's this for your wager, Zane?" Tristan taunted. "It's a Grade A, Level one weapon. They call it the Indigo Blade."
After a quick glance at the Indigo Blade, Zane simply nodded. He turned to Ethan and said, "Hold on to the Crystal Cubes for me."
[SFX: VAULTING IMPACT]
With that, Zane vaulted into the battle ring.
[SFX: LEAP IMPACT]
Seeing him finally in place, a thrill shot through Tristan, though he kept his face a mask of indifference. His only fear had been Zane backing out. Tristan shoved the Indigo Blade at Ethan and leaped into the ring after him.
In the ring, Zane stood tall, his fists resting casually on his hips.
The energy inside him began to slowly circulate.
To a strong candidate like Tristan, Zane looked almost comically relaxed. But beneath that calm exterior, he was on high alert. Anyone who'd reached Tristan's level possessed a killer instinct.
[SFX: THUNDER CRACKLE]
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
Tristan was done waiting; he wanted to end this fast. The second he was in the ring, he sneered. With a crackle of thunder, streaks of lightning flashed at his feet, enveloping his body in pure energy. He shot straight toward Zane.
Tristan was using Thunderstrike, a powerful upgrade path.
The technique allowed him to generate Life Energy with the speed and force of a lightning bolt—a truly incredible sight.
[SFX: LIGHTNING STRIKE]
As Tristan closed the distance, looming larger and larger, Zane held his ground. When Tristan was just six feet away, his fist transformed into a massive streak of raging lightning. In the blink of an eye, it slammed right at Zane's head.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Tristan was an expert at channeling his Life Energy into a thunderbolt. The sheer power of the blow was enough to leave Zane dazed.
But even as Tristan's fist flew at him, Zane didn't flinch. Instead, he tightened his own fists.
On impact, streaks of purple light flared across his body as he met the Thunderstrike head-on with the Shadow Fist—a move from his own Purple Flash Fist technique.
The crowd watched, stunned, their jaws hanging open. The power for such a technique was drawn from the very marrow in a process known as Core purification.
A successful purification could completely transform the body, granting the user a massive boost in speed, strength, and senses.
Zane recognized the skill. Evil Jace had used it on him once before, and it had put Zane in a tough spot. He hadn't been able to even see his opponent then, and he couldn't now.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACT]
Another Thunderstrike slammed into Zane. This time, he dodged just enough that the attack only grazed his thigh, a powerful bolt of lightning wrapping around his leg. Luckily, Zane's body was solid, tough enough to absorb the force without any real damage.
[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACKLE]
"Can't keep up, can you?" Tristan taunted, clearly relishing the chance to pummel Zane like a punching bag. "Let's see how long you can take this!"
Tristan lunged at Zane repeatedly, each strike accompanied by a savage taunt.
"Quite the dodger, aren't you?"
"How much longer can your little dance last?"
"Stand and fight, coward!"
[MUSIC: PREDATORY TENSION]
A lethal game unfolded—predator stalking prey with merciless precision.
The crowd watched, transfixed, as Zane evaded death by mere inches. Yet with each passing moment, the space between them narrowed, Tristan's blade gleaming with hungry anticipation.
The question wasn't if the mouse would escape the cat—but what would happen when those razor claws finally connected.
