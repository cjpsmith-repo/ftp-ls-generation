Ep 1009 - Straight Down

Word count: 1081

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

He wasn’t exactly thrilled about being stuck with hard labor right after reaching the Upper World, but Zane wasn’t the type to hold a grudge.

If he’d let Yolande bring him over, or even just tagged along with the Demi-Immortals, he wouldn’t be in this mess.

But he’d wanted to make it on his own, and this was the price. He just had to deal with whatever came his way and keep pushing forward.

[SFX: WHOOSH OF FLIGHT STOPPING]

Zane flew for a while before he noticed Freya was still tailing him.

[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]

He stopped and turned around. “Why are you following me?”

The woman offered a small, knowing smile. “Oh, no reason, Zane. I just have a feeling it’d be good luck to mine with you.”

“You didn’t ask if I was okay with that,” Zane said, his voice low.

His words seemed to catch her off guard.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FLUTE]

Honestly, you just didn’t see a Life-and-Death Realm warrior talk that way to a Soul Sea Realm master. You’d think someone at his level would be desperate to team up with a master, so why did Zane seem so completely uninterested?

The woman’s smile returned. “Alright. Well, do you mind if I follow you?”

Zane just shrugged. “Do what you want.”

[SFX: WHOOSH OF MOVEMENT]

Zane shot off toward the mountain range without a backward glance.

[AMBIENT: MINE ENTRANCE]

[MUSIC: TENSE DESCENT]

Zane picked a mine at random and dropped into the tunnel. The walls were scored with centuries of excavation marks — every useful vein in the upper levels had been stripped long ago. He didn’t slow down. Freya followed, watching him push deeper without hesitation, wondering if he was really heading straight for the bottom.

The cave walls gradually shifted from dark brown rock to translucent quartz, faint glimmers of embedded ore catching the dim light. It was quieter here. Deeper.

Then Freya felt it.

Something was moving above them — silent, no aura, clinging to the ceiling in the dark. She opened her mouth to warn him.

[SFX: SWORD FLASH]

She didn’t get the words out. Without turning around, without breaking stride, Zane’s hand moved once. The Streamer was in his grip and gone again in the same motion — a single backward slash that cut through the darkness like it knew exactly where to go.

[SFX: BODIES DROPPING]

Silence.

“Just level-ten beasts,” Zane said, still walking.

Freya stared at the dark shapes crumpling behind them. Level-ten beasts — equivalent to Life-and-Death Realm warriors. Dispatched without a glance, mid-stride, before she’d even finished sensing them. She closed her mouth.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]

Whatever this man’s cultivation level said about him, his true strength was something else entirely.

She’d pegged him as just some Life-and-Death Realm warrior, but an arrogant one at that. It made her think he must have some serious skills to back it up. Her gut told her he was special—and that following him would be very good for her luck.

Over the years, plenty of warriors had gone into the mountain range and come back loaded with treasure. Of course, plenty more never came back at all, their bodies left to the elements on the mountainsides.

[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WINDS]

The range was made up of over a hundred massive mountains, each soaring a hundred thousand feet into the sky. They were dotted with mine shafts of all sizes, carved out over generations by the warriors who’d come before.

Zane had already gathered some intel on where to find the Aether crystals in these mountains.

The distribution was straightforward: low-grade stones in the upper layers, mid-grade in the middle, top-grade deeper down, and Sovereign Stones only at the very bottom. The danger scaled the same way — the upper and middle layers were manageable for Soul Sea Realm warriors, but the lower levels were a different story.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS EXPLORATION]

The upper layers showed it clearly. Every wall was scored with old chisel marks — generations of miners had been through here, stripping every viable vein they could reach. Top-grade stones in the middle layers were increasingly scarce for the same reason. There was no point in stopping. Zane pushed straight for the bottom.

If a mountain range like this were discovered in the Lower World, or even in the Heartlands, it would be considered an unbelievably rich mine.

But the Soul Sea Realm masters here didn’t bother with low-grade Aether Crystals. They needed them in such massive quantities—we’re talking trillions—that picking up a few strays was a complete waste of time.

But when Freya walked over to a seemingly ordinary wall, her face lit up. “It’s not just one crystal,” she exclaimed. “It looks like there’s a whole vein growing in here!”

When Zane got closer, he peered through the translucent quartz wall and saw a huge cluster of supreme Aether crystals hidden inside. There had to be several hundred of them.

“See? I told you! My gut is never wrong. I knew my luck would turn if I stuck with you,” Freya said with a grin. “Let’s split them, fifty-fifty!”

Zane just gave a noncommittal nod.

In fact, Freya’s hunch was spot on. Back when Zane was in The Heartlands, people always talked about his insane luck. The consensus was that good fortune just followed the guy around.

A warrior like Maddox had also had great luck, but his was the kind that leeched off the people around him to fuel his own power. That’s what had happened with Marilyn and Simon. He’d drained their good fortune, and in the end, Zane was the one who ended up having to kill them.

Zane’s luck, on the other hand, seemed to spill over onto those around him, like Tia, Cora, and even the portly merchants from the World Merchants’ Guild.

Freya had the right idea because she was sensitive to that kind of energy. It wasn’t something you could prove, so she’d learned to trust her intuition.

[SFX: ICE BLAST]

[SFX: SHATTERING CRYSTAL]

As a warrior who practiced ice law, she leaned to the side and casually flicked her hand. A blast of sharp, freezing air shot out and spread across the quartz, flash-freezing the entire cave wall. The intense cold made the incredibly hard stone brittle, and countless cracks spiderwebbed across its surface.

When she gently reached out and touched it, the wall shattered instantly, revealing several hundred supreme Aether crystals sparkling within.
