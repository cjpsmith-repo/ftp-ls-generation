Ep 1181 - Transformation

Word count: 1042

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
It’s hard to comprehend the kind of power a Supreme Lord wields. If they all cut loose at once, they could probably shatter the entire Star of Golden Sand in a blink. But this wasn’t a fight to the death, and you could tell. They were holding back, even as they all fought over the Elysian Keep.
Still, the power radiating off them was on a scale no ordinary person could even imagine.
[SFX: FRUSTRATED ROAR]
As Addison let out a roar of frustrated shock, the other Supreme Lords made their move, all lunging for the miniature Elysian Keep resting on the desert floor.
Each of them had come with their own agenda. But the Queen of Punishment had walked straight in and attacked without a word of warning, and that changed everything. She had made herself the problem.
The Queen of Punishment’s eyes narrowed. 
[SFX: MASSIVE THUNDER CRACK]
[SFX: ELECTRICAL VORTEX SWIRLING]
She raised her Saint Thunder Meteor Hammer, and with a single, almost gentle shake, unleashed a bolt of lightning a hundred thousand feet long. Countless tendrils of electricity swirled around her, creating a massive vortex. The raw power humming in that bolt—the mastery of thunder—was almost absolute, the kind of force even another Supreme Lord would have to respect.
The others’ faces tightened with a mix of shock and anger. The Queen of Punishment had just attacked without a shred of hesitation, and the raw audacity of it caught them all off guard.
Everyone was rattled. Everyone except the Queen, who remained perfectly calm, focused on her prize. She wasn’t about to let anyone else snatch what she came for.
[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]
But when the lightning finally faded, she looked down, stunned. The miniature Elysian Keep was gone.
*How childish,* she thought, a flicker of amusement crossing her mind. *Thinking you can just run away.* Using some cheap spatial trick to escape from this many Supreme Lords was a complete fantasy.
She raised a hand. 
[SFX: MASSIVE CRACK SPLITTING EARTH]
Instantly, a massive crack split the edge of the desert, circling the entire sandy expanse in less than a second. 
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING EARTHQUAKE]
A deep rumble shook the planet as the desert, the soil, the bedrock, and even the planetary mantle itself were torn free and lifted into the air. From space, it must have looked like a giant using a massive ladle to scoop a chunk right out of the Star of Golden Sand.
The move left a gaping pit thousands of miles deep.
[SFX: ENERGY WAVE SWEEP]
As the Queen held the continent-sized landmass suspended in the void, she swept it with another wave of power from her Saint Thunder Meteor Hammer. 
[SFX: HAMMER DRILLING INTO ROCK]
She swung the Hammer again — driving it directly into the heart of the floating rock. Another violent bolt erupted on impact, tearing through the desert from the inside out.
[SFX: SMALL OBJECT SHOOTING FREE]
The miniature Elysian Keep shot out from the disintegrating mass. The Queen’s lip curled. She stepped forward, giving chase.
But the other Supreme Lords had been waiting for exactly this moment. In an instant, several tore through space and converged on the Keep from every direction.
[SFX: TRANSFORMATION HUM]
At that exact moment, burrowing deep inside the floating rock, the Elysian Keep changed its shape, transforming into a simple, palm-sized stone.
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
Just as it settled into its new form, another Supreme Lord made his play. 
The Queen moved to intercept — but she wasn’t the only one with a plan. Three others closed on her at once.
[SFX: ROARING RING OF FIRE]
A blood-red ring of fire materialized in one Supreme Lord’s hand. The moment it was unleashed, the heat it gave off was extraordinary — cracks split the surrounding space, the void itself unable to withstand it. The ring swept straight toward the Queen.
Another Supreme Lord moved with eerie calm. He raised his hand, connected his thumb and index finger, and fixed his gaze on the Queen. He said one word: “Crack.”
[SFX: SHARP CRACK]
A strange fracture appeared in the space around her — spreading fast, closing on her body. It tore straight through her Pool of Faith. A bloody wound split open across her right shoulder.
[SFX: ARMOR FLARING — DENSE LIGHT PULSE]
The Saint Sky Armor blazed. A dense cascade of light erupted from it, halting the crack before it could spread further. The armor had saved her — barely.
But the ring of fire was already closing in, and two more Supreme Lords moved to flank her. Four on one was too much even for the Queen. She had no choice but to fall back.
In the moment her attention broke, the miniature Elysian Keep burrowed back down and disappeared into another stretch of desert below.
The remaining Supreme Lords watched it go — and none of them panicked. They all understood the same thing: the Keep couldn’t leave the planet. No matter how it hid, it could not escape all of them. What mattered now was making sure it didn’t fall into someone else’s hands first.
[SFX: ROARING FLAMES IGNITION]
One of the others copied the Queen’s move, seizing control of the massive landmass himself before engulfing it in searing flames.
[SFX: SAND CRYSTALLIZING AND MELTING]
The fire was so hot that the desert sand fused into countless transparent crystals, which then immediately began to melt. 
[SFX: OBJECTS TUMBLING AND FALLING]
Soon, the hundred-mile stretch of desert was almost completely incinerated, with only a few scattered items tumbling out of the molten slurry and falling back toward the massive crater below.
Disguised as just another piece of debris, the Elysian Keep tumbled through the air before landing quietly at the bottom of the pit.
The Supreme Lord, who had burned a continent, had no idea that the very thing he was looking for had just slipped right through his fingers.
[SFX: CONFUSED GRUNT]
“Huh?” A flicker of confusion crossed his face as he stared at his empty hands. He’d almost had it. How could it have just disappeared? He’d scorched the entire landmass, and there was nothing.
“There were no spatial fluctuations,” another Supreme Lord observed calmly. “The Keep is still on the planet.”
“Hmph. I know it’s still here.” 
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DIVINATION]
[SFX: FINGERS TRACING PATTERNS IN AIR]
Another’s fingers danced in the air, weaving invisible patterns as he tried to divine the artifact’s location. Finding it should have been simple enough, as long as no one was actively carrying the Crown of Destiny. And at this range, it should have been even easier.
The diviner suddenly looked back toward the Star of Golden Sand, his brow furrowed. 
“It’s definitely still on the planet,” he muttered, “but… what just happened?”
