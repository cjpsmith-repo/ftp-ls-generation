Ep 153 - Fish Food
Word Count: 1151

[AMBIENT: CRYSTAL CAUSEWAY]
[MUSIC: TENSE DANGER]
The crystal causeway gleamed with deceptive serenity, its translucent surface masking lethal danger beneath. Within moments of stepping onto the shimmering path, the entire group sensed the threat—just as they had when the Abyssal Pikes had claimed their first victims at the water's edge. 
Eyes widened in recognition, bodies tensed, and without a word, they abandoned their advance, frantically retreating toward the shore they had so confidently left behind.
[SFX: SPLASHING WATER]
Their proximity to the shore allowed a swift retreat, but they couldn't outpace the lurking threat beneath.
[SFX: VIOLENT SPLASH]
 Two Abyssal Pikes shot out of the water, dragging a pair of Adept-level creatures under before anyone could react.
Zane stood perfectly still on the shore, a small frown on his face as he watched the pond. In a way, he was glad these nobles were testing the waters for him.
It seemed the Elysian Keep was far more dangerous than any of them had imagined.
Everyone had assumed the Elysian Keep would be safe, shielded by a massive enchanted barrier meant to keep giant beasts out. If that barrier ever failed, the whole place would be overrun. But no one had expected the threat to be *inside* the walls—a school of Abyssal Pikes blocking their path.
[AMBIENT: WATER RIPPLING]
[SFX: FISH MOVEMENT]
 The group lingered on the shore, glancing nervously at the water. A dense school of the piranha-like Abyssal Pikes swarmed near the crystal causeway. There had to be thousands of them in the lake-sized pond.
Anything that dared to cross the causeway—or even get too close—would be instantly torn to shreds by the leaping fish.
They stared in frustration at the Alchemist's Laboratory and the Armory in the distance. No one wanted to walk away from a treasure trove like this empty-handed.
The long-haired woman, Old Wilson, Seymour, and the other Soul Realm masters exchanged loaded glances.
Without a word, the Soul Realm masters rose into the air, burning their Vitality, and flew straight over the pond toward the central spire. The Adepts left on the shore could only watch. They couldn't fly. The causeway was the only way across.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Zane inhaled deeply, centering his thoughts on the treasure trove of glittering Dragon Scales nestled within his Storage Ring—over a hundred of them, each pulsing with ancient power. His heartbeat quickened as determination flooded his veins. 
The causeway beckoned. With his jaw set and eyes narrowed in concentration, he summoned his courage and launched himself forward, muscles coiled like springs finally released into action.
[SFX: WATER EXPLOSION]
 The moment he moved, the water exploded.
[SFX: SWISHING SPLASHING]
[SFX: FISH ATTACK]
 Countless Abyssal Pikes launched themselves at him, jaws gaping to reveal rows of razor-sharp teeth.
[SFX: WATER SPLASHING]
[SFX: POWER SURGING]
But they all missed. One after another, they crashed back into the water behind him with frustrated splashes.
Each time Zane felt his energy dip, he instantly drew on the power of the Dragon Scales, a fresh surge of power flooding through him.
Back on the shore, the Adepts could only stare, jaws slack, at Zane's impossible speed.
Sure, a powerful Adept Stage warrior might have matched his speed. In theory, they could have done what he was doing. But it took more than just strength—it took guts.
They weren't willing to dance with death. Zane, clearly, was.
[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
 By now, Zane had already made it smoothly across the first half of the crystal causeway. On the second half, though, the Pikes got smarter. Some of them further down the path started leaping into the air, anticipating his approach.
"Let's see how the kid handles this," one of the nobles muttered.
"He's about to be fish food," another sneered. They watched on, practically giddy at the thought of Zane's gruesome end.
But even at that incredible speed, Zane’s senses were sharp, mapping out every obstacle and danger around him.
[SFX: POWERFUL LEAP]
 As he closed in on the fish lying in wait, Zane planted his foot and launched himself high into the air.
[SFX: WATER RUSHING]
 With a powerful flick of their tails, the Abyssal Pikes shot up from the water, snapping their jaws at Zane.
“Phoenix Crystal!” he yelled.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]
 Zane channeled the energy from the crystal embedded in his right arm, just in the nick of time. As the power surged through him, his body felt lighter, his speed multiplying mid-air.
The fish were slower and clumsier out of the water, and the burst of speed was all he needed to narrowly escape their snapping jaws.
Dodging the last few Abyssal Pikes on the final stretch, Zane landed on the central spire, completely unharmed despite the constant threat.


[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
 The Soul Realm masters, who had flown to the central spire earlier, were stunned to see him make it across. Old Wilson’s face twisted into a glare of pure hatred.
*This kid sticks to us like a damn stray,* he thought.
Zane didn't miss Wilson's murderous glare. He just gave a casual shrug. "You know," he said, "that black wolf I made from my Vitality is still fighting those two golems outside. It'd be a shame if I had to call it in here."
At Zane's threat, the long-haired woman shot Wilson a sharp look. "Wilson. Not now."
[SFX: BARRIER SEALING]
The moment the last person entered the pavilion, pink barriers slammed down across all eight exits. No one was getting out.
In the center of the floor sat a massive formation. Around its perimeter, human-sized sculptures lay scattered — each one belonging to a specific position in the formation. The intent was clear: place every sculpture correctly to break the barrier and open the exits. Place one wrong, and the consequences could be fatal.
Nobody moved.
To everyone’s surprise, Zane spoke up. “What if I give it a try?”
"You?" Wilson shrieked, glaring at Zane. "You think we'd trust *you* with this? You should be more worried about yourself!" he sneered.
Zane wasn't surprised. Of course they wouldn't trust a stranger in a mask with something so critical. The mood soured, the weight of the life-or-death puzzle pressing down on them all.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DECISION]
 After a tense silence, the long-haired woman finally sighed. "Fine, I'll do it," she said. "I studied the Runic Cipher a while back. I know a little, but I'm no expert."
No one argued. It wasn't that they were confident in her abilities—far from it—but they were out of other options.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY FLOW]
 With no one objecting, the long-haired woman stepped forward and began her calculations. After a moment, she looked up and released a stream of Vitality from her hands. 
Everyone held their breath as they watched the energy form a glowing cap over the head of one of the sculptures before slowly lifting it into the air.
