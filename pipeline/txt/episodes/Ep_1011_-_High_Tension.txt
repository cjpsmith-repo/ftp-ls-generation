Ep 1011 - High Tension

Word count: 1033

[AMBIENT: MYSTERIOUS CAVE]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Deep in the cave, the air cold and still, Zane and Freya stood motionless before the cluster of pale stones, close enough to touch, with a sleeping Hydra coiled between them and everything they needed.

Neither of them had ever actually laid eyes on a Sovereign Stone before.

The Soulbound Sect erected an enchanted barrier around the mine and imprisoned all arrivals from the Lower World inside.

The exit price: one hundred Sovereign Stones. Pure extortion.

At least it shattered any illusions quickly, teaching warriors from the Lower World how powerless they truly were after ascension.

[SFX: MAGICAL HUMMING]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHIMES]

The stones before them were practically humming with Vitality.

But getting their hands on them wasn’t going to be easy. They were sitting right next to a level-twelve beast.

At his current strength, Zane could probably hold his own against a Soul Sea Realm master. This level-twelve beast, however, was on the level of a Spirit Supreme Realm master. On top of that, beasts lived far longer than humans, and this Hydra was likely a Legendary Beast. If it came down to a fight, Zane didn’t stand a chance.

As for Freya, she had just broken through to the Soul Sea Realm. She might have thought she was stronger than Zane now, but he had his doubts.

They both stared at the cluster of stones, their eyes flicking to the sleeping Hydra. They were frozen, debating if the prize was worth the risk.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Giving up wasn’t an option. But the fear of getting killed was very, very real.

Just then, a faint smile touched Freya’s lips. “I have something… a treasure that can conceal most of my presence. The Hydra’s still asleep. I think I can sneak in and grab them.”

Zane didn’t say a word. The risk was huge, but so was the reward. He gave her a single, solemn nod.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

Freya pulled out a light green, gossamer shroud, as thin as a cicada’s wing. As she slipped it on, a flicker of surprise crossed Zane’s face.

[AMBIENT: HUSHED SILENCE]

It was just as she’d said. The moment the shroud settled over her, her presence almost completely vanished. Even with his incredibly sharp senses, Zane would’ve had a hard time tracking her if he wasn’t already watching. He could no longer feel her, though his eyes could just make out the faint green shimmer in the air.

That shroud was Divine Weapon-level, at minimum. He was certain of it.

Although she kept her steps light and controlled, Zane could see the tension in her. But she wasn’t falling apart. She steadied herself and kept going.

For a moment, he considered asking her to hand it over — let him go in instead. But after watching her first careful steps, silent and controlled as a cat, he let the thought go. This was her risk to take.

Beasts like this slept for centuries at a stretch — sometimes longer, when an advancement was near. Snakes hibernated. This one had clearly been down here for ages. It wasn’t going to wake up. Freya kept telling herself that with every step.

[SFX: THUNDEROUS VOICE]

[MUSIC: SUDDEN STING]

A voice detonated across the universe, passing through world after world, filling every corner of existence without exception. It was a Roaring Token — and whoever had sent it wasn’t whispering.

The Hydra stirred. One of its nine heads rolled slowly, tongue flicking out with a wet rasp. Then it stilled.

Freya froze.

Her foot had come down on a palm-sized stone. The stone skittered. Three short knocks rang out through the cave — sharp, clear, and very, very loud.

[SFX: STONE ROLLING ON CAVE FLOOR]

[MUSIC: HELD BREATH]

Neither of them moved. Neither of them breathed.

Two of the Hydra’s heads lifted — slow, heavy, as if dragged up from fathoms of sleep. They hung there, swaying faintly, tasting the air.

Then they dropped.

Freya stood motionless for a long moment. Then she exhaled — a breath so slow and quiet it was barely a sound at all. She turned her head and found Zane’s eyes. She gave him a small, shaky smile.

And kept going.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]

She made it to the Sovereign Stones, and her eyes lit up. She counted quickly — thirty-nine in all, clustered on the branch like pale fruit.

She reached the branch, her hand closing around one of the stones. She pulled.

[SFX: HEAVY SILENCE]

Nothing.

She tried again — thirty percent of her strength this time. The stone didn’t shift.

Her hand didn’t move an inch, and neither did the milky-white branch. It was like they were both forged from solid steel and fused together.

Even an iron tree should have snapped under that kind of force. Clearly, this wasn’t going to be so simple.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Freya took another deep breath. This time, she put sixty percent of her strength into the pull, but the Sovereign Stone didn’t budge.

Finally, she channeled ninety percent of her power.

A frustrated flush crept up her neck, but the Sovereign Stone remained stubbornly attached to the branch.

She finally realized brute force wasn’t going to work.

Shooting Zane a helpless look, she gave up and walked back to his side.

[SFX: DEFEATED SIGH]

“I can’t get it off,” Freya said, her voice tight with frustration. To get so close only to fail at the last second… she was clearly crushed.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE FLUTE]

This was all the Soulbound Sect’s fault. If they were ever running low on Sovereign Stones, they’d send Spirit Supreme Realm masters into the mines to dig. But their reserves were usually so full that the high-level warriors couldn’t be bothered. Besides, hunting for stones was risky and took way too much time.

To get Sovereign Stones, you needed a special tool to excavate them—at the very least, a Divine Weapon.

A Sovereign Stone was formed over eons, slowly crystallizing from the earth after absorbing a massive amount of the world’s Vitality.

Why would anyone think they could just pluck one off a branch like a piece of fruit?
