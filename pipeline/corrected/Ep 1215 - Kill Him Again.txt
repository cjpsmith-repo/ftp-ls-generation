Ep 1215 - Kill Him Again

Word count: 984

[MUSIC: DARK BROODING TENSION]
[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND WIND]
This was the ultimate humiliation. To be taken out by a human warrior with a basic move—it was unthinkable. 
The rage inside Lance just kept building.
The only reason Zane had won so easily was his raw power. At his current level, Lance simply couldn’t stand up to that kind of brute strength. But that didn’t mean he was just going to roll over and accept it. 
Sure, Zane had a strong body and freakish innate strength, but neither of those things impressed Lance. He had far more skill and a much better record, having taken out countless opponents in his time. More than that, he’d mastered the deep secrets of Fire Law—a power that was his and his alone. He’d even managed to figure out the Law of Causality all by himself.
And yet, he’d been killed by this human before he could show off an ounce of what he could really do. Zane had played him. Plain and simple. How was he supposed to just accept that?
The second he revived, he stormed out of the city, his mind already racing. His biggest worry was that Zane had already found a place to hide inside the sprawling metropolis. But while waiting to respawn, he’d made a decision. It didn’t matter where Zane went—Lance would hunt him down. As long as Zane was trying to rack up Illusion Points, Lance would be on his tail.
Standing on the city walls, his gaze swept the area, and he was stunned by what he saw.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING STRINGS]
Not far off, Zane was just floating in mid-air, looking like he hadn’t moved an inch since killing him. As Lance stared, Zane met his eyes, and a slow, challenging smile spread across his face.
Seeing that arrogant smirk, the fury in Lance’s chest surged straight to his head.
Without a second thought, he leaped from the wall. 
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[SFX: MASSIVE WINGS IGNITING]
The pair of purple, flaming wings on his back flared to life as he rocketed straight for Zane.
Normally, Lance was a cautious fighter. Competition in the Ogre Flame World was brutal, and as the successor to a World Lord, he’d learned long ago not to rely on talent alone. He’d initially seen Zane as a ‘prize pig’ for the taking—some timid, average joe. But that pig had just turned around and beaten him senseless. Zane had gotten so deep under his skin that he’d lost all reason. When warriors flew into a blind rage like this, their strength would sometimes surge beyond its normal limits.
Zane didn’t move a muscle as the wings bore down on him. He’d already done the math on the timing. While he stood there looking perfectly still, he was quietly drawing out threads of chaotic energy beneath the surface, shaping them into the same Thunder Calamity array he’d used before—and burying the whole thing a foot underground, completely out of sight.
[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ENERGY BUILDUP]
But just as Lance was about to unleash his attack, the warriors watching from the city stared in astonishment.
[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLING]
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS SKY PRESSURE]
Every warrior in the Illusion Battlefield had reached at least the Soul Sea Realm—which meant every one of them had weathered a Tribulation of their own at some point, minor ones at least, some even a major one or two. They knew exactly what they were looking at the second the sky changed.
[SFX: SPINNING MARK]
A red hexagonal mark bloomed to life between Lance’s eyebrows, spinning slowly as it gathered power. “Brat,” he snarled, “I’ll show you something you’ve never seen before.”
[SFX: FLAME IGNITING]
Flame rose from his forehead—but not ordinary flame. Where fire was normally split into just an outer layer and an inner one, this burned in six distinct layers at once, each a different color, beautiful and monstrous in the same breath. It was a profound meaning Lance had carved out of the Fire Law entirely on his own, unique in the whole universe. He’d never once lost a fight after calling on it.
He noticed the strange pressure gathering in the sky above—the same instant he started forming the flame—and dismissed it without a second thought. Whatever was happening up there, it wasn’t meant for him. He wasn’t the one breaking through to a new level. His only target right now was the human floating in front of him.
[SFX: SHOCKED GASPS FROM CROWD]
“Is that… is the Heavenly Tribulation aimed at *Lance*?!”
“Did that human actually summon it?”
“No way, that’s impossible!” someone shouted back. “I’ve never heard of anything like that! The Tribulation only happens when a warrior is about to level up. It’s the wrath of the heavens!”
The Heavenly Tribulation was a punishment, brought down on warriors because the heavens didn’t like them getting too strong. How could a human order it around? None of them had ever heard of a power like that, so they quickly decided it couldn’t be true.
It had to be a coincidence. Zane was just waiting there, and Lance happened to charge him at the exact moment the Tribulation was about to strike.
What an insane coincidence.
[SFX: MASSIVE THUNDER STRIKE]
[SFX: BODY DISINTEGRATION]
Lance never even finished forming the flame. The Tribulation hit him first, mid-charge, a white waterfall of lightning pouring straight down out of a clear sky.
When the thunder faded, Lance’s body was gone. 
[SFX: ENERGY ABSORPTION WHOOSH]
A speck of red light shot back toward the city, and at the same time, motes of yellow light dissolved into Zane’s body.
Killing Lance the first time had earned him one hundred forty thousand Illusion Points. This time, he got another seventy thousand. That brought his grand total from Lance alone to two hundred ten thousand.
[MUSIC: CALM VICTORIOUS STRINGS]
“I wonder if he’ll be back for more,” Zane murmured to himself with a casual smile. “Thirty thousand Illusion Points isn’t chump change.”
A shiver went through the other warriors as they watched him. Now, they were pretty sure Zane *had* summoned the Heavenly Tribulation.
But how on earth did a human do that?
