Ep 390 - The Danger Inside

Word count: 1204

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC ABYSS]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DANGER]

Disciples scrambled over each other in the chaos, clawing their way out of the abyss. Survival was all that mattered, every man and woman for themselves. 

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

Finn vaulted from one floating rock to the next, his Sword Step technique a blur of motion. Each leap carried him higher, closer to the exit above. Smaller stones blocking his path exploded into dust as waves of his sword intent cleared the way.

Nearing the top, something gnawed at him—a feeling he couldn't shake. Finn risked a glance back for his friends.

His blood ran cold. Far below, Zane was plummeting straight toward the drake, falling faster with each passing heartbeat. The mysterious disciple from Cloud Hall clung to him, dragging them both towards the creature's gaping maw. Time seemed to slow as Finn watched his friend disappear into the darkness, powerless to help.

 He shrieked, his voice raw with terror. "ZANE!"

Finn’s cry cut through the pandemonium. The others, still clambering up the rockface, whipped their heads around, their own faces twisting in shock at the sight of Zane.

[MUSIC: DARK SINISTER]

A cold sneer twisted Frank’s lips as he watched the disciple from Cloud Hall drag Zane toward the drake's gaping jaws. He’d known that disciple was shady. Hell, he’d even suspected they were plotting something. But since they hadn't made any overt moves, Frank hadn't bothered to look into it.

He just never imagined they’d go this far—dragging Zane straight into the mouth of a drake!

You had this coming, Zane, Frank thought. Still… all that Nether Phantom essence you're carrying. If you die in that abyss, I’ll never get my hands on it. What a damn waste. Then again… you’re already this strong as an Adept. You make it to the elite disciples in Cloud Hall, and you’d probably be one of my biggest rivals.

No matter how cocky he was, Frank couldn't deny the facts.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS CRESCENDO]

In that case, Frank thought, a genuine smile touching his lips, the belly of a drake is the perfect grave for you.

[SFX: WIND RUSHING—FADING TO MUFFLED ROAR]
Zane twisted in the air, watching the flood dragon's massive jaws yawn open below them. The creature's throat glowed with a sickly phosphorescent light, illuminating rows of serrated teeth, each as long as his forearm.

"This is your fault!" the woman shrieked, her hair whipping wildly as they plummeted.

Zane blinked. Wait - the disciple from Cloud Hall was a woman? 

Before Zane could process this development, the dragon's maw engulfed them. 

In the split second before the darkness swallowed them, her voice cut through the rush of wind. "Because I chose you."
Zane had no time to ask what that meant.

The woman's scream cut off abruptly as  They tumbled through the creature's gullet, buffeted by slick, pulsing walls that squeezed and pushed them deeper into its belly.

[AMBIENT: WET ORGANIC INTERIOR]

[SFX: SQUELCH]

"Luckily, the inside of the drake is soft," Zane thought, landing with a wet squelch. "Otherwise, a fall like that would've seriously injured us."

"Get your hand off me!" the disciple shrieked.

Before Zane could react, his hand was seized, his arm twisted, and he was flung to the side.

Landing in a heap, Zane finally realized what he’d been touching. It wasn't the dragon’s flesh. It was the disciple, who was very much a woman. He pulled back his hand.

“Yikes - sorry,” Zane apologized, glaring into the pitch-black where he thought she was. "But I don't get it - why the disguise?" 

[SFX: MAGICAL LIGHT BLOOM]

She just snorted. A dim light bloomed in her palm, pushing back the oppressive dark.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

To Zane’s surprise, she didn’t seem to care about hiding her identity anymore. With a flick of her wrist, she wiped away her facial disguise, revealing her true face.

In the faint glow, her white disciple’s robe made her look almost angelic, though it couldn't completely hide the curves beneath. Dark hair spilled over one shoulder. Zane was stunned by her natural beauty. For a second, he was completely speechless.

"You're… beautiful," Zane said, breaking the silence. "I don’t get it. Why pretend to be a guy?"

"That's none of your business," she shot back, her voice like ice.

Zane just grinned. "It wasn't. But you kinda made it my business when you dragged me down here. I think that earns me an explanation. We’re stuck inside a Drake, right? So what was the plan? And are you really just first Level of the Soul Realm?"

Zane tried to understand.Her power feels like first Level, he thought, but she saw through my foundational swordplay in a heartbeat. And then she shattered it with her bare hands. That shouldn’t be possible for someone at her level.

Frank is supposed to be the strongest of the new Soul Realm disciples. Compared to fresh breakthroughs from The Heartlands, he's near the top. I could see Frank reacting that fast. With his spear, he might even be able to break my foundational swordsmanship. But with his bare hands? No way.

But the Cloud Road Trial bans anyone above the Third Level. So she can’t be stronger than that. The conflicting facts left Zane completely stumped.

Finally, she answered him. "That's a stupid question," the woman said flatly. "I couldn't have joined the Cloud Road Trials if I wasn't."

[AMBIENT: EERIE WHISPERS]

[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]

Zane's mind raced as he processed her words, but before he could respond, the Cobalt Dragon's presence stirred within him.

"This Drake has been dead for centuries," the dragon murmured, its ancient voice tinged with hunger. "Yet its corpse still moves, still hunts. Do you understand what that means, boy?"

Zane frowned inwardly. "A puppet?"

"Precisely. And not just any puppet—one crafted by masters of the Sixth-Tier set who once ruled these lands."

The Cobalt Dragon’s voice resonated in his mind. "The Drake’s spirit… I can sense it hidden nearby. I want to devour it!"

"Its spirit?" Zane thought back, skeptical. "You just said this thing died a long time ago." As far as Zane knew, when the body died, the spirit went with it.

"Controlling a puppet this powerful isn’t simple," the Cobalt Dragon explained. "The most effective method is to extract the spirit, refine it, and seal it near the body."

What kind of power is that? Zane thought, a chill running down his spine. This Sixth-Tier sect… they didn’t just kill a drake. They drained its blood, ripped out its spirit, and turned the whole thing into a puppet. How is that even possible? Was this the Blood Fiend Emperor’s work?

[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]

The Cobalt Dragon’s voice was grim. "Be warned, I can feel the seal on its spirit weakening. Drakes are lustful, vicious creatures by nature. You and the woman need to be careful."

Zane nodded, his eyes locked on the woman's back as she walked ahead of him. After watching her for a moment, he asked, "I have another question. Do you notice something strange about her? How is she so terrifyingly strong?"

Zane thought the two of them were trapped together inside a monster. But what if she was the real monster, and he was trapped with her?

[SFX: ECHOING HISSES]

Before the Cobalt Dragon could answer, a chorus of hisses echoed through the darkness—and from the shadows emerged dozens of serpentine forms, their scales gleaming with a deadly sheen.
