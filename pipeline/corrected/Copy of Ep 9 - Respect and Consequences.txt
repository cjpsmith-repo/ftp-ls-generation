Ep 9 - Respect and Consequences
Word Count: 1259
[AMBIENT: PRISON CELL]
The next morning, Zane was surprised when no guard came for him. Since getting beaten up was a key part of his "fabrication process"—his secret method for forging his body into a weapon—Zane got dressed and headed to the Training Hall on his own.
On the way, Zane couldn't help but think about how strange his uncles and their sons were acting, openly defying House Lowell rules. Perrin and Andrew, especially, seemed to have no respect for the family's code. Maybe that's why some of the servants had gotten so cocky lately.
But just because others ignored the rules didn't mean Zane had forgotten the principles he was raised on. And it sure as hell didn't mean they could expect him to look the other way when they broke them right in his face. He wasn't some stickler for the rules, but he had his own code.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]
[SFX: DISTANT COMBAT SOUNDS]
When Zane got to the hall, the vibe was off. By now, word had spread that Zane had thrashed Darren and Grey yesterday.
[MUSIC: GRIM DETERMINATION]
Two years ago, he'd been framed and convicted, becoming a slave to his own family, House Lowell. Like the other convicts, he was forced to serve as a human punching bag in the training hall.
He'd always just taken it. No matter how brutally the Lowell kids beat him, he endured it, swallowed his pride, and kept his mouth shut. A total pushover. Everyone seemed to have forgotten he was once a young master of House Lowell.
They'd forgotten how strong he was—one of the youngest ever to reach the Strength Level.
But after yesterday, they were starting to remember. They realized Zane wasn't just going to take whatever they dished out anymore. The Lowell kids could beat on him without consequence—they were family, after all. But outsiders weren't getting a free pass to treat him like dirt. And now, the Lowell kids were looking at him with a glimmer of respect—or maybe it was fear.
So when the trainer, Master Corrin, told the kids to pick their punching bags, nobody dared to choose Zane.
This threw Zane for a loop. He knew how crucial the beatings were for his fabrication process, and he'd actually been looking forward to a good thrashing today. Zane forced a bitter smile. He needed this to strengthen his body, but with no one willing to pick him, he was in a bind.
He couldn't exactly walk up and ask someone to beat him up. Being a punching bag wasn't a desirable job, and it would look insane to actually ask for a beating. Besides, he couldn't tell them the real reason he needed it.
[MUSIC: SOMBER DISAPPOINTMENT]
As the other punching bags were chosen, Zane was left alone in the corner of the hall. He was seriously bummed.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: STONE GOLEM COMBAT]
He finally walked over to Melvin Lowell, who was sparring with a Stone Golem.
"Melvin," Zane said, "What's the point of fighting that thing? Spar with me."
"I don't know..." Melvin said, frowning. After his own run-in with Zane—not to mention hearing what happened to Darren and Grey—he wasn't exactly eager to tangle with the former young master.
"I'm a punching bag. It's my job to help you practice," Zane insisted, patting the leather vest on his chest. "Don't worry, I can take a hit."
Zane's offer put Melvin in an awkward spot. How would it look if he backed down from a "punching bag"? The Lowell trainees were supposed to be tougher than the men they beat on every day. He'd look like a coward. The others would definitely make fun of him. But was it worth risking Zane's temper and getting thrashed like Darren and Grey?
No matter what anyone else thought, Melvin was worried. He wasn't as strong as he used to be. If his strength was a hundred before, now it was barely fifty or sixty.
Melvin finally shrugged, accepting. He couldn't afford to look weak.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING ROOM]
[SFX: SPARRING SOUNDS]
As soon as they started sparring, Zane understood Melvin's hesitation. His hits were weak, not nearly forceful enough to trigger the strengthening process. It was nothing like the last time Melvin had gone all out on him. Zane was starting to get annoyed.
"More power!" Zane grunted. "Hit me hard, right here! Your punches are too slow. What are you holding back for? Okay, better, but still not as good as last time."
A punching bag coaching a Lowell kid was weird enough. One begging to be hit harder was downright bizarre. A group of trainees had stopped their own drills to watch, their mouths hanging open. They had no idea what was going on in Zane's head.
Melvin, however, ignored the gawking crowd. At first, Zane's critiques pissed him off; it felt like he was being mocked. But as he started to notice his own form improving, he pushed his frustration aside and focused. His limbs felt looser, his power returning to normal.
[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCHES]
[SFX: BODY FALLING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE TRANSFORMATION]
The force of Melvin's punches slammed into Zane's chest, radiating deep into his body. Zane collapsed, thrashing on the floor as a wave of pain washed over him, followed by a spreading warmth. He could feel it happening—the impurities in his bones being cleansed away in a process as delicate as spinning silk. With every punch, his bones grew purer, stronger. He could feel himself hardening from the inside out.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]
[SFX: PUNCHING SOUNDS]
To Zane, Melvin's punches felt like an Aether Crystal, fueling a fundamental change in his body. The joy was indescribable. He just had to remember to act like he was in agony after each blow. He let pain twist his face, but inwardly, he was ecstatic. It took all his willpower not to scream, "Hit me harder!"
[SFX: WATER DRIPPING]
Zane smiled as a guard went to refill the Stockpot—the hall's makeshift clock. A small hole in the pot let water drip out at a steady pace, taking exactly an hour to empty. Zane had been eagerly counting the refills. Three refills. Three hours had passed.
[SFX: STOMACH RUMBLING]
The thought made Zane's stomach rumble. It meant the Lowell servants would be bringing lunch for the trainees and their punching bags. Usually, this was a miserable time for the punching bags.
The Lowell kids were served fragrant, hot meals, while the bags got stale bread and cold water.
But today, Zane was too ravenous to care. The intense, three-hour session had left him starving; the strengthening process burned a massive amount of energy, and he'd devour anything, tasteless or not.
[SFX: CERAMIC BOWL PLACED DOWN]
Zane grabbed his chunk of bread and was about to shove it in his mouth when a ceramic bowl suddenly appeared in front of him, filled with fragrant, delicious meat. He looked up, surprised to see Melvin.
"Here," Melvin said, handing the bowl to Zane. "Let's eat together."
Zane didn't refuse. He smiled gratefully and scooped up a handful of meat.
[SFX: EAGER CHEWING]
"You shouldn't have beaten Grey," Melvin whispered. "Andrew is going to make you pay for it," He glanced nervously over his shoulder.
Zane paused mid-chew, the rich taste of meat—a luxury he'd almost forgotten—suddenly less satisfying. He swallowed hard and met Melvin's gaze.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED TENSION]
"Let him come," Zane said quietly, his fingers unconsciously tracing the hardened contours of his newly strengthened bones beneath his skin. "I need the practice."
