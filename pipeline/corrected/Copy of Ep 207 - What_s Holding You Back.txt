Ep 207 - What's Holding You Back?

[MUSIC: TENSE UNDERCURRENT]
James looked at Ethan, who seemed so grateful to be reunited with his family's precious little bracelet. A sly grin spread across his face. "Just as I thought," he mused. The kid was so easy to fool. He slipped back into his regal persona and said smoothly, "Alright then, today---"
"Wait!" Ingrid stepped forward, cutting him off mid-sentence, her eyes flashing with indignation.
James's face tightened into a mask of barely contained fury. Ever since ascending to head of House Moon no one had dared interrupt him---his word had been law, his authority absolute. Until today. First these impudent youths, and now her.
Recognizing Ingrid, he forced his features into a semblance of composure, though a muscle twitched in his jaw. "Is there a problem, Miss Solomon?" he inquired with artificial politeness.
"You almost killed Zane," she said, her voice sharp as a newly forged blade. "Are you just going to brush past that as if it were nothing?"
Ingrid wasn't merely upset---she was bursting with righteous anger. Long known for her unforgiving nature, she harbored grudges with unwavering dedication. When someone crossed a line with those she cared about, that transgression became forever etched in her memory. James's attempt on Zane's life had crossed every boundary of decency, and the calculating look in her eyes made it clear this offense would never be forgotten.
James's expression darkened. The day's humiliations had already piled high enough to wound his considerable pride, yet Ingrid continued to press, digging into his wounded ego with relentless precision. His voice dropped to a dangerous whisper. "Miss Solomon, like I said, we all share the blame for what happened. It was merely a misunderstanding." He paused, attempting to salvage his dignity. "If you feel I went too far, I sincerely hope you won't hold it against me."
Ingrid scoffed, the sound dripping with contempt. "I warned you explicitly not to do anything reckless. Did you hear what I said? If Uncle George hadn't intervened, would you have actually stopped attacking Zane?" She leaned forward, her gaze penetrating. "So tell me honestly, do you really think I can simply shake this off?"
"Do you really want to keep dragging this out, Miss Solomon?" James shot back, feeling trapped.
"Yes, I do," Ingrid replied without missing a beat, her shoulders squared with unwavering resolve.
"You're really pushing your luck!" James had tolerated enough. His temper finally snapped. As the head of House Moon, he'd never been so humiliated. He refused to let some young woman walk all over him like this. He wouldn't be pushed this far.
Just then, George, still floating in mid-air, spoke up with a lazy smile. "Well, if Ingrid isn't satisfied, I'm happy to help settle things." He looked at Ingrid. "Just say the word."
Ingrid was about to tell George to teach James a lesson when Brenda, standing beside her, grabbed her sleeve.
Brenda's face was pale. She pleaded in a shaky voice, "Ingrid, please... let my uncle go. Can we just drop this?"
Brenda was James's niece, but she was also Ingrid's best friend. She was caught right in the middle.
Seeing the desperate look on her friend's face, Ingrid's resolve softened. She sighed and said to George, "Uncle George, it's fine. Let's just drop it."
[SFX: ICE CRYSTALS SWIRLING]
George harbored no ill will toward James. The moment Ingrid declared the matter settled, he dropped it without protest. A mischievous smile played across his lips as the ice crystals surrounding him coalesced into a swirling vortex, enveloping his form completely before streaking skyward like a crystalline comet.
With his father's Bloodstone Bracelet now circling his wrist---its familiar weight both comforting and bittersweet---Ethan felt no desire to linger in this tense atmosphere.
"Zane," he said quietly, "let's go. We've accomplished what we came for."
Zane nodded, relief washing over his features as he gestured for Dustin and Rhys to follow. The confrontation had left him drained, yet strangely exhilarated.
Dustin leaned toward Tyson, whispering something inaudible that made the warrior's expression harden. Tyson adjusted his grip on the imposing Woodcutter's Axe, its blade gleaming ominously in the light, and fell into step behind them. His vigilant eyes continuously scanned their surroundings, ready to defend against any further threats as they made their departure from House Moon's increasingly uncomfortable gathering.

Zane smiled. "I was starting to think you were a coward," he said. "Turns out you've got more guts than I gave you credit for."
Ethan just looked at him, confused. "Guts? When have I ever shown that?"
"You're not afraid to die," Zane said, his questions coming one after another. "You're not even afraid to kill yourself. So what else is there to be scared of? What's holding you back? If you confronted James right now, yeah, he might try to kill you. But how is that any worse than what you were just about to do?"
"If you're not afraid of death, then you should throw everything you have into getting stronger. When you're strong enough, you can walk right up to James and demand answers. What's he going to do to you then? Since you already don't care if you live or die, why not die fighting for something that matters?"
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL REVELATION]
Ethan's heart pounded in his chest as Zane's words hit him.
He might have seemed like a soft kid who just took whatever life threw at him, but deep down, he was resilient. He had to be, or he never would have made it into the Skyreach Sect, even with Zane's help.
"Zane's right," he thought. "He's absolutely right." Life could be a long, miserable road. What a waste it would be to die without even fighting back. It was better to try and fail than to never try at all. The worst that could happen was death, and he was already staring that in the face.
Zane's words had cracked open a door Ethan thought was sealed forever. He'd never been ambitious. He'd joined the Skyreach Sect and just gone through the motions, doing the bare minimum and never pushing himself.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]
But now, something inside him had shifted. He had a new goal: to crush Yates and make James answer for his father's death. And to do that, he needed to get strong.
As the realization settled in, his eyes lit up with a fire Zane hadn't seen before. He clenched his fists, his whole body trembling not with despair, but with a surge of raw, new purpose.
[SFX: AMBIENT MOON MANSION]
Back at Moon Mansion, a steward found Yates and pulled him aside.
"We've finished the count," the steward said, his voice low. "That boy destroyed three hundred and thirty-six of the houses in the compound."
Yates stared at him. "How many houses do we have?"
"Five hundred and sixty-one."
Nearly half. Most of them passed down from their ancestors.
Yates's hands balled into fists. He turned back toward the gate — the direction Ethan had gone. "Ethan," he whispered. "I will kill you with my own hands."
