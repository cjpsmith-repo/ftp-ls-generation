Ep 836 - The Ceremony

Word count: 1275

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING SQUARE]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]

Clutching the Soulcradle Wood, Zane hurried back toward the square. He was trying to play it cool, but a triumphant grin kept breaking across his face. The various Soul Sea Realm masters milling about could see it, and they knew he’d gotten the wood to save someone’s life. They just had no idea who.

War Emperor York and the Holy Maiden, on the other hand, looked absolutely furious. They couldn’t believe the Little Monster they’d sent had failed to kill Zane. Worse, the kid had walked away with an arsenal of incredible gifts. How were they supposed to explain this kind of screw-up?

Even though the Cobalt Dragon had said Lavender’s soul could probably hold on for a day or two, it was just an educated guess. There was an equal chance it could shatter into nothing in the next few hours.

“How do I use this?” Zane asked the Cobalt Dragon, his voice tight with urgency.

[SFX: CEREMONIAL HORN]

[AMBIENT: LARGE CROWD MURMUR]

Just as the words left his lips, a shrill voice echoed across the square. “The duels are concluded. We will now begin the Sacrificial Ceremony! Open the Sacred Feather Sea!”

[MUSIC: EPIC ADVENTURE THEME]

At the announcement, every saint-level genius turned their attention to the central platform. The fights had been nothing more than a preliminary test of strength; the real event—the exploration of the Sacred Feather Sea—was about to begin.

Zane couldn’t have cared less about some ceremony. Only one thing mattered right now: getting Lavender’s soul safely into the Soulcradle Wood.

If she hadn’t fallen into such a deep sleep, she could have entered the wood on her own. But she was unconscious, and the Cobalt Dragon explained that Zane would have to transfer her soul himself.

[AMBIENT: QUIET OUTDOOR SPACE]

Scanning the sprawling square, Zane found a quiet, out-of-the-way corner. He sat down, crossed his legs, and drew his focus inward.

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL MYSTICAL]

Inside the vast expanse of his mind, he found her. Lavender's soul hung silent and still, fine cracks spiderwebbing across its surface. Zane flew to her, wrapped his own spiritual form around hers, and began to gently pull her free.

Following the Cobalt Dragon's instructions, he pressed the Soulcradle Wood to the space between his eyebrows.

The moment Lavender's soul crossed the threshold of his mind, it found the wood on its own — drawn to it the way water finds a channel. She flowed in without resistance.

[SFX: SOFT RESONANT HUM]

A few breaths passed. Then the wood pulsed — a faint red glow spreading slowly across its surface. When Zane touched it, it was warm.

He let out a long, slow breath.

“Incredible,” he murmured.

Lavender's soul was severely damaged, but the wood would nourish it. That was what the name meant — cradle, not cage. Given time, it might even heal her completely. And if it couldn't do that on its own, he would find something that could. He had time now.

He held the warm wood in his palm and let himself feel it for a moment. She hadn't been with him long. But since she'd woken, she had stood beside him through crisis after crisis — not because she had to, but because she chose to. He would not forget that.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL INNER REALM]

Before he left his own mind, his gaze fell upon the soulforge. The light emanating from the Cobalt and Red Dragons coiled on its surface had dimmed considerably. The price he'd paid in his battle with the Little Monster had been steep. But the rewards were immense — his soul had risen to an entirely new level, and he could now connect his physical body to the entire world, offloading any force he couldn't handle across the whole of the chaotic realm.

[SFX: CROWD EXCLAMATION]

[MUSIC: EPIC CEREMONIAL THEME]

He opened his eyes. On the high platform, the ceremony had already begun.

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY BEAM ASCENDING]

A massive pillar of light erupted from the platform and shot straight into the sky.

Zane watched it and reached instinctively into the world's memory. He already knew what this was.

The Sacred Feather Sea had once been the Holy Land of the Dragon-blooded — a civilization that, at its peak. Before they were destroyed, they performed a blood sacrifice of every creature in their clan. That sacrifice became a curse, manifesting as the golden light that now covered the surface of the sea. Any creature that entered without protection would be consumed by it.

What the kingdoms were doing now was temporary — dispersing just enough of that curse to allow entry. They couldn't have devised this method on their own. The upper world's humans had shown them how.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CEREMONIAL SWELL]

[SFX: COLLECTIVE ENERGY RELEASE]

Around the pillar of light, the Soul Sea masters ascended one by one — the rulers at the highest positions, dozens of others ranged below them, all releasing their Vitality in concert.

The shrill voice rang out again. “Together — disperse the curse of the Sacred Feather Sea!”

Slowly, the pillar of light began to tilt. It leaned out over the water, then touched the golden surface — and the sea erupted.

[SFX: MASSIVE WAVES CRASHING]

[AMBIENT: ROARING WIND AND WATER]

Enormous waves rose from the golden sea, sweeping outward in every direction. The golden light churned and writhed as the pillar eroded it from within, shrinking at a visible rate. The sea thrashed like a living thing trying to throw off a wound.

Even Zane, who already knew what to expect, was stunned by the sheer scale of it.

[AMBIENT: CALM WATER]

After some time, the roiling energy slowly settled. The massive column of light began to fade. A faint golden shimmer still danced on the water's surface, but it was a pale imitation of the brilliant light from before.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

[SFX: LIGHT FADING]

When the column vanished completely, a figure was left standing in its place. He wore a simple, steel-gray robe, and his hair was snow-white. He wasn't scowling, but he radiated an undeniable authority that settled over the square like a heavy blanket. This was the white-haired priest.

“Alright! The Sacrificial Ceremony is complete,” the priest announced, his voice the same shrill, piercing one from before. “Everyone can now receive their Dragon Blood and enter the Sacred Feather Sea!”

As Zane’s eyes fixed on the priest, the man seemed to notice his gaze. He gave Zane a dismissive, casual glance before turning to order the masters from the seven kingdoms to begin handing out the vials of Dragon Blood.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

I can’t get a read on his strength, Zane thought, his heart suddenly hammering against his ribs.

Even a master from the Soul Sea Realm would have trouble hiding their power from Zane’s senses now. But this priest… there was nothing. Zane was completely stumped.

[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]

There was only one possible explanation. This priest was far beyond the Soul Sea Realm.

“Aren’t only masters at or below the Soul Sea Realm allowed in the Lower World?” Zane asked the Cobalt Dragon, his mind reeling.

[AMBIENT: DISTANT THUNDER]

Theoretically, even Soul Sea Realm masters were barely tolerated by the laws of the chaotic world. The moment a warrior from the Life-and-Death Realm appeared, the heavens would strike them down with a lesser Celestial Tribulation. 

And for those who had just broken through to the Soul Sea Realm, the heavens would send the greater Celestial Tribulations.

Something told Zane the Sacred Feather Sea was going to be far more dangerous than anyone had let on.
