Ep 516 - Everybody Wants Runes
WORD COUNT: 1063
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
Zane hadn't saved Elara, but he'd managed to kill Marilyn. And her death just threw a wrench a mile wide into Maddox's whole Soul Harvesting Ritual.
Maddox had spent decades hunting for women with the ultra-rare Violet-Core Physique. Right when he thought he'd finally gathered them all, Zane went and killed his most prized asset. Finding a replacement with the same qualifications could take forever.
It could take years, decades, maybe even centuries.
He might never find one. But that didn't change the fact that Zane was now in a race against time. Killing Marilyn might have bought him a few decades, but there was no guarantee when the next person with a Violet-Core Physique would be born—or when Maddox would find them.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC BUILD]
For now, all Zane could do was get stronger. Strong enough to get Elara back from Maddox, and fast.
The worst part was that he couldn't get through to her at all. Her senses were completely walled off, leaving her numb to the world around her. Even seeing her own brother hadn't sparked a single flicker of recognition in her vacant eyes.
[AMBIENT: ARENA RUINS]
[SFX: SETTLING DEBRIS]
Back in the arena, the battle ring had totally collapsed after Maddox vanished. Dust and rubble were all that was left of the once-grand stage.
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
Many of the warriors who had stayed in the stands were still shaking from what they’d just witnessed. A few of them, however, were sizing up Zane.
"That Zane guy… he's got to be the craziest warrior in the history of the Heartlands," someone whispered. "Pulling a stunt like that right in front of Maddox? Killing Marilyn? What was he thinking?"
"Seriously. Even a Spirit-Tribulation warrior would be shaking in their boots just standing near Maddox, let alone a Soul Realm practitioner," another murmured back. "But Zane didn't just threaten him—he killed Marilyn right under his nose."
"And the craziest part? Maddox just… took it. He just left! My god, is that even the same Maddox?"
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Everyone there had always feared Maddox as much as they worshipped him. His power, his status as the strongest man alive—it was just a fact of life. But a few minutes ago, some kid who was leagues below him in power had completely humiliated him. Not only that, the kid killed one of his inner circle right in front of him. It was the ultimate slap in the face, and somehow, the kid was still breathing.
If they hadn't all seen it with their own eyes, no one would have ever believed it.
[SFX: EXCITED LAUGHTER]
Zane's friends watched from the stands, awestruck at what he'd just pulled off.
Finn grinned. "I knew it! Zane actually did it!"
Lewis nodded. "Too bad nobody took you up on that bet. You could've made another fortune." Finn had already won big betting on the earlier matches with Lewis's Aether crystals. The fact that Finn hadn't kicked back a single cent had Lewis feeling a little sour.
"He did it," Patrick agreed, his brow furrowed. "But this is just the beginning. He has a long, long way to go." Many warriors pursued immortality, yet few achieved it. Most fell along the path—some becoming forgotten bones, others serving as stepping stones, still more consumed by greed.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]
Meanwhile, Kieran and the other rune masters had begun their investigation.
After centuries of development, rune techniques had formed an almost perfect system throughout the Heartlands. Breakthroughs had become extraordinarily rare. Few novel runes had emerged because predecessors had already explored every viable path.
Yet Zane's demonstration had completely overturned Toby's fundamental understanding. In two hours, he'd drawn over a hundred runes and blocked Marilyn's attack. Toby sensed that because of Zane, rune technique in general might advance by leaps and bounds.
[SFX: STONE SCRAPING]
Kieran excavated the buried runes with his bare hands. Three large stone slabs emerged, each engraved with three intricate Four-Star Runes.
When the three masters each received a slab, they immediately channelled their soul force into it.
Shock blossomed across their faces.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]
"Just as I suspected," Kieran breathed. "These runes bear no resemblance to anything used in the Heartlands! The craftsmanship—how can it be so exquisite?"
"These runes… they're nothing like what we have in the Heartlands!"
"The axis splits straight down the middle—that's a cardinal sin in runic theory! How can they still be so intricate?"
"Look at all these extra strokes in the top half… but then at the bottom, it all resolves perfectly. Who even thinks of something like this?"
[AMBIENT: QUIET STUDY HALL]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
The three of them were completely mesmerized, poring over the unique runes, trying to decipher the profound meaning behind each divine texture.
The elegance and complexity of the runes on those stone slabs were beyond anything the experts in the Heartlands could have imagined. After all, the divine textures of the upper world were the product of billions of years and countless cycles of rebirth. To compare them, the divine textures were like ancient masters who'd lived for millennia, while the runes of the Heartlands were like newborn infants. The gap was just immeasurable.
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
Those three four-star divine textures were a revelation. Over the next decade, the three of them would only manage to grasp a fraction of the divine textures' true nature. Eventually, they would abandon the old rune patterns of the Heartlands entirely, elevating their craft to a whole new level. And while they would never surpass Zane's own techniques, they would kick-start an era of innovation that would leave even the great Arnold in the dust.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC SCHEMING]
Seeing the rune masters dismantling Zane's rune-inscribed slabs, the other sect leaders were practically salivating, desperate to grab a piece for themselves. But a quick thought of the World Merchants' Guild—and the certainty that the Guild would never let them walk away with the slabs—made them reconsider. Maybe it would be easier to just ask Zane himself for help. Their minds immediately started racing, plotting ways they could bargain with him.
[SFX: THROAT CLEARING]
Just then, however, the old man at the head of the tournament cut through the scheming with an obvious, urgent question. "So... what are we going to do about the rest of the tournament?"
