Ep 1039 - Proving His Worth

Word count: 1204

[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

The king radiated an aura of pure power and regality. When he saw Zane charging through the massive army, he rose in one smooth, elegant motion.

[SFX: EARTH RUMBLING]

[SFX: METAL FORMING]

He let out a deafening roar, and the very landscape—the hills, the rivers, everything—screamed as it converged, twisting and solidifying into a longsword in his hand. He brought it crashing down on Zane.

The strike was a flash of brilliant light, but hidden within it was a deadly, world-ending intent.

But Zane wasn’t scared.

He’d been struggling against the endless waves of phantom soldiers, sure. But now, with the entire landscape condensed into a single blade, the battlefield had simplified. He wasn’t worried at all.

[SFX: POWERFUL ROAR]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

“Power of Dragon Scales!” Zane roared, unleashing the force of three thousand Dragon Scales. He swung his own sword in a powerful upward arc, and the two beams of light collided in mid-air.

But to his surprise, the strain was too much. His top-grade Relic-Class weapon couldn’t take it. It shattered.

[SFX: SWORD SHATTERING]

[SFX: METAL SPINNING THROUGH AIR]

Only half of the longsword remained, a jagged piece of metal sent spinning through the air—straight toward Fenton.

[SFX: METAL IMPACT]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

Fenton had been completely absorbed in watching Zane’s attack, never imagining a shard of a broken sword would come flying his way. He couldn’t react in time. The jagged blade slammed into his chest.

Fenton didn’t so much as flinch. His eyes were glued to Zane. Dying in the Illusionary Arena didn’t matter. All he cared about was seeing if Zane could survive this attack. If Zane lived, Fenton lost. If Zane died, Fenton won.

With Zane’s sword broken, how could he possibly block the king’s attack now?

A top-grade Relic-Class weapon just wasn’t strong enough for Zane anymore. A standard sword was only a couple of inches wide, its blade sharp but thin. Zane’s body could handle the power of five or six thousand Dragon Scales.

[SFX: SWORD BREAKING]

Since he’d just channeled the force of three thousand—on top of the immense power coming from the king’s sword—the Relic-Class weapon never stood a chance. It had simply snapped.

Zane couldn’t get a better sword in the Lower World. If he’d had access to The Armory in The Elysian Keep, things would be different. But he’d only cleared two stages of its true path, so he couldn’t use it yet.

[SFX: METAL CLASH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

In that split second, Zane tossed aside the useless hilt and caught the king’s massive blade with his bare hand.

[SFX: FLESH TEARING]

Blood burst from his palm the moment the blade bit in. But his other fist was already moving.

[SFX: DEVASTATING IMPACT]

[MUSIC: CLIMACTIC STRIKE]

He drove it into the king with the force of five thousand Dragon Scales. The king shattered — dissolving into fragments of golden light that scattered and vanished.

Fenton’s sword technique broke apart with it.

[AMBIENT: ILLUSIONARY ARENA — FADING]

In the arena, Fenton hadn’t moved. The broken sword was still buried in his chest. He looked at Zane.

“I lost,” he said.

Zane shook the blood from his hand. “Yes,” he said. “You lost.”

Fenton’s mouth twitched into something close to a smile. “This is a little uncomfortable. Let’s take this back to The Crucible.”

A moment later, both fighters opened their eyes.

[AMBIENT: THE CRUCIBLE]

Fenton crossed the floor and stopped in front of Zane. He bowed — a deep, unhurried bow.

“I used to carry certain assumptions about warriors who came up from the Lower World,” he said. “I see now that I was wrong. The Lower World is not as weak as I believed.”

Around them, the warriors of the Myriad Spirit House were quiet. Most of them nodded. They had grown up with the same assumptions — that ascenders who made it were flukes, that the gap between worlds was unbridgeable. What they had just watched made that harder to hold onto.

Zane just smiled and said nothing. He didn’t need to.

From the outside, none of them had seen the illusions inside the sword light. What they had seen was Zane barely holding on, his sword snapping — and then, somehow, punching Fenton’s technique apart with his bare fist. Nobody could explain it. They accepted it anyway.

Over the next few days, a handful of top-ranked warriors who had missed the fight came looking for a challenge. They didn’t get one. The others turned them away — if you want to fight Zane, beat Fenton first.

Zane returned to his corner and sat down. The reverence in the room was unmistakable — he could feel it in the way people moved around him, gave him space, kept their voices low when they passed. He didn’t particularly care. The strong were always strong. If he let other people’s opinions move him — in either direction — what right did he have to stand in this world at all?

He closed his eyes and went back to work.

Elsewhere in the Soulbound Sect, not everyone was at peace.

[AMBIENT: QUIET TEMPLE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Lately, the Saint Lord of the Soulbound Sect had been in a perpetually foul mood.

He desperately wanted to talk to Zane, to find some way to salvage the situation. He wondered if there was anything he could offer to make it worth Zane’s while to stay with the Soulbound Sect.

If Zane stayed, the Sect’s young talents would have a serious edge over their rivals in the other two tenth-grade sacred places in The Psychean Realm. With Zane’s power, they might even be able to unseat the Psychean Sanctum itself. The competition between the younger generation didn’t just decide bragging rights; it determined the pecking order of the great powers for years to come.

But then he remembered the Thirteen Palaces, and the thought died in his throat. The Palaces had already laid their claim on Zane; they knew exactly how valuable he was.

When he first saw Zane’s sword break in the Illusionary Arena, he’d considered offering him a second-rank, maybe even a low-grade Divine-Class weapon as a gift to win him over. But he’d immediately dismissed the idea. The Thirteen Palaces could match any offer he made, and likely double it.

The Saint Lord sighed, a bitter taste in his mouth. Letting a talent like that slip through his fingers… it was a mistake that would gnaw at him for a long, long time.

[AMBIENT: MEDITATION CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STRINGS]

Meanwhile, in the Myriad Spirit House, Zane was meditating quietly. He could already feel the Sixth Small Heavenly Tribulation gathering. He wondered if facing a Celestial Tribulation in the Upper World was any different from facing one in the Lower World.

Time passed in silence as he trained and waited. But the big shot who was supposed to see him never showed.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

A month later, an elder from the Soulbound Sect finally entered the Myriad Spirit House. He informed Zane that representatives from the Thirteen Palaces had arrived and were currently meeting with the Saint Lord.

Had they finally come to collect?
