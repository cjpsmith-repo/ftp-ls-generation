Ep 697 - Get Out of Town

Word count: 1043

[AMBIENT: TENSE ROOM]

“Besides the news about Lady Cora, we’ve picked up something else interesting,” Darius said, taking in Maddox’s grim expression.

“What is it?” Maddox asked, his voice flat.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

“Zane,” Darius answered simply. “He’s back.”

A cruel light sparked in Maddox’s eyes. “Zane,” he scoffed, the name tasting like venom and victory. “So, he actually came back.”

Whispers of Zane’s death had been circulating through the Heartlands for months, but Maddox never bought a single one. He’d seen Zane win the Wheel of Fortune with his own two eyes—a sure sign the man was blessed by destiny. People like that didn’t just die so easily. Maddox wouldn’t believe it until he was the one to put Zane in the ground himself.

“So, what’s the plan?” Darius asked, treading carefully around the malice radiating from his leader.

[MUSIC: DARK STRATEGIC THEME]

“Pull back our attacks on the Ethereal Spirit Sect for now,” Maddox commanded. “Have everyone camp a hundred miles out and wait for Simon’s men to show up. No one makes a move until I get there.”

Bringing Simon, the so-called Poison King, into the fold had been a gamble. The man was a former enemy, but Maddox himself had extended the offer to join his Corrupted Divine Kingdom. After weighing his options, Simon had proven pragmatic enough to set aside old grudges for the promise of power. Now he was on his way, a host of freelance warriors at his back.

The Ethereal Spirit Sect was a prize worth the trouble. There was a reason they were the number one sect in the Heartlands. Their protective magical array, while not as formidable as the one at Cloud Hall, would still chew through his army. And then there was Xander, a man Maddox knew better than to underestimate. With the combined power of the Septuplets, who fought as one, they were as deadly as a master from the Life-and-Death Realm.

It would be a brutal fight, but forcing their surrender would be a massive gain for his kingdom.

As Darius hurried off to deliver the orders, a strategy was already clicking into place in Maddox’s mind. A low, humorless laugh rumbled in his chest. “Let’s see you get out of this one, Zane.”

[SFX: WHOOSH OF LIGHT]

With that, he dissolved into a beam of light and shot toward Ethereal City.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CITY]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Back in the city, the air was thick with tension. Within the walls of the Ethereal Spirit Sect, Elder Xander stood at the heart of a massive Runic Cipher, his gaze fixed on the troubled sky. The Septuplets stood with him, their positions forming the distinct pattern of the Big Dipper, their faces grim as they stared up at the horde of warriors darkening the horizon.

Then Maddox's light streaked away from the formation and curved toward the city.

"Elder Xander," one of the Septuplets said quietly. "Maddox is heading for Ethereal City."

Xander's expression shifted. He knew exactly what that meant. Cora. He thought of how much he'd indulged her, how long he'd let her dig her heels in, and felt a familiar pang of regret. He wanted to go after Maddox — his strength was close enough that, here in the city, it might matter. But he couldn't leave.

"Should we pursue?" another of the Septuplets asked. "With him gone, we could strike."

Elder Xander shook his head. "It could be a trap. We hold position." His voice was steady. "We defend to the death."

The Septuplets said nothing more.

[AMBIENT: DINING ROOM]

At the lunch table, the conversation had been easy enough. Brent had leaned across and told Zane he intended to come to Cloud Hall and issue a formal challenge — and that Zane had better accept. Zane had smiled despite himself and agreed. Robin had smiled too; he knew his adopted son well.

Then Brent's hand had gone to his ring. He pulled out a small token. It had been yellow for weeks. Now it was red.

[SFX: HURRIED FOOTSTEPS]

"The sect is under attack," Brent said, already on his feet. He was gone before anyone could speak.

Zane shot to his feet. “I’m getting Lady Cora out of the city,” he told Robin.

Damn it. If they hadn't stayed for lunch, he and Cora would be hours away from Ethereal City by now. He couldn’t believe his timing—a casual meal with House Kensington, and the sky literally starts falling.

Robin nodded, his face pale. “This way. I’ll take you to her.”

[AMBIENT: URGENT FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

While Brent scrambled to get back to the Ethereal Spirit Sect, Robin led Zane at a dead run toward Cora’s chambers.

[AMBIENT: QUIET CHAMBER]

Inside, Cora was stewing. She was supposed to be leaving the estate, and she was furious about it. A table laden with her favorite dishes sat untouched; her mind was such a mess that she couldn't stomach a single bite.

[SFX: MASSIVE CRASH AND SPLINTERING WOOD]

Her thoughts were cut short by a deafening crash. Zane didn't bother with the door—he simply came through it, shattering the wood frame and two decorative screens into a thousand pieces.

Those screens were fine alabaster, priceless treasures she’d spent two years acquiring. Seeing them in ruins sent a hot spike of fury through her.

[MUSIC: ANGRY DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

“You!” she shrieked, pointing a trembling finger. “You’re going to pay for those!” The absolute nerve of this guy! He had no respect for her at all.

Robin, however, completely ignored his daughter’s tantrum. “Cora, forget the screens! You have to go now!” he yelled, his voice strained with desperation. This was hardly the time for a lecture, and he was at a loss for what to say to make her understand the danger.

[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]

Zane didn't bother trying to reason with her. He closed the distance in three long strides, grabbed her wrist in a non-negotiable grip, and snarled, “Let’s go.”

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE DESTRUCTION]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION THEME]

Without another word, he launched himself straight up, blasting through the ceiling in an explosion of splintered wood and twisted iron.

Her entire room, once a sanctuary of fine things, was reduced to rubble in the blink of an eye.
