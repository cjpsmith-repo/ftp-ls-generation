Ep 936 - Old and New Friends

Word count: 1125

[MUSIC: TENDER EMOTIONAL]

[SFX: ENERGY FLARE]

As Zane recounted his close calls, a spark of Vitality flared at Tia's fingertips. A sharp, icy chill seeped through his clothes and pressed against his chest.

A chill like that normally wouldn't faze him, but this one was potent enough that he felt the piercing cold even without raising his Vitality to block it. He knew what it was: her way of scolding him for being so reckless.

Wrapped up in their own world, they lost all track of time, an hour drifting by as they just talked.

[SFX: STRANGE BIRD CAWS]

The quiet was broken by a series of strange caws echoing from high above. A furry shape was plummeting toward them.

The little creature was a natural flier, able to bypass the restraints of the fifth layer of the Spatial Law and jump right out of refracted space.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT CRASH]

But having only just been born, its natural skill was still clumsy. It completely misjudged the distance and, with no real control over its speed, it tumbled out of the sky and slammed into the ground like a meteorite, carving a huge crater in the earth.

Tia let out a little gasp, her brow furrowing in annoyance. Their quiet moment was shattered. She glared at the creature in the crater. “What is that thing?”

[SFX: BABY DRAGON CAW]

The Ancestral Dragon had been left behind on The Compass Wheel. Clearly unhappy about being separated from Zane for so long, it had decided to just jump.

It shook its head hard, flinging dirt everywhere, then scrambled straight for Zane.

Zane was still holding Tia, and the little Ancestral Dragon wedged itself right between them, shoving her aside with a series of disapproving squawks.

Zane didn't know whether to be annoyed or amused. After all the trouble he’d gone through to get the little dragon, they hadn't had much time to bond on the way back from the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. He never figured it would be the jealous type.

The Ancestral Dragon had imprinted on Zane, seeing him as family. And in its possessive little mind, no one else was allowed to get that close.

Tia, meanwhile, was just irritated. The creature’s appearance was… odd. It looked almost like a furry seahorse, and it took her a second to even realize it might be a dragon. Frowning, she reached out to shove it away.

[SFX: SHARP CRY]

[MUSIC: DANGER STING]

The moment her hand moved toward it, the little dragon's cry turned razor-sharp. A burst of power erupted from its small body — raw and instinctive — and Tia stumbled back several steps, her expression shifting from irritation to genuine wariness.

It wasn't just Tia. Cora, standing nearby, felt it too — a pressure that had nothing to do with cultivation rank. The people waiting outside the palace felt it as well. Not the aura of a warrior. Something older. Something that felt like it ruled.

Only Dorothea's expression remained unchanged.

[SFX: MAGICAL PULSE]

The flower in her right eye turned slowly. She extended one hand, and a faint halo drifted outward, settling over the dragon like a net. The pressure vanished. Tia and Cora steadied themselves.

“It's just hatched,” Dorothea said calmly. “Its intelligence is about that of a three-year-old child. Don't provoke it.”

Zane scooped the dragon up and glared at it. It glared back, unrepentant. He still hadn't figured out what to do with the thing — he'd leave that to the Cobalt Dragon once everything settled down.

“Is this actually a dragon?” Tia asked, studying it with open skepticism. It looked nothing like any dragon she had ever imagined.

Zane nodded.

[SFX: BABY DRAGON GROWL]

The dragon made a low, hostile sound in Tia's direction.

“It's kind of cute though,” Cora said.

The dragon's demeanor shifted instantly. It made a strange, pleased sound, wriggled out of Zane's arms, and launched itself at Cora — licking her face with its small tongue in a display of enthusiastic affection.

Zane stared. He had no idea what to make of that.

Tia was less amused. Her eyes moved past the dragon to Dorothea — to the casual way she had suppressed the aura, the faint halo produced without any apparent effort. 

She tried to read Dorothea's cultivation and found nothing she could place. Whatever Dorothea's Vitality was, it was purer and more condensed than anything Tia had encountered, even among the Soul Sea masters she had faced.

“Zane,” Tia said quietly. “Who is she?”

“I told you about the Demi-Immortal Race,” Zane said. “She's one of them. All seven of them came back with me.”

Tia absorbed that. Then: “Why?”

Zane's expression shifted slightly. “That's the other thing I needed to tell you. This visit — it's short. I'll be leaving again in a few days.”

[MUSIC: TENSION STRINGS]

“You're leaving again?!”

Tia and Cora said it at the same time. Neither had expected him to be back for less than half a day before announcing another departure.

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR ATMOSPHERE]

Nearby, the Compass Wheel continued its slow rotation, but now six people stood upon it. Beside them lay Brianna, the girl from the Dragon-Blooded.

[SFX: POWER SURGE]

Every single one of them, from the six standing to the one lying down, radiated an incredible power. The aura pulsing from them was far beyond that of even the mightiest warriors of the Soul Sea Realm.

Hobbes and the others exchanged uneasy looks. The last group Zane brought back was intimidating, but these people were on another level. The really troubling part? They didn’t seem to be on friendly terms with Zane, either. Any one of them looked like they could destroy The Heartlands with a flick of the wrist. Pissing them off would be suicide.

They were cold and aloof, broadcasting a clear 'stay away' vibe. Even Kieran, who could usually talk his way through anything, couldn't find an opening. The sheer difference in power was suffocating; it stole the words right out of his mouth.

[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

Zane met the cold glare of the young man in black with an indifferent look of his own. “It's only been a couple of hours,” he said flatly. “What's the rush?”

“Hmph,” the young man in black snorted. “I have plenty of time. I just have no reason to waste it on you.”

Zane offered a faint smile, his gaze drifting right past the young man to settle on someone else. He cupped his hands respectfully toward Vorn, who stood a short distance away. “Vorn, thank you for getting me back to the Heartlands. There's just one last thing I need to do before we go. I need to borrow The Compass Wheel.”
