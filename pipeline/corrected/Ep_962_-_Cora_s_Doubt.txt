Ep 962 - Cora’s Doubt
Word count: 1033
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
The old man’s words took Zane by surprise, too, but it wasn’t the Divine Weapon that had him reeling.
Sure, Divine Weapons were incredibly rare and valuable. But with Zane’s current knowledge, seeing one wasn’t a total shock. He had a Black Star Pickax of his own, after all.
What really floored him was what the old man had just said.
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]
Zane knew that warriors at the Soul Sea Realm could manifest a Soul Sea within their own bodies. But those who reached the Spirit Supreme Realm could take it a step further, solidifying their own life force—their Vitality—to build an entire world of their own. For instance, he knew of a girl who had worked on her Inner World for a hundred thousand years, only to create a tiny island where the creatures were still in a primitive state.
Warriors at that level had long since outgrown the need for things like Storage Rings. Their Inner Worlds made such small trinkets obsolete.
[SFX: DRAMATIC REVELATION CHORD]
This old man also stored his treasures within his own body. But according to him, there was a master—a master at the Soul Sea Realm—living inside that world. That was inconceivable.
An Inner World was formed from solidified Vitality, and the life within it was sparked by borrowing the power of thunder, breeding and evolving at an agonizingly slow pace.
Once those beings developed a certain level of sentience, they could then tap into the Vitality of the Inner World they inhabited and begin to cultivate on their own.
But the space within these worlds was limited, and the resources were scarce. They were, after all, miniature worlds.
And yet, somehow, someone inside this old man’s Inner World had managed to cultivate all the way to the Soul Sea Realm. Just how vast was his Inner World? Just how powerful was this old man?
Seeing the shock written all over Zane’s face, the old man just waved a hand. “Alright, I’ve given you both your gifts. Your wedding is the main event here, so don’t mind this old man. Go on, get to it. We can talk later if you want!”
Tia bowed respectfully, humbled by the incredible gift. A Divine-Class weapon might be a simple trinket in the old man’s eyes, but to her, it was a priceless treasure. She knew he’d only given it to her for Zane’s sake, but that didn’t lessen her gratitude in the slightest.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR WEDDING VENUE]
[MUSIC: GENTLE ROMANTIC MELODY]
Just then, Cora appeared in the distance, dressed in her plain, everyday clothes. She’d stayed in the kitchen as long as she could — she didn’t want to watch this. She was generous, but she wasn’t that generous. The only reason she’d come out at all was the Ancestral Dragon. It had vanished without warning, and the thought of losing it had pulled her out of the kitchen and all the way here.
“Cora!”
“Sister!”
Xander and Brent shouted at the same time, relief washing over them.
The Ancestral Dragon, still cradled in Cora’s arms, seemed to realize Zane was perfectly fine. It let out two short cries — then twisted around and launched itself back toward the kitchen and the meal it had abandoned.
[MUSIC: TENSE UNDERTONE]
Truth be told, the members of House Kensington weren’t exactly thrilled about today’s wedding. They didn’t want it to happen. But it would have been rude not to show up, so when Zane invited them, they swallowed their resentment and came.
Before Cora appeared, Xander and Brent had been worried sick, afraid the whole day would be miserable for her.
Cora just gave her brother and great-grandfather a small nod.
She’d already made her peace with not being jealous of Tia — or told herself she had. But seeing them together, standing there as a perfect match, was something else entirely. That kind of feeling didn’t just disappear because you decided it should.
Anyone with the Black-Violet Core Physique had the potential to master the Violet Core Realm—a power so rare it was almost unheard of in the entire universe.
The thing was, after activating her Violet-Core Physique, Cora had only ever used it defensively, pulling massive swords from within it or once yanking Zane and Tia into the Violet Core Realm to save them. Other than that, she never used her powers and actively avoided that part of herself.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
Cora was turning to leave when a voice reached her — not through the air, but directly into her mind. “Little girl. Over here.”
She looked around, baffled. No one nearby seemed to have spoken. Then she spotted the old man, watching her with a glint in his eye.
She almost kept walking. But he’d said something — something about Zane — that made her stop and sit.
At some point during the exchange, Zane’s attention had drifted toward Cora and the old man. Tia noticed. A sharp jolt of Vitality shot from her fingertips into his hand. Zane flinched, met her gaze, and offered a silent, sheepish apology. It was her wedding day, after all.
The old man looked at her for a moment. “Ever think about becoming a master? I could find you a teacher, you know.”
Cora stared at him, her expression turning strange. She felt like he was just talking nonsense. She was only sitting here listening to him because he’d promised she could stay with Zane for countless years. Now he was rambling about teachers and masters. What did any of that have to do with Zane?
Her patience was wearing thin again. “No, thanks,” she said dismissively. “Not interested.”
[SFX: DRAMATIC EMPHASIS]
“What? Not interested?” The old man gawked at her. “Do you have any idea what I’m offering? You could become a cultivator so powerful you could look down on everyone else in the entire universe!”
“So what if I’m more powerful than everyone?” Cora shot back. “What’s the fun in that?” She was starting to think the old man was messing with her. She stood up, ready to leave.
[MUSIC: REVELATION STRINGS]
Watching her, the old man paused for a moment before it finally clicked. He now understood: this young lady had absolutely no interest in martial arts. But instead of being disappointed, he realized he’d just found the perfect way to convince her.
