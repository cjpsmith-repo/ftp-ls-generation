Ep 1319 - Who Is That Woman?

Word count: 1095

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL RISING]
[AMBIENT: MASSIVE ARENA CROWD MURMUR]
The competition for the top spots in the Illusion Battlefield was heating up, reaching its most intense stage yet.
On the massive pyramids in the Heartlands, the names and ranks of the warriors flickered and shifted constantly. 
[SFX: CROWD ROAR]
A roar went up from the spectators, each person completely hooked, cheering for their favorite competitor.
“Lawson’s in first! That’s insane! Wasn’t he ranked in the two hundreds just a minute ago?”
“Nigel’s back in the top three! Wow, that was fast!”
Making it into the top one hundred was a huge deal. Those warriors were seen as the most promising talents in the entire universe, and every species across the cosmos was watching the rankings, rooting for their own.
The entire Illusion Battlefield was split into fifty different supreme worlds, each filled with powerful fighters. But so far, none of these top-tier warriors had gone head-to-head. At this point, no one really knew who was truly the strongest.
[MUSIC: GENTLE HOPEFUL STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: QUIET MACHINERY HUM]
Meanwhile, Tia was channeling her Vitality into the machinery around her, but her eyes were glued to the names flashing on the pyramid. When she saw Zane’s name listed among the top one hundred, a wave of relief washed over her so powerful it almost made her dizzy. She knew enough about how the Battlefield worked to understand exactly what that ranking meant for his chances.
Every warrior was fighting for a spot on that list. The stakes were impossibly high—the universe itself seemed to favor those who made it. Their people back home treasured them, because their success could determine the fate of their entire civilization. A world’s legacy could be wiped out at the end of a grand era, with only a few precious things carrying over to the next.
“What group is Cora with? How did she get to sixtieth place?” someone exclaimed nearby.
[MUSIC: TENSE BROODING]
Tia felt a hot surge of frustration. How could Cora be ranked higher than Zane?
“Don’t really know her,” someone else replied. “But I heard she has some serious connections. If I remember right, she’s from the Violet Core Realm.”
*Violet Core Realm. Violet-Core Physique.* Tia’s mind clicked. “So that’s it.” She remembered Cora controlling several massive swords with her incredible power, but she’d never guessed the Violet Core Realm had enough influence in the Upper World to practically guarantee someone a spot in the top one hundred.
A flicker of disbelief crossed her face—someone that powerful, taken care of so quietly? She’d been nothing but disrespected since she ascended, and it stung to think she might have been better off never coming up here at all. In the Jadeheart Sanctuary, apparently, being merely Soul Sea Realm counted for nothing.
[SFX: WHIP CRACK WHOOSH]
A sudden *whoosh* of air snapped her out of her thoughts as the shadow of a whip cracked toward her.
The whip’s phantom tip exploded right in front of her face. It never touched her skin, but the force of the impact shot toward her like a sharp blade.
[SFX: ICE CRYSTALS FORMING]
In a flash, ice crystals bloomed across her face, perfectly spaced, forming a protective lattice of pure Vitality.
[SFX: ICE SHATTERING]
The blade-like force slammed into the ice, shattering the crystals with a sharp crack that echoed the whip. The impact would have sliced her face open, and while she could regenerate from almost anything, a blast that strong would have taken time to heal. She was lucky the crystals had formed on instinct.
Beneath the glittering shards, her expression was pure fury.
[SFX: CHAIR SCRAPING]
“What do you want?” Tia shot to her feet, glaring at the middle-aged woman standing nearby. She was volunteering her Vitality to power the Jadeheart Chamber of Illusions, not serving as some slave.
The woman who had attacked her just smirked. “I’ve already told you, no one is allowed to talk here. You just keep making the same mistake. Consider that a warning. If it happens again, it won’t just be a crack of the whip.”
“We were talking quietly. It wasn’t affecting our work,” Tia said, her voice icy. “Besides, other people are talking too. Why are you always coming after me?”
[SFX: WHIP CRACK]
The woman gripped her whip, a sneer twisting her lips. “The people around you? Let me remind you who you are. You’re just a warrior who ascended from the Lower World, aren’t you?”
“Yes, I am,” Tia answered, her chin high.
[SFX: BLADE WHISTLING THROUGH AIR]
Before the woman could say another word, a broken black blade came spinning silently out of nowhere and flashed past her in the blink of an eye.
[SFX: FLESH SLICING]
[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]
It sheared clean through her right arm.
Blood sprayed as she let out a scream. The other warriors pouring their Vitality into the room barely reacted—losing a limb wasn’t shocking to people like them—but they rose to their feet anyway, watching. An attack like this, in front of the Sanctuary’s own leaders, meant whoever had done it had to carry real weight here.
“Who did this? Why?!” the woman shrieked, clutching the stump of her arm. She was sharp enough to know better than to have made an enemy of someone this dangerous without realizing it—but she couldn’t think of a single thing she’d actually done wrong.
A figure stepped out of thin air. Her cultivation was clearly immense—Divine Transformation realm—and more than a few sharp-eyed warriors in the room recognized her at once: the Ninth Palace Master of the Jadeheart Sanctuary, herself ranked in the sanctuary’s own top hundred.
“I cut off your arm to teach you some manners,” she said coldly. Then she turned toward Tia, and her expression melted from ice into warmth. “Is this... Tia?”
“I’m Tia,” she said, still caught off guard by the sudden, careful politeness.
“Would you mind coming with me?” Priscilla asked.
Tia’s confusion was written all over her face, but she gave a slight nod. “Of course. But I still need to channel my Vitality…”
“You don’t have to worry about that,” Priscilla said, her smile never wavering. “Please, just follow me.”
So, Tia left her station and followed her.
The entire room watched as Priscilla personally led Tia away.
[AMBIENT: CROWDED ROOM MURMURS]
The moment they were gone, the space erupted in whispers.
“Who is that woman? Why is Priscilla being so nice to her?”
“Well, she is beautiful. I bet some big shot from the Jadeheart Sanctuary has his eye on her.”
“I doubt it! If looks were all it took, why are all the other gorgeous women still stuck here with us?”
[SFX: LEATHER CREAKING]
The middle-aged woman just stared after them, cradling the stump of her ruined arm, gritting her teeth in a silent mix of fury and humiliation.
