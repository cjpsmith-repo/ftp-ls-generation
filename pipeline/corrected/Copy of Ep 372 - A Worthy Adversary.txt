Ep 372 - A Worthy Adversary

Word count: 1101

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

[AMBIENT: CAVE ECHOES]

The fireball erupted from the cave's mouth like a miniature sun—tall as a man, radiating waves of heat that distorted the air around it. Pure, lethal danger screamed toward Lewis, who stood just outside the entrance. A direct hit would vaporise him in an instant.

Lewis cursed himself, his mind racing. He'd been inches from the blue treasure box when the fireball exploded outward. The heat seared his face even from this distance. He had no choice but to scramble backward or be burned to a crisp.

[SFX: TUMBLING FALL]

He dodged, throwing himself to the side, but the sheer force of the blast caught him anyway. The concussive wave sent him tumbling off the platform, his arms flailing as he fought for balance.

[AMBIENT: ARCHWAY]

Back at the archway, the masters watched it all play out on the Scrying Slate.

The old man in white from the Providence Sect slapped his thigh with genuine disappointment. "Damn, what a shame. He almost had it."

Toby shook his head slowly."An inch farther, a step closer, and that box would've been his."  His voice carried a note of respect—Lewis had shown real courage, even though it ended in failure.

But the other masters weren't so disappointed. In fact, a few of them looked downright pleased by Lewis's misfortune, their lips curling into barely concealed smirks. One less competitor meant better odds for their own disciples.

[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]

The thirteen disciples of Cloud Hall had watched the entire spectacle unfold from below, their formation still intact as they tracked the cloaked figure's desperate escape attempt.

"Get ready to finish him!" one of the Cloud Hall disciples hissed, his voice tight with anticipation. Their eyes locked on the cloaked man's falling form, muscles tensed and ready to strike the moment he landed.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

Then, a voice cut through the chaos. "You idiots! Move!"

[SFX: WHOOSHING FLAMES]

The Cloud Hall disciples broke their stance, scattering in every direction as the scorching boulder of pure flame barreled past. They had been setting up the Formation of the Twelve Signs to use against the man in the cloak, but the fireball completely ruined their plan.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Lennon was caught off guard, too. Even as the best of the Cloud Hall disciples, he had no choice but to drop the formation. Still, he was sure he could take the cloaked man on his own. He was about to move in for the kill himself.

But in the chaos of the broken formation, the man in the cloak made his move. 

[SFX: POWERFUL WING FLAP]

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSH]

His cloak billowed out, revealing a pair of massive, bird-like wings. With a powerful downstroke, he launched himself into the sky, soaring over the forest and vanishing from sight. Lennon’s chance was gone.

"He can't run forever," Lennon said, his voice cold as ice. "I'll find him." 

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRONE]

Just then, he sensed a new presence nearby.

As he was silently cursing the cloaked man’s escape, a figure appeared on the far side of the valley, heading for the enchanted barrier.

[SFX: EXCITED SHOUT]

"Look! It’s that guy—the one at the Third Level of the Adept Realm!" a disciple who spotted him first shouted excitedly.

Lennon, however, didn’t look pleased at all.

[SFX: SHARP COMMAND]

"Quiet," he snapped. "I see him."

The other Cloud Hall members exchanged surprised glances. Lennon's true strength was beyond their comprehension; he was always so calm and collected. It was rare to see anything get under his skin.

[MUSIC: TRANSITION STING]

Zane ran as hard as he could, his eyes fixed on the shimmering barrier ahead. The enchanted wall pulsed with deadly energy, its wind blades visible as faint distortions in the air. Behind him, he knew the other disciples were watching him. But it didn't matter; he had faced contempt from higher-realm disciples, survived a Red Fire Snake, and earned his place on this trial through skill, not privilege.

[SFX: DEEP BREATH, FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]

He summoned his Shadow Phantoms with a thought. The six dark figures materialized around him, identical copies born from his martial technique. Where others might hesitate before such a barrier, Zane had already calculated his approach. His phantoms would serve as both shield and distraction, their ability to reform making them perfect for this deadly obstacle. He moved toward the barrier's edge, ready to test his strategy.

[SFX: WIND BLADES SLICING]

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLE]

The six Shadow Phantoms stood firm against the volley of razor-sharp wind blades. An agile fighter would have dodged. Zane just let his indestructible body take the hits. Two phantoms moved to intercept the blades, their solid forms getting carved up in the process. The wind left deep gashes across their bodies.

[SFX: SHATTERING]

After a moment, the two phantoms at the front couldn't withstand the assault any longer and shattered into dust.

Instantly, another pair stepped forward to take their place, shielding Zane from the ongoing barrage. He had six in total, and the two that had just been destroyed were already reforming, ready to cycle back into the fight.

[AMBIENT: QUIET FOREST]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS]

Meanwhile, hidden in the forest, Lewis pulled back his hood. He’d shed the cloak, revealing a handsome face framed by striking red hair and a mischievous grin. He was, after all, known as the most charming man in the Providence Sect.

He hadn't fled, despite Lennon's fury. He’d simply slipped into the woods to see what would happen next. Only someone with supreme confidence—or a death wish—would risk their life for a bit of entertainment.

Failing to snag the blue treasure box hadn't soured his mood in the slightest. In fact, Lewis was having a great time.

[MUSIC: INTRIGUED CONTEMPLATION]

He watched Zane, a flicker of amusement in his eyes. "Now this is interesting," he murmured to himself. "How is he blocking those wind blades?" 

Lewis studied the six shadowy constructs with the keen eye of someone who'd spent years analyzing combat techniques. "Those constructs are bizarre," he continued, his voice tinged with both admiration and curiosity. "They reform the second they're shattered. If I'm not mistaken, that has to be a tier-five martial arts skill."

He crossed his arms, reassessing everything he thought he knew about this trial. A slight frown creased his brow as he contemplated the implications.

I figured my only real competition here would be Lennon and Frank—and neither of them could actually take me. But then you have this guy, barely at the Third Level of the Adept Realm, pulling out this incredible inner power and these wild abilities. Maybe I’ve been underestimating the field.

Lewis shook his head slowly, a rueful chuckle escaping his lips. "Maybe I've been underestimating the field," he admitted quietly, though the prospect seemed to energize rather than concern him. His eyes gleamed with renewed interest. "This trial just got a hell of a lot more fun."
