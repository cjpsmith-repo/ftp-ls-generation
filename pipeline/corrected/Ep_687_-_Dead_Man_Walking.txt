Ep 687 - Dead Man Walking

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

The year Zane left without a word was the same year the Celestial Tomb of Vesper was unsealed. Warriors from every major sect flocked to it, chasing rumors of incredible power. Many found it, but just as many found their graves. The upheaval sent shockwaves through the power structures of The Heartlands.

Zane had conquered the Wheel of Fortune first, but when other warriors returned to their sects, he'd vanished. Cloud Hall remained silent. Most assumed him dead.

The conclusion came easily. Enchanted Lamps meant nothing inside the tomb stayed secret. Tales of its mind-bending trials spread quickly. If Spirit-Tribulation masters could be obliterated instantly, what chance did a Soul Realm warrior have?

“So, everyone thinks I’m dead?” Zane asked, a tired smile touching his lips. He knew he shouldn’t have just disappeared on everyone. “Never mind that now,” he said, waving it off. “I need to speak with your leader.”

[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL DRUMS]

“I don’t care if you’re Zane Lowell or not,” one of the sect’s Spirit-Tribulation masters shot back, clearly unimpressed. “You’re not one of us. You’d better get moving.” The Heartlands were in chaos, and he was in no mood to deal with outsiders, no matter how famous their names were.

[SFX: ENERGY SWIRL]

As he spoke, a pale green Vitality began to swirl slowly around Master Croy's body. He cultivated a poison technique — nothing compared to the great poison scriptures, but vicious enough in the right hands.

“Master Croy, he just saved our lives!” Tessa cried out.

“Tessa, shut your mouth!” Master Croy snapped, his glare silencing her. “You three, get over here. Now.” He turned his cold gaze back to Zane. “Listen, friend, the whole region is a mess. If you don’t leave now, don’t blame me for what happens next.”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Zane had only come to the Sea Wyrm Sect to get information about the current state of the Heartlands. He never expected to be met with such open hostility from its masters.

His expression hardened. “Your people tried to kill me while I was unconscious,” he said, his voice dropping to an icy calm. “I think it’s time we settled that score.”

“Don’t you dare stand there and slander my people!” Master Croy retorted. “They would never do something like that!”

“Master Croy,” Tessa began, trying to intervene.

“Be quiet!” he cut her off again before she could get a word out.

[SFX: ANGRY MURMUR]

“Hmph. I bet you’re just here to stir up trouble,” another master sneered. “Don’t be so arrogant. The Sea Wyrm Sect is a Third-Grade sect. You’re just a beginner at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. The nerve on you.”

[SFX: DEEP RESONANT PULSE]

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Zane's eyes flashed. He said nothing — but something shifted in the air around him. His soul had grown vast and still over long months of cultivation, and now he let a fraction of its weight fall outward.

It hit like a collapsing mountain.

The two masters staggered. The pressure crushed down on their chests, forced the breath from their lungs, and sent a splitting pain raking through their skulls. They had faced dangerous men before, but this was different — this was something deep and structural, pressing on the very foundations of their spirit.

Behind Zane, Tessa's face went white. The two disciples beside her buckled at the knees. None of them could understand how the calm young man standing in front of them had become something so terrifying in a single breath. Tessa's eyes, wide with fear, held something else underneath — a bright, involuntary awe.

Master Croy's jaw tightened. He couldn't afford to let an outsider make a display like this at his own gate. He rallied his Vitality and prepared to strike.

[SFX: RUSH OF AIR]

Lavender slipped out of Zane's sword, already holding her spear. The tip found Master Croy's throat in an instant — one tremor away from the end. Her red eyes were clear and calm and utterly without mercy. Master Croy understood immediately, with absolute certainty, that if he moved, she would not hesitate.

He went very still.

[SFX: GUST OF WIND]

Sea Wyrm Sect's leader, Elder Marsh, came to a stop above the gate, hovering in the air with the ease of a man entirely comfortable with his own power. His eyes swept the scene — and landed on Lavender. Whatever doubt had remained in him dissolved at once. Everyone who knew Zane Lowell's legend knew about the sword spirit who could take human form. There was no one else in the Heartlands like him.

"Young hero Zane," he called out, his voice carrying genuine warmth. "Forgive me for not coming out to meet you sooner. What brings you to the Sea Wyrm Sect?"

Zane's expression didn't soften. "Three Core-Forged warriors from your sect attacked me while I was healing in the ocean," he said evenly. "I came for an explanation."

Shaw's face darkened. He turned a sharp look on Master Croy. "Who are they? What Core-Forged men of mine would do something like this?"

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Tessa flinched, then her expression shifted to one of profound apology. “It was Master Croy, sir. He wouldn’t let me. I tried to report it several times.”

Elder Marsh shot Master Croy a furious look before his features softened into a kind smile as he turned to Tessa. “Tell me everything. From the beginning.”

Shaw hadn't become Sect Leader by being a fool. He could guess Zane's motives. If the accusation was false, he'd defend his sect's name. If true, that was another matter.

Tessa quickly recounted the whole story: how they’d found Zane at the bottom of the ocean, dragged him onto a reef to recover, and then been ambushed by the freelance warriors right after running into Elder Holt.

When she finished, Elder Marsh fixed Master Croy with another piercing glare. He then turned to Zane, his tone polite and sincere. “Zane, this is entirely our fault. I am deeply sorry. Please, come to my private chambers so we can discuss this further.”

[AMBIENT: QUIET SECT INTERIOR]

Zane found himself curious. Even for a Third-Grade Sect, the Sea Wyrm had made a name for itself in The Heartlands, and the men he’d just faced had a dangerous edge—a killing intent you didn’t see in the average disciple. He hadn’t planned on bringing any of this up, but the two masters had forced his hand with their abrasive welcome.

He wasn’t about to turn down the Sect Leader’s invitation now.
