Ep 525 - Trinity Killing Array
Word count: 1138
[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
Zane had learned long ago to avoid speaking in absolutes.
The world of cultivation held too many variables, too many hidden factors that could shift the balance of power in an instant.
So when Elder Ingrid had posed her question, he'd given her the only honest answer he could offer.
"Eighty percent sure." The same measured response he'd provided moments earlier.
A weighted silence descended upon the hall.
"Eighty percent? Ha!" Emanuel Drew sneered. "Kids these days talk a big game. Aren't you afraid of biting off more than you can chew?"
Zane didn't even look at him. He knew Emanuel was just trying to get a rise out of him. Besides, it wasn't an empty boast. Zane had already blocked one of Maddox's punches during the Martial Tournament using an enchanted barrier. Maddox hadn't been using his full strength then, but Zane had only needed to tweak a few divine textures to deflect the attack.
Thankfully, Emanuel's taunt fell flat. The other elders maintained their silence, exchanging meaningful glances. They weren't blind—each had witnessed the extraordinary power radiating from Zane's divine textures. What they'd seen with their own eyes carried more weight than any boastful claim.
One elder stroked his beard thoughtfully. “Then let's see what this Order-protecting array can accomplish. If it doesn't work, we still have the option of surrendering Zane to Maddox.”
[AMBIENT: FOOTSTEPS ECHOING]
After a moment of silent communication through their Vitality, the elders reached consensus. They began filing out, their robes whispering against the polished floor.
[SFX: DOOR SLAM]
As the others left, Emanuel shot Zane one final glare, his jaw clenched tight with barely suppressed fury. Without a word, he stormed from the hall, his footsteps reverberating with indignation.
Once the last echo faded and Zane stood alone in the vast chamber, he glanced toward the heavens. A small smile played across his lips—the expression of someone who knew more than he'd revealed. With deliberate casualness, he made a subtle gesture, fingers tracing an almost imperceptible pattern in the air.
[AMBIENT: ICE PALACE]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STRINGS]
Miles away, inside the Ice Palace, Tia—deep in Secluded Cultivation—felt an Vitality talisman flare to life in her hands. A knowing smile spread across her face. You knew all along.
Of course, I knew you were spying on me, Zane sent back.
Tia rolled her eyes. Why would I ever bother spying on you?
You should probably thank me. I just single-handedly calmed down your entire council of elders, Zane projected with a laugh. Still, keep an eye on Emanuel. The guy's ambitious. He'll try to pull something when it matters most.
Tia's smile faded. Yeah, I know.
She had seen Emanuel's ambition firsthand. He was behind half the trouble the elders stirred up, always the one fanning the flames. But House Drew was a Third-Grade House in Cloudhaven. Getting rid of him wouldn't be easy without a damn good reason.
We don't have much time, Zane's thoughts came again. I'm going to set up the Order-protecting array now.
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Zane gathered a pile of divine textures and filled Cedric in on the plan. Cedric’s face lit up. He’d been dying to see the legendary Order-protecting array in action.
From a bird's-eye view, the suspended citadel of Cloud Hall looked like a giant, oval egg. According to Zane's design, the four thousand, nine hundred, and three divine textures would be deployed in three batches.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL EXPLANATION]
"This follows the Trinity Killing Array Formation," the Cobalt Dragon explained. "A scaled-down version of something far greater."
Zane glanced up. "Trinity Killing Array?"
"One of the Three Great Mysterious Arrays—the most powerful formations ever conceived. What we're building is only an imitation—perhaps a billionth of the real thing. But it'll be more than enough."
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
At the first placement point, Zane set a divine texture down and channeled energy into it. The paper burned to ash, leaving the rune etched into the ground.
[AMBIENT: BUSY ACTIVITY]
Cedric and his assistants followed Zane's instructions, placing textures throughout Cloud Hall—on walls, floors, archways, rooftops. Their positions looked random, but occupied exact, deliberate points.
Two Inner-Core deacons carried a large formation plate—the control mechanism for the entire array.
[SFX: HOURS PASSING]
After a full month of work, the formation was complete.
[AMBIENT: CLEAR OPEN SKY]
Dozens of elders and house leaders hovered above the sect, watching.
Tia floated nearby. "Are you ready?"
"Ready," Zane said. "The Aether Crystals are in place."
He took a slow breath. All four thousand, nine hundred and three textures had to function perfectly together.
[SFX: FORMATION PLATE TURNING]
He rotated the Array Plate.
[SFX: DIVINE TEXTURES LIGHTING UP IN SEQUENCE]
[MUSIC: BUILDING CRESCENDO]
Light blazed across Cloud Hall. One texture lit up, then dozens, then hundreds—merging into one colossal divine texture hovering over the sect. At its center, a bolt of lightning tore through the air.
SFX: THUNDER CRACK]
A low rumble of thunder faded, leaving a crackle of arcane energy in its wake.
"First area is clear," Zane said, turning the Array Plate in his hand.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL MIST]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
At the second location, where the Madness Trap lay buried, a faint mist clung to the ground. It looked completely ordinary until over a thousand divine textures flared to life, then just as quickly went dark. The trap was set.
Zane turned the Array Plate one last time, and the textures in the final location lit up in unison.
[SFX: MAGICAL ACTIVATION]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
As they activated, a fine mist began to form high in the air—a strange and beautiful sight. Sunlight caught the swirling vapor, painting a rainbow across the sky. The mist then coalesced, forming a massive enchanted barrier that shimmered into existence like a giant, inverted bowl covering all of Cloud Hall. This was the 'Light of Water,' the primary defense of the Trinity Killing Array.
A cool breeze swept over the onlookers as they stared up at the countless ripples dancing across the barrier's surface.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]
"It's just a big bubble," an elder scoffed, flying toward the edge of the shield. "I bet it'll pop with one touch. How is that supposed to stop Maddox? I doubt it could even handle my spear!"
A black spear materialized in his hand. He aimed it at the barrier and began to channel his Spear Intent.
[SFX: BATTLE CRY]
[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]
With a furious shout, the elder charged, driving his spear forward with the incredible power of his Spirit-Tribulation Realm cultivation.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
The spear slammed into the barrier and simply stopped, its force creating a cascade of shimmering ripples that spread out from the point of impact. Nothing else happened.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]
The other elders stared, their skepticism melting into stunned delight.
