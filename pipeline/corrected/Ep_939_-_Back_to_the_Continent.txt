Ep 939 - Back to the Continent

Word count: 1154

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: DEPARTURE THEME]

The Spirit Supreme warriors from the Demi-Immortal Race had bought Zane some breathing room in the Heartlands, but now, it was time for him to go.

Tia and Cora weren't happy to hear it. He’d only been with them for a couple of hours, and he had to leave already? 

But Tia knew the powerful warriors on The Compass Wheel weren't exactly Zane's friends, so this wasn't the time to push it. All she could do was hope he’d come back to the Heartlands as soon as he was done.

Meanwhile, Zane figured it would be too much of a hassle to bring Brianna with him to the main continent. He decided it was best to leave her in the care of the World Merchants' Guild.

Of course, Dorothea wasn't happy about it. The Cobalt Dragon had already told Zane that while the Soul Sealing Spell was tough to break, he—the dragon—already had a few leads and didn't need any help from the Demi-Immortal Race. So, Zane wasn't too worried about Dorothea being bent out of shape. Besides, it seemed like the girl couldn't stand her anyway.

[SFX: MAGICAL RUNE ACTIVATION]

[AMBIENT: AERIAL WIND]

Once Vorn finished his preparations, he waved a hand, etching a blood-red, cross-shaped rune into the air above Oracle City. He also left a locator talisman behind.

A moment later, the Compass Wheel lifted slowly into the sky, heading southeast just as Zane had directed.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Thanks to the memories of the world of chaos, Zane already had a clear picture of the world map in his head. Land made up only twenty percent of the surface; the other eighty percent was just endless ocean. The main continents, like the Divine Kingdom Continent and the Heartlands, were all clustered on the western side. North of the Divine Kingdom Continent was a huge stretch of untamed wilderness.

The main continent, however, was the exception. It was isolated on the far side of the world of chaos, and because of that, it had developed its own unique cultures and traditions.

[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND]

[SFX: SHIP CREAKING]

A strong wind howled against the sides of the Compass Wheel. Once it cleared the southeastern edge of the Heartlands, it shot forward, picking up incredible speed.

Without a teleportation array, getting to the main continent meant crossing nearly half the world of chaos. Even for Spirit Supreme warriors, a trip like that wasn't going to be quick.

Zane knew he could just pop back to Vesper's celestial tomb and use the teleportation array to get there directly, but he didn't fully trust the seven warriors from the Demi-Immortal Race. There was no way he was going to wake Vesper from his long sleep just for them.

[AMBIENT: OCEAN WAVES]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

On the horizon, the blue of the sky and sea bled into one another until it was impossible to tell where one ended, and the other began.

Most of the time, the Compass Wheel was silent. The Demi-Immortals sat cross-legged, eyes closed, deep in meditation. Spirit Supreme Realm warriors had already opened their Inner Worlds — Zane had seen Brianna's. He guessed the others were probably managing theirs.

[SFX: BABY DRAGON CRY]

The Ancestral Dragon, curled up beside Zane, suddenly stirred. It let out a soft cry and fixed its large eyes on him without blinking.

Zane looked back at it. He still hadn't figured out what to do with the thing.

He reached inward toward the Cobalt Dragon. “What am I supposed to do with it?”

The Cobalt Dragon's voice drifted back. “The imprint is already set. Forcing it out would harm the dragon — that's likely why the Demi-Immortals didn't simply take it from you. Once they confirm your title, they'll probably let you raise it yourself.”

“Raise it myself?” Zane stared at the little creature. He'd never intended any of this. Retrieving the egg had been the Cobalt Dragon's idea from the start.

“Don't look at me like that,” the Cobalt Dragon said. “That imprint is stronger than the bond between Fiona and her phoenix. And the Ancestral Dragon's talent puts a True Phoenix to shame. When it grows, it will be invaluable to you. It matures quickly — you won't need to do much.”

Zane looked at the little dragon again. It had already curled back up and fallen asleep beside him.

He sighed. Then nodded.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

In the quiet of his mind, the Cobalt Dragon fell silent for a moment. The Red Dragon's presence stirred beside him. “Brother,” the Red Dragon said, “if the clan is heartless, why should we be righteous? Wouldn't it be better — just the nine of us, setting up something of our own?”

The Cobalt Dragon's great head shook slowly. “We are True Dragon Clan, in the end. It isn't wrong to think of it. But — alas.”

He said nothing more. But Zane felt the weight of it. If the Ancestral Dragon grew alongside him, and Zane one day helped rebuild the Stellar Body — perhaps there was a path forward. A new clan. The thought brought the Cobalt Dragon something close to hope, and something close to grief, in equal measure.

The Compass Wheel was incredibly fast—it had only taken a few hours to get from the Divine Kingdom Continent to the Heartlands. Still, the journey to the main continent took two full days.

[SFX: DISTANT BEAST ROAR]

[AMBIENT: OCEAN DEPTHS]

Along the way, they flew past several massive, fierce beasts. They spotted a sea beast with a twenty-thousand-foot-long body and a giant bird with a wingspan of over ten thousand feet, so enormous it looked like a floating palace. But these level-eleven beasts could sense the power radiating from the Spirit Supreme warriors on The Compass Wheel and knew better than to mess with them.

Dorothea and the other warriors couldn't have cared less about the beasts. There was hardly anything in the Lower World that could capture their attention. 

The only thing that mattered on this trip was the Tower of Sin.

[AMBIENT: OCEAN WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

After two straight days of travel, a continent finally came into view. It looked exactly as Zane remembered it from the day he'd left.

“So,” Delroy asked, his voice flat, “where on this continent is the Tower of Sin?”

Zane glanced over at him. “It's in the center.”

A cold smile touched Delroy's lips. “Good. Wade, let's head over there.”

But Zane just shook his head. “Getting to the Tower of Sin… it's not going to be that easy.”

“What are you talking about?” Delroy demanded, his brow furrowing. The others frowned, their skepticism clear. For all of Zane's seriousness, they'd have to see this supposed difficulty for themselves.

Zane’s expression was grim. “Because no one above the Spirit-Tribulation Realm can set foot in the Cursed Land. Anyone who tries… dies.”
