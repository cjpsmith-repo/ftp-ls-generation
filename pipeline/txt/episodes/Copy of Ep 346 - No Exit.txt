Ep 346 - No Exit

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
The force of Zane’s attack caught the old man completely off guard, his eyes widening with genuine astonishment.
Any master swordsman, even those with far greater power, would have found something peculiar about Zane’s technique. His movements flowed with brilliant precision, but they had a deceptive simplicity that masked their deadly intent.
It wasn’t that Zane’s moves were inherently unbeatable—far from it.
The raw power gap between them simply stretched too wide for even the best tactics to overcome. Still, there was something in the boy’s eyes—a fierce determination that made Uncle Kyle hesitate for the briefest moment.
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
[SFX: ENERGY SHIMMER] As Zane flicked his sword outward in a graceful arc, Uncle Kyle twisted his body with surprising agility for his short, stocky frame. The old man’s face revealed nothing as he summoned a short spear from thin air.
The weapon materialized with a shimmer of energy, its polished shaft gleaming in the palace light as he lunged directly toward Zane’s Streamer.
[SFX: METAL CLASH]
The spearhead glanced off the Streamer with a sharp, ringing sound. The impact didn't stop either weapon, but Uncle Kyle's spear dipped slightly. He looked shocked, clearly not expecting Zane's sword to have that much kick. His face darkened. With a sneer, he poured a stream of gray Vitality into his spear, intending to crush Zane's blade with pure power.
The force sent Zane stumbling back a few steps, but he recovered instantly, lunging at the old man again.
[SFX: RAPID SWORD STRIKES]
One strike. Two. Three. Four.
Every one of Zane’s moves was flawless—a masterclass in fundamental swordsmanship. But it was useless. Uncle Kyle batted away every attack with his Vitality-infused power.
Zane's expression was grim. His power was just too far beneath the old man’s. He was the superior swordsman—he knew he could out-duel the man's short spear technique—but skill meant nothing when his opponent could just shrug it off. Though rare and formidable, Uncle Kyle’s spear was still a low-class Sacred Weapon, more than enough to take care of an ordinary person.
But Lily was no ordinary person. And as Zane’s sword spirit, she sure as hell didn’t have an ordinary weapon.
[SFX: DARK MAGICAL ENERGY]
Her weapon, Blood Drinker, was a middle-grade Sacred weapon that caught the light with an ominous crimson sheen.
On top of that, the technique flowing through her body was a form of Malice, an ancient and forbidden art that few could master without losing themselves to its darkly powerful influence.
[SFX: SWORD BREAKING SPEAR]
The instant the Blood Drinker’s edge met the spear’s wooden shaft, a flash of red light erupted, and the low-grade Sacred weapon snapped in half.
[SFX: SWORD CUTTING FLESH]
Uncle Kyle reacted instantly, leaping back the moment his spear broke.
But Lily was faster. The Blood Drinker still bit into his chest, drawing a wellspring of blood. He scrambled backward as Lily pursued him, her movements a beautiful, deadly dance, intent on finishing him.
"Let’s go!"
Lily’s footwork was as delicate as a dance, but her sword was savage.
Even so, she was no match for the old man in a straight fight. She’d only caught him off guard, and once he regrouped, she wouldn’t be able to hold him for long.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: TENSE CHASE]
Zane grabbed Isla’s hand, and they took off running. Isla felt a powerful force pulling her along, her feet barely touching the ground.
It felt like she was floating.
[SFX: WALL EXPLODING]
After a mad sprint, a vermilion wall blocked their path. The palace walls, all painted the same deep red, were at least three feet thick.
But Zane didn’t even break stride. He slammed his shoulder straight into it, and the wall exploded outward, leaving a gaping hole.
Zane grew more desperate, a caged animal pushing his strength and speed to their absolute limits. But Isla, trailing just behind him, simply smiled.
After being trapped in Radiant Hall for so long, seeing how powerful Zane had become filled her with blissful happiness. She was almost drunk on the feeling, silently following his broad back, as if she didn’t care whether they escaped the Palace or not.
More importantly, Isla knew that it was impossible for Zane to get her out of there.
"Your Highness, let us stop him," the guests from Silversun Hall offered, ready to go after Zane.
Ray ignored them. His eyes were fixed on the fight between Lily and Uncle Kyle.
Uncle Kyle was one of Ray’s most trusted followers and a family elder.
If Kyle couldn’t take down this little girl, Ray didn’t want to think about what would happen.
As Lily’s sword dance began, Uncle Kyle panicked. With his spear broken, he struggled to block her relentless attacks.
But after a few desperate exchanges, Kyle found his footing. He fought back with raw Vitality, blasting gusts of wind from his palms. Streams of power flowed from his body, finally pushing back against Lily’s assault and forcing her to retreat.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL GARDEN]
[MUSIC: ENCHANTED MELODY]
Zane and Isla burst into a garden that felt like something out of a fairytale. Birds sang, the air was thick with the scent of flowers, and trees cast dappled shadows on the ground.
[SFX: LUTE PLAYING]
Beneath a weeping willow, a young man in a purple hat sat with elegant poise playing a haunting melody on a lute.
Zane stopped dead in his tracks. The musician himself wasn’t a threat; Zane’s heightened senses immediately gauged his power—initial Soul Realm, at best. Nothing to worry about.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
What froze the blood in Zane’s veins were the three figures standing beside the musician. They stood with military precision, clad in coarse linen archers’ uniforms, their black belts cinched tightly at their waists, hands resting on weapons with practiced ease.
Try as he might, Zane couldn’t get a read on their true strength. He could feel that the two flanking figures had weaker auras, but the one in the middle radiated power that was stronger than Master Jasper. Of every master Zane had ever faced, only Sir Kenneth might have been stronger.
Isla looked up at him with a sad smile. "See? I told you it was okay. I can’t leave the Palace. No one can get me out - not even you."
