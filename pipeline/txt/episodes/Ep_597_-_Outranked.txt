Ep 597 - Outranked
Word count: 1084
[AMBIENT: TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]
Elder Kingsley was on high alert for the Ogres.
The second Zane set foot in the Tower of Sin, he’d pissed them off. They might have let it slide if Zane hadn't been so damn impressive, but now… now they wouldn’t just break the Tower’s rules to kill him.
Sure, the Ogres held a lot of seats, but only about a quarter of the hundred or so available. Breaking the rules would cost them dearly.
But now, things were different.
They'd learned their lesson with Amber and the Demon Night's meteoric rise to power. With that memory still fresh, the Ogres couldn't just stand by and watch Zane do the same for the Humans.
On top of that, Zane's name had already shot past Amber's and was now planted firmly in the top row. Once someone like him fully realized his power, he could wipe both of the Ogres' sacred places right off the continent.
A threat that huge would terrify any race. The Ogres were strong, but they weren't immune to fear. Faced with annihilation, they wouldn't care about rules. They wouldn't give a damn about the so-called "safe zone." They would kill Zane no matter the cost, even if it turned every other race against them.
Not only were more Ogre War Lords pouring onto the tenth floor of the Tower, but the number of Demon Night War Lords was on the rise, too.
[SFX: BOOMING LAUGH]
Suddenly, a booming laugh shattered the tension. A heavy pressure settled over the crowd, and every creature staring at the wall whipped their heads around.
Another level-ten Ogre War Lord was striding toward them.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
A chill ran down the spines of Elder Kingsley, Olivia, and the other Humans. "Malcolm," one of them breathed. "He's a seat holder."
There were two ways to measure a race’s power on the main continent: the strength of its sacred places, and the power of its seat holders in the Tower of Sin.
The Ogres held the most seats, and their holders were almost always level-ten War Lords.
And the Ogre in front of them, Malcolm, was one of the most powerful.
[AMBIENT: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]
Malcolm strode forward, flanked by a retinue of War Lords, his arrogance rolling off him in waves. The other races instinctively backed away, intimidated by his sheer presence. When Boris and the other Ogre War Generals saw him, they approached and gave the traditional Ogre salute, a fist pressed to the chest.
Malcolm’s massive, round eyes fixed on the wall.
Seeing Zane's name floating high in the top row, he let out a cold, humorless laugh. "Zane Lowell. When he walks out of that chamber, I'll take his life myself."
[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]
The crowd went quiet. A seat holder knew the rules better than anyone. If he was saying this openly, it meant the Ogres had already made their decision at the highest level.
Elder Kingsley stepped forward. "Lord Malcolm, this is a safe zone. What you're describing is a violation of the Tower's rules."
Malcolm's eyes slid to him. "And who are you to speak to me?" He let out a short, contemptuous laugh. "You're not even a seat holder. You don't have the standing."
Elder Kingsley's jaw tightened.
The surrounding creatures whispered among themselves, their voices low and resigned.
[SFX: WIND GUST]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ENTRANCE]
Then a cold gust swept across the floor.
A figure in white appeared — there one moment, blurred the next, as though the air itself couldn't quite hold him. When the flickering stilled, a Human warrior in white robes stood looking at Malcolm with calm, steady eyes. A level-ten War Lord. A seat holder.
Elder Kingsley, Olivia, and the others bowed immediately. "Master Fabian."
Malcolm's eyes narrowed. "Fabian. Where's Jarrod? The two of you are usually joined at the hip."
"Aren't I here already?" said a voice from the opposite direction.
A figure in black stepped out of the shadows — so still until that moment that he'd been invisible against them. Jarrod. The second of the four Human seat holders.
The two of them were sword masters. Separately, formidable. Together, their combined technique could match any seat holder in the Tower.
Elder Kingsley felt some of the tension leave his shoulders.
Fabian's gaze moved to Malcolm. "You can't touch him." His voice was quiet, but it carried. "The rules we all agreed on are not yours to discard. If the Ogres can eliminate a genius in the safe zone today, whose prodigy is next? Think carefully before every race here asks themselves the same question."
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
Around them, the crowd stirred.
"He's right. Beat Zane in the arena if you want. But killing him in the safe zone — that's not the Ogres' call to make."
"The Tower belongs to all of us. Not just them."
"If they do this, the other races need to decide whether it's time to level the Flaming Place and the Dread Place once and for all."
The Flaming Place and the Dread Place. The two sacred pillars of the Ogre race.
Inside the Tower, most races were no match for the Ogres. But on the continent? If all fourteen major races teamed up—especially with help from the Demon Night's Amber—crushing the Ogre strongholds was a very real possibility. That threat was the only reason the Ogres had ever ceded so many of their seats in the Tower to begin with.
[MUSIC: THREATENING BRASS]
Malcolm snorted, his gaze sweeping over the other races with a cold, dismissive smile. "Rules? Rules are made by the strong. We, the Ogres, are suspending the rules of the safe zone. Any objections?"
A new voice cut through the air just as Malcolm finished speaking. "A bit unreasonable, wouldn't you say?"
Another tall figure had appeared.
Until that moment, Malcolm hadn't shown a hint of fear. He wasn't afraid of Fabian and Jarrod combined; the four human seat holders were a minor nuisance to the Ogres.
But at the sound of this new voice, a flicker of something—maybe fear—crossed his face. He demanded coldly, "What? Is the Demon Night getting involved in this?"
Whether in the Tower or on the main continent, only the Demon Night could truly go head-to-head with the Ogres. If they sided with the other races, things would get very ugly.
[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]
And that was why, for the first time, Malcolm looked afraid.
