Ep 432 - Lucky Light

Word count: 1171

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL RISING TENSION]

A warm, satisfying fullness washed over Zane, making every part of him feel intensely alive. His muscles thrummed with newfound vitality. He closed his eyes, savoring the moment, letting the energy course through his meridians like liquid sunlight.

Then—bam. 

[SFX: SUDDEN IMPACT]

Vitality knotted in his stomach, a tight, unfamiliar pressure that stole his breath. Panic jolted through him. His eyes snapped open. The energy started spinning, a wild vortex completely beyond his control. For a heartbeat, he feared something had gone catastrophically wrong.

"Whoa, this is…" Zane's fear evaporated as understanding dawned on him. His racing heart steadied. This is it. 

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

[SFX: MAGICAL BREAKTHROUGH]

He'd finally broken through to the fifth Level of the Adept Realm. A massive, uncontrollable grin spread across his face, the kind of smile that made his cheeks ache. Relief and exhilaration flooded through him in equal measure. After everything, his body had finally caught up to his ambition, if only by a single step.

Is this from those yellow spots of light? he wondered, a pleasant tingle still humming through his body. The sensation lingered like the echo of distant bells, promising more to come.

[SFX: MYSTICAL DRAGON VOICE ECHO]

"Hey, look who figured it out," the Cobalt Dragon's voice boomed in his head, pulling him back to reality. "That's the Lucky Light of my kind, kid. And let me tell you, it's a serious boost. Especially for fighters still in the little leagues." Zane could practically feel the dragon rolling its eyes.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED DRAMATIC THEME]

Zane knew what he had to do: get stronger. To him, his progress felt like it was crawling at a snail's pace, even though by Cloud Hall standards, he was advancing ridiculously fast. But "fast" wasn't enough. Not when he still had no word on his sister, Elara. Not when he was about to go up against a guy like Maddox, whose true strength was a total unknown. His breakthrough on the Cloud Road had taught him a lot, but his raw power hadn't kept up. That had to change, and it had to change now.

His mind raced. "This Lucky Light is incredible! So, what if someone who's already in the later stages of the Inner-Core Realm came in here? Would they just… break through to the next level?"

The Cobalt Dragon snorted. "Nice try. The Lucky Light only gives a leg up to fighters below the Inner-Core Realm. It does zilch for anyone at that level. The only reason there’s so much of it now is that the Dragon Soar Arena has been gathering dust for thousands of years. And since you just soaked most of it up, anyone who stumbles in here after you is just getting the leftovers."

Each time the Dragon Soar Arena was opened, it had to recharge its Lucky Light over centuries. This time, Zane had taken nearly the entire supply. Any other fighters who managed to get this far would find only scraps. By being the first to arrive in millennia, he’d hit the jackpot. And that was why the Cobalt Dragon had been so adamant that he stay put.

"I see," Zane murmured, a determined glow on his face as he imagined what it would take to gather all his attributes and rise to the very top.

The second floor environment dissolved around him. Zane moved to the stairs and climbed.
Just as he reached the third floor, he heard it — the low groan of the arena door opening, then closing.
"Someone came in," he said.
"The young master, most likely," the Cobalt Dragon replied. "Don't panic. The arena's formations scale to the climber. His beasts will be far stronger than yours — he won't catch you."
Zane paused. "But Lucky Light doesn't work on him. So why is he climbing at all?"
"You'll find out at the top," the Cobalt Dragon said, and offered nothing more.
The third floor shifted around him — the tower walls fell away, replaced by a dense forest. Massive golden pythons coiled through the trees, and the moment they spotted him, they lunged.
[SFX: HISSING — RAPID SWORD STRIKES]
Zane was already moving, sword drawn, cutting toward the first one.

[AMBIENT: TENSE OUTDOOR ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Meanwhile, outside the arena, a woman's sharp voice sliced through the tense air. "Your young master is in there, isn't he? I’ve heard the rumors for years—that House Kendrick has dragon's blood. It seems they weren't just stories."

"Yes, our young master is inside," a warrior from House Kendrick confirmed, puffing out his chest. "And only his Dragon Crystal Pendant can open the arena. That means the Dragon Soar Arena belongs to House Kendrick—"

He never finished. Lady Darkmere simply held out her hand. 

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS CRESCENDO]

With a flick of her wrist, an identical Dragon Crystal Pendant materialized in her palm. "You mean a pendant like this?" she purred, a cruel smirk playing on her lips. "Because I happen to have one, too. Looks to me like the Dragon Soar Arena belongs to Bloodwood Cliff."

[AMBIENT: TENSE WIND]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Lady Darkmere glanced at the winged dragon. The beast would be a nuisance, but nothing she couldn't handle.

"Lady Darkmere," the Kendrick warrior, a man at the middle stage of the Inner-Core Realm, started to protest. But she silenced him with a single, dismissive glance. He immediately shut his mouth. In the world of martial arts, strength is the only thing that gives you a voice.

For any sect or family, the Dragon Soar Arena was a priceless treasure. The Lucky Light it produced, even if only every few centuries, was an unparalleled tool for training new disciples. Zane might have cleaned it out this time, but it would eventually replenish, making ownership of the arena a long-term play for power.

Perched atop the Dragon Soar Arena, the winged dragon fixed its eyes on Lady Darkmere. The beast had terrorized countless cultivators since its arrival, yet now, for the first time, it sensed the presence of a genuine threat. Its scales rippled with tension, and a low rumble built in its throat—half warning, half acknowledgment of a worthy opponent.

[SFX: MASSIVE CROW FLOCK CAWING]

Lady Darkmere began calculating her next move to seize the arena, her mind already mapping out the dragon's weaknesses. But a cacophony of cawing shattered her concentration. 

A massive flock of crows blotted out the sun, transforming the sky into a living storm of black feathers that descended toward them with perfect coordination.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

Lady Darkmere's expression curdled with disgust, her jaw tightening as recognition dawned. She had encountered this particular interference too many times before. "Raven," she spat, her voice sharp with frustration and barely contained fury. "That old buzzard is always sniffing around my treasures, getting in my damn way."

Lady Darkmere's fingers twitched toward her weapon. One obstacle she could handle with ease, but two? The situation had just become a lot more complicated.
