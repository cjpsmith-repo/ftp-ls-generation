Ep 746 - Your Turn

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
The warriors of Oracle City were losing their minds.
Just moments before, a nervous hush had blanketed the arena. Everyone knew the chasm in power between Zane and Maddox was just too wide. Maddox had stared down four Life-and-Death Tribulations, and each one had forged him into a more formidable warrior. Zane, on the other hand, was only at the middle stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. Sure, it was an incredible achievement for someone his age, but against a veteran like Maddox, it felt like a death sentence.
People still talked about how Zane, back when he was only in the Soul Realm, could take on Core-Forged warriors and even prodigies hailed as saints. But the leap from the Soul Realm to the Inner-Core Realm was a single step compared to the vast, empty gulf between the Spirit-Tribulation and Life-and-Death Realms.
That’s why Tia and the old tycoon had both practically begged Zane not to accept the challenge. It was common sense: the only one who could truly challenge a Life-and-Death warrior was another Life-and-Death warrior. Fighters at that level were the undisputed masters of The Heartlands. Anyone from a lower realm, no matter how gifted, just couldn’t compete.
Of course, both Tia and the tycoon were watching from the sidelines, coiled and ready to intervene if things got too ugly. Zane’s safety was their priority. It was an unspoken rule of these order battles. You could send your champion into a duel, but you’d never let them be killed. It was a tradition that had held for a thousand years, and Simon would do the same for Maddox if the tables were turned.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT DRUMS]
But then Zane had landed a clean hit on his very first attack, and a wave of pure elation had washed over the World Merchants’ Guild. That single strike proved his strength was still growing at a terrifying rate, confirming he had every right to challenge a man so far beyond his station.
[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD CHEERING]
"He's unbelievable!" one warrior shouted over the din. "Not only did he break into the middle stage in just two years, but he's actually going toe-to-toe with a Life-and-Death master!"
"Yeah, but Maddox is no joke," his friend argued back. "It's amazing that Zane can stand up to him at all, but this has to be his limit. There's no way he can actually beat him."
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
On the wall, Tia's expression had gone complicated. She wouldn't say it out loud, but she knew — Zane was stronger than her now. That didn't make watching any easier.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]
The crowd buzzed, their debates echoing through the arena.
Across the field, Maddox glanced down at the wound in his side. He flexed — a single controlled contraction of the muscles across his back — and the wound sealed itself shut. He looked up. "Not bad," he said, his voice carrying. "You can actually injure me. That's worthy of praise." His eyes went flat. "But it's far from enough."
Zane smiled. "Aren't you forgetting something? Aren't you afraid of the poison?"
Maddox's expression changed. An uninjured Life-and-Death warrior had nothing to fear from ordinary poison. But this was Zane Lowell — a man who could get his hands on a top-grade Relic. Who knew what he might have smeared on that blade?
The hesitation lasted only a second. Zane laughed. "Just kidding." His voice turned contemptuous. "The so-called number one warrior in the Heartlands — turns out he's just a cowardly rat."
He was already moving as the last word left his mouth, sword swinging toward Maddox. Having been burned by the Formless Sword once,
Maddox wasn't about to let it find him a second time. He broke off and counterattacked, momentum surging as he drew back his fist. "Supreme Emperor Fist!"
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
The colossal golden fist crashed down, driving Zane into the earth with the force of a collapsing mountain.
Zane suddenly reappeared at the edge of the pit, looking almost bored. He shrugged, casually brushing dirt from his clothes. A trickle of blood crept from the corner of his mouth, but he wiped it away with the back of his hand as if it were nothing more than a nuisance. He shot Maddox a cold, condescending smile. "That was just okay."
[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]
Maddox seethed, his face twisting in fury at Zane's smirk. "You're all talk!" he roared. "Try this on for size!"
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT WHOOSH]
He slammed his fists down a second time. "Supreme Emperor Fist!" The colossal golden fist materialized in the air once more — as tall as a city wall, blazing like a small sun, its light so intense that most warriors had to shield their eyes. It came hurtling toward the ground to crush Zane into dust.
The impact was catastrophic. A pit several miles wide and thirty meters deep opened up at the city gate, the ground convulsing with the force of it.
On the walls, Tia watched with a faint crease of worry between her brows. Zane's body was extraordinary — she knew that better than anyone. But this was Maddox's full strength.
At the west corner of the city, Cora stood very still. She had been keeping her expression composed all morning. She couldn't anymore.
[SFX: CROWD CHATTER]
"Maddox's punch could flatten a mountain," someone near her muttered.
"Zane can't withstand that. Nobody can."
"He injured Maddox — that was already beyond anything we had a right to expect. This is his limit."
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
But Zane was faster this time. Smarter. He didn't wait for the impact. With a wave of his hand, he unleashed his own power, and the swirling starlight within him surged toward his fingertips.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
"Like I said," Zane said, his voice laced with contempt, "that's all you've got. You just don't know when to quit." He let out a cold laugh and drove his own fist toward the sky.
[SFX: POWER CHARGING UP]
"Stars Sink and Earth Quake!"
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: SHATTERING GLASS]
A cataclysmic explosion of sound ripped through the air as Zane's body transformed into a blinding ray of light. He didn't go around the golden fist — he punched straight through it, shattering the massive construct layer by layer.
Maddox was frozen, his mind unable to process what he was seeing. Before he could even think to react, Zane was right in front of him, his form solidifying from the blinding light.
"Now it's your turn to go down," Zane murmured, his own fist already crashing down.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Unlike Zane, Maddox didn't have an indestructible body. A direct hit like this wouldn't just wound him. It would obliterate him.
