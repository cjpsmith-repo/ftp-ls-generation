Ep 306 - A Hell of a Fight
Word count: 1046
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
On the Skyreach Sect list, only one name remained between Zane and the top: Patrick.
Despite Evan’s first-place ranking in the last competition, he had sequestered himself for intensive training. Such isolation at Evan’s caliber typically stretched for at least three months, possibly extending to a full year. The odds of him showing up for the All Peaks Competition were slim to none.
This circumstance left Patrick as the final obstacle in Zane’s path.
Victory over Patrick would elevate Zane to number one among all disciples within the Skyreach Sect—a thought that sent a thrill of anticipation through his veins.
The upcoming matchup between Finn and Patrick had generated tremendous excitement throughout the Sect. Whispers and speculations filled every corner of Misty Peak as disciples debated the potential outcome with fervent intensity.
Zane had beaten Finn before, but this didn’t make Finn a weak fighter.
The truth lay elsewhere—gaining any advantage against Zane had proven nearly impossible, earning him the nickname "the monster" among his peers.
The other disciples struggled to understand how someone at the Second Level of the Adept Realm could wield such overwhelming power. The only thing that made sense was to label him a monster—something with capabilities that ordinary cultivators could barely understand.
Both Finn and Patrick had shown extraordinary skill from the outset of the tournament, their techniques dazzling spectators and competitors alike. The arena buzzed with anticipation as everyone awaited what promised to be a spectacular confrontation between two prodigies of the sword. The tension in the air was almost palpable, hanging heavy like storm clouds before unleashing their fury.
It was going to be a hell of a fight.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
When the referee called their names, Patrick and Finn walked to the battle ring.
Patrick, quiet as ever, spoke softly, "Finn, I’ve been looking forward to this."
"Me too," Finn agreed with a nod.
They’d been sizing each other up for a while now. Nothing else needed to be said.
After mastering the Apex of Sword Intent, Finn had spent time training in the Sword Tempering Pool, and his skills had sharpened considerably.
But his fight with Zane had been a humbling experience. It made him realize that no matter how powerful you get, there’s always someone stronger out there.
Patrick, on the other hand, was always so unassuming; he hadn’t really shown his true strength yet. He was a dormant volcano, quiet on the surface, and no one knew what kind of power he’d unleash when he finally blew.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Finn made the first move. Using his Sword Step technique, he closed the distance to Patrick in a flash.
At first, it looked like a battle of pure skill. Finn clearly had the edge, armed with both his sword and his Sword Step technique.
But the second Finn got close enough to strike, he realized how badly he’d misjudged Patrick.
Patrick was still unarmed, and he wasn’t even using The Eternal Cycle for this match. Even so, he was more than enough to handle him.
[SFX: ENERGY FLARE]
Golden light flared around his hands as he simply slapped Finn’s sword aside with his bare palms.
[SFX: SWORD CLASH]
Finn lunged with his sword a dozen times in quick succession, each thrust precise and deadly. Patrick’s movements, however, displayed flawless speed and timing as he parried every single strike. His large, powerful hands effortlessly deflected the gleaming blade with seemingly casual grace.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
The crowd gasped and murmured in collective shock, their eyes fixed on the golden energy circle protecting Patrick’s palms. "If that golden circle breaks, Patrick’s a goner!" someone shouted, voicing the tension that gripped every spectator.
As the minutes crawled by, visible strain appeared on Patrick’s normally composed face. The golden circle protecting his hands began to deteriorate, fine cracks spiderwebbing across its luminous surface like fractured glass. It trembled violently now, pulsing with unstable energy that threatened to collapse at any moment.
Finn, sensing weakness in his opponent’s defense, felt a surge of triumph course through his veins. His eyes narrowed with predatory focus as he gathered his strength for one decisive strike. Drawing a deep breath, he channeled all his thunder-infused sword intent into his blade and roared with savage intensity, "Break!"
[SFX: TEARING SOUND]
An instant later, a loud tearing sound ripped through the air.
Finn’s Extreme Thunder Sword Intent finally ripped the golden circle to shreds.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
But the fight was far from over. Patrick’s brow furrowed as a brilliant golden light washed over him. He stretched out his arms, and a massive shadow swelled up behind him.
The crowd gasped as a divine figure materialized from the shadow. But unlike the serene, smiling divine figures you usually see, this one was radiating pure fury.
Guardians like this usually have two expressions. They can appear peaceful and serene in meditation. But when they mean to punish the wicked or warn people to repent, their faces twist into an aggressive, wrathful glare.
"It’s The Immovable Guardian!"
"How can Patrick summon The Immovable Guardian?"
"Is he a descendant of The Immovable Guardian?"
One disciple blurted out the questions, and everyone around him just stared at him like he was an idiot.
A divine being like this was completely different from a Legendary Beast. Besides, monks in temples aren’t allowed to have relationships, so it’s impossible for them to have kids.
So how could a divine being have descendants?
Realizing his screw-up, the disciple's face flushed bright red. "Maybe Patrick is the reincarnation of The Immovable Guardian!" he stammered.
"Yeah, that must be it! How else could he have learned so many of those ancient spiritual techniques?" another disciple shouted in agreement.
"No wonder he’s so powerful! If he’s the reincarnation of The Immovable Guardian, Finn doesn’t stand a chance against that shadow!"
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TEMPLE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS CHANTING]
Patrick himself looked calm, but the shadow of The Immovable Guardian looming behind him was terrifying, its face a mask of pure rage.
"The Immovable Guardian’s Palm!"
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY BLAST]
As Patrick cried out the name, he clasped his hands and aimed a single finger at the Extreme Thunder Sword Intent.
Suddenly, a massive palm materialized out of thin air and swept toward it.
