Ep 672 - The Celestial Fragment

Word count: 1171

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TEMPLE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

The Demon Night were unique. Unlike every other race, they had soul statues that let them communicate directly with the upper world, which meant their people in the Lower World were always in lockstep with their counterparts above. Other races weren't so lucky. Sure, some had secret methods for reaching out, but the connection was never as strong.

Take the Violet Heart Sanctuary—they knew next to nothing about what their people were doing in the upper world, completely clueless about the power humans actually wielded up there.

Lavender, Zane's sword-spirit and one of the three Demon Night queens, was the one they called for violence and war. That, right there, was the reason for Yolande’s hesitation. Her fears weren't exactly unfounded; humans and the Demon Night were rivals more often than not. The secret of the Dark Nether Cave was a human affair, and it was too important to risk.

Hearing Yolande, Lavender let out a laugh like ringing bells. "You don't have to worry about me," she said. "I've already lost everything. Zane is my only hope now." She had gambled the last shred of her soul on him, betting that he was her one shot at a comeback. Until that day came, she was his sword-spirit, and that was that.

Zane leveled a hard stare at Yolande. "Tell me."

But Yolande just shook her head. "It was a naive thought. Besides, the Celestial Fragment will go to Zane in the end, anyway."

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Lavender’s head snapped toward Yolande, her eyes wide with disbelief. "Did you just say Celestial Fragment?"

The Demon Night queen had thought she was past being surprised by anything this world had to offer, but Yolande had just dropped a bomb right in the middle of the room.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

The others—Fabian, Jarrod, Malcolm, Timothy, Esther, and the two high-ranking Demon Night War Lords—were more baffled by Lavender’s sudden outburst than by Yolande’s words.

Yolande met Lavender’s shock with a challenging look, raising a single, elegant eyebrow. "The Celestial Fragment is a human matter," she stated plainly. "It has nothing to do with the Demon Night."

Lavender's expression slowly settled. She glanced at Zane, and something like wonder crossed her face. "This is fate," she said quietly. "You traveled thousands of miles to reach the main continent. And now here you are — about to inherit a Celestial Fragment." She shook her head. "Who could have seen that coming?"

Zane wasn't interested in fate. He looked at Yolande. "If it's that important, why didn't you just take it yourself? You didn't need me for that."

Yolande was quiet for a moment. "I had no Crystal Butterfly. Without it, the Celestial Root stays at the bottom of the abyss — there's no other way to draw it up, and without the Celestial Root, the scorpion's mouth stays shut. And even if I'd solved all of that — I'm a clone. My soul isn't whole. The Celestial Fragment requires a complete soul to carry it. I can't refine it."

[MUSIC: QUIET WEIGHT]

The cell was silent. Zane let that settle.

"So where is it?" he asked.

"I searched Huey's mind before he died," Yolande said. "That's how I learned the natives' language. And that's how I know — the fragment was sealed long ago and handed to their prophet for safekeeping. It became their sacred object." She glanced toward the bars. "That's what I told them when we arrived. That I knew their prophet. That's why they brought us here instead of killing us."

"So we're here to steal their god," Jarrod said flatly.

"Yes," Yolande replied.

"And then how do we leave?" Esther asked.

"Once we have the sacred object, we can go."

The group found corners of the cell and settled in. There was nothing to do but rest. With their Vitality completely suppressed, the coming fight would be purely physical — and a physical fight against unknown numbers in an unknown space was going to drain them. Better to conserve every bit of strength they had.

Time moved strangely in the self-contained world. No sun, no moon, no stars — just a flat, featureless sky that gave nothing away. They had no idea how long they waited.

[SFX: SHUFFLING FOOTSTEPS]

Then movement outside the cell. A procession of green-skinned creatures approached, parting around a figure at their center. The leader was older — hunched, slow-moving, wearing a withered circlet on his head with three fist-sized skulls hanging from it. He leaned on a crooked walking stick.

"Is that the prophet?" Zane asked.

Yolande stood, studied the figure, and shook her head. "I don't know." She walked to the bars and began to speak.

[SFX: GUTTURAL CHANTING]

The exchange went on for some time — the elder muttering in his rasping language, Yolande answering in kind, her face giving nothing away. Then the elder slammed his staff into the ground, turned, and walked off. His guards leveled their spears, grunted a few threats at the group, and followed him out.

"Well?" Zane asked.

Yolande blinked once. "Communication failed. They plan to execute us tomorrow."

Everyone was on their feet.

[AMBIENT: EERIE DIMENSION]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

This whole pocket dimension they were trapped in was strange. It was built from the Vitality the Mad Scorpion had siphoned from the world, the same energy that fueled the green-skinned creatures who called it home. The rules here were bizarre and unique, but the world was as much a cage for its inhabitants as it was for Zane and his companions.

[SFX: METAL CLINKING]

Zane walked to the bars of their cell and pointed at the green-skinned guard who'd taken his Storage Ring. The creature was fumbling with it, trying to figure out how it worked. When it saw Zane pointing and gesturing for it to come closer, the guard mumbled something and shuffled toward the cell. The second it was within arm's reach, Zane’s hand shot through the bars like a viper, snatching the ring back.

Before the guard could even process what happened, Zane drove his foot into the iron bars of the prison. He wasn't using any special energy, just his own raw, incredible strength.

[SFX: METAL CRASH]

[SFX: CRUSHING IMPACT]

With a deafening crash, the five-foot-wide section of bars buckled and collapsed, crushing the green-skinned guard flat beneath its weight.

[SFX: BONE CRUNCH]

Zane stepped out of the ruined cell, walked over to the guard, and brought his heel down on its head, finishing the job with a sickening crunch.

Zane tapped the reclaimed Storage Ring and pulled out a long spear, tossing it over his shoulder to Lavender without looking. She caught it effortlessly. For himself, he summoned his Shattered Dagger, hooking the ring on its hilt around his thumb.

In this strange world where his Vitality was useless, a purely physical weapon like the Shattered Dagger was invaluable.

[SFX: BLADE WHOOSH]

With a simple flick of his wrist, the blade flew forward, slicing through the air.
