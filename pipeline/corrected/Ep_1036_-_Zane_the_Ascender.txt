Ep 1036 - Zane the Ascender

Word count: 1030

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE THEME]

Inside the Illusionary Arena, the two fighters, Zane and Fenton, circled each other cautiously.

[SFX: METAL CLASH]

[SFX: SPARKS FLYING]

A massive golden wind blade and a smaller teal one crashed into each other, showering the arena in sparks.

The two attacks were night and day. Fenton’s was infused with the power of metal, making it wickedly sharp—as if it could cleave the world in two. Zane’s, by contrast, looked almost ordinary, with just a few faint glimmers of light dancing on its surface.

To any onlooker, it was a lion pouncing on a rabbit. The winner seemed like a sure thing.

[SFX: EAR-SPLITTING SHRIEK]

[SFX: INTENSE SIZZLE]

The moment the two blades collided, an ear-splitting shriek tore through the Illusionary Arena, followed by an intense sizzle.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE ENERGY BURST]

That’s when the hidden power within Zane’s teal blade exploded outward.

[SFX: BEAST ROAR]

[SFX: RIPPING METAL]

The teal blade morphed into a vicious beast, sinking its fangs into the golden blade and ripping it apart.

In a flash, Fenton’s attack was completely overwhelmed, swallowed whole by Zane’s.

But the teal blade wasn’t finished. Without losing a bit of momentum, it shot straight for Fenton.

[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]

Fenton’s instincts kicked in. His figure flashed, and he threw himself backward, retreating fast. Even as he moved, his hand kept working — he slashed five, six times in quick succession, each strike precise and controlled, gradually breaking apart the incoming blade until the last of it dissipated.

He wasn’t hurt. Not physically.

[AMBIENT: SILENT HALL]

[SFX: CROWD ERUPTION]

Back in The Crucible, the watching warriors from the Myriad Spirit House went dead silent for a beat—before the entire hall erupted.

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMUR]

“What the…?” someone stammered, too stunned to finish.

“How is that possible?” another cried out. “Fenton has mastered the fourth level of Wind Law! Zane’s only at the third—he shouldn’t stand a chance!”

“Exactly! And Fenton’s also mastered the fourth level of Metal Law. That fusion should make him way stronger.”

Amid the uproar, jaws were on the floor. Some warriors were literally rubbing their eyes, certain they were seeing things.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]

Just then, the Saint Lord of the Soulbound Sect shot to his feet, his eyes locked on the arena. Disappointment and frustration twisted his ancient features.

The other elders of the Soulbound Sect sat in stunned, defeated silence.

“What is going on? How could this be happening?” the Saint Lord demanded, his gaze fixed on the Illusionary Arena.

[SFX: WOOD SPLINTERING]

He slammed a palm down on the armrest of his purple and gold chair. It turned to dust. He had always carried himself with quiet refinement — to see him lose composure like this told the other elders everything.

“Damn it,” he said. “This is a loss.”

“Zane is definitely only at the third level of Wind Law,” an elder murmured, “but the power he just displayed… it’s far beyond that.”

The Saint Lord and the elders had mastered the fifth, even sixth levels of Law Power. They knew exactly what each level of the Wind Law was capable of. Even with some other technique to boost his strength, Zane shouldn’t have been able to produce that kind of force.

An elder frowned and added, “He didn’t even use a martial skill. Even if he had, there’s no technique that should let a third-level Wind Law overwhelm Fenton’s fusion technique. It’s simply impossible.”

The word hung in the air. Impossible — and yet they had all just watched it happen.

[MUSIC: LOW STRINGS]

The Saint Lord settled back into what remained of his chair, his jaw tight. He had known, of course, that the Thirteen Palaces had selected Zane — had watched the ascender himself when word first came down, and hadn’t seen much. Born with unusual strength, nothing more. Since the Thirteen Palaces wanted him, he’d let it go as a goodwill gesture.

That calculation had just collapsed.

The ascender was worth something. Real something. And now, having already handed him over for free, there was no graceful way to go back to the Alliance and ask for compensation. The window for that had closed the moment the Soulbound Sect waved Zane through.

He said nothing. The other elders said nothing.

Finally, one of them spoke quietly. “We could use this as an opportunity. Send Fenton to the Thirteen Palaces alongside Zane. The Alliance would take that as a favor.”

The Saint Lord knew it was the right move. He had already thought of it himself. But it was a cup of water on a burning cart — it didn’t come close to making him whole. He exhaled slowly and said nothing.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

The attack hadn’t physically hurt Fenton, but the blow to his pride was staggering. Every great fighter has an earned ego, and Fenton’s had just been knocked down hard.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

Fenton was a smart guy, open-minded even, but he was also immensely proud. He was usually gracious, but that was because no one had ever truly challenged his superiority. His confidence was so absolute that he never felt the need to show off.

But now, Zane’s attack had shattered that quiet certainty. Confusion filled his eyes as a single question replayed in his mind: How did I lose? In one exchange, everything he thought he knew—everything built on years of relentless training—had been completely upended.

It just didn’t make sense.

[SFX: FLESH TEARING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

Then, in a sudden, sharp movement, Fenton bit down hard on his own forearm. Blood welled from the deep teeth marks, and the jolt of pain snapped him back to the present. Every last trace of arrogance vanished from his face, replaced by a deadly focus. He was a true warrior, and he wasn’t about to fold.

“You’re the real deal,” Fenton said, his voice low and steady. “The Thirteen Palaces were right about you. I was wrong.”

He met Zane’s eyes. “In our entire generation at the Soulbound Sect, you’re the first person to make me fight with everything I have.”

[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

With that, he reached into his Spatial Sanctum and drew a sword: Regis-Bane.

Regis-Bane was a Low Grade-One Divine Weapon.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL WHISPERS]

The moment his hand closed around the hilt, an eerie red light erupted from the blade. As the glow washed over Zane, he was hit with an overwhelming aura—it felt like the combined life force of a billion different creatures, a sensation that was both deeply unsettling and completely awe-inspiring.
