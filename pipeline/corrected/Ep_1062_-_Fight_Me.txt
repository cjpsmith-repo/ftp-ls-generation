Ep 1062 - Fight Me

Word count: 1149

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[SFX: ENERGY WAVES]

Just as they’d thought, waves of Vitality exploded outside his manor.

[SFX: RATTLING WINDOWS AND DOORS]

The hostile energy was so intense that the doors and windows of Zane’s home rattled nonstop, as if a violent storm were brewing just outside.

[SFX: PAINED SCREAM]

[SFX: WHOOSHING]

[SFX: DOOR CRASH]

A few seconds later, a pained scream sliced through the air. Zane frowned at the whooshing sound that followed. An instant later, his manor door splintered open with a deafening crash.

It was Garrett’s body, sent flying with enough force to crumble the door to pieces.

[SFX: BODY CRASHING]

[SFX: WALL BREAKING]

Garrett shot through the doorway without slowing down, crashing into the ground and carving a deep gash across the floor before plowing straight through the far wall. He blew a massive hole through it, flying out the other side of Zane’s manor.

Zane stood there, completely bewildered, a helpless smile touching his lips. This was the second time something like this had happened.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]

The warriors gathered outside the manor let out a collective sigh.

“Damn, even Garrett got taken out that easily!”

“Is this woman from the Life-and-Death Realm really that unbeatable? Is she going to take down every single warrior in Cloudswallow Hall by herself?”

“I really hope that’s just talk. Somebody’s gotta beat her!”

As they muttered amongst themselves, their eyes kept darting toward Zane.

[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORIENTAL]

Both Layla and Zane were new to The Mist Palace, and they’d even ended up as next-door neighbors.

They were both warriors from the Life-and-Death Realm, though Layla’s power was a bit higher. She was at the ninth level, while Zane had only just broken into the fifth.

Watching Layla take down one Cloudswallow Hall warrior after another, people couldn’t help but compare her to Zane.

They were both incredibly powerful, and everyone was dying to know who’d win if they ever actually fought.

Too bad Zane seemed completely wrapped up in his training and had zero interest in challenging Layla. Sure, he’d caused some chaos when he first arrived by taking Thundergust, but that whole mess had blown over pretty quickly. Everyone knew Zane wasn’t the type to go looking for a fight.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON WOOD]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Zane followed the gash carved into his floor toward the splintered doorway. Just as he reached it, Layla, dressed in green, approached from the other side.

Before Zane could get a word out, Layla said, “I’ll pay for the damages.”

[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL DRUMS]

Zane’s frown deepened. “This isn’t about money. This is the second time. I’m starting to think you’re doing this on purpose.”

Layla just gave a slight, knowing smile, not admitting to a thing. “It wasn’t intentional. Things get heated during a challenge.”

Zane’s jaw tightened. “The Mist Palace has a dedicated challenge space where no one gets hurt, and warriors can fight at full strength. So why do you keep accepting challenges right outside my door? You’re disrupting my cultivation.”

Layla studied him for a moment, then let out a small sigh. “Simple. Fight me once, and I’ll stop.”

“Not interested.” He already knew what she was after. He just genuinely had no time for it.

Layla smiled pleasantly. “Then I’m afraid the accidents will keep happening. Things do get heated in a challenge.”

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS RETREATING]

Zane watched her go, rubbing his temple. How had he ended up with this problem?

A short while later, craftsmen arrived to repair the damage. Layla had been efficient about that much, at least.

[AMBIENT: DISTANT BATTLE SOUNDS]

But the peace didn’t last. Layla’s challenges continued — and the rank of her opponents kept climbing.

Sixtieth in Cloudswallow Hall. Thirty-second. Seventeenth. Ninth.

[SFX: GROUND SHAKING]

[SFX: WALLS CRACKING]

Then came the warrior ranked fourth. That fight lasted nearly two hours. Warriors at this level could split mountains with a raised hand, and for once, Layla didn’t end it in a single move. The destruction spread far beyond Zane’s palace — a dozen surrounding residences were leveled to the ground. When the dust finally settled, the only structure left standing in the entire area was Layla’s. She had apparently reinforced it with some kind of protective rune technique.

Zane sat on the last intact section of floorboard, surveying what used to be his home.

He regretted not just accepting her challenge from the start.

The fourth-ranked warrior of Cloudswallow Hall had been defeated. Layla dusted off her hands, then floated over to where Zane sat, light as a bird landing on a branch. She looked down at him with an expression of perfect innocence.

“I’m sorry,” she said. “I’ll compensate.”

Zane just looked at her. He had nothing to say.

True to her word, she had the entire area rebuilt within a few hours.

[AMBIENT: QUIET PALACE GROUNDS]

Word had spread quickly through the Mist Palace. Layla had defeated the fourth-ranked warrior of Cloudswallow Hall — she was now considered a serious contender for Passing the Torch. As for the top three, they were already deep in seclusion, preparing. They had no time for challenges, and so the cycle finally came to an end.

The disturbances outside Zane’s door stopped. Quiet returned.

He was focused on establishing communication channels with the stars, hoping to trigger a few more. Activating them was brutally difficult, but he was determined to make as many attempts as it took.

Back in the Lower World, Zane used to spend nearly an entire night letting his senses leave his body; a whole day could pass in what felt like the blink of an eye.

Now that he was in the Upper World, so much closer to the stars, his training took less time. So, every night, Zane would patiently try to connect with any star that caught his attention.

Thanks to his persistence, the number of stars he’d managed to activate had grown to six.

[SFX: DISTANT GASPS]

[AMBIENT: HUSHED MURMURING]

One night during that month, six stars blazed to life simultaneously in the sky above the Mist Palace. The light lasted only moments, but warriors across the grounds saw it. Speculation spread for days — a cultivation method tied to the stars was among the most powerful anyone could practice, but no one could determine whose breakthrough had triggered the phenomenon.

Zane remained silent. The enemies ahead - the Queen of Punishment from the Demon Night, and whoever held Elara captive - demanded relentless training. He couldn’t afford to rest.

[AMBIENT: GRAND PALACE HALL]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

A month later, a powerful voice echoed across the Mist Palace.

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

Yolande’s figure appeared in the sky, hovering alone like a pure white lotus. Her voice rang out, clear and strong, “Passing the Torch begins!”

After her announcement, several other figures floated out to join her—Timothy and the other vice leaders of The Mist Palace.

“All warriors of The Mist Palace who can pass through the Red Cloud Ring, follow me!” Yolande then pointed a finger toward the sky.

[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY SURGE]

A small ring appeared in her hand and immediately shot upward before expanding, spreading down over them.

The crimson ring grew to more than a hundred feet wide, hovering high above the heads of the assembled warriors.

Zane looked up at the ring — and wondered what was waiting on the other side.
