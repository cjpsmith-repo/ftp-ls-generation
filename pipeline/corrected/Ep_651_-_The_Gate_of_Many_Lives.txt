Ep 651 - The Gate of Many Lives

Word count: 1175

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL TENSION]

The group watched as Yolande held out her hands, her long, pale fingers weaving through the air. With each fluid motion, she channeled her Vitality, spinning it into one glowing, intricate pattern after another.

“Divine textures,” Zane breathed, his eyebrows shooting up. He recognized them instantly. They looked almost exactly like the ones the Cobalt Dragon had taught him.

Divine textures aren’t unique to the Upper World, the Cobalt Dragon’s voice echoed in his mind. Humans can master them, too.

“What is she doing?” Zane asked him.

The dragon’s attention was also fixed on the symbols taking shape in Yolande’s hands. If I’m not mistaken, she’s trying to seal that thing.

“Seal it?” Zane’s eyes flickered with doubt. She was weaving those complex symbols using nothing but her own energy and her bare hands. Could that possibly be enough?

The origin of divine textures is more complex than you can imagine, the dragon replied coolly. If you ever make it to the Upper World, you’ll learn more about them.

As Yolande worked, the number of symbols grew, their glowing threads becoming more and more elaborate. Ten, twenty, then fifty. When she had a hundred of them hovering in the air before her, she swept her hand back.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER CASCADE]

They rained down in a shimmering cascade behind the group.

“Freeze!” Yolande commanded, her voice ringing with authority.

In an instant, the mournful wails behind them cut off. The silence was deafening.

“Did she do it?” Zane whispered, a spark of hope in his eyes.

[MUSIC: TENSE RELIEF]

Esther let out a deep, shaky breath, the tension finally leaving her shoulders. It looked like Yolande had actually pulled it off.

“I’ve only sealed it temporarily,” Yolande urged, her voice tight. “It won’t last long. We have to go. Now.”

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

Her words shattered the brief moment of relief, and a fresh jolt of fear sent them scrambling. They broke into a dead sprint, not stopping until they had covered several dozen miles. Even then, no one dared to look back.

“Ugh,” Fabian groaned, the sound long and ragged. The worst of the pain from the Psyche Spike was finally easing, but his head was still throbbing. He knew, though, what had just happened. “Zane,” he said, his voice thick with exhaustion, “you saved my life back there. Thank you.”

“Can we look back now?” Esther asked, her voice trembling slightly. A gnawing fear still had its claws in all of them.

Yolande nodded. “I think we’ve lost it.”

Only then did they risk turning around.

Zane's eyes flared with pale green light as he activated his spirit sight, scanning the darkness far behind them. At the very edge of his vision, he could just make out a faint white point of light, pulsing weakly. Yolande's divine textures drifted around it like a cage, and the light strained against them — struggling, but held.

[SFX: FAINT DISTANT PULSE]

"Don't look at it," Yolande said sharply, her hand snapping out to block his line of sight. "It can feel your gaze."

Zane blinked and killed the light in his eyes immediately. The white point vanished back into the dark.

"It can feel that?" he said, genuinely shaken.

Yolande let out a slow breath. "Yes. You were lucky just now. Fortunately, it hasn't fully matured yet."

Zane stared at her. "Matured? What is it?"

Yolande shook her head. "I can't say it. I can't even think it. If I name it, it will know — and it will come."

Everyone fell silent, staring at her.

"I know what it is," Lavender said quietly.

"Then what—" Zane started.

"She already told you," Lavender cut him off, her voice flat. "You cannot say it. You cannot think it." She held his gaze until he closed his mouth.

[MUSIC: UNEASY QUIET]

That settled it. No one pressed further. Whatever the thing was, none of them had any desire to experience it a second time.

It was Esther who broke the silence. She had been scanning their surroundings with quiet intensity, and now her eyes went wide. "We're here. We actually made it."

She pulled a folded map from her coat — a detailed chart of the Dark Nether Cave's inner passages — and spread it open, pointing to a small mark near their position. "I made this mark three years ago. There's a relic site here. The remains of a powerful fighter." Her voice dropped. "We tried to reach it last time, but there were more than ten Venomous Ghost Martens guarding it. We lost people trying to draw them away. In the end, we had to pull back."

Fabian and Jarrod exchanged a glance, the weariness in their faces giving way to something sharper.

Zane checked the Crystal Butterfly in his palm. The pale flame it cast had dimmed — they'd drifted off course from the inheritance. But he didn't mind. If Esther was certain there was something worth finding here, it wasn't a detour he'd regret.

"Let's go in," he said.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS THROUGH UNDERGROWTH]

Esther led them through a stretch of dark forest, the trees pressing close on either side, their roots gnarled and pale in the cave's dim light. When they emerged on the other side, everyone stopped.

There, standing alone in the open, was a gate.

It was completely plain, with no carvings or markings of any kind. No walls, no roof, no house — just a small, single gate, the kind you might find leading to the yard of any normal house. Standing here in the middle of nowhere, it looked deeply wrong.

“This is the relic?” Fabian asked, his voice laced with disbelief.

[AMBIENT: EERIE CAVE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

“Yes,” Esther said, her senses still on high alert. “There should be hidden words on it.” Keeping her distance, she shot a stream of Vitality toward the gate.

The last time she was here with the other members of Demon Night, they had just revealed the hidden inscription when a pack of Venomous Ghost Martens ambushed them, forcing them to retreat.

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]

As Esther’s Vitality washed over the gate, a line of large words slowly materialized on its surface.

[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]

I am Adolph. I spent my life chasing ghosts, and my dreams died here.

Yolande stared at the gate, a flicker of grief crossing her face as she seemed to get lost in thought.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

Lavender’s brow furrowed. “Adolph is dead?” she said, her voice filled with disbelief. “I can’t believe he died here, too.” As she spoke, she shot a quick, mysterious look at Yolande.

“My Queen, who is Adolph?” Esther asked quietly.

A faint, ironic smile touched Lavender’s lips. “He was a man whose name could have shaken the world. I just never expected him to die in a place like this.”

Hearing his name, Yolande bit her lip but said nothing.

“So this gate must be a treasure he left behind, right?” Esther pressed.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]

“Exactly,” Lavender said in her usual deadpan tone. “This is his life’s treasure: the Gate of Many Lives.”

In truth, she was just as seasoned as Yolande, but the secrets of the Dark Nether Cave were born from human history, and she would never know them as intimately as Yolande did.
