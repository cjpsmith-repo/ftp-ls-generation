Ep 770 - Uninvited Guests

Word count: 1094

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The Sea Wyrm Sect's gates had barely registered the arrival of the voidstorm. The disciple who had given the warning was still shaking when Elder Marsh rounded on him and demanded answers.

"But—but," the messenger stammered, his words catching in his throat.

"You can't even get a sentence out! What good are you?" Shaw snapped. This was one of his most promising men, usually calm and collected, but right now, he was just babbling like an idiot. A knot of unease tightened in Shaw's stomach. Something was very wrong. "Spit it out. What happened?"

"Outside," the man finally managed, his voice trembling. "There are so many people outside! An army of warriors."

"Warriors? How strong?" Shaw pressed.

"I… I don't know, sir. You have to see for yourself."

Shaw snorted, but his confusion was turning to dread. What kind of force could reduce one of his best men to this state? He strode out of his home and looked up.

[SFX: WHOOSH OF WIND]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

 The sight stole the air from his lungs. Floating silently against the sky were at least a hundred figures, their combined presence a crushing weight.

"What is this power?" he muttered, his eyes locked on the terrifying army. He tried to draw a breath, but the cold air caught in his throat, sending him into a violent coughing fit.

Shaw was a warrior at the mid-stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, a powerful man by any local standard. But faced with the scene above, he couldn't stop hacking, as if his own body had turned against him.

When the coughing finally subsided, his eyes were wide with a dawning, frantic horror. "Life-and-Death Realm masters… They're all Life-and-Death Realm masters! A hundred of them! No—wait." His gaze fixed on the figures at the front, whose auras dwarfed the others. "The ones in the lead… they're far stronger. Are they… legendary Soul Sea Realm masters? Oh my God."

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

 After a moment of pure shock, his mind began to race, desperately trying to make sense of the impossible. These warriors couldn't be from The Heartlands. There was only one other possibility: they had to be from the union of seven divine kingdoms, from across the Chaotic Ocean of Stars.

The news of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom's clash with the World Merchants' Guild and the Cloud Hall in Oracle City had already spread like wildfire. Rumors had been flying that the York Divine Kingdom was preparing to invade the Heartlands.

But Shaw wasn't worried — not really. He never was. The Sea Wyrm Sect was a third-rate power. When the Corrupted Divine Kingdom had been on its rampage, Shaw hadn't fought back; he'd submitted without hesitation, even announced publicly that he wished to join them. He'd used sect business as an excuse to avoid the assault on Oracle City entirely. Maddox hadn't given a third-rate sect a second thought. And when the Corrupted Divine Kingdom fell, the Sea Wyrm Sect walked away untouched. Shaw considered this a point of personal pride.

So if the York Divine Kingdom wanted the Heartlands, he would do exactly what he'd always done. Submit. Survive.

What gave him pause was the sheer scale of what he was looking at. Nine Soul Sea Realm masters, dispatched so casually — just how deep did the York Divine Kingdom's reserves run?

He was still turning that thought over when one of the figures broke away from the group and descended toward him. It was Dorian. 

He glanced at Shaw with the mild disinterest of a man looking at furniture. "You. Which direction is Oracle City?"

Shaw's face split into a wide smile. He'd guessed right — they were headed for Oracle City, not here. "Southwest, sir! Southwest!" He was already reaching into his Storage Ring. "Please — take this." He held out a map, his own personal one, hand-drawn with golden lines marking every city and road in the Heartlands. "It will take you straight there."

Dorian took it without a word and turned back toward the sky. As an afterthought, he tossed something down.

Then the entire group wheeled southwest and was gone, streaking across the sky like a cascade of falling stars.

Shaw watched them shrink into the distance until his shoulders finally dropped. The pressure of a hundred-odd auras lifting off him all at once was like putting down a stone he hadn't realized he was carrying.

He looked down at what Dorian had thrown him. It was a dagger, plain-looking, unremarkable.

Then his senses caught up with his eyes.

"This… this is…" His voice had gone strange. "This is a — a Relic!"

The messenger standing beside him blinked. This was the same man Shaw had just lectured about composure. About a warrior's temperament. About how panicking never solved anything.

He said nothing. He just watched his Sect Master stammer and shake, and kept his thoughts very carefully to himself.

[AMBIENT: CITY PANIC]

[SFX: WARNING BELLS]

The group's passage across the Heartlands did not go unnoticed. The rumors spread faster than the travelers themselves.

Panic erupted throughout Oracle City. On the city walls, a Core-Forged Realm warrior stared up at the sky, his face pale. He mumbled to himself, "A hundred Life-and-Death warriors… How can anyone fight that?"

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

 As warning bells began to toll across the city, the three leaders of the World Merchants' Guild flew into the air, followed by twenty of their Spirit-Tribulation Realm elders. They gathered in a tight, desperate cluster, their faces etched with horror as they gazed up at the invaders.

Zane had promised he would ask for help from Soul Sea Realm masters, but Kieran and the others knew the truth. It would have been a miracle if he managed to convince even one or two to come. A favor from a warrior of that level came at an astronomical cost.

Still, they had clung to that hope. Even one or two Soul Sea masters defending Oracle City would have given them a fighting chance. After all, the York Divine Kingdom was powerful, but they wouldn't risk their most valuable assets on a backwater like The Heartlands. They wouldn't send their entire force of Soul Sea masters just to crush them.

But now, the divine kingdom had arrived before Zane's promised reinforcements.

[MUSIC: DARK HOPELESS STRINGS]

Kieran’s voice was bitter, stripped of all hope. "There are nine Soul Sea Realm masters and ninety Life-and-Death Realm masters. At this point, it doesn't matter if Zane's help shows up or not."
