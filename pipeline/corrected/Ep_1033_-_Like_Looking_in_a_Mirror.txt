Ep 1033 - Like Looking in a Mirror

Word count: 978

[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

At Fenton’s words, Zane actually clapped.

Not many people thought like that. For all his arrogance, Fenton’s attitude earned Zane’s respect. That was the mindset of a true master.

Zane didn’t know a thing about the alliance, but he could tell from the crowd’s faces that it was a huge deal in the Upper World.

But for a warrior, believing in yourself was everything. Gold shines no matter where it is. A shortcut is nice, but relying on someone else instead of your own two feet was putting the cart before the horse.

“That’s right. Couldn’t agree more,” Zane said with a smile, getting to his feet.

Fenton replied coolly, “I don’t need your agreement.”

“Fair enough. But I’m accepting your challenge,” Zane said.

“Good. I respect the courage, at least,” Fenton nodded.

He couldn’t imagine losing. Even in the Life-and-Death Realm, he had weathered nine Small Heavenly Tribulations. He hadn’t opened a Soul Sea yet, but his power already dwarfed that of any ordinary warrior from the Soul Sea Realm.

If he lost to some kid at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm, he’d be a laughingstock. Even if Zane was chosen by the alliance, he must have pulled some kind of trick. But to Fenton, none of that mattered.

[SFX: EXCITED CHATTER]

“He took the bait! The kid actually accepted the challenge!”

“Haha! I’m not a fan of Fenton picking on the new guy, but I can’t wait to see this kid get wrecked!”

“He looks so calm now. Just wait. I want to see him begging for mercy when Fenton’s done with him!”

A buzz went through the fighters in the Myriad Spirit House—they knew they were in for a show.

The clueless kid from the Lower World might have no idea what was coming, but everyone else knew exactly how strong Fenton was. It wasn’t just Fenton who was supremely confident; the other fighters had an almost blind faith in him.

[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]

“This way, then,” Fenton said, gesturing with his hand.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]

Zane finally left the corner.

It was the first time he’d even stood up since walking into the Myriad Spirit House.

Zane followed Fenton through the building, the rest of the fighters trailing behind them like a hungry pack.

Word spread fast. Disciples who’d been training in the secret realm cut their sessions short and came running. Those in closed-door cultivation ended their seclusion early. Warriors scattered across the Holy City scrambled for the nearest transmission array, praying Fenton would take his time.

[SFX: VOICES ECHOING THROUGH THE HALL]

”Closed-door cultivation? End it now — Fenton’s about to fight!“

”A good show? I’m coming!“

”Where? How long do we have?“

By the time Zane and Fenton reached the hall, the crowd had swollen from a few dozen to hundreds, then past a thousand, pressing two thousand, three thousand, four thousand. Almost all of them were Soul Sea Realm. Only a handful were Life-and-Death Realm.

Zane glanced back at the mass of people behind him and said nothing. In the Lower World, something like this would never draw a crowd this size. Up here, even a minor exchange between two fighters could stop an entire city. He filed that away.

[SFX: MULTIPLE FOOTSTEPS]

When they reached the hall, Zane looked around. The space was large but not large enough — at their level, a real exchange would tear the roof off in a few moves.

”I’m not sure this hall is appropriate,“ he said.

Fenton didn’t laugh. He gestured toward two diamond-shaped crystal sigils floating at the center of the hall. ”This is where we exchange pointers. There’s an Illusionary Arena inside.“

Zane looked at the sigils. ”Do you mean like an Illusory Array?“

Fenton considered that for a moment. ”Sure, you can understand it that way. It won’t remember anything you want to hide. It’s just like looking in a mirror.”

What made it different from an ordinary Illusory Array was this: an Illusory Array used array formations to generate illusions around a physical body.

The Illusionary Arena worked differently — it used the fifth layer of Space Law, a principle called Refraction, to project a perfect copy of the warrior inside. The original body stayed in the hall. The projection fought. Weapons, techniques, cultivation — all of it copied exactly. It was far more sophisticated than anything Zane had encountered in the Lower World.

Fenton waved his hand. The two diamond-shaped crystal sigils drifted down from where they floated in the hall. He took one and pushed the other toward Zane. “Pour your Vitality into it, and you’ll enter.”

But his flicker of hesitation was all the crowd needed.

[SFX: MOCKING LAUGHTER]

“Look how careful he is! This kid from the Lower World is terrified his secrets will get out!”

“I wonder what those secrets are even worth.”

“The poor are always so skittish. You get used to it.”

Still, Zane had every reason to be cautious.

Forget the soulforge in his mind or the Celestial Fragment—if the Roaring Token was ever revealed, he’d be a dead man.

Even Supreme Lords would kill for a Roaring Token with unlimited uses. Zane had every right to be paranoid.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL VOID]

[SFX: CRYSTAL ACTIVATION]

Ignoring the jeers, Zane centered himself and activated the diamond-shaped crystal sigils.

[SFX: DIMENSIONAL SHIFT]

The moment he activated them, he felt a strange lurch, as if he were sinking into another dimension.

But he wasn’t actually going anywhere. He was still standing right there in The Crucible, only now an identical projection of himself stood in his place.

So this is the Illusionary Arena, Zane thought, a slow smile spreading across his face. You can fight with everything you’ve got in here. Even if you die, it’s just the projection. No real harm done, which means we can go all out.

He examined his surroundings. The space was a vast, featureless void that stretched out endlessly in every direction.

[SFX: ENERGY FLICKER]

A short distance away, Fenton’s projection flickered into existence, having entered the arena as well.
