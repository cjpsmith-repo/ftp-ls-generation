Ep 667 - Hold On To Zane

Word count: 1018

[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND]

[SFX: RUMBLING EARTH]

The clash between the Mad Scorpion and the Celestial Root threw up a hurricane of wind, tearing away more than a thousand feet of soil from the chasm’s edge. The two-thousand-foot pit they'd dug was rendered completely useless, the fierce gales making it feel no deeper than a hundred-foot ditch. They couldn’t stay put, so they scrambled out, hearts hammering against their ribs. The moment their heads peeked over the rim, the sheer chaos of the scene made them tremble.

They could only watch in dread as the battle raged over the abyss, all of them sharing the same thought: just being here and still being alive was a miracle.

[SFX: MONSTROUS ROAR]

The Mad Scorpion let out a wild roar and charged the Celestial Root, which met its ferocity in kind. But the scorpion’s terrifying, ear-splitting shriek didn't seem to faze the root at all. Instead, the plant-like monster twisted its massive, mountain-thick body and swung its grotesque head, coiling for an attack of its own.

[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]

[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]

A thunderous boom ripped through the air. The Mad Scorpion’s size was deceptive; it looked impossibly huge and slow, but it moved with shocking speed. Just as the Celestial Root lunged, the scorpion’s pincer slammed down on its head with perfect timing. The impact sounded like hundreds of thunderclaps going off at once, forcing the rest of the group to block their hearing just to keep from going deaf.

[SFX: REPEATED HEAVY IMPACTS]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

The Celestial Root just shook its head and roared, charging again. But the Mad Scorpion was ready, delivering another brutal slap that could have leveled a mountain range if it had landed on the main continent. After a few more bone-jarring impacts, the incredibly fierce Celestial Root was finally beaten into submission.

[AMBIENT: EERIE FOREST]

[SFX: CREATURE FEEDING SOUNDS]

As the beastly scorpion began to feed, Lavender glanced at Zane, sending a thought directly into his mind. "You know, the Celestial Root… It's just a vegetable."

"A vegetable?" he thought back, his eyes glued to the monstrous victor.

"That's right," she replied with a mental smile.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

"It's feed for Greater Legendary Beasts. If you don't believe me, ask that Cobalt Dragon."

Curious, Zane did just that. Hey, is that true?

"Don't listen to her nonsense!" the Cobalt Dragon shot back, his voice dripping with indignation. "I've been fasting for years!"

What did you eat before that? Zane pressed with a smile.

"Before I reached inedia, I wouldn't have eaten this either!" the dragon shot back. "The True Dragon Realm has far better things than Celestial Roots. It may be a great tonic, but the taste — only brutes like that scorpion can stomach it. Why would a True Dragon lower itself to eating something so ugly?"

Lavender was right, of course. Before Super Divine Beasts could fast, they needed staggering amounts of food to grow. Other Super Divine Beasts were too dangerous and too rare to hunt reliably. So most of them grew up as vegetarians — and a Celestial Root the size of a mountain was exactly the kind of meal they needed.

"What do we do now?" Zane asked Yolande.

Everyone looked at her. After everything — the wind, the pit, the roaring — their nerves were shredded. Even Esther and Fabian, who had faced more than most, looked rattled. The scale of what they were watching was simply too much.

Yolande didn't answer the question. Instead, she said: "Hold on tight. Everyone — hold on tight to Zane."

Then she grabbed his arm.

[SFX: RAPID SCRAMBLING]

Before he could ask what she meant, the others were already moving — reflex overriding reason. Esther grabbed one arm. Fabian and Jarrod grabbed the other. The Demon Night War Lords piled on behind them.

Malcolm was fast. He grabbed the back of Zane's neck.

Timothy was last. By the time he got there, there was no Zane left to grab. He settled for Malcolm.

"Hey—" Zane started. "Could you at least—"

Nobody listened. Bones creaking, he gave up.

Lavender stowed her spear in his ring, shrank into her sword spirit form, and slipped inside him without a word.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Then the suction started.

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

The Mad Scorpion's mouth opened wider, creating a terrifying vortex. The suction pulled them off their feet, and everyone clung to Zane for dear life. They spun and dangled helplessly in the air, watching in horror as the Celestial Root was torn apart and pulled, piece by piece, into that gaping maw.

"What is happening?!" Malcolm bellowed at Yolande, his grip on Zane's neck tightening. "You're leading us into its mouth?!"

Esther's expression was thunderous. "Yolande. Explain. Now."

"We were safe in the pit!" Fabian cried. "We could have stayed down there — now we're going to be swallowed—"

Yolande blinked at them, perfectly calm. "To leave the fog, we have to enter its mouth. I planned this from the beginning. I didn't do anything wrong."

"You could have warned us!" Jarrod managed, white-knuckled on Zane's arm.

"No need," Yolande said. Her small nose tilted toward Zane's chest, and she sniffed — apparently taking a moment to smell his shirt. "If you do as I say, you may not die. If you don't, you definitely will."

That settled the argument.

[AMBIENT: CAVERNOUS ECHOES]

To the Celestial Root, it was just a mouth. To them, it was a cavernous abyss tens of thousands of feet high, big enough to swallow ten mountain ranges whole. They weren't even ants or dust to the Mad Scorpion; they were nothing. It probably couldn't even see them.

A few ten-foot-wide boulders were also sucked in with them, and some came hurtling straight for the group. Yolande and the others shattered them with desperate attacks before they could crush anyone. After being tossed around above the chasm, they were finally pulled past the monster's teeth.

[SFX: ENERGY DRAIN]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

The moment they crossed that threshold, the Vitality in Zane's body just… vanished.

Malcolm's and Timothy's faces fell in panic. Their Forceful Energy was gone, too. Zane had both, and found himself in the same boat — both powers disappearing in the exact same instant. Just moments before, they'd been using that energy to keep themselves stable in the turbulent air.

[SFX: FALLING WHOOSH]

Now, with nothing to hold them, they were helpless, tumbling down into the absolute darkness.
