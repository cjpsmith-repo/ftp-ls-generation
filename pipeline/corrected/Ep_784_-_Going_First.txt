Ep 784 - Going First

Word count: 1046

[AMBIENT: BUSY REGISTRATION HALL]

 The woman at the registration desk, a cultivator at the Inner-Core Realm, began taking down Zane’s information. As he watched her prepare a wooden token, Zane hoped she wouldn’t ask where he was from. He and Francis had already discussed this, deciding it was best to invent a new identity from scratch. Francis had told him not to sweat it, explaining that the divine kingdoms didn’t track the origins of freelance fighters. All that mattered was their strength and their ability to bring back resources from the Sacred Feather Sea. Once the kingdoms got their cut, they couldn't care less where you were born.

Besides, the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms was an island territory. For tens of thousands of years, they’d had so few visitors from other continents that, in the eyes of the Emperors, any warrior on their soil was considered one of their own.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 Zane answered every question smoothly until the woman asked for his name. He paused for a beat, thinking. Using his real name was out of the question; he couldn't risk the Holy Maiden of the York Divine Kingdom recognizing him, so he made up an alias on the spot. “Zane Jones.” It would have to do.

[SFX: WOODEN TOKEN SLIDING]

 After sealing his information in the token, the woman gave him a polite smile and handed it over. "The first round is in three days. All registered independents and the fighters from our martial house will be gathered then. I hope you do well."

Real encouraging, Zane thought, catching the undisguised pity in her tone. In her eyes, he was just another nobody, cannon fodder for the first round.

He couldn't blame her. The competition in the divine kingdoms was brutal. The Crystal Martial House was only offering fifty Harlen Tokens, which meant nearly six hundred of their own warriors—most of them at the late stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm—were all fighting for a handful of spots. On top of that, another two or three hundred independent warriors like Zane had signed up, and the majority of them were just as strong.

The woman had probably sized him up in an instant. A twenty-year-old reaching the middle stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm was impressive, sure, but he was years away from having a real shot. She likely even felt a little sorry for him, seeing him throw himself into a meat grinder that included powerhouse disciples and even princes who were hell-bent on securing a token. Her professional smile couldn't quite hide the sympathy in her eyes.

Zane just smiled back, pocketed the token, and left.

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

[AMBIENT: VAST TRAINING GROUND]

 Three days later, he returned. An attendant guided him through the depths of the Martial House and into a vast open square already packed with Spirit-Tribulation warriors. The air was thick with restrained power — no hostility, no posturing, just the particular stillness of people who had been in enough fights to know better. Unlike fresh sect recruits buzzing with nerves and excitement, these warriors — some decades old, some centuries — stood calm and composed, hands behind their backs, eyes forward. Only the weak telegraphed their intentions. Veterans saved that energy for when it counted.

When Zane's gaze reached the center of the square, he stopped.

Three Soul Sea Realm masters stood there overseeing the proceedings. Three — just for a single Martial House exam. He thought of the Violet Heart Sanctuary back in the Cursed Land, a sixth-rank Holy Land by any measure, which also counted three Soul Sea masters among its ranks. And the Crystal Martial House was just one of countless houses serving one of four kingdoms. 

The sheer depth of power here was staggering. No wonder the Divine Kingdoms had always looked at the Heartlands like a backwater. The pride he'd seen on the faces of kingdom warriors in the Celestial Tomb of Vesper hadn't been empty arrogance — it had been backed by exactly this.

Even so, Zane didn't shrink back. He found a corner, clasped his hands behind his back, and waited.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

Almost immediately, he felt a gaze sweep toward him. He turned. Randy was watching him from across the square — still competing, still fighting for his own token despite his title. It made sense, Zane thought. In a world where the monarch held power through sheer strength alone, a title meant nothing without the cultivation to back it up. 

Soul Sea Realm rulers lived for tens of thousands of years and had children beyond counting. Any prince who coasted on his father's name would find himself with nothing the moment that father was gone — outmaneuvered by stronger siblings, or swallowed whole by rival powers. Randy was here because he had no choice but to be. None of them did. Their eyes met. Zane gave him a faint smile. Randy looked at him coldly and looked away.

[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]

[AMBIENT: LARGE CROWD MURMUR]

 A commanding voice cut through the murmurs in the square, and the thousand-plus Spirit-Tribulation warriors fell silent. One of the three Soul Sea Realm masters stepped forward.

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY PULSE]

 He reached out a hand and pressed it lightly against the ground. Colorful patterns bloomed across the metal floor of the square, spreading outward from the center — and at their outermost edge, a bold circle of red lines.

"Thirty warriors will participate in the first round," he declared, his voice carrying across the silent square. "Of these thirty, only three will advance. In other words, ninety percent of you will be eliminated. You will enter the circle in batches. Force your opponents outside the red line using your own strength, and you advance. Combat is permitted. Killing is not. Violators will be severely punished."

Zane closed his eyes, listening intently as the master read through the list. He didn't hear his alias called. He wasn't in the first group.

Going first had its drawbacks. You went in blind, with no knowledge of the rules or the terrain, while everyone else got to watch and learn. Then again, a clever fighter could turn that uncertainty into an advantage.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Soon, thirty Spirit-Tribulation warriors stepped forward and entered the red circle in the center of the square. A tense energy crackled among them. This was it — the preliminary round. 

For the warriors of the Harlen Divine Kingdom, a Harlen Token was everything, and anyone eliminated here would lose their chance for good.
