Ep 735 - Twenty Percent

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Owen had offered to take them straight to the World Merchants' Guild headquarters, and the three of them journeyed through a city that had transformed into a fortress since the war began.
Geographically, the Chaotic Ocean of Stars was supposed to be an impassable moat, a natural barrier protecting them. But the divine kingdoms had histories that stretched back far longer than The Heartlands, and you had to be a fool to think they didn’t have warriors capable of crossing it.
Zane’s recent trip to the celestial tomb had made one thing brutally clear: even the Life-and-Death Realm experts, the supposed top-tier fighters in The Heartlands, were barely qualified to be errand boys for the Holy Maiden and that guy from the Divine Kingdom.
If those kingdoms ever decided to get truly involved, who in the Heartlands could possibly stand a chance against the Corrupted Divine Kingdom?
[MUSIC: TENSE PIANO]
A grim look settled over Zane’s face. "Did you figure that out on your own?" he asked.
Owen shook his head. "It’s pretty much the consensus at the Merchants' Guild. Tia, your leader at Cloud Hall thinks so, too."
"Tia?" Zane’s eyes lit with a flicker of hope. "She’s in Oracle City?"
At the mention of his leader, Cora, who had been quiet until now, turned to look at him, a faint, curious smile touching her lips. Zane was too focused on Owen to notice.
Owen, however, looked genuinely confused. "Zane, how can you not know what Cloud Hall is up to?" he asked. He’d just assumed Zane was a core member, so it was bizarre that he seemed completely out of the loop on the pact between Cloud Hall and the Merchants' Guild.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
Zane managed a tight, embarrassed smile. How was he supposed to explain the whole situation with Tia? He wasn’t even sure there was a situation. She’d just turned cold on him for no reason he could figure out, and he was completely at a loss. This time, Cloud Hall had mobilized all its top fighters, and he was the only one left behind. How was he supposed to be okay with that?
"I was deep in training, totally cut off from everything," Zane said, forcing a casual tone. "Can you fill me in?"
"Tia didn't really stick around," Owen replied smoothly. "She's been hunting Corrupted Divine warriors pretty aggressively, so she rarely comes back to the city."
Tia had broken the arrangement from the start. Life-and-Death warriors on both sides were supposed to hold back until the decisive battle — the moment one moved, it signaled the final clash. She'd ignored that entirely, going rogue and hunting alone through occupied cities, cutting down Corrupted Divine Kingdom warriors by the hundreds. It had caught both sides off guard.
The Guild was worried. Her tactic worked against ordinary warriors, but Maddox and Simon were Life-and-Death warriors too — if they set a trap, she couldn't fight them both. Everyone had tried to talk her out of it. The Cloud Hall elders were her subordinates and couldn't order her. The Guild was only an ally. She ignored all of them. In the past month, she'd spent fewer than four days in Oracle City.
The original plan had been for Cloud Hall and the World Merchants' Guild to join forces and formally declare war. As the only Life-and-Death Realm expert from her sect, Tia was supposed to anchor the defense in Oracle City. Sooner or later, a decisive battle for the city was inevitable, and the Merchants' Guild had spared no expense on their defensive runes. Their resources dwarfed any single sect’s, and their preparations were formidable.
Zane followed Owen into the conference hall at the Guild’s headquarters.
[AMBIENT: LARGE CONFERENCE HALL]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
Since the war began, the city had become blanketed in hundreds of thousands of runes layered across every street and wall, forming a single vast array. It wasn't as powerful as the Trinity Killing Array, but what it lacked in refinement it made up for in sheer scale. The Runic Tower drew array masters from across the world, and the Guild's wealth meant the runes had been laid without any thought for cost.
As soon as he walked in, he saw a room full of familiar faces. Saint Morphen and the other elders from Cloud Hall were there, along with the three heads of the World Merchants' Guild. The only person missing was the ancient tycoon who practically ran Oracle City.
"Zane! You're finally here!" Kieran exclaimed, his round face flushing with excitement.
Zane dipped his head in a respectful nod. "Master Kieran. Master Hobbes. Master Burns."
[MUSIC: SUBTLE TENSION]
The truth was, the Guild had been asking Tia to summon him for weeks, ever since they’d heard he was back. She’d put them off every time, telling them he was in seclusion. Even though they were allies, Zane was still part of Cloud Hall, so the Guild couldn't exactly force the issue.
After a few brief pleasantries, Kieran got straight to the point. "Zane, now that you're here, I assume you'll work with us to fight Maddox?"
It was a clever way to phrase it. Kieran knew the bad blood between Zane and Maddox was personal and deep, so he framed the request around their rivalry instead of the larger war.
Zane gave a short, firm nod. "Of course."
"Excellent! Please, head to the Runic Tower," Kieran said, his voice buzzing with excitement. "With you on our side, I'm confident our chances of defeating Maddox will go up by at least twenty percent!"
Twenty percent.
[MUSIC: MAGICAL EXCITEMENT]
The number shocked everyone in the room, but it didn't take them long to connect the dots. If Zane could single-handedly create a magical array stronger than Cloud Hall's own protective formation, what could he do with the full backing of the World Merchants' Guild? The boost in their odds suddenly seemed not just possible, but entirely plausible.
The only question was whether he would actually agree. And he just had.
