Ep 580 - Sit Down, Don
Word count: 1109
[AMBIENT: DEEP UNDERGROUND CAVERN]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORCHESTRAL]
Kingsley's words were still ringing in his ears when the questions started in Zane's mind.
The Tower of Sin was a bizarre, inverted structure, its peak buried deep within the earth.
To reach the bottom, Zane would have to enter from the very top.
Vesper had told him an inheritance of incredible power was hidden at the bottom—something so important that Vesper had sacrificed his own reincarnation for it. There had to be something huge down there.
But Zane had no idea what it was. He turned to Elder Kingsley. "What's at the bottom of the Tower?"
Elder Kingsley shot him a strange look. "You don't know?"
Nearly every sacred place on the continent was built in the shadow of the Tower. Every warrior knew that to reach the highest echelons of power, you had to brave its depths. Only by reaching the bottom could someone even dream of standing at the very top of the continent.
Even if the kid wasn't from the Violet Heart Sanctuary, how could he be so ignorant?
But it wasn't exactly a secret, so Elder Kingsley decided to explain.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]
"At the very bottom of the Tower, there's a massive cavern we call the Dark Nether Cave. Most of the year, it's flooded with something called Dark Nether Death Light. Even a level ten War Lord would be incinerated the moment they stepped inside."
[SFX: DRAMATIC STING]
"Dark Nether Death Light?" Zane echoed, his eyes wide. "It can kill a level ten War Lord just like that?"
Seeing the doubt on Zane's face, Elder Kingsley clarified."That's why we can't go in for most of the year. But for one month, every year, the Light weakens significantly. That's our window. It's when every race on the continent rushes into the cave, searching for their chance."
The Tower was vital to the sacred places for two reasons. First, it was the ultimate training ground. Any warrior with enough talent and a high enough cultivation could use the 'Lucky Light' found inside to achieve incredible breakthroughs. The second reason, of course, was the Dark Nether Cave.
The treasures and power each sacred place had pulled from the Dark Nether Cave were the stuff of legend.
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
The Violet Heart Sanctuary itself, for example, was founded by a human who won a seat in the Tower and discovered something extraordinary in the depths.
"Do you need a seat to enter the Dark Nether Cave?" Zane asked.
Kingsley shook his head. "Not necessarily. Each seat comes with five entry spots. As long as you reach the tenth floor when the cave opens, we can allocate you one of ours."
"So with four seats, you have twenty spots total?" Zane said.
"That's right." Kingsley nodded. "Get to the tenth floor in time, and we'll get you in."
Zane felt something sharpen in his chest. "Then I owe you one, Elder Kingsley."
"We help each other," Kingsley said simply. "The human race has been struggling for a long time. It's not that we lack strength at the top — the Sanctuary's senior elders are formidable. But it's been years since a new generation of powerhouses emerged. The Demon Night alone produces several god-tier geniuses every year. We've had three in the last decade — and one of them died in this Tower." He paused. "If that trend continues, when the elders are gone, there'll be nothing left."
He didn't say it as a guilt trip. Just a fact.
Zane said nothing.
[SFX: TAVERN DOOR CRASH]
[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL DRUMS]
A booming laugh echoed as two Ogre War Lords, massive as mountains, shouldered through the entrance.
"Afraid the human race won't have any spots this year," the first announced. "Human brat, it won't help you."
His companion turned to Zane with a grin. "Join the Ogres instead. We hold twenty-two spots. Plenty of room—even for a human."
Elder Kingsley's expression darkened. This kind of open provocation was rare even by Tower standards. War Lords coming down from the upper floors just to taunt a War Fighter — it was beneath them. But the Ogres had twenty-two seats, and they knew it. They could afford to be shameless.
He was about to respond when Zane stood up.
Zane looked at the two of them pleasantly. "Does the Ogre clan have a War General named Boris?"
"Of course," the first one said. "Strongest genius from the Flaming Place."
"He's decent," Zane said.
The Ogre puffed up. "Decent? He's the best we have—"
"I'm not talking about his talent," Zane said, his tone shifting. "I'm saying his character is miles above yours. You're War Lords. Boris is only a War General, and he carries himself better than either of you. Strip away your cultivation rank and what have you got? Nothing."
[MUSIC: DARK THREATENING]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
The first Ogre's face went ugly. He stepped forward, and a massive aura crashed down toward Zane like a falling mountain.
Elder Kingsley quietly rose to intervene. Zane waved him off without looking.
He took two steps toward the Ogre instead.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Most War Fighters would be trembling by now, buckling under the weight of a War Lord's soul pressure. Some might even be physically injured by the force of it.
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]
Fighting was forbidden here in the safe zone, but Ogres were notorious for pushing their weight around. They almost always got away with it, too.
Don, the first Ogre War Lord, hadn't planned on making a big scene. He just wanted to scare the hell out of this human kid.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
But Zane just stood his ground, a faint smile playing on his lips. He met Don's glare without a flicker of fear and sneered, "A level one War Lord? You think that makes you special? Your aura is pathetic—weaker than Boris's, even. Take away your rank, and you're nothing."
Elder Kingsley watched, a mix of surprise and delight on his face at Zane's composure. What the elder didn't know was that Zane couldn't be intimidated by aura alone—not even by a War Sage from the Sanctuary, let alone some low-level War Lord. The War Sages on this continent were no stronger than the Life-and-Death Realm warriors from The Heartlands. Back home, Zane had faced down Maddox's suffocating pressure without so much as flinching. This Ogre's attempt was a joke.
"Arrogant bastard!" Don thought, a flicker of helplessness in his eyes. His aura wasn't touching the kid. The rumors were true. This human was a monster.
"Arrogant?" Zane laughed out loud. "You want to see arrogant? Fine. Let me show you what a real aura feels like."
[AMBIENT: ENERGY CRACKLING]
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
With that, Zane's gaze locked onto Don, his eyes flashing with power.
Don had just wanted to frighten a War Fighter. What he got was something else entirely.
