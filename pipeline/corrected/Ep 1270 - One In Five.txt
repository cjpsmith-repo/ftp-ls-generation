Ep 1270 - One In Five

Word count: 1014

[MUSIC: TENSE PULSATING]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL HALL WITH ENERGY PULSES]
Janet and the short warrior moved like lightning, just a blur of motion that easily put them in the top three strongest of the group. Even as the square beams of light pulsing from the floor slowed them down, they were weaving a web of traps across one side of the hall. 
[SFX: CRACKLING THUNDER]
She was using a technique she called the Lofty Wood—a fifth-level Wood Law ability that was one of her clan’s most powerful—while his thunder technique was just as incredible, though he never said what it was.
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: ENERGY TRAPS ACTIVATING]
They zipped back and forth, setting a new trap with every square of floor they reached. In just five minutes, they’d covered a third of the hall. 
[SFX: MASSIVE BOULDERS FIRING]
It was a stupidly exhausting method, draining their Vitality so much they had to pause and recover every time the giant boulders fired. But it was their only shot. 
Zane’s plan was to cover every spot a boulder could land, then trigger all the traps at once to obliterate the dark red grimace on the far wall.
[SFX: STONE CUBES WHOOSHING]
The others did what they could to help, all while dodging the massive stone cubes. But just as they were hitting the halfway mark, something went wrong.
[SFX: OMINOUS BLACK ENERGY PULSE]
A pulse of black light erupted from the floor tiles, washing over the hall in a strange, silent wave. 
[SFX: MAGICAL DISSIPATION]
It didn’t harm anyone, but when it faded, every single trap they had laid was gone.
“Wh-what?!” the short warrior sputtered.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STOPPING ABRUPTLY]
Zane’s jaw tightened as he took in the clean floor. Janet’s hands were clenched into fists at her sides, her face a mask of fury. The rest of the warriors just stood in stunned silence. All that work, all that energy, had vanished in an instant. The message was clear: the hall wouldn’t let them set traps
[MUSIC: FRUSTRATED TENSION]
[SFX: HEAVY STONE RUMBLING]
As if to rub it in, the giant square boulders rumbled back into a line, getting ready to fire their beams again.
Janet had the boulders’ patterns memorized, but Zane was moving on pure instinct. 
“There’s another way,” Zane said, already unslinging the dark golden bow from his back. “We’ve got five million Illusion Points riding on this hall already — nobody’s walking away now. Everyone pick a boulder and watch it. The second you see that red face mid-roll, call it out. I’ll put an arrow through it before it disappears again.”
No one argued. Whatever chance they had, they’d take it.
[SFX: BOULDERS ROLLING AND SCATTERING]
Each of them fixed their eyes on a different stone as the boulders scattered again, then gathered, then scattered — over and over, the pattern never quite the same twice.
“Not here…”
“Not here either!”
“Not here…”
Finding the face at all was hard enough — barely a one-in-five chance on any given roll. Catching it in the split second before the stone finished turning was worse. But no one in the group so much as flinched. Everyone had already staked five million points on this gamble; no one was backing out now.
Then, finally—
“Found it!”
The Demon Night cultivator’s shout cut through the hall, and every head snapped toward the boulder beside him. 
[SFX: BOWSTRING SNAP]
[SFX: ARROW WHOOSH]
In the same instant, the sound of a bowstring snapping taut rang out — Zane’s reflexes were fastest, and his arrow was already in the air, a streak of black light aimed true.
[SFX: SWORD LIGHT SLASH]
But from another direction, a blade of pure sword-light lanced out at the very same target.
“Damn it!” Zane and the short warrior swore in unison — the boulder had already rolled halfway over, and one more turn would bury the pale red face for good. Both attacks were too fast to pull back.
[SFX: COLLISION BURST]
[SFX: ARROW CLATTERING ON CEILING]
The two strikes met head-on, colliding in a burst of light and refracting apart — Zane’s arrow deflected skyward into the ceiling, the sword-light glancing off the stone’s flank instead of striking true.
Groans of disappointment went up around the hall. The Demon Night cultivator looked the most frustrated of all. “That face wasn’t easy to find,” he muttered bitterly. “If it’d just been one of us working alone, we might already be through.”
The short warrior only smiled, easy and unbothered. “It’s fine. There’ll be another chance.”
[SFX: BOULDERS ROLLING AND SCATTERING]
The boulders scattered and reset, and the group shifted positions to match. Four more rounds passed. No one found the face.
On the fifth attempt, Zane stared down the boulder in front of him, irritation creeping into his expression as it rolled past without revealing anything.
Then, from somewhere ahead of him, the Demon Night woman’s voice rang out. “Over there!” She was pointing — not at his boulder, but at one behind him.
He spun, and in the same motion, he threw the small skull he’d been carrying right underneath it.
[SFX: MUFFLED IMPACT]
[SFX: STONE GRINDING TO HALT]
There was a muffled thud. Everyone stared as the massive boulder froze mid-flip, unable to complete its rotation. For all its incredible weight, the boulder couldn’t crush the tiny skull. It was just stuck there, propped up at a weird angle.
“Watch out for the beam!” Janet yelled from across the room.
[SFX: ENERGY BEAM CHARGING UP]
Zane was right in the line of fire. If that beam hit him, he was dead. Simple as that.
But with the skull propping it up, the tilted boulder sent its energy beam shooting harmlessly toward the ceiling. 
[SFX: ENERGY BEAM REDIRECTING]
[SFX: BODY HITTING STONE FLOOR]
Zane hit the floor with a dull thud, having thrown himself face-first to get out of the way. Lying on the stone, he pulled a black arrow from his quiver. A small smirk touched his lips as he looked past the tilted boulder toward the dark red grimace. The beams would last for a few more seconds—plenty of time.
[SFX: ARROW NOCKED]
[SFX: BOWSTRING RELEASED]
He nocked the arrow, aimed, and let it fly.
[SFX: ARROW WHISTLING THROUGH AIR]
The black arrow shot forward, zipping straight through the gap created by the skull and the propped-up boulder. Even as it flew, a flicker of doubt crossed Zane’s mind. This was just a theory, after all. He had no real proof that destroying the grimace was the key.
[SFX: ARROW IMPACT ON STONE]
The arrow struck home, burying itself deep in the carved, angry face.
The moment it hit, every single beam of light in the room vanished. The entire hall fell completely silent.
