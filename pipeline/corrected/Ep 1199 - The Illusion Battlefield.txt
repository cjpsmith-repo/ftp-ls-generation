Ep 1199 - The Illusion Battlefield

Word count: 1112

[MUSIC: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
[SFX: ENERGY PULSING]
Two beams of light shot from Livingston’s metal head, pulsing with energy. Even with a simple puppet body, his feelings were plain as day, and right now, that brilliant glow was pure excitement.
“How is that even possible?” he cried out.
He just couldn’t wrap his head around it. This massive piece of Star Essence Iron was impossibly heavy, yet Zane had just lifted it like it was nothing. It was something so far beyond what Livingston could have imagined that it felt like a dream.
Zane gave the sword blank an easy swing and then grinned at Livingston. “Want to give it a try?” he asked, holding out the pincers.
Livingston took them, and his mechanical jaw dropped. He couldn’t feel the weight of the sword blank at all. “Incredible!” he said, his voice buzzing. “This tool is amazing!” 
He’d had high hopes for The Armory, knowing everything inside was special, but this… this blew all his expectations out of the water. He turned the pincers over and over, but there was nothing unusual about them. He couldn’t figure out how they made a chunk of super-dense iron feel completely weightless.
“They’re made from Destiny Stone,” Saul explained, his voice as flat as ever. “In theory, they can lift anything, no matter how much it weighs.”
Saul actually looked surprised for a second. “What’s that?”
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]
“It’s a Heavenly Destiny from the last great era,” Livingston said, the historical data scrolling through his mind. “It was held in the Crown of Destiny, which belonged to the Supreme Lord who wielded it!” This so-called destiny was actually a Law of Causality; once the Supreme Lord mastered it, he could lift anything—even the sun, moon, and stars.
“Oh, impressive,” Saul said, his flicker of interest already gone. He turned back to Zane. “You won’t have to worry about the sword’s weight. There should be more Destiny Stones in The Armory. Once the Star Essence Iron is forged, we can make the hilt from them. You’ll be able to swing it freely.”
Zane just nodded. Based on the materials alone, this sword was going to be nearly indestructible. But its true power would all come down to Livingston’s skill at the forge.
“This is great!” Livingston was thrilled. 
He gripped the pincers and, with a practiced flick of his wrist, tossed the sword blank into the air.
[SFX: METAL CLATTERING]
In his excitement, he’d loosened his grip at the same time.
“Watch out!” Freya screamed, her voice sharp with panic.
The sword blank was only four inches long, but its weight was astronomical. If it hit the floor, it wouldn’t just leave a dent; it could punch straight through the keep.
[SFX: PINCERS SNAPPING SHUT]
But Livingston was already moving. He waited, completely still, until the blank reached the top of its arc and began to fall — then snapped the pincers shut around it with a clean, practiced click. Not a hair off.
He placed it back in the furnace without a word, looking utterly unbothered.
Freya let out a slow breath.
“Hundreds of millions of years at the forge,” Livingston said mildly. “Did you think I wouldn’t catch it?”
Then his expression shifted back to business. “There’s still the question of how I actually work the thing. The pincers handle the weight — but ordinary hammers won’t touch Star Essence Iron. It’s too dense. I need the right tools.”
“Follow me,” Saul said.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
He led them through a door at the back of the Armory into a storeroom. Floor to ceiling, wall to wall — rare materials and specialized tools, more than any of them could take in at once. Livingston went very still for a moment. Then he started moving through it, his mechanical eyes darting from shelf to shelf, his excitement building with every step.
He didn’t say anything. He didn’t need to.
This was the best place he had ever been in his life.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS POLITICAL TENSION]
The Battle of Destiny had killed four Supreme Lords, releasing four Crowns into the universe. Over the following nine months, all four appeared - and every major faction unleashed everything they had to claim them.
Only Supreme Lords and World Lords could participate. Six World Lords died. Not a single Supreme Lord fell.
The Thoughtless Minds claimed two Crowns. The Demon Night and demons each took one - but both had lost Supreme Lords in the Battle of Destiny, so they merely replaced their losses.
Only the Thoughtless Minds gained ground. They’d lost no one and now commanded two additional Supreme Lords - an unprecedented shift in power. Already controlling four thousand Supreme Worlds under the Supreme Lord of Original Sin, they had grown even stronger.The Alliance and The Hallowed Concord watched the numbers and felt the ground shift beneath them.
Outside the Keep, World Lords from every major faction had taken up positions at a safe distance — close enough to watch, too far to be caught in anything. They weren’t going anywhere. Zane wasn’t going anywhere either.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE SOLITUDE]
[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION CHAMBER]
Zane, however, knew nothing about the political storm brewing in The Heartlands. Since he couldn’t leave The Elysian Keep, he did the only thing he could: he sealed himself away to train.
Livingston never once stepped out of the Armory. Zane returned to the archives, spending time with the handbooks Gus had pointed him toward before he faded.
The days bled into weeks, the weeks into months. One year passed, then two, then three. Before he knew it, five years had gone by.
For most warriors, five years was a drop in the ocean of their long lives. Freya, for instance, was far older than she looked. But for Zane, five years was a sixth of his entire existence. 
[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]
[AMBIENT: SWIRLING Chaos Energy]
In that time, he endured three major Heavenly Tribulations, pushing his power to the middle stage of the Soul Sea Realm. Inside him, the Seed of the World Tree flourished in the Chaos Energy filling his Soul Sea, its trunk now sprouting from the swirling surface.
[MUSIC: EPIC REVELATION]
[SFX: MASSIVE HEAVEN TREMOR]
Then, one day, the heavens shook.
This time, it wasn’t the appearance of a Crown of Destiny. All one hundred and twenty-eight Supreme Lords were still alive, so there was no reason for new ones to emerge. But across the hundred thousand supreme worlds of the universe, every powerful being turned their attention to the vibration.
They all knew what it meant. 
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC BATTLEFIELD ANTICIPATION]
The Illusion Battlefield was about to begin.
