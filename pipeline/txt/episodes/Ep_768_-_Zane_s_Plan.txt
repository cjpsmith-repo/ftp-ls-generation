Ep 768 - Zane's Plan

Word count: 1102

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

Zane had made his promise to Hanson — a place in the Cloud Hall and a thousand supreme Aether crystals from the Guild — but the real prize wasn't the heartstones themselves. It was the question of where they had come from. If Hanson's family records held even a fragment of an answer, Zane needed to hear it.

Hanson nodded reluctantly. “The ancient heartstones were left by my ancestors. So…. I don’t really know where they came from.”

That did it. A murderous calm settled over Zane’s face. He stared down at the man, his voice dropping to a frigid whisper. “You’re playing me.”

The raw killing intent that rolled off him wasn't just a feeling; it was a physical force that slammed into Hanson, stunning him. 

[SFX: HEAVY THUD]

 His face went ghost-white, and his legs buckled, sending him crashing to his knees. “I wouldn’t dare!” he gasped. “Please, just let me finish!”

“Speak,” Zane commanded.

“The heartstones were definitely passed down by my ancestors,” Hanson said, the words tumbling out in a rush. “But seven hundred years ago, House Lee saved someone—a master from the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms! He’s the reason our house grew so powerful. My ancestors never told me the history of the stones, but I’m guessing they came from him.”

The Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms. 

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

 The name landed with the weight of a new possibility. Hanson’s theory made a terrifying amount of sense. Zane had never heard of anything like the ancient heartstones in The Heartlands, and if an artifact containing such miraculous life force was native to this region, someone would have found it by now.

It wasn't a perfect answer, but it was a lead. The energy in these stones wasn’t nearly enough to wake Tia. 

 He had to go to the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms.

Zane pulled himself from his thoughts and saw Hanson staring up at him, his expression a pathetic mix of hope and fear. 

“Follow me,” Zane said.

[AMBIENT: GUILD HALL]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 Relief washed over Hanson’s face as he scrambled to his feet, sticking close behind Zane as they headed straight for the World Merchants’ Guild.

The atmosphere inside the Guild was anything but calm. While Zane had been with Hanson, a quiet war of words had been unfolding in the conference hall. 

Kieran was inclined to trust Zane — somewhat blindly, he admitted, but Zane had never given him reason not to. Hobbes and Burns, however, were not willing to stake their lives on that trust. 

Hobbes's argument was blunt: the York Divine Kingdom wasn't coming for the Heartlands. They were coming for Zane. Lord Wallace's projection had made that clear — he knew Zane was hiding something, and it was that secret drawing the Divine Kingdoms here. If Zane had brought this trouble to their door, why should the Guild share the burden? It couldn't be called burning bridges — it was simply the instinct to survive. And when the elders around the table began to nod quietly, the argument gained traction.

Zane had promised to handle the Soul Sea Realm masters from the York Divine Kingdom himself, but that hadn’t stopped Kieran and the others from preparing for a battle they knew they couldn’t win. No matter how many brilliant minds they put on the problem, they couldn’t come up with a single viable plan.

The Heartlands and the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms were in completely different leagues. While the Heartlands had never had a true sacred place, the Union had a long history and a terrifying number of Soul Sea Realm warriors, possibly making it even stronger than the main continent. They all remembered how Maddox, a single warrior in the Life-and-Death Realm, had nearly wiped out both Cloud Hall and the Guild.

[SFX: CROWD CHATTER]

The debate was still raging when someone at the door announced: "Zane requests an audience."

[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]

Kieran straightened. "Let him in."

Zane led Hanson into the conference hall. Kieran glanced at the beggar trailing behind him, mildly surprised. "Brother Zane — who is this?"

"One thousand supreme Aether crystals," Zane said with a faint smile. "Paid by the Guild."

The three Guild leaders stared at him. That was the reward for curing Tia. Did that mean…

"He cured her?" Kieran looked from Zane to the unremarkable figure of Hanson and back again.

Zane shook his head. "Yes and no." 

He told them everything — the heartstones, the trace of Raw Vitality, the eight crystals that had barely moved the needle. When he finished, the hall was quiet.

"Then you're planning to go to the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms," Kieran said. It wasn't a question.

"After I deal with the York warriors," Zane said. "Then yes."

“My plan is simple,” Zane announced, his voice cutting through the grim silence. “It’s true I can’t fight a Soul Sea Realm master. But since the York Divine Kingdom is sending theirs, we’ll just meet them with our own.”

[SFX: SHOCKED GASPS]

 “What?” The word echoed around the meeting hall.

Soul Sea Realm masters? The Heartlands didn’t have any warriors that powerful.

“Zane, are you kidding?” an elder from the Guild asked, shaking his head in disbelief.

“I’m not,” Zane said, his calm a stark contrast to their panic.

Confused glances were exchanged across the table. It made no sense. Hobbes finally voiced the question on everyone’s mind. “If you have Soul Sea Realm masters on your side, why didn’t they help you when you fought Maddox?”

The memory hung in the air: Zane, nearly killed. Tia, still lying in a coma.

[MUSIC: EPIC DRAMATIC STRINGS]

 A shadow crossed Zane’s face. “Because Maddox was mine to kill,” he stated, his voice ringing with absolute certainty. “To forge my warrior’s spirit, I had to be the one to end him. This is my trial—the first step to conquering the entire universe!”

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY PULSE]

 His words seemed to shake the very air in the room as an undeniable authority radiated from him. It was nothing like Maddox’s crude, overbearing power, which simply crushed anything in its path. This was true command, a presence that bent the world to its will.

The realization settled over them, silencing the room. He could have taken a safer route, but instead, he’d risked everything—his life, Tia’s life—to face his trial head-on. He chose the hardest path.

[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]

 After a long, heavy silence, Kieran leaned forward. “Alright,” he said, his voice steady. “So where are these Soul Sea Realm masters you’re talking about?”
