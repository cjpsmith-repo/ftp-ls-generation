Ep 1060 - Smell Of Gunpowder

Word count: 1206

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORCHESTRAL]

The real secret of Thundergust wasn’t the sword itself, but the shadow it cast. Zane had figured that much out the first time he’d wielded it, a mystery he’d been mulling over ever since. Every swing unleashed a blast of lightning and a blade of wind, but it also conjured a human-like figure that flickered alongside them.

Strangely, Zane felt a deep connection to it.

He’d tried to control the figure countless times, but with no luck. Eventually, Lavender had concluded there must be a special cultivation method for the sword, one that Zane would have a hell of a time finding on his own.

[SFX: SWORD RESONANCE]

So when the former leader of the Mist Palace mentioned it, Zane’s eyebrows shot up. “The mind method for the Thundergust?”

The old man nodded. “It seems you’ve already tried to use the sword. I’m guessing you can’t control the Nether God Shadow inside it.”

“Yeah, I’ve tried a bunch of times, but I just can’t get the hang of it,” Zane admitted.

“It’s not something you can master just by trying,” the old man said, producing a runestone.

[SFX: MAGICAL GLOW]

He passed it to his disciple, Layla.

Layla started to walk it over to Zane, but then a sly idea seemed to cross her mind. She gave the runestone a gentle toss.

[AMBIENT: TIME DISTORTION]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL STRINGS]

It floated into the air, drifting slowly toward him. She wasn’t using her Vitality or any crude force. Instead, she was meticulously controlling the flow of time within the space between them.

Time Law? Zane thought with a wry, internal smile.

It was a fundamental application, the kind of thing any warrior who understood the basics could pull off. But her control was impeccable. She had calculated the distance to him perfectly, containing the effect within that narrow corridor of space.

She was showing off. It was obvious.

Zane had the sword she’d wanted, and clearly, she wasn’t over it. She wanted to make a point—to show him the massive gap between their abilities.

The truth was, Layla didn’t really care about Thundergust. She was confident in her own destiny. As long as she stayed alive, she was fated to become a legend in this new era. As the world of chaos unfolded, she would rise as one of its greatest warriors.

And this Zane was a nobody.

Zane felt the wave of hostility from the woman in the green dress, and it struck him as more curious than intimidating. He certainly didn’t let it get to him.

As the runestone drifted toward him, it hit the halfway point, and its progress slowed to a crawl. It was still moving, inching through the air like a worm. If you weren’t looking closely, you might think it was frozen in midair. Zane just smiled, unfazed, and took a single step forward to pluck it from the air.

[MUSIC: SHARP SHIFT]

Layla’s eyes narrowed. She had expected some reaction — surprise, unease, anything. What she got was a smile.

“You’ll be participating in Passing the Torch,” she said. It wasn’t a question.

Zane glanced at her. “Passing the Torch? What’s that?”

The faintest look of contempt crossed her face. “You don’t even know what it is.” She let that land, then added, “As expected of an ascender.”

Yolande put a hand to her head. “I should have told you sooner. Forgive me.” She looked at Zane. “You need to participate in Passing the Torch. It isn’t optional.”

Zane shrugged. “Fine. But I’d like to know what I’m signing up for.”

“Think of it as the Thirteen Palaces’ version of the Tower of Sin,” she said simply.

That got his attention.

She didn’t elaborate. She didn’t need to. Zane had lived that tower — every floor, every test, every judgment it had made of him. If Passing the Torch operated on the same principle, he already understood what was at stake.

Yolande kept her expression neutral, but inwardly she was quietly amused. Layla was talented — she wouldn’t deny that. But the girl had spent her whole life being told how exceptional she was, and it showed. A little humbling would do her good. And if anyone was going to deliver it, Zane was the right person for the job.

[MUSIC: LOW TENSION]

Zane turned back to Layla. “Then I’ll participate. Why do you ask?”

She held his gaze. “I will leave you far behind.”

“And then?” he asked. The smile hadn’t left his face.

She had no answer for that. She glared at him, turned sharply, and went back to her master’s side.

Elder Dunn and Yolande exchanged a glance but said nothing. The world of warriors had always been like this — rivalry was just another way of sharpening the blade.

Zane looked down at the runestone in his hand, then gave Elder Dunn a respectful nod. “Thank you. Whatever else happened today, this is worth more than the sword itself.”

Elder Dunn smiled but didn’t reply.

[MUSIC: QUIET CONTEMPLATION]

After leaving Yolande’s hall, Zane took some time to learn what he could about Passing the Torch.

The Tower of Sin comparison was apt, but only up to a point. The Tower had been built to forge fighting will — every floor a battle, every battle a test of how far a warrior could be pushed. Passing the Torch leaned differently. It was built to measure potential and talent directly, and somewhere inside it, there was said to be a Human Inheritance — something old and significant that no warrior would want to miss.

Zane filed that away. He wasn’t going to turn down an opportunity like this.

The runestone contained the Nether God Mind Method. With this technique, a warrior could finally command the Nether God Shadow within the Thundergust.

The first time Zane had ever used the sword, the shadow had done little more than throw a simple punch. But after just a few days of training with the mind method, the figure’s movements had become incredibly complex.

[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]

[MUSIC: DYNAMIC ACTION]

In the vast training hall, Zane swung the sword, and the shadow figure shot forward. It took a confident step, spun, and weaved through the air before executing a perfect somersault and vanishing.

[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Zane swung the sword again. Another shadow erupted from the blade, a bright glow flaring from its fist. In just a few seconds, the shadow performed an entire set of fluid boxing maneuvers before fading away.

Zane slowly sheathed his sword, lost in thought.

One strike created one Nether God Shadow. A simple thought sparked in his mind. What about multiple strikes?

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

With that, Zane drew on a massive well of Chaos Energy from his core, pouring it into the sword. A murderous glint flashed in his eyes as he unleashed a blistering series of slashes with the Thundergust.

[SFX: MULTIPLE WHOOSHES]

He struck ten times in the space of a single breath.

For any skilled swordsman, ten thrusts in the blink of an eye was hardly a challenge.

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]

But as Zane’s final strike cut through the air, ten distinct Nether God Shadows materialized before him, each one flowing through a different set of complex moves.

If the sword’s original owner, the former leader of the Mist Palace, could have seen this, he would have been floored.

He had given Zane the key, never imagining the boy would so quickly build a whole new door.
