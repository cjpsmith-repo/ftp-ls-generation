[AMBIENT: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
 "How did that even happen?" a Soul Realm expert demanded.
Wilson’s face twisted in a mask of rage. "I don't know! There's something off about that guy. He stole my Vitality manifestations and used them to juice up one of the wolves, made it insanely fast! I'm going back there to finish him."
The woman shook her head, her long hair whipping around. "No. If you go now, you'll draw the attention of those two golems, and all of this will have been for nothing. We can't have that."
"So we're just supposed to let him tag along?" one of the men shot back, glancing angrily over his shoulder.
"We'll have plenty of time to deal with him once we're through the inner gates. That shouldn't be too much of a challenge for us, now should it?" the long-haired woman sneered.
The kid had taken out Yves and Leon like it was nothing. Sure, he had a few unusual tricks up his sleeve, but so what? He was a nobody compared to true masters of the Soul Realm.
"Good. It's settled then," the woman ordered. "Hawthorne, you and Seymour will take care of him the second we're through the inner gate."
[SFX: MAGICAL RUNES MATERIALIZING]
 With an agreement reached, they went back to ignoring Zane. The woman held out a hand, and dozens of runes materialized in midair. 
[SFX: POWERFUL WIND GUST]
 A powerful gust of wind washed over the group as the runes shot toward each person's head.
The runes boosted their speed, making everyone at least thirty percent faster.
[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT]
 The square stretched on for miles, but with their new speed, the group crossed to the inner gate in what felt like a single heartbeat.
Zane had used a Phoenix Crystal to strengthen the black wolf, leaving it to prowl the massive square on the other side of the doorway. Even though the wolf wasn't right beside him, it was still under his command. With its lightning speed, luring the golems over would be a piece of cake.
Since Zane could summon the two golems with a single word and guarantee no one would survive, the group had to think twice before making a move against him.
[SFX: ANGRY SHOUT]
 "You're asking for it!" Wilson shouted, his temper finally snapping at Zane's quiet confidence.
"Such a clever boy," the long-haired woman commented, giving Zane a venomous look. Murder chilled her beautiful eyes. "I have to admit, that's actually smart. Normally, someone who hasn't even reached the Adept Stage has no place here. But you have a point. If we don't work together, we're just asking for more trouble inside the Elysian Keep."
A wide smile spread across Zane's face, flashing a row of bright white teeth. "Exactly. Fighting each other isn't going to get us anywhere."
Zane's words left the group speechless, from the Adept Stage practitioners to the Soul Realm experts. They couldn't believe the nerve of this kid, talking to them like they were equals. Even faced with this much danger, Zane stayed calm, analyzing the situation and planning his countermoves.
His composure was as admirable as it was terrifying.
Although the members of the Seven Great Houses were furious with this turn of events, they reluctantly shelved their plan. They knew that if they tried to kill Zane now, they wouldn't make it out of this alive.
That didn't mean they accepted him as one of them. They could barely stand having him around, but exploring this place came first. They'd deal with him later.
The area between the outer and inner gates was just a massive, empty square they'd already cleared.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]
 But what lay behind the inner gate was a whole different world.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL REALM]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON CRYSTAL]
 After passing through the doorway, they found themselves on a crystal-paved causeway that stretched out across a vast pond.
To an immortal of the Elysian Keep, it might have been a simple pond. But to Zane, it was as vast as a lake.
[SFX: EXCITED GASPS]
 "The Alchemist's Laboratory!"
"And the Armory!"
"Look! The Archives!"
 Shouts of excitement erupted from the group, their eyes wide with greed as they took in the sights.
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL WONDER]
 In the middle of the causeway stood a central spire that seemed to stretch as high as a mountain. Branching off from it were seven different paths, each leading to one of the workshops.
Pills, weapons… just imagine the treasures waiting inside.
[SFX: FOOTSTEP ON WATER]
 One of the men, overcome with excitement, stepped right off the causeway, trying to run across the water's surface to get to the workshops faster. But the second his foot touched it, the placid water began to boil.
[SFX: WATER BOILING]
[AMBIENT: DANGEROUS WATERS]
 Dozens of massive, red Abyssal Pikes with sharp, golden scales erupted from the water. They were a blur of motion, their maws gaping as they shot toward the man.
He may have been a Soul Realm master, but the ambush caught him completely off guard. He just froze, his mind blank, with no idea what to do.
"Fly, you idiot!" one of the others screamed.
Reaching the Soul Realm granted the ability to fly by burning one's Vitality.
The master should have been able to escape the Abyssal Pikes, but like most of the pampered men from the Great Houses, he'd never been in a real life-or-death fight. His mind went blank with panic.
[SFX: WHOOSH OF FLIGHT]
 The shout finally snapped him out of it. Burning his Vitality, he shot straight up into the air.
But it was too late.
[SFX: IMPACT AND CRASH]
 Dozens of Abyssal Pikes slammed into him in midair, dragging him back down out of the sky. He crashed hard onto the causeway, the writhing fish piling on top of him and blocking most of the path.
[SFX: FEEDING FRENZY]
[MUSIC: HORROR STRINGS]
 The man's screams were swallowed by the frenzy as the fish swarmed him, only to dive back into the pond moments later.
When it was over, nothing was left on the causeway but a wet, red smear. The man was gone.
