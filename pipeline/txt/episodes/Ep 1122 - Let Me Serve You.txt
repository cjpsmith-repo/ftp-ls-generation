Ep 1122 - Let Me Serve You

Word count: 1130

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Everything Freya knew about the supreme worlds was a patchwork of rumors and tall tales, stories of a place so immense you couldn’t even begin to wrap your head around its scale. A normal warrior from the Soul Sea Realm would have to spend their entire life savings just to use a teleportation array connecting two of them. Hell, even just getting across their own Soulbound Sect was a journey in itself.
And yet here was Zane, a warrior who had traveled more than a thousand worlds away—and actually come back. The thought alone was staggering.
Freya had no idea what Master Sloan meant by the “Thirteen Palaces,” but she was slowly piecing together a picture of Zane’s history. He was no ordinary warrior. So why had he shown up in an Ascending Well? And why had he been working in the mines right alongside her? The contradictions tangled in her mind, a knot she couldn’t begin to loosen.
Zane spoke of his travels openly, but Sloan, to his credit, didn’t press him on the real reason he’d gone.
After a while, Sloan changed the subject. He turned to Freya, his gaze kind but direct. 
[SFX: THOUGHTFUL PAUSE]
“So, you’re Julius’s niece, and you also ascended here to our Soulbound Sect. Have you given any thought to what you’ll do now?”
The man was sharp. He could see that Freya and Zane were friendly, but he’d also figured out from their conversation that Zane was just passing through. He had saved her by chance, and it was pretty clear he wouldn’t be taking her with him to the Dark World. 
Sloan’s question wasn’t just a question; it was an opening, a quiet offer of a place for her in the Soulbound Sect now that she was on her own.
A slight smile touched Zane’s lips. Master Sloan was a wise man, alright. Zane stayed quiet, content to let the older man wait for Freya’s answer. He and Freya were acquaintances, nothing more; they hadn’t known each other long enough to even be called friends.
The question hit Freya like a physical blow. “I… I don’t know,” she stammered.
Her mind went completely blank. She’d come here looking for her uncle, but he’d turned out to be a bitter disappointment, offering no help and even trying to trade her away like property. Now he was dead, and she was just… adrift. She had no plan, no clear answer to give.
[MUSIC: WARM CONTEMPLATIVE]
Sloan’s next words came as a total shock. “If you’re looking for a place here,” he said, his smile gentle, “I can help you join the Myriad Spirit House of our sect. It would give you a chance to become a city lord one day. I can even hold your uncle’s former position for you. What do you think?”
The Myriad Spirit House? A city lord? Freya’s thoughts spun into a chaotic mess.
Then her eyelashes stilled. Something settled behind her eyes.
She stood up. Walked to Zane. And before either man could react, she dropped to one knee in front of him.
[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]
“You saved my life,” she said, her voice steady despite everything. “I have nothing to offer you in return. But if you’ll let me, I’d rather stay by your side than take any of this. Even as a servant. I won’t complain.”
Her expression left no room for misreading. She meant every word.
Sloan said nothing. He kept his face neutral, but privately he thought: smart girl. She’d read the room perfectly. Everything he’d just offered her existed because of Zane. If she stayed close to him, everything she could gain from the Soulbound Sect would look small by comparison.
But his read was only half right. 
Freya wasn’t calculating. She just knew — whatever was ahead of her, it wasn’t a city lord’s posting in a mid-tier sect. She hadn’t ascended for that. She’d told herself when she left the Lower World that she wasn’t going up there to be comfortable. If comfort was the goal, she should have stayed home. Here, she’d be a high and mighty existence among ordinary people. Up here, she was nothing — and she was willing to stay nothing, as long as she was moving forward.
Zane looked at her for a moment. Then he reached down and helped her to her feet.
“No servant talk,” he said. “But if you’re going to follow me, you need to be ready for real danger.”
[MUSIC: QUIET RESOLVE]
She nodded without hesitation.
Zane accepted that. He’d figure out the practicalities — if things got truly dangerous in the Dark World, there were ways to keep her out of harm’s way. It didn’t need to be said aloud.
[AMBIENT: QUIET TRAINING HALL]
Zane was still focused on his training, never taking a single break. Beyond the physical practice, his mind kept returning to the Gods-Intimidating Strike he had just learned. He still couldn’t grasp the deepest part of the technique and was beginning to realize he might never be able to execute that perfect sword strike unless he could somehow shed all his emotions.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE ETHEREAL]
It was a tall order, but Supreme Lord Sword had done it perfectly.
He wasn’t getting ahead of himself, comparing his own skills to a Supreme Lord’s. It was just that he was starting to understand the secret behind the technique. Legends from the Upper World claimed that a person could only become a True God after abandoning all emotion. The very few Supreme Lords who managed to transcend the heavens were the ones who could successfully sever every last tie to their mortal side, achieving the nature of a True God. Only then were they qualified to ascend.
Letting go of all emotion was a lifelong struggle, even for those powerhouses. Zane thought of the Supreme Lords he’d heard cursing each other out with Roaring Tokens, their tempers flaring instantly. That kind of anger was one of the hardest emotions to control. Even with their unbelievable strength, it was a constant battle.
Supreme Lord Sword had not yet become a True God himself, but his signature strike was powerful enough to take one on—legend even said he had injured a True God once. There could be only one explanation. 
He must have found a way to use the Gods-Intimidating Strike in a state of pure emotionlessness, even if he couldn’t get rid of his emotions entirely. If he could, he would have become a True God long ago.
So how did he do it? Zane turned the question over and over in his mind. 
There had to be a workaround, some method Supreme Lord Sword had found to reach that state, even if only for a moment.
