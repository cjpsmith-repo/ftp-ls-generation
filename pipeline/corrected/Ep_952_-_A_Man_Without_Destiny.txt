Ep 952 - A Man Without Destiny
Word count: 1127
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Vorn was about to make his pitch to Kade.
It was a tradition among the Demi-Immortals: any warrior who earned a title from the Talent Tablet got an invitation to join their ranks, no matter their race.
Now that Kade had earned the Silver Berserker rank—a rank even higher than Vorn’s own—he was damn sure going to get an offer.
Amber would have gotten one too, but she’d only reached the second row of the tablet. That was a huge deal on the main continent, but here, in the Demi-Immortal Race, it was barely a passing grade. The second row was the absolute minimum they’d even look at.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
But before Vorn could get a word out, the human girl spoke up, her eyes telling him she’d already figured out who they were. The fact that she could tell just by looking at them spoke volumes about her power. Instantly, Vorn, Delroy, and the others were on guard.
Zane’s gaze swept over the group before landing on Yolande. A faint smile touched his lips.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]
Yolande nodded back, returning it. Then she went on, “Kade belongs to the Violet Heart Sanctuary. He already has a place waiting for him with the humans—a high one, at that—once he ascends to the upper world. I suggest the Demi-Immortal Race stays out of it.”
Hearing this, Delroy and his clansmen scowled. That was not the answer they were looking for.
They’d just confirmed Zane’s talent and stumbled on another powerhouse in Kade. There was no way they were letting either of them slip through their fingers. They were dead set on recruiting both of them.
[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL TENSION]
Vorn’s voice turned cold. “And who are you? What human faction do you speak for?”
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
“That doesn’t matter,” Yolande said, shaking her head. Her voice stayed eerily calm. “I’m just making it clear: you are not taking Kade with you.”
“So what?” Delroy snorted. “The ascending passage from this world of chaos collapsed ages ago. If we don’t take him to the upper world, he’ll be stuck here for the rest of his life.”
“You don’t need to worry about that,” she said, shaking her head again. “Fixing the ascending passage won’t be a problem.”
[MUSIC: THREATENING DRUMS]
Vorn took two steps forward, his eyes raking over Yolande. “You’ve barely even reached the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. How exactly do you plan on stopping us?”
“And you’re just a bunch of guys at the Spirit Supreme Realm,” she shot back. “Why wouldn’t I be able to stop you?”
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PAUSE]
At her words, the seven Demi-Immortals just stared at each other, stunned into silence. How the hell did this mysterious girl know their cultivation levels?
A rare smile crossed Yolande’s face. “Don’t worry. Kade’s fate means he was never meant to join the Demi-Immortal Race.” She paused, letting that land. “As for Zane — that’s his own choice to make. I won’t interfere with it.”
The Demi-Immortals exchanged glances. They could let Kade go — a Silver Berserker was remarkable, but there was still a gulf between that and a Gold title. What confused them was Zane. He was human. Why would this woman be willing to release him to them?
“What does that mean?” Vorn said slowly.
Yolande’s expression didn’t change. “Zane’s fate appears to be the Battle of the Great Era. But in reality — he’s a fateless one.”
“A fateless one?”
[SFX: SHARP SILENCE]
The words hit the room strangely. Dorothea and the young man in black looked at each other, baffled.
But Delroy went very still. Something had shifted behind his eyes — not confusion, but recognition. He opened his mouth, closed it, then shook his head slowly, as if trying to shake the thought loose.
[MUSIC: UNEASY UNDERCURRENT]
“Shall we continue this somewhere less public?” Yolande said mildly, glancing at the crowded tenth floor around them.
[AMBIENT: LOW CROWD MURMUR]
She was already moving toward the living area. The others followed — but behind them, the tenth floor had come alive with whispered argument.
“Did she say Demi-Immortal Clan? As in — the ones who built this tower?”
“If they’ve come to reclaim it—”
“They built it. It’s theirs. What could we even do?”
The anxiety rippled through the crowd.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]
The young man in black had been turning the word over in his mind since they sat down. He finally looked at Delroy. “What’s a fateless one?”
Delroy frowned, collecting his thoughts. “When I was in the clan’s Great Realm Pavilion, I read a book called Destiny. It said that everything in the universe is predestined. Our fate is fixed — no deviation, no exception.”
His eyes drifted to a wooden tray sitting on the stone table beside him. He picked it up.
“If I let this go, what happens?”
“Well, obviously, the tray falls on the ground,” Dorothea replied, giving him a weird look. It was such a strange question.
Delroy nodded and let go.
[SFX: WOODEN TRAY CLATTERING]
The wooden tray clattered to the floor. “See? You knew it would fall.”
“Come on,” Dorothea chuckled. “Anyone could’ve told you that.”
Delroy pressed on. “You knew I’d let it go. You knew its height, and you could guess how fast it would fall and where it would land. That’s how you predicted what would happen.”
“Yeah, that’s just common sense,” Dorothea agreed with a nod, though she couldn’t shake the feeling he was leading them somewhere… strange.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
“But what if,” Delroy said, his voice ringing with intensity, “what if someone could predict everything that happens across the entire three-point-six-billion-year history of this era, simply because they witnessed its birth?”
“That’s impossible!” the young man in black finally burst out, glaring at Delroy. “Can you predict what I’m gonna do next? No! And an era is three-point-six billion years. There are countless beings in the universe. I know some of the powerhouses in our clan can predict the future, but they only get a tiny glimpse into the primordial chaos. You know that better than I do, Delroy! It’s impossible!”
As the young man got more and more worked up, Yolande, who had been swinging her feet idly, chimed in. “What if it’s not a person… but the heavens themselves?”
Delroy nodded. “The heavens have already laid out the path. The birth and death of every single creature have already been decided. Everything is predestined—even me standing here, saying these exact words.”
“The heavens…” the young man in black trailed off, looking completely baffled.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
Just then, Yolande turned to Zane. “Now do you get it?” she asked. “What it means to be a Man Without Destiny?”
