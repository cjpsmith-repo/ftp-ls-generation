Ep 995 - Four’s a Crowd
Word count: 1072
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ORIENTAL THEME]
For most warriors, the path of cultivation led to a familiar destination: unlimited power, fame, or the attention of beautiful women.
But Zane walked a different road. He wasn’t driven by money or glory—he was obsessed with cultivation itself, fueled by a pure passion for the art.
He’d already forged his own path once by creating the Formless Sword. Now, he was attempting an attunement method no one had ever dared to try, blazing a trail into the unknown. He knew the risks were high, but he had a gut feeling it would lead to something incredible.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS]
The massive fireball that erupted from his training grounds sent a shockwave through Cloud Hall, but the alarm quickly faded when the warriors learned it was just Zane.
No one dared to complain. He wasn’t just any disciple—he was a member of the Elite House, the number one warrior in the Heartlands, and the husband of Cloud Hall’s own leader. Thinking of him as a mere disciple was impossible now.
[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL HALL]
[MUSIC: SUBTLE TENSION]
With his mission to the Eastern Territories complete and Isla safely back by his side, Zane could finally settle in at Cloud Hall and focus on his training. Or, he could try. Being in the company of three beautiful women wasn’t exactly the peaceful retreat he’d imagined. A quiet tension simmered beneath the surface; none of them were thrilled with the idea of sharing a husband, but for now, the arrangement held.
Cora was the picture of carefree, spending her days teasing the Ancestral Dragon or just lounging by Zane’s side. With all the resources Cloud Hall offered and the natural advantage of her Violet-Core Physique, she should have broken through to the next realm by now. But she didn’t seem to take her training seriously at all, content to remain at the early stage of the Inner-Core Realm. At least, that’s how it looked to Zane.
Tia had her complaints about it. Cora wasn’t a Cloud Hall disciple, which meant Tia had no authority over how she spent her time — but that didn’t make watching her monopolize Zane’s days any easier to swallow.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
But he had his doubts about her. He couldn’t shake the memory of the old man from the Demi-Immortal Race. Zane had tried to tap into the World’s Will, hoping to see what the old man and Cora had discussed, but he came up empty. What he did find was this: Cora had vanished from Cloud Hall that same night and entered the Violet Core Realm. What happened inside was a blank — the World’s Will didn’t reach there. He could only assume the old man had used some extraordinary power to erase those memories entirely.
Since it involved her privacy, Zane couldn’t ask her directly. Besides, Cora had clearly prepared herself — she had no intention of telling him anything. He had no good options.
What surprised him most was Isla.
She had thrown herself into cultivation with a focus that put everyone else to shame. From the moment she arrived at Cloud Hall, she barely left the training rooms. During the three months Zane was lost in the pain of the Primordial Chaos, Isla had jumped from the fourth level of the Soul Realm to the eighth — nearly catching up to Cora, who had been cultivating for years longer. She was close to the pinnacle of the Soul Realm now and showed no signs of slowing down.
The green pill was doing its work too. Ever since Zane had swallowed it at the wedding, his cultivation speed had climbed to a level that felt almost unreasonable. He could feel it in every session — the difference between before and after was stark.
[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC]
Not long after returning to Cloud Hall, the second Life-and-Death Tribulation arrived. It came with real ferocity, but Zane was ready — he channelled the tribulation’s force into the Sacred Mount Lodestone, letting the stone absorb what would have torn another warrior apart. The tribulation passed without costing him much.
Half a year later, the third Life-and-Death Tribulation came. He crossed it the same way.
[MUSIC: RISING ORCHESTRAL]
The Heartlands were changing too. A new generation of warriors was coming into its own. Finn was now the youngest elder in Cloud Hall’s history — still in his early twenties, his Heaven Sword growing more fearsome with every passing month. Tia had already spoken to Zane about it: she knew they were both preparing to ascend, and Cloud Hall would need a successor. Finn was the obvious candidate in terms of character, talent, and temperament. Whether he could hold off the other rising geniuses for the position was another question.
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING TRAINING GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: EPIC BUILDING]
It wasn’t just Cloud Hall. The Bloodwood Cliff, Mysterious Luna Hall, the Black Mountain Order, the rebuilt Ethereal Spirit Sect — all of them were pouring resources into their young warriors. The Heartlands that Zane had grown up in bore little resemblance to what it was becoming.
Zane had never used the token before and had no idea how it worked. [AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]
The moment he activated it, though, a torrent of information flooded his mind, instantly laying its secrets bare.
The rush of insight conjured a shimmering image of the Elysian Keep right in front of him—a perfect, miniature replica of the one in the Cloudy Mountains.
Zane stepped into it without hesitation and was instantly transported. [AMBIENT: VAST ETHEREAL HALL]
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
Though he’d entered a miniature, the space that opened around him was impossibly vast and grand, an intricate illusion woven by the token.
“So that’s how it works,” he thought. A perfect escape route. If he ever found himself in real trouble, he could just pull out the token and hide inside this illusory palace.
Since he was already here, he decided to head for The Archives, a place promising a limitless well of knowledge.
Instead of hunting for attunement handbooks, Zane focused his search on Weaponsmithing and divine texture. The Eternal Scribe whispered recommendations in his ear the entire time, but Zane ignored it, browsing a few tomes of his own choosing. In one of them, he stumbled upon an unexpected entry: the Sacred Mount Lodestone.
According to the text, the Lodestone could, in fact, be collected.
It contained the raw power of all five elements—metal, wood, water, fire, and Earth—and was said to be virtually indestructible.
That was precisely why no one in the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms had ever bothered trying to claim it.
