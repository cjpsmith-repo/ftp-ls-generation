Ep 52 - Titan Strike
Word Count: 1168
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
The book in Zane's hands was titled "Titan Strike", and its pages were dedicated entirely to offensive techniques.
He carefully turned to the first page, his eyes narrowing at the tiny, cramped script that filled it. It took him a moment, but then it hit him: this was a translation. From Goliath.
The knowledge in this book came from the ogres, and somehow, someone had managed to decipher their language. Zane couldn't even begin to guess who.
"Wait, is this a method for ogres?" Zane wondered.
He knew their cultivation methods, like the legendary Titan Body enhancement formula, were brutally demanding. They required a level of physical strength most humans could only dream of.
Even after achieving Physical Enhancement, a human was still worlds weaker than an ogre—their bodies were just built on a different scale. A human at the Bone Refining Level, for example, was only about as strong as a four or five-year-old ogre child.
Of course, that was for an average person. Zane wasn't average.
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
His body had been refined by Evil Jace, bringing his strength to a level comparable to a Legendary Weapon.
The impossible physical barrier that would stop any other human was, for him, an open door. This method wasn't just possible for him—it was perfect.
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
Zane slipped the Titan Strike into his Storage Ring and stepped out of the private room.
[AMBIENT: QUIET LIBRARY]
[MUSIC: TENSE ENCOUNTER]
Back in the main Level 4 Archives, he found an old man in a white robe waiting for him, his eyes sharp and piercing.
The man stood with his hands clasped behind his back, carrying an air of quiet grace and nobility. A wave of relief washed over Zane; he sensed no hostility from him.
"The power of the Titan Strike is immense," the old man said, his voice calm. "It must be fate that you found it. But that technique will draw unwanted attention. If anyone asks what you chose..."
He paused, waving a hand toward the stacks. A book shot off a distant shelf and flew neatly into his grasp. He held it out to Zane. "Tell them it was this one."
Zane took the book, his eyes widening as he read the title on the cover: Psyche Spike. He couldn't believe his luck. Out of all the books in the Level 4 Archives, he was walking away with two.
A wide grin spread across Zane's face as he slipped the second book into his Storage Ring. He gave the old man a deep, respectful bow and turned to leave.
"Wait," the old man called out just as Zane was about to take a step. "Aren't you even a little curious about who I am?"
"What?" Zane froze, turning back to the old man. In all the excitement, the thought hadn't even crossed his mind. He winced, mentally kicking himself. "Oh, right. My apologies. You are...?"
The old man waved a dismissive hand, a chuckle in his voice. "No need for all that. You laid the politeness on a little thick with that bow. It's fine." He smiled. "The Skyreach Sect is vast, but something tells me we'll be seeing each other again. Assuming you're as special as I think you are."
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
Relieved he hadn't offended the man, Zane offered a smile, then turned and finally left the archives. The old man watched him go, a long, slow sigh escaping his lips. "After all these years," he whispered to the empty hall, "the book has finally chosen its owner. If that isn't fate, I don't know what is."
Zane was long gone by then, so he didn't hear the old man's words. Even if he had, their meaning would have been lost on him.
[AMBIENT: EMPTY LIBRARY HALLS]
Descending through the archives, Zane found Level 3 completely empty. Level 2 was deserted as well. It figured. Lingering in the Celestial Library wasn't smart; the longer you stayed, the harder it was to fight off the crushing pressure of the vengeful beast's will. The others must have grabbed their techniques and gotten out as fast as they could.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOORS]
Zane was the last one out of the Celestial Library. When he stepped into the daylight, the groups from the other thirty-two peaks were already gone. Only the pledges from Misty Peak remained, waiting for him.
"Sorry to keep you guys waiting," Zane said, bowing his head slightly.
"Hey, don't worry about it," one of them said, clapping him on the shoulder. "We're all Misty Peak brothers now. Gotta look out for each other. Welcome aboard."
This group of pledges was made up entirely of members from the Minor Houses. With no one from the Great Houses around, the vibe was surprisingly relaxed and easy-going.
Master Isla Novak, who'd been standing a short distance away, glanced over as he joined them. A curious smile touched her lips. "So, did you make it to the Level 4 Archives?"
Zane nodded, and started to tell her what he'd chosen. She cut him off.
"Don't." Her tone was easy but firm. "Whatever you found in there, keep it to yourself. In the Skyreach Sect, you learn to guard your secrets. That's the first real lesson."
Getting into the archives felt like a victory, a sign of his power. But he knew true power was a different game.
The strongest person was always the boss—that was a universal rule. If he were truly powerful, he wouldn't need to be here, scrambling for techniques in the Skyreach Sect. If he were truly powerful, he could rescue Elara from Infernal Peak with a single word. But he wasn't. He was weak.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL STRINGS]
The thought of his sister, the person he cared for most in the world, brought a flicker of sorrow to his eyes. He quickly pushed it down, his expression hardening with resolve as he turned to the friendly pledge from before. "Rhys Bolton, I need to ask you something."
Rhys kept his warm smile. "Sure thing," he nodded. "What's up?"
[MUSIC: TENSE UNDERTONE]
"Where, exactly, is Infernal Peak?" Zane asked, his voice low and careful.
Rhys's eyebrows shot toward his hairline. "Infernal Peak?" he repeated, his friendly tone laced with surprise. "Why on earth are you asking about a place like that?"
"No special reason," Zane replied, trying to sound casual. "Just curious."
[AMBIENT: DISTANT WHISPERS]
"Well, it's a lone mountain way out in the northwest corner of the sect," Rhys explained, still looking at him funny. "It's where they lock up anyone who breaks a major rule or screws up really bad. I've never been anywhere near it myself, but the stories... they say the conditions are hellish. Supposedly, thousands of disciples are imprisoned there."
Rhys finished, his eyes searching Zane's face. "You don't have a friend locked up there, do you?"
Zane ignored the question. His gaze was fixed and intense. "Is there a way to get inside?"
