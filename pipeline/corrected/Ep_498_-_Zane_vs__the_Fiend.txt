The old legends say the River of the Netherworld is the path to Hell. The souls of the worst criminals, those who’d committed unspeakable crimes, were said to be engulfed in hellfire and cast into that infamous stream to drown on their way to the inferno.

It was an ancient story, passed down through generations. But here in The Heartlands, such tales felt like folklore from another world. Hell, sin, the netherworld, reincarnation—the origins of these words were a mystery, and only fragments of their meaning had survived as myths.

Some believed "sin" was just another name for the weight of a person's Malice. While everyone was born with this energy, anyone who chose the path of a killer was guaranteed a journey of torture in the afterlife. Sin was the tool that tempted and twisted a soul until it broke.

It became an anchor, dragging a person’s conscience down into the River of the Netherworld. Sin kept the soul trapped there, drifting aimlessly in the murky depths until it was found by a Netherworld Fiend—a ghostly monster born from the abyss of Hell itself.

[SFX: SWORD RESONANCE]

"So the Nameless Saber can summon a Netherworld Fiend," Zane thought, his eyes narrowing as caution warred with curiosity. A chill ran down his spine at the implications. "Definitely a powerful Relic-Class weapon."

His gaze dropped to the iron sword at his side. The plain, unadorned blade seemed almost pitiful in comparison. How was he supposed to draw out its full potential when he didn't even know what that potential was?

Mythic weapons and other high-level blades typically possessed special skills—abilities that could turn the tide of battle in an instant. Some could slice through any defense as if it were paper. Others held unique properties that defied conventional understanding.

Zane's iron sword, like his protective gear, came from Dragonfall Palace. Since it was merely standard-issue equipment, he had no idea what capabilities it might possess. Perhaps it was so common that the palace staff hadn't bothered to explain its properties—just another blade in an endless armory. Or perhaps it contained the raw power of a Relic-Class weapon but lacked the special features that made such weapons legendary.

A familiar weight settled in Zane's chest—the same feeling he'd experienced when first seeing the map of the Heartlands. He tried not to get his hopes up. Everyone at Dragonfall Palace received the same loadout, which meant the odds heavily favored it being just a basic blade. Nothing special. Nothing that would help him bridge the vast gulf between his current strength and what he needed to rescue Elara.

Still, a small part of him wondered. Had to wonder. What if there was something more to this sword, some hidden quality waiting to be discovered?

Eden’s taunting voice snapped Zane out of his thoughts."So what if you've mastered three types of Law Power? If you'd reached the Inner-Core Realm, maybe you'd be a challenge. But you'll never get the chance to grow stronger, because you're dying right here, by my hand!"

[SFX: BEAST ROAR]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

Eden gave him no time to react, surging forward in a blur of motion. At the same time, the Netherworld Fiend he'd summoned with his blood roared to life, its twisted form scrambling across the ground before launching itself ferociously at Zane.

A smile tugged at Zane's lips despite the danger. "Two against one? I have an assistant, too!"

The casual response ignited something dark in Eden's eyes. Fury radiated off him in waves as he and his creature unleashed a flurry of coordinated attacks. The Fiend's claws slashed from the left while Eden's saber cut from the right—a pincer movement designed to leave no escape.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

In response, a brilliant red light erupted from Zane as he summoned his sword spirit, letting the glow envelop him. "And you think that's enough to beat me?" Zane asked, his expression unreadable.

Lily materialized in a flash of light, her blade already singing through the air toward the Fiend's exposed flank.

"If that's all you've got, you're dead," Eden fumed, leveling the tip of his Nameless Saber at him. "What the hell was that?"

"I don't even need half my strength to deal with you." Zane chuckled and sent a silent command to Lily.

[SFX: SWORD SHEATH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]

She immediately broke off her fight with the Netherworld Fiend, sheathed her massive sword, and shot toward Eden.

At the exact same time, Zane lunged at the fiend, and just like that, they’d swapped opponents.

Eden was confused but had no time to think. His new opponent was a warrior of incredible skill.

[SFX: BLADE CLASH]

She moved like a dancer, swinging the greatsword in her hands with deadly precision. Eden had to give it his all just to keep up.

Her technique was so refined, so flawless, that it seemed to neutralize his special abilities. The best he could manage was a shallow scratch on her enormous blade.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]

Meanwhile, Zane confronted the beast head-on, their battle erupting into a brutal clash for dominance.

The Netherworld Fiend loomed before him—a terrifying sight that easily towered over two men. Its grotesque form radiated malevolence, each movement promising death. Yet Zane felt no fear, only the cold clarity that came with battle.

He channeled his Dragon Scales, feeling them shimmer beneath his skin as he met the creature's savage strikes. Every blow sent shockwaves through his bones, but he held his ground, refusing to yield even an inch.

The fiend's claws raked toward his throat. Zane twisted, letting the attack whistle past his ear, and drove his fist into the creature's exposed ribs. The impact resonated through the arena.

[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]

With one final, vicious strike, Zane seized the fiend and hurled it against the enchanted barrier. The creature's massive body collided with the supposedly impenetrable wall.

[SFX: SHATTERING GLASS]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]

The supposedly unbreakable barrier shattered into a million pieces.

A welcome warmth flooded him as Primordial Energy surged through his body. In the past, Zane hadn't grasped the true value of this energy. Only now was he starting to understand how powerful it really was.

"That's it," Zane beamed. The beast's persistence had given him the perfect chance to test his limits. He had just channeled the power of five hundred Dragon Scales from the Cobalt Dragon, and the force was unbelievable.

In his excitement, he’d almost forgotten about the massive crowd of warriors whose eyes were locked on him.
