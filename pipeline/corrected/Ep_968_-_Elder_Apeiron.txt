[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TEMPLE]
[MUSIC: DARK MYSTERIOUS]
Cora stood rooted to the spot, the Ancestral Dragon clutched tight against her chest, her mind spinning as the two women faced off around her like she was a prize at auction.
On one side, the Siren’s Kiss Sect; on the other, the Violet Ascendancy Sect — and neither of them was willing to let her walk away.
Sure, the promise of pleasure was a hell of a lure, but it wasn’t enough to tempt men into joining the Siren’s Kiss Sect—a place few dared to enter, because no amount of pleasure was worth dying for.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
The women of the Siren’s Kiss Sect didn’t need to use force. Their seduction was a masterclass, weaving a web so intoxicating that no man ever wanted to leave. Once caught, men would lose themselves in a haze of ecstasy until their bodies withered and their Vitality was completely drained. They risked shattering their core power, and in the worst-case scenario, it meant death.
Cora would never join a place like that. Sure, she wanted a man, but that man was Zane, and only Zane.
On the other hand, the Violet Ascendancy Sect forbade love, which was just as much of a deal-breaker.
That wretched old man played me, she fumed.
She was stuck here. How could she possibly get away with these two watching her every move? For a second, she considered using the spatial anchor to jump back to the Heartlands, but what if that just dragged Zane into her mess?
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
As these questions raced through her mind, Leah and Krista started arguing.
They went back and forth, neither willing to give an inch. Cora had a First-Rank Violet-Core Physique; she was a prize neither of them was about to lose.
Just as their argument boiled over into a shouting match, a commanding, elderly voice sliced through the noise. “Will you two be silent!”
[AMBIENT: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
Both Leah and Krista froze, their fight forgotten as an old woman hobbled into view.
[AMBIENT: STONE HALL]
[SFX: CANE TAP]
She leaned heavily on a cane, her face etched with a stern expression.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]
A second ago, they were at each other’s throats; now, they stood ramrod straight and greeted her with deep respect. “Elder Apeiron!”
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHOIR]
The Violet Core Realm was known for three famous sects and one legendary tower. The first was the Violet Ascendancy Sect, the second was the Siren’s Kiss Sect, and the third was the Crescent Moon Sect. But the real power that loomed over them all was Apeiron Tower.
And the old woman before them was none other than its master: Elder Apeiron herself.
The old woman hobbled toward Cora, her cane tapping against the floor. “Good, very good! That old fool actually found a fine student for me!”
Elder Apeiron turned to Cora. “Show me your right hand, child.”
Cora blinked, bewildered, but extended her hand. There, visible in her palm, was a Location Talisman — the kind unique to the Demi-Immortal Race.
Krista’s eyes went wide. “Elder Apeiron, I found her first!” she whined.
Elder Apeiron turned a cold glance on Krista. “See? Do you need me to explain?”
Krista’s protest died in her throat. “No,” she said stiffly. “I didn’t know before.”
Leah said nothing — just smiled.
Elder Apeiron looked back at Cora with a kind smile. “So then, child — will you accept me as your master?”
Cora chewed her lip. “You won’t… make me stop thinking about a man, will you?”
The old woman blinked. For a long moment, she said nothing.
Leah burst out laughing. “Ha! She sounds like one of mine! Are you sure you don’t want to reconsider?”
“No!” Cora’s face went red. “That’s not what I meant! I just meant — never mind.”
Elder Apeiron recovered her composure, her smile warm. “The old man told me about you. I know you have little interest in martial arts for its own sake — that you cultivate for someone. I won’t place too many restrictions on you. But the path of martial arts ultimately depends on the heart.” She studied Cora carefully. “The question is whether you can persevere — for his sake.”
Cora didn’t hesitate. “I can.”
“Good.” Elder Apeiron nodded. “Then it’s settled.”
While Cora’s fate was being decided worlds away, back at Cloud Hall the night had already given way to morning.
[AMBIENT: INTIMATE ROOM]
[SFX: QUICK MOVEMENT]
Tia stirred first, reaching over to pinch Zane’s nose — only to find him very much awake.
Then, with lightning speed, he grabbed her hand.
Tia pouted. “You’re so annoying! Let go!”
[MUSIC: ROMANTIC STRINGS]
But Zane had no intention of letting her go. He just smiled and pulled her into a tight hug.
“Hmph. Last night, you...” she started to complain again.
Zane cut her off with a laugh. “Hey, let’s not talk about that. You nearly froze me solid!”
“You deserved it! You were being naughty,” she shot back playfully. “Do it again, and I really will freeze you to death!”
“I’m not afraid of a little frostbite,” he murmured, his voice low and suggestive. “If you don’t believe me, we could always try again.”
“No way! What do you take me for, an idiot?”
Their morning passed in a haze of soft touches and easy laughter. After a while, they finally got up to start their day.
Tia twisted her hair up into a bun, revealing her small, delicate earlobes. It was a Heartlands tradition for a new bride to wear her hair that way for three days after the wedding, a style that transformed her from her usual playful self into a poised, dignified young woman.
[AMBIENT: QUIET HALLWAY]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Zane, meanwhile, headed straight for the old man’s quarters. The mysterious old man still hadn’t left Cloud Hall, and for the life of him, Zane couldn’t figure out why he was sticking around.
Cloud Hall had given the old man a sitting room for his stay. When Zane arrived, he found him lounging and sipping on some mulled cider.
Zane gave a respectful bow as he entered. The old man chuckled, then his eyes flicked toward the entrance just as another figure flew in from outside.
[SFX: WHOOSH OF WIND]
It looked like Cora was back.
Zane thought it was strange. Cora looked completely exhausted, as if she’d just finished some long, grueling journey. Did she leave Cloud Hall yesterday? he wondered. Where could she have gone?
He remembered seeing the old man talking with Cora yesterday, and now here she was, rushing over first thing in the morning. He couldn’t help but wonder what the hell had happened between them.
