Ep 354 - The Crimson Sect

Word count: 1147

They always said the earlier someone mastered the Spatial Law, the better.

But truth be told, Zane had no idea what this mysterious power was really supposed to feel like.

[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]

The four companions shot through the shimmering teleportation channel, their bodies suspended between realities. Zane felt his consciousness stretching, perceiving dimensions he'd never noticed before. The brilliant light surrounding them flickered and then abruptly vanished, plunging them into momentary darkness. 

[SFX: SUDDEN HALT]

Just like that, with no fanfare or warning, they had arrived at their destination. The transition was so sudden that Zane felt a peculiar emptiness in his stomach, as if part of him had been left behind in the void.

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT RUINS]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS EXPLORATION]

The channel opened onto a platform on a small hill, overlooking stretches of ancient ruins that looked thousands of years old. Most of the buildings had crumbled away, making it hard to imagine that a grand palace had once stood on this spot.

Sir Kenneth gathered the three of them, and they flew off the platform toward the ruins.

[AMBIENT: FLYING]

Up ahead, Zane spotted some black dots in the sky. As they drew closer, he realized it was a group of people.

The man leading them radiated power—Zane guessed he was nearly on par with Sir Kenneth. The nine people behind him were all beginners in the Soul Realm, but they carried themselves differently from regular warriors. It was obvious they were the cream of the crop.

[SFX: DISTANT VOICES APPROACHING]

The group's leader greeted Sir Kenneth with a laugh. “Well, well, Kenneth. You’re here early this year.” 

Sir Kenneth's smile froze into an icy mask as he regarded the approaching man, his eyes betraying a history of rivalry and unspoken grievances. They were definitely not friends.

The leader, Eddy Winston, carried himself with unmistakable arrogance. He held the same prestigious rank as Sir Kenneth: an external steward for the Cloud Hall. 

Eddy looked over Sir Kenneth's group and let out a slow whistle. "Brother Kenneth — two Adepts? Really? Is the Skyreach Sect that thin on talent these days?"

The Crimson disciples laughed. One of them called out: "Did they just send those two here for the scenery?"

Sir Kenneth said nothing. Last time he'd opened his mouth, he'd promised great things from a disciple who slept through the entire trial. He wasn't making that mistake again.

"Enough." Sir Kenneth's voice was quiet, but a wave of pressure rolled off him and washed over the nine Crimson disciples. They went rigid.

Eddy raised a hand, catching the edge of it with a barrier of his own. He smiled. "Brother Kenneth, the young men are just talking. No harm meant."

The Cloud Hall stood as a formidable Tier Four sect, a towering institution that commanded authority over numerous smaller Tier One and Tier Two sects throughout the region. 

Like a vast tree with many branches, it absorbed the most talented disciples from these subordinate organizations each year, cultivating exceptional warriors under its prestigious banner. The Skyreach Sect represented merely one branch of this impressive hierarchy—a Tier Two sect that flourished under Sir Kenneth's leadership. Beyond serving as its head, Sir Kenneth also held the distinguished position as one of the Cloud Hall's trusted stewards, a testament to his remarkable abilities and political acumen.

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR GATHERING]

[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]

Eddy Winston, meanwhile, presided over the Crimson Sect, another Tier Two institution within the Cloud Hall's vast network. Despite sharing the same Tier Two classification as the Skyreach Sect, the disparity between them was as plain as day.

Eddy had arrived with nine disciples from the Crimson Sect, each one a beginning Soul Realm cultivator. Their crimson robes formed an impressive phalanx behind their master, exuding confidence born of superior resources and training.

Sir Kenneth, on the other hand, had brought only three disciples from Skyreach. This difference spoke volumes about the political hierarchy that existed even among supposedly equal sects.

Zane, Finn, and Patrick exchanged uncomfortable glances, the weight of this realization settling heavily upon their shoulders. Without their master saying a single word, they could sense the vast chasm separating the two organizations. The Crimson disciples' subtle smirks and dismissive glances only reinforced this painful truth, stirring a mixture of indignation and determination within the Skyreach trio.

Despite being the same Tier, the Crimson Sect hailed from the Heartlands—a region that was richer, more populated, and way bigger. It only made sense they'd have a deeper pool of top-tier talent than the Skyreach Sect from the Eastern Territories.

So, naturally, the Crimson Sect had more powerful disciples, and the Cloud Hall gave them more slots for the Cloud Road. You couldn't really blame the Cloud Hall; it just made sense to give more resources to the stronger teams. It was the same everywhere.

“The young men were just talking amongst themselves. Don't pay them any mind.”

This time, Eddy let the "young men" comment slide. But 

Finn and Patrick weren't about to stand by while their master was put in an awkward position.

Since Zane was the champion of the All Peaks Competition, they both looked to him as their leader. The two of them shot a look at Zane, waiting for him to take the lead.

But Zane was still reeling from the teleportation array, his mind stuck on the Spatial Laws. He was completely out of it, just staring blankly into the distance. Patrick and Finn had no idea what was going on inside his head.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TELEPORTATION AFTERMATH]

The young disciple was dazed. He'd gotten a glimpse of the Spatial Law, but inside the teleportation channel, nothing had felt different. Now that he was out, though… everything had changed. He could feel a massive shift in the space around him.

Every single thing seemed to have a razor-sharp, clear outline. It was like he was seeing the world in perfect 3D, somehow able to sense the exact height, length, and width of it all.

This had to be a side effect of the Spatial Law. At first, he’d thought it would be useless, but now it just felt like his brain was scrambled. He couldn't focus.

[SFX: URGENT VOICE CALLING]

“Zane! Zane!”

[SFX: HANDS CLAPPING ON SHOULDERS]

A pair of hands clapped down on his shoulders.

Zane snapped out of his daze. “What?” 

He realized it was Finn and Patrick trying to get his attention.

[SFX: MOCKING LAUGHTER]

But seeing how spaced-out Zane was, the Crimson Sect disciples burst out laughing.

“Hahaha! Look at that guy—the Level Three Adept. He looks like a total moron! You think something’s wrong with his head?” one of them snickered.

“Yeah, you’re probably right,” another one added, laughing too. “I have no idea how an idiot like that is gonna survive the Cloud Road Trial.”

The Crimson Sect disciples were clearly enjoying themselves. To them, Zane's bizarre behavior was hilarious, and it only made them more confident.

Zane couldn't wait to prove them all wrong.
