Ep 840 - The Lodestone

Word count: 1072

[SFX: ARROW VOLLEY]

[AMBIENT: BATTLE TENSION]

Behind Fredrick, a storm of arrows suddenly blotted out the sky, raining down on them. Each arrowhead gleamed with a faint blue light, and against the dark sky, they looked like a thousand shooting stars.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

It was a beautiful, deadly sight—everyone knew that blue gleam meant poison.

“What the hell?” Renzo’s voice trembled. “How are we supposed to get through that?”

Just a moment ago, they’d been facing a few hundred arrows lined up in a neat wall. Getting through that wouldn't have been too hard. But this was something else entirely.

[SFX: MASSIVE ARROW DEPLOYMENT]

Hundreds of thousands of arrows now stood between them and their goal. If the first volley was a wall, this was a fortress.

“Let's go around!” Randy shouted, his eyes wide with urgency.

“It's no use,” Fredrick said, shaking his head. “We already tried. We scouted the perimeter—this thing seems to stretch across the entire Slow Arrow Field.”

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS RETREATING]

As they debated, the group started backing away. As they retreated, more and more warriors began to gather, all falling back from the impossible barrier. Even Gregory, the Crown Prince of the Star Field Divine Kingdom, was among them.

“This is insane,” one warrior muttered. “I've never seen anything like this in the Slow Arrow Field before.”

“Did something go wrong this year?” another asked.

“Let's just get clear of this volley,” someone else suggested. “We can move forward once it's gone.”

[AMBIENT: ANXIOUS CROWD MURMUR]

The growing crowd buzzed with concern until Camilla spoke up. “What if we head into the sea and get off on the other side? Would that work?”

The moment she finished speaking, everyone stared at her like she was an idiot. She flushed, and Randy quickly broke the awkward silence. “I forgot to mention—this is the only path to the Feather Emperor's Secret Land. There's no other way through.”

And so, dozens of teams—hundreds of saint-level geniuses—were forced into a full retreat.

[SFX: MAGICAL ARROWS VANISHING]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

As soon as the last of them was clear of the Slow Arrow Field, the arrows began to vanish, one by one.

“Again!” Fredrick gritted his teeth and charged forward, his team right behind him.

These were supposed to be the most powerful young elites from across the divine kingdoms. They weren't about to let a minor setback stop them. Once again, everyone followed Fredrick's lead, stepping back into the Slow Arrow Field.

This time, not everyone made it through cleanly. Some warriors grew careless under the pressure — surrounded on all sides, arrows drifting in from every angle — and couldn't get clear in time.

[SFX: BLUE LIGHT FLASH]

[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]

The blue light spread, and they were gone.

The others kept moving.

But then, further in, the sky darkened again.

[SFX: DISTANT RUMBLE]

[SFX: MASSIVE ARROW WAVE]

Another massive wall of arrows — identical to the first — came rolling toward them from ahead.

[MUSIC: GRIM REALIZATION]

Fredrick said nothing. The other crown princes said nothing. The humiliation of it settled over everyone like a weight. They explored the Sacred Feather Sea every year. They were the finest warriors the seven divine kingdoms produced. And they couldn't get past the Slow Arrow Field.

If word got out, no one would believe it.

Randy pulled Zane aside. “Something's wrong. This has never happened before. Something must have happened inside the Secret Land.”

“Doesn't matter,” Zane said. “We have to get in regardless.”

Randy nodded. “Of course. We're not turning back.”

Randy frowned. He'd been to this island before and never seen anything like this. Whatever was happening inside the Secret Land, it was serious.

[AMBIENT: TENSE MURMURING]

Just as the crowd began to waver, a calm voice cut through.

“Everyone — follow behind me. I can get us through.”

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

The speaker was Callan, the Wind Crown Prince and Fiona's brother. He didn't have his sister's reputation. She had overshadowed him entirely, and most of the other crown princes barely acknowledged him. Fredrick, in particular, had never hidden his contempt.

“You?” Fredrick said with a cold smile. “What exactly do you have?”

“Something to block the arrows,” Callan said.

A ripple of skepticism moved through the crowd. 

Someone spoke up: “Those arrows are made of Meteorite Iron. What could possibly block them? Someone tried once — brought a ten-foot stone, solid enough that even a Spirit-Tribulation warrior would struggle to split it. The moment the arrows hit it, the Time Law reversed, and they accelerated a thousandfold. The stone was riddled with holes in an instant. The warrior died with it.”

“I know,” Callan said. His expression didn't change.

Fiona stepped forward from beside him. “My brother has it,” she said simply. “He really does.”

The crowd fell quiet. Fiona's word carried more weight than anyone else's in the square.

[SFX: MAGICAL FLASH]

[AMBIENT: AWE MURMUR]

Callan tapped the ring on his chest. A flash of light — and a massive stone materialized before him.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

Its surface had a dark green, mottled luster. 

Normally, a warrior could only sense the power of Earth Law from a stone, but special stones like this could contain other kinds of Law Power. This one, for instance, radiated five distinct energies: Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, and Earth.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]

Zane recognized it immediately. He’d seen a stone like this before—a lodestone. The World Merchants' Guild had used one during his pre-qualifying test for the Martial Tournament. The story was that they were dug up from the Sacred Mount Lodestone at the bottom of the Chaotic Ocean of Stars. 

But the Guild only had a small piece. The one Callan had just summoned was massive.

Everyone stared at the lodestone, speechless. They all knew what it was, and they knew it was incredibly difficult to mine. Even a master of the Soul Sea Realm would have to expend a massive amount of energy just to break off a single piece from the Sacred Mount.

But in the end, it was useless. While it held the power of the Five Elements, the energies were too complex and tangled. No warrior could use it to understand a Law, and it was worthless for crafting pills or weapons.

So what exactly did Callan think it was going to do against half a million poisoned arrows?
