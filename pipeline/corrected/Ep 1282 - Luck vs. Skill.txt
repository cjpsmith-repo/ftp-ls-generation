Ep 1282 - Luck vs. Skill

Word count: 998

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ETHEREAL STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY CRACKLING]
The river of energy was eating him alive. 
[SFX: ENERGY SURGING]
It lapped at the Otherworldly Swordsman’s body, and not even the Laws of the Heavens that governed the Hall of Causality could save him. 
The Dark Iron Sword in his hand, a weapon that had the power to defy the heavens themselves, was failing.
Before he’d even entered the Illusionary Arena, the sword had warned him that a special force here would try to repel it. No other souls, no other living beings, could enter with the chosen warrior. Zane had learned that the hard way—he’d been forced to leave Lavender and the Cobalt Dragon behind, unable to even communicate with them now. The battlefield’s projection had accepted only him.
But the swordsman, a man named Larkin, had brought his weapon anyway. His sword had torn its way through the rules of the arena, forcing its way inside because it knew he wouldn’t get far without it. With that power at his side, Larkin had been certain he’d take first place. In his mind, the so-called saint-level geniuses and Ace Talents were nothing.
He’d almost made everyone else believe it, too. The Hall of Causality was a death trap where one wrong move meant the end. In the Second Hall, they’d been swarmed by countless ropes that were supposed to be impossible to cut, each one trying to trap and crush them. 
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
[SFX: ROPES SHATTERING]
But Larkin had just swung his sword and shattered the very Laws of the Heavens that powered them. He got all ten warriors in his group through the hall alive, earning their absolute admiration. He seemed invincible. Following him, they thought they might actually survive this and all rank in the top ten.
Then they reached the Third Hall and ran into another group. After what they’d seen Larkin do, his team figured the seven new warriors would be easy to handle.
[MUSIC: TENSE PULSATING DRUMS]
They had no idea what was coming.
[SFX: BODY FALLING INTO ENERGY]
One moment, Larkin was the picture of confidence; the next, he was falling into the river of energy.
“I… can’t accept this,” he choked out, his words dissolving almost as quickly as his body. 
Watching from the bank, Janet felt her stomach drop. She’d recognized Larkin the instant he’d emerged from the darkness—the warrior who’d made his name in a single battle, infamous across the Hallowed Concord for humbling one of its own prodigies. 
She’d trusted Zane, but she’d never truly believed his strength matched a prodigy’s; if anything, she’d always assumed her own strength, and that of their other allies, outmatched his. Now Zane had dragged this man to the edge of death—but not through a clean win. It was nerve and misdirection that had done it, not raw power, and before she could even process what she’d just watched, Larkin was gone, swallowed by the river. It looked, for all the world, like he was dead.
Reckless as the win was, it was enough to make Janet sigh in disbelief. Whatever else was true, Zane’s luck clearly ran deeper than Larkin’s skill.
“Bring me up!” he demanded, his voice cutting through the corrosive current.
[SFX: GOLDEN LIGHT FLARING]
The sword answered, its surface blazing with a sudden, dazzling gold. It hauled Larkin’s sinking body back toward the surface—a feat that cost the weakened blade dearly. At full strength, it could have carried him through the river untouched. Weakened as it was now, it could only hold the line a little longer.
[SFX: BODY BREAKING SURFACE]
Larkin’s head broke the surface, his face corroded almost past recognition by the pure energy of the current. Still, he’d survived it. Warriors on both banks stared in disbelief.
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
“He’s still alive? This guy’s a freak,” Coleman muttered, shaking his head.
Janet and the others on Zane’s side could only gasp. For a moment, they feared he might somehow fly free of the river entirely—though flight was forbidden in the Hall, and in his state, that was unlikely. On Larkin’s side, hope flickered to life in his teammates’ eyes.
But Larkin didn’t leave the river. Staying afloat was already costing the sword everything it had left. Instead, he stared up at Zane, hatred burning in his ruined face, and let out a wild laugh.
“Boy, you win this time! But it’s luck, not strength, that beat me!”
Zane said nothing, his expression unreadable. Privately, he was stunned Larkin had survived the river’s corrosion this long at all—even his own miraculous body would have been eaten alive in an instant. 
Something was protecting Larkin. Was it the sword? Or something greater still?
“Keep winning,” he sputtered, coughing as the energy flooded his lungs. “So we can meet again. And when we do… no cheap tricks. I *will* defeat you.”
Zane could barely make out the words. 
[SFX: SWORD LIGHT FADING OUT]
The swordsman’s final defiance faded as the light from his weapon died, its power finally exhausted.
He sank fast. 
[SFX: BODY DISSOLVING]
[SFX: ENERGY CURRENT RUSHING]
Without the sword’s protection, his body dissolved in an instant, melting away until he was just another shimmering strand in the current. 
[SFX: MASSIVE ENERGY BURST]
The moment he vanished, a massive cloud of Illusion Points burst from where he’d been.
“So many…” someone whispered.
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
Everyone’s eyes lit up. Based on what they’d seen so far, the swordsman had just dropped tens of millions of points.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS SHIMMERING]
[AMBIENT: FLOWING RIVER]
The specks of yellow light hovered in the river, a glittering treasure that didn’t sink or rise. Every warrior on both banks stared, their minds racing. But what good were all those points? Who would be crazy enough to step into that river to get them?
Zane’s expression soured with disappointment. What a waste. He couldn’t help but think it was a shame he hadn’t been the one to kill the swordsman himself. If he had, all those points would have flowed right into him.
[SFX: GRAPPLING HOOK LAUNCH]
[SFX: METAL CHAIN EXTENDING]
Shaking his head, he shot a long grappling hook into the darkness across the chasm. A thin smile touched his lips as he turned his back on the river. 
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
With the most troublesome enemy out of the way, there were only seven left.
