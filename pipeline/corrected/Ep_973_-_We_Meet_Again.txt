Ep 973 - We Meet Again
Word count: 1015
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT DRUMS]
The disciple’s face was twisted with arrogance and a crazed fury, his attacks growing wilder by the second.
But a greenish-gray aura shrouded Ethan’s body. His opponent couldn’t seem to knock him down, no matter how relentless the barrage. The unusual energy shimmering around him hinted that Ethan had a few tricks of his own.
[SFX: CROWD CHEERING]
“Come on, Ethan, you got this!”
“Don’t you dare lose to some punk from Skytop Peak!”
“Defend our honor! Show them why we’re Number One Peak!”
Zane smiled at the uproar. The rivalry between Skytop Peak and his own Misty Peak was as fierce as ever.
Misty Peak had clinched first place the year Zane defeated Finn and Patrick. Finn, being from Skytop Peak, had landed his home in second. It was almost funny how, after all this time, the standings hadn’t budged.
[SFX: HARD IMPACT]
Ethan had managed to block most of the attacks, but one finally smashed through his defenses, sending him flying toward the edge of the battle ring.
The black-robed disciple sneered, stalking toward him. He raised his weapon. “So, you gonna crawl off this stage, or am I gonna have to toss you off myself?”
[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]
[SFX: VITALITY SURGE]
Ethan gritted his teeth, his jaw set stubbornly as the Vitality inside him began to surge. Even at a clear disadvantage, he was gearing up for another round.
“You just don’t know when to quit, do you? Fine. I’ll end this for you,” the Skytop disciple snarled, raising his weapon to bring it crashing down.
[SFX: SHARP WHISTLE]
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
A sharp voice cut through the noise. “Enough!”
[MUSIC: ELEGANT STRINGS]
The crowd parted as a woman made her way to the front. She wore a yellow vest over a dress that accentuated her slender figure. Her face was still breathtaking, but the four years since he’d last seen her had etched a new maturity there.
“Lady Novak,” Zane murmured, his eyes lighting up.
Isla didn’t see him floating in the air above. She walked to the edge of the battle ring and said coolly, “You’ve done well, Ethan. Surrender.”
“But, Lady Novak—” Ethan hesitated, unwilling to give in.
Isla offered a small, firm smile. “This isn’t perseverance, it’s recklessness. A true warrior knows when to retreat.”
Looking at Ethan’s stubborn pride, Isla was reminded of Zane. Not everyone was born with that kind of talent. She knew perseverance was crucial, but it could only get you so far without raw ability to back it up.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS RUNNING]
But her words seemed to have no effect. Ethan just pushed himself to his feet and charged forward again.
The Skytop disciple’s eyes flashed with contempt. His speed multiplied in an instant — a lightning kick swept Ethan’s legs clean out from under him, slamming him to the ring floor. Then he raised his fist, driving it down toward Ethan’s Vitality core.
[SFX: BODY IMPACT]
Isla lurched forward — but she was too slow.
[SFX: RUSH OF AIR]
A white figure dropped from the sky and landed on the ring between them. Without a word, Zane raised one hand and gave the Skytop disciple a single, gentle push.
It didn’t look like much. But the disciple felt an irresistible force slam into him — he skidded back a dozen steps across the ring before he could stop himself, staring at the figure now standing over Ethan with wide, stunned eyes.
Ethan looked up from the ground. His expression went blank.
“Zane L- ” He couldn’t finish the name.
[SFX: CROWD ERUPTION]
From somewhere in the crowd, a familiar voice rang out. “Zane!” Then another. Then a dozen more.
Dustin and a cluster of inner court disciples had been watching from the sidelines. Even with how much Zane had changed, they knew him. The mountainside erupted.
“It’s Zane Lowell!”
“He’s back — Zane’s actually back!”
The Skytop disciple’s smugness had evaporated. He knew that name. Everyone in the Heartlands knew that name. His gaze flicked between Zane and the ring beneath his feet, and he said nothing.
When Isla heard the shouting, her body went still. She turned slowly — and found him.
For a moment, she just stared. Then, without a word, she turned away and slipped back into the crowd.
[SFX: SOFT FOOTSTEPS — QUICK]
Isla sighed inwardly. She had been pouring everything she had into Misty Peak, desperate to find and nurture more talented disciples. She gave them all the support she could, praying for better results. It was the only thing that made the waiting bearable.
Four years wasn’t an eternity, and a part of her never truly expected Zane to return. In that time, she’d only heard bits and pieces of his story. He had provoked a powerful warrior from The Heartlands, vanished, then resurfaced in search of a master doctor. After that, he’d left for some foreign land she could barely fathom, all to save the leader of the Cloud Hall.
The Eastern Territories were so far from the Heartlands that news traveled at a crawl. Each scrap of information sent her on an emotional roller coaster—elation, then worry, then a flicker of hope—only for the cycle to begin again with the next rumor.
And now, Zane was back. Have I just been waiting for him all this time? she wondered. Her thoughts tangled into an impossible knot. She couldn’t think straight, and she certainly didn’t have an answer.
[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]
[MUSIC: ROMANTIC PIANO]
“Instructor Novak!”
Isla kept walking. She didn’t look back.
He tried again. “Isla!”
“I’m not your instructor anymore,” she said. “And you can’t call me by my name — I’m older than you.”
A beat of silence. Then: “Friend?”
She didn’t reply.
Suddenly, a gasp escaped her as a pair of strong hands wrapped around her waist from behind.
“Let go!” Isla hissed, her voice tight with a mix of shock and embarrassment.
She was still a master, and with so many of her students watching from the mountainside, a hot blush crept up her neck.
Her resistance only made Zane hold her tighter, his embrace lingering. “I’m sorry I’m late,” he murmured, his voice thick with regret.
He had promised he would come back.
[MUSIC: PLAYFUL FLUTE]
Tucked away behind a large rock on the other side of the peak, Cora and Tia watched the scene unfold. Cora smiled knowingly. “See? Told you.”
