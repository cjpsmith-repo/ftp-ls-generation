Ep 1004 - The Vitality Pressure

Word count: 1115

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WATER CAVERN]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]

The plainly dressed girl wasn’t the only one staring at Zane — but she was the only one who hadn’t yet decided what to make of him.

You could only ascend to the Upper World after breaking through to the Soul Sea Realm. It was practically cosmic law.

The warriors in the Ascending Well were all at the Soul Sea Realm, so what on earth was a Life-and-Death Realm warrior doing here?

“This water is nice and all, but we can’t just float around in here forever! Let’s get out of this well!” one of the warriors finally grumbled.

The others immediately agreed. One of them kicked off, swimming for the surface.

[SFX: IMPACT THUD]

[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]

But just as he got halfway there, a muffled thud echoed in the water. It was like his head had smacked right into an invisible wall. He tumbled backward, splashing back into the well.

“Looks like there’s some kind of invisible barrier up there.”

Zane glanced coolly toward the top of the well. Then, with a single leap, he shot upward and reached out to touch the empty space above.

[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER HUM]

Sure enough, his fingers met a smooth, hard surface. An invisible, enchanted barrier sealed the mouth of the well. To get out, they’d have to break it.

Zane was confident in his own strength, but he held back. He didn’t know the first thing about this place, and it was better not to do anything reckless. He wasn’t afraid of trouble, but he had no desire to stir it up for no reason.

[SFX: WATER SPLASH]

Zane dropped back into the water. “There’s definitely an enchanted barrier,” he said quietly. “Someone put it there.”

[SFX: GRUFF LAUGHTER]

The other warriors nodded, their gazes lingering on Zane. A burly, bearded warrior eyed him with growing interest, then suddenly let out a laugh. “You’re only at the Life-and-Death Realm. How in the hell did you get here? Something about that just ain’t right.”

“He’s right. A Life-and-Death Realm warrior ascending to the Upper World? I’ve never heard of anything like it!” another warrior chimed in.

“I thought I was the weakest one here since I just formed my Soul Sea,” someone else admitted. “Never thought I’d see someone from the Life-and-Death Realm!”

There was a trace of disdain behind the words — they were Soul Sea Realm warriors, after all. A Life-and-Death Realm cultivator barely registered as a peer. Only the plainly dressed woman held her tongue. In her view, a warrior who broke the rules of ascension wasn’t someone to dismiss. If her instincts were right, his true strength was nothing like what his level suggested.

Zane’s expression didn’t change. He stretched out in the water and said nothing.

[MUSIC: QUIET TENSION]

Half a month passed.

There was nothing to do but wait. The enchantment sealing the well blocked outside Vitality entirely — no one could cultivate without drawing on their own stored Crystal Cubes. The well was comfortable, but it was small, and after a few days the silence grew heavy. No one spoke much. They simply floated, conserved their energy, and waited.

[SFX: DISTANT FOOTSTEPS]

Then, on the fifteenth day, they heard it — the measured clank of armored boots approaching from above.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

An armored warrior appeared at the rim of the well and looked down at them with flat, unimpressed eyes. He let out a short laugh. “Half a month ought to be enough. I hope you lot from the Lower Realm have managed to wash the smell of dirt off yourselves.”

The words landed like a slap. Every warrior in the well stiffened. These were the top existences of their world — kings, champions, warriors who had been worshipped by entire nations. To be looked down on like this, by an armored grunt they’d never heard of, was a humiliation none of them had been prepared for.

Zane simply smiled. He’d expected something like this. Every world had people like that.

The armored warrior caught the angry stares below and smiled wider. “I know you’re not happy about it. But I’ll tell you something useful — that well you’ve been soaking in is a purification well. Without it, you wouldn’t last ten breaths out here. The Vitality in this world isn’t like what you’re used to. It’s not a docile sheep. It’s something far more dangerous. The well prepared your bodies for it. Be grateful.”

He reached up and undid the enchantment.

[SFX: BARRIER DISSOLVING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE SURGE]

The moment the barrier came down, something crashed into them from above — a wave of dense, violent Vitality unlike anything they had ever felt. It wasn’t abundant. It was aggressive. It pressed down on them like a physical weight, churning against their Soul Seas, destabilizing the inner worlds they had spent lifetimes building.

[SFX: GASPS — SPLASHING WATER]

The reactions were immediate. Warriors who had dominated entire continents clutched at themselves, their faces twisting with discomfort. Some submerged back into the well water — the only thing that still felt safe. Even the plainly dressed woman’s expression tightened.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL TENSION]

Thanks to the Primordial Chaos Tactic, Zane’s core had changed completely. It was now filled only with chaos energy, which left him unaffected by the wild Vitality of Heaven and Earth swirling around them.

Zane looked at the powerful Soul Sea Realm warriors, confused. “The Vitality of Heaven and Earth is intense, but should they really be reacting like this?” he asked the Cobalt Dragon in his mind.

If Soul Sea Realm warriors couldn’t handle this Vitality, what about everyone else in the Upper World? The resources there were supposed to be plentiful, but not everyone was a master. There had to be countless mortals, Adept-level creatures, and others at the Soul Realm.

If even these powerful warriors couldn’t take it, how were the weaker ones surviving?

[SFX: DEEP WATER AMBIENCE]

A sense of amusement from the Cobalt Dragon echoed in Zane’s mind. It’s not strange at all. The reason is simple. Think of fish that live in the deep sea—they can’t survive in the shallows. If you dragged one up from the depths, its body would probably explode from the change in pressure.

Zane nodded. I get it. Because of the water pressure.

Exactly, the Cobalt Dragon replied. The Vitality of Heaven and Earth works the same way, creating what you could call Vitality Pressure. These warriors may be at the Soul Sea Realm, but they need time to acclimate to this new environment. After a little while, the discomfort will pass.

But I didn’t feel this Vitality Pressure on my last trip to the Upper World, Zane pointed out.

He hadn’t just been to the Upper World; he’d also visited the Violet Core Realm. Tia and Cora had been with him, and none of them had felt anything strange at all.

He wondered how much more he would miss them as this process went on.
