The Golden Flames shifted, bleeding into a furious golden-red that pulsed with an unbearable heat.
The fire's power had skyrocketed, and Zane's face drained of color as the Solar Flare rushed him. Just as he'd feared, it was on a whole other level now.
[SFX: FIRE IMPACT]
[SFX: GRUNT OF PAIN]
A searing pain tore through Zane as the golden-red flames slammed into him.
Just like that day in the cellar, his body began to glow, turning an impossible, deep crimson.
Normally, a fire this hot would boil a man from the inside out, leaving nothing but a dried husk to be incinerated into ash.
But Zane's body was anything but normal.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
Instead of being destroyed, he began to glow, his skin turning red-hot like a freshly forged, mystical weapon. From deep within his pores, tiny flecks of Gold Leaf were drawn to the surface, hovering just above his skin until his entire body shimmered with a brilliant, golden light.
As his body glowed cherry-red, the golden flecks morphed into ornate, arcane Ancient Runes that began to spin. They spun faster and faster, a hypnotic blur, until a Golden Maelstrom of tiny whirlpools formed on the surface of his skin.
[SFX: FIRE WHOOSHING]
The whirlpools grew, their suction pulling the Solar Flare right out of the furnace and into Zane's body.
The fire wasn't destroying him—it was refining him.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TRANSFORMATION]
His body was actually pulling the fire into itself. Flames streamed into every pore on his arms, legs, and neck. The agony was beyond anything he had ever known, a searing pain that burned every inch of him from the outside in. Zane clenched his jaw, doing everything he could just to endure it.
But this physical torment was nothing compared to the soul-deep agony he'd endured from the black flame in the cellar. He could feel the intense heat burning away the toxic Bone-Shatter Powder that had poisoned him.
As his body reacted to the flames, Zane felt something stir in his mind: a giant furnace, dormant until the black flame awakened it.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE BURST]
Now, it roared to life, its flames blazing out of control. The inferno washed over his consciousness, then erupted from between his eyebrows!
[SFX: EERIE SHRIEK]
The black flame materialized before him, writhing like a living thing.
[SFX: HISSING DISSOLVE]
It let out an unnatural shriek and shot toward the Hellfire Blade. Forged by Evil Jace, the blade already glowed red-hot, but the moment the black flame touched its supposedly priceless, top-tier metal, the weapon dissolved instantly, leaving nothing but a few drops of molten iron.
Zane stared, his mind reeling. "What the hell is this black flame? How can it be so terrifying?"
After the Hellfire Blade was gone, the eerie flames wrapped around the droplets of molten iron and vanished back into his Mindscape. A jolt of panic shot through him. His body might be as tough as a mystical weapon, but molten metal pouring into his head? That couldn't be good.
But to his astonishment, he felt... fine. Perfectly fine. In his mind's eye, he watched the black flame carry the drops of iron toward the Cobalt Dragon etched on the wall of the Ancient Cauldron. The massive vessel was decorated with nine dragons, their eyes all closed, except for the Cobalt Dragon.
[SFX: MIGHTY DRAGON ROAR]
As Zane watched, the dragon lifted its head, let out a silent, mighty roar, and swallowed the molten iron.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
After swallowing the iron, a magical change rippled through the dragon. Its dull, gray scales began to transform. On its tail, a single scale lit up, glowing with the light of a perfect emerald.
It was unbelievable—the remains of the Hellfire Blade had activated a scale on the Drake's Sigil!
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: POWER BUILDING]
As Zane watched in awe, a surge of energy flowed from the scale and flooded his body. His stamina skyrocketed, a wave of intense power was building inside him!
The implications hit him all at once. "If the black flame keeps consuming mystical weapons... can I activate more scales? All of them? That has to be it! Yes, that's it!"
A grin of pure joy spread across his face as he connected the dots. His time in the kiln had given him a deeper understanding of The Great Forge. If a single Dragon Scale could boost his power this much, what would happen if all ten thousand of the Cobalt Scales were lit?
And it wasn't just the Cobalt Dragon. Above it were white, purple, black, and Storm Drakes—nine dragons in total, carved into The Great Forge.
He couldn't even begin to imagine how powerful he'd become if he activated all their scales.
Zane wet his lips, shaking his head as he tried to wrap his mind around the sheer scale of it. This was beyond anything he could have ever imagined.
But as the excitement settled, the sheer difficulty of it sank in.
Evil Jace had said the Hellfire Blade was a top-tier mystical weapon, incredibly valuable. If the essence of a treasure like that only activated a single Dragon Scale, how many more would it take to light up the thousands of others?
"Even if I melted down every treasure in the entire Eastern Territory," he realized, "it would barely be enough to activate the Cobalt Dragon, let alone the other eight."
[AMBIENT: ROARING FIRE]
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
With a newfound determination, Zane settled cross-legged in the roaring fire. The Golden Maelstrom on his skin kept pulling in the Solar Flare, continuing to forge his body.
Outside the furnace, Evil Jace stared in, his expression shifting from skepticism to confusion.
He couldn't sense his Hellfire Blade anymore—had it somehow fused with the boy? But he couldn't sense the boy's Life Energy, either.
When he noticed the flames he'd fed into the furnace were dying down, a wild thought struck him. "Could I have actually created a Spirit Weapon? Did I succeed?"
When a weapon's creation neared completion, it entered the Foundation Stage. During this stage, the treasure would absorb a massive amount of fire before it was finished, and the amount depended on its rank. Higher-level weapons devoured more fire.
He remembered forging the Hellfire Blade; it had absorbed fire for two hours during its Foundation Stage. Three hours had already passed today.
Since a higher-class weapon needed more time to absorb fire, Evil Jace realized the new treasure in the furnace had to be a Spirit Weapon!
The thought sent a thrill through him, his heart pounding.
He didn't hesitate. Jace poured more of his own fire into the furnace, but it was like feeding a black hole; the furnace devoured every last spark he could muster.
[SFX: ROARING FLAMES
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
Six hours later, Evil Jace was panting, drenched in sweat. He was exhausted.
But was it enough? He knew how crucial the Foundation Stage was. If he mismanaged it, the weapon would fail to form. Gritting his teeth against his own impatience, he could do nothing but wait and keep feeding the dying flames.
[AMBIENT: QUIET FORGE]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS BUILDUP]
Twenty hours later.
[SFX: COLLAPSE TO FLOOR]
Squeezing the last bit of fire from his body, Evil Jace's legs gave out. He collapsed, utterly exhausted and gasping for air.
He pulled out several Void Capsules and popped them into his mouth.
Still unsure if he had succeeded, Evil Jace stood before the furnace. He reached out, turned the handle, and pulled the door open.
[SFX: METAL IMPACT]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STING]
Just as it swung wide, a golden fist slammed into his face with brutal force.
