Ep 978 - The True Path
Word count: 1049
Tia smirked at Saul as they stood in front of the True Barrier. “Looks like this thing isn’t budging.”
The power behind Zane’s punch had been way stronger than Tia expected, but it still hadn’t been enough to even crack the barrier. She was starting to think Saul was just messing with them.
Unfazed by her jab, Saul simply replied, “Just because Zane can’t break it doesn’t mean no one can.”
“Seriously?” Cora asked, frustrated. “If even Zane can’t get through this thing, who else possibly could?” The three of them had thrown everything they had into backing Zane up.
Saul didn’t bother arguing. He just turned to Zane. “You’ve already tried brute force. Now, use the thunder token like I told you. At your current level, you can’t break through the True Barrier on your own.”
But Zane just laughed. “I don’t need it.”
[SFX: METALLIC MATERIALIZATION]
As he spoke, a pickax materialized in his hand.
When Saul saw it, a flicker of genuine surprise finally crossed his face. “The Black Star?”
Zane nodded.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
This time, he swung the Black Star and, without a moment’s hesitation, smashed it straight into the True Barrier.
[SFX: LOUD SHATTER]
With a deafening crack, the indestructible barrier shattered like glass, crumbling away into nothing.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WONDER]
The group moved forward through the gap. As they walked, the scenery shifted around them — the high walls and green tiles of the Keep’s inner courtyard dissolved away, replaced by open ground stretching in every direction. Exotic flowers bloomed on both sides of the path, vivid and strange. And running straight through the middle of it all was a single narrow ridge — so thin that only one person could walk it at a time. It stretched away into the distance without end.
Zane, Tia, and Cora all stared.
“This … is the true path?” Zane asked.
Saul nodded. “That’s right.”
Cora tilted her head at the thin ridge. “It’s just a footpath. What’s so hard about it?”
Saul’s expression didn’t change. “You’re welcome to try.”
Cora stepped forward — and Zane’s hand shot out and pulled her back.
“Will this path be life-threatening?” he asked Saul.
Saul’s tone was flat. “The true path is not an illusion. Every challenge on it is real. It can absolutely be life-threatening. This path is designed for Soul Sea Realm warriors. A small number of exceptional Life-and-Death warriors can attempt the first stage — but only the first.”
Zane kept his grip on Cora’s arm.
Isla stepped forward. “Zane — if it’s that dangerous, you should wait. Come back when your cultivation is stronger.”
Tia frowned and nodded. “She’s right. You’ve only just broken through to the Life-and-Death Realm. Rushing this isn’t wise.”
Zane then turned to Saul. “You’ve been in The Elysian Keep this whole time. How do you know about this pickax?”
Saul’s surprise vanished, replaced by a faint smile. “Across the universes, warriors have created countless rankings for magical weapons, but the most famous is the Cosmic Spirit Index. After it was established, the various races all made their own copies. Whenever a new Divine Weapon is forged, the Spirit Index automatically detects it and adds it to the list.”
“The Cosmic Spirit Index?” Zane arched an eyebrow.
Saul nodded. “If I remember right, as a Divine weapon, your pickax ranks somewhere in the top nine hundred. The Law of Causality that it contains is on a very high level. But since it’s just a pickax, it’s difficult to wield its full power. If it were reforged into something like an axe or a sword, it could probably rank in the top six or seven hundred in all the universes.”
Zane nodded, taking it all in. He knew next to nothing about the upper worlds and was fascinated by the Spirit Index. “So, there are over ten thousand weapons on the list? And they’re all divine-class?”
Saul shook his head. “No, the number of divine-class weapons is actually quite small. A lesser universe might only have a handful.”
[MUSIC: MAGICAL CONTEMPLATION]
Zane turned that over quietly. A lesser universe. The phrase sat strangely in his mind. A universe with over a hundred thousand supreme worlds — and Saul called it small. That meant there was something beyond it. An end. And whatever lay past that end was something Zane couldn’t yet begin to imagine.
“The Index isn’t fixed, either,” Saul added. “It updates constantly — weapons rise and fall as they’re used and reforged. Your pickax has spent most of its existence being swung at rock faces rather than enemies. It may have slipped below the top one thousand by now.”
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TRANSFORMATION]
As Zane listened, a strange sensation washed over him. The Vitality inside his body, which should have been slowly solidifying, was liquifying instead. He could feel his power surging, growing several times faster than before.
That old man may not have thought the pill was valuable, but Zane knew it was something special. The thing was practically alive—it could run and even attack people. Whatever the case, its effects were blowing his mind.
All he knew for sure was that his training was accelerating. Even though he’d spent most of the past two months with Tia and Cora and only a few days actually training, Zane could feel his power nearing its peak. He figured he was about to face the Lesser Celestial Tribulation and finally break through to the second level of the Life-and-Death Realm.
With his current strength, getting from The Heartlands back to The Eastern Territories and into The Elysian Keep wouldn’t be hard at all.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]
Still, even though what Tia had argued made sense, Zane shook his head. He had to try. “We’ve come all this way. How can I just give up now?”
Saul smiled slightly. “I knew you wouldn’t give up so easily. I should warn you, though. Your true strength is on par with a Soul Sea Realm warrior, so you actually have a shot at clearing the first stage of the true path of the Elysian Keep. As long as you can get a token, you’ll be set. You could even use it to open The Armory or The Archives, which would give you some incredible rewards. I think you should go for it.”
“And what if he gets hurt?” Cora snapped. “Are you going to take responsibility for that?”
Saul just ignored her.
