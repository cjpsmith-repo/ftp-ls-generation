Ep 729 - The Truth Comes Out
Word count: 1038
[AMBIENT: SOMBER WIND]
[MUSIC: TRAGIC STRINGS]
The fall of the Ethereal Spirit Sect was a body blow to the entire Heartlands.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
News of the massacre spread like a plague. The Corrupted Divine Kingdom had finally broken the siege of Ethereal City and slaughtered everyone inside. It wasn't just war — it was a statement.
The independent warriors who'd joined the Corrupted Divine Kingdom needed to be rewarded, and the sects of the Heartlands needed a reminder of what defiance cost. The mortals of Ethereal City had assumed the fighting was above them. That whoever won would simply hang new flags from the walls. They couldn't have been more wrong.
[SFX: DISTANT SCREAMS]
[MUSIC: DARK CHOIR]
The Corrupted Divine Kingdom didn't want subjects; they wanted ruin. The moment the gates fell, Maddox’s warriors poured in, burning and looting everything in their path. They left no one alive.
When word reached the elders of Cloud Hall, the news was crushing. Zane’s first thought was of Cora. He knew this would shatter her world.
[AMBIENT: MEETING HALL]
[SFX: HURRIED FOOTSTEPS]
Just then, a steward hurried into the meeting hall. "House Kensington from Ethereal City is asking for an audience!" he announced.
"Let them in," Tia ordered. "Bring them here."
Cora frantically wiped the tears from her face. How many got out? Is Brent okay? What about my father?
[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
A few moments later, a small group entered the hall. The man in the lead wore the black robes of the Ethereal Spirit Sect. He clasped his hands in a formal greeting. "Greetings, Leader Tia."
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Cora’s eyes scanned their faces, a desperate hope dying with each unfamiliar one. She didn't recognize any of them. "I thought you were from House Kensington," she said, her voice trembling. "You're all from the sect. Where are the members of my house? Where is my father? My uncles? Where are Brent and Caleb?"
She was right. These weren’t her people.
The man at the front was Pierce Preston, a sect member who had been on good terms with House Kensington. He was a prodigy who had reached the Spirit-Tribulation Realm in his thirties and was one of Cora’s most ardent admirers. He’d once spent weeks searching the mountains just to gather Radiant Light Seeds for her birthday.
She had thrown them away without a second thought. He had never forgotten it.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
But he didn't look at her now. He looked at her the way someone looks at the source of everything that went wrong.
"Your father is dead," he said. "Maddox killed him personally. Brent is missing." He let that land. Then: "Thanks to you."
What Cora couldn't see — what none of them could see from his face — was that Caleb had no real intention of handing her to Maddox.
The Ethereal Spirit Sect was finished. Even if Elder Xander was released, it wouldn't matter. The Heartlands belonged to the Corrupted Divine Kingdom now, and a man who knew which way the wind blew could still carve out a place for himself — especially if he had the right leverage.
The Violet Core Physique was worth more than any sect loyalty. He had a plan, and it had nothing to do with saving anyone.
Cora stared at him. "What do you mean, Thanks to me?"
"Maddox didn't attack the Ethereal Spirit Sect for its inheritance. He came for you. If you'd joined him when the Sect Master asked you to, none of this would have happened." His voice was cold and precise. "The city. Your family. All of it. Because you refused."
"No," Cora said. "That's not—"
"Your great-grandfather is alive," Caleb cut in. "Maddox has him. He'll release him in exchange for you."
A silence fell over the hall.
Cora's face went blank. Then, slowly, something shifted in it — a flicker of something that might have been relief, followed immediately by dread. She understood what was being asked. She nodded, almost to herself. Then she shook her head. "I can't," she whispered. "I can't do it."
"You don't have a choice," Caleb said, and reached for her.
[SFX: VIOLENT IMPACT]
[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]
Before the man could say another word, Zane was on him. He moved in a blur, slamming the warrior against the wall and pinning him there by the throat. "Your leader is that important to you, huh?" Zane snarled, his voice dangerously low. "Well, my sister is the only thing in this world that matters to me. Your leader is worthless. Got it?"
Without waiting for an answer, Zane drove his fist into the man's chest. Even for a warrior of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, the blow was devastating.
[SFX: BODY CRASH]
The man was flung across the room like a broken doll, crashing hard against the far wall of the meeting hall before slumping to the floor.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
The other Ethereal Spirit Sect members recoiled, their faces a mixture of shock and fury.
"You're completely unreasonable!" Caleb shouted, his face red with rage. "Is this how you treat your allies?"
"First," Zane said, his voice flat, "Cora isn't Ethereal Spirit Sect property. She's House Kensington. Your surname isn't Kensington. Second, handing her to Maddox doesn't just harm her — it completes his ritual and puts every warrior in the Heartlands at risk. And secondly—" he glanced at Caleb— "it would harm my sister. So no."
Tia and the Cloud Hall elders remained silent, their expressions unreadable. Zane just sneered at Caleb, refusing to waste another breath on him. His sister's safety was the only thing that mattered.
Watching Zane, Cora felt a fierce, protective warmth bloom in her chest. He had defended her honor without a second thought. She realized then that she had no reason to hide, no reason to back down.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]
She stood taller and met Caleb's furious glare with one of her own. "Actually," she announced, her voice ringing with newfound confidence, "Zane has another reason."
Zane knew exactly what she was about to say. He started to move, to stop her, but the words were already out, cutting through the tense silence.
"I'm no longer a virgin!"
