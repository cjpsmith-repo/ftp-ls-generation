Ep 809 - A Fair Fight

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Wherever you find people, you’ll find conflict. The seven divine kingdoms were no exception. Though they were all closely connected and relied on each other for survival, and no large-scale war had ever broken out between them, that didn’t mean there wasn’t competition. In fact, the very closeness of their alliance only made the secret rivalries that simmered beneath the surface more fierce. Since they couldn’t just conquer one another’s land, they found other ways to get an edge. 

That was the real reason they’d all built so many martial houses—each kingdom was desperate to find and train the next generation of talent, securing their own power.

As the ruler of the Harlen Divine Kingdom, the Emperor knew exactly how valuable a man like Zane was. The implications were staggering; Zane was already incredibly powerful.

The Emperor spoke with a calm politeness, but there was no fear in his voice as he addressed War King York. In recent years, the York Divine Kingdom had surged ahead, claiming the top rank in overall strength among the seven kingdoms. The Harlen Divine Kingdom, by contrast, was ranked last. When it came to the personal strength of the rulers themselves, however, the gap between the Emperor and York was much narrower.

York’s patience was clearly wearing thin. “Emperor,” he growled, “what exactly are you trying to say?”

“My meaning is clear,” the Emperor replied, his voice steady. “Whatever you call this boy, he is a member of the Harlen Divine Kingdom.”

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

A cold, mocking smile spread across York’s face. “So you’re saying my son died for nothing?”

A faint smile touched the Emperor’s own lips. “If I’m not mistaken, York, you have more than twenty thousand descendants, don't you?”

Some rulers were famous for their appetites, but York was in a league of his own. His imperial harem spanned a million square miles—the size of a medium city—and housed tens of thousands of beautiful women who had borne him nearly twenty thousand princes. He probably couldn't even remember all their names. In the divine kingdoms, princes were hardly a rare commodity. They all had to rely on their own talent and strength to claw their way into their father's notice. The less talented were simply cast aside.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

York's expression hardened. “I may have many sons. But this one was talented and favored, and his relationship with the Holy Maiden was close. Are you telling me I can't ask for an explanation?”

The Emperor's smile didn't waver. He had seen through York from the start. All this — the soul attack, the accusations, the righteous fury — had been leading to one thing: compensation. York wanted something. The Emperor had no intention of giving it to him.

“I will give you an explanation,” the Emperor said evenly. “But I will say it again — Zane Jones is a warrior of the Harlen Divine Kingdom.”

He stepped aside and turned toward Zane. “Jones.” His voice was calm, carrying just enough weight to land. “Randy asked you to fight for us. Perform well. But be careful.”

It was a simple warning, but its meaning was clear to everyone watching: the Emperor knew exactly what York's fighters might try to do. He couldn't stop an “accident” in the ring. But he wanted Zane to know he was seen.

Zane cupped his hands toward the Emperor. “Understood.”

[MUSIC: WARM STRINGS]

[SFX: STORAGE RING]

[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]

As Zane turned toward the barrier, he slowly drew a long sword from his Storage Ring. In the sunlight, it glowed with a faint golden luster.

Logan's eyes narrowed. “A top-grade Relic?”

Up on the platform, the Emperor's gaze moved to Randy. “Did you give him that?”

Randy shook his head. “Zane never asked me for a weapon. That's his own.”

The Emperor looked back at the sword for a moment, then shook his head slowly. “This man's origins are not as simple as the Heartlands.”

Randy nodded. “I understand. But Zane is a good man, and we get along well. We just clicked, you know? So I gave him the Ancient Heartstones as a gift.”

“You did the right thing,” the Emperor said. “If we can convince someone like him to stay in our kingdom, the benefits he could bring us are immeasurable.”

Listening to the exchange, the Crown Prince slouched in his seat, his displeasure obvious. He and Randy had been rivals for years, and now Randy had been made a Lord. Even though he was the heir apparent, the Crown Prince knew the competition wasn't over. 

Unless he reached the Soul Sea Realm and their father abdicated, Randy still had a chance to challenge him for the throne. Now, with a powerhouse like Zane in Randy's corner, his own hopes seemed to be fading, and a knot of anxiety tightened in his stomach.

As Zane approached the barrier, Logan was waiting. His contempt had gone quiet — replaced by something more calculating.

“Your soul is strong,” Logan said. “I'll give you that. But I'd like to see how strong the rest of you is.”

Zane smiled. “Don't worry. I won't use soul attacks.”

[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]

The declaration landed like a stone in still water. Logan's eyes narrowed. Zane had a Fighting Soul Realm advantage, and he was giving it up voluntarily. What kind of confidence did that take?

York's voice cut across the square. “Good. Then let's keep it that way.” The words were smooth, almost generous — but everyone understood what he had just done. He had made Zane's promise public, sealing it off as an option. If Zane used his soul now, he would look like a man who couldn't keep his word.

Logan nodded slowly. He had always believed no one in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm could match him. Zane handing away his greatest advantage only made him more certain. He would win. But he also understood what York expected of him now — this wasn't just about winning.

As the battle began, Logan let his remaining scorn fall away, his focus narrowing. Layers of shimmering Spatial Armor materialized around him as he swept the Spatial Blade through the air, sending countless ripples of multicolored light dancing through the sky. 

He took a single step forward, and a trail of afterimages blurred behind him.

[SFX: SPATIAL WHOOSH]

In the next instant, he reappeared right beside Zane.
