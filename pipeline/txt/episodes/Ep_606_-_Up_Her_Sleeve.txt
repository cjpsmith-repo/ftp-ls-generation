Ep 606 - Up Her Sleeve
Word count: 1096
[AMBIENT: TRAINING ARENA]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Sword fighting styles were always evolving, but they all came down to the same core principles. No matter how flashy a technique looked, it was always just a variation on the fundamentals.
Usually, Cheryl could read an opponent's next move just by watching their wrists. But Zane was something else. His swordsmanship followed no pattern she recognized, leaving her completely unable to predict his next strike.
They exchanged only a few blows before Zane shattered her defense, the tip of his longsword aimed right at her heart.
[SFX: TELEPORT WHOOSH]
With a frustrated hiss, Cheryl teleported away just as the blade lunged for her.
In truth, she never expected to break his guard with swordsmanship alone. Not after seeing his bizarre style in the fight against Boris. But that didn't matter. She didn't need to overpower him to win.
After all, a battle between warriors was rarely about pure skill. It was about who had the better trick up their sleeve.
The moment she reappeared across the arena, a smirk spread across her face.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL RISE]
Her eyes flashed as she unleashed her plan. "Shadow Trinity!"
[SFX: SWISHES]
In a blur, three menacing shadows erupted from her body, shooting to three corners of the arena.
Zane eyed the three shadows, a slight frown creasing his brow. Before he could make sense of them, Cheryl was already moving.
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
She launched herself upward and drove her sword straight at his head.
"Frost Breaking Sword!"
A blade of sharp sword light pierced downward. Zane sneered and flicked his longsword — not just deflecting it, but shattering it entirely, his counter already angled for her shoulder. Another half second and she'd have been cut clean in two.
Cheryl wrenched herself sideways in midair, caught by some invisible pull, and vanished into the shadow in the nearest corner. An instant later, a new shadow bloomed from her body and drifted to another point in the arena. Three shadows again. Always three.
"These shadows…" Zane murmured, turning slowly.
He understood the problem now. Every time her attack failed, she simply stepped into a shadow and reappeared somewhere else. She was nearly impossible to pin down.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Cheryl smiled and came at him again — lower this time, body dropped, rushing in fast.
Zane swung hard, sword essence flooding the ground in a ten-foot arc in front of him. Cheryl passed straight through it, close enough that he could hear her breathing, and her blade was already at his throat.
[SFX: TELEPORT WHOOSH]
He went through space.
Reappearing behind her, he slashed — but she was already gone, pulled sideways by that same strange force, dissolving into another shadow. A new one appeared in the corner she'd just vacated.
Zane stood still for a moment, sword at his side.
Speed. That was the only answer. He had to seal all three shadows at once before she could choose between them.
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]
He didn't give her another moment. He lunged — and as Cheryl moved to merge with a shadow and counter, he dodged the strike and accelerated toward the next one. A white light flashed in his other hand as the shattered dagger materialized.
[SFX: WEAPON WHOOSH]
In one fluid motion, he hurled the dagger at the farthest shadow, charged the second one himself, and then — mid-stride — flung his sword at the last.
[SFX: MULTIPLE BLADES CUTTING AIR]
Suddenly, all three of her escape routes were under attack. No matter which shadow she chose, she’d be hit. She was trapped.
Her Shadow Trinity gave her incredible mobility, but it also locked her to those three points. And now, Zane had an attack aimed at every single one. What do I do? The choice was impossible.
[SFX: SHADOW TELEPORT]
In a desperate gamble, her form flickered and reappeared right where Zane was—at the shadow he was charging on foot.
Her logic was simple, born of pure instinct. The shattered dagger pulsed with a dangerous aura, but Zane's sword—a Relic-Class weapon—was a guaranteed kill. She couldn't block it. Her only real choice was to face the man himself. He’d thrown his sword, leaving him unarmed.
A swordsman without a sword was a tiger without its claws. And Zane had just thrown his away. This was her chance.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
It was a fatal miscalculation.
The moment she materialized, Zane didn't swing a fist. He reached out and caught her sword between two fingers.
Cheryl pulled. Nothing.
She pulled again, harder, flooding her body with vital energy. The blade didn't move. It was like it had been swallowed by stone.
The sword's name was Gentle Snow — a holy weapon, almost weightless in the hand, perfectly suited to her style. She'd trained with it for years. Cherished it.
[SFX: SHARP CRACK]
Zane snapped it in two.
"You—"
The calm smile Cheryl had maintained through the entire fight vanished. She stared at the broken blade in his fingers, something raw crossing her face. It wasn't just a weapon. And he'd broken it like it was nothing.
[SFX: DARK ENERGY SURGE]
"Go to hell!"
A pulse of green light flared above her head and sank into it. The change was instant. Pitch-black light erupted from her fingertips, her nails lengthening into vicious dark claws.
[MUSIC: MOUNTING DANGER]
The sight of those claws made Zane's breath catch in his throat. He was confident in his body's resilience, but something primal, something instinctive, warned him those claws were different. Whatever dark energy coated them bypassed ordinary defenses. He couldn't let them touch him.
But Cheryl didn't give him the choice.
[SFX: AGGRESSIVE SWIPE]
She lunged forward, swiping with her claws like a cornered lynx, each strike aimed at something vital—throat, eyes, the arteries in his neck. The precision was terrifying. This wasn't wild fury; this was calculated murder dressed in rage.
Zane backpedaled hard, dodging by inches, his full attention locked on her hands.
And in that singular focus, he made a critical error. He forgot about the shadows.
[SFX: SHADOW TELEPORT]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
He retreated into a corner—and Cheryl's form dissolved like smoke, reappearing in the shadow directly behind him. His instincts shrieked danger half a heartbeat too late.
"Crap!"
He'd been far too careless, too focused on the obvious threat while ignoring her shadow techniques. The same arrogance that had gotten him into this mess now threatened to end it.
And now, trapped in this corner with Cheryl materializing behind him, there was nowhere left to go.
