Ep 805 - Zane's Turn To Fight

Word count: 1070

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

With Edwin’s defeat, the Harlen Divine Kingdom was fresh out of top-tier warriors. The only ones left standing were Lord Randy and the Crown Prince himself.

The continent’s First Tier was reserved for saint-level geniuses, talents so rare they were practically myths. Finding someone who could hold their own against the best of them was damn near impossible. There was a chasm in strength between the First and Second Tiers; even the strongest of the second-stringers would be annihilated by a fighter like Logan in a single blow. Sending them in would be a pointless sacrifice.

The Crown Prince had never actually seen Zane fight. He wasn't there when Zane passed the Crystal Martial House's examination, but Edwin had told him about it personally—a lone warrior had appeared, someone good enough to be ranked in the First Tier. It sounded too good to be true, and the prince’s curiosity was piqued. He desperately needed someone with that kind of power, which was why he’d rushed all the way to Crane City. At the time, he figured winning Zane over would be simple, especially since Edwin had mentioned Randy was already his enemy.

He just never expected Randy to crash the party.

Based on Edwin's account, the Crown Prince had a rough idea of Zane's strength—probably on par with Edwin himself, maybe a little stronger. But even so, he likely wasn’t a match for Logan. That wasn’t the real problem, though. The real question was, would Zane even listen to him? What if he asked Zane to fight and the man just said no? What then?

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Seeing the Crown Prince lost in his own head, a smirk played on Randy’s lips. He knew this was his moment.

The Wind Divine Kingdom passed without sending a single fighter. The Star Field crown prince sent up another challenger — their best remaining hope. This one lasted longer than the others, making it all the way to the seventh strike before Logan took his arm. The Star Field section fell silent. War King York turned to the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom with a slow smile. “I wonder — is there anyone left in your kingdom who can still fight? Or is your Crown Prince going to have to fight Logan himself?”

The Emperor’s jaw tightened, his face darkening. The bastard, he thought. He knows the answer. He’s just saying it to humiliate me. This match was just a preliminary bout before the real exploration of the secret lands, nothing more than an appetizer. York was pushing it. But as quickly as the anger flared, it died, replaced by a weary resignation.

York was right. He was out of options.

Should he really send the Crown Prince into the ring? It was an option, sure, but winning wouldn’t bring much glory, and losing would be a total disgrace. If the prince was defeated, how could he ever be seen as worthy of the throne? He had to think this through.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Just then, the Holy Maiden of the York Divine Kingdom sashayed over to her emperor. 

She giggled, her voice a playful taunt. “From what I hear, only Edwin from your kingdom was even remotely qualified. Now that Logan’s taken his arm, who’s next? Lord Randy? Or maybe the Crown Prince himself?”

The words hit their mark, and a flash of anger crossed the Crown Prince's face.

Besides its royal family, the York Divine Kingdom had a large, clandestine order. The Holy Maiden was its most important figure, with a status equal to the Crown Prince’s—and in some ways, her power was even greater. A prince had to wait for his father to abdicate or die, and even then, he had to reach the Soul Sea Realm to be considered for the throne. That could take thousands of years. But a Holy Maiden commanded the entire secret order from the moment she was chosen, granting her extraordinary authority.

The Crown Prince offered a cold smile and glanced back at Zane. Even if he didn't think Zane could actually win, he couldn't let anyone think his kingdom was out of fighters.

“Who said my kingdom has no one left?” the Crown Prince snapped suddenly. His eyes locked on Zane, who stood quietly in the square. “Zane Jones, you’re up! Fight for me!”

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

When Zane heard his fake name, he frowned slightly. He shot a look at Randy, who gave him a subtle, almost imperceptible shake of his head. Zane gave a small nod in return but said nothing.

The Emperor looked over, puzzled. “Who is this?”

Before the Crown Prince could answer, Randy stepped forward. “Imperial Father,” he said clearly, “this is my friend, Zane Jones. He's a talent that's recently come through the Crystal Martial House. His strength is no lower than Edwin's. In fact—” he paused, and a rare note of sincerity entered his voice, “—even I am no match for him.”

A murmur ran through the steps. The Emperor's eyebrows rose.

The crowd in the square had its own reaction.

[AMBIENT: CROWDED SQUARE MURMURS]

The square was filled with warriors, none of whom recognized the name. They had no idea who the Crown Prince was talking about.

“If the prince thinks this guy is good enough, he must have some skill,” one warrior muttered.

“So what?” another shot back. “Logan is basically untouchable in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. What’s this guy going to do? Besides, he’s only at the middle stage. I bet he can’t even take one hit.”

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

As the warriors in the square realized Zane was only at the middle stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, a buzz of conversation rippled through the crowd. The consensus was clear: the Harlen Divine Kingdom was out of real fighters and had been forced to send in a nobody.

Even the powerful warriors in the Soul Sea Realm wore strange expressions.

Zane gave a small nod. After watching Logan dismantle fighter after fighter, he already knew the truth of it — there was no avoiding this. He'd seen enough. Time to fight, as himself this time.

The Emperor of Harlen had a hopeful look in his eyes, but it curdled into disappointment the moment he saw Zane’s cultivation level.

Across the square, the Holy Maiden was also staring at Zane, a genuinely puzzled look on her face.
