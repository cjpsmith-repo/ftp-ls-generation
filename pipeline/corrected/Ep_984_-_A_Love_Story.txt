Ep 984 - A Love Story
Word count: 1194
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL LIBRARY]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
When Zane pulled his consciousness back, he found Tia, Cora, and Isla standing dazed beside the massive volume. They’d sent their minds into the book, too, guided by The Eternal Scribe. That meant they were probably lost somewhere inside the Chaotic Storm Chronicle.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
A long moment passed before the three women pulled themselves out, their eyes wide with the same shock he’d felt. They had seen it all.
Truth be told, they hadn’t really understood it either. They’d grasped the words, sure, but the sheer scale of what it all meant was way over their heads. The world described on its pages had left them speechless, wondering just how powerful its author must have been.
Seeing their expressions, the Eternal Scribe beamed. This was the exact reaction he’d been hoping for. As the keeper of the archives, he’d read nearly every volume and took immense joy in sharing them with anyone who passed through. Over the long years, he’d sailed an ocean of books and unearthed countless secrets. He yearned to share his thoughts, but with no one to listen, it was a damn shame.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
So, seeing this group so completely floored by the Chaos filled him with an almost giddy delight. “The Chaotic Storm Chronicle is but a drop in the ocean compared to what these archives hold,” he told them. “The works left by our Master are priceless treasures. It would take you lifetimes to read them all. Come! Let me recommend a few more.”
Zane, Tia, and Isla looked intrigued. Cora, however, just pursed her lips. “It was just some blurry world. Kinda boring, honestly. I bet whatever you recommend next will be just as dull.”
“What?”
The Eternal Scribe bristled. It was as if she’d just called his most prized possessions garbage, and he couldn’t stand for it. “Young lady, what are you talking about?” he snapped. “The books in this archive are as countless as the stars! There’s a book here for everyone. What kind of book do you like? I’ll find it for you!”
[AMBIENT: VAST LIBRARY]
Cora had only skimmed the Chaotic Storm Chronicle. And while she’d been just as stunned as the others, it just wasn’t her kind of book. So when the Scribe offered to find one just for her, she gave him a sly grin. “I doubt you have a single book in this whole place that I’d actually like.”
“Hmph!” The Eternal Scribe snorted, his face falling. “Impossible. You name it, I’ll find it. If I can’t find a book to your liking, then I am not worthy of my title!”
Cora tilted her head. “Romance,” she said. “Stories with feeling. The kind that keep you up at night.” She paused. “I don’t think you have anything like that.”
Zane slowly shook his head. Tia’s expression went wooden. Isla pressed her lips together, suppressing a smile.
Back where Cora grew up, scholars wrote stories like that — not the kind anyone displayed openly, but the kind that passed between women and made them cry. Cora had always had servants fetch them for her when she had nothing better to do. Tia and Isla had never had that kind of time. They were always cultivating, always managing something.
The Eternal Scribe stared at Cora for a moment. Then he threw his head back and laughed.
“Come with me,” he said.
“There’s actually one?” Cora blinked.
He didn’t answer. He simply turned and walked toward a shelf to the side. When he reached it, he raised one finger and gave a book a light tap.
[SFX: MASSIVE OBJECT SLIDING]
The enormous volume slid smoothly from the shelf and settled to the floor without so much as a thud.
Zane watched this with his mouth slightly open. He had needed Dragon Scales just to rip a book free. The Eternal Scribe had done it with one finger. Whatever this familiar was, the Keep had made him well.
Under the Scribe’s guidance, Cora stepped up to the book and poured her perception in. A moment later, she was gone — eyes glassy, utterly still.
Zane, Tia, and Isla exchanged a look. Then, one by one, curiosity got the better of them.
[AMBIENT: QUIET LIBRARY]
[MUSIC: SOFT MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
When they all came back out, the reactions were not equal.
Cora’s eyes were red. Her nose was running. She stood there blinking rapidly, clearly fighting a losing battle.
Isla’s expression was soft and distant — moved, though trying not to show it.
Tia looked thoughtful in a way she clearly hadn’t expected. Her Martial Dao Heart had kept her from losing control, but it had been closer than she’d like to admit.
Zane just looked confused. He had followed the story from beginning to end and felt absolutely nothing. It was just a series of events. He glanced at the three women and decided to keep that opinion to himself.
Then Cora gave up the fight entirely and threw herself into his arms, sobbing.
“The woman in the book — she was too pitiful,” she managed, muffled against his chest. “I’m never reading anything like that again.”
The Goddess Path Tia practiced was incomplete, and she knew she had a long way to go. She was desperate to keep up with Zane’s training and strength, but it felt like the gap between them was only growing wider. If there was a suitable attunement handbook here that could help her close that distance, she was determined to find it.
She’d been thinking about their future together a lot more than Cora had. It had taken the Scribe’s prodding for Cora to even realize Zane was destined for the upper world, but Tia had known for a long time. And she was already preparing to follow him. If she couldn’t keep up, she’d be left behind in the Lower World. There was no way she was going to let that happen. This archive was a golden opportunity, and she wouldn’t miss it.
Isla didn’t think quite as far ahead as Tia. For her, it was simpler. She didn’t need to match Zane’s power; she just wanted to stay by his side.
The Eternal Scribe’s face split into a smug, satisfied grin. “Would you like another?”
Cora pulled back, wiping her eyes. Then, after a pause: “...Do you have more?”
Zane was speechless.
[AMBIENT: ECHOING CAVERN]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
The archive was impossibly vast, a place that felt bigger on the inside. Each bookshelf was the size of a mountain range. They wound their way between them, feeling like they were walking through a cavernous valley. Saul followed quietly at the back. As they walked, the Eternal Scribe gestured around them. “These books were also written by our Master.”
He pointed to different sections.
After what felt like an eternity, the Scribe stopped dead in his tracks. “These,” he announced, “are the attunement handbooks our Master created. This entire row is filled with handbooks designed for women.”
Zane’s eyes lit up. “Are there any with Transcendent Divine Might among them?” he asked.
The Eternal Scribe just laughed at him.
