Ep 1237 - The Crescent Blade

Word count: 986

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: MEDITATION CHAMBER]
Most warriors in the Soul Sea Realm could refill their reserves with just three Sovereign Stones, but Sloane wasn’t most warriors. As the star of House Kincaid, she’d had her Soul Sea personally expanded by the family patriarch to three times its normal size, a necessary modification for her chosen path. The Godly Way of the Five Elements burned through vitality at a terrifying rate.
It was ironic, really. Zane’s Chaotic Soul Sea would have been a perfect match for it, its raw Chaos Energy far more potent than any standard reserve.
[SFX: STONES CRUSHING]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
Needing that energy now, Sloane crushed ten Sovereign Stones in her palm. A flood of pure vitality surged through her, replenishing her nearly-depleted Soul Sea in a dizzying rush.
Zane, for his part, hadn’t moved an inch. He stood right where he was, a faint, unhurried smile on his face, looking for all the world like he had nowhere else to be.
The surge didn’t settle quickly. It took a full fifteen minutes for the storm inside her to finally calm, her Soul Sea slowly stilling back into something resembling order.
She’d expected to use her cultivation and her strength to bring him to heel. Never had she imagined coming out the other side at a disadvantage instead.
As her power returned, she stared at Zane, her mind reeling.
But was it just power? No, that wasn’t right. She’d seen raw, innate strength before. Norton was the perfect example—he and his father were born brutes, berserkers blessed with unbelievable physical might. His father was a World Lord, well-known among the other so-called Thoughtless Minds for his philosophy: get strong enough, and you can solve any problem by punching through it. They believed that with enough pure physical force, they could one day shatter space itself and ascend to godhood.
It was a ridiculous notion, maybe. No one in this era had ever come close, but that was their path. For some, strength was the only answer; for others, not so much. The universe was full of warriors and countless ways to train. Still, Norton had been a true successor, the strongest Soul Sea warrior she’d ever seen in a contest of pure muscle.
And Zane had killed him. He’d killed Norton and Keith both. It wasn’t just strength; it was something else entirely, something that had overwhelmed them completely.
Who was he, really? Sloane knew every one of the young geniuses to come out of Thoughtless Minds by name. This man wasn’t one of them. He wasn’t from anywhere she recognized at all.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
Around them, murmurs rippled through the crowd on the wall, still turning over what they’d just watched. 
Someone pointed out this made the second time now that Sloane had been forced to retreat on the Golden Wind — the first being when Zane summoned the Sacred Mount Lodestone. Was it possible this kid’s strength actually outmatched hers? 
Others weren’t ready to go that far, debating instead what kind of power Zane had used at all — none of them had sensed a trace of True Essence in him, which made no sense for someone capable of that kind of force. Whatever the truth was, one thing was agreed on all sides: Sloane had been the one pushed back.
Sloane’s expression remained composed and coldly beautiful even now, giving nothing away outright — but plenty of the onlookers claimed they could read the truth in it all the same.
Zane let her recover fully before making his next move. He was confident he could beat her eventually, so long as she didn’t turn tail and flee into the main city — and from what he’d seen of her so far, that kind of retreat didn’t fit who she was at all.
As Sloane’s mind raced, Zane was already moving.
[SFX: SWIRLING MIST]
[MUSIC: INTENSE RISING TENSION]
“The Eight Smoky Melodies.” He whispered the words just as Sloane appeared before him.
Sloane had the strength to go toe-to-toe with him — that much was clear now. But brute exchanges like the one they’d just had weren’t really her game. Fighting head-on had never been where she was strongest.
Sloane rose lightly onto her toes. Her crescent-moon embroidered shoes looked like plain cloth at a glance — deceptively delicate, the kind of small, exquisite things that only seemed to belong on her — but now they began to glow faintly, the light spreading toward the tips until it sharpened into a true crescent blade at each foot.
Zane’s guard shot up the instant he saw it. He was confident he could beat her, but when it came to Sloane, even a moment’s carelessness could cost him everything.
A faint smile crossed Sloane’s lips. She crossed her arms lightly in front of her, and at the gesture, the Golden Wind rose to answer.
[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT WHOOSH]
Her Golden Wind technique had her crossing the distance in a heartbeat. She was suddenly right there, hanging upside down in the air above him, her eyes burning with killing intent.
[SFX: BLADE WHOOSH]
With a vicious spin, she brought the Crescent Blade attached to her boot arcing down in a blinding half-circle aimed straight for his head.
[SFX: CROWD GASP]
The whole exchange was so fast the crowd barely registered it. One second, Sloane was crushing stones; the next, she was inverted in the air, bringing a blade down on Zane.
Zane felt a spike of cold relief. If he hadn’t triggered the Eight Smoky Melodies at that exact moment, he’d be headless.
[SFX: DEFENSIVE MIST WHOOSH]
He threw himself backward, but the mist-like defense felt sluggish. It wasn’t like before. Back then, he could effortlessly weave through attacks, the technique always maintaining a perfect three-inch buffer between him and danger. He’d dodged an ogre’s arrow in the wilderness with exactly that much room to spare.
But Sloane… she was on another level. Her speed blew right past the limits of the Eight Smoky Melodies.
[SFX: BLADE CUTTING AIR]
The Crescent Blade shattered his three-inch safety zone, closing in until it was a hair’s breadth from his face.
