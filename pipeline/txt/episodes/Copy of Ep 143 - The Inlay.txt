Ep 143 - The Inlay
Word Count: 1259

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TREASURE] 
 Among the three treasures, the Ambrosia Pill stood as the genuine prize—a rare artifact coveted by cultivators throughout the realm. The two Spirit Weapons, while seemingly worth considerably less in their damaged state, had caught Zane's discerning eye for reasons beyond their apparent value. He had selected them with calculated precision.
Examining them more closely under the soft glow of the palace lamps, Zane confirmed his initial suspicion with a satisfied smile. Their true worth lay not in the weapons themselves but in the magnificent Phoenix and Emerald Crystals embedded within their broken forms. The crystals gleamed with an inner light, pulsing with ancient power that called to something deep within him.
To any ordinary cultivator or weaponsmith, these crystals would have represented nothing but useless fragments of once-great armaments—beautiful perhaps, but functionally worthless. But for Zane, with his unique ability to absorb and transform materials through his body's mysterious runes, they represented an extraordinary opportunity. His heart raced at the possibilities they offered. 
These weren't mere trinkets; they were a veritable goldmine of potential that only he could fully exploit.
[AMBIENT: PALACE INTERIOR]
 After leaving the imperial treasury, Zane was given a large room in the palace. Outside, the army was already sending out crews to clear the rubble left by the demons' destructive rampage. With the battle won, Zane figured he’d leave Silverspire in a couple of days.
The room itself was great—spacious and comfortable. The only downside? It was right next to Yolande Shaw's. Dealing with her was simple, in theory: stay on her good side. Don't provoke her. Or, at the very least, keep enough distance so she didn't get any ideas about locking him in that damn box again.
[SFX: WATER SPLASHING]
 After settling in, he took a long bath, finally washing away the grime of the past few days. 
[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
 Feeling human again, he found a shadowy corner of the room and sat down, pulling the broken Crystal Butterfly from his Storage Ring to get a better look.
From what Zane knew, Legendary Arms weren't typically set with crystals. 
That meant the weapon’s power had to come from the crystal itself, making it more of a Legendary Weapon. The Crystal Butterfly must have been an incredible blade in its day, serving its owner well. A shame it was broken.
"Alright, first thing's first: extract this Phoenix Crystal," Zane murmured, his fingers tracing the gleaming gem embedded in the broken blade. He attempted to pry it loose with careful pressure, but the crystal remained stubbornly fixed in place. A flash of frustration crossed his face as he considered simply tearing it out with brute strength, but the thought vanished as quickly as it had formed. The gem, with its pulsing inner light, was far too valuable to risk damaging through hasty actions.
"Patience," he reminded himself, drawing a deep breath to calm his eagerness. The crystal seemed to mock him with its obstinance, its fiery depths catching the room's dim light. He turned the fragment over in his hands, examining it from every angle while his mind raced through possible solutions. The Phoenix Crystal represented power—power he desperately needed—but destroying it in the process of extraction would leave him with nothing but regret.
There had to be a smarter way to do this, a technique that would preserve the crystal's integrity while freeing it from its metal prison.
[SFX: BOOK PAGES FLIPPING]
 Then it hit him—he remembered reading something about removing crystals in Principles of Forging. He quickly pulled the book from his Storage Ring and flipped through its pages. It was more than just a manual; it was a summary of all of Barrett's skills and experience, filled with the legendary forger's personal tricks and secrets.
Zane found what he was looking for—a page detailing methods for removing embedded crystals. He read the instructions carefully, word for word, but his face fell.
He mumbled, “According to this, I need Soul Fire to get the crystal out… Great. This just got a lot harder.”
[SFX: FLAME RUSHING FORWARD]
He closed his eyes and called up the Black Fire. The moment it slipped free from between his brows, it let out a happy chirp — and shot straight for the broken weapons.
"Wait — those aren't yours to smelt!"
Zane lunged and grabbed both weapons out of reach. The Black Fire hovered in the air, twisting left and right as if genuinely confused.
It had its own mind. It wasn't going to just do what he asked.
He thought for a moment, then snapped off a section of Tronson's shaft — the part with no crystal — and held it out. The Black Fire paused, then darted forward and swallowed it whole. The dense metal melted in an instant, and more than forty drops of Celestial Essence appeared in its wake. 
Zane blinked. Forty drops from the shaft alone. He filed that away.
Then he raised the Crystal Butterfly blade and pointed at the runes, speaking slowly and clearly, the way you'd explain something to a child. "Activate these runes. That's all. When the crystal comes out, the rest of the blade is yours."
The Black Fire chirped — and immediately went for the Phoenix Crystal.
"No — the runes."
He pulled the blade back. The Black Fire hovered, twitching.
This was going to take a while.
[AMBIENT: WORKSHOP AMBIENCE]
[MUSIC: FOCUSED DETERMINATION]
 It took four long, painstaking hours, but Zane finally managed to ‘teach’ the Black Fire what he wanted it to do: flow directly into the Glyphs.
[SFX: MAGICAL HUMMING]
 The moment the Black Fire seeped into the Glyphs on the Crystal Butterfly, they lit up with a faint glow. Zane watched as the Glyphs began to shift, the outer and inner rings spinning in opposite directions. He leaned in closer, seeing the Phoenix Crystal at the center begin to wobble.
He gave the blade a gentle shake.
[SFX: CRYSTAL FALLING]
 With a final twitch, the Phoenix Crystal popped loose and fell to the ground.
“Thank God, it’s finally out,” Zane said, a massive grin spreading across his face. He waited for the Black Fire to withdraw from the Glyphs, then tossed the now-empty blade into the air. 
[SFX: FLAME METLING METAL]
 The Black Fire shot toward the metal like a starving wolf claiming its prize, devouring it in seconds. The blade vanished, leaving behind only a few drops of Celestial Essence.
Once freed from the Glyphs, the Phoenix Crystal had lost its vibrant color and shine. Zane set it aside, his mind already on the next step.
"Okay," Zane muttered to himself, "now I just have to figure out how to embed this thing in my arm."
The mere notion of embedding a crystal into his living flesh sent chills cascading down Zane's spine. Though he existed as a Spirit Weapon in human form, the concept of inserting a foreign object into his arm remained profoundly unsettling. Yet necessity drove him forward—the immense power this sacrifice would yield justified the risk.
Activating the Glyphs etched into his arm required the Black Fire's touch, a dangerous proposition. Despite the flame being an extension of himself, Zane needed extraordinary precision to avoid having its destructive energy ravage his own flesh.
"I've staked everything on greater gambles before," Zane muttered, his jaw clenching with steely determination as he lifted the crystal. 
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
 His eyes narrowed, reflecting the dim light as he positioned the Phoenix Crystal above his trembling arm. "The question isn't whether I should do this—but whether I'll survive what comes next."
