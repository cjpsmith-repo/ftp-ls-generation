Ep 187 - The Bigger They Are, They Harder They Fall
Word count: 1123
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Bob smirked at Zane, his eyes gleaming with malicious confidence as he raised his voice to the swirling white flakes circling his sword.
“Icy Seal! Freeze!”
[SFX: ICE FORMING]
The moment the delicate snowflakes touched the gleaming tip of his blade, they transformed into jagged ice crystals that swarmed up Zane’s right hand like hungry predators. Within seconds, the entire right side of Zane’s body vanished beneath a thick, glistening layer of frost that radiated an unnatural chill.
Zane’s brow furrowed as the biting cold penetrated deep into his muscles and bones. A flicker of concern crossed his face as he channeled his energy, trying to shatter the ice with sheer force. Despite the tremendous power surging through his veins, this peculiar ice wouldn’t yield. Not a single crack appeared on its brilliant surface.
“Ha ha!” Bob’s laughter rang out, dripping with smug satisfaction. He twirled his sword casually, reveling in his apparent victory. “That’s not some common chunk of ice. You can’t break it with brute force.” His eyes narrowed as he studied his immobilized opponent. “So, what’s the plan this time, kid? Even Soul Realm masters struggle to break my Icy Seal.”
[MUSIC: POWER SURGE]
Unfazed, Zane leveled a calm, almost contemptuous gaze at Bob. The corners of his mouth curved into a sly grin as he deliberately raised his left hand. The Emerald Crystal---extracted from the broken spear Tronson and embedded in his left arm back at the Elysian Keep---pulsed once, twice, then erupted with brilliant crimson energy that illuminated his face with an otherworldly glow.
[SFX: MAGICAL IMPACT]
With calculated precision, Zane slammed his left fist against his frozen right arm. A glowing, blood-red sigil materialized on the ice’s surface, its arcane patterns burning with intensity. From this central point, hairline fractures shot outward like a spiderweb, the crackling sound echoing through the chamber as ancient power confronted elemental ice.
The sensation of strength returned to his imprisoned limb. Zane inhaled deeply, savoring the moment before clenching his right fist with renewed determination.
[SFX: ICE SHATTERING]
With a deafening crack, the Icy Seal that had trapped his arm and body shattered into a cloud of crystalline dust.
“That’s\... impossible!” Bob stared, his eyes wide with disbelief, his confident posture crumbling like a sandcastle against the tide.
The Icy Seal represented the ultimate expression of his power, a technique he’d poured countless hours into, sacrificing sleep and comfort to perfect its execution. He’d mastered it only recently, his chest swelling with pride when his father nodded in rare approval. This confrontation marked the first time he’d deployed it against an actual opponent; he’d been certain it would cement his reputation as an undefeatable boss.
His father, a man of few compliments, had assured him that only the Burning Vitality possessed by masters in the Soul Realm could shatter the Icy Seal. No other force---certainly not brute strength---should have prevailed against it. That’s why Bob had been so sure of his victory.
"What the hell is this guy?" he thought, sweat beading on his forehead. "How did he just do that?"
[MUSIC: TENSE DEFEAT]
Bob’s mind reeled, his carefully constructed worldview crumbling around him. For someone whose pride formed the cornerstone of his identity, this defeat threatened his entire identity.
Every move Zane made seemed to anticipate his strategy, as though the younger cultivator could read his intentions before he even formed them. A single counterattack now might prove fatal, and for the first time in his privileged life, Bob found himself paralyzed by indecision.
“Why can’t I beat him?” he questioned silently, panic edging into his thoughts. “I’ve never lost to anyone before---not in training, not in tournaments, not ever!”
Throughout the cultivation world, Bob’s pride was legendary. It was the driving force that had pushed him to advance his skills with such remarkable speed. Second place had always been unacceptable to him; anything less than total victory felt like humiliation.
Now, confronted by Zane’s inexplicable power, that pride lay shattered, leaving him hollow and disoriented. But vulnerability was not an acceptable feeling. As always, it turned immediately into rage.
[MUSIC: RISING ANGER]
Bob’s eyes burned red, his face twisting into a ferocious snarl. A wild voice screamed in his heart, "I will not fail!"
“Go to hell!”
[SFX: BODY FALLING]
But it was no use. The fact was, Bob had already lost.
Bob threw his sword aside. A golden pattern blazed to life on his palm.
Zane felt it instantly — a dense, concentrated power radiating from that mark that made the hair on his arms stand up. Whatever that thing was, it wasn't a technique. It was a talisman. A family trump card, inscribed directly into his hand.
If Bob activated it, this wouldn't end cleanly.
[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT]
Zane didn't let him.
Before Bob's arm could rise, Zane's foot connected with the side of his head.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
Bob swayed once. Twice. Then hit the ground.
The golden pattern on his palm faded.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS]
As the disgraced fighter crumpled, Leo appeared, shaking his head in awe. He had been training in seclusion ever since returning from Dragon Valley. Thanks to his recognized potential, House Brandt had given him access to a private training tower and a steady supply of cultivation potions, so it was no surprise he’d made huge progress.
Still, watching Zane, Leo felt the gap between them was as wide as ever.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
“I may have climbed a few ranks in the Adept Realm,” Leo said, walking closer, “but when it comes to actual combat, I’m nowhere near your level.”
[MUSIC: MOODY VICTORY]
Everyone knew that a fighter’s rank didn’t always equal their true power. It was a basic truth of their world; plenty of high-ranking cultivators had no real fighting ability to speak of. Leo knew that even at the fifth rank of the Adept Realm, he couldn’t have handled Bob. Yet Zane, who was only at the first rank, had just dismantled him. It was clear they were playing two different games.
Leo’s eyes fell on Zane's clothes. “Wait, you’re still an outer disciple?” He was surprised to see him in the plain white robe. “A talent like you would have been promoted ages ago on any other peak.”
Zane just shook his head. “Yeah, I am.” He’d spent so little time at the Skyreach Sect since joining, caught up in one thing after another, that he’d completely neglected his sect duties.
Besides, Zane didn’t really care about the title. Even as an outer disciple on Misty Peak, Isla made sure he was treated as well as any inner disciple.
Leo couldn’t wrap his head around it. In his mind, the mentors at Misty Peak had to be blind not to see Zane's worth. “Well, in that case,” he said with a shrug, “you should just come over to Sunspire Peak.”
It was time this cocky young man take his place among the real contenders.
