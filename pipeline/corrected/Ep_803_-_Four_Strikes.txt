Ep 803 - Four Strikes

Word count: 1096

[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT ARENA]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

Out of all the combatants from the Star Field Divine Kingdom, only six had managed to claw their way into the First Tier.

Even after Titus’s defeat, there were still plenty of warriors itching for a fight. After all, that count of six didn’t even include anyone from the Star Field royal family, and everyone knew their crown prince was practically guaranteed a spot.

In comparison, the Harlen Divine Kingdom—Zane’s home—was considered the weak link. Only Gregory and Edwin were thought to have a real shot at the First Tier. With Gregory already out of the running, that left Edwin as their only hope.

Of course, that was just what the other kingdoms assumed. Zane had only ever shown a fraction of his skill back at the Crystal Martial House, and the other three divine kingdoms had no idea he even existed.

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

At the command of the Star Field crown prince, a warrior named Willard stepped into the ring. If Willard lost to Logan, it would be the second time their kingdom had been defeated by him, right after Titus. The prince had personally put him forward, which meant Willard had to win. Another loss would be a serious blow to their kingdom’s reputation.

But the warriors from the Star Field Divine Kingdom seemed confident. As Willard shot up into the enchanted barrier in the sky, a wave of excitement and hope rippled through his countrymen. The fighters from Wisteria Martial House were the most fired up; though Willard wasn’t one of them, he was from their kingdom, and a victory over Logan would be a victory for them all.

Willard had a plan. Since Logan had boasted he could win in just three strikes, all Willard had to do was survive them.

[MUSIC: FAST-PACED ACTION]

Instead of meeting the first attack head-on, he ran.

Willard was famous for his speed, and his agility was incredible. Logan’s first strike did nothing more than nick the hem of his robe. He then began zipping around inside the enchanted barrier, clearly trying to stay clear of Logan’s Spatial Blade.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

Watching from below, Zane couldn’t help but shake his head. Fortune favors the bold, he thought. If you don’t even have the guts to face your enemy, how can you possibly win? Does this guy really think Logan will just let him go after three strikes? So naive.

The three-strike boast was just Logan showing off, a mind game to pressure his opponent. He’d never said he’d give up if he couldn’t land a hit.

Despite Willard’s frantic maneuvering, Logan was a master of Spatial Laws.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

He took a single step, closed the distance instantly, and swung his sword. The Spatial Blade shot toward Willard like an invisible, venomous snake.

[SFX: SWORD SLASH]

Fear flashed across Willard’s face. He twisted violently in the air, pulling out every trick he knew, but the blade still grazed his neck, slicing off a thick lock of his long hair.

Instead of being discouraged, a grin spread across his face. He’d dodged the second strike.

[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD CHEERING]

The warriors from the Star Field Divine Kingdom roared, fully aware he was just trying to weather the initial storm.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TONE]

From his seat at the top of the steps, the Crown Prince of the Wind Divine Kingdom, who had been silent until now, looked down at the cheering crowd and muttered, “A bunch of idiots.”

Many others, Zane included, felt the same. So what if Willard dodged all three strikes? He couldn’t keep running forever. He had no real path to victory.

A moment later, Logan launched his third attack.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

The Spatial Blade swung right past Willard’s back, tearing a huge gash in his robe. With a final, desperate twist of his body, Willard dodged it. He was completely unharmed.

“Three strikes!” Willard shouted, his face alight with pure joy. He laughed as if he’d already won. “That’s three! You couldn’t beat me! You were wrong!”

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS DRONE]

His celebration was cut short. The Spatial Blade hadn't stopped moving. A fourth strike — quieter than the rest, barely a line in the air — swept across beside him before he even registered it. Then the line disappeared, and Willard's arm was on the ground.

[SFX: CROWD GASP]

Willard stared at Logan, pain overtaken by fury. “Three strikes! We agreed on three strikes!”

“F—” someone in the Star Field section started. “He didn't follow the rules! Three strikes, that was the deal!”

“Despicable! Shameless!”

Logan looked at them with something close to pity. “Which ear of yours heard me say the victor would be decided in three strikes?” he said quietly. “I said three strikes to take you down. Three wasn't enough. So I used a fourth. What exactly is your complaint?”

[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]

The Star Field warriors opened their mouths. Nothing came out.

Logan turned to face the square. “You're too weak,” he said, his voice carrying easily across the arena. “Besides making noise down there, what else can you do? If you have the ability, come up and challenge me.”

“You're only late-stage Spirit-Tribulation!” someone shouted back, unconvinced.

Logan shook his head slowly. “I'm not particularly strong. In fact, I'm quite weak. It's just that most cultivators in this world are like you — trash. My talent is only slightly higher than yours. My effort is only slightly greater. But that's enough to make me a king in front of you.”

[SFX: SLOW CLAP]

War Emperor York brought his hands together from his seat at the top of the steps. “Well said, Logan. Well said.”

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Logan's casual dominance rippled through the crowd. Though he hadn't spoken, the Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors grasped the implication—his pride was reserved for peers in the seven kingdoms. Only when measured against masters in the upper world did he consider himself weak.

His unspoken sentiment resonated with the Soul Sea Realm masters. These ancient warriors, who had lived for over ten thousand years, shared one deep regret: failing to ascend to the upper world.

Within the union, they were undisputed rulers. Yet as warriors, their ambitions stretched beyond the continent. The Soul Sea Realm was never the final goal. After millennia of frustration, many had left to search the endless seas for a path into one of the hundred thousand great worlds.

None had ever succeeded.

Zane had a feeling his low profile wasn't going to last much longer.
