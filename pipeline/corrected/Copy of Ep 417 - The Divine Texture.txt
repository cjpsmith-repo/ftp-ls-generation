Ep 417 - The Divine Texture

Word count: 1263

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]

The Cobalt Dragon's voice resonated through Zane's consciousness, carrying a weight that demanded attention. "First, we must establish the proper foundation. The terminology matters more than you realise." A pause followed, deliberate and meaningful. "What we're about to undertake—it isn't rune-making, despite surface similarities. We're working with something far older, far more fundamental. We call it divine texture."

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

In the Heartlands, runes were everything. They were far more than just letters or symbols; they were the very language of power, more vital to a warrior than the steel in their hands.

This ancient magic was woven into the fabric of life. The grand Attunement Sanctums at Cloud Hall were built from massive arrays of runes. The most prized weapons weren't just sharp, but were etched with runes that infused the blades with incredible power. Runes formed the protective wards that guarded entire cities, and they were the secret to the searing flames used by the greatest alchemists and smiths. All of it was thanks to the rune masters who dedicated their lives to deciphering this power, unlocking wonders the world would never have known otherwise.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]

But the Cobalt Dragon's divine texture was something else entirely—a whisper from a much larger, more powerful world. Its design was so profound, its principles so complex, that the common runes of the Heartlands seemed like children's scratches in comparison. This was power on a level few could even comprehend.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]

"The divine texture…" Zane said, his voice tight with excitement that he couldn't quite hide. "So, can I start learning it now?"

"You can," the Cobalt Dragon replied. "But be warned. Even with the Soul Essences, your spirit is still developing. You may not be able to handle the strain. If you start now, you will only be able to grasp the most basic form. Are you certain?"

Zane’s eyes flashed with determination. "You have to start somewhere, right? If I don't take that first step, I'll never get anywhere."

"True enough," the Cobalt Dragon conceded. "Drawing the divine texture will drain your spiritual power, but in doing so, it will also forge it. This is the perfect time for you to begin."

[AMBIENT: CROWDED HALL]

[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]

Walking into the main hall, Zane was stunned. He'd never seen so many people packed into one place. What really threw him, though, was the sight of several Inner-Core Realm masters scattered among the crowd.

[AMBIENT: CLOUD HALL INTERIOR]

Zane raised an eyebrow. So he'd insulted them. Big deal. It wasn't even him—it was Tia pulling his strings. Did they really have to make such a scene over it? He figured the masters couldn't be from the Elite House.

He was right. They weren't. They were here because of him. For the last two days, tales of his audacious behavior had been the talk of Cloud Hall.

Every disciple in the Elite House knew about the arrogant new Adept who had shown up and challenged them all. The story had quickly spread beyond their ranks, reaching other members, masters, and eventually, even some of the elders.

Toby Dillon was one of those elders.

He had taken a special interest in Zane during his trial on the Cloud Road, seeing a spark of incredible potential in the boy. He'd been prepared to vouch for him, to help him navigate any trouble. Toby genuinely believed Zane was destined for greatness at Cloud Hall.

So when he heard that this same promising kid, now a brand-new member of the Elite House, had challenged the entire hall, he was shocked. It was ambitious, to say the least. And incredibly reckless.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Curious, Toby decided to see the spectacle for himself. As an elder, it wouldn't be right for him to be seen at the grey building, so he cloaked himself in invisibility to observe.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

The moment Zane entered the building, Toby let out a soft gasp. With his immense power, he could perceive what others couldn't: faint, shimmering threads of light clinging to Zane's body.

What's this? He's being controlled!

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL REVELATION]

Toby frowned, his mind racing. The technique was unmistakable—it was puppetry, stripping the target of their free will. And the most infamous puppet master in all of Cloud Hall was Saint Morphen. As a wise and respected elder, Toby connected the dots in an instant.

"I see," he murmured to himself. He knew Zane. The boy had skill, maybe even the right to be a little cocky, but he wasn't stupid. He would never pull a stunt like this on his own.

If Zane wasn't the one behind it, then this whole mess had to be orchestrated by the leader of Cloud Hall herself.

[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD MURMUR]

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Toby understood the performance perfectly, but the disciples in the hall saw only an arrogant rookie who needed to be taught a lesson. For two days, their anger had been simmering. Now, it was about to boil over. Fists clenched, knuckles cracked, and a low growl rippled through the crowd. They were ready to tear him apart.

[SFX: VIOLENT BRAWL]

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC SHOUTING]

The hall erupted. A punch flew at Zane’s head, and he ducked. Another came from his left, and he twisted away just as a third grazed his shoulder. Fists were flying from every direction, a chaotic storm of knuckles and furious shouts.

Zane's mind raced. He couldn't fight them all. Not here. Not now.

A gap opened in the chaos—just for a heartbeat—between two disciples who stumbled into each other. Zane didn't hesitate. He channelled energy into his legs and launched himself forward, slipping through the narrow opening like water through cracked stone.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: FRANTIC CHASE]

Shouts erupted behind him. Footsteps thundered.

He sprinted down the corridor, vaulted over a stone railing, and dropped into the courtyard below. His boots hit the ground hard, but he rolled with the impact and kept running. The crowd's roar faded behind him as he darted through narrow alleys between the floating pavilions, weaving through clusters of startled disciples until the sounds of pursuit finally died away.

[AMBIENT: QUIET DUSTY SHOP]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CALM]

Later, after slipping away from the chaos, Zane found himself in a dusty little shop.

Behind the counter sat an old man, his head bowed low. He held a magnifying glass in one hand, staring intently at a heartstone pendant in the other. He was completely absorbed by the tiny, intricate characters etched into its surface—a strange, ancient language Zane didn't recognize.

"Excuse me!" Zane called out as he stepped inside.

The old man jumped, startled out of his concentration. He looked up, his eyes landing on Zane. A flicker of confusion crossed his face when he saw that the boy was only an Adept. Most patrons of this market were at the Soul Realm level or higher, not least because you had to be able to fly from Cloudhaven to even get here. An Adept in Cloud Hall was rare enough; one in this market was unheard of.

"What do you want?" the old man asked, his tone sharp. He was clearly annoyed by the interruption and saw no reason to be polite to a mere Adept.

"I need to buy some materials and a brush for drawing runes," Zane said, his eyes scanning the shelves and avoiding the old man's irritated glare.

"Materials and a brush?" The old man let out a short, mocking laugh, his gaze turning scornful. "You want to draw runes? You?"
