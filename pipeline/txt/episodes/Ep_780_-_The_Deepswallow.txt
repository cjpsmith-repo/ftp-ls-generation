Ep 780 - The Deepswallow

Word count: 1042

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[SFX: WHOOSH]

 Through the connection forged by their slave seal, Zane felt a jolt of pure terror from Francis. Something had gone horribly wrong. It was the kind of primal fear you feel when a predator materializes right in front of you. 

As Francis shot into the sky, Zane was right behind him.

[AMBIENT: DEEP OCEAN WAVES]

 It was a good reminder that in this endless universe, humans were far from the only ones in charge. The sea, especially, was a different world. It covered far more of the planet than land, and because of that, it swarmed with tens of thousands of times more beasts. They thrived in the vast, deep oceans, with more resources and space to grow than any land-dweller could ever dream of.

Zane remembered his first trip into the Celestial Tomb of Vesper, not far from The Heartlands, where he’d run into beasts as strong as Life-and-Death Realm cultivators. But that was nothing compared to this. Here, in the center of the Chaotic Ocean of Stars, there were creatures that could rival Soul Sea Realm warriors, and whispers of things even stronger.

[SFX: MASSIVE WATER SPLASH]

[SFX: RUMBLING EXPLOSION]

 Just as they were climbing, a massive black shadow blotted out the red vortex below. With a sound like a collapsing mountain, a colossal creature erupted from the water and, impossibly, launched itself into the air.

[SFX: MONSTROUS ROAR]

 “What is that?” Zane yelled, staring back at the monster closing in on them. A cold dread washed over him. 

"A Deepswallow," Francis said, his voice strangled. "There are four of them in the Chaotic Ocean of Stars. Keep going up — this thing can jump hundreds of thousands of feet!"

"Four of them? You actually know that?"

"Everyone in the Divine Kingdoms knows. Years ago, the four kingdoms sent Soul Sea Realm masters into this ocean to steal one of their cubs. One of the Deepswallows was so enraged that it chased them all the way to the continent and didn't stop until it had reduced a third of the York Divine Kingdom's territory to rubble. Ever since then, the moment one of these things senses a human, it doesn't hesitate."

Zane was speechless. He’d heard these things were powerful, but this was something else entirely. 

While he was still processing it, the beast had already cleared the water, its whole enormous body pouncing straight for them.

[MUSIC: FRANTIC CHASE]

 “Higher! Get higher!” Francis shrieked, his voice choked with panic.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: HIGH ALTITUDE WIND]

 They rocketed upward, punching through the cloud layer and into the deep sky.

“Can that thing really jump this high?” Zane asked, glancing down. But a thick blanket of clouds completely blocked his view.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE BURST]

Just as he thought they might have lost it, the creature burst through the clouds again, still gaining on them. The sheer force of its ascent tore a gaping hole in the cloud cover, roiling the air around them.

“Oh my god! Faster, keep going!” Zane shouted, the fear finally cracking his voice.

He kept climbing, but then he saw it—huge, violent gusts of wind blowing straight toward them. Golden lines swirled within the currents, and he recognized them instantly.

[SFX: VIOLENT WIND GUSTS]

[AMBIENT: STORM WINDS]

 “Storm-winds ahead!”

These weren't normal winds. These were the storm-winds of the upper sky, said to crush anything caught in their grip. Zane had seen them before, in a way. To train his Stellar Body, he’d used the gravitational pull of stars to project his Soul Sense across millions of miles. Every single time, his consciousness had to pass through a layer of these very winds.

But that was just his Soul Sense. This time, it was his actual body.

[AMBIENT: HOWLING STORM WINDS]

[SFX: WIND ROAR]

 He knew these winds could tear apart almost anything. Even if his body was as tough as a top-grade Mythic Weapon, he wouldn’t survive a direct hit. As the gale roared closer, Zane twisted his body in a sharp, mid-air turn, diving to get behind the deadly current.

[SFX: MONSTROUS ROAR]

Behind them, the Deepswallow kept charging upward — straight into the storm-winds. The golden currents tore across its back, opening massive gashes. Blood cascaded down through the clouds like a waterfall. The beast glared at them with enormous, hate-filled eyes, then dropped its head and plunged back into the clouds below.

Zane steadied himself, still catching his breath. "Your kingdoms have quite a grudge with that thing."

Francis wiped the sweat from his forehead. "They stole its cub. What did they expect?"

Zane shook his head. He looked up. "We should fly above the clouds. Stay out of its range."

Francis's face tightened. "Above the clouds is worse."

"Worse how—"

Before he could finish, Francis nodded toward the distant sky. Far off in the sea of clouds, several massive dark shapes drifted toward them — enormous predatory birds, their outlines vague but unmistakable.

[SFX: DISTANT SHRIEK]

Neither of them said another word. They dove back below the clouds and kept flying east.

[AMBIENT: EERIE OCEAN]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

 As they flew on, they passed through dark red clouds of blood left behind by the injured beast. Their journey across the Chaotic Ocean of Stars was one danger after another. 

[MUSIC: ENCHANTING SIREN SONG]

 At one point, a cluster of misty reefs appeared on the surface, home to sirens whose beautiful singing drifted up to them, promising rest for weary travelers.

Zane and Francis were both nearly lured in. 

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

 It was only when Zane got closer and saw faint, black figures standing on the rocks that the Cobalt Dragon in his mind roared him back to his senses. He didn’t know what those terrifying shadows were, but he knew that getting any closer meant death.

Francis, however, just smiled faintly and kept flying toward the reef.

Zane had no choice. He activated the slave seal, yanking Francis back from the brink and forcing him to follow.

[AMBIENT: CALM OCEAN WAVES]

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL ORCHESTRAL]

 After that close call, they traveled with extreme caution. 

 More than ten days later, after navigating the treacherous, monster-filled waters, Zane finally spotted it in the distance: a continent.
