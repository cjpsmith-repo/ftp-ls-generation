Meanwhile, Zane barreled toward Cloudhaven, his body cutting through the air like an arrow. For days and nights without rest, he had flown without pause, burning through a small fortune in supreme Aether crystals to maintain his relentless pace. The crystals' energy hummed through his meridians, keeping exhaustion at bay even as his muscles screamed for respite.
Ever since his return from the main continent, one desire had consumed him—reaching Cloud Hall. He'd never imagined the journey would entangle him in so much trouble. Each delay had tested his patience, each confrontation had drained precious time he couldn't afford to lose.
But those obstacles felt distant now, fading like nightmares at dawn's approach. As the sun crested the eastern mountains, its golden rays spilled across the landscape, transforming the world from shadow to light. There, on the horizon, Cloudhaven materialized through the morning haze—its towering spires and floating pavilions unmistakable even from this distance.
A genuine smile spread across Zane's face, the first in days. His chest tightened with an emotion he hadn't allowed himself to feel during the grueling journey: relief. The sight of those familiar structures stirred something deeper than mere satisfaction. Despite everything—the challenges, the enemies, the impossible odds—he'd made it back.

[SFX: OMINOUS DRONE]

He pushed his speed, flying straight for Cloudhaven. He’d personally set up the sect-protecting arrays around Cloud Hall, magical defenses that made it impenetrable to any outside force. Other than the Corrupted Divine Kingdom, no one should have been able to touch it. But the Corrupted Divine Kingdom was busy attacking the Ethereal Spirit Sect; Maddox’s forces wouldn’t be anywhere near here. Besides, if Cloud Hall had been in real trouble, House Kensington would have sent word.

There was no point in guessing. As soon as he breached Cloudhaven’s airspace, Zane headed straight for the Cloud School, one of the more respected sects operating under Cloud Hall’s banner.
Two Inner-Core warriors met him at the entrance.
"State your business," one said — then registered Zane's cultivation level and went still.
"I'm looking for your Sect Chief," Zane said. "Where is Cloud Hall?"
"Who's asking?"
"Zane. Cloud Hall."
The two men looked at each other. The panic on their faces was unmistakable.
The first said nothing. The second broke after a moment. "Thirty miles northwest."
Zane was already moving.
Behind him, the first warrior turned on the second. "Why did you tell him? Our family's plan — it's finished. We're all going to be implicated."
"What choice did I have?" the second shot back. "He's Spirit-Tribulation Realm. If I'd kept quiet, he'd have gotten it out of me anyway, and I wouldn't have survived it."
A long silence. "This is bad. Now that he's back..."
He had a pretty good idea of what was happening. The warriors' reaction told him the trouble wasn't external. An outside attack wouldn't have made them panic at his return. Someone had been counting on him not coming back, which meant Tia was the one in danger.

The protective barrier surrounding the floating fortress was like a massive, shimmering eggshell. Zane shot toward a control node at its base, reached out, and channeled a sliver of energy into the Matrix. A gap shimmered open, just wide enough for him to slip through. Once inside the hidden space, he rushed toward the heart of Cloud Hall.
His defenses were the best in The Heartlands. Not even the Corrupted Divine Kingdom could have broken through the arrays he’d built. An attack from the inside, however, was a different story.

After mastering the Goddess Path, Tia had quickly recovered her full strength. And after completing the cultivation of nine rebirths, she was finally ready to ascend to the Life-and-Death Realm. For someone like Tia, reaching the realm itself wasn’t the hard part; the real challenge was surviving the Life-and-Death Crises that came with it.
Anyone who broke through to that level had to face these crises, and their intensity was unique to each warrior. The more potential you had, the stronger you were, the more terrifying the trials became. Tia’s journey along the Goddess Path had been incredibly difficult, but it had granted her far greater strength than her peers. It also meant her Life-and-Death Crises would be more dangerous than anything an ordinary warrior could imagine.
