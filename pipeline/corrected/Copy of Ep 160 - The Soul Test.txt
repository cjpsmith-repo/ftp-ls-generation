Ep 160 - The Soul Test
Word Count: 1029
[MUSIC: INTENSE SUFFERING]
[SFX: MOANS OF PAIN]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL STAIRCASE]
Zane's soul writhed as though it was suspended over an inferno, each lick of invisible flame searing through his essence. Despite his legendary resolve, the torment was worse than anything he'd endured. He collapsed in a heap, his body convulsing uncontrollably as guttural moans escaped his lips. 
[SFX: FALLING TUMBLE]
 As he tried, his foot slipped on the two-thousandth step, sending him tumbling back down to the one below. The Soul Thorns pricked him, a sharp, familiar pain, but it was almost a relief compared to the fire.
He looked up at the endless steps and sighed. "This is so much harder than I thought. How am I supposed to get past this?"
Zane had a will of iron; he never backed down from a challenge. Sheer determination had gotten him through countless impossible situations. But for the first time, he actually felt doubt. That unearthly flame was terrifying... completely overwhelming.
 "I can't quit now, even if I wanted to," Zane murmured. There was no way out of The Elysian Keep. Besides, Saul promised he’d be accepted if he could just pass this test.
But how could he climb any higher when he couldn't even stand on the two-thousandth step?
It didn't make sense. Zane's soul was strong—it could handle almost any pain. He shouldn't be afraid of a little fire. He'd already endured the black flames inside his own mind, and this was nothing compared to that. So why couldn't he handle it now?
"No, this can't be right!" he muttered.
The Soul Test was supposed to be dangerous, sure. Difficult, but not impossible. There had to be a way through.
[MUSIC: REVELATION MOMENT]
Standing on the 1,999th step, Zane frantically searched his mind for a solution until a revelation struck him. "Of course! How could I have been so blind? The Serene Void technique... it completely slipped my memory," he whispered, a flicker of hope igniting within him.
The Serene Void had come to him unexpectedly during his mastery of the Psyche Spike—a remarkable state of absolute detachment where consciousness retreated into a perfect Sphere of Nothingness. In this transcendent condition, his essence became merely an impartial observer watching his physical form from afar, untethered from sensation or suffering.
"If I can immerse myself in that state now," Zane reasoned, his fingers trembling slightly with anticipation, "I could transcend this agony." His breathing steadied as determination hardened his resolve. This wasn't just an option—it represented his sole path forward, the only conceivable means to conquer that final, forbidding step.
[AMBIENT: MEDITATIVE SILENCE]
Zane closed his eyes and surrendered to meditation, reaching deep within himself for that familiar emptiness he had once discovered. The chaotic world around him gradually receded, its sharp edges softening into insignificance. When he finally opened his eyes again, everything appeared through a veil of detachment—colors less vibrant, sounds muffled as though traveling across great distances. His own physical form seemed merely a vessel, disconnected from his essence, a curious object he observed rather than inhabited.
"Alright," he whispered, his voice echoing strangely in his ears as though spoken by someone else entirely, "let's try this again." A peculiar serenity washed over him, replacing the earlier panic with cool determination. The pain that had threatened to consume him moments before now hovered at the periphery of his awareness—acknowledged but powerless. 
In this transcendent state, Zane felt neither fear nor anticipation, only a profound stillness as he prepared to face whatever awaited him on the final step.
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL ASCENSION]
Enveloped in The Serene Void, Zane ascended to the next step, his movements fluid and detached as though his body responded to commands from a distant consciousness.
[SFX: SOUL TEARING]
The instant his foot connected with the two-thousandth step, a new agony seized him—not the familiar burn of fire, but the sensation of his very soul being violently torn apart. The pain transcended physical boundaries, reaching into dimensions of suffering he had never imagined possible. His essence, the very core of his being, seemed to splinter into countless fragments, each separation bringing fresh waves of torment that echoed through the emptiness he had so carefully cultivated.
[SFX: AGONIZED SCREAM]
[AMBIENT: SUPERNATURAL HORROR]
 "Ahhh!" he screamed as a skeletal figure materialized and began to chew on him.
The agony of being devoured, of his very essence being consumed, was beyond words.
This was an illusion, but if he couldn't break free, it would destroy him all the same.
As Zane screamed, a voice cut through the agony—Saul. "Hold on to your belief!"
"My belief?" Zane gasped, his mind reeling. "What is my belief?"
[SFX: BONES CRUNCHING]
 Even as the skeleton gnawed on him, Zane desperately tried to think.
His father's death had hardened Zane's heart, turning it to stone. He’d faced down phantasms of savage beasts without flinching. But when an illusion had taken his father's form, it had struck a nerve, leaving him raw and vulnerable.
“My belief...” The pain made it almost impossible to think, but he fought through it, clawing for the answer. "It's The Unceasing Pursuit of Mastery. I chose this path. I have to see it through. Nothing can shake my determination."
The thought became a mantra, a shield.
"Even if my own father stood in my way, I wouldn't hesitate! I wouldn't give up! No one will ever stop me from achieving mastery!"
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
 With that realization, his eyes began to glow with renewed strength.
[SFX: MAGICAL DISINTEGRATION]
 The skeleton's grip weakened, its form dissolving until it vanished completely.
Zane gasped for air, his body slumping in relief. If his resolve had wavered for even a second longer, his soul would have been torn to pieces.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TRANSFORMATION]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT]
 Wasting no more time, he pushed himself to his feet and started climbing again. When he finally reached the top, the world around him dissolved, the stone steps melting away until he found himself standing back in the main hall.
Saul was there, a wide, thrilled smile on his face. "Congratulations! You passed the Soul Test. You are now the temporary owner of The Elysian Keep."
