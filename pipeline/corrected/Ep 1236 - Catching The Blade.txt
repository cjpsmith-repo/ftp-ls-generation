Ep 1236 - Catching The Blade

Word count: 979

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUND ATMOSPHERE]
On a grand scale, a warrior’s strength was measured by their Aura and raw power. But true mastery wasn’t about brute force. It was about something subtler: absolute control, the ability to perfectly manage your own power and speed.
The classic test was the bird-in-hand trick. 
[SFX: BIRD WINGS FLUTTERING]
A warrior would hold a bird, and just as it tried to fly, they’d shift their palm just enough to make it lose its footing and fall back. It was a neat display, but even beginners in body refining could learn the “bird-stopping skill.” 
The real masters could place a bird on their head or shoulder and keep it from flying away. An even harder test involved flies or mosquitoes. Because they’re so much smaller, you had to sense the exact instant they tried to take off and remove the surface supporting them. It took incredible precision.
[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDING STRINGS]
But stopping a mosquito was child’s play compared to what Zane was doing right now.
[SFX: METALLIC SWORD RAIN]
[SFX: BLADE WHOOSHING]
Sloane had already seen how remarkable he was during the sword rain, but as she lunged, she finally understood the secret to his skill. It was impossible. While circling her blade, Zane was maintaining a perfect three-inch distance from *every single one* of the descending golden swords. It was a flawless, terrifying display of absolute control.
She’d never seen anything like it. *How was he doing that?* The question burned in her mind as she finally recognized him for what he was: a worthy opponent.
Zane didn’t give her another second to process. 
[SFX: SWORD SWING WHOOSH]
He shot backward and swung Thundergust right at her.
But Sloane didn’t dodge. She stood her ground, her entire focus locked on his movements, determined to unravel the secret of his perfect control. 
[SFX: METAL BLADE CAUGHT]
As the blue arc of his sword descended, Sloane raised her right hand—and caught the blade.
[SFX: CHAIN LINKS GLITTERING]
The Five-Element Finger Chain glittered on her right hand, its links connecting her fingers in a protective mesh. With it, she caught his sword and held it fast.
The five rings along her fingers flared with a strange light as she gripped it — a color eerily close to the one that had radiated off the Sacred Mount Lodestone. It was the power of the Five Elements fusing together, the same principle behind the Lodestone itself. But her cultivation wasn’t there yet; whatever degree of fusion she could manage now was nothing next to the real thing.
Zane was stunned. He was always the one catching his opponent’s blade, not the other way around. He’d never expected this to happen to him. It was a real shame he hadn’t had time to acquire any Mystic or Relic-Class weapons on his journey. 
He’d planned to ask Livingston to forge something for him to absorb, but the man had been completely engrossed in his work, consumed with turning a sword blank into a true blade. Livingston had barely left The Armory in years.
For a moment, Sloane almost told him to stop right there. Forcing this much of her Five-Element power out of her was no small feat — she’d wanted to save it for whoever she’d face next in the Illusion Battlefield, not waste it here. But before the words could leave her mouth, the power surging up through Thundergust cut them off entirely.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Bit by bit, she felt herself being forced downward, sinking beneath him despite her grip. 
It wasn’t a position she was used to — among cultivators at her level, she was the one who looked down on others, not the reverse.
A flicker of pure stubbornness crossed her otherwise cold expression. She was a Kincaid. There were too few people alive who could actually challenge her, and she wasn’t about to let this be the moment that changed.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE BUILDING]
Deep within her, something answered. 
Sloane’s Inner World held a Sea of Vitality unlike any other Soul Sea Realm cultivator’s — a rarity so unique that no one else alive shared it. Where most warriors could only draw on their reserves gradually, she could empty the entire sea in a single burst. She did exactly that now, pouring everything she had straight into the Five-Element Finger Chain.
The five rings blazed brighter. For less than a breath, Sloane’s figure rose again, climbing back above him.
Even so, with Zane’s power now doubled, Sloane was on the losing end again. 
It wasn’t a fair fight to begin with. Zane had already lit six thousand Dragon Scales when their swords first met — and the instant he felt her Five-Element power push back, he ignited six thousand more. Twelve thousand Dragon Scales, blazing at once, doubled his output in a heartbeat. 
He could have gone further, too. His body could handle nearly forty thousand Dragon Scales lit at once if it came to it — the only thing holding him back was how little Celestial Essence he actually had on hand.
She finally realized how foolish it had been to fight him head-on. Worse, her own Vitality was completely gone. 
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
“This should be over, right?” Zane murmured with a faint smile.
[SFX: SWORD ENERGY SURGE]
Sword intent flared to life along Thundergust, surging down the blade and shooting straight for Sloane.
If it hit, she’d be killed and forced to respawn back in the main city. It wasn’t that he was merciless—he just needed the Illusion Points, and that meant he had no choice.
“Hmph.” Sloane snorted. 
[SFX: WIND WHOOSH]
Before Zane’s sword intent could even cross the distance, a Golden Wind swept over the spot where she stood, and she vanished. Not even Zane could track that kind of speed.
She reappeared a safe distance away, tapped her wrist, and pulled out a handful of Sovereign Stones. 
[SFX: STONES CRUSHING]
Crushing them in her palm, she released their Vitality as a white fog and instantly absorbed it, replenishing the energy in her body.
Relying on speed alone, she was practically untouchable. 
But in a contest of pure strength, Zane clearly had the advantage.
