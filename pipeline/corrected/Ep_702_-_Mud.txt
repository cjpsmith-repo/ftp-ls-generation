Ep 702 - Mud
Word count: 1129
[AMBIENT: SWAMP ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
After flying over countless mountains beyond Ethereal City, they finally reached a vast plain shrouded in a thick, swirling mist. They had arrived at the largest swamp in The Heartlands—The Dreaming Marshes.
The Dreaming Marshes teemed with beasts so fierce that no one from the Heartlands had ever charted its depths and lived to tell the tale.
In the open sky, Maddox had Zane dead to rights, making escape all but impossible. But down here, in the tangled, murky terrain of the marshes, Zane finally had a shot at losing him for good.
As Zane plunged into the swampland, a sneer twisted Maddox’s lips. “You think you can hide in the Dreaming Marshes? Nice try, Zane, but your luck just ran out!”
[SFX: MAGICAL CRACKLE]
Maddox pulled out a Message Rune and crushed it in his fist. As the sigil began to burn away into ash, he spoke into the dying embers.
“Simon, a rat just scurried into the Dreaming Marshes. I’ve already taken care of the Septuplets of the Ethereal Spirit Sect, and it won’t be long before I crush their entire sect. Tell Darius to fall back three hundred miles. Get your people over here!”
Zane had made a fatal mistake. Running here, of all places? He wasn't finding a sanctuary; he was walking into his tomb.
[SFX: FLAME WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
A moment later, a small flame flickered to life behind Maddox’s ear as Simon replied through his own rune. A low chuckle echoed from the magic. “The Dreaming Marshes is my backyard,” his voice crackled. “Don’t worry. I’ll help you catch your rat.”
Of course, their alliance was one of convenience. Simon was nearly his equal in power, and they weren’t exactly friends. Simon’s goal was to establish a sacred place; Maddox dreamed of building his own divine kingdom. As the two most powerful warriors in the Heartlands, it only made sense to work together when their goals aligned.
Still, Maddox knew they were just using each other. A showdown was inevitable once they got what they wanted. He was sure Simon had an ace up his sleeve, but he was just as confident he could take him down when the time came.
For now, though, there was no conflict. Their ambitions ran surprisingly parallel, and the partnership was working out perfectly.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SWAMP]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
The second Zane crossed into the Dreaming Marshes, he dropped his altitude, flying so low he was practically skimming the warped ground. The terrain was a deathtrap. Shimmering pools dotted the landscape, their waters looking crystal clear but hiding a lethal secret—a single sip could kill even the most powerful creatures.
Zane flew low, doing his best to hide his scent. To conceal his Vitality and aura, he even had Lavender retreat inside his body, her energy compressed to a speck of red light. The effort forced him to slow to a crawl, but it was his only chance.
Inside the marshes, he could no longer sense Maddox's aura. That meant Maddox couldn't lock onto him either. As long as he kept moving carefully — suppressing his presence, widening the gap one slow step at a time — slipping out the other side was possible.
He was still turning that over in his mind when Cora opened her mouth to speak.
Zane's hand shot out and covered her mouth before a single sound escaped. His expression was hard.
"Not a word," he said, his voice barely a breath. "Unless you want to die."
She went still. Her brown eyes fixed on him — cold, unafraid, and not particularly impressed.
Then Zane felt it. A wave of perception rolling through the marsh like a slow tide, methodical and searching. Maddox.
[SFX: SPLASHING MUD]
[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]
Without a word, Zane grabbed her and pulled them both down into the nearest quagmire. He didn't use a drop of Vitality — any fluctuation would give them away. The mud swallowed them whole.
Cora twisted against him immediately. She'd never been dirty a day in her life, and the black mire was already coating her face, her ears, her hair.
"You're not afraid of death," Zane breathed against her ear, "but you're afraid of a little mud?"
She stopped fighting.
They lay at the bottom of the quagmire, motionless, as Maddox's perception swept over them and moved on. Neither of them surfaced until it was gone.
When Zane finally dragged them up onto the bank, Cora was unrecognizable — her white robes turned completely black, mud packed into every crease and fold. She looked nothing like the sheltered daughter of House Kensington. She looked like someone who had been through something.
She was still catching her breath when Zane went rigid beside her. His hand came up — a sharp, silent warning.
In the grass a few feet away, a small black snake was watching them. Its scales were glossy and smooth, catching the dim swamp light with an almost oily sheen. A feather-like crest rose from its head.
Cora recognized it instantly. Everyone in the Heartlands had heard of the Black Feathered Snake. Its venom was legendary — fast, total, and without antidote. Even a War Sage struck by one would be dead before they hit the ground. Cultivators died every year trying to harvest its poison for market.
[SFX: HISSING STRIKE]
[MUSIC: SHARP PERCUSSION]
The snake lunged. Zane's arm was already moving — he thrust it in front of Cora, and the fangs sank into his forearm instead.
When he pulled his hand back, he grabbed the snake with it — caught it behind the head, pulled it free, and tore it apart with his bare hands. He dropped the pieces into the grass like they were nothing.
"I'm fine," he said quietly.
Cora stared at him. She'd heard the stories — the things he'd survived, the things he'd done. But watching him shake off Black Feathered venom like a mild inconvenience was something else entirely.
She didn't say anything. She just followed when he moved.
[SFX: SPLASHING MUD]
She’d always had access to the best resources for her training. That, on top of being a heaven-level talent, meant she’d reached the Inner-Core Realm without ever needing to test herself in dangerous places like this.
This, in a way, was the first real thing she’d ever experienced.
[AMBIENT: MARSH WETLANDS]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Zane moved slowly through the marsh with Cora trailing him. Terrified of being detected, neither of them dared to use their Vitality to fly.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL HARP]
As she stumbled through the muck, her eyes suddenly lit up. A small, clear pond shimmered ahead—a chance to finally wash off the filth.
She hurried to catch up and tapped Zane on the shoulder. “I want to take a bath.”
Zane shot her an amused look, his eyes flicking from her to the pristine little pond. He bent down, plucked a leafy plant from the ground, and casually tossed it into the water.
[SFX: SIZZLING HISS]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
A sharp, sizzling hiss cut through the air. The moment the plant hit the surface, it turned black and shriveled as smoke poured from it. The crystal-clear water was pure poison.
He turned back to her, a small smile playing on his lips. “Still want to take that bath?”
