Ep 519 - Hand Him Over
Word count: 999
[AMBIENT: TELEPORTATION CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
After leaving the tournament, the Cloud Hall delegation moved straight to the teleportation array in the city — a massive formation built and operated by the World Merchants' Guild, which Cloud Hall had the privilege of using. In a flash of light, they were gone.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
They emerged inside Cloud Hall's gates, where the sect's elders had already assembled at the main entrance, lined up in a formal welcome.
[SFX: CROWD SPEAKING IN UNISON]
"We welcome the Hall Master," the elders said together, bowing their heads.
But Tia's feet had barely touched the ground before she was already moving.
"Cloud Hall is now on full alert!"
[AMBIENT: SURPRISED MURMURS]
The elders exchanged uneasy glances. Full alert? What could have possibly happened at a martial tournament to warrant that?
"Hall Master — what happened out there?" Emanuel Drew, one of Cloud Hall's most senior elders, called after her.
Tia didn't slow down. She threw the answer over her shoulder without so much as a glance back.
"A powerful enemy is coming."
[AMBIENT: TENSE HALL]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Both men knew it would take a group with serious power—and no scruples—to challenge Cloud Hall.
“Could it be the Ethereal Spirit Sect?” one of them wondered aloud.
“The World Merchants’ Guild, maybe?” another elder guessed, trying to pinpoint who could have shaken Tia so badly. But neither guess felt right. Cloud Hall had always maintained a diplomatic, even respectful, relationship with both powers. Beyond those two, they couldn't think of any other major players who'd have a reason to come after them.
But before they could press the pacing woman for answers, Tia’s expression hardened. The discussion was over.
Her voice cut through the tension, cold and sharp. "Send Cedric to my palace!" Cedric was Cloud Hall's foremost runic master, an Inner-Core Realm cultivator whose lifelong dedication to runes had made him indispensable.
[AMBIENT: WIND GUST]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TRANSITION]
Her robes billowed behind her, her hair catching the breeze, as she strode away and vanished into Cloud Hall.
Sensing the shift, her entourage fell in line behind her, not daring to so much as glance at the elders. Zane and the others in her inner circle kept pace with Tia, but Saint Morphen, one of Cloud Hall's senior Spirit-Tribulation elders, hung back.
[AMBIENT: TENSE HALL ATMOSPHERE]
Having traveled with Cloud Hall's elite disciples, Saint Morphen figured it was on him to explain things to the crowd Tia had left in her wake. He scanned the elders' questioning, desperate faces, let out an exasperated sigh, and finally spoke.
"[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
"This time, Cloud Hall's enemy is Maddox."
[SFX: CROWD GASPS]
A wave of sharp gasps rippled through the crowd. A few of the elders looked like they were about to faint.
[SFX: CHAIR SCRAPING]
"What?!" Emanuel shot to his feet. "Why in the world would you provoke a monster like him?"
[MUSIC: DARK FOREBODING THEME]
Maddox was the kind of man whose name alone was enough to make ordinary people tremble. He could be a saint one moment and a demon the next. If you stayed out of his way, Maddox wouldn't give you a second thought. But if you managed to piss him off, you wouldn't live to tell the tale.
The disciples of Cloud Hall went to the Martial Tournament expecting to boost their reputation. Instead, they’d brought home a terrifyingly powerful enemy who was probably already on his way to burn them to the ground.
[AMBIENT: TENSE COUNCIL CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
And then the dam broke.
[SFX: ERUPTION OF ANGRY VOICES]
"This is outrageous!" one elder burst out, slamming a fist down. "Ten thousand years of Cloud Hall's legacy — about to be dragged through the dirt because of some nobody in the Soul Realm?"
"Offending Maddox for the sake of one boy!" another elder spat. "Does the Hall Master think nothing of the millions of warriors who depend on this sect? Is she trying to doom us all?"
A third voice rose above the others, sharp and bitter. "I said it from the start — she was too young for this position. Talent isn't everything. We never should have backed her."
"What exactly happened out there, Saint Morphen?" Emanuel Drew demanded, stepping forward to plant himself in front of him, arms crossed. "I want a full report. Don't leave a single detail out!"
Saint Morphen frowned at the elder's tone. But as disrespectful as Emanuel was being, the crisis at hand was far more important.
He recounted everything — the tournament, the confrontation, how Tia had stepped forward to shield Zane from Maddox's fury.
[AMBIENT: HEAVY SILENCE]
When he finished, the council chamber felt like the air had been sucked out of it.
[MUSIC: DARK, DELIBERATE UNDERSCORE]
Emanuel's face had gone dark. He stood very still for a long moment, turning it all over in his mind. Then, slowly and deliberately, he said:
"There is one way to end this before it starts."
The room leaned in.
"Hand over Zane."
Saint Morphen said nothing. He just stared at the floor. The bitter truth was that Emanuel wasn't entirely wrong — handing Zane over might be enough to extinguish Maddox's rage and spare Cloud Hall the coming storm.
But would Tia ever agree to that?
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
Saint Morphen already knew the answer. If Tia had been willing to hand Zane over, she never would have stepped in front of him in the first place. And once Tia's mind was made up, she was as stubborn as a mule. That meant there was no point in even discussing how to avoid the coming fight. No one — not the elders, not even the previous leader, Elder Imogen — could change her mind once it was set.
As Saint Morphen stood there in silence, he couldn’t shake the feeling that Tia must have seen something he didn't when she’d defended Zane at the match. Deep down, his loyalty was with Tia. And he had a gut feeling that Zane was an investment that would pay off for Cloud Hall.
As for the cluster of elders already plotting to make a scene and embarrass themselves in front of the top disciples, Saint Morphen couldn't have cared less.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS WALKING AWAY]
He just turned and walked away, hands shoved in his pockets, leaving them to their scheming. Ultimately, their theatrics were Tia's problem to deal with.
