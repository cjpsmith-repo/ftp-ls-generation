Ep 706 - Another Way
Word count: 1019
[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND PALACE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Everyone had their own agenda.
Zane wanted out — out of the marsh, out of The Heartlands, away from all of it with Cora still breathing.
Maddox wanted Zane dead and Cora in his hands.
And Simon? Simon wanted Cora dead before Maddox could ever touch her. As for Zane himself — dead or alive, it made no difference to Simon. Everything Zane carried would go to Maddox anyway.
But Cora was another matter entirely.
Ten miles disappeared in moments. Below, half-hidden by moss and overgrowth so thick it choked out almost every trace of light, a dark opening yawned in the earth. The underground palace.
Zane didn't slow down. He pulled Cora in with him and dropped into the dark.
[SFX: WHOOSH — CAVE ENTRANCE]
[AMBIENT: DRIPPING CAVE — TOTAL DARKNESS]
Above, two figures materialized out of the fog and hung in the air over the cave mouth.
Maddox's eyes swept the area. Zane's Vitality signature had vanished — cleanly, completely, as if he'd been swallowed whole. His expression shifted.
"He went into the underground palace." Simon's voice carried just the right amount of surprise.
Maddox looked at him. "How did he know it was there?"
Simon shook his head slowly. "He probably didn't. He was out of options and spotted the entrance. Lucky instinct."
The answer was smooth. Too smooth.
Maddox was a suspicious man by nature — it was part of what had kept him alive this long — and the timing sat wrong with him. Zane had been running in one direction and then abruptly changed course, diving straight into a hidden entrance that most warriors in The Heartlands didn't even know existed. That wasn't luck. But he had no proof, and Simon's face gave him nothing. He filed it away.
He turned to the Spirit-Tribulation warriors who had caught up behind them and did a quick count.
Two short.
"Where are the others?"
One of the warriors stepped forward. "One was killed by Zane in the marsh. The other strayed into a Water Serpent's territory. It took him before anyone could intervene."
Maddox's eyes sharpened at the mention of the Water Serpent. A creature powerful enough to take a Spirit-Tribulation warrior without warning was worth knowing about. He said nothing, but the interest was there. He'd deal with it later.
"It doesn't matter," he said. "He's in the palace now. There's nowhere left to run." He moved toward the entrance. "We go in."
Simon followed, his expression unreadable. Inwardly, the satisfaction was cold and clean. The underground palace was his trump card — a labyrinth he had explored twice at great personal cost and found nothing in but death. No treasures, no ancient secrets, just corridors that went nowhere and dangers that came without warning. He had barely made it out both times. Zane was good, but he was carrying a girl with no combat experience and no knowledge of what was down there. The palace would do what Simon couldn't do openly.
He almost felt sorry for the boy.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ENTERING CAVE]
[AMBIENT: PITCH BLACK LABYRINTH]
[MUSIC: TENSE DARK STRINGS]
Inside, the darkness was total. No light filtered down from above. The palace had been built by hands long dead — ancient stonework, winding corridors, paths that split into three and four directions every dozen yards. It was a maze with no map and no logic Zane could find.
He moved by perception alone, choosing forks at random. The only strategy available to him was simple: the more turns he took, the harder he was to track. He kept moving.
Cora stayed close behind him, one hand brushing the wall.
The silence stretched. Then —
"It's so dark in here," Cora said. "I don't like it."
Zane said nothing.
A few more turns. Another fork. He picked left.
"Can you find a way out? I don't want to stay here."
Still nothing from Zane. Lavender hovered nearby in the dark, silent for once, watching Cora with cold eyes. She had plenty to say. She just didn't say it.
Cora stopped walking. "Zane. I'm talking to you."
He stopped too. For a long moment, he just stood there, one hand against the stone wall, his back to her. When he finally spoke, his voice was flat. "There is no way out. Not right now."
"Then what are we doing?" she asked.
"Buying time." He paused. "And thinking."
He was quiet for too long. When the words came, they came without drama, almost like he was reading from a ledger. "Maddox needs three women with the Violet Core Physique to complete his Soul Harvesting Ritual. He already has two." He let that land. "You're the third."
Cora went still.
"My sister is one of them," Zane said. "He's had her for a long time. That's why he needs you. As long as you have the physique — and as long as you're a virgin — you're exactly what he needs to start the ritual."
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL PIANO]
The silence that followed was heavy and complete.
"So that's why," Cora said quietly. Not a question.
"That's why." He pushed off the wall. "I'm going to go back and hold him off as long as I can. You take every fork you find and keep moving. Put as much of this place between you and him as possible."
Cora stared at his back in the dark. "You'll die."
"Maybe."
She didn't say anything for a moment. She knew he was headed on a suicide mission.
He started moving, and she called after him. "Wait."
He stopped.
Cora's voice, when it came, was steady — not the voice of someone frightened, but of someone who had already made a decision and was simply announcing it. "If I'm no longer a virgin, Maddox can't use me for the ritual."
Zane turned around carefully."That's right."
"Then it solves the problem." She met his eyes in the dark. "And you don't have to go die for nothing."
[MUSIC: QUIET STRINGS]
Lavender made a sound that might have been a laugh and turned away, her red light dimming to almost nothing.
Zane looked at Cora for a long moment. She didn't look away.
"Are you sure?" he said.
"I just told you what I want," she said. "Don't make me say it again. Besides, it might not be so bad."
