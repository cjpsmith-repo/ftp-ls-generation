Ep 899 - Asking to Die

Word count: 1071

[AMBIENT: DARK VOID]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The red ripples of her consciousness snapped shut, trapping Zane’s soul inside her mind. He was a prisoner.

[SFX: ECHOING LAUGH]

A soft laugh echoed through the darkness.

“You want to run?” she asked, her voice dripping with amusement. “Your soul hasn’t even reached the Nightjade level. You really think you can get away from me?”

The casual dismissal sent a chill through him. A Nightjade Fighting Soul was nothing to her. He was in serious trouble.

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

Zane threw a few desperate attacks against the edges of her consciousness, but it was useless. His own soul’s power was limited, and he didn’t dare burn through it all. Realizing he wasn’t getting out of here anytime soon, he stopped fighting and let himself float in the void, gathering his thoughts.

After a moment, he projected his voice into the darkness. “There’s no point in keeping me here,” he said, trying to sound reasonable. “Why don’t you just let me out?”

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

Silence was his only answer. With his soul disconnected from the outside world, he couldn’t control her body. He was completely blind and powerless, trapped in the pitch-black prison of her mind.

He tried again, pushing his consciousness out, hoping to get any kind of reaction. “Hey! Say something! You were stuck in that Crystal Coffin for who knows how long, and I’m the one who got you out! Besides, your legs are still chained up. If you want to be completely free, you need to let me go so I can break those chains for you!”

Still nothing. No matter what he said, she wouldn’t respond.

Zane swore under his breath, cursing himself for ever trying to enter her mind. He could have easily taken over any of the warriors back on the thirteenth floor, but no, he’d gotten greedy. He’d wanted this strange, mysterious girl, and now he was paying the price.

The fighters on the thirteenth floor were strong, but they were only a little more powerful than the average kingdom leader. They were nothing compared to this woman, who was clearly at the Spirit Supreme Realm. He knew the others had wanted her body, too, but he was the one who’d actually made it inside. He supposed it was only natural for her to be a little unhinged after being locked away for so long.

Then the red ripples came for him.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

[SFX: RUSHING ENERGY]

They shot toward him from all sides, fast and relentless. Zane’s soul dodged — left, right, pulling away — but the ripples were endless, and the space was finite. Every direction he moved, more came. Within moments, they had filled the entire mind, leaving him nowhere to go.

[SFX: CRUSHING GRIP]

A massive red hand reached out from the ripples and closed around his soul. He felt himself yanked downward, pulled deep into the darkness.

[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]

[MUSIC: STRANGE ETHEREAL THEME]

He fell. As he fell, he seemed to pass through clouds. Lightning crackled in a vast sky, blazing white bolts twisting through the air. Below him, a wide sea churned and heaved. An island sat at its center, blanketed in emerald green, and as he watched, the lightning struck the forest, and a fire broke out.

The red hand set him down at the center of the island.

Around him stood a group of primitive people — leaf clothing, tree-trunk spears, expressions that carried no particular intelligence. They were making strange sounds. Among them, four carried a simple palanquin of wooden sticks, and from the palanquin a pair of slender legs dangled.

A pair of golden eyes looked down at him. That face wore a faint, mocking smile.

It was the girl. Her soul.

The primitive people carried her forward and set her down at her signal.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS BASS]

With his options dwindling, Zane decided he had no choice but to threaten her.

“Where is this?” he asked first.

The girl’s smile widened. “Warriors of the lower realm really are ignorant. You don’t even know this place?”

Zane frowned. He had already guessed it. He just hadn’t wanted to be sure. 

He knew the theory — at Life-and-Death Realm, Vitality begins to liquefy; at Soul Sea Realm, it opens an inner sea; at Spirit Supreme Realm, that inner world solidifies further and begins to give birth to life of its own. He had only heard about it. Seeing it was something else entirely.

He kept his voice flat. “Spirit Supreme Realm. An inner world born from your dantian. It’s nothing I haven’t heard of before.”

The girl’s entire demeanor shifted. She clapped her hands, genuinely pleased. “That’s right!”

“Why did you bring me here?” Zane asked.

“You tried to take my body,” she said, blinking. “You failed. That makes you my captive. Do you understand your situation?”

Zane looked at her. Whatever personality she might once have had, years sealed alone in that Crystal Coffin had done something to it. This was going to be complicated.

“Let me out,” he said, his tone hardening. “My friends are waiting for me. If I don’t show up, they might destroy your body.”

[SFX: SUBTLE MAGICAL SHIMMER]

A flicker of interest. She finally frowned. “Your friends? You mean those little guys at the Soul Sea Realm?”

He replayed her words in his head. Little guys at the Soul Sea Realm. Those “little guys” were War Kings and War Kings, ancient monsters who had lived for tens of thousands of years. To him, they were titans. To her, they were nothing.

His stunned silence seemed to amuse her. “You think they can hurt me? Not after you opened my coffin with that Black Star pickax. I’d like to see them try. Let them come.” 

She paused, then let out a wild, almost manic laugh. “Oh, look. It seems some idiot is actually coming over right now.”

The girl’s presence stilled for a second.

[SFX: MAGICAL FLASH]

When she focused again, a golden light flashed in her pupils.

“One of those little Soul Sea Realm guys,” she announced with a lazy drawl. “I already killed him.”

[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]

“What?” Zane was floored. How could anyone kill a Soul Sea Realm warrior that fast? It shouldn’t be possible. “You’re lying,” he said grimly.

The girl’s presence sharpened, turning cold and dangerous. “Lying? Why would I lie about something like that? I didn’t want to kill him, but you threatened me. I had to teach you a lesson.” Her voice was laced with chilling satisfaction. “I wiped out his body and soul completely. Anyone who dares try to steal the body of a dragon-blooded is just asking to die.”
