Ep 317 - The Famous Evan
Word count: 1142
[AMBIENT: LARGE STADIUM CROWD]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ENTRANCE]
Evan’s entrance was so bizarre—and frankly, so ridiculous—that the hundred-thousand-plus spectators in the stadium were completely stunned.
I mean, the guy could fly. That put him at the Soul Realm of power, at least. And you don’t get to that level without some serious talent.
So why was he acting like such a complete moron?
"Who the hell is this clown?" one of the disciples finally blurted out, unable to hide his confusion.
"Hey, quiet down! Don’t call him that," another disciple whispered back. "Didn’t you see how respectful Patrick was being? If I’m right, that’s the famous Evan. He’s way stronger than Patrick!"
"Isn’t he supposed to be in his secret attunement chamber? What’s he doing here? Is he done with his training already? Wait, does that mean the All Peaks Competition isn’t over yet?" the first disciple asked, his voice now buzzing with excitement.
After watching Zane beat Patrick—or, well, watching Patrick forfeit—everyone was just waiting for an elder to announce the final rankings for the Skyreach Sect List. No one in their wildest dreams expected the legendary Evan to just show up at the very end.
[SFX: CROWD EXCITEMENT]
A thrill shot through the crowd. They might get a showdown between Zane and Evan after all.
Each of the hundred thousand disciples from the Skyreach Sect had paid one point—worth about two Crystal Cubes, enough to support an average family for a year—to watch the tournament. They definitely didn’t want the show to be over just yet.
But seeing Evan acting like a total airhead, the audience worried if the fight would even happen.
"Hey, Patrick! You look like you just kicked a puppy. What’s with the long face? Did you lose or something?" Evan asked with a dopey grin.
He had been qualified to compete based on his power level before, but that was a while ago. When he walked out of his secret attunement chamber, his power had skyrocketed from the Third Level of the Soul Realm all the way to the sixth. This not only blew past the competition’s power cap but also put him just shy of the realm’s highest level. Basically, it wouldn’t be long before he was on the same level as the Guardians, or even some of the weaker elders.
On top of that, everyone knew Evan had a solid foundation. He wasn’t like the many Guardians and elders in the Skyreach Sect who, unable to break through on their own, had to rely on refined pills to reach their current strength. When it came to actual combat prowess, he might not be that far behind them.
Given all that, letting Evan compete now would be completely unfair.
"No, Elder Gates! I’m a disciple of the Skyreach Sect, through and through! Why are you trying to kick me off the Roster?" Evan argued, trying desperately to convince everyone. "Please! I have to compete in this year’s All Peaks Competition, I just have to! My name is still on the participants’ list, right? Even though I had a breakthrough?"
Elder Gates hesitated. Evan's case was definitely a special one. He was, in fact, registered on the list of eligible disciples, but he'd been in his secret attunement chamber this whole time and only showed up at the very end.
"Just let him fight! What’s the big deal? It’s just one more match!" a disciple yelled.
"We want to see it! Evan is a disciple of the Skyreach Sect, too. Why can’t he compete?" another disciple shouted, not about to lose the chance to see another awesome fight.
"Yeah, I second that! Why not? It’s just one more match!" another added loudly.
[AMBIENT: STADIUM CROWD NOISE]
In a flash, disciples all over the stadium started kicking up a fuss.
From their point of view, getting to watch one more match—especially one between Evan and Zane—would be a huge bonus. After all, they had each spent a point on the tournament, and even if Evan looked like an idiot, there was no denying he was incredibly powerful.
[SFX: CROWD CHANTING]
Immediately, the hundred thousand disciples of the Skyreach Sect got hyped, and a chant began to ripple spontaneously through the stadium: "One more fight! One more fight!"
Looking down at the roaring crowd, Elder Gates started to waver, but something still held him back. Of course, Zane had already pulled off so many miracles. But since he and Evan weren’t even in the same league, power-wise, Zane had no chance of winning. It wouldn’t be a fair fight.
In fact, he had a nagging feeling Evan was just trying to bully Zane.
Seeing so many people supporting him, Evan stared at Zane arrogantly, like he was looking at a lamb for the slaughter. He turned back impatiently. "Elder Gates, look at them! Everyone’s waiting. Come on, make the call already!"
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
Just then, the situation took a dramatic turn.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ENTERING RING]
Elara, who had been standing outside the battle ring, suddenly walked inside and yelled furiously, "Evan!"
When Evan heard his name, his eyebrows shot up. He knew that voice. Then it hit him. He remembered exactly who that voice belonged to, and his face fell, twisting into a bitter grimace. He asked in a trembling voice, "You... you’re..."
"If you insist on fighting," she declared, her voice cutting through the noise, "you’ll compete using only pure swordplay."
Elder Gates had seen Zane’s swordsmanship firsthand. While he wasn’t the strongest of the elders, he held a respectable rank and knew a thing or two. He knew Zane’s swordplay was formidable, so he immediately supported Elara’s proposal. He was clearly trying to set a trap for Evan, too.
Standing next to Elder Gates, Patrick hid a smile. He had fought Zane, so he knew exactly what was going on. This time, Evan’s walking right into it. He has no idea how good Zane’s swordplay really is. Sure, Evan would crush him in a real fight, using all his power. But pure swordplay? Hahaha... Evan, you’re about to get absolutely wrecked, he thought, trying not to laugh out loud.
[MUSIC: CONFIDENT SWAGGER]
"Compete in pure swordplay?" Evan replied carelessly, rubbing his nose. "I, have never backed down from a sword fight with anyone! All right! This final match will be a competition of our swordsmanship skills only!"
Previously, when Elara had seen Zane condense his sword spirit, she’d guessed that his skill with a blade wasn’t half-bad, but she had no idea just how formidable he truly was. She had proposed the condition simply because she didn’t want to see Evan steamroll Zane with his overwhelming power.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS FORESHADOWING]
What she didn’t know was that she had inadvertently set the perfect trap for Evan—just like she had years ago when she’d defeated and humiliated him.
