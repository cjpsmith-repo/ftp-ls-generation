Ep 342 - Gifts and Bribes
Word count: 1086

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
[SFX: LIGHT SCANNING SOUND]
Normally, when Sten’s left eye scanned someone, it revealed two distinct colors: black and white.
The black areas indicated vulnerabilities—weak points in a person’s defenses.
Everyone had them. Even the most flawless defensive cultivation harbored a vulnerable spot. Hit that precise spot, and you could take anyone out of the fight for good.
But when he looked at Zane, Sten saw only a blinding, uniform whiteness.
Not a single trace of darkness marked any portion of his body.
This wasn’t the first time it had happened. Sten’s Shatter-Sight usually excelled at picking out an adversary’s weakness, but there were exceptions. If he was dealing with an opponent of overwhelming power—such as a master at the peak of the Soul Realm—or someone wielding a high-rank defensive weapon, his sight was useless.
Zane, however, was only a Level Three Adept. So why couldn't he see anything? Did Zane truly not have any weaknesses?? Or was Sten’s own power level too low to find them?
[SFX: HEARTBEAT INTENSIFIES]
The uncertainty gnawed at his core like a ravenous beast. Sten ran his fingers through his hair, frustration evident in the tightness of his jaw and the furrow between his brows.
Sten’s Shatter-Sight was a remarkable gift—a talent coveted by many—but it was hampered by his own limitations. Unfortunately, he lacked the natural talent or powerful soul necessary to elevate it to its full potential.
Each day, this bitter truth haunted him, a constant reminder of what could have been. If he could only reach the same level as Richard or Jill from the Skyreach Sect, with their formidable foundations and refined techniques, he might actually stand a chance against someone of Finn’s caliber. The thought of such achievement both tantalized and tormented him, dangling just beyond his desperate reach.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REFLECTION]
After Prince Ray took him under his wing, Sten had desperately consumed every available panacea, forcing his premature ascension into the Soul Realm. This reckless approach left his foundation critically compromised—a shortcut that now served as his prison, virtually guaranteeing he would remain trapped in the Soul Realm for the rest of his life.
His survival in the prince’s entourage depended entirely on his Shatter-Sight ability, the singular talent that made him valuable enough for Prince Ray to retain his services. The bitter irony of his situation wasn’t lost on Sten; his greatest gift had become both his salvation and his limitation.
Confronting an opponent he couldn’t read was not just bizarre but deeply disturbing; it challenged the very foundation of his identity and worth.
What Sten didn't know was that Zane had meticulously transformed his body into a Top-Grade Spirit Weapon, leaving him with virtually no weaknesses. Sten’s specialized vision could only penetrate fighters beneath the Soul Realm threshold, or weapons weaker than low-grade Spirit Weapon quality.
Someone called out impatiently from the crowd, their voice cutting through his mounting anxiety. "Sten, what are you doing? Haven’t you found this guy’s weakness yet? If you see it, get in there and fight!"
[SFX: CROWD GRUMBLES]
The spectators’ expectations weighed heavily upon him. His Shatter-Sight was legendary, letting him spot an opponent’s fatal flaw at a glance. They had witnessed countless fighters fall to devastating disadvantage once their weaknesses lay exposed. Their confidence in his ability remained unshaken, even as Sten’s own certainty crumbled silently within.
[SFX: VIOLENT IMPACTS]
Meanwhile, Zane dispatched his challengers with effortless brutality, tossing them across the arena as though they were nothing more than discarded toys. Each swing of his arm carried devastating force, his expression betraying neither mercy nor restraint as he unleashed his full power.
[SFX: BONES CRACKING]
[MUSIC: GRIM FIGHTING]
The first unfortunate youth who faced Zane’s wrath escaped with minor injuries, a rare mercy in the unfolding spectacle. Those who followed, however, met a far grimmer fate. Bodies crumpled under his relentless assault, leaving contestants doubled over and gasping, crimson spattering the ground as they coughed blood through broken lips. The sickening snap of fracturing bones echoed throughout the chamber, drawing winces even from hardened observers.
Prince Ray, maintaining a façade of concern, handed out expensive healing pills with calculated generosity. Despite his apparent benevolence, the prince’s eyes gleamed with satisfaction at the entertainment Zane provided. Even with such precious remedies, the battered challengers faced a fortnight of painful recovery ahead—a steep price for their ambition and pride.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
Finally, no one else dared to challenge Zane. Not one of Prince Ray's young followers could touch him.
The older masters, those at the peak of the Soul Realm, could have beaten Zane, but they wouldn't stoop to "sparring" with someone so far beneath them.
After clearing the floor his own way, Zane bowed again to the prince.
"Your Highness," he said, his voice cold as stone, "if I cannot see the Sky Princess, then I’ll be on my way to find her myself."
Ray, however, was perfectly calm, his eyes seeming to see right through Zane's tough exterior. He simply nodded. "Zane, I have something to show you. After you see it, you can see her."
[SFX: CLAPPING HANDS]
Ray clapped his hands, the sharp sound echoing across the marble floor.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
The soft patter of approaching footsteps filled the palace courtyard, accompanied by the distant chirping of birds and the gentle rustling of ornamental trees swaying in the breeze.
[MUSIC: EXOTIC MYSTERIOUS THEME]
A moment later, a procession of elegantly dressed maids emerged from behind the palace columns, each carrying ornate boxes of varying sizes.
The containers gleamed with intricate golden embroidery, hinting at precious contents within.
Haunting, unfamiliar melodies drifted through the air as three foreign women glided into view behind the maids. They wore dresses made of the finest silver that hugged their forms, the woven metal catching the light with each graceful movement.
Unlike the women of The Eastern Territories, these strangers possessed striking features—deep-set eyes that sparkled with mystery, pale skin that seemed to glow from within, and impossibly long, elegant legs.
The atmosphere in the courtyard shifted instantly. Every male gaze locked onto the women as though bewitched. Several noblemen visibly struggled to maintain their composure. Even the most disciplined among them shifted uncomfortably, betraying their interest.
Zane’s expression darkened as he observed the spectacle. His jaw tightened, disgust evident in the hard line of his mouth and the cold fury building behind his eyes. This blatant attempt to distract him with flesh felt not only transparent but deeply insulting.
He turned to Ray, his voice cutting through the silence like a blade of ice. "Your Highness, what the hell do you think you’re doing?"
