Ep 737 - I Quit
Word count: 1028
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Kieran tried to step in, but it was already too late. Zane was past the point of listening to anyone.
So much for Toby’s advice to sweet-talk her. Zane had walked in planning to talk things out with Tia, but his notoriously short fuse had already been lit. She wouldn’t listen, and instead of hearing him out, she’d thrown a few cutting remarks his way that set him off completely.
Tia saw the thunderous look on his face and sneered. "What's the matter? Did I strike a nerve?"
"A nerve?" Zane shot back, a cold smile spreading across his lips. "No, you’re right about one thing. You might be the leader of the Cloud Hall, but as of right now, I quit."
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMUR]
A collective gasp sucked the air from the room. In a matter of seconds, the situation had spiraled from a tense argument into a full-blown crisis, and it looked like a real fight was about to break out. Tucked away in a corner, Owen stayed quiet, figuring it wasn’t his place to get involved. He was just stunned. He couldn't believe the kid actually had the guts to talk to the head of the Cloud Hall like that.
Tia just scoffed. "You quit? You think it’s that easy? This isn't some club you can just walk away from.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BASS]
Anyone who leaves without permission gets hunted down by our full force."
At that, Kieran, Hobbes, and Burns finally moved, physically putting themselves between the two. Kieran even raised a finger to his lips, silently pleading with Zane to just shut up.
But Zane wasn't backing down. "I'd like to see them try."
The words hung in the air, and just like that, all the fight seemed to drain out of Tia. Her defiant expression crumbled, her eyes suddenly welling with tears.
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
[AMBIENT: WIND WHOOSH]
But instead of letting them fall, she unleashed a blinding wave of Vitality and shot out of Oracle City in a flash of light, vanishing over the horizon.
"Miss!" one of the elders cried out.
"After her!" another shouted. They were all worried; her injuries weren't life-threatening, but she still needed a few days of recovery in the city.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
Without a word, Saint Morphen transformed into a streak of light and took off in pursuit.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
Zane, on the other hand, just let out an angry snort and stormed out of the hall in the opposite direction. Cora, of course, followed close behind him. She knew she couldn't solve his problems or offer any real comfort—she just wasn’t that important to him—but she followed anyway.
Behind them, Kieran, Hobbes, and Burns stood in silence for a moment.
"What exactly is their conflict?" Kieran said, shaking his head. "The Hall Master of the Cloud Hall was actually crying in front of all of us."
"They weren't arguing like a superior and a subordinate," Hobbes said.
Kieran nodded slowly. "No. They were arguing like a couple." He looked at the others. "That's the only explanation that makes sense."
The three of them shook their heads. Of all the times for this.
The Guild had arranged a courtyard residence for Zane — small, clean, well-appointed. He dropped into a chair and stared at nothing.
Cora quietly brewed a pot of tea and set a full cup in front of him without a word. After a moment, she leaned in from behind. "Zane."
"What?"
"You're not being magnanimous enough."
He looked at the ceiling. Then he nodded. She was right. He knew she was right. He was twenty years old, a warrior through and through, but when it came to this — to people, to feelings — he was completely out of his depth. He hadn't handled it well. He hadn't handled any of it well.
Lavender materialized beside him, glancing at Cora with a faint, sardonic look. "Isn't this what you wanted?"
Cora met her gaze steadily. "It is," she said. "But he's not happy."
"How do you know?"
"I just know."
Lavender said nothing more.
For the next two days, Zane did almost nothing. He sat in the courtyard, stared at the window, and thought about Tia's face when the tears came — the ones she hadn't let fall. He drank twenty jars of wine trying to dull the feeling. It didn't work. His body burned through it without registering so much as a buzz. The courtyard smelled like a tavern.
Cora wanted to go to him. She held back. She wasn't the cause of the knot in his chest, and she knew she wasn't the one who could untie it. She stayed in her room and waited.
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY]
[MUSIC: DETERMINED MARCH]
Two days later, Zane made his break official. He signed up with the World Merchants' Guild, immediately took a mission, and threw together a small team.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON ROAD]
Leaving Cora behind in Oracle City, he headed east—straight toward the front lines where the Corrupted Divine Kingdom was slowly devouring territory.
Normally, wars between the Sects followed a predictable script. You’d have the weaker fighters from both sides clash first, grinding each other down until one side gained a slight edge. That was the cue for the mid-tier combatants to join the fray, paving the way for the top-tier warriors. The whole thing was a slow, strategic buildup, and once the best of the best finally faced off, the war usually ended quickly and decisively.
There was also the "Lightning War" gambit, a high-risk move where leaders went all-out from the start to take down the enemy's best fighters, ending the conflict before it could truly begin. All sorts of tricks and strategies were fair game.
[SFX: DARK RUMBLE]
But the Corrupted Divine Kingdom was different.
[MUSIC: UNSETTLING MYSTERY]
For starters, they were using a bizarre strategy of not even deploying their top fighters. They were sending only their weakest members into battle — and somehow, that was enough to overwhelm the combined forces of the Cloud Hall and the World Merchants' Guild.
Zane was about to find out exactly what that meant.
