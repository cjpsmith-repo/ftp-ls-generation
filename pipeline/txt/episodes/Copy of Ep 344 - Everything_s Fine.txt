From the moment Zane Lowell stepped into Silversun Hall, his eyes looked past the opulent chamber’s gilded columns and vaulted ceilings. The murmur of aristocratic voices, the shimmer of fine silks, the political maneuvering—none of it mattered to him. His heart pounded with a singular purpose: to find Isla Novak.
Prince Ray watched Zane’s searching gaze with calculated interest. His extensive network of spies had already told him about the connection between the young cultivator and Isla. He knew she had served as Zane’s mentor at Misty Peak, guiding his remarkable ascent through the cultivation ranks.
What perplexed Ray, however, was the intensity burning in Zane’s eyes.
This depth of concern suggested something far beyond the typical respect between student and mentor. Was there an emotional entanglement he could exploit? A vulnerability to manipulate?
Whatever the reason, it didn’t really matter. All Ray needed was for Zane’s concern to be real, and it was.
[SFX: BACKGROUND CHATTER FADING]
Zane tuned out the background chatter, the noise fading into a low buzz.
He was getting tired of this. Finally, he turned to Ray.
"Your Highness," he said, his patience wearing thin, "It’s been a while. Where’s the Sky Princess? Are you just messing with me?"
"Relax." A slick smile flickered across Ray’s face again. He was clearly enjoying making Zane wait. "The Sky Princess is nearby; let’s go see her."
[MUSIC: TRANSITION]
[AMBIENT: QUIET HALL]
[SFX: SOFT LIGHT AMBIENCE]
Nearby, a soft, gentle light filled the Radiant Hall. The once-bustling place was now eerily quiet, staffed only by a few essential servants. No one else was allowed anywhere near it.
[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLY STRINGS]
Being under house arrest was its own special kind of hell. Bored out of her mind, Isla had taken up embroidery, learning from an old servant.
To pass the time, her nimble fingers were now guiding a silver needle and colorful thread through a swatch of white satin. Isla was a quick study and had picked it up with ease.
Intricate patterns bloomed across the fabric—neat, flowing, and vibrant. She'd already surpassed her teacher.
But at the moment, her hands were still. She stared blankly at her work, the familiar weight of depression settling over her. Trapped. Powerless.
Is the All Peaks Competition over? she wondered. I wonder how Zane Lowell did. Did he even make it into the top hundred? Honestly, if Zane managed to place in the top one hundred, she would be unbelievably proud. The thought brought a faint, wistful smile to her lips. If Zane had really pulled it off, Misty Peak’s standing would improve dramatically.
She had personally selected him from among thousands of students. It was proof that she truly had an eye for talent, a knack for finding a diamond in the rough. Her loyalty was to Misty Peak; being a mentor there was a life. This was just a cage.
If she'd never left, she wouldn't be tangled up in the political games of the Imperial City. But because of her brother, staying away wasn’t an option. After all, Farrell, the Crown Prince, was her own flesh and blood.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
Her prolonged confinement had made Isla raw and hypersensitive. When she looked up to find Zane standing beside Ray, a sharp pang of betrayal struck her heart, stinging her eyes with unshed tears.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
It felt like she'd discovered a prodigy, poured her heart into training him, only to watch him sign with the rival team. A wave of nausea rolled through her, even though she knew it was foolish to have pinned all her hopes on one young man. In the grand scheme of things, Zane alone couldn’t have had much of an impact anyway.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
"Lady Novak!" Zane’s face lit up the second he saw her. He was blissfully unaware of the storm of betrayal raging inside her. He was simply glad to have confirmation of what he had desperately hoped—that she was safe in the middle of palace intrigues.
In Zane’s heart, Isla transcended the role of mentor; she was like the protective older sister he had always longed for but never known. During those merciless days of training and tribulation, her steadfast guidance and unwavering faith in his abilities had been the only thing to get him through. This devotion was why he’d rushed to the Palace the moment he returned to the Imperial City. He had to show his respect for her and make some attempt to repay her kindness.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
But something was different. She stood stiffly and looked at Zane as if he were a stranger. Her eyes were like ice, and when she spoke, her voice was just as cold. "Yes? Is there something you wanted?"
In her mind were thoughts of betrayal and disappointment. So you’ve chosen Ray’s side. It’s a surprise for sure, but maybe I’m the fool for not seeing it coming. I guess I wasn’t that important to you after all. What are you even doing here? Why did you even bother coming to see me? To show off your new royal connection? "Are... are you okay?" Zane stammered, his voice barely audible as shock washed over him. The coldness in her eyes pierced through him like winter frost, leaving him completely bewildered by this unexpected hostility. His mind was racing to understand what could have possibly transformed his beloved mentor into this distant stranger before him.
[SFX: BITTER LAUGH]
An icy smile stretched across Isla’s lips, never reaching her eyes.
"I’m fine. Perfectly fine. I grew up in the Palace, after all. This is my home, my sanctuary. Why wouldn’t I be fine?" Her voice carried a brittle edge that contradicted her words.
Zane stood utterly lost, his mind struggling to bridge the chasm that had suddenly appeared between them. He studied her frigid expression, searching for any hint of the warmth she’d once shown him, trying desperately to understand what he was missing. But no matter how hard he examined her guarded features, he couldn’t understand what had transformed their relationship so dramatically. How could she possibly claim to be "fine" while essentially being a prisoner in her own home?
"But..." Zane trailed off, his voice catching in his throat. His hands hung uselessly at his sides, the words he wanted to say evaporating before reaching his lips. He was at a complete loss.
What the hell was going on with Isla?
