Ep 1119 - The Shocking Sword Strike

Word count: 1106

[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
To Julius, Zane was just another fool overestimating his own strength, reckless for even trying to fight him. But for Zane, this was a chance to finally test his limits.
He knew the power of a Spirit Supreme Realm warrior was on another level entirely compared to someone from the Soul Sea Realm. Back in the Lower World, he’d seen the seven paragons of the Demi-Immortal Race in action, and their strength had left him stunned, in awe of what they could do. This guy, though? He was just a city lord in the Soulbound Sect. He might be at the same rank, but Zane doubted he was anywhere near the same level as those seven. And he was damn curious to find out for sure.
[SFX: SHATTERING SHADOW]
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE FLIGHT TAKEOFF]
The moment his Nether God Shadow shattered, Zane shot backward, blasting right through the city’s No-Fly Ban without a second thought.
*Even stronger than I thought,* he realized. *This guy’s just a city lord, so his talent can’t be anything special. But that blood spear… its power is way beyond what I was expecting.*
His mind raced, processing the new data. 
[SFX: WIND BLADES SLICING]
[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACKLING]
As the blood-red longspear lunged for him again, he flicked his own sword, sending another volley of wind blades and lightning at Julius. It was just as useless as the first time. 
[SFX: BLOODY TENDRILS DISSOLVING ENERGY]
The blood spear was a bizarre weapon; its tip seemed to unfurl into a tangle of bloody tendrils that mixed with Law Power, completely dissolving his attack into nothing.
“It’s no use, kid,” Julius sneered. “For someone from the Soul Sea Realm, you’re impressive, I’ll give you that. You’ve got some real power. But your understanding of the Law is way too shallow.” 
[SFX: SPEAR RAISED WITH POWER SURGE]
The glowing net overhead, already streaked with a crimson aura, deepened to a dark, menacing red. 
[SFX: BLOOD THREADS WEAVING]
From it, tiny threads of blood shot down, weaving together into a massive cage that trapped both Zane and Julius inside.
Perched on the Heavenly Phoenix Horse, Freya’s hands tightened on the reins, her knuckles white. Zane had been forced back again and again, and now he was trapped in that cage with nowhere left to run. He was completely cornered.
“It’s over,” Julius gloated, the greed on his face no longer hidden. “You and the girl ascended at the same time, right? I have no idea how you got your hands on a Grade-One Divine Weapon and that Heavenly Phoenix Horse in just one year, but it doesn’t matter. Everything you have is about to be mine!” 
He dropped all pretense, his eyes burning with a furious, covetous fire as he leered at Zane.
Zane didn’t answer. He just looked at Julius for a moment — then raised Thundergust and held it in front of him.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SHIFT]
Something changed. It happened in less than a breath — a slight contraction of his pupils, a shift in his bearing so subtle it was almost nothing. Almost.
Julius felt it and didn’t know what to do with it.
There was no buildup. No Law activation. No gathering of power. What came next didn’t look like a killing move. It didn’t look like anything.
Zane stabbed forward.
[SFX: SINGLE CLEAN SWORD NOTE]
It was the most ordinary-looking strike imaginable — just a narrow, quiet blade of sword intent, cutting through the air without ceremony. Julius’s blood flowers bloomed in response, layering over each other, ready to dissolve it the way they’d dissolved everything else.
They didn’t.
[SFX: SWORD PASSING THROUGH]
The sword intent passed through them like they weren’t there.
Julius had just enough time to feel confused before the blade found him. In that instant — the instant of contact — he felt something he hadn’t felt in decades.
Fear.
This was the Gods-Intimidating Strike. Imperfect, incomplete, and already unstoppable.
[SFX: SWORD PIERCE]
Julius’s body came apart.
[SFX: BODY DISSOLVING]
It went fast — torso first, the rest following in pieces. The last thing to go was his head, still glaring at Zane as it began to dissolve at the edges.
Zane met his gaze, his voice perfectly even. “So — what price did I end up paying?”
Julius said nothing. His head broke apart and scattered between heaven and earth.
[SFX: GLASS SHATTERING]
The No-Fly Ban shattered like glass. 
[SFX: ENERGY WAVE EXPANDING]
The energy from the strike continued outward, a blade of pure intent stretching for tens of thousands of miles before the force he’d gathered finally faded.
[AMBIENT: DISTANT ECHOING RUMBLE]
Even after it dissipated, the ripple of that single sword strike could still be felt by countless warriors across the land.
But Julius wasn’t entirely gone.
[MUSIC: DARK MAGIC]
[AMBIENT: DRIPPING BLOOD CHAMBER]
Deep in the city lord’s mansion, in a sealed room that smelled of old blood, a mass of crimson essence drifted in the air and slowly pulled itself into the shape of a man. Julius had cultivated the Dark Blood Art for years — and with it, a Blood Soul Body, a second life hidden away against the day his first one failed him. His main body was gone, but this was not the end.
The blood figure screamed.
[SFX: ECHOING WAIL]
Then it stopped. Something had caught its attention. A familiar presence, approaching fast from the west — a Hall Master of the Soulbound Sect. If the Hall Master could be reached, if he could be told what had happened here, there was still a chance.
The blood figure surged out of the chamber.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS DISTANT POWER ECHO]
Within a hundred-thousand-mile radius, the lords of more than a hundred cities emerged from their homes, their faces grim as they stared up at the lingering echo of power that had come from the west. The Soulbound Sect was supposed to be a peaceful place; most warriors wouldn’t dare cause this kind of trouble. A fight this intense could only mean one thing: a Spirit Supreme Realm warrior was going all out.
[SFX: RUSHING WIND WHOOSH]
Dozens of city lords immediately began rushing toward Clearhaven. The first to arrive was a hall master from the Sect itself.
His name was Sloan. Zane knew him—or at least, they’d met. When Zane had first left the mine, it was Sloan and another master, Hill, who had brought him to the Holy City.
[MUSIC: TENSE PULSATING]
[SFX: FAST APPROACHING WHOOSH]
Sloan had just arrived on the scene when a bloody figure glowing with red light came rushing straight for him.
