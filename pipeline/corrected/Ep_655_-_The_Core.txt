Ep 655 - The Core

Word count: 1041

[MUSIC: DARK THREATENING STRINGS]

Malcolm's words still hung in the air as he pulled a black fruit from his Storage Ring. He crushed it in one massive hand, and it instantly disintegrated into dust.

[SFX: CRUSHING FRUIT]

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

A plume of black smoke billowed out, coiling around him like a living thing.

“I made you a promise five months ago, Zane,” he growled, his voice muffled by the smoke. “Today, you die.”

[SFX: TEARING FLESH]

[SFX: WINGS UNFURLING]

The smoke gathered at his back, hardening and shaping itself into a pair of enormous black wings that seemed to tear right out of his flesh. At the sight of them spreading wide, both Lavender and Yolande went rigid.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL REVELATION]

"The Ogres… they actually found his inheritance," Lavender whispered, her voice crystallizing into something cold and brittle.

Zane sensed raw power radiating from those wings, a force that made his skin prickle with warning, but he couldn't identify what he faced.

"It's the Fruit of the Dark Sky Mage," Yolande said, her usual composure cracking.

"The Dark Sky Mage?" Zane's heart hammered against his ribs. The name alone carried weight, though he'd never heard it before.

Lavender nodded. "He wielded the power of a world lord but never claimed a world for himself. Think of him as a freelance powerhouse from the Heartlands, beholden to no one—and feared by everyone who knew his name."

"And the fruit?" Zane pressed, watching Malcolm's transformed silhouette with growing dread.

She shook her head, and for the first time since he'd met her, genuine fear flickered across her features. "A massive, temporary power boost that turns even mediocre cultivators into monsters." Her next words came as a barely audible whisper. "Get ready to run."

The weight of those words settled over Zane like a shroud. If Lavender—who had faced down Soul Realm masters without flinching—was telling him to flee, then Malcolm had just become something far beyond dangerous.

This was never part of the plan. The original idea had been simple: stall. Esther and the top War Lords from Demon Night were more than a match for Timothy's crew. All their group had to do was keep Malcolm busy until the others were free. United, they could have taken him.

But this changed everything. Lavender had never counted on him having the Fruit of the Dark Sky Mage. It wasn’t just the raw power; the fruit could unlock a secret, devastating technique if the Ogres had managed to find the mage’s complete inheritance.

[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

Gripping her spear, Lavender lunged, sending an invisible wave of deadly energy hurtling toward Malcolm. “Go!” she screamed. She knew the attack wouldn’t kill him—it wasn’t meant to. It was meant to buy time. Zane’s time.

Zane didn't hesitate. This wasn't the time for pride.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

The moment Lavender yelled, he shot backward like an arrow loosed from a bow.

Esther heard the order and immediately abandoned her push against Timothy, the Queen's command overriding everything else. She and the two Demon Night War Lords broke off and streaked after Zane.

[MUSIC: URGENT CHASE THEME]

Malcolm's voice rang out behind them, almost cheerful. "You can't escape, Zane. Just give up, and I'll make it quick."

Behind Malcolm, Timothy gathered the three Twilight Group War Lords and gave chase. But the inner circle was not forgiving, and it wasn't long before the gap between them and Malcolm began to widen. Timothy's group fell further and further back until they were little more than distant shapes in the dark.

Garrick said nothing. He had done his part — found Zane, led Malcolm here. Killing Zane was never his job. He would have turned back in an instant if he thought Malcolm wouldn't cut him down for it.

Lavender wasn't running. She planted herself between Malcolm and the group, spear whirling.

[SFX: SPEAR STRIKE]

The invisible wave shot out again — the same flickering white light, the same suffocating pressure that made the air feel solid.

"Impressive technique," Malcolm said, almost admiringly. Then he swung.

[SFX: SABER SLASH]

[SFX: BODY IMPACT]

His blade tore through her in a single stroke, shredding the sword spirit apart. For a moment, she was gone — just a fading crimson light spiraling back toward Zane.

Then she was back.

[SFX: ENERGY REFORMATION]

The spear came up again. The same stance. The same wave. Malcolm's jaw tightened.

He cut her apart a second time. She came back a third. A fourth.

Each time she reformed, the crimson light that surrounded her was a little dimmer. The sword spirit's energy wasn't infinite — constant shattering and reassembling was a drain that even she couldn't sustain indefinitely. If Malcolm kept this up long enough, she would have no choice but to retreat into Zane's body to recover, leaving him completely exposed.

The situation was dire. Esther, Fabian, Jarrod—everyone—was scrambling for a way to shake him.

This wasn't a retreat; it was a desperate flight for survival, and they all knew they couldn't outrun him forever.

Ten miles stretched into a hundred, then two hundred.

And that’s when they all noticed it.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

The world ahead of them was wrong.

When they’d first entered the inner circle, the landscape had faded to a dull gray. Now, it had vanished entirely. There were no trees, no grass, no mountains—just an endless expanse of black, barren ground.

“There’s a fog rising,” Zane said suddenly, his voice tight. “It's forming all around us.”

“Fog?” The color drained from Esther's face. Even with thirteen lifetimes of experience, her heart clenched in her chest.

Fabian and Jarrod glanced around. “I don’t see any fog,” Fabian said.

But Yolande saw it. “He’s right,” she insisted. “It’s there. Just… very thin.”

A look of pure dread washed over Esther’s face. “Oh, no,” she whispered, skidding to a halt. “I think we’ve entered the core.”

As she spoke, the faint mist thickened, rising from the black earth in tangible wisps. Within seconds, it was a dense, swirling haze that none of them could deny.

The moment they stopped, Malcolm landed before them, a cruel sneer on his face. “Don't stop now; we were having so much fun.”
