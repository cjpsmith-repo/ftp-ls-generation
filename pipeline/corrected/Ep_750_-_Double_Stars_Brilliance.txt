[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]
Lavender was Zane’s sword spirit, which meant her power was a direct reflection of his. As he grew stronger, so did she. Zane knew most martial artists at his level wouldn’t stand a chance against her one-on-one. But Maddox was a different story. Taking him on alone was just too much for her.
Of course, Zane already knew that. Lavender couldn’t beat Maddox. And even if she could, Zane would never allow it.
[MUSIC: DARK DRAMATIC]
His entire life was dedicated to reaching the pinnacle of the martial path, and at just twenty years old, he was already well on his way, having reached the middle stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. But Maddox had become his nemesis.
If Zane wanted to keep climbing, he had to be the one to kill Maddox. Otherwise, the failure would become a roadblock in his mind, a ghost haunting him forever and holding him back from his ultimate goal.
[AMBIENT: WIND SWEEPING]
Lost in thought, Zane couldn’t help but think back to when he’d first returned from the main continent. If he’d just asked Dorian, or even a powerhouse like Amber, to come with him to The Heartlands, this all could have been over ages ago. He could’ve wiped Maddox off the map without breaking a sweat. He knew, without a doubt, that neither of them would have refused his request.
With their Soul Sea Realm cultivation, they could have flattened anyone in the Heartlands. Amber was, especially, a once-in-a-generation talent, exceptional even among her peers. A Life-and-Death Realm warrior like Maddox would have been nothing to her; he wouldn’t have survived a single blow.
But after thinking it all through, Zane had chosen to face Maddox himself. Nothing was more important than overcoming this obstacle with his own two hands.
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD-UP]
And now, the time had finally come.
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC BATTLE]
[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]
The moment Lavender’s attack managed to slow Maddox, Zane grabbed Tia. They swirled through the chaos of Oracle City, making another desperate dash for the safety of the city wall.
[AMBIENT: FULL-SCALE WARFARE]
[SFX: CLASHING METAL]
By now, the battle had erupted into full-blown war.
The army of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom was a terrifying force, but the alliance between the World Merchants' Guild and Cloud Hall was holding its own. The Guild, in particular, was not to be underestimated. As a renowned Sixth-Grade sect, their true strength dwarfed anything Cloud Hall could ever hope to muster.
[SFX: THUNDERING FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: MECHANICAL GRINDING]
Hundreds of mountain-sized golems, forged from steel and iron, thundered across the battlefield, charging the enemy ranks under the guidance of the Puppet Technique.
[SFX: CREAKING WOOD]
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS RUMBLE]
On the other side of the field, the two coffins — one black, one white — had been opened. Two enormous zombies emerged, moving through the battlefield like the wind. In the time it takes an incense stick to burn, they had already cut down five or six Spirit-Tribulation warriors.
Across the battlefield, the three Guild leaders were still fighting. The ancient tycoon was dead, and the grief on their faces was real — but there was no time. Simon had engaged them directly, and a Life-and-Death Realm battle demanded everything they had.
[MUSIC: DARK MENACING STRINGS]
[SFX: HOWLING WIND]
Amidst the chaos, Maddox laughed, a wicked, grating sound. He unleashed the Life and Death Wind from his Nameless Saber.
[SFX: SMALL LIGHT RETURNING]
[AMBIENT: QUIET AMID CHAOS]
Then a small point of crimson light drifted back toward Zane and settled into his body. Lavender's voice came from within, quiet and careful. "He beat me. The moment he did, he drilled into the ground. Watch your feet."
Zane nodded, eyes sweeping the earth around them. He didn't know which direction Maddox would come from.
He turned the problem over as he searched. Every technique he'd thrown at Maddox had been absorbed or neutralized. The black mist, the fiend body, the Life and Death Wind — all of it pointed to one conclusion: Stars Sink and Earth Quake wasn't going to be enough. He needed the second move.
The Stellar Body contained eight moves in total. Zane had been working through the second move in seclusion. He had some grasp of it — it required communicating with both the first and second stars — but he wasn't proficient. Not yet.
It didn't matter. He had no other option. And he knew, with cold clarity, that he would only get one chance to use it.
The two starlights on the surface of his body began to slowly separate, drifting toward his hands — orange gathering in one palm, purple in the other.
[SFX: BLADE WHOOSH]
Maddox erupted from the ground directly in front of them, the Nameless Saber already swinging. Two streams of shimmering yellow light spun from the blade. "Time for you both to die!" he roared.
[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]
Zane froze for a half-second as the sickeningly bright yellow winds shot toward them, but pure reflex took over. He yanked Tia down to the ground with him.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
[SFX: SUPERNATURAL WIND HOWL]
To everyone’s horror, the winds seemed to possess a will of their own, gliding unnervingly right for Tia. The Life and Death Wind was an unnatural phenomenon that triggered a primal fear in all living things. As it swept through the air, every heart on the battlefield nearly stopped. Everyone could only stare, transfixed by the two glowing streams of yellow light, as if they were watching their own deaths approach.
Saint Morphen’s face fell, and a grim shadow passed over the faces of the Cloud Hall warriors. The three leaders of the World Merchants' Guild looked on, their expressions turning dark. Even our own master couldn’t withstand that wind, they thought in unison. How could Zane and Tia possibly survive?
Only Simon was smiling, a cruel sneer twisting his lips. Zane is dead this time, he thought, savoring the moment.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: EPIC RISING ORCHESTRAL]
Just then, a dazzling light flickered in Zane’s hands. He pressed his palms together, and two beams of starlight—one orange, one purple—suddenly merged into one. In that single instant, the world itself seemed to fracture.
[AMBIENT: COSMIC VOID]
[SFX: CELESTIAL RESONANCE]
The sky plunged into darkness, as if night had fallen in the blink of an eye. The only light came from two brilliant stars that blazed in the artificial night, their orange and purple glows amplifying one another. That ethereal light cascaded down, bathing Zane’s hands in its otherworldly sheen.
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY RELEASE]
His expression was as still as a frozen lake as he slowly opened his hands, his voice cutting calmly through the silence. “Double Stars Brilliance.”
