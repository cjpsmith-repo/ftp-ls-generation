[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The Cobalt Dragon's voice resonated in Zane's mind, smooth and unwavering. "Jump. Get to the Dragon Soar Arena gates as fast as you can. I'll handle things from here."

Relief flooded through Zane's chest, loosening the knot of tension that had been building there. The Cobalt Dragon hadn't steered him wrong yet. Every time he'd followed its guidance—from the Blood Fiend Emperor's vault to the divine texture training—things had worked out, even when the path seemed impossible. With that kind of confidence radiating from his ancient companion, Zane felt ready to trust it completely, even with his life hanging in the balance.

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

[SFX: ECHOING SCREAMS]

He forced himself to remain still as the Adept Realm warriors were seized and hurled off the mountain, one by one. Their screams echoed across the peaks before being swallowed by the rushing wind. Zane's heart hammered against his ribs as he watched each figure disappear over the edge. 

[MUSIC: GROWING DANGER]

Then rough hands grabbed him. Two Inner-Core warriors seized his arms and legs, their grips like iron vises. They swung him back and forth, building momentum, his body suspended over the mountain creek far below, where the winged dragon circled, waiting. For a brief moment, Zane glimpsed the creature's massive shadow rippling across the water's surface.
Then they released him.
Zane hit the soft soil at the base of the creek feet-first, sinking ankle-deep on impact. He wrenched himself free and ran — the dragon roar had bought him seconds, not minutes.

[SFX: DRAGON GROWL]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

As Zane plummeted through the air, his stomach lurched. Below, the winged dragon tore into the warriors who had fallen before him, its massive jaws ripping through flesh and bone with horrifying efficiency. The creature's scales gleamed crimson in the fading light, slick with blood. Just as Zane's body hurtled downward, the beast finished devouring its last victim and whipped its head around. Those ancient, predatory eyes locked onto him with terrible focus.

The dragon craned its serpentine neck upward, muscles rippling beneath its armoured hide. Its jaws gaped wide, revealing rows of teeth like curved daggers.

Now, Cobalt Dragon! Zane screamed in his mind, desperation flooding through him as he wrenched himself free of his bonds. His heart hammered against his ribs. He was hopelessly outmatched; the Cobalt Dragon was his only shot at surviving this nightmare.

"Huh," the young man in purple muttered from his vantage point above. A flash of genuine surprise crossed his aristocratic features as Zane broke free mid-fall. The expression vanished as quickly as it came, replaced by a cruel smirk that twisted his handsome face. Pointless, he thought, arms crossed with casual arrogance. Struggle all you want, little mouse. There's no escaping that beast. Your fate was sealed the moment we threw you.

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL RISE]

But just as the winged dragon's jaws were about to snap shut over Zane's falling body, a mighty roar erupted from the Cobalt Dragon, echoing directly in Zane's mind with such force that his skull seemed to vibrate.

[SFX: MASSIVE DRAGON ROAR]

The sound thundered through the mountain creek, raw and primal—the voice of an ancient sovereign that had ruled the skies when the world was young.

[SFX: DRAGON SHRIEK]

The ferocious winged dragon shrieked, recoiling in pure terror. It scrambled back, cowering in the Dragon Soar Arena, its wide eyes wild with alarm.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The fear was instinctual. Winged dragons were distant descendants of Drakes, a lineage cursed with a primal terror of True Dragons—Legendary Beasts whose power was absolute.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]

The watching warriors stared, mouths agape. Shock seized the young master’s face as he took an involuntary step forward.

He stopped himself, knowing the winged dragon was far beyond his own power.

"How?" he muttered, his eyes raking over Zane, who was now scrambling away. "How did he produce a roar that powerful? Is he hiding some kind of weapon?"

 [MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]

Then, a memory surfaced, and the cruel smirk returned to his face. "Well, even if he escapes the dragon, he's not getting into the Arena!"

Clutching the dragon crystal pendant at his chest, his expression hardened. The medallion was his House's most priceless treasure, passed down for generations. It was the only key to the Dragon Soar Arena; without it, the arena was sealed.

[SFX: ROCKS TUMBLING]

Scrabbling for purchase, Zane kicked at the mountainside to slow his descent.
Wait… is this a magic array? But the beast didn't vanish… Is it real? No, that’s impossible!' Zane’s mind raced as he landed. 

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Where was he? Had he been teleported somewhere? This wasn't the Dragon Soar Arena.

The Dragon Soar Arena door was sealed shut. He hit it shoulder-first and found no give. "It's closed!" he told the Cobalt Dragon. "Get closer." 

[SFX: STONE GRINDING] 
A thread of the dragon's soul reached invisibly into a circular mark on the door. A low creak echoed through the creek. The door swung open. "In!" 

Zane dove through. The door slammed shut behind him.

The young master stared at the closed door, then at the pendant. His jaw tightened. "Throw them all in. Now."
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL INTERIOR]

He was inside a tower. Faint white lights blinked across the walls. A staircase rose through the center. He climbed to the second floor — dragon reliefs covered every surface, carved beasts frozen mid-roar. Then the floor beneath him turned to ice.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL TENSION]

The Cobalt Dragon heard his frantic thoughts and chuckled. "Of course it’s not an illusion," its voice echoed in Zane's soul. "This is far beyond that. It's an arena woven from divine textures—something you couldn't possibly comprehend. All you need to do is climb to the top."

[SFX: DARK ENERGY WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS ATMOSPHERE]

Just then, a black mist—Malice—rose from a polar beast's corpse and shot toward him. He recognized it instantly: pure curse energy, the kind only generated when a creature dies full of hate. That settled it. These beasts were real.

[SFX: MAGICAL IMPACT]

A speck of yellow light followed, flying out of the monster’s body. Before Zane could react, it slammed into his gut, sinking into his flesh.

'What the hell was that?' Zane thought, feeling the light merge with his core energy. But there was no time to figure it out. More polar monsters were closing in, ready to pounce.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]

[SFX: SWORD SLASHES]

Zane exploded into motion, weaving between the snarling beasts. He found an opening and thrust his sword, then another, and another, carving a path through the horde. In a flash, a mountain of bloody corpses was piling up around him.

With every beast he cut down, the clouds of Malice thickened, and more specks of yellow light shot into his body.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS BUILDUP]

Soon, a strange pressure began to build in his stomach, a feeling of fullness right where his energy was stored.

'What the hell is going on?' Zane wondered.
