Ep 990 - Student and Master
Word count: 1080
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]
In the world of martial arts, they always say the bigger the risk, the bigger the reward. It was a principle Zane had always lived by.
But even if this whole brutal ordeal, this three-thousand-step gauntlet, was just some sick joke from the owner, he’d take it. He didn’t care about a prize; he just wanted this nightmare to be over.
Zane knew his luck was insane, but he also knew it couldn’t save him from everything. Sooner or later, he’d have to face a real challenge. And this test was one hell of a challenge. All he could do was grit his teeth and push forward.
[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Every step was pure agony, a battle just to lift his foot. This had to be one of the worst experiences of his life.
Zane had slowed to a crawl after passing the halfway point. He needed more and more time to recover between each step as his body started to buckle under the strain.
By the two-thousand-step mark, he had to rest for hours between each movement. By twenty-five hundred, it took a full day and night of recovery just to manage another hundred steps.
His three women were with him the entire time, their eyes never leaving him, ready to help in an instant.
As for Saul and The Eternal Scribe, beings who had been around for ages, the time it took Zane to walk this path was less than the blink of an eye.
At his current pace, Zane was managing about a hundred steps a day. The final seven hundred steps took him seven more days.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TEMPLE]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]
At the end of that seventh day, he finally planted his foot on the very last print.
He might have breathed a sigh of relief, maybe even collapsed right then and there, but he never got the chance.
[SFX: MAGICAL LIGHT BURST]
A brilliant beam of light erupted behind him.
[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHOIR]
The light coalesced into a figure at the start of the path. The figure traced the same footsteps Zane had just agonizingly taken, but it crossed the entire three-thousand-step distance in a single, impossible instant.
[SFX: ANCIENT BOOK OPENING]
The figure brushed past Zane and walked toward the huge book, reaching out and flipping open the cover that had been sealed shut to them before.
“It’s open!” The Eternal Scribe shielded his eyes, staring at the figure. “Could that… could that be the Master?”
Saul squinted, trying to see through the glow. “Maybe? I can’t be sure.” Neither of them could make out any clear features; it was like trying to see a face in the sun.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
The book opened to the first page, which revealed the image of a door. It might have been just a picture in a book, but to Zane, it looked so real he felt like he could walk right through it.
[AMBIENT: CELESTIAL REALM]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL MEDITATION]
The figure reached out and touched the drawing of the door. It pushed gently — and the door swung open on its own, revealing a pitch-black space behind it. The figure stepped inside, then turned and pointed directly at Zane.
Zane didn’t hesitate. He stepped forward and followed the figure through.
[SFX: DOOR CLOSING — DEEP RESONANT THUD]
[AMBIENT: ABSOLUTE DARKNESS]
The moment Zane crossed the threshold, the drawn door swung shut behind him. The light from outside vanished. He was in complete darkness — he couldn’t see his own hand in front of his face.
Then a voice came from somewhere in the black.
“You walked my Extreme Path. That kind of persistence is its own talent. But you didn’t know what was in this book when you started. What kept you going?”
Zane thought about it for a moment. Then he smiled. “A path that hard has to have a reward worth having. That was enough for me.”
A burst of warm, rumbling laughter filled the darkness. “Frank enough! I like it, I like it!”
[AMBIENT: CELESTIAL CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT REVELATION]
The darkness dissolved. Seven-colored light bloomed in every direction, slow and flowing, tracing countless shifting patterns across the space.
The figure proposed a new idea: one could refine their body using starlight, a method similar to the cultivation of the Stellar Body. The concept was possible, in theory, but an idea alone wasn’t enough. Proposing a method was a far cry from working out all the practical steps.
Theory and practice could be worlds apart. Sometimes, the gap between them is so huge that the final result has nothing to do with the original idea.
But this wasn’t some regular warrior from the Heartlands. This was the master of The Elysian Keep. His power transcended this world, maybe even the heavens themselves. Any concept he proposed was bound to be flawless.
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
“There is one condition,” the figure said. “This method must be cultivated before you open your Inner World. Once the Divine Sea forms and the Inner World takes shape, the window closes — Chaos can no longer be exchanged for Vitality. It will be too late.”
Zane went still. He was at the Life-and-Death Realm — his Vitality had solidified, but his Inner World hadn’t formed. He still qualified. Barely.
“That’s why you couldn’t practice it yourself,” Zane said.
“Perhaps,” the figure said. “This method is still an embryonic form — I deduced it, but I never completed it. The path ahead may be a dead end. Or it may open something entirely new. Either way, you’ll have to find the end of it yourself. So, do you dare?”
Zane nodded, his voice firm. “Yes, I do!” he said, without a shred of hesitation.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
A low, amused chuckle seemed to emanate from the figure. “Very well then. Kneel.”
Zane’s eyebrow shot up. “Kneel?”
The figure’s laugh was a warm, rumbling sound. “Is there a problem? Second thoughts? You want to learn the secrets in my handbook, but you’re not willing to become my student?”
The thought stopped Zane cold. To be honest, since he’d started his martial arts journey, Zane had never really had a true master.
Isla had been a mentor, sure, but their relationship was never that formal. And Tia was the head of Cloud Hall, which made him more of an underling than a student.
He’d never had anyone he could truly call ‘master.’ He sure as hell never thought he’d find one after enduring a trial by fire and staring into a magical book. It was all happening so fast, and Zane felt a wave of hesitation wash over him.
[SFX: HEARTY LAUGHTER]
“Hahaha! Believe me, I never thought I’d take on a student, either. You seem a little hesitant. Don’t tell me you’re going to make me beg?”
