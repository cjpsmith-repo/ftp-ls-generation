Ep 240 - A Strong Contender
Word Count: 1291
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD CHEERING]
Lady Hawkins clapped Mack on the shoulder. "Nice one, Mack! But save your strength next time. That piece of trash from Misty Peak wasn't worth a magic crystal."
Magic crystals were what array masters used to boost their power. During the fight, Mack's array hadn't been strong enough to completely trap Eliot. But once he used the crystal, the array's power surged, leaving Eliot totally defenseless.
[SFX: TENSE SILENCE]
Master Warren helped the injured Eliot off the arena floor. When he heard Lady Hawkins call his disciples trash, he went rigid with fury, his mustache twitching. "You...!"
Lady Hawkins shot Master Warren a cold look, a smirk twisting her wrinkled face. "What? You know your peak always comes in last. You have one promising disciple, and he's only at the Consummation of the Adept Realm. Oh, right---we just beat him senseless. What else would you call someone like that but trash?"
Ninety-Foot Peak had ranked eleventh among the Thirty-Three Peaks and was on the rise. Besides, Mack was only eighteen and wasn't even their top disciple. They were ambitious this year, aiming for the top five, and were saving some of their best fighters for later.
[AMBIENT: ANGRY MURMURS]
The insult left the Misty Peak disciples seething. They glared at Mack and Lady Hawkins, but there was nothing they could do. The two of them were stronger than most of the Misty disciples combined, so they had no choice but to swallow their anger.
After Master Warren gently set Eliot down, he pulled out a pill to give to him.
But before he could, Zane held out a crimson pill of his own. "Master Warren, give him this."
[SFX: GASP OF SURPRISE]
Warren's eyes narrowed, but as he focused on the pill Zane was offering, they shot wide with shock. It was a Crimson Cloud Pill.
Fifth-grade pills were expensive, but with Zane's wealth, he could easily afford them and usually kept a few on hand.
Ever since Zane had taken his spot as Misty Peak's top disciple, Eliot had been nothing but supportive. He'd even walked Zane through all the rules and details of the All Peaks Competition, giving him a much better idea of what to expect.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED REVENGE]
Looking at what they'd done to Eliot, Zane made a silent vow: he would make Ninety-Foot Peak pay.
"Don't underestimate him," Eliot cautioned, his voice strained. "That magic array is incredibly powerful." It wasn't that Eliot doubted Zane's strength. But breaking a magic array required spiritual power, something that was notoriously difficult to cultivate. Even at the Consummation of the Adept Realm, Eliot's own spiritual power was weak. Zane was only at the second tier. His main advantage was his raw, natural-born strength, but that alone would be useless against an array. That's what had Eliot so worried.
But he didn't know that Zane's spirit had been forged as solid as a rock in the great furnace. Thanks to his training with the Psyche Spike, he'd reached a state of pure focus, pushing his spiritual power far beyond that of his peers. It would be hard to find anyone who could match him, even among disciples at the Consummation of the Adept Realm.
Eliot wasn't trying to discourage Zane, only warn him. Zane just nodded, promising he'd be careful if he ended up facing Mack.
While they talked, another eighteen fighters entered the arena to compete. This round, however, included no disciples from Misty Peak.
[SFX: CROWD ROARING]
Suddenly, the entire crowd roared as a new figure stepped into the arena.
[MUSIC: NOBLE THEME]
The man they were cheering for was dressed in a white silk robe---a clear cut above the plain cloth of the outer disciples. He carried himself with a noble air, the Cerulean Blade strapped to his back.
"It's Finn Donovan from Skytop Peak!" someone in the crowd yelled. Everyone was surprised to see him this early; with his strength, they'd all figured he wouldn't appear until the later rounds.
"Doesn't matter how strong he is, he's not a personal disciple. Still has to start from the beginning like everyone else."
"Just watch. We can learn a lot from seeing the top guys fight. It's a good way to sharpen our own skills."
Finn's appearance sent a ripple of discussion through the crowd.
"Finn," Zane thought, shaking his head slightly. He had no idea who the guy was. Apparently, he was famous in the Skyreach Sect, but Zane had never even heard his name. He felt a flash of self-consciousness, worried he was coming off as some ignorant newcomer.
When it came to Skytop Peak's best, Zane only knew of Dalton. He knew their peak's true strength could never be underestimated---after all, Dalton wasn't even their top disciple, yet he was strong enough to face a demon general. But this Finn Donovan... just by standing there, he radiated a commanding presence that immediately put Zane on edge.
Zane kept his expression neutral, but Warren must have seen his confusion. "Finn comes from a humble background," he explained quietly, "but he's a once-in-a-generation talent---the kind we haven't seen in five hundred years. He reached the Consummation of the Adept Realm at just eighteen. On top of that, his understanding of sword intent is deeper than anyone's, and he even broke a record set by a master from the Soul Realm. If you draw his name in the arena... you're better off just forfeiting."
After Eliot's brutal defeat, Master Warren's spirits had plummeted. He was pinning all his hopes on Zane, but he knew the odds were slim.
Whether or not he was the strongest disciple in the Skyreach Sect was up for debate, but one thing was certain: Finn was, without a doubt, the most popular.
His popularity was fueled by his humble beginnings. His legendary journey---from an unknown boy to a prodigy who shattered a record set by a Soul Realm master---only added to his fame.
[SFX: REFEREE ANNOUNCEMENT]
Finn's opponent was already waiting — sword drawn, posture tight. The moment the referee called the match, he launched his strongest technique without hesitation, three sword strikes building in power.
Finn didn't draw his sword. He didn't step back either. He moved — strangely, almost without seeming to move at all — closing the distance in an instant. Two fingers found the hilt of the incoming blade.
[SFX: SWORD CLATTERING TO GROUND]
The sword hit the arena floor. Those same two fingers came to rest an inch from his opponent's forehead. The arena went silent.
"I... I surrender." The referee forgot to call it. He stood there a moment before remembering himself.
"Finn wins!"
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMPETITION]
The competition in the arena raged on. These group fights were designed to cut the field down by three-fourths, forcing every disciple through a grueling gauntlet of at least two or three rounds.
Later, another three disciples from Misty Peak took their turn in the arena, but their performance was dismal. Aside from Zane, who won his second-round match, every other disciple from their peak was defeated.
[SFX: ARENA BELL]
When the eighth round began, it was Zane's turn again.
[AMBIENT: CROWDED ARENA]
[SFX: ANNOUNCEMENT ECHO]
"Zane of Misty Peak versus Tavish of Sea Peak!" the referee's voice boomed, loud and clear.
At the announcement, Master Silas of Sea Peak let out a sharp laugh, while Warren's face went dark.
Tavish was one of Silas' favorites. Master Warren could tell Tavish was one of Sea Peak's seeded competitors, and his strength was not to be underestimated. Sea Peak had been laying low for the past few years, secretly building up their power to make a splash at the competition.
This was going to be a tough fight for Zane.
