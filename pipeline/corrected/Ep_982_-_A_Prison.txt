Ep 982 - A Prison
Word count: 1120
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL STRINGS]
Saul’s body, a creation of the Elysian Keep’s master, was eternal. He was immune to the Five Aging Processes, his form unbreakable.
Even if he wanted to die, he couldn’t. His soul was locked so tight inside his immortal shell that there was no hope of it ever scattering to the wind.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]
For Saul, guarding the Elysian Keep was a mind-numbingly boring gig, and death was a luxury he couldn’t afford. If someone—anyone—would just take over the Elysian Keep, it would be a massive relief.
All the previous candidates had tried to seize control, but every last one of them had failed. Saul had even held out hope for Vesper’s return, but in the end, he too had been claimed by the Five Aging Processes.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]
But now, Zane was slowly mastering the Elysian Keep. He hadn’t just secured the token of water; he’d even managed to claim the token of the mountain.
“Carry the Elysian Keep with me? You can move it?” Zane asked, his curiosity piqued.
Saul nodded, a hint of a smile on his face. “The mountain token is the most unique of the six. It’s a projection of the keep itself—the front door, basically. You can think of it as the Elysian Keep. As long as you have that token, you can return here whenever you want.”
Saul’s mood seemed to be lifting. “Or look at it this way,” he added, pointing at Cora. “The Elysian Keep is like her Violet-Core Physique, and the token is a space node that leads right to it.”
“So that’s how it works,” Zane mused. This changed everything. If he wanted to come back, he wouldn’t have to trek tens of thousands of miles to the Eastern Territories. He could just use the token in his hand.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
“Do you want to continue?” Saul asked.
Zane had already snagged two tokens in a remarkably short time, but this time, he decided against it.
He had a feeling the next stage would throw a whole mess of Spirit Supreme Realm warriors at him. And yeah, he could redirect any attack thrown his way, but the Sacred Mount Lodestone had its limits. If a mob of those warriors all unleashed the fifth layer of their Original Laws, the power would be completely off the charts.
If the Lodestone shattered, the resulting overflow of power could tear apart the very foundations of the world of chaos. That wasn’t a risk Zane was willing to take. Besides, with the mountain token in his pocket, he could just come back when he was stronger.
“Can the others come with me to The Archives?” Zane asked.
Saul’s answer was simple. “Holding the token makes you the master of this Keep. Who enters is your decision.”
Zane nodded. That was all he needed to know.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
They turned and began making their way back. Then, without warning, a violent and vicious aura erupted from somewhere deep in the Keep.
It hit them like a physical force. Tia’s face went pale. Zane’s expression darkened.
Beside him, Cora and Isla crumpled to the ground.
Zane caught Isla before she hit the stone floor. Tia steadied Cora. Both women were unconscious — the aura had simply overwhelmed them.
“What was that?” Zane demanded, his eyes cutting to Saul.
Saul’s expression shifted — something between resignation and urgency. “Someone who’s getting impatient,” he said. Then, without waiting for a response, he turned and flew toward the depths of the Keep.
Zane stood there for a moment, looking after him. Then he pulled two calming pills from his Storage Ring and pressed them into Cora’s and Isla’s hands, waiting until the color slowly returned to their faces.
[AMBIENT: DEEP WITHIN THE KEEP]
[MUSIC: DARK MYSTERIOUS THEME]
Deep in the Keep’s backyard, Saul touched down without a sound. In the corner, a massive beast lay chained to the ground, its head turned toward a pit nearby, issuing low, restless sounds.
Saul walked past it without breaking stride. “What’s the hurry?” he said.
The beast’s roaring softened, but it kept its gaze fixed on the pit. Saul looked down. Below the iron fence at the pit’s edge was absolute darkness.
“You’ve endured this long,” Saul said into the dark. “A little longer won’t kill you.”
No answer.
“The young man has four tokens now,” Saul continued. “None repeated. He has the Water and the Mountain. In time, he may reach the Heaven and Earth tokens.” He paused. “I know you don’t think much of him. But this time may be different.”
Still nothing.
Saul turned to leave. Then a voice came from the pit — low, unhurried, carrying the weight of someone who had long since stopped counting years.
“Let me train him. A hundred years — I’ll make him the strongest in the universe.”
“No,” Saul said, without turning around. “The master’s rules stand. He can’t enter here until he holds the Heaven and Earth tokens.”
Silence.
Saul walked away. Behind him, the voice came again, quieter now, almost to itself:
“I’d wait a million years if I had to. But the monsters in the lower levels of this prison can’t.”
No one answered that.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING CORRIDOR]
Shaking off the cryptic comment, he and Tia helped Cora and Isla Novak to their feet. Only then did they all head for The Archives.
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT STONE CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORCHESTRAL]
The gate to The Archives was, of course, ridiculously tall. Zane craned his neck back but could barely make out the top of the massive door frame.
“Can I open this with just the token of water?” Zane wondered aloud, pulling it out.
[SFX: MAGICAL RUMBLE]
[SFX: STONE GRINDING]
The moment he did, a golden light shot down the center of the gate from top to bottom. A low rumble vibrated through the stone, and a seam appeared, splitting the enormous door in two.
Zane braced himself, expecting to use all his strength. A gate that looked as tall as a mountain had to be heavy, right? [SFX: HEAVY GATE OPENING]
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT LIBRARY]
But when he pushed, it swung open with a surprisingly light touch. The scent of old paper and ancient wood drifted out to meet him.
Books didn’t usually have a scent of their own, but these must have been made from some special kind of pulp. After countless years, that rich, natural fragrance was still there, captivating and clean.
“Let’s go!” Zane grinned at Tia, stepping across the threshold first. Tia, Cora, and Isla followed right behind him.
Though it had been sealed for ages, the Archives were completely free of dust. Before them stood bookshelves the size of small mountains, holding books that were just as huge. Each one had to be thirty feet tall and twenty feet wide.
“How am I supposed to read these?” Zane groaned, his earlier excitement deflating as he stared up at the giant tomes.
