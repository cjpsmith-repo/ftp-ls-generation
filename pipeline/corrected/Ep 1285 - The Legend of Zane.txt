Ep 1285 - The Legend of Zane

Word count: 1072

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
[AMBIENT: QUIET STONE CHAMBER]
The warrior saw the confusion on Tia’s face and explained flatly, “You can’t measure a Roaring Token in Aether crystals. It’s impossible for a regular warrior to buy one. But if you could, I’d guess it would cost millions of Sovereign Stones, at least.”
“Millions?” Tia echoed, stunned.
Even though she’d just arrived in the Upper World, Tia had heard that sacred places forced warriors to mine hundreds of Sovereign Stones for their freedom. Each Sovereign Stone traded for over ten thousand supreme Aether crystals - making millions of Sovereign Stones worth *hundreds of millions* of crystals.
Before ascending, the Cloud Hall leader had gifted Tia half their entire cache of supreme Aether crystals.
She had about two hundred thousand of them locked away in her Storage Ring.
A moment ago, she’d actually been thinking about buying two Roaring Tokens. Now, after what the warrior said, she was completely floored. The fortune she’d brought with her was nothing here.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC REALIZATION]
Where she once thought she was wealthy, she now felt like a beggar.
The warrior had vastly understated the price - Roaring Tokens actually cost billions of crystals. In the Upper World, these priceless treasures remained beyond reach for most warriors, reserved exclusively for World Lords and Supreme Lords.
“Of course, the Saint Lord of the Jadeheart Sanctuary and the other World Lords are sure to have one or two,” the warrior added, shaking his head in awe. Compared to the masters of the great powers, a warrior like him was just an ant.
Tia pressed her lips together, her expression falling. She hadn’t really expected to find a Roaring Token, but the name sounded familiar, and she was disappointed to hear it was so out of reach.
The warrior sighed. “Then again, crazy things happen. A few years ago, some guy was broadcasting through a Roaring Token.”
Tia’s eyes widened, and she leaned in. “With a rank like that, he should be training at The Lucent Hall, right?”
The warrior let out a short, sharp laugh. He looked her up and down for a second before asking, “You just came up from the Lower World, didn’t you?”
Tia nodded. She knew there were over a hundred thousand supreme worlds, but she didn’t have a real grasp on how they all fit together. To her, the tenth-grade Jadeheart Sanctuary was supposed to be a paradise for the powerful.
“Yeah, that makes sense,” the warrior said. “For a powerhouse like that, even the famous Thirteen Palaces couldn’t hold him, let alone the Jadeheart Sanctuary. They say right after he ascended, he left the tenth-grade sacred place and was accepted straight into the Thirteen Palaces. With the rank he’s achieved now, he’s considered top-tier talent in the entire alliance.”
“He’s more than that, honestly,” the warrior went on, warming to the subject. “That guy’s an anomaly. He’s only at the Soul Sea Realm, but he ascended from the Lower World with something called the Elysian Keep—used it to kill Heavenly Venerables, more than once. I don’t know how he pulled that off. At this point, there’s not a soul in this universe who doesn’t know the name Zane Lowell.”
[MUSIC: AWE AND WONDER]
Tia went very still. Her first thought was that she’d misheard him. Her second was that it had to be someone else with the same name—the universe was vast enough for that kind of coincidence.
But the mention of the Elysian Keep snagged something in her memory.
“He’s told some good stories with that thing, too,” the warrior added, grinning like he half-believed he’d lived them himself. “There’s one about a nobody who takes down some kind of legendary serpent. Real crowd favorite.”
That did it. Tia knew that story—it had circulated through her home region for years, the kind of tale only someone who’d actually been there would tell. Zane had told it to her once, long before either of them had ever imagined a world like this one.
Piece by piece, it all clicked into place. The Elysian Keep. The vanished valley. The story. She was almost certain now. And then she remembered—the Roaring Token itself had been one of three treasures his master had given him before they parted, though even Zane hadn’t understood what the thing was for at the time.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL RISING STRINGS]
A proud, brilliant smile lit up Tia’s face.
Back in The Heartlands, she’d had absolute faith in her husband. But this was the Upper World, a place filled with countless geniuses. She hadn’t dared to get her hopes up too high. To hear that Zane had accomplished so much was beyond her wildest dreams. A fresh wave of confidence washed over her, giving her the hope she needed to find him.
“Tia, what’s with all the questions?” the woman in red asked from beside her. “Do you know him?”
[SFX: CROWD COMMOTION]
A commotion rose from the crowd gathered ahead of them, all eyes fixed on the towering pyramid of names.
“This Zane actually jumped from over nine thousand all the way to one hundred thirty!”
“That kind of strength—he might really reach the top!”
“Don’t count on it. The higher he climbs, the harder it gets. There are too many geniuses ahead of him for it to stay this easy.”
The warrior chuckled, eyes still on the pyramid. “That’s Zane, all right. People used to say he just got lucky, that his real strength and talent didn’t add up to much. Now look at him—jumped straight to one hundred thirty. If he cracks the top ten thousand after this, that’ll really be something.”
Tia searched the pyramid herself. At first her eyes skimmed right past the top without catching his name—there were too many warriors, and with hundreds of millions competing across the Illusion Battlefield, the odds of spotting one name among them felt impossibly small.
Then she saw it. Two golden characters, floating near the top: Zane Lowell.
Tia smiled as she looked up at the pyramid. “Have you forgotten what I told you? I came here to find my husband.”
The woman in red was still confused. “I know that. So what?”
Tia’s voice was electric with excitement. 
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT REVELATION]
“My husband’s name is Zane Lowell. And if I’m right… that man at the very top of the pyramid is him.”
She didn’t say it loudly, but the woman in red and the warrior both heard her perfectly. 
[SFX: STUNNED SILENCE]
They just stared, their eyes wide with disbelief.
