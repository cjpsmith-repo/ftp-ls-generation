Ep 347 - Brotherly Hate
Word count: 1094

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
Confining the Crown Prince and the Sky Princess to house arrest wasn’t just the Third Prince’s idea. The truth was, the Seventh Prince was in on it, too.
The Crown Prince wasn’t much more than a pawn, but he was an indispensable piece on their political chessboard. His position, though largely symbolic, could not be easily dismissed. With succession to the imperial throne still up for grabs, neither Prince Ray nor Prince Edmund dared move openly against him.
"Strike against the Crown Prince now," Ray had once confided to his closest advisors, "and Edmund will immediately paint me as a traitor hungry for power."
Edmund had identical concerns. Any premature action would no doubt paint the aggressor as a usurper. Their mutual suspicion created a temporary stalemate that protected the Crown Prince like an invisible shield.
The brothers’ unspoken understanding followed a simple logic: whichever prince could get a clear upper hand could then eliminate the Crown Prince with impunity. At that point, with sufficient backing and momentum, no one would dare say a word against the rising prince’s actions.
[AMBIENT: QUIET EVENING]
[MUSIC: HARP MELODY]
A young man in a purple hat was calmly sweeping his fingers across a harp. The music swelled from powerful chords to soft, mournful melodies.
The guy could really play.
"Who’s that?" Zane asked.
"That’s Edmund, the Seventh Prince," Isla told him.
At the sound of Isla’s voice, Edmund stopped playing mid-note. A smile spread across his face as he called out, his voice bright and clear, "Isla! Good evening!"
Isla bit her lip. "It’s, ah, good to see you too, Edmund."
"The Palace was so noisy tonight I couldn’t get a wink of sleep," Edmund said with that same easy smile. "So I came out here to play a bit. I wonder if you and your friend would care to stay and listen?"
"We’re not interested," Zane said flatly. He had no time for this guy’s games. Something about Edmund’s whole vibe told Zane he couldn’t be trusted. He grabbed Isla’s arm and started to pull her toward the hole he’d just blasted in the wall.
[SFX: GUST OF WIND]
As Zane started to move, a sudden gust of wind whistled past him. Two guards materialized before the opening, blocking their escape route.
They had moved with such incredible speed that Zane’s trained eyes failed to track their movement. One moment, they flanked Edmund, the next they occupied the breach in the wall—as though they had been stationed there all along, phantoms materializing from thin air.
"Our Seventh Prince is rarely in the mood for an audience," said one guard, his voice carrying the cold authority of someone accustomed to obedience. His eyes, hard as stone, fixed on Zane with unmistakable warning. "I strongly recommend you stay and listen."
With the two men blocking his path, Zane tilted his head, a dangerous glint flickering in his eyes. His fingers twitched imperceptibly near his sword hilt as he assessed his opponents.
"Get out of my way, you pathetic toads," he snarled, his voice low but carrying the unmistakable edge of someone who had faced death and emerged victorious.
The lead guard’s jaw twitched, a muscle pulsing beneath his skin. He stepped forward, towering over Zane, his hand resting casually on his weapon.
"You little rat," he hissed through clenched teeth. "Consider yourself fortunate our Seventh Prince exhibits such generosity tonight.
Another time, another place—your head would already be rotting on top of the gates."
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ROYAL THEME]
Just then, another heavy presence settled over the courtyard. Ray, the Third Prince, had arrived with his own entourage, including one absolute giant of a man. The royal palace clearly had no shortage of heavy hitters; this newcomer looked every bit as strong as the guards flanking Edmund.
Ray shot Edmund a sharp look before a tight smile stretched across his face. "My dear brother! So carefree, playing the harp on such a dark and windy night. I heard you were planning on hunting down Zane.
Doesn’t sound very noble of you, does it?"
The two princes couldn’t stand the sight of each other these days. They were like fire and water, and everyone in the palace was just waiting for the day their rivalry finally erupted into open war.
The moment he saw his brother, Edmund’s friendly mask dissolved, his expression turning to ice. "What’s it to you?" he snapped.
"It has everything to do with me. Zane agreed to be my guest at Silversun Hall today."
[AMBIENT: TENSE COURTYARD]
A tense silence fell over the courtyard, heavy with simmering hostility.
The two princes locked eyes across the garden, their mutual hatred palpable. Behind each royal figure, their followers remained motionless as statues, hands hovering near concealed weapons, awaiting the slightest nod that would unleash chaos. These confrontations had become a familiar dance in the palace—a delicate ballet of veiled insults and political maneuvering—but never had the tension reached such a dangerous precipice.
Everyone present understood the gravity of the moment. These brothers had clashed dozens of times through venomous words and political schemes, each encounter ending in reluctant stalemate. But something felt different today—a line had been crossed. If either prince gave the command now, the pristine marble pathways and elegant flowering bushes would soon run red with the blood of loyal servants and innocent bystanders alike.
[SFX: LOUD ROAR]
While the two princes were locked in their standoff, Zane suddenly roared, "Enough!"
Zane’s shout stunned both princes into silence. For a second. Then, faint, almost identical smiles touched their lips. They might be wary of each other, but they weren’t worried about Zane. He hadn’t even reached the highest stages of the Adept-level. Sure, he was strong for his rank, but not enough to matter to them. If he joined one of them, great. If not, it was no big loss.
Zane shot them both a dark look. "Your Highnesses, I might not be strong enough to get Isla out of here tonight. But can I ask you something?"
"What is it?" Ray asked.
Edmund rested his fingers on the strings of his harp, his cold eyes locked on Zane.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS THREAT]
"Right now, with all your men, you can probably stop me," Zane said, his voice quiet but ringing with power. "But if you don’t let us leave, I’ll be back one day, once I’ve finished my training. And when that day comes, do you really want to risk watching me tear this empire of yours to the ground?"
