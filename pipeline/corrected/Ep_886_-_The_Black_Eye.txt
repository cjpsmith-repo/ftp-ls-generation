Ep 886 - The Black Eye

Word count: 1009

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS SILENCE]

[SFX: REALITY TEAR]

A thin line tore through the sky, appearing from nowhere. The moment it did, everyone froze, Zane included, as if some invisible force had paralyzed them on the spot.

[SFX: PRESSURE WAVE]

An immense pressure washed over him, an aura so powerful it seemed to transcend the very limits of what a living being could possess. 

Their bodies turned to stone, their blood ran cold, and their hearts seemed to stop beating. All that was left was a raw, terrified consciousness. Under the weight of that single line in the sky, their very souls felt like insignificant specks of dust.

[AMBIENT: FEARFUL CROWD MURMURS]

Whispers of disbelief and terror rippled through the assembled masters.

“What is that?”

“How can something be so powerful? It’s just a line, but I can feel it crushing me!”

“I feel… so small. Like I’m nothing at all.”

These were masters of the Soul Sea Realm, men who had lived for tens of thousands of years. They were ancient, their minds honed by millennia of experience, their warrior spirits meant to be as unyielding as diamond.

[MUSIC: PSYCHOLOGICAL HORROR]

But under the suffocating pressure of that thin line, some of those spirits finally began to crack.

[SFX: PRESSURE INTENSIFIES]

The power wasn’t spread evenly, though. It was focused entirely on Zane. The others were just caught in the periphery; he was bearing the full, crushing brunt of it.

Zane tilted his head back, his face a mask of cold focus as he stared at the impossible line. “Cobalt Dragon,” he sent the thought, his voice urgent in his own mind, “what is that?” He didn’t have time to overthink it. This was a life-or-death crisis, and he needed answers—any answer that might offer a way out.

But the dragon’s reply was simple, and more terrifying than any complex explanation. I don’t know.

Even the Cobalt Dragon, with all its ancient knowledge, had never seen anything like this.

But Zane was about to find out for himself.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL TENSION]

[SFX: SPATIAL RIFT]

The line stretched dozens of feet across the sky before it began to split in two. As the halves pulled apart, their ends remained connected, forming the distinct shape of an eye socket. And in the center of that socket, a pupil coalesced from the darkness: The Black Eye.

Zane’s expression tightened with caution. “Whose eye is that?” He felt no warmth from it, only a sharp, piercing hostility aimed directly at him.

No, he corrected himself, a deeper horror dawning. It’s not even hostility. It was the same look a person might give an ant on the sidewalk. Not malice, just a complete and utter disregard. The kind of look that precedes a casual, thoughtless stomp.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

The realization hit him like a physical blow. This is the Reincarnation Tribulation. And that… that is the World’s Will. 

Even the Little Monster, an incarnation of that very will, had never truly understood what it was. Zane had spoken out of respect for this world and its inhabitants, and this was his reward.

The eye blinked. A beam of black light shot from the pupil — and then the eye blinked again and was gone, vanished from the sky as though it had never been there. But the light remained.

[SFX: REFRACTING ENERGY]

[MUSIC: BUILDING DREAD]

It didn’t travel in a straight line. It bounced and refracted across the sky at sharp angles — thirty degrees, then seventy feet in another direction, then again — zigzagging closer and closer to Zane with each pass. There was no rushing it and no predicting it. It was simply closing in.

The Soul Sea masters watched in silence. They had made Zane their hope — their key to the ascending passage — and under any other circumstances, they would have died before letting someone take him from them. 

But not one of them moved. Not one of them could even form the thought of resistance. The black light made that impossible.

Zane’s mind raced. His Vitality shield — useless. His body refinement — useless. The Stellar Body — useless. There was only one option left.

He reached into the world’s memory — that vast, complete record that had flowed into him when he absorbed the world’s will — and pulled up every mountain in existence. Over five million mountains in total.

He excluded the Eastern Region. Then he activated Power Transfer.

“I am the representative of this world,” he said quietly, his voice steady. “I ask you to help me pass through this disaster.”

He was speaking to the world’s will — not to any single mind or consciousness, but to the collective body of every creature that had ever lived here. He knew it couldn’t hear him the way a person could. He said it out of respect. For the world. For its creatures. For himself.

[SFX: ENERGY BEAM]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

The refracting light completed its final angle and arrowed straight for him.

Everyone could only stare helplessly as the scene unfolded. Faced with that terrifying light, no one even considered fighting back. There was nothing they could do.

[SFX: DARK ENERGY PULSE]

The beam struck Zane in the chest and sank into his body without a sound. In the next instant, his entire being turned pitch black—his lips, the whites of his eyes, his fingernails, even the fabric of his clothes were swallowed by the absolute darkness.

[MUSIC: INTENSE HORROR STRINGS]

The black light coursed through him, wrapping around every single cell with the clear intent to annihilate him. Inside his body, his brain, organs, bones, and every last drop of blood were stained black, corrupted by a power bent on wiping him from existence. If it succeeded, not a single hair or cell would be left. Not even a particle of him would remain in this world.

A uniquely terrifying power had been unleashed, and the cells and tissues of Zane’s body struggled to withstand the impossible force radiating from within him.

[AMBIENT: RUMBLING EARTH]

[MUSIC: CATASTROPHIC CRESCENDO]

As the energy began to swell, the first mountain gave way.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

[SFX: DISINTEGRATION WHOOSH]

A peak in the western Heartlands, having absorbed a sliver of the overflowing energy, exploded from the inside out. No rocks tumbled down its slopes; it didn’t even have time to collapse. 

Before the echo of the explosion had faded, the entire mountain simply vanished into thin air.
