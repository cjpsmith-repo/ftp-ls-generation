Ep 320 - Can I Borrow Your Sword?
Word count: 1195

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Even with his ridiculous antics, Evan was still the toughest opponent Zane had ever faced.
If this battle allowed for refinement techniques or Vitality, Zane would’ve been toast a long time ago.
After all, the power gap between them was huge. Zane's foundation was solid, sure—which made him stronger than most warriors at his level.
But Evan's foundation was just as solid. He hadn’t juiced himself up with pills; he’d earned his strength the old-fashioned way, through pure grit and relentless training.
Still, Zane wasn’t sweating the fact that Evan was stronger. He knew if he just kept grinding, he'd reach the Sixth Stage of the Soul Realm, too. And eventually, he’d surpass everything Evan had ever accomplished.
Leveling up wasn’t the goal right now, though. All that mattered was the sword in his hand.
"Your sword style is... weird," Evan said. "Basic swordsmanship is supposed to be the simplest way to use a sword, but yours is even simpler. I don’t know how you’re pulling it off, but I’ll find a way to crack it!"
With that, Evan squared his shoulders and prepared for another attack.
As he gathered himself and lunged, his focus was so sharp you could cut glass with it; no one dared to meet his eyes. In that instant, Evan was a different person entirely—nothing like the unfocused, sometimes goofy, yet proud fighter everyone knew.
The sudden shift caught the audience completely off guard; most of them had no idea what they were even seeing.
[SFX: SWORD TREMBLING]
"I’ll crush your simplistic little style with my most complex one!" he declared. As he launched his assault, the sword in his hand began to tremble violently.
[SFX: RINGING SWORD]
Suddenly, it rang out, the sound echoing as the blade vibrated so hard it left a blur of afterimages in the air.
[SFX: THOUSAND STEEL BLADES]
Like a thousand peonies blooming at once, the blurs of steel took form under Evan’s careful control. He created a garden of a thousand flowers made of pure steel—as beautiful as they were deadly, a testament to his martial brilliance and the unique skills he kept up his sleeve.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE]
If Zane had reached the Consummation of the Adept Realm, Elara thought, shaking her head as she snapped back to the present, he wouldn’t need to play these games. At that stage, once a fighter’s power was maxed out, any extra energy went into cultivating their soul, forging a direct connection to the universe itself.
And entering the Soul Realm would bring even greater changes. His strength, reflexes, eyesight—his very sense of timing and coordination would be supercharged.
Lost in her thoughts, Elara glanced back at the fight. Her brother had a ridiculously sharp eye. If he ever reached the Soul Realm, the Sword of a Thousand Blossoms would be a joke to him; he’d spot the weakness in Evan’s first move. But Zane still had a long road ahead of him. For now, Evan was just too damn strong.
[SFX: SWORD CLASH]
"Clang!"
The new attack sent Zane stumbling back another few steps.
He was getting dangerously close to the edge of the combat field. One more push and he'd have nowhere to run. The fight would be over.
[SFX: TRIUMPHANT LAUGHTER]
Sensing his advantage, Evan burst into triumphant laughter.
With a massive grin, he yelled at Zane, "Looks like you’re out of tricks! You and that sword of yours are finished!" He sneered at the thought.
[SFX: SWORD SINGING]
Evan’s sword sang out again as a thousand phantom blossoms launched a storm of attacks. He had absolute faith in his skills, convinced Zane couldn’t possibly see through his lightning-fast moves. The only outcome he could imagine was Zane’s defeat.
Watching from a short distance away, Elder Gates shook his head. Zane was a good disciple and a skilled fighter, but he wasn’t a match for Evan.
Not yet. Even though they were only competing with swordsmanship, Evan’s superior power gave him a clear and unfair advantage.
Zane had managed to block two of Evan’s attacks, but he’d hit his limit and was on his last legs. All he could do now was maintain a defensive "circle parry," with no energy left to launch an attack of his own.
In a sword fight, playing defense was a sucker’s game. "The best defense is a good offense" wasn’t just a saying; it was gospel. Most schools didn’t even teach defensive skills anymore, focusing entirely on overwhelming the opponent. As long as you could pin someone down with a relentless assault, victory was practically in the bag.
That was Evan’s strategy now. Overwhelm Zane with a flurry of attacks, then finish him off on the third strike.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
But just as everyone thought Zane was about to lose, something impossible happened.
[SFX: SWORD BREAKING]
[SFX: SWORD CLINK]
As Zane deflected the strikes, the light clink of steel on steel rang out.
[SFX: LOUD SWORD CRACK]
But then, a sharp CRACK! echoed across the entire combat field. It was Evan’s sword. The front half of the blade had snapped clean off.
"W-what?"
Evan froze mid-attack, holding the broken sword, his eyes wide with disbelief.
Everyone, including Zane, just stared at him in dead silence.
Nobody saw that coming. What kind of fighter breaks their own weapon mid-match? It was just... embarrassing.
Evan turned to Elder Gates. "Can I borrow your sword?"
Elder Gates shook his head. "I don't use one."
Evan's gaze swept across the hundred thousand disciples. "Anyone? Anyone at all?"
Dead silence.
[SFX: SOFT GIGGLE]
From the sidelines, Elara couldn’t help but let out a little giggle, a smug look spreading across her face.
Only she knew why. She raised an eyebrow, practically taunting him.
"No, no! Not you!" Evan stammered, his face flushing as he realized his mistake. "I mean—you have Cloudrender now. Can I... can I please borrow it, My Queen?" He knew he had to ask nicely. He’d lost that sword to Elara, fair and square, and now he was at her mercy.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
The desperation in his voice was music to Elara’s ears, and a satisfied smirk grew on her face. But just as Evan's hope flickered to life, her smile vanished. "Nope," she said, her voice as cold as ice. "You can’t."
"Wh-what? Did you just say no? You little—" Evan bit his tongue before a curse could escape. Elara was his last hope. Cloudrender had been his sword, an extension of his own body for years. Getting it back would give him the edge he desperately needed. He had no choice but to swallow his pride.
But the truth was, Elara was stalling. She'd seen the look on Zane’s face—he was deep in thought, figuring out how to beat Evan. She was just buying him time.
Besides, this was hardly a fair fight to begin with. It only seemed right to help her brother win.
[MUSIC: CONFIDENT RESOLUTION]
Seeing they were at a stalemate, Zane decided to step in.
He opened his eyes, a confident smile touching his lips as he said to Elara, "Let him borrow the sword."
