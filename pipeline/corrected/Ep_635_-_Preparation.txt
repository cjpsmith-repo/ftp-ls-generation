[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE WIND]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
After gathering all the information he could, Zane thought he’d braced himself for the dangers of the Dark Nether Cave. But hearing it from a master of the Soul Sea Realm made the threat feel a lot more real. The cave wasn't just dangerous; it was a deathtrap. Seventy-six top-tier War Lords had teamed up to explore it, and not a single one had come back alive. What the hell was hiding in its depths?
Vesper knew exactly how dangerous this was, and he sent me here anyway, Zane thought. Is this just another one of his games? But it didn't matter. Nothing was going to stop him. The chance to enter the cave was a huge temptation for anyone, but for Zane, it was essential—an opportunity he might never get again. He wasn't about to miss it.
Dorian managed a tight smile, his faith in Zane warring with his fear. He knew it was pointless to try and talk him out of it.
[AMBIENT: CROWD CHEERING]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]
The tour of the human sanctuary that followed felt more symbolic than anything. With Olivia leading the way, Zane drew cheers and applause everywhere he went.
The younger generation at the Violet Heart Sanctuary, in particular, looked at him with something close to awe. In their eyes, he was unbeatable. The guy who’d conquered the Tower of Sin in a way no one had ever seen and single-handedly driven off the Ogres? He was a living legend. They were all intensely curious—how could someone so young be so powerful?
While the rewards from the Violet Heart Sanctuary couldn't compare to what he’d received from the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, Zane accepted them with genuine gratitude. He understood their situation.
The Heavenly Feather Sanctuary had gifted him a top-grade Relic-Class sword—the very weapon Amber had originally intended for Lavender. It seemed like some things just had a way of finding their rightful owner. The humans, with their limited resources, simply couldn't match that.
[AMBIENT: TELEPORTATION HUM]
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
After the formalities, Dorian got down to business. He asked Zane to inform the Demon Night that the Violet Heart Sanctuary wanted to forge an alliance with the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, then walked him toward the teleportation array.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
"You drove the Ogres from the Tower of Sin," Dorian explained, his voice low, "but their two sacred grounds are still out there. If they decide to declare war again, we won't stand a chance without help."
Alliances between different races were rarely worth the trouble, but Dorian was banking on Zane's connection to the Demon Night. He was hoping Zane could at least buy his people enough time to continue their training.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]
Back in the Tower, Zane passed Dorian's request to Esther. She agreed without hesitation. The details — what the Violet Heart Sanctuary would offer, how long the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary would extend its protection — were for Dorian and Amber to work out between themselves. That wasn't Zane's business.
[MUSIC: DRIVEN MONTAGE]
The months that followed were quiet ones. The Tower's points system was meaningless to Zane now — he moved freely through every floor. What mattered was time, and he used all of it. He borrowed the Tower's time-altered cultivation rooms and trained without rest, pushing his Stellar Body and his other techniques as hard as he could push them.
The challengers kept coming. They always did. Early War Lords at first, then mid-rank, then seventh and eighth level. Zane worked through them all, floor by floor, the fights growing harder and the Lucky Light thinner as the quality of his opponents rose. Some of the challengers were even human — warriors from the other races who wanted to test themselves against the man who'd rewritten the Tower's rules.
By the time several months had passed, Zane had climbed to the fourteenth floor, and his cultivation had reached the ninth level of War General. Peak War Lords were within reach.
[AMBIENT: TOWER CORRIDOR]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS PULSE]
Then, one morning, Zane stepped out of the cultivation room and stopped.
The walls were glowing. A faint red light ran along every surface — ceiling, floor, walls — flowing in steady pulses, like blood moving through veins.
"Tower Spirit," Zane said. "What is this?"
The Spirit's flat, emotionless voice answered immediately. "The Dark Nether Death Light has weakened."
"The cave is open?"
"You could say that."
[SFX: STONE SPLITTING]
[SFX: RUSHING AIR]
Zane didn't wait. The floor split beneath his feet, and he dropped straight through, falling from the fourteenth floor all the way to the top of the Tower in a single motion.
[AMBIENT: CROWDED HALL]
[MUSIC: TENSE GATHERING]
The hall was already filling up. War Lords from every race were assembling — people who had entered the Dark Nether Cave before and knew exactly what the red light meant. Each of the Tower's 128 seats entitled its holder to five entry spots. That was 640 total. The humans alone now had more than a hundred.
Just as they neared the array, Fabian waved him over. "Yolande is going with you."
[MUSIC: CURIOUS TENSION]
Zane stopped, surprised. Yolande had been on the tenth floor of the Tower of Sin for ages without making any progress. "She is? Why?"
"She applied for a spot on her own," Fabian explained quickly. "So I gave it to her. She's human, after all, and one of our most talented. After everything she's contributed over the years, she's earned the right to go."
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
As if on cue, Yolande walked over, her expression unreadable. She stared right at Zane. "I'm coming with you to the cave."
Zane’s confusion deepened. She’d been in the Tower of Sin for so long, she must have had countless opportunities to enter the cave before. So why now? Why with him?
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
Before he could ask, a beam of crimson light shot from his body, slowly coalescing into the familiar form of his sword spirit. Lavender stood before them, looking as calm and severe as ever.
Her gaze fixed on Yolande. "Zane," she said, her voice cutting through the air, "don't let her come with you."
"What? Why not?" Zane was floored. He’d always had a good impression of Yolande, and she’d sided with him before. Letting her tag along didn’t seem like a big deal.
Lavender took a step toward the other woman. "You know what's in that cave, don't you?" she asked, her tone an accusation.
Her words instantly seized the attention of every War Lord in the hall. To them, both Yolande and Lavender were mysteries. If anyone knew the secrets of the cave, it was them.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
Suddenly, the room fell silent, everyone hanging on every word of the silent standoff between the two women.
The more they knew, the better their odds of survival.
