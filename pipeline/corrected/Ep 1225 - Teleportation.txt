Ep 1225 - Teleportation

Word count: 999

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL VOID SPACE]
The Teleportation Token pulsed once, and the world dissolved around Zane. 
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
He reappeared in a strange, mystical space, standing in a daze as he looked down at the countless red dots scattered at his feet like fallen stars. He’d figured the token would let him use some grand teleportation technique, instantly zipping him millions of miles across this new world. He never expected to be pulled into a place like this—it was beyond anything he could have imagined.
“These red dots… they must be the sanctuaries,” he thought, a slow realization dawning. “This must be the main hub.”
The dots spread out below him, distributed evenly across a dark, nebulous surface. One of them glowed brightly, and not far from it was another dot representing a sanctuary. He recognized the bright one as his initial entry point into the Illusionary Arena; he’d been dropped on the ass-end of nowhere, just as the map had shown. That nearby sanctuary wasn’t too far off, but the rest were scattered at immense distances. Flying to even the closest one would take forever.
“So, is this thing just a glorified map?” he muttered. If the token could only show him the sanctuaries but not take him to them, then all the work he’d put in to get it would have been a complete waste of time.
Deciding to test his theory, Zane drifted through the void, heading straight for one of the red dots. 
[SFX: MAGICAL SPINNING INTENSIFYING]
As he floated directly above it, the dot began to spin. 
[SFX: BRIGHT ENERGY BURST]
A brilliant white light erupted from its center, spinning faster and faster as it pulsed with the raw power of spatial magic.
“A teleportation channel,” he realized, his eyes widening. “It looks like it connects directly to the sanctuary.” A thrill shot through him. *Wait a second. If that’s true… does this mean I can jump to any sanctuary I want?*
His heart hammered against his ribs. If he could really do this, he could raid every single sanctuary in this world.
The pocket dimension itself wasn’t huge, maybe a few dozen miles across. As Zane moved around, he found that every time he passed over a red dot, it would blossom into a swirling portal, offering him a path. 
[SFX: PORTAL WHOOSH]
If he took it, the channel would disappear behind him. Tens of thousands of sanctuaries, which meant tens of thousands of personal gateways. 
Near the center of the map, three light spots caught his eye — a dull, greenish-gray, nothing like the vibrant red scattered everywhere else. He drifted above each in turn, but none of them responded, no matter how long he lingered. Whatever those three sanctuaries were, he couldn’t reach them yet. He filed the mystery away and moved on.
He settled on a red dot near the center of the map instead. The logic was simple enough: sanctuaries clustered closer together in the heart of this world, which meant more warriors crossing paths and more frequent, higher-stakes clashes between them. If he wanted action, the center was where to find it.
He stepped through.
[SFX: SPATIAL PRESSURE HUM]
The moment the channel closed around him, his ears began to ring — a deep, disorienting pressure building behind them. This wasn’t like an ordinary teleportation channel, the kind that only folded distance down by a hundredfold. This one carried him instantly, no matter how far the destination actually was — the kind of ability normally reserved for Sector Lords alone. His body wasn’t built to handle that level of spatial law bearing down on it all at once, and it showed.
[SFX: FREE FALL WHOOSHING]
As the ringing in his ears grew sharper, the ground rushed up to meet him. He tried to catch himself mid-air out of instinct — and found an invisible force dragging him straight down instead, refusing to let him fly.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
He hit the ground hard, the impact loud enough to turn heads. Only once he’d pushed himself up did it click: this city had a restriction in place. No flying allowed inside the walls — not even for him.
[AMBIENT: HUSHED WHISPERS]
Dozens of warriors nearby, mid-meditation only a moment ago, were now staring at him with open confusion.
“Did that guy just... fall from the sky?”
“That’s not possible. Nobody flies inside a main city — how did he even get up there in the first place?”
“So how did he just appear, then?”
Zane let them wonder. He had no intention of explaining himself, and he wasn’t sure yet whether the token would even work again once he left — best to keep it to himself either way.
[MUSIC: UPBEAT ADVENTUROUS]
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY CROWD]
Looking around, it was clear enough this was a human city — the architecture gave it away, and so did the faces in the crowd. He figured it was best to keep his new mode of travel to himself; let people wonder.
He was here for one reason: to find these so-called “prize pigs” and take their Illusion Points.
With that in mind, he started to head for the outskirts to try his luck. But before he could get far, a voice stopped him.
“Hey. You new here?”
[MUSIC: TENSE OMINOUS]
Zane stopped and turned, meeting the warrior’s gaze. “Yeah. Why?”
“We’ve got rules,” the man said, eyeing him up and down. “Newcomers have to register. And if you want to leave here in one piece, you’ve got to hand over half your Illusion Points to the City Lord.”
Zane blinked. “A City Lord? This is the Illusion Battlefield, not some kingdom. Besides, my points are tied to me. How would I even give them to someone?”
“Couldn’t tell you about other sanctuaries,” the warrior replied with a shrug, “but this one has a Lord. And paying is easy. You just let him kill you once. The system automatically transfers half your points.”
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
It finally clicked, and Zane didn’t know whether to laugh or get pissed off. A “City Lord.” The title caught him off guard, but the strategy was all too familiar. It sounded just like Thane—another thug using his power to shake down other warriors for their hard-earned points.
