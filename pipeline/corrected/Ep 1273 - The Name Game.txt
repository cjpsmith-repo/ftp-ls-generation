Ep 1273 - The Name Game

Word count: 1018

[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
[AMBIENT: CROWDED HALL MURMURS]
When the short warrior stepped aside, every eye in the hall landed on Zane. Of the eight fighters left, he was the only one who hadn’t taken a swing at the massive rock yet.
By now the tally stood well over twenty million points combined, with Janet’s three point one million and Jeffrey’s three point nine million topping the list. Every eye in the room did the math, wondering what Zane could possibly add to it.
He gave Janet a faint smile before walking toward the stone square. Everyone watched, leaning forward in anticipation, wondering what kind of attack he’d unleash. 
[AMBIENT: DISAPPOINTED CROWD MURMURS]
But as Zane settled into his stance, a wave of disappointment washed over the room.
He just dropped into a simple horse stance, clenched his right fist, and stared at the rock. It looked like he was gearing up for nothing more than a regular punch. 
The rock itself bore the marks of everyone who’d gone before him. It looked like it could take a hundred more hits and still stand. Zane didn’t so much as peek at the damage.
A few people exchanged confused glances. They wondered if he even cared about earning Illusion Points. The short warrior’s palm strike had also looked simple, but you could see the mastery in it up close—the effortless control over his power. This just looked… plain.
“Zane, are you really just going to punch it?” Janet asked, unable to stop herself. “Don’t you want to use a weapon? It might get you more points.”
Zane just shook his head. “I don’t need one.” He understood her doubt, but he wasn’t in the mood to explain himself.
A weapon channeled power, sure, but it also diluted it—every ounce of force had to pass through steel or wood before it reached its target, and something always got lost along the way. His fists didn’t have that problem.
He knew that with all forty thousand of his Dragon Scales activated, his raw physical strength was probably on par with his Gods-Intimidating Strike. But raw strength had its limits; warriors rarely fought up close, which was why he usually relied on his skills.
He let the power from his Dragon Scales flood into his right arm, then threw a punch that looked completely ordinary.
No one had any idea just how terrifying the force behind that fist really was.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT THUD]
Zane’s knuckles connected with the rock with a dull *thud*.
For a moment, nothing in the hall moved. Even the settling dust from the earlier explosion seemed to hang suspended, as if the room itself were holding its breath.
“Ha! Looks like he’s going to be dead last,” the ogre warrior grinned from the sidelines. Like Zane, he’d used a simple punch earlier and had only scraped together about four hundred thousand Illusion Points. 
As an ogre, his natural strength was leagues beyond a normal human’s. Even though he’d held back, using less than sixty percent of his power, he was sure he’d still hit harder than this guy.
But then Zane pulled his fist back. For a second, nothing happened. 
[SFX: FAINT MAGICAL SHIMMER]
Then, a few spots of light—Illusion Points—floated out from the rock.
One, two…
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS RISING TENSION]
The glowing yellow motes drifted from the shattered stone like fireflies escaping into twilight, dancing and swirling through the air in mesmerizing patterns. Everyone stared, transfixed. Their expressions shifted slowly - from smug certainty to dawning doubt.
At first, only a few hundred points appeared, winking into existence one by one.
The crowd exchanged uncertain glances. Some held their breath, waiting for the stream to end and confirm their expectations.
But the flow didn’t stop.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
But then the stream of light thickened, and the count shot into the thousands, then hundreds of thousands, climbing higher and higher. 
The glow lit up the faces of everyone watching, washing the ogre’s smug grin away and replacing it with something closer to disbelief. No one was laughing now.
With that one punch, he earned back the five million points he’d paid to enter, and then some.
His was the final attack. 
[SFX: ROCK CRACKING]
[SFX: STONE CRUMBLING TO DUST]
As the last point registered, cracks spiderwebbed across the rock’s surface until it suddenly collapsed into a pile of fine dust.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS MOUNTAIN WIND]
A collective sigh went through the remaining warriors as they considered the road ahead. They’d scored an incredible number of points in the Second Hall of Causality, but what could possibly be next? They felt a flicker of hope, but it was overshadowed by a heavy sense of dread. The Second Hall hadn’t been easy—two of them were already dead. What was the Third Hall going to throw at them?
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE STAIRS]
After leaving the hall, they stepped back onto the winding stairs and continued their climb up the mountain.
The stone steps wound upward into darkness, the air growing colder with every level, and no one seemed eager to break the quiet after everything they’d just survived.
The silence stretched on for a while before Janet finally broke it. “We should introduce ourselves,” she said. “We’ll need to cooperate to get through these halls. We should at least know each other’s names, right?”
Working together had already forged them into a temporary team, but what they’d just been through had deepened that trust.
Hearing her, the short warrior from The Hallowed Concord was the first to speak. “My name is Jeffrey.”
“Jeffrey?” another warrior asked, his eyes wide with surprise. “You mean the famous monk from The Hallowed Concord?”
The short man just smiled and nodded. “Yes, that’s me.”
“I’m Zane Jones,” Zane added quietly.
“Coleman!” the ogre warrior boomed.
The woman from the Demon Night blinked softly. “My name is Hailey,” she said.
After everyone had shared their name, Janet smiled. 
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
“You’re all very honest,” she said, her eyes landing on Zane. “Except for him. He’s the only one who used a fake name.”
