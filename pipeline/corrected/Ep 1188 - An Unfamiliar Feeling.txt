Ep 1188 - An Unfamiliar Feeling

Word count: 1026

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE]
The battle ignited, and nearly twenty Supreme Lords scrambled back at once, terrified of getting caught in the crossfire. 
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]
But Ranell, the Queen of Punishment, and the handful of others with her didn’t just stay—they charged straight into the fight.
Normally, picking a winner in a battle between these World Lords was all but impossible. They had all mastered Spatial Transference, which meant that even if you couldn’t beat your opponent, you could always escape. With a single jump, a warrior could cross a million miles in an instant, making them impossible to pin down. There was an old story about two World Lords who fought and chased each other for over ten years, their conflict spanning a hundred thousand different worlds. You couldn’t even define a battlefield when the fighters could just vanish.
Even among the Supreme Lords, defeating an opponent was one thing, but killing them was another story entirely. It was unheard of to kill a Supreme Lord who wanted to escape. The only way it ever happened was if several others ganged up on them, or if one of the absolute top-tier masters of the Law of Causality got involved.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER ACTIVATION]
But now, five of them were trapped in the Desperate Melee, confined to a space just one hundred miles wide.
[SFX: ENERGY BARRIER IMPACTS]
No matter what they tried—Spatial Transference, secret escape techniques, any method they could think of—they couldn’t break free from the shimmering band of light that contained them. They were stuck inside that hundred-mile zone, able to jump around within the massive battleground but never beyond it.
Nothing like this had ever happened.
Because of this, the fighting within that small bubble of space was unbelievably fierce.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: DISTANT MAGICAL WARFARE]
Inside the Elysian Keep, now just a single grain of sand adrift in the heart of the storm, Zane and Lavender silently watched the sky. They had front-row seats to a whole new kind of war.
Lavender’s eyes were fixed on the chaos above. She hadn’t spoken since the battle began.
“Will she die here?” she said quietly. Not a question for Zane, exactly. More like something that had slipped out.
She didn’t need him to answer. She already knew Zane had promised to help her kill the Queen of Punishment himself — and she had never doubted he meant it. But she didn’t want that. Didn’t want him to carry that risk for her sake. 
She was the Queen of Slaughter. Her will had been forged through a lifetime of killing. And yet, watching the storm of light above her, she found herself caught between wanting it to be over and not wanting the price of it to fall on him.
It was an unfamiliar feeling. She wasn’t sure she liked it.
[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY WAVES]
The sky above was just a chaotic smear of impossible colors and blinding flashes of light. Beyond that, the details were lost in the sheer scale of it all. Zane couldn’t even begin to comprehend a battle on this level; it was too immense, too fast. The only things he could vaguely identify were the powerful waves of Water Law he knew were coming from Magnus, and the shredded shadows flitting through the chaos, which had to be Gunter from the Demon Night. Even though all he could perceive was the raw, clashing Law Power, the swirling vortex of light left him breathless. It was a terrifying, beautiful sight.
“It’s a shame that the tether didn’t pull her in with the others,” Lavender said, still watching. 
Zane had no answer for that either. The Desperate Melee had been Saul’s idea — improvised, unrepeatable. It had caught who it caught.
Two hours into the battle, the universe trembled.
[SFX: LOW DEEP RESONANCE]
Outside the hundred-mile zone, the watching Supreme Lords all looked up at once. They hadn’t seen it happen — the fighting was too dense, the light too blinding — but they felt it. 
A Heavenly Destiny had gone unanchored. 
When a Supreme Lord died, the Heavenly Destiny they carried lost its bearer, and the resulting absence sent a shudder through the Celestial Law itself, rippling outward across the universe.
The first Lord was dead.
One hour later, the universe trembled again. The second.
[SFX: LOW DEEP RESONANCE]
Not long after that — the third.
[SFX: LOW DEEP RESONANCE]
On the edge of the conflict, the Queen of Punishment nursed a gaping wound in her thigh. 
[SFX: BLOOD SIZZLING]
Fresh blood trickled down her leg and dripped from her toes, but before a single drop could touch the ground, it sizzled and evaporated into nothing.
She had done everything she could, and it hadn’t been enough. She couldn’t save Gunter.
In fact, he was the third Supreme Lord to fall. After he died, Ranell had been forced to retreat from the heart of the battle. 
[SFX: DESPERATE BATTLE CLASH]
The fighting there was just too intense; even she couldn’t withstand the frantic, desperate attacks from the remaining lords.
[SFX: LOW DEEP RESONANCE]
A few minutes later, the fourth Heavenly Destiny came loose. The universe shuddered one last time.
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
She had come all this way herself, so certain of her victory. She never imagined she would not only fail to eliminate Lavender and seize the Crown of Destiny, but also lose Gunter in the process.
With his death, the Demon Night had lost another Supreme Lord—and the head of their Board of Elders, no less. This was a catastrophic blow to their power. When she returned, the other two Supreme Lords would almost certainly hold her responsible for the disaster.
A long, heavy sigh escaped her. A profound sense of helplessness washed over her, something she hadn’t felt in centuries. Zane and Lavender were right there, so close, and yet she couldn’t touch them. The raw, burning frustration of it was a new kind of pain. Defeat was a foreign, bitter taste in her mouth. She had no choice but to give up.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
And she wasn’t the only one left disappointed and enraged.
Of the five Supreme Lords who had been trapped, four were now dead.
A Supreme Lord was the symbol of an entire race. With their protection, a people could thrive across all the hundred thousand worlds of the universe. 
With four of them now gone, the races they represented would never, ever accept this outcome.
