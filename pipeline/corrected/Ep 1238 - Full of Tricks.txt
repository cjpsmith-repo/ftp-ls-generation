Ep 1238 - Full of Tricks

Word count: 1060

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]
[SFX: BLADE SLASH]
The moment Zane unleashed his Eight Smoky Melodies, a wave of force blasted him backward—but not before a crescent blade sliced a bloody, inches-long scar across his face.
That split-second tumble was a pure survival instinct, and it was the only thing that saved him. If he hadn’t thrown himself back, Sloane’s attack would have taken his head clean off.
Sloane landed a hundred feet away, perched on the summit of Sacred Mount Lodestone, and scoffed when she saw her powerful attack had done little more than scratch him.
[SFX: GOLDEN WIND WHOOSH]
With a flicker of golden wind, she vanished.
To onlookers, Sloane appeared to rush her next attack. But she knew the truth - her strike had been flawless. Yet he’d still dodged what no opponent had ever seen coming.
Zane, meanwhile, stared at the delicate embroidered shoes on Sloane’s feet, a crescent blade still glinting from its hiding spot beneath them. He wiped the blood from his cheek. The wound was superficial; his body would heal it in no time. Still, the shock remained.
*This woman is full of tricks.*
As Sloane prepared her next assault, Zane’s mind raced. If he didn’t find a way out of this, it was only a matter of time before he lost.
[SFX: GOLDEN LIGHT FLASH]
Another flash of gold lit up from the mountain peak. Here she comes again, he thought, flipping in the air as he drew deep on his chaotic energy, pushing the Eight Smoky Melodies to its absolute limit. White mist thickened around his body.
It wasn’t enough. Even at full circulation, the crescent moon was faster. It crossed the three-inch buffer that had never failed him before and cut into him again.
[SFX: BLADE CUTTING FLESH]
This time, the blade opened up the flesh on his upper arm. He’d seen it coming just late enough to make a choice — twist his body, take the hit on his left arm instead of somewhere worse, and use the opening to strike back. The instant the blade bit down, his right hand shot out, closing for Sloane’s ankle. If he could just catch her, drag her down, end this here—
[SFX: GOLDEN WIND WHOOSH]
His fingers grazed the back of her foot. Before he could close his grip, the golden wind flared, and she was gone, his hand closing on empty air.
For one bare instant, something flashed across Sloane’s face — not quite fear, but close enough to it. He’d almost had her. She hadn’t expected that, and it showed for exactly as long as it took her to smother it.
Then she was moving again, no pause, no hesitation. She knew he couldn’t match her speed — not really. All she had to do was keep the pressure on, never let up, never give him a second chance at what he’d almost pulled off. So she didn’t.
[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]
[SFX: RAPID BLADE STRIKES]
Streams of blood sprayed into the air as Sloane continued her relentless assault, striking and retreating in a blur of motion.
[SFX: EXCITED CROWD CHEERING]
“Haha, look at that! Sloane’s getting serious now,” one of the spectators yelled. “Her attacks just flow like water, one after another. She’s not giving him a second to breathe!”
“With her strength and speed, I bet she could take out a warrior in the middle stage of the Spirit Supreme Realm, easy.”
“See? A pro’s a pro. How could this guy ever hope to win? And isn’t this just half of what she can do? I heard Laney is in the Illusion Battlefield, too. Sloane somehow entered as two different people! I wonder if she’ll merge with Laney in a later fight?”
[SFX: WHOOSHING BLADE STRIKES]
Sloane’s attacks grew sharper, more ferocious with every pass. She aimed for his vitals—eyes, throat, stomach—each strike a killing blow delivered at lightning speed.
But Zane’s instincts were on a razor’s edge. He managed to avoid the worst of the damage at the last possible second, but the wounds were piling up. She was slicing him to pieces, bit by bit.
“Die!”
[SFX: GOLDEN WIND BLAST]
Another blast of golden wind swept over him. A deadly smile played on Sloane’s lips, a hint of anticipation in her eyes. She’d found an opening. This, she knew, would be the attack that ended it.
[SFX: BLADE WHOOSH THROUGH AIR]
But this time, her blade hit nothing but air.
[SFX: CROWD GASPS]
The warriors watching from the wall gasped, looking at each other in confusion. How could Sloane possibly miss?
Because Zane had left the opening on purpose.
Sloane didn’t really care that she’d missed. To her, that kind of deception was a cheap trick. She respected real strength, not mind games.
But she was unbelievably fast. As she corrected her stance, Zane kept dodging, his mind scrambling for a way out. 
And then it hit him. He still had that one magic weapon he’d never used before. He had no idea if it would even work, but it was all he had left.
It wasn’t just any trinket. The Death Chain had come to him from fragments his Master had left behind. Once the chain locked two people together, there was only one way it ended: one of them had to kill the other. It didn’t care about rank or realm. Even a Heavenly Venerable couldn’t break free of it. If Zane used it on someone stronger than himself, it would be nothing short of suicide. But the choice of who to bind, and when, was his alone to make.
Sloane’s eyes caught on the bright, unfamiliar shackle materializing in his hand, and confusion clouded her face. *What is he planning? Does he actually think that thing can hold me?* It didn’t seem possible.
[SFX: RAPID WIND WHOOSHES]
[SFX: ETHEREAL CHAIN MATERIALIZING]
[SFX: GOLDEN WIND STREAKS]
Streaks of golden wind flashed as Sloane flickered in and out of sight, but no matter how much she struggled, she couldn’t get more than ten feet away from Zane. A chain, dark and ethereal, now connected them. No matter how fast she was, she was tethered.
Zane was a mess, covered in bloody cuts, but the moment the Death Chain locked onto Sloane, he knew he’d already won. The chain’s maximum length was only ten feet. 
[SFX: CHAIN RATTLING]
[SFX: HEAVY PULL]
With a sharp tug, his immense power yanked her back toward him.
Realizing she couldn’t escape, a determined look hardened Sloane’s face. She stopped struggling. Instead, she drained every last drop of Vitality from her body. 
[SFX: ENERGY DRAINING HUM]
[SFX: GLOWING INTENSIFIES]
As the Five-Element Finger Chain on her hand began to glow, all that energy transformed into a Five-Element Palm Print that shot toward Zane, radiating pure killing intent.
