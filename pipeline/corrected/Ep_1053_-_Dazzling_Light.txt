Ep 1053 - Dazzling Light

Word count: 1201

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

While Yolande was shocked by the magnitude of Zane’s power, it wasn’t a total surprise. She’d already absorbed the memories of her two clones, who’d gotten to know Zane a bit.

Still, he’d completely blown her expectations out of the water.

Harvey, however, was even more stunned. He just stood there, frozen in the entrance to the hall.

He already had a serious chip on his shoulder about anyone from the Lower World, and on top of that, he and Yolande couldn’t stand each other. So, naturally, this whole situation infuriated him.

Even if he hadn’t stumbled upon them right then, news that Zane had claimed the Thundergust would have reached him eventually.

That sword… it was so valuable that even he, the vice head, was only ever permitted to borrow it.

And now, it belonged to this kid. For good.

A junior, barely at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm. He hadn’t been at The Mist Palace for a full day, and he’d already snatched up its greatest treasure.

You could practically feel the rage boiling off of him. If Yolande hadn’t been standing right there, he might have just pulled rank and ripped the sword straight from the boy’s hands, consequences be damned.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

But he just glared, his eyes burning with hatred for both Zane and the sword in his hand. The blade he’d once coveted now just looked… tainted. Vile.

[SFX: ANGRY FOOTSTEPS]

Finally, he spun on his heel and stomped into the Hall of True Mysteries.

[MUSIC: LIGHT TRIUMPHANT THEME]

“Congratulations,” Yolande said, a small, triumphant smile on her lips. Seeing Harvey so miserable for the second time today was its own reward.

Zane just gave a curt nod, his mind elsewhere. All he could think about was Lavender.

Yolande read the worry on his face instantly. “Not here,” she murmured, her voice low.

[AMBIENT: QUIET HALL]

She guided him into a quieter, more private hall.

Once she was sure they were alone, Yolande took out the Soulcradle Wood and handed it to him.

Zane took the wood, his focus narrowing. He could immediately feel the difference. Lavender’s soul was so much stronger now—stronger than his own, even.

[SFX: MYSTICAL GLOW]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL WONDER]

He was stunned to see a faint, silver glow radiating from its surface.

“Her soul…” Zane breathed, his eyes wide. “It’s reached the Argent Fighting Soul!”

When a soul becomes powerful enough, it can enter the Fighting Soul Realm, allowing it to directly aid its physical body in combat. Zane’s own soul had achieved the Nightjade Fighting Soul, but the Argent Fighting Soul was a huge leap forward—a far more powerful and advanced state.

Yolande nodded. “Her soul was originally at this level, but only a wisp remained — it couldn’t manifest. I’ve restored sixty percent of her soul energy.”

An innocent expression crossed Yolande’s face. “She’s pretending to be asleep,” she said. “It seems she doesn’t want any contact with me.”

Zane understood immediately. He knew Lavender’s temperament. Her body was gone, her soul shattered to a wisp — and through all of it, the only person she’d ever truly trusted was him. He said nothing. He simply took the Soulcradle Wood from Yolande’s hands.

Over the next two hours, Yolande sent someone to arrange temporary quarters for Zane. As a new arrival, he was assigned to live among the warriors of Groundstone Hall. Even so, the accommodations were far from modest — the Mist Palace’s standard for even its entry-tier members was a small palace unto itself, fully appointed and immaculately kept.

[AMBIENT: QUIET NIGHT]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

It wasn’t until that night that Zane finally took out the Soulcradle Wood.

[SFX: SOUL IMPACT]

The moment he turned his focus inward to examine it, before he could see anything at all, a soul attack hit him like a battering ram. Even with his own soul strength, his mind went utterly blank.

When he came back to himself, Lavender’s soul was already standing in front of him. She was looking at him quietly.

She was different. Fuller. The sharp, blade-like quality of her presence had returned — and then some.

A curious voice—Lavender’s—echoed in his mind.

[SFX: TELEPATHIC WHISPER]

“You’re only at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm, yet you’re in the Upper World. You didn’t ascend on your own. Someone must have helped you, right?”

It was supposed to be an unbreakable rule: no one could use the ascending passage until they reached the Soul Sea Realm. She’d never heard of a warrior making it on their own before then.

Zane shook his head. “No,” he replied, his voice earnest. “I came on my own. Through the passage.”

“But… how?” she pressed, her confusion clear even in his mind.

A gentle smile touched Zane’s lips. Lavender had been asleep for so long. He began to tell her everything—about his journey to the Divine Kingdom Continent and all that had happened since.

He had barely finished speaking before Lavender’s soul rushed forward and merged back into her Sword Spirit.

[SFX: SOUL MERGE]

With her soul restored to this level, she didn’t need to exist as a Sword Spirit — her soul alone was strong enough to fight. But Zane didn’t question it. She had her reasons.

A moment later, she reappeared outside his body. She stretched out her arms and stepped close, pulling him into a quiet embrace.

Zane went still. “Why were you in such a hurry to merge?”

“Because I can only hug you like this.” Her voice was as cold and measured as ever. But there was something underneath it — something that almost sounded like warmth.

He understood.

After a long moment, Lavender stepped back and floated quietly in the air. “Thank you,” she said.

Zane shook his head. “I should be thanking you. Your soul was shattered because of me.”

She blinked. Then, with unusual deliberateness, she said, “You can’t say that.”

“Why not?”

“You’re important to me. If my soul shatters, you’ll avenge me. But if you die —” she paused, “— I have no hope at all.” Her tone was matter-of-fact, as if she were simply stating the obvious.

[AMBIENT: DISTANT WIND]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

At that same moment, thirty thousand supreme worlds away from The Mist Palace, a beautiful woman in a yellow robe stood silently before a statue, her eyes burning with a cold hatred.

She was stunning, with a beauty that seemed almost inhuman—an impression confirmed by her long, pointed ears. She was no human. This was the Queen of Punishment, one of the Demon Night race’s matriarchs, and a foe who had already crossed paths with Zane twice.

Her palace, like all homes of the Demon Night race, housed statues of their three queens. Lately, her attention had been fixed on one in particular: the statue of Lavender, the Queen of Slaughter.

She knew her rival was on the brink of death. The statue’s life-light had dwindled to a faint flicker, a soul that could be snuffed out at any second. The Queen of Punishment was savoring the wait.

[SFX: MAGICAL PULSE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

But earlier today, that had changed. The statue had abruptly flared to life, bathed in a blinding crimson glow.

The light pulsed with a heavy, raw aura of slaughter, a sight so potent it was painful to look at.

The Queen of Punishment had been waiting for that light to go out. Instead, it was burning brighter than ever.
