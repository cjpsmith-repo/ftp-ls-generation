Zane, on the other hand, was from the weakest peak—Misty. No one thought there was anything special about him. Zane might have won all his fights so far, but most disciples were either confused about how he was doing it or just chalked it up to dumb luck. Very few believed Zane had any real power—except for the disciples from his own Misty Peak.
