Ep 783 - The Harlen Token

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CROWD]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

 The young man standing before Zane was clearly from a well-to-do family. He had a chiseled face, a polished demeanor, and a robe that screamed custom-made. The four bodyguards flanking him, all radiating the power of the Life-and-Death Realm, were the real tell. This guy was a big deal. He might even be a prince, Zane thought, like the one he’d met ages ago in Vesper’s celestial tomb. If not royalty, he was at least a major player here in the Harlen Divine Kingdom.

Zane couldn't be sure, and since he was still getting the lay of the land, his first instinct was to keep his head down. But trouble, it seemed, had a way of finding him regardless.

"Are you also a candidate?" the young man asked, his tone dripping with condescension. Despite the polite manners and fancy clothes, the guy’s ego was practically a physical presence.

"Yes," Zane answered bluntly, trying to keep the irritation out of his voice.

"Great," the young man said with an arrogant smirk. "I haven't seen any solo warriors around here at your level. I'm participating in the assessment as well. You'll follow me." 

 It wasn't a suggestion; it was an order.

Zane froze. Follow him? He shook his head. "I appreciate the offer, but I've always worked alone. I'm not really the 'follower' type."

[AMBIENT: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 One of the guards immediately bristled. "Who the hell do you think you are? Our leader is giving you a chance, and you're turning him down?"

The guard's anger didn't faze Zane. "If you really think that highly of me," he said, turning the man's own sentiment back on him, "then you wouldn't want to make things difficult, would you?"

His response made the young noble chuckle. "Good. You've got guts. I like that." He glanced back at his guards. "Let's go."

The sudden retreat caught Zane completely off guard. He'd been bracing for a fight, and he knew taking on four warriors of their caliber would have been a serious drain. The guy’s easy dismissal was both confusing and a relief. Even though Zane was confident he could handle them, he preferred to save his strength. Better to keep his energy in case he had to run from a real monster—someone at the Soul Sea Realm.

[AMBIENT: QUIET RELIEF]

 Francis let out a quiet sigh beside him. "Whew, good thing you kept your cool."

"Why?" Zane asked.

"Unless I'm mistaken, that's Randy, a Prince of the Harlen Divine Kingdom," Francis said. "Second only to his brother the Crown Prince, among the younger generation."

"Second in line? At his age?"

"Soul Sea Realm monarchs live for tens of thousands of years. The York Divine Kingdom's monarch alone is said to have tens of thousands of sons. From the moment they're born, those princes are already in a fight — for resources, for their father's attention, for survival. Some spend their entire lives without ever exchanging a single word with him. The ones without strength end up worse off than ordinary people; at least a commoner can live quietly. A powerless prince is just a target." 

Francis shook his head. "To claw your way to a king's title at that age, in that kind of competition — his strength can't be underestimated. Don't cross him again. Taking him on directly might be manageable. But offend the Soul Sea Realm warriors standing behind him, and there's no walking that back."

Zane nodded and followed in the direction the young prince had gone.

[AMBIENT: COURTYARD GATHERING]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

 The courtyard was filled with warriors from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, and they were a different breed entirely. These were veterans who had been on the martial path for so long that standard training resources were practically useless to them. Even a place like the Tower of Sin could only forge top-level War Lords; fighters at this stage had to push their own limits, surviving near-death experiences and seeking out that one great opportunity that could finally lead to enlightenment.

That opportunity was what they all craved. They had all come here for the same reason as Zane: to represent the Harlen Divine Kingdom and earn entry into the Sacred Feather Sea. Some were solo, like him, while others had come from third- or even fourth-grade martial houses, hoping a stronger faction would give them a better shot. Switching allegiances wasn't seen as a betrayal; in the end, all martial houses served the divine kingdoms.

What Zane hadn't expected was the scale of it. 

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

 The examination courtyards ran in a long row from the Martial House entrance all the way to the foot of the mountain, one for each cultivation level. Zane walked the length of them slowly, taking it in. The first courtyard — Adept Realm — was barely recognizable as a line anymore. It was a mass of people, the tail of it curling around the base of the mountain and out of sight. 

Francis said waits of one or two months were normal. One or two months, just to register. And yet every one of them was there, because for an Adept Realm warrior, the resources a sixth-rank Martial House could offer — pills, techniques, private cultivation chambers — were worth any wait. The next courtyard over was Soul Realm, shorter but still enormous. Then Inner-Core, a few hundred at most. Each level smaller than the last, the lines thinning as the cultivation rose.

By the time he reached the Spirit-Tribulation courtyard, there was practically no one there. Lower-level warriors flooded in for the resources the kingdom provided — pills, techniques, cultivation rooms. For someone at the Adept or Soul Realm, months in a queue was a fair trade. But Spirit-Tribulation warriors had long outgrown that kind of support. What they needed couldn't be provided by any house.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

[AMBIENT: QUIET COURTYARD]

 As soon as Zane stepped into the main courtyard, a woman at the Inner-Core Realm approached him with a welcoming smile. "Are you here to participate in the test for the Harlen Token?"

The Harlen Token was his ticket. According to an agreement among the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms, each kingdom issued a set number of these tokens every year to warriors who proved their worth. They were essentially a license to enter the Sacred Feather Sea.

Of course, Zane nodded. It was the whole reason he was here.
