Ep 1133 - The Nine-Turn Passage

Word count: 1098

[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND TUNNEL]
[MUSIC: TENSE MYSTERY]
A hidden passage yawned open before the group, releasing a cold breath of ancient air from somewhere far below.
“Two passages?” Adrian asked, surprised.
Lincoln smirked. “Take the one on the left,” he said, “and you’ll never lay eyes on the Dark Underground Palace for the rest of your life.”
Lincoln had only agreed to this because Adrian had helped secure his freedom. He hadn’t counted on Adrian bringing seven Spirit Supreme Realm warriors - none of whom were fools, and certainly not allies. They’d likely attack the moment he opened the entrance.
Still, Lincoln trusted his own strength - and Zane’s even more. If they behaved, everyone would profit. If they caused trouble, they’d die.
[SFX: HEAVY STONE DOOR OPENING]
[SFX: DARK ENERGY WHOOSH]
The moment the hidden passage swung open, a wave of chilling, dark energy rolled out from the depths. It felt like a hundred icy hands grabbing at them, a silent shriek promising to tear them to pieces.
“Whoa,” Abner breathed, his voice tight. “That is some serious dark energy.”
“Good thing I brought this,” Adrian said from the side.
[MUSIC: MAGICAL DISCOVERY]
He reached into his Storage Ring and produced a handful of pale yellow branches — the prepared countermeasure, exactly as Lincoln had instructed them before setting out. Freya conjured two of the branches and handed one to Zane.
[SFX: FLAME IGNITING]
Zane raised his hand. Two small balls of flame curled out from his palm and caught the ends of both branches. The wood was dense and dry but burned slowly, giving off only a thin thread of smoke and a steady dark red glow.
It was enough. Where the glow touched the dark energy rolling out of the passage, it dissolved — the cold retreating like frost pulling back from heat.
“Let’s move,” Lincoln said, branch in hand, and led them into the tunnel.
The seven brothers filed in after them. As they walked, Zane caught something from the corner of his eye — the faint movement of lips. A Vitality sound transmission, passed between the brothers, too quiet to intercept. He couldn’t hear a word. But he didn’t need to. The looks on their faces told him enough.
His own lips moved, barely.
“Those fellows don’t look friendly,” he said, the transmission pitched for Lincoln alone.
Lincoln’s reply came back dry and easy. “Is Brother Zane afraid?”
“Not really.” Zane’s expression didn’t change. “Just reminding you.”
“Heh.” A beat of silence. “It’s fine if they don’t make a move — I’ll stick to the agreement. But if they do?” Lincoln’s voice carried a quiet certainty. “They’re courting death. Am I right, Brother Zane?”
“Of course,” Zane said. And that was the end of it.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: HIGH ALTITUDE WIND]
But they weren’t the only ones watching. 
High above in the Violetwood Sanctuary, members of the Demon Night were tracking their every move.
“They’ve found the second passage,” the Demon Night’s lord observed, his voice calm. 
[SFX: RUSTLING LEAVES]
He stood balanced on the highest leaf of a massive tree, the palm-sized perch holding his weight without so much as a tremor. “It seems they came prepared. They didn’t just stumble in here blind.”
[SFX: WOMAN LAUGHING SOFTLY]
A woman standing nearby smiled. “They’re only at the second passage. That doesn’t mean much with seven more to go. Besides, the secret of the Nine-Turn Passage isn’t exactly well-kept. Anyone can learn the path by asking around the Dark World. The real question is whether they actually have a way to open the palace itself. All we can do is wait and see.”
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS EXPLORATION]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING STONE PASSAGE]
The three great races - including the Ogres and Demon Night - watched intently, hoping Lincoln’s group had obtained their information from another world. That would suggest they possessed a genuine key to open the palace. 
Each tunnel stretched for hours. Lincoln led them through alternating corridors - one cold and lightless, one bright and open - choosing turns without hesitation. They rested between passages, then continued. Two full days and nights passed before the final tunnel ended.
“Just how long was that?” Third Brother muttered, glancing back the way they’d come. “We must be hundreds of thousands of miles from Auspice City by now.”
Adrian, who had been quiet for most of the journey, smiled faintly. He held up a finger and traced a slow circle in the air. “The tunnels are long, yes. But they double back. Loop around. We circled Auspice City twice — underground.” He let that land. “We’re directly below it right now.”
Lincoln gave a single cold nod. “Adrian is right. Now follow me.”
After a couple of hours trekking through the dim passage, Lincoln stopped again. 
[SFX: PORTAL OPENING]
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
He reached out and, with a familiar motion, opened another portal—this one leading into a corridor bathed in light. 
[AMBIENT: DEEP UNDERGROUND CHAMBER]
When they stepped through, the space opened up around them without warning — vast and dark, the ceiling lost somewhere above. And there, rising from the ground like a mountain given form, stood a statue.
[MUSIC: AWED SILENCE]
It was a nine-headed snake, carved on a scale that made the group feel like insects at its base. Each of the nine heads had its mouth wrenched open wide, frozen mid-strike, as though the sculptor had caught the creature at the moment of attack. But five of the nine heads were wrong — broken clean from the neck, the stumps ragged, leaving only four intact.
“Five of them are broken,” Freya said quietly, studying the damage. She couldn’t tell if it was age or violence.
Lincoln looked up at the statue for a long moment, reading it. Then he rose into the air and flew straight toward the seventh head.
In a holy land of the Demon Clan far above — the Demon Energy Holy Land — a warrior built like a small mountain watched the scene play out through his surveillance array and broke into a broad grin.
“The seventh head. This one can actually open the seventh tunnel.” He turned to the warriors behind him. “Get the disciples ready. If he pulls this off, I want our people inside before the dust settles.”
The ancient Supreme Lord had created nine different ways to open his underground palace, and each method was passed down in absolute secrecy. No two were alike, making them nearly impossible to crack. To make matters worse, the Lord had placed a particularly nasty enchanted barrier inside. For a Soul Sea or Spirit Supreme warrior, the barrier was a massive obstacle. For a World Lord or a Supreme Lord, it would be trivial to break—but that was the trap. If the barrier took even a scratch of damage, everything inside the palace would be instantly destroyed. That one little detail meant no one could ever force their way in.
