Ep 424 - Bet on It

Word count: 1062

[AMBIENT: QUIET SHOP]

[MUSIC: TENSE NEGOTIATION]

Being a rune expert was like having a license to print money. The training was a steep investment, but the massive returns meant Cedric had built himself a hefty savings account over the years. 

Still, ten supreme Aether crystals was a huge chunk of change, even for him.

The shopkeeper, who was even wealthier, wasn’t about to pay that much. A meditation rune was only good for steadying one’s nerves during practice; it was completely useless in a fight. It simply wasn't worth the exorbitant price.

Besides, the biggest mystery was where Zane had even found such a valuable rune.

Everyone knew the most expensive runes came from Arnold, the legendary "Son of Runes." Though their power had long since faded, his works still commanded sky-high prices at auction. They were the last he ever created, and his death left a void in the Heartlands that no one had since managed to fill since. His final runes were essentially collector's items.

But the rune sitting right in front of them was still active. That meant its creator was still alive. It might not be a collector's item yet, but a living expert of this caliber could crash the entire market for Arnold's work, rendering his "collectible" runes worthless overnight.

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

A rune’s value skyrocketed after its creator died, just as Arnold's had. But a living expert with this level of talent? That was a treasure beyond measure. Any sect would kill for such an artist. Letting them slip away would be a catastrophic mistake. As the gears turned in his mind, the old man realized he couldn't just walk away from this.

[SFX: COINS CLINKING]

A small smile touched Zane's lips. "Alright. Ten supreme Aether crystals, it is."

[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

The moment Zane agreed, Cedric immediately pulled ten gleaming supreme Aether crystals from his Storage Ring and handed them over.

When an item is one-of-a-kind, its price can soar into the stratosphere, affordable only to the ultra-rich. But the more of that item that exists, the less each one is worth. The old man had already considered this, which was why he’d let Cedric win the bidding war.

"Are these still active?" the shopkeeper asked, his eyes scanning the new set of runes carefully.

Zane didn't say a word. Instead, he channeled seven streams of Vitality from his wrist into the seven divine textures. 

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL HUM]

Instantly, they ignited, flooding the room with a brilliant green glow.

The old man’s eyes gleamed. He couldn't believe it. For a split second, his heart hammered against his ribs, but he quickly reined in his emotions, forcing himself to remain objective. "Interesting," he said, his voice steady. "I'll take all of them. But as for the price…" He trailed off, his gaze sweeping over the runes again as he calculated their market value.

He wasn't about to pay ten supreme Aether crystals for each, not when seven more had just appeared on the market. "Your runes appear to be a new type," the old man said with a sly grin, "and given the perfect brushwork, they're probably thirty percent more effective than standard ones. But, as you know, meditation runes have limited applications. So… how about five supreme Aether crystals for all seven?" He was shamelessly lowballing, pricing each rune at less than a single crystal. It was a take-it-or-leave-it power play.

But his offer was actually quite reasonable. These divine textures weren't for combat; they were just prototypes from Zane's practice sessions. Even if they were more efficient than the typical meditation runes in the Heartlands, they couldn't be sold for an outrageous price. The old man was mostly paying for the novelty—a pattern he'd never seen before and considered well worth studying.

Cedric had shelled out ten supreme Aether crystals in the heat of the moment, driven by a stubborn refusal to let the old man win. The old man had simply backed down, stunned by the bid. Now, it looked like he was about to get the last laugh.

Zane smiled. "Deal." After all, he had nothing to lose. They were just prototypes, and five supreme Aether crystals was more than a fair price.

[SFX: ANGRY OUTBURST]

Cedric exploded the instant Zane agreed."No! I object! That's not fair!" He’d just paid ten supreme Aether crystals for a single meditation rune, and now this old man was getting seven of them for half that price? He felt completely scammed. He wasn’t about to let the old man walk away with seven runes for a paltry five crystals.

"Why not?" Zane asked coolly. He wasn't one to hold a grudge, but Cedric’s earlier mockery still stung. The man had tried to humiliate him, and Zane wasn’t about to let it slide.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Cedric shot back, his voice thick with bitterness. "I just gave you ten supreme Aether crystals for one rune!" "How can he buy all seven for only five? The difference is insane! It's not fair!" The initial joy he’d felt from acquiring a perfectly crafted rune evaporated, replaced by the sour taste of being cheated.

Zane ignored him, taking the five crystals and the calligrapher's supplies from the old man.

As he turned to leave, Cedric blocked his path. "Come on, man, please!" he begged.

The old man just shook his head, watching Cedric make a complete fool of himself. In the Cloud Hall, Cedric was held in higher esteem than most Elders, his status respected by all. No one would have ever imagined seeing him eat humble pie in front of someone he considered so far beneath him.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]

Zane stopped. He stared at Cedric, then his gaze drifted to the dark green runic quill. A slow, knowing smile spread across his face. "Fine," he said, "you've twisted my arm. I'll reconsider, but on one condition."

"Name it!" Cedric said, jumping at the chance.

[SFX: DRAMATIC STING]

"If I can prove I drew these runes myself," Zane chuckled, his eyes locked on Cedric's, "you give me your runic quill." He watched the man’s expression shift, enjoying every second of this.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

Cedric was trapped. He had dug his own grave. He couldn't stand the thought of losing his priceless runic quill, especially not to someone he saw as a nobody. But he had to save face.

If he agreed and Zane failed, he'd regain his reputation and put this arrogant kid in his place. 

But what if he lost? What if Zane could actually prove it?
