 A bewildered murmur rippled through the assembled pledges, their faces etched with confusion and curiosity. Questions darted between them like nervous birds.
"Why has Isla Novak summoned all of us to the plaza?" someone whispered, voicing the thought that occupied everyone's mind.
[AMBIENT: AUTUMN BREEZE]
 The autumn breeze carried a chill as they waited, shifting from foot to foot. It took another fifteen minutes for every last pledge from Misty Peak to trickle in and gather, their collective anxiety mounting with each passing moment.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
 Isla Novak stood on the raised platform, her posture rigid with tension. Her piercing gaze swept anxiously over the sea of faces until she found him—Zane, standing near the back of the crowd, his expression guarded yet attentive. A flicker of relief crossed her elegant features, softening her countenance momentarily before she squared her shoulders and addressed the assembly. Her voice emerged slow and deliberate, each word carefully measured.
"I've gathered you here for a task," she announced, her tone betraying the gravity of whatever lay ahead.
"What kind of task?" a tall pledge with auburn hair questioned, his voice carrying above others.
"Has to be something big if she called all of us," remarked another, nervously fidgeting with the hem of her tunic.
"We usually just volunteer for stuff," a third voice added, tinged with apprehension. "Why the big summons? Something feels different this time."
"Most of you have never encountered a demon before," Isla said. "Understand this: what makes them dangerous isn't only their strength. Every demon innately radiates demonic energy — and that energy targets the human mind directly. It instills fear. It makes you hesitate. It is why human cultivators have lost to demons far weaker than themselves. The first step to killing a demon is not strength. It is the ability to hold your mind steady against that pressure."
She paused, letting that sink in. 
A fresh wave of whispers erupted like sudden rainfall, students turning to one another with furrowed brows and concerned glances.
[SFX: CROWD FALLING SILENT]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]
 The pledges buzzed with confusion. Isla waited patiently, letting the murmurs fade to silence. Then she announced, her voice carrying across the plaza, "Your task is to chop the demon."
"A demon?"
"Chop a demon?"
"You can't be serious."
"They're sending *pledges* after a demon? Are they trying to get us killed?"
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
 The crowd erupted in hushed, panicked whispers again. Some of the older pledges started filling in the newer ones. Any warrior in the Empire grew up hearing the old battle cries: ‘Chop the demon’ and ‘Kill the Ogre.’ The strange part was that, hundreds of years later, ‘chop the demon’ was still terrifyingly relevant. ‘Kill the Ogre,’ not so much.
[MUSIC: EPIC HISTORICAL]
 The continent's blood-soaked history was etched into every child's memory: humans, demons, and ogres trapped in an endless, vicious triangle of warfare. As centuries passed, the power dynamics shifted dramatically. The once-mighty human kingdoms and fierce ogre clans gradually weakened, their resources depleted and populations decimated, while the demonic forces only grew more formidable with each passing generation.
The situation deteriorated until demons stood at the precipice of total victory, poised to extinguish both rival races from existence. Facing such dire extinction, humans and ogres refused to accept their fate meekly. Ancient hatreds were reluctantly set aside as the timeless wisdom prevailed: when confronted by a greater evil, former enemies must unite.
Thus emerged a fragile alliance born of desperation, solidified through strategic marriages between royal bloodlines. The imposing Ogre Princess Aria, known for her tactical brilliance and surprising diplomatic skills, wed into the illustrious Empire, while the graceful Lorelei Sinclair from the gleaming Sunstone Citadel—beloved for her compassion and fierce determination—became betrothed to a distant Ogre warlord whose territories bordered the most dangerous demon strongholds. These unions, uncomfortable as they might be for the participants, served as the tenuous bonds holding two historically antagonistic races together against the nightmarish forces that threatened to consume them all.
 Isla's commanding voice sliced through the impromptu history lesson, silencing the murmurs instantly.
 "Your task will be to follow a designated trail in the Sky Forest to locate and slay demons," she announced, her steely gaze sweeping across the anxious faces before her. "We are not asking you to hunt down demons in The Crucible," she clarified, noting the pallor spreading across many young faces. "This is merely a test, not a declaration of war. Visualize it this way: five concentric circles will be marked throughout the forest, each wider than the previous one."
She paused, allowing the information to sink in. A slight smile—perhaps of anticipation, perhaps of amusement—played at the corners of her lips as she observed the mixture of terror and intrigue rippling through the crowd.
"Over the next three days," Isla continued, her voice dropping to an almost intimate tone that somehow carried to every ear, "we'll evaluate which among you possesses the courage and skill to venture closest to this demon. The top three who slay the most demons will each receive a mid-grade Spirit Weapon as their reward."
A mid-grade Spirit Weapon. The words landed like a thunderclap. Even a high-grade Mysterious Weapon was considered generous for a pledge — a mid-grade Spirit Weapon was a full tier above that. The crowd's fear began competing with something else: ambition.
“The apprentices who demonstrate such valor will earn not just points, but the supreme honor of slaying it themselves!"
[SFX: CLOTH WHIPPING]
 With a dramatic flourish that betrayed her royal upbringing, she flicked her wrist, whipping away the yellow cloth that had concealed the creature.
[SFX: CROWD GASPING]
[AMBIENT: MALEVOLENT AURA]
[MUSIC: DARK MAGIC]
 A wave of terrified gasps swept through the crowd as a palpable aura of malice washed over them. The pledges in the front stumbled back instinctively, and the whole group surged backward until they were a good seventy feet away.
The demon had a vaguely human shape, but it was bloated and grotesque, its body covered in pasty, brownish-yellow skin. Its two bulbous eyes swiveled wildly in its head, occasionally locking onto one of the pledges.
"It's looking at me! It's looking at me!" one of them shrieked, falling to the ground. He scrambled backward on his hands and feet, kicking wildly as a dark stain spread across the front of his pants.
Though some maintained their composure, they instinctively recoiled and edged backward whenever the demon's unsettling gaze swept across them, their bravado crumbling under its malevolent stare.
Isla surveyed the retreating pledges with icy disappointment etched across her features. Her lips curled into a contemptuous frown as she shook her head. "This demon has been thoroughly restrained and cannot retaliate," she declared, her voice cutting through the tense silence. "If any of you aspire to claim the glory of slaying it, you must first demonstrate the courage to approach it!"
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
 The challenge hung in the air like a gauntlet thrown, the words still resonating through the assembly when a solitary figure broke ranks from the mass of hesitant pledges. With deliberate steps that echoed across the suddenly silent courtyard, he advanced forward and executed a crisp, military salute.
"Master Novak," Johnson announced, his voice unwavering despite the palpable fear surrounding him, "I request the honor of being the first to attempt this trial."
