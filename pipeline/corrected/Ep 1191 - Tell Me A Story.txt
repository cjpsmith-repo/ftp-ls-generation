Ep 1191 - Tell Me A Story

Word count: 1013

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Zane was just about to put the Roaring Token away when he heard her voice once more, saying she’d ask around about Elara - for a price. 
He froze, a flicker of hope cutting through him.He snatched the token back up and activated it. “So you’ll help me?” he asked, unable to keep the excitement out of his voice.
“I can ask around,” she replied. “That’s it. No promises I’ll actually find this Elara of yours.”
“You said you had a condition,” Zane pressed, his stomach twisting into a knot. “What is it?”
A million possibilities raced through his mind. What could she possibly want? He’d do anything, he told himself, but he was stuck here in Elysian Keep. If she needed him to run some errand, it could jeopardize his own mission. That was a risk. But for Elara, he had to take it. He couldn’t give up now.
After a moment of silence, a soft chuckle echoed from the token. “Just one condition. Tell me a story. I’m dying of boredom in here.”
Zane’s head just… dropped. He was so stunned, his thoughts screeched to a halt. When he finally managed to look up, he didn’t know whether to laugh or cry.
Lavender’s voice cut in flatly from the side. “Like I said. The woman’s an idiot.”
[AMBIENT: UNIVERSE-WIDE MURMUR]
She wasn’t the only one with that reaction. Across the universe, the creatures who had been listening to the broadcast went quiet for a moment — and then erupted. A Roaring Token was one of the rarest objects in existence. Most Supreme Lords had fewer than a hundred in their entire lifetimes. The idea of burning through them to tell a story was so absurd it had rendered the better part of the universe briefly speechless.
“Is there anything more extravagant than this?” the murmurs ran. “Only someone with an unlimited token could even attempt it. Who is this woman?”
But it wasn’t just Zane who was bewildered. As the silence stretched, stories began to flood his mind. Coming from a place as vast and chaotic as his home world, there was no shortage of them—epics and legends he’d never even read himself. One, however, stood out. The most famous story, passed down for generations. It was called “The Heroic Wife.” He’d go with that one.
[SFX: TOKEN ACTIVATION HUM]
He activated the token again. “If you can really help me find a way to Elara,” he announced, “then I’ll gladly tell you a great story.”
“Of course,” the woman’s voice whispered back.
[MUSIC: ANCIENT STORYTELLING MELODY]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ECHO]
“A long time ago,” Zane began, “there was a mountain. At the foot of that mountain…”
The tale of “The Heroic Wife” was set a thousand years in the past, a legend told throughout the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms. Zane had never actually read it; he was reciting it now, word for word, from the memories flooding his mind. And though he was speaking only to her, the power of the Roaring Token was absolute. 
His voice wasn’t just reaching her; it was broadcasting across every corner of the universe. And with that, trillions of beings had no choice but to listen.
It was the first time in history anyone had ever told a story to the entire cosmos.
Zane found himself getting lost in the tale. Honestly, it was a genuinely captivating story. It followed a woman from the State of Althea whose husband was framed by a traitor, and her vow to make the man responsible pay, no matter the cost. 
The twists and turns of the plot had countless listeners on the edge of their seats.
He went on for two hours straight and had barely covered half of it.
“…the woman’s sword attack set off a chain of events…”
The story had reached its most critical moment.
And Zane stopped.
[AMBIENT: UNIVERSE-WIDE SILENCE]
It wasn’t for effect. Freya had appeared at the far end of the courtyard, making her way toward him with a question about her cultivation. He set the token down and turned to her.
What followed, across the hundred thousand worlds, was an eruption.
[SFX: DISTANT OVERLAPPING VOICES — INDIGNANT]
Ordinary warriors had no Roaring Tokens and no way to reach him — they could only curse at the sky. But the Supreme Lords did have tokens. And after a moment of stunned silence, they started using them.
“And then? What happened after the sword attack?”
Another voice, flat and imperious: “If you’re going to tell it, finish it. Don’t stop there.”
“What are you dragging your feet for?”
One by one, Supreme Lords who had spent lifetimes hoarding their tokens were burning them — to demand the end of a story. Zane didn’t respond. He was still talking with Freya.
Then the mysterious singer’s voice cut through all of them.
“He’ll finish when he’s ready. This story was told to me. None of you has any say in when he continues.”
The Supreme Lords went silent. They kept their tokens and waited.
Of course, with the sheer diversity of cultures and languages out there, some of them probably had no idea what he was talking about. Still, a staggering number of creatures were listening intently—not just mortals, but Soul Sea Realm warriors, World Lords, and even a few Supreme Lords had paused to hear the tale.
After another hour, he finally raised the token again.
[SFX: DEEP BREATH BEFORE CONTINUATION]
“Now, where were we? Ah, yes. After the tragedy that struck her husband, she no longer cared about her own life…”
He told the rest of the story over six straight hours. 
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS SWELLING]
By the time he reached the incredibly tragic but beautiful ending, even Zane himself was moved. The people of his world might not have been the most powerful, but whoever wrote this thing was a true genius.
[SFX: WOMAN SOBBING THROUGH TOKEN]
When the last word faded, the only sound was the woman’s soft, broken sobs coming through the token. The ending had clearly gotten to her.
And she wasn’t the only one. 
[AMBIENT: UNIVERSE-WIDE SILENCE WITH DISTANT WEEPING]
Across the universe, countless others who had been hanging on his every word were just as touched. They just didn’t have a Roaring Token to let everyone hear them crying.
