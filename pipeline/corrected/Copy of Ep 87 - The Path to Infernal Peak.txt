Ep 87 - The Path to Infernal Peak
Word Count: 1073
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
The air in the room crackled with tension as Novak and Zane stood face to face.
The sheer force of their personalities seemed to press on the walls, but it was Novak's abrasive energy that dominated the space. Her hostility was a fortress she'd built brick by brick, a defense mechanism forged in the isolation of the Sunstone Citadel, where she'd grown up cut off from the rest of the world.
With a childhood like that, who wouldn't build walls?
But she was still royalty, and no one dared to cross her. She might have left the palace behind, tried to forget the royal blood in her veins, but she'd inherited her parents' stubbornness. It was a part of her she could never outrun.
What she wanted, she got. Period.
It was why the other masters at the Skyreach Sect, the few who knew her true identity, only challenged her when it was absolutely necessary.
The moment they saw the slightest flicker of irritation in her eyes, they'd instantly back down, swallowing their words to avoid a fight. Master Novak was as beautiful as she was terrifying, with a Brimstone Dragon for a temper that woke up the second someone got on her nerves.
And you did not want to be the one to wake the dragon. Right now, as Zane stood before her, it was clear the dragon was stirring.
[AMBIENT: PEOPLE GATHERING]
Drawn by the scent of a coming storm, several masters from Misty Peak hurried over, trying to figure out what had set her off. Her face was a thundercloud, her opinion on Zane's request written in the hard line of her mouth.
The mountain at the heart of their argument was located at the very edge of the Skyreach Sect, a place no one in their right mind would visit.
[AMBIENT: VOLCANIC RUMBLING]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DARK]
As Zane finally drew close, a jagged, black monolith clawed at the sky, blocking his path.
Not a single tree or blade of grass grew on its slopes.
[SFX: SULFUROUS WIND]
From a distance, the mountain was pure, imposing black. It was obvious why they called it Infernal Peak. A dense column of gray smoke poured relentlessly from its crater, as if the underworld itself were exhaling. The smog carried the sharp, rotten-egg stench of sulfur, choking the air for miles around.
A knot of dread tightened in Zane's gut.
To think Elara might be trapped in a hellscape like this... The closer he got, the more the smoke burned his lungs, but he pushed forward. The thought of his sister was a familiar anchor in the storm, a flicker of light that always pulled him forward.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON CHANGING TERRAIN]
He finally reached a line etched into the earth, as sharp as a blade. On the side where Zane stood, the ground was lush and vibrant. But on the other side, everything was coated in black gravel. It was the border between heaven and hell.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMERING]
Fascinated, he took a step toward the blackened earth, but two figures shimmered into existence, blocking his path. They were dressed in torn, ragged clothes, looking more like beggars than guards.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
The moment their eyes fell on him, Zane's skin prickled with a thousand tiny needles. An instinct deep in his bones—honed through countless life-threatening encounters—screamed that these two were dangerous. Far more lethal than Randall had been, perhaps even rivaling powerhouses like General Gavin himself.
"Headed to Infernal Peak, boy?" one of them asked, his voice gravelly like stones grinding together. They stood perfectly on the dividing line, their weather-beaten faces impassive as their calculating gaze swept over Zane, assessing his worth and threat level in an instant.
"Yeah, I am," Zane replied, squaring his shoulders despite the cold dread pooling in his stomach. "Who are you?"
"We're the guards of Infernal Peak," the taller one responded, a faint smirk playing at the corner of his cracked lips. "Since you're headed in, we ought to know if you're familiar with the rules." His ragged clothing couldn't disguise the coiled strength beneath—the mark of someone who had survived countless battles.
They didn't display outright hostility, but Zane couldn't shake the persistent feeling of unease crawling up his spine. They studied him with an odd curiosity, their eyes reflecting the harsh wisdom of those who had witnessed countless fools march to their doom. He caught them exchange a fleeting, knowing glance—the kind that silently acknowledged a kid with his apparent low power level was a rare, and probably doomed, visitor to their domain.
"One thing before you go in," the guard said. "Once you cross that line, the sect's protection ends. If someone kills you in there, the Skyreach Sect won't intervene. With your cultivation level, that's worth knowing."
"It's one hundred Points to get in, right?" Zane asked, maintaining a steady voice despite the thundering of his heart. The closer he came to finding Elara, the more real the danger became—yet he couldn't turn back now.
[SFX: ENERGY SCANNER]
Before they could answer, he pulled out his ID and held it out. One of the guards took it and channeled his orange-red Vital Energy into the device. After a quiet hum, one hundred Points were deducted, and he handed it back.
[AMBIENT: SCORCHING HEAT]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Stepping past them was like walking into a furnace.
[SFX: CRACKLING FIRE]
The view from the foot of the mountain was staggering. The entire peak was engulfed in fire. The ground itself seemed to be made of glowing charcoal, smoldering under a permanent haze of smoke. Everything he'd heard was true.
From the mountainside, he could see scattered valleys below. Some were buried in ash, while others churned with rivers of molten magma.
Zane had already passed several people from the Skyreach Sect—exiles, banished here as punishment. The people he crossed paths with were no pushovers. In fact, they were all terrifyingly powerful. Some had even transformed into Nature Spirits, and judging by their menacing auras, a few were even stronger than Randall.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
Most couldn't care less and ignored him as he passed. A few, however, fixed him with hungry, predatory stares. He felt their eyes on his back, and soon, the crunch of footsteps began to tail him.
[SFX: WEAPON UNSHEATHING]
The message was clear: he wasn't welcome here. Realizing he was prey on Infernal Peak, Zane slipped the Shattered Dagger from his Storage Ring. His hand tightened around the hilt as he pressed on, searching for any sign of his sister, hoping they could both make it out of this hell alive.
