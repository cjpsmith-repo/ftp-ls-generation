Everyone looked at Lavender to see what would happen next.
Maurice wasn't stupid. He knew all about the powerful Heavenly Feather Sanctuary on the main continent. But would they really go to war for the Violet Heart Sanctuary over this so-called “Queen”? He had his doubts.
Sure, the Demon Night’s top War Lords seemed to respect her, but if a real war broke out between the major sacred places, he figured the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary wouldn’t actually want to get involved.
Esther Sterling rose to her feet. "If our Queen gives the order," she said, her voice carrying the full weight of her rank, "the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary will go through fire and water."
Lavender glanced at her, then at Maurice, and spoke simply. "Whatever Zane says, they'll do."
She didn't elaborate. She didn't need to. Almost everything she had left — every hope of completing her revenge — rested on Zane. Of course, she would back him.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]
Maurice's eyes moved slowly back to Zane.
Zane's smile hadn't changed. "That's why I gave you a month," he said.
"What do you mean?" Maurice asked.
"Use it," Zane said. "Try to flatten the Violet Heart Sanctuary. See how the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary responds."
Maurice was quiet for a moment. Then, slowly, he understood.
If Maurice had been with Zane on his last trip there, he would’ve seen just how deep that loyalty ran. To the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, Lavender was a living god. Her word was law. If she ordered them to attack the Flaming Place, they would do it without a second thought, and the consequences for the Ogres would be devastating.
But that wasn't the only thing Maurice understood. The one-month grace period had done something else entirely — it had passed the decision out of his hands.
Whether the Ogres stayed in the Tower of Sin was no longer his call. It now rested with the Soul Sea Realm masters at the Flaming Place and the Dread Place. Whatever happened next, the responsibility wasn't his to carry alone.
Thinking about it, Maurice felt a wave of relief wash over him.“Ah, I get it now,” he said, standing. He turned to the Ogres beside him and ordered, “Get Bradley’s body out of here. Today’s negotiations are over!”
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
Maurice started for the exit, but after a few steps, he paused and looked back at Zane. “Thank you,” he said gruffly.
The thank-you caught most of the warriors in the hall off guard. Only the sharpest ones, like Lavender and Esther, understood what he meant. Everyone else was just confused. Zane had killed Maurice’s comrade and backed him into a corner that might cost the Ogres their place in the Tower of Sin. Why on earth would he be grateful?
Zane just smiled and shrugged. “No need. We’re just on different sides.”
After the Ogres had gone, Fabian sidled up to Zane. “Zane, will the Demon Night really help our Violet Heart Sanctuary?” he muttered.
“We’ll see,” Zane said with a cryptic smile. “Besides, if the Demon Night doesn’t step in, I can always choose not to kick the Ogres out.”
Fabian’s blood ran cold. He knew he had to get this news back to the Violet Heart Sanctuary immediately so they could prepare for whatever was coming.
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Suddenly, a high-stakes game of war was unfolding across the main continent, all centered on the Tower of Sin.
The last ten years had been surprisingly quiet for such a chaotic place. Aside from the constant expansion of the Flaming Place and the Dread Place into new territories, the other races had mostly kept to themselves. Faced with growing pressure from the two Ogre sanctuaries, they had no choice but to swallow their anger and suffer the expansion in silence, both in the Tower and on the continent.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
But now, the Flaming Place and the Dread Place had finally made their move, declaring open war on the Violet Heart Sanctuary. The news sent shockwaves across the entire continent. The Giants’ sacred place was situated right between the Ogres’ territory and the humans’ Violet Heart Sanctuary, and an open declaration of war between sacred places usually meant one side intended to completely wipe out the other.
What no one could understand was why the Ogres would bother. Destroying the Violet Heart Sanctuary would gain them little — and it meant provoking the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, which loomed behind the Demon Night. The other races watched and waited, certain the humans were finished.
[SFX: DRAMATIC GONG]
[MUSIC: DEFIANT HORNS]
Then the Violet Heart Sanctuary declared war back.
The continent didn't know what to make of it. The Violet Heart Sanctuary declaring war on the Ogres' Sacred Land was like a child squaring up to an armed soldier. It defied every calculation. The humans had nothing — no numbers, no Soul Sea Realm masters to match the Ogres' combined strength. And yet they hadn't run. They hadn't begged. They'd thrown down a challenge.
Which meant they were relying on something. Or someone.
Bit by bit, the pieces filtered out from the Tower of Sin. Every sacred place had spies inside. The picture came together slowly, and then all at once: the humans were relying on Zane.

Ten days later, the Demon Night declared war on the Ogres' Sacred Land.
During the one-month grace period Zane had given them, life inside the Tower of Sin was business as usual—a chaotic mess of slaughter and plunder, just as it had been for decades. On the main continent, however, the three major races were busy making moves, testing each other’s limits.

[SFX: ARMOR CLANKING]
The Violet Heart Sanctuary was on high alert as powerful Soul Sea Realm masters from the Flaming Place and the Dread Place marched toward their territory. At the same time, Amber led a massive force of elite warriors from the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary and set up camp right on the Flaming Place’s border.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE THEME]
The message was crystal clear: if the Flaming Place and the Dread Place attacked the Violet Heart Sanctuary, then both Ogre sacred places would be burned to the ground in retaliation. Even though taking on both sanctuaries at once would mean devastating losses, the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary couldn’t defy an order from Lavender.
This was no time to worry about the cost.
