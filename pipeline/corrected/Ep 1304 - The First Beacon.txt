Ep 1304 - The First Beacon

Word count: 1170

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]
Supreme Lord Sword had never been particularly interested in the warriors who passed through the Corridor of the Emperor. His own path to power, the Godly Way of Sealed Emotion, was a forbidden one. He’d made peace with that long ago, and with it, the idea that his techniques would die with him.
So, he ignored them. Countless warriors had trudged past his flame, and he hadn’t given a single one a second thought. A few exceptionally talented swordsmen had even navigated the rain of swords without flinching, their sheer determination carrying them up the mountain. But in the end, he’d driven them all away with his Gods-Intimidating Strike.
But this one… this one was different. 
This kid actually *understood* the Gods-Intimidating Strike.
[MUSIC: AWE AND WONDER]
And his understanding wasn’t just good—it was breathtaking. He had ninety-nine percent of it down. One final piece, just one, and he would achieve the technique’s absolute perfection. On top of that, the kid had already opened thirty-five petals on his spiritual lotus, leaving him just one shy of true Consummation.
To Supreme Lord Sword, it was like discovering a flawless gem with a single, tiny piece missing. And he was holding that missing piece right in his hand. All he had to do was reach out and set it in place, and Zane would be perfect.
Zane stood frozen, absorbing the praise in stunned silence.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
After looking him over from head to toe, Supreme Lord Sword finally asked, “Have you ever had a master?”
“Yes,” Zane answered honestly.
[MUSIC: BITTERSWEET MELODY]
A flicker of disappointment crossed the old swordsman’s ethereal eyes, but it vanished as quickly as it came, replaced by a faint smile. In a way, he was already half a master to Zane, having taught him the Gods-Intimidating Strike through the trial itself.
“Do you know that the Divine Path of Sealed Emotion is a forbidden one?” he asked.
Zane nodded. “Yes. I found that out a while ago.”
“Then do you know *why* it’s forbidden?”
“I don’t,” Zane admitted.
He was, after all, only at the Soul Sea Realm. His knowledge of cultivation was limited to the Spirit Supreme Realm and below. He knew that a warrior at that level could manifest beings from their own mind, but the vast gulf between the Spirit Transformation Realm, World Lords, and a Supreme Lord was a total mystery. As for the Godly Ways, he knew them only by name. When it came to what they really meant, he was like a kid staring at a world far too complex to grasp.
“It’s okay that you don’t know,” Supreme Lord Sword said, having no intention of explaining. Instead, he got to the heart of the matter. “Your Gods-Intimidating Strike is nearly perfect, but it’s still missing one thing. Do you know what it is?”
[MUSIC: TENSE PULSATING]
At that, Zane’s expression turned dead serious. He’d been stuck at ninety-nine percent for six long years, that final, crucial element always just out of reach.
Six years, and nothing had moved it. He’d thrown himself into Eight Smoky Melodies and every other technique he had, hoping something would shake the missing piece loose. Lavender had searched for an answer on his behalf. Even the Cobalt Dragon had weighed in. None of it worked. In the end, it was the Cobalt Dragon who’d said it plainly: if Zane couldn’t find the answer in himself, then it had to come from somewhere outside him.
“Senior, please enlighten me,” Zane said.
Supreme Lord Sword studied him for a long moment. “If you want your answer, go to the Sword Manor. What you’re missing, you’ll find there.” It was no ordinary place—founded generations ago by sword cultivators with nothing but their own conviction, it had grown over countless years into the greatest holy land the sword path had ever known. The Killing Sword Mountain stood inside its borders.
“The Sword Manor,” Zane repeated, committing the name to memory.
[SFX: SWORD HUMMING]
Supreme Lord Sword raised a hand, and the white sword drifted to Zane’s side, a thread of green light tracing down his arm. When it faded, a faint sword-shaped mark lingered on his skin a moment before sinking beneath the surface and disappearing.
“That’s all I can do,” Supreme Lord Sword said, something complicated behind his eyes. “Go on, now.”
[SFX: DEEP BOOM]
The mountain, the swords, all of it dissolved at once, folding back into the flame on the torch. In that last instant, Zane thought he caught something troubled behind Supreme Lord Sword’s calm—but whatever weighed on a True God wasn’t something Zane could do anything about. He let the thought go.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]
[AMBIENT: DISTANT UNSEEN PRESENCE]
Elsewhere, within his own flame, Supreme Lord Sword’s gaze lingered on the boy’s retreating back.
Someone had gone to enormous lengths to bury this boy’s fate from the world. So why push him down the very path meant to be walked alone?
He didn’t have an answer. Only the question.
[AMBIENT: VAST ECHOING HALL]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
Lost in thought, Zane walked on until he saw a bright opening ahead: the exit of the Corridor of the Emperor. He stepped out into a vast, circular hall where all five corridors converged. A few other warriors were already there, waiting.
“Janet, Hailey!”
The two women from his group had made it through.
“Zane, we’re so glad you’re okay!” Janet said, her smile bright and sweet.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
As the three of them looked at each other, they could all sense a subtle shift, a new depth to one another that hadn’t been there before. It was the kind of change that was bound to happen after brushing up against the Godly Way. Even if a warrior didn’t find a path that suited them, just passing the soul flames of the True Gods left an imprint. The aura alone was a trial and a blessing.
“The Fifth Hall of Causality was pretty easy,” Hailey said, her tone as flat as ever.
“Which True Gods did you meet?” Janet asked, her eyes alight with excitement. “I ran into the legendary Master Sheldon!”
Zane just nodded and casually named a few of the figures he’d encountered—it wasn’t a secret—but he left out his meeting with Supreme Lord Sword.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ECHOING]
[AMBIENT: MEDITATIVE CHANTING WHISPERS]
As they were talking, a figure flickered at the mouth of another corridor, and Coleman stepped into the hall.
His hands were pressed together in a prayer-like gesture, his eyes slightly closed as he mumbled something under his breath. He looked like a monk who had just reached enlightenment. Once he was fully out of the corridor, he smiled at the others.
“I received guidance from The First Beacon,” he announced.
In The Hallowed Concord, they didn’t have the concept of a Godly Way. Their Monastic Path was essentially the same thing, and The First Beacon was one of their most revered spiritual ancestors.
