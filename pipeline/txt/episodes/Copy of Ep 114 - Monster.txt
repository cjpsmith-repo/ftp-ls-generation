[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
[SFX: KNIFE THROUGH FLESH]
Zane's Shattered Dagger sliced through demonic flesh with unparalleled precision, its edge so keen that virtually nothing could resist its lethal bite. He wielded the blade with calculated savagery, each deliberate stroke carving progressively deeper wounds into the demon general's form. His movements, though appearing frenzied to observers, followed a deadly rhythm born of rage and determination.
[SFX: DEMONIC SHRIEK]
"Die! Die! Die!" he roared, his voice raw with fury. "Return to the abyss where you belong!"
Common knowledge held that demons possessed remarkable regenerative abilities; even grievous injuries could mend themselves within moments if given the slightest opportunity. Zane, acutely aware of this demonic resilience, pressed his advantage relentlessly. The creature's agonized shrieks only fueled his assault as he refused to grant his adversary even a heartbeat's respite.
Blood splattered across his face and chest as he continued his merciless onslaught, his eyes burning with an intensity that transcended mere combat. This wasn't simply about survival anymore—it had become execution, punishment, retribution. The knife flashed in the dim light, each strike more devastating than the last.
[SFX: FINAL KILLING BLOW]
[MUSIC: VICTORY THEME]
When he finally ceased, the demon general had been reduced to an unrecognizable mass of severed limbs, mangled torso, and detached head strewn across the blood-soaked earth. Chest heaving, Zane stood over the remains, his knuckles white around the knife's hilt as he scrutinized the carnage, ensuring the abomination had truly ceased to exist. Satisfaction mingled with exhaustion in his expression as he confirmed his grisly work was complete.
[SFX: RETREATING FOOTSTEPS]
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD AFTERMATH]
At the sight of their general's horrific end, the Demonic Grunts broke rank and fled for their lives. The demon’s blood was splattered all over Zane, cloaking him in a heavy aura of death.
The apprentices from Misty Peak just stared, a flicker of fear in their eyes. But it wasn't the demon's lingering malice that scared them. It was Zane. The sheer brutality of the kill had shaken them to the core.
In that moment, Zane embodied Death incarnate, his blood-drenched figure evoking the visage of the Grim Reaper himself. Though merely half a step into the Nature Level, the murderous aura radiating from his form pulsed with such intensity that even a seasoned Nature Spirit would have retreated in terror. The air around him seemed to vibrate with the aftershocks of his unbridled rage.
[MUSIC: SOMBER AFTERMATH]
Cliff, his earlier antagonism dissolved by what he'd witnessed, quietly sheathed his sword and approached Zane with newfound respect. He studied the glistening wound on Zane's shoulder, noting how the young warrior seemed barely aware of the injury. Reaching to his belt, Cliff extracted a small, ornate bottle containing a luminescent liquid.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMERING]
"Here," he offered, his voice softer than usual as he shook a pearl-sized pill into his calloused palm. "Take this. It's a Healing Potion from the Apothecary." His eyes, usually sharp with judgment, now reflected a complex mixture of awe and gratitude as he extended his hand toward the blood-spattered savior. Zane took it without a second thought and swallowed it. The Healing Potion wasn't nearly as potent as the Crimson Haze Pill Isla had given him, but for a Core Apprentice, even a single Healing Potion was a small fortune.
When they first got off the Skycruiser, Cliff and the other Varsity members had talked plenty of trash about Zane. But Zane had just saved all their lives. A Healing Potion felt like the least he could do to make up for it.
Meanwhile, Quentin and the other Varsity members moved to the bodies of the Demonic Grunts, methodically harvesting a small, gray bead from each corpse.
[SFX: POISON NEEDLES WHISTLING]
"Demon Hearts!" Zane hissed, his keen eyes narrowing to slits as he recognized the deadly projectiles. Each gleaming needle carried enough venom to drop a full-grown man in heartbeats. The lethal poison, harvested from the rarest venomous creatures of the Blackwood, would render Cliff's flesh necrotic within moments of contact.
[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
In a fluid movement that defied human limitations, a shadow darted forward.
It was Zane, his face set with grim determination.
[SFX: NEEDLES STRIKING BODY]
Without hesitation, he had positioned himself as a living shield between Cliff and certain death, his body absorbing the dense cloud of metallic spikes that whistled through the air. The needles struck him with such force that they should have shredded flesh from bone, embedding themselves across his torso and limbs in a terrifying constellation of potential death.
"Zane, are—are you okay?" Cliff stammered, his voice cracking with shock and nascent guilt. The color had drained from his face as he stared at his unlikely savior, expecting to witness the gruesome final moments of the man who had already saved his life once today.
[SFX: NEEDLES FALLING]
Zane merely offered a faint, almost bemused smile. With casual indifference, he brushed the deadly needles from his clothing as one might sweep away autumn leaves. "I'm fine," he replied, his voice carrying an unsettling tranquility. "Feels like getting pricked by embroidery needles. They can't even break the skin." His eyes held a distant look, as though he himself was only beginning to understand the true extent of his own transformation.
Cliff and the others were stunned into silence. The Needle Storm was one of the Blackwood Clan's most infamous secret weapons. The attack was inescapable, relying on sheer numbers and speed. The needles themselves were impossibly thin, designed to pierce a Nature Creature's Life Force and deliver a fast-acting poison directly into the bloodstream. They were nearly impossible to defend against.
And yet, Zane had just shrugged them off like they were nothing.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
As they gazed at his serene countenance, a singular thought seized their minds: monster.
Nothing else could explain the phenomenon before them.
A being who dismembered demons with his bare hands and emerged from a lethal Needle Storm without a scratch defied human classification. He existed beyond mortality—an immortal beast walking among men.
[SFX: NEEDLE FLICKING]
Zane flicked the last poisonous needle from his shoulder with casual indifference and pivoted toward the distant bonfire, gesturing for the team to follow. He had advanced barely three paces when a commanding voice sliced through the silence.
"Stop!"
