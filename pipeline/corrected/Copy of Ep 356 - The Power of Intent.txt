Ep 356 - The Power of Intent
Word count: 1031

[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUND]

It's no wonder Zane and the others were so confused. Gaining sword intent was a mysterious process, without any formalized method; some cultivators were just lucky enough to grasp it through their own intuition and perseverance.

Finn's mastery of sword intent had transformed him into a powerful opponent, capable of striking down adversaries from practically anywhere. Throughout the All Peaks Competition, he had barely come across a worthy challenger who could match his skill—until Zane. That was because Zane, like Finn, had a profound understanding of sword intent; they had both reached its Apex during the tournament, their blades singing with power that few could comprehend, let alone withstand.

With two disciples reaching the Apex of Sword Intent in a single competition, Alistair’s “Accomplished Sword Intent” suddenly didn’t seem so special anymore. His pride suddenly appeared hollow compared to what Zane and Finn had manifested.

In truth, achieving that level of mastery never came easily to anyone. 

Second-tier sects put a huge emphasis on sword intent, elevating it above most other martial pursuits. Even Cloud Hall actively encouraged its disciples to study at least its fundamentals, their elders insisting that cultivators who grasped even the faintest hint of sword intent had significantly enhanced prospects for advancement. 

Zane had often noticed how Sir Kenneth's eyes would light up with rare approval whenever sword intent was demonstrated properly. This widespread recognition of its value drove all sects to urge their members toward its pursuit with almost religious fervor.

[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]

Streaks of sword intent crackled to life along Alistair’s Dragon Sword. A smug smile twisted his lips as he pointed the tip of his blade at Finn. “Draw your sword,” he demanded. “Let’s see what you’ve got.”

Hearing the challenge, Finn just offered a faint smile. His Blood-Corroding Sword trembled slightly in his hand as, in response, his own sword intent began swirling around the blade like tiny cyclones. The energy grew more distinct with each passing moment, the hidden power within it coalescing into a perfect form.

Pushed past his breaking point by the psychological pressure, Alistair finally made his move.

[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]

“Streaming Swordsmanship!”

[SFX: SWORD CUTTING AIR]

As he swung his Dragon Sword, ripples spread through the air. Alistair lunged, his blade tracing perfect, hypnotic curves in the sky that created a strange illusion. It looked as if he could end the fight right there, stabbing Finn with a single, unstoppable thrust.

[SFX: SWORD SLASH]

Both Finn and the audience saw the blade coming straight for him.

[SFX: ELECTRIC CRACKLE]

For a split second, Finn’s mind clouded over. But then, a flicker of lightning crackled in his mind’s eye, and his thoughts became crystal clear.

“The Streaming Swordsmanship uses the properties of flowing water to trick the eye. Impressive,” Finn noted coolly, “but your sword is no match for my Celestial Blade!”

Just as Alistair’s attack was about to land, Finn executed a Sword Step, dodging left and then right.

“Sword Step… he’s mastered that, too,” Alistair mumbled to himself. Seeing Finn so easily evade his Streaming Swordsmanship, Alistair’s frustration boiled over. He was starting to think the gods themselves must have unfairly blessed Finn.

What Alistair didn’t know was that Finn’s skill had nothing to do with blessings from the gods. It was forged in relentless training.

Finn hadn’t taken a single day off since he first picked up a sword. For more than a dozen years, he’d carried his blade everywhere, even when he ate and slept.

To make the kind of progress Finn had, a warrior couldn't just rely on luck. It took grueling effort, relentless hard work, and the persistence to endure any hardship.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[AMBIENT: THUNDER RUMBLING]

After putting some distance between them, Finn raised his Blood-Corroding Sword toward the sky. Instantly, the very energy of Heaven and Earth began to surge. A moment later, streaks of faint thunder and lightning crawled across the clouds.

“This is… The Celestial Blade!” The color drained from Alistair’s face as he felt the terrifying aura wash over him.

Nearby, Eddy’s eye twitched. Finn has mastered The Celestial Blade. He was finding it harder and harder to believe that Finn was only ranked third in the Skyreach Sect. You could beat him half to death, and he still wouldn’t believe it.

Could someone at Level Three of the Adept Realm really be a match for this? How was that even possible?

But whether Eddy believed it or not, Finn had already proven his strength.

[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLING]

[MUSIC: SWELLING VICTORY]

In the churning sky, Finn summoned streaks of heavenly thunder.

When Finn fought Zane during the All Peaks Competition, he hadn't used The Celestial Blade combined with heavenly thunder. Even though he had already mastered merging thunderbolts with his sword intent back in The Sword Tempering Pool, he still needed time to adjust to the new Extreme Thunder Sword Intent he’d just learned.

But after a month of focused training, he had become far more skilled with it.

Alistair had no answer for it. When the thunder came down, it was over.

[SFX: SNORT AND SNEER]

Watching the display, Sir Kenneth snorted and sneered, “Well, we’ll see. Finn is plenty talented, but he only took third place at this year’s All Peaks Competition. The Skyreach Sect just has a limited number of spots, otherwise more of our disciples would’ve joined the Cloud Road to fight it out with your rather large crew.”

If Alistair's skills qualified him for the Cloud Road, then Tracy and Jacob would have been eligible, too. But unfortunately, the Skyreach Sect could only choose three disciples.

“Oh?” Eddy pressed, his curiosity getting the better of him. “Kenneth, if Finn is this gifted and still only came in third… and the winner was some kid who’s only at Level Three of the Adept Realm… I’m confused. How in the world did that guy over there beat Finn?”

In fact, every disciple from the Crimson Sect was wondering the same thing. Since none of them had seen the match, it was hard to believe Zane was actually strong enough to defeat someone like Finn.

Kenneth just laughed at their questions. “There's really only one way to find out," he said with a sly grin. "Send one of your senior disciples to go fight Zane. Then you'll see for yourself exactly how special he is.”
