Ep 1222 - The Tome of Abiding Past

Word count: 1160

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT STONE CHAMBER]
The starlight that had consumed the inner sanctum faded, and with it, the golden glow from the four Dragon-Taming Sentinels.
[SFX: STONE GRINDING AND REFORMING]
But a moment later, a new light flared within their stone bodies, even brighter than before. The statues, which had been shattered into rubble, pulled themselves back together in seconds, looking good as new.
Zane emerged from the shadows, and the flicker of surprise on his face hardened into a cold glare as he took in the four pristine sentinels.
He was stunned. This was the first time he’d ever used Multiple Stars Brilliance, the most explosive move in the Stellar Body technique, and he knew what kind of power it packed. For it to fail… that was something he hadn’t prepared for.
These Dragon-Taming Sentinels were in the final chamber, so he’d expected them to be tougher, but this was something else entirely. Nothing he did seemed to leave a permanent scratch.
This was bad.
What he didn’t know was that someone was secretly pulling the strings, patching up his handiwork.
The sentinels didn’t give him time to think. 
[MUSIC: TENSE COMBAT DRUMS]
[SFX: Dragon Scales SHIMMERING]
[SFX: HEAVY STONE FOOTSTEPS CHARGING]
The Dragon Scales etched on their stone shoulders shimmered as they charged him again, closing in from four sides. Any other warrior would have probably given up by now, but for Zane, this was just one more problem to solve.
His expression was unreadable as he raised Thundergust again. 
[SFX: SWORD DRAWN]
The long, narrow sword had been hanging at his side, but now a faint, deadly intent coiled around the blade before seeping into the air. His gaze was ice-cold as he watched them approach, his focus so absolute he could have been a statue himself.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
Hidden deep within the sanctuary, something flickered in the eyes of the giant Gilded Warden. *Just as I expected,* he thought. *The boy cultivates The Divine Path of Sealed Emotion.* The Warden watched, impressed. *Even though his training isn’t complete, he’s already grasped its cold, cruel sword intent. To do that at his level—the Soul Sea Realm—is pushing his limits. If he can just comprehend that final piece…*
[SFX: LOTUS SPINNING]
The Warden raised the lotus resting in his hand, and it began to spin slowly before him. A single golden seed grew from a fork along its stem. He plucked it free with a huge, careful hand.
[MUSIC: CHANTING TONE]
“All things return to their origin,” the Warden intoned, his voice low and ancient. “The heart of the past cannot be grasped. The heart of the present cannot be grasped. The heart of the future cannot be grasped…”
As he spoke, the seed melted into a single drop of pure golden liquid, rolling and turning in the air before him. Slowly, it began to take shape — limbs, a torso, a head — until it had formed into a small golden figure, its build unmistakably Zane’s.
[SFX: LOW RESONANT HUM]
But partway through the reading, something changed in the Warden’s huge eyes — first surprise, and then, unmistakably, resentment. The golden figure should have taken on Zane’s exact likeness. Instead, its face stayed wrapped in a formless haze, refusing to resolve no matter how long the Warden watched.
“A Fateless,” the Warden finally said, something almost bitter in his tone. “How does a Fateless come to walk in here…”
The reading could go no further. After a long moment, the Warden let it go. “Forget it, then. Let’s see how long you can last.”
[SFX: MAGICAL HEALING ENERGY PULSE]
The golden light healing the sentinels was unnaturally powerful, a gift from the sacred scripture known as the *Tome of Abiding Past*. The Warden’s own heart was rooted in that philosophy: that through the trials of life and death, all things could be restored to how they were before. That was the power now flowing through the statues, knitting them back together every time they were shattered.
But a statue was still just a statue, and that golden light wasn’t endless. 
[SFX: ENERGY FADING]
As Zane attacked again and again, the glow began to weaken.
[MUSIC: SHARP ORCHESTRAL HIT]
[SFX: SWORD FLASH]
He’d already gone cold inside — no thoughts, no hesitation, just a calm, absolute killing intent. Thundergust flashed out in a single stroke, and thin lines opened across all four sentinels’ waists at once.
[SFX: MUFFLED IMPACTS]
Four dull, wet-sounding thuds followed as the statues split in half, top and bottom crashing to the ground separately.
[SFX: GOLDEN LIGHT FLARING]
But the cuts flared gold almost instantly, and some strange, stubborn force dragged the pieces back together before they’d even finished falling. Whatever this ability was, it might as well have been indestructible.
Zane didn’t slow down.
“Again.”
[SFX: SWORD FLASH — RAPID]
“One sword.” The fan-shaped arc of his blade split them again before they could fully reform.
“Two sword.” He didn’t wait for them to recover this time, cutting through the halves to make quarters.
“Three sword.” Quarters became eighths, then smaller still, until what had been four statues were four scattered piles of rubble — even the dragon carvings on their shoulders reduced to fragments no bigger than his palm.
Every broken edge still glimmered faintly gold, the pieces trembling as they strained to find each other and click back into place. He wasn’t going to give them the chance. Once he’d gone this far into the emotion-severed state, there was no half-measure left to take — either he broke these statues for good, or he stayed like this and kept cutting until he did.
[MUSIC: TENSE COMBAT DRUMS]
“Keep trying to recover,” Zane’s emotionless voice echoed through the chamber. “I’ll just grind you all to dust!”
[SFX: STONE SHATTERING]
[SFX: LIGHT FLICKERING]
He swung Thundergust, and the stone sentinels exploded into even smaller fragments. As the light flickered and dimmed, Zane felt a flicker of hope. The battle was almost over.
[SFX: INTENSE MAGICAL FLARE]
[SFX: STONE VIBRATING]
But just as that thought crossed his mind, the golden glow in the rubble flared, suddenly ten times brighter than before. The broken pieces of stone began to vibrate violently, straining to fuse back together.
“What!”
Zane knew something was wrong. The light had been fading—he’d seen it himself. How could it suddenly surge like this?
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
He kept his sword moving, but his eyes darted toward the depths of the sanctuary, a cold suspicion dawning on him.
*Did someone just interfere?*
Meanwhile, the Gilded Warden, whose enlightened heart had already reached the Sovereign State, fell into silent thought. He had been impressed when he saw Zane execute such a flawless, ice-cold sword move. But now, he realized something even more astonishing. The kid was only at the Soul Sea Realm, yet he was using that martial skill without expending any of his Vitality.
How was that even possible?
