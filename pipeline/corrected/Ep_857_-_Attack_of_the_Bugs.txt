Ep 857 - Attack of the Bugs

Word count: 1040

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS CREATURE SOUNDS]

[MUSIC: INTENSE CHASE]

The level-eleven beasts were stronger than Zane could have ever imagined. He put every ounce of his incredible speed into outrunning them, finally diving into the ring of light at the base of the tree. 

He’d been moving too fast to stop. The moment he cleared the halo, he slammed into the ground outside the pyramid and plowed a crater into the earth.

Camilla blinked at him from across the trading post. “Brother — what happened to you?”

Zane climbed out of the crater. His body was fine — a Relic-Class frame didn’t take damage from a fall like that — but his blood was still churning from the impact. Above him, the halos were already flickering.

[SFX: BODY IMPACTS]

Bloody chunks rained down from the densely packed circles of light. Then the geniuses started coming through — pale-faced, most of them smashed out rather than stepping out, crashing into the ground around him. They didn’t have Zane’s body. The landing was not kind to them.

He’d had one terrifying thought as he fell: what if they followed him out? The warriors outside stood no chance against monsters like these. But the bugs didn’t seem interested in him anymore.

[SFX: SWARM BUZZING]

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Spotting the other humans still digging into the tree, the swarm descended on them like a tornado.

Callan was so focused on mining the twenty-seventh floor that he almost missed the commotion. When he finally looked up, the color drained from his face.

“What the hell are those things?” he stammered, staring as a swarm of massive purple bugs descended on them.

[SFX: TERRIFIED SCREAM]

“Ahhh! Help! Help me!” Not far from him, one of the warriors screamed as a single bug snatched him from the tree and flung him into the air.

The gravitational pull here was stronger than on Earth, yet the creature tossed the man around like a rag doll. The power was unbelievable.

“Help me!” the warrior shrieked, helpless against the creature’s strength.

Once he was airborne, the rest of the bugs swarmed him.

[SFX: VIOLENT TEARING]

[MUSIC: HORROR STRINGS]

Their long, thin legs latched on, and they began to rip him apart. They weren’t even eating him—the bugs only consumed ore. They were just tearing him apart for sport.

A warrior, even one from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, couldn’t survive being torn limb from limb by a pack of level-eleven beasts. The man’s screams were cut short.

[SFX: BODY IMPACT]

A moment later, what was left of him fell from the sky in a shower of torn flesh and gore.

[SFX: URGENT FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Callan and the other warriors went pale. Even a fool knew it was time to run. They all leaped from the tree and sprinted for the bottom.

[AMBIENT: BUSY MARKETPLACE]

Outside the pyramid, most of the warriors were oblivious to the chaos that had just erupted inside. They were still haggling with Camilla over the price of their ores. She’d already raked in nearly seventy thousand supreme Aether crystals.

[SFX: COINS CLINKING]

That was a staggering amount of wealth, even for the crown prince of a divine kingdom. Most national treasuries only held two or three hundred thousand. Camilla had made a quarter of that in just nine hours.

In the past, a sum like that would have made her day. Right now, the crystals barely registered. Ancient Heartstones were another matter entirely.

Camilla was quietly frustrated. She’d done everything she could — bought every available stone at inflated prices, dangled rare ores as bait — and it still wasn’t close to what Zane needed. The mine could only produce so many in a year. That was simply the limit.

“Zane Jones!”

A Star Field Divine Kingdom prince hauled himself out of a crater nearby, blood at the corner of his mouth, face white with fury. “You led the purple bugs down to the lower floors!”

The geniuses trading outside went still. It clicked immediately — green Stone-Eater Bugs didn’t go below the thirtieth floor, purple ones didn’t go below the fortieth. Everyone had watched Zane enter the fortieth floor. The conclusion was obvious.

Zane met the prince’s glare. “I didn’t do it on purpose.”

“Someone is dead, and that’s all you have to say?”

Zane shrugged. He couldn’t have predicted what the bugs would do. And it wasn’t going to change anything. He was going to search every floor of this tree until there wasn’t a single Ancient Heartstone left to find.

The prince saw it on his face and turned away in disgust. “Nobody go back in there,” he announced to the crowd. “The purple bugs could be anywhere on the upper floors now.”

“I’m not dying in there,” one of the warriors declared. “I’m going to go scavenge instead!”

“Yeah, let’s go. Better than getting ourselves killed.”

Hearing this, a handful of other warriors agreed, looking both outraged and resigned.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS DEPARTING]

They broke off into small groups and trudged away, circling the pyramid to search elsewhere.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL UNDERTONE]

That’s when Randy walked over. “Zane,” he said, a wry smile on his face. “Don’t tell me you were the one who led those purple bugs down here.”

Zane just nodded. It wasn’t like he could deny it.

“How many were there?” Randy asked. He didn’t seem too broken up about the other warriors—especially since the dead guy wasn’t from their Harlen Divine Kingdom. Just another case of bad luck.

“I think five,” Zane replied.

Randy was quiet for a second, processing that. 

Zane had lured five of those things down at once and gotten away without a scratch. The guy was unbelievable.

“You shouldn’t go back in, Zane,” Randy advised. “The fortieth floor is too dangerous. If you get surrounded by five or six of those things, I don’t think even you could escape.”

But Zane just shook his head, his expression firm. No matter how dangerous it was, he had to go back.

Randy could see he wasn’t going to change Zane’s mind, so he dropped it.

“What’s all this talk about scavenging?” Zane asked before he could head back in.

Randy grinned.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]

“They’re going to go pick through the trash heaps—the old armory, abandoned workshops—hoping to find weapons that aren’t cursed. This whole place is called the Feather Emperor’s Secret Land. It used to be a mine for the Dragon-blooded, and a lot of their weaponsmiths came here to forge stuff. The most famous one was the Feather Emperor himself.”
