Ep 902 - She's After Us

Word count: 1121

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACK]

“I’ll get you out of this Inner World right now,” the girl said, giving a casual wave of her hand in Zane’s direction. 

An invisible force immediately wrapped around him, and his soul shot straight into the sky. As he soared upward, a nearby bolt of lightning cracked through the air, making him flinch.

[SFX: WHOOSH TRANSITION]

The moment he left the Inner World, Zane’s consciousness snapped back into the girl’s mind. As his awareness of her surroundings returned, he heard her voice echo in his thoughts.

“I’ve sealed off the power you released from the Soul Core,” she told him. “It should be fine now.”

As she finished speaking, strands of gray light sank back into her body. Though Zane’s soul was still tethered to her mind, her physical senses were slowly coming back to her.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]

Meanwhile, the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom, War King Wind, and the other warriors stood on the platform, their expressions grim. 

A heavy, depressing atmosphere had settled over them. The longer they waited, the more their hope dwindled. Four hours had passed since the incident, and there was still no word from Zane. They all suspected his soul had been destroyed long ago, but none of them wanted to be the one to say it out loud.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

“We never should have come to the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land,” one of the masters from the Soul Sea Realm said, his face etched with regret. “None of this would have happened if we hadn’t gone for the ancestral dragon egg. And we dragged Zane into it… Now he’s gone.”

“Give up on the Secret Land?” another master shot back. “You think the Master Priest would have stood for that?”

“The egg is a big deal, I know, but Zane was more important,” a third argued. “Besides, it’s not like we’re getting the egg this year anyway. We’ll just have to try again next year.”

“Look, the bottom line is that Zane insisted on coming here himself,” someone else cut in. “It’s not our fault, and it’s not his either. It just happened.”

The argument among the powerful warriors began to escalate.

“Quiet!” War King Wind roared, surging to his feet. Even though every warrior on the platform was a master from the Soul Sea Realm, the authority of an Emperor was absolute. The bickering stopped instantly.

[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]

“I’ll take over a corpse on the twelfth floor,” he announced, his voice tight with anger. “Then we’re heading to the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land.” Their squabbling, on top of his foul mood over Zane’s fate, had pushed him over the edge.

Fiona hadn't said a word through any of it. She just stood apart from the others, her eyes fixed on the stone pillar.

“Father,” she said quietly, “wait a little longer.”

War King Wind looked at his daughter. Even through someone else's body, the grief in her eyes was impossible to miss. He let out a long breath. “Fiona… I'm afraid Zane is already gone.”

Callan shook his head at his sister. “Give it up.”

Fiona pressed her lips together and said nothing. She kept staring at the pillar.

The three War Kings — the Harlen Emperor, War King Wind, and Scott — descended briefly to the twelfth floor to occupy corpses now that the crystal coffin had been opened. When they returned, the Emperor gave the order to move out.

[MUSIC: RELUCTANT DEPARTURE]

The group began moving toward the back of the necropolis, where the stairs led down toward the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. No one was happy about it. They just had no other choice.

Then Fiona moved.

“Sister!” Callan's shout rang out across the platform.

[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: SUDDEN TENSION SPIKE]

She was already crossing the floor toward the stone pillar, closing the distance fast. The Soul Sea masters ahead of her spun around. The Emperor launched himself toward her like an arrow.

Fiona stopped just in front of the woman's motionless body. She looked up at the closed golden eyes and said, steady and calm, “What did you do to Zane?”

Inside, Brianna's voice drifted into Zane's thoughts, amused. “Another one with a death wish. Is she your old flame?”

“Don't touch her,” Zane said immediately.

Brianna said nothing. A faint smile was all he got.

The gray light from the Soul Core had already finished seeping into Brianna's body. The fusion was complete. Zane tried moving her fingers once, twice — then slowly opened her eyes.

It all clicked for Zane in an instant. The girl had just killed a Soul Sea Realm warrior. These men had no idea who was controlling her body now; all they saw was a terrifyingly powerful woman who had just murdered one of their own. It would have been weirder if they hadn't run.

The War King reached Fiona in the same instant those golden eyes opened. He grabbed her and pulled her back hard.

“She's awake — move! Everyone move!”

Fiona was swept along before she could say another word, dragged down the far staircase with the rest of them scrambling close behind.

Since he didn’t know his way around the Secret Land, his only choice was to follow the group from the Union of the Seven Divine Kingdoms. With that thought, Zane grabbed the Storage Ring he’d set aside and pulled out the Black Star. 

[SFX: CHAINS BREAKING]

He swung the weapon in a clean arc, and the chains binding the girl’s legs shattered.

Then, he leaped from the pillar.

Wow, this body is incredible, he thought, feeling the raw power of a Spirit Supreme Realm warrior coursing through him. This is on another level.

[AMBIENT: DUNGEON CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

His own strength was respectable, on par with a Life-and-Death Realm warrior, but without his Dragon Scales, he was no match for a master from the Soul Sea Realm. This was different.

[SFX: HEAVY LANDING]

The moment his feet hit the floor, he felt an unfamiliar weight in his chest shift and settle. A strange look crossed his face. Man, a woman’s body really is different.

[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: CHASE THEME]

But there was no time to dwell on it. Zane quickly found his balance, his feet tapping lightly on the ground before he shot forward like an arrow, chasing after the fleeing emperor and his men.

[AMBIENT: CORRIDOR ECHOES]

Up ahead, one of the warriors shouted back to the group. “We’re safe, guys! That woman’s legs are still chained up!”

“Hmm, no need to run then,” another said, relieved.

“That was too close,” a third warrior panted. “Where did she even come from? I thought Spirit Supreme Realm warriors were banished from this world. Why is one here now?”

[MUSIC: SUDDEN HORROR STING]

Just as they started to relax, one of the masters glanced over his shoulder. The color drained from his face when he saw the woman diving toward them at an impossible speed.

[SFX: PANICKED SCREAMING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE PURSUIT]

“Run!” he shrieked. “She’s still after us!”
