Ep 1201 - Gathering

Word count: 1060

[MUSIC: EPIC ANTICIPATORY ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: COSMIC HUM]
You could feel the buzz across the entire universe. All the rising stars, the next generation of power players, were practically vibrating with excitement for the start of the Illusion Battlefield. Heirs to World Lords and Supreme Lords were geared up and ready, some even managing to get their hands on newly discovered Divine-Class weapons. A Soul Sea Realm warrior couldn’t unlock a weapon’s full power, of course, but it would still give them a massive, undeniable edge.
Every Soul Sea Realm warrior from every corner of the Upper World was set to enter the battlefield within five days. For those in a tough spot, this was more than an opportunity; it was a miracle. Imagine being a warrior trapped in some treacherous, forgotten corner of a supreme world, struggling to survive for hundreds of years with no way out. 
[SFX: CELESTIAL BEAM DESCENDING]
Then, a single beam of light shot down from the heavens. Stepping inside it meant temporary safety, an escape from immediate danger.
The Illusion Battlefield operated on the same principles as the countless Illusionary Arenas scattered across the worlds. A warrior didn’t enter with their physical body, only a projection. Standing in that protective light was like being shielded by the universe itself, so no one had to worry about what happened to their real body. 
That simple spot of light was backed by the universe’s strictest Law of Causality, an absolute rule that not even a Supreme Lord could break.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: DRIPPING WATER IN STONE CHAMBER]
Meanwhile, in a prison deep within a tenth-grade sacred place, a Demon Night woman was chained to a massive bronze pillar. Black raindrops, each one incredibly corrosive, dripped from the ceiling. 
[SFX: LIQUID HISSING]
A single drop could burn a massive crater into the ground; on a person, it dissolved flesh and blood. It was a uniquely cruel form of torture favored by the Demon Night. A prisoner would be tied to the bronze pillar and left for the rain to destroy them. Their body would be riddled with wounds, but no matter how excruciating the pain, they were not allowed to die. The pillar itself had been personally infused with raw vitality by the Demon Night Queen of Life. So after a full day of torture, the pillar would release a surge of life energy, healing the prisoner completely, readying them for the next day.
This woman had endured that cycle of torment day after day, year after year, her body and soul subjected to a level of torture that defied imagination.
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
This woman was Amber—once leader of the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, a Demon Night master who had ascended to the higher realm only after Lavender’s sudden descent left her no choice. She’d hesitated again and again before going through, knowing a war between queens was no place for someone like her. She ascended anyway. The Queen of Punishment never forgot it.
Over the years, Amber had heard Zane’s voice echo across the universe more than once. Each time, relief mixed with something close to pride—this little fellow who’d once walked the main continent now commanded the attention of the entire Upper World. If he ever learned what had become of her, he might find a way to help. But buried this deep in the prison, there was no way to send word. If she’d known this would happen, she’d have crippled her own cultivation and lived out a quiet life in the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary instead.
It was the mysterious girl’s voice that had pulled her back from despair, more than once. That girl seemed to carry real weight in the higher realm—even Heavenly Venerables treated her with caution. And yet she kept pestering Zane to tell stories. Those stories had become a small mercy in the dark, something to hold onto between one corrosive drop and the next.
[SFX: SOFT CHIME]
A spot of light flickered into being just in front of her.
“What is this?” Amber’s eyes narrowed, confusion cutting through years of numbness. It was only as the mysterious girl’s voice explained—this was the entrance to the Illusion Battlefield, open to any Soul Sea Realm warrior—that understanding dawned.
She could enter. This was a chance handed to her by the universe itself, and she wasn’t about to let it slip away.
[AMBIENT: CHAINS DRAGGING]
Bound as she was to the pillar, the light sat only a single step beyond her reach. She pressed her legs together and inched forward, every motion a fight against the restraints.
[SFX: LIQUID HISSING]
A black raindrop struck her thigh mid-struggle, boiling into her flesh, carving a fresh pit into already-ruined skin. She didn’t make a sound. Years of this had burned the reaction out of her long ago.
“Just a little further,” she told herself, stretching until her whole body went taut as a wire.
[SFX: CELESTIAL BEAM DESCENDING]
Her foot crossed into the circle of light. A force seized her instantly, locking her in place, and her consciousness dropped into darkness.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: COSMIC ENERGY HUMMING]
At the same time, something strange was happening in every Illusionary Arena across the universe. These arenas, normally places for warriors to practice and spar, were suddenly and completely sealed off, impossible to enter. 
[SFX: MASSIVE STRUCTURE MATERIALIZING]
Then, above each one, a gigantic pyramid made of names began to form. The names of every Soul Sea Realm warrior from all one hundred thousand supreme worlds were appearing, side by side, the list growing at an incredible speed.
Across the universe, there were over one hundred Supreme Lords, over two hundred thousand World Lords, and hundreds of billions at the Spirit Supreme Realm. But the number of warriors at the Soul Sea Realm was in the trillions. 
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWDS ACROSS WORLDS]
Everyone in the one hundred thousand supreme worlds stared up at the pyramids, curiously searching. The Illusion Battlefield hadn’t officially started, so the names were all jumbled together at the bottom, unranked. Trying to find a specific person was like looking for a needle in a haystack. Still, all the great races and their leaders kept their eyes glued to the lists, hoping to see the names of their own prodigies rise to the top.
Over the next five days, the number of names on the pyramids just kept climbing as a staggering number of warriors joined the fray.
Zane sat quietly on the ground, his eyes closed, resting.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
One hundred and twenty hours passed. Five days and five nights. 
Then a clear, cold voice echoed through the silence, and Zane’s eyes snapped open.
