Ep 953 - Activate the Tower of Sin
Word count: 1134
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL CELESTIAL REALM]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ORCHESTRAL]
The heavens orchestrated the grand era, knowing the fate of every soul across a hundred thousand worlds like a story they’d written themselves. They decided when a person was born and when they would die, every laugh and every tear.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
But there were always glitches in the grand design. Throughout history, a few were born whose destinies the heavens couldn’t touch. In other words, the gods had no idea when or how they’d be born, and no clue how their lives would unfold.
Zane looked completely stunned. He was a man without destiny.
[SFX: DRAGON RUMBLE]
Even the Cobalt Dragon in his mind was caught off-guard, letting out a heavy sigh. I can’t believe it. To think I’d be shown up by a human girl... Zane, not even the heavens know what your future holds.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
Yolande slowly rose to her feet. “That’s why I didn’t interfere when Zane showed up and claimed the golden celestial rank. As for you, members of the Demi-Immortal Race… if you want to persuade Zane to join you, I won’t get in your way. But you will not lay a hand on Kade, who just earned his Silver Berserker Rank.”
The seven members of the Demi-Immortal Race fell silent. In their eyes, Zane was a total anomaly.
Max turned to Zane. “So, will you join our Demi-Immortal Race?”
Zane thought for a moment, his thick brows furrowing. Then, a small smile touched his lips. “Doesn’t look like I have much of a choice, does it?”
The young man in black sneered. “Glad to see you understand your situation.”
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
“Well, since no one can read my fate anyway,” Zane replied, his smile returning, “joining the Demi-Immortal Race doesn’t sound so bad. But I have one condition.”
His friendly expression vanished, replaced by cold steel.
“What is it?” Max asked.
“For the next five hundred years, you will leave the Tower of Sin on the main continent alone.”
[MUSIC: SERIOUS ORCHESTRAL]
Zane had known his move from the start. The tower was the lifeblood for the many races of the main continent, especially the Humans and the Demon Night. Even if the threats from the Flaming Place and the Dread Place were gone tomorrow, the Violet Heart Sanctuary still wasn’t strong enough on its own. He couldn’t just let the Demi-Immortal Race take over.
Max turned the offer over in his mind. Five hundred years was nothing against what they were getting in return.
But before he could answer, Delroy cut in. “Three hundred years,” he said. “Not five hundred. In exchange, we fully unfold the Tower of Sin. Right now it’s only running at half power — thirty floors instead of fifteen. The Lucky Light alone would be worth far more to your people than an extra two hundred years.”
Zane’s eyes lit up for a moment — then narrowed. “The tower sits in the Cursed Land. Anyone above War Sage gets attacked the moment they enter. What’s the point of thirty floors if no one above that level can use them?”
Delroy smiled faintly. “A teleportation array. We inscribe one inside the tower ourselves — one that the tower won’t absorb. War Sages, Soul Sea Realm masters, anyone — they’d be able to enter and leave freely. The curse still applies inside, but as long as they don’t stay longer than a month, it won’t be a problem.”
[MUSIC: DAWNING REALIZATION]
Zane went quiet. He looked at Yolande. “Fabian and Jarrod — are they still at the bottom of the tower?”
Yolande nodded. “They broke through to War Sage and have been cultivating there ever since.”
“Then they’d finally be able to leave.” Zane let out a slow breath, and the last of his resistance went with it. “Three hundred years,” he said. “Deal.”
Yolande sent word to the humans and the Demon Night: evacuate the Tower of Sin.
[MUSIC: SOLEMN MARCH]
When the tower was clear, Yolande led the seven Demi-Immortals to the top. From the bottom, Fabian and Jarrod were brought up.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE STAIRS]
When Fabian saw Zane, his face broke into a wide grin. Jarrod was right behind him, and for once, his usual composure cracked.
Then they heard about the teleportation array.
The relief that crossed their faces was something beyond words. They had made their peace with the Cursed Land long ago — with the cave, the tower, the narrow world they’d been confined to. But peace and acceptance were not the same thing. The thought of walking back into the Violet Heart Sanctuary as War Sages, under their own power, was almost too much to take in.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
“What gives them the right? The Tower of Sin belongs to the main continent!”
“I know, but what are we supposed to do? The legends say the tower belonged to the Demi-Immortal Race in the first place. So it’s not like they don’t have a claim, right?”
“So what if we don’t like it? Did you hear? Those guys are at the Spirit Supreme Realm! You know what that is? It’s a whole level above the Soul Sea Realm. They could wipe out our entire sacred place with a snap of their fingers!” Truth was, the man had no idea what the Spirit Supreme Realm actually was, only that its warriors were leagues more powerful than anyone from the Soul Sea Realm.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING]
[AMBIENT: EARTHQUAKE]
As the crowd of creatures watched, the entire Tower of Sin suddenly began to shake violently. A deep boom rolled through the air.
[SFX: BUILDINGS COLLAPSING]
Buildings in the nearby villages trembled as if a localized earthquake was tearing through the land, and some of the flimsier houses collapsed into dust.
“Are they… are they going to make the whole tower just fly away?” someone stammered.
[SFX: METAL GROANING]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
The tremors went on and on, and then the base of the Tower of Sin began to stretch, groaning like a living creature waking from a long slumber. As it extended, the whole structure climbed higher and higher before their very eyes. The first floor shot into the air, leaving the two giant warriors who guarded its entrance standing on the ground below, utterly dazed.
Flying was forbidden in the Cursed Land, so no one dared take to the air, no matter what was happening.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
The shaking lasted for nearly an hour. When the ground finally fell still, the part of the tower above ground had grown significantly, now standing a staggering sixteen stories tall. The section below ground remained at fourteen floors.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]
Delroy had kept his word. The Tower of Sin was now fully awakened.
