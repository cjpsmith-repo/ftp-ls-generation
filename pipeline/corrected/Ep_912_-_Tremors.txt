Ep 912 - Tremors

Word count: 1096

[AMBIENT: MYSTERIOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Fiona had been acting strange ever since they'd set foot in the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land.

Sure, the drakes were a pain. They were bizarre, unsettling creatures, but they weren't exactly a death sentence. They were just cubs, after all. As long as you were smart enough not to get swarmed, you could handle them.

[SFX: CREATURE GROWL]

Zane glanced over at Fiona before grabbing one of the drakes by the scruff of its neck. “I don’t think this is what’s scaring her,” he said, holding the squirming thing up. “It’s just a baby.”

Even though it looked like a dragon, it wasn't one—not yet. But just the sight of the young beast was enough to send a chill down one's spine. It was easy to imagine what it would be like when it was fully grown.

And if this thing was just a cub, that meant a mother was lurking somewhere nearby. Was she here, in this very land? 

It wasn't a hard conclusion to come to; most of the warriors had already put it together. The real question was what kind of beast gave birth to these things. How strong was she? And could we even fight her?

The thought alone made the men frown.

“What if we end this year’s exploration right now?” Scott suggested, his voice tight. “There will be other chances.”

What he wasn't saying out loud was that with Zane around, the ascension passage to the upper world would be opening soon anyway. The four kingdoms had poured everything into the Reverse Spirit Channel for one reason — to reach the upper world, where they wouldn't have to fight over scraps like the Ancestral Dragon Egg. If that path was nearly open, why risk their lives here?

“But what do we tell the white-haired priest when he asks?” one of the experts asked cautiously.

“Tch,” another warrior snapped, his voice dripping with anger. “Why didn’t he just come in here himself? He’s stronger than any of us, but he’s bullying us because we can’t ascend yet. He’s just dangling junk from the upper world in front of us to get us to do his dirty work!”

War King Wind listened in silence. Then he nodded. “Alright. When the White-Haired Priest asks, we all say the same thing — we failed. Something went wrong inside the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. We pulled back for safety.”

No one argued. No opportunity here was worth more than their lives.

But before anyone could move, the ground shifted.

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

The tremor came from somewhere below — slow and heavy, like something enormous knocking against the earth from beneath.

“What's that sound?” War King York's face darkened.

[SFX: EARTHQUAKE]

[SFX: CRACKING STONE]

The tremor deepened into a full quake. Across the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, buildings that had stood untouched for tens of thousands of years began to sway. Crystal spires cracked. Walls buckled. The ground split in thin jagged lines running the length of the street.

[SFX: BUILDINGS COLLAPSING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DANGER]

“Go! Whatever that is, it has nothing to do with us!” Scott shouted, and broke into a run.

The Soul Sea masters transformed into streaks of light, burning back the way they'd come. The drakes were still out there — no one cared anymore. They'd been coming here for years and had grown careless. The regret hit all of them at once: they should have turned back at the Fast Arrow Zone.

Zane ran with them. His reason was different — he was here for the Cobalt Dragon, not the Ancestral Dragon Egg — but retreating was still the right call.

Then a black blade light tore across the street.

[SFX: BLADE SLASH]

[SFX: BODY SPLITTING]

It moved faster than anyone could react. The corpse at the front of the group was sheared clean in half. The Soul Sea master's soul slipped free from the ruined body and hovered to one side, shaken.

Everyone skidded to a halt.

“Who's there!”

War King Wind scanned the street. The blade light had carried Vitality — this wasn't a beast or a puppet.

On the right side of the road stood a tower, its foundation so solid it hadn't shifted an inch during the quake. The door to its second floor swung open.

[SFX: DOOR CREAKING]

A young man stepped out — long dark hair, heavy stubble, a pure black broadsword resting on his shoulder, a lazy look on his face like he'd been interrupted from something more interesting. He sniffed the air and squinted at the group.

“Huh. Strange. Why are there corpses running around?” He tilted his head. “They're not zombies — they've taken over Dragon-Blooded bodies. Big Sister, what exactly is going on here?”

[AMBIENT: EERIE WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FLUTE]

As they argued, a silver-haired woman watched them from a distance, her expression indifferent. “They used Soul Cores to animate the corpses of the Dragon-Blooded to get in here,” she observed. “They must be from this world of chaos.”

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

Just then, a tall, sturdy man strolled down the street toward the group, a massive, fiery red scythe resting casually on his shoulder.

“Looks like we’re in luck,” he remarked, his eyes scanning War King Wind and the others. “We were running low on bait to lure that thing out. But these… live souls inside dead bodies? Perfect.”

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

“Friend,” War King Wind said, his voice dropping to a dangerous low. “What’s that supposed to mean?”

The silver-haired woman offered a sinister smile. “Who said we were friends? I thought we were clear. We’re here to capture the Evil Dragon. And to do that, we need to borrow your bodies. And your souls.”

War King York scoffed. “You’ve got a hell of a lot of nerve. You think you can keep all of us here? Don’t make me laugh.”

Even though they couldn’t use their full power while inhabiting these corpses, they were still Soul Sea Realm masters. They weren’t about to go down without a fight.

The silver-haired woman let out a musical laugh. “Ha! Nerve? You know, no one’s ever said that to me before.”

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

The burly man with the scythe walked around to the other side of the street, completely ignoring Zane’s group. He spoke quietly to the woman. “Dorothea, just grab them. Max is getting impatient.”

Dorothea gave a slight nod, her enchanting smile never faltering. 

[MUSIC: OMINOUS CRESCENDO]

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

Then, the flower tattooed in her right eye began to spin.
