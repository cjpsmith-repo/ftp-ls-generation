Ep 919 - Just Light and Air

Word count: 1035

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Without hesitating, Zane pulled out the Black Star Pickax. 

Time was running out. If Dorothea showed up now, he’d be in a world of trouble.

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

But the moment he swung the pickax and struck the chains, something impossible happened. As a divine-class weapon, it could destroy just about anything in the universe. So when the pickax passed right through the chains as if they were smoke, Zane could only stare.

“An illusion?” he muttered, completely baffled. 

The chains and the Ancestral Dragon Egg they were wrapped around looked so solid, so real. 

[SFX: WHOOSH]

He reached out, and his fingers slid right through them, feeling nothing but empty air. “What the hell is going on?”

The chains and the egg were perfectly visible, yet entirely intangible. He just couldn't wrap his head around it.

[MUSIC: REVELATION THEME]

A faint smile touched Brianna's lips. “If I’m right, the Ancestral Dragon Egg is sealed in another layer of space. That’s why you can see it but can’t touch it.”

He darted forward and reached for the egg again. His hand passed straight through it, same as before. Just light and air.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]

“Another layer of space?” Zane’s mind spun. He was skilled in Spatial Law—his connection to it was stronger than any other, even after the Baptism by Laws had opened him up to all of them. But he was stuck at level two. He knew if he’d had enough time in the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms to find the totems for the third and fourth layers, he might have had a chance.

But there had been no time. From the moment he’d entered the Harlen Divine Kingdom, it had been one thing after another: scrambling to find Ancient Heartstones, the competition at the West Dragon River, and then getting thrown into The Sacred Feather Sea. He’d barely had a moment to breathe, let alone practice.

“Yes,” Brianna said, shaking her head. “If I’m not mistaken, this is the fifth layer of the Spatial Law, known as Refraction. The egg is sealed in a completely different space, and what we’re seeing is just a reflection. You can’t just take it.” 

She sighed. “Unfortunately, I don’t specialize in Spatial Law. I only ever understood the first layer.”

Zane’s brow furrowed. “So that’s why War King Wind and the other rulers could never get the egg?”

“The higher you go with Spatial Law, the harder it gets,” Brianna added. “The first level is manageable. The second is a wall most never clear. The third requires the talent of a god-rank genius. The fourth — even in the Upper Realm, warriors at that level are the core of their clans. As for the fifth…” She paused. “It's probably not something any ordinary warrior ever reaches.”

“The Demi-Immortals can't take it either,” Brianna said. “They have no one who specializes in Spatial Law. So they found another way.”

“What way?”

“They're not trying to extract it. They're hatching it. Change the flow of time fast enough, and the egg ripens on its own. When the Ancestral Dragon emerges, they take the living creature instead of the egg.”

“How long has it been running?” Zane asked.

“Hard to say,” Brianna said. “The four kingdoms have been coming here for years. Under thousandfold acceleration, even a single year outside means nearly a thousand years have passed in here. The egg may have been ripening for far longer than anyone realizes.”

Zane considered this. “But I'm inside the time field now, too. If I wait here, I age along with it.”

“Yes,” Brianna said. “If you want to wait for it to hatch, you'd need to leave the field and wait outside — or hope it's already nearly done.”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Just then, Dorothea arrived — climbing the steps slowly, one by one, her expression composed. Zane's mind raced, searching for a way to stall her. A risky idea sparked in his head.

“I'll forgive you this time,” she said as she climbed. “You smashed Delroy's barrier. That's going to take some explaining. Come out quietly, and we'll leave it there.”

Zane held her gaze. He ran a quick calculation. Even if he were in his own body right now, at full strength, these Spirit Supreme Realm warriors would swat him like an insect. He had no options except one.

Even at full strength in his own body, fresh off his Life-and-Death breakthrough, he would have been an ant in front of these people. Controlling Brianna's body at thirty percent made it worse. Running was finished. Fighting was finished.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

He turned to face her. “The Demi-Immortal Race,” he said, his voice steady. “You’re all a part of it, aren’t you?”

Dorothea’s expression flickered with surprise. “Huh? How do you know about that?”

He smirked. “Because I’m a member, too.”

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

She let out a short, disbelieving laugh. “No way. Every member of the Demi-Immortal Race has a special mark. If you were one of us, I’d be able to feel it.”

Zane’s smile widened, his posture seeming to relax. “You’ve probably noticed this isn’t my real body, right?”

Dorothea nodded. It almost made sense. She could feel her power over Soul Cores working on the girl, which meant Zane was using one to control this body. But the body was clearly alive, and the contradiction had bothered her. His claim, that this wasn’t his true body, offered a bizarre but plausible answer.

“So you’re saying you were recognized by the Demi-Immortal Race?” Dorothea asked, her smile turning into a predatory smirk. “But you’re just a warrior from the Lower World. How could you possibly have gained our approval?” She clearly didn’t believe a word of it.

[SFX: CRACKING SOUND]

Out of the corner of his eye, Zane saw the crack on the Ancestral Dragon Egg slowly spreading. He had to keep her talking. “A tower,” he replied quickly. “I was recognized by the Demi-Immortal Race inside a tower.”

He wasn’t even lying. The Tower of Sin had, in fact, belonged to the Demi-Immortal Race.

But he needed to distract her for as long as he could.
