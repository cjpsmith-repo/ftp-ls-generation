Ep 244 - The Celestial Inferno
Word Count: 1360
[AMBIENT: TRAINING ARENA]
[MUSIC: TENSE OBSERVATION]
Zane stood motionless in the center of the arena, as the last flames of Mack's latest magic array dissipated around him. The spectators fell silent, their collective breath held in disbelief at the sight before them. What should have been Zane's defeat had transformed into a display of power that sent ripples of unease through the gathered disciples.
His eyes, now gleaming with an otherworldly intensity, fixed on Mack. The array master's confident smirk had vanished, replaced by a carefully masked apprehension as he realized his supposedly unbeatable technique had accomplished nothing against this Misty Peak anomaly.
Between them, fragments of the once-prized Star-Shifting Flag still smoldered on the arena floor---a testament to the unexpected turn this match had taken.
Mack played it cool, but he was watching everything. It was all an act---the trash talk, the insults he threw at Zane. He wanted everyone to think he saw him as a nobody.
In reality, he was memorizing every little detail about his potential opponents in the arena.
Mack had his own method for sizing up the competition, from Finn of Skytop Peak and Victoria of Maiden's Peak to even Anders from his own team.
He'd clocked Zane's strength, his speed, and his seemingly impenetrable defense.
[SFX: COMBAT STANCE]
So even though he looked relaxed, Mack was on high alert. The second he faced Zane, he went straight for his Star-Shifting Flag.
The Star-Shifting Flag was considered one of the top Spirit Weapons, not for its raw damage, but for the spinning magic array and enchanted barrier woven into its very fabric.
Anyone who got too close would be sent spinning, completely out of control. The effect wouldn't stop until the enchanted barrier was shattered or the flag itself was destroyed.
But breaking the Star-Shifting Flag? That was damn near impossible.
Even warriors who had reached the Soul Realm found shattering the enchanted barrier to be a long, grueling process.
[SFX: GLASS SHATTERING]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC IMPACT]
But none of those warriors were Zane. He just punched the barrier, and it shattered like glass. It should have been impossible, but it happened. The barrier wasn't supposed to break from brute force, but Zane had done it with pure, raw power.
Mack was floored.
He had no idea that the Emerald Crystal on Zane's left arm was the ultimate counter to any enchanted barrier. Even a barrier cast by a master in the late stages of the Soul Realm could be snuffed out by the crystal's power. The one on the Star-Shifting Flag didn't stand a chance.
The enchanted barrier was created by inscribing runes, a skill Mack had mastered long before he ever reached the Soul Realm. In that, he was a prodigy.
But the Celestial Inferno Array was a different kind of terrifying.
"The power of celestial fire is locked inside this inferno array," Mack laughed, a wild edge to his voice. "I was saving this for the top disciples. But since you just had to go and smash my Star-Shifting Flag, I guess you get the special treatment. Trust me, you're going to regret this."
He pulled an array tablet from his robe, and his fingers danced across its surface. Lines of Vitality shot from the tablet, linking to the array flags.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ARRAY ACTIVATION]
Trapped inside the Celestial Inferno Array, Zane felt the world tilt on its axis.
[SFX: REALITY DISTORTION]
A second later, he was plunged into absolute darkness.
The inferno array completely sealed off anyone inside from the outside world. The audience couldn't see a thing.
This wasn't Zane's first run-in with a magic array. He knew the flags created illusions to mess with an opponent's head.
Places like the Skyreach Sect's Bloody Mountain and the other bizarre training grounds all used magic arrays. But none came close to the sheer scale of the landscape inside The Elysian Keep.
[AMBIENT: SCORCHING WASTELAND]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
When the array swallowed him whole, Zane found himself somewhere else entirely.
The heat was scorching. The dry, cracked earth looked like the wrinkled skin of an old woman. All around him, Zane saw several fiery pits gaping open.
Not far off, Mack hovered in the air, a twisted smirk on his face.
"You know what my favorite thing in the world is, Zane? Watching my enemies suffer," Mack gloated. "Watching your face burn is going to make my day. Your skin will blister, your features will melt. Your pain is going to be so, so satisfying! Ha ha! I'm going to burn every last inch of you, and you'll be on your knees, begging me to stop."
[SFX: VIOLENT FIRE ERUPTION]
Lights flashed on the array tablet in his hand.
[AMBIENT: SULFUROUS AIR]
The fire pits around Zane flared violently, and the air filled with the smell of sulfur.
"Is any of this real? Is that even Mack?" Zane stood perfectly still, senses on high alert. He wouldn't make a move until he understood how this inferno array worked.
[SFX: DEAFENING EXPLOSION]
[AMBIENT: ROARING FIRE]
With a deafening roar, geysers of flame erupted from the countless pits, clawing at the sky. Then, dozens of stone men, wreathed in fire, began to crawl out of them.
The stone men glowed red-hot, with flames trailing behind them like fiery cloaks that licked at the air.
[SFX: CHARGING STONE CREATURES]
They lurched to their feet and charged straight for Zane. As the air grew hotter, he felt like his face was being cooked.
Zane had to admit, the Celestial Inferno Array was formidable. His plan had been to coast through the group competitions, hiding his true strength and relying only on his body as a weapon. But he now saw that the disciples of the Skyreach Sect were on another level compared to the warriors from the outside world.
An ordinary warrior at the peak of the Adept Realm wouldn't have lasted a second against him. But Mack was a genuine threat. Zane had made a mistake underestimating these disciples. They weren't the easy fights he was used to.
"Pretty impressive. Not even most practitioners at the Soul Realm could do that," Mack called down, a cold smile on his face as he hovered in the sky. "But the fact that you think you can actually beat me? That's a joke."
Zane rushed the nearest stone man. The heat intensified with every step — scorching, then unbearable. He pulled back.
"You're god in here, is that it?" Zane called up.
Mack grinned. "In my array, yes."
"Then you have a very small imagination." Zane set his jaw and charged again.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
His fist drove through the stone man's chest. Cracks split outward from the impact point and the golem exploded apart.
Above him, Mack screamed — a short, sharp sound. The stone men were infused with his soul force. Every one Zane destroyed sent a jolt of pain through him.
[SFX: RAPID IMPACTS]
Zane blew apart two more before Mack recovered. "Burn everything — explode!"
[SFX: VIOLENT FIRE ERUPTION]
The nearest stone man detonated. The blast swallowed Zane whole.
[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FIRE]
The fire crackled and spat across the ground. An eternity seemed to pass before the flames finally began to die down.
The inferno array was designed to kill. It conjured illusions so real they could damage a person's spirit, even if their physical body remained untouched.
If Zane were "burned" to death in here, his soul would be shattered, leaving him a drooling, mindless husk.
"Burn! Burn! After this thing has cooked your brain, I'll drag you out and teach you some manners! Ha ha!" Mack grinned, savoring the thought of the agony Zane must be in. He pictured him burning alive, begging for mercy, like a fish dropped into a sizzling hot pan with no escape.
[MUSIC: POWERFUL REVELATION]
[SFX: PULSING ENERGY]
When the fire finally receded, a figure stood on the scorched earth. A golden light pulsed from his body, radiating the power of a god. He looked terrifyingly powerful.
Mack hadn't expected this. Not in a million years. His jaw dropped, his eyes wide, as if he were staring at a ghost.
