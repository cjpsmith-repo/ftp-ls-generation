Ep 1118 - What Price

Word count: 1078

[MUSIC: DARK SCHEMING]
The warrior’s blood-red robes snapped in the wind. 
[SFX: HORSE NEIGHING]
He could tell right away that the horse Zane was riding was no ordinary animal. The creature puzzled him at first, but then he sensed it: the unmistakable bloodline of a spirit beast. This wasn’t just any horse; it was the legendary and priceless Heavenly Phoenix Horse.
Everyone knew that even the most powerful martial artists couldn’t just fly across a supreme world—it would take forever. Even with a teleportation tunnel, a warrior from the Spirit Supreme Realm would still be looking at a long journey. There were only a few ways to get around quickly: master spatial transference, use a top-tier flying treasure, or ride a spirit beast. 
But capturing a spirit beast was incredibly difficult, and taming one was practically impossible.
The warrior’s eyes narrowed. The fact that this young man was riding a *tamed* Heavenly Phoenix Horse changed everything. He knew exactly what that meant. It was the kind of prize that even the Saint Lord of the Soulbound Sect would kill for. 
If he could get his hands on that horse, he wouldn’t keep it—a man of his rank couldn’t possibly hold on to something so valuable. But if he offered it to one of his superiors… the rewards would be staggering.
As the thought took root, greed coiled around his heart.
But the warrior wasn’t a fool. You didn’t just stumble upon a creature like this in the wild. He glanced at the young man again. Anyone with a tamed Heavenly Phoenix Horse had to have powerful connections.
[MUSIC: DECEPTIVE CHARM]
“Freya,” he said, his expression shifting into something that almost resembled warmth. “As your uncle, I’ve only ever wanted what’s best for you. Why would you want to leave?”
Freya knew there was no going back. “Uncle Julius,” she replied, her voice cold. “I traveled all this way to find you after I ascended, hoping you’d help me. But you just wanted to use me as a human cauldron and sacrifice me for someone else’s training. Why would I ever agree to that?”
Julius forced a smile. “Freya, you must have misunderstood. I’ve worked hard to get where I am.”
His attention shifted to Zane. “And this young hero behind you — who might you be?”
Zane’s expression didn’t change. “We ascended around the same time. Met by chance. That’s all.” He paused. “Your own niece. And you’d offer her up like a bargaining chip.”
[MUSIC: MOUNTING DANGER]
Julius’s smile thinned. An ascender. That was all he was.
The caution that had been holding him back quietly dissolved.
“Meddling in other people’s affairs in the Upper World carries a price,” Julius said, his tone almost gentle. “I’d hate for you to learn that the hard way.”
“What price?” Zane asked.
“Your life.” Julius sighed softly — then turned his back and raised one hand.
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]
Six warriors stepped forward from behind him. Late-stage Soul Sea Realm, all of them — too far beneath Julius to dirty his hands with, but more than enough to handle one ascender who’d barely crossed into the Soul Sea Realm.
Julius didn’t even watch. He was already thinking about the horse.
[SFX: SIX SIMULTANEOUS IMPACTS]
He heard the sounds before he turned around. Six soft thuds, one after the other — barely a second apart.
When he looked back, all six of his men were gone. Not retreating. Down. Scattered across the street below like broken kites, at least one of them having carved a crater into the wall of the residence.
Zane was still sitting on the horse. He had the same faint, mildly curious expression on his face. “What price?” he asked again.
[SFX: TENSE SILENCE]
Julius stared. Early-stage Soul Sea Realm — and he’d just dropped six late-stage warriors without moving from the saddle.
His gaze dropped to the sword in Zane’s hand. He felt the energy coming off it, and something cold moved through him. That wasn’t a Grade-Two weapon. It wasn’t even close. Grade-One Divine Weapon — maybe higher.
A tamed Heavenly Phoenix Horse. A Grade-One Divine Weapon. And power that made no sense for someone who’d just ascended.
On the horse behind Zane, Freya’s eyes were wide. She’d seen him do extraordinary things in the mine, but this was something else entirely. But her uncle was a late-stage Spirit Supreme Realm master. Whatever Zane was, surely that gap was too wide.
Julius’s gaze settled on Zane. The surprise on his face hardened back into contempt. “Interesting,” he said, a flicker of contempt in his eyes. “But you’re still not strong enough.”
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS POWER]
[AMBIENT: OPPRESSIVE ENERGY WAVE]
Then, a violent, malicious aura exploded from him, washing over the city like a suffocating tide. 
[SFX: CROWD GASPING AND TREMBLING]
Throughout Clearhaven, the weaker warriors and ordinary people trembled, most not even daring to look up at the sky.
From a shadowed corner of the city, a group of warriors from the Life-and-Death and Soul Sea Realms watched the confrontation unfold.
[SFX: SHOCKED WHISPER]
“The lord of Clearhaven is fighting!”
“And who’s his opponent? Some kid who looks like he just entered the Soul Sea Realm?”
“Don’t underestimate him. That young man took down six late-stage Soul Sea warriors in an instant. You think he’s some core talent from one of the sacred places?”
High above, a swirling vortex of boiling blood formed in Julius’s hands, twisting and hardening until it took the shape of a longspear. 
[SFX: SPEAR SPINNING]
[SFX: MENACING POINT]
[SFX: BLOOD VORTEX SWIRLING]
“You’ll find out soon enough what that price is,” Julius said.
Zane’s expression hardened, but he didn’t waste a word. 
This was his first time facing a master of the Spirit Supreme Realm. 
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE PERCUSSION]
[SFX: LIGHTNING BOLT CRACKLING]
[SFX: WIND BLADE WHISTLING]
With a sharp swing of the Thundergust, he sent a bolt of lightning and a blade of wind screaming toward Julius. 
[SFX: MULTIPLE SHADOWS RUSHING]
At the same instant, dozens of Nether God Shadows blurred into existence, rushing the warrior from all sides.
“Is that it?” Julius bellowed.
[SFX: RAPID SPEAR STRIKES]
[SFX: SHADOWS SHATTERING]
His blood-red spear shot out in a blinding flurry. Each strike was precise, each one shattering a Nether God Shadow into nothing. But he wasn’t done. 
[SFX: SPEAR THRUST WHOOSH]
After clearing the illusions, his spear shot forward, aimed straight for Zane with the force of a thunderbolt.
