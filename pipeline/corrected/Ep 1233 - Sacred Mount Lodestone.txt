Ep 1233 - Sacred Mount Lodestone

Word count: 1132

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL TENSION]
[AMBIENT: CITY WALL CROWD MURMURS]
“That guy’s got insane vitality,” someone muttered from the city wall. “Anyone else would be a red smear on the ground by now.”
“Maybe he has some kind of protective treasure?” another offered. “But even then, there’s no way it could stop that.”
“I think this is the first time Sloane’s ever used her Five-Element Finger Chain,” a more knowledgeable warrior chimed in, his voice filled with awe. “It’s a Divine Weapon, ranked two hundred thirty on the Cosmic Spirit Index. That’s the Kincaid family’s greatest treasure!”
“Is he seriously still trying to fight?”
[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD CHATTER]
The warriors lining the walls were buzzing, captivated by the spectacle unfolding before them. In the entire world, there were only about three thousand Divine Weapons, and most were wielded by World Lords and supreme beings far beyond their reach. To see one in action was a once-in-a-lifetime event, and the crowd savored every second.
[SFX: MASSIVE DESCENDING WHOOSH]
“The Five-Element Finger Chain was forged from a Five-Colored Divine Stone,” the informed warrior explained as the giant finger descended. “It holds the power of five different Original Laws!”
A deafening boom echoed as Zane’s fist met the colossal finger. Everyone expected him to be crushed.
[SFX: COLLECTIVE CROWD GASP]
[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACT BOOM]
When the dust settled, Zane was still standing in the small space he’d carved out between the finger and the ground, a defiant smile on his face.
[SFX: MASSIVE STONE SHATTERING]
[SFX: DEBRIS FALLING]
A moment later, the enormous index finger, a construct of pure Earth Law, simply shattered, exploding into nothing.
He’d actually broken it with a single punch.
“That… that guy…”
The crowd on the city wall was stunned into silence.
[SFX: FLESH TEARING]
The exact moment the giant finger shattered, a matching wound split open on Sloane’s own finger. “Ow, that actually hurt,” she murmured, frowning at the bead of blood welling up from the cut. 
For any other warrior, the pain would have been nothing. Even a novice in Body Refining wouldn’t flinch at losing an entire finger, let alone from a tiny scratch like this.
But Sloane had always been strange about pain — even as a child, the smallest scratch could bring tears to her eyes. She popped the injured finger straight into her mouth and sucked the blood away without a second thought. 
High above her, the giant made of Earth Law mimicked the gesture perfectly, raising its own massive hand to its mouth — a strangely comical sight for something built to level mountains.
Zane wrenched free of the giant’s grip and shot straight for Sloane. She startled, caught off guard by just how much stronger he was than she’d assumed — though she didn’t let it rattle her. Still nursing her finger, she swept her free hand out again and again, slapping at him with the giant’s palm. 
Each strike left a fresh print in the earth, and Zane tumbled through the blows like a small boat riding out a storm — vanishing into the wave of each slap, only to surface again between the giant’s fingers a moment later, still closing the distance.
When he’d closed to within thirty or forty feet, Zane cut through space itself and reappeared at the giant’s foot — if he wanted to reach Sloane, he’d have to break through its defenses first. 
[SFX: HARD IMPACT]
But before he could press the advantage, Sloane, still absorbed in her finger, lightly kicked out. The giant’s foot caught him square in the face and sent him flying, his body bouncing like a rubber ball. She didn’t even watch him go — she was too busy examining the cut on her finger.
[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]
The kick threw him more than ten miles. He caught himself in midair before he could hit the ground, staring back at the towering giant with a bitter half-smile. “Just the law of earth, and this much trouble already,” he muttered. He shook the thought off — a little difficulty wasn’t going to make him back down. In a blur, he shot forward again.
Watching him close in a second time, Sloane finally let her guard rise. She could feel a genuine thread of danger in this fight now — not enough to think she’d lose, but enough to make her stop holding back. “Let’s try that again,” she said, reaching out to pluck him from the air.
[MUSIC: DARK ANTICIPATION]
Zane answered by throwing both hands forward. A palm-sized, seven-colored mountain flared to life in front of him, spinning faster and faster.
“Sacred Mount Lodestone — go!”
[SFX: MOUNTAIN EXPANDING]
The mountain he’d forged with a secret technique of his own surged in size as it spun, swelling into a colossal peak that came crashing down toward the earthen giant. Startled, Sloane threw a punch to try to shatter it outright — the giant mirrored her, driving its own fist into the mountain with everything it had.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING TREMOR]
It barely shuddered. The mountain’s mixed elemental energy made it almost impossible to break down, and its weight was staggering to match. Even from inside the giant, Sloane could feel the crushing pressure bearing down — she couldn’t move at all.
[SFX: STONE CRACKING UNDER PRESSURE]
Cracks spiderwebbed across its stony shoulders, the immense pressure threatening to pulverize it.
A faint smile touched Zane’s lips. With her construct pinned, he saw his opening to get to Sloane herself. He couldn’t help but feel a thrill of anticipation, thinking about all the Illusion Points she was carrying.
But as he closed in, a dangerous glint flashed in Sloane’s eyes.
[SFX: MAGICAL DISPEL]
With a flick of her wrist, she dispelled the Earth Law binding her construct.
[SFX: GIANT VANISHING]
The giant vanished.
[MUSIC: CATASTROPHIC DESCENT]
[AMBIENT: RUMBLING MOUNTAIN FALL]
And with nothing to hold it up, the entire Sacred Mount Lodestone came crashing down.
As the mountain plummeted, dooming anyone caught beneath it.
Sloane calmly tapped a ring on her right thumb. A faint wisp of golden wind coiled around her.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION VANISH]
A moment later, she was gone.
[SFX: METEOR IMPACT]
The Sacred Mount Lodestone hit the ground with the force of a meteor, shattering the earth and sinking several hundred feet before it finally came to a rest.
To everyone’s shock, Sloane reappeared. The same golden wind that had whisked her away now deposited her gently on the very peak of the mountain she’d just dropped.
[AMBIENT: VIOLENT WIND GUSTS]
[SFX: TELEPORTATION ARRIVAL]
The battle’s aftershock churned the air into turbulence, wind howling wildly around her as her ivory-white streamers snapped and cracked behind her.
[MUSIC: EERIE CALM]
From her new perch, she looked down on Zane, her expression unsettlingly calm.
