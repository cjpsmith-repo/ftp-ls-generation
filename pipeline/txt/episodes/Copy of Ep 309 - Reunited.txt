Ep 309 - Reunited

[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION SPACE]
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
[MUSIC: EAGER ANTICIPATION]
Zane sat cross-legged on Master Jasper’s emerald leaf, his eyes closed in meditation as they soared through the sky toward Infernal Peak.
Though his body remained still, his mind churned with thoughts of Elara and the revelations Kenneth had shared.
The Violet-Core Physique, the mysterious lord from The Heartlands, the Soul Harvesting Ritual—each piece of information added weight to his shoulders. A cool breeze swept across his face, but did nothing to calm the storm brewing within. He needed to grow stronger, much stronger, and quickly. The path to the Soul Realm stretched before him, seeming impossibly long when his sister’s safety hung in the balance.
Sensing the turmoil in Zane, Master Jasper spoke without turning around.
"The news about your sister weighs heavily on you. I may not have the leader’s foresight, but I’ve gathered some small wisdom over my centuries. I hope you’ll hear it."
"I’d be honored to hear it, Master Jasper," Zane said, his voice quiet with respect.
Master Jasper was the most powerful elder in the Skyreach Sect, second only to Kenneth and the deputy leader. But for all his authority, he wasn’t part of the Cloud Hall. The Hall was a continental power that paid little mind to its remote branch in the Eastern Territories, content to just appoint a leader and a deputy to run things.
"You have a rare talent, which is why the leader is watching you so closely. If you can pass the test of the Cloud Road, you’ll earn the attention of the Cloud Hall itself. From there, you could become a figure of true power, achieving things I can only dream of. But you must remember this: until you are strong enough, you have to learn patience.
Keep your head down. The wrong kind of attention will get you killed.
Only by surviving can you ever hope to follow your path—and save your sister from hers."
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
The words hit Zane like a physical blow. He stared at the elder’s back, stunned.
It was as if the old man had peered directly into his soul.
After all, Elara had always been his weakness. He could never think clearly where she was concerned. When Kenneth had revealed her situation, Zane had lost it, his composure shattering right there in front of the leader.
Thankfully, Kenneth was a patient man and likely hadn’t taken offense.
It was hard enough for an inner disciple to even make it into the All Peaks Competition, let alone an outer one. Only the absolute best of the inner disciples were chosen.
[AMBIENT: SECLUDED CLEARING]
[MUSIC: FOCUSED MEDITATION]
A gentle breeze swept across the secluded clearing where Elara had retreated to practice her cultivation. Surrounded by Infernal Peak’s twisted pines and jagged rock formations, she sat cross-legged on a flat stone, her slender fingers manipulating threads of violet energy that danced between her palms.
The mountain’s oppressive atmosphere—which terrified most visitors—had become a strange comfort to her over the years. Here, hidden from prying eyes, she could freely explore the limits of her Violet-Core Physique without fear of discovery. Today, her thoughts wandered to her brother, wondering if he had survived in the outside world, if he remembered her at all. Little did she know how drastically her isolation would soon end.
Even with his talent, she thought, there’s a huge gap between him and the top inner disciples. I wonder what peak he’s on. Misty Peak is the lowest-ranked of the Thirty-Three Peaks, and they don’t have many strong disciples. If he’s on Misty, he might actually have a shot... A small smile touched her lips as she gazed at the face she’d sculpted from pure Vitality—Zane’s face.
[SFX: RIPPLING VITALITY]
Just then, a ripple of Vitality disturbed the air. Elara’s head snapped up. An enormous, emerald-green leaf was gliding toward her, Master Jasper at its helm.
"Who’s that?" she murmured, squinting at the second figure standing behind him. As they drew closer, she recognized the silhouette. Her eyes widened. Confusion gave way to shock, then to utter disbelief. It couldn’t be.
[SFX: LEAF LANDING]
A moment later, Master Jasper’s giant leaf touched down smoothly on the platform. Zane stepped out from behind the master and stared at his sister, stunned.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL REUNION]
Brother and sister stood frozen, their eyes locked.
They’d dreamed of this moment a thousand times, but now that it was here, it felt completely unreal.
Master Jasper stood silently to one side, a silent observer. In his long life, he’d witnessed countless partings and reunions, but the sight still moved him. The path of a warrior was a cruel one, yet it was not entirely without the warmth of family.
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]
"Zane!" Elara scrambled to her feet and rushed forward, throwing herself into his arms without a second thought.
She clung to him, the years melting away until she was no longer a young woman of sixteen, but the little girl who had idolized her big brother.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL REUNION]
After so many years apart, they never imagined they’d see each other again, especially not like this.
Zane stroked his sister’s hair, a whirlwind of emotions churning inside him. They held each other for a long moment, just soaking it in, before finally pulling back to get a real look at each other.
She’d grown. Elara was nearly as tall as he was now. The little girl from his memories had vanished, replaced by the beautiful young woman standing before him.
"Zane, how did you get here?" Elara asked, her voice breathless.
"This place is forbidden... but Master Jasper brought you." Her eyes fell to his black robes. "You’re an inner disciple. Incredible! Does Father know? He must be so proud?"
Zane’s heart sank at this. Should I tell her? I can’t keep it from her. Even if I don’t say anything now, she’ll find out eventually.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REVELATION]
After a breath of hesitation, Zane said it, the words blunt and heavy.
"Our uncles murdered him."
"Wha-what?" Elara stammered, the news hitting her like a physical blow.
"Bryson and Ken... they’re animals," Zane explained, his voice turning to ice. "They killed him to take his place as head of the family."
[SFX: ENERGY VIBRATION]
A sharp hum vibrated through the air. The Vitality around Elara’s body exploded, coalescing into countless, needle-sharp spines. Her Violet-Core Physique meant she held more Vitality than most, so much that it often seeped out, unable to be fully contained.
Now, that excess energy had a purpose.
The spines pulsed with a terrifying light, a raw, external manifestation of the murderous rage boiling inside her.
