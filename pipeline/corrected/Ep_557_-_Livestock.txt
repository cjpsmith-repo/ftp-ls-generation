Ep 557 - Livestock
Word count: 1045
[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND]
As the kids' voices faded, silence settled back over the area. Zane didn't have the energy to so much as glance around, much less get his bearings.
The good news was that he didn't have to worry about his Storage Ring. It was keyed to his unique signature; no ordinary person could open it. His treasures were safe.
They sounded like kids from a regular family. Which means no powerful warriors in the mix, he thought. No one who could break into the ring.
Right now, his only problem was getting his strength back, forcing his muscles to respond. At the very least, he had to be able to move.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Time dragged on. The sky bled from blue to black, swallowing the landscape in darkness.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON GRAVEL]
[AMBIENT: NIGHT CRICKETS]
After what felt like an eternity, he heard footsteps crunching nearby, followed by the low murmur of voices.
"Is he in there?"
"Yeah, Dad!"
"Alright, go take a look, but be careful."
[SFX: TORCH FLAMES CRACKLING]
A moment later, a flickering yellow glow appeared at the rim of the crater. A few middle-aged men holding torches peered down, their faces etched with wary curiosity.
Zane could tell they weren't warriors.
One of them leaned over the edge and called out, "Hey! Who are you down there?"
[AMBIENT: CRATER SCENE]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
All Zane could do was blink. He couldn't make a sound.
"We gotta get him out of there," another man said.
[SFX: ROPE TIGHTENING]
Two of them immediately scrambled down into the crater. One looped a thick rope around Zane’s torso and tossed the other end to the men above. "Pull!" he shouted.
[SFX: GRUNTING EFFORT]
"Gods, why is he so heavy?" one grunted.
Zane appeared lean, perhaps a hundred fifty pounds. Yet four men hauling the rope couldn't budge him.
His body had been tempered repeatedly to Mythic Weapon strength. The density of his flesh and bone far exceeded normal limits—even beyond seasoned warriors whose bodies naturally densified to withstand the forces they commanded.
[SFX: WHIP CRACK]
[SFX: HORSE WHINNY]
After a hushed discussion, a whip cracked. A courser whinnied, hooves digging into dirt. The rope went taut with real power, and Zane finally slid up the crater wall.
[SFX: CART WHEELS ON DIRT]
[AMBIENT: OPEN GRASSLAND — NIGHT]
They laid him on a handcart pulled by four horses. Eventually, tents appeared—a sizeable nomadic settlement across the grassland.
Inside the tent, the middle-aged rescuer crouched down, holding the Storage Ring.
"My son took this. It belongs to you." He set it beside Zane, then pulled the boy forward. "Apologize."
"I'm sorry," the boy whispered hoarsely.
Zane noticed bruise marks on the boy's hand. He wanted to say it was fine, but couldn't make a sound.
[MUSIC: QUIET UNDERSCORE]
When they left, silence fell. Zane lay alone in darkness.
The purple spatial storm had burned through every reserve of energy. His eight extraordinary meridians were packed solid with residual spatial energy—enough to permanently cripple an ordinary warrior. But Zane's body was different. The blockage needed clearing, not healing.
He worked through the night, condensing essence from the air and pushing it thread by thread through his meridians, dissolving the spatial energy. By dawn, he'd cleared his spine. Not much. But enough.
[AMBIENT: MORNING CAMP SOUNDS]
[SFX: TENT FLAP OPENING]
The tent flap opened. Lori entered, carrying flatbread and a water skin. She knelt to feed him.
“Thank you," Zane said.
She froze, eyes wide, then spun toward the entrance. "Father! The strange man can talk!"
Her father entered quickly, relief evident on his face. "You're awake. Good." He crouched beside Zane. "Where are you from? Do you know where you are?"
"No. Where is this?"
The man's expression shifted—confusion, then pity. "An Ogre ranch. You didn't know?"
Zane's mind raced. An ogre ranch—with human herdsmen in tents. He'd assumed they were free nomads. But if this was ogre territory…
[SFX: DISTANT SHOUTING]
"Sir, the Envoy is here! All families, young and old — come out!"
The man stood. "Excuse me. When the Envoy comes, we all have to present ourselves." He slipped out through the tent flap, leaving it open behind him.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR FIELD]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
The tent flap was open. Twisting his head, Zane saw a crowd of seven or eight hundred people gathered in a nearby field. Men, women, and children of all ages stood in silent, orderly rows.
[SFX: OGRE GROWLS]
Facing them were two massive ogres. They stood at least ten feet tall, with grayish-brown skin and long fangs jutting from their mouths. So these were the ogres they were talking about, he thought.
Zane had seen ogres before—he'd even killed a level-five mythic one to pass The Gauntlet.
[AMBIENT: TENSE VILLAGE ATMOSPHERE]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
The two ogres paced back and forth before the silent humans. Finally, one of them spoke, its voice a low rumble dripping with arrogance. "The Governor's birthday is today. There's a banquet. We need five of you!"
A middle-aged man, who seemed to be their leader, stepped forward, his face pale. "My lord… surely five is too many?"
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
"No bargaining!" the ogre snarled. "And we need young ones this time." Its massive finger jabbed toward the crowd. "You. You. You… and you."
The ogre’s eyes swept over the rows of people, then locked onto Lori. "Hmm. She looks fresh. Tender. We'll take her, too."
At this, the man grew frantic. "Sir, you can't! The Governor's own orders—no one under fourteen!"
"What do I care about orders?" the ogre sneered. "It's his birthday. He's feeling festive. And he wants something tender to celebrate. Ha!"
[SFX: WHIP CRACK]
With a booming laugh, it flicked its wrist, sending a rope snapping through the air toward Lori.
[SFX: GIRL SCREAMING]
The girl shrieked and tried to scramble away, but she stood no chance. The rope coiled around her in an instant, yanking her tight.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION RISING]
Watching from the tent, Zane's eyes narrowed. A cold, sharp flicker of killing intent ignited deep within him.
[MUSIC: HORRIFIC REALIZATION]
And then he understood. This wasn't a village. It was a pasture.
These people weren't residents—they were livestock. The ogres were raising them for slaughter.
