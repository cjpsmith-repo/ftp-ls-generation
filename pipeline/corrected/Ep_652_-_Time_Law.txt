Ep 652 - Time Law

Word count: 1294

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The moment Lavender said the treasure’s name, a charge went through the group. If they could actually get their hands on it… could it be what they thought it was?

But Lavender’s next words shattered their excitement. “Don’t even think about it,” she said, her voice sharp. “You can’t take the Gate of Many Lives. It’s useless. You can’t do it, and neither can Amber. Don't believe me? Just try.”

Of course, the temptation of the Gate was too much to resist, especially for Fabian. If they could bring it back to their clan, it would instantly become their most valuable treasure. He and Jarrod exchanged a look—they weren't buying it.

[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY PULSE]

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL HUM]

As Fabian and Jarrod approached the Gate, the ancient words carved into its surface seemed to swell, growing larger and larger before their eyes. Each character pulsed with a blinding aura, and they finally understood why it was so difficult to get close. The light itself was pressing against them.

“This is insane,” Fabian gasped out. “Completely insane.”

The Gate had endured for ages, its carved symbols still radiating terrifying power despite the passage of time. The original owner's strength must have been incomprehensible.

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

By the time they finally reached the Gate, they were drenched in sweat and gasping for breath.

They leaned against the frame to rest, both lost in thought. Zane was watching them from a distance, standing beside Lavender.

“Serves you right,” Lavender sneered.

Zane just held up his hands, a clear sign he wanted no part of this. He wasn’t getting involved.

“My Queen,” Esther asked quietly, “what good is this Gate if we can’t take it with us?”

A mysterious smile touched Lavender’s lips. “You can’t take it, but that doesn’t mean it isn’t useful.”

“What? How?” Zane chimed in.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]

Lavender explained.“ The Gate of Many Lives’s greatest power is allowing you to experience the endless cycle of life, suffering, death, and rebirth. It can strengthen your warrior’s spirit.”

Esther, Zane, and the others exchanged skeptical looks.  For warriors from the Lower World, their spirits had to be sharpened through trial and tribulation—taking hits, pushing limits, holding fast to one's will. A warrior's spirit was simply their force of will, part of their character. How could anyone just… train that?

Seeing their doubtful faces, Lavender said, “In the upper world, there are all sorts of unbelievable ways to train. Since the Gate of Many Lives is right here, I’m sure Yolande wouldn’t mind if you all went inside to strengthen yourselves. Right, Yolande?”

That only deepened their confusion. What did Yolande have to do with any of this?

But Yolande gave a cautious nod. “You’re right. I don’t mind letting you in.”

“You don’t?” Esther stared at her, completely bewildered. “Are you the owner of this Gate?”

Yolande didn’t answer, and Lavender clearly had no intention of explaining. Still, she’d called the Gate magical and said it couldn’t be taken. Even with all their doubts, they weren’t about to pass up an opportunity like this.

“I’ll go first,” Fabian announced, stepping up to the Gate.

Taking it was impossible, but nothing was stopping him from going inside.

[SFX: HEAVY DOOR CREAKING OPEN]

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

He gave it a light push, and the massive door swung open. In the center of the gateway, a small whirlpool of light spun slowly, counter-clockwise.

The sight made Fabian hesitate. “Is it dangerous?"

Lavender shook her head. “Not at all. You can go in.”

Fabian nodded and stepped forward. [SFX: TIME RIPPLE]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL SHIMMER]

The moment he crossed the threshold into the whirlpool, a golden light flashed, and everyone felt a strange ripple in the air, like a shift in time itself.

Zane breathed, his eyes fixed on the fading light. “It’s the Time Law!"

Compared to Spatial Law, the Time Law was infinitely rarer and more mysterious — and whatever was woven into this Gate far exceeded anything on the continent.

The group settled in to wait. Cultivation took time — sometimes days, sometimes years. [SFX: FOOTSTEPS EMERGING]

But Fabian walked back out after only a few breaths.

No one spoke for a moment. Then the speculation started.

"It must be the Time Law. Something that compressed time inside could be running completely differently. He might have been in there for days."

Jarrod stepped in front of Fabian and waved a hand across his face. "Well?"

Fabian reached up and swatted it aside without looking at him. "Don't," he said quietly. "I'm thinking."

Jarrod stared at him. "You walked into a magic door and came out thinking. What in the world is inside that thing?"

Fabian finally turned to look at him, and there was something different in his eyes — something steadier and further away. "You should go in. It's worth it. I promise."

Jarrod studied him for a long moment, then stepped through the Gate.

He came out just as quickly. He stood blinking for a moment, the corner of his mouth curling into something that wasn't quite a smile. "So that's how it is…"

Yolande and Lavender said nothing. They clearly understood.

Esther exchanged a glance with the two remaining Demon Night War Lords, then stepped forward and entered the Gate. When she emerged, her eyes were completely still — black and calm as deep water, faintly luminous. Without a word, she walked to Lavender's side.

The two War Lords followed. They came out quietly and stood apart, each lost in their own thoughts.

Then it was Zane's turn.

He asked Lavender what was waiting inside. She only smiled and shook her head. "You'll understand when you go in."

Zane turned to face the Gate.

He stepped inside.

[SFX: DEEP RESONANT HUM]

[MUSIC: ANCIENT ETHEREAL]

The Time Law surged around him like a tide. Sound disappeared. The darkness closed in, and for a moment, he was completely alone — no cave, no group, nothing.

Then a vast golden spiral appeared before him, turning slowly in the void. Before he could react, it pulled him in, and the world dissolved into light.

[AMBIENT: IMPERIAL CITY BATTLE]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

Suddenly, the world changed. Zane found himself standing on the wall of an imperial city, a golden sword in his hand, looking out at an army in the distance.

He tried to move, but his body wouldn’t respond.

Then, against his will, his arm raised the sword, and a voice that wasn’t his roared to the forces below, “My loyal generals and soldiers! The imperial city has fallen. After I take the throne, I will rule this land with all of you!”

“Charge!”

[SFX: SIGNAL FIRES BLAZING]

Below the wall, an army clad in golden armor surged toward the city. Signal fires blazed from every tower, and the streets were already running with rivers of blood. As Zane’s eyes scanned the chaos, dozens of emerald-green lights shot into the sky before him. The first person he recognized was Isla Novak. She was flanked by several warriors at the Inner-Core Realm.

“Zane Lowell, you traitor!” Isla’s voice was filled with venom. “The Palace will kill you, no matter the cost!”

She and the other warriors descended upon him like a tidal wave of light.

[SFX: SWORD CLASH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

A wild laugh escaped the lips of the man whose body Zane was in. He raised the golden sword and charged forward to meet their attack, and the two sides clashed in a vicious battle.

Zane was trapped inside, a passenger with no control over his actions or words. All he knew was that he was now Zane Lowell, a traitor to the Empire, fighting to seize power. If this man defeated the warriors before him, he would rule the entire Eastern Territories as its new emperor.

Is this real? A memory? Or just some twisted dream?

Zane calmly watched the intense battle unfold from his strange, detached viewpoint. It was like he was an outsider watching a play, and he was somehow stuck in the lead role.
