Ep 772 - Table Manners

Word count: 1112

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

 Zane had no idea what was going through Kieran’s head, but when the guy started stammering, he just figured he was floored by the sight of Dorian's crew.

Trying to put him at ease, Zane said, “Kieran, these are my friends. We’ve been fighting side-by-side for the last two years.”

“Friends?”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 The relief that washed over Kieran was immediate. He’d been so worried, but hearing Zane had made new allies while he was away changed everything. With a group of warriors this powerful around, he didn't have to be afraid of the York Divine Kingdom anymore. But that relief was quickly followed by a flood of questions. Where had Zane been for the past two years to meet people like this? And why on earth would warriors of their caliber befriend someone with a much lower power level?

Hobbes, never the patient type, was practically vibrating with the need to pull Zane aside and get the whole story. But staring down a hundred stone-faced warriors, he wisely kept his mouth shut and decided to wait for a better moment. As for Burns, he was his usual quiet self, his expression unreadable. Deep down, though, the shock of seeing Dorian's group hit him just as hard as anyone else.

“Friends? That’s amazing!” Kieran finally broke out of his stupor, a huge grin spreading across his face. “If you’re friends of Zane’s, you’re friends of ours. Welcome to the World Merchants’ Guild! Please, let us host you for dinner—you’ll be our guests of honor. Come on in.”

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

 He gestured them inside, but Dorian and the others just stood there, completely still. Not a single nod, not even the hint of a polite smile. Kieran may have been one of the three influential leaders of the World Merchants’ Guild, a big name in the Heartlands, but in Dorian’s eyes, he was a nobody. The warriors from the main continent respected two people: Zane and Lavender. No one else mattered.

The silence stretched, and Kieran’s enthusiastic smile began to falter. He scratched the back of his head, looking utterly lost as he turned to Zane for help.

Seeing his friend’s awkwardness, Zane just smiled. “Dorian,” he said, his voice calm, “it was a long journey. You all must be tired. I’d like to invite you to a gathering in the city.”

[SFX: WHOOSH OF FLIGHT]

Dorian finally nodded, and his group followed Zane, taking to the air and flying toward Oracle City.

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

[MUSIC: FESTIVE ORCHESTRAL]

 The grand hall in Oracle City was completely transformed. Over a hundred long tables were arranged in a single, impressive line, with servers weaving between them carrying platters piled high with incredible food. The World Merchants' Guild had pulled out all the stops, putting on a display of hospitality the likes of which no one had ever seen. 

[SFX: LOUD EATING NOISES]

What followed left Kieran and the other Guild elders staring with their mouths open. These warriors — Life-and-Death masters, Soul Sea Realm powerhouses — ate like men who had never seen a proper meal in their lives. They praised every dish in one breath and swallowed it whole in the next, piling their plates high and going back for more.

From his seat of honor, Zane observed the warriors devouring their meals with ravenous intensity. The main continent bred only war—Holy Lands locked in eternal struggle, every race battling for survival around the Tower of Sin. No room existed there for culinary refinement. You consumed what sustained you and trained what kept you alive.

The Heartlands possessed what the main continent lacked: centuries of peace that had nurtured rich culture—food, music, art. Things that flourished only when survival wasn't a daily battle.

These warriors wielded unmatched strength. But they'd never experienced anything like this feast.

As the night wore on and the food worked its magic, the mood loosened. Dorian and Kieran fell into easy conversation, Dorian asking questions about the Heartlands and Kieran — never at a loss for words — answering every one. 

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

But even as the feast unfolded, a knot of anxiety tightened in Zane's gut. All this rare food was a stark reminder of another problem. Tia's body was still being consumed, and while the ancient heartstones from Hanson had replenished her Raw Vitality, it was only a temporary fix. They needed to find more, and fast.

When the night was over, Zane brought Dorian to Tia's bedside. Dorian reached out and took her pulse, then was quiet for a long moment.

"She isn't gravely ill," he said finally. "Her Raw Vitality is simply exhausted. Whatever replenishes it — that's what will cure her." He shook his head. "I've never come across such a thing on the main continent. But the Dark Nether Cave — it's possible something exists there. The Violet Heart Sanctuary will search it and send word if we find anything."

Zane thanked him. He believed Dorian meant it. But he also knew he couldn't afford to wait. Tia's Raw Vitality was still draining, and no one could say how much time she had left. The Divine Kingdoms were the only real lead he had.

His plan was to head to the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms to search for clues, but he couldn't do that until the threat from Lord Wallace was dealt with.

[MUSIC: ANXIOUS PIANO]

 With Dorian's warriors settled in Oracle City, all Zane could do was wait. He waited for Lord Wallace of the York Divine Kingdom to arrive, growing more anxious with each passing day. After nearly a month with no sign of him, Zane found himself regretting breaking the Octogram Mirror. He could have used it to taunt Wallace, to provoke him into crossing the Chaotic Ocean of Stars sooner.

[AMBIENT: FESTIVE MARKETPLACE]

While Zane was tied up in knots, Dorian and his people were having the time of their lives. The Heartlands might not have been a great place for training, but it was perfect for a vacation. For the first time, they weren't constantly fighting for survival. They could actually relax and enjoy the local food and customs. The Demon Night warriors were just as content, considering it the highest honor to simply accompany their Queen. Their only fear was that she might eventually send them away.

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

 Thirty-three days after their arrival, six streaks of light finally tore across the sky above the Chaotic Ocean of Stars.

Crossing the ocean was no simple feat. Even with the Star Storm gone, the waters were teeming with berserk beasts—some level ten, others even level eleven, as powerful as any warrior at the Soul Sea Realm. 

Even a master like Lord Wallace had to travel carefully, praying he didn't provoke one of the ocean's true monsters.

Six streaks of light — and Lord Wallace was finally coming.
