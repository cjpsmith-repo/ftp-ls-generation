 The World Merchants' Guild had issued a strict order: no one was to know that Zane had left the Heartlands.

It didn't take a genius to see that the Guild had the entire region in a stranglehold, and with Zane now guiding them, they were effectively running the show. This all started after he defeated Maddox. The other Fourth-Grade Sects knew to stay in their lane. After Zane led a band of unknown masters on a lightning-fast campaign that wiped out the divine kingdom's most powerful figures, they had neither the guts nor the strength to challenge him. They quickly bent the knee, relieved that Zane, for whatever reason, had no interest in founding his own kingdom.

If he had, who in the Heartlands could have possibly stopped him? If he’d decided to merge the might of Cloud Hall with the World Merchants' Guild and swallow every other faction, the entire region would have been his for the taking.

But either way, Zane’s influence was absolute. That’s why the Guild was keeping his location a secret. As long as the York Divine Kingdom believed he was still in the Heartlands, they wouldn’t dare make a move. 

Kieran and the others were pretty sure that if the York Divine Kingdom ever committed to an all-out war, not even Zane could stand against their full might. But the kingdoms in the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms all had their own agendas, and they weren’t about to exhaust their resources for a backwater territory like the Heartlands.

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

[MUSIC: EPIC ADVENTURE]

 Far above the Chaotic Ocean of Stars, two streaks of light shot east. After sending Dorian and the others on their way, Zane and Francis began their journey across the massive ocean, timing their crossing for a rare lull between storms that could last for months.

[AMBIENT: OCEAN WAVES]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

 As they traveled, Francis filled Zane in on the York Divine Kingdom and the rest of the Union. 

Unlike the Heartlands, this continent was completely controlled by the divine kingdoms. There were no independent sects. Starting your own faction was the same as building a rival power base, and the kingdoms would never allow it. 

Three of the original seven had been destroyed ages ago, but since then, no new kingdoms had ever risen to take their place, creating a kind of enforced unity across the land.

But warriors still needed to cultivate. The kingdoms still needed to raise the next generation. And so, within each divine kingdom, a different kind of institution had taken root: the Martial House.

Martial Houses existed across all four kingdoms — large ones, small ones, scattered everywhere. They functioned much like the sects of the Heartlands, but every single one answered to the kingdom it served. Independent cultivation was possible, but without a Martial House, a warrior had no access to resources, no path to advancement, and no standing. If you wanted to grow strong, you joined a house.

"If you want to enter the Sacred Feather Sea," Francis said as they flew, "you'll need to join one."

Zane's expression shifted. "Why?"

"The Sacred Feather Sea is a land of miracles," Francis replied. "The four kingdoms have monopolized it for generations. No independent cultivator is qualified to enter — let alone mine ancient heartstones from it."

Zane's brow furrowed. "It's a sea. How do four kingdoms guard an entire sea?"

"It's not as vast as the name suggests," Francis said. "It's more of a lake — enormous, but enclosed. The kingdoms have built fortresses and great cities all along its shores. Nearly seventy percent of the entire population lives near that water, and ninety percent of the realm's strongest warriors. You cannot set foot there without authorization."

Zane absorbed this in silence. It complicated everything. His original plan had been simple: cross the ocean, slip into the Sacred Feather Sea, mine enough ancient heartstones from the Feather Emperor's Secret Realm, and get out. Return to Tia. End of story.

"There are four Secret Territories inside the Sacred Feather Sea," Francis continued. "To enter, you must belong to a Martial House — and not just any house. Fifth rank or above. If you want a quota to get in, you must join a house and become its most outstanding warrior."

Zane was quiet for a moment. He'd felt this resistance before — at the Tower of Sin, when the Violet Heart Sanctuary had pressed him to join their ranks, and he'd walked away. 

He wasn't built for allegiances that meant nothing to him. Cloud Hall couldn't contain him anymore, not with the strength he'd reached. But in his bones, he was still one of theirs.

Still, joining a Martial House would be temporary—a means to an end. 

Tia's life came first — everything else could wait.

"Is there any other way in?" he asked.

Francis gave him an odd look. "You could fight your way past the War Kings and War Emperors guarding the shore." A pause. "With your strength, honestly, getting a quota through a Martial House would be the easier path."

Zane let out a slow breath and let it go. A Martial House, then. Temporary. Just long enough to get inside.

Just as Zane was settling into that thought, the ocean surface in front of them violently buckled.

[MUSIC: SUDDEN DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

 A massive whirlpool tore itself into existence.

[AMBIENT: MASSIVE OCEAN WAVES]

[SFX: WHIRLPOOL ROAR]

 Even with the storms gone, the Chaotic Ocean of Stars was a mess of mountain-sized waves. As the whirlpool formed, more than ten of these colossal swells were sucked into its spiraling vortex, crashing into one another with terrifying force.

“That’s a huge whirlpool!”

It was enormous, nearly a tenth the size of the Heartlands itself. The edges churned with a white froth of countless bubbles, while the center remained a deep, placid blue. But at its very bottom, it was pitch-black, a gaping maw that looked like a direct passage to Hell.

Francis’s eyes went wide. “Quick, fly higher!” he yelled, his voice tight with panic.

 He might have been enslaved to Zane’s will, unable to disobey an order, but that didn’t mean he wasn’t still terrified of dying.
