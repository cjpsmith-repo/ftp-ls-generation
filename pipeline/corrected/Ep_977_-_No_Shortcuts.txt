Ep 977 - No Shortcuts
Word count: 1072
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CAVE]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
“This is the True Barrier,” Saul said, his eyes on the pale yellow wall.
Zane stepped forward, reaching out to touch its smooth surface. It blocked their path completely. According to Saul, the only way forward was to shatter it.
But since Zane had the True Path Token, he had the option to just pass right through.
Zane’s eyes glinted as he stared at the wall. “So, there’s really no way for me to break it?”
“You can give it a shot,” Saul said with an amused smile.
“Can I give it a try?” Tia asked, stepping forward.
“Go for it,” Saul replied.
Seeing her eagerness, Zane just smiled and stepped aside.
Tia loved a challenge. She and Zane were both in the Life-and-Death Realm, and while she figured there was a gap between their abilities, she didn’t think it could be that big. This was the perfect chance to test her own strength.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: BUILDING INTENSITY]
The others gave her space as Tia moved gracefully to the front of the True Barrier. Her gaze locked onto the wall as the Vitality inside her began to surge.
[SFX: ICE CRYSTALLIZATION]
[SFX: PHOENIX CRY]
A sharp cry echoed as ice crystals swirled through the air, condensing into a magnificent Ice Phoenix at her side.
The temperature dropped sharply as the Ice Phoenix spun faster, its cold aura radiating outward in waves. Beside Zane, Isla and Cora instinctively braced against the chill. Without a word, Zane extended a quiet pulse of Vitality around them — a casual shield that cost him nothing.
[AMBIENT: QUIET STUDY]
“The Goddess Path,” Saul noted. “It’s a decent attunement method, but that’s an incomplete version. It can’t be fully mastered—it’s just an abridged copy.”
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
Tia’s heart skipped a beat. In the Heartlands, the Goddess Path was considered a top-tier skill. For a man with Saul’s vast knowledge to call it merely “decent,” the complete version must be unbelievably powerful. And yet, he was calling hers an abridged copy.
Her master, Elder Imogen, had been the one to teach her the Goddess Path, but even she didn’t know its true origins.
“Abridged?” Tia asked.
Saul nodded. “That’s right. The full method has been lost. I’ve spent years going through every text in The Archives. I’m sure of it.”
Abridged or not, Tia was too far along to change her path now. She had no choice but to see this one through to the end.
Cora snorted quietly to herself. Everyone was making such a fuss. She said nothing — just thought: Wait until I’ve trained with Nameless. Then we’ll see.
[SFX: ICE MAGIC WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: COLD WIND]
With a delicate twist of her fingers, Tia commanded the Ice Phoenix to coil around her arm. She fixed her gaze on the wall, and an icy chill radiated from her.
Then she struck.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT]
Her fist hit the True Barrier full force. A snow-white imprint bloomed across the surface — spreading, spreading — then stopped. It contracted. Shrank. And disappeared without a trace.
Tia lowered her fist. The wall was unchanged.
Zane watched quietly. That punch could have leveled anyone in the Heartlands — Elder Imogen, rogue Life-and-Death cultivators, it didn’t matter. None of them could have withstood it. And the barrier hadn’t moved.
Tia gave a faint smile and shook her head. She had expected this. She still found it shocking.
She stepped aside.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Watching them, Isla felt a familiar anxiety creep in. In just a few short years, Zane had become impossibly strong. What kind of man was he becoming? A place as small as The Heartlands could never contain him. The realization sent a wave of panic through her. She couldn’t even begin to imagine how powerful he would eventually be.
What Isla didn’t realize was that Cora had already faced this same fear. Two months ago, after the old man warned her that Zane’s progress would leave her in the dust, Cora had taken action, traveling to the Violet Core Realm to find a master who could help her improve her own attunement.
But Cora had a special physique—a way to handle the crisis. What did Isla have? How could she ever hope to keep up with him?
She remembered fighting with the old man in charge of the entry test when Zane first joined the Skyreach Sect, just to get him accepted. Now, that same boy was so powerful she could only stare up at him in awe.
A pang of despair hit her. Zane had come back and confessed how he felt about her, but she was powerless to do anything about it. She felt like a puppet on a string, unable to change a single thing.
Zane stepped up to the True Barrier and pressed his palm flat against its surface. He reached out with his senses, probing the Law Power woven into it — something subtle, not a Source Law, something older and stranger.
“This barrier,” he said slowly. “It’s built on the Law of Causality, isn’t it?”
Saul raised an eyebrow. “Correct. The original master of this Keep set it up casually — a barrier of pure Law of Causality. At your current level, you cannot penetrate it.” He paused. “If you don’t want to waste time, I’d suggest using the Thunder token.”
Zane pulled his hand back. He glanced at the token in his Storage Ring — and left it there.
He knew the shortcut was available. He also knew that cultivators who lived by shortcuts never went far. There were some you couldn’t avoid, and some you had to refuse. This was one he had to refuse. He needed to know where his limits actually were.
[MUSIC: RISING POWER]
He rolled his shoulders and faced the wall.
Deep inside him, the Dragon Scales began to light up, one by one.
[SFX: CRACKING JOINTS]
His knuckles cracked. Then his elbows. Then his spine. The power flooding through him was immense — five thousand Dragon Scales, his current limit. His body began to expand, doubling in size as the energy strained against the boundaries of his flesh.
“Zane—” Tia started, her voice tight with worry.
He was already moving.
[SFX: BOOM]
As Isla was lost in her spiraling thoughts, a deafening BOOM! ripped through the air.
Zane had thrown his punch.
[AMBIENT: RUMBLING ENERGY WAVES]
The impact sent a shockwave of energy blasting outward, forcing Isla and Cora to stumble back to keep their footing. Unfazed, Tia and Saul simply watched as Zane’s fist met the True Barrier.
[SFX: CRACKING STONE]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
The entire wall trembled violently. Spiderweb cracks raced across its surface for a split second before sealing themselves shut again.
His attempt had failed.
