Ep 605 - Against the Rules
Word count: 1159
[AMBIENT: TOWER ARENA]
[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
The silence that followed Malcolm's challenge was the loudest thing Zane had ever heard.
A seat holder challenging a War Fighter? That was unheard of in the Tower of Sin.
The seat holders were all tenth-level War Lords—some would even say peak-level. They were just one step shy of becoming War Sages. And Zane? He was a tenth-level War Fighter, two whole ranks beneath them.
As powerful as Zane was, with all his potential, he stood no chance against Malcolm.
The rules of the Tower of Sin were clear: fighters from the upper floors couldn't challenge those on the lower ones. By challenging Zane, Malcolm wasn't just breaking that rule—he was shattering it. The consequences were unimaginable, and you could see it in the shocked faces all around.
If an Ogre seat holder was the first to break the rule, what was to stop the other races from doing the same? Their fighters would swarm the lower floors.
If that happened, the Tower of Sin would descend back into total chaos.
And no one wanted that.
[SFX: FOOTSTEP]
Perhaps that was why, just as Malcolm took a step toward the arena, a delicate but firm voice cut through the tension.
"An Ogre War Lord, challenging a tenth-floor fighter? That's against the rules."
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Everyone's heads whipped around to the source of the voice: Cheryl, clad in green.
Everyone knew Cheryl and Boris were the strongest warriors on the tenth floor. But why would she call out Malcolm? Did she not realize he was breaking the rule on purpose just to kill Zane? Or was this the Demon Night making a play to maintain order in the Tower?
It wasn't out of the question. After all, the Demon Night held a significant advantage in the Tower, controlling the second-most seats right behind the Ogres. If anyone had the strength to stand up to them, it was the Demon Night.
But plenty of onlookers had heard the Demon Night's seat holder, Madeline, speak earlier. Her expression had made it clear they wanted no part in this. And there she was now, still sitting on the sidelines, looking completely unbothered. So why was a junior like Cheryl stepping up?
As Malcolm started for the arena, the color drained from Olivia, Fabian, and Jarrod’s faces. Fabian wrestled with himself, wondering if he should try to stop him.
Once Malcolm attacked, this wouldn't just be a fight—it would escalate into all-out war between the Humans and the Ogres.
The Violet Heart Sanctuary was no match for the combined might of the Flaming Place and the Dread Place. The power gap was staggering: the Humans held a mere four seats, while the Ogres controlled over twenty. Once they started fighting, the consequences would be unimaginable.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Malcolm's massive eyes narrowed as he sneered at Cheryl. "What have you done to earn the right to lecture me, girl? Where's Madeline?"
He wasn't interested in Cheryl. He wanted to know where the Demon Night's seat holder stood.
Cheryl held her ground, expression unchanged.
From the sidelines, Madeline finally rose. Her voice was quiet but carried easily across the arena. "The rules of this Tower were set by all of us together. As I see it, Cheryl has every right to challenge Zane herself. What happens after her challenge—" she glanced at Malcolm, "—is your business."
The meaning was plain. The Demon Night would not interfere with whatever the Ogres chose to do. But Cheryl wanted her fight first.
Malcolm held Madeline's gaze for a moment, then let out a short, contemptuous laugh. "Fine. I'm not in a hurry." He stepped back, clasped his hands behind him, and waited.
In his mind, it made no difference. Zane wasn't leaving the Tower today. Whether Cheryl fought him first changed nothing.
[AMBIENT: CROWD EXHALE]
A ripple of reaction moved through the human section. For just a moment, when Cheryl had spoken up, Olivia had felt something lift in her chest. A possibility. But Madeline's words had closed that door firmly. The Demon Night wasn't riding to the rescue. Cheryl simply wanted her turn.
Olivia looked back at the arena. Even if Zane beat Cheryl, Malcolm was still waiting. There was no path through this. She couldn't find one, no matter how hard she looked.
She watched Zane standing in the center of the arena, sword at his side, and felt a deep, helpless weight settle over her. He was still smiling. How was he still smiling?
Many others in the crowd were asking the same question. Where did that calm come from? What did he know that they didn't?
[SFX: LIGHT FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Cheryl stepped into the arena. A slender sword appeared in her hand. She looked at Zane evenly. "I want to challenge you."
Zane nodded, raising his sword.
"You had the strength to beat Boris," Cheryl said. "But that doesn't mean you can beat me."
"We're already in the arena," Zane said. "Let's skip the rest."
Something flickered in Cheryl's eyes. "You're very arrogant. I hope you can keep that up."
[SFX: WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Her figure blurred. With a single step, she crossed seventy feet in the blink of an eye, appearing right beside Zane. She leveled her sword, the tip aimed squarely between his eyes.
[SFX: GASPS FROM CROWD]
"The Spatial Law!" someone in the crowd gasped.
[SFX: SPATIAL DISTORTION]
Zane just offered a faint smile. With a step of his own, his body flickered, and he vanished through space.
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
Cheryl's sword passed through empty air. Zane had already reappeared behind her, slashing out in the exact same manner.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]
"They're both using the Spatial Law?"
"I thought Zane was a body refiner! Turns out he cultivates Vitality, too!"
"How many tricks does this kid have up his sleeve?"
With both fighters wielding the Spatial Law, pinning down a winner was nearly impossible. The Law allowed them to move with total freedom, making it incredibly difficult to corner an opponent.
Because of this, the fight became a dizzying blur of motion, the pace accelerating with every passing second.
[SFX: WHOOSH TELEPORT]
Zane teleported behind Cheryl, but in a flash, she was at his side. Before her sword could find its mark, he was gone again.
Their forms flickered in and out of existence, dazzling the crowd. They moved so fast that, despite their different clothes, some onlookers couldn't even tell them apart.
[SFX: RAPID SWORD STRIKES]
In a blur of teleportation, Zane finally found an opening. Their swords met in a rapid-fire clash—seven, eight times in the span of a heartbeat.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
When it came to pure swordplay, Cheryl was outmatched. She had watched Zane’s fight with Boris, had even seen his Formless Sword technique firsthand, but she still had no way to counter it.
It was like fighting a ghost—his moves were simply invisible.
