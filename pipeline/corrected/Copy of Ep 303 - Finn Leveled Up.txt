Ep 303 - Finn Leveled Up
Word count: 1202
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL MOUNTAIN]
[MUSIC: EPIC ADVENTURE]
[SFX: SWORD FLYING THROUGH AIR]
It might have looked ridiculous for Zane to hurl his sword with all his might, but it was the right move. Anyone who came here aimed for the peak of Killing Sword Mountain. That spot offered the ultimate prize, but it wasn’t nearly that simple.
To get to the peak, he would have to overcome a series of challenges, defeating the sword intents hidden within the blades that lined the single path to the summit. Those spectral energies blocked the way, and Zane had to get past them.
But he knew he couldn’t defeat the spirit of the next sword. His only real option was to throw his sword toward the mountaintop and hope for the best. If he could pull it off, his sword would land far higher than he could ever climb.
The Streamer, boosted by his own might and the dragon's power, carved a brilliant silver arc through the air.
[SFX: SWORD FALLING]
[SFX: SWORD SLAMMING INTO ROCK]
It shot upward with so much force that for a few breathless moments, it seemed to defy gravity itself before finally losing momentum and arcing back toward the earth. It slammed dead center into the mountain ridge, right where all the top-grade Sacred weapons were lodged. As a lower-grade Mythic Weapon, the Streamer stood out among them, an unmistakable intruder.
Watching this, the Cobalt Dragon was left speechless. Killing Sword Mountain was a sacred place with its own unbreakable rules. Breaking them meant instant expulsion. Even someone as powerful as the Cobalt Dragon didn’t dare interfere, afraid he might violate the rules and face punishment. But what Zane had done... it wasn’t technically cheating. He just threw his sword. Since it was the weapon, and not Zane himself, attempting to reach the top, it wasn’t a violation.
[SFX: SWORD HUM — DEEP AND RESONANT]
A faint light pulsed from the Streamer. Then Zane felt it — a connection forming deep in his bones, as though an invisible thread had been drawn between his body and the mountain itself. It was unlike anything he'd ever felt.
[SFX: WORLD COLLAPSING — DISTANT RUMBLE]
Then the mountain dissolved. The whole world of Killing Sword Mountain crumbled to ash around him, and his consciousness snapped back.
[SFX: SILENCE — ABRUPT]
As his mind cleared, the Cobalt Dragon's voice came through, quieter than usual.
"You really surprised me. The path of Malice — it's a true path of the strong. Make good use of it." A pause. "The Drake Bone Spear fragment I absorbed wasn't nearly enough for something like me. I need to sleep for a while."
And then he was gone — dormant, his presence fading to silence.
[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]
From a distance, Zane watched Finn, who had been lost in a state of epiphany for two hours and still showed no sign of stirring. A knot of excitement and worry tightened in Zane's gut. He was worried because the world was full of unknown and dangerous teleportation spots. He had no clue where Finn had been taken, or why he hadn’t woken up yet.
[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]
Then, in a flash, Finn’s eyes snapped open.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS EXPLOSION]
[MUSIC: INTENSE POWER]
As his gaze sharpened, a fierce sword intent erupted from his body. It swirled into a massive cyclone that clawed at the sky, shot through with terrifying flashes of crackling thunder and lightning.
[SFX: PRIMAL HUM]
"Is that... thunder in Finn’s sword intent?" Zane’s eyes narrowed.
The blue lightning arcing around Finn looked unbelievably potent.
Combined with his sword intent, it even gave off the faint hum of some celestial law.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
Zane smiled faintly when he heard the crowd murmuring about Finn’s new skill.
His greatest gain from the epiphany was a direct connection to Killing Sword Mountain itself. It wasn’t something the audience could see, but he could feel it deep in his bones. Once he started practicing the killing sword skill, the benefits would slowly reveal themselves. His second biggest gain was the sword spirit. Lily, born with a powerful, dark aura, was now a formidable ally he could count on from the moment of her creation.
But he hadn’t let her appear publicly. She remained hidden in the depths of Zane’s mind, her aura completely contained. Because of that, no one had any idea that a whole new person was concealed within him.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT REVELATION]
Finn’s sword intent surged for a long time before it finally settled.
He took a deep breath and let it out slowly, a peaceful, happy smile spreading across his face. For someone as stoic as Finn, a look like that could only mean one thing: he had gained something immense while he was gone.
And he had.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL REALM]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
The moment he grasped the Apex of Sword Intent, he had been pulled into a place called The Sword Tempering Pool.
As a devoted student of the sword—its powers and its legends—Finn had paid close attention to the stories of the masters. He’d read about The Sword Tempering Pool in an ancient text, which described it as a semi-sacred place.
So, when he found himself with the chance to enter it, how could he possibly contain his excitement? After all, only a handful of people out of tens of millions of swordsmen ever got a chance like this. Not everyone could perfect their sword intent, and even for those who did, there was no guarantee they'd be granted access to such a hallowed ground.
Training in The Sword Tempering Pool didn’t just refine his sword intent; it could also imbue it with a special property.
So Finn had submerged his blade in the pool and began to practice. After what felt like an eternity, he had infused his sword intent with the property of thunder, and his swordsmanship had improved by leaps and bounds. He was a completely different fighter now.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]
The two stood facing each other, the air between them growing thick with tension.
In the stands, the disciples whispered eagerly among themselves.
"So, who do you think is stronger now, Finn or Zane?" one disciple asked his friends.
"Come on, it’s obviously Finn. Look at that thunder and lightning! His sword intent is insane. I think Finn’s got this in the bag," another answered confidently.
"Yeah, and it looks like Zane gained a lot less," a third commented.
"When Zane woke up, there was nothing. But Finn is literally crackling with lightning. Looks like Zane’s epiphany was kind of a bust."
The state of epiphany was a massive opportunity for any martial artist, but there was no guarantee it would yield anything incredible. Anything could happen. You heard stories all the time about people entering an epiphany and coming out with nothing to show for it.
[SFX: CRACKLING THUNDER]
Finn raised his Blood-Corroding Sword slightly. Now that he had mastered the Apex of Sword Intent, the cyclones of energy around him felt far more aggressive, laced with the barely contained power of thunder and lightning. Finn knew the power he'd absorbed from The Sword Tempering Pool was anything but ordinary. It was far beyond anything a mere mortal could imagine.
This time, he was sure he could finally defeat Zane.
[SFX: MYSTICAL SHIMMER]
As Finn gathered his power, brimming with confidence, Zane called upon the sword spirit in his mind. And beside him, Lily slowly faded into view.
"Wait, what? Who is that?" a disciple shouted, his voice cracking.
"There’s another person in the arena! And she’s... beautiful! Where did she come from?!"
