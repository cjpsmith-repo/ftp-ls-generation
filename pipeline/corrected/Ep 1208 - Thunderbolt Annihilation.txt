Ep 1208 - Thunderbolt Annihilation

Word count: 990

[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE THEME]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL COMBAT ARENA]
Zane’s first line of defense was a wall of dozens of Nether God Shadows, backed by a spinning wheel of sword light that shielded him from within. You’d think a defense like that would be unbreakable.
It wasn’t.
Not against the three Celestial Mice. Their speed was on a completely different level, far beyond anything Zane had prepared for.
[SFX: RAPID WHOOSHING]
[SFX: GHOSTLY SHADOWS DISPERSING]
The three tiny, golden blurs zipped straight through the ghostly shadows, moving so fast they were practically invisible. Zane’s spinning sword wasn’t a solid barrier, either—it was just an illusion created by the rapid motion of his blade, and it was full of tiny gaps just big enough for the mice. They shot through the cracks in an instant and were on him.
[SFX: SHARP BITES]
[SFX: BODY IMPACT]
Three sharp, stinging bites, and blood beaded on his skin, just like before. One of them had gone for his collarbone. He’d thrown himself back on pure instinct, a split second that saved his throat from being torn out.
The wounds themselves weren’t large, but the fact that they’d been able to bite him at all was the problem. Most attacks barely even scratched Zane, but these things were different. Their teeth were unstoppable, leaving clear puncture marks on his skin.
[SFX: HIGH-PITCHED MOUSE SQUEAKING]
In the blink of an eye, the three mice reappeared a safe distance away, chittering to each other in a series of high-pitched squeaks. Zane could have sworn he saw a flicker of mockery in their tiny, black-bead eyes. Legendary beasts were known for their intelligence, and these Celestial Mice were clearly no exception.
[SFX: DISTANT SQUEAKING]
Watching them, Zane felt himself grow calm. He was up against the legendary Celestial Mice, ranked number one among their kind—a species said to be second only to the greater legendary beasts themselves. The stories claimed they could reproduce at an insane rate. He was only facing three right now, which was manageable, but if he were up against a hundred of them? He’d be running for his life, no question.
He couldn’t match their speed. He couldn’t even track their movements. How was he supposed to kill something he couldn’t even see?
They had a strange pattern, too. If he stood still, they stood still. The moment he so much as flinched, they launched a perfectly coordinated attack. It was a level of teamwork most human warriors could only dream of.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Zane stood there, lost in thought. A moment later, a faint smile touched his lips.
[SFX: SUBTLE ENERGY HUM]
“Eight Smoky Melodies.”
He didn’t move. The mice didn’t either—not at first. Then the first shadow lunged, and the world blurred.
[SFX: RAPID BLURRING WHOOSHES]
[SFX: OVERLAPPING IMPACTS, NEAR-MISSES]
One mouse came from the left, and Zane’s body simply wasn’t there anymore—still floating, still upright, but three inches clear of its teeth. The second came from another angle entirely, and he matched it without seeming to try, the gap between them never closing past a hair’s width. The third joined a heartbeat later, and still he held the same maddening distance from all of them at once, drifting up and down in the air with his eyes calm and his face unreadable.
To anyone watching, it would have looked like four shadows colliding in a single instant, impossible to tell apart.
[SFX: WHOOSHES]
As he dodged again and again, threads of chaotic energy spun loose from his body. Half of it fed straight back into sustaining Eight Smoky Melodies. The other half sank quietly into the ground beneath him, where it gathered into a faint, expanding ring—a thousand feet across—humming with Thunder Law.
This was the second technique his big brother had once recommended to him. He still didn’t know what level it belonged to, only that it drank down chaotic energy at a terrifying rate—enough that an ordinary Soul Sea Realm warrior couldn’t have powered it even by emptying themselves completely. He’d felt its strength before, back in the Immortal Mansion. This was the first time he’d ever used it in a real fight.
[MUSIC: RISING MYSTICAL POWER]
[SFX: ENERGY RIPPLING]
[SFX: SMOKE SWIRLING]
Ripples spread across the surface of the chaotic energy around him as white smoke began to swirl from his body once more.
[SFX: URGENT MOUSE SQUEAKS]
This time, the mice’s squeaks grew sharp, urgent.
Something in their blood recognized the danger before their eyes did. Without a sound passing between them, the three mice broke apart, bolting in three different directions.
“Can’t escape,” Zane said quietly, watching them scatter.
[SFX: DESCENDING PRESSURE WAVE]
[SFX: AIR WARPING DISTORTION]
A massive pressure suddenly descended from the sky as a mysterious power began to warp the air itself—the kind of phenomenon that usually only happened during a tribulation. 
But Zane wasn’t waiting for one. He was calling it down on purpose.
[SFX: LIGHTNING BOLT IMPACT]
[AMBIENT: CONTINUOUS THUNDER STORM]
A thick bolt of lightning, coiled like a dragon, slammed down from the heavens with a flash of white-hot energy. 
[SFX: MULTIPLE LIGHTNING STRIKES CASCADING]
Lightning struck everywhere at once, over and over again.
Even then, the three Celestial Mice didn’t give up. They pushed their speed to the absolute limit, weaving through the tiny gaps between the forks of lightning, trying to find a safe spot.
But there wasn’t one. The Thunderbolt Annihilation was overwhelming, covering nearly every inch of ground. As the lightning grew denser and the bolts grew thicker, the gaps between them vanished. The three mice had nowhere left to run, nowhere left to hide.
When the sky finally cleared, everything was still.
The three Celestial Mice lay smoldering on the ground, their bodies charred black. 
[SFX: BODIES DISINTEGRATING]
[SFX: ENERGY POINTS RUSHING]
A moment later, their corpses disintegrated, and from each one, a thousand Illusion Points shot toward Zane.
“Three thousand Illusion Points,” Zane murmured, a grin spreading across his face.
His main goal had been the cave, so getting such a valuable reward for killing the mice was a nice bonus. 
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Still, he knew that in the grand scheme of things, three thousand points was nothing. A warrior could earn that much just by killing sixty other fighters. In the Illusion Battlefield, the most aggressive players had already taken out thousands of opponents, raking in rewards that made his look like pocket change.
