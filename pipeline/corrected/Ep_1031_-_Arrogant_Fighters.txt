Ep 1031 - Arrogant Fighters

Word count: 948

[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL MURMURS]

When the other fighters saw that Zane had no intention of actually training in the Myriad Spirit House, it didn’t sit right with them.

“Who does this guy think he is?”

“The nerve. Seriously, does he think he’s too good for the Myriad Spirit House?”

“Some guy from the Lower World, looking down on this place? Unbelievable. What a jerk.”

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The mood inside soured as the trash talk about Zane started to fly. Before long, rumors about the arrogant fighter from the Lower World spread all the way to the Holy City. For some nobody from the Lower World to disrespect a sacred training ground that most of them only dreamed of entering—it was an insult.

Zane had become public enemy number one. At first, they’d thought it was a joke. Now, they were genuinely offended.

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]

Then, on the sixth day, a crew showed up at the Myriad Spirit House.

Zane recognized them the second they walked in. They were the same young hotshots who’d tried to jump him right after he arrived in the Holy City. Two of their strongest had been so badly beaten in that fight they’d spent the last few days just learning how to stand again.

Most of them were kids of the Soulbound Sect’s elders—talented, for sure, but spoiled rotten and arrogant as hell. They’d heard the rumors about some upstart disrespecting the Myriad Spirit House, and they weren’t about to let that slide.

Once their two injured friends were finally back on their feet, the whole crew had returned to their home turf at the Myriad Spirit House.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

Their anger, already stoked by the rumors, boiled over. They stormed inside and spotted a guy meditating in the corner, his eyes closed. But as they got closer, their jaws dropped.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

“No way… it’s him.”

“Hmph. We’ve been looking for this asshole all over the city. And here he is.”

“So, what’s the move? We’re gonna teach him a lesson, or what?”

[SFX: DRAMATIC STING]

After their last run-in, the group had immediately started digging into Zane’s background. The Holy City was massive, and finding one person was like looking for a needle in a haystack. They never imagined they’d just stumble upon him here—or that he was the same jerk from the rumors.

“And get this—he’s a complete nobody. Just some trash from the Lower World. How pathetic is that?”

“Hold on, let’s not get cocky. There’s something off about his strength. We should get someone else to deal with him.”

“I’ll go get him! Mervin Thorne would be perfect for this.”

Zane’s eyes stayed closed. But the corners of his mouth curved up slightly. He’d heard every word.

He’d known from the start that ignoring them wouldn’t make them go away. If they were this determined to make noise, the least he could do was let them bring someone worth his time.

They retreated. A day later, they were back — and this time, they’d brought someone with them.

The warrior they brought looked to be in his early twenties, with a hint of ruthlessness between his brows and three blood-colored scars cutting across his face. His name was Mervin Thorne.

He walked straight up to Zane. “I heard an ascender joined the Myriad Spirit House and decided to just sit in the corner alone,” he said, his voice cold. “If you’re waiting for someone, fine. But how about a spar while you wait?”

Zane opened his eyes. “A spar?”

Mervin nodded.

Zane shook his head.

Mervin’s expression darkened. “What — you don’t dare?”

[SFX: TENSION RISE]

The tension in the room crackled. The other top talents from the Soulbound Sect were furious. Zane wasn’t just looking down on Mervin Thorne, one of the Vanguard One Hundred. He was looking down on all of them.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Mervin took a few deep breaths, trying to rein in his anger. “I don’t care if you think I’m qualified,” he said to Zane, his voice tight. “You’re accepting this challenge. And if you don’t, you can’t blame me for what I do next.”

The Myriad Spirit House had a strict no-fighting rule outside of sanctioned duels. But right now, the crowd was so worked up, the rules didn’t seem to matter. Even if Mervin Thorne broke them, nobody would stop him. All they wanted was to see Zane get his ass kicked.

Mervin knew it, too, and he was more than willing to use it to his advantage.

Through all the hostility, Zane remained perfectly calm. “You aren’t qualified,” he said, his voice low and serious. “I mean it. If you force this, you’re only going to humiliate yourself. Are you sure you’re ready for that?”

Rage pounded in Mervin Thorne’s ears. Zane’s words made his fists tremble.

Mervin was strong — genuinely strong — but he was a loner, and words weren’t his weapon. He couldn’t think of a single thing to say. He just stood there, staring at Zane like he wanted to swallow him whole.

Talk was over.

[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

He began to channel his Vitality. If Zane wouldn’t accept, he’d make him fight.

[AMBIENT: CROWD GASPS]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC SHIFT]

Just then, a fighter dressed in white appeared at the edge of the crowd. “He’s telling the truth, Mervin,” the newcomer said, his voice cutting through the tension. “You’re not strong enough to face him.”

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED WHISPERS]

“Fenton!”

“Fenton Summers is actually here!”

“Is he here for Zane, too?”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS THEME]

The gap between Fenton and the second-ranked fighter wasn’t just large — it was considered unimaginable, even among the top talents of the Soulbound Sect. His arrival silenced the room completely.

The crowd didn’t erupt. They went quiet. Fenton Summers, the undisputed crown of the Myriad Spirit House, had just taken the side of a nobody from the Lower World.
