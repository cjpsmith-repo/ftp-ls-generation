Ep 951 - Berserker
Word count: 1139
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
To reach the Life-and-Death Realm, every warrior first had to pass through the Lesser Celestial Tribulation.
And for nearly everyone who earned a title on the Talent Tablet, the tribulation meant one thing for sure: they would summon a lotus flower.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
What Max didn’t know was that Zane had already faced his Lesser Celestial Tribulation on The Feather Emperor’s Island. At the time, the Spirit Supreme Realm warriors from the Demi-Immortal Race had been stuck in the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, trying to figure out how to lure out the Evil Dragon. They were completely oblivious to what had happened—or just how far Zane had truly come.
And only now did the critical question finally dawn on Max.
He knew that almost all titled warriors could summon a lotus flower. But what kind had appeared for Zane during his first Lesser Celestial Tribulation?
A three-petal lotus? A five-petal one? Or maybe even seven or nine petals?
Zane’s golden celestial rank was incredibly rare. That meant that even on his first tribulation, he’d likely summoned a seven- or even nine-petal lotus flower. A Greater Lotus Flower wasn’t out of the question, either.
[MUSIC: TENSE PIANO]
“Zane,” Max asked, his voice low and unable to hold back any longer. “When you went through the Lesser Celestial Tribulation, did a lotus flower appear?”
The question was laced with a newfound respect. His entire attitude toward Zane had shifted.
Even Delroy’s arrogance had evaporated. A truth he would have dismissed as impossible had just been proven right before his eyes. In that moment, he knew he had nothing to be proud of in Zane’s presence.
[AMBIENT: SILENT TENSION]
Every warrior from the Demi-Immortal Race watched Zane, their eyes fixed on his face as they waited for his answer. They were all just as curious.
Zane hesitated, feeling the weight of their stares. Finally, he answered, “Yeah. It did.”
“How many petals? How many did your lotus flower have?” Delroy blurted out, even more desperate to know than Max. All his earlier arrogance was gone.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHOIR]
No one could explain the exact connection between the lotus flower and a warrior’s talent, but the principle was simple: the more petals, the greater one’s potential to defy the heavens.
Zane just smiled. “How many petals did yours have?”
Max was quiet for a moment. Then, evenly: “Seven. After entering the Extreme Divine Stage, my lotus opened seven petals.”
[SFX: QUIET EXHALE — IMPRESSED]
Seven petals was already an extraordinary achievement. Even among Demi-Immortal geniuses, few could claim as much.
Delroy shrugged. “Five, in my last tribulation.”
“Five as well,” said Dorothea, with a small smile. “Though I never earned a title on the Talent Tablet. My talent falls short of theirs — but five petals all the same.”
Zane nodded slowly. “Thirty-five,” he said.
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
The young man in black nearly choked. Delroy and Dorothea stared as though the words hadn’t reached them yet.
Only Max stayed composed. He let out a quiet laugh. “Only one petal can reach perfection,” he said. “Heh.”
“What does that mean?” Zane asked. “What happens when a lotus reaches perfection?”
Max smiled. “When you open the last petal, you’ll understand.”
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
At that moment, Max ordered his puppet to walk toward Kade.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
A young man had just stepped out of the Talent Tablet — ordinary-looking, tenth-level War General, but with the easy confidence of someone who already knew what the result would say. That was Kade.
A flicker of doubt crossed Kade’s face. He’d just emerged from the Talent Tablet, completely missing the eight strange guests who had materialized on the tenth floor of the Tower of Sin. They looked human, but all he could sense were their soul waves—no breath, no signs of life.
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]
The top human War Lords stared at the newcomers warily. They’d been on high alert since the strangers appeared, but Kade’s test results had been so distracting that no one had confronted them yet.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT SWELL]
And those results were staggering. The Talent Tablet had lit up the moment Kade walked out — a layer of silver light spreading across the wall, coalescing into a single word.
“Berserker: Kade.”
The name appeared just below Zane’s.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED CROWD]
“Kade — he actually did it! He’s ranked second, right behind Zane!”
“Second on the entire Talent Tablet — what is happening to the human race? Two prodigies like this, back to back?”
“Dorian’s already leading the assault on the Twin Devil sacred lands. At this rate, they’ll be rubble before the year’s out.”
[MUSIC: EERIE DISBELIEF]
The Demi-Immortals went silent. Max, Delroy, the young man in black, Dorothea — all of them stared at the name on the wall with expressions like they’d seen a ghost.
After a long moment, Delroy spoke. “I’ve genuinely seen a ghost. This world makes no sense.”
A Silver Berserker title. Kade had just surpassed all seven of them on the Talent Tablet.
Even Max had only earned a silver title — and not a particularly high one. In the Demi-Immortal Race, a silver title was rare enough to make you a core member, the kind of warrior the faction placed in positions of real authority. That was exactly why the other six deferred to Max on this mission.
And here, in a lower realm, a young human had just matched it.
Suddenly, someone with sharp eyes spotted Zane standing a few feet from Max and the others.
[SFX: EXCITED SHOUT]
“Zane! Hey, that’s Zane! I don’t believe it!”
“Zane’s back on the main continent!”
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
But just as cheers were about to erupt, someone noticed something was off. “Wait… something’s wrong. He looks like Zane, but he doesn’t feel human at all.”
“That’s because it’s a puppet! Who are you, pretending to be Zane?”
The main continent was only familiar with crude puppets; no one there had the skill to create something as lifelike as the one Wade had crafted. The puppet was a near-perfect replica of Zane, but there was still an uncanny valley between a real person and a fake. Any powerful warrior could tell something was off with a single glance.
[AMBIENT: CROWD SILENCE]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STAIRS]
As confusion and caution rippled through the crowd, a young girl in a colorful dress descended the stairs. Her voice, sharp and cold, cut through the tension.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
“That is the Zane you know,” she stated. “But it seems his power has reached the level of a War Sage, forcing him to send a puppet into the Cursed Land. That technique… it’s the Puppet Art of the Demi-Immortal Race, isn’t it? And these seven with him—you’re all from the Demi-Immortal Race, too.”
