Ep 976 - My Mind’s Made Up
Word count: 1087
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Saul’s words left Tia reeling.
Zane had gone from an Adept to a Life-and-Death Realm warrior in just four years. And Saul was telling him he was too slow? That kind of leap was almost unheard of. In all her time in the Heartlands, Tia had never met anyone that gifted.
Just how high were this guy’s standards?
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
But Saul wasn’t finished, and his next words were even more stunning. He sighed, looking Zane over. “Yes, I see it. You may only be at the Life-and-Death Realm, but your foundation is more solid than most. Your actual strength is closer to a Soul Sea Realm warrior. But it’s still not enough. At this level, you can’t break the barrier to the true path of the Elysian Keep, let alone walk it. I’m sorry. You’ll need to train for a few more years, then come back.”
Tia was floored. Not enough? she thought. Was this place even in the Eastern Territories? A Life-and-Death warrior wasn’t qualified to pass? Just what was the Elysian Keep?
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHIMES]
But Zane didn’t look discouraged in the slightest. Instead, a smile touched his lips. “What if I have this?”
[SFX: MYSTICAL SHIMMER]
He reached into his Storage Ring and pulled out another token, this one carved with the word “Thunder.”
The token was a gift from Vesper. After suffering the Five Aging Processes, Vesper had been reduced to a mere wisp of a remnant soul. The token was useless to him in that state, so he’d given it to Zane, telling him it was the key to activating the true path of the Elysian Keep.
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT STONE CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
The Elysian Keep was crucial to Zane’s plans. He was determined to control it before he ascended to the upper world, and the only way to do that was to cross its true path. There was no other way.
[SFX: METALLIC CHIME]
Saul’s gaze fell to the token in Zane’s hand. “That’s Vesper’s,” he said, his voice calm. “I take it he’s passed on, then. I’m surprised he gave it to you.” He paused. “Yes, this will let you enter the true path without breaking the barrier. But at your current strength? You probably won’t even clear the first stage.”
“What’s the reward for passing the first stage?” Zane asked, his eyes lighting up.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
Saul replied, “The Elysian Keep holds eight tokens in total: Heaven, Earth, Water, Fire, Thunder, Wind, Mountain, and Valley. You might manage to collect six of them, but the Heaven and Earth tokens… I’m afraid those are out of your league.”
“Six tokens? But I already have two of them—one for ‘Thunder’ and one for ‘Wind’.”
Saul nodded. “Each token unlocks something different. The Wind token grants access to the outer courtyard — you already have that. The Thunder token opens the true path — you have that too. The Valley token opens the alchemy room. The Fire token gives you control of the weapon-refining workshop. The Water token grants entry to the library. The Mountain token…” He paused, something shifting in his expression. “You’ll learn what that one does when you reach it.”
Zane turned this over in his mind. Six tokens, six different chambers — each one a piece of the Keep that had been locked away from him. He glanced at the Wind and Thunder tokens already in his hand. Two down.
“And the Heaven and Earth tokens?” he asked. “What do those unlock?”
Saul’s expression didn’t change. “Ask me again when you have the other six. Until then, I won’t answer that.”
Zane let it go. Saul’s rules were Saul’s rules.
“Can I use an outside power source?” Cora asked suddenly.
[SFX: ETHEREAL RESONANCE]
Saul gave Cora a long, assessing look. “The Violet-Core Physique,” he noted. “Your body has hidden spatial nodes linking you to the Heartlands.” He shook his head. “But no. You cannot draw on external power here.”
[MUSIC: SOMBER PIANO]
Saul had called her Violet-Core Physique by another name, but Cora barely registered it. All that mattered was that she couldn’t use her powers. Without them, she knew she wasn’t strong enough to pass the trial on her own. She decided then and there to sit this one out.
“Is the true path dangerous?” Cora asked, her voice quieter now.
Saul considered her for a moment. “That depends entirely on Zane.” He left it at that.
Cora looked at Zane. Zane looked unbothered. That, somehow, was both reassuring and not.
[AMBIENT: ECHOING CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Before Saul could lead Zane toward the true path, another thought struck him. He turned back. “Last time I passed the trial — can others take it too?”
Saul raised an eyebrow. “Yes. But only you can receive tokens.”
Zane nodded. That was enough. He looked at the three women. Even if Tia already had her own cultivation method, the contemplation pictures might still be useful to her — and to the others. It was worth asking.
The reactions were exactly what he expected. Tia was immediately willing — she wasn’t afraid of any trial. Isla hesitated; her cultivation was the lowest of the three, and she wasn’t sure she could pass. Saul told her the difficulty would adjust to her level. That settled it for her. Without her Violet-Core Physique, Cora was the weakest of the group, and she knew it. She shook her head. Zane didn’t push her.
Her mind was made up. So, with Saul leading the way, Tia and Isla went first.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]
The trial was over in less than an hour. Both women passed.
[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]
[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]
Tia emerged looking calm and collected, but Isla was another story. Her face was ghost-white long after she’d left the illusion, and she only started to relax after Zane gave her a pill to soothe her nerves.
Tia’s reward was the Cloud and Flying Birds Picture, a prize second only to the legendary Tapestry of Sun, Moon, and Stars.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WONDER]
Given Tia’s incredible talent and strength, her success wasn’t surprising. But Isla’s result was a genuine shock. Despite her terror, she had managed to obtain the third-ranked contemplation picture: the Nine-Day Cycle Picture.
A pang of jealousy hit Cora as she saw Tia and Isla receive their contemplation pictures. She knew her limits, though. Without her Violet-Core Physique, she was the weakest of the three of them, hands down. She silently vowed to train harder in the Apeiron Tower. Next time, she wouldn’t be the one left behind.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
Once the women had completed their trials, Saul turned to Zane. “Your turn.”
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
[SFX: ECHOING FOOTSTEPS]
He led Zane down a long, narrow road that seemed to stretch into nowhere.
Finally, a pale yellow wall materialized out of the gloom ahead of them.
