Ep 22 - You Should Feel Honored

[SFX: ROARING FIRE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

The Weapons Forge roared to life, its ancient surface blooming with blood-red glyphs that cast Evil Jace's face in demonic light. Zane pulled against his restraints, the metal cutting into his wrists as panic surged through him. This wasn't how his story was supposed to end—trapped in a madman's cave, about to be melted down into a sword.
But the massive, uncrossable chasm between his own power and what Evil Jace could do left him with no illusions. His growth had been too slow, his breakthrough too late. Just when he'd finally felt that spark of real power growing inside him, fate had decided he was worth more as material than as a person.
All hope vanished like morning mist. Zane slumped against his restraints, reduced to nothing more than a cornered animal awaiting slaughter at Evil Jace's whim.
[SFX: METAL HAMMERING]
While he wallowed in despair, his captor bustled about the cave with unsettling enthusiasm. The rhythmic clang of metal echoed from a distant corner as Evil Jace hammered at some unseen project.
[SFX: HEAVY OBJECT DRAGGING]
Minutes later, the madman disappeared into the shadowy depths of the Weapons Forge, dragging a heavy object behind him, its weight scraping against the stone floor.
From his prison, Zane strained his eyes but could discern nothing beyond the bars that confined him. The darkness seemed to swallow everything beyond his immediate surroundings, leaving him isolated with his mounting dread.
Evil Jace eventually emerged from the shadows, his face alight with anticipation. He settled before the forge in a peculiar cross-legged posture that suggested some ancient ritual.
[SFX: SINGLE LOUD CLAP]
His weathered hands came together in a single, deliberate clap—the sound cracked through the cavern like a whip against stone.
[SFX: MAGICAL ACTIVATION]
[SFX: DARK MAGIC]
At that precise moment, the dormant face of the Weapons Forge awakened with terrifying brilliance, transforming the once-dark chamber into a theater of horrors.
Intricate, elegant Glyphs bloomed across its surface, one after another, bathing the entire cave in a dazzling red glow.
[SFX: AGONIZED SCREAMS]
[SFX: DRIPPING BLOOD]
The light fell on the creatures skewered to the walls, and they began to twist on their spikes. Their agonized screams echoed off the stone as their blood trickled down, siphoned directly into the channels of the Weapons Forge. The blood filled the carvings, making the crimson light burn even brighter, even more mesmerizing.
Once the forge was roaring, Evil Jace retrieved a sword. It was black and white, and Zane recognized it instantly: the Hellfire Blade, a legendary top-grade weapon.
Evil Jace held it for a moment, his gaze softening with the kind of pride and tenderness a father might show his son. He stroked the flat of the steel like a lover's skin, his whole posture relaxing.
The sight of such affection on that monstrous face sent a chill through Zane. His heart plummeted.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSE]
This guy wasn't just evil; he was a complete madman, and something terrible was about to happen.
After meticulously polishing the blade, Evil Jace finally turned his head, fixing Zane with an unnerving, twisted smile.
[SFX: SHACKLES RATTLING]
"You should feel honored," he said as he walked over to the cage and pulled Zane out. "You get to become part of my Hellfire Blade."
Arguing with a lunatic was pointless. Zane just shook his head, a cold dread settling in his gut as he followed his insane captor.
Against someone this powerful, what could he even do? He was out of luck and out of time. Zane let out a heavy sigh, figuring he'd soon be nothing but ash. A damn shame he'd never get his revenge. He wasn't even strong enough to try.
A wave of helplessness washed over him, and he silently cursed whatever gods had left him so damn weak. But as Evil Jace started pulling him, a spark of defiance flared up.
He wouldn't make it easy. He went limp, forcing the bastard to drag his dead weight every single step of the way.
[AMBIENT: DARK FORGE]
[SFX: DISTANT EMBERS CRACKLING]
They stopped right at the mouth of the Weapons Forge, and Evil Jace shoved him inside. The interior was pitch-black, save for a few embers glowing faintly in the hearth and the silhouette of a stone table in the center.
[SFX: MAGICAL POWDER BURST]
Evil Jace followed him in, placed the Hellfire Blade on the table, and shot Zane a ruthless smile before turning to leave. But just as he reached the entrance, he paused, as if a thought had just occurred to him. He turned back. With a flick of his wrist, a cloud of yellow powder burst from his fingers, blanketing Zane.
The dust had a strangely sweet fragrance, and Zane instinctively inhaled a small amount.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DREAD]
A wave of profound weakness immediately washed through him. Bone-Shatter Powder. He recognized the cursed drug instantly. It would paralyze his tendons, muscles, and bones within moments, rendering him completely immobile.
His last-ditch plan—grab the Hellfire Blade, smash the forge, take Evil Jace down with him—vanished. "Damn it all to hell!" he thought, feeling the strength drain from his limbs. All of it, gone, because of that damn powder.
His body felt like it was turning to jelly. Worse, he could feel his Life Energy sputtering out, leaving him so weak he couldn't even lift a finger.
"Is this it? Is this how it all ends?" The hunter had become the prey. He had no one to blame but himself for being too weak, for letting himself get cornered without a real fight.
[SFX: ERUPTION]
He gritted his teeth, fury and despair warring within him. And in that moment, a pillar of flame erupted from the heart of the Weapons Forge.
[AMBIENT: ROARING FIRE]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC]
The bright, yellow flames bloomed like a massive Golden Sunpetal—this had to be Evil Jace's fire. It erupted in an instant, roaring to fill the entire chamber.
Searing heat washed over Zane's face as the flames completely engulfed him. With a sharp, crackling sizzle, his clothes disintegrated into nothing but ash, leaving him exposed to the inferno. Golden fire danced across his skin while intense heat drew glistening beads of sweat from his pores, each droplet evaporating almost instantly.
Zane squeezed his eyes shut and gritted his teeth, every muscle in his body tensing as he braced for an agony beyond imagination. His mind raced with terrifying visions of his flesh melting away, of becoming nothing more than another component in Evil Jace's twisted creation.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
But as seconds stretched into what felt like eternity, the expected torment never arrived.
"What the hell?" he thought, confusion replacing his terror.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
[SFX: GENTLE FLAMES]
There was no agony, no burning sensation consuming his flesh. Instead, he felt only a gentle warmth enveloping him, reminiscent of standing in sunlight on a crisp autumn morning. The golden flames caressed rather than consumed, bringing comfort rather than destruction. It wasn't even uncomfortable—quite the opposite. The warmth seemed to seep into his very bones, like an embrace from a long-lost friend.
