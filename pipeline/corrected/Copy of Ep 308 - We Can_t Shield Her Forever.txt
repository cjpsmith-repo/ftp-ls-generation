Ep 308 - We Can’t Shield Her Forever
Word count: 1089
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
All thoughts of the All Peaks Tournament vanished from Zane’s mind as rage flared inside of him, threatening to bubble over and cause serious damage.
How could he not be furious? He'd built his entire life around two things: his cultivation path and his beloved little sister, Elara.
He had sacrificed everything to get this far, only to be told it was all for nothing. The dream that had been within his grasp was now sand slipping through his fingers. And he was supposed to just let that go?
[SFX: TENSE SILENCE]
Sir Kenneth met Zane's glare, completely unfazed. A calm smile touched his lips. "There’s no need for all this anger. Elara was sent to Hell Mountain for a reason. And as her brother, you have a right to know why."
"Then spit it out," Zane growled, his eyes burning with hate.
He knew Kenneth could kill him with a single move, but he didn’t care.
Fueled by a raw mix of rage and determination, he stood his ground without a flicker of fear.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
"Elara is, by far, the most promising student the Skyreach Sect has ever seen," Kenneth said slowly. "As you know, few women join the Sect, leaving Maiden’s Peak nearly empty. Of those who do, only a handful show any real promise. Even our best, like Jill, barely made a showing in the tournament." He paused, letting the weight of his words sink in. "But Elara... Elara is different."
"She possesses a talent the others could only dream of. I have very high hopes for her—in fact, I see more potential in her than I do in you, Zane. Do you even know our purpose here? Why we establish the Skyreach Sect in the Eastern Territories?"
"We exist to gather all the talent this region has to offer. Once we find someone with true potential, we send them to Cloud Hall for advanced training. The world of warriors is a brutal one, Zane. Only the strongest survive. If you can reach that level, you might just be able to free your sister. I believe you can do it, someday. But not now."
"Right now, you’re too weak. You haven’t even begun to scratch the surface of your potential. You’re too weak to achieve your dreams and far too weak to protect the people you love. You might have talent, but this world is vast, and it's overflowing with talent. There are countless gifted people out there, but only a fraction of them ever become great. Not many can survive the trials and come out stronger on the other side."
[SFX: HEARTBEAT INTENSIFIES]
Each time Kenneth said "too weak," the words landed like a physical blow, and a cold dread washed over Zane.
It was clear Elara was in more trouble than he'd ever imagined—trouble so deep that even someone as powerful as Kenneth couldn’t simply let her go.
And the conditions Kenneth had laid out felt impossible.
Enter the Soul Realm, and then push beyond it? Become a warrior of the Inner-Core Realm?
Those levels of power seemed like a distant myth. But Zane knew Sir Kenneth wasn’t mocking him. He was showing him the sheer scale of the forces they were up against—and how outmatched he would be if he tried to free his sister now. Compared to warriors from the Inner-Core Realm, Zane was no more than an ant. A real fight would be over before it even began.
"Sir, I still don’t understand," Zane said, his voice tight. "If my sister is that important to Cloud Hall, why was she sent to Hell Mountain? Is it because of her potential?"
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
Kenneth nodded, then, after a moment, shook his head with a heavy sigh.
"It’s more than that. Beyond her skill in martial arts, she has a unique gift. Her body is perfectly designed to store energy. She has what we call a Violet-Core Physique."
"Violet-Core Physique?" Zane echoed, his brow furrowing. He’d never heard the term before.
"You heard right," Kenneth confirmed. "It’s an incredibly rare talent, passed down through bloodlines and found only in women. When a woman with a Violet-Core Physique reaches the Inner-Core Realm, her own core manifests as a Violet-Core Elixir. It’s a completely different class of power, far beyond what ordinary warriors can achieve. A woman with that power stands leagues above her peers."
"I see. So my sister is... exceptional," Zane said, the pieces starting to click into place. "Are you saying someone wants to take that talent from her? Can a Violet-Core Physique be stolen?"
He felt he was finally getting close to the truth.
"A gift like that is one in a trillion. And since it’s a gift only women can possess..." Kenneth's expression turned grim. "There is one thing that can be taken from them: their purity. A powerful lord in The Heartlands is trying to create something called a Soul Harvesting Ritual. To complete it, he needs to find three women who not only have the Violet-Core Physique, but who have also reached the Inner-Core Realm."
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
Purity. Lust.
The words hit Zane like a punch to the gut, and his face darkened with a fresh wave of fury. He knew that for every righteous path to power, there were countless evil ones.
"We can’t shield her forever," Kenneth continued. "That lord already knows about Elara's talent. He's visited the Sect several times, and we’ve had to lie, telling him she’s in deep seclusion. The second he spots her outside of Hell Mountain, he’ll know it was all a ruse. All of us—and especially Elara—would be in grave danger."
"I understand. Thank you for telling me, Sir. I’ll go see her now," Zane said, his voice surprisingly steady.
The storm of anger and fear still raged inside him, but he forced it down. He knew freaking out wouldn’t change a thing. Hearing the truth about Elara’s situation, Zane willed himself into a cold calm. What he needed now wasn’t an explosion, but a solution.
"Master Elwood," Kenneth ordered, "take Zane to Hell Mountain."
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
At his master’s command, Master Elwood summoned his transport. An emerald leaf, easily five feet across, shimmered into existence at his feet.
"Get on, Zane," Master Elwood urged. "I’ll take you."
[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]
[AMBIENT: HIGH ALTITUDE WIND]
After bowing his head to Kenneth in a silent show of respect, Zane stepped onto the leaf. The moment he was steady, it lifted from the ground and shot into the sky.
The thought of seeing his sister made Zane’s heart hammer against his ribs. He could barely contain himself.
