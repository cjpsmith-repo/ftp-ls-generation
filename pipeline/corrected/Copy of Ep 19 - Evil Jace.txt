In Castermere County, a Master Weaponsmith commanded fame and fortune unmatched by other professions. Noble houses typically unfurled lavish red carpets for these artisans, each family desperate to commission legendary weapons that might elevate their status above rivals.
Evil Jace, however, inspired a drastically different reaction. Rather than welcoming him with open arms, the powerful families of the region actively plotted his demise, their hatred for him surpassing even their desire for superior weaponry.
Their animosity was well-founded. His methods transcended mere cruelty, venturing into territories of horror that made even hardened warriors shudder.

[SFX: AGONIZED SCREAMS]
The notorious craftsman quenched his freshly-forged blades in the blood of living victims, their screams adding a macabre soundtrack to his creative process.
[SFX: ROARING FORGE FIRE]
[SFX: HUMAN SCREAMS]
At times, when particularly inspired, he simply hurled struggling captives directly into his roaring forge—human sacrifices consumed by flames in service to whatever dark rituals enhanced his creations.
[SFX: SWORN BEING DRAWN]
[SFX: SCARED WHISPERS]
Survivors spoke of weapons that seemed to whisper with the voices of the dead when drawn from their scabbards.
Numerous houses had assembled hunting parties to eliminate this monster, yet each attempt ended in failure.
Jace proved remarkably elusive, slipping through elaborate traps with uncanny intuition, while demonstrating power that belied his unassuming appearance. Those who confronted him directly rarely returned to share their experiences.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
Thus, when Evil Jace himself crashed into the tense standoff between Zane and House Lowell, snatching the boy with casual efficiency, every member of the once-proud family stood paralyzed with shock.
Their expressions shifted from murderous rage toward Zane to wide-eyed terror at this unexpected development, the color draining from even the most battle-hardened faces.
"Let him go, Jace!" Bryson roared. "That boy is a member of House Lowell!" He was on high alert. He might have been just half a step into the Nature Level, but he knew Evil Jace was in another league entirely.
Jace just snickered. "If he's one of yours, why are all your swords pointed at him?" he asked. "Looks to me like you want him dead. I can handle that for you. Problem solved, right?"
"Damn you, Jace! He's a traitor to my house, which means he's mine to deal with!" Bryson threatened. "Anyone who crosses House Lowell lives to regret it. I suggest you back off!" A swarm of Violet Blades materialized in the air around Jace, humming with energy.

"If Milo Jennings were here, maybe I'd think twice. But you?" Jace threw his head back and laughed. "Ha! You and the rest of House Lowell aren't worth the dirt on my boots!"
[SFX: CRACK OF BLADES]
"You bastard!" Bryson's face went dark with rage. "I'll kill you myself!" With a sharp crack, the Violet Blades shot forward, streaking toward Jace.
"That's rich!" Jace cackled. "You can try!"

[AMBIENT: PANICKED CROWD]

Terror erupted among the ranks as they scattered in panic, their training forgotten in the face of genuine danger.

Several unfortunates hesitated a crucial moment too long. The forge crashed into them with merciless force, leaving bodies crumpled and bleeding on the cold ground, their labored breaths hanging by the thinnest of threads.
Their agonized cries pierced the morning air, a stark testament to how quickly fortunes could change.
Bryson pursued with determined fury, his face contorted with rage and humiliation.
[SFX: BLADES SLASHING]
His Violet Blades—weapons that had intimidated countless enemies—slashed furiously at the rolling fortress.
[SFX: METAL CLINKING]
Each strike produced nothing but a futile clink against the impenetrable armored shell, the sound mocking his inadequacy.
Frustration burned in his eyes as the massive forge continued its devastating path through the yard, leaving more of his prized students broken in its wake. Their moans of pain seemed to personally indict his failure to protect them.

[AMBIENT: FADING MECHANICAL SOUNDS]
Eventually, the Bronze Forge veered sharply to the left, engines roaring with defiant power as it rumbled beyond the estate's boundaries and vanished from sight, taking Zane with it into an uncertain fate.
Bryson clenched his teeth, watching it disappear. He started to give chase, but Owen grabbed his arm. "Brother, stop!" Owen warned. "Jace is too dangerous. Don't be a fool."
"And the traitor, Zane?" Bryson asked, his voice flat.
Owen sneered. "That lunatic? Anyone Jace takes ends up as fuel for his forge. Trust me, Zane isn't getting out of there alive."
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD AFTERMATH]
[SFX: INJURED PEOPLE MOANING]
Bryson looked at the carnage around them, a strange feeling of dread settling in his gut. He glanced at Owen, then snapped, "Get the injured treated! Fetch my Medical Vial!"
The yard was filled with the cries of the injured—dozens of Lowell disciples with broken bones and mangled limbs.
The uninjured onlookers watched the scene, completely demoralized. The endless fighting between Zane and his uncles, Bryson and Owen, was only accelerating House Lowell's downfall.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REMINISCING]
Back when Zane's father, Milo, was alive, scum like Evil Jace would never have dared to pull a stunt like this.
Of course, that wasn't the only reason for their decline. House Lowell was once the most powerful family in all of Castermere County, but they had fallen from grace, not even ranking in the top three anymore.
In the old days, members of House Lowell could walk the streets with their heads held high, and no one would dare look at them wrong.
Now, they commanded no respect, not in town and not even on their own property.
Sometimes, they even had to bribe their own servants just to get anything done. None of the younger generation truly believed House Lowell could ever recover.

[MUSIC: GRIM DETERMINATION]
Crouched inside the metal furnace, Zane kept perfectly still.
As the former heir of the House, Zane knew all about Evil Jace.
His father had told him more than once that if he ever got his hands on the man, he would cut him down with his Azurite Blade.
Zane had heard all the horror stories—the rumors that Jace used human sacrifices to forge his weapons.
After everything else he'd been through, getting captured by Evil Jace felt like the cruelest twist of fate imaginable.
