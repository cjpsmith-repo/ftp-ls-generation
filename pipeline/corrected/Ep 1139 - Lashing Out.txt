Ep 1139 - Lashing Out

Word count: 1066

[MUSIC: MAGICAL BATTLE]
They were all falling at slightly different speeds.
After a few moments of freefall, they figured out that while they couldn’t fly, they could change their body position to control their descent. Tucking into a head-first dive made you go faster, while spreading out your arms and legs to let your clothes catch the air like a parachute slowed you down. No one had a clue how to get out of this mess, so all they could do was fall as slowly as possible and try to stay close together.
Just as they were getting the hang of it, they plunged straight into a thicket of dense vines. The things were alive, humming with a strange, sentient energy.
[SFX: VINES RUSTLING AND WHIPPING]
As Zane and the others were swarmed, the vines morphed into long whips that cracked through the air, lashing out at them.
[SFX: RAPID WHIP CRACKS LIKE FIRECRACKERS]
The sharp snap echoed around them, sounding like a string of firecrackers going off.
Everyone but Zane immediately threw up protective shields of Vitality. Most of the Ogres did the same, summoning their own Forceful Energy. But the power coiled in those vines was far beyond anything they had imagined.
[SFX: SHIELD SHATTERING]
[SFX: BODY IMPACT AGAINST STONE]
When a whip struck, it tore a warrior’s shield to shreds. He was slammed violently against the chasm wall before continuing his downward spiral, his body limp. Since no one could fly or change direction, dodging the vines was nearly impossible. They held some kind of strange, intense power that blew right through any defensive treasure or cultivation method anyone tried. The whips struck their bodies directly, and the pain was so sharp it was nearly fatal, a searing agony that felt like it was attacking both body and soul.
[SFX: AGONIZED SCREAMS ECHOING]
Agonized screams echoed through the abyss as the warriors writhed, their faces twisted in pain.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSTAINED STRINGS]
Freya’s face had gone white. Each lash of the vines sent her body spinning — the pain wasn’t just physical, it drove straight through to the soul, a deep, burning agony that was hard to even think through. But she made no sound. Not a groan, not a cry. She clenched her teeth and endured it in silence.
She’d already been a burden once, back at the palace entrance, when the Cannibal Moths had forced Zane to guide her through the dark. She wasn’t going to be dead weight again. She didn’t want him to look down on her. She didn’t even want him to notice.
[SFX: VINES WHIPPING]
Zane was the only one who remained calm as the vines whipped at him. They were powerful, but they couldn’t even scratch his body, which was as tough as a Divine Weapon. The force of the blows just sent him spinning, and any pain he felt was instantly washed away by a warm current flowing through him.
But when he turned, he saw Freya get hit.
[SFX: IMPACT WHOOSH]
A vine lashed out, sending her body spinning uncontrollably through the air. He spread his arms and legs wide to slow his fall, closed the distance between them, and pulled her into his arms.
Another vine cracked toward them. Zane shifted, putting his back between Freya and the whip. It struck him instead.
He didn’t flinch. “My skin is tough enough,” he said simply. “A few more hits won’t matter.”
Freya felt the impact transmit through his body rather than hers. She understood what he was doing. She stopped struggling and went still.
She didn’t speak. She was afraid that if she opened her mouth, she might cry — not from pain, but from something harder to name.
[MUSIC: QUIET EMOTIONAL UNDERSCORE]
She had ascended to the Upper World full of hope, and the Upper World had answered by being nothing like she imagined. Her mood had been low for a long time. But here, in the dark, falling through an abyss while vines tried to tear them apart, something shifted. 
She stayed still and let him shield her.
[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION UNDERSCORE]
Watching her get hit like that solidified something in Zane. He was confident in his own talent. Just as Lavender had pointed out, compared to the other geniuses of the Upper World, Zane didn’t lack talent, just resources. He decided then and there to gather as many as he could along the way. That was the only way for him to actually grow stronger up there.
The vine section didn’t end quickly. Two hours passed — two full hours of falling through the lashing dark, every warrior in the abyss enduring the same punishment. The screams never fully stopped. By the time the vines finally thinned and then disappeared entirely, leaving open air below them again, every face in the group looked hollowed out.
[MUSIC: SOMBER RELIEF]
From somewhere below Zane, he heard an Ogre warrior’s voice, raw with exhaustion and fury. “What the hell kind of place is this?”
Another voice answered — steadier, as if reciting something memorized. “The Saint Lord said the Endless Abyss has no bottom. Every time you reach the end of a trial and fall through, you reincarnate into the next level. Each reincarnation gives you part of the Supreme Lord’s inheritance. That’s the design.”
A long silence followed that.
He was pulled from his thoughts by a timid voice.
“Zane? Zane? Could you, um, let me go?”
Hearing her, Zane realized they had fallen past the section with the vines. There was no reason to hold her anymore. He loosened his grip, gently letting Freya go.
With an embarrassed smile, he said, “Sorry.”
Freya’s face turned a little red. Staring at his awkward expression, she found him surprisingly interesting. She made up her mind right then: no matter how tough it got in the abyss of the Underground Palace, she was sticking close to Zane.
[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]
The other warriors did their best to steady their fall, trying to maintain a consistent speed. They’d already been falling for what felt like an impossible distance since entering the palace, with no idea when it would end. After that brutal encounter with the vines, everyone was on high alert.
It was then that Zane asked Lavender, “Any idea what happens if we keep going down?”
“Hmm?” her voice echoed in his mind. “Let me think. It’s been a hundred thousand years since I was last here. I need a moment.”
After a pause, she continued,
[MUSIC: OMINOUS FOREBODING]
“If I recall correctly, the second trial is The Blade Mountain Trap.”
“The Blade Mountain Trap?”
Zane didn’t know what that meant, but just hearing the name sent a chill down his spine.
