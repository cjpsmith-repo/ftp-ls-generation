Ep 794 - Being Watched

Word count: 1024

[AMBIENT: SCHOOL COURTYARD]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Randy gave Zane an easy grin, and Zane had the immediate sinking feeling that things were about to get a lot more complicated.

 Considering Randy had challenged him to a fight just a few days ago, it was more than a little weird to see him now, acting like they were long-lost best friends.

Zane managed a tight, fake smile. “I've been fine, thanks,” he said, the words coming out clipped.

[SFX: PAPER RUSTLE]

 Randy slung an arm around Zane’s shoulders like they were old pals, plucking the golden invitation from his hand. He gave it a quick once-over before turning to the Crown Prince. “Sorry, but Zane's already promised to come to my place today,” he announced. “Your invitation is a little late.”

When the hell did I agree to that? Zane thought. One minute, he was staring at the Crown Prince’s invitation, wondering how to get out of it, and the next, Randy had materialized out of thin air, acting all chummy.

 Still, Zane couldn't help but feel a flicker of gratitude. Randy might be an arrogant hothead, but there was a certain integrity to him. The Crown Prince, on the other hand, was too slick by half, and Zane didn't trust him one bit.

Randy had just handed him the perfect excuse, and Zane wasn’t about to waste it. He nodded, playing along. “That’s right, Your Highness. Lord Randy already invited me over.” As he spoke, he gave Randy’s hand a firm pat—a clear signal to get his arm off his shoulder.

The arm didn’t budge. If anything, Randy’s grin only widened.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

 This whole dynamic was a political minefield. Back when Randy’s power level was still low, the Crown Prince wouldn't have given him the time of day. But Randy had grown stronger, climbing the social ladder until he could finally stand as an equal to his older brother. 

Still, there was a difference. The Crown Prince was over a decade older and the undisputed heir to the throne, with a vast network of supporters. Randy, despite earning the title of Lord for his strength, had a much smaller circle.

The Crown Prince had heard rumors of the warrior who’d bested Randy at the Crystal Martial House, and his interest was piqued. An exceptional fighter like that needed to be on his side. He’d traveled all the way from the capital for the sole purpose of recruiting Zane, and now his own brother was ruining everything.

Of course, he saw right through their little act. He was the Crown Prince; he knew a political maneuver when he saw one. He also knew when to press his advantage and when to fold. Forcing the issue here would be a mistake. 

So, he took the high road, letting out a hearty laugh. “What a shame! It seems I was a step too slow.”

“You certainly showed up fast. I’m impressed,” Randy shot back.

A flicker of anger crossed the Crown Prince’s eyes, but his smile never wavered. 

[SFX: WHEELCHAIR ROLLING]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]

 He simply spun his wheelchair around and departed, his entourage of masters trailing behind him.

That was his brother’s favorite weapon: a quiet, suffocating cruelty. Randy’s growing power was a direct threat to the Crown Prince’s claim to the throne, and when it came to succession, family ties were meaningless. 

Since their father forbade any direct action, the Crown Prince had found other ways to make Randy’s life a living hell. He’d trapped his younger brother in a beautiful, gilded prison, surrounding him with people he couldn't trust, ensuring he was utterly alone.

But the isolation went deeper. Randy needed to build a team to enter the secret lands of the Sacred Feather Sea, and he couldn't do it alone. The Crown Prince had, of course, offered his help, but Randy knew better than to accept a knife handed to him by his own enemy. The trouble was, there was no one else to ask.

Every talented young master was already allied with the Crown Prince. The only warriors left were in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, and most of them weren’t powerful enough. Randy had managed to scrape together a few fighters who might make it to the Second Tier, but his small force was a joke compared to his brother's.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

 Which brought him to Zane. When they'd first met, Randy's instinct was to fight. But the master of the Crystal Martial House had seen the bigger picture, advising him that this powerful young man could be the ally he so desperately needed.

As the Crown Prince's entourage disappeared around the corner, Randy dropped his voice. "My brother does things flawlessly. He'll have someone watching you by nightfall. If you don't come now, he'll be back."

Zane looked at him for a moment, then nodded. He had no particular desire to spend the afternoon at Randy's residence, but between the two brothers, the choice was obvious. Following a schemer kept you permanently on edge. Randy was aggravating, but at least you knew where you stood.

Randy's residence was in the south of the city — large, immaculately kept, the kind of place built to impress. As they approached the gate, four Life-and-Death Realm guards came rushing out, visibly agitated.

"Your Majesty, where did you go? We've been searching—"

Randy looked at them with cold eyes. "Do I need to report my whereabouts to you now?"

The guard who'd spoken lowered his head. "No, Your Majesty."

"Good. Guard the door."

Their faces darkened, but they obeyed. Randy watched them go, then glanced at Zane with a thin smile. "Funny, isn't it?"

Zane caught the edge in it. "Those men—"

"Every person in this residence," Randy said, his voice dropping, "from the guards to the servants to the last maidservant in the kitchen — my brother put them here."

[SFX: DOOR CLOSING]

[AMBIENT: QUIET INTERIOR]

 Once inside the main hall, Francis waited by the door as a servant brought them both a cup of cider. The moment the drinks were served, Randy dismissed him with a wave.

[MUSIC: TENSE PIANO]

 He looked straight at Zane, his gaze sharp and direct. “Why do you want to enter the Sacred Feather Sea?” he asked, his voice low. “I think we can help each other.”
