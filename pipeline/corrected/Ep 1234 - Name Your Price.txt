Ep 1234 - Name Your Price

Word count: 1053

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL TENSION]
[SFX: MYSTICAL HUMMING RESONANCE]
Sloane couldn’t believe what she was seeing. 
She’d never expected to see a Sacred Mount Lodestone in her entire life, let alone here.
She wasn’t the only one, either. 
[AMBIENT: CROWD GASPS AND MURMURS]
All around the battlefield, the other fighters were gaping at it.
“Where the hell did that kid drag a mountain from?”
“He almost crushed Sloane! That thing just shattered her Earth Element Power!”
“I could lift a regular mountain, but that thing… it’s insanely heavy.”
“Look at it—glowing with all those colors, radiating the aura of the Five Elements. It has to be a Sacred Mount Lodestone.”
None of them had seen it coming. Nobody expected Zane to pull out a move that crazy just to break Sloane’s attack.
Sloane herself hovered in the air, her gaze locked on the colossal mountain below. The shock was still there, but her expression hardened into something sharp and focused.
She knew the sight of it better than almost anyone alive. House Kincaid had reserved a Sacred Mount Lodestone of her own — smaller than the one Zane had just summoned — and she’d spent long stretches cultivating inside it before ever setting foot in the Illusion Battlefield. 
That was part of why the shock cut so deep: she wasn’t looking at a stranger’s trophy. She was looking at something she understood intimately, wielded by someone who apparently understood it even better.
“That’s the Sacred Mount Lodestone,” she said, her voice ringing with certainty. “You refined it yourself.”
Zane just stared back, his face unreadable. He’d had the Lodestone for a while but almost never used it in a fight. He had no idea why she was so worked up about it.
“Yeah, that’s the one,” he said with a shrug. “What’s the big deal?” He’d found the instructions for refining it in an old text, the *Myriad Manifestations of Nature*.
To him, it was just a powerful tool. He didn’t realize that what he’d done was supposed to be impossible. 
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]
The five elements inside a Sacred Mount Lodestone are so perfectly intertwined that they can’t be refined by any normal Law Power. Some warriors in the Upper World had tried using the Earth Law to refine regular mountains, but those were far too fragile to be useful in a real battle. 
A Soul Sea Realm fighter could shatter the ground with a single stomp, let alone break apart a normal mountain. Only something as indestructible as a Sacred Mount Lodestone could ever be used as a true weapon, but almost no one in history had ever managed to refine one.
Even at her current strength, Sloane could only manage to shatter a small piece of a Sacred Mount Lodestone with a single full-force palm strike — and that alone was considered nearly impossible for other Soul Sea Realm fighters. Even Zane, for all that he’d refined the thing outright, found it difficult to break a piece off it himself.
The secret, as Zane had discovered, was the Blood Refinement Technique—a lost art he’d stumbled upon in the archives of The Elysian Keep. He had no clue it was something people in the Upper World had been searching for.
But Sloane knew. Thanks to her Five-Element Finger Chain, her entire attunement was built around combining the Five Elements.
She understood that the Lodestone wasn’t just a rock; it was a marvel of the universe, a perfect fusion of the Original Laws of the Five Elements. That kind of fusion wasn’t just a technique—it was one of the fabled paths to achieving the Godly Way. In a universe with thousands of World Lords and countless fighters who had surpassed realms she could barely imagine, only a handful had ever succeeded on that path.
*And this kid did it?* she thought. *Does he really think that’s enough to beat me?*
Even after he’d shattered her attack with a legendary artifact, she still didn’t see him as a real threat. Sure, he was decent, but she was Sloane Kincaid. There was no way he could actually beat her. 
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
Still, it made her curious. *How can he be so confident?*
“What method did you use to refine the Sacred Mount Lodestone?” she asked, unable to keep the eagerness out of her voice despite herself. As a daughter of House Kincaid, the weight of her family’s expectations sat heavy on her shoulders — if she could learn to refine one herself, it would be an immeasurable boost to her cultivation of the Five Elements.
“Why should I tell you?” Zane said, the faint smile still on his face.
Sloane’s brow furrowed slightly at that. “If you’re willing to teach me the method, I’ll let you go.”
“Let me go?” Zane’s smile only widened. “You sure you’re actually a match for me?”
“Name your price,” she said, her tone shifting from combat to business. “My family, House Kincaid, will do our best to make it happen. And I’m not talking about some reward here in the Illusion Battlefield. I mean for real, out in The Heartlands.”
Her confidence was absolute. House Kincaid wasn’t just some powerful family; they were the third-largest house within Thoughtless Minds, one of the three great human powers. In her eyes, they could grant any wish a boy from the Soul Sea Realm could possibly dream of.
[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL RISING]
“My price?” Zane echoed. A slow smile spread across his face as he looked her over. “Not interested. A fight sounds way more fun.”
[SFX: SWORD MATERIALIZING]
As he spoke, his sword, Thundergust, materialized in the air before him. The smile on his face vanished, replaced by a hard, serious line.
He couldn’t care less about House Kincaid. Honestly, if she hadn’t been so arrogant, he might have just taught her the Blood Refinement Technique for free. The *Myriad Manifestations of Nature* was just a collection of his Master’s old essays, and the technique was a minor entry among thousands.
But he couldn’t stand her attitude.
His original plan had been simple: hang around the ogre city and ambush her to steal her Illusion Points. But now she was here, offering to “spare his life” in exchange for his secrets? No way in hell.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE TENSION]
Sloane’s eyes darkened at his open defiance, her voice dropping into a dangerous chill. “Looks like you need to be taught a lesson. Fine. I’ll make you understand the gap between us.”
