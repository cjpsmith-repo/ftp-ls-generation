Ep 32 - Two Crystal Cubes
Word count: 1157
[AMBIENT: FADING POWER]
The Dragon Scale flickered, its last emerald light fading to darkness. Zane's heart sank—until a sudden explosion of brilliant green light erupted from the scale, bathing his mindscape in radiance.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
Power surged through his veins like liquid lightning, and he barely contained a primal roar as the second scale ignited to life.
Five drops. Just five drops of Celestial Essence had unlocked this new threshold.
As it lit up, an incredible wave of strength washed through him. He clenched his fists, fighting back a roar as he reveled in the intoxicating feeling of power.
After a moment, Zane forced himself to calm down. Two activated dragon scales was a decent start, but he needed to get stronger and fast. If he could just light up a few more, he could crush Perrin without breaking a sweat.
The dilemma stared Zane in the face—he had already smelted every last one of his mysterious weapons. Despite his wealth of Crystal Cubes, he found himself back at the beginning, unable to progress. The market's exorbitant prices meant his fortune could purchase merely one or two Basic Enchanted Weapons, a negligible contribution to his growing power.
His thoughts drifted to the Shattered Dagger nestled in his Storage Ring. Though it appeared worthless at first glance, an unmistakable energy pulsed from it—surely indicating a high-tier artifact. The urge to melt it down for essence gnawed at him, yet wisdom prevailed. If the knife possessed the extraordinary qualities he suspected, destroying it would constitute an irreversible blunder.
Priorities crystallized in his mind. Above all else, Zane needed to secure his position within the Skyreach Sect. Everything else could wait.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR CROWD]
[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS THEME]
He navigated toward the sect grounds, following the serpentine path that eventually curved rightward, guiding him alongside other eager applicants into a vast, sunlit square. Zane's heart quickened with anticipation as he observed the scene before him.
[SFX: HOVERING SKYCRUISERS]
Overhead, several magnificent Skycruisers hovered with graceful authority, their polished decks lined with stern-faced students whose vigilant eyes swept across the gathering crowd, maintaining strict order throughout the Preliminary Exam.
The vessels cast shifting shadows across the square, reminding all applicants of the power and prestige that awaited the successful few.
As the final stragglers filtered into the square, a distinguished white-bearded elder stepped forward, his weathered face betraying years of cultivation experience. He cleared his throat and addressed the crowd in a resonant voice that carried effortlessly across the assembly.
"Most of you know the drill," he announced, his eyes twinkling with a hint of amusement, "but let's go over it one more time. From this square, you will run straight ahead. It doesn't matter if you're at the Bone, Organ, or Marrow Refining Level. Simply keep running until the Crystal Talisman on your chest changes color. Once it does, you've passed the Preliminary Exam and are qualified for the main Entrance Exam."
Zane fingered the talisman hanging against his chest, its cool surface a constant reminder of what was at stake. The rules seemed straightforward enough—no refining level restrictions, no speed limit. Just run until the talisman changed color. Yet something in the elder's tone suggested hidden complexities.
With furrowed brow, Zane mentally rehearsed the instructions while studying the path stretching from the square. It appeared deceptively ordinary—paved with smooth limestone slabs and bordered by meticulously trimmed grass, indistinguishable from any common road in the Empire. The path twisted and turned capriciously, eventually snaking out of sight beyond a distant hill. Despite its innocent appearance, Zane couldn't shake the feeling that this seemingly simple challenge concealed unexpected trials.
The applicants waiting for the exam seemed pretty relaxed, even cocky. Their training had made them strong, so a simple run sounded like a piece of cake. Most of them were sure they'd breeze right through.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
The white-bearded elder gave a signal, and the exam began.
[SFX: STARTING WHISTLE]
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: PEOPLE STUMBLING AND FALLING]
The first wave of candidates shot forward. Almost immediately, chaos erupted. The runners at the front suddenly stumbled and pitched forward, eating dirt as if they'd run into an invisible wall. Only a few reacted fast enough to catch their balance, staggering for a moment before pushing onward.
That little display tipped everyone else off—there was something weird about this road. The next groups were far more careful, their caution slowing them to a crawl. They looked like they were trying to run while carrying a thousand pounds on their backs.
[SFX: HEAVY PRESSURE WHOOSH]
After a few more groups took off, it was Zane's turn. He walked to the edge of the square and took a slow, deliberate step onto the path. The effect was immediate—an invisible pressure slammed down on him. When he tried to lift his other foot, his legs felt like they were made of lead.
[AMBIENT: EXTREME ATMOSPHERIC PRESSURE]
It was a bizarre sensation. The moment he stepped onto the road, the pressure was everywhere, weighing down on every inch of his skin.
Just then, a guy next to him, Ethan Grant, let out a grunt and nudged him, showing off a bracelet on his wrist.
"Last time, I barely managed half a mile," Ethan wheezed, his face flushed with exertion. "Now, thanks to this little marvel, I can actually move forward. It neutralizes half the pressure weighing us down."
"A bracelet?" Zane examined the accessory with newfound interest. It appeared unremarkable—merely a simple golden bracer encircling Ethan's wrist, its surface catching occasional glints of sunlight. Who would have imagined such an ordinary-looking object could counteract this oppressive invisible force?
"Is that some kind of mysterious weapon?" Zane inquired, his eyes widening with curiosity as his mind raced through possibilities.
Ethan's face brightened with pride as he nodded vigorously. "Precisely! A mysterious weapon indeed." He lowered his voice conspiratorially. "Cost me two Crystal Cubes—practically everything I've saved over the past year."
"Two Crystal Cubes? That's all?" Zane couldn't mask his astonishment, a surge of excitement coursing through him. The price seemed absurdly low for something so valuable. "Are mysterious weapons truly that affordable?"
If Ethan's claim held truth, the implications were staggering. Zane could acquire dozens—perhaps even hundreds—of such items. The wealth of Crystal Cubes nestled within his Storage Ring suddenly represented far more potential than he'd initially realized.
"No, no, you've misunderstood," Ethan clarified hastily, shaking his head as disappointment flickered across his features. "The "rental fee" is two Crystal Cubes. I must return the bracelet after completing the exam. Ownership of such items remains well beyond my means."
[MUSIC: RENEWED DETERMINATION]
Rental fee? These wealthy students had every advantage possible. Zane was more determined than ever to prove he was worth it, even if he didn't have the support.
[SFX: LABORED FOOTSTEPS]
The pressure bore down on Zane like an avalanche, each step forward demanding superhuman effort. Around him, students with their rented mysterious weapons pushed ahead, their advantages clear as day.
[SFX: MAGICAL PULSING]
But as Zane gritted his teeth and forced his leg forward, something stirred deep within—the Green Scale pulsed with emerald fire, hungry for challenge.
