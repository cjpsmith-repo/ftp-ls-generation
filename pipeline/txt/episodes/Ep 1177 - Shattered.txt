Ep 1177 - Shattered

Word count: 1079

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: DEEP SPACE VOID]
The two Demon Night warriors hunting them were a man and a woman: Maverick and Tanya.
They were the Queen of Punishment’s most skilled lieutenants, their strength easily rivaling that of Taryn, Maren, Cassian, and Paige. But their long service had earned them rewards the others could only dream of. Both of them wielded Divine-Class weapons.
[SFX: CRYSTAL RESONANCE HUM]
The crystal sword in Maverick’s hand was the Infinite Crystal Sword. The more power he poured into it, the more identical blades he could create, each one just as powerful as the original. On the Cosmic Spirit Index, it was ranked two thousand one hundred and twenty.
Tanya’s weapon was a machete named Slash. Its creator was an eccentric weaponsmith who had only ever forged a handful of weapons, each with a single-word name and a rank in the top three thousand on the Cosmic Spirit Index. And every single one was imbued with a Law of Causality.
[SFX: BLADE IGNITING WITH ENERGY]
When Tanya swung Slash, the blade erupted in a light so blinding it was impossible to look at directly, as if a star had been set into its surface. With a single wave, she unleashed her attack.
[SFX: ENERGY BLADE SWOOSH]
[SFX: SLICING ENERGY WAVE]
The light fanned out, a glowing scythe slicing across the vastness of space, ready to cut anything in its path clean in half. Slash’s Law of Causality was simple and deadly: it could cut anything. It was a power very similar to the Black Star Pickax, whose own law allowed it to penetrate any barrier.
Tanya had aimed her swing perfectly, intending to slice the Wheel of Reincarnation in two.
[SFX: MASSIVE METAL WHOOSH]
But just as the fan of light was about to connect, the massive Wheel shot upward, dodging the attack with impossible agility.
The truth was, Zane was the one controlling it. He knew he couldn’t dodge the dense storm of Maverick’s Crystal Swords, but he was confident he could evade the slow, spreading arc of light from Tanya’s blade. He wasn’t some idiot who only knew how to run.
Maverick couldn’t help but laugh. The Wheel of Reincarnation was a massive target, yet somehow, Tanya had missed. It was hilarious.
Tanya herself couldn’t believe it. The Wheel had looked so vulnerable. Her jaw tightened.
[SFX: BLADE SPINNING]
Anger flashed in her eyes. She wasn’t done. Slash began to move again — faster this time, spinning in her grip until it became a wheel of light. Dozens of arcs exploded outward from her as the center, vertical and horizontal and diagonal all at once, filling the space around her with overlapping blades. There was no blind spot. The arcs moved a hundred times faster than her first swing. Even Maverick had to fall back to avoid being caught in them.
[SFX: TEARING METAL]
The Wheel of Reincarnation had nowhere to go. The saber arcs tore through it from every angle — and this time, they didn’t miss. In an instant, the great Wheel was cut into several massive pieces. The fragments held their momentum and kept flying forward, but thin lines had opened across every surface. After a moment, those lines widened, and the pieces cracked apart into huge shapeless chunks, tumbling and rolling through the void.
[SFX: DEBRIS ROLLING IN SPACE]
Zane stood on one of the spinning fragments, feet planted, body rolling with it as it rotated slowly through space. His eyes stayed fixed on Maverick and Tanya. A faint smile crossed his face.
He reached into his robes and drew out the Keep token. In a heartbeat, it glowed, twisted, and transformed into the miniature Elysian Keep. Then he was gone.
[SFX: SOFT VANISHING POP]
Tanya stared at the spot where he’d been. “There’s another spatial treasure?”
She hadn’t expected it. Neither had Maverick. After destroying the enormous Wheel, they’d assumed the young man had nowhere left to hide.
The fact that this little Elysian Keep was giving them so much trouble was a humiliating stain on their reputation.
[MUSIC: CALM STRATEGIC]
[AMBIENT: COMMAND DECK HUM]
Inside the real Elysian Keep, Zane stood on the command deck, Saul just a step behind him. Before them, a crystalline wall served as a viewscreen, showing the scene outside.
The space they were actually flying through wasn’t part of the normal universe at all, but some strange dimension Zane didn’t recognize. The Elysian Keep their enemies were chasing was just a projection, an illusion cast by the real ship, which was safely hidden away.
“Looks like we can’t shake them,” Zane said, watching the two warriors on the screen.
Saul offered a faint smile. “Zane, don’t worry. Those two can teleport, so we can’t outrun them. But they’re just World Lords. Even if the keep stood perfectly still, they could never break its defenses.”
[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT]
Outside, the projection was moving at a speed neither Maverick nor Tanya had anticipated — several times faster than their full sprint. They fell back immediately. But they weren’t outrun. Maverick slashed space open and stepped through, crossing hundreds of miles in a single stride, reappearing directly in the projection’s path. Tanya did the same, cutting off the other side.
The projection didn’t slow. It spun ninety degrees in an instant and slipped past Tanya’s blade. Then it threaded through Maverick’s rain of crystal swords without touching a single one.
They tried again. Over and over, the same pattern, the same result. They had destroyed the enormous Wheel of Reincarnation without breaking a sweat. And now they couldn’t lay a finger on this small, darting shape.
The humiliation of it was extraordinary.
Zane nodded. He didn’t have a solution to this whole mess yet, but at least he and Lavender were safe. For now, all he could do was take it one step at a time and see how long the Queen of Punishment was willing to play this game.
The chase went on for two more days, pulling them closer and closer to a bright yellow star in the Middle Sky Region.
[MUSIC: RELENTLESS PURSUIT]
[SFX: ENERGY BLASTS HITTING SHIELDS]
The whole time, Maverick and Tanya threw everything they had at the ship, but they still couldn’t lay a single scratch on the little projection of the Elysian Keep.
[AMBIENT: VOID DISTORTION]
[SFX: BILLOWING SMOKE]
It billowed outward, and from the growing cloud, a giant hand shot out, reaching for them both.
