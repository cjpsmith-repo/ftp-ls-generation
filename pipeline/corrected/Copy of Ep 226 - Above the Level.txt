Ep 226 - Above the Level
Word count: 1226
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TRAINING GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL MEDITATION]
The levels on the Seven-Star Platform were designed to test the newcomers of the Skyreach Sect.
For someone with Zachary's current strength, withstanding a hundred shots of Arcane Aurora would mean training at level four. At level five, he could only take about eighty-six. Level six, maybe fifty-seven. Level seven, just forty. And on level eight, he could barely handle twenty-one.
Zachary's strength was at Level Eight of the Adept Realm.
As far as the steward could remember, not even masters at the peak of the Adept Realm could take a hundred shots of Arcane Aurora on level eight. According to his records, the best anyone had ever done was eighty-nine shots.
The steward was sure it was impossible for anyone who hadn't reached the Soul Realm. And masters at that level usually trained on level nine, or even ten.
"No, it can't be Zane," the steward muttered to himself. "It might be Zachary, but definitely not Zane." But that didn't make sense either. The steward knew Zachary's strength, and there was no way the kid had improved that much overnight. The last time he went all out, he only managed twenty shots. A hundred shots today? The whole thing felt like a dream.
After a moment, a thought struck him, and he smacked his forehead. The Seven-Star Platform wasn't far. Why not just go see for himself?
[SFX: HURRIED FOOTSTEPS]
So, the steward hurried over to the Seven-Star Platform.
The magic array concealed whatever was happening inside, but he could see the people on the platform itself.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY]
[SFX: MEDITATION HUMS]
When he arrived, he saw Zachary and Zane. Zane was sitting cross-legged, completely lost in thought. A storm of images flashed through his mind, each one breaking down the most basic swordsmanship into its finest details.
[SFX: SWORD SWING]
Upward Chop.
Intercept.
Circle Parry.
Sword Flick.
Sword Raise.
Basic swordsmanship wasn't flashy, but it was the purest form of the art. Refined and simplified by countless swordsmen, every move was direct and efficient, with no wasted motion.
But thousands of images flooded Zane's mind, explaining a single, simple chop in excruciating detail, from the moment the sword was drawn to the final cut.
Then, just as suddenly, the images began to collapse, disappearing faster and faster.
In the blink of an eye, only about ten images remained for the "chop."
\*'This basic swordsmanship...'\* Zane thought, staring at the vision. The "chop" looked the same, but the longer he watched, the more mysterious it felt. He knew it was a basic move, yet it felt like something... more.
Once simplified, it felt like the swordsmanship merged all the sword intent between heaven and earth into a single, perfect movement.
Zane looked at the other basic moves: sword flick, horizontal sweep, sword lift.
Each movement looked ordinary, but held a deep mystery that made it feel completely different from the common swordsmanship everyone knew.
"This isn't basic swordsmanship!" Zane's mind raced. "Or if it is, it's a form that's never existed before!" He was so stunned that he could only grasp the faintest idea of how it all worked. He knew this was a huge opportunity and he had to use every second to study it.
[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION SPACE]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
Meanwhile, the steward of the Seven-Star Platform, having just registered a new group of disciples, was rushing back. He had to get to Zane.
He'd figured Zane would be fine as long as no one else chose level eight. But now, a few inner disciples had just picked that exact level---and two of them were from noble houses. The steward had no choice but to let Zane know.
If he just started the array without warning, Zane---still sitting there on the ground---would get hit by Arcane Aurora flying from every direction. That was the last thing he wanted, so he had to alert him.
[SFX: ENERGY BURST]
After stepping onto the platform, the steward walked straight toward Zane. But when he was about fifteen feet away, several sharp waves of energy suddenly shot out in front of him, warning him to stay back.
[SFX: SHARP ENERGY WAVES]
Sharp bursts of power flew right at him.
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
Luckily, the steward had reached the Adept Realm and his reactions were sharp. He instinctively leaped back several steps, but the fabric on his chest was slashed to ribbons by the force.
"It's a protective sword intent," the steward murmured, a sour look on his face. He hadn't even done anything.
Zachary stared, just as stunned as the steward. He hadn't realized a protective sword intent was now guarding Zane. "Impossible! How did he create a protective sword intent so fast? He just started using a sword, didn't he?" Zachary blurted out.
"Just started using a sword? What are you talking about?" the steward asked, turning to Zachary. Seeing that Zane was protected, he didn't dare get any closer and risk breaking his trance. He just stood there, watching from a distance.
Zachary pointed casually at Zane and explained, "Zane's practically a beginner with a sword! He blocked all one hundred waves of Arcane Aurora using nothing but basic swordsmanship!"
"Basic swordsmanship?" the steward repeated, stunned. He couldn't believe what he was hearing.
"And that's not even the half of it," Zachary added. "He had an epiphany around wave sixty — just sat down, closed his eyes, and when he opened them his sword intent had completely changed. Then after he cleared the hundred, he went straight into another one."
The steward stared at him. "Two? In one session?"
Zachary nodded.
The steward was quiet for a moment. A single epiphany was the kind of thing a martial artist might experience once or twice in an entire lifetime. Two in an afternoon was not something he had a framework for.
Resisting a hundred waves at the eighth difficulty level was an unbelievable feat. Desmond, a black-robed inner disciple who regularly trained on the Seven-Star Platform, had a personal best of seventy-three waves---a record he was quite proud of. He was curious to know just how many waves Zane had blocked. It would show him the exact gap between his own ability and that of this outer disciple who had just acquired the slender sword intent.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
"Huh? A hundred waves," Zane replied casually, then walked right off the platform.
"Wh-what?" Desmond stammered, staring dumbfounded at Zane's retreating back. His mouth opened and closed a few times, but no words came out. The other disciples in black robes standing behind him were just as shocked.
Zachary couldn't help the small, pleased smile that touched his lips as he watched their reactions.
Standing beside them, the steward hid a smile of his own. At least he wasn't the only one whose mind had been completely blown. As the steward of the Seven-Star Platform, he knew Desmond practiced on level eight all the time and was always proud of his record of seventy-three waves. There was something deeply satisfying about the embarrassed look on his face now.
Noticing Desmond was still slack-jawed, the steward decided to twist the knife. "It's true," he added. "Zane resisted one hundred waves. As an Adept, he's the only one who's ever done it on the eighth level. And for the record, he's only at Level Two of the Adept Realm."
Desmond and the rest of the black-robed disciples stared with wide eyes, utterly floored.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT]
With that, the steward and Zachary turned and left the platform, fighting to hide their smirks.
