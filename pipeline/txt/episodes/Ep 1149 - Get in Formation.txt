Ep 1149 - Get in Formation

Word count: 1025

[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
[AMBIENT: DEEP CAVERNOUS WIND]
At first, the Endless Abyss had seemed almost disappointingly easy—nothing like the legendary danger zones of the Upper World. Sure, some ancient Supreme Lord had poured a ton of effort into carving it out, but the warriors plunging into its depths had treated it like any other trial: a place to score some loot and see what they were made of.
They couldn’t have been more wrong. Down here, one wrong move didn’t just get you hurt. It got you killed.
[SFX: BODY EXPLOSION]
[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]
A warrior had just been torn apart, his body exploding in a rain of gore that drifted down slower than the living. The bloody pieces formed a grotesque canopy, chasing them into the dark.
“Another one’s down!” a fighter screamed, his voice cracking with terror.
[SFX: SHARP WHISTLING PROJECTILE]
The words were barely out of his mouth when a sharp whistle shot up from below, growing louder with every passing second.
[SFX: ICE CRYSTAL WHOOSH]
Zane, who was falling near the left wall, watched as a man-sized, diamond-shaped crystal of ice rocketed past, narrowly missing him.
Then one shot straight at Freya.
[MUSIC: SUDDEN DANGER]
She threw herself sideways, bending almost double, trying to get clear — nothing to push off, nothing to grab. The other warriors watched, already writing her off.
A black streak cut across the abyss.
[SFX: BOWSTRING SNAP]
[SFX: CRYSTAL SHATTERING]
The crystal exploded in a burst of freezing air. Freya emerged coated in frost, still breathing. She turned. Zane was facing downward, longbow in hand, the bowstring still trembling.
Her expression shifted — relief, then something quieter. She hadn’t wanted to be a burden. She’d told herself she wouldn’t slow him down. She’d been so certain. And yet here she was, dusted in frost, alive only because of a shot she hadn’t even seen coming.
Zane saw that Freya was shivering violently, her own ice-based abilities no match for the unnatural chill radiating from the projectiles. He fished a pill from his Storage Ring and tossed it to her. 
It was a Heating Pill—nothing fancy, but it would kick her vitality into overdrive and flood her system with warmth. She caught it gratefully and swallowed it without a second thought.
Dozens of warriors were falling in a loose cluster, but the ice crystals were shooting upward far faster. Even when they saw one coming, it was almost impossible to get out of the way. Falling through open air gave them zero leverage. Back in the Blade Mountain Trap, they could at least push off the walls to dodge. Here, they were just helpless targets.
The icy energy was terrifying, and the crystals were too fast to dodge, but they were, at the end of the day, just ice. The trick wasn’t to avoid them, but to shatter them before they got close. As long as they stayed alert and didn’t let a single one make contact, they might just survive.
Everyone braced for the next volley. Human warriors gripped throwing knives; those from the Demon Night notched arrows to their bows.
[SFX: WHOOSHING ICE PROJECTILES]
Two more ice crystals shot up from the abyss, closing the distance in a blur.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY BLASTS]
This time, however, two sharp cracks echoed through the cavern. A green and a purple streak of light slammed into the projectiles in a pair of perfect shots.
[SFX: CRYSTAL SHATTERING]
The two diamond-shaped crystals instantly exploded into clouds of fine, glittering mist. The shots had come from two women of the Demon Night.
[MUSIC: TENSE COOPERATIVE STRINGS]
An unspoken truce settled over the falling warriors. For a moment, it didn’t matter if you were human, Ogre, or from the Demon Night. Survival was the only thing that mattered, and it was clear they could only manage that together.
As they continued to fall, everyone got the message. People stopped holding back, taking it upon themselves to intercept the deadly projectiles. The ones doing most of the work weren’t the humans, but the Demon Night warriors. Nearly every one of them carried a longbow, and their skill put the humans and Ogres to shame.
[SFX: CRYSTAL SHARDS WHISTLING]
At first, the crystals came one or two at a time, but soon the volley grew into a storm. Three at once, then four.
[SFX: INTENSIFYING CRYSTAL BARRAGE]
The danger intensified with every passing second.
[SFX: ESCAPE RUNE ACTIVATION]
[SFX: BODIES SHOOTING UPWARD]
When the first warrior activated their escape rune and shot back up, nobody said a word. A second followed. Then a third. The opportunities were real — but so was the dying. Not everyone had a reason compelling enough to stay.
Zane did. He wasn’t here just for himself.
Then someone got a bright idea.
“Freya.” Zane’s voice was quiet. “Adjust your position — stay directly above me.”
She shifted until she was in line with him, his body between her and whatever came next. The other warriors saw the arrangement and followed.
[SFX: MULTIPLE BODIES REPOSITIONING]
A human warrior shifted his trajectory, tucking himself in right behind Zane. An Ogre followed, then one of the Demon Night. Soon, a whole column of bodies was forming behind him, a falling pyramid with Zane as its unwilling point.
He glanced up at the perfectly organized stack of bodies descending above him and had to fight a wry smile. He couldn’t blame them. It was a smart, if cowardly, move. In this formation, all they had to do was fall, while he had to destroy every crystal that came their way.
So, with Zane at the tip of the spear, the pyramid of humans, Ogres, and Demon Night warriors continued its high-speed plunge.
[SFX: CRYSTAL IMPACTS FADING]
After what felt like an hour, the number of ice crystals finally began to dwindle. The air, though still frigid, was slowly starting to warm.
It felt like they’d made it. The worst was over.
A collective, unspoken sigh of relief rippled through the group.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DARK STRINGS]
[SFX: CHILLING ECHOING LAUGH]
And that’s when a soft, chilling laugh echoed up from the darkness below.
