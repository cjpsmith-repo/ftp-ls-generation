Ep 1261 - Balancing The Elements

Word count: 1132

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TEMPLE CHAMBER]
The Law of the Five Elements stood apart from all the other Original Laws. In many ways, it was the most fundamental power in the world, since just about everything was made up of some combination of the five.
It was easy enough for any warrior to master the Law of a single element; plenty of attunement handbooks were based on that very principle. The real test came when you had to blend two or three types of Law Power for a special martial art, switching between them to get the right effect. That kind of thing took an incredible amount of practice and a deep level of understanding. 
For most warriors, just comprehending five different Laws was a monumental feat. For Zane, it wasn’t so difficult.
But the secret method of House Kincaid demanded a perfect balance between all five: Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, and Earth. That would be a huge challenge, even for him. The truth was, balancing the Five Elements was the foundation of an unbelievably powerful martial art. The Five-Element Palm Print Sloane had used was a hybrid, mixing this secret method with a standard palm technique. It was powerful and dynamic, sure, but since her two bodies hadn’t yet merged into one, she couldn’t perfectly balance the elements herself.
[SFX: BRIGHT METALLIC RESONANCE]
Just then, a light flared from Zane’s thumb, so blindingly golden it stung to look at it directly. This was Metal Law.
[SFX: CRACKLING FLAMES]
A warm, flickering flame bloomed from his index finger— Fire Law.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING EARTH]
A heavy, grounding energy pulsed from his middle finger— Earth Law.
[SFX: FLOWING WATER WAVES]
Shimmering waves of water swirled around his ring finger— Water Law.
[SFX: VIBRANT NATURE PULSE]
And his pinky finger thrummed with vibrant green life— Wood Law.
Though Zane couldn’t perfectly balance the five Law Powers either, the unique edge his Chaos Energy gave him allowed him to hold them all firmly in place on his hand.
The Woman in White stared, her eyes wide with shock as she watched the powers gather on his fingers. 
Like Sloane, she was a member of the Thoughtless Minds, from a family just as prominent as House Kincaid. And while she was beneath Sloane in both status and talent, she knew one thing for certain: the fusion of the Five Elements was a secret technique unique to House Kincaid. More than that, it was the primary attunement method used by the family’s own patriarch.
As far as she knew, it was a secret that was never taught to outsiders. So how had this young man not only refined a Sacred Mount Lodestone but also learned House Kincaid’s most guarded technique?
[SFX: ENERGY STRAINING]
The five elements countered one another in an endless cycle: metal against wood, wood against earth, earth against water, water against fire, fire against metal. Any imbalance would collapse the entire structure. Only perfect equilibrium would allow them to reinforce rather than destroy each other.
Understanding it and achieving it were two different things. He pressed his palm against the Lodestone anyway.
A palm print bloomed across its surface.
[SFX: ENERGY COLLAPSING]
But almost immediately, the water surged toward the flame, and the flame bolted for the wood in a panic. The five powers collided and collapsed in his hand.
Zane huffed out a bitter smile. No surprise there.
“House Kincaid’s Five-Element fusion technique is nothing to take lightly,” the Woman in White said, watching him. “You’ve only just started learning it, haven’t you?”
Zane glanced up and gave her a small nod. He wasn’t about to bother hiding it — not here. In the Illusion Battlefield, every warrior around him was Soul Sea Realm or better anyway. There was nothing left to protect by staying quiet.
“I recall Sloane herself spent six full years reaching just the first tier of mastery,” the woman went on, certain she was doing him a favor. “You won’t manage this in an afternoon.”
Zane only smiled and said nothing. He drew five more threads of Chaos Energy from within himself and let them pour back into his hand.
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
Five points of light gathered and twisted together — and collapsed even faster than before.
The Woman in White’s expression settled into something like quiet satisfaction, as if she’d expected nothing less. What she didn’t understand was that the harder something proved to be, the less likely Zane was to walk away from it.
He tried a third time. But this time, he didn’t just force the five powers together — he rearranged them. 
[MUSIC: MAGICAL DISCOVERY]
Water shifted to his thumb. Fire moved to his pinky. Metal slid to his middle finger. Spread out this way, the powers that fed on each other’s weaknesses no longer sat side by side. The repulsion wasn’t gone, but it eased considerably.
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
As she watched, he began to draw out the magnetic power from the Lodestone.
[SFX: MAGNETIC BUZZING]
[SFX: STONE IMPACT]
Another palm print appeared on the far side of the stone, its shape and size nearly identical to the one he had left before.
[SFX: ENERGY DISPERSING]
But he couldn’t hold the delicate balance any longer. 
The powers of the Five Elements scattered from his hand, dissipating into the air.
Yet in the exact instant the powers broke, Zane’s eyes flashed with understanding. 
He immediately wrapped his hands around the Sacred Mount Lodestone and spun it like a top. 
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]
As his hands moved, a gray ray of light formed in his palm.
[SFX: ENERGY HUMMING]
The light hovered over his skin like a glowing brand. It held there for a moment, then quickly began to fade. 
[SFX: ENERGY FADING OUT]
In just three seconds, it vanished completely.
*So this is magnetic power?* Zane thought.
It was too bad that fighting was forbidden in this room. Otherwise, he would have loved to test its strength. Sloane had bragged about how incredible this skill was, but Zane hadn’t taken her seriously. After all, he was the owner of The Elysian Keep, which was basically a mountain of treasure. He wasn’t exactly hurting for new attunement handbooks.
What he didn’t know was that he was seriously underestimating this magnetic power.
It was a key component of *The Godly Way of the Five Elements*. While it might have been a step below Zane’s own Divine Path of Sealed Emotion, it was still a Godly Way—one of the ultimate paths to power.
As for the woman watching from the side, her jaw dropped. Seeing him draw out even a flicker of magnetic power left her completely stunned.
*Didn’t he just start learning their secret technique?* she thought, her mind reeling. *How is this even possible?*
