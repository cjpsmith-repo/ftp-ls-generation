Ep 34 - The Finish Line
Word count: 1123
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR TRAINING COURSE]
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
He recognized these individuals weren't the surrendering type, based on their previous encounter. Zane's muscles tightened in anticipation of conflict, though his gaze remained fixed on his objective: the next Stone Tablet gleaming in the distance.
As he narrowed the gap between them, a scholarly-looking young man with thin-rimmed spectacles and pristine robes stepped deliberately into his path. The scholar raised his hand with an air of authority that suggested he was accustomed to being obeyed.
[SFX: AUTHORITATIVE FOOTSTEP]
"That's quite far enough. Your Entrance Exam journey ends here," the scholar announced with clinical precision, his voice carrying a hint of condescension. Zane felt a flicker of surprise that they hadn't simply ambushed him outright—perhaps they enjoyed the psychological advantage of intimidation first.
Feigning ignorance, Zane furrowed his brow and asked, "My exam's finished? According to whom? Who dispatched you to block my progress?" His heart raced despite his casual tone, mind already calculating escape routes.
The scholar's lips curled into a mirthless smile as he released a dry, hollow laugh that never reached his cold eyes. "I'm not obligated to provide explanations to someone of your... station. Simply accept that you've reached your terminus here. Abandon any aspirations you might harbor—the Skyreach Sect has no place among its ranks for individuals like yourself."
His words hung in the air, sharp and dismissive, clearly intended to crush Zane's spirit before they crushed his body.
Normally, facing four guys at the Marrow Refining Level would be terrifying. Even someone at the peak of Core Strength wouldn't stand a chance. To them, Zane was just an easy target.
He wasn't getting any answers, so his mind raced, trying to figure a way around them. But he let none of it show on his face. Instead, he slumped, putting on a gloomy look. "But... I haven't passed the exam yet," he mumbled. "What am I supposed to do now?"
"That's your problem," the scholar snapped. All four of them smirked down at him, exchanging glances that all said the same thing. "This kid's delusional if he still thinks he has a shot at passing. Is he for real?"
[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]
While their guards were down, Zane's eyes shot to the sky behind them. "Hey, look!" he yelled. "An examiner! On a Skycruiser!"
The Skyreach Sect examiners were there to keep disciples in line. Zane was betting these guys wouldn't dare attack him with one watching.
As they whipped around to look, a twisted part of Zane almost hoped they'd attack him. He knew that taking a beating from Marrow Refining Level fighters was the only way to trigger that rush of warmth again.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS UNDERTONE]
It had happened just moments ago, when one of them hit him with a Venom Palm. A strange heat had spread through him, washing away the pain and steadying his breath, like he'd just taken an Aether Crystal.
The poison hadn't touched him. His body was like some kind of strange artifact—toxins were useless against it. After all, who ever heard of poison harming a mystical weapon?
Opportunities like this seldom presented themselves. Zane found himself genuinely tempted to remain motionless and allow them to unleash their full strength upon him. A thorough thrashing from four Marrow Refining fighters might work wonders for purifying his body, each blow potentially triggering that mysterious healing warmth he'd experienced earlier.
Yet the gleaming Stone Tablet stood tantalizingly close—the finish line for the Core Strength Level examination.
All that separated him from success was a short sprint across the clearing. Zane's eyes darted between his attackers and his objective, weighing his options with calculated precision.
"Better secure my place first and get this Crystal Talisman to turn red before something else goes catastrophically wrong," Zane contemplated, his fingers unconsciously brushing against the talisman hanging at his chest.
The prospect of deliberately taking a beating, while intriguing from a cultivation perspective, carried too many unknowns.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE SPRINT]
With a decisive exhale, he abandoned the tempting idea of a free beating and launched himself toward the Stone Tablet, his muscles coiling and releasing with explosive power as he sprinted across the remaining distance.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: HAND SLAP ON STONE]
The four of them gave chase, but he was already too close. They could only watch in frustration as he closed the last few feet and slapped his hand on the stone.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
The moment his hand touched the tablet, his Crystal Talisman bled from green to a deep, visceral red.
A huge smirk spread across his face. Zane turned and flashed his now-red Crystal Talisman at the four guys behind him. His triumphant grin was all it took to make their blood boil.
Before the exam, they'd been hired to "take care of" some kid from the Core Strength Level.
Being so much stronger, they'd laughed it off. Taking out some weakling sounded like a joke, an easy job.
They never imagined he'd actually slip through their fingers. And now, he had the nerve to rub it in their faces. The sheer audacity of it. The four of them were steaming, their faces hot with humiliation.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
The scholar shot Zane a venomous glare. "That's it. We're finishing this kid, no matter what."
"He passed the exam," the strongest of the four grunted, his expression uncertain. "Killing him now, with examiners around... that might be pushing it."
The scholar was livid. "Burt said he'd handle the fallout! This kid's a nobody from the outer provinces, not The Capital. He's got no connections. We kill him, who's gonna come looking for revenge?"
The other three exchanged a look. That seemed to be all the reassurance they needed. Fueled by a shared sense of humiliation, they moved as one.
[SFX: RAPID PALM STRIKES]
The man unleashed a volley of eight rapid-fire palm strikes. Even for a Marrow Refining fighter, putting out four thousand pounds of force left him winded.
[SFX: IMPACT THUD]
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
The strikes slammed into Zane's back. The force shoved him forward, but it also triggered that familiar rush of heat inside him. A satisfied groan escaped his lips as he felt the warmth flush the impurities from his system.
By the time the sensation faded, the others had surrounded him.
"Frost Wolf Fist!"
"Explosive Elbow!"
"Crushing Blow!"
[SFX: MULTIPLE COMBAT IMPACTS]
All four of them unloaded on him, each blow landing square on his back and knocking him forward. At an incredible rate, the toxins in Zane's body began to mix with his sweat, seeping out of his pores as a dark-red gunk.
The more they wailed on him, the more frustrated they became. Zane's body was impossibly tough. Not even an Ogre body-builder was this solid.
"Who the hell is this kid?" One of them yelled out. Unleashing more attacks. But the more they attacked, the more Zane stared up at them.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TRANSFORMATION]
They had no idea what he was becoming.
