Ep 299 - The Climb
Word Count: 1282
[SFX: SWORD CLASH]
[MUSIC: EPIC ODYSSEY]
The moment the low-grade Mysterious weapons unleashed their sword intent, Zane’s Streamer responded with a brilliant silver flash, shattering the incoming waves like glass against stone. A satisfied smile crossed his face as he felt the connection between himself and his weapon growing stronger with each challenge.
Zane maintained his steady climb, his breathing controlled despite the increasing difficulty. Next came the sword intent from the middle-grade weapons—a force considerably more potent than the earlier ones. He felt a momentary flutter of apprehension in his chest, quickly replaced by determination.
But the heightened challenge made no difference. His own sword intent tore through them with the same effortless precision, leaving only silence in its wake.
He ascended the mountain path with remarkable agility. The constant assault of sword intent from the lesser weapons failed to slow down his progress, each confrontation affirming his growing mastery.
Every time Zane shattered a blade’s intent, the sword itself would fall silent. Its vibrations ceased abruptly, and its metallic body released tiny motes of light, like fireflies, that drifted straight into Zane’s body.
"Strange," he muttered to himself, initially attempting to sidestep the luminous particles. After several futile attempts, he realized they were impossible to avoid. Since they appeared harmless, he quit trying to avoid them, watching with curiosity as they disappeared into his skin, leaving a warm, tingling sensation behind.
Furrowing his brow, Zane glanced down at his arms where the lights had vanished. "What are these little lights?" he asked, a mixture of wonder and wariness coloring his voice.
"Haha! Those are sword spirits," the Cobalt Dragon explained from within his mind. "The swords have spirits hidden inside them, like the seeds of a sword beast. The low and middle-grade Mysterious weapons only have a few spirits each. If you gather enough of them, little by little, your own sword will eventually be able to grow a sword beast."
So that was it. The more sword intent he destroyed, the more sword spirits he'd collect.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WINDS]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL JOURNEY]
Killing Sword Mountain, despite its fearsome reputation, wasn’t really all that large. Before long, Zane had methodically demolished the intent radiating from countless low and middle-grade Mysterious weapons.
By the time he reached the imposing base of the main peak, he found himself confronting the high-grade weapons—formidable challenges that would have terrified him just months ago.
Even though the intent radiating from these weapons struck with heightened intensity and razor-sharp precision, Zane merely smiled. The attacks that would have overwhelmed most cultivators barely registered as threats to him now. His eyes gleamed with quiet determination as he pressed forward, a testament to how far he had come since those dark days in the Lowell House cellar.
[SFX: SWORD SLICING THROUGH AIR]
As Zane pushed on, the Streamer in his hand danced like a silver-white ribbon, slicing through all the sword intent around him.
[AMBIENT: LOW BUZZING HUM]
The air thrummed with a low, buzzing hum.
[SFX: SWORDS CLATTERING]
As he shattered one wave of intent after another, the swords themselves fell silent at his feet, and a storm of colorful sword spirits rushed into him.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS THEME]
Zane passed the foot of the mountain, moving from the area of high-grade Mysterious weapons into the range of the Mythic ones. He was getting close to the mountainside now.
"This Killing Sword Mountain isn't as tough as everyone says," Zane remarked, looking up at the steep incline ahead.
Hearing the comment, the Cobalt Dragon chuckled. "Well, it only seems easy because of your skill. The owners of these swords had all reached a master's level of sword intent. Since yours is even more perfected and powerful, this part of the climb is bound to feel simple by comparison. But no matter how strong your intent is, you'll be lucky to make it halfway up the mountain. For your first time here, your progress so far isn’t bad."
[SFX: MYTHIC WEAPON RESONANCE]
Then a Mythic weapon ahead let out a sharp cry. Before Zane could react, a figure poured out of it — a purple-haired girl, no physical body, faint as a soul. She pulled the sword from the ground and came at him.
"What is that?" Zane asked.
"A mature sword spirit. Kill her and you absorb her spirits." [SFX: SPIRIT SWORD CLASH]
Her swordsmanship was exquisite — she blocked his first two strikes cleanly and countered with the third. Zane steadied himself and answered with three basic forms. The third cut her apart. She dissolved into purple light that rushed into his body. The sword spirit in a single Mythic weapon dwarfed everything he'd collected below.
More spirits followed. Tigers. Pythons. Crocodiles. A dragon. Each one harder than the last. Against low-grade Mythic weapons he needed three moves. Against mid-grade, ten or more. His pace slowed. 
[SFX: SUCCESSIVE SPIRIT CLASHES]

Not bad. The Cobalt Dragon's words sank in. As it turned out, his performance was just a little better than average.
But Zane had no intention of stopping to plant his sword here. He had to test his limits. Real strength was what mattered, and reality had a way of showing you just how harsh it could be, no matter how determined you were.
Besides, the rules here were strict. The Cobalt Dragon wasn’t allowed to help. Zane was completely on his own.
In that case, how was he ever supposed to get past the Mythic weapons and reach the ridges?
"What's the actual goal here?" Zane asked.
"Plant your sword in the mountain. The higher you plant it, the greater the permanent benefit — and the stronger your connection to the Killing Sword Mountain."
Zane looked up past the mountainside toward the ridge above. Sacred Weapons up there. He could feel their pressure from here.
"I want to plant it at the ridge."
"Impossible. The sword intent above the mountainside is stronger than yours. The ridge is beyond you — for now." The Cobalt Dragon's tone was final.
"I'll try anyway." 
[SFX: WIND FROM ABOVE]
"Huh? There's a low-grade Mysterious weapon here!" Zane's eyes lit up when he spotted it jutting from the mountainside. The sword’s hilt was plain, styled like a standard-issue longsword. It didn’t look special at all.
However, two words were carved on the hilt: "Demon Night."
"Look at this," Zane said, pointing at the simple sword. "So much for it being impossible to reach the ridges. Someone managed to plant a low-grade Mysterious weapon right here on the mountainside!"
It really stuck out among all the powerful Mythic weapons surrounding it.
The Cobalt Dragon sounded surprised, too. Then he smiled. "Oh, that's from the Demon Night. No wonder they could leave a low-grade Mysterious weapon this high up."
"The Demon Night?" Zane asked, his curiosity piqued. The name on the hilt meant nothing to him.
"The Demon Night aren’t exactly human," the Cobalt Dragon explained with a faint smile. "You could say their race is superior to most others. They possess terrifying strength. It’s said that their children begin training while still in the womb. So when they're born, even as babies, they're already brimming with power equivalent to a human who has reached the Adept Realm. In other words, they are born as Adepts."
"Born as Adepts," Zane breathed, completely stunned.
Countless human warriors trained their entire lives, overcoming every obstacle at great personal cost, just to reach the Adept Realm. Zane couldn’t wrap his head around a species where babies were born already at that level.
It was so unfair. No wonder a swordsman from the Demon Night was able to plant a low-grade weapon this far up the mountain.
They were truly a race capable of things beyond imagination.
His mind reeled. What else didn’t he know about?
