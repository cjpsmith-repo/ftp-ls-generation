Ep 1156 - A Killing Frenzy

Word count: 991

[MUSIC: DARK AMBIENT DRONE]
Killing is an addiction. The more lives you take, the easier it is for the darkness to take root inside you.
[SFX: ETHEREAL WHISPERS]
Those little red specks of light floating through the chasm were pure, concentrated Malice—each one the psychic residue left behind from the slaughter of a hundred thousand living beings. And because everyone was constantly breathing them in, the effect was starting to build.
That said, there was one exception to every rule. If a person’s heart were truly pure—untouched by any desire to kill—no amount of Malice could reach them. A child, innocent and unformed, would have floated through this abyss without so much as a quickened pulse. 
But there were no children here. Every fighter in this chasm had grown up inside slaughter. The Malice didn’t create darkness. It only fed what was already there.
[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]
[SFX: RACING HEARTBEAT]
Freya’s chest heaved, and a flicker of red appeared in her normally calm, gentle eyes. Like the other fighters, her heart was starting to pound, a wild, restless energy building inside her. A powerful urge to tear apart every single thing in sight surged through her veins, and she could feel her sanity starting to fray.
[SFX: SHARP GASP]
[SFX: RUSHING COLD WIND]
With a sharp gasp, she summoned a stream of freezing air. But instead of aiming it at the others, she wrapped it around her own body.
This wasn’t some protective barrier of energy. 
[SFX: ICE CRACKLING AND FORMING]
It was raw, biting cold, a physical shock to the system that instantly encased her in a thin layer of ice. The jolt was sharp enough to cut through the haze, giving her a precious moment of clarity.
The other fighters, however, were already gone.
[MUSIC: VIOLENT PERCUSSION RISING]
The ogres were the first to snap. They were bloodthirsty by nature, so the Malice already festering inside them was stronger than it was for the others. This massive wave of dark energy finally made their restless blood boil over.
[SFX: BESTIAL ROAR]
[SFX: FLESH TEARING]
“Aargh!” one of them roared, his muscles bunching so hard the veins on his arms looked like they were trying to crawl out of his skin. He threw himself at a human fighter standing right next to him.
[AMBIENT: ECHOING CHASM]
The human, though tiny compared to the ogre, didn’t even try to back down. He just planted his feet and met the monster head-on.
[SFX: BONE-CRUSHING IMPACT]
[SFX: BODIES SLAMMING ROCK]
The sickening crunch of their collision echoed through the chasm as they both went flying backward, slamming hard into the rock wall.
Both were badly hurt. Blood poured from their eyes, ears, and mouths, but their faces showed no sign of pain. 
[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS CHARGING]
They just pushed off the wall and charged each other again, using the momentum to add even more force to their attacks.
Just a moment before, everyone had been crowded into the center of the chasm to avoid the Malice clinging to the walls. 
[MUSIC: CHAOTIC FRENZIED STRINGS]
[SFX: FRENZIED CROWD]
Now, that same energy had sparked a bloodthirsty frenzy, turning them into a pack of rabid animals.
“I’m going to kill you!”
“Go to hell!”
The carnage built quickly. Before long, one ogre had his head taken clean off. A Demon Night fighter went down next, then two humans. The bodies were barely noticed—every survivor was too far gone to care.
[SFX: STRUGGLING, CLOTH TEARING]
Across the chasm, a Demon Night woman had been caught off guard by a frenzied ogre. She wasn’t fully lost—her mind was still holding on by a thread—but it hadn’t been enough to keep her out of his grip. And compared to an ogre’s raw strength, her slender frame was nothing. He could tear her apart without even trying.
[SFX: STRAINING EFFORT]
[SFX: ARROW CUTTING AIR]
Zane had been watching.
The Malice hit him the same way it hit everyone else—his pulse had spiked the moment the red specks touched him, something dark and restless surging up from the part of him that knew how to kill. But after a few breaths, it had stopped climbing. 
Without thinking about it, without even meaning to, he had begun channeling the absorbed Malice directly into the Killing Sword Mountain—the same connection he’d forged the day he drove his sword into that peak and made it his. However much darkness flooded in, it flowed straight through him and into the mountain’s depths. He was a conduit, not a vessel. The Malice passed through without taking root.
He raised his bow and put an arrow through the air between the ogre and the woman.
[SFX: ARROW IMPACT, HEAVY THUD]
The ogre’s grip broke. He staggered, shook his head—and the frenzy pulled him elsewhere.
Lost to the madness, the fighters attacked whoever was closest. And since Zane was standing right next to Freya, he became her first target.
[SFX: ICE BLADES WHOOSHING]
Her fingers became a blur, launching a volley of transparent ice blades straight at him.
Zane could only manage a wry smile. 
[SFX: ENERGY BLASTS]
[SFX: ICE SHATTERING]
He threw a few quick punches, unleashing waves of concussive energy that shattered the ice blades on impact. As he defended, he steadily closed the distance between them.
At her current power level, she was no match for him. The moment he was in range, he reached out to grab her.
But with her mind gone, the Malice had pushed her combat instincts into overdrive. 
[SFX: SHARP KICK IMPACT]
[SFX: ICE MATERIALIZING]
She twisted away from his grasp, kicking wildly at his chest. In one fluid motion, she flipped backward as two sharp ice hairpins materialized in her hands, and she lunged, aiming to drive them straight into his ears.
In a twisted way, the Malice made her deadlier, pushing her instincts to their absolute limit. The ferocity she was showing now was far beyond her normal capabilities; an ordinary fighter would have been overwhelmed in seconds.
But Zane was far from ordinary.
[SFX: ICE PICKS WHOOSHING]
A faint smile played on his lips as he tilted his head back, letting the deadly ice picks whip past his face. As her arms crossed in the follow-through, he caught both of her wrists in one hand and yanked her off balance, pulling her in close.
Even with her hands pinned, she didn’t stop fighting, twisting and struggling to break his grip.
