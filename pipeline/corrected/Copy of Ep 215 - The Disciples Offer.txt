Ep 215 - The Disciple's Offer
Word count: 1168
[SFX: METALLIC CLINK ECHOES]
[SFX: BILLOWING SMOKE]
The commotion from the furnace echoed through the chamber, interrupting the showdown between Zane and the disciple from The Seventeenth Court, who was trying to force him out.
Neither man moved for a heartbeat, both recognizing the distinctive noise that often preceded disaster in a weaponsmith's workshop.
Soon, a plume of black smoke billowed out of the refining furnace.
[AMBIENT: WORKSHOP FURNACE]
[MUSIC: IMPENDING DANGER]
When the disciple saw the smoke, his face went white. He yelled, "Harrison! What's wrong? Is the fire dying?" If the fire wasn't hot enough to melt the materials, the whole furnace would explode.
It was wishful thinking; the fire didn't seem to be dying. If anything, it was growing. If it kept going at this rate, the whole place could explode.
The intense heat created a massive pressure difference between the inside and outside, and the furnace's thick walls could only handle so much. On top of that, certain materials reacted unpredictably inside a refining furnace, and those reactions had to be managed with skill. That's why any good weaponsmith needed a ton of experience and quick thinking to handle a crisis.
It was exactly why someone like Evil Jace could craft weapons better than any disciple in the Skyreach Sect, even with no formal training. If he'd ever studied with them, he wouldn't just be making top-grade Mythic weapons---he'd be forging Spirit Weapons by now.
Zane saw the disciple at the furnace controls starting to panic. If that furnace detonated, the consequences would be nothing short of disastrous.
The refining furnaces on Quenching Peak represented extraordinary investments, each meticulously constructed with refined copper and intricately lined with precious metals that few could afford. An explosion would devastate not only the equipment but also wreck the disciples' futures, as replacing such specialized equipment was an expense they'd never be able to afford.
[SFX: PEOPLE SCRAMBLING]
Panic seized The Seventeenth Court's disciples as they darted about in frenzied desperation, their faces contorted with fear and uncertainty. Nothing like this had ever happened to them, and it showed.
"I'll fetch an instructor!" one disciple called out, his voice cracking with anxiety as he edged toward the door.
"Are you mad? This thing's about to explode any moment! We'll all be dead before you return!" another countered, his eyes wide with terror as he gestured wildly at the increasingly unstable furnace.
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC WORKSHOP]
The workshop descended into utter chaos. Disciples collided with one another, knocking over tools and materials in their haste. Some stood frozen in horror while others darted between workstations, seeking anything that might help. Their collective fear hung heavy in the air, as palpable as the heat coming off the rumbling furnace.
[SFX: FURNACE ROARING]
"Harrison, extract the sword blank from the contaminating materials!" a tall, thin disciple shouted over the noise.
Harrison shot back, "I'm trying! But the blank is already fused with the other materials. I can't get them apart!" He was pouring Vitality from his hands into the furnace to keep the fire stable, sweat dripping down his face from the intense heat and the stress.
"Come on, Harrison, you got this!" the other disciples cheered. But it was no use; Harrison's strength was draining. He was on the verge of collapse.
Zane watched the unfolding crisis with detached interest. At first he felt indifferent to the furnace's impending destruction. After all, this was their business; he was just a visitor in their world. And hadn't that one disciple just tried to kick him out?
But then a sudden realization struck him---he needed to borrow their refining furnace to activate his sword. Without it his carefully laid plans would crumble to dust.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
With newfound urgency, Zane strode forward, his movements deliberate and purposeful. The tension in the workshop intensified as all eyes turned toward this unexpected intervention.
"What do you think you're doing?" one of them asked, his voice tight with panic.
The outer disciple who'd been trying to push Zane out stepped forward and threw a punch.
He never connected. Zane shifted his weight almost imperceptibly — a ghost-step — and the fist sailed past. He kept walking toward the furnace at the same unhurried pace.
[SFX: DISCIPLES SHOUTING]
Two more disciples moved to cut him off. Same result. Zane slipped through them without breaking stride, his movement so fluid it looked effortless. The disciples pulled up short, staring.
He reached Harrison and placed a hand on his back. "Relax," he said quietly.
[SFX: VITALITY SURGE — LOW PULSE]
A steady current of Vitality poured from Zane's palm into Harrison's body. Harrison felt it immediately — pure, dense, far stronger than anything he'd produced himself. The net of fire he'd been desperately holding together suddenly tripled in strength, locking the sword blank and the reacting materials in place.
Harrison's hands steadied. The violent churning inside the furnace slowed, then stopped.

[SFX: SMOKE CHANGING COLOR]
The transformation happened almost immediately. The ominous black smoke that had billowed from the furnace gradually shifted, yielding to a pristine white stream that rose steadily into the air.
Though this change didn't guarantee the forging would be a success, it was a serious improvement---one that caused the disciples to breathe sighs of relief.
[SFX: PERSON COLLAPSING TO GROUND]
[SFX: HEAVY SIGH]
A satisfied smile spread across Harrison's face as he sank to the ground. They'd stopped the furnace from blowing. He was just grateful to have survived the close call. First things first, he had to thank the person who helped him.
[SFX: PERSON STANDING UP]
Harrison rose to his feet and offered Zane a deep, reverent bow. "Thank you so much. Seriously. If it wasn't for you, that furnace would have exploded, which would've put us in huge trouble. I'm Harrison West, lead disciple of The Seventeenth Court," he declared, his voice still trembling slightly from the near disaster.
Zane returned the gesture with a bow of his own. "I'm Zane Lowell, a disciple from Misty Peak."
Harrison's eyes lit up with curiosity. "Did you come to Quenching Peak to forge a Mythic weapon?" he inquired, studying Zane's face for a reaction.
When Zane remained silent, Harrison nervously filled the void. "I'm still mastering the art of weaponsmithing myself. As you just saw, we were trying to make a top-grade Mythic weapon, and it almost ended with the furnace exploding. We're lucky you showed up," he admitted, rubbing his soot-stained hands together. "So if you're looking to forge a low or mid-grade Mythic weapon, we can definitely help you with that. It's the least I could do."
The gratitude in Harrison's eyes was unmistakable. Zane contemplated the generous proposal, his fingers absently tracing the outline of the unfinished blade concealed within his robes.
The black fire within him stirred at the thought of what he truly needed. "I appreciate that, Harrison, really. But please don't trouble yourself on my account. I just need to borrow your refining furnace for a little while."
"What?" Harrison exclaimed, his expression shifting from gratitude to suspicion in an instant. His eyebrows furrowed as he scrutinized Zane with newfound wariness. "But you're not a weaponsmith, and you certainly aren't a disciple from Quenching Peak."
Zane swallowed hard. Was the goodwill he'd just earned from saving their lives gone already?
