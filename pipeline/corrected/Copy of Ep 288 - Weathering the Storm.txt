Ep 288 - Weathering the Storm
Word count: 1023
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL DRAGON THEME]
While Jacob’s Drake spine weapon was trying to kill Zane, something incredible was happening.
Inside Zane’s mind, nine enormous dragons were coiled around the Soulforge, so lifelike they seemed to breathe.
At first glance, they might have passed for mere carvings etched into the furnace’s surface. But Zane had a gut feeling they were more than just decoration. He suspected they were living, thinking beings.
For now, only one of them, a Cobalt Dragon, was slowly stirring as Zane's Celestial Essence seeped into it. But even as it began to wake, it hadn’t said a single word to him.
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
So how did he know it was conscious? Twice, when he’d been in deep trouble, the dragon had roared, its power saving his life. The first time was in the Celestial Library, when he faced the crushing will of the Vengeful Beast during his cultivation. The second was in the Elysian Keep, during a desperate battle against a swarm of furious ghosts.
Other than those two roars, the nine dragons were like statues, lying silent and dormant on the furnace.
[SFX: DRAGON WHISPER]
But today, the Cobalt Dragon had actually spoken to him. Why now? And it had made a demand: it wanted to devour Jacob's bone spear.
Truth be told, the Soulforge was the reason he’d even made it this far. He'd worked his ass off, sure, but without the furnace strengthening his body and soul from the very beginning, he might never have even made it out of his family home.
Zane had no clue where the furnace came from, but he knew this artifact, with its nine dragons and the black fire it produced, was more powerful than anything he could imagine. It was a secret he had to guard with his life.
Even when he used the Soulforge’s black fire to melt down weapons and extract their Celestial Essence, Zane was always careful to have Dustin handle the sale. He never dared do it himself, terrified of the attention it might draw.
[SFX: INTENSE PRESSURE WAVE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS POWER]
The pressure of a dragon’s will was sharper and far more terrifying than any human’s.
Ninety percent of that raw power was flooding directly into Zane, while the remaining ten percent bled out into the stands around the battle ring.
As the horrifying pressure washed over the other disciples, their faces went pale. The less experienced among them looked like they were caught in a blizzard, their teeth chattering loud enough for the whole arena to hear.
[SFX: SOFT THUD]
With a soft thud, one of the disciples collapsed in a dead faint.
Sir Kenneth glanced at Master Elwood. "This year’s All Peaks Competition has been one surprise after another," he remarked. "But I never would have guessed Jacob would get his hands on the spine of a Drake."
A Drake’s spine could be forged into a weapon or refined into a powerful pill. Anyone who consumed a pill like that would have their body reforged with a trace of a Drake’s bloodline!
A treasure that valuable was enough to make even Kenneth take notice.
Of course, given his status, Kenneth wouldn’t dream of trying to steal the bone spear from Jacob.
"Three years ago, I said Jacob was a lucky man," Master Elwood commented. "The more he advances, the more opportunities seem to fall into his lap. I’m afraid Zane might actually lose this time."
Sir Kenneth just laughed. "You say Jacob is lucky, but what about Zane? Look at that throwing blade," he said, pointing. "Think about the Titan Strike, and the sword techniques he mastered on his own. Does any of that seem ordinary to you?"
"You’re right, Sir," Master Elwood said quickly. "My bias against his low cultivation level is showing. But the Drake’s aura is overwhelming many of the disciples. I need to help them!"
[SFX: LEAPING SOUND]
Seeing the pained looks on the younger disciples’ faces, he leaped from the high platform.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY]
With a wave of a wrinkled hand, a surge of emerald green Vitality formed a curtain of light, shielding the stands.
The Drake’s power had made the crowd feel like they were stranded naked in a snowstorm. If it went on any longer, it could cause permanent damage to their souls.
But as Master Elwood’s light curtain washed over them, they felt as if they’d been wrapped in thick, warm blankets, completely safe from the biting chill.
If a mere ten percent of that power had caused so much agony, what was happening to Zane, who was taking the other ninety percent head-on? Everyone stared, desperate to see if the young disciple was even still standing.
Now shielded from the Drake’s oppressive aura, the crowd turned their full attention back to the ring. They had to see what was happening to Zane.
Zane's soul was far stronger than the average person's. When he'd entered the Hall of Fourth-Tier Attunements, he hadn’t needed the Cobalt Dragon's help; he had fought back the Vengeful Beast’s pressure all on his own.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Inside the arena, Zane felt his very soul shake like a leaf in a hurricane, threatening to tear loose at any second.
But somehow, he was stubbornly holding his ground.
This was, without a doubt, the most dangerous fight of his life. He'd won every match so far, but he'd never been pushed this close to the edge.
Honestly, the power gap between him and Jacob wasn’t that wide. The real difference was that Jacob was leaning on the power of his spear, not his own strength. Zane, on the other hand, was weathering the storm with nothing but the sheer force of his own soul.
He wanted to see just how much he could take.
[SFX: BLOOD DRIPPING]
A moment later, a trickle of blood seeped from the corner of Zane’s mouth. A soul attack could turn a person into a drooling idiot or shatter their mind, but it didn’t usually cause physical wounds.
He had bitten his own tongue—hard—just to stay conscious.
[MUSIC: DEFIANT THEME]
Zane took a sharp breath, his voice dangerously low. "Who the hell do you think you are? You think you can make me kneel? You?" He let out a harsh laugh. "You’re not good enough to even try."
