Ep 243 - Breaking Barriers
Word count: 1193
[AMBIENT: COMBAT ARENA]
[SFX: FIGHTING SOUNDS]
Tavish groaned, wincing from both the physical and emotional pain he was feeling. How was he getting beaten this badly by a nobody?
No matter how hard he pushed, Tavish couldn't even touch Zane. Since the start of the match, Zane had been fighting with nothing but pure speed and strength---he hadn't used a single drop of his Vitality.
"There's no way this guy is only a Level Two Adept," he thought. "He's gotta be some kind of monster."
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
Seeing Zane striding toward him, a cold smile playing on his lips but not reaching his eyes, a shiver of pure dread snaked down Tavish's spine. The look in Zane's eyes said he was already a dead man.
Fear struck Tavish deep in his gut. He couldn't take this anymore. He scrambled to yell at the referee, "I give up! I, Tavish of Sea Peak, give up!"
[SFX: REFEREE WHISTLE]
The referee announced the result of the match, his face impassive. "Zane wins. Tavish loses."
[SFX: BARRIER POWERING DOWN]
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
As the match ended, the glowing curtain of the enchanted barrier receded. Clutching his chest, Tavish practically scrambled off the arena like the devil himself was on his heels.
[SFX: CHEERING]
"Zane, you're a beast!"
"That's right! You gotta be tough on guys who go back on their word!"
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT]
The win had the students from Misty Peak fired up. Meanwhile, the crowd from Sea Peak was dead silent, their faces ashen.
This time, Sea Peak hadn't just lost the match; they'd lost face. "If Tavish had just beaten Zane, we could've salvaged this," the Sea Peak members thought. "In the world of fighters, the winner takes all. If Sea Peak had won, we'd be the ones celebrating. So what if he broke his promise? You think the Misty kids are gonna cry about it?"
Believing a win was in the bag, many of the Sea Peak students had their own trash talk locked and loaded, ready to fire back at anyone who called them out.
But Tavish lost to Zane. And that, nobody had seen coming.
It was completely unbelievable to the students from Sea Peak. Even having watched the battle, they couldn't wrap their heads around why Tavish had seemed so weak, barely able to land a single threatening attack, even after blowing past the three-move limit he'd boasted about.
He still had a chance to advance to the next round. But after a beating like that, even if some miracle medicine could heal Tavish, there was no way he could fight again anytime soon.
It was no wonder everyone from Misty Peak despised Mack.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
"Zane, watch out for Mack's magic arrays. That bastard's setups are a real nightmare," Eliot warned before Zane took the stage. After his own fight with him, just thinking about Mack's arrays made Eliot's heart hammer in his chest.
Zane nodded. "Got it. I'll be careful."
Master Warren chimed in with his own advice. "Look, if you realize you're outmatched, just forfeit. There's no need to force it. That kid's a real monster underneath it all."
Since Zane had already won two fights in a row, he was practically guaranteed a spot in the next round. Master Warren didn't want him to push his luck, hoping the advice would keep him safe for the battles to come.
Zane nodded, but he didn't really hear the advice. In competitions like this, most contestants were just fighting for bragging rights. But now that Zane was in the All Peaks Competition, he was in it to win the whole thing.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED BATTLE THEME]
With a fire in his eyes, Zane stepped into the arena.
"Huh? Another one from Misty Peak? Looks like it's my destiny to wipe out all of Misty Peak today." Seeing Zane, Mack had flashed a deceptively innocent smile. But that harmless act was just a cover for his brutal, sadistic nature.
"Yeah, I guess so," Zane replied coolly. Though he'd kept a straight face while watching Mack torture Eliot, Zane had to fight the raw urge to storm the arena and tear Mack limb from limb. But this was the All Peaks Competition. With several saints, elders, and even the head of the sect watching, Zane couldn't just rush in and attack, no matter how much he trusted his own strength.
Licking his lips, Mack's grin turned predatory. "That guy, Eliot\... he's the best you've got over at Misty Peak, right? You see what happened to him? If you're scared, don't worry, you can forfeit right now!"
As he taunted Zane, Mack gestured to the referee at the side of the arena, a disarmingly genuine smile on his face.
No one could deny Mack looked like a cheerful kid. His tan skin and solid build gave him the look of a healthy, energetic guy. His fighting style, though, was the complete opposite.
"Forfeit?" Zane's eyebrows shot up. He strolled toward Mack, cracking his knuckles. "I'd love to see you try and make me."
Zane's body was tempered to be nearly as hard as a Mid-Grade Spirit Weapon, and his knuckles were no exception. A loud crack echoed with every flex.
[SFX: KNUCKLES CRACKING]
[AMBIENT: ARENA BATTLE]
[SFX: THUNDEROUS KNUCKLE CRACK]
The sound ripped through the arena like thunderclaps, completely drowning out the clang of steel and the shouts from the other eight matches.
Curious glances shot toward Zane from all over, students disbelieving that a person could make such a booming sound just by cracking their knuckles.
Mack's smile didn't waver. He planted a flag and stepped back. "Body refining." He said it like a verdict. "Marrow Refining is the ceiling. No matter how hard your body gets, it has limits. True Vitality doesn't."
Zane smiled. "That's it? Not impressed!"
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
Zane's fist slammed into the flag with brutal impact. Streaks of emerald light flared from the enchanted barrier protecting it.
Zane pulled back, frowning. He'd been rushing straight at Mack when the flag flickered and suddenly he was circling it — like something had redirected his path without him choosing to. Strange.
"By the way, this magic array flag of mine is a top-grade Spirit Weapon---the Star-Shifting Flag," Mack yelled, his voice dripping with smugness. "How do you expect to damage that?"
He'd bought himself just enough time to set up a larger, more complex array. His smirk melted into a lazy grin as he leisurely pulled out another flag, getting ready to build his next trap.
"You said I couldn't damage it?"
With a sneer, Zane hammered another vicious blow into the Star-Shifting Flag.
[SFX: VIOLENT BLOW]
His left hand slammed hard into the flag. This time, along with pure force, he channeled a sliver of energy from the Emerald Crystal into the blow.
An enchanted barrier this flimsy was a joke to Zane. It was nothing compared to Yolande's; not even in the same league.
With that in mind, Zane figured a tiny fraction of the Emerald Crystal's power would be more than enough.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER SHATTERING]
In a flash, crimson cracks spiderwebbed across the flag's surface, and the enchanted barrier shattered into nothing.
"What?"
Across the arena, Mack was still holding a handful of flags he hadn't placed yet. Staring at the unbelievable sight, Mack's jaw went slack, his eyes so wide they looked like they were about to pop out of their sockets.
