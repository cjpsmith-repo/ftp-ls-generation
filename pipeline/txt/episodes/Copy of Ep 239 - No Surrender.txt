Ep 239 - No Surrender
Word Count: 1198
[MUSIC: MAGIC BATTLE]
Magic arrays were built with special flags.
An array master's main weapon in battle was a pre-set array. You couldn't just whip one up in the middle of a fight; they had to be prepared ahead of time, with the magic stored in these flags. When the time was right, all the master had to do was plant the flags in their proper spots and activate the trap.
The only real way to beat an array master was to stop them from ever setting up. Once the array was active, they basically had the home-field advantage, and taking them down became a nightmare. Even a basic array was insanely powerful, let alone something like a deadly magic inferno.
[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSH]
Eliot's spear sliced through empty air, missing Mack completely. Unfazed, he switched targets, lunging for the next best thing: the black array flag planted in the ground.
[SFX: MAGICAL ERUPTION]
[AMBIENT: DARK MAGIC]
The flag seemed to sense the attack. It erupted, unleashing a burst of thick, black fog that rushed toward Eliot, trying to swallow him whole.
It was a soul-based attack, straight from Mack.
Truth was, Mack wasn't trying to build a full array with that single flag. It was just a defensive trap. The moment Eliot went for the flag, it was designed to spring, ensnaring him in the soul-corroding fog.
No warrior would willingly let an opponent set up a magic array. But array masters were a tricky bunch, and Mack had just caught Eliot in one of his classic ploys.
"You really think you can destroy my array flag? Dream on!"
[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDUP]
A cold smile stretched across Mack's face as he pulled out more flags---red, blue, green---and began planting them in a circle around his trapped opponent.
[SFX: FLAG PLANTING WHOOSH]
Eliot was already struggling against the first flag. Seeing more pop up was the last thing he wanted. He frowned, the whoosh of each new flag hitting the ground grating on his nerves.
Mack laid out six more, forming a hexagon with Eliot right in the center. But Eliot had an almost supernatural sixth sense. He could feel the killing intent radiating directly from Mack. The array might spin up illusions that looked just like his opponent, maybe even mimic his aura, but Mack wasn't powerful enough to create a perfect copy. Instinctively, Eliot could still pick out the real Mack from the fakes.
[SFX: SPEAR THRUST]
He spun and drove the spear straight at Mack's chest. Mack hadn't expected to be found — he barely threw himself sideways in time. The spearpoint caught nothing but fabric, slicing his shirt open as he scrambled clear.
"This guy…" Mack's expression darkened. He hadn't counted on Eliot's perception being that sharp.
[SFX: CRYSTAL IMPACT]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
"I didn't expect you to be so perceptive. I'll admit, I underestimated you. But that ends now! Let's see you get out of this!" Mack snarled through gritted teeth. He pulled out a long, thin crystal and slammed it into the magic array's control plate. The moment it connected, a raw wave of energy surged from the plate.
[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDUP]
The plate was the array's command center, and by feeding it an extra crystal, Mack had just cranked the power into overdrive.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
Eliot's composure finally shattered. Trapped inside the supercharged array, his eyes went wide with a terror only he could see. He began striking wildly with his spear, completely bewildered by the phantoms swirling around him.
Once again, Mack gripped his black and red stick. A stiff, cruel smile fixed on his face, he snuck up behind the disoriented warrior. While Eliot was busy thrusting his spear at nothing, Mack swung the stick with all his might, slamming it into Eliot's left shoulder.
[SFX: POWERFUL ATTACK CHARGE]
"Celestial Bludgeon!" he roared, unleashing his most powerful move.
As a magic array master, Mack was more of a trapper than a brawler. Still, he was at the Peak level of the Adept Realm, and with Eliot lost in the illusion, there was nothing to stop the blow.
[SFX: VIOLENT IMPACT]
[SFX: BODY FLYING THROUGH AIR]
The impact connected with a sickening crunch, sending Eliot flying.
Even after a hit like that, he rolled and scrambled back to his feet, using his spear as a crutch. He gripped the weapon and lunged past Mack, but his opponent had already danced out of the way, leaving Eliot's spear to cut through empty air.
Mack smirked. He'd thought that blow would have shattered the man's shoulder, but he was tougher than he looked. It didn't matter. He had all the time in the world.
Mack pressed his advantage, striking Eliot from behind again and again while easily dodging his wild, desperate swings.
[SFX: HEAVY BLOW IMPACT]
He struck Eliot a second time, a heavy blow landing squarely on his back.
[SFX: VIOLENT IMPACT]
The force was too much. Eliot cried out, a spray of blood erupting from his mouth.
From the stands, Master Warren shook his head. "What a shame," he thought. "Just rotten luck to be matched against a magic array master. He can't break the illusion, so all he can do is stand there and get beaten to a pulp."
Seeing the top disciple of Misty Peak defeated so brutally was infuriating.
"Eliot just needs to give up," another master muttered with a frown. "Why is he still fighting? He won't be eliminated for losing one match. I never took him for being this stubborn!"
Meanwhile, Zane watched the arena, his jaw tight. It would be easy for Eliot to admit defeat, but Zane understood. Eliot's commitment to the warrior's path was absolute. For him, giving up would be like betraying his entire reason for becoming stronger.
If Isla were here, she would never stand for this. If she saw someone from Misty Peak being beaten so gruesomely, she would have already stepped in.
"He's from our Ninety-Foot Peak. So, Master Warren... what exactly are you planning to do about it?" an old woman said, walking over with a cane.
It was Lady Hawkins, the master of Ninety-Foot Peak. Though her own power wasn't the highest, she held an esteemed position in the Skyreach Sect. Rumor was, even the most powerful saints showed her deference, addressing her as Mistress Hua.
At her words, Warren froze. She was not someone he could afford to cross.
[SFX: REFEREE ANNOUNCEMENT]
But the image of Eliot, broken and bleeding in the arena, stoked his anger. The All-Peak Competition wasn't a deathmatch. Mack's cruelty was more than Warren could stomach. Just as he was about to argue, the referee's voice cut through the air, declaring Mack the winner. Master Warren's head snapped back to the stage. Eliot had passed out.
The rule of the competition was simple: if a fighter gave up or could no longer continue, they lost. With Eliot unconscious from his injuries, the referee had no choice.
[AMBIENT: SILENT CROWD]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
Shooting Mack one last, searing glare, Master Warren leaped into the arena. As the crowd watched in silence, he gathered Eliot into his arms and carried him away.
