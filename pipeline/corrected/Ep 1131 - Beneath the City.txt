Ep 1131 - Beneath the City

Word count: 1063

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS AMBIENT]
[AMBIENT: BUSY CITY STREET]
Lincoln had messaged Zane directions, so he and Freya made their way through Auspice City until they found themselves in one of the less glamorous districts on the outskirts.
The area was a jumble of people from all walks of life, mostly ordinary folks trying to get by. A few of the wealthier residents could afford to hire warriors as house guards, so you’d occasionally see some low-level fighters from the Soul Realm milling about. It wasn’t exactly a high-powered neighborhood. So when two warriors from the Soul Sea Realm showed up, they definitely turned some heads.
A group of young boys stopped their game to watch Zane and Freya pass, their eyes wide with admiration. The energy coming off the pair was a world away from the guards they were used to—it was stronger, sharper, almost magnetic. 
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
Drawn by that power, the boys started following them from a safe distance.
Freya stuck close to Zane, her brow furrowed. “What are we doing in a place like this?” she murmured, taking in the rundown streets.
A short walk later, they spotted a large manor that seemed out of place among the more modest buildings. 
[SFX: HEAVY DOOR OPENING]
A portly man, the landlord, greeted them at the entrance with a deferential dip of his head and quickly ushered them inside.
To most people, he was just another wealthy merchant. His entire fortune was probably only worth a thousand or two in supreme Aether crystals, an amount most warriors would laugh at. But for a regular person to have earned that much on their own was a massive achievement—it was about as much as a minor guild hall might spend in an entire year.
The landlord had no idea who Zane was, and neither did the handful of guards he’d hired. They couldn’t even begin to guess at Zane and Freya’s power level, let alone their identities. All the man knew was that these two were far beyond him, and he was sharp enough to understand what that meant. He always treated warriors with absolute respect, no matter their rank. Whenever they showed up, he would personally welcome them and send his own family to stay elsewhere in the city, leaving just a few servants behind. It was simple self-preservation. He knew that people with their kind of power could destroy decades of his hard work in a heartbeat, and there’d be nothing he could do about it.
[AMBIENT: DARK CELLAR WITH DRIPPING WATER]
Zane and Freya were led down to a room that looked like a cellar. 
[SFX: OIL LAMPS EXTINGUISHED]
The oil lamps on the walls had been extinguished, leaving the space in near-total darkness. The landlord had been given strict instructions not to light any of them, and he wasn’t about to disobey. He just wanted this to be over. Three generations of his family had built this place. He didn’t know what these people were after. He only knew he couldn’t afford to find out.
Beyond the cellar’s entrance, the space opened up. A round table sat at the center, and arranged around it were seven or eight figures, each doing their best to suppress their aura. They weren’t doing a perfect job. Zane caught the edges of what leaked through as he stepped inside, and a quiet watchfulness settled over him.
[SFX: CHAIR SCRAPING STONE]
“Brother,” Lincoln said from one side of the table, his voice easy despite the charged air. “Glad you made it. Sit down.”
[MUSIC: CONSPIRATORIAL STRINGS]
The reason for all the secrecy was simple: Lincoln had promised Adrian thirty percent of the treasures from the Dark World in exchange for help escaping. Adrian was brilliant, but he didn’t have the muscle to pull off something that big on his own, so he’d brought in seven of his so-called brothers.
These seven were independent warriors from the Upper World — and strong ones. When Zane and Freya took their seats, one of them didn’t bother with pleasantries.
“Adrian.” The man’s voice was flat, contemptuous. “Why did you bring these two along? What use are a pair of wastes to us?”
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
Lincoln’s eyes went hard.
Before he could answer, another one spoke up. “Thirty percent, split seven ways, was already an insult. Now you want to cut it further?”
“The agreement was thirty percent to Adrian’s group,” Lincoln said, his voice level. “The other seventy is mine to distribute. That’s what was agreed.”
“Yours to distribute.” A third man laughed, low and cold. “And what exactly qualifies you to keep seventy percent? You’re just opening a door.”
Lincoln leaned back. “Then find it yourself. The Underground Palace is right beneath this city. I’m sure men of your caliber won’t have any trouble.”
The room went very still.
Then one of them — the one who’d laughed — let his aura off the leash.
[SFX: LOW RUMBLING PRESSURE WAVE]
It hit the cellar like a fist.
[SFX: WALLS GROANING]
[SFX: GLASS SHATTERING]
The walls groaned. Above them, somewhere in the manor, something shattered. Every mortal within miles would have crumpled where they stood.
Lincoln didn’t move. Neither did Zane.
“Kill us,” Lincoln said quietly, “and good luck finding your way in.”
The aura held for a long moment. Then, slowly, it receded.
It was Adrian who broke the silence — a dry, unhurried laugh from the corner of the table.
He’d managed to de-escalate the tension, pouring oil on troubled waters until the open hostility in the room had simmered down. Of course, the seven brothers still didn’t want Zane there. He was a nobody. But Lincoln was the only one who knew how to get into the palace, and with Adrian’s careful persuasion, they had no choice but to bite their tongues for now.
It helped that Lincoln was no pushover. He had his own techniques — ones that meant a fight, if it came to that, wouldn’t be as one-sided as the seven brothers assumed. That knowledge sat quietly in the room, unspoken but understood.
[SFX: SERIOUS TONE]
“Look, the Underground Palace is way more complicated than you can imagine,” he said, his voice level despite the tension. “We need a solid plan before we go in. We can’t just rush in blind.”
He explained that they’d be up against some serious dark energy inside. 
[MUSIC: DETERMINED PREPARATION]
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY STREETS]
After they’d hashed out a strategy, the group left the mansion and fanned out across Auspice City to gather the supplies they’d need before regrouping at Lincoln’s designated spot.
Whatever was waiting for them in the dark beneath Auspice City, they were going to need every bit of it.
