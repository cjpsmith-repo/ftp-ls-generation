Ep 216 - Into the Fire
Word Count: 1096
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN FORGE]
[MUSIC: TENSE NEGOTIATION]
Harrison stared at the young man before him, utterly bewildered by his casual dismissal of what should have been impossible. The gathered disciples exchanged nervous glances, their earlier mockery transformed into uneasy silence.
Harrison cleared his throat, struggling to maintain his composure as a senior disciple. Whatever this outsider truly was, he clearly possessed abilities far beyond ordinary cultivation techniques.
This had to be a first. No disciple from Misty Peak had ever come all the way to Quenching Peak just to borrow a refining furnace.
Harrison had heard stories, of course---tales of gifted disciples who were as good with a hammer as they were with a sword. But those guys were always from the big noble houses with powerful families backing them. Besides, the furnaces the nobles used were way more advanced than anything on Quenching Peak. So why on earth would a disciple come here to borrow one?
"It's really not that complicated," Zane said, his voice perfectly calm. "I just need to borrow your furnace for a bit. Don't worry, I'm not forging anything---just performing an Infusion."
Zane's explanation only deepened Harrison's confusion, his brow furrowing as he processed the request.
An Infusion? The idea made absolutely no sense. From everything Harrison had learned throughout his years at Quenching Peak, Zane couldn't possibly be a weaponsmith, so how could he perform an Infusion of all things?
Infusion was the final, most sacred step in weaponsmithing---that pivotal moment when a true craftsman poured their profound understanding into a weapon, bestowing it with a grade and purpose. It constituted the very essence of Weaponsmithing itself. The depth of that understanding, cultivated through years of dedicated study and practice, ultimately determined a weapon's final power and potential.
There was a common saying among weaponsmiths: you can't build something from nothing. The truth behind these words had been proven countless times throughout history.
If Zane truly wasn't a weaponsmith, he couldn't possibly comprehend the intricacies of Celestial Law. And without that fundamental knowledge, how could he possibly Infuse anything?
The whole idea left Harrison utterly baffled, yet something in Zane's confident demeanor suggested he knew exactly what he was doing.
Harrison sighed, running his fingers through his hair as he considered the request. Despite his reservations, he saw no real harm in allowing this strange visitor to try. With a subtle flick of his wrist, he gestured for Zane to wait, curiosity gradually replacing his skepticism.
He'd see what this kid had up his sleeve, but only after Harrison himself had forged his own weapon.
[SFX: SMITHING SOUNDS]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STORYTELLING]
As Harrison worked, he remembered the old stories. One about a weaponsmith who spent a whole month on a single Infusion, eating and sleeping right in front of his furnace because he felt it wasn't perfect yet. After a month of non-stop work, he finally created a mythic weapon.
Then there was the other one, about a weaponsmith caught in a freak storm. With lightning crashing and winds howling, he only had thirty minutes for the Infusion. Against all odds, he also managed to create a mythic weapon.
Tales like these fostered misconceptions, creating the illusion that Weaponsmithing relied primarily on luck rather than craftsmanship.
In truth, mastery of technique far outweighed any element of chance. A skilled hand guided the metal's transformation, not blind luck or divine favor.
For an apprentice at Harrison's level, the Infusion process demanded patience and precision---usually between two and four hours for a single weapon. Each moment required absolute concentration, the slightest lapse potentially ruining the entire process.
Zane appeared perfectly at ease with the wait, staying out of the way to observe Harrison's work. His eyes tracked every movement with unusual intensity. Something in his focused gaze suggested he understood more about the process than he revealed.
Harrison felt the weight of that silent observation but found his hands steadier than usual, his movements more precise. The familiar heat of the furnace warmed his face as he worked, sweat beading on his brow while he channeled his energy into the forming weapon.
To Harrison's astonishment and barely concealed delight, the process flowed with unprecedented smoothness. The metal responded to his touch as if they shared a secret language. Barely an hour later, Harrison drew back the dancing flames with a flourish, his heart racing with pride.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TRIUMPH]
[SFX: EXCITED SHOUT]
"Yes! It's done!" Harrison yelled.
The other disciples from the Seventeenth Court, who had been watching from a distance, erupted in cheers. "Harrison, you did it!"
"Come on! Open the furnace! Let's see the first top-grade Mythic weapon from our court!" someone shouted, their voice thick with excitement.
[SFX: FURNACE OPENING]
Harrison strode over to the furnace and heaved the lid open. A blast of scorching air washed over him, and he had to channel fire along his arm to shield himself from the blistering heat.
[SFX: METAL SLIDING]
When he pulled his hand back, he was holding a brand-new sword.
[SFX: GLEAMING METAL]
Its silver blade caught the light, throwing off a brilliant gleam.
Harrison admired the sword. "A real top-grade Mythic weapon! I'm naming it Water-Seeker!" The other disciples swarmed him, all pushing to get a better look.
Amid the chaos, Zane walked slowly toward him. He smiled. "You did it, Harrison. A top-grade Mythic weapon."
Harrison noticed Zane gave the blade only a casual glance, seemingly unimpressed. "My Infusion is done," Harrison said, a little defensively. "So, when are you going to use the furnace for yours?"
"Right now," Zane replied, calm as ever.
[SFX: METAL CLANKING]
Harrison stepped aside, gesturing for Zane to go ahead. Ignoring the glares and confused murmurs from the other disciples, Zane placed his own sword into the refining furnace.
[SFX: FURNACE ROARING]
"Zane, shouldn't you be channeling fire?" Harrison asked, his confusion mounting. He'd just noticed something else---Zane had plunged his bare hands right into the blistering heat of the furnace and wasn't burned at all.
"Oh, it's not that hot," Zane replied with an easy smile. "I don't need any fire."
Harrison was speechless.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
He knew Zane was powerful---he'd felt it firsthand with the Vitality transfer---but this was something else entirely. The furnace was connected to underground lava; he'd just used its fire to Infuse a weapon. How could Zane possibly say it wasn't hot?
You could probably roast a pig in there in two seconds flat.
But Zane's arms were completely fine after he put the sword inside. Not a single mark.
How was that even possible?
