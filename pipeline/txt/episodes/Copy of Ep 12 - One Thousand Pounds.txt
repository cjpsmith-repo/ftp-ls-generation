Ep 12 - One Thousand Pounds
Word Count: 1220
[SFX: COMBAT SOUNDS]
What settled the minds of the House Lowell kids was that Zane had been their personal punching bag for two years. Since he was still alive and kicking, they just figured he was unbelievably tough.
Lucky for Zane, these kids weren't paid to think about how a punching bag could take that much abuse. Their job was to practice hitting people, not to study them. Even if Zane had some kind of special defense, they couldn't have cared less.
[SFX: BATH WATER SPLASHING]
[MUSIC: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
Meanwhile, more and more toxins were being purged from Zane's body. Every time he took a bath, the water that spilled onto the floor was almost as thick as milk. His plan was working. The amount of toxins flushed from his system each day was what a normal person might get rid of in a month.
The thought made Zane grin; every day of his training was as effective as a month for anyone else. His progress felt like a damn miracle.
Fueled by this, Zane pushed himself to the absolute limit. He volunteered for beatings during the day and spent his nights practicing, never stopping for a second. The changes in his body and his growing understanding of martial arts lit a fire in his soul. He was barely sleeping, but his eyes were always clear and sharp.
[SFX: CRACKLING FIRE]
[SFX: MYSTICAL TRANSFORMATION]
Another reason for his boundless energy was the mysterious Dragon's Breath Forge. The forge was purifying his soul, and each time it did, the pain was just as searing as the last. It felt like his soul was being ground into dust and rebuilt a thousand times over. He'd collapse, praying for it to end. And yet, when the agony subsided and he realized he'd survived, his spirit soared. He knew his soul was being tempered, too.
Of all the parts of the human body, the soul was the hardest to train. Anyone could strengthen their body, even people who have never practiced martial arts. The soul, however, was a different story—tucked away in some secret place, invisible and untouchable.
As the Path of Serenity taught, the soul was The Shimmering Shore, a place most people could never hope to reach.
Supposedly, the Skyreach Sect possessed advanced methods that could influence the soul, like those in the Forbidden Grimoire. But those were heights Zane couldn't even imagine reaching, and he pushed the thought away. He had to focus on the here and now: refining his body, day after day.
[MUSIC: TIME PASSING MONTAGE]
Time flew. It had already been twenty days since he'd taken down Grey and Darren.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
[SFX: CRACKING STONE]
His fist slammed into the thick stone wall. Zane froze, stunned. He'd only used about seventy percent of his strength, but the impact had sent cracks spiderwebbing across the cellar wall. He knew that if he'd gone all out, the whole thing might have come down. The thought was a little terrifying, but it also brought a smile to his face. It had been less than a month since he started, and he'd improved this much.
When he began, Zane had just entered the Bone Refining Level. Now, after such a short time, he felt like he'd already reached his peak. The results of strengthening his bones were obvious in the sheer power of his strikes. The force behind his punches was now close to a thousand pounds.
[MUSIC: EPIC FANTASY THEME]
One thousand pounds. That was the weight of an Ancient Cauldron. For warriors, achieving this level of Immense Power was a major milestone. Only then could you officially call yourself a true warrior.
And Zane had done it in less than a month. That kind of speed was unheard of in House Lowell. You'd be hard-pressed to find anyone that gifted in the entire Empire.
After all, purifying the body by sweating out toxins was a process that could take decades, since the body only released a tiny amount each day.
But purifying it by taking a beating every day? That was a thousand times faster.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]
Today, when Zane went to the Training Hall, he noticed something was off. The other kids seemed hesitant to use him as a punching bag, but after he insisted, they finally let loose with a barrage of punches.
[SFX: PUNCHES]
Zane felt nothing. It was like their strength had completely vanished. He was about to tell them to hit harder, but he held his tongue. He watched them for a moment and realized they weren't holding back at all; they were swinging with every ounce of strength they had.
So why couldn't he feel it?
Worse, each punch generated far less warmth in his body. Where their hits used to create a rush of heat the size of his thumb, now it was barely the size of his pinky.
With less heat to purify him, Zane started to worry that the whole process was becoming less effective.
[AMBIENT: QUIET CELLAR]
[MUSIC: TENSE UNDERTONE]
That evening, back in the cellar, he watched the toxins seep out of his skin. His fears were confirmed. The amount being expelled from his body had been cut in half.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT QUICKENING]
A knot of anxiety tightened in his chest. He had no idea what was happening. Was it possible his body was just getting too tough?
According to the Advanced Forging Theory, his body's durability was now on par with a Basic Enchanted Weapon. Through constant refinement, a weapon's strength would gradually increase.
But once it hit a certain point, basic forging methods stopped working.
Most of the students in the Training Hall were only at the Skin Refining Level.
Even Melvin, whose natural power put him on par with someone at the Strength Level, was still in that group. The hundreds of pounds of force they were throwing at him just weren't enough anymore. The effect on his body was negligible.
It looked like he needed stronger people to beat him up if he wanted to keep refining his body and getting results. But Zane had no idea how to make that happen. He couldn't exactly walk up to the family elders and ask them to use him as a training dummy.
[MUSIC: ANTICIPATION BUILDING]
Family Training Day was only a few days away.
On that day, all the students would go all out. The punches would definitely be stronger then.
More importantly, if he could just get through that day, he'd earn his freedom and could finally leave House Lowell.
Since he could barely feel the Lowell kids' punches anymore, he wasn't worried about getting hurt.
[AMBIENT: QUIET CELLAR]
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
Zane sat in his cellar, the dim lamplight glinting in his bright, determined eyes as he went over his plans.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REMEMBRANCE]
It had been two years since his family was framed, two years he'd spent as nothing more than a quiet slave. But he had never forgotten the searing hatred he felt when his father was murdered. He had swallowed his pride and lived in shame because he was too weak to do anything else.
Now, by some miracle, he had the Theories of Alchemy, and they were transforming him.
[MUSIC: RESOLUTE DETERMINATION]
He had to seize this chance.
