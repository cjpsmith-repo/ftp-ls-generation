Ep 1184 - Showing Off

Word count: 1070

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
It was the kind of thing you just didn’t see happen. 
[SFX: MASSIVE STELLAR IMPLOSION]
A star, more than eighty thousand miles in diameter, had just been… refined. Gone. Only a Supreme Lord could pull off a feat like that.
They were so powerful that not a single grain of sand could escape their heightened senses. So when they finally noticed this particular grain, they were genuinely surprised.
The Supreme Lords had seen the little Elysian Keep before but hadn’t given it a second thought, dismissing it as just another spatial treasure. But now, looking closer at this tiny speck of sand, they realized their mistake. It wasn’t just any treasure. 
It was the *only* thing that had survived the tempering of Supreme Lord Armond’s fire.
Though they couldn’t see the secrets hidden inside the grain of sand, it was clear that the tiny speck *was* the little Elysian Keep—its very essence. 
[SFX: WHOOSHING MOVEMENT]
It floated in the void for a moment, then shot off to the north.
[SFX: SPATIAL TEAR]
One of the Supreme Lords just smirked, stretched out a hand, and ripped a hole in the fabric of space.
[SFX: DIMENSIONAL COLLAPSE]
The grain of sand was immediately sucked into the tear, and as the surrounding void folded in on itself, its power easily trapped the speck inside.
“A spatial treasure this powerful is something else,” another Lord said, shaking his head.
It takes a lot to get a Supreme Lord excited. Most wouldn’t even blink if a Divine-Class weapon appeared right in front of them. But this Elysian Keep was different. It was packed with so many mysteries that even *they* couldn’t figure it out, and it had withstood Armond’s fire.
Naturally, they all wanted it. Their original plan—grab the Crown of Destiny and the Roaring Token, then destroy the treasure—went right out the window. An artifact this rare was meant to be owned.
“Your name is Zane, isn’t it?” one of the Supreme Lords called out, his voice echoing in the void. “You can come out now. The Saya Clan may not be a major power, but if you hand over that spatial treasure and the Crown of Destiny, we can guarantee your safety.”
“Safety?” Supreme Lord Ranell of the Ogres scoffed. “Ha. Kid, you’re only at the Soul Sea Realm. I’ll make an exception and take you on as my disciple. All you have to do is give me that Crown of Destiny and this shapeshifting Elysian Keep.”
The Queen of Punishment listened in silence. Every other Lord here wanted the Crown and the Keep — that was their game. Her game was different. Her goal was Lavender. If Zane struck a deal with any one of these Lords, Lavender might walk away alive. That was the worst outcome she could imagine. She was furious — but with only Gunter at her side, there was nothing she could do against this many. She kept her mouth shut and waited.
[MUSIC: DARK FOREBODING STRINGS]
A moment passed. Then Zane’s voice rang out through the void, easy and unhurried.
“I’m truly honored. But I have other uses for the Crown of Destiny. If you want me to hand it over — keep dreaming.”
The Lords’ eyes went cold.
“Useless to talk to him,” Addison said, black smoke already billowing behind him. He reached out toward the grain of sand.
“There’s no need for you, Addison.” The Lord who had already trapped the grain cut him off. He raised both hands, pressed his thumb and index finger together, and fixed his gaze on the tiny speck. 
[SFX: CRACK]
A crack split the space around it — the same technique he had used against the Queen of Punishment. Anything touched by it was torn apart. In the universe, only the Saint Sky Armor and a handful of other supreme divine artifacts had ever resisted it.
The crack reached the grain of sand and passed over it.
The Lord lowered his hands. He stared.
The grain was untouched. Floating quietly. As if nothing had happened at all.
For the first time in tens of thousands of years, something in this universe had genuinely surprised him.
Another Lord stepped forward, shaking his head. “Looks like you’re out of tricks, Old Man.” 
Two weapons appeared in his hands — a long thin awl of dark metal and a heavy, broad-faced hammer. Both ranked on the Cosmic Spirit Index. He set the tip of the awl against the grain of sand, raised the hammer, and brought it down hard on the awl’s tail.
[SFX: SINGLE RESONANT STRIKE]
The sound it produced wasn’t loud. But it traveled. It spread across half the starry sky in an instant. Warriors cultivating on distant stars — a trillion miles away — felt it land in their chests like a physical blow. Some spat blood. The weakest among them didn’t survive it. Tens of millions of lives, gone, from a single strike’s echo alone.
At the tip of the awl, a finger-sized spark bloomed — and faded.
The grain of sand was still a grain of sand.
Twenty Supreme Lords stood in silence.
[MUSIC: DARK FOREBODING STRINGS]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
“I’ll handle this,” Supreme Lord Ranell declared, stepping forward. As a mid-rank Supreme Lord, he was on another level entirely from the others who had already tried.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUMMING]
He held a saber—the Imperial Mystic Bone Saber. This wasn’t just any blade; it was forged from the last Mysterious Bone Demon in the universe and ranked twenty-seventh on the Cosmic Spirit Index. The Mysterious Bone Demons had a unique, terrifying talent: they could disrupt the very Vitality between heaven and earth and absorb it endlessly. It was an ability so destabilizing that the heavens themselves couldn’t tolerate their existence, which was why the race had been exterminated. The Ogres’ master weaponsmith had captured the last living one and forged this saber from its remains.
[SFX: BLADE SLASH]
[SFX: ENERGY TEARING]
Ranell slashed out. 
[SFX: VIOLENT ENERGY SURGE]
A crescent of pure light tore through the sky, and in its wake, the very energy of the universe went haywire, surging violently in every direction. The sheer power contained in that single strike was something no ordinary martial artist could even begin to comprehend.
[SFX: WHOOSHING BLADE]
In an instant, the beam of light from the saber shot straight for the grain of sand.
