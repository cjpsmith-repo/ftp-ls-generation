Ep 550 - Why Are You Crying?

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]
Zane had seriously underestimated the immortal’s power.
Up until now, he’d managed to block a few attacks, relying on his unique body. Sure, it was easy enough to pierce his skin or leave small cuts, but actually tearing him apart? That was a whole different story.
Take Maddox, the number one warrior in The Heartlands. He could probably shatter a top-grade mythic sword because it’s thin and brittle. But what if it were a top-grade mystic metal pillar, thick as a tree trunk? Maddox might be able to punch a hole through it, but he’d never be able to snap it in two.
Zane's body was basically that pillar. You can imagine the terrifying force it would take to actually break him.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
But the sheer killing intent radiating from the immortal was beyond anything Zane could have comprehended.
The immense pressure tore at the flesh on his arms. His bones, harder than steel, began to splinter under the strain. What was even more terrifying was that the force wasn't just crushing him—it was piercing straight through his back, like a giant, invisible hand was trying to squeeze him into pulp.
[SFX: BONE CRACKS]
His spine began to fracture under the unbearable weight.
[MUSIC: TRAGIC STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
Even knowing he stood no chance against the immortal's destructive power, Tia couldn’t stop the tears from streaming down her face as she watched him struggle. "Zane!"
At her core, Tia was just a normal woman. Yes, she had somehow become one of the top warriors in The Heartlands, but that was never her dream. Her warrior spirit had only awakened after the Cloud Hall discovered her talent. All she’d ever really wanted was a quiet life. She just longed for someone to be there—someone she could bicker with when she was upset, someone who’d remind her to wear a jacket when it got cold.
Maybe Zane was that person. The one who could bring that warmth into her life. The thought shattered her as she watched the man she cared for being crushed to death.
But for everyone else, it wasn't grief—it was stupefaction.
They'd all seen what had happened to Elder Luna. A Life-and-Death Realm expert, erased without resistance. His body was crushed by an invisible force and wiped from existence.
And now Zane—a Soul Realm warrior—was using his bare body to fight back against that same power.
"This is a guaranteed kill. The rules of the game demand it. And he's actually resisting it?"
"How tough is his body? The Mysterious Luna Hall leader got wiped clean. This guy's still standing!"
"He can't hold on. Nobody can hold on against that."
[MUSIC: DESPERATE STRUGGLE]
[SFX: STRAINED GROWL]
Zane clenched his teeth. He was pulling everything he had—dragon scale power flooding through him, the Vital Spirit buried deep in his body surging to the surface. Both at once, fighting the invisible hand.
But the Annihilation power was pushing back harder. If he drew on any more dragon scales, combined with the Vital Spirit and the crushing force already bearing down on him, his body would give out before the power did.
"AAARGH!"
[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]
A manic roar tore from his throat. The muscles across his shoulders, chest, and back split open. Blood shot out in jets under the pressure, spraying against the barrier around him, misting the air red before dripping onto the disc below.
Tia pressed her hand flat against the barrier, a faint icy mist drifting across her face. She watched. And somewhere beneath the grief, a small, stubborn flicker of hope refused to go out.
Maybe. If it was anyone else, no. But if it was Zane—maybe.
She'd seen too many miracles from him. She wanted to close her eyes. Maybe when she opened them, he'd be standing.
She didn't close them.
She watched his bones crack. Watched his body warp and twist under the invisible hand, distorted into something unrecognizable. More blood. More fractures.
"You didn't even get to save your sister," she murmured—and the grief on her face slowly hardened into something else.
[MUSIC: BUILDING CONFUSION]
The crowd stirred.
"Wait—why hasn't he disappeared?"
"Elder Luna's vanished completely. But Zane's body is still there. It's destroyed, but it's still there."
"Is his body just… too hard to erase?"
Nobody had an answer.
[SFX: MAGICAL EXPLOSION]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL RISE]
Suddenly, a brilliant curtain of light erupted around Zane, slamming into the invisible hand.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS BOOM]
[SFX: EARTHQUAKE RUMBLE]
[AMBIENT: CRUMBLING STONE]
The shield of light fought violently against the immortal’s killing power. The entire chasm shook from the force of their clash. The floating discs everyone stood on, and even the chasm walls themselves, trembled violently.
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]
The warriors of the Life-and-Death Realm grew pale, feeling the overwhelming energy wash over them.
What kind of power was Zane using to fight an immortal?
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
This power wasn't his. It was coming from the protective amulet Madame Vermillion, the leader of the Dragonfall Palace, had given him. Just like the immortal’s attack, this was a technique from the upper world.
[AMBIENT: SHIMMERING MAGICAL BARRIER]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CHOIR]
Lying beneath the shimmering light, Zane was in total shock. He’d never imagined that one small amulet could unleash such incredible force. It was essentially taking his place, absorbing every bit of the damage meant for him, and completely neutralizing the destructive power.
[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
But the immortal's attack was relentless, programmed not to stop until Zane was dead.
[SFX: FADING ENERGY CLASH]
The clash raged for a few more moments before the strength of the invisible hand finally began to fade, and the chaos started to die down.
[SFX: CRYSTAL SHATTERING]
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]
At last, the curtain of light around Zane dissolved. The amulet from Madame Vermillion shattered into dust. It was a one-time-use item; it couldn't protect him forever.
[AMBIENT: QUIET AFTERMATH]
[MUSIC: GENTLE EMOTIONAL STRINGS]
Zane pushed himself back to his feet, ignoring everything else. He just looked at Tia and smiled.
"Why are you crying?"
