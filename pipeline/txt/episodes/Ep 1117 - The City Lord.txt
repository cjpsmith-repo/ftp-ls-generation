Ep 1117 - The City Lord

Word count: 1031

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: QUIET TOWN SQUARE]
Nobody started trouble in Clearhaven. It might have been just another small town in the Upper World, but it had the full backing of the Soulbound Sect. Picking a fight here was a fast way to get on the bad side of the Thirty-six Halls, and by extension, the Sect itself. And its Saint Lord was a being far beyond the power of any World Lord.
One of the guards stepped forward, eyes hard. “Just what do you think you’re doing?”
“What do you think?” Zane asked, a faint smile playing on his lips. “Is it not clear? I’m taking this woman with me.” He turned to Freya. “Let’s go.”
His simple confidence sent a jolt through her. After everything he must have been through, he was still here for her. The thought was so overwhelming it almost hurt. But it was impossible. Even if he’d broken through to the Soul Sea Realm, he couldn’t fight a whole city.
“Zane, you have to go,” Freya whispered, shaking her head. “I can’t leave.” A wave of regret washed over her. Why had she used the Message Rune? It had been a desperate, hopeless prayer. But he was the only person she knew in this entire Upper World, and sending for him had felt better than doing nothing. The chances he’d even get it were practically zero, yet somehow, he had. And for what?
Patience had run out. The four guards closed in, surrounding Zane completely as their intense gazes demanded his surrender. 
[SFX: SWORD DRAWN]
[SFX: WEAPON HUM]
The leader’s look was glacial as he drew a silver shortsword that hummed with a dark energy. 
“I gave you a chance to walk,” he said, his voice flat. “You’re not leaving now.”
[MUSIC: URGENT BATTLE THEME]
[AMBIENT: COMBAT RUSH]
The moment the words were out, all four lunged.
The leader closed the distance in an instant, shortsword driving straight for Zane’s chest — no room to dodge, no time to think. But Zane didn’t dodge. His figure swayed almost imperceptibly, and the blade passed clean through him as though he weren’t there at all. He was still standing in exactly the same spot, still smiling — except now there was something cold behind his eyes.
[SFX: SWORD SING]
His hand came up. A long sword materialized in his grip. One motion — barely a flicker — and gray shapes erupted outward in every direction.
[SFX: FOUR RAPID IMPACTS]
All four guards left the ground at once.
[SFX: CRASH INTO STONE WALL]
They came down hard — scattered across the street, one of them skidding straight into the wall of the residence. None of them got up.
Freya couldn’t move. She’d been watching from the balcony, braced for the worst, and now she stood there with her mouth open. Four Soul Sea Realm warriors. One move. She hadn’t even been able to track what he’d done.
“Zane.” Her voice came out unsteady. “You need to leave. Right now. This is Soulbound Sect territory — it doesn’t matter how strong you are, you can’t fight the whole Sect—”
He didn’t look up at her. Instead, he turned toward the residence, raised the sword again, and swept it in a clean arc. Sword light rippled out in a wave.
[SFX: RESTRICTIONS DETONATING]
The divine textures woven around the building came apart with a series of sharp cracks, spitting black smoke as they collapsed. The entrance was clear.
For a moment, Freya just stared. The restrictions that had kept her locked inside for days — gone in a single slash. She looked at Zane, then at the Heavenly Phoenix Horse waiting behind him, and something shifted in her chest. The despair that had settled over her like stone began, just slightly, to loosen.
“Okay,” he said simply, extending a hand. “Let’s go.”
She didn’t hesitate.
[SFX: SWIFT MOVEMENT]
[SFX: HORSE WHINNY]
He pulled her onto the Heavenly Phoenix Horse and swung up behind her.
He slapped the horse’s neck, and it let out a sharp cry that echoed off the stone buildings before launching itself into the sky, its hooves finding purchase on nothing but air.
The climb felt almost gentle at first, but within seconds, they were soaring. 
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER FLARE]
Just as they broke ten thousand feet, a massive net of shimmering light flared to life, stretching across the entire sky and blocking their path.
[SFX: PANICKED VOICE]
“It’s a No-Fly Zone,” Freya choked out, her voice tight with panic.
The barrier didn’t seem to faze Zane in the slightest. He drew his sword, its light rippling out like water as he slashed at the net. 
[SFX: SWORD DRAWN]
[SFX: METALLIC CLANGS]
The impacts rang out with a series of loud, metallic clangs, but the glowing threads didn’t even fray. A flicker of confusion crossed Zane’s face. That shouldn’t have happened.
[SFX: DEEP BOOMING LAUGH]
As he tried to figure out why his attacks were useless, a deep, booming laugh echoed from somewhere below.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS CONFRONTATION]
[AMBIENT: TENSE STILLNESS]
“Well, now, this is a first,” a voice declared, dripping with amusement. “Who dares cause such a ruckus in *my* city?”
[SFX: WHOOSHING LIGHT STREAKS]
[AMBIENT: CRACKLING CRIMSON AURA]
Ten streaks of light shot up to meet them. The man at their head was cloaked in a blood-red aura, his robes the same crimson color. His eyes glowed with a fierce, unsettling power, like someone who had just come into a dark and terrible new strength.
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
As the man drew closer, Zane felt Freya go rigid behind him. Her face was chalk-white. “Uncle…” she breathed.
[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION STRINGS]
Zane’s eyes narrowed, his expression becoming deadly calm as he studied the man. “A Spirit Supreme Realm master,” he stated, more than asked. “That’s your uncle?”
“Yes,” Freya nodded numbly. “And he’s the lord of Clearhaven.”
Just as the words left her mouth, the man in the blood-red robe came to a halt before them. He gave Zane a dismissive glance, but then his eyes landed on the Heavenly Phoenix Horse. They widened, and a flicker of raw greed lit up his face.
