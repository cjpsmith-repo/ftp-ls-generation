Ep 1008 - Sovereign Stones

Word count: 1019

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The black mountain range loomed before them, silent and immovable, and the weight of what it meant was just beginning to sink in.

They had to face it—every last one of them might be a powerful Soul Sea Realm warrior, but this wasn’t their turf. Here, they had to play by someone else’s rules.

After a long moment, one of the warriors stepped forward. “So, if I pay a million supreme Aether crystals… I can skip the service?”

The armored man replied, “Not exactly. This isn’t about an exemption. The whole point of the service is to mine Aether crystals. If you can front the cost, you’re free to go.”

[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]

[AMBIENT: CRYSTAL RESONANCE]

With a light tap, a river of Aether crystals cascaded to the ground, piling up into a small hill right in front of them.

[SFX: SHIMMERING HUM]

They glittered like diamonds, pure and translucent, humming with an almost impossibly rich energy. There was no mistaking it—every single one was a supreme Aether crystal.

Even for newcomers used to incredible wealth, the sight of that shimmering mountain of crystals made their hearts pound.

“There. One million supreme Aether crystals,” the warrior said. “Am I free to go?”

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

The armored man gave a satisfied nod, but then his expression shifted. “A million supreme crystals are enough to join the Soulbound Sect,” he said, shaking his head slightly. “But to leave as a free warrior… that’s a different story.”

The warrior didn’t flinch. He produced a second Storage Ring and dumped another mountain alongside the first. Then he turned and pointed to the man behind him. “Him too.” The armored man waved them through. The two left without a backward glance. The rest watched in silence — envy, and the quiet sting of knowing they couldn’t do the same.

[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Zane put it together: Sovereign Stones were the real prize. A hundred of those would buy his freedom. The only question was whether he could actually find them in the dark, imposing mountain range looming ahead.

He’d left a considerable portion of his crystals behind in Cloud Hall when he ascended, bringing only around two hundred thousand with him. Not close to enough. The Black Star Pickax was worth more than any quota they could name — but he wasn’t selling it.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

The armored men herded the warriors to a central gathering point. Escape was a fantasy—Spirit Supreme Realm masters were stationed all around, their presence a silent, suffocating threat.

[SFX: PICKAXES CLANGING]

[AMBIENT: MINING CAMP]

Off to the side, a few warriors flew in, pickaxes slung over their shoulders. They dumped the Aether crystals they’d mined, had their hauls tallied, and then shot right back out. It was a relentless cycle of digging and returning, each of them desperate to earn their freedom and get the hell out.

As the newcomers shuffled into the gathering place, they were issued the same gear as everyone else: a pickax and a token.

No one wanted to be a miner, but they all clung to the same thin hope of a quick exit. They were thinking just like Zane: if a hundred Sovereign Stones was the price of freedom, then their mission was simple—find them, and fast.

[SFX: CRYSTALS DUMPING]

[AMBIENT: BUSY CAMP]

Just as the new arrivals were about to set out, a group of more than a hundred warriors flew back to the gathering place. They dumped out their day’s haul of Aether crystals and lined up to turn them in.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]

But Zane’s brow furrowed when he saw the quality of their take. The pile was huge, but it was mostly low-quality, mid-grade crystals. He spotted only a handful of top-grade and supreme ones mixed in, and not a single Sovereign Stone in the whole lot.

Zane approached one of the miners. “Hey, man,” he started, “why are you guys only digging up mid-grade stuff? It must take forever to get enough of these to buy your way out. Wouldn’t it be faster to just hunt for Sovereign Stones?”

[AMBIENT: DEEP MINE CAVERN]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The math was staggering. Their target was a hundred Sovereign Stones—the equivalent of a million supreme crystals. A single supreme crystal was worth a hundred top-grade ones, which meant they’d need to mine a hundred million top-grade crystals to get free. The pile these guys just brought in was barely a drop in the bucket.

The other warriors who’d arrived with Zane were clearly thinking the same thing. They all looked over, waiting for the miner’s answer.

To their surprise, the miner just shook his head, a bitter smile twisting his lips. “What else are we supposed to mine? You can’t just dig up what isn’t there.” He gave Zane and the others a knowing look. “You’re all new, huh?”

“Sovereign Stones are only found at the deepest levels,” the miner continued. “Level eleven, level twelve beasts down there — Soul Sea Realm strength or above. The Sect’s own warriors won’t risk it. That’s why they need us.” He shrugged. “I’d rather spend fifty years on ordinary crystals than lose my life in a week.”

[MUSIC: SOMBER PIANO]

Zane had been planning to go straight for the Sovereign Stones himself. But hearing how rare—and dangerous—the hunt was, the other newcomers lost their nerve. They decided to stick to mining the regular Aether crystals. It was slower, but at least it was safer.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON GRAVEL]

[AMBIENT: DESOLATE MOUNTAIN WIND]

One by one, they trudged off to start their work. The sooner they started, the sooner they could leave this godforsaken mountain. No one wanted to be stuck here a second longer than they had to be.

Freya clutched her new pickax, her face a mask of sullen despair. She’d clearly been expecting her uncle to rescue her, but no one had come. Now, with no other choice, she followed the others toward the mines, looking utterly defeated.

[SFX: WHOOSH OF FLIGHT]

[MUSIC: DETERMINED ORCHESTRAL]

Zane studied the mountain range in the distance. He stashed his token and the standard-issue pickax, then took to the air, flying straight for it.

The old miner’s warning about the danger of finding Sovereign Stones echoed in his mind, but Zane wasn’t about to give up. He had a divine-class weapon made for mining. And he was damn well going to use it to buy his freedom.
