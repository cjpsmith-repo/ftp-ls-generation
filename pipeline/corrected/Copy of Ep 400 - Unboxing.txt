Ep 400 - Unboxing

Word count: 1179

[AMBIENT: TREASURE VAULT]

[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS STRINGS]

Tia and Zane had agreed on a fifty-fifty split of whatever they found.

[SFX: TREASURE BOX OPENING]

They started with the red treasure boxes. Each one held a dark item: Soul Essence, refined from a Nether Phantom. It was valuable stuff—you could sell it for a hell of a price or use it to strengthen your soul with zero side effects. Zane’s share came out to seven.

After they put the red boxes away, they moved on to the blue ones. These contained something even rarer: the Lifeblood of the Nine-Headed Dragon. Out of the six blue treasure boxes, Zane got three.

[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]

Zane’s excitement buzzed under his skin as he eyed his growing pile of red and blue boxes. He couldn’t wait to see what was in the rest and how much he’d get. Then, he just… stared at Tia, not saying a word.

[MUSIC: CONFLICTED EMOTION]

"What are you staring at?" Tia snapped. She had a short fuse to begin with, and a shirtless Zane just gawking at her was starting to get on her last nerve. She forced herself to take a breath and focus. Even though she’d promised to split everything down the middle, a bitter pang of regret hit her. If it had been any other disciple, Zane wouldn’t be getting half of anything.

But Tia knew how to see the bigger picture. She could look past a few material treasures for the good of Cloud Hall. Besides, in her eyes, Zane himself was the most priceless treasure Cloud Hall could ever hope to find.

Finally, his eyes still locked on her, Zane spoke. “With all this stuff, I think my Storage Ring’s about to max out.” He gestured helplessly at his hand.

The Storage Ring he was using was his original one. Evil Jace’s ring had been bigger, but it had always been more than enough for Zane’s needs—until now. With this massive haul, he’d be hard-pressed to fit everything inside.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

A silver streak cut through the air in front of him. Tia had tossed him a Storage Ring.

Zane caught it, his eyes widening as he felt its capacity. It was huge.

Next came the silver boxes. Tia opened all three — each one held a soul jade slip recording one of the Blood Fiend Emperor's cultivation techniques.

Zane already had the Malevolent Cycle of Life and Death. He had no use for more. He slid all three across to Tia without a word.

She didn't argue. Even she couldn't cultivate them — but as rewards for Cloud Hall elders, they were extraordinary.

[SFX: RUSTLING ITEMS]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]

As they continued sorting through the gold boxes, they found a strange stone carving. An average student would’ve probably mistaken it for a regular rock, maybe even picked it up and tossed it aside without a second thought.

But Tia noticed the way Zane was looking at it. It was different. He wasn’t just looking; he was studying it, like he knew exactly what it was. Still, she was tired of his whole act. She scoffed, assuming he was just pretending. There was no way he could actually comprehend Spatial Laws.

Considering his current level, it was already incredible that he’d mastered the Apex of Sword Intent and could give his sword spirit a human form. On top of that, Tia had sensed his soul was far stronger than others at his rank. But even with all that, grasping Spatial Laws this easily? Impossible.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

At first, Tia watched him with pure scorn. But her expression quickly melted from ridicule to slack-jawed astonishment. It was unbelievable.

Zane hadn't shown off his control over space once since starting the Death Road. He’d only used it a single time to grab the silver treasure box, and only Toby and a few Sect leaders had seen it on the Scrying Slate. Tia had been completely in the dark.

Until now.

[SFX: MAGICAL RESONANCE]

As the light from the stone carving began to dim, she realized he wasn't faking it. He was actually absorbing the Spatial Laws from the stone.

It was just like with the blue stone carving back at Cloud Hall. The power held within the stones wasn't infinite. A fully charged carving gave off a faint light, but as an elder meditated on it, drawing out the power, that light would fade. Once it was drained, the stone had to recharge on its own before anyone could use it again. It was why Cloud Hall had such strict time limits for using them.

The same thing was happening right here, with this defective gold carving. As Zane focused, the golden light was fading. It could only mean one thing: he was successfully absorbing the Spatial Laws.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CONTEMPLATION]

A moment ago, Tia had been ready to lay into him. Now, all thoughts of mocking him evaporated. She was just floored by his talent, more impressed than ever. Deep down, she was completely recalibrating what she thought this young man was capable of.

When she first met Zane, she’d written him off as just a top talent from a second-rate sect. His training was decent, but he was just one promising kid among many. After all, everyone who qualified for the Death Road had potential. Frank was the most impressive of the bunch, but guys like Finn, Patrick, and Lewis were no slouches either.

But after Zane unleashed his Titan Strike and demonstrated his perfect sword intent, she realized he might even eclipse Frank.

The power he possessed was beyond anything she could have imagined. And now, seeing him grasp Spatial Laws… Tia knew he was something else entirely.

Another gold box held an array pattern—a complex defensive formation designed to protect an entire sect. Deploying it would be a monumental task. Tia showed it to Zane, explaining what it was.

He didn't argue when she claimed it. A powerful tool for a sect, sure, but it had no personal use for him.

Now, only one unopened box remained: the black one.

Tia’s hand trembled slightly. The gold boxes had held items that dwarfed everything they’d found before. Turning to this final, solitary black box, her expectations were sky-high. What could possibly be more valuable than what was in the gold boxes?

She examined it first. It looked… odd. All the other boxes had been red, blue, white, or gold. This was the only box that was pure black. If the Emperor had left some kind of trap or curse inside, opening it could be a death sentence for them both.

But when Tia carefully lifted the lid, all they saw was a dark, simple oil lamp nestled inside.

She pulled it out, turning it over in her hands. It looked like nothing, just a cheap lamp you might find in any home. Zane leaned in for a closer look as she held it, a flicker of confusion on his face. Then he saw Tia’s eyes go wide, her breath catching in her throat.

"This is… the Enchanted Lamp from the Celestial Tomb of Vesper!"
