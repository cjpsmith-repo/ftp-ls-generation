Ep 271 - All Yours
Word Count: 1032
[AMBIENT: CROWDED ARENA]
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
Jill and Tracy stood in the center of the arena, commanding the attention of every spectator in the packed stands.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
Murmurs rippled through the male contingent of the crowd. Both women were strikingly beautiful, and they also each had a reputation for being ruthless competitors. This irresistible combination explained why this match had generated such intense anticipation.
"Who’s your money on?" asked one spectator, leaning toward his companion.
"Probably Tracy," came the reply. "She’s one of the master’s personal students, plus she has several years’ experience over Jill. Her technique is practically flawless."
Another voice cut in, "Who cares about technique? I just hope they really get after it. Maybe they’ll tear each other’s clothes off or something! I’d pay good money to see one of them half-naked!"
The commentary from the men grew increasingly boisterous, their remarks deteriorating into vulgarity as they abandoned all pretense of discretion. These crude observations echoed across the arena, reaching the combatants with perfect clarity. Jill merely tilted her head, shooting the offenders a withering glance.
Tracy, on the other hand, felt her blood begin to boil. Known for her volatile temperament, she clenched her fists at her sides, knuckles whitening as she struggled to maintain composure. Her eyes narrowed dangerously, scanning the crowd for the loudest offenders.
While most students from The Thirty-Three Peaks knew Tracy only by reputation, she had earned notoriety among elite fighters not just for her technical brilliance but for her merciless combat style—coupled with a temper that outmatched even the most volatile male competitors. Anyone foolish enough to dismiss her as some delicate flower inevitably discovered their error through painful, often humiliating lessons.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON PLATFORM]
Tracy slowly walked to the edge of the arena and hopped down.
"Wait, why’d she leave the platform? Where’s she going?"
"A fighter isn’t supposed to leave before the match is over, right? Is she forfeiting?"
[MUSIC: THREATENING BUILDUP]
"Uh oh, Bastian, I think you’re doomed. She’s breaking all the rules and heading right for us. We were way too loud with our commentary!" one of Bastian’s companions hissed, shrinking back in his seat.
[AMBIENT: HUSHED CROWD]
The crowd fell silent as Tracy marched purposefully toward the Misty Peak section. Her eyes locked onto Bastian Kane, a prominent student who held the sixth-highest rank in his peak. His earlier swagger evaporated with each deliberate step she took.
The audience’s collective breath caught as they witnessed this unprecedented breach of arena etiquette—competitors simply didn’t confront spectators mid-match. Bastian’s face drained of color, his companions edging away from him as if distance might spare them from Tracy’s approaching wrath.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Tracy stopped right in front of him, her gaze like chips of ice. She stared him down for a few seconds, and then a slow, humorless smile stretched her lips. Her stunning looks only made the grin more menacing. "You want to see my body?" she asked, her voice deceptively sweet.
Bastian might have a big mouth, but he wasn’t a complete idiot. Tracy was smiling at him like an old friend, but he could feel the raw fury simmering just beneath the surface.
Bastian had one of the worst records in the All Peaks Competition. He’d already been knocked out of the tournament that day after losing seven fights and winning only one. He loved to talk big, but now, he was scared silent. Tracy wasn’t messing around.
He knew admitting it would be a death sentence, so he frantically shook his head and stammered, "N-no. You misunderstood. Th-that wasn’t me—"
But Tracy cut him off, still holding that same chilling smile. "Well, it’s not entirely out of the question," she said.
"What?" The students around them stared in disbelief. Was she for real?
"You just have to gouge your own eyes out after," Tracy added coolly.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURING NERVOUSLY]
Hearing that, a palpable chill swept through the crowd. They knew she wasn’t kidding.
"N-no, please," Bastian begged, still shaking his head.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC INTERRUPTION]
"Fine," a new voice cut in.
Tracy’s gaze snapped from Bastian to Zane, who had just stepped between them.
"If you beat me, I’m all yours," Zane said flatly. "As long as you promise the same thing if you lose."
Tracy’s eyes narrowed as she studied him. "And you let Bastian go," Zane added.
He hadn’t planned on helping Bastian, but now that he was in the middle of it, he figured he had to see it through. Zane knew he'd have to fight Tracy eventually in the tournament anyway. He might as well make the bet with her now.
Still, he was a little uneasy. If he won, that was fine. He wasn’t actually interested in her offer and would just let it go. But he could only imagine how a hothead like her would make him suffer if he lost.
While Zane was lost in thought, Tracy suddenly shook her head. "Let him go? I'm still pissed."
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
In the blink of an eye, a gray light flared in her palm and shot toward Bastian.
Zane hadn’t seen the sudden attack coming. She was too close for him to stop her. All he could do was yell, "Tracy, no!"
[SFX: IMPACT THUD]
But it was too late. The gray light slammed into Bastian’s chest, punching straight through his body and out his back.
Bastian’s eyes went wide with terror. He clapped a hand over the gushing hole in his chest. The wound was on his left side. If his heart had been an inch over, he’d be dead.
Zane focused his Soul Power on the injury and let out a quiet breath. She hadn't seriously hurt the idiot. She'd chosen the perfect angle, missing his heart and any other vital organs by a hair’s breadth. The wound looked horrific, but it was mostly for show. It might not even leave a scar.
But he was definitely in a world of pain.
So this was her punishment for Bastian’s disrespect.
[AMBIENT: ARENA BACKGROUND]
[MUSIC: TENSE AFTERMATH]
Her anger spent, Tracy turned and walked back toward the arena, a satisfied smile playing on her lips.
