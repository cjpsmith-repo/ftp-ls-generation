Ep 223 - The Arcane Aurora
Word count: 1196
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ACADEMY HALL]
The Seven-Star Platform bustled with activity as disciples filed through its various entrances.
The steward collected the IDs on autopilot, barely glancing at the disciples' faces. But Zane's ID made him do a double-take. An outer disciple? What was an outer disciple doing here? In all his time as a steward, he'd never seen one in this area. This was not a place they were supposed to be.
The steward's gaze finally flicked up to Zane. He glanced back down at the disciple's ID, a flicker of shock on his face. "You're Zane Lowell, right?" he asked, his voice slow. "I have to remind you, this is the entrance for level eight and above on The Seven-Star Platform. Based on your skill level... I don't think you're ready for this. I'd suggest you try one of the other entrances."
The disciple in the black robes---the one who'd already tried talking Zane out of this---had just finished signing in. He turned to the steward and said, "You're wasting your breath. I told him the same thing, but he's dead set on trying the eighth level!"
The steward figured Zane just didn't get the rules and was about to explain more. But after what the other disciple said, he realized Zane wasn't ignorant; he was just stubborn.
"Listen, Lowell," the steward pressed on, "The Seven-Star Platform isn't just about the magic arrays. The glacial heartstone also unleashes the Arcane Aurora. That stuff can seriously hurt you!"
"Save it," the disciple in black cut in. "I already explained all that to him. He's made up his mind."
At that point, the steward just shrugged. He was a steward on The Seven-Star Platform, not Zane's mentor. His job was to warn disciples about the danger. If someone refused to listen, that was on them.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
The steward took Zane's ID and handed him a token. The disciple in black then drew his sword, the blade singing with a crisp chime as it left the scabbard. A self-satisfied smile touched his lips as he told Zane, "This sword is called Wyrmfire. It's a middle tier Spirit Weapon!"
Even though it was only a middle tier Spirit Weapon, the disciple in black robes was damn proud of it; he'd earned it with his own two hands. He might have come from a noble family, but they weren't exactly a powerhouse. And since he was from a minor branch of the family, his own status wasn't very high.
After all, it was rare for an disciple from the Skyreach Sect to own a middle tier Spirit Weapon. Most of them were lucky to have a top tier Mystic weapon or maybe a low tier Spirit one.
"It's a nice sword," Zane said, his eyes scanning the area. "But honestly, I have no clue how you're supposed to train here." The magic array felt crude, and there wasn't much to look at.
There was just gray mist everywhere, a grid of smoke obscuring the view. It was a far cry from the delicate, intricate arrays on Bloody Mountain.
The disciple in black pointed. "See the two pillars up ahead?"
Zane looked where he was pointing and saw two pillars, each crowned with a glowing crystal ball.
"Follow me!" the disciple said, sword in hand. They walked over to the pillars. "See the names inside?" he asked, motioning for Zane to look.
Zane focused on the orbs and saw their names carved within. One crystal ball read "Zane Lowell," the other, "Zachary Anderson." Zane figured the disciple in black must be Zachary Anderson.
"This crystal ball is yours," Zachary explained patiently. "The second the magic array powers up, the glacial heartstones will start blasting out the Arcane Aurora! It'll come at you from all sides, and you have to cut it down with your sword. The power of the Aurora gets stronger the higher the level. If you're using a regular sword, a single blast will probably shatter it." He lightly traced the flat of his own blade with his fingertips. The familiar way he did it told Zane he'd used this sword against the Arcane Aurora many times.
Zane put it together quickly: the test was to protect the crystal ball. If the ball shattered, the session was over. The swordsmen were here to hone their "sword intent" against the relentless Aurora.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]
A deep rumble shook the air from above.
"Time to draw your sword! Hurry! The Arcane Aurora is coming!" Zachary warned. "Each crystal can only take three hits before it shatters, and then you're out. Be careful!"
Zane nodded and pulled his sword, Streamer, from his Storage Ring.
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
[SFX: MELODIC SWORD HUM]
A clear, melodic hum hung in the air as he unsheathed the blade.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
This was the moment Zachary had been waiting for. He wanted to see just what kind of sword this kid would pull out.
But when Zachary saw the Streamer in Zane's hand, he was completely stunned.
The sword looked ordinary enough at first glance — no imposing presence, nothing like the Wyrmfire's golden dragon scabbard. But then Zachary caught it: a faint silver light flowing along the blade, and beneath it, the unmistakable trace of something far beyond a Mythic Sigil.
His throat went dry. "That... that's a Sacred Weapon."
Zane glanced at him. "Probably."
Probably. Zachary felt the word land like a stone. A second-tier Adept, still in white robes, carrying a Sacred Weapon like it was nothing. It was offensive. The sword was wasted on him — if Zane couldn't unlock its power, it was no better than a stick of wood. Zachary decided he was going to prove exactly that.
Visually, Zane's Streamer was nothing compared to his Wyrmfire. It looked like a completely ordinary sword. 
Meanwhile, Zachary was feeling confident. He'd improved a lot, and he was sure he could last at least another half an hour on the eighth level this time.
After dealing with the first wave of Arcane Aurora on his side, Zachary glanced over at Zane. He was eager to see how Zane could possibly plan to use a weapon that looked so... basic.
[SFX: SWORD MOVEMENT]
Zane hadn't moved an inch. He just stood there, silent, waiting for the Arcane Aurora to approach. Once it was almost on top of him, he suddenly moved, his Streamer slicing through the air. It was just a simple chop.
"A simple chop?" Zachary muttered, his face a mask of disbelief.
As a swordsman, he knew the basic forms inside and out. \*Has this guy not practiced any other sword styles? Does he only know the basics?\* To use a basic form at this level was suicide.
\*What in the world is he thinking?\* Zachary was completely bewildered. He felt like he was losing his mind.
[SFX: SHARP SWORD SLICE]
The sword sliced through the air.
[SFX: BLINDING LIGHT EFFECT]
That one simple chop instantly created a half-moon of blinding silver light. Following the path of Zane's blade, the arc of sword radiance sliced through the incoming Arcane Aurora.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
Unlike the roaring, dragon-shaped energy from Zachary's sword, Zane's attack made no sound as it collided with the Aurora. The light cut cleanly through the thick wave of power, and the Arcane Aurora simply... vanished. Without a sound, without a trace.
