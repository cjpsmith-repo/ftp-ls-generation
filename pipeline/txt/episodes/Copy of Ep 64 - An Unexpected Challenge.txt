Ep 64 - An Unexpected Challenge
Word Count: 1384
[SFX: MYSTICAL RESONANCE]
Having touched the state of self-forgetfulness through soul refining, Zane could feel the intent radiating from the Kingpin. What he couldn't wrap his head around was why a statue, carved from rock by simple craftsmen, would give off such a powerful aura.
Unable to solve the riddle, Zane pushed the thought aside and followed the other disciples into Dragon's Keep.
[AMBIENT: STONE FORTRESS]
[SFX: CRACKLING TORCHES]
The crackle of kerosene torches echoed off the stone walls the moment he stepped inside. Their flickering light threw dancing shadows across the fort, blanketing the place in an eerie, mysterious gloom.
Shane led the group toward a building. "Alright, you can all bunk here for now," he announced. "Just follow the rules of the Keep. Anyone who wants to hunt the Razor swarm with the imperial army, wait for an assignment. If you'd rather go it alone, you're free to leave whenever."
The announcement stirred a ripple through the Skyreach Sect disciples.
A quartet of disciples stepped forward, their leader addressing Shane with a respectful nod. "We appreciate the timely rescue today, but we've decided to venture out independently," he declared, adjusting his weapon belt with practiced ease.
Shane flicked his wrist dismissively, his weathered face—carved by years of frontier battles—betraying neither surprise nor disappointment at the disciples' decision. "Suit yourselves. The swarm doesn't care who you hunt with," he remarked, his tone carrying the weight of a man who had witnessed countless young warriors make similar choices.
[AMBIENT: FORTRESS GATES]
[MUSIC: SOMBER DEPARTURE]
After exchanging brief farewells and a few hushed words of caution among themselves, the four disciples vanished into the gathering dusk, their silhouettes quickly swallowed by the massive fortress gates. The last one glanced back once, uncertainty flickering across his face before the shadows claimed him.
Their departure triggered an exodus. Group after group broke away, each drawn by the allure of freedom and potentially greater rewards. Some left with confident strides, shoulders squared and weapons gleaming, while others departed with nervous glances back at the safety of the Keep's walls, fingers fidgeting with talismans or weapon hilts.
[AMBIENT: EMPTY COURTYARD]
[MUSIC: ISOLATED TENSION]
When the dust settled, only four disciples remained standing in the torchlit courtyard, Zane among them. The dancing flames cast long shadows across their faces, highlighting their isolation amid the emptiness where their peers had stood moments before.
[MUSIC: TENSE UNCERTAINTY]
Zane shifted his weight uncomfortably, watching the last independent group disappear beyond the gate. His fingers absently traced the outline of his storage ring, seeking reassurance from the familiar touch. This marked his first encounter with an insect swarm of such magnitude, and the thought of facing it alone sent an involuntary shiver down his spine. Without established allies or knowledge of the terrain, joining the imperial forces seemed not just practical but prudent—a rare concession to caution from someone who typically preferred his own path.
The other three who chose to stay alongside him—each with faces etched with their own private calculations and concerns—clearly harbored personal motivations for aligning with the army's disciplined approach rather than risking the chaotic wilderness alone. Their eyes darted between one another, silently acknowledging their shared decision.
Zane recognized one of them—the guy with the chestnut ponytail who'd saved him back on the Skycruiser. Their eyes met briefly, a flash of mutual recognition passing between them like a current. The other two were a stocky, hot-tempered disciple named Max, and his close friend Rex—a sharp-eyed young man who carried himself with quiet discipline
Seeing that a few had decided to stay, Shane offered a small, appreciative nod, the torchlight softening his battle-hardened features for just a moment. He turned to his soldiers, his voice resuming its commanding edge. "Alright, your work here is done for the day. Dismissed!"
[AMBIENT: TAVERN INTERIOR]
With his men gone, Shane led the four remaining disciples into a drab, gray-painted building. It was a tavern. The moment they stepped inside, a wall of sour air hit them—a thick mix of old sweat and stale smoke that made all four of them recoil.
Shane seemed to expect their reaction, a wry smile touching his lips. "Fair warning," he said. "The living conditions here at Dragon's Keep aren't quite what you're used to back at the Skyreach Sect."
Leo laughed. "No need to apologize. If you wanted comfort, you wouldn't come to Dragon's Keep."
Shane grinned at that, found them a table, and hollered for wine. A moment later an ancient jar arrived — thick with dust, unceremoniously cracked open — and the waiter sloshed it into cups without ceremony. The smell that rose from it was unexpectedly rich.
They raised their cups.
"I haven't asked your names," Shane said.
They went around the table. Zane. Max. Rex. Leo.
Shane noted them down, then lifted his cup. "You stayed when the others didn't. On behalf of the Emerald Task Force — thank you."
"We stayed because it's useful to us," Zane said, setting down his cup. "Though I'm curious — why don't the other disciples want to work with the imperial army? You clearly have effective methods."
Leo answered before Shane could. "Efficiency. Four or five disciples working together can move fast and keep everything they earn. Follow the army and you move at the army's pace — and sometimes they ask for a cut of the crystal cores."
Zane nodded. That made sense. What Leo didn't say — but Zane could see plainly enough — was the contempt running in both directions. The sect disciples thought the soldiers beneath them. The soldiers thought the disciples arrogant and undisciplined. Shane's hospitality was genuine, but it was also strategic. Four extra fighters were worth a jar of wine.
"And you?" Zane asked Leo. "You're Nature Level. You don't need the army's help."
Leo shrugged. "I came to see the place. Crystal cores don't interest me much."
Zane had no answer for that.
"Let's see what these sect boys are made of!" a booming voice cut through the tavern's din.
[SFX: GROUP LAUGHTER AND MURMURS]
The place erupted in a low rumble of laughter and murmurs. A burly, bearded soldier was clearly trying to pick a fight, playing to the crowd of imperial soldiers who already seemed to hold a grudge against the disciples.
The tension in the room spiked. Shane stepped in, his voice low and firm. "Zane, don't mind him. Troy's just got a big mouth and a bad history with some of your sect mates."
"Are you kidding me?" Troy scoffed. "What Skyreach pup could ever get the best of me? Shane, stay out of this." He brushed off Shane's attempt at peace, his eyes locked on Zane. "Hey, kid. How about you and me go a round? Show us what you've really got."
Zane didn't even blink at the challenge. This was a distraction. He was only here to hunt the Razor swarm, earn points, and gain experience on his path to the Eternal. He tuned the man out.
Leo regarded the provocation with cool indifference, his features betraying no emotion as he casually sipped his drink. Max and Rex, however, bristled visibly at the insult, their faces flushing with indignation on behalf of their fellow disciple.
[SFX: STOOL CLATTERING]
"I'll gladly demonstrate what we're capable of!" Max retorted, his voice sharp with anger as he lunged forward, knocking his stool backward with a clatter. His eyes flashed dangerously, fists already clenched at his sides. "Let's see that arrogance of yours after you've faced one of us in combat!"
"Have you completely lost your mind?" Rex interjected, grabbing Max's arm while shooting Troy a contemptuous glare. His voice dripped with disdain as he addressed the room. "An High-tier candidate challenging someone at the Marrow Refining Level? Even a child would recognize such an imbalanced match as dishonorable."
Though Rex intended to defend him, highlighting the power disparity—and Zane's inferior position—cut deeper than the soldier's mockery. With his companions watching, walking away no longer seemed viable.
[MUSIC: TENSE DETERMINATION]
A steely determination washed over Zane as he rose deliberately to his feet. He stepped between his defenders, his voice barely audible yet commanding absolute attention.
"Rex. Max," he said, his eyes never leaving Troy's sneering face. "Stand down."
[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]
The tavern fell silent as Zane took a single step forward.
