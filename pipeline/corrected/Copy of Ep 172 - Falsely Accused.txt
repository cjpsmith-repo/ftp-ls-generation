Ep 172 - Falsely Accused
Word Count: 1051

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
 Saint Viana’s words struck Isla like a physical blow. She flinched, the color draining from her face as if she’d just ripped open an old wound. The sudden, raw grief in her eyes was hard to look at.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
 *Whoosh!*
Just then, a silver streak of light shot from the ground, aimed straight for Saint Viana.
She just waved her hand dismissively, flicking the light away like it was a fly.
[SFX: LANDING THUD]
 In that same instant, Zane vaulted onto Isla's flying chariot, which was hovering a hundred feet in the air, landing squarely beside her.
“Hey, old lady! Get your kicks picking on a younger girl? That’s pathetic. Seriously pathetic,” he spat.
Isla was Zane’s martial arts master, though she was only a few years older than him. It felt strange to hear him call her a “girl,” but standing next to the three-hundred-and-sixty-year-old Saint Viana, she had to admit, the label fit.
As he stood in front of her, the sharp scent of sweat told Isla he was just as on edge as she was.
The masters of the Skyreach Sect and all the Great Houses knew why Isla had come to them, why she’d accepted a humble post as a master on Misty Peak.
It wasn’t a secret, but it was a truth no one ever dared to speak aloud.
No one ever dared bring it up to her face.
When she’d first arrived at Misty Peak, Isla had hoped to leave it all behind, to start over.
But today, Saint Viana had shattered that hope, dragging the truth out into the open without an ounce of decency and plunging her right back into that suffocating pain.
Still, a small part of her was relieved Zane was here, stepping in for her.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
 Saint Viana’s eyebrows shot up. She seemed to finally notice Zane, shocked that Isla would even think about using an Emperor’s Token for some worthless outer disciple.
“Are you really going to waste an Emperor’s Token to save this kid?” she asked, her voice low and cold, her earlier rage replaced by an unnerving calm.
“How will we know if I don’t try?” Isla replied coolly.
Her words seemed to stun Saint Viana. Her face was a mask. “Fine. But you have your own problems to deal with in this Sect. You can’t protect him forever.”
She sneered. “Besides, even if *I* let this go, the Sect won’t. He killed one of our own. He broke our code, and everyone saw it. Not even *you* can save him from that.”
“I have no intention of protecting Zane if he’s truly done wrong. He’ll face the Sect’s laws like anyone else,” she shot back. “But you, a Saint, were about to execute him on the spot. Can you tell me why?”
Isla saw right through this attempt to hide behind the Sect’s rules and wasn’t letting her off the hook.
Saint Viana just grunted. After a tense moment, she said, “Regardless, this boy needs to explain himself. Now.”
From where he stood, Zane shot her a look of pure disbelief.
[MUSIC: ANGRY OUTBURST]
 “A reason? You want a reason, you old fool? I’d just finished The Demon Purge and was on my way back to Misty Peak when your goons jumped me for no reason. They accused me of being a Demon spy with zero proof. Murry and the others beat me half to death, and when they realized I wasn't going down easy, they brought in Frey to finish the job! You think I was just gonna stand there and let them murder me?”
“You think I’d just roll over and die?” Zane demanded, his voice thick with fury. “Tell me, what would you have done if you were in my shoes?”
Saint Viana scoffed. “That’s your side of the story. And it’s not a very believable one.”
[AMBIENT: COURTROOM TENSION]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
 With that, she floated slowly to the ground and approached Murry, who had been watching everything with a calculated stillness. “Murry,” Saint Viana said, her voice ringing with authority. “You’re a judge of the Hall of Judgment. It’s your duty to tell us exactly what happened here.”
A slick, rehearsed smile spread across Murry’s face. “My honored Saint Viana, please, don’t believe a word this liar says. The Hall of Judgment received intelligence that he was conspiring with Demons during the Purge. As a judge, it’s my duty to protect the disciples of our Sect. What if he’d been possessed by a Demon general and had infiltrated us? I had to investigate the threat.”
Saint Viana nodded. “You were right to do so, Murry. Continue.”
“He refused to cooperate with the Hall of Judgment’s examination. Worse, he hurled all sorts of baseless accusations! I ordered him disciplined for his insubordination, but he resisted violently and fled!”
Isla’s gaze softened as she looked at Zane.
“I’m sorry, but you’ll have to stay here for a few days,” she told him quietly. “But don’t worry. Once the trial starts, they won’t be able to frame you.”
A small smile touched Zane’s lips. “Lady Novak, I’m not worried. My life isn’t worth much anyway. Anyone who thinks they’re strong enough is welcome to try and take it.”
As he spoke, Zane shot a pointed look at Saint Viana.
[AMBIENT: TROOP OF OFFICERS]
With that, Isla guided her flying chariot to the ground. Zane would be held on Mount Judgment until his trial. A squad of officers was already there to escort him.
Just as they were about to lead him away, Zane stopped. He turned back to Isla, his voice low. “Lady Novak, can you tell me something? The court trial two years ago… who was judged?”
His question made Isla go still. A long moment passed before she whispered the name. “Elara.”
Zane managed a tight smile. “Who was the judge? And who was the prosecutor?”
“Can we talk about this later?” Isla asked, shaking her head slightly, discomfort clear in her voice. Honestly, she didn’t want to answer him at all.
“Please tell me,” Zane insisted. His voice was calm but firm, his eyes burning into hers. He'd never been closer to finding his sister, but there was still so much he didn't know.
