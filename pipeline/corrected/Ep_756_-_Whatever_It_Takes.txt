Ep 756 - Whatever It Takes

Word count: 1129

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL REALM]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

 The change hit fast. 

Zane’s body kept stretching, bones and muscles unfurling as if someone had hit fast-forward on his life. When he’d shrunk earlier, Cora had folded his clothes away. Now, with him growing by the heartbeat, he was completely naked.

Lavender watched without a flicker of expression. Cora caught her staring and felt her cheeks go hot. She knew Zane’s body better than anyone—at least she’d thought so—but having another woman stand there and look at him like this made her skin prickle.

Then it struck her: Lavender had been inside Zane this whole time. If anyone knew him intimately, it was her.

The thought made Cora’s stomach dip. Had Lavender heard every soft thing she and Zane whispered to each other? Every promise, every breath? The heat in Cora’s face deepened until she wanted to hide.

Lavender noticed the color in Cora’s cheeks and blinked, puzzled. But Cora was always a little offbeat, so she let it go.

[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION WHOOSH]

 Zane kept growing—first the size of an eight-year-old, then an awkward pre-teen, until his features sharpened back into the young man he was. Twenty again. Whole again.

They hovered over him, quiet and intent. Was he going to wake up?

After what felt like forever, his body shuddered. A moment later, his eyes opened.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL VIOLET REALM]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL SYNTH]

 The Violet Power Realm spread out around them, sky drenched in deep purple. Ribbons of purplish-black light—like a warped aurora—curled overhead. On the horizon, a massive violet sun poured out its eerie glow.

“Where am I?” Zane asked, taking it in before his gaze slid to Lavender and Cora.

“This is the Violet Power Realm,” Lavender said at once. “Cora brought you here.”

Cora forgot everything else and threw her arms around him. The relief on her face said everything.

 Zane held her for a heartbeat, then asked, “Where’s Maddox?”

“He’s still alive,” Lavender said simply.

“He didn’t die?” Zane murmured, a flicker of regret crossing his face. Then he spotted Tia lying beside him. His expression snapped tight. He eased Cora aside and reached for Tia.

Her skin was cold. He pressed two fingers beneath her nose. She was breathing — barely.

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

 Back in the fight, Maddox had unleashed the Life and Death Wind twice—one blast slammed into Zane, the other into Tia.

Zane knew exactly how lethal that wind was. Even that ancient powerhouse hadn’t been able to withstand it. He’d only survived because of the Celestial Fragment. Tia didn’t have that kind of protection. By all logic, she shouldn’t have made it.

“How did she survive?” he asked, looking to Lavender.

“She was rescued,” Lavender said.

“By who?” he asked, thrown.

 “The Master of the Elysian Keep.”

Zane nodded slowly. Then, without a word, he reached into his chest and took out the Celestial Fragment. He set it gently on Tia and waited.

The three of them were silent. The fragment glowed faintly — and then went still. Tia didn't stir.

[MUSIC: QUIET DREAD]

His voice was flat. "Is it useless?" 

Lavender said nothing for a moment. Then: "The wind devours life force directly. It carries the Law of Causality — touching it means certain death. The fragment offset part of the karmic link for you. But it isn't responding to her."

Zane looked up. "When you went to the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, the Sacred Tree poured its Vitality into you. Could that reach her?"

Lavender shook her head. "That Vitality is bound to the Demon Night. Another bloodline can't bear it. If Tia were Demon Night, it might work. She isn't."

[MUSIC: RISING FURY]

Zane was quiet for a long moment. Then he stood up, fists clenched at his sides.

"I will find another way. Whatever it takes — Tia does not die." He turned to Cora. "Take me back out."

Cora watched the rage in his face — all of it for Tia — and felt something quiet move through her chest. She already suspected the answer to the question she was afraid to ask. She nodded anyway.

[AMBIENT: LARGE CROWD MURMURING]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

 Out in the square before Oracle City, the crowd still stared up at the sky, shell-shocked by what they’d just seen.

The fight between Zane and Maddox had been terrifying—bigger, wilder than anything any of them had ever imagined. Every move had looked strong enough to break the world.

The giant black ball still hung overhead. It had shrunk, but the pressure it gave off pressed on the chest like a hand.

 No one knew how it had ended. Was Zane alive or dead?

Maddox sneered up at the sphere. “This time, you have to be dead for sure, Zane.”

He trusted the Life and Death Wind. He believed even the ancient greats couldn’t stop it. In the entire Lower World, no one could.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

 He wasn’t at the peak of the Life-and-Death Realm yet, so he’d only managed to use that move twice. And killing Zane hadn’t even been his real aim—he wanted Cora. For that, the Trinity Killing Array would’ve been enough.

But Zane had snatched her and slipped away. Worse, he’d taken her Pristine Essence. Maddox hadn’t expected Cora to gamble everything to save Zane.

He should have seized her himself, early on, instead of scheming from a distance. If he had, the Soul Harvesting Ritual would already be complete. That chain of missed chances — every link of it traced back to Zane — made his jaw tighten. That boy's luck was unnatural.

If not for the chain of cause and effect binding his hands, he would’ve finished Zane long ago, and his disciples from the Corrupted Divine Kingdom wouldn’t have had to face the massive sword Cora had summoned.

[AMBIENT: CITY WALL RUINS]

[SFX: DISTANT BATTLE SOUNDS]

 On the city wall, plenty of Spirit‑Tribulation Realm fighters watched with hard, tired eyes. The three leaders of the World Merchants’ Guild were still locked in a stalemate with Simon, and no one was eager to throw the first punch again.

To the people of Oracle City, as long as Zane was alive, there was still a sliver of hope. If he was dead, that was it.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC DEFEAT]

 “I say we just surrender,” one Spirit‑Tribulation fighter muttered, sitting on a shattered stretch of wall.

“We’re warriors of the World Merchants’ Guild. We’d die before we kneel. Why should we submit to them?” someone snapped back.

“We can’t stand against Maddox. What’s the point of throwing our lives away?”

“Better to die on your feet than live crawling.”

Opinions clashed in low voices, then fell quiet. In their hearts, most of them still clung to that last, thin thread.

[MUSIC: RISING DANGER]

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY PULSE]

Just then, Maddox spotted a node drifting through the air. He recognized it instantly — the anchor point of the Violet Power Realm, drifting right toward him.

Zane was coming back out.

His face split into a cold smile. "Let's see how you survive this time."
