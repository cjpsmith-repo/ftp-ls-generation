Ep 1318 - The Grim Cycle

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: CITY UNDER SIEGE]
The moment Zane flew into the city of ogres, all eyes were on him. 
Everyone who saw him was clearly stunned. They all remembered what he’d done to Slade, and now they were looking at him like he was some kind of unbeatable monster.
[SFX: WHOOSHING FLIGHT]
Zane barely gave them a glance as he soared overhead. These guys only had a few hundred thousand Illusion Points between them, and there was no point wasting time on small fry. His target was Slade. End of story.
Slade’s tell-tale red dot zipped to the west side of the city and touched down. Zane followed close behind, watching as the dot expanded into a human-sized ball of light.
[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]
The Cosmic Order was powerful, sure, but it couldn’t instantly bring back all the warriors dying in the city-wide slaughter. Slade would need a second for his body to fully reform. Anyone who entered the Illusion Battlefield was basically just projecting their body into it, and the one thing Zane couldn’t figure out was why their gear didn’t respawn with them.
He held his own sword in one hand and the spear he’d taken from Slade in the other, waiting by the glowing mass of energy. He didn’t have to wait long.
[SFX: LIGHT FLICKERING]
[SFX: GLOWING ENERGY HUM]
Zane smiled, raising the spear.
Slade never stood a chance.
[SFX: SPEAR THRUST]
[SFX: BODY IMPACT]
The second he materialized, Zane was already there, running him through. It was easy. But it had to be driving Slade absolutely insane. Just like before, Slade’s points didn’t spill out.
[SFX: SOUL WHOOSH]
The red dot that held his soul simply shot off toward the other side of the city.
Zane took off after it again. He figured Slade had to be running out of Exemption Medallions by now.
[SFX: REPEATED SPEAR STRIKES]
The whole thing became a grim cycle: Slade would revive, and Zane would be right there waiting to finish him off with a spear strike. Finally, after the fourth kill, Slade was out of medallions.
[SFX: MASSIVE LIGHT EXPLOSION]
This time, when he died, half of his accumulated Illusion Points burst from his body in a shower of light. Zane drew back his spear and let the massive surge of points wash over him.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING STRINGS]
That explosion of light caught everyone’s attention. It was enough points to launch some low-ranking scrub straight into the Vanguard One Hundred.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE WITH DISTANT BATTLE]
A few of the nearby warriors stared at the drifting points with naked hunger, doing the math on what a fraction of that haul could do for their own rank. But none of them moved. Taking so much as a single mote from Zane wasn’t worth the risk—not from the man who’d just spent the last several minutes gutting an Ace Talent over and over without breaking a sweat.
But Zane wasn’t finished. He chased the red dot down again, and a fifth kill dropped Slade clean out of the top fifty in the world. Desperate, Slade offered him a trade the moment he reformed: call it off, and he’d let Hailey go.
Zane didn’t answer. 
[SFX: SPEAR STRIKE WITH POINT SHOWER]
He killed him again anyway—the sixth time, points falling to a fraction of where they’d started. 
When Slade weakly repeated the offer, Zane only said, “No need.” He didn’t trust a man like Slade to keep his word, and it didn’t matter either way—he already knew he’d settle things properly once he brought Lavender home.
The kills kept coming after that, one after another, Slade’s fortune collapsing by half each time. Eventually Zane stopped counting the numbers at all—he wasn’t chasing points anymore, just making sure nothing of Slade’s was left standing. By the twelfth kill, Slade had barely a hundred thousand points to his name, and his rank had cratered from fourth in the entire world to somewhere in the tens of millions.
Not far away, Janet, Coleman, and Ansel were doing their own share of damage—the warriors trapped in the city with them were too afraid to fight back and handed over their points rather than risk it.
“I got them,” Zane said into his comms. “All of Slade’s points.”
Hailey let out a sigh of relief. “I saw.”
“Are you ready?” he asked.
She hesitated for a beat.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
“Even if I have to face the Punishment of Eternal Night… I’ll get through it. I’ll fight to survive. I’m still holding out for that day.”
The Punishment of Eternal Night she’d mentioned was a terrible fate known only to members of the Demon Night.
Zane shook his head.
“I won’t make you wait that long.”
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL STRINGS]
His words hit her deep, and her eyes shone as she looked at him. Most people feared pain and frustration, but for warriors like them, that was the easy part. The real enemy, the thing that broke people, was hopelessness. Amber was a perfect example. She was on the verge of snapping, trapped in that prison, and hope was the only thing keeping her from shattering completely. It was the only thing giving her the strength to hold on. And Zane was the one person who could give them that hope—the one thing they all so desperately needed.
He didn’t make promises lightly. Every time he gave his word, he felt the weight of it settle on his shoulders, a responsibility he had to see through. But even without making a formal promise, he knew things had already gone too far. He couldn’t just stand by and do nothing.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL RISING]
Taking a moment, Zane finally checked his ranking.
The result shocked him.
He’d broken into the Vanguard One Hundred—seventy-seventh place in the entire Illusion Battlefield.
And not just that—he was ranked second in the entire supreme world. The points he’d just taken from Slade had made a hell of a difference.
But if he was second… then who the hell was first?
