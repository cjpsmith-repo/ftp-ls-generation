Ep 1258 - The Power Of Ogres

Word count: 1142

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ATMOSPHERIC]
[AMBIENT: WIND THROUGH ANCIENT TREES]
Instead of heading back to the Demon Night’s main city with Aurora and Amber, Zane split off on his own.
The pace they’d set through the Deadwood Gate was fast enough for any normal warrior racking up Illusion Points, but for Zane, it was practically a crawl. 
Besides, he knew his presence was just siphoning Illusion Points from Aurora. He figured Aurora was probably just as happy to see him go.
[SFX: WHOOSHING SONIC BOOM]
Outside the gate, a crowd of Demon Night warriors watched as he vanished in a streak of light, rocketing north.
Amber stared at the empty sky where he’d disappeared, her expression a knot of hope and desperation.
Aurora glanced over, knowing exactly what was running through Amber’s mind. Zane was her last real shot at winning back her freedom, and that meant everything, no matter how slim the odds.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL RISING STRINGS]
Trying to sound encouraging, Aurora said, “You know, I think he’s already strong enough to take on the top talents from the major powers. He might even have a shot at breaking into the top hundred on the Illusion Battlefield.”
Making the top hundred was a monumental achievement. Anyone ranked that high was considered a peerless warrior, one of the true prodigies of the universe—the kind of fighter who was blessed with a rare bloodline, inhuman talent, or just plain unbelievable luck. They were the aces, the best of the best.
Amber was quiet for a long time, her eyes still fixed on the horizon. When she finally spoke, her voice was low and certain. 
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLUTE THEME]
“Zane will win the Illusion Battlefield.”
The conviction in her tone made Aurora’s eyes widen. Sure, Zane was unbelievably strong, far beyond any ordinary Soul Sea Realm warrior. But the competition he was facing now was on an entirely different level. He was going up against the absolute best from every race in the universe.
Amber’s faith felt a little blind.
But Aurora wasn’t about to say that out loud. If Zane could actually pull it off, he’d be ecstatic for Amber. Maybe he really was the one who could save her from that living hell in three years.
The Deadwood Gate’s fruit had already been claimed for this cycle — it would be another twelve hours before it opened again. With nothing left to gain by waiting around, Aurora turned the group back toward the city to rest and regroup in the meantime.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BUILDING TENSION]
Zane’s Teleportation Token had recharged enough to use again, but every activation came with a long cooldown afterward, and he wasn’t about to waste it. Continuing north on his own two feet would get him to a main city just the same.
Before he flew, his body shook violently, shaking off the little skulls. He turned into a streak of light and shot forward. The skulls gave chase without missing a beat, matching his speed mile for mile — whenever he slowed even slightly, they closed the gap and sank their teeth in again. 
After roughly ten thousand miles, a massive walled city rose up ahead of him. One glance at the city’s architecture and its ogre-worked stonework told him exactly what he was looking at.
He eased his speed, studying the layout as he approached.
Outside the walls, ogre warriors clashed in open, chaotic combat. 
Under different circumstances, Zane had planned to bounce between cities like this one, picking off isolated stragglers for easy points. For now, that plan would have to wait. He had a swarm to deal with first.
[SFX: CROWD GASPING]
As he shot toward the gates, ogres both inside and outside the walls caught sight of him — a streak of light with a cloud of skulls trailing behind.
“What the hell is that!”
“That one up front — that’s a Human Race cultivator.”
“What’s he doing all the way out here?”
[MUSIC: DARK ANTICIPATION]
An ogre warrior stepped into his path, a massive spear balanced on one shoulder, grinning with a sinister edge. “Well, well. Where’d this Human brat wander in from? You want past me and into that city, you can leave your little dream right here—”
[SFX: SUDDEN BLUR OF MOTION]
He didn’t get to finish. Zane’s flight path curved without losing a shred of speed, arcing straight around the ogre.
[SFX: WET SLICING SOUND]
A spray of dark blood burst into the air. The ogre who’d tried to block him fell in two pieces before most of the crowd even registered what had happened.
A dead hush fell over the onlookers. The ones who’d been running their mouths a second ago now stood frozen, terrified of being next.
[AMBIENT: OGRE CITY ATMOSPHERE]
[SFX: SKULLS SLAMMING INTO BARRIER]
[SFX: BONE CHATTERING]
The moment Zane stepped back into the ogre city, the cloud of palm-sized skulls swarming behind him slammed into an invisible wall. The protective field around the city held them back, a buzzing, chattering mass of bone just outside the boundary.
Curious whether the barrier alone might be enough, Zane stepped back out through the gate to test it.
[SFX: SKULLS SWARMING]
Instantly, the swarm surged back onto him, biting hungrily, as if they’d been starved the whole time he was inside. He stepped back through the gate a second time, and just as before, the invisible wall snapped shut behind him, leaving the skulls stranded on the other side.
Zane stood on the city wall, a frown creasing his brow. 
[SFX: SKULL SNAPPING]
Then his hand shot out, snatching one of the snapping skulls from the air just beyond the barrier. He stood on the absolute edge—one step forward would place him outside the city’s protection, one step back would pull him deeper inside. 
[SFX: MAGICAL RESISTANCE HUM]
As he tried to drag the skull over the invisible line, he felt a firm, unyielding resistance.
It obviously wasn’t coming from the skull itself. Zane had enough raw strength to hurl one of the things tens of thousands of feet if he wanted to. No, this felt different. It was like the air above the city was a forbidden zone, a field that actively repelled the skulls.
Aurora had been right. If he could just get them inside, the city’s own rules would probably destroy them for him.
The problem was, he couldn’t get them in at all.
Still standing on the wall, Zane gripped the skull and pulled with everything he had. The resistance was immense. 
[SFX: STRAINING EFFORT]
[SFX: DRAGON SCALES AWAKENING]
Veins bulged on his arms as he poured his incredible strength into the effort, but the skull refused to budge another inch into the city. As he strained against the unseen force, he felt the faint, familiar power of his Dragon Scales beginning to stir.
