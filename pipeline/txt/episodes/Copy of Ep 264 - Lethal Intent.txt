Bystanders held their breath as the tension between Zane and Adrian grew to an unbearable pitch.
Adrian’s latest insult struck Zane as particularly over the top. "Rookie? Are you kidding me?" Zane snorted. "Adrian, you’re not that much older than me. You might have hit the Soul Realm first, but that doesn’t mean you’re stronger! Who are you calling a rookie?"
Zane had already established himself as one of the most promising talents among The Thirty-Three Peaks. His victory over Cecil, accomplished while merely at the Second Level of the Adept Realm, had been called miraculous by observers. Following that with his defeat of Richard, utilizing his mysterious purple and black Vitality, had only elevated his reputation to unprecedented heights.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURING]
The crowd at the All Peaks Competition was already buzzing after learning that Zane might be Elara’s older brother. Now, hearing him talk back to Adrian, they were floored by his audacity.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATION]
Zane had already shown incredible potential, marking him as one of the most promising disciples from The Thirty-Three Peaks. For a cultivator at the Second Level of the Adept Realm, his victory over Cecil was nothing short of a miracle. On top of that, defeating Richard with that strange purple and black Vitality of his was considered an even more impressive feat.
But Cecil and Richard were just top disciples from The Thirty-Three Peaks. They couldn’t hold a candle to the likes of Finn, and Shayna, and they had a long way to go before they could even be compared to the personal disciples.
Despite being only at the Second Level of the Adept Realm during the last tournament, Jacob commanded power that was miles ahead of his peers—a testament to his status as a personal disciple.
Jacob had cultivated not just physical strength but an aura of inevitable victory that clung to him like a second skin, making even seasoned fighters question their own abilities when standing in his shadow.
The All Peaks Competition had a long history, and in every single tournament, the top three spots were always swept by personal disciples. It was rare for a regular disciple from The Thirty-Three Peaks to even crack the top ranks. When the personal disciples really cut loose, the others couldn’t even break into the top twenty.
This made the gap between them crystal clear. Though few in number, the personal disciples had access to the best resources the Skyreach Sect could offer and received direct instruction from their masters. As a result, each one was a powerhouse in their own right, with their own unique skills.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
Even among this elite circle, Adrian was a standout. His name appeared at fifteenth position on the Skyreach Sect List the previous year, though such rankings were notoriously unreliable for personal disciples. They rarely fought in public and improved so fast that the list could never keep up.
This ambiguity meant that despite his official standing, Adrian might possess strength rivaling the top-ranked fighter. Fellow disciples were terrified of him, their voices faltering in his presence as though they addressed a venerated elder rather than a peer. His reputation cast a long shadow across the competition grounds, inspiring both awe and fear.
Against this backdrop, Zane’s casual disrespect toward Adrian struck onlookers as unbelievable arrogance bordering on madness. Whispers rippled through the crowd as they watched the exchange unfold, many shaking their heads at what they perceived as foolhardy bravado.
Adrian typically maintained an impassive demeanor, but Zane’s cutting remarks had begun to penetrate this cool façade. Though struggling to preserve his dignified bearing—and extremely aware that countless eyes scrutinized his every reaction—Adrian’s complexion grew alarmingly pale, betraying the rage simmering under his skin.
A cold smile finally broke through. "Not bad, Zane," Adrian said quietly. "You actually managed to get under my skin. I know exactly what you're doing — rattle me, make me sloppy, hope I hand you an opening." He tilted his head slightly. "It won't work. But you're going to pay for trying."
Years of rigorous training had taught Adrian the fundamental principle that emotional control was crucial for a warrior. The battle, he reminded himself, would provide ample opportunity to respond to Zane’s provocations in the language they both understood best—combat.
They entered the ring, each ready to get this over with quickly.
[AMBIENT: HIGH PLATFORM]
Up on the high platform, Sir Kenneth had been watching in silence. Now his expression had hardened into something else entirely.
"Master Jasper," he said, his voice low and deliberate. "Am I to understand that a disciple of this caliber — Elara's brother, no less — has been competing in this sect for months, and no one thought to inform me?"
Master Jasper chose his words carefully. "He was taken in by Isla Novak. It's possible she... managed the flow of information."
Sir Kenneth's eyes stayed on the ring. "I don't care whose doing it was. Pull Zane Lowell's cultivation records. Every training ground, every reported result. I want to know exactly how this was missed." He paused. "And I want to know how far that young man can go."

Each blossom, deceptively beautiful yet deadly, carried the crushing weight of ten anvils. Should even one attach to his body, Zane’s mobility would be slowed to a painful crawl, leaving him vulnerable to Adrian’s next attacks.
[SFX: CRUSHING PRESSURE]
At that moment, Adrian unleashed his signature technique—the Lethal Intent of the Lotus—sending waves of oppressive force cascading down upon his opponent. The invisible pressure threatened to flatten Zane against the arena floor, the air around him growing dense and suffocating.
He actually believes he can immobilize me with such elementary tactics? Zane thought, a defiant smile playing at the corners of his mouth despite the mounting pressure. How delusional.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]
The crushing pressure of the Lethal Intent began to give way. Zane broke into the Sword Step, darting around the ring while his six Shadow Phantoms smashed through the incoming lotuses.
[SFX: WHOOSHING ATTACKS]
With a series of whooshes, the phantoms blurred into motion. The incredible force of their attacks shattered the lotuses, scattering their petals across the battle ring. A moment later, the petals dissolved back into Primordial Vitality and vanished into the air.
But as powerful as the Shadow Phantoms were, Zane could only summon six at a time. Adrian, on the other hand, could conjure hundreds of lotuses with a single wave of his hand. While the phantoms destroyed most of them, a few slipped through the gaps and kept coming for Zane.
Still, Zane wasn’t worried. He knew he could handle the weight of a single lotus. He was even confident he could absorb it with his Shadow Vitality.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
But to his shock, the moment his Shadow Vitality touched a lotus, its color instantly shifted from a vibrant red to a sickening, lurid crimson.
[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]
The color change sent alarm bells screaming in Zane’s head. He stopped dead in his tracks and shot backward at an incredible speed.
