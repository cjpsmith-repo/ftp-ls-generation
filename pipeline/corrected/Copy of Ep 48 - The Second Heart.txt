Ep 48 - The Second Heart
Word Count: 1407
[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
If he could just pierce its skin and hit the second heart, the Ogre would go down. It was his only shot at getting out of this alive.
[SFX: POWERFUL WIND GUST]
As he tried to figure out his next move, a powerful gust of wind ripped past his side. Zane glanced over just in time to see the Ogre's massive palm swinging right for him.
"Shit! No time to get away!"
This time, there was no dodging. He had to fight.
[SFX: QUICK MOVEMENT]
In a split-second, Zane made his move. He gritted his teeth, spun, and leaped onto the back of the Ogre's hand. He scrambled up its arm and jumped to its shoulder. With a quick somersault, he flipped over the Ogre's head, his nose just inches from the two sharp horns sprouting from its skull.
After that series of calculated moves, Zane finally landed squarely on the Ogre's back. And there it was—the distinct bulge he was looking for.
"This is it! Taste my Purple Flash Fist, you monster!"
[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]
Zane summoned all his strength, channeling it into his fist as a purple light flared around it. Then he slammed it into the bulge—the monster's second heart. If he could just break through the thin layer of skin, his Purple Flash Fist would crush its heart to pulp.
But to his horror, the moment his fist made contact, it slipped. The skin was impossibly slick!
"I failed," the thought hit him with a wave of pure dread.
In the next instant, the Ogre pinpointed his location and swatted at its own back as it turned.
[SFX: LOUD IMPACT CRACK]
A loud crack echoed through the air. The Ogre had landed a direct hit. The force of the blow launched Zane, sending him flying dozens of feet through the air like a ragdoll before he slammed into the ground.
Outside, the masters who'd been watching through other disciples' feeds let out a collective sigh.
"What a shame. That was his only opening."
"The skin's too tough. You can't break it at his level — not without a Spirit Weapon."
"If he'd had one, the Ogre would be the one on the ground right now."
A few of the watching disciples turned and bolted — and the moment they ran, the masters lost Zane's feed entirely. Most of them leaned back and shook their heads. It was over. Elimination was inevitable.
Only Isla kept watching. Her feed still showed him clearly — and what she saw surprised even her. The blow that should have shattered him had done less damage than it had any right to. His body had absorbed most of it, converted it to warm current. Only a fraction had landed as real injury.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEP]
The Ogre actually paused, studying him for a moment. Then it raised its massive foot, ready to stomp him into oblivion. It must have thought he was finished, because it raised its foot with agonizing slowness. That slowness was a mistake. It gave Zane just enough time to roll out of the way.
Fighting through the searing pain, he scrambled to get up.
He tried to jump to his feet, but—
[SFX: PAINFUL GRUNT]
"Ugh, that hurts!"
[SFX: BODY FALLING]
Zane refused to just lie there and die, but the movement sent a crippling wave of pain through him, and he couldn't stop the cry that tore from his throat.
The pain felt bone-deep, seizing his muscles and making his body stiff. The leap he'd planned was impossible now.
[SFX: OBJECT BEING PULLED FROM STORAGE]
"New plan, then." Without a second's hesitation, he pulled the Shattered Dagger from his Storage Ring, gathered what little strength he had left, and hurled it at the Ogre.
"Please, let this work," he prayed. This was it. If this didn't even leave a scratch, he was done for. Kicked out of The Gauntlet for sure.
[SFX: KNIFE FLYING THROUGH AIR]
[SFX: KNIFE HITTING GROUND]
Zane held his breath, his eyes glued to the knife. He watched, stunned, as it shot forward like a dart and sank deep into the Ogre's back. But it didn't stop. Impossibly, it passed clean through the monster's body without slowing, clattering to the ground on the other side.
He was speechless. Only two possibilities made sense. Either he'd somehow become strong enough to throw a knife clean through an Ogre—obviously impossible—or the beat-up old shattered dagger was just that damn sharp. He was betting on the knife.
The knife had done what his fist couldn't. It had to be the blade.
"This thing is incredible," Zane breathed, marveling at its power.
[SFX: MONSTER ROAR]
[SFX: MAGICAL DISSOLUTION]
The huge Ogre roared in pain and stumbled around before dissolving into specks of light. He had actually killed the lone Level Five Beast.
[SFX: SOFT CHIME]
[SFX: CRYSTAL BOUNCING]
With a soft chime, a pure black crystal fragment fell where the Ogre had been, bouncing once before landing right in his hand. This was the one thing he needed to pass The Gauntlet.
Holding the fragment, Zane couldn't help but curse whoever designed this Magic Circle. He would have much rather faced a mob of Level Three Phantom Beasts than this one Level Five Ogre.
He already had plenty of the other colored fragments. Right now, the knife was the only thing on his mind.
[AMBIENT: FOREST RUSTLING]
His body still screaming from the Ogre's hit, Zane limped forward. The knife had landed somewhere in the bushes, out of sight.
"I have to find that knife," he thought desperately. "How could I lose it?"
After searching carefully through the undergrowth, he finally spotted a small hole in the ground.
"Damn it," Zane thought. "With that kind of force, it must be buried deep."
[SFX: FRANTIC DIGGING]
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL DISTORTION]
He dug frantically, clawing at the dirt until he was several feet down, but still found nothing. Just then, his surroundings began to blur and shift. The Other Side was collapsing. He had completed The Gauntlet; the Magic Circle was dissolving.
[AMBIENT: BLOODY MOUNTAIN WILDERNESS]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TRANSITION]
A moment later, he was somewhere else entirely. He was back on Bloody Mountain, surrounded by its signature red plants. The hole he'd just been digging was gone.
And the pain was gone too. Every injury — the Ogre's blow, the Cobalt Fiber cuts — all of it had vanished the moment the illusion dissolved. None of it had been real. His body was completely whole.
"My Shattered Dagger!" he screamed in his head. "Without it, how am I ever going to get my revenge on Perrin?"
[SFX: SKYCRUISER APPROACHING]
A Skycruiser appeared over the treeline and settled beside him. An examiner jumped down and gestured for him to board. Zane took one last look at the red mountain, then climbed on.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR GATHERING — MASTERS TALKING]
Outside, the masters were deep in discussion about other candidates — the strong performers from the Great Houses, a few standout commoners. No one was watching for Zane. As far as they were concerned, the Ogre had finished him.
Then Zane stepped off the Skycruiser.
He walked up to the counting table and set down his crystals — green, yellow, purple — and then, almost as an afterthought, placed the black shard on the table.
[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]
The chatter stopped.
An elegant middle-aged man stepped forward. "There is only one black crystal shard. It drops from the Level-5 Beast on the fifth layer. In the entire history of this Magic Circle, no one has ever brought one down."
He looked at Zane. "Until now."
[MUSIC: MOMENT OF TRIUMPH]
The silence held for another beat. Then it broke all at once.
[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]
"Zane is coming to Misty Peak!" Isla Novak's clear voice cut through the chatter, instantly silencing the other masters. She was certain he was hers; she'd been the first to see his potential, the one who'd taken him under her wing.
But as much as the other masters respected Isla, they weren't about to back down from recruiting such a promising disciple.
"Isla, you need to compete fairly, just like the rest of us," Master Jasper Finch challenged her calmly.
"Come on, Isla. You can't pull this every time."
"Just because you have a famous peak doesn't give you the right to be so aggressive, Isla."
The other masters voiced their objections in unison, forming an unprecedented alliance against her. Isla's brazen attempt to claim Zane, coupled with her customary superiority, had finally exhausted their patience.
[MUSIC: TENSE POWER PLAY]
Isla merely dismissed their protests with a contemptuous gesture. Detecting the dangerous shift in her demeanor, her colleagues wisely abandoned their resistance. They understood the unspoken threat—challenge her authority too forcefully, and she possessed sufficient influence to orchestrate their expulsion from the Skyreach Sect.
"Very well," Isla conceded with an exaggerated sigh. "We shall allocate disciples according to their rankings and personal preferences."
[MUSIC: SCHEMING UNDERTONES]
Left with no alternative, the masters grudgingly accepted her terms. Little did they suspect the depths of Isla's determination to secure Zane as her disciple, nor did they glimpse the intricate scheme already unfolding in her mind. As the final tallies were recorded, a subtle, enigmatic smile crept across Isla Novak's lips—the kind that promised secrets yet to be revealed.
