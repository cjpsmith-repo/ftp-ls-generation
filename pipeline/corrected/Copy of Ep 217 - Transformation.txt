Ep 217 - Transformation
Word count: 1057
[AMBIENT: BLACKSMITH FORGE]
[MUSIC: GROWING POWER]
The gathered disciples of The Seventeenth Court watched in stunned silence as Zane casually thrust his bare arm into the scorching heat, completely unaffected by temperatures that would have incinerated ordinary flesh.
Harrison's eyes widened as Zane manipulated the controls with practiced ease, adjusting the underground lava flow to achieve the precise temperature required for his mysterious infusion process.
A faint smile played across Zane's lips as he felt the familiar stirring of power within his core---the moment of transformation was nearly at hand.
Zane slid the unfinished sword into the refining furnace and shut the door without a second thought. He then strolled over to the seat Harrison had just vacated, right in front of the furnace's burner.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY MATERIALIZING]
Once Zane was seated, the Corrupt Vitality materialized in his hand, wrapping around it in seconds.
The Celestial Essence was a very big deal. He was trusting Dustin to auction it for him, mostly because... well, who else was there? Besides Dustin, he didn't know anyone capable of handling something so valuable. Plus, he needed to keep it quiet---he couldn't have anyone but Dustin and himself knowing he possessed it. That made Dustin his only real option.
And there was no way in hell Zane was letting the disciples of The Seventeenth Court in on his little secret. He'd have to be clever about this. The Corrupt Vitality was a deep, brooding purple. He let the energy completely envelop his hand and the Storage Ring before drawing out the Celestial Essence.
The Celestial Essence was completely hidden within the swirling Corrupt Vitality.
[SFX: ENERGY FLOWING]
With everything in place, Zane directed the stream of Corrupt Vitality toward the refining furnace's burner, the Celestial Essence concealed inside. His technique was crude; a real master would probably see right through the ruse.
But these guys from The Seventeenth Court? He was betting they wouldn't notice a thing.
[SFX: THICK ENERGY STREAM]
The Corrupt Vitality formed a thick, dark purple stream, carefully feeding the hidden Celestial Essence into the furnace, one drop at a time.
Watching the flow of dark purple energy, Harrison's eye twitched. "What a waste," he thought, already calculating a dozen better ways to use a resource like that.
Anyone with a brain knew you applied Celestial Essence to the blade, and only the blade. If you were short on it, you'd focus every last drop on the cutting edge to maximize its power. Even if some of the essence was lost that way, the result would still be miles better than just spreading it thin.
But Zane clearly lacked that kind of finesse. He was just letting the Celestial Essence drip all over the place, coating the handle as much as the blade itself.
Even this unfinished sword couldn't absorb the Celestial Essence forever. There was a limit, one that depended on the skill of the weapon refiner. Zane poured hundreds of drops into the furnace all at once. The entire sword was drenched in it. The whole thing---blade, handle, even the pommel---drank it in until it was completely saturated, unable to absorb another drop.
Once it was full, Zane withdrew the unabsorbed Celestial Essence from the furnace and tucked it back into his Storage Ring.
The whole process was nearly over, and it had taken only thirty minutes.
When the disciples of The Seventeenth Court saw Zane disperse the Corrupt Vitality, they just looked confused. Harrison stared, baffled. "You're done? Already? That was... fast."
From what they knew about weapon refining, the process could be long or short, but finishing this quickly was unheard of.
That alone convinced Harrison that Zane was just screwing around.
Zane flashed a relieved smile. "Yep. Sword's all refined."
"Zane, open the furnace. Let's see what you did," Harrison said, his curiosity getting the better of him.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]
Zane walked over to the refining furnace. The moment he lifted the lid, a brilliant silver light erupted from within.
A low hum filled the air.
[SFX: ENERGY BEAM]
A razor-sharp beam of energy blazed from the furnace and shot across the room, slicing clean through a section of The Seventeenth Court's wall.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
[SFX: SHOCKWAVE]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
As Zane reached in and pulled out the sword, the silver light enveloping it surged toward the heavens. An invisible shockwave of power rippled out from the blade.
[SFX: DEEP RESONATING THRUM]
A deeper thrum resonated through the very stone, and for a fleeting moment, everyone on Quenching Peak felt it.
[AMBIENT: BUBBLING MAGMA]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONES]
On the far side of the peak, the ground-fire vein snaking up from Infernal Peak converged into a ten-foot-deep basin. But it wasn't filled with water. It was a churning pit of magma, aptly named the Fire Pool.
Sitting around the Fire Pool were the two masters of Quenching Peak: Jerry and Mark. Both were seventh-stage Adepts---not the most powerful fighters, perhaps, but their knowledge of weapon crafting was second to none. While true grandmaster status was still a distant dream, they were the best the Skyreach Sect had to offer.
Both men felt the sudden surge. "That... that felt like the birth of a Sacred Weapon," Jerry breathed, his eyes wide with disbelief. "Could it be Enio? But why would a grandmaster like him stoop to using an ordinary copper furnace on Quenching Peak?"
Mark shook his head. "I don't think so, either. But Enio is the only one in the Sect capable of forging a Sacred Weapon. Who else could it possibly be?"
A Sacred Weapon outranked a Mythic Weapon the way a king outranked a knight — an entirely different order of power.
[SFX: WHOOSHING MOVEMENT]
"Well, we won't figure it out sitting here," Jerry suggested. "Let's go see for ourselves." He shot to his feet and streaked toward the source of the energy, Mark right on his heels.
Even as weapon refiners, their power as seventh-stage Adepts granted them incredible speed. They crossed the peak in a flash.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL HUMMING]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL WONDER]
Back in The Seventeenth Court, the sword in Zane's hand was utterly transformed. Its shape hadn't changed, but the once-ordinary metal now pulsed with an otherworldly silver light. If you looked close, you could see faint, intricate runes now etched across the blade's surface.
Harrison and the other disciples were speechless, their jaws hanging open. They stared, wide-eyed and dumbfounded, at the sword in Zane's hand. Words failed them, their faces a perfect mask of disbelief. 
This random kid had actually pulled off the impossible. He didn’t even know the power of the weapon he’d made.
