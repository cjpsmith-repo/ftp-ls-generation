Ep 810 - Into the Void

Word count: 1006

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

Logan’s fights were brutally short. He’d teleport, slash his sword, and it was over before his opponent could even process the attack. The strategy was simple, but his mastery of the Spatial Law made it unstoppable. Taking down top-tier warriors from other kingdoms was a piece of cake.

After all, the Spatial Law was incredibly difficult to master. Most people had no clue how to even begin cultivating it, let alone wield it in a fight. The moment Logan breached its third layer, his power eclipsed that of every other warrior at his level.

[SFX: WHOOSH TELEPORT]

So when he teleported to strike Zane down, he was stunned to see his target simply vanish. He felt the ripple of it in the air—the unmistakable signature of the Spatial Law.

So he knows it, too, Logan thought, startled.

[SFX: SWORD SLASH]

He reflexively swung his Spatial Blade, but it only cut through empty air.

[SFX: WHOOSH TELEPORT]

Zane reappeared a short distance away, a sarcastic smirk playing on his lips.

Seeing that smirk, heat flooded Logan’s face. No one at his level had ever dared to look at him like that, especially not some kid from the middle stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

A buzz of disbelief rippled through the crowd. 

“I can’t believe it! Jones knows the Spatial Law, too! He’s got to be at the second level, at least!”

“But Logan’s already on the third layer! I wonder who’s going to win this!”

“So what if it’s the third layer?” someone else argued. “I doubt he’s completely mastered it. He’s probably only grasped ten percent, so his advantage isn’t that huge. This is going to come down to pure fighting skill!”

“If Zane doesn’t use that soul attack of his, I still think his chances are slim.”

Among the stands, the other god-tier geniuses gasped. They couldn’t believe Zane’s luck. Not only did he possess a powerful soul, but he was also skilled in the most feared technique of all.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

“Looks like I need to take you seriously,” Logan said, the arrogance finally draining from his voice. He tightened his grip on the Spatial Blade.

“You weren’t serious before?” Zane shot back with a grin, knowing he was getting under his skin.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

“Die!” Logan snarled. He took a single step forward and vanished again.

Zane’s eyes widened. The speed was unreal. He couldn’t sense Logan’s presence at all—it was as if he’d been completely erased from existence. 

 This was the third layer of Spatial Law: not teleportation between two points, but true concealment inside the void itself. The first layer connected space nodes — step in one place, emerge from another. The second layer let a warrior float briefly outside space, like a stone thrown skyward before gravity pulled it back. 

But the third layer was something else entirely. Logan wasn't moving through space. He was on the other side of it.

Zane had learned the first layer from the Obelisks of Souls. He'd pushed to the second in the Tower of Sin. The third was beyond anything he'd had the chance to study. He had no method to detect Logan — not with soul perception, not with raw instinct.

He had one option.

[SFX: MAGICAL ACTIVATION]

Zane activated his Magic Vision.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]

The world instantly washed over in a sea of green. Even if Logan was tearing through the void, he couldn’t do so without leaving a trace. Sure enough, Zane’s vision picked up a faint black line stretching through the air—the path Logan was carving through space. The line was invisible to the naked eye and undetectable by soul perception, but to Zane, it was as clear as day.

He knew he had to play it cool. Instead of staring directly at the line, Zane let his eyes dart around the arena, pretending he had no idea where Logan was.

Meanwhile, Logan circled him cautiously, on high alert. He planned to exploit Zane’s biggest blind spot by attacking from straight ahead.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

As the black line crept closer, Zane put on a look of hesitation. But the moment it was within striking distance, his expression snapped into a sly smile, and he lunged, thrusting his sword forward with explosive force.

[SFX: METAL CLASH]

His timing was perfect. His blade slammed against the Spatial Blade just as Logan materialized.

[SFX: SPATIAL TEAR]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The rift Logan had just stepped through still hung shimmering in the air behind him, just large enough for a person to pass through. Seizing the opportunity, Zane not only drove Logan back with the force of his blow but plunged into the spatial tear right behind him.

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

When they separated, Logan held his blade up. A few thin drops of red ran slowly down the transparent edge. He looked at Zane with a strange expression, then shook his head and smiled — almost carelessly. He stuck out his tongue and licked the blade clean.

“Your body is something else,” he said. “That cut should have taken your head off.”

A thin line of blood traced Zane's neck. Zane touched it with two fingers. He had to admit it — Logan was extraordinary. An ordinary late-stage Spirit-Tribulation warrior wouldn't have lasted three exchanges. The neck cut had been perfectly timed, perfectly placed. If his body hadn't been what it was, the fight would already be over.

He looked at Logan evenly. “There won't be a next time. Even in the void, I can see you.”

Logan's smile didn't reach his eyes. “Is that so?”

He disappeared again.

[AMBIENT: CROWD TENSION]

Down in the stands, the York Divine Kingdom warriors were unmoved.

“Logan is invincible in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. Always has been.”

“War King York has already made his intentions clear. Logan isn't just here to win.”

“Jones isn't walking away from this.”

The Crystal Martial House warriors heard every word. They gripped the railing and leaned forward.

“Come on, Jones!”

“Watch for the next strike — move fast, don't let him get set!”

Whatever his origins, he was representing their kingdom. That was enough.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

The enchanted barrier, erected by the Soul Sea Realm masters to contain the duel, now held an empty arena. Both fighters had vanished.
