Ep 657 - Full Circle

Word count: 1167

[AMBIENT: THICK FOG]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The fog rolled in so thick and fast that the fight just… stopped.

One moment, two sides were locked in combat; the next, they were just shadows in a sea of gray. They pulled back, an unspoken truce hanging in the air, but kept a wary distance.

Malcolm had been on their heels for a solid thirty miles. By his guess, they'd only been in this soupy mist for four or five, and at the speed they were flying, it should have only taken a few minutes to punch through to the other side.

A knot of worry was tightening in everyone’s chest. A few minutes stretched into five, then ten, and the fog remained as dense as ever. They looked at each other, the same uneasy question in their eyes. At their pace, they should have covered dozens of miles by now, yet there was no sign of the mist thinning, just an endless wall of gray in every direction.

The first whispers of panic began to set in.

A grim look settled on Garrick’s face. “Damn it! We’re done for,” he muttered, his voice low and gloomy. “It’s just like the rumors. Once you’re in, you don’t get out.”

“Shut up,” Malcolm snapped. He was trying to project calm, but his own gut was churning with anxiety. He knew more than rumors. The Ogres had once sent a full war party into this fog — their largest ever. Whatever was in here had killed them all at once, and no one had ever figured out what.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ATMOSPHERE]

Zane, however, seemed unfazed by the obvious panic. He stopped suddenly and looked down at the ground. "Hold on," he said. There, half-hidden in the black earth, was a small mark — a cut in the soil, clean and deliberate. "I left that before we started moving." He looked up, his expression complicated. "We've come full circle."

"That's impossible," Fabian said. "We were flying in a straight line."

Zane shook his head. "You think you were. Without a reference point — a mountain, a landmark, anything on the horizon — you can't actually hold a straight line. You drift without feeling it."

"Are you saying a group of peak War Lords can't walk straight?" Garrick said, his voice edged with offense.

Zane glanced at him. "I'm saying nobody can. Not in this."

He let out a quiet sigh, gave Garrick an indifferent look, and walked toward him. When he was just an arm’s length away, he ripped a strip of cloth from his own sleeve. He nodded toward an empty patch of fog. “Come here.”

“What are you planning?” Garrick eyed him cautiously, his hand inching toward his weapon.

“Relax. I just need you for an experiment,” Zane said simply, crossing his arms. “I’m not going to fight you.”

Zane held up the strip of cloth. Looking Garrick directly in the eye, he said, “I need you to walk. No flying, no Vitality, no special senses. Just walk forward on instinct.”

“What is this?” Garrick asked, his confusion written all over his face.

“Just do as I say,” Zane urged.

Garrick glanced back at Malcolm, who gave him a nod and a hard, unflinching stare

[SFX: CLOTH RUSTLING]

Left with no other choice, he let out a defeated sigh. Zane immediately used the cloth to blindfold him. “Now,” Zane said, his voice right behind him. “Move forward.”

[AMBIENT: ECHOING FOOTSTEPS IN VOID]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DRONE]

Garrick took a hesitant first step into the gray void.

“Go on, keep going!” Zane’s voice prodded from behind.

With that constant encouragement, Garrick’s hesitation slowly faded, and his steps grew quicker and more confident. He crossed one hundred feet, then two hundred, then three hundred. Soon, he’d gone over four hundred feet, his figure gradually swallowed by the fog like a stone sinking into a lake.

“Where am I?” his voice called out, thin and cracking with worry. “Can I stop now?” Garrick wasn’t exactly brave, and being alone and blind in the Dark Nether Cave was getting to him. Who knew what kind of monster might leap out and swallow him whole? He could feel his heart hammering against his ribs.

Malcolm saw something odd and started to speak, but Zane held up a hand to stop him.

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHISPER]

At the same time, Zane used his Vitality to send a thought directly to Garrick. “Go! Keep going! You’re almost there!”

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

Hearing the urgent voice in his head, Garrick didn’t hesitate. He broke into a run, pushing forward.

Everyone else watched, their eyes glued to Garrick’s every move.

[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]

A collective gasp went through the group as they saw him complete a wide, perfect circle, ending up right back where he started, standing beside Zane. As he came to a stop, Zane pulled the blindfold from his eyes.

Garrick blinked, his eyes adjusting to the dim light. He saw everyone standing around him, staring. Tilting his head, he asked nervously, “You… you all followed me?”

They just shook their heads.

"But I was walking in a straight line," Garrick said, bewildered.

"You thought you were," Zane said. "In normal conditions, you aim for something — a peak, a tree line, a point on the horizon. Here, all of that is gone. Your body starts to compensate for things that aren't there, and before long you've curved without feeling it."

[MUSIC: TENSE REALIZATION]

A dawning horror crept over Zane. He realized that while Garrick had been walking, the rest of them hadn’t been standing still. They had drifted—sometimes left, sometimes right, sometimes far, sometimes unnervingly close. It was as if they were all trapped in a shifting, invisible dimension. He didn’t dare say it out loud, though. It would only cause more panic.

What he also didn't say was that he had his own compass. His Storage Ring had been growing warmer since they'd entered the fog — the Crystal Butterfly's flame tracking the inheritance ahead. Hotter meant moving toward the core. Cooler meant moving away. As long as he followed the heat, he had a bearing.

But the fog kept interfering. The temperature lurched — rising, dropping, rising again — not smoothly but in sudden shifts, as if the distances inside the fog were stretching and compressing at random. They weren't just disoriented. The space itself wasn't behaving.

He said nothing about this. There was no point.

For now, they all started moving forward again, but far more cautiously. It wasn’t just Zane now; others began stopping to leave marks on the ground, desperate for some point of reference.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[SFX: SLOW FOOTSTEPS]

Five minutes passed. Then ten. Then fifteen. They were still walking, but they were getting nowhere.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

The minutes bled into hours. After two hours, they were still pushing on, exhaustion setting in. After four, their spirits—and their hopes of ever escaping—had hit rock bottom. The marks they’d been leaving were useless. They’d find the first mark, but then, instead of the second, the sixth would suddenly appear right in front of them. It was maddening.

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]

A suffocating panic seized them. They weren’t just lost in a fog; they were trapped in a massive, invisible maze.
