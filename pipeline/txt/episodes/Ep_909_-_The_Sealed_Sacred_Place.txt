Ep 909 - The Sealed Sacred Place

Word count: 1085

[AMBIENT: MYSTERIOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane stared at the arched gateway of the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, a silent observer. 

He knew that there were mysteries waiting on the other side, and that getting through wouldn't be simple. But this wasn’t the first time the four rulers had come here. They had to have a plan.

As if on cue, the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom and War King Wind stepped forward. Instead of charging the gateway, they flanked it, moving to opposite sides. The two men produced matching red, dragon-shaped keys from their Storage Rings and slid them into small, hidden holes in the stone. After sharing a brief nod, they turned the keys in unison.

[SFX: STONE GRINDING GEARS]

With a jarring crack, the sound of grinding gears echoed through the air. The Emperor and War King Wind stepped back as the massive arched gateway began to rotate.

It spun three full times before stopping. The red light that had been glowing in the eyes of the dragon sculpture overhead faded, replaced by a soft, jade-green light. 

[SFX: MAGICAL HUM]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY]

A moment later, a shimmering teleportation array materialized in the center of the arch.

The sudden appearance of the portal left Zane stunned. A teleportation array? he thought, confused. The Secret Land was right there in front of them. Why would they need this? It seemed redundant.

The Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom noticed his expression. “What you see before you is an illusion,” he explained. “It’s incredibly dangerous. Walk in there, and you’re walking to your death. In the past, many from the Union of Seven Kingdoms were fooled by it. A lot of them died.”

“An illusion,” Zane muttered to himself. The beautiful, lifelike city was just a trap, designed to mislead invaders. He was floored. If they had walked in there blindly, their chances of survival would have been close to zero.

“Only through this teleportation array can we enter the real Heavenly Stellar Secret Land,” the Emperor continued. “It’s open. Let’s go.”

Wasting no more time, the masters of the Soul Sea Realm filed into the array one after another. Zane nodded and followed them through.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CRYSTAL CITY]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL WONDER]

After a brief, disorienting shimmer of spatial travel, an azure city materialized before him. The entire metropolis looked as though it were carved from a single, massive crystal. The edges of every building, large and small, pulsed with a soft, jade-blue light, making the city feel like something straight out of a fairytale—something completely unreal.

“It’s so beautiful,” Fiona couldn’t help but exclaim.

Beside her, Randy nodded in agreement. “It really is. Honestly, I’d be more willing to believe this city is the illusion, not the other one.”

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]

The city's beauty ran deeper than crystal and light. This place had a history.

In the early days, the Dragon-Blooded Holy Land and the seven Divine Kingdoms had developed in parallel, largely unaware of each other. When their borders finally touched, the first contact was friendly enough. It lasted less than ten years.

What followed was a war spanning tens of thousands of years. The Dragon-Blooded held an enormous advantage — the Dragon Soar Arena. While ordinary warriors needed to reach the Soul Sea Realm before ascending to the upper world, the Dragon-Blooded could send their young warriors up to train and return long before then. 

By the time the seven kingdoms had grown into quasi-seventh-rank forces, the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land was already eighth-rank.

The kingdoms would have been swallowed whole if the upper world hadn't intervened. Through the Reverse Spirit Channel, human forces from above began sending aid — weapons, resources, reinforcements. Only then did the kingdoms find the strength to fight back.

The Dragon Slaying War lasted over a hundred years. When it ended, three of the seven kingdoms had been destroyed. The Dragon-Blooded, rather than surrender, performed a blood sacrifice — billions of lives consumed in an instant — sealing the entire Sacred Feather Sea and destroying the ascension path in the same stroke.

Every Soul Sea master who had ever set foot in this place carried the bitterness of that last fact. They had gained the Dragon-Blooded inheritance. They had grown powerful because of it. And they could never ascend because of it. All the treasure in the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land couldn't change the fact that their lifespans would one day run out, and they would die here in the Lower World.

The place was beautiful, but it was a beauty born from a horrifying past. Most of the clan members had no idea what was planned; when the ritual began, they simply vanished into clouds of bloody mist, leaving nothing behind. That mist became a golden spell, sealing the entire Sacred Feather Sea for countless years, until the Union of the Seven Kingdoms finally discovered the ruins.

The city itself looked frozen in time. Windows stood open. Doors hung wide. Flying chariots sat scattered across rooftops exactly where they'd been left, a hundred thousand years of dust the only sign that anything had changed.

[SFX: ECHOING FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

The footsteps of the eighty-odd warriors echoed as they entered the empty city, the sound setting many of the younger members on edge. Seeing Fiona, Callan, and a few others looking so nervous, War King Wind let out a hearty laugh.

“Easy, kids. Don’t be afraid,” he said. “The dangers of this place aren’t here. This is just an empty city. We’ve been here many times before, and we’ve never run into a single living thing.”

It was true. Because of the blood sacrifice, there were no creatures left alive in the Sacred Feather Sea. In all his travels across the sea and its three secret lands, Zane had only encountered one person who was even remotely alive: Brianna, the girl whose body he now inhabited. And since her soul was trapped, separated from her body, she was essentially among the living dead.

“Okay,” Fiona said, managing a smile and a nod. Still, the empty sacred land gave her an uneasy feeling she couldn't shake. An inexplicable jitteriness settled under her skin.

[SFX: BONE CHEWING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

That’s when she heard it—a faint rustling, like an animal chewing on bones.

[SFX: SHARP CRACK]

A sharp crack, and then another.

“Huh?” Fiona froze at the strange noise, her head snapping around to scan their surroundings. 

But the sound was already gone.
