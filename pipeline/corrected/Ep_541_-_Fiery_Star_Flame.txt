The immortal’s power was beyond anything the practitioners from the Heartlands could even comprehend.
Even elders from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm struggled against the punishments; most didn't last long.
The rewards, however, were just as mind-blowing. Five hundred years of life, granted in a single stroke—that was a power only a legendary immortal could wield.
[SFX: CRACKLING FLAMES]
"I've never even heard of Fiery Star Flame. What kind of fire is that?"
"Whatever it is, Zane's a goner. I can feel the heat all the way from here. It's a real shame, too. Bloodwood Cliff was thinking of hiring him to build a Sect-protecting array. We'd even set aside a pretty hefty budget for it."
"How is he so unlucky?" Brent muttered, pursing his lips. He hadn't expected Zane to get hit with a punishment after just three steps.
Even Kieran from the World Merchants' Guild frowned. "His aura is strong and positive, and his karmic luck is off the charts. I've never seen anything like it, honestly. I don't get why it would suddenly desert him here."
The ancient tycoon watched the purple light enveloping Zane and smiled faintly. "It's true, the kid's fortune is strong. But a lucky break is just a moment. True luck is what lasts a lifetime."
Kieran didn't reply, but he thought to himself that a lifetime of luck was a long way off. First, Zane had to survive this.
[AMBIENT: INTENSE HEAT WAVES]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane squinted, watching the purple flames float around him. It had been a long time since his Spirit Weapon Body had gotten any stronger. And even though the Cobalt Dragon's seventh brother had helped forge his body, he knew it was nowhere near its full potential.
Using different kinds of rare flames to temper the body was the key—but such flames were nearly impossible to find. He'd encountered a few before, but they'd had no real effect. They simply weren't strong enough.
But these flames… Zane narrowed his eyes. Even before the Fiery Star Flame reached him, he could already feel its terrifying power.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
Then something strange happened. Golden symbols began to bloom across the surface of his skin, swirling into vortexes—and those vortexes began to pull. A fierce, hungry suction force burst outward from his body, drawing the flames toward him before they could even fully descend.
His whole body was trembling. Not in pain—in hunger. It was craving the Fiery Star Flame.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
The suction was so powerful it pulled in every last tongue of flame, leaving none behind. Zane sat upright and still, letting the fire pour into him. The golden light blazed across his skin. His entire body turned red, like a statue cast from molten iron—and yet, he felt no pain. If anything… he felt warm. Comfortable. Almost lazy.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Tia stared at the spot where Zane was, her fists clenched tight. She'd done her research on him, of course. She knew his body was special. Back at the Skyreach Sect, they said his physique was like a Spirit Weapon—impervious to blades and able to withstand fire.
But the flames he’d faced before were nothing compared to this Fiery Star Flame. She had no idea if he could actually take it.
Tia was staring, wide-eyed, at the purple light. But then, her face suddenly flushed bright red, and she whipped her head aside.
What was going on?
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
As the last of the light vanished, the reason became clear. Zane was sitting on the round disk, perfectly safe. Except… he was completely naked. The Fiery Star Flame had incinerated his clothes.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHOIR]
His body, now on full display, seemed to hum with a faint glow. The crowd was stunned into silence by the sight of his near-perfect form.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
Besides Tia, every other warrior stared at Zane in utter shock, including Lady Darkmere and the Holy Maiden of the Divine Kingdom.
The Holy Maiden’s gaze fell on Zane, sitting not far away. For the first time, a flicker of genuine surprise crossed her face.
[MUSIC: CONFIDENT STRINGS]
Her big brother was right: in her eyes, everyone here was as insignificant as an ant. And while she knew this trial was about luck, whose luck could possibly be better than hers?
The kid was only in the Soul Realm, and his luck didn't seem that great—he'd only moved three steps in two rolls. But his strength… that was clearly something else entirely.
The man from the Divine Kingdom and the other warriors in his group were just as stunned.
A Soul Realm practitioner like him, suppressed by the tomb's energy, should have been no stronger than an Adept. So how could he have blocked the Fiery Star Flame? Even a Life-and-Death Realm warrior would have struggled against it. It seemed impossible.
"All I can say," Kieran said, shaking his head with a smile, "is that this young man is never short of surprises."
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]
Meanwhile, Zane glanced down at his chest—and froze. Three marks. He hadn't expected that.
The seventh brother's voice came to him, calm and indifferent. "This is a blessing in disguise. In others' eyes, this was misfortune—but for you, it was anything but. True great luck isn't a smooth road. It's walking into adversity and turning it into your own destiny. The Fiery Star Flame is a fire hot enough to forge stars. It was exactly what your body needed. A pity, though… You were so close to the next threshold."
Three marks. Zane's heart leapt. He was now a high-grade Mythic Weapon body—one step away from Relic-Class.
Zane would have been happy if the flames had just tempered his body into a low-grade weapon. He never imagined they'd be powerful enough to forge it all the way into a high-grade Mythic Weapon.
No wonder I felt such a strong pull toward the Fiery Star Flame, he thought. He hadn't understood it then, but it all made sense now.
Once his body reached the strength of a high-grade Mythic Weapon, he was only one step away from becoming a Relic-Class weapon. And for Zane, a stronger body meant he could borrow more Dragon Scales.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
A thousand? Two thousand? Five thousand?
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
What would happen if he unleashed that kind of terrifying power? Zane wasn't sure if it was enough to take down Maddox just yet, but he licked his lips at the thought. His eyes found Maddox, standing not far away.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Maddox suddenly felt a spike of killing intent wash over him, and his brow furrowed. He knew exactly where it was coming from: Zane. The little brat was getting bolder, starting to underestimate him.
He'd have to remind Zane who he was dealing with.
