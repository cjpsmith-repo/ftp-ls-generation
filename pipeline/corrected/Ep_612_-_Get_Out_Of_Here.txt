Ep 612 - Get Out Of Here
Word count: 1148
[AMBIENT: TENSE TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
To put it mildly, the situation looked dire for the Demon Night—and worse for Zane.
Normally, the Demon Night held the advantage. Though they had fewer top-level War Lords than the Ogres, their members' overwhelming strength compensated.
The appearance of Colin and Neil, two Ogre War Sages, changed everything. Both the Demon Night and humans now faced a massive disadvantage.
And yet, Zane still had the nerve to chew out the Ogres. Anyone watching would have sworn he'd lost his mind.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION BUILDING]
The smirks on the War Sages' faces faded, as did the smiles on the other Ogre War Lords. This was supposed to be their day—the day they proved their dominance.
With two War Sages inside the Tower of Sin, their power had skyrocketed. The Ogres' sacred places couldn't match the Demon Night's Heavenly Feather Sanctuary in the outside world, but here? In the Tower? No one could stand against them.
And of course, this brat couldn't help but steal the spotlight.
Colin sneered, his voice cutting through the tension. “So this is Zane, the so-called superhuman talent. You think having the Demon Night at your back means you can run your mouth? Or are you just too stupid to realize what’s happening?”
Even Esther Sterling and the elite Demon Night warriors grew furious with Zane. They'd sworn to protect him for their queen, even at the cost of their lives, but with Lavender still bound to him, they had no choice. Esther wondered how to extract him alive. After his provocation, reconciliation seemed impossible. The Ogres controlled the Tower now.
[AMBIENT: WHISPERED TENSION]
Fabian muttered to Jarrod, using his Vitality to send a private message. “What the hell is that guy doing? He can’t possibly be this dumb.”
“You think he has another ace up his sleeve?” Jarrod sent back, just as baffled. Nothing about Zane’s behavior made any sense.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Only Olivia watched Zane with a glint in her eye. He had a trump card; she was sure of it. What could he possibly have that made him fearless in the face of two Ogre War Sages?
Olivia couldn’t guess, but she knew she didn’t need to. She was confident Zane could turn this around, no matter how hopeless it looked.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
Esther Sterling took matters into her own hands.“Zane, that's enough out of you!” Forcing her temper down for the queen’s sake, Esther turned to face the Ogres. "Colin, Neil—I don't care how you got in here. But you cannot touch Zane. Think carefully. There's still room to negotiate."
Colin laughed. It wasn't a friendly sound.
"Negotiate?" Neil's voice was flat. "The only negotiation is that the boy dies. Then we'll see exactly what the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary does about it."
The two War Sages let their power loose.
[SFX: DEEP RESONANT PULSE]
[MUSIC: DARK PRESSURE THEME]
The wave of energy that rolled off them hit the Demon Night War Lords like a wall. Even Esther felt it—a weight pressing down on the air itself, making every breath heavier. The faces around her went tight. Everyone understood what two War Sages unleashing their power at once meant.
A fight was no longer avoidable.
[MUSIC: SHARP TENSION BREAK]
Across the crowd, Zane laughed.
It wasn't nervous laughter. It wasn't defiant posturing. He pointed directly at the two War Sages, his voice carrying clean across the chamber.
"What a pity. You went through all that trouble to get in here—and now you're about to die."
A confused silence rippled through the floor. Every eye turned to him.
Esther stared. "Zane. Do you know what you're saying?"
"Of course," he said.
"You're certain you can deal with two War Sages?"
Zane smiled faintly. He didn't answer directly. Beside him, Lavender stood perfectly still, saying nothing. As his sword spirit, she knew exactly what he'd experienced inside the Talent Tablet. She already understood what was coming.
Zane looked back at the two War Sages. His voice dropped, quiet and absolute.
"Both of you. Get out of the Tower of Sin."
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL HIT]
Before anyone could react, two invisible, colossal hands materialized out of thin air and clamped down on the two Ogre War Sages. This was why Zane was so confident. After all, he wasn't just some talented rookie; he had achieved Celestial Rank, an honor almost unheard of.
Zane had obtained the recognition of the Tower itself, and the Tower's spirit followed his word.
[MUSIC: STUNNED SILENCE THEME]
[SFX: GASPS FROM THE CROWD]
The realization hit the room all at once. The Tower of Sin was alive. It had a consciousness. Everyone had theorized it for years—the way it absorbed the blood of fallen warriors, swallowed their weapons, held the memory of every battle fought inside its walls. But no one had ever made contact with that consciousness. No one had ever earned its recognition.
Until now.
The invisible force hadn't come from any living creature. It had come from the Tower itself.
[SFX: RUMBLING STONE]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING TOWER]
The two invisible hands began dragging the War Sages toward a crack in the tower wall. Maurice, Malcolm, and the other top-tier Ogre War Lords could only watch in frozen terror. There was nothing they could do.
If the hands had grabbed a couple of their War Lords, it wouldn’t have been a big deal. They’d just get tossed out of the Tower of Sin, and a trip to the Cursed Land outside wouldn’t hurt them.
But for a War Sage, being thrown into the Cursed Land was a death sentence.
[SFX: RUMBLING EARTH]
The entire chamber held its breath, watching as the two Ogre War Sages were unceremoniously hauled away. The Tower itself was buried deep underground, and the invisible hands dragged them straight through the tenth-floor walls, up through layers of solid earth, and finally burst through the surface into the open air.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR WIND]
Standing beside the Tower’s entrance, Colin and Neil stared at each other, sheer panic written across their faces. Moments ago, the two Sages had been radiating overwhelming pride. Now, they could feel Death itself closing in.
“We have to get back in!”
If they could just make it back inside the Tower of Sin, they might have a chance. The curse’s power wasn’t instantaneous.
[SFX: WHOOSHING ENERGY]
Fueled by terror, they both unleashed every ounce of energy they had and shot toward the tower entrance like twin comets.
The two giant guards at the gate saw the blurs of light screaming toward them, felt the immense power radiating off them, and wisely scrambled out of the way to see what would happen.
[MUSIC: DARK HORROR THEME]
Colin and Neil were moving at an incredible speed, but it wasn't fast enough. Black spots were already beginning to bloom across their skin.
