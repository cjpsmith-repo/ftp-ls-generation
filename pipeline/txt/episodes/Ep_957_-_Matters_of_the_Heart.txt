Ep 957 - Matters of the Heart
Word count: 1020
[MUSIC: ROMANTIC STRINGS]
Cora melted into Zane’s embrace, lost in a bubble of pure bliss. In this moment, nothing existed but him. Right here, right now. As long as he was with her, nothing else mattered.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
But that smile was a dagger twisting in Tia’s heart.
[SFX: SHARP RING]
A sharp ring echoed as the ice halberd slipped from her numb fingers.
[SFX: ICE SHATTERING]
It shattered on the ground, exploding into a thousand crystal shards of Vitality that dissolved back into the essence of the world.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]
“Master, I want to go back,” Tia murmured, turning her back on the scene. Her posture was ramrod straight, every step elegant even in retreat. She was an Ice Phoenix returning to Cloud Hall—heartbroken, but with her dignity intact. Zane, however, could see the pain she was trying so hard to hide.
“Tia!”
“Tia!”
The shouts came at the same time, one from Zane, the other from Elder Imogen.
Elder Imogen shot Zane a look that could melt steel before hurrying after her student.
[AMBIENT: QUIET HALL]
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
A few elders lingered, and one of them, Saint Morphens, offered Zane a wry, sympathetic smile.
The young man’s path to power had been a thing of legend, his uncanny luck seeing him through one near-death experience after another. His love life, on the other hand, was a total disaster.
It wasn’t unheard of for powerful warriors to have multiple partners. Even a regular Soul Realm warrior in The Heartlands might have a few companions.
But Tia was different. Saint Morphens had a sinking feeling she would never accept sharing Zane. On top of that, Imogen was so fiercely protective she’d never let it stand. He could only hope Zane was smart enough to figure this out on his own.
Zane’s first instinct was to go after her, but Cora was still curled up in his arms like a frightened kitten. She seemed completely overwhelmed, and he was totally out of his depth. This wasn’t his arena. He was a warrior, forged in battle and dedicated to the martial path. He didn’t know the first thing about matters of the heart.
[AMBIENT: ICE PALACE]
[SFX: WIND HOWLING]
Meanwhile, Tia had retreated to her ice palace, where a cold mist swirled through the air.
There was loneliness in her smile — and something else underneath. Stubbornness. She wasn’t even sure herself what she was being stubborn about. Did she resent Cora? Or Zane? She didn’t know.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]
The icy mist parted. Elder Imogen stepped through it slowly, her footsteps quiet on the cold floor.
Tia looked up and forced a smile. “Master.”
Elder Imogen studied her for a long moment. “If you’re sad, don’t force it.”
“I’m not sad,” Tia said, still smiling.
“I was your age once,” Elder Imogen said quietly. “Don’t pretend with me.”
Tia fell silent. There wasn’t much to say to that.
Elder Imogen sat down beside her. Outside, she had been firmly on Tia’s side — loudly, publicly, without hesitation. But here, alone, her voice was different. “No matter how you look at it, Zane is an exceptional man. And warriors live long lives. Taking more than one partner — it’s not unheard of. It’s not even unusual.”
Tia’s fingers moved. Tiny snowflakes began to form in her palm, spreading outward from their four corners until a single palm-sized snowflake rested in her hand, turning slowly. “If he’s so exceptional,” she said, “then every woman in the world should marry him.”
Elder Imogen let out a short laugh. “Every woman in the world? He’d never survive it. But you’re half right.” She paused. “You’re too stubborn about this. And if you want my honest opinion — Cora is smarter than you.”
“Smarter?” Tia looked up, genuinely stung.
“Not in talent. Not in martial arts. In this.” Elder Imogen gestured vaguely at the space between them. “Emotional intelligence. You’re still a child in that regard. So is Zane, for that matter — which is half of why you’re both in this mess.”
[MUSIC: RELUCTANT UNDERSTANDING]
Elder Imogen wasn’t wrong, and Tia knew it. Cora had followed Zane and quietly set aside every difficult edge of her character. No demands. No conditions. She had made Zane her entire world and stepped into it without hesitation. It was a simple thing — and it was devastating in its simplicity.
How many people wouldn’t respond to that?
Tia was quiet for a long moment. Finally, a flicker of understanding lit up her eyes.
“I can’t do it right now,” she said.
Elder Imogen let out a quiet sigh of relief. Can’t do it now wasn’t the same as never. It looked like her student was finally seeing reason.
[AMBIENT: QUIET CLOUDHAVEN CORNER]
Meanwhile, in a quiet corner of Cloudhaven, Zane and Xander managed to get a moment alone. Xander immediately launched into thanking him for helping rebuild the Ethereal Spirit Sect. Without him, the Corrupted Divine Kingdom would still be using their ancestral home as a base of operations, and the sect would have been nothing but a memory.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
But as Zane listened, his mind kept drifting back to Tia. The image of her, expressionless as she floated away, made his chest ache.
Xander was sharp. He’d stepped up for Cora earlier because she had no one else. But with Zane here, it wasn’t his fight anymore. Besides, from the looks of it, Cora was winning. And if he was being honest, Zane had made this mess himself.
After they finished talking, Xander and Brent got up to leave.
[SFX: SHOULDER CLAP]
As they walked away, Brent grinned and clapped Zane on the shoulder. “So, when’s the wedding? You gonna make an honest woman out of my sister?”
[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
The second the men from the Ethereal Spirit Sect were gone, Cora’s soft smile vanished. Her expression turned serious.
“I think you should go to Cloud Hall,” she suggested, her voice even.
“What? Why? I’m not going,” Zane insisted.
A small, knowing smile touched her lips. “Are you sure about that?”
