Ep 1259 - Weapons

Word count: 1211

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: WINDY CITY WALL]
Zane dug his heels into the brick of the city wall, pouring everything he had into dragging the palm-sized skull inside. He unleashed a torrent of raw power, the kind of strength that could level mountains. 
[SFX: STONE GRINDING]
A normal wall would have crumbled like a sandcastle under the strain, but this city in the Illusion Battlefield was indestructible. The stone beneath his feet didn’t even tremble.
[SFX: METALLIC IMPACT]
The full force of his effort slammed into the small skull, yet it wouldn’t budge. It was like hitting an invisible, unbreakable barrier at the city’s edge. Even activating his Dragon Scales didn’t give him enough leverage to drag the damn thing over the line. 
As he struggled, the skull ground its teeth against his palm, its tiny fangs sinking into his fingers. A sharp, searing pain shot up his arm, and he was forced to let go.
[SFX: TEETH GRINDING]
[SFX: BONE FANGS PIERCING FLESH]
[SFX: DRAGON SCALES ACTIVATING]
It was impossible. The city’s indestructible barrier, the very thing he’d hoped to use against the skulls, was also what kept them out. 
He now understood that these things were a curse from the Sorcerer Race, designed to attack a target relentlessly, and the city’s rules were preventing them from entering. The only way to drag one in would be to have enough power to shatter the city’s defenses—and if he was that strong, he could just crush the skulls with his bare hands anyway.
Zane watched the skulls bobbing in the air just beyond his reach. 
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
He shook his head and paced along the top of the wall, the whole swarm trailing him like a shadow.
“These stupid things,” he muttered.
He was at a loss, staring at the swarm of chattering skulls. Below, a crowd of Ogre martial artists watched him pace, their confusion growing with every step. They had no idea what he was trying to do.
After a few more steps, Zane stopped, a new idea taking shape. 
[SFX: POWERFUL LEAP]
He suddenly leaped from the rampart, snatched another skull from the air, and, with a massive surge of power, hurled it straight at the city wall. 
[SFX: SKULL WHISTLING THROUGH AIR]
The skull shot forward like a cannonball, a tiny, bone-white meteor.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT EXPLOSION]
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER REVERB]
It slammed into the same invisible barrier, stopping dead in its tracks.
[SFX: RICOCHET WHISTLE]
The skull bounced off at a vicious angle and buried itself straight into the ground outside the city, vanishing beneath the dirt entirely.
[SFX: MUFFLED UNDERGROUND RUMBLING]
For a while, there was no telling how far it had traveled underground. Then, some distance away, it burst back up out of the earth, righted itself, and shot straight back to Zane, sinking its teeth into his back once more.
[SFX: SKULL BITING]
A pair of Ogre warriors nearby exchanged a low murmur, thinking themselves quiet enough not to be heard.
“Huh. Guess that Human brat’s trying to get rid of the things chasing him.”
“No idea where he found those. Tough as they are, I’d bet they’re sturdier than a Divine Weapon.”
Zane’s mood, already sour, curdled further — and something in his expression shifted.
“Fine,” he muttered. “If I can’t get rid of you, I’ll just farm points off you instead.”
The nearest Ogre warriors caught the change in him immediately, faces going pale. One of them, quicker than the rest, bolted for the city gate — figuring that once inside, he’d be safe.
Zane didn’t give him the chance.
[SFX: SKULL HURTLING THROUGH AIR]
He hurled one of the little skulls after him with tremendous force.
[SFX: HELMET SHATTERING]
[SFX: WET IMPACT]
The skull tore straight through the warrior’s helmet and caved his skull in like an overripe melon. A burst of Illusion Points scattered into the air, drifting toward Zane.
He didn’t bother counting them. He just pocketed the points, yanked another skull free from the swarm on his back, and went to work — flinging them at every Ogre warrior still in range.
[SFX: RAPID IMPACTS]
[SFX: BODIES FALLING]
In just a few breaths, seven or eight Ogre warriors lay dead. The rest scattered — some fleeing into the safety of the city, others bolting for the wilderness rather than risk coming anywhere near him.
Zane didn’t bother chasing anyone down. He simply circled the city at a walk, one skull gripped tight in his fist, cutting down any warrior unlucky enough to still be outside. The stronger ones took a bit more effort. The weaker ones died in a single strike.
Inside the walls, despair set in fast. None of them had expected a threat like this to simply appear out of nowhere — and a human, of all things.
By his second full circuit around the city, there wasn’t a single warrior left outside the walls.
“That’s about as clean as it’s going to get,” Zane said, shaking his head. “Time to move on.”
It was a full half hour before any of the city’s warriors dared step back outside, casting wary looks in the direction he’d disappeared.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
The Twilight Group might be a minor race in the Upper World, but their architecture was incredibly detailed and precise.
He took it all in as he walked toward the city walls. He’d run into their assassins before on the main continent. They were experts at what they did, but a knack for stealth and backstabbing could only get you so far. They were never going to be a major power.
His eye twitched as he reached the city walls. 
[SFX: EERIE SKULL HOVERING HUM]
The hundreds of little skulls were already there, hovering just outside the barrier, waiting for him. The Teleportation Token had thrown him at least ten million miles, but it was as if the skulls had simply ignored the distance, arriving the exact moment he did. 
For one brief moment, back in that strange in-between space right after teleporting, he’d actually thought he might have finally shaken them. The magic of the Sorcerer Race really was something else.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
[AMBIENT: ABANDONED CITY RUINS]
Zane figured he might as well use the skulls to his advantage—let them “clean up” the Twilight Group’s city for him. After all, their famed assassination skills were useless against his Magic Vision. It made clearing this place out much easier than the ogre city; after he picked off the first few who dared to show their faces, the rest of them stayed hidden behind their walls.
With the city effectively neutralized, it was time to move on. Zane’s expression was cold as he quietly left. This time, however, he didn’t pull out his Teleportation Token. 
[SFX: METAL SCRAPING]
Instead, he produced the iron ticket.
He’d wiped out the easy targets in two cities and racked up a decent number of Illusion Points, but his rank was still dropping. His upper-world ranking had slipped below three hundred, and his overall rank in the Illusion Battlefield had fallen past seventeen thousand.
If he kept picking off low-hanging fruit around these sanctuaries, his rank wasn’t just going to stagnate—it was going to keep falling.
