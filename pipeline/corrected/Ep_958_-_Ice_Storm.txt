Ep 958 - Ice Storm
Word count: 1167
[MUSIC: SOFT ROMANTIC PIANO]
Zane stared at Cora’s knowing smile for a long moment. Then he sighed. She was right, and they both knew it.
He had to go to Cloud Hall.
Cora might have been a little clueless about most things, but when it came to love, she saw things with perfect clarity.
She knew she couldn’t have Zane all to herself. And while a part of her hated the idea of other women getting close to him, she understood he was destined for greatness. He was already the strongest person in the Heartlands at his age; there was no way he was going to be stuck in some backwater region forever.
Just getting to be with a man that powerful and talented would be more than enough for her. Who was she to try and hold him back?
Tia had stormed off, but Cora let her go. It wasn’t that Cora felt sorry for her—she was no pushover—but she knew how important Tia was to Zane. She’d only spoken up because she couldn’t stand to see him so torn up.
“And do what? What am I supposed to say to her?” Zane asked, forcing a weak smile.
Cora smiled back, leaning in to whisper in his ear. “Doesn’t every girl want to be pursued a little? She might be a hall leader, but she’s just as clueless as anyone when it comes to love. Just go say something sweet. It’ll make her feel better.”
A real smile touched Zane’s lips this time. He kissed her lightly on the forehead. “You sound like you’re an expert.”
Cora’s cheeks flushed. She playfully rolled her eyes, her gaze turning coy. “Hey, I’m not that experienced! I’m just trying to help you out.”
Cora’s advice settled something in him. The whole situation was a mess, he knew that, but she was right. Some things you just had to face head-on.
[SFX: RUSTLING FABRIC]
[SFX: CREATURE SQUAWK]
After he got Cora settled, Zane turned to head for the Cloud Hall. But just then, the Ancestral Dragon in the bestiary bag on his back poked its tiny head out. It let out a happy squawk at Cora, its eyes bright with curiosity.
“This little guy,” Zane said, shaking his head with a small, fond smile.
He’d gotten the small bestiary bag from the Demon Night members back in the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary. While his Spatial Sanctum couldn’t hold living things, the bag was designed specifically for that purpose. The space inside wasn’t massive, but it was more than enough for the young Ancestral Dragon to curl up in.
The little guy was probably just desperate for some fresh air after being cooped up for so long, and something about Cora must have caught its attention.
[SFX: SMALL THUD — CREATURE LANDING]
Before Zane could react, the dragon had wriggled free of the bag entirely and launched itself at Cora, landing against her with a soft thud and nuzzling in. Cora let out a surprised laugh.
Zane watched, puzzled. The dragon was bonded to him — that was expected. But why Cora? Was it something to do with her Violet-Core Physique? Some connection he didn’t understand yet? He couldn’t work it out. He filed it away and decided to leave the dragon with her for now. It seemed happy enough.
[SFX: WIND RUSH — FAST DEPARTURE]
He turned and flew toward the Cloud Hall.
[AMBIENT: ICY CAVERN]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The Cloud Hall’s sect-protecting array was still active, but Zane went through the main entrance. The guards at the gate recognized him immediately and stood aside.
Inside, the Ice Palace breathed cold air from every crack and seam. Zane felt it before he reached the entrance — a wave of chill that made even his body register the drop in temperature. He stepped through.
[SFX: ICICLES — WHISTLING THROUGH AIR]
Three pale blue icicles shot out of the mist straight at him.
Zane sidestepped easily. Three more came. He caught these, let them drop to the floor with a crisp clatter, and kept walking.
[SFX: RAPID ICICLE VOLLEY]
They kept coming — faster, harder, in larger clusters. A dozen at a time, then more. Zane moved through them steadily, dodging some, catching others, dropping them as he advanced. By the time he reached the heart of the palace, he’d caught hundreds.
[MUSIC: QUIET STORM]
Tia was sitting barefoot on the cold floor, surrounded by thousands of icicles floating in slow orbit around her. The moment she saw him, the grievance on her face sharpened into fury.
She raised her hand. Every icicle launched at once.
Zane stopped walking. He closed his eyes.
“Dodge!” Tia screamed. “You can’t stop it — dodge!”
It was too late. The volley hit.
[SFX: ICE SHATTERING]
Thousands of icicles struck Zane simultaneously, shattering on contact into crystals that cascaded over him and piled up fast. Within moments, he was buried.
Tia stared. Then she ran.
[SFX: BAREFOOT RUNNING ON ICE]
She dropped to her knees and started digging with her hands — pulling at the shards, throwing them aside, calling his name. It didn’t even occur to her that she could dissolve the ice herself. These were her crystals, her Vitality; a single thought would have cleared them in an instant. But panic had swallowed that thought whole.
A hand shot out from the frozen rubble.
[SFX: ICE CRACKING]
It clamped around her waist, yanking her back as Zane slowly pulled himself out from beneath the shards.
Seeing Zane alive, a wave of shock and dizzying relief washed over Tia. But it was instantly followed by a fresh surge of anger and hurt.
She had been terrified. Her own power had skyrocketed since reaching the Life-and-Death Realm, and she’d poured everything she had into that blast. She never dreamed he could walk away from it without a scratch.
Tia knew Zane’s power had jumped when he became a Life-and-Death Realm warrior, but she had no idea it had grown this much.
“Get your hands off me!” Tia shouted, her voice tight with fury and embarrassment as she struggled in his arms.
But Zane had no intention of letting go. He just tightened his arms around her waist, holding her flush against him. Against his current strength, her struggles were useless.
After a few moments of fruitless struggle, Tia’s energy gave out. Her body went limp in his embrace, but her glare was still pure fire.
[SFX: ICE CRYSTALLIZATION]
[AMBIENT: FREEZING WIND]
Then, a defiant idea sparked. Streams of Vitality erupted from her body, condensing into a storm of ice crystals that swirled around them. The shards wrapped around Zane and herself, forming a massive ice coffin that grew thicker with every passing second, sealing them both inside.
The cold was biting, but the furious look on Tia’s face only fueled the fire he felt for her.
[MUSIC: TENSE ROMANTIC STRINGS]
Zane knew he was terrible at smoothing things over with words. But actions? Actions he could do.
