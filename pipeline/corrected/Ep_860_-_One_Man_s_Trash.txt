Ep 860 - One Man’s Trash

Word count: 1003

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT RUINS]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

As Zane and Fiona passed the returning warriors, a few couldn’t help themselves.

“What’s going on with Princess Fiona of the Wind Divine Kingdom? She’s been following him around all day.”

“She even lent him her semi-divine weapon back at the West Dragon River...”

“That guy has unreal luck with women. If Fiona looked at me like that, I’d die happy.”

They kept their voices low. Neither Zane nor Fiona acknowledged it.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE PATH]

The straight road ran about two or three miles before a long wall came into view. As they drew closer, Zane saw it wasn’t a wall at all — it was a dragon carved in stone, its body coiled into a full circle, its head and tail forming the pillars of the armory’s main entrance.

[AMBIENT: VAST WAREHOUSE]

[MUSIC: EERIE AMBIENCE]

They stepped through the dragon-gate and into a compound of continuous warehouses. A handful of saint-level geniuses were still trickling out, empty-handed and frustrated.

“The weapons should be in here,” Fiona said, glancing around. She walked straight into the nearest warehouse.

[SFX: HEAVY DOOR CREAKING]

Inside, rows of copper chests lined the walls — pure copper, specially treated, still gleaming with a brass luster despite their age. Most had been pried open. Weapons lay scattered across the floor, spilled from their boxes like discarded junk.

At first glance, the weapons looked pristine. Brand new, even. But look a little closer, and you’d see it — a faint black smoke drifting from each one, barely visible, curling up from the blade or haft like a slow exhale.

[SFX: FAINT HISSING]

Fiona crouched and picked up a mid-grade Mythic short spear. She turned it in her hands. A thin wisp of black smoke escaped from the shaft.

“What a strong curse,” she murmured.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Zane barely glanced at the curse. He was thinking about something else entirely. He moved deeper into the warehouse, leaving Fiona to examine the spear.

The copper chests stretched in rows down both sides of the aisle, each one open, the weapons inside thrown into disorder by the warriors who’d already searched them. These were the leftovers — everything the previous scavengers hadn’t bothered to take.

He reached into one of the boxes and pulled out a large saber. Standard Dragon-blooded issue by the look of it. Mid-grade Mythic, but the craftsmanship was extraordinary.

He checked over his shoulder. Fiona was still near the entrance, absorbed in the spear.

He closed his eyes and reached inward, toward the Soulforge.

[SFX: BLACK FLAME IGNITING]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS PULSE]

The black flame answered immediately, coiling out from between his brows. It circled once in the air, almost thoughtfully, then wrapped itself around the saber.

The metal softened. Then it dissolved. The flame gathered what remained — refined it — and condensed it into a single gleaming ball.

Celestial Essence. Enough to light up twenty Dragon Scales from a single mid-grade Mythic weapon.

But Zane didn’t absorb it yet. He was looking at something on the surface of the ball — a faint thread of black smoke, clinging to the Celestial Essence like a stain.

[MUSIC: UNEASY UNDERTONE]

The black flame could melt anything. But it hadn’t burned the curse away. The curse was still there, wrapped around the Essence.

Zane frowned. This was the problem he’d been afraid of. If the curse transferred when he absorbed it, the consequences could be severe.

He thought of something else. The Cobalt Dragon had mentioned once that a large enough supply of Celestial Essence could be used to rebuild a body — not to its original scale, but enough. There were countless weapons in this armory. Mythic weapons as far as he could see, and probably Relic-Class weapons in the deeper warehouses. 

If the Essence from all of them could be refined and used, it might be enough to give the Cobalt Dragon a new form. Not the full length of a true dragon — more like an ordinary flood dragon. But it would be enough to fight.

The only question was the curse.

“Let’s find out.”

[SFX: CELESTIAL ESSENCE ABSORPTION]

He let the ball of Essence enter through his forehead.

[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]

He waited. One second. Five. Ten.

Nothing.

His body had absorbed it cleanly. Above the Soulforge, twenty Dragon Scales lit up — bright green, steady. No curse. No reaction. No pain.

Zane let out a slow breath and smiled. Maybe I was worrying too much.

[SFX: BLACK FLAME SURGING]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT BUILD]

The black flame didn’t wait for further instruction. It drifted forward from between his brows and dove straight into the nearest copper chest.

Inside were over a hundred mid-grade Mythic sabers. The flame moved through them like a wave, and the metal softened and ran as if the blades were candles held to a torch. One after another, they dissolved, and the Celestial Essence streamed downward, drop by drop, each bead catching the light like mercury before joining the growing mass below.

A hundred sabers. More than ten thousand drops of Celestial Essence. Enough to light up two thousand Dragon Scales.

[SFX: BLACK FLAME DRILLING]

The black flame moved from chest to chest without stopping, and the Celestial Essence kept flowing. Zane sat cross-legged on the warehouse floor and absorbed it all.

[SFX: DRAGON SCALES ACTIVATING]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL HUM]

Hours passed. Above the Soulforge, Dragon Scale after Dragon Scale lit up — green light spreading across the Cobalt Dragon’s still-sleeping form like a slow tide coming in.

The Cobalt Dragon was still unconscious, recovering from blocking the Little Monster’s soul attack. He had no idea what was happening. Zane found himself wondering what the dragon’s face would look like when he woke up.

After several hours, Zane paused and counted.

Fourteen thousand Dragon Scales.

A thousand Dragon Scales matched the full physical force of a Soul Sea Realm master. Fourteen thousand was a number he couldn’t fully comprehend yet — the power would be unimaginable. The problem was his body. Even with his current strength, he couldn’t channel that much at once. Not yet.

[MUSIC]

But that would change. It had to.

He looked down the long aisle of copper chests. Most of the mid-grade Mythic weapons in this section were gone now, reduced to Essence and absorbed. 

Somewhere in the deeper warehouses, there would be high-grade Mythic weapons. Relic-Class weapons. More than he could count.

He wasn’t done yet.
