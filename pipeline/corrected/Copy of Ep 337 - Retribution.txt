Ep 337 - Retribution
Word count: 1164

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
The assembled lords exchanged glances of utter disbelief. Zane’s demands bordered on lunacy, but what choice did they really have?
Just moments ago, he’d severed Chuck’s and Ford’s hands without breaking a sweat, leaving the mighty lords of the great houses completely helpless. His ultimatum required not just the return of the properties they’d seized, but additional compensation three times their value.
In truth, if he’d demanded thirty times the amount, their position would remain unchanged—totally powerless to refuse.
A crushing sense of foolishness washed over the lords. They'd actually tried to gauge Zane’s strength, even coming up with elaborate plans to gang up on him. Instead, he had strode confidently into their midst and crushed their ambitions with devastating power that left them trembling.
Chuck said nothing, even though Zane had killed his son. Years of his calculated maneuvering had dragged House Hawthorne to the top of Castermere County, and his shrewd mind saw the futility of resistance against someone this powerful. The blood still dripped from his severed wrist as his face grew increasingly pale.
Every scheme and strategy Chuck might come up with seemed childishly inadequate against Zane’s raw, uncompromising strength. Zane had already taken down the strongest fighters in Castermere County. Chuck’s mind raced desperately for anyone who might be able to stand against him.
[AMBIENT: DEAD SILENCE]
The room was completely silent. Every lord sat paralyzed, their ambitions and schemes reduced to ashes before Zane’s overwhelming might. They were out of options, their spirits shattered.
The vast properties they had schemed and maneuvered to acquire would be returned to House Lowell. Beyond this humiliation, the triple compensation demanded would drain their coffers severely. Such a financial blow would devastate their houses for generations to come.
This bitter reality settled in their stomachs like poison as the silence stretched painfully between them.
[SFX: WATER SWIRLING]
The gentle sound of water swirling broke the stillness. Zane casually played with the porcelain teacup in his hand, its contents dancing in rhythmic patterns without him touching the liquid. The assembled lords watched, transfixed and horrified, as the water obeyed forces beyond their comprehension. Waves of invisible power radiated from the young man, commanding the elements with effortless grace while he regarded them with cold detachment.
Zane spoke softly, his voice carrying through the chamber. "Fascinating how fragile things can be."
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
[SFX: GLASS CRACKING]
The pressure in the room intensified gradually, thickening the air with Zane’s potent Soul Power intertwined with something far more terrifying—a chilling Malice. The delicate glass of the window lattices began to splinter with sharp, staccato cracks that echoed through the chamber like gunshots. Several lords flinched with each fracture, their composure finally breaking.
The Malice continued to build, becoming almost tangible. Throats constricted painfully as breathing grew labored. No training or wealth could shield them from this suffocating display of power. It was impossible to just sit there.
[SFX: WATER BUBBLING]
"If you can’t decide in ten seconds, I’ll make it four times," Zane said slowly. The water in his teacup began to bubble, tiny droplets jumping and splashing as if it were boiling.
"I agree!" the Lord of House Blackwood yelled, finally breaking.
"House Blackwood will return the property to House Lowell! And we’ll pay three times its value as compensation!"
"We agree, too! We’ll compensate!" the Lord of House Hill bit out through gritted teeth. "We’ll return the mines and the gambling businesses to House Lowell!"
In truth, none of them could take it anymore, but each had been trying to be the last one to fold. Now that one had caved, the others lost their nerve and quickly followed suit.
Finally, Chuck nodded, his voice flat. "House Hawthorne also agrees to cover the losses of House Lowell."
"Good. You’ve made the right choice," Zane said with a faint smile.
His eyes then shifted to Ford.
Even though Ford had already agreed, Zane had unfinished business with him. "Ford."
"Where is my father's sword?"
Ford's remaining hand trembled. "I'll give it back. I'll compensate six times — just let me go. Please."
"Six times." Zane shook his head slowly. "My father's death isn't a debt you can pay off."
Ford ran for the door.
[SFX: DOOR THROWN OPEN]
He stopped.
Lily — Zane's sword spirit — was already there, Blood Drinker in hand, her killing intent filling the doorway like a wall of pressure. Ford had no time to speak.
[SFX: MALICE STORM]
[SFX: BLOOD SPLATTER]
It was over in an instant. The lords stared at the doorway. Lily's white clothes were spotless.
No one said a word.

[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL ESTATE GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL THEME]
Having served House Lowell for decades, the old servant’s heart swelled with joy at the recent transformations. The new housekeeper had not just improved working conditions but tripled everyone’s wages—an unprecedented generosity that left him profoundly grateful. How could he not be ecstatic? Master Zane had breathed new life into the once-declining estate, restoring both its dignity and prosperity.
[SFX: SWEEPING BROOM]
[SFX: GATE CREAKING]
The servant meticulously swept the cobblestones as he moved with renewed purpose. The morning sun cast long shadows across the courtyard when the massive oak gate creaked open, revealing a striking young man whose confident stride betrayed his noble bearing.
"Good morning," Master Zane acknowledged with a respectful nod, his voice carrying the quiet authority that had come to define him. Behind him followed a girl so beautiful it took the servant’s breath away. She smiled, and it felt like the heavens parted just for him.
The servant stood transfixed, broom suspended mid-sweep, as the pair walked past. Only after they had vanished around the corner did he realize he’d been too awestruck to return their greeting.
He leaned against his broom, watching the space where they had been, a wistful thought crossing his mind. The master of House Lowell... leaving just like that. Something in the young lord’s determined gait suggested this was no ordinary outing, but perhaps the beginning of another remarkable journey.
[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE TRANSITION]
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING COUNTY]
The problems in Castermere County were more or less settled. Even with Zane gone, House Lowell was set to thrive. It was now the top house in the county, even if its members weren’t the strongest fighters. The compensation from the five Great Houses alone amounted to nearly sixty percent of all the wealth in Castermere County.
Zane didn’t have much left to do here. He had more important things to deal with elsewhere.
He remembered how Isla Novak hadn’t shown up until the very end of the All Peaks Competition.
[MUSIC: TENSE POLITICAL]
It was common knowledge that the princes were all vying for the throne of the Empire. Except for the Skyreach Sect, which refused to pick a side, the Seven Great Houses and countless smaller families had all thrown their weight behind a prince, hoping for a massive payout when their choice took the throne. The air in the Imperial City had grown thick with tension.
Isla was Kane Novak’s eldest daughter. As the Sky Princess, she was inevitably caught up in the struggle.
The world of politics was cruel. Zane worried about the proud princess.
Was his dignified, noble princess safe?
He had to find out.
