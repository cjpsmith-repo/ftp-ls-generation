Ep 523 - The Madness Trap
Word count: 1240
[AMBIENT: TENSE CITY ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
In just half a month, suffocating dread had settled over Cloud Hall, the city of Cloudhaven, and the five Second-Grade sects under its command. The tension hung thick in the air—a constant, sharp-edged fear that kept everyone glancing over their shoulders, wondering when the storm would finally break.
Emanuel had ensured the story of what Zane did at the Martial Tournament spread like wildfire through every corner of the Heartlands.
[MUSIC: DARK DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
The bare facts alone defied belief: a Soul Realm warrior had killed Maddox's woman right in front of him and simply walked away without a scratch. No retaliation. No consequences. Just silence from the most feared man in the region.
The news itself sent shockwaves through the cultivation world, but the rumors—fueled by exaggeration and embellishment with each retelling—transformed Zane into something far more terrifying than a mere prodigy. Whispers painted him as an unstoppable demon, a force of nature that even the mighty Maddox dared not challenge. Some claimed he possessed forbidden techniques from ancient times. Others swore he'd made a pact with entities beyond mortal comprehension.
His story became a legend whispered in hushed tones in every tavern and teahouse, spoken with equal parts awe and terror. Parents invoked his name to frighten disobedient children. Young cultivators debated whether such power was even possible for someone so young. And through it all, one question echoed in everyone's mind: if Maddox wouldn't touch him, who possibly could?
Rumors swirled—about Tia's decision, about the looming threat of Maddox leveling Cloud Hall.
When word reached Cloudhaven, fear gripped the city. What if Maddox came? Could Cloud Hall survive?
[MUSIC: TENSE DISCUSSIONS]
Meanwhile, inside Cloud Hall itself, things were about to boil over. At first, Tia’s authority was enough to keep a lid on the dissent. But as the panic mounted, her control started to slip. Finally, she went into seclusion to focus on her training, leaving a power vacuum in her wake.
With nowhere else to aim their frustration, the elders, clan leaders, and Sect leaders turned their collective anger on Zane. The moment they heard he was with Cedric, setting up the Sect-protecting array, they marched off to find them.
Through all of this, Emanuel Drew was having the time of his life.
[MUSIC: DEVIOUS SCHEMING]
Before Tia's arrival, he'd been the likeliest candidate for Hall Master—a wound that had never healed.
Where others saw catastrophe, Emanuel saw opportunity. Maddox was powerful, yes, but a lone wolf whose ambition was to establish a divine kingdom and seize the entire Heartlands. A fourth-grade sect like Cloud Hall was barely a footnote in his plans. Otherwise, a warrior of his strength would have absorbed them long ago.
The math was simple. If Maddox cut through Tia and Elder Imogen, and Emanuel stood on the right side when the smoke cleared—who would dare challenge him for the top seat?
For now, he played the righteous, concerned elder. Stoking panic, positioning himself as the voice of reason, rallying the crowd—it let him vent frustration and win hearts simultaneously.
Every angry elder and house leader marching on Cedric's workshop today was there because Emanuel had put them up to it.
[AMBIENT: ANGRY MOB OUTSIDE]
Cedric stepped forward, planting himself between the crowd and Zane. "What exactly do you think you're doing?"
"Hand over Zane. That's all we want," an elder shot back.
"Cedric," another voice called, almost cordial in its menace, "We've always respected you. Step aside and we'll leave you out of this."
Under normal circumstances, most would never have dared speak to Cedric this way. As one of Cloud Hall's foremost runic masters, they'd often needed his help. But with Cloud Hall seemingly on the edge of ruin, the usual courtesies had vanished.
The crowd poured into the hall.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Zane stood there—brush already dipped, the Cobalt Dragon's soul force coiling around it, preparing the second five-star texture.
[MUSIC: MAGICAL CREATION]
[SFX: ELECTRIC CRACKLE]
A hairline arc of electricity split the air—thin as a thread, humming with the complete Law of Thunder. Everyone stepped back instinctively.
[SFX: CROWD CHATTER]
"What is that kid doing?"
"Just grab him!"
"Hold on. Let's see what happens."
Zane heard every word. He glanced at them once—a single, indifferent look—then turned back to the page.
[SFX: BRUSH STRIKING PAPER]
[MUSIC: VIOLENT THUNDER THEME]
[SFX: CRACKLING ELECTRICITY]
The instant the brush touched paper, the hall exploded with light.
Violent arcs of electricity erupted from the brush tip, crackling outward in every direction. With each stroke, a new bolt of lightning tore across the page—raw, thunderous marks, as though the Law of Thunder itself was being chiseled into paper by force.
The flash was nearly blinding. Yet Zane's eyes stayed wide open, unblinking, staring straight into the heart of it.
In that moment, he looked less like a runic master and more like the lord of thunder itself.
The crowd that had stormed in moments ago had gone completely silent.
Zane spun the brush once, drew a smooth, perfect circle, pressed his thumb firmly to the corner—and sealed it.
The lightning subsided. The hall exhaled.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED MURMURING]
"Was he actually drawing a rune?"
"That was... something else. Maybe this array really can hold Maddox off."
Zane's expression didn't change. He set the brush down, walked to the edge of the hall, and plunged both hands into a basin of cool water. The lightning wouldn't have hurt him, but the heat it generated was another matter.
[SFX: WATER SPLASHING]
[SFX: PAPER RUSTLING]
He shook the water off, dried his hands carefully, and returned to his seat.
Cedric stepped forward without a word, lifting the completed Wrath of Thunder and replacing it with fresh runic paper. Then he stepped back and waited.
Zane picked up his brush again.
[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]
Once more, the Cobalt Dragon’s Soul Power coiled around him.
[SFX: MYSTICAL HUM]
Unlike the crackling lightning from before, his brush now pulsed with a dreamy, mesmerizing color.
The Madness Trap’s key ingredients—things like “Charm Bone” and a “thousand-year-old fox heart”—were notoriously difficult to handle, designed specifically to create powerful illusions. Even Zane had nearly lost himself just trying to channel their energy.
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL ENCHANTMENT]
[AMBIENT: DREAMLIKE ATMOSPHERE]
An illusion bloomed from the tip of his brush, revealing the strikingly beautiful face of Tia.
In the vision, she was clad in a soft robe,  moving slowly toward him. Her usual cold, guarded eyes were gone, replaced by a look of warmth and affection.
[SFX: ELECTRIC CRACKLE]
The Cobalt Dragon was about to yank him from the trance, but before it could act, a tiny spark of lightning flared across Zane’s face. His eyes instantly cleared.
“Ah, that’s right,” the Cobalt Dragon mused, a hint of surprise in its ancient voice. “Your will is strong enough to shatter the illusion on its own. Proceed.”
Of course it is, it thought a moment later. I should have expected nothing less.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL MIST]
Zane nodded and began to draw. Lines of illusory color streamed from his brush onto the paper, a pink and purple mist rising with each delicate stroke.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
But while Zane could break free from the spell, the others were not so strong-willed.
Cedric and the rest of the crowd stared into the mist, their eyes vacant, as if the dreamy, purple haze was drawing their very souls from their bodies.
