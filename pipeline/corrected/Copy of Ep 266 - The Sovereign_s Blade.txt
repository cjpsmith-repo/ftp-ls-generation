Ep 266 - The Sovereign’s Blade
Word count: 1009
[SFX: DEMONIC ROAR]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
As Zane’s demonic fist barreled toward him, Adrian’s eyebrows shot up. With a wave of his hands, he summoned his own weapons, and hundreds of lotus flowers, born from his martial arts, bloomed in the air before him.
This was hardly their first fight. Adrian remembered facing those fists back on Infernal Peak. Zane was just an Initiate Adept back then, but his fists had somehow evened the odds. It was a reckless move, pure and simple.
The kid—a total novice, a mere Initiate Adept—had the nerve to pick a fight with him. Adrian had been stunned. What made Zane do something so damn foolish? Still, he had to admit, those fists were impressive as hell, especially coming from someone so young.
When Zane was only an Initiate Adept, he could use Raw Vitality to fuel those powerful fists. But now that Zane had fully entered the Adept Realm, his Raw Vitality had refined into Vitality. That meant his fists were bound to be denser, heavier, and a hell of a lot more powerful.
But Adrian Warrick was a rare genius himself, the pride of House Warrick. He wasn’t some run-of-the-mill disciple; he stood head and shoulders above the rest. A fight against him was never going to be easy.
And Adrian had already figured out how to defend against Zane's Titan Strike.
[SFX: MAGICAL CONVERGENCE]
The lotus flowers surrounding him converged into a perfect triangle, with a wide gap separating each of the hundred delicate blossoms. Adrian channeled his energy, his eyes narrowing in concentration as he launched the triangular formation directly toward Zane’s approaching fists.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT]  The moment Zane’s massive fists connected with the lotus array, his shadow phantoms began to devour the blossoms in the front row with voracious hunger. Within seconds, the exquisite red lotus arrangement succumbed to the corrupting influence of Shadow Vitality, each flower transforming from vibrant crimson to a diseased black.
[AMBIENT: CROWD REACTS]
"Wait — why are the lotuses turning black?"
"That's Zane's Vitality. It's not just hitting them — it's eating them. His Vitality is consuming Adrian's technique whole."
"Look at the gaps in that formation. Adrian left those on purpose. He already knew — he's been planning for this."
The metamorphosis spread like a plague through Adrian’s carefully crafted formation, the darkness consuming his beautiful technique with ruthless efficiency. Adrian watched in horror as his prized lotuses—symbols of his family’s legacy—began to wither under Zane’s relentless assault.
[SFX: MAGICAL EXPLOSION]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
Then, dozens of the red lotuses exploded at once. A wave of crimson fire washed over Zane’s fist phantoms and the blackened flowers, incinerating everything to ash.
It was a last-ditch effort, detonating his own lotuses along with Zane's phantoms. Adrian was out of options. Zane's shadowy fists were just too sinister, too powerful. After running through the possibilities, he knew there was no other way to break through.
And even with that, all Adrian had managed was a draw.
If he hadn’t, the Vitality churning inside Zane’s fist phantoms would have devoured every last lotus. He’d have lost, plain and simple.
"I’ve broken through your fists," Adrian said, his tone casual as fresh lotuses bloomed at his side. "Got any more tricks up your sleeve?" Zane’s cultivation method might be rare, but Adrian’s mastery of the Lotus Scriptures was profound. Plus, he had the clear advantage in raw power. He had every reason to be confident.
Deep down, though, a sliver of fear pricked at Adrian. Zane was improving too fast. His martial arts skills were growing at an unbelievable rate.
’It’s only been six months, and he’s already strong enough to go toe-to-toe with me,’ Adrian’s mind raced. ’Give him three years, and I won’t stand a chance!’
[MUSIC: TENSE DETERMINATION]
This time, Adrian had to pull out all the stops. He had to defeat Zane, and he had to make it look easy—to shatter his confidence and crush his spirit.
Progress in the martial arts is all about will. But a crushing defeat can weaken that will—or shatter it completely. A loss like that haunts you, stalls your growth, and leaves you unable to get any stronger.
As Adrian saw it, raw strength was the only thing that had gotten Zane this far in the All Peaks Competition. No matter how strong Zane seemed, his cultivation was still just at the Second Level of the Adept Realm. His only real advantage was that creepy, purple-black Vitality of his.
Once Adrian figured out how to counter it, he’d have Zane on the ropes. A guy with that level of power was a joke, a clown. Adrian figured he had all the time in the world to play with him in the ring.
"Tricks?" Zane repeated, not really answering. A faint smile touched his lips.
He had plenty of cards left to play, but he had no intention of using them on Adrian. Adrian was strong, sure, but he wasn’t the final boss. To win this competition, Zane had to stay calm and keep his best moves in reserve.
[SFX: SWORD MATERIALIZING]
Adrian extended his hand, summoning an ancient blade that materialized in his grasp. The elegant sword gleamed, its immaculately preserved edge retaining an impossible sharpness that belied its age.
[MUSIC: EPIC ROYAL THEME]
The moment the sword was in his grip, Adrian’s entire demeanor transformed dramatically. Earlier, surrounded by his white lotuses, he had projected the aura of a mighty emperor looking down on his subjects with disdain. Now, wielding this ancient weapon, he embodied something far more terrifying—a resurrected god-king whose piercing gaze swept across the arena with boundless ambition.
His eyes, cold and merciless, reflected centuries of inherited arrogance as he stood taller, his shoulders squared with the confidence of one who recognized no equal in the lands he considered his.
[SFX: CROWD GASPS]
"Whoa, that’s an antique Sacred Weapon!" a disciple shouted, his voice cracking with excitement. "An antique Sacred Weapon!"
"Oh my god, did he rob an ancient king’s tomb or something?" another gasped, completely stunned. "How else do you get a Sacred Weapon like that?"
"I can feel the aura from here," a senior disciple murmured, his eyes wide. "It’s just a sword, but... I can sense the sovereign will of an ancient king within it. That weapon is no ordinary relic. Whoever wielded that blade in its time must have been a terrifyingly powerful warrior!"
Everyone stared, stunned by The Sovereign’s Blade now held in Adrian's hand.
