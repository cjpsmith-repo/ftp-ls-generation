Ep 613 - Zane Changed Everything
Word count: 1010
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[AMBIENT: TOWER INTERIOR]
Neil and Colin, the two Ogre War Sages, shot toward the doors of the Tower of Sin like a pair of living cannonballs.
Even with the doors sealed shut, they had cranked their power to the absolute max, moving so fast they could have punched a hole straight through the tower’s stone wall, let alone a set of doors. It wasn't a question of if they’d get through, but how much of the entrance would be left standing.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: RUMBLING FOUNDATION]
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC CROWD PANIC]
As everyone had braced for, the doors didn't just open—they vaporized into a cloud of splinters. The two Ogres stormed onto the first floor, the sheer force of their entry shaking the foundation. While the War Fighters and War Generals on the lower levels were thrown into confusion, the combatants on the tenth floor and above knew exactly what was happening.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
[SFX: METAL CLANGING]
Down on the first floor, a brutal fight was already underway. A lithe War Fighter from the Twilight Group, a master of stealth, circled a hulking warrior from the Giants. He moved like a shadow, a blur of motion looking for that single, perfect opening to land a killing blow. But the Giant was a fortress, his massive black iron rod a spinning shield that left no vulnerability exposed.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS CRASH]
Just as their standoff reached its peak, the deafening crash from the entrance ripped through the hall. Every head snapped around. And there they were: two Ogres, standing in the ruined doorway, their presence sucking the air from the room.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS CHOIR]
Pure, undiluted fear washed over the faces of every creature present.
“How strong are they?” someone whispered, their voice trembling. “I can’t even get a read on their power.”
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
“They look like Ogre War Lords.”
“No way,” another fighter argued, shaking his head. “Two years ago, I ran into a top-tier Ogre War Lord, and he didn’t feel this intimidating. These two… they have to be War Sages.”
“That’s insane! How could a War Sage even get in here? The curse is supposed to kill them the second they set foot in the Cursed Land!”
The fighters argued in hushed, panicked tones, their eyes locked on the two intruders.
[AMBIENT: HEAVY BREATHING]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Meanwhile, Neil and Colin were gasping for air. They’d burned through a staggering amount of energy just to break in. Even with bodies hardened by years of refinement, they were bent over, panting.
“That was way too close,” Neil heaved.
“See? The Cursed Land isn’t as tough as everyone says.” But as Colin turned to his partner, his triumphant smirk twisted into a mask of shock and horror.
[MUSIC: HORROR CRESCENDO]
He raised a trembling finger. “You… your face…”
“What’s wrong?” Neil asked, but his confusion evaporated the moment he saw the raw terror in Colin’s eyes.
[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]
A cold dread washed over him, and he knew.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS TENSION]
[SFX: DRIPPING LIQUID]
Large black spots had begun to bloom across their skin. Some of them split open, weeping a thick, black fluid that trickled down their faces.
The curse was finally showing its teeth.
[MUSIC: HORROR STRINGS]
And then it accelerated.
[SFX: BODY TRANSFORMATION]
What started on their skin moved deeper. Their flesh began to give way, then the muscle beneath it, then the bone. The bodies that had seemed like immovable mountains just minutes ago were coming apart—melting, collapsing inward, piece by piece. They screamed. The sound of it filled the first floor and didn't stop until there was nothing left to scream.
In the end, all that remained of Colin and Neil was a dark, spreading stain on the ground.
[AMBIENT: HORRIFIED SILENCE]
The Ogre fighters on the first floor stood frozen.
"What… what just happened?"
"Those two weren't peak War Lords. They were War Sages. They were cursed to death."
"But how did they even make it this far? If they were War Sages, the Cursed Land should have taken them the moment they stepped outside."
Nobody had an answer. The fighters could only stare at what was left.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]
Back on the tenth floor, the Ogre War Lords watched in stunned silence. They could never have imagined that Zane would find some bizarre, unthinkable way to literally kick two War Sages out of the tower. That single, impossible act would haunt their nightmares for years to come.
Maurice understood something that the others didn't. Colin and Neil had never truly beaten the curse.
The Tower of Sin could absorb and suppress a portion of it—enough to buy a War Sage a month inside its walls before the curse eventually took hold. That was the secret the ogres had been sitting on for ten years. A hidden passage at the top of the Tower was discovered when the Deep Shadow Cave opened. Colin and Neil had never left. They'd stayed hidden, cultivating in silence, waiting for the right moment.
It had been a perfect plan. Until Zane threw them out.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]
For Fabian and Jarrod, two of the top human War Lords, it was a moment of pure ecstasy. Zane kept revealing his abilities one by one, and each was more mind-blowing than the last. Just when they thought they’d processed one incredible feat, he’d turn around and pull off an even bigger miracle. At this point, Fabian, Jarrod, Olivia, and Dylan had been so repeatedly shocked by Zane that they were almost numb to it. If someone told them he was a savior sent from the heavens, they’d probably just nod along, too stunned to argue.
No one had expected the Ogres to send in War Sages, but Zane had dealt with them effortlessly. Who else but a savior could do something like that?
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL PIANO]
Over in a corner of the arena, Olivia’s eyes welled with tears. In her heart, there was only Zane. He was perfect. What started as a simple crush had blossomed into pure, unwavering adoration.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
A few minutes later, the scouts that Esther Sterling and Maurice had sent to the upper floors returned. After they delivered their reports, a faint, satisfied smile touched Esther’s lips. Maurice, on the other hand, looked utterly defeated.
Neither of them announced the fate of the Ogre War Sages. They didn’t have to. Their faces said it all.
