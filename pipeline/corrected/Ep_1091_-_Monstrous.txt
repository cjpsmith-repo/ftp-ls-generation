Ep 1091 - Monstrous

Word count: 1089

[MUSIC: LIVELY BATTLE]

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

For Zane and Layla, the first few rounds of the trial were barely a warm-up. But for the crowd watching from the outside, it was all about the clock—speed was the only measure of strength they had.

“Zane’s a step behind her,” one observer noted. “He’s quick, sure—faster than most—but not Layla-quick.”

“Does it really matter who’s faster?” another countered. “It’s not like speed is the only thing that wins a fight. I’m sure Zane can handle himself.”

“It’s a huge deal,” the first man insisted. “Speed and strength go hand in hand. You see warriors slow down after the fifth round, and it makes the sixth a nightmare. They barely scrape by in the seventh and usually wash out completely by the eighth.”

The palace leaders, watching from their own vantage point, offered no comment.

[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]

[SFX: METAL CLANG]

With a series of sharp whooshes, Zane’s sword swept through the air, and the bronze-armored soldiers on his platform fell to pieces. He’d cleared the round.

But by then, Layla had already finished her own third round. She could have moved on to the fourth disk instantly, but she didn’t. Instead, she stood at the edge, waiting for him to catch up—a deliberate, petty taunt. Every time they started a new round together, she’d finish first just to rub it in his face.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

The fourth round began. Suddenly, a pack of strange, white-furred beasts materialized on the platform.

Maybe her little game had finally gotten to him, because Zane suddenly moved with a whole new level of intensity. The moment the round started, he channeled a sliver of Chaos Energy into Thundergust. The blade shot forward in a blur, delivering more than ten stabs in the blink of an eye.

[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]

[SFX: ENERGY BURST]

With a sharp crackle, a dozen bolts of light, pulsing with wind and thunder, erupted from his sword. In an instant, every single one of the white beasts on the stone platform was gone. He had cleared the fourth round.

These beasts were far stronger than the soldiers from the earlier rounds. When Layla raised her own sword to attempt the same overwhelming attack, her first strike only made the creatures cough up blood. It took a second volley to finally put them down.

In the fourth round, Zane had beaten her.

[MUSIC: PLAYFUL TENSION]

She had been planning to do the same thing as before—wait for him at the edge of the fifth platform. But this time, he was already there, standing calmly in the middle of the disk with a small, knowing smile on his face as he watched her finish.

“Hmph!” Layla let out a sharp, annoyed snort and immediately triggered the fifth round, refusing to give him the satisfaction.

[MUSIC: FAST-PACED COMPETITIVE THEME]

Now it was a real race. With both of them competing on pure speed, the pace of the trials grew faster and faster.

By the rules, every round should have gotten harder, and every warrior should have slowed down because of it. Zane and Layla did the opposite. In the fifth round, Layla cleared it in four breaths; Zane needed four and a half — she was faster. In the sixth, Zane took three breaths to her three and a half — he pulled ahead. The seventh, they tied at three breaths each. The eighth dropped to two breaths apiece. By the tenth round, the two of them together had burned through less time than it took to draw a single breath.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

Out in the crowd, warriors who’d needed minutes — some as long as half an hour — just to clear the first ten rounds stared in disbelief.

“Too monstrous,” one of them muttered, shaking his head. “I’m not her match, and I never will be.”

“No surprise there,” another shot back. “I asked around — she walked into Mist Palace and beat every genius the Heavenly Palaces had to offer, and that’s with her at the ninth level of the Life-and-Death Realm. Her real strength can’t even be measured.”

“And him,” a third added, nodding at Zane. “Fifth level of Life-and-Death Realm, and the gap between them is even bigger. He’s worse than monstrous.”

Even so, plenty of warriors weren’t convinced. Whatever method let the two of them push so far through the Law Storm, it didn’t prove anything about real combat strength — and in the Illusory Space, there were no shortcuts.

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

As Zane approached the later rounds, the mechanics of the trial were explained to him. The tenth round was where things got interesting. More than ten sparring partners would be sent into the Illusionary Arena to fight a single warrior, and with two hundred warriors taking the trial at once, that meant a massive force was needed.

[AMBIENT: DISTANT CROWD MURMUR]

On the other side of Willow Island, over thirty thousand sparring partners were kept on standby. They were all talented fighters from various sacred orders who just hadn’t been good enough to make it into the Thirteen Palaces. They had to compete with each other just for a chance to fight in the trials, so they trained with a desperate intensity. Since no one could actually die in the Illusionary Arena, they were encouraged to go all out, using any means necessary to win.

They were envious of the warriors who had gotten in, and that jealousy made them vicious. When they got the chance to fight someone from the Thirteen Palaces, they took it personally. Even if they were completely outmatched, they’d still do everything they could to land as many blows as possible before being defeated.

Zane nodded slightly as he took in the information. It sounded like a hassle, but nothing he couldn’t handle.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

[AMBIENT: ARENA ATMOSPHERE]

As he stepped onto the eleventh platform, he was met with a dozen hardened fighters.

Something about them was different — sharper, more present than the constructs from earlier rounds. Some wore cold smiles; others just watched him with steady, calculating eyes.

“You’re... warriors too?” Zane asked. “From the Illusory Space?”

“That’s right,” one of them said, smiling. “We’re from the other side of Willow Island. For us, this is its own kind of trial.”

[SFX: AGGRESSIVE SHOUT]

“Look at this guy!” one of them yelled, a greedy light in his eyes. “He’s only at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm! This is our chance, boys! There’s a huge reward if we take him out here!”

The dozen men circling him looked less like fighters and more like a pack of starving wolves that had just cornered a lone sheep.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

[SFX: WEAPONS DRAWN]

With a collective roar, they surged forward, a chaotic wave of desperation and steel aimed right at him.
