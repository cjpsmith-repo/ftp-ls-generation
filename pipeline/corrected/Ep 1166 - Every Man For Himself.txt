Ep 1166 - Every Man For Himself

Word count: 1017

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
[SFX: MASSIVE OBJECT RISING]
The moment the Wheel of Reincarnation lifted off the ground, three Saint Lords materialized in the sky high above Auspice City, their faces tight with disapproval as they stared down at the massive, ascending disc.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]
Down below, the warriors of the Dark World were already buzzing with wild theories. Some had guessed the sprawling underground palace was some kind of transport vessel, but seeing a magic treasure of this scale rise into the air still caught everyone off guard.
Up above, however, things were more complicated. The three Saint Lords represented different factions, each with their own agenda. The Saint Lord from the Violetwood Sanctuary couldn’t just let Lavender go; letting her escape would endanger his entire sanctuary. He was torn, but he knew what duty demanded.
[SFX: MASSIVE BOW DRAWN]
He produced an enormous bow — dozens of feet from tip to tip — and in one fluid motion, nocked an invisible arrow, drew back, and released.
[SFX: INVISIBLE ARROW LAUNCH]
[SFX: DEEP RESONANT IMPACT]
The arrow struck the Wheel of Reincarnation with a thunderous bang. The wheel didn’t move.
Watching it hold firm, the Saint Lord of Violetwood Sanctuary let out a quiet breath. He hadn’t wanted to attack Lavender. And now, with the Wheel beyond his power to break, that choice was out of his hands. He could only hope she got out fast.
Meanwhile, Yosef — the Ogre Saint Lord — watched the rising Wheel with hungry eyes. He had no intention of letting it go.
He let out a cold laugh. 
[SFX: COMMANDING SHOUT]
“Heavenly Ogre Palm Print!”
A Saint Lord wasn’t technically a World Lord, but their power was close enough to make them terrifyingly dangerous. 
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC COMBAT STRINGS]
The instant he struck, all the energy in the air above Auspice City twisted into a chaotic vortex. 
[SFX: ENERGY VORTEX SWIRLING]
[SFX: MASSIVE PALM MATERIALIZING]
A massive palm print materialized out of thin air, growing until it was easily as large as the Wheel of Reincarnation itself.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL HUMMING]
Deep inside the wheel, the illusion of the underground palace dissolved around Zane, leaving him standing alone amidst a web of three thousand six hundred glowing divine textures. From his vantage point, he could see everything happening outside. A grim look settled on his face as the giant, glowing hand began its descent.
His only real shot was escaping with the Wheel of Reincarnation. If it was destroyed, he’d have to use the token to get back to The Elysian Keep. That was his absolute last resort, a lifeline he refused to use unless he had no other choice.
The sheer power radiating from the palm print was unlike anything Zane had ever witnessed; it felt like it could wipe out all life in its path. Even with his specialized body, he knew a direct hit would vaporize him instantly. Livingston had been confident in the wheel’s durability, but watching that attack plummet toward them, both Zane and Lavender looked anything but certain.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: METAL VESSEL SHUDDERING]
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
A deafening explosion rocked the sky. The palm print slammed into the Wheel of Reincarnation, and the entire vessel shuddered violently, dropping several dozen feet in an instant.
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
Zane planted his feet, his hands a blur as he manipulated the divine textures, fighting to stabilize the wheel and keep it from crashing back to the ground.
From somewhere deeper in the Wheel, Livingston’s voice cut through the chaos, dripping with satisfaction.
“Heh. A sector lord, and he still can’t crack it. Did I not tell you?”
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: HIGH ALTITUDE WIND]
Yosef watched from above, a smirk on his face. The kid had some skill, he’d give him that, but a simple threat wasn’t going to work. He figured Freya was just a pretty face, not nearly as important as some made her out to be. There was no way Zane would give up a treasure like the Wheel of Reincarnation for her. He’d have to be more persuasive.
“The consequences for keeping the Wheel of Reincarnation are very simple,” Yosef said, his voice carrying easily over the wind. 
[SFX: SNAKE HISSING]
As he spoke, the two small snakes coiled tighter around Freya’s pale neck, their slender, forked tongues flicking in the air. “These Iron Snakes will make this woman suffer so much she’ll beg for death.”
Zane’s expression darkened as he stared at Freya, held helpless in the distance. 
[SFX: SHARP SNEER]
He was quiet for a long moment, then a sudden, sharp sneer cut across his face. “I get it,” he called out. “You’re the Saint Lord from the ogres’ sacred place, right?”
“So what if I am?” Yosef snorted.
“I was just thinking,” Zane said, his voice laced with mock curiosity. “Is that sacred place of yours important to you? I seem to remember it’s somewhere up north in the Dark World. I wonder what you’d do if I decided to go smash it to pieces!”
[SFX: FURIOUS ROAR]
“How dare you!” Yosef roared, his face flushing red with fury.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL SURGE]
[SFX: DIVINE ENERGY ACTIVATION]
[SFX: MASSIVE STONE WHEEL SPINNING]
But Zane just waved a hand, activating over a hundred runes at once. 
[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND VELOCITY]
[SFX: MASSIVE OBJECT SHOOTING THROUGH AIR]
The entire Wheel of Reincarnation slowly spun on its axis and then shot off to the north like a stone skipped across water.
The wheel was a transport vessel once used by the Supreme Lord himself, and it was unbelievably fast. Zane knew he could use it to block the Saint Lords’ attacks, but he couldn’t fight back—the divine textures had no offensive power. But relying on the wheel’s massive size and its indestructible shell, he could still wreak absolute havoc on the ogres’ home.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT EXPLOSION]
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE PALM STRIKE ON METAL]
In a flash, Yosef was on them again, catching up from behind and slamming another palm strike into the Wheel of Reincarnation, desperately trying to stop him.
