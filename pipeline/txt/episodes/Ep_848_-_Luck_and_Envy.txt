Ep 848 - Luck and Envy

Word count: 1082

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CAVE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Sixteen Ancient Heartstones encircled the glowing object at their center, which pulsed with a flash of light so blinding that Zane couldn’t quite make out what it was. Without a second thought, he hefted the pickaxe over his shoulder.

[SFX: PICKAXE STRIKE ON STONE]

He already knew the leaves near the top of the pyramid were thicker, their blue color more vibrant, their texture more like solid stone. Still, their sheer toughness caught him completely off guard. He slammed the pickaxe into the surface, and the impact didn't even leave a scratch.

“What the…” Zane’s eyes widened.

Even with a low-grade weapon, his raw strength was enough to split a mountain in two without even tapping into his power. How could a single leaf be this durable?

But he hadn’t come all this way for the Ancient Heartstones just to give up now.

[SFX: DRAGON TRANSFORMATION]

[MUSIC: EPIC BUILDUP]

A wave of power surged through him as his Dragon Scales materialized across his skin. He raised the pickaxe again, channeling all his might into one single, devastating strike.

[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT CRACK]

[SFX: CRACKING STONE]

This time, a thin fracture snaked across the surface.

“Not so tough after all,” Zane muttered, a faint smile touching his lips. Seeing his attack had worked, he kept swinging, the cracks spreading with each blow until, with one final smash, the leaf shattered.

He used the pickaxe to sweep away the broken shards, revealing the cluster of Heartstones underneath. His expression softened as he looked at them. Tia was waiting for these. Her life depended on them. He carefully collected the stones one by one, placing them safely in his Storage Ring before turning his attention to the object they had been guarding.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL HUM]

[SFX: SHIMMERING LIGHT]

Through the leaf, it had just been a blur of light. Now, he could see it was a perfect, square crystal. Light rippled within it like water, casting a brilliant white glow.

No idea what this is, he thought, but it’s coming with me. 

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

Without hesitating, he placed the shimmering ore into his Storage Ring, then leaped from the platform, plunging into the void below. A ring of flashing light enveloped him, and he plummeted down the side of the pyramid in a controlled fall.

[SFX: HEAVY LANDING]

When his feet finally hit solid ground, Callan, Fiona, and Randy were there waiting for him.

But before Randy could say a word, a commotion erupted nearby.

[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD]

“Look at that! The first time in, and he finds a Fire Stone!”

“Crown Prince Fredrick — truly blessed!”

“I'll pay anything for it. Name your price!”

Fredrick stood at the center of the crowd, holding a round stone with a faint flame flickering inside it. The sight of it had driven the gathered warriors half mad with want. A Fire Stone was a treasure used to forge divine fire-element weapons — in the Harlen Divine Kingdom, a single one was worth five top-grade Mythic weapons.

Randy glanced over, unimpressed. Callan reached into his Storage Ring without a word and produced a Fire Stone of his own, tossing it casually in the air and catching it. 

Callan's talent had never matched his sister's, but his luck was another matter entirely. He'd found a bigger Fire Stone than Fredrick in a fraction of the time.

[SFX: CROWD GASPS]

The crowd went quiet for a moment, then erupted again.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

One of the York warriors turned cold eyes toward Zane. “And what about you? What did Zane Jones of the Harlen Divine Kingdom manage to find? Care to show us?”

The implication was plain. Zane had made York look bad, and they hadn't forgotten it.

Zane looked at them for a moment. “None of your business,” he said. He turned away. “Boring.”

He didn't say it unkindly. These were talented warriors, no question — but they'd grown up in kingdoms where open conflict between the great powers was rare and killing rarer still. Compared to what passed for normal in the Heartlands, let alone the main continent, they were children playing at rivalry. It wasn't their fault. It was just the world they'd been handed.

A slow smile spread across Randy's face as he opened his mouth to ask what Zane had found, but Fiona slipped forward quietly, catching him off guard before he'd even had a chance to look her way. She held out a handful of Ancient Heartstones.

“Here,” she said with a warm smile. “These are for you.”

Fiona didn't miss much. After the battle at the West Dragon River, she'd overheard the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom mention that Zane needed these stones. Whether she'd been actively searching for them or had just stumbled upon them, she had gathered quite a few. And while Zane might refuse other gifts, she knew he wouldn't turn this down.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]

More than twenty Heartstones tumbled from her Storage Ring into his hands. Ancient Heartstones were fragile — unlike most ores — and he cupped his palms carefully, afraid to drop a single one.

When the other warriors saw her so generously hand over her prize, a few of them turned green with envy. Ancient Heartstones weren’t as rare as Fire Stones, but they were still incredibly valuable.

Zane didn’t want to take them for free. He had a dozen of his own now, but simply trading them felt wrong—it would cheapen her gesture.

[MUSIC: WARM HEARTFELT MELODY]

As he searched for the right response, an idea sparked, and a real smile curved his lips. “Thank you. But I have something for you, too.”

He thought of the strange, beautiful object he’d found nestled among the Heartstones. The flickering crystal light was mesmerizing. It felt like the perfect gift for her.

[SFX: SHARP GASP]

[MUSIC: SUDDEN TENSION]

The moment he took it out, however, Randy gasped, the sound catching in his throat and turning into a choked cough. He stared at Zane, his eyes wide with something that looked a lot like fear.

“Zane,” he sputtered, “are you sure you want to give that to Fiona?”

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING STRINGS]

The crystal cube in Zane’s hand was instantly the center of all attention. Its brilliant white light seemed to pull every eye in the clearing toward it. 

Most stared in simple shock, but the saint-level geniuses among the crowd looked at it with undisguised, ravenous greed.
