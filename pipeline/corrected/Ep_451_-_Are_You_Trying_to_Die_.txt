Ep 451 - Are You Trying to Die?

Word count: 1078

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD MURMUR]

[SFX: DISTANT RUMBLE]

The onlookers stared at the sky as they chattered excitedly, their voices rising with speculation, completely oblivious to the true significance of what was unfolding before them.

"What's that light?"

"Wait—do you think an immortal is descending from the heavens?"

"It must be! The Dragon Soar Arena connects directly to the Upper World. Who knows? We might actually witness one with our own eyes!"

Some pointed skyward with trembling fingers. Others clutched their companions' arms, eyes wide with wonder.

The warriors standing guard, however, felt their hearts quicken for an entirely different reason. Someone had just returned from the Upper World—and they knew exactly who it had to be. The Adept. The one who'd vanished from the arena several months ago, leaving behind nothing but questions and failed attempts to follow.

After the Dragon Soar Arena was relocated to Oracle City, every major sect had scrambled to unlock its secrets. They'd sent their finest warriors through all six layers, each one clutching the dragon-shaped crystal pendant, desperately trying to activate the ancient teleportation array. Masters of the Soul Realm had attempted it. Prodigies with legendary bloodlines had tried. Even elders with centuries of cultivation experience had stepped forward.

Yet no matter what techniques they employed, no matter how much spiritual energy they channeled, none of them could breach the barrier. The portal remained stubbornly closed to all.

Until now.

[SFX: MAGICAL FLASH]

[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]

The moment the white light flared, any warrior attempting the trip to the Upper World was just unceremoniously dumped right back outside.

The Adept was the only one who'd ever made it. After he went through, the portal just stopped working for everyone else. There was no doubt he was special—the only one who could use it.

To figure out why, several rune experts had examined the top of the arena. They concluded that something was wrong with the teleportation array's runes.

Of course, these experts in the Heartlands weren't privy to the secrets of the divine texture. So while they correctly guessed the runes were the problem, they could never pinpoint the real issue. Without knowledge of the divine texture, they were completely stuck.

Zane descended from the sky and landed at the Dragon Soar Arena's edge, unaware of the scrutiny he'd attracted. Every watching warrior's pulse quickened—this was him, the mysterious Adept who'd breached the portal. Dozens of eyes tracked his movements, each observer desperate to uncover how he'd accomplished what no one else could. They needed him, needed his secret, and they would do whatever it took to extract it.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

For this very reason, both the young master and the leader of House Kendrick had been hauled into Oracle City for questioning.

Recalling his encounter with the Adept, the young master had described his appearance, age, and power level—every detail he could dredge up.

But there were countless Adepts in the Heartlands. With so little to go on, identifying him was next to impossible. The truth remained a mystery.

That’s why every sect had posted warriors to watch the Dragon Soar Arena. Their mission: wait for the Adept to return and learn the secret to making it work.

The Cloud Hall had sent one of their best: Dominic, a top disciple from the Elite House who'd reached the peak of the Soul Realm.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

The instant the white light appeared, Dominic began to move. He pushed off the ground, his simple shoes nearly silent as he shot toward the arena's gate.

This Adept was the key to everything. Without a working teleporter, the arena was just a glorified training ground. He was the one who made it valuable again.

The arena was a great place for training, though. Dominic himself had practiced the Longfist style there for decades, mastering it to the point where he could distill it to its purest form.

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

Dominic reached the gate just as Gareth from the Mysterious Luna Hall materialized from the shadows, blocking his path. The two men locked eyes—one desperate to secure the Adept, the other determined to claim the prize for himself. Without warning, Dominic's fist shot forward, channeling his full Core Strength into a devastating strike.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE PUNCH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT DRUMS]

A single, explosive punch sent Gareth stumbling backward, his boots scraping against stone!

"Dominic, what the hell do you think you're doing? Are you looking for a death wish?" Gareth roared, his face flushing crimson. The humiliation burned—he'd struck first, yet found himself retreating. Fury coursed through his veins.

Dominic's chest heaved as he held his ground, but even he knew the limits of his power. He'd stopped one opponent, yet a dozen more circled like vultures.

[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]

Mason from Bloodwood Cliff cackled, his voice dripping with malicious glee. "You're coming home with me, little guy." His hands shot toward Zane like a predator's claws.

'Damn it,' Dominic thought, dread pooling in his stomach. Wrestling with someone like Gareth demanded his full attention. How could he possibly stop others who matched that strength?

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
The Vitality coating Mason's hands shimmered with razor-sharp intensity. He lunged toward Zane's face, fingers splayed—but his true target lay lower. The high attack served merely as misdirection; he aimed to shatter Zane's legs. He figured the kid would be easier to haul away if he couldn't walk.

Zane saw Dominic block the first guy and was about to say something, but he never expected another warrior to jump him before he could even get a word out.

This was starting to piss Zane off.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]

"Are you trying to die?" Zane asked, his voice dripping with ice.

Clenching his fist, he drifted back like a ghost, easily evading Mason’s lunge.

Then, faster than lightning, Zane’s fist shot out. Before Mason could even blink, it slammed hard into his chest.

[SFX: BONE CRACK]

A sickening crack echoed through the air.

[SFX: BODY IMPACT]

[SFX: CRASHING DEBRIS]

Mason, a warrior at the peak of the Soul Realm, was sent flying. His body smashed through a nearby handrail, plowed through a group of onlookers, and crashed through the walls of several houses before finally skidding to a halt.

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED SILENCE]

The other warriors, who’d been lunging for Zane just a second ago, froze in their tracks. Their hands were still outstretched, and they stood there, stunned, like a pack of mindless zombies.
