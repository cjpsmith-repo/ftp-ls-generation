Ep 265 - Hit and Run
Word count: 1133
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE BURST]
[AMBIENT: INTENSE FIRE]
A single step backward was Zane’s only defense. The instant he shifted his weight, the lotus in front of him detonated with terrifying force, unleashing supernatural flames that bloomed into the shape of a massive crimson flower. It roared with such ferocity that Zane felt his skin tighten against the blistering heat.
This was no ordinary fire—it radiated with an ancient malice, as if it could devour not just flesh but incinerate the world itself. Sweat instantly evaporated from his brow as he witnessed the inferno’s hungry tendrils reaching toward him, their intensity so overwhelming that for a heartbeat, Zane believed he might be witnessing the birth of a small sun.
[SFX: BODY CRASHING AGAINST BARRIER]
Even though Zane jumped back just in time to dodge the blast’s epicenter, the heat and shockwave still slammed into him. The force launched him twenty feet through the air, and he crashed so hard against the enchanted barrier that it shimmered violently.
Adrian approached him slowly, a cold smile spreading across his face.
"I know all about your strange Vitality. That little trick you used to absorb my lotuses back on Infernal Peak. Did you really think I’d fall for the same thing twice?"
He threw back his head and laughed. "Are you delusional? You think that freakish Vitality is enough to beat me? Let me set you straight: you’re dreaming. I gave you three years because you weren’t even worth my time then, and you still aren’t now. Beating you was a joke. And today? I’ll end you in a heartbeat."
[SFX: MAGICAL LOTUS BLOOMING]
As he spoke, a stream of lotuses bloomed from his hands, crashing into Zane's six Shadow Phantoms. And with every unhurried word, the lethal intent he poured into them grew stronger. Suddenly, another lotus, this one pure white, appeared among the chaos, blooming slowly in the center of the fighting platform.
Adrian's lotuses were usually the size of a man's hand and came in different colors, each with a unique power—the red lotus, for example, contained immense explosive force. Now, standing on the white lotus, his fingers pointed toward Zane.
[SFX: DRAMATIC POWER ACTIVATION]
"Point of the Petal!"
[SFX: AIR COMPRESSION]
[AMBIENT: GRAVITY DISTORTION]
In an instant, the air grew heavy, becoming impossibly dense. The gravity throughout the battle ring warped, compressing into a single point aimed right at Zane.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
The weight of the entire world seemed to descend upon his shoulders, crushing every fiber of his being. His lungs struggled for breath as the immense pressure bore down on him.
[SFX: BODY SLAMMING GROUND]
[SFX: STONE FLOOR SHATTERING]
Zane’s body slammed into the ground with the force of an avalanche. The stone floor beneath him splintered and cracked, creating a deep crater that imprisoned his battered form. Dust billowed upward as fragments of rock scattered across the arena.
Adrian observed the destruction with cold detachment, a hint of disdain flickering across his otherwise composed features.
"Gravity alone is enough to restrain you," he remarked, shaking his head. "You’re not even worth drawing my weapon for."
Determination burning in his eyes, Zane struggled to his feet. His joints protested with each movement, but his will remained unbroken.
More than a hundred dragon scales flared to life across Zane's body. Power surged through him like a tide. He fixed his eyes on Adrian and charged straight at the white lotus.
Adrian, maintaining his serene stance atop the white lotus, simply pointed his finger toward Zane once more, his expression betraying neither effort nor concern.
The impact resonated throughout the arena with a sickening boom as Zane was once again flattened into the crater.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMURS]
The fight had captured the attention of every disciple from the Skyreach Sect. No one could look away.
"Adrian is unbelievable," one disciple whispered in awe. "But Zane has that freakish natural strength. We’ve all seen it. And now Adrian’s holding him down with one finger… The guy’s not human, I swear."
"You don’t know what you’re talking about," the disciple next to him shot back. "One finger? You think he’s a god? It’s not his own strength. He’s using that white lotus to channel the gravity of the entire battle ring! Zane is being crushed by the weight of the whole damn arena!"
"Still, that takes insane skill," the first disciple sighed. "And Zane’s still trying to get up. If that were me, I’d be a smear on the floor."
[SFX: BODY IMPACT BOOMS]
[MUSIC: DETERMINED FIGHTING]
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
While the disciples talked, Zane wasn’t panicked. Though he was being slammed into the stone again and again, he got back up every single time. The truth was, the gravity wasn’t truly hurting him. Instead, a strange warmth bloomed in his core, renewing his power with every crushing impact.
His body was actually growing stronger under the pressure. Appearances meant nothing to Zane. All that mattered was getting stronger. All that mattered was winning.
Adrian, meanwhile, was savoring this. Still stinging from Zane’s earlier challenge, he wanted to drag out the humiliation and show every disciple that Zane was nothing compared to him. He was enjoying himself and hoped it would last.
More craters fractured the stage until the entire ring was a pockmarked wasteland, looking more like a disaster zone than a fighting arena.
Adrian, still standing serenely on the white lotus, smiled down at Zane.
"Since you won't use them yourself — let me remind everyone what you're hiding." His voice carried easily across the arena. "That throwing knife of yours. Your soul attacks. Why so shy, Zane? Show us what you've got."
[AMBIENT: CROWD STIRS]
The effect was immediate.
"He has a throwing knife? He hasn't used it yet?"
"Soul attacks? At the second level of the Adept Realm? How is that even possible?"
The whispers spread fast. Adrian hadn't said it to help Zane — he'd said it to strip away every advantage Zane had left. Whatever surprises Zane had been saving, they were surprises no longer.
As the disciples watched in amazement, Zane finally dodged the next gravity assault, his Sword Step becoming faster, more fluid. He was careful to hide his sword intent, though; he couldn’t use his step to attack, not like Finn could. Adrian was tough, but he wasn’t the only rival Zane had to worry about. He had to save some tricks for the final rounds.
"Is that all you’ve got? Running like a coward?" Adrian yelled, chasing him relentlessly while preparing another Point of the Petal.
[SFX: HIGH SPEED MOTION]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGES]
But this time, the moment Adrian unleashed the force from his fingertip, Zane exploded into motion. He moved with twice the speed, leaping high into the air as his six Shadow Phantoms, who’d been waiting patiently, surged forward in a coordinated attack. In a blink, Zane and his phantoms had Adrian completely surrounded.
"Oh, I can do a lot more than run," Zane said, a ghost of a smile touching his lips.
As he spoke, a massive fist of pure Vitality materialized above him and shot toward Adrian like a cannonball.
