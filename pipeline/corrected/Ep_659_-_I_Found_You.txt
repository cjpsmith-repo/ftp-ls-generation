Ep 659 - I Found You

Word count: 1175

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS CAVE]

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL DRONE]

The rumors proved accurate. Dark Nether Death Light poured from the mysterious red dot glowing ahead.

Initially, the fog had weakened the light, emboldening creatures from the Tower of Sin to explore. Now, as the group crept closer, the radiance intensified with each step.

The fog had shielded them from the deadly glow, but its power grew stronger. A question haunted everyone: if it was this dangerous now, how could they survive reaching the source? Their determined pace slowed to a crawl.

They'd relied on the "no risk, no reward" mantra, hoping to push through and escape this nightmare. Now it felt like a death march. Whether an absurdly powerful beast or something worse awaited them, the Dark Nether Death Light alone could kill them.

[SFX: BODY COLLAPSE]

Garrick crumpled to the ground, his nerves completely shot. “I can’t go any farther. That light will kill me!”

The rest of them stopped, too. Garrick might have been a coward, but he wasn’t wrong. None of them were strong enough to withstand that light.

Yolande glanced at Garrick, then, as if exhausted herself, she gracefully gathered the hem of her dress and sat down beside Zane. “It doesn't matter if we keep walking or not,” she murmured.

“What’s that supposed to mean?” Malcolm asked, completely lost.

Yolande just hugged her knees. “You'll see,” she said softly.

Malcolm was used to Yolande’s cryptic answers, so he let it go. They’d been traveling for half the day, anyway; a break was probably a good idea. Esther pulled a water bottle from her Storage Ring and took a long swig, and the others took the chance to rest, too. The whole ordeal had worn them down to the bone.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

But they hadn't been sitting for long when Fabian pointed a trembling finger toward the horizon. “That… that red dot! I think it’s getting bigger.”

Everyone looked up, fear and confusion warring on their tired faces. He was right. Even though they hadn't moved an inch, the red dot was visibly larger.

“What is happening?” Garrick cried, burying his face in his hands.

Remembering what Yolande had just said, Malcolm shot her a glare. “You knew this would happen, didn’t you?”

[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]

Yolande nodded, her expression grim. “Yes.

Even standing still, the Spatial Law in this fog is dragging us toward the core. This place is a quicksand trap. The moment you step in, you can't leave. Struggling only pulls you down faster."

Malcolm stared at her. Then he turned to Lavender. "Why aren't you afraid?"

Lavender's expression didn't change. "Why would she be?" she said coolly. "She's a clone. One of a million. If this one dies, it's no great loss to her."

The words landed like a stone. Everyone looked at Yolande. She said nothing.

Esther's voice broke the silence. "Let's keep moving."

No one argued.

They hadn't gone far when the first of them appeared — a pale shape half-buried in the black earth. Then another. Then dozens.

[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]

Skeletons.

Human skeletons, scattered across the ground in every direction, so ancient they crumbled to white powder the moment Jarrod reached down to touch one.

"How long have these been here?" he murmured, brushing the dust from his fingers.

No one answered. They kept walking, and the bones kept multiplying — more and more of them, until the ground ahead was blanketed in white.

[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STOP]

Then Yolande stopped. She placed one finger against her lips.

No one spoke. They'd learned by now that when Yolande signaled silence, you obeyed.

Her expression was unreadable as she walked forward into the bones. With every step, her wooden clogs reduced the ancient skeletons beneath her feet to powder. The group followed in silence.

[AMBIENT: DEEP CRATER ECHO]

[SFX: BONES CRUNCHING]

The ground sloped downward into a massive crater. At the bottom, the bones were piled higher — a small mountain of them, heaped at the center. Yolande descended without hesitation.

[SFX: WIND GUST]

[SFX: POWERFUL WIND BLAST]

[SFX: BONES DISINTEGRATING]

When she reached the bottom, she drew a slow breath and released it — but this was no ordinary exhale. It carried Wind Law, a concentrated gust that swept through the mountain of bones and dissolved it instantly into a great rolling cloud of white dust.

For a moment, Yolande disappeared entirely into the white.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC REVELATION]

[AMBIENT: SILENCE]

Then the dust settled.

She was kneeling on the ground, and before her sat a mummy — perfectly preserved, seated cross-legged in a posture of cultivation, its features still recognizable after all these years. Unlike the skeletons, it had not crumbled.

Yolande reached forward and wrapped her arms around it.

"I found you," she said softly.

Zane stared. "What is she doing?"

Lavender's voice was quiet. "That is the lord of the Righteous Sun Realm. A world ruler." She paused. "He was also Yolande's brother."

"Her brother died here, too," Zane said slowly.

The words rippled through the group. Fabian, Esther, even Malcolm — all of them had caught the most important part. A world lord. A being of incomprehensible power, dead at the bottom of this pit. The treasures on a figure like that would be beyond anything available on the main continent. Beyond anything they had ever imagined.

A glint of pure greed flashed in Malcolm’s eyes, and in the eyes of a few others standing with him. Leaving this place seemed next to impossible, sure, but no plan ever survives contact with reality. If they somehow got out of this alive and had to live with the regret of missing an opportunity this huge… well, that was a fate worse than death.

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

With that single thought driving him, Malcolm lunged forward, charging down the slope toward the bottom of the crater.

But Yolande was already kneeling by the mummy, deftly slipping a bracelet from its withered wrist. A small, golden skylark dangled from the clasp.

[SFX: METAL CLINK]

“A Storage Bracelet?” Malcolm’s eyes lit up. Weapon refiners could forge those personal dimensions into all sorts of things—rings, pendants, bracelets.

Just as he was about to dive for it, Yolande glanced over her shoulder, her voice cold as ice. “If you ever want to walk out of this fog, I’d advise you to keep your hands to yourself.”

The other warriors had followed Malcolm down into the crater. Seeing him about to make a move, Esther and the others tensed, ready to jump in.

When they heard her warning, their eyes went wide.

“You mean… you know how to get us out of this fog?” Malcolm asked, his voice low and dangerous.

Yolande didn't answer. She just carefully tucked the bracelet away, her expression impossible to read. Then, she pulled a yellowed paper talisman from her own Storage Ring and gently placed it on the mummy’s chest.

[SFX: SHARP GASP]

[MUSIC: HORROR CRESCENDO]

The second her fingers made contact, the mummy’s eyes snapped wide open.
