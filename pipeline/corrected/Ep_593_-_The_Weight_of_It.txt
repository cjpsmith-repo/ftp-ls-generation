Ep 593 - The Weight of It
Word count: 1009
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]
[SFX: SPATIAL RIFTS]
The spatial rifts were getting faster.
Without an understanding of the Spatial Law, the rifts would tear you apart in a heartbeat. Not even a Relic-Class weapon could withstand that kind of power.
Zane's body was a different story. His immortal physique was as durable as a Mythic Weapon—a feat he alone could claim in the entire universe. But even he would be ripped to shreds if he got sucked into one of those rifts.
[MUSIC: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
But as the rifts surged toward him, a spark of understanding lit Zane’s eyes. In an instant, the mysterious power of the Spatial Law flared to life, wrapping around him like a protective cloak.
[SFX: SPATIAL DISTORTION]
What happened next was bizarre.
The very rifts that could shred a Relic-Class weapon slid right past him. He floated on their chaotic surface, completely untouched as they rolled under him.
Mastering the second level of the Spatial Law meant you could weaponize the rifts, but first, you had to learn to survive them.
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC VOID]
[SFX: RIPPLING SPACE]
Rifts bloomed all around Zane. If one pulled him in, he’d simply reappear on the other side, his body like a small boat on a stormy sea. The rifts were just waves, and as they rolled past, he bobbed along, unharmed. He was riding wave after wave of tearing space.
Better yet, he was actively studying the rifts, deciphering the very essence of space itself. It was slower than learning from the stone, sure, but still leagues faster than any normal method.
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD]
Meanwhile, outside, Zane's name kept climbing the tablet wall.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
It was already in the sixth row, level with Olivia’s, when it suddenly shot upward.
[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD MURMUR]
"The fifth row!"
"The fourth!"
The crowd’s shouts followed his name as it blasted up like a rocket, finally settling in the fourth row. Seconds later, it appeared right next to the names of Boris and Cheryl.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: ROARING CROWD]
Olivia sighed with relief. No matter what happened next, humanity finally had a genius who could stand against the Ogres and the Demon Night. Her gut told her that as long as Zane was in the game, he could guarantee them a place at the table.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
But that relief was tangled with worry. The Ogres had already marked Zane as a threat to be eliminated. Now, with his talent on full display, they’d be gunning for him at the first opportunity.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
Just then, a warrior in a striking blue robe walked through the crowd.
Olivia, Dylan, and the other human representatives bowed. "Elder Kingsley."
Elder Kingsley gave a brief nod. His eyes found Zane's name on the wall immediately, and something flickered behind his normally unreadable expression. "He's already in the fourth row?"
"He should still go higher," Olivia said, without a trace of doubt in her voice.
Elder Kingsley said nothing more. But the fact that he'd come down from the upper levels at all said enough. Whatever Zane's final result, he was here to make sure it was reported.
The weight of that wasn't lost on anyone standing nearby. Even Dylan, jaw tight, couldn't argue with what the rankings meant. Fourth row: a guaranteed seat.
Second row, where Amber's name still glowed: the kind of genius who could lead an entire race into a new era. Third row—well. No one had ever reached the third row. But the general wisdom held that anyone who did would rise to great power.
Every step up was a chasm wider than the last. And yet Olivia had spoken with total certainty.
Even Dylan believed her.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS TENSION]
Back inside the chamber, the spatial rifts slowly subsided. The darkness settled around Zane like a held breath.
"Is it over?" he murmured.
No answer came. He stood still, waiting.
Then a soft light bloomed around him—and the next round began.
[SFX: CRUSHING PRESSURE]
This was just a test of talent, he reminded himself. The pressure was mounting, but its goal wasn't to crush him—it was just trying to find his physical limits.
The force came from every direction at once, pressing inward, lifting him off the ground.
Zane tried to fight it with raw strength. It didn't budge.
He reached for his vital essence. The moment it began to stir, it vanished—snuffed out instantly, as though it had never existed.
"What—"
He tried again. Nothing.
Dragon Scale Power. He called on it with everything he had.
Nothing.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
A cold flash of panic crossed his face. He wasn't afraid of an opponent stronger than him. But losing his own power—that was something else entirely.
Everything was gone except his body itself.
As Zane focused on that thought, the pressure intensified.
That kind of force would flatten a solid iron ball into a sheet of metal; a normal human body wouldn't stand a chance.
But Zane’s body was far beyond normal. As strong as a top-tier Mythic Weapon, he had long surpassed human limits. His bones, several times harder than iron or steel, weren't about to be crushed so easily.
[SFX: BONE CRACK]
Zane endured, but then a sharp crack echoed from deep within his skeleton. The pressure was squeezing the very space between his bones, grinding them together.
He was being pushed to his absolute physical breaking point.
This power is insane, he thought. I doubt even a War Lord could command this much force.
Any longer and his body would give out. But just as he reached his limit, the crushing light vanished.
[SFX: HEAVY THUD]
[MUSIC: RELIEF]
The pressure disappeared, and Zane crumpled to the ground.
[AMBIENT: QUIET HALL]
Meanwhile, on the tablet wall outside, Zane's name flickered and disappeared for a breathless moment. The gathered disciples leaned forward, some smirking in anticipation of his failure. Then his name blazed back into existence.
A stunned silence fell over the crowd.
Zane's name was now in the second row.
Right next to Amber's.
