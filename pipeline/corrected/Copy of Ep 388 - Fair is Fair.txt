Ep 388 - Fair is Fair

Word count: 1255

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

The giant cloud of Vitality swirled overhead.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Eventually, all eyes fell from the sky, landing on Zane. He stood motionless at the center of the clearing, his breathing steady, his expression unreadable. The ground around him was scorched black, littered with the remnants of destroyed Nether Phantoms. A faint shimmer of residual energy still clung to his skin, pulsing with each heartbeat before gradually dissipating into the air.

Unlike the others, Finn and Patrick weren’t all that surprised. They’d seen Zane’s power plenty of times and knew exactly how strong he was. He’d used his Corrupt Vitality to swallow Richard’s Hymn of Justice back on the Proving Ground, but nothing on this scale. Still, they never dreamed his power could absorb actual Nether Phantoms.

Few people had ever witnessed the true extent of Zane’s Corrupt Vitality, so it was no wonder they’d underestimated him. The Imperial Army leaders, like Yolande and Hugh, had been among the first to see it. A handful of cultivators who’d entered The Elysian Keep, like Seymour, had also gotten a taste of Zane’s real power. But the soldiers were keeping their mouths shut, and cultivators like Seymour had died in the Keep before they could spread the word.

So, except for Finn and Patrick, the rest of them—Lewis, Frank, Lois, and the disciple from Cloud Hall—were completely floored. Hearts pounding, they could barely believe what they were seeing. The idea that an Adept could pull off something like this was unthinkable. 

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

A stunned silence fell over the group as they tried to process it all. As the shock finally began to fade, their minds turned back to Zane's question - what to do with such a huge surplus of Vitality.

Lois shrugged. “We don’t have any magical item that can store this much Vitality. I’m afraid we’ll just have to let it go to waste." She knew of an artifact called the Cup of Vitality that could temporarily hold it, but even if they had one, its capacity was a joke compared to the massive cloud swirling before them.

Zane gave her a casual smile. “In that case, let’s just let it pop.” 
[SFX: BUILDING ENERGY HUM]
[MUSIC: BLINDING CRESCENDO]
The cloud detonated. A wave of white light erupted outward, so intense that every person in the clearing threw up their hands and squeezed their eyes shut. For a moment, the entire micro-world was nothing but white.
[SFX: SILENCE / SLOW FADE]
When the light died, they opened their eyes. The cloud was gone.

[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION]

As he spoke, Black objects began to materialize where the Nether Phantoms had been destroyed. They looked nearly identical to the ones from the red treasure boxes.

Lewis knew exactly how valuable these things were. They could strengthen a person’s soul—something nearly impossible to boost through normal training or magical pills. You just had to swallow one to reap incredible benefits, making them unbelievably precious. There was, however, a slight difference. The objects from the treasure boxes seemed alive, automatically finding their way into the recipient's brain to take effect. But these new black things just lay on the ground, inert.

[SFX: QUICK GRAB]

Impatience getting the better of him, Lewis snatched one up to find out why for himself.

Zane frowned. What an idiot. 

He was about to stop him when Lois spoke, her voice ice-cold. “Those are raw Nether Phantom essences.If you swallow one, it’ll corrode your organs in a second. Go ahead. If you want to die, that is.”

The essence was already at Lewis’s lips. At her words, he recoiled, dropping it as if it had burned him. “Seriously? Why?” 

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

She explained coolly. “The black object you got on the Hidden Trail was a Soul Essence. It comes from the same source as these, but it’s been purified. The entire reason Nether Phantoms exist on the Cloud Road is to create Soul Essence. A secret method is used to remove the toxins from the phantom’s core.”

Zane’s mind raced. “So you’re saying these hundreds of raw essences could be refined into, what, ten or so pieces of Soul Essence?”

If a single piece of Soul Essence could give your Soul Power a slight bump, ten pieces would mean a major boost.

Lois gave a curt nod, confirming his guess.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]

A spark of excitement lit up everyone’s eyes.

The Death Road was the single biggest obstacle on the Cloud Road, and the only reason anyone dared to walk it was for the hope of finding some legendary reward. High risk, high reward—they all knew the deal. But they’d fought and nearly died on this path only to come up empty-handed, and the disappointment was crushing.

To see hundreds of raw Nether Phantom essences just lying here… it was a massive opportunity.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL GLOW]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

It felt like the sun breaking through after a dark, endless night.

Even unrefined, the essences were priceless for their potential. Back in the Heartlands, pills that could strengthen the soul were not only incredibly rare and outrageously expensive, but they also came with nasty side effects. They might boost your soul, but you always paid a price.

Frank stepped forward, his voice carrying the authority of someone accustomed to command. "Listen, we need to be practical about this.Zane gets thirty percent — he did the most work. Finn gets twenty. The rest is split between me, Lois, and Patrick."
He said nothing about Lewis.
Lewis's eyes narrowed.
"And me?" Lewis's voice had changed — lower, steadier, stripped of its usual nervous energy. His eyes shifted, two points of deep red light blooming in the dark. "I ran when I needed to run. But don't mistake that for weakness."
He reached into his cloak and drew a broad-backed blade.
Frank's eyes sharpened. Another one.

  I propose we pool all the essences together and divide them equally among everyone present."

His tone suggested the matter was settled. "It's the fairest approach. We're all in this together, after all. Besides, without our knowledge of the Death Road and our experience fighting Sky-Reavers, none of us would have survived to claim anything." He crossed his arms, waiting for their agreement as if it were a foregone conclusion.


[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
The proposal sounded reasonable on the surface, but it completely ignored who had actually done the work. Frank was trying to claim credit for survival when it was Zane and Finn who had saved them all from the Nether Phantoms.

Finn’s voice cut through the greedy excitement. “Enough! Frank, your plan is ridiculous. Lewis doesn’t like it, and neither do I or Zane.”

Frank’s expression darkened. “What’s that supposed to mean?”

He’d always seen himself as the leader, but now his grip on the team was slipping. Or maybe he never had it in the first place. Maybe it was all in his head. Losing control made him uneasy. As far as he was concerned, a supreme talent like him was a born leader. When he went on adventures with other disciples from Cloud Hall, he was always the one calling the shots.

For two people to openly defy him… that had never happened before.

[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]

The air crackled with tension. Zane glanced at Frank. “Finn and I took out the Nether Phantoms. I’ll be taking fifty percent.”

“And I’ll take twenty,” Finn added flatly. “The rest is yours to split.”

Patrick didn’t say a word, but his stance was clear. He stood with Zane. The one who does the work gets the biggest cut. Besides, without Zane and Finn, they’d all be dead right now, with nothing to divvy up anyway.

But would the others see it that way?
