Ep 693 - Two Options

Word count: 1027

[AMBIENT: CROWDED SQUARE]

[SFX: WHOOSHING LIGHTS]

A short while later, several streaks of light blazed from the Kensington estate, instantly capturing the attention of the warriors gathered outside, all hoping to win Cora’s hand. One of the lights shot directly down into the square, and from the crowd, a young man shouted a single name.

[SFX: EXCITED SHOUT]

“Zane!”

It was Brent.

Two years ago, during the Martial Tournament, an unexpected turn of events had cost him his chance to challenge Zane, a regret that had stuck with him ever since. He’d grown significantly stronger in the time since, but Zane had vanished without a trace, and rumors of his death were everywhere. So when Zane suddenly appeared, Brent was thrilled—if a little unsettled, suspecting it might be an imposter. He rushed out immediately.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]

But to his shock, it was really him.

Zane was just as surprised to see Brent. He knew the guy was a saint-level genius from the Ethereal Spirit Sect, but he’d had no idea he was connected to House Kensington.

“Brent. It’s been a while,” Zane greeted him coolly.

“You reached the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, too? Fight me!” Brent shot back, wasting no time on pleasantries and completely ignoring the fact that this was neither the time nor the place.

Obviously, Zane wasn't about to agree. He hadn't come here for a brawl with Brent; he was here for the daughter of House Kensington.

“Brent, stop messing around!” A middle-aged man appeared right behind him, his voice sharp. The more observant warriors in the crowd immediately recognized him as Robin Kensington, the head of the house.

Robin clasped his hands in a respectful gesture toward Zane. “I heard you’d arrived. It is a great honor. This isn’t the right place to talk. Please, come inside.”

Zane returned the gesture with a nod and followed the Kensington warriors into the estate.

[AMBIENT: GRAND ESTATE INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

On the way, Robin couldn’t help but wonder what had brought Zane to their doorstep. He’d been gone for two years, with no one knowing where he was. He had no ties to their family, so this wasn't a social call. It was a safe bet he was here for Cora and her Violet-Core Physique. Robin had been worried about her for a long time, and Zane’s arrival felt almost fated.

Once they were settled in the main hall, Robin cut straight to the chase.

“Zane, if I may ask,” he began, his tone serious, “are you here for my daughter, Cora?”

Zane nodded. "I'd like to take her to Cloud Hall for now — keep her out of sight until this situation is resolved. The sect-protecting formation there should be able to hold Maddox off for a while."

Robin's expression flickered with something between relief and disappointment. Brent was an adopted son; Zane was no stranger to the family by reputation. If he could be persuaded to take Cora as more than a temporary ward, the benefits to House Kensington would be considerable.

But Zane's tone was careful and evasive, and Robin was too sharp to miss it. He said nothing — just filed it away and thought: let him meet her first.

Brent said nothing either. But there was a quiet grin on his face. If Zane became his brother-in-law, he'd eventually get his fight one way or another.

Robin clapped his hands and told a servant to fetch Cora.

"I've spoiled that girl," Robin said, sighing. "Since you're here, Zane, you know the situation. We're out of time. I need to find someone she'll go with — or I don't know how this ends."

The servant hadn't told Cora who was waiting for her. As far as she knew, it was another suitor who'd talked his way into the hall. She walked in already braced for it — elegant face like a white lotus, expression like a closed door.

Lavender's voice slipped into Zane's mind, quiet and amused. "The old man wants to hand his daughter off to you. Though from the look on her face, maybe he just can't find any other takers."

Zane ignored her.

"Cora, come and greet our guest," Robin said quickly.

She glanced at Zane with cool indifference. "I don't need to greet anyone. Whoever you are, you're not qualified to marry me. Please leave."

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Robin's hand shot up, ready to slap her, but she stood her ground, having no intention of dodging.

"Don't think that because Elder Xander dotes on you, you can behave however you like under this roof!" Robin snapped.

[SFX: SWIFT MOVEMENT]

Before the blow could land, Zane moved between them, catching Robin’s arm.

“Lord Kensington, please, calm down,” Zane said, his voice even. “I have something to say to Miss Cora.”

Robin couldn’t very well lose his temper in front of a guest. He had no choice but to swallow his anger and lower his hand.

Zane turned to the girl in front of him. “Miss Cora, you’ve misunderstood. I’m not here to win your hand.”

She scowled. “I know. You’re just here to ‘get to know me.’ You want to show off how strong you are, charm me with fancy words, and tell me what great house you’re from and how much potential you have. Just admit it,” she sneered. “You’re here for the same reason as all those other guys outside. There’s a word for people like you: hypocrite. You’re even more disgusting than the ones waiting out there.”

“Ha!” The sound of Brent’s laugh cut through the tense silence.

Cora snapped, whirling on him. “Brent! What are you laughing at? What’s so funny?”

Zane was speechless. He never would have guessed this quiet, elegant young woman could be so fiery and outspoken. Her imagination was clearly running wild.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

His expression hardened. “I’m not trying to get to know you,” he said, his tone serious now. “You have the Violet-Core Physique, which makes it too dangerous for you to stay here. I’ve come to take you away from Ethereal City.”

“Take me away?” she asked, her eyes narrowing with suspicion. “Just you?”

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

“You can either leave with me, or you can die right now,” Zane replied. His voice was eerily calm, but the words were the cold, hard truth. “The choice is yours.”
