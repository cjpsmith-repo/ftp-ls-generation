Ep 1227 - Pay The Price

Word count: 1106

[MUSIC: TENSE MYSTERIOUS]
[AMBIENT: CITY GATES WITH DISTANT CROWD MURMUR]
“Wait, he’s not the City Lord?” Zane asked, confused.
“Nah, our City Lord doesn’t need to get her hands dirty racking up Illusion Points,” the other warrior explained. “This guy’s one of her subordinates. He does the work, gathers the points, and then hands them over.”
Zane processed that. “Handing them over,” of course, just meant the City Lord would eventually kill her own subordinate.
So, instead of personally hunting down every warrior in the city, she had this guy do it for him. Once he’d collected a nice little hoard, she’d just kill him and take everything.
As the logic clicked into place, Zane couldn’t help a wry smile. He had to hand it to her—it was an impressive, if brutal, system. This City Lord was only a Soul Sea Realm warrior, but in no time at all, he’d established a whole new set of rules in the Illusion Battlefield and was already putting them into practice. Whoever he was, he was sharp.
[SFX: SWORD SWING]
“Next batch!” the blue-robed warrior called out, swinging his black sword. His eyes, sharp and predatory, scanned the area as he calculated his next move.
“Get going,” the warrior who’d brought Zane over muttered, giving him a nudge. “Just pay the half, and they’ll leave you alone.”
About seven or eight warriors were already lingering by the city gates, all of them looking deeply uncomfortable. 
[SFX: MULTIPLE FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
One by one, they walked up to the blue-robed warrior and let themselves be killed, handing over half of their Illusion Points in the process.
Zane felt a surge of annoyance as he followed them into the line. His target was the City Lord, not some lackey standing guard at the gate. She had to have amassed a fortune in Illusion Points by now, and killing her was the most efficient way for him to claim his share. He just hadn’t expected her to keep her distance and send a proxy instead.
The blue-robed warrior’s gaze swept over the other warriors before landing on Zane. He felt a faint, almost imperceptible ripple of power coming from him, but he didn’t think much of it. No one in this city dared to defy the City Lord—not unless they planned on staying here forever.
Surprisingly, there were a few who did just that. Some warriors couldn’t stomach being shaken down like this, but they were too weak to fight the system. So they just stayed put in the city, cursing the City Lord and her people every chance they got.
But what good did that do? In the end, strength was the only thing that mattered, not words.
“You. Stand still,” the blue-robed warrior said coldly. 
[SFX: SWORD SWINGING THROUGH AIR]
“I’m already registered,” Zane replied calmly.
“That’s not how this works,” the warrior said, eyes narrowing. “You come back and let me kill you two more times before you’re allowed to leave. Three times total.”
Zane’s smile only widened — though his mood had soured. The Illusion Battlefield was supposed to be a level playing field, open to every warrior in the universe regardless of standing. Instead, this crew wanted to use their backing to write their own rules and decide the fate of everyone weaker than them.
Word among the gathered warriors was that the City Lord carried a Kincaid family treasure — a supreme artifact that let her come and go without a trace, appearing and vanishing like a ghost. It was the reason no one who ever tried to run actually got away, and why, in the end, everyone gave in and registered.
“Another one who thinks he’s special,” a nearby warrior murmured, watching Zane. “He’ll come around. In the Illusion Battlefield or out of it, House Kincaid isn’t something a warrior like him can stand against.”
“His points are getting wiped clean today,” another added, with a trace of pity.
The blue-robed warrior’s eyes narrowed on Zane’s calm expression. Something about it needled him. “What are you laughing at?”
“I’m laughing at you,” Zane said. “In your dreams.”
The warrior’s face went dark. “You’re courting death!”
[SFX: SWORD SLASHING DOWNWARD]
He whipped the black sword down at Zane without another word.
Zane didn’t move to dodge. He simply raised his hand and closed it around the spinning blade.
[SFX: METAL GRINDING]
The warrior had expected Zane’s hand to be shredded to ribbons, bone and all. Instead, the grinding shriek of metal against skin rang out — and the blade slowed, then stopped dead, its edge curling where Zane’s fingers held it.
The warrior’s eyes, however, suddenly shot wide with a hint of raw fear. He’d followed his master for years and had seen his fair share of skilled fighters, even hearing tales of the prodigies from the Thoughtless Minds. 
[SFX: HAND CATCHING BLADE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
But he’d never seen anyone catch a sword—let alone a Divine one—with their bare hand without so much as a scratch. This guy wasn’t just talented; he was a monster.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE ON CITY WALL]
Up on the city wall, the other warriors watched the scene unfold in dead silence. Honestly, they were glad to see it. Some of them had been forced into the City Lord’s service and had grudgingly paid up their points. Their pride had screamed at them to resist, but they were powerless against House Kincaid.
Watching Zane put this guy in his place was a release valve for all their pent-up anger. And from the looks of it, things were about to get interesting.
[MUSIC: CONFIDENT SWAGGER]
“I told you,” Zane said, a small smile playing on his lips as he held the black sword’s blade between two fingers. “The only way you’re taking my points is in your dreams.” He squeezed.
[SFX: SHARP METALLIC SNAP]
A sharp crack echoed through the silence as Zane broke the black sword clean in two.
The blue-robed warrior sucked in a sharp breath. That sword was a low-grade Divine Weapon, a personal gift from the City Lord herself. Sure, losing a weapon in the Illusion Battlefield didn’t mean it was gone in the real world, but a spike of cold terror shot down his spine anyway. The sheer, casual ease with which Zane had broken it was terrifying.
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS FLEEING]
[AMBIENT: CITY GATE COURTYARD]
With a whoosh, the warrior spun on his heel, his mind already made up. He had to get away from Zane, right now. He bolted toward the city gates.
“Whoa, hold on,” Zane called out with a grin. “You think you can just try to kill me and then run away? You can go, sure. But you’re leaving your Illusion Points behind.”
There was no way he was letting a prize pig like this get away. The warrior’s points couldn’t compare to the City Lord’s hoard, but he was bound to have a pretty decent amount on him.
