Ep 578 - The Heavenly Kill
Word count: 976
[AMBIENT: TOWER ARENA]
[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE]
Zane knew exactly why he’d come to the Tower of Sin. First, to get stronger. Second, to claim its legendary inheritance.
On this continent, races like the Ogres and the Demon Night made Humans look pitifully weak. Zane had already pissed off the Ogres the second he got here; he wasn’t stupid enough to pick a fight with the Demon Night, too.
[SFX: SWORD STRIKE]
Even though he’d only used the flat of his blade to swat Teresa's backside, the blow was surprisingly hard. She stumbled, her face burning with humiliation.
The Demon Night were known for their elegance and pride—public humiliation like this was unthinkable. The moment she hit the ground, Teresa sprang back to her feet like a cat, flipping over and landing ready to attack.
But Zane was faster.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
His sword was already at her pale neck, the tip of the Relic-Class blade humming with power. One twitch, and he could take her head clean off.
Teresa was furious, but she wasn't an idiot. She’d read the rules of the Tower of Sin until she knew them by heart.
Back home, she was a noble, respected in her sacred halls. No one would have dared touch her. But here, she was just another fighter. The Demon Night influenced the Tower, sure, but no one could protect her in the arena. The rules were forged by a pact between races, and they didn’t bend for anyone—not even a saint-level genius like her.
If Zane killed her, the Demon Night would hunt him to the ends of the earth for revenge. But revenge wouldn't bring her back.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane's voice was calm."Surrender, and you live."
Swallowing her pride, Teresa choked out, “I… admit defeat.”
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY TRANSFER]
In a flash, the Lucky Light from Teresa’s token streamed into Zane’s.
Lucky Light was the reward for winning a match, and the amount you got depended on how much your opponent had.
Teresa was a War General of the Demon Night, a fighter of incredible skill. The only people who ever dared to challenge her were other War Generals, some even stronger than she was. As a result, the Lucky Light he won from her was ten times more than anything he’d earned so far.
It paid to be powerful.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS DEPARTING]
Teresa shot Zane a look that could kill before stalking out of the arena.
Phoebe let out a quiet sigh of relief.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Whispers broke out across the stands.
"No wonder Boris told us not to throw our lives away. This guy's a human War Fighter — and he just beat a god-tier genius from the Demon Night sacred land."
"She's not just any War General. She can't be treated like an ordinary fighter. And he still took her apart."
"If this human keeps climbing floor by floor… the human race might actually win another seat."
Zane looked out at the crowd. "Anyone else?"
[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]
Silence.
No one moved. Teresa had already scared most of them off. Now that Zane had beaten Teresa, the deterrent was ten times stronger. Word from the first floor had already spread — everyone knew what he'd done there. No one was stepping up.
After a long pause, Zane turned and walked off the stage.
[AMBIENT: TOWER CORRIDOR]
[MUSIC: TENSE LOW STRINGS]
Teresa was shaking with rage.
The Demon Night War Lord caught up to her in the corridor, keeping pace beside her in silence.
"I must kill him," she said through her teeth.
"He's not simple," the War Lord said carefully. "You're not his match right now."
Teresa's voice dropped to something cold and flat. "I don't care. If ordinary methods won't work, I'll use the Heavenly Kill."
The War Lord's expression changed instantly. "Don't be foolish. The Heavenly Kill is a last resort. You know what the elders said."
"This is a last resort."
He paused, choosing his words. "Teresa, you must be smart about this. You'll face him again — you're both climbing the same Tower. Wait until you're stronger. Challenge him then." He glanced back in the direction of the arena. "Besides, there's a Demon Night woman traveling with him. I don't know which branch she's from, but I'll find out."
Teresa went quiet. Then, darkly: "And what if he gets stronger too?"
The War Lord had no answer for that. He could only sigh.
In truth, he agreed with her. But the boy hadn't killed her. That was something. And a setback like this — humiliating as it was — might be exactly what Teresa's martial heart needed.
[AMBIENT: ARENA FADING]
[MUSIC: VICTORIOUS THEME]
With no other challengers, Zane left the arena, Phoebe trailing close behind.
Like Teresa, Zane wanted to get to the top of the Tower as fast as possible. He had no interest in fighting these low-level scrubs, but the rules forced his hand.
[AMBIENT: BUSY TOWER QUARTERS]
When Zane reached the living quarters on the second layer, he noticed a lot of women hanging around.
They were from all races—Ogres, Orcs, Humans. He even saw some from the Demon Night. They were all dressed provocatively, their clothes designed to show off every curve.
"What’s with all the women?" Zane asked.
"Escorts," Phoebe said bluntly. "The warriors in the Tower are under a lot of pressure. They come here to blow off steam. You'll find them on every layer, all the way to the tenth."
Zane wrinkled his nose. He saw Humans and even some from the Demon Night, but there were also Orcs and Ogres. The thought of being with any of them made his stomach turn.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
He had to get out of there. But just as he turned to leave, a Human woman stepped out, blocking his path. She smiled up at him. "Hey there. Someone wants to have a word with you."
The woman was stunning. Zane gave her a slight smile. "So, how many Aether crystals for a night?"
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
The woman’s smile evaporated, her face twisting in fury.
Zane gulped. His joke had not gone over well.
