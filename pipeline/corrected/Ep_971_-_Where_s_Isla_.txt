Ep 971 - Where’s Isla?
Word count: 973
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN MONASTERY]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE EASTERN STRINGS]
Carter’s mouth was still hanging open as the words sank in — the leader of Cloud Hall, standing right in front of him at the Skyreach Sect gates.
The Skyreach Sect was a Second-Tier Sect, technically under the Cloud Hall, but for most of its disciples and stewards, the Cloud Hall might as well have been a myth. Getting in was next to impossible. The All Peaks Competition, held every three years, offered the top three contestants a chance to walk the Cloud Road. But with hundreds of thousands of disciples competing, the odds were astronomical—something like one in a hundred thousand.
And even then, the winners had to face off against disciples from four other Second-Tier Sects. It put things in perspective. The leader of the entire Skyreach Sect would barely rank as a steward in the Cloud Hall—not even high enough to be an elder. That’s how far out of reach the Cloud Hall truly was.
[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]
Zane clearly overestimated how well the steward would take the reveal of Tia’s identity. Carter’s jaw went slack, his eyes wide with a fear that stole his voice as he stared at Tia.
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
Elders from the Cloud Hall had visited before, but never the leader herself. For a beat, Carter’s mind went blank, but his training kicked in. He snapped into a deep bow. “It is an honor to have you visit the Skyreach Sect, Magister Tia,” he said, his voice trembling with respect. “I’ll inform our leader at once!”
Whether this woman was truly the Cloud Hall’s leader was a problem for Kenneth to solve. Carter’s only job was to deliver the message.
[SFX: WHOOSH OF WIND]
[MUSIC: URGENT DRUMS]
Without another word, he leaped onto his sky-chariot and shot toward the heart of the Skyreach Sect.
It didn’t take long. One by one, the stewards turned back to the three figures at the entrance, shifted uneasily on their feet, and then — as if by unspoken agreement — dropped into deep bows.
When Carter’s Flying Chariot came screaming back moments later, and he saw the scene below him, he didn’t need anyone to explain. He jumped down and fell into line beside them.
[SFX: HURRIED FOOTSTEPS]
Moments later, a big, burly man came hurrying toward the entrance. It was Sir Kenneth, the leader of the Skyreach Sect.
“Sir Kenneth!”
“Sir Kenneth!”
The stewards exchanged uneasy glances. They were good at reading a room, and seeing their leader practically running told them everything they needed to know. The three visitors were a very big deal, even if the stewards still had no clue who they were.
[MUSIC: WARM NOSTALGIC STRINGS]
When Kenneth arrived, the first person he saw was Zane, and a proud smile broke across his face. Zane had always been self-reliant, earning every resource for his training on his own. Kenneth had watched him grow, but he never would have imagined that in just a few short years, the boy would become the strongest warrior in all the Heartlands.
Kenneth had heard about Zane’s recent wedding to Tia. The Skyreach Sect had sent an expensive gift, of course, but that was the extent of their involvement. The only guests invited were leaders of the Third-Tier sects. Despite its affiliation with the Cloud Hall, the Skyreach Sect, as a Second-Tier Sect, simply hadn’t been important enough to warrant an invitation.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
And now, here Zane was, back in the Eastern Territories. While Zane’s family was nearby, Kenneth had a strong suspicion about the real reason for his visit: the Sky Princess, Isla.
As Kenneth led Zane, Tia, and Cora on a tour of the Skyreach Sect, Tia glanced at Sir Kenneth and said mildly, “I understand there was some trouble with your Guardians. Something about a demotion that wasn’t entirely deserved.”
Sir Kenneth felt his scalp prickle. He straightened. “This subordinate deserves a reprimand. The Skyreach Sect has not been managed strictly enough.” He lowered his head. “Steward Carter will be reassigned to a post befitting his abilities. I give you my word.”
Behind him, the stewards exchanged wide-eyed glances. Carter stood very still, not daring to breathe. He hadn’t expected this. A few words from the Hall Master, and his entire fate had just been rewritten.
As they walked, Zane fell a half-step behind. Sir Kenneth kept his eyes forward, but Zane could see the faint pride in his expression — and beneath it, something quieter. Before the All Peaks Competition, Kenneth had given Zane nothing. No resources, no backing, no advantage. Everything Zane had built, he’d built alone. That Kenneth now walked beside him with the Hall Master on his other side was not lost on either of them.
Zane sent him a private message using his Vitality. Sir Kenneth, where is Lady Novak?
Sir Kenneth had been expecting the question. He answered just as discreetly, After you left the Eastern Territories, Isla Novak was held in the royal palace for a time. About six months later, I brought her back here, to the Skyreach Sect. She’s returned to Misty Peak and has resumed her duties as a master.
Kenneth had given Zane his word: as long as he was in charge, no harm would come to Isla Novak. He’d told Zane he couldn’t help her reclaim the throne, but he could absolutely guarantee her safety.
But about a year after making that promise, news of Zane’s astonishing success at the Martial Tournament reached the sect, and Kenneth had a change of heart. He was well aware of the bond between Zane and Isla Novak, and he knew it was only a matter of time before Zane became a major figure in the Cloud Hall.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT DRUMS]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
The day after the news of Zane’s victory arrived, Kenneth had marched straight into the palace. In front of all the assembled government and military leaders, he’d kicked King Ray right off the throne and personally freed Isla Novak from her house arrest.
