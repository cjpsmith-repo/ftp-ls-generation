Ep 1129 - Try It Out

Word count: 1158

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CONTEMPLATION]
Lavender had weighed her options before ever setting foot in the Oaken Pavilion. She was confident that, with her power, this backwater outpost of the Dark World would bend to her will.
But what was the point? Taking it over would be a waste of time and, frankly, a liability. It wouldn’t help in her fight against the Queen of Punishment, and it would put her squarely on her enemies’ radar. Everything she did had to be secret. The fewer people who knew about her, the better.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON WOODEN FLOOR]
The Oaken Pavilion was a trade hub established in Auspice City by the Violetwood Sanctuary, a place that did a lot of business with other local factions. It was always flush with treasures and resources, but after a quick look around, Lavender just shook her head. Nothing caught her eye.
After all, the Dark World was just a remote corner of the Upper World, a far cry from the Mist Palace. The resources here might seem priceless to an ordinary warrior, but to her and Zane, they were worthless. She was more interested in the true, hidden treasures of the Dark World.
[SFX: STONE CLINKING]
Still, while the pavilion’s general stock was unimpressive, she did walk away with a whole lot of Sovereign Stones. Every year, the pavilion collected the Stones from its trade with other races and handed them over to the Violetwood Sanctuary. It meant they had a massive stockpile.
To avoid raising suspicion at the Sanctuary—and to protect Nyla, the woman responsible for the handover—Lavender only took about a fifth of the stash. 
[SFX: HEAVY SACK MOVEMENT]
Even so, she walked away with nearly thirty thousand Sovereign Stones.
With the changes his body had gone through, Zane didn’t really need Sovereign Stones anymore. But you can never have too much treasure, and it might come in handy someday. He’d once risked his life for a hundred of them; to any warrior of the Soul Sea Realm, thirty thousand was an unimaginable fortune.
After two days, Nyla resigned from her post at the Violetwood Sanctuary. 
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON FOREST PATH]
She made up an excuse to leave, and with the runestone Lavender had given her, she set off for the Eternal Night World.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: QUIET CITY NIGHT]
Meanwhile, Zane slipped quietly back to his place in Auspice City to wait for Lincoln. A few months ago, they had agreed to meet in half a year, and now there were only about forty days left. Zane couldn’t shake the feeling that Lincoln might not make it on time.
[MUSIC: TRAINING MONTAGE PERCUSSION]
He spent the next month and a half deep in his training.
Progress came on two fronts. His grasp of the laws was deepening steadily, and the Stellar Body was responding — he found himself connecting with additional stars more easily than before. He suspected the Luck-Devouring Snakes had something to do with it. When he’d absorbed them on Willow Island, his luck had spiked to something almost absurd — the world bending toward him for a few hours like he was the center of it. That hadn’t lasted. But something had settled into a new baseline. He couldn’t measure it precisely, but the stars came easier, and that was evidence enough.
The other front was less satisfying.
[MUSIC: QUIET TENSION]
At the Soul Sea Realm, a warrior’s Vitality was supposed to begin liquefying — condensing under its own pressure until it pooled and gathered like a tide. But Zane’s Chaos Energy showed no sign of doing anything of the sort. It just kept rolling through his Vitality core like smoke, dense and restless and entirely unwilling to condense.
The Primordial Chaos Tactic offered no guidance on this. His master had speculated that Chaos Energy would eventually behave like Vitality — that it too would form a Chaos Sea. That speculation, it was becoming clear, was wrong. At least at this step.
Zane had spent days frowning over the problem, going for walks when the walls of his residence started to feel close, trying to shake loose some angle he hadn’t considered. He brought it to the Cobalt Dragon, who turned it over carefully and admitted he had no answer. The Cobalt Dragon knew the Vitality system. The Red Dragon knew body refinement. Neither of them had ever encountered a Chaos cultivation path.
But the Cobalt Dragon had an idea.
Back when Zane had first set foot on the martial path, he’d practiced a technique from the demon tribes — the Titan Strike. The strikes and forms in it weren’t much by current standards, but the Corrupt Vitality it produced had a unique property: it devoured. It absorbed other energies and converted them.
The Cobalt Dragon’s reasoning was simple: if the Chaos Energy couldn’t liquefy on its own because there wasn’t enough of it, why not give it the ability to devour — to take in Vitality from outside and build its own mass? Chaos Energy amplified every technique it touched exponentially. There was every reason to think it could do the same with the Titan Strike’s devouring property.
Zane turned this over for a moment. Then he nodded. On a path no one had ever walked before, there were no rules to break.
As he began to practice the mental forms of the Titan Strike, the Chaos Energy inside him started to churn. 
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
[SFX: MYSTICAL WHOOSH]
A single thread pulled away from his core, snaked through his body, and shot out from his fingertip. 
[SFX: SWIRLING MIST]
A thin, gray strand of energy, dense as mist, swirled around his hand.
He wondered if this new energy, filtered through the Titan Strike, would have the devouring power he was looking for. A flicker of nervousness tightened his chest, but he immediately smothered it with a rush of excitement. He almost laughed. *So what if this fails? I’ll just find another way. Who’s going to stop me? Nothing to be nervous about.*
With that, he reached into his Storage Ring and pulled out a supreme Aether crystal. 
[SFX: CRYSTAL CHIME]
[SFX: PULSING ENERGY HUM]
The small stone pulsed, visibly containing streams of thick Vitality.
[SFX: GENTLE CRYSTAL TAP]
[SFX: ENERGY HISS]
Instead of crushing it, Zane gently tapped the stone with his finger, creating a small hole. A wisp of pure Vitality leaked out. Before it could dissipate, he injected the Chaos Energy from his fingertip directly into the crystal, his eyes locked on it.
*It’s not working?*
Through the crystal’s transparent shell, he could see the gray Chaos Energy swirling around, refusing to merge with the milky-white Vitality. It wasn’t even trying to devour it.
His lips tightened. The experiment was a failure. He’d been too optimistic.
He sighed. *Oh well.*
[SFX: CRYSTAL EXPLOSION]
Just as Zane was about to tuck the aether crystal back into his Storage Ring, it exploded in his face.
