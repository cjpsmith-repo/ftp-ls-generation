[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS THEME]

Among the divine textures, the one-star versions represented the most fundamental level—the foundation on which all greater power was built.

The ranking system measured a texture's destructive potential by its stars. Zane had learned this from the Cobalt Dragon during their intense training sessions, when his soul still throbbed from the effort of crafting even the simplest patterns. 

A seven-star divine texture, despite being no larger than a human palm, possessed enough concentrated power to obliterate vast swathes of land in a single instant. The thought both terrified and fascinated him.

He'd seen the shopkeeper's ancient texts describing such weapons—entire mountain ranges reduced to ash, cities swallowed by the earth, rivers boiled dry. The gap between his current abilities and such devastating force felt insurmountable, a chasm so wide it made his recent victories seem trivial.

Its power was so immense it could annihilate the entire Heartlands—a world-ending weapon in the wrong hands.

Zane was a long way from mastering something like that. Even the Cobalt Dragon couldn't create one anymore, its Soul Power long since depleted. And even if it could, Zane lacked the necessary materials.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]

However, Zane was far more interested in a four-star divine texture known as “Soul Breath.” This particular texture offered a unique benefit: it could double his cultivation speed with no negative side effects.

His relatively low cultivation level had been his biggest worry since arriving in the Heartlands. Acquiring the Soul Breath texture would be a game-changer, allowing him to grow stronger at an incredible rate.

At only eighteen, Zane had reached the fourth Level of the Adept Realm—a respectable achievement back in Castermere County. But in the Skyreach Sect, it was merely average. And now, in the Heartlands, it felt like nothing at all. While his foundation was solid enough to challenge masters in the Soul Realm, he knew he wouldn't survive against someone at the peak of that realm.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING SECT GROUNDS]

[SFX: WHOOSH OF WIND]


Rather than borrow a flying chariot from Cloud Hall, Zane wanted to test a divine rune he'd just finished — one designed specifically to let cultivators below the Soul Realm fly by borrowing the wind. He stepped off the edge of Cloud Hall, a thousand meters up, and let the rune do its work. Combined with the Soaring Phoenix Crystal, he drifted down like a leaf caught in a current, bleeding speed gradually until his boots met the ground without so much as a thud.

Two Adept guards tilted their heads back, gazing at Cloud Hall floating a thousand meters overhead like a celestial palace among the clouds. Their minds raced to make sense of what they'd just witnessed. If this young man had descended from that legendary place, then everything they thought they knew about cultivation had just been shattered.

Cloud Hall was one of the most prestigious institutions in all the Heartlands, reserved exclusively for cultivators of exceptional talent. Its disciples were spoken of in hushed, reverent tones. Yet the idea that an Adept—someone at their own cultivation level—could fly defied every principle they'd been taught. Flight was a privilege of the Soul Realm and beyond, a boundary as absolute as the difference between heaven and earth.

Zane had noticed the two guards the moment his feet touched the ground, but their stunned expressions barely registered. His thoughts were already racing ahead to his destination. Without sparing them another glance, he strode purposefully toward Cloudhaven, his boots kicking up small clouds of dust on the well-worn road.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY]

[MUSIC: GRAND ORCHESTRAL]

As soon as he entered the city, the difference between the Heartlands and the Eastern Territories was stark.

Home to both the Cloud Hall and the Cloud School, Cloudhaven was effectively the capital city of the Heartlands. Its sheer scale dwarfed the imperial city back in the Eastern Territories.

The Imperial City had its own power players—the Seven Great Houses, the royal family, and even the Skyreach Sect, a Tier-Two sect. By comparison, House Novak was merely a Tier-Two force, while the Seven Great Houses operated outside any official ranking system.

Cloudhaven, however, was in another league entirely. It hosted eight Tier-Three sects, and that wasn't even counting the Cloud School, which was linked to the prestigious Cloud Hall.

Walking the streets of the city, Soul Realm practitioners were everywhere. If you were lucky, you might even spot a few from the Inner-Core Realm, typically moving in small groups while visiting to buy supplies.

Here, no one gave Zane a second glance. Though he’d been well-known elsewhere, in Cloudhaven, he was a nobody. For Zane, who hated being the center of attention, the anonymity was a relief. It meant he could move through the city without any trouble.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON COBBLESTONE]

Following the old man’s directions, he quickly found the Guild’s branch office.

Zane had done his research on the World Merchants' Guild. He’d learned that although the guild wasn't on any official power lists, it was incredibly influential—even a little stronger than Cloud Hall and on par with the Ethereal Spirit Sect, the most powerful sect in the Heartlands.

[AMBIENT: QUIET STREET]

[MUSIC: PEDESTRIAN SCENE]

Given the Guild's legendary power, Zane half-expected a grand, imposing fortress. He even felt a pang of self-consciousness, wondering if his clothes were fine enough to get him through the door. But the reality stunned him. The Guild branch was incredibly low-profile, tucked away among a row of ordinary houses. If it weren’t for the two Soul Realm practitioners guarding the gate, no one would ever guess its importance.

One of the guards stepped into his path. "Stop. World Merchants' Guild business only — move along."
The other looked him up and down and added, "This isn't a place for someone at your level, friend. Save yourself the trouble."
Without a word, Zane reached into his coat and produced the invitation card the old man had given him. He held it out.
The guard took it. His expression shifted the moment he read the name on it.
[SFX: HURRIED FOOTSTEPS WITHIN]

The two guards broke out in a cold sweat, feeling the sheer weight of Michael’s presence. They were completely baffled. Why is he being so polite to this kid?

"My name is Michael Nash," Michael said with a warm smile. "Zane, may I invite you inside? We can discuss this in detail over some mulled cider." His tone was so deferential that it was as if he were speaking to a superior.

The guards’ earlier arrogance curdled into terror. They had been so dismissive, and now they were terrified the young man would complain about them. This could be the end of their careers.

Zane nodded politely. As he started to follow Michael inside, he glanced back at the two guards, his expression completely neutral.

[SFX: RELIEVED EXHALE]

A silent, collective sigh of relief escaped them.

His face was calm. If he wasn't too annoyed, they figured, maybe he wouldn't rat them out.

[AMBIENT: GUILD INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

As Michael and Zane’s figures disappeared into the building, the guards were left standing in stunned silence, wondering just who in the world that young man was.
