Ep 1210 - Bare Hands

Word count: 1050

[MUSIC: TENSE PULSATING]
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD]
Zane didn’t bother to hide his approach; his gaze locked on Thane with a dangerous intensity. But the ogre was so lost in his own battle-crazed high that he didn’t notice a thing until Zane was just three steps away. 
The Bronze Blood had taken over, and Thane was moving on pure, thoughtless instinct. He hadn’t even gotten a good look at Zane before he was already swinging. 
[SFX: DUAL AXES WHOOSHING]
His two massive axes, reeking of gore and glowing with a faint crimson light, cut a heavy arc through the air—a bloody blur of motion.
It wasn’t about technique; with the Bronze Blood surging through him, Thane didn’t need any. The raw power behind the swing was enough to shatter mountains. Anything caught in its path would simply be obliterated. Most fighters would have backed away, looking for an opening. Going head-to-head with an ogre in the throes of their Bronze Blood was suicide.
But Zane didn’t move. He stood his ground, a mere three inches from the twin arcs of death. As the axes blurred past, impossibly fast and heavy, he moved *with* them. 
[SFX: WIND RUSH]
It was like watching a leaf caught in a whirlwind—swept along by the storm but never torn apart.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
The ogre warriors watching from the city were speechless. From their vantage point, it looked like Zane was somehow glued to the axes. It was a bizarre illusion—less like Thane was trying to chop him to bits and more like he was desperately trying to shake him off.
“What is he doing? How is that possible?” one of them finally sputtered.
“Some kind of attunement handbook?” another suggested. “Something that makes him stick to the blades?”
“That’s stupid,” a third scoffed. “If he was stuck, why wouldn’t Thane just slam the axes into the ground and finish him?”
As if on cue, that was exactly what Thane tried next. 
[SFX: AXES WHOOSHING DOWN]
Convinced this strange man was somehow stuck to his weapons, he raised the axes high and brought them crashing down toward the ground.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT ON STONE]
But just as the blades were about to pulverize him, Zane slipped through the three-inch gap between the metal and the stone, completely unharmed.
[SFX: GROUND CRACKING]
The axes didn’t just strike stone—they split it. A jagged crack tore open at the point of impact and kept going, racing out across the field for what looked like ten miles, carving a small canyon into the earth.
The ogres watching stared, shaking their heads in disbelief. It was impossible, but it had happened right in front of them.
“This human’s like some kind of slippery loach,” one ogre muttered, shaking his head.
“How did he do that?” one murmured.
“There was no flicker of Vitality,” another pointed out. 
“You’re right. But he’s not a body refiner, either. I didn’t feel any burst of Forceful Energy.”
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]
A stunned silence fell over the crowd before they all erupted at once, arriving at a terrifying conclusion.
Zane hadn’t used Vitality or Forceful Energy. He had just stopped those two massive axes with nothing but his own physical strength.
“What kind of freak is this guy?” someone muttered.
Even with the Bronze Blood roaring through him, Thane paused, sizing up the man in front of him properly for the first time.
“It’s you!” he said, recognition dawning.
Zane was one of the two human warriors Thane had crossed paths with before the Illusion Battlefield even opened—the ones who’d cut down eleven Ogre warriors in the blink of an eye. 
Thane had written them off at the time. In his mind, anyone with real confidence camped the city gates and farmed points off warriors too afraid to leave; these two had walked out into the open instead, which he’d taken as nothing but bravado. He had no idea how poorly that gate-camping strategy actually paid compared to hunting.
To Zane, men like Thane—content to sit behind the walls and pick off the desperate—were just sheep waiting to be culled. Now that he was back, he intended to do exactly that.
“It’s me,” Zane said, a faint smile still on his face.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[AMBIENT: ARENA TENSION]
[SFX: DUAL AXE SWING]
“Ha! Good. Let’s see how long you can keep hiding,” Thane roared, and struck again—one axe arcing down from above, the other sweeping up from below, a pincer aimed to cut Zane into three pieces.
[SFX: HANDS GRIPPING METAL]
This time, Zane didn’t move to dodge. The white smoke wreathing his body dissipated. He opened both hands, eyes locked on Thane, and as the axes closed in from opposite directions, he caught them. Bare-handed. One in each fist, stopped dead in the air.
[SFX: METAL GROANING]
His fingers sank into the metal, carving ten deep grooves into the surface of the Low Grade-One Divine-Class weapon.
Every race in this universe had carved out its own specialty. Humans leaned into true cultivation, channeling Vitality through their cores. The Ogre race built their strength through Body Tempering, hardening flesh and bone past anything a blade should be able to touch. The Demon Night Clan didn’t need to train for it at all—they were simply born strong. Each path, pursued to its limit, could carry a warrior to the same heights.
Thane wasn’t just an Ogre. He was an outstanding Body Tempering master, one of the best his clan had to offer. And yet here was a human—someone who, by every read the crowd could take of him, wasn’t channeling Vitality and wasn’t a body refiner either—beating him at the one thing Ogres were supposed to own outright, bare hand against forged steel.
But as shocked as the spectators were, Thane was a thousand times more so. He was a descendant of the Golden Family, a wielder of the Bronze Blood. His power and bloodline were top-tier, enough to make him a core disciple in a tenth-grade sacred place. When his blood surged, his strength more than tripled; combined with his training, not even a warrior at the Spirit Supreme Realm should have been able to withstand his axes.
[SFX: METAL STRAIN]
And yet… this human, this man who didn’t even look particularly strong, had just stopped his attack cold. By squeezing the blades with his bare hands.
A hot wave of humiliation washed over Thane as he stared at the faint smile on Zane’s face.
[SFX: STRUGGLING GRUNT]
To make it all worse, he couldn’t pull his axes free.
