Ep 942 - Your Life Ends Here

Word count: 1174

[AMBIENT: TOWER OF SIN]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The Ogre War Sage before him had left the Tower of Sin years ago, having shattered the limits of the top-level War Lords to become a War Sage himself.

That ascension meant he could no longer enter the Cursed Land or the Tower of Sin.

It was also why Zane's name was burned into his memory, even though he'd never actually laid eyes on the man.

[SFX: MONSTROUS LAUGHTER]

Hearing Esther cry out the name, the Ogre’s face split into a monstrous grin. “Zane Lowell! I was so disappointed when I heard you’d fled the main continent. To think you’d have the guts to come back just to die! How delightful. How very delightful!”

A fresh wave of dread washed over Esther. This was the worst possible time for him to appear. She could see he'd grown stronger, but he was still just a first-level War Sage.

Of course, Zane’s strength could never be judged by his rank alone. Back when he was only a War Lord, he’d taken down a full-fledged War Sage.

The real problem was that the Flaming Place and the Dread Place had launched a full-scale sect war against the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary. They’d brought several Soul Sea Realm warriors, and against that kind of power, Zane stood no chance. She trusted him against any War Sage. But a Soul Sea domain was another matter entirely.

“Zane, run!” Esther screamed, her voice raw with desperation. “The Sanctuary is falling! You have to get out of here, save yourself!”

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

Zane, however, didn’t seem to hear her. His expression darkened. “Why would the Flaming Place and the Dread Place dare attack the Sanctuary?” he asked, his voice low and dangerous. “Isn't Amber in charge?”

Before Esther could answer, the Ogre War Sage cut in with a booming laugh. “Now that you're here, there's no getting away! It’s a fool's dream to think you can escape me!” 

He had been hearing stories about Zane for years. Every Ogre in the Flaming Place and the Dread Place hated Zane with every fiber of their being.

When Zane drove the Ogres from the Tower of Sin, he had effectively gutted the future of both the Flaming Place and the Dread Place. Cut off from the Tower's resources, their decline was inevitable.

After their top War Lords were forced to return home, every Ogre—from the mighty War Sages down to the lowly War Fighters—cursed Zane's name. This very War Sage had been so consumed with rage that he’d torn his own home to shreds. No wonder he was itching for payback.

If he could have set foot in the Cursed Land, he would have hunted Zane down and torn him limb from limb. But that had been impossible. Now, Zane had come to him. How could he possibly let this chance slip away?

[SFX: POWERFUL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

Ogres were massive by nature, but this War Sage was built like a small mountain. The air itself shrieked as he charged, closing the distance to Zane in an instant. The saber came down with everything he had — ten full layers of Vitality behind the blade, a strike he'd never held back on in his life.

It landed.

Zane didn't move.

Not a sway. Not a step back. He absorbed the force without a flicker of expression, routing it outward through Power Transfer until it dispersed harmlessly into the earth beneath them.

The Ogre stared.

“You're called Saroyan, right?” Zane said.

Saroyan's face went blank. “How do you know my name?”

“I've lived for one hundred and ninety-two years, three months, and thirteen days,” Zane continued, his voice unchanged.

“You — how do you know that?!”

[MUSIC: EERIE REVELATION]

Esther was just as stunned. There were countless War Sages among the major races on the main continent. Even the Violet Heart Sanctuary's intelligence network couldn't place a name and an exact age together like that. How did he know?

Zane didn't answer. He only shook his head slowly.

“Your lifespan ends here.”

[SFX: METAL SNAP]

He reached out and grabbed Saroyan's saber by the blade. With a single twist, the mid-grade Relic snapped at the middle and fell into two pieces.

Something broke in Saroyan's expression. It wasn't just the display of strength — it was the other thing, the uncanny certainty in Zane's eyes, the sense that this man knew everything about him and had already decided how this ended. An Ogre War Sage. A race that did not flee.

Saroyan fled.

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

[SFX: NECK SNAP]

He didn't get far. Zane's figure became a thin line, crossing the distance in an instant. His hand closed around Saroyan's neck.

One crisp sound. The Ogre War Sage's head left his shoulders.

[AMBIENT: AERIAL BATTLE]

[SFX: ENERGY BEAMS]

High above the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, over a hundred beams of light still streaked across the sky, clashing in a chaotic dance of battle. The Demon Night forces were clearly losing.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

Taking in the desperate battle, Zane turned his focus back to Esther, his voice cutting through the chaos. “You still haven't answered me. How could they dare to do this? Where is Amber?”

A bitter, defeated look crossed Esther's face. “She's facing her tribulation.”

“The Greater Celestial Tribulations?” Zane pressed, his voice sharp.

“Yes,” Esther confirmed, her voice laced with fury. “And the Flaming Place and the Dread Place are using it as an opportunity to destroy us.”

Reaching the Life-and-Death Realm made you a War Sage, but it also meant you had to face a Celestial Tribulation. Warriors in the Life-and-Death Realm faced the Lesser Celestial Tribulation, still, for those who ascended to the Soul Sea Realm, the Greater Celestial Tribulations awaited—a trial far more terrifying than the last.

And as incredible as Amber's talent was, the Greater Celestial Tribulations were no joke.

In the past, the Tower of Sin had enforced a fragile peace across the main continent. The different races mostly kept to themselves, avoiding open war. If Amber had faced her tribulation back then, the Flaming Place and the Dread Place would never have dared to make a move against the Sanctuary.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

But now, with the Tower of Sin lost to them, their power was fading. As the Demon Night and the Humans grew stronger by the day, it was only a matter of time before the Ogres were wiped from existence. 

With Amber locked away, facing her tribulation, they decided to bet everything they had on one last, desperate gambit.

Esther watched Zane in silence for a moment. She'd seen what he just did to Saroyan — a fourth-level War Sage, killed in a single exchange. His strength already rivaled a Soul Sea master's.

But there was one thing he didn't have. A domain. If one of their Soul Sea warriors moved against him and severed his connection to the world's will, he'd lose that edge in an instant. 

That was the danger she couldn't shake.
