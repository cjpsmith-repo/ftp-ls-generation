Ep 808 - Protect Zane

Word count: 1114

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CONTEMPLATIVE]

War King York's soul attack had crashed into Zane and done nothing, and now every warrior in the square was waiting for someone to explain how that was possible.

The only explanation the crowd could come up with was that the War King, York, must have been showing Zane incredible mercy. There was no other way to make sense of it. He couldn’t have possibly gone all out.

When a martial artist creates a Soul Sea, they’re essentially building an entire world inside themselves to cultivate their soul. But resources in the Lower World are scarce—nothing like what’s available in the Upper World—so there just aren’t many ways for fighters to train their souls down here. Most have to wait until they’ve already reached the Soul Sea Realm, but in the Upper World, talented fighters could reach the Fighting Soul Realm long before that.

Zane, however, was a glaring exception.

Then again, maybe it wasn't so surprising. For starters, he hadn’t actually trained his soul in the Lower World.

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

He’d used the Dragon Soar Arena to travel to the Upper World, where he faced the trial of an Owl Beast’s evil spirit. The ordeal pushed his soul to improve at a ridiculous speed, and on top of that, he absorbed a massive amount of soul power, which shot him all the way to the Fighting Soul Realm.

Down here, the strongest warriors in the Soul Sea Realm could easily crush anyone still in the Life-and-Death Realm. The gap in their soul levels was just too massive. Only by reaching the Fighting Soul Realm could someone even hope to bridge that chasm.

War King York stared at Zane with a complicated expression. 

He could feel it now — Zane's soul wasn't weaker than his. It was stronger. 

If Zane had chosen to fight back, the wisp of soul York had launched would have been destroyed. He was certain of it. Zane had chosen not to retaliate, most likely wary of what York could do with his full strength behind him.

After a long silence, York spoke. His voice came out almost against his will. “Good. Good. Good.” He said it three times, each one slower than the last. “At your age, with your cultivation — a soul close to the Fighting Soul Realm. You are indeed something.”

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED MURMUR]

The crowd erupted. Everyone had assumed York held back. Now York himself was saying he hadn't — that Zane's soul had simply withstood it.

The Holy Maiden didn't miss a beat. “That's right!” she called out. “Wallace told me himself — Zane destroyed the Spirit Container he set up in the Heartlands. A soul that powerful could only belong to one person. Elder Roland doesn't need to come. There's no question. This is Zane Lowell.”

The warriors gathered here were the best of the best, the saint-level geniuses from the union of seven divine kingdoms. They were all First and Second Tier talents; you rarely saw a Third Tier fighter in a crowd like this. Most of them had a good shot at reaching the Soul Sea Realm one day, but for now, it was just a distant possibility. Still, that didn’t stop them from being arrogant. After all, they were the top fighters in the entire union.

But right now, they were completely stunned. It wasn’t just Zane’s incredible power that shocked them. There were plenty of ways to measure a fighter’s strength; the soul was just one part of the equation.

Even so, just knowing his soul was that powerful was enough to make them burn with jealousy. They couldn’t believe what they were seeing and hearing.

Similarly, the crown princes and princes of the four kingdoms couldn't just stay quiet about it.

“It's only his soul that's strong. He must have stumbled onto some freak opportunity. I doubt the rest of him could withstand a single real blow.”

“I have soul-protecting treasures from my father. His soul strength means nothing to me.”

“Let's see if he can even beat Logan first. Then maybe I'll consider going down there myself.”

They told themselves whatever they needed to. It was the only way to feel better.

[SFX: MURMURS]

The three masters from the Crystal Martial House, all at the Soul Sea Realm, were even more surprised by York’s announcement. They had seen Zane’s strength firsthand and knew what he was capable of. Not only was his understanding of the Laws exceptional, but his physical power was terrifyingly high. Now, finding out his soul was just as potent, they realized Zane was a truly flawless warrior. They couldn’t help but wonder: if he was this strong now, how monstrous would he become once he finally broke through to the Soul Sea Realm?

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The Crown Prince’s eyes were locked on Zane. “A soul close to the Fighting Soul Realm? How is that even possible? Why him? If only I could…” His thoughts trailed off as he replayed the last few moments in his head. York had launched a surprise soul attack, only for Zane’s soul to hold its own, proving just as strong.

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

With that revelation, York’s desire to kill Zane intensified. If he unleashed his full Vitality, Zane surely wouldn't survive. He can’t survive this, he tried to convince himself, though a nagging seed of doubt had already been planted.

Randy opened his eyes. They were shining. He had always known Zane was extraordinary — but this was beyond anything he had imagined. He kept his face still and sent his voice quietly through a thread of Vitality directly to the Emperor.

“Father. York's killing intent is rising. If he moves again with his full strength behind it—”

He didn't finish. He didn't need to.

For the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom, things were different. If a talent this extraordinary hadn’t appeared in his kingdom, that would be one thing. But now that Zane was here, his identity known to all, the Emperor had to protect him, no matter the cost.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

With a flash of movement, the Emperor materialized between Zane and York. After a tense silence, he spoke, his voice calm but firm.

“York, that’s enough. You’re a king. Don’t you think attacking a kid like this is beneath you? It doesn’t matter what name he goes by—he is a member of the Harlen Divine Kingdom.”

His expression was resolute, his meaning crystal clear: whether Zane had killed the prince of the York Divine Kingdom or not was irrelevant. He was going to protect him, no matter what.
