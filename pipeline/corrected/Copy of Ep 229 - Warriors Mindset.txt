Ep 229 - Warrior's Mindset
Word Count: 1146
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN TRAINING GROUND]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CULTIVATION]
Ben, an inner disciple from Misty Peak in the Skyreach Sect, tagged along with a few other disciples to watch the trials for the outer disciples.
He wasn't too worried. At Level Seven of the Adept Realm, he was head and shoulders above the eighth-ranked inner disciple on Misty Peak.
A trial like this was basically a warm-up for them. Even the magic array itself was just a regular training ground for Misty's inner disciples.
Ben's personal best was a solid eighteen thousand, while the eighth-place guy had only ever scraped together fifteen thousand. It was a massive gap, and Ben figured he wasn't in any danger of being caught.
But lately, he'd been hearing a rumor: an outer disciple named Zane Lowell was supposedly going to represent Misty Peak in the All Peaks Competition.
Only the top seven disciples from each peak got to go, and the spots were determined by rank. If Zane somehow made the cut, Ben would be kicked out of his spot.
At first, Ben just blew it off. No way could an outer disciple be stronger than an inner one. But then, after a training mission fighting Demons, another inner disciple, Cliff Lang, swore that Zane was some kind of monster who had even taken down demon generals.
Now, Cliff was a solid, well-ranked inner disciple, but he wasn't in the top ten. What he'd said could have been an exaggeration---just some overblown intel he was passing along.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT QUICKENING]
Still, a knot of anxiety tightened in Ben's stomach. This outer disciple had taken down demon generals, something Ben wasn't sure he could pull off on his own.
Based on that alone, Zane could probably snag a spot in the top seven.
A warrior's mindset was everything, especially after a loss. If they were small-minded, a defeat wouldn't inspire them to get stronger. It would just become a mental block, a ceiling they could never break through.
[SFX: ANNOUNCEMENT BELL]
Each disciple was in and out in about five minutes, though a few took closer to ten.
As the trial wore on, the scores just kept climbing.
So far, the thirteenth-ranked disciple held the high score with three thousand, one hundred points.
That score was enough to qualify for an inner disciple spot. However, his future now depended on how the remaining twelve disciples performed. If three of them scored below three thousand, he'd be in. But the odds were slim.
The thirteenth disciple's success just went to show that the overall skill at Misty Peak was on the rise. The competition was getting cutthroat.
When the twelfth, eleventh, and tenth disciples finished their trials, it was announced that all of them had broken three thousand points.
The thirteenth-place disciple's heart sank. The higher-ranked disciples weren't going to score lower than him. It meant he was out, even with a score over three thousand.
[MUSIC: CONFIDENT SWAGGER]
When Dustin Blackwood emerged from the white fog, a self-satisfied smirk was plastered across his face. He was clearly happy with his performance.
[SFX: CROWD REACTION]
The instructor then boomed, "Dustin Blackwood, four thousand, thirty-three points!"
He was the first to break the four-thousand mark.
From there, the rest of the trial held no real suspense. The only question left was how high the top scores would climb. These outer disciples were the best of the best; as long as they didn't choke, they were all guaranteed to become inner disciples.
[MUSIC: ANTICIPATION BUILDS]
After the instructor announced Henry's score of five thousand, five hundred, it was finally Zane's turn.
[AMBIENT: ANXIOUS CROWD]
Every eye in the place was locked on Zane.
The crowd was buzzing, expecting him to blow everyone else out of the water---maybe even double Henry's score.
A few disciples shot a look over at Ben. They'd all heard the rumor about Zane aiming for a spot in the top seven. But they also knew Ben's personal best was a staggering eighteen thousand, a score Zane had no chance of touching.
"So, Ben, getting nervous?" an inner disciple next to him drawled.
Ben glanced over. It was the second-ranked inner disciple. The smug, gloating look on his face made Ben's blood boil. If Zane actually managed to snag the highest score among the inner disciples, Ben would be the one getting booted from the competition.
Heat flooded Ben's face. He was being made into a laughingstock. The second-ranked disciple was at Level Nine of the Adept Realm, practically at the peak. Ben was so far beneath him in rank that all he could do was grit his teeth and take it.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING ARENA]
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
Inside the trial, Zane knew he had to go all out.
[SFX: WHITE FOG RUSHING IN]
The white mist closed around him. A moment later, figures materialized — dozens of them, then more. Half-step Adepts, by the look of them. Zane almost laughed.
He raised Streamer and swept it in a wide arc. The Sword Qi fanned out, and the figures dissolved into fog almost before they'd taken a step. Dozens gone in seconds. Then a hundred.
"That's it?" he murmured.
The fog thickened. New figures emerged — second-tier Adepts this time, moving faster, striking back in the gaps between his swings. None of them could break through his defenses, but clearing them took longer. He worked through them methodically, the Streamer a constant blur.
When the last one dissolved, he rolled his shoulder and waited.
The third wave came.
[SFX: LOUD IMPACT]
The moment Zane swung at the nearest figure, it effortlessly parried his blade. The block caught him completely off guard.
"These sixth-level Adept figures are amazing," he thought. "Way stronger. But do they really think they can handle me?"
[SFX: METAL SCRAPING METAL]
A cold smirk played on Zane's lips. He swung the Streamer hard. A high-pitched shriek of metal scraping metal tore through the air as his blade met one of the figure's weapons. He sliced clean through both the weapon and the warrior in one fluid motion, and the figure dissolved back into white fog.
But as soon as Zane took one down, four more charged him. He couldn't take them all on at once; he needed a strategy.
He sidestepped, narrowly dodging their coordinated attack. In the same breath, he launched his counter, moving like a lone, deadly snowflake caught in a blizzard.
"Flick the sword!" Zane yelled.
It was one of the most devastating moves in basic swordsmanship. His sword hand pulled back as he raised his right foot, his entire body coiling like a spring.
[SFX: FOOT STOMP]
Then, he stomped his right foot hard on the ground.
Channeling the explosive force from the stomp, Zane's body became an unstoppable force, a towering mountain crashing down on the four figures like a massive boulder breaking free from a cliff.
