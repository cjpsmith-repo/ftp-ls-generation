[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Zane's fingers blanched against the rough edge of his cot, tension radiating through each digit.
"Elara sent to Infernal Peak." The phrase echoed in his consciousness like an executioner's final pronouncement.
He had endured the humiliation of enslavement. He had survived becoming a living target for Lowell children's combat practice. Even the theft of the Aether Crystal registered merely as one more possession wrenched from his grasp.
But his sister—she represented the vulnerability he couldn't protect, the final thread of his unraveling world that, once cut, would leave him with nothing.
She was a prodigy, chosen by the Skyreach Sect as a disciple at just thirteen—the only reason she'd escaped the family coup.
For two years, Zane had been trapped here, with no way of knowing what had happened to her. But now, Perrin's vague threat about her had a knot of pure anxiety tightening in his gut.
[SFX: IMPACT SOUNDS]
[SFX: GRUNTS OF PAIN]
Lost in thought, Zane left himself wide open. He took several hard shots from the Lowell kids before the sharp sting of a few more finally jolted him back to the present.
[MUSIC: SOMBER UNDERTONE]
[SFX: LIMPING FOOTSTEPS]
[AMBIENT: QUIET CELLAR]
That evening, Zane limped back to the cellar, his entire body screaming.
[SFX: PAPER HITTING FLOOR]
"This'll help you heal," House Lowell steward said, tossing a small paper packet at Zane's feet before turning to leave.
After all, living punching bags weren't made of steel. Without some kind of healing salve, they'd die from internal injuries within days.
So the steward handed out just enough medicine to get them back on their feet. But it was always the cheapest stuff, barely enough to do the job.
[SFX: PAPER TEARING]
Zane tore open the packet. His eyes widened. Just one Vita Shard.
[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL]
"Darren!" Zane called out, his voice sharp. "Why is there only one Vita Shard in here?"
"You should be grateful you're getting anything," the steward sneered. "What's the problem? Not enough for you?"
"The rule is three Vita Shards a day for every servant," Zane shot back, his eyes glinting. "There's only one here. You're skimming off the top. Stealing from House Lowell is a serious crime. You got a death wish, Darren?"
"Hey! I'm afraid of dying, sure, but I'm not afraid of you," Darren spat, loving the chance to rub salt in the wound. He had never liked Zane. "You're just a servant. What are you gonna do, start a rebellion? I can't stand that look on your face. You still think you're the young master? Take a good look in the mirror, Zane. This is your reality now!"
Zane's anger didn't flare at the insult. It froze. All the heat vanished, leaving him eerily calm. He straightened up, his eyes locking onto Darren's with an ice-cold hatred that made his expression go completely blank.
It worked. Darren shifted, a flicker of unease in his eyes. Seeing the fire banked behind Zane's cold stare, he felt a prickle of fear. But instead of backing down, Darren puffed out his chest and jabbed a finger hard into Zane's shoulder. "What are you lookin' at? You want a piece of me? You think you can take me?"
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
Suddenly, an unseen force erupted from Zane's chest. Darren's body convulsed as if struck by lightning. His knees gave out, and he collapsed, scrambling backward on the stone floor.
[SFX: BODY HITTING FLOOR]
"You—you're just a servant! Are you trying to start a revolt?" Darren shrieked, scrambling to his feet. He was trying for a tone of command, but the raw panic in his eyes betrayed him.
[SFX: KNUCKLES CRACKING]
Zane took two slow steps forward, cracking his knuckles. "A steward who isn't even a Lowell dares to act so high and mighty? You really think I won't put you down?"
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS RUNNING]
[SFX: IRON GATE SLAM]
Darren wanted no part of this. He spun around and scrambled out of the cellar, shooting one last, hateful glare at Zane just before the heavy iron gate slammed shut.
"Freakin' psycho!" his voice echoed from the hall.
"Psycho?" Zane sneered, shaking his head. The steward wasn't even worth his time.
[SFX: FLINT STRIKING]
[AMBIENT: QUIET CELLAR]
He sat down, using a flint to light the oil lamp before numbly flipping through a book. But it was no use. He couldn't focus. His mind kept circling back to his sister, Elara, and the threat in Perrin's voice.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATION]
Zane frowned, the weight of his dilemma crushing him. He couldn't stay here. He'd only just reached the Strength Level; getting out of this cellar would be hard enough, let alone reaching the Skyreach Sect to find Elara. He began to pace the tiny cellar, his chest tight, his heart pounding in his ears. He was a caged animal, frantic for a way out.
For two years, Zane had resigned himself to his fate, never mustering the will to find another way. Time had stripped him of his talent, his confidence, and eventually, his belief in himself.
He stood in the center of the room, a slave. Years of being a human punching bag had left his body weak, and the daily beatings made it impossible to practice his cultivation when he returned to the cellar. If he stayed, one of the Lowell kids would eventually kill him.
He had to do something.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
The more he thought, the more the walls of the cellar seemed to close in. His frantic gaze landed on the book sitting on the table: the Codex of Ascension.
"A book full of useless theories on life," he muttered to himself. "Didn't work for my father, and it sure as hell hasn't worked for me. What's the point of even keeping it?"
[SFX: PAPER BURNING]
A tidal wave of anger and helplessness washed over him. He stomped over, snatched the book from the table, and thrust it into the oil lamp's flame. A smile touched his lips as the old paper sizzled, curled, and caught fire.
But the moment the flames took hold, a wave of regret crashed over him. "You idiot," he whispered in despair. "Why get angry at a book?"
He stared, gutted, as the pages turned to ash. 'It just teaches you how to be a good person,' he thought, the irony twisting in his stomach. 'What's right, what's wrong. You can't blame a book for your own weakness. You're the one who's a lamb for the slaughter.'
[SFX: FIRE DYING DOWN]
By the time the fire died down, the book was nothing but a pile of ash.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
But then, a faint golden light pulsed from within the ashes. For a second, Zane just stared, speechless.
"What is that?"
He carefully brushed the soot aside and picked up the small, shimmering piece of gold that was emitting the light.
Zane studied the thin gold foil. He'd read the book cover to cover and had never seen it before. It must have been hidden in the binding. He wondered what good a sliver of gold foil was. For a family as powerful as House Lowell, gold was worthless.
And yet, when Zane focused his gaze on it, a strange vision flooded his mind.
The foil was covered in countless tiny words he couldn't decipher.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TRANSFORMATION]
The foil dissolved in his hands, shattering into thousands of shimmering motes of light—each one a single, golden word. They surged toward him in a silent swarm, blanketing his face, his eyes, his neck, his arms—every inch of his body.
The last mote drove itself directly into his brain. Zane's whole body seized, as if struck by a hammer, and for a single, terrible instant the golden light blazed across every inch of him at once.
[SFX: MAGICAL BURNING]
Zane tried to scream but couldn't. The golden characters sank beneath his skin, burning like liquid fire as they disappeared into his flesh. His veins illuminated from within, tracing golden patterns across his body.
[MUSIC: ANCIENT POWER AWAKENING]
In his mind, a voice whispered: "The true Codex reveals itself only to the worthy."
And then, for the first time in two years, Zane felt power—ancient and terrible—stirring within him.
