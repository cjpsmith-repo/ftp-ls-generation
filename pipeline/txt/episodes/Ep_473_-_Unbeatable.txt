As three giant obsidian crabs lumbered into range, the archers on top of Zane's small tower drew their bowstrings. The synchronized motion was eerily precise. They fired, loosing twelve sharp arrows that sliced through the sky, all aimed at the massive creatures.

The archers were identical, from their stoic expressions to the color of their eyes—a pale, lifeless grey that reminded Zane of polished stone. It was no surprise; they were constructs, all born from the same magic array.

[SFX: ARROWS WHOOSHING]

The arrows blazed with different colors as they flew—crimson, azure, emerald, gold—a shimmering rainbow streaking across the sky before arcing down toward the crabs,

[SFX: ARROW IMPACT CRACK]

A sickening crack echoed as the first arrow found its mark, punching through the obsidian shell with devastating force. The impact was so visceral, Zane flinched as if the arrow had pierced his own flesh. He could almost feel the phantom pain radiating through his chest

The combined power of that volley was comparable to an attack from a master at the first level of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. They were absolutely lethal—far beyond anything Zane could have achieved on his own at his current cultivation level.

[SFX: MONSTER SHRIEK]

[SFX: COLLAPSE]

The giant crab shrieked, a piercing cry of pain that reverberated across the battlefield. Its massive legs buckled beneath it as it collapsed into a helpless, wounded heap on the ground.

[MUSIC: WONDEROUS DISCOVERY]

Wow, Zane thought, stunned into silence. One volley. That's all it took. The jump in power from level eleven to twelve was staggering. Then again, discovering and exploiting that loophole in the array's design had made things a whole lot easier.

It was a lucky break—an advantage the other cultivators, who were struggling desperately against their own giant crabs, clearly didn't have.

[AMBIENT: OBSERVATION HALL]

In the entire history of the magic array, Zane had become the first to ever achieve something like this. The examiner watched, his expression unreadable as he studied the young cultivator below. Was Zane an anomaly—the first and last to ever accomplish such a feat? Had they just witnessed the emergence of someone truly exceptional?

Round after round, more cultivators were eliminated. Arrows flew in deadly volleys. Beasts fell, their massive bodies dissolving into mist. The air grew thick with tension as each successive wave culled the competition further. By the tenth round of attacks, only twenty-two cultivators remained, their faces etched with exhaustion and determination. The reality was stark: only three would advance to the main tournament.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

With the round complete, the examiner raised his hands, manipulating the array. "On to the next level!" he announced.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

All eyes were on Zane. Would he pass? Tia was a nervous wreck, her eyes glued to the Scrying Slate where the final, decisive moments of the qualifying test played out. Zane's performance would determine everything. He'd upgraded his tower to level twelve, a feat no other cultivator had come close to. The others who had managed to defeat the giant crabs and earn points had only reached level ten. Tia was in awe; the archers in that level twelve tower were as powerful as masters from the first level of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. The best of the best.

Based on that alone, Zane should have no trouble passing.

Zane, for his part, was completely relaxed. He had this in the bag.

[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSITION WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: EERIE VOID SILENCE]

Suddenly, the light around him faded. The bright sky of the magic array bled into blackness. The grassy meadow dissolved into nothingness, plunging Zane into total darkness.

"The second level," Zane muttered to himself.

Everything vanished except for one thing: his tower. Zane stood frozen as it floated alone in a sea of pure darkness.

[SFX: MYSTICAL MATERIALIZATION]

Then, one by one, other towers materialized in the void, each separated by a stretch of empty space.

Zane’s confidence didn't waver. There was no way he was failing this test. So this is the second part, huh? He counted them. Twenty-one towers in total, including his own. He was looking at the other survivors, each standing on their own platform.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

"What's with that guy? His tower is huge!"

"How is that possible? Did he cheat? All our towers are level ten. Why is his twice the size of mine?"

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMURS]

"It has to be level eleven, or even twelve! But how could he get extra points? How did he upgrade so high?"

Whispers turned to murmurs as the other cultivators stared at Zane's tower. When they saw its sheer size, they knew it wasn't just on another level—it was a fortress. Nervous glances shot between them, their eyes darting from their own hard-won towers to Zane's. They had risked everything for their upgrades.

Seeing his tower felt like a slap in the face. It felt like he'd cheated.

"What the hell?" one of them finally spat out.

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

Unconcerned, Zane simply waved his hand. Instantly, his twelve archers raised their bows, acting on his command like soldiers on the front line. He was here to win. There was no time for regret, only for the fight.

A master in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm could easily defeat countless cultivators from the Inner-Core Realm. By that logic, Zane's tower shouldn't just withstand their attacks; it should crush them. The archers in the level ten towers were only as strong as mid-level Inner-Core cultivators. Zane was in a different league. He could already taste victory.

[SFX: ARROWS WHISTLING THROUGH AIR]

With a sharp hiss, a dozen powerful arrows screamed through the air.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE DESTRUCTION]

They flew true, striking the other cultivators' towers one by one. In the blink of an eye, the flimsy structures were obliterated, reduced to nothing but drifting ash.

[AMBIENT: DEAD SILENCE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

A dead, horrified silence fell over the survivors. Watching the towers disintegrate made the threat terrifyingly real. They finally understood. In this magic array, Zane was king. He was unbeatable.
