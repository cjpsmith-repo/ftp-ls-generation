Ep 1298 - Walk On By

Word count: 1075

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL CHAMBER ECHOES]
[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACK]
A crack of lightning, sudden as a summer storm, tore through the space, and the hall began to put itself back together.
[SFX: STONE FRAGMENTS FLYING]
[SFX: STONES SLOTTING INTO PLACE]
Shards of stone erupted from the pillars, the pieces seeming to know exactly where they belonged as they flew into place, slotting together with impossible precision.
[SFX: MASSIVE STRUCTURE MATERIALIZING]
A roof, beams, eaves, walls, doors, and floors all materialized out of the chaos, as if an invisible force were building a grand hall right before their eyes. In a matter of moments, a magnificent, circular chamber stood before them, with five distinct corridors branching out from it.
“Five paths?”
“So, what, we’re supposed to split up?”
“I thought this was about teamwork.”
No one knew what to make of the new layout, and they all exchanged confused looks. But they understood the implication. If they wanted to get through this place, they had to play by its rules. If the hall wanted to separate them, they had no choice but to go along with it.
[SFX: MAGICAL HUM RISING]
A low hum filled the air as the doorways to each corridor began to glow with a shiny blue light.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SWIRLING]
The light swirled and coalesced, forming legible words over each archway. Each door now bore the name of one of the warriors.
Janet, Hailey, Coleman, Ansel, and Zane Lowell.
“Zane Lowell?!”
Every head whipped around to stare at Zane.
Only now did it click for Janet — the “Jones” he’d given them had been the fake part. She’d sensed the lie back when they first met and brushed it off as nothing. Now she understood exactly why he’d hidden his real surname.
“I knew it!” Janet’s eyes lit up, a huge grin spreading across her face.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT REVELATION]
“I told you there was something more to you. I just didn’t realize you were the Zane Lowell!”
To the younger generation, Zane was a living legend. It wasn’t even about his strength; his story had simply spread to every corner of the known worlds. People had heard the tales so many times they could recount every detail by heart. Even the most isolated warriors living out in the middle of nowhere had heard of him.
[AMBIENT: COSMIC BROADCAST HUM]
After all, for the last six years, someone had been using a Roaring Token to broadcast his story across the universe. You couldn’t avoid it, whether you wanted to listen or not. But the stories were so gripping that everyone got hooked, waiting months on end for the next installment after the storyteller always left them hanging with the same line: “And as for what happened next… you’ll have to find out next time.”
After he took down the four Supreme Lords, this was how Zane had spent his time. Trillions of lives across the universe had been captivated by the tales of his adventures.
Janet, Coleman, Hailey, and the rest were no exception.
“Are… are you really Zane Lowell?”
“Yes,” Zane said. “I’m Zane Lowell.”
Coleman let out a bark of laughter and clapped Zane on the shoulder. “Hah! You’ve been killing me for years, you know that?”
“How exactly did I kill you?” Zane asked, genuinely confused.
Coleman explained — he’d spent long stretches in seclusion, trying to master a meditation technique that demanded total stillness of the six senses. No distractions allowed. Unfortunately, Zane’s broadcasts didn’t care about that. Every time the story picked back up in his ears, his focus shattered along with it. It was a miracle he hadn’t gone half-mad from it — though his progress had definitely suffered for it.
“Same here,” Ansel chimed in, nodding along. “I’ve lost count of how many times that voice pulled me right out of my cultivation.”
Hailey only smiled and said nothing.
Questions came pouring in after that — about the Roaring Token, about the immortal mansion, about anything and everything. Zane answered what he could and glossed over what he couldn’t, patient but unhurried.
Finally, he raised a hand toward the five corridors behind him. “I think we should get back to the matter at hand.”
Janet stuck her tongue out, sheepish. “Fine, fine — I’m a little too curious for my own good. I knew you’d made it into the Illusion Battlefield too, but I never expected to actually run into you here.”
“Right now, the Hall matters more than I do,” Zane said. “Let’s see if these corridors really are locked to us the way they look. I’ll try mine first.”
Zane took a step to his left, stopping in front of the next corridor over. Janet’s name was written above it, marking it as her path.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER REPULSION]
The moment he tried to step into it, however, an irresistible force blocked his way.
Zane didn’t fight it. He understood. This was the hall’s game, and if it wouldn’t let him in, he wasn’t going to force his way through.
[MUSIC: SOLEMN DEPARTURE]
[AMBIENT: MULTIPLE CORRIDOR ECHOES]
The reality of the situation settled over the group: they would have to finish the rest of this journey on their own. After a moment of silent preparation, they each turned to their designated corridors.
Ansel looked the most dejected of them all. He knew he hadn’t really pulled his weight on the journey so far. He’d just been a tagalong, relying on everyone else to get by. From here on out, he was on his own, and he wasn’t sure he could handle it.
The anxiety was written plainly on his face as he stepped into his corridor with a long, drawn-out sigh.
[AMBIENT: VAST EMPTY CORRIDOR]
Zane gave the long, dark corridor ahead a final glance before stepping inside.
It was incredibly wide, easily broad enough for a carriage to race through. The carpet was so soft underfoot it was almost deceptive, lulling him into thinking this path might not be so bad.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STOP]
After walking a few dozen feet, Zane stopped.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
[SFX: TORCH CRACKLING]
A single torch hung on the wall ahead, its flame burning brightly and illuminating the passage. He could feel a strange power radiating from it.
“There’s a powerful soul in that flame,” Zane muttered to himself, his eyes fixed on the torch. He had no way of knowing how strong it was, but if he could feel its pressure on his own soul from this distance, its true power had to be something else entirely.
Is this the test? he wondered. Am I supposed to do something about it, or just ignore it?
He decided to just walk on by.
[MUSIC: INTENSE DANGER]
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE PRESSURE WAVE]
But the moment he drew level with the torch, a crushing pressure exploded from the flame, wrapping around him completely.
