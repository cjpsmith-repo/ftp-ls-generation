Ep 94 - Walls of Power
Word count: 1222
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD AFTERMATH]
The second Isla showed up, Adrian was gone.
It wasn't that he was scared of her power; he was just wary of who she was and the family she came from. The other guys scrambled to help Perrin to his feet before rushing after Adrian.
[SFX: SKYCRUISER ENGINE POWERING DOWN]
"What was that about, making a promise like that to him?" Isla asked, hopping down from her Skycruiser and striding toward Zane.
Zane was fiercely protective when it came to anything involving Elara. He considered Isla's question for a moment before answering, "I don't want a guy like Adrian anywhere near Elara."
"You know he's not just some top disciple, right? He's the only son of the head of House Warrick. The entire sect values him. Is he really that bad?" Isla asked, a confused line creasing her brow.
"Yeah," Zane shot back without a second's hesitation. "Really bad."
Isla offered a small, knowing smile. "You might be the only person who thinks so. Adrian is the guy every girl in the Capital wants to marry."
[MUSIC: TENSE DETERMINATION]
"He's not good enough for my sister," Zane said, his voice turning to ice. "He can marry whoever he wants, but he's staying away from Elara."
Isla's breath caught when she saw the raw determination on his face. She'd completely misread him. She had no idea he was Elara's brother, or that he'd fought so hard for those Points just to get her off Infernal Peak.
His plan was naive, but Isla couldn't help but sigh. That kind of devotion between siblings was rare. In her eyes, Zane was just too innocent, too blind when it came to his sister.
"Isla, I'm going up the mountain to see Elara," Zane announced, already turning toward the path that led to the summit.
Isla shook her head emphatically, her expression softening with reluctance. "You can't see her, not yet."
[MUSIC: TENSE DETERMINATION]
"Why?" Zane demanded, his eyes narrowing to slits as his hands curled into fists at his sides. The familiar weight of disappointment settled in his chest. First they'd told him he needed Points to visit anyone imprisoned on Infernal Peak. Then Adrian had crushed his hopes by declaring him ineligible altogether. And now this princess stood before him, becoming yet another barrier between him and Elara.
"Why the hell is everyone blocking me?" he raged internally, a storm of frustration threatening to overwhelm his composure. His jaw clenched so tightly it ached. "I've finally made it all the way to Infernal Peak after everything I've been through. What more do they want from me? Why can't I just see my sister?"
"The individuals confined at Infernal Peak's summit aren't ordinary prisoners, Zane," Isla explained, her voice gentling as she noticed the desperation etched across his features. She tucked a strand of hair behind her ear, choosing her words carefully.
"This isn't simply about status or Points. You can't merely stroll up there as an Outer Disciple, regardless of your determination." She gestured toward the imposing peak looming above them. "There's an ancient Runic Power Circle sealing the entire summit—a barrier designed to contain those within and repel those without. Someone at your current level of cultivation would be incinerated attempting to breach it. Even I can't enter without proper authorization and preparation. You need special permission from those who control the seals."
Zane's face fell. Everything he had done—escaping House Lowell, joining the Skyreach Sect, hunting the Razor Swarm—it was all for this. After all that effort, all the challenges he'd clawed his way through, the idea that he still couldn't see his sister was unbearable.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON ROCKY PATH]
Filled with a new resolve, Zane started walking up the mountain anyway.
But he'd forgotten how busted up his body was, and before he got more than a few steps, Isla was in front of him, pushing a pill into his mouth.
Before he could protest, Isla swept him onto the Skycruiser with a gesture and took them up toward the summit. About a hundred feet from the peak, a wall of blue light sealed the mountain — the Runic Power Circle, impenetrable and absolute.
Zane gripped the railing and searched the summit. His eyes, sharpened by the Marrow Refining Level, found her.
Elara stood at the edge of a stone platform, straight-backed, a pillar of purple light rising from her body. Alive. Unbroken.
They looked at each other across the distance.
Zane stood up and made a single hand gesture.
Isla didn't understand it. But Elara smiled.
It was something she'd seen him do since they were children. It meant: I will protect you.
"Let's go," Zane said quietly.
Only after the Skycruiser had descended far enough that Elara could no longer see them did Zane allow himself to sink down onto the deck.
[SFX: BODY HITTING DECK — EXHAUSTED]
"Don't worry. My investment in you will pay off eventually. With interest," Isla Novak remarked, her tone resolute yet accompanied by a subtle smile that softened her aristocratic features. "So, as your creditor, you'd be wise to heed my advice." She brushed a speck of dust from her immaculate sleeve before turning gracefully toward her Skycruiser, her movements betraying the refined upbringing of Imperial nobility.
[SFX: PILL SWALLOWING]
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
[MUSIC: DREAMY FADING]
The moment the Crimson Haze Pill slid down his throat, Zane gasped as an inferno erupted in his stomach. The initial burning sensation quickly transformed into a pleasant warmth that cascaded through his battered body, seeking out each laceration and bruise with methodical precision. He marveled as the Level 5 potion demonstrated its remarkable potency; gashes that should have taken weeks to heal began closing before his very eyes, muscle and skin knitting together with astonishing speed.
A profound drowsiness washed over him as the healing energies coursed through his veins. Zane fought against the encroaching darkness, struggling to maintain consciousness as his eyelids grew increasingly leaden. His last fleeting thought before succumbing was of Elara, her face swimming in his fading vision as the world dissolved into velvet blackness. His body, finally surrendering to the potion's powerful sedative effect, collapsed into a deep, restorative slumber.
It was probably the best sleep he'd had in years.
[AMBIENT: BRIGHT MORNING]
[SFX: WAKING UP SOUNDS]
Back when he was a prisoner in the Lowell family's basement, Zane forced himself awake before dawn every single morning to study. But when he woke up today, the sun was already high in the sky.
"Wow, I really slept in," Zane mumbled in surprise, stretching and taking stock of his body.
Every trace of his confrontation with Adrian on Infernal Peak had vanished. The Crimson Haze Pill had proven itself far more potent than mere healer's tales suggested. Invigorated beyond expectation, Zane sprang to his feet with renewed purpose. Time was a luxury he could no longer squander.
[AMBIENT: MEDITATIVE ATMOSPHERE]
Settling into a cross-legged position, he began channeling his power through the Titan Strike technique. The ancient tome he'd borrowed from the library had initially frustrated his every attempt at mastering its Advancement method. Countless hours of practice had yielded nothing but disappointment—until yesterday, when Adrian's overwhelming force had pushed him beyond his limits.
Zane's eyes snapped open, his pupils dilating with anticipation. The question that now consumed his thoughts hung in the air like a blade suspended by a thread: could he summon that dark, forbidden Corrupt Vitality again? Or had that mysterious power, which had saved his life and terrified even a Soul Realm cultivator, abandoned him forever?
