Ep 166 - True Vitality
Word Count: 1155

[AMBIENT: TENSE STANDOFF]
Zane stared at the crowd, which looked at him with murderous anger. 
The Soul Realm masters were the backbone of the Seven Great Houses. If they’d all been killed in the Elysian Keep, it would have been a stain on their honor they could never wash away.
"I don't have to prove a damn thing. Find out for yourselves—it's not my problem," Zane said, his voice sharp with anger. "I'm leaving. Anyone who gets in my way dies."
He turned to go, but a shout cut through the tension. "You're not going anywhere until you explain what happened to my family! I won't let you leave here alive!"
[SFX: ENERGY MANIFESTATION]
An Adept darted forward with remarkable speed, positioning himself directly in Zane's path. His eyes blazed with righteous fury as he channeled his energy, manipulating his Vitality with practiced precision. 
The emerald aura around his hands coalesced into a small but ferocious dragon, its scales gleaming. The creature, an extension of the Adept's will and power, lunged toward Zane, its crystalline jaws stretched impossibly wide to devour him. The air crackled with tension as the manifestation of the Adept's rage hurtled forward, leaving a trail of shimmering green particles in its wake.
[SFX: ANGRY ROAR]
 "Get out of my way!" Zane roared, unleashing a blast of Corrupt Vitality from his palms.
The Corrupt Vitality shattered the small green dragon in mid-air. Zane charged the Adept, his own hand already shimmering with a constellation of tiny, sparkling stars.
Zane didn't even bother with his Titan Strike. He just wrapped his hand in raw power, fueled by his Corrupt Vitality.
[SFX: IMPACT STRIKE]
 The moment his hand struck the Adept, the sparkling stars erupted.
[SFX: MAGICAL EXPLOSIONS]
 Each star, no bigger than a grain of rice, detonated with a force so massive it stunned the other Adepts. The explosions cascaded, one after another, until they faded into nothing.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS POWER]
As the brilliance faded, the Adept had simply vanished—not defeated, not wounded, but utterly obliterated from existence.
The Soul Realm masters stood frozen in stunned silence, a cold, visceral awe creeping through their veins. Their collective breath caught as the implications sank in. To wipe out an opponent with a single strike transcended their understanding of power. Not one among them, despite their vaunted status, could unleash such devastating force with a mere touch.
Terror rippled through the remaining Adepts like a physical wave. Their faces blanched, draining of all color as sweat beaded on trembling brows. 
"He was barely a half-step Adept when he went into the Elysian Keep," someone whispered, voice cracking with disbelief. The speaker's fingers clutched his robes, knuckles white with tension. "But he killed Leon without effort. Look at him now—he's converting Raw Vitality into true Vitality right in front of us. His power is growing at a terrifying rate. He's a full Adept now… we're no match for him."
Zane swept his gaze across the assembly, his eyes reflecting the cold calculation of a predator assessing prey. The air around him seemed to shimmer with energy.
"Anyone else want to try me?" he asked, his voice soft yet carrying to every corner of the courtyard, each syllable sharp as a blade against the silence.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
 The courtyard went dead silent. Not a single Adept dared to move. Then, after a tense moment, a young man stepped forward.
"You need to explain exactly what happened in the Elysian Keep," he said to Zane.
"And what if I don't want to?" Zane shot back, his eyes burning with fury.
The sheer rage in Zane's eyes made the young man flinch, and he took an involuntary step back. He steadied himself before continuing. "If you won't explain, then you will compensate the Seven Great Houses for our losses."
[SFX: HARSH LAUGHTER]
 Zane threw his head back and laughed, a harsh, barking sound. He took two steps forward; the young man took two steps back. 
"Go home," Zane said. "Tell your elders I'm going to eradicate these Great Houses one by one. Then decide if I'm arrogant." *This has to be the young master of the Price Family,* he thought.
He walked. He had somewhere to be and no way to get there fast enough on foot. He tried three stables before giving up — nothing but ordinary horses, useless for the pace he needed. A stableman caught him on his way out. "I know who has what you're looking for," the man said. "The Price family. Young master rides it through the streets. Fast as lightning." He paused. "He's not easy to deal with."
Zane gave him a coin and headed for the Price family gate.
[AMBIENT: STREET COMMOTION]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL]
 The bystanders lining the street gasped, bracing for the worst. They could already picture the horse flipping the cart, trampling the old man and children beneath its hooves.
[SFX: HORSE HOOVES STOPPING ABRUPTLY]
 Just as the horse was about to crash into the cart, Zane moved. He leaped between them, grabbing the stallion’s forelegs and stopping it dead in its tracks.
[AMBIENT: BUSY STREET]
[SFX: HORSE NEIGHING]
 "This is no place for you! Get the cart clear, now!" Zane told the old man, his voice firm but calm as he held the thrashing horse’s forelegs off the ground.
The old man, momentarily stunned by Zane’s sudden appearance, stared with wide, panicked eyes. He snapped out of it a second later, scrambling to pull the cart to safety.
Struggling to stay on his mount, the young man shouted, "You bastard, what do you think you're doing? Let go of my horse!"
Zane's expression darkened at the curse. He carefully lowered the horse's legs, making sure not to injure the animal.
[SFX: WHIP CRACK]
 "Are you crazy? How dare you stop my horse? You got a death wish?!" As soon as the horse was steady, the young man cracked his whip right at Zane’s face.
Zane snatched the whip out of the air with his fingers and wrapped the end around his hand.
Just then, attendants from the Price Manor gate rushed over, shouting, "Let go of the whip, you fool! How dare you lay a hand on our Young Master? Get on your knees, now, if you want to live!"
[SFX: SHARP YANK]
[SFX: THUD]
 Zane sneered at them. Instead of letting go, he gave the whip a sharp yank, pulling the young man clean off his horse.
The Young Master, so accustomed to deference, looked utterly dumbfounded. He couldn't process that someone had not only dared to cross him but had just made a public fool of him.
[SFX: SWORDS UNSHEATHING]
 The attendants, however, reacted instantly, drawing their blades and leveling them at Zane. 
"You've really done it now," one of them snarled. "If you'd just kneeled, we might've let you off with a hundred lashes. But for that stunt, we're going to cut you to pieces!"
