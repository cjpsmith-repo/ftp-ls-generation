Ep 1074 - The Purification Pool

Word count: 1106

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

The two warriors were reeling.

They both realized it at the same time: the razor-sharp energy humming from the words carved into the rock was just… gone. It had to be Zane. They couldn’t think of anyone else.

But they didn’t dare stop him and demand an explanation—not that he’d likely have one. The kid probably had no idea what he’d just done. So all they could do was watch his retreating back, then turn to stare, dumbfounded, at the giant rock.

Without that energy crackling off them, the characters were just characters. The brushstrokes that had seemed so sharp and powerful a minute ago now looked flat, their edges dull. There was nothing special about it at all. If anything, it was a letdown.

“That kid just sucked the life right out of the words,” Landry grumbled, his expression sour. “What the hell is going on?”

Damian just shook his head, a look of defeat on his face.

[SFX: STONE CRACKING]

Just then, a sharp crack split the air. A fissure spiderwebbed across one of the characters. The two men stared, stunned. This massive rock had stood here for ages without so much as a scratch, and now it was cracking right in front of them. It was bizarre. They looked at each other, a flicker of excitement suddenly lighting up their faces. Out of all the warriors who had come and gone, they were the only two who had stuck it out.

Maybe, just maybe, this was it—the moment that would change their lives.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TRANSITION]

Meanwhile, Zane had no clue that the characters on the rock had fractured the moment he left.

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT COURTYARD]

He arrived at his destination to find a collection of magnificent ancient buildings that looked as if they had stood there for centuries. Yolande was waiting for him, along with about two dozen other people.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

“Yolande, we’re still missing one from The Mist Palace,” an old man in simple cotton clothes said, his face tight with impatience. “It’s been hours. If he doesn’t show up soon, I’m sealing the Purification Pool.”

Passing the Torch was a monumental opportunity for any warrior from the Thirteen Palaces. The fact that someone was holding up the entire event was clearly starting to grate on the old man’s nerves.

Yolande just shrugged. “Just wait.”

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

“My, my, Yolande,” a tall, slim woman in an elegant dress sneered. “Aren’t you special. If this were anyone from any other palace, I imagine the Purification Pool would’ve been sealed hours ago.”

Harvey folded his arms and let a slow smile spread across his face. “Honestly? I wouldn’t hold your breath. That little guy is probably still out there staring at the Supreme Lord Sword’s poem.” He paused for effect. “Could be a few thousand years before he figures it out.”

A ripple of laughter moved through the assembled palace leaders.

“A few thousand years,” one of them repeated, shaking his head. “Yolande, you’re not actually going to make us wait that long, are you?”

“It doesn’t matter if one person is missing,” another added. “Why stake your reputation on this kid? What could he possibly have that’s worth all this?”

The curiosity in the room was genuine — hundreds of warriors from across the Thirteen Palaces had qualified for Passing the Torch, and every palace leader had rushed here from their own supreme world to oversee it. Being held up by a single unknown from the Mist Palace was not something any of them had anticipated. They wanted to see what this kid looked like.

Yolande said nothing. Inwardly, she had high hopes for Zane — but she wasn’t going to say that. She also couldn’t help wondering: had he gotten anything at all from the Supreme Lord Sword’s poem?

“You won’t really make us wait a thousand years, right?” the tall woman said, and the edge in her voice made it clear it wasn’t really a question.

Yolande’s expression didn’t change, but she reached out with a soul transmission toward Zane — only to stop. A figure was already cutting across the sky toward them.

[SFX: WHOOSH OF ARRIVAL]

Zane touched down in front of the group and pressed his hands together toward Yolande. “Sorry for making you wait so long.”

The old man in cotton clothes cut in before Yolande could respond. “Long wait? Humph. You’re not qualified to make us wait this long.”

The other palace leaders studied Zane in silence. Fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm. That was what they were looking at. Eyebrows rose. Was there really no one else in the Mist Palace who could have filled this spot? What was so special about this particular kid?

He slipped into the water and bobbed on the surface, unable to sink.

Yolande spoke quietly. “Go on and sink. The Purification Pool will clear your soul for a while and make your senses several times sharper.”

What Zane didn’t know was that the warriors from the Thirteen Palaces had already turned the pool into a proving ground.

[AMBIENT: UNDERWATER ECHOES]

The water was incredibly buoyant. The pool was only about twenty feet wide and deep, but sinking was already a struggle. If it were any larger, you probably couldn’t sink an entire mountain range in it.

As a result, many warriors took pride in their ability to touch the bottom, using it to show off their strength. It wasn’t easy. Most of them couldn’t make it all the way down and ended up just floating somewhere in the middle. Of course, the Thirteen Palaces had their share of powerhouses. People like Layla, Jasper, and a handful of others had reached the bottom without a problem—in all, more than ten warriors had managed the feat.

Honestly, it didn’t really matter if you sank or not. It was just a little competition the warriors had cooked up among themselves.

“Go on, young man,” one of the palace leaders said to Zane with an encouraging smile. “Give it a try. Let’s see how deep you can go.”

Zane glanced over at Yolande, mouthing silently, Is it important?

She gave a subtle shake of her head.

With that, Zane completely ignored the palace leader who had spoken to him. He just flipped onto his back and floated peacefully in the water, making no effort to sink at all.

[MUSIC: DISAPPOINTED TROMBONE]

A collective frown rippled through the palace leaders, the old man’s smile fading along with the others’.

Had they really waited all this time for this guy? What a disappointment.
