Ep 642 - Zane's Goal
Word count: 1100
[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE]
[SFX: PANICKED FOOTSTEPS]
Everyone scrambled out of there as fast as they could.
Even in the outer circle, a place like this was a death trap. One wrong move and you were dead. A faint green light flared in Zane’s eyes as he backed away, every muscle coiled and ready. The Goliath Bull-Toad’s tongue was lightning-fast, but Zane had managed to put some distance between them, giving himself just enough room to dodge.
But a pounce? If that thing decided to leap, it would be over in an instant. No one would survive.
[SFX: HEAVY SPLASHING]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The toad heaved itself out of the vast pond. It took two hulking steps forward, then froze. Its massive body was a shadow in the darkness, but its two eyes—gleaming orbs the size of small houses—were fixed coldly on him.
"Thank God," Zane breathed, his chest tight. "It's not coming after us." He kept his eyes locked on the shadowy beast as he retreated, only turning to rejoin the others when he was sure he was out of its reach.
That toad had been a fixture in this cave for ages, its location clearly marked on the map. The rumor was that it never left the pond—no one knew how it got there, it had just always been there. The creatures in the Dark Nether Cave were just… different.
Jarrod had been quietly counting heads. When he finished, his face went still. Out of the hundreds who had entered the cave, fewer than fifty humans remained.
"Anyway," one of the men muttered, "we can just wait it out and go back in later."
[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
"I don't want to stay in this damned place a second longer," another shot back. "They say you have a slim chance of making it out of here alive, but from what I've seen, it feels more like a one-hundred-percent chance of dying!"
[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
As the others argued, Fabian exchanged a look with Jarrod and slowly nodded. This trip was already a disaster. They’d barely made it inside and had already hit a wall. The smart play was to cut their losses and head back to the Tower of Sin.
But the final call wasn’t theirs to make. Fabian’s gaze drifted to Zane. He was the one who had the final say.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
Zane must have felt his stare, because a faint smile touched his lips. "If you guys want to go back, I'm not going to stop you. But I'm not leaving. I'm staying here."
Lavender said nothing, but her steady gaze was answer enough. The faint flames flickering in her red pupils told them everything: she was staying with Zane.
Yolande then broke the silence. "I'm not leaving Zane's side. If he's exploring the Dark Nether Cave, then so am I."
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
Zane’s answer put Fabian in an impossible position. To be blunt, every top-level War Lord there—including Fabian himself—was completely expendable compared to Zane. The Violet Heart Sanctuary was counting on him to control the Tower of Sin. They could lose every War Lord on this expedition and just train more, but without Zane? The human race would slide right back to where it started. Hell, the only reason the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary of the Demon Night had even allied with them was because of Zane’s sword spirit, Lavender. When you got down to it, everything came back to Zane. A seventh-grade sanctuary had no business teaming up with a place like Violet Heart, which barely even qualified as sixth-grade.
Fabian wasn’t the only one thinking it; he could see the same conflicted calculations on the faces of the other senior War Lords. But they also knew how insane it would be to press deeper into the cave.
[SFX: DISTANT RUMBLING]
"The Dark Nether Cave is too dangerous right now," Fabian tried, hoping he could still change Zane’s mind. "I think the Tide of Chaos has started. Shouldn't we make the smart choice and pull back for now?"
"Tide of Chaos?" Zane asked, his tone skeptical.
"Yes, but that's just a guess. They say it happens in this cave once every ten years. When it does, the demons get active — the danger level spikes. Most groups cancel their exploration when they suspect it's started." Fabian held Zane's gaze. He wasn't just making conversation. He wanted Zane to think about the people around him.
Zane understood the concern. He'd seen the Bull-Toad. He had no illusions about what this cave was.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
But he also knew his situation. He'd been on the main continent for nearly a year. Every day that passed, something could be going wrong in The Heartlands — something he'd have no way of knowing about until it was too late. If he walked away now, he'd have to wait another full year for the Death Light to weaken again. A year in which he could get stronger, yes — but a year in which anything could happen to the people waiting for him.
He couldn't do it.
"I'm sorry," he said, looking at Fabian steadily. "I know how dangerous this place is. But I can't wait another year."
[AMBIENT: ECHOING CAVE]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS DEPARTING]
Fabian took a long breath. He couldn't leave Zane alone in here — but he couldn't drag people who'd already decided to leave back into danger either. He made his call.
"Jarrod and I are staying," he said, raising his voice so everyone could hear. "The rest of you — head back to the Tower."
[SFX: MULTIPLE FOOTSTEPS DEPARTING]
The relief on their faces was immediate. After a few hushed exchanges, the remaining War Lords and their War Generals turned and headed back toward the entrance, leaving only a handful of the most dedicated — or most desperate — behind.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Once they were gone, Fabian turned back to Zane. "Well, since we've decided to stay, we'll go with you as far as we can. Zane, whatever you came into the Dark Nether Cave to find must be pretty incredible."
The Dark Nether Cave was said to hold countless treasures and opportunities. Even explorers who only stuck to the outer circle had found amazing things. Of course, after years of being picked clean, the prizes in the outer ring were getting harder to find, but lucky adventurers still stumbled upon priceless objects from time to time.
"I don't know," Zane answered vaguely.
A strange look crossed Fabian's face. "You don't know? Well, do you at least have a target?"
"Maybe," Zane said. "If I can, I want to reach the core of the Dark Nether Cave."
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
The words hung in the air. Fabian and Jarrod just stared, their faces masks of disbelief.
What is this kid thinking? The core of the Dark Nether Cave? He's insane.
