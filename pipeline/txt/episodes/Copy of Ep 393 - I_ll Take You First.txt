Ep 393 - I'll Take You First

Word count: 1006

[MUSIC: TENSE NEGOTIATION]

Zane smiled at Tia, satisfaction gleaming in his eyes. "One last thing. I want half the treasures of the Sixth-Tier Sect."

[SFX: ANGRY GASP]

Tia's face flushed crimson with rage, her composure shattering. "Half the treasures? Do you have any idea what that's worth?" Her voice rose sharply, echoing off the furnace walls. "You couldn't even handle it. It's way out of your league!"

She took a step toward him, fists clenched at her sides. The audacity of this boy—this mere Adept Realm cultivator—demanding such an outrageous share made her blood boil.

Cloud Hall was only a fourth-Tier sect, but it still commanded respect throughout The Heartlands. A fifth-Tier sect was considered top-tier, the pinnacle of power most cultivators could even dream of serving.

But a Sixth-Tier sect? If the Blood Fiend Emperor's organization hadn't collapsed, it would have been the most powerful force in the entire region—perhaps in several regions combined. All the other sects, no matter their strength or pride, would have had to bow down before it.

Even with the Blood Fiend Emperor's sect destroyed and its treasures picked clean over the years by scavengers and opportunists, whatever remained hidden was still a legacy beyond Cloud Hall's wildest dreams. The scraps alone could elevate an entire sect.

Clearly, Tia thought Zane had lost his mind with greed.

Zane just chuckled, unfazed by her fury. The sound was light, almost playful, which only seemed to infuriate her more. "Don't you worry about me. I've got a big appetite." He paused, letting the words hang in the superheated air between them. "Of course, you could always say no… and try cracking that barrier on your own."

He gestured casually toward the shimmering enchantment protecting the Soulforge, his expression one of perfect innocence.

[SFX: INTENSE FLAMES]

[SFX: STEAM HISSING]

The Fiery Dragon Spirit roared, slamming the Ice Phoenix to the ground. Its intense heat instantly vaporized the phoenix's icy shell, sending up clouds of steam.

Tia had no choice but to quiet her rage and channel more freezing air into the Ice Phoenix through her gem.

'Looks like I have to take his deal,' Tia thought, fuming. 'His demand is insane, but how much can one guy really carry? Maybe it's not as bad as it sounds. Besides, he's a disciple. He should have some sense of honor.'

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC RESOLUTION]

After running through her options, Tia knew she was trapped. She gritted her teeth. "Fine. I promise. I swear on my warrior's vow: I won't harm you, and you'll get half the treasures!"

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Tia was furious. She had schemed and fought so hard to get here. The thought of someone snatching half her prize was almost too much to bear.

[AMBIENT: ECHOING CHAMBER]

Hearing Tia's vow, Zane smiled, turned, and strode toward the enchanted barrier. Then, he threw a punch.

[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]

[SFX: BARRIER CRACK]

A sharp crack echoed through the chamber.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY CRACKLING]

The barrier didn't shatter, but a spiderweb of thin, glowing red fissures spread out from the point of impact.

This barrier was worlds stronger than Yolande's. Even channeling the Emerald Crystal's energy, Zane could only make a crack that was almost invisible to the naked eye.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

"Emerald energy?" Tia stared, her eyes wide with disbelief.

'He's channeling Emerald energy with his bare hands,' she thought frantically. 'How is that possible? Emerald Crystals are practically myths. Their uses are limited, making them nearly impossible to find. Anyone lucky enough to get one embeds it in a weapon. But Zane's punch… it was like the crystal was inside him. How? The human body rejects most gems. Some are straight-up poisonous. And even if it isn't toxic, an Emerald Crystal’s raw power should tear a person apart.'

[SFX: ICE PROJECTILES]

[SFX: CRYO EXPLOSION]

The ice darts missed, slamming into the cavern wall. A blast of intense cryo-energy erupted on impact, coating the stone in a thick layer of frost that immediately splintered.

'Damn, that's some serious ice power,' Zane thought. 'One hit from that and I'd be a human popsicle.'

But for all its terrifying power, the ice was useless against the Drake's spirit. The creature was impossibly nimble, weaving between every dart Tia threw. Still, the constant dodging was slowing it down, letting Tia close the distance.

[SFX: CREATURE MOVEMENT]

[AMBIENT: DARK CAVERN]

Realizing it couldn't shake her, the spirit dove into a crack in the wall.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

[SFX: ICE CRYSTALLIZATION]

Now possessed by the Ice Phoenix, Tia wielded its power with deadly grace. She spread her arms, and two razor-thin swords of ice materialized in her hands. With a single, fluid motion, she swung them, shattering the crumbling wall and charging into the new opening.

Zane watched, impressed by her raw power. He didn't hesitate, diving into the breach right after her.

[AMBIENT: EERIE ORGANIC CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

The second he was inside, a wave of wrongness washed over him.

[SFX: PULSATING FLESH]

This wasn't a man-made tunnel. The walls were soft and fleshy, pulsating slightly like living tissue. Floating in the space above them were clusters of strange, bulbous glands.

Tia looked up and shouted, "You won't escape!"

She leaped into the air and shot upward, two long ice feathers trailing from her back like streamers.

[SFX: ICE BLADES SLICING]

But just then, the floating glands began to fall. Tia instinctively spun her swords, slicing through them all.

[SFX: WET HISSING]

A series of wet hisses filled the air.

[SFX: GLANDS BURSTING]

[AMBIENT: TOXIC SMOG]

The glands burst, releasing a thick, yellow smog that instantly filled the chamber. The stench was nauseating—cloyingly sweet and fishy, with the metallic tang of old blood.

[SFX: SPIRIT WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

As Tia was swallowed by the yellow haze, the Drake's spirit whipped around and shot toward Zane. "You!" it shrieked. "I'll take your body first!"

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PAUSE]

Hearing this, Zane paused, a smirk playing on his lips. "Are you sure that's what you want to do?"
