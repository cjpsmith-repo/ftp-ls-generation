Ep 987 - Path of Footprints
Word count: 1105
[AMBIENT: QUIET LIBRARY]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Aside from the missing rune, the book didn’t look like much. It was a little thinner than the others, with a dark red cover just a shade deeper than its neighbors.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
But as he leaned in, Zane spotted a thin layer of energy shimmering across its surface.
“What’s this?” Zane asked, his eyes fixed on the energy radiating from the cover.
The Eternal Scribe just shook his head. “Just a Dust-Repelling Spell,” he explained. “Even though I clean the Archives every day, you can never get rid of all the dust. The Master cast the spell on most of the books.”
Saul circled the book a few times, then shook his head, stumped. “I know the Master. This isn’t his style. He wouldn’t leave a puzzle he knew couldn’t be solved. And even if he did, why stick it here in the Archives, of all places?”
“So, where is this Master of yours now?” Zane asked.
The thought struck him then—if the puppet the Master created was immortal, the man himself must have been an unbelievably powerful warrior. It was hard not to wonder where someone like that could have gone.
“The Master went into the depths of the Chaos,” Saul muttered. “And he never came back.”
“The depths of the Chaos,” Zane repeated under his breath. The name reminded him of the Chaotic Storm Chronicle—a void that seemed to stretch on forever. You could spend a lifetime exploring a place like that and never see the end of it.
Meanwhile, the Eternal Scribe was spiraling. He was in charge of the Archives, yet he was completely clueless about this book. After all the countless years he’d spent here, it was downright humiliating.
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT LIBRARY]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
After a long, pensive moment, the Eternal Scribe finally spoke. “Whatever it is, the book is here. It has to be an attunement handbook, and it has to be meant for use.”
“Oh, cut the crap,” Saul sneered.
But the Eternal Scribe shook his head. “The Master collected countless attunement handbooks in his lifetime. He didn’t just collect them—he learned every single one until he’d mastered them all.”
Zane was floored. “You mean he practiced all of these? All tens of thousands of them?”
“Far more than that,” the Scribe said. “There are way more than just tens of thousands of handbooks in the Archives. With the Master’s talent, he could master a new one every single day. It was just that simple for him.”
The thought left Zane speechless. A level of talent like that was almost impossible to wrap his head around.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
“But in the last ten thousand years before he left, one thing really bothered the Master,” the Eternal Scribe continued, his voice dropping. “He wanted to absorb the Chaos itself — to take it into his body and use it as his own.”
Zane stared at him. “Absorb the Chaos?”
The Scribe nodded. “The Master believed that Chaos is an Origin Power — the source from which everything evolved. His reasoning was simple: if Vitality itself evolved from Chaos, why couldn’t Chaos be cultivated as a form of Vitality?”
Zane was silent. The logic held together. But the scale of it was staggering — no cultivator he’d ever heard of had dared attempt something like that.
“He spent ten thousand years testing the theory,” the Scribe continued. “He verified it, refined it, and eventually compiled a cultivation method based on his findings. But there was a restriction — one he could never overcome himself. He couldn’t practice his own method.”
The Scribe’s gaze drifted back to the sealed book.
“I believe this is that method.”
Even Saul, who had never once conceded ground to the Scribe, gave a slow nod. “…That actually makes sense.”
[MUSIC: BUILDING INTENSITY]
Zane looked at the book with new eyes. He wasn’t short of cultivation methods — far from it. The Malevolent Cycle of Life and Death, the Stellar Body — he hadn’t even pushed either to their full potential. The Stellar Body was an incomplete Transcendent Divine Might; he’d only reached its second move. And Tia’s Goddess Path had already hit a ceiling.
That was the problem with incomplete methods. No matter how exceptional, they had cracks built into them. If he was going to face the geniuses of a hundred thousand worlds in the Upper Realm, those cracks would matter.
A cultivation method conceived by the master of the Elysian Keep — one so powerful he couldn’t even practice it himself — was something else entirely.
Zane’s frustration got the better of him.
[SFX: MAGICAL HUMMING]
He summoned the black star pickax from his Storage Ring, its power humming in the quiet library.
[SFX: QUICK MOVEMENT]
Panic flashed across the faces of Saul and the Eternal Scribe. In an instant, Saul lunged for Zane’s wrist. “Don’t!” he yelled.
“The Master hates it when his books are destroyed!” the Eternal Scribe explained, his voice a panicked rush. “The Law of Causality in that pickax is powerful, sure, but you’re dreaming if you think it can break this cover. Trying will just set off the alarms, and then you’ll really be in for it!”
Seeing their terrified reactions, Zane let out a sigh of defeat and sent the black star pickax back to his Storage Ring. A wave of helplessness washed over him. Forget learning the hidden attunement; he couldn’t even get the damn book open.
[AMBIENT: QUIET LIBRARY]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Just then, Isla, who had been quiet until now, spoke up. “This bookshelf… it looks different.”
Saul blinked. “What? Where?”
[SFX: DISCOVERY CHIME]
She pointed to the base of the massive structure. They all followed her gaze and saw them: faint footprints right at their feet. They were barely visible against the pitch-black surface of the bookshelf, and it was a miracle she’d spotted them at all.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]
“Footprints!” Saul exclaimed. “Where the hell did these come from?”
The Eternal Scribe’s eyes went wide with disbelief. “I’ve cleaned the Archives countless times,” he stammered. “I’ve never seen these footprints before!”
“Well, no wonder. You’re an idiot,” Saul shot back with an eye roll.
“Saul,” Zane said, his eyes still locked on the prints, “how big is your Master?”
“The Master is huge,” Saul answered. “He can change his size at will, but he usually keeps himself about the height of this bookshelf.”
But the bookshelf was the size of a mountain. Zane stared back down at the floor.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]
How could a giant like that leave such tiny footprints?
