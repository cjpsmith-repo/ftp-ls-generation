Ep 1065 - The Final Say

Word count: 1061

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Every eye in The Mist Palace tracked Zane as he rose — slow, unhurried, utterly unbothered by the doubt rising from below.

A small smile played on Yolande’s lips. She had total faith in Zane.

She didn’t know how many petals his lotus flower had; her clones hadn’t sent back any memories of it. Zane had undergone his Celestial Tribulation while on the Divine Kingdom Continent, and at the time, one of her clones was in the Eastern Territories and the other was on the main continent.

She hadn’t bothered asking Zane about it. He’d already earned the golden celestial rank in the Tower of Sin; it would be pathetic if he couldn’t even handle the Red Cloud Ring.

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

Zane kept a steady pace, flying slowly up toward the ring.

Down below, all the warriors who had failed watched, their eyes glued to him, curious to see if he’d actually make it.

Even though Zane’s pace was slow, the Red Cloud Ring was only a few hundred feet up. In just a moment, he was right in front of it. In its resting state, the ring looked like a glowing-hot iron hoop, streaked with veins of dark red light.

With everyone watching, Zane drifted silently toward it.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

The Red Cloud Ring clearly wasn’t putting up its transparent barrier to block him. But the moment he passed through, the ring seemed to sense something. A streak of scarlet light began to flare across its surface.

[SFX: BRIGHT FLASH]

“Whoa, look at that light! It’s way more intense than it was for Layla.”

“No way. The ring was already at its max for her. How could anything be stronger than that?”

“Just watch. There’s always a bigger fish out there.”

Yolande watched in silence, something unreadable moving through her eyes.

Up above, Harvey’s expression had been darkening with every passing second.

[AMBIENT: WIND WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

High in the air, floating above the Red Cloud Ring, Layla bit her lip and stared at the glowing hoop.

Why did she see Zane as a rival? It wasn’t just because he’d claimed her master’s blade, Thundergust.

Her master had secretly confided in her that Zane shared her destiny: to one day lead the world of chaos. And more than that, his fate was shrouded in uncertainty, a future no one could predict.

Zane. He was worthy of being her rival.

As the light kept building on the ring, Layla felt something she rarely felt — unease. With her pride, she believed there were few geniuses in the entire universe who could stand shoulder to shoulder with her. Yet the ring was still climbing. She didn’t want Zane to surpass her here. She hadn’t wanted to admit that until now.

Layla couldn’t take the so-called “talents” of The Mist Palace seriously. Jasper, for example, might be powerful and gifted, but in her eyes, he wasn’t even in her league.

That was why, ever since joining The Mist Palace, she could have trained in seclusion without any distractions. But instead, she’d chosen the manor in Groundstone Hall just to be near Zane.

She’d tried provoking him. She’d destroyed his home. He hadn’t reacted to any of it. She couldn’t exactly attack him without warning — so this was the only path left.

[SFX: MAGICAL POWER DOWN]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Suddenly, the brilliant light from the ring just... vanished. It blinked out the instant Zane passed completely through.

[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]

Zane hovered there, staring at the dead ring. He had no idea what had just happened.

Up above, Layla exhaled quietly. She told herself it didn’t matter — the ring was just a screening tool, not a measure of true talent. It didn’t prove anything.

But she had been watching that light build, and she hadn’t wanted it to surpass hers. Now that the ring had gone dark, she couldn’t deny the small wave of relief that washed over her.

[SFX: SOFT MAGICAL HUM]

Yolande drifted to the ring. She reached out and touched it lightly with one finger — and the black iron hoop bloomed red again.

She glanced at Zane. “Want to try again?”

Zane nodded. He looped back below the ring and floated up through it a second time.

The result was identical. The light built, then died. The ring went black.

Yolande studied it for a moment, then said simply, “You’ve passed.”

Harvey turned to Yolande. “Palace Master, this goes against the rules. To participate in Passing the Torch, a warrior must pass through the Red Cloud Ring. Zane didn’t.”

“He passed through the ring,” Yolande said flatly.

“There was a problem with the ring when he did.”

“There was a problem,” she agreed. “He still passed.”

Harvey’s smile turned cold. “By that logic, if the ring simply isn’t activated, anyone could walk through and qualify. What’s the point of the selection at all? Is that fair to everyone else here?”

[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD MURMUR]

Less than ten percent of them had made it through the Red Cloud Ring. The other ninety percent were now out of the running for Passing the Torch, and their disappointment was curdling into bitter frustration.

[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

When they heard Harvey, they started shouting along with him.

“Yeah! If you’re gonna let him pass, then everyone should get a pass!”

“Make him do it again! That was a total cheat!”

Their voices swelled into a chorus of protest.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]

Hearing the uproar from below, Yolande’s expression turned glacial, her eyes ice-cold.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

She already knew what the problem was, but she had no intention of explaining it to them. The moment Zane passed through the Red Cloud Ring and the light sputtered out, she’d understood. He had activated the Greater Lotus Flower.

The Red Cloud Ring was only calibrated to register a Lesser Lotus Flower; anything more powerful would simply overload it. That’s exactly why the light had died.

Based on her earlier deductions, Yolande had figured Zane’s lotus flower would have at least seven petals, maybe even nine like Jasper’s, or the top-tier nine-petal flower like Layla’s. But she had never, ever expected him to have activated a Greater Lotus Flower while only at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm.

Still, her guess was based purely on the ring’s reaction. How many petals did his Greater Lotus Flower actually have? Eleven? Thirteen? Or was it as high as fifteen? She’d never asked.

[MUSIC: DARK DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

She met Harvey’s accusations with a cold, dismissive laugh and stared him down. “In The Mist Palace,” she said, her voice dangerously quiet, “who has the final say?”
