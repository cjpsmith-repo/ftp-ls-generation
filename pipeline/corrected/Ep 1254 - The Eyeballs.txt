Ep 1254 - The Eyeballs

Word count: 1177

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE AMBIENT]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL FOREST WITH DISTANT ECHOES]
Zane sighed, checking his ranking again. The Illusion Battlefield was just crawling with competitors.
It wasn’t just about raw strength, either. Every single warrior in the Soul Sea Realm had their own story, their own lucky breaks. Guys with that kind of strength and luck could just monopolize spots like The Deadwood Gate, raking in Illusion Points and leaving everyone else in the dust.
He couldn’t help but feel a pang of frustration. If it weren’t for Amber, he could’ve easily chased off that group of Demon Night warriors and claimed The Deadwood Gate for himself. When it opened, he would have waltzed right in and scooped up four million Illusion Points without breaking a sweat. What Sloane was doing—taking over a whole city and shaking down warriors for points—was nothing compared to the easy score he’d just missed.
That had to be why Aurora was so wary of him. As the descendant of a World Lord, he had his own ambitions for the rankings. Handing over those twenty-one fruits must have stung.
“Hey, check it out,” Zane said, pointing ahead. “There’s a path to the thirteenth branch. What do you say we head up?”
Amber shook her head. “We can’t go there.”
Aurora offered a small smile. 
“You can try it if you want, Zane. The thirteenth branch is the very top of the World Tree. It’s way beyond our league, but I bet the reward is incredible.”
That was all Zane needed to hear. He nodded, his mind made up. “I’m giving it a shot.”
He was already in the top twenty thousand of the entire Illusion Battlefield, but that wasn’t even close to his goal.
Worry creased Amber’s forehead. “We tried climbing it once. We think it’s where the Sorcerer Race passes down its legacy. The second we got close, we were hit with some weird spells.It knocked a few of us out cold before we could retreat. You really need to think this through, Zane.”
Zane just gave her a confident smile. “If it’s too much, I’ll just leave.”
Since he’d already made up his mind, Amber knew there was no point in arguing.
Rowan and the others watched with open anticipation. They’d tested the edges of the thirteenth branch themselves more than once, and every time, they’d stopped short of actually setting foot on top. None of them had ever dared to see it through. Now they wanted to know if Zane could.
Under the weight of everyone’s stares, Zane stepped onto the stone plate at the base of the climb — the same kind he’d used seven times already, climbing trunk after trunk to get this far.
[SFX: SKULLS COUGHING]
[SFX: GLOWING SCRIPT ACTIVATING]
The small skulls set into the plate coughed out their strange, rattling sound, and a fresh line of glowing script spread out beneath his feet. Without waiting, Zane let it carry him upward, flying straight for the crown of the World Tree.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RISING STRINGS]
His confidence wasn’t just for show. Zane’s physical strength wasn’t superficial—it wasn’t just in his muscles, bones, or skin. Over time, his internal organs had absorbed so many different kinds of flames that they were as tough as Divine Weapons. So while the strange force on this path might have seized another warrior’s heart and left them crippled, for Zane, it was just an annoyance. 
[AMBIENT: VAST TREETOP, WIND HOWLING]
At the top of the World Tree, the crown spread out into a huge, gray canopy. The white clouds that had followed him the whole climb had gone the color of lead the moment he arrived.
The instant his feet touched down, a strange feeling settled over Zane — half-familiar, like he’d felt this exact sensation somewhere before. He stood still for a few breaths, digging through his memory, but came up empty. Wherever the feeling came from, he couldn’t place it.
Spread out before him, thick brown-and-yellow branches formed a flat, open floor. And scattered across that floor sat skulls the size of small houses, dozens of feet across.
“These have to be Giant skulls,” Zane muttered, looking them over. “Looks like the Sorcerer Race turned them into shelters.”
He didn’t know much about the Giant Race. They stood twice as tall as Demon Night warriors, but they moved slowly, and their talent for cultivation had never been much to speak of. Innate divine strength was the one thing nearly every one of them was born with — and little else. In the crowded, competitive Upper World, brute strength alone wasn’t enough to matter. The Giant Race had faded into a minor clan, a footnote among the powers that actually shaped things.
With that settled in his mind, Zane started forward to explore the crown.
Maybe it was just because he was alone this time, but the whole place felt heavier, gloomier than it had any right to be. He hadn’t noticed the quiet at all with Aurora and the others around him. Alone now, he could hear his own heartbeat.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT THUMPING]
A few steps in, he stopped cold.
His own heartbeat was suddenly far too loud in his ears.
But the second he stood still, the sound started to fade, and within a few breaths it was gone entirely.
He started walking again.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT THUMPING, LOUDER]
The moment he moved, it came roaring back.
A cold smile crossed his face. Now he was sure of it — he’d walked straight into some kind of witchcraft.
Witchcraft wasn’t much of a force in the universe anymore. Ever since the Sorcerer Race vanished, whatever systematic teachings they’d once had had gone with them. A handful of scattered techniques still survived here and there, but nothing whole. What made witchcraft dangerous wasn’t its scale — it was how quietly it could kill, striking before the victim even knew to defend themselves.
Zane couldn’t see through it. He didn’t try to. He just let his heartbeat pound louder and louder in his ears and kept walking anyway.
After about five minutes, however, he stopped.
“This feels wrong,” he muttered. “I think I’m just walking in circles.”
The path was lined with sixty or seventy giant skulls, and after passing the same ones a few times, he knew he was trapped in a loop.
With that thought, Zane closed his eyes. 
[SFX: MAGICAL ACTIVATION WHOOSH]
He activated his Magic Vision, a green light flaring in his pupils as his gaze swept through the faint, lead-gray fog.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
What he saw made his breath catch in his throat.
[SFX: DEEP RESONANT HUM]
Deep within the mist, massive eyeballs were just… floating. He couldn’t tell how far away they were, which made it impossible to guess their true size, but he knew they were colossal. They hung there silently in the air, their gaze locked right on him.
It didn’t matter what kind of creature they belonged to. Being watched like that was enough to make his blood run cold.
