But it wasn't just the blue box. All of them—the two gold boxes and the black one from the very top—were being pulled up into a single, glowing beam of light.

"What the hell is going on?" Lennon muttered, so wiped he couldn't even manage to yell.

And it wasn’t just happening to him.

[AMBIENT: ICY WIND HOWLING]

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

Meanwhile, on the fourth stage of the Cloud Road—a place called the Thrilling Icy Plain—Frank had just defeated three Demonic Ice Golems. His victory finally cleared the path to the next stage.

To take down the three golems, he’d had to use more than just his skills—he’d been forced to use three talismans his father had given him. Those talismans were priceless. Frank had faced life-or-death situations plenty of times before, but had never once touched them. This time, though, he hadn't hesitated.

He really hadn't had a choice. The rewards for clearing the fourth stage were supposed to be massive, so he could only imagine what he'd get for taking down the golems themselves.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON ICE]

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

Climbing a set of steps carved from ice, he found a steaming pool of blood.

"A blood pool," he whispered. He looked up at the wall, where the words "Drake's Blood" were carved in an elegant, flowing script.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT DISCOVERY]

Drake's blood! Frank was floored.

It was nothing compared to True Dragon's blood, sure, but it would still give a massive boost to any warrior who soaked in it.

Suddenly, using those three talismans felt more than worth it.
He took a step toward the pool.
[SFX: RUSHING LIGHT]
A beam of light dropped from above and wrapped around it. Before Frank could react, the pool lifted off the ground and shot upward, following the beam into the sky.
Frank's spear hit the ground and he launched himself after it — but the moment he touched the beam, it flung him back hard. He tumbled down the ice steps.
[SFX: BODY HITTING ICE]
"Frank!" Lois caught him before he hit the bottom, her face white.
Frank shook her off, staring up at the empty space where the blood pool had been. "What the hell is going on?"

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL GATEWAY]

[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATION]

Cloud Hall had invested decades of resources and effort into training these disciples. If all their top students perished here, it would cripple the entire organization for generations.

Toby Dillon leaned forward, his knuckles white as he gripped the edge of the observation table. Around him, the other masters stood in tense silence, their eyes fixed on the Scrying Slate.

"All we can do now is wait and see," Toby muttered, his voice tight with anxiety. "I just hope this isn't some kind of bad omen."

The slate displayed beams of light shooting down at random intervals across the mystical square. Each flash represented a treasure descending—or a disciple's fate being sealed. 

Watching the chaotic display unfold, Toby felt the color drain from his face. They had sent their students into a trial they barely understood, chasing treasures from a fallen Sixth-Tier Sect. Now, all they could do was observe helplessly as events spiraled beyond their control

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL SQUARE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Standing in the square, Zane finally got it. He understood why Tia had told him to stay near the stone terrace, and he knew what those spots of light appearing all over the square meant.

They were targets—drawing the treasures down from the Cloud Road in those beams of light.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT CRASHES]

Suddenly, a series of loud crashes echoed across the square as several treasure boxes slammed onto the stone ground.

[MUSIC: WONDROUS DISCOVERY]

"The boxes!" Zane cried out, his eyes wide. The red treasure boxes… they were the exact same ones from the hidden trial! He knew how much effort it took to get just one of them, and now they were just… lying here for the taking.

Another crash startled him.

[SFX: MULTIPLE HEAVY IMPACTS]

Before Zane could even process it, another wave hit: six or seven blue boxes, three silver, two gold, and a single black one. He was completely stunned.
Zane took a step toward the chests.
[SFX: SHARP GRAB]
Tia's hand shot out and yanked him back.
[SFX: MASSIVE CRASH — GROUND CRACKING]
A heartbeat later, the Drake's Blood pool slammed into the square directly where he'd been standing, shattering the stone beneath it. The blood inside was so viscous that even the droplets that splattered out slowly crawled back in.
Zane stared at it. Then at Tia.
"Don't move until everything's landed," she said flatly, already looking back at the sky.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
Something enormous hit the square — not a chest, not a pool. A tree. Ancient, towering, its roots tearing up the stone as it landed.

Tia's eyes widened in recognition as she looked up at the majestic tree that stretched over their heads. "That's a Heavenly Fruit tree," she breathed, her voice carrying a note of awe that Zane had never heard from her before.

His jaw hung open. He just stood there, staring, completely frozen.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL ANTICIPATION]

A single silver box had given him the manual for the ‘Malevolent Cycle of Life and Death,’ an art left behind by the Blood Fiend Emperor himself—an ability that would make him infinitely more powerful. He couldn't help but wonder… what was inside the gold ones? Or that black one?

The curiosity was killing him.

Tia's voice broke him out of his haze. "You've gotten one of these before, haven't you?"

Zane just gave a slight shrug and a nod.

Tia's eyes scanned the chests, her gaze settling on the silver ones. Her brow furrowed. "You got a silver one, didn't you?"

There had been five silver treasure boxes in the valley. The Head of Cloud Hall took one, leaving four. But now she only saw three. It was obvious someone else had claimed one, which was why she’d asked.

When Zane nodded again, Tia’s eyes widened in shock. She knew exactly how hard it was to get a silver box.

Tia was strong enough to get one herself, but she'd only ever tried once. It wasn't until much later that she accidentally stumbled upon the true secret of the Cloud Road trial. After that, she'd decided going to the Sea of Raging Fire was a total waste of time.

Because no matter how powerful someone was, getting all the treasure boxes at once was impossible.

Besides, each heavenly fruit had a unique effect, and those magical properties made every single one priceless.

"I can't believe the Heavenly Fruit Tree is planted here, on the Cloud Road," Tia muttered to herself. "And to think it once belonged to the Blood Fiend Emperor… This is more than I ever imagined. It’s hard to even fathom how powerful his organization must have been… There are ten fruits on the tree now. That means someone ate the one from the secret auction. Otherwise, there wouldn't be ten left."

Zane wasn't sure why Tia was so fired up, but he could tell the tree was a huge deal. He figured half of all this treasure was his anyway, and with ten fruits on the tree… it only seemed fair he should get five. Yeah, that sounded like a pretty sweet deal.

[SFX: FALLING SWORD]

[SFX: CRASH]

Just as Zane was wondering what the heavenly fruits might taste like, another shadow blotted out the sky. It shot straight down, slamming into the ground with a deafening crash.

It was a sword—a massive blade, standing ten feet tall where it had buried itself in the stone.
