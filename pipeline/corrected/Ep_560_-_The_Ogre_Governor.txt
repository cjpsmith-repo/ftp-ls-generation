Ep 560 - The Ogre Governor
Word count: 1096
[AMBIENT: DARK OPPRESSIVE ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: FOCUSED UNDERSCORE]
Zane ignored the kneeling crowd surrounding him. Time was a luxury he couldn't afford—each passing moment brought stronger opponents, and he needed to recover before the next Ogres arrived.
Two hours crawled by before sensation finally crept back into his legs. Sharp tingles spread through his calves and thighs as circulation returned. His muscles trembled with the effort, weak as a newborn's.
Another two hours passed in agonizing slowness. When feeling at last returned to his hands, he flexed his fingers experimentally—stiff, but functional.
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
[SFX: MAGICAL HEALING]
He reached toward his chest with deliberate care, located the Storage Ring, and withdrew a healing pill. The moment he swallowed it, warmth spread through his core. The purple spatial storm had done more than merely block his meridians; beneath the surface, internal injuries festered like hidden wounds. The pill's energy began knitting torn tissue and mending damaged pathways.
[SFX: DISTANT RUMBLING]
[MUSIC: APPROACHING DANGER]
Three hours later, as the pill's effects deepened and warmth continued spreading through his meridians, a black line materialized on the distant grassland. Zane squinted against the glare, his vision still slightly blurred from the spatial storm's aftermath.
The dark smudge grew larger with each passing heartbeat, slowly resolving into a terrifying sight—over a thousand Ogres mounted on massive coursers, their armor glinting like scales on some monstrous serpent as they thundered across the plain.
Behind him, the humans scrambled into a tighter cluster, their brief flicker of hope extinguished by this fresh wave of terror. They believed Zane was a god—their savior descended from the heavens. But could even a divine being defeat such overwhelming numbers? So they watched Zane's back with desperate, wide-eyed anticipation, clinging to faith because they had nothing else left.
Zane felt their gazes like physical weight pressing against his shoulders. His legs still trembled beneath him, barely recovered. His soul remained depleted, his whole body tender from the spatial storm's ravaging.
He was no god. But he was all they had.
[SFX: HEAVY ARMOR CLANKING]
[AMBIENT: ARMY GATHERING]
An Ogre in a golden helmet and matching armor towered over the faceless horde. His booming voice cut through the ranks, and his comrades pulled back on the reins of their massive war-mounts, glaring at Zane from a hundred yards out.
"Human, are you from the Violet Heart Sanctuary?" the Ogre leader bellowed.
[SFX: OGRE HORDE ROAR]
The horde behind him roared, echoing the question.
Violet Heart Sanctuary? Zane frowned. The name meant nothing to him. Then his eyebrows shot up.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
"A sanctuary… on this continent?" he mumbled. A thought took shape. Judging by the Ogre's tone and the way the others reacted, it had to be a human stronghold.
Maybe he'd been wrong. Maybe the state of humanity on this continent wasn't as pathetic as he'd first believed. There might have been more of his kind out there after all.
Zane’s expression darkened. He shook his head. "You don't need to know where I'm from," he spat.
The Ogre leader chuckled, looking down on him. "As long as you're not from the Violet Heart Sanctuary, I don't care who you are." He jabbed a thick finger at Zane as the others snickered. "A puny seventh-rank warrior dares to waltz into my territory and kill my people? You've got a death wish, human."
The Ogre leader finished his rant with a wave of his arm, signaling the attack.
[SFX: EARTHQUAKE RUMBLE]
[AMBIENT: CHARGING ARMY]
A moment later, the ground began to tremble like an earthquake as thousands of Ogres charged straight for Zane.
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]
As the thundering horde bore down on him, Zane took a slow, steadying breath. They were dead wrong if they thought their numbers would make him back down. Instead of retreating, he smirked and started walking forward to meet their charge head-on.
He cracked his knuckles and rolled his neck, a grin spreading across his face. Lucky for him, they had no idea who they were dealing with.
His soul swept outward as he walked. All the Ogres who came within range of him trembled and fell from their horses, one after another. From beginning to end, Zane didn't raise his hands. He just silently strolled among the thousands of them.
[SFX: MACE IMPACT]
Then one got through. A particularly powerful Ogre rushed at him, swinging a mace the size of a tree trunk. Zane erased its soul mid-charge, but the body's momentum carried the blow home.
Zane was sent skidding across the ground.
He climbed back to his feet. And kept walking.
Behind him, Craig's face was drenched in tears. "A god!" he screamed. "He's a god!"
[MUSIC: OMINOUS AWE]
The Ogre army, which had surged forward like a flood, seemed to hit a reef. Every soldier who rode within range of Zane died in silence. The ones behind veered away, pulling their mounts sideways rather than pressing forward. The great column split around him on both sides — a thousand Ogres parting for one walking man.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The Ogre leader, the governor of this army, had initially dismissed Zane's power as pathetic—nowhere near enough to handle a full-scale assault. But then a shiver ran down his spine as he realized the truth: the man hadn't been fighting with physical strength. He was using his soul.
The governor hesitated. That had to be how the first patrol was wiped out. Seeing some of his charging warriors begin to falter, he decided to take matters into his own hands. As their leader, he had to set an example. He was, after all, the strongest of them all.
[SFX: BATTLE CRY]
He unleashed a battle cry and charged forward, his massive frame shaking the ground with each thunderous step.
Gritting his teeth, the governor knew the truth all too well—Ogres were notoriously vulnerable to soul attacks. Their physical might came at a cost: spiritual defenses as fragile as paper. What Zane didn't know was that the governor possessed a secret, a trump card he'd acquired at great cost specifically for encounters like this.
If his soul attacks can't touch me, he's mine!
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
The thought consumed him as he thundered toward Zane, confidence surging through his veins. His warriors would see their leader triumph. They would witness what happened when an Ogre governor faced down a human cultivator and emerged victorious.
The governor's lips pulled back in a savage grin, already imagining the moment when his club would crush this arrogant outsider into the dirt.
