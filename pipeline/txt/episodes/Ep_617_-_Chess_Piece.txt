Ep 617 - Chess Piece
Word count: 1196
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
"Am I just a chess piece?" Zane asked Lavender, the word catching in his throat.
Lavender's expression was dead serious. "Exactly. The Era of Evolution has begun. Talent is blooming everywhere, but you are the one destined to lead this age of chaos. As long as you survive, you're guaranteed to become the main character of this new world."
Zane had never really understood what they meant by the Era of Evolution. Both the Cobalt Dragon and Vesper were always secretive about it, stopping short whenever the conversation reached the important parts.
"What does that even mean?" he pressed.
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]
"Haven't you noticed?" Lavender said. "This era is overflowing with geniuses. Countless prodigies are emerging across this world alone — and there are over a million worlds beyond it, each with more talent than you can imagine."
Zane nodded slowly. She was right. Back at the Skyreach Sect, mastering the Accomplished Sword Intent was a once-in-a-generation event. The Apex was a complete myth — and then in a single competition, both Finn and he had appeared. After that, the Dragon Soar Arena had been crawling with saint-level geniuses. Normally, any one of them was practically guaranteed to reach the Soul Sea Realm, and yet not one had.
Every sect in The Heartlands had been asking the same question for years. Now he finally understood why.
Lavender stared at him, her expression shifting. The composure slipped, just slightly. "I need you to help me," she said. "Please."
The word sat strangely in the air. The Queen of Slaughter of the Demon Night, asking a War Fighter for help.
Zane hesitated. Agreeing wasn't wise—Lavender had only a fragment of her soul remaining, and once they returned to the upper world, he could find himself facing the entire Demon Night alone. He was still turning it over when the Cobalt Dragon's voice drifted through his mind, calm and unhurried. "Say yes. The Demon Night will be of great use to you. If you help her reclaim her throne, the benefit will be considerable."
"I don't care about the benefits," Zane said, looking straight at Lavender. "You've saved my life more times than I can count. I can't make you any grand promises—but if there's ever anything you need from me, I'll be there."
[MUSIC: GENTLE EMOTIONAL PIANO]
The gloom on Lavender's face lifted. She stared at him for a long moment before lowering her head. "Thank you."
A small smile touched Zane's lips. "But you're still my sword spirit. You can't stay attached to my body forever."
"I'll find a way to reconstruct my body," she said. "But not right now."
Suddenly, an idea sparked in her eyes. "Your biggest goal is to save your sister. I can order Amber to bring the Demon Night to The Heartlands. We can help you kill Maddox."
Zane's eyebrows shot up. The offer was tempting. Maddox's strength was roughly equivalent to a third-level War Sage. The Heavenly Feather Sanctuary had at least a million warriors at that rank, plus Amber at the Soul Sea Realm. Their combined strength could crush him easily.
Still, Zane shook his head. "Maddox is an obstacle I have to tear down myself."
If he couldn't overcome it alone, the failure would fester into an inner demon that would haunt him for the rest of his life. The day Maddox's giant face appeared in the sky above his home, he had sworn to be the one to take his life. That hadn't changed.
Lavender didn't argue. She simply turned and walked toward the palace entrance.
[AMBIENT: GRAND PALACE INTERIOR]
When the Demon Night members filed back in and saw the shattered statue and the black greatsword buried in the ground, their faces went white. The Statues were sacred—touching one accidentally was enough to warrant severe punishment. That their own queen had destroyed it left them frozen, too stunned to speak.
Zane kept his expression neutral, though inwardly he was fighting back a laugh. If they'd seen the two queens screaming at each other like that, they'd never recover.
"Clean it up," Lavender said, her queenly composure fully restored. "Don't erect the statue again."
Amber stared. "My Queen—the three statues cannot be absent. It is the rule of every Demon Night holy land."
Lavender considered this for a moment. "Fine. Carve a new one." She paused. "Make it uglier."
Zane nearly choked. Once she held a grudge, she didn't stop.
[SFX: HEAVY DOORS OPENING]
[AMBIENT: TREASURE VAULT]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL WONDER]
Amber led them down through the holy tree—through the palace, past the roots, and finally into the treasury. Lavender needed a new weapon. The black greatsword she'd been using was powerful but low-grade, and Cheryl had gouged the blade during their fight in the Tower of Sin. It was on the verge of shattering.
The sheer scale of a seventh-grade sacred place's treasury hit Zane the moment he stepped inside. Back in The Heartlands, a single low-grade Relic-Class weapon was a priceless artifact. Here, more than a dozen of them were simply plunged into the ground around a luminous pearl-like ornament.
No wonder Fabian had laughed when the Ogres offered ten of them for his head.
"The finest weapon in the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary is a top-grade Relic-Class sword," Amber said, and reached behind the pearl to pull a long ornate box from the earth.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
The blade was pale gold, shimmering with an ancient light that spoke of ages past. "I found it in the Dark Nether Cave," Amber said. "I'm not a sword user, so I sealed it away."
Zane's eyes went wide. What kind of place produced weapons like that?
Lavender glanced at it and shook her head. "Give it to Zane."
Amber's face flickered. She had nearly died retrieving that sword. Giving it to a foreign warrior stung.
"Can't I?" Lavender said, sensing her hesitation.
Amber lowered her head immediately. "My Queen, it is yours to give."
Zane took the sword, his pulse quickening. A top-grade Relic-Class weapon. He already had one, but there was no ceiling on weapons for a warrior—only better.
"I need a spear," Lavender said. "That's my weapon now."
When she was young, she had used a sword. But the day she was chosen as Queen of Slaughter, she had switched to the spear—and she had wielded it far longer than she ever had a blade. Using a sword as her sword spirit had been a convenience, not a choice. With a spear in hand, she could show her true strength.
Amber summoned a silver spear from across the vault with a wave of her hand. Mid-grade Relic-Class—a step below the sword, but still formidable.
Lavender took it, looked forward, and thrust it into the air without warning.
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
The wave of energy that cracked from the tip didn't just startle Zane. It visibly stunned Amber—a master of the Soul Sea Realm, frozen in place, completely unable to comprehend what she had just witnessed.
Lavender's power as a sword spirit was tied to Zane's own. But her true understanding was on another level entirely.
It always had been.
