Ep 948 - Amber's Return

Word count: 1091

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

[SFX: WHOOSHING LIGHT]

A streak of light tore across the sky, heading for the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary at blinding speed.

Lacey and Chantel both sagged with relief the moment they recognized it.

It was Amber. 

Her arrival sent a jolt of hope through them, but it was followed by a wave of confusion. How did Zane know she was coming back? Was he clairvoyant or something?

Even Esther Sterling, standing nearby, found herself wondering the same thing. She was reminded of the fight with Saroyan, when Zane had pinpointed the exact length of his opponent’s life, right down to the day. It was like he knew everything that happened in the world. Was he some kind of god walking among them?

[SFX: WIND BILLOWING]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The sound of Amber’s frantic approach cut through their thoughts. Her light purple robe billowed behind her as she descended, and raw anger hardened her features as she took in the ruined landscape.

She knew this would happen. Since the forces of the Dread Place had been bold enough to hunt her, she’d figured the Flaming Place would make a move on the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary. There was no way Lacey and Chantel could fend off an entire invasion on their own.

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FIRE AND SMOKE]

Thick smoke still curled over what was left of the Sanctuary, and her frown deepened into a scowl. If it had been truly destroyed, she swore she would hunt down every last Ogre from both the Flaming and Dread Places, even if it was the last thing she did.

But the sight of Lacey and Chantel, alive and unharmed, let some of the tension drain from her. That relief became certainty when she spotted a familiar young man in a white robe.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

“Zane!”

[AMBIENT: SANCTUARY GROUNDS]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]

Her mind cleared the moment she saw him. The Sanctuary's victory against the Ogres… it had to be his doing.

[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]

“Amber!”

Lacey and Chantel rushed to her as soon as she landed. “Amber, you're okay! The Dread Place… they knew where you were. They ambushed you during your tribulation.”

“You mean these three?” Amber asked, her hand shooting up to reveal three severed heads, held aloft by their hair. One belonged to the leader of the Dread Place.

All three had been masters of the Soul Sea Realm. And all three had died by her hand.

A small smile touched Zane’s lips. Amber was even tougher than he’d thought. Not only had she survived the greater Celestial Tribulations, but she'd also killed the three Soul Sea Realm Ogres who'd come for her.

As Amber walked toward Zane, her eyes moved past him to Dorothea and the young man in black standing behind him. She felt it immediately — the pressure radiating off them, faint but vast. Her first instinct was Soul Sea Realm, but that wasn't quite right. They were something beyond that.

She looked back at Zane. The pieces fell into place.

“Zane,” she said carefully. “Just now —“

Zane nodded. “I have to thank these two. Without them, the Sanctuary wouldn't have made it through.”

Amber didn't hesitate. Her figure shifted, and she dropped to one knee before Dorothea and the young man in black. “The Demon Night will engrave this kindness into our memory forever.”

[MUSIC: CURIOUS INTRIGUE]

Dorothea looked indifferent. The young man in black laughed — warmer than anyone had ever heard from him. “It was nothing! Besides —“ his eyes moved over Amber with sudden interest, “— you've been recognized by the Demi-Immortal Clan, haven't you? I can feel the mark on you. Second row of the Talent Tablet, if I'm not wrong.”

Amber rose. “Yes.”

“Then we're of the same clan,” the young man said, and launched into an animated conversation with her.

Zane watched and rolled his eyes. The young man had never once smiled at him like that — and Zane also carried the Demi-Immortal mark.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Just then, Zane held up a strange, pulsating branch.

[SFX: SHARP GASP]

“The Soulcradle Wood!” Amber gasped.

Her heart plummeted. That specific branch was only used to preserve the last flicker of a life. It meant Lavender was on the brink of death.

[MUSIC: SORROWFUL PIANO]

Zane’s face was a mask of grief. “Lavender took a soul attack meant for me. Her soul was on the verge of collapsing. I had no choice but to place her inside the Soulcradle Wood.”

Amber’s teeth ground together. “We'll use the holy tree's life force again. It has to be enough to wake My Queen.”

But Zane just shook his head. “I don't think it will be.”

The entire Heavenly Feather Sanctuary was devastated. They would need the holy tree’s power if they ever hoped to restore it to what it was.

As it was, the massive tree was scarred and pitted from the battle. Using its power for Lavender now would likely drain it dry.

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL CRESCENDO]

“My Queen is more important!” Amber shot back. “I can feel the holy tree's will. It's willing to sacrifice itself to save her!”

Zane could only sigh. “This is on me. I'll find a way to bring her back,” he said, his voice firm. “Besides, the tree's energy wouldn't be enough to fully repair her damaged soul anyway.”

The power of the Soulcradle Wood in his hand was just as potent as the holy tree's. Its healing was slower, but for now, it was enough to keep Lavender's soul from fading completely.

[AMBIENT: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Not long after, Dorian arrived from the Violet Heart Sanctuary. The attack had been sudden — by the time word reached the humans, and they moved, it was already over. He said a few words of consolation to Amber, then his expression hardened.

“The next step is to remove both sacred places from the main continent entirely. Their Soul Sea masters are gone. Before they can produce a new one, even the Violet Heart Sanctuary alone is strong enough to finish them.”

Amber nodded.

[AMBIENT: INSIDE HOLY TREE]

[SFX: RUSTLING PAPERS]

Inside the Holy Tree, Wade laid out the list of materials he needed for the puppets. Amber looked it over. Most were available in the Sanctuary's stores — but two or three she had never heard of. Lower-world resources had their limits.

“Substitute them,” the young man in black said to Wade.

Wade thought it over, then named several alternatives. They went back and forth a few times before settling on a final list. It would take a day or two to gather everything. The Demi-Immortals settled in to wait.

Just then, a girl in a yellow robe from the Demon Night race stepped forward, bowing respectfully to Amber.

“Ailsa, what is it?”

Ailsa was a third-level War Sage. Amber couldn't help but be curious as to why she was here.
