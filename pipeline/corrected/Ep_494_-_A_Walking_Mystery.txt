[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORIENTAL]

Treasures like the Heavenly Feather Crown served a far greater purpose than just decoration—they granted cultivators extraordinary abilities beyond their natural limits.

The Crown, for example, could amplify a practitioner's mastery over specific Laws, transforming a modest understanding into formidable power. Simply wearing such an artifact accelerated one's cultivation speed, shaving years or even decades off the arduous journey toward higher realms.

Zane found himself captivated by the possibilities. How many cultivators had risen to prominence not through talent alone, but through possessing the right treasure at the right moment? The thought both excited and troubled him—he'd relied on his own strength thus far, but perhaps that stubborn pride had been holding him back.

[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]

Then there was the Time Copper Coin, which belonged to the World Merchants' Guild. It held a sliver of the Time Law, and wearing it while cultivating granted an additional six hours every single day.

Those six hours seemed modest at first glance, but Zane's mind raced through the calculations. A cultivator's lifespan stretched across decades, even centuries—those accumulated hours compounded into years, perhaps decades of additional training time. The ancient tycoon from the World Merchants' Guild had reportedly lived three hundred years beyond his natural span, all thanks to that very coin. Zane felt a pang of envy imagining such an advantage.

But these subtle enhancements paled in comparison to the immediate, visceral power of weapons and armor.

Armor could intercept a fatal strike; a blade could end an opponent's life in a single breath. Zane remembered how his own Mythic weapon had saved him countless times.

Still, weapons alone offered incomplete protection. What value did a collection of Relic-Class swords hold if their owner lay dead in the dust? Zane had witnessed too many talented cultivators fall because they'd invested everything in offensive power while neglecting defense.

A Relic-Class protective garment, on the other hand, could absorb a devastating blow that would otherwise pierce straight through to the heart. It represented the difference between a close call and a funeral.

[AMBIENT: COUNCIL CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Of the three leaders, Hobbes remained, as usual, the most impatient. "What the hell is with this Zane kid? The guy's a walking mystery!"

Kieran offered a slightly defeated smile, though admiration flickered in his eyes. "Cloud Hall got lucky with him. It looks like they're a shoo-in for fifth-grade status now."

For years, Cloud Hall had been pushing toward that coveted promotion, inching closer with each passing season. Their strategy had always centered on Tia. As long as she mastered the Goddess Path, she stood a solid chance at breaking into the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. Once that happened, their elevation would be guaranteed, placing them on equal footing with the prestigious Ethereal Spirit Sect.

But now, Cloud Hall had stumbled upon an entirely unexpected asset in Zane. With his perfect brushwork, he was positioned to become the most powerful runic expert the Heartlands had seen in generations.

Yet being a runic expert alone wouldn't suffice. Such specialists weren't fighters, after all. A top-tier runic master could certainly enhance their reputation and attract wealthy patrons, but it wouldn't provide the raw martial power necessary to secure fifth-grade status.

Zane, however, defied categorization. He wasn't your average runic expert confined to a workshop.

For one, he'd completely mastered the first layer of the Fire Law—a feat that typically required decades of dedicated cultivation.

Beyond that, he was a body refiner whose physique surpassed almost anyone they'd encountered, his flesh tempered to a degree that bordered on the supernatural.

And now, just to silence Eden's provocations, he had casually produced a Relic-Class protective garment as though such treasures were commonplace possessions.

The three leaders sat in stunned silence. A thousand thoughts raced through their minds, each more bewildering than the last, but none could make sense of the young man.

Even Eden, for all his earlier bravado, seemed momentarily at a loss.

Where had this kid come from? What resources had shaped him into such an anomaly?

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

Benson from Cloud Hall had long considered Eden insufferable. Watching him stand there in the arena with that smug expression, Benson made his decision. He launched himself from the platform to join him.

[SFX: WATER RIPPLES]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TENSION]

The instant Benson's feet touched the stone, ripples radiated outward from beneath him, transforming the entire arena floor into what appeared to be a vast, shimmering pool. The effect was mesmerizing—solid stone suddenly resembled liquid crystal, each ripple catching the light.

[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD REACTION]

Eden merely stared at Benson, his face an impenetrable mask of calm indifference. To him, Benson represented nothing—not a challenge, not even a minor obstacle. In this entire tournament, only one person posed a genuine threat: Brent from the Ethereal Spirit Sect. Everyone else was beneath his notice, unworthy of serious consideration.

[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]

Benson drew his pale blue sword, its edge gleaming like ice in sunlight. He leveled it directly at Eden's chest. "Eden, draw your sword!"

His voice carried the weight of Cloud Hall's collective fury. Zane had just salvaged their reputation through sheer determination, yet Eden had shamelessly proclaimed his intention to claim Tia as his own. The insult burned in every Cloud Hall disciple's heart—a slight they could neither forgive nor forget.

Eden's gaze flickered briefly to Benson's sword, regarding it with the same disdain one might show a child's wooden practice blade.

[MUSIC: DARK DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Then, in a heartbeat, the façade shattered. The calm vanished, replaced by a ruthless aura that twisted his handsome features into something predatory and dangerous. His eyes darkened with barely contained rage.

He was already seething from his earlier humiliation, and now this nobody—this insignificant disciple—dared to challenge him. The audacity of it ignited something savage within him.

[SFX: WIND WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

An invisible force erupted from his body, violent and overwhelming. As it swirled around him in a vortex of raw power, several blades of wind materialized from nothing, dancing across his fingertips like living creatures eager for blood. His expression shifted into something feral, predatory.

Then, like a lion lunging for prey it had already marked for death, he shot straight toward Benson with terrifying speed.
