Ep 413 - Spear Of Annihilation

Word count: 1089

[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUND]

[MUSIC: TENSE AFTERMATH]

The elite disciples of Cloud Hall watched, utterly speechless.

Most of them were at the fourth, fifth, or even sixth level of the Soul Realm—technically higher than Frank. But in a real fight, not one of them could touch him. Frank was a heaven-level talent, his true strength far exceeding his rank; he regularly crushed warriors at much higher levels without breaking a sweat.

And now, some kid from a lower realm was actually challenging him.

They couldn't wrap their heads around it. The guy was only a fourth-level Adept.

Frank was a fresh Soul Realm warrior, sure, but he should have been able to annihilate an Adept with a single blow. Maybe they’d understand if both fighters were in the Soul Realm, but Frank also had a mountain of unique talents and resources. His own father had personally overseen his training, and a single one of his meals probably cost more than most warriors earned in a lifetime. How could anyone possibly compare?

But this Zane kid? The chasm in power between a fourth-level Adept and an initial-stage Soul Realm warrior was supposed to be insurmountable. The fact that this rookie had the nerve to challenge a prodigy like Frank was unbelievable.

Or at least, it had been. Now, they were starting to understand the quiet confidence Zane had shown earlier. He actually had the strength to back it up.

[SFX: DEBRIS FALLING]

[MUSIC: AGGRESSIVE DRUMS]

As the other elites stood frozen in shock, Frank clawed his way out of the crater. His hair was a wreck, and his eyes blazed with crimson fury.
From the white spear, something shifted. A Dragon Tattoo emerged across Frank's skin — blood-red at first, then deepening to black, death aura rising from it like smoke off a funeral pyre. The crowd went still.
"Frank—" Marcus started, his voice tight. "Are you actually going to use that?"
Nobody needed to answer. Everyone recognized what was coming.

Tia smiled as she took in the scene. "Let's see how Zane handles this. I think he can do it. The power of the Malevolent Cycle of Life and Death is every bit as strong as the Spear of Annihilation."

She knew all about the Spear of Annihilation. Before the trial, she'd been watching Frank's technique closely, even considering asking him to break the enchanted barrier. In the end, though, she’d chosen Zane. Looking back, it almost felt like fate.

The Blood Fiend Emperor had been a titan of his era. If he’d chosen the Malevolent Cycle as his primary Cultivation Method, how could it possibly be weaker than Frank’s inherited skill? Even though Zane had only been practicing it for a short time, Tia had faith in him. Besides, this was the perfect test.

Saint Morphen, despite hearing Tia’s claims that Zane could help them claim the treasures of the Cloud Road, still harbored doubts. Like everyone else, he just couldn't get past Zane's low power level.

Seeing Tia’s complete lack of concern, he decided to hold his tongue and turned his attention back to the Scrying Slate.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]

Wisps of a ghastly gray death aura swirled around Frank's white spear, an energy so potent it felt like it could scrape against the other warriors' very souls. Holding it, Frank looked less like a man and more like a phantom dredged up from the Underworld.

[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]

"My Spear of Annihilation destroys everything in its path!" he roared. "Let's see you stop this, Zane!"

[SFX: POWER SURGE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

Zane's expression hardened as he faced the terrifying power coalescing around the spear. He instantly activated his Exterminating Wheel. At the same time, he drew three shards of pure Malice from the Killing Sword Mountain.

Cultivation Methods that used Malice were notoriously powerful. A single shard could amplify a skill exponentially; three was an entirely different level of devastation.

Zane hadn't managed to store much of it. He’d gained a small amount back at House Lowell after killing the Blood Prisoner, gathered a bit more on the Cloud Road, and then harvested a decent supply from the hundreds of Nether Phantoms he’d slain.

He hated using so much of his reserves in a single fight, but against Frank, he had to go all out.

[SFX: BATTLE CRY ECHO]

Frank bellowed again."Zane Lowell, you die today!"

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]

[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]

The Spear of Annihilation shot forward, crackling with a suffocating death aura that made the onlookers’ hearts seize in their chests. The technique was a direct inheritance from Frank's father, Emanuel. No one knew its true limits, but they could all feel the immense, world-ending force behind it.

[SFX: SWORD SWING]

[SFX: IGNITION BURST]

In that same instant, Zane swung his sword, and the three shards of Malice ignited.

[SFX: BONE CRACK]

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD GASPS]

When the blinding light faded, a collective gasp swept through the crowd. Frank was on his knees, clutching his right arm. It hung at a sickening, unnatural angle, the bone clearly shattered. The injury was devastating; even if it healed, that arm would never be as strong as it once was.
When the light faded, Frank's white spear was disintegrating — fracturing piece by piece, crumbling like rotten wood from tip to hilt. The shattering traveled up the shaft and into Frank's hand. His arm twisted at a wrong angle, the bones snapping inch by inch.

[SFX: BONE CRACK]
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD GASPS]

Frank screamed and hit the ground.

Marcus was at his brother's side instantly, forcing a white elixir between his teeth before straightening up.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

Marcus's expression darkened. He rose to his feet, a cold smile twisting his lips as he stared down at Zane. "I never expected you to be this powerful. You didn't just survive Frank's Spear of Annihilation… you broke his arm." He clenched his jaw, his voice tight with barely contained rage.

Zane just blinked, a calm, almost lazy smile touching his face. "Save it. Frank would've killed me if I hadn't broken his arm. If anything, I was being merciful. For trying to take my life, I should have taken his."

[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: AGGRESSIVE COMBAT]

"Go to hell!" Marcus snarled, his eyes flashing with menace as his blood-red spear materialized and shot toward Zane like a bolt of lightning.

Marcus was a ninth-level Soul Realm master, right on the cusp of the realm's peak. He was leagues stronger than any opponent Zane had ever faced.

[SFX: LEVITATION HUM]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ASCENSION]

But just as the spear was about to impale him, Zane's body suddenly lifted off the ground. He was flying.

[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]

A stunned silence fell over the elite disciples. Jaws hung open. They were completely floored.

Wasn't Zane just an Adept? If so, how in the world could he fly?
