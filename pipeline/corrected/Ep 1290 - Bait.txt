Ep 1290 - Bait

[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL FOREST CAVERN]
[SFX: WOODEN CAGE SNAPPING SHUT]
The cage of Lofty Wood sprang open like a monstrous jaw and snapped shut, swallowing the flame kitten whole.
Zane paused mid-stride, glancing over his shoulder to see if the trap had worked. He wasn’t the only one; everyone held their breath, their eyes locked on the Frosty Cage.
[SFX: DESPERATE KITTEN MEOWING]
[SFX: THRASHING AGAINST WOODEN BARS]
A desperate, panicked meow echoed from within. The little kitten thrashed wildly, its fiery body slamming against the wooden bars as it tried to break free.
Janet’s jaw was tight with concentration. The Lofty Wood was an extension of her own will, and she had to focus every ounce of her being just to hold the cage together. 
[SFX: CRACKLING FLAMES AGAINST WOOD]
At the same time, she poured her Vitality into the wood, frosting it over to weaken the Fire Law radiating from the kitten. Normally, fire would burn through wood without a second thought, but this Lofty Wood was something else entirely, seemingly immune to the flames.
Still, this was no ordinary animal. It was a Law Creature, born from the molten heart of the cavern, and its power was immense. Janet could feel it straining against her hold, threatening to break free at any moment.
[SFX: FIERCE KITTEN MEOW]
[SFX: WOOD CREAKING AND BENDING]
The kitten’s violent struggles bent and warped the Frosty Cage, but the bars held firm. 
But the effort was taking a toll on Janet. 
[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]
[SFX: FRANTIC SCRATCHING]
The color drained from her face, leaving her pale and slick with sweat. The kitten, however, seemed to have limitless energy, its attacks growing more frantic by the second.
[SFX: STONE CRUSHING]
[SFX: ENERGY ABSORPTION WHOOSH]
With a flick of her wrist, Janet pulled out a Sovereign Stone and crushed it in her palm, absorbing the burst of Vitality. She was already running on fumes. It was staggering how much energy it took just to contain this tiny creature—it was draining her faster than a full-blown battle.
Seeing her falter, Zane acted. 
[SFX: CHAOS ENERGY GATHERING]
[SFX: ICE BLAST WHOOSH]
He waved a hand, pulling a thread of Chaos Energy from his own body and shaping it into a blast of pure cold that shot toward the caged kitten. The other warriors who commanded the Ice Law followed his lead, adding their power to his, hoping to finally subdue the creature.
[SFX: INTENSE FREEZING WIND]
Under the combined assault, the flame kitten’s movements grew sluggish. Its frantic cries softened into a weak whimper. 
[SFX: FLAMES FLICKERING OUT]
[SFX: WEAK WHIMPER]
The flames that formed its body flickered and dimmed, and its fiery fur turned dull and matted. After a few more moments, it stopped fighting altogether and lay down quietly inside the cage.
[SFX: LAVA DRIPPING]
“We did it,” Janet sighed, her hand trembling as she finally let herself relax. As she did, tiny rivulets of molten lava dripped from the creature’s now-still form.
“You people are insane. Actually caught the thing.” Coleman had crept back to a safe distance—close enough to watch, far enough to bolt if it broke loose. 
Janet knelt beside the Frosty Cage, studying the kitten’s dimmed, exhausted little form. Its cat eyes blinked back at her, pitiful. “This little thing,” she murmured, “it’s kind of sad, isn’t it?”
“Don’t waste the sympathy,” said Coleman, who of everyone had the least to spare for the creature. “The whole Illusion Battlefield is just a projection of Cosmic Order. Everything in it—the kitten included—stops existing the moment this place dissolves. It was never really alive to begin with.”
He wasn’t wrong. Janet knew the logic as well as anyone. But knowing a thing and feeling it were two different matters, and some part of her still felt for the creature anyway.
“So how do we actually get it to the giant?” Zane asked, glancing at the distance still separating them from their target. “Can’t just chuck a cage that far.”
“Leave that part to me.” Janet’s expression was complicated as she looked down at the kitten one last time. She wrapped her fingers around the end of the Lofty Wood. “Sorry, little guy. I just need to borrow you for a bit. If this doesn’t work, I promise I’ll put you right back where I found you.”
[SFX: WOOD EXTENDING]
She gave her wrist the faintest shake, and the wood obeyed—stretching, lengthening, carrying the cage out ahead of her like a fishing line. A thousand feet. Two thousand. Three. Four. Five thousand feet, and still extending, until the cage hung directly in front of the lava giant.
Nothing. The giant didn’t so much as glance at it.
Zane’s jaw tightened. If this didn’t work, they had no other way across.
“One more try,” Janet muttered, and shook her wrist again. The cage swayed at the end of the impossibly long branch.
[SFX: WEAK KITTEN MEOW]
The kitten let out a soft, exhausted cry.
The lava giant’s stone eyes snapped open. It lunged, jaws opening wide to swallow the cage whole.
“Pull back!” Janet yanked her wrist, and the wood retracted in a blink—the giant’s massive hand closing on empty air where the cage had been a heartbeat before.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CLIMACTIC STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: EXPLOSIVE CHAOS]
[SFX: MASSIVE GROUND QUAKE]
A thunderous explosion rocked the cavern, the ground shaking so violently that Zane and the others staggered, fighting to keep their balance. 
[SFX: GIANT FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
The lava giant had started to move.
[SFX: MOLTEN WHIRLPOOL CHURNING]
The moment the giant lifted its leg, the molten sea at its feet swirled into a massive whirlpool. 
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT SPLASH]
When it stomped down, its foot punched through the dark, crusted surface of the lava, sending out tidal waves of searing liquid fire. This wasn’t the thick, dark-red magma you’d see in a volcano; this stuff was different. It shimmered with the combined power of the Earth and Fire Laws, glowing a brilliant, blinding red and flowing with the speed of water.
“Fall back!” Zane yelled.
They all realized the spreading lava was just as dangerous as the giant itself. 
[SFX: FRANTIC FOOTSTEPS RUNNING]
They scrambled toward the back of the cave, desperate to outrun the approaching waves. No one wanted to be touched by that stuff. Even Zane was on high alert. A small, reckless part of him wondered if this special lava could be used to strengthen his body, but this was hardly the time for a baptism by fire.
Janet scrambled back too, pulling the caged kitten along with her.
The lava giant’s movements were deceptive—so huge they looked slow, but it crossed the cavern with terrifying speed. Each stride covered more than a mile. 
[SFX: MOLTEN DROPLETS RAINING]
At the same time, its massive arm swung forward, raining drops of molten rock from its body. 
[SFX: KITTEN SCREAMING]
[SFX: GIANT FIST DESCENDING]
The group dodged and weaved as the giant’s fist, thousands of feet wide, came smashing down, aimed directly at the screeching flame kitten in the Frosty Cage.
