Ep 73 - Unseen Power
Word count: 1197
[AMBIENT: TENSE OUTDOOR ATMOSPHERE]
Something strange was unfolding in the yard.
There were ten ranks to the Nature Level, with a massive power gap between each one.
Leo, Graves, and Roman had all just broken through to the Nature Level and were only at the first rank. Warriors at this level could generate Vitality—an energy they could weaponize to kill an enemy without a sound.
Randall and Jon, two of the Empire's best soldiers, had already reached the fourth rank. At that level, their control over Vitality was incredible, giving Randall a huge advantage over newcomers like Leo. But Randall's real edge was his mastery of the chill intent, which could frostbite an opponent's soul in the blink of an eye.
Jon's mouth twitched. He was only here as a favor to Leo; otherwise, he would've stayed far away from making an enemy as dangerous as Randall. They were both fourth-rank, but Randall's chill intent was a game-changer. If it came to a fight, Jon would have to be on constant guard against that special ability. It would be a hell of a battle.
But what in the world was happening right now? Jon was completely baffled.
Unless he was mistaken, the kid with Leo wasn't anywhere near the Nature Level. He was still at the Marrow Refining Level—he couldn't even release Life Energy, let alone generate Vitality. So where the hell did he get the nerve to challenge Randall?
Liam's jaw was still on the floor. He'd known Zane's background. And yet Randall's chill intent hadn't touched him, and one snort from Zane had put Randall on the ground screaming. Liam couldn't process it.
Leo said nothing. But behind his eyes, something had shifted. He'd thought he had a clear read on Zane. Now he realized he'd still sold the kid short. How many more cards was he holding?
Whatever the answer, one thing was certain: his decision to help had been the right one. The Brandt family might genuinely need this strange, impossible person one day.
Randall was seeing red. To be publicly humiliated by this kid still stuck at the Marrow Refining Level! And Jon had seen the whole thing. If word got back to the other soldiers, Randall's authority would be shot. No. He had to kill this kid to save face.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[AMBIENT: SUDDEN COLD]
As Randall scrambled to his feet, Vitality surged through him, and the air in the yard instantly turned frigid.
Despite the hot summer day, a chill cut right to the bone of everyone watching. The temperature must have plummeted by thirty degrees.
[MUSIC: THREATENING TENSION]
"You really have a death wish, don't you?" Randall growled at Zane. "You picked the wrong guy to mess with. Now you've pissed me off, and you're going to regret it. I'm going to make you suffer before you die."
As he spoke, a terrifying pressure radiated from Randall's body. An instant later, he focused it all on Zane.
[SFX: CRUSHING PRESSURE]
Zane's body went rigid. The pressure felt like a massive iceberg bearing down on him—if it hit, he'd be crushed to pieces. Randall's power was immense.
This was no ordinary Nature Level warrior.
"Jon?" Leo called out, giving him a meaningful look.
Jon got the message. He stepped in front of Zane. In a low voice, he said, "Just let it go, Randall. Beating on a kid isn't going to make you look good."
[SFX: ANGRY ROAR]
"Get out of my way, Jon! He's mine!" Randall roared.
Normally, Randall might have listened. But he was past that now, completely lost in his rage. All he could think about was destroying Zane.
"You think you can do whatever you want just because the Sterlings have your back, Randall?" Jon's face hardened. It was clear Randall wasn't going to be reasonable.
[SFX: ICE CRYSTALLIZING]
"So what if I do? You wanna start something?" Randall clenched his fists. A white frost instantly coated his hands, the air shimmering with a bitter cold. This was his Vitality.
"You think that scares me?" Jon didn't even flinch.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
Seeing the two were about to throw down, Leo's patience finally snapped.
"I'm not in the mood to watch you two posture," he said, his voice laced with annoyance. "Just let Shane go. Jon, if you can't handle this, I'll go get my uncle."
He'd figured Jon could handle it. Randall had a choice—the easy way or the hard way—and it was pretty clear which one he was picking.
Jon's expression tightened. If Leo's uncle got involved, this whole thing would blow up to a whole new level. Jon did not want that. "Leo," he said quickly, "there's no need to bother your uncle with something this small."
"Well, it looks to me like he has no intention of letting Shane go," Leo said with a shrug. "Got any other bright ideas?"
Jon turned back to Randall. "You heard him. Let Shane go, or the general gets involved. Trust me, that's a mess you don't want to be in."
The dragon general was one of the top commanders at Dragon's Keep, and no one knew the true depths of his power.
Randall's fury flickered. He glared at Zane, then at Leo, weighing it up. The Brandt family. The general. He gritted his teeth.
"Fine," he said. "Let him go."
[SFX: CELL DOOR OPENING]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: GASP OF SURPRISE]
Shane looked like he'd seen a ghost. His body was covered in whip marks, but he stood straight, eyes clear. "Zane... dude, you're alive! You're actually alive!" he exclaimed, his voice a mix of disbelief and excitement.
"Yeah, Shane, I made it," Zane replied with a small smile.
"That's incredible... just, wow," Shane muttered.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REFLECTIVE]
During his imprisonment, Shane had been haunted by the vivid memories of his comrades perishing in the Razor swarm attack. The physical abuse he endured behind bars paled in comparison to the crushing weight of guilt that consumed him. Nothing tormented him more than the image of Zane—a young disciple he barely knew—selflessly volunteering to distract the Razorwing Queen, seemingly sacrificing his life just to ensure Shane's survival.
That noble act had left an indelible mark on Shane's conscience, transforming his sleepless nights into a theater of regret and self-recrimination.
Guilt devoured Shane from within, gnawing at his conscience relentlessly. He shouldered the burden of responsibility, tormenting himself with endless recriminations. Had he been more vigilant, had he detected the irregularities in their patrol route earlier, his comrades would still draw breath.
The sight of Zane standing unscathed in the courtyard struck Shane with an overwhelming surge of emotions—disbelief intertwined with exhilaration. How anyone could have survived that massacre defied comprehension.
Zane watched Leo handle it all — a few words, a name dropped, and Randall had folded. That was power. Not strength, not fists — lineage and leverage. Zane had none of that. What he had was himself.
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC]
"Zane, it was Randall all along!" Shane whirled around, his accusatory finger quivering as he pointed at the true culprit. "We never had any quarrel between us! Yet you deliberately altered our patrol route without clearance, condemning my entire squad to death!" His voice rose to a crescendo, eyes blazing with vengeful determination. "Mark my words—I'll sacrifice my last breath before allowing you to escape justice for this treachery!"
