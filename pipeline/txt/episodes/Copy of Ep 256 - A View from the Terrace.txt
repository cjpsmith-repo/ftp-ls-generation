Ep 256 - A View from the Terrace
Word Count: 1023
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
Zane struggled to get his bearings as a murmur rippled through the crowd.
"Holy crap, Richard’s attack is insane. He sent Zane flying with just the Deathly Canticle. He’s way stronger than him. Zane’s definitely losing this round."
"I think it’s worse than just losing. That ’Slay’ glyph... it's got this eerie power. I got a bad feeling just looking at it. It looked like it threw Zane's own force right back at him. That means he just took a hit from his own attack on top of the glyph’s power. I don’t see how he’s getting up after that."
"Oh, I get it! He got slammed by his own ridiculous strength. I hope he can’t get up. Yeah, there’s no way he can stand after that. I’ve lost a ton of money betting against him every round. If he keeps winning, I’ll be flat broke! I gambled on Richard being stronger this time. If Zane goes down, I’ll get my money back and maybe even make a profit!"
"Don’t worry. He’s not getting up. Even if the guy were made of solid iron, he couldn’t take a hit like that."
Indeed, aside from the loyal Misty Peak contingent and the sparse few who’d wagered on Zane’s victory, the vast majority of spectators actively yearned for his defeat. They all silently prayed to the universe that he would remain sprawled on the arena floor, their collective desire for his downfall palpable in the tense atmosphere.
Zane didn’t get up, not from inability but from strategic calculation. His mind raced through potential countermeasures as he lay there, each labored breath a performance to buy precious seconds.
[SFX: LABORED BREATHING]
[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATIVE]
The impact had been jarring—even painful—but far from debilitating. He assessed Richard’s formidable Glyph Casting technique with grudging admiration, recognizing its elegant potency while his eyes, half-lidded to feign greater injury, scanned for weaknesses. The cool stone against his back provided an odd moment of clarity as he deliberately prolonged his apparent defeat, weighing his options with methodical precision.
[SFX: ENERGY FLARE]
After a moment, Zane realized it was impossible. He couldn’t break the technique. Frustrated, he let his own power flare. Purple-black Vitality ignited around his hands, blazing like dark flames in a terrifying display.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY]
Within the roiling purple-black energy, tiny sparks of starlight glinted, hinting at the power of hidden constellations. The sight sent a fresh wave of fear through the audience.
"Whoa—Zane’s using his Vitality? I completely forgot he was an Adept! He hasn’t used it once until now!"
"That's what I've been saying! Some people hide their true strength during these competitions. Why couldn’t Zane do the same?"
"Yeah, but his Vitality... it looks so powerful you can feel the pressure from here. It feels totally unique. I wonder what kind of attunement method he’s using."
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
[MUSIC: TENSE REVELATION]
Everyone was stunned. Since the start of the competition, Zane had only used his raw strength and tough physique to crush his opponents. They weren’t used to seeing him use Vitality. Worse, most of them had bet on Richard. If Zane won, they'd all lose their money.
Only a few had bet on Zane, hoping a newcomer could prove himself. Seeing his performance now, it was their turn to gloat. "I knew it! I knew Zane had an ace up his sleeve! Even though he’s only at the second grade of the Adept Realm, he’s won every single fight. The guy’s the real deal!"
[AMBIENT: TERRACE OVERLOOK]
High on the terrace overlooking the arena, Sir Kenneth was shocked when he saw the Corrupt Vitality gathering on Zane’s hands. He studied the energy, his eyes as sharp as knives, and muttered slowly, "What is that? If I’m seeing this right... the Vitality in Zane’s hands is Corrupt Vitality!"
Master Jasper, next to him, looked just as surprised, his face turning serious. "Yes, that’s definitely Corrupt Vitality! This kid actually mastered it. But how? How did he get into the Celestial Library and learn the Titan Strike?"
"Haha," Kenneth said, a dry, surprised laugh. "Titan Strike has been sitting in the Celestial Library for hundreds of years. In all that time, only three people before him ever managed to obtain it. Two of them failed to master it, and the third was forbidden from even practicing it by Uncle Kent himself. I never expected someone could actually master the Titan Strike."
The "Uncle Kent" he mentioned was the old man Zane had met in the Celestial Library. Kent was a highly respected figure, hence Sir Kenneth’s respectful title.
"The Titan Strike is a Tier Five Cultivation Method. It’s said to be impossible for humans to master," Master Jasper said, a note of envy in his voice. "I have no idea how this kid managed it."
For some unknown reason, they chose not to report this to their leader. They had also blocked several reports about Zane that had shown his incredible talent during his training.
"Sir, very few people can recognize the Titan Strike," Jasper analyzed. "I’m afraid that even if Zane uses the skill in public, no one will know what it is."
Sir Kenneth nodded calmly. "Yes, you’re right. But I’m going to investigate this. If someone has been hiding this information on purpose, knowing how serious this is, I will make them pay."
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Master Jasper was stunned by the vow. Even though it had nothing to do with him, he could feel the chilling killing intent in Kenneth’s words. A shiver went down his spine. He had thought this was just a remarkable display of potential. He never expected Kenneth to take it this seriously. He wondered, just how hard is it to master this Tier Five Cultivation Method?
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
Zane, of course, couldn’t hear their conversation. His knowledge was limited to The Eastern Territory; he had no idea about the legendary history behind the Titan Strike. He had just chosen this technique as a way to smash through Richard’s defense.
And it looked like he just might be able to pull it off.
