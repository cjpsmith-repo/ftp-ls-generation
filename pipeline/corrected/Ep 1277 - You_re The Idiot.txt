Ep 1277 - You’re The Idiot

Word count: 1063

[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]
[AMBIENT: CAVERNOUS HALL WITH THICK FOG]
Damon stood on the far side of the ravine, shouting taunts at Zane and the others, but his words just vanished into the thick fog that blanketed their side. For a long moment, the only reply was silence.
Then, a single voice drifted out of the mist, ghost-like and hollow.
“Idiot.”
Damon froze, stunned. A second later, his face flushed with rage. “How dare you call me that?”
“Idiot,” another voice echoed, this one sounding almost bored.
“You’re the idiot!” Damon roared, his voice booming through the cavernous hall.
“Liar,” Zane’s voice shot back, cutting through the fog. “Didn’t you just say you wouldn’t fight back or trade insults? I called you an idiot twice, and you folded instantly. Why should I trust a single thing you say?”
“I, uh—” Damon was completely blindsided. He’d been trying to get a rise out of them, and now he was the one sputtering, completely thrown by Zane’s simple trap. He had no idea what to say.
“Damon, ignore them,” Larkin’s voice called out from the darkness beside him. “They can’t hide in that fog forever.”
Damon nodded, his confidence creeping back. “You’re right! Let’s see how long you can last!”
A soft laugh came from within the mist. Janet glanced at Zane, a flicker of amusement in her eyes. “I didn’t realize you were so good at running your mouth.”
Zane just shrugged with a small smile. But in the next instant, his left hand shot out, grabbing the woman by the arm and yanking her a step to the right. 
[SFX: HOOK WHIPPING THROUGH AIR]
A heartbeat later, a hook whipped across the ravine, landing exactly where she had just been standing.
Thanks to his reflexes, she was safe.
“That was too close,” she breathed, a cold sweat breaking out on her skin. If that hook had connected, the ten enemies on the other side would have torn her apart. Her story would have ended right there.
“Someone over there can see through my fog,” Jeffrey said, his expression grim. “I don’t know if they’re tracking our sound or if it’s something else.”
It made a twisted sort of sense. Just as Zane’s own eyes could pierce some of the Dark Laws, it seemed one of their enemies had a way to see through his Illusive Fog.
“I can take them out,” Hailey, quiet until now, said softly. 
[SFX: BOW CREAKING]
She gave the Moonburst Flamebow in her hands a gentle lift.
Zane shook his head, a small smile playing on his lips. “No need. I’ll handle the one who threw that hook. But they’ll have other tricks. We need to spread out.”
Since they’d cleared the Second Hall of Causality, Zane had naturally become their leader. No one questioned him; they just trusted he had a plan.
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT DRUMS]
A moment later, Zane was a blur of motion, reappearing in the middle of the fog. 
[SFX: CHAIN RATTLING]
His eyes were locked on the Ogre across the ravine as he gripped the long chain of his own hook. The rules of this place were strange. Once you were caught by a hook, you became weightless and couldn’t fight back. Your energy was sealed, but you were also immune to harm.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
The real fight—and the killing—only started when the hook was released.
Zane’s face stayed perfectly calm. Whatever tricks the Ogre had up his sleeve, one solid hit would end this instantly. He briefly considered shoving Damon straight into the ravine below—the river of energy down there would kill him outright, no contest. But a guy this confident had to be banking on something. Better to play it safe.
[SFX: HOOK LAUNCHING]
Zane’s arm snapped forward, and the hook shot across the ravine before Damon even had time to react. It caught him clean—no armor, no defense—and hauled his massive body over, weightless and helpless.
[SFX: CHAIN RATTLING]
Damon threw back his head and laughed instead of struggling. Rhombus-shaped crystals burst up across his skin like plates of hardened glass, his Vitality condensing into place around him in an instant.
It was Forceful Crystal Armor—the same Transcendent Divine Might his father had spent a lifetime mastering. Anyone who’d earned the five million Illusion Points to even set foot in this Hall was already a monster back in their own city, but Damon had full confidence his armor could weather whatever came next.
[SFX: CHAIN WHIPPING THROUGH AIR]
With his defense in place, Damon spun and hurled his own hook toward Larkin, banking on the armor to buy him the time he needed.
Zane didn’t attack. He only smiled.
Damon caught the smile and clocked it—but he didn’t care. Let him smile. It wouldn’t matter once his armor was up.
That’s why Coleman had thrown his hook the instant Damon had formed his Forceful Crystal Armor. 
[SFX: CRYSTAL ARMOR SHATTERING]
The diamond-like plates, which had cost Damon a massive amount of energy to create, were instantly shattered by the Celestial Laws woven into the hook.
[SFX: HOOK SNAGGING]
[SFX: BODY DRAGGED ACROSS STONE]
The warrior snagged Damon, dragged him across the chasm, and then immediately retreated. He had no intention of facing Damon head-on.
Panic flashed across Damon’s face. His armor, a technique that drained his reserves, had been broken by a simple hook. 
“Forceful Crystal Armor!”
Reacting instantly, Damon ignored the warrior and focused on reforming his armor. 
[SFX: CHAIN WHIPPING THROUGH AIR]
At the same time, he began to swing his own hook, desperately trying to pull Larkin to his side for backup.
But this time, it was Janet who threw her hook from even farther away.
[SFX: CRYSTAL ARMOR SHATTERING]
[SFX: CHAIN WRAPPING TIGHT]
[SFX: BODY SLAMMED TO GROUND]
Damon’s crystal armor had barely solidified when her hook shattered it all over again. The chain wrapped around his muscular torso, and he was yanked off his feet and dragged across the ground.
[MUSIC: RELENTLESS PERCUSSION ASSAULT]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING CHAINS AND IMPACTS]
It became a brutal game of tug-of-war. The moment one hook released him, another latched on, relentlessly dragging him back and forth. His Forceful Crystal Armor kept forming only to be crushed by the power in the hooks, over and over again. He never even got the chance to throw his own hook to save Larkin.
