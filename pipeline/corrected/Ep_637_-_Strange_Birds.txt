Ep 637 - Strange Birds

[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
Over the years, the Dark Nether Cave had claimed countless War Lords, swallowing whole those who gave in to its temptations. But the pull was too strong to resist. A lucky break inside could mean a massive leap in power, and besides, warriors are born fearless, living for the thrill of adventure.
The place was the very definition of high-risk, high-reward. If you wanted to get ahead, you had to be willing to gamble—sometimes with your own life. A safe, quiet life was easy enough to find, but for those who chose the path of a warrior, why settle for being average?
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
The second Jarrod fell silent, the tension broke. Everyone plunged into the darkness, descending toward the cave floor.
[AMBIENT: VAST CAVERN ECHO]
The cavern was impossibly vast, so tall you could see entire mountains towering in the distant gloom, their peaks not even coming close to the ceiling.
[SFX: HEAVY THUD]
[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]
Wind rushed past Zane's ears as he hit the ground with a solid thud. Beside him, Lavender materialized — a spectral warrior armed with a long spear, floating like a phantom at his side. She'd committed to helping him, and she was clearly going all in.
"Are those plants?" Zane's sharp eyes had spotted something black growing on the ground. It didn't make sense. Even a top-level War Lord couldn't withstand the Dark Nether Death Light, so how could a simple plant survive?
Fabian overheard him and answered carefully, "They seem to be resistant to the Death Light. As for how, or what's really going on with them… I'm afraid I don't know either."
Zane let it go. The world was full of mysteries, and he couldn't waste time trying to solve every single one.
[SFX: SHARP SCREAM]
Once all the human warriors had landed, Fabian scanned their surroundings. He was just about to lead the group forward when a sharp scream echoed from behind them.
[MUSIC: SUDDEN TENSION]
"Quiet!" Fabian snarled, his voice a low, angry hiss. "Are you trying to get us killed? You have to be silent in here. Who screamed?"
Even with their numbers several times larger than usual, they'd be in serious danger if the monsters in the outer reaches of the cave heard them coming.
"That wasn't one of us," someone whispered back.
[SFX: MONSTROUS SCREECH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE HORROR ORCHESTRA]
Everyone turned toward the source of the scream. In the dim, red-tinged gloom, a monstrous black bird materialized from the shadows. It was easily a hundred feet long, with a beak that took up half its body. Above that massive beak, two scarlet eyes were fixed on them.
The most terrifying part, though, was the Orc War Lord clamped in its jaws. Half the warrior's body dangled grotesquely, the other half already swallowed. The Orcs had descended from the Tower of Sin, too. Clearly, they'd run into trouble.
There was more than one. In the red-tinged dark, more black shapes were circling — over ten of them, diving and banking in silence. Some of the Orc warriors tried to flee back up toward the Tower's entrance. They didn't make it.
"Shadow-maws," Fabian said through his teeth. His expression was grim. Three years ago, he'd nearly died to three of them and lost four of his War Lords in the process. There were more than three times that number here now.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: FRANTIC CHASE]
"Move!" Fabian ordered, leading the other humans forward.
As the group's leader, he couldn't afford to turn and fight the Shadow-maws. Panic was setting in, and he was one of the few who knew anything about this place. One wrong turn and they'd all be wiped out. He had to lead them down the right path, away from the danger.
Several Shadow-maws peeled off from the Orcs and banked toward the humans.
"Fire!" Fabian snapped.
Two War Lords at the rear spun and unleashed twin torrents of flame. The blasts spread and merged into a dense net of fire that sealed off the space behind them. The Shadow-maws shrieked and veered off, circling the edges of the fire net but refusing to break through.
"They're afraid of fire," Fabian called out. "Keep moving!"
But the reprieve was short. After a few passes, the birds gained altitude, crested the fire net, and came down on the other side.
"Ah!"
Another horrifying scream ripped through the air. A tenth-level War General — one of the Violet Heart Sanctuary's most gifted young talents, given a spot this year because the humans finally had the seats to spare — had been running with his head down when a Shadow-maw took him. Half his body was gone in an instant.
One of the War Lords spun and drew his bow, a Vitality-forged arrow already nocked. He loosed it clean — fast, powerful, aimed straight at the bird's eye.
It didn't even scratch the feathers.
[SFX: ARROW WHOOSH]
[SFX: CREATURE SHRIEK]
The Shadow-maw turned its scarlet eyes on the archer. A single dive and the War Lord was gone.
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD CHAOS]
[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
The faces of the human warriors went pale. These things lived in the Death Light and were practically immune to conventional attacks. Fire was the only thing that worked — and they were running out of room to use it.
[SFX: MONSTROUS SQUAWK]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
A monstrous squawk echoed from above. Another Shadow-maw circled, then folded its wings and dove straight for Zane.
[SFX: DESPERATE SHOUT]
Fabian and Jarrod shouted at the same time, their faces twisting in alarm.
"No!"
"Protect Zane!"
Before they'd left, Dorian had made them both swear an oath: protect Zane with everything they had, even if it cost them their lives. And while Zane was strong, they had no reason to believe he could stand up to one of these seemingly invincible birds.
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]
As the Shadow-maw screamed toward him, Zane braced himself, unsure if he could actually win this fight.
Ten feet away, Yolande stood perfectly still in her wooden clogs, watching the bird approach. She subtly pressed two fingers together, a flicker of power gathering at her fingertips. But then her eyes shifted to Lavender, and she hesitated, letting her hand fall.
Lavender, for her part, looked completely unfazed. As the monster closed in, the long spear in her hand shot forward.
[SFX: SPEAR THRUST]
[MUSIC: POWERFUL CRESCENDO]
The thrust looked almost casual, but it carried a terrifying, silent power.
