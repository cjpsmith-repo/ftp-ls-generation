Ep 133 - The Clash of Realms
Word count: 1162
[AMBIENT: NIGHT SKY]
The moon hung in the sky like a silver platter. With not a cloud in sight, the view was incredible.
Zane glanced around and saw more than a dozen people floating high in the sky not far off. As he drifted closer, he recognized them.
They were the Soul Realm warriors he’d seen at the governor's house.
[AMBIENT: SUPERNATURAL PRESENCE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Floating opposite them were four gigantic creatures, each one radiating a terrifying, evil energy. But this was different from the aura of the Demonic Grunts and their commanders. This was an evil you could actually see—a shimmering, malevolent haze. A chill shot down his spine.
"Their energy is so strong... These have to be Netherlords!" Zane thought.
A Netherlord was the most powerful of all demons, the absolute top tier. In the old days, a Netherlord's appearance meant catastrophic destruction and death on a horrifying scale. Before the Empire was founded, a Netherlord was a nightmare even for the strongest warriors.
An old text told of one that tore a path of destruction through the Eastern Territory, wiping hundreds of cities off the map. It and its minions slaughtered every last person, leaving the entire northwest a barren wasteland, devoid of human life for thousands of miles.
The situation was finally brought under control when the Empire was founded, along with the Skyreach Sect. Over the last thousand years, thanks to new secret skills, Meditation Techniques, powerful treasures, and a wave of gifted individuals, humanity had managed to push them back. Humans in the Eastern Territory fought back, even managing to kill several Netherlords.
Still, no one in their right mind would ever underestimate the strength of a Netherlord. Especially not four of them, working together. Worse, they could amplify each other's power.
The imperial army had fought and defeated demons plenty of times, but they'd never managed to kill this one—a Netherlord named Aldrich. No one had predicted he could become this powerful. A moment ago, even Axel's eyes couldn't track the Netherlord's speed.
Aldrich’s lips curled into a sneer. "Humans… just as delicious as I remember. What do you say? I'll give you one last chance. It's not too late to surrender. Otherwise, I'll eat every last one of you, just like I did that other guy."
[MUSIC: HEROIC DEFIANCE]
"Never!" Axel shot back, his voice like ice. As the governor, he couldn't show a hint of weakness. "Everyone, be careful! His speed is off the charts. Don't let him get close!"
Before Axel could finish, Aldrich was already gone.
[SFX: RUSH OF AIR — IMPOSSIBLY FAST]
He reappeared beside one of the Soul Realm warriors in an instant. Three bone spurs drove through the man's body. What followed was worse than a killing blow — the spurs began to drain him, flesh and blood pulled through them in a slow, horrifying tide. In seconds, the warrior had shriveled to nothing. His skin drifted down through the dark air like a discarded coat.
The silence that followed was absolute.
"His speed has improved," Axel said, his voice tight. "Don't let him get close!"
[SFX: POWERFUL WING FLAP]
But even as Axel shouted his warning, Aldrich moved. With a single flap of his massive wings, he shot forward, a blur of motion no one could track.
One moment, he was gone, the next, he was right beside Yolande Shaw, his claw shooting out to grab her.
"I think I'll start with this tender little morsel!" the Netherlord snarled.
Yolande Shaw's face was a blank mask, but her reaction was infinitely faster than the warrior Aldrich had just swallowed. She flicked a finger, and a Glass Ward shimmered into existence like a shield in front of her.
[SFX: GLASS SHATTERING]
Aldrich's raw power was insane. Zane had thrown everything he had at a similar ward and failed to even scratch it. But when Aldrich's claw grabbed this one, a sickening crunch echoed through the air. To everyone's shock, his talons punched a hole right through the enchantment as he lunged for the girl.
Floating in the air, Yolande watched the Netherlord’s claw reaching for her. She didn't flinch, just blinked her gray eyes. A flash of light pulsed across the fractured ward.
[SFX: SHARP CRACKING]
A sharp cracking sound followed as the ward instantly sealed itself shut. The Netherlord's claw, still halfway through, was sliced clean off.
[SFX: MONSTROUS HOWL]
Aldrich let out a bloodcurdling howl of pain that seemed to rattle the dark sky itself.
[SFX: ENERGY SHARD]
But as the Netherlord howled, another razor-sharp shard of energy shot out from the ward, lancing toward him. It wrapped around his mangled arm, and the light flashed again, morphing into a box that trapped the limb completely.
[SFX: SERIES OF SHARP CRACKS]
A series of sharp cracks echoed from within the box as the light inside pulsed, slicing Aldrich's arm into bloody chunks.
The spectators winced collectively at the ghastly spectacle unfolding before them. Each person involuntarily pictured themselves in the Netherlord's position, silently offering gratitude to whatever deities they worshipped that it wasn't their own flesh being systematically carved apart by Yolande's merciless technique.
An icy tremor cascaded down Zane's vertebrae, settling like a cold stone in his gut. "Killer Girl" wasn't merely a colorful sobriquet—it was a title Yolande had earned through demonstrable, terrifying skill. In this moment, with her emotionless efficiency and lethal precision, she appeared far more frightening than the demonic entity she battled. Her cold calculation made the chaos of battle seem almost orderly.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
Aldrich, his massive frame convulsing with agony, finally comprehended the gravity of his situation. Abandoning his aggressive stance, he retreated hastily, establishing just enough distance to evade her deadly range while maintaining a semblance of dignity. The Netherlord's grotesque visage contorted into an expression of unbridled hatred, his yellowed eyes narrowing to venomous slits as they fixed upon the woman who had so effortlessly mutilated him.
"Woman, I'm going to devour you, bones and all!"
Yolande didn't even react, just stared back at him. She was facing the fiercest of the four Netherlords, yet no matter how many times he attacked, he couldn't get anywhere near her.
[SFX: EXPLOSION]
As they circled each other, Zane heard another explosion. His eyes darted to the source of the sound. It was Axel, the God of Life, swinging a massive hammer right into another Netherlord.
A blow that could have leveled a small mountain only wounded the beast. Its body was unbelievably tough.
“Damn,” Zane thought, sighing. “In a fight like this, all I am is a cheerleader.”
Maybe Aldrich was feeling the pressure from his allies, because his attacks on Yolande grew even more frantic. Her expression was still a calm mask, but Zane could see the strain beginning to show.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER SHATTERING]
"Bang!"
[SFX: BONE PROJECTILE LAUNCH]
Aldrich shattered another of her wards, then unleashed something unexpected—a bluish-gray bone spur erupted from his body without warning. The projectile, however, bypassed Yolande entirely. Instead, it hurtled directly toward Zane, who hovered defenseless in its path.
"Crap!"
[MUSIC: SLOW MOTION DANGER]
Time seemed to slow as Zane watched death streak toward him, the bone spur closing the distance with terrifying speed, its razor-sharp point aimed straight at his heart.
