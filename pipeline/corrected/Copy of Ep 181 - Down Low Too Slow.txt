Ep 181 - Down Low, Too Slow
Word Count: 1105
[AMBIENT: UNDERWATER PRESSURE]
As Zane swam deeper into Emberfin Lake, the Heavy Water pressed against him from all sides. The crushing pressure that had initially threatened to overwhelm him now felt almost familiar, as his Shadow Vitality fishtail propelled him through the dense liquid.
[AMBIENT: DEEP WATER ENVIRONMENT]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS EXPLORATION]
Sunlight barely penetrated these depths, casting everything in an eerie blue glow. The ancient attunement sanctum’s secrets surrounded him---a training ground where countless Acolytes had tested their limits before him, though none quite like this.
With each powerful stroke, Zane felt his body adapting, growing stronger against the immense resistance that would have crushed ordinary cultivators. The silence was absolute except for the muffled movement of water against his ears as he pushed ever deeper into the lake’s mysterious embrace.
It had taken Zane a while just to get six feet under, but once he did, a powerful flick of his fishtail sent him shooting forward like an arrow.
Before he knew it, he was already closing in on the one-hundred-and-fifty-foot mark.
The moment he reached one-fifty, three yellow dots blinked to life in the murky depths near him. They started as specks of light but quickly ballooned in size. As they came into focus, Zane’s eyes narrowed with recognition.
"Three Gillwretches."
Not long ago, facing even a single Gillwretch in these crushing depths had proven a total nightmare. All he could do was run for his life. But things had changed.
Instead of fleeing from the trio of Gillwretches, Zane executed a powerful twist of his shadow-formed tail and launched himself directly toward them.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
The three yellow Gillwretches detected his approach and swiftly arranged themselves into a defensive triangle---one guarding the front, two positioned behind. Their webbed fingers clutched obsidian spears whose razor-sharp tips glinted menacingly in the heavy water, reflecting what little light penetrated these crushing depths.
[SFX: WATER RUSHING]
Zane propelled himself forward with determined focus. As he neared the lead Gillwretch, he executed a violent twist, his shadow-formed tail whipping around easily despite the water’s resistance.
The powerful maneuver launched him into a controlled spiral, allowing him to dodge the spear the lead Gillwretch had hurled at him with terrifying force. In the same fluid motion, Zane pivoted back and grabbed the creature’s enormous, scaly head between his palms.
“Die!” he snarled, bubbles escaping from his mouth as the word dissolved into the depths.\ A Gillwretch’s head was its toughest feature, but Zane’s vise-like grip was a death sentence. This vulnerable point was the one place he could apply his full, monstrous strength.
[SFX: GRUNT OF EFFORT]
[SFX: HEAD BEING TORN OFF]
With a final, savage wrench, muscles straining against the water’s pressure, he tore the creature’s head apart.
[SFX: BURST OF MAGICAL ENERGY]
The Gillwretch died instantly, erupting into a shower of colorful light before dissolving into nothingness.
Of course, the Gillwretches in Emberfin Lake weren’t real. They were just sophisticated holograms projected by the intricate magical array surrounding Emberfin Lake. In these dense, crushing waters, the only truly living things were a handful of shimmering Emberfins.
[SFX: WATER SPLASHING]
The water churned violently as the remaining two Gillwretches lunged at Zane, swinging their spears at him from different angles.
But Zane was already too fast for them. The boy who had floundered helplessly against the oppressive weight of the Heavy Water no longer existed. In his place swam a predator who had figured out this place’s secrets.
A fierce exhilaration coursed through him as he realized the spears’ trajectories posed no threat. His newly formed shadow tail whipped powerfully, launching him straight up with explosive force.
“Too slow,” he thought, a savage smile playing across his lips as he soared beyond their reach.
The sudden vertical movement caught the Gillwretches off guard, unable to track his newfound speed. Suspended momentarily above his opponents, Zane flipped a full one-eighty, his body responding with fluid precision despite the crushing pressure. His eyes narrowed with predatory focus as he angled himself straight down, arms outstretched like talons as he plummeted toward the confused creatures.
Before either Gillwretch could pull back its spear, Zane's hands closed around both shafts.
[SFX: SPEARS WRENCHED FROM GRIP]
One fluid motion — he twisted, reversed them, and drove both black tips straight through their owners.
[SFX: DUAL IMPACT]
Two bursts of colorful light. Then nothing. The water was still.
[SFX: SUDDEN INTERRUPTION]
[AMBIENT: DIFFERENT LOCATION]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE]
The training chamber fell silent as the last ripples on Emberfin Lake’s surface faded away. In the observation gallery overlooking the sanctum, two figures stood watching the display that tracked Zane’s location.
Senior Adept Leo leaned against the railing as he studied the readings, while beside him, a steward from House Lowell shifted uncomfortably, clearly troubled by what they had just witnessed in the depths below.
For disciples from the Great Houses, pride meant more than points from the Skyreach Sect or even a pile of crystals.
But Leo shook his head. “Zane isn’t from one of the Great Houses. He wouldn’t do that.”
[MUSIC: DETERMINED TENSION]
And Leo was right. That kind of pride meant nothing to Zane. Besides, even with thousands of crystals to his name, he hated wasting them. Shelling out two hundred points for just two hours of access already stung. Why would he throw away a single second?
The Array Plate only tracked Zane’s position, not what was actually happening down there. And since the Emberfins weren’t part of the magic array, the plate couldn’t see them either. That’s why Leo and the steward had no clue Zane had stopped---not to give up, but to get his bearings in the Heavy Water and form a plan.
[SFX: WATER RUSHING]
So when he finally started moving again, the Array Plate showed him cutting through the water at an unbelievable speed.
“This\... this is...” The steward's eyes were glued to the lake’s speed indicator, his jaw slack.
Leo was just as floored. He’d stuck around out of curiosity, hoping Zane might pull off something unexpected, but this... This completely blew his expectations out of the water.
It was unbelievable.
Leo himself couldn’t move that fast now, let alone on his first time in the Heavy Water.
[SFX: IMPACT SOUND]
On the Array Plate, the small dot representing Zane zipped across the screen and slammed into the three yellow dots. A moment later, all three vanished.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
Leo and the steward stared in stunned silence. The hand on the nearby clock had barely moved half a tick.
The steward swallowed dryly, finally breaking the long silence. “He’s\... been in here before, hasn’t he?”
