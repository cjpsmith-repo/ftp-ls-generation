Ep 567 - Into the Array
Word count: 1108
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]
Zane and the Ogre War Lord circled each other cautiously.
The Ogre race had a secret method for soul training, but this one hadn't reached the Fighting Soul Realm yet. The War Lord was confident Zane couldn't destroy his soul; he had a secret weapon protecting it. Still, there was a real chance Zane could damage it, so he eyed the incoming attack warily.
But Zane's real problem wasn't the War Lord in front of him. It was the other two closing in from behind.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]
Gritting his teeth, he drew on his forceful energy again, feeling his reserves dwindling. His body blurred into a streak as he shot forward.
The situation was grim—Zane was in deep trouble this time.
The Heartlands had been dangerous, sure, but with the Cloud Hall's protection, he'd always managed to overcome his opponents. Even when they were a full realm stronger, their actual power was usually on his level.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
But this was completely different.
Zane was a seventh-rank warrior. But on this continent, the ranks were different: War General outranked War Fighter, and War Lord outranked War General. That put a War Lord two full realms above him. And to make matters worse, he was fighting three of them at once.
Any ordinary War Fighter would have been killed by now. Zane had somehow managed to run for over a thousand miles, fueled by his Vitality and forceful energy.
But running wasn't going to solve this.
If all three Ogre War Lords came at him at once, Zane knew he didn't stand a chance.
"What do I do?" he thought desperately. Facing what felt like certain death, he reached out to the Cobalt Dragon in his mind.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]
The Cobalt Dragon's voice was calm. If there's truly no other way, I can use my soul to attack and kill them. But I'm afraid it will force my soul back into a deep sleep.
When the Cobalt Dragon devoured the Owl Beast's spirit, its own soul was re-energized. A part of that spirit had been used to awaken its seventh brother. Zane had taken a small piece to create a soul seed, and the dragon had consumed the rest. The Owl Beast's spirit had a powerful soul, but for a True Dragon, it was barely a snack.
At this point, the dragon's suggestion was the only option left: one more soul attack.
But if the Cobalt Dragon fell asleep again, it would become nothing more than a lifeless statue on the soulforge.
No! Zane couldn't risk it.
Then our options are limited, the Cobalt Dragon said.
He was still turning it over in his mind when a voice cut through the wind, quiet and clear.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
"There are three teleportation arrays twenty-five miles ahead, to your left. Take the middle one. It will get you out."
It was Phoebe.
Zane didn't fully trust her. But falling into the Demon Night's hands was still better than being dragged to the Ogre Holy Land. He didn't have time to think twice.
[MUSIC: FAST-PACED ACTION THEME]
He banked hard and changed direction.
The terrain shifted beneath him — jagged mountains, dense forest, everything blurring past at speed. Then he saw it. A mountain that looked as though it had been split clean in half by a single sword stroke, the top half lying on its side, the bottom sheared flat. And on that flat surface, three circular patterns.
Three teleportation arrays, side by side.
Zane's eyes narrowed. Three arrays meant three destinations. Phoebe had said the middle one. But what if it led straight to Demon Night territory?
He took a breath. In this situation, he could only gamble.
[SFX: BODY HITTING GROUND]
[SFX: ROLLING IMPACT]
He dove without slowing, hit the ground hard, and rolled onto the middle array. Before he'd even steadied himself, a dozen top-grade Spirit Stones flew from his Storage Ring. He drove them into the array with a wave of his hand.
[SFX: ARRAY ACTIVATION HUM]
Blazing white light began to pulse and spin.
"Come on—"
The War Lords arrived above the mountain almost instantly. The light was still building. Zane stood in the center of the array, watching three enormous shadows descend toward him.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
The white light swallowed him whole.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: NIGHT ATMOSPHERE]
Phoebe couldn't understand what Lily saw in Zane. She had to get to the bottom of it, which was why she'd followed them.
"But that young man doesn't trust me," Phoebe mused. "He could have run straight to us at Demon Night. Once Elder May gets here, those Ogres will be dead meat. He's only a human War Fighter, but damn, he's fast. At least he's safe for now."
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY]
With that thought, she gracefully leaped into the teleportation array. The people of Demon Night always kept their cool, no matter the situation.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND]
The small teleportation array could send someone ten thousand miles in an instant. It didn't take long for Zane to shoot through the vortex and reappear on the other side.
As the blinding white light faded, a desolate world spread out before him.
Gray plains stretched out as far as he could see, utterly devoid of color. The whole barren landscape looked lifeless, as if nothing had ever grown here. What is this place? he wondered.
[SFX: MAGIC HEALING ENERGY]
With a bitter smile, Zane crushed another supreme Aether crystal, feeling the energy replenish his reserves. He gathered his strength, preparing to leap into the air.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
But just as he was about to take off, an incredibly powerful force slammed him back down to the ground.
The surge of power was so overwhelming that he couldn't fight it.
Such a powerful restraining force… it must be a No-Fly Ban!
A chill shot down his spine. He was getting more confused by the second. No-Fly Bans were usually restricted to sects. Why would there be one this powerful out here in the middle of nowhere? What in the world is this place?
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: THREATENING DRUMS]
Just then, the array flared to life behind him. Three enormous figures stepped through the blazing white light—the same Ogre War Lords who had chased him for more than a thousand miles.
Zane's face darkened. Even the teleportation array hadn't been enough to shake them.
His only option now was to put his life on the line and fight with everything he had.
