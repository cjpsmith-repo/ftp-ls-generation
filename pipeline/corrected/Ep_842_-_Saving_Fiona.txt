Ep 842 - Saving Fiona

Word count: 997

[SFX: ARROWS WHISTLING]

[AMBIENT: BATTLE TENSION]

The air filled with a deadly hiss as arrows rained down, their shafts and tips shimmering with Dragon Arsenic. A single scratch from one of these would be enough to kill an ordinary warrior on the spot.

But this group was anything but ordinary. They were a collection of saint-level geniuses, each with their own unique ways of staying alive. As a storm of poisoned shafts descended from all sides, they dodged and weaved, and remarkably, only a few fell.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Fiona, however, wasn't so lucky. She’d been backed into a corner with nowhere left to run.

She was the princess of the Wind Divine Kingdom, a title that made her more important than your average crown prince. In any other situation, warriors would be tripping over themselves to save her, hoping to earn the favor of her kingdom. 

But this situation wasn't normal; it was desperate. No one was about to throw their life away against a hail of Dragon Arsenic arrows, especially not these. The thing was, they didn't just stick in you—they were enchanted to speed up on impact, drilling into their targets like vicious, poisoned corkscrews. Trying to redirect one would only send it straight through her faster.

“Fiona!” Callan yelled, his voice cracking with desperation when he saw her predicament. They had been in a relatively safe spot near the front, but now she was completely pinned down, arrows closing in from every angle.

A wave of hopelessness washed over Fiona as her eyes darted around, searching for an escape that wasn't there.

[SFX: MAGICAL VIBRATION]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TENSION]

Thinking fast, she felt the red jade bracelet on her wrist begin to vibrate, unleashing a mysterious wave of Law Power. Time Law! Maybe I can stop them!

[SFX: WHOOSHING ARROWS]

It was a useless gesture. The arrows were already enchanted with a Time Law far stronger than her own. Her power washed over the battlefield, but the arrows didn't slow by a fraction of a second. When two masters of the Time Law clash, the stronger one simply overpowers the weaker. Instead of stopping the arrows, Fiona’s power was twisted and redirected, slamming into the warriors around her and slowing their movements to a crawl.

Realizing what she’d done, she yanked her power back in a panic. She’d almost gotten them killed. It was no surprise when she heard a few of them spit her name like a curse. 

She was out of options. Time Law was a bust, and she didn't dare risk using another skill.

Despair settled over her like a weight.

She thought of her father. He'd tried to give her a divine-grade protective garment before she left — close-fitting, virtually impenetrable. She'd refused it. She always felt he coddled her too much, that she'd never grow if she kept hiding behind his protection. It had felt like the right call at the time.

She understood now that she'd been naive. The arrowheads were close enough that she could see the blue shimmer of the Dragon Arsenic on their tips, advancing toward her like the slow sweep of a scythe.

[SFX: ARROW IMPACT]

[SFX: FLESH TEARING]

Just as the first arrows were about to find their mark, two of them blasted clean through Zane’s hands before vanishing without a trace. His flesh was supposed to be as durable as a Relic-Class weapon, yet the arrows had punched right through. It was hard to even fathom the kind of power they carried here, in the Slow Arrow Field.

He hadn't pushed the lodestone back to her. He'd crossed space and come back for her directly — abandoning the stone when they were only a few dozen feet from the exit.

“Jones! What are you doing!” someone screamed from behind. “We were almost out! Push the stone, you idiot — do you want to kill us all?!”

Zane rolled his eyes. They didn't seem to understand their own situation. He didn't particularly care about strangers. But Fiona had left an impression on him — specifically the moment before War King York's strike, when she'd offered him her semi-divine weapon without hesitation. He wasn't going to let her die for it.

He had grabbed the tips of the two arrows on the right — not the shafts, the tips. Grabbing the shaft would have triggered the acceleration enchantment and sent them straight through her. Grabbing the tips let him absorb the force and redirect it, using the impact to knock her clear. It worked. She stumbled out of the kill zone. The arrows punched through his palms and disappeared.

Fiona could only stare, completely stunned. She was safe, but the man who had just saved her was surely doomed.

[SFX: LIQUID DRIPPING]

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS SILENCE]

Dark blood gushed from the wounds in Zane’s palms as the blue-tinged Dragon Arsenic spread like ink through his bloodstream. Within seconds, the poison had raced from his wrists, up his arms, and into the rest of his body.

“Stop staring!” one of the warriors shouted. “He's a goner! We have to push the lodestone ourselves!”

“Zane can't help us!” another yelled. “Everyone, on the lodestone, now! And be careful—don't touch him, or the poison will get you, too!”

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]

The other warriors knew they couldn't count on Zane's monstrous strength anymore. He was poisoned and as good as dead. It was up to them to move the lodestone, or they’d all die right here.

Five or six of them rushed forward, slamming their shoulders into the massive stone and pushing with everything they had. But for all their combined might, the geniuses barely managed to budge it a foot. It was nothing—a pathetic display compared to what Zane had done all by himself.

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

While the others grunted and struggled, Callan, Fiona, Randy, and Camilla couldn't look away from Zane. Randy and the others said nothing. After everything they'd seen him do, they'd long since stopped questioning his decisions.

The poison has spread through his whole body, Fiona thought, her mind reeling, so why isn't it corroding his flesh?

A few feet away, the exact same question was echoing in Callan’s head.
