Ep 200 - Lopsided Battle
Word count: 1130
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
The ornate hall fell silent as Yates Moon, his face flushed with indignation, stepped forward. Crystal chandeliers cast dramatic shadows across his features as he pointed his spear accusingly at Zane.
“You dare humiliate me in my family home?” Yates snarled. “No one disrespects House Moon and gets away with it!”
Yates’s jealousy radiated palpably; Ingrid’s obvious preference for this Misty Peak nobody had shattered his carefully cultivated composure.
Without waiting for a response, he drew his spear, the weapon gleaming dangerously in the candlelight as party guests hastily retreated to create an impromptu arena amidst the silk-draped tables.
[SFX: CROWD WHISPERS]
"Whoa, Yates has gotten even better! Look at that---the energy glowing from his spear is a good two inches longer than it was before."
"Yates is a natural! He just made inner disciple, and he’s already improving at a scary-fast pace."
"That kid from Misty Peak is about to have a very bad day. Yates is going all out. All twelve of those strikes are aimed right at his vitals!"
Yates's crew from Eagle Peak whispered among themselves. As fellow inner disciples, they all knew each other's moves.
Meanwhile, Ethan, Rhys, and Dustin had stepped back, not looking the least bit worried about Zane.
And why would they be? Ever since they'd met him, Zane had a knack for taking down opponents who were supposedly way out of his league. The usual math of judging someone by their power level just didn’t add up when it came to Zane.
They still had no clue how powerful Zane really was. But they knew one thing for sure: anyone who underestimated him was in for a rude awakening.
[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]
Zane stood perfectly still, a calm smile playing across his face as he observed Yates’s desperate advance. The tension in the air was palpable, but Zane remained unbothered.
Yates’s spear was a blur of motion, cutting through the air with remarkable precision. Most onlookers gasped at the display---that kind of speed was extraordinary for a Fourth-Level Adept, a testament to years of rigorous training and natural talent.
But that's only how it looked to the untrained eye.
To Zane’s heightened senses, Yates’s movements unfolded with almost painful slowness, like a performer executing an elaborate dance in suspended time. Each muscle twitch, each shift in balance revealed itself with crystal clarity.
The corner of Zane’s mouth twitched slightly as he suppressed a chuckle at what Yates clearly believed was his masterful technique. The gap between them wasn’t merely one of skill---it was a chasm of fundamental understanding.
[SFX: RAPID SPEAR STRIKES]
In a flash, Yates jabbed his spear at Zane twelve times, each strike aimed at a critical point that would leave him crippled.
Zane knew that even with Yates’s speed, the twelve strikes couldn’t possibly land at once. He had all the time in the world to dodge and counter.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
Just as the twelve spear points were about to connect, Zane vanished in a blur.
A vicious smile twisted Yates's face. “Got him! Go to hell!” he thought, a thrill shooting through him at the idea of running Zane through. "So what if Ingrid likes you? Doesn’t matter now. You’re dead!"
[SFX: SPEAR STRIKES MISSING]
But to Yates's shock, the satisfying thud of his spear hitting flesh never came.
Not a single one of his twelve strikes had even grazed Zane, let alone made contact.
He was just now realizing that all twelve thrusts had pierced Zane’s afterimage, not the man himself.
He wouldn't have been surprised to hit an afterimage once or twice, but all twelve times? How?
Impossible! Zane couldn’t be that fast!
It was no wonder Yates could strike so quickly. His spear was light as a feather, and his Tier Four Cultivation Method gave him enough power to punch through a brick wall.
But since every strike whiffed, the real question was how Zane was moving so fast, weaving between every stab from every angle.
James watched his son’s performance with mounting disappointment. "Yates is no match for him. They’re not even in the same league," he thought to himself as he watched the lopsided fight unfold.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Yates’s frustration and rage finally boiled over. The energy radiating from his spear grew longer, thicker, more powerful than before. But the added power cost him speed. Instead of aiming with precision, he started stabbing wildly, a storm of desperate thrusts.
[SFX: STABS THROUGH AIR]
[SFX: FAST MOTION]
Zane was still a phantom, darting back and forth at an incredible speed, weaving through the whirlwind of spear strikes.
After a furious flurry of nearly fifty thrusts, it finally dawned on Yates: no matter how hard he tried, he wasn’t going to land a single hit. 
Zane glanced at him. "The most important thing for a warrior is a calm heart. Especially with a fast technique like yours — you need to be calm while moving. But your heart is already in a mess."
"Shut up!" Yates screamed. "I don't want to hear it!"
He attacked again, faster and harder than before. But his speed had actually dropped. When he was calm, twelve strikes in a blink. Now, barely eight.
[SFX: FRANTIC SPEAR STRIKES]
In the back, James watched. Zane had just described a principle it had taken James forty years to understand — and he'd said it mid-fight, like it was nothing.
His son was completely dominated by anger. James said nothing.
With a raw-throated roar, he swung his spear in a wide, desperate arc, aiming to sweep Zane off his feet.
Yates screamed, his voice cracking. "I'd like to see you dodge this!"
[SFX: SPEAR IMPACT]
But Zane didn’t even try to dodge. He simply reached out and caught the shaft of the spear mid-swing.
Yates yanked back, but the spear wouldn’t budge. It felt like it was fused to Zane's grip.
It was only then that the terrifying truth hit him: Zane's strength was on a completely different level.
"What level is he?" Yates’s mind screamed, his eyes wide with horror. Zane wasn’t just faster; he was impossibly strong. How could a First-Level Adept be this powerful? It was completely unfair!
In Yates's mind, a practitioner's power was supposed to match their rank. That was the only thing that made sense. He knew power level wasn’t everything, but it was supposed to be a guidepost. A rule. Otherwise, what was the point of having ranks at all?
But with Zane, the entire system just fell apart.
[SFX: VIBRATING METAL]
Zane tightened his grip on the spear and gave it a slight shake. A violent vibration immediately shot up the weapon and into Yates’s arms.
Zane had learned this vibration technique from the Gillwretch in Emberfin Lake. As his hand quivered, the lightweight spear shaft began to resonate at the same frequency.
[SFX: SPEAR CLATTERING]
The intense vibration instantly numbed Yates's hands. He couldn’t hold on. The spear clattered to the ground.
The crowd watched, breathless with anticipation of what would come next.
