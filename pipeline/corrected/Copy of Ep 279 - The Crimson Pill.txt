Ep 279 - The Crimson Pill

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE]
The force of Sir Kenneth’s blow resonated through the cosmos, shaking the very foundations of Heaven and Earth as if reality itself trembled at his might.
A slender beam of yellow light erupted from his clenched fist, cutting through the atmosphere like a divine arrow as it rocketed towards Saint Viana across the vast sky. Every spectator stood transfixed, their collective gaze following that singular streak of vengeance.
Despite his status as a Soul Realm master, Saint Viana’s composed demeanor had shattered into naked terror. He channeled every last drop of his Vitality reserves into this desperate flight, his body accelerating to speeds that defied belief.
In the blink of an eye, his figure diminished to a mere speck against the backdrop of distant clouds, his silhouette barely distinguishable even to the keenest observers.
But Kenneth was relentless. The golden light from his strike seemed to tear through the fabric of space with impossible precision. It closed the vast distance in an instant, homing in on the fleeing master with unerring accuracy.
[SFX: EXPLOSION]
Those with the sharpest eyesight watched in horror as the light slammed into Saint Viana. He simply... exploded, a shower of blood raining down from the heavens.
Even from that incredible distance, Kenneth had killed Saint Viana with a single punch. A cold dread washed over the crowd. Kenneth’s power was beyond anything they could have comprehended.
Saint Augustus was rooted to the spot, the color draining from his face as he stared at the bloody mist that was once his comrade.
For a fleeting second, he considered running. If they split up, maybe one of them could make it. But he crushed the thought just as quickly. It was childish. Absurd.
[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
Kenneth's eyes bored into Saint Augustus. "Augustus," his voice was ice. "You’ve done much for the Sect over the years. And Viana forced your hand. For that, you'll only be punished."
[MUSIC: TRANSITION]
Thanks to Zane's shocking performance, the disciples of the Skyreach Sect finally had a true measure of the young fighter's strength.
Before his match with Johanson, people thought Zane might have a shot at the top ten. No one expected him to actually beat Johanson, a personal disciple favored to make the top five. And nobody could have imagined that Zane wouldn’t just beat Johanson, but utterly dominate him at his own game: assassination.
In the eyes of the other disciples, Zane's power had become immeasurable.
But for Zane, none of that mattered as much as the three words Kenneth spoke to him.
"You’re not bad."
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL HOPE]
Sir Kenneth was the leader of the Skyreach Sect, practically a god in the Eastern Territory.
There was once an emperor of the Empire, a man named Kane, who was stronger than Kenneth. But ever since Kane vanished, Kenneth had been the undisputed number one fighter in the Eastern Reaches.
To hear "You’re not bad" from the number one fighter in the entire region... it was the highest honor Zane could imagine.
Now that he had earned such high praise from Kenneth himself, who would ever dare look down on him again?
Still, even with Kenneth's stamp of approval, no one actually thought Zane could win first place in the All Peaks Competition.
But there was no denying it now: Zane was a powerhouse. The disciples had to admit he was strong enough to be ranked among the absolute best in the Skyreach Sect.
[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT GROUNDS]
After the interruption, the tournament resumed, and Zane made his way back to the base of Misty Peak.
Master Warren and the other disciples from Misty Peak had been sick with worry, forced to watch helplessly. With the leader and the Saints present, they hadn’t dared to utter a word.
They all held their breath until they saw Zane land safely on his feet. As he returned, they watched with envy as the leader handed him a pill. 
Zane turned the pill over in his hand. It was small and black, and he couldn't identify it.
Master Elwood appeared at his side. "Take it," he said quietly. "It's a Pill of Crimson Essence. It will clear any internal damage and strengthen your blood." His tone was matter-of-fact, but there was something in his expression — and in the faces of the two or three elders nearby who had recognized it — that told Zane this was no ordinary reward.
They had no clue what the Pill of Crimson Essence was, but if it came from the leader, it had to be an unbelievable treasure.
[SFX: PILL BEING CONSUMED]
Finally, Zane sat down and took the pill.
The Pill of Crimson Essence had a coppery tang, dissolving on his tongue the moment he touched it. The scent of blood was so strong he felt like he was gulping it down fresh, not swallowing a pill.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT INTENSIFIES]
After a few minutes, Zane could feel a thrumming pulse throughout his body. His face grew hot, turning a deep red as blood surged through his veins at twice its normal speed. The pill was definitely working.
It was designed to boost his vitality and circulation. He knew the rapid blood flow was good for the body, and the faster it pumped, the bigger the boost.
As the pill’s effects peaked, Zane felt the blood practically boiling in his veins.
But it wasn’t painful. Instead, a surge of pure energy flooded him from head to toe.
He smiled, taking a deep, satisfied breath.
[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT ARENA]
[MUSIC: ENERGETIC COMPETITION]
While Zane was meditating and absorbing the pill's power, the tournament continued in full swing.
When Zane's name came up in the next draw, the referee paused. Zane was still sitting with his eyes closed, deep in the pill's effects. A reward from the sect leader — wasting even a fraction of it felt like a transgression. The referee hesitated, considering whether to skip the round.
Zane opened his eyes.
"Don't break the rules for me," he said. "I've finished refining it. I'm ready."
The referee's expressionless face softened slightly. "Then please proceed."
[SFX: CROWD STIRS — SURPRISED MURMUR]
To the other disciples, the assassination attempt was just a shocking detour in the All Peaks Competition. Zane's performance had left them stunned. It was a complete surprise that he was a master of soul attacks. Even Johanson, who had honed his soul for years, couldn't take the assault, so a fighter like Jose never stood a chance.
Training the soul was a monumental task.
Most fighters would go their entire lives without ever touching that level of skill.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
In the next few rounds, whenever an opponent was matched against Zane, they forfeited the second the match was called. So, Zane's next few matches became a quiet procession of him walking to the arena, watching his opponent immediately surrender, and walking right back to his seat.
[MUSIC: TENSE FRUSTRATION]
Tracy was practically vibrating with frustration.
After several rounds of drawings, she still hadn’t been matched with Zane.
She'd even considered asking someone to rig the drawing so she could finally face him.
But after what happened, even a fool could see the match between Johanson and Zane wasn’t a coincidence. The two Saints had orchestrated the whole thing to kill Zane. At this point, even with her special status in the Sect, no one would dare help her rig the drawing.
Each time it was her turn, she would whisper his name over and over, praying he'd be her opponent. But time and again, the name on the slip was never his.
Soon enough, though, it would be her time. And then Zane would see exactly what she was made of.
