Ep 237 - Predator Becomes Prey
Word Count: 1213
[AMBIENT: BATTLEGROUND]
Langdon took pride in his formidable strength. It defined him, shaped his reputation, and fueled his confidence in battle.
When he channeled his full might into a swing, even seasoned warriors of the Soul Realm approached with caution, respecting the devastating power behind his blows. For someone like Zane---merely a youth who had barely ascended to the second-grade of the Adept Realm---being ensnared in Langdon's notorious Deadly Swamp Trap represented nothing short of a death sentence.
[MUSIC: HIGH STAKES BATTLE]
With determination flashing in his eyes, Zane threw up his hands in a desperate attempt to block the hammers before they could crush into him.
"You're dead meat!" Langdon roared, his voice echoing across the arena as a smile spread across his face. The familiar rush of impending victory coursed through his veins.
He scoffed inwardly at the spectacle before him. What kind of idiot would try to catch his legendary hammers with their bare hands? These weren't ordinary weapons---each carried enough concentrated force to pulverize Zane's entire body into a bloody mist. Langdon savored the moment, already imagining the satisfying crunch of bones beneath his copper hammers.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
[MUSIC: SURPRISING REVELATION]
Zane's hands darted out with lightning precision, catching the hammers mid-swing.
Langdon stood paralyzed, his jaw dropping from shock. The opponent he had dismissed as insignificant hadn't just attempted something foolhardy---he had succeeded with alarming ease. Cold sweat beaded on his forehead as the implications sank in.
"Impossible!" Langdon gasped, his voice cracking with a mixture of fear and astonishment. The certainty that had fueled his confidence moments ago evaporated like morning dew. "No one can stop those hammers bare-handed!"
"No way!" exclaimed a spectator from the stands, leaning forward with widened eyes. "That kid's a monster, too." The words rippled through the crowd, transforming their earlier derision into stunned respect as they witnessed the unthinkable unfold before them.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
"So that's how a second-grade Adept made it into the All Peaks Competition," a student murmured. Langdon was famous for his raw strength; even warriors in the Soul Realm couldn't afford to face him head-on. But Zane had just stopped his hammers with nothing but his hands. Just how strong was this guy?
Swinging the hammers was one thing. Catching them was another.
Even Langdon, for all his arrogance, would never dare try to catch his own hammers once they were in motion.
The fact that Zane had actually pulled it off meant only one thing: his strength surpassed Langdon's by a wide margin. Langdon swallowed hard, an icy dread creeping into his gut as the realization dawned on him---this might be a battle he couldn't win.
What troubled him even more was that he had hurled those bronze hammers with every ounce of his power. Such an attack should have shattered the bones of any warrior at the peak of the Adept Realm foolish enough to block them bare-handed. Yet there stood Zane, utterly unscathed, as though the devastating blow had been nothing more than a gentle tap.
"What is this guy made of?" Langdon wondered.
The impact had taken nearly a third of Zane's power, but he'd calculated the risk with precision. His body, forged through countless trials, had withstood the blow exactly as he'd anticipated.
He lifted his gaze to meet Langdon's bewildered stare, a confident smile spreading across his face. The corners of his eyes crinkled slightly, betraying a hint of satisfaction at his opponent's shock.
To Langdon, that expression resembled nothing so much as the grin of a predator who had cornered its prey. His stomach twisted with a mixture of fear and indignation.
Strength had always been the bedrock of Langdon's identity, the foundation upon which he had built his reputation and pride. The very notion that someone else might surpass him in this domain was unthinkable, an assault on everything he believed about himself.
Rage surged through his veins like molten metal. His massive frame trembled with barely contained fury as his fingers tightened around the handles of his hammers. The thought of being humiliated by someone he had dismissed as inferior ignited something primal within him---a desperate, burning need to reassert his dominance.
[SFX: BATTLE CRY]
"Wild Striking!" Langdon roared.
[SFX: WHOOSHING WEAPONS]
His hammers became a blur, a chaotic storm of bronze.
[SFX: METAL IMPACT]
But no matter which way Langdon swung, Zane batted the hammers away like they were nothing.
For Zane, this was just practice.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: THE BATTLE INTENSIFIES]
Each time his hands connected with the bronze hammers, a warm current of energy sparked to life inside him, pooling in his gut. The energy was making him stronger. He could end this fight whenever he wanted, but why rush it? He just kept defending, parrying the hammers with his bare hands.
[AMBIENT: CORNER OF THE ARENA, DISTANT CROWD NOISE]
In the far corner, Patrick sat cross-legged with his eyes closed, pale yellow Vitality drifting around him in slow, shifting shapes. Tracy stood beside him, watching the fight.
"His defense — how does it compare to yours?" she asked.
"His body refinement is impressive, but it has a ceiling. Still, a Second-tier Adept with that kind of body. Interesting."
Tracy's smile didn't waver. "I'd like to fight him." Something in her eyes made it sound less like curiosity and more like a promise.
Langdon's clan wasn't rich or powerful; it was nothing compared to the Seven Great Houses.
Good-quality hammers were hard to come by. Getting his hands on two top-grade Mythic weapons was next to impossible. He knew he couldn't afford to lose these hammers and just buy new ones. His only option would be to find a master smith to repair them.
He had to win. A loss here meant losing more than just the fight---it would cripple his combat ability. If his hammers were destroyed, he had no idea what he'd use in his next match.
"Rolling Lightning Hammers!" Langdon shouted.
He suddenly dropped into a low crouch, sweeping the hammers in a vicious arc toward Zane's legs.
He was betting Zane's legs weren't as ridiculously tough as the rest of him.
So far, all his attacks had been aimed at Zane's chest and head. Rolling Lightning Hammers was designed to attack an opponent's less-guarded lower body. If this landed, Langdon was sure Zane's legs would shatter.
Then, Zane would have no choice but to beg for mercy.
The move was sudden. Langdon saw a flicker of surprise on Zane's face and felt a surge of hope.
But before he could press his advantage, Zane's fists shot down like pistons.
[SFX: LOUD IMPACT]
Zane's iron fists slammed into the bronze hammers, driving them deep into the arena's reinforced floor. The impact created two deep craters.
Langdon yanked, trying to pull his hammers free, but they wouldn't budge. Zane had them pinned as if they were fused to the stone.
The participants of the All Peaks Competition were all strong-willed; none of them gave up easily.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC BATTLE]
Realizing he couldn't win without playing his trump card, Langdon made his choice. He abandoned his weapons. With a roar, his Vitality erupted from his body, instantly crystallizing around him into a suit of earthy yellow armor.
"Earthen Armor!"
