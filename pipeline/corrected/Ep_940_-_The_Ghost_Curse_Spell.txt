Ep 940 - The Ghost Curse Spell

Word count: 1034

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: DARK ATMOSPHERIC]

Vesper had tried to cross over to the main continent before, but the Cursed Land had apparently scared him off. His workaround was to build a massive teleportation array linking the two continents, and then he just waited in his celestial tomb for years until Zane finally showed up.

That’s what led Zane to believe Vesper had to be a Spirit Supreme Realm expert. If a being that powerful couldn’t get past the Cursed Land, then the seven of them didn’t stand a chance.

Zane had walked right into the Cursed Land before without any issues. But that was back when he was only in the Soul Realm. Now that he’d reached the Life-and-Death Realm, he knew the curse would be a whole different story.

As Zane gave them a quick rundown of what they were facing, Max’s expression hardened. Still, he gave the order to keep The Compass Wheel moving forward.

They flew swiftly over the main continent until, before long, a sharp line cut across the landscape below. On one side of the divide, the world was alive and normal.

On the other, it was pitch-black, devoid of plants, creatures, or any sign of life.

“So this is the Cursed Land,” Max asked, staring down in awe.

Zane nodded. 

Delroy spoke up, “Let's go down and take a closer look.”

As Spirit Supreme Realm experts, they were far from reckless. They knew better than to just charge into a place like this on their own.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: DARK ATMOSPHERIC]

The Compass Wheel descended slowly, and the seven of them stepped off one by one. Zane followed last, the Ancestral Dragon still cradled in his arms as he jumped down. The little creature’s eyes went wide with fear as it took in the blighted land below, clearly terrified that Zane was about to carry it into that darkness.

[SFX: DRAGON WHIMPER]

Seeing the little creature trembling, Zane gently stroked its head. “Don't worry,” he murmured. “I'm not taking you in there.”

Still, Zane was curious. The Ancestral Dragon had only just hatched. Despite its innate power, it was still just a level-one beast, so the curse shouldn't have any effect on it. The fear, he figured, must be pure instinct.

Meanwhile, Max’s brow was furrowed as he surveyed the border. “Delroy, what’s your take?”

Delroy took two steps forward until he stood right at the edge. One more pace and he'd be inside. He paused — then reached his hand across the border into the Cursed Land.

“Be careful,” Zane said. “The curse is powerful. I watched it dissolve two War Sages into nothing.”

Delroy didn't pull back. He was testing it — seeing how long the curse took to act and how strong it was.

He didn't have to wait long.

His expression shifted. He yanked his hand back and retreated fast. “Dorothea!”

[SFX: MAGICAL PULSE]

The flower in Dorothea's right eye spun rapidly. A pale pink light bloomed from it — and under that light, what had been clinging to Delroy's hand became visible.

[SFX: FLESH DISSOLVING — WET SOUNDS]

[MUSIC: HORROR STING]

Finger-sized ghosts. Hundreds of them, packed together, swarming over his hand and biting. His protective Vitality and barrier meant nothing — the flesh was already turning to pus, liquefying slowly where they fed.

“Cut it,” Delroy said, his voice flat. He glanced at the young man in black.

The young man in black didn't hesitate. His black blade flashed.

[SFX: BLADE SLASH]

[SFX: BONES CLATTERING]

Delroy's hand — already stripped to bone — dropped to the ground. The ghosts swarmed the bones, gnawing them clean in seconds. Then they scattered, disappearing back into the darkness of the Cursed Land.

The young man in black stared at the spot where they had vanished. “What in the—“ He visibly shuddered. The ghosts themselves weren't powerful. But they had passed straight through a Spirit Supreme Realm barrier as if it weren't there.

[SFX: REGENERATION SOUND]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL THEME]

Delroy raised his stump. Golden blood welled from the wound, flowing steadily, taking shape — bones first, then meridians, then skin. In moments, a hand had formed, indistinguishable from the original. He flexed it once and said nothing.

Zane watched in silence. In the lower realm, regrowing a severed limb always left a flaw. Delroy had done it without one.

[AMBIENT: CURSED WASTELAND]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

It was a hard-and-fast rule: the Cursed Land was off-limits to anyone at the War Sage or Soul Sea Realm.

“The Ghost Curse Spell has a specific target,” Dorothea said, her tone flat. “It’s not that complicated.”

“Then what’s the plan?” the burly man demanded. “How are we supposed to get to the Tower of Sin?”

Delroy’s gaze remained locked on the Cursed Land. “I refuse to believe this entire place is blanketed by the Ghost Curse Spell.”

A sly smile touched Dorothea’s lips. “You’re about to find out, aren’t you?” 

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY BURST]

The moment the words left her mouth, the flower in her right eye began to spin. It cast a pink light that exploded outward, a hundred times wider than before.

In the sudden wash of pink light, Zane, Max, Delroy, and the others all froze.

[AMBIENT: GHOSTLY WAILS AND WHISPERS]

[SFX: SUPERNATURAL WRITHING]

The Cursed Land was teeming with an endless tide of ghosts. They were packed so tightly they writhed against each other, a squirming mass of spectral forms. It was a chaotic crush of bodies, with ghosts consuming one another in a desperate frenzy. But as soon as one was devoured, another would materialize to take its place.

[MUSIC: PANIC STRINGS]

The young man in black visibly shuddered, his voice tight with panic. “Dorothea, turn it off! Turn off the light!”

He wasn't the only one. Every face—except for Max’s and Dorothea’s—was a mask of pure horror.

Even Zane, the only one in the group who had actually been inside the Cursed Land, felt a cold knot tighten in his stomach. 

He’d walked through this place before, believing it was empty. He never would have guessed the void was actually packed shoulder-to-shoulder with ghosts. 

A deep, primal chill snaked down his spine.
