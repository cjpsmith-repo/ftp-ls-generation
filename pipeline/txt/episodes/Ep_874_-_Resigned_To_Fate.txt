Ep 874 - Resigned To Fate

Word count: 1104

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

For years, the Union of Divine Kingdoms had managed to coexist, their fragile peace overseen by the Humans from the upper world. The other kingdoms had always tolerated the York Divine Kingdom, mostly thanks to a secret pact and a mysterious figure known only as the Little Monster. The entire continent functioned like one big, sprawling, and occasionally dysfunctional alliance.

But now, War King Wind had finally had enough. He just couldn’t stand by and watch War Emperor York’s behavior any longer.

Of course, Fiona was part of it, too. He’d seen the way she looked at Zane, the worry in her eyes. That settled it.

[SFX: INTENSE HEAT WAVE]

A wave of intense heat rolled off War King Wind, a clear sign he was about to make his move.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]

Around them, whispers broke out.

“He looks furious. He’s not messing around this time.”

“This is the second time something like this has happened. It’s been ages since the kingdoms had a real fight.”

“Is War Emperor York actually going to do something about it?”

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]

The Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom stepped forward, his voice calm but firm. “York, if you can’t control the Black Star, you should give it back to Zane.”

Scott moved to stand beside the Emperor, a silent show of solidarity. For the first time, it seemed no one was on York’s side.

[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]

Fury flashed across War Emperor York’s face, but he didn’t hand over the pickax. Instead, his body flickered, and he shot straight for the pyramid. He wasn’t about to admit defeat. Even if he couldn’t control the Black Star, he had to try. It was a divine-class weapon, after all—it had to be leagues stronger than any normal relic.

Seeing him rush toward the forty-fifth floor, the other rulers could only sigh. “Forget it,” War King Wind grumbled. “If he wants to try, let him.”

[AMBIENT: EERIE PYRAMID INTERIOR]

[SFX: INSECT WINGS FLUTTERING]

York slipped through the narrow door on the forty-fifth floor and landed on a massive leaf where a purple Stone-Eater Bug was resting. The creature chirped and flapped its wings, ready to pounce, but it never got the chance.

[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCH]

York threw a punch, sending an invisible shockwave of power through the air that blasted the bug away. Normally, he might have been interested in capturing a creature like that, but with the Black Star in his hands, he couldn’t be bothered.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BUILDUP]

He took two steps forward, raised the pickax high, and with a determined glint in his eyes, swung it down with all his might.

[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT CRASH]

A tremor ran through his Soul Sea as he poured every ounce of his Vitality into the strike.

The force was enough to shake the entire tree.

The leaf didn’t crack. Not even slightly. Its surface remained smooth and unmarked, as though York hadn’t touched it at all.

York stared at the leaf for a long moment. Then he gritted his teeth and let out a slow breath. He knew what it meant. Without the Law of Causality behind the strike, the pickax was just a heavy piece of metal. And the Law of Causality would only answer to its master.

This thing isn’t meant to be in my life.

He jumped down from the leaf and dropped back to the pyramid’s edge. Scott and War King Wind exchanged a glance. Neither of them said a word. Neither of them needed to.

[SFX:WHOOSH]

York’s expression was thunderous. He gripped the Black Star Pickax one last time — then hurled it at Zane with every ounce of force he had left, a sharp whistle cutting through the air as it flew.

“Zane, be careful!” Fiona called out.

Zane’s eyes flashed. Three thousand Dragon Scales surged through his body in an instant. He raised his hand, extended two fingers, and caught the pickax cleanly as it arrived.

[SFX: IMPACT ABSORBED]

The Soul Sea masters watching went very still. They had all felt the force behind York’s throw — enough that even they would have needed to brace for it. Zane had caught it with two fingers, his expression unchanged, as though he’d plucked a leaf from the air.

Their expressions shifted through several changes at once.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane smiled faintly and turned back toward the pyramid. He didn’t look at York again.

He went straight to the forty-fifth floor. That was where the egg-sized stones were — fourteen of them, each carrying enough Raw Vitality to fully restore Tia. Everything else could wait.

The moment he stepped onto the forty-fifth-floor leaf, Soul Sea masters flooded in behind him, filling the leaves of the forty-fifth, forty-sixth, and forty-seventh floors. The ores up here were sparse, but nearly every one of them was a treasure — materials for Mythic weapons, or divine ones.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

On the leaves below, the powerful Soul Sea Realm masters watched, chattering among themselves with anticipation.

“Is one of their members dying? Do they need a life crystal?”

“No idea. He’s not from our union, anyway.”

“I wonder if the Black Star can actually break that leaf.”

“I have my doubts. It’s supposed to be a divine-class weapon, but no one’s ever seen what it can really do.”

For years, the top three layers of the giant tree had been like transparent treasure chests with no key. Now that the key was in their possession, everyone was hoping for a miracle.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

But where York used brute force, Zane understood the artifact required something else entirely. As the focus shifted to him, excitement flashed in his eyes. He knew this would work. He approached the leaf and gave it a light tap with the pickax.

[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY PULSE]

He used almost no physical strength, but as the Black Star swung, a mysterious power rippled out from it—the Law of Causality.

[SFX: SHARP CRYSTAL PING]

A sharp ping echoed in the silence.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

The masters held their breath. The power was simple, yet absolute. They knew instantly it was a force they could never hope to resist, something that could pierce through anything.

The moment the pickax touched the leaf, the invisible force field protecting it began to crack. This was the leaf’s own Law of Causality, its absolute defense. When the two forces met, the Black Star’s law simply overwrote the leaf’s.

[SFX: GLASS SHATTERING]

With nothing more than a light tap from Zane, the leaf began to shatter, breaking apart layer by layer.
