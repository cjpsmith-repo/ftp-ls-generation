Ep 790 - The Fall of a Lord

Word count: 1012

[AMBIENT: ROYAL PALACE]

[MUSIC: IMPERIAL THEME]

 As the favorite son of the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom, Prince Randy was used to getting his way. He was a First-Tier talent, and if another warrior didn’t feel like quitting, well, that was just too bad for them. The entire kingdom was his father’s playground, and no one living there was stupid enough to defy the royal family. Offending them was a death wish.

That’s just how it was in the divine kingdoms. Compared to the leaders of sects, the royals were infinitely more arrogant and ruled with an iron fist. Even mighty warriors from the Soul Sea Realm treated Randy with the utmost respect, all because of his name—never mind that he was only at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

 But Zane didn't show a flicker of fear. He just kept walking forward as the red circle shrank, his eyes locked on the prince in the center.

Seeing this, Randy just smiled. “What about you?” he asked, his tone laced with amusement. “Giving up?”

They’d only crossed paths twice, but Randy could already tell Zane was the proud type, confident in his own strength. He trusted his judgment on people, and he knew a guy like this wouldn't back down easily.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]

 Of course, Zane wasn’t giving up. He’d come all this way for the ancient heartstones to save Tia, and it had been anything but an easy journey. Still, he wasn’t sure he wanted to fight Randy right here, right now.

From what little he’d seen, the prince was definitely not weak. Randy had only made one move against him earlier, but it was enough for Zane to guess he was probably a little stronger than Esther Sterling. He hadn’t seen Amber fight in the Tower of Sin, but he figured Randy was on par with her when she’d first become a top-level War Lord two hundred years ago. It was just a rough estimate, of course. He had no idea what Randy was truly capable of.

Despite the uncertainty, Zane felt no fear. He’d killed Maddox, a warrior from the Life-and-Death Realm. He seriously doubted this prince could be stronger than that.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 The real problem was that this was only the first round. He couldn’t bring himself to go all-out unless his life was on the line. Zane’s mind raced, trying to figure out how he could win this without showing everyone his hand. 

If Randy knew what Zane was thinking, he probably would have passed out from pure rage. Luckily for him, he couldn’t read minds. All the prince wanted was to test Zane’s strength for himself.

Time ticked by, and the red circle kept shrinking. Zane never stopped walking, closing the distance between them.

Thirty feet. Twenty. Ten.

For warriors at their level, a distance this short rendered half their attacks useless. They were practically breathing the same air, staring each other down in a silent battle of wills. 

[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]

 As they closed to within five feet of each other, Randy's lips curved slightly. A thread of pale blue Vitality appeared — and then, beside it, a second thread emerged. Two completely different energies. One born from within his own body. The other drawn from heaven and earth.

[SFX: ENERGY FUSION]

 And then they merged.

Zane's eyes sharpened. Even with everything he'd seen, he'd never encountered this. Two fundamentally different types of energy fused into one seamless force. What kind of cultivation technique allowed for that?

[SFX: POWER SURGE]

 He'd planned to meet Randy with his Vitality alone — enough for any late-stage Spirit-Tribulation warrior. But he immediately revised that calculation. He reached for the Dragon Scale.

Half its power. That would have to be enough.

[AMBIENT: HEAVY BREATHING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

 This was about to become a contest of pure, raw strength.

[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]

 Randy spread his palms and pushed forward — no punch, no technique. A pure test of force. Zane answered the same way, both hands forward, his Vitality erupting alongside the Dragon Scale's power.

[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACT]

 Their palms met.

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

[SFX: STONE CRACKING]

 The sound was dull, almost quiet. But the patterned floor beneath their feet — the same floor that had survived an entire battle between thirty Spirit-Tribulation warriors without a scratch — split apart. Cracks raced outward from the point of contact in every direction.

Even though the Kingdom had plenty of princes, Randy’s talent made him special. He’d been showered with endless resources his whole life, giving him knowledge that far surpassed anyone else’s in the kingdom. 

And that knowledge told him one thing: Zane’s power was completely unnatural for a warrior at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. Even someone who had clawed their way through a life-or-death crisis would struggle against a force like this. How was he doing it?

*[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]*

[AMBIENT: OCEAN WAVES]

 It felt less like a person’s energy and more like an ocean tide pressing in on him—unstoppable, immense, and threatening to drown him where he stood.

Though he was overwhelmed, Randy refused to back down. He’d boasted too much to retreat now. He was determined to see this through. Part of him wished Zane would just step out of the circle and admit defeat. He’d even personally give the guy a Harlen Token. After all, that was the whole point of this exam.

It was just one token!

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]

 But it was too late for that. Pinned by Zane’s suffocating power, he had no other choice.

So, under the stunned gaze of the entire crowd, Randy retreated.

One step. Two steps. Three.

Without a single flashy move, he crossed the red line. But that wasn’t even the most shocking part. As he kept backing away, his footing grew unsteady. 

[SFX: BODY FALLING TO FLOOR]

 And then, in a final, humiliating moment, his legs gave out and he collapsed right onto the floor.

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD]

 Everyone stared, completely speechless.

Randy, a First-Tier talent, was considered practically unbeatable among his peers. In the entire Crystal Martial House, only Edwin could match him. Beyond them, across the whole Harlen Divine Kingdom, only the mysterious man from the Divine Kingdom was said to be his equal.

This hadn't been a fight to the death. It was just a simple contest of raw strength.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS CONCLUSION]

 And in that contest, Randy had been utterly and completely crushed.
