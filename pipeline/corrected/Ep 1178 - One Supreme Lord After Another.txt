Ep 1178 - One Supreme Lord After Another

Word count: 982

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING THROUGH VOID]
A giant hand shot out of nowhere, snatching Maverick and Tanya from the air. 
For all their power, they were helpless to resist as its grip closed around them.
Tanya’s face went dark. The hand had her pinned, her machete trapped with her. 
[SFX: ENERGY CHANNELING HUM]
She channeled her energy into the blade, and *Slash* erupted in a circular screen of light, pushing outward against the colossal fingers even as Maverick unleashed his own power, struggling to break free.
“Who are you?” Tanya yelled. “We’re with the Demon Night, on orders from our queen!”
A sneer echoed from the sky. “The Demon Night, huh? Then get lost.”
The voice was followed by action.
[SFX: LIGHT SCREEN SHATTERING]
The giant hand simply *pinched*, and the screen of light shattered with a deafening crack. 
[SFX: POWERFUL THROW WHOOSH]
Then, with a casual flick, it flung them both into the air.
The power of a Supreme Lord was on another level entirely.
[SFX: TUMBLING THROUGH SPACE]
That one casual toss sent them tumbling through the sky for tens of thousands of miles before their momentum finally bled away, leaving them drifting in the vast emptiness.
They exchanged a grim look. They were some of the strongest World Lords alive, but against a Supreme Lord, they were nothing. 
The gap was just too vast. Without a Crown of Destiny—without the Heavenly Destiny it bestowed—you could never reach that level of power.
“So, what now?” Maverick asked.
Tanya gave a cold snort. “Our queen and Supreme Lord Gunter have to be on their way. Even if this new player gets the Crown, he won’t be able to escape with it.”
She knew it took time to absorb the Heavenly Destiny from a Crown. Even if this Supreme Lord gave it to one of his own followers, they couldn’t assimilate its power instantly. The process required communing with the Heavens, rooting the warrior to the spot. If he just grabbed the Crown and ran, he might get away, but every other Supreme Lord in the universe would hunt him down. 
A Crown of Destiny was never a private affair; its appearance always drew the great races into a feeding frenzy. They were both frustrated. If the queen had just sent them after Zane from the start, instead of wasting Cassian, Maren, Taryn, and Paige on the task, this would all be over by now.
[SFX: BLACK SMOKE DISPERSING]
Meanwhile, the black smoke that had produced the giant hand began to clear. From it stepped a broad figure clad in black armor, eyes wide as bells, body massive and imposing. He glanced at the miniature Elysian Keep as it fled into the distance and gave a sinister sneer. Then the black smoke rolled back around him, and he gave chase, moving faster than before.
Inside the Keep, Zane watched through the crystalline viewscreen as the tiny projection shot toward the surface of the pale yellow star ahead. At his direction, it dove straight for the desert below and burrowed in.
Above ground, the enormous figure of the Supreme Lord materialized from the black smoke, looking down at where the Keep had disappeared into the desert. His voice reached Zane through the walls.
“I don’t know which clan you’re from, little guy. All I want is the Crown of Destiny. Hand it over, and I’ll spare your life.”
Zane said nothing. The Keep stayed still.
[SFX: GIANT HAND FORMING AGAIN]
The black smoke thickened. A second giant hand began to take shape, spreading wide to envelop the area.
But before the hand could close, something cut across its path. Blue threads appeared from nowhere, weaving fast, crossing and knotting into a dense net that spread itself directly in front of the descending hand. 
The hand pushed through — threads snapping one by one under the pressure — but held long enough for a second net to drop silently from above, settling over the Supreme Lord himself.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: DESERT WIND]
Back in the desert, shimmering blue threads began to weave together in midair, slowly forming the delicate figure of a beautiful young woman. 
[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION]
As the threads solidified into flesh and fabric, she offered a slight, knowing smile to the Ogre Supreme Lord watching her.
[SFX: DEEP GROWL]
“Zenia! You’re asking to die,” the Ogre Lord growled.
[SFX: PLAYFUL LAUGH]
“Easy there, Addison,” Zenia replied, her smile widening playfully. “I’m just here for the Crown. No need to get so bent out of shape.”
The Ogre Supreme Lord—the renowned Addison, one of the oldest and most powerful beings in the universe—was not amused.
Zane had heard of Supreme Lords his entire life. Now, for the first time, he was looking at one through the viewscreen — and the weight of it landed differently than he’d expected. 
Even at this distance, even through the Keep’s walls, there was something in Addison’s presence that made his heart beat faster. That gaze. Whatever law it carried, it pressed against the world itself. He filed it away. He also couldn’t help wondering: in a universe this vast, how had Addison found the Crown so quickly?
Zane’s heart sank. He’d never expected two of them to show up at once. He had absolute faith in the Keep’s defenses, but the thought of facing two Supreme Lords at the same time was staggering.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]
Just then, a ball of purple fire erupted with enough force to scorch the heavens. 
[SFX: SAND CRYSTALLIZING]
A wave of heat washed over the desert, turning the sand to glass as a figure stepped out of the inferno.
[SFX: BOOMING LAUGH]
“I always thought I was fast,” he boomed with a laugh, “but I never figured you two would beat me to it!”
*Another one?* Zane thought, his heart skipping a beat. A bitter smile touched his lips. The lure of the Crown was even stronger than he’d imagined. In the span of a few moments, three Supreme Lords had arrived. He couldn’t help but wonder how many more were on their way.
[SFX: SPATIAL DISTORTION]
[MUSIC: ESCALATING DOOM]
And right on cue, as the thought crossed his mind, a fourth figure began to materialize in the sky.
