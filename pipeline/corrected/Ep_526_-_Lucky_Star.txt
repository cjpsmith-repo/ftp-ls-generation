Ep 526 - Lucky Star
Word count: 1214
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The Light of Water looked deceptively fragile, like a thin shield of water that would shatter with a single touch.
The elder had put eighty percent of his power behind that spear.
An attack from a Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior could shatter a mountain, but it couldn't even scratch this enchanted barrier.
"The force just... spread out. Across the whole barrier," the elder exclaimed. "This thing is incredible! Whoever made it was a master."
The moment his spear met the shield, a gentle force from within seemed to absorb the impact, diffusing the power of his strike across the entire barrier and allowing it to hold strong without a single crack.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
Spurred on, the other elders unleashed everything they had on the Light of Water.
Blades, swords, fists, and spears—each elder hammered it with their own signature techniques.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACTS]
A kaleidoscope of brilliant light exploded against the barrier, sending ripples across its surface. From a distance, it looked like nothing more than a gentle breeze stirring a shimmering lake.
But no matter how hard they struck, the enchanted barrier held firm.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
[MUSIC: AWE-INSPIRING CHOIR]
Everyone froze, mouths agape, staring in disbelief at the undamaged shield.
Multiple Spirit-Tribulation elders had given it their all, and they'd failed.
[AMBIENT: TENSE MURMURS]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
"I've never seen anything like it," one elder mumbled. "A barrier in the Heartlands holding off attacks from six Spirit-Tribulation warriors at once… Not even the Ethereal Spirit Sect's protective array is this strong."
"It can block our attacks, sure," another elder said, his voice laced with worry. "But is it enough to stop Maddox?"
Just because the barrier could withstand their combined assault didn't mean it could stop Maddox. He was on a whole other level—a Life-and-Death Realm warrior who’d survived three of its trials. His power was in a different league entirely.
[SFX: CREAKING DOOR]
Suddenly, the door of a thatched hut in the corner of Cloud Hall rattled and creaked open.
The small hut was Elder Imogen's seclusion chamber, off-limits to everyone but Tia.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]
Elder Imogen emerged from the hut, leaning on her walking stick. She seemed to move with a deliberate slowness, but it was an illusion. In just a few steps, she was floating in the air high above Cloud Hall.
"Master," Tia said, gliding toward her the moment she appeared.
The elders bowed respectfully. Her appearances were rare, but she was the bedrock of their community.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
The leader nodded, her gaze briefly flicking to Zane. Then, in a flash, she was at the edge of the enchanted barrier, pointing her walking stick at its shimmering surface.
At her level, her true essence remained almost entirely concealed. The strike appeared casual—an old woman tapping her cane. Yet everyone felt it: a bone-rattling pressure that seized their hearts.
Every eye was fixed on that walking stick.
[SFX: BARRIER SHUDDER]
The barrier shook. Massive ripples tore outward from the impact point.
Elder Imogen drew back and struck again.
[SFX: SECOND IMPACT]
The barrier surged like a windswept lake but held firm. Not a crack.
A soft laugh escaped her.
"Not bad at all. I used everything just now, yet this barrier had reserves left." She glanced at Zane, warmth touching her expression. "Zane is Cloud Hall's lucky star."
She continued, almost to herself. "People with great luck fall into two kinds. Some carry fortune lightly—it spills onto those around them. Others burn bright but draw luck inward, leaving none for anyone else. Luck cannot be predicted."
"But I believe Zane belongs to the first kind. His presence will bring Cloud Hall fortune."
[MUSIC: GRAND DECLARATION]
[AMBIENT: MURMURING ELDERS]
The words carried weight. Elder Imogen had staked her authority behind Zane. She held no official title, but as Grand Elder, nobody wanted her as an enemy.
From this moment, causing trouble for Zane meant crossing both of them.
With the array in place, Cloud Hall breathed again. The shadow of Maddox had hung over them for weeks. As one week became two, then a month, then three, Maddox never appeared.
[MUSIC: FOCUSED CULTIVATION THEME]
Zane used the time well. Secluded in Cloud Hall's private cultivation chamber, he threw himself back into training with the same focus he'd poured into the array. By the time he emerged, three months had passed — and he had climbed three full levels, stepping out into the light as a seventh-level Soul Realm warrior.
He hadn't come out on his own. Tia had summoned him.
[AMBIENT: ICE PALACE]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
At the Ice Palace, Tia handed him a name card bearing the World Merchants' Guild seal. All warriors possessing an Enchanted Lamp were summoned to the Lantern Festival—five days away.
"Five days?" Zane looked up.
Tia nodded.
Only they knew the Enchanted Lamp was in Tia's possession. The Guild had issued a general summons at the Martial Tournament; now they were ensuring no one forgot.
Zane read further. His eyes sharpened.
The Guild had listed treasures believed hidden inside the celestial tomb—calculated, they claimed, by one of their ancient masters.
"The Flower of Life and Death," Tia said quietly before he reached it. Urgency flickered beneath her steady gaze. "That's what I need. The Immortal's Bone and Nine Nether Lantern Grass are listed too, but they're not why I'm going."
The Flower of Life and Death. Three words that could drive every Heartlands warrior to madness. Beyond the Soul Realm, every breakthrough hit a wall. But past the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, that wall had teeth—crossing into the Life-and-Death Realm meant walking the line between living and dying. Many never survived.
The Flower could help Tia break through that threshold. More than that, it could help Elder Imogen survive her next life-and-death trial.
That was why Tia wasn't willing to leave it to chance.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
The Cobalt Dragon's seventh brother spoke in Zane's mind."You know, Zane, that Immortal's Bone would be perfect for you. If you're serious about mastering the Transcendent Divine Might, you'll need that bone. It's key to building the pathforged realm."
"The Immortal's Bone?" Zane's eyes lit up for a second, but his expression quickly turned doubtful. "The treasures are tempting, I'll give you that. But something feels… off. Doesn't this whole thing seem strange to you, Tia?"
"Strange?" Tia tilted her head slightly.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]
"Think about it," Zane explained. "Vesper was an immortal. He built this tomb for himself. The last thing he'd want is a bunch of people raiding his final resting place. So why do these Enchanted Lamps just magically appear? And how did some old merchant from the World Merchants' Guild know exactly what treasures were inside?"
Tia was quiet for a moment before asking, "You think it's a trap?"
"It's a possibility," Zane said with a nod.
The rumor was simple: light all the Enchanted Lamps, and the holders gain entry to the celestial tomb. But it was just too convenient. Who made the lamps? Why scatter them across the Heartlands? The whole setup had been bizarre from the beginning.
In fact, Zane wasn't the only one with doubts. For hundreds of years, people had whispered that the whole thing was a scam, or worse, an elaborate trap.
But a warrior's path is paved with risk. Trap or not, the promise of the tomb's treasures was more than enough to draw countless fighters to its doorstep.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
As they were talking, a powerful pressure suddenly washed over Cloud Hall.
[SFX: TENSION STING]
The expressions on Tia's and Zane's faces changed in an instant.
