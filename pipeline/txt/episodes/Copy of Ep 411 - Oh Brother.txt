Ep 411 - Oh, Brother

Word count: 1148

[AMBIENT: COLD WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]

Thick clouds shrouded the peak of Cloud Hall. The palace at the summit wasn’t made of brick or stone; it bore no resemblance to ordinary architecture. Instead, it looked as if it were sculpted from a single, massive block of mysterious ice, as though some ancient craftsman had carved it from the heart of a frozen star.

The ice itself was crystal clear, radiating waves of chilling mist that would defy even the most scorching summer sun. The cold emanating from those walls wasn't merely physical—it carried an otherworldly quality that made the air itself seem brittle. Anyone foolish enough to approach without protection would feel their blood freeze within seconds, transforming them into a lifeless statue before they could even scream.

[SFX: ICE CRACKLING]

Tia lounged with languid grace, her bare feet dangling over the edge of an ice-carved throne. A faint smile played across her lips as she gazed at the tall Scrying Slate hovering before her. The smile didn't quite reach her eyes—those remained sharp, calculating, touched with something between amusement and irritation. A small ice phoenix nestled in her arms, its crystalline feathers fuller and more brilliant than ever.

A middle-aged man in green robes stood beside her throne, his posture rigid in contrast to her casual pose.  His expression remained grim, his weathered features betraying his discomfort with whatever scheme was unfolding.

"Alright, Morphen. You're up," Tia said, her tone deceptively light.

Saint Morphen was one of the Cloud Hall's Elders, a master of spatial and illusionary arts. He and Emanuel Drew were considered the twin pillars of the Cloud Hall, equals in both power and cultivation, though their temperaments couldn't have been more different. Where Emanuel embraced authority with iron conviction, Morphen preferred wisdom tempered by caution.

He looked at Tia, his voice laced with hesitation. "Are we sure about this?" 

Tia shot back, wrinkling her nose. "What's the problem? That kid is so infuriating. I've been dying to teach him a lesson for a while now!" 

"He might have taken half the treasures from the Cloud Road, but he's still one of our own," Saint Morphen reasoned, shaking his head. "You said it yourself—the boy has a once-in-a-generation talent, practically a saint-level genius. He earned what he took. We should be nurturing that talent, not punishing it."

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Tia’s eyes narrowed into icy slits, her voice turning cold enough to match the room. "I know what I'm doing, Morphen. Just do as you're told." 

A wave of pressure washed over Saint Morphen. He nodded stiffly. "If you truly believe this is the best way to train him, then I won't stand in your way."

In truth, Saint Morphen wasn’t worried about Zane’s safety; he knew the boy would be fine. It was Tia’s attitude that concerned him. She's just so young, he sighed to himself.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TENSION]

He raised his hands. Ten points of light flared to life at his fingertips, then stretched into white-hot tendrils of energy that shot out from the ice palace and plunged into the world below.

[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]

Meanwhile, Zane stood frozen, staring at the lingering image of Tia where her projection had vanished. Something about her parting words gnawed at him. "A gift? What gift?" he asked the empty air, his voice tight with suspicion. "Can you be a little more specific?"

[SFX: ENERGY WAVE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The words had barely left his mouth when a ripple of raw energy washed over Zane—invisible to the others, but unmistakable to him. Every instinct honed through countless battles suddenly screamed danger. His muscles tensed.

Then his body stopped belonging to him.

He felt it first as a wrongness — thin lines of force threading into his muscles, cutting him off from his own limbs. His legs moved without his permission. He cursed Tia's name silently as his body turned and walked, then broke into a run, crossing Cloud Hall on its own while he raged helplessly inside.

[SFX: HEAVY DOOR KICKED OPEN]

His foot connected with the arena door hard enough to slam it against the wall. Then his arm rose — middle finger extended at the assembled disciples — and his own voice came out of his mouth: "A bunch of trash. Who dares to challenge me?"

The arena went dead silent. Zane wanted to disappear.

Then, as suddenly as it had begun, the control released. He rolled his wrist, feeling his fingers again.


Finn and the other disciples exchanged confused looks, their expressions questioning Zane's sudden shift from casual conversation to coiled readiness.

Zane straightened, his hand instinctively moving toward where his sword would normally rest. 
He turned to leave, already calculating the fastest route out of the arena, but a loud sneer from behind stopped him cold.

"Think you can just run your mouth and walk away?" The voice dripped with contempt. "Not a chance!"

Zane's jaw tightened. He recognized that voice.

It was Marcus Drew. Emanuel's son.

[SFX: HEAVY DOORS SLAMMING]

[MUSIC: ESCALATING TENSION]

With a flick of his wrist, the heavy doors to the Proving Ground slammed shut.

"Ha! You've gotta be kidding me," Marcus scoffed. He stalked toward Zane. "An Adept among the Cloud Hall's elite? That's a rare sight. And you've got the nerve to challenge us all? You arrogant punk. You wanted to know who had the guts to take you on? Well, you're looking at him!"

Zane held up his hands, trying for a placating smile. "Whoa, hold on. That wasn't me just now. I can explain."

Frank's voice carried across the space as he called out to his brother. "Hey, Marcus, that's Zane Lowell. He's the guy who took first on the Cloud Road this year!"

[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]

A murmur rippled through the crowd. They might not have met him personally, but the name carried considerable weight. Everyone had heard the rumors about the new disciple who was supposedly a powerhouse despite his low cultivation level. 

"Oh," Marcus said, his voice dropping to a dangerous register. He took a step closer, studying Zane with narrowed eyes. "So you're the one who pissed off my father. Don't bother trying to explain anything. I'm not interested in your excuses."

Frank stepped in front of his brother, placing a restraining hand on Marcus's chest. The gesture seemed protective, almost patronizing. "Marcus, you don't even have to lift a finger. Let me handle this." He rolled his shoulders, loosening them as if preparing for a casual sparring session. "I'll put him in his place. These small-town hicks come here thinking they own the place. Someone needs to knock them down a peg or two."

[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]

Zane stared at the two brothers, his mouth hanging slightly open. Heat crept up his neck—not from embarrassment, but from rising anger. He'd been about to protest, to explain that he was being set up, that this entire situation was orchestrated by forces beyond his control. But after hearing Marcus's comment about his father, something inside him snapped. He clamped his jaw shut, grinding his teeth together. The urge to defend himself evaporated, replaced by something colder and far more dangerous.

Fine. If they wanted an arrogant asshole, he'd give them exactly that.

"You want to teach me a lesson?" Zane's voice was pure ice, each word deliberate and measured. His brow furrowed as he met Frank's condescending gaze without flinching. "Funny. I was just about to say the same thing to you."
