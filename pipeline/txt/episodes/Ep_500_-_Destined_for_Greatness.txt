Ep 500 - Destined for Greatness

Word count: 1142

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]

Everyone has a weak spot.

For Zane, that weakness was his younger sister, Elara. He would have sacrificed his life for her without hesitation, without regret. The mere thought of harm coming to her could shatter his otherwise impenetrable resolve.

His dream burned with singular intensity: to reach the pinnacle of the martial path and unravel the deepest secrets of their world. His focus was absolute, his determination forged in the fires of tragedy and loss. Nothing could shake his resolve when it came to cultivation.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

But Eden… Eden was a different story.

His background couldn’t have been more different from Zane’s. Zane had clawed his way up from a brutal, abusive childhood, scarred by his uncle's betrayal and his father's murder. The memories of those dark years still haunted him in quiet moments. For Zane, martial arts wasn't merely a path among many options—it was the only path to a better life, the sole means of protecting those he loved and avenging those he'd lost.

Eden had never known such desperation, such hunger born of suffering. He was the son of Maddox, the top-ranked warrior in the Heartlands. As the man's only child, Eden had been born with every advantage: incredible talent, sharp insight, and resources beyond imagination.

With his talent and his father's backing, it was a foregone conclusion that Eden would one day rule a sacred land—maybe even a divine kingdom.

That level of entitlement was exactly why Eden, a warrior at the mere Inner-Core Realm, had the audacity to propose to Tia.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]

In his mind, greatness wasn't merely a possibility—it was his birthright. He was destined for it.

That upbringing forged an unshakable self-confidence in Eden. The other young elites of the Heartlands appeared to him as nothing more than a bunch of wannabes playing at power. He looked down on every single one of them.

Challenges didn't frighten him; that unwavering confidence was the engine for his martial achievements. After all, no one in his generation across the Heartlands could touch him. If Zane hadn't appeared, Eden would have walked a clear path to becoming the strongest warrior of his era. The future had seemed so clear, so inevitable.

But people like him always harbor a fatal flaw: they cannot handle failure. A single defeat shatters their entire world like glass beneath a hammer.

Eden was a brilliant warrior. The harder he trained, the stronger he became. But all that effort rested on a foundation of superiority. The moment someone actually defeated him, that foundation would crumble. He would begin questioning everything—wondering if all his hard work had been meaningless, if his father's pride in him had been misplaced, if the destiny he'd always believed in was nothing but a comforting lie.

[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]

Zane surpassed Eden in every way: mastery of Laws, equipment—two Relic-Class weapons versus none—and talent. Eden's only advantage was cultivation level, which felt like a mockery given Zane's youth.

Eden's world was shattered. His pride, confidence, and arrogance—everything he'd built his identity upon—crumbled into dust. The martial spirit he'd cultivated so carefully fractured under the crushing weight of defeat, splintering like ice beneath a boot heel.

Then he saw his father approach.

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: GROWING DANGER]

Maddox materialized on the arena floor, his sturdy frame radiating the immovable strength of ancient mountains. Each footstep reverberated through the stone like thunder, announcing his arrival with terrible finality. He fixed cold, merciless eyes on Zane, and the temperature in the arena seemed to drop.

Tia moved without thinking, positioning herself between them. Icy mist flickered around her feet, responding to her rising alarm.

Three World Merchants' Guild leaders materialized simultaneously, their faces etched with grim determination. They understood the mathematics of this confrontation all too well. Maddox stood as the Heartland's strongest—unbeatable in single combat. Together they might prevail, but the cost would be catastrophic. Blood would stain these stones, and not all of it would be Maddox's.

"Maddox, surely you won't attack a junior at the Inner-Core Realm?" Kieran asked, injecting every ounce of moral authority into his voice, hoping to shame the man into restraint.

Maddox ignored them and snorted dismissively at Tia. "You are very bold!" His voice carried the weight of mountains.

[SFX: GRINDING PRESSURE]

He looked at Zane and widened his eyes slightly. Mountain-like pressure crashed down on Zane, invisible but crushing, threatening to grind his bones to powder.

Tia's face drained of color. She crossed her fingers in an intricate pattern, preparing a secret technique she'd sworn never to use except in mortal peril. Even at full strength, she couldn't match Maddox—she knew this with bitter certainty. But she would try anyway. She had to.

Zane grasped her hand, his touch surprisingly gentle, and pulled her behind him with quiet firmness. "Thanks, but I don't need you to take care of me," he said softly, his voice carrying an edge of something she couldn't quite identify—pride, perhaps, or stubborn honor.

Tia's heart raced, pounding against her ribs like a caged bird. Heat flooded her face, unbidden and unwelcome. Then reality crashed through the momentary warmth. He intends to withstand Maddox's pressure alone? He's only at the Inner-Core Realm! This is suicide!

"Be careful!" she shouted, her voice breaking with genuine fear.

The pressure rolling off Maddox was immense. Even a seasoned warrior from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm would suffer soul damage from it. For a warrior from the Inner-Core Realm, it was instant death.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

[MUSIC: GROWING PANIC]

The three heavyset men exchanged worried glances. Maddox isn't even using a skill, they thought grimly, just his raw aura. He's trying to crush the kid. Zane's in deep trouble… he might not make it out of this alive.

"What's he trying to prove, playing the hero?" one of them scoffed. "Stepping in front of Tia like that… Does he really think he can take on someone from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm?"

"You're just mad because he's holding Tia's hand, aren't you?" another shot back with a grin.

"He's got talent, sure, but the kid's a dead man," the first one countered. "His soul is going to be crushed. He'll be a walking corpse—a mindless shell. He won't last three days like that."

Most of the warriors in the crowd couldn't figure out what Zane was thinking. If he'd just stayed behind Tia, he'd only get hurt, they thought. He's a prodigy, a rare talent. As long as he's alive, he has a future. He could've trained, gotten stronger, and come back for revenge someday.

Most warriors knew when to retreat and live to fight another day. They all assumed Zane knew that too, which made his decision to stand his ground completely baffling.

After all they'd seen him do, did the kid really want to die like this?
