Ep 169 - Hit Me With Your Best Shot
Word count: 966

[AMBIENT: JUDGMENT HALL]
 Zane stood perfectly still, waiting for the punishment coming his way. He kept his Vitality in check, but ready.
[SFX: WEAPONS READIED]
[MUSIC: JUDGMENT TIME]
 The four men took their positions, Sky-Shatter Cudgels gripped tight. In perfect sync, they swung at Zane.
All four were fifth-level Adepts, their strength incredible. On top of that, the Sky-Shatter Cudgels were designed to hit with an unmatched, pulverizing force.
[SFX: IMPACT THUDS]
 The weapons slammed into Zane’s body with a series of dull, meaty thuds.
Zane didn't even flinch. *The Hall of Judgment really has its methods,* he thought. *A few hundred hits like this would turn a regular Adept to pulp. But my body is basically a middle-grade Spirit Weapon. These little taps aren't going to hurt me.*
As the cudgels rained down on him, a pleasant warmth started spreading through his stomach.
The four Adepts kept whaling on him. It wasn't every day they got to cut loose like this.
[SFX: RHYTHMIC WEAPON IMPACTS]
They struck him over a hundred times without a pause.
Zane still hadn't moved a muscle. He just stared at Murry Cook, who was watching from a distance.
Confusion creased Murry’s face. The Sky-Shatter Cudgel was supposed to be the perfect weapon for breaking an Adept. In the past, it had always worked, beating them half to death. So why did this kid look completely fine after taking so many hits?
[SFX: ANGRY SHOUT]
 “Hit him harder!” Murry yelled.
[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]
 The four men were fifth-level masters with seemingly endless reserves of power. At Murry's command, they poured more force into their swings. Soon, sweat was pouring down their foreheads as they hammered blow after blow into Zane.
The cudgels pounded him over and over, but all Zane felt was that pleasant warmth spreading from his stomach, almost making him feel relaxed. He could see the shock in their eyes.
[SFX: ENERGY TRANSFORMATION]
The four men just stared back at him, utterly at a loss. They were still holding their Sky-Shatter Cudgels, unsure of what to do.
There was no point in hitting the kid anymore; he obviously didn't feel it. They were just wasting their energy. But they couldn't stop, not until Murry gave the order.
Their swings grew weaker, their resolve crumbling with each useless blow.
[MUSIC: TENSION BUILDING]
 “You’re not even trying anymore. Just stop,” Zane sighed, shaking his head. At the same time, the power coiled inside his body began to build, making the very air around him tremble.
[SFX: WEAPON SHATTERING]
 The next time a Sky-Shatter Cudgel hit him, it rebounded off the immense force now radiating from his body. The weapon shattered into pieces that clattered to the ground.
[SFX: ROPE SNAPPING]
 With a casual flex of his hands, Zane snapped the Thorned Rope of Vitality tied around his wrists.
“My time is too valuable for this nonsense. I didn’t come back to the Skyreach Sect to play these games,” Zane said, pointing a finger at Murry on the platform. “I know who’s behind this, but I’m warning you, stay out of my way. If you don’t, you’ll regret it.” Then he turned to leave.
This whole ridiculous show was not why he’d returned.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
 “How dare you! You dare defy me in public!” Murry was actually thrilled. He'd been itching to make a move on Zane, but he hadn't had a good enough reason to kill him.
He could have accused Zane of working with demons and had him executed, but he lacked the authority to make it stick. So, he had sentenced Zane to a beating, hoping the cudgels would do the job for him. But Zane had survived it without a scratch. Murry couldn't help but wonder what the kid's body was made of to be so impervious to a fatal bludgeoning.
If Zane had just taken the punishment, Murry wouldn't have known what to do next.
But now, Zane had openly defied him in front of everyone. He had mocked the Hall of Judgment, which gave Murry the perfect excuse.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
 “You dare to rebel against the Hall of Judgment and attempt to escape! According to the rules, I can execute you right here and now!” Murry shouted, holding up the Pen of Judgment as he moved to get behind Zane.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
 Just then, a figure appeared in the gateway, followed by a dry laugh. “I just happened to be passing by. What’s with all the shouting, Master Cook?”
It was Felix.
His hatred for Zane was bone-deep.
During the Demon Purge, Zane had beaten Yale to within an inch of his life. He had also killed Josef, who had been appointed as a counselor to the Imperial Army with the backing of House Sterling.
The incident had infuriated House Sterling. Their leader had declared that any member of their house who ran into Zane was to kill him on sight.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS THREAT]
 And so, Felix had come.
But today, Zane had broken through to the next level and become an Adept.
The shift from Raw Vitality to pure Vitality was a massive upgrade.
On top of that, he had studied the Tapestry of Sun, Moon, and Stars and learned to harness the power of the stars. His Corrupt Vitality had grown immensely.
[MUSIC: INTENSE STANDOFF]
 Felix’s expression was fearless. He could tell Zane’s body had changed, but Felix had his own reputation for strength in the Skyreach Sect, and his power had recently grown even more. “Hah. You’re pretty arrogant for someone who just hit the first-level Adept Realm. I wonder, if you ever make it to the Soul Realm, are you going to try and kill everyone in House Sterling? I doubt you’ll get the chance, though. You’re dying today!”
“Oh? If I ever make it to the Soul Realm?” Zane wrinkled his brow, pretending to think about it. “I wouldn’t stop with just House Sterling. If any of the top Seven Great Houses get on my bad side, I’ll take them all down. Bring it on, old man!"
