Ep 145 - Racing Heartbeat
Word Count: 1130

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL TENSION]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONES]
 The Crimson Cage was no joke. Even a Netherlord like Aldrich would have had his work cut out for him trying to break it. Enchantments that powerful were usually reserved for enemies in the Soul Realm.
Anyone else would have given up the moment their first enchantment failed to hold Zane, but Yolande wasn't just anyone. She had an unshakable composure. So when Zane shattered her Magic Ward with ease, her expression didn't so much as flicker. 
She simply cast the Crimson Cage without a second thought.
She leaned against the edge of the cage, the fabric of her colorful dress swaying around her sandaled feet.
"Think you can break this one, too?" she challenged, a faint smile playing on her lips.
Zane stood in the center of the cage, the Menacing Aura pulsing off of it in waves. His eyes locked on hers, and a slow grin spread across his face. "I didn't think you knew how to smile. Turns out, it's a good look on you."
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
 She blinked, a flicker of confusion in her gray eyes. Then, with a flick of her wrist, jets of Menacing Aura shot from the cage walls straight at him.
He knew what the Menacing Aura could do—it was designed to warp your mind, to drive you insane. Under its influence, weaker souls could be driven to turn on their own.
[SFX: ETHEREAL PULSING]
 He felt a strange heat as the Menacing Aura seeped into his consciousness, probing at his soul. A thrill shot through him. He didn't fight it. Instead, he closed his eyes, letting the Menacing Aura push into his Astral Plane.
"You're in the Soul Realm," Zane remarked, his expression darkening with apparent gravity. "I'm merely a high-level candidate. How could someone at my level possibly break an enchantment of this magnitude?"
“She's remarkably trusting,” he mused inwardly, suppressing a flicker of amusement. “This deception is almost embarrassingly simple.”
"How can you determine your capabilities without attempting?" she challenged, tossing her head with conviction. Her gray eyes held a glimmer of expectation that betrayed her otherwise stern demeanor.
"I simply... cannot. I recognize my limitations," he responded, dramatically throwing his hands upward in a theatrical display of powerlessness. The gesture concealed the calculating thoughts racing behind his carefully composed features.
A flash of disappointment crossed her eyes. She stood there for a moment, lost in thought. "Just try," she said suddenly. "If you can actually break it... I'll give you a reward."
"Oh, really?" Zane's eyebrows shot up. He studied her, a hint of suspicion in his voice. "And what kind of reward are we talking about?"
"Anything you want," she said, her expression earnest. "As long as it's within my power to give."
"Well now…" A slow smile spread across Zane's face. That was exactly what he'd been waiting to hear.
Zane hadn't expected her to offer a deal so quickly. For a split second, he almost felt bad for tricking her. But then he remembered how long she'd kept him trapped in this cage, and any sympathy evaporated.
"Alright," he declared. "I'll give it a shot!"
[MUSIC: DETERMINED TENSION]
 With deliberate steps, Zane approached the edge of the cage, squaring his shoulders with newfound determination. His eyes narrowed in concentration as he summoned the dark, swirling power of his Nether Essence from deep within. The familiar coldness spread through his veins, a reminder of battles hard-won.
The peculiar nature of Nether Essence made it perfect for his strategy—he required only the smallest fragment for his plan to succeed. Once introduced to the cage's structure, it would feast voraciously on the Menacing Aura, multiplying its strength with frightening speed. A predator among predators.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
 Drawing a measured breath that steadied his racing heart, Zane clenched his fist until his knuckles whitened. He could feel the power gathering there, coiling like a serpent ready to strike. With explosive force, he drove his fist against the shimmering barrier of the Crimson Cage. The impact sent vibrations up his arm as he expertly channeled the cage's own Crimson Energy, forcing it to tear a small, jagged opening in its otherwise impenetrable surface.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY]
[SFX: CRACKLING POWER]
 Just as he had anticipated, the Menacing Aura rushed toward the breach like water seeking its level, desperate to maintain the cage's integrity. A smile of satisfaction tugged at Zane's lips—this time would be different. In that crucial moment, he had slipped a minuscule portion of his Nether Essence into the breach. It spread with elegant malevolence, like a drop of midnight ink dissolving in clear water, beautiful in its destructive potential.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
As the Menacing Aura tried to close the gap, the Nether Essence fought back, devouring it without mercy. It spread like a virus. A fist-sized black blotch appeared on the cage, staining the crimson a deep, sickly purple. Within moments, the entire cage had been corrupted, turning the same dark, purplish-black.
“Perfect,” Zane thought, a thrill running through him. “The entire enchantment is mine now.”
Before Yolande could process what had happened, he tapped the corrupted cage. "Break," he commanded.
[SFX: MAGICAL VANISHING SOUND]
 At his command, the Nether Essence—and the cage along with it—vanished into thin air. Yolande blinked, her composure finally cracking for a moment. He closed the distance between them and leaned in, a confident smirk on his face. He stopped just inches away, his gaze dropping to her lips.
"That's too easy," she said, her voice quiet. "I can do more than that. Think about it and ask for something else."
"No," Zane replied, his smile widening as he held her gaze. "This is enough."
[MUSIC: SOFT ROMANTIC THEME]
 Yolande frowned slightly, as if she couldn't quite figure him out. Then, with a quiet resolve, she closed the small space between them. She rose onto her tiptoes, leaning in. He caught the faint, calming scent of her as she drew near. Her lips, soft and hesitant, brushed against his cheek—a quick, light peck before she pulled back just as suddenly.
Her expression was a blank mask. Without another word, she turned and walked back to her room.
[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]
 Once inside her chamber, Yolande collapsed onto the training mattress spread across the floor. She inhaled deeply, shutting her eyes in an attempt to regain her composure. Yet a foreign restlessness seized her, something entirely at odds with her disciplined nature.
Drawing her knees toward her chest, she rested one arm atop her vibrant dress while her fingers absently played with its hem. Her brow furrowed as she gazed vacantly through the window.
"How peculiar," she whispered into the silence of her quarters. "Why does my heart refuse to quiet?"
