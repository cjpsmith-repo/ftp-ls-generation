Ep 846 - Pickaxes

Word count: 991

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT CITY]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL WONDER]

The civilization the Dragon-blooded had built within the Sacred Feather Sea was a breathtaking sight. People walking the roads couldn't help but stare, their gasps of wonder echoing through the air as they admired the magnificent buildings, whose style was completely alien to anything human.

“If only I could stay here forever,” Camilla sighed.

“Stay here forever?” Renzo scoffed. “So the lingering power of the blood sacrifice can crush you to death? Bold choice.”

He was right. A potent influence from the sacrifice still saturated the city, and only the Dragon-blooded could withstand it. Rumor had it that some of them still lived here, hidden away.

Unlike the others, Zane tuned out the scenery, sticking close to Randy. His only goal on this trip was to find as many Ancient Heartstones as he could. It was the entire reason he’d come to the Sacred Feather Sea.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DISCOVERY]

Randy led the group forward until a massive pyramid loomed up before them.

[SFX: ECHOING WIND]

Its design was unlike anything Zane had ever seen. From base to peak, its entire surface was a honeycomb of narrow doorways, each just tall and wide enough for a single person. It looked like the pyramid was covered in at least ten thousand of them, maybe more.

“Which one is the entrance?” Zane asked, his eyes scanning the countless openings.

To his surprise, Randy just laughed. “They’re all doors, and they’re all entrances.”

“But there are so many,” Camilla said, looking at Randy with a curious tilt of her head. “How can they all be entrances?”

Randy smiled. “There are exactly one hundred thousand entrances to the Mine of the Dragon Lineage.”

[SFX: MAGICAL PORTAL ACTIVATION]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

Just as he finished speaking, shimmering blue rings of light pulsed to life against the side of the pyramid.

[SFX: HEAVY THUDS]

A moment later, warriors began dropping out of them, landing with a series of heavy thuds on the structure's lower levels.

Zane recognized them as the elite geniuses who had come through the Slow Arrow Field with him. About half had chosen to linger in the Spectral Plains, but the rest had clearly rushed ahead, becoming the first to enter the mine.

As Zane watched them land, he noticed each one carried a golden pickax. Something about the tools snagged his attention. “Am I seeing things?” he muttered. “Are their pickaxes actually low-grade Mythic weapons?”

Randy let out a knowing laugh. He reached into his Storage Ring and pulled out a pickax, tossing it to Camilla. She caught it and immediately looked offended. “We have to dig?” Randy ignored her and kept going — one each for Barry and Renzo. Then he produced two more, which were a deep azure blue. “Low-grade Mythic for them,” he said. “High-grade Mythic for us.” He threw one to Zane.

Callan and Fiona were doing the same on their end, working through their group and handing out pickaxes one by one.

Zane turned it over in his hands. Back in the Heartlands, warriors went to war over a single Mythic-grade weapon. Here, one had been forged into a pickax.

“Buddy, you're a little out of the loop.” He started toward the pyramid. “The mine is nothing like what you're imagining. Ordinary tools won't even scratch it. But spend on the pickax and the mine pays you back a hundred times over. Here —” he walked to the nearest narrow door at the pyramid's base and stepped through without another word. He vanished.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

Camilla stared at the empty doorway. “He just walked off mid-explanation.”

Less than ten breaths later, a blue ring of light appeared on the pyramid's upper face. Randy dropped out of it and landed in front of them, holding a fist-sized ore. He held it up. “Fire Crystal,” he said, and tossed it aside without a second glance. “Worthless.”

Zane watched the ore hit the ground. If that was worthless, he was beginning to understand what kind of mine this was. He tried, out of habit, to reach into his world memory for any record of it. Nothing came back. Of everything the world's memory held — every living being, every event from ancient times to now — this place had left no trace.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WONDER]

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL SHIMMER]

Whether it was the main stem or the leaves, everything flickered with a magnificent, otherworldly light. The plant’s core color was a crystalline light blue, but within that crystal, countless particles shimmered with hundreds of different shades.

Whoa, Zane thought, what exactly is this thing?

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CAVE]

The thought had barely crossed his mind before his feet touched down on one of the giant leaves. He had stepped through a door on the pyramid's bottom tier and landed here, on one of the lowest leaves of a colossal plant, with several more layers stretching up high above him. The layout seemed to mirror the doors on the pyramid outside.

He glanced down and saw that the surface of the leaf was embedded with ores. 

His heart skipped a beat. Had they grown directly from the plant itself? He finally understood what the colorful, shimmering specks were—they were all different kinds of ores.

A sense of wonder seized him as he stared at the giant plant. There are so many wonders in this world, he thought. I bet the plant itself doesn't produce these. It probably just pulls them from deep underground through its roots.

The leaf under his feet contained several different colored ores. He scanned them but didn’t see the ancient heartstone he was looking for. Still, since he was already here, he figured he might as well grab what he could. He hefted the pickax in his hand and swung it hard at the leaf.

[SFX: METAL STRIKING STONE]

[SFX: ECHOING CRACK]

[SFX: SPARKS FLYING]

A sharp report echoed as sparks flew everywhere. When Zane’s pickax struck the blue leaf, it only managed to create a small fracture. Even a Relic-Class weapon couldn’t cut clean through it.

“Whoa, this thing is tough!” Zane exclaimed.

Randy had been right. It was obvious that any pickax below Mythic-grade wouldn't even scratch the surface.
