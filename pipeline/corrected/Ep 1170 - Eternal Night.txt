Ep 1170 - Eternal Night

Word count: 1248

[MUSIC: TENSE ETHEREAL STRINGS]
The moment Cassian saw Lavender, his eyes softened.
The long spear he’d been about to run her through wavered and then lowered. Across the void, the other three warriors stopped with him, their assault on the massive Wheel of Reincarnation coming to an abrupt halt.
[SFX: WINGS BEATING]
[SFX: SOFT LANDING]
With a gentle beat of her wings, Lavender descended, landing softly behind the plate. She stared at the four of them, her expression unreadable. “Why did you stop?”
No one answered. The chase here had been a grueling ordeal, but the Wheel of Reincarnation had only ever been a distraction. They all knew they could destroy it. The real question, the one that had haunted them the entire time, was what came next. Could they really bring themselves to kill Lavender?
Their gazes met, a silent exchange of shared despair.
Lavender saw it all in their eyes — their impossible situation.
[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSH]
A cold, dangerous smile touched her lips as she gave the spear in her hands a casual wave. “Who’s first?”
No one moved. While Lavender was strong enough to wield Faith Energy, she wasn’t powerful enough to fight all four of them at once. Together, they could kill her in an instant. But they just hovered there, frozen like statues. Not a single one of them charged. As the Wheel of Reincarnation continued its silent flight through space, the two sides were locked in a strange, suffocating stalemate.
Suddenly, a sharp laugh echoed through the cosmos. It was her — the Queen of Punishment.
“If you won’t do it, I will!” she declared, her voice ringing with cruel delight. “It’s been a long time since I’ve used the Punishment of Eternal Night!”
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
The faces of the four warriors went dark. They all knew the Punishment of Eternal Night. It was one of the most twisted punishments the Demon Night had. The physical pain was nothing. It was a weapon that tore the souls of its victims from their bodies, imprisoning them in the Demon Night’s soul prison to suffer endless torture, never truly living or dying. It was a fate reserved for the vilest of traitors.
And now, she was threatening to use it on their families.
“I’ll give you five minutes to think it over,” the Queen’s voice dripped with a sickly sweetness that made it clear how much she was enjoying this. “After that, you won’t have a choice.”
Cassian moved.
He came straight at Lavender — fast, direct, no weapon raised. At the last moment, he hurled his spear aside. It spun away into the dark.
Lavender leveled the Nightshard Spear. The same technique, the same invisible wave of spear intent — his own, turned back on him. Even with the Pool of Faith behind it, her version wasn’t a tenth of his strength.
She didn’t step back.
[SFX: WET IMPACT]
[SFX: BODY SLIDING DOWN METAL]
Cassian’s chest met the tip of the Nightshard Spear and drove forward onto it. The spear pierced through and came out the other side. The force of his charge carried him down the shaft until he was standing at her side, close enough to touch. Blood ran down the spear and dripped onto her hand.
Lavender stared at him. Her mouth opened. Nothing came out.
Another figure came. Then another. Then the last.
Each time Lavender tried to redirect the spear, Cassian tightened his grip on the shaft with both hands and held it in place. One by one, Maren, Taryn, and Paige drove themselves onto it without hesitation, without slowing, without a sound.
All four of them were on the Nightshard Spear now.
Cassian loosened his grip on the shaft. That beautiful face — more suited to a painting than a battlefield — wore a helpless smile. He looked up at her.
“My queen… no. Can I call you by your name?”
Lavender nodded, barely.
He opened his mouth. Closed it. Tried again. The words wouldn’t come.
Behind him, Maren spoke quietly.
“Cassian loves his queen.”
The silence that followed was absolute. Among the Demon Night, a queen could not take a clansman as a partner — the position could never be inherited; only a warrior of pure ancient Demon Night blood could bear the title. It was a feeling he had carried for a hundred thousand years and never once spoken aloud.
“Do you remember the ferry?” Cassian said at last, his voice fading. “We had just broken through to the Life-and-Death Realm. We were training there. I left something behind — for you. If you can find it…”
He smiled.
“May my queen soar again.”
“May my queen soar again.”
“May my queen soar again.”
“May my queen…”
[SFX: DEEP RESONANT CRACK]
A soft sound came from inside each of them — like something vast giving way. Their inner worlds, each one containing millions of living creatures, warriors, and beasts cultivated across a hundred thousand years, collapsed all at once. Everything inside died in the same instant, without warning, without resistance. That was the law of the inner world.
Lavender let go of the Nightshard Spear.
She took a step back. Then another.
The four of them didn’t fall. They stayed upright, still impaled, eyes open and fixed on her, the last trace of a smile on each of their faces.
[SFX: KNEES HITTING METAL]
She dropped to her knees.
When her voice finally faded, the silence that followed stretched across the void.
[SFX: ANGUISHED CRY]
Inside the Wheel of Reincarnation, Zane, Freya, and Livingston heard it — a sound like something breaking that was never meant to break.
[MUSIC: TRAGIC MELANCHOLIC]
[SFX: SPATIAL TEAR]
Then, Zane was there. He tore an opening in space and appeared at the edge of the plate to find Lavender sitting alone. Before her stood four dead warriors, each one pierced clean through by the Nightshard Spear.
[SFX: ETHEREAL ENERGY HUM]
They had all been powerful enough to travel the universe, stronger than most grand World Lords, and even in death, a powerful aura still radiated from their lifeless bodies.
Zane floated over, coming to a stop behind Lavender. She was his sword spirit. They were one and the same. Even without seeing the grief on her face, he could feel the waves of her pain washing over him.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REALIZATION]
And then he understood. It hit him all at once. They hadn’t been defeated. They’d been trapped. They couldn’t bring themselves to kill their queen, but they couldn’t bear to let their families suffer the Punishment of Eternal Night, either. Caught in an impossible conflict, they had made their choice. Dying on Lavender’s spear was the only way out — a final, desperate act that kept them from directly disobeying the Queen’s command.
“Lavender, get up,” Zane said quietly.
She didn’t move.
“Get up!” he repeated, his voice sharper this time.
Still nothing.
[SFX: MYSTICAL LEVITATION WHOOSH]
With a single thought, Zane exerted his will, and Lavender’s body began to float off the ground against her will. She was the physical form of his sword spirit; he could control her if he chose to. But ever since her soul had taken root in the body, she had been the one in charge.
Now, under his control, she floated in the air to face him.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE WITH FAINT WIND]
Her eyes were completely empty, all the fire that once burned in them now gone.
