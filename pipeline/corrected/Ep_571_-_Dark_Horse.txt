Ep 571 - Dark Horse

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Behind Zane stood a level-nine Ogre War Fighter in crimson armor, gripping a massive blood-colored axe. His expression promised violence.
In the Tower of Sin, you stuck with your own kind. It was the only way to control the resources. So whenever a talented newcomer showed up, the other races would send their best fighters to take them out while they were still green.
The Ogres were one of the top three powers in the Tower. There was no way they were just going to let Zane walk.
The Ogre's taunt stopped Zane at the edge of the arena.
He needed the Lucky Light here; it was different from Dragon Soar Arena's version, but still valuable. Just as importantly, he needed Malice.
The "Malevolent Cycle of Life and Death" was an incredibly powerful cultivation method, but his Malice was so low he was stuck using only the "Exterminating Cycle." If he wanted to keep getting stronger, he needed more. And until now, chances to get it had been few and far between.
If he could score both Malice and Lucky Light right here, he’d be set.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]
Zane turned, a faint smile touching his lips. "I accept your challenge."
The level-nine Ogre War Fighter let out a booming laugh. "Good. My name is—"
Zane cut him off, his smile vanishing. “I don't care what your name is.”
"Why not?" the Ogre asked, a little slow on the uptake.
"Because you're about to die."
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY SURGE]
The words had barely left his lips when the power inside Zane erupted. He vanished.
The Ogre War Fighter might have been slow-witted, but his instincts were sharp. The instant Zane disappeared, the Ogre jerked his axe upward in a defensive arc.
[SFX: BONE-CRUNCHING IMPACT]
He only got it halfway up before Zane's fist connected dead center with the blade — ninety percent of his full strength behind the blow, no borrowed power, no tricks. It was enough to bend the axe in half.
The curved weapon whipped back around on its owner. The Ogre flexed every muscle, trying to hold his ground through sheer brute force.
It wasn't enough.
[SFX: CRACKING BONES]
Under the crushing pressure, the Ogre's bones gave way. Before he could recover, Zane drove a single finger straight at his skull.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]
Dark blood sprayed across the arena floor and was swallowed up by the stone.
Silence fell over the crowd.
[MUSIC: STUNNED AFTERMATH]
If the first fight had been a showcase of technique and speed, this one was something else entirely — raw, overwhelming power. The kind that didn't leave room for debate.
Creatures from a dozen different races stood frozen, struggling to process what they'd just seen.
A human suppressing an Ogre War Fighter in a pure strength contest? The Ogres weren't the strongest race on the continent, but they were balanced — formidable across the board. In individual power, they bowed to almost no one. And humans were never known for brute force.
So how?
[SFX: CROWD WHISPERS]
Whispers rippled through the crowd.
Spies from every faction were already taking notes.
With two kills on the board, no one stepped forward to challenge Zane. The mockery about his race had gone quiet.
In the Tower of Sin, strength reset every expectation — and whatever schemes were being hatched in the shadows, no one could afford to look at Zane the same way again.
[AMBIENT: TOWER CORRIDOR]
[MUSIC:  EXPLORATION THEME]
As they stepped off the stage, Phoebe smiled and fell in beside him. "I'll take you to log your points. There's more to the Tower than just the arena."
She wasn't wrong. Beyond the fighting stage, the Tower had a full range of facilities built up around it — cultivation rooms for rent, brokers trading in information, vendors offering technique scrolls and secret methods. Everything a fighter could need, all running on points.
After his two bouts, Zane had earned a top-grade True Origin Stone and twenty points.
"What can I do with these?" Zane asked.
Phoebe smiled at him. "Anything you want."
[AMBIENT: BUSY TOWER FLOOR]
[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL TENSION]
He'd barely finished collecting his reward when three humans approached — two men and a woman, all warriors, all coming in fast like they had somewhere to be.
They clocked Phoebe immediately, and something flickered across their faces.
The one in front looked Zane over with a measured smile that didn't quite hide the condescension behind it. "You're Zane? Word is you just dropped two Ogres on the first floor." He gave a short nod. "Good. My name is Dylan — I'm with the Violet Heart Sanctuary. Come with us."
Zane stiffened, annoyed at this stranger's tone. "What gives you the right to order me around?"
"You think you're hot stuff? You wouldn't last three days in here without the Violet Heart Sanctuary protecting you!" Dylan shot back. To him, the fact that he'd personally come down from a higher floor was a huge honor, and Zane wasn't just ungrateful—he had the nerve to be arrogant about it!
"How long I last in here is none of your business," Zane said. Without a second glance, he brushed right past him. Phoebe just offered an apologetic smile and followed.
[SFX: ANGRY FOOTSTEPS]
After Zane left, Dylan's face was still flushed with anger. "That punk!"
But Dylan's companion didn't agree. "Why antagonize him? The guy's genuinely powerful. If he levels up this fast in the Tower, he could actually secure a place for us humans."
"You're really pinning your hopes on him? A human War Fighter?" Dylan sneered.
"Could you have beaten an Ogre War Fighter at his level? You wouldn't have even dared to set foot in the Tower back then," a female warrior named Olivia shot back coldly.
"Olivia—" Dylan started, but she cut him off.
"Enough." Olivia shook her head. "Let's just wait and see. That woman from the Demon Night clan seems to be advising him. I doubt he's stupid enough to pick fights he can't win. I'll go talk to him in a bit."
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: TOWER MURMURS]
The humans had taken notice, which meant the other races couldn't afford to be left behind. All across the first floor of the Tower, eyes were on Zane. News of him winning two matches back-to-back was spreading like wildfire.
[MUSIC: DARK THREATENING]
And aside from the humans, every other race had the same thought: kill Zane.
