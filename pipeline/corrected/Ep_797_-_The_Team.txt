 Hester moved with the grace of a songbird, but the charged atmosphere in the room made it impossible for Zane to enjoy the performance. 

Lord Randy was glaring down at her, a crazed look twisting his face, and Zane got the distinct impression that tormenting his wife was the man’s favorite way to blow off steam. The moment the song ended, Zane was on his feet, murmuring an excuse to get the hell out of there.

 He couldn't care less about Lord Randy’s petty competition with the Crown Prince. His own goals were all that mattered. Randy had given him a few Ancient Heartstones, even if it wasn't nearly enough. 

More importantly, it had given him a much clearer picture of what The Sacred Feather Sea truly was, and his determination to reach it had solidified into an obsession. He wasn’t about to just sit around and wait for an invitation.

Beyond his usual training, he needed to find more Ancient Heartstones. Crane City was the second-largest city in the Harlen Divine Kingdom, and its Crystal Martial House was a top-tier training ground, on par with a Sixth-Grade sect. The divine kingdom didn't have sects, but it was brimming with powerful family houses. 

Back in The Heartlands, a third-grade house was the pinnacle of power; no one had ever even seen a fourth-grade one. Here in Crane City, though, fourth-grade houses were common, and there were even a handful of fifth-grade families whose leaders were masters at the fourth or fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm.

Zane figured that if Lord Randy had Ancient Heartstones, then the other major houses in Crane City must have them, too. If they were willing to trade them for Aether crystals, he’d make the deal in a heartbeat. So, after finishing his daily regimen, he spent every spare moment asking around.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

 The result was a crushing disappointment.

Ancient Heartstones could restore a person’s life force, an irresistible prize for any warrior. They were an absolute necessity, the kind of thing you used the second you got your hands on it. No one kept a surplus. Since the stones were so impossibly rare, Zane had no choice but to give up his search. His only hope was the expedition to the Sacred Feather Sea. The problem was that he had no say in when that would happen. Only the union of the seven divine kingdoms had the authority to grant warriors entry.

[SFX: DOOR KNOCK]

[AMBIENT: INDOOR ROOM]

 About two weeks later, Lord Randy showed up at his door. He skipped the small talk and got straight to the point. "Come with me," he ordered. "I'm going to introduce you to my teammates."

"Your teammates?" Zane asked, his curiosity piqued. 

He'd known Randy was forming a team — that was the whole point of their arrangement. What he hadn't known was that the kingdoms had spent years optimizing how to do this. 

After countless expeditions, they'd settled on the same answer every time: five. Five was the ideal team size for navigating the Secret Lands. Too few and you couldn't cover the dangers; too many and coordination became its own problem. Every kingdom sent several such teams in each cycle. Randy's team was one of them — and until now, it had only had three.

He didn't press for details, simply following Randy out and toward the Crystal Martial House.

Inside, a woman named Camilla scoffed, giving Zane a challenging look. Beside her sat Renzo and Barry, all three of them late-stage Spirit-Tribulation warriors from fifth-rank aristocratic families in the city — top second-tier talents, every one of them. They'd been training together in preparation for the expedition, and none of them had needed to sit the assessment. Randy had handled their tokens himself.

"Well well well, you're here!" Camilla said, her eyes bright. Then her gaze slid to Zane. "Who's this?"

Randy put a hand on Zane's shoulder. "This is Zane Jones. He'll be joining us."

Camilla's eyes went over Zane once, slowly. Middle-stage Spirit-Tribulation. Unknown face. She looked back at Randy. "You're joking."

"No way, Randy. You've got to be kidding me. Are you just messing with us?"

"You really think so?" Randy asked, a smirk playing on his lips. He knew they wouldn't be convinced until they saw Zane in action. "He might be the pillar of this team."

Renzo and Barry exchanged a look. The pillar? In any team Randy assembled, that role had only ever belonged to Randy himself. A middle-stage Spirit-Tribulation independent warrior outranking him was a claim that needed proving.

"If you think I'm messing with you, why don't you spar with him and see for yourself?"

The moment the words left his mouth, every head in the room swiveled toward Zane, their eyes alight with interest. It was clear they were all eager to put him to the test.

A flash of frustration crossed Zane's face. "Is this really necessary?"

[SFX: WHOOSH]

 "You don't get to decide," Camilla snapped, and then she lunged.

[SFX: RAPID STRIKES]

 Her hand shot out, and in the space of a breath, a thousand phantom hands seemed to materialize before Zane’s eyes, a dizzying, overlapping blur of strikes that cut off his every escape route.

She's insanely fast! 

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

[SFX: WHOOSH]

 Zane’s eyes widened. He felt a unique power erupting from her body, a type of Law he had never encountered before.

Time Law?

He knew that the two most powerful Laws in the universe were Time and Space. He had mastered the Spatial Law, but he’d never met anyone capable of manipulating Time. He certainly never expected to witness it firsthand from this woman. That had to be how she could unleash so many attacks in the blink of an eye.

 "Camilla!" Renzo shouted from the side. "Lord Randy said to spar with him, not hurt him!"

Knowing Camilla’s tendency to go overboard, Renzo was worried. If she seriously injured a warrior who was only at the middle stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, Randy would hold her responsible.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 Randy, however, remained perfectly calm, his expression unconcerned. 

It was as if he wasn't worried about Zane at all.
