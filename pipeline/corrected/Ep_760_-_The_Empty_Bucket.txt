Ep 760 - The Empty Bucket

Word count: 1041

[AMBIENT: SOMBER HOSPITAL ROOM]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

 Zane’s chest tightened as he gazed down at Tia’s still, peaceful face. 

He’d always been good at walling off his feelings, but there was no hiding the sorrow that threatened to pull him under now. He had won his battle against Maddox, but the victory felt hollow, coming at a cost he never could have imagined. Most of Tia’s Raw Vitality had been stolen, leaving her little more than a ghost.

Outside, the crowd roared his name, hailing him as a hero. The cheers were just noise. 

[SFX: POWERFUL WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: DISTANT CROWD CHEERING]

 Ignoring them, Zane took to the sky and flew toward Oracle City, his mind a storm of grief and fury.

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

 The three leaders of the World Merchants’ Guild followed close behind, their faces grim. The boy’s warning about the York Divine Kingdom sending forces to the Heartlands had shaken them to the core. If that happened, their home would face the greatest crisis in its history.

War wasn't new here. At its worst, the continent had been torn apart by the great Sect Wars, a free-for-all where every faction clawed for supremacy. Even the conflict started by the Corrupted Divine Kingdom was just another chapter in a long, bloody history.

But those wars had always been internal struggles. The York Divine Kingdom was from another continent entirely. A fight against them would be a battle against foreign invaders, and a single one of their Soul Sea Realm masters could wipe the Heartlands from existence.

Sure, they had Zane, whose strength was almost beyond belief, but he was still just a warrior at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. Against a Soul Sea Realm master, he stood no chance. With the Star Storm in the Chaotic Ocean of Stars finally over, those masters could now cross over with ease. 

A crisis was brewing on the horizon, and they were powerless to stop it.

[AMBIENT: PALACE COURTYARD]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

Three days later, Zane stood silently before the palace entrance, his gaze locked on the sky. An old man emerged, his shoulders slumping in a sigh of regret the moment he saw him.

Oracle City was the wealthiest city in The Heartlands, a hub for the world’s most famous weapon-smiths, alchemists, and healers. This man, Walter Reed, was one of them—a physician thought to work miracles.

"How is she?" Zane asked, his voice tight with a hope he barely felt.

[MUSIC: SOMBER PIANO]

Walter Reed was a patient man, and he explained carefully. "The Hall Master of Cloud Hall isn't sick," he said. "Since the moment we draw our first breath of life, the force in our bodies is set. Think of the body as a bucket filled with life force. As time passes, the bucket slowly empties. Warriors cultivate to expand the bucket — ten times, a hundred times, a thousand times larger — which is why they live so much longer than ordinary people. But the bucket still empties eventually. That's what it means to die of old age."

He paused. "The Hall Master's bucket is far larger than any ordinary warrior's. But ninety-nine percent of what was in it is gone. Only the faintest trace remains — just enough to keep her body from failing completely. That's why she looks peaceful. To put it plainly, her condition is not much different from death."

"Is there a cure?" Zane asked.

Walter shook his head. "There are medicines in the Heartlands that can prolong life. But they work by repairing the bucket — slowing the leak. They don't refill it. The Hall Master's problem isn't a leaking bucket. Her life force was taken. Nothing here can replace what's gone."

Zane nodded once. "I understand."

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

After Walter left, Zane went inside. The maidservants stepped aside without a word. Tia lay on the wooden bed, unchanged — as if she had simply fallen asleep and chosen not to wake up.

He stood over her for a long time.

He remembered the road back from Wavecrest City. He had made a promise to himself then: if he survived what was coming, he would hold a Dual Cultivation Ceremony with her. The crisis had passed. Maddox was dead. He had won.

And she was still like this.

He started to speak. The words didn't come. He swallowed them and let the silence hold instead. But in his chest, the intention settled into something hard and immovable.

I won't let you die.

He turned and walked out.

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE DRUMS]

[SFX: MARCHING ARMY]

At the main entrance, the three Guild leaders and a host of Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors were already assembled and waiting. 

Owen stood apart from the others, dressed for combat, an emerald sword at his waist. He had been ready to move since the day Maddox died. His girlfriend, Faye, had been imprisoned in the Ethereal Spirit Sect. She was the other woman with the Violet-Core Physique, and after Maddox seized the Sect as his headquarters, he'd taken her there. 

Owen had the cultivation of an Inner-Core warrior. He couldn't have gone after her alone. So he had waited.

Now they were going.

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

[SFX: MAGICAL HUMMING]

 A few days later, Zane and the others appeared in the sky above the Ethereal Spirit Sect, finding all of its defensive magic arrays already glowing with power. Even though the Corrupted Divine Kingdom had taken over, the arrays Arnold had left behind were not completely destroyed. And though Maddox was dead, the world was never short on ambitious men.

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

 The remaining warriors from the Corrupted Divine Kingdom clearly hadn't given up. They were using the sect's own defenses in a last-ditch effort to survive, banking on one single hope: hold out until the masters from the York Divine Kingdom arrived to turn the tables.

"Arnold's designs are impressive, but they can only hold off Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors," Zane observed, his voice cold. "Break them."
The three Guild leaders possessed strength rivaling the Life-and-Death Realm. Between them, Zane and a host of other powerful warriors, the arrays wouldn't last long. The Corrupted Divine Kingdom was already finished. Most of its fighters were dead, injured, or had scattered to the winds, choosing to live in hiding rather than die for a lost cause.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: SHATTERING GLASS]
[SFX: WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]
The air itself seemed to shatter as the defensive arrays crumbled under the relentless assault. 
Without a moment's hesitation, Zane became a blur of light, shooting straight toward the heart of the Ethereal Spirit Sect.
