Ep 1123 - A Secret Sect

Word count: 1032

[SFX: HORSE HOOVES ON STONE]
After more than two weeks at Twilight Bloom Hall, Zane took his leave of Sloan. The hall master saw them off with considerably more warmth than the occasion strictly required — he was not a man who forgot where his interests lay.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CONTEMPLATION]
For the past few days, Zane had been wrestling with the same thought.
He didn’t know much about Supreme Lord Sword, other than the fact that he was the master of Killing Sword Mountain. It was only now dawning on him that the sword plunged into the mountain’s peak—the same white sword shadow he’d seen years ago, back when he was just an Adept—must have belonged to the man himself.
But did you need *that specific sword* to pull off the Gods-Intimidating Strike?
The thought came and went, but Zane immediately shot it down. The strike was about a level of understanding so deep it was almost divine. Anyone who couldn’t execute it perfectly probably just wasn’t thinking straight. If a warrior truly got it, they could unleash that same incredible power with nothing more than a blade of grass. Sure, the Supreme Lord’s two-foot sword was a hell of a treasure, but it was really just a tool, a way to make the Gods-Intimidating Strike more effective.
He couldn’t shake the thoughts, but while Zane was lost in his head, Freya was already packed and ready to move.
“We’re here,” she said, her voice pulling him back to the present. She pressed a runestone in her palm, pouring her Vitality into it. 
[SFX: RUNESTONE ACTIVATION]
[SFX: MAGICAL HUMMING]
The stone flared, and a shimmering, three-dimensional map exploded into the air around them. It was a simple but brilliant piece of two-star divine texture. On the map, Freya pointed to a glowing dot that marked their current location: Twilight Bloom Hall.
Her tone was all business. “I spent the last few days digging into the Psychean Realm and found the Dark World. There’s a rift here,” she said, tracing a path on the glowing projection, “that will take us to the supreme world channel. It’s a shortcut. From there, we just head north to the inter-realm channel.”
Her finger moved to a point at the center of the Dark World. “Lincoln’s mark is here — right in the middle of the whole region. It’s a major trading city. Creatures from over a hundred different races pass through there. The three biggest auction houses are each run by one of the three great races. It’s famous even outside the Dark World.”
Her finger tapped a new region. “The thing is, the Dark World isn’t Alliance territory. It’s technically close, but it’s a free-for-all. You’ve got humans, the Demon Night, Ogres… all kinds of powerful factions.”
Zane blinked, surprised. “You figured all that out in just a few days?”
Freya nodded. “It wasn’t hard to get the information at Twilight Bloom Hall. I just asked around.”
He was struck by how much she’d changed. The Freya he used to know had no idea which supreme worlds were even next to each other.
She’d started talking again before he could say anything — still rattling off details about trade routes and racial territories — when she caught him staring. She stopped mid-sentence and stuck her tongue out. “Anyway. We should just go.”
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING DIVINE CITY WITH AERIAL TRAFFIC]
Following her lead, they set off through Auspice City. Just like in the Holy City of the Soulbound Sect, the sky was crisscrossed with glowing paths laid out by divine texture, and they fell into the organized streams of travelers flying overhead.
Before entering the city, Zane had quietly put the Heavenly Phoenix Horse away in the bestiary bag. The horse had nearly gotten him killed once already — Julius had spotted it immediately and it had been the whole reason for the trouble. A multi-race trading city was exactly the kind of place where something that conspicuous would draw the wrong kind of attention.
The first things Zane noticed were the Ogres — several of them, each the size of a small mountain, moving through the city streets with the unhurried ease of regulars. He’d crossed blades with their kind enough times on the main continent to know how aggressive they could be. But here, there was none of that. No hostility, no sizing-up. Everyone — human, Ogre, and everything in between — was here to trade. Business had a way of smoothing over old grudges.
Soon, they found themselves before a building so massive it was hard to take in all at once. It was a huge, circular structure supported by what had to be hundreds of colossal stone pillars. 
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]
A complex circular rune was etched into the ground at its entrance, and a steady stream of warriors, mostly human, flowed in and out.
“What is this place?” Zane asked, mostly to himself.
He saw Freya’s shoulders tense. Even though she’d done her homework on the Dark World, there was no way she could know every building in every city—and there were probably a hundred large cities here. 
The flash of panic in her eyes was unmistakable; she hated not knowing.
[SFX: SOFT LAUGH]
Zane couldn’t help but laugh, a soft, easy sound. Her seriousness was one of her best qualities, but she didn’t need to treat every casual question like a test.
“Hey, it’s okay,” he said with a smile. “I was just wondering. I’ll go ask someone.”
Freya shook her head, already stepping forward. “No, I’ll go—”
[SFX: APPROACHING FOOTSTEPS]
But before she could finish, a man in a long white robe approached them, his eyes fixed on Zane. 
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
“Looking to go inside?” he asked with a knowing smile. “I can show you the way.”
Zane felt a spark of interest, but Freya cut straight to the point. “What is this place?”
The man puffed out his chest, looking proud. “This? This is the Radiant Light Sect. Ever heard of it?”
[SFX: THOUGHTFUL HUM]
“A secret sect?” Zane murmured, his eyes lighting up. He couldn’t help but think of the holy maiden from the divine kingdom.
