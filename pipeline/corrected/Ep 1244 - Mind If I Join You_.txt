Ep 1244 - Mind If I Join You?

Word count: 1005

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION CHAMBER]
Back when Zane ascended, Amber probably hadn’t made it to the Upper World yet.
By then, the passage between worlds had been repaired, so the climb was finally clear. A huge wave of people from the lower world started making the journey, and plenty of warriors from the Soul Sea Realm decided to ascend. 
Zane sifted through his memories, but he couldn’t recall seeing Amber on the main continent before he left. He just figured she must have made the climb, too.
After all, Amber was talented. She might have only ranked in the second tier of the Talent Tablet, but that was more than enough to do well in the Upper World. Plenty of geniuses from all sorts of races had gathered at the Celestial Rank, and Amber was more than capable of joining them. 
But the Upper World was enormous—a hundred thousand supreme worlds spread out across it—and even with all his power, Zane hadn’t been able to find a single trace of her.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY MARKETPLACE]
So when he heard her name suddenly cut through the noise of the city, Zane’s head snapped up, his eyes wide.
*Could it be the same Amber?*
The mission was to the Deadwood Gate, which was to the north—the same direction Zane was already heading. And the reward was five hundred thousand Illusion Points. Even for Zane, who was sitting on millions of points already, an offer like that was tempting.
“Take me with you! I want to help Aurora and Amber!” a warrior called out, stepping forward eagerly.
The Demon Night woman running recruitment glanced at him and shook her head. “You? If you’re not strong enough, you’ll just end up losing all the Illusion Points you worked so hard for.”
She paid his embarrassment no mind, her gaze sweeping over the rest of the crowd. “Anyone else think they’re strong enough to join?”
“I do!”
A man from the Demon Night clan leaped down from the top of the city wall. Clad in black armor, he flew toward the group with a confident smile.
The woman looked him over and nodded. “You’re in.”
“Me too.” A woman with fiery red hair stepped out of the crowd. 
Her hair seemed to crackle with raw Vitality, and a large, dark-green bow was slung across her back, pulsing with the power of the law of life.
Soon after, three more warriors from the Demon Night clan joined the party.
Zane, who had been watching from the side, finally walked over. “Mind if I join?” he asked, his voice clear.
The recruiting woman’s eyes narrowed as she looked him over, a flicker of caution crossing her face. She couldn’t get any real read on his strength, and that alone told her he wasn’t ordinary.
“A human cultivator? Is this some kind of joke?” one of the Demon Night warriors snapped. “The Deadwood Gate belongs to our clan. Outsiders don’t get to walk in.”
Zane’s expression went still as he listened to the objections pile up. His mind was already made. Whether or not this Amber turned out to be his Amber, he was going to the Deadwood Gate to find out. If they wouldn’t let him join, he’d simply follow at a distance — he didn’t imagine they could shake him.
Before he could say as much, the woman leading the group spoke up. “The human cultivator comes with us.”
[AMBIENT: CROWD OUTRAGE]
The objections came fast. 
“We agreed the Deadwood Gate stays with our clan — we found it first!” 
“Why let an outsider in?” 
“If Aurora finds out, he’ll have your head for this!”
“If Aurora blames anyone, it’ll be me.” She walked toward Zane, resolute. “My name is Rowan. Welcome aboard.”
Zane gave her a small smile and a nod. “Zane Jones.”
The pushback didn’t stop. “Why bring this random human along, Rowan? What are you thinking?” “He’s probably dead weight. Why drag dead weight into the Deadwood Gate?”
Rowan didn’t blink. “If he’s dead weight, why are you so worried about him coming?”
No one had an answer for that.
Zane stayed quiet at the edge of the group, a faint smile on his face, and let it drop.
Fifteen minutes later, Rowan had pulled together seven cultivators total — six Demon Night, all of them strong, and Zane the only human among them.
The other warriors immediately eyed him with suspicion. For them, being on guard was just second nature. The Demon Night clan was surprisingly tolerant, though. If he’d tried this with Ogres or even other humans, they would have attacked him on the spot instead of just letting him tag along.
[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS FLIGHT]
[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]
With a whoosh, the group of eight took to the sky and sped north.
For most people, a twenty-four-thousand-mile journey would be an epic trip. But for warriors of the Soul Sea Realm, who could travel at incredible speeds, it was just a short hop. Zane had once covered nearly forty thousand miles in the Enchanted Barrier Tower, so this felt like nothing.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DISCOVERY]
[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND]
About half an hour later, they began to see dead trees dotting the wilderness below. The trees were completely withered, little more than lifeless stumps rooted in the earth.
Zane stared down at them. “These withered trees… they still seem to have some Vitality in them,” he noted.
Rowan, flying beside him, smiled. 
“That’s right. The dead trees have become spirits. They’ll rise up and attack anyone who trespasses in the forest.”
“Really?” Zane’s eyes flickered with curiosity.
“There are tons of them here,” Rowan added, “but the reward for killing them is tiny. You only get two Illusion Points for each one.”
“Huh, interesting.” Zane had been thinking the forest might be an easy way to rack up some points, but the reward was laughably low. Two points per spirit? He’d have to kill ten thousand of them just to get twenty thousand points. Definitely not worth the effort.
[SFX: CREAKING WOOD]
Just then, two massive dead trees appeared ahead. Their trunks twisted and intertwined, forming a huge wooden gate.
[MUSIC: FOREBODING THRESHOLD]
[AMBIENT: HAUNTED FOREST WHISPERS]
This was the Deadwood Gate.
