Ep 1186 - Only One Can Survive

Word count: 1125

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
A black-and-white tether of raw energy shot out from the central spire of The Elysian Keep with blinding speed. 
[SFX: ENERGY BEAM CRACKLING]
[SFX: CHAINS LOCKING]
By the time the Supreme Lords even realized what was happening, the beam had already latched onto five of them, chaining them together. The others could only stare, frozen in shock.
This was the Desperate Melee, the same deadly trap Zane had accidentally triggered on his first visit. Once you were caught, only one person could walk away alive.
“Does that thing work on Supreme Lords, too?” Zane asked, watching the tether from inside the keep.
The last time he’d been caught in one of these, it was with a bunch of Soul Realm warriors, and none of them could break free. But these were Supreme Lords. Surely it would be different.
Saul just smiled. “Why wouldn’t it? They’re *only* Supreme Lords. Each one carries a single Heavenly Destiny, that’s all. They can’t escape a force this powerful.”
“*Only* Supreme Lords? *Only* a Heavenly Destiny?” 
Zane was floored. “How can you talk about them like that?” The way Saul said it, you’d think he was talking about common street thugs, not beings at the pinnacle of existence.
“Think about it, Zane. A Heavenly Destiny is just a universal law of cause and effect,” Saul explained, looking completely unconcerned. “And given the rules of the Desperate Melee, it would be a genuine miracle if they managed to break free.”
“How far can they get before the tether pulls them back?” Zane asked.
Saul glanced out at the five. “A hundred miles. That’s the boundary. For you or me, that’s a meaningful distance. For a Supreme Lord —” he almost looked amused — “it’s a single step.”
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]
The five Supreme Lords caught in the trap—Addison of the Ogres, Gunter from the Demon Night, Magnus, and two others—didn’t seem worried at first.
In fact, Gunter was smiling. “This energy tether seems to be some kind of restraint,” he noted. “I can feel an intent coming from it, but I can’t quite make out what it wants.”
“I think,” Addison said, a slow sneer spreading across his face, “it wants us to fight until only one of us is left standing.”
“This is ridiculous,” Magnus scoffed. 
[SFX: WATER BLADES FORMING]
[SFX: ENERGY SLASH]
He waved his hand, and shimmering blades of water formed around it, poised to slice the band of light in two. But when the water sliced through the tether, the beam didn’t even flicker. It remained perfectly intact.
[SFX: MULTIPLE MAGICAL ATTACKS]
The other Supreme Lords tried their own methods, but the tether, barely two fingers wide, held firm against every single attack. 
Then Gunter moved.
[SFX: THUNDERCLAP DISPLACEMENT]
No announcement, no warning — he simply vanished, putting every ounce of his power into the attempt. Of the five of them, he had survived the longest, and he knew better than anyone that when something couldn’t be explained, the smart move was to leave.
The tether stretched with him. One mile. Ten. Fifty.
[SFX: TETHER STRAINING]
At one hundred miles, it stopped.
[SFX: MASSIVE REVERSAL FORCE]
An invisible force seized him and dragged him back. Gunter erupted — the full weight of his power unleashed, enough to crack a star in half — and it didn’t matter. The tether reeled him in like he weighed nothing.
The other four had been watching. Now they moved — in four different directions, each certain they would fare better.
They didn’t.
One by one, the tether hit its limit and snapped each of them back. When the five reconvened, the easy confidence was gone. They carried the supreme law of cause and effect in their very bodies — and this strip of black-and-white light had overruled it. None of them had words for what that meant.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Then Zane’s voice rang out — not just to the five Lords, but outward, spreading across the stars like a second broadcast.
“If you want to undo this tether, there’s only one way. You five will have to kill each other. In the end, only one of you can survive.”
Across the universe, trillions of beings heard the impossible: a Soul Sea Realm warrior had trapped five Supreme Lords and ordered them to kill each other. No frame of reference existed for such an act. Even if Zane died when the Melee ended, he had already achieved more than most warriors could dream of in a thousand lifetimes.
The five of them remained chained together, and now, they were starting to look intrigued.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
When a warrior’s training reached its peak, mastering the mind became more important than honing the body. But at that moment, Yolande’s mind was a complete mess. She couldn’t get her thoughts straight, couldn’t find her usual calm.
She had been the one to send Zane out. A young fighter with potential — she’d pointed him toward the wider world and thought little more of it. She hadn’t anticipated any of this. 
If Zane had provoked a World Lord, she would have stepped in without hesitation. But dozens of Supreme Lords? There was nothing she could do. There was nothing anyone at her level could do. This had passed entirely beyond her reach, and all she could do was watch.
And she wasn’t the only one left speechless.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
[AMBIENT: DISTANT CROWD MURMURS]
A whole lot of people who knew Zane were just as floored. We’re talking Master Sloan and Master Hill from the Soulbound Sect, their own Saint Lord, Lincoln from the Dark World—even talents from The Mist Palace like Jasper and Layla were watching in disbelief. 
They all knew Zane was a terrifying talent, but last they checked, he was a Soul Sea Realm warrior. If he’d just gotten a little stronger than them, that would be one thing.
But this was barely half a year later, and he was standing up to Supreme Lords. And based on what he was doing, he seemed ready to kill four of them without breaking a sweat.
These weren’t just any fighters; they were four of the most powerful beings in existence. There were only a handful of Supreme Lords in the entire Alliance.
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL ICE WIND]
Far away, in the Western Sky Region, Layla stood on a frozen star, surrounded by scattered purple ice crystals. Her expression was impossible to read. 
Zane, the person she had once considered her greatest rival, had reached this level of power in what felt like the blink of an eye. The star’s polar region was the perfect place to train, a location her own master had brought her to.
Just then, the old man appeared beside her, his gaze fixed on the distant Middle Sky Region. His face was also etched with surprise. “He can’t be doing this on his own strength,” he murmured. “He must have a trick up his sleeve. The Illusion Battlefield is only a few years away, Layla. You need to focus on your own training.”
