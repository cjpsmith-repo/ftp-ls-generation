Ep 878 - The Thunder Law

Word count: 1076

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Zane had wrapped himself in Vitality, activating his Stellar Body for protection, but it did nothing to quiet the gnawing unease in his gut. His eyes stayed as cold and sharp as ever.

He couldn’t help but remember Tia’s Life-and-Death Tribulation. The first wave of lightning had been the weakest, with each one that followed growing exponentially stronger. This, however, was just the first wave, and it was already terrifyingly intense. He didn’t dare imagine what would come next, and frankly, he couldn’t afford to. If he couldn’t dodge it, he’d have to meet it head-on at the perfect moment.

[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]

As the first lightning dragon closed in, the dark cloud cover overhead began to churn, spinning slowly like a vast, dark whirlpool. A hole ripped open in the center, and a single, sharp ray of light shot down, looking for all the world like someone had punched a hole straight through the sky.

[SFX: BLINDING FLASH]

For a split second, the beam pulsed, then it was washed out by a blazing white glare that blinded the onlookers below. To their absolute horror, the light itself began to coalesce, forming a massive dragon’s claw that descended from the opening.

The powerful warriors of the Soul Sea Realm could only stare, wide-eyed and breathless.

The claw reached out and tore at the very fabric of the sky. The thick layer of dark clouds was shredded like paper, scattering into formless wisps as the crowd watched in stunned silence. A moment later, the rest of the dragon followed.

[SFX: MASSIVE ROAR]

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

A blazing white head formed entirely of crackling energy let out an earth-shattering roar aimed directly at Zane.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE THUNDER]

Lightning arced across its impossibly long body, followed by another deafening explosion that shook the ground.

Zane’s eyes flickered, taking it all in. He forced himself to go still, his expression smoothing into a placid mask. He narrowed his eyes on the descending beast, his dry lips parting to whisper a simple challenge. “Come on.”

[SFX: GROUND SPLITTING CRASH]

[MUSIC: CLIMACTIC DRUMS]

With a final, ground-splitting crash, the colossal dragon circled once in the air before diving straight for him. It was as large as a mountain range, but as it charged, it shrank rapidly, concentrating all of its raw, chaotic power for the final, devastating strike.

Celestial Tribulations are disasters sent from the heavens, Zane thought, a grim resolve settling over him. There’s no running from them. I just have to face this thing head-on.

Power flooded into his body in waves. One thousand Dragon Scales. Two thousand. Three thousand. Four thousand. His limit was five thousand — but pushing all the way there risked injuring himself before the tribulation was even half done. Four thousand was as far as he could go. 

The bones throughout his body cracked and shifted. His frame doubled in size, towering over the other warriors like a giant — and still no larger than an ant compared to the dragon descending from above.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

He shot to his feet, a vibrant, powerful aura erupting from his body just as the lightning dragon lunged.

“It’s just a lightning dragon!” Zane declared, his voice ringing with forced confidence. “All for show! Nothing compared to the real thing!”

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL CRESCENDO]

He didn’t wait for it to reach him. He leaped — charging straight up toward it.

[SFX: BARRIER SHATTER]

The moment Zane met the dragon, his purple Vitality barrier shattered instantly. It had never stood a chance against this level of tribulation.

[SFX: DEEP IMPACT]

The lightning dragon drove into him — and hit the starlight of the Stellar Body instead.

Zane’s eyes were flat. He could feel it already. The thunder powering this dragon was fourth-level, close to fifth. The Stellar Body wouldn’t hold for long.

He was right. Beneath the dragon’s corrosion, the starlight on his body began to dim at a visible rate. Above the dark clouds, the two stars that had pierced through began to fade.

Then they were gone.

[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACKLING]

The lightning wrapped around him like hands — dense, crackling arcs that covered every inch of his body. A bone-deep pain tore through him all at once.

His body held. An ordinary late-stage Spirit-Tribulation warrior would have been ash. Zane’s frame was something else entirely. But the lightning left its mark — black scorch marks spread across his skin, arc after arc, until his entire body had gone pitch black, as if he’d crawled out of a coal mine.

[SFX: ENERGY WAVE]

Power surged through him as he met the creature’s attack. He drove upward through the dragon’s body until he reached its abdomen. There, he stopped. He planted himself, spread his arms, and released the Dragon Scale power outward in a tide — an invisible blade that swept in every direction at once.

[SFX: MASSIVE SLASH]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]

For a split second, everything froze—and then the dragon was split cleanly in two, right down the middle.

The warriors watching from a distance couldn’t believe their eyes. Zane had actually done it. He’d sliced the lightning dragon in half.

[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

Zane let out a ragged breath, but his relief lasted only a heartbeat. The two halves of the dragon suddenly shot out to either side, twisting and reforming into two smaller, equally vicious dragons that charged at him once more.

This thing is basically indestructible, he thought, his expression turning grim. How am I supposed to beat it? I can’t just absorb it.

They gave him no time to think, closing in on him from the left and right.

What do I do now? 

Anxiety tightened its grip around his chest as he found himself trapped between the twin assaults.

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHISPER]

Just then, a familiar voice echoed in his mind, tinged with something like a faint smile. Zane, are you forgetting your own unique talent?

It was the Cobalt Dragon.

My talent? Zane thought, his eyes darting between the two approaching dragons.

You’ve been through the Baptism by Laws, the Cobalt Dragon reminded him. You understand the Thunder Law better than most. Like I’ve told you, a Celestial Tribulation is a disaster, yes, but it’s also an opportunity. The most talented warriors in the upper world actually hope for stronger tribulations because they offer a greater chance to grow.

[MUSIC: RISING REVELATION]

Zane’s eyes narrowed. An opportunity… to master the Thunder Law?

Of all the Original Laws, the Time Law was by far the most difficult to comprehend, followed by the Spatial Law. The others—Thunder, Fire, Ice, and Metal—were all considered relatively easier. He had already managed to grasp the basics of the Time Law after his baptism. 

By comparison, understanding the Thunder Law should be a walk in the park.
