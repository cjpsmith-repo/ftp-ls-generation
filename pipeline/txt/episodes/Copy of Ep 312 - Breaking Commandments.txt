Ep 312 - Breaking Commandments

Patrick slowly stood, as everyone in the arena watched in stunned silence, witnessing the unthinkable—the composed Patrick being pushed to his limits by an opponent who hadn’t even drawn his legendary weapon.
He desperately needed to recover his equilibrium, so he began to silently recite the monastic commandments. Even though Patrick was renowned for his discipline, he struggled to contain the emotions surging through him. The unexpected power behind Zane’s strikes had shaken his confidence, forcing him to confront his own limitations.
"Don’t complain. Don’t get angry. Don’t be arrogant. Don’t be impatient. Don’t be greedy. Don’t give in to lust," Patrick recited the tenets in his mind.
He was sharp, but he wasn’t a machine. Only the truly enlightened masters could entirely suppress their emotions—a state of being Patrick had yet to achieve. No mortal cultivator, regardless of discipline, could just shut off their feelings at will.
Still, Patrick’s self-mastery was leagues beyond his peers. Even though Zane’s devastating blow had momentarily shaken his composure, he gathered his scattered thoughts with remarkable swiftness. The silent mantra drew him back to his center.
[SFX: WATER RIPPLING]
His mind transformed into a tranquil ocean, flat and serene. Momentary ripples threatened the surface, nearly erupting into towering waves of frustration. But beneath Patrick’s iron will, the waters settled, returning to perfect peace, a testament to years of rigorous mental training.
[MUSIC: MAGICAL MYSTERY]
"How did you get this strong?" Patrick asked, his piercing gaze studying Zane with newfound respect tinged with apprehension. "There has to be a ceiling for that kind of power. What you’re doing..." he paused, searching for the right words, "it completely defies the natural limits of an Adept cultivator."
During Zane’s earlier display, Patrick had seen something remarkable.
While most spectators lacked his refined perception, he had spotted the raw, impossible force radiating from his opponent—a brilliant aura that shouldn’t exist at their cultivation level.
Patrick was right—Zane’s strength vastly exceeded the known limitations for an Adept. The disparity wasn’t merely noticeable; it was staggering.
But Patrick reasoned that kind of overwhelming power should come with a cost. It demanded a body built to withstand such forces; otherwise, the sheer magnitude would have shredded Zane apart from the inside out, leaving him a broken husk. And yet there he stood, unscathed and composed, as if wielding such devastating strength were perfectly natural.
The truth was, Zane’s body had been reinforced—strengthened hundreds, maybe even thousands of times over, until it was on par with a middle-grade Spirit Weapon. He could easily handle his own terrifying strength.
That, however, remained his most closely guarded secret. He certainly wasn’t about to reveal it to Patrick, of all people. Instead, Zane merely offered a faint smile. "You mind if I don’t answer that?" he asked, his tone casual yet carrying an unmistakable edge of finality.
"Of course," Patrick replied with a respectful nod. He squared his shoulders, drawing himself up to his full height. "But I’m still confident in my own power."
The assertion hung in the air between them, a declaration of unwavering self-belief. The words had barely left his lips when the golden ward surrounding him shimmered once before dissolving completely into the atmosphere.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
A torrent of golden Vitality erupted from his body, encasing his skin in a luminous sheen until he looked like he was forged from pure gold.
At first glance, it was similar to the state Zane entered when those mysterious runes crawled across his skin. But the two phenomena were fundamentally different. When Zane absorbed the flame, the runes had swirled like tiny, golden whirlpools. Patrick, however, was completely sheathed in a solid-looking shell of golden Vitality, as if he’d been dipped in molten metal.
[SFX: CROWD GASPS]
"The Form of the Adamant Soul!" a disciple screamed. "He’s using the Form of the Adamant Soul!"
Watching the transformation, Finn let out a quiet sigh. He’d already forced Patrick to reveal his Shadow of the Immovable Guardian, but it seemed the guy still had more aces up his sleeve. The Form of the Adamant Soul was clearly another one of his trump cards.
Form of the Adamant Soul? Zane thought, eyeing Patrick’s gleaming golden form. He clenched his fists, bracing himself.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
The fight fell into a strange rhythm. Patrick would land a flurry of three or four strikes for every one of Zane’s. But that single counterblow from Zane carried more force than all of Patrick’s attacks combined, a stark reminder of who held the real power.
"Whoa, Zane got hit again!" one disciple yelled.
"I can’t even watch," another muttered. "If I took a beating like that, I’d be dead ten times over."
"Dead? You’d be lucky!" a third scoffed. "You’re underestimating Patrick. A solid hit to the chest from him would punch a hole straight through you."
"There’s no way Zane can keep taking this! He’s gotta be on his last legs!"
From the stands, their logic was sound. But they couldn’t see the reality of what was happening in the arena.
Patrick had been counting on his Form of the Adamant Soul, saving it specifically to counter Zane’s Streamer—what he’d assumed would be the biggest threat.
Except now, Zane wasn’t using his Streamer at all. And there was nothing Patrick could do to force him.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
But the outcome was nothing like he’d imagined. With every blow he landed, Patrick’s shock grew, chipping away at his composure. The placid ocean in his mind was now a raging tempest, with colossal waves crashing against his focus.
Zane... he didn’t even seem to care about getting hit.
Patrick knew the power behind his own fists. He might not have Zane’s raw, innate strength, but his attacks were infused with every ounce of his Vitality. No normal body should be able to withstand that kind of punishment. And yet, Zane was taking it.
How tough is his body? Patrick thought, his mind reeling. It’s not human. He’s built like some kind of beast!
[SFX: FRUSTRATED SHOUT]
"Damn it! I never should have used the Form of the Adamant Soul!"* In his frustration, Patrick completely forgot the doctrines of the Monastic Path. He had just broken one of the commandments: Do not swear.
