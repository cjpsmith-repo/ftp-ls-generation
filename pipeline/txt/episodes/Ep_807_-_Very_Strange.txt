Ep 807 - Very Strange

Word count: 1106

[AMBIENT: GRAND THRONE ROOM]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

The Holy Maiden had terrible timing.

One more round and Zane would have had his entry into the Sacred Feather Sea secured.

Now she was about to unravel everything.

She glared at Zane and gave a firm nod. “I’m not mistaken, Your Majesty. It's him. Zane Lowell.”

War Emperor York’s eyes narrowed, flashing like a viper’s as he fixed his glare on Zane. He was already convinced she was telling the truth. But with a representative of the Harlen Divine Kingdom standing right there, he couldn’t just act on impulse. He choked back his rage and asked the one question he dreaded the answer to.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

“Kid. Did you kill my son—the envoy from the Divine Kingdom?”

Zane just shrugged, his expression unreadable. “I don’t know anyone by that description, and I don’t know this woman. I have no idea what you’re talking about.”

The Harlen Emperor couldn’t just stand by and watch this happen. Two of his champions had already been defeated, and with War Emperor York cornering the very man his own Crown Prince had recommended—a man even Randy held in high regard—he knew he had to step in.

“War Emperor York,” the Harlen Emperor said patiently, “Zane is one of ours. He already told you he’s not the one you’re looking for. Isn’t it a little out of line to keep pressing him like this?”

The man’s name was York, and the entire York Divine Kingdom belonged to his house. In theory, that made them an eighth-grade noble family.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

A flicker of worry crossed Randy’s face. He knew Zane’s background was a complete blank. Someone with his kind of talent couldn’t have just materialized out of thin air. Both he and the Crown Prince had secretly looked into Zane’s identity and found something shocking—he wasn’t from the Harlen Divine Kingdom at all. When they hit a dead end with Zane, they started digging into the guard who was always with him. That only deepened the mystery. 

The guard, a Life-and-Death Realm warrior named Francis, was actually from the York Divine Kingdom and served as a steward in some secret order. It all just made Zane’s identity more of a puzzle. Of course, Randy didn’t really care where Zane came from. All that mattered was that Zane was an asset.

The Crown Prince, on the other hand, was now certain the Holy Maiden had been telling the truth. This guy, Zane, had to be from the Heartlands. The Crown Prince had tried to recruit him, but Zane hadn't budged, making it crystal clear he answered to no one. So, he wasn't about to step in and defend Zane now. But he wouldn’t expose him, either. Zane was, for the moment, fighting for the Harlen Divine Kingdom. The Crown Prince couldn’t do anything that would put his own kingdom at a disadvantage; his father would be deeply disappointed.

As the Harlen Emperor finished speaking, it was clear that War Emperor York had completely ignored him. He just snorted, his eyes locked on Zane. “I don’t need you to tell me the truth. I’ll find out for myself.”

[SFX: CRACKLING SOUL ENERGY]

[MUSIC: DANGER CRESCENDO]

A powerful ripple surged from between War Emperor York's eyebrows and tore through the air toward Zane. The crowd erupted.

[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]

“A soul attack — he's using a soul attack!”

“A Soul Sea Realm warrior's soul pressure alone can reduce a Spirit-Tribulation fighter to an idiot. And he's not just releasing pressure — he's attacking directly!”

“That man is finished. Who told him to cross War Emperor York?”

[AMBIENT: INTENSE PRESSURE]

Down in the square, the Spirit-Tribulation warriors who had been jostling and shouting moments before fell suddenly silent. The sheer weight of York's soul pressure rolled over them like a wave, pinning every last one of them in place. Whatever argument had been brewing died instantly.

[SFX: SOUL WAVE IMPACT]

The soul attack crashed into Zane like a wave breaking against a cliff. Zane didn't move. He didn't fight back. He didn't call on the Cobalt Dragon to devour it. He simply stood there, eyes clear, and let it come. His soul was close enough to the Fighting Soul Realm that an attack at this level couldn't reach him — but he knew better than to retaliate. One countermove against a Soul Sea Realm warrior and the situation would spiral past anything he could survive. So he endured, and the jade-green light flickering at the edges of his soul swallowed the attack whole.

Randy closed his eyes. His jaw tightened. He had known Zane long enough to know this wasn't just a useful fighter — Zane was someone he genuinely considered a friend. And right now there was nothing he could do.

The Harlen Emperor hadn't been fast enough to stop York's strike. All he could do now was appeal for mercy. He sighed. “Alright, York! No matter what, he’s representing our kingdom. Don’t destroy his Raw Vitality—at least let him live!”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

If Zane’s soul were completely shattered, his Raw Vitality would vanish with it. But if a remnant of his soul remained, he wouldn’t die. He’d just become a mindless fool. The Emperor hadn’t been fast enough to stop York’s attack, so all he could do was plead for Zane’s life. 

But as the Emperor’s words faded, a strange expression seized York’s face. He just stared, unblinking, at Zane.

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ENERGY]

The air crackled with a tense stalemate. Many of the powerful Soul Sea Realm warriors sensed that something was wrong.

It wasn’t just the Harlen Emperor. Scott from the Star Field Divine Kingdom and War King Wind from the Wind Divine Kingdom felt it, too. They were all baffled, their eyes darting between a placid-looking Zane and the stunned War Emperor York.

Zane’s eyes were clear, full of life. He didn’t look hurt in the slightest.

What was going on?

Could York have held back?

That seemed impossible. The four rulers had sat on their thrones for years; they knew each other’s temperaments inside and out. War Emperor York was grim, ruthless, and arrogant. If he decided to strike, he would never hold back.

And yet, Zane seemed perfectly fine. What happened?

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Back on the stairs, the powerful Soul Sea Realm warriors wore expressions of utter confusion, completely unable to make sense of the situation. As the silence stretched on, it wasn’t just them. Even the Life-and-Death and Spirit-Tribulation warriors began to notice that something was very, very strange.

Zane didn’t seem to be hurt at all.
