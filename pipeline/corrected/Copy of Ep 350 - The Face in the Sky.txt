Ep 350 - The Face in the Sky
Word count: 1157
[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
Upon returning to the Skyreach Sect, Zane threw himself into his training with relentless dedication, refusing even a moment’s rest. The weight of his upcoming challenge pressed heavily on his mind as the Cloud Road loomed only two weeks away. Had his obligations to House Lowell not demanded his attention, he would have spent every conscious moment honing his cultivation techniques.
Meanwhile, his companions had vanished into their own training regimens.
Finn hadn't left the Seven-Star Platform for a second. The platform not only helped him refine his swordplay but also built up his raw strength.
Patrick, on the other hand, hadn’t gone to any of the attunement locations. His discipline, the Monastic Path, didn’t benefit from training grounds. His progress came solely through meditation.
Each young cultivator, bound by friendship yet isolated in their individual journeys toward strength, worked with quiet determination as the crucial deadline approached.
Zane's sister, Elara, didn't return to Hell Mountain. With only two weeks before Zane had to leave for the Cloud Road, she wanted to be with him. Elara so rarely got to spend time with her brother that every moment was precious.
Usually, she would just sit and watch Zane practice, her gaze fixed on him as he pushed himself to his limits. All she really wanted was simple: to be near him. She couldn’t ask for anything more.
But even that modest wish seemed too much to ask, since she'd have to say goodbye so soon. Often, she found herself yearning for a simpler life—one where she and Zane had been born into an ordinary family without the burden of legacies. They might have lived peaceful, unremarkable lives in some quiet corner of the world, free from the constant threats and political machinations that haunted them. The siblings could have shared everyday joys rather than strategies for survival, and their greatest worry might have been next season’s harvest rather than ancient enemies emerging from storms.
"What would it be like," she wondered silently, "to pursue happiness instead of power?" The relentless pursuit of cultivation felt like chasing shadows sometimes—an endless, exhausting journey toward an ever-receding horizon that few ever reached. The weight of their family name and the expectations it carried pressed down on her shoulders like physical chains, binding them to paths they hadn’t chosen.
Once a person chose the path of a cultivator, hardship just came with the territory. The potential gains were immense, but so were the losses.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN TOP WINDS]
One day, Zane had decided to train at the very top of Misty Peak. He was focusing his Vitality, trying to break through the bottleneck into the Fourth Level of the Adept Realm.
In truth, Zane had already built up enough power to break through on his own; he could have even made it easier with a few pills. But using them would have left his foundation unstable. Besides, taking a shortcut now would only make it harder to reach higher levels down the road.
Every one of Zane's advancements had happened naturally. A breakthrough would only come when his Vitality reached its absolute limit. Because he had never relied on outside help, his foundation was incredibly solid.
[AMBIENT: BREWING STORM]
As Elara watched Zane, a storm began to brew.
Thick clouds, like giant tufts of cotton, were pulled together by some invisible hand.
[SFX: RISING WIND]
Elara's hair fluttered in the rising breeze. As the wind grew stronger, it whipped her hair across her face in a tangled mess. Zane sensed the danger, too, his brow furrowing.
[SFX: THUNDER CRASH]
The clouds thundered as they smashed into each other, churning and swirling until, impossibly, they began to form the shape of a human face.
[SFX: BOOMING VOICE]
The face's mouth opened. "Sir Kenneth! Come out!"
The words hit like thunder. Sir Kenneth shot into the sky to meet it.
But Elara's reaction was nothing like the awe or confusion of the other disciples.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
The moment the booming voice from the clouds fell silent, Elara scrambled to her brother and buried herself in his arms. Her slender frame trembled with a raw, uncontrollable fear.
The whole time she was locked away on Hell Mountain, Elara had known what fate awaited her. But she wasn’t as afraid back then, because she'd figured she could just choose to die. If she was forced to do something she didn’t want to, she could always find a way to end her own life. At least then, her body would be untouched by the man she despised.
Back then, Elara was desperate. She'd had no hope, and with nothing to lose, she wasn’t afraid of anything.
But now, because of her brother, she had hope. And once she had it, she wanted to see it grow, dreamed of it all coming true. But with that hope came a new, suffocating fear—the fear of losing it all.
That was the fear that made her tremble now.
[MUSIC: TENSE EMOTIONAL]
Zane held his sister, trying to calm her. She felt like a frightened fawn in his arms, her heart hammering with terror.
"Elara, it’s okay. I’m right here," Zane murmured, his own eyes fixed on the massive face above. The challenges that lay ahead were coming into sharp focus.
He knew he would have to face this all by himself. And that meant he would have to kill this man.
[MUSIC: MOUNTING DREAD]
Sir Kenneth whispered urgently to the colossal face hovering in the sky.
The massive face listened intently before its lips curled into a cruel smile.
"Okay! Good," the face thundered back, its voice reverberating across the landscape like distant thunder. "But hear this: if Elara Lowell fails to appear before me within a month, I shall erase the Skyreach Sect from this world completely!" The entity’s eyes gleamed with malicious delight. "Question my resolve if you dare. The consequences will be... instructive."
A chilling laugh erupted from its mouth, echoing across the valley and sending birds scattering from nearby trees.
After its booming laughter faded, the face’s massive eyes swiveled toward Misty Peak.
Zane’s body went rigid. He could feel the weight of its glare, a crushing physical pressure. But he didn’t look away. His eyes burned with defiance as he stared right back at it.
[SFX: MAGICAL DISSOLUTION]
"Oh?" The spectral entity appeared taken aback by Zane’s unwavering defiance, its colossal features shifting into an expression of unexpected intrigue.
The pressure intensified briefly, as if testing Zane’s resolve, but the young cultivator stood his ground. The being studied him silently, ancient wisdom and unfathomable power swirling in its ethereal eyes.
Then, offering one final, bone-chilling smile that promised future reckoning, the massive face dissipated into wisps of dark energy, gradually melting away until the sky remained pristine and cloudless, bearing no evidence of the terrifying manifestation that had dominated it mere moments before.
