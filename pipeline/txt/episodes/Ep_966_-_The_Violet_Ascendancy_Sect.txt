Ep 966 - The Violet Ascendancy Sect
Word count: 1011
[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
The swords were already flying — millions of them, filling the sky in a roaring cascade of steel — and Bridget, the woman in the purple robe, fought back a bitter surge of jealousy.
She had to admit it: Cora’s control over the swords was unreal. And to summon millions of them so effortlessly? That meant her Violet-Core Physique had to be an incredibly high grade.
Lost in thought, Bridget guided the skyship down in a slow descent.
[SFX: METALLIC WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHIMES]
Cora was lost in her own world, swept up in the dance of the swords. A spark lit her eyes as she began plucking them one by one from the spinning whirlpool of blades, arranging them in the air to form words of shimmering steel.
“In the long, cold night, when I think of you, only loneliness finds me.”
Cora stared at the sentence hanging in the sky, her expression turning wistful. She might have looked joyful as she played, but she couldn’t escape the profound loneliness in her heart.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON DECK]
The woman in purple had already stepped off the skyship. Looking up at the words, she sneered.
[AMBIENT: DESOLATE TRAINING GROUND]
[SFX: AWKWARD SILENCE]
Bridget had her opening speech ready — but the words died in her throat. This woman’s Violet-Core Physique was extraordinary. If she claimed her as a disciple without her own master’s blessing, she’d be expelled from the sect. She needed to tread carefully.
She wasn’t the only one in the Violet Core Realm who would have sensed those swords. She had to move fast. Bridget slipped a Message Rune from her sleeve and burned it — a silent summons sent across the entire realm to her master.
“With a talent like yours,” Bridget said instead, choosing flattery over ambition, “you could have any man in the universe once your training is done. Why are you wasting it wallowing in misery?”
Cora had been so focused, she hadn’t even noticed the skyship or the woman standing there. The voice shattered her concentration. She turned, studying the stranger for a long moment before answering, “It doesn’t matter how great other men are. None of them could ever be him.”
[MUSIC: DISMISSIVE FLUTE]
The woman in purple scoffed. “I doubt that.” To her, Cora was just some Inner-Core Realm warrior who obviously knew nothing about the Violet Core Realm. Probably from some backwater Lower World. Warriors from the Lower Worlds rarely amounted to much.
And if Cora was only at the Inner-Core Realm, the man she was pining for couldn’t be that powerful—Spirit-Tribulation Realm at best. Even if he was a prodigy on his little world, the most he could ever be was a Life-and-Death Realm warrior. A complete nobody. Women from the Lower Worlds had such small minds, she thought. No idea how many truly powerful men were out there in the universe.
Cora’s irritation grew—she had no desire to talk about this anymore. But then a thought stopped her. “Are you my master?”
The Old Man had told her that once she entered the Violet Core Realm, a ‘master’ would find her. Since this woman had just appeared out of nowhere, Cora made the obvious leap.
The woman in purple was completely taken aback.
She was more than qualified to take on an Inner-Core Realm disciple — but this was no ordinary Inner-Core warrior. If she rashly accepted someone of this caliber without permission, her own master would have her expelled from the sect. She couldn’t afford that.
“Not quite,” Bridget said, a careful smile on her face. “But my master will be here soon. Wait just a moment.”
[SFX: SWORDS HUMMING]
Unbothered, Cora turned back to the Ancestral Dragon and began arranging the swords again — new words taking shape in the sky: “The heart of a ruler shifts like the moon — drifting east, then west, never still.”
Bridget stared up at it, speechless. Wasn’t it just a man? Was it really necessary?
[SFX: WHOOSH OF ARRIVAL]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Before she could find her voice, another figure materialized in the air.
Rays of purple light condensed above the skyship, and a figure slowly took shape — a woman in a pale violet robe, beautiful and composed, appearing to be in her thirties. The moment she arrived, her eyes went straight to Cora. They didn’t leave.
The newcomer had just gotten word from her disciple about a woman with a high-ranking Violet-Core Physique—suspected to be a Third-Rank Blue-Violet Core, at the very least. The news was so significant, she’d rushed over from the Violet Ascendancy Sect herself.
There were countless Violet-Core Physiques in the universe, but most were common Pale-Violet or Blue-Violet—sixth and fifth-rank. A fourth-rank was already exceedingly rare, and any woman who possessed one was an automatic candidate for the sect. So, a potential third-rank? She had to see it for herself.
But when she finally laid eyes on Cora, she was completely floored. This wasn’t a third-rank physique. It wasn’t even a second-rank. It was so much higher.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
This was the legendary First-Rank Black-Violet Physique.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]
A First-Rank... How was that even possible? It was supposed to be a myth. The beautiful woman was stunned into silence.
[SFX: KNEE DROP]
[AMBIENT: REVERENT SILENCE]
“Master,” Bridget said softly, dropping to one knee before the newcomer.
Shaking off her awe, the beautiful woman looked to her disciple. “Bridget, stand.”
As Bridget rose and took her master’s arm, the beautiful woman then glided over to Cora, stopping right in front of her. “What is your name?” she asked, her voice calm but commanding.
Realizing this must be the master she was promised, Cora bowed her head. “My name is Cora Kensington.”
“Cora Kensington,” the beautiful woman repeated, the name rolling off her tongue.
Cora nodded.
A smile touched the beautiful woman’s lips.
[MUSIC: CEREMONIAL STRINGS]
“I am Krista Donovan, the leader of the Violet Ascendancy Sect. Accept me as your master, and you will join our ranks.”
