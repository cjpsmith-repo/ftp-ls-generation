Ep 515 - Maddox Doesn't Forgive
Word count: 1235
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC BATTLEGROUND]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
In the world of warriors, survival of the fittest reigned supreme. No philosophy, no moral code—only raw strength mattered.
Those with power were practically worshipped. Others would kneel before them, press their foreheads to the ground, and accept any pronouncement as truth. Call a cat a dog, and they'd swear on their ancestors it was right.
Power alone opened the gates to fame, fortune, and authority.
A warrior blessed with a younger sister who had a Violet-Core Physique? Someone like that would normally present his sister to someone like Maddox without hesitation. The exchange would be simple: her body for protection, perhaps even a modest reward. Such a transaction would secure his family's prosperity for generations.
But on this day, Zane—who looked as weak as an insect in the ruins of the arena—had shown everyone his dignity wasn't for sale.
He hadn't flinched when confronting Maddox, the warrior hailed as the strongest in the Heartlands. He'd eliminated Marilyn without a moment's hesitation.
Now, as Maddox's fury radiated through the wasteland like heat from a forge, Zane stood utterly calm. His gaze remained fixed on Elara, his younger sister. She was the only person in this vast world who truly mattered. Every breath he took, every risk he accepted, every enemy he faced—all of it led back to her.
[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND]
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
The wind was picking up, howling stronger and stronger.
Natural energy crackled through the air, wild and chaotic, responding to the killing intent that saturated the atmosphere.
[SFX: CRACK]
The black basalt under Maddox's feet, already fractured, began to crumble into dust.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION RISING]
[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]
A murderous energy was building inside Maddox, a volcano on the verge of erupting. His hand unconsciously tightened around Elara's neck, her face turning crimson. She gasped, her chest heaving as she fought for air.
Maddox hadn't expected Zane to actually kill Marilyn. The boy possessed no fear.
A Life-and-Death Realm expert who had survived three tribulations, defeated by an Adept Realm youth!
Maddox wanted to crush Elara's neck, but he'd spent decades gathering the three Violet-Core Physiques. With Marilyn dead, killing Elara would delay the Soul Harvesting Ritual indefinitely.
He set the other girl aside and stepped from the platform, walking through the air toward the arena. "Since you killed Marilyn, you die as well!"
Tia stepped up, determination hardening her features. "Zane is Cloud Hall's disciple."
Maddox sneered. "Little girl, step aside. You aren't enough for me to defeat!"
An old voice rang out. "If my disciple isn't enough, then neither am I, correct?" A stooped figure appeared in the air, walking with a cane. Despite her slow steps, the elderly woman moved with remarkable speed. Within moments, she stood beside Tia.
"Master..." Tia's voice turned timid.
Elder Imogen, the leader of Cloud Hall, shot Tia a disappointed glare. Tia's careless words proved she'd been following Zane, putting their entire sect at risk.
But at the end of the day, Tia was still her most beloved student. She honestly couldn't care less what happened to Zane, but she wouldn't let anything happen to Tia.
“Elder Imogen,” Maddox said, shaking his head. "That may be true. But you're not strong enough to stand against me, either."
Elder Imogen, the leader of Cloud Hall, Tia's master and the former head of the sect, was one of the strongest warriors in the Life-and-Death Realm. However, she had only passed through a single Heavenly Tribulation after entering that realm. On top of that, she was getting old. Her vital energy and blood were failing, so there was no way she was a match for Maddox.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ANCIENT POWER]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING VOICE]
Just as a fight was about to break out, a voice—even older, more ancient than Elder Imogen's—echoed through the air. "In the Martial Tournament, a competitor's life and death hinge on their own strength. This is a rule the World Merchants' Guild has upheld for centuries. Maddox, are you telling me you're unaware of this?"
Maddox had been moving slowly on purpose, testing the waters to see who might intervene.
Of everyone he knew, there were only two people Maddox was truly wary of: Elder Imogen, and this ancient tycoon from the World Merchants' Guild—a man who had lived for over seventeen hundred years. If not for him, the leader of Cloud Hall would be the only one at the tournament who could give Maddox pause.
Xander and the leader of the Mysterious Luna Hall might pose a minor threat, but since this didn't concern their sects, they had no reason to get involved.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]
Unfortunately for Maddox, the ancient tycoon had decided to make an appearance.
Maddox's brow furrowed.
One-on-one, he was confident he could take down any of the leaders from the fourth or fifth-grade sects, probably even the ancient tycoon himself. But facing all of them at once? That would put him at a serious disadvantage.
If the three heads of the World Merchants' Guild teamed up against him, that would be hard enough. Now, with Elder Imogen and this thousand-year-old monster in the mix, things were getting complicated.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
"So you're saying no one answers for my disciple's death?" Maddox demanded. Even as The Heartlands' most powerful warrior, here in Oracle City—crawling with powerful figures—he was being cornered.
"An eye for an eye is fair," the ancient voice replied. "But settle arena disputes outside the city walls. For now, this is over."
Elder Imogen wouldn't challenge Maddox. He was too terrifying. If he sought revenge, how could her sect survive?
She knew Tia had interfered on her own, provoking Maddox. Zane bore no blame. But she'd always spoiled Tia and couldn't bring herself to be angry with her student. So Zane took the heat.
[MUSIC: COLD SILENCE]
Maddox's eyes swept to Tia. A cold smile crossed his face. "I'm very interested in destroying Cloud Hall. What do you think?"
"That's your own business," the ancient voice replied.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS DEPARTING]
Maddox gestured to his servants, who collected Marilyn's body. With Elara in tow, he turned to leave.
[MUSIC: DESPERATE URGENCY]
"Wait."
Maddox stopped and fixed Zane with a measuring stare.
"You won't kill Elara, will you?"
Maddox said nothing. His silence made the answer clear—Zane wasn't worthy of response. He walked away, his presence fading like a storm promising to return.
[MUSIC: HEAVY AFTERMATH]
[SFX: BARRIER DISSOLVING]
Before anyone could speak, Elder Imogen crossed to Zane—and swung her cane hard across his chest.
[SFX: CRACK OF IMPACT]
[SFX: BODY SLAMMING INTO STONE]
Zane crashed into the wall, cracks spiderwebbing around him.
[MUSIC: QUIET FURY]
"Master—" Tia whispered.
"Maddox does not forgive," the old woman snapped. "Cloud Hall will live under his shadow from this day forward. How are we supposed to survive?"
[SFX: RUBBLE SHIFTING]
Zane stepped from the broken stone. "You told me Marilyn would be at the tournament."
"I never told Tia to throw herself into harm's way! To drag all of Cloud Hall into this!"
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
She had expected Zane to handle Marilyn and vanish—not for Tia to plant herself in Maddox's path.
She knew Tia bore the blame, but couldn't raise her hand against her prized student. So Zane absorbed the strike instead.
[MUSIC: QUIET IRONY]
And from that blow, he absorbed more Primordial Energy than most warriors saw in a year.
