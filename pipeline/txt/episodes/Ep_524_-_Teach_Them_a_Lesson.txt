Ep 524 - Teach Them a Lesson
Word count: 1280
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL HALL]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
As the tip of Zane’s brush sliced through the air, a thick, mysterious mist instantly flooded the hall.
[SFX: ENCHANTING SONG ECHO]
With it came an enchanting song and a disorienting wave of hallucinations.
Cedric’s assistants were the first to fall, their eyes going vacant. Cedric himself was next, followed by the stewards, and finally, Emanuel Drew’s entire entourage.
This group was packed with elders and leaders from various orders and houses. Many were powerful masters at the late stage of the Inner-Core Realm; a few had even reached the early stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. Yet not one of them could resist the illusion bleeding from a single, unfinished divine texture.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
When the first line was complete, Zane risked a glance at them. A flicker of shock went through him as he saw the twisted expressions on their faces.
The five-star divine texture was terrifyingly powerful. He hadn’t even fully activated it, and its aura alone was enough to disorient warriors at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. If the power of these three divine textures was combined with the four thousand, nine hundred three-star and four-star ones… Zane couldn’t even begin to imagine it.
Just the thought made his heart hammer in his chest, his hand trembling so hard the brush shook.
[SFX: MYSTICAL VOICE ECHO]
"Be careful," The Cobalt Dragon’s voice echoed in his mind, sharp and focused. "Drawing a divine texture requires absolute concentration. One slip, and the whole thing is ruined. You'll have to start from scratch."
Fortunately, Zane managed to steady himself just in time, his grip on the brush tightening. [MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC BUILD]
He threw himself back into drawing the final divine texture.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL STRINGS]
As he drew the upper half of The Madness Trap, a soft, pink-and-purple mist bled from the strokes, filling the air with an overwhelming, hypnotic allure.
[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL DRUMS]
But as he started on the bottom half, the whole atmosphere shifted. Zane's brushstrokes turned sharp and vicious. A wave of raw killing intent erupted from the paper as a dark fog billowed into the room. It was the thunder of charging cavalry in the dead of night, a murderous aura that swallowed the hall whole.
Driven by this vicious energy, Zane’s strokes became brutally firm, each line flowing with the grace of a dancing dragon, a soaring phoenix.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Zane shot a cold look at the group of elders. These people were a real pain. He knew they must have given Tia a hard time these last few days. It was time to teach them a lesson.
And so he leaned into it — deliberately channeling the Cobalt Dragon's soul force through the texture, driving the killing intent directly into the crowd. The Cobalt Dragon's soul was immense at the best of times. After consuming the Owl Beast's evil spirit, it had grown even more formidable. What these elders were feeling wasn't just the passive effect of an unfinished rune. Zane was using it as a weapon.
[SFX: PAINFUL SCREAM]
"Aargh!"
[SFX: BODY THUD]
With a soft thud, one of the stewards, still lost in the haze, was overwhelmed by the killing intent. His eyes rolled back in his head, and he collapsed.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
With the Cobalt Dragon's soul force amplifying the effect, the menacing illusions warped the faces of the elders, order leaders, and house leaders.
Cedric, despite being only at the Inner-Core Realm, held on. His soul was tougher than most — a lifetime spent with runes had seen to that. He was barely hanging on, but he didn't go down.
[SFX: FINAL BRUSHSTROKE — LIKE A BLADE FALLING]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
Zane's final stroke came down like a giant blade cleaving through the air.
"Seal!"
[SFX: MIST DISPERSING]
The Madness Trap was complete. Every wisp of fog, every trace of killing intent, every thread of illusion — gone in an instant, as though a storm had broken and the sky had cleared all at once.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
The elders came back to themselves, gasping for breath. They looked around the hall — at each other, at the bodies on the floor — and then, slowly, at Zane.
Fear. Plain, unmistakable fear, on the faces of warriors two full realms above him.
[MUSIC: QUIET TENSION]
Emanuel stared at Zane with a dark, complicated expression. He hadn't been at the tournament. He'd heard the rumors, of course, but he'd always put Zane's survival down to luck — a one-time thing, not a reflection of real strength. Luck ran out. Strength didn't.
But this was something else. A Soul Realm boy had just brought a room full of Spirit-Tribulation masters to their knees without throwing a single punch. Emanuel turned it over in his mind, and for the first time, a cold certainty settled in.
This boy needs to disappear. Quietly. When no one is watching.
The thought had crossed his mind before. But now it felt less like an option and more like a necessity.
Zane set down his brush. "What brings you all here?" His voice was calm, almost bored.
Emanuel stepped forward, recovering his composure. "You provoked Maddox and brought disaster down on Cloud Hall. Walk out these gates yourself, face his anger, and clear Cloud Hall's name."
"And if I don't?"
Emanuel's eyes flashed. "You're a Soul Realm disciple. The fact that we're speaking to youat all is already a courtesy. What qualifications do you have to refuse?"
Zane almost smiled. "Since it's a courtesy, I don't need it." He turned back to his work. "Please leave."
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
He knew exactly what they were—men hiding behind authority because they feared Tia and Elder Imogen. Without those two, he'd have been dragged out days ago.
[MUSIC: TENSE SILENCE]
Emanuel's jaw tightened. Killing Zane would cost him the moment Tia found out. It had to be done quietly, or not at all. Not here, not in front of witnesses.
[SFX: PAPERS SHUFFLING]
Zane went back to work, sorting completed divine textures into neat stacks by type. Each had to be placed in precise locations throughout Cloud Hall so they could resonate as a whole.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS SHUFFLING]
He glanced at the elders, who were reluctantly shuffling toward the exit. Can’t really blame them, he thought. People look out for number one. Who wants to get dragged into a disaster because of some kid?
"Elders," Zane said, his voice cutting through their murmurs as he stacked another pile. "You just saw the power of a single five-star divine texture for yourselves. All four thousand, nine hundred and three of these are designed to work together. Even if the order-protecting arrays can't kill Maddox, they'll be more than enough to keep him out."
"How confident are you?" one of them asked, his voice laced with suspicion. They weren't ready to bet the fate of the Cloud Hall on Zane. The whole idea of a fourth-grade Sect staking its survival on some kid in the Soul Realm was absurd.
And yet… after feeling the power of that one five-star texture, a seed of doubt had been planted. If a single one could mess with their minds so completely, what could the entire array do?
[MUSIC: EPIC REVELATION]
If this thing could actually stop Maddox, it would instantly become the Cloud Hall's greatest treasure—hell, the greatest treasure of the entire order. It would be ten times more powerful than the one Arnold from the Ethereal Spirit Sect ever created.
Zane looked up, his gaze steady and calm. "Eighty percent sure," he said, his voice firm.
