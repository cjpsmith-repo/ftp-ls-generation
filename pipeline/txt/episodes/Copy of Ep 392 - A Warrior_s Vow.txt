Ep 392 - A Warrior's Vow

Word count: 1190

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FLAMES]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Fiery evil spirits manifested in countless forms, each more dangerous than the last. The creatures lurking in the Sea of Raging Fire were typically minor threats—barely worth a cultivator's attention. But this Soulforge was more powerful than anything Zane had encountered before.

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

[SFX: PHOENIX CRY]

The flames had coalesced into a Fiery Dragon Spirit, roughly the length of a man's arm but radiating power far beyond its modest size. As the creature slithered through the air, its serpentine body left trails of distorted heat that made the atmosphere shimmer. The temperature spiked so dramatically that Zane felt his skin prickling, sweat evaporating before it could even form. Each breath scorched his lungs.

But the Ice Phoenix Tia had summoned from her sky-blue gem matched the dragon's power perfectly. The magnificent bird spread wings that scattered prismatic light across the chamber, its presence dropping the temperature so rapidly that frost began forming at their feet. 

Zane glanced at Tia. Her expression remained coldly focused, but he noticed the slight tremor in her hand as she maintained control over the phoenix. Even for someone of her hidden strength, commanding such power clearly demanded everything she had.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BATTLE]

[SFX: ENERGY CHANGES]

[SFX: BURST OF FLAMES]

The two spirits were locked in a stalemate. It was a classic elemental standoff—ice quenches fire, but fire melts ice.

Every time the Ice Phoenix dove at the dragon, the Fiery Dragon Spirit’s glow would dim. But a moment later, a burst of flame would erupt from the Soulforge and feed into the dragon, fueling it back to full strength.

And every time the Fiery Dragon Spirit lashed out, a few ice crystals on the phoenix’s body would melt away, causing it to shrink.

When Tia saw the Ice Phoenix had dwindled to a certain point, her hand flew to the sky-blue gem. A stream of cold Vitality flowed out as her fingers stroked the stone. The instant the chill reached the Ice Phoenix, it swelled back to its full size.

[AMBIENT: INTENSE HEAT WAVES]

Their fight was a perfect deadlock. The Ice Phoenix had Tia to replenish its chilling energy, and the Fiery Dragon Spirit had its own source. As long as the fire in the Soulforge furnace burned, the spirit couldn't be destroyed.

"While I hold this thing off, you figure out how to break the enchanted barrier," Tia ordered Zane.

"The enchanted barrier?" Zane muttered, his eyes scanning the back of the chamber. 

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

Just then, he caught a faint purple shimmer forming around the Soulforge. He was suddenly reminded of his Emerald Crystal—an artifact made specifically for breaking enchantments.

This was Tia's new plan. It was possible Frank's Blood-Dragon Spear couldn't pierce the barrier, but Zane's Titan Strike had a much better chance—as long as he had enough Vitality to power it.

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

"Not yet… we're close," Tia said through gritted teeth, pulling out four supreme Vitality crystals. "That's it! Just a little more!" she urged, gripping them tightly and drawing out the Vitality locked inside.

The Vitality within a single supreme crystal was so pure and powerful that Zane probably couldn't have handled it. But Tia was different. She absorbed the energy from all four at once, a testament to her insane internal capacity for power.

Sure enough, the moment she absorbed the Vitality, she began funneling it all into Zane.

His Corrupt Vitality devoured every drop she sent him, converting it into a swirling mass of purple-black energy. 

[SFX: CRYSTAL SHATTERING]

[AMBIENT: ENERGY CRACKLING]

Just as he was about to drain her dry, she pulled out a few more supreme crystals, shattering them to absorb their power.

They repeated the process over and over, and the cloud of Corrupt Vitality surrounding Zane grew larger and more menacing.

Even though this was nothing compared to the power he’d amassed from the Nether Phantoms on the Death Road, it was still an immense amount of energy.

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

Meanwhile, the Fiery Dragon Spirit grew more and more agitated, its attacks becoming fiercer. Realizing she couldn't afford any more distractions, Tia refocused completely on the battle between her phoenix and the spirit.

Tia shouted to Zane, her voice ringing with hope. "Go! Now! Smash that enchanted barrier to pieces! I’m counting on you!" 

The last time she'd tried this, she had failed spectacularly. The Fiery Dragon Spirit had ambushed her mid-attempt, shattering her plans. She had brought Zane along this time because she knew she couldn't do it alone. With his help, she was determined to succeed.

[AMBIENT: DARK ENERGY SWIRLING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane stretched his hands toward the ceiling and began to gather the roiling Corrupt Vitality.

The sheer force he could unleash with this much Vitality would be massive. It could potentially put his power above even the top masters of the Soul Realm, maybe even matching the lower levels of the Inner-Core Realm.

If he had a continuous supply of Vitality, his power could theoretically last indefinitely, growing stronger and stronger. Of course, that was just in theory. It wasn't a practical combat technique. You rarely get the chance to just drain Vitality from someone else, and the whole process was way too slow. No opponent would be dumb enough to just stand there and let you power up.

[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

Zane didn't move toward the Soulforge. Instead, he turned to face Tia directly, his expression calm but resolute. “Before I do this, I need you to swear that you won't kill me afterwards. A Warrior’s Vow.”

Tia laughed, shaking her head. "You actually think you're in a position to make demands? Do you have any idea who I am? What I've accomplished? And you—a  little Adept Realm cultivator—dare to demand a Warrior’s Vow from me?"
A Warrior’s Vow was no small thing. Break it, and a heart demon took root — one that could erupt without warning and consume you from the inside. Even resisting it would poison your path forward. No warrior gave one lightly.

Zane just sighed heavily. "Fine. Guess you don't need me to break that barrier, then."

"How dare you?" Tia yelled, her voice tight with frustration.

[MUSIC: COLD DETERMINATION]

Zane just shrugged, which only infuriated her more. "There's not much I wouldn't dare," he said coldly. He was safe inside the flood dragon puppet as long as he wasn't reckless. He was done wasting time with her. He should just find a way out and leave.

Seeing Zane actually start to walk away—while she was still locked in a desperate battle with the Fiery Dragon Spirit—completely threw her. They were so close, and now everything was about to fall apart.

After he'd taken a few steps, Tia's jaw clenched. "Okay, you win," she spat.

[MUSIC: DARK PROMISE]

"Zane, right?" she said, her voice dripping with ice. "I'll remember that name. And I swear, I will make you regret this. But for now… fine. I won’t kill you, I swear!"

Zane smiled. “Great. There's just one more thing. And you're really not gonna like this one.”
