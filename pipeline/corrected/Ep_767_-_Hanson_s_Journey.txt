Ep 767 - Hanson's Journey

Word count: 1036

[AMBIENT: TENSE ROOM ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane couldn't believe the turn this day had taken. A beggar had just been shown inside — a man carrying ancient crystals that pulsed with Raw Vitality, the first real hope anyone had brought to Tia's door.

 It was a long shot, but Zane was desperate enough to take any chance he could get.

He couldn't waste another second; there was only one way to find out if it could save Tia. Tia, once so full of life, was fading. The Life and Death Wind had drained her almost completely, and he didn't know how much longer she had. He just had to pray she could absorb the energy from this crystal.

[AMBIENT: QUIET SICKROOM]

 He ushered the beggar into Tia's room, the air thick with tension. Zane held out his hand, his voice flat and cold. "Give it to me."

The beggar flinched, taking a step back and hunching defensively. "And if it works? What do I get?" he demanded, his eyes darting around cautiously.

"You'll get your reward," Zane replied, his patience already fraying, "provided this isn't a waste of my time."

But the man shook his head. "What you're offering is useless to a warrior in the Adept Realm like me," he said, his tone shifting. "I want to join the Cloud Hall. Or the World Merchants' Guild."

Patience was a luxury Zane didn't have. He shot the man a look that could cut steel. "If the crystal works, I can make that happen. Now stop wasting my time and give it to me."

The man nodded and handed over the crystal. The moment Zane's fingers closed around it, he noticed how warm it was — not the cold smoothness of ordinary stone, but something almost alive with the energy inside it. He crossed to Tia's bedside and held it above her.

He hesitated. He wasn't sure how to release the Raw Vitality from within.

"It has to be crushed," the beggar said quietly. "That's what the family records say. The energy will release on its own once the stone breaks."

[SFX: CRYSTAL CRACKING]

 Zane pressed his fingers together. A hairline fracture split across the surface, and wisps of pale white light seeped out through the cracks.

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

The light was strange — luminous, unlike anything he had seen before. Instinctively, he pulled his hand back, afraid of absorbing it himself. The wisps hovered above Tia for a breathless moment. Then they sank into her.

He watched. He waited. And then — barely, almost imperceptibly — her aura strengthened. Just a thread. Just enough.

It worked.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: QUIET RUINS]

The beggar’s name was Hanson Lee. A century ago, his house had fallen, leaving nothing but ruins and eight ancient crystals—the family's most precious heirlooms, left untouched for generations. But then the World Merchants' Guild made an announcement: a massive reward for anyone who could save a woman named Tia. The notice mentioned a need for Raw Vitality, and Hanson decided to take a chance. House Lee was history, but maybe he could build a new future for himself.

According to the old family records, the crystals contained Raw Vitality, and from the look on Zane’s face, they actually worked.

Zane turned from Tia and fixed his gaze on Hanson. "How many crystals do you have?"

Hanson was no fool. He knew that showing up with all eight pieces at once would get him a terrible deal. Worse, some warrior stronger than him might just take them, and that would be the end of that. 

So he’d hatched a plan. Before even entering Oracle City, he’d found seven different spots to hide the other crystals. Only he knew where they were.

The problem was, he’d never expected Zane to see right through him. Hanson fidgeted, his mind racing for an answer. That slight hesitation was all the confirmation Zane needed.

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

 "How many more are there?" Zane asked again, his voice dropping, harder this time. "Where are they?"

 "I… uh…" Hanson stammered. He’d planned this all out—he was going to make Zane swear a warrior's oath to keep his promise. That would have been his leverage, even after giving up the other seven stones. But under Zane’s glacial stare, the words caught in his throat.

Zane’s patience finally snapped. "I will keep my word," he ground out. "You will be a member of the Cloud Hall, under its full protection. Now answer me." He had no more time to bargain. He needed to know.

 "Seven!" The word tumbled out of Hanson’s mouth in a rush. "I have seven more. I buried them all outside Oracle City."

"Seven…"

Zane grabbed Hanson by the shoulder. "Take me."

[SFX: RUSH OF WIND, RAPID MOVEMENT]

Under Hanson's guidance, Zane unearthed all seven crystals and returned with him to the hall. Then he crushed them one by one, holding each above Tia, guiding every wisp of pale light down into her.

He waited a long time. She didn't move.

[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]

 "It's not enough," Zane said quietly.

Hanson tensed. He already knew. Still, he had to ask. "You'll still keep your promise?"

"The World Merchants' Guild will pay you one thousand supreme Aether crystals," Zane said. "The Relic — I can't give you that. What you brought didn't wake her." He paused. "But there's one more thing I need from you."

Hanson's face had already broken into relief. One thousand supreme Aether crystals was a fortune — more than House Lee had held even at its peak. He hadn't dared hope for a Relic. Something like that would only paint a target on his back.

"Tell me everything," Zane said, his voice low and intense. "Where did these crystals come from? Your family records — what do they say? Every detail. One thousand supreme Aether crystals, and you'll become an elite disciple of the Cloud Hall."

With the Corrupted Divine Kingdom and the Ethereal Spirit Sect now gone, the Cloud Hall was the most powerful order in the Heartlands, second only to the World Merchants' Guild itself. Warriors from every corner of the world were clamoring to join, and the competition was brutal.

For an Adept with mediocre talent like Hanson Lee, getting in was a fantasy. And now, Zane was offering him a chance to live that fantasy. An opportunity to change his entire life.

How could he possibly let it slip away?
