Ep 1049 - Still Rising

Word count: 1151

[AMBIENT: VAST ECHOING HALL]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

The Hall of Arms was massive, but the dome arching above Zane topped out at only about two hundred feet.

Without the crushing gravity, any warrior could’ve easily shot to the top.

[SFX: HEAVY PRESSURE WHOOSH]

By the time Zane hit fifty feet, the gravity felt like a mountain pressing down on his shoulders.

But it was nothing to Zane. He kept a steady pace, rising without so much as breaking a sweat. His eyes were locked on the pinprick of blue light at the dome’s center—his target.

Another twenty feet up, and he was high enough to brush his fingers against the lowest tier of glowing white dots.

Zane could see all kinds of Grade-Three weapons shimmering inside the white lights, but he refused to be distracted. He looked away and kept going. He could only choose one weapon, and picking one this low would just get him dragged back down.

[SFX: INTENSE GRAVITATIONAL PULSE]

At seventy feet, the gravitational pull doubled, and the pressure really started to build.

But it was still nothing, and he kept rising.

[SFX: POWERFUL GRAVITY SURGE]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL HUMMING]

At a hundred feet, the gravity spiked again. Now, he was surrounded by floating Grade-Three Divine Weapons.

He kept going up.

When he hit one hundred twenty feet, the gravity surged again. The lights pulsing around him were now Grade-Two Divine Weapons.

He kept rising.

[AMBIENT: SILENT HALL]

Down below, the warriors in the Hall of Arms had fallen completely silent.

They were just now realizing Zane’s calm expression wasn’t some act.

He couldn’t fake it even if he wanted to, not when it took every ounce of strength just to be up there.

When Ryder reached this height, his face had been beet-red with strain. But Zane’s expression hadn’t changed in the slightest. He showed no sign of effort.

He kept rising.

At one hundred thirty feet, the gravity pressed harder still. Zane passed through it without breaking stride.

Then, at one hundred forty feet, it hit.

[SFX: GRAVITY SURGE]

The gravity didn’t increase — it doubled all at once, compounding on top of every previous doubling. The cumulative effect was staggering. For a moment, Zane’s body trembled.

The low voices that had been building in the hall dropped even further. Every eye was on him.

The elder let out a quiet breath. There it was. He’d finally hit his ceiling. If Zane could have maintained constant speed all the way to the top, he would have been something beyond description — and the elder had long since stopped believing that kind of talent existed.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Then Zane stopped trembling.

In an instant — less than a breath — he adapted. His pace steadied. The perfectly constant speed resumed, as calm and unhurried as it had been at fifty feet.

The elder went very still, his shock plain on his face.

“That boy’s climbed so high, and he’s still maintaining his speed,” he muttered. “The gravity should be crushing him by now.”

Usually, warriors shot up fast and then slowed to a crawl as they climbed. But Zane had maintained complete control all the way past one hundred forty feet — and after that tremble, had simply adapted and kept going. If he suddenly unleashed his full power and really went for it, just how high could he go?

Just moments ago, the elder had wanted to discourage Zane from even trying.

But when he’d seen Zane’s expression — serious, settled, certain — he’d held his tongue. Young people needed drive. Impossible ambitions were worth having.

Even if Zane failed, the attempt would do him good. He’d placed the Thundergust in the dome without another word — and genuinely hadn’t expected Zane to reach it.

That assessment had already changed. At one hundred twenty feet, with Grade-Two weapons drifting around him and Zane still rising at a steady pace, the elder had quietly revised his prediction.

The red orbs — Ryder’s four swords still floating in the dome — were now a real possibility. Getting a Grade-One Divine Weapon on a first visit to the Hall of Arms would be extraordinary. Remarkable even by the standards of the other twelve palaces.

He still didn’t think Zane could reach the blue orb. But a Grade-One? Possibly.

[AMBIENT: GRAVITY CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: INTENSE SUSPENSE]

One hundred forty feet was where the gravity jumped sharply to a new level. That was where most warriors broke. The elder had watched it happen countless times. He’d given Zane until one hundred forty feet.

Now, he was scrambling for a Message Rune.

[SFX: MAGICAL RUNE ACTIVATION]

As it flared to life, he recorded a message.

[AMBIENT: HALL OF ARMS]

“Master, there’s a young man here… he’s going for a weapon. Looks like a new recruit for the Mist Palace. I don’t know who brought him in, but… this kid is something else.”

Before he could finish, Yolande’s cool voice cut in, “I know.”

“How?” the elder asked, startled. He hadn’t even finished his thought, and the Palace Leader wasn’t even in the Hall of Arms. How could she possibly know who he meant?

[MUSIC: CONFIDENT THEME]

“I’m the one who brought Zane to the Mist Palace,” Yolande’s voice came back. “Let him take whatever he can get.”

The connection cut out. She had promised Zane she would help him restore Lavender’s soul, and she intended to keep her word.

Yolande hadn’t been surprised. Of course, the elder was talking about Zane. There were probably other warriors in the Hall of Arms right now, but she was certain Zane was the only one who could get that old man this worked up. The two recruits Harvey had brought in were nothing special; they wouldn’t have made the elder bat an eye.

No matter what, she had a lot of faith in Zane.

[AMBIENT: QUIET HALL]

Across the hall, Ryder stood very still. He’d been in Groundstone Hall long enough to know what he was seeing — someone doing effortlessly what had nearly destroyed him. He regretted staying. Watching it happen in real time, step by steady step, was a different kind of pain than simply hearing about it later. He said nothing.

Matthew had even less to say. The gap between him and Zane wasn’t something words could bridge. He glanced at his brother’s face and kept his mouth shut.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

After the Message Rune went dark, the elder looked back up at Zane. So, this was the boy the Palace Leader had personally brought in.

Still, the elder thought, just being brought in by the leader herself didn’t explain this. The Thirteen Palaces were designed to scout and train talent for the Alliance. Every year, the leaders and vice-leaders brought in countless gifted prospects.

But even among the best of them, a talent like Zane’s was almost unheard of.
