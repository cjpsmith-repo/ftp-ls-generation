Ep 1100 - Stars

Word count: 1204

[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

If it had been any other Soul Sea Realm warrior, the force of that punch would have blown them to pieces.

Lincoln Rhodes had smashed countless opponents in the Villain World with that single move, and his Form of the Steel Spirit had kept him alive through every battle. He knew exactly how powerful he was, and he was damn proud of it. It had been a long time since an opponent had actually surprised him.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE SHOCKWAVE]

But the moment his fist connected with Zane, a force as vast and deep as the ocean shot through his entire body. The golden light enveloping him flickered, and he was sent stumbling backward across the platform, only stopping when his heels hit the very edge.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

He stared in disbelief. “That kind of power…” he muttered, his mind reeling. “This kid’s body is actually tougher than mine!”

Every attunement method had its own unique strengths. He wasn’t sure what law Zane followed, but it definitely wasn’t the Metal Law. For warriors who practiced the Metal Law, the ultimate prize was the Form of the Adamant Soul. So how had Zane made his body so tough?

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

Zane flexed his hand. He’d put the strength of eight thousand Dragon Scales into that last punch. A normal Soul Sea Realm warrior could barely handle two or three thousand. He’d knocked back War King York with a single strike, and a hit with the force of eight thousand Dragon Scales would have killed the man on the spot.

Zane watched Lincoln from across the platform, seeing that his opponent had only been forced back. Other than that, he seemed completely unharmed. The man was far stronger than he’d expected.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

Outside the crucible, a collective sigh rippled through the crowd. Fights like this, up close and personal, were always the most brutal.

The mood began to shift. It wasn’t just about who would win anymore. They wanted to see someone bleed.

Lincoln’s eyes locked onto Zane. “So that’s your trump card,” he said, his lips splitting into a wide grin. “A body like a Divine Weapon and terrifying strength.” The confidence was back in his eyes, but he was clearly being more careful now. “But that alone might not be enough to beat me.”

[SFX: STONE CRACK]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The next moment, a crack appeared on his forehead, revealing a small, pale green stone embedded within.

[SFX: WHISPER ECHO]

A voice cut through Zane’s mind—Lavender, hidden and watching. “Zane, be careful. There’s no time to explain everything; just know that once it activates, his strength is going to spike—hard. It’s strange that something this dangerous would even be allowed into Passing the Torch.”

[AMBIENT: HUSHED ARENA]

Outside the crucible, the Lord of the Sunset World’s gaze had fixed on the stone in Lincoln’s forehead, his expression shifting from confusion to something closer to alarm. “Both of these siblings carry the Wrath of the Ocean? That’s...”

Another Palace Lord turned toward Yolande. “Layla’s fate is clearly tied to the Battle of the Worlds. What about her brother—does he carry something similar?”

Yolande considered this for a long moment. “That’s not something we can answer by looking. We’d need to have it divined.”

Zane understood enough to know the stone meant trouble. He set his stance, bracing for whatever came next.

But before either of them could move again, a voice rang out across the arena—sharp, furious, and unmistakably Layla’s.

“Get away from me! I am not in the mood for this. Push me again, and I will end you!”

Both Zane and Lincoln froze, their fight forgotten, and turned toward the platform next door.

The ghost cultivator had pressed his advantage too far—gusts of cold wind howled around Layla as a ring of skull-shaped phantoms closed in, boxing her into a corner. “Little girl, it’s a trial,” he sneered. “I can’t just let you walk away. Yield, or—”

[SFX: SWORD CLATTER]

He never finished. Layla flung her sword aside, and her whole body arched backward, black hair whipping through the air. The crack on her own forehead split wider, and the green stone beneath it flared to life.

[MUSIC: RISING CRESCENDO]

A single point of light bloomed between her eyebrows—and the instant Zane laid eyes on it, every instinct in his body screamed that it was lethal.

[SFX: BLINDING FLASH]

The light swelled, becoming a sphere, then doubling, tripling in size, swallowing Layla whole before spreading outward to engulf the entire platform. The ghost cultivator had nowhere left to run. He vanished into the light without a sound.

[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]

When the glow finally faded, even Zane couldn’t hold back a sharp breath. The illusory platforms in this space were said to be indestructible—they’d absorbed battle after battle without a mark. This one hadn’t survived. Where the platform had been, only a single stone pillar remained, its top burned into a smooth, curved hollow. Layla knelt at its center, pale and trembling, her hair pooling around her like ink.

[MUSIC: QUIET PIANO]

“Brother... do your best,” she murmured, her voice barely audible. Whatever she’d just done, it had cost her dearly—it looked like she’d burned through her own life essence to do it. The old man overseeing the trial didn’t send her out. Instead, he let her stay, kneeling there, to watch the rest of the fight.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

From the sidelines, Adrian watched, completely uninterested in whatever history Lincoln had with Layla. All he cared about was seeing him defeat Zane. He’d once thought Layla was weaker than Zane, but after seeing the power she’d displayed, he was stunned—and nervous for Lincoln. He really didn’t want him to lose this fight.

“Fate is cruel,” he muttered, shaking his head. “No matter what we do, some things just don’t go our way.” He resigned himself to whatever came next. “In the end, it’s seventy percent effort and thirty percent destiny. Nothing more to be done about it now.”

Lincoln let a faint smile touch his lips, his chest swelling with pride for his sister.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

After activating the Wrath of the Ocean, the golden light around his body blazed even brighter. He clenched his fists and turned back to Zane. “Fight me!” he roared.

[AMBIENT: VAST OPEN SKY]

[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]

Zane just nodded. A silent understanding passed between them. At this point, the outcome didn’t matter. Lincoln was just as proud as Layla, but he fought with principle. It wasn’t every day you met an opponent like this.

A thrill of excitement hummed through Zane’s body. His expression grew serious as he met Lincoln’s gaze.

[SFX: CELESTIAL RUMBLE]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

And then, high in the endless starry sky of the Orange Sea Realm, a few distant points of light suddenly flared to life.

These were stars that had always been there, hanging far above the universe, shining down on countless worlds. But they were usually too remote to be seen, and it was rare for human eyes to witness them shine so brilliantly.

[SFX: ENERGY BEAM DESCENDING]

Right now, though, those few rays of starlight shot straight down toward the crucible.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

The instant the stars flickered, the Lord of the Sunset World frowned, his expression twisting from confusion into pure shock. “He hasn’t cultivated the Stellar Body, has he?”

A palace leader standing nearby echoed his surprise. “That’s the Transcendent Divine Might that Supreme Lord Star left behind ages ago. Where in the world did Zane learn it?”
