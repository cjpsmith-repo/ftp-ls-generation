Ep 960 - The Wedding Guest
Word count: 1057
[AMBIENT: BUSY KITCHEN]
[MUSIC: LIGHT TENSION]
Cora was keeping busy in the kitchens of Cloud Hall. Her grip on the kitchen knife was unnaturally stiff, but her hands moved so fast the blade was just a blur of cold, silver light.
Today was Tia and Zane’s wedding, and her mind was a million miles away. The knife, woven from wraithgold threads, was incredibly sharp. Spacing out, Cora didn’t even notice the deep gashes she was carving into the ironwood chopping board.
The chatter of voices from outside kept pulling at her attention.
Cora pursed her lips in frustration as her chopping grew more frantic, reducing the vegetables on the board to a watery pulp.
[SFX: DRAGON CAWS]
A soft caw came from the floor. The Ancestral Dragon was curled into a tiny ball nearby.
Ever since the Ancestral Dragon had snuck a taste of Cora’s cooking a while back, it had all but forgotten about Zane. The little glutton had been glued to Cora’s side almost every day since.
Cora was in a daze again, her focus pulled toward the commotion outside as her hands slowed. The Ancestral Dragon cawed again, trying to rush her.
“Be patient. Your food will be ready in a minute,” Cora said with a smile, completely oblivious to the vegetable sludge she’d created.
[SFX: BOOMS]
High above Cloud Hall, sparks of shimmering light rocketed into the sky, bursting into a beautiful, multi-colored cloud. Cora saw it all clearly, but she didn’t stop. She’d already hacked the ironwood chopping board to splinters.
“Caw, caw,” the Ancestral Dragon whimpered again.
[AMBIENT: TENSE KITCHEN ATMOSPHERE]
“Just a minute,” Cora soothed, a faint smile still plastered on her face. But it never reached her eyes. It was a mask, rigid and unmoving.
[SFX: DRAGON CAWS]
[SFX: LOUD BANG]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]
Cora slammed the kitchen knife down on the furnace. Her whole body trembled as a powerful energy flared out from her. “Didn’t I tell you to wait?”
[SFX: DRAGON CAWS]
Sensing she was about to snap, a shiver went down the Ancestral Dragon’s spine. It quickly coiled into a tight ball, hiding its head.
[AMBIENT: GRAND WEDDING HALL]
[MUSIC: CEREMONIAL WEDDING THEME]
Meanwhile, in the Martial Arts Hall, the grand wedding ceremony was well underway. Warrior weddings were typically solemn, formal affairs, and the cost of a single part of the rite could be enough to feed a normal family for a hundred years. Tia and Zane’s wedding, however, was a far more relaxed event.
Surrounded by a chorus of well-wishes and congratulations, Zane stood in the middle of the Martial Arts Hall, waiting for his bride.
[MUSIC: ROMANTIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
Soon, she and her attendants came into view, floating slowly toward him on a cloud. All eyes were on Tia, breathtaking in a ceremonial white gown and flanked by her beautifully dressed attendants.
[SFX: DISTANT LAUGHTER]
The laugh came from far away — from the horizon, it seemed — and rolled down over the whole gathering like a wave. Every head turned. No one could pinpoint the source.
Zane’s brows drew together. He didn’t know who was coming. Today of all days.
Then a figure dropped from the sky. An old man in white.
[MUSIC: UNEASY CURIOSITY]
A ripple of unease moved through the crowd. The Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors present tried to read him and got nothing. Two or three Life-and-Death Realm independents in the back did the same. Also nothing. Every face in the hall carried some version of the same expression.
Zane’s own read: above Spirit Supreme Realm. Possibly far above. The kind of power that didn’t announce itself.
“Just having you here to celebrate our wedding day is more than enough,” Zane said warmly to an unexpected, disheveled old man who had appeared. “Please, sit and enjoy the celebration!”
The other guests nodded in agreement.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]
Nearby, Brent watched the old man with undisguised interest. He had no idea who this was, but the timing was perfect. His sister was in a kitchen somewhere in this building, holding herself together with both hands. If something — anything — disrupted this ceremony, he wouldn’t exactly be heartbroken.
Not everyone in the hall was celebrating in good faith. The heads of the Black Mountain Order and a handful of other factions that had once clashed with Zane were present — smiling, bowing, saying the right things. But underneath it, they were watching carefully. They had submitted because they had no choice. If this mysterious old man turned out to be trouble for Zane, none of them would be rushing to help.
Two maids hurried over with an extra table, placing it in a seat of honor. Despite his messy appearance, they knew he was a being of unimaginable power; they could tell from the way the top masters of The Heartlands deferred to him.
To their surprise, the old man just shook his head. “No!”
Zane’s eyes flashed with confusion. “I’m sorry?”
“A wedding is one of the biggest events in a person’s life! How could I possibly come without a gift?” the old man insisted, shaking his head firmly.
A wave of helplessness washed over Zane. He fought the urge to roll his eyes, keeping a polite smile fixed on his face. Why do old men get so stubborn with age? he wondered. One day, I’ll probably be just like him.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
However, before Zane could say anything, a few of the bolder guests started shouting.
“If you want to give them a wedding gift, then show us!”
“You’re just trying to build suspense. You’ve already got it picked out, right?”
“Then let everyone see what it is!”
These guests weren’t particularly powerful—only at the primary stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm—but it was Zane’s wedding day. They figured that no matter how powerful this old man was, he wouldn’t dare make a scene.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BUILD]
The old man chuckled and nodded. “Ha ha, how’d you know I already had a gift prepared?” With that, he vanished in a flash, reappearing directly in front of Zane before anyone could even react.
Then, he stared hard at Zane, his eyes wide.
[SFX: BODY THUD]
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED GASPS]
To the shock of everyone in the hall, Zane’s own eyes fluttered shut, and he collapsed to the ground.
