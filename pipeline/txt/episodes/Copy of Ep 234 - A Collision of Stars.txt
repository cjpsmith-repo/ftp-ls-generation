Ep 234 - A Collision of Stars
Word Count: 1230
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
With just three days left until the All Peaks Competition, the ranking lists across all Thirty-Three Peaks were about to be frozen. During this sacred three-day period, all duels would be suspended, cementing the final roster of competitors in stone---a tradition as old as the competition itself.
[AMBIENT: BUSY ARENA]
Because of this impending freeze, the day before the lists were finalized was always a whirlwind of frenzied activity in arenas across every peak. The air crackled with tension; sweat glistened on determined faces as disciples fought with everything they possessed.
Cultivators who had strategically hidden their true abilities now unleashed their full potential in desperate, last-minute attempts to claim coveted positions. Meanwhile, those already fortunate enough to hold positions on the list battled with the ferocity of cornered beasts, their faces etched with the grim knowledge that a single misstep could shatter their dreams.
Hearts pounded, breaths came in ragged gasps, and the clash of weapons echoed across training grounds as years of preparation culminated in these final, crucial moments.
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC ARENA SOUNDS]
Misty Peak's arena was just as chaotic as the rest.
Unlike Zane and Eliot, whose positions remained unchallenged due to their formidable reputations, every disciple ranked from third to seventh faced desperate challengers determined to claim their spots.
In a remarkable turn of events that left spectators gasping, the ninth-ranked disciple toppled the complacent sixth seed with a lightning-fast series of strikes. Even more impossibly, the twenty-second-ranked disciple defeated the seventh seed with a stunning display of tactical brilliance that drew thunderous applause from the crowd.
As the final gong sounded, signaling the end of challenges, a heavy silence fell over the arena. Disciples collapsed in exhaustion or celebrated with tearful embraces, the culmination of years of training decided in these frantic hours.
With the rankings now frozen until after the competition, the master of Misty Peak submitted the finalized list of participants.
Then came the waiting.
[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION ROOM]
[MUSIC: CALM MEDITATION]
For the next three days, Zane didn't set foot in Emberfin Lake or on the Seven-Star Platform. He stayed in his room and meditated.
He poured his focus into two techniques: the Titan Strike, a fundamental method for weaponizing Vitality, and the Psyche Spike, a far more esoteric art for attacking the soul itself. Both were incredibly deep and complex.
Alongside his meditation on those techniques, Zane continued to study the Tapestry of Sun, Moon, and Stars.
The first time he'd laid eyes on the tapestry, he had felt an irresistible pull, a connection that had since vanished on subsequent viewings.
After more than twenty hours of fruitless meditation, he was about to give up when the world around him began to distort. He assumed it was exhaustion — his soul drained from the effort — and blinked to clear his vision. The spinning only accelerated. Before he could make sense of it, the space folded around him and pulled him in.
The sun, moon, and stars had always been a source of endless mystery, the subjects of countless myths and legends.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL SPACE]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL WONDER]
Even as a consciousness within this space, far from the actual sun, Zane was still staggered by the raw power radiating from it.
"My consciousness is inside the Tapestry," he thought. "It has to be fate that brought me here. It's frustrating I've barely scratched the surface of what the Tapestry of Sun, Moon, and Stars can teach me. At my current level, the sun is just... too much to comprehend."
Zane turned his gaze toward another sphere, its bright light much calmer and gentler than the sun's overwhelming force.
[AMBIENT: NIGHT SKY]
Looking at the sun made his eyes ache. Staring at this sphere was like a cool, soothing balm.
It was the moon.
Zane watched a shadow creep across its surface, carving out the familiar shapes of its cycle.
And behind these two celestial giants were countless stars.
These stars were what he truly wanted to understand.
During his last meditation, he'd managed to form a handful of them from his Corrupt Vitality. The resulting explosions had released an incredible amount of energy.
But Zane knew that was just a parlor trick compared to their true potential. He felt driven to unravel the mysteries of the tapestry, one piece at a time.
[AMBIENT: MEDITATIVE ATMOSPHERE]
Since he was already here, he settled down cross-legged and focused on the brilliant stars in the distance.
"This Tapestry is incredible," he thought, awestruck. "How did the master who made this manage to capture the entire universe in a single image?"
His eyes gleamed, and he sank into a deep meditation.
[SFX: RUSHING ENERGY]
Suddenly, a star pulsing with a terrifying, raw energy rushed toward him from the distant darkness.
"Is that going to hit me?" he wondered, instinctively trying to dodge.
But he was only a consciousness here, not a physical body. He was powerless to move.
[MUSIC: COSMIC WONDER]
The star shot past him in the blink of an eye, its sheer mass creating the illusion of a near-miss despite the vast distance. Then it collided with another star, far off in the void.
The two stars imploded, and the resulting energy release was more than ten times greater than anything he had ever created.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
The light from the explosion formed a colossal ring, beautiful and utterly deadly.
"A collision of stars?"
Until now, Zane had only ever used his Vitality to detonate the stars. The energy released from a collision... it was on a whole other level. In that instant, he felt a revelation click into place, a breakthrough shimmering just beyond his grasp.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN SUMMIT]
On the morning of the competition, three mentors waited at the summit platform beside a Skycruiser. Zane scanned the group. Isla wasn't there.
She'd promised to be back before the competition. He pulled one of the mentors aside. "Has Master Novak returned?"
The mentor shook his head. "She hasn't been back since she took her leave. I don't know any more than that."
Zane said nothing. He boarded the Skycruiser with the others, his worry filed quietly alongside everything else he couldn't act on yet.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Master Silas had definitely already seen the list of competitors for the All Peaks Competition. He was clearly just asking Master Warren about it to get a rise out of him and embarrass Misty Peak.
The disciples behind him sized up the Misty Peak group with smug, mocking stares.
Master Warren snorted. "What do you mean, 'just them'? Misty Peak may only be sending seven, but each one has earned their place!"
"You think you'll do better just because you have more people?" he shot back. "Think again, Silas. I doubt numbers matter one bit!"
[SFX: MOCKING LAUGHTER]
Master Silas' eyes swept over Zane and the other Misty Peak disciples before he burst out laughing. "Earned their place? Are you serious? It looks like you're scraping the bottom of the barrel! Why else bring someone who's only in the second tier of the Adept Realm?"
The skinny old man cackled, his laughter echoing in their faces.
Since Zane had been standing in the middle of the group, no one had paid much attention to his cultivation level. But Master Silas' jab put him squarely in the spotlight.
Suddenly, every eye was on him.
