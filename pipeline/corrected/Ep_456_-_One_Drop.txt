Ep 456 - One Drop

Word count: 997

[AMBIENT: STUNNED CROWD]

[MUSIC: MAGICAL MYSTERY]

The crowd fell silent. The weight of Kieran's words settled over them.

What he was really suggesting—though he'd never say it outright—was that once Zane reached his full potential, even Arnold would find himself outmatched.

And it wasn't mere speculation. Zane had already achieved perfect brushwork, and he was barely eighteen. Kieran had spent hours examining the meditation rune Michael brought him, scrutinizing every stroke and curve with meticulous care. The flawless execution had impressed him, certainly, but what truly unsettled him was the design itself—a configuration unlike anything in his decades of study.

No teenager should know how to craft something so sophisticated. The complexity suggested years of accumulated wisdom, perhaps even insights from a master's lifetime of work. But Michael wasn't prone to exaggeration or deception. His reputation at the Guild depended on accuracy.

If Michael vouched for the boy's authorship, then it had to be true, however improbable it seemed. Kieran had no choice but to take his word for it.

"Mister Kenneth, are you and Zane just here to mess with us?" Lady Darkmere asked, completely baffled.

Raven was shaking his head too. They weren't going to believe Zane could rival Arnold unless they saw it with their own eyes. What was that supposed to mean, anyway? Arnold’s skill with runes was the stuff of legend. How could some kid's runes even be in the same league?

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Hobbes snarled, his face twisting into a mask of fury."Kieran's not trying to mess with you. It sounds insane, but it's the truth!"

The three powerhouses couldn't have been more different. Kieran was unpredictable but sharp. Hobbes, on the other hand, was a constant storm of rage, like a bear with a thorn in its paw.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

"Hobbes, get a hold of yourself," Kieran said, shaking his head, annoyed. "Look, if you're having this much trouble believing it, I can just show you the runes Zane drew. Maybe then you'll believe me."

Watching this unfold, Tia only grew more suspicious. Something wasn't right. Could Zane really be that talented? Why would he offer to fix the rune on the sixth floor for free? Did he have some other motive for being here?

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA ATMOSPHERE]

Most leaders shared the same suspicion.

As Kieran prepared to retrieve Zane's work, Zane shook his head. "Why bother? Send someone to test the Dragon Soar Arena."

The Soul Realm experts fell silent. They'd been so absorbed in Kieran's revelation that they'd forgotten their original purpose.

"Right! Vincent, you go try it!" Raven tossed a Dragon Jade Pendant to a Black Mountain Order warrior.

Vincent approached the platform. Ascending to the Immortal Realm was every cultivator's ultimate dream.

Envy flickered across the gathered faces as Vincent stepped onto the platform.

Half an hour later, Vincent reached the summit.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: HAPPY VICTORY]

Brilliant white light erupted from the platform's peak. Every powerful cultivator in Cloudhaven watched intently.

[SFX: LIGHT SURGE]

Blazing light shot skyward, piercing the heavens before vanishing.

"He succeeded!" Excitement animated every face. Vincent had ascended. Zane's words were true.

Tia's expression grew strange. Was Zane truly that talented? Why repair the rune willingly? Was there a hidden trap?

[SFX: CRYSTAL CARD REVEAL]

After Vincent left the arena, Raven held up a crystal card. "This is Vincent's life crystal," he announced. "If he dies, this card shatters."

[SFX: CRYSTAL SPINNING]

With that, he casually tossed the card onto the arena’s peak, where it began to spin. They wouldn't know if Vincent was safe for a while, but none of them considered that he might not be coming back at all.

[AMBIENT: CALM ARENA]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

With solid proof that the arena was secure again, the powerful warriors of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm decided to relax. They had all the time in the world, so there was no need to rush a test run.

Honestly, none of them were that thrilled about ascending to the upper world. What they really wanted was control over the arena—a guaranteed escape route.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]

The stronger these warriors became, the more they feared death. Even with their long lifespans, they knew the end was inevitable. Time was a blade hanging over their heads, waiting to fall. The second they felt old age creeping in, they'd scramble for any treasure that could extend their lives. They'd even risk it all to reach The Empyrean Realm, just for a shot at growing stronger.

If a breakthrough in their training failed them, they could always pin their hopes on finding another chance in the upper world. That was the mindset of most of the powerful warriors in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CONVERSATION]

"So, we're good? Can I go?" Zane asked, tilting his head at Kieran.

Kieran just nodded. "Now that the Dragon Soar Arena is taken care of, I doubt anyone in Oracle City has the power to stop you."

The Spirit-Tribulation warriors watching from the sidelines decided to let it go. After all, there was no honor in going after a kid from the Soul Realm. Still, they needed to discuss this in private. The arena had private chambers for its most important guests, a category Tia certainly fell into.

[AMBIENT: GRAND PALACE INTERIOR]

[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER ACTIVATION]

The moment Zane stepped into the palace, Tia waved her hand. A massive snowflake pattern bloomed across the ground, expanding into an enchanted barrier that sealed them inside.

"Now, no one can eavesdrop," Tia said, her voice low. "Be honest with me. You said anyone who ascends to the upper world is imprisoned for a thousand years. So how did you get back?"

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

[SFX: STORAGE RING OPENING]

A wide grin spread across Zane's face. He pulled a small container from his Storage Ring and tossed it to her. "According to the laws of the dragon-blooded," he explained, "any human who carries even a single drop of true dragon's blood becomes one of us. This is for you."

[SFX: PULSING ENERGY]

Tia stared at the vial. Inside, a single drop of blood pulsed with a life of its own. For all her power and experience, she felt her throat go dry, at a complete loss for words.

"So," she finally breathed, "this is true dragon's blood?"
