Ep 42 - Marked by the Fanes
Word Count: 1212
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Zane had no idea, but miles away, just outside Bloody Mountain, a man's hands were balled into tight, white-knuckled fists.
[AMBIENT: HUSHED GATHERING]
Outside the mountain, the mentors of the Skyreach Sect huddled around glowing Engraved Slabs, each one a window into the treacherous landscape. This was the most crucial part of The Gauntlet for them—the observation period.
They were all scouting for raw talent, hoping to snag the most promising students for their own peaks before another mentor did. While the students who passed would ultimately get to choose their mentors, an early endorsement could sway any decision.
Each participant in The Gauntlet had been issued a Student ID, a small token that broadcasted their location and status directly to the mentors' slabs.
Right now, Chris Fane's expression was grim. He might have been the mentor for Dragon's Tooth Peak, but he was a member of House Fane first. His house had sent four of its own to compete, and Chris was under strict orders to ensure all of them passed, no matter the cost.
The success rate for commoners paled in comparison to that of Great House students—a stark disparity that reflected the Empire's entrenched social hierarchy. While barely two hundred commoners would emerge victorious from the thousand who entered The Gauntlet, an overwhelming one hundred and seventy scions of Legacy Houses would pass from their initial two hundred participants.
This imbalance was no mere coincidence but a carefully cultivated reality. From their earliest years, common folk were indoctrinated with cautionary tales about the Great Houses' power and retribution. Parents whispered warnings to their children about the dangers of challenging nobility, while village elders recounted grim fables of commoners who dared rise above their station only to face devastating consequences. This generational fear manifested as hesitation during crucial moments, causing many talented commoners to falter when confronted by those with prestigious bloodlines.
[SFX: DISAPPOINTED GRUMBLINGS]
While other mentors scanned their Engraved Slabs, shaking their heads in disappointment before moving on to the next prospect, a sly smile tugged at the corners of Isla Novak's mouth.
She was glued to her own slab. If anyone were standing beside her, they would have seen a crystal-clear image of Zane, not the static-filled blur everyone else saw.
The reason no one else could see Zane's performance was simple: she'd tampered with his Student ID, using a flimsy excuse to "inspect" it earlier.
Her little trick kept him off everyone else's radar, ensuring no one else would try to recruit him. She was determined to have him for her peak.
But now, a flicker of worry crossed her face. Did that last fight draw too much attention?
Isla sighed. Even though Zane's own feed was hidden, the mentors could still piece things together by watching the students he interacted with. She just had to hope he could gather enough crystals to pass without causing another scene.
Then again, those legacy kids were practically begging for trouble. Isla couldn't suppress a wicked little grin.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
But her quiet scheming was about to be overshadowed by a much louder, angrier problem.
When the icons for two of his family's students blinked out on the slab, signaling their elimination, Chris' face turned thunderous.
House Fane had dispatched four of its scions into The Gauntlet. Three hailed from minor branches of the illustrious family, mere footnotes in the Fane genealogy. The fourth, however, was Joe Fane—third son of the family patriarch and a direct descendant in Chris's bloodline. He represented their trump card, the cultivator upon whom their collective ambitions rested.
Chris had vehemently promised his kin that all four would successfully complete the trial. His word, once unassailable, now teetered on the precipice of disgrace as two had already been eliminated. The thought of facing his father's cold disappointment made his stomach churn.
With mounting fury eclipsing his better judgment, Chris located Joe's Student ID on the Engraved Slab.
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC]
His fingers trembled slightly—not from hesitation but from barely contained rage. Without a moment's consideration for the consequences, he channeled his voice through the connection, his pride overriding any semblance of prudence.
The action constituted an egregious violation of The Gauntlet's sacred protocols—rules that had stood for centuries and carried severe penalties for transgressors.
Chris, his face flushed with indignation, didn't spare a single thought for such trivialities. Family honor transcended mere rules, and he would gladly weather whatever punishment awaited to preserve it.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSE]
"Joe, Blake and Paxton have been eliminated! Someone took them down!" Chris whispered with fierce intensity over the slab, his knuckles whitening as he gripped the communication device.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: MAGICAL BEAST GROWLS]
"What? Who had the audacity to target my people?" Joe demanded, his voice laced with disbelief and rising anger. He sprinted through the third layer of the Magic Circle, deftly maneuvering as six Level 3 Phantom Beasts pursued him relentlessly, their shadowy forms lunging at his heels.
"Some nobody called Zane Lowell," Chris snarled. "He took out Blake and Paxton single-handedly. Blake, Joe — my own nephew." His voice dropped. "I've sent you his location. He's still in Zone 2. Find him."
He paused, then added, "And if it comes to it — use the pill."
A beat of cold silence.
"Don't doubt my strength, Uncle," Joe said flatly.
[SFX: COMBAT SOUNDS]
Without another word, Joe planted his feet, spun once through the air, and drew his dagger in a single sweeping arc. The blade traced a long bright line through the dark — and all six Level-3 Phantom Beasts came apart at once, dissolving into motes of light before they hit the ground.
Chris stared at his slab. He probably shouldn't have mentioned the pill.
[SFX: COMBAT SOUNDS]
Without missing a beat, Joe weaved between the creatures' attacks, his movements fluid and precise. Sweat beaded on his brow as he maintained his pace, demonstrating the combat prowess that had earned him his fearsome reputation.
"Some nobody called Zane Lowell," Chris snarled, venom dripping from every syllable. "Ever heard of him? The arrogant upstart clearly has a death wish. He's the one who eliminated Blake and Paxton single-handedly."
Chris hesitated momentarily, his voice dropping to a menacing whisper that carried the weight of family honor. "Listen, I recognize you're invincible in there—the strongest competitor by far. But he defeated Blake." He allowed those words to penetrate, knowing the significance they carried. "Blake, my own nephew, Joe. A disgrace we cannot tolerate." His fingers trembled slightly as he transmitted the coordinates. "I've just sent you Zane's location. Find him and make this right."
[SFX: PHANTOM BEASTS GROWLING]

[MUSIC: DARK DETERMINATION]
As Chris finished speaking, a transformation swept across Joe's features. His expression hardened into something glacial and dangerous, eyes narrowing to predatory slits. Without uttering a word, he abruptly altered his course, abandoning his original path and charging directly toward the coordinates Chris had provided, leaving the confused Phantom Beasts behind as his murderous intent radiated like a physical force.

[MUSIC: TRANSITION STINGER]
At that moment, Zane was pushing deeper into Bloody Mountain. He'd just finished off a pair of Level 2 Phantom Beasts and was finally catching his breath.
He let out an exhausted sigh, but the relief lasted only a moment before a smug laugh cut through the air, making his face tighten with annoyance.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
[AMBIENT: TENSE STANDOFF]
Barring Zane's path stood a formidable assembly of about twenty youths, their pristine attire unmistakably marking them as Great House scions. They had established an impromptu checkpoint across the narrow passage, their leader stepping forward with a smirk.
"Toll for passage," the youth announced, eyes gleaming with predatory satisfaction. "Every crystal fragment you possess."
"You've got to be kidding me," Zane thought, his muscles tensing as he assessed the overwhelming odds. The brief respite he'd hoped for evaporated like morning mist, replaced by the cold realization that twenty pairs of hungry eyes now fixed upon him—and the precious shards he carried.
