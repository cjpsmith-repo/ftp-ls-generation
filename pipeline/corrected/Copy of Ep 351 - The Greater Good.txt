Ep 351 - The Greater Good
Word count: 1257

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

As the massive face faded away, Zane noticed a sneer directed specifically at him, sending a chill down his spine.

Warriors with such extraordinary power lived in an entirely different league. To beings of that caliber, a woman's mere physical beauty held no significance. Such entities could command the attention of any desired companion with a mere gesture or thought.

This creature wasn't pursuing Elara for her aesthetic appeal; it sought to harness her unique spirit for a Soul Harvesting Ritual—a far more sinister and calculated objective than simple carnal desire.

Maybe witnessing Zane's protective stance beside Elara had momentarily startled the entity. But Zane understood the harsh reality: someone with such immense power wouldn't waste their time on people like Zane, who amounted to little more than insignificant specks in the grand cosmic order.
That final sneer spoke volumes -  pure disdain mingled with contempt and utter indifference. It was the look of a predator playing with its food before it devoured it.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY DISSIPATING]

Elara didn’t lift her head until the face was completely gone. She was surprised by the intensity of her own fear—it had been a long time since anything had truly terrified her. But maybe she only felt this fragile because her big brother was here.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

"Zane, what are we going to do?" Elara cried out, her voice tight with panic. "I can't let him take me!"

She was clearly terrified of what was coming, and a wave of helplessness washed over Zane.

The truth was, Zane couldn't get Elara out of the Skyreach Sect. For one, at his current level, he was no match for Sir Kenneth. For another, the man who’d projected that colossal face had already left some kind of mark on Elara. Even if Zane managed to get her away, they'd never be able to hide.

"Elara, that thing can't do anything to you until you've reached the Inner-Core Realm," Zane said, his voice deliberately steady despite the storm of rage building within him. He put a reassuring hand on her trembling shoulder. “For now, all we can do is prepare and wait.”

This situation illustrated exactly why so many warriors were hesitant to make binding vows of such gravity. The consequences were often far more severe than most could bear, and the price of failure was unimaginable. Zane watched Elara's eyes, still wide with terror, and felt the weight of his responsibility crushing down upon him. 

He had to become stronger—not just for himself, but for her survival.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

But Zane knew that the master with the enormous face was now a wall standing in his way—an obstacle he had to tear down if he ever wanted to reach the pinnacle of the martial path. Vow or no vow, Zane knew that to reach his true potential, he would have to kill the man behind that giant face.

He’d only said the words out loud to quiet his little sister's fears.
Elara looked up at him, her smile small and unconvincing.
Zane saw right through it. He met her eyes. "You don't believe me."
She didn't answer.
"Elara." His voice was quiet but absolute. "I swear on my martial path that I will kill him. Every word of that is true."
Elara grabbed his arm. "You don't have to swear that — if you can't fulfill it—"
"I know what it means," Zane said. "But he's already standing in my way. Oath or no oath, he has to die. This just makes it official."

[SFX: BOOMING CLAP]

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR HILLSIDE]

As he descended from the sky, Sir Kenneth clapped his hands together, the sound booming. Isla Novak was right beside him. The princess had been on a nearby hillside, but the moment she saw the strange sight in the sky and heard the massive voice, she knew it involved Elara and had rushed over in her Skycruiser.

[SFX: ECHOING LAUGHTER]

"Excellent! Just excellent!" Sir Kenneth declared, his laughter echoing across the grounds. "The disciples of the Skyreach Sect have real backbone. Isla, you have a good eye. A fine addition to Misty Peak!"

"Sir Kenneth," Zane acknowledged, rising to his feet with a respectful nod, his face betraying none of the turmoil churning within him.

Sir Kenneth returned the gesture. "I take it you heard what he said."

"Every word," Zane replied, his voice steady despite the cold dread settling in his stomach.

Sir Kenneth's shoulders slumped slightly, an unusual display of vulnerability from the normally imposing master. "That man wields power beyond comprehension. I don't cross him, although it pains me deeply. I have no choice but to hand Elara over to him. The consequences of defiance would be catastrophic for us all. I hope you don't hold this against me, Zane. I have to think about the greater good."

Zane responded with a casual shrug that belied the fierce determination hardening in his eyes."You're overthinking this, Sir Kenneth. How could I possibly blame you for something that's out of your hands?"

[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]

Sir Kenneth sighed. "I fully support your warrior's vow to kill him, but I have to warn you—it's a hell of a task. In all the Heartlands, there are only a handful of people who could even go toe-to-toe with him. But finding someone who could actually kill him? I'd say that's next to impossible."

No one in The Heartlands was strong enough to kill him?

The thought surprised Zane for a second, but it quickly sank in. The man was truly one of the most powerful figures in all the Heartlands.
"One more thing," Zane said. "What's his name?"
Sir Kenneth paused. "In the Heartlands, they call him the Eastern Evil King. His name is Maddox."
Zane repeated it quietly and nodded.

In a fight between top-tier warriors, there was always a winner and a loser. But actually killing your opponent was a whole different game. These warriors weren't just punching bags or mindless beasts; besides their incredible power, they were masters of escape. If one decided to flee, it was almost impossible for the other to land a finishing blow.

But even with the shocking news, the fire in Zane's eyes didn't dim in the slightest.

[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN TOP]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC FAREWELL]

The next morning, it was time for Elara to go. Standing on the flying platform, she kept looking back over her shoulder as she walked toward the Skycruiser, her reluctance to leave Zane written all over her face.

With one foot on the stairs of the enormous Skycruiser, Elara suddenly spun around. She ran back to Zane and threw her arms around him, hugging him tight.

They’d barely spent any time together since she was ten. After this goodbye, neither of them knew when—or even if—they would ever see each other again.

[SFX: SKYCRUISER RISING]

But they couldn't put it off forever. With a heavy heart, Elara finally boarded the massive Skycruiser, which began to rise slowly into the air.
Waiting at the top of the stairs was her escort — a woman named Marilyn, sent by Maddox himself. She smiled warmly at Elara as she stepped aboard, the picture of graciousness.
Zane watched that smile from the ground and felt something cold settle in his chest. He burned it into his memory.
He would wipe it from existence.

Elara sat in a daze, staring out the glass window of the vessel. On the ground below, Zane waved gently, then struck a pose.

Elara recognized the posture instantly. She’d seen it back on Hell Mountain, and she knew exactly what it meant.

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL PROMISE]

He wasn't giving up on her. He would protect her, no matter what.
