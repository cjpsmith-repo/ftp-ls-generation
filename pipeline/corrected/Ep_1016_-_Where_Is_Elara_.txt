Ep 1016 - Where Is Elara?

Word count: 1023

[AMBIENT: TENSE CAVE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

When Ernest and his crew had surrounded the two newcomers, onlookers sighed. Looked like this was going to be a bad day for the poor kids. They were about to lose all their Aether crystals, and it was a safe bet the woman was in for some serious trouble from these thugs.

They watched from a safe distance. Most of them had been shaken down by Ernest before and weren’t eager for a repeat. All they could do was watch with pity, hoping the new arrivals wouldn’t get too banged up.

[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCH]

[SFX: BODY IMPACT]

But to their absolute shock, instead of getting pushed around, the kid from the Life-and-Death Realm simply hauled off and punched Ernest, sending him sailing. A sickening crack echoed from the cave as his body slammed into the wall, and everyone flinched.

Realizing there was a bigger shark in the pond, they all scattered, flying far away from the area.

[MUSIC: UPLIFTING TRANSITION]

Zane just shook his head at the sight. He turned to Freya with a small smile. “Let’s go turn in these Sovereign Stones,” he said, then shot off toward the collection point without another word.

Freya scrambled to keep up. They flew in silence, but the look in her eyes said it all.

At first, she’d assumed she was the stronger one. Her cultivation level was higher, after all, and her uncle was the lord of a major city in the Soulbound Sect. Freya was used to having the upper hand, both in strength and status. She had even planned on doing Zane a favor by recommending him to her uncle, thinking she could get him to take the young warrior under his wing.

But after what she’d just seen, Freya realized how ridiculous that was. Zane didn’t need her help. He didn’t need anyone’s help. If anything, it was the other way around.

It stung her pride, but she couldn’t deny the wave of gratitude that washed over her. He’d saved her from a nasty confrontation.

[AMBIENT: BUSY COLLECTION POINT]

[SFX: CROWD CHATTER]

Soon, they arrived at the collection point. The place was bustling with warriors milling about. Some were turning in their Aether crystals, others were grabbing a quick break, but most just headed straight back to the mines. Everyone was desperate to gather the million supreme Aether crystals they needed to get the hell out of this place.

[SFX: MURMURING CROWD]

When Zane pulled out his haul of Sovereign Stones, every warrior there stopped to stare, their eyes green with envy.

Unfortunately for them, the collection point was crawling with guards from the Soulbound Sect, so a robbery was out of the question.

[SFX: ARMORED FOOTSTEPS]

“Hey, you’re the warrior who just signed up, right?” the armored man in charge of collections asked as Zane walked up. He remembered this kid. A hundred and thirty-seven supreme Aether crystals on his first day. The kid was a beast.

Zane nodded. “Is there a problem?”

The guard chuckled. “No problem at all. The sooner you pay, the sooner you leave. That’s a good thing.”

Zane set half the Sovereign Stones on the counter and slid them toward Freya. He registered them in her name. He acted fairly. Strength meant there was no need to be greedy.

“We’re done here for today. Meet me back at the settlement at noon tomorrow — we’ll go back in together.”

Freya nodded. “I’ll be here.”

They parted. Freya found a quiet corner and settled in to meditate.

Zane didn’t head back to the settlement. He went back into the mine, found an empty passage deep enough to be undisturbed, and carved a small cave out of the rock with a few strokes of his sword. Stone bed, low ceiling, no light. He set a concealment Divine Texture at the entrance — fully hidden — and sat down.

He turned the Roaring Token over in his hands. He injected Chaos Energy into it. The token began to emit a faint golden light.

All Zane had to do was speak into it, and his voice would echo across the entire universe.

[SFX: MAGICAL RESONANCE]

Zane gripped the token tightly and shouted, “Elara Lowell, are you there?”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Freya’s heart hammered against her ribs when she heard the name. The person Zane was looking for was named Lowell, and her first name was Elara. Elara Lowell and Zane Lowell. There was no way those names weren’t connected.

No, it has to be a coincidence, she told herself, trying to rationalize. Zane had surprised her with his strength, sure, but there was no way someone like him could afford something as valuable as a Roaring Token.

[AMBIENT: COSMIC ECHO]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CHOIR]

She wasn’t the only one who was confused.

Across the universe, countless beings were puzzled by the voice that had just rung out.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

The voice was clearly calling for someone, but “Elara Lowell”? The name meant nothing. A nobody. Was it really necessary to use a Roaring Token just to find some random person?

“Maybe he’s the heir of some Supreme Lord. Probably has so many Roaring Tokens he doesn’t mind wasting one!”

“Talk about money to burn! A whole Roaring Token? What a waste!”

“Eh, it wouldn’t be the first time. I heard some Supreme Lord’s son burned through a hundred of ‘em just to propose to another Lord’s daughter. The rich are just built differently.”

[AMBIENT: QUIET CAVE]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

In the small, hidden cave, Zane waited.

Lavender had said the young man who took Elara was stronger than even a Supreme Lord. A man that powerful would surely have a Roaring Token. He had promised Zane that Elara would be safe. If she heard him, she would definitely reply.

But Zane waited for a long time, and silence was his only answer. The hope on his face slowly darkened into a grim mask.

He could only think of two reasons for the silence: one, Elara didn’t have a Roaring Token to answer him, or two, she was outside this universe entirely.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TONES]

The first possibility meant Elara’s situation was far worse than he had dared to imagine. She could be imprisoned, or worse.

Maybe that young man was just cruel, refusing to give her a Roaring Token to answer her brother’s call.

One thing was for certain: Zane would stop at nothing until he found her.
