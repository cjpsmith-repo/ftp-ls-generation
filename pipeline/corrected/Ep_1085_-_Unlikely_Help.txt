Ep 1085 - Unlikely Help

Word count: 1012

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Just to be safe, Zane stretched out a single hand. If these flames really could burn him to ash, he figured he could at least pull his arm back in time. Better to lose an arm than his whole body, right?

He was being naive.

[SFX: WHOOSH OF FLAMES]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

The moment his fingertips brushed the purple fire, it shot up his arm and engulfed his entire body at a speed he couldn’t even process. He had no time to react, no time to do anything but feel the searing heat flood into him, spreading through every bone and organ.

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FIRE]

“This is bad,” Zane thought, his face grim. He’d never expected the flames to be this powerful. He gritted his teeth, forcing himself to endure the scorching blaze. Whether he lived or died was out of his hands now.

He’d been tempered by fire before, but his body’s reaction this time was faster and far more intense. The flames cooked him from the outside in, turning his skin bright red, like a statue being melted back into molten steel.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SWIRL]

At the same time, dozens of golden vortexes spun into existence across his skin, whirling rapidly. A wave of relief washed over Zane as they began to absorb the purple flames.

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FLAMES]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Even so, he couldn’t shake the worry. These were the flames left behind by the Supreme Lord of Profound Flame, after all. He was just glad his body had reacted so quickly; otherwise, he’d have been cooked through. Now that he’d absorbed some of the fire, he decided to wait for his body to adjust before attempting to cross the rest of the flame pool.

[SFX: INTENSE WHOOSHING FIRE]

But as the golden vortexes frantically devoured the purple flames, he realized something was wrong. They were consuming the fire way too fast, like starving animals that hadn’t eaten in a thousand years. With every pulse, they’d swallow the surrounding flames in a flash, but it made no difference. The purple fire on his body didn’t die down. In fact, it burned even brighter, making Zane himself glow. From a distance, he must have looked like a burning pillar, a column of fire shooting straight from his head toward the top of the Enchanted Barrier Tower.

These flames just keep coming, he thought. It feels like I’ll never absorb them all.

Just then, a familiar voice cut through the roar of the fire. “Did you really think the Immortal Profound Flame could be extinguished that easily?” It was Lavender, her tone almost amused.

“What do you mean it can’t be extinguished?” Zane asked.

“This flame wasn’t created by the Supreme Lord of Profound Flame,” Lavender said. “He took it from the Divine Fire World — an entire realm made of fire, where every kind of flame exists, and the strongest of them slowly develop their own intelligence. He spent nearly ten years hunting down a single wisp of it, then cultivated it to the scale you’re seeing now. Its defining characteristic is that it cannot be put out.”

“Cannot be put out,” Zane repeated. “So it’s just going to burn on my body forever?”

“For tens of thousands of years, at minimum,” Lavender said. There was a faint note of laughter in her voice. “Running around on fire for ten thousand years does have a certain style to it.”

Zane did not find this funny. He looked down at the purple flames wreathing his body and thought through the implications. He couldn’t walk around like this. He couldn’t wear clothes. Worse — Storage Ring was sitting not far away. Tia’s Storage Ring wasn’t high grade. If the Immortal Profound Flame reached it, it would melt in an instant.

He had no idea what to do.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Then Cobalt Dragon’s voice came through, quieter and more measured than Lavender’s. “Stop teasing him. Zane — you’re forgetting about the Ancestral Dragon.”

The Ancestral Dragon ignored all Laws — that much he knew.

Back in the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, Zane and even the Celestial Paragon-level warriors couldn’t get into the refracted space to retrieve the egg, but the Ancestral Dragon had slipped through the barrier with ease.

“The Ancestral Dragon.”

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

A spark of inspiration lit up his eyes. He glanced at his bestiary bag but didn’t reach for it. Instead, he flicked his fingers in its direction, and in a flash, the baby dragon shot out and pounced right into his arms. The little creature had been sleeping for most of the time since they’d ascended. When Zane had let it out occasionally at the Mist Palace, it had spent the whole time looking around — searching for Cora, or more precisely, for whatever Cora had been cooking. It wasn’t interested in her, just her food.

The little guy had been cooped up in the bag for a while now, and even though the space inside was huge, it was still stuffy. It was good for him to get out and stretch.

The dragon hopped onto Zane’s body, completely ignoring the Immortal Profound Flame, just as it had ignored the refraction before. And just as Zane had hoped, the flames didn’t harm it in the slightest.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL DISCOVERY]

He finally understood: the little creature was immune to all Original Laws.

Zane held out his hands in front of the Ancestral Dragon, where the purple flames still raged.

[SFX: FIRE CONSUMING]

The dragon tilted its head, then opened its tiny mouth and simply swallowed the fire burning on Zane’s palms.

[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]

[SFX: FLAMES CRACKLING]

Soon, Zane was flying across the flame pool. He was still letting his body get coated in the Immortal Profound Flame as he flew, but he wasn’t letting the Ancestral Dragon clear it all off. He would wait until he’d crossed the entire two hundred miles.

[AMBIENT: TOWER EXTERIOR]

Just then, outside the Enchanted Barrier Tower, the other warriors realized Zane had started moving again.
