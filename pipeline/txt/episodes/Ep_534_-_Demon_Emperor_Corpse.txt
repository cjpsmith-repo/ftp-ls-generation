[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Back in the Heartlands, these corpses would have been trivial—even Cloud Hall's Inner-Core Realm students could dispatch them easily. But the Tomb of Celestial Mist suppressed everyone's power. Though two students had reached the final Inner-Core stage, the suppression reduced their strength to Soul Realm level, making the corpses formidable opponents.
During the Martial Tournament, Zane—a Soul Realm warrior—had defeated a Divine-level Inner-Core talent. After that victory, Cloud Hall stopped viewing him as merely Soul Realm. Yet inside the Tomb, where suppression affected everyone equally, Zane's strength should have plummeted. He shouldn't have stood a chance against these creatures.
But a moment ago, Zane had cut them down without even breaking a sweat, right in front of everybody.
You didn't have to be a genius to connect the dots: Zane was on the same level as Emanuel, Saint Morphens, and the other elders. The students were completely floored.
A Soul Realm warrior jumping a whole power level to challenge someone in the Inner-Core Realm was basically a miracle. But Zane had pulled it off, proving he had the talent and raw strength to back it up. They hadn't wanted to believe it, but they were slowly coming around.
In the Martial Tournament, he’d single-handedly defeated Eden and killed Marilyn. If it wasn't for Maddox's threats, he never would've dropped out to set up the sect-protecting magic arrays for Cloud Hall. Maybe if he'd kept fighting, even Brent from the Ethereal Spirit Sect wouldn't have stood a chance. A lot of them hated to admit it, but they had to face facts.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BUILD]
But skipping two entire power levels to match a Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior? That was just unheard of.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
"Zane, how did you do that?" one of the students asked.
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The two elders, both in the early stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, also turned to Zane, waiting for an explanation.
Zane just spread his hands with a small smile. But before anyone got an answer, he vanished.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
In a flash, he reappeared fifty or sixty feet away and struck again.
With one clean swipe, his sword connected. [SFX: SWORD SLASH]
[SFX: HEAVY THUD]
Another corpse staggered, dropped to its knees before him, and collapsed with a heavy thud.
Tia's icy-blue halberd was just as deadly.
[SFX: ICY BLAST]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Each swing unleashed a blast of frigid air that froze a dozen corpses solid. Then, she'd level the halberd and charge.
[SFX: SHATTERING ICE]
The sound of shattering ice echoed as she charged again. The frozen corpses exploded like glass statues, littering the ground with icy fragments.
Elder Imogen didn't move. These corpses were troublesome, but they were a small problem. If she had to spend her strength on small problems this early, there would be no point continuing deeper into the tomb.
Under the combined assault of Tia, Emanuel, Saint Morphens, and the other elders, the reanimated corpses fell one by one. Their numbers dwindled fast.
Then the last one shattered under Tia's halberd — and silence fell.
It lasted about two seconds.
[SFX: DISTANT ROAR]
A thunderous roar rolled out of the darkness ahead. Then came the sound of heavy footsteps, each one shaking the ground.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
Tia stepped forward, placing herself between the group and whatever was coming. She held the halberd at her side and stared into the dark.
The footsteps intensified. A massive figure burst from the shadows.
Demonic energy slammed into them like a wall.
The Spirit-Tribulation elders held firm, barely. Those below Emanuel and Saint Morphens paled. Disciples went white, teeth chattering.
Zane remained composed. He'd faced demonic energy in White Emperor City—Demon Soldiers, Demon Generals, even a Demon King. But nothing like this. Without his Combat Soul Realm breakthrough, he couldn't have withstood it.
The Demon race had been nearly exterminated in the Heartlands. Survivors fled east, tearing open a space crack for their staging ground—the reason Zane had trained at White Emperor City. Even there, the Blue Cloud Sect had contained them.
The Demon hierarchy extended far beyond Soldiers and Kings. At the apex stood Demon Emperors—ten times more powerful, three times larger.
Someone had reanimated one as a corpse.
"A Demon Emperor Corpse," Elder Imogen said, her voice flat. She stepped forward, walking stick in hand. "I'll handle it."
"There's no need, Master." Tia was already moving. "I'll take care of it."
Ever since Tia had stepped into the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, she hadn't shown her full strength. Those who'd tried to estimate it put her at early-stage — but her real power was something else entirely. Early-stage in name only. In truth, she fought at the level of a late-stage Spirit-Tribulation master.
This was the first time anyone was going to see it.
[SFX: METAL CLANG]
The Demon Emperor Corpse towered over her — standing three times her height, its massive claws slashing down toward her in a single devastating strike.
Tia caught them on her halberd with one hand. Just like that — stopped dead.
Then she twisted her wrist and began to push down.
Despite her delicate frame, she now had the massive Demon Emperor Corpse pinned with her halberd. To the creature, the weapon must have felt like it weighed a million, maybe even ten million pounds. But in Tia's hands, it was as light as a feather.
Finally, the Demon Emperor Corpse buckled under the pressure, dropping to one knee. Even then, it was still several feet taller than Tia.
The Demon Emperor Corpse had no mind of its own—it was just an automaton programmed to kill. It was no match for Tia, but it didn't know any better. Any sane creature would have given up, but the mindless corpse kept fighting back with all its might, resisting the halberd.
[SFX: GUTTURAL GROANS]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
The strange groans escaping it grew louder as its red, multifaceted eyes locked onto Tia.
Suddenly, Tia sneered.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
She yanked the halberd back, spun in a tight circle, and swung the massive weapon around, smashing it into the Demon Emperor Corpse's side.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT]
The impact was explosive.
[SFX: HEAVY CRASH]
The blow sent the Demon Emperor Corpse flying, and it crashed hard into a nearby wall.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

[SFX: MAGICAL POWER SURGE]
"Absolute Freezing!"
[AMBIENT: ICE CAVE]
[SFX: FREEZING WAVE]
Instantly, a terrifying wave of cold erupted from her. The Tomb of Celestial Mist, already thick with a dark aura, plunged into a deep freeze, as if they’d been teleported to an ice cave.
[SFX: ICE FREEZING]
Just as the Demon Emperor Corpse started to push itself up, its entire body froze solid. It was caught mid-motion, trapped in a block of ice, completely immobile.
[SFX: ICE SHATTERING]
With a sharp crack, Tia swung her halberd one last time. And just like the corpses before it, the frozen Demon Emperor Corpse shattered into a cloud of fine, icy dust.
