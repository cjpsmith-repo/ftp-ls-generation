Ep 980 - Forceful Energy
Word count: 1076
[AMBIENT: EERIE DUNGEON]
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE THEME]
As a warrior of the Life-and-Death Realm, Zane knew he should’ve been able to handle these creatures easily.
But what shocked him was how their bodies just seemed to soak up the force of his attacks.
The old Zane, back when he was just an Adept, would’ve been turned to dust by a single punch from his current self. And yet, this thing had somehow absorbed the blow. It looked like it had taken a serious hit, but it was still on its feet.
[SFX: WHOOSHING ATTACKS]
One after another, they lunged at him. They didn’t seem to use any particular attunement method, but their bodies could still somehow take his punches.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACTS]
Zane held back, his punches sending the creatures flying. But every single one of them just got right back up, their faces twisting into faint, unsettling smiles.
He wondered if they were actually intelligent, or if whoever created the Elysian Keep’s true path had designed them this way on purpose.
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT CRESCENDO]
The creatures rushed him again. This time, Zane didn’t hold back.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE PUNCHES]
He unleashed the Forceful Energy in his body in a flurry of powerful attacks. Anything that got close was instantly obliterated by a single punch.
[SFX: LIQUID SPLASH]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
It wasn’t the way they burst apart that surprised him—it was the color of the liquid that sprayed out from them. “Golden blood?”
He’d always known that only the Demi-Immortal Race had golden blood. Zane was an outsider they’d accepted, but his own blood hadn’t turned yet. Golden blood was the one thing that defined that elite race.
There had to be a connection.
[SFX: ENERGY SWIRL]
[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ENERGY]
His punches shattered the creatures into a spray of golden liquid, which immediately broke down into streams of Dark and Light Energy. The energies swirled together, reforming into solid, humanoid figures that charged at him all over again.
And this time they were faster. Stronger. Zane pushed harder — and they pushed back harder still, matching him step for step. He could feel it: every time he raised his power, theirs rose with it. Adept level. Inner-Core. Spirit-Tribulation. Life-and-Death.
They were copying his exact cultivation level in real time.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
A raw power radiated from them as they closed in. “Forceful Energy!” he realized, a jolt of shock running through him.
But there was something else, something weird… their movements mirrored his own fighting style. Then it hit him. They were copying him.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Rage flared in his chest. “I’ll tear you apart from the inside out!”
A little borrowed Forceful Energy wasn’t going to be enough to stop him from crushing these so-called Adepts. He took a deep breath, circulating the Vitality through his body and pushing it to its absolute limit.
But the ridge gave him nowhere to go. The path was barely a shoulder’s width — no room to sidestep, no room to dodge. When a cluster of them surged forward together and hit him as one, Zane was forced back several steps. He caught himself at the edge of the ridge.
He stopped fighting and started thinking.
His goal wasn’t to win. It was to pass. Every second he spent brawling was a second wasted — and the path wasn’t getting shorter. He’d been tangling with them out of instinct, and instinct was going to get him killed.
He burst forward, scattering the nearest group with a shockwave of Dragon Scales energy, and ran.
Just then, he remembered something the Cobalt Dragon had told him about powerful creatures that take on human form. The Dragon himself had a human counterpart named Josh.
But they weren’t actually transforming into humans.
[MUSIC: EPIC REVELATION]
The Cobalt Dragon’s explanation suddenly clicked into place. Their transformations were based on a far more powerful race that just happened to look almost identical to humans: the Sacred Race.
And that wasn’t all. Every race—Human, Demon Night, Ogre, you name it—had inherited unique traits from this ancient Sacred Race. Those traits were what led them down different paths, making them adopt different systems of attunement.
Not just humans. Every primate race in the upper world — demons, beast-kin, giants — carried a fragment of the Sacred Race in their bloodline. When powerful beasts reached the stage of transformation, it wasn’t the human form they were mirroring. It was the Sacred Race. Humans just happened to look similar.
The Demon Night resembled the Sacred Race the most. Their babies were born as natural Adepts, giving them a massive head start over other races. But that gift came with a price: the Demon Night had far more females than males, which made their population grow at a crawl.
“I get it now,” Zane thought.
But that just raised another question. If the Sacred Race was so perfect, why had he never heard of them? Why wasn’t there any trace of them left? Did they just… vanish?
[SFX: WHOOSHING ATTACKS]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
He was about to ask the Cobalt Dragon for his take on it when a sudden barrage of attacks rained down on him.
They were too strong now; he couldn’t afford to take a single hit. All he could do was run, weaving and dodging the onslaught. But they just kept coming, their numbers and power growing with every passing second.
Behind him, Saul’s voice cut through the path. “The Sacred Race are the creators of laws. From birth, they comprehend all of them automatically. Use one law on this path — they will use every law.”
Then he felt it — his body slowing. Not fatigue. Something external. The flow of time around him had thickened.
Time Law.
He reacted instantly, pushing his own Time Law outward in a pulse, crashing it against theirs. The compression shattered. He burst free and kept running.
[SFX: ELEMENTAL ATTACKS]
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC BATTLEFIELD]
All kinds of Law Power flew at him—wind blades, fire swords, bolts of thunder.
Back at the entrance of the path, Tia, Cora, and Isla watched in silence. From where they stood, Zane hadn’t moved at all. He was a frozen figure in the swirling black and white mist, perfectly still.
“He’s still going,” Saul said quietly, watching. “He’s discovered something useful. But it’s going to get much harder from here.”
He shook his head. “He can’t take much more of this.”
