Ep 1002 - Growing and Changing

Word count: 1222

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL VOID]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

A warrior wasn’t supposed to get pushed out of the world of chaos until they reached the Soul Sea Realm—the point when a new world had formed inside their body.

But Zane was only at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm. He shouldn’t be feeling that repulsive force yet. So why was it happening now?

Besides, hadn’t he already fused with the will of the world of chaos? Why was it rejecting him?

He searched for answers, but the world of chaos offered none. It wasn’t a single entity he could question, just a collective consciousness of all living things. Asking it for help was useless.

[MUSIC: INTENSE STRUGGLE]

Still, Zane fought against the force, doing everything he could to suppress his own power.

He poured all his strength into resisting it, and to his alarm, it was barely enough. This isn’t right, he thought, a cold knot of confusion tightening in his gut.

When he’d used the will of the world of chaos to scout the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms and the main continent, Zane had noticed Amber and the other Soul Sea Realm masters feeling it too. But for them, it was a minor annoyance, something they could easily push down. It was nothing like the all-consuming struggle that was tearing him apart—and he wasn’t even supposed to be feeling it at all.

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

Ever since he broke through to the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm, the invisible repelling force had only grown stronger every single day. It felt like it was increasing exponentially.

At first, he’d been confident, telling himself he could fight it off with enough effort.

[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]

The days that followed proved him devastatingly wrong.

The second day was just a mild push. By the third, the force was so relentless it shattered his focus on training. On the fourth day, the struggle was written all over his face in a mask of pain. With each passing day, Zane had to pour more and more of his power into just holding his ground.

Tia saw the sudden change in him and grew deeply worried, thinking he might be sick.

But that was impossible. A man of his power was immune to any normal disease. This suffering wasn’t caused by some simple illness.

When she finally asked him what was wrong, Zane just tried to play it cool, assuring her everything was fine.

[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]

But on the fifth day, Zane couldn’t take it anymore. He gathered his three wives and finally told them the truth: the world of chaos was pushing him out. His ascension was coming, and he would have to leave them.

[SFX: HEAVY SILENCE]

Stunned silence fell over his three wives as their beautiful faces went pale.

They had always known Zane would one day ascend to the Upper World. It was his destiny. A man as powerful as him was bound for a higher plane, and they’d made their peace with that. But he was still at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm! Why was the world tearing him away from them so soon?

They wanted to scream, to cry, to cling to him and never let go, but the sight of the raw suffering etched on their husband’s face stole the breath from their lungs.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Zane had the same question. He turned to the Cobalt Dragon, the only one who might have an answer.

The dragon was quiet for a long moment. Then, slowly, he offered his best guess: the Primordial Chaos Tactic. Normally, a warrior’s Inner World opened when they reached the Soul Sea Realm — and that was what triggered the world’s rejection. But Zane’s Inner World didn’t hold Vitality. It held Chaos Energy. The world couldn’t categorize it. Couldn’t contain it. So it was pushing him out early.

Even the Cobalt Dragon admitted it was only a theory. There was no precedent for what Zane had done. But it was the only explanation that made any sense. Zane had no choice but to agree.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

A wave of depression washed over Tia. She just shook her head. “This isn’t the right time.”

Cora shook her head, but a faint, determined smile touched her lips. “It doesn’t matter. I’ll just tell him when I see him again.”

Tia stared at her. “What are you talking about?” she asked, a knot of unease tightening in her chest at Cora’s words.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

“Nothing,” Cora said, her chin lifting. “I’m leaving The Heartlands tomorrow. I’m going straight to the Ethereal Spirit Sect to say goodbye to my great-grandfather and Brent.”

“You’re going to the Violet Core Realm?” Tia pressed.

Even though Zane hadn’t said it out loud, Tia knew he was counting on her to look after Cora and Isla. She was the strongest warrior left in The Heartlands, after all.

Cora nodded. “I am,” she said, a flicker of her old, willful self returning.

“I forbid it,” Tia ordered, her brow furrowing. “You can’t leave.”

But Cora just snorted. “You have no right to stop me.”

The two of them argued back and forth, completely missing the fact that Isla had gone quiet beside them.

Isla knew she was the weakest of the three. It meant she would have to work ten times harder than them just to catch up. The road ahead would be brutal, but a new fire of determination had been lit within her. She was dead set on training, on ascending, on seeing Zane again, no matter what it took.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

By the sixth day, Zane was burning through Dragon Scales just to keep himself anchored — channeling batch after batch, feeling them dim as the world pushed harder. By the seventh, even that was barely holding.

Before the night was out, he pulled Tia aside and warned her about the one danger she might not see coming: the reverse side of the world. She listened without a word. When he finished, she nodded — and didn’t let go of his hand.

[MUSIC: BITTERSWEET STRINGS]

That night, he let go.

[SFX: SILVER LIGHT SURGE]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL ASCENSION]

Silver light erupted around him, blazing until the room dissolved. He smiled at his three wives — quiet, unhurried — and then the light swallowed him whole. He shot into the night sky like a second moon, visible for five hundred miles in every direction. Rumors spread before dawn. No one realized the Heartlands’ greatest warrior had just left their world behind.

[MUSIC: SOMBER PIANO]

[SFX: HEAVY SILENCE]

The three of them stood in the empty hall.

Then Tia made a small, involuntary sound — she turned away, but not before they saw her put a hand on her stomach. It was a gesture so tender and protective, it could only have meant one thing.

Cora was at her side in two steps. “How long?”

“A few days,” Tia said quietly.

She’d known. She’d suppressed every sign of it in front of him, waiting for a moment that never came.

“Why didn’t you tell him?” Isla asked.

Tia shook her head. “It wasn’t the right time.”

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CRESCENDO]

What she didn’t know was that her own incredible destiny was waiting for her just around the corner.
