Ep 689 - Unexpected Information

Word count: 1131

Elder Marsh knew Zane was only at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, but he was also keenly aware of the young man’s relationship with Tia. It felt like a safe bet that Zane was on the fast track to a key leadership role in the Cloud Hall. On top of that, Marsh was hoping to ask Zane for help setting up one of their Sect-protecting arrays. The problem was, asking for a favor that big felt… awkward. They weren’t family, not really friends, and he had no real claim on Zane’s time. Still, he was glad they’d at least met. He’d just have to find a better time to bring it up.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Suddenly, a streak of purplish-black light tore across the sky, covering miles in just a few seconds.

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

Even at his own impressive speed, Zane figured it would take him a couple of days to reach Cloudhaven. To cut that time down, he decided on a detour. Instead of making a beeline for his destination, he’d first head to Ethereal City, a major hub east of The Heartlands.

Most major cities had a powerful Sect backing them. Cloudhaven was supported by the Cloud Hall, just as Ethereal City was supported by the Ethereal Spirit Sect. Even Oracle City, home to the World Merchants’ Guild headquarters, followed the pattern. More importantly, these cities had public teleportation arrays. If Zane could get to the one in Ethereal City, his trip to Cloudhaven would be a whole lot faster.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL THEME]

Two days later, he was there, hovering in the air above the ancient city.

At the gate, armored soldiers were checking everyone who passed through. Ordinary people with no cultivation moved freely. Warriors got stopped.

Zane was stopped.

The soldier who approached him couldn't see through his cultivation at all — his expression shifted slightly at that — but he kept his tone polite. "Friend, Ethereal City has a custom these days. Independent cultivators aren't permitted entry. May I ask which sect or family you're affiliated with?"

Zane raised an eyebrow. "Independent cultivators can't enter the city?"

"That's right." The soldier gave a practiced smile. "You'll understand, given the current climate. If you happen to be unaffiliated, I can point you toward someone. With your bearing, the Ethereal Spirit Sect would take you in without hesitation."

Zane descended toward the gates, pulling an Acolyte’s Sigil from his Storage Ring.

[SFX: METALLIC GLEAM]

The token, which he’d received when he joined the Cloud Hall, gleamed as he held it out to the guards.

Humming to himself, one of the armored warriors took the sigil and made a show of inspecting it. He wasn’t from the Cloud Hall, so he had no clue if it was real or fake, but appearances had to be maintained. He scratched his chin thoughtfully, giving it a good, long look. Plenty of stubborn, independent warriors refused to join a Sect but still needed to do business in the big cities, so counterfeit sigils were common.

After a moment, the guard nodded and handed the token back. The guards stepped aside, and with a brief nod of his own, Zane walked into Ethereal City.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY MARKET]

[MUSIC: TRADITIONAL FLUTE]

Instead of rushing straight for the teleportation array, Zane found himself drawn in by the city’s unique charm. He decided to find the famed Silvermoon Pavilion to rest for a bit before moving on.

[AMBIENT: FLOWING RIVER]

The pavilion itself wasn’t huge—only three stories high—but it was built right on the bank of a clear river, and the view from its windows was famously beautiful and calming.

Zane ordered a hearty stew with fresh bread and settled in, leaning his head on his hand as he stared out at the water.

[SFX: RHYTHMIC BOWL TAPPING]

[MUSIC: CHEERFUL FOLK SONG]

At a nearby table, a few scholars were shaking their heads and chanting an old song. As they got more into it, they picked up their spoons and started tapping them against their bowls, creating a surprisingly pleasant rhythm. Zane found it amusing and even clapped for them when they finished, earning a few grateful smiles in return.

Most of them weren’t warriors. Zane sighed, a little wistful. An ordinary life like that seemed pretty nice. But he knew there was no turning back from the path he’d chosen. He might never reach his ultimate goal, but he knew one thing for sure: if he gave up now, he’d be lost forever.

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

[AMBIENT: ROWDY LAUGHTER]

Just as the scholars started another tune, a group of loud, disruptive warriors stomped into the pavilion. They were all at the Soul Realm, already drinking and laughing boisterously.

Zane paid them no mind at first — until their words caught his ear.

"I'm exhausted," one of them muttered, dropping heavily into his seat. "The Ethereal Spirit Sect doesn't treat us like disciples. We're slaves. That's all we are."

"Keep your voice down," another hissed. "You want the inner disciples hearing that?"

Zane studied them quietly over the rim of his cup. Forced recruits. Independent cultivators who'd been swept up in the crackdown and pressed into service were still raw about it.

"I just want this whole mess to end," a third one grumbled. "Patrolling the city every day like a trained dog…"

"Someone ought to tell that Kensington girl to just find herself a husband already," another said, lowering his voice. "Then Maddox loses interest, and the rest of us can breathe again."

Zane went very still.

[SFX: SHARP TENSION]

The Kensington family. A girl Maddox was fixating on. There was only one thing that would hold his attention like that.

She had the Violet Core Physique.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

In the blink of an eye, Zane was gone from his table and standing right beside theirs.

[AMBIENT: TAVERN MURMUR]

The scholars’ eyes went wide. They knew they’d just witnessed a true master in action.

The warriors, startled by his sudden appearance, fell silent. One of them glared up at him. "The hell was that for?"

[SFX: CRYSTAL CLINK]

Zane didn't even look at him. He just casually dropped a supreme Aether crystal onto the table with a sharp clink.

"I'll cover your tab," he said, his voice dead serious.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The warriors gasped, their eyes glued to the glistening crystal. Everyone knew the Silvermoon Pavilion was ridiculously expensive; an average person couldn’t afford a single meal there. A warrior could manage, sure—a low-grade Aether Crystal or two would cover food for half a month.

But the supreme Aether crystal Zane had just tossed out was worth a million of those. With that one crystal, he could have bought the entire Silvermoon Pavilion and eaten like a king every day for years.
