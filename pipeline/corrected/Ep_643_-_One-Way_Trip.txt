Ep 643 - One-Way Trip
Word count: 1186
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS CAVE WIND]
[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Everyone knew the outer circle of the Dark Nether Cave was a death wish. People went in, and they just didn't come out. It was as simple as that. Even standing at the entrance, Fabian and Jarrod had no intention of setting foot inside. The risk was insane, the odds of survival practically zero. You needed intense, advanced preparation just to attempt the inner circle, and even then, years of training were no guarantee. Some of the most powerful War Lords in history had met their end in that abyss.
But for every horror story, there was a legend of incredible treasure. The lucky few who made it back alive returned with rewards that could change a man’s destiny. And that was enough to keep a steady stream of fools and heroes coming.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION BUILD]
While most of those brave souls were aiming for the inner circle, Zane had his sights set on the core.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]
That was a whole different level of crazy. The core wasn't just dangerous; it was a myth, a place from which no one had ever returned. According to the map, the outer circle was riddled with threats, the inner circle had fewer, but the deepest parts were a total mystery. As for the core, the map was just a blank space with a few vague directional lines. It didn’t need any warnings scrawled on it. Everyone knew it was a one-way trip.
[MUSIC: SHOCKED REVELATION]
Fabian stammered, his eyes wide with a mix of awe and terror. "Zane, are you… are you kidding me?”
Zane just chuckled. “If it helps you sleep at night, sure. It’s a joke.”
He wasn’t being completely honest, but he couldn’t exactly lay out the whole truth. When Vesper gave him the token and tasked him with finding the inheritance, the details had been fuzzy. Vesper had never been in the cave himself, so all he knew was that the prize was somewhere deep inside. His only instruction was to follow the Crystal Butterfly—it would light up when he got close.
And Zane wasn't reckless. He understood that no matter how strong his destiny was, he could still die. The Crystal Butterfly in his pocket was dark and still. He'd start with the outer circle. If he found nothing, he'd push to the inner one. If the inner circle came up empty — he'd face that decision when he reached it. The core was a last resort, not a plan. He wasn't going to die in vain.
Despite his easygoing act, Zane wasn't an idiot. He knew he was walking a razor's edge.
[AMBIENT: EERIE CAVE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
They pressed deeper into the outer circle. The environment shifted constantly around them — the terrain rougher, the air heavier. And the red light was getting stronger.
Even weakened, the Dark Nether Death Light was no joke. An ordinary person would have been killed by it long ago. Everyone around Zane — Fabian, Jarrod, Yolande — had wrapped themselves in a faint shell of Vitality to hold it back. Zane felt the prickling sensation but didn't bother shielding himself; it wasn't enough to slow him down. Lavender felt nothing at all.
They had been moving for about an hour when the sound of fighting reached them from the left. Then six figures burst from the undergrowth, sprinting hard.
[SFX: ARROWS WHOOSHING]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Two of them carried longbows. They spun mid-run and loosed arrows into the trees behind them.
[SFX: MONSTROUS CLAW STRIKE]
[SFX: BODY IMPACT]
Before the arrows even landed, a massive greenish-black claw shot out from the trees and snatched both archers from the air. There was no scream — just the sickening sound of impact, and then they were gone.
"Go! I'll cover you!"
The strongest of the remaining four turned and faced the claw alone.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: HEROIC ORCHESTRAL]
Light erupted from her body — brilliant, layered, threaded through with the shadows of flowers. Wherever it spread, petals bloomed in the air. Countless colorful fragments swirled outward and plastered themselves across the ghostly claw, covering it completely.
"Explode."
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: GROUND RUMBLE]
The detonation shook the ground. The claw recoiled. The four survivors ran.
They rushed toward Zane — and stopped short when they saw who was standing there.
"My Queen!" The lead figure stepped forward, her voice urgent. "This place is too dangerous — please retreat with us at once!"
It was Esther Sterling. The other three were Demon Night War Lords.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
A strange sort of calm had fallen over the area. The presence of the massive toad had driven most of the other vicious monsters into hiding, giving warriors a rare chance to explore more freely. Of course, that just meant new, different dangers.
"There are only four of you left?" Fabian said, his surprise unconcealed.
Esther shook her head. "I sent the others back. I suspected the Tide of Chaos had begun."
Fabian nodded slowly. "Same. I had mine return to the Tower."
The four Demon Night members moved to kneel. Lavender stopped them with a word. "This place is dangerous. No need for formalities."
Fabian turned to Esther. "Why did you stay?"
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]
"Because the Tide of Chaos brings opportunity." Esther's voice was steady. "The chaos moves the monsters that normally block certain paths. Places that couldn't be entered before — they open up." She paused. "The danger increases. But so does what's available."
Her skill was exceptional, and she and a few members of Demon Night had already managed to find several Black Nether Beads.
Each bead was a treasure, as valuable as a mid-rank sacred weapon. But they had come at a heavy price.
[MUSIC: SOMBER PIANO]
Two of Demon Night’s top War Lords hadn’t made it back.
[AMBIENT: REVERENT SILENCE]
Spotting Lavender, Esther approached with a deep reverence, holding out her findings. “My Queen, we recovered two Black Nether Beads. Please, accept them.”
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]
Lavender’s soul, even as a mere fragment of its true self, was unbelievably powerful. Having reached the highest echelons of cultivation, she was far beyond the warriors of this Lower World. It also meant that strengthening her soul any further was next to impossible. Nourishing pills and spiritual techniques had no effect. Even absorbing the entire life force of the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary’s holy tree would barely make a difference.
[SFX: MAGICAL RESONANCE]
But these Black Nether Beads were different. They were treasures from the upper world, known for their ability to repair damaged souls. Lavender knew they would be useful. She gave a slight nod of acceptance, gesturing for Zane to take the beads and keep them for her.
[MUSIC: SOLEMN DEVOTION]
For Esther and the rest of Demon Night, that simple gesture was the highest honor. The beads might have been priceless to them, but nothing compared to the privilege of serving their Queen.
Zane tucked the beads away. The outer circle still stretched out before them, and somewhere beyond it, the inner circle waited.
