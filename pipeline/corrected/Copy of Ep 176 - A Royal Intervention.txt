Ep 176 - A Royal Intervention
Word Count: 1128

[AMBIENT: HALL OF JUDGMENT]
 A deafening silence fell over the chamber as the execution blade halted mid-swing. All eyes turned from Zane's kneeling form toward the unexpected interruption that had frozen even the mighty Saint Augustus in his tracks. 
A graceful woman appeared at the entrance of the Hall of Judgment, walking toward them with purpose. It was Zane’s tutor, Isla Novak.
Color had returned to her face, and she radiated an energy she'd lacked just days before. There was a new air about her.
Though her own power was nothing compared to the saints she faced, Isla's presence was undeniably intimidating.
She held herself with an authority that spoke not just of uniting a nation, but of ruling one. It was an innate command, a birthright possessed only by the king’s direct descendants.
[MUSIC: REGAL DETERMINATION]
 After the humiliation she'd endured at Saint Viana's hands, Isla had spent two days in seclusion, wrestling with her own inaction. The shame had clung to her like a shadow, darkening her thoughts until she could barely recognize herself. It wasn't until her eleventh-hour decision to attend the proceedings at Mount Judgment that clarity finally pierced her fog of self-doubt. 
She could no longer evade her responsibilities—duties she had been neglecting while others suffered in her absence. The realization struck her with the force of a physical blow. And with that awakening came a chilling thought that sent ice through her veins: Had they all forgotten who she really was?
After all, she was Isla Novak, firstborn daughter of the emperor himself. Once known throughout the land as the Sky Princess, she was adored by thousands and respected, even worshipped, across nations. The memory of that power surged through her veins, rekindling a fire long dampened by doubt and circumstance.
The saints present seemed to falter under her regal, almost arrogant, presence. Their eyes darted away from her penetrating gaze, shoulders tensing as the atmosphere in the hall shifted palpably. Even Saint Augustus, notorious for his unwavering confidence, seemed momentarily uncertain as Isla's royal authority reasserted itself.
[SFX: ECHOING FOOTSTEPS]
"Answer my question," Isla demanded, her perfectly arched eyebrow rising as she fixed Saint Augustus with a gaze that seemed to pierce through his very soul. "Why did this trial begin before my arrival? Who gave the order to proceed? I require an explanation, Augustus. Now." She paused deliberately, allowing the weight of her royal displeasure to settle across the hall, her voice cutting like a polished blade. “A life for a life may appear just in principle. But if Zane killed Felix in self-defense, would he still be expected to die?”
"No, of course not!" Saint Augustus huffed indignantly, color rising to his cheeks. He took a deep breath to compose himself. "Our investigation during the proceedings revealed that on the day in question, Zane was causing a disruption at the Hall of Judgment. Felix, acting honorably, merely stepped in to help Murry in subduing him when, most unfortunately, he had no idea that..."
Isla cut him off. "And did Zane admit to this? Are you saying he confessed?"
"No, that account came from Murry's testimony," Saint Augustus replied. "As for Zane, he told us a completely different story."
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
 "I see. Then my next question is, why did you believe Murry and not Zane?" Isla's tone shifted from sharp to glacial as she stared down at Saint Augustus. The fire in her eyes made it clear to everyone that she was not negotiating.
"Uh, well, that is…" Saint Augustus stammered, fumbling for an answer before she cut him off again.
"It’s because the man who was killed was from the noble House Sterling, while Zane is a commoner. Isn't that it?" Isla's disgust was plain in her commanding voice. "If Felix had killed Zane that day, would you have even cared?"
Saint Augustus fell silent, but Saint Viana, standing beside him, spoke up, his voice clear and steady. "Your Highness, as the master of the Hall of Judgment, Murry is an upright and highly respected man. Compared to Zane, his word was far more credible. If Your Highness had been here, I'm sure you would have sided with Murry over the statement of a murderer."
Saint Viana’s words were a clever defense of the court's decision, pinning the blame on both Zane's guilt and Isla's absence.
[SFX: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
 Isla looked Saint Viana up and down, a dismissive gesture that made Saint Augustus shift uncomfortably. After a tense silence, Isla announced, "If that's the case, then why didn't you wait for me? I have evidence that proves Zane is innocent."
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
"You have evidence?" Saint Augustus' face was a mask of surprise.
Saint Viana laughed. "Your Highness, you can't be serious. I witnessed Zane's heinous crime with my own eyes. What possible evidence could exist that has escaped my attention?" His voice dripped with condescension.
She clapped once. Two attendants entered carrying a large Engraved Slab, which they set beside her. Isla touched it with a thread of Vitality.
[SFX: MAGICAL HUM]
An image bloomed across its surface. The Hall of Judgment. The day of the incident. Murry's men dragging Zane in. The interrogation. Felix arriving — not as a passerby acting honorably, but called. The Sevenfold Phantom Blade unleashed first. Zane taking the sword. The kill.
[SFX: TENSE SILENCE]
Every scene Murry had described, played back in full. Every lie, visible.
The Saints said nothing. There was nothing to say.
Isla deliberately turned away from Viana, acting as though the man's words were nothing but passing wind. She fixed her gaze upon the head judge. “Saint Augustus, I believe you owe me an explanation for this precipitous judgment.”
Saint Augustus's shoulders tensed visibly. Though he desperately wished to pass the blame onto Saint Viana, he knew it was useless. The weight of royal scrutiny pressed down upon him, leaving only one dignified option. 
With reluctance, he bowed his head in submission. "I offer my deepest apologies, Your Royal Highness. My regrettable negligence has led to today's rushed verdict. I humbly beg your forgiveness," he murmured, the words clearly painful for a man of his stature to utter.
[SFX: COLD TONE]
 "I can forgive you," Isla said coldly. "But I wonder if Zane will. If I hadn't arrived when I did, he'd be dead right now. You should ask him for forgiveness."
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
 As the weight of her words settled in the hall, the faces of Saint Augustus and all the other saints grew grim.
Zane was just a low-ranking disciple in the Skyreach Sect. None of them could understand why Isla would make such a demand.
Her meaning was unmistakable: she was forcing a noble saint to apologize to Zane.
