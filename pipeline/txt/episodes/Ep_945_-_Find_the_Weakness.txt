Ep 945 - Find the Weakness

Word count: 1144

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

Lamar's right arm was powder. The Ogres above the Sanctuary had gone silent, and the Demon Night warriors — moments from collapse — had found something they hadn't expected: a reason to hold on.

[SFX: CRASHING TREES]

Lamar was sent flying, crashing through one towering tree after another. The forest was already a wasteland from the earlier battles, littered with charred trunks that stood like skeletons against the sky.

[SFX: INTENSE FIRE ERUPTION]

After plowing through the last of the trees, a wave of intense fire erupted from his body. Lamar finally dug his feet into the scorched earth, catching his balance. His gaze fell to his empty right shoulder, and the shock on his face curdled into pure rage.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Then someone shouted the name, “Zane Lowell,” and Lamar froze, the rage on his face replaced by sheer disbelief.

He never would have imagined—not in his wildest dreams—that this skinny human was the one who’d driven the Ogres from the Tower of Sin.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

A booming shockwave of flame exploded from Lamar, so hot it instantly turned the last of the thousand-year-old trees to ash.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

Lamar slowly floated higher, his huge eyes locked on Zane with a terrifying calm. The silence that fell over him was far more menacing than his earlier fury. Everyone watching knew he was finally getting serious.

“So, you're Zane Lowell,” Lamar growled, his voice a low rumble. “I guess seeing is believing. To have this kind of power when you've just become a War Sage… No wonder you drove my people from the Tower of Sin.”

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

Lamar had been furious when he first heard that Zane had chased a pack of high-ranking Ogre War Lords from the Tower of Sin. For the Flaming Place and the Dread Place, being routed by a single human War Lord was the ultimate humiliation. The ones who'd fled were treated like garbage by the other Ogres.

When Lamar had chewed them out, the disgraced War Lords couldn’t even meet his gaze. But he’d still seen a flicker of defiance in their eyes. He hadn't understood it then. Now, facing Zane himself, he got it. You couldn't measure this human's strength by his rank.

“Although I don't know how you did it,” Lamar continued, his voice dropping low as he began to close the distance, “the power behind that punch was enough to earn my respect. Unfortunately, you're a War Sage without a domain.” 

His eyes narrowed. “Let's see if raw strength alone is enough to beat me.”

[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]

Then the Raging Fire Domain ignited around him — a roaring wall of flame that swallowed the air and set the forest below the Sanctuary ablaze. Heat waves spread outward in every direction, warping the light, making the entire Heavenly Feather Sanctuary shimmer like a mirage.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

There wasn't a trace of fear in Zane's eyes. He was already calculating his next move. His punch had cost Lamar an arm, but he knew he was on borrowed time. His spiritual weapon body wasn't strong enough to withstand a Soul Sea Realm master's assault. That was his weakness.

And Lamar had figured it out.

“The force from your punch,” Lamar said as he advanced, “felt like it fell into some abyss. You're retreating because you're afraid I'll cut off your connection to it — aren't you?”

Zane's eyes tightened. Lamar didn't know what the abyss was. But he'd deduced enough.

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

For the first time, Zane fell back.

The Demon Night warriors watching felt it — that flicker of hope guttering. Their last chance, retreating before a wall of fire.

[SFX: BLUE LIGHT FLASH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC INTERVENTION]

Just as Lamar's domain was about to swallow him, a cascade of blue light poured down from above. Lacey's Water Domain cut between them like a curtain, blocking Lamar's path.

“Lamar, going all out against a junior?” Lacey said coldly. “Your opponent is me.”

Lamar sneered. “Lacey, you're no match for me. As for Zane —“ his eyes flicked past her, “— he's not a junior. Even at first-level War Sage, he's qualified to be my opponent.”

“What an honor,” Zane said flatly.

He stepped behind Lacey, the dark golden longbow already in his hand. He nocked a black arrow and glanced at her. “Hold him. Do whatever you can.”

Lacey bit the tip of her tongue. Her body swayed as she burned her essence blood — and suddenly the Water Domain erupted into hundreds of blue tornadoes, spinning outward like tentacles toward Lamar.

“Hold me back? In your dreams!” Lamar clenched his remaining left fist and sent flaming blades screaming through the tornadoes, slicing them apart.

[SFX: ARROWS WHISTLING]

But while the two Soul Sea masters clashed, the black arrow left Zane's fingers.

Lamar was ready this time. He twisted — and it grazed past his ear by a hair.

Zane nocked another. Then another. Then a fourth.

With one arm gone and Lacey's tornadoes constantly reforming around him, Lamar couldn't concentrate. The arrows kept coming, each one aimed at his head, each one a fraction closer than the last.

More than once, an arrow whizzed past his ear, nearly taking his head clean off.

“We must help Master Lamar!” one of the Ogres on the ground roared.

Seeing their leader in trouble, the Ogre War Sages surged forward to help.

[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

[SFX: CLASH OF WEAPONS]

But the Demon Night War Sages weren't about to let them. They met the charge head-on, and the ground below erupted into an all-out brawl once again.

Lamar let out a string of curses. He couldn't believe he'd been backed into a corner like this. If something didn't change, Zane would kill him. It was only a matter of time.

Just then, three beams of light streaked across the horizon.

Three more Soul Sea Realm masters.

As Lamar recognized them, his face lit up with relief. At the same moment, Zane and Lacey’s expressions darkened.

“Aldous! Reuben! Help me kill the one with the arrows!” Lamar roared, barely fending off another one of Lacey’s attacks.

Two of the newcomers were the other Soul Sea Realm masters from the Flaming Place: Aldous and Reuben. They had been in pursuit of Chantel, one of the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary’s own masters.

Chantel, who was just a little stronger than Aldous and Reuben combined, had been leading them on a wild goose chase. 

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

She had baited the two Ogre masters away from the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary from the very beginning, leading them on a ten-thousand-mile loop across the main continent before finally circling back.

Three Soul Sea masters where there had been two. Chantel was already spent. And Zane had just run out of easy problems to solve.
