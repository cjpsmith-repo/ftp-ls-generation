Ep 335 - Hard Justice
Word count: 1292

At his current level, Zane had two main ways to use the Psyche Spike in a fight.
The first was a sneak attack. It was devastating against anyone with a weak soul or poor mental defenses. If Zane's Psyche Spike landed, even a split-second blackout could cost them the fight—or their life.
The second was for pure punishment. The feeling of having your soul pierced was an agony beyond words. The human body has its limits, but a soul can't pass out. No matter how much it hurts, the soul is forced to endure every last shred of agony.
[SFX: WHOOSHING SOUND]
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
Zane unleashed the Psyche Spike without a second thought.
It hissed as it stabbed, unseen, into Bryson’s mind. A low groan escaped Bryson’s lips, and the twisted look on his face showed everyone the torment he was in. His suffering sent a clear message to everyone watching: This is what happens when you betray House Lowell.
Bryson's face contorted. Sweat poured down his forehead. But he clenched his jaw and said nothing.
"Painful?" Zane said coldly. He held it a moment longer, then shifted his gaze to Owen.
Owen broke immediately. "It was Ford Stone! He killed your father himself — I swear it wasn't me, I only—"
Bryson closed his eyes. Useless to the end.
Zane nodded once. Now he knew that as long as Bryson was alive, the members of House Lowell would live in fear, worried he'd somehow regain power. If Zane didn't kill him now, he'd just come back for revenge, and every last one of them would pay the price. They wanted Bryson dead. It was the only way they would ever feel safe again. For the sake of the younger generation and the future of the House, Zane was determined to eliminate that threat for good.
Bryson Lowell had to die.
[SFX: COLLECTIVE SIGH]
Zane heard a sharp, collective sigh of relief as they all watched Bryson take his last breath.
[SFX: DARK ENERGY WISPS]
Several wisps of dark energy—Malice—escaped Bryson’s body. Zane knew Bryson had been a student of The Blood Prisoner and had learned the Blood Evil Force, but he was still surprised by the sheer amount of Malice Bryson had collected. To get this strong, Bryson must have killed far more people than Zane had ever imagined.
Finally, the Malice that had escaped Bryson's body gathered and hardened into a blood crystal. Counting the ones he already had, Zane now had four.
He was already thinking about how to use the Malice stored in the crystals. Since he was collecting this dark energy and would likely find more, he figured he should learn a cultivation method to put it to good use. But first, he had to deal with the rest of the problems in House Lowell and get rid of the traitors.
[MUSIC: SOLEMN DECLARATION]
Zane turned to the members of House Lowell, his voice ringing out, loud and serious. "Bryson Lowell has been executed. But Owen Lowell still needs to be sentenced. He was an accomplice in my father’s death, but his crime doesn’t warrant death. I’ll let all of you decide his punishment. Tomorrow, I will pay my respects at the Lowell ancestral graves. I will personally visit the tombs of our ancestors and pray for the future of House Lowell."
After his speech, Zane handed control of the Martial Arts Hall over to his uncles and the other elders. With Bryson gone, his job was done. He trusted that the elders, now back in charge, could handle the rest better than he could.
When Owen heard Zane's announcement, a little color returned to his ghostly face. He'd been so sure he was going to die; the thought of being spared had never even crossed his mind. As he watched Zane's strong, broad back turn and walk away, Owen's eyes filled with a new kind of awe. His nephew wasn’t a boy anymore. He was a man.
[AMBIENT: QUIET GARDEN]
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC]
Despite Elara’s special constitution and incredible talent, even now, she couldn’t break free from her restraints. Although Sir Kenneth had allowed her to come home with Zane, he had warned him not to even think about helping her escape. It would be pointless. No matter where she ran, he could find her.
At first, Zane had considered taking Elara to the Elysian Keep—a place whose mysteries ran so deep, even he couldn’t fully grasp them.
But Zane was only the temporary master of the Keep. There were a lot of things he wasn’t allowed to do there, and he couldn’t be sure it would be a safe enough hiding place for Elara.
[AMBIENT: SUNSET GARDEN]
[MUSIC: GENTLE EMOTIONAL]
The sun began to set, and Elara was still on the swing. The last rays of light shimmered on her, making her look as beautiful as a painting. No words could do her justice.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]
Watching her wide smile, Zane clenched his fists. He made a silent promise to himself to protect his little sister until his last breath.
If it ever came down to it, he wouldn’t hesitate for a second to sacrifice himself for her. She was the most important person in the world to him, his precious treasure, the only family he had left.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]
What Zane didn’t know was that his path was already written by the Laws of the World. Everything he would face, everything he would endure, was already set in stone. It was going to be a brutal journey, but Elara Lowell would be his guide—the light that cut through the darkness.
This was their destiny, and neither of them had any idea.
[AMBIENT: QUIET MORNING OUTDOORS]
[MUSIC: SOLEMN]
The next morning, the elders of House Lowell followed Zane up the hill in the backyard of Lowell Manor to the family cemetery. It was a sacred place.
[AMBIENT: CEMETERY STILLNESS]
Several tombstones stood silently on the hill, the air quiet and dignified, a peaceful resting place for the ancestors of House Lowell.
Zane prayed devoutly to the founders of the family. As the new leader of House Lowell, this was the first ritual to mark his new position.
[SFX: INCENSE BURNING]
Servants had lit three sticks of incense. Zane held them and bowed three times before his father’s tomb.
[MUSIC: VENGEFUL THEME]
After finishing the ritual, he left the graveyard, his expression hardening, a murderous look filling his eyes. Some people in Castermere County were about to pay for their sins.
[AMBIENT: TENSE MEETING ROOM]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS]
Meanwhile, in a room on the top floor of the Crescent Building in Castermere County, five people were gathered around a table, their discussion grim.
These were the leaders of the five most powerful families in Castermere County.
Returning stolen assets to Zane Lowell might appease him, perhaps with additional compensation. Even Chuck might suppress his rage over Darcy’s death. But for Ford Stone, the situation was dire. He’d personally killed Zane’s father, Milo Lowell; no payment could erase such a deed. Once Zane discovered the truth, Ford’s demise was inevitable.
Now that Bryson was dead, his cowardly brother, Owen, must have already ratted him out. It was only a matter of time before Zane came for him.
House Hawthorne, House Carter, and House Blackwood could all just give back what they'd stolen to appease Zane and beg for forgiveness. Their leaders could make offerings to the Lowells to ensure their families’ safety.
But Ford Stone would have to give up his life. Nothing less would satisfy Zane’s burning hatred. To pay for what he'd done, Ford would have to die, just to hope that Zane would spare House Stone.
Was he ready to make such a sacrifice?
