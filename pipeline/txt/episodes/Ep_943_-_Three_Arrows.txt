Ep 943 - Three Arrows

Word count: 1117

[AMBIENT: TOWER OF SIN]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The Ogres' assault on the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary was total. War Sages clashed across the sky in streaks of colliding light while below, the Sanctuary's grounds burned. 

Zane had barely registered the scale of it before Esther's warning cut through the chaos — Amber was gone, sealed away in her tribulation, and the Flaming Place and the Dread Place had chosen this moment to stake everything on wiping the Demon Night from the map.

“Okay. So that's how it is,” Zane murmured, nodding to himself.

[SFX: VOLCANIC EXPLOSION]

[AMBIENT: RAGING FLAMES]

Just then, a volcanic explosion tore through the sky. A wave of searing heat washed over the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, scorching the plant life.

But it wasn't just the plants. Even the War Sages couldn't stand the intense heat, and they all scrambled for cover, desperate to escape the inferno.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

As the flames parted, an Ogre rose from the heart of the fire. Crowned with a three-pronged circlet and clad in golden armor, he held a dark gold ghost staff in one hand—a magnificent, terrifying sight.

He was chasing a woman in blue. A shimmering, light blue Water Pattern Domain protected her from the flames, but no matter what she did, she couldn't shake the hulking Ogre on her heels.

“Lacey,” Esther Sterling whispered, her voice tight with worry.

Zane recognized her. It was Lacey, an expert from Demon Night, who had reached the Soul Sea Realm.

[AMBIENT: TOWER OF SIN]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]

The Flaming Place and the Dread Place had long held control of the Tower of Sin, thanks to having three more Soul Sea Realm experts than the Sanctuary. But that advantage meant nothing against Amber. She was strong enough to take on three or four Soul Sea Ogres by herself, and her power was the only thing maintaining the fragile peace between them.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

But Lacey wasn't Amber. Right now, she was being completely overpowered by the Soul Sea Ogre.

[SFX: BATTLE CLASH]

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC BATTLEFIELD]

Watching the chase, Esther and the other Demon Night War Sages—already locked in a fierce battle with their Ogre counterparts—felt their morale shatter. Six of their War Sages had already fallen. Their cries still echoed across the Sanctuary.

The Demon Night lines had been pushed back again and again, their dead leaving gaps that no one could fill.

[SFX: MENACING LAUGHTER]

“Lacey, Lacey, Lacey,” the Ogre rumbled with a nasty chuckle. “The Heavenly Feather Sanctuary is about to be wiped off the map. There's no point in fighting. All you women are so beautiful... why not be our brides? It's a much better fate than being a corpse, don't you think?”

[MUSIC: DEFIANT THEME]

Despite the taunts, Lacey's composure didn't break, and the Water Pattern Domain around her held firm. “Even if you destroy the Sanctuary, you Ogres will pay a price you can't imagine when Amber returns,” she said, her voice like ice. “Lamar, do you really think she'll let you live?”

On the main continent, Amber's name carried the same weight as Zane's did in the Heartlands. Both were considered the greatest warriors of their people.

[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]

Lamar's laughter only grew louder. “Amber? You're still counting on her? Do you really think her hiding place is a secret to us?” His eyes gleamed. “The Dread Place's warriors have already tracked her down. You think transcending a tribulation is something you can do while being hunted?”

[SFX: WATER RIPPLING DISTORTION]

Lacey's composure cracked. The Water Pattern Domain around her began to ripple wildly, its steady rhythm breaking apart.

“And if we take her alive —“ Lamar grinned, his voice dropping to something worse than a shout, “— we won't even destroy her. We'll soak her in our secret potion. her beauty, preserved forever. A perfect ornament for the Flaming Palace.”

“You —“ Lacey's voice fractured. Amber was the Demon Night's only hope. If she died during her tribulation, the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary would never be rebuilt.

Lamar raised his ghost staff again and charged, flames roaring higher around him. “The entire Sea God Continent,” he bellowed, his voice filling the sky, “is destined to fall under our iron hooves! Every last race — every last —“

[SFX: WHOOSHING PROJECTILES]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

As Lacey struggled to hold her barrier, she spotted three streaks of black light screaming toward them. Her eyes widened, and she whipped her head around, tracing their trajectory back to a lone human youth.

Zane. The name hit her like a physical blow. She knew that face — she'd seen it once, years ago, before he disappeared from the main continent entirely. Zane. Why did he come back?

Behind him, Esther had already grabbed at his arm. “Zane, don't — he's Soul Sea Realm, you can't —“

[SFX: BOWSTRING RELEASE]

Zane's expression didn't change. The bowstring had already left his fingers.

[SFX: WHOOSHING ARROWS]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

The black arrows were coming in too fast to dodge. I'll just have to block them! Lamar thought. With a roar, he swung his massive ghost staff to meet them head-on. The staff was as thick as a tree trunk, wielded by a bronze arm as wide as a man's chest.

Ogres were powerful, but their craftsmanship was crude—far behind the Divine Kingdom Continent, and even the Heartlands. The best an Ogre smith could produce was a mid-grade Relic-Class weapon. In contrast, smiths from the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms could create divine-class weapons, though that required help from the upper world. Without the Reverse Spirit Channel, not even they could forge such items.

That meant the ghost staff in Lamar's hand was, at best, a top-grade Relic-Class weapon.

[SFX: RAPID ACCELERATION CRACKS]

The arrows didn't come in straight. Each one accelerated — once, twice, three times — a series of sharp cracks splitting the air as they built speed. Nine sounds in total, three per arrow, each crack louder than the last.

Lamar heard three sounds he didn't understand. Not impacts — something else. He turned his head, frowning.

Then the second set hit. Three more cracks, sharper, faster. Then three more.

By the time he understood what was happening, it was already too late.

[SFX: IMPACT THUDS]

[SFX: ARROWS PIERCING FLESH]

A split second later, the three sharpened shafts punched right through his body.

Lamar hadn't taken the arrows lightly. The moment he'd spotted them, he'd abandoned his pursuit of Lacey and put all his energy into defense.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REALIZATION]

But as the arrows shattered his staff and tore through his flesh, he realized his mistake. He hadn't just underestimated their power—he had fatally misjudged it.
