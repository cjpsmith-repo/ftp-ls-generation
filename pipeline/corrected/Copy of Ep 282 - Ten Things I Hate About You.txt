Ep 282 - Ten Things I Hate About You
Word count: 1090
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
When the referee announced the next match, a slow, predatory smile spread across Tracy’s face.
Standing confidently in the center of the battle ring, she locked eyes with Zane, her gaze gleaming with anticipation. "I’ve been waiting for this!" she chirped, barely containing her excitement. "Looks like your luck just ran out, Zane. There’s no running from this one!"
Zane met her eager stare with a faint, tired smile. "Honestly," he said, his voice flat with genuine boredom, "unlike you, I’m not looking forward to this at all."
"Why not?" Tracy asked, pouting as she tilted her head.
"Because I have no idea what to do with you when I win," Zane replied simply, his shoulders giving a slight shrug of indifference.
Their challenge hung in the air between them: the loser would be at the winner’s beck and call.
"You really think you can beat me?" Tracy asked, arching an eyebrow while her fingers twitched slightly at her sides, betraying her growing irritation.
"I do," Zane affirmed with a simple nod, his calm demeanor unwavering despite the tension crackling between them.
"Fine." Her playful tone vanished instantly, replaced by something cold and dangerous. "I was just going to mess with you a little, but now I’ve changed my mind." She stepped closer, her voice dropping to a menacing whisper. "When I beat you, you’re going to be my horse. You’ll get down on all fours, and I’ll ride you whenever I damn well please."
[SFX: METALLIC GLEAM]
With that, she reached into her hair and pulled out two long Stiletto Hairpins. Each a foot in length, they gleamed in the light, clearly doubling as her weapons.
"Whoa, did she just say… a horse?" a student in the crowd muttered. "The stakes are insane!"
"That’s just cruel," another added, shaking his head. "If Zane loses, he’ll be totally humiliated!"
But everyone remembered what Tracy had done to Bastian. No one dared to speak up. You never knew what that little demon would do if you got on her bad side—she had a history of ignoring tournament rules and dishing out her own brand of punishment.
"Fine," Zane replied coolly. "But only if you can actually beat me."
"Still underestimating me," Tracy said with a final pout. "You’re going to regret it!"
[SFX: FLICK OF WRISTS]
With a flick of her wrists, the two Stiletto Hairpins in her hands blurred, becoming twin streaks of blue-gray light that shot straight for Zane.
Tracy’s speed was already legendary in her previous matches. Many students from the Skyreach Sect had been in awe of it, thinking it was flawless. But just as the deadly points were about to pierce him, Zane sidestepped with an almost lazy grace.
Tracy was clearly done playing around.
Patrick always said that if Tracy's raw talent was ranked number two among the Sect's students, it was only because no one dared claim number one. Her talent was unmatched, but she despised practicing, often training for a few days before slacking off for weeks. It was a waste of potential, but even so, her power was undeniable.
[SFX: BATTLE CRY]
"Feather Pricks!" Tracy shouted.
[SFX: SHARP METALLIC CLICK — SIXTEEN RAPID BEATS]
Without warning, Tracy turned both Stiletto Hairpins inward and pressed them against her own body in swift succession — temples, arms, calves, neck — sixteen acupuncture points, each leaving a faint red mark. Threads of Vitality mixed with something far older bled free from each one.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY]
[SFX: SHIMMERING AIR]
The air around her shimmered, and suddenly there wasn’t one Tracy, but several afterimages, all moving at an impossible speed. An object moving that fast could create phantom images, but this was different. The very energy pouring from Tracy had changed, becoming ancient and immense.
A gasp rippled through the crowd. They’d all grown up on legends of the great Legendary Beasts, but none had ever seen one.
And now, they understood. Tracy, one of their own Chosen, carried the blood of a Legendary Beast: the Blue Phoenix.
The legends named the most powerful of these creatures The Four Cardinal Beasts—the Cobalt Dragon, the Crimson Rosefinch, the White Tiger, and the Black Tortoise. But other mighty beasts like the Roc, the Nine-Colored Stag, and the Blue Phoenix were spoken of in the same hushed tones.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TRANSFORMATION]
As her bloodline’s power surged, a colossal wave of Vitality radiated from Tracy’s slender form. It gradually condensed behind her, materializing into the shimmering outline of a majestic Blue Phoenix. Even though it was only a faint shadow, the apparition pulsed with an intense, suffocating power that pressed against everyone present like an invisible tide.
Even the normally impassive supervising Elders abandoned their detached demeanor, leaning forward with undisguised fascination. Their eyes widened, fingers gripping their armrests as they witnessed this rare phenomenon. Tracy’s current strength posed no threat to masters of their caliber, but the awakening of a Legendary Beast’s bloodline represented something far more significant—a potential that transcended ordinary cultivation boundaries. Some of the older Elders exchanged knowing glances, their expressions betraying both awe and concern at this unexpected development.
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
"I don’t know what it is about you, but I really can’t stand you," Tracy declared, her voice transformed into something glacial and commanding. "I hate your face, I hate the way you act… It’s way past time for someone to teach you a lesson." Her entire presence had changed. The playful, teasing girl had vanished, replaced by a regal aura befitting a goddess who had never known the touch of mortal concerns.
Zane stood dumbfounded, a chill running down his spine despite his considerable power. What could I possibly have done to make her hate me this much? he wondered, searching his memories for any interaction that might have offended her.
According to Patrick’s earlier remarks, this bloodline power wasn’t something Tracy could summon at her discretion. Something about him had triggered this profound transformation, waking up the ancient power slumbering in her veins.
He had strutted into the arena anticipating an easy win, his confidence buoyed by his recent successes. Never had he imagined she was hiding an ace like this. After her transformation, her presence alone pressed against him like a crushing weight, the air around her shimmering with untapped potential.
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
Zane’s expression hardened as his fingers moved deliberately toward his Storage Ring.
He’d originally planned to reserve his sword for more anticipated opponents like Finn or Patrick. But facing this transformed Tracy, he realized he had no other choice. The cold calculation of combat reality settled in his mind—if he continued to hold back against such unleashed power, simple as that.
It was time to get serious.
