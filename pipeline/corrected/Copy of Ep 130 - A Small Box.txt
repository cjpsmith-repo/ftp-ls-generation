“This is bullshit! I can't die crammed in a box like this,” he thought.

Just then, Yolande knelt in front of him. She reached out, her fingers crackling with energy for another spell. This one, he knew, would tear him in two. He was completely helpless, unable to move an inch. He swallowed hard as despair washed over him.
[SFX: SWORD FLYING THROUGH AIR]
 At that exact moment, a small steel longsword whipped past her head, embedding itself in the wall behind her.
"The military office doesn't have time for this," she said, her voice flat as she addressed the person who threw it. "I'll lock him up and hand him over after the demons are defeated."
"You're not seriously going to keep him locked in there that long, are you?" Lennie asked, forcing a smile.
"Why not?" Yolande asked, blinking her gray eyes.
A headache started to throb in Lennie's temple. Zane knew the feeling. “Is she insane? I'll die if I have to stay curled up like this for that long!”
Stuck in the small box, Zane could barely lift his head or straighten his back. After just a few minutes, his entire body was already stiffening up.
“I'd rather die than be stuck like this for over ten days,” he thought. And knowing Yolande, she wasn't bluffing.
Before Lennie could come up with another way to change her mind, Yolande stood.
"My mind's made up. He stays in the box until the demons are defeated."
[SFX: DRAGGING SOUNDS]
 With that, she turned and walked away. Inside the cube, Zane was dragged along with her, scraping and bumping against the ground like a dog on a leash.
"Hey! Hey!" Lennie shouted after her. But it was obvious she wouldn't back down unless he could beat her in a fight. Lennie was confident in his skills, but remembering the raw power of her enchantment, he thought better of it.
Ingrid watched in horror as Yolande dragged Zane away like a lost puppy.
"Uncle, what are we going to do?" she asked, her voice trembling.
"There's nothing I can do right now," Lennie sighed. "We just have to hope he can hang in there until we can get him some help." He tried to sound reassuring, but even the Lord of Silverspire couldn't change Yolande's mind once it was set.

 Zane was miserable. He was starting to think Yolande was torturing him on purpose. The clear box scraped and bounced along the cobblestones, flipping over every few feet. More than once, he ended up face-down, tumbling helplessly as she dragged him through the streets. Soldiers and townsfolk started to gather, staring at the bizarre spectacle.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL PRISON]
 "Yolande, please release me from this contraption," Zane implored, his voice dampened by the transparent walls of his magical prison.
[SFX: PRISON BOX FLIPPING]
 The box suddenly flipped, leaving Zane face-to-face with the dusty floor. He strained his neck muscles, managing only to glimpse her delicate feet as they moved across the wooden planks.
"You violated established protocols," Yolande replied, her tone maddeningly calm and methodical. "What compelling reason could justify your liberation?"
"I committed no transgression!" Zane protested, frustration evident in his rising voice. He appealed to her logical nature, hoping reason might penetrate her rigid sense of justice. "That arrogant Preston attempted to end my life! Then his brother Josef intervened, leaving me no choice but to defend myself with lethal force! And as if that weren't enough, Hugh appeared with murderous intent as well!"
[SFX: BOX DRAGGING]
[AMBIENT: SMALL ROOM]
 His desperate explanations fell on deaf ears as she dragged his prison into a small, sparsely furnished room and deposited him unceremoniously in the corner. 
[SFX: FABRIC RUSTLING]
 Zane's heart nearly stopped when, without warning or apparent concern, Yolande began untying the elaborate knots of her Rainbow Gown. The fabric shimmered with otherworldly colors as it loosened around her slender frame. Her complete lack of self-consciousness left him simultaneously bewildered and uncomfortable, his cheeks burning with embarrassment at this unexpected turn of events.
After she'd stripped down, she stared at Zane for a moment, her big, gray eyes unblinking. For the first time, a flicker of an expression—a slight frown—creased her brow.
[SFX: FABRIC COVERING BOX]
 Then she covered the clear box with her Rainbow Gown before walking over to a large wooden barrel and climbing inside.
Zane had read enough books to know what a gentleman was supposed to do, and he was pretty sure staring wasn't on the list. He knew it was messed up, but for a guy his age, it was impossible not to feel a jolt at that moment.
"If you were truly concerned about my seeing you, you might have secured the covering more thoroughly," he mused silently. "Why leave such a tantalizing gap?"
Zane couldn't fathom what thoughts swirled through Yolande Shaw's enigmatic mind. Yet through that deliberate opening in the fabric, she remained perfectly visible to his imprisoned gaze.
[MUSIC: TENSE INTERNAL CONFLICT]
 "Stop being despicable! Avert your eyes!" his conscience demanded fiercely.
But his willpower faltered with each passing moment, his vision betraying him as it repeatedly, inevitably returned to the forbidden view before him.
