Ep 994 - Chaos Energy

[AMBIENT: ICY WIND]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
There’s a limit to how much pain a man can take. The fact that Zane had endured that level of agony for so long without shattering proved just how unbreakable his will was.
When the last of the pain finally bled out of him, a wave of pure relief crashed over him.
He’d survived three months of torture.
Cora and Tia had no idea it was over.
[SFX: HARP STOPS]
[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]
As Zane slowly pushed himself into a sitting position, Cora’s harp fell silent. Tia leaned closer, her voice a soft, worried whisper, “Zane? Are you okay?”
Looking at their frantic faces, a wicked little thought sparked in his mind. He decided to have a little fun with them. He gave his head a weak shake and twisted his face into a mask of pain. “I… I don’t think I’m gonna make it this time,” he rasped, forcing a weak cough.
The light instantly went out of Tia and Cora’s eyes. A few feet away, Isla, who had been lost in prayer for him, scrambled over and grabbed his hand.
The three women leaned in, a heavy silence falling over them.
[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]
[MUSIC: PLAYFUL STRINGS]
Then, in a blur, his arms shot out, pulling all three of them into a tight embrace. Cora and Isla shrieked. With a booming laugh that echoed off the ice, he launched himself right off the palace roof, holding them tight.
[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]
[MUSIC: SOARING ORCHESTRAL]
Suspended in the air, the world fell away, and for a moment, so did their worries.
Isla’s eyes were wide with wonder, the shock melting into awe. Zane’s arm was a solid, warm band around her, and she could feel the heat of his body through their clothes. Cora started laughing, playfully pounding on his chest. “You’re terrible!” she scolded.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
[SFX: WIND WHOOSH]
But Tia was a different story. Her gaze was sharp enough to cut, her face a thundercloud of fury. A second later, a bone-chilling white mist began to swirl around them.
[SFX: ICY WIND GUST]
“Hey! It’s freezing!” Cora yelped, the playful mood instantly shattered.
The frigid air bit at their skin, making Isla and Cora shiver.
[AMBIENT: ICE PALACE INTERIOR]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON ICE]
With a sharp huff of indignation, Tia slipped from Zane’s grasp and stormed back inside the ice palace.
For the last few months, a fragile peace had settled between the three women. With Zane unconscious, there was nothing to fight over. But now that he was awake, the old cracks were already starting to show. You could bury jealousy, but you could never kill it.
Zane managed a weary smile as he released Cora and Isla, following Tia back inside to try and smooth things over.
[AMBIENT: QUIET NIGHT]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Later that night, showered and changed, Zane found himself alone on a platform in Cloud Hall. It had taken a lot of sweet-talking to calm Tia down, but he finally had a moment to himself.
He sat cross-legged and turned his perception inward.
What he found stopped him cold.
The liquefied Vitality that had filled his core for years — dense, viscous, utterly his — was gone. Every last drop of it. In its place, a gray mist swirled slowly through the space where his Vitality had been. Chaos Energy. Formless, vast, and entirely unfamiliar.
“Did I actually do it?” he murmured.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DEEP BASS]
The Cobalt Dragon was quiet for a long moment. When he spoke, his voice carried something Zane had almost never heard in it: genuine disbelief.
“Chaos into Vitality… He actually did it.” A slow exhale. “Every warrior who has ever lived has cultivated Vitality seized from Heaven and Earth. That is the iron law of the universe. Not even a Supreme Lord can break that shackle. And yet here you are.”
He seemed to be speaking as much to himself as to Zane.
Zane stared inward at the gray mist. “So what does this mean for me? If my Vitality is gone, how do I advance through any of that?”
The Cobalt Dragon was silent.
“I don’t know,” he finally said. “This has never happened before. It’s beyond what I can reason through. Even your Master — the one who created this method — couldn’t answer that question. He never walked this path himself.”
The unknown stretched out ahead of Zane like an unmarked road in the dark.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
He drew a slow breath and tried something simple: running the Chaos energy through his meridians the way he used to run Vitality.
The moment it moved, a sharp pain lanced through him.
He gritted his teeth — then it was gone. Three seconds, maybe four. Then nothing.
He exhaled. Tried again. Smoother this time.
A stream of vigorous Chaos energy gushed out from his palm.
His thoughts drifted back to the Fire Law and the unbelievable power he’d just witnessed. He remembered the fireball swelling to an impossible size before the circular Light of Water enveloped it, reflecting a brilliance that rivaled the sun.
[SFX: WHISPER ECHO]
“That power is insane,” Zane whispered to the empty hall.
He was damn lucky he’d aimed it at the sky. Even then, the fallout had scorched parts of Cloud Hall, sending students scrambling to douse the flames. After the blast, the light had hung in the air for a few moments, a burning afterimage, before finally winking out.
Zane stared up at the sky where the light had been, the Cobalt Dragon’s words about Primordial Chaos echoing in his head.
Other warriors trained their Vitality, an energy born of and bound by the heavens. But the energy humming in his veins now… this was different. This was Chaos Energy, drawn from the primordial void itself.
He’d only used a sliver of it to trigger the Fire Law. What would happen if he applied the techniques from his Master’s attunement handbook? His Master had theorized the Primordial Chaos tactic but had never dared walk the path himself.
But for Zane, it wasn’t a theory. Chaos Energy had completely purged the Vitality from his body. He was a new slate. With this raw power, he could forge his own attunement method, one that could shatter the very limits his Master had only ever dreamed of.
[MUSIC: EPIC RISING ORCHESTRA]
A thrill shot through him, his heart pounding with the sheer scale of it all. The future wasn’t just open; it was an uncharted universe of possibilities.
No one had ever walked this path. It would be a brutal, lonely journey of trial and error. But Zane knew, with a certainty that settled deep in his bones, that if he could just see it through… he wouldn’t just be following history.
He would be making it.
