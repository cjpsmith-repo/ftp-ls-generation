Ep 262 - White Bone Armor
Word Count: 1043
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: HIGH STAKES BATTLE]
[SFX: WHOOSHING SOUND]
Stanley’s golden threads shot out toward Jacob, expanding into an intricate spiderweb of shimmering strands. The legendary technique, known as the Golden Razornet, was Stanley’s most lethal weapon—a beautiful yet terrifying sight that glinted ominously in the arena light.
Typically, Stanley used this technique to corner his opponents within the deadly net. Once they were ensnared, his victims were methodically sliced into ribbons—a gruesome, bloody spectacle that had earned him both fear and respect throughout the Peaks. He had deliberately withheld this technique until now, saving it for when victory truly mattered. But facing a master’s prized student left no room for restraint.
Stanley’s palms grew damp with sweat as he concentrated. The stakes were impossibly high; one miscalculation, and he would be the one painting the arena floor crimson with his lifeblood.
"Arrogance before power is a fatal mistake," Stanley muttered under his breath, his eyes narrowing as he assessed Jacob’s approach.
Though Stanley had anticipated Jacob might possess some countermeasure against the Razornet, the young prodigy’s behavior defied all logic. Rather than executing an evasive maneuver, Jacob strolled casually toward the gleaming web, his face betraying not even a hint of concern.
Stanley’s eyes widened in disbelief as he watched his opponent deliberately walk into what should have been certain doom. Breaking the Razornet from within was considered virtually impossible—even for Soul masters.
The fish is on the hook, Stanley thought, a surge of anticipation coursing through his veins. Time to reel him in.
A cold, calculating smile crept across Stanley’s face, his eyes glinting with predatory satisfaction. People who underestimated his abilities always ended up paying for it in blood. Jacob’s genius status meant nothing in the face of the Razornet’s lethal efficiency.
Jacob’s insufferable confidence—that casual dismissal of Stanley’s most feared technique—was going to be his undoing. Stanley savored the moment, already imagining the shock that would replace that smug expression when the threads began to tighten.
[SFX: SHARP METALLIC TWANG]
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
Snapping back to the present, Stanley gave a precise, calculated tug on his fan. A single golden thread twitched in response, followed by another, until the entire network of threads came alive with deadly purpose.
Woven in an intricate geometric pattern he had perfected through years of practice, the threads rippled and twisted with hypnotic grace. Within seconds, they transformed into a massive web that surged toward Jacob with predatory intent.
[AMBIENT: CROWD FALLS SILENT]
A hush fell over the crowd, their faces turning grim.
The gold thread was as thin as a hair, but as sharp as a steel sword. It could slice right through bone.
"My Golden Razornet can cut through a Spirit weapon, let alone a person’s fragile flesh and bone," Stanley sneered. "I know you spent three years on your training, Jacob, but you’re dreaming if you think you can withstand it."
[SFX: METALLIC NET CLOSING]
As the net closed in, the space around Jacob shrank.
He walked a few more slow steps, then stopped. He glanced casually at Stanley, and to Stanley's utter shock, the man was smiling.
It was a smile full of disdain, contempt, and a chilling indifference—a complete and utter lack of fear.
[SFX: MIST EXPLOSION]
[SFX: BONE CRYSTALLIZATION]
Suddenly, a crimson mist exploded from Jacob’s body. A split second later, the mist hardened, crystallizing into gleaming white bone. Layer upon layer of it encased his arms, knees, and chest—a ghastly suit of bone armor.
[AMBIENT: DARK ENERGY PULSE]
A cold, sinister energy pulsed from the sleek, jade-like bones, washing over the arena in a dark wave. A shiver ran down Stanley's spine. For a terrifying second, he felt like he wasn’t facing Jacob anymore, but some ancient, malevolent god.
"A Dark Art!" Stanley choked out, his face paling as he felt the corrupt energy radiating from Jacob. He never would have imagined he'd study the Dark Arts.
"It’s true. That’s definitely a Dark Art!" a voice in the crowd whispered loudly. "He reeks of blood and death. I can feel it from here. I can’t believe Jacob would go down that path."
"It’s not an easy path," another murmured. "But if you’re determined, the rewards are huge. Practitioners of the Dark Arts are far stronger than normal warriors."
But more than the Dark Art, it was the dismissal that enraged Stanley. If Jacob had treated this as a real fight from the start, Stanley could have accepted defeat if he was outmatched. But this… this humiliation was unbearable. It was like he wasn’t even there. He had to teach Jacob a lesson. Arrogance has a price.
[SFX: MAGICAL ATTACK]
"Gold Flower Fan!"
[SFX: METAL FAN SNAP]
With a flick of his wrist, Stanley snapped his fan open and hurled it toward Jacob. At the same moment, the Spirit Weapon’s special skill flared to life.
Special skills were a rarity in the Eastern Territory. A Mid-Grade Spirit Weapon with a skill was as valuable as a Top-Grade one without. It was why Zane’s Phoenix and Emerald Crystals were so precious—they could elevate a weapon to a whole new level.
And now, his Gold Flower Fan's power was fully unleashed.
[SFX: WHOOSHING BLADES]
High in the air, the fan multiplied into a swarm of shadowy blades. Each one spun wildly as it streaked toward Jacob, moving with an intelligence of its own and homing in on the weak points in the White Bone Armor—the joints at his elbows and knees, his waist, and his neck.
If he could hit those spots, Stanley thought, this fight wasn’t over. He could still cripple him.
[SFX: SWIRLING MIST]
A ghost of a smile touched Jacob's lips. He didn't even bother to move. Another crimson mist swirled around him, and when it cleared, the White Bone Armor had sealed over every gap, covering him from head to toe.
The sight left a bitter taste in Stanley's mouth. Jacob did have every right to look down on him. There was nothing Stanley could do. The White Bone Armor was flawless. As long as Jacob was inside it, he was untouchable.
[SFX: VIOLENT IMPACT]
The thought had barely formed when Jacob slammed into him.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS FADE]
In the next moment, everything went black.
