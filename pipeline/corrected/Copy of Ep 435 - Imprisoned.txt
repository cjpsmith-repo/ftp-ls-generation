Ep 435 - Imprisoned

Word count - 1221

[AMBIENT: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS THREAT]

Zane's impromptu entrance had drawn unwanted attention—a squadron of heavily armed guards now descended upon him with hostile intent.

He studied the burly squadron that fanned out to encircle him. These weren't ordinary sentries, but Zane had no reliable way of gauging their true cultivation level. The uncertainty gnawed at him. After everything he'd survived, would he fall here, to faceless guards in an unfamiliar city?

[SFX: MARCHING FOOTSTEPS]

The circle closed around him. The head guard, perched atop a bird-like monster whose talons scraped against the stone, surveyed Zane with undisguised contempt. His face twisted into a sneer as he delivered his verdict. "He's not clan, but he used the Dragon Soar Arena without permission. Lock him up. A thousand years. Then we'll turn him loose!"

[SFX: MAGICAL BINDING]

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ENERGY]

The moment the guard finished speaking, small green orbs materialized in each warrior's palm. Before Zane could react, the spheres erupted with threads of emerald light that lashed out like living serpents, coiling around his limbs and torso with crushing force.

The bindings constricted instantly, pinning his arms against his sides. Zane tried to channel his Dragon Scales, but the mystical restraints seemed designed specifically to suppress cultivation energy. A cold realization settled over him—these weren't ordinary prison techniques.

Without ceremony or hesitation, the guards seized him roughly by the shoulders and began hauling him toward Cerulean City. His feet dragged across the ancient stone as they moved with practiced efficiency, clearly accustomed to dealing with trespassers.

As they approached the city gates, Zane's eyes climbed the walls — massive, each one tall as a mountain, covered in divine rune patterns so large each stroke stretched for miles. He stared. "Did you draw those?"

"Heh." The Cobalt Dragon's disdain was palpable. "This is a minor receiving city for Dragon-blooded from the lower realms. It doesn't warrant my attention."

Zane opened his mouth to protest, to explain that the Cobalt Dragon itself had brought him here, but the lead guard's contemptuous glare silenced him before he could speak. They weren't interested in explanations. To them, he was simply another unauthorized intruder who had defiled their sacred arena.

The irony wasn't lost on him—saved by dragon blood, taught by a dragon spirit, only to be imprisoned by dragons themselves.

[MUSIC: BITTER IRONY]

A thousand years? Are you kidding me? he thought with a bitter smile. "Cobalt Dragon," he muttered, "your True Dragon Lineage doesn't exactly roll out the welcome mat!"

[SFX: ECHOING DRAGON VOICE]

[MUSIC: ANCIENT POWER]

"Ha! That's what happens when you don't carry the blood of the True Dragon Lineage," the dragon's voice echoed in his mind, laced with amusement. "Since you're an outsider from the Lower World, you get punished for using the Arena without permission. Don't worry, they'll let you go in a thousand years." He chuckled as if it were nothing. "I made that rule myself, ages ago."

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL REALM]

[MUSIC: EPIC LORE]

The True Dragon Lineage was massive. It wasn't just the dragons themselves; it was a web of different races all tied by blood, like the powerful Dragon-blooded. Many of these races lived in the Lower World, so the True Dragon Lineage had built Dragon Soar Arenas to transport their own to the upper world. Any outsider who dared to use one was to be imprisoned for a thousand years. It was an absolute rule.

The young master of House Kendrick, a master who had reached the Inner-Core Realm, had once tried to use an Arena. But the Cobalt Dragon had altered the divine texture on it, causing him to fail. Ironically, that failure saved his life. He would have died in that cell, since even an Inner-Core master only lives for about eight hundred years.

Zane was speechless. He wasn't even twenty. At his current level, he’d be lucky to live to three hundred. He wouldn't survive long enough to see his own release.

"Relax," the Cobalt Dragon said, his voice a calm rumble in Zane's mind. "I am of the True Dragon Lineage. Even if I can't show myself, I won't let them harm you." Hearing that, Zane felt a flicker of relief.

[AMBIENT: PRISON]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

 Before long, they arrived at the prison.

A guard led Zane down a dim corridor lined with iron-barred cells. The air hung thick with dampness and the acrid smell of unwashed bodies. At the end of the passage, the guard unlocked an empty cell and gestured for Zane to enter.

[AMBIENT: PRISON CELL]

[SFX: METAL DOOR CLANG]

Zane stepped inside. The door clanged shut behind him with grim finality.

He surveyed his surroundings. 

The other prisoners were not what he'd expected. Soul Realm warriors, Inner-Core masters, even a few at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm — all from different lower realms, all caught using Dragon Soar Arenas without permission. Overlords of their own worlds, locked up like wayward students.

Settling onto the bench, he retrieved his brush and materials from his storage ring. Even here, he wouldn't waste time.

His hand moved with practiced precision, channeling energy through the brush as he began etching a meditation rune. Each stroke flowed seamlessly into the next, the divine texture taking shape beneath his fingertips. Within minutes, the intricate pattern glowed faintly in the shadows, its soft luminescence pushing back the darkness.

[SFX: AGGRESSIVE FOOTSTEPS]
One of the prisoners — an Inner-Core warrior who'd clearly been someone important back home — stepped toward him. "I'm talking to you. You think you're too good to answer?"
Before Zane could respond, two guards appeared at the fence, spears leveled. "Quiet. Next one who makes trouble gets another century added."
The prisoner sat back down without another word.

[SFX: MURMURING CROWD]

 When the other prisoners saw the Glyph Zane had drawn, they began to whisper among themselves. "Incredible… to have perfect brushwork at his age!"
It was rare to see a runic expert with such skill anywhere.
But the hand that drew the Glyph wasn't Zane's—it was the Cobalt Dragon's. Once the drawing was finished, the runic quill left a line of strange characters in the paper’s margin. Zane couldn't read them, but he knew they had to be the Dragon Glyphs the Cobalt Dragon had mentioned.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHIMES]

"It's done," the Cobalt Dragon declared. "Give this to the guards and tell them to get it to the city lord." Zane nodded, gathering the supplies from the floor.

[AMBIENT: PRISON DUNGEON]

[SFX: METAL BARS KNOCK]

He walked to the bars of his cell and knocked.

"What do you want?" a gruff voice demanded. A guard ambled over, his expression cold.

"Please give this to the city lord," Zane said, holding out the Glyph paper.

[SFX: GUARDS LAUGHING]

"The lord?" The guards looked at each other, then burst out laughing. "Our city lord? Kid, do you have any idea who you're talking about?"

"These Lower World runts are all the same. Think they're so important."

"This is why they get a thousand years," one of the guards sneered. "It's the only way they learn their place."

Zane ignored their insults. He just waved the Glyph paper slightly. "Why don't you take a look before you run your mouth?"

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[SFX: PAPER SNATCH]

The guard shot him a dirty look, then glanced down at the paper. The moment he saw the Dragon Glyphs in the margin, his eyes went wide. 

He snatched the paper from Zane's hand, his voice trembling as he demanded, "Where did you get this?"
