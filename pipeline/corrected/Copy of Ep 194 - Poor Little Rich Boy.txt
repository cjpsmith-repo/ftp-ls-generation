Ep 194 - Poor Little Rich Boy

[AMBIENT: TENSE HOUSEHOLD]
[MUSIC: SOMBER BACKGROUND]
The young men stood at the front door, shocked at the way the butler spoke to Ethan.
But Ethan wasn’t surprised by the butler’s condescension. Ever since he was a kid, he'd been looked down on and treated like dirt by the rest of House Moon. And since he wasn’t strong enough to fight back and had no family to back him up, he just had to take it.
He was used to it, so it didn’t really faze him. Zane, Dustin, and Rhys, however, were definitely not going to take this insult lying down.
Who the hell did this butler think he was? Just because he'd climbed from servant to head butler didn’t give him the right to talk to them like that.
Of the three, Dustin was the most pissed off. Back at House Blackwood, no servant would ever dare talk to their master that way. Dustin frowned. “Ethan, is this seriously how your staff talks to you?”
Ethan just smiled, about to say something, but the butler, Ralph, overheard Dustin and bristled.
The butler puffed up his chest and sneered at Dustin. “You got a problem with me, fatso? What did I do wrong?”
Dustin’s scowl deepened at the man’s sheer arrogance.
Dustin was usually a pretty easy-going guy, but when someone got on his nerves, he had no problem putting them in their place. If Dustin lost his temper, he’d ruin the entire party for House Moon. Anyone who pissed off a member of House Blackwood learned a lesson they wouldn’t soon forget.
But Dustin bit his tongue. The last thing he wanted was to cause a scene and make things harder for Ethan.
Ethan was mortified by the butler’s attitude. He was used to taking abuse in his family home, but the same energy definitely didn’t apply to his friends. As the host, he couldn’t just stand by while his staff disrespected a guest from House Blackwood.
Ethan snapped at the man. “ Ralph! These are my friends! Show them some damn respect!”
The butler just chuckled. “Oh yeah? And who do you think---”
[SFX: SUDDEN INTERRUPTION]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
Ralph couldn’t finish his sentence.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSE]
A sudden, deathly chill shot down Ralph’s spine. It felt as though an apex predator had fixed its gaze directly through him, dissecting his very essence as an involuntary shiver wracked his rigid frame.
When he turned, his face drained of all color, he met Zane’s piercing gaze.
Though Zane’s expression remained unnervingly calm, he projected just a sliver of his Vitality---enough to place an invisible, crushing weight upon Ralph’s spirit. All that cultivating had given Zane the ability to pressure a man’s very soul, literally strangling the words in his throat before they could form.
Zane released his spiritual grip on the trembling butler and turned to his friend, struggling to keep his tone light. “Ethan, let’s just go inside,”
Ralph watched them go, color slowly returning to his face. By the time they reached the door, he'd convinced himself it was nothing. Just a stare. He opened his mouth to get the last word in.
Zane turned.
[SFX: PSYCHIC IMPACT]
Ralph's voice died. His hands flew to his head as he crumpled to his knees, a wail tearing out of him. He rolled on the marble, completely senseless, while the two servants beside him stared in open-mouthed horror.
Zane turned back and walked inside without breaking stride.
Ethan glanced between Ralph’s terrified expression and Zane’s composed demeanor. He wasn’t sure what just happened, but the butler’s ghostly pallor gave him a pretty good idea that it was Zane’s doing. A mixture of gratitude and unease flickered across his features.
He clapped Dustin on the shoulder, feeling the tension in his friend’s muscles. “Sorry about that, Dustin. I know Ralph’s an arrogant ass, but he’s not worth your time or energy. Let’s just head inside, okay? The night’s still young.”
Dustin just nodded, his jaw still clenched with lingering anger as he decided the glorified house manager wasn’t worth another moment of his consideration. His eyes, however, darted toward Zane with newfound appreciation. He fell into step behind Ethan and Rhys, their footsteps echoing across the polished marble.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON MARBLE]
Zane brought up the rear, his face once again a mask of casual indifference that betrayed nothing of the power he had so effortlessly wielded moments before.
[AMBIENT: INDOOR MANOR PARTY]
[MUSIC: ELEGANT PARTY]
Once inside, Ethan revealed that since his father’s passing, his uncle had relegated him to the status of an outsider rather than embracing him as family.
“Your uncle’s a real piece of work,” Dustin growled. “Has no one in your bloodline shown you any loyalty?” The revelation of Ethan's circumstances only intensified his disgust toward House Moon.
Following his father’s death, the family should have encircled Ethan with support and protection. Instead, they had systematically alienated him, treating him with thinly veiled disdain. Dustin suspected they would have expelled him entirely from the family estate had societal expectations permitted such a brazen act. The thought of such callous treatment toward one’s own kin made his stomach churn with indignation.
[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]
Rhys shook his head, his voice tight with anger. "Growing up in a family that cold... that's just messed up."
After taking a sip of tea, Zane said lightly, “Well, that's all the more reason for us to have your back. So, what’s the plan when they start pulling crap like that again?”
Ethan’s story hit a little too close to home for Zane. Both of them had lost their standing after their fathers’ deaths, which only made Zane more determined to help his friend.
Seeing the frustration on their faces, Ethan said, “Thanks for being here, guys. I could really use the support right now.”
Dustin laughed. “Then it’s settled. Anyone from House Moon gives you a hard time, I’ll take care of them.”
Normally, Dustin wasn’t the type to get mixed up in another family’s drama.
Even though House Moon wasn’t a major power, it was still a noble house in the northern region, and Dustin had no reason to make an enemy of them. It wasn’t that he was afraid---a fading family like House Moon was nothing compared to one of the Seven Great Houses.
But House Blackwood operated on a simple principle: never act without a clear benefit. So why was he so willing to stick his neck out for Ethan?
[MUSIC: THOUGHTFUL DETERMINATION]
This time, though, Dustin had his reasons.
From what he’d seen, Zane was a smart bet.
His gut was screaming that this kid was going to do unbelievable things, and soon.
