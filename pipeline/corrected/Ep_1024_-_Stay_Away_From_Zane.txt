Ep 1024 - Stay Away From Zane

Word count: 1103

[MUSIC: DARK BROODING]

“Stay away from Zane.”

Rylan’s face went dark at Clark’s words.

Back in the Lower World, his name had carried weight. He was feared, respected, and it had forged a pride in him that bent for no one. Anyone who’d dared to cross him before had paid with their life.

There was no way in hell he was letting Zane get away with humiliating him. He’d already imagined a hundred different ways to make him pay. And now here was Clark, telling him to back off? The rage boiled in his gut.

“Why?!” he demanded, the logic completely lost on him.

Hawthorne just shrugged. “No particular reason. Just a friendly heads-up. Take it or leave it, that’s on you.”

Just as they were turning to leave, Clark paused, leaning in close to Rylan’s ear. “That kid… the day he ascended? It took him all of a few seconds to adjust to the Vitality Pressure.”

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS FADING]

And with that, the two armored warriors turned and walked away without a second glance.

Their departure left Rylan wrestling with himself. He’d brush off advice from just about anyone else, but he and those two went way back. They were blunt, sure, but he knew they were just looking out for him.

Could what Clark said be true? A few seconds? That’s all it took Zane to adapt to the Vitality Pressure after coming out of the Ascending Well? A deep frown creased Rylan’s forehead.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Rylan remembered his own ascension. It had taken him a full five days to acclimate, and even that was considered lightning-fast—a sign of his own potential. But a few seconds? A feat like that wasn’t just impressive; it was the kind of thing that would put Zane squarely on the Soulbound Sect’s radar.

Plenty of warriors born in the Upper World looked down on ascenders from below. But the Soulbound Sect wasn’t like that. They were always hungry for talent, and they didn’t give a damn where you came from. All that mattered to the tenth-tier sacred sect was potential.

The armored warriors had reported Zane’s insane adaptation time the moment he’d cleared the Ascending Well.

The power gap between the two worlds was massive, but every so often, someone truly extraordinary made the climb. The Soulbound Sect knew this, and from the moment Zane first picked up a pickaxe, their spies had been watching his every move.

Luckily for him, Zane had set up a divine texture the day before, right before using the Roaring Token. It had thrown up a temporary shield, hiding him from those prying eyes.

[AMBIENT: OPEN FIELD WITH WIND]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE FLUTE]

Meanwhile, the very person sparking all this rage and curiosity was meditating in an open field, eyes closed to the world.

Every time his warrior’s spirit stirred within him, Zane felt a crushing sense of urgency. His mind raced with a laundry list of things he had to do—at the top of which was helping his sword spirit, Lavender, recover.

Zane’s efficiency in the deep levels was high. He moved fast, and if things ever went truly sideways, he had a trump card — a token that could pull him into his immortal mansion in an instant. He hadn’t needed it yet. Once, he and Freya found a cluster of fifty or sixty Sovereign Stones on a single plant, but three level-twelve beasts were circling it. He took Freya and left without a second thought.

The next day, he and Freya went back into the mine and returned without incident. The day after, the same. On the third day, still nothing from Rylan.

On the fourth day, Rylan appeared in the settlement. When he spotted Zane, he actually smiled and nodded a greeting.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

The watching ascenders understood immediately: Rylan had backed down. No one knew why. Everyone had their theories.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL MELODY]

Weeks passed. When their count reached eighty Sovereign Stones, Freya’s mood lifted noticeably. Twenty more and they could leave the mine and formally join the Soulbound Sect — she’d be able to reach her uncle.

Zane told her he was aiming for two hundred.

Freya stared at him. Two hundred meant buying independent status — walking away from a tenth-tier sect entirely. She couldn’t understand why anyone would do that. Zane didn’t explain. They were temporary partners, and that was all she needed to know.

[AMBIENT: MILITARY COMMAND ROOM]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]

Back at the mine’s command post, two figures in blue robes had just arrived — and the armored warriors were falling over themselves.

An armored warrior snapped to attention. “Yes! He’s in our mine, for sure!”

“Where is he?” Master Sloan asked.

The armored warrior shifted awkwardly. He couldn’t exactly keep tabs on the kid around the clock, so he had no idea where Zane was at that exact moment.

“Master Sloan, Zane headed into the mine earlier today,” another warrior chimed in.

Master Sloan gave a slow nod. “Then we’ll wait for him here.”

Two of the Sect’s top leaders, waiting around for some freshly ascended kid?

The Soulbound Sect ran thirty-six halls, each one managing a thousand cities. Hall masters were among the rarest figures in the entire organization. For two of them to show up at a remote mine was the kind of thing that happened once in a generation.

It was shocking—and more than a little unsettling. What kind of strings could Zane possibly have pulled to get this kind of treatment?

They all remembered the orders from on high: keep an eye on Zane because of his freakishly fast adaptation. They’d had a hunch the Sect was interested, but was that one little feat really enough to bring two masters all the way out here? If the armored warriors were being honest, they were jealous as hell. Sure, they held power over the ascended warriors, but they’d all seen it before: the very people they treated like slaves often went on to achieve things they could only dream of.

Putting two and two together, the lead armored warrior stepped forward. “Master Sloan, Master Hill… since you’re here for Zane, there’s something you should know.”

“What is it?” Master Sloan asked.

“He’s already turned in his hundred Sovereign Stones,” the warrior explained. “He could’ve left the mine, but he chose to stay. He’s trying to gather another hundred to buy his freedom and become an independent warrior.”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Master Sloan remained silent, but a low, sinister laugh rumbled from Master Hill. “An independent warrior? Ha. I’m afraid that’s not his decision to make.”
