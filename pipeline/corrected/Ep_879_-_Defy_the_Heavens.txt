Ep 879 - Defy the Heavens

Word count: 1012

[SFX: LIGHTNING STRIKE]

[MUSIC: EPIC REVELATION]

The realization hit Zane like a bolt of lightning, nearly stopping his heart: he could understand the Thunder Law. He could control it.

He’d been so hyper-focused on just surviving the Celestial Tribulation that it never even occurred to him that he could use it instead of just fighting it off.

[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

A deafening crack of thunder sent him diving for cover as two lightning dragons lunged from the sky. As he dropped, the twin dragons twisted around each other, their bodies merging into a single, even more massive beast that came roaring right for him.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE LIGHTNING BURST]

With a final, explosive crack, the merger was complete, and thick bolts of lightning shot out in all directions. Some slammed into the ground and were swallowed by the earth, but others shot straight for the powerful cultivators watching from afar.

The masters had already summoned their own energy to throw up protective shields, but the stray bolts lashed out like whips, striking their defenses and making the barriers shudder violently.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ENERGY]

As Zane held his ground, he stared up at the colossal lightning dragon and began to analyze the raw power contained within it. He ticked the principles off in his head: the first layer was Annihilation, the second was Purification, and the third was Heavenly Tribulation. The very crisis that warriors faced when they tried to break through to the Life-and-Death Realm was probably named after that third layer.

[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]

[AMBIENT: ELECTRIC ENERGY HUMMING]

But this… this was something new. The raw power thrumming inside that colossal dragon was the Fourth Layer of the Thunder Law.

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

Zane went completely still, his mind racing to decipher the mysteries hidden within the blinding arcs of electricity. His Baptism by Laws gave him an edge; he could grasp the lower levels of any Law with relative ease, though the higher ones were always a struggle. Still, trying to learn a new law while it was actively trying to kill you wasn’t exactly ideal, and the lightning dragon wasn’t letting up. It charged again and again, intent on swallowing him whole.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL ENERGY FLOW]

This time, Zane didn’t fight back. He didn’t even move. He just let himself get lost in it, absorbing every principle, every flicker of energy, as the pieces of a cosmic puzzle snapped into place in his mind.

One breath. Ten. Twenty.

The lightning kept coming, kept eroding. He could smell his own flesh burning. The fragments of the Law were pouring in — first layer, second, third, fourth — and in his mind the puzzle was taking shape. One-third complete. But his body was giving out faster than the puzzle was coming together. If he stayed here and kept taking it, his body would be destroyed before he reached the fourth layer.

He hadn’t wanted to use Power Transfer for this. He’d wanted to face the first wave on his own strength. But his body had decided for him.

He exhaled. “Transfer.”

[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]

[AMBIENT: DISTANT CROWD MURMURS]

The masters watching from a distance could only sigh in defeat. They could see him standing motionless inside the lightning — and then, without warning, the Sacred Feather Sea erupted.

[SFX: MASSIVE WAVE CRASH]

[AMBIENT: ROARING OCEAN]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

A wave rose from the sea — then another, and another. The redirected force of the lightning dragon was pouring straight into the water. The waves built on each other, growing taller and taller until a tsunami hundreds of feet high was bearing down on Feather Emperor’s Island.

Three Soul Sea masters looked at each other.

“Activate a barrier — block it!”

“If we block it and it bounces back, it’ll destroy the cities on the coastline.”

“Then we disperse it.”

[SFX: ENERGY BARRIER ACTIVATION]

Two barriers went up, catching the tsunami between them, the force slamming back and forth until it finally spent itself. They had barely lowered their hands when another tsunami formed further out and came rushing in.

They exchanged a tired look and went to work again.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DETERMINATION]

Back at the center of the storm, Zane wasn’t afraid of the lightning anymore — Power Transfer had seen to that. He kept his eyes open and his mind moving. Half done. Second and third layers — complete. The fourth is something else entirely.

“The fourth layer of the Thunder Law alone — even I couldn’t survive that kind of lightning,” War King Wind said quietly, watching the blazing white column where Zane had disappeared. “And yet he’s still in there.”

Scott frowned beside him. “The Power Transfer is keeping him alive, but the dragon isn’t weakening. It doesn’t seem like it ever will.” He shook his head. “How does he get out of this?”

The other Soul Sea masters had gone quiet. Every one of them was thinking the same thing — that even now, at the peak of their strength, they couldn’t have endured a tribulation like this. And Zane was still in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. He had attracted a counterattack from the heavens that none of them could have survived. The thought sat heavily.

An existence that defied the heavens was ultimately not tolerated by the heavens.

[SFX: ENERGY DISSIPATING]

But after five minutes or so, a murmur went through the crowd. Something was wrong—or rather, something was different. The massive lightning dragon was slowly shrinking.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

“Is it finally over? But why is it fading so fast?”

“It’s definitely getting weaker. What the hell is going on?”

“This is just bizarre. It’s like something is draining the power right out of it!”

The masters were just as bewildered, staring at the impossible scene unfolding before them. But Fiona just pursed her lips, a spark of anticipation in her eyes. Zane had a habit of shattering expectations. She was worried, of course, but she had absolute faith he’d find a way through this, just like he always did.

[SFX: ENERGY DISSIPATING]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

And it was shrinking fast. What was once the size of a mountain range collapsed into the size of a small mountain in seconds. It kept shrinking, dwindling, until it simply… vanished.

[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

And there stood Zane, right where he’d been all along. 

His body was charred black from head to toe, but his eyes were impossibly bright, shining with a new, fierce light. 

Deep within them, you could almost see tiny flickers of lightning dancing.
