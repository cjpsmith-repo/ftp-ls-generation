Ep 1044 - The Hall of Arms

Word count: 1048

[MUSIC: PLAYFUL MISCHIEVOUS]

Zane’s performance was flawless—he even managed to predict the young warrior’s next words before he said them. Of course, the kid was completely stunned.

He stammered, “How did you know what I was—”

“How did I know what you were going to say?” Zane cut in. “Simple. It’s pretty easy to guess what an idiot is thinking!”

Even Yolande, the leader of The Mist Palace, couldn’t help but let out a laugh.

The hawk-nosed man was furious. He leveled a cold, sharp glare at Zane.

Meanwhile, the two young talents from the Upper World were glaring at Zane like they wanted to tear him to shreds.

It was a trick Zane had picked up from the old man of the Demi-Immortal Race, who had an infuriating knack for predicting what people were about to say.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

“Kid, real warriors fight with their fists, not their mouths,” the hawk-nosed man said, his voice dripping with ice.

“I don’t need lessons from you. I already have a master,” Zane shot back.

“A master?” The man chuckled. “I wonder what caliber of master some nobody from the Lower World could possibly find.”

A flicker of concern crossed Yolande’s face. Did Zane even have a master? As far as she knew, he had always walked his own path.

Hearing this, Zane kept a perfectly straight face. “I don’t know.”

“You don’t know your own master’s level? That’s absurd!” The man shook his head in mock pity.

Then Zane added, “But he’s someone respected and revered across the entire universe.”

A beat of silence. Then Zane added, “Of course — that’s in my mind.”

The hawk-nosed man went still. He’d been played. His composure — the composure of a World Lord — had been rattled by a few words from a kid, his train of thought hijacked without him noticing. He couldn’t reprimand Zane either. Everything Zane had said was technically respectful.

He gritted his teeth. “Let’s go. To the Hall of Arms.”

[SFX: RAPID WHOOSH]

In an instant, the man and his group were gone.

Yolande looked at Zane with new eyes. “I really underestimated you.”

Zane shrugged. “I can’t help it. I’ve met too many people like this. I’m used to it.” He paused. “I couldn’t exactly fight him. So my mouth was the only option.”

[AMBIENT: PALACE CORRIDOR]

As they walked, Yolande’s mood was noticeably lighter. She told Zane about the hawk-nosed man: his name was Harvey, and he was the Deputy Palace Master of the Mist Palace.

His background was formidable — son of a Supreme Lord. After joining the Mist Palace, he’d tried to compete with Yolande for the Palace Master position and had been working to force her out ever since. They had never gotten along.

Zane driving him off had clearly made her day.

At the top of the stairs, Yolande held out her hand. “Give it to me.”

“What?” Zane asked.

“Don’t you want to help Lavender recover her soul?”

“Now?” His face lit up with surprise.

“Not fully,” she said. “Lavender was once a half-step World Lord. Complete recovery takes time. But I can wake her up and restore half her soul.” She waited.

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL STRINGS]

Zane reached into his Storage Ring and carefully produced the Soulcradle Wood. He held it for a moment — Lavender had been asleep inside for so long. What expression would she have when she woke and found herself in a new world?

He handed it over.

Yolande took the Soulcradle Wood and gave him two things in return: a scroll and a small jade pendant. “The scroll has a map of the Mist Palace. Everyone who enters the Thirteen Palaces gets one free weapon pick from the Hall of Arms. What you can get depends on you.”

“What grade?” Zane asked. “Are there Divine Weapons?”

Yolande nearly laughed. “What do you think the Thirteen Palaces are? The top geniuses of three thousand worlds train here. Anything below a Divine Weapon in the Hall of Arms would be an embarrassment.”

Zane nodded. In the fight with Fenton, his weapon had been shattered — only in the illusory arena, so not literally, but the gap was real. He needed a proper weapon. He wasn’t letting this chance go.

While Yolande began working on Lavender’s soul, Zane opened the scroll and set off through the Mist Palace alone.

[AMBIENT: GRAND PALACE HALL]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]

The Mist Palace was one of the legendary Thirteen Palaces, a nexus for talent drawn from countless sacred places. Any one of the warriors here was skilled enough to be a core member of a tenth-grade sacred place. Even an organization like the Soulbound Sect had to pull a ton of strings just to get someone like Fenton a spot, hoping he’d find better resources and opportunities here.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ECHOING]

[AMBIENT: VAST PALACE CORRIDORS]

After wandering through the massive, winding halls of the Mist Palace for a while, Zane finally found the Hall of Arms.

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL ENTRANCE]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Two guards stood posted at the entrance, and they were on a whole other level than the ones on the stairs. These were warriors at the Spirit Supreme Realm.

Right then, a crowd of young warriors, all around Zane’s age, was gathered near the guards. The oldest couldn’t have been more than thirty. They were all huddled together, gesturing toward the Hall of Arms.

[SFX: EXCITED CHATTER]

“I heard the Hall of Arms just got a new shipment— Grade-One Divine-Class weapons.”

“Big deal. It’s not like any of us can actually get our hands on one.”

“I’m gonna try my luck!”

“Yeah, right! It costs three hundred Sovereign Stones for just one try. If you’ve got that kind of cash to burn, be my guest!”

Zane figured these warriors must be the cream of the crop, sent here from their own supreme worlds. To put it mildly, he was confused.

Three hundred Sovereign Stones just for a chance to pick a weapon from The Hall of Arms?

Zane had a little over a hundred Sovereign Stones on him—nowhere near enough. He’d never heard of such a rip-off.

[MUSIC: DISAPPOINTED PIANO]

Zane, who had been about to head right inside, stopped dead in his tracks.
