Ep 918 - The Ancestral Dragon Egg

Word count: 1063

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]

Before Zane could land another blow, a snow-white flower bloomed out of thin air beside him. 

Dorothea stepped from its petals as quietly as a whisper, a faint, knowing smile playing on her lips as she took in the scene.

“Delroy’s barrier isn't going to break that easily,” she said, her voice soft but firm. “You might as well save your strength.”

Zane eyed her. “What's inside?”

She let out a small, amused laugh. “If I tell you, will you come with me quietly? I promise you won't get hurt.”

Zane just grinned. “You'll have to tell me first.”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

“This is a sacred place,” Dorothea explained, her tone turning serious. “The Ancestral Dragon and the Evil Dragon both originated here. Now, at the end of this era, my people—the Demi-Immortals—have a duty to collect them. We have to stop the Evil Dragon, but we also have to secure the Ancestral Dragon.”

“So, the Ancestral Dragon is in this spire? Or is there a catch?” Zane's eyes narrowed, a glint of cunning in them.

Dorothea tilted her head, her smile unwavering. “Yes.”

[SFX: METAL IMPACT]

[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLE]

“Great!” In a flash, Zane pulled the Black Star pickax from his Storage Ring and swung it down hard against the shimmering barrier.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

At first, Dorothea wasn't worried. Vorn had been paranoid that Zane would go rogue, barge in, and disrupt the Ancestral Dragon egg’s incubation, but she knew that was impossible. Delroy's enchantment was simply too strong to be broken.

Besides, she could tell the woman in front of her wasn't even truly alive—just a body animated by a Soul Core, which meant it couldn't channel its full power. 

Dorothea couldn't fathom Zane's bizarre movement technique or his incredible speed, but it didn't matter. Speed was one thing; raw power was another. The barrier was protected by a unique Law of Causality. No ordinary warrior from the Spirit Supreme Realm could ever hope to scratch it.

[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

But the moment Zane pulled out the pickax, her composure shattered. The white flower in her eye began to spin wildly, its pure petals bleeding into a sickly, venomous red.

“The divine-class weapon—Black Star!” she screamed.

[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]

[SFX: MASSIVE GLASS SHATTERING]

Before the words fully left her mouth, Zane had already brought the pickax crashing down. The entire barrier didn't just crack—it disintegrated. As a single, connected entity, a break in one spot was a break everywhere. With a sound like shattering glass, the whole thing collapsed into a cloud of glittering dust.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]

Across the Secret Land, mid-battle with the Evil Dragon, Delroy stopped. His face changed.

“Something's wrong,” he said.

Vorn turned. “What is it?”

“Someone just broke my barrier.”

A beat of silence fell over the group. They'd come into the lower realms with everything going their way — a Demi-Immortal elder had used a secret technique to more than double Vorn's luck before they descended. The Evil Dragon, the Ancestral Dragon, the whole operation had been proceeding exactly as planned. This was the one thing they'd been afraid of.

The burly man dodged a claw swipe and switched positions. “Has to be that woman who escaped.”

“Impossible,” Vorn said. “She couldn't break Delroy's barrier.” He looked toward a young man in black. “Go.”

The easy smile left the young man's face. His expression went flat, and his figure dissolved into shadow, streaking toward the spire.

[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]

From outside, Dorothea watched Zane step toward the barrier's threshold — and then simply vanish. Not flee. Vanish. One moment present, the next gone, as if he'd never been there.

She understood immediately. The time flow inside the barrier was ten thousand times faster than outside. The dozen breaths she'd spent hesitating at the threshold had already translated into ten thousand breaths of time for Zane on the inside.

Dorothea gritted her teeth and stepped through.

But the gap between them was already enormous.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]

Inside, Zane felt nothing unusual. Time moved normally from where he stood. He glanced back. The rest of the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land had vanished — Time Law had swallowed the view, leaving only the immediate area around the spire visible.

Zane moved to the front door, ready to break through. Before he could raise the pickax, a faint light traced the door frame in a slow circle — and the door swung open on its own.

Behind it was darkness.

Zane stepped through. This had been his goal from the beginning — the Ancestral Dragon Egg. He'd arrived here by pure accident, chased in by circumstance. But here he was.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ATMOSPHERE]

Inside, a set of glowing steps spiraled up into the vast emptiness. Zane leaped onto them and started climbing, taking them two at a time.

[SFX: HEAVY CHAIN RATTLING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BUILD]

After a moment’s ascent, he spotted it: a colossal chain hanging in the void at the top of the stairs. As he got closer, he saw a small, luminous object tangled in its center, bound so tightly by countless links that it was almost one with the metal.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]

Zane stared, his eyes widening. “This is…”

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHISPER]

“The Ancestral Dragon egg,” a calm voice echoed in his head. Brianna had obviously seen it, too.

A faint smile touched Zane’s lips, but it vanished when he heard the doubt in her voice. “You can't take it,” she told him.

“Can't take it? What do you mean? Why not?” he shot back in his thoughts.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

“If it were that easy,” the girl pointed out gently, “the Union of Seven Kingdoms and their Emperors wouldn't have been trying and failing for so many years.”

Zane had to admit she had a point. The stellar realm wasn't overwhelmingly dangerous, but even the Soul Sea warriors from the Seven Kingdoms, who had come here countless times, had never succeeded.

“They're just chains. I think I can handle them,” Zane muttered, shaking his head, though a flicker of doubt crossed his face. 

He figured the others had failed because the chains were just too tough to break. But he had the Black Star. Smashing them shouldn't be too hard.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]

He had to at least try.
