Ep 616 - You Witch
Word count: 1100
[AMBIENT: EMPTY PALACE HALL]
With the palace now empty, it was just Zane and his sword spirit, alone.
The queenly aura that had surrounded Lavender slowly faded, and in its place, she looked just like any other young member of the Demon Night. They hadn't really spoken since she’d revealed herself in the Tower of Sin.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
After a moment of silence, Zane finally asked the question that had been bugging him. "The weapon on this statue is a spear, so why was your sword stuck on Killing Sword Mountain?"
"That's the Heavenly Fate," Lavender replied, her voice flat. "The holy spear of my people. I was a swordswoman before I turned eighteen."
And just like that, it all clicked into place. If Lavender was the queen of the Demon Night, she should have been more than powerful enough to reach the peak of that mountain. It never made sense for her to just abandon her sword halfway up. Zane had assumed that when she’d mastered the Apex of Sword Intent, she simply didn't have the power level to make it to the top. But if she'd switched her entire fighting style, that was a different story.
"But why did you become…"
Before Zane could even finish, Lavender cut him off. "You'll find out soon enough."
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY PULSE]
She walked over to the blue statue and raised her hand. A faint light glowed in her palm, revealing a strange rune before she slammed it hard against the stone.
[SFX: STONE RUMBLING]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
A low hum filled the hall as the statue erupted in brilliant blue light.
As the light dimmed, the statue's features began to shift. A cruel smile spread across its face.
Lavender's contacting another Demon Night queen, Zane realized. These scattered sacred places likely connected the Lower World to the upper realm through statues—a method for transmitting techniques and information without physical resources.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
That thought evaporated the moment he heard Lavender’s voice, dripping with unbelievable nerve. "You witch," she mocked. "You didn't really think I'd stay dead, did you?"
[SFX: ECHOING LAUGHTER]
A hollow laugh echoed from the statue's moving lips. "You little witch, you're still alive? And reduced to a sword spirit in someone else's body!"
Zane nearly choked. The blue statue was the Queen of Punishment, and Lavender was the Queen of Slaughter—brawling like street fighters. No wonder Lavender had cleared the room.
Lavender shot back, her expression gleeful. "Who cares? Killing you only takes a thought."
"Poor you," the statue mocked. "Trapped in this world, clinging to existence inside a human boy."
Lavender's fury flickered before a cold smile replaced it. "Then take a good look at the man beside me."
The statue turned to Zane. "A human War Fighter? You think—"
It stopped.
The mockery drained from its face.
"Open your eyes properly," Lavender said, her voice like ice. "He carries the Fate of the World's Battle."
[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]
The statue finally spoke, its voice tight with barely contained fury. "I didn't expect your luck to be this good. You actually found someone like him."
"Jealous?" Lavender asked.
"Yes!" The statue's composure cracked entirely. "I've always been jealous of you! Since we were children! Your luck has always been better than mine—you've always gotten everything first! That's why I destroyed you! And it won't matter that you found him. You don't have the power to turn this around!"
"We'll see about that," Lavender said.
The statue's voice turned vicious. "Do you really think I'll give you the chance?"
[SFX: WHOOSHING IMPACT]
[MUSIC: SUDDEN ACTION]
The meteor hammer swung straight at Zane's head.
He hadn't seen it coming. The moment registered half a second too late—he twisted hard, and the hammer missed by inches, cratering the stone floor where he'd been standing. He straightened up, heart hammering.
The statue wrenched the hammer free. Several of its spikes had snapped off on impact. The statue was stone, not a true weapon—powerful enough to be a threat, but not invincible.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
[SFX: SHATTERING STONE]
When it swung again, Zane drove his fist into the hammerhead and shattered it.
He said nothing. Whatever was happening between these two was old and deep and none of his business.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
"Sword."
Lavender held out her hand toward Zane. He knew exactly what she wanted—the black greatsword he'd been carrying for her. He nodded and tossed it across.
[SFX: BLADE SINGING]
She caught it, leapt, and floated above the statue's head. "You think you can kill someone who carries the Fate of the World's Battle with a lump of stone? You've always been naive."
"So what if I failed?" the statue spat back. "I won in the end, didn't I? Kill your last Divine Clone, and the Demon Night will simply choose a new Queen of Slaughter. You'll be nothing but history."
"Then try it," Lavender said.
[SFX: STONE SHATTERING]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC IMPACT]
She brought the greatsword down and split the statue's head clean in half. The pieces scattered across the floor. But even then, half a face remained, lips still moving.
"Then I'll wait for you… you little—"
[SFX: STONE CRACKING]
The sword came down again. Silence.
Lavender stabbed the blade into the ground beside her, and the fury drained out of her in an instant, leaving her looking hollowed out and miserable.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
Zane just stood there, completely floored. He had no idea what to say.
After a long, stunned silence, he finally managed, "You… you came all this way just to pick a fight with her, didn't you?"
"Yeah," she admitted quietly.
Zane wanted to say something comforting, but he didn't have a clue about the history between her and that woman in the statue.
Lavender's gaze locked onto his, her expression empty. "You'll help me, won't you?" It was the first time she had ever asked him for anything.
His gut screamed at him. This was an internal war within the Demon Night, a clan every bit as powerful as the True Dragons. Getting involved was a monumentally stupid idea. The three statues represented queens whose power was beyond anything he could even imagine.
"I don't think I'm strong enough," Zane said, shaking his head.
Even as a new War General, he was completely out of his league. He couldn't even handle Amber from the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, let alone the queen in charge of punishments or the countless other warriors from the Soul Sea Realm who could crush him with a single finger.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORCHESTRAL]
"It's true, you're not strong enough now," Lavender said, her eyes boring into his. "But you are the most important piece in this game. Eventually, you will return to your rightful place."
Zane didn't know what that meant yet, but something in her voice told him he was already committed, whether he'd agreed to it or not.
