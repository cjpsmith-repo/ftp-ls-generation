What truly infuriated them was Zane's inexplicable ability to absorb every punch without showing the slightest sign of injury. Their bewilderment turned to frustration as their understanding of combat fundamentals seemed to crumble before their eyes.
"Damn it! I'm completely gassed!" wheezed one of the attackers, pausing to gulp desperate breaths of air. His arms hung heavy at his sides, muscles trembling with fatigue. The combination of the oppressive environment and their relentless assault had drained his reserves entirely. "I can barely lift my fists anymore."
He collapsed to his knees with a dull thud, shaking his head in disbelief as sweat dripped from his brow. "This insane pressure... I'm totally wiped, and that's before trying to wail on this kid. Holy crap."
His voice carried a note of genuine awe mixed with exhaustion.
The four-thousand-pound pressure zone had been specifically engineered to challenge cultivators at the Marrow Refining Level. Under ordinary circumstances, these fighters would have navigated it with reasonable comfort.
However, attempting to subdue Zane while simultaneously battling the crushing weight of the path depleted their energy reserves at an alarming rate. Their labored breathing and trembling limbs betrayed their complete exhaustion.
[SFX: SUDDEN SHRIEK]
Just as they appeared ready to abandon their mission, the bespectacled member of their group suddenly shrieked, his voice cracking with unexpected excitement and relief. "He's bleeding!"
His keen eyes had detected dark crimson seeping from beneath Zane's skin—unmistakable evidence of injury. A triumphant smile spread across his face, revealing teeth clenched from exertion. "I was starting to think this kid was invincible," he crowed, pointing frantically at their target. "But look at him! We got him! He's wounded, he's done for! Let's finish this!"
His words sparked renewed determination in his companions' weary eyes as they gathered their remaining strength for one final assault.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DETERMINATION]
Seeing that Zane was finally bleeding was like a shot of adrenaline for the four men. They couldn't afford to let their client down. Psyching themselves up, they went back to hammering Zane with everything they had left.
Just as their assault began to wane, a wave of disappointment washed over Zane. Each blow they landed had been feeding his strength, transforming pain into power through his unique cultivation technique.
Now, watching their strikes lose intensity, he felt cheated of potential growth. The sensation of his body fortifying itself—muscles tightening, bones hardening, energy swelling—had become almost addictive.
Their premature exhaustion threatened to halt his transformation at a critical juncture, precisely when the Entrance Exam demanded his peak performance.
Zane's eyes narrowed as he calculated his options, unwilling to surrender this unexpected opportunity for advancement.
The irony wasn't lost on him—these men meant to destroy him, yet their violence had become his greatest asset.
[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL MOUNTAIN SETTING]

Thirty miles from the scene of brutality, a conversation of an entirely different nature unfolded.
"And that way," Isla Novak said with a sly smile playing across her lips, "the other instructors cannot possibly complain when I claim such a promising disciple, can they?"
"Uh..." The chief examiner's mouth hung slightly open, his brow furrowed with concern. He hadn't anticipated such brazen manipulation from Isla and found himself deeply uncomfortable with her proposition. Beads of sweat formed along his hairline as he contemplated the consequences.
The Skyreach Sect had long designated the Dakota Peaks as training grounds for its inner circle and pledges, a tradition steeped in centuries of cultivator wisdom. The majestic mountains, with their swirling mists and ancient energy flows, provided the perfect environment for honing extraordinary talents.
To foster the competitive spirit essential for advancement, the sect had established an intricate, meticulously designed set of rules. The most critical of these governed the rivalry between the Dakota Peaks—a competition that transcended mere disciples and extended to their instructors, who staked their reputations on their students' achievements.
[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
Every instructor faced rigorous quotas and ambitious goals, their status within the sect hanging in the balance. This pressure transformed the recruitment process into a fierce battleground where they fought relentlessly over the most gifted applicants. Any candidate who mastered the preliminary test and conquered the Entrance Exam, demonstrating exceptional talent and power, would find themselves at the center of a tempest as instructors scrambled desperately to claim them.
[SFX: ANGRY CROWD MURMURS]
If Isla Novak manipulated the system for Misty Peak's advantage, she would ignite a firestorm of resentment. The chief examiner envisioned the outrage—instructors storming the administrative halls, demanding justice, their faces contorted with indignation.
Isla had already antagonized numerous peak masters by bending rules to benefit Misty Peak. Her imperious attitude left a trail of resentment wherever she went. Yet given her formidable background and connections that reached into the highest echelons of power, not a single soul possessed the courage to challenge her directly. Her cold eyes promised swift retribution to any who dared.
The chief examiner, whose authority extended only to the Entrance Exam, certainly wouldn't risk becoming the target of Isla's wrath. His position, secured through decades of loyal service, felt suddenly precarious. The most he could attempt, leveraging his seniority, was to offer her a gentle admonishment. And if she dismissed his concerns, as she inevitably would with that characteristic flick of her wrist, he knew wisdom dictated retreat.

Regarding the young man enduring the savage beating, the chief examiner theorized he must possess either an extraordinary Crafting Skill or some rare physiological anomaly. Nothing else could explain his supernatural ability to withstand such punishment without visible injury. The boy's eyes, burning with determination even as blows rained down upon him, hinted at depths yet unexplored.
While the examiner marveled at such unusual talent, he reminded himself that such abilities, though uncommon, weren't unprecedented. The Skyreach Sect regularly recruited prodigies from among the Empire's elite—young geniuses whose potential defied ordinary limits. This particular candidate impressed him, certainly, but compared to the true monsters who occasionally emerged from the cultivation world—those terrifying savants whose power bent reality itself—he remained merely noteworthy.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]
The boy had undoubtedly conquered the Entrance Exam, his stoic resilience carrying him through. Yet the chief examiner's eyes darkened as he contemplated the next trial—a challenge that had broken countless promising cultivators before. Whether this unusual youth would triumph or join the ranks of forgotten aspirants remained a mystery that only time would unveil.
As for the boy taking the beating, the chief examiner figured he either had some kind of unique Crafting Skill or a bizarre physique. It was the only way to explain how he could take a beating like that without a scratch. While the examiner was surprised to see a talent like that, it wasn't entirely unheard of. The Skyreach Sect recruited its fair share of prodigies from the Empire's best and brightest. This kid was impressive, but he was nothing compared to the real monsters out there.
Sure, he'd passed the Entrance Exam, but the next trial was another story entirely. Whether he'd make it through that was anyone's guess.
