[MUSIC: TENSE COMPETITION]
Tracy barely registered the referee’s announcement through her simmering frustration. "Next up, Wynn of Skytop Peak versus the personal disciple, Patrick!"
She caught Patrick’s eye and motioned toward the stage with a sharp flick of her wrist. "Patrick, you’re up!" Her voice carried an edge of impatience, the day’s disappointments weighing heavily on her.
He responded merely with a soft, reassuring smile—the kind that suggested absolute confidence without arrogance—before striding toward the battle ring with measured steps. His calm demeanor stood in stark contrast to her agitation.
Unlike the other personal disciples, with their notorious swagger and combative attitudes, Patrick had a gentle disposition. He cultivated friendships rather than rivalries, never engaged in petty arguments, and maintained an air of tranquil mystery that made it difficult to assess the true extent of his abilities. This enigmatic quality only enhanced his reputation.
But one didn’t make it to second rank on the Skyreach Sect List without being ridiculously talented. Patrick’s skills, even though they were rarely displayed in full, were whispered about with a mixture of awe and speculation among the other disciples.
Evan, the only fighter ranked above Patrick, had sequestered himself for intensive training and hadn’t entered the tournament at all. If Evan didn’t come out of his seclusion and show up before the competition concluded, Patrick was the undisputed favorite to claim the title of most power fighter in this year’s All Peaks Competition. Tracy felt a twinge of hope watching him approach the ring—perhaps their fortunes were about to change.
Anyone who had clawed their way into the top ten on the Skyreach Sect List commanded tremendous respect. These elite fighters evolved at an astonishing rate, their skills sharpening daily through rigorous dedication. None of them ever committed the fatal error of underestimating their peers—a wisdom born from witnessing the devastating consequences of such cockiness firsthand.
[SFX: CROWD CHEERING]
When it was Patrick's turn to fight, the personal disciples finally perked up and started cheering. So far, their performance in the tournament had been a train wreck.
In past competitions, the personal disciples had always been the ones to beat—one year, they'd even swept the top twenty spots. It would be a total embarrassment if none of them could turn things around this time.
But a repeat performance was looking impossible. A shocking number of personal disciples had already been knocked out by fighters from the Thirty-Three Peaks.
The usually arrogant personal disciples were getting seriously frustrated. This tournament was nothing like the others. They were desperate for one of their own to step up and save their reputation, and all eyes landed on Patrick.
"Patrick, take him down!"
"We’re counting on you! Show them what we’re made of!"
[AMBIENT: CHEERING CROWD]
In previous tournaments, this kind of cheering would have been unthinkable; the disciples usually liked to maintain an air of detached superiority. This time, however, had shattered their complacency. Never before had they witnessed such talent emerging from the Thirty-Three Peaks, fighters whose skills rivaled—and sometimes surpassed—their own.
The unexpected defeats had humbled them, transforming their customary arrogance into desperate hope as they rallied behind Patrick, their voices betraying a newfound respect tinged with fear. The shifting dynamics of power hung palpably in the arena’s charged atmosphere, forcing even the most prideful disciples to acknowledge that their presumed dominance was no longer a sure thing.
[AMBIENT: FIGHTING ARENA]
Patrick’s opponent, Wynn, stepped into the ring, a heavy machete in his hand. He was on a winning streak and radiated confidence.
Wynn had spent three years in bitter seclusion for this. Personal disciples had knocked him down repeatedly in the last competition, and he'd been sharpening himself ever since — all for the chance to reclaim his reputation on this stage.
"Another personal disciple," he sneered, sizing Patrick up. "How many hits do you think you can take from me?"
Patrick just smiled, holding up three fingers. "It’ll only take me three moves. Two to defend, and one to attack."
The implication was clear: Patrick only needed one real attack to win. He was calling his shot. As a top fighter from Skytop Peak, Wynn wasn’t about to let that slide.
"Talk big, huh? Fine! Let’s see you defend! Try blocking this three times! Sky-Sunder Chop!"
[SFX: SWORD SLASHING AIR]
At his command, the machete blurred into a hundred gray slashes, surrounding Patrick in a storm of steel.
[SFX: METAL CLASHING]
The sharp ring of metal on metal echoed through the arena. Patrick's defense was unbreakable.
"Titan's Guard," Patrick said simply. "That's one move. I have two left."
"You’ve blocked twice," Wynn pointed out, a sharp grin on his face. "That means you’ve only got one move left, right?"
With a slight nod, his expression turning serious, Patrick said, "Yep. Only one move left."
Wynn arched an eyebrow, waving his machete at Patrick again. "Then show me how you’re gonna beat me with one move!"
It was obvious Patrick couldn’t block a third time. With only one move left, he had to choose: attack or defend.
Wynn's machete was devastating. Even though he hadn’t broken through Patrick’s defense, anyone else would have been sliced to bits by now.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
"Show me what you’ve got! Reaper’s Chop!"
[SFX: POWERFUL BLADE SWING]
The arena trembled as Wynn roared and swung his machete for the third time, the gleaming blade carving through the air with a thunderous whoosh. The Reaper’s Chop—his ultimate technique, one he had withheld from every opponent before this—unleashed an immense wave of power that rippled visibly from the weapon’s edge.
Unlike the focused precision of sword intent, which relied on finesse and control, cutter intent embodied raw, overwhelming force. Wynn’s eyes blazed with primal satisfaction as he channeled his Vitality into the strike, feeling the familiar rush of dominance course through his veins.
Like a battle-hardened conqueror claiming his territory, Wynn commanded his machete with an unstoppable, domineering aura. The crowd fell silent, collectively holding their breath as the machete descended with devastating purpose.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
Patrick’s face remained eerily calm despite the impending danger. His eyes, previously showing flickers of concern, now reflected absolute certainty. The corner of his mouth curved into the slightest smile—not of arrogance, but of profound understanding.
And that’s when Patrick made his move—his final move, the one and only attack he had been patiently setting up since the battle began. His body shifted with fluid grace, hands forming a complex pattern that seemed to bend the very fabric of reality around him.
[SFX: POWERFUL TECHNIQUE ACTIVATION]
Patrick’s voice resonated with quiet authority as he spoke it into action.
"The Eternal Cycle!"
