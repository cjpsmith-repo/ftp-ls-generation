Ep 733 - War

A strange quiet had settled over Cloud Hall. As Zane walked out of the practice room, he couldn’t shake the feeling that something was off. Fewer people were milling about than usual, but he tried to push the thought aside. It wouldn’t occur to him until much later that not a single person he’d passed had mentioned the war.
Cora, of course, was lost in her own world, oblivious to his unease. Her cheeks were still burning from what she’d said moments before, and she buried her face in his chest, unable to meet his eyes. Zane’s fingers found hers, warm and firm, and he gently led her back toward his room.
[MUSIC: ROMANTIC PIANO]
Back in the underground palace, danger had been a constant shadow. Maddox’s pursuit had been so relentless that there had been no time for this, no time to simply enjoy the quiet intimacy they’d found. But safe within the walls of Cloud Hall, there were no interruptions, no threats looming in the dark.
They spent the night lost in each other, a tangle of limbs and soft breaths. By morning, the initial fire had given way to the deep, easy comfort of sleep.
[AMBIENT: EMPTY HALLS]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
The next day, however, the unease Zane had brushed off returned as a knot of dread in his stomach. Something was terribly wrong. The halls were eerily silent, home to only a scattered few. It felt abandoned, even emptier than before.
He went looking for Finn and the others, but they were gone. He checked the meeting hall, but it was empty, too. It was well past dawn, a time when the elders should have been gathered for their daily discussions, yet the room was deserted. With every empty space he found, his confusion mounted.
[SFX: DOOR CLOSING]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
He stepped back into the corridor, pulling the door to the meeting hall closed, and nearly ran right into Toby Dillon.
Toby was one of the elders, his skin wrinkled and his hair silver with age, but his eyes were sharp, and his posture was solid. He was one of the few who had stayed behind.
Apparently, with everything going on, a handful of elders had to remain at Cloud Hall to keep an eye on the Trinity Killing Array. The Corrupted Divine Kingdom was too cunning to just sit back and wait for destruction.
They had already made the first move. Since the Corrupted Divine Kingdom hadn’t formally declared war, the World Merchants' Guild had taken it upon itself to do it for them. Joining forces with Cloud Hall, the two strongest sects in The Heartlands had declared war on the Corrupted Divine Kingdom on the very same day.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]
The Guild's move hadn't surprised anyone who understood its true strength — beneath its merchant facade, it was a hidden colossus, closer in power to a sixth-rank sect than the modest profile it kept. The Corrupted Divine Kingdom had known better than to move against it carelessly.
And just like that, the biggest war the region had seen in a thousand years had begun.
In the month since, both sides had traded wins and losses. One moment, Cloud Hall and the Guild were celebrating a victory; the next, they were reeling from a bitter defeat. The Corrupted Divine Kingdom had lost two outposts, but they were also slowly, relentlessly advancing toward the central district.
Everyone in The Heartlands knew this was the final, decisive conflict. The winner would take everything, holding the fate of the entire region in their hands. If Maddox won, the Heartlands would be swallowed by his divine kingdom. But if the allied sects could defeat the Corrupted Divine Kingdom, a new sacred power might rise from the ashes.
This was a war for the future itself.
For now, things had reached a stalemate. The two sides were watching each other, a pair of coiled snakes, each waiting for the other to flinch. Eventually, someone’s patience would break, and the final battle would begin.
[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM TENSION]
Zane and Elder Toby stared at each other awkwardly, until Zane finally spoke up. "Tia is still in Oracle City, isn't she?"
Toby didn't answer directly. He just looked at Zane, his expression earnest. "Cloud Hall needs you, Zane."
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]
A cold understanding washed over him. "I see," he said, the words feeling flat. So this was it. He had no intention of backing down, but a sting of betrayal cut through him. A war… how could Tia not tell him about something so important?
"Our leader seems to be in a foul mood lately," Toby mentioned casually.
Zane blinked, surprised by the change of subject, but he knew exactly what the old man was getting at. He gave a wry smile, unsure how to even begin explaining the situation.
"Look," Toby continued, his voice dropping conspiratorially. "It’s not as complicated as you think. You just gotta learn how to keep all the women in your life happy."
Zane just stared, stunned by the blunt advice. He knew Elder Toby would never dare say something like that if Tia were anywhere within earshot. For such an old man, he must have been a real handful back in his day. But he had a point.
Toby left him with that parting thought. Zane remained on the balcony, watching the clouds drift over the peaks of Cloud Hall. Is it really that simple? he wondered. Can I really make her happy just by… trying?
After a while, he went back to his room. Cora had just stepped out of the bath, her black hair wet and loosely tied back, a healthy glow about her. She looked up when he walked in.
"Come with me," he said.
"Where?" she asked.
"Oracle City."
"Okay." She didn't ask why. In the past, she would have interrogated him until he explained himself fully. Now she just smiled and went to get ready.
She already knew her place in his heart wasn't the highest. She'd known it since the underground palace — what had happened between them had been driven more by his sense of responsibility than anything else. He didn't love her. She understood that clearly. But she also understood that his sense of responsibility was real, and as long as she didn't push too hard, didn't ask for too much, that thread wouldn't break. She was treading carefully. She was happy doing it.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
The teleportation array was already flickering, shuttling people back and forth from the front lines. In a flash of white light, Zane and Cora appeared in Oracle City.
A long line of merchants was queued up at the array, waiting to get out of the city. Their sudden arrival—a strikingly beautiful man and woman materializing out of thin air—drew more than a few stares of open admiration.
"Zane!"
Before they could even take a step out of the array, a familiar voice called his name.
