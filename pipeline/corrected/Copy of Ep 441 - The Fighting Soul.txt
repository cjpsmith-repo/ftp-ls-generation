Ep 441 - The Fighting Soul

Word count: 1177

[MUSIC: MAGICAL DISCOVERY]

The Dragonfall Palace and Dragonblaze Hall were both run by the Dragon-blooded—organizations that were so out of Zane's league that even contemplating them felt presumptuous.

Still, gaining entry to the Dragonfall Palace would be a game-changer for him. The thought alone quickened his pulse. Access to their resources, their techniques, their masters—it could accelerate his cultivation by light-years.

Right now, nothing mattered except growing stronger. The revelation of his knack for multiple Laws had ignited something fierce within him—a hunger he hadn't felt since those early days in House Lowell cellar, desperately studying his father's Codex by lamplight. Zane was eager to study the remaining obelisks, to absorb every fragment of ancient wisdom they contained. Each Law he mastered would become another weapon in his arsenal, another step toward the power he needed.

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL OBELISKS]

After circling the forest of obelisks several times, studying the intricate carvings that adorned each monument, Zane settled before the obelisk of the Fire Law. The stone radiated a subtle warmth even from a distance, and faint crimson veins pulsed through its surface like captured embers.

He took a steadying breath, feeling the weight of what he was about to attempt. Then, without hesitation, he lowered himself to the ground beneath the ancient monument and slipped into meditation. The world around him faded as his consciousness reached toward the mysteries carved into the stone above.

Over the next three days, he worked his way through the obelisks, climbing nine in all. 

He did pass one by: an obelisk radiating a sharp sword intent laced with lightning, its Law evolved from metal and thunder combined. He recognized it immediately as the same extreme thunder sword intent Finn cultivated. He left it alone. 

Nine obelisks in three days left Elder Howard speechless and, nearby, Christopher quietly seething.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING ARENA]

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

Meanwhile, back in The Heartlands, the fight for the Dragon Soar Arena was heating up.

Every faction was staking its claim on the arena, and they weren't shy about trash-talking their rivals. They dredged up every possible justification to prove they were the rightful owners.

More groups jumped into the fray, including unaffiliated fighters—powerful cultivators who weren't tied to any faction. With apprentices and children to provide for, and some hoping to establish factions of their own, they were just as desperate to claim the arena.

[SFX: BATTLE CRY]

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

The arguments raged on, but as the debates grew more heated, everyone—factions and independent fighters alike—realized there was only one way to settle this: brute force. Only the strongest would claim the prize.

But for all their desperation, none of them had a clue what they were actually fighting for. They saw the arena as a gateway to higher realms, but in reality, it was a one-way ticket to oblivion. The only thing waiting inside was death. Any human who entered would be trapped by the Dragon for a thousand years, and with no way out, even the most powerful warriors would eventually wither away and die.

What's more, when the Cobalt Dragon had transported Zane away, it had also altered the divine texture carved into the arena. Now, even if someone managed to get past the sixth floor, the strange pattern would just spit them right back out instead of sending them higher.

If any of them had known the truth, they would have felt like fools. Going to war over a deathtrap? It was pure insanity.

[MUSIC: DIPLOMATIC TENSION]

But just as things were about to spiral out of control, the World Merchants' Guild stepped in to mediate.

The World Merchants' Guild wasn't a warrior faction, but it held immense power and influence throughout the Heartlands. More importantly, it maintained good relationships with all the major factions and had a sterling reputation for neutrality. They were the only group that could possibly step in and broker a peace.

And the proposal they put on the table was simple: move the arena to the neutral ground of Oracle City.

But there was more to it than that. The Guild proposed a full martial arts competition — open to all sects and independent fighters, held in Oracle City three months hence. 

There was one strict condition: no participant could exceed the early stage of the Soul Realm. The winner would claim the Dragon Soar Arena outright. Second and third place would receive generous rewards from the Guild itself. 

After weighing the alternative — sending their elder-level powerhouses into open war across the entire Heartlands — every major faction accepted.

[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS]

[MUSIC: UPLIFTING PROGRESSION]

Zane’s learning speed was blowing everyone away.

After another three days, Elder Howard led him to a new area.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TRAINING GROUNDS]

"The obelisks are the first trial for everyone who comes here," Elder Howard explained, his voice patient and his smile warm. "A deep understanding of the universe's fundamental laws is far more important than raw power or fighting techniques. Only those who pass this test can move on to the next phase of training: learning to cultivate your Soul Power."

"Soul Power," Zane repeated thoughtfully, nodding to himself.

Formal training for his soul? That kind of thing simply didn't exist back in the Eastern Territories. Sure, he'd strengthened his soul in the soulforge and learned the Psyche Spike to control its power, and absorbing the nether phantom's essence had given him a hell of a boost. But none of it was systematic. It was all patchwork.

He desperately needed this focused training. Even though his soul was already stronger than most people his age, he knew he was just scratching the surface. He needed to learn the proper techniques to truly master it.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS ENERGY HUM]

[SFX: CRACKLING ELECTRICITY]

When they finally arrived, Zane looked up to see a strange black sphere floating in the sky. It had to be two hundred feet across, and purple lightning crackled across its surface. As he stared, he could just make out something dark writhing within it.

"What is that?" Zane asked, a heavy spiritual pressure radiating from the sphere and pressing down on him.

Elder Howard's voice was low. 

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

"It's a prison," Elder Howard explained, his face grave. "Inside dwells the soul of a dead Owl Beast—one that's been desperately clawing for escape for years. All that time trapped within the sphere has twisted it into something far worse than it was in life. An evil spirit." He paused, letting the weight of his words settle. "And not just any evil spirit. It's become a Fighting Soul. A Nightjade Fighting Soul, to be exact."

Zane felt a chill run down his spine despite the warm air. The purple lightning crackling across the sphere's surface seemed to pulse in rhythm with something alive—something furious. "Fighting Soul? A Nightjade Fighting Soul?" 

He struggled to keep his voice steady, though confusion and unease warred within him. The spiritual pressure emanating from the prison made his chest feel tight, as if invisible hands were squeezing the air from his lungs.
