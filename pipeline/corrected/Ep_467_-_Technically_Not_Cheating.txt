Ep 467 - Technically Not Cheating

Word count:1042

[AMBIENT: OBSERVATION CHAMBER]

Just as the elders discussed Zane's remarkable performance in the pre-qualifiers, the examiner's gaze shifted to the Scrying Slate. A subtle change in the magical display caught his attention, and he straightened in his seat. "Alright. The fourth wave is starting."

The words had an immediate effect. All conversation ceased. It was time to see what Zane would do next.

Zane had shockingly shot up to first place during the third round after killing the black tortoise. But at the time, Michael and the others had been so focused on Owen—a warrior at the Consummation of the Soul Realm—that they’d completely missed the moment Zane’s sword spirit, Lily, took the creature out.

They could tell Zane’s sword spirit was different just by looking at her, but why? What made her so special?

All they could really process was that she was unbelievably beautiful. Her long, pointed ears gave her an otherworldly look, not quite human, and with her flawless features and graceful form, it was impossible to guess her origin.

Michael Nash leaned forward in his seat, eyes fixed on the Scrying Slate with keen anticipation. He was eager to witness Zane's next move, to see how the young warrior would handle the incoming fourth wave.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

Meanwhile, the onslaught from the magic array grew more vicious with each successive wave.

Not a single warrior had been defeated during the first round against the three-eyed spiders or the second against the spear-wielding ogres. The towers remained intact, their defenses holding firm.

The third wave had changed everything. The black tortoise's relentless assault had already damaged seven towers, and competitors began falling one by one, their defenses crumbling under the mounting pressure.

Zane checked the time. Sixty seconds left in the fourth wave.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: DISTANT RUMBLING]

He peered into the distance. What’s that?

[AMBIENT: WIND OVER GRASSLAND]

[SFX: WINGS FLAPPING]

After soaring through the air to eliminate the last of the crow zombies—their corrupted forms dissolving into wisps of dark energy—he surveyed the broader battlefield. The grassland conjured by the magic array stretched before him in rolling, uneven terrain. Hills rose and fell like frozen waves, creating natural barriers that obscured his view.

His breathing had steadied now, the adrenaline from the recent combat settling into focused awareness.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane squinted, spotting another warrior on a different patch of grassland, fighting his own swarm of crow zombies. It looked like the guy was in trouble. The nasty creatures were swarming him, spewing clouds of corpse poison, and he looked completely overwhelmed.

Zane glanced back at his own tower. Since he’d already cleared the area, he was confident it would be safe until the next wave.

The rules said the more crow zombies you killed, the more points you got. But did they say anything about not killing another competitor’s crow zombies?

[MUSIC: MISCHIEVOUS ORCHESTRAL]

[SFX: WHOOSH]

A mischievous grin spread across Zane's face as he shot toward the other warrior's area. The rules hadn't forbidden this approach—and if there was one thing he'd learned from his time at Cloud Hall, it was that exploiting loopholes often proved more valuable than raw strength alone.

[AMBIENT: OBSERVATION ROOM]

When the elders realized what Zane was doing, they fell silent, exchanging incredulous glances.

Michael leaned forward, his fingers drumming against the armrest as he studied the Scrying Slate. "That kid…" A mixture of disbelief and reluctant admiration colored his voice. "What is he thinking?"

The old man's mustache twitched, and his eyes crinkled with barely suppressed amusement. "Isn't it obvious?" He gestured at the slate where Zane's figure grew smaller in the distance. "He's going to steal another warrior's kills! The boy's got nerve, I'll give him that."

Michael shook his head slowly, though a faint smile tugged at the corner of his mouth. In all his years observing these trials, he'd never witnessed such audacity from a mere Adept Realm cultivator. The kid either possessed remarkable confidence or spectacular foolishness—possibly both.

[AMBIENT: TENSE EXAMINATION ROOM]

The examiner frowned. “He may have defended his tower and cleared his area quickly, but there are still rules. This is only the first stage of the pre-qualifiers. Fighting between warriors is supposed to be for the second stage, after they survive the first ten waves! He's blatantly breaking the rules we laid out!”

[MUSIC: MISCHIEVOUS STRINGS]

The old man, shaking with laughter, shot back, “I don’t see it that way. The rules don’t forbid this strategy. It never said he couldn’t.”

“Maybe not, but it’s cheating!” the examiner insisted, clearly disgusted by Zane’s actions. Warriors were supposed to protect their own towers in the first stage and only start poaching from others in the second. No one had ever dared to steal another person’s beasts for points this early in the game.

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC BATTLE]

[SFX: CROW ZOMBIE SCREECHES]

Most warriors were barely holding their own against the swarms. The idea of finishing early was unthinkable. So when Zane swooped in and started killing another warrior’s crow zombies, it made absolutely no sense to the guy. Even though he was struggling, there was no way in hell he’d let someone steal the points that were rightfully his.

[SFX: SWORD SWING]

"Get the hell away from me!" The warrior charged at Zane, swinging his blade in a wild arc that betrayed his exhaustion.

"I'm just trying to help you out," Zane said with a sly grin, his tone deliberately casual as he continued obliterating crow zombies with ruthless efficiency, a stark contrast to the flailing desperation of the warrior beside him.

The warrior's face flushed crimson, humiliation burning hotter than his fatigue."Who said I needed help? Get out of my area! Who the hell do you think you are?"

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT THEME]

Zane just laughed, the sound bright and utterly unbothered. The warrior's indignation rolled off him like water. His focus remained crystal clear: passing the pre-qualifiers. This unexpected swarm represented a windfall of points, granting him a huge advantage heading into the second stage.

Only a fool would pass up such an opportunity for the sake of another competitor's bruised ego. If it made him a new enemy, what's the worst that could happen?
