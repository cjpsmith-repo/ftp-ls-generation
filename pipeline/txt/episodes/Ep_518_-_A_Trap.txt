Ep 518 - A Trap
Word count: 1071
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC TENSION]
[AMBIENT: BACKGROUND CROWD ROAR]
The pressure bore down on them like a physical weight, threatening to crush their resolve. For Tia, Elder Imogen, and Zane, the rest of the Martial Tournament had faded into a dull roar—the cheering crowds, the clashing weapons, all of it reduced to meaningless noise. Their hearts hammered against their ribs in frantic rhythm. A high-pitched whine screamed in their ears, drowning out rational thought.
Their eyes met. Without a word spoken aloud, they understood. The three of them gave a single, tight nod, the gesture heavy with finality.
They were out of options.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[SFX: FIGHTING SOUNDS]
Down in the battle ring, Brent—one of the tournament's most celebrated fighters—toyed with some unfortunate solo warrior. His opponent had reached the middle stage of the Inner-Core Realm, a cultivation level that might have posed a genuine challenge against most competitors. Against Brent, however, a certified saint-level genius whose talent eclipsed ninety-nine percent of his generation, the man was nothing more than a plaything.
God, this is boring, Brent thought, stifling a yawn. He'd imagined testing himself against worthy opponents, pushing his limits, perhaps even discovering new depths to his abilities. Instead, he'd spent the past three rounds crushing fighters who couldn't even force him to use his full strength. The disappointment gnawed at him.
He watched his opponent struggle to regain footing, saw the determination in the man's eyes despite knowing he was outmatched. For a brief moment, Brent felt something almost like respect. This warrior had trained for years to reach the Inner-Core Realm and had probably sacrificed everything for his cultivation. And here Brent was, treating him like an inconvenience.
But sentiment wouldn't change reality. Deciding he'd prolonged the inevitable long enough, Brent released an exaggerated sigh and threw a lazy, final strike. The blow sent his opponent crashing to the mat with a resounding thud.
[SFX: BODY HITTING GROUND]
[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD CHEERING]
He gave the crumpled heap on the arena floor a dismissive glance, shrugged, and smoothed out his clothes before hopping off the platform. The fighter was hyped to draw his card, and when his fingers brushed over the carved letters, his eyes lit up. "Challenge." A wide, predatory grin split his face.
He held the card high, waving it for everyone to see, and the crowd absolutely lost it, their cheers echoing his excitement. His gaze locked onto the raised platform where the Cloud Hall members sat. This was it. The fight he’d been waiting for since the tournament started.
Zane, he thought, a thrill shooting through him as he stretched his arms. You’re mine.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BUILD-UP]
But just as the challenge was about to be issued, a single voice sliced through the stadium's roar, sharp and clear as a blade.
"Wait!"
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
The word hung in the air like a thunderclap. Everyone froze. Heads snapped toward the Cloud Hall platform, thousands of eyes searching for the source.
It was Tia.
She stood at the edge of the platform, her posture rigid, her expression unreadable. Yet her voice carried across the stunned silence with unwavering authority, each syllable deliberate and measured. "Cloud Hall withdraws from the tournament."
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMUR]
The words landed like stones dropped into still water, sending ripples of disbelief through the arena. For a heartbeat, no one moved. No one breathed.
Shock registered on faces throughout the stadium—cultivators who had traveled from distant regions, merchants who had wagered fortunes, disciples who had trained for years for this moment. All of them stared at Tia as if she had just announced the sky was falling.
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC CROWD BUZZ]
“What?”The word rippled through the crowd before it erupted into a chaotic buzz of confusion.
Brent's grin evaporated. He watched, stone-faced, as Zane rose and walked off with the rest of his clan. Fine, Brent thought, his jaw tight. Run for now. We’ll meet again.
[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]
A moment later, Zane passed a tiny, folded piece of parchment to Kieran. Kieran unfolded it, his brow furrowed in curiosity. As his eyes scanned the words, they flew wide open. His heart hammered in his chest. He blinked, rereading the note as a cold sweat beaded on his forehead.
Zane had just given him the terrible secret of the Dragon Soar Arena. A chill snaked down Kieran's spine as the truth settled in his gut: Anyone who entered the Dragon Soar Arena without true dragon's blood was not just defeated or lost.
They were imprisoned for a thousand years.
"No wonder..." Kieran mumbled, swallowing hard against a dry throat. "No wonder nobody who went in ever came back out. This is... huge."
It all started to make a horrifying kind of sense. He thought of Vincent, the disciple the Black Mountain Order sent to the upper world right after Zane returned. They had even hung his life crystal card at the Arena's entrance as proof he was inside.
The card never shattered, so he wasn't dead. But Vincent never came back. No one could explain it, only that he had to still be alive in there. Somewhere.
[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSE]
Then the World Merchants' Guild, trying to solve the mystery of their own missing people, sent an entire team in. Not a single one returned. Their life crystals also remained intact. It got so desperate that the Guild threatened to execute their families if they didn't come back by a set date.
Even with their loved ones on the line, nobody returned. It was a terrifying, unsolvable puzzle—why would warriors willingly abandon their families to stay trapped in the Dragon Soar Arena?
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
And now, Kieran realized, Zane had the answer. It wasn’t that they didn't want to come back. They couldn't. They were trapped. Imprisoned for a millennium.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT]
A fresh wave of cold sweat broke out across his skin.
And I was about to go there myself, he thought, a sick feeling churning in his stomach. He let out a shaky breath. Thank God Burns had talked him out of it.
With hands that weren't quite steady, Kieran passed the now-crumpled note to Hobbes and Burns. They read it, and their faces went pale. They looked at each other, their eyes wide with the same dawning horror.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]
"The Dragon Soar Arena," they breathed in unison, "is a giant trap."
