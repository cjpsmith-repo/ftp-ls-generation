Ep 109 - A Hidden Secret
Word Count: 1067
[AMBIENT: AWESTRUCK CROWD]
The varsity members were supposed to lead the charge on the demon-slaying task. For the pledges, this was just a training exercise. 
No one actually expected them to face a demon and kill it, but if they could just learn to handle the raw fear rolling off the evil spirit, it would be a huge step in their growth.
[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCH IMPACT]
 So it took everyone by surprise when Zane actually killed the demon… with a single punch!
Several pledges had witnessed the astonishing truth: Zane hadn't channeled even a whisper of Life Energy into that devastating punch. It was nothing but the raw, unfiltered power of his physical form.
In their world, mere muscle wasn't the currency of true might. Humans ascended beyond ordinary limitations precisely because they could harness Life Energy and Vitality, unlocking an arsenal of supernatural abilities that transformed them into something greater than flesh and bone. Yet here stood Zane, having obliterated a Nature-Level demon using only his physical strength—a feat that defied conventional understanding. 
 The shocking display left everyone frozen in disbelief, their minds struggling to process what their eyes had witnessed.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
 A cold shiver ran through the crowd as the implications settled in their consciousness. If Zane could shatter a demon with bare muscle alone, what terrifying heights might he reach when actually wielding his Life Energy? 
The question hung in the air like a storm cloud, darkening the thoughts of numerous pledges who suddenly felt the comfortable distance between themselves and Zane stretch into an unbridgeable chasm. Some felt their stomachs knot with envy, others with dread, as they contemplated facing him in future challenges.
Even Isla Novak couldn't hide the surprise in her eyes. In such a short time, Zane had become even more powerful. And it was true. Thanks to the one hundred dragon scales he’d activated, Zane’s power was now ten times what it had been. Even a powerhouse like Randall would have ended up just like that demon if he’d taken a punch at Zane’s current level.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT THEME]
 A thrill shot through Isla. This was exactly why she’d fought for him. 
By increasing his strength so much in so little time, he’d proven her right. It made the fight she'd picked with the other peak masters just to get him for Misty Peak feel completely worth it. She wondered what they would think if his power kept growing like this. 
Would they be kicking themselves then?
Though she was secretly ecstatic, Isla kept her face a mask of stone. She glared at Zane and snapped, "I told you to get close. Why did you kill it?"
Hearing her, Zane just smiled. "I accidentally got a little too close. There weren't any protective measures for a situation like that, and when the demon tried to bite me, I fought back. Sorry. Didn't mean to."
"I see. So you're saying this was my fault?" Isla frowned at his sly explanation.
Zane just gave a smiling nod, not denying it for a second.
"Since you've killed the demon, this test can't continue," Isla grunted, well aware of Zane's habit of not listening to anyone. After a moment’s thought, she addressed the pledges, "If any of you still want to attempt the test, come see me. I'll arrange for another demon. In three days, I will announce the names of the five shortlisted pledges. Don't be late."
[SFX: SKYCRUISER ENGINE STARTING]
 With that, Isla shot one last glare at Zane before striding toward the Skycruiser parked off to the side. Without another glance at Zane or the other students, she took off.
[AMBIENT: STUDENTS MURMURING]
[SFX: BONES CLATTERING]
 After she was gone, the remaining students crowded around the demon's skeleton. Zane's punch had practically vaporized its flesh and blood, splattering it everywhere. All that remained was the bare skeleton.
The value of the remains was on everyone's mind. What Chase had talked about was undoubtedly valuable. But Zane was thinking about the book. If Principles of Forging was just some basic guide, it would be worth less than a single Crystal Cube. That meant Zane had taken a huge loss, considering he’d paid over twenty thousand Crystal Cubes for it.
[SFX: PAGES FLIPPING]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS INTRIGUE]
 Zane flipped through Principles of Forging, his mind racing. Felix had wanted this book badly. Even the students from Chase's school were determined to get their hands on it. There had to be a secret hidden inside.
As he thumbed through the pages, his fingers snagged on something. Normally, all the pages in a book are the same thickness. But this one felt different, thicker than the others.
Were the two pages glued together?
[SFX: PAPER TEARING]
 He carefully worked at the edge of the page. Just as he’d thought, there was something inside. He gently tore the seam and pulled the two pages apart.
[SFX: FABRIC RUSTLING]
 Tucked between them was a thin, white piece of satin.
"Ice silk," he realized with sudden recognition. "This is crafted from winter silk." The Frost Mites, elusive creatures dwelling in the northern territories, produced a uniquely cold silk that artisans wove into exquisite satin fabric. This luxurious material, known as "winter silk," possessed remarkable qualities—refreshingly cool against the skin, wonderfully breathable, and offering unparalleled comfort. Memories flooded back to Zane of his childhood, when he had been privileged enough to wear garments fashioned from this rare fabric, the distinctive sensation permanently etched in his mind.
Delicately, he pinched the white satin between his thumb and forefinger, lifting it toward the light. A faint illustration revealed itself on the surface—crude yet unmistakable in its purpose. Though the linework appeared hastily drawn, closer examination confirmed his suspicion.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]
 "Misty Peaks... Dragon Valley," Zane whispered, his voice trailing off as excitement built within him. His brow furrowed in concentration as he studied the peculiar markings. "There's a symbol here labeled 'The Elemental Matrix,' surrounded by the numbers three, six, two, and nine," he added, his pulse quickening at the potential significance of this discovery.
[SFX: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
 It had to be a treasure map.
Chase was a legendary master weaponsmith, but only a few of his treasures had ever been found after his death. Could this be a map to where he hid the rest?
But where in the world were Misty Peaks and Dragon Valley?
