Ep 1084 - The Flame Pool

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Once Zane stepped into the inner circle of the Law Storm, he vanished from sight. But the palace masters watching from afar could still track his progress, sensing his position as he pushed deeper and deeper. It was a hell of a spectacle, and they were all holding their breath, their eyes fixed on the invisible point of energy that was him.

The masters of the palace knew the Enchanted Barrier Tower like the back of their own hands. So when one of them brought up the flame pool, a ripple of understanding went through the group. They all knew exactly what he was talking about.

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FLAMES]

The flame pool was located at the twenty-nine-thousand, eight-hundred-mile mark. It was the very spot where the Supreme Lord of Profound Flame had left behind his Immortal Profound Flame.

Ages ago, a cataclysmic war between several Supreme Lords had nearly torn the entire Orange Sea Realm apart. The raw power they’d unleashed had scoured the world, leaving it a lifeless wasteland. In the aftermath, the Alliance had seen an opportunity, turning the ruined land into a brutal training ground they called Passing the Torch. They’d made the best of a bad situation.

Over millennia, the lingering power from that war had slowly broken down, its fragments weaving themselves into the massive Law Storm that now raged within the tower.

But some remnants were too stubborn to fade.

[AMBIENT: ROARING INFERNO]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS CHOIR]

The Immortal Profound Flame was one of them—a power that could defy space and time. Even after countless years, swallowed by the Law Storm, it still burned with a fierce, eternal hunger.

It wasn’t that the flame was completely untouched. A good portion of it had disintegrated into fire Law fragments, which had then been absorbed by the storm. But when some of the palace masters had gone in to investigate, they’d found the flame pool was still incredibly dangerous.

For the masters themselves, the flame pool was a manageable hazard. But for anyone at Zane’s level—a warrior in the Life-and-Death or Soul Sea Realm—it was pure hell.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Yolande knew it. The other masters knew it. The Lord of the Sunset World knew it, too.

That was why one of them had insisted to Yolande that there was no way Zane could reach the thirty-thousand-mile mark. It was like asking a newborn to walk into a bonfire. And yet, Yolande had confidently claimed this “child” of hers could do it. The very idea was laughable.

But Yolande wasn’t laughing. She’d been to the flame pool herself — at her level, it had been nothing. But Zane’s cultivation was where it was. No matter how formidable his body, it would be difficult for him to cross. What worried her most was that he was too stubborn to stop. If he pushed in knowing he wasn’t strong enough and got himself killed in there, that would be on her. A fateless person didn’t mean someone whose fate was powerful or who couldn’t die. It just meant no one could predict what would happen to them.

[MUSIC: TENSE RISING STRINGS]

Twenty-nine thousand, six hundred miles.

Twenty-nine thousand, seven hundred miles.

Twenty-nine thousand, eight hundred miles.

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FLAMES]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

The crowd couldn’t see him anymore, but they could feel that he’d stopped.

“Told you,” one of the palace masters said. “Even if the Immortal Profound Flame is less than a hundred-thousandth of its original power, Zane still can’t get through.”

In the square, the warriors were arguing just as fiercely.

“He has to break thirty thousand!”

“You don’t know what’s in there. Someone investigated the residual power from the Supreme Lord of Profound Flame years ago. They said even a Soul Sea Realm warrior who touched it would be burned to ash. What do you think Zane is made of?”

In a corner of the square, Layla sat quietly, her knees drawn up, expression unreadable. She said nothing.

Zane knew the benefits of pushing forward. He knew the potential for growth. But when he finally stopped before the flame pool, a wave of pure instinct made him hesitate.

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

The pool stretched two hundred miles across — not a pool at all, really, but an inland sea of fire, its scale comparable to the great lakes of the Divine Kingdom Continent. Purple flames surged upward from its surface in towering columns, punching straight through the surrounding Law Storm and reaching all the way to the dome of the tower, cutting off his path entirely.

He studied it carefully. The Law woven into these flames was beyond his ability to read — fifth-level Fire Law at minimum, possibly higher. Stranger still, threaded through it was the Law of Endless Vitality fused with the Law of Life. Fire was destruction. Life was its opposite. Fusing the two should have been nearly impossible. And yet here it burned.

He could control the compressed Law Storm through the Primordial Chaos Tactic. But these flames? They were something different. He had no handle on them at all.

This was a remnant of a Supreme Lord’s power. It might have weakened over the ages, but the raw, menacing energy rolling off the purple flames was enough to make his skin crawl.

Can my body really take this? he thought. Is this where my confidence finally gets me killed?

He’d always believed his body was immune to any flame. But this… this was something else entirely. Something ancient and infinitely more dangerous.

Then another thought cut through the fear. Back on the Divine Kingdom Continent, his body had been forged to the level of a Spirit Weapon. But he’d never found a flame powerful enough to push it further. Every tempering required a stronger fire than the last. And here, right in front of him, was the most powerful flame he’d ever encountered.

This wasn’t just an obstacle. It was an opportunity. If he could survive the tempering, his body could reach a new level entirely — and with it, the amount of power his Dragon Scale Shards could bear would increase as well. He’d come out of this stronger than he’d gone in.

That thought settled something in him.

To die here, after coming so far, would be more than a failure—it would be a tragedy. And he refused to let that happen.

[SFX: CLOTHES BURNING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE RISING TENSION]

With that final thought, Zane started walking toward the edge of the pool. The closer he got, the more intense the heat became, pressing against his face in shimmering, visible waves. When he was still a hundred feet away, his clothes didn’t just smolder—they burst into flames, turning to white ash that scattered into the air. He flexed, his bare skin now exposed to the oppressive heat, and pushed forward.

“This is the most powerful flame I’ve ever encountered,” he murmured, a note of respect creeping into his voice.

He walked right up to the edge of the pool and stared down into the churning, violent flames.

[SFX: HAND PLUNGING INTO FLAMES]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CLIMAX]

Then, without another moment’s hesitation, he reached out and plunged his hand into the fire.
