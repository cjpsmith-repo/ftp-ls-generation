Ep 734 - Catching Up
Word count: 994
[AMBIENT: QUIET INDOOR SPACE]
Zane and Cora had barely stepped out of the teleportation array when a familiar voice cut through the noise of the crowd.
A handsome young man was standing there, a warm smile on his face.
Recognition hit him. “Owen?” It had been over two years, but there was no mistaking him.
[MUSIC: NOSTALGIC STRINGS]
Zane remembered him instantly—Owen, the saint-level genius from The Fleecy Downs. They’d met during the Martial Tournament, both driven by the same improbable goal: to kill Marilyn and stop Maddox’s Soul Harvesting Ritual.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
But Owen had failed. Marilyn had defeated him, and the loss had been devastating. She’d nearly destroyed him.
The man standing before him now, however, was a world away from the one she’d left broken. He radiated a new strength, having already reached the late stage of the Inner-Core Realm. From the looks of it, he was on the verge of breaking through to the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. Owen was an independent warrior with incredible talent and even better luck; opportunities just seemed to fall into his lap. He was easily on par with Brent, well on his way to becoming a powerhouse in his own right.
“You joined the World Merchants’ Guild?” Zane asked.
Owen nodded, his smile widening. “I did. And look at you—already at the middle stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.”
It was strange, Zane thought, how their paths remained so closely aligned. One was fighting to save his sister, the other to rescue the woman he loved.
Back at the tournament they'd been two young fighters nobody expected much from, circling the same impossible target. Now here they were again, older and stronger, the target still standing. There was something almost funny about it, if you were in the mood to laugh.
Owen was clearly glad to see him. “Come with me,” he said quickly. “You just got to Oracle City, so you’re probably not up to speed on what’s happening.”
Zane nodded, taking Cora’s hand as they followed Owen from the teleportation array.
Zane turned to Cora. "This is Owen — we met during the Martial Tournament, back before you and I crossed paths." Owen gave her a polite nod, clearly curious but too tactful to ask. Cora smiled back, bright and easy. Zane glanced around at the crowded array platform. "Give us a minute?"
Cora nodded and drifted a few steps away, turning to look out at the city as if she were simply taking in the view. Zane turned back to Owen. "Alright. Tell me everything."
“Are all the Cloud Hall members at the headquarters?” Zane asked, his gaze sweeping over the streets. Toby had given him the broad strokes, but he was missing the details.
Owen’s expression turned grim. “The fighters from Cloud Hall and the Guild have been integrated. Only a handful of us are left at HQ.Most have already left the city to fight.”
[AMBIENT: DISTANT BATTLE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Zane had known the war between the factions was bad, but this was worse than he could have imagined.
The merchant districts were half-empty, stalls shuttered and streets cleared. Caravans were moving out through the gates in long, slow lines. On the walls, defensive formations glowed faintly — arrays that hadn't been lit in years. Oracle City was bracing itself.
Just days ago, both sides had lost hundreds of warriors fighting over a single city — most of them War General level. But what alarmed him more was what Owen said next.
"We've also lost two Spirit-Tribulation elders."
Zane frowned. "Spirit-Tribulation? To who?"
"That's the problem," Owen said. "They were killed by a group of War Generals."
Zane stared at him. That shouldn't have been possible. A Spirit-Tribulation warrior could cut through hundreds of War Generals — and even if they couldn't win, they could always run. Speed alone made them untouchable.
"The Corrupted Divine Kingdom has a new tactic," Owen said. "They're combining hundreds of War General-level warriors into a single formation. The combined force hits like a Life-and-Death warrior concentrated on one target. Anyone at the Spirit-Tribulation level who gets caught in it unprepared — it's fatal."
Zane was quiet for a moment. "That's not something Maddox invented."
He thought of Tia out there. Finn. The others. If they didn't know about this tactic — if they'd gone in expecting a conventional battle — they'd be walking into something they weren't prepared for.
"No," Owen agreed. "It's too refined. Too complete. Our Alliance Master thinks it came from one of the Union of Seven Divine kingdoms."
A knot of anxiety tightened in Zane's chest as he thought about his friends from Cloud Hall out there in the thick of it.
"Have you seen Tia?" Zane asked.
Owen hesitated. "Not personally. Word is she's been at the front. Pushing hard." He paused. "Too hard, some people are saying."
Zane said nothing. He already knew what that meant.
[MUSIC: TENSE REVELATION]
Owen went quiet for a moment. Then: "There's something else. The Alliance Master thinks whoever passed them this tactic didn't just hand it over out of goodwill. Someone out there wants Maddox to win — and they have the resources to make it happen."
“The Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms?” Zane’s eyes widened. “You think it’s possible?”
Owen met his gaze and nodded grimly. “It’s very likely one of the divine kingdoms is backing Maddox.”
[MUSIC: DARK MEMORY]
[SFX: ETHEREAL ECHO]
The name hit Zane like a physical blow. His mind flashed back to the Celestial Tomb of Vesper—to the Holy Maiden and the man from the Divine Kingdom he’d killed. He could still hear her vow of revenge echoing in his ears. Back then, Simon had helped her break a curse, and in return, Elder Roland had promised him the internal version of the Scripture of Five Poisons.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL REALIZATION]
The pieces started clicking into place. The Chaotic Ocean of Stars was supposed to separate the Heartlands from the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms. But what if it wasn’t enough? What if they were already crossing over?
Zane looked at Owen. "We have to go to the World Merchants' Guild."
