Ep 611 - What's So Funny?
Word count: 1015
[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
The teleportation arrays scattered across the continent had one thing in common: they killed the strongest warriors who used them.
[MUSIC: TRAGIC STRINGS]
Ages ago, countless War Sages and a handful of Soul Sea Realm masters from every race had been lured into them, only to find themselves transported straight to the Cursed Land to die. They'd walked into those arrays thinking they were going somewhere safe, somewhere familiar—and ended up in the one place in the world that would kill them just for existing.
It wasn't long before an unwritten rule spread across the main continent: War Sages and Soul Sea Realm masters couldn't use teleportation arrays. Period. You never knew where one might take you, and a wrong step into the one leading to the Cursed Land meant a slow, humiliating death.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]
So when two War Sages just appeared out of thin air in the Tower of Sin, the peak War Lords who witnessed it were completely floored.
It wasn't possible. Everyone knew it wasn't possible. And yet there they stood.
The Demon Night and the ogres had even experimented with this before, trying to get their own peak War Lords to level up while still inside the Tower.
The Cursed Land had a rule: fight there, and you die. But that restriction didn't seem to apply inside the Tower of Sin, which got the other races thinking. What if a War Lord broke through to become a War Sage inside the Tower? Could they cheat the curse?
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
The results were horrifying.
At first, it seemed to work. The warriors who managed to ascend to War Sage inside the Tower weren't struck down instantly. There was no immediate retribution, no bolt from the sky.
But the reprieve was short-lived. After just ten days, the curse took hold, and the new War Sages started dying, one by one.
Maybe because they were in the Tower, the death wasn't as quick as it was for those who teleported directly into the Cursed Land.
[AMBIENT: EERIE TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: TRAGIC VIOLIN]
Instead, the warriors who'd ranked up inside the Tower died slowly. Agonizingly. Their bodies rotted away, piece by piece, leaving them to suffer unimaginable pain until the very end.
That nightmare was enough to terrify the peak War Lords of every race. The mere thought of it kept them from even attempting to level up in the Tower. Instead, whenever they felt a breakthrough coming, they made sure to get the hell out of the Tower and the Cursed Land altogether. Better to leave and never come back than to die rotting from the inside out.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]
The ogres had been on a downward spiral for a while now. Ever since Amber showed up and led the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary to dominance, the Demon Night had been pushing the ogres around for the last century—both outside the Cursed Land and within the Tower of Sin.
What had once been ogre territory, ogre dominance, ogre pride, had been slowly and methodically dismantled, seat by seat.
And even though it made them furious, there wasn't a damn thing they could do about it. Most of the time, they just had to grit their teeth and take it.
[AMBIENT: TENSE TOWER]
Among the Demon Night War Lords watching, several recognized the two Ogre War Sages. They'd known them back when they were still peak War Lords—seat controllers, ranked somewhere past forty. Back then, they were nowhere near Amber's level.
Then the Deep Shadow Cave had opened. The two of them had gone in like so many others, chasing fortune, chasing the kind of luck that could change everything. Word filtered back eventually. They hadn't made it out.
Everyone had assumed that meant they were dead. Until now.
Amber had understood immediately. So had Elder May.
The moment those two figures stepped out of the array, the pieces fell into place. This was where the ogres' confidence had come from. Not posturing. Not desperation. They'd known. They'd been waiting for this moment the entire time, playing a patient game while everyone else scrambled around them.
Not arrogance. Certainty.
It's over, Jarrod said quietly, his eyes fixed on the two War Sages. The words came out flat, like he wasn't even surprised—just confirming something he'd been dreading.
Fabian said nothing. His expression was worse than Jarrod's.
[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]
A single War Sage was enough to dominate the entire Tower. With the strongest fighters in here usually being peak War Lords, having two of them on the ogres' side was an unbeatable hand.
There was no play left to make. The balance that had held for a century—the careful, grinding dominance the Demon Night had built under Amber's shadow—had just been wiped out in an instant.
With this kind of firepower, the Tower of Sin was about to fall under ogre control once again.
[AMBIENT: WILD LAUGHTER]
The laughter started with the Ogre War Lords and rolled upward to their two new Sages. A hundred years of humiliation, pouring out all at once. It filled the tenth floor like a physical thing, reverberating off the walls, drowning out everything else.
[MUSIC: DARK CRESCENDO]
The wild laughter from the Ogre War Lords and their two new Sages grated on Zane's nerves like metal scraping stone. He watched them celebrate, their massive forms shaking with mirth. How can they be laughing right now? The question burned in his mind. These brutes had just gained enough power to tip the balance of an entire realm, and all they could do was cackle like children who'd found a new toy.
Drawing a deep breath, Zane channeled his anger, feeling it coil in his chest like a serpent preparing to strike. When he released it, his voice thundered across the chamber: "What's so funny, you idiots? It's not that big a deal to be a War Sage."
The ogres' laughter had been casual, with no real power behind it. Zane's shout, on the other hand, carried the full weight of his cultivation.
[AMBIENT: DEAD SILENCE]
A dead silence fell over the tenth floor, so absolute that the air itself seemed to hold its breath.
You could have heard a pin drop onto the ancient stone.
