Ep 1278 - Disarm

Word count: 1109

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL]
[AMBIENT: CAVERNOUS HALL WITH ECHOES]
[SFX: CHAINS RATTLING]
Damon thrashed against the hooks, a puppet caught on strings held by different people, screaming with rage.
“You guys got here before us! You had a head start, and you still didn’t use your brains!”
Zane’s voice, though quiet, cut through the cavernous hall and found him easily. “Getting through this hall isn’t about brute force,” he said, looking straight at the captured man. “You could be a master from the Soul Sea Realm, an Ace Talent with a Divine Weapon, and it wouldn’t matter. The Law of Causality in your own weapon is nothing against the rules laid down by this place.”
[SFX: METAL HOOK SCRAPING]
He held up one of the long hooks. “The key is to use the rules. I don’t know the official name for the magic in this thing, so I’m just calling it ‘Disarm.’ Once it hooks you, you lose all ability to resist. Even an Ace Talent is helpless. That’s because you’ve been disarmed.”
On the far side of the hall, hidden in the fog and darkness, Zane’s words sent a ripple of panic through the nine people waiting there.
Their group had been the first to enter, setting their trap long before Zane’s group ever arrived. They had every advantage. But in their arrogance, they’d never bothered to look closely at the hooks they were holding. It wasn’t an oversight so much as a complete dismissal.
Their team of ten included an Ace Talent, two children of Supreme Lords, and two descendants of great World Lords. The other four, besides the Ace, were all but destined to rule the chaotic battlefields of the universe. Why would a team like that give Zane’s group a second thought?
[SFX: HOOK SNAPPING TAUT]
It had been Damon’s idea to let himself get hooked. The plan was to have them pull him over, and then he would drag Larkin across with him. What they had planned next was simple, brutal, and involved a lot of torture. They only dared to try something so reckless because they were utterly confident in their own power.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
[AMBIENT: THICK SWIRLING FOG]
Now, shrouded in fog, they could only guess at what was happening to Damon.
[SFX: METAL HOOK SCRAPING]
“The guy who’s talking… he’s interesting,” Larkin said, picking up his own hook to study it. He’d never really considered the depth of the hook’s power, only that it was protected by Celestial Laws and couldn’t be broken. And that once it snagged someone, the pull was absolute.
“So, what’s the play?” another warrior asked. “Damon’s not dead yet. Are we going to try and save him?”
Larkin spread his stick-thin arms, helpless. “How? Unless he manages to lure me across, there’s nothing to be done. If he does, I’ll kill them all myself.”
The rest of the warriors fell quiet. Damon’s cries kept echoing through the fog, shrill and mocking—like he was laughing at their own arrogance. No one enjoyed being played for a fool, least of all a group this proud.
[SFX: HOOK WHOOSHING]
Coleman wore a satisfied smile as he hauled Damon in with another throw of his hook. He felt no kinship for a fellow Ogre—most of his own Illusion Points had come from clansmen he’d killed for exactly that purpose. This one simply wasn’t on his side.
“He’s mine now,” Coleman said.
[SFX: HOOK WHOOSHING]
A second hook flew in right behind his—Zane’s. But once Zane pulled Damon in close, no other hook followed.
Damon gave up on the plan entirely. Roaring, he swung both massive palms at Zane, done with waiting to lure Larkin across—he just wanted to crush the human standing in front of him.
[SFX: PALM STRIKE WIND]
The twin blows carried enough force to level a building. Zane’s expression didn’t change.
“It’s over,” he said.
[SFX: BLADE SLICING]
In the same instant, Coleman leapt in low and fast, and the Blade of Hallowed Censure swept clean across Damon’s throat.
“Everything comes back around,” Zane said, almost gently, and pressed a palm flat against Damon’s chest. Vitality surged into him, tearing his insides apart from within.
[SFX: BODY COLLAPSING]
A thin line of blood widened across Damon’s neck, and his massive body dropped face-first at Zane’s feet. As it dissolved, countless points of light streamed off it—every last one of them flowing straight to Zane.
“...That’s not actually a real saying,” Coleman muttered, unimpressed. Then, sharper: “Why do all his points belong to you? He still had over two million left in him!”
“Don’t worry,” Zane said, smiling. “They’ve still got nine people left.”
“This one wasn’t their strongest,” Janet said, watching what remained of Damon disperse.
“Agreed,” Zane said, glancing toward the dark space across the ravine. “The one running the Dark Law is stronger. And there’s probably someone else back there we haven’t seen yet—otherwise this guy wouldn’t have been so bold.”
Hailey spoke up, her voice a low hiss. “Based on how he’s talking, that guy thinks he can kill every single one of us.”
“Anyone with that kind of confidence has to be an Ace Talent,” Janet added.
[MUSIC: TENSE REALIZATION]
[AMBIENT: QUIET HALL MURMURS]
The other fighters scowled as they all came to the same, grim conclusion.
 There were over a hundred warriors in the entire universe who could be called Ace Talents. With fifty supreme worlds in the Illusion Battlefield, that averaged out to about three per world—though some had more, and some had none at all. Janet had started this trial with two Ace Talents on her side. Now, it seemed she was up against a third.
“So how do we fight an Ace Talent?” Coleman asked quietly. He was strong, but he knew he was out of his league.
[MUSIC: CONFIDENT STRATEGIC]
Sensing the shift in the mood from across the hall, Zane smiled faintly. “Even if they have an Ace Talent over there, we can still take them down. But they’ve probably figured out the rules by now, so I doubt they’ll let us hook them again. How about we let them hook me instead?”
“What?” Coleman said, completely stunned.
Everyone knew how powerful the hooks were. The ‘Disarm’ effect neutralized almost any known power or martial art. The only beings who might stand a chance against the Celestial Laws were the Supreme Lords themselves. Even a World Lord would be helpless, trussed up before he even knew what was happening. Zane knew all of this. Why would he suggest something so insane?
Zane just laughed at the man’s dumbfounded expression. “I’m kidding,” he said. “I’m not that stupid.”
