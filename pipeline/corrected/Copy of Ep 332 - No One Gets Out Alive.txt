sEp 332 - No One Gets Out Alive

[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
A crimson blur streaked across the manor grounds, with Owen Lowell desperately racing behind it. His heart pounded in his chest as he responded to Bryson’s agonized scream, his brotherly instinct driving him forward.
When he took in the scene, his mind reeled as he struggled to process everything. This can’t be happening. How the hell could Zane beat Bryson? The shock of Bryson’s defeat, however, paled in comparison to the horror that followed. When the Blood Prisoner casually referred to Bryson as his "disciple," Owen felt as though the ground had disappeared beneath his feet.
Bryson, a disciple of that... thing? Owen’s stomach churned with revulsion. He had known that Bryson kept the Blood Prisoner imprisoned in the dank recesses of their cellar—a dangerous asset held in reserve specifically to counter Zane’s growing threat. But Owen had been vehemently opposed to this arrangement from the beginning, his moral compass rebelling against such a grotesque alliance.
The Blood Prisoner wasn’t just dangerous; he was a monster, that sustained his unholy powers by eating human flesh and drinking blood. To Owen, collaborating with such an entity was the same as making a deal with the devil. Anyone who got too close was just his next meal. For Bryson to take that kind of risk... Owen knew his brother must have been truly desperate.
The realization sent a chill down Owen’s spine as he thought about what fears must have driven his brother to such extremes. Yet never in his darkest imaginings had Owen suspected that Bryson had gone this far, actually becoming the beast’s disciple and embracing those vile, forbidden techniques.
Shoving his confusion aside, Owen focused on the immediate crisis. His boots pounded against the earth as he maintained pursuit of the crimson figure, his jaw set with grim determination. The moral issues and family betrayals would have to wait. First things first: deal with the immediate threat. They needed to survive the nightmare that had been unleashed upon House Lowell.
[SFX: VIOLENT IMPACT]
Zane had hit Bryson with a single, open palm. The force catapulted Bryson clean through the wall of a nearby house, leaving nothing but splintered wood and crumbling stone in his wake. Without hesitation, Zane strode through the debris toward his fallen uncle. Even though he’d only used half his true strength, Bryson hadn’t even registered the attack quickly enough to dodge it.
[SFX: DEBRIS CRUMBLING]
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED MURMURS]
Moments later, the transfixed members of House Lowell watched as Zane emerged from the ruined structure, dragging a limp figure by the collar.
The man’s face was covered in blood, his features barely recognizable, but they still knew it was him—Bryson Lowell, the usurper, now reduced to a broken heap with a single, almost casual, blow.
Zane looked at them, his voice carrying across the stunned courtyard as he displayed his vanquished uncle. "This is the price of treachery."
A complex wave of emotions swept through the members of House Lowell.
The silence that followed was a collective realization that they had witnessed something extraordinary. They had all clearly underestimated Zane’s strength, mistaking his years of quiet suffering for weakness rather than the forging of something unimaginable.
[SFX: HEAVY LANDING IMPACT]
Just then, the blood-red figure landed in their midst. A collective gasp went through the crowd as recognition dawned, and the color drained from every face in House Lowell.
His entire body was the color of blood, skinless, nothing but raw flesh and exposed muscle. An overpowering stench of gore, like a slaughterhouse from hell, rolled off him in waves.
They recognized him instantly. The official wanted posters had described the notorious villain in detail. Panicked whispers broke out among the members of House Lowell.
[AMBIENT: PANICKED WHISPERS]
[MUSIC: RISING HORROR]
"The Blood Prisoner!" A young man’s voice cracked with terror as he hissed the words. "He’s been hiding inside our manor this whole time!"
Another whispered, his face blanching to the color of parchment.
"Bryson called him master. Could our own leader truly have made a deal with that thing?" Horror dripped from every syllable as his trembling fingers clutched at his collar.
A third man stepped forward, his eyes widening with sudden comprehension. "That explains everything. I noticed Bryson had a strong scent of blood on him. He must have learned the Blood Evil Force from the Prisoner!" His face contorted with disgust. "That explains why our people have been disappearing! Bryson was using them for his training!"
Bryson gasped, his voice breaking into a mad laugh even as he struggled for breath. Blood bubbled between his lips as he fixed Zane with hate-filled eyes. "He’ll eat you alive," he wheezed, madness in his gaze. "No one escapes the Blood Prisoner’s hunger. No one!"
[SFX: SLITHERING CRIMSON ENERGY]
"Is that so?" Zane replied calmly, ignoring the crimson threads of energy that were beginning to snake from the Blood Prisoner's hands. He kept his eyes locked on Bryson. "I think I’ll let you live. Just so you can watch how pathetic your new master really is."
[SFX: BODY THROWN]
With that, Zane tossed Bryson aside like a sack of trash and walked right into the path of the oncoming blood strings.
The Blood Prisoner had planned to finish Zane in a heartbeat. But Zane’s arrogance made his eyes go cold. "An Adept-level whelp dares to act so smug before me?" he hissed. "They say you’re some kind of prodigy. Let’s hope you don’t live to regret that pride."
"Regret what?" Zane asked, shaking his head.
[SFX: MAGICAL TENDRILS]
He made no move to dodge the blood strings. Instead, he simply held out a hand, letting the crimson tendrils coil around his wrist like snakes.
[AMBIENT: CROWD GASPS]
A ripple of shock and disbelief went through the crowd from House Lowell.
They'd expected Zane to beat Bryson—after all, he was with the Skyreach Sect now. But the Blood Prisoner? That was a whole different league. Even if Zane was the strongest in the Sect, the Blood Prisoner was a Soul Realm master.
Even the lowest-level Soul was practically a god in a place like Castermere County. An Adept could run this county; what did that make a Soul Realm master?
So why was Zane just letting the blood strings wrap around his hand? Was he not afraid of being captured?
They couldn’t believe what they were seeing. Why the hell wasn’t he fighting back?
