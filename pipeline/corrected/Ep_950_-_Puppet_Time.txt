Ep 950 - Puppet Time

Word count: 1150

[AMBIENT: EERIE WORKSHOP]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

It was unnerving how lifelike the eight puppets were, each one a perfect replica of a member of their group.

Zane sized up the line of puppets until his gaze landed on his own double. His face fell. “Why is my puppet so much weaker than all of yours?”

They were made from some pretty complex materials. The puppets for the seven Demi-Immortal masters were each as strong as a Life-and-Death Realm warrior, but Zane’s was only on par with a Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior.

The young man in black snorted. “Sounds about right, doesn't it? You're still way weaker than the rest of us.”

Zane just rolled his eyes. He wasn't about to waste his breath arguing with the jerk.

“Alright, what's done is done,” Vorn cut in. “Let's just get these things running so we can finally get into the Cursed Land.”

Everyone nodded. They'd been waiting for days and were itching to get into the Tower of Sin. 

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY PULSE]

One by one, they each channeled a piece of their soul into their puppet. 

[SFX: MECHANICAL ACTIVATION]

As they did, the puppets’ eyes snapped open, blinking to life.

[MUSIC: TENSE PIANO]

But Zane was stuck. He had no clue how to split his soul, which meant he couldn't bring his puppet to life.

His soul was on the verge of the Nightjade Fighting Soul Realm, but he wasn't quite there yet. He’d never learned the soul-splitting technique; all he could do was change the shape of his soul.

Splitting your soul meant peeling off a fragment to create a totally new, independent consciousness.

Lavender's soul, for example, was just a fragment. Her body and her main soul had been destroyed, and that single piece was all that was left.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Zane was completely lost. The young man in black shot him a mocking smile, clearly enjoying his struggle. Thankfully, Dorothea stepped in. “Zane, let me help you,” she said softly. “First, try to push a part of your soul out of your body.”

Zane did what she said, focusing until a sliver of his soul emerged from between his eyebrows. 

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHIMES]

The flower in Dorothea's right eye began to spin, and an invisible force pulsed outward. Zane felt a gentle pull, and a thin wisp of his soul separated from the whole.

Now Zane could control the separated piece. He guided it into his puppet, and its eyes flickered open.

The eight of them sat cross-legged on the floor as their puppets stepped onto The Compass Wheel. 

[AMBIENT: CURSED LAND]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS RUSH]

The Compass Wheel barely slowed as it crossed into the Cursed Land. The ghost curse swarmed the puppet bodies — and did nothing. They were immune. The wheel cut straight through without stopping.

Then the Tower of Sin came into view.

“As expected,” Vorn's puppet said. “It really is the Tower.”

The young man in black glanced up at it. “It hasn't unfolded yet.”

Zane looked at him. “Unfolded? What does that mean?”

Vorn just smiled. “You'll see.”

[SFX: COMPASS WHEEL LANDING]

[AMBIENT: TOWER ENTRANCE]

Wade brought the Compass Wheel down at the Tower's entrance. The two giants guarding the doors took one look at the Demi-Immortal puppets and pulled them open without a word. Life-and-Death Realm constructs, controlled by Spirit Supreme masters — no one in the Tower was going to stop them.

[AMBIENT: CROWDED TOWER FLOOR]

[SFX: CURIOUS MURMUR]

The moment they entered, the stares began. Races from across the main continent crowded the lower floors, and not one of them had ever seen anything like the puppets. They couldn't feel a trace of life from them.

“What are these things? They don't look human.”

“Wait — that one looks like Zane Lowell.”

“Isn't he dead? And — is that thing made of mica gold?”

The puppets ignored them and kept moving.

[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS ORCHESTRAL]

Floor by floor they climbed. Zane took in the familiar world of the Tower in silence. He hadn't expected to come back. He certainly hadn't expected to come back like this — a soul fragment in a puppet, riding up floors he'd once fought his way through.

The young man in black fell into step beside him. “Ready to have your lie exposed?”

“Ready to swallow your weapon?” Zane replied.

The young man's puppet face was unreadable, but something shifted behind his eyes. Zane looked too calm. Too certain.

[AMBIENT: TENTH FLOOR]

[MUSIC: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

The tenth floor was as fierce as ever. A crowd had gathered around the Talent Tablet — someone was being tested. The noise dropped as the puppets entered.

Vorn took a few steps onto the floor and stopped.

He didn't need to push through the crowd. Zane's name sat alone at the very top of the first row, visible from anywhere on the floor. He stared at it.

Then Delroy stopped. Then Wade. Then the young man in black — whose puppet's mouth fell slowly, completely open.

Dorothea's eyes lit up. She laughed softly. “I knew it. My premonition was right.”

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL CRESCENDO]

“A Golden Celestial Paragon,” Vorn breathed, his voice filled with awe. “It's really... that rank. No one's earned it since Aliza became a Supreme Lord four hundred thousand years ago.”

Four hundred thousand years might sound like a long time, but it was just a blink of an eye considering how rarely a Golden Celestial Paragon appeared.

To put that in perspective, a single era lasts three-point-six billion years. In all of known history, only six warriors had ever earned that rank. The one before Aliza had come tens, maybe even hundreds of millions of years prior. The time span was just staggering.

The locals watching didn't fully grasp what they were seeing. They knew Amber held the second row — the greatest genius in the history of the main continent. Zane was two full grades above her, sitting alone at the top. That much was clear. What they couldn't know was what it meant to the Demi-Immortals standing beside them — warriors who understood exactly how impossible that title was.

A smug grin spread across Dorothea's face. She was damn glad she'd never tried to mess with Zane. Betting on him had been the right call. She giggled and turned to the young man in black. “Didn't someone make a bet with me?”

[MUSIC: RISING TENSIONS]

The young man in black went pale. He was a serious guy, and while his puppet couldn't blush, his real body back in the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary was probably burning red with humiliation. He obviously wasn't going to swallow the Black Nether Saber, but now he was scrambling for something to say, anything to save face.

Vorn couldn't have cared less about their stupid bet. Something far more important had just occurred to him.
