Ep 137 - The Battle Of Life Force

The warrior’s face twisted in pain. He tried to channel his Life Force and shatter the chains, but it wouldn't gather. Bound tight to the Titan’s Pillar, he plummeted toward the ground.
This was one of the Pillar’s deadliest abilities. Anyone bound to it was completely cut off from their Life Force, which was locked away so tightly that not even the strongest warriors of the Soul Realm could break its hold. They were forced to rely on pure physical strength, fighting like ordinary men.
Two other warriors were snagged by the Titan’s Pillar, falling one by one toward the earth. Only Axel, with his incredible brute strength, was powerful enough to shatter the Pillar itself. Two other warriors from the Soul Realm had been just quick enough to dodge the attacks with their unique, swift movements.
[SFX: CHAINS BREAKING]
"The Pillar's not our only problem—the Dreadlord Orion himself is a monster!" Axel shouted. "It's just the three of us now! We have to give this everything we've got to stop him!"
Hefting his massive hammers, he charged straight for Orion. The other two warriors, both skilled in hand-to-hand combat, flanked him, and together, they managed to hold back Orion’s relentless assault.
[AMBIENT: SWIRLING HELLFIRE]
While the three men fought desperately against the Netherlord, Zane kept absorbing the Hellfire. He pulled it in faster and faster, until nearly half of it was swirling inside his body.
The surrounding black mist began to thin, revealing glimpses of Aldrich and Yolande. 
What they saw wasn't encouraging. Yolande's face had gone pale. Her Vitality was still flowing — steady, colorless, invisible to most — but Zane could feel it thinning. A Dreadlord's demonic energy dwarfed anything a human could sustain. She was going to run out first.
The Commander saw it too. He blocked a blow from Orion and shouted across the battlefield.
"Zane! Yolande loses, Silverspire falls. Can you help her?"
Zane didn't move. He lay on the barrier and looked up at the two figures locked together in the fading mist. He was still angry — she'd trapped him, dragged him into this, treated him like a criminal. Part of him felt she'd earned whatever happened next.
Then he thought about what happened after. If she lost, the Dreadlords would finish the city. And then they'd finish him. He wouldn't even register as an inconvenience.
In the swirling fog, Aldrich’s face was terrifying. The Hellfire was his greatest weapon; as long as he had it, he was unbeatable.
[SFX: IGNITING FLAMES]
At Axel's command, one of the warriors summoned his Life Force, igniting flames beneath his feet to boost his speed as he prepared to rush Yolande. Every warrior from the Soul Realm knew this was a critical moment. Yolande and Aldrich were locked in a contest of Life Force,, leaving them both vulnerable. Neither could spare an ounce of power to defend against another attack; the slightest distraction could cause them serious injury.
The warrior chosen for the job was known for his incredible speed—it was why Axel had picked him. He could move faster than a bullet and had used that same speed just moments ago to narrowly dodge the Titan's Pillar.
But just as he was about to make his move, Orion moved even faster, cutting him off before he could get anywhere near Yolande Shaw.
Orion had tried to stop Zane from absorbing the Hellfire, knowing how crucial it was to Aldrich. But with that battle already lost, he glanced around and reassessed. He saw Aldrich locked in a battle of Life Force with the young human girl and was certain she was doomed. Seeing the Soul Realm warriors change their plan from fighting him to saving the girl, Orion knew he had to stop them at all costs.
With everyone so focused on the fight, they had all forgotten one person: Zane, still lying on the Light Barrier.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
Lying there silently, Zane felt a scorching heat spread through his body, as intense as the molten lava he had once endured in the trials. The sensation crawled beneath his skin like liquid fire, threatening to consume him from within.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
Golden swirls danced across his flesh, absorbing the last remnants of Hellfire, methodically converting it into a raw power that reforged his body from head to toe. Each muscle fiber, each bone, each vessel seemed to unravel and reconstruct itself anew.
[SFX: PAINFUL GROAN]
"Ugh, I never want to feel that again!" Zane screamed in his head, his teeth clenched against the agony that mercifully began to subside. The pain had been unlike anything he'd experienced before—worse even than when he'd first discovered his unique abilities.
He sat up cautiously on the Light Barrier, his movements tentative as he shook his arms to loosen his stiff muscles. The battlefield chaos continued around him, yet for this brief moment, his attention turned inward. That's when he felt something strange—an unfamiliar warmth pulsing rhythmically on his chest.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
"Whoa, what's this?" he whispered, fingers tracing the intricate pattern etched into his skin. His eyes widened with realization. "There's a new Spirit Rune on my chest!"
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT DISCOVERY]
Zane was thrilled. The last time he absorbed Corefire, a Spirit Rune had appeared on his chest, marking him as a Psionic Weapon. Now he had two! The first Rune had made him the equivalent of a common enchanted weapon. A second one meant he’d reached a whole new tier. A smile spread across his face.
But he was also confused. From experience, he knew absorbing fire strengthened his body. How did absorbing that black fog do the same thing? He didn't understand that the black mist *was* Hellfire—a special kind that very few people even knew existed.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMERING]
Besides the new Rune, Zane noticed something else: two streaks of unique Glyphs had appeared on his body.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REALIZATION]
He knew what Axel said was true. If Yolande Shaw lost this Soul Duel with Aldrich, the human cultivators would be critically weakened. Silverspire would fall into chaos, overrun by demons. And Zane himself could be crushed by a Netherlord with a flick of a finger.
[SFX: STRUGGLING TO STAND]
With that thought, Zane reluctantly climbed to his feet on the Light Barrier. His slow, careful movements brought a flicker of a smile to Axel and the other cultivators, but their hope quickly soured into worry.
After all, this was Aldrich, a legendary Netherlord whose mere presence struck terror into the hearts of seasoned warriors. Zane stood merely as a youth, his Half-Nature Level cultivation laughably insignificant against such overwhelming might. In typical Life Force confrontations, even a moment's lapse in concentration could prove fatal. Yet the chasm of power separating a Netherlord from Zane was so vast that Aldrich might crush him without sparing a single thought for the effort.
Nevertheless, desperate circumstances demanded desperate measures. In this darkest hour, with all other hopes extinguished, Zane remained their sole, fragile chance at survival.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: HEROIC CONFRONTATION]
The world itself seemed to hold its breath as Zane mounted the Wall of Light and advanced toward the towering figure of Aldrich—each step carrying the weight of Silverspire's fate.
