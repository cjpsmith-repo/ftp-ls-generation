Ep 834 - Who Dies First

Word count: 1053

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

[SFX: IMPACT CRASH]

The other emperors were moving to stop War King York, but before any of them could act, Zane’s counterattack sent him flying, a spray of blood erupting from his lips.

Everyone knew he was stronger than your average warrior, but no one had a clue he was this strong. The power behind that punch was just unreal.

[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]

The gap between a Spirit-Tribulation warrior and a Soul Sea master was supposed to be a chasm, a difference so vast it was almost laughable. And yet, a kid from the lower realm had just drawn blood from one of the most powerful men alive. Even the ancient masters, men who had lived for tens of thousands of years, had never seen or heard of anything like it.

But the other Soul Sea masters watching knew the truth. York had been careless. He’d completely underestimated his opponent, assuming a kid in the middle stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm wasn't worth his full attention. He hadn't bothered to shield himself with his Vitality or even activate his domain.

If York had used his domain from the start, Zane wouldn't have stood a chance. That was his fatal mistake, and it was born of pure pride. A warrior as mighty as him, using his domain to fight a kid? If word got out, he'd be a laughingstock. It would look weak, pathetic even.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

War King York landed hard, skidding several hundred feet away. His eyes burned with a murderous glare. If looks could kill, Zane would have died a thousand times over. That single punch had been strong enough to make him bleed, and as he wiped the blood from the corner of his mouth, a hot flush of humiliation burned through him. Everyone had seen it—the other emperors, the saint-level geniuses, and even the princes of his own York Divine Kingdom.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSIONS]

“For someone still in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm,” York said, his voice dangerously low, the murderous look on his face sharpening into a fierce fighting spirit, “you've earned the right to be taken seriously.”

He was a veteran forged in countless battles, his spirit honed long before he'd reached the Soul Sea Realm. He didn't belittle Zane anymore. What had just happened was a fact — and York was warrior enough to acknowledge it.

“Fighting a warrior of the Soul Sea Realm is a rare experience,” York said, his voice dropping to something almost calm. “Let me show you my domain.”

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY]

With York at the center, tiny white threads began to drift outward in every direction, slowly at first, then faster, weaving through the air and coiling around each other into small, dense cocoons.

“Cloud Silk Domain.”

Zane's expression darkened. He had merged with the will of the world — but that didn't make him invincible. A Soul Sea domain was different from a physical strike. It operated on a higher level entirely, imposing its own laws over everything it touched. If it fully enveloped him, it could sever his connection to the world's will completely.

He was in real danger.

[SFX: SUDDEN FOOTSTEP]

[MUSIC: HEROIC INTERVENTION]

But before the threads could close around him, a figure stepped forward and stood between Zane and the War King.

It was the Harlen Emperor.

“York,” he said quietly, “why kill a junior?”

[SFX: MURMURING CROWD]

War King Wind and Scott both rose from their seats.

“Zane did nothing wrong,” Wind said. “You have no cause to kill him.”

Scott leaned forward. “You told us for years that once the Little Monster broke through, its talent would help us advance beyond the Soul Sea Realm. The Little Monster is gone — but Zane has inherited that same ability. Doesn't that mean our hopes now rest with him?”

A quiet ripple of agreement moved through the other Soul Sea masters. They nodded. The Little Monster was finished, but if Zane truly carried that power, he might be more reliable than the creature ever was.

[AMBIENT: ANXIOUS CROWD MURMURS]

Whispers rippled through the crowd of warriors.

“Is this for real? Are two Soul Sea masters actually going to fight?”

“I've never seen a battle between them in my entire life. If they go all out, won't they tear the world apart?”

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

“You idiot! If the Harlen Divine Kingdom fights the York Divine Kingdom, it could start a war. We'd all end up as cannon fodder!”

The Spirit-Tribulation warriors watched, terrified, wondering if they should start running before they got caught in the crossfire.

York's face was black. He looked at the Harlen Emperor standing in his path and said coldly, “Those who stand in my way — die.”

That was too far. The Harlen Emperor had spent the entire day tolerating York's outbursts, playing the role of the patient elder. But this was his threshold.

A sharp light entered his eyes. The easy manner dropped away entirely.

“Die?” he said. “York — let's find out who dies first.”

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY SURGE]

An old but immovable aura rolled off him, vast and unhurried. In terms of cultivation, they were equals — both pinned at the peak of the Soul Sea Realm by the limits of the lower world. But in terms of raw experience, the Harlen Emperor had lived more than five thousand years longer than York. And as long as he hadn't begun to decay, York knew he couldn't win.

[MUSIC: POWERFUL DRAMATIC STRINGS]

With a single look, the Harlen Emperor wiped away every trace of York's aggression. Faced with that immense power and unshakable stance, York knew better than to make a move.

The Harlen Emperor's gaze swept coldly over York. “In that case,” he announced, his voice ringing with finality, “the warrior from our Harlen Divine Kingdom has won this battle.”

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ECHOING]

York shot one last, venomous glare at Zane, clearly furious with how it all ended. With a sharp, disgusted snort, he turned and stalked back up the stairs.

[MUSIC: GENTLE HOPEFUL MELODY]

[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL HALL]

War King Wind and Scott both gave Zane a final, lingering look. Before turning to leave, Wind even offered him a small, kind smile that seemed to hold a world of meaning.
