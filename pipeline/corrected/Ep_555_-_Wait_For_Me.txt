Ep 555 - Wait For Me
Word count: 1161
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TOMB]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Zane had expected to find Vesper's body in the coffin. He was surprised to find it packed with treasures instead—rewards for anyone who passed the Wheel of Fortune.
The whole setup struck Zane as weird.
Vesper had built this celestial tomb to lure fighters from The Heartlands, so he had to know they'd essentially be grave robbers. Zane was one of them, after all.
He just couldn't wrap his head around Vesper's logic. Instead of just killing the people who came to steal his stuff, he used the Wheel of Fortune to test their luck and then reward the winners.
"Why reward us?" Zane asked, his tone skeptical. "We came here to rob your tomb."
[SFX: ETHEREAL WHISPER]
Vesper snorted. "My specialty is karma," he explained. "You reap what you sow. I lured you all here, and some of you died in the Wheel of Fortune because of me. It's only fair I offer some compensation."
But Zane didn't buy it. If Vesper really specialized in karma, shouldn't their reward be death, since they'd all come to rob his tomb with bad intentions? Zane just shrugged. It was Vesper's call, and he wasn't about to argue. Besides, treasure was waiting.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRONE]
He walked over to the coffin and saw a set of jade-colored bones resting inside. He looked at them, then glanced back at the crystal ball where Vesper's spirit resided.
"Those aren't mine," Vesper clarified quickly. "They're the bones of a Legendary Beast."
Zane nodded. That explained it. This celestial tomb wasn't actually Vesper's. No matter how into karma you were, nobody just gave away their own bones.
[SFX: MYSTICAL RESONANCE]
So, without a second thought, Zane reached in and claimed the legendary beast's bones as his reward.
Getting his hands on an immortal's bones had been his whole reason for coming to the celestial tomb. He needed them to figure out the key to the Pathforged Realm. But since Vesper's bones were off the table, maybe a legendary beast's would do the trick.
After glancing over the other treasures, Zane turned back to Vesper. "So how do I get these to the other fighters?"
Vesper pointed to his left. "There's a passageway over there," he said. "You can choose their rewards as they finish the trial. Someone's about to pass it right now, so you'd better hurry!"
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY HUM]
[SFX: SHIMMERING LIGHT]
Zane hurried to the wall behind the coffin, where a shimmering light curtain functioned like a Scrying Slate, displaying the Wheel of Fortune's interior. Beside it sat a teleportation array for sending out treasures.
The scene surprised him. Tia, previously in second place, remained stuck while Maddox and the Holy Maiden had caught up. Maddox neared the finish line.
Zane glanced at the coffin's contents—all valuable. He pocketed a Relic-Class short sword from the top, then rummaged until he found a battered, low-grade Mythic weapon and tossed it onto the array.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
The weapon disappeared.
In the corridor beyond, Maddox emerged from the Wheel of Fortune, his expression smug. Zane finishing first merely proved superior luck—nothing more. His own fortune remained unmatched in the Heartlands.
He rounded the corner and froze. A weapon rested on the teleportation array.
"A reward?" His eyes brightened, then narrowed as he examined it.
A low-grade Mythic weapon. A cheap short sword that practically embarrassed itself.
Maddox's face cycled through disbelief, confusion, and barely suppressed outrage. An immortal's tomb offering such garbage? He turned it over repeatedly, searching for hidden qualities.
Nothing.
He pocketed it with wounded dignity and departed.
Through the curtain of light, Zane sneered. "Robbing a tomb and expecting a reward? A Mythic weapon is generous."
From the crystal ball, Vesper observed, caught between exasperation and quiet amusement. The young man he'd awaited two thousand years was distributing junk prizes. But Zane was his chosen one—he'd allow it.
[MUSIC: BUILDING ANTICIPATION]
The third fighter to complete the Wheel of Fortune was Tia.
Zane turned back to the coffin. His hands moved quickly, pulling out treasure after treasure and stacking them onto the teleportation array. He sent down nearly half the coffin's contents.
[SFX: CASCADING TREASURE]
Tia turned the corner in the corridor and stopped dead.
In front of her was a mountain of treasure.
"So many…?" she whispered.
Then she saw them. Four Flowers of Life and Death, their petals still faintly glowing. She'd come to this tomb for exactly that — the Flowers had been her goal from the start.
She didn't hesitate. She swept everything into her ring and walked on, her expression somewhere between stunned and satisfied.
Zane watched her through the curtain of light, and something quiet settled over him.
He didn't know how long this journey to the Tower of Sin would take. Three years? Five? The uncertainty gnawed at him. Elara was still out there, still in Maddox's hands. And no matter how urgently he needed to grow stronger, every year was a year she was waiting.
The Holy Maiden was the fourth one out. Zane decided to have a little fun. He pulled a cheap Mythic-rank weapon from his Storage Ring—a ridiculously huge hammer—and tossed it onto the teleportation array.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
[SFX: HEAVY METAL THUD]
It materialized in the passageway right in front of her.
[AMBIENT: STONE PASSAGEWAY]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
The Holy Maiden's face was still a sour mask, probably from the prince's death. As she walked down the passageway, she came across the giant hammer.
Her brows furrowed. "A Mythic weapon?" she whispered in disbelief.
For a revered maiden of the divine kingdom, it was the first time she’d ever laid eyes on such a piece of junk.
"What the hell is wrong with this immortal!" she yelled. She snatched the hammer and, in a fit of frustration, hurled it against the passageway wall.
[SFX: HAMMER SHATTERING]
The wall remained intact, but the hammer shattered on impact.
Vesper endured the tirade, though none of this was his fault. He simply let Zane continue.
Zane distributed prizes based on allegiance and whim. Rivals received worthless junk; Cloud Hall members and their leaders got valuable treasures. Emanuel Drew, fortunate to have survived the Wheel of Fortune, received only a cheap Mythic weapon. Their satisfaction meant nothing to him.
[AMBIENT: TOMB ENTRANCE CROWD]
[MUSIC: ANTICIPATORY ATMOSPHERE]
Soon, the number of fighters in the Wheel of Fortune dwindled. Everyone who made it out was sent back to the tomb's entrance, where a small crowd was already waiting for their friends.
Zane, though, stayed behind the curtain of light, his eyes fixed on Tia. He watched her looking around, her eyes scanning the crowd as if searching for someone. For him.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL PIANO]
He was tempted to burn a Message Rune, to send her a sign, anything, but he held back.
He just stared at her image in the light and whispered softly, "Wait for me."
