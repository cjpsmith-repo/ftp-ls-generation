Ep 142 - The Crystals
Word count: 1155

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL VAULT]
[SFX: MYSTERIOUS ENERGY]
The resources Silverspire had saved up over the years couldn't hold a candle to the Skyreach Sect or the Sunstone Citadel, but they still dwarfed what the Seven Great Houses had. All kinds of Spirit Weapons and treasures were piled haphazardly on wooden shelves and shoved into corners as if they were nothing more than junk.
Zane could pick any three treasures he wanted, and he was more than happy to take them up on the offer. He wasn't particularly interested in Spirit Weapons; he already had a good amount of Celestial Essence, which he could easily trade for a weapon anytime he needed. He scanned the top shelf, his eyes glazing over the rows of blades and bows.
Axel watched him, surprised. A Spirit Weapon was practically useless to a warrior in the Soul Realm, but for someone at Zane's level, it should have been a top priority. 
His complete lack of interest was… odd.
Trying to be helpful, Axel pointed to a pair of ornate swords. "These are the Twin Blades. They're Legendary Arms, incredibly powerful. If you choose them, they'll only count as one of your three picks. Trust me, they're worth it."
Zane merely nodded, maintaining his silence. His calculating eyes continued to survey the vault, searching for something that truly called to him. Axel observed the young man's indifference with growing curiosity, having no choice but to accompany him as he ventured deeper into the cavernous treasury.
[AMBIENT: ECHOING FOOTSTEPS IN STONE VAULT]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]
 They wandered through aisles of gleaming artifacts, their footsteps echoing against the stone floor. Suddenly, Zane halted, his gaze transfixed by a distant wall. 
Three damaged treasures hung there, each radiating a powerful energy despite their broken state. 
He'd seen those runes before — today, in fact. After absorbing the Hellfire, the same markings had appeared on his own arms. Embedding runes. Slots designed to accept crystals and bond them to a weapon. Which meant his body could accept crystal embeddings, just like a Spirit Weapon.
Something about them resonated with him on an instinctual level. He approached slowly, drawn by an inexplicable connection, and pointed to the first—a shattered blade whose fragments still shimmered with latent power.
[SFX: MAGICAL RESONANCE]
 "This one," he whispered, his voice barely audible.
Axel's eyebrows rose in astonishment. He studied Zane's face, searching for any hint of uncertainty, but found only unwavering determination in those steel-gray eyes.
"Alright, Zane," Axel finally conceded, stroking his beard thoughtfully. "Since you've chosen the blade as your first treasure, it's yours." He couldn't fathom why anyone would select a broken weapon when whole ones were available, but something told him this young man saw value where others perceived only ruin.
The broken Spirit Weapons had been hanging in the vault for a long time. Most people who earned entry were soldiers in the imperial army. 
The army had its share of Master Weaponsmiths, but they weren't front-line fighters, so they rarely earned enough accolades to get a pick from the vault. And the soldiers who did get in? They almost always went for the fully assembled Legendary Arms, which were plentiful and much more useful in a fight. Hardly anyone ever chose the broken artifacts.
"What about the one next to it?" Zane asked, his gaze shifting.
[SFX: CRYSTAL PULSING]
 Next to the blade of the Crystal Butterfly hung another broken weapon: a red spear, shattered into several pieces. Just like the first, a blood-red crystal was embedded in the spearhead. As they watched, a dim red light pulsed inside it, growing brighter, then fainter, like a slow, rhythmic heartbeat.
"That spear is called Tronson, another masterwork," Axel explained with scholarly patience, his eyes reflecting respect for the ancient weapon. "The Emerald Crystal embedded in the spearhead grants it unparalleled piercing capabilities—it can shatter a Magic Ward with a single thrust."
"Shatter a Magic Ward?" Zane's eyes widened as he leaned forward, his fingers hovering inches from the gleaming crystal. The prospect immediately captivated him, memories of helplessness flashing through his mind.
He had developed a profound wariness of Magic Wards after being imprisoned in Yolande Shaw's transparent cages more times than he cared to remember. The feeling of being trapped, powerless against an invisible barrier, had left a mark on his psyche. 
While Tronson might not possess sufficient power to break Yolande's particularly sophisticated wards, such a weapon could prove invaluable against other magical barriers he might encounter. His heart quickened at the thought of never again feeling that particular vulnerability—of having an answer to those who would trap him like an insect under glass.
[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE]
 Axel nodded. "Yes. The Emerald Crystal is unbelievably powerful. Tronson was destroyed by the Netherlord Porter. Porter was a master of the Magic Ward, and at first, even our strongest warriors couldn't touch him. But the Emerald Crystal shattered his defenses. We finally defeated him, but Tronson was shattered in the process."
[SFX: ANCIENT BATTLE ECHOES]
 Looking at the pieces of the broken spear, Zane could almost feel the intensity of that long-ago battle.
"I'll take this one, too," Zane said, pointing at Tronson.
"Of course. I'll have them taken down for you," Axel said with a smile. "There's one crystal left. Are you interested in that one as well?"
Zane glanced at the last weapon on the wall—a massive, broken axe. He shook his head. 
The crystal on it shone brilliantly, but with two of his three picks already made, he had his eyes on something else.
 That Ambrosia Pill, for instance, had become nearly impossible to find on the open market.
The reason was simple: its properties were extraordinary. Swallow one and any injury would heal instantly — as long as the person was still breathing. It also repaired soul damage, and for three days after taking it, boosted the user's strength by three full cultivation levels. Useless to anyone at Soul Realm or above, but for everyone below that threshold, it was the finest life-saving treasure in existence.
It hadn't always been so expensive, but its price had skyrocketed. 
These days, a single Ambrosia Pill could fetch almost a hundred thousand Crystal Cubes—far more than any normal person could afford. It had reached the point where family elders would buy one for their most promising young prodigy. 
The person buying it wasn't the one using it, and the person using it could never afford to buy it themselves. The price was simply through the roof.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT DECISION]
 "This Ambrosia Pill will be my third choice," Zane announced, giving a satisfied nod.
[SFX: TENSE SILENCE]
 Axel’s smile tightened. To be honest, he had been saving that pill for a promising young member of his own family, planning to award it to them once they achieved something noteworthy in the army. 
Now Zane had claimed the pill as one of the three promised treasures, forcing Axel's hand. The Supreme Commander of the Imperial Army could only offer a reluctant nod, bound by his own word.
The kid's eye for value wasn't just sharp—it was devastating.
