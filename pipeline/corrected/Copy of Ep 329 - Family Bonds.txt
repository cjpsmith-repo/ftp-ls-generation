Ep 329 - Family Bonds
Word count: 1073

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DREAD]
The man Owen Lowell named the "Blood Prisoner" cast a shadow of terror across Castermere County and its neighboring territories.
[SFX: ECHOING SCREAMS]
This monstrous figure acquired his unholy strength through an ancient demonic ritual—he consumed the blood and flesh from living humans.
Their screams, it was said, fueled his transformation. This gruesome practice explained the mysterious disappearances that had plagued the local counties over recent years, leaving families broken and communities paralyzed with dread.
At first, the disappearances went unnoticed. But fate intervened when a young hunter, seeking shelter during a storm, stumbled upon the Blood Prisoner’s hidden cave. The sight that greeted him—mounds of human bones piled carelessly like discarded trash—was too gruesome to be believed. Within days, the horrific truth came to light.
Once the authorities were notified, the Empire dispatched its finest warriors, grim-faced men and women who had seen horrors before but steeled themselves for what awaited. The prestigious Skyreach Sect also posted a substantial bounty for his capture, attracting their elite disciples to join the hunt for this monstrous villain.
[SFX: BATTLE SOUNDS]
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]
As powerful as the Blood Prisoner had become through his dark practices, he was no match for the coordinated assault of so many skilled fighters.
Cornered like the beast he had become, he fought with desperate ferocity, wounding several pursuers before sustaining grievous injuries himself. Bleeding and weakened, he slipped away into the shadows, forced to seek refuge wherever he could.
[AMBIENT: QUIET FAMILY HOME]
[MUSIC: SOMBER TONE]
Meanwhile, Perrin Lowell arrived at the family estate, having recently been expelled from the Skyreach Sect. He reluctantly told Bryson Lowell that Zane had made remarkable progress in his training and secured acceptance into the Sect—an honor Perrin himself had failed to maintain.
When Bryson discovered that Zane, the nephew he had betrayed and his sworn enemy, not only survived but thrived as a disciple of the Skyreach Sect, the shock made him physically ill. The Sect, the ultimate training ground for warriors, would transform Zane into something terrifying. In five or ten years, his nephew would no doubt return, his power magnified beyond imagination. The thought of facing such retribution left Bryson trembling. What would he do then?
[AMBIENT: NIGHT SOUNDS]
[SFX: RESTLESS TOSSING]
The pressure was crushing. He spent sleepless nights haunted by the thought of Zane.
So, after Bryson recovered, that constant fear drove him to relentlessly improve his own strength.
He didn’t just become an Adept; he pushed himself all the way to the third level of that stage.
It was common knowledge that as a warrior aged, their bones hardened, making it that much harder to make significant progress. The only reason Bryson had achieved such a high level was the threat Zane posed.
But even with this achievement, Bryson wasn’t satisfied. He knew just how powerful the Skyreach Sect was—Perrin’s own experience proved it.
Perrin had become an Adept in a short time, and that was with just a little help from one of the noble houses, House Warrick.
If Zane had enough time to truly develop his skills, Bryson knew he still wouldn’t stand a chance.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSE]
Just when Bryson was at a loss, he ran into the Blood Prisoner, who was on the run.
The Blood Prisoner was undeniably powerful, but his injuries had forced him to flee aimlessly. In the end, he took refuge in the cellar of House Lowell.
Bryson didn’t find the Blood Prisoner at first; the fugitive was an expert at hiding. However, after several more people from the area were reported missing, Bryson realized something was wrong.
By then, news about the Blood Prisoner was everywhere. It didn’t take long for Bryson to figure out that the fugitive had hidden himself right there in House Lowell.
But he kept the discovery to himself.
The other family members grew nervous as they watched Bryson. After all, not a single one of them could stand up to him.
But Bryson now had a secret weapon in the fight against Zane. But first, he needed to remind these young people exactly who he was.
Bryson's voice cut across the training field like a whip. "Zane Lowell. You betrayed this family. How dare you show your face here."
Zane looked up at him without flinching. "This is my home. Why wouldn't I come back?"
[SFX: SWORD SWISH]
Bryson’s sword moved in a blur. Before anyone could react, its tip was right at Barnaby’s throat. Just before Bryson could do any real damage, another sword appeared.
[SFX: METAL CLASHING]
[SFX: SWORD SHATTERING]
With two sharp cracks, Bryson’s sword shattered.
[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL TENSION]
Zane lowered his sword, the Streamer, and looked up at Bryson. "Is this how you treat the people of House Lowell?" he asked coldly. "Are they just pawns for you to command? To do with as you please? In your eyes, is there any difference between your own family and an animal?"
"Zane’s right," Barnaby added, his voice firming. "The moment I did something you didn’t like, you tried to kill me. What do you think we are to you? Bryson, we share the same last name. We’re family. Have you forgotten that?"
He felt like he had nothing left to lose. His only choice was to put his faith in Zane’s strength.
And just now, that faith had been rewarded. Zane had protected him from Bryson’s attack.
So Barnaby spoke without fear. He was just a young man with no real standing in the family, yet here he was, calling out Bryson to his face.
He never would have imagined doing something like this before Zane arrived.
Bryson ignored Barnaby completely. His eyes were fixed on the Streamer in Zane’s hand.
His thirteen swords were all middle-grade Mysterious Weapons.
It had taken Bryson a great deal of time and money to acquire those thirteen swords.
And yet, Zane’s sword had just shattered one of them in an instant. The gleaming blade immediately captured his full attention.
It had sliced through his weapon as easily as cutting vegetables. What level is that sword? he wondered. Is it a low-grade Spirit Weapon weapon? A middle-grade, perhaps? But Bryson never would have guessed the truth. The thought that Zane’s sword was a Sacred Weapon had never even crossed his mind.
After all, he had never even seen a Top-Grade Spirit Weapon in his entire life, let alone a Sacred Weapon.
