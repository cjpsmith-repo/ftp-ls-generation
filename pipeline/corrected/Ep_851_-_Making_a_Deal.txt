Ep 851 - Making a Deal

Word count: 1026

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING MARKETPLACE]

Zane was thrilled to have someone as savvy as Camilla handling the translucent Ancient Heartstones. He couldn’t care less about their market value or the commission; he just wanted them sold.

“Alright, I’ll leave this with you, then!” he said, and with a quick wave, he was gone.

Camilla watched him go, knowing her own limits. As a fighter, she was good enough to mine the nineteenth and twentieth floors, but even if she worked herself to the bone, she’d never pull treasures like Zane did. It was far smarter to stay here and manage his haul. Maybe business was where she could finally show what she was made of.

She turned to the crowd gathering in front of her, a warm, professional smile spreading across her face. “Folks, feel free to take a look! I can even offer a special discount today, but payment is in Ancient Heartstones only!”

[AMBIENT: ECHOING CAVE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Meanwhile, Zane was already making his way up the Mine of the Dragon Lineage, and before long, he’d reached the thirtieth floor.

This level was usually reserved for masters who had reached the Life-and-Death Realm, but Zane was feeling lucky. He slipped through a narrow doorway set into the massive trunk and landed silently on one of the mining tree’s green leaves. The leaves here were twice as wide as those on the floor below, their color darker, their texture tougher. The higher you went, the harder they were to break.

Zane had come for treasure, but the second his boots touched the leaf, a chilling wave of killing intent washed over him.

[SFX: CREATURE CHITTERING]

[MUSIC: HIGH TENSION]

He wasn’t alone.

Crouched silently beside him on the massive leaf was a mineral-devouring bug. The creature was enormous, as tall as two men, with six spindly legs and a pair of unsettling brown compound eyes. The scales along its abdomen shimmered with a faint green light.

[SFX: CREATURE MOVEMENT]

[SFX: CREATURE HISS]

Zane rose to his feet with painstaking slowness, trying not to draw its attention, but the slightest weight shift was enough. The bug’s head snapped toward him, and it lunged.

A frown creased Zane’s brow as the monster charged. He’d thought the blood sacrifice had wiped out every living creature in the Sacred Feather Sea. What was this thing doing here?

[SFX: SHARP IMPACT]

[SFX: LEAF TEARING]

The bug’s spear-like foreleg shot forward, punching a clean hole right through the leaf under Zane’s feet.

Damn, that leg is sharp, he thought, dodging back. And incredibly powerful.

He wasn’t wrong. Back on the twenty-ninth floor, it had taken the full power of over a hundred Dragon Scales just for him to break one of these leaves with his pickax—and that was a low-grade Relic-Class weapon. This bug had pierced it like paper.

He’d dealt with insects before. Whatever the species, the abdomen was always the weak point. Zane ducked under the next thrust, closed the distance, and drove his palm hard into the creature’s underside. Vitality surged through the strike.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

[SFX: SCALES CRACKING]

With a sharp crack, a broad section of scales exploded off the bug’s abdomen. The bug let out a piercing shriek — and threw open its wings.

[SFX: CREATURE SHRIEK]

Zane straightened — and stopped. Through the broken scales, the bug’s abdomen was transparent. Packed inside, glinting in the dim light, were dozens of Ancient Heartstones.

[SFX: WINGS BEATING]

The bug snapped its wings fully open and leapt to the adjacent leaf.

[SFX: GRAVITATIONAL PULL WHOOSH]

Zane moved to follow — and immediately felt the pull. A tremendous gravitational force seized him the moment he neared the leaf’s edge, dragging him back toward the circle of light. He couldn’t fight it. Step off your leaf and the mine spat you straight back out; you had to climb the pyramid all over again.

The bug seemed to know it. It raised both forelegs and rattled them at him.

[SFX: CREATURE CHITTERING]

Zane ignored it. He turned, crouched, and got to work on his own leaf. He worked steadily, prying loose several colorful, strangely shaped ores, while the bug, satisfied he’d given up, hammered its own forelegs into the adjacent leaf and began to feed.

Then Zane paused. Leaf to leaf, he had a clear line of sight straight into the creature’s transparent abdomen. He counted carefully.

Fifty. Sixty. More.

Ancient Heartstones — dozens of them, packed inside the bug.

[MUSIC]

He had to kill that bug. He quickly located the narrow door he’d used to enter and slipped into the one directly beside it — but first he had to climb back down.

The moment he stepped off the leaf, the gravitational force did the rest. He dropped through the circle of light and landed outside the pyramid. He was already moving toward the entrance when Camilla’s voice cut through the noise of the trading post.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING MARKETPLACE]

“I’ve already sold enough to get you ten Ancient Heartstones and two thousand supreme Aether crystals!” she called out, clearly impressed. The other top-tier fighters had plenty of Aether crystals, but the Ancient Heartstones were what really mattered.

Pleased, Zane tapped his Storage Ring, sending a stream of seven or eight more translucent Heartstones shooting toward her before he turned and rushed right back into the mine.

“Wow,” Camilla breathed, catching them. “A Violet Crystal, a Crescent Moon Stone, and a Crystal Heartstone…”

She’d announced the names as a marketing tactic, and it worked perfectly. The other fighters had instantly swarmed her, their faces masks of envy.

“How is that guy so lucky?” one of them complained. “Even on the thirtieth floor, there’s no way you should be able to dig up that many valuable stones in a single trip!”

“A few others went up there,” another added bitterly. “They came back with a couple of common items and two broken pickaxes. Why is he so different?”

“He’s strong, and he’s got insane luck on top of it,” someone else muttered. “It’s just not fair.”

What they failed to understand was that for a select few, luck was a form of strength all on its own.

[AMBIENT: MINING TREE INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Back on the thirtieth floor, Zane slipped through the narrow door beside the one he’d used before. Each door corresponded to a different leaf — which meant this one would land him right next to where the bug was feeding.

If he was fast enough, it wouldn’t even hear him coming.
