Ep 725 - I'm Here For Zane
Word count: 1063
[AMBIENT: QUIET TRAINING GROUNDS]
Zane had brought gifts back from his two years away — Relic-class weapons for each of them, the kind of treasure that didn't exist in the Heartlands — and the looks on his friends' faces were worth every mile.
Of the three men standing before Zane, only one had eyes for the pair of shortswords. Finn was a dedicated swordsman who already had his own blade, and Patrick didn’t use one at all. That left Lewis, whose biggest strength was his speed. With his low cultivation level, though, that speed was mostly good for running away. A pair of shortswords like these? They would finally give him some real offensive power. He looked so thrilled, it was almost comical.
Zane just smiled and nodded at him.
[SFX: SWORD GRAB]
Before Zane could even properly offer them, Lewis’s hands shot out and snatched the swords right out of his grasp. The sudden move made Zane and Patrick laugh, but Finn just shot Lewis a disapproving look.
Patrick, ever the calmest of the group, simply watched with a gentle smile. His training followed the Monastic Path, and back in the Skyreach Sect, he’d actually been stronger than Finn. The problem was that his monastic order was fading. The once-famous Monastery of the Enlightened Heart was now barely a Third-Grade sect, forcing Patrick to rely on his own understanding to train. It made his progress painfully slow compared to Finn’s. Since he didn’t use a weapon, Zane had brought him a defensive artifact instead—a low-grade Relic-Class artifact. Paired with his Form of the Adamant Soul, it would make his defense terrifyingly strong.
Still, even with each of them holding a new low-grade Relic-Class weapon, they couldn’t just walk around showing them off. The Heartlands weren’t like the main continent; Relic-Class weapons were incredibly scarce here. Only elite warriors, those at the Life-and-Death Realm or the final stages of Spirit-Tribulation, were supposed to have them. If they carried these on a mission, they’d become an instant target for anyone with bad intentions.
[AMBIENT: CLOUDHAVEN CITY]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
While the four of them were catching up, a beautiful woman was slowly making her way down into Cloudhaven.
Zane had left Cora behind in Pyreton, but after hesitating for a long time, she had finally decided to follow him. She looked delicate, as if she’d never known a hard day in her life, and it was true that she was naive to the harsh rules of their world. Her innocent appearance made her a frequent target.
If she were still her old self, making it to Cloudhaven alone would have been impossible. But after fully activating her Violet-Core Physique, she had become deceptively powerful. Any predator who saw her as an easy mark was in for a nasty surprise. They’d assume she was just some weak woman, easy to handle even if she’d somehow reached the Inner-Core Realm. They’d be dead wrong.
The warriors who came after her on the road to Cloudhaven made the same mistake. The moment they moved on her, she unleashed a storm of giant swords that cut them down before they could even think about running.
The worst of it had happened the day before, at a small city she'd stopped in to rest. The place was less a city than a gathering point for independent warriors who'd abandoned the major sects — rough men with no rules and no oversight. They'd come for her one after another, and she'd killed them one after another. But killing a few had brought more, and more had brought still more, until she'd had no choice.
She summoned every sword she had — more than she'd ever called at once — and brought them all down at the same time. When the light cleared, the city was gone. Every building. Every person. Flattened.
She'd stood at the edge of the rubble, shaking. Then she'd turned and flown toward Cloudhaven as fast as she could.
[AMBIENT: GATE COURTYARD]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Stepping up to the gates of Cloud Hall, she spoke timidly to the guard on duty. "I… I'm looking for Zane."
"You're looking for Zane?" the guard shot back. "He's been gone for a long time. He's not here!"
Cloud Hall had been in turmoil recently, but only the higher-ups knew the full story. This guard was just a rank-and-file warrior, completely unaware that Zane had returned. For the past two years, he’d given the same answer to everyone who came looking for the man, including official visitors from the Runic Tower.
Cora’s face fell, her brow furrowing. "What? Zane's not here?" That was impossible. Zane had been on the run from Maddox, but he’d found a way to shake him. He had to be back in Cloudhaven. This guard was lying to her.
A new thought soured in her mind: Zane had told him to lie because he didn't want to see her.
Why had she even followed him all this way? She told herself she hated him. She told herself he owed her something — though even she couldn't say exactly what. Neither answer felt quite right, and she didn't want to look too closely at what the real one might be.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
The timidity in her voice evaporated, replaced by a cold edge. "Stop lying to me. Tell Zane to come out here."
Despite her anger, she was still a traditional woman at heart. She truly believed Zane owed her something after what they'd been through, although she wasn’t even sure what she wanted from him.
"I already told you! Zane is missing. He's not at Cloud Hall!" the guard snapped, his patience wearing thin. An Inner-Core Realm warrior or not, he wasn't scared of her. The enchanted barrier would stop her cold; not even a fighter from the Life-and-Death Realm could break through it.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER HUM]
Cora slowly walked right up to the shimmering barrier. With a dark look on her face, she yelled, "This is the last time I'm going to say this. Get Zane out here! Tell him to come see me!"
"Shut up, you crazy woman! Go home!" the guard shouted back, turning his back on her and walking toward the entrance of the hall. "You're not seeing Zane here!"
You stupid man, she thought to herself. You're going to regret this.
