Ep 1064 - Passing Through

Word count: 1029

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL PALACE]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

Ever since Zane had arrived in the Upper World and met Yolande, she’d felt like a stranger, and that feeling still hadn’t faded.

To Zane, the Yolande standing in front of him was identical to the two clones he’d known back in the Lower World. But underneath, she was someone else entirely. The person he knew was gone, and this Yolande had taken her place.

Even though this Yolande held the memories of those two clones, they were just two drops in an ocean of a million others. The only reason she even remembered Zane so vividly was that she’d specifically dredged those memories up from the minds of her countless counterparts. If she hadn’t done the same for Jasper, he would’ve been a complete stranger to her, too.

Jasper had come to The Mist Palace with a thing for Yolande. She’d noticed him, sure—his skills were impressive, and she knew what he was after. But that didn’t change the fact that he was, for all intents and purposes, a stranger.

Zane shook his head. Jasper’s persistence was futile. Yolande, daughter of a Supreme Lord, already possessed unlimited power and status. Why would she be impressed by mere strength?

Of course, none of that was Zane’s problem.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

Down below, warriors were still flying toward the Red Cloud Ring, one by one. Those who made it through gathered behind the leaders of The Mist Palace, while the failures walked away, their disappointment palpable. The crowd of hopefuls was starting to thin out.

“Aren’t you gonna go?” Layla asked, sidling up next to Zane.

“You can go first,” Zane said.

“I was actually hoping you’d go first,” Layla shot back. “But since you asked so nicely, I will!”

[SFX: WHOOSH]

With that, Layla sprang lightly from the ground, soaring into the air. After a graceful loop, she zipped toward the Red Cloud Ring, turning to flash Zane a triumphant grin just before she shot right through it.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[AMBIENT: DEEP HUM]

The instant Layla passed through, a scarlet beam of light erupted from the Red Cloud Ring, spreading rapidly around its edge as a deep hum filled the air. At the same time, dazzling rays of light surged around the ring like crashing waves that refused to recede, making it glow like it was carved from pure scarlet crystal.

[AMBIENT: HUSHED MURMURING]

The vice leaders exchanged quiet looks. They remembered this one. Not long ago, she’d used a Tiger’s Roar Token to challenge every warrior in the Mist Palace — an act that had annoyed more than a few of them. They’d looked into it afterward and confirmed she was Elder Dunn’s direct disciple. Since he’d placed her here himself, they’d decided to leave well enough alone.

Now, watching the ring, they understood why he’d taken her on.

Yolande studied the glow. “For the ring to react like that, her lotus must be at nine petals.”

“That would be my read as well,” one of the vice leaders said. “If I’m not mistaken, she’s at peak nine-petal. There’s a chance she grows a Greater Lotus Flower.”

“A Greater Lotus Flower…” another vice leader murmured. “Elder Dunn’s luck is something else to have found a treasure like this.”

Yolande nodded faintly. “She should have a place in this era.” She paused. “Though nothing is certain. The great battle has only just begun.”

[AMBIENT: QUIET OBSERVATION]

What Yolande kept to herself was this: Layla’s arrogance was going to be a problem. She’d heard what had been happening — the repeated provocations, the destruction, all of it aimed at Zane. To put it plainly, that woman was courting death. No amount of heaven-defying luck could protect someone determined to do that.

But it wasn’t her place to intervene. Zane had his own path. She’d let him walk it.

She glanced down. “Zane. Why haven’t you come up yet?”

The two people everyone was buzzing about were Layla and Zane. A lot of warriors from The Mist Palace were dying to know who was actually stronger.

Zane had only fought twice at The Mist Palace. First, he’d beaten some warrior from Groundstone Hall. The second time, he’d nearly killed Brock. But since Brock wasn’t even ranked in the top two hundred of Cloudswallow Hall, it was hard to gauge Zane’s true strength from that fight.

Layla, on the other hand, had taken down more than ten people in a row. She’d crushed warriors ranked in the top few hundred of Cloudswallow Hall, some in the top dozens, and had even beaten their fourth-ranked fighter.

So, even though most of the warriors couldn’t stand Layla, they had to admit she was probably way out of Zane’s league.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL WIND]

Up above the ring, Layla kept her expression calm as the scarlet glow slowly faded around her. She was aware of every eye on her. She didn’t mind.

This wasn’t what she was here for. The Mist Palace was a starting point, not a destination. What she wanted was a place among the top geniuses of the entire universe — a role in the only great battle of the era. Passing the Torch was the first step.

And Zane had refused her every time she’d tried to challenge him. Fine. She’d find another way to settle it — inside the event itself.

“Hurry up! Get up here!” Layla called down, holding her hand out to Zane. Her five slender fingers were spread wide, as if she could pluck him right out of the air from hundreds of feet away.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane nodded, channeling his Chaos Energy before slowly floating up toward the Red Cloud Ring.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

“He doesn’t look too confident, does he?”

“No one even knows how many petals his lotus flower has. What if it’s only, like, three? You think he’s scared he can’t even make it through the Red Cloud Ring?”

“No way!”

[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]

“He’s not gonna make it. A warrior knows their own strength. Look at him, he’s hesitating. He’s totally scared he’s going to fail.”
