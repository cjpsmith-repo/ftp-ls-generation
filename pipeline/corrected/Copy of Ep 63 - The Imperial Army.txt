Ep 63 - The Imperial Army

[AMBIENT: BATTLE SOUNDS]
[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]The Skycruiser groaned as the Razorwings finally compromised the hull's critical systems, their blades slicing into the power source located at the vessel's base. 
Zane felt the floor tilt violently beneath him as the engine's rhythmic hum turned into a dying shriek. With a deafening roar of twisting metal, the once-proud ship plummeted through the canopy of the Eastern Territory, shearing through ancient trees before slamming into the earth.
As smoke curled from the shattered wreckage, the survivors scrambled into the clearing, their transport now nothing more than a broken shell. There was no time to catch their breath; the crash had only served to draw more of the level-three monsters from the surrounding woods. Desperate and cornered, the disciples began heaping the carcasses of the initial swarm into a grim barricade, preparing for a relentless wave of predators that showed no signs of stopping.
There was a saying that went around The Sunbelt: "See one Razorwing, and there are two thousand more you don't." It was an exaggeration, sure, but it got the point across.
Even the most powerful refiners—those on the verge of the Nature Level, or even those who had already reached it—would think twice before tangling with a full-blown swarm.
The Skyreach Sect disciples were powerful, and right now, they'd managed to cut down hundreds of the things. Razorwing corpses were piled high around the wreckage, forming a gruesome ridge.
But for every one they killed, it felt like two more took their place. The wave was endless.
The Nature Level disciples had begun throwing everything they had at the swarm — arrows of condensed energy punching through a dozen Razorwings at once, jets of True Fire erupting across the sky and burning scores of them to cinders. The display was extraordinary. It rallied the others, and for a moment the line held.
Then someone shouted: "Blade Insect Leaders!"
[SFX: MONSTROUS SHRIEK FROM ABOVE]
They descended from the sky in a mass — hundreds of them, purple where the others were green, twice the size, their bodies bristling with spikes. The ear-splitting screech they made was nothing like the ordinary Razorwings. They hit the line like a wave.
Whatever momentum the disciples had built evaporated. A new kind of dread moved through the group. They'd been fighting endlessly, their energy was draining, and now this.
Everyone's eyes said the same thing: were they going to die here?
[SFX: HUMAN SCREAM]
A sudden scream ripped through the chaos.
[SFX: BATTLE CRY]
Driven by either desperate courage or sheer madness, one disciple suddenly broke formation and charged headfirst into the swarm.
[SFX: CHITTERING SWARM]
[SFX: HUMAN SCREAMS]
His battle cry died in his throat as the creatures descended upon him. It was over in seconds—too swift for anyone to intervene. A tide of chittering, razor-edged bodies engulfed him, dragging him down beneath their collective mass. His screams pierced the air briefly before falling silent.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]
When the creatures scattered, they left nothing behind—not a scrap of clothing, not a drop of blood. He had simply vanished, consumed entirely.
A paralyzing dread crept through the remaining disciples as they witnessed their comrade's horrific end.
Some averted their eyes too late; others stood transfixed, unable to look away.
The grisly scene etched itself into their memories—the way he disappeared beneath the writhing mass, the terrible efficiency with which the creatures had stripped him to nothingness. Many had seen death before, but never so complete, so merciless.
None spoke, but their tightened grips on weapons and widened eyes revealed a singular thought: they would not surrender to such a fate.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE SOUNDS]
[MUSIC: TENSE COMBAT]
Shoulder to shoulder, they stood, backs pressed together, each silently vowing not to be the next to fall.
But the waves of Razorwings just kept coming. They knew that once their energy gave out, it was over. Despair began to set in, their strikes growing sluggish and sloppy.
Just as their line was about to break, a shout cut through the din. "Hold on!"
[SFX: MARCHING FOOTSTEPS]
From the edge of the woods, a large group of men emerged.
They were serious, uniformed, and moved as one, their synchronized march a stark contrast to the disciples' frantic defense. Each soldier carried a set of 'Drawing Straws' on their back.
"The imperial army!" a disciple breathed, a wave of relief washing over the group.
The Empire dedicated a huge portion of its army to fight the "insect aggression" every year. Unlike the disciples, the imperial soldiers moved as a single, disciplined unit. An individual soldier might not match a disciple's power, but their collective strength was a force to be reckoned with.
[AMBIENT: ORGANIZED MILITARY FORMATION]
The soldiers formed neat rows with practiced efficiency.
A young man with a sharp crew cut stepped forward, his voice cutting through the noise with undeniable authority. "I'm Shane, Centurion of the Emerald Task Force. Disciples of the Skyreach Sect, listen up! You want to live? Follow us!"
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
But after Shane gave his order, the disciples didn't move. Their eyes were glued to the mountain of Razorwing corpses.
They hadn't come here for a rescue—they'd come to earn Points.
The Sect's rules were simple: one point for every twenty Razorwings, confirmed by collecting the crystal core from its chest. A pile of carcasses that big represented a massive haul. They'd fought too hard to just walk away from it. Leaving now, with all that loot on the ground? It didn't sit right with any of them.
It didn't help that the imperial army and the Skyreach Sect had a history. The two groups couldn't stand each other, and their rivalry often boiled over into brawls.
One of the disciples started, "Can't you just give us a minute? We just need to—"
Shane cut him off with a scoff. "I know exactly what you want to do. And I'm telling you to think again. Our scouts have detected more legions swarming this way. I'm not risking my men's lives for your points. If you want to stay, be my guest. We're leaving!"
[AMBIENT: JUNGLE SOUNDS]
[SFX: RAZORWING SCREECHES]
Grumbling, the disciples realized they had no choice. They fell in line with the soldiers. A few of the stronger cultivators, those at the Nature Level, glanced back at the field of corpses, tempted to stay and gather a few cores. They were confident they could fight their way out if needed. But one look at the sky killed that idea. The number of Razorwings circling overhead was growing by the second. The rainforest was dangerous enough on a good day; staying behind now would be suicide.
Every year, for reasons no one understood, hordes of monstrous beasts poured out of the rainforest to assault the Empire.
It was the sole purpose of Dragon's Keep to hold them back. And of all the beasts that clawed their way out of the jungle, the Razorwings were by far the most troublesome.
[AMBIENT: FORTRESS ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: IMPOSING THEME]
From the outside, Dragon's Keep was deceptively simple. Its stone walls and moat were unadorned, save for a single, massive dragon's head sculpted from black stone and mounted above the gate.
The dragon's eyes gleamed with an unnatural brilliance, as though the sculptor had captured living fire within the stone. The entire monument exuded such overwhelming power that even the most prideful Skyreach disciples fell silent in reverence as they passed beneath it.
[SFX: MYSTICAL RESONANCE]
Yet Zane froze mid-step, his attention locked on those shimmering eyes. Something stirred within him—a peculiar sensation he couldn't identify, growing stronger with each heartbeat.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
The truth hit him like a physical blow. While others merely felt awe, Zane alone sensed it—a potent spiritual pressure pulsating from the core of the stone dragon's head, calling to him, as if the ancient sculpture held secrets meant only for his discovery.
