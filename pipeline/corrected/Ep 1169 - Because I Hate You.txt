Ep 1169 - Because I Hate You

Word count: 1012

[MUSIC: OMINOUS FOREBODING]
“Cassian.”
The sound of Lavender’s voice, thin and strained, drifted from the Wheel of Reincarnation, echoing through the vast emptiness of space.
[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSH]
[SFX: ARMOR IMPACT]
A series of sharp whooshes sliced through the void. Cassian swung his spear in a brutal arc and dropped to one knee, his eyes as dark and cold as ink.
“My Queen.”
He was draped in a snow-white robe, his long, silver-gray hair spilling down his back. His face, delicate and fine-boned, was more beautiful than that of any of the women who fought alongside him. He was the only man in their group; the other three were Maren, Taryn, and Paige. As Cassian knelt, they immediately followed his lead, their movements sharp and synchronized.
From within the Wheel of Reincarnation, Lavender looked out at the four of them, her gaze intense. “How have you been?”
The simple question seemed to strike them like a physical blow. The four warriors trembled, the weight of their troubled pasts crashing down on them all at once.
Cassian slowly rose to his feet. That beautiful face — so out of place on a warrior — twisted into something helpless. He lowered his head.
“My Queen… I’m sorry.”
He bowed again, deeply. The other three followed without a word, their expressions mirrors of the same grief.
It all started seven hundred years ago, when a strange event shook the universe to its core.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS FOREBODING]
[SFX: HEAVENS CRACKING]
A crack split the heavens, and from it, a corpse fell, landing on a planet known as the Dark Purple Star. Rumor had it that the body was that of a True God. The news sent a shockwave through the upper worlds, and countless races scrambled to send their best warriors to investigate. The Demon Night was no different. Lavender had dispatched Cassian, Maren, Taryn, and Paige to the planet herself.
She remembered seeing them off from her Hollow Hall that day. They were her finest, a team so powerful that, together, they could even take down a Supreme Lord. And since Supreme Lords rarely bothered with this sort of thing, the mission should have been simple.
But the moment they left, a terrible, nauseating feeling had seized her. She knew something awful was about to happen, but she couldn’t place it. Normally, she would have consulted Supreme Lord Leroy, but he was in secluded cultivation, and no one dared disturb him.
[SFX: HEAVY CHAINS CLANKING]
Then she heard the clanking of heavy chains.
[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]
Standing before the Hollow Hall was the Queen of Punishment, clad in the brilliant blue Saint Sky Armor and wielding her devastating Saint Thunder Meteor Hammer. They were two of the Demon Night’s most powerful Divine-Class weapons, both ranked in the top fifty of the Cosmic Spirit Index. The hammer’s lightning — thin as a finger, crackling around its head in restless loops — carried the eighth level of the Law of Lightning and something far worse: the law of karma. Every warrior who had ever faced that hammer was dead.
The instant Lavender saw her armed for war, she knew exactly what was coming.
[SFX: WEAPON MATERIALIZATION]
[SFX: ENERGY RIPPLING]
The Nightshard Spear appeared in her hand. Taking a fighting stance, she drew on the energy from her Pool of Faith, its surface rippling with power. She knew that if she were at her peak, with her spear and the full force of the Pool, she might have stood a chance.
Ten breaths passed. The Hollow Hall was completely silent.
Then Lavender felt it — the Pool of Faith going still beneath her. Not empty. Sealed.
[SFX: ENERGY BEING SMOTHERED]
She couldn’t draw from it at all.
“You sealed the Pool of Faith,” she said, her voice cold. “In the Demon Night, that’s a capital crime. The Board of Elders won’t allow this.”
The Queen of Punishment said nothing. She just walked forward, her spiked heels striking the floor of the Hollow Hall in slow, deliberate clicks.
“Why?” Lavender asked, her spear leveled, its tip glinting even as the power behind it drained away.
The Queen of Punishment smiled.
“Why? Let me think… because I hate you. It’s that simple.”
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT BOOM]
An invisible force slammed into the Wheel of Reincarnation with a massive boom, shaking everyone inside. The plate didn’t shatter, but a deep dent now marred its surface — the result of Cassian’s spear.
The other three warriors were right behind him, chasing the plate and hammering it with their own weapons.
[SFX: RELENTLESS WEAPON STRIKES]
The sound of their strikes rang out in a relentless, percussive rhythm.
Zane frowned, his jaw tight, but said nothing.
[AMBIENT: HIGH-SPEED CHASE]
He poured all his focus into steering the Wheel of Reincarnation, pushing it to its absolute top speed. It was no use. Cassian, Maren, Taryn, and Paige were like shadows, relentlessly attacking as they gave chase.
[SFX: CRACKING GLASS]
[SFX: ENERGY SHIELD SHATTERING]
Soon, thin cracks spiderwebbed across the plate’s surface, the splintering sound growing louder and more frequent.
[SFX: MULTIPLE EXPLOSIONS]
The divine textures protecting them flickered and then exploded, one by one.
“They’re just too powerful,” Livingston said, a bitter smile twisting his lips. “The Wheel of Reincarnation can’t stop them. It’s going to break apart.”
Freya held her breath, her anxiety a physical weight in her chest. She knew she was useless here; the only thing she could do was stay quiet and not distract Zane.
“Let me out!” Lavender suddenly demanded.
“No!” Zane shot back without a second thought. “Your soul has only recovered sixty percent of its strength. You might be able to handle a Spirit Transformation warrior, but you can’t block a single strike from a World Lord.”
“The Pool of Faith isn’t sealed,” Lavender countered, her voice firm. “I can buy us more time.”
She didn’t say what they both knew: that Cassian and the others weren’t just powerful. They had learned everything they knew from her. If anyone could buy time against them, it was Lavender herself.
[SFX: DIVINE TEXTURES ACTIVATING]
Before he could argue, she activated several runes around them.
[MUSIC: HEROIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY FIELD]
A moment later, Lavender appeared outside the Wheel of Reincarnation, the Nightshard Spear gripped tightly in her hand.
[SFX: MASSIVE WINGS UNFURLING]
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY BURST]
The instant she was free, enormous wings formed of pure Faith Energy erupted from her back.
[SFX: SPEAR THRUST]
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
With a single, powerful swing of her spear, she planted herself directly in Cassian’s path.
