Ep 430 - Golden Opportunity

Word count: 1228

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

With three Inner-Core masters standing guard, nobody so much as breathed. They were all paralyzed, haunted by the image of the last guy who tried to fight back—a Soul Realm practitioner who'd been hit so hard he was practically turned to pulp.

Even Zane, who was usually the first to gamble with his life, stood perfectly still. He knew his odds of escaping three masters were slim to none, and it was a bet he wasn't willing to make. He kept his mouth shut, a knot tightening in his stomach. A primal instinct screamed that something terrible was coming.

[SFX: MENACING FOOTSTEPS]

One of the masters spoke casually as his creepy eyes swept over the crowd."You move, you die." 

No one had a choice but to obey. 

[AMBIENT: MARCHING CROWD]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

A tense silence fell as over a thousand practitioners, mostly from the Adept Realm, were marched down the main road. The sheer number of captives was staggering; at this rate, they’d round up every Adept in the region. Were they really just after the Amethyst Thunderoak on Twilight Peak?

Guards flanked the procession, and one of them suddenly tossed a set of ochre ropes at Zane and his group. Not daring to defy the Inner-Core masters, they let themselves be bound and shoved into the captive line.
A young man in purple robes arrived on the path ahead. The three Inner-Core masters bowed in unison. "Young master."
He looked them over without expression. "How many?"
"One thousand and thirty-six, young master."
A faint smile crossed his face. "Good work. I'll see you rewarded when we return to the sect." He turned and walked on without another word.

[SFX: ROPE TOSS]

As Zane was forced to shuffle along, his mind raced. What was the point of capturing so many people? He knew getting the Amethyst Thunderoak was dangerous, but this felt like overkill. Then his gaze snagged on a pendant worn by the young man in purple. It looked familiar, but before he could place it, a tremor jolted through his mind.

[SFX: PSYCHIC PULSE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHIMES]

What's going on? The jolt was startling. Zane knew it was the Cobalt Dragon. He focused his thoughts inward. What's wrong?

[SFX: EERIE LAUGHTER ECHO]

A moment later, a sound like dry laughter echoed in his head.

Zane's frayed nerves snapped. What the hell is so funny? he shot back. In case you haven't noticed, I'm in deep shit here! Is this a joke to you? He was already scrambling for a way out, and helplessness was starting to sour in his gut. The dragon’s detached amusement was the last thing he needed.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

"Deep shit? I don't think so," the Cobalt Dragon's voice replied, impossibly calm. "From where I'm sitting, this looks like a golden opportunity."

Golden opportunity? Zane was completely lost. What kind of opportunity could there possibly be with four Inner-Core masters breathing down their necks? Still pissed at the dragon's cryptic nonsense, he demanded, I don't have time for your games. Just spit it out.

As infuriating as the ancient being could be, Zane knew it saw the world on a scale he couldn't comprehend. Everything he valued—his mastery of brushwork, his hard-won sword intent, the treasures he’d bled for—was nothing more than dust to the Cobalt Dragon. So when it called something a "golden opportunity," he had to listen.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

The Cobalt Dragon's voice carried an edge of anticipation. "Like I said—a golden opportunity. But you'll need my help. Stop thinking about running and play along. I think I know exactly what he's after. And if that man finds what he's seeking, I can help you break through to the Soul Realm in an instant."

The words struck Zane like a physical blow, eclipsing everything else the dragon had revealed. His heart hammered against his ribs. Breaking through to the Soul Realm—the threshold that separated true masters from talented cultivators—had seemed months away, perhaps even a year. Yet here was the Cobalt Dragon, offering it to him in an instant, as casually as one might offer a cup of tea.

But the promise came wrapped in danger. Zane's hands trembled slightly as he processed the implications. Whatever the young man in purple sought on Twilight Peak, it had to be extraordinary enough to tempt even an ancient being like the Cobalt Dragon. 

His curiosity burned hotter now, a fierce flame consuming his initial panic. What could possibly be valuable enough to warrant this risk? What treasure is hidden on this godforsaken peak that's worth gambling my life?

The Cobalt Dragon remained silent, offering no further explanation. As always, the ancient being revealed only what it chose, when it chose, leaving Zane to navigate the treacherous waters between trust and self-preservation.

[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WILDERNESS]

Zane had assumed they were all just bait. But to his surprise, the captors seemed invested in keeping them alive. Two of the Inner-Core masters scouted ahead, effortlessly slaughtering any beasts that crossed their path. The creatures of Twilight Peak were vicious, but they were no match for the masters.

The young man in purple periodically checked his Dragon Crystal Pendant, adjusting their course as they went deeper into the mountains. 

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Then, without warning, everything changed. Two Inner-Core masters stormed toward the line of captives. They grabbed several practitioners and, with brutal force, hurled them toward a mountain creek.

[SFX: BODIES FALLING]

The prisoners tumbled down the rocky bank, but they never hit the water. 



At the center of the creek stood a tower of light — thirty meters tall, shimmering with a strange, shifting glow. The young master's composure finally cracked. He let out a short, triumphant laugh.
"The Dragon Soar Arena. Everyone thought that lightning meant the Thunderoak had emerged. They were wrong. It was this." His eyes gleamed. "I finally found it."
His masters offered their congratulations. Then he waved his hand. "Feed them to it."
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
[SFX: WINGS FLAPPING]
Atop the tower, a massive winged dragon raised its head and roared. It stretched its neck toward the falling captives and swallowed them whole.

[AMBIENT: TERRIFIED CROWD MURMURS]

The grotesque display sent a wave of terror through the remaining captives. Any lingering hope that the winged dragon was just for show evaporated in an instant. 

This was a feeding.

No one moved. A few captives straightened up and shouted.
"Kill me then! I won't walk into that thing's mouth for you!"
"Do your own dirty work!"
The two Inner-Core masters didn't argue. They waded into the line, grabbed the nearest captives by their bindings, and hurled them down the bank.

One by one, more practitioners were thrown into the creek. The rest could only watch, paralyzed, as the two Inner-Core masters worked their way down the line, grabbing person after person and tossing them to their doom. The winged dragon devoured them all without hesitation.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DANGER]

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

Only eight people remained between Zane and the front of the line. His turn approached with terrifying inevitability. Panic seized his chest, constricting his lungs as his mind raced through impossible scenarios. Each scream from the creek below sent fresh waves of terror through his body. He watched another practitioner disappear into the water with barely a splash, swallowed whole by the creature lurking beneath.

His hands trembled despite his attempts to steady them. This wasn't like facing opponents in the arena, where skill and technique mattered. This was pure, helpless slaughter—a feeding line where cultivation meant nothing against overwhelming power.

Desperation overrode pride as Zane threw his thoughts inward, reaching for the only ally who might save him. Alright, what's the plan? His mental voice carried an edge of hysteria he couldn't suppress. I'm trusting you not to let me end up as dragon chow!
