Ep 1102 - Handing Out Prizes

Word count: 1143

[AMBIENT: ARENA EXIT]
[MUSIC: UPLIFTING HOPEFUL]
Layla emerged from the Illusionary Arena, practically glowing. Just before she left, her brother had told her he’d be released soon and promised to visit her at The Mist Palace right after. They were going to be reunited, and soon.
As she stepped out of the crucible, she turned to Zane with a huge, smug smile. After all, she was the only one who’d passed the ultimate trial in the arena.
Sure, the opponent she’d faced had been a pushover compared to her brother, but that was the whole point. Zane had only lost because he’d gotten the short end of the stick, drawing her brother as his opponent. 
In a straight fight, she knew Zane would probably wipe the floor with her, but the results were the results. Besides, a little good luck was its own kind of strength.
Zane just gave a small nod, his expression giving nothing away.
The lack of a reaction was a huge letdown for Layla. Wasn’t he supposed to be furious that her brother had knocked him out of the running? She couldn’t find a single hint of frustration on his face, and without the satisfaction of seeing him upset, her victory felt a little hollow. But then she remembered her brother was coming, and just like that, her mood lifted again.
[AMBIENT: CEREMONIAL HALL]
[MUSIC: FORMAL ANNOUNCEMENT]
Once Zane and Layla were clear of the arena, the old man took center stage to announce the rankings and hand out the rewards. First came the Tribulation Exterminating Pills, a consolation prize of sorts that most warriors from the Thirteen Palaces of the Alliance received just for participating in Passing the Torch.
Next were the more impressive rewards. A handful of warriors who’d managed to climb three thousand miles in the Enchanted Barrier Tower earned themselves a low-grade, Divine-Class weapon. It was a serious prize for warriors at their level, but reaching that height had been no easy feat, and the list of qualifiers was short.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]
Finally, the old man read out the most anticipated list: the names of those who had reached a staggering ten thousand miles. They were each entitled to a Tenth Tribulation Introducing Pill, a prize far more valuable than any weapon.
[SFX: MYSTICAL CHIME]
Of course, both Zane and Layla were on that list. After the small group gathered, the old man pulled out an alabaster pot and opened it, revealing the pills meant for those about to face their tenth Heavenly Tribulation. If they could pass it and unlock their Soul Sea, their power would skyrocket, securing their place in the Upper World.
[MUSIC: QUIET UNEASE]
Looking around at the gathered group, Zane noted that everyone else here was either Divine Sea Realm or had already reached the ninth level of the Life-and-Death Realm. He was the only one at the fifth level. It put a small dent in his mood—a reminder of just how far ahead of him some of these warriors already were.
[SFX: COLD HISS]
“These pills lose their potency fast once they’re out of the pot,” the old man said, his breath fogging in the cold air rolling off it. “The moment it touches air, the clock starts. Don’t sit around admiring it—swallow it the second it’s in your hand, or it’ll be about as useful as a rock.”
“Wait—so the tenth tribulation really is the ceiling?” one of the cultivators nearby asked. “I’ve never heard of anyone going past it.”
The old man’s expression didn’t change. “The tenth is about as close to perfect as it gets. But... there may be someone out there, somewhere, who’s pulled an eleventh. It’s the kind of thing legends are made of, but it doesn’t concern any of you.”
[SFX: MYSTICAL CHIME]
Pills shot out from the pot one by one, dropping into open palms—each one a strange, faceted brown shape, cold to the touch. Zane turned his over once. He was already close to breaking through to the next level on his own; in his hands, the pill was almost a formality. A faint, wry smile crossed his face.
Layla caught the look and grinned. “Oh, come on—hand it over. I want to see what happens if I take two of these back-to-back. Maybe I’ll pull an eleventh myself.”
The old man only gave her a flat look and a slight shake of his head, clearly unimpressed.
“I’ll trade you for it,” Layla pressed, turning to Zane. “A Divine-Class weapon, straight up. Fair deal, right?”
Zane just shook his head, mouth curving slightly. “No need. I can afford to waste this one.”
“Suit yourself.” Layla shrugged it off easily enough.
One by one, the cultivators swallowed their pills. Zane popped his into his mouth without ceremony.
The world’s energy here ran thick and rich—the Orange Sea Realm was good ground for a tribulation, with the palace masters standing watch over the proceedings. The strongest of the cultivators spread out across an open stretch of land, putting some distance between each other before settling cross-legged to let the pill’s power take hold.
The pill could call down a tribulation, but it wasn’t guaranteed—there was always a chance it simply wouldn’t take. And even for those it worked on, the timing varied from one cultivator to the next.
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
“I can’t believe it—Jasper’s starting his tenth tribulation already,” someone muttered in the crowd. “That looks intense.”
“What did you expect? The guy’s no slouch,” another person shot back. “He placed third in the Law Storm test. If it weren’t for monsters like Zane and Layla, he’d have been number one.”
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL WONDER]
“Look! The lotus flower! It’s a nine-petal!”
Sure enough, a shimmering, nine-petaled lotus flower materialized above Jasper’s head, spinning slowly in the air as it bloomed. 
By any normal standard, his talent and strength were well above most of the other young warriors in the Alliance, and he should have been the center of attention. But with Zane and Layla in the mix, he was completely overshadowed. Even the palace masters and the Lord of the Sunset World barely gave him a second glance.
[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]
[AMBIENT: CRACKLING LIGHTNING]
A deafening boom ripped through the air as Jasper’s tribulation began in earnest. Divine lightning crackled from the sky, followed by tendrils of poison that swirled through an unnerving mist of shadows. It was, by all accounts, an extraordinary Lesser Celestial Tribulation.
The palace masters, however, watched from the sidelines with casual indifference. For warriors of their caliber, a trial of this magnitude was hardly worth batting an eye over.
“Jasper’s tribulation is impressive,” one of the palace masters remarked calmly. “He’ll be a top contender once he reaches the Soul Sea Realm. Still… it makes you wonder what Layla’s will look like.”
