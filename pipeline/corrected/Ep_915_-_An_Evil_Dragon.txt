Ep 915 - An Evil Dragon

Word count: 1099

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

War King York had always believed his destiny was to ascend to the upper world, his future limitless. So how, how, could he be dying like this?

Ever since Dorothea’s power had paralyzed him, his mind had become a maelstrom. This was the single greatest crisis of his life, a feeling of helplessness that dwarfed even the time he’d hunted a young Deepswallow in the crushing depths of the ocean. 

He knew, with a soul so battered, he couldn't hope to fight back. No matter how hard he racked his brain, he couldn't find a single path to survival.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]

So when Dorothea eased the pressure, he didn’t scream as the other warriors had. He understood this was his only chance. He had to make them see him not as a victim, but as a dignified emperor ready to pay any price for his own skin. And he was going to take it.

[SFX: FISHING ROD WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS CRATER]

Wade gave the fishing rod in his hand a light flick. This time, he wasn't just tossing the bodies into the crater. He planned to hang them right over the edge, a gruesome lure for whatever was lurking below. The rod’s tip was no thicker than a pinky, the thin green line as fine as a strand of hair, yet it held the weight of six or seven bodies without even straining.

“An Emperor, huh?” Wade chuckled, the sound sharp in the tense air. “Just how big is this York Divine Kingdom of yours?”

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

“Thirty million acres of land! Four billion people!” War King York yelled, his voice strained with desperation. Suspended in midair, he had a perfect, horrifying view into the massive crater. 

[SFX: DEMONIC GROWL]

At the very bottom, a pair of huge, demonic red eyes stared back. He was running out of time. “I’ll give you my throne! I will make you the emperor of the York Divine Kingdom!”

“Thirty million acres,” Wade repeated, his tone a mix of disbelief and amusement. “I mean, how big is that, really? It sounds big.”

The York Divine Kingdom wasn't small; of the original four divine kingdoms, it was the largest. But to Wade, it was clearly a joke.

From a short distance away, Dorothea commented calmly, “Thirty million acres is about one ten-millionth the size of a Saint in the Demi-Immortal World. It's not even as big as my garden. These people really are narrow-minded.”

“If that's not enough — take the whole continent,” York said, his desperation cracking through. “All four kingdoms combined. Whatever you want.”

Even as the words left his mouth, he knew. Nothing he had could tempt these people. There was nothing left to offer.

[SFX: GROUND TREMBLING]

[MUSIC: RISING DREAD]

The ground began to shake again.

Dorothea's eyes lit up. “Boss — it's moving. It can't hold back any longer. It's working!”

Vorn nodded. “Everyone, stay sharp. It's coming out.”

All seven Demi-Immortals shifted into fighting stances at the crater's edge. Even they weren't relaxed. Whatever was down there had earned their full attention.

York watched the red eyes below him rising. Getting closer.

[SFX: MASSIVE ROAR]

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

A pure black dragon's head exploded out of the pit.

It was unlike any dragon Zane had ever imagined. True dragons carried a natural majesty — powerful but composed, almost peaceful. This thing was the opposite. Six pairs of sharp horns jutted from its skull. Its eyes blazed blood-red, pouring out an evil aura so thick that any ordinary warrior caught in it would lose their mind on the spot.

[SFX: SNAPPING JAWS]

The dragon's mouth opened wide, lunging for the corpses dangling above it.

York's face collapsed into pure despair. There was no escaping this.

Then the fishing line snapped taut. Wade flicked his wrist and hauled York and the others straight up, jerking them out of reach at the last possible moment. The dragon's jaws closed on empty air.

Wade hadn't done it to save them. He was purely pulling the lure higher, drawing the dragon further out of the pit.

It worked. The dragon was young, powerful, but not yet cunning. It stretched its body upward, enormous head rising, chasing the dangling figures.

[SFX: BODIES HITTING GROUND]

Wade swung the rod and flung York and the others clear, dumping them onto the ground in a heap. “It's up to you guys,” he said.

The burly man was already moving.

[SFX: SCYTHE IGNITING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT DRUMS]

He leaped from the crater's edge, the fiery red scythe erupting in flames as he brought it around in a massive arc toward the dragon's neck.

“Ha! Break!”

[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]

The blow landed. The power behind it was staggering — even Zane felt it from a distance, and he couldn't begin to estimate the full force of it. The scythe itself had to be a divine-class weapon. And yet when it connected with the dragon's scales, it produced nothing but a shower of sparks and the shriek of metal on metal. The neck didn't even flinch.

Zane stared. “What kind of dragon is this?”

Brianna didn't answer.

He glanced toward her. She had gone completely still, her eyes fixed on the creature below, her expression unreadable.

“Hey.” Nothing. “Hey.”

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

After a long moment, she spoke, her voice barely above a murmur. “Those drakes… they were summoned by this thing. I never thought — after all this time trapped here — I never thought I'd actually live to see an Evil Dragon.”

“The Evil Dragon is the antithesis of the Ancestral Dragon,” she said. “It’s a being born from the faith of my people.”

“A faith-based creation?” Zane asked, completely lost.

She tried to explain. “The thing is, neither the Ancestral Dragon nor the Evil Dragon is technically real. They’re manifestations—beings created by the collective imagination of my people.”

“Beings created from imagination?” That just made even less sense to him.

The girl seemed to understand his confusion. As a warrior from the Lower World, Zane couldn’t be expected to know how things worked up here. 

She spoke gently. “It’s not as strange as it sounds. It’s the same principle as what you call Visualization. You focus on an image in your mind, and with enough Vitality, you can make it real, right?”

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL WHISPERS]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

She leaned in, her voice dropping. “So, imagine if an entire race focuses on the same idea, all at once. That’s what happened with a secret order of humans. They got a trillion people to all visualize the same thing—what they called ‘faith,’ of course. For a hundred thousand years, they all believed in an imaginary sword blank. Eventually, their combined belief actually brought it into existence. Then, the most skilled weaponsmith of their time used it to forge a legendary weapon.”

Zane was only half listening. His eyes were still on the dragon.
