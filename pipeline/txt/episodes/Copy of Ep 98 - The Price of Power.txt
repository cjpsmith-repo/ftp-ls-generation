Ep 98 - The Price of Power
Word Count: 1072
[AMBIENT: EXCITED AUCTION HALL]
The Sky-sunder Axe ultimately fetched a staggering five thousand Crystal Cubes, drawing gasps from several bidders throughout the auction hall. Zane merely smirked, unbothered that another collector had claimed the impressive weapon. His eyes reflected calm calculation rather than disappointment.
"Their loss, my gain," he thought to himself, mentally tallying the resources he could acquire instead.
He could easily procure a serviceable magic weapon from the Capital's marketplace for merely a tenth of that exorbitant sum. The craftsmanship might lack prestige, but it would serve his purposes adequately. Besides, what truly captivated Zane's interest wasn't the weapons' raw destructive potential or their prestigious origins, but rather the Celestial Essence contained within their Spirit Weapon cores—a resource far more valuable to his unique cultivation path than any flashy armament with an inflated price tag.
[MUSIC: SERIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT]
After quickly auctioning off a few more items, Alec's tone turned serious. "And now for the main event of this morning's auction," he announced, his voice dropping. "As you all know, two years ago, The Capital lost its Master Weaponsmith, Barrett.
It was a devastating loss for the entire Empire. Fortunately, his legacy lives on through his many students, some of whom have stepped up to continue his work."
Alec paused, letting the weight of his words settle. "Next up for auction is Barrett's personal journal: the Principles of Forging. It's said he guarded its secrets with his life. For anyone who dreams of making a name for themselves in weaponsmithing, this book is the key. It holds a lifetime of Barrett's knowledge—the very techniques that will allow its owner to achieve mastery!"
[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD WHISPERS]
[MUSIC: RISING ANTICIPATION]
As Alec concluded his introduction, a palpable wave of excitement rippled through the audience. Hushed whispers erupted into animated conversations, with attendees leaning forward in their seats, eyes gleaming with anticipation.
The Empire boasted three Master Weaponsmiths, yet none commanded the reverence and admiration that Barrett had inspired during his lifetime. His legendary "Principles of Forging" represented the pinnacle of weaponsmithing knowledge—a treasure trove containing every intricate technique he had perfected throughout his illustrious career. Zane observed the hungry expressions around him, understanding immediately why the atmosphere had shifted. Every Legacy House harbored the ambitious dream of claiming a Master Weaponsmith for their lineage, a dream that typically remained unfulfilled despite vast fortunes and influence.
"Barrett's genius was unmatched," whispered an elderly gentleman nearby, his voice tinged with nostalgia. "I once witnessed him forge a blade that could slice through granite as though it were parchment."
But they were nearly impossible to secure. House Warrick had one of the three great masters under contract, the Skyreach Sect had another, and the third worked exclusively for the Sunstone Citadel. No amount of money from the other Great Houses could buy their loyalty.
So, the chance to cultivate their own Master Weaponsmith? That would be a complete game-changer. Everyone knew just owning the book wasn't a guarantee—after all, Barrett had taught dozens of students, and none had reached his level. Still, not a single Great House was about to let this opportunity pass them by.
"That book belonged to our master! Return it immediately!" cried a passionate voice from the crowd.
"It's his personal property! You have no right to auction his legacy!" echoed several others, their faces flushed with indignation as they rose in unison from their seats.
[SFX: RUSTLING CLOAKS]
Eight figures stood defiantly, their matching crimson cloaks billowing slightly as they confronted the auctioneer. Each cloak bore an intricately embroidered furnace emblem on the chest—a symbol instantly recognizable to most attendees. These were devoted disciples from the College of Artifice, the prestigious institution Barrett himself had established with his own hands and nurtured through decades of dedication.
[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD]
The auctioneer acknowledged their protest with a sympathetic but firm glance before returning his attention to the proceedings. Despite the modest physical appearance of the journal—merely several weathered sheets of parchment bound in simple leather—its value transcended its humble form. As the bidding escalated beyond twenty thousand Crystal Cubes, faces throughout the chamber fell in disappointment. Wealthy merchants and minor nobles reluctantly lowered their placards, their dreams of acquiring Barrett's wisdom evaporating before their eyes. Only the truly affluent could continue competing for such an astronomical sum.
The disciples of the College watched helplessly, their expressions shifting from outrage to despair as the price of their master's intellectual treasure climbed beyond their collective means to reclaim it.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
The bidding war came down to two men: Felix and an old man who had been going head-to-head with him from the start.
[SFX: LOUD DECLARATION]
"Twenty thousand!" Felix declared, his voice ringing out. He'd just jumped the bid by a full six thousand Crystal Cubes.
[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]
At the sound of the number, the old man flinched and slowly shook his head. It wasn't a matter of will; he simply couldn't go any higher. There weren't many people in the world who could drop twenty thousand Crystal Cubes on a whim.
Seeing the old man's crestfallen expression, Felix allowed himself a wry smile. His opponent had been relentless, but that last jump had finally done the trick.
"Do I hear any other bids?" Alec called out from the stage. "Going once... going twice... If not, the "Principles of Forging" goes to Mister Felix!"
Just then, a calm voice cut through the silence from one of the side balconies.
"Twenty thousand, one hundred."
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
The speaker was Zane. His sudden entry into the bidding—with such a massive number—left Rhys and Ethan completely stunned.
"Zane, are you out of your mind?" Rhys hissed.
"Do you even "have" that many Crystal Cubes?" Ethan whispered, sweat breaking out on his forehead.
They'd merely accompanied Dustin Blackwood to the auction house seeking a glimpse into the lifestyle of the elite, a taste of excitement beyond their mundane existence. The treasures on display commanded prices of hundreds, even thousands of Crystal Cubes—sums so astronomical that ordinary Outer Disciples could scarcely comprehend them, let alone acquire such wealth.
Zane's unexpected bid of twenty thousand, one hundred Crystal Cubes struck them with the force of a physical assault. This was no frivolous entertainment; the auction operated by ironclad rules. One couldn't casually toss out figures for amusement. Winners who failed to produce payment faced consequences beyond imagination—punishments that could destroy not just fortunes, but lives.
