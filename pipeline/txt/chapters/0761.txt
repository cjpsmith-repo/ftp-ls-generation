After that, the Divine Core girl registered Luo Zheng's name one by one.
Initially, Luo Zheng was worried that the girl would ask about Luo Zheng's background. He had discussed it with Cao Junfeng before this, and he had simply made up an identity.
Cao Junfeng told Luo Zheng not to worry, the Divine Nation wouldn't ask about his background, he could just treat him as an independent warrior who lived in seclusion.
After all, for the Black Iron Divine Nation, it didn't matter who he was. What mattered was that he had enough strength to fight for the resources in the Heavenly Feather Saint Ocean. Once he obtained those resources, the Divine Nation would get a share as well. Therefore, they didn't care about where he came from.
Besides, the Divine Nation was surrounded by the ocean, and for the past tens of thousands of years, all the warriors had come from the Divine Nation itself. There were very few visitors from other realms. In the eyes of the Divine Nation's Master, all the warriors from the Divine Nation belonged to the Divine Nation.
Therefore, Luo Zheng answered all the questions asked by the Divine Core girl. However, when Luo Zheng's name was mentioned, he hesitated for a moment, then he replied, "My name is Luo Tianxing."
The name Luo Zheng wasn't suitable in the Divine Nation, it was mainly used to guard against the Great Yu Divine Nation's Holy Maiden.
After finishing the registration, the girl smiled, then she sealed all the information in a bamboo plate. After that, she handed it over to Luo Zheng. "The first test will be held three days from now. At that time, all the warriors from the Martial Palace and independent warriors will participate. I hope you'll have a good performance!"
Although the Divine Core girl's words sounded pleasant, she had actually made a conclusion about Luo Zheng's fate. This warrior named Luo Tianxing was destined to be a supporting character, and he would never be able to escape his fate of being eliminated.
The competition in the Divine Nation was extremely fierce.
There were less than 50 spots in the Profound Jade Martial Palace, which meant 50 Black Iron Tokens.
In the Profound Jade Martial House alone, there were close to six hundred Nihility Tribulation Realm martial artists competing for the spot. Most of these Nihility Tribulation Realm martial artists were at the late stage of the Nihility Tribulation Realm. Their cultivation was much higher than this young man in front of him. Not to mention, the martial artists cultivated by the Profound Jade Martial House were much stronger than those independent martial artists!
Not only that, among the 50 places, some princes also participated in the competition. For example, Prince Yan, who had just finished his registration, was almost certain to get a Black Iron Token.
In addition, there were 200 to 300 independent martial artists like Luo Tianxing. Independent martial artists dared to sign up for the assessment and fight for the Black Iron Token. Their cultivations were basically at the peak of the late-stage False Tribulation Realm.
Although the youth in front of her seemed to have a good foundation and was already a martial artist at the middle stage of the False Tribulation Realm at the age of twenty, it was still too early for him to obtain the Black Iron Token.
The girl only thought this in her heart, but Luo Zheng also saw the meaning behind her modest smile.
Luo Zheng just glanced at him. How could he, who could see through people's minds, not understand?
Of course, he didn't care about this young girl's thoughts. He returned a smile to the young girl, put the bamboo token into his bag, and left the Profound Jade Martial House.
Three days later, Luo Zheng once again entered the Profound Jade Martial House. After showing the bamboo token in his hand, there was a person to guide him into the depths of the Martial House.
What appeared in front of Luo Zheng was a large square. There were already many martial artists gathered in the square, and each of them was emitting the aura of the False Tribulation Realm.
When Luo Zheng's gaze moved to the center of the square, his eyes suddenly focused. Almighty Divine Sea Realm experts! Moreover, there were three of them!
The Divine Nations really lived up to their reputation. Just a Martial House alone had three almighty Divine Sea Realm experts. Moreover, it was very likely that the Profound Jade Martial House had more than three almighty Divine Sea Realm experts!
However, the Profound Jade Martial House could be regarded as a sixth-grade sect. Even if it was separated from the Divine Nations, it could still be regarded as a Holy Land.
In the Sea God Continent, the human race's Purple Heart Holy Land could only be regarded as a sixth-grade Holy Land. It also had three almighty Divine Sea Realm experts. Based on this calculation, the Profound Jade Martial House could indeed be compared to a "sixth-grade Holy Land". Such a Martial House could only be regarded as a subordinate of the Black Iron Divine Nation. The number of experts in the four Divine Nations was indeed innumerable!
No wonder for so many years, they had regarded the Central Domain as a barren land. There were also very few Divine Nations' martial artists who were willing to go to the Central Domain.
Thinking back, when he encountered the Divine Nation Prince and the Divine Nation's Holy Land in the Heaven Mist Immortal Tomb, they were disdainful of the Central Domain. The pride and arrogance on their faces were also backed by strength.
Even so, Luo Zheng didn't shrink back in the slightest.
As the saying goes, the heart is as still as water. Luo Zheng's state of mind was incomparably calm. He knew that the road ahead was difficult and miserable, but he still pressed forward with indomitable will. There was no other choice. This was his character, and also fate.
After entering the square, the person who guided Luo Zheng told him to wait here and then floated away.
Around the square stood about a thousand False Tribulation Realm martial artists. After entering the False Tribulation Realm, one could be considered an accomplished expert. They all maintained a certain level of vigilance between each other, but they didn't reveal any hostility. On the contrary, most of them had a calm and composed appearance.
If one casually revealed hostility to others, not only would it attract some unnecessary trouble, but it would also put one at a disadvantage — only the weak would glare at others all day long. Weren't experts all indifferent?
Because of this, the atmosphere was somewhat strange.
Seeing this scene, a knowing smile appeared on Luo Zheng's face. He also stood in a corner with his hands behind his back.
Who knew that as soon as he stood still, he felt a gaze sweeping towards him. He turned his head and saw that it was the young "King Yan".
It seemed that even though he had been conferred the title of King, this King Yan still wanted to participate in the assessment himself and work hard to obtain the Black Iron Token!
What Cao Junfeng said was true. Even the prince of a Divine Nation wouldn't receive too much preferential treatment in this world where experts were as common as clouds. He still had to rely on himself to fight for everything.
The Divine Nation's approach was actually correct. Why could the Monarch control a Divine Nation? After all, he relied on his own strength.
If the princes and princesses behind him were too lazy to cultivate because of the Monarch, who would be able to inherit the position of Monarch after the death of the first Monarch? It would probably be taken away by other powerful Divine Sea Realm experts in an instant.
Only by constantly nurturing Divine Sea Realm experts, and also the outstanding ones among the Divine Sea Realm experts, could the Divine Nation continue to exist!
Noticing that the King Yan was staring at him, Luo Zheng gave him a faint smile.
The King Yan just glanced at Luo Zheng coldly and then shifted his gaze elsewhere.
After a while, when everyone had arrived, a deacon handed over a name list. One of the three Divine Sea Realm experts who were in charge of the assessment said, "The preliminary assessment for the Black Iron Token has begun. The content of this assessment is the simplest. Everyone will enter the square in batches. If you squeeze your opponent out of the red line with your own strength, you will be the winner! You can fight in the square, but you can't kill! Violators will be severely punished! "
As the Divine Sea Realm expert spoke, he reached out his hand and patted the ground lightly. Colorful patterns appeared on the ground in the center of the square.
The ground of the square was made of some unknown metal. It looked extremely heavy. The outermost circle of the patterns was arranged with a circle of red lines.
After hearing the rules announced by the Divine Sea Realm expert, many warriors of the False Tribulation Realm didn't have much expression on their faces.
Seeing this scene, Luo Zheng recalled the day when he had just entered the Blue Cloud Sect. Every time he heard the content of the assessment, many warriors would discuss it animatedly.
In comparison, the quality of the thousands of False Tribulation Realm warriors in front of him was naturally different. Divine geniuses like Luo Zheng and the King Yan, who had entered the False Tribulation Realm at a young age, were different. Most of the warriors of the False Tribulation Realm were not young, ranging from dozens to hundreds of years old. There were even warriors of the False Tribulation Realm who were hundreds of years old.
Naturally, they wouldn't act like rookies who had just entered the martial arts world and act like they were curious about everything.
Soon, the Divine Sea Realm expert began to announce the name list of the first round of assessment.
In this round of assessment, 30 people would participate. Among the 30 people, only three would survive. That was to say, 90% of the people would be eliminated.
Luo Zheng closed his eyes slightly and listened attentively to the names announced by the Divine Sea Realm expert. Soon, the 30 names were announced, but he didn't hear the words "Luo Tianxing". It seemed that he wouldn't be in the first round.
There were advantages and disadvantages to being called in the first round. The disadvantage was that they weren't familiar with the rules and the environment. After observing the rules, the latecomers could take precautions in advance. The advantage was obvious. Since no one was familiar with the rules, they could win in the chaos!
Soon, 30 warriors of the False Tribulation Realm entered the square at the same time and entered the red circle in the center of the square.
