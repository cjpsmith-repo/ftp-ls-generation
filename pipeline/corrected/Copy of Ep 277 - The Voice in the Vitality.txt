Ep 277 - The Voice in the Vitality
Word count: 1164
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION] 
Johanson struggled to take in this new turn of events. Saint Augustus’ voice resonated through his Vitality, commanding and uncompromising.
The original plan had been elegantly simple: Johanson would kill Zane under the guise of an unfortunate accident. Fatal mishaps weren’t uncommon in high-level matches between masters of their caliber. With both of them wielding such devastating power, the first strike needed to be decisive and final. Any moment of hesitation would create an opportunity for Zane to retaliate and claim victory.
During intense combat, it's almost impossible to pull back a full-force attack once it’s been launched. Johanson’s fighting philosophy was built entirely on deadly opening maneuvers—swift, merciless, and overwhelming. This approach made an "accidental" death the perfect cover for a calculated assassination in the arena. The audience would witness merely another tragic consequence of power unrestrained, while Zane’s promising future would be permanently extinguished before their eyes.
[SFX: TENSION RISING]
On paper, Saint Augustus’ plan was flawless. But no one had counted on Zane.
Johanson had been confident his soul attack would be more than enough to devastate his opponent, only to find himself reeling from Zane’s unexpected counterattack. The tables turned dramatically as Zane’s soul technique struck with precision and devastating force.
Even more astonishing was how Zane’s shattered dagger utterly outperformed Johanson’s prized snake-shaped short sword, a weapon he had spent years perfecting. The disparity in their weapons’ quality left Johanson speechless, his confidence crumbling with each passing moment.
"How could this possibly be happening?" Johanson thought, cold sweat beading on his brow as realization dawned.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC IMPACT]
The truth crashed upon them like a thunderbolt—Zane had deliberately restrained himself in his previous matches. His earlier victories, impressive as they were, represented a fraction of his actual power.
With the devastating gap in their abilities now exposed, the carefully crafted plan to fake an accidental death fell apart. Johanson trembled as he felt Zane’s murderous intent radiating toward him in waves. His instincts screamed danger as his heart hammered against his ribs. For the first time in years, genuine fear gripped him. He knew, with absolute certainty, that he would die if he didn’t concede right away.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
But Saint Augustus’ voice cut through his panic.
"Keep fighting, Johanson!" he commanded, his tone leaving no room for argument.
Johanson groaned in frustration as he processed his demand. 
He wanted to refuse. But he couldn't find the words. It was Augustus who had protected him when the Shadow Group fell — who had argued for his admission to the Skyreach Sect when no one else would, and elevated him to inner disciple on top of it. 
How could Saint Augustus possibly expect him to continue a hopeless battle against this monster? Zane’s broken blade had already done serious damage. What else was he capable of? Johanson didn’t want to wait around to find out.
"Master, if I don’t surrender, he’ll kill me!" Johanson shot back through his Vitality, his mental voice quivering with undisguised terror. His fingers trembled as he clutched his weapon, eyes darting frantically between Zane’s imposing figure and the rapidly diminishing protection of his own techniques.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
Zane was already ripping through his shadow defense. It was just a matter of seconds before it shattered completely, and then it would be too late to run.
Inside Johanson's shadow, unseen by anyone in the arena, a vortex had begun to form. It was hidden within the same patch of darkness Johanson was using for cover — tucked so perfectly out of sight that neither Zane nor the crowd had any chance to react before it was ready to fire.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE — BARELY AUDIBLE, BUILDING]
"Don’t worry," Saint Augustus' voice echoed in his mind. "I have another plan. I can seize control of the arena's magic array and use it to strike Zane. All you have to do is make it look like the attack is coming from you. I'm sure we can kill him in one hit!"
"You can really do that? Aren’t you worried the elders will find out we’re cheating?" Doubt flooded Johanson’s eyes as he watched Zane tear his shadows to shreds.
To be fair, the All Peaks Competition wasn’t always on the level. Tracy, for example, had bent the rules, but the Referee let it slide because she hadn't crossed the line the elders had drawn.
But this was something else entirely. Saint Augustus was talking about hijacking the arena’s magic to commit murder. If the elders found out, the consequences would be dire.
Johanson wasn’t sold, but he didn’t see a way to refuse Saint Augustus. His ties to the Shadow Group were the biggest reason he'd gotten this far. But the group had been reeling since their last defeat; a Saint wouldn’t normally waste his energy on a low-level disciple.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BUILDUP]
Today was different. Saint Augustus had been conserving his power, all to kill Zane with a single, overwhelming blow.
There was no way Zane could block a direct attack from a master at the peak of the Soul Realm. But that didn’t matter. He still had to try.
Zane's will to fight burned brighter than any other disciple's. Even though he suspected someone was helping Johanson, even though he felt like a rabbit being hunted by a lion, he stood his ground, fearless and determined to win.
Fear was a useless emotion, a shield the weak used to hide from the shame of defeat.
[SFX: ENERGY SUMMONING]
Zane summoned his six Shadow Phantoms, lining them up to block the incoming purple light.
[SFX: MAGICAL CRACKLING]
[SFX: ENERGY DISINTEGRATION]
The phantoms, powerful in their own right, disintegrated the instant the light touched them.
Zane knew it was no use. He couldn’t handle an attack on this level.
If he'd seen it coming, he might have pre-emptively used his Corrupt Vitality to devour the shadows and build a counterattack. But even that wouldn't have been enough. This was the full, concentrated force of a Soul Realm master — not a casual strike, not a warning. Whoever had fired it had been saving up for this exact moment.
With his phantoms gone, he fell back on his last resort: his shattered dagger. He hurled it straight into the heart of the light.
The sharp blade sliced the beam in two. It didn’t matter. The twin streams of purple light kept coming, rocketing right for him.
Out of options, Zane scrambled to dodge.
The crowd gasped, a wave of anxiety washing over them. People gripped their seats, their minds racing to understand what they were seeing.
"Oh no! Zane can’t take it!"
"Johanson’s going all out! Is he sacrificing his own life force for that attack?"
"If that’s Johanson’s real power, he could win the whole competition! I mean, who could stop that? Finn? Patrick? Not even Tracy or Wynn! No one in this tournament could block an attack like that!"
But just as everyone braced for Zane's death, a tremendous roar echoed from the mountains nearby.
[SFX: BOOMING VOICE]
"HOW DARE YOU!"
The voice boomed through the valleys, a deep, resonant rumble like an ancient beast waking from a thousand-year sleep.
