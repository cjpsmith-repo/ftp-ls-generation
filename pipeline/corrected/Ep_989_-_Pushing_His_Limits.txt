Ep 989 - Pushing His Limits
Word count: 1123
[MUSIC: TENDER PIANO]
Nine steps in, barely a fraction of the way there, and already Zane was bleeding from a dozen wounds.
Just then, Cora sauntered over, pulling out a delicate, white silk handkerchief to gently dab the sweat from Zane’s brow.
It only took a few pats for the pristine silk to be ruined, stained with blood and grime.
Cora just smiled, her touch surprisingly gentle as she cleaned his face. “You can do this.”
She wasn’t as frantic as Tia and Isla. It wasn’t that she didn’t care—far from it. She just liked seeing Zane pushed to his limits. In Cora’s eyes, a man was never more attractive than when he was fighting through hell and coming out the other side.
Zane gave Cora a grateful nod and reached for the pills in his tunic. But Tia was a step ahead, already holding out a milky-white pill. This one was priceless, a guaranteed restorative from Cloud Hall itself. She pressed it into his hand.
[SFX: MAGICAL HEALING WHOOSH]
Zane tossed the pill into his mouth and swallowed. The moment he did, the potent medicine, combined with his own rapid healing, went to work. The bloody gashes were knitting themselves closed before their eyes. Though the wounds had looked gruesome, none were particularly deep. In moments, Zane was as good as new, the only evidence of the ordeal being the shredded holes the blood arrows had torn in his clothes.
[MUSIC: EPIC REALIZATION]
Zane finally understood. This path wasn’t about danger; it was a pure test of endurance. It didn’t matter if you were a Soul Sea Realm master or a legendary Spirit Supreme—the trial was the same for anyone who dared to walk it.
The only way forward was to push himself to the absolute brink.
Turning back would be easy. All he had to do was walk away. But that would mean everything he’d endured so far was for nothing.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED ORCHESTRAL BUILD]
“I’m not afraid of some little test,” he murmured, his resolve hardening. His vision narrowed, focused on the thousands of steps still ahead. The path was brutal, but he didn’t even flinch.
“Let’s go!”
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[SFX: BLOOD SPLATTER]
Zane let the power of the Dragon Scales surge through him again. With every agonizing step, fresh blood spurted from his body.
This time, he made it ten more steps. The price was twelve new wounds.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
The second he stopped, Tia was at his side, pushing another pill into his mouth. “This is an Ice Muscle Pill. I’ve got about forty of them, and maybe fifty Rejuvenating Pills,” she muttered, her voice tight with worry. “I don’t know if it’ll be enough. There have to be at least three thousand footprints…” She trailed off, wondering if her entire collection of elixirs could get him to the end.
Hearing this, Cora quickly rummaged through her own Storage Ring. But instead of medicine, she only found neatly folded outfits in various styles and a few pretty trinkets. Not a single pill.
“I still have some,” Isla offered quietly.
Tia just scoffed, her eyes flashing.
Isla’s face went red. She didn’t argue. The best pill she carried was fifth-grade — she knew exactly how that compared to what Tia had in her hands.
Saul cleared his throat. “Pity the alchemy room isn’t open yet. I could’ve helped.” He paused, something distant crossing his expression. “Actually… when the Master left, there was a furnace mid-brew in there. It’s been going ever since.” He glanced upward, as if calculating. “No idea what state it’s in now.”
The Eternal Scribe looked at him sharply. Zane filed the information away without a word.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL STRINGS]
[SFX: GENTLE EMBRACE]
Cora stepped forward, wrapping her arms around Zane and pulling him into a warm hug. He sagged against her, the ordeal having left him completely drained.
“You smell like blood,” she murmured, her face close to his.
His own voice was weak. “What’s this for?” He didn’t pull away, leaning into her embrace.
Cora smiled. “Moral support.”
Zane let out a weak chuckle. “You really think I’d quit?”
She shook her head, tilting her face up to look at him, her eyes shining with absolute certainty. “No. My husband is a mighty warrior. Giving up isn’t something you do.”
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
Of course, it still stung that Zane had held such a grand wedding for Tia back at Cloud Hall. But Cora soothed the bitterness with a private fantasy: her own wedding, once she joined him in the upper world, would be a thousand times grander. She buried her jealousy under the weight of her ambition.
She’d invite every powerful master in the universe. Her guest list would be a who’s who of legendary figures, from the old man to Elder Apeiron. She’d even have all the Supreme Lords attend—a title she hadn’t even heard of before meeting the Elder, but that didn’t matter. It was a plan so audacious that the denizens of the upper world would be left speechless.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED ORCHESTRAL]
Zane smiled down at her, patting her head. “Of course I won’t,” he said. He gently stepped out of her embrace and turned back to the path.
He changed into a fresh robe and stepped back onto the footprints. Within a hundred steps, it was soaked through and shredded, indistinguishable from the last one.
Two hours passed.
Zane reached the halfway point — one thousand seven hundred steps behind him, roughly the same ahead.
The Eternal Scribe shook his head slowly. “This test is too cruel. Three thousand steps… any warrior can push to their limit once or twice. But three thousand times? That’s not a test of strength. That’s a test of will.”
Saul, for once, agreed. “One or two hundred steps would have proved the point. I don’t know why the Master needed it to be this long.”
Neither of them said what they were actually thinking: they weren’t sure Zane could finish.
Zane’s face had gone pale. His legs felt like lead. The blood loss was mounting — even with a Relic-Class body and a steady supply of high-grade pills, blood wasn’t infinite. He was too exhausted to think. There was nothing in his vision but the next footprint, and the one after that, each one looking up at him like a small, patient demon.
He didn’t stop.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]
[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]
There was no real danger here. It was just a grueling, relentless trial that demanded he shatter his limits with every single step. Three thousand footprints. That meant shattering his limits three thousand times.
Even a Relic-Class weapon would probably be ground to dust under that kind of pressure.
