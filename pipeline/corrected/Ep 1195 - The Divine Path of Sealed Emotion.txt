Ep 1195 - The Divine Path of Sealed Emotion

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]
The power radiating from the blade made the color drain from Gus’s face. 
[SFX: PSYCHIC SCREAM]
A silent scream tore through his mind.
[SFX: SWORD CLATTER]
[SFX: BODY SCRAMBLING ON GROUND]
In a split second, he dropped his sword and threw himself backward, scrambling to get away from the crushing force.
[SFX: MASSIVE ENERGY SURGE]
But when Zane unleashed the full might of the Gods-Intimidating Strike, not even he could control the immense power he’d summoned.
[SFX: ENERGY PIERCING FLESH]
[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]
[SFX: SHIMMERING DISSOLUTION]
Gus twisted mid-air, his strange movement technique blurring his outline — but he wasn’t fast enough. A few drops of golden blood scattered as the strike grazed him. 
He reappeared a few feet away, breathing hard, and forced a bitter smile.
“We can talk,” he said. “I have something to tell you—”
Zane was already moving.
[SFX: SWORD THRUST]
[SFX: ENERGY PIERCING FLESH]
[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]
The piercing energy shot straight for Gus, impaling him. It passed through his chest as if it were air, exiting from his back in a spray of golden blood. The droplets scattered like shimmering petals before dissolving into wisps of dark and light energy that slowly faded away.
“You should’ve done that ages ago!”
[MUSIC: GENTLE MELODY]
From a distance, Freya watched, a small smile forming as she exhaled in relief.
In her heart, she felt that Zane was invincible. Aside from World Lords and Supreme Lords, when had he ever lost to someone at the Soul Sea or Spirit Supreme Realms?
Unlike Lavender and Livingston - who’d immediately recognized Gus’s immense power - Freya couldn’t sense the danger. She’d only seen some unarmed man repeatedly disarming Zane, who seemed unable to counter, and it made her incredibly nervous.
Gus knew Zane was a fellow apprentice, yet attacked without mercy. The guy was clearly bad news.But when Zane’s devastating strike finally landed, her racing heart began to slow.
[MUSIC: TENSE REALIZATION]
The smile on Gus’s face froze.
Even at the Soul Sea Realm, Gus possessed a sharp eye for technique.When Zane unleashed his sword intent, Gus recognized the Divine Path of Sealed Emotion - a path requiring complete emotional shutdown during execution. Warriors shouldn’t even begin grasping it until reaching World Lord status. Even Supreme Lords couldn’t fully master it. Yet the aura radiating from Zane’s blade felt authentic.
*Is this kid really only at the Soul Sea Realm?*
Gus had a sinking feeling Zane was a lot more than he let on.
He stopped. But Zane didn’t.
[SFX: SWORD THRUST]
[SFX: FLESH TEARING]
[SFX: GOLDEN BLOOD SPLATTER]
Strike after strike. The Gods-Intimidating Strike again and again, instant and relentless, each one fired the moment the last landed. Gus twisted and flickered, his strange movement technique keeping his vitals just out of reach — but only just. Each dodge left more of him behind. A gash across his leg, deep enough to show bone. A slice through his side. Golden blood darkening the path beneath his feet.
“I have something important to tell you—” he tried.
Zane gave no sign of hearing him. In the emotionless state, Gus’s words were nothing. There was only the target and the blade.
What was making Gus’s stomach drop wasn’t the technique itself. It was that Zane kept firing it.
The Gods-Intimidating Strike had a brutal cost. A Soul Sea Realm warrior running on Vitality could use it twice before their reserves ran dry. Two strikes, and then stop — that was the ceiling. 
But Zane wasn’t stopping.
Gus had felt it from the first strike: there was no Vitality in this body. What Zane ran on was something else. Denser. Rawer. More fundamental.
Chaos Energy.
The word landed in Gus’s mind like a stone.
He’d heard his master speak of it once, long ago — a cultivation method the Master had spent enormous effort developing, certain it would work in principle, equally certain no one in the universe could ever practice it. 
Chaos Energy wasn’t just different from Vitality. It was thousands of times denser. A warrior who cultivated it would carry reserves so vast that the Gods-Intimidating Strike’s cost barely registered — a drop taken from an ocean. The technique could be fired endlessly.
His master had said: if he ever found someone who could actually cultivate it, he would take that person as his third disciple without question.
Gus stared at the young man methodically cutting him apart and felt something close to awe.
[SFX: MECHANICAL TWITCHING]
Meanwhile, Saul watched the fight unfold, his mechanical companions twitching with more animation than any ordinary puppet.
“I never thought Gus would have such a hard time with Zane,” he said, almost to himself.
Freya shot him a look. “Isn’t it obvious?” she said after a second. “Zane has a weapon, and Gus is unarmed. Plus, he’s only at the Soul Sea Realm.”
She wanted to argue that, for an unarmed guy at the early stage of the Soul Sea Realm, Gus was actually doing terrifyingly well. But something about the grandeur of the Elysian Keep kept her quiet. She felt too embarrassed to pick a fight with Saul here.
Saul just smiled. “You don’t get it,” he said. “Gus has the bloodline of a king from the Sacred Race. He’s one of the best of his people. Our master took him in personally—how could he be weak? You think he’s just some run-of-the-mill Soul Sea warrior? Not a chance.”
The “master” Saul mentioned was, of course, Zane’s master, too.
Lavender, Livingston, and Freya had no idea about Gus’s background, but Saul knew everything.
Gus was someone who had literally surpassed the heavens; he could hold his own even in the upper world. So, even at the early stage of the Soul Sea Realm, he *should* have been leagues stronger than Zane.
But Saul was just thinking out loud. He knew their master would be thrilled to learn about Zane’s real strength. As for the fight itself, Saul had no intention of stopping it.
[SFX: BLOOD SPLATTER]
More golden blood arced through the air.
[SFX: FLESH TEARING]
[SFX: ENERGY DISSIPATING]
By now, Zane had hacked away most of Gus’s body. His severed limbs dissolved into fading wisps of dark and light energy as they hit the ground.
Soon, only his head and torso were left. He couldn’t dodge anymore.
Zane finally slowed his assault. 
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
[SFX: CROUCHING DOWN]
He walked over to what was left of Gus and crouched down. “Speak,” he said. “Now we can have a proper talk.”
