Ep 933 - Familiar Faces

Word count: 1116

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]

Zane shot his hand out to stop Dorothea from attacking. “Don’t!”

He knew the power of a Spirit Supreme Realm warrior was terrifying. A Soul Sea Realm fighter could wipe out a mountain range with a flick of the wrist; an attack from Dorothea could flatten all of Oracle City in a heartbeat.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY BLASTS]

The rays of light slammed into Zane and Dorothea but didn't leave a scratch. Zane's body was as tough as any Relic-Class weapon, and Dorothea’s own Vitality flared to form a shield. The Third- and Fourth-Grade runes were completely useless against them.

[SFX: MULTIPLE FOOTSTEPS RUSHING]

“Who are you?”

“The Heavenly Stellar Palace is a vital location for the World Merchants' Guild! All trespassers will be considered enemies!”

In a flash, dozens of figures appeared. Their leader was a handsome man with sharp, bright eyes, a curved blade held at the ready. He stared at Zane, his expression stunned.

[MUSIC: UPLIFTING REUNION THEME]

Zane broke into a smile and shouted, “Owen!”

“Holy crap! Zane!” Owen looked him up and down, his eyes lighting up with relief and joy. “You’re actually back!”

This wasn’t the first time Zane had left the Heartlands, or even the longest they’d gone without word from him. More often than not, people just assumed he was dead. And while his trip to the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms had them worried, they’d had a feeling he’d make it back. They just never thought it would be this soon.

“But what about the Chaotic Ocean of Stars?”

Every two weeks, the World Merchants' Guild received reports from the far eastern edge of the Heartlands. For the past few months, a colossal storm had been churning over the Chaotic Ocean of Stars.

It was powerful enough to halt even the warriors of the Soul Sea Realm. Any fighters from the Divine Kingdom Continent would have to wait for the storm to break before they could even think of entering the Heartlands. That storm was their greatest natural defense.

With it raging, the Divine Kingdom Continent and the Heartlands were completely cut off from each other. So no one could figure out how Zane had possibly made it back.

[MUSIC: TENSE PIANO]

Seeing the question in Owen's eyes, Zane just smiled. “So, why is the Heavenly Stellar Palace on lockdown?”

The World Merchants' Guild was the single most powerful faction in the Heartlands, and Oracle City was their stronghold. No one would be dumb enough to try and force their way in.

But the Guild had posted guards all around the Heavenly Stellar Palace, armed and ready for a major fight.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

Owen’s expression soured. “Zane, you’ve been gone for half a year. You have no idea what’s been going on. Simon has been raising all kinds of hell.”

Zane's brow furrowed for a moment. Then his expression settled into something cold and certain. “Let him live a few more hours.”

Owen blinked. A few more hours? What did that even mean? Even if Zane had broken through to Life-and-Death Realm — and Owen wouldn't put it past him — tracking Simon down in the vast Heartlands would take time and effort. No one could just casually promise to deal with him within hours.

He filed that away and said nothing.

[SFX: AERIAL DESCENT]

[MUSIC: WARM REUNION THEME]

Three more streams of light descended from above. Kieran, Hobbes, and Burns landed in the courtyard one after another, their faces breaking into wide smiles the moment they spotted Zane.

“Zane!”

“Brother Zane!”

“You're back!”

They had trusted him completely. The Ancient Heartstones might be difficult for anyone else to obtain, but for someone with Zane's extraordinary fortune, it was only a matter of time. Seeing him here confirmed everything they had believed.

Zane nodded with a smile, though his heart was already inside the palace.

Then Kieran's gaze moved past him — to Dorothea. His smile faltered slightly. Hobbes and Burns noticed too. All three of them studied her with barely concealed unease.

They were late-stage Spirit-Tribulation warriors. They couldn't read Soul Sea Realm cultivation precisely, but they could feel the difference. Dorian and the Demon Night masters had that quality — something vast and heavy, just beneath the surface.

This woman had nothing beneath the surface. She simply had no ceiling they could locate.

Soul Sea Realm was the limit of this world. There had never been anything beyond it in the Heartlands. And yet—

The three exchanged a glance and said nothing.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]

“This is—“ Zane started, then paused. He wasn't sure how to put it. His identity hadn't been confirmed with Dorothea's group, and she certainly hadn't agreed to call him a friend.

Dorothea glanced at him, then at the three Guild leaders. “We're friends,” she said simply.

Zane nodded. “Kieran — I have the Ancient Heartstones. I'm going in.”

Everyone nodded in understanding, but no one moved to follow him. They knew about his history with Tia and decided to give them some space. Besides, the Heavenly Stellar Palace was basically a forbidden zone in Oracle City. Other than Lady Cora and a few of her handmaidens, no one was allowed inside.

[AMBIENT: PALACE COURTYARD]

Zane headed straight for the palace with Dorothea right behind him. A young handmaiden was already waiting by the door. When she saw him, she bowed her head respectfully. “Mister Lowell. Miss Tia is in the garden behind the palace, listening to the harp. Please, follow me.”

“Listening to the harp?” Zane’s breath caught. “She’s awake?”

Just yesterday, when he’d snuck a peek at Tia, she had still been deep in sleep. And now she was awake and listening to music.

The handmaiden simply smiled. “You will see.”

[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL GARDEN]

[MUSIC: GENTLE HARP]

They passed through the Heavenly Stellar Palace and entered a sprawling garden filled with exotic flowers and lush green grass. A clear pond, dotted with beautiful pink water lilies, shimmered to one side. The grounds looked peaceful, but Zane could feel the hum of hundreds of defensive runes hidden all around.

He smiled as the clear, beautiful sound of a harp drifted through the air.

Not far off, he saw Tia dressed in ice-blue, reclining on an elegant daybed.

[MUSIC: ENCHANTED HARP MELODY]

Nearby, a girl in a purple dress sat skillfully playing the harp. As her fingers danced across the strings, her Vitality seemed to flow with them, weaving her own life force into the music and giving it a haunting beauty.

She was lost in the melody when she heard footsteps behind her. Without turning, her voice came out cool and even. “Tilly? Is that you?”
