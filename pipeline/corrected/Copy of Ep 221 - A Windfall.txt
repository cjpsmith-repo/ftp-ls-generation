Ep 221 - A Windfall
Word count: 1153
[AMBIENT: COLD RAIN]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE]
Zane emerged from Quenching Peak, leaving behind the drama of the treacherous cultivators.
A frigid, persistent drizzle---a rare sight in the dead of winter---greeted him as he stepped outside. The icy droplets struck his face, but he barely noticed their sting.
His gaze drifted up, settling on the imposing silhouette of Infernal Peak that filled the distant horizon. A muscle twitched in his clenched jaw as memories of his sister flooded his mind. The fortress-like peak had once seemed an impossible obstacle, a prison beyond his reach. Now, with each passing day, as his power swelled within him, he knew that her liberation was only a matter of time.
"Soon," he whispered, his breath forming a small cloud in the cold air. The word carried both a promise to her, and a threat to those who kept her captive.
With reluctance, Zane tore his eyes from the peak. The rain intensified, plastering his hair to his forehead as he turned away and began his descent down the muddy path, each purposeful step bringing him closer to his ultimate goal.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
The All Peaks Competition was his golden opportunity, a chance to show everyone what he was really made of. It was the ultimate test. And even though the competition was still a ways off, Zane wasn't about to waste a single second getting ready.
He'd spent the past week in Emberfin Lake pushing his limits, and it had paid off. Somewhere in the middle of a brutal session — his body screaming, the pressure of the water crushing down on him — something had cracked open. He'd broken through to the second tier of the Adept Realm.
The competition was held only every three years, and as it drew closer, a frantic energy was already buzzing among the disciples.
The outer disciples were scrambling for a promotion to the inner circle, while the inner disciples were clawing for higher rankings. Even the top-ranked members were training relentlessly, desperate to score well.
From Skytop Peak, ranked first among the Peaks, all the way down to last-place Misty Peak, every single disciple---from the mediocre to the prodigies---was pushing their limits.
And Zane, of course, was no exception.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: DETERMINED PREPARATIONS]
Training in Emberfin Lake was going to cost him a ton of points. He used to practice in there for two hours at a time, but now, he'd need four, maybe even six. The drain on his Student ID would be insane. Every single session would cost him hundreds of points.
To make sure he had enough, Zane had to trade away his Crystal Cubes. He had no other choice but to let the Skyreach Sect rip him off on the exchange rate.
He'd made a killing in Crystal Cubes when he auctioned off the crystal core. To any other outer disciple, it would have been a fortune. But now, seeing Zane's tense expression, Dustin could tell his friend was already hungry for more.
Though he was curious, Dustin didn't pry. He just smiled and said, "I sold all that stuff you gave me at the last auction."
"What was the final price?" Zane asked. He may not have been at the auction, but he wasn't about to be lowballed. Word about the crazy prices at The Blackwood Auction House always got around fast. Zane could find out from just about anyone.
Dustin grinned, deciding to drag it out. "Take a guess!"
Seeing his friend's beaming face, Zane knew the Celestial Essence sale had been a success. The price had to be good---definitely no lower than last time. After a moment, he hazarded a guess. "One hundred and fifty thousand Crystal Cubes?"
Dustin shook his head, his expression unreadable.
"Two hundred thousand?" Zane threw out, his hope rising.
"Higher!"
"Two hundred and fifty thousand?" Zane's eyes widened. The last time he was at The Blackwood Auction House, he'd watched the Celestial Essence go for one hundred and twenty thousand. Had the price really more than doubled thanks to Dustin's hustle?
Dustin laughed, puffing out his chest. "Two hundred and eighty thousand crystal cubes!"
"That high?" A huge grin broke across Zane's face. How could the same Celestial Essence shoot up to two hundred and eighty thousand? That was a massive jump from the one hundred and twenty thousand last time. He just couldn't wrap his head around it.
[MUSIC: UPBEAT CELEBRATORY]
"It went for way more than I expected. The bidding was insane this time," Dustin said, shaking his head. He looked straight at Zane and smiled. "You got lucky! Turns out two major houses are in the middle of reforging their Mythic Weapons!"
"Two major houses?" Zane asked, still confused.
"Yeah, House Sutton and House Walker. The word is, both of them are trying to upgrade their Mythic Weapons from low-grade to mid-grade."
Dustin went on to explain. A weapon only gets one shot at Infusion during forging, and that single shot determines its grade for life. Zane thought of the Streamer, how Chase had left its Infusion unfinished---a chance that he himself had been lucky enough to take.
"Once a weapon's been forged and infused," Dustin continued, "upgrading it is a nightmare. You can either melt it down and start over, or embed crystals for a tiny boost. The only other option is to force an upgrade with Celestial Essence and some secret techniques."
But Celestial Essence was so rare you could go years without seeing any on the market.
As Dustin finished his explanation, he finalized the transfer.
[SFX: SIGIL FLASH]
A moment later, Zane's Student ID flashed, the massive balance of Crystal Cubes now recorded.
"All done! A successful deal," Dustin said happily. "But Zane... last time, you mentioned you had more Celestial Essence. Were you serious?"
He knew Zane rarely lied, but this was different. It was hard to believe anyone could just produce so much Celestial Essence on demand. Dustin felt like he had to see it with his own eyes to believe it.
If Zane really did have more... it would be incredible. Celestial Essence was the most sought-after item in the entire Empire. Dustin could sell as much as Zane could provide, but did he really have more?
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS INTRIGUE]
A sly grin played on Zane's lips. After stowing the token from The Blackwood Bank, he led Dustin to a more secluded spot and pulled out a small vial.
"Here's a hundred more drops of Celestial Essence," Zane said with a light chuckle. "Sell it all at once or break it into a couple batches. Your call."
Dustin's eyes practically bugged out of his head. "You really do have more! And... a hundred drops?"
He was completely speechless. In fact, a crazy thought hit him: the hundred drops he was now holding in his hand were probably just a fraction of what Zane really had.
Dustin realized that every time he thought he had Zane figured out, his friend would find a new way to surprise him.
What would be next?
