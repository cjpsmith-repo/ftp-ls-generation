Ep 1077 - The Tempest

Word count: 1204

[AMBIENT: BATTLE PREPARATION]

[MUSIC: EPIC DRUMS]

The man in the simple cotton clothes gave the signal, and at his direction, the crowd of warriors surged toward the Enchanted Barrier Tower.

Of course, the “tower” wasn’t a tower at all. It was more like a pocket dimension—a vast, sealed-off space where a raging Law Storm had been trapped. The churning storm made the space inside seem endless, but it did have a boundary. Deep within, a terrifying power lingered, a remnant from an ancient battle between several Supreme Lords. Anyone unlucky enough to stumble into it would be torn to pieces in an instant.

But that kind of danger was a good hundred thousand miles deep. There was no way the younger fighters from the Thirteen Palaces could ever make it that far, so for them, the trial was relatively safe.

[SFX: ENERGY WAVE]

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC STORM]

Thousands of warriors streamed into the entrance. The moment they stepped across the threshold, they were hit with a powerful wave of raw Law Power.

[AMBIENT: VIOLENT TEMPEST]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Zane cautiously lifted his head, his eyes scanning the chaos ahead. The Laws churned together into a violent, elemental tempest that filled every corner of the space. Even just a few feet inside, everyone felt an intense pressure closing in. Most of the warriors immediately threw up shields of protective Vitality to ward it off.

Only a handful of the most talented fighters didn’t bother, like Jasper, Layla from the Mist Palace, and the top prospects from the other twelve palaces.

And then there was Zane. He wasn’t using any protective Vitality either, but not because he didn’t want to. The simple truth was, he didn’t have any Vitality left. While his Chaos Energy was similar—you could even argue it was a stronger version—it didn’t spread out in a nice, even shield when he summoned it. Or maybe he just hadn’t figured out how to control it properly yet. Either way, this was definitely not the time or place to start experimenting.

[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

The warriors of the Thirteen Palaces had been pulled from countless different supreme worlds, and any one of them could have been a core member of a top-tier sect. Here and now, every single one of them was trying to outshine the others. The competition was brutal.

This year’s test wasn’t about speed; it was about pushing as deep as you could into the Law Storm. After entering, everyone began to venture inward, some fast and some slow, the initial crowd still bunched together.

As the group pushed deeper, loose fragments of Law began striking Zane without warning. A cluster of Fire Law condensed out of nowhere and slammed into his chest — a brief, searing heat, like touching a hot iron. A moment later, Wind Law fragments collided nearby and sent a volley of tiny wind blades slicing across his arm. He didn’t flinch, but he felt every one of them.

This was what walking unprotected actually meant.

Then something else caught his eye. A small ball of condensed Time Law was drifting toward him, spinning lazily in the current. He reached out and poked it with one finger. The moment he made contact, it slowed — almost stopped — as his own Time Law instinctively pushed back against it. He held it there for a breath, then let his Law pulse once, and the ball popped like a soap bubble.

He watched the dispersing fragments with quiet interest.

[SFX: SOFT ENERGY DISSIPATION]

There was something about the way the Laws intertwined in here — not fusing, just colliding and tangling — that tugged at the edge of his memory. A sense of familiarity he couldn’t place. He turned it over as he walked, but the answer wouldn’t come.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Just then, the man in the cotton clothes pointed and let out a laugh. “That kid is actually in last place!”

Everyone turned to look. Sure enough, Zane was at the very back of the group. Thousands of warriors had formed a long, snaking line moving through the storm, with Zane all alone at the very end.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

The sight drew a fresh round of stares and whispers.

The palace leaders watching from the outside also smirked. Zane had been the last to arrive for the gathering, and now he was dead last in the test. It was an odd pattern. A few of them even glanced over at Yolande with mocking expressions, and she could practically feel them shaking their heads in disapproval.

The old man in cotton clothes hadn’t finished. He turned his gaze across the line of warriors and pointed again — this time toward the front. “That girl, though. I’ve made up my mind. She’s my pick for this whole event.”

Every eye shifted to Layla.

“The Mist Palace?” one palace leader said.

“Layla Rhodes,” another confirmed quietly.

“She’s remarkable, I’ll grant you that. But it’s early to draw conclusions.”

The old man shook his head with unusual firmness. “I’ve been judging talent for a long time. This one is different. The gap between her and an ordinary genius isn’t a matter of degree — it’s a matter of kind.”

The other palace leaders’ expressions tightened. The Thirteen Palaces competed as equals; no one liked hearing one palace praised above the rest.

“With respect,” one of them said, “Layla Rhodes is Elder Dunn’s disciple. It’s hardly a fair comparison — strictly speaking, she isn’t even a Mist Palace warrior. This was Elder Dunn’s arrangement, putting her here for Passing the Torch.”

A murmur of agreement moved around the table. The tension eased slightly.

Yolande, for her part, simply nodded. “That’s true. She isn’t mine.”

Her voice was perfectly calm. She hadn’t been counting on Layla.

She had faith in Zane’s strength, but even she couldn’t help but wonder what he was up to.

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC WIND STORM]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Zane, however, was in complete control, moving forward at his own steady pace. The fragments of Laws swirling around him were a chaotic mess, impossible to comprehend. This wasn’t the place to try and grasp any of them; this whole dimension was just pure, raw power.

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

Meanwhile, the dozens of warriors at the front of the pack had already covered eight hundred miles and were starting to sprint toward the one-thousand-mile line. Everyone wanted to be the first to cross it.

According to the rules, there was no special reward for being first. But the man in cotton clothes would announce the names of the top ten warriors to pass the mark, a declaration that would bring great honor to their palaces. It was exactly the kind of recognition every warrior craved.

Layla was pushing herself hard, but not for the glory of the Mist Palace. She was a proud fighter, and there was no way she was letting herself fall behind. Bracing for the final sprint, she refused to lose.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

[SFX: ENERGY BURST]

With a burst of energy, she charged through the Law Storm at an incredible speed.

She was currently in third place, but in her mind, there was only one goal: number one.
