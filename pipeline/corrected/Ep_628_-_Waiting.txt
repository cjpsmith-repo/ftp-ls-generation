The Violet Heart Sanctuary was born from the old Purple Heart Order, and the order itself took its name from a spring. High on Sun Mountain, the Purple Heart Spring bubbled up from the earth, its waters flowing through a special mineral found nowhere else. The combination gave the spring a faint, ethereal purple glow. Legend had it that anyone who drank from its waters would be blessed with a long, healthy life, free from sickness.
Once, the Purple Heart Order had been nothing more than a small, second-rate faction. But as pressure from other races mounted, they were forced to absorb other human groups, eventually growing into the last true haven for humanity. The spring that started it all became the sacred heart of the sanctuary. While its waters had little effect on powerful warriors, the people still took great pride in drinking from it, a symbol of their survival and their home.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Three men now stood beside its shimmering surface.
One, a strikingly handsome man wearing a purple crown, was Dorian Sinclair, the current leader of the Violet Heart Sanctuary. An aura of power radiated from him, so immense it was impossible to measure—the mark of a warrior who had reached the Soul Sea Realm. The other two were ancient, their faces etched with the lines of centuries, but their eyes were as sharp as lightning. They, too, were masters of the Soul Sea Realm, their long lives granting them a perception others lacked.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
And right now, they all sensed danger. Ogres had gathered at the sanctuary’s outer perimeter, a silent, menacing presence. They hadn’t attacked yet, held back only because Amber, a powerful ally, was leading her own forces toward the Ogres’ two sacred sites in a strategic standoff.
But even with that temporary stalemate, the people of the Violet Heart Sanctuary were on edge. Most of them were unwilling to pin their hopes on Zane, some low-level War General they’d never even met. From the lowest-ranking warriors to the powerful War Sages, none of them could bring themselves to trust him.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SANCTUARY]
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
Dorian had already spread the news of Zane’s incredible feat in the Tower of Sin, but it had backfired. Instead of inspiring hope, it fueled disbelief. Many dismissed Zane as a fraud, and some War Lords and War Sages had even begun quietly moving their families away from the sanctuary, convinced it was doomed.
The defections left a bitter taste in Dorian’s mouth, but there was nothing he could do. He couldn’t enter the Cursed Land to fight, nor could he face an Ogre master of the Soul Sea Realm. At first, he'd been hesitant himself — trusting a first-level War General he'd never met over his own judgment didn't come naturally. But when the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary sent its own representatives to coordinate strategy, that hesitation had dissolved. Whether he liked it or not, the fate of the Violet Heart Sanctuary was now tied to that mysterious young man in the Tower.
From where Dorian stood, the situation actually favored them. The Ogres were putting on a show. Beyond destroying one small second-rank sect on the sanctuary's outer edge, they hadn't advanced a single step. They couldn't — not with Amber and her forces camped on their doorstep.
His only option was to wait. One month. As long as they survived for one month, they would have their answer.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL RISING THEME]
If what his man Fabian said was true—if Zane really could drive out all the Ogres—then humanity wouldn't just get five seats in the Tower of Sin. They could claim every last seat the Ogres were forced to abandon. Even if Zane gave a few to the Demon Night race as a courtesy, the humans would still walk away with at least a dozen.
That kind of power would change everything. The balance in the Tower of Sin reflected the balance on the main continent. With so many seats, the number of human masters would explode. In a short time, the Violet Heart Sanctuary would become a true powerhouse, and humanity would finally rise again.
And Dorian was still young. As long as he survived, he would get to see that revival firsthand, all under his leadership. The thought of it, of seeing his people and his sanctuary truly prosper, was enough to make his heart ache with hope.
"No matter what, we have to be ready,” one of the elders declared, his voice firm. “We will face what’s coming and fight the Ogres with everything we have.”
“Still, it would be better if Zane came here,” the other elder mused. “His presence would calm the people.”
A wry smile touched Dorian’s lips. “Good luck getting that guy to come here. I’ve had Fabian ask him twice, but he won’t budge. If we make it through this month, I can only hope he’ll finally pay the sanctuary a visit.”
[MUSIC: DARK STRATEGIC TENSION]
The Ogres were not having an easier time of it.
Their position had become a trap with no good exit. Attack the Violet Heart Sanctuary, and Amber would raze the Cursed Land to the ground — and even if they won, Zane would still expel them from the Tower. Back down, and they'd lose the Tower in a month anyway. After that, cut off from their greatest training ground, their decline was only a matter of time. Powerful races had been swallowed by history before. The Ogres knew it could happen to them.
All they had ever wanted was to use the Violet Heart Sanctuary as leverage against Zane. Now their own sacred lands were the ones being threatened, and the weapon they'd chosen had turned in their hands.
The Soul Sea Realm masters deliberated endlessly. No one had a clean answer.
[AMBIENT: WILDERNESS]
[MUSIC: STRATEGIC TENSION]
Meanwhile, Amber had her orders from her leader, Lavender: take her warriors to the Ogre territories and simply watch. As long as the Ogres didn't attack the Violet Heart Sanctuary, Amber was not to move against their sacred places.
She sat at the peak of a dark mountain deep in the Flaming Place's territory, a purple robe settled around her shoulders, black volcanic smoke drifting past below. Thousands of active volcanoes of every size dotted the landscape as far as she could see — that was why they called it the Flaming Place.
Amber had entered the Tower of Sin two hundred years ago and had been clashing with the Ogres ever since. They had a long and bloody history, and she had always wanted a chance to crush them, but the opportunity never arose. A direct assault on their sacred sites would have been costly, even for her powerful seventh-grade order, and would have left them vulnerable to other opportunistic races.
But now, she realized Zane’s emergence had handed her the perfect weapon. She could weaken the Ogres to the point of extinction without losing a single soldier. Even if Lavender hadn’t given the order, Amber would have made the exact same move.
She had no particular love for humans, but as far as she was concerned, the enemy of her enemy was a friend.
The month was ticking down, and no one on the continent — not the Ogres, not the humans, not even Amber — knew what Zane would do when it ran out.
