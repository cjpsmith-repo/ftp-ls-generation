Ep 410 - A Little Present

Word count: 1143

[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

A wave of horror washed over Zane. "Did I really just punch a hole through the floor of Cloud Hall?" 

The power of the Exterminating Wheel was far beyond what he’d anticipated; the raw force of it still seemed to hum in his bones. A single ray of light now pierced the gloom, shining up through the neat, circular hole he’d just made.

[SFX: WARNING ALARM]

[AMBIENT: ECHOING HALL]

"All disciples in the practice rooms, clear out now!"

The voice of Steward Shaw boomed through the hall, overriding the long, wailing note of a warning flute. He was one of the two stewards who had handed out the Cloud Hall Tokens a few days ago. Hearing the command, Zane let out a reluctant sigh, put away the Exterminating Wheel, and walked out the door.

Outside, several other disciples were looking at Steward Shaw with confusion. They clearly had no idea why the alarm was blaring or why they’d been pulled from their training.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

"Who was it?" Steward Shaw demanded, his voice tight with barely contained rage.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]

Before he could say more, Zane stepped forward. "I'm sorry, Steward Shaw. I accidentally punched a hole in the floor of my practice room."

This particular room occupied the lowest level of Cloud Hall. Not far beneath its floor lay a massive floating stone, its ancient surface covered in glowing warning runes that pulsed with ominous light. As far as anyone knew, no one was ever supposed to touch it—the consequences were left deliberately vague, which made them all the more terrifying.

Cloud Hall protected all its practice rooms with layers of shielding, but the designers had made a calculated decision about this floor. The rooms here only required a Red Cloud Hall Token for access, which meant most people training down here were fresh disciples from the Elite House—young cultivators still finding their footing, their techniques unrefined and their power limited. Since the recruits were considered too weak to cause any real damage, the administrators hadn't bothered installing shields that were as strong as those on the upper levels.

It was a reasonable assumption under normal circumstances.

Unfortunately for Steward Shaw, Zane Lowell was anything but normal.

[AMBIENT: QUIET HALLWAY]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS FADING]

As soon as Steward Shaw finally left, Finn let out a huge sigh of relief and threw an arm around Zane with a mischievous grin. "Thanks, man! I can't believe whatever you said actually worked!"

[MUSIC: CURIOUS STRINGS]

Zane looked him over, a curious glint in his eye. "Wait a minute. Did you just break through to the next level?"

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]

Finn nodded, chuckling. "I had a feeling I was getting close back on the Cloud Road, but I didn't expect to finally break into the Soul Realm just now."

There was a huge barrier between the Adept Realm and the Soul Realm, and Finn had been stuck at the peak of the Adept Realm for a while. He might not have been as freakishly talented as Zane, but he was just as good as any other elite in Cloud Hall. It was only a matter of time before he had the breakthrough he needed.

"By the way," Finn asked eagerly, "did you ever figure out how to distill the essence of the nether phantom?"

They had both collected a ton of it back on the Death Road, but Tia had warned them it was extremely poisonous in its raw state. Even though Zane wasn't particularly afraid of poison, he wasn't stupid enough to swallow it without a good reason.

Remembering that the essence was supposed to strengthen the soul, Zane asked, "You really need it right now?"

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

Finn nodded, his smile turning bitter. "Practicing The Celestial Blade requires an incredibly strong soul. As it is, I can barely handle it."

To use the technique, a person had to withstand the immense power of the natural world and channel that force through their sword. But how could anyone land a powerful strike if they couldn't even handle the backlash?

Finn's soul had been strong enough before, but after breaking into the Soul Realm, it couldn't keep up. The problem was that his breakthrough allowed him to draw in far more ambient energy, tapping into even greater celestial momentum. But with that greater power came an even heavier burden on his soul.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

The explosion had happened because he’d lost control of the energy he’d gathered. It imploded, detonated, and blew his entire practice room to pieces.

[MUSIC: GENTLE HOPEFUL MELODY]

Zane gave him a small smile. "I'll ask around about how to purify the Nether Phantom's essence for you. In the meantime, I've got something that might help. Use this for now." With that, Zane tapped his Storage Ring and pulled out a small red treasure chest.

Finn opened it.
A black shadow shot out and hit him between the eyes before he could react. He almost flinched — then didn't. He closed his eyes.
[SFX: LOW RESONANT HUM]
For a long moment he was still. When he opened them again, his gaze was sharp as a blade. "This strengthens the soul."
Zane nodded. "Refined Essence of the Nether Phantom. From the red treasure chests on the Death Road. I have a few left — don't make it a big deal."
Finn looked like he wanted to say something. Zane cut him off. "We're from the same sect. Leave it."

Zane's thoughts drifted to who he could even ask. The only person that came to mind was the mysterious disciple who had saved him from the Drake’s jaws—Tia. He hadn’t properly thanked her yet, mostly because he hadn't run into any real trouble since arriving at Cloud Hall. Thanks to her, he could train to his heart's content.

Life here was a world of difference from when he'd first joined the Skyreach Sect. Here, people actually showed him respect.

Then again, she still owes me, Zane thought. 

[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]

He pulled out his communication token and channeled a small amount of Vitality into it.

"And why should I be thanking you? I haven't forgotten I'm still owed half of that Heavenly Fruit Tree. Plus a few other things. So who should really be thanking who here?"

[MUSIC: PLAYFUL MISCHIEVOUS TUNE]

Tia's voice immediately shot back, dripping with amusement. "Are you serious? You're unbelievable. Besides, your power level is still way too low; it's definitely not the right time for you to be eating any heavenly fruit. Just keep working on getting stronger. But... since training in a practice room must be so boring, I'll give you a little present. Heh heh."

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Her laugh sent a chill straight down Zane's spine.

He had a very, very bad feeling about what was coming next.
