Ep 1063 - I Came Here For You

Word count: 1040

[AMBIENT: LARGE CROWD MURMURING]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Warriors from Groundstone and Cloudswallow Halls spilled out of their homes, all eyes glued to the crimson ring hanging high in the sky.

The crowd was a mix of nervous energy—some were practically buzzing with excitement, while others looked utterly crushed.

Only those who passed through the Red Cloud Ring would earn the right to follow the palace leader and take part in the Passing the Torch ceremony. But not everyone would make it.

Truth was, most warriors in The Mist Palace already had a good read on their own abilities. They knew if they had what it took to get through the ring.

The excited ones were confident; the gloomy ones figured they didn’t stand a chance.

“I heard you can only get through if you’ve activated a lotus with at least five petals!”

“Not exactly. Some people have made it with a three-petal lotus. But yeah, most who succeed have at least five.”

“I only had a three-petal lotus during my trial last year. Am I totally screwed?”

The warriors murmured among themselves, their gazes fixed on the ring.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Just then, a figure shot into the sky, flying straight for the Red Cloud Ring.

“That’s Justin Fields from Cloudswallow Hall! He’s a shoo-in!”

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

Justin rocketed upward, reaching the Red Cloud Ring in a heartbeat. Without a moment’s hesitation, he sailed right through.

[SFX: MAGICAL FLARE]

The second he was through, the ring flared with a dazzling scarlet light.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]

“Whoa, that light is blinding! I heard Justin unlocked a five-petal lotus a few years back. Judging by that glow, he must have a seven-petal one by now!”

After clearing the ring, Justin went to stand behind one of the vice leaders—the one who’d brought him into The Mist Palace in the first place. Justin was his protégé, plain and simple.

The vice leader offered a small, proud smile. Justin, meanwhile, shot a smug look down at the scene below.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]

A pattern emerged as more warriors rose toward the ring. The ones who flew up without hesitation — full speed, no second-guessing — tended to sail straight through. Those who slowed as they approached, visibly reconsidering, mostly bounced back. A few overconfident ones rushed up at full tilt only to be flung back by the barrier, landing badly to muffled laughter. And then there were the ones who’d already written themselves off, who tried anyway and somehow passed — their faces splitting into pure disbelief and joy the moment the ring let them through.

After the pattern had played out for a while, another warrior rose to the challenge.

[SFX: BARRIER SHIMMER]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TONE]

But as this warrior charged the ring, it pulsed with black light. A transparent barrier shimmered into existence, blocking his path.

“Did he fail?” someone muttered.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]

Zane watched from where he stood. There was no point rushing in while the crowd was still thick — he’d wait until the numbers thinned.

That was when he noticed Layla looking at him.

He frowned. He’d read her well enough by now. She’d been patient, watching and waiting, hoping for a chance to measure herself against him. The problem was that he had no interest in giving her one. He wasn’t here to prove anything to anyone. He’d moved past that a long time ago. Layla, clearly, hadn’t.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Layla’s voice came from beside him, light and casual. “The more lotus petals you’ve opened, the brighter the ring glows. I’m curious — how many have you opened?”

Zane glanced at her. “Not sure.”

“You just don’t want to say.” She smiled.

He shrugged. She wasn’t wrong.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Then a blue-robed figure shot upward — fast, deliberate, radiating a fierce aura.

“That’s Jasper Reed!”

“The top warrior in Cloudswallow Hall!”

The moment Jasper passed through the ring, it blazed with a light so intense that the faces of every warrior below flushed crimson. Of the hundreds who had passed before him, none had triggered a reaction that bright.

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY FLARE]

Jasper flew straight to Yolande. He didn’t stand to the side like the others. He looked at her directly and said, “I joined the Mist Palace for you. Give me twenty years — I’ll prove I’m a match for you.”

[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]

The surrounding warriors fell uncomfortably silent. He’d just declared himself to the palace leader — in front of everyone.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Hearing the whispers, Yolande’s lips tightened. She said coldly, “Jasper, the woman you met was nothing more than a clone—an illusion from my attunement method. Stop chasing this ghost.”

[AMBIENT: GRAND PALACE HALL]

At her rebuke, Jasper Reed gritted his teeth and moved to stand behind her. He looked dejected, but his eyes still burned with an unwavering fire.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

“What’s with that guy?”

“You haven’t heard? When our palace leader used the Soul Split Spell, she sent a million fragments of her soul to train in the Lower World. Jasper was a warrior there who fell in love with one of her duplicates!”

“He’s a strange one. Story is, when he ascended to the Upper World, he started out more than sixty supreme worlds away. The fact that he even found his way to The Mist Palace is a testament to his sheer grit and a whole lot of luck.”

“He might be obsessed, but his talent is the real deal. People respect that. As long as he wants to be here, the Mist Palace won’t turn him away.”

“He’s fixated on one of the leader’s clones. But now that she’s whole again, she’s not the person he fell for!”

Zane was floored by what he heard.

And then, it all clicked into place.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

So, before ascending to the Upper World, Jasper Reed had met one of Yolande’s duplicates in the Lower World. A clone. And now he was here, trying to prove his talent and strength to win over the real Yolande.

It was a lost cause. Zane shook his head slightly. Poor guy was hung up on someone who didn’t even exist.
