Ep 928 - The Compass Wheel

Word count: 1090

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]

Zane had agreed to take the Demi-Immortals to the Tower of Crystal. 

The seven of them stood powerless against the sealed Evil Dragon. Their only hope was to summon more powerful Demi-Immortal warriors from the upper world, but right now, getting to the Tower was a more immediate problem.

Even though Vorn and his group didn't buy that Zane had actually earned the Golden Celestial Paragon title, they were desperate to see how he’d been recognized by the Talent Tablet. Was his talent really on the first or second row? Or was it all a bluff, and he’d just scraped by with a simple bronze title? They needed answers.

Zane just shrugged. “I’ll take you to the Tower of Sin, Tower of Crystal, whatever you want to call it. But I have something important to do first.”

His original plan had been simple: get the Ancient Heartstones and head straight back to The Heartlands to save Tia. That all went sideways when he promised the Cobalt Dragon he’d venture into the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land and retrieve the Ancestral Dragon Egg. He’d succeeded, but now, who would get to keep the hatchling was another question entirely.

Even with the Ancestral Dragon imprinted on him, he knew the Demi-Immortals would never just let it go. 

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

To make matters worse, the White-Haired Priest was standing right there, watching him with a predatory gaze. Zane knew the man wouldn't back down easily.

Still, Zane wasn’t too worried. Neither the priest nor the other Demi-Immortals had been openly hostile yet. Besides, he had the Golden Celestial Paragon title as his ace in the hole. All he had to do was play along until he could get back to The Heartlands.

The young man in black scoffed. “Something important? Or is that just an excuse? Are you afraid of being exposed as a fraud?” He figured that even if Zane had the mark, it just meant he’d been recognized—not that he was anything special.

Zane shot him a sharp look. “I’m not from the Divine Kingdom Continent. I only came here to find some Ancient Heartstones to save someone’s life. If you don’t believe me, then go find the tower yourself. This world isn't too big for you, is it?”

He had a point. While this world of chaos wasn't massive, finding the main continent was like searching for a needle in a haystack. The Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms was relatively close to the Heartlands, but the tower was an incredible distance away. It wasn't that the Demi-Immortal warriors couldn't find it, but without Zane’s coordinates, doing so quickly was impossible.

“Hmph. That’s easy enough to solve,” the young man sneered. “We’ll just search your soul. Dorothea is the best—”

Dorothea's voice cut through cleanly. “Stop. He has the Mark. If he joins us one day, he'll be a colleague. Can you not speak like that?”

The young man in black let out a short laugh. “A colleague. A Lower World warrior who at best got his name on the second row — that's a colleague?”

Delroy and the burly man exchanged a look and laughed along.

Dorothea turned to face the young man in black, her expression serious. “If it's true — if he really has the gold title — what do you plan to do?”

The young man in black twirled his broadsword slowly and set it down in front of him. “If it's true,” he said, “I'll swallow this blade.”

“Really?” Dorothea's eyes lit up.

“Really.” He nodded hard.

Zane watched with a strange expression. That broadsword was divine-class. He almost wanted to see it happen.

“Enough,” Vorn said. “Since you need to save someone, we'll take you. It won't cost us much time.”

Zane almost laughed out loud. The Star Storm was still raging across the Chaotic Ocean of Stars. Crossing it alone at Life-and-Death Realm was survivable — barely. Having seven Spirit Supreme warriors escort him was something else entirely. He hadn't expected this particular stroke of luck.

“Then let's go,” he said.

[SFX: RUNE ACTIVATION]

Vorn reached into his robe and produced a blood-red rune. He tossed it toward the ground, and it flared on impact, burning a large red cross into the earth.

A warrior from the Divine Kingdoms nearby leaned toward another. “What is that?”

The White-Haired Priest's voice came from behind them, cold and flat. “A locator talisman. Demi-Immortal Race issue. They can return to this exact spot at any time.”

He said nothing more. His face had gone very still. He had watched Zane bloom the Greater Lotus Flower. He had seen the Demi-Immortal Mark settle onto him. He had watched the Ancestral Dragon choose him. Every piece of it was slipping out of reach.

[SFX: SPATIAL TEAR]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Vorn raised his cane again. With a casual wave and a sharp, downward slash, he tore an irregular spatial rift open in the air.

“Delroy, open it,” Vorn commanded.

Delroy nodded and walked toward the hundred-foot-tall tear in reality. He stretched out his hand, and an enchanted barrier shot from his palm. 

[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL PORTAL]

Crackling sounds filled the air as the barrier expanded, forcing the rift wider and creating a stable, swirling aperture.

Hank then pulled out The Compass Wheel. 

[SFX: WIND WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY]

As it caught the wind, the several-dozen-foot-wide disc swelled to an immense size—clearly their mode of transport. The young man in black and the others followed Vorn onto it.

Dorothea turned to Zane. “Alright, let’s go.”

Zane nodded, then looked over at Fiona and Randy’s group, his expression softening with fondness. He hadn’t been on the Divine Kingdom Continent for long, but he’d made real friends here.

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL STRINGS]

They could only stare back, a flood of memories rushing through them. Zane had saved them, time and again. Without him, none of them would have made it back from the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. Even their fathers, the powerful masters from the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms, owed him their lives. Gratitude swelled in their chests, and the thought of leaving him now was unbearable. But with these imposing Spirit Supreme Realm warriors watching, no one dared to speak. Even the four rulers remained silent.

Their eyes met across the distance. Zane offered a small smile. “I’ll be back!”

[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: URGENT DRAMATIC]

The words had barely left his mouth when Fiona became a fiery red blur, sprinting straight for him.
