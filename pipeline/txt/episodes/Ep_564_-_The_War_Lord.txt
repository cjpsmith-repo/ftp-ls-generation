Ep 564 - The War Lord

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
Zane was just a boy when he watched a vicious plot steal his family from him, slaughtering them before his eyes. The trauma left him scarred and adrift for years, but it never managed to turn him into a cold-hearted man.
Honestly, he’d never imagined that mastering the Apex of Sword Intent would lead him to Killing Sword Mountain and set him on the path of slaughter.
But even then, Zane hadn't accumulated much Malice back in The Heartlands. He wasn’t bloodthirsty by nature—he only ever killed when his own life was on the line.
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]
Here on this chaotic continent, though, Zane didn't have to think twice about killing his enemies. They weren't Human. The Ogres charging him were an alien race that wouldn't hesitate for a second to kill him.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]
[SFX: WHOOSHING BLADE]
Zane drew his sword—a Relic-Class weapon—and became a blur. Waves of sword intent pulsed from the blade as he shot through the Ogre ranks like a rocket.
If the Ogres were lumbering eagles, Zane was a nimble skylark, weaving between his prey. A single brush of his blade was all it took. In the next instant, it was over.
[SFX: MULTIPLE BODIES FALLING]
Ogres crashed to the ground one after another, their eyes wide with shock. They died baffled, wondering how a mere seventh-rank warrior could be so damn powerful.
In a flash, half of the hundred-odd Ogres hit the dirt.
[SFX: ROAR]
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
The second War General had enough. With a roar, forceful energy erupted from his massive frame. A pair of bronze hammers the size of boulders appeared in his hands, and he swung them at Zane with everything he had.
[SFX: SPLITTING METAL]
Zane's sword barely twitched. The hammers split cleanly in two.
A Relic-Class weapon against low-grade Spirit Weapon iron. There was no contest.
[AMBIENT: DENSE FOREST]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
In the forest, the humans watching had gone very still.
"Is that... a Relic-Class sword?" one of them breathed.
"A seventh-rank fighter carrying a Relic-Class weapon." The bearded general shook his head slowly. "He has to be Holy Land-trained. Has to be."
"Forget the sword," the silver-haired young man said flatly. "A seventh-rank fighter just pulverized a fifth-rank War General with his bare body. Stop trying to explain him with ordinary logic."
[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]
Back on the battlefield, Zane's forceful energy surged—and something unexpected happened.
A beam of milky-white light burst from his Confluence Platform, and his speed exploded. Even he couldn't control it. The sword stroke meant for the War General's chest went wide, catching only his neck as Zane shot past him like a bolt of lightning—and slammed into a hill a thousand feet away.
[SFX: ROCKS CRUMBLING]
[AMBIENT: CRATER DEBRIS]
One moment Zane was there, the next he’d vanished, and a cloud of dust erupted from a distant hill.
[AMBIENT: DENSE FOREST]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
"How did he get that fast all of a sudden?" A man hidden in the forest couldn't help but gasp.
"Shh, quiet," the bearded man next to him whispered, his face a mask of confusion. "Something's not right. His speed just… exploded. Looked like he couldn't even stop himself."
They weren't the only ones confused. The Ogres were just as baffled, and honestly, Zane had no idea what just happened either.
[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACKLE]
When that forceful energy erupted from his body, his speed had surged so intensely it felt like he was riding a bolt of lightning.
He looked down at his body, searching for an answer. The Phoenix Crystal embedded in his arm hadn't done much for his speed in a while; he’d already outpaced its benefits. So it couldn't be that.
But Zane remembered it clearly: the surge had come the exact moment the forceful energy shot out a beam of milky-white light. The two had to be connected. But how?
[SFX: ROCKS CRUMBLING]
[AMBIENT: CRATER DEBRIS]
Zane had left a deep crater where he’d slammed into the hill. He pushed himself to his feet, slowly climbing out, when the Cobalt Dragon's seventh brother’s voice echoed in his mind.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]
Ha! Feeling a little weird, kid?
'You know what that was?' Zane projected back.
'I do. Part of that energy burst came from your Confluence Platform,' the seventh brother replied.
'The Confluence Platform does that?' Zane asked, bewildered.
The seventh brother explained. 'Boosting speed isn't its real purpose. That's what the Wind Gate Platform is for, once you get to the Pathforged Realm. But your Confluence Platform is made from the bones of a Cloud-Chaser Lion. They're some of the fastest legendary beasts out there, second only to the Celestial Mouse. You've inherited a piece of its natural talent.'
'Really? I never would've guessed,' Zane thought, a thrill cutting through his surprise.
This was a game-changer. The Body Refining system had a major drawback compared to Vitality cultivation. While a warrior could theoretically manifest their forceful energy, circulating it was slow and clumsy, making it almost useless in a real fight. Vitality, by comparison, was far more versatile and powerful.
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Back in the sky above the pasture, the remaining Ogres had reached a grim conclusion. They couldn't stop him. One by one, then all at once, they scattered.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
The humans watching from the forest were speechless. Ogres had never been known to run. In every war between the races, even when outmatched, Ogres charged until they dropped. Seeing them break and flee was like watching the sun rise in the west.
Zane floated toward them slowly. And they ran.
Then a new voice rang out from above.
[SFX: HEAVY WINGBEATS]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BRASS]
"Human boy. You will stay."
Zane's eyes sharpened. A single figure descended from the sky—an Ogre unlike the others. His skin had turned a deep, burning red. The fangs at the corners of his mouth were scarlet. He moved without urgency, radiating the kind of power that didn't need to hurry.
"Interesting little warrior," the Ogre said. "You've killed my generals. Let's see if you can kill a War Lord."
Zane's expression darkened. An Ogre War Lord. His current strength wasn't enough—not even close.
He could run. Alone, he'd be gone in a heartbeat. But the hundreds of ordinary humans below him couldn't run. And Zane had never been good at leaving things unfinished.
[SFX: HEAVY THUD]
Before Zane could fully process it, the Ogre War Lord landed with a ground-shaking thud and charged, a mountain of muscle and fury barreling toward him.
It grew larger and larger in his vision as it thundered closer. An overwhelming sense of danger crushed down on him, his heart seizing in his chest. This thing could kill him. It was going to kill him.
In that split second of despair, Zane summoned the only thing he could: Lily, the sword spirit he had created.
[SFX: MAGICAL SUMMONING]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]
A beautiful girl materialized from thin air, and with her, the massive sword that once belonged to the Blood Fiend Emperor.
Watching from a distance, the three members of the Demon Night were stunned into silence. One of them, a young woman perched on a high branch, stared at Zane's sword spirit.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Her eyes went wide. Her jaw dropped.
