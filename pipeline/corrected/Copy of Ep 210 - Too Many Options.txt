Zane stared at the sword in awe. "Nine edges forged into one? That's insane."
At first, he'd thought the Nine-Edge Sword's blade looked too thick, but now that he understood the story behind the name, he saw it in a whole new light. *Even if the nine layers are paper-thin,* he thought, *it has to be ridiculously hard to press them into a blade this slim.*
The shopkeeper nodded. "Chase's skill as a weaponsmith was the stuff of legend. Only a genius like him could dream up such a brilliant way to use the materials and forge a sword this powerful."
[SFX: FORGE FIRE]
The shopkeeper continued, explaining how Chase used a special, highly flexible steel that was perfect for this forging method. First, he would heat the steel until it was soft and pliable. Then, he'd place the metal on an iron plate that was also being heated in a special refining furnace, one filled with a unique kind of charcoal.
"This charcoal, mined from Cloud Mountain, can burn for over three months straight," the shopkeeper explained. "After being heated that long, the iron plate glows red-hot but stays perfectly flat."
[SFX: HEAVY METAL ROLLING]
Next, a dozen brawny men would work a massive roller---weighing over ten thousand pounds---to continuously flatten the steel on the iron plate.
[SFX: HISS]
After seven days of non-stop work, the steel was compressed into a thin sheet, fused tight to the iron plate. But instead of plunging it into cold water like in a typical forging, the plate was left to cool at room temperature, allowing the steel sheet to separate on its own.
This painstaking process was what created the ultra-thin steel sheets needed for the Nine-Edge Sword.
[SFX: METAL COOLING]
After preparing nine of these sheets, Chase would patiently merge them, layer by layer, to create his masterpiece.
But as magnificent as the Nine-Edge Sword was, there were even more fantastical weapons available for Zane to peruse.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL DISCOVERY]
The next sword he looked at was something else entirely. It glowed with a powerful green light, as if it were carved from pure jade.
Compared to the Nine-Edge Sword, this green sword was its polar opposite. The Nine-Edge Sword was so heavy it would intimidate anyone without serious strength; most would struggle to even lift it. This sword, however, was as light as a feather. As Zane held it, he felt almost no weight in his hand at all.
"This one is called 'Green Wave.' It's another high-grade Weapon. Like the other two here, Chase made it late in his life. He had nearly exhausted his talent, but he was still pushing his limits, trying to create one last Mythic Weapon."
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ANCIENT THEME]
For thousands of years, the unwritten rule in The Eastern Territory was simple: a weaponsmith could only be called a master if they had successfully forged a mythic weapon.
Brady from House Warrick, Chase, and the weaponsmith at the Palace were all considered masters because each had managed to forge a mythic weapon using their own unique skills.
What really made Brady famous was that he had only ever forged two mythic weapons in his entire life, and that alone was enough to make his name known across the world.
Chase, on the other hand, was far more renowned and respected than Brady. He had created a staggering thirteen mythic weapons, putting him in a league of his own. In the entire history of The Eastern Territory, he was the only weaponsmith to ever achieve such a feat, leaving behind an untouchable legacy.
All thirteen of those weapons were made when he was at his peak. He tried one last time to forge another mythic weapon in his final years, but his immense talent had already begun to fade.
The three swords in front of Zane were Chase's last attempts.
He poured every last drop of his inspiration and strength into making these three swords before he finally passed away. Some said he could have lived for several more years if he hadn't pushed himself so hard at the end.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
Zane drew the sword, the blade sliding from its sheath with a soft, effortless whisper.
[SFX: MAGICAL SWORD VIBRATION]
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL RINGING]
[MUSIC: FAE HARP MELODY]
The moment it was free, the green blade began to vibrate, and a sound echoed through the room---a clear, ethereal ringing, like a fae playing a melody on a harp.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL DISCOVERY]
Zane lifted the sword, watching it move. The blade was so thin and translucent he could see right through it, tinting everything on the other side a soft, shimmering green.
"It's a beautiful sword," he praised, marveling at the perfect craftsmanship.
Zane knew a thing or two about weapons from his time at a weapon house in Alba Imperia and, of course, The Elysian Keep. Even so, he couldn't help but be blown away by how stunning and delicate this sword was.
[SFX: SWORD SHIMMER]
The shopkeeper smiled. "It certainly is. It's forged from the wings of Golden Cicadas. That's what gives it that color and strength."
The Golden Cicada was a level-seven beast. It was almost unheard of for a creature like that to be sold in one piece. Zane remembered the crystal core of a single Fire Scorpion Lion---a beast of a similar rank---once selling for seventy thousand crystals at auction. That core was only good for boosting the fire used by a weaponsmith or an alchemist.
The Green Wave was primarily forged from the wings of Golden Cicadas, which were nearly as valuable as the crystal cores of Fire Scorpion Lions.
On top of that, the wings required extensive processing and refinement to become the mighty Green Wave, not to mention it was made by the legendary Chase himself. 
"What are we looking at for both?" Zane asked.
"The Nine-Edge Sword — thirty thousand Crystal Cubes. The Green Wave — eighty thousand."
Honestly, Zane thought the price was a steal.
He hadn't come prepared to spend this much, so he didn't have nearly enough crystals on him, but that wasn't going to be a problem.
With the Blackwood Bank token Dustin had given him, he could pay on credit. As for the crystal cubes he owed, he knew Dustin would cover them as soon as he got the total.
Zane wasn't worried. He still had fifty drops of Celestial Essence that Dustin was holding for him, and that was worth far more than a few tens of thousands of crystal cubes.
The real problem was, how was he supposed to decide between these incredible pieces?
