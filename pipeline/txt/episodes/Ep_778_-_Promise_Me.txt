Ep 778 - Promise Me

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 Dorian and the others watched Zane, a look of pure admiration on their faces. They knew Zane wasn't just going to let the Life-and-Death Realm master kill himself—this was the ultimate test of a warrior’s will.

At Zane’s words, a gleam returned to the master’s eyes, the madness slowly clearing. He looked almost normal again, but at Zane’s unspoken command, he suddenly raised his hands. 

[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]

 Green Vitality swirled around his fingers, condensing into a cluster of razor-sharp thorns aimed directly at his own head.

With that kind of power, there was no doubt—if that hand connected with his skull, he’d be dead before he hit the ground.

 Just as he was about to strike, Zane gave a flat order. "Stop."

The master’s hand froze in midair. He stared at Zane, his expression blankly questioning, waiting for the next command like a puppet on a string.

"Don't kill yourself," Zane ordered coolly. "Tell me your name."

The master nodded once. "My name is Francis Carter."

 "Alright, Francis Carter. Follow me." Zane turned and flew down without a second glance, completely confident that he would be obeyed. And without a moment's hesitation, Francis Carter nodded and followed right behind him.

The slave seal was a unique and powerful one. It didn't turn its victim into a mindless puppet; instead, it left their consciousness intact. As long as Zane's orders weren't out of the ordinary, Francis would act like a normal person, and no one would ever suspect a thing. But no matter what, he was incapable of disobeying a direct command.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT RESOLUTION]

 And just like that, the greatest crisis in the history of the Heartlands was over in less than two hours.

Kieran and the others could finally breathe again. The moment Dorian and his masters had arrived, the fate of the York Divine Kingdom was sealed. Now, there was nothing left to worry about.

It was an undeniable fact: whether he wanted the title or not, Zane had become the undisputed leader of the Heartlands. He could defeat anyone in the region single-handedly, and that wasn't even counting the hundred powerful warriors from the main continent who answered to him. Everyone knew it. Luckily for them, Zane had no ambition to unite the Heartlands and forge his own kingdom. If he did, who could possibly stop him?

[MUSIC: OMINOUS FORESHADOWING]

 But they were wrong. Zane did have an ambition—one far greater than anyone in the Heartlands could ever imagine. His goals stretched far beyond this small world.

Dorian pulled him aside before the day was out. He offered to send warriors with Zane to the Divine Kingdoms — several of them, powerful enough to make any enemy think twice. Zane shook his head. The Divine Kingdoms were stronger than even the main continent, and arriving with an armed escort would only draw the wrong kind of attention. He wasn't going there to fight. He just needed to find enough Ancient Heartstones to save Tia and get out. Starting a war with the four Divine Kingdoms was the last thing he wanted.

Dorian didn't press it. Besides, he and his warriors were in no hurry to leave. Oracle City had turned out to be a remarkably pleasant place to spend time — the food, the culture, the simple absence of anything trying to kill them. Amber was holding things together on the main continent. For once, they could just breathe.

Zane set his departure for seven days out.

He also made a decision about Tia. He wouldn't send her back to Cloud Hall. With him gone, old grievances among the founding families might quietly resurface — people's greed had a way of growing in an absence. The Guild was different. Kieran and the others had no quarrel with Tia, and the strength to protect her. She would stay with them.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Seven days later, in the Heavenly Stellar Palace, Cora leaned gently against Zane's back. She'd been staying there throughout, watching him sit beside Tia's bed and sighing. She felt the jealousy — she couldn't help it — but she also felt sorry for him. There was nothing she could do to change any of it. She could only accept that this man was about to cross an ocean for someone else.

"Can I come with you?" she asked softly, keeping her voice as gentle as she could.

"No," Zane said, without hesitating.

She'd expected that. She didn't get angry. "Just this once," she tried again. "Please."

Zane exhaled. He turned and looked at her — really looked at her — and reached out to touch her face gently. "Not this time," he said.

Cora pressed her lips together. Then, after a moment, something shifted in her expression, and she smiled. "Then promise me something. Next time I ask you for something — anything — you have to say yes."

"Promise you what exactly?" Zane asked.

"I don't know yet," she said, her face perfectly serious. "But when I ask, you have to say yes."

Zane looked at her for a long moment. Then he nodded.

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR WIND]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

 It was finally the day the hundred masters were set to leave.

Naturally, the three leaders of the World Merchants' Guild came to see them off personally, bearing priceless gifts. To everyone's surprise, however, Dorian and his warriors refused everything. A low-grade Relic-Class weapon might be a treasure here, but to men like Dorian, it was little more than a trinket.

What truly baffled the locals was Dorian's actual request: he asked for a few of the guild's most famous chefs and packed away a huge supply of the Heartlands' unique ingredients.

The chefs themselves, all warriors who had only reached the Adept or Soul Realm, were ecstatic. They'd served the guild their whole lives, but this was the opportunity of a lifetime. If they could please these Soul Sea Realm masters, maybe they'd be rewarded with a chance to break through to the next level of their own training.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

Before leaving, Zane took one last, long look at Tia, still lying motionless on the bed. 

[SFX: DOOR CLOSING]

[MUSIC: DETERMINED ORCHESTRAL]

 The moment he stepped out of the Heavenly Stellar Palace, however, the sorrow vanished from his face, hardening into pure determination.

He rendezvoused with Dorian and the others, his new thrall, Francis Carter, trailing silently behind him. While Dorian's group prepared to return to the main continent, Zane had a different destination in mind. A brief lull in the storms raging across the Chaotic Ocean of Stars had opened a window. 

 He was going to take it, cross the ocean, and finally enter the territory of the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms.
