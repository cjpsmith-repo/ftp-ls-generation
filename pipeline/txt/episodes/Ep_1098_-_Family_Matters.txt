Ep 1098 - Family Matters

Word count: 1089

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The Lord of the Sunset World, usually so calm, now looked completely baffled. “Who is this guy?” he asked.

Yolande just blinked, saying nothing.

[SFX: MURMUR OF REALIZATION]

Just then, one of the leaders of the Thirteen Palaces spoke up. “I remember him. He’s the young guy who clashed with Supreme Lord Winfred’s son during the Celestial Fruit Assembly.”

That incident was only a little over a decade ago, and the moment he said it, the memory clicked for the other palace leaders, too.

“His name is Lincoln Rhodes, right?” another leader chimed in. “That prodigy from the Major Martial World. The Gossamer Pavilion was ready to bend the rules to recruit him, but his beef with Winfred got him exiled to the Villain World. Layla Rhodes… Lincoln Rhodes… Are they actually brother and sister?”

[AMBIENT: CROWD CHATTER]

Meanwhile, outside the crucible, the younger fighters from the Thirteen Palaces were having a much bigger reaction than the leaders. To the palace heads, Lincoln Rhodes was just another talented kid they barely remembered from a minor squabble. It made sense that someone like the Lord of the Sunset World wouldn’t have a clue who he was.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]

But to the younger generation, Lincoln Rhodes was a legend. The second his burly figure appeared, a wave of recognition shot through the crowd.

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

It was easy for the everyday fighters to root for Lincoln, the guy who’d gotten a raw deal from a vindictive lord. All kinds of stories about him had been floating around for years, including a wild rumor that he once fought his way into Hell itself to save a friend from a horde of evil spirits. The Alliance’s top brass might have been clueless about those tales, but to the rank-and-file fighters, they were gospel.

Inside the Illusory Space, Layla could hardly believe what she was seeing. She’d always known her brother had crossed someone powerful and been thrown into the Villain World—and that he had eight hundred years left to his name. That had been her secret all along, the one thing she’d never told a single soul: she would grow strong enough, fast enough, to pull him out before that clock ran out.

Now, looking at his ragged clothes, all she could think of was how tall and strong he’d looked when she was small. Her eyes filled with tears.

“Layla. It’s really you.” Lincoln’s grin widened—he could still see traces of the little girl he remembered in her face. After everything he’d endured in the Villain World, no rules, no resources, just survival, he never expected to see his sister again, let alone see her grown into someone like this.

Layla nodded, eyes brimming, too overwhelmed to find words.

“Good. Very good,” Lincoln said. “Of all the days for us to find each other again—it’s the same day I win my freedom. Beat this kid, and I walk free.”

[MUSIC: GROWING TENSION]

Layla’s face changed. “Wait—you get your freedom by beating him?” She’d had no idea there was a reward like that on the table.

“Every death-row prisoner in this trial gets the same deal,” Lincoln said.

Layla’s eyes lit up, and she turned to Zane. “Zane—can you just... lose? Please?”

Zane blinked. An opponent had appeared out of nowhere, turned out to be Layla’s long-lost brother, and now Layla—of all people—was asking him to throw the match?

“I’ll forfeit too,” Layla pressed on. “I’ll give up my spot in the final trial completely—our results would still come out even. Please, Zane. I’ll owe you anything. Just name it.”

Zane hesitated. He’d never really thought of Layla as a true rival—Passing the Torch had always just been a test to him, nothing more. And he understood the weight behind her request better than she knew: he had a sister of his own out there, and still no word of where she’d ended up. If their places were reversed, he’d be asking the same thing.

He was about to say yes.

[MUSIC: TENSE PAUSE]

“Layla.” Lincoln’s voice cut through, sharp and proud. “Since when do you beg for anyone?”

“But—” Layla started.

“I’ve gotten this far on my own,” Lincoln said, cutting her off. “I’m not about to let my little sister go around asking favors on my behalf. Don’t you have any faith in your brother? You think I can’t handle this kid?”

The Rhodes family had never been anything special—minor nobility from some backwater border world, nowhere near the great houses of the universe. Whatever Lincoln had built, he’d built himself, fistfight by fistfight, all the way to the edge of a Gossamer Pavilion invitation. He’d never taken a handout in his life, and he wasn’t about to start now—not even from his own sister.

Zane let the offer go. There was nothing left to say. Watching the two of them, equally proud, equally stubborn, he could only shake his head. Families.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC DRUMS]

On the stage, Lincoln sized up his opponent. “So, you’re the one I’m up against. Not bad, for a guy at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm,” he said, his voice a low rumble. “Don’t listen to my sister. Come at me with everything you’ve got. If I can’t even handle an opponent this far below my level, then I don’t deserve this freedom.”

Zane just rolled his eyes. God, these siblings are arrogant, he thought.

[MUSIC: ANXIOUS STRINGS]

Layla, on the other hand, was a wreck. She knew her brother. There was no talking him down from something like this, and a knot of anxiety twisted in her gut.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

“Make your move!” Lincoln’s voice boomed across the arena. Even though Zane was only at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm, Lincoln was taking this seriously.

Since he was insisting, Zane held his sword, Thundergust, horizontally and gave a slight, formal dip of his head.

[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]

[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACKLE]

A moment later, a beam of light shot from the sword’s tip, and a whirlwind of lightning and wind blades carried his figure charging straight for Lincoln.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]

Outside the crucible, the buzz around Lincoln Rhodes only grew louder.

“Why didn’t Zane just forfeit?”

“I think he was going to, but Lincoln wouldn’t let him.”

“What? Lincoln’s a powerhouse. Why would he need Zane to forfeit? That’s a joke.”

And then, sword still blazing with lightning and wind, Zane closed the distance—and the fight was on.
