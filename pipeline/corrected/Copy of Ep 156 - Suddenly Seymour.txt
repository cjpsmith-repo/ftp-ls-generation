Ep 156 - Suddenly Seymour
Word count: 1143

[MUSIC: TENSE CHASE]
[SFX: ENERGY ATTACKS]
The Phoenix Crystal granted Zane extraordinary speed to evade Seymour's relentless attacks, but he knew it wouldn't last. Fatigue would eventually claim him, and without a counteroffensive strategy, his life would surely end beneath his opponent's blade."Just dodging him isn't enough" he contemplated, his body instinctively dropping beneath another vicious lunge from Seymour. "I have to strike back."The carefully crafted strategy Zane had initially conceived—combining his Assimilation Attack with Corrupt Vitality—had disintegrated the moment combat commenced. Seymour transcended the realm of ordinary Adepts; he stood as a formidable Soul Realm master, his power radiating with terrifying intensity.Desperation clawed at Zane's thoughts as he analyzed his dwindling options. "If I could just maneuver the black hawk into position," he calculated, sweat beading on his brow, "I might stand a fighting chance against him." His heart pounded against his ribs as frustration mounted. "Damn it all—he moves with such blinding speed that my hawk can't approach. What's my alternative?"
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
 Seymour didn't give him time to figure it out. He drew his sword and lunged, Burning Vitality flaring on his back. His shadow stretched and warped, swallowing the ground as he closed the distance.
[SFX: BLADE IMPACT]
[SFX: HUMAN GROAN]
 The monstrous shape yanked Zane back to reality, but it was too late. A searing pain shot through his shoulder.
Zane didn't pull back. He grabbed the blade with his other arm, clamping down on it with everything he had. The sword bit deep — through skin, through flesh, scraping bone — but he held. Seymour yanked. The sword didn't move.
He let go of the hilt and pulled back.
"What the hell?" Seymour grunted, annoyed. The strike had connected, but it hadn't done nearly the damage he'd intended.
His blade might not have a traditional edge, but it was still a mid-grade Spirit Weapon. He’d meant to take Zane’s arm clean off, then split him in two. Instead, the impact felt like hitting a solid steel wall.
"Well now, that's a surprise," Seymour mused, hovering in the air. "Your bones can't be that tough. Must be some kind of protection. No matter. I'll just have to chop you up piece by piece."
[SFX: MAGICAL GLOWING]
The Spirit Glyphs etched across Zane's chest had transformed his flesh into something extraordinary—a living, breathing mid-grade Spirit Weapon.Despite this remarkable protection, each strike from Seymour's blade sent waves of excruciating pain through his body. The agony felt like molten metal poured directly into his veins. Zane swallowed hard, tasting copper, as a chilling certainty settled in his mind—if this man walked away today, he wouldn't live to see another dawn."No more running," he thought, clenching his jaw as grim resolve crystallized within him. The familiar weight of fear pressed against his chest, but he pushed it aside. "Hiding won't save me, and it certainly won't eliminate this madman. To defeat him, I must embrace something wilder, more dangerous than his own insanity.""Surrender already!" Seymour bellowed, his face contorted with frustrated rage. Spittle flew from his lips as he added, "Even if you achieved the Adept Realm, you'd still be hopelessly outmatched! By the gods, you wouldn't stand a chance against me even if I were gravely wounded!"
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE ROAR]
[AMBIENT: ENERGY FLAMES]
The Blue Vitality erupted across Seymour's back, illuminating the arena with an eerie blue-green glow. He transformed into a brilliant streak of blue light, hurtling toward Zane with predatory precision and murderous intent."This is it," Zane whispered to himself, digging his heels into the ground and bracing for impact. His eyes, cold with resolve yet burning with defiance, narrowed as he calculated his final gambit. The taste of blood lingered on his tongue, a metallic reminder of his mortality. "Whatever it takes. Even if it costs me my life." 
[SFX: HEARTBEAT]
His heart hammered against his ribcage—not from fear, but from the terrible clarity that comes when one stands at the precipice between survival and oblivion. In that moment, Zane felt strangely calm, as though his entire journey had been leading to this desperate confrontation.
[SFX: IGNITION BURST]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
 In a blinding instant, over a hundred Dragon Scales erupted within Zane's body, their collective power surging through his veins like liquid fire. 
He orchestrated his attack with deadly precision, first propelling the jagged remains of the flying blade toward Seymour's position, its broken edge gleaming with malevolent purpose. Without hesitation, he commanded the black hawk—its eyes glowing with the corrupt Vitality that now controlled it—to follow the blade's path. 
[SFX: HAWK CRY]
The bird shot forward like a shadow given form, its wings cutting through the air with unnatural speed as it pursued the target Zane had marked for destruction. The twin assault represented everything Zane had become: calculated, merciless, and unwilling to rely on a single strategy when his survival hung in the balance.
As Seymour turned to face the hawk, a sharp spike of soul energy shot from between Zane's brows. It lasted less than a second — but Seymour's eyes went briefly vacant, his movement stuttering.
That was enough.
[SFX: SHIELD IMPACT CRACK]
 The black hawk slammed into Seymour's Cerulean Ward, and spiderwebs of red cracks instantly fractured its surface. A strange crimson energy began to seep through the fissures, widening them. Seymour frantically tried to channel his Vitality to repair the shield, but the red energy fought him back, blocking the flow.
[SFX: SHIELD BREAKING]
 "No! This is impossible!" Seymour shrieked as he felt the corrupting red energy breach his Cerulean Ward. Pure terror washed over him; he knew exactly what would happen the instant his shield gave way.
"I don't want to die!" Seymour begged, his voice cracking. "Please, just let me go! I swear I'll never come after you again!"
Zane stood just beyond the splintering Cerulean Ward, watching Seymour's face crumble with desperation. "That's not what you were saying a minute ago," he said, his voice flat and cold.
"Please! I-I didn't know you were this strong!" Seymour pleaded. "I really don't want to die. I can't die here."
"You're forgetting something," Zane said, pointing a finger at him. "In the Desperate Melee, there's only one winner."
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY CRACKLING]
 The Cerulean Ward, now webbed entirely in red, pulsed unstably.
[SFX: SHIELD SHATTERING]
 With one final, sharp crack, it shattered into a million pieces of light.
The moment the shield broke, Seymour spun to flee the path of the black hawk.
[SFX: IMPACT THUMP]
He darted away, but his escape was cut short by a sickening thump. Seymour’s eyes went wide with horror, his face a mask of agony.
[SFX: FLESH PIERCING]
 A hole had been punched clean through Seymour's skull, blood gushing from the wound. He collapsed to the ground, lifeless.
The shattered dagger had been hovering there the whole time. Zane had thrown it just outside the shield, betting Seymour would try to run.
In his panic, Seymour had run right into his own death.
[MUSIC: SOMBER VICTORY]
 Seymour was dead. The battle was over. As the last of his Raw Vitality faded, Zane slowly drifted back down to the ground.
