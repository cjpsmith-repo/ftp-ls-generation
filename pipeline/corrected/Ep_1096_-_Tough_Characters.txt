Ep 1096 - Tough Characters

Word count: 1144

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

Of course, Layla could handle these annoying opponents, but getting hit with one cheap shot after another was starting to get on her nerves.

After brutal fights, Zane and Layla reached the twentieth round - a record for the Thirteen Palaces. Their opponents were all middle-stage Soul Sea Realm warriors who had survived at least four Greater Celestial Tribulations. Fighting multiple Soul Sea Realm fighters simultaneously presented an entirely different challenge.

This was the round where Zane and Layla finally started to feel the heat. The gap between their Life-and-Death Realm abilities and the Soul Sea Realm was massive.

The biggest problem was the domains used by the Soul Sea Realm warriors; the sheer pressure from each one was causing them a ton of trouble.

[AMBIENT: OBSERVATION HALL]

“Looks like things are finally getting tough for them,” one of the palace leaders observed. “They’re not going to get past the twentieth round, are they?”

The Lord of the Sunset World just nodded. “It’s hard to say. This is a many-on-one fight, which is completely different from the test in the Enchanted Barrier Tower. The opponents are on another level, too.”

“Zane can already fend off attacks from Soul Sea Realm warriors, and he’s only at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm,” another leader remarked, his focus clearly on the young man. After Zane’s incredible performance in the tower, he had everyone’s attention. “I can only imagine what he’ll be capable of once he breaks through to the Soul Sea Realm.”

Another palace leader, however, had a different question.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FLUTE]

He looked over at Yolande. “I think I missed it. How many petals did Zane’s lotus flower have?”

At that, everyone turned to Yolande, their curiosity piqued. Before any warrior entered Passing the Torch, they went through a selection process where the Red Cloud Ring was supposed to sense their strength and estimate the number of lotus petals they’d activated.

But Yolande just shook her head. “I don’t know.”

“What do you mean, you don’t know?” a palace leader asked. “Did he fail the test?”

“No,” Yolande said. “When Zane passed through the Red Cloud Ring, he maxed out the sensor, and it just shut down. It was impossible to tell how many petals he activated.”

A ripple of shock went through the leaders.

The Lord of the Sunset World’s eyes flashed. “Are you telling me it’s possible Zane activated the Greater Lotus Flower?”

One of the Palace Lords raised another point. “After Passing the Torch ends, isn’t it tradition for warriors to attract their tribulations? About a hundred or so usually do, every cycle — they take what they’ve learned in here and push straight through to a breakthrough.”

Yolande’s brow creased. “...I’m not sure that’s wise. Not for him.”

“Why not?” the Palace Lord asked. “It’s standard practice. Half the geniuses who come through here end up doing it.”

Yolande hesitated a moment longer, then let out a slow breath. “...We’ll see what Zane wants to do.”

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Out among the thousands of warriors watching from outside, the crowd had gone tense again, eyes locked on the images flickering across their tablets.

[SFX: GROUND CRACKING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]

Six Soul Sea Realm warriors had Zane boxed into a pincer formation — six against one, and every one of them outranked him by realms. If word of this got out, it would be humiliating for them regardless of how it ended.

Adrian had told them beforehand: don’t break formation, don’t lose your nerve. No matter what, force this kid to use everything he has.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS BUILD]

But as the fight wore on, something strange happened — Zane wasn’t tiring. If anything, he was getting stronger, and the six of them could feel themselves being pushed back, suppressed by a Life-and-Death Realm “little guy.”

[SFX: EARTH RUMBLING]

[MUSIC: DESPERATE SURGE]

One of them threw up a domain of solid, unbreakable earth, planting himself in front of the other five — his strongest defense, the kind of wall that should have stopped anything.

[SFX: GROUND SHATTERING]

[MUSIC: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT]

Zane went through it like it was made of glass.

What followed looked less like a fight and more like a tiger let loose in a flock of sheep.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

At the exact same moment, Layla’s fight ended too. The pale green stone flared between her eyebrows — Wrath of the Ocean, her hidden trump card, finally unleashed.

Round twenty, passed. Both of them, together. The best result this trial had ever seen.

[MUSIC: BUILDING MOMENTUM]

But it wasn’t over.

Round twenty-one.

Round twenty-two.

Round twenty-three.

Round twenty-four.

By now, everyone had learned their lesson. In the tower, they’d guessed Layla’s limit and watched her blow past it to twenty-eight thousand two hundred miles. They’d guessed Zane wouldn’t pass thirty thousand, and he’d hit thirty-six thousand. Nobody was foolish enough to guess anymore — not after getting their faces slapped twice already.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

Meanwhile, back in the Illusionary Arena, the groups of warriors were being divided by strength, corresponding to the eleventh through twenty-fifth rounds. The fighters for the final rounds were prisoners held in three cages on a high platform. The twenty-fifth round was the final test.

Adrian had been calling the shots from the start. Even though his strategies against Zane and Layla hadn’t worked, everyone had seen how effective his thinking was, so they were happy to let him take charge. Now, he stepped back onto the high platform overlooking the enormous square.

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]

“If we’re going to take on that woman, we’ll need to send these condemned prisoners into the arena.”

“I heard that they’re some seriously tough characters from the Villain World.”

“And if they beat those two in the final round, they get their freedom. You can bet they’ll go all out for a prize like that, right?”

[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND ARENA]

[SFX: CAGE RATTLING]

With all eyes on him, Adrian walked over to a burly man in one of the cages. “Two people have made it to the final round,” he said. “A man and a woman. Which one do you want to fight?”

The burly man scoffed. “Are you kidding me? You think I’m going to pick the woman?”

“I’d suggest you do,” Adrian said quietly. “The man is incredibly strong. His power is off the charts, and I still haven’t seen any sign of his limits.”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

After all these rounds, he still hadn’t gotten what he wanted, and what really ate at him was that he was no closer to figuring out Zane’s true strength.

“How strong could he be?” the burly man asked.

“He’s at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm,” Adrian answered honestly.

The burly man’s eyes narrowed as he gave Adrian a cold stare. “Is this some kind of a joke? You’re trying to tell me a guy at that level is strong?”

Even if the kid was a top talent among the Thirteen Palaces, the fact that he was still only at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm was what really bothered the burly man.

He had no idea what was in store for him.
