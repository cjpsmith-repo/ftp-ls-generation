Ep 1079 - Ascenders

Word count: 1093

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

While everyone else was slowing down, Zane kept a steady pace, eventually catching up to the main pack of warriors.

Without even breaking his stride, he passed one. Then another. And another. He just kept moving, leaving a trail of exhausted competitors in his wake.

The warriors falling to the back knew they were probably done for. They just didn’t have the speed or the strength to keep up. Faced with the roiling chaos of the Law Storm ahead, some were already thinking about quitting. Every warrior had their own goals, after all. Some knew they weren’t the strongest and would be perfectly happy to walk away with a Tribulation Exterminating Pill and call it a day.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

The dangers were becoming more frequent. A pocket of warped space shimmered into existence inside the storm, and one warrior stumbled right into it. He was trapped. He tried to scramble back, but it was too late.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[SFX: BLADE SLASH]

A blade of black wind materialized out of thin air and sliced his arm clean off.

[SFX: FLAMES ROARING]

[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]

Another warrior accidentally triggered a shift in the local laws, and in a flash, he was engulfed in red-hot flames. At the same time, a volley of sharp golden lights shot through him, leaving deep gashes all over his body.

After the one-thousand-mile mark, things like this were happening all the time. More and more warriors were getting seriously hurt, forced to drop out.

The fighters in the Second and Third Tiers had slowed to a crawl, every one of them moving with extreme caution.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

But the warriors in the First Tier were still moving at an incredible speed. They were the best of the best from the Thirteen Palaces, the chosen few from the elites of three thousand worlds. On top of that, most of them were powerful enough to have activated the nine-petal lotus flower. Whether through raw talent or sheer hard work, they were just on a completely different level.

Because of this, the First Tier had pulled way ahead. By the time the Second Tier had barely struggled past the seventeen-hundred-mile mark, Layla, the confident leader of the pack, had already sailed past two thousand. The Third Tier was even farther behind, just now reaching fifteen hundred miles and looking like they were in real trouble.

Zane found himself somewhere in the middle of that struggling Third Tier.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS THEME]

As he moved, the storm offered glimpses of things he hadn’t expected. Purple ice crystals had formed across the ground in repeating geometric patterns — shaped that way by the Laws themselves, each formation holding a faint, distinct power. A little farther on, a series of terraced pools filled with what looked like purple liquid turned out to be a lightning pool, actual bolts condensed into something that moved like water. He appreciated both from a careful distance and kept walking.

Meanwhile, Layla, holding her first-place position, was the center of everyone’s attention. She had a special background, and even though she was competing for the Mist Palace, the other palace leaders didn’t really treat her like one of their own. She was something else entirely.

Many of the palace leaders were quick to sing her praises.

“That girl is going to be a force to be reckoned with,” one of them said.

“I agree,” another replied. “The old man sure knows how to pick them.”

The old man in cotton clothes turned his gaze across the table. “Those two behind her — Jasper and Zane. Both ascenders, correct?”

Yolande nodded once. “Yes. Both of them.”

“Ascenders.” The old man let the word sit for a moment. “Most of them come up and disappear without a trace. But every so often—” he glanced back toward the tower— “they produce someone who changes everything.”

“You’re not suggesting they could match the record,” one of the palace leaders said.

“Eighteen thousand miles,” another murmured. “No one has come close to that in living memory.”

The old man only smiled and said nothing.

They continued to murmur about Layla’s potential while the other warriors fought for their lives below.

[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND STORM]

Layla herself seemed completely unbothered, neither tense nor excited. After passing three thousand miles, she took a moment to rest. The next leg of the journey, from three thousand to ten thousand miles, wasn’t about pure speed, so she wasn’t in a hurry. She bit her lip and glanced back into the storm. Why isn’t Zane here yet? she wondered.

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

In a surprising turn of events, Zane finally broke out of the Third Tier and caught up with what was left of the Second. By the time he reached them, they had already passed the three-thousand-mile mark themselves. The Second Tier had started with over a thousand people. Now, only about three hundred remained. Seventy percent of them had been knocked out. The competition was brutal, and for those still standing, the prize felt so close, yet so impossibly far away.

[SFX: VINES TIGHTENING]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

A few hundred feet ahead, Jasper was tangled in a mass of golden vines that wound around his arms and legs. They weren’t alive — they were Wood Law, condensed naturally by the storm. Jasper was wrenching at them with everything he had left, his aura guttering. The vines held.

Zane floated up. “Do you need help?”

Jasper’s head snapped up. “No.”

“You can’t break those on your own,” Zane said.

“I want to prove my own strength,” Jasper said through gritted teeth. “I’m not relying on anyone.”

Zane read it immediately. “Because of Yolande?”

Jasper went still. “You called her by her name.”

“You’re not the only ascender who knows her,” Zane said simply. Then, quieter: “The Yolande you knew was a clone. When she was reabsorbed, that version stopped existing. Whatever you’re trying to prove — you don’t need to prove it to her.”

Outside, the palace leaders had gone quiet. Yolande’s expression shifted, just slightly. The old man covered a laugh with his hand.

Zane didn’t wait for a response. He walked on.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Back outside, attention shifted to a single point deep in the storm.

Layla had reached sixteen thousand miles.

Two thousand from the record - the mark that had stood untouched across every Passing the Torch since it was set.

The palace leaders stopped talking. Even the old man leaned forward.
