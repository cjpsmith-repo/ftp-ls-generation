Ep 590 - All the Way Up
Word count: 1068
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT STONE CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
The wall was covered in names, carved right into the stone and organized into nine neat rows.
It seemed whoever built the Tower of Sin had sorted everyone’s talent into nine distinct levels. The bottom row, Zane figured, was for the least talented.
He also noticed that when Yolande’s name appeared, every other name in the ninth row slid one spot to the right, pushing the last one clean off the wall. It made sense. As massive as the wall was, it couldn't possibly list everyone who’d ever been tested.
"That's normal," Olivia explained. "Everyone starts on the bottom row. But your name can climb from there."
"Got it," Zane nodded.
He had no idea what kind of test she was facing in there. All they could do was watch the wall and the names.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
A moment later, her name vanished from the ninth row, only to reappear in the eighth, knocking the last name on that line off the list.
Then it left the eighth and jumped to the seventh. Before they could even really register it, it was gone again, reappearing in the sixth row.
"So fast!" Olivia breathed.
[AMBIENT: CROWDED TOWER HALL]
On the tenth floor of the Tower of Sin, everyone kept a close eye on the other races. Whenever someone entered the Talent Tablet, a crowd would gather to watch the rankings shift. Some even collected the data and sent it up to the highest floors.
Every race wanted to produce a powerful, talented warrior. And at the same time, they hoped no one else would. That's why the Talent Tablet always drew such a crowd.
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Yolande’s name was now in the sixth row, which meant her talent was already on par with Olivia’s. It even bumped Olivia's own name back a spot.
Reaching the sixth row wasn't unheard of. In a few years, Olivia’s name would probably be pushed off the list entirely.
The crowd murmured, watching. Then the name jumped again.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
But Yolande's name was only in the sixth row for a split second before it leaped to the fifth.
[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]
A wave of shock rippled through the onlookers.
Only a handful of people had made it to the fifth row in recent years. Besides the Ogres and the Demon Night, only the absolute top prospects from a few other races had ever reached that high.
As for the humans, Olivia was the best the Violet Heart Sanctuary had to offer, and even she'd only ever made it to the sixth row.
The name held on the fifth row. The crowd exhaled.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
"Fifth row. That's already extraordinary."
"I thought she might make fourth. Guess not."
"Still, no human has ever placed this high. That's something."
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
Then the name moved again.
It jumped to the fourth row, landing ahead of Boris.
"Fourth row!"
"Called it — her talent has to be above Boris's."
"Of course it is. No one's beaten her on this floor in six years."
[SFX: DRAMATIC WHOOSH]
Before the noise had even died down, the name jumped again. Third row.
[AMBIENT: CROWD SILENCE]
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
A stunned silence fell over the crowd.
"Third row. She's second only to Amber in the entire history of this continent."
"If she'd just fight for the human race instead of playing by her own rules… she could change everything."
"What's the point? Yolande belongs to herself. Always has."
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]
No one could believe what they were seeing.
Then the name moved one final time. It settled into the second row — and pushed Amber's name back a place.
The crowd erupted.
[SFX: CROWD UPROAR]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Two hundred years ago, when the Ogres had dominated the Tower so completely that every genius from every other race was hunted down and killed the moment they showed promise, it was Amber who put an end to it. No assassination attempt touched her. No open challenge stopped her.
She climbed all the way to the top, claimed a seat, and killed twelve Ogre War Lords in the process — carving those seats away from the Ogres by force. When she finally left the Tower, she reshaped the entire continent.
Amber was a legend. Not just in the Tower — everywhere.
And Yolande had just pushed her name back.
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD WHISPERS]
"She displaced Amber—"
"That's impossible—"
"Who is this girl?"
Then, just as the noise was reaching its peak, the name moved again.
It climbed into the first row.
The crowd went silent.
Everyone in the Tower knew the five names in the top row. They were the most famous, most talented figures there were—impossible to forget. And everyone had their own theories about who they really were.
In fact, their ideas were pretty similar to what Zane was thinking.
Most people figured that a place as incredible as the Tower of Sin didn't belong in the Lower World. They believed the five names in the first row belonged to powerful beings from the upper world, their talent on a level so far beyond anything on the main continent it was literally the stuff of legend.
No one ever thought they’d see a new legend being made right in front of them.
But there it was. Yolande’s name had reached the first row, joining the legendary five. No one from the main continent had ever done that. Not Amber, not any of the other top talents. Ever.
[SFX: DOOR CREAKS OPEN]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
Her name settled into place, and the light on the wall slowly faded. The door creaked open, and she stepped out, her nine-colored skirt swaying with each step.
She glanced back at her name on the wall, her expression a complete blank. No joy, no excitement. It was as if this was the only possible outcome.
She walked right up to Zane and asked coldly, "Your turn?"
Zane nodded and walked toward the door without a second thought.
From behind him, Olivia called out, "Good luck, Zane!"
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL HUM]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BUILDUP]
He turned, gave her a quick nod, then stepped through the doorway and into the darkness. The surrounding lights dimmed, and the wall lit up once more, glowing with that same endless light.
