Ep 983 - The Eternal Scribe
Word count: 1108
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT LIBRARY]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
This was a kick in the teeth. The two stages of the Elysian Keep’s true path had been a nightmare, and now that he’d finally made it to the archives, there was no way in hell he was leaving empty-handed.
Zane vaulted onto a bookshelf in the first row.
Every book on the shelf had a cover made of some kind of metal. They were solid and ice-cold to the touch, and the books themselves felt unbelievably heavy. Zane couldn’t tell what the pages were made of, but he could tell the material was strong enough to be used in Weaponsmithing.
[SFX: HEAVY STRAIN]
He grabbed the side of a massive book and threw all his strength into pulling it. The book didn’t budge an inch.
“Is this thing really that heavy?” Zane muttered, his face twisting in disbelief. No way. This is impossible.
His full power could tear mountains from the earth, but he’d never imagined a single book could be this heavy.
Zane had always prided himself on his strength, but now he couldn’t even budge a single book. A frustrated scowl tightened his face. This time, he let the power of the Dragon Scales surge through him and gripped a corner of the book with everything he had.
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]
With a final roar of effort, he ripped it free from the shelf.
[SFX: MASSIVE OBJECT FALLING]
Below, the three women looked up just in time to see a book the size of a house come crashing down toward them, their faces frozen in shock.
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]
Horror seized him. “Move!” he roared. “Get out of the way!”
Zane had no idea how much the book weighed, but he guessed it was as heavy as a mountain range—hell, it felt like it had the weight of twenty-three mountains crammed into it. If that thing hit them, not even Tia could survive. Cora and Isla were even more vulnerable; they had to get clear.
Tia reacted in a flash. Her hands shot out, snatching Cora and Isla and yanking them to safety just in the nick of time.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT BOOM]
The instant the three women were clear, the massive book slammed down right where they had been standing.
Zane braced for the impact, but even though the ridiculously heavy book smashed into the floor, not a single crack appeared. It was as if someone had just dropped a paperback.
Zane couldn’t even guess how many countless years the Elysian Keep had existed. If it were built from ordinary stone, it would’ve crumbled to dust ages ago. Whatever this place was made of, it was something else entirely.
Zane jumped down, turning to the women with an apologetic, helpless smile.
[MUSIC: TENSE SILENCE]
Tia shot Zane a look that could freeze fire. She was not impressed.
[SFX: HEAVY PAGES TURNING]
He walked to the edge of the book and forced open the cover. The first page revealed rows of light-golden text — twisted, distorted, like runes but unlike any runes he’d ever studied. Not divine runes. Something else entirely. Something older.
Zane stared at them for a long moment. Then the others gathered around.
Nobody said anything. They were all looking at the same thing.
“I don’t understand a single word,” Zane admitted, a helpless smile crossing his face.
Tia leaned in. Cora squinted. Isla tilted her head. None of them could read it either.
[SFX: BOOK OPENING — DISTANT SHELF]
[MUSIC: GENTLE ETHEREAL THEME]
On a shelf further into The Archives, a book opened by itself.
Wisps of smoke curled upward from its pages. Then, slowly, a figure took shape within the smoke — a kindly-looking old man who stepped forward and walked toward them. He moved as if carried by the clouds, unhurried and serene.
He stopped in front of Zane and looked down at the open book on the floor.
“It’s a fine book,” he said mildly. “But you can’t simply open it and read it. I’m afraid you won’t recognize a single word that way.”
“Did I open it wrong?” Zane asked.
The old man nodded. “Close it. I’ll show you the correct method.”
Zane did as he was told. At that moment, Saul arrived from the corridor behind them.
“Ah,” Saul said, and for once, there was something warm in his voice. “Long time no see, old friend.” He turned to Zane. “This is the Eternal Scribe — the familiar who manages The Archives.”
The Eternal Scribe glanced at Saul with mild displeasure. “After all these years, you’ve only now brought someone worthy to the library. The master trusted you to guard this place. I’d say the evidence speaks for itself.”
Saul had the grace to look slightly abashed.
The Eternal Scribe turned back to Zane without further comment. He pointed at the book’s cover, and a golden spiral pattern bloomed across the metal surface.
“Pour your perception into that,” he said. “Don’t try to read the words. Let the book show you.”
Zane focused on the spiral and did as he was told.
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC VOID]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
The world inside the book was pure chaos. It was a space Zane couldn’t begin to describe—not total darkness, but pierced by faint rays of light from the edges. The whole place was shrouded in a gray mist, with a black-and-white fog churning through the air. Zane recognized that fog from the true path of the Elysian Keep. It was made of Dark and Light Energies.
[SFX: BOOM]
A massive bolt of lightning tore through the Chaos out of nowhere. I can’t even begin to comprehend the Law Power in that, Zane thought, stunned.
The Thunder Law within that bolt was perfect, absolute. All the lightning he’d ever witnessed before suddenly seemed flawed and incomplete by comparison. This wasn’t just about raw power; it was about perfection. He couldn’t be sure, but Zane guessed this had to be the tenth layer of Thunder Law.
Zane’s perspective whipped through the Chaos at incredible speed. He had no control; it was as if his consciousness was tethered to the book’s original owner, forced to witness the breathtaking scenes they had recorded. He was just a passenger on a journey through memory.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
After what felt like an eternity, the vision released him. Zane stumbled back from the book, a look of pure shock on his face.
If he was right, the Chaos he’d just seen was a place outside the universe itself. But what could possibly exist beyond the universe? And what did those strange, wild visions even mean?
The book offered no answers.
It was called the Chaotic Storm Chronicle — a journal of the original master’s travels through the endless void beyond the universe’s edge. The original owner had traveled an unknowable distance through the Chaos, but beyond that maelstrom, there was only more chaos.
It seemed to stretch on forever, without end.
