Ep 1161 - Faith Energy

Word count: 1091

[MUSIC: CALM ETHEREAL]
All across the universe, in countless temples, people prayed. 
But faith was a funny thing. It only existed if you believed it did. And if hundreds, thousands, or even millions of people knelt before a clay statue, that statue could become a god. 
This was the power of belief. To the warriors of the Upper World, it was known as Faith Energy.
A single believer meant nothing. But when trillions believed, the power became staggering - countless streams feeding into rivers, rivers into oceans. These oceans of faith formed the Demon Night’s Pools of Faith.
On their own, the Demon Night queens were hardly the strongest fighters in the universe; the three of them were merely on par with World Lords. But by drawing on the Faith Energy from their followers—channeled through millions of statues—they could defeat even the Supreme Lords. Their true strength came from belief, perhaps even more than from their own training.
[MUSIC: TENSE RISING POWER]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
As this energy now swirled around Lavender, her aura flared, and the blood-red glow in her eyes burned with a terrifying light.
[SFX: COMMANDING SHOUT]
“Now! Break!” she commanded.
[SFX: CRIMSON BEAM SHOOTING]
[SFX: RAPID DESCENT WHOOSH]
From the depths of the abyss, a thin, crimson light shot out. 
Then two more beams. Three. Then, countless — tearing through the ghost sphere from every direction at once, until the wails of a thousand ghosts merged into a single deafening cry and the sphere collapsed entirely.
[SFX: ARMOR MATERIALIZING]
In the silence that followed, Faith Energy condensed around Lavender. Blood-red armor formed plate by plate across her body. From her shoulders, long spikes extended outward and back - the longest stretching nearly ten feet. Two rows curved wide on either side like enormous wings, filling almost the entire width of the abyss.
She no longer resembled herself. She resembled something worshipped for a thousand years.
Zane felt it before he understood it - an instinct pulling toward submission, hardwired into living things that recognize the genuinely divine. He pushed it down, but it cost him. Even he wasn’t immune.
The Faith Energy had tainted her with divinity. Not a true god - not quite. But close enough that the difference didn’t matter.
“Give me the spear,” Lavender said. “And get ready.”
Zane handed over the Nightshard Spear without a word. He understood immediately. He twisted his body — legs up, torso curled, making himself as compact as possible.
Lavender raised the spear above him.
[SFX: SHARP CRACK]
She brought it down hard. The instant it connected, Zane straightened — legs driving against the shaft with everything he had — and the combined force of her strike and his push launched him downward like a bolt from a crossbow.
[SFX: RAPID DESCENT WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND DESCENT]
Lavender followed alongside him, the spiked wings of her armor cutting the air. Any danger that appeared in the abyss, she met with a single strike of the Nightshard Spear — one swing, cleared. Zane didn’t even have to slow down.
They passed the fourth cave without stopping.
“There’s another cave,” Zane said.
“Keep going,” Lavender said.
He kept going.
What should have taken four hours took less than one. The fourth round of the Endless Abyss fell behind them.
[MUSIC: ELEGANT REGAL]
[AMBIENT: PALACE HALL FOOTSTEPS]
Many supreme worlds away from the Dark World, the Queen of Punishment walked with a faint smile on her face, her ten-foot-long dress trailing silently behind her on the polished floor.
A well-dressed man from the Demon Night followed in her wake. He was lean and lanky, with silver hair and a handsome face, but what truly set him apart were his long, pointed ears. Among the Demon Night, perfectly straight ears were considered the height of beauty.
The Demon Night was a matriarchal race, and their women were almost always more skilled than the men. It was rare for a man to even approach the power of a World Lord. This one, however, was a notable exception, making him both impressive and unusual.
[SFX: COLD COMMANDING VOICE]
“Kaelan,” the Queen of Punishment said, her voice flat.
“Yes, my queen.” Kaelan placed a hand over his chest, ready for her command.
[SFX: AUTHORITATIVE DECREE]
“Seal off Lavender’s Pool of Faith,” the Queen ordered. “Then send Cassian, Taryn, Maren, and Paige to the Dark World.”
Kaelan hesitated. “But… the Board of Elders?”
“They won’t say a word,” the Queen replied, her expression unreadable. “Anyone who speaks against me ends up dead.”
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL OMINOUS]
Sealing a Pool of Faith was an unprecedented taboo. The three pools absorbed Faith Energy from trillions of Demon Night followers - the very source of Lavender’s power. Once sealed, she could no longer draw from it.
The Pools belonged to no single queen but to the entire race. Sealing one by force would trigger massive backlash from the Board of Elders.
The Queen of Punishment had left it open deliberately - a trap. She’d expected Lavender to draw from it eventually, revealing her location. But Lavender had never touched her Pool since ascending.
It was the golden-haired woman who had changed that. Her bloodline activation had triggered the Power of the Three Queens without Lavender’s intent — forcing her hand, and forcing the Queen of Punishment’s.
Now that Lavender was exposed and already drawing, there was no longer any reason to leave the Pool open. Better to seal it and cut off the source before she could use it to do any more damage.
This all played into the high stakes of Zane’s own quest: the Amethyst Thunderoak.
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT FOREST AMBIENCE]
After a hundred thousand years, how much had it grown? Had it become an entire forest? If Zane could just get his hands on its sacred shoots, he could unlock the secrets of the sixth level of the Thunder Law and gain unbelievable power.
But to get to it, he needed Lavender’s help to get through the fifth circle, and time was running out for her. 
Two things limited her. Distance from the Pools — she was drawing across an enormous gap, and the connection grew less stable the further she stretched it. And her incomplete form — without a body of her own, she couldn’t channel the full depth of what the Faith Energy could give her. Whatever power she had now was a fraction of what she could do if she were whole.
Zane might have been able to clear the circle on his own eventually, but they didn’t have that luxury. They had to move as fast as humanly possible, and worry about the Amethyst Thunderoak once they were through.
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC]
[SFX: LIGHTNING WHOOSH]
With a deafening rush of air, Zane shot past the fourth cave at lightning speed, just as he had the third. 
[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACT]
A moment later, he plunged into the fifth circle—the final ring of the Endless Abyss.
