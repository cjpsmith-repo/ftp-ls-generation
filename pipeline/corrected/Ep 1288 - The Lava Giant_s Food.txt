Ep 1288 - The Lava Giant’s Food

Word count: 1026

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
[AMBIENT: DEEP CAVE]
Zane kept searching, working his way along the cavern’s edge as the others fanned out behind him, all scanning the rock for anything they’d missed. 
The walls here were rougher, more broken, riddled with stone pillars and stalagmites jutting out at odd angles, but they were spaced too far apart to be used as footholds. 
Zane ground his teeth in frustration. Another dead end.
Getting through the third hall hadn’t been so bad—at least it had given them clues, like the long hooks on the side that helped them piece the puzzle together. But this Fourth Hall? It wasn’t giving them a damn thing to work with. 
[AMBIENT: BUBBLING LAVA LAKE]
How in the world were they supposed to cross the massive lake of lava bubbling in front of them?
Zane’s gaze dropped to the molten rock, fixing on the giant standing waist-deep in it. 
[SFX: FLAMES SPURTING]
[SFX: LAVA OOZING]
The creature’s body was a mess of cracks that spurted flames and oozed lava. As he watched it, an idea sparked.
“Maybe the path *is* the lava giants,” he murmured.
Janet, who had been studying them too, heard him and smiled. “I was thinking the same thing.”
A mountain-sized lava giant stood near their side of the lake. A couple thousand feet behind it was another, and another after that. They were lined up almost perfectly, like a makeshift bridge leading to the exit on the other side.
“But how could we possibly convince them to help us cross?” Jeffrey asked, skeptical.
The idea was nuts, but no one had a better one.
Zane nodded toward the closest giant. “I’ll get that big guy’s attention and draw it over.” He pulled out his bow and notched a black arrow from his quiver.
No one was sure it would work, but they didn’t dare stop him. Zane had been the key to getting them this far; if he had a plan, they let him run with it.
[SFX: ARROW WHOOSH]
The black arrow shot forward with a sharp *whoosh*. It had a long way to travel. 
[SFX: SONIC CRACK]
About halfway to its target, the arrow let out a sonic crack and kicked into a second stage of acceleration, suddenly flying faster and with even more power.
[SFX: ARROW IMPACT EXPLOSION]
The arrow slammed dead center into the giant’s chest, a target too massive to miss, and a pulse of black energy exploded from the point of impact. They all held their breath, staring at the lava giant.
But after a few tense breaths, nothing happened. The giant kept turning slowly, as if the arrow had never landed at all. Whatever Zane and the others were to a creature this size, they were mosquitoes—not even worth noticing.
“It’s useless,” Zane muttered, and turned to Hailey. Her bow was a Divine Weapon—if his arrow hadn’t worked, maybe hers would.
She understood the look before he even asked. Drawing the Moonburst Flamebow, she took aim.
[SFX: BOWSTRING VIBRATING]
[SFX: FLAME BURST IMPACT]
A spinning ball of flame roared from the bow and slammed into the giant, spreading fire across its molten body. But the giant only seemed to drink the flames in—every trace of it vanished into the cracks along its skin. It made sense, in hindsight: a creature born from the Fire Law itself couldn’t be burned by fire.
“It’s useless too,” Hailey said, shaking her head.
Just then, a large stone sailed over their heads and smashed into the giant’s shoulder. 
Coleman stood there, still holding his throwing pose, grinning sheepishly. “I wanted to try too.”
[SFX: STONE SHATTERING]
The rock shattered instantly against the giant’s massive frame.
“That rock’s nothing but a pebble to something that size,” Jeffrey said flatly—he’d never had much patience for Coleman, not since the day he’d threatened to kill him. Demon Race, and about as useful as a rock himself, as far as Jeffrey was concerned. “What exactly did you think that was going to do?”
Coleman just grinned and pointed. “Hey—it hit!”
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING GROWL]
Then the giant shuddered, and began to move.
Coleman’s grin vanished. “Wait—it’s not actually coming after me, is it? I was just having some fun!”
Zane, Jeffrey, and Janet all exchanged baffled looks. Neither the arrow nor Hailey’s flame had so much as made the giant blink—so why would a thrown rock provoke it?
“Coleman, where did you even find that rock—” Janet started to ask, but by the time she turned around, he was already gone, sprinting for the mouth of the Hall.
Jeffrey snorted. “Guess he was born in the wrong body. Didn’t figure a Demon Race warrior for the type to run from a little movement.”
“Wait, look!” Hailey said. “It wasn’t even bothered by the hit.”
Everyone looked. The giant hadn’t flinched. 
[MUSIC: WHIMSICAL PLAYFUL]
Instead, its attention had been drawn by something else entirely: dozens of flame kittens that had suddenly appeared in the molten lava, swimming around its legs. It wasn’t Zane’s arrow that had made the creature finally move, but these things.
[SFX: KITTENS MEOWING]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS IN LAVA]
The lava giant lumbered forward, trying to grab them. 
[SFX: SPLASHING LAVA]
The flame kittens were quick, darting through the viscous lava, but only a few managed to escape the giant’s massive hand as it scooped them out of the lake.
[SFX: CREATURE EATING]
Once the creature had a fistful of them, it shoved them straight into its mouth.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE BOOMS]
[SFX: SHRILL KITTEN CRIES]
[SFX: FIRE ERUPTIONS]
The flame kittens let out shrill cries as they exploded inside the lava giant’s mouth, erupting in bursts of wild, writhing fire.
Janet’s eyes lit up. “The flame kittens! They must be their food. It’s *eating* them!”
“So you’re saying the only way to get their attention is to use those things as bait?” Zane murmured, watching the giant chew.
Janet nodded, but her forehead creased with worry. “Yeah, but look at them. Those flame kittens don’t exactly look easy to deal with. Are we really going to have to catch them just to get that thing to look at us?”
