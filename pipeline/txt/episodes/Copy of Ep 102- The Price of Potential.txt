Ep 102 - The Price of Potential
Word Count: 1088
[AMBIENT: PRIVATE CHAMBERS]
[MUSIC: GRAND ORCHESTRAL THEME]
House Blackwood had built a reputation to rival even the Seven Ruling Families of the Empire—an achievement born not from coincidence but from their extraordinary wealth and strategic acumen. Their influence stretched across the realm like an invisible web, connecting powerful figures through mutual interests and calculated alliances.
They transcended the role of mere business investors; they were architects of human potential. With an uncanny ability to identify promising talent before others recognized it, House Blackwood nurtured relationships with those they believed would shape the future. As a self-made powerhouse that had risen from modest beginnings, they recognized in Zane the same fierce determination and untapped potential that had once defined their own ascent. The glint in Corbin Blackwood's eyes whenever he looked at Zane betrayed this recognition—a mixture of curiosity and anticipation for what this young man might become.
So, while they wouldn't openly side with him in his showdown with Felix Sterling, they would definitely have his back behind the scenes.
"Typically, The Gilded Gavel takes a ten percent commission from any auction," Corbin Blackwood informed Zane. "But you're a friend of Dustin's. For you, we're waiving the fee."
"No, I couldn't ask you to do that," Zane said, politely refusing. Ten percent of his auction earnings was a fortune, but he hated the idea of owing anyone a favor.
"If you refuse, then you're telling me we're not friends," Corbin insisted. He felt they owed Zane a little something after staying quiet during the whole mess with Felix Sterling a few minutes ago. Now that he was putting it like that, Zane felt backed into a corner. He had no choice but to accept.
"Thank you," he managed.
[SFX: CURTAIN SWISH]
[SFX: ENERGY HUM]
With a soft swish, Corbin swept back the curtain to the back hall, revealing a literal mountain of Crystal Cubes.
"Zane, the Phoenix Gem fetched seventy thousand Crystal Cubes," Corbin elaborated, his voice carrying a hint of admiration. "After subtracting the twenty-five thousand for Barrett's Principles of Forging, you're left with forty-five thousand—quite the handsome sum for a single day's transaction."
[SFX: CRYSTAL TINKLING]
[MUSIC: AWESTRUCK WONDER]
Ethan, a fellow pledge and one of Zane’s friends at Misty Peak, stood motionless alongside Rhys, their jaws slack with astonishment as they gazed at the glittering crystals scattered across the table. The light danced off each facet, creating a mesmerizing display of wealth that seemed almost unreal.
"Zane... that Phoenix Gem belonged to you?" Ethan finally managed, his voice cracking with disbelief. The pieces suddenly aligned in his mind—Zane's unwavering confidence during the bidding war, his casual dismissal of Felix Sterling, his absolute certainty in acquiring the forging manual. "No wonder you weren't intimidated. You're practically a millionaire in the making."
"Good heavens," Rhys whispered, running trembling fingers through his hair as he leaned closer to the pile of crystals. His eyes reflected their brilliance, wide with wonder and a touch of envy. "I've never witnessed such wealth in my entire life. Not even in my wildest dreams."
Coming from ordinary backgrounds, none of them had ever had much to their names. Even though they were all pledges from Misty Peak, the difference between them and Zane now felt like a chasm.
It was incredible that he had earned so much so soon after joining the Skyreach Sect.
But a new problem hit Zane: what was he supposed to do with it all? His Storage Ring barely had enough room for a fraction of the cubes. There was no way he could carry them, and even with Ethan and Rhys helping, it was an impossible task.
He’d been so focused on the sale, he’d never considered the logistics of it.
Seeing the awkward look on Zane's face, Dustin Blackwood just smiled. "Don't worry, Zane. You don't have to haul these around. Check them out, and then we'll deposit them in the Sacred Vault for you."
[AMBIENT: BUSY RESTAURANT]
[MUSIC: ELEGANT DINING]
As a resident of The Capital, Ethan navigated the city's establishments with practiced ease. After exchanging a few words with the host at the Golden Prairie Diner, the trio was escorted to an exclusive private room on the second floor. The interior surpassed even the elegant façade of the building—plush velvet chairs surrounded a polished mahogany table, while ornate lanterns cast a warm, inviting glow across the richly decorated walls.
Settling into his seat, Ethan leaned forward with a glint of excitement in his eyes. He straightened his collar before addressing the waiter, "I've heard whispers throughout The Capital that Hazel's singing transcends the ordinary—that her voice can transport listeners to another realm entirely. Might we be fortunate enough to experience her performance this evening?" His voice carried a mixture of hope and anticipation as he glanced at his companions.
"Uh…" The waiter’s smile tightened. He sized them up—the white robes of Skyreach pledges, no obvious signs of wealth. He’d seen their type before, kids with more ego than coin, looking to stir up trouble. Hazel was the grill’s headliner, and her fee was astronomical.
Noticing the waiter's hesitation, Ethan just shrugged at Zane. "Well, it's your call."
Zane was frugal, but he wasn't cheap. He met the waiter's skeptical gaze. "Are you worried we can't afford it?" he asked, his voice even. "Is this enough?"
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
[SFX: CRYSTAL CUBES CLATTERING]
As he spoke, a dozen glittering Crystal Cubes tumbled from his Storage Ring and clattered onto the table.
The waiter's eyes widened to saucers. A single crystal cube represented generational wealth, enough to sustain an entire family for decades. The small fortune casually displayed on the table exceeded Hazel's fee several times over. His skepticism dissolved instantly, replaced by an obsequious smile that revealed his suddenly ravenous ambition.
"Absolutely, distinguished sir! One moment, if you please," he purred, his voice transforming into honeyed subservience. "I shall personally arrange for Hazel's attendance without delay."
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS HURRYING AWAY]
With a bow so deep it nearly folded him in half, he backed away before turning to rush from the room, footsteps quickening as he disappeared.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ELEGANT]
Minutes stretched like honey in winter. Then, from beyond the doorway, came the delicate rhythm of approaching footsteps—unhurried, deliberate, commanding attention with each step. A fragrance drifted into the room, an intoxicating blend of night-blooming flowers and exotic spices that seemed to caress the senses.
The perfume thickened the air, announcing her imminent arrival with an almost supernatural presence that made conversation falter and heartbeats quicken. The very atmosphere seemed to hold its breath in anticipation.
