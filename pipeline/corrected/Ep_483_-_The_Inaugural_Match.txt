Ep 483 - The Inaugural Match

Word count: 1054

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT ARENA]

[MUSIC: DARK EPIC]

It was a cruel world these powerful warriors lived in.

Throughout the Eastern Territories, warriors who reached the Adept Realm commanded respect wherever they traveled. Yet those who made it to the Soul Realm wielded power that transcended mere admiration—they inspired awe, fear, and sometimes worship.

For the hundreds of prisoners trapped in the arena's depths, fate had become a cruel mockery of their former glory. Each had once been a strong warrior in their own right, celebrated in their home regions, feared by their enemies. Now they served as nothing more than expendable fuel for Oracle City's spectacle, their lives measured only by how many years they could add to another's existence. Their past triumphs, their hard-won victories, their years of disciplined cultivation—all of it meant nothing in the face of their current degradation.

[SFX: HEAVY DRUM STRIKE]

[SFX: GROANING CHAIR]

After striking the drum, Kieran tossed the drumstick aside with casual disdain. The massive man squeezed his considerable frame back into his reinforced chair, which groaned under his weight.

[AMBIENT: SILENT ARENA]

An expectant silence descended over the arena like a heavy blanket. Most warriors remained motionless in their seats, their minds still reeling from the otherworldly sensation of the golden light that had washed over them moments before.

[SFX: VOICE AMPLIFICATION MAGIC]

After allowing the silence to build, Kieran channeled his Vitality into his throat and voice, amplifying his words until they boomed across the crowd like thunder rolling through mountain valleys. "Before the Martial Tournament begins, I have an announcement to make. And I want every single one of you to spread the word far and wide."

The crowd stirred uneasily, puzzled by his sudden shift in tone and the cryptic nature of his message. What could possibly warrant such a dramatic buildup?

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

Kieran continued, “Someone in our World Merchants' Guild has discovered that the ninth Enchanted Lamp from the Celestial Tomb of Vesper has appeared.”

The announcement dropped like a stone in a silent lake.

[AMBIENT: CROWD ERUPTS IN DISCUSSION]

After a stunned pause, the crowd erupted into fervent discussion.

"An Enchanted Lamp from the Celestial Tomb of Vesper?" someone gasped. "The same ones from the Festival of Illumination? The last one has actually been found?"

A woman near the front leaned forward, her eyes wide with disbelief. "Incredible! It's been a thousand years since the last lamp was lit. For it to turn up now…"

"How can they be certain?" another voice challenged, though the speaker's trembling hands betrayed his excitement.

An older cultivator stroked his beard thoughtfully. "You know what they say—anyone who lives over a thousand years is practically a monster. The World Merchants' Guild has some ancient powerhouses in its ranks. I'd wager one of them figured it out."

Zane felt the weight of history pressing down on him. A thousand years. The number seemed impossible, yet the reverence in every face around him confirmed the magnitude of this discovery. His pulse quickened despite himself.

[AMBIENT: FESTIVAL CROWD]

[MUSIC: FESTIVE FLUTES]

The Festival of Illumination was held every year in The Heartlands. Ordinary folk would light lanterns and hang decorations, releasing prayer lanterns into the night sky while their children ran laughing through the streets with smaller lanterns of their own.

For ordinary people, it was just a holiday.

[MUSIC: EPIC DRUMS]

But for warriors, the Festival of Illumination meant something more. They weren’t interested in ordinary lanterns. Their dream was to light one of the Enchanted Lamps from the Celestial Tomb of Vesper.

They had celebrated countless Festivals. And now, this year, the final lamp had appeared.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC HORNS]

“If the person who found the last lamp is ready, they can light it at this year’s Festival of Illumination. Then, we can all enter the celestial tomb and claim what we desire,” Kieran declared, his words a clear challenge to whoever possessed the final lamp.

[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]

Tia's eyes met Zane's. She had retrieved the Enchanted Lamp from the Blood Fiend Emperor's treasure chest. Only they knew this secret.

Zane was conflicted. The Celestial Tomb was an Immortal's resting place—whatever lay inside would prove invaluable. But the Martial Arts Conference demanded his full attention.

"I also possess a Spirit Lamp!" Kieran's voice rang out. "I hope all Spirit Lamps will be lit simultaneously during this year's Festival! If we divide the inheritance among ourselves, our overall strength will rise to another level!"

With that, the three chairs descended, and the Guild leaders landed on the stage.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT FANFARE]

Kieran strode to the center of the platform. "The Martial Arts Conference has officially begun!"

Attendants carried forth a green jade platform engraved with dragons and phoenixes—a lottery box full of nameplates.

[SFX: DRUM ROLL]

Kieran drew two nameplates. “The opening match: independent warrior Dawson Jones versus Segar Sutton of the Mysterious Luna Hall!”

"Dawson Jones? The Divine Walker who ventured into the Chaotic Ocean of Stars alone?"

“Who is this Segar Sutton? I've never heard of him. Dawson will wipe the floor with him.”

[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

The Mysterious Luna Hall was a deeply secretive sect, aligned with neither the Righteous nor the Evil Factions. The group itself was known, but its members were ghosts. Among the hundreds of thousands of warriors in the stands, not one had ever heard the name Segar Sutton.

Elder Luna overheard the chatter but said nothing. He sat in an old wooden armchair, a sharp gleam in his eyes.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]

“Segar, it’s time,” he said. “You got lucky, drawing the first match. It would be a shame to leave that arena without drawing blood. Go kill him.”

Not far away, an ordinary-looking young man nodded.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

He leaped from one of the platforms and strode onto the field.

This wasn't some friendly competition between schools; the Martial Tournament was a matter of life and death. There were no rules and no holds barred. No one cared who the fighters were. There was no room for pity, because showing mercy to an opponent was a death warrant for yourself.

[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER RISING]

[AMBIENT: ARENA TENSION]

A curtain of green light slowly rose, enclosing the entire field.

Not long after Segar Sutton took the field, Dawson Jones entered from the other side.

There were no ceremonies, no formal greetings. The two challengers were like caged beasts, waiting for the barrier to drop. The tension was so thick you could cut it with a knife.

[SFX: BARRIER DISSOLVING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]

With a soft whoosh, the light curtain vanished. Dawson Jones stomped his foot, and waves of Vitality rippled out from under him. He shot toward Segar like a lightning bolt.
