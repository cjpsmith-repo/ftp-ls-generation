Ep 391 - The Real Prize

Word count: 1137

[AMBIENT: WET ORGANIC INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]

[SFX: OTHERWORLDLY SNAKE HISSES]

Zane's boots slid across the slick, pulsing floor of the Drake's insides as he steadied himself. 

Around him, ethereal serpents of flame coiled through the damp air. The snakes writhed and hissed as they circled the chamber like predators waiting to strike.
[SFX: MECHANISM CLICK]
[AMBIENT: HUM OF ANCIENT POWER]
His companion moved to the corner of the chamber and pressed her palm against a hidden panel, pushing a thread of Vitality into it. 
Lines of light raced along the walls — and the Drake's entire interior blazed to life. What had seemed like a dim, fleshy tunnel was something else entirely: a palace, sealed inside a beast.

The Cobalt Dragon's voice echoed in his mind. "That girl, the Cloud Road Disciple? Her name is Tia; I've been watching her since you two hit the Death Road. She's on a whole other level than you, Zane. But she must need you for something. Why else would she have pulled you onto that floating stone with her?"

"The real question is how she got this powerful," Zane muttered. It was the one thing he couldn't wrap his head around, weighing on him like an anchor. How could she be this strong if she just hit the Soul Realm?

The Cobalt Dragon went quiet for a moment. "My guess? She's trained in a rare technique. Something like the Cultivation of Nine Rebirths or the Twelve Cycles of Penance. In fact, there's almost no way this is her first time reaching the Soul Realm."

Zane's eyebrow shot up. "Seriously? If that's true, how did she even get on the Cloud Road? It doesn't make any sense."

A faint smile seemed to color the dragon's tone. "Those methods are brutal. They force the user to start over from scratch, again and again. Take the Cultivation of Nine Rebirths—it makes you give up your power nine times. Each time, you have to rebuild from the very bottom with the same fire and determination. Only then can you break through to a whole new level of power. This probably isn't her first rodeo at this stage. Her real power could be late Inner-Core Realm... or even higher."

Zane was floored. Late Inner-Core Realm? Or even higher?

[SFX: VIOLENT TEARING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

Just then, Tia's hands shot out, snatching a puppet snake clean out of the air mid-strike. Vitality flared brilliant white in her palm, crackling with barely contained power. With a sickening twist of her wrist, she ripped the construct in two as though it were nothing more than parchment.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

"Whoa…" The word escaped Zane's lips unbidden as his heart hammered against his ribs. A tremor ran through him—not from fear exactly, but from the sudden, visceral understanding of just how outmatched he truly was. That level of strength was terrifying. He'd only caught a flicker of her unique Vitality signature, yet it was abundantly clear her raw physical power transcended anything he'd encountered before.

His mind raced. She didn't even use a weapon. Just her bare hands. The casual brutality of it unsettled him more than any elaborate technique would have. There was something deeply unnerving about someone who could destroy a Core Strength-level construct with the same effort most people used to snap kindling.

Zane swallowed hard, his earlier confidence wavering. Whatever game Tia was playing, whatever her true objective on the Cloud Road, he was now certain of one thing: crossing her would be suicide.

[AMBIENT: FLESHY CAVERN]

[SFX: TEARING FABRIC]

All he could do was follow in her wake. Tia snatched the last few puppet snakes with her bare hands, tearing each one apart and tossing the pieces aside like trash. She shredded them like they were wet paper.

Zane, however, was practically giddy. The materials alone were valuable, but the intricate engineering made the destroyed puppets priceless.

Tia noticed Zane gathering the broken puppets and shot him a look of pure disdain. "You don't need to pick up that junk. Help me get what I came for, and that stuff will look like pocket change."

The jab stung, but Zane ignored it, collecting the "corpses" of all six puppet snakes and stashing them in his Storage Ring before jogging to catch up.

[AMBIENT: EERIE PULSING]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

After trekking for a while through the Drake's bizarre, fleshy innards, Zane spotted a red light pulsing up ahead. Something was flickering wildly inside it. "What's that?" he asked.

Tia's face lit up, and she broke into a run. "The Key!" 

Zane hustled to keep up.  "A key? To what?" 

[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]

[AMBIENT: FURNACE CHAMBER]

The red glow came from a round, three-legged furnace, the kind used for alchemy. It was blazing with fire, and hovering in the heart of the flames was a long, narrow spar that seemed to pulse with a slow, steady rhythm. That's the 'Key'?

"It's the Key to all the treasures on the Cloud Road!" Tia breathed, her eyes glued to the crystal in the Soulforge, her voice filled with a reverence Zane hadn't heard before."Before the Sixth-Tier Sect fell, they hid everything here. Whoever gets that Key gets it all."

[MUSIC: EPIC REVELATION]

"You mean... the Blood Fiend Emperor's entire hoard?" Zane's eyes widened, and he drew a sharp breath. The Blood Fiend Emperor commanded a Sixth-Tier Sect. Cloud Hall was barely a fourth-Tier.

No wonder the prizes on the Cloud Road were so insane. The Blood Fiend Emperor must have stashed part of his treasury here. The road was so long, it was practically a vault…
"I've been here once before," Tia said, still staring at the crystal. "I couldn't get it. There's an enchanted barrier around the furnace — I can't break it alone."

Zane glanced at her. "So that's why you brought me."

"Frank's Spear Intent has penetrating power — he was my first choice. But your Demonic God Fist, with my Vitality behind it, is more likely to punch through." She said it flatly, like it was simple arithmetic. "That's why I chose you instead."

Zane said nothing. He was starting to understand exactly how long she'd been planning this.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Her face set with determination, Tia gathered her Vitality and blasted it straight at the furnace.

[SFX: EXPLOSIVE FLAMES]

[AMBIENT: ROARING FIRE]

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

The moment her power hit, the Soulforge erupted. Flames exploded outward, twisting and coalescing into the shape of a snarling dragon!

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FLAMES]

Tia yelled, her voice tight."Get back! That's a Fiery Dragon Spirit! A fire elemental—and it's unbelievably strong!" For the first time, her expression was grave as she squared up to the creature. She flipped her hands over, revealing a sky-blue gem that pulsed with a chilling cold, dropping the air temperature in a heartbeat.

Tia tapped the gem, then stretched out a finger. She traced small circles on its surface, as if coaxing something out. After a few rotations, she flicked her finger forward and shouted, "Go!"

[SFX: ICE PHOENIX CRY]

[AMBIENT: FREEZING WIND]

Zane couldn't believe his eyes.A brilliant Ice Phoenix erupted from the gem. It let out a piercing shriek and soared straight for the Fiery Dragon Spirit.
