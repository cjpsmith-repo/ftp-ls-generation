Ep 43 - Steel and Status
Word Count: 1200
[AMBIENT: FANTASY WILDERNESS]
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
Kids from the Great Houses had it made— Powerful boosting items by the handful, the best training money could buy, and powerful bloodlines passed down from their house elders.
Most of them were born halfway to the finish line compared to normal people. It turned a lot of them into entitled brats, living reckless lives on their Houses' name. They consumed anything to get stronger, but it left them with inflated egos and no real fighting skills.
Not that Zane thought every kid from the Great Houses was like this. If they were, their houses wouldn't have held power in the Capital for centuries.
There had to be plenty of them who were genuinely gifted, who trained their asses off. But the guys he was looking at right now? They clearly weren't in that league.
These guys were too lazy to hunt beasts themselves, so they just ripped off other participants for their crystal fragments instead. The sheer audacity of it—expecting something for nothing—made Zane's blood boil. How could people be so shameless?
He hung back and watched.
They were only targeting the commoners, letting anyone with a Great House sigil pass without a word. Did the Skyreach Sect really not care that they were robbing people in broad daylight?
Zane wasn't sure, but it was clear this had been going on for a while. A few years back, some students had figured out this was the perfect chokepoint between the second and third levels.
They'd set up a roadblock and charge a "toll" of crystal fragments for any commoner trying to pass.
It was an easy way to score fragments, thin the herd, and bully anyone who couldn't fight back.
[SFX: ROAR AND SWORD SWING]
[SFX: WIND GUST]
Suddenly, a massive, towering man with a greatsword let out a roar and charged the pass. He was a beast of a man with arms like tree trunks, and as he swung his sword, the force of it sent a gust of wind howling past the faces of the Great House students, making them flinch.
If these rich kids were any good, they wouldn't be here shaking down other students for scraps in the first place.
Faced with the big man's assault, the bullies started cursing. They couldn't stop him, so they scrambled to either side of the path, leaving a wide-open gap.
[SFX: CROWD RUSHING]
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC BATTLE]
That was all the encouragement the other students needed. Seeing their chance, the commoners who'd been hesitating surged forward, sprinting after the towering man through the pass. More and more joined the rush as they saw others breaking through. Just like that, the Great Houses' blockade was broken, and the commoners poured through the pass like a river bursting a dam.
Zane moved with them, swallowed by the crowd.
The Great House students' faces twisted in outrage. They never saw it coming—not one guy breaking through, and definitely not a whole mob right behind him.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT BREAKTHROUGH]
By the time they reacted, dozens of students had already slipped past and made it to the third level of Bloody Mountain.
"Stop them!" the one in blue screamed.
Seeing the chaos, the other privileged disciples knew their easy payday was about to run right past them.
Individually, these disciples from the Great Houses were weak, but the weapons and Enchanted Tools their houses lavished on them made them dangerous.
They quickly regrouped, trying to plug the gap and reform their checkpoint.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Meanwhile, the disciples following the big man pressed their attack. Backed into a corner by their relentless enemies, the commoners had only two choices: fight back or be eliminated.
In a situation like this, anyone could find a sudden burst of strength, and the fight quickly got ugly.
[SFX: PAINFUL SCREAM]
[SFX: GLOWING SWORD STRIKE]
"Aaahh!" a disciple screamed as three Great House members ganged up on him. One of them drove a longsword that glowed with a brilliant green light straight into his stomach.
Even though the Magic Circle prevented actual death, the pain was one hundred percent real. After his scream, a bright light enveloped the disciple, and he was gone.
But the Great House disciples weren't having it all their own way.
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD CHAOS]
[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
In the chaos, a Legacy House member tried to shove a commoner out of his path. The ordinary-looking disciple seemed weak, but he was far stronger than he looked. The noble's eyes went wide as a strange, sharp pain shot through his body. He started to twitch and tremble before dropping to the ground, dead.
Instantly, countless specks of light enveloped the fallen disciple, and he vanished from the battlefield as if he had never existed.
[SFX: SWORD SWISH]
Zane moved like a phantom through the chaos, his body flowing with practiced precision. A blur of calculated motion, he sidestepped a clumsy sword thrust that would have skewered a less vigilant opponent, ducked under a wild swing that disturbed the air above his head, and slipped right through their sloppy blockade. His heart pounded in his chest as he navigated past the checkpoint, inching closer to relative safety.
The Great House disciples, unaccustomed to failure, seethed with indignation. Their aristocratic pride wounded, they desperately sought an outlet for their mounting frustration. When they witnessed Zane break through their ranks with such effortless grace—something their privileged upbringing had taught them was impossible for a commoner—they swarmed toward him like hornets from a disturbed nest.
"Get him!" one of them shouted, his voice cracking with rage.

Once clear, his first instinct was to keep moving — Zone 3 was waiting, and this wasn't his fight.
Then he saw Ethan.
Of all the people to be at this checkpoint, Ethan was the last one Zane expected. He was only at the Bone Refining Level — he had no business pushing past Zone 2. But there he was, stumbling forward through the chaos, too weak to draw much attention, which was the only thing keeping him alive.
It almost worked.
A Legacy House disciple had spotted him. The man slipped in quietly behind Ethan, a dagger in hand, its blade flickering with a deep red light. He was lining up a clean strike at Ethan's back — a vital point. One hit and Ethan was done.
Zane was already moving. He closed the distance in a heartbeat, grabbed Ethan by the collar, and hauled him sideways. The dagger sliced through empty air.
Ethan stumbled, caught himself, and turned — only then realizing what had nearly happened. The color drained from his face. "Brother Zane—"
No time. The attacker spun toward Zane, humiliated and furious, and drove the dagger straight for his throat. The angle was low and vicious, designed to end the fight in one move.
[SFX: COMBAT EXCHANGE]
Zane's fist came up.
"Purple Flash Fist!"
[SFX: SEVEN RAPID IMPACTS]
The punch connected with the disciple's chest. Seven sharp detonations fired through the man's body in rapid succession, each one deeper than the last. He crumpled and hit the ground — and the light took him.
Sensing the mob encircling him, Zane glanced at Ethan Grant's wide-eyed face. Without hesitation, he shoved his new companion toward safety, his palms firm against Ethan's shoulders.
[SFX: MELEE SOUNDS]
"Kill him!" screamed another disciple, spittle flying from his lips as he brandished an ornate dagger.
A red-faced youth with trembling hands gripped his weapon tighter. "My cousin's out because of these peasants! Make him pay!" he bellowed, grief and anger warping his aristocratic features.
"They're actually fighting back!" another exclaimed with genuine shock, as if the very concept defied natural law. "Kill him!"
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
The Great House disciples were a furious, disorganized mob, losing all reason as they rushed at Zane like a pack of wolves. Half of them were at the Core Strength Level, the other half at the Marrow Refining Level. And every single one of them was armed with a weapon that outclassed a rare arcane blade.
Amid the chaos, Zane's mind crystallized into icy clarity. Panic, he knew, would only hasten his demise. His eyes narrowed to calculating slits, focusing on the disciple draped in blue—the unmistakable source of command.
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]
The disciples surged forward in a violent wave, but Zane moved with deliberate precision. He slipped through their front line like water through fingers, weaving toward his target with singular purpose. Cut the head off the snake—the tactical imperative burned in his consciousness.
The blue-robed figure's eyes widened as he realized, perhaps too late, that he had become the hunted rather than the hunter. Zane lunged forward, his body coiled like a spring about to unleash devastating force...
