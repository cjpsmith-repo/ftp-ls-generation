Ep 202 - Raising the Stakes
Word count: 1054
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD]
[SFX: ENERGY WEAPONS FLYING THROUGH AIR]
James Moon smiled with wicked satisfaction as his arsenal hurtled towards Zane.
The Soul Realm master's fingers danced through the air, manipulating Vitality with practiced precision as he prepared to demonstrate the vast gulf between their cultivation levels.
The partygoers held their breath, any hope of recovering the evening was long gone, and the chance that they would see someone die tonight seemed very high indeed.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
[SFX: METAL CLANK]
With a sharp clank, a blue bola shot toward Zane, but he shattered it with a single punch.
[SFX: WHOOSHING SOUND]
The bola was barely gone when a volley of sharp arrows rained down on him. He dodged them easily, weaving through the barrage like a butterfly flitting between branches.
[SFX: ARROWS FLYING]
[SFX: SWORD SHATTERING]
The last arrow whistled past as a colossal sword descended toward Zane's exposed head. Without hesitation, he pivoted and slammed his palm against the flat of the blade. The weapon shattered instantly, energy radiating from his hand. The fragments dissolved back into raw Vitality before vanishing completely into the air.
"Damn," Zane muttered, sweat beading on his forehead. "So many weapons. It never ends."
Masters of the Soul Realm truly operated on another plane compared to Adepts. The difference was an unbelievable power gap that Zane was experiencing firsthand. His heart hammered against his ribs as he assessed his opponent.
Everyone in cultivation circles acknowledged that dividing Vitality among multiple weapons diminished the potency of each individual piece. This fundamental principle gave Zane hope. With his exceptional speed and formidable defense, he calculated he had a genuine opportunity to exhaust the old master's reserves.
But he was catastrophically mistaken.
[SFX: HIGH STAKES BATTLE]
The power of the Soul Realm was even greater than he could have imagined. James unleashed a relentless barrage of weapons---swords, spears, axes, and maces---a constant torrent hurtling toward Zane from every conceivable angle. The air itself seemed to vibrate with deadly intent.
Worse still, each weapon manifested unique properties and attack patterns. As Zane desperately fought them off, his mind scrambled to anticipate what might come next. His muscles burned with exertion, and his breath came in ragged gasps.
Reduced to pure reaction, Zane found himself dodging one attack after another solely on instinct, his body moving before his mind could process each threat.
[SFX: ANGRY SHOUT]
"Hmph! You cocky little brat! You'll pay for that arrogance!" James's nostrils flared. He was the master of House Moon, and this kid was showing him absolutely no respect. He was going to teach this kid the meaning of the massive gap between an Adept and a master of the Soul Realm.
As he spoke, the blue Vitality radiating from his skin intensified. All the weapons he'd created dissolved, the Vitality flowing back to swirl around him.
Zane watched him cautiously. His opponent was switching things up.
A master of the Soul Realm had plenty of tricks up his sleeve.
[SFX: ENERGY VORTEX FORMING]
James waved his arms, drawing a circle in the air. The streams of Vitality followed his movements, spinning faster and faster into a large vortex that converged in his hands. First, a hilt formed in his fist. Then, as the Vitality pulsed, a blade materialized from the energy.
"He's condensing all his Vitality into a single sword?" Zane watched, confused. It looked like a simple sword. If this was James's only other trick, his power wasn't all that impressive after all.
[SFX: SPINE CHILLING SENSATION]
"No, it can't be that simple. It can't just be a sword." Suddenly, Zane's eye twitched. A chill shot down his spine. He trusted his instincts, and right now, they were screaming that he was in serious trouble.
Zane was sure of it---James wasn't just forming a sword. As the weapons dissolved and the Vitality formed that vortex, he'd spotted a flicker of milky-white light within it.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
Celestial Essence. Zane stared at the essence swirling in the vortex, his brow furrowing.
Celestial Essence wasn't easy to come by. When a weaponsmith forges a weapon, they might grasp a few earthly and celestial laws, which they can then imbue into the steel. The better the weapon, the more Celestial Laws it held.
He knew plenty of weaponsmiths in the Empire who could decipher Celestial Law and use it to forge Mythic and Spirit weapons.
But he only knew of one person who could actually break down a weapon and extract the Celestial Essence from within: himself.
Zane could extract Celestial Essence by using the Black Fire in his mind to melt down a weapon.
But how could James create it so easily?
[SFX: MAGICAL DISSOLUTION]
The weapons he'd thrown at Zane weren't even real; they were just illusions formed from Vitality. Could James really extract Celestial Essence just by dissolving those illusions?
"No. Absolutely not. That's impossible!"
If James could actually pull something like that off, House Moon wouldn't be in decline.
Zane decided the Celestial Essence he was seeing couldn't be the real deal. It had to be some kind of illusion James had created with his Vitality. It definitely wasn't the same as the Celestial Essence Zane himself possessed.
Still, Zane had to admit, the technique was impressive. Anyone who reached the Soul Realm had to have a few incredible abilities up their sleeve.
[SFX: SWORD BEING RAISED]
Raising the sword high above his head, James slashed down at Zane.
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
As he swung, the tip of the blade unleashed a blast of energy shaped like a crescent moon---pure sword energy.
[SFX: ENERGY WHOOSH]
The second it formed, it shot toward Zane with terrifying power.
[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
"Watch out!" Ingrid cried, her voice tight with anxiety. Her heart leaped into her throat the second she saw the attack. Where was her family? She'd already used the Signal Talisman to call for them.
The power behind the strike was on par with a Mythic Weapon, far more powerful than a Spiritual one. Even though the blast of sword energy looked simple, it flew at Zane with ferocious power. If he couldn't dodge it, he'd be sliced in two.
Ingrid's hand hovered over her small alabaster box, hesitating. Should she jump in? Could she even help?
Dustin's brow furrowed. The man had no shame. How could James pull a stunt like this in front of everyone? James was a master of the Soul Realm, and it shocked Dustin that he would use such a merciless attack against Zane.
It was as shameless as a grown man going all out against a kid.
Could Zane possibly get out of this one?
