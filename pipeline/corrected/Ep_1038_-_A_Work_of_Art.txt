Ep 1038 - A Work of Art

Word count: 953

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

This technique, creating the state with his sword, came at a steep price: his Life-Essence. And once a warrior’s Life-Essence was spent, it was nearly impossible to recover.

But Fenton refused to lose to Zane.

He wasn’t naive enough to think he was invincible among his peers. Sure, to most warriors in the Soulbound Sect, Fenton seemed untouchable, but in the Psychean Realm, he was only ranked third. The top two spots were held by warriors of unbelievable talent—including the son of the World Lord himself.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Zane had only just arrived in the Upper World.

In every way that mattered—resources, environment—the Lower World just couldn’t compare. That’s why warriors who ascended from below were always seen as weaker than those born and raised in the Upper World.

It was a belief practically carved into the bones of every upper-world warrior, and Fenton was no exception.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]

As his Life-Essence poured from him, the golden state within the painting was stained with a fierce, bloody aura.

[SFX: THUNDERING HOOVES]

[SFX: CLASHING SWORDS]

This was no peaceful kingdom. It was a battlefield where warhorses thundered and heroes clashed for dominion. Countless blood-stained soldiers flooded the Sword Painting, their crimson serving as the final, brutal strokes.

Fenton had gathered all the power he needed.

[SFX: POWERFUL SWORD SWING]

Without warning, he swung his sword.

[SFX: SHARP CRACK]

A sharp crack split the air.

[SFX: BLADE STRIKE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

As his blade struck, a razor-thin flash of silver light sliced the Painting in two. All the images within it twisted and warped, pouring into the beam of light as it surged toward Zane.

“Here it comes,” Zane muttered, backing away as he braced for the full force of Fenton’s attack.

The power in this strike was... different. Though forged from the Metal and Wind Laws and fueled by Fenton’s own Life-Essence, the Painting generated a strange, potent intent that warped his senses. He couldn’t afford to take this lightly.

But Zane wasn’t the same warrior he’d once been. He had plenty of ways to counter this. The simplest and most direct was Sacred Mount Lodestone. It was so impossibly heavy that not even masters of the Spirit Supreme Realm could move it; a mere sword strike wouldn’t budge it an inch.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]

[SFX: CLASHING SWORDS]

But Zane didn’t reach for it.

He wanted to break this sword himself — with his own strength, nothing borrowed. Fenton was the best the Soulbound Sect had to offer. If Zane couldn’t measure the real gap between himself and an upper-world genius here, in a fight with no lasting consequences, then when could he?

His sword intent spiraled outward, and the Chaos Energy in his body began to rotate slowly with it. His sword intent had reached perfection — and with Chaos Energy flowing through it, it was still sharpening, still growing.

He made his decision. He would meet Fenton’s technique with his blade alone.

The moment he entered the beam, the soldiers were everywhere — surging toward him from every direction, blades raised.

Zane fought desperately, but for every one he cut down, two more took its place.

He pushed harder. Time Law flickered to life around his blade — and in a single breath, his sword struck thousands of times, each slash precise, each one landing before the last had faded.

[SFX: RAPID SWORD SLASHES]

The collisions rang out in a continuous roar.

It still wasn’t enough.

Beyond the rank-and-file soldiers, generals in crimson armor joined the fray, their strength far surpassing the others and pressing Zane even harder.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

He was trapped.

[AMBIENT: CRUCIBLE MURMUR]

Out in The Crucible, the watching warriors were baffled. They had seen Zane’s power — they knew what he was capable of. But he had charged straight into the sword light without a trump card, without a defensive treasure, without anything. Either he didn’t take Fenton seriously, or he was walking into his own destruction.

Nobody could tell which.

At the observers’ platform, the Saint Lord had gone quiet. The outcome of this fight no longer concerned him — he already knew he couldn’t keep Zane. But something about the way Zane had charged in made him pause.

Beside him, the elder watched with a slight frown. “Unconventional,” he murmured. “Every move unexpected. But this one... using sword intent alone against the Painting is a fool’s errand.” He paused. “And yet — the way this boy thinks...”

He trailed off. He couldn’t quite finish the thought.

[SFX: ENERGY BEAM IMPACT]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

An attack like this can’t be endless, Zane thought, his expression turning thoughtful even as he unleashed another wave of sword intent.

He shot upward, but the beam of light from Fenton’s sword latched onto him, refusing to let go. Zane had only a split second to brace himself before it slammed down, forcing him back into the endless assault of phantom soldiers and generals.

“There!” he yelled, a flash of insight cutting through the chaos.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

The beam of light had absorbed the entire Painting. It was made of people, mountains, and rivers—and where there are people, there is always a ruler. As Zane bought himself a moment of breathing room, he spotted him: a king in yellow robes, hidden deep within the torrent of light.

The soldiers, the generals, the king... they had to be created by some kind of astral projection, a technique far more complex than anything he’d ever seen in the Lower World.

“That’s him!”

[MUSIC: HEROIC CHARGE]

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

This time, Zane didn’t retreat. He charged straight for his target: the king. If he could kill the king, the light from the Sword Painting would vanish.

Watching Zane charge, Fenton’s lips curled into a smirk. “So, you found him,” he drawled. “But do you really think you have what it takes to beat him?”

[SFX: RAPID SWORD SLASHES]

[SFX: SHATTERING ILLUSIONS]

Answering with his blade, Zane unleashed a flurry of slashes, shattering countless illusions and carving a path through the phantom soldiers to stand face-to-face with the king.
