[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL ORCHESTRA]

The shadow creature was right behind them. Max had torn a rift in space, and the Compass Wheel was racing for it — but the creature's mouth was already open, wide enough to swallow them whole.

[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER]

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

Seizing the opening, Delroy slammed his palms together, and a shimmering barrier of magic snapped into place over the tear in reality. A rushing wind echoed from behind them, a chilling reminder that their time was running out. The thing was closing in, and fast.

[AMBIENT: DEEP RUMBLING]

Even without his magical sight, Zane could make out the silhouette of a colossal head. Squinting into the darkness, they could just make out faint shadows shifting within that cavernous maw, terrifying shapes that sent a collective shiver through the group. The Shadow Creature was gaining on them at a terrifying speed. If they didn't make it through the rift and out of the void in time, they'd be swallowed whole.

[SFX: BLADE SLASH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]

In a sudden, desperate move, Hank drew a blade. Gritting his teeth, he sliced open his wrist and slapped his bleeding palm flat against the Compass Wheel. A stream of golden blood, not red, flowed from the wound and was instantly absorbed into the ancient device.

“Let's go!” Hank yelled.

[SFX: MAGICAL SURGE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

As if his blood had flipped a switch, the wheel erupted in brilliant rays of light and surged forward, its speed suddenly doubling. The flash illuminated the shapes moving inside the creature's mouth — horrifying silhouettes, dozens of smaller shadow creatures nestled in that cavernous maw. 

The group's collective gasp was sharp and simultaneous. Most of them stared in stunned horror. Max and Delroy, however, only looked tighter around the eyes — nervous, but unsurprised. They already knew what they were dealing with.

[SFX: CREATURE ROAR]

[AMBIENT: VOID RUMBLE]

The burst of speed was just enough. The Compass Wheel shot through the spatial rift, and they escaped the void just as the creature’s jaws would have snapped shut.

[SFX: DARK ENERGY WAVE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

As its prey slipped away, the shadow creature unleashed a wave of pure malice that washed over them. It was an aura thick with violence, cruelty, and the bitter refusal to admit defeat.

[AMBIENT: SPACE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

“Hmph. That thing's a creature of the void,” Max scoffed as they burst into real space. “It's a fish out of water out here. It can't survive, so it won't dare follow.” He sounded confident, but the color still hadn't returned to his face.

[SFX: SHARP WHISTLE]

The words were barely out of his mouth when the sound hit them — a high shrieking whistle from every direction at once. Millions of wind blades were already circling above the wheel, green and black, layered so thick they blotted out the stars.

“What the—“ the young man in black started.

The green blades carried the fourth layer of Wind Law. The black ones carried the fifth. They had all heard Zane warn them that the Star Storm was dangerous, and they had filed it away as the concern of a Lower World warrior who didn't know what real power looked like. They knew better now.

[SFX: SCYTHE SWING]

[SFX: METAL IMPACT]

The burly man moved first. He brought his scythe around in a full arc and cut through seven or eight of the black blades. The counterforce hit like a wall — he'd used nearly everything he had, and the entire Compass Wheel rocked violently beneath them.

More blades were already incoming. Tens of thousands of green ones. Thousands of black.

“Delroy!” the young man in black snapped. “Barrier — now!”

Delroy shot him a look, clapped his hands, and threw up a barrier around the group. It held for exactly one second.

[SFX: BARRIER SHATTERING]

The black blades ate through it like it wasn't there. Delroy pulled his hands back. “My Law of Causality can't stop a higher-level Law of Origin,” he said flatly. “Especially not at this volume.”

The young man in black said nothing. He drew his broadsword and started firing — hundreds of black blade energies slashing forward into the storm. They cut through the green blades cleanly. The black ones swallowed them whole.

[SFX: BLADE ENERGIES CUTTING]

Several black wind blades punched straight through his attack and arrowed toward Zane.

“Watch out!” Dorothea shouted — but she was already spinning away from three of her own, and couldn't reach him.

The young man in black saw it too. “Idiot — those will take your hand off! Even my blade risks cracking against fifth-layer Wind Law. Don't—“

Zane clenched his fist. 

His expression had shifted the moment he saw them coming — a flash of recognition, not fear. Then he made his decision.

Dragon Scale power surged through his arm. He punched.

[SFX: HARSH IMPACT]

The black wind blades — fifth-layer Wind Law, the kind that had just shredded Delroy's barrier — shattered on contact. Zane's hand was unmarked.

The young man in black stopped moving. He forgot about the blades still coming at him. He just stared.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

It wasn't just the attacker. Dorothea, Delroy, Max, and Wade had all seen it, and their own shock was just as profound. They knew Zane had been recognized by the Demi-Immortal Race, that he was supposedly special. But he was still just a warrior from the Lower World. In their minds, he still had to prove he was worthy of that high regard.

Truth be told, none of them had ever really bothered to gauge his actual strength. After all, what was a warrior from the Life-and-Death Realm to masters of the Spirit Supreme Realm like them? They barely gave the white-haired priest—another Spirit Supreme master—the time of day. Their kind were elites, and the power gap between a human and a Demi-Immortal of the same realm was immense.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

But here Zane was, shattering every assumption they’d ever had about him. 

How had he just obliterated wind blades infused with five layers of Wind Law? Even if he had some special trick, it shouldn't have been possible. 

An ordinary Spirit Supreme warrior would have been sliced to ribbons by that attack, yet Zane hadn't even gotten a scratch.
