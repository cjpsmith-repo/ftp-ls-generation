Ep 84 - Clash of the Rings
Word count: 1169
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGES]
"Damn it, Boyd's going way too far. This is just a team challenge. Why's he using moves like that? Is he trying to kill Zane?"
"That ring is solid iron, and the Cyclone Rings just sliced through it like it was clay. If one of those things hits a person, it'll cut them clean in half!"
"This is insane. How is Zane going to get out of this?"
The entire crowd was on edge, holding their breath as they watched Zane.
[SFX: WHOOSHING METAL RINGS]
Just as the Cyclone Rings closed in, Zane twisted his body at an impossible angle, slipping right between them. In the same fluid motion, he dodged another four rings whizzing above and below him. Once he was clear, he broke into a dead sprint, charging straight for Boyd.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Everything up to this point had been a warm-up. Now, the real fight—raw, hand-to-hand combat—was about to begin.
Boyd's face was a mask of calm as he watched Zane rush him, not a hint of panic in his eyes. Instead, his expression turned deadly serious. With a flick of his wrist, the seven Cyclone Rings Zane had just dodged instantly changed direction and swarmed him from behind.
Powered by Boyd's own Vitality, the rings were as light as air and moved at blinding speed. Zane was fast, but now he had a choice: keep his momentum going or try to outmaneuver the razor-sharp rings chasing him. If he didn't dodge, they'd slice him to pieces long before he ever reached Boyd.
This was why Boyd just stood there, his gaze steady. He'd used the Cyclone Rings to take down dozens of rivals. He knew their power, knew what a direct hit would do. He was sure this time would be no different. No one would be foolish enough to try and block them with their own body.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
But this time, Boyd was wrong.
[SFX: METAL CLANG]
If Zane's body was only as tough as a standard mystic weapon, he would've been terrified of their cutting power. But when the first Cyclone Ring slammed into him, it didn't cut him to ribbons. Instead, it just ricocheted off to the side with a sharp clang.
[SFX: CROWD GASPING]
While the ring hadn't cut through his body, it had shredded his white robe, exposing his back to the stunned crowd. On his bare skin, there was only a single, thin line of blood. A collective gasp went through the audience, followed by dead silence. They couldn't believe it. Such a lethal weapon had barely left a scratch.
[SFX: METALLIC SHRIEK]
And the shock was only just beginning. The remaining Cyclone Rings continued their assault, each one crashing against Zane's body with the same metallic shriek as the first.
[SFX: LOUD METAL CLANG]
The second ring bounced away.
[SFX: METAL CLANG]
The third was deflected.
[SFX: METAL CLANG]
The fourth ricocheted harmlessly into the air.
[SFX: METAL CLANG X 3]
One after another, the Cyclone Rings struck Zane's back, rang out like a hammer on an anvil, and then fell away, unable to do any real damage.
In every other fight, Boyd's Cyclone Rings had shredded his opponents. Now, they couldn't do more than leave a shallow gash on Zane's skin. It didn't matter what angle they hit from or how much force was behind them; they couldn't cut him apart.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC SURGE]
Seeing this, Boyd's composure finally broke. He was just as stunned as the crowd—if not more. A cold knot of fear and dread began to form in his stomach. But even as the shock of Zane's impossible toughness hit him, his training kicked in and he forced himself back into focus. The rest of the crowd, however, was still completely lost in the moment.
As the top pledge at Eagle Peak, Boyd's skills went beyond his powerful weapon. He had his own strength and, more importantly, a sharp sense of danger. And right now, that instinct was screaming at him.
[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCH]
Meanwhile, Zane, having weathered the metallic storm, closed the distance and threw a punch. His fist was a blur of stone-hard force, slamming forward with immense power—the raw strength he'd unlocked after awakening the Dragon Scale Shards. Each punch came like a relentless wave, one after another.
But his rival wasn't a pushover. Boyd snapped out of his shock and threw his hands up just as Zane's fist was about to land. Vitality erupted between his palms, spinning into light green rings.
[SFX: ENERGY FORMING]
"Tectonic Rings! Form up!" Boyd yelled, his hands a blur of motion.
This was Boyd's defensive technique. Circles of green energy spun into existence and merged into a solid shield. The barrier shimmered in the sun, holding firm as it met Zane's powerful punch.
[SFX: METAL RINGS CLATTERING]
The Tectonic Rings held, rattling against each other with a sharp clatter. But while the shield protected Boyd from the direct hit, the force didn't just vanish. It transferred straight through the rings and into Boyd, shoving him backward.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
The punch was so powerful that it sent Boyd flying right out of the battle ring!
He wasn't, however, about to set a record for being defeated in a single blow. Boyd was no ordinary fighter. If he had gotten into the Nature Level on pure grit and hard work alone, he'd just be another Core Apprentice. But he was the top-ranked Outer Disciple of Eagle Peak.
You don't become the top pledge in any of the Sentinel Peaks by just trying hard; it was impossible. Boyd was a prodigy, the best Eagle Peak had, with the potential to become the top Core Apprentice.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
"I bet Zane's intimidated by Boyd's power, though," a girl murmured to her friend. "I mean, Boyd's a nature creature. He has a huge advantage."
"But it's crazy for them to keep fighting like this, isn't it?" Chloe asked, her voice laced with anxiety. "When is it going to end?"
Isla Novak, however, wasn't nearly as worried. She was mostly calm, even letting a small, sly smile slip when Chloe wasn't looking. She wasn't worried because she had already accepted this stalemate. She was fully prepared to lose the challenge. And if they happened to win? Well, she certainly wouldn't complain.
Chloe, on the other hand, was growing more concerned. Even though Boyd was a nature creature, he hadn't been at the Nature Level for long. His Vitality wasn't infinite. If this kept up, he'd eventually gas out and lose once his energy was drained.
She wasn't the only one who realized it. Boyd knew it, too. He could use the Tectonic Rings to fend off Zane, but he knew that constantly burning through his Vitality was a losing game. He was on a timer, and eventually, he'd run out of steam.
[MUSIC: TENSE DETERMINATION]
Defeat in the Divisional Championship loomed as an intolerable disgrace for Boyd.
[SFX: RUSHING MOVEMENT]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
After Zane's ninth devastating blow sent him reeling backward, Boyd transformed the momentum into a desperate counterattack, propelling himself toward the arena with newfound determination. His eyes blazed with defiance as he channeled virtually every remaining essence of his Vitality into what would be his final, defining gambit—a last stand that would either redeem him or destroy him completely.
