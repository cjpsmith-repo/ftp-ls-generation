Ep 1287 - Here, Kitty

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: CAVERNOUS HALL WITH DISTANT RUMBLING]
They stood at the edge of the fire sea, staring up at the lava giant as it turned its molten head their way, the whole party frozen in the same held breath. None of them moved. None of them spoke. 
The giant didn’t either, its burning eyes simply tracking them, waiting to see who would make the first mistake.
Janet was a natural leader. Normally, heading into a new hall, everyone’s eyes would have been on her; she was the only one who had any idea what they were walking into. But not this time. Now, it was clear to everyone that the real leader was Zane.
He just stood there, staring at the mountain-sized giant, his face a mask of concentration as the rest of the group hung back, watching him, waiting to see what he’d do. 
Finally, he scratched his head and let out a frustrated sigh.
“What is this thing?”
Janet, who was standing beside him, just shook her head. She couldn’t believe he didn’t even know what a lava giant was. 
Jeffrey and Hailey shot him the same incredulous look. Only Coleman seemed to miss the memo. 
He was too busy staring at the giant, muttering, “That thing looks stronger than me.”
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING GROWL]
“It’s a Law Creature,” Janet explained, her tone almost conversational. “Created by the Original Laws. This one is made from Earth Law and Fire Law. A giant this big… it was probably born from the sixth level of the Fire Law’s power.”
Law Creatures weren’t exactly rare. Low-level ones could pop up anywhere—a thunder spirit born from a storm, a fiery wisp from an immortal flame. But the really powerful ones, like the golden wind blades Zane had fought in the Chaotic Ocean of Stars, took ages to form from the strongest energies of the Original Laws. 
This giant was definitely one of the powerful ones.
The group fell silent, all of them staring at the impossible sea of fire, trying to come up with a plan. Coleman paced along the edge, circling it twice before he suddenly stopped. “What’s that?” he exclaimed, pointing. 
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: BUBBLING LAVA LAKE]
A series of fiery red waves rippled across the surface. As they watched, small bubbles of lava started to form. 
[SFX: LAVA BUBBLES BURSTING]
They burst, sending spirals of flame dancing into the air.
[AMBIENT: SOFT FELINE MEWLING]
Small, foot-long shapes of flame drifted upward from the bursting bubbles - tiny kittens crafted from living fire. Their heavy-lidded eyes blinked slowly at the group, radiating an almost drowsy innocence. Zane watched as more surfaced behind the first wave, one after another in an endless procession, as though the entire lake teemed with these curious creatures.
Coleman leaned in, grinning. “Aw. Kind of cute, actually.” He reached out and gave one a friendly little wave.
[SFX: LOW GROWL]
The kitten growled back, low and unhappy. It floated toward Coleman - slow, clumsy, drifting more lazily than a man could walk. 
Then it shrieked.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE ROAR]
[MUSIC: SUDDEN DANGER]
In an instant, the kitten ballooned to ten times its size and lunged. Coleman’s grin vanished. The heat radiating off the creature hit like a small sun at their backs, and the air itself seemed to warp and shimmer. What had been adorable moments before now became a nightmare of flame and fury, its heavy-lidded eyes blazing with sudden, terrifying intelligence.
“Everyone, disperse!” Coleman didn’t need to be told twice—he was already running, his armor hissing and glowing red-hot from the trailing heat. 
He couldn’t shake the thing. If it caught him, there’d be nothing left but ash. “Help! Someone help me!”
Janet didn’t hesitate. “Help him,” she said. Retreating didn’t mean leaving him behind.
[SFX: ICE CRACKLING]
Jeffrey’s skill with Ice Law was nothing to scoff at, and he snapped his hand forward, launching three icicles at the beast. They didn’t even reach it—the ice turned to steam before it could land a single hit.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Zane didn’t hesitate. He surged forward on a burst of Vitality, closed the distance in a blink, and drove his foot into Coleman’s back plate.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
The kick sent Coleman rocketing clean out of the hall, his speed doubling in an instant. Denied its target, the giant flame-kitten drifted to a stop, then dove back beneath the surface of the lake.
“That thing,” Cassandra said—the quiet one of the group, speaking up for the first time since they’d banded together, eyes still fixed on the lava, “was born from the seventh level of the Fire Law. Not the sixth.” She was the only one among them who’d studied the Fire Law in any real depth—which meant, for once, no one doubted her.
Janet’s brow furrowed. She’d guessed sixth. She’d underestimated it.
“Who cares what level it is!” Coleman grumbled. “That doesn’t matter. We need to figure out how to cross this thing.” He stared at the massive lake of fire and shuddered, a memory of the kitten’s power sending a chill down his spine.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
There were no bridges, no ropes. It was a dead end. And this wasn’t just normal lava, either. A Soul Sea Realm warrior could swim through a regular volcano without much trouble; the heat could never breach their protective Vitality. 
[SFX: INTENSE HEAT WAVE]
But the sheer power of Fire Law infused in this lake was a different story. It was something they couldn’t hope to resist.
That was the rule of this hall. Every hall had its own unbreakable rules. The only exception Zane had ever seen was his own trick with the small skull, the one he’d used to stop that giant stone block from flipping. The skulls he carried on his back pulsed with a power that could rival Celestial Law itself—strong enough to not just hold back the rock, but to defy the very rules that governed it.
The intense heat rolling off the molten sea forced Zane to take a few steps back. 
He sighed, his eyes scanning the cavern walls, desperately searching for any way—any way at all—to get across.
