Ep 1058 - Asking For Trouble

Word count: 1019

[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]

[SFX: CRACKING STONE]

Still sitting cross-legged inside the closed manor, Zane poured his Chaos Energy into the Thundergust and let it loose.

An invisible wave of energy erupted from the Thundergust and cracked the gate.

[SFX: CRACKLING LIGHTNING]

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

From the blast, a dark blue bolt of lightning and a pitch-black wind blade twisted together, forming a blurry figure that charged straight for Brock.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

“What was that?” Zane stammered, his eyes wide with shock.

The fourth level of the Wind Law and the fourth level of the Thunder Law.

Until this very moment, Zane had only ever managed to grasp the third level of his Law Powers, whether it was Wind, Thunder, or Fire Law. But when he poured his Chaos Energy into the Thundergust, it had unleashed power on a whole other level.

[SFX: SWORD HUMMING]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY]

Lavender hugged her knees, peering at the light from Zane’s sword through the crack in the gate. “This sword enhances your Law Power,” she said, a note of respect in her voice. “Not bad for a Grade-One Divine Weapon.”

“Law Power enhancement?”

“Yep. The sword gives your Law Power a boost.”

“So, if I can master the fourth level, does that mean this sword will let me use the fifth?” Zane’s eyes lit up with excitement.

Lavender shook her head. “It’s not that simple. The gap between the fourth and fifth levels is massive. Controlling the first few levels is one thing, but the higher levels are a whole different ball game. This sword can give your power a nudge, but it can’t just jump you up a whole level.”

Zane nodded, the excitement fading into understanding. “Oh, I get it now.” Even so, it was still a hell of a perk.

And that wasn’t all. There was still the matter of that blurry figure the sword had created.

Brock, the warrior from Cloudswallow Hall, was supremely confident in his own strength. As far as he was concerned, this new guy at The Mist Palace had just gotten lucky stumbling upon the Thundergust.

He’d even figured Zane wouldn’t have the guts to actually fight him.

The figure closed the distance in an instant. Brock’s pupils reflected the flash of lightning — every hair on his body stood up. Before he could finish drawing breath, he reached for his life-saving technique.

[SFX: HEAVY BLADE DRAW]

His Shieldsword — a Grade-One Divine Weapon, blade one and a half feet wide and three feet long, built more like a shield than a sword — came up in front of him. Earth Law flooded into it, thickening the blade, making it dense and immovable.

[SFX: CRACKLING LIGHTNING STRIKE]

Lightning crashed into the Shieldsword, leaving black branching patterns across its surface like spreading snakes. Then the wind blade hit the center.

[SFX: METAL SPLITTING]

The Shieldsword split clean in two.

Brock’s heart broke with it. But his life mattered more. He kept retreating — except the figure was faster. It closed in and threw the simplest punch.

Brock raised both arms to block.

[SFX: BONE CRACK]

Both arms snapped. The punch landed on his chest anyway and sent him flying backward like a kite with a broken string, crashing into the ground.

[SFX: POWERFUL BLAST]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]

He shot a venomous glare at the manor before making a hasty retreat.

Zane stared at the slowly fading figure, genuinely baffled. “How did my sword produce a person?”

Lavender glanced over, unhurried. A Grade-One Divine Weapon was nothing extraordinary in her eyes — not after the Roaring Token had already defied all common sense. “If I’m not wrong,” she said, “that figure’s moves can be changed. It can do more than one punch.”

Zane’s brow went up. Now that she mentioned it, when he’d swung the sword, he’d felt something. A faint connection, like a thread of awareness. He’d assumed it was the sword itself. Now he realized it had been the figure. He filed it away. He’d need time to work out how to control it.

After a long moment, a figure flashed out from the crowd, picked Brock up, shot a venomous glare at Zane, and left.

None of the other warriors dared to stick around, let alone get any closer to Zane’s manor.

Brock was from the prestigious Cloudswallow Hall, and he’d been nearly crippled by a single attack from Zane’s sword. If that could happen to him, the crowd of Groundstone Hall warriors knew better than to make the same mistake. That was just asking for trouble.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

Within moments, the area in front of Zane’s door was completely empty.

A small smile played on Zane’s lips at the sight. Finally. A little peace and quiet.

[AMBIENT: TENSE PALACE ATMOSPHERE]

He hadn’t expected to cause such a scene with just one swing of his sword. The people in the Mist Palace were a tough crowd, but he was pretty sure no one would be looking to mess with him for a while.

“So, what are your plans now?” Zane asked Lavender carefully.

His current strength was nowhere near enough to survive in the Upper World. A warrior from the Spirit Supreme Realm could crush him easily, let alone the World Lords.

The Queen of Punishment, on the other hand, wielded the power of a World Lord. At Zane’s current level, he wasn’t even qualified to breathe the same air as her.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

“I’ll wait,” Lavender said with a cryptic smile.

“Wait for what?” Zane asked.

“For you to get stronger,” she replied simply.

Zane was left speechless, caught somewhere between wanting to laugh and wanting to cry.

At this point, his power had already peaked at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm. His next step should have been to prepare for the Celestial Tribulation.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

But just as he was getting ready, someone else arrived at the Mist Palace.

To Zane’s utter shock, it was the previous owner of the Thundergust—who was also the former leader of the Mist Palace himself.
