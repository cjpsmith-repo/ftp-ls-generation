Ep 663 - The Watchdog of Hell

Word count: 1139

[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND RUMBLING]

[SFX: PLANT BURSTING FROM EARTH]

As the warriors fled, the plant shot up from the ground, a flower bud forming and blooming in the space of a heartbeat.

"Die!" Malcolm yelled, throwing a punch straight at the bizarre stalk.

[SFX: EXPLOSION]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

A shockwave of power slammed into the plant with a muffled thud, enveloping it in smoke. But when the haze cleared, Malcolm’s jaw went slack. The plant was completely unharmed. The flower continued to unfurl, a ghostly face slowly taking shape on its petals.

"Hurry! We need to get higher!" Fabian shouted.

Zane was already moving.

[SFX: ENERGY SHIELD ACTIVATION]

[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]

The instant he heard Fabian’s warning, he shot skyward. The orange amulet on his chest flared, expanding into a protective bubble of light that encased them all.

But as they soared into the air, the green stalk grew right alongside them, effortlessly matching their speed.

"Faster! Don't stop!" Jarrod roared.

Everyone pushed themselves to their absolute limit. By the time the flower had fully bloomed, revealing the sinister face within, they had put some distance between them and the creature.

[SFX: TERRIFYING SCREAM]

[MUSIC: HORROR CRESCENDO]

A terrifying scream echoed from behind, and a wave of pure terror washed over the group.

"It's not over! Look at the ground!" one of the War Lords from the Twilight Group yelled, his face pale with dread as he pointed down.

They all risked a glance.

[SFX: MULTIPLE PLANTS ERUPTING]

Below them, the ground was peppered with countless green dots that were appearing by the second. They weren't individual sprouts—they were all part of the same entity. The true body of the monster was buried deep underground, and now its tendrils were bursting to the surface, racing to intercept them.

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

"Looks like our only option is to outrun it," Zane yelled, his voice tight. "Give it everything you've got! Follow me!"

[SFX: ENERGY BURST]

The moment he spoke, a powerful energy burst from him. His speed surged by a third, leaving a thin trail of smoke as he streaked through the fog.

Seeing Zane accelerate, the others scrambled to keep up, desperate to stay inside his protective barrier. Fabian and Jarrod exchanged a grim look and merged their energy, becoming a single gray shadow that managed to match Zane's blistering pace.

Yolande, however, needed no such tricks. She flew effortlessly beside Zane, keeping pace no matter how fast he went.

[SFX: WINGS BEATING]

Malcolm, meanwhile, had grabbed Timothy by the arm, practically dragging his companion along. The pair of black wings on his back beat furiously as he struggled to follow. Falling behind now meant certain death, and he knew it.

Esther Sterling pushed herself to the brink, too. A faint white light pulsed around her and the two War Lords from the Demon Night as they trailed just behind Malcolm’s group.

The two remaining Twilight Group War Lords couldn't keep up. Built for stealth and concealment, they weren't made for this kind of sprint. When Zane surged, the gap opened in an instant — and they fell outside the dome.

"Wait for us!"

"Help!"

Their cries came from behind and were quickly swallowed by the sound of the plants closing in. No one looked back. No one slowed. The crisis had started with Garrick, and whatever fate found his surviving companions, no one was going back for them.

Everyone huddled close to Zane — even Malcolm.

The chase seemed to go on forever. Fabian and Jarrod burned through Vitality stone after Vitality stone. Even Zane crushed two before it was over.

Finally, the green dots thinned, slowed, and stopped appearing altogether.

"Rest, rest for a while," Fabian said, dropping onto the ground the moment they landed.

Esther didn't sit. She was staring straight ahead. "I'm afraid there's no chance for that," she said coldly.

Everyone looked up — and fell silent.

[MUSIC: AWE AND DREAD]

The red dot was no longer a dot. The fog had thinned to almost nothing, and what lay ahead defied description. A pit wide enough to swallow cities opened in the earth. Sleeping inside it was a creature whose black-skinned head alone was the size of ten mountains. Two fangs jutted from its jaw, each as tall as a mountain peak. The red circle of light they'd been chasing this whole time was emanating from the top of its skull.

Everyone instinctively dropped their voices.

"What… what kind of monster is this?"

"Is this a divine beast? I've never seen anything so huge—"

"Something that size is sleeping under the main continent. If it ever wakes up—"

Lavender giggled — loudly. Every head whipped toward her. "What are you all so afraid of?" she said. "If that thing woke up that easily, the main continent would've been gone a long time ago."

Yolande nodded. "She's right. It won't wake."

Malcolm stared at it, his expression complicated. He'd come here to kill Zane. That ambition felt very small now. If this thing ever opened its eyes, the entire Ogres' Sacred Land wouldn't even register.

Yolande tugged at Zane's arm. "Let's keep moving."

The fog was almost gone now. The Dark Nether Death Light poured down in full force, and the air around them blazed deep red. No one needed to be told what a single step outside the dome would mean. Almost by instinct, everyone pressed closer to Zane — even Malcolm.

"Yolande, what is this thing?" Esther asked. "Is it a Legendary Beast? Or something stronger?"

"It's neither," Yolande replied bluntly. "It's a creature called the Mad Scorpion. It's the watchdog of Hell."

"The watchdog of Hell?" The words echoed through the group, followed by stunned silence.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

They had only ever read about Hell in ancient myths and legends; no one knew if it was a real place. Yolande’s casual statement had floored them.

[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]

Esther took a shaky breath. "You mean… Hell is real?"

Yolande just kept flying, shaking her head. Her answer was maddeningly vague. "Hell might exist."

"What's that supposed to mean?" Esther pressed. She could tell Yolande was holding something back, but there was nothing she could do to force an answer. Still, she thought, it was better than a flat-out refusal to talk.

[AMBIENT: DARK VOID RUMBLING]

After a moment of silence, Zane stared down at the monstrous entity spreading out below them. "Where are we even going?" he demanded.

"Forward," Yolande said, her voice indifferent.

"And what's ahead of us besides more of this?" Zane shot back. Was she leading them straight into an abyss? Or right down the monster's throat?

To his shock, Yolande said, "Your Crystal Butterfly will give you the answer."

Zane flinched, his guard shooting up instantly. "How do you know I have a Crystal Butterfly?"
