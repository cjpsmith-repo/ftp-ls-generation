Ep 464 - Upgrading The Tower

Word count: 983

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL MURMURS]

Within the World Merchants' Guild, no one dared underestimate Zane. The magic array before him hadn't been constructed for Soul Realm warriors—not even those who'd reached their peak.

The massive formation was designed to test warriors of the Inner-Core Realm. Even the handful of peak Soul Realm cultivators who were able to enter typically watched their defensive towers collapse by the sixth or seventh wave, their faces pale with exhaustion and wounded pride.

That Zane stood here at all, preparing to face this trial, spoke volumes about the Guild's expectations. The observers gathered in the viewing chamber exchanged meaningful glances, their expressions a mixture of skepticism and curiosity. Some whispered behind raised hands, placing quiet wagers on how many waves this young upstart would survive.

The air itself seemed to thicken with anticipation.

[MUSIC: INTRIGUING STRINGS]

"We'll see," Michael Nash said, shaking his head. "There's no denying, it's wild that a runic expert gamed the entrance exam just by changing the color of a lodestone." He had to admire Zane's skill with runes, but this array didn't test for that. This was a test of raw strength.

"Honestly, I'm curious how the kid managed to pass the exam, too," the examiner said with a grin.

Zane's power level was almost laughably low—a mere third-rank Soul Realm warrior. Among the assembled spectators, most of whom had reached the Inner-Core Realm, he naturally became an object of curiosity and quiet amusement.

The examiner shifted his weight, glancing toward the entrance. "I dispatched someone to retrieve the exam records from the old man earlier," he explained, his tone casual. "Though I've no idea whether he's actually on his way yet."

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

As if summoned by the very mention of his name, an elderly figure appeared in the doorway—the mustached official who had overseen registration. His robes swished softly as he entered, and his sharp eyes immediately swept the room.

Michael Nash's face brightened with genuine surprise. "Speak of the devil!" he exclaimed, gesturing welcomingly. "We were just discussing you."

The old man acknowledged the greeting with a brief nod, but his attention had already fixed on the massive Scrying Slate dominating the chamber's far wall. The light from the slate cast flickering shadows across his weathered features. "So," he inquired, his voice carrying an edge of urgency that seemed out of place for such a routine inquiry, "how is that warrior, Zane Lowell, performing in the array?"

The question landed like a stone in still water. Both Michael and the examiner exchanged puzzled glances, their earlier ease evaporating into confusion.

"Why this sudden interest in his score?" Michael asked, leaning forward slightly. His brow furrowed as he studied the old man's expression, searching for some explanation for this unexpected concern.

The old man just offered a cryptic smile and shook his head, saying nothing. He narrowed his eyes, focusing on the Scrying Slate where Zane's picture and personal info were displayed.

"Ranked three hundred and ninety-second after the first wave?" A flicker of surprise crossed the old man's face. "That's… low," he muttered.

The score was far lower than he'd anticipated; he was sure Zane had more potential than that.

The old man's reaction only deepened Michael and the examiner's confusion. "What's the big deal?" Michael said. "That's a pretty normal score for a runic expert."

"Maybe he's just aiming to qualify for the main tournament," the examiner chimed in. "Wanna make a bet on it, Michael?"

Michael just laughed, clearly uninterested in the wager.

[AMBIENT: MAGIC ARRAY TRIAL]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

While they conversed, the second wave of attacks erupted inside the magic array.

Zane studied the points displayed beside his tower, his eyes narrowing with interest. He had to admit—the design was clever, almost elegant in its simplicity. The system had awarded him a total of one thousand points for eliminating the three-eyed spiders.

He hadn't kept count during the chaotic melee, but apparently he'd slain exactly one thousand of the creatures. One point per spider. A straightforward exchange.

These points represented more than just a score. They were the key to claiming one of the three qualifying spots, yes, but their true value lay elsewhere—they could be invested directly into his tower's defenses.

Zane recalled the examiner's warning, the man's tone grave as he'd explained the trial's escalating difficulty. As the waves progressed, the beasts would grow increasingly scarier, eventually becoming too powerful for any warrior to handle alone, regardless of skill or cultivation. That's why strategic use of points was critical. Upgrading the tower wasn't just an option—it was the only way to amplify its defensive capabilities and secure additional support against the coming onslaught.

His decision crystallized instantly. One thousand points to reinforce!

Without hesitation, Zane invested everything he'd earned into the tower's enhancement. The risk of holding nothing in reserve didn't concern him. Survival demanded strength now, not later.

[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]

[SFX: MAGICAL COIN TRANSACTION]

His point counter plummeted to zero in an instant. Red light erupted around the tower, brilliant and fierce, before dissipating to reveal the transformation.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

The reinforced structure now stood three feet taller than before. Golden filigree traced elegant patterns along its edges, the metalwork gleaming with an almost liquid quality that spoke of enhanced durability. Zane's breath caught as he noticed the most significant improvement—a second bowman had materialized on the battlements, his spectral form identical to the first. His defensive capability had doubled.

A surge of satisfaction warmed his chest. The gamble had paid off.

He tilted his head back, studying the intricate golden patterns that now adorned his tower. How much further could this structure evolve? His imagination ran wild. If one thousand points had produced such dramatic results, what transformations might ten thousand bring?

[SFX: OMINOUS BELL CHIME]

[MUSIC: HIGH SUSPENSE]

The deep, resonant toll of a bell shattered his contemplation. The sound emanated from somewhere high above, reverberating through the air with an authority that demanded attention. Zane's muscles tensed instinctively.

The second wave was about to begin.
