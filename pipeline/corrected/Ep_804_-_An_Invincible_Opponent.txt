Ep 804 - An Invincible Opponent

Word count: 1193

[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT ARENA]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Even after earning a nod of praise from the Emperor of the York Divine Kingdom, Logan just smiled. “Who’s next?” he asked.

Logan's Spatial Blade had already severed two fighters' arms. The initial excitement among Spirit-Tribulation martial artists evaporated into stunned silence. Every warrior on the First Tier was a champion from their house—you don't reach that level without unshakable confidence.

But Logan transcended them completely. Even the most celebrated talents hesitated.

They still had to journey to the Sacred Feather Sea. Losing an arm wasn't fatal, but recovery took time—a serious handicap for upcoming trials. This tournament was meant as entertainment before the real journey. Though it had evolved into a stage for young warriors from the four divine kingdoms to demonstrate their power, compared to exploring the secret lands, it remained a sideshow.

With that in mind, most of the First Tier fighters began to shrink back.

What they didn’t realize was that the imperial families watching from the stairs were starting to get annoyed. The Soul Sea masters kept their faces neutral, but inwardly, they were fuming at both Logan and War King York. The Star Field Divine Kingdom had been shut down again, which meant it was now up to the Wind and Harlen Divine Kingdoms to answer the challenge.

The emperor of the Wind Divine Kingdom said nothing, and the crown prince and his brothers behind him followed his lead.

The Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom, however, let his gaze sweep over Randy and the Crown Prince, his eyes flashing. His silent stare was a clear command.

Randy felt a jolt of nervous energy under that look. He was just about to suggest Zane when the Crown Prince beat him to it, his voice ringing out clearly. “Edwin will fight for me!”

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Randy’s expression turned to ice, but he kept his mouth shut.

Fine, he thought. If you’re so desperate for a comeback, go for it. Edwin is your last hope. If he gets beaten—or worse, loses an arm—it’ll gut your team. You’ll have a hell of a time in the secret lands after that. Let’s see if you step up after Edwin gets crushed.

The Crown Prince was, in fact, getting desperate. He’d been hyping up Edwin and Gregory to prove his own judgment to the Emperor, to show he had a knack for managing people. But Gregory had let him down, getting dismantled so easily in his match.

Now, Edwin was his only shot.

The Shadow Martial House was the most powerful in the entire Harlen Divine Kingdom. While it was technically ranked as a sixth-grade sect alongside the Crystal Martial House, they weren’t even in the same league. As one of the brightest stars from the Shadow Martial House, Edwin was more than capable of clutching this win.

“Edwin, I’m counting on you,” the Crown Prince said, stepping forward to clap a hand on his shoulder.

[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]

[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSH]

Edwin pulled a long spear from his Storage Ring. The crowd stirred immediately.

“A high-grade Relic — for a Spirit-Tribulation warrior?”

“The Harlen Divine Kingdom is known for its weapon refiners. More sacred artifacts come out of that kingdom than anywhere else. Even so, a high-grade Relic spear is rare.”

[SFX: THUNDERCLAP]

Edwin shook the spear once. The shaft flexed like a whip and cracked the air with a sound like a thunderclap.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

Logan looked at him and held up five fingers. “Five strikes. That's all I'll need.”

Edwin snorted. “Save the theatrics. Whether it's one strike or fifty, the outcome is the outcome.”

A smile crossed Logan's face. “This time, my opponent is not bad. It seems I should be more serious.”

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]

Outside the York Divine Kingdom's corner, the mood across all three other kingdoms shifted. Nobody liked Logan. But nobody could deny he had every right to be exactly as arrogant as he was.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

The spirit of the spear coiled around Edwin’s arm, and his own fighting intent surged to meet it. A low growl rumbled in his chest. 

He was done talking. Gripping his spear, he ran the fingers of his other hand along the shaft.

[SFX: SERPENT HISS]

With a soft hiss, the weapon seemed to come alive, transforming into a coiling snake that wrapped around Edwin’s body, its tip aimed straight at Logan.

[SFX: SWORD SLASH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

A smirk touched Logan’s lips. He gave the Spatial Blade a lazy wave, then struck.

The moment Edwin activated the spirit of the spear, it acted like a hunting beast with a razor-sharp sense of smell, lunging for Logan the instant he was in range.

[SFX: METAL CLANG]

The spear tip slammed into Logan’s body, but just as it made contact, the shimmering Spatial Armor materialized around him once more. The attack only managed to carve a deep gash into the armor; it couldn't break through. Shattering it in a single blow was impossible.

Seeing this, the expressions of the Harlen warriors turned grim.

Everyone had assumed Logan couldn’t form his Spatial Armor while wielding the Spatial Blade. They’d figured it was one or the other, or maybe he just hadn’t needed the defense until now. But this guy had an impenetrable shield and a sword that could cut through reality itself. How could anyone possibly beat that? The odds were zero.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

Just as they feared, a now-serious Logan completely gave up on dodging. He let Edwin’s spear hammer against his armor while the Spatial Blade in his hand blinked through space, relentlessly slashing at his opponent.

[SFX: BATTLE STRIKES]

[SFX: RAPID SWORD SLASHES]

A third strike. A fourth. A fifth.

[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSH]

Then something no one expected — Edwin's spear coiled around the Spatial Blade itself, locking it in place, and the tip drove straight for Logan's face. Logan tilted his head just enough. The tip grazed his ear.

But the spear had a mind of its own. It twisted in midair and came back for the back of his head.

[SFX: BLADE DISSOLVING]

[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]

For the first time, Logan let go of the Spatial Blade. It dissolved back into nothing as he dropped back, putting distance between them.

[AMBIENT: COLLECTIVE EXHALE FROM THE CROWD]

The Harlen warriors breathed again. Nobody had pushed Logan to retreat before. Nobody had forced him to abandon his weapon. It wasn't a win, but it was something.

Logan reformed the Spatial Blade in his hand with a casual wave. “Not bad,” he said. “Not bad at all.”

[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]

[SFX: BODY IMPACT]

After the fifth blow landed, Logan slid to the side. A spray of blood erupted through the air as another arm tumbled from the sky.

The Crown Prince’s confident facade finally broke, his brow furrowing in raw disappointment. What was he supposed to do now? Was he really going to have to fight himself?

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Just then, his gaze fell on a lean, wiry figure in the square. 

It was Zane, his eyes locked on the fight.
