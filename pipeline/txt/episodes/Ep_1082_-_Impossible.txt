Ep 1082 - Impossible

Word count: 1092

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TOWER INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

For the most part, everyone figured the show was over. The trial in the Enchanted Barrier Tower was, as far as anyone was concerned, already decided.

Layla hadn’t just broken the old record set by Supreme Lord Illusion; she’d pulverized it, setting a new one so impossibly high it felt like no one would ever reach it again. Sure, Zane had also surpassed Lord Illusion’s old record, but nobody thought he had a prayer of touching Layla’s. Eighteen thousand miles was already an unbelievable feat. Twenty-eight thousand? That was just another world.

Since Zane was the last one left inside the tower, every eye was on him. But as time ticked by, he just… sat there, still as a stone, like a monk lost in meditation.

A frown tightened Layla’s lips, her eyes glinting in the sun. Even she had to admit, the first eighteen thousand miles were a major hurdle, but it was manageable. After that, the Law Power became so dense that the fragments compressed into a churning vortex—the inner circle. Lord Illusion had probably stopped there because of that Law Storm. But Layla hadn’t even hesitated.

The moment she unleashed her Wrath of the Ocean, she’d plowed right through it. The inner circle was intense, but it hadn’t been much of a challenge for her.

What Zane was facing was that same compressed vortex — the point where all the Law fragments from the ancient battle had been crushed together until they formed something closer to a gray fog than a storm. It looked almost slow from the outside. But the compression made every single step forward a battle.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Zane stood perfectly still in the middle of it, eyes closed. Not because he couldn’t push through. Because something had caught his attention.

The condensed storm in front of him looked familiar. The way all the source Laws had been compressed to their absolute limit — it reminded him of the chaotic energy inside his own Vitality core. Not similar. Nearly identical.

His thoughts moved slowly, carefully. All things in the universe evolved from chaos. The source Laws were the foundation of everything — and when they were crushed together like this, stripped of their individual character, they began to resemble the elementary form of chaotic energy itself.

Origin energy could be seized and cultivated by any warrior who practiced the true essence system. So what about this? If the condensed Law Storm had taken on the nature of chaotic energy… could the Primordial Chaos Tactic control it?

He didn’t hesitate long. He began to run the Primordial Chaos Tactic — not to stir the chaotic energy already inside him, but in reverse, reaching outward, treating the storm itself as raw origin energy to be drawn and controlled.

His master had never anticipated this. In the universe, true chaotic energy was almost nonexistent. But Zane had never been someone who followed a road already paved. He’d chosen this path. He would have to find his own way.

[SFX: LOW RESONANT HUM]

After a single breath, an invisible fluctuation spread out from his body. His eyes were still closed, but the corners of his mouth curved slightly upward. Around him, the gray Law Storm — which had been rolling in every direction — began to shift. Slowly, almost imperceptibly, it parted. Rolling to either side, opening a path.

[AMBIENT: WAITING CROWD]

Outside the tower, no one noticed. To the palace masters and the Lord of the Sunset World, Zane was still just standing there.

Meanwhile, everyone was left wondering what, exactly, was going through Zane’s head.

Two minutes passed. Then five.

[MUSIC: TENSION BUILDING]

An hour went by. Then two. Then four.

The heads of the palaces and the Lord of the Sunset World remained perfectly calm. For seasoned warriors of their power, a few hours was nothing more than the blink of an eye.

But the other warriors in the plaza were starting to get antsy. It wasn’t that they were impatient—these were fighters who had endured years of grueling, mind-numbing training. A few hours of waiting was a walk in the park. Some of them had spent whole years in secluded cultivation with only themselves for company. But the Passing the Torch competition was more than just this one trial, and the anticipation for what came next was making the time drag.

[AMBIENT: RESTLESS CROWD MURMUR]

A restless murmur started to ripple through the plaza.

“What is he doing? He’s holding everyone up.”

“Who knows? Just keep it down.”

“Is he going to give up or keep going?”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Then, a shift. “Looks like he’s moving.”

The whispers changed tone, from impatient to curious. At first, he was just walking. Then he was airborne, flying slowly.

“He’s picking up speed!” someone shouted, and suddenly the crowd was buzzing.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

“Wow, he’s moving really fast now!”

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

The buzz turned into a roar of disbelief. “But he hasn’t used his Vitality! He’s not using any secret techniques. How is he so fast?”

“Oh my God!”

[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]

The same warriors who’d been complaining moments ago were now gaping in awe, their gripes about the delay completely forgotten. Zane was moving even faster than Layla had.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Even the palace heads leaned forward, their composed expressions cracking with astonishment. The Lord of the Sunset World watched Zane with an intensity that bordered on disbelief, trying to make sense of what he was seeing. He knew Layla had unleashed the Wrath of the Ocean to cover twenty-eight thousand miles so quickly. But Zane… Zane was relying on nothing but his own power. And for the life of him, the Lord couldn’t figure out how he was doing it.

“Zane might actually be more powerful than Layla,” one of the palace heads breathed, his jaw slack.

“It looks that way,” another shot back, “but he hasn’t even hit twenty thousand miles yet. I doubt he can break her record.”

Yolande’s expression remained as serene as ever, but the faintest hint of a private, knowing smile played at the corners of her lips.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PIANO]

Layla, on the other hand, couldn’t hide the shock in her eyes. She pursed her lips, murmuring to herself. “I knew you wouldn’t stop at eighteen thousand miles. You’d be a pretty poor rival if you did.” Her voice was a low whisper. “But getting past twenty-eight thousand miles?”

She gave a slight shake of her head. “That’s impossible.”
