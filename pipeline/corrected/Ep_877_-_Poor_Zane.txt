Ep 877 - Poor Zane

Word count: 1038

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]

The path of a warrior is supposed to be predictable. When they reach the Life-and-Death Realm, their Vitality liquefies, allowing their core to store far more power. Upon reaching the Soul Sea Realm, that core transforms into a vast internal ocean, a space so immense that they can create an entire world within themselves. At the highest echelons, a warrior could even nurture life in that ocean—creatures that could reproduce, build civilizations, and maybe even learn to cultivate power on their own.

During this stage, the liquefied Vitality was meant to slowly solidify, forming continents on the surface of the Soul Sea. 

Logically, at Zane’s current level, he should have been nowhere near solidifying his Vitality. 

And yet, for some reason he couldn’t fathom, it was happening anyway.

The Cobalt Dragon was stunned. Even with all his ancient knowledge, he couldn’t make sense of it.

[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

As the Vitality in Zane’s body hardened, its rotation slowed. And in that exact moment, a dark cloud appeared in the sky.

[AMBIENT: WORRIED CROWD MURMURS]

Murmurs rippled through the crowd below. 

“Where did that come from? The sky was clear a second ago.”

“It’s getting bigger! Fast!”

[SFX: DEEP OMINOUS RUMBLE]

[MUSIC: APOCALYPTIC ORCHESTRA]

Everyone looked up. The cloud splashed across the clear blue sky like a drop of ink in water, but what was truly terrifying was how quickly it expanded. In the blink of an eye, it had consumed the sky above Feather Emperor’s Island. 

One moment it was day, the next, the sun was gone. It took only thirty seconds for the cloud to blot out the entire Sacred Feather Sea. 

Five minutes later, it had covered the whole of the Divine Kingdom Continent.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]

The vast lands of the seven divine kingdoms were plunged into an unnatural darkness. Oil lamps were lit in towns and cities as worried elders looked to the sky, muttering prayers that the gods weren’t angry.

Staring up at the roiling darkness, Zane felt a powerful, malicious intent aimed squarely at him. It was the ill will of the heavens themselves.

Is this my first Lesser Celestial Tribulation? he wondered.

He’d seen one before. When Tia faced hers, the phenomenon was huge, but it had only covered the area directly above the Cloud Hall. This dark cloud, however, seemed endless. He could only imagine how powerful the tribulation itself would be.

He swallowed hard. Looking at the lightning dragons circling in the darkness above him, a genuine flicker of fear crossed his face. He even regretted drinking the nectar from the Tribulation Flower’s stamen.

It would be a lie to say he wasn’t afraid. Even the Soul Sea masters around him had gone pale at this level of power — and the tribulation wasn’t aimed at them.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

“Such a powerful aura,” the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom sighed. “This is the trial Zane has to face.” 

He shook his head. You’re just too different, kid. Too special. It’s a shame. If the heavens want you dead, you have no choice but to die. There’s no resisting it.

“Look at this power,” War King Wind said quietly. “How can Zane survive this?”

A chill ran down the spines of the other Soul Sea Realm warriors. Zane was probably done for.

York said nothing aloud. But the thought was plain on his face: a peerless genius — and yet too heaven-defying. If the heavens want him dead, he has to die. There’s no resisting it.

War King Wind turned to Fiona and Callan. “Get behind me. Stay close.” He pulled them back. The overflow from a tribulation this powerful was enough to kill a Spirit-Tribulation warrior outright.

Scott did the same with Randy. The other Spirit-Tribulation geniuses on the island, those without a Soul Sea master beside them, scattered as far as they could.

Fiona looked back at Zane. “Father — will he be all right?”

War King Wind didn’t answer. Zane couldn’t be judged by common sense. Even he wasn’t confident. He had no optimistic answer to give.

Fiona went quiet. She already knew.

Then, below them, Zane unclenched his hands. His face had changed — the fear was gone, replaced by something harder. He had no room to retreat. This was a step he had to take. Every warrior’s path led here eventually. He looked up into the darkness.

“Come,” he said.

[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

[AMBIENT: STORM WINDS]

A bolt of lightning tore through the dark clouds, revealing the shimmering forms of lightning dragons circling directly above him. 

The thunder rolled across the entire continent, growing louder and more intense, closing in on Feather Emperor’s Island.

It’s here, Zane thought, his eyes glinting. He began to draw on the power in his core, but it was far harder now. Because his Vitality had solidified, pulling on it felt like trying to move a mountain. 

The solid mass barely budged, but he had no other choice. He had to get used to it. Over time, this struggle had made his energy conduits far thicker and more resilient than those of any ordinary warrior.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

“Vitality,” he said, and a light purple barrier of energy enveloped him. Then, he spread his consciousness outward, reaching for the stars. “Stellar Body.”

[SFX: CELESTIAL BEAM]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHOIR]

Two streaks of starlight suddenly pierced through the thick clouds, converging on Zane’s body.

“That starlight!” a Soul Sea Realm warrior exclaimed. “He’s using the power of the stars themselves! What kind of technique is that?” The man had spent his entire life visualizing the stars in his own training, but he’d never been able to draw down a single mote of actual starlight, no matter how clearly he pictured it. He couldn’t comprehend how Zane was mobilizing constellations from so far away. It was simply incredible.

Seeing the starlight sheath Zane’s body, the other experts fell silent. They had all assumed he’d shown his full strength back at the West Dragon River. Now it was clear he was still hiding a major advantage.

Just how many aces does this kid have up his sleeve?
