Ep 600 - Don't Cry
Word count: 983
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
The tenth Soul Echo was unlike anything Zane had faced before.
In that moment, Zane drew upon the Transcendent Divine Might, pulling raw starlight into his very being.
The Stellar Body technique had eight moves in its first stage. The Red Dragon had explained that each move corresponded to one of the eight platforms of the Pathforged Realm.
Every time Zane forged a new platform, he’d unlock a new move.
But Zane had only forged one platform so far. Luckily, the Confluence Platform unlocked the very first move of the Stellar Body: Star Shakes the Earth!
[SFX: CELESTIAL RUMBLE]
[AMBIENT: COSMIC ATMOSPHERE]
High above, in the vast, dark expanse, a single star suddenly flared. What was once a pinpoint of light swelled in an instant, growing a hundred times brighter until it shone like a polished stone.
This was the unique power of the Stellar Body. It allowed a warrior to absorb starlight directly, boosting their own abilities.
[SFX: ETHEREAL GLOW]
A faint, ethereal glow, like captured starlight, now enveloped Zane's body.
Because he was only connected to a single star, the light was still dim. With more connections, the light would intensify. The ultimate goal was to become a star in human form
The man recognized the technique instantly. The power of the Stellar Body was completely different from that of his own Tapestry of Sun, Moon, and Stars. The Tapestry was more tangible, built on visualizing the cosmos to draw power. This—this was raw attunement.
A faint smile touched the man’s lips. "You're lucky to have the Transcendent Divine Might when you're just a War Fighter," he said. "As for your question, you'll have to find the answer yourself." He paused, a challenge glinting in his eyes. "If you want to pass this test, you'll have to withstand one punch from me."
He didn't wait for an answer.
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
As he clenched his fist, the Channels along his arm bulged, one after another. A normal human’s Channels were green. This man’s pulsed with brilliant gold.
[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]
He roared, "Celestial Locus Devour!"
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE ENERGY BURST]
The energy he’d been building erupted in a flash. Even though the man had lowered his power to a level-ten War Fighter, the force he unleashed sent a jolt of shock through Zane.
Zane had held his attack, hoping to ask about the Stellar Body’s origins. But the man's challenge made it clear: there would be no answers, only a fight. Fine. Zane cast the questions aside and prepared to go all out.
"Star Shakes the Earth!"
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE]
The power they unleashed was staggering. Though both were technically level-ten War Fighters, they were hitting with a force far beyond that of a War General. Even an ordinary War Lord couldn't summon such an explosive burst of energy.
[SFX: BLAZING ENERGY]
The raw power in Zane’s body surged as starlight flooded his fist. The light condensed, his fist blazing as if he were holding a miniature sun. He threw the punch.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
The man’s counterpunch was just as devastating. As raw energy poured from his fist, it formed a massive vortex, its center glowing with the turbulent radiance of a raging sea.
[MUSIC: CLIMACTIC ORCHESTRAL HIT]
[SFX: THUNDEROUS COLLISION]
A roar like a tidal wave slammed their ears as the two energies collided in a flash of light so brilliant it would have blinded any onlooker.
This wasn't a fistfight anymore. It was a pure contest of power. The one left standing would be the winner.
[SFX: BLINDING FLASH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Blinded by the glare, Zane pushed forward. He couldn't see, but he knew what he had to do — keep moving, keep pushing, tear through whatever was in front of him.
The power of starlight was far greater than he had imagined. Even everything he had wouldn't have been enough to break through that vortex by brute force alone — but beneath the steady, multiplying pressure of the stars, his fist kept moving. The vortex resisted, flared, and then broke.
[SFX: SHATTERING IMPACT]
Zane's fist drove through and connected.
[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]
The man's body came apart. Not violently — it simply dissolved, piece by piece, until only his head remained, floating in the dark void, regarding Zane with calm eyes.
"Good," the head said. "Talent and strength both. You have earned the recognition of the Demi-Immortal Race."
"What does that mean?" Zane asked. "What do I get?"
The faintest smile. "No reward. This is simply your life."
Zane frowned. "My life? What life?"
The head was already fading, the voice growing thin. "Once you reach that step, you will understand. At that time…. Don't cry."
[MUSIC: HAUNTING STRINGS]
The head dissolved. Zane stood alone in the void, those last two words hanging in the air around him.
Don't cry.
He had no idea what that meant. And somehow that made it worse.
[AMBIENT: ECHOING TOWER INTERIOR]
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
The Tower Spirit's voice drifted from the darkness, ancient and measured. "Young man. You have passed the final test."
Zane's chest still ached from whatever he'd just witnessed—that dissolving head, those final cryptic words. He pushed the confusion aside and focused on the present. "What's the reward?"
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Silence stretched between them. Then the spirit spoke again, each word deliberate and weighted with significance. "You have been granted the highest authority in this Tower. You may pass freely through its walls and floors. And you may expel any living creature from its grounds—at will."
Zane stared into the void, his mind racing. The implications crashed over him like waves. Walls couldn't contain him. Enemies couldn't trap him here. He could banish anyone—even those stronger than himself.
His pulse quickened. "Does that mean the Tower of Sin belongs to me?"
"No. The Tower cannot be claimed by you. Only the Demi-Immortal Race can refine it. You have earned their recognition — nothing more. Your strength is not yet sufficient to take true ownership. For now, you may wield limited control, with my assistance."
[MUSIC: DARK REALIZATION THEME]
Zane turned the spirit's words over in his mind. Pass through walls. Expel any living creature.
He now held the power of life and death over every creature in the Tower of Sin.
