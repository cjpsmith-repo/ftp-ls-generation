Ep 1253 - Silent Prayers

Word count: 1173

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
[AMBIENT: LARGE STONE HALL]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
When the three men walked into the hall, the one on the left chuckled.
“So, these are the warriors who just ascended to our Jadeheart Sanctuary.”
The most handsome of the three stood in the middle, and every woman’s eye was on him the moment he entered. “Anyone who makes it to the upper worlds is the best of the best from below,” he explained, his voice smooth and casual. “They use special elixirs from a young age to preserve their youth, even alter their features. It’s why they’re all so beautiful.”
The women from the Soul Sea Realm had been waiting for some time, and they were getting anxious. 
Finally, the woman in the lead stepped forward.
“Excuse me,” she said, her voice steady. “Why are you keeping us here? What does the Jadeheart Sanctuary want with us?”
One of the men chuckled. “Don’t worry, we don’t have any bad intentions. It’s just that it’s difficult for women to ascend to the upper worlds, so we decided to save you the trouble of mining.”
[SFX: MURMURS OF DISBELIEF]
A collective skepticism rippled through the hall. They’d all been through hell to get here; they knew a lie when they heard one.
“If that’s the case, why are we locked in here?” another woman asked, a tight smile on her face. “Why not just let us go?”
Standing in the corner, Tia frowned. If they were free, she could find Zane. But looking at the men’s faces, she knew it wasn’t going to be that simple.
And just as she thought, the man only laughed. “The Jadeheart Sanctuary is the most powerful force in the Trinity Realm. You came all this way, and now you want to pass up the rare chance to join us? Our leader, Master Hayes, is practically a World Lord, and he’s looking for disciples. You’re all lucky enough to be considered. Follow me. But be warned—he has high standards. Not just anyone makes the cut. Good luck, ladies.”
The words sent a ripple of quiet panic through the room.
[SFX: ARMORED FOOTSTEPS SURGING FORWARD]
At a signal from the middle man, a squad of armored soldiers poured into the hall, gray bracelets already in hand.
“Restriction Bracelets,” one of the women whispered, backing away. “They’ll cut us off from our own Vitality!”
A few of the women tried to fight it, throwing wild strikes at the soldiers closing in. It was hopeless. Between three Spirit Supreme Realm masters and dozens of armed Soul Sea Realm soldiers, there was no contest. One by one, the bracelets clicked shut around their wrists.
[SFX: METAL BRACELET LOCKING]
Tia didn’t resist. She only reached for the ring on her finger, turning it slowly. She’d given her very first Storage Ring to Zane, long ago, and had Cloud Hall’s craftsmen forge her a new one after. Now, as the cold metal closed around her wrist, she held onto that ring and prayed silently that wherever he was, he would come find her.
Somewhere in the Illusion Battlefield, Zane had no idea Tia had ascended at all.
[AMBIENT: FOREST HIGH IN THE WORLD TREE]
The World Tree held thirteen branches in total, each one guarded by something different. On one branch, a venomous creature waited among the frost-slick bark. On the next, a swarm of stinging insects. On the one after that, something stronger and more solitary - strong, but still no match for brute force.
[SFX: CREATURE HISSING, ICE CRACKING]
Whatever venom the first guardian carried could have killed Aurora or Amber outright. Zane didn’t slow down for it, plowing through as if the poison meant nothing to him at all.
[SFX: SWARM BUZZING]
The swarm came next, driving hundreds of stings into him at once. He took every hit like a stone might, unaffected, unbothered, and kept climbing.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
The last of the three put up the only real fight, but Zane’s raw strength won out in the end.
By the time they reached the twelfth branch, the Demon Night warriors following him were staring like they’d seen a monster. Twice before, Aurora and Amber had led their people this high up the World Tree. Both times, they’d lost warriors along the way. This time, with Zane clearing the path, they hadn’t lost a single one.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON BRANCH]
At the end of the branch, there was a circle of round red fruits.
“Forty of them,” Aurora said, counting.
“You’re saying that these fruits contain a large number of Illusion Points?” Looking at the fruits, Zane had a strange look on his face.
“A hundred thousand each,” Aurora confirmed.
Zane did the math without meaning to. Alone, he could have climbed the World Tree and claimed every fruit himself—four million Illusion Points in one trip. Aurora had said this gate had already opened three times. If he’d gone alone each time, that was twelve million points, just from fruit. It finally explained something that had been nagging at him: he’d earned five million points killing Sloane, and it had barely cracked him into the top ten thousand. 
“Everyone gets one,” Aurora said, already handing the first fruit out to the nearest Demon Night warrior. One by one, each of them received a single fruit — a hundred thousand points apiece, more than most of them had earned in the entire climb so far.
“Okay, I’ve got an idea,” Aurora said, his blue eyes fixed on Zane. “It’s gonna tank my ranking, but it’ll be great for yours. You take seventy percent of these. What do you think?”
“Sure,” Zane nodded. “But what about Amber? Doesn’t she need any?”
Amber immediately shook her head. “I don’t need them.”
“Why not?” Zane pressed.
Aurora answered for her. “If Amber gets a huge boost of Illusion Points, she’ll shoot right up the ranks. With so many Demon Night warriors watching that list, she’s sure to be noticed. The last thing she needs right now is that kind of attention—it’s better for her to just blend in and be forgotten.”
Her physical body was still trapped in a Demon Night prison, after all. If she broke into the top five million warriors in the entire Illusion Battlefield—which, with her strength, wouldn’t even be hard—it would be like setting off a flare. If the Queen of Punishment happened to notice, the consequences would be unthinkable. It might have been a long shot, but it wasn’t a risk worth taking.
Zane looked at Amber, his gaze deep and understanding. “I get it,” he said softly.
[SFX: CRUSHING FRUIT]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
He took the fruits from Aurora and began crushing them, one by one. With each one, a burst of Illusion Points erupted and flowed into him.
In total, the twenty-one fruits netted him a staggering two-point-one million Illusion Points—not much less than he’d gotten for killing Sloane.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE TENSION]
He checked his ranking, expecting a huge jump. 
In this supreme world, he’d climbed to rank two hundred sixty-seven. But on the leaderboard for the entire Illusion Battlefield? He hadn’t just failed to improve—he’d actually dropped to the fifteen-thousandth spot.
The warriors ahead of him were racking up points at a speed he could barely comprehend. How would he ever catch up?
