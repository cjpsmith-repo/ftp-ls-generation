Ep 1168 - Into Hiding

Word count: 1079

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL MYSTERIOUS]
Freya was walking through a void.
[SFX: SPATIAL DISPLACEMENT WHOOSH]
One moment, the palace had been all around them; the next, it was gone. It felt like she was on a solid floor, but when she looked down, it was completely transparent. Far, far below, mountains and rivers stretched out to the horizon. The whole thing was deeply weird.
[SFX: CURIOUS QUESTIONING VOICE]
“Zane, where are we?” she asked, her curiosity getting the better of her.
“The Wheel of Reincarnation,” he replied.
Freya’s shoulders slumped. That was about as helpful as no answer at all. Where did he even get a massive, space-faring… thing?
Zane must have seen the look on her face, because he smiled and quickly filled her in on everything that had happened after she’d been taken. As he spoke, the impossible situation started to make a terrifying kind of sense.
Freya’s jaw practically hit the floor.“So, The Endless Abyss is actually a giant space tool?” 
Spatial tools weren’t exactly unheard of, not in the upper or lower worlds. But a tool like this—the Wheel of Reincarnation—one that could apparently block a full-force attack from a World Lord? That was just unbelievable.
A World Lord. The name said it all, really. The master of an entire world. Across the whole universe, with its trillions of lives, they were second in power only to the Supreme Lords.
Yosef had been forced to let her go because Zane had cornered him. Freya wasn’t sure how to feel about it. Yosef had used her to threaten Zane, but Zane hadn’t run. He hadn’t abandoned her. He’d figured out a way to save her. 
A warmth bloomed in her chest at the thought. He hadn’t left her behind.
“So where are we going now?” Freya asked after a moment.
[MUSIC: TENSE URGENT STRINGS]
“We have to get out of the Dark World,” Zane said, his expression turning serious. “If we don’t go now, we might not get another chance.”
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]
The Wheel of Reincarnation was tough, sure—tough enough to block Yosef’s attacks for now. But Yosef was just one man, and for all his power, he wasn’t a true World Lord. The real ones were much stronger. And now that Lavender’s identity was out, the Queen of Punishment would move fast. She’d send people to hunt Lavender down. World Lords, grand World Lords… maybe even Supreme Lords. Not even Zane could handle those kinds of odds.
The situation was still dire. There was no time to relax.
“Where will we go after we leave the Dark World?” Lavender asked, her question echoing Freya’s.
“Hmm?” Zane paused, a flicker of surprise on his face. He actually considered the question, and a hint of hesitation crept into his eyes.
Should he go back to The Alliance? He could probably hide among their three thousand worlds. As a member of The Gossamer Pavilion, he had his privileges. But bringing them there now, with his and Lavender’s identities exposed, would just be putting a target on everyone’s back. And what about Lavender? Where could she possibly go?
He shook his head. “No. We’re not going back to The Alliance.”
“Then where?” Lavender asked.
“We go into hiding,” Zane said. “That’s all we can do for now.”
As he spoke, his mind drifted elsewhere — to the reason he’d come to the upper world in the first place. Finding Elara. That goal had never loosened its grip on him. But he still had no clue where she was. If she wasn’t somewhere in this world… had she already gone somewhere beyond it?
He filed the thought away. There was no time to chase it now.
[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]
The Wheel of Reincarnation cut across the sky of the Dark World, passing over city after city — Demon Night settlements, human districts, Ogre strongholds — each one erupting in stunned silence as the colossal disc blazed overhead. Several hours passed. By the time they neared the edge of the realm, they’d crossed nearly half the Dark World.
[SFX: DEEP SPATIAL RUMBLE]
The realm boundary loomed ahead. The designated passageway was far too narrow for something the size of the Wheel. Zane activated several divine runes and drove it straight into open space instead.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
The Wheel’s surface ignited in a blaze of white light as it punched through into the void between worlds.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC REFLECTIVE STRINGS]
Once, they were all young members of the Demon Night. Then Lavender became a queen, and they became her royal guards.
[SFX: DISTANT ECHO]
Zane remembered Cassian nearly dying to protect her while they were exploring The Endless Abyss. He would have gladly given his life for her back then. Now, it looked like he was here to take it.
[MUSIC: DARK TRAGIC STRINGS]
A hundred thousand years later, the four of them had become World Lords themselves, their power far beyond that of an ordinary one. They had been Lavender’s inner circle, and now the Queen of Punishment had sent them to hunt her.
It wasn’t that the queen lacked other options. She had countless powerful warriors at her command, many of them stronger than those four. Sending them was a message. A flex. It was a calculated, cruel choice, as if she were saying, *Lavender, the people who were once closest to you have all betrayed you. What makes you think you can stand against me?*
That was the sheer arrogance behind it. That was why she sent Cassian, Maren, Taryn, and Paige.
Hearing the shift in Lavender’s voice, Zane pushed the Wheel of Reincarnation to move faster. He glanced over at her, his expression blanking as he saw the pain and disappointment on her face. He had no idea what to say.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: VESSEL SHAKING VIOLENTLY]
Just then, another violent tremor rocked the entire vessel.
One of the four—a martial artist in a white robe, holding a pale green spear—had struck them. 
[SFX: SPEAR STRIKE IMPACT]
With a single thrust, he’d nearly flipped the entire Wheel of Reincarnation.
Zane’s eyes narrowed as he wrestled with the runes of the Wheel, his whole body straining. It took everything he had, but he finally managed to stabilize them.
That was when Lavender spoke. “Let me talk to them.”
Zane said nothing. He just activated another rune. 
[AMBIENT: ECHOING VOID]
A moment later, Lavender’s voice drifted out from the Wheel of Reincarnation, broadcasting into the empty void.
