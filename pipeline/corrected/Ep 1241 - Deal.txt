Ep 1241 - Deal

Word count: 1033

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ANTICIPATION]
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
No one had actually expected Zane to win.
Sure, the fight had been heart-pounding, but nobody really believed the new guy would manage to take down someone as powerful as Sloane. As the shock wore off, whispers rippled through the crowd. Who was he? Where did he come from? Who was his mentor? Suddenly, everyone was dying to know.
“Sloane’s back!” someone shouted.
Heads whipped around. 
Sure enough, Sloane had been revived and was now walking slowly toward the top of the city wall.
“She didn’t leave the city after coming back to life,” one person muttered. “She’s going up the wall instead.”
“She’s not going to challenge him again?”
“Does that mean she’s admitting she lost?”
Everyone had figured she’d rush back for a rematch the second she was revived. Sloane had never struck them as the type to give up, but maybe they had her all wrong.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]
Meanwhile, Zane was still perched at the top of Sacred Mount Lodestone, looking for all the world like he was waiting for her. He’d been expecting a major showdown, so he was surprised when she chose to climb the city wall instead.
He looked down at her from the mountain, a small smile playing on his lips. “Not coming back for round two?”
Sloane just shook her head.
“No,” she replied, her voice firm.
It wasn’t that she was scared. The whole point of the Illusion Battlefield’s first phase was to rack up as many Illusion Points as possible, and she couldn’t do that by losing to Zane over and over again. Sloane was stubborn, but she wasn’t dumb enough to fight a battle she knew she couldn’t win.
“We’ll have our chance to fight again, sooner or later,” she told him, a sly smile touching her lips. “And when we do, I hope you’re ready.”
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RISING TENSION]
The next time they met, she’d have already merged with Laney. And when that happened, there was no way she would lose.
Zane just shrugged, rubbing the back of his head. “Sure. I have a question, though.”
“What is it?”
“How did you absorb the Illusion Points from that warrior in the blue robes?”
He’d already asked her once, but she’d ignored him. He brought it up again because it was bugging him. No matter how powerful she was, there was no way Sloane could just bend the rules of the Illusion Battlefield.
[SFX: SOFT CHIME]
Sloane’s lips curved. This time, she decided to help him. A small golden pill materialized in her palm, pulsing faintly. “This pill carries my imprint,” she said. “Any Illusion Points from a cultivator who dies near something I’ve marked with it flow straight to me — no matter where I am when it happens.”
[MUSIC: THOUGHTFUL REALIZATION]
Realization crashed over Zane. He smacked a hand against his forehead. It was so simple he couldn’t believe he hadn’t thought of it himself. A cultivator could scatter their imprint across an entire main city — hidden in traps, in bait, anywhere a victim might stumble — and never have to lift a finger. The kills would come to them.
Others had already figured out the same trick. Layla was one of them — her ranking had been climbing fast, and now Zane understood why. 
Rather than hunting down strong cultivators, she’d started scattering clusters of strange seeds through every main city she passed. Within hours they’d bloom into fist-sized dandelions, delicate and beautiful, each ringed with clear, needle-thin hairs. 
The prettier something looked, the more likely it was to kill you. Any cultivator who wandered too close would trigger the hairs to burst outward, driving microscopic needles into their body. The needles tracked their Vitality through the meridians, then released a poison that spread through their entire Vitality core. Layla would already be long gone by then, wandering on to the next city, and the points would simply flow to her.
Zane’s realization also solved something else. There was no reason to keep dragging Sacred Mount Lodestone around as leverage anymore. A grin tugged at his mouth. “I understand,” he said, and leapt up from the peak of the mountain.
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
A ray of light flared in his hand. The mountain beneath him groaned and began to spin, shrinking rapidly until it collapsed into a miniature version of itself, small enough to fit in his palm. He pocketed it without ceremony.
Sloane’s chest tightened as she watched. She’d planned to use Zane’s need for the mountain as a bargaining chip — to force him into handing over his refining method before she’d let him leave. Now that plan crumbled. He hadn’t needed her permission at all.
Just as he was about to leave, Sloane’s lips pursed, and she called out.“Wait!”
Zane stopped and turned back, raising an eyebrow.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS NEGOTIATION]
“Let’s make a deal,” she projected, her voice ringing with a strange energy. She couldn’t let him walk away that easily. His method for refining Sacred Mount Lodestone was too important—she needed it for her training in the divine art of the five elements.
“What kind of deal?” Zane asked, his eyes fixed on her.
[AMBIENT: QUIET CITY STREET]
Five minutes later, he found himself standing in front of a quaint little house on a corner in the city of ogres. Zane sighed, looking at the peaceful home. He couldn’t help but feel a little jealous. Sloane got to lounge around here and still managed to collect a staggering number of Illusion Points, while he was out there fighting tooth and nail for a score that wasn’t even a tenth of hers.
[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL COURTYARD]
[SFX: MYSTICAL WHISPER]
Inside, Sloane stood in the middle of a courtyard, a row of blank runestones in her hands. Her voice was a delicate whisper as she breathed a single word—“Seal”—and held one of the stones out to Zane.
“I’ll teach you my method for refining the magnetic power of the mountain through this runestone,” she said. “But that’s it. How you use it from there is up to you.”
Zane nodded and reached for it.
[MUSIC: TENSE UNCERTAINTY]
But Sloane hesitated, her hand stopping just short of his. 
A sudden doubt gripped her. What if she gave this to him and he just walked away? What guarantee did she have that he would teach her his own method in return?
