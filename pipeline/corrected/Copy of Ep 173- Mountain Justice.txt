Ep 173 - Mountain Justice
Word Count: 1055
[MUSIC: TENSE INTERROGATION]
Zane stared at Isla, desperate for information about his sister.
The longer he stared, the more she felt like she was in the hot seat. After a long, tense moment, she finally broke. “It was House Walker that brought the charges. Saint Viana oversaw the trial, along with Saint Aldric.”
“Oh, I see.” A faint smile touched Zane’s lips, but his eyes, locked on Saint Viana, were filled with pure, murderous rage.
House Walker wasn’t getting away with this.
And neither were Saint Viana and Saint Aldric.
Zane knew Elara. She would never make a mistake like that. They must have put her through something horrible during that trial.
[MUSIC: DARK DETERMINATION]
Every last one of them had to die.
Zane silently vowed to take care of them all as Murry’s men escorted him forward, the weight of his sister’s suffering burning like acid in his veins.
House Walker, Saint Viana, Saint Aldric---they had tormented Elara, his innocent sister, and for that transgression, he would make sure that their bloodlines ended.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS AND CHAINS]
Murry ordered the men from the Hall of Judgment to take Zane into custody.
As they led him away, Zane passed the platform just as Vivian, who had been unconscious on the ground, began to stir.
Earlier, in the chaos of the fight, she had come at him with a flurry of willow leaf darts. Zane had simply batted her aside with the flat of his massive sword, sending her crashing into a wall and knocking her out cold.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REALIZATION]
The moment Vivian's eyes fluttered open, a terrible feeling washed over her.
“Felix!”
[SFX: HORRIFIED GASP]
Scrambling to her feet, she saw him. His body, lying in two pieces on the ground.
[SFX: AGONIZING SCREAM]
A scream of pure agony tore from her throat. Her head snapped toward Zane, her eyes burning with a hatred so intense it was almost a physical force. “I’ll kill you!”
Zane glanced at her, a casual, dismissive look, and shook his head. The woman had guts, he’d give her that. It was clear her hatred for him would be a fire that never went out. He paused, the corner of his mouth ticking up in a smirk. “If you ever get the chance.”
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS WALKING AWAY]
With that, he let the men from the Hall of Judgment lead him away.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN PRISON]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS THEME]
On the mountain behind the Hall of Judgment sat a prison for temporary detainees. Some in the Skyreach Sect called it Little Infernal Peak, a place where disciples were often thrown without a proper hearing, sometimes left to rot for over half a year.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON GRAVEL]
As Zane followed the guards up the mountain path, he noticed several circles on the ground made of round stones, with runes carved into each one.
“Hey, what are the stones and runes for?” Zane asked, his voice casual.
One of the guards snorted. “Those runes are serious business. This mountain ain’t as tough as Infernal Peak itself. If you guys started a real brawl, you’d tear the whole place down. So, the Order set up these runes to seal your Vitality.”
“These runes can really seal Vitality?” Zane asked, feigning surprise.
“Heh. Don’t believe me? Go on, give it a try,” the guard said with a smirk. He'd obviously seen that look a thousand times.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY FADING]
Zane nodded and reached inward for his Corrupt Vitality. But where he expected a surge of power, he felt... nothing. Just a void.
“It’s true,” Zane frowned. Having his Vitality sealed made him feel exposed. If someone attacked him now, he’d have to rely on his own physical strength.
[AMBIENT: DUSTY MOUNTAIN CLEARING]
On the mountain behind the Hall of Judgment was a flat, dusty clearing that served as the temporary prison of Mount Judgment.
“No way!”
The big guy couldn’t believe it. Ever since he was a kid, no one who'd challenged him in a contest of pure physical strength had ever stood a chance. This new kid was only in the first grade of the Adept Realm. How could he possibly be resisting him with just his body? But the proof was right there, straining against his own grip.
[SFX: VIOLENT STRUGGLE]
Zane caught the big guy's hand, twisted it back hard, and used the momentum to vault onto his back. In one fluid motion, Zane wrenched the man’s arm up behind him while his other hand clamped down on the back of his neck. “Leave me alone,” Zane said, his voice cold as ice. “Or I’ll snap your neck.”
As he spoke, he squeezed. But this guy wasn’t going down that easily. When Zane applied more pressure, the man just shook his head violently, the thick layer of fat on his neck helping him shake Zane’s hand loose.
At the same time, the big guy spun, his powerful arms wrapping around Zane. He lifted, and suddenly Zane’s feet were dangling in the air. The man started spinning, faster and faster, before letting go with a grunt, throwing Zane with all his might.
[SFX: BODY SLAMMING INTO WALL]
[SFX: WALL COLLAPSING]
Zane slammed into a stone wall at the edge of the prison. With a deafening crash, the wall collapsed, burying him under a cascade of rubble.
The big guy spat on the ground. “Just a kid. You can’t touch me when it comes to pure strength.”
[SFX: RUBBLE SHIFTING]
The rubble shifted. Zane pushed himself up, a smile playing on his lips. “I wasn’t looking for trouble,” he called out, brushing dust from his shoulders. “Honestly, I was just planning on keeping to myself. But you’ve changed my mind. It’s pretty boring in here, and you look like a decent workout.”
[SFX: KICKING STONES]
With that, Zane kicked the broken stones aside and started walking toward him.
A wary look crept into the big guy’s eyes. After getting slammed through a wall, this kid just stood up like it was nothing. He was a lot tougher than he looked.
But that didn’t mean the big guy was scared. He was still confident in his own raw power.
The other inmates in the yard watched, a new excitement in their eyes. They realized the big guy might have finally met his match.
