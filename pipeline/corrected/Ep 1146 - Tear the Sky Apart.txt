Ep 1146 - Tear the Sky Apart

Word count: 1027

[MUSIC: SEETHING TENSION]
Every warrior in the cavern—Ogres, Humans, and the shadowy members of the Demon Night—stared daggers at Zane, their faces masks of raw menace.
“He swallowed the Seed of the World Tree!” someone finally spat out.
A brutish voice followed. “So we cut his stomach open and get it back.”
But that idea was shot down just as quickly. 
“Impossible. Once a Core Treasure is swallowed, it enters the warrior’s Inner World. If we kill him, his Inner World collapses, and the seed will be destroyed along with it.”
[SFX: CROWD GROANING]
A wave of frustrated groans filled the cavern. 
“Then there’s nothing we can do.” Once a Core Treasure was consumed, not even the person who swallowed it could get it out, let alone anyone else.
Zane just floated there at the top of the cave, motionless. He was just following Lavender’s advice: rest for a moment after swallowing the World Tree’s seed and let it sink safely into the Sea of Chaos in his belly. Down below, the other warriors were practically vibrating with murderous rage, but Zane didn’t give them a second glance.
[MUSIC: QUIET INTERNAL WONDER]
He felt it happen gradually. The seed drifted down through the Sea of Chaos, slower and slower, until it finally touched the bottom. 
Then something changed. 
Thin tendrils pushed out from its surface, stretching into the Chaos Energy around them, and took root. The moment they did, Zane felt the seed’s existence like it had always been part of him.
The corners of his lips curved. “That’s enough.”
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND CAVERN]
While the others were still arguing, one of the ogre warriors broke from the pack. 
 A wide, ferocious grin split his face, revealing a mouth full of jagged teeth. It was pretty clear he was imagining swallowing Zane whole.
“Kid,” he boomed, his voice echoing off the stone walls. “I warned you. You swallow that seed, and this Underground Palace becomes your grave.”
[SFX: POWERFUL AURA WAVE]
The ogre unleashed his aura, and even on the small platform across the cavern, the sheer force of it made the other fighters stumble back.
“That’s the Ancient Evil Ape Bloodline!” someone hissed.
“And it’s pure,” another added, awestruck.
One of the humans spoke up, his voice low and urgent. “Once he comes down, we attack together. Maybe the seed hasn’t reached his core yet.”
Zane had already proven he was no pushover, and that had made them cautious. But with the ogre and his legendary bloodline taking the lead, their caution evaporated. Killing him was a long shot, but it was their only shot. If they didn’t act now, the chance to claim the World Tree’s seed would be gone forever.
Greed was a powerful motivator. The promise of what that seed could do—the power, the escape from their barren worlds—was enough to cloud anyone’s judgment.
The Demon Night warriors, however, held back. The blonde woman leading them pulled her people into a corner. She was definitely disappointed Zane had gotten the seed, but she couldn’t deny he’d earned it. He didn’t have the backing of a major power like the Thirteen Palaces, where a Grade-One Divine Weapon might be special but not unheard of. For a warrior from the Dark World, a weapon like that was a treasure you’d only dream of, just like the seed Zane had just swallowed.
She was also quietly puzzled. She’d watched his every move through those wind tunnels — hundreds of paths, chaos on all sides — and she still couldn’t explain how he’d found the right one.
Above them all, a faint killing intent crept into Zane’s eyes. He leaned forward slightly, let the spiraling updraft release him, and the wind caught him immediately — sweeping him into a tunnel and shooting him toward the other side of the cave.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[SFX: BLADE DRAWN]
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
The second Zane drew his sword, he was a blur, shooting forward.
The reaction was instant — not from the warriors inside the cave, but from the human Saint Lord watching through his surveillance array outside.
The moment Thundergust cleared its scabbard, the Saint Lord went very still. He knew that sword. He’d seen it before, in another man’s hands. There was only one way it could have ended up here.
*Elder Dunn’s sword,* he thought. *So. He came from the Mist Palace.*
His expression shifted — admiration giving way to something more complicated. That explained everything. The wind formation, the strength, the composure. This boy wasn’t some stray talent from a forgotten world. He had a lineage behind him.
Inside the cave, the crowd saw only the grade.
[SFX: STUNNED MURMUR]
“A Grade-One Divine Weapon—”
“How does an ascender have a Grade-One Divine Weapon?”
“He’s carrying that and the World Tree Seed—”
The greed that had been simmering boiled over. The seed was already reason enough to kill him. Now there was the sword too.
[SFX: DEEP ROAR]
[SFX: HEAVY HAMMER RAISED]
The ogre roared and raised a massive, dark golden hammer to meet him. 
[SFX: PULSING ENERGY]
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
An earthy yellow light pulsed from the weapon, and the ogre’s aura flared, suddenly several times more intense.
“Earth Law, go berserk! Tear the sky apart!” he bellowed.
Most ogres just focused on brute strength, but this one was different. He was as tough as any of them, but he’d also mastered the Earth Law. The combination of raw power and elemental magic made his hammer strike terrifyingly strong.
The watching warriors held their breath. A hit like that looked powerful enough to smash Zane and his sword into paste. Even if the sword was a Grade-One Divine Weapon, the blade looked thin, almost delicate. It could bend, or worse, shatter.
[SFX: RAPID HEARTBEAT]
Freya’s heart was pounding in her chest as she watched Zane charge. But then she saw it—the ghost of a smile on his face. In that instant, a strange calm washed over her. He knew exactly what he was doing.
As the ogre brought the hammer down in a devastating arc, Zane didn’t dodge. He didn’t even try.
