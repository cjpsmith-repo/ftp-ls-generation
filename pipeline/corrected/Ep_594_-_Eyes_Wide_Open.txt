[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Every eye on the tenth floor was fixed on the wall, and no one was breathing.
They all knew the stories of Amber's miracles, but no one was jealous.
The reason was simple: they weren't even in the same league. Jealousy was a luxury they couldn't afford.
Just getting their name anywhere near Amber's was an impossible dream, let alone performing miracles of their own. Since ancient times, countless beings from every race imaginable had entered the Tower of Sin. The number who'd managed to clear the tenth floor and take the Talent Tablet test was staggering, almost beyond calculation.
And yet, not a single one had ever earned the right to have their name etched beside hers.
Anyone whose name appeared in the top four rows was already considered a giant within the Tower. They were all destined to become masters of the Soul Sea Realm—legends who would be virtually unbeatable back on the main continent.
As for the second row? Those individuals were destined to usher in a new era.
So what happened today was monumental. Not one, but two warriors had earned their place in the top two rows.
And both of them were human.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
The first was Yolande, whose origins remained a complete mystery. When her name shot to the first row, the crowd could only sigh in awe.
She hadn't advanced in years and offered little support to the Violet Heart Sanctuary beyond bestowing her Lucky Light on others.
The other races preferred this arrangement. If Yolande decided to advance, claim a seat, and ascend to a higher realm, the consequences would be unimaginable.
With first-row talent, she would certainly eclipse even Amber's accomplishments and could single-handedly lead humanity to dominance over all other races.
But then, right after Yolande, Zane’s name had blazed onto the second row.
The two couldn't have been more different. While Zane shared Yolande’s mysterious origins, he burned with an ambition to climb higher. That ambition was exactly what made him dangerous.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
Boris wasn't the only one who knew it. Several other Ogre War Generals shared his sentiment, and some were already plotting Zane’s assassination. No price was too high. They'd willingly sacrifice ten of their own seats—they'd even strike a bargain with the humans—if it meant Zane was dead.
They were desperate to prevent the rise of another prodigy like Amber.
Boris stood apart from the others, his eyes fixed on Zane's name. A cold premonition had settled in his chest.
He was proud. He wanted to face Zane in the arena and win it cleanly. That hadn't changed.
But pride and survival were different things. From where he stood, as a member of his race, the fear was real. If Zane was allowed to grow, the Ogres—who had flourished for so long—could fall because of him.
"I still have a chance," Boris thought. "When he walks into the arena, I'll do whatever it takes to finish it. Even if it costs me everything."
Then Zane's name disappeared from the second row.
[AMBIENT: HUSHED CROWD]
The crowd went still.
A heartbeat later, it reappeared—in the first row. Pushing Yolande's name one place back. Pushing back the other ancient names already etched there— names the creatures of the continent didn't even recognize, names they could only imagine belonging to god-like existences.
And now Zane's name stood among them.
[MUSIC: SURPRISING TRIUMPH]
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE CROWD REACTION]
The crowd exploded with disbelief.
"First place—"
"Zane's name is in first place!"
"What kind of talent does that even mean? God-level is fourth row. What do you even call first row?"
The answer was simple. You called it Zane.
Even the battles in the arena had stopped. Warriors from every race on the tenth floor had set down their weapons and turned to stare at the wall.
The uproar spread in every direction.
"If he grows up, the Violet Heart Sanctuary becomes one of the three great races. Maybe the greatest."
"The Ogres won't let that happen. When they can't kill him in the arena, their War Lords at the top will ignore the rules and finish it themselves."
"Zane never should have taken the test. If he'd landed on the fifth or fourth row, he might've been fine. But now everyone's seen what he's capable of. The Ogres are spooked. They won't let a threat like that live for long."
"They learned that lesson in blood with Amber. They're still kicking themselves for not killing her when they had the chance. They won't make the same mistake twice. They'll break any rule, pay any price to eliminate him. And that's not even mentioning the Demon Night. They're lurking in the shadows, too. Zane's got a target on his back from all sides."
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Olivia pressed a hand over her mouth. Her hunch had been right. She'd believed in him, but even she hadn't dared to imagine this.
She and Elder Kingsley exchanged worried glances.
The crowd was right. There was no way the Ogres would let this slide. Bending the rules was nothing to them; they wouldn't hesitate to send a War Lord to eliminate Zane and protect their dominance.
And while the Violet Heart Sanctuary had its own masters in the Soul Sea Realm, none of them could enter the Tower of Sin. No one could get to Zane to help him. Besides, the humans only held four seats at the top of the Tower.
How could four warriors possibly stand against more than twenty Ogre lords?
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
The situation was dire. A storm was gathering, and it was heading straight for Zane. But the man at the center of it all was still inside the testing chamber, completely oblivious to the chaos erupting just outside.
