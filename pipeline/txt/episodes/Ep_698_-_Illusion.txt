Ep 698 - Illusion

Word count: 1134

[SFX: CRASHING DEBRIS]

[AMBIENT: COLLAPSING BUILDING]

Zane had to get Cora out of there. No time to bother with things like doors.

He shot into the air and smashed through the support beams. The instant they shattered, the entire estate began to buckle, sending a cascade of rubble raining down into the courtyard.

"Oh no! My… my courtyard! You oaf!" Cora cried out.

Zane let out an internal sigh. He didn't have the time — or the patience — to sit her down and explain the life-or-death situation she was in. Honestly, if this whole mess wasn't for Elara, he wouldn't be wasting a second on her.

With Cora in tow, Zane shot toward the teleportation array. Ethereal City was massive, but at his current speed, the journey would be short.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

[AMBIENT: HEAVY ATMOSPHERIC PRESSURE]

But then, a crushing presence descended from the sky. Zane's whole body went rigid; he knew that power.

His expression hardened. "Maddox is coming."

At the sight of the figure descending from the heavens, all the color drained from Cora's face. A moment ago, she'd been lost and confused, but now, a horrifying clarity washed over her. The dark warnings from her family's elders were coming true right before her eyes.

[SFX: THUNDEROUS VOICE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CONFRONTATION]

"Zane Lowell!" Maddox roared from above. "You're fast, I'll give you that. If you'd stayed hidden, you might have lived a little longer. But coming here? You're just asking to die! I'm ending you today!"

Maddox had been seething with frustration, unable to touch Zane while he was tucked away safely behind Cloud Hall's protective array. But that was then. Now, Zane was out in the open, completely exposed. After all this time, Maddox finally had him exactly where he wanted him.

Maddox knew Zane had a knack for slipping through his fingers, but this was personal. He wouldn't be able to rest until he killed the man himself. In just two years, Zane had reached the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. He was only at the first level, but give him another few years and he'd be at the Soul Sea Realm — and then it would be too late. Maddox had even diverted his assault on the Ethereal Spirit Sect just for this moment. That was how seriously he took the threat.

Zane said nothing and kept moving. As they neared the river, two columns of black smoke rose ahead of them. His heart sank. He flew over the burnt platforms without slowing down.

The arrays were gone. Both of them. He'd known it was possible — likely, even — but seeing the scorched wreckage made the reality land differently. There was no shortcut to Cloudhaven. Whatever happened next, they were on their own.

Maddox didn't push his speed. Zane was going nowhere. The arrays were ash, Elder Xander was pinned in the sect, and Zane was dragging dead weight. He let the distance hold and enjoyed it.

[MUSIC: DARK PURSUIT]

Zane ran the numbers. He knew exactly what Maddox knew, because Maddox was right. Alone, he could have outrun him — his current cultivation was enough for that. But he wasn't alone. Cora was pulling at his speed with every second, and no amount of technique changed that. He needed a different approach.

He made a decision.

He yanked Cora toward him.

"Ah—!" She screamed. Suddenly, she was in his arms, then on his back, his grip locked around her waist. Even now, a flush crept up her face. "What are you doing?!"

He ignored her and sent a surge of Vitality through his system. In an instant, his speed doubled.

Behind them, Maddox blinked. Then he cursed. "Damn it!" He surged forward, burning hard to close the gap.

[SFX: SONIC RUSH]

The burst was extraordinary while it lasted. Even Maddox, for just a moment, fell behind. Zane became a line on the horizon, and the gap between them stretched wider than it had any right to.

But the burst couldn't hold. Vitality at that output burned fast — far faster than steady flight. Zane felt the drain starting almost immediately, the reserves dropping with each passing second. He'd bought distance. Not safety.

[SFX: WIND RUSHING]

Cora lay against his shoulder, the wind a constant roar in her ears, loud enough to drown out everything else. She'd never left Ethereal City before. Not once in her entire life. She'd imagined it, of course — pictured what it might be like to travel, to see the world beyond the city walls. She hadn't imagined it would be like this, on a stranger's back, running for her life with Maddox closing in behind them.

As the first wave of Vitality faded, Maddox's speed climbed back up. Steady. Unhurried. The gap between them began to narrow.

Maddox made no effort to hide what he was doing. He wasn't racing. He was walking Zane down, the way a hunter walks down exhausted prey — patient, certain, in no particular hurry. He had all the time in the world.

Zane yelled back over his shoulder, "Since I'm going to die either way, I might as well try to find a way out! You're a sect master — cut the theatrics!"

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]

"I am no sect master," Maddox declared, his voice dripping with arrogance. "I am the Lord of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom."

While the rest of the world might have seen the Corrupted Divine Kingdom as just another faction, no one dared call it that to Maddox's face. To him, it was always the Corrupted Divine Kingdom.

"Hah! You've got some nerve calling that a divine kingdom," Zane sneered.

"A dead man running his mouth," Maddox snarled. "Fine. I'll just have to make your death especially painful."

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]

He surged forward, a brilliant energy crackling around his fist as power gathered within him, just waiting for an opening.

A wave of pure despair washed over Cora as she felt the suffocating power emanating from Maddox. This was it. She was going to die, slung over some stranger's shoulder.

But then, the impossible happened.

[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]

Specks of crimson light bled from Zane's body, coalescing into a solid form right beside them. It was Lavender, his sword spirit. She materialized near Cora, shot her a look that was pure mockery, and then plucked the spear right out of Zane's hand before launching herself at Maddox.

Cora was stunned. She'd heard rumors that Zane's sword spirit was unique, but a spirit with facial expressions? A spirit that was smirking at her? Sword spirits didn't work that way. They didn't have faces like that. They didn't look at people.

It had to be an illusion, she decided.

Her eyes were just playing tricks on her.
