Ep 341 - Double Vision

Word count: 1244

[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCH IMPACT]
Doug's spiral attack lasted maybe a second before Zane shut it down with a single, devastating punch. The confrontation ended as swiftly as it had begun, leaving spectators stunned by its brevity.
Sparring matches like this traditionally served as demonstrations of skill rather than brutal contests. Their purpose was simply to establish superiority, and Zane had accomplished this with effortless grace, proving himself to exist in a true league of his own.
Despite his defeat, Doug gazed up at his opponent with undisguised awe, his features transformed into a portrait of genuine respect. The sting of loss faded beneath the weight of witnessing true excellence.
Remarkably, Zane had won without drawing a single weapon or channeling even the slightest trace of Vitality. He’d remained perfectly still throughout the encounter, never shifting from his original position.
Doug’s mind struggled to comprehend the magnitude of what he’d just encountered—Zane's true strength was on a level he couldn't even begin to wrap his head around.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
The loss stung, but it lit a fire in Doug’s spirit, intensifying his desire to join the Skyreach Sect. He was new to the Soul Realm, and he reasoned that with dedicated training, he might still have a shot.
Little did Doug know that a prodigy like Zane dazzled even the Skyreach Sect itself. Just three years ago, Evan was the only one who’d captured attention during the All Peaks Competition. This season, however, the Sect had gotten very lucky, delivering extraordinary talents like Finn, Patrick, and Zane simultaneously.
Doug turned to Ray and bowed his head in shame."Your Highness... I must beg your forgiveness. Please forgive me; I let you down."
Ray waved off Doug’s apology. "Think nothing of it. A talent like Zane is a once-in-a-millennium find. There’s no shame in losing to him."
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
Once in a millennium? Incredulous glances darted through the assembled crowd. Was the prince indulging in hyperbole? The Eastern Territories witnessed millions of births across a thousand years. The idea that only one person could hold those kinds of gifts struck many as impossible.
But despite Zane’s effortless win over Doug, many of Ray’s other fighters—some of them much stronger than Doug—were not impressed.
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMURS]
"Your Highness, let me have a crack at him!" a man named Sten snarled, rising to his feet as Doug slumped back to his seat. A large, intimidating figure, Sten looked like he'd be more at home on a wanted poster than in a prince’s fancy hall. The black patch covering his left eye added to his imposing presence, though Zane couldn’t tell if it concealed an actual injury or was just an affectation designed to make people nervous.
"Proceed," Ray replied with a measured nod, shifting his gaze toward Zane, a subtle smile playing at the corners of his mouth.
Sten drew a black blade from his waist — an unusual weapon, dark as midnight — and leveled it at Zane.
"You only won because of that freak body of yours. Let's see you block cold steel."
Zane considered the blade. "I won't block it bare-handed," he said flatly.
Sten took that as surrender and charged. He swung down with both hands — a crushing, mountain-splitting blow aimed at Zane's head.
[SFX: SWORD CLASH]
Streamer came up in a clean horizontal guard. The black blade connected and stopped dead. In the same motion, Zane turned his wrist and stabbed back.
Sten threw himself sideways, rolling clear of the counter. He came up on his feet, breathing hard, his face pale.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
Ray began to clap slowly. "I'd heard you defeated Evan with nothing but basic sword technique. I didn't believe it until now."
Several swordsmen in the crowd nodded in spite of themselves. Everyone knew basic sword technique. No one used it against a real opponent. No one but Zane.
Sten said nothing. He had no moves left — and he knew it.
The way Ray saw it, everyone and everything carried a price tag—a philosophy that had served him well in his political life. He could tell Zane was obviously not interested in joining his side, but he bet that Zane would change his tune if the price was right.
Exceptional ability like Zane’s needed vast resources for proper cultivation, resources that even the prestigious Skyreach Sect might struggle to provide. Ray understood the fundamental nature of ambitious fighters—their insatiable hunger for power, for advancement, for advantage. This universal desire would be his leverage, the prince mused, as he watched the young prodigy with predatory interest.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BUILDUP]
Sten slowly reached up and unfastened the black patch that covered his left eye. The movement was deliberate, almost ceremonial, drawing every gaze in the chamber.
No one in Prince Ray’s entourage had ever seen him remove this mysterious accessory. Most of them assumed that the eye beneath was missing—a war wound or birth defect perhaps—and carefully avoided the subject, never bringing it up for fear of triggering his notorious temper.
[SFX: CROWD GASPS]
But as the patch fell away, a collective gasp rippled through the assembly. The eye underneath looked completely intact, healthy, and functional. It did have, however, one extraordinary and unsettling characteristic that left the onlookers transfixed with a mixture of awe and unease.
[MUSIC: SURPRISING REVEAL]
A voice in the crowd shouted in disbelief. "Two pupils!"
Sten’s left eye had not one, but two distinct pupils—an unsettling, almost supernatural aberration that sent a chill through the onlookers.
Zane stared, transfixed by the biological impossibility before him.
Never in his life had he seen anything like it. The dual pupils gazed outward with an eerie intensity, as though they could perceive layers of reality invisible to normal sight.
Ancient folklore whispered of such rare beings—those blessed or cursed with double-pupiled vision could supposedly see right through an opponent’s defenses, identifying vulnerabilities that no ordinary fighter could detect. People with eyes like that were so incredibly rare that no one knew for sure if it was true.
Most dismissed these tales as nothing more than superstition. The rarity made scientific verification impossible, relegating such accounts to the realm of myth. But Sten’s confident revelation asset suggested that there had to be some truth to the legends after all. Zane tightened his grip on his sword, but his mind stayed perfectly calm. He didn’t really mind if Sten could find his weaknesses. If anything, it'd be a good way to learn a thing or two about his own blind spots.
[SFX: SCANNING BEAM OF LIGHT]
Sten’s strange left eye narrowed, and a faint beam of light shot out, washing over Zane’s body.
The legends were true: his twin pupils could pinpoint an enemy's weaknesses. The only catch was that using the power left him with a splitting headache that could last for days. It took a soul of immense power to wield, and Sten had no way of training his.
Because of that, he never used it unless he absolutely had to.
But today was different. He couldn’t stomach the thought of being so thoroughly humiliated by Zane. The kid had made him lose face, and Sten was dead set on getting it back.
However, as the light from his eye washed over Zane, Sten saw something he never, ever expected. His jaw went slack.
His confidence evaporated in an instant as he struggled to process what he saw.
What in the world happened to this kid?
