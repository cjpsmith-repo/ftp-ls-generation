Ep 908 - Into the Sea

Word count: 1181

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]

The automaton wasn't alive, and Zane knew better than to think a simple slit throat would be enough to take it down. But the move served its purpose. He brought his heel down, crushing its head, and the headless body froze solid on the stone pillar.

Zane calmly reached for the long, dark-gold bow. 

It was as tall as he was, its wood etched with bright gold lines that seemed to pulse with a mysterious power. He still had no idea what the bowstring was made of; it was as thin as a human hair but impossibly flexible and sharp enough to have sliced Brianna's finger when she’d barely touched it.

“A semi-divine weapon,” Zane mused, turning it over in his hands. “Too bad it’s a bow.” He’d never used one in his life and had zero experience, but he knew a long-range weapon like this could work wonders in the right situation.

After a moment, he reached out and easily plucked the quiver from the automaton’s back.

Divine-class arrows! 

[MUSIC: TENSE DISCOVERY]

The realization hit him with a thrill. Even without any special abilities, the raw power they packed was terrifying. These things could kill a powerful Soul Sea Realm warrior in a single shot and punch straight through a giant shield made of Lodestone. Brianna had warned him that if one of these hit her, she wouldn’t survive either. Which meant he had to be damn careful.

“Too bad there are only a few,” he muttered, peering inside. He counted just eight black arrows. They were just like the Heavenly Thunder Halberd—incredibly powerful, but single-use. He only had eight shots.

But the moment his hand closed around the quiver, something shifted. 

[SFX: MAGICAL RESONANCE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

His eyes widened as he felt an aura radiating from it—the unmistakable signature of Spatial Law.

A Spatial Sanctum?

Normally, a sanctum would only recognize its master, but its owner had been a mindless automaton. Wiping its mark would be easy. Zane focused his will, effortlessly erasing the faint impression left on the quiver, and then sent his consciousness inside to explore. A slow grin spread across his face.

It wasn't eight arrows. Or even eighty.

There were hundreds.

And they weren't all black. He found two other types tucked away. One was blood-red, with a tiny, ghoulish face embedded in the arrowhead. The other was a deep, vibrant green that seemed to pulse with life. He could only imagine what they did.

He stowed the bow and quiver in his Storage Ring.

[SFX: DOUBLE WHOOSH]

As he was taking stock of his new arsenal, a pair of arrows trailing blue light shot toward him.

Their aim was sloppy. Zane dodged nimbly, landing right back where he started. The two arrows, both tipped with Dragon Arsenic, missed him completely. 

War King Wind's voice called out from inside the shield box. “Open it up — let him back in!”

Zane waved him off. “Keep moving. I'll fly overhead.”

With Brianna's body, the ordinary blue arrows were no threat. Only the Dragon-Blooded Archer's black shafts could have hurt him — and that problem was solved. There was no reason to squeeze back into the box.

[AMBIENT: VAST OCEAN WIND]

[MUSIC: EPIC DISCOVERY]

So the formation pressed forward without him, the shields still catching the rain of blue arrows but moving faster now. Zane rose high above the Path of Heroes, high enough that the dragon statues' volleys couldn't reach him. From up there, he could finally see the full length of the path stretching out below — and at its far end, something extraordinary.

[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]

The sea simply stopped.

Where the Path of Heroes ended, the golden water halted as if an invisible wall held it back. Not a drop flowed past. Beyond that threshold was a vast, circular abyss dropping away into darkness — so deep that even from ten thousand feet up, Zane couldn't see the bottom.

He kept watch from the air as the box inched forward. No new Dragon-Blooded Archers rose from the sea. Apparently, there had only been one. As the group entered the second half of the path, the arrow volleys began to thin. By the final fifth, the Soul Sea masters stowed their shields entirely and broke into a full sprint.

[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]

[AMBIENT: CASCADING WATER]

[SFX: RUSHING GOLDEN LIQUID]

In moments, they reached a fan-shaped platform at the path's end. The golden sea surged up to its edges and then bent, flowing downward at a perfect ninety degrees, cascading along the walls of the abyss like a curtain of liquid gold.

“How do we get down?” Zane asked.

War King Wind smiled faintly. “Follow me.”

He walked calmly to the edge of the platform and stepped off.

He didn't fall.

[SFX: GRAVITY SHIFT]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WONDER]

He simply stood on the vertical face of the path below, as if the world had quietly rotated around him. One by one, the others followed, each stepping off and finding their footing on what should have been a sheer drop. When Zane took his own step, he felt it — a sudden, complete shift in gravity, reorienting itself around him the moment his foot left the platform.

He looked around. Golden seawater flowed above his head, to his left, to his right — in every direction but the road beneath his feet. He was standing inside a vast cylindrical abyss, the Path of Heroes now a vertical road leading down into the heart of it.

“The Dragon-Blooded were geniuses,” Zane breathed, shaking his head in amazement. “They managed to alter the world’s gravity to build their sacred core down here.” No wonder the sea was flowing upside down.

Hearing his quiet awe, the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom offered a small smile. “The Heavenly Stellar Secret Land has many wonders, but it is equally filled with dangers. We must be careful. Once we reach the main site, you follow orders. No reckless actions.”

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT RUINS]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Zane nodded, his gaze already fixed on the distance. The road snaked forward, leading toward a huge, ancient city looming in the haze. So this was it. The Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, once the heart of the Dragon-Blooded, was now just a treasure-filled ruin for the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms to plunder.

[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]

[AMBIENT: VAST UNDERGROUND SPACE]

For now, the path ahead seemed clear. Zane fell in with the other Soul Sea Realm warriors, and they all followed the road toward the city. The fifty-or-so-mile journey was a quick five-minute sprint for fighters of their caliber. They soon arrived at the end of the road, where a massive, arched gateway formed by a dragon statue marked the entrance.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

[SFX: LOW RUMBLE]

This was the way into the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. But as the crowd drew closer, Zane noticed the dragon carving high above had an eerie red glow in its stone eyes, watching them all approach. 

Getting through this gate wasn’t going to be that simple.
