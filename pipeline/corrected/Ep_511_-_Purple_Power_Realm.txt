Against most Inner-Core Realm warriors, this uncanny ability granted him an overwhelming advantage. They simply couldn't track him, couldn't predict where he'd emerge next. But against Marilyn, trapped inside her Violet Power Domain, the technique proved worthless.
His attempts grew increasingly desperate. He couldn't even touch her. Each time he materialized for a strike—blade gleaming, muscles coiled—she dissolved into violet smoke, her sharp, clear laughter echoing through the arena like wind chimes in a storm.
