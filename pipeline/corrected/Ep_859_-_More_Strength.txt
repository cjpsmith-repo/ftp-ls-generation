Ep 859 - More Strength

Word count: 1143

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL FOREST]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Zane stared down through the massive leaf beneath his feet, his mind racing. Just below the surface, a cluster of round, translucent Ancient Heartstones glimmered, and he was trying to figure out how in the world he was supposed to get them out.

Just give it a shot, he told himself.

[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]

Taking two steps back, he crouched, gathering his Vitality into his legs. Power from the Dragon Scales flooded his body, a constant, thrumming current he no longer found difficult to handle. With his body now ridiculously strong, the strain was nothing like it used to be. He drew on every last ounce of strength he had, then closed his eyes and opened a channel to the stars.

[SFX: CELESTIAL FLASH]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

“Twin Stars—” he muttered, and the sky above the Feather Emperor’s Secret Land flashed as two brilliant stars answered his call. Their immense power surged into him.

“—Brilliance!” he yelled, launching himself off the leaf.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

The branch’s strange, localized gravity didn’t affect him here, so he shot ten feet into the air, twisted into a quick flip, and dove straight down. He aimed his fist, now charged with incredible momentum, at the giant leaf below.

This single punch contained every bit of power he could muster, amplified by his Stellar Body. It was infinitely stronger than the one he’d used to injure War Emperor York.

[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACT]

[AMBIENT: GIANT TREE]

The impact echoed like a thunderclap striking the ground, a sound so violent that anyone else on the giant tree would have heard it clearly.

[SFX: INSECT WINGS FLUTTERING]

Beneath the leaf, a handful of terrified Stone-Eater Bugs scattered, spreading their wings and fleeing in a panic.

Zane’s fist remained pressed against the leaf.

[SFX: BLOOD DRIPPING]

Streaks of blood seeped from between his knuckles, trickling down the massive green surface. Even with a body as tough as a Relic-Class weapon, the leaf was just tougher. The punch had felt like smashing an egg against a rock; the leaf didn’t even have a crack, but his hand throbbed with excruciating pain.

These top three layers are even tougher than the golden leaf below, he thought, not particularly surprised. He’d expected as much. No wonder even the Soul Sea Realm masters couldn’t get these things out.

[MUSIC: TENSE REALIZATION]

The pickaxe was the only answer.

If he could just get his hands on the Black Star pickaxe, he was sure he could dig the heartstones out. But the pickaxe itself was wedged deep inside the golden leaf. No one from the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms had managed to free it, and Zane knew it would be a nearly impossible task for him, too.

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

He dropped from the forty-fifth floor, falling fast. On the forty-first floor, two purple Stone-Eater Bugs clocked him as he passed — they spread their wings and gave chase. But he was falling too fast. After a few seconds, they gave up and returned to their leaves.

His mind kept turning as he fell. The golden leaf was far softer than the top three floors — meaning the Black Star Pickax was still his only real path. He’d thought about whether multiple warriors attacking the same leaf at once could work. But Randy had already answered that: only one warrior could land on a leaf per door. Anyone else who tried the same door got squeezed out automatically before they could land. The mine’s rules made a coordinated assault impossible.

Every option led back to the same place. Strength. More strength.

[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]

Zane stood at the foot of the pyramid and stared up at its peak. He didn’t move for a long moment. The word repeated in his mind like a pulse.

Strength. Strength. More strength.

[SFX: GRUMBLING CROWD]

Then, a dozen warriors came trudging back around the left side of the pyramid, muttering as they went.

“What a waste of time. The arsenal’s been picked clean for years. I found two low-grade Mythic weapons after searching for hours.”

“Might as well come back and mine. At least the bugs might have cleared out.”

Zane watched them go. Then something clicked.

[MUSIC: REVELATION THEME]

[SFX: REALIZATION SOUND]

Weapons. Cursed weapons — millions of them. Useless for fighting. But as long as a weapon wasn’t physically destroyed, the Celestial Essence inside it didn’t dissipate. It stayed locked in the metal.

He could refine it out.

His heart started pounding. Before, his body couldn’t have handled it — a thousand Dragon Scales had already pushed his previous body to its limit. But he’d broken through since then. His current body could take far more. Five thousand Dragon Scales. Ten thousand.

With that kind of power, the golden leaf wouldn’t be a problem. The Black Star Pickax would come free. And after that—

He stopped himself. One question first: did the curse on the weapons destroy the Celestial Essence, or just lock the weapon itself? He wouldn’t know until he looked.

There was only one way to find out.

Camilla looked up from her pile of ores. “You might find one or two Relic-Class weapons at most in that cursed arsenal. You’re better off just mining here.”

She was right that the weapons weren’t the point. If he sold the mountain of Heartstones piled up next to her, he’d have enough to buy five or six Relic-Class weapons — far more valuable than anything he’d find in that trash heap.

Randy crossed his arms. “With your strength, there’s no reason to do something that reckless.” But even as he said it, he had a feeling Zane wasn’t going there for the weapons.

“It’s fine. I’m just curious,” Zane said with a smile. “I’ll come back and mine later!”

Just as the words left his mouth, he looked up and met Fiona’s gaze. He’d been busy, but he was always aware of his surroundings, and he knew she’d been watching him closely.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

As their eyes met, she gave him a soft, gentle smile and jumped from the side of the pyramid. The oppressive power of blood sacrifice prevented anyone from flying here, so she wrapped herself in a fiery red barrier of Vitality, gliding the few dozen feet down to land neatly in front of him.

“Mind if I come with you?” she asked. “I’d like to see it for myself.”

Her goal in this place wasn’t treasure or heartstones. She had come purely for the experience.

Zane smiled and nodded.

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT RUINS]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

He turned and led the way, heading around the pyramid and down the straight path to its left. 

As they walked, a few saint-level geniuses were returning from the abandoned arsenal, and they stared at Zane and Fiona with open curiosity as they passed.
