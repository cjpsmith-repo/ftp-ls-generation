[AMBIENT: OBSERVATION CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

As the Skull Ghost Hand hovered over Zane with lethal intent, the elders watched in silent dread, certain they were about to witness his obliteration.

"Stoney's gonna skin him alive," the old man said, shaking his head. "If he uses that Skull Ghost Hand, Zane won't even have time to blink, let alone dodge."

Confusion flickered in Michael Nash’s eyes. He couldn't wrap his head around Zane's confidence. The kid was just standing there, letting Stoney wind up a spirit attack. And the craziest part? He wasn't even trying to move.

Zane had been playing it so smart up to this point. If he’d just kept using that sword spirit to protect his tower and poach points from the other fighters, his tower would be miles ahead of anyone else’s. The whole trial would’ve been his for the taking.

"He's just getting cocky," Michael Nash sighed, shaking his head at the kid's recklessness. "If that thing kills him, he gets booted right out of the magic array. All for nothing."

The examiner said nothing, his expression grim. He was already convinced Zane had cheated somehow. If the kid got himself eliminated now, it would just be the system correcting an anomaly.

The elders of the World Merchants' Guild leaned forward, their expressions taut with anticipation.

[SFX: PAINFUL SCREAM]

A scream ripped through the air, raw and agonized.

But to everyone’s shock, it wasn’t Zane.

It was Stoney.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

The black markings that had crawled across Stoney's face like living shadows were dissolving, retreating as though burned away by invisible fire. Color fled from his skin, leaving him deathly pale. His features—once handsome and confident—contorted into something grotesque, a twisted mask of agony and impotent fury. His eyes bulged, bloodshot and wild, as tremors wracked his frame.

Zane stood before him, utterly untouched. Not a scratch marred his skin. Not a hair displaced. He might have been carved from stone himself for all the concern he showed.

[SFX: SWORD SLASH]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT THEME]

Then Zane simply turned away. He dismissed Stoney as one might dismiss a failed experiment—with neither triumph nor contempt, just complete indifference. His sword sang through the air as he returned to his work, each strike reducing another Man of Basalt to rubble and dust. The rhythm of destruction resumed as though the confrontation had been nothing more than a brief interruption.

[MUSIC: STRANGE DISCOVERY]

“What in the world just happened?” Michael Nash’s jaw went slack. He stared in disbelief as Zane dismantled the basalt men, one after another. With every flick of his sword, another stone figure exploded. Watching the kid's fluid, almost effortless movements, Michael was floored by the fighter from the Soul Realm.

The old man and the examiner were just as dumbfounded. None of them could begin to fathom how Zane had so completely dismantled Stoney in the blink of an eye, leaving him in such a pitiful state.

[SFX: AGONIZED SCREAM]

“Ahhhhhh!”

Stoney’s agonized scream seemed to stretch on forever.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SPIRITUAL ENERGY]

The Skull Ghost Hand was an attack forged from Stoney's own soul—a spiritual assault rooted in the Discipline of Vile Malice, not unlike how Zane could manifest his own soul as a Psyche Spike.

Against any other fighter in the trial, it would have been a decisive blow. The technique was designed to shred an opponent's consciousness from within, leaving them a hollow shell. No ordinary cultivator possessed the spiritual fortitude to handle an attack like that.

But Stoney had made a fatal mistake in targeting Zane.

Zane's soul had already ascended to the Fighting Soul Realm—a transformation that placed him in an entirely different category of existence. Where Stoney's soul remained tethered to mortal limitations, Zane's had been tempered and refined into something approaching the divine.

The moment Stoney's attack touched Zane's consciousness, it encountered an immovable fortress. His Fighting Soul didn't just resist the invasion—it crushed it, shattering the spectral hand like glass against stone. The backlash tore through Stoney's own spirit with devastating force.

Zane felt a flicker of pity. Stoney had gambled everything on a technique that should have worked. In another circumstance, against another opponent, it would have secured victory. But here, the gap between their spiritual cultivation was just too large.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

"Weird," Zane grumbled to himself. "Could've sworn someone was watching me." He gave his head a little shake, pushing the thought aside as he walked over to his tower.

“Over thirty-six thousand points!” A satisfied grin spread across Zane's face as he read the score.

“Upgrade!” Zane commanded, spending the thirty-three thousand points needed.

[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

The instant he gave the order, his tower was engulfed in a brilliant red light. And as soon as the first light faded, a second one flared to life.

Two levels in a row.

Just like that, Zane’s tower was now a full level higher than any other fighter’s.

[AMBIENT: WIND BLOWING]

[SFX: FLAGS FLAPPING]

The newly upgraded tower was wider, now surrounded by a gleaming silver-white fence. Four flags, each bearing the symbol of the World Merchants' Guild, snapped in the wind from its corners. Up top, six archers now stood guard.

Their gear had changed dramatically—the bows in their hands radiated a fiery aura, and the arrows in their quivers were inscribed with glowing fire runes. Zane could feel the power thrumming from them; he was certain each archer was as strong as a fighter from the Soul Realm. He couldn't help but wonder just how powerful this tower could get if he kept going.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

The fifth wave of beasts had taken a heavy toll, causing a sharp jump in eliminations.

Only a few fighters had been knocked out during the third wave, but the fourth had claimed more than twenty. The fifth had wiped out another forty. The numbers were climbing fast.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

And with the sixth wave on its way, there was no telling how many would survive.
