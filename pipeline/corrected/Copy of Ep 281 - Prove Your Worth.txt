Ep 281 - Prove Your Worth
Word Count: 1189
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
The Monastic Path was unrivaled, shrouded in secrecy but commanding undeniable power that few dared challenge. Its mysterious techniques inspired both awe and fear among cultivators across the realms. The Skyreach Sect’s enormous Celestial Library didn’t have a single book with information about these methods, leaving everyone to wonder where Patrick had learned such formidable skills.
Patrick had confidently promised to defeat his opponent in merely three moves, but so far he’d only executed one. Twice, he’d blocked Wynn’s attacks with an unbreakable defense forged from pure Vitality, the golden barrier shimmering with otherworldly energy.
The spectators now watched with bated breath as Patrick’s expression darkened with concentration, preparing for his devastating counterattack—a technique he called The Eternal Cycle.
According to ancient texts, The Eternal Cycle was the final destination for souls after death. Rather than simply vanishing into nothingness, these souls were sent through Six distinct Realms of existence. The three good realms—Heavenly, Demigod, and Human—offered peace and enlightenment, while the three evil realms—Animal, Ghost, and Hellish—promised suffering and torment.
As Patrick prepared to unleash this cosmic force, the very air in the arena seemed to grow heavy with anticipation, pressing down upon all who watched with wide, disbelieving eyes.
[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]
Suddenly, six small golden orbs appeared around Patrick. Each one pulsed with a bright, golden light and was engraved with a different character representing one of the Six Realms.
Patrick hadn’t mastered the technique yet; that's why the orbs were in this state. If he’d reached a higher level of The Eternal Cycle, each orb would contain a miniature world, much like the space inside a Storage Ring.
But where a Storage Ring could only hold inanimate objects, these six small orbs could contain actual, living worlds. If he ever got to the point where he could create six worlds inside them, Patrick's power would be off the charts. Even a powerhouse like Kenneth would probably go down in seconds.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY]
[SFX: WHIRLING AIR]
The golden orbs materialized and immediately began to spin faster and faster, creating an ethereal vortex in the air before Patrick’s composed figure. His attunement method appeared deceptively simple—The Eternal Cycle manifested merely as a tranquil, unassuming whirlpool that hid its devastating potential.
Patrick’s eyes reflected the golden light as he maintained perfect concentration, his breathing steady despite the immense energy coursing through his body. This was the essence of monastic cultivation; it presented itself with subtle elegance rather than ostentatious display, but seasoned warriors recognized the deadly force contained within its graceful form.
[SFX: MACHETE SWING]
"All flash, no substance! I’ll shatter it!" Wynn roared, swinging his prized machete to break the vortex.
The Reaper’s Chop constituted Wynn’s ultimate move, one he had deliberately withheld during previous matches, simply because he thought his previous opponents were unworthy of its lethal artistry. Though brimming with arrogance, Wynn also had acute tactical awareness. He recognized how powerful Patrick was—after all, one doesn’t rise to second position among the Skyreach Sect through luck.
A cold calculation flickered behind Wynn’s eyes as he channeled his entire essence into this attack. The old saying crossed his mind: even a lion goes all out to catch a rabbit. Patrick’s abilities were in another league entirely, and Wynn couldn’t afford to hold anything back. The familiar weight of his weapon felt different now—heavier with purpose, alive with potential.
"This one strike," Wynn thought with grim determination, "will show them all what Skytop Peak truly represents."
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY BLADE]
He condensed his cutter intent into a gray blade of light, a force far more potent than one made from Vitality. The first time Wynn had perfected this move, he'd used a single swing of his Reaper’s Chop to slice a two-hundred-foot mountain clean in half.
The machete was a top-tier weapon. A master warrior could effortlessly split mountains and crack the earth.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
As the fierce cutter intent flared to life, the members of the Sect’s Chosen, who had been cheering for Patrick, suddenly looked uneasy.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACT]
Wynn’s gray blade of light sliced through the air with terrifying precision, cleaving Patrick’s golden orbs as they attempted to intercept. The crowd gasped as Patrick’s confident expression morphed into shock. His incomplete Eternal Cycle technique, despite its formidable appearance, crumbled against Wynn’s perfected Reaper’s Chop.
[SFX: BARRIER SHATTERING]
The blade continued its arc, connecting with Patrick’s hastily erected barrier and shattering it completely. Patrick stumbled backward, blood trickling from his mouth, his robes torn and smoking. He raised a trembling hand in surrender as Wynn stood tall, machete gleaming in the arena light.
Wynn's gray blade of light tore into the vortex — slicing through the outermost layers like a blade through silk. Wynn sneered. He could feel himself winning.
Then the advance slowed. Then stopped. His cutter intent hung suspended in the vortex's heart, seized.
He couldn't pull it back.
[SFX: VORTEX REVERSAL — A LOW, RISING HUM]
Patrick extended one finger. The vortex reversed. So did everything inside it.
Wynn knew his own technique better than anyone alive — and what came screaming back at him was stronger than anything he'd thrown. He spun to run. He wasn't fast enough.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT — BARRIER WALL CRACK]
Wynn raised his machete in a desperate block —
The reflected Reaper's Chop hit him like a collapsing mountain. The force blasted him backward into the arena's barrier wall. He struck it hard and slid slowly down its surface, leaving a smear of blood on the shimmering light curtain. Some of the wounds were deep enough to show bone.
Patrick turned away. "Three moves," he said quietly. "More than enough."
[SFX: CROWD ERUPTION]
The Sect's Chosen erupted. The disciples of the Thirty-Three Peaks went very quiet.
"What are you all so quiet about now?" one of them shot back. "You guys only have Patrick! We’ve got Finn, Zane, and a whole stable of other geniuses. We still have a shot at winning the All Peaks Competition this year!"
"Zane? The kid who’s only at the Second Level of the Adept Realm? And Finn, who’s just at the consummation of the Adept Realm?" a member of the Sect’s Chosen sneered. "Get real! You’ve only got those two, and neither of them could touch Patrick! You guys really need to stop daydreaming."
The insults started flying between the Thirty-Three Peaks and the Sect’s Chosen, and the trash talk quickly exploded into a shouting match. The elders and the referees from the Skyreach Sect just let them go at it.
A true warrior had to have a fiery spirit. The entire Skyreach Sect was like one big fighting pit, where disciples were encouraged to clash. The last one standing would be nurtured into the most talented and powerful warrior of their generation.
But these were people, not animals. The fights within the Sect weren’t meant to be a bloodbath. The rules were designed to stoke the disciples’ fire and push them to unlock their true potential.
[SFX: CRUSHING POWER WAVE]
The arguments grew more intense, and just as a full-on brawl was about to erupt, two elders descended from the sky. They unleashed two waves of crushing power that instantly silenced the entire crowd.
"That’s enough! You prove your strength in the arena, not with your mouths!"
The uproar in the audience finally died down. But now, both the Sect’s Chosen and the disciples from the Thirty-Three Peaks were fired up, determined to earn their pride in the next rounds.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: BATTLE ANTICIPATION]
The Referee finally announced the next match. "Zane from Misty Peak versus Tracy of the Sect’s Chosen."
Tracy smiled to herself. Here we go.
