"You're pretty tough for a Core Strength kid," Jace sneered. "But you're still gonna die here today. And when you do, I'll find a use for that body of yours to make up for my losses!"
With that, he lunged forward, transforming into a mere blur of shadows.
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
Slice!
[SFX: FLESH CUTTING]
The sword carved into Zane's flesh, opening another gash across his skin. Blood erupted from the wound a heartbeat later, warm and sticky as it trickled down his torso.
Each successive attack left another vicious cut on Zane's trembling body. The metallic scent of his own blood filled his nostrils as desperation crept into his mind.
Though his strength was formidable, he couldn't match Jace's lightning speed. The weaponsmith danced around him like a demon, each strike precise and merciless. At this pace, Zane recognized the grim truth—he faced certain death.
His breath came in ragged gasps as his mind raced through dwindling options. There remained only one possibility, one desperate gambit that might save him.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
He had to take a risk. A monumental one.
All at once, Zane's eyes fluttered shut, his arms falling limp at his sides as if he'd just given up. If he couldn't even touch Jace, what was the point in trying?
"Giving up so soon, kid?" Jace cackled.
As a seasoned fighter, Jace felt a flicker of suspicion at Zane's sudden surrender.
[SFX: SLASHING CUTS]
But he wasn't about to let his guard down for a second. Without hesitating, he kept slashing, his blade a silver streak that opened two more gashes on Zane's body.
"Heh heh, let's see how much more punishment that body can endure!" Jace's piercing laugh ricocheted off the cave walls, sending chills down Zane's spine as the weaponsmith lunged forward with predatory grace.
Initially feinting a frontal slash, Jace abruptly shifted his trajectory, transforming into a blur of motion as he zipped behind his prey.
[SFX: FIRE IGNITION]
His hands erupted in brilliant Bloomfire, the flames casting ominous shadows across his gaunt face. With another shrill laugh that betrayed his growing bloodlust, Jace propelled himself toward Zane's unprotected back.
This time, the weaponsmith aimed for Zane's head, intending to rip it clean off and end this troublesome encounter. The air crackled with deadly energy as he closed the distance.
[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]
"Now!" Zane's mind screamed, adrenaline flooding his system.
Just as Jace hovered mere inches away, Zane's eyes snapped open, alert and focused.
He had patiently awaited this precise moment, counting on his opponent's arrogance.
Before Jace could deliver his killing blow, Zane pivoted and threw his arms up in a defensive maneuver that was anything but passive.
[SFX: HARD BLOW]
The instant Jace's flaming hands made contact, Zane's own hands clamped down on the weaponsmith's skinny arms, his grip tightening like a steel vise forged in determination.
"Got you!" Zane roared, satisfaction and fury mingling in his thunderous voice.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
[SFX: STONE FLOOR SHATTERING]
He slammed Jace into the ground with every ounce of his strength. The hard stone floor couldn't take the impact, shattering under the force and leaving Jace dazed in the middle of a huge crater.
Jace shook his head, trying to get his bearings as he scrambled to climb out. Zane wasn't about to give him the chance.
"This man is beyond reason—powerful and lightning-fast," Zane thought, his heart hammering against his ribs. "If he escapes that crater, I'll never have another chance to subdue him."
Blood trickled from his wounds as he stared down at his trapped opponent. "Fortune favors the bold but rarely offers second chances," he whispered through gritted teeth, his voice hoarse with exhaustion and determination.
[MUSIC: FRANTIC COMBAT]
[SFX: SERIES OF STRIKES]
With the feral instinct of someone fighting for survival, Zane leapt into the crater and unleashed a furious barrage of strikes—each punch flowing into the next with desperate precision.
His fists connected with devastating impact, each blow carrying the weight of two years of suppressed rage and newfound power.
[SFX: BONE CRUSHING IMPACTS]
The sound of bone and flesh yielding beneath his knuckles echoed through the cavern, a grim percussion to his labored breathing.
When finally his arms burned with exertion, Zane paused, drawing ragged breaths into his aching lungs.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE AFTERMATH]
[MUSIC: SOMBER VICTORY]
He swiped a trembling hand across his sweat-drenched brow, leaving a smear of blood—both his and Jace's—across his forehead. Looking down at the weaponsmith's motionless form amid the shattered stone, Zane felt a cold, hollow sensation spreading through his chest despite his victory.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT PAUSE]
He wasn't breathing.
Did he really just kill someone halfway to the Nature Level? The thought was staggering. Zane was only at the Core Strength Level.
Of course, Jace had burned through most of his energy after a full day and night of Weapon Forging.
Still... if he'd been a split-second slower, Jace would've snapped his neck. He'd be the one lying dead. Life was so damn unpredictable.
It took Zane a moment to let it all sink in. He'd just beaten a notorious master with his bare hands. After a grim moment of satisfaction, he forced himself to move, needing to check on the eight people chained to the forge.
[SFX: LABORED FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: CRACKLING EMBERS]
As he got closer, he saw they were already gone. It looked like all the flesh and blood had been cooked from their bones. Jace's fire had burned them to death. The sight was so gruesome Zane had to squeeze his eyes shut and turn away.
"You poor bastards," he murmured.
After pulling himself together, Zane started searching the huge cave. He'd already looked twice and found nothing but broken copper and scrap iron. Standing in the middle of the cavern, he frowned.
An average Master Weaponsmith was rich; an established one like Jace couldn't be this broke. Treasure must be concealed somewhere in this place.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
After several more minutes of futile exploration, inspiration struck Zane. He hastened back to the crater and extracted Jace's remains.
The body lay before him—a grotesque, pulverized mass barely identifiable as human. Zane's gaze methodically traversed the corpse until it fixed upon the right hand, where a black ring adorned the middle finger.
"Could it be?" he whispered, his heart quickening with anticipation.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY FLOW]
As his trembling fingers made contact with the cold metal, an unusual sensation—like liquid ice—coursed through his hand and up his arm. The feeling wasn't unpleasant, merely foreign, as if the ring itself were acknowledging his touch.
"Just as I thought," Zane murmured, a grim smile playing across his lips as vindication replaced his earlier frustration. "A Storage Ring."
