Ep 852 - Hitching a Ride

Word count: 1120

[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

The narrow door closed behind Zane, and the thirtieth floor dropped away beneath his feet.

His body was a missile aimed at the giant leaf below. He plummeted, the distance closing in a blur—a thousand feet, five hundred, then one hundred. His eyes were locked on the huge bug gorging itself on the ores embedded in the leaf’s surface. As he fell, power coiled in his fists, ready to be unleashed.

[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]

“Die!” Zane roared, channeling the full, bone-shattering force of his descent into a single punch. He was desperate for the Ancient Heartstones, so desperate he’d kill this thing for even a sliver of one. As he struck, he activated the power of five hundred Dragon Scales, his arm glowing with energy.

He aimed straight for the creature’s back, but just as the blow was about to land, it twitched. With a powerful stomp of its hind legs, the bug flipped sideways, completely evading his attack.

[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]

Zane’s fist slammed into the leaf instead, sending a violent tremor through the massive plant and its stem.

[SFX: INSECT SQUEAKING]

The bug landed twenty feet away and waved its sharp forelegs, as if taunting him. Zane glanced down. His punch had spiderwebbed the leaf with cracks, but a quick scan of the area revealed no sign of any Ancient Heartstones.

With a body as tough as a Relic-Class weapon, Zane knew he could smash through these leaves without a pickax if he had to. The bug was fast, sure, but he was faster. 

“You want to provoke me?” he growled, sinking into a low crouch. A flicker of determination hardened his gaze.

The bug continued its squeaking, waving its forelegs in a clear gesture of mockery. That was all the invitation Zane needed.

[MUSIC: ACTION PERCUSSION]

He exploded forward, crossing the distance in an instant with his arms outstretched, ready to snatch it from the leaf.

But the thing was impossibly agile, quicker than even some Life-and-Death Realm warriors. Its danger sense was purely instinctual. Before Zane’s hands could close around it, the bug’s powerful sense of smell caught his scent.

[SFX: INSECT WINGS BUZZING]

It spread its wings, preparing to launch itself off the leaf and into the open air.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

“You’re not going anywhere!” Zane shouted, unleashing a final burst of energy that shot him forward another two feet. He launched himself completely off the leaf, and for a heart-stopping moment, gravity took hold. But before he could fall, his fingers closed around the massive bug, snatching it right out of the air.

[SFX: INSECT SQUEALING]

The bug thrashed violently, stamping and kicking, but Zane’s grip was iron. It couldn’t shake him — but it couldn’t stop falling either. Half the gravitational force dragging Zane down was now pulling on the bug too. 

It beat its wings frantically, fighting to stay airborne, and for a few chaotic seconds, it managed it — the two of them spiraling wildly around the massive trunk, the bug zigzagging left and right as it struggled to climb, Zane hanging from its legs like deadweight.

[SFX: WIND RUSHING]

[SFX: WINGS BEATING FRANTICALLY]

Up on the twenty-seventh floor, Randy had been working a leaf of his own when a shape flashed past him — then circled back. He blinked. Nothing was supposed to move like that in here. Even a Soul Sea master couldn’t fly against the mine’s gravitational rules. He dropped his pickax and ran to the edge of the leaf.

His jaw dropped. “Brother — Brother Jones…”

Zane was down there, dangling from a giant green bug, the two of them corkscrewing around the tree in a widening gyre.

He wasn’t the only one staring. Two-thirds of the warriors on the leaves had stopped mining entirely.

“Is that Zane? Why is he — why does he have a Stone-Eater Bug?”

“How is he even holding onto it? That thing is faster than a Life-and-Death warrior!”

“I heard only Soul Sea masters have ever caught one of those…”

Under the eyes of the entire mine, Zane and the bug dropped through the circle of light at the base of the tree and vanished.

[AMBIENT: OPEN AIR]

[MUSIC: CHAOTIC ACTION]

The moment they cleared the gravitational field, the bug surged upward, wings hammering the air, and shot straight for the sky — dragging Zane with it.

Zane let it climb. Then he twisted his body hard at the waist, swung his legs up, and hauled himself onto the creature’s back.

[SFX: CARAPACE CRACKING]

The bug’s forelegs snapped back instantly, stabbing at him with both spear-points at once.

“Not a chance.”

Zane swayed, letting both strikes pass wide, then brought the edge of his palm down hard across the creature’s back. The carapace split. He hit it again and the wings tore free.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

Without its wings, the bug dropped like a stone.

[SFX: CRUNCH]

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

Just as they were about to hit the ground, Zane crushed its head. The exoskeleton was surprisingly tough, and even without a head, its six legs thrashed wildly for a few moments before finally going still.

Zane dusted himself off and hopped down from the creature’s carcass.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

Camilla was already running toward him, a pile of ores clutched in a makeshift bundle she’d made from her shirt. She’d been in the middle of negotiating with a warrior she was fairly sure had at least ten Ancient Heartstones hidden in his Storage Ring — but that could wait.

“Zane,” she gasped, her eyes wide with shock, “how in the world did you catch a Stone-Eater Bug?”

“Oh, so that’s what this is called?” Zane asked, nudging the bug with his boot.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]

Just then, Lord Randy hurried over from the safety of the glowing ring, his face a mixture of relief and astonishment when he saw the dead creature. It wasn’t just him, either. The commotion had drawn the other prospectors out from the circle of light, and they all hurried over to crowd around Zane.

Lord Randy let out a booming laugh at Zane’s question. “That’s right! It’s a Stone-Eater Bug. They’re the ones that eat the ores right out of the tree.”

Zane watched the so-called saint-level geniuses gathering around, a puzzled look on his face. What’s the big deal? he thought. Sure, there are Ancient Heartstones in there — but that can’t be all they’re worked up about.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TONE]

Before he could voice his confusion, someone in the crowd spoke up, their tone grim. “I wouldn’t touch that thing if I were you, Zane. You’ll end up dead for sure.”
