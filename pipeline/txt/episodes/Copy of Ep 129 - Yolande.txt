[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
[SFX: FLESH TEARING]
The rival Apex Dragon savagely dismembered the Shadow Dragon, its claws tearing through ethereal flesh with merciless precision.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TRANSFORMATION]
As the shadowy creature dissolved into nothingness, something unexpected occurred—the Nether Essence, rather than dissipating, surged forth like a malevolent tide and engulfed the golden dragon.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
A remarkable transformation unfolded before their eyes. The magnificent golden scales darkened, first to bronze, then to obsidian, as if consumed by an inner corruption. Within a single heartbeat, the majestic creature morphed into something far more sinister. Its form expanded, muscles bulging with newfound power, eyes gleaming with predatory intelligence. Where once stood a noble beast, now loomed a nightmarish predator, radiating malevolence and raw, untamed strength.
Zane observed the metamorphosis with quiet satisfaction, a slight curve forming at the corner of his lips. The pain of his earlier wounds seemed distant now as he savored this unexpected victory.
"You took a loss," he remarked softly, his voice carrying across the battlefield with surprising clarity. "Don't let it define you—though I doubt you'll have much choice in the matter now."
The imperial general could only stare, stunned by the transformation. "How...?" he stammered. "How is that possible?"
Watching from a distance, the students of the Skyreach Sect were speechless.
[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT THEME]
Zane stood triumphant, Void Energy crackling around him like raw lightning.
[SFX: DRAGON SWOOPING]
The victorious dragon circled once in the sky, then shot toward the imperial general. The man’s face went white as the true scope of the dragon's power washed over him. In a blind panic, he couldn’t think of a single way to save himself.
[SFX: MAGICAL SUMMONING]
Then, desperately, he summoned another golden dragon into the air above his hand.
[AMBIENT: AWESTRUCK CROWD]
The students of the Skyreach Sect couldn't believe what they were seeing.
"That general is supposed to be a master from the Soul Realm, but what an idiot. Does he have a death wish?"
"He's a disgrace. I can't believe how stupid he is."
[SFX: DRAGON DEVOURING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE CRESCENDO]
Their assessment was spot on. Zane's enormous black dragon swallowed the new Apex Dragon whole, and in doing so, tripled in size. The general’s dragon had been massive—large enough to coil around a palace—and now Zane's was three times bigger.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
The colossal creature gleamed as it circled overhead, looking like it could shatter the world if it chose to descend.
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
With a simple wave of his hand, Zane gave the order. The dragon obeyed, rocketing toward the general like a cannonball, its roar a screech that tore through the air.
The general was frozen in place, a wave of regret crashing over him. He had defended Josef.
There were so many capable men in Silverspire—why did he have to back Josef? He never imagined he’d lose his life over one mistake. But he hadn't done anything wrong! The kid was just a High-level candidate, and he was a master of the Soul Realm. How could he possibly be outmatched by someone so much weaker?
He was trapped in a spiral of desperate, frustrated thoughts, terrified and indignant all at once.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER FORMING]
Just as the dragon dove, its jaws about to close around him, a barrier flashed into existence.
No matter how the Shadow Dragon clawed and bit at the barrier, it couldn't break through. Zane pushed more Vitality into it — nothing. Then he felt his reserves give out entirely. The dragon shuddered, darkened, and dissolved into nothing.
He exhaled slowly. Whoever had intervened, they were on a different level.

It was a translucent box, a ward so powerful it could shield a general of the imperial army. It was indestructible; the dragon could hammer against it for an eternity, and it wouldn't break.
[SFX: MAGICAL CONJURING]
Then a woman appeared — descending not by flight but by foot, transparent stairs materializing beneath her with each step. Her wooden clogs clattered against them as she walked down unhurried, her nine-colored dress swaying around her.
A murmur swept through the watching disciples.
"Is that— that's Yolande."
"The Maiden of Slaughter? Here?"
"I've never seen her in person. They say she has no feelings at all."
She looked younger than expected — thirteen, maybe fourteen, with round cheeks that still carried a child's softness. But her eyes were something else entirely: snow-white, shrouded in a pale mist, completely unreadable. No warmth, no hostility, no curiosity. Nothing.
Yolande waved a hand, and countless more boxes materialized in the sky, hurtling straight for Zane.
[SFX: IMPACT THUD]
He dodged one after another, his movements skillful and precise, but there were simply too many. He was driven back to the ground just as a box slammed down from above, trapping him.
[SFX: FIST HITTING STONE]
Zane ran a hand over the smooth, unyielding wall. Gritting his teeth, he slammed his fist into it. Nothing. It was completely indestructible. He remembered Josef’s shield of light; his punches had at least made that waver. This translucent prison didn't even tremble.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]
"I will break you," Zane vowed, his voice resonating with unwavering determination despite his confinement.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
Suddenly, over a hundred dragon scales flared to life in his mind, each one pulsing with ancient power. He drew a deep breath, feeling the weight of desperation pressing against his chest. With gritted teeth, he channeled their ferocious strength, summoning every ounce of his power into one devastating blow against the translucent prison. His muscles strained and veins bulged as the impact reverberated through his bones. Still, the box didn't move—not even the slightest tremor.
"Nether Essence. Devour!" he commanded, his eyes blazing with defiance.
[SFX: PRIMAL ROAR]
[AMBIENT: DARK ENERGY SWIRLING]
He unleashed the Nether Essence with a primal roar, willing the dark energy to consume the magical barrier that held him. The shadowy tendrils clawed frantically at the walls, seeking any weakness. But for the first time since discovering this overwhelming power, it failed completely, dissipating against the ward like smoke against glass. Cold dread settled in his stomach as his most reliable weapon proved useless.
"Shattered Dagger!" Zane called out in desperation, his hand trembling slightly with mounting fear.
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
Zane pulled the dagger from his Storage Ring. The razor-sharp blade, which could slice through almost anything, couldn't even leave a scratch.
[SFX: BOOTS CLATTERING]
Meanwhile, Yolande approached, the long, colorful fabric of her dress swaying as her work boots clattered against the ground. She stopped right in front of him.
"It's no use," she said calmly. "You can't break my Magic Ward."
"A Magic Ward? So this is an enchantment," Zane murmured, his brow furrowed in thought.
[SFX: MAGICAL CONJURING]
[AMBIENT: GLOWING LIGHT]
"Since you refused to select your demise, I shall decide for you," Yolande declared. With a graceful wave of her alabaster fingers, she conjured another Magic Ward—this one manifesting as a razor-thin blade of light. The radiance emanating from it seared Zane's vision as she began guiding it through the wall of his prison.
Zane froze, paralyzed as his breath seized in his lungs. The enchanted blade advanced inexorably toward his neck, its purpose unmistakable—one more inch and his head would be severed from his shoulders, his journey ending in a heartbeat.
