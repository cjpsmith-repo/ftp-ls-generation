Ep 460 - The Lodestone

[AMBIENT: SCORCHING HEAT]

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

[MUSIC: HIGH STAKES TRIAL]

The noon sun beat down mercilessly, scorching the earth and turning the air thick with heat.

Zane walked past groups of warriors, their confused stares and hushed whispers trailing in his wake. He tuned them out, refusing to let their judgment penetrate his focus. His eyes remained locked on the lodestone ahead.

He stopped before the massive stone, feeling its presence like a weight against his chest. The crowd's murmurs faded to background noise as he leaned in to examine it more closely.

[SFX: MYSTICAL PULSING ENERGY]

The lodestone pulsed with a strange energy, unlike anything he'd ever encountered. A faint tremor ran through his fingertips as he reached toward its surface—not quite touching, but close enough to feel the power radiating from within. Even the Law Power humming inside it was completely different from any he had studied. It felt ancient, layered, as though multiple forces had been compressed and bound together by some technique far beyond his current understanding.

Zane's heart quickened. This wasn't just another trial. This was an opportunity to glimpse something profound—something that might bridge the gap between where he stood now and where he needed to be.

The Original Laws were the most fundamental forces in the world, woven into the fabric of existence. Just as mountains and boulders held the Law of Earth, rogue flames contained the Law of Fire, and the vast emptiness of space was filled with the Law of Space. Everything followed a law—a specific path, a hidden rule.

But most ordinary people couldn't sense them, let alone understand or use them. It took a special kind of talent to comprehend the laws known to humanity.

This lodestone contained the power of seven Original Laws: Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth, Wind, and Electricity. All seven were woven together in perfect balance, giving Zane a very strange sensation.

The challenger before him had used the Wind Law to activate the same law stored inside the stone. That’s what had made the lodestone change color.

What if I used the combined power of all seven? The thought struck him out of nowhere, wild and audacious. He wasn’t sure where an idea like that had even come from.

He had no idea he wasn't the first to think of it.

The lodestone was a truly unique material, neither metal nor iron. It was so hard that no blade or spear could scratch its surface. Even a master in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm couldn’t chip it with a full-force strike. It was harder than diamond, possessing incredible strength and durability.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL MOUNTAIN WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]

This very stone was originally found on Sacred Mount Lodestone, deep within the Chaotic Ocean of Stars. It wasn’t discovered by humans; it had formed at the mountain’s peak and broken off after millions of years of natural weathering.

Some masters theorized that only someone who had mastered all seven of the mountain’s Original Laws could ever hope to process or break down the lodestone. For now, that was a feat impossible for any master in The Heartlands, making the stone unbelievably valuable.

[SFX: DEEP DRAGON RUMBLE]

“Combine seven laws? That’s a bold thought, kid,” the Cobalt Dragon’s voice rumbled in his mind. The ancient dragon had been mostly silent since devouring the Owl Beast's spirit, busy absorbing its Soul Power to recover his own strength.

“What do you mean? Is combining seven types of Law Power really that difficult?” Zane asked, puzzled by the dragon’s comment.

“Yes. Very. Besides, this lodestone is no ordinary rock.”

[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]

A powerful challenger could change the stone's color and make it last for about twenty seconds—an impressive feat. Weaker challengers, those below the ultimate level of the Soul Realm, might manage a few seconds, which was still enough to pass. The challenger who went before Zane had used his ultimate Soul Realm power to change the lodestone’s color for nine seconds. Even that was considered impressive.

But now, a new warrior is currently breaking the record.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

[SFX: CROWD GASP]

A challenger at only level three of the Soul Realm had just made the color last for more than thirty seconds.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

When the stone changed color, many were convinced it had finally broken. Thirty seconds passed, and it still hadn't reverted from red to its original green. The longer it glowed, the more suspicious the crowd became.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Forty seconds ticked by.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Then a full minute.

The lodestone remained a brilliant red, radiating heat and light with no sign of returning to its natural state.

[SFX: CROWD WHISPERS]

"I can't believe it… the lodestone actually broke!"

"Maybe it finally gave out after being punched so many times," someone ventured uncertainly.

"Punched broken? Are you kidding me?" another voice countered with sharp disbelief. "That thing fell from the sky onto a sacred mountain. No way a few fists could crack it. It's harder than diamond!"

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

The crowd pressed closer, their eyes fixed on the glowing stone. Whispers rippled through the gathered warriors, speculation mixing with awe. Zane stood motionless, his breathing steady despite the uncertainty gnawing at him. Had he truly damaged a sacred artifact? The implications would be catastrophic.

After a full five minutes—each second stretching like an eternity—the color finally began to shift. The deep red slowly faded, bleeding back into green like dawn reclaiming the night sky.

Relief washed through Zane, though he kept his expression neutral. He walked over to the mustached old man officiating the test, his steps measured and deliberate. "So, did I pass?"

The official stood frozen, staring at the lodestone as though it had spoken to him in a forgotten language. His weathered face remained blank, caught between wonder and disbelief. As an expert who had administered this test for decades, he understood far more than the warriors around him. To his immense relief, the lodestone bore no damage whatsoever—no cracks, no wear, no structural weakness. It was working perfectly. But the duration of that crimson glow... in all his years, he had never witnessed anything like it.

Zane's voice cut through the old man's thoughts, snapping him back to the present. He looked up, meeting the young cultivator's steady gaze. His expression still carried traces of shock, but professional duty reasserted itself. "Yes," he managed, his voice hoarse with amazement.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]

"You passed!"
