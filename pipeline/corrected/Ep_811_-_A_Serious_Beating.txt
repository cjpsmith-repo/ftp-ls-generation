Ep 811 - A Serious Beating

Word count: 1004

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Even though Zane knew plenty about space, he still couldn't find a way through the void.

Logan’s attacks were lightning-fast, and he would vanish just as quickly. Zane could barely sense his opponent, only managing to get a lock on him in the split second before a strike landed. For the most part, he was a sitting duck.

But that wasn't going to last. Zane had a plan. Since he could only pinpoint Logan’s location in that single moment before he attacked, he braced himself and waited.

Three, four, maybe five seconds crawled by. Then, a prickling sensation on his neck gave Logan’s position away. Zane didn't waste the opportunity. He deflected the strike and shoved Logan back toward the void, but he didn’t let him go alone.

[SFX: SPATIAL RIFT WHOOSH]

Zane slipped through the spatial rift right behind him.

That’s when Logan realized he’d been careless. In his confidence, he’d made the rift just a little too wide, and now Zane had followed him in.

[SFX: DISAPPEARING WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: CONFUSED CROWD MURMURS]

The two fighters vanished into thin air, leaving the crowd in the square completely baffled. Where did they go? Everyone just stared blankly at the sky, unsure of where to even look.

Only the powerful Soul Sea Realm masters and a handful of Life-and-Death Realm warriors kept their eyes trained on the empty space above, as if nothing strange had happened at all.

Seeing into the void required a special skill. The Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom, for instance, had eyes that glowed with a golden light, allowing him to see through anything. 

War King York had the Eyes of the Darkest Night, which granted him the same ability. Others used magical items, like one Soul Sea master who held a transparent crystal to his eye, watching the battle unfold in a dimension hidden from everyone else.

[AMBIENT: VOID DIMENSION]

Inside the void, the two were already locked in a fierce battle.

[SFX: SWORD CLASH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

Zane's sudden arrival threw Logan off, but only for a second. He raised the Spatial Blade and brought it crashing down on Zane again and again — fast, ruthless, precise. His style was built for maximum damage in minimum time. It had served him perfectly against every opponent he'd ever faced.

But Zane's Formless Sword had a way of making that irrelevant.

Every time Logan's blade came in, Zane's sword was already there — not blocking so much as redirecting, absorbing, dissolving. It looked almost casual. Sword after sword, Zane's state of mind grew calmer, his movements more relaxed. 

Logan gritted his teeth. He was not someone who gave up. He had clawed his way to the top of the York Divine Kingdom's younger generation through pure diligence and talent, with no noble background to fall back on. He was not going to fold.

“Break for me!”

[SFX: SWORD TECHNIQUE ACTIVATION]

The Spatial Blade erupted. Thousands of sword shadows burst outward in a chaotic storm, Logan's most powerful technique, the one he saved for opponents worthy of it.

Zane's expression didn't change. His sword swung out — loose, almost lazy, like a woman waving a fan.

[SFX: SINGLE CLEAN RING]

One sound. The storm of shadows collapsed.

In the half-second Logan needed to process what had just happened, Zane smiled at him. Logan blinked — and in that instant, an enormous, irresistible force poured through Zane's blade and into his sword arm. His grip almost gave out. The Spatial Blade was part of him, forged from the Law of Space itself, so it stayed in his hand — but his guard was wide open.

Zane's fist came through the gap.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

The first punch hit the Spatial Armor square in the chest. The armor held. Logan was pushed backward, borrowing the force to create distance. Not enough, Zane noted.

He closed the gap immediately.

Logan's blade came back in — desperate now, not calculated. Zane knocked it aside without looking at it.

[SFX: SECOND HEAVY IMPACT]

The second punch landed. Three hundred Dragon Scales behind it. A sound like cracking ice filled the void as hairline fractures spread across the Spatial Armor in a fine spiderweb pattern.

Logan flew backward.

What kind of strength was this? Not only was his swordsmanship untouchable, but his raw physical power was on another level entirely. Logan thought of the strongest young warrior he knew — the prodigy of the Nine Cauldron Martial House, the one fighter among the younger generation he had always considered his true benchmark. That fighter was formidable, but he had gaps. Weaknesses.

Zane had none.

As proud as he was, Logan had no choice but to face it. I'm not his match.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

Down in the square, the warriors, all at least at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, stood perfectly still, patiently waiting to see how the fight would end. The powerful Soul Sea masters might have already known the outcome, but everyone else was completely in the dark.

The warriors from the Harlen Divine Kingdom could only put their faith in Zane.

[SFX: MOCKING LAUGHTER]

But the fighters from the York Divine Kingdom just laughed at them.

“These guys are so naive! Logan has mastered the third layer of the Spatial Law. He’s invincible in there. How could Zane possibly win? What a joke.”

“Logan is strong enough to take on a Life-and-Death Realm master. Zane’s only at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. They’re delusional!”

“Just let them dream. They’re in for a rude awakening. Once Logan comes out on top, they’ll see.” The people from York were brimming with confidence, remembering the terrifying power Logan had already displayed.

[SFX: ENERGY WAVE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

Just then, a powerful wave of energy washed over them from the sky, so strong that nearly everyone felt it at once.

“They’re coming out!”

[SFX: DIMENSIONAL CRACK]

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

All eyes snapped up as a strange crack split the air. A figure stumbled out, clearly hurt.

“The battle’s over. Looks like Zane was no match for Lo—” The warrior from the York Divine Kingdom couldn't finish his taunt. His jaw dropped as he realized the pathetic, battered figure that had emerged was none other than Logan.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

“Logan? He looks terrible!” The sight sent a shockwave of disbelief through the York contingent.

It was clear that Zane had given him a serious beating.
