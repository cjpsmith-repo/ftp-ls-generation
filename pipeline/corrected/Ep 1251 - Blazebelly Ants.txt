Ep 1251 - Blazebelly Ants

Word count: 1175

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
[AMBIENT: WORLD TREE BRANCH ATMOSPHERE]
Aurora stepped aside, his eyes quietly tracking Zane from head to toe. 
It wasn’t just him; Rowan and the other Demon Night warriors were all doing the same, trying to reconcile the guy in front of them with the person they’d expected. 
But Aurora’s gaze held more than just curiosity. He’d claimed he would kill the Queen of Punishment within three years—a boast so insane it was almost fascinating. He still couldn’t imagine how Zane planned to pull it off, and made a mental note to ask Amber about him later.
After a few more minutes of quiet conversation, Amber’s breathing had finally settled back to normal. *Okay,* she thought, taking a steadying breath. *Coming to the Illusion Battlefield was the right call.*
Once everyone was ready, Aurora led them to the end of the branch, where they stepped onto a flat, circular plate. 
[SFX: MAGICAL RUNES ACTIVATING]
[AMBIENT: PLATFORM RISING]
Golden runes swirled to life under their boots, and the platform lifted them silently upward, carrying them to the eighth branch of the World Tree.
Every branch below this one, Aurora and the others had already cleared on the way up, cutting the ants down before anyone else even had to lift a hand. The eighth branch was different. This one, they’d have to fight through themselves.
[AMBIENT: INSECT CHITTERING AND CRACKLING FLAMES]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON BRANCH]
The moment they stepped off, Zane saw it was crawling with giant ants. 
Their crimson shells were covered in sharp spikes, but the strangest part was their bellies—they were translucent, with flickering flames dancing inside like living lanterns. If they weren’t so obviously dangerous, the sight would have been almost comical.
“Blazebelly Ants,” Aurora said, his voice low and serious. He’d been through the World Tree three times and knew exactly what they were dealing with. “They explode when you kill them. A big explosion. Everyone, spread out.”
[MUSIC: BUILDING ANTICIPATION]
The others immediately gave each other space, their caution obvious. 
Aurora wasn’t done. He drew a small, blackened branch from his side—little more than a twig—and gave it a light flick. A handful of leaves lifted off it, hanging in the air for a heartbeat before he moved his fingers, and the leaves shot forward like a volley of blades.
[SFX: LEAVES SLICING THROUGH AIR]
The nearest Blazebelly Ant never had a chance. The leaves tore clean through its shell, shredding it in an instant. Wounded and dying, the ant’s abdomen flared brighter, and it lunged straight at Aurora.
“Watch out for this one!” Aurora called, already retreating.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE DETONATION]
[AMBIENT: SMALL SHOCKWAVE AND FALLING DEBRIS]
The ant went off behind him, the blast rolling out in a tight, contained burst. A thin column of smoke curled upward, and the scorched smell of it drifted across the branch. Even Zane, for all his strength, knew that catching one of these at close range unprepared would leave a mark.
Aurora turned back to the group, brushing a bit of ash from his sleeve. “That’s it. Not difficult, if you’re careful.”
Zane knew they were all watching him, waiting to see what he was made of.
For a second, he just blinked, caught off guard. Why is everyone looking at me? Then it clicked—they wanted to see if he could do the same.
A slow smile spread across his face. “Guess I’ll give it a shot, then.”
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
He broke into a dead sprint, but instead of attacking the nearest ant, he veered just close enough to tap its back with the tip of his boot before speeding past. 
[SFX: INSECT SCUTTLING]
Provoked, the ant scrambled after him at a shocking speed. 
Zane just kept running, ignoring it completely as he did a quick flip-kick to brush past another one. *Tap.* Now two were on his tail.
The rest of the group watched, their nerves fraying as the train of deadly creatures grew longer.
“What is he *doing*?” someone muttered.
“He’s baiting all of them!”
“Is he crazy?”
[MUSIC: INTENSE RISING TENSION]
This was a suicide run. A single Blazebelly Ant wasn’t much of a threat on its own, but the force of its death explosion was terrifying. The usual strategy was to lure them out one by one—a slow, grueling process. Watching Zane charge headfirst into the swarm made Rowan’s heart leap into his throat; he almost started after him.
Amber, however, was starting to smile. She didn’t know Zane all that well, but she’d heard the stories that had trickled out of the Tower of Sin. This was his style. Playing by the rules wasn’t his thing. He was the kind of person who charged straight into the fire, and more often than not, his sheer audacity saw him through.
Zane didn’t slow down. 
[SFX: MASSIVE INSECT SWARM SCUTTLING]
The line of skittering, fiery ants behind him grew and grew, until it was a massive, undulating train that covered the ground.
“There are so many,” one of the warriors whispered. “If they all go off at once…”
“Isn’t he scared of getting blown up? He’ll lose a ton of Illusion Points if he dies!”
Aurora had been watching with focused curiosity, but as the others spoke, his eyes widened in sudden, horrifying realization. 
“Get back!” he screamed, his voice cutting through the tension. “Everyone, back to the main trunk! Now!”
His warning snapped them out of it. As the group scrambled back, Zane finally stopped. 
[SFX: SHARP GRAB AND TWIST]
He spun and lunged at the lead ant, catching it by one of its pincers as it snapped toward him. With a surge of power, he twisted its head clean off, then closed his other hand around its abdomen before it hit the ground.
The flaming abdomen tumbled through the air, its inner flame blazing brighter with every turn, and landed right in the thick of the pursuing ants.
That was all it took. 
[SFX: MASSIVE CHAIN EXPLOSION]
[MUSIC: CATASTROPHIC ORCHESTRAL CRESCENDO]
[AMBIENT: ROARING FLAMES AND DEBRIS]
A chain reaction ripped through the entire swarm, a deafening series of explosions that blurred into one cataclysmic blast. A mushroom cloud of dust and fire billowed into the air. 
[SFX: TREE SHUDDERING]
The shockwave tore outward like a tidal wave, so powerful that the World Tree itself, as massive as a mountain, shuddered from the force.
From his calculated safe distance, Zane planted his feet and braced himself, his expression locked in concentration as the main force of the shockwave washed over him.
Fortunately, Aurora’s warning had come just in time. 
[SFX: BODIES SLAMMED AGAINST BARK]
When the invisible wall of pressure hit the rest of the group, it slammed them against the unyielding bark of the tree trunk, pinning them there and squeezing the air from their lungs. 
If he hadn’t screamed at them to fall back, the blast would have hurled them off the branch or smashed them into the tree with lethal force. 
They would’ve been severely injured, if not outright killed.
