Ep 520 - Tia Has Her Reasons
Word count: 1124
[AMBIENT: ICE PALACE INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
After Elder Imogen passed the torch to Tia, she gave her free rein, promising to only step in for the direst emergencies. So after a quick chat and a few words of advice, the former leader was gone, leaving Tia to handle everything on her own.
The current crisis was Tia's first real test. Her predecessor wouldn't be around forever—there was always a chance she wouldn't survive the next Heavenly Tribulation. As the new leader, Tia had to rely on her own strength and wits to see the organization through.
[SFX: SHOES DROPPING]
[AMBIENT: QUIET ICE PALACE]
The moment she was back in the ice palace, Tia kicked off her shoes and collapsed into a large chair, the Ice Phoenix settling on its perch near her head.
Zane stood off to the side, waiting for Cedric to arrive.
The sect-protecting magic array was their top priority, so they planned to get started the second they got back.
It was just the two of them in the palace. Zane looked at Tia’s exhausted face. "You're under a lot of pressure," he observed quietly.
A faint smile touched Tia’s lips. She looked up at him. "You're the one who should be feeling the pressure."
"I'm not worried. As long as I have enough time, I'm confident I can build the array," Zane told her, his voice serious. "What's there to be pressured about?"
Tia offered a playful smile. "Well, if you're not worried... then why should I be?"
[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERIC]
But despite her light tone, she was drowning in stress. To the outside world, leading Cloud Hall was a coveted honor, a position people would kill for. For Tia, it was just a massive weight. She felt like she was constantly walking on thin ice, each decision threatening to plunge her into the freezing depths below. The responsibility of a Fourth-Grade sect was overwhelming enough on its own, and now a full-blown crisis was looming.
Zane knew she was just trying to lighten the mood, so he let it go. All that mattered now was getting the magic array built. And fast.
[AMBIENT: PALACE HALL]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
But before Cedric arrived, a group of Cloud Hall elders showed up.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Led by Emanuel Drew, they stormed into the palace and made a beeline for Tia. Emanuel shot Zane a nasty look before turning to their leader. "Hall Master," he began, his voice deceptively polite, "why haven't you handed this man over?"
He didn't wait for an answer. "He's the one who offended Maddox, not us! Why should all of Cloud Hall pay for his mistake?"
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]
The moment the elders entered, Tia's exhaustion vanished, replaced by the commanding presence of a leader. She met Emanuel's eyes, her stare as cold as the palace walls. "I have my reasons."
"Reasons?" one of the other elders scoffed. "What is there to even consider, Hall Master? Are the lives of millions of our disciples, five Second-Grade sects, and all of Cloudhaven worth less than this one Soul Realm whelp?" he demanded. "Cloud Hall has stood for tens of thousands of years. If it falls because of this boy, he'll be the greatest sinner in our sect's history!"
[SFX: ANGRY GRUMBLES]
Zane stood motionless among the crowd of elders, eyes closed.
[MUSIC: COLD TENSION]
Tia waited for silence, then fixed her gaze on Emanuel. "Hand Zane over—can you guarantee Cloud Hall's safety if I do?"
Emanuel straightened. "He provoked Maddox. The wisest course is to expel him before he drags us down further."
Tia's eyes swept the room. "If Zane hadn't killed Marilyn, Maddox would have completed his ritual in two years, crossed into a higher realm, and proclaimed himself emperor. He wouldn't just destroy Cloud Hall—the World Merchants' Guild, the Ethereal Spirit Sect, everything would fall. So you tell me: did Zane harm Cloud Hall—or save it?"
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
Emanuel's mouth opened. Nothing emerged.
Another elder recovered. "Even so, why must Cloud Hall bear this burden? Why not the World Merchants' Guild or the Ethereal Spirit Sect?"
"So that's the kind of elder you are." Tia's voice cut like ice. "Does cowardice qualify you to hold your position? Is Maddox so terrifying you'd rather grovel than stand?"
[SFX: ERUPTION OF VOICES]
"Maddox or not, Zane must be handed over!"
"This boy will bleed Cloud Hall dry!"
The voices crashed together, then faltered.
[SFX: WIND DYING DOWN]
The temperature had plummeted. Cold crept into their lungs. The elders pulled their robes tighter.
[MUSIC: COLD, QUIET DANGER]
Tia hadn't moved, but her eyes swept the room like ice shards. "You've forgotten who the Hall Master of Cloud Hall is. I will handle Maddox."
Behind her, the Ice Phoenix spread its wings.
[SFX: RUSH OF COLD AIR]
The temperature plummeted further.
Emanuel stared at Zane, then turned and left. The other elders followed without a word
[AMBIENT: PALACE ENTRANCE]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
At the entrance, the departing elders nearly collided with Cedric.
"Hello, Elders! Good to—"
They swept past without acknowledgment.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS FADING]
Cedric blinked. "Did someone slip something into their tea?"
He stepped inside and immediately felt the biting cold. He pulled his collar up and made his way to Tia, bowing respectfully. "Cedric reporting to the Hall Master."
"I need you to do something," Tia said calmly.
"Whatever you need." Cedric kept his voice even, recognizing her lingering anger.
"The array from the Sixth-Grade sect I gave you," she began, ignoring the fuming elders completely. "Have you made any headway?"
"Well..." Cedric hesitated. "That array is... immensely complex. I'm afraid it would take me three to five years just to fully comprehend it."
"Scrap it," Tia ordered bluntly.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Cedric stared. "What? Scrap it?" He couldn't have heard her right. An array from a sacred order was a priceless treasure. And she was telling him to just... toss it?
[MUSIC: AUTHORITATIVE BRASS]
"From now on, you'll be assisting Zane in creating a new one. All of Cloud Hall's resources are at your disposal." Her look was firm. "And to be clear, you will be assisting him."
The emphasis on that last word left Cedric reeling. "Starting today?"
"Yes. Today," Zane cut in.
"Must we be so hasty?" In Cedric's experience, inscribing runes demanded a calm, focused mind. The masters of the Runic Tower were ritualistic about it: bathing, clean robes, burning incense—every preparation had to be perfect.
A project like this usually takes a year, sometimes several. You couldn't just throw it together overnight. What was the rush?
[MUSIC: URGENT PERCUSSION]
"You think we have time to kill?" Zane chuckled. "If we don't get a move on, Maddox could be at our gates by tomorrow."
Zane's answer only confused Cedric more. Even if Maddox gave them a whole month, building a new sect-protecting magic array from scratch was impossible.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
Of course, if Cedric had seen Zane in action at the Martial Tournament—effortlessly inscribing over a hundred runes in a matter of moments—he wouldn't have been confused at all.
He'd find out soon enough who he was really dealing with.
