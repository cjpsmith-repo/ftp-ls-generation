Ep 826 - The Price of Power

Word count: 1116

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

“Son of the Plane?” Zane repeated, thrown by Lavender's words. What in the world was she talking about?

Lavender explained patiently. “This world of chaos you're in—it's like its own separate plane of existence, its own entity. Just like the stars have wills of their own, so does this world.”

The world of chaos wasn't a single mind, but a mirror reflecting the collective desires of all living beings. Since most inhabitants were human, the Son of the Plane took human form—except for telltale details: forked tongue, wolf-like fangs, solid black eyes. These features connected him to all other life in this world.

Not every world of chaos gets a son. He is born from destruction, appearing only when irreversible damage has been done, to repair the world.

“So, if I'm right,” she concluded, “this Little Monster was born with one mission: to fix the ascending passage.”

[MUSIC: REVELATION THEME]

Her theory clicked into place. The passage to the upper world had been destroyed by a dragon, leaving the Soul Sea Realm masters stranded. Of course, that’s why this creature—the Son of the Plane—had been born.

But even as she put the pieces together, Lavender knew something was off. “He's supposed to be the embodiment of this world's will,” she mused, shaking her head as she watched the creature, “but his personality is… twisted. Something must have gone wrong while he was growing up.”

As it turned out, Lavender was right. When a world of chaos needed to be restored, its Son would be born by chance into the body of a living being, destined to live a normal life until adulthood, when his mission would be revealed. He would then draw on the plane’s power to make his repairs.

[AMBIENT: DARK FOREST]

[MUSIC: TRAGIC ORCHESTRAL]

This Little Monster, however, had lived a long, miserable life. Born with a monstrous appearance, he’d been abandoned in the Sevenwaters Forest right after birth. The beasts there, instinctively sensing what he was, never harmed him. Instead, they raised him as one of their own.

[SFX: BATTLE SOUNDS]

That peace was shattered when warriors from the Nine Cauldron Martial House came to the forest to train and began slaughtering the very beasts that had been his family. Seeing his kind fall one by one at the hands of humans sparked a cold, unforgiving rage inside him, turning his heart against the race that had cast him out.

Lavender’s sad smile returned. “Still, this could be a good opportunity for you. The Son of the Plane is the embodiment of this plane's will, and he carries that power inside him. When he uses his own Vitality, he releases this world's innate gift, which can boost your power level. The Lucky Light in the Dragon Soar Arena and the Tower of Sin would look like a joke next to this.”

The Lucky Light she mentioned was a boon even for masters at the late stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.

“Keep fighting him,” Lavender urged. “The longer you stay in there, the more you'll gain.”

A wicked grin split the Little Monster's face as he stared down at Zane. “How does it feel? Not bad, right? Your mind feels clearer, doesn't it? Your power level is shooting up. I bet it's blowing your mind.”

[SFX: EVIL LAUGHTER]

He threw his head back and cackled.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

The golden Vitality in his hands surged and condensed — and then he hurled it.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

A golden spear screamed through the air toward Zane.

Zane twisted and let it pass. It didn't stop. The spear hit the barrier behind him — the same barrier that had held against Fiery Shine's Nirvana Flames — and punched straight through it.

[SFX: BARRIER TEARING]

The Soul Sea master maintaining the barrier snapped his hand up. The hole sealed in an instant, but his expression had shifted. The spear hadn't overpowered the barrier. It had dissolved it — the golden Vitality had simply unraveled the force holding it together, as if the barrier's own laws had turned against it.

The other Soul Sea masters watching exchanged glances. They had known for years that the Little Monster's Vitality was strange. Now they were seeing exactly how strange.

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLE ATMOSPHERE]

[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]

Zane didn't give him the satisfaction of a reply. He just kept weaving through the air, dodging the golden spears and silently absorbing the radiant energy they left in their wake. The monster knew the energy could boost someone's power, but since he was still oblivious to his true nature, he had no idea he was handing out the plane's innate gifts.

Inside, Zane could feel it working. He'd been stuck at mid-stage Spirit-Tribulation since his time at Cloud Hall — close to the edge of late-stage, but not quite there. Now, with every spear he dodged, the threshold was getting closer.

[SFX: SHOCKED GASP]

“That guy is absorbing the monster's magical energy!” one of the Soul Sea Realm masters shouted, mistaking the innate gifts for simple magic.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Watching from the sidelines, War King York's expression soured. This was not going according to plan. He and the Holy Maiden had brought this monster here to kill Zane, not to supercharge him.

The Soul Sea masters present could still control the Little Monster — their personal domains, the laws they had forged themselves, were strong enough to resist the plane's will for now. But if this creature ever broke through to the Life-and-Death Realm, that might no longer be true.

[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]

The monster's gifts weren't endless. Once he used too much power, his golden Vitality would fade to a useless gray. To prevent that, the Nine Cauldron Martial House had a dark secret: they would periodically kill gifted individuals and secretly feed their energy to the monster. Only then would his Vitality burn gold again. In other words, the monster was a living vessel, devouring the talents of other warriors so that their power could be harvested for someone else's gain.

The Little Monster himself had no idea any of this was happening. The plane's will hadn't awakened in him yet. He was just a twisted creature who loved to kill, and Zane was the most interesting opponent he'd found in years.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]

At first, the monster hadn't wanted to use his Vitality, but he was clearly no match for Zane in raw strength. 

And now, Zane had figured out his secret. He just kept circling, baiting the attacks and soaking up the magical energy from the creature's life force—a turn of events War King York had never imagined in his wildest nightmares.
