Ep 905 - The Dragon-Blooded Archer

Word count: 965

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

Even after possessing new bodies, the mighty warriors of the Soul Sea Realm could still channel about seventy percent of their original power. 

But none of them expected a single arrow to pack such a terrifying punch.

A few of the younger warriors stiffened at the sight of it, their faces tight with tension. 

Noticing the nervousness spreading to Fiona and Callan, War King Wind offered a reassuring smile. “Easy, now,” he said, his voice calm and steady. “We've walked the Path of Heroes plenty of times. There isn't much danger out here.” 

He’d planned to warn them that the real threat lay deep inside the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, but seeing the fear rippling through the group, he held his tongue. No sense in starting a panic.

[SFX: SERIES OF ARROWS]

The clang of the first arrow had barely faded when three more whistled in from the right.

“Right side! Brace yourselves!” War King Wind yelled.

The two Soul Sea masters on the right flank dug in, tightening their grip on the heavy shields. 

[SFX: METAL IMPACT]

[SFX: ARROWS SHATTERING]

The arrows slammed into the metal with a deafening clank and shattered on impact. Because they’d been ready, the shield formation only shuddered for a moment before the warriors steadied it and resumed their slow, grinding advance.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]

Callan let out a relieved laugh. “The arrows in this 'Fast Arrow Zone' are supposed to be impossible to dodge, but with these shields? It’s not even a problem,” he said, a confident smile spreading across his face.

With so many powerful elders protecting them, the princes and other young royals were already starting to relax. The initial fear was fading.

[AMBIENT: INTENSE ARROW BARRAGE]

[SFX: WHOOSHING ARROWS]

The air filled with the constant whoosh of arrows. As their shield box trudged deeper down the Path of Heroes, more dragon-shaped sculptures rose from the sea, and the volley of blue arrows intensified. They came from everywhere at once—some materializing out of thin air, others launched from the dragons, which arched their necks to fire volleys high into the sky that rained down on the group.

But it was useless. The eight giant shields formed a perfect, impenetrable cage. No matter where the arrows came from, they couldn't get through.

They weren't moving fast, but after about five minutes, they had covered nearly a third of the path. 

“The Path of Heroes isn't that long,” Scott said to Zane. “At this pace, half an hour and we're through.”

Zane nodded. He had nothing to worry about on this stretch — he was along for the ride.

Arrows continued to hammer against the shields, a constant, deafening rhythm. War King Wind kept his focus on the other Soul Sea masters, reminding them to stay alert. Inside the formation, ninety powerful warriors were packed shoulder-to-shoulder, squeezed so tightly there was barely room to breathe, let alone move.

Then something changed.

About two hundred feet off to the right, a statue rose from the golden sea — but it wasn't a dragon.

It was a person.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

[SFX: SLOW RISE FROM WATER]

Carved from white jade, every detail rendered with uncanny precision — face, hands, the curve of each finger. Its eyes were set with scarlet gemstones that caught the light like live coals. And unlike the dragon statues, its limbs moved. It reached back over its shoulder, drew a black arrow from the quiver on its back, and nocked it against a dark golden longbow.

A chill ran up Zane's spine. He couldn't explain it — just a cold, instinctive warning from somewhere deep in Brianna's body.

Brianna's voice cut into his thoughts immediately. “Idiot. It's not the right side — it's the left. And you've already caught its attention.”

“What is that thing?” Zane asked.

“The Dragon-Blooded Archer,” she said. “One of the elders' finest creations. And those shields won't stop it.”

“What?”

“The shields aren't solid Lodestone — they're fragments pieced together. That archer's arrows are in a different category entirely. I'd be surprised if the shields hold.”

The longbow was already drawn.

[SFX: BOWSTRING TENSION]

Zane's eyes snapped to Callan and Randy. He grabbed them both and wrenched them sideways.

“Down! Everyone get down!”

The words came out in Brianna's voice — clear and sharp — and only a handful of Soul Sea masters reacted in time. The rest were still turning their heads when the black arrow hit.

[SFX: PIERCING STRIKE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

Just as another volley of blue arrows glanced off the shields, a flicker of black light pierced through the giant metal plates as if they were made of paper.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

In the sudden, ringing silence that followed, everyone stared as four people stood frozen in place.

A single, small hole was drilled neatly into each of their foreheads. The black light had gone straight through their skulls. All four were Soul Sea masters, their souls at the Fighting Soul Realm level. But the arrow's power had been absolute. It had punched through their minds and instantly shattered their souls from within.

[SFX: HEAVY BODIES FALLING]

With their souls gone, the now-empty bodies collapsed to the ground with a series of dull, heavy thuds.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Sheer terror flooded the eyes of both the crown prince and Randy. The arrow had struck the exact spot where they had been standing just a moment before. If Zane hadn't pulled them aside… that black light would have been the last thing they ever saw.

[SFX: CROWD PANIC]

Panic erupted.

“What was that?!” someone screamed.

“That statue—the human-shaped one! Its arrows can get through the shields!”

“What do we do? We can't move fast enough! We're sitting ducks in here!”

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

A single arrow had just executed four powerful Soul Sea warriors. The bubble of their confidence had burst, replaced by cold, hard fear. The real problem was their cage. The shield box that had been their salvation was now a trap. The space was so tight that dodging was impossible, but if they broke formation, the endless storm of arrows outside meant certain death.
