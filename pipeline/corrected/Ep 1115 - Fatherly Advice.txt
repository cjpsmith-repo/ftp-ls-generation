Ep 1115 - Fatherly Advice

Word count: 1049

Zane left on the back of the Heavenly Phoenix Horse, a legendary beast so rare it was almost a myth even in the upper worlds. 
[SFX: PHOENIX HORSE WINGS FLAPPING]
It was a gift from Yolande, a nod to everything he’d accomplished.
[AMBIENT: PALACE INTERIOR ECHOING HALLS]
Once he was gone, she turned and disappeared back into the depths of her palace.
Deep inside, a flight of stairs was etched with an intricate, divine pattern. 
[SFX: DIVINE SIGILS ACTIVATING]
[SFX: ETHEREAL MATERIALIZATION]
Yolande placed her foot on the first step, activating the sigils, and a shadowy figure coalesced from the empty air.
“Father,” she said with a dip of her head.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DIVINE POWER]
The figure solidified into Supreme Lord Healum, one of the three great leaders of The Alliance, who just happened to be Yolande’s father.
Supreme Lords were the highest beings in the universe, and Yolande would never dare disturb him for anything less than critical. Theirs was a formal relationship, but a family one nonetheless.
Healum’s expression was a mask of indifference. Even looking at his own daughter, his voice was utterly devoid of emotion, hollowed out by a position that had long since demanded he abandon ordinary feelings like love. 
[SFX: COLD EMOTIONLESS VOICE]
“Has that brat left?” he asked.
Yolande nodded.
“I will do everything in my power to aid your plan,” Healum warned, his tone flat. “However, there are no true secrets in this world. If you can conceive of it, so can they.”
[SFX: SOFT KNOWING LAUGH]
A small, sweet smile touched Yolande’s lips. “Of course. Plenty of people know Zane is a Man Without Destiny. His path is a line no one can chart. All I’m doing is giving him a gentle nudge from the shadows. Who knows what the future holds?”
“True,” her father conceded, his voice as cold as ever. “The most difficult thing for a schemer is to think outside their established patterns. You’ve never been particularly good at scheming, Yolande, but you’ve handled this well.”
“Thank you, Father,” she replied, his usual iciness rolling right off her.
“But you must still be cautious. Zane’s luck is a rare thing, and his ambitions aren’t simple. Helping the Demon Night’s Queen of Slaughter reclaim her throne, for instance… that will be incredibly difficult. It would be a shame if he were to die in the attempt.”
“Yes, Father. I understand.”
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC INTROSPECTIVE]
Her father rarely said so much. A Supreme Lord existed at a level where they could sever all emotion to grasp at true divinity; he was barely human anymore. He’d never shown her an ounce of warmth since the day she was born, and today was no different. Still, hearing him say this much made something in her chest feel warm. For a second, she could almost pretend it was concern.
Healum had taken a great interest in Zane ever since she’d first told him about the boy. This was the first time he’d ever praised her, not since she was a young warrior just starting out. And even though she’d spent years telling herself she didn’t need his approval, it felt good to finally have it.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE JOURNEY]
[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING THROUGH VOID]
Meanwhile, as the Heavenly Phoenix Horse carried Zane through the channels between supreme worlds, he found himself thinking through what came next.
At his current strength, Zane couldn’t help Lavender reclaim her throne alone. But she had allies - Leroy Flynn stood firmly behind her, and old subordinates waited in hiding for their queen’s return. Lavender had also accumulated resources to accelerate Zane’s growth. Below the Soul Sea Realm, cultivation depended on pure talent. No pill or resource could safely speed progress without destabilizing one’s foundation. Above it, cultivation tied directly to the Inner World - expanding it, drawing tribulations into the body, building a small world within the Soul Sea. That kind of progress could be fueled. The Tenth Tribulation Introducing Pill proved how resource-dependent upper-realm cultivation had become.
The Dark World’s secret treasure was Lavender’s opening move. The first batch of what she intended to give him.
Zane found himself reflecting on his old life. It was almost laughable, thinking back on how limited his understanding of distance had been.
The universe was just so impossibly vast. A single supreme world was bigger than he could properly imagine. Back in the Lower World, the most powerful Message Rune could only transmit a signal about thirty thousand miles. Even the higher-quality one Freya had given him topped out at sixty thousand.
[MUSIC: SUBTLE TENSION BUILDING]
In his old world, sixty thousand miles could get a message across an entire continent.
But here? It was nothing. During the Passing the Torch event, he’d covered half that distance in a single, desperate push through the Law Storm. In a supreme world, sixty thousand miles was like walking down to the corner store.
The Heavenly Phoenix Horse moved at a speed no warrior could match on foot. Within a month, Zane had already reached the far edge of Alliance territory — crossing realm after realm in quick succession.
Zane thought of Yolande. Without the Gossamer Token and the horse, a journey like this would have cost an ordinary warrior decades and everything they owned. Cross-realm passage fees alone could drain a lifetime of savings. He had neither problem.
If he could hear Freya’s Message Rune, that meant she was within that radius.
He didn’t know Freya all that well. They’d had a simple, mutually beneficial arrangement to get out of that mine, and he’d helped her leave a little early. Of course, looking back, he realized that even if he hadn’t been there, someone would have come for him eventually.
[SFX: TELEPATHIC WHISPER]
*Is she in trouble?*
The problem was, there was no telling when she’d activated the rune. The signal could have been echoing in this channel for months, with no way for him to know. She’d told him once that one of her uncles was a city lord in the Soulbound Sect. With connections like that, Zane figured she’d have a much easier time than the average warrior who’d just ascended.
[SFX: HORSE HOOVES SLOWING]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL VOID CHAMBER]
A flicker of hesitation crossed his face. But it only lasted a moment before he reached forward and gently patted the Heavenly Phoenix Horse’s neck. Following Yolande’s instructions, he guided the magnificent beast to a halt, then turned it around, pulling out of the supreme world channel.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED HEROIC RESOLVE]
He would never abandon a friend in need. He had to find out what kind of trouble Freya was in.
