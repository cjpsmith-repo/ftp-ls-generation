Zane stared at the unfinished blade in the woman's hands. He looked at her, not sure if this was another test. "Really?"
She nodded, her golden eyes still reflecting astonishment at what she had witnessed. "Take it," she said, pressing the weapon into his palms. "This blade has waited for someone worthy since Chase abandoned it. Perhaps it was meant for you all along."
Zane accepted the offering with reverence, feeling the weight of both metal and expectation.
The mysterious proprietress stepped back, her golden fire spirit hovering nervously at her shoulder. "I sense greatness in your future, young Adept---something far beyond the Skyreach Sect," she whispered, her voice carrying the weight of prophecy. "Consider this blade my investment in that future."
Zane stared at the sword, unable to believe it. He'd gotten his hands on an unfinished weapon with limitless potential, and he hadn't had to spend a dime. Talk about a lucky break.
Still, he knew nothing in life came for free. Sooner or later, there'd be a price to pay.
Unlike his last visit, Zane now realized that neither the shopkeeper, nor the woman who owned the Weapon Pavilion were ordinary people.
He didn't care much about the shopkeeper, but the woman... her power was on another level. Zane couldn't even begin to guess her true strength, and the mystery only deepened his curiosity. The fact that she'd raised a fire spirit---an unbelievably rare creature---only added to her mystique.
"Damn, a small-time weapons shop like the Weapon Pavilion is hiding people this powerful," Zane thought. "The Empire really is full of hidden masters."
He thanked them both and made his way out of the store. For now, Zane wasn't sure if they had some other reason for laying low in the Weapon Pavilion. But now that they were on his radar, he'd be keeping a close eye on them.

[MUSIC: SERENE MORNING]
Zane returned to the Skyreach Sect early the next morning to find the Peaks shrouded in heavy clouds. After passing through the main gates, he skipped Misty Peak entirely. Instead, he headed for the back gate, making his way toward Quenching Peak.
Quenching Peak sat right next to Infernal Peak and wasn't one of the official Thirty-Three Peaks. An active branch of the Infernal Peak volcano ran straight through it, making it the perfect spot for forging weapons for the disciples of the Skyreach Sect.
Quenching Peak recruited its own disciples, but they came to learn the art of weaponsmithing, not martial arts. So while disciples on the Thirty-Three Peaks were judged by their combat prowess, here, it was all about how good you were with a hammer and forge.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
As Zane walked the flat path up Quenching Peak, he passed rows of buildings with identical roofs. No matter how long he'd been with the Skyreach Sect, the sight of these distinctive steel and stone structures still amazed him. When he reached the top, he was struck by the realization that not a single building was made of wood.
He didn't have to wonder why. For one thing, Quenching Peak was an offshoot of Infernal Peak, an active volcano, which meant the air was scorching hot year-round. Plus, with sparks constantly flying during the smithing process, a wooden building would be a raging inferno just waiting to happen.
[AMBIENT: VOLCANIC HEAT]
[SFX: DISTANT SMITHING SOUNDS]
Zane had come to Quenching Peak hoping to use one of their refining furnaces. Finding one turned out to be easy. He didn't even need directions---all he had to do was look up and follow the plumes of black smoke.
[SFX: CRACKLING FURNACE]
Following the smoke led him up a steep path and into a courtyard. There, a bronze furnace taller than a man stood waiting. He knew right away it was a refining furnace.
[MUSIC: TENSE FLASHBACK]
The sight brought back a sharp memory: being held captive in Evil Jace's cave, trapped inside a similar furnace while the man tried to smelt him down. Zane had wanted to keep that furnace, but it was too damn big for his Storage Ring, so he'd been forced to leave it behind.
[AMBIENT: BUSY WORKSHOP]
[SFX: FURNACE ROARING]
Evil Jace had been an Initiate Adept weaponsmith, and while his refining techniques were unique, he was no match for the disciples at the Skyreach Sect. The furnace in front of Zane proved it. Its shiny bronze surface was etched with intricate runes, and it was obviously far more advanced than the one from Evil Jace's cave.
Seven or eight disciples bustled around the furnace, working hard. Zane could see they were split into inner and outer disciples, just like on the Thirty-Three Peaks. But unlike the other Peaks where they trained separately, here they worked together like a well-oiled machine, each person's job dictated by their rank.
The outer disciples were stuck with grunt work---lugging supplies, fetching tools, and running errands. They scurried back and forth, while the inner disciples stayed close to the bronze furnace.
[SFX: FIRE STREAM]
A disciple in black robes knelt on the stone patio, his hands extended as he channeled a steady, brilliant stream of fire into an opening at the furnace's base. The flames danced and flickered, casting dramatic shadows across the workshop.
"Don't worry, I'm not trying to forge a weapon," Zane explained earnestly as he approached one of the outer disciples, a young man with calloused hands and soot-stained cheeks. "I just need to perform an Infusion on an existing blade."
[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDUP]
The outer disciple's lips curled into a contemptuous sneer. "Whatever." His dismissive grunt embodied the age-old clash of cultures. The martial artists of the Thirty-Three Peaks were fighters, not smiths---a distinction that defined their separate worlds. Though the world was a big place and occasionally produced rare prodigies who mastered both disciplines, this stranger before him, with his unmarked hands and combat-ready stance, clearly belonged to the fighting class.
"I'll spell it out for you," the outer disciple snapped, his patience wearing thin. "You're not welcome here. If you're bored and looking for entertainment, find it somewhere else. This is sacred work, not a spectacle for tourists." His voice rose as he stepped forward, arms outstretched. "Get out of here, I need to close the door!" he demanded, pressing his palms against Zane's chest and attempting to force him backward toward the exit.
[SFX: SUDDEN COMMOTION]
