Ep 421 - Eyes on the Prize

Word count: 1186

[AMBIENT: QUIET WORKSHOP]

[MUSIC: FOCUSED DETERMINATION]

Zane finished his first meditation rune, but he didn't even pause. He'd bought enough materials for ten divine patterns, and wasn't about to let a single scrap go to waste. Immediately, he jumped onto the next one.

Even a master couldn't craft runes forever. Each stroke drew from the reserve of one's soul power, a constant drain that accumulated with every passing moment. When that reservoir ran low, exhaustion crept in like fog—thick, heavy, and suffocating. The mind grew sluggish, thoughts scattered, and the razor-sharp concentration required for such delicate work evaporated.

Zane's formidable soul power gave him an advantage most cultivators could only dream of. His hands moved with steady precision through the second rune, then the third. The familiar rhythm settled over him—grind, mix, draw. Each completed pattern brought a small surge of accomplishment, fueling his determination to continue.

He managed to complete eight patterns in a row, an achievement that would have impressed even seasoned rune-crafters.

But as he put the finishing touches on the eighth, a violent wave of nausea hit him. His head began to pound like it was about to split open, shattering his focus. Drowsiness washed over him, his hand jerked, and the runic quill dug a jagged, errant line across the delicate drawing. It was ruined. A single slip was all it took.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]

[SFX: SOOTHING ENERGY WAVE]

"This is your first time,” the Cobalt Dragon’s voice echoed in his mind. “You can’t afford to burn through your soul power like this. Exhaust it completely, and you risk permanent damage—a price you cannot pay.” As the dragon’s words faded, a pleasant coolness washed through Zane’s mind, soothing the frantic throbbing.

The dragon had been far more present since consuming the Spirit and Blood of the Drake, its strength now great enough to lend Zane aid.

The warning hit Zane like a physical blow, and his chest tightened. He’d been too reckless, too impatient to master the patterns in a single go. You can't just force this, he realized. You don't learn something this complex overnight. He knew he had to stop. Now.

Crafting runes was far too complex to master quickly. Even the greatest rune-crafters and array masters had dedicated decades to their art. Zane was a complete novice trying to succeed on a deadline, but Tia’s situation couldn’t wait.
With that, Zane set aside the runes and returned his focus to his cultivation.
The Elite House challenges had become a daily fixture. Each morning, Saint Morphen's puppetry yanked Zane out of his room and deposited him in the gray building whether he liked it or not. 
The disciples had started coordinating — targeting his weak points, stacking numbers, forcing him to dig deeper each time. He'd burned through his dragon scales, his Psyche Spike, even called on Lily before the day was done. Today's final opponent had been a sixth-level Soul Realm disciple - tough, but not tough enough. 
After sending him packing, Zane shook out his hands and headed for the market.

[AMBIENT: PRIVATE CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: PENSIVE SCHEMING]

Tia paced across the polished floor of her private quarters, her expression tight with frustration. She had just learned that Zane was wasting precious time studying runes instead of focusing on his cultivation breakthrough.

"He's distracted," she said sharply, turning to face Saint Morphen, who stood near the doorway. "Zane should be advancing to the Soul Realm, not playing at rune-craft like some idle scholar."

Saint Morphen chuckled softly. "My lady, it's only natural for someone his age to be curious. Once he sees for himself how difficult runes are, he'll give it up. You needn't worry."

"No," Tia snapped. "I can’t wait for him to figure it out. Zane must reach the Soul Realm, and he doesn’t have time to waste dabbling in runes. He needs to stop. Now."

"No," Tia said. "I don't have time to wait. Send Cedric to the market and have him crush the boy's confidence. I want Zane to give up runes entirely — Cedric should be able to manage that in a single conversation."
Saint Morphen glanced at her, sighing internally. This is about her, not him. She was at the final stage of her own attunement—the Goddess Path—and should have been focused on her own breakthrough. Zane was talented, yes, but he was still just a kid.

But Saint Morphen would never dare say that aloud. Tia, the formidable head of Cloud Hall, was famously stubborn; once her mind was made up, it rarely changed.

Without another word, Tia activated a Message Rune. 

[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]

"Cedric, my quarters. Immediately."

Cedric? Saint Morphen’s brow furrowed in confusion. He stared at Tia. "My lady, what could you possibly need Cedric for? Surely you're not asking him to teach Zane?"

Saint Morphen stared at her. "You want Cedric to... discourage him?"

[MUSIC: CURIOUS TENSION]

Cedric Vance was Cloud Hall's foremost rune-crafter. Tia had entrusted him with the order-protecting magic array she had acquired from the Cloud Road, and he had been buried in his research ever since. The magic array was a relic from a sixth-grade sect, a defensive system of mind-boggling power and complexity. Just the resources required to build it would be astronomical, but the potential was thrilling. Once constructed, it would make Cloud Hall virtually impervious to any attack from the other sects in the Heartlands. Its completion was worth any cost.

Because of this, Cedric had been in seclusion, dedicating every waking moment to deciphering the array, determined to bring it to life as soon as possible.

He was deep in his studies when Tia's summons came. In the next instant, he was a streak of light crossing the sky to the ice palace. 

[AMBIENT: ICE PALACE]

[SFX: WHOOSH OF LIGHT]

He bowed low before her. "Miss, how may I be of service?"

Tia gave a slight nod. "Cedric, report your progress on the order-protecting array."

"Miss, I am calculating the material requirements now," Cedric replied. "I will have a complete manifest for you shortly, though it will take some time."

"Good. I have another task for you today," Tia said.

Saint Morphen watched, his curiosity giving way to quiet disbelief. She'd pulled Cloud Hall's finest rune-crafter out of seclusion just to discourage a student.

Saint Morphen watched, his curiosity piqued. Is she really going to have him teach Zane?

[AMBIENT: BUSY SHOP]

[SFX: DOOR CHIME]

A cheerful voice cut through the quiet of a shop. "Old man, long time no see! How've you been?"

The manager of the World Merchants' Guild, who practically lived in his shop at Cloud Hall, looked up. His face broke into a genuine smile at the sound of the familiar voice. 

"Business is business," the manager chuckled. "But it’s always better when you stop by. The whole Hall is counting on that genius of yours." He gestured for an assistant to bring his old friend a cup of mulled cider.

[MUSIC: CURIOUS STRINGS]

As the man stepped further into the shop, his gaze landed on Zane, and a small, curious smile touched his lips. "Well now, who do we have here?"

"A student from your Elite House," the manager explained. "He's just getting his start with runes. Oh, that reminds me." 

The old man’s eyes twinkled as he turned to Zane. "You're in luck. If you're serious about learning this craft, you should be talking to him. Zane, this is Cedric Vance, the best rune-crafter in Cloud Hall."
