Ep 79 - The Flying Dragon General
Word Count: 1096
[AMBIENT: WINDSWEPT HILLSIDE]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
On the hillside stood General Gavin, a commanding figure with a rugged face etched by years of battle and eyes that reflected both wisdom and unyielding authority.
Towering above most men, his intimidating presence was further enhanced by intricate armor adorned with dragons that seemed almost alive in the shifting light.
As the renowned Flying Dragon General and vice general of Dragon's Keep, he had just demonstrated his extraordinary power by shattering ice crystals with merely a pointed finger—a casual display of strength that left onlookers breathless.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT POUNDING]
Zane watched in stunned silence, his heart pounding against his ribs. "Holy crap," he thought, swallowing hard as cold sweat beaded on his forehead. "This guy's power is unreal. He could probably level a mountain with a glance."
"Randall, is this how you repay my kindness?" Gavin's voice cut through the air like a blade, each word dripping with dangerous disappointment. His eyes narrowed as he stepped forward, the ground seeming to tremble beneath his feet. "Three promotions, special training, my personal recommendation—and this is what you do? You have disappointed me beyond words."
"G-General," Randall stammered, his face drained of color as his knees visibly shook. Terror radiated from him in palpable waves. "I-I didn't mean to betray you. It's just... House Sterling offered so much... I couldn't resist..."
Gavin's unexpected appearance had scared Randall half to death, reducing the once-confident officer to a quivering mess. And in that moment, everything clicked in Zane's mind: the chase, his narrow escape—it had been an elaborate setup from the very beginning, with Randall as Sterling's puppet.
"House Sterling?" Gavin scoffed, his contempt evident in the curl of his lip. "I don't care how powerful House Sterling thinks they are. The imperial army stands on its own honor and is not for sale!" His voice rose thunderously, echoing across the hillside. "As an officer, you swore an oath, accepted a sacred duty. But you had the gall to ignore military protocol and sell your loyalty like a common mercenary. You've truly let me down, boy."
Gavin shook his head slowly, disappointment etched into the lines of his weathered face. Then he fixed his piercing gaze directly on Randall and barked, "Any last words before you face the consequences of your treachery?"
Randall's answer was to run.
It didn't help. Gavin took one step — a single step that covered hundreds of feet — and appeared behind him. He extended one finger and tapped Randall lightly on the back.
[SFX: BODY HITTING GROUND]
No wound. No blood. Randall simply spat once, crumpled, and stopped moving.
Gavin turned. His eyes found Zane and stayed there — a long, measuring look, as if he were reading every secret Zane had ever kept. Then he spoke to Jon without looking away from Zane. "Keep this quiet. I don't want it affecting morale."
He walked away. Within a few steps he was gone.
Zane's heart was still hammering long after.
Throughout the confrontation, Jon had stood silently nearby, observing with calculating eyes. He had aided Zane earlier, not from kindness, but because he believed in the young man's potential to become a valuable asset to House Brandt. The raw power Zane had demonstrated against the Razorwings had convinced Jon that this unpolished gem might one day shine brilliantly—though only time would reveal his true destiny.
[SFX: BODY DISPOSAL]
[AMBIENT: WINDING PATH]
[MUSIC: SOMBER CONTEMPLATIVE]
After disposing of Randall's body with clinical efficiency, Jon exchanged a meaningful glance with Zane before returning to Dragon's Keep, his mind already plotting future possibilities. Zane, still processing the violent justice he had witnessed, turned away from the grim scene and made his way along the winding path to meet Shane on the main road, his thoughts turbulent with all that had transpired.
Shane trusted Zane implicitly, remembering how his friend had previously escaped a Razorwing Queen unscathed, and felt certain Zane would handle Randall effectively during the chase.
[AMBIENT: FOREST WAITING]
Leading the Emerald Task Force, Shane and his soldiers waited right where they were. Two hours passed before Zane returned.
"Zane! Where's Randall? Is... is he...?" Shane asked, his voice trailing off. He was secretly wondering if Zane had killed Randall himself; after all, he knew Zane had been strong enough to take the crystal core from the Razorwing Queen.
Zane nodded. "Yeah, he's dead."
"You really killed him?" Shane asked, his eyes wide.
Zane just shrugged and gave a small smile. "Me? How could I have killed him? No, the General is the one who took care of Randall."
"The Flying Dragon General?!" Shane's face was a mask of awe at the mention of the vice commander from Dragon's Keep.
"Now that Randall's been executed, the Flying Dragon General wants this kept quiet. Tell your men," Zane cautioned.
"Right, of course! You can count on it. My men won't breathe a word about what happened here," Shane replied, his expression guarded. He never would have imagined the Flying Dragon General would get involved personally.
[AMBIENT: MILITARY MARCH]
After eliminating the Razor swarm with the Emerald Task Force, everyone returned to Dragon's Keep.
[AMBIENT: TAVERN NOISE]
[MUSIC: CELEBRATORY DRINKING SONG]
Relieved, Shane took Zane drinking to celebrate. Despite Zane's warnings, Shane nearly revealed everything while becoming thoroughly intoxicated.
Later, Zane purged the alcohol with his Energy and assessed his rewards: numerous crystal cores, including one from a Scorpiomane, plus his newly forged Adamant Body.
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
Sitting on his bed, Zane pulled two short halberds from his Storage Ring—the ones he'd taken after killing Graves and Roman Stryker. They were Legendary Weapons. Even though his Shattered Dagger had split one clean in half, it didn't matter. They'd both melt down just fine.
He communicated with the furnace in his body and fed both halberds into the black fire. They dissolved fast — and produced more Molten Iron Essence than he'd expected. The halberds were a matched pair; together their power had rivaled a Spirit Weapon, and that quality showed in the yield.
[SFX: FURNACE RUMBLE]
[SFX: DRAGON SCALES LIGHTING]
In his mind's eye, three more scales lit up on the Cobalt Dragon — dark green, steady. Seven scales now. He was getting stronger.
But sitting there, Zane felt the gap clearly. His power had outrun his cultivation level by a wide margin. He was still Marrow Refining Level — still technically a junior by any measure. Power without realm was a ceiling he'd hit eventually.
He'd need to push his realm up. And given how his body worked, the fastest way to do that was to take more hits — to let that warm current keep doing its work. He'd have to find stronger opponents when he got back.
He'd only taken on the Razor swarm mission to collect enough Points to get into Infernal Peak. The thought of seeing Elara again sent a jolt of excitement through him.
[AMBIENT: BUSY LANDING AREA]
[SFX: WAR WAGON TOUCHDOWN]
After the giant War Wagon touched down on the Bluesky Parking Ground, Zane and the other disciples from the Skyreach Sect rushed out. Zane headed straight for Misty Peak. He'd accepted the mission from Isla Novak, so he figured he owed her a report.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR MOUNTAIN PATH]
As he walked, Zane was lost in thought, replaying the Razor swarm mission in his head. About halfway up the Central Peak, he ran into a large group of pledges, all gathered near the practice grounds, looking nervous and talking amongst themselves.
"Rhys, Ethan, what's happening here? Why is every pledge gathered?" Zane inquired, surveying the unusual assembly.
At his appearance, both Rhys Bolton's and Ethan Grant's faces brightened with recognition.
"Zane! There you are!" Rhys exclaimed, his face splitting into a broad grin. "You vanished from the peak for nearly a month. Been enjoying yourself in the Capital all this time?"
"That's not his style," Ethan interjected with a slight frown, his perceptive eyes studying Zane intently. "Something significant must have drawn him away." Ethan's uncanny ability to read people compensated for what he lacked in strength.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS FORESHADOWING]
Little did they know that Zane's return would trigger a chain of events that would shake Misty Peak to its very foundations.
