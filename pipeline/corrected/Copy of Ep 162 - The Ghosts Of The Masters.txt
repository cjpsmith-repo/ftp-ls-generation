These legendary beasts, feared throughout the realm for their regenerative abilities and deadly venom, proved remarkably fragile against his broken weapon.

The Hydra's venom—renowned throughout the realm as its true weapon—was meant to be the genuine challenge of the third round. A lethal poison that its nine heads could spray in all directions, bringing even the mightiest warriors to their knees. Yet for Zane, it might as well have been water, rendering the beast's most formidable defense utterly worthless. He had slaughtered the creature effortlessly, his broken blade more than sufficient for the task.

 But the remaining Hydras seemed to catch on. As Zane charged the next one, it didn’t bother with venom. Instead, it lunged, its jaws clamping around him in an attempt to swallow him whole.

 Caught off guard, Zane had no time to dodge. He was engulfed by the beast. But only seconds later, the creature's head exploded from the inside out, spraying blood everywhere.

Zane had every advantage in this round, which helped him finish in record time. For one, his immunity to all toxins made the Hydras’ main weapon a joke. For another, there were fewer opponents than in the previous rounds. He was advancing, and fast.

 “Congratulations on passing this round,” Saul announced. “You can now claim the Cloud and Flying Birds Picture as your prize. It’s an ancient painting. I’m sure you could learn a lot from studying it. The choice is yours.”

But Zane would not be swayed. Saul merely shrugged at his determination. “Good luck to you.”
The chamber plunged into an eerie silence, broken only by the faintest whispers at the edge of perception. Otherworldly tension filled the air as unseen presences gathered around him.
Zane closed his eyes tightly, abandoning sight to rely entirely on his other heightened senses to locate his adversary.

Clenching his jaw, Zane forced himself upright, fighting off the vertigo that threatened to overwhelm him as he prepared for the next onslaught.
[MUSIC: CHALLENGING FIGHT]
But his luck ran out. When he tried the same trick on the other ghosts, it failed. They renewed their assault, and the pain became unbearable. He could feel his very soul starting to fracture, growing weaker by the second.

But from where I’m standing, your soul attack is clumsy and basic. You’ll probably end up dead before you get the chance to quit.”
Zane was so weak he could barely stand, but he wasn’t giving up. He gritted his teeth and shook his head. “Just give me a little more time. I’ll defeat them.”
“How?” Saul shot back. “Your soul attack is amateur hour. Turning your soul into a spike is a primitive technique, and these ghosts are far more sophisticated. You were lucky to kill that one. You should quit while you still can. They’re about to attack you again. What can you possibly do now?”
