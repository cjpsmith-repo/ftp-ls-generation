Ep 459 - Pass Or Fail

Word count: 975

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY CROWD]

[MUSIC: ENERGETIC FESTIVAL ATMOSPHERE]

Oracle City was electric. Every restaurant, hotel, and café overflowed with a mix of tourists, hopeful fighters, and masters of every skill level.

It felt like half the fighters in The Heartlands had descended on the city for the Martial Tournament. Obviously, not everyone was good enough to actually compete, but they'd all made the trip just for a chance to see the top masters from different schools face off. For most, the journey was worth it if they could just pick up a new move or two from watching the pros.

Some were even hoping to achieve a breakthrough just by watching the matches. No guarantees, of course, but hey, it was worth a shot.

[AMBIENT: BUSY CROWD]

Zane tried asking for directions to the Martial Tournament registration booth, but nobody seemed to give him the time of day. After getting ignored a few times, someone finally pointed him in the right direction.

The tournament was supposedly open to all fighters in The Heartlands, but with a limited number of spots, there was a catch: pre-qualifying matches. They were designed to weed out the weaker contenders and find the best of the best for the main event.

But the qualifiers were only for fighters from lesser-known schools, and you had to have at least reached the Soul Realm to even try. Only those who passed got a shot at the actual tournament.

Meanwhile, the big-name schools got anywhere from three to ten reserved spots each. That meant fighters from places like Cloud Hall, Bloodwood Cliff, and the Ethereal Spirit Sect could waltz right past the qualifiers. A few top-tier independent warriors could also snag a reserved spot and skip the line.

[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCH IMPACT]

A dull thud cut through the noise. It was a powerful punch, but it hadn't left so much as a scratch on the stone.

[AMBIENT: BUSY TESTING BOOTH]

[SFX: PAPER RUSTLING]

The old man running the booth gave the green lodestone a bored glance, scribbled a note, and barked, "Failed! Next!"

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

The fighter stared, stunned by the result, but there was nothing he could do. Shoulders slumping in defeat, he just shook his head and shuffled away from the booth.

"What a waste of a supreme Aether crystal," Zane thought, wincing for the guy. "Didn't even get a second shot. Man, that stinks." This was just the World Merchants' Guild's racket, plain and simple. Totally unfair. But then again, what in this world was?

It all made sense now—why so few fighters were actually signing up. A supreme Aether crystal wasn't just pocket change; it was worth over a million low-grade Aether crystals. For the big-shot schools and wealthy families of The Heartlands, that was nothing. But for a regular fighter, it was a fortune. Even most who'd reached the Soul Realm couldn't afford to just toss one away like that.

Still, for those who tried and failed, the crystal wasn't a total loss. They just chalked it up as the price of admission to watch the greats up close. Pass or fail, there were no refunds.

After the first guy trudged off, another fighter stepped up to try his luck. He had a crew cut, calm eyes, and a navy-blue machete strapped to his back.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]

He approached the old man, casually tossed a supreme Aether crystal on the desk, and strode toward the pale green lodestone without a word.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

"Think this guy's got it?" someone in the crowd muttered.

"He's at the peak of the Soul Realm. I bet he makes the stone change color."

"Don't be so sure," another voice countered. "We already saw two others at his level wash out."

The crowd's murmurs filled the air. Listening in, Zane finally put a name to the test: the stone was a lodestone, a special kind of magnetic rock so dense that even fighters at the peak of the Soul Realm struggled to leave a mark on it.

The fighter with the machete walked up to the lodestone and just… stood there. Relaxed. He gave a casual wave of his hand toward the stone.

[SFX: SHARP WIND WHISTLE]

[MUSIC: MAGICAL REVELATION]

For a heartbeat, nothing happened. Then a sharp whistle sliced through the air as a concentrated gust of wind shot forward and slammed into the stone with tremendous force.

Zane was genuinely impressed as he studied the man's technique.  Whoa, sharp and powerful… He's channeling the Wind Law. The fighter hadn't even reached the Inner-Core Realm, yet his mastery over elemental forces was undeniable.

Technically, anyone in the Soul Realm could attempt the pre-qualifying test, but very few actually passed. The lodestone presented a massive hurdle, and for all anyone knew, additional trials might await beyond it. The uncertainty alone was enough to discourage most candidates.

So far, most fighters who'd made it through had reached the absolute peak of the Soul Realm, with a few even having broken into the Inner-Core Realm. All told, fewer than ten Soul Realm cultivators had passed. Of those, most had already achieved the eighth or ninth level—the best of the best.

Zane watched them with a mixture of admiration and determination. Each successful candidate represented years of dedication, countless battles, and the kind of perseverance that separated true masters from ordinary fighters. He knew what the crowd would think when they saw him step forward. A third-grade Soul-Illuminated cultivator had no business being here.

But they didn't know what he'd survived. They hadn't walked the Death Road or faced the Blood Fiend Emperor's legacy. They hadn't been puppeted by a Fusion Realm master or challenged Cloud Hall's elite day after day.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]

And now, stepping up to the plate, was Zane: a fighter from the third grade of the Soul-Illuminated Realm. The spectators who were just there for the show sized him up. To them, he looked like a lightweight.

You could feel it in the air—they were all just waiting for him to fall flat on his face.
