Ep 89 - Family Business
Word count: 1394
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WILDERNESS]
Wispy clouds swirled across the pale blue sky, like steam unfurling from a hot spring.
[SFX: PEBBLE FALLING AND CRACKING]
A thousand feet below, a small pebble broke loose from the mountainside. It ricocheted down the rocky slope before shattering against the ground with a sharp crack.
[MUSIC: TENSE SEARCHING]
Lost in his frantic search for Elara, Zane was startled by the sound. He'd been pacing in circles on the mountainside, scouring every inch for what felt like hours, but he'd found no trace of her.
He froze, his mind racing. "Where in the hell could she be?"
Zane glanced up at the summit towering above him, his brow furrowed. Could she be trapped somewhere higher up? With a frustrated sigh, he started climbing again.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN PATH]
[SFX: CLIMBING FOOTSTEPS]
As he scaled the rocky incline, Zane saw a group of people making their way down the path from the summit. Leading them was a handsome, noble-looking man dressed in deep Council Robes. In the growing dusk, the man seemed to glow with a faint light of his own. Zane watched them get closer.
He knew that members of the Skyreach Sect stuck to three colors. Pledges wore White Robes, and Varsity members wore Shadow Cloaks. But the Council Robes were different. Those were reserved for a select few, the disciples personally mentored by the masters in Enhancement skills. The robes were a sign of their elite status.
Zane's eyes locked onto the man.
Then he saw who was walking behind him.
Perrin. And Caleb.
Zane's jaw tightened. He stepped forward. "Perrin. Where is Elara?"
Perrin's face went through several expressions in quick succession before settling on a cold smile. "Zane. I was going to give you a few more days before dealing with you. I never expected you to show up here yourself."
Adrian glanced between them. "Peiran — who is this?"
Perrin hesitated. "An unfilial son of my family," he said. "No one worth your attention."
Adrian's eyes moved back to Zane. "What's your connection to Elara?"
"She's my sister," Zane said.
Something shifted in Adrian's expression.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]
"He has an Aura," Zane muttered, a chill running down his spine. "A killer's intent."
He noticed the man was shrouded in a soft, lotus-shaped light. The murderous aura around him was surprisingly subtle, like a tangle of thorns handled so often they'd grown too smooth to draw blood. It was a part of his very nature. Still, Zane sensed a deadly threat coiled within that deceptively calm presence.
[SFX: SURPRISED MURMURS]
"She misses you very much." Having said this, Adrian bowed to Zane again.
Everyone knew Adrian was one of the most distinguished members of the Skyreach Sect. Few could ever earn his respect, let alone a bow. And yet, he had just bowed to Zane. Twice.
Perrin broke out in a cold sweat as he watched the scene unfold, his back quickly becoming soaked. The deference Adrian showed Zane piqued his curiosity.
"We've never met," Zane said, his voice wary. "Why the warm welcome?"
Adrian shocked them all with yet another bow. "It was my good fortune to meet Elara," he answered with a polite, practiced smile. "And if I have my way, I'll one day call you brother-in-law."
Fury ignited in Zane's chest. So that was it. His eyebrows shot up as Adrian spoke of marrying his little sister as if it were a done deal. He snorted.
"You? Marry Elara?" Zane's voice dripped with contempt. "Keep dreaming. You're not good enough for my sister, and I don't give a damn about House Warrick."
In his heart, Elara was the only family Zane had left. Hearing this man talk about her so casually, especially after just meeting him, enraged him. Zane couldn't stand that kind of smug arrogance. There was an old saying about not hitting a man who was smiling at you, but Zane was about to make an exception.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Adrian's smile finally faltered, his expression darkening with humiliation. Before he could respond, Caleb, his face twisted in anger, cut in.
"Damn it!"
Zane was taken aback by the sudden outburst, but before he could react, Perrin stepped forward, his voice booming, "You don't know what's good for you! How dare you humiliate Adrian!"
Despite the fury in his voice, Perrin felt a wave of relief. He'd been planning to kill Zane himself, but now the idiot had gone and offended Adrian—a disciple personally trained by a master.
"You moron, Zane," Perrin thought gleefully. "You just pissed off Adrian. You're as good as dead now. You brought this on yourself."
Zane just sneered. "So what? Perrin, what are you good at besides running your mouth?"
"You're just an Outer Disciple, Zane!" Perrin spat, his face red with rage. "How dare you speak to me that way? You're nothing! Today, I'm going to teach you the meaning of real strength before I crush you!"
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
As he finished speaking, streaks of blood-red light radiated from his body—his Vitality.
Perrin had leveled up several times recently. After intensive training, he'd broken through to become a Nature Creature, thanks to the considerable support of House Warrick. He typically used the Purple Flash Fist, but the Vitality now pulsing from his body glowed like plasma—a skill he'd learned from a tier-three refinement method of the Warrick family.
Perrin was certain he could crush Zane, who he believed was still stuck at the Marrow Refining Level, in a single move.
"Really?" Zane's lips curled into a sly grin. He clenched his fists, his eyes fixed on Perrin, sharp as daggers. "You're so sure of yourself," he sneered. "Come on, then. Show me."
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
The sharp, coppery smell of blood filled the air around Perrin. He had plenty of reasons to want Zane dead, far beyond him being a family outcast who'd just insulted Adrian. This was the moment he'd been waiting for. He lunged, ready to unleash his Shadow Fist.
[SFX: SUDDEN POWER DISRUPTION]
But in the blink of an eye, it was over.
[SFX: BODY COLLAPSE]
Perrin crumpled, his power vanishing as if it had never been there. Zane had somehow crippled him, severing his ability to use any of his Training methods.
Perrin came in hard — blood-red Vitality exploding from his body with every punch, the corrosive essence eating holes in Zane's robe where it landed. But when it hit his skin, it only left black marks. Zane's body didn't care.
He dodged and circled, letting Perrin exhaust himself. "You said you could crush me with one hand, Perrin. Back at the estate. Remember?"
"Shut up!" Perrin roared, throwing everything into a final charge.
Zane found the gap in his ribs. One punch. Perrin staggered.
Then the Psyche Spike.
Perrin's charge died mid-step. Zane caught him by the head, braced, and drove the Inch Punch into his chest — seven muffled cracks as the vital meridians shattered one by one.
He let Perrin drop.
"We share a surname," Zane said quietly. "So I'll let you live. But when I go back to the Lowell estate — Nolan and Owen are dead men. And they'll kneel in front of my father's grave for ten thousand years."
Perrin crumpled, his power gone. He was crippled.
Adrian, standing off to the side, watched it all in silence, seemingly lost in thought.
Caleb, however, couldn't just stand by. He and Perrin were close. He'd expected an easy win for his friend, especially after his breakthrough. But the exact opposite had happened. Zane had taken Perrin down instantly, shattering his Energy Channels. Fury surged through Caleb at the realization that Perrin's life as a warrior was over.
"Brother, why did you just stand there?" Caleb demanded, turning on Adrian. "Why didn't you stop him?"
Adrian just shook his head. "This is between Perrin and Zane—family business," he replied coolly. "It's not our place. Besides, Zane is Elara's brother."
[MUSIC: SOMBER REFLECTION]
Adrian's mind wandered to the past as he spoke. He vividly recalled interrogating Perrin about Elara's family—how Perrin had stuttered nervously, evading every direct question with calculated deflection. In that moment, the truth had crystallized: he was being manipulated. Perrin had intentionally concealed Zane's existence, driven by his own avarice.
The scheme had been clear—Perrin exploited Elara as leverage to extract valuable resources from House Warrick. Adrian's jaw tightened at the thought.
Had Perrin simply revealed the truth from the beginning, perhaps Zane wouldn't harbor such profound, venomous hatred toward his family—a hatred that now threatened to unravel everything Adrian had built.
