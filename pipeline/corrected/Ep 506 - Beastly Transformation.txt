Ep 506 - Beastly Transformation
Word count: 1029
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]
Zane's gaze locked onto Marilyn as she taunted Owen before the packed arena, her words carrying across the hushed crowd.
Two powerful forces were now converging on a single target. Owen's public challenge and Zane's private vendetta against Marilyn had collided in ways neither could control.
Throughout the entire competition, Marilyn had maintained an incredibly low profile. Before this battle, she'd possessed almost no presence, practically invisible as she stood behind Maddox on the elevated platform. Zane had barely registered her existence among the other disciples.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
But the moment she spoke, everything shifted. Power suddenly began radiating from her in waves that made Zane's skin prickle.
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
"Owen from The Fleecy Downs, also known as the killer of The Shadow Wolf King," she declared, her voice carrying a musical quality that made the mockery all the more cutting. A charming smile played across her lips, beautiful and cruel in equal measure. "Let me guess... that one is the woman you love, right?"
With a casual smirk that sent ice through Zane's veins, Marilyn pointed at the woman beside Elara. The captive stood as still as a statue, her face utterly expressionless—her six senses had been sealed, leaving her trapped in darkness and silence. Even so, a refined aura still clung to her, speaking of cultivation and grace that imprisonment couldn't erase.
Zane recognized the cruelty in Marilyn's tactics—she wasn't just threatening Owen. She was savoring his pain, drawing it out like a connoisseur sampling fine wine.
[SFX: BLADE VIBRATION]
[MUSIC: DANGEROUS STRINGS]
A low hum filled the air. Owen's hand, gripping his scimitar, trembled, and the blade let out a soft whistle. He looked up at Marilyn, his eyes flashing with dangerous, contained rage.
"What's it to you?" he hissed.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CONFRONTATION]
Raising his scimitar, Owen turned his fury on Maddox. "You!" he shouted. "The number one warrior in The Heartlands. You stole the woman I love. Prepare to die for taking her from me!"
[AMBIENT: DEAD SILENCE]
A hush fell over the crowd.
It was a bold declaration, nothing short of a direct challenge to the number one warrior in The Heartlands. Maddox, however, was completely unfazed. He just smirked, amused that Owen even had the nerve to address him. To someone of Maddox's stature, the so-called killer of The Shadow Wolf King wasn't worth a second glance, and he didn't dignify the outburst with a response.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]
Marilyn giggled. "Aren't you being a little childish?" she asked, her voice dripping with false innocence. "As a warrior, you should know that power is everything. How can you blame us for taking the woman you love when you were too weak to protect her? Aren't you supposed to be some saint-level genius?"
[SFX: CROWD GASP]
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD BUZZ]
At Marilyn's words, the arena erupted in a buzz of whispers.
"What? A saint-level genius?"
"Another saint-level genius? And he's an independent?"
"He should've joined a major sect! They'd all be fighting over a guy like that!"
"A saint-level genius is one in a million. They're meant to rule, not serve. Why would he join a sect?"
"Kid should've kept his head down until he was actually powerful. Clearly, he doesn't get it. Sucks for him he had to piss off Maddox. He's a dead man now."
Marilyn smiled, soaking in the crowd's reaction. "You're a saint-level genius, but you have no idea how to keep a low profile. If I were you, I would have hidden myself away to train, far from the world. Then, once I was strong enough, I'd come back for my revenge," she said. "Honestly, even if you couldn't get revenge, you could just go steal someone else's woman. Coming out here to beg for a death sentence was just plain stupid."
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
[SFX: WIND WHOOSH]
Owen's face twisted with rage. "Do you think everyone is as shameless as you?" he roared. Unable to take any more, he swung his scimitar at Marilyn, and a glowing green tornado materialized out of thin air, ripping across the platform straight toward her.
Was strength really all that mattered? More than anything else? Did being strong give you the right to walk all over people? To steal someone’s love?
Many of the warriors in the crowd saw themselves in Owen. If they were in his shoes, hearing those words, they’d be itching to kill that woman, too. And just like that, despite her beauty, any admiration the crowd had for Marilyn curdled into disgust.
In their world, betrayal and murder for the sake of treasure were common. Backstabbing your own sect wasn't unheard of. But you didn't brag about it. To flaunt such wickedness so openly, in front of everyone, was truly shameless.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
At that moment, however, surrounded by the roaring crowd, Zane was getting extremely nervous.
Owen's appearance was wrecking all of his plans.
Zane had been holding back, carefully concealing his true abilities. Destroying Eden's warrior spirit had only been step one—a gambit to provoke Maddox. If Maddox still didn't recognize him, step two was to assassinate Marilyn in a surprise attack. He had even gone so far as to tamper with the arena's enchanted barriers to ensure the battlefield was under his control.
But Owen's sudden attack was a variable he hadn't accounted for. It would be one thing if Owen was actually strong enough to kill her. But if he just ended up wounding her? A paranoid, injured Marilyn would be on high alert, and that would be a disaster for Zane.
After a minute of chasing Marilyn to no avail, Owen groaned in frustration and planted his feet in the center of the arena. The audience fell silent, waiting with bated breath to see what their new hero would do.
[SFX: TRANSFORMATION SOUND]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
Suddenly, Owen’s eyes began to glow as green spots mottled his face. His ears elongated, growing pointed, while his fingernails shot out, sharpening into deadly claws.
"Beastly Transformation?" Zane's brow furrowed as he watched the metamorphosis. Maybe now, he thought, we'll see a real fight.
