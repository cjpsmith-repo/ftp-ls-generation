Ep 1226 - Eight Thousand

Word count: 1020

[MUSIC: TENSE URBAN ATMOSPHERE]
[AMBIENT: BUSY CITY STREETS]
Out here in the main city, shaking people down for their Illusion Points was a whole different game. It was an organized system, happening right out in the open.
Some guy had declared himself “City Lord” and was forcing every warrior inside the walls to play by his rules. The main one? Fork over half of your Illusion Points—which was basically the same as letting the guy kill you once. It was an unbelievably arrogant demand.
The warrior must have seen the outrage on Zane’s face because he leaned in with a warning. “I’d listen if I were you. Go register. If you don’t, you might not ever leave this city.”
“What’s that supposed to mean, ‘not ever leave’?” Zane asked. His voice was cold, but his interest was suddenly piqued.
If this “City Lord” was really milking every warrior for their points, the guy had to be sitting on a massive stash. 
[MUSIC: DARK SCHEMING STRINGS]
Zane couldn’t stop a slow, predatory grin from spreading across his face. This was one hell of a juicy target.
Taking him down would solve a lot of problems, and land Zane a huge pile of Illusion Points in one fell swoop.
The warrior just chuckled. “The City Lord runs this place. If you don’t register and pay up, the second you step outside those walls, you’ll be swarmed by other warriors looking to take you out. How are you gonna survive that?”
[MUSIC: CONFIDENT BATTLE THEME]
A dangerous glint flashed in Zane’s eyes. He loved a good challenge. That scenario might be a problem for an ordinary fighter, but Zane wasn’t an ordinary fighter.
Besides, the city was huge. Even if he wasn’t a match for the City Lord, he could probably find a way to slip out. It was a perfect trap, though. Most of the warriors trapped here hadn’t even meant to come in the first place; they’d just wandered too close while exploring the wilderness and gotten killed by the legendary beasts that roamed the area.
According to the rules of the Illusion Battlefield, dying in the wild meant you respawned in the nearest main city. For them, this was it. They’d revive inside these walls only to find out they’d have to die a second time and hand over their points just to leave safely.
But Zane had no intention of getting in line to hand over anything. Illusion Points were far too valuable to just give away.
His target was the City Lord himself.
[MUSIC: TENSE BUREAUCRATIC]
[AMBIENT: REGISTRATION HALL MURMURS]
The line crept forward. After about five minutes, it was his turn.
“Name,” the warrior handling registration barked, his voice flat.
“Zane Jones,” Zane said quietly. He didn’t trust this guy, so he brought out his old alias.
“Faction? World? Sacred place?” the warrior droned, barely looking up from the parchment he was scribbling on.
“Why do you need all that?” Zane asked.
“Just answer the questions,” the warrior snapped, finally lifting his head to glare at him.
A wry smile touched Zane’s lips. This “City Lord” had to have some serious backing to get this many warriors to fall in line. After all, everyone was supposed to be equal here. It reminded him of Thane and his little scheme with the ogres, but Thane had only managed to trick a handful of them. Once the others figured out what was really going on, they’d stopped taking his orders.
The only way this guy could pull it off was if his status outside the Illusion Battlefield gave him real power over these people.
“The Alliance, the Psychean Realm, the Soulbound Sect,” Zane replied automatically.
The warrior gave him a quick once-over, then motioned to another fighter. “Alright, take him to the city exit. Collect his points, then he’s free to go.”
[MUSIC: OMINOUS MARCH]
[AMBIENT: CITY GATE FOOTSTEPS]
Zane’s expression was a complete blank as he followed the other warrior toward the north gate.
[AMBIENT: FORTIFIED WALL WITH WIND]
When he reached the edge of the city, he finally understood the warning. Soul Sea Realm warriors stood posted every hundred feet along the top of the wall, their black armor making them look like iron towers. They held light-golden sabers, their faces grim and unforgiving.
“Friend, where did these warriors come from? Why are they all dressed the same way?” Zane asked.
The warrior shot him a disdainful look, clearly enjoying the chance to show off what he knew. “These are the City Lord’s guards. They work for him — of course they’re dressed the same way.”
“Guards? You’re allowed to bring guards into the Illusion Battlefield?”
“Our City Lord comes from House Kincaid, one of the most powerful families around. When the Illusion Battlefield opened, House Kincaid had high hopes for him — they sent ten thousand of their best Soul Sea Realm warriors in alongside him. Warriors from the same origin tend to land near each other. Most of them, close to eight thousand, ended up right here in this city with him.”
“So from the moment he stepped into the Illusion Battlefield, he already had eight thousand Soul Sea Realm warriors as his personal guard,” Zane said.
“Now you get it,” the warrior said.
Just ahead, a warrior in blue robes stood before a small group of fighters, his expression cold. 
[SFX: SWORD DRAWN]
As Zane watched, the man drew a black longsword, the blade flaring with brilliant light. 
[SFX: RAPID SWORD STRIKES]
[SFX: BODIES FALLING]
He moved in a blur, cutting through the group in seconds. Seven or eight red motes of light—the souls of the fallen warriors—zipped back toward the city to respawn.
[SFX: SPARKS ERUPTING]
[SFX: ENERGY STREAMING]
Instantly, a shower of yellow sparks erupted where they’d fallen. Their Illusion Points. The entire cloud streamed directly into the blue-robed warrior.
*That’s a fast way to rack them up,* Zane thought. This guy must have collected a fortune by now. “Is that the City Lord?” he asked the warrior leading him.
To his surprise, his guide just shook his head. “No. He’s not.”
