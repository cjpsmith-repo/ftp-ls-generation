Ep 1255 - Sorcerers and Shadows

Word count: 1213

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: VOID ECHOES]
Thirteen giant eyeballs hung in the void. They had to be some kind of vital symbol for the Sorcerer Race, maybe even representations of their most powerful beings.
Zane had a theory that this massive Illusion Battlefield was a construct of the heavens’ all-powerful will, a place that held all of its memories. In this grand era, it contained everything: civilizations that were born, civilizations that were wiped out, and those that had somehow survived. They all appeared here, one by one. The temple he’d explored earlier, for example, was a relic of The Hallowed Concord.
And this World Tree looming before him held the secrets of the Sorcerer Race.
Across the vast universe, over billions of years, countless civilizations had formed. Some were fleeting, vanishing into the river of time like driftwood. Others, like humans and the Demon Night, only grew stronger and thrived. 
He’d found a Teleportation Token back in that temple, one that let him travel freely throughout this supreme world. So what was waiting for him at the top of the World Tree?
[SFX: TELEPORTATION TOKEN ACTIVATION]
Besides, the Teleportation Token was working again, so he could get out of any jam. 
Technically, nothing here could really kill him — it was all just a projection. But other warriors were still out there fighting, and Zane wasn’t about to back down.
He’d just checked his ranking, too. Even after racking up over two million Illusion Points, he’d actually dropped a few spots. The warriors ahead of him had clearly found some other way to farm points. 
If he didn’t get in on the action, his rank was going to tank.
Beyond the gray fog, the huge eyeballs just floated silently, unmoving. Zane wasn’t worried about them.
[MUSIC: TENSE PULSATING]
[SFX: WHOOSH OF MOVEMENT]
He hesitated for a beat, then launched himself into the air.
He was done wasting time trying to navigate the maze on the ground. 
With a single leap, he shot upward. No matter how complicated the labyrinth was, it couldn’t reach into the sky, and there were no rules against flying.
Below him, the treetop spread out like a hill, sloped upward at a shallow angle, its surface easily the size of a main city. 
At the very top, a ball of purple light swayed gently — too distant to make out clearly, but steady enough to use as a marker.
[SFX: STEADY WIND]
Zane flew straight for it, using the light as his reference point. But the longer he flew, the more his eyes narrowed. The distance between him and that purple glow wasn’t shrinking. 
Looking down, he realized he was hovering directly above the same three skulls he’d started at.
“There’s some kind of power binding me to this spot,” he muttered. “Is this Spatial Law?”
No ordinary maze could pull this off - only one built on Spatial Law could trap someone who was flying freely through open air. Understanding that, Zane took a single step forward — and vanished, reappearing yards ahead in a blink.
[SFX: SPATIAL TELEPORT FLICKER]
He repeated the motion, each flicker of movement bringing the purple light a little closer.
“It’s working.”
A grin spread across his face. But just as he moved to teleport again—
[SFX: SHARP IMPACT]
—he slammed into something invisible. His body jolted, and he was flung backward through space, landing right back where he’d started.
A short, bitter laugh escaped him. There wasn’t much else to do but accept it.
As his own heartbeat thudded quietly in his ears, Zane’s brow furrowed. This whole situation was turning into a headache.
He landed on the treetop once more, his eyes drifting toward the giant skulls scattered around him. He hadn’t paid them much mind before. But now, moving back and forth as he had been, he noticed something: the farther he got from the skulls, the quieter his heartbeat became. The closer he got, the louder it grew.
It was that small detail that finally caught his attention.
He walked toward the nearest one, reached out, and ran his hand along its surface.
[SFX: CRACKING BONE]
A crack split across the skull’s surface, running from cheekbone to crown. Zane struck it again with his palm, and this time it gave way entirely.
[SFX: SKULL SHATTERING]
Something small tumbled out from within — a bony, ragged figure no bigger than a third of a grown man, wrapped in tattered cloth.
[SFX: WHOOSH OF ESCAPE]
Before Zane could react, it vanished in a blur, slipping straight into another skull nearby.
“A shadow creature,” Zane murmured, recognition dawning. He’d seen their kind before, back in the lower realms — a whole pack of them had chased him once, years ago. He hadn’t forgotten that bony, wasted shape.
Why would something like that be hiding out here, of all places? He couldn’t say for sure. But he noticed one thing: the moment he’d driven that creature out, his heartbeat had gone completely silent. 
He didn’t hesitate any longer. Zane threw himself at the next skull, driving his whole body into it.
[SFX: BONE CRUNCHING]
Against his hardened frame, the skull crumpled like paper. But this time, not one but two black shapes burst free from its shattered eye sockets.
“Two?” Zane blinked. “They’re all hiding in these things?”
Dark figures, not very big but incredibly fast, darted through the mist. 
[SFX: RAPID DARTING MOVEMENT]
[SFX: CLOTH TEARING]
Even with his movement skill activated, Zane only managed to grab a piece of tattered cloth trailing behind one of them as it shot past.
[SFX: HISSING DISSOLVE]
The strip of fabric in his hand instantly dissolved into plumes of black gas that began to eat away at his fingers. Poison. On anyone else, it would have dissolved their bones in seconds, but Zane’s expression didn’t even flicker as he kept up the chase.
“Gila!” one of them shrieked.
Zane went after it, but the second figure reached out with jagged fingers, its curved nails like the claws of a wild animal, and began chanting an incantation. 
[SFX: INCANTATION CHANTING]
He knew the ancient spells of the Sorcerer Race were powerful, but he didn’t have time to worry about that. He had to catch this thing first.
The creature was quick, but Zane was faster. When he reached out again, it had nowhere to dodge. 
[SFX: BODY SLAM]
[SFX: CLOAK RIPPING]
He slammed it to the ground and ripped off its cloak in one fluid motion. 
[SFX: DISSOLVING FOG]
The moment the cloak came off, it dissolved into wisps of black fog, revealing the figure underneath. It was humanoid, but its entire body was pitch-black. Its hands and feet weren’t covered in skin, but in a shiny, hard shell that encased its whole body.
“Gila!” the creature Zane had pinned to the ground screeched, seeming to realize it couldn’t escape. 
[SFX: DARK INCANTATION]
It started chanting another incantation in a strange, guttural language.
[SFX: VORTEX SWIRLING]
[SFX: SKULLS EMERGING]
Two black vortexes swirled into existence on either side of Zane. From each one, a palm-sized skull emerged.
They looked exactly like the ones he’d seen arranged on plates earlier, with one difference: their mouths were wide open as they shot toward him, ready to bite.
