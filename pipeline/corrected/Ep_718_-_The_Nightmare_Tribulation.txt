Ep 718 - The Nightmare Tribulation
Word count: 982
The crimson clouds had begun to churn downward toward Tia. With a flick of her finger, she'd activated the crystal card again; the entire ice palace trembled around her as she prepared to meet the second wave.
[SFX: ICE CRACKING]
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL PALACE]
The ice palace shuddered violently, its massive pillars cracking and collapsing in on themselves until they were nothing but a shower of palm-sized crystals. The shards swirled together high above Tia’s head, pulsing with a cold, otherworldly light.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
From a crystalline card in her hand, a small Ice Phoenix burst forth. It soared into the gathering light, absorbing the raw energy and growing in an instant from a fledgling into a magnificent, fearsome creature.
[SFX: PHOENIX SCREECH]
[SFX: FIRE ROARING]
[AMBIENT: INTENSE HEAT]
With a piercing screech, the phoenix dove headfirst into the churning red clouds above. A wave of heat rolled through the Cloud Hall as the clouds roiled with menacing flames. The moment the phoenix plunged into the inferno, its icy form began to hiss and steam away. But for every part of it that vanished, a stream of crystals from the ruined palace flowed into it, replenishing its frigid energy and keeping it aloft. As the great bird carved its way through the fire, the scorching heat in the hall finally began to recede.
[MUSIC: CALM TENSION]
Lavender allowed herself a faint smile. “Tia was more prepared than I thought. This second wave of the Celestial Tribulation is fiercer than the first, but she’s handling it even better.”
Zane nodded, his eyes fixed on the spectacle. “This Life-and-Death Tribulation doesn’t look so bad after all,” he said, a little too confidently. “I bet I can handle it when it’s my turn.”
Lavender actually laughed at that. “Don’t be so sure. When you face your trial, it’ll probably be ten times stronger than this.”
She’d meant it as a casual jab, but she trailed off when she noticed Zane staring at her, his expression completely blank.
“What?” she asked. “You don’t believe me?”
Zane just shook his head. “I’d never doubt you.”
“Then why are you looking at me like that?”
He gave a slight shrug. “It’s just… I’ve never seen you smile like that before.It’s beautiful.”
[MUSIC: TENDER ROMANTIC PIANO]
It was true. In all the time he’d known her, since the day he’d first found her as his sword spirit, Lavender had been a stone wall of stoicism. Her face was always an emotionless mask. When she finally started speaking to him in the Tower of Sin, her presence had been so intimidating that it was hard to breathe in the same room, let alone look her in the eye. He’d only seen her break that composure once, during a heated argument with another Queen of Fate at the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary. But this—this was different.
[AMBIENT: GENTLE WIND]
[SFX: SOFT PETALS FALLING]
His thoughts were cut short as the new threat finally reached Tia. The sky was now filled with a slow, gentle rain of flower petals.
"The third wave is the Nightmare Tribulation," Lavender said, her voice suddenly tight. "Why is there a Nightmare Tribulation?"
It was the first time Zane had heard genuine alarm in her voice. "What's wrong with it?" he asked.
"It's extraordinarily rare," she said. "In all of Demon Night's recorded history, it has appeared just over a thousand times — across countless worlds, across countless ages. Of all those who faced it, only one person survived."
Zane felt the blood drain from his face. "One person."
"One." Lavender's eyes stayed fixed on Tia. "The first two waves suited her well. But no one has an advantage against the Nightmare Tribulation."
"Then we have to help her," Zane said immediately. "The Celestial Fragment — that's what you meant before, isn't it? That's the solution."
Lavender glanced at him. "Yes."
"How do I use it?"
"Not yet," she said, stopping him before he could move. "The tribulation is also a tempering. If she endures it herself, she comes out stronger for it. We wait. If she truly cannot hold on, then you go." She looked back at the sky. "And there is something else you should know about that fragment."
Zane waited.
"It isn't a piece of the current Cosmic Order," Lavender said. "What exists now — the order that governs this world — that isn't what the fragment came from. It came from something older. A previous Cosmic Order, one that existed long before the current one."
"There was one before?" Zane asked.
"Yes." Lavender said nothing more. Below them, the petals were already beginning to fall.
Tia’s expression was grim. She could feel the lingering power from the last trial and knew this one wouldn’t be as straightforward.
[SFX: PHOENIX CRY]
[MUSIC: EPIC TRANSFORMATION]
Spreading her hands, she watched as the Ice Phoenix let out one last cry and dove straight for her. In a flash of light, the creature didn’t collide with her but fused into her, its icy form crystallizing into a suit of shimmering blue armor that covered her from head to toe. Now in her complete form, Tia shot into the sky to face the petals head-on.
“They don’t look that scary,” Zane murmured, watching the delicate flowers drift down. Unlike the raw power he’d felt from the last two tribulations, these petals seemed to have no energy at all. They just looked like… flowers.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
“Every single one of those is a nightmare,” Lavender corrected him, her voice suddenly grave. “The Nightmare Tribulation is so difficult because it doesn’t attack the body. It attacks the soul.”
Onscreen, the petals seemed harmless enough.
[SFX: SHATTERING CRYSTAL]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]
A light touch from Tia was enough to shatter one into a million glittering fragments. But with every petal she destroyed, a new nightmare flooded her mind.
A few dozen were manageable, but soon she had smashed through hundreds, and the true nature of the trial began to take its toll. Her face went deathly pale.
With each soft touch of a petal against her armor, her entire body trembled, caught in a battle no one else could see.
