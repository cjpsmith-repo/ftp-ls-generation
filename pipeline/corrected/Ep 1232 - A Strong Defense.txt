Ep 1232 - A Strong Defense

Word count: 1090

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ENERGY FIELD]
Zane’s first round of attacks was just a test run, but that didn’t mean it was anything less than lethal.
[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACKLING]
[SFX: WIND WHOOSHING]
[SFX: RAPID PUNCHES AND KICKS]
The lightning and wind he summoned were razor-sharp, and the shadow clones he controlled moved with deadly precision, each attacking with a flurry of punches and kicks in its own distinct style. It was an overwhelming assault for any opponent.
As the three waves of attacks bore down on her, Sloane’s expression hardened. She raised a hand, pressing the tips of her thumb and forefinger together while her other three fingers remained elegantly raised. 
A faint, yellow light began to pulse from the ring on her little finger.
“Earth Protection,” she murmured.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING POWER]
The light bled from the ring, rapidly coalescing into the shape of a thousand-foot-tall giant that completely enveloped her. The figure radiated a heavy, unshakable power.
[SFX: LIGHTNING IMPACT SIZZLE]
[SFX: WIND BLADE SCRAPING]
[SFX: MARTIAL ARTS STRIKES ON BARRIER]
Zane’s lightning slammed into the golden giant, leaving behind a scorch mark that sizzled and vanished in the blink of an eye. The wind blade that followed only managed to gouge a shallow, inch-deep line before its energy dissipated. 
Even the shadow clones, with their perfectly coordinated martial arts, couldn’t put a single dent in the powerful defense. They couldn’t get anywhere near Sloane, who stood calmly at the giant’s core.
“Now that’s a strong defense,” Zane said, raising an eyebrow. He’d known the five rings on Sloane’s hand were special, but he hadn’t imagined their power would be on this scale. And she’d only used one of them.
After effortlessly blocking his assault, Sloane raised her palm toward him. 
A faint smirk touched her lips as she slammed her hand down.
[SFX: MASSIVE HAND WHOOSH]
The giant mimicked her move perfectly, its colossal hand crashing down toward Zane.
[SFX: EARTH-SHATTERING IMPACT]
[SFX: VIOLENT TREMORS]
An earth-shattering roar echoed through the air as the ground shook violently. Anyone looking down from above would have seen a gigantic palm print carved into the empty plains beside the city, with countless cracks spiderwebbing out from the impact zone as the tremors continued to spread.
[SFX: RAPID WHOOSH]
Zane shot backward, narrowly dodging the blow just as the massive palm landed. He stared at the crater it left behind, but he had no time to recover. 
His jaw tightened, but underneath it, something in him was thrilled. Ever since ascending to the Upper World, finding Elara had been the only thing that mattered. That didn’t mean he’d given up on his own growth — but between constantly running from Celestial Masters hunting him, he’d never once gotten the chance to actually test himself against a true peer. 
The Illusion Battlefield had finally given him that: real geniuses, drawn from every race in the universe, all in one place.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]
Hundreds of thousands of warriors had gathered on the city wall by now, drawn in by the noise. 
“Do you know how rare it is to actually be called a prodigy?” one of them was saying. 
“Every big faction only gets a handful. The ones who make it become Sector Lords one day — maybe even Celestial Masters. Every race pours everything they have into raising theirs.” 
“Yeah, well, that’s exactly why the rest of us Soul Sea Realm warriors never get anywhere,” another muttered.
Despite the force behind it, the city itself hadn’t so much as trembled — only the sound of the impact had carried that far.
The giant was already raising its hand to strike again, and again, and again. From within her shield of light, Sloane’s eyes were fixed on the chaos outside.
[SFX: REPEATED HEAVY IMPACTS]
[SFX: DEBRIS STORM]
The relentless assault churned the earth, kicking up a storm of soil and gravel that at least blunted the force of the blows. But as the ground was cleared away with each successive slam, the impacts became direct.
Zane rolled clear of the first strike, but Sloane’s other hand was already sweeping in low, a flat backhand slap that caught him mid-air and drove him straight into the ground.
“Your strength,” Sloane said, watching him disappear into the dirt, “is weaker than I expected. You’ve only shown me the power of the earth element.”
[SFX: GIANT HAND DIGGING]
She curled her left hand and dragged it through the soil, dredging Zane up out of the crater and cupping him inside the giant’s palm. Then she brought her hands together — slow, deliberate, almost like applause.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS CLAPPING]
Each clap of her own small hands became a deafening boom as the giant’s palms crashed together around him, over and over, the sound rolling out across the plains. Between claps, the soil and gravel packed inside the giant’s palms scattered just long enough to reveal Zane’s battered outline before the hands closed over him again for the next strike.
On the wall, the crowd winced with every impact. “That kid has to be paste by now,” someone said. “That Sloane Kincaid — she’s terrifying.” Then a pause. “Wait... why haven’t I seen any Illusion Points burst out? Shouldn’t he already be dead and back in the city reviving?”
And yet… something wasn’t right. Sloane’s eyes widened.
[SFX: DUST SWIRLING]
Even as the giant’s palms slammed down on him, she could see through the swirling dust that Zane’s aura hadn’t weakened in the slightest. There wasn’t a single visible injury on his body.
*No wonder he killed Norton so easily,* she thought, a frown creasing her brow. *His body… it’s unbelievably strong.*
She changed her strategy. 
[SFX: GIANT HAND SCOOPING]
Raising her left hand, she commanded the giant to scoop Zane up, trapping him in its massive palm. Then, she raised her right hand and brought her index finger down in a pressing motion.
[SFX: MASSIVE FINGER DESCENDING]
[SFX: CONCENTRATED FORCE WHOOSH]
Lying on his back in the giant’s grip, Zane watched as its colossal finger descended upon him, imbued with a terrifying, concentrated force far greater than the palm strikes.
But at that exact moment, he smiled. He raised his own fist to meet the giant’s descending finger.
Compared to the finger, Zane’s fist was practically invisible—the tip of the giant’s digit alone was several times thicker than his entire body. It was a shocking sight. 
To the world, Zane looked like an ant about to be crushed, completely trapped with no way out.
