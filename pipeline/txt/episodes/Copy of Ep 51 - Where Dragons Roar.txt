Ep 51 - Where Dragons Roar
Word Count: 1361
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL LIBRARY]
After passing the Level 2 Archive, Zane took a look around.
Only four other people had made it this far with him. One was a towering, muscle-bound guy who never went anywhere without his greatsword.
Zane already knew the guy was a physical powerhouse with endless stamina, but he hadn't pegged him for having such a strong soul, too. "Figures," he thought. "The Skyreach Sect is crawling with talent."
[AMBIENT: EERIE LABORATORY]
[SFX: PULSING ENERGY]
Up ahead, the Level 3 Refinement Lab came into view, an eerie purple light pulsing from within.
The five of them stepped inside, and right away, everyone but Zane looked green.
One guy, who looked like he was from a Legacy House based on his clothes, went pale as a ghost the second he crossed the threshold.
It was obvious he was struggling against the will of the vengeful beast.
[SFX: PILL SWALLOWING]
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
The student fumbled in his pocket, pulled out a Stimulant, and swallowed it in one gulp. When he opened his eyes a moment later, they were shining with a golden light. "Must be something to boost his soul," Zane realized, helping him fight off the beast's will.
"Guys from the Great Houses have it so easy," Zane muttered, shaking his head. How was a regular person ever supposed to get their hands on an Energy Capsule like that?
The five of them scanned the lab. Inside, the Level 3 training manuals were finally accessible. In the Eastern Territory, all training methods were split into four tiers. Level 1 manuals were a dime a dozen—you could pick one up at any martial arts club.
Level 2 Techniques, though, were much rarer, usually passed down through smaller Houses. The Purple Flash Fist that Zane practiced was a Level 2 technique, and for a house like House Lowell, which wasn't particularly rich or powerful, a manual like that was a closely guarded secret.
[SFX: ATTACK SOUNDS]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Zane quickly discovered that with every physical blow, the shadows were also attacking his soul. A sharp, stabbing pain erupted deep inside his mind. For anyone else, it might have been a fatal blow, but Zane's soul was stronger. Still, the agony was intense.
Each shadow that struck him felt like a spike driving straight into his head. It wasn't physical pain, but spiritual—an attack from which there was no cover, no escape. He had no choice but to endure it.
His soul throbbed with each impact. A single shadow he could handle, but as they came one after another, the pain became unbearable. He felt himself starting to break under the relentless assault.
The spiritual world was a total mystery; he'd never even heard of someone attacking another person's soul before.
Naturally, Zane had no idea how to fight back.
[MUSIC: INTENSE STRUGGLE]
He gritted his teeth, squeezing his eyes shut as the pain intensified, feeling like they were about to pop out of his skull. For a split second, he wanted to give up. But he couldn't. He had to push through. The mental torture he'd endured back at House Lowell, constantly being torn apart and crushed by the Cobalt Dragon's will, was so much worse than this.
"This is nothing," Zane growled to himself, trying to psych himself up. "Just a workout for my soul!"
Shaking his head, he forced himself deeper into the hall, eventually pushing his way into the Level 4 Archive. As he neared a small wooden bookcase, he noticed something strange, even through the haze of pain from the Purple Shadow's attacks: a hidden passageway behind the main hall.
"A passageway?" Zane thought, stunned. "But... isn't Level 4 the end of the line?" He'd been told his whole life there were only four tiers of power methods. He'd never heard of anything higher.
Suddenly, his curiosity got the better of him. Should he check out the passageway and see if there were higher-tier power methods?
He was on the fence, his eyes darting to the Level 4 Power Methods on the bookcase. They were incredibly tempting. Just a little while ago, he would have been thrilled for a chance at a Level 3 manual, and now Level 4 was right in front of him.
[MUSIC: CURIOUS TENSION]
But after a moment of hesitation, his curiosity won out. Most people never even got a chance to step foot in the Level 4 Archive. He'd come this far—how could he not see if there was something more?
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT CORRIDOR]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ECHOING]
With his mind made up, Zane headed for the passageway. It was narrow and plain, undecorated, the floors and walls decayed with time. The second Zane stepped into the ten-foot-long corridor, the attacks from the Purple Shadow vanished.
"Why isn't the vengeful beast's presence here?" he wondered.
[AMBIENT: STONE CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]
Confused but curious, Zane pressed on. At the end of the corridor was a small, circular stone chamber. In the center stood a black monument.
[SFX: REVELATION MOMENT]
Zane's eyes widened. It was a graveyard.
[SFX: STONE CHAMBER SILENCE]
The Celestial Library sat atop this site. The purple light, the shadows, the crushing pressure — it all originated here. This was the Vengeful Beast's tomb. And its Will had been radiating outward for centuries, strong enough to drop grown cultivators at the door.
[SFX: STONE CRACKING]
[MUSIC: SUDDEN TERROR]
The tombstone split open. What emerged was not a shadow — it was the soul body of the Vengeful Beast itself, massive and fully formed, rendered in dense purple light. Its dark eyes fixed on Zane, and even at a distance the pressure of its gaze hit like a hammer to the inside of his skull.
He stepped back carefully. If he could reach the corridor without provoking it—
[SFX: SHADOWS RUSHING]
He looked back. The corridor was sealed. Purple shadows packed the passage wall to wall, pressing toward him like a tide. Trapped — soul body ahead, shadows behind. The shadows had been tearing at him for minutes and he was still standing. The soul body had barely moved and his mind was already starting to crack.
Choose the lesser threat.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: DESPERATE SPRINT]
Zane drove forward into the shadows, taking the soul-spike pain in waves. Behind him the soul body closed fast. He turned his head once — its dark eyes filled his vision, his own pale face reflected back at him.
That was as close as it got.
[SFX: BEAST CLAWING]
[SFX: DRAGON GROWL]
The beast was about to lunge, but it froze mid-motion. Instead, it just clawed at the floor every time the dragon growled. The Wraith's core let out a low, frustrated howl.
A small smile touched Zane's lips. He hadn't realized the shadows and the vengeful beast were terrified of the Cobalt Dragon's growl.
[SFX: FEROCIOUS HOWL]
The core flinched back with every step Zane took toward it. As he advanced, steady and deliberate, the core scrambled back onto the tombstone, letting out one last ferocious howl before retreating into the black stone itself.
Zane could still feel the raw hatred in its final scream. Just moments ago, he'd been seconds from having his soul obliterated. If the Cobalt Dragon hadn't woken up to save him, he'd be gone. But the danger was over now.
[AMBIENT: QUIET STONE CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: CURIOUS EXPLORATION]
A small smile spread across Zane's face as he finally started to explore the stone chamber.
Approaching the bookcase he'd glimpsed earlier beside the black monument, Zane discovered it contained merely two ancient tomes.
"Only two books?" he whispered, his voice echoing in the chamber's stillness. "What could they be?"
[SFX: DUST PARTICLES]
His fingers trembled slightly as he extracted one volume from its resting place. The book felt impossibly old beneath his touch, its cover concealed beneath decades—perhaps centuries—of accumulated dust. Zane brushed his palm across the surface, sending particles dancing through the air like disturbed spirits.
As the dust settled, etched letters emerged from the darkness, revealing themselves in bold, commanding script. Zane's breath caught in his throat as he read the title that materialized before him:
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
Titan Strike.
