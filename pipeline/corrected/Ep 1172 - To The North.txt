Ep 1172 - To The North

Word count: 1194

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
In the upper world, revealing yourself meant death. A single Roaring Token - usable infinitely - was worth killing for, even among Supreme Lords. Yet Zane hadn’t hesitated.
Lavender knew he had The Elysian Keep as insurance, but the Queen of Punishment and numerous Supreme Lords were already hunting him. Zane could match Spirit Supreme Realm experts, but against Spirit Transformation warriors, World Lords, or Supreme Lords, he’d have no escape.
Too impulsive - but that was Zane. He always followed his heart, ready to pay the price.
His gaze fell to the bodies of Cassian, Maren, Taryn, and Paige. “What should we do with them?” he asked quietly.
Lavender let out a soft sigh. “We’ll keep them for now. If I can ever return to the Demon Night, I’ll bury them in the queens’ tomb.”
Burial in the queens’ tomb was the greatest honor a warrior of the Demon Night could receive, a privilege reserved for their most powerful heroes and those who had made incredible sacrifices. Lavender couldn’t give them that honor now, but she was determined to one day. 
Following her instructions, Zane carefully stored the four of them away.
Once he was done, Lavender floated to the edge of the Wheel of Reincarnation, stopping before a spear that was stuck point-down in its surface. 
[SFX: METALLIC RESONANCE]
Cassian had thrown it there just before taking his own life.
“The North Dawn Spear,” she murmured, her voice distant. “Forged from North Dawn crystal. I gave this to him. I can’t believe he was still using it after all this time.”
[SFX: METAL SCRAPING STONE]
Lavender reached out, her fingers closing around the shaft as she pulled it free. She stroked the cool metal, a faint, sad smile touching her lips as a memory flickered behind her eyes. Then, turning, she placed the spear carefully into Zane’s Storage Ring.
[SFX: DEEP SPATIAL HUM]
“North,” Lavender said quietly.
Zane glanced at her. “North?”
She nodded. “Cassian left a message before he died.”
Her voice softened as the memory surfaced. Long ago, during their training, she had asked him where he most wanted to go when it was all over — when he was finally free of obligations and rank and war. He had thought for a moment, then pointed upward, to the brightest star visible from that place. The Northern Twilight Star. He had said it more than once over the years: that when he was finally done with everything, that was where he would go first.
Without hesitation, Lavender turned the Wheel of Reincarnation and set their course for the Northern Twilight Star.
[SFX: LOW RUMBLING ACCELERATION]
But first, there was work to do. The Queen of Punishment would not stop with one wave of attackers — they both knew that. Whatever came next would be worse.
Zane guided the Wheel back to the mouth of the Endless Abyss and sent Freya down into it one more time. She descended to the third and fourth caves — places none of them had stopped to loot during the chaos of their escape. The third cave held defensive treasures left behind by its ancient owner. The fourth held rare forging materials, many of them from the previous cosmic era — things that had long since vanished from the current age. Livingston’s eyes lit up the moment she brought them back.
“These materials—” he began.
“Later,” Zane said. “We need to move.”
[MUSIC: TENSE PREPARATION]
Livingston fell quiet, but his metal fingers were already sorting through the materials with reverent care.
With everything gathered, Zane reached into his robes and drew out a small token. He poured a trace of energy into it.
[SFX: LOW HARMONIC TONE — BUILDING]
The token began to glow. Then, slowly, it softened — like wax left too close to a flame — twisting and warping until it had transformed into a miniature palace no larger than his palm. He crouched and set it gently on the surface of the Wheel.
[SFX: DEEP BOOM]
It expanded rapidly, growing from palm-sized to a structure eighty feet across — still small relative to the vast deck of the Wheel, its walls rising only to chest height from the outside, like a perfectly crafted scale model of something far greater.
Lavender watched without surprise. She had seen this before.
Freya and Livingston had not.
Livingston’s headlights flickered wildly. He leaned forward, staring at the miniature palace with an expression caught somewhere between confusion and alarm. As the greatest weaponsmith of the previous cosmic era, there was no divine artifact he could not read — no material he could not identify, no Law he could not parse. But this token was different. The Laws woven into it were unlike anything he had ever encountered. He opened his mouth, closed it, and opened it again.
“What… is that thing made of?”
Zane didn’t answer. “Come on,” he said, and stepped through the small door.
[SFX: REALITY WARPING WHOOSH]
As the Wheel of Reincarnation cut through the deep black of space, Zane led them toward The Elysian Keep. Freya and Lavender followed him inside, and the moment they crossed the threshold, the world seemed to warp around them. 
What had looked like a small, unassuming palace from the outside was suddenly a place of impossible scale, with towering walls that rose thousands of feet into the air.
Freya’s eyes went wide with wonder. The Elysian Keep was entirely new to her, and she couldn’t begin to guess where Zane had found a place like this. How could a warrior from the Soul Sea Realm possess something so magnificent?
“This place…” Livingston whispered, his face a picture of pure astonishment.
Freya’s curiosity was piqued. She could tell the palace was extraordinary, but she didn’t understand what had left the old puppet so stunned.
Livingston, however, saw something she couldn’t. As his gaze landed on the towering walls, the two beams of light on his head began to flicker erratically.
“Oh my God!”
[SFX: RAPID METAL CLANKING]
The old puppet scurried forward, moving with a speed that seemed impossible for his clanking frame. 
[SFX: METAL SCRAPING STONE]
He threw himself against one of the walls, stretched out a metal claw, and gently scraped a line across a pale brick. The material seemed soft, his claw leaving a shallow groove. 
[SFX: ORGANIC WIGGLING SOUND]
But what was truly astonishing was that, a moment later, the groove began to wiggle and then smoothed itself out, leaving the surface perfectly unblemished.
“This… this is…” Livingston’s voice was practically glowing with excitement. 
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT DISCOVERY]
“A brick made of Radiant Sand!” He stumbled back, staring up at the impossibly high wall. His headlights blazed. “The whole wall… it’s all Radiant Sand. Every brick. All of it!”
Freya tilted her head. “What’s Radiant Sand?” To her, it just looked like a regular brick.
“It’s only the best material in existence!” 
[SFX: FRANTIC METAL CLAW WAVING]
Livingston was completely overwhelmed, waving his claws around frantically. “This is impossible! I’d bet my life nobody in the entire universe has ever gathered this much Radiant Sand. Wow! I think I’m losing my mind!”
