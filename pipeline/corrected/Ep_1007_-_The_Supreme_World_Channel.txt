Ep 1007 - The Supreme World Channel

Word count: 1016

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

Even before stepping onto the teleportation array, Zane noticed its divine textures were unique.

Back in the Heartlands, teleportation arrays were built with runes and powered by Aether crystals. The principle behind them was simple enough. The runes created a teleportation channel connecting to an array somewhere else, drastically cutting down travel time. A flight that used to take warriors several days, like the trip from Oracle City to Cloudhaven, was cut down to less than five minutes.

But this array’s structure was completely different. The divine textures were unfamiliar, but Zane could make an educated guess about their function.

It didn’t just expand outward to form a channel; it seemed to connect to something else entirely. What that was, Zane had no clue.

[SFX: TELEPORTATION ACTIVATION]

The armored warrior activated the array, and the ascending warriors lined up. A flash of white light, and one by one, they stepped into the passage.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL BLUE GLOW]

At first, the ascending passage seemed ordinary enough—just like the ones in the Lower World. The whole channel glowed a brilliant blue.

[SFX: DIMENSIONAL SHIFT]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

But after a short trip through the blue light, they emerged into something else entirely. A massive teleportation channel opened up before them.

“This channel... it’s so stable!” Zane said, his eyes widening in awe.

[AMBIENT: DEEP PURPLE ENERGY RESONANCE]

This new, giant channel was a deep purple. A normal channel was maybe thirty or forty feet wide; this one had to be over a thousand feet across.

But what really shocked Zane was its stability. It was far more solid than a normal channel. The space around them felt frozen, like nothing could possibly break through it.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]

Zane turned the question over in his mind, then directed it inward. “What is this?”

The Cobalt Dragon’s response came with a note of quiet respect. “A Supreme World Channel. You need to be at least a World Lord to build one. The teleportation arrays in the Lower World max out at around a hundred thousand miles before the channel becomes too unstable to hold — you remember the spatial storm you nearly died in, crossing from the Heartlands to the main continent? This is a different order of magnitude entirely. A Supreme World Channel can span an entire Upper World.”

“There’s a fee,” Zane observed.

“There always is. Building one takes enormous effort, and maintaining it costs more. A short hop — two or three million miles — runs one supreme Aether crystal per person. A full cross-world traversal costs a million.” The Cobalt Dragon paused. “All the wealth you accumulated in the Divine Kingdoms wouldn’t cover a single crossing.”

Zane said nothing. He’d thought he was rich.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Just as they entered, two warriors flew over from the other side of the channel to meet them.

They looked down on the warriors from the Lower World with disdain. One of them chuckled. “Can’t take ‘em all in one go. Looks like we’ve got over a thousand new ascendants.”

The armored warrior escorting them frowned, muttering under his breath, “A bunch of useless trash. The test is about to start. How can the Soulbound Sect expect any of these grimy Lower Worlders to pass?”

[SFX: HEAVY BAG DROP]

[SFX: CRYSTAL CLATTER]

Then, he turned to the guards and pulled out a massive bag from his Storage Ring, spilling what had to be a million supreme Aether crystals onto the ethereal platform. “It’s a big group this time,” he said. “How about a discount?”

The guard eyed the glittering pile and smirked. He pushed a small pile of a few hundred crystals back toward the warrior. “Don’t ask for a discount. The Soulbound Sect’s footing the bill, after all.”

A million supreme Aether crystals. To any Soul Sea Realm warrior, it was a staggering fortune.

The Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms was considered wealthy, but their combined national treasuries held less than two hundred thousand supreme Aether crystals. The idea of paying a million just to join the Soulbound Sect was insane.

[SFX: CROWD GRUMBLES]

“I don’t want to join your damn Soulbound Sect!” one warrior finally exploded. “I’ll just be an independent warrior!”

“An independent warrior?” the armored escort chuckled. “Fine by me. All you need is two hundred Sovereign Stones.”

[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]

Two hundred Sovereign Stones. A collective gasp went through the crowd. That was the equivalent of two million supreme Aether crystals.

Each Sovereign Stone was worth ten thousand supreme Aether crystals — a purer, rarer form of solidified Vitality that made the finest crystals from the Lower World look ordinary by comparison.

“That’s insane! Why?!” the warrior yelled, his face red with anger.

The armored warrior’s smile never wavered. “Because you flew into Soulbound Sect territory. You play by our rules now.”

His tone was condescending and all too familiar.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

A hush fell over the Soul Sea Realm warriors. They had struggled and fought their way up from the Lower World to reach this point. They knew exactly what this was.

There was no point in asking why; the answer was painfully obvious. They were just Soul Sea Realm warriors. Rebelling against one of the great sacred places of the Upper World was suicide.

[MUSIC: SOMBER MELODY]

Seeing their downcast faces, the armored warrior showed a sliver of pity. “Look, just suck it up and get to work. Believe it or not, the Soulbound Sect is actually pretty lenient. Some of the other sacred places are a hell of a lot harsher.”

[SFX: WHOOSHING CHANNEL]

An hour passed in silence. Then the armored warriors peeled off through a side tunnel and the group dropped back into an ordinary teleportation channel. A moment later they emerged into open air — and a black mountain range loomed before them, steep and forbidding, stretching as far as the eye could see.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

“This is where you serve,” one of the warriors said. “This range is a resource mine. Low-grade, high-grade, top-grade stones — and at the deepest levels, Sovereign Stones. Your quota is one hundred Sovereign Stones. Can’t manage that? Dig out the equivalent in lesser material — one million supreme Aether crystals’ worth. You don’t leave until the debt is paid.”

Another wave of disbelief rolled through the crowd. Most of them had ascended carrying only a fraction of that. The black mountains just waited.
