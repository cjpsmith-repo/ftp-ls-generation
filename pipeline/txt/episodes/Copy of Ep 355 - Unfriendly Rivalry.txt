Ep 355 - Unfriendly Rivalry

Word count: 1082

[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS]

As the arrogant disciples from the Crimson Sect erupted into mocking laughter at Zane's expense, Sir Kenneth's jaw tightened. He recognized exactly why his young protégé looked so disoriented and distant. The spatial perception ability manifesting in Zane was rare and powerful—something these ignorant fools couldn't begin to comprehend.

"SILENCE!" he thundered, his voice carrying the weight of decades of command.

The laughter died instantly as Kenneth stepped forward, his imposing figure radiating barely contained fury. "You better be damn sure you're better than someone before you laugh at them!" he growled, each word dripping with menace. "The boy you mock so freely might just be the one to humble you all before this day ends."

Then he turned to Zane. "You picked something up about the Spatial Laws in that voidstorm, didn't you? It's normal to be a little disoriented. You'll get your bearings soon enough. Hell, even we old-timers would kill for a shot at the Spatial Laws, but very few ever get a handle on them. You need to build on that. Don't let it go to waste."

Zane nodded. "Got it," he replied, his voice steady despite the lingering disorientation.

Eddy's jaw slackened in disbelief. "What?" Kenneth's tone carried unmistakable certainty, and Eddy knew the veteran cultivator wasn't one for bluffing. Throughout his years in the cultivation world, he had encountered occasional rumors—whispered stories about extraordinary prodigies who somehow managed to grasp the elusive Spatial Laws while inside teleportation tunnels. Rarer still were the near-mythical figures who had survived direct exposure to a voidstorm and come out on the other side with unprecedented insights.

Narrowing his eyes, Eddy scrutinized Zane's face, searching for any sign of deception in the young man's features. After a tense moment, he shook his head dismissively. "I don't buy it, Kenneth!" 

The implications were staggering—to grasp even the faintest hint of Spatial Laws was a privilege beyond measure. Despite Eddy's and Kenneth's considerable advancement and decades of cultivation, neither had ever been given the chance to learn these profound mysteries, much less incorporate them into their training regimens.

Kenneth responded with a casual shrug, his face betraying neither concern nor the need to convince. "Wasn't talking to you, Eddy," he remarked coolly, meeting his gaze with practiced indifference. "And nobody's asking you to believe me." His words hung in the air between them, a subtle challenge wrapped in dismissal.

[SFX: MOCKING LAUGHTER]

"He's full of it! It's impossible!"

"No way someone at Grade Three of the Adept Realm could touch the Spatial Laws!"

After all, the Crimson Sect disciples came from the Heartlands; they were experienced and worldly. Some had heard of the Spatial Laws, but none had ever seen them in action, so they refused to believe Zane had. They figured Kenneth was just running his mouth to save face.

In fact, Eddy thought the exact same thing.

"All talk?" Zane raised an eyebrow. The world was still spinning, but the sarcastic jabs from Eddy and his disciples cut right through the haze. Since Sir Kenneth had his back, he wasn't about to disappoint him.

The words tumbled out of him. "Alright, since everyone from the Crimson Sect feels free to run their mouths, let me, a disciple of the Skyreach Sect from the Eastern Territories, tell you exactly what I think. In my opinion, the Crimson Sect is a joke! You have nine Soul Realm warriors here, and not a single one of them is a match for me! I'll knock all your damn teeth out!"

No one expected Zane to be so blunt. Even Kenneth was taken aback for a second. Heh, he thought. Now this is my kind of kid.

Kenneth had a bold personality himself and couldn't stand yes-men. He was more than pleased; Zane was defending the honor of the Skyreach Sect.

Finn stepped forward before anyone else could move. "Zane is the champion of the All Peaks Competition — the number one disciple of the Skyreach Sect. If you can't get past me first, you don't get to him."
The Crimson disciples stared. Then exchanged glances. The Adept-level kid was their number one?
"Fine," one of them said. "We'll deal with you first."

[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

A short time later, on the spacious sparring platform, Finn and a young man from the Crimson Sect faced each other beneath the vast sky. The tension between them hung in the air.

The young man shook his head, a smirk at the corners of his mouth, his voice dripping with condescension. "You're Finn, right? I'm Alistair Mahoney, and I don't think you're worthy of being my opponent." 

"Why's that?" Finn asked, his voice deliberately flat.

Alistair snorted, drawing himself up to his full height. "Because I'm number one in the Crimson Sect, and you're just number three in the Skyreach Sect."  His hand rested casually on his sword hilt, a gesture meant to intimidate. "You should feel honored that you even get the chance to fight me! Most cultivators would tremble at the opportunity I'm granting you."

[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]

A rare smile touched Finn's normally stoic face. "I agree with what Zane just said. The Crimson Sect is pathetic. And its best disciple? Barely average." With that, Finn drew his Blood-Corroding Sword from its sheath.

[SFX: SWORD DRAWING]

Alistair threw his head back and laughed before drawing his own sword—a green blade that was clearly a middle-grade Mythic Weapon. "This is the Dragon Sword. Middle-grade, Mystic. Since my weapon outclasses yours, I'll do you a favor and show you what real Accomplished Sword Intent looks like!"

[AMBIENT: CROWD CHEERING]

Hearing this, the other disciples from the Crimson Sect went wild. Unlike the Skyreach Sect’s All Peaks Competition, they had The Crimson Crucible. In this year's Crucible, Alistair had been unstoppable, demolishing all his opponents with his Accomplished Sword Intent.

"Yeah, Alistair! Show these guys what real skill is!"

"Ha! I bet they're about to lose their minds!"

"Alistair is only nineteen, and he's already mastered Accomplished Sword Intent! The guy's going to be a legendary swordsman. What he's done is insane, even for The Heartlands!"

Zane, Patrick, and Finn just glanced at each other, confused.

To them, Accomplished Sword Intent wasn't anything special, so they couldn't figure out why everyone was freaking out about it. 

Was the skill level of the top dog from The Crimson Crucible really this weak? If so, Finn was about to make this Alistair jerk wish he had never been born.
