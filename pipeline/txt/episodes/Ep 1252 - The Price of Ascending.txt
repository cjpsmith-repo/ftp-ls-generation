Ep 1252 - The Price of Ascending

Word count: 1157

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: BODY IMPACT AGAINST TREE]
The force of the explosion slammed Aurora’s back against the tree trunk, and a sharp cry of pain escaped her lips. If she’d known he was going to deal with the ants *like this*, she never would have asked him to try.
[SFX: DEBRIS SETTLING]
After the dust settled, the group cautiously emerged from behind the tree to survey the damage. 
[SFX: MULTIPLE FOOTSTEPS ON WOOD]
[SFX: RUSTLING MOVEMENT]
They scrambled onto a wide branch, their legs still shaky. 
Except for Aurora, Amber, and Rowan, everyone else was pale with shock. Even from this distance, the blast had left them with minor injuries, and a few of them felt their internal energy lock up from the impact. 
It was a good thing they’d all stocked up on healing pills; they were popping them now, trying to recover. 
A moment later, they all froze, stunned by what they were seeing.
“You’ve got to be kidding me…” one of them whispered.
“How is he even standing?”
Zane stood firm on a branch right in the middle of the blast zone, completely unscathed. 
Aurora’s lips tightened as she looked up at him. She couldn’t help but pout at the sheer unfairness of it all. They were both Soul Sea Realm warriors—so why was the gap between them so massive?
Amber, on the other hand, just let out a soft sigh. She wasn’t surprised. This was exactly what she’d expected. *He’s grown so much in just seven years,* she thought, her gaze fixed on him. *Now I’m the one looking up to him.*
With the Blazebelly Ants vaporized, the path ahead was clear. 
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS ON BRANCHES]
What should have been a grueling, hours-long trek through the eighth branch took them less than five minutes, all thanks to Zane. The group stepped onto the platform at the end of the branch and continued their climb.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL POOL CHAMBER]
Meanwhile, in the alliance, a small figure climbed out of an ascending pool in the Trinity Realm. 
[SFX: WATER SPLASHING]
[SFX: DRIPPING WATER]
Water dripped from her body, but a sudden gust of frigid air flash-froze the droplets into tiny ice shards that clung to her skin. 
[SFX: ICE CRACKLING]
[SFX: ICE SHARDS TINKLING INTO WATER]
With a sharp shake, she sent the ice tinkling back into the pool below.
“The air here…” Tia murmured, looking around. “It’s buzzing with energy.”
Eager to explore, she started to walk away from the edge of the pool.
[SFX: ARMORED FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
“You, you, and you,” an armored warrior barked, pointing. “Stop right there. Nobody moves.”
[MUSIC: OPPRESSIVE TENSION]
Tia’s eyebrows shot up, her eyes flashing with defiance for a split second before she caught the sheer power rolling off him. He was a master, easily in the late stage of the Soul Sea Realm. She immediately backed down, obediently joining a crowd of other warriors who had also just ascended from the Lower World.
They had all arrived with high hopes for what awaited them, completely unaware that the competition here was even more brutal than what they’d left behind. 
“All ascenders, follow me,” the warrior called, falling into step ahead of them. “You’re standing in the Jadeheart Sanctuary, a Tier-Ten Holy Land. Cross an Upper World at full Soul Sea Realm speed, and it’ll still take you a hundred years. Remember that, before you get any ideas about wandering off.”
[SFX: MURMURING CROWD REACTION]
A ripple of disbelief ran through the group. 
Tia felt her stomach drop. She was Master of Cloud Hall—her cultivation had finally broken through to the Soul Sea Realm after years of hard climbing, all so she could ascend and find her husband. She’d left behind their son, Gavin, barely seven years old, to do it. Now, hearing the true scale of the search, that goal suddenly felt like it was slipping through her fingers.
*Zane’s talent was probably enough to make him a key fighter in some tenth-tier sacred place,* she thought, a wave of despair washing over her. But the Upper World was vast, filled with countless supreme worlds. She had no idea which one he’d landed in.
“So what happens to us now?” one of the ascenders called out.
“You’ll be mining,” the warrior said flatly. “The Illusion Battlefield opened recently, and it pulled half our workforce straight into it—any Soul Sea Realm warrior can enter, no exceptions, and our own deacons are furious they’re barred from going themselves. So the mine’s short-handed, and that’s where you come in.”
“How long do we have to dig?” another ascender asked, unease creeping into her voice.
“Depends on your strength,” the warrior said with a short laugh. “Lucky, talented ones clear it in three to five years. Some poor souls are still down there after a hundred.”
Was she really going to be stuck mining here for decades? If she’d known this was her fate, she never would have ascended. She would have stayed in the Lower World with Gavin.
But it was too late for regrets. 
[AMBIENT: ECHOING STONE CORRIDOR]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS MARCHING]
The armored warriors herded them toward a teleportation tunnel, moving them along like cattle.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS STOPPING]
Halfway through their journey, another guard entered the tunnel and stopped directly in front of Tia. 
“You. Stop. You won’t be mining.”
“Huh?” she asked, completely thrown.
The warrior just shook his head. “Follow me.”
Her eyes darted around, trying to make sense of what was happening. Everything in this Upper World was so strange. Still, not having to mine had to be a good thing, right?
[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WONDER]
[AMBIENT: VAST CITY ATMOSPHERE]
Tia was separated from the group and led through the shimmering teleportation tunnel. After a long walk, they emerged into a sprawling city of impossibly tall spires. Her eyes went wide with wonder. 
[SFX: MASSIVE DOOR OPENING]
Soon, she was guided into a hall so vast its ceiling was lost in shadow.
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD OF WOMEN]
Dozens of women were already gathered there, and every single one of them was at the Soul Sea Realm. Tia took the chance to talk to a few and quickly learned they were all newly ascended, just like her. They’d all been brought here with no explanation. Leaving wasn’t an option; armored guards stood posted at every exit.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DREAD]
That’s when a cold knot of dread formed in her stomach. Something was very wrong.
No one knew why they had been brought here, so all they could do was wait and focus on their training. 
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
For half a month, Tia and the other women did just that, until one day, three young men walked into the hall. 
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[SFX: POWERFUL AURA PRESSURE]
The air grew heavy with their presence. They were all masters at the Spirit Supreme Realm.
