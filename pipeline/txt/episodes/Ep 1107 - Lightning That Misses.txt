Ep 1107 - Lightning That Misses

Word count: 1112

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
[SFX: HOWLING WIND]
The wind howled, a relentless force that whipped around the onlookers. Everyone stared, dumbfounded, at the bizarre spectacle unfolding before them.
A torrent of questions flooded Zane’s mind. *Is this my luck?* he thought, his doubt spiraling. *A person’s luck is something they’re born with. Does that mean this giant has been with me since birth? Through the Eastern Territories, the Heartlands, the main continent, the Divine Kingdom… even my ascension? Why would my luck look like… this?*
[SFX: CHEWING SOUNDS]
The golden, ethereal serpents—Luck-Devouring Snakes—still hung in the air, methodically chewing away at the giant silhouette.
There were eight of them in all.
The Lord of the Sunset World was completely bewildered. In the Upper World, they had a deep understanding of luck. It was an invisible, intangible force, but they knew it was there, and they even had ways to influence it. But fate was supposed to be formless. To see it take on a physical, visible shape was unheard of. 
And the Luck-Devouring Snake was supposed to be a myth, a ridiculous campfire story about a creature that eats good fortune. Seeing it happen for real was one thing; seeing it happen to Zane was something else entirely.
Something about the giant’s outline nagged at Zane. He’d seen this shape before—he was almost sure of it. But where, or when, or who it belonged to, he couldn’t say. The memory stayed just out of reach.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
Just as the shock began to settle in, the giant silhouette moved.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
[SFX: EXPLOSION OF DUST]
The wind died down in the immediate vicinity, making the giant’s form startlingly clear. It raised a massive hand and brought it down hard on its own body, right where the creatures were feeding. In the next instant, one of the golden Luck-Devouring Snakes was crushed, exploding into a shower of glittering dust.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
The Lord of the Sunset World’s jaw went slack. He stared, wide-eyed, his mind a complete blank. The other palace leaders looked just as stunned, watching a scene that defied their wildest imagination. Even the wisest among them had no framework for what they were seeing.
[SFX: REPEATED IMPACTS]
The giant’s arm swung again, slapping its own chest. A second snake burst into golden powder. Then a third, a fourth, and a fifth, each one obliterated with a resounding, silent impact.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Six. Seven.
That left one. But the last snake was gone from view—it had burrowed straight into the giant’s own body. The giant reached in, almost casually, and drew it back out between two massive fingers. A final, silent crush, and the eighth snake scattered into golden dust like the rest.
The powder didn’t just fall. It drifted toward Zane, settling over him, sinking in as though it belonged there.
The palace leaders were speechless for a long moment before they broke into hushed, urgent whispers. To them, the Heavenly Tribulation was one of the absolute forces of the universe. It was infallible. How could the Heavens possibly make a mistake? Zane’s luck was proving to be so unbelievably strong that not a single bolt of lightning had even struck him yet. The whole thing was just too strange.
“All eight of them—absorbed, just like that,” one of the palace masters murmured. “The stronger something is, the more good fortune tends to cling to it. I wonder if there’s some benefit in taking those things into yourself.”
No one had an answer. Luck-Devouring Snakes belonged to legend, not to anyone’s actual experience—nobody in the gathered crowd had the faintest idea what absorbing one, let alone eight, might actually do.
As the wind finally died and the giant’s outline faded into nothing, Zane let out a slow breath. Does that count as surviving my first tribulation? He turned the thought over, almost calm now—
—and then the sky cracked open again.
[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]
Purple-black lightning tore down out of the clouds, thick as a dragon’s body, and slammed into the ground a few feet from where Zane sat. The impact gouged a crater into the earth.
It had missed.
Another bolt came down on his right—just as close, just as violent. It missed as well.
“Lightning that misses?” someone in the crowd said, baffled. “I’ve never heard of such a thing.”
More bolts followed, one after another, each one carrying enough force to level an ordinary Soul Sea Realm warrior. Each one landed just outside the edge of where Zane sat. None of them so much as grazed him. Before long, the ground around him was a ring of smoking craters—and Zane, at the center of it, remained untouched.
“His fate’s been strengthened,” one of the palace masters said slowly. “Has to be—those Luck-Devouring Snakes did this.”
“Permanent, or temporary?” another wondered. “I don’t even know which possibility worries me more.”
“How could something like that possibly be permanent?” a third cut in. “If it were, it’d be terrifying beyond words.”
They went back and forth, none of them quite able to settle on an answer. The Heavenly Tribulation was supposed to be the most precise force in the universe—how could it simply fail to land, over and over?
A flicker of surprise crossed Zane’s own face as he dodged another arc of lightning. He couldn’t explain it either, but he could feel it—a sudden, overwhelming surge of good fortune.
[MUSIC: SUDDEN DANGER]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
Yolande seemed to have the same thought. She stood abruptly, her eyes fixed on Zane as he navigated the tribulation. With a flick of her wrist, she sent an invisible wave of energy surging toward him. The strike held only a thousandth of her true power, but it was more than enough to be lethal.
But then, something impossible happened.
[SFX: VOIDSTORM ERUPTION]
[AMBIENT: COSMIC DISTORTION]
Before Yolande’s attack could even reach Zane, the very air in its path seemed to twist and tear. A voidstorm erupted from nowhere, a swirling vortex of nothingness that slammed into her palm strike and utterly neutralized it.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
The other palace leaders saw it, and their confusion deepened into sheer disbelief.
Zane was still busy dodging lightning, completely oblivious to the fact that his life had just been saved by a cosmic anomaly. The voidstorm couldn’t have come from him; it had just… formed. But why? 
The Orange Sea Realm was a shattered realm, but years of management by the Alliance had made it one of the most stable, predictable places in existence. The chance of a spontaneous voidstorm appearing there was practically zero.
And yet, one had.
