Ep 2 - Kill Them with Kindness

[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
The training hall plunged into silence as Perrin Lowell strode in, his footsteps reverberating against ancient stone walls that had witnessed generations of Lowell warriors honing their craft. Today, however, marked a sinister departure from tradition. Raw power emanated from him in palpable waves—stolen power that clung to him like ill-fitting finery.
"You sense it, don't you, cousin?" Perrin's predatory gaze locked onto Zane among the huddled slaves. "That's the essence of true potential. That's the birthright you'll never reclaim."
Bile rose in Zane's throat as Perrin's lips twisted into a triumphant smirk that promised further humiliation.
[SFX: CRYSTAL POWER HUMMING]
"You know, our family has two Aether Crystals. And, well, I helped myself to one a little while ago," Perrin Lowell said, puffing out his chest. "This thing is incredible. The raw power inside just flooded my body, pushing me straight from the Strength Level to the Bone Refining Level. And get this—that was just from one-tenth of its power! The rest is still humming inside me, feeding my muscles. I'm just a hair away from Core Strength, and after that, the Marrow Refining Level will be a cakewalk!"
Zane gritted his teeth. Those two crystals were House Lowell's most valuable treasure, sacred heirlooms left behind by their ancestors.
They were kept in a sacred place known only to a select few in the family. Most weren't even allowed to look at them, let alone use them.
There are five levels to physical training, starting with the Skin Refining Level. From there, a warrior moves to the Strength Level, and then the Bone Refining Level. The final two are the Core Strength Level and the Marrow Refining Level. Mastering each stage is a lifetime's work for most people. It's considered a miracle for someone to even reach the third stage—Bone Refining—before turning thirty.
Anyone who did was hailed as a true talent by the entire House.
And according to the old rules, only those who reached that third stage before the age of sixteen were worthy of using an Aether Crystal.
Someone who could reach the Bone Refining Level before sixteen would be a prodigy, powerful beyond measure. But in centuries, no one in all of Castermere County had ever accomplished such a feat.
Which is why the Aether Crystals had sat untouched for the last three hundred years.
[MUSIC: HEROIC FLASHBACK]
Before his world fell apart, Zane Lowell had been that prodigy. At just fourteen, he'd already reached the peak of the Strength Level. His talent and drive had earned him praise from the king of Castermere County himself, who had called Zane the future of House Lowell, a generational talent.
[SFX: VIOLENT IMPACT]
[SFX: GROANS OF PAIN]
But at that critical moment, his own family imploded. His father was murdered by his own brothers, and Zane was cast out, forced into servitude—becoming a prisoner and a human punching bag. Unable to train, he'd lost any chance of ever claiming the crystal.
Perrin, on the other hand, was painfully ordinary.
He'd barely scraped his way to the Strength Level by sixteen. He had no right to the Aether Crystals, but he'd taken one anyway.
That Aether Crystal should have been Zane's. Instead, this talentless oaf had stolen it.
Even after two years of trying to accept his fate, Zane's heart was anything but calm. A fresh wave of rage washed over him.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
"Perrin Lowell, you son of a bitch! How dare you defy the ancestors' rule and take the sacred crystal for yourself?"
"Hah! You're nothing but a bug, a complete nobody," Perrin sneered. "I've reached the peak of the Bone Refining Level. I can lift a thousand pounds with one hand. You're a slave I could crush with my thumb. My training's done for the day, and I feel like celebrating by finding a new punching bag."
Ignoring Zane completely, Perrin pointed at a random slave in the crowd.
"You'll do."
[SFX: CROWD PARTING]
He hadn't chosen Zane, but a trembling, middle-aged man. The man's eyes went wide with terror when Perrin's finger landed on him. The thick leather armor he wore might absorb a normal blow, but it would be useless against someone who'd reached the Bone Refining Level. A dark stain spread across the front of the man's trousers as he began to shake uncontrollably.
[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]
Perrin took a deep breath and settled into the opening stance of the Purple Flash Fist—a martial art exclusive to House Lowell. Showing off, he channeled his inner strength, and a faint purple aura enveloped his body.
"Please, young master! Mercy, I beg you!"
Seeing the power gathering around Perrin, the slave's eyes filled with pure desperation. He knew a single punch would kill him. He collapsed to his knees, sobbing and pleading for his life.
[SFX: KNEES ON STONE]
"Boom!"
[SFX: PUNCH]
[SFX: BODY SLAMMING AGAINST WALL]
Perrin, of course, paid no attention to a slave's begging. He drove his fist straight into the man's chest. The leather armor crumpled like paper. The man's body shot backward, slamming hard against the training hall wall before slumping to the ground in a lifeless heap.
Perrin had killed him with a single blow.
"Such power, young master! No one in our generation can match you!"
"The young master will bring glory to House Lowell! He'll carry the family on his back!"
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
A chorus of praise, equal parts fear and admiration, erupted from the other youths. Perrin soaked it in, a smug look on his face, before turning his vicious gaze back to Zane.
Zane took an involuntary step back. He was far stronger than the man who now lay dead, but even he knew he couldn't survive a direct hit from Perrin.
Perrin laughed, stepping forward and jabbing a hard finger into Zane's shoulder. Twice.
"Relax. I'm not going to kill you. Not yet," his voice low and menacing. "I want you alive. I want you to watch me and know, every single day, that I, Perrin Lowell, am so much better than you ever were."
Perrin fell silent and started to walk away. He paused at the door, turning back as if a thought had just struck him. "Oh, right. I almost forgot."
"I heard our dear cousin, Elara, isn't doing so well at the Skyreach Sect. Word is, she pissed off someone important. Got herself sent to Infernal Peak as punishment. But don't you worry," he said, a cruel grin spreading across his face. "Once I reach the Core Strength Level, I'll go rescue her myself." He let out a sharp, ugly laugh.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT INTENSIFIES]
The words hit Zane like a physical blow. His chest tightened, and his heart hammered against his ribs. Elara was his sister.
'Infernal Peak.' The name alone was a death sentence. No one returned from that place unchanged—if they returned at all.
[MUSIC: DETERMINATION THEME]
As the guards dragged him back to his cell, something cracked inside Zane—not his resolve, but the chains he'd placed on his own power. For two years, he'd accepted his fate. For two years, he'd been a slave.
But no more.
[SFX: EMBER IGNITING]
His sister needed him. And somewhere deep in his core, in a place Perrin and his family couldn't touch, the last ember of his cultivation stirred to life."
