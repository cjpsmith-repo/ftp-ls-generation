Ep 889 - Divine Emperor’s Secret Land

Word count: 1147

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING MARKETPLACE]

The mountains were gone, and somehow, in the span of a few minutes, that catastrophic fact had been filed away and replaced by something more immediately interesting.

Zane was pulling priceless ores out of his Storage Ring, and the Soul Sea masters were reaching for their Aether crystals.

From what Lavender had told him, Aether crystals were a primary currency in the upper world, so when these powerful Soul Sea Realm warriors started offering him mountains of the stuff for his ores, Zane wasn’t about to refuse.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

Just as he wrapped up the thought, a warrior who practiced the Water Law stepped forward. “I could really use this Water Crystal,” he said, his eyes fixed on the ore. “Thirty thousand supreme Aether crystals for it. What do you say?”

[SFX: COINS CLINKING]

Thirty thousand was a serious offer, and Zane agreed without a second thought. The first deal was done.

His Storage Ring was a treasure trove, with individual pieces fetching anywhere from thirty to fifty thousand supreme Aether crystals. A few of the rarest ones could even top a hundred thousand. These warriors were loaded, and they could afford his prices. 

If Zane sold everything he had, he was looking at a haul of nearly three million. He was about to become richer than entire kingdoms.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]

Once the ores were sold off, Zane pulled out the Soul Cores and presented them to the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom. These things were the keys to the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. As far as Zane was concerned, they were useless to him personally—he had no idea how they worked—but they were his ticket inside. The whole reason these masters had braved The Feather Emperor’s Island was to hunt Stone-Eater Bugs, hoping for just one or two cores. They’d hit the jackpot, walking away with over a hundred, which meant a sizable expedition could now explore the secret land.

Before the Soul Cores were even fully distributed, the Holy Maiden walked toward him. Zane’s eyes narrowed. She was his only real enemy among the four kingdoms — it was largely because of her that War King York had come after him in the first place.

“What do you want?” he asked flatly.

[SFX: CRYSTAL SHIMMER]

She didn’t answer immediately. Instead, she held up a diamond-shaped purple crystal and pointed it at him.

Zane tensed. “What is that?”

“Just a recording,” she said calmly. “Nothing more.”

Before Zane could object, the White-Haired Priest explained: the crystal was a recording vessel that could pass through the Reverse Spirit Channel.

As Holy Maiden, she had to report the warrior who had opened thirty-five lotus petals—nine in the first layer, twenty-six in the second.

Zane smiled. That explained everything. The sudden warmth. The hostile masters now treating him like family.

Zane let the crystal record him without argument, though he wondered which branch of the human race in the upper world would receive this. Vesper’s people? Some other force? He didn’t know enough to guess—and decided not to ask.

“Alright!” War King Wind’s voice boomed. “Now that the Soul Cores are distributed, it’s time for the Divine Emperor’s Secret Land! Zane, you’re with me!” He laughed, pulling Zane to his side.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

[SFX: CROWD GRUMBLES]

A current of grumbling passed through the other Soul Sea Realm masters. Everyone knew he was just trying to curry favor with Zane to get a leg up, and they wouldn’t put it past him to offer up his own daughter to seal the deal. But they also couldn’t deny that Wind had defended Zane earlier, so it was only natural for Zane to stick with him.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]

War King Wind wrapped Zane, Fiona, Callan, and the rest of their group in a stream of Vitality and swept them into the sky. The other Soul Sea masters rose alongside them, carrying their own charges. 

Randy and his team were among those brought along — this time, with so many Soul Cores in hand, there were enough spots for crown princes and select princes as well. The other god-tier geniuses who hadn’t made the cut had already begun the long journey home.

[MUSIC: LIGHT COMEDIC STRINGS]

As they flew, War King Wind talked. He asked about Zane’s background — where he was from, his family, his sect. It was the kind of conversation a matchmaker has, dressed up as small talk, and Zane could see exactly where it was going.

Then the War King asked directly: “Are you married?”

“No,” Zane said. “But there is a promise.”

War King Wind’s face fell. He’d been building toward a proposal. A princess couldn’t be a concubine — that much was non-negotiable, no matter how bright the young man’s future was.

It seemed everyone was impressed with Zane these days, including Fiona. She’d had a massive crush on him ever since he defeated her at the West Dragon River, but she was far too shy to act on it. Her father, on the other hand, was shamelessly trying to play matchmaker, which only made her shrink with embarrassment.

“Father,” Fiona said, her voice tight, ears burning red. “Please stop.”

The War King just smiled benevolently. “What’s wrong with asking? I’m thinking of you.”

Fiona glared at him. When she’d heard Zane say he’d already made a promise, something small and involuntary had flickered across her face — and she was furious at herself for it.

[AMBIENT: DARK OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: FOREBODING DRUMS]

Thankfully, a looming black island appearing on the horizon broke the awkward tension. Even from a distance, they could all feel a dark, oppressive energy rolling off it in waves. This was the Divine Emperor’s Secret Land, one of the four hidden lands of the Sacred Feather Sea.

[AMBIENT: DESOLATE GRAVEYARD]

[SFX: DISTANT ECHOING WHISPERS]

The place was less an island and more a graveyard—a final resting place for millions of dragon-blooded whose corpses were scattered across its surface. After the dragon-blooded’s sacrificial ritual, every living creature in the sea had been vaporized into a bloody mist that formed a powerful curse. The corpses, not being truly alive, were left untouched.

Honestly, if it weren’t for the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land hidden within, the island would be worthless. Any treasures buried with the dead were trinkets to a Soul Sea Realm master. 

The real problem was that the blood sacrifice’s power was so concentrated within the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land that even they couldn’t survive it. Their only way in was to use the Soul Cores to possess the bodies of the dead dragon-blooded, and that was exactly why they were here.

[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STOPPING]

Just as they set foot on the island, they realized they weren’t alone. Another group of Soul Sea Realm masters was already there, waiting.
