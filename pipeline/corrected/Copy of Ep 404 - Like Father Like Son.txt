Ep 404 - Like Father, Like Son

Word count: 1186

[AMBIENT: CELEBRATORY CROWD]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]

Surprisingly, most of the disciples who dared to take the Death Road made it out of the Cloud Road alive. The outcome was a shock, with Zane finishing in first place. But for the leaders of the Luminous Sect and Providence Sect, they were just relieved their disciples had survived at all. All things considered, it was a good day for most.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

While everyone else celebrated, the head of the Duskfall Sect looked grim. His brow furrowed. "Wait a minute," he demanded, his voice tight. "Dermot isn't back. Where is he?"

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SOMBER CELLO]

The mention of Dermot's name cast a heavy silence over the disciples. They all turned to the Duskfall leader, but no one dared to speak. Frank glanced around the silent crowd, and when it was clear no one else would, he finally broke the tension. "The Death Road took him," Frank said grimly. A Nether Phantom swallowed him whole while he was picking an herb.

As the disciples had emerged from the Cloud Road one by one, the Duskfall leader had felt a growing dread. With each new face that wasn't Dermot's, his certainty grew. He’d already known the truth in his gut, but he needed to hear it aloud. Frank’s words were just the confirmation he’d been dreading.

It was the brutal reality of a warrior's life: even the strongest could be snuffed out in an instant by a single misstep. Dermot had been brave enough to follow Frank onto the Death Road, but as the fourth-ranked disciple in his sect, he just didn't have the strength to survive it.

Taking the Death Road had been a massive gamble for him. If he’d pulled it off, he could have secured a top-six spot in the final rankings. But luck wasn't on his side, and not even being on Frank’s team could save him. He’d rolled the dice and lost his life.

The leader of the Duskfall Sect was deeply disappointed, but there was nothing to be done.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FLUTE]

Unlike the news of Zane’s victory, Frank’s story about the Drake piqued the curiosity of Toby and the other sect leaders. The story just didn't add up. How could Zane have survived a fall into a Drake's mouth without a scratch, let alone finish ahead of everyone else?

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

Even Toby, with all his power, would avoid a full-grown Drake at all costs. He might survive an encounter, but not without serious injury. So how had Zane escaped completely unharmed? Maybe it was just dumb luck, but even then, he should have been torn up, not fresh enough to claim first place. The only logical explanation was some kind of hidden connection to the creature.

[AMBIENT: TENSE HALL]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Sir Kenneth was on the edge of his seat, waiting for Toby to press the issue so they could all finally hear the truth from Zane himself.

[SFX: FOOTSTEP]

But before Toby could even ask, Zane stepped forward. He bowed respectfully. "Elder Toby, I apologize. It's not that I won't tell you, but I swore an oath to the other disciple not to reveal what happened."

It was obvious Zane wasn't going to budge, and he didn't seem to be lying about the oath, either. And just like that, Frank's attempt to smoke out the truth went up in flames. Zane saw right through the petty little trap and thought it was pathetic.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE FLUTE]

Toby studied Zane for a long moment, then took a deep breath. "Zane, I respect your decision to honor your oath, and I won't force the issue. But I must ask: who was this disciple that pulled you into the Drake's maw? Was he truly from Cloud Hall?"

Zane knew that this, he couldn't hide. The security of Cloud Hall was at stake.

"He said he was from Cloud Hall, and I believe him," Zane replied with a slight shake of his head. "But he didn't tell me much else about who he was."

Toby sighed. "The Cloud Road is a test, meant to select our finest disciples. But I also believe that everyone is entitled to their secrets. I'll let this go for now, Zane. I only hope that one day, you'll be able to give us an answer."

Zane nodded and offered a small, relieved smile. As he stepped back, he shot a glance at Frank.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

Frank’s face fell. He watched Toby politely drop the subject, then saw the flicker of a smug look on Zane's face. He seethed. It was completely unfair. He couldn't believe Zane had gotten off so easily.

What Frank failed to grasp was that the game had changed. In surviving the trial and taking first place, Zane had become Toby's new prize. His potential and value had now far surpassed Frank's. Toby wasn't about to alienate a rising star by forcing him to reveal his secrets. For Toby, there was a time and place for everything, and this wasn't it. Besides, with Zane now counted among Cloud Hall's elite, it was wise for Elder Toby to stay on his good side.

Elder Toby's word was final. The subject was closed, and no one would learn what really happened to Zane inside the Drake.

Once things settled down, the disciples gathered behind their leaders to wait for any remaining stragglers to return from the Cloud Road.

[SFX: FAST RUNNING]

A few hours later, a dark figure shot out from a distant ruin, moving so fast it was impossible to identify them. Toby gave a slight nod, acknowledging the newcomer. But his attention quickly shifted back to the day's real anomaly: Zane.

Watching him from across the clearing, Toby felt a prickle of unease. He knew Emanuel’s temper all too well, and the man was not the type to accept someone outperforming his precious son. Toby worried Frank's father Emanuel was about to make a scene and try to humiliate Zane.

Sir Kenneth noticed it too, but as a steward, he was powerless to intervene with Emanuel present. He could only hope Toby would step in before things went too far.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

Just then, Frank leaned over and whispered something to Emanuel. A cool, calculating smile spread across Emanuel’s face as he started walking toward Zane.

He still couldn't wrap his head around it. The kid who'd beaten his son was a nobody, a mere Fourth-Level Adept. It was the most ridiculous thing he'd ever heard. After Frank’s report, Emanuel was convinced Zane had only won through a lucky break.

[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL DRUMS]

Emanuel smiled at Zane, his tone slick.  "So, Frank tells me your name is Zane. Congratulations on winning first place. As one of Cloud Hall's new elites, you'll be receiving our focused attention. However, my son shared some… concerning details about your trial. For the good of Cloud Hall," he continued, his voice dripping with false concern, "I believe you owe us a full account. Explain to us exactly how you managed to finish first."
