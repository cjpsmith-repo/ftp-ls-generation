Ep 925 - Fact-Checking

Word count: 1042

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Vorn stared at the young woman before him, his mind reeling. 

A Golden Celestial Paragon? Impossible.

It wasn’t just upper-world arrogance, a reflexive dismissal of anyone from the Lower Worlds. He knew better than that. Countless beings from millions of lesser worlds had ascended, their talent and warrior spirit undeniable. Many had gone on to achieve incredible things, some even becoming World Lords or Supreme Lords. But the reality was, a massive gap separated warriors from the two realms—a fundamental disadvantage baked into their training from the moment they were born.

The energy of heaven and earth, for instance, was exponentially more potent in the upper world. It only made sense that creatures nourished by it would be stronger. Add to that the vast differences in resources, inherited knowledge, and martial traditions, and the conclusion was obvious. They simply couldn't believe what Dorothea was claiming.

[MUSIC: TENSE PIANO]

“Dorothea, you’re forgetting the most important detail,” Delroy pressed, cutting through the stunned silence. “She claims a Demi-Immortal recognized her, but I don’t sense the Mark on her. At all. She’s lying.”

A ripple of understanding went through the group. They’d been so thrown by Dorothea’s assertion that they’d missed the obvious. To earn that title, a warrior’s name had to appear on the second row of the Talent Tablet or higher. When that happened, the tablet secretly bestowed the Mark of the Demi-Immortal Race upon their body. The fact that they couldn’t sense it on this woman meant one of two things: she was either lying or she’d failed the test completely.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ATMOSPHERE]

Dorothea nodded, her own conviction wavering. “I know. I didn’t sense it either. But this body isn’t hers—the soul inside is just borrowing it.” 

With everyone arguing against her, she was starting to doubt her own judgment. The evidence seemed to fit, but the absence of the Mark was a fatal flaw in her theory. Without it, the woman couldn't possibly be a Golden Celestial Paragon.

Throughout all of this, Zane said nothing. Fighting off seven Spirit Supreme warriors on his own was never a realistic option — not in his own body, let alone Brianna's. He listened, and he thought.

There was only one way out of this. He had to prove it. Fortunately, he hadn't been lying.

“We can settle this easily enough,” a tall, sturdy man said. “We just need to find the soul's real body and check for the Mark.”

Before anyone could respond, Zane spoke. “My real body is outside the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land,” he said. “If you don't believe me, come with me.”

The burly man's eyes moved to the Soul Sea masters standing frozen nearby. He waved a dismissive hand. “What about these rats? Too much trouble to drag them along. Just kill them and be done with it.”

The young man in black didn't wait for a second opinion. He drew his broadsword and walked toward War King Wind and the others, black blade energy already spreading along the flat of the blade.

[SFX: BLADE HUMMING]

The Soul Sea masters couldn't move. Couldn't speak. They could only watch him come, anguish and fury burning in their eyes.

Zane's golden pupils flashed. “Stop.”

The young man in black didn't stop. The blade rose.

“If you find the Tower of Crystal,” Zane said, his voice cold and level, “and I prove I have the gold title — what then?”

The blade held. The young man in black looked at him. Something in Zane's tone — the complete absence of doubt — made him pause.

“Oh?” He smiled slowly. “You sound very sure of yourself.”

“I am,” Zane said.

“And if you're lying?”

“Then there's no one in this world who can stop you,” Zane said. “Do whatever you want — with me, and with all of them. You won't have any trouble.”

[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY SURGE]

As he spoke, a thin line sliced across the sky, stretching from the heart of the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land all the way to the horizon.

Dorothea stepped forward. “Stand down,” she said to the young man in black. “Verify it first.”

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

He held for a moment longer. Then he lowered the blade — and in one sharp motion drove it sideways, slashing a line of black energy dozens of feet into the ground that tore all the way to the edge of the Secret Land.

“Fine,” he said. “Let's try it your way, Dorothea.”

[SFX: MAGICAL RELEASE]

The flower in Dorothea’s right eye spun gently, and the paralyzing force that held War King Wind and the others dissipated. The possessed corpses were free. Even so, none of the Soul Sea Realm masters dared to run. The bodies they inhabited couldn't channel Vitality, leaving them unable to fly or even move quickly. Against seven masters from the Spirit Supreme Realm, escape was just a faster way to die.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

“Let’s go,” Delroy said coolly, his eyes fixed on Zane. “Your real body can’t be too far from the secret land, can it?”

“How far could it be?” the burly man sneered. “This world isn’t that big.”

Zane ignored the taunts. 

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

He turned to War King Wind and the other captured warriors. “We go together.”

The Soul Sea masters didn't fully understand what had just been negotiated. The talk of titles and towers was beyond them. But they understood this much: Zane was trying to prove something, and these seven had agreed to wait. That was more than they'd had a moment ago. A small, fragile hope flickered in their chests — the first they'd felt since Wade's fishing line had dragged them into the crater.

The warriors from the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms gave a silent, grim nod and followed him. The seven powerful masters from the Spirit Supreme Realm trailed them like patient predators.

It took about five minutes to leave the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. 

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL PORTAL]

[SFX: TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]

Once the teleportation array was restored, the entire group stepped back into the Sacred Feather Sea, intending to retrace their steps along the Path of Heroes.

[SFX: METAL SHIELDS RAISED]

As they reached the Path, the mighty warriors of the Soul Sea Realm instinctively raised their massive shields, preparing for the onslaught of blue arrows.

Delroy just smiled. “No need for those,” he said, waving a hand. 

[SFX: ENERGY BARRIER ACTIVATION]

In a flash, a shimmering barrier of Vitality erupted around them, effortlessly enclosing the entire group.
