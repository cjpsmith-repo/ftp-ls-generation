Ep 468 - Zane's On a Roll

Word count: 985

[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT GROUNDS]

[MUSIC: STRATEGIC TENSION]

The examiner had laid out the rules pretty clearly: the main goal of the first phase was to guard your own tower. But there was no rule against crossing into someone else's territory.

So Zane did just that, slipping into a neighboring territory and starting to poach their crow zombies. It didn't take long to piss off the warrior guarding the area.

[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]

Roaring with fury, the guy whipped out a massive saber and charged after Zane.

But Zane wasn't about to get caught. He weaved around the warrior's attacks like they were nothing, fluid and untouchable.

[AMBIENT: TOXIC FOG]

"Let's see how far you can run," the warrior growled, chasing Zane right up to the edge of a thick cloud of corpse poison. He smirked, sure that the moment Zane touched the toxic fog, it'd be all over.

[SFX: SPLASH]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]

But to his shock, Zane plunged straight into the fog without a second's hesitation and emerged on the other side, completely unharmed. Then he just went right back to slaughtering crow zombies.

"What the hell?" the warrior yelled, his jaw hanging open. "Is this guy immune to corpse poison or something?" he muttered to himself.

Zane didn't even glance at him, his focus entirely on racking up kills.

The other warrior was stumped. What was he supposed to do? Even if he caught Zane, the guy could just duck back into the toxic fog. All he could do was stand there and watch, helpless.

As Zane was cutting down another crow zombie, a new threat made him flinch.

[SFX: ARROWS WHISTLING]

A sharp thwang cut through the air, and he looked up to see three arrows streaking across the sky, aimed right for him.

A quick glance told him they were fired from the other warrior's tower.

He sidestepped them easily.

Luckily, the tournament was only in its fourth attack stage, which meant the tower defenses were still limited. You could only upgrade your array defenses to handle the next wave of beasts—the first upgrade was for the second wave, the second for the third, and so on. It was a smart system that not only helped the warriors survive but also kept them from relying too heavily on their towers instead of their own strength.

If Zane could poach enough points from other territories, he could upgrade his tower even faster. Hell, if he got enough, he could even get a fifth upgrade, ready for an attack level that hadn't even started yet.

"I need to farm as many points as I can from these other territories," Zane mumbled to himself.

[SFX: DEEP BELL TOLL]

Just then, a deep bell tolled across the sky, signaling the start of the fifth attack.

[AMBIENT: RUMBLING EARTH]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

Instantly, countless stone figures began to rise from the grassland. These were the Men of Basalt, the fifth wave of beasts from the magic array—hulking warriors carved from black rock, far stronger than the Black Tortoises.

"Lily!" Zane called, summoning his sword spirit.

A bold plan was forming in his head: he'd let Lily handle the Men of Basalt here while he went to steal points from another competitor.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

On his command, Lily shot toward the nearest stone man, her thirty-foot-long blade descending on its head.

[SFX: LOUD CRACK]

A sword spirit as powerful as Lily, combined with her blade, was a devastating force. Few beasts in the Heartlands could withstand such a direct hit.

[SFX: HEAVY CRASH]

The Man of Basalt was cleaved clean in two, the two halves of its body crashing to the ground.

There were about two hundred of the stone men in his territory. They looked indestructible, but Lily began to cut them down one by one. Their only weakness was their speed—they were even slower than the Black Tortoises.

'Lily should be able to handle these things,'* Zane thought. *'And with the tower upgraded four times, she'll have plenty of backup.'

[AMBIENT: GRASSLAND WIND]

[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS STRINGS]

Satisfied, he took off, sprinting toward the adjacent grassland.

[AMBIENT: OBSERVATION CHAMBER]

The examiner couldn't help but cry out, "He's already got a taste for it! He's going after another competitor's points again!"

Michael Nash and the old man exchanged an impressed smile. "He's confident enough to let his sword spirit handle the Men of Basalt alone. That's a skill in itself," Michael noted.

The old man nodded. "This kid is interesting. And smart."

The examiner sighed. "I bet the warriors he's poaching from don't think so. At this rate, he'll gain way more points each phase than anyone else. He could probably upgrade his tower to level eleven or even twelve by the time the ten rounds are over."

Michael Nash, who wasn't as familiar with the magic array's finer points, asked curiously, "Level eleven? Level twelve? I thought there were only ten attack waves in the first phase. Shouldn't the tower only be upgradeable ten times?"

[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]

[SFX: WHOOSH]

As a figure appeared in the distant sky, rushing toward him, surprise flickered across Zane's face, but it quickly twisted into a nasty sneer when he recognized who it was.

The pale-faced warrior was known for holding a grudge; the guy had slaughtered an entire Second-Grade sect after reaching the Inner-Core Realm. Zane had already pissed him off during their last meeting in the hall, so he knew it was only a matter of time before the man came looking for revenge.

What the warrior hadn't counted on was Zane coming to him first, before he even had a chance to hunt the guy down.

'This is weird,'* the warrior thought, completely baffled. *'Doesn't he have his own Men of Basalt to deal with? Why is he running around over here? He doesn't seem worried about his tower at all. If it gets destroyed, he's out.'

[SFX: SWORD SLASHES]

[MUSIC: CHAOTIC BATTLE THEME]

The pale-faced warrior was even more stunned when Zane, instead of challenging him, just started mowing down his Men of Basalt without so much as a glance in his direction.

He couldn't make any sense of Zane's actions.

What the hell was this kid playing at?
