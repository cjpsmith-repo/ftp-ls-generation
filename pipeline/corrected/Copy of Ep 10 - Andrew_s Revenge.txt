Ep 10 - Andrew's Revenge
Word Count: 1191
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TRIALS]
[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]
Zane's uncles had let him live all these years, but not out of kindness. They just didn't see him as a threat. And using him as a punching bag was a convenient way to make sure he never got any real training.
In fact, they hoped the beatings would eventually weaken him, or even kill him. That's why he'd stayed with House Lowell, taking it all. Zane knew full well that if he ever truly fought back, his uncles wouldn't hesitate to kill him.
"But hey, you really gave it to Grey for us. We can't stand that guy. He's been getting more and more out of control," Melvin said with a knowing smirk.
Except for the kids from the second and third branches, pretty much everyone secretly admired Zane for standing up to a bully like Grey. No one in the Training Hall said it out loud, but they all felt the same way.
Life in House Lowell had gone downhill ever since the takeover. The two new branches were awful. They slashed the allowances for the extended family members and even cut back on supplements for training and development. Even some of the staff had gotten arrogant, acting high and mighty with the other Lowell members. It seemed like every resource, every advantage, was funneled directly to Perrin and Andrew.
It's no wonder the other Lowell kids felt like they were getting screwed over.
Everyone missed the old days, back when Zane's father ran the house. The family structure was strict, sure, but he was fair. There wasn't any of this backstabbing, and no one dared to take advantage of anyone else. The staff knew their place and showed respect. It was a shame the new branches running House Lowell didn't have a fraction of that discipline or integrity.
That longing for the old days festered among the Lowell kids. They were too scared to talk about it openly, worried one wrong word could bring trouble down on their families. With the new rules, any sign of dissent could be used against them. In just two years, House Lowell had become a shadow of its former self.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
And Zane had a plan. He knew that if he could just get the chance, he would build his strength until he was powerful enough to purge the rot from his family, once and for all.
[AMBIENT: MANSION EXTERIOR]
[SFX: FALLING TO KNEES]
Grey stood before the Third Branch's massive mansion, his head wrapped so thick with gauze that only his eyes, nose, and mouth were showing. He dropped to his knees with a hard thud.
A pathetic whine escaped him. "Master Andrew, you have to help me!"
[AMBIENT: ELEGANT MANOR INTERIOR]
At the cry, a teenager in a light blue outfit leaned back in his chair. This was Andrew, the second young master of House Lowell—handsome, but dripping with arrogance.
"I heard you wanted to make Zane your personal servant," Andrew said, tilting his head with a mocking laugh. "To do what, bring you your meals and fluff your pillows?"
"Master Andrew, that's not true!" Grey cried, putting on a show.
Andrew just laughed, cutting him off. "Even if his family lost everything and he's basically a servant now, Zane's still a Lowell. Did you forget he used to be the young master? It'd look pretty bad for me, the second young master, to have him as a servant. And you, a nobody, thought you could pull it off? What were you thinking? You deserved to get your ass kicked."
Grey bowed his head low. "I know I was wrong, Master Andrew, but please, you have to help me."
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
He squeezed out a few more fake tears as he pleaded and made a subtle gesture. A moment later, a middle-aged woman hurried over and knelt quietly beside him.
She was Grey's wife, and she'd been Andrew's nanny ever since his mother passed away when he was a boy. Andrew was closer to her than anyone, treating her almost like his real mother.
"Auntie, you don't need to kneel. Please, get up," Andrew said, his tone softening instantly. He waved a hand. "Grey, you can get up too."
"Master Andrew, does that mean you'll help me?" Grey asked, his voice thick with excitement.
[SFX: CHAIR SCRAPING]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC SCHEMING]
Andrew rose from his chair and paced a few steps. "My cousin Perrin said he wanted to keep Zane alive, to force him to watch how powerful our family becomes. But Zane's a nobody. With Perrin getting ready to leave for the Skyreach Sect, I'll take care of him."
Grey knew exactly what he meant. The bandages around his mouth stretched into a grin. "Thank you, Master Andrew! Thank you so much!" he exclaimed.
"But we'll have to wait a little," Andrew said, touching his forehead. "I just took the last Aether Crystal, and I need to be in seclusion to absorb its power. Let's get our revenge on Family Training Day. I'll choose Zane as my opponent for the Fatal Duel. It's the perfect chance to kill him with my own two hands."
A wicked smile spread across Andrew's face at the thought of finally putting Zane in his place.
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING TRAINING GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: CEREMONIAL FANFARE]
Family Training Day was a huge event. The elders of House Lowell would inspect the skills of all the children, and the best performers would be showered with rewards. So, naturally, every Lowell kid fought their hardest to win.
But for the slaves, it was something else entirely: a rare shot at freedom. Any slave who could survive the Fatal Duels held that day would be granted their freedom. It was a dream even the lowest of them clung to. Without that chance, the slaves would have given up long ago, broken by the sheer hopelessness of their lives. That single rule, that glimmer of hope, was what kept them going. It was why they endured the daily beatings in the Training Hall, all for that one shot.
The problem was, the Fatal Duel was a joke. The slaves, beaten down daily and nursing all kinds of injuries, stood no chance against the healthy, well-trained kids of House Lowell. Over the years, countless slaves had died on Family Training Day.
"An excellent plan, Master Andrew. We'll do it just like that. Let the little bastard live for another month!" Grey bowed deeply a few more times before scrambling to his feet. Under the gauze, his eyes burned with hatred.
[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
After Andrew was gone, his wife turned to him, her voice pleading. "Zane is just a boy, a poor kid. Why do you have to push him to his death? I know he shouldn't have hit you, but... there's a difference between teaching someone a lesson and killing them."
Grey snorted, glaring at her. "What do you know about anything besides your soft-hearted nonsense?"
Scolded, his wife cowered. She opened her mouth to argue, but the words wouldn't come. Finally, she just shut it and lowered her head.
