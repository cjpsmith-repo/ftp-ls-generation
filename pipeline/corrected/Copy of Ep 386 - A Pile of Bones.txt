Ep 386 - A Pile of Bones

Word count: 1146

[AMBIENT: TENSE FOREST]

Zane's instincts screamed a warning. He whirled around and spotted Dermot crouched over a peculiar herb, completely oblivious to the dark figure rising from the shadows behind him. "Watch out!"

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: TERRIFYING ATTACK]

A skeletal hand shot from the shadowy mass with terrifying speed, clamping over Dermot's face before he could even register the threat.

At Zane's desperate shout, everyone spun around. Dermot's eyes bulged with terror as the dark figure seized him, its fingers digging into his skull. He clawed at the thing's arm, his mouth opening in a scream that never came.

Dermot tried to scramble away, his feet kicking frantically against the ground, but the creature already had him in its grip.

The group stood paralyzed, horror rooting them in place as the black mass enveloped Dermot like a living shroud. He thrashed violently, his hands beating against the tar-like substance that wrapped around his torso, then his legs. The desperation in his movements made Zane's stomach turn. This wasn't a clean death in battle—this was something far worse.

Dermot's struggles grew weaker with each passing second. The thing was draining him, consuming him from the inside out.

[SFX: BONES CLATTERING]

A moment later, Dermot's body went limp. The dark figure consumed him completely, then spat out a small pile of bones onto the ground with a wet, hollow sound.

"R-Dermot… that thing just swallowed him!" Lewis stammered. His voice cracked as he stared at the remains. "Those are his bones!"

His eyes had gone wide with a fear so raw it made his teeth chatter. He'd seen death before—they all had—but never like this. Never something that could devour a man whole, reducing him to nothing but scattered fragments in mere seconds. 

Finn's hand tightened on his sword hilt until his knuckles turned white. The blade trembled slightly—not from weakness, but from the rage and horror warring inside him. Dermot had been annoying, yes, but he'd been alive just moments ago.

Zane forced himself to breathe, to think. His Dragon Scales had protected him from fire and blade, but would they stop something that consumed from within? He didn't want to find out.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC HORROR CRESCENDO]

The disciple from Cloud Hall's voice cut through their shock like a whip crack. "It's a Nether Phantom! Everyone, be careful!"

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS RUMBLING]

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[SFX: SPREADING OOZE]

No one had time to process the name. All that mattered was getting away. But just as the warning hit their ears, more of the dark mass began to ooze across the ground, spreading out and closing in on them.

[SFX: URGENT FOOTSTEPS]

Frank turned around and faced everyone, his face grim. "Everybody, run!"

This Nether Phantom was way more terrifying than a Sky-Reaver. At least you could kill a Sky-Reaver. They had no idea what this thing even was, let alone how to fight it. It was just a sticky, tar-like mass. Frank wasn't even sure it was alive. What good was his spear going to be against that?
He found out. Frank pulled his spear and drove it through the nearest mass — and watched the hole seal itself in seconds, the substance flowing back together as if nothing had touched it.
"Physical attacks don't work," he said, his voice stripped of its usual confidence.

[SFX: SQUELCHING GRAB]

[MUSIC: INTENSE HORROR CRESCENDO]

"Ahh!" Lois's scream tore through the air as one of the phantoms latched onto her leg, its tar-like substance burning cold against her skin. The creature began dragging her down toward the spreading darkness.

[SFX: SPEAR STRIKE]

Frank's heart lurched. He spun on his heel, spear already moving in a desperate arc. The weapon plunged into the ooze, clutching Lois's calf, and he felt the sickening resistance of something that wasn't quite solid, wasn't quite liquid. Gritting his teeth, he twisted the spear and yanked Lois free with his other hand.

"Move!" He pulled her upright, and they bolted together across the shrinking patches of safe ground. His mind raced—if even touching these things could kill, how long before they ran out of places to run?

[AMBIENT: SPREADING OOZE SOUNDS]

By now, the phantom's ooze had covered most of the ground, leaving them with almost nowhere to run. Stopping for even a second meant getting caught.

Running into Nether Phantoms wasn't unheard of in a confined world like this. The dark creatures hunted anything alive, drawn in by the Vitality they possessed. The Phantoms had likely sensed the group the moment they arrived, following them undetected as a slow-moving, shadowy liquid.
Lewis hit the front of the group first, his movement technique carrying him ahead of everyone else — and then stopped dead. More phantoms blocked the way forward, massed together in formations three feet tall, like slow-moving hills of darkness. They were surrounded on all sides.
Everyone pulled together back-to-back, watching the walls of black close in.

"Frank, we've gotta get out of here!" Lois's voice cracked with terror as she stumbled over the uneven ground, her hand clutching his arm. "I don't want to end up a pile of bones like Dermot!" 

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC DETERMINATION THEME]

Panicking, Finn knew physical attacks were useless. 
"Finn." Zane's voice was quiet but sharp. "Thunder. Evil creatures can't stand it."
The only thing that could hurt them was the heavenly thunder from his Extreme Thunder Sword Intent. He had to try.

[SFX: CRACKLING ELECTRICITY]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

With a sharp crackle of energy, Finn shouted, "Extreme Thunder Sword Intent! Heavenly Swords! Four swords in one!"

[SFX: LIGHTNING STRIKE]

[SFX: EXPLOSION]

The instant his attack hit two of the Nether Phantoms, they were engulfed in lightning, sparking violently before evaporating into nothing.

A flicker of hope crossed Finn's face. "It worked!" 

"That's the Apex of Sword Intent!" Frank muttered in disbelief. "How is that possible?"

How could an Adept-level kid pull that off? Frank himself hadn't reached the Apex of Spear Intent until he'd entered the Soul Realm. He never would have believed someone at the peak of the Adept Realm could master it.

[MUSIC: AWE AND WONDER]

And the most amazing part? The lightning he'd summoned wasn't made from Vitality—it was real heavenly thunder. Vitality-condensed lightning couldn't even compare to the power of the real thing. Frank was blown away.

"Patrick, this kid from your Sect is seriously powerful!" Frank said, his awe cutting through the panic. He realized Finn had just as much potential as he did. Once Finn hit the Soul Realm, he’d be a force to be reckoned with. Frank had to admit, he'd completely underestimated him—and right now, he was damn glad to be wrong.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

But Patrick's next comment caught everyone off guard. "Finn is strong, no doubt," he chuckled, "but Zane is even more powerful. I'm no match for him either. He's the strongest disciple in our entire sect."

[SFX: DRAMATIC STING]

"What?" The shock made Frank forget for a second that they were still fighting for their lives. "You mean Zane? The guy who's only a Level Three Adept?" he asked, his voice laced with disbelief. "You're saying he's stronger than you?"

His claim was too crazy to be real. How could that kid be stronger than all of them?
