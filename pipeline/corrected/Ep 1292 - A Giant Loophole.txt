Ep 1292 - A Giant Loophole

Word count: 1108

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: FROZEN CAVERN WITH WIND]
[SFX: PITIFUL CAT MEOW WITH ECHO]
A pitiful meow echoed from the Frosty Cage.
“Don’t be scared,” Janet cooed from her perch on the Lava Giant’s head, her voice soft despite the chaos. “This big guy can’t eat you.”
[SFX: HEAVY LANDING THUDS]
A moment later, Hailey landed beside her, followed by Coleman—and then Ansel, who scrambled up yelling, “Don’t eat me!”
[SFX: GUTTURAL MONSTER CRY]
[SFX: POWERFUL VACUUM SUCTION]
Just then, the Lava Giant let out a guttural cry, and a powerful vacuum erupted from its massive mouth.
Two warriors were still on the giant’s shoulders—a quiet fighter from the Twilight Group and another warrior named Rango.
[SFX: BODIES YANKED THROUGH AIR]
They were yanked violently toward its mouth before they could even think to jump.
“AH!” Rango screamed, leaping with everything he had, but it wasn’t enough to escape the pull. He was sucked down the giant’s throat in a horrifying instant
[SFX: BODY SUCKED DOWN THROAT]
[SFX: TERRIFIED SCREAM]
The Twilight warrior didn’t jump.
[SFX: ROLLING ON ROUGH SURFACE]
He dropped and rolled over the creature’s shoulder, scrambling for the shelter of its neck—but the suction caught him mid-roll, dragged him backward through the open air, and pulled him down the giant’s throat a heartbeat after Rango.
They’d entered the Fourth Hall of Causality with seven, and in a single breath they were down to five. Janet had been right—the more people they had, the better their chances. They had to work together to survive these trials, but it seemed like losing people was just part of the deal.
[SFX: GIANT ROAR]
[SFX: MASSIVE HEAD THRASHING]
With a roar, the Lava Giant thrashed its head, but the flat top was so wide that even as it tilted, they managed to keep their footing.
“What do we do now?” Ansel asked, his voice tight with urgency. Just because the giant’s head was spacious didn’t mean it was safe. 
[SFX: GIANT ARMS SWINGING]
Its massive arms were already winding up to take a swing at them.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane stared out into the sea of fire, his mind racing. This wasn’t just one giant. From up here, he could see at least ten more of them standing shoulder to shoulder in the molten lake. His original plan had been to use the Flame Kitten to lure them over one by one, creating a bridge of giants to get to the other side. But now, he was realizing how easily that could fall apart. One wrong move and their only advantage would be gone.
“Let’s go—it’s coming,” Hailey said quietly, eyes fixed on the arc of the giant’s swinging arm.
The giant looked slow from a distance, but up close, its true speed was another matter—there’d be no outrunning it if they waited for that arm to actually arrive.
Then something clicked behind Zane’s eyes.
“Throw the kitten out—now!”
“What?” Janet didn’t follow.
“Stretch the Lofty Wood as far as it’ll go and dangle the cage out in front of it. Like fishing.” Zane didn’t have time to explain further—he wasn’t even sure it would work, but they were out of better ideas.
[SFX: WOOD EXTENDING CREAK]
Janet nodded and let the wood extend, pushing the Frosty Cage out ahead of them.
Coleman caught on first. The giant’s intelligence was low—stretching the wood out was the same as dangling the kitten just out of its reach.
[SFX: GIANT ARM WHOOSH]
Sure enough, the giant’s swing changed direction mid-motion, lunging for the kitten instead of the group on its head.
“Farther!” Zane called.
“On it!” Janet fed the wood out further, the cage swinging six, then seven hundred feet ahead of them—just beyond the giant’s reach, no matter how it stretched.
The giant clapped its massive hands together and caught nothing but air.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEP IN LAVA]
It took a step forward instead, straining after the dangling kitten.
Zane, Coleman, and the others exchanged a look of pure disbelief.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT RELIEF]
It had actually worked.
“This thing,” Janet said, a small smile breaking through despite herself, “might be the dumbest thing I’ve ever met.” She tightened her grip on the wood, eyes on the terrified kitten hanging in the distance. “Sorry, little guy. Looks like I need you a while longer.”
Step by step, the giant carried them forward into the lake, chasing a meal it could never quite reach. 
[SFX: MAGMA WAVE RIPPLING]
Every stride sent a wave rippling through the magma, the heat spiking with each footfall—but they were moving, and that was all that mattered.
“I think I overestimated how smart these things are,” Janet said, almost giddy with disbelief. A simple turn of the Lofty Wood in her hand was enough to steer the giant’s entire path—as far as it was concerned, nothing existed in the world except that kitten.
Zane’s eyes swept over the row of distant giants, evenly spaced across the lake. “They’re lined up almost like stepping stones. Maybe this is how we’re supposed to cross—maybe we just found a shortcut nobody was meant to find.”
There was no brute-forcing a Hall of Causality. The only way through was working within its rules—and it looked like they’d stumbled onto a loophole in this one.
Carried along by their unwitting mount, they closed in on the center of the lake. At this rate, it wouldn’t take long to cross entirely. Whether that would be enough to clear the whole Fourth Hall, though, was still anyone’s guess.
The other giants hadn’t seemed to notice them before, but now one of them had spotted the kitten dangling in the air. 
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS IN LAVA]
It was starting to lumber toward them.
[MUSIC: URGENT DANGER STRINGS]
“What’s happening?” Even Zane was shocked. Luring one of them was the plan. Attracting a second one was a disaster.
“I don’t know,” Janet said, frowning as she tightened her grip on the wooden cage. “Maybe it spotted my kitten.”
Coleman’s face was grim.
“There are plenty of you in this hellhole!” he yelled at the approaching giant. “Go chase something else!”
But yelling was useless. If the giants were smart enough to understand them, they wouldn’t be in this mess to begin with. It was just another rule of this place: once a giant saw the kitten, it had to try and eat it.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
The second Lava Giant took a huge step forward, its arm lunging for the kitten. That seemed to enrage the giant they were riding. Instead of swatting at them, it turned and slammed both of its massive fists into the newcomer. 
[SFX: MASSIVE COLLISION]
[SFX: EARTHQUAKE RUMBLE]
The sound of the two monsters colliding was like an earthquake and an avalanche all at once.
“Everyone, get down!” Zane yelled.
[SFX: BODIES HITTING STONE]
The impact threw them all flat against the giant’s stony scalp. 
[SFX: SCRAMBLING ON ROCK]
They scrambled for purchase, their fingers digging into cracks and clinging to the rock pillars sticking out of its head. 
This was their only hope. They were in the middle of a literal sea of fire. One slip, and you were toast.
