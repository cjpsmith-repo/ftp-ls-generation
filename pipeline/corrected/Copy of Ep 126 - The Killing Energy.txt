Ep 126 - The Killing Energy
Word Count: 1327
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD]
Zane stood in the crowd, his eyes locked on the general. The guy was a stranger, and Zane was sure he’d never seen him before.
"Why can't I leave? And who the hell are you?" he asked, his voice low. Getting called out like this could only mean one thing: trouble.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
Suddenly, a figure emerged from the side, his head completely wrapped in gauze. "Zane, I swore you'd die with your boots on," he sneered. "Did you really think you could just run for it?"
Even with his face covered, leaving only his eyes visible, Zane recognized the voice instantly. It was Preston. Before the demon attack on the city, Zane had knocked him around a few times.
"Oh, it's you, Preston. What's the matter? Go crying to your big brother?" Zane asked casually, his hand tightening around the hilt of his flying blade.
The general in front of him was clearly powerful, and he was flanked by two soldiers. The soldiers looked like Level Six Masters, but from the way they focused their energy, their real strength was nothing to scoff at.
Then again, Zane's own power far exceeded what was typical for his level, so he knew better than to judge anyone by rank alone.
"I'm Josef," the general announced. "My little brother here told me all about you. Seems you've made enemies of quite a few members of the Sterling family—Connor, Felix... and now you've laid a hand on Preston? Not many people are stupid enough to cross my family. But you came to Silverspire, so now I'm going to 'take good care of you.' Here's my suggestion: cut off your own arms, put on this chain, and come with me. Otherwise, you're a dead man!"
[SFX: HEAVY CHAIN SLAM]
As soon as he finished, Josef whipped out a heavy prisoner's chain and slammed it onto the ground with a deafening clang.
"Cut off my own arms?" Zane's eyebrows shot up before he burst out laughing, like he'd just heard the funniest joke in the world. "You're just some general's errand boy," he said, fearless.
Dalton stepped forward. "Think carefully, Josef. You can maybe get to Zane. But there are thirty of us. You start a massacre inside Silverspire and the Skyreach Sect will want answers. A counselor with Family Sterling behind him isn't untouchable enough for that."
Josef's composure flickered. Then he smiled.
"Fair point. So here's another option — anyone who wants a safe escort out of the city, come stand with me. Right now."
A beat of silence. Then, one by one, disciples began crossing over. Most of the other peaks had no quarrel with House Sterling. By the time the shuffling stopped, only Eagle Peak, Misty Peak, Maiden's Peak, and Ingrid remained behind Zane.
[AMBIENT: CROWD DISPERSING]
Josef smirked as the crowd around Zane began to thin. "Everyone except Zane can leave," he announced smugly. "I'd say only a fool wouldn't know what to do right now!"
[SFX: BLINDING FLASH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
But just as he finished speaking, a blinding flash erupted from the hand of the young woman standing behind Zane. The light rapidly converged, taking the shape of a dragon made of pure Primal Energy before it shot toward Josef with incredible force.
"Whoa!" In that split second, a primal fear seized Josef. The thought of dying sent an icy chill down his spine, and he threw himself aside, barely dodging the Concussive Blast.
[SFX: BUILDING COLLAPSE]
The blast didn't lose any momentum, slamming into a house behind Josef. The entire building imploded, collapsing in a shower of debris.
[AMBIENT: DUST AND DEBRIS]
Dust and smoke billowed from the wreckage.
After his close call, Josef's face was grim. Even his soldiers were stunned by the young woman's raw power. Though Preston’s face was still hidden by the gauze, his eyes were wide with terror.
"A Concussive Blast? That's House Solomon tech!" Josef roared, his furious gaze landing on Ingrid.
Despite the destruction she’d just caused, Ingrid looked completely unfazed, a slight smirk playing on her lips. She didn't say a word, just moved to stand closer to Zane. His presence alone was a calming anchor, a wave of relief washing over her.
The Great Houses rarely came to blows. They all knew the strength of the others, so there was an unspoken rule of caution. But this Solomon girl had just thrown all that out the window with her reckless attack.
Josef had never intended for this to turn into a real fight, but she'd just tried to vaporize him with a Concussive Blast. If he hadn't dodged, he would've been a smear on the pavement!
[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDUP]
Enraged by her audacity, Josef bellowed, "I don't give a damn who you are! For that stunt, you're dead today. No... first, I'll let my men in the barracks have their fun with you, and *then* I'll kill you! You two, get them! There could be demons hiding in the crowd. Kill anyone who resists!"
Zane’s expression remained calm as he listened to the order, but a deadly glint flickered in his eyes.
Zane had been hesitant. He didn't want a fight with Josef and a crowd of people who'd bought into his lies, especially not inside Silverspire.
He wasn't afraid, just reluctant. He much preferred Dalton's earlier approach—using words to make Josef doubt himself.
But Josef's order to kill had thrown any chance of a peaceful resolution out the window. There was no talking his way out of this now. And anyone who truly knew Zane would recognize the terrifying stillness that had settled over him—a mask for the rage boiling just beneath the surface.
[SFX: LIGHT FOOTSTEP]
Calmly, Zane took a single, fluid step forward, his movement so light it was like he was drifting on air.
The moment his foot touched down, something erupted from beneath it. The stone cracked — not just underfoot but outward, fractures racing across the floor, climbing the walls, spreading to the beams overhead. The whole room seemed to splinter from a single point of contact.
The two guards moved to intercept, conjuring thick earth-yellow Vitality shields between themselves and Zane — the kind that had stopped Demon Commanders cold. Zane's fists drove into them and sank deep but didn't shatter them.
So he changed approach. Corrupt Vitality bled from his knuckles into the shields.
[SFX: CORRUPTION CRACKLE]
The shields began dissolving from within. The guards felt it immediately and released them — better to lose the shields than have that energy reach their bodies. It left their chests open.
Two punches. Both guards went down. Zane had held back — they were just following orders.
The moment his foot touched the ground, Josef’s face went blank with confusion. He was a Level Six Master, but his power was a cheap imitation, bought with Energy Capsules instead of earned through real training.
He had no actual combat strength to back it up; his skills were in strategy, not a real fight.
He knew exactly how strong his two guards were. Together, they could take down four Demon Commanders. So how… how had Zane just neutralized them both without breaking a sweat? Josef couldn't wrap his head around it.
Preston had warned him not to underestimate Zane, that he was more than just some High Smith. Josef had brushed it off, confident his plan—and his two guards—were more than enough to handle one man.
But Zane's power was on another level entirely. Josef was utterly lost. He could only stumble backward, his mind reeling.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: INTENSE CHASE]
Primal terror seized Josef's core, propelling him into desperate flight. As he bolted through the chaos, he whirled and hurled a volley of throwing knives toward Zane—a futile gambit born of sheer panic rather than strategy.
[SFX: WHISTLING KNIVES]
Zane advanced through the lethal storm without hesitation, the blades whistling harmlessly around him. His eyes, cold and unblinking, locked onto Josef's retreating form as he closed the gap between predator and prey with methodical, unstoppable strides.
