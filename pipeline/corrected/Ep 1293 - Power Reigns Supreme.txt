Janet was in the most precarious spot. She was holding the slender Lofty Wood in one hand, dangling the flame kitten just out of reach to keep it from getting caught in the fray. That left her with only one hand to keep from falling.

“That was too close,” Janet gasped, her chest heaving as Zane held her. She kept the Frosty Cage held high, her other arm trembling. She wasn’t about to let the little flame kitten die because of her mistake.

Zane’s jaw tightened as he took in the fight still raging beneath them. Their only hope was that the giant carrying them held its ground—if it went down into the lake, they went down with it.
