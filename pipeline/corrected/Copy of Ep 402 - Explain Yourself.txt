Ep 402 - Explain Yourself

Word count: 1202

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Several leaders, including Elder Toby Dillon, were waiting anxiously under a massive, arched gateway. Of the five, Sir Kenneth was by far the most on edge.

Three of his disciples from the Skyreach Sect had entered the Death Road, and he hadn’t heard a thing since. He didn’t know what happened, but his gut screamed that they hadn’t made it. The Providence Sect, the Luminous Sect, and Cloud Hall had each sent only one elite disciple down that path. The Skyreach Sect, on the other hand, stood to lose all of its top prospects at once, which made the other sects seem fortunate in comparison.

At first, Kenneth had such high hopes. Any one of his three disciples could have easily ranked first or second in any other sect’s trial. He had imagined them making a huge splash, bringing honor to the sect—and to him. He never expected to suffer such a devastating loss on the Death Road.

Kenneth wasn't the only one; the leaders of the other sects and Toby also wore worried expressions. Frank's disappearance might not impact Elder Toby’s status, but he had no idea how he was going to explain it to Frank's father, Emanuel.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Just as worry clouded their faces, the Aether crystals embedded in the gateway suddenly flashed to life. The cyclone of energy beneath it began spinning faster, its rotation accelerating with each passing second.

Toby straightened, his eyes widening. "What's happening? Is someone coming out?"

 Kenneth took an involuntary step forward, his voice sharp with disbelief.  "How is that possible? No one could pass the trial this quickly!"

For a heartbeat, hope flickered across their faces—wild, desperate hope that perhaps their disciples had somehow survived. But reality crashed down on them as they recalled what they'd witnessed on the Scrying Slate. Most of their disciples remained trapped in the fourth stage, still struggling against its deadly challenges.

Kenneth's heart sank. Whoever was emerging, it couldn't be one of his three. In all likelihood, they were gone—swallowed by the Death Road's merciless depths.

[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]

[MUSIC: DANGEROUS JOURNEY]

The spiritual energy coursing through Zane's body had been fortified by not one, but two legendary sources—the blood of both a Drake, or Flood Dragon, and a Nine-Headed Dragon. Their combined essence had transformed his internal reserves into something far beyond what any ordinary Adept Realm cultivator could possess.

When he stepped through the gateway and its overwhelming power crashed against him like a tidal wave, his body seized for a single heartbeat. Every muscle locked. His breath caught in his throat. The pressure threatened to crush him from the inside out.

But then his enhanced spiritual energy surged in response, flooding his limbs with warmth and strength. The paralysis shattered almost as quickly as it had formed, leaving only a faint tingling sensation in its wake. Zane's eyes snapped open, clear and focused, as he emerged into the open air beyond the gateway.

He had survived what should have killed him.

[AMBIENT: TENSE OUTDOOR CONFRONTATION]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane barely had time to orient himself before a sharp voice cut through the air.

The moment Zane stepped through, Kenneth's voice broke the silence.
"Zane." Just his name — but the way Kenneth said it carried something close to reverence. He had always believed this boy's luck ran deeper than skill or training. Watching him walk out first, ahead of everyone, ahead of Cloud Hall's own disciples — something no one had ever managed — only confirmed it.
"What happened in there? Where is my disciple?" Lady Sigmund demanded, her eyes blazing as she stepped forward from the crowd of sect leaders. "Where is Lois?"

Her question hung in the air like a blade, drawing the attention of everyone present. Kenneth's expression tightened with concern, while Elder Toby watched the exchange with calculating interest.

Lady Sigmund’s arrogance was already grating on Zane. He was a stubborn person by nature, and if she had just asked him nicely, he might have told her about Lois. But she hadn’t.

So he just replied flatly, "I have no idea."

"No idea? How is that possible?" Lady Sigmund sneered. "You took the Death Road with Frank and the others. I saw it with my own eyes! How dare you lie to me? If you don't tell me the truth, I'm afraid your luck will run out today! So, you'd better start talking, or—"

[SFX: WHOOSH OF INCOMING ATTACK]

Her rant was cut short as she sensed a dangerous force hurtling toward her.

[SFX: MASSIVE GROUND IMPACT]

[SFX: CRUMBLING STONE]

She took a sharp breath and leaped back just as the shadow of a huge fist slammed into the ground at her feet. The impact left a deep crater, obliterating the flagstones and the earth beneath them.

Kenneth had poured all his strength into that strike. His face was a thunderous mask of rage, his eyes wide and blazing. But even in his fury, his control was precise. Zane was standing only a few feet from Lady Sigmund, yet the damage was completely contained to where she had been standing, leaving him untouched.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC BATTLE THEME]

"You witch!" Kenneth roared. "How dare you bully my disciple right in front of me?"

[SFX: STAFF BRANDISHING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT CRESCENDO]

A mix of fear and fury flashed across Lady Sigmund's face as she brandished her staff, ready to strike back. "You! You dare threaten me? Do you think I'm afraid of you?" The power in that fist was terrifying; if she hadn't dodged in time, she would have been severely injured, if not killed.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

Kenneth sneered, clenching his fists and standing his ground. "Then bring it on!" 

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]

Toby stepped between them, his presence firm. "Everyone, calm down!" He turned a cold gaze on Lady Sigmund. "That's enough. If you continue to cause trouble, I will have to teach you a lesson myself."

As an elder, Toby held a higher status and possessed strength that even the likes of Lady Sigmund and Kenneth couldn't match. He had finally run out of patience with her infuriating behavior. Zane was just a kid who had barely survived the trials, and Toby couldn't stand by and watch her treat him so cruelly. Besides, he already thought very highly of the promising young man.

Toby then turned to Zane, his expression softening into a smile. "If you don't want to talk about it, that's your right. You can keep your secrets."
"Frank and the others," Zane said. "They're fine. We ran into trouble on the Death Road — I fell into one of the abysses. But they'd already cleared it before I went down. They're still on the Cloud Road. They'll be out."
Toby exhaled. Around him, the tension in the other leaders' faces eased.

The other leaders were also dying to know if there was a shortcut on the Cloud Road. If there was, they could tip off their own disciples in the trial three years from now. But since Zane refused to say, they couldn't force him. They all had the same thought: they'd just ask him later. In their minds, Zane was a shoo-in for Cloud Hall. Once he was a disciple there, they’d have plenty of opportunities to ask about this supposed shortcut.

[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL OUTDOORS]

[MUSIC: CALM MEDITATION]

Instead of waiting at the gateway with the leaders, Zane walked off, found a quiet spot, and began to meditate.

[SFX: SWIRLING WIND]

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL GATEWAY]

Within two hours, the cyclone under the archway began to whirl again. This time, Finn emerged from it.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The moment Finn appeared, the first person he saw wasn't Toby or the other leaders. It was Zane, meditating a short distance away. 

Seeing him, Finn froze on the spot.
