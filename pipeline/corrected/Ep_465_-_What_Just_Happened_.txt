Ep 465 - What Just Happened?

Word count: 1169

[AMBIENT: WIND ON HIGH TOWER]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

Zane vaulted to his feet, standing at the top of the tower as the horizon unfolded before him.

The view was breathtaking.

But as his gaze swept toward the far end of the grassland, he saw something strange.

[SFX: DISTANT MARCHING]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

Squinting against the glare, he made out a group of Ogres charging straight toward him. They wore silver armor that left one shoulder bare, and each warrior gripped a spear as they advanced in a tight, disciplined formation. The precision of their movement sent a chill down his spine—these weren't mindless brutes.

"Spears... they're planning a long-range attack," Zane muttered, his mind racing through tactical possibilities. He felt a flicker of admiration despite the danger. Whoever trained these creatures understood warfare. He raised an eyebrow, weighing his options for only a heartbeat before making his decision. Without hesitation, he charged straight at his enemies.

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

The Ogres moved with surprising speed, their heavy footfalls creating a rhythmic thunder across the grassland. Zane calculated the distance. If they advanced much closer, they could launch their spears in a devastating volley and reduce his tower to rubble. His companions would be exposed, vulnerable. The thought sent a surge of protectiveness through him.

That left him with no choice but to intercept them. He had to cut them down before they reached throwing range. His heart pounded—not from fear, but from the familiar rush of combat. Every muscle in his body coiled with purpose as he closed the gap between them.

As he ran, the two bowmen on the tower provided cover, felling two of the Ogres with a pair of well-aimed arrows.

Just as Zane closed the distance, ready to strike, the Ogres did something he didn't expect.

[SFX: DEAFENING ROAR]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

They let out a deafening, unified roar—a sound so violent it could shatter the eardrums of anyone within a mile. In the wake of that thunderous bellow, their eyes flared blood-red, burning with savage intent. They raised their spears and charged directly at Zane.

"What the hell?" Zane's pulse quickened. "They can attack on their own now?"

[SFX: CLASHING METAL]

[SFX: WHOOSHING SPEARS]

Caught off guard, he dodged, weaving through a rain of spear thrusts as he swung his Streamer to return the favor.

These Ogres were a whole different beast from the three-eyed spiders. The spiders had only ever targeted his tower, never paying him any mind. These Ogres, however, were actively trying to kill him while charging at full speed. The rules were changing, and he had no idea what would come next.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE GRASSLAND]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

Luckily, there were fewer of them.

He surged forward. While dodging their spears, he swung his sword in wide arcs, slicing through their throats. He could take down four of them with a single swing.

Zane could have finished them off in an instant, but he held back. He’d recently had a breakthrough with The Unburdened Blade back in the upper world—a set of basic swordsmanship he believed could be simplified even further, stripped down to its absolute essence.

Right now, his understanding of The Unburdened Blade was still shallow. He had no idea how much more he could pare down the basic movements or what other changes he could unlock. He knew he needed a lot more practice to make any real progress. So, even though it was inefficient, he stuck to the basics to take them down.

[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: ENLIGHTENMENT CHIME]

As he fought, another small piece of The Unburdened Blade clicked into place.

This was just the second wave of the pre-qualifying matches; Zane had no intention of trying too hard. There was no need to go all out yet. He didn't care if it took him longer than the other fighters to kill the Ogres, who were now scattered across the grassland. So far, the first stage had been easy—nothing had posed a real challenge.

[SFX: BODIES FALLING]

Once hunted by ogres, Zane now became the hunter, cutting down the javelin-wielding demons. Within moments, most lay dead at his feet, their blood staining the ground.

[MUSIC: SUDDEN DANGER]

His expression shifted—two had escaped! Their eyes glowed crimson as they charged toward the tower, javelins raised high and ready to strike.

The tower could withstand only twenty attacks. These ogres carried four javelins between them! A cold knot of fear tightened in Zane's chest.

[SFX: ARROW SHOTS]

Two sharp pops rang out. Arrows pierced their chests, dropping them instantly before they could unleash their weapons.

"That was close!" Zane exhaled sharply, his heart still racing.

He'd grown careless while honing his basic swordsmanship, too absorbed in perfecting each movement. Fortunately, the upgraded tower's archers had saved him from a costly mistake.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]

He poured the three thousand points straight into the tower without hesitation. To reach the top three, Zane would need to steal points from others in the second stage by destroying their towers—a brutal truth he'd accepted.

[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]

After a red flash, golden patterns appeared on the tower's surface, shimmering with power. Three archers now stood atop it, wielding upgraded bows strung with dark green arrows that hummed with lethal energy—arrows that even Zane found threatening.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

The third wave launched—Black Tortoises with formidable defenses. Their shells gleamed like polished obsidian, and they moved with surprising coordination, their formation tighter than the previous waves. Zane couldn't rely on basic sword skills this time. These creatures demanded something more—something that would pierce through layers of natural armor that had taken centuries to harden.

[MUSIC: STRATEGIC TENSION]

The shells of the black tortoises were incredibly hard, perfect for crafting defensive armor. In the hands of a skilled weaponsmith, they could even be forged into Mythic-grade gear.

The number of black tortoises swarming Owen was starting to dwindle. In the blink of an eye, he’d already killed almost half of them. At this rate, Owen was on track to take first place in the third wave. Michael Nash was about to burst with pride, but a glance at the edge of the Scrying Slate stopped him cold. He scanned the progress of the other participants, and his eyes widened in disbelief.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL REVELATION]

Zane’s name.

[SFX: DRAMATIC STING]

Something about Zane’s progress shocked him.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The Scrying Slate showed that Zane had already completed the third wave.

[SFX: SHOCKED GASP]

"What just happened?" Michael Nash stared at the Scrying Slate in disbelief.

The old man and the examiner exchanged confused glances.

Without a word, Michael switched to Zane's feed. Their eyes widened. Zane had already slaughtered every black tortoise around him.

They'd missed something incredible.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

"Look! What's that?" the examiner said, pointing at something next to Zane on the screen.

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL WONDER]

Beside Zane, a stunning girl was standing on her tiptoes, holding a colossal sword that had to be thirty feet long.

Michael Nash hadn’t seen this coming at all; Zane had completely upstaged him. A bitter smile touched his lips. He said cautiously, "I take back everything I said. Zane is… unpredictable. His talent isn't limited to drawing runes. We can't think of him as just a runic expert." He stared at the screen, his voice filled with awe.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

"That girl beside him is a sword spirit, and it has a human form!"
