Ep 623 - Man vs. Orc
Word count: 1122
[AMBIENT: TOWER ARENA]
[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]
Zane held his ground against the berserk Orc War Lord. He wasn't about to back down. This was just one of many fights he’d face on the upper floors of the Tower of Sin, and while his power had been growing fast, he still needed to test his true strength. An opponent like this was the perfect sparring partner.
Zane knew he’d been blessed with good fortune, but luck wasn’t something you could ride forever. The only way to survive was to get stronger, again and again.
[SFX: MASSIVE WEAPON SWING]
The Orc lifted its massive battle-ax, ready to bring it crashing down. Zane’s eyes narrowed. He met the blow with his own sword, and a shockwave powerful enough to make a lesser man drop his weapon shot up his arm, making it tingle sharply.
[SFX: METAL CLANKS]
The sound of steel on steel rang through the arena as they traded blows, neither giving an inch.
[SFX: METAL SHATTERING]
But the next time the Orc’s ax came down, Zane’s blade didn’t just block it—it slid clean through the thick steel, slicing the weapon in two. The Orc had been a lone fighter, so his gear wasn’t the best. His battle-axes were top-grade Mythic weapons, sure, but they were no match for Zane’s Relic-Class sword.
With his weapons destroyed, the Orc’s assault came to an abrupt halt. But it wasn’t over.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS ROAR]
He tossed the broken handles aside and let out a roar that seemed to shake the very foundations of the arena. Shimmering black scales began to bloom across his body, coating him in layers of dense, powerful energy.
I know this one! Zane thought, his mind racing. Beastly Transformation.
He’d seen it before, back in the Heartlands when Owen had used it. The difference was, for an Orc, this ability was innate—a fundamental part of their beastly bloodline.
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
Once he was fully armored in the black scales, the War Lord charged.
Zane scoffed. “You think a layer of scales can stop my blade?”
[SFX: METAL CLASH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
A top-grade Relic-Class weapon was unbelievably hard, impossibly sharp. Unfazed, Zane stood his ground and raised his sword. Against his weapon, those scales should have been like paper. He swung down, expecting to slice through flesh and bone, but that’s not what happened.
[SFX: POWERFUL HIT]
The moment his blade met the scales, a powerful force erupted from the point of impact and sent his sword bouncing clean out of his grip. The scales hadn't been armor. They'd been a trap — a coiled spring waiting for exactly that strike.
[SFX: BODY IMPACT]
Before Zane could recover, the War Lord slammed into him like a boulder. The collision sent him flying thirty feet across the arena floor. He hit the ground hard, his sword clattering down beside him.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Olivia and the others in the stands leaned forward, their confidence shaken.
Zane climbed to his feet, expression calm. He understood it now. The scales had been bait all along — lure the sword strike, explode the force outward, then charge in the instant the blade was gone. He'd walked right into it.
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL RISE]
The War Lord bared his teeth. "Human brat — I'm going to eat your flesh!"
He charged again, all fury and momentum.
[SFX: HEAVY THUNDERING FOOTSTEPS]
Every eye in the arena went to Zane's empty hands. His sword was still on the ground. The right move — the obvious move — was to fall back, create distance, buy time. Instead, Zane didn't retreat a single step. He dropped into a low stance, drew a slow breath, and clenched his fists.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
Deep inside his body, his dragon scales opened one by one. Power surged through him like a flood breaking a dam, converging on his right fist until every vein stood out against the skin. Without his high-grade Relic body, the force alone would have shattered his own hand from the inside.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
The crowd shook their heads.
"He's going to get his hand crippled."
"He's using his weakness to charge straight into the Orc's strength. What is he thinking?"
[SFX: DEVASTATING PUNCH IMPACT]
Zane's fist connected squarely with the War Lord's chest.
The black scales — the same scales that had sent Zane's sword flying — exploded on contact. They couldn't absorb this.
The full force of Zane's strike had nowhere to deflect. Half the War Lord's body caved inward as if squeezed through a narrow door, the impact folding him mid-charge. He stumbled forward a few lurching steps, then crashed to the ground. Blood poured from him in a torrent — far more than any other race would have lost — and was absorbed instantly into the Tower floor.
[SFX: FAINT SHIMMER]
A thin stream of Lucky Light spiraled out from the War Lord's signaling token and drifted toward Zane's chest. Not much — the Orc had barely accumulated any — but it was something.
[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]
"It's not just the Law of Space. His body and raw strength are on another level too."
"Most cultivators who train both Law and Body end up mediocre at both. He doesn't have that problem."
"The day he becomes a War Lord… he won't even need the Tower's power. No one in here will be able to touch him."
Zane walked over to his longsword, sheathed it, and placed it in his Storage Ring before leaving the arena. Just as he stepped out, a sixth-level Ogre War Lord approached him. The moment the Ogre appeared, Olivia and Cheryl, who had been watching from a distance, went on high alert.
Even though Zane could command the Tower’s power to protect himself, neither the Demon Night nor the humans were taking any chances. No one wanted anything to happen to him, so both factions had sent people to guard him in secret.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
“Zane!” the ogre called out.
Zane raised an eyebrow. “What do you want?” he asked, his voice flat.
“Maurice wishes to speak with you,” the ogre replied, referring to his people’s elder.
“When?”
“Tonight.”
Zane nodded. “Got it. Thanks.”
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS FADING]
Having delivered his message, the ogre turned and left without another word. As soon as he was gone, Cheryl and Olivia hurried over.
Zane flashed a smile at Olivia. “You were right. They’re already eager to negotiate.”
Cheryl nodded, all business. “I’ll go tell Esther Sterling.”
No one could guarantee Maurice would negotiate in good faith. As the holder of the second seat in the Tower of Sin, he definitely had a few tricks up his sleeve. Esther Sterling’s presence was crucial; she was the only one who could truly intimidate him.
Cheryl quickly went to inform Esther, while Olivia did the same for Elder Kingsley.
[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
As night fell, a procession of top-level War Lords descended from the upper floors. All four human seats were present, and Esther Sterling arrived with the other three seats from the Demon Night.
Since the ogres were the ones who had called for the meeting, the outcome of tonight’s talks would be incredibly important.
