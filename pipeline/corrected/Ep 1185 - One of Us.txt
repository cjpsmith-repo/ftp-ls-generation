Ep 1185 - One of Us

Word count: 1240

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
[SFX: BLADE WHOOSH]
[SFX: ENERGY STRIKE]
A blinding flash lit up the void as Supreme Lord Ranell’s saber sliced through the air, striking the single grain of sand with perfect accuracy.
[SFX: CRACKING ENERGY]
The instant the steel tip connected, he saw a hairline crack spiderweb not across the grain, but across the crescent of light his own blade had produced. In the end, the attack couldn’t even nudge the tiny thing. 
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE DISSIPATING]
The immense power he’d unleashed was completely wasted, shooting past its target to pierce thousands of stars across half the universe before finally vanishing into the darkness.
The grain of sand just floated there, rotating slowly in place.
The outcome left Ranell stunned. He was a Supreme Lord. How could he fail to even scratch a speck of dust?
He raised his saber for another attempt, but another Lord stepped forward, signaling that it was his turn.
[SFX: MULTIPLE ENERGY BLASTS]
One by one, they took their shot, throwing everything they had at the grain of sand. They unleashed every Law of Causality they commanded, but nothing worked. A full day passed. They tried origin laws, obscure Celestial Law techniques, and when those failed, one of them smeared poison on the grain. Even Zane, watching from inside, didn’t know what to make of that.
It wasn’t just the Supreme Lords who were in disbelief. Zane and Lavender, hidden inside the Elysian Keep, were just as shocked by its incredible durability. The Keep had always been Zane’s last line of defense, his sanctuary.
Lavender floated beside him in silence. She knew the Keep was extraordinary — but she wasn’t its owner, and she’d never been able to accurately gauge its true power. Now she knew. Her eyes were bright with something that looked almost like excitement.
Just moments ago, he’d been sick with nerves, but watching them fail, one after another, brought a wave of relief so powerful it was almost dizzying.
It seemed he had seriously underestimated his Master and the true power of the Elysian Keep. He knew his Master was a being who had surpassed the heavens, and that the Keep was extraordinary, but he’d never dreamed it was *this* powerful.
*What was the world above the heavens even like?* Zane wondered, a strange sort of amusement bubbling up inside him. *And just how powerful was his Master, really?*
Outside, the Queen of Punishment watched in silence. Then she raised the Saint Thunder Meteor Hammer and struck the grain herself. 
She had to — if they escaped with the Keep, there would be no second chance.
The hammer came down. The grain spun on.
Several more Supreme Lords arrived, bringing the total to nearly thirty, all from different races, representing the various powers of the universe. A low hum filled the void as another burst of light announced a new arrival. 
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
The light slowly condensed into the figure of a man, and the sight of his face made everyone’s expression shift.
Though he appeared human, this newcomer was from the Demi-Immortal Race — a hybrid clan, small in number but extraordinarily powerful, and famous for being difficult. Their arrival always meant complications.
“Marcello,” Supreme Lord Ranell sneered. “Don’t tell me the Demi-Immortal Race decided to get a piece of the action, too.”
While the Demi-Immortals were a diverse group, their relationship with the Ogres was famously terrible.
Marcello’s gaze was hard and cold.“A piece of the action?” he scoffed. “Our race has been involved from the start. And we won’t be demanding a thing from the young man.”
“What are you talking about?” Ranell asked, his curiosity piqued.
The other Supreme Lords leaned in, just as confused.
The Demi-Immortal pointed to the tiny grain of sand spinning in space. 
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION SWELL]
“Because the young man inside that treasure is one of us.”
[SFX: COLLECTIVE SHOCKED MURMURS]
The announcement sent a ripple of shock and anger through the assembled Lords.
“One of your race? I don’t buy it,” Ranell snorted. “For all we know, he’s an Ogre.”
The other Supreme Lords quickly muttered their agreement. There was no way they were just going to take Marcello’s word for it.
But Marcello remained perfectly calm. “Zane passed our race’s test and earned the golden celestial rank. He is an important member of our people.”
[MUSIC: TENSE DELIBERATION]
This was a genuine shock. Golden celestial ranks were incredibly rare, appearing only a few times in a grand era. 
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
But they all quickly masked their surprise with skepticism. Who knew if he was just bluffing? It could just be a flimsy excuse to take the young man—and the Crown of Destiny—for himself. Not a chance.
“So what if he has a golden celestial rank?” one of the Supreme Lords muttered with contempt.
Marcello smiled, understanding their hostility perfectly. 
“Don’t worry,” he said. “I’m not here to fight you for the Crown of Destiny. It’s important, sure, but my race doesn’t value it that highly.”
He paused. “I have two things to say. The first is for all of you.” His tone didn’t change, but something in it did. “The old man of our clan says: anyone who kills Zane — Supreme Lord or not — the Demi-Immortal Race will come for them.”
Thirty Supreme Lords went quiet. They knew who Marcello meant. The old man of the Demi-Immortal Race stood at the very peak of this universe — the same level as the leaders of the three great human powers, the Demon Night’s highest authority, the Ogres’ supreme elder. There were only a handful of beings at that tier in all of existence. 
“The second message is for Zane.” Marcello looked at the grain of sand. “The old man says, Come visit when you can. He has things to discuss with you.”
Without another word, Marcello dissolved into motes of light and was gone.
“Hey, kid, you hear that?” another Supreme Lord yelled toward the grain of sand. “The Demi-Immortals say they’ll protect you. Why don’t you come on out of that Elysian Keep, and we’ll spare your life. If you don’t, I’ll sever the Vitality between heaven and earth, and you’ll be trapped in there for a million years. You’re only at the Soul Sea Realm! You won’t live for more than a hundred thousand, so don’t test our patience!”
A single empty threat from the Demi-Immortal Race wasn’t about to scare them off. These Supreme Lords had all the time in the world.
“Do you really think a few words are enough to scare us?” Ranell sneered. “Let’s see how long you can hold out, kid.”
The Queen of Punishment’s expression darkened further. The Demon Night had always had close ties with the Demi-Immortal Race — nearly a third of their most talented young warriors had gone through the Demi-Immortal clan’s trials over the years. If she killed Zane now, the fallout would extend far beyond this moment. She was trapped between the only thing she wanted and every reason she couldn’t have it.
Hearing their threats, a sharp light flashed in Zane’s eyes. He turned to Saul. 
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING STRINGS]
“Looks like there’s no reason to hold back. Since these guys are so stubborn, let’s just do what you suggested.”
Saul smiled. “You’re too soft on them. Killing a few Supreme Lords is no big deal. Besides, their deaths will free up more Crowns of Destiny. I can’t wait to see what kind of chaos that kicks up.”
[SFX: ENERGY BEAM WHOOSH]
The moment he finished speaking, a beam of light shot out from the grain of sand, so fast that none of the Supreme Lords had time to react.
