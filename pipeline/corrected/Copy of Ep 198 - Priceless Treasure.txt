Ep 198 - Priceless Treasure
Word count: 1150
[AMBIENT: PARTY CHATTER]
[MUSIC: UPBEAT SOCIAL GATHERING]
The table Yates had picked out for her was the last place Ingrid wanted to be, so she steered her friends toward the one where Zane and the others were already sitting. Soon enough, their corner of the room was alive with chatter and laughter.
Rhys, Ethan, and Dustin dove right into the cheerful conversation. In the Eastern Territory, tradition dictated that women stayed reserved and let the men lead, but at a party for the Great Houses, no one paid much attention to those antiquated rules. Laughter kept erupting from their table.
You had to hand it to Ethan---the kid was a born optimist. Even after getting pushed around by nearly everyone when he first got home, here he was, talking and laughing like nothing had happened. It was as if he'd completely wiped the ugliness of the afternoon from his memory. Every few minutes, he'd crack a joke that sent the girls into fits of giggles.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
As James led Yates to their table, their laughter felt like tiny daggers in Yates’ heart.
“Miss Solomon,” James greeted Ingrid, his smile a little too wide. “I’m so honored you could attend the House Moon party today!” He was laying it on thick.
As the head of House Moon, James was a powerful man. His family was influential, and he was a major player in the Soul Realm. But even for the treasured daughter of the elevated House Solomon, this level of fawning was a bit much.
When someone's that polite, it usually means they want something. In this case, it was painfully obvious what that was.
Word traveled fast among the Great Houses, and everyone knew his son, Yates, had a massive crush on Ingrid. James also knew that while House Moon had seen better days, a marriage alliance with House Solomon could rocket them right back to the top.
[SFX: BOX OPENING]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]
With a smooth, practiced air, he opened a simple but elegant box. Inside, a stunning red bracelet rested on a velvet cushion, seeming to glow from within. “This is a Bloodstone Bracelet,” James announced. “A small token of our respect, Miss Solomon. Please, accept it.”
[MUSIC: TENSE UNDERTONE]
The second Ethan laid eyes on the bracelet, the smile fell from his face, replaced by a dark, thunderous scowl. But everyone else was so mesmerized by the gift that his sudden shift went completely unnoticed.
Bloodstone was an incredibly rare treasure.
[AMBIENT: VOLCANIC RUMBLING]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS MYSTICAL]
Deep in the northern continent, Mount Frostfire’s molten flow carried deadly poison. Those exposed faced blackened faces, boils, and death. Common folk avoided the mountain, especially near eruptions. Only Adepts could safely harvest the fresh, lethal liquid.
[SFX: VOLCANIC ERUPTION]
These Adepts risked everything, traveling to Mount Frostfire to harvest the Bloodstone that was blasted from the mountain’s core during an eruption. But the mountain rarely gave up its treasures. Only a few dozen to a few hundred pieces of Bloodstone were found each year, forcing most Adepts to rely on pure luck. Finding even one or two pieces meant you were set for life. A single Bloodstone was worth so much it could make an Adept rich overnight.
When worn, the stone infused the skin with blood and vitality. Over time, it would strengthen the wearer's own life force, boosting their power and even elevating their spirit.
These incredible properties made the stone priceless.
Finding a piece larger than a small stone was almost unheard of. Most people considered themselves lucky to find one just big enough for a pendant.
[SFX: JEWELRY CLINK]
[MUSIC: WONDER AND AWE]
The bracelet James was offering appeared to be carved from a single, fist-sized piece of Bloodstone. If that was true, it was worth an absolute fortune.
But the price tag never even crossed Ingrid's mind.
Her standing in House Solomon meant she could have almost anything she asked for---even the family’s most sacred heirloom could be hers in an instant. On her last trip to Alba Imperia, she'd threatened to sell that very heirloom, the Aethelstone of Solomon, just to make Lennie cough up the money to bail Zane out of trouble.
Compared to an irreplaceable family treasure like that, Ingrid owned plenty of things that could easily outshine this bracelet. So, while it was a priceless rarity to everyone else, to Ingrid, it was just another trinket.
None of that mattered, though. She couldn’t accept it. She wasn’t a close friend or relative of House Moon, which made accepting such an extravagant gift completely inappropriate---especially since this was the first time she'd ever met James or even set foot in his mansion.
After studying the jewelry for a moment, Ingrid slowly shook her head. “Uncle Moon, I’m sorry, but I can’t accept your generous gift.”
Yates felt his heart sink at her refusal, but he couldn’t take no for an answer. He gently lifted the Bloodstone Bracelet from its box. “Miss Solomon,” he pleaded, “my father only wishes to show his admiration. It's a small gesture. Please don't say no.”
Zane set down his cup.
James and Yates pressing her after a clear refusal — that was enough. He was about to say something.
But Ethan beat him to it.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
But before Ingrid could respond, Ethan, who had been dead silent, shot to his feet.
“Sorry to interrupt,” he said, his voice dripping with sarcasm as he stared at James. “I was just wondering something, uncle.”
James's brow furrowed in annoyance at the interruption, but he forced a smile. “What is it, Ethan?”
[SFX: DRAMATIC SILENCE]
Ethan looked him dead in the eye. “When my father died, you denied him the funeral rites of a Great House, and I was wrong not to fight you on it. I let you walk all over me when I came home because it didn’t seem worth the trouble. But now... I only have one question. My father’s Bloodstone bracelet vanished after he died. So why is it in your hand right now?”
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
Ethan’s question sent a jolt of panic through James. A flicker of horror crossed his face before he smoothed it back into a blank smile, reminding himself of his status. “You’re confused, Ethan,” he said with practiced calm. “This isn’t the same bracelet your father owned.”
“Oh, really?” Ethan shot back. “Uncle, do you think I’m a fool? Or just too young to remember? Either way, you’re wrong. I remember every detail of my father’s bracelet. On the inside, it has ’Good Fortune’ carved in large script, with tiny runes etched beside it. So tell me, uncle, is there really another one just like it in the world?”
[SFX: GASPS]
The moment the words left Ethan’s lips, every head turned to the bracelet in Yates's hand. Just as Ethan had described, the words “Good Fortune” were clearly visible on the inner band.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL REVELATION]
Every eye in the room snapped from the bracelet to James and Yates. The two of them stood frozen, their faces pale masks of horror, utterly trapped with no way to squirm out of the disaster they had created.
