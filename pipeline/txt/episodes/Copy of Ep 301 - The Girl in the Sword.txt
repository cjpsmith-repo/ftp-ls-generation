Ep 301 - The Girl in the Sword
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ENCHANTING]
Word count: 1000
Zane stared in awe at the stunning woman who’d just materialized from his sword. According to the Cobalt Dragon, she was most likely the sword’s original owner. And she was perfect in every possible way.
But Zane’s sharp eye caught subtle nuances that distinguished her from ordinary humans. Her ears, most notably, captured his attention. Long and elegantly slender, they tapered to delicate, angular points reminiscent of a cat’s, but with an ethereal quality all their own.
Despite this otherworldly feature—or maybe because of it—she radiated an irresistible charm that left him momentarily breathless. Her legs stretched longer than those of typical women, yet they complemented her willowy frame so well that he couldn’t imagine her proportioned any other way.
As he studied her, Zane felt an inexplicable connection, as though her very presence resonated with something deep within his soul.
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL REALM]
"Why assume such beauty belongs only to humans?" The Cobalt Dragon remarked thoughtfully, his voice carrying ancient wisdom. "The people of the Demon Night have looked this way since time immemorial. From their point of view, we’re the ones who look like them, not the other way around."
"Wait..." Zane’s voice faltered as realization dawned on him. "Are you saying this girl is real? She actually exists? " The possibility sent his thoughts spiraling in unexpected directions, a mixture of fascination and disbelief washing over him.
[SFX: SWORD RESONANCE]
The Cobalt Dragon nodded. "Every sword on Killing Sword Mountain belongs to someone, and they’re all alive. If an owner dies, their sword is buried deep within the mountain. The fact that her sword is here means she’s still out there, somewhere in the world."
Zane's gaze lingered on the girl, a flicker of awe and confusion in his eyes.
So his sword spirit had, for whatever reason, taken the form of a real woman from Demon Night. What would it be like to meet the real her someday?
Of course, the odds of that were basically zero. According to the Cobalt Dragon, Demon Night wasn’t even in the same world as his.
[MUSIC: GENTLE REVELATION]
"I’ll call her Lily," Zane said with a playful grin.
Having a sword spirit this developed was a huge power-up for Zane. At the very least, Lily could act as a backup, helping him push higher up Killing Sword Mountain. It was more than just a mystical force; he felt a real spiritual connection to her, like she had a life all her own.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN RIDGE]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ENCOUNTER]
Just as he reached the mountain ridge, another figure materialized before him—a spirit cloaked in blue.
[SFX: SPIRIT MANIFESTATION]
"What? A kid at the Second Level of the Adept Realm?" the blue figure demanded, his voice dripping with disbelief. "How did you make it to the mountain ridge?"
Startled, Zane's hand tightened on his sword hilt. "You’re a sword spirit... but you can talk?" he shot back. "It’s like you have a fragment of a soul!"
Zane was floored. He’d cut down plenty of powerful sword spirits, even ones that could manifest a physical form, but they were all just mindless puppets. Their swordplay was flawless, but they were predictable. Zane could always outsmart them because they couldn’t think.
But this blue figure didn’t just talk; he seemed genuinely surprised.
He clearly had a mind of his own—and maybe even a sliver of a soul.
"I just don’t see how a Second Level Adept got past the mountainside," the blue figure muttered, shaking his head. He looked more annoyed than confused. "Well, this is as far as you go. Plant your sword. There’s no space for you any higher."
Of course, everyone wanted to plant their sword as high as possible. The higher you went, the richer the resources you could draw from Killing Sword Mountain.
But those resources were finite, just like at the Skyreach Sect.
That’s why every blade on the mountain, from the Mysterious and Spirit all the way up to Mythic weapons, would unleash its spirit to stop anyone climbing past. You had to earn every single step with your own strength.
[MUSIC: TENSE DETERMINATION]
The blue figure’s decree just made Zane’s jaw tighten.
He was just grateful for the chance to be on Killing Sword Mountain at all; he’d be damned if he didn’t climb as high as he possibly could.
He'd fought tooth and nail just to get this far, with his new spirit at his back. Why the hell would he stop now?
In the real world, Zane knew he could never take on someone of this blue figure's caliber — a cultivator so powerful that even Sir Kenneth might be outmatched.
But this was Killing Sword Mountain. And all this guy was is a sword with a tiny piece of a soul.
How could Zane possibly back down?
"I’m not planting my sword here," Zane stated flatly. "I’m going higher."
The blue figure let out a hollow laugh. "Higher? You, a Second Level Adept? Kid, I was aiming for the summit of Killing Sword Mountain itself. Turns out, I wasn’t strong enough."
"I have my own sword spirit. I made it this far," Zane countered, a cold smirk on his lips as he raised his sword. "What makes you so sure you can stop me?" He had no time for this ghost’s life story. He just needed to cut him down and keep climbing.
"Arrogant punk!" the spirit roared. "I’ve spent hundreds of years honing my craft! My understanding of the blade is beyond your comprehension. I am more than enough to stop you!"
Suddenly, the blue figure’s sword flared, unleashing waves of frigid air. The blade became encased in an icy blue aura, its killing intent so powerful it seemed to freeze the very air.
Zane just sneered. "Hundreds of years, and you’re still stuck guarding a ridge? Sounds to me like you’re not that strong after all."
[SFX: SWORD POWER SURGE]
Without another word, Zane channeled his sword spirit. Instantly, his Streamer exploded with a killing intent so potent that it felt like it was clawed straight out of hell.
