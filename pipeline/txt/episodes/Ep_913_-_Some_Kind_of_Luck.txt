Ep 913 - Some Kind of Luck

Word count: 1037

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Across the world of chaos, warriors from the Soul Sea Realm were used to being at the top of the food chain. It had been a long, long time since anyone had dared to look down on them. 

So, even though their new captors were impossibly strong, most of them were seething. Not that the newcomers seemed to care.

The flower-like pattern in Dorothea’s eyes spun slowly as she spoke, her voice distant and cool. “They’re piloting these bodies with Soul Cores. All I have to do is disrupt the cores’ power, and their souls will be trapped inside these corpses forever.” 

A soft, chilling laugh escaped her lips. “Max must have some kind of luck to have these just delivered to us. They’ll make perfect bait. His luck really never runs out, does it?”

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

As her laughter faded, a thin, pink mist drifted out from the flower in her eye.

[MUSIC: DARK MYSTICAL CHIMES]

“The power of fragrance,” she murmured. “Spiritual Confinement.”

[SFX: MAGICAL PULSE]

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

A faint, sweet smell spread through the air, washing over the captive warriors. In an instant, they all felt it—the connection to the bodies they were controlling had been severed. They couldn’t move a muscle.

Panic erupted in their minds.

[SFX: DISTORTED VOICES]

“The Soul Core… it’s stopped working! How is this possible?”

“It was that woman! She did something to the cores!”

“I can’t get my soul out of this body!”

[SFX: MAGICAL SHATTERING]

These masters had hijacked their new bodies using the Soul Cores, but with a single, effortless move, Dorothea had shattered that link. But the true horror was beginning to sink in. A few of them, thinking they could just abandon the bodies and flee, tried to pull their souls free. It was useless. They found themselves locked tight inside the skulls of their hosts, with no way out.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

“Stop trying,” Dorothea said, her voice flat, as if she could sense their frantic, silent struggles. “A handful of souls from the Fighting Soul Realm can’t break my Spiritual Confinement.”

[SFX: HEARTBEAT]

At that moment, a cold dread washed over Zane. He was frozen, too.

He was inside the girl’s body—a living one—but he was still relying on a Soul Core to control it. The Soul Sealing Spell placed on her prevented anyone from taking her over completely, so the core had been his only option.

Dorothea moved slowly down the line of frozen warriors. Then she stopped.

She was looking at Zane.

“Interesting,” she said, tilting her head. “Why is there a living body here?” She stepped closer, examining Brianna's face with open curiosity. “And at such a level… controlled by a Soul Core. How very strange.”

She leaned in. Before Zane could do anything — not that he could — she pressed her lips to his cheek.

“If I don't need this body later,” she said softly, “I think I'll keep you.”

[SFX: SHARP CRACK]

[SFX: THUNDER]

Inside Zane's mind, lightning split the sky of Brianna's inner world. Thunder rolled across the island. Her soul was shaking with fury.

“Disgusting. Zane. I'm going to kill her.”

“I can't even move,” Zane said. “I can't protect myself, let alone go after anyone.”

Brianna's rage simmered but didn't fade.

[SFX: SYNCHRONIZED FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]

“Now, everyone turn around,” Dorothea said, a faint smile playing on her lips as she walked past the line of frozen warriors. In perfect, eerie unison, every single body turned to follow her.

“Who are these people?” Zane asked quietly. “Do you know them?”

Brianna's answer came with a cold edge. “They're from the Demi-Immortal Race. Hunters.”

Zane went still. The Demi-Immortal Race — he'd encountered them before, on the main continent. The Tower of Sin had belonged to them. He'd passed their Talent Tablet test. But he'd never truly understood what they were.

“What do they want?”

“The same thing they always want,” Brianna said. “To destroy the Celestial Law and end the cycle of reincarnation. They call themselves Resetters. They're not a tribe — they're a collection of super geniuses from across every race who decided that the best thing for the universe is to return it to zero. No Celestial Law. No reincarnation. No living things born, no living things dying. Just silence.”

Zane turned this over. “What's the point of that?”

[AMBIENT: MENTAL ECHO CHAMBER]

“They think it’s important, which just proves they’re a bunch of idiots,” Brianna's voice echoed in his mind, laced with exhaustion. “Very powerful idiots, though. That woman just disrupted the Soul Cores’ power, but I can fix it. When I get a chance, I’ll restore the connection, and you can get out of here.”

“But what about them?” Zane asked, his gaze fixed on the captive warriors—all the top masters from the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms, including their four rulers, were now puppets.

“Who cares if they live or die?” she shot back.

Zane frowned. She had a point; it wasn’t his problem. He didn’t owe the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms anything. Still, if there was a chance to save them, he couldn’t just walk away.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Meanwhile, the bodies under Dorothea’s control shuffled forward, following her like a procession of zombies. The warriors trapped inside could only watch, helpless, as their own bodies marched them toward an unknown fate. As they moved, drakes began to swarm out from the surrounding landscape, rushing toward the strange parade.

Dorothea didn’t even break her stride. 

[SFX: BEAST ROARS]

[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]

Just as the beasts were about to pounce, the flower in her eye spun gently, releasing another wave of that sweet fragrance. The drakes stumbled, their aggressive charge turning into a drunken stagger before they collapsed, completely immobilized.

[AMBIENT: FOOTSTEPS ON GROUND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ATMOSPHERE]

After a few more minutes of slow, steady marching, they passed Crystal Lake and continued toward the center of the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSIONS]

[SFX: GROUND RUMBLING]

Suddenly, a series of loud, booming explosions echoed from just ahead. The entire ground began to shake violently, trembling as if it were about to crack wide open.
