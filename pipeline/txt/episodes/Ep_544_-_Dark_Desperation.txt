Killing Zane wasn't the answer. The real problem was finding a way to break the Holy Maiden's losing streak.
Still, Zane had pissed off the Divine Kingdom, and he was going to pay for it.

The Holy Maiden and the man with her were the weakest of the Divine Kingdom's group, but even they were Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors. The black-robed figures standing behind them were even scarier—all at the Life-and-Death Realm.
"I don't care what it takes. I want him dead," the Holy Maiden of the Divine Kingdom said, her voice like ice.
Zane acted like he hadn't heard a thing, just sitting quietly on his disc and waiting for the light to find him again.

She stepped onto it.
"Extreme Icy Zone."
Zane exhaled. As the seventh brother predicted, all six options were punishments. But Tia's luck would guide her to the easiest.
[SFX: GLASS SHATTERING]
[SFX: ICE MAGIC WHOOSH]

"You okay?" Zane asked.

Zane shook his head. Remarkable.
The Holy Maiden's disappointment flickered across her face.
Then the beam landed on her.
Her face fell.
[MUSIC: GROWING DREAD]
Her calamity curse hadn't been lifted. Whatever she rolled, it would be a punishment. The only question was whether she could survive it.

[SFX: DIE ROLL]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Her expression darkened. Four discs materialized beneath her feet. The fourth one pulsed with slow, rolling circles of black light.
The man from the Divine Kingdom stepped forward, his voice tight. "Little Sister—if you can't hold on, use the protective treasure."
The Holy Maiden gave a single, stiff nod. Then she stepped forward onto the black disc.
[SFX: DARK MAGICAL RESONANCE]
"Dark Desperation."
[MUSIC: DIRE TENSION]

"She won't last three breaths."
"That black light… it reminds me of the Death Ray."
"Whatever it is, she can't block it."

[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]

[SFX: POWERFUL LAUGH]
A bold laugh rang through the chamber, carrying weight that silenced everyone.
White light lanced outward, shredding the black. The Holy Maiden stood on the disc, pale but alive. Above her, a translucent figure dissolved into nothing.
She'd summoned an inherited protective treasure to break the punishment—the kind only powerful bloodlines possessed.
"Little Sister." The man from the Divine Kingdom rushed forward. "Are you alright?"
"I'm fine," she said quietly. "But the treasure is spent."
[MUSIC: AGGRESSIVE TENSION]

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
