Ep 553 - Sun Moon Stars
Word count: 1097
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Zane stared at the jagged crack marring the crystal ball. Vesper’s voice was fading, growing weaker with every word. "I have a token," Zane said, his brow furrowed with worry. "But your crystal ball… It's cracked. Are you okay?"
Vesper’s voice crackled with an anxious energy that defied his weakening state."Forget about me! Show me the token! Now!"
Zane didn’t hesitate. He reached into his Storage Ring and pulled out the token. It’s not like The Elysian Keep was doing him any good just sitting in there. The small artifact, etched with the word “wind,” had to be some kind of key.
The crystal ball floated closer. From within, Vesper seemed to stare at the token in Zane’s hand before letting out a long sigh. “So that’s it. You’re the new owner of the Elysian Keep.”
"Then… you're…" Zane’s words caught in his throat.
A laugh echoed from Vesper, and the crystal ball spun toward a nearby wall.
[SFX: STONE GRINDING]
A low grinding sound rumbled from deep within the stone, and a section of the wall slid open. “Go on. See what’s back there.”
Curious, Zane walked over to the opening and pulled out two tokens. One was an exact copy of his own, right down to the word “wind” carved into its surface.
But the other was etched with the word “thunder.”
The shape and style were almost identical to his. It was obvious all three belonged to the Elysian Keep.
Seeing the stunned look on Zane’s face, Vesper’s voice came out calm and steady.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
"I am the third owner of the Elysian Keep.”
Zane took a sharp breath. He remembered learning that the Keep had passed through several hands before his. He’d passed the trials to become its new master, but he never, ever expected to meet the third one here.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
In a celestial tomb, of all places.
“And this one?” Zane asked, his voice trailing off as he held up the token etched with the word “thunder.”
Vesper seemed to smile.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
“The ‘thunder’ token is the key to the True Path—a special course within the Elysian Keep. I only ever managed to activate it once myself.”
"Where did the Elysian Keep come from?" Zane asked. "Why would the First Host just leave it behind?"
“The Elysian Keep has no name,” Vesper said. “The First Host never gave it one, and if they didn’t, then no one is worthy of that honor. Not me. Not you. Not the second owner. Not a single soul in all the hundred thousand great worlds.”
Zane blinked, completely stunned.
[MUSIC: QUIET WEIGHT]
A hundred thousand great worlds. Even the True Dragon Realm — legendary, ancient, beyond the reach of almost any living being — was just one among them. And none of those worlds were worthy of naming the Elysian Keep? It seemed almost too much to believe.
"Since you've become its master," Vesper continued, "the thunder token is yours. Consider it a gift. You can't enter the True Path yet — not until you've broken through into the Life-and-Death Realm. As for the Keep's origins… I can't tell you much. Only this: it is far larger than anything you've imagined."
Vesper paused, then shifted. "You became a temporary master, so you should have received a visualization diagram. Which one did you get?"
"The Sun-Moon-Star Diagram," Zane said.
[SFX: CRYSTAL TREMBLING]
The crystal ball shuddered violently and dropped straight to the floor with a sharp crack. A new fracture bloomed across its surface.
"Vesper, are you alright?"
The ball slowly rose again, and Vesper spoke, his voice unsteady. "I'm fine. Are you certain? The Sun-Moon-Star Diagram?"
Zane nodded. He drew a thin thread of energy from his palm, and tiny motes of starlight drifted out from it.
A long silence followed. "The starlight visualization… You actually obtained the last diagram. The one visualized by the first master themselves. That supreme existence…" Vesper trailed off, muttering to himself.
"Who was the first master?" Zane asked.
"Someone whose identity you are not yet able to comprehend," Vesper said simply. "You don't need to know."
"Forget it," Vesper added, his voice dropping. "I'm a dead man who can't even enter the cycle of reincarnation. Not even as a blade of grass. What right do I have to lecture anyone?"
[MUSIC: MILD TENSION]
Something stirred in Zane's chest. For all the strangeness of this encounter, there was something quietly tragic about Vesper—a man who had cut away his own soul and waited two thousand years in the dark.
He hadn't expected to feel sorry for an immortal.
He spoke carefully. "Vesper, you still haven't told me—why did you set up the luck trial? What were you looking for?"
Vesper's voice carried mild embarrassment. "When I discovered you had the Elysian Keep token, it threw me off. That wasn't something I anticipated. But I still haven't finished calculating your karmic luck. Return to the chart."
[SFX: MAGICAL GLOW]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL PALACE]
As he stepped back, words began to glow once more on the walls of the Grand Palace of Starlight.
“The Sun.”
“Shade Star.”
“Seven Red.”
“Eight White.”
“Four Green.”
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: EPIC CRESCENDO]
Golden light flared, and more threads shot out with every passing second, streaking past Zane's body. There were enough of them to swallow him whole, yet every single one kept a careful distance.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
Starlight Astrology was a dizzyingly complex art, one most outsiders could never hope to comprehend.
Vesper started muttering to himself, calculating Zane’s fate. Zane had to strain to make out the muffled words.
About fifteen minutes later, Vesper’s voice finally cut through the noise.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS CRESCENDO]
“Two Black and Five Yellow… no, wait. The Sun and the Moon… that’s not it, either. It’s still not right! Ah! I see it now! You are destined to lead the world into chaos!”
[SFX: CRYSTAL TREMBLING]
Before Vesper could say more, the crystal ball trembled violently and crashed to the ground.
[SFX: CRACKS]
The cracks that already marred its top and bottom were now joined by a fresh spiderweb of fractures spreading from its center. Zane found himself wondering if Vesper even had eyes in there. If he did, the web of cracks would have made it impossible to see a thing.
“Vesper, are you alright?”
That was the third time in just a few minutes the crystal ball had hit the floor.
[SFX: EERIE FLOATING]
[MUSIC: UNSETTLING AMBIENCE]
[SFX: HOLLOW LAUGH]
After rolling a half-turn on the dusty ground, the sphere slowly floated back into the air. A hollow, rattling laugh rang out from within it.
Was his brain damaged? Zane thought, then caught himself. Vesper was just a fragment of a soul. Technically, he didn't even have a brain.
But the laughter kept coming — and it didn't sound like madness.
It sounded like relief.
