But I do possess a Divine-level talent." He paused, letting the weight of his words settle over the crowd. "It's merely a matter of time before I reach the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. "There's nothing in the histories about it. The entire Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms has produced only six individuals of that caliber, and every single one founded their own divine kingdom!"

"Unbelievable. A powerful father and Divine-level talent? The man stands in a realm beyond us!"

The murmurs rippled through the assembled warriors like wind through tall grass. Eden absorbed their awe, feeling it wash over him like warm sunlight. The corners of his mouth curved into a proud smirk, though something harder glinted in his eyes—a hunger that went beyond mere satisfaction.

He'd waited years to make this declaration, to watch doubt and dismissal transform into wonder and fear. His father's influence had opened doors, certainly, but this talent was his alone. The power thrumming beneath his skin, the cultivation potential that set him apart from these ordinary practitioners—that belonged to him.

He let his gaze drift across the crowd, savoring each widened eye, each slack jaw. Yes, they understood now. They finally grasped what stood before them.

[AMBIENT: QUIET CONVERSATION]

Listening to the chatter, Brent from the Ethereal Spirit Sect leaned toward Philip. "Philip, he says he has Divine-level talent, too! You told me that it was almost impossible."

Philip just sighed and rolled his eyes, staying quiet. Brent could be such a handful.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

But secretly, Philip had to agree. It was strange how many people with incredible abilities were popping up in the Heartlands all of a sudden. It felt like the power of the disciples across all the sects had skyrocketed overnight. In the past, it was a huge deal for even a top disciple from a major sect to grasp a sliver of Law Power while still in the Core-Forged Realm. But over the last decade, more and more warriors at that level were showing up who had already mastered it.

It was like geniuses were suddenly sprouting up like weeds.

But now, the Ethereal Spirit Sect alone had at least ten warriors with Saint-level talent, and even one with a Divine-level one.

What was happening to the Heartlands? How could it suddenly produce so many prodigies?

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

The disciples from Cloud Hall thought Eden was insufferably arrogant. Benson, in particular, was itching for a chance to knock the guy down a peg in the arena.

But after finding out Eden had a Divine-level talent, a wave of helplessness washed over him.

As a warrior with Celestial-level talent himself, Benson was already considered one of the top elites at Cloud Hall.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Benson strode toward Eden, extending his hand. "Enough! Let's settle this in the arena!"

Eden ignored him, smiling at Tia instead. "Lord of Cloud Hall, please accept my gift."

Tia wanted to tell this arrogant youth where to put his gift, but the consequences gave her pause. Maddox's presence loomed large. Though he hadn't spoken, this matter must have received his approval. What scheme were father and son plotting?

Laughter erupted from below. "Haha, you think you can trick my Lord with a Relic-grade weapon? Do you believe the Lord of Cloud Hall is some child who'll follow you for a piece of candy?"

The speaker was Zane.

Maddox fixed his cold stare on him. "Who are you to speak of Relic-grade weapons? Have you even seen one?"

Zane said nothing; he just walked towards Tia.

[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]

Light flashed as he withdrew protective armor from his spatial ring.

"Little Tia, this armor's a bit ugly, but it's still Relic-grade." He walked directly before her and placed the armor—issued by the Dragon Bloodline Clan—upon her shoulders.

Tia froze. She hadn't even identified the armor's grade before hearing Zane call her "Little Tia" before this massive crowd. Anger flashed across her face, but before she could unleash her fury, Zane fitted the armor onto her.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: MAGICAL DISCOVERY]

The moment it was on Tia's body, ripples of light danced across its surface, forming an energy shield that enveloped her completely. Simultaneously, a warm aura flowed into her, increasing her physical strength by half.

Within that warm embrace, Zane's offensive behavior seemed insignificant. Defensive treasures commanded far greater value than offensive ones. If someone offered a mid-grade Relic weapon for this armor, she would refuse without hesitation.

"This armor... not bad," Tia said, smiling. "Just a bit ugly, but I can make do."

Zane smiled back at her. "It's fine if it's ugly. I'll find you a prettier one later."

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]

[SFX: SURPRISED CHATTER]

In the stands, several warriors toppled over.

"Whoa, Zane has guts. He just completely trashed Eden's Heavenly Feather Crown. Eden must want the ground to swallow him whole! And Zane promised to find her a prettier one? Are Relic-Class weapons really that easy to come by?"

"Zane is so generous! He just got a Relic-Class weapon and gave it away like it was pocket change."

[MUSIC: TENSE UNDERTONE]

As the crowd’s chatter shifted from the garment to Zane and Tia, a shadow fell over Eden’s face.

This guy, a mere fourth-grade soul-illuminated cultivator, had just pulled out a piece of Relic-Class protective armor and gifted it away like it was nothing.

Even though the Heavenly Feather Crown was also a Relic-Class weapon, it was just a crown—an accessory. It couldn't begin to compare to the value of a full suit of protective armor.

[SFX: FRUSTRATED SIGH]

But what really bothered Eden was who in the hell made that armor? And why was it so damn ugly?

It never occurred to him that the Relic-Class weapon was just a standard-issue uniform for officers and soldiers of the Dragonfall Palace. Almost everyone there had a set. It was armor designed for pure function, not fashion.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PAUSE]

In reality, the World Merchants' Guild didn't just have the one publicly known Relic-Class weapon, the Time Copper Coin.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

Yet the two secret Relic-Class weapons hidden in the Guild's vault paled in comparison to the protective armor Zane had just given to Tia.
