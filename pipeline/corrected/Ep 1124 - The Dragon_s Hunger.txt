Ep 1124 - The Dragon’s Hunger

Word count: 1133

[MUSIC: MAGICAL CONTEMPLATION]
As Zane took in all this information, the man in white robes nodded. “The Radiant Light Sect is the largest secret sect in the human world. Have you ever considered joining us?”
Freya stood at Zane’s side, her eyes wide with curiosity. She was a warrior who had ascended, same as Zane, and she knew just as little about this Upper World. She’d managed to dig up some basic information about Zane’s destination, which was the only reason she’d been able to answer his questions on the road. But a secret sect? This was completely new to her.
Zane didn’t answer the man’s question.
The man didn’t press Zane. Instead, with a look of earnest sincerity, he gestured for them to follow him toward the sect’s headquarters.
The building was huge and imposing. 
[AMBIENT: MASSIVE CROWD MURMURING]
As they stepped inside, Zane was met with the sight of people—everywhere. The vast square was packed with what looked like at least a million souls. There were all kinds of people, from old men to children barely ten years old, most of them ordinary folks with no combat training.
[AMBIENT: PRAYER CHANTING]
They were arranged in concentric circles, kneeling on the ground and murmuring prayers toward the center of the square. Zane noticed that the people in the inner circles had higher levels of training; he could even sense the power of about a dozen Spirit Supreme masters near the front. The outer rings, meanwhile, were filled with common citizens.
Finally, Zane’s gaze fell on the focal point of all this devotion: a large, transparent sphere. A light green liquid sloshed around inside, filling about a third of it. 
[SFX: PSYCHIC HUM]
As he watched, he felt a faint pressure brush against his mind, a psychic weight emanating from the sphere. It wasn’t malicious, but it wasn’t benevolent, either. It was just… there, a constant, low-level hum of power that didn’t seem to have any real effect.
Then he felt something else — a faint stirring against his side. His bestiary bag.
[SFX: MUFFLED THUMPING]
He sent a thread of his perception inside to investigate. What he found surprised him. The Ancestral Dragon was throwing itself against the walls of the bag — small head leading, over and over, with single-minded aggression. 
A few feet away, the Heavenly Phoenix Horse had pressed itself to the far corner, watching the little creature warily.
“Settle down,” Zane told it silently. “Now isn’t the time.”
The Ancestral Dragon had always been obedient — in its mind, Zane was the one who had brought it into the world. But today it refused to stop.
[SFX: WHISPERED QUESTION]
“What is that?” Freya whispered.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVERENT]
[SFX: ARMS SPREADING]
The man in white spread his arms wide, his face suddenly alight with fervent worship. “This is our faith. Our God is still in its embryonic stage. As long as we offer our most sincere devotion, our True God will grow, reveal Himself, and deliver every living creature from suffering!”
He’d clearly given this speech a thousand times; the words rolled off his tongue effortlessly, just like Freya reciting place names from a map. But every glance and gesture was practiced, designed to sell the message. You could see how easy it would be to get swept up in it, to let yourself be brainwashed by the sheer conviction.
It was a good pitch, probably effective on regular people or even warriors with weaker wills. But Zane felt nothing, and he could see a flicker of disapproval on Freya’s face.
[SFX: TELEPATHIC VOICE ECHO]
Just then, Lavender’s voice echoed in his head. *What he’s saying sounds holy, but it’s just a way to gather Faith Energy.*
She went further. Long ago, she said, a being known as Malachi had spent one hundred thousand years collecting the Faith Energy of trillions of living beings — and used it to hatch a sword embryo. That sword was now ranked second on the Cosmic Spirit Index. The Radiant Light Sect’s appetite, it seemed, hadn’t been satisfied by that achievement. Now they were running the same play again, trying to hatch a second.
Zane remembered — Brianna Shaw had told him something about this once.
“So that sphere in the center of the square — is that the sword embryo?” he asked inwardly.
“No,” Lavender said. “It’s just a container. The light green liquid inside is concentrated Faith Energy, gathered over countless years. The embryo hasn’t formed yet.”
At that same moment, Lavender answered the question that had been nagging at him since the Ancestral Dragon started going wild in its bag. “It’s sensed its food.”
“Food?” Zane turned the word over. “Faith Energy?”
“Yes,” she said. “Faith Energy is the only thing that allows the Ancestral Dragon to truly grow.”
That explained everything. The moment they’d stepped into this shrine, the little creature had locked onto the sphere like a hunting dog catching a scent — and it hadn’t let go since.
Zane’s first thought was to release the Ancestral Dragon right here and let it devour the energy. But he couldn’t do that, not with so many people around. Besides, for all the years this energy had been accumulating, there wasn’t that much of it in the sphere. The thing clearly wasn’t going anywhere. He’d need a better plan, a real opportunity to get the Ancestral Dragon the meal it needed. 
For now, he’d just watch and wait.
With that thought, Zane turned to leave.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATIONAL]
[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT WHOOSH]
The white-robed warrior, who had been deep in his sermon-like trance, suddenly snapped to attention. Seeing Zane and Freya heading for the exit, he moved in a blur, appearing right in front of them. He tried to keep his friendly tone, but there was an edge to it now. “My friend, you’ve seen our Radiant Light Sect. Why not join us? The benefits are beyond anything you can imagine.”
Zane’s reply was quiet. “I don’t need any benefits.”
“Faith can purify your heart and mind,” the man insisted, his smile fixed in place. “It would do you good.”
“I don’t need it,” Zane said, his expression turning cold. “I thought I was clear.”
[MUSIC: OMINOUS THREATENING]
The man’s smile finally dropped. “Everyone in Auspice City knows that when you walk into the shrine of our Sect, there are only two ways you leave. You’re either carried out dead, or you’re kneeling in that square. You see,” he said, pointing to a section of the crowd, “even those members of Demon Night, the ones who worshiped their precious queens, ended up joining us.”
[AMBIENT: SUBDUED PRAYER MURMURS]
Zane followed his finger and saw them—a few grim-faced members of Demon Night, kneeling and praying with the rest of the flock.
