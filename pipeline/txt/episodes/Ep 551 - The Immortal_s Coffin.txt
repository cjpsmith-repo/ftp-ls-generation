Ep 551 - The Immortal's Coffin
Word count: 1116
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]
How could anyone be so damn chill?
Tia had seen Zane pull off miracles before, sure. Challenging fighters way out of his league and somehow winning? That was classic Zane. But surviving an attack from an immortal? That was something else entirely.
[SFX: MAGICAL EXPLOSION]
Everyone saw the protective amulet explode in a flash of power—the very thing that had saved his life.
If Tia was right, the amulet had to be from the Upper World. Nothing else could have blocked an immortal's power.
Zane's lazy smile earned him a death glare from Tia. "You bastard," she muttered under her breath.
She had given up, resigned herself to praying for a miracle that, to her bitter disappointment, never came. It was all on Zane and whatever tricks he had up his sleeve.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
The Holy Maiden of the Divine Kingdom’s eyes were dark and cold, like a bottomless lake at midnight.
She hadn't expected any trouble from these... ants from the Heartlands. She’d had her chance to kill Zane, to crush him, and she had failed. The fact was a bitter pill she couldn't swallow.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
Worse, it felt as if the other girl—Tia—had cursed her.
[MUSIC: INTENSE THREATENING DRUMS]
The Holy Maiden lowered her head, letting her hair curtain her face. "When I get home," she said, her voice a low simmer of rage, "I will bring my forces and burn the Heartlands to the ground."
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[SFX: MAGICAL SPINNING LIGHT]
As the beam of light spun, a knot of anxiety tightened in the fighters' chests.
The Wheel of Fortune was pure luck; there were no rules. Not even someone as strong as Maddox had been able to resist its pull. And yet, Zane had defied it, walking away from death's door without a scratch.
Like Tia, most people had figured it out: Zane had used something from the Upper World to block the immortal's deadly strike.
What they couldn't wrap their heads around was his sheer, dumb luck. Anyone else who'd set foot in the Dragon Soar Arena would've been locked away for a thousand years. But Zane? He didn't just escape; he came back loaded with treasures—Relic-Class armor, swords from the Upper World. And that amulet that had shattered to save him... it was clearly even more powerful than all the rest.
[AMBIENT: HEAVY SILENCE]
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
The other fighters stood in silence, their envy a bitter taste in their mouths. They had seen it all.
They had waited for decades, some for centuries, and had paid an enormous price just for a chance to light the Enchanted Lamps of the Celestial Tomb of Vesper.
Among them were warriors from the Life-and-Death Realm, leaders of powerful clans. And in the end, they were all shown up by some rookie from the Soul Realm.
"Don't get discouraged," someone said, though the words rang hollow. "First place doesn't guarantee he gets the Immortals' inheritance."
"True," someone else countered, "but it gives him a hell of an advantage."
[SFX: MAGICAL LIGHT]
The beam halted on Maddox. He punched the die—it showed five.
After five steps, he landed on an emerald disc. "The Light of Longevity rewards you with five hundred years of lifespan," the voice announced.
Murmurs rippled through the crowd.
"Five hundred years? His luck is extraordinary."
"Always has been. You don't reach the world's peak as a lone warrior without fortune."
Zane watched the green light envelop Maddox, his eyes narrowing. With such luck, finding the next Violet-Core Physique would be trivial—a realization that sent cold urgency through him. Time was running out.
The wheel continued. Most fighters had advanced ten to thirty steps toward the abyss's center. Zane still led, followed by Tia, then the Holy Maiden.
[MUSIC: BUILDING MOMENTUM]
At last, it was Zane's turn again.
He grinned. "Alright — one big push to the finish line."
[SFX: DIE ROLLING]
He punched the die hard. It tumbled and rolled... and landed on six. A yellow disc lit up beneath his feet.
"Throw again," the voice announced.
[AMBIENT: GASPS FROM CROWD]
Zane stepped onto the yellow disc without breaking stride and punched again. The die rolled.
Six.
[SFX: CROWD GROAN]
The crowd groaned.
Tia watched him, an unexpected sadness loosening in her chest. When they'd first met on the trial road, she'd noticed him only because he'd cultivated a Demon Race technique. Back then, his sole use had been breaking through the barrier in the flood dragon's stomach.
But he kept surprising her. His talent unfolded at frightening speed. On the martial path, he might surpass even her.
Yet time was running out. Without his desperate need to save his sister, thirty years would carry him into the Life-and-Death Realm. Such a waste.
Of course, Tia didn't know that the Life-and-Death Realm wasn't even close to Zane's real goal. He wanted the pinnacle—the very peak of the martial path and the fundamental mysteries beyond it.
[SFX: DIE ROLLING]
The die kept rolling—six after six after six.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT SWELL]
Zane was now ninety-four steps in, standing at the heart of the abyss. He looked up. Above him, he could see the great die hanging in the air.
Every eye in the arena was fixed on him.
He didn't hesitate. He hit the die one last time.
[SFX: DIE ROLLING]
Six.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
Envious glares followed Zane as he left, punctuated by a few muttered insults.
A smile stayed on Tia's face long after he was gone, her eyes curving into bright crescents. It wasn't just that he'd won—it was the smile he'd given her right before taking that final step.
[SFX: MAGICAL TELEPORTATION]
When the blue light surrounded Zane, his body seemed to dissolve into countless motes of light that drifted upward.
This felt familiar—a lot like the transport on the fifth level of the Dragon Soar Arena. Is this another long-distance jump? he wondered. Am I being sent to the Upper World?
But when the light faded, Zane realized it was only a short-range teleport. He couldn't feel the time pass, but he knew the trip from the Dragon Soar Arena to the Upper World hadn't been this fast.
[AMBIENT: DARK CAVERNOUS PALACE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Now, he found himself standing in a dark, cavernous palace. A chill wind cut through the silence, and there, directly in front of him, stood a massive coffin.
"Is that the immortal's coffin? It's huge," Zane muttered. The sheer scale of it reminded him of the Elysian Keep, where everything had been built for giants.
He was alone in the dark — and somewhere in this palace, the immortal's inheritance was waiting.
