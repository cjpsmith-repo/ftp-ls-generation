Ep 83 - The Binding Rings
Word Count: 1076
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
This wasn't the first time Zane had faced a nature creature. Just a few days ago, he'd killed Graves and Stryker, and it was his beat-up Shattered Dagger that had gotten the job done.
But this was a battle ring. While the Skyreach Sect didn't have strict rules against weapons, Zane knew that dagger could seriously injure, or even kill. In a challenge like this, he wasn't trying to take a life.
[SFX: HUMMING METAL]
The Aegis Band pulsed with Life Force. Gleaming with a metallic shine, it floated in the air, humming as it expanded and shot toward Zane, ready to wrap around him.
"Awesome!" Zane grinned.
[SFX: ENERGY FLARE]
He was ready for it. A streak of purple light flared around his body as he threw a powerful punch, slamming it straight into the ring.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
[SFX: METAL CLANG]
A sharp clang echoed as the metal band collided with Zane's fist. It flattened on impact and went flying sixty feet off the battle ring.
[SFX: BUZZING SOUND]
Despite this, Finn just chuckled. Watching his ring sail through the air, he simply stretched out his hand. A buzzing sound filled the arena as the Binding Ring zipped back to its master, snapping back to its original shape the moment it landed in his palm.
In the blink of an eye, Finn launched the Binding Ring at Zane again.
[SFX: ENERGY FLARE]
Simultaneously, a second ring materialized in Finn's opposite hand, crafted from the same shimmering Life Force. He launched it toward Zane with lightning speed, not allowing his opponent a moment to recover from the first assault.
[SFX: DOUBLE IMPACT]
Metal met flesh as Zane's powerful fists intercepted both rings in mid-flight, sending them hurtling across the arena floor with remarkable precision.
"It's over. You've lost," Finn declared, his voice laced with premature triumph, a smirk playing at the corners of his mouth as he surveyed what he believed was his inevitable victory.
"I'm still in the ring, aren't I?" Zane retorted, his jaw set with determination, eyes flashing with defiance despite the growing pressure of the battle.
The rules of a battle ring challenge were absolute and universally understood by all competitors—victory could only be claimed by rendering an opponent unconscious or physically removing them from the designated fighting area. Neither condition had been met, and Zane knew it, clinging to this fact like a lifeline amid the mounting tension.
Hearing Zane's argument, Finn sneered, thinking he was just looking for a technicality.
"Fine," he shot back. "If you want to play it that way, I'll just toss you out myself! Then we'll see who the winner is!"
[SFX: METAL RINGS JINGLING]
The moment the words left his mouth, Finn stretched out his hand and pointed at the Binding Rings now clamped around Zane's arms. Instantly, the rings trembled and jingled, as if they'd received an order. Then, they began to drag Zane toward the edge of the battle ring.
[SFX: SHARP PSYCHIC ATTACK]
Just as Zane was being pulled to the very edge, a sharp, stabbing pain erupted in Finn's head. It felt like an attack on his very soul, and the world went white. He almost blacked out.
"Ahh!" Finn let out a horrifying scream, clutching his head with both hands.
[SFX: CONFUSED CROWD MURMURS]
The scene unfolding in the ring left the crowd completely baffled. The students from Misty Peak had already braced for Zane's defeat, seeing him trapped by the rings. Against a nature creature like Finn, they knew his loss was inevitable.
But even in defeat, they felt Zane had brought honor to Misty Peak.
He had taken down three of Eagle Peak's fighters, each with a single punch. It was an incredible feat, something no other Outer Disciple in the Skyreach Sect was likely to ever accomplish. Since Zane had clearly given it his all, the Misty Peak students had resigned themselves to his loss.
[SFX: SHARP PSYCHIC ATTACK]
But just as he was about to be dragged out of the ring, the unexpected happened. Finn was on his knees, clutching his head and screaming in agony.
"What the—did he just get a sudden cramp?"
"Haha! That'd be great, but he's a nature creature with a superhuman body. How could he just get sick?"
"Zane's hands are both bound. There's no way he could've done anything. So if he's not sick, why is Finn in so much pain?"
Finn's bizarre reaction sparked a heated debate among the Misty Peak students. No one could figure out what was going on.
The Varsity members in their Shadow Cloaks, however, saw something the others missed.
Glenn, one of the members, murmured to the others, "See? Told you. This guy is way too powerful for the Marrow Refining Level. He's not just using brute force. He's mastered something else."
The members around Glenn were stunned. "I didn't see anything. What could he have possibly done to make Finn scream like that?"
"If I'm not mistaken," Glenn said, his eyes narrowed, "Zane just hit him with a soul attack."
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
"Now you'll see the real power of a nature creature! Cyclone Rings!"
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
The moment Finn screamed the words, an Aegis Band appeared on his hand. This one looked different from his usual Binding Rings. Its entire edge was lined with razor-sharp teeth. The Cyclone Ring was also much, much faster.
[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]
[SFX: SPINNING METAL RINGS]
In an instant, seven Cyclone Rings materialized in the air around Finn, spinning wildly.
"Go!" Finn thrust his hand out, his eyes cold and empty.
Zane's defiance had ignited Finn's fury. Humiliation burned through the nature creature at being challenged by a mere Marrow Refining cultivator. No one who provoked Finn's wrath escaped unscathed.
[SFX: RINGS CUTTING THROUGH AIR]
The seven Cyclone Rings hurtled toward Zane with lethal precision. Two arced high overhead, descending like predatory birds. Three shot directly at his torso, slicing through air with bullet-like velocity. The final pair rolled across the stone floor, carving deep channels as easily as a blade through water, targeting his ankles with murderous intent.
Confronted by this synchronized assault, Zane froze. The brutal reality of his predicament crystallized in an instant—he faced overwhelming odds. This was the true, terrifying power of a nature creature unleashed. As the deadly rings converged from all directions, Zane realized with cold clarity that underestimating such beings would be a fatal mistake.
