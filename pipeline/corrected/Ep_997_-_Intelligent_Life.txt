Ep 997 - Intelligent Life
Word count: 960
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
A surge of joy shot through Zane when he saw it. The black wind blade! He’d finally found one.
It wasn’t heading his way, so Zane immediately changed course, flying straight into its path.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
With a muffled impact, the wind blade, humming with five layers of the Wind Law’s power, slammed squarely into Zane’s chest.
[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]
“Power Transfer!”
The moment it hit, Zane reached out with the World’s Will, his focus zeroing in on Sacred Mount Lodestone.
[SFX: STONE SCRAPING]
His eyes flickered. A faint white scratch appeared at the mountain’s base.
A wind blade carrying five layers of the Wind Law had only left a tiny mark. How long would it take him to cut through this whole damn mountain?
Disappointment flickered across his face.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED STRINGS]
But Zane wasn’t the type to give up. During his time in The Heartlands, he hadn’t just been training; he’d also been preparing for his journey to the upper world.
He knew that being prepared was everything. If a little effort now could get him what he wanted, why wouldn’t he take that chance?
[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND]
[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]
So he pushed forward. The deeper he went, the more black wind blades he saw, and the fewer aquamarine ones there were.
[SFX: THUMPS]
The black blades shot around unpredictably, and Zane did his best to throw himself in their way. With every collision, another stream of power shot toward the base of Sacred Mount Lodestone.
Ten streams of power.
A hundred.
A thousand.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
To anyone else, these black wind blades were lethal, like the Grim Reaper’s scythe. But Zane couldn’t wait to charge right at them.
He flew into them again and again. Soon, he’d smashed into three thousand of them. When Zane checked Sacred Mount Lodestone again, he saw that the power from all three thousand impacts had been transferred to its base.
And after all that, he’d only managed to carve a gash into the mountain about as thick as his thumb.
Zane stared, speechless. A bitter disappointment settled in his gut.
All that effort—gathering the power of thousands of black wind blades—had barely scratched the thing.
He knew mountains were thinnest at their peak and massive at the base. At this rate, how many black wind blades would it take to break Sacred Mount Lodestone? Hundreds of millions? Billions, maybe?
[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND STORM]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
If that was the case, doing this with wind blades alone was damn near impossible. Sure, there were plenty of black blades in the storm over the Chaotic Ocean of Stars, but the strongest ones would be near the center.
He’d been to the storm’s center once before, when the weather had briefly calmed. He’d found powerful beasts lurking there — nothing else. Now that the storm was raging, those beasts were probably hiding on the ocean floor. The blades near the center would be far stronger than anything out here.
He wasn’t afraid of the double-colored wind blades, but if he ran into some powerful creature in the eye of the storm, he wouldn’t stand a chance. Hell, would he even be able to get away?
[MUSIC: DETERMINED DRUMS]
After a moment’s hesitation, Zane squared his shoulders, gritted his teeth, and charged forward anyway.
As he pushed deeper, a new blade appeared in his path — and it moved wrong. Not shooting in a straight line like the others, but flickering: transparent for an instant, then black, then transparent again, the two states alternating so rapidly it seemed to phase in and out of existence as it flew. It was this constant switching that made it stand out — that, and the fact that Zane couldn’t pin down exactly which layer of Wind Law it carried. Higher than five. Possibly six.
He flew straight at it.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
The collision was unlike anything the black blades had produced. Just the shockwave from the impact — before the transfer — was enough to carve a bottomless whirlpool into the ocean directly below him. The water around Sacred Mount Lodestone, tens of thousands of miles away, began to boil. Bubbles surged up in such volume that the blue ocean turned milky white, like a bowl churned to foam.
Zane checked the mountain.
A three-foot gash had been cut clean into its base. One blade. Three feet.
[SFX: STONE SCRAPING]
[MUSIC: RISING TRIUMPH]
“It’s working.”
Three thousand black blades had left a scratch the width of his thumb. One double-colored blade had left a three-foot cut. The math was not subtle. Zane’s face broke into a grin.
As he moved toward the storm’s center, he started running into more and more double-colored wind blades. Each one of those carved a gash ten feet deep into Sacred Mount Lodestone. The mountain was enormous, but under this kind of relentless assault, it would have to break apart eventually. Zane’s resolve hardened.
[SFX: ETHEREAL SHIMMER]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHOIR]
But after traveling another two hundred miles deeper, Zane discovered a new type of wind blade. They were golden.
They weren’t just shooting out randomly like the others. They moved together in a tight group, like a flock of birds gliding through the storm.
“Are these things... sentient?” Zane wondered, staring in amazement.
Wind blades were just a byproduct of the storm. The stronger the blade, the more of the Wind Law’s power it held. But no matter how powerful, it shouldn’t be able to think. At the end of the day, it was just a supercharged gust of wind.
The universe was full of mysteries. Even though Zane had merged with the World’s Will—a connection that made him feel damn near omnipotent—some things still completely baffled him.
[SFX: SHARP WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: RISING DANGER]
As he was watching the golden clusters, a few of them seemed to notice him. One peeled away from the group, circling in the distance. And then, the entire flock broke formation and came straight for him.
In that split second, only one thought screamed through his mind.
Run.
