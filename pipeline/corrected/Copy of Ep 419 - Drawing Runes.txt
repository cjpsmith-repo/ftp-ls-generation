Zane couldn't believe how perfectly things had turned out. A simple translation—courtesy of the Cobalt Dragon's ancient knowledge—had transformed the entire situation.

The shopkeeper's attitude had completely reversed. Where moments ago the old man had ignored Zane completely, now he offered to sell his materials at a thirty percent discount. Seeing how sincere the old man was, Zane felt compelled to return the favor. He continued translating the remaining phoenix script etched into the jade, watching as the shopkeeper absorbed each word with the intensity of a scholar discovering lost scripture.

The old man's lips moved silently, committing every syllable to memory as though his life depended on it.

When Zane finished, the old man’s tired eyes lit up. "Where did you learn the Ancient Phoenix Language?" The question was wrapped in a newfound respect, almost reverence. The shopkeeper leaned forward slightly, his earlier disdain completely gone. As far as anyone knew, the Ancient Phoenixes had once soared through these very skies, but the species had vanished ages ago, leaving neither descendants nor teachers behind. The language should have died with them.

Zane felt the weight of the question but had no intention of revealing his secret. A slight smile played at the corners of his mouth. "Sorry, can't tell you," he said, keeping his tone light and friendly. "But if you ever come across other words or texts you can't decipher, feel free to bring them to me. I'm pretty sure I can handle them."

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

Hearing Zane's bold promise, the Cobalt Dragon immediately grumbled in his mind. Hey! Just because I helped you out this time doesn't make me your personal, on-call translator. I may know a lot of languages, but I don't know every single one in existence!

The old man’s expression soured at the sheer arrogance of the claim. If the kid had said something like that before translating the phoenix text, he would’ve been tempted to smack him. The world was full of countless bizarre languages from species that had long since vanished. Who could possibly know them all? This kid was way too full of himself.

But… he had just translated the Ancient Phoenix Language without breaking a sweat. The old man swallowed his pride, figuring he might actually need this kid's help again. So, despite the boast, he kept his tone respectful. "Right, of course. Are you a disciple of Cloud Hall? You have the look of someone from the Elite House. Could I get your name? I may have more work for you in the future."

Zane smiled, gave the man his name, and said, "Now, about my materials?"

The old man nodded and barked an order to his two clerks, who emerged from the back of the shop. He snatched the list from Zane, tossed it to them—"Get these ready"—and turned back to settle the payment.

Parting with one hundred twelve thousand Crystal Cubes stung, but Zane paid the man and left the market with his supplies.

The shopkeeper watched him go, a complicated look on his face. He let out a sigh. What a strange kid, he thought. And what a waste of good materials. A hundred twelve thousand Crystal Cubes was no small sum. Young people these days are just impossible to figure out.

The raw materials couldn't be used in their natural state. Each component required meticulous processing before it could serve its purpose. Drawing divine textures demanded technical precision far beyond ordinary cultivation techniques, and Zane remained a complete novice in this ancient art. Yet with the Cobalt Dragon guiding him through every delicate step, he found himself handling the intricate work with surprising confidence.

[SFX: MORTAR ****&**** PESTLE]

The Potent Azurewood Roots came first. Zane ground them slowly, watching the fibrous material transform into fine powder beneath his careful attention. The texture had to be absolutely uniform—even the smallest inconsistency could corrupt the final result.

[SFX: SNIPS]

Next came the Frost-Leaf Herb roots, which required an entirely different approach. Zane cut them with precise movements, then kneaded the pieces between his fingers until they reached the proper consistency. Only then could he mix them with the cloud ash, watching as the pale grey powder absorbed the herb's essence.

The entire process tested his patience. One careless gesture, one moment of distraction, and the whole batch would become worthless. Zane felt the weight of each decision, knowing that these expensive materials represented more than just Crystal Cubes—they represented his first real step toward mastering something that transcended the cultivation methods of his world.

An hour crept by as he worked. Sweat beaded on his forehead despite the cool air. His fingers ached from the repetitive motions, but he didn't dare rush. Finally, the last component settled into place. Zane stepped back, examining the prepared mixture with a mixture of exhaustion and anticipation. Everything was ready.

[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]

He picked up the runic quill with careful deliberation, the slender instrument feeling both weightless and impossibly heavy in his grip. A deep breath filled his lungs as he willed his trembling hands to steady. The Cobalt Dragon's instructions echoed through his mind with crystalline clarity, yet despite this guidance, nervous anticipation fluttered in his chest like a caged bird. This moment marked his first attempt at drawing divine texture—a whisper from a world far beyond his own.

[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

Closing his eyes, he gathered his concentration into a single point of focus. The familiar sensation of his soul stirring answered his call, and soon a pale yellow glimmer of soul power shimmered to life between his eyebrows. This was nothing like using the Psyche Spike, which demanded he forge his soul into a devastating weapon—fast, ferocious, meant to pierce and destroy. Divine texture required something entirely different: delicacy, precision, an artist's touch rather than a warrior's strike. The energy had to flow like honey, not lightning. The slower and more controlled the stream, the better the result.

[SFX: MAGICAL WRITING]

[MUSIC: DELICATE PRECISION]

Zane channeled his Soul Power down his shoulder, through the muscles of his arm, past his wrist, and into the waiting quill. His hand moved with painstaking slowness, bringing the charged tip toward the prepared paper. The moment of contact sent a tingle through his fingers as the quill touched the surface, beginning to trace the very first stroke of something that went far beyond ordinary cultivation.
