Ep 1160 - The Ghosts

Word count: 1158

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
Freya knew she couldn’t solve Zane’s problems for him, no matter how small they were. But from the look on his face, this was no small problem—he was in serious trouble. Since she couldn’t do anything to help, she had to at least be there for him.
[SFX: DIVINE POWER SURGE]
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY WHOOSH]
Just then, a divine power flared to life, and a purple light wrapped around her, catching her in midair. 
[SFX: RAPID UPWARD MOVEMENT]
A moment later, it was pulling her rapidly upward, away from the abyss.
Zane was now falling more than three times faster than before, every muscle in his body screaming with tension. He’d never imagined this trip to the Underground Palace would go so sideways. 
But nothing in life was fixed. His fate wasn’t set in stone — no one could predict how this would end.
Still, what he was facing wasn’t just a huge problem—it felt like an impossible one. He had to use this time falling to explore the Endless Abyss. Lavender had only ever made it to the fourth lap, but she’d sensed that an even bigger secret was hidden in the fifth. Maybe that secret was the key to getting him out of this mess.
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
The wind roared in his ears as his descent accelerated, the walls of the abyss blurring past. Soon enough, another stone platform appeared in the darkness below.
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
“The third cave!” Zane’s eyes locked onto it, his heart caught somewhere between hope and dread.
“Don’t go in there,” Lavender’s voice warned from inside his mind. “It’s full of protective treasures. They’re tempting, but it would take way too long to get them.”
He took her point. With a sharp nod, he held his course, letting his gaze sweep past the third cave as he plummeted deeper. 
[SFX: WHOOSH]
He shot past the entrance and into the fourth lap of the Endless Abyss, his focus narrowing.
“This next part is trickier. Be careful,” Lavender warned.
“Got it,” Zane replied, his voice tight. 
[SFX: DRAGON SCALES GLOWING]
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS ENERGY BUILDUP]
He gripped his sword as the Dragon Scales embedded in his body began to glow, a massive reserve of power building inside him for whatever was coming next.
Lavender, still a presence inside him, was just as nervous. But seeing the grim determination on his face gave her a flicker of confidence.
*Don’t worry, Zane,* she thought. *They’ve already found me anyway. I’ll help you the rest of the way down.*
[MUSIC: DARK CREEPING TENSION]
[AMBIENT: GHOSTLY WHISPERS]
Before he even saw them, he heard them. A low, chorus of wails rose from somewhere below — thousands of voices bleeding together into a sound that was less like crying and more like pressure, like something pressing against the inside of his skull. Then the walls came into view.
They were covered in ghosts.
Countless spectral figures of every size clung to the rock face on both sides of the abyss, packed so densely the stone beneath them was invisible. When they heard him coming, they turned. When they saw him, they opened their mouths.
The fourth lap of the Endless Abyss was the domain of a Demon Night Saint Lord, and every one of these creatures was his. Not warriors. Not fighters. Ghosts — his underlings, bound to this place, clinging to the dark.
[SFX: COLLECTIVE GHOSTLY HOWL]
Because Zane’s speed was so extreme, the first wave had no time to react. He shot through them like a stone through fog, the cold of their presence washing over him, but nothing latching on. For a breath, he thought he might clear them entirely.
He didn’t.
After falling for some time, the ones he’d passed began peeling off the walls behind him. They couldn’t match his speed descending, but they didn’t need to — they rose above him, filling the shaft, sealing off the top of the abyss until there was nothing above him but a writhing mass of black.
Then the ones ahead began jumping from the walls to intercept.
[SFX: GHOSTS SWARMING]
A ghost built like a gorilla launched itself off the rock face, its entire body radiating pure black energy, arms wide to wrap around him.
“Die.”
Zane’s Thundergust came up. One thing Lavender had made clear: fancy techniques and wind blades were useless against these creatures. Only lightning worked.
[SFX: LIGHTNING BOLTS CRACKLING]
He unleashed a bolt from the blade — a streak of white light that split the dark and hit the gorilla-ghost dead center. It vanished instantly. But the strike didn’t frighten the others. If anything, it made them worse.
They poured off the walls in waves. Zane cut through them as fast as he could, lightning crackling from every swing, each bolt annihilating a cluster — but for every ten he killed, thirty more took their place.
The first ghost to actually reach him was small — barely the size of a child. It landed on his shoulder, and the cold hit immediately, a deep seeping chill that had nothing to do with temperature. He felt it moving into him.
“Scram.”
He pulled the lightning inward through the Thundergust. Instead of firing outward, he let it coil around his body like a silver dragon.
[SFX: LIGHTNING WRAPPING, BODY SURGE]
The ghost burned off. But as soon as it was gone, two more landed in its place. Then three.
As his speed dropped, more and more ghosts latched onto him, clambering all over his body.
First ten, then a hundred, then a thousand.
From a distance, Zane’s figure completely disappeared. 
[SFX: GHOSTLY MASS WRITHING]
The ghosts clung to each other, wrapping around him to form a huge, writhing ball that drifted slowly toward the bottom of the abyss.
His fall had slowed to a crawl, the sheer mass of spectral bodies dragging against the air.
“What do I do?” Zane grunted, trapped inside the ghostly sphere. 
[SFX: LIGHTNING BOLTS CRACKLING]
[SFX: DARK ENERGY SEEPING]
The dark energy was seeping into him, making his movements sluggish. He’d tried to expel it, but it was no use. The energy was already circulating through his body, creeping closer and closer to his core with every second he remained trapped.
Lavender’s voice was faint, but she could feel his rising panic. “Let me deal with them,” she said quietly.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT DIVINE SURGE]
[AMBIENT: RADIANT ENERGY HUMMING]
[SFX: SPIRIT WAVE EXPANDING]
[SFX: CRIMSON LIGHT BLAZING]
In that instant, her spirit flowed out of him, her form expanding like a wave of light to wash over the mass of ghosts. At the same time, a crimson radiance blazed up from the endless space below, gathering around her like a cloak.
Before, Lavender had been afraid of revealing her location, so she hadn’t dared to draw on her Pool of Faith. But now that she’d been discovered, she had nothing left to fear.
