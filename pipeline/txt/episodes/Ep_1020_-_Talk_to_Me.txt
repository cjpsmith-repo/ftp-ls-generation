Ep 1020 - Talk to Me

Word count: 1110

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL HARP]

The mysterious woman’s song had just faded across the universe — and every faction from the smallest sect to the greatest Holy Land was still holding its breath, waiting to see if the beasts would come.

When the last note finished, the woman giggled. “So? Was I any good?”

[SFX: CROWD CHATTER]

“You were amazing!”

“Absolutely heavenly!”

“I’ve never heard anything so beautiful.”

It killed the Supreme Lords to waste their precious Roaring Tokens on this mysterious woman, but they knew they didn’t have a choice.

She might have sounded young, but she was someone none of them could afford to get on the wrong side of. After all, pissing her off could spell doom for the entire universe.

The woman snorted. “I wasn’t asking you guys. I was asking him!”

It was obvious who she meant.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

When the Supreme Lords realized the kid who’d been counting had gone silent, they started to panic. “Say something, kid!”

[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE]

[SFX: MAGICAL ACTIVATION]

Zane smirked to himself inside his cave. He activated the Roaring Token in his hand.

“You’re a great singer,” he said.

“Really?” she chirped. “Then… can I sing for you every day?”

[MUSIC: COMEDIC TENSION]

Her voice was beautiful, sure, but the Supreme Lords nearly had a collective heart attack at the thought. Sing every day? How were they supposed to appreciate her music with the threat of a universe-ending beast hanging on every single note?

Unfortunately, it wasn’t their call to make. They couldn’t risk offending her, so all they could do was pray Zane would shut her down.

“Okay,” Zane replied simply.

“Awesome!” she squealed. “By the way, I don’t even know your name. What should I call you?”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

That got the Supreme Lords’ attention. A name would make finding this kid a hell of a lot easier. He and the singer were a total mystery, and everyone wanted to know how they’d gotten their hands on so many Roaring Tokens without running out.

But Zane wasn’t an idiot. After calling out two Supreme Lords, there was no way he was about to broadcast his name to the entire universe. If anyone found out who he was, his special Roaring Token would be as good as gone.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS THEME]

“Sorry, that’s a secret,” he said.

“Aww, that’s a bummer,” she pouted. A collective gasp went through the Supreme Lords—he’d definitely offended her now. But then she quickly added, “Oh well! It doesn’t really matter, as long as you’ll still talk to me.”

“If you have time, I’m happy to keep you company,” Zane said.

It was just something to say. He had no real interest in chatting with a woman of unknown origin — he had too much on his mind already. But there was no point being rude about it.

He put the token away and closed his eyes. Several hours had passed since he’d first started counting. He had cultivating to do.

[AMBIENT: BUSY SETTLEMENT]

[MUSIC: CURIOUS CHATTER]

The next morning, when Zane returned to the settlement, everyone was still talking about it.

“Who do you think that mysterious young man is?”

“Has to be a Supreme Lord’s descendant. Only someone like that would burn tokens without blinking.”

“Has to be.”

One of the armored guards on duty let out a short laugh. “Don’t embarrass yourselves. A Supreme Lord’s descendant wouldn’t waste tokens like that. Not even a Supreme Lord would. Whoever that was… their origin isn’t something any of us can speculate about.”

“Then who is he?” someone asked.

The guard spread his hands. “I have no idea. I don’t think anyone in the entire universe does.”

He wasn’t entirely right. A handful of people had recognized the voice.

Yolande was one of them. She had the memories of two clones who’d encountered Zane directly. She knew he’d been looking for his sister. Voice plus context — there was no coincidence that specific. She was certain.

The Demi-Immortal Clan had also taken notice. But they couldn’t be sure. A universe of trillions of living beings could easily produce two warriors with the same voice. Without more to go on, they could only guess.

[AMBIENT: MINE ENTRANCE PATH]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Freya had rested through the night. On her way back to the settlement that morning, she noticed the stares — warriors sizing her up as she passed, a few of them calling out invitations to mine together. She ignored every one of them. She knew exactly what those invitations meant.

But her mind kept circling back to yesterday.

She’d listened carefully to every word. The mysterious young man’s voice — it was identical to Zane’s. Not similar. Identical.

But that was impossible. Zane had just ascended. There was no way he had a Roaring Token, let alone thousands. It had to be a coincidence.

She found him waiting at the settlement. He gave her a nod. “Let’s go.”

They fell into step together toward the mine. She started asking questions — careful ones, roundabout ones. Zane felt it immediately. She was trying to confirm something. He kept his answers easy and neutral and gave her nothing to work with.

He wasn’t going to admit it. And without an admission, she couldn’t be sure.

Neither of them noticed the eyes tracking their every move.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

Among the newcomers to the Upper World, a clear hierarchy had already taken shape.

The fighters were split into three factions, and the strongest was run by a guy named Rylan.

The bald fighter Zane had nearly slapped into next week was one of Rylan’s guys. A nobody like that would never have the guts to try and rob someone without some serious backup.

Rylan had already gathered all the Sovereign Stones he needed. He’d been planning to cash in his ore and leave, but decided to stick around and hoard even more. The idea was to use the surplus to buy training resources once he was out.

The leaders of the other two factions had the exact same idea. In fact, a whole tribute system had sprung up in the mine. Now, fighters didn’t just have to give their earnings to the Soulbound Sect—they also had to kick a percentage up to the three factions.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

The six fighters who’d bailed yesterday had gone straight to Rylan to complain. As the top dog in the mine, Rylan took Zane’s little stunt as a direct challenge to his authority.

New guys were always cocky, clueless about who really ran the show. And anyone who crossed Rylan’s crew tended to just… disappear.

Without a trace.
