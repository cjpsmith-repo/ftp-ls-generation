Ep 696 - An Army of Outcasts

Word count: 1076

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

It all happened in a flash.

A handful of Ethereal City guards were stationed by the teleportation array, but they were only at the Soul Realm—nowhere near fast enough to react. Destroying a teleportation array was the kind of scorched-earth tactic that hurt everyone and helped no one, which was why nobody ever thought someone would be crazy enough to try it.

When the crowd saw two Core-Forged warriors pull out a purple-black bead, they scrambled back, creating a wide circle of empty space.

[SFX: CRACKLING LIGHTNING]

They watched as the bead shot a thin crackle of lightning into the heart of the array. For a split second, there was silence. Then the lightning erupted, unleashing a blinding flash and a wave of raw spatial energy.

A massive explosion tore through the air.

[SFX: DEAFENING BLAST]

[AMBIENT: CHAOS AND SCREAMS]

The deafening blast swallowed the area in a searing white light. The warriors who’d been waiting in line and the guards standing watch were instantly vaporized, gone before they could even scream.

From a safe distance, panicked shouts broke out.

“They destroyed the teleportation array!”

“That was a void-storm bead! It’s packed with Thunder and Spatial Law—it’s made to permanently wreck an array!”

“Why would they do this? What’s their plan?”

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ENERGY]

It wasn’t easy to take out a teleportation array. The connection between the two points was incredibly stable. You could smash the platform to pieces, but the link itself would remain, and you could just rebuild the base. A void-storm bead, though, was another animal entirely. It was designed for one purpose. The thunderous energy obliterated the physical structure, but the real damage came from the Spatial Law woven into it. That power corrupted the dimensional coordinates so completely that the connection could never be reestablished.

[SFX: DISTANT EXPLOSION]

Just as the two warriors succeeded, another teleportation array about a mile away, this one leading to Oracle City, was destroyed in the same manner. In a matter of minutes, the two primary routes to Cloudhaven and Oracle City were completely severed.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The realization dawned on the crowd with chilling speed.

“What in the world is going on?”

[AMBIENT: PANICKED CROWD MURMURS]

“Wait… I get it. They have to be from the Corrupted Divine Kingdom!”

“They’re cutting off reinforcements from Cloud Hall and the World Merchants’ Guild. This has to mean Maddox is about to attack.”

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

Not far away, built into the face of a tall mountain at the edge of the city, the Ethereal Spirit Sect's walls were already sealed behind a pale violet barrier. Thousands of runes covered the mountainside in a circular formation — a pattern that had been protecting the sect for generations.  Before Zane built the formation at Cloud Hall, it had been the most powerful sect-protecting array in the Heartlands.

Above the city, ten thousand warriors hung in the air. Most were at the Soul Realm; a smaller number had reached the Core-Forged; a few dozen had broken through to Spirit-Tribulation. Every one of them wore black robes with two characters on the chest: Corrupted Divine Kingdom.

These were independent cultivators — men who had spent their lives being squeezed out by the sects, who had traveled alone while sect disciples traveled in groups, who had watched treasures taken from them simply because someone else had more bodies to throw at a fight. Maddox had found them, named their grievance, and told them he would overturn the system entirely. Most of them had stopped asking whether he could actually do it.

Maddox stood at the front of the formation.

"Brothers!" he called out, his voice carrying across the entire host. "This is the Ethereal Spirit Sect — the sect that calls itself the greatest in the Heartlands. Who here has felt their boot on your neck?"

[SFX: CROWD ROAR]

The roar that came back shook the air.

Ethereal City had braced for this. In the world of warriors, the strong took what they wanted; it was the law of the jungle. If the independent warriors had the power of the sects, they would have done the same thing. Maddox had stoked their ambition and resentment so effectively that none of them stopped to question his ultimate goal.

He needed the Ethereal Spirit Sect to fall — not just for Cora, but because every independent cultivator in the Heartlands was watching. If he brought down the number-one sect, his following would multiply overnight. And somewhere underneath all of it was the thing he actually needed: to break through to the Soul Sea Realm. He had been stuck at the bottleneck for too long. The third Violet Core Physique was the key.

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]

“Excellent!” Maddox roared, his voice sharp and cold. “Then today is the day the Ethereal Spirit Sect falls!”

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

Just then, a figure drifted out from the ranks of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom. He was a pale, scholarly-looking man whose power resonated at the middle level of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. In his hand, he held an ancient treatise.

This was Darius, the military adviser for the Kingdom. He was once a well-known independent warrior in the south, but he’d made an enemy of the Mysterious Luna Hall, and they had slaughtered his entire family in retaliation.

Darius had been in hiding for years, but when he heard Maddox was planning to establish his own divine kingdom, he knew his chance for revenge had finally arrived. From his perspective, it didn’t matter if Maddox actually succeeded. All that mattered was using the man’s power to crush the Mysterious Luna Hall. They were, in their own ways, just using each other.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

“I just received word,” Darius said, his voice a low murmur. “The young woman from House Kensington isn't at the Ethereal Spirit Sect. She’s in Ethereal City.”

“Oh?” Maddox’s eyes narrowed. “It seems Xander doesn’t take me seriously at all. Fine. I guess I’ll be paying a visit to Ethereal City, then.”

“The two teleportation arrays there have already been destroyed,” Darius confirmed. “However, I suspect House Kensington would rather die than surrender her.”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

Maddox frowned, a flicker of caution crossing his face. Darius was right. House Kensington wouldn't hand over the girl with the Violet-Core Physique without a hell of a fight.

Well, if it was a fight they wanted, it was a fight they'd get.
