Ep 591 - How Far Will He Go?

[AMBIENT: TOWER HALL]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane's name appeared on the ninth row.
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]
Every race watched the tablet intently. His performance had already elevated him to Boris and Cheryl's level, generating massive anticipation.
No human had ever reached the tenth floor of the Tower of Sin as a mere tenth-level War Fighter—not even legendary Amber.
But the eras differed. Amber had clawed her way up from the bottom, accumulating far more Lucky Light than Zane. That relentless pressure forged her skills at a terrifying rate.
Zane advanced freely, gaining less Lucky Light and fewer opportunities to sharpen his power.
The Ogres hadn't grown fearful—they'd grown weaker. They couldn't waste their best fighters on him as they had with Amber. Since their assassination attempt failed, they let him reach the tenth floor, where Boris would finish him.
If they'd forced him to grind through the lower floors, he might have grown strong enough to defeat even Boris.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL PIANO]
While the other races watched with keen interest, it was the humans who held their breath.
Olivia felt a knot of anticipation tighten in her stomach. She knew every human here understood exactly what Zane represented. For the Violet Heart Sanctuary, he was a shot in the arm—the first real dose of hope they’d had in a long time, a chance to finally change their fate.
He'd been a legend before he'd even set foot in the sanctuary. Now, everyone from the newest students to the Soul Sea Realm elders was watching, captivated by his power.
"So, how far do you think he'll actually go?"
"He's gotta make it to the fourth layer, at least. I'm telling you, his potential blows Boris's out of the water."
"Exactly. Even if he can't beat Boris now, give him time. Zane's ceiling is way higher than anything Boris could ever dream of."
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Boris, standing nearby, said nothing. But frankly, he agreed with them. He'd never been one to overestimate himself or underestimate his opponents. At his level, it was absolutely impossible to have rushed to the tenth floor of the Tower of Sin. Even at his peak as a tenth-level War Fighter, he could challenge those a rank or two above him—but a fifth-rank War Lord had once handed him a loss.
By that measure, Boris knew he couldn't hold a candle to Zane.
But that didn't discourage him. Talent was innate—there was nothing to be done about it. What he had was luck and persistence. He could still carve out his own legend.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]
Meanwhile, inside the Talent Tablet chamber, Zane had always been curious about one thing: how exactly did this test work?
Trials were straightforward—they measured raw strength, throwing illusion after illusion at a warrior until he broke. But talent? That was something else entirely.
[SFX: MAGICAL RUMBLE]
After a moment, a shaft of light descended before him. Nine stone tablets rose from the ground.
Zane froze.
[MUSIC: EPIC REVELATION]
He could feel it immediately—a deep, resonant power radiating from each tablet. The power of law. And not just any laws. They were his laws, each one a perfect mirror of the forces already living inside him.
This was no coincidence. The Talent Tablet had sensed the laws within him and arranged the tablets accordingly.
Zane approached the nearest tablet and spread his perception across all nine, letting it sink in.
"Second-level law," he murmured, a trace of amazement crossing his face. He hadn't expected to find this kind of opportunity inside the Tower of Sin.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE MEDITATION]
Zane scanned all nine tablets, then made his choice: the one engraved with the law of space. He settled in and began to comprehend.
The ranking on the outer wall meant nothing to him—false reputation was a totem for a race, useful for morale, but to Zane it was worthless. Comprehending the law of space was worth infinitely more. He planned to work through all nine tablets before he left.
[SFX: MYSTICAL FADE OUT]
But the moment he touched his chosen tablet and his perception locked in, the other eight quietly dimmed—and vanished.
Zane stared at the empty space where they had been.
"How—"
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]
He let out a slow breath. One tablet. He'd only get one.
At least the Spatial Law tablet remained. He had no choice but to make the most of it.
Among all laws, this one had always resonated with him the most. Now, with a finished stone tablet in front of him, his comprehension moved quickly.
Just like Yolande's before, Zane's name began to climb.
[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]
Ninth row.
Eighth row.
Seventh row.
[SFX: DRAMATIC WHOOSH]
In a flash, his name was sitting right next to Olivia's.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
"He's climbing fast! Even faster than Yolande did. You think he’ll make it all the way to the first row?"
"Doubtful. The Talent Tablet doesn't measure speed. How fast the name rises means nothing for the final rank. Remember that saint-level genius last time? His name sat on the ninth row for two hours before it jumped straight to the fourth."
"Look! It stopped on the sixth row. Does that mean his talent is on par with Olivia's?"
"Hey, what's the rush? Just watch. As long as he's still inside, it ain't over."
Olivia heard the murmurs but kept her silence. For some reason, she had a gut feeling Zane was going to pull off something incredible. The first row, like Yolande, might be too much to hope for. But the third? That felt possible. A rank that would put him second only to Amber herself.
Even Yolande, who normally couldn't care less about this stuff, was staring intently at the rankings on the vermilion wall. It looked like she was waiting for the result, but with her, you could never really be sure.
Dylan, on the other hand, looked like he'd just swallowed poison.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
The sixth row. Zane had already climbed to the sixth row, and the sight was a crushing blow to Dylan's ego. He was supposed to be a saint-level genius, considered Olivia's peer. But after his own test, his name had stalled out on the seventh row.
The seventh, eighth, and ninth rows were crowded with names. His rank was mediocre, and it wouldn't be long before he was pushed off the list entirely. He just stared at Zane’s name glowing in the sixth row, a bitter taste of defeat in his mouth.
