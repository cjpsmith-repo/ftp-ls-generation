Ep 581 - Unbelievable
Word count: 992
[AMBIENT: BUSY TAVERN]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The tavern was humming with conversation and the smell of food when a powerful aura exploded from Zane, rolling out like a shockwave. The surge hit Don first, since he was standing right in front of him.
The force of it made Don’s eyes fly wide. The human War Fighter before him felt like a fearless predator.
But Zane had just arrived at the Tower of Sin, so he wasn't actually launching a soul attack. He'd learned to hide his aura ever since his soul reached the Fighting Soul Realm. Besides, there were way too few humans in the tower; starting a fight was a terrible idea. If the other races decided to gang up on him, Zane would be toast.
After all, the Tower of Sin was crawling with tenth-level War Lords.
[SFX: DARK ENERGY SURGE]
As Zane's aura erupted, he also unleashed the Malice he’d absorbed from the Killing Sword Mountain. The combination of his soul's pressure and that surging, dark energy made him look like a god of death.
A waiter, carrying a pitcher of wine to a table, suddenly felt Zane’s aura wash over him.
[SFX: GLASS SHATTERING]
His knees buckled, and the pitcher crashed to the floor, shattering. All the color drained from his face as he scrambled to grab a nearby table to keep from collapsing.
But the waiter only felt a fraction of the pressure. Zane’s aura kept swelling, aimed squarely at the two Ogre War Lords—Don and his friend.
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]
[SFX: HEARTBEAT]
In that instant, Don felt his chest tighten, like a steel band was crushing his lungs. He couldn't breathe. How could this young human have such a terrifying aura?
[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]
As Zane slowly amped up the Malice, Don’s entire body began to tremble, his teeth chattering uncontrollably as he staggered two steps back.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]
Zane's intimidation worked. Both Ogre War Lords trembled—newly ascended to their rank, they'd felt invincible. In the Tower of Sin hierarchy, War Fighters occupied the bottom, War Generals the middle, and War Lords the top.
After their breakthrough, they'd heard rumors of a powerful human and decided to challenge him, expecting an easy victory.
They found none. As Zane's pressure intensified, the two War Lords retreated step by step until they reached the tavern's entrance. Their expressions darkened. They clenched their massive fists and glared at him, speechless.
[SFX: ENERGY RELEASE]
Then Zane pulled the Malice back and released his soul pressure all at once. Just like that, he was himself again—relaxed, composed, a faint smile on his face, looking for all the world like a young man without a care. No one watching would have believed that the same person had just driven two War Lords out of a tavern with nothing but his aura.
Don ground his teeth. The fury in his eyes burned hot, but he didn't step back inside. He jabbed a finger at Zane. "I'll be waiting for you in the arena."
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
With that, Don and his companion turned on their heels and stormed out.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]
Outside the tavern, a crowd had gathered—Warriors and Generals from a dozen different races, all of them standing with their jaws open. They had just watched a human War Fighter force two Ogre War Lords out the door using nothing but soul pressure.
"Is that really a human Warrior?"
"Eighth-level. Name's Zane. Only just arrived at the Tower a few days ago…"
"If the Ogres can't take him down in the arena, this human might actually climb all the way to the top. The human race could earn another seat."
"Another seat? When has anything like this ever happened? A War Fighter's pressure forcing War Lords to retreat—I'd have called it impossible. If those two weren't Ogres, I'd think the whole thing was staged."
[MUSIC: EPIC REVELATION]
This was the first time Olivia had ever seen Zane truly unleash his aura. Until now, she’d assumed that Zane, being just an eighth-level War Fighter, had won his previous fight by some kind of fluke.
Plus, Dylan had gotten on Zane's bad side from the moment they met.
And Zane was definitely cocky; he didn't seem to take the Violet Heart Sanctuary seriously at all.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL SANCTUARY]
Still, Olivia, Dylan, and the others had received a message from the Sanctuary telling them to stay on good terms with Zane. If his power kept growing, he might be able to win another seat for the human race, and every race on the main continent considered the seats in the Tower of Sin to be priceless.
The Violet Heart Sanctuary rarely sent its people into the Tower of Sin—and for good reason. No matter how talented the warriors they sent, they died too quickly. The Tower had a way of swallowing geniuses whole. So when word reached them that Zane had walked in and immediately carved out space for himself, it had turned heads.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT THEME]
By the time Olivia ran into Zane again, he had already defeated Teresa, the saint-level prodigy from the Demon Night.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL STRINGS]
That news had sent a shockwave through Olivia’s heart.
For the first time, she felt like she’d met a man who could actually capture her heart.
So, even though she was furious when Zane had mistaken her for a prostitute and asked for her price, she’d eventually let it go.
For a warrior that powerful, arrogance wasn't foolishness—it was just pride.
By then, Zane already held a special place in Olivia’s heart. She figured that even though he was only an eighth-level War Fighter, he could probably take on a ninth or even a tenth-level War General.
She’d worried she was overestimating him, but she never would have dreamed he could force two Ogre War Lords to back down with nothing but his aura.
It was unbelievable.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ATMOSPHERE]
Suddenly, Zane seemed mysterious, powerful… and almost completely out of her league.
Right now, most of the talented humans came from the Violet Heart Sanctuary, and the rest were from smaller groups allied with them.
So where in the world did Zane come from?
