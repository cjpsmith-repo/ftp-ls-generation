The final fragment of gold script penetrated Zane's brain with a nauseating crunch. His spine contorted into a perfect arch as his scream vanished—not from unwillingness, but because his vocal cords paralyzed against pain beyond comprehension.
Foreign knowledge cascaded through his consciousness, shattering barriers like a raging flood through fractured stone.
"Godforge Technique," intoned a voice as primeval as the earth itself, resonating not in his ears but directly within his mind. "The Original Method of Forging. Transform flesh into forge, body into weapon. Purify the vessel through suffering, cleanse it for the awakening of true vitality."'Is this some kind of weapon forging?' Zane didn't know the first thing about it, but he knew that master gunsmiths were swimming in money.
For all its wealth, House Lowell couldn't even get a low-level master gunsmith to look their way. That alone told him how much power they held.
But to use your own body as a weapon? What the hell did that mean?
[SFX: BODY TEMPERATURE RISING]
The moment the thought crossed his mind, something impossible happened. His body temperature skyrocketed.
Zane felt like he was being cooked from the inside out, the heat climbing until it was unbearable. He scrambled toward the water tank in the far corner of the cellar, the one left for him to wash up with.
[SFX: WATER BOILING VIOLENTLY]
He plunged into the tank without a second thought, squeezing his eyes shut as he sank. The water around him instantly erupted into a hissing, boiling frenzy. Bubbles frothed over the sides as the tank began to rattle violently, thick steam billowing into the air.
Before his body had a chance to cool, all the water had evaporated, leaving the cellar shrouded in a dense mist. The dousing hadn't lowered his temperature at all. In fact, he felt even hotter.
[SFX: BODY HITTING FLOOR]
[SFX: SKIN CRACKING]
Unable to take it anymore, he writhed on the dry floor of the tank. Cracks began to spiderweb across his skin, a dark red light glowing from within, like he was a piece of red-hot iron. He thrashed helplessly at the bottom of the tank as the mist swirled around him.
It wasn't just his body. Zane's mind was being torn apart and rebuilt, too.

Its deep eyes held the ancient wisdom of a million years, and it regarded Zane with an unreadable expression. He could feel an immense, crushing power rolling off it in waves.

The agony of having one's soul set on fire was beyond comprehension. Zane couldn't even pass out. This was all happening inside his mind, to his soul itself. All he could do was clench his jaw and pray for the end.
"Just let me die!" he screamed in his mind, his sanity fraying.
Death would have been a welcome escape, but it was a luxury he couldn't afford. He was helpless, unable to even bite his own tongue to end the torment.

Burned to slag, hammered flat, then reforged. Burned, hammered, and reforged all over again. The torturous cycle repeated endlessly.

[SFX: CONTROLLED FLAME]
In his mind, the giant furnace had stopped spinning, but the black flame inside still burned, now with a steady, controlled heat that seemed less terrifying.
[MUSIC: REVELATION]

The world was full of unbelievable things. Some master gunsmiths used all kinds of twisted methods to craft their weapons. Some committed countless murders just to temper their creations in human blood. Others stole human souls to forge them into cursed, sentient weapons.
But a technique for forging one's own body into a magical weapon? Zane had never heard of anything so insane.
After the initial shock wore off, Zane realized this might not be a bad thing.
Magical weapons in this world had five stages. The first was the Mysterious Weapon stage. After that came Spirit Weapon, then Mythic Weapon, Sacred Arms, and finally, Divine Arms. Each stage was divided into top, middle, and lower grades, and any one of them was worth a fortune.
[SFX: CURRENT HUMMING]
His body had just been forged. And even though it was only a lower-grade Mysterious Weapon, Zane felt a current of power humming through him.
It was a strange feeling, thinking of himself as a weapon. A bitter smile touched his lips.
[AMBIENT: DAWN BREAKING]
He glanced at the cellar's only vent and saw the first rays of dawn. He'd been so consumed by the ordeal that he'd lost all track of time. But after a sleepless night and the terrifying experience, he wasn't tired.
In fact, he felt rested, energized even.
He took a deep breath, his mind surprisingly calm. All the anxiety from the night before was gone.
It was like something he'd once read: your fate is sealed from the start, down to every bite of food and sip of water. The only way to face it is with a clear head.

His jaw tightened as power hummed beneath his skin. For the first time in two years, he wasn't dreading what came next. The corner of his mouth twitched upward.
