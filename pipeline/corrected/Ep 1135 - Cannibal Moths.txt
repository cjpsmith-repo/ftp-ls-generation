Ep 1135 - Cannibal Moths

Word count: 1028

[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
The Lantern Pearl was the only light in the suffocating darkness, but it was swarmed by tiny, finger-sized black moths. They seemed to hate the light. 
[SFX: FLUTTERING WINGS]
A few suddenly dove at the pearl, and just like that, the last glimmer of hope was extinguished. 
[SFX: LIGHT EXTINGUISHED]
The moths, along with the light, vanished back into the shadows.
[SFX: SHOCKED GASP]
Abner was stunned speechless. “They’re...”
[SFX: TERRIFIED SCREAM]
“Cannibal Moths!” one of the others screamed. “And this place... this must be the Nether Water!”
“That dripping we’ve been hearing...”
“We have to get out of here, now!”
[MUSIC: RISING PANIC STRINGS]
The seven brothers, who had been mocking Zane just moments before, were suddenly gripped by pure terror.
These weren’t green recruits. The brothers had spent years crossing the universe together as independent warriors, seen things that would break ordinary men. But even they knew exactly what a Cannibal Moth meant. That was the worst part.
Two of them didn’t wait.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: VITALITY FLARE]
They bolted straight for the tunnel they’d come in through, green Vitality flaring around their bodies as they ran.
Zane opened his mouth. “That side is also—”
Too late.
[SFX: OIL LAMPS FLICKER]
[SFX: THUNDERCLAP]
[SFX: DUAL SCREAMS]
[SFX: BONES CLATTERING]
At the tunnel entrance, a pair of oil lamps flickered to life as the brothers passed — and then went dark with a sound like a thunderclap. Two screams split the air, followed by the hollow clatter of bones hitting stone.
Silence.
The tunnel was gone. There was no way out.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS DRONE]
[AMBIENT: SWARM OF WINGS IN DARKNESS]
Zane’s blood ran cold. He knew exactly what these were. In the vast encyclopedia of magical creatures, the Cannibal Moth held a terrifying reputation. It was ranked twenty-first on the Magical Insect List—a catalogue of the universe’s most powerful and bizarre insects. For comparison, the beautiful but lethal Ice Crystal Butterfly was only fifty-first, and its freezing poison could trap a person’s very soul in a block of ice.
Cannibal Moths were far worse. They began as eggs, dormant in the Nether Water for thousands, even hundreds of thousands of years. But once they sensed living prey, the eggs would cause the Nether Water to flow outward. As soon as the water dripped into the open air, the moths would hatch and begin their hunt in the dark.
Worse still, they could devour more than flesh. It was said the moths could consume a soul whole, and no ordinary method could stop them. Not fire. Not lightning. Nothing.
Through his enhanced vision, Zane could see what the others couldn’t. 
[SFX: MOTHS HATCHING AND TAKING FLIGHT]
He watched as drops of Nether Water fell from above, each one instantly hatching into a finger-sized black moth that took flight. It wasn’t just that they were moths; he’d sensed a powerful energy coming from them—the rare Dark Law. He’d encountered it once before, back in the Enchanted Barrier Tower. 
It was the enemy of light, capable of swallowing it whole, which explained the absolute blackness of this underground palace.
No one dared produce any light now. They all understood — even a flicker would paint a target on them. In the dark, they were blind. 
All of them except Zane.
The moment he saw them, Zane knew these were no ordinary creatures. That’s why he’d been so desperate for Lincoln to get the entrance open.
But the brothers had been too busy acting fearless to heed his warning. Zane decided not to waste any more breath on them. It wasn’t his job to babysit.
[SFX: PANICKED SHOUTING]
He turned away from their panicked shouts and looked back up toward the Hydra statue. A small bit of relief washed over him—the moths weren’t spreading in that direction. If they had swarmed Lincoln, he’d never be able to open the gate.
[SFX: TENSE LOCK PICKING]
Lincoln, for his part, knew something had gone terribly wrong, but he was deep in concentration, using a complex technique he’d learned from a man in the Dark World to unlock the door. He couldn’t risk the distraction. “Just a minute!” he called out, his voice tight with anxiety.
[SFX: DESPERATE YELLING]
“We don’t have a minute! What’s taking so long? Open the damn door!” Abner swore into the darkness. He never imagined they’d run into something like this. He’d already lost three brothers in the blink of an eye.
[SFX: HORRIFYING SCREAM]
Just as he finished speaking, another horrifying scream tore through the blackness. The Cannibal Moths had claimed another victim.
[SFX: BODY SHOVED]
[SFX: SWARM ENGULFING VICTIM]
The instant the scream died, Abner whirled around and shoved the person closest to him, sending the man flying straight into a gathering swarm.
“Abner, that was Gilbert!” Byron yelled, his voice choked with fury and disbelief.
“If I hadn’t, we’d all be dead!” Abner shot back, his voice chillingly cold.
[SFX: STUMBLING FOOTSTEPS BACKWARD]
A wave of despair crashed over the remaining survivors as they stumbled backward. The moths were everywhere. They had no idea if retreating was the right move, but pure instinct took over.
In the darkness, Freya kept her teeth clenched tight. She was terrified — anyone would be — but she hadn’t made a sound or caused Zane a moment’s trouble. She’d made her choice when she followed him here. She wasn’t about to regret it now.
Zane found her hand in the dark. “Don’t be nervous,” he said quietly. “Stay with me.”
She nodded. She didn’t trust herself to speak.
“Everyone gather close.” Adrian’s voice came through the black, steady and controlled. “When Lincoln opens the palace, we move in. Panic won’t help anyone.”
Abner echoed him without argument. “You heard him. Gather.”
Zane, at least, had an advantage. His vision allowed him to map the swarms in the dark, and he carefully steered Freya around them, pulling her close. He’d already guided them past three or four clusters, but the dripping sound was relentless. 
[SFX: RELENTLESS DRIPPING]
More moths were hatching with every passing second, and the safe space around him and Freya was shrinking fast.
