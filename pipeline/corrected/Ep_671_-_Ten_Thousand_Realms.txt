Ep 671 - Ten Thousand Realms

Word count: 1104

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Escaping the Mad Scorpion’s guts didn’t mean they were free of the fog. Yolande had been right about that. No matter how hard they struggled, a strange force kept pulling them toward the chasm below.

It was obvious none of them stood a chance against the Dark Nether Death Light.

[SFX: MYSTICAL GLOW PULSE]

If it weren’t for the Crabstone Amulet glowing on Zane’s chest, they would’ve been dead already. In a situation this dangerous, all they could do was trust Yolande.

"Why were they trying to kill us?” Zane asked, his curiosity getting the better of him.

“This scorpion is their world,” she explained. “It’s their job to kill trespassers.”

“So what did you say to them?” Zane pressed, giving her a hard look.

“You’ll see,” she said, shutting down the conversation.

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Trapped in the vast net, they could only watch as the world zipped by beneath them. They shot over rivers and mountains at an impossible speed.

After what felt like two hours, a massive wooden wall erupted from the horizon, studded with hundreds of sharpened spikes—the first line of defense for a city built by the green-skinned creatures.

A handful of guards on the wall tugged on the net’s ropes, guiding them over the fortifications

[AMBIENT: PRISON COURTYARD]

[SFX: HEAVY THUD]

They landed with a thud in a dusty corner, right next to a prison cage made of crude iron bars. Once they were untangled from the net, the creatures leveled their spears at them. One of them jabbed a finger toward the cage, grunting an order for them to get inside.

All eyes went to Yolande. She gave a slow, deliberate blink—her signal that it was safe to cooperate. She went first, walking calmly toward the cage, and the others fell in line behind her.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

But just as Zane reached the entrance, a guard stepped in front of him, blocking the way with his spear. The creature grunted something guttural and meaningless, but its eyes were locked on Zane’s chest. Zane followed its gaze and saw that the spear tip was pointed directly at his Storage Ring. The creature’s face was a mask of pure, undisguised greed.

Zane didn’t need a translator. He knew exactly what the thing wanted.

The faces of everyone inside the cage went dark. They understood immediately — without the Storage Ring, the Crabstone Amulet was gone. And without the Crabstone Amulet, the Death Light would cut them all down the moment they stepped outside this world.

"Don't give it to him," Esther said, her voice cold.

Fists clenched. Bodies tensed. They couldn't circulate their Vitality, but they still had their bodies — and that would be enough to deal with these creatures if it came to it.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

His Storage Ring. Everything he owned was in there—his treasures, his wealth, a massive amount of Celestial Essence. He'd even just put the Crabstone Amulet, the only thing keeping them alive against the Death Light, into that ring.

The green-skinned guard reached out to take it.

"Zane." Yolande's voice was quiet but certain. "Give it to him."

"But—" Zane's jaw tightened.

"The ring will be returned," she said. "And they don't know how to recognize a master. They can't open it. Trust me."

Zane held her gaze for a long moment. Then, slowly, he pulled the Storage Ring from around his neck and placed it in the creature's outstretched hand. The guard mumbled something and smiled. Zane watched him go, his eyes narrow and cold.

[SFX: IRON BARS SLAMMING SHUT]

He turned and walked into the cage. The gate clanged shut behind him. He looked straight at Yolande.

"Whatever your reasons are," he said evenly, "you owe me an explanation. Now."

Everyone's eyes were on her.

Yolande was quiet for a moment. Then she said, "The inheritance you're looking for — it's here. Inside this world."

That much Zane had already suspected. It wasn't enough.

Yolande continued. "The Dark Nether Cave is a battleground. What you're really looking at is a conflict between two Empyreans — and what they're fighting over is a fragment."

"Empyreans." Zane's eyes flickered. He'd first heard the word from the Cobalt Dragon — the Stellar Body itself had been created by a being called Empyrean Star. For a warrior to forge something like that, their power was beyond imagining.

"Our people aren't unified in the upper world," Yolande continued. "The human race is divided into three major forces. The largest is led by Malachi, four thousand realms under his banner. Second is Lumen, with three thousand. The smallest is Soren, who commands two thousand."

[MUSIC: SWELLING TENSION]

Fabian's eyes went wide. "Four thousand realms? That's — if you combine all three, that's over ten thousand."

Jarrod shook his head slowly, lips pressed together. Like the others who'd come up from the main continent, he'd always assumed that humans were the weaker race in the upper world. They'd never imagined anything like this.

"Ten thousand realms," Jarrod murmured. "I never would have believed it."

Nearby, the two Ogres, Malcolm and Timothy, stood in stunned silence. Their hearts hammered in their chests at what Yolande had just revealed. Back in the Tower of Sin, the Ogres had never shut up about being the “perfect race.” Humans, in their view, were frail, weak, and inferior—no match for an Ogre’s talent, intelligence, or strength.

But Zane’s power was a direct contradiction to everything they believed. And to now learn that Humans had somehow become a terrifying force in the upper world…

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

Lavender appeared then, a soft, almost condescending smile on her face. “Controlling a hundred thousand worlds is impressive,” she said, “but it’s hardly something for Humans to brag about. They don’t even have the guts to fight the Demon Night.”

Yolande just glanced at her and shook her head. “Your people are strong, I’ll give you that. But you have so few children. We couldn’t surpass you in the last thousand years, sure. But in another hundred? How many warriors will you have left to fight us then?”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The Demon Night were incredibly powerful, but their population growth was glacial. Back when they’d conquered countless worlds, they never had enough people to actually occupy them. Now, their empire had shrunk to no more than a thousand worlds of chaos.

“A race’s power isn’t measured by how many worlds it holds,” Lavender countered. “Or have you forgotten that?”

[SFX: SHARP BREATH]

Yolande shook her head again, cutting the debate short. “Let’s drop this. The real issue is the conflict between Malachi and Lumen. I didn’t get into it with Zane earlier because, frankly, I didn’t think it was my place to speak of such matters in your presence.”

But the truth was, that last remark was aimed directly at Lavender.
