Ep 1012 - I Got This

Word count: 1023

[AMBIENT: CAVE INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane knew exactly what he was seeing.

Freya couldn’t take the Sovereign Stones, but that didn’t mean he couldn’t.

[SFX: BEAST BREATHING]

Freya’s face tightened in frustration. “We need to get out of here,” she said urgently. “If that beast wakes up, we’re toast.”

But Zane just held out his hand to her. “Hand it over.”

Freya stared at his open palm, confused. “What?”

“Your shroud,” Zane said. “Let me borrow it.”

Realization washed over her. “You’re going to try it?” she asked, her voice laced with disbelief.

Zane nodded. Freya didn’t know what to think. She was a whole realm of power above him. If she couldn’t get them, how could he possibly manage?

[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]

As if he’d read her mind, he cracked a smile. “Don’t worry. I got this.”

She was skeptical, but there was something about his determined expression that made her feel like she had to let him try. A little voice in the back of her head insisted he would actually succeed.

Besides, they were already here. It would be a total waste to leave empty-handed. If Zane wanted to give it a shot, who was she to say no?

[SFX: FABRIC RUSTLING]

She sighed, slipping the shroud from her shoulders and placing it in his waiting hand.

The shroud was designed to mask one’s presence. The moment Zane pulled it on, his energy signature vanished, his aura becoming nearly impossible to detect.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Then, without another word, he shot toward the Sovereign Stones like a lightning bolt.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Freya barely had time to process his recklessness. Her hand flew to her mouth in shock, her heart pounding with every silent step he took. She held her breath, not daring to make a sound.

As he got closer without alerting the beast, she finally let out a shaky sigh of relief. This guy is completely nuts, she thought.

When he reached the Sovereign Stones, Zane tried to pull one from the ceiling, just as Freya had.

[SFX: STONE SCRAPING]

It wouldn’t budge.

Zane channeled the power of the Dragon Scales, giving it another hard yank.

He activated a thousand Dragon Scales, but it was useless. The Sovereign Stone didn’t move an inch. This time, though, he didn’t bother trying to use more strength.

He had enough power to move mountains, which meant only one thing: there had to be some kind of Law of Causality woven into the jades. Brute force wasn’t going to work.

[SFX: Storage Ring ACTIVATION]

He turned his back to Freya. Under the shroud’s cover, she couldn’t risk using her perception — she wouldn’t see a thing. He pressed his Storage Ring quietly and drew out the Black Star Pickax.

He extended it slowly and pried at the base of the nearest stone.

[SFX: SOFT CRACK]

It dropped cleanly into his palm.

He paused. Then it hit him — they were all on the same root. Why do this one at a time? He repositioned the pickax at the base of the entire cluster and pried upward. The Law of Causality released all at once. The whole thing came free.

He slipped the Black Star Pickax back into his Storage Ring, then pocketed the entire cluster in one motion. He turned around and gave Freya a small wave.

Her eyes went wide. She had used everything she had and failed. He’d made it look like nothing. Her instincts had been right. Then, just for a moment, a quieter thought crept in — would he keep them all? She dismissed it almost as quickly as it came. No. That wasn’t who he was.

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

He was already walking back toward her when all nine of the Hydra’s heads lifted at once.

The Sovereign Stone cluster had been releasing overflow Vitality for as long as the beast had slept here — a steady current that had drawn it to this spot in the first place. The moment Zane pulled the cluster free, the flow stopped. Every head sniffed simultaneously.

[AMBIENT: CAVE ECHO]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

Freya saw it first. Zane’s back was still to the beast. Her face went white. She covered her mouth and pointed frantically at something behind him.

[SFX: DEEP ROAR]

Just then, a booming voice echoed through the cave. “A mere human from the Life-and-Death Realm dares to steal my Sovereign Stones? Your escape is pointless. I suggest you stop running. My nine brothers have been hungry for a very long time.”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane’s eyes widened. So, the Hydra could talk. That intelligence, combined with its raw power, made it a terrifying opponent.

Hydras were legendary beasts, on par with Cobalt Dragons—and far smarter than any human.

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]

[SFX: ROCK CRUMBLING]

He didn’t look back. He shot toward Freya like an arrow. Behind him, one of the Hydra’s heads crashed down onto the rock where he’d been standing, splitting it into pieces. The whole cave shook.

[MUSIC: FRANTIC CHASE]

“Quick!” Zane yelled, already sprinting back the way they came. “Through that passage up ahead!”

Zane reached Freya and grabbed her by the arm, hauling her forward. She was still frozen, eyes fixed on the beast. He dragged her for two full strides before she snapped out of it and started running properly.

[AMBIENT: CAVE ECHOES]

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

They sprinted through the winding passages, the Hydra storming right behind them.

“Is there any way to slow it down?” Zane yelled over his shoulder.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

The words were barely out of his mouth when Freya spun around. Vitality exploded from her, instantly forming a swirling ring of snow.

[SFX: ICE FORMATION]

“Snowfall Domain!”

Normally, a domain moved with its caster, but Freya launched this one backward at the charging Hydra before turning to keep pace with Zane.

[SFX: TAIL SLAM]

[SFX: SHATTERING GLASS]

As the Hydra crashed through the Snowfall Domain, its movements slowed. But just a moment later, it swung its massive tail, slamming it against the ground.

The domain Freya had created shattered like glass.
