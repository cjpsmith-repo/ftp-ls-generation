Ep 548 - A Demonic Dragon
Word count: 1057
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT MYSTICAL WIND]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Once, seven divine kingdoms lived in harmony with the dragon-blooded. But that peace shattered the day the dragon-blooded vanished, and three of the seven kingdoms fell, leaving only four.
These four kingdoms claimed the main continent of this world, their wealth eclipsing the entire Heartlands. Their heritage was ancient, stretching back to the very dawn of time.
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
As the man from the Divine Kingdom swung his Serpentine Sword, a surge of Vitality erupted, coalescing into the form of a massive snake behind him.
[SFX: DEMONIC ROAR]
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
A bizarre creature took shape—it had the head of a huge dragon but the slender, coiling body of a serpent. Its eyes blazed with a demonic red glow, and a terrifying aura rolled off it in waves.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
"A Demonic Dragon," Elder Imogen breathed, her voice tight with horror as she stared at the monster.
Tia bit her lip. "Zane," she whispered, her eyes filled with worry.
All the other warriors from the Heartlands who had reached the Life-and-Death Realm, including Maddox and the ancient tycoon, looked grim.
"The Divine Kingdoms are on a whole different level," someone exclaimed. "Even at the early Spirit-Tribulation Realm, with his power suppressed, he can still pull off something like that. He’s the real deal."
"Zane's screwed! He's only at the Soul Realm, and his power's been suppressed, too," another warrior added. "It's weird, though. The guy's taking him so seriously. Isn't this like using a sledgehammer to smash a fly?"
"Either way, Zane's a dead man. You just don't pick a fight with someone from a divine kingdom. They're out of our league."
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]
Zane ignored the chatter, his focus locked on the man and his Demonic Dragon.
Against an ordinary Spirit-Tribulation warrior, suppressed to early Inner-Core Realm, Zane would have no concern—his unsuppressed power could kill such an opponent instantly.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
[SFX: DEEP DRAGON ROAR]
This man was different. His cultivation method pulsed with overwhelming power, and something about the Demonic Dragon resonated with Zane's own bloodline.
The cultivation method was left by the dragon-blooded, the Cobalt Dragon explained. After their extinction and the Rising Dragon Platform's disappearance, the four Divine Kingdoms seized their inheritance. This Prince cultivates one of their highest-tier techniques, substituting a demon dragon for the True Dragon blood he couldn't obtain.
Zane understood. But the danger remained.
[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]
[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]
He raised his iron sword—and began to swing it slowly.
One circle.
Two circles.
Three circles.
Four circles.
[MUSIC: DARK RUMBLING TENSION]
Four rings of killing intent materialized around him, quietly orbiting. The Exterminating Wheels. He'd spent a long time mastering them, but had never shown them to anyone. He wasn't a bloodthirsty man—killing intent didn't accumulate easily. He'd kept this technique as a last resort.
Facing a Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior from the Divine Kingdoms, he didn't hesitate.
[MUSIC: DARK RUMBLING TENSION]
[SFX: SERPENT HISS]
The Prince barely glanced at the wheels. He raised his Serpentine Sword almost lazily, and the Demonic Dragon coiled around him—then lunged.
"Dragon Snake Devour!"
[SFX: DEMONIC ROAR]
The dragon's massive head surged toward Zane, jaws wide.
Zane raised his sword vertically and reached out with his mind—connecting to the distant Slaughter Sword Mountain.
Release.
[SFX: MASSIVE ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DREAD]
The killing intent that erupted from his body hit the crowd like a physical force. One moment, they'd sensed nothing from him—the next, it was as though a demon who had slaughtered millions of creatures was standing on that platform. Life-and-Death Realm warriors who had seen everything flinched.
"This brat—how much killing intent does he have? Isn't he afraid of the backlash?"
"How was he hiding it? I felt nothing from him before. Nothing!"
"Too many secrets. I can't figure him out."
[SFX: WHOOSHING WHEELS]
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]
The killing intent flooded into the Exterminating Wheels. Then Zane's sword flickered—and the wheels shot forward.
[SFX: PIERCING IMPACT]
All four tore straight through the Demonic Dragon's skull, shredded through its long snake body, and burst out the tail. The dragon dissolved. The four killing intents continued—straight toward the Prince.
The Prince's face went white.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER ACTIVATION]
A light curtain snapped up around him. The four wheels slammed into it.
[SFX: SHATTERING]
The curtain held—but the jade amulet hanging at the Prince's chest exploded into dust.
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT THEME]
"A good protective treasure," Zane said, already moving. "It saved you once. It won't save you again."
[SFX: CLASH OF BLADES]
Their swords met. The power of five hundred dragon scales behind Zane's strike knocked the Serpentine Sword clean from the Prince's grip.
[SFX: BONE CRACK]
Blood burst from the Prince's hand.
The Prince turned to run. The sight of him scrambling away filled Zane with contempt.
This guy was supposedly some noble with incredible talent, someone who’d spent his life crushing others with his status. He’d never learned to properly size up an opponent; he just charged in, expecting to overwhelm everyone. He wasn't an idiot, but he was a coward. The second he faced someone he couldn't beat, he’d try to act dumb and bolt.
Zane shook his head and moved, blocking his path. "Are you really from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm? You're way too weak for me."
[SFX: SWORD SWING]
With that, Zane raised his sword and brought it down.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
"NO!" Gong's voice ripped through the chasm, making Zane hesitate for a fraction of a second.
"If you kill him, I swear I'll kill you," the holy maiden of the divine kingdom warned, her eyes like chips of ice fixed on Zane.
That moment of hesitation was all the man needed.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
He twisted his body like a snake, dodging Zane’s sword at the last second. But even as his body avoided the blade, his mind was slammed with a wave of pain so intense it felt like his very soul was being ripped apart.
[SFX: BLADE PIERCE]
[MUSIC: DARK DRAMATIC IMPACT]
The man from the Divine Kingdom dropped to his knees, writhing, his eyes rolling back in his head. In the next instant, a silver light flashed, piercing straight through his skull. Zane had ended it cleanly with a Shattered Dagger.
The truth was, even suppressed, the man from the Divine Kingdom wasn't much weaker than Zane. His arrogance was his downfall. He never expected Zane to be so strong and was caught completely off guard.
Still, even if he had gone all out from the start, he likely would have lost anyway. After all, Zane’s power hadn't been suppressed in the slightest.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
 [MUSIC: SOMBER AFTERMATH]
A heavy silence fell over the chasm.
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED MURMURS]
A Soul Realm warrior killing someone from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm… It was unheard of.
Every warrior there could only stare at Zane, completely and utterly speechless.
