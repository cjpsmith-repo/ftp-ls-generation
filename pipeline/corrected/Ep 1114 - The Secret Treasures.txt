Ep 1114 - The Secret Treasures

Word count: 988

[MUSIC: CASUALLY CURIOUS]
Zane was a little surprised that Lincoln had actually come to see him. He’d thrown the match, sure, but that was because he saw a bit of his own story in Layla’s desperation.
Lord knew Zane’s own problems were a whole lot bigger. He had no clue where Elara was, and even after using the Roaring Token to blast his voice across the entire universe, he’d come up with nothing.
Lincoln, true to his character, was a straight shooter. 
The moment he arrived, he bowed his head in a gesture of profound gratitude. It might have been a small thing for Zane to throw a match, but for Lincoln, getting his freedom back was everything. 
He’d pissed off the son of a Supreme Lord a while back and gotten himself banished to the Dark World for it, his future shot. For a warrior, a lifespan of only eight hundred years is practically a death sentence. This wasn’t just a lucky break; it was a new life.
Layla, meanwhile, sat quietly next to her brother, a complicated mess of feelings churning inside her. From the second she’d arrived at The Mist Palace, she’d pegged Zane as a rival, constantly trying to get a rise out of him. But he’d never taken the bait. And now, he’d gone out of his way to help her brother win his freedom.
Lincoln’s expression turned serious. 
[MUSIC: SUBTLE TENSION RISING]
“I came here for my sister,” he said, “but there’s something else, something important I need to talk to you about.”
“What is it?” Zane asked, his curiosity piqued. He’d figured this was just a thank-you visit.
Lincoln’s eyes darted around the room, his whole demeanor suddenly wary. 
[SFX: SCROLL UNFURLING]
[SFX: ENERGY BARRIER ACTIVATION]
He pulled out a pale yellow, shimmering scroll and channeled his energy into it. A faint ripple in the air was the only sign of the invisible barrier that spread out, enclosing the three of them.
Seeing that level of caution, Zane leaned forward. What could possibly be so important that it required this much secrecy?
Lincoln took a breath and began to explain. The Dark World, he said, had once been something worth talking about — a prosperous, great world with a long history. But hundreds of millions of years of depletion had hollowed it out. Its sects had packed up and left one by one until the whole place was good for nothing except dumping criminals. 
That was what it had become: a land of exile, lawless and brutal, where the only rule was that there were no rules. Cultivators killed each other there just to survive.
When Lincoln had first arrived, he’d nearly died. More than once. But his luck had held — barely. While being chased down yet again, he’d stumbled into an abandoned cave dwelling. And inside, he’d found something: clues to the secret treasures of the Dark World.
“Secret treasures of the Dark World?” Zane said.
Lincoln’s eyes lit up. “Yes. And I want to invite you to explore it with me.”
Faced with the invitation, Zane’s expression was more uncertain than Lincoln expected. He didn’t know enough about the Dark World treasure to feel the full weight of it — the significance that would have made even a Sector Lord sit up straight was lost on him.
At that moment, Lavender’s voice reached him quietly. “Zane. Say yes.”
“Why—”
“Say yes first.”
Lavender rarely spoke up unprompted. When she did, Zane had learned not to question it. There weren’t many people in the universe he trusted completely — she was one of them.
“All right,” he said. “I’m in.”
Lincoln hadn’t expected agreement to come that quickly — he’d been braced for negotiation. He reached into his robes and produced a map he’d drawn in advance, marking the meeting point deep inside the Dark World. “Half a year from now,” he said, handing it over. “Meet me at this location.”
Zane took the map and studied it for a moment. “Where exactly is the Dark World?”
Lincoln blinked. Layla, sitting beside him, let out a short laugh. “Brother — Zane’s an ascender. He hasn’t been up here long.”
Lincoln absorbed this. Then, slowly, he explained. When he finished, a smile crossed Zane’s face. 
The Dark World was adjacent to his home realm — the great world he’d ascended from, sitting right at the edge of Alliance territory. The other side of the Dark World was outside the Alliance entirely. With that kind of distance, even realm-hopping continuously would take months. Now the six-month timeline made sense.
[AMBIENT: QUIET EMPTY ROOM]
After the siblings left, Lincoln’s offer echoed in his mind. *The secret treasures of the Dark World.* He turned to the empty air. “Lavender,” he said, “what’s the real story? Why did you want me on this team so badly?”
[SFX: ETHEREAL MATERIALIZATION]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
Lavender slowly materialized in front of him, a faint smile on her lips. “The secret treasures are a legend from a hundred thousand years ago,” she explained. “But I was there, so I happen to know a few of the real secrets. And some of those things are very important to you.” 
She tilted her head. “I have no idea if this Lincoln really has a way to get them, but it’s definitely worth a shot.”
Zane listened, nodding slowly. She had a point.
He couldn’t just sit around the Mist Palace and train forever. Besides, he’d promised Lavender he’d help her reclaim her throne.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: PALACE COURTYARD]
Half a year wasn’t a lot of time for warriors in the Upper World—just crossing through the supreme worlds could take months. After a few quick preparations, Zane went to say goodbye to Yolande.
She wasn’t surprised by his sudden departure. Even the warriors from the Thirteen Palaces were always looking for chances to go out and test themselves. On top of that, she’d promised him from the start that she wouldn’t hold him back.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS FADING]
[SFX: PORTAL ACTIVATION]
With nothing left to say, Zane quietly left The Mist Palace and stepped into the shimmering portal of the supreme world channel.
