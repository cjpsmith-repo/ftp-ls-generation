Ep 961 - A Divine Weapon
Word count: 1166
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMUR]
Zane lay on the ground, stunned — struck down in front of his own wedding guests by an old man no one had ever seen before.
A few of the fighters, however, had smirks on their faces. Zane had shot up like a rocket, hogging the limelight for what felt like forever. Seeing him finally get knocked down a peg was a welcome sight.
Tia’s expression hardened, her cheeks flushing with anger. She stomped her foot, and the pearl circlet on her head wobbled as she broke into a run, heading for Zane.
Xander, the leader of Cloud Hall, and the three burly alliance heads all watched nervously. None of them had a clue what was about to happen.
[SFX: TIME STOP WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
But before anyone could even twitch, time just stopped.
Back in the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, when they’d hatched the Ancestral Dragon Egg, the Celestial Rank warriors had accelerated the flow of time. A single day inside the hatchery was only a few seconds in the real world. Time there slipped through your fingers like sand; a single year in The Heartlands was a thousand years inside that barrier.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
And now, here in the Cloud Hall, time had slowed to a crawl—a thousand times slower.
Everything was happening at one-thousandth of its normal speed. People were moving slower than snails; in fact, you could barely see them move at all.
What was even more terrifying was the sheer scale of it. Time had effectively frozen in a thousand-mile radius around the Cloud Hall. It was the complete opposite of the hatchery’s enchanted barrier, both in power and direction.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Birds hung motionless in the sky. Smoke rising from distant chimneys stood in solid columns, refusing to break apart. The ripples on the surface of Emberfin Lake were locked in place. The entire world of The Heartlands seemed to be holding its breath.
Somehow, though, the Ancestral Dragon wasn’t affected at all.
“Gagoom,” the Ancestral Dragon cried out. For all its power, it had the intelligence of a human toddler. The shift in time didn’t affect its body, but it was confused about why Cora had suddenly stopped moving.
Then, a powerful instinct kicked in.
[SFX: POWERFUL WHOOSH]
It sensed something and shot out the window, flying straight for the Hall.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TENSION]
The old man smiled faintly and glanced up at the sky, letting out a soft sigh. “Time waits for no one. You’ve gotten a lot stronger, but the resources in this Lower World are just too scarce. Your progress is still too slow. Let me give you a little push.”
Just as he finished speaking, the Ancestral Dragon swooped in.
[SFX: DRAGON CRY]
“Gagoom, gagoom,” the creature cried, frantic, when it saw Zane lying on the ground with his eyes closed.
The old man ignored it. He reached into his robes and produced a dark green pill, holding it out on his withered palm.
[SFX: SHARP WHOOSH]
The moment it was exposed to the air, the pill shot straight up toward the sky.
“You’ve picked up a little intelligence, and now you think you can run?” The old man flicked his wrist. An invisible force seized the pill and dragged it back down.
[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]
The pill wasn’t done. Green light pulsed inside it, condensing into a bolt of lightning that lashed out at the old man’s hand.
He didn’t even flinch. He closed his fingers around it and, with a single squeeze, tore the pill apart. It dissolved into a churning mass of dark green cloud.
[SFX: LOW RESONANT HUM]
“Go,” he said softly.
The green cloud spiralled slowly, then narrowed to a trickle and flowed down into Zane’s mouth. After a moment, it was gone — completely absorbed.
Only then did the old man walk back to his seat. Without asking, he poured himself a cup from the wine pot, took a sip, and blinked.
[SFX: TIME STOP WHOOSH — REVERSE]
Time snapped back.
Tia’s momentum carried her forward — she stumbled half a step before catching herself, expression still blazing.
Around the hall, everyone blinked and stirred, as though waking from a dream. They could all see what had just happened. They could all think. But none of them had been able to move.
That was the Law of Time. Not a full stop — even the greatest masters of time could only slow it, never truly freeze it. But slowing it a thousandfold across a thousand-mile radius, without so much as a gesture? In all of The Heartlands, there was not a single warrior known to have mastered the Law of Time. It was rarer and harder than even the Law of Space. The crowd had assumed this old man was powerful. They were realizing now they had no idea.
And then there was the pill.
Whatever it had contained, the green lightning it had released in those few moments could have killed most of the people in that room. A pill that could think. A pill that could fight back. Some of the fighters who’d been smirking earlier now felt faintly ill.
“I may not have said anything,” the old man mumbled, “but did you really think they were yours to keep?”
Everyone stared at each other, completely baffled. Was this guy nuts? Why was he talking to his own stomach? Was he some kind of madman?
[SFX: BODY MOVEMENT]
Just then, Zane pushed himself up, a grin spreading across his face. “You got experts in your Inner World who use Divine-class Weapons?”
The old man rolled his eyes. “There’s this guy inside me. He reached the Soul Sea Realm and has been squatting in there ever since, calling himself some ‘sacred emperor.’ He even stashed away the thousand Divine Weapons I had stored in my Inner World. Actually thinks they’re his now. The nerve of that guy. Unbelievable! Now give them back!”
[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHIMES]
He then pointed a finger, and a bright gold bracelet materialized out of thin air, floating right into his hand. He laughed. “I came in such a rush, I didn’t have time to get you something nice. This bracelet was all I could think of. It’s a little simple, I know. I’m almost embarrassed to offer it. But it is a Divine Weapon, so I figure it’ll get the job done.”
The bracelet then floated over to Tia.
“A Divine Weapon…”
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMURS]
Jaws dropped. People stared, unable to process what they were seeing and hearing.
The most powerful treasures in all of The Heartlands were a handful of mid-grade Relic Weapons, and even those were so rare that most people had only ever read about them in books. Practically no one had ever seen one in person.
And this old man had just called a gift like that ‘too simple’. It really burned the other fighters up.
