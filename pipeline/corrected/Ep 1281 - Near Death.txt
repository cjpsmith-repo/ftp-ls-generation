Zane knew he was about to be dragged into the shadows, but he already had a plan. He’d seen this coming.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE FLAME BURST]
He used the fiery blast from Hailey’s Moonburst Flamebow as cover. The energy from it was incredible, but the flames washed right over him without leaving a mark—fire couldn’t hurt him. 
Her attack was the perfect distraction. He was counting on one of them to take the bait and hook him; then, he could finally take them down.
[SFX: ENERGY WHOOSH]
The kill wasn’t his, though, so the burst of Illusion Points from the incinerated warrior shot straight toward Hailey.
The most surprising thing, however, was that the other team’s ace somehow withstood the blast. 
He just charged straight through the inferno, sword raised, coming right for Zane.
[SFX: SWORD SLICING AIR]
In a flash, Larkin was on him, his longsword slicing through the air. But before it could connect, Zane sensed something deeply wrong with the weapon. It looked old and unremarkable, like some basic, unranked piece of junk. But his gut screamed that it was far more than that. The pressure rolling off the blade was immense.
One hit from that thing, and he was dead. He was sure of it.
[SFX: HOOK SNAG AND YANK]
Just as that thought crossed his mind, a hook snagged him and yanked him backward.
* “Bring him back!”* the dark iron sword commanded, its voice pure authority.
[SFX: HOOK WHOOSHING THROUGH AIR]
In the next instant, a second hook shot toward Zane. 
Larkin wasn’t about to let him kill someone and just get away. 
[SFX: STEEL HOOK LATCHING]
His hook flew like a steel viper, latching onto Zane just as Janet’s had.
[SFX: METAL HOOKS COLLIDING]
[SFX: HOOKS FLYING LOOSE]
The impact of the two hooks—one from his ally, one from his enemy—sent them both flying loose, as if repelled by an invisible force.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC FALLING STRINGS]
With both hooks gone, Zane had nothing to hold him in the air. He began to plummet into the deep ravine below.
[SFX: RUSHING ENERGY CURRENT]
For a split second, he glimpsed the multicolored river of energy flowing lazily at the bottom. He knew the rules of the Hall: that harmless-looking current was a death sentence. Anyone who fell in was gone for good.
Neither side was willing to lose a man.
Zane felt no real fear at the thought of falling. There was no true death in the Illusion Battlefield—only defeat, and a trip back to the main city. So instead of scrambling to save himself, he went on the attack. Before he’d even begun to drop, he hurled his own hook at Larkin.
[SFX: HOOK LAUNCHING THROUGH AIR]
The sword sensed the danger first and hurled a warning into Larkin’s mind, but it came a breath too late. Zane’s hook was already faster.
[SFX: HOOK CATCHING FLESH]
It caught Larkin clean and hauled him off the edge, dragging him down into the fall alongside Zane.
“This guy is unforgivable!” the sword roared, its fury cutting through the air like a blade of its own.
[SFX: TWO HOOKS LAUNCHING SIMULTANEOUSLY]
Neither of them had actually landed a killing blow—so at the last second, both sides yanked their men back. Coleman’s hook caught Zane first; an ally’s hook caught Larkin a heartbeat later.
[SFX: METAL HOOKS COLLIDING]
But Larkin wasn’t finished. He whipped his hook out again, knocking Coleman’s line loose, and Zane started to fall once more.
[SFX: HOOK SNAPPING LOOSE]
Zane answered in kind, lashing his own hook across Larkin’s line and shaking it free. Down they went together, a second time.
[SFX: BODY TWISTING MID-AIR]
Larkin twisted hard in the open air, spinning to face him, and a blade of pure sword-light tore free from the dark iron sword itself—smooth, controlled, and terrifyingly sharp even with only a fraction of Larkin’s Vitality behind it.
[SFX: SWORD LIGHT SLICING AIR]
Zane drew Thundergust in answer. Lightning and wind blades howled out of the blade, and dozens of Nether God Shadows surged toward Larkin.
Neither of them so much as flinched, even as they kept falling toward the river below.
Zane’s eyes went wide. There was no dodging that sword-light—not with the position he was in. One clean hit, and it was over.
[SFX: HOOK CATCHING AND YANKING]
But at the last possible instant, another hook snagged him and hauled him sideways, the sudden twist just enough to pull him clear of the blade. Larkin was saved the same way, another hook wrenching him out of the strike’s path a breath before it connected.
[SFX: MULTIPLE HOOKS LAUNCHING]
Once again, their hooks shot out.
They kept falling, only to be yanked back up by their respective teams. It became a deadly dance. Seven times they fell, and seven times their allies pulled them back from the brink, their hooks clashing in mid-air every single time.
Neither of them dared to let their guard down, but Larkin was secretly reeling. This guy looked so ordinary, yet he was unbelievably strong. He had completely underestimated Zane. Even with his powerful sword, he couldn’t gain an edge, and a knot of anxiety tightened in his gut.
[SFX: MASSIVE FIRE EXPLOSION]
Just then, another explosion of fire erupted right over Larkin’s head.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS BOOM]
[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]
The massive blast created a wall of fire, cutting off the area of Dark Law from everything else. The warriors inside the zone couldn’t see Larkin through the flames.
[SFX: HOOKS WHOOSHING THROUGH AIR]
The two hooks meant to save him flew right past their target—and headed straight for Zane.
[SFX: BODY FALLING INTO ENERGY RIVER]
[SFX: HOOK CATCHING FLESH]
With nothing to catch him, Larkin fell, plunging directly into the river of energy. Zane, on the other hand, was accidentally snagged by one of the enemy hooks and dragged into the Dark Law zone on the other side.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS SUSTAINED STRINGS]
The moment Zane’s feet hit solid ground, he caught a glimpse of Larkin out of the corner of his eye. 
The man was staring up at him as he sank into the colorful current, his eyes burning with cold, undiluted hatred.
