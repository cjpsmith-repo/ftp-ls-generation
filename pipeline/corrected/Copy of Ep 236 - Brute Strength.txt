Ep 236 - Brute Strength

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]
[SFX: SURGING MAGIC]
Everyone in the arena held their breath as the opponents faced off. Benjamin, his face etched with concentration, maintained his defensive formation while carefully observing his opponent. Jill stood opposite him, her delicate frame belying the deadly intent in her eyes.
She looked at the swirling rings of Vitality that Benjamin had conjured, her gaze calculating as she searched for weaknesses in his defense. Neither competitor moved for several heartbeats as the tension mounted.
The rings of Vitality spinning out from Benjamin were dense, but not impenetrable. The second a gap opened in the fluttering storm, Jill made her move.
[SFX: WHOOSHING MOVEMENT]
She shot forward, weaving through the rings of Vitality with fluid grace.
Benjamin had been feeling pretty smug. The delicate appearance of his opponent had lulled him into a false sense of security; he'd been sure this match would end swiftly in his favor. But before his self-congratulatory thoughts could fully form, a blur of yellow charged directly toward him, slicing through his defenses.
[SFX: BATTLE CRY]
[SFX: BLADE SWINGING]
A primal howl escaped his lips as he swung his scarlet machete in a wide arc, aiming the gleaming blade at Jill's advancing form before unleashing three powerful swings.
"Three Strikes of Bloody Hell!" he bellowed, pouring his essence into the technique.
[SFX: METAL CLASHING]
Jill kept smiling throughout the exchange, her expression almost angelically sweet. The contrast between her gentle demeanor and the deadly precision of her movements unsettled Benjamin deeply. Beneath that exterior lurked pure, unyielding strength that radiated from her like heat from a forge.
The Emerald Sword clutched in her hand appeared absurdly small next to Benjamin's scarlet machete---barely longer than a pair of elegant chopsticks. But the tiny blade danced through the air, effortlessly deflecting the crushing force of his prized technique. Each parry sent vibrations up Benjamin's arms, shocking him with the realization that he had gravely underestimated his opponent.
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
Benjamin's eyes widened in disbelief. He scrambled to launch another attack, but a flash of cold light shot out, and the cold steel of the Emerald Sword was suddenly pressed against his neck.
[MUSIC: VICTORY FANFARE]
"Jill wins! Benjamin is defeated!" the referee announced.
It was over so fast Eliot barely registered what had happened until the referee called the match. He'd expected Jill might pull out some secret move to turn things around, but he was floored by the sheer speed and ferocity of her victory.
At that moment, Eliot shot Zane a look. The guy really had a sharp eye.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: COMPETITIVE TENSION]
The first nine matches wrapped up quickly. As one group of disciples filed out of the arena, the next group was already marching in.
Zane was in the second group.
"Huh? Am I seeing things? Is that guy really only at the second-grade of the Adept Realm? Where'd he even come from?"
"Yeah, he's only second-grade. Still, to make it this far as a second-grade Adept\... he's gotta be pretty good. Remember Jacob three years ago? He was only second-grade too, and he made it into the top hundred. You can't underestimate anyone. Never judge a book by its cover."
"Bullshit! You can't compare this guy to Jacob. Jacob was a freak of nature, a personal disciple! Fighting and climbing the ranks is just a game to him. His Attunement Method, his Vitality---they're on another level. This kid? He's from Misty Peak. They're dead last in the whole Sect. That tells you all you need to know. It's a joke that a second-grade Adept is one of their 'top' disciples representing them at the All Peaks Competition. My little brother is an eighth-grade Adept, and he didn't even get picked to compete for our peak! If I'd known the bar was this low, I would've just sent him over to Misty Peak!"
[AMBIENT: OBSERVERS' GALLERY, MURMURING CROWD BELOW]
Up in the observers' gallery, Sir Kenneth's gaze drifted down to the arena floor. He leaned toward Master Elwood and pointed.
"What's this? Misty Peak sent a second-tier Adept?"
Master Elwood shifted uncomfortably. The competition rosters were ranked by raw strength — no one had intervened in the selection. But Sir Kenneth's tone made it sound like a failure. "Some disciples encounter unexpected fortune," he offered. "Challenging those above your level isn't unheard of." He didn't believe his own words as he said them.
Sir Kenneth was quiet for a moment, watching the boy take his place on the arena floor. "Perhaps his strength is not bad," he said finally, and left it at that.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
The moment Zane stepped into the arena, he found himself facing a mountain of a man. The guy had to be over six and a half feet tall, with two copper hammers slung across his back. They were the size of watermelons, and each one had to weigh at least five hundred pounds.
"You're Zane, right?" the big man rumbled. "Heard you've cultivated your corporeal body. I'm Langdon. Good to meet you."
Langdon had the look of an honest, simple guy, but he'd ranked in the top hundred three years ago. He was not someone to underestimate.
Most disciples in the Skyreach Sect focused solely on cultivating their Vitality, treating physical training as an afterthought. The five steps of corporeal body training were all about purifying the body to better sync with a practitioner's Vitality.
But Langdon was impressive because he wasn't just about brute strength; he was also incredibly skilled with his Vitality.
[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]
As he lifted the massive copper hammers, a wave of Vitality pulsed out from his feet. It was a thick, yellowish-brown, and you could almost feel the crushing weight of it just by looking.
"Deadly Swamp Trap!"
His Vitality oozed across the ground, wrapping around Zane's legs in thick, heavy layers, cementing him to the spot.
Zane let out a small gasp. "Interesting." Langdon's strategy was unique, he'd give him that: trap your opponent, then crush them with the hammers. It was a clever two-step combo, but to Zane, it felt almost childish.
The Deadly Swamp Trap was a death sentence for any Adept who couldn't fly, sure. But what about a practitioner in the Soul Realm? Someone who could just... take to the air? "How does he deal with stronger opponents?" Zane wondered.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
Murmurs spread through the disciples.
"That second-grade kid looks like he's done for. Think those hammers will cripple him?"
"Man, what rotten luck, drawing Langdon. No Adept can get out of his Deadly Swamp Trap."
It wasn't about whether Zane would win or lose---most didn't care. They were wondering if he would even survive. After all, accidental deaths in the arena weren't exactly unheard of.
"Come on, Zane, you got this!" Master Warren whispered urgently. "You and Eliot are Misty Peak's only hope. Don't go down in the first round!"
Just as Langdon's two huge hammers were about to crash down on him, a small smile touched Zane's lips. "Here we go."
