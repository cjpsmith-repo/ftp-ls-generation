Ep 345 - Jailbreak

[MUSIC: TENSE UNCERTAINTY]
As Zane studied Isla, trying to figure out what troubled her, she was doing the same to him. She struggled to understand the look on his face, which varied between profound shock and unmistakable disappointment.
Zane’s demeanor seemed distinctly off—a reaction that puzzled Isla until revelation dawned on her: she had completely misread his intentions. It was her mistake, assuming that he'd thrown in his lot with Ray.
Finally, he screwed up enough courage to look her in the eyes. "I heard you were under house arrest. I came here to get you out."
He didn’t understand why she was being so cold, but ultimately it didn’t really matter. The purpose of his journey stayed clear in his mind, and he was prepared to do whatever it took to make it happen. Even if Isla fought him on it, he wouldn’t back down. That just wasn’t who he was.
She stared at him with a stunned expression of her own. "You’re here to... get me out?" A moment of silence hung between them. "Can you actually do that?"
The idea seemed impossible. Even if Zane had sworn his loyalty to Ray, the prince would hardly just hand over that kind of authority. Ray already commanded plenty of talented people who were eager to serve him.
Zane was just an Adept and could hardly be considered an essential asset.
Zane gave her a small, encouraging smile. Won’t know unless I try, right?"
[MUSIC: MOUNTING DREAD]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
At Zane’s calm, confident reply, Ray chuckled and strolled over, his silk robes whispering against the marble floor. "You know, it’s not entirely out of the question, you taking Isla with you."
"’It’s not?’" Isla mumbled, the prince’s words so shocking she could scarcely trust her own ears. A flutter of hope, dangerous and unbidden, rose in her chest despite her better judgment.
In the royal family’s deadly games, showing mercy toward one’s enemies was unheard of. The idea of Ray releasing her so easily went against everything she understood about palace politics. It violated the fundamental rules she had lived by for years.
Had Zane made it into the Top fifty? No, that wouldn’t be enough to capture Ray’s attention this dramatically. Top twenty, maybe? That might earn Ray’s respect, but hardly enough to just let her walk free.
Top ten? Top five? Top three? Her guesses grew bolder, each one sounding more ridiculous than the last. Unable to make sense of the situation, she had no choice but to ask directly. "Zane, how did you place in the All Peaks Competition?"
Zane just rubbed the back of his neck, his expression surprisingly sheepish for someone who had stood so boldly against a prince moments ago. "I, uh... I won first place, Lady Novak," he admitted softly, his voice carrying a hint of apology, as though concerned the news might somehow distress her.
"First place..."
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT REVELATION]
Isla’s voice trailed off, her mouth open in astonishment. The revelation struck her like a physical blow. She had to be the last to learn this extraordinary news—and somehow, she was also the most shocked. A strange mixture of pride, disbelief, and something dangerously close to admiration washed over her as she stared at the young man before her.
After all, she was the one who cared most about him, the one who’d been hoping against hope for him to succeed. If for no other reason than the fact that she was his mentor.
But stuck under house arrest in Radiant Hall, she’d been completely cut off from any news. All she could do was guess and worry about how he was faring in the All Peaks Competition.
And now he was just... telling her he’d won. He'd said it so quietly, but the words struck her like a bolt of lightning. The news was staggering.
Isla knew Zane wouldn’t lie to her, but it still felt impossible. She hadn’t seen the Skyreach Sect’s official announcement, hadn’t laid eyes on the final rankings, hadn’t witnessed the fight for herself.
She could feel the change in his power—he’d reached Level Three of the Adept Realm. She could sense that easily. But so what? How could a Level Three Adept have fought his way through the best disciples from all Thirty-Three Peaks? How could he have beaten students personally trained by Elders and Guardians?
Despite her disbelief, she had to accept it. It was the only way Ray’s offer made any sense.
Putting herself in Ray’s shoes, she knew she’d have made the same choice if Zane had truly won first place among all the disciples of the Skyreach Sect.
In fact, Ray’s own words were all the proof she needed.
While bored out of her mind in Radiant Hall, Isla had run the numbers, calculated the odds... she'd figured that maybe, just maybe, Zane would be able to...
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
As Ray stared him down, Zane felt a bead of sweat trace a path down his spine. It had been a long time since he’d felt pressure this intense.
"You aren’t actually going to try and kill me, are you, Ray?" Zane asked, his voice cold.
Ray shook his head, unnervingly calm. "Honestly? I wouldn’t dare.
You’re number one in the Sect now. What would I tell Sir Kenneth if you suddenly turned up dead?"
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
"Stop this nonsense," came a booming voice from the doorway. Uncle Kyle strode into the hall, his arrival instantly shifting the tension in the room.
Ray’s eyes flickered toward the door, "Perfect timing, Uncle Kyle. Our young friend here seems determined to leave with the princess. Maybe you could help me explain why that’s impossible."
"Stand down, boy," Kyle commanded, positioning himself between Zane and the exit. His short but muscular frame blocked the doorway completely. "You may have impressed everyone at Misty Peak, but you’re in the Imperial Palace now. Different rules apply here."
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
"Then why are you standing in my way?" Zane shot back. With a flick of his wrist, his sword was in front of him.
[SFX: CRYSTAL ACTIVATION]
The Phoenix Crystal on his right arm flared to life, and deep within him, every Dragon Scale ignited as he prepared to unleash his full power.
Evan hadn’t even reached the final stage of the Soul Realm, and Zane had barely managed to match his swordplay. This old man in front of him... his natural talent and foundation might not be as pure as Evan’s, but he was anything but weak. In terms of raw power, Zane knew he was completely outmatched.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Facing a master of this caliber, Zane knew he had no choice.
He had to go all out.
