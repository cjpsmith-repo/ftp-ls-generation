Ep 103 - When Music Met Power

[AMBIENT: ELEGANT RESTAURANT]
[MUSIC: SOFT CLASSICAL BACKGROUND]
 Four young attendants trailed behind the girl in the emerald dress, each bearing a different instrument—a hammered dulcimer with intricate woodwork, a polished lute with silver strings, an ancient zither that gleamed in the soft light, and a fiddle whose varnish spoke of countless performances.
"Please, my honored guests," she murmured with a graceful bow, her voice like silk against the room's hushed atmosphere, "call me Hazel." The soft-spoken performer wore her long, raven hair unbound, cascading over her bare, porcelain shoulders in gentle waves. With wide, amber eyes that sparkled with both innocence and wisdom, she resembled a pure woodland spirit stepped directly from ancient folklore. Something about her presence made the air itself seem lighter, more enchanted.
This was Zane's first time in such an establishment, and he found himself captivated not just by Hazel's beauty, but by the refined atmosphere that enveloped them all. His fingers drummed nervously against his thigh as he tried to appear at ease.
With a warm smile that crinkled the corners of his eyes, Ethan extended a welcoming hand toward her. "We'd love to hear you sing, Hazel," he said, his voice betraying his eagerness despite his attempt at casual sophistication.
[MUSIC: HAUNTING LUTE MELODY]
 Hazel acknowledged the request with a demure nod, then stepped back with the fluid grace of a dancer. She positioned a carved wooden chair at the perfect angle, settled into it with practiced ease, and accepted the lute from an attendant whose movements matched her own refined precision. Her slender fingers caressed the strings before beginning a gentle, haunting melody. When she opened her mouth to sing, her voice—sweet and clear as a nightingale's at dusk—filled the private room with such pure emotion that Zane felt his breath catch. Her lyrics spoke of lost love and distant mountains, weaving a spell that held them all transfixed.
[SFX: PLATES AND GLASSES CLINKING]
 As they listened in reverent silence, waiters glided into the room bearing steaming plates of exquisitely arranged food and crystal decanters of amber drinks. Zane exhaled softly, savoring the moment. The Golden Prairie Diner commanded premium prices, but witnessing the seamless harmony of impeccable service, sumptuous cuisine, and transcendent entertainment, he couldn't deny that every Crystal Cube spent here delivered extraordinary value. For a brief moment, the weight of his burdens lifted from his shoulders.
[SFX: DOOR OPENING ABRUPTLY]
[MUSIC: SUDDEN STOP]
 Before Hazel could finish her song, a rough voice boomed from the doorway. "Are you kidding me? You wouldn't sing for me yesterday, Hazel. Said you had a sore throat. But here you are, singing for *them*?"
A large man in a Shadow Cloak sauntered in, a few others in matching cloaks trailing behind him. They were all Varsity members of the Skyreach Sect. A waiter scurried to the large man's side, bowing and apologizing profusely.
Hazel stopped singing, her brow furrowing in disgust. "Mr. Klein," she said firmly. "I did have a sore throat yesterday. I have guests now. If you want to hear me sing, you'll have to come back another day."
"Yes, Mr. Klein, Hazel already has guests," the waiter pleaded, bowing low. "If you'd please come with me, we have other talented singers..."
[SFX: VIOLENT KICK]
 "Screw you!" Spencer Klein shouted, kicking the waiter to the ground. "Who gives a damn about her guests? I'm a guest, too, and I'm telling you, Hazel is singing for me. Now!" Spencer roared, his glare sweeping over Zane and the others.
Noticing their White Robes, he sneered. "Just a bunch of pledges." He paused, his eyes landing on Zane. "You three don't mind if she sings for me, right?"
Zane set down his cup. "Everything works on first come, first served. We invited her. Come back another day."
Klein's face darkened. He cracked his knuckles. "First come, first served? Let my fist decide that."
"Then scram," Zane said quietly.
The word landed like a slap. Klein's men couldn't believe what they'd heard. Klein himself went red.
[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT]
Almost no one saw Zane move. One moment he was in his seat. The next he was in front of Klein.
[SFX: THREE SHARP SLAPS — RAPID SUCCESSION]
Three slaps. Clean and unhurried. Klein's head snapped left, right, left. Before he could find his voice, Zane was already behind his men — a single punch to each chest. They flew backward through the railing and crashed onto the first floor below.
[SFX: WOOD SPLINTERING — BODIES HITTING FLOOR]
Klein spun around. "Brothers, tear them apart—"
He stopped. His men were gone.
Zane returned to his seat and reached for the wine. "Do you need help getting out, or can you manage yourself?"
[SFX: HEAVY KICK — BODY TUMBLING DOWN STAIRS]
Klein did not manage himself.
[AMBIENT: RESTAURANT AMBIENCE]
The room exhaled. The waiter materialized at Zane's elbow and refilled his cup without a word.
The public humiliation stung. Ethan and Rhys exchanged a dark look. They didn't want to back down, but these guys were Varsity members of their own Sect. It was infuriating, and it only made them more resentful of Zane. How had he gotten so strong, so fast? In truth, while Zane was close to reaching the Nature Level, his strength hadn't grown at the rate his friends imagined. The real difference was that Zane had converted his Energy into Corrupt Vitality, and the power gap between his old second-tier Purple Flash Fist and the fifth-tier Titan Strike was massive. But all Ethan and Rhys could feel was a bitter sting of jealousy as they watched their own progress crawl in comparison.
[AMBIENT: RESTAURANT AMBIENCE]
[MUSIC: SOFT BACKGROUND MUSIC]
 After Hazel sang a few more songs, Zane and his friends had eaten their fill and were ready to leave.
After settling the bill, Zane offered Hazel a tip of two Crystal Cubes.
But Hazel refused. "That's incredibly generous of you, sir," she said, "but I can't accept it."
Zane just gave a quiet nod. Hazel might be in a tough spot here, but he knew he couldn't be her savior.
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING STREET]
 Stepping out of the Golden Prairie Diner, the three of them knew it was time to head back. Ethan and Rhys started toward the Sect, but Zane walked off in the opposite direction. He had something to take care of before he returned.
While every other disciple was scrambling to earn Points, Zane's main goal was to rescue Elara. It gave him a drive that no one else at the Skyreach Sect could match. At the Sect, Crystal Cubes could be exchanged for Points—the going rate was two million cubes for one million Points.
 Armed with knowledge of a fresh method to acquire Crystal Cubes, Zane set off with anticipation coursing through his veins. Following directions from a local vendor, he navigated his way to The Capital's sprawling marketplace—a bustling hub of commerce that stretched across several blocks. The avenue teemed with colorful establishments: an ornate Alchemy Shop with bubbling concoctions visible through its windows, the renowned Miracle Pharmacy where cultivators haggled over rare medicinal herbs, and the glittering Treasure Room that promised artifacts of immense value.
As Zane meandered through the crowded thoroughfare, his eyes darted from storefront to storefront, absorbing the sights and calculating possibilities. Merchants called out to him, sensing the weight of his coin purse, but he moved past them with purposeful strides. 
[AMBIENT: THINNING CROWD]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]
 Then, at the far end of the market where the crowds thinned, he halted abruptly. Before him loomed an imposing structure of dark stone and gleaming metal, dwarfing the surrounding shops. A weathered sign, etched with characters that seemed to shimmer with hidden power, proclaimed simply: "The Armory." Something about the building called to him—a resonance that vibrated through his core, as if the weapons within sensed his unique abilities and beckoned him forward.
