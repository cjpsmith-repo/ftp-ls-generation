[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

As Zane entered the third cycle, he was hit with a strong dose of reality.

The pressure on the soul in the third circle was double that of the second, and a staggering four times what it was in the first.

[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]

The moment Zane crossed the line, the invisible force slammed into him, almost knocking him off his feet. It seized his body instantly, pushing and pulling at his soul, squeezing it like a wrung-out towel. The intensity was so great he could barely breathe, but he didn't fight back. A small smile even touched his lips as he completely surrendered to the crushing weight.

He'd seen this coming. He was just grateful he’d had time to prepare. The instant he entered the third circle, he braced himself and sprinted straight for the tiny Warding Lantern glowing softly in the center.

[MUSIC: FAST-PACED CHASE]

Christopher, however, didn't slow down one bit. He was a powerful and relentless opponent, sticking right to Zane’s heels no matter how fast he ran. They burst into the third circle at almost the exact same time.

[SFX: DRAMATIC STING]

But unlike Zane’s calm acceptance, pure shock flashed across Christopher’s face at the sudden spike in pressure.

[SFX: GLASS CRACKING]

The Sapphire Shield protecting his soul was no match for the force in the third circle. The protective barrier, which had been flawless just moments before, suddenly cracked.

His soul was naturally stronger than Zane's. But now that it was exposed to four times the pressure, he felt himself nearing his breaking point. He grimaced, pain twisting his features.

But forging a soul had nothing to do with a fighter's stored energy. It was a trial of will, which was why some practitioners who’d reached the later stages of the Inner-Core Realm, and even some masters in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, never managed to reach the Fighting Soul Realm.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]

"Christopher's lost his damn mind! There's no way he can catch him!"

"This is pointless. Zane is always one step ahead. Christopher's just burning through his strength. What's he even gonna do if he catches him?"

"Don't you guys get it? Back in the Lower World, Christopher's a big deal—a true prodigy. He can't afford to lose face, not after that guy already beat him once. It makes sense he's not willing to lose to him again."

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Christopher’s three companions chattered on, finding his stubbornness ridiculous. He ignored them, never letting up for a second, driven by a fierce desire to overtake Zane and kill him. He had no idea, however, that with every lap of their chase, Zane’s soul was actually growing stronger.

At first, the crushing force was unbearable the moment Zane left the protection of a Warding Lantern, forcing him to dart from one to the next. But soon, he could skip a lantern, then two, the distance between them growing with each pass.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Finally, Zane completed his first lap in the third circle and appeared right behind Christopher. It was only then that Christopher realized just how far he’d fallen behind. Humiliated, he spun on his heel and started chasing Zane in the opposite direction.

[AMBIENT: CROWD LAUGHTER AND CHEERS]

The ridiculous sight sent the watching warriors into fits of laughter and cheers. Christopher looked like a complete fool, totally played by Zane.

They continued their game of cat-and-mouse in the third circle for nearly four hours. Finally, Zane seemed to have had enough fun. 

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

He flashed a brilliant smile at his opponent before casually slipping into the fourth circle.

Here, the pressure was eight times stronger than in the first.

As Zane disappeared into the fourth circle, Christopher burned with a desperate rage. He was determined not to lose this round. His mind was consumed with getting revenge. From the sidelines, his three companions watched nervously. Sensing his reckless intent, they screamed at him.

[SFX: PANICKED SHOUTING]

"Christopher, what are you doing? You can't handle the pressure in there! Get back here!"

His Sapphire Shield, his pride and joy, had been useless against four times the pressure. How could he possibly survive a force that was eight times stronger?

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

For Zane, reaching the tiny Warding Lantern in the fourth circle was a piece of cake. But Christopher, completely ignoring his friends' warnings, charged right after him. He was totally unprepared for the disaster that awaited him. The instant he stepped into the next circle, his soul began to collapse.

[SFX: SHATTERING GLASS]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Gasps shot from his companions. Shards of Christopher’s soul scattered like broken glass, wandering aimlessly through every corner of the fourth circle as newly formed evil ghosts.

Gasps shot from his companions. Christopher's soul was disintegrating at visible speed — shards peeling away, unable to hold together under eightfold pressure. 

Then, in a last desperate measure, a faint flame flickered to life across the surface of his soul. It bought him just enough to stagger back into the third circle. 

He collapsed against the nearest Warding Lantern, face ashen. The lamp could repair a damaged soul — but not one that had been burned.

Then the Owl Beast moved. It fixed its enormous eyes on Zane, opened its beak, and let out a sharp cry.

[SFX: OTHERWORLDLY SHRIEK]

Small dark shapes materialized throughout the fourth circle — miniature versions of the creature itself, wandering and circling.

"What the hell…?" Zane muttered, frowning at the unexpected turn of events.

[AMBIENT: EERIE WHISPERS]

The fourth circle was already dangerous enough, situated near the evil spirit of the Owl Beast, with pressure eight times that of the first. He had focused all his energy on conquering that challenge. Now what was he supposed to do with all these new evil ghosts swirling around him?

The ghosts didn't dare get close, wary of the light from the Warding Lantern. As long as Zane stayed within its protective glow, he was safe.

[SFX: GHOSTLY SWOOSH]

[MUSIC: MOUNTING DANGER]

But the moment he tried to step out, the ghosts swarmed him like a tidal wave. Zane shook his head, realizing he was trapped.

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

Then, a voice boomed in Zane's mind. It was the Cobalt Dragon, who had been completely silent ever since Zane began his training. Zane got a bad feeling. The dragon only ever spoke up when things were about to get complicated.

"Zane, once you make it to the sixth circle, I will devour the evil spirit of the Owl Beast," the Cobalt Dragon said, his voice calm and direct.

"Eat the ghost of the Owl Beast?" Zane exclaimed, his jaw dropping in disbelief. "How is that even possible?"

[MUSIC: SHOCKING REVELATION]

The dragon's words were insane. The evil spirit of the Owl Beast possessed unimaginable power. It was hard enough to reach the Fighting Soul Realm, but taking on a creature whose power was beyond that? That was a whole other level of crazy.
