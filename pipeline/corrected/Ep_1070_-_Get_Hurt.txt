Ep 1070 - Get Hurt

Word count: 1064

[AMBIENT: QUIET ISLAND WIND]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Yolande had told Zane that most warriors spent tens of thousands of years on this island, just watching the stone and trying to unlock its secrets.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

But to hear that Damian himself had been studying it for twenty thousand years? That just floored him.

Willow Island was beautiful, sure. But being stuck here for twenty thousand years? That meant staring at the same barren rock day in and day out. Zane couldn’t even wrap his head around how soul-crushingly boring that must have been.

Zane turned to Landry. “And you?”

Landry let out a dry chuckle, a hint of pride in his voice. “Sixty thousand years.”

Sixty thousand years.

Zane’s breath caught in his throat.

He was barely in his twenties. And this man, Landry, had been rooted to this one spot for sixty thousand years, all to decipher the puzzle left by Supreme Lord Sword.

[MUSIC: DARK PHILOSOPHICAL THEME]

Landry seemed to expect the shock; he just chuckled softly. “Sixty thousand years is nothing, kid. There was an old-timer who was here for a hundred and thirty thousand. Finally left after hitting the Five Aging Processes. Figure he’s long dead by now.”

The two men seemed friendly enough, and Zane didn’t get any sense of malice from them. As they talked, it came out that Zane was on the island for the Passing the Torch event.

[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]

Landry’s expression turned serious. “Passing the Torch… that means the Thirteen Palaces have high hopes for you. You’ve got a long road ahead of you, kid. My advice? Don’t get fixated on that stone. It’s got this weird pull, and once it sinks its teeth into you, it’s hard to shake loose. I’d hate to see you throw your life away on it like we did.”

“If you know it’s a waste of time, then why are you still here?” Zane couldn’t help but ask.

A bitter smile twisted Damian’s lips. “Heh. After all these years we’ve poured into this place, you think we can just… walk away? Wouldn’t that mean it was all for nothing?”

“Honestly, kid, we know we’re not getting anything else out of this rock,” Landry admitted, his smile just a flicker of resignation. “But what’s the point of leaving now? We’re too old to make a name for ourselves somewhere else. Might as well see it through to the end... and die here.”

[SFX: SAND SHIFTING]

Without another word, Damian flipped and dropped into a pit in the desert floor. It was ten feet deep, the walls worn mirror-smooth from twenty thousand years of sitting — the shape of his body carved into it perfectly. Landry settled into his own depression a few feet away, eyes snapping to the rock with an intensity that looked almost like fury. In an instant, both men were gone — deep in meditation, as if Zane had never been there.

Zane hadn’t really gotten it before, but listening to them, he was starting to understand their logic. On the long road of the martial path, perseverance was everything. A true warrior, one who could endure the impossible, had to have an unshakable will.

Spirit Supreme Realm warriors like these two would be formidable figures in any Holy Land in the Upper World. Their willpower was beyond question. The problem wasn’t the perseverance — it was the direction. Walk far enough down the wrong path, and eventually there’s no turning back. They already knew they were wrong. They’d just walked too far to care.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Zane looked back up at the rock. After a few dozen breaths, the shadows came again — faint at first, barely outlines. But as his focus deepened, they solidified. After some time, they looked like real swords. Fully formed. Gleaming.

And as they became more real, the pain became more real with them. Every pass through his body felt sharper, more precise — not the dull discomfort from before, but something that made his heart clench like a fist was squeezing it.

[SFX: SHARP INTERNAL IMPACT]

This is an illusion, he told himself. My body isn’t actually injured. It didn’t help. The pain didn’t care what he called it.

All that was on his face was pure defiance. He released a sliver of his sword intent — and watched the white sword shadow swallow it whole. Then that same intent rebounded and slammed straight back into him.

[SFX: SHARP PIERCING SOUND]

He’d been wounded by his own power. A sweet taste hit the back of his throat and he spat blood.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

Though both men were deep in meditation, the exchange passed between them in an instant — Vitality carrying their thoughts like a whisper. They’d seen it all on this island. They quickly came to a conclusion: Zane was putting on a show. “He just wounded himself on purpose,” Damian thought. “What’s the point of a cheap trick like that in front of us?”

Between the two of them, they’d seen everything this rock had to offer in the way of human foolishness. People who swore they’d seen Supreme Lord Sword’s ghost and dropped to their knees to beg him as a master, addressing empty air with tears streaming down their faces. People who claimed to be surrounded by a sword array and spent years trying to slash their way out of nothing. Every variety of self-delusion had played out in front of this stone.

The one thing they couldn’t figure out was why. Why would he bother faking it for them? They were has-beens who had wasted their lives here. Zane, on the other hand, was here for Passing the Torch — one of the top prospects from the Thirteen Palaces. With a future that bright, he had no reason to fool a couple of old relics like them.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

Zane, of course, was oblivious to their suspicions. All that was on his mind was the white shadow still circling in the air.

“Again!”

[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

This time, Zane didn’t send his sword intent out naked. He wrapped it around the steel of Thundergust. He waited, patient, letting the white sword shadow drift closer, and then he slashed.

Since the white sword shadow was just an illusion, the clash was completely silent. Zane’s blade actually managed to deflect it this time, but the shadow just looped around in the air and shot straight back into his body.

Zane frowned. Blocking it was useless. He could see the white sword shadow perfectly, yet he was powerless against it.

But then he realized something surprising.

[MUSIC: REVELATION CHIME]

This time, he wasn’t hurt.
