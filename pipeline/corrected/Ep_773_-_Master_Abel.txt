Ep 773 - Master Abel

Word count: 1051

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

The six warriors were a mixed bag. Two were heavy-hitters from the Soul Sea Realm; the other four were veterans of the Life-and-Death Realm.

It seemed the folks in the Heartlands had guessed right: the union of the seven divine kingdoms saw their homeland as a complete joke. To them, the divine kingdoms were the world. Anything across the great ocean was just empty space, hardly worth a thought. Sure, a few explorers had made the trek over the centuries, but the Heartlands were notoriously hard to reach. It just wasn't worth the trouble, let alone the staggering cost of an invasion.

So, for as long as anyone could remember, the only people who bothered coming out this far were fugitives—warriors who’d pissed off someone powerful back home and had nowhere else to run.

But this time was different.

The York Divine Kingdom’s Holy Maiden had given Lord Wallace a direct order: wipe out Cloud Hall, a minor Fourth-Grade sect. At first, Wallace couldn't wrap his head around it. Why would the Holy Maiden care so much about some tiny sect tens of thousands of miles away in the Heartlands? Then he learned her real target was a young man named Zane, who’d apparently gotten on her bad side.

Honestly, if that were the only reason, Wallace never would have come. A Soul Sea Realm warrior like him didn’t have to jump just because the Holy Maiden said so.

No, he was here for the secret locked away in Zane’s mind. He’d caught a terrifying glimpse of it during that disastrous skirmish where a sliver of his own soul had been devoured. And what he saw was a temptation too great to ignore.

[MUSIC: AMBITIOUS ORCHESTRAL]

Just thinking about it sent a jolt of excitement through him. He knew he was playing the long game. If he could claim that secret for himself, his future would be limitless. One day, the entire York Divine Kingdom might be kneeling at his feet. He could hardly wait.

The journey from the divine kingdoms was brutal, to say the least. On top of the immense distance, Wallace and his men had to carefully navigate around the lairs of monstrous sea beasts. After several grueling days, the six of them finally made landfall on the eastern coast of the Heartlands.

[AMBIENT: COASTAL WIND]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON SAND]

 "Wallace, there’s a small sect over there," Master Abel, the other Soul Sea Realm warrior, pointed out. "Let's go take a look." Abel was an old friend and had only come along for the ride, invited personally by Wallace.

None of them thought this trip was the least bit dangerous. In a backwater like the Heartlands, they figured even a Life-and-Death warrior could be king. As far as they were concerned, this place was their playground.

[SFX: EARTH CRACKING]

Wallace didn't slow down. He dropped out of the sky into the Sea Wyrm Sect's main courtyard and landed with one foot — and the ground cracked apart beneath him, fractures spreading outward across a hundred acres in every direction.

Elder Marsh came storming out of the hall. Then he saw what was standing in his courtyard, and the fury on his face vanished — replaced instantly by his most polished smile.

Inside, he was screaming. Another Soul Sea Realm warrior. Since when did they grow on trees? Hundreds had already passed through heading to Oracle City, and not a word had come back about what happened. He didn't dare ask. Questions like that got you killed.

"Masters," Marsh said pleasantly. "What brings you to the Sea Wyrm Sect?"

Wallace looked straight through him. "Oracle City. Which direction?"

Marsh was already reaching into his Storage Ring. He produced a fresh map and held it out with both hands. "Marked clearly, sir. Follow the route, and you'll find it."

Wallace took it, memorized it at a glance, and dropped it. The six of them rose into the sky without another word.

Marsh watched them disappear. When they were far enough away, he spat. Dorian had at least left him a Relic. These two gave him nothing.

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT RUINS]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORCHESTRAL]

Wallace pursued a secret tied to the dragon-blooded—once a formidable race, now reduced to legend after a cataclysmic war with the seven divine kingdoms. A True Dragon had appeared on the battlefield, destroying three kingdoms before the dragon-blooded were finally exterminated in the modern era.

Despite their extinction, the dragon-blooded left behind countless ruins and inheritances. Warriors had grown rich plundering these sites for generations. Any secret connected to them remained priceless.

Wallace’s confidence was infectious. Abel found himself starting to believe him—just a little, but it was something. 

After all, it wasn't unheard of for powerful exiles to flee to the Heartlands. Most just withered away and died out here, but it was possible one of them had brought a monumental secret with them—a secret this kid Zane had stumbled upon.

In less than a day, the six streaks of light arrived at Oracle City's gates. Wallace studied the walls. The battlefield had been cleaned up, but his memory of the place from the Octogram Mirror was exact. This was the city.

"I just don't know if the kid is still here," Wallace said.

Abel glanced at him. "What's this secret really about? Don't tell me you crossed the ocean for the Holy Maiden's sake."

Wallace smiled. "Since I invited you, I'll share it with you. All I can say is — it's connected to the Dragon-blooded."

Abel went very still. "You're not joking with me."

"Why would I joke with you?" Wallace said, a confident smile spreading across his face. "Just wait. Once I catch this kid and search his soul, you'll see for yourself."

"If this kid really does have a dragon-blooded secret, he can't be a pushover," Abel said, his expression turning cautious. "You think he has backup?"

Wallace just laughed. "The kid's not bad," he admitted. "Mid-stage Spirit-Tribulation, but he somehow managed to kill a fourth-grade Life-and-Death warrior."

"Really?" Abel's eyes narrowed. The specific realms didn't mean much to a master like him, but the gap between them did. 

[SFX: TENSION STING]

A Spirit-Tribulation warrior killing someone from the Life-and-Death Realm? That was more than impressive. It was practically unheard of.
