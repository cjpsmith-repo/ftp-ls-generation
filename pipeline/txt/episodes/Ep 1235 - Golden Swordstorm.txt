Ep 1235 - Golden Swordstorm

[AMBIENT: QUIET TRAINING GROUND]
Sloane’s expression hardened. She raised her wrist again, the delicate chain bracelet dangling like a string of crystal droplets as she flexed her fingers.
“Metal Element Power.”
[SFX: MAGICAL SWORD MATERIALIZING]
[SFX: SHIMMERING ENERGY BLADE]
A faint golden light flared from the ring on her finger, slowly coalescing into the shape of a sword. The blade was a formless shimmer bound to her hand, and it flickered brilliantly, a dangerous, unpredictable weapon.
She’d made her choice.
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT WHOOSH]
A gust of golden wind swept past, and in the blink of an eye, Sloane’s figure dissolved into it, vanishing from sight.
Zane scanned the empty space around him. A split second later, he threw himself backward.
A storm of golden swords rained down from the sky with a series of sharp whistles, plunging deep into the ground where he’d just been standing. 
Every blade that struck earth left a bottomless-looking hole behind it — within moments, the open ground around him was pocked with craters, the whole battlefield turning into a wreck of torn earth.
[SFX: MULTIPLE SWORD IMPACTS]
[SFX: GROUND EXPLOSIONS]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD WITH WHISTLING PROJECTILES]
The rain of swords covered a massive area. No matter how fast he retreated, he couldn’t seem to escape the storm. There were simply too many, a relentless golden downpour he couldn’t possibly dodge forever.
[SFX: SWORD PIERCING FLESH]
One of the blades finally found its mark, slamming into his collarbone.
If it had been anyone else, the sword would have punched right through, pinning them to the ground. Instead, the blade just bit into Zane’s flesh, lodging itself in his shoulder.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]
On the city wall, the spectators couldn’t help but murmur in admiration at his incredible durability. Zane, however, was reeling. How was a single golden sword this strong? Plenty of them figured he was simply posturing, angling to squeeze more out of House Kincaid before he inevitably gave in.
Sloane had summoned this storm from just one ring on her Five-Element Finger Chain, a display of the profound mysteries of the Metal Law. If even this barrage could break through his spiritual defenses, he couldn’t imagine the power of the main sword in her hand. 
Far away, Sloane floated in the air, her cold eyes fixed on Zane’s flickering form. “If you can’t handle this, you can always retreat to the city. That’ll give you some time to think about what you really want.”
[SFX: SWORD PIERCING FLESH]
Another golden sword found him, punching clean through the side of his thigh and leaving a bleeding hole against the bone. Blood ran freely down his shoulder and leg now, giving him a thoroughly battered look — though in truth, the wounds barely registered. 
For a moment, unbidden, his thoughts drifted to Layla. Her strength hadn’t been anything to scoff at six years ago, though it was still well short of his own — and back then, she’d only just entered the Soul Sea Realm, same as him. 
He wondered whether she’d made it into the Illusion Battlefield yet, and how she’d stack up against someone like Sloane now.
“The condition—” Zane started, his eyes narrowing into a grin as his figure vanished from where he stood, tearing through space straight for her.
But the instant he arrived, Sloane merely raised a hand and gave it a light wave. The space beneath her twisted, and Zane found himself deposited right back where he’d started, as if he’d never moved at all. At her level, Time Law and Spatial Law were basic tools — the same trick that had cost him dearly against Norton worked just as well here.
“Accept your fate. Let’s have a good talk,” Sloane said, her expression unreadable. “Your body might rival a divine artifact, but it won’t hold up against my Dazzling Golden Rain forever.”
Zane ignored her. Accepting his fate now would mean giving up on the Illusion Battlefield entirely — may as well walk out the front gate. Getting close to her through brute movement clearly wasn’t going to work.
Wisps of white mist began curling around his body once more.
“Eight Smoky Melodies.”
The technique didn’t draw on Law Power at all, which meant Sloane’s Five-Element Finger Chain had nothing to grab hold of. 
The instant it took hold, his movements turned impossibly light, every gap in the sword rain suddenly wide enough to slip through.
“What movement technique is this?” Sloane demanded, watching him twist and slide through the golden storm like water finding its way downhill. She couldn’t read a shred of pattern in it. 
Even at a stalemate, the fight was a hell of a show. The other martial artists were captivated, watching Zane weave through the rain of swords. Some watched with greedy delight, hoping to decipher the secrets of his technique for themselves. But the core principle of the Eight Smoky Melodies was to adapt and exploit the situation. There was no pattern, no secret. They could watch his fluid movements all day and never see through it.
Sloane couldn’t hold back any longer. This man… she refused to believe she couldn’t handle him.
[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND TRANSFORMATION]
Focusing her thoughts, she dissolved into the golden wind once again.
“Here she comes,” Zane murmured. He’d been letting himself get battered by the Golden Swordstorm for this exact moment, waiting for her to get impatient.
The wind flashed behind him, and Sloane reappeared. 
[SFX: BLADE MATERIALIZING]
[SFX: SWORD THRUST]
The light in her palm shot out, extending into a ten-foot-long golden sword that she thrust straight for his back.
But it was like Zane had eyes in the back of his head. 
[SFX: RAPID SPINNING MOVEMENT]
His body twisted into a tight spiral, moving with the golden blade, circling it while always maintaining a precise distance of three inches.
Then, in a single fluid motion, he was right in front of her. 
[SFX: BLADE SLICING AIR]
Thundergust flashed in his hand, a streak of blue light arcing toward her face.
It was the closest they’d been since the fight began.
Sloane hadn’t expected it. He’d flown backward with such impossible control, moving so close to her sword yet always keeping that three-inch buffer between them. 
His precision was simply breathtaking.
