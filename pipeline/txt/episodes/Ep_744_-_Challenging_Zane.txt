Ep 744 - Challenging Zane
Word count: 1261
[AMBIENT: TENSE CITY ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL BUILD]
The city braced for battle.
Daily, the Runic Tower dispatched hundreds of runes, weaving glowing defensive nets across Oracle City. Warriors abandoned training—with the enemy so close, last-minute drills were pointless. Now they waited, tension thick in the air.
Spies from the World Merchants' Guild returned with consistent reports: the Corrupted Divine Kingdom marched openly toward Oracle City, making no attempt at subtlety—an arrogant display of force.
Their leader, Maddox, was so confident he could crush the city that he abandoned stealth entirely. He relished that Cloud Hall's warriors had joined the fight.
Had Tia and Zane remained in their own territory, breaking through their sect's protective enchantments would have been troublesome. Now, with both the Guild and Cloud Hall gathered in one place, he vowed to annihilate them in a single strike. Once they fell, no one in the Heartlands could oppose him. The other major sects—Mysterious Luna Hall, the Black Mountain Sect, Bloodwood Cliff—would have no choice but to surrender. Maddox intended to slaughter them all.
The reports started pouring in, each one a hammer blow to the city’s morale.
With every update, the Corrupted Divine Kingdom’s army crept closer, and the weight of their advance pressed down on the defenders.
[MUSIC: TENDER ROMANTIC THEME]
In the midst of the chaos, Tia had finally had a few days to recover. She and Zane were wrapped up in each other, their love a small pocket of warmth against the looming dread. In a quiet moment, he’d told her his two biggest dreams: to save his sister and to marry Tia in a ceremony so grand that everyone would know it. That was his promise—if he made it out of this alive.
Tia had playfully pushed him away. “I’m the leader of Cloud Hall, and you’re just a disciple. People would talk.”
But Zane never gave a damn what other people thought. He was going to marry her, even if he was still just a disciple when he did it.
[AMBIENT: WAR CAMP PREPARATIONS]
[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]
After that, they barely saw each other. Both were consumed by preparations for the battle. The elders of Cloud Hall were just relieved to have Tia back, alive and well. With the fight just around the corner, they needed their leader more than ever. Their alliance was outmatched, and her presence was the only thing holding their courage together.
[MUSIC: QUIET MORNING]
[AMBIENT: COURTYARD]
The morning of the battle, Zane stepped out of his quarters to find Cora waiting. She was in casual clothes, composed as always, but there was something careful in her eyes — a worry she was doing her best to keep off her face. Without a word, she reached up and straightened his collar, smoothed a crease from his shoulder.
"Be careful," she said quietly.
Zane nodded and left.
She stood in the courtyard and watched the direction he'd gone for a long moment. Then, very softly, as if to herself: "Don't worry. I won't let you die."
The tenderness in her eyes vanished. She turned back inside and reached into her Storage Ring, drawing out a black robe — the disciple uniform of the Ethereal Spirit Sect. She'd argued her father into giving it to her when she was fourteen. She hadn't worn it in years, and the fit showed it; her figure had changed, and the chest was uncomfortably tight. But today she had to fight. For Zane. For her family. For the Ethereal Spirit Sect that no longer existed.
She slipped out through the side door.
[MUSIC: DARK MENACING THEME]
From across the field of battle, Maddox’s eyes were locked on one person. Zane. The kid had grown so powerful, and Maddox wanted that power for himself. All he had to do was kill him, crush him, and absorb everything he was. Then, he would be unstoppable.
The Corrupted Divine Kingdom's army stretched as far as the eye could see — a black mass of independent cultivators arranged in tight square formations, Spirit-Tribulation warriors hanging in the sky above them. But among the formations, strange objects stood out against the dark ranks. More than a hundred Inner-Core warriors held aloft an enormous lampstand, its massive wick unlit at the center. Beside it sat two coffins — one black, one white. No one on the city walls knew what was inside them.
The army had come to a halt ten miles from Oracle City's gate.
On the walls, the three Guild leaders stood with grim faces. The ancient tycoon of the World Merchants' Guild sat cross-legged in his rattan chair, eyes closed. Tia stood with the Cloud Hall's Spirit-Tribulation masters behind her, watching the enemy lines.
Zane arrived in a streak of light. The moment he landed, his eyes found Tia's across the crowd. Something quiet and fierce passed between them — then he turned and fixed his gaze on Maddox.
Maddox stepped forward and poured his Vitality into his voice, sending it rolling like thunder across ten miles of open ground and into every street and courtyard of Oracle City.
"The Corrupted Divine Kingdom will unify the Heartlands!" he declared. "Heaven's will is absolute — those who follow it will prosper, and those who defy it will be destroyed. Any warrior who submits now will be welcomed. Any who resists has chosen their own end!"
The city fell silent. Behind him, his army answered with a rhythmic roar.
The Guild leader drew breath to respond. Zane spoke first.
"Bullshit."
[SFX: DARK ENERGY SURGE]
That one word pushed out on a wave of Vitality that hit just as hard as Maddox's thunderclap, reaching every corner of the city just as clearly. Simple, absolute, a complete dismissal.
“Zane! You finally found the nerve to face me?” Maddox sneered. “Last I recall, you were running for your life with your tail between your legs. Going to be a coward again today?”
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: EPIC DRUMS]
It was an old, familiar script—the leaders of two armies challenging each other to a duel before the real fighting began. Even in wars between mortals, a one-on-one fight could galvanize the winning side and shatter the loser’s morale.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
So Maddox was challenging Zane.
It was a strange choice. Maddox was a warrior of the Life-and-Death Realm, considered the strongest in the Heartlands. By all rights, he should have challenged Tia, the leader of Cloud Hall, or the ancient tycoon who commanded the World Merchants' Guild. But it was Zane he wanted. Zane he had to destroy.
[SFX: POWERFUL WHOOSH]
The ancient tycoon’s eyes slowly opened. He floated up from his chair, his voice rough and hoarse as it rolled toward the enemy lines. “The so-called number one warrior in the Heartlands, challenging a junior? You have no shame. If you’re looking for a fight, how about you and I have a little fun?”
His voice carried just as clearly as Maddox’s, reaching every corner of Oracle City.
[SFX: WIND RUSH]
A moment later, Tia also rose into the air, a cool smile on her face. “Maddox, I’m the leader of Cloud Hall,” she called out. “If you want a fight, I’m right here. Or are you afraid to challenge anyone but a disciple?”
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]
Maddox just let out a cold snort, his eyes like daggers as he ignored them both. He stared straight at Zane. "Zane," he repeated, his voice dropping to a low growl that was somehow more menacing than his earlier shout. "Do you dare?"
