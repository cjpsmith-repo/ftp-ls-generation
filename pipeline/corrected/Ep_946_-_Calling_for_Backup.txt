Ep 946 - Calling for Backup

Word count: 1156

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

“What? You want us to kill the archer?”

The two Soul Sea Realm Ogre masters stared at Lamar, confused. They’d figured he would handle Lacey, no problem. They never expected to find him on the ropes.

[SFX: ARROW WHOOSH]

Another bolt of black light shot out from behind Lacey. The arrow grazed Lamar’s cheek, carving a thumb-sized gash into his rugged face.

If that arrow had been just a couple of inches to the side, it would’ve shot straight through his head and killed him instantly.

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

“You two! What are you waiting for? Get over here and kill that kid! That's Zane Lowell!” Lamar roared, his voice seething with fury. Aldous and Reuben were just standing there like statues while he was getting torn to shreds.

“Zane Lowell?”

“That kid is Zane Lowell?”

Reuben and Aldous had already seen the power behind Zane’s arrows. With Lacey tying him up, Lamar was completely pinned down.

“Ha! So that's Zane Lowell!” Aldous laughed. “Lamar, I'll handle him for you!” He broke formation, flying right past Lacey and making a beeline for Zane.

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND BLADES]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

Seeing this, Chantel, one of the Demon Night’s Soul Sea Realm masters, didn’t wait for an order. She whipped around to intercept Aldous, unleashing tens of thousands of dark blue wind blades to block his path.

“You're with me,” Reuben said, moving to block Chantel.

The Ogres had three Soul Sea Realm masters to the Demon Night’s two. Just like that, the odds turned completely against them.

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC BATTLEFIELD]

[SFX: CLASHING WEAPONS]

Down below, far from the masters dueling in the sky, countless War Lords, War Generals, and War Fighters were locked in a bloody, life-or-death struggle across the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary.

Even so, the Demon Night’s forces were formidable. In the battles between the lower-ranking War Lords, War Generals, and War Fighters, they consistently held the advantage, pushing the Ogres back step by step.

But right now, the Demon Night soldiers on the ground had lost the will to fight. All eyes were fixed on the battle raging in the sky.

Their victories on the ground meant nothing; the real war would be decided by the masters overhead. If the Demon Night’s Soul Sea masters fell, it was all over. 

A wave of despair washed over them as they watched the three Ogre masters join the fight.

[SFX: WEAPON CLATTERING]

“Our queens… they can’t protect us anymore, can they?” a woman from the Demon Night whispered, her weapon slipping from her hand and clattering to the ground.

[AMBIENT: COMPASS WHEEL]

[MUSIC: TENSE DELIBERATION]

High above, on the Compass Wheel, the Ancestral Dragon was in a frenzy. It hopped back and forth, gurgling frantically, sensing Zane's danger through the imprint burned into its soul. It threw itself against the barrier Delroy had put around it — too small, too weak, unable to break through no matter how hard it tried.

Delroy watched the battle below and sighed. “Let's go down and help. We can't really let this kid die here.”

The young man in black snorted. “He knew we'd help. That's why he charged down so recklessly.”

Vorn smiled faintly. “In any case, he brought us here. What's so hard about dealing with a few Ogres?” He glanced sideways. “Dorothea. Young man. Go.”

Dorothea nodded and stepped off the Compass Wheel. The young man in black muttered something under his breath, then followed, his black broadsword catching the light as he dropped.

[AMBIENT: AERIAL BATTLE]

Below, Lacey bit the tip of her tongue a second time and burned more essence blood. She couldn't do it indefinitely — each burn took a long time to recover — but she had no choice. The water tornadoes surged wilder than before.

Then she noticed something strange. Zane had stopped firing.

He was just standing there. The dark golden longbow at his side, an arrow nocked but not drawn. Smiling.

Lacey didn't know him well, but she knew his reputation. He wasn't someone who gave up. So why —

Her eyes swept the horizon. And then she saw them.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ARRIVAL]

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

A man and a woman, closing fast. The man carried a black broadsword. The woman had a flower mark in her eye. Both looked strange — and both radiated something that made Lacey's breath catch.

Their strength surpassed the Soul Sea Realm. By a distance she couldn't measure.

The Demon Night were not cut off from news of the Upper Realm — their King Statue kept that channel open. Lacey had heard enough to recognize what she was sensing. These were Upper Realm warriors.

The Ogres had no such knowledge. To them, the realm above Soul Sea was a blank. They stared at the approaching pair with expressions caught between awe and terror, unable to name what they were seeing.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

For one suspended moment, both sides froze — Demon Night and Ogres alike — watching the two newcomers descend, unable to tell whose side they were on.

It was Dorothea and the young man in black.

“You can't seriously be talented enough to earn the golden celestial rank,” the young man in black scoffed at Zane.

Zane just shrugged and shot the young man a smirk. “Once I hit the Spirit Supreme Realm, I'll be able to handle ten of you, easy.”

Dorothea laughed. “You know, that's entirely possible!”

Honestly, when the young man in black and Dorothea were at the Life-and-Death Realm, neither of them could have dreamed of taking on a Soul Sea Realm warrior. But Zane could. Even among the Demi-Immortal Race, he'd be considered a prodigy.

The young man in black, however, had never liked Zane and never missed a chance to get a dig in.

The fighters from both the Demon Night and the Ogres could only stare as the trio bantered back and forth.

A jolt of excitement shot through Lacey, Chantel, and the Demon Night’s War Sages when they saw who the newcomers were with. 

Just moments ago, they had been on the verge of breaking, the tension stretched to its limit. Now, they could finally breathe. Watching Zane trade barbs with Dorothea and the young man, they knew the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary was safe.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

The Ogres, on the other hand, felt a pit of ice form in their stomachs. From the three Soul Sea Realm masters down to the War Fighters on the ground, every single one of them felt their hearts plummet.

Zane was clearly friends with these powerful warriors, and for the Ogres, that was a death sentence.

[MUSIC: TRAGIC DOOM]

For the Ogres’ two sacred places, this battle was an all-or-nothing gamble. And the arrival of Dorothea and the young man in black signaled the beginning of the end for their entire race on the main continent.
