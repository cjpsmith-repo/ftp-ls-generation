Ep 151 - The Black Wolf

[SFX: LEOPARD GROWLS AND SQUEALS]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]
Despite its speed, the Storm Panther couldn't outrun the violent tremors convulsing through the ground beneath its paws. The creature darted frantically across the fractured terrain as each powerful bound barely kept it ahead of certain doom.  
Zane watched with a mixture of awe and dread. 
[AMBIENT: CROWD WHISPERS FADING]
 The crowd’s whispers died down as they spied movement in the group of the Great Houses’ members. 
The long-haired woman stretched out a hand. Her hair flowed around her as a pale-blue orb of light pulsed from her body, vanishing as quickly as it appeared.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY PULSE]
 A moment later, that same blue light reformed around the Storm Panther, wrapping it in a protective shield.
[SFX: TREMOR HITTING MAGICAL SHIELD]
[SFX: LEOPARD FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: GOLEM FOOTSTEPS]
When the tremor hit, the shield flickered, but it held, absorbing the brunt of the shockwave. The Storm Panther was unharmed and kept running, the two golems still hot on its heels.
[SFX: APPROACHING SHOCKWAVE]
 The shockwave swept forward, barreling toward the members of the Seven Great Houses—and toward Zane.
[SFX: PANICKED CROWD SOUNDS]
[SFX: BODIES HITTING THE FLOOR]
Several members scattered frantically, seeking shelter in whatever crevices they could find. The tremor's energy pulsated with devastating force—far beyond what any Adept-level cultivator should survive.
Yet the long-haired woman, Wilson, and two other Soul Realm masters remained unmoved. They stood firm as ancient monoliths, allowing the tremor to cascade over them without concern.
[SFX: IMPACT TREMOR]
Across the square, Zane held his ground. He sensed the approaching wave before it crashed into his frame, the impact nearly toppling him backward. The sensation surprised him—not pain, but a warm current surging through his veins, penetrating deep into his core. 
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TRANSFORMATION]
His body tensed instinctively to resist, but a sudden realization made him pause. *A breakthrough.* The words echoed in his mind as clarity dawned. This was an opportunity. If he embraced this scouring energy rather than fought it, the tremor might cleanse his core completely, propelling him to the next cultivation stage. 
Heart racing with anticipation, Zane surrendered to the sensation. He welcomed the burning rush, letting it flood every channel in his body. The pain intensified, but with it came exhilaration, the feeling of boundaries stretching, of limitations dissolving.
[SFX: TREMOR PASSING, FADING]
[MUSIC: TRANSFORMATION MUSIC FADING]
When the tremor finally dissipated, Zane searched within himself, expecting transformation. His excitement crumbled into bitter disappointment when he found that his core was essentially unchanged. 
A grimace twisted his lips. The tremor had fallen short—too brief, too weak. He'd been so close; a few seconds more at that intensity would have completed the cleansing process his core desperately needed.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATION]
Not far away, the long-haired woman and the other Soul masters stared, their composure cracking. They had watched the tremor roll right over Zane’s body.
*Was he trying to get himself killed?* He hadn't budged an inch, facing it head-on just like they had.
They’d been certain he would be disintegrated.
But as the dust settled, the masked boy was still standing there. He was just…standing. Staring straight ahead.
The Adept-level practitioners hiding in the corners had missed the whole thing. They had no idea Zane had just stood fearlessly in the face of that power.
Sheer shock was written on the masters' faces. What was this kid made of? How could he withstand a force like that? He was only halfway to the Adept Realm, not even able to use Vitality for protection. So how did he just tank a hit that should have obliterated him? What a freak. If they didn't kill him now, he would become an absolute monster one day.
 Wilson blinked, watching in stunned disbelief as his own creature turned on him, completely out of his control. He tried to tell himself it didn't matter. He still had two more dire wolves. He could still kill the boy. 
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
[SFX: WOLF FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: WOLVES HOWLING]
Waving his hand, he ignited the Vitality of the remaining two wolves. They scraped their claws on the ground, let out a synchronized howl, and shot toward Zane.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
 Zane didn’t hesitate. He sent his newly turned black wolf to meet them.
[SFX: WOLF SNARLING AND SLASHING]
 Now a creature of pure Corrupt Vitality—forged from what it had consumed of Wilson’s own power—the black wolf circled the first dire wolf. It lunged, its claws slashing deep and infecting the other beast. In seconds, the dire wolf’s fur darkened as the Corrupt Vitality consumed it, turning it into another one of Zane’s puppets.
Zane could have done the same to the last dire wolf. He raised his hand, ready to unleash the Titan Strike, but then a thought stopped him cold.
*If my Corrupt Vitality can swallow them and put them under my control… what would happen if I added the power of the Phoenix Crystal?*
[SFX: CHANNELING SOUND]
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
 In an instant, he channeled the Ogres Shaking the World technique into his fist. This time, as he punched the dire wolf, he also pushed the Phoenix Crystal's energy into it.
The wolf crumpled under the force of the Titan Strike, whining as it dropped to its knees. A moment later, its fur began to change. Another black wolf now served him.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
 But this one was different. A faint, bright purple energy shimmered around it—the energy of the Phoenix Crystal.
"Let's see how fast you are," Zane thought, pushing the Phoenix-infused wolf forward.
[SFX: WHOOSHING SOUND]
[SFX: WOLF SNARL]
 The wolf exploded forward like a bolt of black lightning, its speed easily ten times greater than before.
The Storm Panther, famed as the fastest creature in the Eastern Territory, would look like it was standing still next to this.
The members of the Seven Great Houses, who had just made it to the inner gate, froze. They stared at the scene unfolding before them, the blood draining from their faces.
This was so much worse than they could have imagined.
