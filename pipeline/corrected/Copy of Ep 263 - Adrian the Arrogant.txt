Ep 263 - Adrian the Arrogant
Word count: 1207

[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT ARENA]
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE THEME]
The match between Jacob and Stanley wasn’t exactly a nail-biter. To be honest, it was a total snooze-fest. Jacob outclassed Stanley in every way, steamrolling him right from the start. There was nothing the guy could do to even defend himself.
But that didn’t mean the top fighters from the Thirty-Three Peaks and the personal disciples in the crowd were ignoring it. In fact, they were glued to every move.
Well, Jacob's every move. No one really gave a damn about Stanley.
Three years ago, Jacob had cracked the top one hundred warriors while he was still just a Level Two Adept. That feat earned him a spot on The Skyreach Sect’s List of Legends. And even though Zane had since shattered that record, nobody was dumb enough to underestimate Jacob or his power.
On top of that, Jacob had spent the last three years training in seclusion and had just hit the Soul Realm. No one had seen him fight in all that time, so his current power level was a total mystery. That’s why every top fighter and personal disciple was watching him like a hawk—he was a serious threat.
Unfortunately for them, Stanley went down so fast that Jacob barely got to show off anything but his insane armor.
Still, that suit of White Bone Armor was more than enough to get everyone talking. Its defense was on par with a common Mythic weapon, which meant it could shrug off just about any attack. Tucked away inside that thing, Jacob was practically a shoo-in to win the whole tournament. If his opponents couldn’t get through the White Bone Armor, they couldn’t touch him.
[MUSIC: STRATEGIC CONTEMPLATION]
Zane, watching from the edge of the battle ring, had his eyes locked on Jacob, too. He was already running through a few tricks he had up his sleeve to get past that White Bone Armor. It looked unbreakable, but Zane knew better.
What was really pissing him off, though, was Adrian. It wasn’t just that Adrian was gunning for him—the guy was also making moves on his sister, Elara.
[SFX: DRAMATIC TENSION]
Thinking about it, Zane's frown dissolved, replaced by a cold, hard glare. It was a look he hadn't worn once since the All Peaks Competition began.
Master Warren and Eliot, standing right beside him, both caught the shift. They’d been around Zane long enough to know he never got that icy look, no matter how tough the opponent. Zane wouldn’t back down from anyone, not even a personal disciple. This had to be about something else.
"You good, Zane?" Eliot asked, his voice low.
A bright, almost unnerving smile spread across Zane's face. "Never better," he said. "Just in a real shitty mood and feeling the need to kick someone’s ass."
"Kick whose ass?" Eliot asked, confused, glancing at the empty arena. Zane’s opponent hadn’t shown up yet.
"Yeah," Zane said, his voice flat. The cold look was back as he started walking, one deliberate step at a time, toward the center of the ring.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
Adrian had been a no-show at the competition until the moment his match was called.
[SFX: CROWD GASPS]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ENTRANCE THEME]
As the cheers died down, he materialized in the sky, descending with his hands clasped behind his back as if walking down an invisible staircase. A lotus flower bloomed under his foot with every single step, leaving a trail that made him look impossibly graceful and noble. Total show-off.
The inner disciples were floored, watching Adrian’s lotus-step descent. Most of them knew it wasn’t an impossible feat for someone in the Soul Realm, but the raw power and sheer presence he projected with each step was still breathtaking.
[SFX: FEMALE DISCIPLES SHRIEKING]
Right on cue, a collective shriek erupted from the female disciples of Maiden’s Peak. Adrian's grand entrance had sent a charge through the entire arena.
"What a tool," Tracy sneered, pouting from her seat among the personal disciples. "Who does he think he is, some kind of prince? Why does he always have to make such a big production out of everything?"
Hearing her, Patrick, who was sitting right next to her, chuckled. "So, you’re itching to go poke a few holes in him, Tracy?"
"You know it!" Tracy shot back, her pout deepening. "If I get him in the ring, I’ll do more than poke a few holes in him. He’s such a poser. I’ll make sure he can’t strike another pose for the rest of his life!"
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMURS]
Zane stood motionless in the center of the ring, silent, his gaze fixed upward as Adrian made his slow descent.
[SFX: FEET TOUCHING GROUND]
Once his feet touched the ground, Adrian greeted him with a smug smirk. "Well, hello, Zane. I have to admit, I'm surprised you clawed your way to the All Peaks Competition this fast. Quite the shock. I still remember what I told you when we met on Infernal Peak—that I'd give you three years to get strong enough to be worth my time."

Zane's expression didn't change. When he spoke, his voice was loud enough to carry across the entire arena.
"First — I don't need your leniency. You don't have the standing. Second — that look on my face isn't fear. It's disgust. My sister is locked up on Infernal Peak, and you're the one who had designs on her. That's what's putting me in a bad mood. Not you."
[AMBIENT: CROWD ERUPTS]
The arena exploded. Not because of Zane's defiance — because of the name.
"His sister? Who's his sister?"
"Elara. He said Elara."
"Wait — Zane Lowell is Elara's brother? The Elara? The one who dragged Maiden's Peak all the way to second place by herself?"
The murmur became a roar. Elara was a legend in the Skyreach Sect — the female disciple who had shattered every expectation, who had been barely fifteen when she rewrote the record books. Most disciples knew she'd been sent to Infernal Peak. Few knew why. Now they did.
Zane already knew all this, of course. Dustin had filled him in while he was investigating what had happened to his sister. In the entire Empire, no one had better intel than Dustin—the man knew everything.
Zane tuned out the murmurs from the crowd, his eyes locked on Adrian. "That stupid three-year challenge? That was all you," he shot back. "Who the hell are you to make some deal with me? You really think that after three years of training, you’d still be a match for me? I’d be so far past you that you wouldn’t even qualify to be in the same ring. But forget three years from now. Let’s talk about right now. You really think you have what it takes to win today?"
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Even Adrian, a personal disciple famous for his cool-headed smile, couldn’t keep the facade up. Zane had just called him out and utterly humiliated him in the middle of the All Peaks Competition, in front of hundreds of thousands of disciples from the Skyreach Sect. He glared at Zane, his eyes burning with enough hatred to tear him apart on the spot.
"Good," Adrian hissed, his voice practically dripping with acid. "Very good. They always say the new generation is meant to surpass the old. Just... fantastic."
He leaned in and lowered his voice. "I can’t wait to squash you like a bug, rookie scum."
