Ep 11 - Night Training
Word count: 1074
[AMBIENT: CELLAR]
[SFX: WATER POURING]
Zane poured a bucket of cold water over himself, watching as milky toxins swirled at his feet and seeped into the ground. He'd already sweated out the surface-level impurities; now that he'd reached the Bone Refining Level, the filth being purged was coming from deep within his bones.
Closing his eyes, Zane savored the feeling—the clean, sharp sensation of a body truly purified.
Darren, the steward, hadn't bothered him since their little confrontation, which meant Zane had the freedom of being unwatched. Every evening, he'd prop his iron bed against the cellar wall and wrap the frame in layers of thick cotton padding, creating a makeshift punching bag.
With the frame pressed tight against the stone, the wall absorbed most of the impact, and the cotton muffled the thud of his fists, keeping his training a secret. Down here, far from the main house, no one would ever know.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TRADITIONAL]
He began with the Purple Flash Fist, the signature style of House Lowell for hundreds of years. Now, it was practically a lost art, its true techniques known to only a select few. Even the extended Lowell family wasn't privy to its deepest secrets.
A small smile touched Zane's lips. Since reaching the Bone Refining Level, the once-daunting complexities of the Purple Flash Fist felt... manageable. He hadn't practiced in two years, so he knew it would be a climb, but with his newfound strength, he could finally deconstruct the forms and practice them on his own terms.
[SFX: SWIFT MOVEMENT]
"Shadow Fist," he whispered, striking the padded frame.
[SFX: MUFFLED IMPACTS]
Seven muffled thuds echoed in the small cell.
The Shadow Fist was the pinnacle of the style, a single punch designed to inflict multiple internal injuries. The number of impacts was the measure of mastery. Seven. Not bad.
But Zane wasn't satisfied. He remembered his father telling him how he could land eight devastating blows at the Bone Refining Level, empowered by Ultraviolet Power. Zane was at the same stage but could only manage seven. His understanding of the technique just wasn't deep enough.
"Not yet," he thought. He had to work harder.
[MUSIC: INTENSE TRAINING]
His punches reverberated against the padding with a rhythmic succession of muted thuds. Since attaining the Bone Refining Level, Zane's power and strength had flourished remarkably, transforming his once-ordinary strikes into formidable blows that could shatter stone.
But lingering doubt gnawed at him. Was this newfound prowess sufficient?
Perrin, already commanding the peak of the Bone Refining Level, delivered strikes that crashed with nearly a thousand pounds of devastating force. Running calculations through his mind, Zane estimated his own punches at seven, perhaps eight hundred pounds—impressive by ordinary standards but woefully inadequate for confronting someone of Perrin's caliber.
The realization settled in his stomach like cold lead. He clenched his jaw, frustration burning behind his eyes as he struck again, harder this time. Not enough. The gap between them remained a chasm he desperately needed to bridge, and quickly.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL STRINGS]
But his real motivation wasn't Perrin. It was getting into the Skyreach Sect.
And getting into the Sect was all about Elara.
A stone settled in his chest whenever he thought of his sister, banished to Infernal Peak. Elara, who was always so kind, so obedient. There was no way she was a troublemaker; she must have been framed to deserve such a cruel punishment. He had to get into the Skyreach Sect, and soon. It was the only way he could protect her.
The thought fueled him.
[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCH IMPACTS]
Each punch landed harder than the last.
The Skyreach Sect was the most prestigious school in the Capital, recruiting countless students every year. Every young person in the city dreamed of being chosen. Not only did the Sect have the best resources, but it also boasted the most experienced masters who could guide their development.
The requirements, however, were brutally strict. Every student had to be a prodigy, like Elara, who had been accepted with ease. It went without saying that the students of Skyreach Sect were a cut above the rest. Just reaching the Bone Refining Level wouldn't be enough for Zane to get in.
[SFX: INTENSE IMPACT]
"I'm in the refinement process now," he thought, gritting his teeth as he slammed another punch into the frame. "My body is changing every day. But if I want to get stronger fast, I need to push myself harder."
Ever since he was a boy, Zane had dreamed of mastering the style, and his dedication had shaped him into a patient and meticulous teenager who improved step by step. His father, when he was alive, had always praised his perseverance.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
But since his father's death and his exile from House Lowell, Zane had stagnated. As a slave, he rarely had a chance to practice, and the despair had crushed his spirit. But now, his confidence was back, forged anew with a clear goal. It was time to make up for lost time.
[SFX: RHYTHMIC PUNCHING]
[AMBIENT: MORNING SOUNDS]
He trained through the night, losing count of the punches he threw. By morning, the cotton padding was practically glued to the wall by the force of his blows. He had to pry it off, carefully separating the compacted fibers.
During the day, his training became even more audacious.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]
[SFX: COMBAT SOUNDS]
The guards no longer bothered dragging him to the Training Hall, so he started going on his own, volunteering himself as a punching bag for the Lowell children.
It was a huge risk. He was getting beaten more often and more severely than any other slave, yet he never showed the expected injuries.
Sooner or later, someone was bound to notice and ask questions he couldn't answer.
Dismissing his concerns, Zane embraced the necessity of these punishments. Each brutal impact served as irreplaceable conditioning—the fastest path to strengthening his body and advancing his cultivation.
The young Lowell scions observed him with puzzled expressions as they delivered their blows.
Their brows furrowed in confusion when, instead of whimpering or begging for mercy like other slaves, Zane maintained an almost serene demeanor. His eyes gleamed with quiet determination, his posture remained unwavering despite the rain of strikes against his flesh.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS UNDERTONE]
Most disconcerting of all, a subtle smile occasionally flickered across his face, as though each painful impact brought him secret satisfaction rather than agony. He carried himself with the dignified bearing of a highborn, standing before them not as a broken servant but as an equal—perhaps even as someone who held the upper hand in a game only he understood.
