Ep 619 - Another Way

[AMBIENT: TOWER HALL]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
The moment Zane set foot back on the first floor of the Tower of Sin, a hush fell over the crowd. Creatures of every race turned to stare, their expressions a mix of awe and something else. For the hulking Ogre War Fighters scattered among them, that something else was pure, unadulterated terror.
And they had every reason to be terrified. Zane wasn't even twenty, a human who’d been in the Tower for a measly three months, yet he’d single-handedly flipped the script. He’d walked in as a nobody, a simple War Fighter, and climbed the ranks to War General so fast it left everyone breathless.
He’d made a mockery of their strongest War Lords and, somehow, used the enigmatic power of the Cursed Land to get two of their revered War Sages killed. You don’t pull something like that off with just luck. Whatever he'd done, it had worked. The infamous Ogre arrogance had been shattered.
It wasn't long ago that Ogres owned this place, strutting from the first floor to the fourteenth like they were gods. As the strongest race on the continent with more seats in the Tower than anyone else, their superiority was a given. No one dared challenge them head-on; aside from the Demon Night, the other races could only swallow their anger. They were untouchable.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]
But now, things were quiet. Too quiet. From the lowest War Fighter to the mightiest War Lord, the Ogres kept their heads down, their usual boisterous shouts replaced by nervous glances. You could practically taste the fear. They were all wondering the same thing: what was Zane going to do now that he was back? Would he have them all kicked out of the Tower? Or would he just… slaughter them?
The rumors were wild. He’d somehow used the Tower’s own power to expel the two Ogre Sages, but no one knew the extent of his control. Could he wipe out every creature inside? Did he have other tricks up his sleeve? The Tower of Sin was a mystery in itself—its origins, the secrets of the Cursed Land, all of it was beyond what anyone on the continent understood.
The bottom line was, since Zane’s little coup, the Ogres were completely exposed, like fish on a chopping block. Everyone was just holding their breath, waiting to see if they’d try to fight back or beg for peace.
[MUSIC: PLAYFUL MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Zane glanced at Esther and the others. "Take the stairs," he said, a faint smile on his face. "I'll take another way."
Esther frowned. There was only one route through the lower floors of the Tower of Sin. What other way was there?
Before anyone could ask, a circular crack split open in the floor directly beneath Zane's feet—just wide enough for one person. He dropped through it without a word.
[SFX: CRACKING STONE]
[SFX: RUSHING AIR]
On the second floor, warriors scattered as the ceiling cracked open and a figure fell through, landing cleanly. Zane barely glanced at them. Another crack opened beneath him.
Then another. Third floor, fourth floor, fifth—cracks splitting open one after the other in a chain, Zane dropping straight through each one in a single vertical line. From the third floor to the ninth, warriors only caught a blur shooting downward before it was gone.
[SFX: HEAVY LANDING]
[SFX: STONE MENDING]
Two or three heartbeats after he'd left the first floor, Zane's feet hit the tenth floor with a solid thud. Above him, the cracks sealed themselves shut one by one, the Tower's walls smoothing over as though nothing had happened.
A stunned silence hung over every floor he'd passed through.
"What was that?"
"A human, I think. Just fell straight through."
"That has to be the fastest anyone's ever moved through this Tower."
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]
Meanwhile, on the tenth floor, Olivia stood at the edge of the arena, catching her breath as her final attack landed. A pulse of Lucky Light shot from her opponent’s body and flew straight into the token on her chest, sealing her victory. A wave of applause broke out around her.
[SFX: APPLAUSE]
As she scanned the crowd, she saw him. Zane was walking slowly toward the arena, raising his hands to join the clapping.
In an instant, every eye on the tenth floor swiveled to him, the applause faltering as a new kind of silence took hold.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]
Even while Zane was gone, the humans had felt the shift. It was subtle at first, but now, with him standing there, it was undeniable. The tide was turning. Any fool could see it.
Just a few weeks ago, a human in the arena was an invitation for ridicule and brutality. Opponents never showed mercy, and matches often ended with crippling injuries, if not death. But lately, their opponents fought with a strange new caution. Even when a human lost, the victor would back off, never pressing the advantage or going for the kill.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL PIANO]
The relief coursing through the human community was intoxicating. Every single one of them knew exactly who deserved their gratitude: Zane. Though they still occupied only four seats in the Tower—a modest foothold in a domain dominated by demons and monsters—it felt like merely a matter of time before that number began climbing. Their future, once entirely dependent on how much the Ogres were willing to concede out of pity or politics, now rested squarely on Zane's shoulders.
And today, after what felt like an eternity, he had finally returned.
The moment Olivia spotted Zane clapping from the sidelines, warmth flooded through her chest. A genuine smile broke across her face, real and unguarded. Her victory in the arena suddenly seemed insignificant compared to this.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON ARENA FLOOR]
Without a second thought, she hopped down from the arena platform, her muscles still humming with residual energy from the fight. The cheers of the crowd faded into background noise as she headed straight toward him, her heart beating faster with each step.
