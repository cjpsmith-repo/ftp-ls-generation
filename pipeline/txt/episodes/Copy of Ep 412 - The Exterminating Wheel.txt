Ep 412 - The Exterminating Wheel
Word count: 1077

[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]

[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]

Honestly, the last thing Zane wanted when he walked into Cloud Hall was a scene. He was still trying to get his head around everything he’d learned on the Cloud Road, and the plan was simple: focus on training, avoid drama.

But Tia clearly had other plans. That girl had played him.

The strength she’d shown on the Cloud Road was only at the Soul Realm level—the Cobalt Dragon had told him she’d wiped her slate clean to start over. Having just returned to Cloud Hall, there was no way she could have powered up that fast on her own. Zane figured some of the masters must be backing her, pulling strings to orchestrate this whole mess.

That woman is unbelievable! Is she trying to get me killed?

Then again, the damage was done. He’d already made a scene, so he had to see it through. This was all Tia’s doing—she’d blindsided him, and now there was no way out. Even if he wanted to run, the trouble had already found him.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Across the hall, Frank’s eyebrows shot up, his eyes glinting with a sharp, fierce light.

Since starting his training at Cloud School, Frank had never truly hated anyone. He cultivated the image of a charming, handsome young man with a certain untouchable presence. He stayed above the fray, dismissing the petty squabbles of his peers as beneath him.

But Zane was different. This, he couldn’t let go. Frank had always seen himself as the top dog, the most blessed of his generation. No one at his level could even touch him. The journey on the Cloud Road, however, had been a brutal blow to his ego. He never imagined that a rival this formidable would emerge from some second-rate sect. And based on Zane’s raw talent and potential, it was terrifyingly clear he was on track to surpass him.

[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]

With three swift, aggressive movements, Frank swung his spear and charged. The long weapon sliced through the air, stirring up a powerful gust of wind.

[SFX: RAPID SWORD CLASHES]

Zane immediately fell back on the defensive, his sword a blur as he worked to parry Frank’s expert spear work. He used a circular guard to deflect the rapid strikes, but the hall's tight quarters worked against him. Every one of Frank’s thrusts was dangerously close, forcing Zane to retreat again and again until a final, three-part combo backed him into a corner.

A flash of arrogance crossed Frank’s eyes. He’d already gotten a good measure of Zane’s strength during the trial on the Cloud Road. Zane was stronger than Finn, maybe even a little stronger than Patrick. But compared to him? He was still a step behind.

If Zane had already broken through to the Soul Realm, Frank wouldn’t be so confident. But unfortunately for Zane…

The kid was reckless. An Adept, daring to challenge him? It was a death wish.

And since he was asking for it, Frank was more than happy to give him a taste of true defeat.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

"Maelstrom Spear!" Frank roared.

[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

He shot into the air, his body and spear seeming to merge into a single, deadly projectile. He rocketed forward, a silver streak aimed directly at Zane, who was trapped with nowhere to run. On the Cloud Road, flying had been forbidden, forcing Frank to rely on explosive leaps from the ground. But here, in Cloud Hall, he could move freely.

Zane’s back was literally against the wall. His face tightened as he watched Frank become one with his spear, lunging at him with ferocious speed. He recognized the technique—it was the same one that had killed the Sky-Reaver. An incredibly powerful attack.

Though Zane’s body was as tough as a Spirit Weapon, he figured it was still a step below the Sky-Reaver's golden form. If the Maelstrom Spear could kill that beast, it could definitely kill him.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

One of the Cloud Hall elites muttered to his friend."Damn, it's impressive that an Adept got this far, but he's not walking away from Frank's attack." 

Marcus chimed in from the side, a smirk on his face. "Not a chance. He's dead. And that's one of Frank's trump cards, but it's not even his strongest! If he used his Spear of Annihilation, even I’d have to watch my back."

"The guy's got the talent and strength to be arrogant, but he's way too impatient," another elite commented, watching Zane with a complicated mix of respect and pity. "I bet he could take us all down once he hits the Soul Realm."

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

In the blink of an eye, the Maelstrom Spear was upon Zane. The rapidly spinning spear tip formed a churning vortex, powerful enough to grind anything it touched into dust.

[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]

Just then, Zane's eyes flashed. A deep, purple-black light ignited at the tip of his Blood Drinker sword, instantly coalescing into a dark, spinning ring.

The onlookers stared, completely baffled.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Marcus had no idea that the black ring was the Exterminating Wheel, a technique from the Blood Fiend Emperor's Malevolent Cycle of Life and Death. But even without knowing the details, Marcus could tell that Frank was in deep trouble. "That’s enough! Let go of Frank!" he shouted, his voice cold.

Zane completely ignored him. "He’s got hands, doesn't he?" Zane sneered. "If he wants to stop, all he has to do is let go of his spear."

It was true. The Exterminating Wheel had locked onto Frank’s spear, not his body. If Frank simply dropped his weapon, he’d be free.

[SFX: WEAPON DRAWN]

"I said, let him go!" Marcus repeated, his voice dropping to an icy threat as he raised his own blood-red spear.

"Right. You Drew guys are all the same. Can't win a fair one-on-one, so you jump in as a pack? Pathetic," Zane fumed. "Fine. I'll release him." With a flick of his wrist, he brought the Exterminating Wheel to a dead stop.

[SFX: CRASHING IMPACT]

[SFX: DEBRIS FALLING]

The instant the wheel's force vanished, all that coiled, forward momentum had to go somewhere. It transferred directly to Frank, launching him into the air like a cannonball. 

He smashed a massive hole in the ceiling before plummeting back to the floor with a heavy, bone-jarring thud.
