Ep 621 - The Zane Problem

The second space was dominated by a massive volcano, lava streaming down its sides into a glowing pool at its base. Each of these one hundred twenty-eight spaces formed a self-contained world—part of the Tower of Sin's original design.
Only masters from the upper world possessed the power to create such realms. Controlling one allowed you to reshape the landscape at will—split earth, flatten mountains, forge new terrain. The sole limitation: no Vitality existed here, making training impossible.
Arranged around the deep lava pool stood twenty-six black stone pillars. Atop them were the twenty-six Ogre seat holders. They had one common goal: to put an end to the Zane Problem.
Maurice stood on the first pillar, his cold eyes sweeping over the others. "Zane is back," he announced, his voice booming across the chamber. "But we still haven't decided what to do about him."
"Let's just kill him," a top-level War Lord said, his voice a low growl. "He's just a War General. We're all top-level War Lords. If we attack him together, we can crush him."
"I agree," another chimed in. "If we back down from a single War General, we'll be a laughingstock! The other races will think we're cowards."
[AMBIENT: CROWD GRUMBLES]
Malcom snorted. "Cut the crap," he snapped. "You think you're stronger than the War Sages, Colin and Neil? Zane handled both of them. What makes you so sure you can beat him?"
His words shut them down, but only for a moment.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
"So what?" one of the War Lords finally shot back, the air growing tense. "Sure, Zane can use the Tower's power to kick us out. But can he actually kill someone with it? I don't think so."
He had a point. Zane hadn't killed Colin and Neil himself; he’d just ejected them from the tower and let the strange power of the Cursed Land finish them off. But as top-level War Lords, the Cursed Land didn't affect them. Even if Zane kicked them out, they could just walk back into the first floor and start climbing all over again.
And if they all rushed him at once, it would be impossible for him to kick them all out in a single move. It was a reckless plan, but it just might work.
The questions lingered. How much control did Zane really have over the Tower of Sin? Could he kill with its power, or was he limited to just giving people the boot?
Only Zane knew the answer.
[MUSIC: LOW URGENT STRINGS]
The debate fractured into two camps. The first insisted they should marshal every last War Lord and crush Zane outright. If Zane died, the problem died with him — and neither the Ogres nor the Human race would make a scene over it. The second camp pushed back hard. Zane was hiding something. His grip on the Tower was deeper than anyone understood, and if they moved against him and failed, they wouldn't even have a seat at the table.
"I have a compromise," said a stocky War Lord near the circle's edge. "Send Twilight Group assassins—War Lord rank. Not to kill, but to probe how far his Tower control extends."
[AMBIENT: CROWD GRUMBLES]
Scorn rippled through the group. "Some compromise," someone muttered.
The War Lord didn't flinch. "If Zane can kill with the Tower's power, we're already dead. Even War Sages couldn't resist. But if he can only eject? We have nothing to fear."
Maurice shook his head. "He'll see through it. The Humans have posted guards around him. Getting a Night Clan War Lord close enough would be nearly impossible."
[SFX: LOW MURMURS]
"Then we negotiate," another War Lord said. "The Ogres haven't been able to fully control the Tower for a long time anyway. Giving the Human race a handful of seats isn't out of the question."
More voices joined in. Maurice let them speak. When the chamber finally quieted, he opened his eyes and said flatly, "We've already gotten word from our superiors. Their instruction is to negotiate. He can rule the Tower all he likes — he can't touch anything beyond the Cursed Lands. If we combined our forces, we could wipe the Purple Heart Sanctuary off the map without breaking a sweat. Let him find out the hard way."
[AMBIENT: TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DRUMS]
Back at the Tower of Sin, Zane was in no hurry to push on to the eleventh floor. Reaching the top wasn't the point. He had come to the Tower to train and to claim the inheritance of the Cursed Cave — and staying on the tenth floor was yielding more Lucky Light than he could have hoped for. There was no reason to rush.
The first few days after his return were steady. Three challengers on the first day — all Champions, all defeated. Three more on the second day, same result. Apparently, there was never any shortage of warriors convinced that where the last man failed, they would succeed.
Meanwhile, Zane was dealing with a different kind of problem. His opponent was an Orc War Lord, a lone wolf who wasn't tied to any of the Orc sanctuaries. The guy was focused solely on his own training and paid little attention to anything happening in the outside world. He’d climbed from the first to the tenth floor of the tower mostly by default, never staying on any floor for more than three days and rarely facing a real challenge.
Apparently, he had no idea who Zane was.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]
"A human War General?" the Orc sneered as he swaggered into the arena. "And a first-level one, at that. Interesting."
[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]
The challenge drew a crowd. Even Cheryl and Olivia came to the stands to watch.
But Zane just frowned at the sight of him.
That look on the human's face seriously pissed off the Orc. "What's with the long face, human boy?" he growled. "You're not satisfied with me as your opponent?"
"No, I'm not," Zane answered bluntly. "You climbed all the way up here without getting a single point of Lucky Light."
Zane had specifically stopped on the tenth floor to farm Lucky Light. There wasn't a trace of it on the Orc’s signaling token, which meant beating him would be a complete waste of time.
[MUSIC: DARK THREATENING STRINGS]
The Orc’s anger flared. Zane’s words made it clear he thought this would be an easy, worthless victory.
He let out a cold, sinister laugh. "I ate some human meat a few years ago. It was delicious," he snarled. "After I beat you, I'm going to enjoy carving you up for a stew."
