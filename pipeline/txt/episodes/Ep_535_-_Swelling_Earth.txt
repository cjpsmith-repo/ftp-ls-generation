Ep 535 - Swelling Earth

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Since Tia had undergone nine rebirths in her training, her power level was in constant flux, making it nearly impossible for anyone to gauge her true strength.
Most people had figured that even if Tia recovered her power and returned to the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, she’d only be marginally stronger than Emanuel and Saint Morphens. But now, it was clear they'd drastically underestimated her. At this rate, she could probably take on a warrior from the Life-and-Death Realm by the time she reached the late stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
After dispatching the Demon Emperor Corpse, Tia surveyed the battlefield with cold satisfaction before gesturing her group forward. The ease of their victory had sent a clear message to anyone watching.
[AMBIENT: DISTANT BATTLE SOUNDS]
Across the other eight paths, warriors faced their own trials. Maddox moved with practiced efficiency, his blade singing through the air. The six cloaked independents from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm needed no coordination—each understood their role instinctively. Together, they carved through the corpses without exchanging a single word, their movements a testament to years of solitary cultivation and hard-won experience.
The contingents from Bloodwood Cliff, the Black Mountain Order, and Mysterious Luna Hall demonstrated why their organizations had endured for centuries. Though their techniques varied wildly—poison against shadow against lunar energy—each group adapted to the relentless assault. They weathered the attacks through sheer determination and coordinated defense, emerging battered but intact from this treacherous leg of the journey.
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]
But the fastest of the nine groups were the warriors from the divine kingdom. The woman in black and the man beside her didn’t even flinch. Instead, a warrior behind them strode forward, raised a golden bow, and unleashed a volley of arrows at the corpses.
[SFX: ARROW WHOOSH]
[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]
The five arrows seemed to have minds of their own, streaking toward the five corpses with an unnatural hunger. The strange thing was, after piercing the corpses’ heads, the arrows didn't lose any momentum. They absorbed the corpses’ energy, glowed brighter, and then each one split into two.
Five arrows became ten, which shot toward the next ten corpses. After absorbing their energy, the ten arrows became twenty, then forty, eighty, and finally one hundred and sixty.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT CRESCENDO]
The storm of arrows wiped out the corpses in seconds. Then, they all merged back into a single, razor-sharp projectile that blasted toward the Demon Emperor as it finally revealed itself. The arrow punched straight through it in a flash.
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
If the warriors in the Heartlands ever saw this bow's power, they'd be left utterly speechless.
Based on its devastating capabilities, the bow represented a game-changing advantage in any war between factions. One arrow multiplied into two, two into four, four into eight—an exponential cascade of destruction. A single projectile could potentially annihilate millions of warriors in mere moments.
"I've heard legends of Gong's Transformation Bow," another guard from the Life-and-Death Realm remarked, unable to suppress the awe in his voice. "It certainly lives up to its name."
The warrior holding the bow—Gong himself—acknowledged the compliment with a faint smile. He secured the weapon with practiced ease, as though wielding such devastating power was merely routine.
The woman in black and her companion maintained their dignified silence throughout the exchange. Neither acknowledged the guards' conversation nor showed any reaction to the weapon that had just obliterated a Demon Emperor in seconds.
Their indifference spoke volumes about the world they inhabited—one where even legendary artifacts warranted little more than a passing glance.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
Back on Cloud Hall's path, the group had been walking for some time when Emanuel suddenly stopped and looked ahead. “It's an herb garden.”
Everyone looked where he was pointing. Sure enough, not far ahead, an enormous garden sprawled out in the darkness of the tomb — untended for thousands of years, overgrown and tangled, but unmistakably alive.
Tia's expression flickered with something close to hope. If the Life-Death Flower was anywhere in the tomb, a herb garden was the most likely place to find it. She moved toward it quickly.
Ahead, a single word was carved into the ground: GIFT.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL DISCOVERY]
Zane’s eyes narrowed when he saw the word. A gift? What’s that supposed to mean?
The Celestial Tomb of Vesper was seriously strange. It was supposed to be a deathtrap, but so far, the corpses hadn't been that tough. A standard Fourth-Grade sect could probably handle them. Besides, who builds a tomb like this? And who gets a horde of corpses to guard it?
But the elders of Cloud Hall weren’t dwelling on any of that.
Maybe I’m just overthinking it, Zane thought as he stepped into the herbal garden.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL GARDEN]
[SFX: GENTLE WIND CHIMES]
The second he crossed the threshold, a symphony of strange and wonderful aromas washed over him.
“Wow…” he muttered, his eyes wide as he scanned the area. The garden was packed with all kinds of exotic flowers and herbs. It looked like it hadn't been tended in thousands of years and had become a chaotic, overgrown tangle.
“This is Dragon’s Smile!” an elder gasped.
“A Dreamland Flower! It’s really a Dreamland Flower!” another cried out.
Meanwhile, Tia flew past the exotic plants, a flicker of disappointment crossing her face. She couldn’t find the legendary Flower of Life and Death.
Though these herbs were valuable, they were only good enough for crafting fifth or sixth-grade healing pills. They were beneath her notice.
Zane picked a few herbs he recognized, choosing only ones that could help stabilize his power. Just then, the Cobalt Dragon’s voice echoed in his mind.
[SFX: MAGICAL COMMUNICATION]
[MUSIC: IMPORTANT DISCOVERY]
“Zane, most of the herbs in this garden are fairly common, but there’s one thing here that is extremely valuable.”
“What is it?” Zane asked. “In a herbal garden, shouldn’t the herbs be the most valuable thing?”
The Cobalt Dragon chuckled. “In the center of the garden, there’s a handful of brown soil. That’s what you want. Take it.”
“A handful of dirt?” Zane asked in disbelief.
“Yes. It’s no ordinary dirt. It is Swelling Earth,” the Cobalt Dragon said calmly.
Zane stared at the soil. No ordinary dirt? What on earth made this stuff so special?
