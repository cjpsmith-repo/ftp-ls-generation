Zane stood his ground, unwavering. Whether facing Yates or James, retreat simply wasn't in his nature. His resolve remained unshaken, anchored in the bedrock of his own capabilities.

His body boasted defenses that would make ordinary warriors envious, yet he harbored no delusions about his opponent. A master of the Soul Realm, wielding a mythic weapon represented a threat that demanded respect.
The crackling energy of the blade hummed with ancient power, promising devastation to anything it touched. Only a reckless fool with a death wish would willingly absorb such an attack, and despite his flaws, Zane had never counted stupidity among them.

It was fast, but Zane was no slouch.

Backpedaling swiftly, Zane launched a desperate counterstrike at the approaching energy, hoping that his Titan Strike would neutralize the threat. He channeled every ounce of his power into a barrage of attacks, attempting to overwhelm the advancing blade with sheer volume.
Each strike manifested as a shadowy, dark purple fist that shot forward with deadly intent. Drawing upon the Phoenix Crystal's immense power, he unleashed an avalanche of spectral fists in a split second, the air around him vibrating with concentrated force.
To his horror, the shadowy fist glided right through the energy blade like smoke, leaving it completely untouched.
"Useless?" he whispered, disbelief etching across his face.
A knot of ice formed in the pit of his stomach, spreading outward through his veins. His attacks had absolutely no effect. This was so much worse than he'd anticipated.

James kept his longsword raised, his fingers subtly guiding its tip to steer the deadly energy chasing Zane down. He'd been about to finish the kid off, and Ingrid's interruption was giving him a window to escape.
But James wasn't worried. If a single attack like this couldn't kill Zane, then what had all his years of training been for?
At Ingrid's words, James just offered a casual smile. "He ran his mouth. Now he pays the price. I'm just teaching him a lesson."
He had no intention of letting Zane go. He might call it a lesson, but how could anyone survive being taught like that?

Seeing that pleading was getting her nowhere, Ingrid decided to play hardball. After all, House Moon was nothing compared to the might of House Solomon.

James's smile vanished. "Is this little girl really threatening me?"
Even as a noble house, House Moon was powerless against the behemoth that was House Solomon. And honestly, the threat was landing. If she brought her family's resources to bear against House Moon, his entire family would suffer.
They wouldn't even need weapons. House Moon wasn't wealthy---their main income came from a few mines south of the Capital. With one word, House Solomon could cut off that financial lifeline and plunge his family into ruin.
James weighed his options. A declining house like his had to be careful; one wrong move could trigger a collapse. But House Solomon had its own enemies. They wouldn't act rashly, and would they really listen to some young girl's plea? Besides, he was the head of his house, a master of the Soul Realm. He wasn't about to let some little girl push him around.
James erupted in laughter, his eyes glinting with dangerous amusement. "Miss Solomon, you truly entertain me. Should I genuinely cross the line, I'll march to House Solomon's doorstep and submit myself for whatever punishment they deem appropriate."

James felt the headache before the thought even finished forming. He could weather House Solomon's displeasure. But House Blackwood? One word from them and his mines went silent.
His sword hand didn't move. But something behind his eyes did.
