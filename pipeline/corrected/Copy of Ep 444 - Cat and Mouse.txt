Without warning, Christopher's blade sliced through the air, aimed directly at Zane's exposed back.

The attack came from nowhere—a betrayal so sudden and complete that Zane's mind reeled even as his body reacted. It had never occurred to him that someone would attempt murder while his soul lay vulnerable inside the black sphere. The rules of Cloud Hall meant nothing to Christopher. Now Zane faced a nightmare scenario: fighting off the crushing weight of the Owl Beast's malevolent spirit while simultaneously dodging Christopher's relentless attacks.

[SFX: SWORD SWING]

"Go to hell!" Christopher roared, lunging forward with his sword extended.

Zane became a blur of motion, weaving through the confined space of the ring. His soul screamed in protest as the Owl Beast's pressure bore down on him, but survival demanded movement. He ducked under a wide swing that would have severed his head, sidestepped a vicious thrust aimed at his heart, and used the precious seconds between attacks to dart toward the nearest Warding Lantern. The moment its light touched him, blessed relief flooded through his frayed soul.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

Christopher pursued him in relentless circles around the second ring, his face twisted with murderous intent. After several laps, something shifted in Zane's perception. 

His soul, battered and desperate, began adapting to the crushing spiritual pressure. The weight that had threatened to shatter him moments ago now felt merely oppressive. He discovered he could skip a Warding Lantern here and there, confident he could reach the next one before the accumulated damage became critical.

Each evasion bought him time. Each moment of survival sharpened his understanding of the battlefield.

[SFX: BLADE TRANSFORMATION]

[MUSIC: DARK POWER SURGE]

Frustrated that he couldn't land a single blow, Christopher’s fury boiled over. He raised the blades that had sprouted from his own hands and snarled, "Spiritual fighting skill! Wraithblades! Let's see you run from this!"

As a master of the Soul Realm's highest level, Christopher had used this very technique to obliterate the soul of an Inner-Core Realm expert. This was his killing blow.

[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Burning green sparks showered from the blades as Christopher’s shadow fell over Zane. He shot forward with impossible speed, leaving no room to escape. If those blades connected, they would shred Zane’s soul to nothing.

A spiritual fighting skill, huh? Two can play at that game.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]

Trapped by the incoming assault, Zane stopped running. He focused his will, peeling off a sliver of his own soul and forging it into a razor-sharp thorn aimed directly at Christopher.

[SFX: PSYCHIC ATTACK WHOOSH]

"Psyche Spike!"

In truth, shaping one's soul into a weapon wasn't a true spiritual fighting skill—it was a more fundamental, if cruder, application of power. But because Zane had done nothing but run, Christopher had written him off as a coward. He was so convinced of his victory that he poured every ounce of his energy into his prized Wraithblades, never expecting a counterattack.

[SFX: PIERCING IMPACT]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]

Zane's sudden assault left him no time to react. The Psyche Spike crossed the distance in an instant, piercing Christopher's soul before he could even process what he was seeing.

[SFX: ENERGY DISSIPATING]

[AMBIENT: ECHOING SCREAM]

"Aaaargh!" Christopher howled, the sound a mix of pain and shock as his Wraithblades dissolved into dust. He’d waited for the perfect moment, only to have his ultimate skill shattered.

Instead of pressing his advantage, Zane simply backed away, creating distance between them again.

[MUSIC: AGGRESSIVE DRUMS]

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

Stumbling forward, Christopher charged at Zane, his face twisted in a mask of pure rage. "You! Go to hell!" he screamed, his hand balled into a fist.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

Facing a master from the highest level of the Soul Realm, Zane remained perfectly calm. A slight, knowing smile played on his lips. "Are you sure you want to do this here?"

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Christopher’s expression faltered. His eyes darted around, finally landing on the armed guards stationed nearby. He froze, his fist still raised. Since the moment he'd arrived, he hadn't been able to get a read on the guards or the elders—an unnerving mystery. It was clear that masters from the upper world were on another level entirely. Starting a fight in front of them would be suicide.

[SFX: AUTHORITATIVE SHOUT]

One of the guards finally barked, "Hey! What do you think you're doing? There's no fighting allowed in here! Anyone who breaks that rule gets a thousand years in prison!"

Defeated, Christopher slowly lowered his fist. He jabbed a finger at Zane. "Sooner or later, I'll get you. If you know what's good for you, you'll stay out of that black ball for good." With one last glare, he retreated.

Zane met the empty threat with a dismissive smile and a simple roll of his shoulders. 

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Then, turning his back on Christopher, he leaped right back into the black sphere.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL VOID]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL RHYTHM]

The instant Zane's soul materialized in the first ring, Christopher plunged in after him from the opposite side, his spiritual form radiating fury.

Zane didn't hesitate. He pushed through to the second ring, resuming his training exactly as planned. The spiritual pressure here was heavier, more oppressive, but he welcomed it—every moment of struggle would make him stronger.

Christopher was relentless. Driven by wounded pride and seething resentment, he stalked Zane's every move through the rings, his determination to humiliate this upstart newcomer consuming him entirely.

Rather than flee in panic, Zane transformed the pursuit into an opportunity. He treated Christopher's chase as live combat training, sharpening his evasion skills under genuine pressure. His contingency plan was simple: if Christopher ever managed to corner him with that spiritual skill again, he'd simply withdraw his soul from the black sphere entirely. The arrogant fool could rage all he wanted in an empty void.

Christopher maintained his hunt with stubborn intensity, refusing to admit it was pointless. They continued their game of cat and mouse, circling through the rings again and again. Christopher's frustration mounted with each failed attempt to trap his prey, while Zane's movements grew increasingly fluid and precise.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED TEMPO]

The moment Zane's soul had fully acclimated to the crushing pressure of the second ring—his spiritual form now stable and resilient—he advanced to the third without a backward glance, leaving Christopher scrambling in his wake.

Zane chuckled to himself. He almost felt sorry for the guy.
