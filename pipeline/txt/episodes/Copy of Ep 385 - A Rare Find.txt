Outside the Cloud Road, there was only one Scrying Slate. So, whenever a leader wanted to check on a participant, the view had to be switched from one disciple to another.

Of all the disciples, Zane and Frank were the ones watched most often.

[SFX: MAGICAL DISRUPTION]

A while back, when Frank and some other disciples entered the Death Road, the Scrying Slate lost its ability to track them. Lady Sigmund was getting anxious, unable to keep an eye on her favorite disciples.

As the leader of the Luminous Sect, she knew exactly how dangerous the Death Road was. No disciple who'd ever gone in had come out alive.

"What is Frank thinking?" Lady Sigmund complained, time and again. "He's dragging Lois into a living hell!"

If she could just see what was happening on the Death Road, Lady Sigmund might have felt a little better. But with the slate dark, she was terrified for Lois.

[SFX: MAGICAL ACTIVATION]

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]

As time went on, Zane, Finn, and Lewis also entered the Death Road. This time, the Scrying Slate was able to follow them.

When Zane’s group finally met up with Frank’s, Toby and the other leaders watched Frank’s face flicker onto the Scrying Slate.

[SFX: BATTLE SOUNDS]

The sight of Frank and Lois, both safe and sound, brought Lady Sigmund a wave of relief. She eagerly watched Frank take down the Sky-Reaver, blown away by his display of strength, and immediately started singing his praises.

[MUSIC: SHOCKING REVELATION]

[SFX: DRAMATIC STING]

But then, the mysterious disciple from Cloud Hall looked directly at the Scrying Slate and gave them a strange, knowing smile. The smile caught every sect leader, Toby included, completely off guard.

How could this disciple from Cloud Hall possibly see them?

[SFX: STATIC INTERFERENCE]

The sect leaders watched, stunned, as the disciple pinched the air between two fingers. In the next instant, the image on the Scrying Slate dissolved into static.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

"He's with Emanuel Drew's son!" Lady Sigmund shouted at Toby. "If anything happens to Frank, how are you going to explain that to Emanuel?"

Toby’s patience with Lady Sigmund was wearing thin. After all, he was the one who led the disciples into this trial. A stranger had infiltrated the trial on his watch—a mistake that would make him look incompetent. He gave her a cold smile. "Explain? What is there to explain to Emanuel? That his son wasn't strong enough to handle the trial?"

"You…!" Lady Sigmund was speechless. She was outranked by Toby. Toby might not have been stronger or higher-ranked than Emanuel, but the gap was negligible. Besides, she knew she was being unreasonable; she couldn't force Toby's hand.

The reality was simple: only disciples below the Third Level of the Soul Realm could enter the trial. Everyone on the outside could only watch. And now that the Scrying Slate was blind, even watching was impossible.

[AMBIENT: EERIE WIND]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRONE]

Of course, the disciples inside the trial, Frank included, had no idea what was happening. The eight of them pushed forward with Frank in the lead. Meanwhile, Patrick was bringing Zane, Finn, and Lewis up to speed.

[SFX: SWIRLING ENERGY VORTEX]

The Death Road was filled with strange whirlpools of energy. Each vortex led to its own separate space with a different path. Sometimes they'd run into two or even three whirlpools at once. The layout was a dizzying maze.

[SFX: CREATURE ROAR]

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

After accidentally stumbling into a Sky-Reaver nest, they found themselves being chased by three of them. Two were small, and Frank managed to kill them, but the big one was on another level. The disciples knew a direct fight with the powerful Sky-Reaver would just attract other dangerous creatures. So they booked it, luring the creature to a safer spot where Frank was finally able to take it down.

"That disciple from Cloud Hall warned us that the Sky-Reaver wasn't the strongest beast on the Death Road," Patrick said, shaking his head. "If that's true, we're in for a world of hurt." The Death Road was way more intense than any of them had bargained for.

The problem was, even after passing through several whirlpools, it felt like they were in an endless loop. Sooner or later, they were bound to stumble into the territory of another powerful beast. 

[MUSIC: OMINOUS CRESCENDO]

If that happened, they could all be wiped out.

"Who is that guy from Cloud Hall?" Zane asked. "How does he know so much about the Death Road?"

Patrick shook his head. "No idea. Frank’s asked him a bunch of times, but the guy just clams up."

As they talked, Zane studied the disciple from Cloud Hall. He wasn't tall, and at a glance, he looked completely ordinary. Still, Zane felt there was more to this guy than met the eye.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Randy, a disciple from the Duskfall Sect, trailed cautiously behind the group, his eyes darting around. 

[SFX: VORTEX OPENING]

The next vortex dropped them into a new space entirely. The dim gray light was gone — here, gusts of frozen air swept through the dark without warning, cold enough to sting exposed skin.

[SFX: COLD WIND]

Lewis pulled his cloak tighter and said nothing. Frank didn't slow down.

Suddenly, Randy spotted something unusual.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[SFX: SUBTLE WIND]

A black plant, not far off, seemed to sway on its own.

[SFX: MYSTERIOUS HUM]

It gave off a faint, dark luminescence. Tucked away in a dark corner, the black plant had gone completely unnoticed by the others.

Ever since finding the Frost-Leaf Herb, Randy had started paying closer attention to the plants around them. Soon enough, he’d found another rare one.

He didn't know what it was, but everything on the Death Road was valuable. This plant had qualities he'd never seen before; he figured it had to be worth a fortune.

Randy crept toward the plant, careful not to draw any attention. He was thrilled with his luck. Frank might be strong, having killed that beast all by himself, but all he got was a single Beast Core. Meanwhile, he had already found two rare herbs. And the best part? None of the other disciples had a clue. If they made it out of the Death Road alive, he was going to be the real winner.
