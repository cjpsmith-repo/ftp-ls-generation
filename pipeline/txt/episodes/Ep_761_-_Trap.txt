Ep 761 - Trap

Word count: 1083

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC RUINS]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

 The Ethereal Spirit Sect was in utter chaos.

When the Corrupted Divine Kingdom first attacked, they didn’t just occupy the Sect; they desecrated it. The hand-carved sign bearing the name “Ethereal Spirit Sect” was torn down from the main hall and smashed to pieces. In its place, the invaders had hung a gaudy, ten-thousand-pound golden plaque that screamed “The Corrupted Divine Kingdom,” a flashy declaration that they owned the place now.

Too bad for them, it only hung there for a few months.

The Corrupted Divine Kingdom had shot into the sky like a firework, only to come crashing back down just as fast. The freelance warriors who’d flocked to their banner, dreaming of a lavish life in The Heartlands, found their ambitions turning to ash with the death of their leader. 

Maddox's son Eden had been left behind in the ruins of their short-lived empire. With him, in the grand hall, were two women with Violet-Core Physiques whose senses had been completely sealed: Elara and Faye, Owen’s girlfriend. Maddox had ordered it done, a secret technique to hold them in stasis until he found the third and final piece of his puzzle. As a side effect, the women’s power had surged, both now resting at the mid-stage of the Inner-Core Realm.

Eden sat on the massive dragon throne, but he’d never felt so powerless.

Just a few months ago, everything had been different. He’d spent his days lost in fantasies of the future, with the Corrupted Divine Kingdom as the sole power in The Heartlands. His father would be king; he would be the crown prince. They’d be untouchable. No one would dare stand against them.

Now, that dream was a pile of rubble at his feet, and he saw just how naive he’d been. Only now did he understand that Zane was the one opponent he and his father could never have hoped to defeat.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

 But what good was that realization now? It was too late. There was nowhere left to run. The few freelance warriors who remained could disappear, change their identities, and start over. He couldn’t.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

Suddenly, several streaks of light cut through the entrance of the grand hall. Zane arrived first. Owen was right behind him, more anxious than anyone.

When Eden saw Zane, something cold moved through him — an instinctive dread, the kind that couldn't be reasoned away. Zane had shattered his martial heart at the Rising Dragon Platform, and that memory had never left him. He forced the fear down and met Zane's gaze with a sneer.

"Zane. You're here."

Zane looked at him without expression, then at Elara and the other woman beside him. "Let them go," he said coldly, "and I'll leave your corpse intact."

"Let them go?" Eden's face twisted. "In your dreams."

[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLE]

 He rose from the throne. Vitality flickered in his hands, threading into thin filaments that coiled around both women's necks like garrotes. One twitch of his fingers and he could take their heads.

Owen couldn't stand it. "Stop!" he roared, and surged forward.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 Eden smiled and gently twisted his fingers. The threads tightened. Blood welled instantly at Faye's neck, a thin red line, and Owen froze mid-stride.

"Come on," Eden said softly, his eyes full of madness. "I promise I can separate her head from her body before you reach me."

Owen gritted his teeth, fists clenched so tight his knuckles went white. "What do you want?" he demanded.

"I don't want anything," Eden said. "I just want you to pay a little price."

Zane's voice cut through the hall. "Let them go. I'll spare your life."

Eden laughed — a short, ugly sound. "Just now you wanted to leave my corpse intact, and now you want to spare my life? You gave in so quickly, Zane. You disappoint me."

Zane said nothing. He was still working out Eden's angle.

"What do you want?" Zane asked.

"I'm a Heaven-grade genius," Eden said, the madness in his eyes sharpening. "If you just spare my life, how does that compensate me for everything I've lost?" He shook his head. "I don't need anything, Zane. I only want their lives."

Zane’s eyes narrowed, the pieces clicking into place. 

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 This wasn’t about escape. This was about revenge. Eden was going to kill Elara right in front of him.

Zane’s mind raced. How do I save her? How do I take him out fast enough?

He didn’t have an answer. 

He knew he was fast—he could cross the hall and kill Eden in the blink of an eye. But Eden’s vitality threads were already wrapped around the women’s necks like garrotes. The slightest twitch of his fingers, the barest hint of intent, and they would be dead. No matter how fast Zane was, Eden’s reaction would be faster.

“You look very calm,” Eden observed, his voice echoing in the cavernous hall. “But it’s an act. I can see the terror in your eyes.”

“You’re right,” Zane admitted, his own voice dangerously steady. “I’m afraid. Afraid you’ll kill my sister. But…”

“But what?” Eden leaned forward, intrigued.

“But,” Zane said, and as the word left his lips, he channeled his Forceful Energy and took a single, explosive step forward.

He’d miscalculated. Just as he’d feared, Eden was faster, more alert. Zane’s body was a blur for a split second, but Eden’s fingers had already tightened. 

[SFX: SHARP CUTTING SOUND]

 The threads bit deeper, and fresh blood welled on the necks of both women.

“Zane!” Owen yelled, lunging forward to grab his arm and halt his advance.

A cruel smirk spread across Eden’s face. “You take me for a fool, Zane?” he taunted. “Do you really?”

All Zane could do was grit his teeth and glare, his eyes burning with a helpless rage. This direct assault, this whole attack—it had just blown up in his face. If he’d known it would come to this, he would have found a way to sneak in, to get his sister out quietly. He never expected this kind of suicidal play from Eden.

But that was the trap. Eden hadn't been waiting for an army; he’d just been waiting for Zane. He’d been waiting for this exact moment, for the chance to kill Elara right in front of him.

This was Eden’s game now. And they were all just pieces on his board.
