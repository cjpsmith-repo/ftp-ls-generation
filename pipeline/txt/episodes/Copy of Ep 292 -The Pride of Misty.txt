Jacob woke up in a haze, his consciousness swimming back through murky waters of confusion. His hand instinctively shot out for his spear, fingers grasping desperately at empty air where the familiar weight should have been. It was gone.
[SFX: PANICKED MOVEMENT]
"My spear… where is it?" Jacob demanded, his voice cracking as a knot of panic tightened in his chest.
The Drake Bone Spear was more than just a weapon to him. It was practically an extension of his own body, a companion through countless battles and triumphs. He felt its absence like a phantom limb, a hollowness that left him vulnerable and incomplete. Years of training, fighting, and bonding with the artifact—all suddenly gone.
"Did you put it in your Storage Ring?" Master Jasper asked with furrowed brows, glancing methodically around the arena. He and Kenneth had already scanned the area thoroughly, but the distinctive spear had vanished without a trace.
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
"No," Jacob said automatically, though a flicker of doubt made him check his Storage Ring anyway. "Impossible," he breathed, his head snapping toward Zane with accusation burning in his eyes. "How could it just…vanish?"
Zane simply held up his hands in a ’don’t look at me’ gesture, his face a mask of practiced innocence that did nothing to quell Jacob’s growing suspicion and dread.
"Jacob, think back on your fight with Zane," Master Jasper urged. "I distinctly heard a dragon’s roar echoing through the arena. What exactly happened?" Though he had already gotten Zane’s version of the incident, the explanation seemed woefully inadequate, leaving too many questions unanswered.
Master Jasper’s piercing gaze swept across Jacob’s face, searching for any flicker of recognition or deception that might illuminate this perplexing mystery.
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR ECHO]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Jacob squeezed his eyes shut, his brow furrowing with concentration as he struggled to piece together the fragmented memories. The fight with Zane lingered at the edges of his consciousness like a half-remembered dream. That dragon’s roar—primal and ancient—had reverberated through his body, rattling his bones and searing into his very soul.
But beyond that terrifying sound, there lay only darkness. His mind grasped at shadows, finding nothing but an unsettling void where his memories should have been. The more he reached for clarity, the more the details dissolved into an impenetrable haze.
[MUSIC: TENSE UNCERTAINTY]
"I... I don’t know," Jacob stammered, shaking his head in frustration. "I remember the roar—it was deafening—and starting the fight. After that, it’s just... blank. I have no idea what happened."
A wave of relief washed over Zane. Thank God. If Jacob remembered that the roar came from him, he’d be toast. He’d already pushed his luck using Corrupt Vitality to cloud their vision—that alone was suspicious enough. Master Jasper and Sir Kenneth were sharp. They weren’t the type you could easily fool.
"The roar came from your spear, didn’t it?" Master Jasper prompted, trying to jog his memory. "Why was it so much more powerful this time? Did the spear explode? Is that what caused it?"
"That’s right," Jacob agreed, but then he hesitated, a thoughtful frown creasing his brow. He still couldn’t remember the details, but Master Jasper’s words sparked something. That last roar... it was far louder than anything his spear had ever unleashed. The shock of it had felt like a white-hot brand on his soul—a sensation he’d never forget.
[SFX: DISTANT ROAR ECHO]
But the origin of that last, earth-shattering roar remained a mystery.
Master Jasper studied Jacob for a moment. "Check your soul."
Jacob frowned — then went still. His soul felt different. Sharper. More stable. Stronger than it had any right to be after what he'd just been through.
"That's… not possible," he said quietly. The Sect had soul-nourishing medicines, but nothing that worked like this — nothing that could produce this kind of jump in an instant. Jacob didn't have an explanation. He wasn't sure he wanted one. The spear was gone. But whatever had replaced it wasn't nothing. 
[SFX: QUIET REALIZATION]
Jacob thought back on the words of Brady, the master craftsman who had forged his weapon. Brady had told him a drake's soul remnant was sealed inside the bone — but whatever had roared in that arena was something else entirely
According to him, a fragment of drake essence lingered within the ancient bone—a soul remnant dating back tens of thousands of years. The once-mighty spirit had diminished to barely a whisper of its former glory, its power nearly extinguished by the relentless passage of time.
Brady had deliberately chosen to preserve what little remained of the drake’s essence rather than destroy it completely. Now, as Jacob struggled to reconcile his fragmented memories with the inexplicable power he’d witnessed, a cold shiver ran down his spine. Whatever had roared during that fateful moment felt fundamentally different—more primal, more terrifying—than anything his spear had ever manifested before.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TRANSITION]
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN CELEBRATION]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT FANFARE]
Back on Misty Peak, jubilant celebrations erupted across the mountain even though the final results hadn’t yet been announced. The crisp autumn air buzzed with excitement as disciples gathered in boisterous clusters, their faces glowing with newfound pride.
For years, being assigned to what everyone dismissively called "the worst of the Thirty-Three Peaks" had crushed their spirits and dampened their ambitions. Many had carried this stigma like a heavy stone, enduring the pitying glances and barely concealed smirks from disciples of more prestigious peaks. And now, these same disciples stood tall, their eyes bright with vindication as they discussed their peak’s meteoric rise.
None of them had ever imagined that a single disciple could singlehandedly catapult them from the bottom straight into the elite top two. The fact that Zane had accomplished this feat while merely at the second level of the Adept Realm left even the most skeptical veterans speechless, their worldviews fundamentally shaken by what they had witnessed.
[SFX: EXCITED CHEERING]
Zane’s best friends—Dustin, Sean, and Ethan—were losing their minds. They were bursting with pride, just like everyone else on the peak, honored just to be in his orbit.
They’d always known Zane was on another level, but this was something else. When the All Peaks Competition started, they’d hoped he might crack the top hundred. But then he blew past the top hundred, the top fifty, the top ten... all the way to the top three. At this point, they weren’t just hoping—they knew he could take the whole thing.
Every match was another miracle. It felt like they were watching a legend being born in real time.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL PRIDE]
Master Warren watched his disciples celebrate, his eyes shining with a pride so fierce it almost hurt. He was overjoyed. Securing the number two spot among the Thirty-Three Peaks was more than he ever could have asked for.
"You know, in the last competition, Zane’s sister, Elara, pulled off her own miracle," one of the masters said with an admiring sigh. "Brought Maiden’s Peak all the way to number two, all by herself. Three years later, her brother’s doing the exact same thing."
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS POTENTIAL]
Master Warren shook his head. "It’s a miracle, yes," he said, his voice low and full of conviction. "But Zane... he’s on a completely different level than Elara ever was."
