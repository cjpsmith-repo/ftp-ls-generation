Ep 545 - Deals
Word count: 1206
[AMBIENT: TENSE MEETING ROOM]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
If this was Kieran, he’d weigh the risks of pissing off the divine kingdom before figuring out the best way to handle things. After all, even though the World Merchants' Guild was the most powerful force in The Heartlands, crossing the divine kingdom was just asking for a world of hurt.
If it was Burns, he’d probably just keep his mouth shut, but he sure as hell wouldn't lift a finger to help them with their Holy Maiden’s curse.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC DRUMS]
But this was Hobbes—the most hot-headed of them all.
Hobbes had never been a fan of people from the divine kingdom. They’d never bothered him directly, though, so he’d never had a reason to pick a fight.
But now, this guy was demanding his help for their Holy Maiden, acting all rude and arrogant about it. He’d even called him "Fatty." Besides his wife, no one had ever dared to call him that.
[SFX: ANGRY FOOTSTEP]
"You! I am the—" the man from the Divine Kingdom started, puffing out his chest.
[MUSIC: INTENSE CONFRONTATION]
Hobbes cut him right off. "I don't give a damn if you're the crown prince of the divine kingdom," he spat. "It makes no difference to me!"
Just a few moments ago, Zane had sarcastically called the man a "prince." Now Hobbes had taken it a step further. It was obvious neither of them cared who this stranger really was.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]
The man was practically vibrating with fury. The thing was, he really was a prince from the union of seven divine kingdoms.
[MUSIC: DIPLOMATIC TENSION]
Elder Roland jumped in. "Friend, can you lift our Holy Maiden's curse? We will, of course, make it worth your while."
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
As hot-tempered as he was, Hobbes wasn't an idiot. He had the power to undo a curse, and now he had two cursed people right in front of him. He just had to see who would make the better offer.
[SFX: SLY CHUCKLE]
"Oh, really?" Hobbes asked, a sly grin spreading across his face. "And I wonder how you're planning on thanking me."
How was Roland supposed to come up with a suitable reward on the spot? Before he could even get a word out, a calm voice cut in from the other side.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]
"When I get the chance, I'll set you up with one of my sect-protecting arrays."
It was Zane.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL WONDER]
Hobbes's eyes lit up. A Sect-Protecting Array from Cloud Hall was legendary—one had nearly killed Maddox. The World Merchants' Guild desperately wanted such power.
If not for the upcoming celestial tomb expedition, Fourth-Grade sects would already be queuing outside Cloud Hall, willing to pay any price for Zane's arrays. Even some third-grade sects were making plans to approach him.
Though less powerful than Fourth-Grade sects, certain third-grade orders possessed treasures that could move kingdoms. Sir Kenneth of the Skyreach Sect, a late Inner-Core Realm warrior, could have easily overthrown the Empire, but his loyalty to Cloud Hall prevented such ambitions.
"Deal," Hobbes said, with a broad laugh. Kieran and Burns both smiled. They trusted Zane's word. Even if "when I get the chance" was vague, they knew he'd follow through.
"Wait!" Elder Roland cut in, his face tight. "The Divine Kingdom can also provide a sect-protecting array."
Hobbes looked at him for a moment, then shrugged. "I've heard your kingdom's rune arts are something special. But compared to Zane? Not even close." He turned away. "I refuse."
With that, Hobbes raised his hand and pointed at Tia.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
A beam of white light rushed toward her. The black cloud above her head swirled, thinned, and dissolved. The curse was gone.
Tia smiled, a rare, unguarded expression. "Thank you, Alliance Leader."
Hobbes waved her off with a grin. "Thank your guy over there."
[MUSIC: BITTER STRINGS]
The faces of the Divine Kingdom's group turned dark.
The Holy Maiden was still cursed. And if the beam of light landed on her again before someone drew another dispel—she was as good as dead. Even Elder Roland, who prided himself on composure, felt the weight of that reality pressing down on him.
He thought bitterly about how they'd ended up here. The Prince and the Holy Maiden had grown up surrounded by reverence. They had no frame of reference for people who simply didn't care who they were. The Heartlands wasn't the Divine Kingdom. These people had no reason to bow.
And now, because of two young people's pride, the most important member of their group was one bad roll away from dying—and every warrior in this abyss was watching.
"Friend, building a divine kingdom isn't something that happens overnight," Elder Roland explained. "Your request is a bit… unreasonable."
[MUSIC: SHIFTING TENSION]
The beam of light kept moving. Round after round swept through the chamber.
The Divine Kingdom's luck, to everyone's surprise, wasn't terrible. They picked up a few rewards. Their punishments were survivable. But the one thing they needed—another dispel—never appeared.
Every time the light swept past the Holy Maiden, the whole chamber seemed to hold its breath. And every time it moved on without stopping on her, she exhaled—but the dread never fully left her face.
Then the beam slowed—and settled on Maddox.
[SFX: DIE ROLLING]
Three discs materialized before him. The third one glowed white.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL TENSION]
The Divine Kingdom Prince's face snapped up, a flicker of desperate hope crossing it.
Elder Roland's eyes locked onto Maddox. This time, he wasn't going to let the Prince open his mouth first.
Maddox stepped onto the white disc.
"You have an opportunity to remove one negative effect from any person."
Elder Roland moved immediately, his voice loud and deliberate—loud enough to drown out whatever the Prince was about to say. "Friend, at this moment, only our Holy Maiden carries a curse. No one else. Would you be willing to lift it? The Divine Kingdom will reward you generously—name your price, and we will do everything in our power to meet it."
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
Before Roland could finish, Simon sneered, "Fine. If that's how you want to play it, I'll just find someone else to use it on." With that, he held out his hand as if to unleash his power.
[SFX: URGENT GASP]
"Wait!" Elder Roland cried out. "Friend, I'm telling you the truth, I swear. I can't help you build a divine kingdom. But what I can offer you is the internal version of your cultivation method."
"What? The internal version?" Simon was stunned.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FLUTE]
A knowing smile touched Elder Roland's lips. "The Scripture of Five Poisons is an incredibly precious cultivation method, yes. But within our divine kingdom, we have both the external version you've practiced and an internal one. The internal version is far more refined, far more powerful. Some of the records in the external version aren't entirely accurate, but you'll find the correct techniques in the one I'm offering."
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Simon considered the offer for a long time, his expression unreadable. Finally, he said, "Swear on your warrior's spirit that you'll keep your word, and I'll lift the Holy Maiden's curse right now."
