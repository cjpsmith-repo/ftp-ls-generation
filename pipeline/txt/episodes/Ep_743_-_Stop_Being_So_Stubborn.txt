[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
The Soul Realm warriors sat in rigid rows, coiled springs of high alert.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
When Zane plummeted from the sky and landed before them, terror washed over their faces.
He had single-handedly dismantled three Spirit-Tribulation masters channeling ten thousand soldiers' combined Vitality. If those titans couldn't stop him, what chance did they have?
Darius had given them a brutal order: hold the line or die. Retreat meant execution. They were trapped between a monster and a death sentence.
As Zane dove, he saw only ants to crush.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
[SFX: VIOLENT SLASHING]
He tore through their ranks, a hurricane of violence, leaving severed limbs and blood. A gruesome red line painted the ground wherever he passed.
The warriors held their ground at first—fear of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom still a powerful leash. But Zane's rampage proved relentless. Waves of suffocating Malice poured from his body, cracking their resolve. No one wanted to die like an insect.
[SFX: SWORDS DRAWN]
[SFX: BLADE STRIKES]
Supervisors drew sabers and cut down the first who fled. But fear is contagious. A few became dozens, then hundreds.
[SFX: MASS FOOTSTEPS RUNNING]
[AMBIENT: STAMPEDE]
Tens of thousands of Soul Realm warriors stampeded in pure panic. Even Darius couldn't control them now. Seeing the chaos, the Inner-Core masters joined the exodus.
These fighters had little loyalty to begin with—they'd signed on for power and spoils. But their own lives mattered more.
With the lower ranks scattered and their Vitality gone, the six remaining Spirit-Tribulation masters had no hope of capturing Tia.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY DISSIPATING]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL STRINGS]
The Great Rune that had caged her flickered and died.
[SFX: WEAPON MATERIALIZING]
[SFX: ICE CRYSTALLIZING]
She raised a hand, and particles of snow-white light swirled around her palm, condensing into a gleaming, ice-blue halberd.
Her cold eyes found Darius immediately. He was already running.
He didn't get far.
The moment Tia moved, the air around Darius thickened. His speed faltered, then halved — not just his movement, but the rotation of his Vitality itself, sluggish and unresponsive, as though the world around him had turned to cold syrup. He looked back, and she was already there, inches away, studying him with eyes like frozen glass.
She blew on him. A single breath.
[SFX: ICE FORMING]
Frost spread across his skin in an instant, racing up his arms, across his chest, swallowing him whole. In the space of a heartbeat, Darius was a statue — a perfect human shape encased in solid ice, folding fan still raised, mouth still open mid-command.
Tia swung the halberd.
[SFX: SHATTERING IMPACT]
The ice exploded. Darius came apart in fragments, raining down across the street below.
[MUSIC: TENSE PURSUIT]
On the other side of the city, the six Spirit-Tribulation elders had scattered the moment the formation collapsed, bolting in six different directions.
Zane went after two of them before he stopped himself. He knew exactly what Tia would do the moment her fight was over. Sure enough, he looked up just in time to see her glare at him across the ruins of the city — cold, furious, and completely unsurprised to find him watching — and then she turned into a streak of white light and shot south.
He went after her at full speed.
[SFX: SONIC BOOM]
Behind them, Finn, Patrick, and Lewis were left standing in the wreckage of Wavecrest City, staring after the two disappearing lights.
"What do we do?" Lewis asked.
Finn was quiet for a moment. "Go back and report," he said. "We'd never catch them anyway. And the fall of Wavecrest City — that's the best news anyone's had since this war started."
[MUSIC: OPEN FIELD AMBIENCE]
[AMBIENT: WIND ACROSS FLAT GROUND]
Tia was fast. But Zane was faster than she expected, and no matter how she pushed, she couldn't shake him. Finally, she stopped, hovering in midair above an empty stretch of flatland at the edge of the Heartlands, and turned to face him. The halberd came up, its tip pointed directly at his chest.
"Why are you following me?" she demanded.
Zane slowed and came toward her steadily. "You're the Hall Master," he said. "You can't just go charging into enemy strongholds alone. Did you ever stop to think about Cloud Hall?"
Her jaw tightened. "What I do is none of your business."
He kept walking toward her.
"Don't come any closer," she warned.
He didn't stop.
[SFX: HALBERD STRIKE]
[SFX: BODY HITTING EARTH]
The halberd came down, and Zane hit the ground like a meteor, cratering the earth beneath him. She hadn't used her full Vitality — she didn't need to. Even a casual strike from a Life-and-Death Realm warrior carried enough force to drive any Spirit-Tribulation cultivator through solid rock. Any other man would have been broken.
Zane climbed out of the crater and walked toward her again.
She hit him again. Another crater.
He got up.
She hit him again.
[SFX: REPEATED IMPACTS]
This went on more than ten times. One after another, craters opened up across the flat ground around them, a ragged constellation of holes in the earth, each one marking the spot where Zane had been driven into the ground and gotten back up. He never changed his expression. He never hesitated. He just kept coming.
The last time she raised the halberd, she couldn't bring it down. She stood there with it hanging at her side, staring at him.
He walked past the halberd and put his arms around her.
“Stop being so stubborn,” he whispered into her hair.
Tia’s body went rigid in his embrace, but then the fury that had been consuming her just melted away. Her eyes filled with tears. She nodded against his chest and whispered back a single word: “Okay.”
They said nothing more, just held each other in the silence of the ruined city.
[AMBIENT: CITY STREETS]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Three days later, Tia finally made it back to Oracle City. News of what she and Zane had done to Wavecrest City had already ripped through the streets like a firestorm. Ever since the war began, Wavecrest had been a strategic thorn in everyone’s side—a fortress too costly to attack. Oracle City had always found excuses not to send troops, terrified that any direct assault would give Maddox the pretext he needed to force a decisive, all-out battle with the allied forces of the Cloud Hall and the World Merchants’ Guild.
At the time, the Corrupted Divine Kingdom had grown so powerful that not even the combined strength of the two great factions was a guaranteed match. Both sides were stockpiling their strength, girding themselves for the inevitable clash.
But now, with Wavecrest City in ashes, the very thing Oracle City had feared most was happening.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
Just as they’d predicted, Maddox lost his patience. Enraged, he mobilized the entire army of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom and began marching straight for Oracle City.
To the World Merchants’ Guild and the other factions, it looked like a straightforward act of retaliation. What they didn’t know was that Maddox’s true motive wasn't the city. It was Zane. He wanted to pry a certain secret from Zane and make it his own before his master, Lord Wallace, crossed the Chaotic Ocean of Stars.
Whatever the reason, the war was here. The decisive battle, one that would engulf more than half of the Heartlands, was about to begin. In Oracle City, every warrior braced for impact as the sky, day by day, grew a little darker.
