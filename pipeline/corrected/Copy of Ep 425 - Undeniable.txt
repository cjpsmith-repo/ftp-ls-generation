Ep 425 - Undeniable

Word count: 1056

[AMBIENT: QUIET WORKSHOP]

[MUSIC: ACADEMIC DISCOVERY]

Zane studied the quill in his hand, turning it slowly as the lamplight caught its dull metallic sheen.

Runic quills were a world unto themselves, and for a master, choosing the right one could make all the difference.

The quill Zane had bought from the old man’s shop was a simple piece of Hematite, a common tool for beginners. While its weight made it easy to control with his soul, Zane found it too cumbersome for fluid movements. It was designed for basic runes, where a single mistake would render the entire inscription useless.

But massive arrays were composed of hundreds, even thousands, of runes. The order-protecting wards Tia had given Cedric, for example, were estimated to contain over thirteen thousand individual sigils. Crafting arrays of that scale demanded absolute perfection. While you could sometimes replace a minor, flawed rune on the periphery, the core—a complex nexus of thousands of intersecting lines—had to be flawless. A single, microscopic error would cause the entire construct to collapse into nothing.

For this reason, a superior quill wasn't just a luxury for an expert; it was a necessity.

Cedric's own quill was carved from the tip of a Fiery Cloud Bamboo, a material that could remain soft and vibrant for a century, all while nurturing the soul force channeled through it. Both the shaft and the brush were crafted from the finest materials, giving it an ideal weight, a comfortable grip, and a perfectly stable tip.

On the open market, it would fetch at least three supreme Aether crystals.

As the Cloud Hall’s foremost rune expert, Cedric owned more than one quill, but the bamboo was his favorite. He never went anywhere without it.

Zane, however, paid no attention to the exquisite tool in his hand. His mind had already dissected the ink's composition down to its fundamental elements, and the previous night's eight repetitions had burned the meditation rune into his memory. Every line, every subtle curve, every precise angle—he knew them all inside and out.

His hand moved with quiet confidence, guided by instinct as much as knowledge. The weight of Cedric's scrutiny pressed against him, but Zane pushed the awareness aside. There was only the paper, the ink, and the pattern waiting to emerge.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

A beam of green light suddenly blazed to life, flowing from the quill's tip and etching a flawless line onto the pristine paper. The glow was pure, unwavering—the unmistakable signature of perfect technique.

"Incredible..." Cedric breathed, his voice tight with shock. "Truly incredible."

The master rune-crafter stood frozen, his earlier arrogance evaporating like morning mist. That green radiance—so pure, so controlled—spoke of a mastery that shouldn't exist in someone so young. His throat worked soundlessly as he struggled to process what his eyes were witnessing.

The old shopkeeper’s expression sharpened instantly. As a member of the World Merchants’ Guild, his instincts were honed to a razor’s edge, and they were screaming that Zane was a priceless asset.

His future could rival Arnold’s, the old man thought, his mind racing. The Guild must remain on good terms with him, no matter the cost! I have to report this to headquarters.

The flawless line stretched slowly across the page, the energy flowing from the ink a clear testament to perfect brushwork. A moment later, the tip lifted from the paper.

“So, this quill is mine now, isn't it?” Zane asked, a slight smile playing on his lips.

Cedric just nodded, his gaze fixed on Zane with the stunned reverence of a man who had just unearthed a legendary treasure.

In that instant, he finally understood Tia’s conviction. She was right. Zane was an anomaly, an investment worth making at any cost.

As the top rune expert, Cedric had heard all the whispers circulating among the elders. They all knew Tia was the one who pushed Zane to challenge the Elite House disciples. And since she was so young, many secretly condemned her for it, calling her reckless. They saw it as her using the entire Elite House as Zane’s personal training dummies. Was any one disciple, no matter how talented, really worth that much?

Tia had a notoriously short temper, so the elders kept their complaints behind her back. Besides, even though her Cultivation Method—the Goddess Path—kept her in the Soul Realm, she was protected by one of the most powerful figures in the entire Cloud Hall.

Before today, Cedric had privately agreed with them. He’d even been reluctant when Tia gave him his task. Why should he be the one to crush a young man’s spirit for runecrafting? It felt like a petty, selfish whim on her part, pouring resources into Zane as if the Cloud Hall was her private treasury.

But now, Cedric was a true believer.

Tia hadn’t been reckless at all. In fact, dedicating the Cloud Hall’s resources to training Zane now seemed like the most logical move in the world. Using the Elite House and that one disciple, Saint Morphen, suddenly didn't seem like nearly enough.

Zane grinned, happily tucking the precious quill away.

[AMBIENT: ICE PALACE]

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Meanwhile, a Message Rune streaked through the air and into an ice palace, burning to ash near Tia’s ear.

Tia’s brow furrowed. Everyone in the Cloud Hall knew this was her sacred training time. No one would dare interrupt her unless it was an absolute emergency.

But as the rune’s message faded into her mind, her eyes shot open. The frost within them seemed to melt away, absorbed back into the depths of her pupils.

[SFX: ICE CRYSTALLIZATION]

With a slight wave of her hand, the freezing fog that permeated the palace scattered, crystallizing into fine shards of ice across the floor.

“Please come in,” Tia said, her voice cold as ever, though her expression was laced with confusion.

What on earth could Cedric want? she wondered. He knows I’m in seclusion. Why would he send a message demanding a meeting now?

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Cedric entered just as the thought crossed her mind. I just asked him to temper Zane’s ambitions in runecrafting. Did something go wrong?

Once she entered her training state, Tia paid no mind to the Scrying Slate. 

She had no idea of the miracle that took place in the old man’s shop.
