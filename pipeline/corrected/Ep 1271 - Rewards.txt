Ep 1271 - Rewards

Word count: 1094

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: CRUMBLING STONE HALL]
[SFX: STONE CRACKING AND SHATTERING]
The ghastly red face carved into the giant stone began to splinter, the cracks spreading like a spiderweb across its surface. 
At their core, the huge blocks were just ordinary stone, but a powerful Law of Causality had protected them, making them impossible to break. It was why the walls of the main cities could stand through even the most intense battles between warriors. Not anymore, it seemed.
“You scared me to death,” the ogre warrior grumbled, though his voice was weak with relief. 
Zane had just loosed an arrow that had missed the guy’s head by a hair.
“Sorry about that,” Zane said with an easy smile. In the middle of all that chaos, he hadn’t exactly been aiming—he’d just been shooting.
The ogre let out a booming laugh. “It’s cool. At least we can finally get out of this damn hall.” No harm, no foul. Even if the arrow had hit, it wouldn’t have been a kill shot. Zane’s bow was a low-grade Divine Weapon from the Lower World, and it was no match for the ogre’s armor.
Turning to one of the hulking stone blocks that now stood inert beside him, the ogre gave it a casual pat. 
[SFX: STONE EXPLODING INTO DUST]
The stone didn’t just crack—it exploded into a cloud of dust and rubble. Just a little while ago, these things had been chasing them all over the hall. Now, the tables had turned, and smashing them to bits felt incredibly satisfying.
“That was amazing,” Janet said, flashing Zane a brilliant smile.
Zane just shrugged and pulled a small skull from his back. “I just got lucky. Never thought this little thing would actually come in handy.” He’d been carrying the indestructible little nuisance around, and now, of all times, it had proven to be the key.
He thought back to the first hall, where his screw-up had almost gotten several warriors killed. A lot of them were still giving him the side-eye for it. But here, in the second hall, his random shot had been the solution. He’d gone from being dead weight to an accidental hero, and he could feel the fragile bonds of their temporary team starting to solidify.
[SFX: MASSIVE STONE BLOCKS SHATTERING]
One by one, the forty-odd giant stone blocks were reduced to rubble.
[AMBIENT: EERIE MAGICAL HUM]
[SFX: DUST SETTLING]
When the dust settled, everyone’s eyes were drawn to the center of the hall, where two glowing masses of Illusion Points hovered—the last remnants of the two warriors who’d been vaporized by the beams of light.
Because neither of the two had been killed by another cultivator, the points belonged to no one—up for grabs to whoever claimed them. Between the two masses, they added up to a few million. Not a small number, and everyone knew it.
“Zane’s the only one who deserves the extra share,” Janet said, her tone leaving no room for argument.
The short-sword warrior nodded. “Agreed. The rest of us split what’s left evenly.”
No one could really argue the point. Zane was the reason they’d gotten through the second hall at all. An extra share wasn’t excessive—it was earned.
[SFX: LIGHT MASS ABSORBING]
Zane didn’t argue either. With an easy smile, he walked to one of the glowing masses and drew it all into himself in one long pull—a nice little bonus on top of everything else.
The second mass, everyone took in turn, several hundred thousand points at a time, in and out fast. 
Now it was time for the next challenge.
[MUSIC: MAGICAL JOURNEY]
With both masses absorbed, the group made for the far end of the hall. The white light barriers that had blocked their path were gone now—but a massive stone block sat squarely in front of the exit.
“Why’s there another one of these things?” someone muttered.
“How’d it even roll over here?” said another.
The ogre warrior cracked his knuckles. “Let me handle it.”
He strode up to the block, planted his feet, and drove both palms into the stone with half his strength behind it.
[SFX: DULL IMPACT]
Nothing. The block didn’t so much as shudder.
Everyone frowned. By all rights, the Law protecting these blocks should already have been broken—every other stone in the hall had gone down like it was made of glass. This one hadn’t budged.
“What the hell...” the ogre said, baffled. “I can’t move this thing at all.”
[SFX: LIGHT SEEPING]
Then the surface of the block began to seep pale yellow light—dozens of small points drifting loose and flowing straight into him.
“Wait—points? It’s giving off points!” The ogre’s eyes went wide. In seconds, he’d pulled in nearly four hundred thousand of them.
Janet studied the block, unreadable. “This is the reward for the second hall, I’d guess. Anyone can claim it. But it looks like the payout scales with how much power you put behind the technique.”
The ogre’s face fell. He’d barely tried—half strength, no technique behind it at all—and still walked away with that much. Furious with himself, he clenched his fists and slammed the block again, full force this time.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACT — NO EFFECT]
Nothing. Not a single point.
He’d already claimed his share. There wasn’t a second helping. He backed off, sulking, while the rest of the group threw him halfhearted looks of sympathy. Nobody needed to say what he’d missed out on.
[MUSIC: ANTICIPATORY TENSION]
The woman from the Demon Night clan stepped up to the reward rock. Everyone knew the drill: you had to hit it with your most powerful attack. To get the prize inside, you had to give it everything you had.
She stood there for a moment, sizing it up. 
Then, after taking two steps back, she pulled out a fiery-red longbow.
*Is that a Divine Weapon, too?* Zane wondered. He hadn’t paid much attention to her until now. She was quiet, always keeping to herself. If she also had a Divine Weapon, her rank among her people had to be high—maybe even higher than Aurora’s.
The woman from the Demon Night clan expertly gripped the bow, drew back the string, and took aim. Zane noticed she hadn’t nocked an arrow.
[MUSIC: RISING MAGICAL POWER]
[SFX: FIRE VORTEX SWIRLING]
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
But then, the fiery-red longbow began to glow, humming with power. A vortex of fire swirled into existence at the center of the bow, condensing and sharpening until it formed a single, crimson flame arrow.
“The Moonburst Flamebow,” Janet murmured to Zane, her voice low. “It’s ranked just under two thousand one hundred on the Cosmic Spirit Index.”
[SFX: BOWSTRING RELEASE]
[SFX: FLAME ARROW WHOOSH]
Just then, the woman from the Demon Night clan released the string, and the flame arrow shot across the hall, flying straight for the heart of the reward rock.
