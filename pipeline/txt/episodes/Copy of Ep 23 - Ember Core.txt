Suddenly, Zane felt nothing at all.
The complete absence of sensation disoriented him so profoundly that his eyes flew open in alarm. Glancing down, he discovered his clothes had vanished, transformed into fine ash that swirled around him in the inferno, leaving him completely exposed. Yet his body remained utterly pristine—not a single hair singed, not the slightest mark on his pale skin.
Bewilderment washed over him as he examined his unblemished flesh amid the raging flames. "Is it because I've ceased fearing the fire?" he murmured to himself, his voice barely audible above the roar of the furnace.
A wild, desperate thought ignited in his mind—a longshot theory, certainly, but what remained to lose when death should have already claimed him? With determined concentration, Zane attempted to channel that same pure ecstasy he'd experienced once before, that singular moment of transcendence in the cellar when everything had changed. The memory of that sensation tingled at the edges of his consciousness, tantalizing yet elusive.
He couldn't prove the connection, but intuition whispered that these phenomena shared a common source. Deep within his core, certainty bloomed—something fundamental had transformed inside him that fateful day, something beyond ordinary understanding, something that might now be his salvation in this crucible of flame.
[SFX: ROARING FURNACE]
As the furnace roared, growing hotter by the second, Zane wondered... "What if I'm some kind of weapon? Is that why the fire can't burn me?"
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE BLAST]
Just then, blasts of Sunfire shot through the air, forming massive, invisible hands that slammed into him.
[SFX: CRACKLING FLAMES]
At the same time, tendrils of flame coiled around the Hellfire Blade lying on a nearby table.
The hotter the fire grew, the warmer Zane felt, but strangely, the intense heat was little more than a mild annoyance.
[SFX: METAL HEATING]
[SFX: MAGICAL PULSES]
The Hellfire Blade, however, was a different story. The blade and hilt began to glow a fierce, cherry red, and the Light-Dark Runes carved into the steel pulsed with a strange light, as if they were drinking in the fire's energy.
"So that's how it works," Zane thought, his fear forgotten. "You have to control the heat, get the metal red-hot, and then infuse that energy right into the blade. It's... fascinating." He watched, detached, as the flames wrapped themselves around the Hellfire Blade.
[AMBIENT: ROARING FURNACE]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]
The spectacle before him bordered on the surreal. Standing amidst the roaring furnace, flames dancing around his naked form, Zane found himself not terrified but utterly captivated by the intricate forging process unfolding before his eyes. The interplay of heat and metal held him spellbound, his impending death momentarily forgotten in the face of such primal craftsmanship.
[AMBIENT: ROARING KILN FIRE]
Beyond the massive kiln's walls, Evil Jace's expression darkened into a perplexed frown, his weathered face creasing with confusion.
Something was terribly wrong with the process.
By this point in his well-practiced ritual, the Hellfire Blade should have been glowing an angry red-hot, its metal crying out to be hammered into new perfection, while the sacrifice—the boy—should have been reduced to nothing but scattered ash and lingering screams. The predictable order of his craft had been disrupted.
[SFX: CRACKLING FLAMES]
When he peered through the viewing port, disbelief widened his eyes. There stood Zane, completely untouched by the inferno, his pale skin reflecting the golden flames rather than succumbing to them. How could this mere stripling still draw breath?
He appeared human in every aspect, yet he emanated an aura that Jace had encountered only in the rarest of materials—the strange, compelling energy of a mysterious weapon waiting to be discovered. Perhaps the boy's body possessed resilience beyond mortal flesh, something akin to the legendary materials of ancient lore.
A slow, wicked grin spread across Evil Jace's face. He lived for this kind of anomaly. He'd always bucked tradition, preferring twisted methods over standard techniques.
The stranger the material, the greater the challenge, the more he loved it.
He'd forged his precious Hellfire Blade by mixing hard and soft raw elements—a practice Master Weaponsmiths considered insane, a guaranteed failure. But Jace had defied all the rules to forge his A-Grade weapon, and his gamble had paid off spectacularly.
And now this. The boy possessed some bizarre power that thrilled him to his core. His Second-Grade Sunfire was ten times hotter than any normal Wildfire, yet the kid was just... standing in it.
[MUSIC: DARK EXCITEMENT]
Evil Jace was beyond thrilled; he was ecstatic.
Watching the flames lick at the boy's unburnt skin, Jace felt a familiar mix of exhilaration and frustration. Either Zane's body was some kind of priceless, fire-resistant treasure, or his own power was failing him. So, what now?
[SFX: SMALL OBJECT BEING WITHDRAWN]
[SFX: MAGICAL MOVEMENT]
After a moment of thought, a decisive look crossed Jace's face. He reached into his pocket and pulled out a small, blue-gray pill. Swirling lines that mimicked flickering flames danced across its surface.
"The Ember Core," he breathed, his voice full of avarice.
The Grade-C Stim was worth a fortune, capable of instantly elevating a fire by an entire grade. He'd traded a Top-Tier Weapon for this single pill—a trade that had gnawed at him for years.
He'd been saving it, unwilling to waste something so precious. Hesitation flickered in his eyes as he rolled the pill between his fingers. Using it today was an enormous gamble.
But then he reminded himself: every great risk he'd ever taken had resulted in a Master-Grade Weapon.
If this bet paid off, he could push the Hellfire Blade beyond its limits. It might even become a Soulbound Weapon.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
The very thought made his heart hammer against his ribs. That was all the convincing he needed. With a surge of adrenaline, Jace popped the Ember Core into his mouth.
[SFX: ROARING FLAME]
The second he swallowed, his throat ignited. A raw, inhuman roar tore from his lips as pure flame erupted from his eyes, ears, nose, and mouth. The fire coursed through him for a blistering second before jetting out in a superheated torrent, straight into the forge.
Thanks to the Ember Core, Jace's fire had jumped an entire grade in power.
[AMBIENT: INTENSE HEAT]
[SFX: CRACKLING FIRE]
The furnace blazed with newfound intensity, and a wave of oppressive heat rolled through the entire cavern. Far from the forge, the prisoners trapped in their cages began to smolder and burn.
[SFX: AGONIZED SCREAMS]
Their tormented screams echoed off the stone walls, mingling with the sickening stench of scorched flesh.
The fire was growing stronger than Jace could ever have imagined. But suddenly he had another concern — could he control it?
