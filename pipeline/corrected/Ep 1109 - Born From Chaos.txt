Ep 1109 - Born From Chaos

Word count: 1188

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
The Tribulations were brutal. Not many warriors were lucky enough to survive one, so it was practically unheard of for someone to face them over and over again.
Even Zane himself had never imagined he could trigger five Small Heavenly Tribulations in a row. Luckily, his body had gotten stronger, and with his insane luck, he’d managed to handle them without much trouble.
After passing the Heavenly Tribulation, his power had skyrocketed. He was now a warrior at the tenth level of the Life-and-Death Realm.
Technically, a tenth level didn’t even exist. Once a warrior hit the sixth level, their Vitality would begin to slowly liquefy. The longer that process took, the more perfect the Soul Sea inside them would eventually become. At the tenth level of the Life-and-Death Realm, a warrior’s Vitality would liquefy almost instantly, creating a perfect Soul Sea and marking their official entry into the Soul Sea Realm.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
But Zane’s case was a little different.
Layla’s and Jasper’s cores were already turning into their own Soul Seas, a natural progression. Zane had checked his own core after breaking through to the tenth level, curious if the Chaos Energy inside him had done the same.
When his master had taught him the Primordial Chaos Tactic, he’d figured that since Chaos Energy was the very stuff that made the universe, it should be able to form a Soul Sea, too. 
But instead of a sea of Vitality, a Soul Sea born from Chaos Energy would be a true world.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: WONDER AND DISCOVERY]
Once a warrior entered the Soul Sea Realm and their inner world opened up, they could slowly grow a civilization inside their own body. 
But there was a Law that governed this kind of world: any creature raised inside it was not allowed to leave. If they tried, the Laws of that world would stop them, breaking them back down into raw Vitality. This specific rule was the strongest Law of Causality in the universe, and so far, no one had ever managed to break it.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
Plenty of legends had been passed down in the supreme worlds, and one of them told the story of a Supreme Lord who fell in love with a woman born inside his own Inner World. The Supreme Lord tried everything he could think of to bring her out, spending thousands of years traveling across the universe in search of a solution, but he never found one.
Even a Supreme Lord couldn’t break the Law of Causality. 
But the Primordial Chaos Tactic was different—or at least, that was his master’s theory. Everything in the universe, living beings and raw materials alike, was ultimately made of the same Chaos Energy that built the universe itself. If that was true, then whatever Zane grew inside a Soul Sea built from Chaos Energy wouldn’t really be “inner-world” life at all—it would just be ordinary life, no different from anything else out here. And ordinary life wasn’t bound by the rule that trapped everything else.
It was a good theory. The problem was, his master was the only person who’d ever attempted this path, and even he couldn’t say for certain whether it held up. Zane would have to find out the hard way, one step at a time.
Right now, though, there wasn’t even a Soul Sea to test the theory on. Zane turned his attention inward and found the Chaos Energy thicker than before, but it still hadn’t liquefied. 
His Vitality had started liquefying the moment he crossed into the Life-and-Death Realm; the Chaos Energy hadn’t budged even after he’d technically crossed into Soul Sea territory. No one else had ever cultivated this technique, so there was nobody left to ask. He’d just have to wait and see.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[SFX: WIND GUST]
With the tenth tribulation behind him, Passing the Torch came to its formal close. The official in the cotton robes raised his hand and dispelled the seal that had enclosed the area for the entire trial.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
The moment the seal lifted, something felt off.
Layla tilted her head back, frowning at the sky. “...That’s a lot of clouds.”
Then someone spotted it: faint sword-shapes, drifting and re-forming within the mist, over and over.
[SFX: WHOOSH OF TELEPORTATION]
The Lord of the Sunset World’s gaze locked onto the clouds. He took one step forward—and vanished. The other palace masters, catching on at the same instant, vanished right after him.
They reappeared at the great rock formation at the edge of Willow Island, where two ragged Soul Sea Realm warriors already sat, staring up at the clouds with something between awe and disbelief—pulling meaning out of the drifting shapes above them.
Within the cloud, countless faint figures performed the same motion, over and over: a single, simple stab. But that one stab carried a weight that made the air itself feel heavier.
“The Gods-Intimidating Strike,” one of the palace masters breathed. “Supreme Lord Sword spent his whole life on this. Just one strike—but it’s the kind of strike that could actually wound an Empyrean.”
“Shame I’m not a sword cultivator,” the Lord of the Sunset World murmured. “Can’t take the sword intent directly. But the idea behind it—I can translate that into my own weapon.” 
[AMBIENT: SERENE MOUNTAIN WIND]
He settled cross-legged in the air alongside the two Soul Sea warriors, head tilted back toward the clouds, and began to comprehend.
None of them were about to let this pass by. Yolande, the other palace masters, even the official in cotton robes—all of them settled in to do the same.
Zane and the others couldn’t teleport like the palace leaders and World Lords, but they weren’t exactly slow, either. They hurried over, and one look at the sky told them exactly what was happening.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
[SFX: SWORD RESONANCE]
Someone had triggered the secret left behind by Supreme Lord Sword.
At this point, nobody cared who had done it or how. This was an opportunity no one was about to miss. Everyone just looked up, trying to absorb as much of the sword intent as they could.
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL REVELATION]
Zane’s eyes shifted to the figures forming in the clouds, and his jaw went slack. He just stared, completely stunned.
The strike looked so much like his own formless sword technique. In fact, they looked almost identical.
Of course, at their core, they were completely different.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Both were a single strike, and both were built on the principle that the greatest truths are the simplest. But Supreme Lord Sword’s move was on another level entirely. Zane’s formless sword was unbreakable because it had no fixed shape. 
Supreme Lord Sword’s strike, meanwhile, was also unbreakable, but for the exact opposite reason: it had an extremely rigid, unyielding form. So even though they followed the same principle, the way they were executed made them polar opposites.
Still, you had to admit, Supreme Lord Sword was better. The man had earned his title; he was basically one of the founding fathers when it came to understanding the art of the sword.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL RISING THEME]
But Zane had an advantage. Since he already understood the formless sword, he had a unique head start on grasping the sheer depth and power of the Gods-Intimidating Strike.
