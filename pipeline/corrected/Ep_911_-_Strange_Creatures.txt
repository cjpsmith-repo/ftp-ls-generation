Ep 911 - Strange Creatures

Word count: 1165

[SFX: FAINT WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: HIGH TENSION]

With a faint whoosh, the blue, crystal-like figure exploded into dust and vanished on the wind.

Everyone froze, stunned. The little drake hadn’t seemed that dangerous—a level-two or three beast at most. But with a single roar, it had blown the corpse possessed by one of their own to pieces. What in the world was that thing?

The master’s soul, which had been piloting the corpse from within its mind, was gone—shattered right along with the body’s entire upper half.

“Get that thing! That’s for Nathan!” one of the masters yelled, his voice raw with rage as he charged toward the drake.

“Be careful! Something’s off about it.”

“Stay clear of its roar!”

The sound had been powerful, yes, enough to obliterate a Soul Sea master. But most of the warriors here were of the same rank. To them, the roar was impressive but not terrifying enough to send them running. No one else moved.

But just as the charging master got close, he dug his heels into the ground, spun around, and sprinted back the way he came.

“Go!” he screamed at the others. “We have to run!”

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

He was met with a sea of confused looks, but their questions were answered a second later when hundreds of identical drakes poured out of a nearby alley.

One of them was dangerous, sure, but manageable. Hundreds of them? That was a different story. That was pure terror.

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

The stunned expressions on their faces twisted into raw fear. War King Wind yelled, “Everyone, run!”

But everyone else had already bolted, with Zane leading the pack.

[SFX: MASSIVE ROAR]

[SFX: SHATTERING CRYSTAL]

Just as they were about to turn onto the main road, a deafening chorus of roars echoed from behind them. Dozens of sonic waves erupted from the alley, blasting ten of the crystal buildings into glittering shards. The sheer power was overwhelming.

It wasn't long before the drakes flooded the main road, their eyes fixed on the fleeing warriors. Each one drew in a deep breath, and it was clear they were about to unleash that deadly sound again.

“Split up! We can’t stay together! Break into two groups and meet at Crystal Lake!” The moment War King Wind gave the order, and the masters splintered off, darting down two separate streets.

[SFX: DEEP GROWL]

[SFX: SONIC BLAST BARRAGE]

The moment they did, dozens of sonic waves blasted at them from every direction. It was a miracle anyone dodged them. Against an onslaught like this, all they could do was run for their lives.

Some of the waves fired high and dissipated harmlessly into the sky. Others angled low, plowing long furrows into the main road. The crystal buildings lining the streets crumbled one after another, raining shards across the ground.

[MUSIC: TENSE CHASE]

Zane broke away from the group and vaulted onto a rooftop, scanning the chaos below. The drakes were everywhere, their little bodies moving with an energy that seemed completely at odds with the devastation they were causing.

“Brianna,” he said quietly. “What are these things?”

Brianna's voice drifted up from somewhere inside him, uncertain. “They carry the blood of the True Dragon Lineage. But it's not pure — diluted, mixed with something else. I genuinely can't tell what they are.”

[SFX: WINGS FLAPPING]

One of the drakes had spotted him. It spread its stubby wings, crossed the rooftop in three bounding leaps, and opened its mouth.

[SFX: SONIC BLAST]

Zane's golden eyes flashed. He shifted his weight to one side, letting the wave shear past him, then stepped in close and drove his elbow hard into the creature's belly.

[SFX: HEAVY THUD]

The drake launched upward like a stone from a sling. It cleared the rooftops entirely, spinning up into the pale sky until it was a tiny speck, then disappeared from view altogether.

Zane stared after it. That strike carried the force of four thousand Dragon Scales behind it — enough to cave in a building. He'd expected the thing to come apart.

[SFX: DISTANT CRASH]

A beat passed. Then, far down the street, the drake hit the ground with a bone-shaking crack.

And got up.

It shook itself off, looked around, and started moving again as if nothing had happened.

“How is that possible?” Zane muttered.

These creatures had no cultivation to speak of — and yet their physical endurance was simply absurd.

[SFX: MULTIPLE GROWLS]

Seven or eight more drakes rounded the corner below, all of them angling upward toward him. Zane didn't wait. He pushed off the roof and flew.

[MUSIC: FRANTIC CHASE]

Sprinting through the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, the masters scrambled to evade the drakes' attacks.

[SFX: DISTANT SCREAMING]

Through the noise of the chase, Zane caught fragments of screams echoing from deeper in the city. Not everyone had made it to open ground in time.

“Help!” It was Fiona.

He turned to see War King Wind's possessed corpse, Victor Vayne, pinned between two drakes in a side alley with nowhere left to go.

War King Wind's fury came out as a roar. “Out of my way!”

[SFX: AXES CUTTING AIR]

[SFX: DOUBLE IMPACT]

The two drakes had no room to dodge. The axes caught them dead-on and drove them straight through the wall of the nearest building.

[SFX: BUILDING COLLAPSE]

War King Wind grabbed Fiona and ran. Behind them, dust and crystal fragments billowed out from the collapsed structure.

Then came the sound of movement in the rubble.

Both drakes crawled free, shook the debris from their backs, and resumed the chase without missing a step.

Luckily, the little creatures weren't particularly fast. Running flat out, the group finally managed to pull away.

[AMBIENT: CALM LAKE]

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

They crossed half the secret land in record time and regrouped at Crystal Lake, the designated meeting point. Everyone was breathless and on edge, their faces pale with lingering fear. 

Some of them, especially War King Wind, had explored this place many times. They knew the safe zones and the danger zones like the back of their hands.

Living creatures weren’t supposed to exist in the main city. Even the most troublesome puppets stayed away from this area. 

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The sudden appearance of these drakes felt like a terrible omen.

Fiona stood at the edge of the lake, her face still ghostly white. Seeing his daughter’s expression, War King Wind felt a pang of guilt and tried to comfort her. “Fiona, those things are powerful, but they’re not invincible.”

[SFX: WINGS FLAPPING]

[SFX: CREATURE STRUGGLING]

Just then, Zane landed nearby, a live drake clutched in his hand. He’d actually flown off, caught one, and brought it back.

Everyone eyed the creature warily, but Zane had already sealed its mouth shut, rendering it harmless.

“See?” War King Wind said, forcing a smile. “Zane caught one bare-handed. There’s nothing to be afraid of.”

But Fiona wasn’t looking at the drake. She was staring right at Zane. “No,” she said, her voice barely a whisper. “That’s not what I’m afraid of.”
