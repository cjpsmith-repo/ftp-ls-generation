Ep 757 - The Situation Reversed

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

 All eyes were glued to Maddox. When he finally spoke, his words were enough to crush what little morale the warriors of Oracle City had left. 

They knew what it meant. He’d found Zane. 

Their last, flickering hope was about to be snuffed out.

Maddox didn’t waste a moment. 

[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]

[SFX: METAL CLASH]

 He lunged forward, his fist glowing with a terrifying power, and threw a punch that slammed into the spatial anchor with a deafening crack. But the sound was immediately followed by his own grunt of pain. A sharp blade shot out from the anchor, shattering the light around his fist and stabbing straight through it.

Even though his fist was covered in a layer of tough, black scales, it was no match for the top-grade Relic-Class weapon. The longsword didn’t just pierce his hand; it drove clear through his forearm, and the force behind it sent him stumbling back. 

[SFX: BLADE PIERCING]

[SFX: SPATIAL TEAR]

 A hand gripped the hilt, and then Zane himself stepped through the shimmering tear in space.

[MUSIC: STUNNED SILENCE]

The pain contorted Maddox's charred face. But beneath the pain, something else moved through him — something he hadn't felt in a long time. Zane was standing in front of him. Alive. Vigorous, even, as if the Life and Death Wind had done nothing to him at all.

Maddox had been ninety-nine percent certain Zane was dead. The wind had never failed, not against anything.

What kind of freak is this?

The question had no answer. And in the absence of an answer, something cold settled into Maddox's chest — a feeling he recognized, and despised. Powerlessness.

He, Maddox, had clawed his way to this height as an independent warrior through sheer will and ruthlessness. His martial heart was iron. He never gave up, never hesitated. But now, staring at the young man who should have been dead, he found he couldn't locate his certainty.

He had prepared everything for Zane. The Life and Death Wind was the enemy of all living things. And it hadn't been enough.

How do I kill him?

The thought had barely formed before Zane caught it. Maddox saw the flicker of recognition in those sharp eyes and knew immediately that he'd made a mistake. He tried to pull his expression back.

Too late. Zane had seen it — that single unguarded instant where Maddox couldn't find his footing. In all the months of running, of being hunted, of watching people die around him, he had waited for that look. Now he had it, and he wasn't going to let go.

With one hand severed and the other now pinned, Maddox could only retreat. But Zane gave him no ground. He held the sword steady and pressed forward, his intent clear — he was going to drive Maddox into the city wall.

Right behind him, Cora emerged from the anchor, carrying an unconscious Tia in her arms.

A wave of relief and excitement washed over the warriors on the walls.

[AMBIENT: CHEERING CROWD]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

 They had been drowning in despair, convinced that no one in The Heartlands was strong enough to stand against Maddox. Zane’s Double Stars Brilliance had already proven he was the only one who could even come close, and now here he was, brimming with energy and looking completely unharmed.

[AMBIENT: CHEERING CROWD]

 “Take him down, Zane! Do it!”

“We’re with you!”

“I’m with you, Zane! In spirit!” Lewis screamed at the top of his lungs from a safe distance.

Patrick could only manage a weary smile. It was a good thing Finn was out cold, or Lewis would be getting an earful right about now. Even Owen and Brent had made their way to The Heartlands to witness the fight.

For most of them, this was the first time they'd laid eyes on him in months. They'd heard the rumors — that he was dead, that the Heartlands had lost its only real answer to Maddox. Seeing him now, sword in hand and unbroken, hit differently than any rumor could.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

 Maddox seethed. His own talent had never been saint-level; he was a heaven-level at best. Reaching the Soul Sea Realm with that limitation was a monumental feat, but his own ceiling had always been a bitter pill to swallow. It was why he’d poured everything into his son, a true saint-level genius.

Zane's eyes were cold. "You want to call yourself a king. Build a Divine Kingdom. But a Divine Kingdom needs a Soul Sea Realm warrior to anchor it. What do you have to open the Soul Sea with your talent?"

The words landed exactly where he'd aimed them. Maddox's jaw tightened.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

 “Talent means nothing,” Maddox growled. “I don’t give a damn about it. Once my Soul Harvesting Ritual is complete, I’ll—”

[SFX: BLADE GRINDING BONE]

 Zane gave the blade in his arm a cruel twist. The tip ground against bone, draining the color from Maddox’s face.

“You won’t get the chance,” Zane said, his voice ice. “I told you. You took Elara. And for that, you’re going to die.”

[SFX: MASSIVE WALL IMPACT]

[AMBIENT: CRUMBLING STONE]

 Before he even finished speaking, Zane slammed them both into the city wall. The impact tore another massive hole in the already crumbling fortifications, sending a shower of bricks and dust into the air. 

[SFX: SWORD PIERCING FLESH]

 The moment they hit, Zane ripped the sword from Maddox’s arm and plunged it deep into his chest.

With one hand severed and the other shattered, Maddox was defenseless.

He had never run from anyone. Not once in his life. The fact that his legs were moving away from this fight, away from a Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior, was almost more than his pride could process. 

And yet here he was. His only option was to run. 

He twisted away and fled through the city gates, trying to pick up speed. 

[SFX: COLLAPSING STRUCTURE]

 The damaged structure groaned around him, and more massive stones and support beams came crashing down in his path.

Zane didn’t hesitate. 

[SFX: DEBRIS PULVERIZING]

[MUSIC: CHASE SEQUENCE]

The sword intent flowing from his hand swirled out, pulverizing the falling debris into dust. He was on Maddox’s heels in an instant, chasing him through the destruction.
