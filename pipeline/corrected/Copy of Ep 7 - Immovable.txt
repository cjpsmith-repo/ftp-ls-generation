Ep 7 - Immovable
Word count: 1104
[AMBIENT: PRISON CELLAR]
[SFX: CHAINS RATTLING]
Zane was too amped to get more than a half hour of sleep. The next morning, he was shoved into a suit of leather armor, cuffed, and led out of the cellar.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED MORNING]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
Reaching the Bone Refining Level had fundamentally changed his body. The difference was subtle, but Zane could feel it in his own stride. It was smoother, more efficient, and wasted less energy. If it weren't for the heavy cuffs digging into his wrists, he felt like he could make a run for it right then and there.
Zane's brow furrowed when he realized the guards weren't taking him to the Training Hall, but somewhere else entirely.
The Lowell estate was massive, but smartly laid out. The Training Hall, the Council Hall, a Community Garden, and The Forge—all were centrally located near the main entrance.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR ESTATE GROUNDS]
He took it all in as they walked, recognizing the path that led deeper into the estate. The guards guided him down a narrow walkway that snaked toward what looked like the backyard of the main house.
Zane kept his expression neutral and his mouth shut.
"Fine", he thought. "I'll play along and see where this goes."
[MUSIC: NOSTALGIC MELODY]
Zane hadn't been back here in a long time. He quietly absorbed the changes. His uncles had added sprawling new homes for their families, tacking them onto the original House Lowell along with several pavilions and a Grand Hall.
Outside, they'd even dug a few ponds, complete with arched bridges.
[SFX: MORNING BIRDS CHIRPING]
The craftsmanship was incredible. Each new structure boasted high roofs supported by intricately carved beams, and golden dragons painted on the walls shimmered in the early morning sun.
Seeing all this new construction gave Zane pause.
The Lowells might be rich, but in this world, only the strong survive.
Warriors shouldn't be chasing fame and fortune. A family that got comfortable, that stopped striving for more... they were just setting themselves up for a fall.
[AMBIENT: ELEGANT MANOR HOUSE]
He was sure of it: his uncles were going to run House Lowell straight into the ground.
[AMBIENT: GARDEN WITH WATER FEATURE]
[MUSIC: NOBLE HOUSE THEME]
After leading him through a garden and past a pond, the guards guided Zane through a narrow passage that opened into a courtyard.
Two men were sitting there, enjoying coffee.
As Zane got closer, he recognized one of them: Darren, the steward who'd been out of sight for the past two days. The other man, who looked to be in his fifties, was draped in expensive satin.
Zane's eyebrow shot up. He knew the second man, too. Grey Hawn. He'd once been a servant for House Lowell. Grey's wife had even been a nanny to Andrew Lowell—Zane's younger uncle's oldest son.
Grey Hawn was a total suck-up. That's how he'd climbed the ladder in House Lowell. Zane had heard the gossip from the guards: as his two uncles gained more power, Grey Hawn rode their coattails, and his own status had soared. Now he was a senior steward.
Ambitious and arrogant, Grey paid little attention to anyone outside the main family's immediate circle. Zane had heard stories about his condescending attitude.
[SFX: DISTANT CONVERSATION]
From where he stood, Zane could just make out their conversation.
[AMBIENT: COURTYARD AMBIENCE]
"This house Master Andrew gifted you, is a masterpiece," Darren was saying. "The Emerald Dragon on the nameplate... that's carved from Adamant Seed, isn't it? That's some top-shelf material."
Grey Hawn chuckled. "You've got a good eye for that sort of thing. But look closer. It's not just the Emerald Dragon. The roof is inlaid with Mosaic Tiles. And that Bronze Lion? That's the work of the best craftsmen in all of Castermere County."
Darren's gaze swept over each detail, clearly impressed. "They really spared no expense." He paused, a thoughtful look on his face. "Your house is perfect, Grey. But you're missing one thing."
"I have everything I could ever want," Grey Hawn replied, puffing out his chest. "What could I possibly be missing?"
Darren chuckled. "Good help. You need someone sharp, hardworking. Someone who can even help you with your meals. And I have the perfect person in mind."
"Oh yeah? And who might that be?" Grey Hawn asked, leaning in.
Darren pointed toward the courtyard entrance. "Take a look, Grey."
Grey Hawn turned and saw Zane standing there in cuffs. The once-famous young master of House Lowell. There was no mistaking him.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Zane's blood ran cold as he processed what they were talking about. The sheer nerve.
He was a slave, yes, but Darren had no right to offer him up like cattle—especially not to a steward. Grey wasn't even a Lowell by blood. The insult burned. Even the extended family wouldn't dare be so openly disrespectful; he was still the eldest son of the main branch, damn it. And here Darren was, pushing Grey Hawn to take him on as a house servant.
Grey Hawn sized Zane up, a slow, condescending smile spreading across his wrinkled face. "Having the boy handle my meals... that's not a bad idea," he mused. "But would my master, Andrew, approve?"
"Don't you worry about that, Grey. You handle all the business for House Lowell. It'll be simple for you to request Zane's transfer from the Training Hall to your residence. He's just a worthless slave now. His only purpose is to be a punching bag in the Training Hall. This would be a cushier gig. The worst he'd have to do is scrub your chamber pot."
[MUSIC: DARK TRIUMPH]
Grey Hawn couldn't suppress his triumphant smile at the proposition. For someone who had clawed his way up from servitude, nothing validated his status more than such a delicious reversal of fortune. The former young master of House Lowell, once untouchable in his privilege, would now be scrubbing away Grey's filth. The irony tasted sweeter than the finest honey wine served at Lowell banquets.
"I accept," Grey declared with a deliberate, measured nod, savoring each moment of his newfound power. His eyes gleamed with anticipation as he imagined recounting this tale to his associates.
"You won't regret it!" Darren exclaimed, his smile broadening across his face. Rising from his seat with newfound energy, he motioned to one of the guards. "Take him inside," he commanded, already envisioning the favor this arrangement would curry with the senior steward.
[MUSIC: DEFIANT RESOLVE]
Zane, however, remained immovable. Despite the humiliation burning in his chest, he transformed his rage into silent strength. His feet planted firmly on the ground, he became a monument of defiance.
[SFX: GUARDS SCUFFLE]
The guards pushed against his back, then shoved harder, their frustration mounting as they failed to budge him even an inch.
[MUSIC: TENSION BUILDING]
Their confusion turned to alarm as they realized something fundamental had changed in the slave they thought they knew.
