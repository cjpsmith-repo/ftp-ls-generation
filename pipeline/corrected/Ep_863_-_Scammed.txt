Ep 863 - Scammed

Word count: 1017

Callan clutched the stone, looking confused by Zane’s question. “I didn’t find it,” he answered, his voice tight. “Fredrick sold it to me. Why? Is something wrong?”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]

The name alone made Fiona’s expression harden. Of all the crown princes in the seven divine kingdoms, her younger brother, Callan, was the only one she considered truly decent—honest to a fault. The rest, she couldn’t stand, but Fredrick of the York Divine Kingdom was at the absolute bottom of that list. 

“How much did you pay for it?” Fiona asked immediately.

“Nine thousand,” Callan said.

Supreme-grade Aether Crystals, of course. An amount like that was a staggering sum, even for a crown prince. Fiona knew it had to be more than half of everything her brother owned. 

Frowning, she took the Black Heart Fire Stone from his hand. 

Something about it just felt wrong.

Noticing her skepticism, Callan rushed to explain. “Fiona, they demonstrated it for me! This ore can withstand intense heat. They said once it’s refined, it can dramatically increase the purity of your flames!”

[SFX: CRACKLING FIRE]

Before he could say another word, a wisp of flame bloomed in Fiona’s palm. She’d test it herself. The flame was small, but the heat rolling off it was immense—the power of the third layer of the Fire Law was nothing to scoff at.

[SFX: STONE CRACKING]

As she brought the pure, concentrated flame closer, the Black Heart Fire Stone didn’t just heat up—it crackled. Fissures spiderwebbed across the intricate patterns on its surface, and in the blink of an eye, the entire stone gave way, melting and crumbling into a pile of useless fragments.

Callan’s jaw went slack. He stared at the worthless pile of dust and rock, completely stunned. Sure, his power wasn’t on Fiona’s level, but he’d also mastered the third layer of Fire Law. He had personally tested the stone with his own flame for several minutes before buying it, and he couldn’t even leave a scorch mark. So how had his sister’s fire destroyed it in seconds?

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The humiliating truth hit him like a physical blow. He’d been scammed.

Zane and Camilla exchanged a look. Fredrick must have pulled a fast one. Callan was a good guy, but he was also painfully honest and a little naive, which made him an easy mark.

Fiona’s normally relaxed demeanor vanished, replaced by a quiet, chilling fury. She hated confrontation, but there was no way in hell she was letting someone get away with ripping off her little brother.

[AMBIENT: MINE ENTRANCE]

[SFX: PORTAL SHIMMER]

Just then, the shimmering ring of light marking the mine’s entrance flickered. Fredrick stepped out, a pickax slung over his shoulder. One by one, his followers emerged behind him, their own portals flashing.

[SFX: CROWD SHOUTING]

Before Fredrick could even open his mouth, his entourage of so-called “saint-level geniuses” was already making a scene, shouting and posturing.

“That’s enough,” Zane cut in, his voice low and sharp, his eyes locked on Fredrick. “I’m only going to say this once. Give Callan his Aether Crystals back, and we can forget this happened. Don’t make this embarrassing for yourself.”

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION RISING]

Fredrick’s face darkened instantly. 

He wasn’t afraid of Fiona. In his mind, she was a pushover, and her brother was an idiot; he could have handled them both easily. But Zane’s involvement changed the equation. 

If Zane had been even a little more diplomatic, Fredrick might have considered backing down just to avoid the hassle. But his tone was insulting, a blatant challenge. To back down now, in front of all his followers, would be admitting he was afraid of Zane—and that was an impression he couldn’t afford to give.

Fredrick looked at the pile of fragments in Fiona’s hand. His expression didn’t change. “Oh? What is this?”

“The stone you sold Callan is fake,” Fiona said. “Return the crystals.”

Fredrick smiled. “Princess Fiona, when did I ever say that stone was real?”

He turned to Callan before Callan could speak. “Did I tell you it was genuine? Didn’t I warn you I couldn’t vouch for its value? Didn’t I say I didn’t want to sell it? You were the one who insisted on buying it. And didn’t you swear to me — your own words — that no matter what, real or fake, you wouldn’t hold me responsible?”

Callan’s face went scarlet. Every word was true. He opened his mouth and found nothing to say. He nodded.

Fiona stared at Fredrick and found herself with nothing to counter. She wasn’t the type for this kind of verbal maneuvering. Fredrick had constructed it perfectly — by the logic of it, her brother had walked into this with open eyes.

[MUSIC: SMUG THEME]

Fredrick spread his hands. “Your brother has admitted it himself. I told him I didn’t want to sell. He insisted. He made a promise. And now you’re coming to me?” He tilted his head. “Isn’t that a little unreasonable, Princess?”

A beat of silence. Then his eyes drifted to her wrist. “That said — I’m not heartless. I’m willing to return the crystals. Lend me that bracelet of yours for a year, and I’ll consider myself unlucky. We’ll call it even.”

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

The Red Jade Bracelet — a semi-divine weapon. Its value was beyond what any ordinary crown prince could dream of. Fredrick had once owned two Heavenly Thunder Halberds at that level, but they were one-use artifacts. He’d burned through one of them escaping the Stone-Eater Bugs. The bracelet on Fiona’s wrist was worth more than everything he had left.

Zane laughed. He couldn’t help it.

Fredrick’s eyes snapped to him. “What are you laughing at?”

“I’m laughing at someone who can be this shameless,” Zane said, still smiling. “To reach this level of shamelessness — I genuinely admire it.”

[SFX]

Fredrick’s followers erupted.

“How dare you—”

“Who does he think he is?!”

“He’s a commoner! In front of the imperial family, he’s nothing!”

 In the divine kingdoms, the gap between warriors and mortals was absolute, and the gap between warriors and the imperial family was just as vast. 

To them, Zane speaking to a crown prince this way was pure insolence. They were desperate to watch Fredrick teach this young punk a lesson.
