Ep 893 - Just Merge

Word count: 1196

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT STONE NECROPOLIS]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Climbing the stairs in the necropolis wasn’t actually that hard. The whole trick was just waiting for the right moment to move. As long as you did that, you were safe. In all the years since the first Soul Sea warrior had blundered in and died, not a single person from the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms had met their end here.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

After waiting for the Soul Purifying Light to flash and fade twice, Zane and Fiona carefully picked their way back to the fourth floor. The moment they arrived, War King Wind thanked Zane, and then he and his men resumed their climb.

Most of the Soul Sea warriors made it to the fifth, sixth, and seventh floors without much trouble. 

But the higher they went, the less time they had between each blast of the Soul Purifying Light, and the pressure started to mount. For the seasoned warriors, it was manageable. For the younger ones like Fiona and the Crown Prince, who were only at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, it was a whole different story.

[SFX: TELEPATHIC ECHO]

The Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom’s voice echoed in their minds. “Crown Prince, Randy—it’s too difficult for you to keep climbing. Even if you made it to the upper floors, you wouldn’t be able to possess the bodies of those powerful warriors. This floor should be your limit. Try to find a suitable body here.”

He was right. Even though these warriors had been dead for ages, possessing their bodies was never easy. The Soul Cores helped lessen the body’s natural rejection, but the stronger the original owner, the fiercer that rejection would be.

The Crown Prince, Randy, Fiona, and the other younger warriors all nodded in understanding. They’d only gotten into the Divine Emperor’s Secret Land with help from their elders, anyway.

This was a trial for them, but no one expected them to hit the jackpot.

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TOMB]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

“Let me help you open this coffin,” War King Wind announced, moving swiftly to the edge of the platform. He stepped into one of the small huts and positioned himself beside a coffin. Closing his eyes for a moment, he murmured a few words under his breath before his voice sounded in everyone’s thoughts. “These bodies have been here for countless years. Show them respect.”

As he spoke, he reached out.

[SFX: COFFIN LID OPENING]

His hands seemed to blur, flowing like a gust of wind over the grooves on the side of the coffin. He nudged the edge of the lid with a sliver of his soul, and with a slight ripple of energy from his Fighting Soul Realm power, the heavy lid floated open right in front of them.

Inside lay the perfectly preserved body of a woman who had reached the Life-and-Death Realm. She was dressed in a light blue palace robe, her face still and pale, as though she had simply fallen asleep. No one knew what methods the ancient Dragon-blooded had used, but even after all this time, their bodies showed no signs of decay.

War King Wind glanced around at the group. “Which one of you will—” He stopped. Every eye in the room was on him. Among all the younger warriors present, there was only one woman. He smiled awkwardly. “Fiona. This one is yours.”

Fiona studied the body in the coffin for a moment, then nodded. “Yes.”

She leapt up onto the coffin’s edge, hesitated, and looked back at her father.

War King Wind stepped forward and pressed his hand to the corpse’s head. The body’s mouth fell open, and its eyes snapped wide.

Fiona startled despite herself.

“Don’t be afraid,” War King Wind said quietly. “Just merge with her.”

Fiona steadied herself, fixed her gaze on the corpse, and entered it.

[SFX: SOFT ENERGY PULSE]

Grey light seeped from the corpse’s head — the Soul Core energy dispersing through the body. A moment passed. Then the fingers moved. The eyes opened slowly. The body flipped out of the coffin in stiff, puppet-like movements and took a few lurching steps.

Gradually, the movements smoothed. Within a few breaths, Fiona had drawn her legs together into her usual poised, upright posture — the same one she always carried, now wearing a different face.

Zane smiled faintly. Even in a borrowed body, she was still entirely herself.

“I… I can control it,” Fiona said. Her voice was completely different — the corpse’s voice, not hers.

“Good,” War King Wind said.

The Soul Sea masters moved to the remaining coffins and opened them. The other bodies were all male, clad in golden armor, with high-grade Relics laid beside them as burial offerings. Randy, the Crown Prince, Callan, and the other princes each chose one and entered.

The whole process took only a short while. Once everyone was settled, the Emperor glanced at Zane. The meaning was plain — would he choose here as well?

Zane’s consciousness had already answered before anyone could ask. “I’ll go up with you.”

War Emperor York turned to Zane. “Your soul is nearly at the Nightjade Fighting Soul level,” he said. “It’s much stronger than ours. You’re more than qualified to go higher.”

So, Zane continued on with the remaining Soul Sea warriors, carefully timing their movements to avoid the Soul Purifying Light.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STRINGS]

Meanwhile, Fiona, Randy, and the others had already found their new bodies. Once their souls were safely housed inside a physical form, the Soul Purifying Light couldn’t harm them. They no longer had to dodge it and could now ascend the steps with ease.

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

They all kept climbing. The eighth, ninth, and tenth floors had fewer coffins, but the bodies inside were far more powerful. Starting on the tenth floor, the coffins all held warriors who had reached the Soul Sea Realm.

“I’ll choose this floor,” one of the Soul Sea warriors declared, stopping without hesitation to claim a body. His own soul had only just reached the Fighting Soul Realm; if Zane hadn’t dug up so many Soul Cores, he wouldn’t have even made it into the necropolis this year. He knew his limits and figured he’d probably die if he tried to go any higher, so he wisely decided to take what he could get.

One by one, other powerful Soul Sea warriors began choosing their bodies on that same floor. By the time the remaining group reached the twelfth floor, another sixty or seventy of them had found and possessed a new form.

[AMBIENT: VAST EMPTY HALL]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

When they finally arrived on the thirteenth floor, the massive group had dwindled to just five. Most of the warriors were now walking up the stairs in their new bodies, but Zane, the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom, War King Wind, War Emperor York, and Scott still hadn’t chosen. 

The five of them stood together on the platform, taking in their surroundings.

The thirteenth floor held only a handful of coffins — and whatever was inside them would determine how far any of them could go.
