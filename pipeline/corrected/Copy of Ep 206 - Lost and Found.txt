Tyson's legendary strength was renowned throughout the realm, but it wasn't wealth or power that kept tethered him to House Blackwood---it was loyalty. He served the family devotedly because they had shown him genuine kindness when others had not, a simple truth that defined his existence.
This profound loyalty exemplified Blackwood House's talent for recognizing potential where others saw nothing. Once an unremarkable family of modest means, they had methodically ascended through society's rigid hierarchy to stand as equals with the Seven Great Houses.
Their rise stemmed not from inherited privilege but from their exceptional ability to identify and cultivate individuals like Tyson---diamonds in the rough who blossomed under their patronage. Jefferson Blackwood, in particular, possessed an intuitive understanding of character that transcended conventional wisdom, enabling him to build alliances with those whose worth others had foolishly dismissed.

The grim determination etched across Tyson's face spoke volumes. His steely eyes conveyed an unmistakable message: James knew that if he so much as twitched, that axe would swing without a moment's hesitation or flicker of remorse.

The courtyard's atmosphere transformed abruptly as an unnatural chill descended upon the gathering. Despite the already bitter winter conditions that had forced servants to bundle in quilted doublets and nobles to don their finest furs to follow the action outside, this new cold cut deeper. Attendants instinctively clutched their collars tighter, their breath materializing in thick plumes before their wide-eyed faces.
[SFX: ICE CRYSTALS FORMING]
Delicate ice crystals began to form in the frigid air, accompanied by an eerie, resonant hum that vibrated through the bones of everyone present. The particles drifted with purpose, swirling together in an intricate dance that gradually coalesced into a human shape. The spectacle held the onlookers spellbound, their expressions a mixture of awe and mounting dread.
When the whirling ice finally settled into coherence, the figure stood revealed in perfect clarity: a young man with striking gray hair that seemed to capture the essence of winter itself. His presence radiated power that transcended his youthful appearance, commanding attention without effort.
James felt his throat constrict, moisture evaporating from his mouth as recognition dawned. A primal fear, rarely experienced by one of his stature, crept through his veins like the very ice that had heralded this ominous arrival.

There was no mistaking him. The young face, the gray hair\... it was George Solomon.
The Solomon Clan was famous for its masters of the needle and concealed weapons---spies and assassins skilled with everything from swords to hidden blades.
But in the last decade, the Solomon Clan had produced quite the anomaly in George.
George was always the odd one out---a quiet, withdrawn man who never quite fit in with his own family. As a young man, he'd turned his back on House Solomon, vowing never to return, and vanished for a decade.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
When he finally resurfaced, his power had grown to an incredible level, and he'd returned to solve some of House Solomon's biggest problems. He was still strange, still withdrawn, but he was family, and he had come home.

Ingrid was close to nearly everyone in House Solomon, but George was the exception. He was so\... odd. She'd barely spent any time with him as a child, and even now, she felt a lingering discomfort in his presence.
[SFX: FAINT MAGICAL SHIMMER]
"Don't worry," George said, a faint smile touching his lips. "The kid's fine. I dealt with the sword energy. Look, here he comes now." He gestured toward the edge of the courtyard.
[SFX: SWIFT MOVEMENT]
A shadow blurred, and Zane darted into the courtyard.
The moment Uncle George had contacted her through the Vitality, Ingrid had begged him to save Zane from the ring of swords. She hadn't known how long he could have survived.
Everyone bullied Ethan. Even a servant could yell at him, and Ethan would just smile and take it.
His standing in the family was lower than that of a servant.
James had never once considered Ethan's opinion. Why would he?
But now, backed into a corner, James had no choice. "Ethan," he said, his voice tight, "what do you want?"
[AMBIENT: TENSE COURTYARD]
Ethan's stomach clenched with nerves. He looked up, his face paling as he found every eye in the courtyard fixed on him.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL TENSION]
But then he thought of his father's suffering, and a cold fury began to burn through the fear. The memory of the Bloodstone Bracelet---his father's personal heirloom, now in James's hands---hardened his expression.
Questions swirled in his mind. "How did my father really die? What actually happened? And why does James have the Bloodstone Bracelet?" The confusion was a dizzying weight.
He knew he'd never be able to rest until he had answers.
But he also knew he couldn't force an answer out of James right now. It was a cold case, years old; any evidence was long gone. Besides, all he had were suspicions, doubts---no proof. Getting the truth from James would be impossible.
Ethan pushed the thoughts aside, his voice ringing out with surprising clarity. "I want my father's Bloodstone Bracelet!"
A wave of relief washed over James. The bracelet? That was nothing.
[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION]

[MUSIC: BITTERSWEET RESOLUTION]
His hands trembling with a mix of excitement and anger, Ethan took the box. He opened it, his eyes landing on the Bloodstone Bracelet inside. After a moment, he snapped the lid shut and put it away. But the victory felt hollow. It was never just about the bracelet. It was about his father's death. It was about the truth.
But after a long moment, Ethan let out a quiet sigh. "That's all," he said. "I want nothing else."
