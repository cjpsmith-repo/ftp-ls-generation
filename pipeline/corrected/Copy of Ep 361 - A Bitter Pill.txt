Ep 361 - A Bitter Pill

Word count: 1199
[AMBIENT: BUBBLING LAVA]
[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
Zane looked down at the churning sea of lava below, its molten surface rippling with deadly heat.
Four disciples stood clustered at the edge, two women from the Luminous Sect, and two men whose postures radiated arrogance.
The moment the men spotted Zane approaching, they exchanged glances and rolled their eyes in open disgust. Zane recognized them immediately as elite members of the Duskfall Sect; he'd seen them when their leader first escorted the group here. Their reputation preceded them—cultivators who thought anyone beneath the Soul Realm wasn't worthy of acknowledgment.
Given that these two snobs looked down on disciples who were already in the Soul Realm, they weren't about to give a lowly Adept like Zane the time of day. 
The reality stung, but Zane couldn't deny it. Except for himself and Finn, every disciple from the Cloud Hall and the other five sects had achieved at least the first level of the Soul Realm. It was a bitter pill to swallow, but at Level Three of the Adept Realm, Zane stood as the lowest-ranked disciple in the entire trial. 
No matter how much he tried to blend in, Zane stuck out like a firefly on a moonless night. A Level Three Adept surrounded by disciples from the Soul Realm was impossible to miss. He could see it in their sidelong glances, hear it in their whispered conversations that cut off whenever he drew near.
[SFX: CRACKLING FLAMES]
 Zane had no intention of crossing the Sea of Raging Fire just yet. Like the other four, he wanted to scope out the challenge first. A hundred-foot-long black wire hanging over the molten ocean caught his eye. *If I can just get to that,* he thought, *I could use my knowledge of the Spatial Laws to travel along it.*
But the hope died as quickly as it came. The ocean of fire was endless. To cross it, he'd need to launch himself over a hundred miles. The wire was a joke; it wouldn't even get him started.
While he waited, the Duskfall disciple was busy boasting to the two women about his future as a Cloud Hall elder. Zane tuned him out — but caught something useful in the rambling: the sea rose and fell. When the tide receded, a path would appear.
[SFX: RUMBLING LAVA]
Sure enough, after about an hour, the magma began to churn and pull back. In its wake, a series of circular stone platforms emerged across the sea — each about the size of a round table, spaced several meters apart. Stepping stones.

[SFX: DISTANT SHOUT]
As Zane stood there, trying to puzzle out a way across the lava, one of the men waved him over. "Hey, you! Yeah, you! Get over here!"
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Zane's expression darkened. A muscle twitched in his jaw as he remained rooted to the spot, staring at the disciple with cold intensity. "Me? What do you want?"
The other disciple from the Duskfall Sect snapped back, his voice dripping with impatience. "Cut the crap and get over here!" 
Zane could hear it in their condescending tone—they thought they could boss him around just because he was a Level Three Adept. To them, he wasn't even a real cultivator. Just some upstart who'd gotten lucky in the trials, unworthy of basic respect.
Heat rose in his chest, and his fingers curled into fists at his sides. His first instinct was to tell them to get lost, to remind them exactly what this "Level Three Adept" had accomplished. But he took a deep breath, forcing the anger down, making himself think strategically rather than emotionally. Pride was a luxury he couldn't afford right now.
This wasn't The Gauntlet, where a magic array would save you from a fatal blow. Here on the Cloud Road, dead was dead for good.
Besides, the goal wasn't to win fights; it was to make it across the road. And on top of that, he was curious what else he might learn from them.
The thought brought a wide, fake smile to Zane's face as he walked over.
 Adler, the Duskfall Sect disciple who'd called him over, gave him a cunning smile. "Since you're so eager to prove yourself worthy of the Cloud Hall, why don't you take the lead? Clear the path for your seniors."
Zane hesitated, studying the four of them. Their expressions ranged from smug superiority to thinly veiled contempt.
"Consider it a learning experience," Adler continued, his tone making it clear this wasn't a request. "Those of us with higher cultivation can observe how the spirits react, allowing us to cross more efficiently afterward."
Zane's jaw tightened, but he forced himself to nod. "Of course. I'd be honored to assist my seniors."
He hated going along with it, but he meant what he'd thought earlier—this wasn't the place for pride. Let them use him as their shield. He'd get across this sea one way or another, and when he did, he'd remember exactly who had tried to sacrifice him to the flames.
He summoned his Shadow Phantoms and set out through the sea of fire. 
[SFX: ENERGY SUMMONING]
[SFX: FIRE EXTINGUISHING]
[AMBIENT: MOLTEN SEA]
One after another, fiery evil spirits slammed into Zane's Shadow Phantoms only to be extinguished, their flames dying out just feet ahead of him. With his phantoms clearing a path, Zane moved forward at a steady, unhurried pace.
The spirits further ahead saw what was happening. They watched their comrades get helplessly sucked toward the phantoms and snuffed out. 
[SFX: SPLASHING LAVA]
 Panicked, they dove back into the molten sea rather than face the same fate.
"Ha! See? I told you making him lead the way was a good idea," Adler chuckled, clearly proud of his little power play.
"He's not half bad," another disciple admitted with a smirk. "Definitely better than your average Adept. Still no match for us, though."
With Zane acting as their living shield, the four disciples had it easy, following in his wake completely relaxed.
[SFX: BUBBLING LAVA]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]
Just then, a massive bubble of lava swelled up from the sea directly in their path. It grew larger and larger until a giant serpent’s head erupted from the molten surface.
[SFX: SERPENT ROAR]
 The color drained from Adler’s face. "Shit! A Red Fire Snake!" he yelled. "What do we do? God, what rotten luck!"
"We take it on together," one of the women from the Luminous Sect said, her voice firm. "A Red Fire Snake is fierce, but the four of us can handle it if we work together."
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
 "Hold on," Adler said, stopping short. A cruel smirk twisted his pale features as he threw out an arm, blocking the others. "Let's see how *he* handles it."
Envy was a poison. In their eyes, Zane would never be their equal, but they couldn't deny he was far more talented than any of them had been at his level. If a prodigy like him actually made it into the Cloud Hall, he'd leave them in the dust in no time.
So, if Zane got a little careless and fell into the lava to be burned to a crisp… or if that giant snake decided to make a meal out of him… well, they wouldn't exactly be shedding any tears.
After all, if someone had to die, it certainly shouldn't be one of them.
