Ep 1120 - Master Sloan

Word count: 1220

The sudden blast of power from Zane’s sword caught Sloan completely off guard.
At first, from so far away, the strike hadn’t seemed all that powerful. But Sloan had sensed a distinct purpose in its aura, something unique that had drawn him in. He’d tracked the source of that strange power all the way to Clearhaven.
[SFX: WHOOSHING APPROACH]
He’d barely reached the outskirts of the city when a human-shaped blood shadow came rushing toward him.
[SFX: SURPRISED VOICE]
Sloan’s brow furrowed. “Julius? What in the world happened to you?”
Sloan was in charge of over a thousand cities, but only about thirty were run by warriors who’d come up from the Lower World. Julius, the lord of Clearhaven, was one of the few he actually knew by name.
[SFX: DISTORTED CRACKLING VOICE]
“Sir,” Julius’s voice crackled, thin and broken. “Some kid… he destroyed my body. Turned me into this… this blood shadow!” A flicker of something that might have been hope pulsed within his misty form. “But sir, this kid—he has some kind of treasure. I bet he’s still in Clearhaven.”
“Oh?” A strange expression crossed Sloan’s face. He couldn’t figure out why someone with a treasure that powerful would be reckless enough to stir up trouble in the Soulbound Sect’s territory. It didn’t make any sense.
“I can take you to him right now, sir!” Julius offered, practically tripping over himself. 
[SFX: QUICK FOOTSTEPS]
He immediately spun around to lead the way, and Sloan followed close behind. 
[MUSIC: GENTLE ROMANTIC]
[AMBIENT: QUIET CITY]
Meanwhile, on the west side of the city, Freya sat frozen on the Heavenly Phoenix Horse, still staring at the spot where her uncle had dissolved.
She’d watched Zane fight a Spirit Supreme Realm master and win. In one move. Her uncle had spent years clawing his way up to city lord, and Zane had erased him like he was nothing. It made no sense. He’d only been in the Upper World for a year — the same as her. Upper World resources were better, but not that much better. Her uncle was proof of that.
She couldn’t figure it out. She had no way of knowing that in that same year, Zane had crossed more than a thousand worlds.
Zane, for his part, wasn’t satisfied.
He turned the Gods-Intimidating Strike over in his mind. The strike had landed. Julius was gone. But the moment he’d committed to it, his state of mind had wavered — just slightly, just for an instant. It wasn’t perfect. The technique demanded complete stillness, emotions sealed off entirely, and he hadn’t managed it. He found himself thinking that only a stone could pull it off cleanly. Or something that had never been alive at all. He was neither.
He filed it away and said nothing.
When the two figures appeared on the horizon, Zane’s mouth curved into a faint smile. He murmured, “It’s okay. I think I know him.”
Following Julius’s blood-mist form, Sloan hurried to the west side of the city. He’d been confused by the description of the intruder, but the moment he laid eyes on Zane, all his questions vanished. 
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Sloan stopped, raised his eyebrows, and gave the young man a slow look, up and down.
Zane’s appearance hadn’t changed much, but since breaking through to the Soul Sea Realm, his power had surged. To Sloan, his entire presence felt stronger, more solid, which only made him more suspicious.
*Wait a second,* Sloan thought, his mind racing. *The leader of the Mist Palace was supposed to have him. He shouldn’t be here. Even if he’d escaped, why in the world would he show up on our turf, all by himself?* For a split second, he wasn’t even sure if this was really Zane.
[SFX: MALICIOUS WHISPER]
“Sir, that’s the kid I told you about,” Julius whispered, his voice dripping with malice. “He’s got a Grade-One Divine Weapon and the Heavenly Phoenix Horse. And the woman with him? She’s the one I was going to send you. The perfect human cauldron for your training.”
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
The words hit Freya like a physical blow. Her own uncle… he wanted to sell her to his boss as a *human cauldron*. This man, right in front of them, was the one her uncle had meant. A wave of nausea washed over her. She felt sick that she’d ever run to her uncle for help when she’d arrived in the Upper World. She’d never imagined her own family could be so monstrous.
Freya tore her eyes away from her disgusting uncle and focused on the man with him. His power was obviously far beyond her uncle’s. She wondered if Zane was strong enough to handle him. 
The thought filled her with a cold dread.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ENCOUNTER]
The stranger looked Zane up and down a few more times before a small, friendly smile broke across his face. He gave Zane a casual wave. “Zane,” he said warmly. “Good to see you again so soon.”
Zane just nodded slightly. “Sloan. You too.”
Both Freya and Julius were stunned into silence. They just stood there, dumbfounded. They’d been expecting a fight, not a friendly reunion. 
Sloan’s smile lingered, but his eyes were still working. “I have to ask,” he said, keeping his tone light. “Last I heard, you’d been taken to the Mist Palace. What brings you back to our territory — alone?”
Zane’s expression didn’t shift. “Just passing through. I had somewhere to be.” He glanced toward Freya. “She ascended around the same time I did. We met in the mine. Her uncle locked her up and was planning to hand her over to someone as a cultivation vessel.”
The casual delivery of that last sentence landed on Sloan like a stone.
[SFX: TENSE SILENCE]
Julius hadn’t just imprisoned a girl — he’d nearly implicated Sloan himself in an offense against someone untouchable.
He kept his face pleasant with some effort.
A moment later, a brilliant smile bloomed on Freya’s face. This man, she realized, was nothing like her uncle.
Even though Julius was just a cloud of blood, Zane could practically see the color drain from his face, a stark contrast to his niece’s beaming relief.
*This can’t be real,* the thought crashed through Julius’s mind. *Sloan knows him?*
Sloan’s voice was smooth as silk. “It seems some poor management on my part led to a terrible mistake. My sincerest apologies.” He glanced at Julius’s shimmering form, and his voice hardened. 
[MUSIC: MENACING ORCHESTRAL]
“I see now that this subordinate of mine is a vicious man. To treat his own family so horribly… he’s sealed his own fate. As of this moment, he’s stripped of his title as lord of this city. I hope that eases your friend’s mind.”
If Julius still had a face, it would have been as white as a sheet. He was terrified. This was his worst nightmare coming true.
[SFX: RAPID WHOOSHING MOVEMENT]
Without a second thought, Julius turned and fled as if the devil himself were on his heels. As a being of pure blood mist, he moved impossibly fast, covering fifty feet in the blink of an eye.
Sloan just sneered and pointed a finger at the fleeing shadow, as if he’d expected it all along. 
[SFX: BLOOD-CURDLING SCREAM]
A blood-curdling scream ripped through the air. Julius’s form imploded, dissolving into several writhing streams of red froth. 
[SFX: WET SQUELCHING COMPRESSION]
The streams churned and twisted as if squeezed by an invisible hand, which slowly kneaded the last of his remains, compressing them into a single, feather-light piece of Lifeblood.
