Ep 633 - Violet Heart Sanctuary

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORCHESTRAL]
Most of the top-level War Lords were already gathered on the fourteenth floor of the Tower of Sin, but the fifteenth floor was where the real power lay. Getting there wasn't easy. You had to fight for one of the coveted seats, and each victory granted the winner their own private space on the floor—basically a small world to call their own.
The problem was that there were only fourteen seats. Even when you counted all the top-tier human War Lords—Fabian, Jarrod, and two other seat holders included—they still came up short.
Anxiety tempered Fabian's excitement. He reached out to Zane through his Vitality, seeking advice.
Zane's solution was brutally simple: recruit level nines. If necessary, even level eights.
In the end, though, all the seats went to top-level War Lords. Fabian refused to let anyone of a lower rank become a seat holder. He somehow managed to convince several top-level War Lords from the Violet Heart Sanctuary to fill the remaining spots. A sanctuary that massive was bound to have a few extra powerhouses to spare.
[AMBIENT: SANCTUARY GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: GRAND CEREMONIAL]
Once that was all settled, Zane finally accepted Fabian’s invitation and made the trip to the Violet Heart Sanctuary himself.
He traveled with Olivia and several of the new seat holders, passing through the Cursed Land and arriving via teleportation array at the peak of a tall mountain on the Sanctuary's edge.
The sound hit him before anything else.
[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD]
"Zane! Zane! Zane!"
The chant rose from the base of the mountain in waves. He looked down and saw a dense mass of people — hundreds, maybe thousands — all staring up at him, cheering as though a great hero had returned home. Zane stared. He'd never expected this.
Beside him, Olivia smiled, watching the crowd below. She'd known from early on that Zane wasn't from the main continent—his power, his manner, the way he moved through this world like it was never quite his. She'd searched every record and found nothing.
If he left one day without warning—and she was beginning to understand that he would—would she be ready?
Hearing the crowd chant his name again, her expression flickered with something unresolved.
[MUSIC: MAJESTIC ORCHESTRAL]
Three streaks of light cut across the sky. The figure in the lead stood on a flying sword, moving fast and sure. He appeared to be in his early forties, handsome, with the composed bearing of someone who had long since stopped needing to prove anything. Behind him came two older men.
From the energy radiating off all three, Zane placed them immediately: Soul Sea Realm. The leader was Dorian Sinclair, head of the Violet Heart Sanctuary. Olivia had briefed Zane on the way — the two elders flanking him were former sanctuary leaders, Sikong Ming and Li Jiuhai.
Dorian landed at the mountain peak and bowed. "I've long heard of your name, Zane. It's an honor to finally meet you in person. You are every bit the hero they say."
"You flatter me," Zane said with a smile.
"You've come a long way. Please — join us in the main hall."
[AMBIENT: SANCTUARY GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: GRAND CEREMONIAL]
Once that was all settled, Zane was escorted into the main palace, where he was treated as the distinguished guest he was. The Violet Heart Sanctuary was built on a broad, gentle hillside paved entirely in white jade — it looked, from a distance, like a celestial palace suspended in the clouds. Flying was forbidden in the central grounds, but Dorian led the group through without restriction.
One thing struck Zane as he passed through: almost everyone he saw was a warrior. There were no ordinary people here. On the main continent, where so many races competed for survival, the weak had very little room to exist. The Human race had learned that early. Everyone in the Violet Heart Sanctuary had learned it too.
Every human he passed looked at him with a mixture of awe and profound respect. Being hosted by warriors from the Soul Sea Realm was a massive honor, but this wasn't his first time. The reception at the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary had been even more over-the-top.
[MUSIC: INSPIRATIONAL STRINGS]
On both sides of the path leading to the main hall, the younger generation of human talents had gathered to watch. Their cultivation levels weren't high, but they were clearly gifted — the kind the Sanctuary screened for every year and raised carefully. Once they reached War General rank, they'd be sent to the Tower. But with the humans' new standing in the Tower, that threshold had shifted. War Fighters could go now. With twenty-two permanent seats behind them, no one had to worry about them being cut down carelessly.
[SFX: CROWD WHISPERS]
The whispers started as Zane passed.
"That's him. I saw his name on the signaling token."
"A War General who beat War Lords from every race—"
"Do you think Senior Olivia will marry him?"
Olivia's face went red. The words had carried. Dorian Sinclair and the two elders caught it too, and allowed themselves a faint smile. Warriors understood that some things came down to fate.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FORESHADOWING]
Among them was a fourteen-year-old kid. As he watched Zane fly past, his face flushed, and his eyes lit up with a strange fire. He didn’t say a word, but in that moment, his warrior’s spirit ignited into a fierce, burning obsession.
One day, he screamed in his mind, I’m going to be just like him!
And a few years later, he actually did it. He became the second person in the Tower of Sin to be recognized by the Demi-Immortal Race, his destiny forever intertwined with the man who had inspired him that day. But that’s a story for another time.

Soon after, Zane was escorted into the main palace, where he was treated as the distinguished guest he was. Every human he passed looked at him with a mixture of awe and profound respect. Being hosted by warriors from the Soul Sea Realm was a massive honor, but this wasn’t his first time. The reception at the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary had been even more over-the-top.
[AMBIENT: QUIET CHAMBER]
Once they were settled, he and Dorian began discussing the future of the Tower of Sin.
Dorian had no complaints about the arrangements Zane had put in place. With permanent seats now secured, the humans no longer had to live in fear of losing control of the Tower. Better yet, Zane had even managed to pull the Demon Night into an alliance with them.
“Zane, I’m guessing you’re planning to leave the main continent, aren’t you?” Dorian asked, his tone knowing.
It wasn’t a difficult conclusion to reach. As long as Zane remained in the Tower of Sin, no one could challenge him. The elaborate protections and alliances he’d forged were clearly designed to safeguard the humans after he was gone.
“You’re right,” Zane admitted with a simple nod. “I’ll probably have to leave after I explore the Dark Nether Cave. That’s why I’m here, actually. I need you to tell me its secrets.”
