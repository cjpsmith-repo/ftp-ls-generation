Ep 333 - Death of a Monster
Word count: 1027

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Zane had brushed aside Bryson's flying sword attack like it was nothing, using only his bare hands. He’d left Bryson so battered and broken that the members of the Lowell family could only stare, their shock quickly turning to awe.
They’d respected Zane before, sure, but after the All Peaks Competition, none of them actually believed he could take on a master from the Soul Realm. He was just a Level Two Adept, after all.
But that was then. Now, he was a Level Three, and he was facing the Blood Prisoner—a wanted criminal from the Soul Realm, a monster hunted by both the imperial authorities and the Skyreach Sect.
What happened next was something no one would have believed if they hadn’t seen it with their own eyes.
[SFX: WRITHING BLOOD TENDRILS]
The blood streaks the Prisoner unleashed wrapped around Zane’s arm, writhing like a thousand worms trying to burrow into his skin. Each tendril pulsed with the Prisoner’s refined blood and a potent Malice, a force strong enough to tear through an iron plate inches thick. Anyone else caught in those ropes of blood would have their flesh and life force drained away instantly, leaving behind nothing but a shriveled, dried-up skeleton.
"Scream, boy! Let me hear you cry!" the Blood Prisoner shrieked, his skinless face twisting into a merciless grin.
But as the tendrils constricted, the Blood Prisoner felt a jolt of shock. His blood streaks, the ones he thought were invincible, couldn’t even break the kid’s skin. How is that possible? He was completely baffled. 
Before the Blood Prisoner could process it, Zane clenched his fist around the tendrils and wrenched his arm downward.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
The Blood Prisoner crashed into the ground with devastating force, carving a human-shaped crater into the stone. A wave of blood-stench rolled outward from the hole.
For Zane, however, killing a creature like this would let him harvest more Malice. He had no time for words. In a blur of motion, he took an unorthodox step, brought the Streamer up, and swung it down in a clean arc, aiming to end it right there.
[SFX: SWORD SLICING AIR]
"Die!" The Streamer’s edge flashed silver as it sliced through the air.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
"Useless," the Blood Prisoner sneered, throwing up an arm. Layers of blood crystals, like jagged red emeralds, erupted from his flesh to form a thick armor. Each crystal pulsed with its own dark Malice, creating a nearly impenetrable defense.
[SFX: CRYSTAL ARMOR FORMING]
Crack! Zane’s blade shattered a handful of the crystals but ground to a halt against the dense armor.
A blood-red light erupted from the Blood Prisoner's body as he hauled himself upright. His entire form was bathed in it — face, hands, chest — until only two red eyes were visible. He was no longer quite human.
[SFX: DARK POWER SURGE]
His aura spiked sharply. Whatever he'd been before, he was stronger now. Zane noted it with mild surprise. The path of Slaughter really was a shortcut.
The Blood Prisoner sneered. "You’ve got some surprising power, kid, but you’re still just my next meal!"
[SFX: FLESH TEARING]
With a sickening tear of flesh, six blood-red bone spikes shot out from the Prisoner’s body. They were several feet long, like grotesque spider legs, and they lunged for Zane.
"Become a part of my blood and flesh!" the Blood Prisoner roared, laughing wildly as the six bone spikes closed in.
[AMBIENT: MYSTERIOUS PRESENCE]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL FEMALE]
Just then, a beautiful young girl floated into the room and asked casually, "Zane, need some help?"
The Blood Prisoner hadn’t sensed her at all. *When did she get here?
Was she here the whole time? How could I not have noticed her? No... why couldn’t I notice her?* The chilling realization hit him: she had to be from the Soul Realm. But who was she?
He might have been taken aback by Zane’s strength, but the kid was still just an Adept. He couldn’t fly. That was the Blood Prisoner’s ultimate advantage, the reason he was so confident he’d win. But with another master from the Soul Realm in the mix? Especially one who felt... leagues more powerful than him? The Blood Prisoner’s confidence evaporated.
"No thanks, sis. I got this. I’m gonna end this now," Zane replied, a calm smile on his face.
What does that even mean? The Blood Prisoner was baffled. Did the kid seriously think he could win this on his own? His tone... it was like he thought he could kill him at any second. Was he that arrogant?
[SFX: BLADE SLICING THROUGH AIR]
Before the Blood Prisoner could process the absurdity of it all, another silver glint shot from Zane’s hand, heading straight for his face. It was the broken throwing blade. It moved so fast it was just a blur, catching him completely by surprise.
[SFX: BLADE IMPACT WITH FLESH]
He tried to dodge, to throw up a defense, but it was too late. 
The blade was fast — but what stopped him was something else entirely. A silent spike of force drove into his mind, freezing his thoughts for just an instant.
An agonizing pain erupted in his neck as the blade bit deep.
"Take the three wisps of Malice to the Killing Sword Mountain. Keep them there," Zane commanded in his mind. Instantly, the wisps vanished from his body and reappeared on the distant mountain. He felt a new current of energy surge through him.
So it’s that easy to store Malice on the Killing Sword Mountain, he thought. I wonder what the limit is.
[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]
While Zane was lost in thought, a figure at the edge of the room made a break for it. Owen.
He couldn’t process what he’d just seen. Zane had killed the Blood Prisoner... just like that. This was the same monster who’d evaded the best fighters from the imperial authorities and the Skyreach Sect time and time again. A man everyone considered invincible in this part of the world.
And now he was dead, his neck sliced open in a single, terrifying instant. A chill shot down Owen’s spine. Run. It was the only thought in his head.
[SFX: QUICK MATERIALIZATION]
But he’d only managed a few steps before the young girl materialized in front of him, blocking his path with a wide, unsettling smile.
"Uncle," Elara asked, her voice deceptively sweet, "where are you off to?"
