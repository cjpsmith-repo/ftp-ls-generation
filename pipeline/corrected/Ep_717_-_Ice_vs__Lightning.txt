Ep 717 - Ice vs. Lightning
Word count: 1028
Zane stood at the jagged hole torn through the roof of the ice palace, watching the lightning dragons swirl in the dark sky above. The Lesser Celestial Tribulation had begun.
[AMBIENT: THUNDER AND WIND]
[SFX: CRACKLING ELECTRICITY]
The lightning dragons circled in the air, their forms crackling with power as they descended from the sky.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
They felt real, alive, and they unleashed a terrifying pressure that swallowed the entire Cloud Hall.
[SFX: CROWD MURMUR PANIC]
Still reeling from a recent internal conflict, the warriors of the Cloud Hall were terrified. The immense aura sweeping over them made it feel as if the world itself were collapsing.
[SFX: GASPS AND WORRIED VOICES]
A panicked murmur rippled through the crowd. “Our leader is facing her life-or-death tribulation!”
“I wonder if she can make it through.”
“If she breaks through to the Life-and-Death Realm, this will be a massive celebration for Cloud Hall!”
[MUSIC: EPIC DRAMATIC]
Reaching the Life-and-Death Realm wasn’t just a step up; it was the line that separated the truly powerful from ordinary warriors across The Heartlands. Plenty of third-tier sects had protectors in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, but warriors from the Life-and-Death Realm were practically legends.
Any clan or sect that had one could instantly ascend to a Fourth-Grade sect—a status held by only a few in The Heartlands, like the Cloud Hall itself, the Bloodwood Cliff, and the Mysterious Luna Hall. The Bloodwood Cliff once had such a master, but he’d died during his own tribulation, and the sect had been struggling to train a successor ever since. A single warrior at that level could change everything. After all, a sect's rank was ultimately measured by its power, and there was no more direct measure of that than its warriors.
And the stakes extended well beyond Cloud Hall's walls. In Cloudhaven, the rebellion had shaken the third-rank founding families more than the hall itself — most of Cloud Hall's own members had stayed neutral, but the families were deeply entangled. They couldn't simply flee; their ties to the hall ran too deep, and the chaos outside made it more dangerous to leave than to stay.
Everyone understood that once Tia survived her tribulation, there would be a reckoning. She wouldn't dismantle the families outright — the damage to Cloud Hall would be too great — but the active rebels would be purged without mercy. The families left standing were quietly doing the math.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Zane’s expression was grim as he stared up at the descending lightning dragons, his mind racing. They looked terrifying. He couldn't see how Tia stood a chance.
While he was lost in worry, Lavender seemed completely unfazed, as if this were just another Tuesday. Seeing the tension etched on his face, she offered a small smile. "Don't worry so much," she said. "This was her decision. She must be prepared."
Zane just gave a tight nod, his gaze snapping back to Tia.
Against the swirling chaos, her small frame looked impossibly fragile.
When the lightning dragons were only ten feet above her head, Tia stretched out a pale hand. A small crystal card appeared in her palm.
[SFX: ICE PHOENIXES ERUPTING]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
She gently flipped it, and a torrent of ice phoenixes erupted from the card. A blindingly cold light flared, spiraling upward to meet the attack.
“That’s pure Law energy,” Zane breathed, his eyes wide as he tracked the flight of the phoenixes.
[SFX: ENERGY BATTLE]
Each phoenix locked onto a single dragon. Where they met, the two Laws — ice and lightning — poured into each other and dissolved simultaneously, neither overpowering the other. The detonations came in rapid succession, each one sending a shockwave rolling across the hall. The bursts of light were blinding. Zane would later learn that warriors in Cloudhaven had seen the flashes on the horizon and stopped in their tracks — only a handful understood what it meant, but those who did knew the situation in the city was about to be decided.
Wave after wave, the ice phoenixes held. Not a single lightning dragon broke through. When the last one dissolved, Zane let out a slow breath.
"That's it?" he asked.
Lavender shook her head. "Not necessarily. Every tribulation is different. Some warriors face one wave, others face several. The rule is simple — the stronger the warrior, the stronger the tribulation." She paused. "And it isn't punishment. The heavens recognize a threat the moment someone nears the Life-and-Death Realm. Opening a Soul Sea means the power to genuinely threaten the natural order. The tribulation exists to eliminate that threat before it fully forms."
Zane glanced at her. "And Tia's technique makes her a bigger threat."
"It makes her tribulation considerably worse than most," Lavender said simply. "Which is why what you just saw is unlikely to be the end of it."
[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]
Just like that, the lightning dragons were gone, disintegrated by the ice. A new surge of power began to pulse across the sky, and the dark clouds overhead slowly bled from black to a deep crimson, as if a colossal fire were roiling within them.
[SFX: HEAT WAVE]
[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]
A wave of blistering heat washed over the area, and a fresh ripple of alarm went through the crowd.
[AMBIENT: ROARING FLAMES]
[SFX: CRACKLING FIRE]
“Will those flames burn down the entire Cloud Hall?” someone yelled.
“She shouldn’t be doing this here!” another warrior shouted. “This Celestial Tribulation is too powerful! Even if she succeeds, the hall will be destroyed!”
“And where else should she do it?” a third shot back. “The vultures from the other factions are circling. If the Corrupted Divine Kingdom got to her, she’d fail for sure. Even weaker fighters would be enough to distract her at a critical moment.”
[SFX: ENERGY SHIELDS ACTIVATING]
All around the hall, warriors activated their Vitality, raising thin shields to block the searing heat. The power of the Inferno Cloud was aimed entirely at Tia; what they felt was just the runoff, and it was almost unbearable.
This time, Zane said nothing. Lavender was right. He had completely underestimated Tia. He’d been gone for two years; he had no idea what she’d been through, what she’d become.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
[SFX: RUMBLING CLOUDS]
The crimson cloud began to churn, rolling downward to swallow her whole.
But Tia's expression remained perfectly calm.
[SFX: CRYSTAL ACTIVATION]
[SFX: PALACE TREMBLING]
With a flick of her finger, she activated the crystal card again. As she pointed toward the sky, the entire ice palace around them began to tremble.
