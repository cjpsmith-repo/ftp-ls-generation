Ep 1138 - The Endless Abyss

Word count: 1086

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Yosef stared, his mind refusing to process what he was seeing. How was this kid—a warrior who’d barely even touched the Soul Sea Realm—breaking free from his hold? 
[SFX: CRUSHING WEIGHT PRESSURE]
His binding technique wasn’t flashy, but it was supposed to be like a giant crushing an insect. But this “insect,” it seemed, was a lot tougher than he’d anticipated.
An invisible, crushing weight pressed down on Zane from all sides, but he fought it. 
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Gritting his teeth, he forced one heavy foot in front of the other, each step a battle as he trudged toward the entrance to the Underground Palace.
Yosef wasn’t the only one stunned. The other Saint Lords, from both the Demon Night and the human factions, watched with open disbelief. Clearly, none of them had seen this coming.
“Well now,” Yosef sneered, a cruel smile twisting his lips. He wasn’t about to be shown up by a child. “This is interesting.” 
[SFX: PRESSURE INTENSIFYING WHOOSH]
He raised his hand again and jabbed a thick finger in Zane’s direction, doubling the pressure.
[SFX: PYTHON CONSTRICTING]
The force slammed into Zane, heavier this time, wrapping around him like a python. Yosef was focusing everything on him, and Zane felt his muscles screaming in protest as the weight intensified. He squeezed his eyes shut for a moment, centering himself. 
[SFX: DEEP BREATH INHALE]
[SFX: DRAGON SCALES RESONATING]
With a single deep breath, he unleashed the power of ten thousand dragon scales coiled within him.
[SFX: WOOD SPLITTING CRACK]
Zane paused mid-stride, and the air filled with a sharp, crackling sound, like a tree splitting in a thunderstorm. To the onlookers, it seemed as if he was physically expanding, his very frame straining against the invisible bonds.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
“What?” Blood rushed to Yosef’s face, his expression contorting with rage. This was ridiculous. A kid who’d just reached the Soul Sea Realm was actually overpowering his technique. 
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: PALM THRUST WHOOSH]
Furious, he abandoned all pretense of a test and lunged forward, thrusting his entire palm toward Zane’s head.
“Yosef, know your place,” the human Saint Lord called out, his voice calm but firm. “You’re a Saint Lord. It’s beneath you to bully a kid.”
He sounded almost bored, as if he couldn’t be bothered with Yosef’s petty display of pride. 
[SFX: MAGICAL FORCE DISSIPATING]
With a casual wave of his hand, the immense force Yosef had aimed at Zane simply vanished.
[SFX: PRESSURE RELEASE WHOOSH]
In the next instant, the crushing weight around Zane disappeared. Then, with a flick of his wrist, the human Saint Lord released the pressure that had been pinning down Lincoln and Freya as well.
The Saint Lord’s words were like ice water on Yosef’s boiling anger, snapping him back to his senses. He forced a chuckle, trying to save face.
“I wasn’t competing with him,” he said, his voice a low grumble. “I just found him… intriguing.”
With nothing left to stop them, Zane led the way toward the palace entrance. There was a dark yellow barrier stretched across the opening — ancient, but still holding. Zane walked straight through it without slowing. Lincoln followed, then Freya.
From nearby, Adrian had been watching the whole thing with sharp eyes. He understood the moment perfectly — if he didn’t move now, under the gaze of three Saint Lords, he wouldn’t get another chance. He raised his voice. “Brother Lincoln — wait for me!”
No one stopped him. He fell into step behind Freya.
Abner watched Adrian go and didn’t hesitate. Whatever had happened to his brothers, standing still wasn’t going to help anyone. He followed.
Then came the three factions’ fighters, pouring in behind them.
The moment Zane crossed the barrier, the ground disappeared.
[AMBIENT: WHISTLING WIND DESCENT]
There was nothing beneath his feet — just open air and a bottomless dark rushing up to meet him. He was falling.
He wasn’t alone. Every single person who had stepped through that barrier was falling with him — Lincoln, Freya, Adrian, Abner, and behind them, the warriors of all three factions, their voices rising in alarm.
Instinct took over — Vitality, the Law of Wind, every trick they had. Nothing worked.
Zane went further. He reached for the Law of Space, intending to fold the distance and teleport himself and Freya to stable ground. 
[SFX: SPATIAL DISTORTION WARBLE]
The space bent — he felt it respond — but the moment the technique activated, they dropped faster. Using Space Law in the abyss didn’t teleport them. It accelerated the fall.
He let it go.
“Lavender,” he said. “What is this place?”
There was a pause — and then, unexpectedly, Lavender laughed. It was a rare sound, brief and genuinely amused. “I just wanted to give you a surprise,” she said. “Welcome to the Endless Abyss. You can’t fly here. You can’t teleport. You can only fall.”
The group continued their descent, the wind whistling past their ears as they plunged into the darkness.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS SUSTAINED STRINGS]
[SFX: DISTANT CONFUSED SHOUTING]
Above them, Zane could hear the voices of the three factions’ warriors — the same confusion, the same failed attempts to halt the fall. No one had found a solution. Everyone was dropping at the same speed into the same dark.
After a while, the panic settled into something grimmer. No one knew what waited at the bottom.
As they fell, Zane scanned the sheer, endless walls of the abyss. He couldn’t begin to imagine what kind of ancient power the Supreme Lord had used to carve out such a place, or why he would even bother. One thing was certain, though: whoever the Creator was, he had gone to incredible lengths to build this fortress of shadows. When his own thoughts hit a dead end, he turned to the one person who might have a clue.
“I don’t know,” Lavender answered simply. “The first time I explored this abyss was about two and a half million years ago, and my own power was only at the Spirit Supreme Realm back then. I’m familiar with parts of the Underground Palace, but I never uncovered all its secrets. The Supreme Lord’s origins are a complete mystery. To this day, no one has figured out who he really was. It’s possible he built this place as his own tomb, a way to choose a worthy successor. But that’s just a guess.”
[SFX: SHARP WARNING VOICE]
“Be careful,” Lavender warned, her voice sharp. “If I remember correctly, the first trial of the Endless Abyss should be coming up right about now.”
Zane’s blood ran cold. He looked down into the blackness.
[MUSIC: RISING DREAD]
[AMBIENT: RUSTLING VINES]
Below them, a pit was coming into view, choked with writhing, dark-green vines. Each one was as thick as a man’s arm and covered in razor-sharp spikes.
They were only a few thousand feet away. 
[SFX: ACCELERATING WIND RUSH]
At the rate they were falling, they were about to be impaled.
