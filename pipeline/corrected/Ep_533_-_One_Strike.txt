Ep 533 - One Strike
Word count: 1042
[AMBIENT: EERIE TOMB WIND]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The moment they stepped inside the celestial tomb, the team’s already frayed nerves went taut. With everyone’s power suppressed, the mental pressure was crushing, and the cold wind howling from the tunnel ahead only made the air feel heavier.
This was an immortal’s tomb, after all. Legends said immortals could bend nature to their will. Even in death, a tomb built by such a being was guaranteed to be riddled with traps.
Most of the Inner-Core Realm disciples from Cloud Hall huddled close to the elders, the only place they felt even remotely safe. It’s not that warriors are afraid of death, but they’re terrified of dying for nothing.
Fortunately, Saint Morphen had his automaton scouting point, leading the way. So far, it had tramped through the caves completely unscathed, a reassuring sight for the rest of them.
But if the automaton was fine, why did Elder Imogen suddenly tell everyone to be on guard?
She was a Life-and-Death Realm expert. Even suppressed, her senses were sharper than anyone else’s.
[SFX: SUDDEN WHOOSH]
The words had barely left her lips when a black shadow exploded from the darkness, lunging at the automaton.
Among the Cloud Hall elders, Saint Morphen was easily in the top three, second only to Elder Imogen and Tia, and right on par with Emanuel Drew. He was the one who had puppeteered Zane back in the Adept Realm, single-handedly taking down a whole group of Elite House disciples. His Puppet Technique was masterful; the automaton in his control fought with the strength of an Inner-Core Realm practitioner.
As the shadow attacked, Saint Morphen reacted instantly.
[SFX: METALLIC SLIDE]
With a single thought, a sleek rapier slid from the automaton’s forearm into its right hand. It was a perfectly designed construct; the weapon was concealed within its arm, emerging without so much as a seam showing.
The automaton immediately brought the blade around, slashing viciously at the dark figure.
[SFX: METAL CLASH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Sparks erupted as the rapier—a weapon on par with a Mythic-grade blade—crashed against the shadow. The impact rang out like metal on metal.
Only then, in the flash of steel, did everyone get a clear look at their attacker.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
"A reanimated corpse," Saint Morphen said, his voice tight. "Someone's weaponized the dead."
The corpse moved with a chilling, unnatural life, clearly bound to a master’s will. Corpse Refining was a twisted, forbidden art, turning the dead into puppets for battle. The stronger the warrior in life, the more formidable their reanimated corpse.
The corpse was only pushed back a few steps. Then it reached out two black arms and grabbed hold of the automaton. Saint Morphen's brow furrowed as he fought to regain control — two flying daggers shot from the automaton's chest, and a blade snapped out from its knee, driving hard into the corpse's ribs.
[SFX: METAL CLASH]
The corpse didn't even flinch. Its flesh was like iron. Then, with terrifying force, it drove both hands into the automaton's chest — and tore it apart.
[SFX: SHATTERING METAL]
The pieces clattered to the ground. Saint Morphen's expression darkened.
The corpse didn't pause. It turned and lunged toward the group.
"Must've been one hell of a fighter," one disciple muttered.
No one laughed.
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: HEROIC STRINGS]
Emanuel stepped forward without a word. A long spear appeared in his hand, crackling with lightning. He thrust it once — a single clean strike, fast as a lightning bolt — straight at the corpse's head.
[SFX: POWERFUL SPEAR STRIKE]
[SFX: SHATTERING BONE]
The head exploded. The corpse swayed twice, then collapsed.
In terms of raw attack power, Emanuel was stronger than Saint Morphen. In Cloud Hall, only Elder Imogen and Tia surpassed him.
Though Emanuel had brought it down in a single strike, no one relaxed. Reanimated corpses weren't something you encountered every day — only a handful of sects in the Heartlands even knew the art of creating them. The ones Cloud Hall had faced before were weak enough for an Inner-Core warrior to handle. But this one had required Emanuel's full strength to put down. Whatever else lay ahead in this tomb was going to be worse.
The group pressed on. The tunnel widened — and then they saw it.
[AMBIENT: EERIE TOMB SILENCE]
On both sides of the path, iron fences stretched into the darkness. And behind every fence, reanimated corpses. Rows and rows of them, black hands clawing through the bars, bodies lurching and jumping without cease. Zane counted at least ten to a row — and the rows went back further than he could see. Three, maybe four hundred in total.
"Both sides are full of them," one of the disciples said, his voice barely above a whisper. Even at Inner-Core level, the sight made his skin crawl.
"Don't panic," an elder said quickly. "They're caged. The fences will hold."
The words helped — briefly.
[SFX: SHARP CRACK]
A crisp snapping sound rang out from both sides of the path simultaneously. The iron fences slid back into the walls.
The corpses poured out.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
"Kill them all!" Elder Imogen's voice cut through the chaos. She raised her walking staff and charged.
Emanuel was already moving. His spear flashed three times in quick succession — three bolts of lightning, three corpses dropping headless to the ground.
Saint Morphen pulled on a pair of bronze gloves and waded in. His footwork was uncanny, flickering between the corpses like a shadow, each punch caving in a skull.
The elders held their own. The disciples were another matter.
"Zane, watch out!" a disciple yelled.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]
Another corpse was closing in on him from behind.
Zane spun at the warning, coming face-to-face with the corpse’s snarling, blackened face. Its jaw was locked open in a ferocious grimace as it lunged.
One of the elders protecting the disciples started to move, knowing he couldn't let anything happen to Zane.
But before the elder could even take a step, Zane simply flicked his wrist. His longsword came up horizontal, the tip of the blade humming with an immense power fueled by the five hundred Dragon Scales churning inside him.
It was just one simple move. A flick of the sword.
[SFX: POWERFUL SWORD STRIKE]
[SFX: SHATTERING BONE]
The corpse’s head shattered like porcelain.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
Besides Emanuel and Saint Morphen, no one had been able to do that, not even powerful elders like Toby. The elder who had rushed to save Zane froze mid-stride, his mouth hanging open in disbelief.
The other Inner-Core disciples just stared, their own battles momentarily forgotten.
