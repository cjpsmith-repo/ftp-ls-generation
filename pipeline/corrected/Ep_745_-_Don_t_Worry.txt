Ep 745 - Don't Worry
Word count: 1138
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane’s fists tightened at his sides, but his expression remained a mask of calm. Inside, though, a current of pure excitement was humming through his veins.
Maddox had been a dead man walking ever since Zane’s days with the Skyreach Sect. Even after he saved Elara, Maddox had to go. The man had become a wall blocking his path, and the only way forward was to tear him down.
He’d waited so long for this. And now, finally, it was here.
[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]
[SFX: WHOOSH]
Zane moved with sharp efficiency, taking his position at the edge of the city wall. Without a sound, he dropped over the side, floating down toward the ground below. A strange paradox took hold: the more the thrill surged within him, the colder and calmer his expression became.
As he descended, Tia's voice cut through the tense air. "Zane! Are you really going out there?"
He glanced back over his shoulder, a bright, reassuring smile spreading across his face. "Don't worry!"
"But—" Tia started, her brow creasing with worry.
[MUSIC: ROMANTIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
Zane cut her off. "Don't worry, Tia. I'm not going to die.I'll come back, and when I do, I'm going to marry you."
[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
Though he hadn't shouted, his words carried clearly to the warriors of Cloud Hall. Saint Morphen and a few of the other elders who were in on their relationship shared a knowing smile. But for the Inner-Core Realm warriors who were out of the loop—like Finn and Lewis—the declaration landed like a thunderclap.
Lewis’s eyes shot wide. He nudged Finn with his elbow. "Finn, you heard that, right?" he whispered, his voice laced with disbelief.
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Finn’s eyes remained closed, thin trickles of fresh blood seeping from the corners. He’d been channeling the power of the heavens through his Sword Eye for two straight days—the only way to bring its true power to bear.
"Is it really that strange?" Finn replied, a faint smile touching his lips.
"Of course it is!" Lewis hissed back, flabbergasted by his friend's calm. "How can you be so casual about this?"
Finn just chuckled and shook his head. "For Zane? No, this isn't strange at all."
Meanwhile, Tia’s face had gone bright red, the heat of Zane's public declaration rushing to her cheeks.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
Zane had already turned to face Maddox, his expression placid but his eyes burning with intent. Channeling his Vitality, he projected his voice so every word landed with deliberate weight. "Two years ago, back when I was in the Soul Realm, I gave you a warning," Zane said. "I told you to let Elara go, and I would spare you. You didn't listen. Now, that chance is gone."
Maddox threw his head back and laughed — a wild, contemptuous sound that rolled across the battlefield. "After all this time, nothing's changed. You're still all talk."
His voice dropped into a sneer. "You seem to have forgotten something. When warriors fight, it's not words that matter. It's fists. It's strength. Let's see if you have any."
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
Zane said nothing. He slowly drew his sword from his Storage Ring. The blade emerged with a faint, inner glow. He gave it a gentle wave, and a soft, almost pleasant hum filled the air.
Maddox drew his nameless saber—once the Heartlands' finest blade. Against Zane's weapon, it looked shabby. The value gap was staggering.
[SFX: STARLIGHT HUM]
[MUSIC: CELESTIAL THEME]
Zane took a slow breath. Faint starlight began to gather on the surface of his body — two distinct colors, shimmering and layering over each other, wrapping him in a strange, otherworldly luster as the Stellar Body condensed around him.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
"Look — stars! Two stars on the horizon!"
"It's the middle of the day. How—"
"They're shining on him. He's drawing power directly from the stars!"
In broad daylight, stars don't show. The sun drowns them out. But the two stars Zane had lit — purple and orange — burned with enough force to punch through the daylight, their beams arcing down from hundreds of millions of miles away and landing squarely on him. Every warrior on both sides of the battlefield could see them.
Maddox's eyes narrowed. A flicker of surprise crossed his face — and then hardened into something more careful. What kind of cultivation method was this?
Zane met his eyes. "You're right," he said coldly. "I'll show you what fists are. I'll show you what strength is."
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE MOVEMENT]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Zane didn't waste another second. Years of waiting for this moment culminated in a single, explosive movement. He shot toward Maddox, sword aimed straight for the heart, and bellowed the name of his technique, "Formless Sword!"
To the War General-level warriors watching from the ground, both men vanished the instant they moved — nothing but a blur, two streaks of light crossing and recrossing each other at impossible speed. The Inner-Core warriors fared slightly better, catching only vague impressions of movement. The Spirit-Tribulation masters on both sides watched with tense, unreadable expressions, their eyes tracking every exchange.
In the crowd, Owen stood rigid, jaw clenched, staring at the two lights. He couldn't see them clearly either. But he didn't look away. Zane's victory wasn't just about morale. The woman he loved and Zane's sister shared the same fate. He stared until his eyes ached, trying to find Zane in the blur.
The blade's trajectory seemed slightly off, aiming for the broader chest rather than the heart, but that was the feint. The true target was the heart itself, hidden within the path of the attack.
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLE ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]
Maddox brought his nameless saber up horizontally. He was at a clear disadvantage against a top-grade Relic, and he didn't dare meet the blow head-on.
[SFX: METALLIC CLANG]
With a sharp tap of his saber, Maddox skillfully deflected the thrust, knocking the blade aside.
But just as the blade was pushed away, Zane murmured, "Not going to work."
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
The tip of Zane's sword traced a liquid circle in the air, instantly redirecting its phantom-like thrust back toward Maddox's chest. The Formless Sword wasn't without form at all; it was a technique of Zane's own creation, where a single, simple move contained the potential for a thousand others.
It was an unbreakable attack.
Realization flashed across Maddox's face—there was no dodging this. He broke into a frantic retreat.
[SFX: SWORD ENERGY WHOOSH]
A cold smile touched Zane's lips as he gave his sword the slightest lift. In that instant, the seemingly simple thrust transformed completely. From the tip of his blade, a razor-sharp beam of light—sword radiance—shot forward, extending the attack's reach. The beam flew straight for the retreating Maddox's chest. This was the core of the Formless Sword: a perfect fusion of every basic technique Zane had ever mastered, giving him absolute freedom in his attack.
[SFX: FLESH IMPACT]
[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]
Panic seized Maddox's face as his body went rigid, his retreat cut short. The beam of light sliced deep into his side, and a spray of blood erupted into the air.
