Ep 1003 - The Well

Word count: 1252

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ASCENSION]

A bright, silver light enveloped Zane, pulling him faster and higher with every passing moment.

An invisible force was pushing him away from the Lower World, propelling him toward the boundless sky.

The Lower World had a way of expelling any living being that grew too powerful, but the Celestial Paragon warriors must have used some kind of secret technique to bypass that repulsive force. For Zane, it was like drowning in reverse—an unstoppable pressure shoving him upward, with no way to fight it.

If he ever wanted to return to the Lower World, he’d probably have to master that same technique himself.

He glanced down at the dark continents spread out below. At first, he could just make out the glittering lights of massive cities like Cloudhaven and Oracle City. But as he soared higher, the continents themselves began to shrink.

Soon, he could see the entire Divine Kingdom Continent and the Heartlands in a single, breathtaking view.

[SFX: HOWLING WIND]

[SFX: METALLIC SHRIEK]

The howl of the wind began to mix with a high-pitched metallic shriek, creating a powerful gale that blasted against him.

But these hurricane-force winds couldn’t touch him. The silver light had formed a protective shell around him, and any normal attack would pass right through his body without doing a scratch.

Once he shot through the layer of storming winds, Zane just kept rising.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ASCENSION]

He’d felt something like this before, back when he was mastering the Stellar Body, but that time he’d been using the gravitational pull of the stars to lift his perception. This time, it was his actual, physical body roaming the heavens. This wasn’t his own strength, though. He was just riding the Lower World’s natural repulsion.

As he got farther and farther away, his connection to the world below grew fainter.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

A sudden, chilling thought struck him.

Down in the Lower World, he could instantly transfer any damage he took to the world itself. But could he still do that now that he had left?

That ability had made him invincible. No matter how powerful the attack, it couldn’t truly hurt him, unless it was strong enough to shatter the entire Lower World—and if an attack was that strong, the world itself would be destroyed anyway.

But now that he was gone?

[SFX: POWER FAILURE WHOOSH]

He focused, trying to shift his power, but nothing happened. A bitter smile touched his lips. The transfer didn’t work anymore.

The moment he left, he’d lost the one thing that made him untouchable.

But not everything.

The Memory of the World’s Will was still there — faint, like a candle seen from a great distance, but present. He still had a thread of connection to the Lower World. He couldn’t transfer force into it anymore, but he could still observe it. He could still watch.

[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE MELANCHOLY]

The ascent took a month. Zane drifted through the vast darkness like a leaf on a still river, the universe spread around him in silence. He closed his eyes and let his awareness sink into the Memory.

He found Tia first. She was in the ice palace at Cloud Hall, sitting still, staring at the sky with a distant expression. Something in his chest tightened at the sight.

Isla was easier — head down, cultivating with quiet intensity, her focus unbroken.

But Cora — he searched and searched and found nothing. No trace of her anywhere in the Lower World.

He turned it over for a long time. With her cultivation level, she couldn’t have ascended. There was only one answer: she was already in the Violet Core Realm. The Memory couldn’t reach there.

He thought back — the Demi-Immortal elder’s visit, the way Cora had been evasive afterward, the small things she’d hidden from him. He still didn’t know exactly what she was planning. But he knew her. Whatever she’d decided, she’d decided it completely. There was no point trying to unravel it from here.

He let it go.

[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]

[SFX: RUSHING ACCELERATION]

After a month of stillness, everything changed at once. The silver light around Zane suddenly scattered — and he was plunging, faster than he’d risen, straight into a vast dark opening below him. He had no time to react.

[SFX: DEAFENING THUNDERCLAP]

The impact was like a thousand thunderbolts detonating at once. The sound alone nearly knocked him unconscious — his mind went white, his thoughts dissolved, and for a long moment he simply wasn’t there.

[AMBIENT: STILL WATER]

[MUSIC: QUIET WONDER]

When his awareness came back, he was lying in water.

[SFX: GENTLE WATER RIPPLE]

He found himself submerged in a square well, the water around him clear and faintly glowing. The sensation was immediately, strangely comfortable — like every cell in his body was exhaling at once.

He wasn’t alone. Several other warriors were in the well with him, all wearing the same dazed expression, all clearly trying to piece together what had just happened.

Zane looked them over quietly. They were all Soul Sea Realm — but recently so. New arrivals, their power still unsettled. Compared to the Harlen Emperor or War King Wind, they were a different class entirely. Even the weakest ruler he’d encountered in the Divine Kingdoms would have outmatched any of them.

They’d ascended the moment the passage opened, he guessed. Warriors who’d just crossed the threshold and immediately taken the chance to leave. It made sense.

What was less clear was what any of them made of him — a Life-and-Death Realm warrior sitting in the same well.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]

A plainly dressed girl spoke first. “I’ve heard some of the sacred orders in the Upper World use something called an Ascending Well to pull in warriors from across the universe. I think this is one. I just don’t know which human sacred order we’ve ended up in.”

[AMBIENT: TENSE GATHERING]

“Does it have to be a human one? What if it belongs to another race?” a bearded man asked, raising an eyebrow.

“It shouldn’t be,” the plainly dressed girl answered. “There’s supposed to be a mysterious link between members of the same race. But… I have heard of warriors ascending right into another race’s supreme world. For example, if a human sacred order was just conquered by the Ogres and we happened to ascend right then, I’m afraid…” She trailed off.

“Afraid of what?” a tall, thin warrior prompted.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The girl offered a faint, grim smile. “If we fell into the hands of the Ogres, we wouldn’t stand a chance.”

[SFX: HEAVY SILENCE]

Her words hit the others like a physical blow.

[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE MELANCHOLY]

Back in the Lower World, they were all legendary experts who had reached the Soul Sea Realm. They had eagerly left everything behind, driven by ambition and a desperate need to reach higher realms, to chase the very pinnacle of the martial path.

But to ascend to the wrong place? To fall into the hands of the Ogres or some other cruel race?

They would be doomed before their new lives could even begin. The fear was palpable—the terror of dying before they had the chance to accomplish anything at all.

Seeing their expressions, the girl smiled. “Don’t worry. It rarely happens. Our luck can’t be that bad.”

[MUSIC: CURIOUS TENSION]

As she spoke, her gaze landed on Zane. How could he only be at the Life-and-Death Realm?
