Ep 1250 - That Zane

Word count: 1036

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
[AMBIENT: QUIET FOREST CLEARING]
Zane didn’t need to ask which “Zane” Aurora meant — the answer was written all over Amber’s face.
He wouldn’t hide anything from her. He offered a faint, knowing smile and nodded.
Just then, Rowan and the other Demon Night warriors came up behind them. Rowan was still reeling from what Aurora had said.
*Zane is… *that* Zane?*
The very same human warrior who had tricked and killed Supreme Lord Gunter. Rowan’s family, a lesser branch of the Demon Night, had been part of the ruthless hunt for him six years ago, back when Maverick was their leader. It was hard to say who was right or wrong in that conflict, but standing face-to-face with the legendary Zane was enough to leave her speechless. 
Still, if he was Amber’s friend, he could probably be trusted. The thought brought a wave of relief to both Rowan and Aurora.
A light returned to Aurora’s eyes, and a genuine smile spread across her face. “I had no idea you and Amber were friends. I’m so sorry for how I acted before. And thank you for helping us.”
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
With a flick of her wrist, a small, black tree materialized in her hand. It stood only four feet high, its branches as thin as her pinky finger and completely bare.
[SFX: RUSTLING LEAVES GROWING]
As she presented it, the branches began to tremble, and in a magical rush, green leaves unfurled from the wood. In the blink of an eye, the barren tree was lush and full. 
[SFX: MAGICAL VANISH]
She made it vanish as quickly as it had appeared and turned her focus back to Amber, who was hesitating.
“You two finally get to see each other again,” Aurora said with a brilliant smile, gently nudging the conversation forward. “You must have a lot to talk about. The thing is, Amber is in a real bind right now.”
“A bind?” Zane’s gaze snapped to Amber.
Amber immediately looked awkward, her composure faltering. 
Back in that prison, all she could think about was getting a message to him. Running into Zane now felt like destiny, but standing here in front of him, her mind went completely blank. 
Aurora must have sensed her panic, because she decided to speak for her. “That’s right,” Aurora said, her voice dropping. “She’s been imprisoned and sentenced to the punishment of corrosive decay. She’s forced to endure a constant, corrosive rainfall that slowly eats away at her body. Day after day, it rots her eyes, festers her skin, and corrodes her very muscles and bones.”
[MUSIC: BITTERSWEET REFLECTION]
Amber’s face had gone pale just hearing it laid out plainly. She’d endured this for seven years. Most cultivators broke within three; even the strongest-willed usually collapsed by five. 
Her time in the Illusion Battlefield, strange as it sounded, had been the happiest stretch of Amber’s life in years. Here, she’d made real friends — Aurora, the rest of the Demon Night around her, people who saw her as a warrior instead of a prisoner. Here, she got to test her own strength against real opponents, on her own terms. But that would all end eventually. She’d go back to the punishment waiting for her, and just thinking about it made her shudder.
Zane’s expression darkened, a cold light flickering in his eyes. “Because of that bitch of a Queen? A shame I didn’t finish her off when I had the chance.”
[AMBIENT: SURPRISED MURMURS]
A ripple of shock ran through the surrounding Demon Night cultivators — a few faces went openly hostile. To most of them, the Queen wasn’t just a ruler; she was untouchable, someone they’d give their lives to defend without a second thought.
It wasn’t blind loyalty, either. The Demon Night’s three Queens drew real strength from their people’s devotion — every ounce of faith directed at them fed a genuine power source, a faith pool that made each of them formidable in her own right. Insulting one wasn’t just disrespect. It was an attack on something the average Demon Night warrior had a personal hand in building.
Aurora, though, stayed calm. Her mother sat among the elders, and she knew better than most that the Queen’s own upper ranks were far from unified behind her. She said nothing to stop the reaction — and without her leading the charge, the rest of the Demon Night held their tongues, even if their expressions stayed tight with disapproval.
[MUSIC: TENSE DETERMINATION]
The only way to save her would be to threaten the Demon Night directly, but most races would never risk an all-out war with them. In the entire universe, Zane was perhaps the only one who could pull it off. After all, he’d already killed four powerful Supreme Lords.
The Demon Night warriors bristled at Zane’s presence, their anger palpable. But since their leader, Aurora, was staying quiet, they held their tongues. 
Their disbelief, however, was impossible to contain. A Soul Sea Realm warrior claiming he could kill their queen in just three years? It was beyond reckless. The gap between the Soul Sea Realm and the realm of World Lords was colossal, with the Spirit Supreme and Spirit Transformation realms separating them. Some warriors spent hundreds of thousands, even millions, of years just to cross that chasm. 
Zane turned from the crowd and looked directly at Amber, his voice steady. “Three years. Give me three years, and I’ll kill the Queen and get you out.”
[MUSIC: FRAGILE HOPE]
“I can endure for three years,” Amber said, her voice firm. A flicker of hope ignited in her eyes.
She didn’t know if he could actually do it, but he had given her a reason to hold on. Back on the main continent, Zane had pulled off one impossible feat after another. He was a miracle worker. There was no one else like him.
Seeing that desperate hope on her face, Zane felt a sharp pang of guilt. This was all his fault—his and Lavender’s. He wasn’t some savior, and he couldn’t rescue everyone from their suffering. 
But he would never, ever stand by and do nothing when someone he cared about was in trouble.
