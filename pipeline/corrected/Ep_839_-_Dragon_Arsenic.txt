Ep 839 - Dragon Arsenic

Word count: 1003

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[SFX: SLOW WHOOSHING]

Arrows are supposed to be fast—projectiles that move like lightning. The ones drifting toward Zane and his group, however, were the exact opposite.

“They seem to be bound by the Time Law,” Camilla observed, her eyes tracking one of the impossibly slow bolts. “That’s why they’re crawling.”

Renzo couldn’t help but laugh. “They’re moving slower than a snail. How are those supposed to hit anything?”

But Randy was quick to shut him down. “Don’t underestimate them. They’re tipped with Dragon Arsenic. It’s a poison that can kill a Soul Sea warrior, and I’ve heard of plenty of Life-and-Death masters who’ve died to it.”

Renzo scoffed. “The poison might be nasty, sure, but a Life-and-Death master? Come on. They’re so slow that an old man could sidestep them. How could anyone at that level possibly get hit?”

Randy just shook his head at the dismissal. “These are defensive measures left by the dragon-blooded. I’m just saying, better safe than sorry. You’ll see soon enough why we need to be on guard.”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The grim finality in Randy’s voice made it clear he wasn’t kidding. Renzo was still skeptical, but he decided to take the warning to heart. It was, after all, better to be safe than sorry.

[AMBIENT: DENSE FOREST]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

With Randy taking the lead, the five of them pushed on toward the forest.

At first, it was almost laughably easy. The arrows drifted in from the front, maybe three to five at a time, and all it took was a careful sidestep to avoid them. But as they ventured deeper into the woods, they finally understood what Randy had meant. It wasn’t the speed that was the problem; it was the sheer, overwhelming number.

Soon, twenty arrows were floating at them at once, coming from all directions. Sometimes they would form a solid wall, completely blocking the path ahead and forcing everyone to either fly over or crawl underneath. 

Renzo's patience ran out.

[SFX: BLADE DRAWN]

One arrow drifted lazily into his path, and he'd had enough. He pulled his dagger and slashed at it.

“Don't—!” Randy shouted.

Too late.

[SFX: ARROW WHOOSH]

The moment the blade made contact, the arrow shuddered — and then shot forward at blinding speed, straight at Renzo's chest.

[SFX: SHARP IMPACT]

Renzo froze. He hadn't even processed what was happening when Zane's palm cracked into his shoulder, twisting him sideways. The arrow tore through his clothes and out the other side — missing flesh by a hair.

[SFX: HISSING SOUND]

[MUSIC: URGENT TENSION]

“Get his clothes off — now!” Randy barked.

A faint blue light was already spreading across the fabric where the arrow had grazed it. Dragon Arsenic didn't behave like ordinary poison. It moved through cloth as if the cloth were skin.

Renzo's hands were shaking. He reached for his collar.

[SFX: FABRIC TEARING]

Zane didn't wait. He drove his palm forward, and the force shredded the contaminated clothing clean off Renzo, scattering it into the wind before the poison could spread.

Randy let out a long breath and reached into his Storage Ring, pulling out a spare set of clothes and tossing them to Renzo. “Put these on. If it weren't for Zane, you'd be dead.”

Renzo stood very still for a moment, the reality of what had just happened settling over him.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED DRUMS]

After that, nobody said a word about the arrows being slow.

Camilla, Barry, and Renzo all shifted — not obviously, but noticeably — closer to Zane. None of them spoke about it. They just moved.

Zane didn't mind. The Dragon Arsenic was the real threat in this zone, and his body was immune to poison. The arrows themselves couldn't hurt him. For the others, this place was genuinely dangerous. For him, it was a question of navigation.

“Don't be careless,” Randy said. “The deeper we go, the worse it gets. As we go deeper, there will be hundreds at a time. Slow as they are, at that density, it becomes nearly impossible to avoid them all. One brush is all it takes.”

[AMBIENT: FOREST WITH ARROWS WHISTLING]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

“I'll take the front,” Zane said, turning to Randy.

Randy nodded without hesitation.

[SFX: MULTIPLE ARROW WHOOSHES]

Further in, the arrows came in hundreds at a time — evenly spaced, floating in formation, pressing forward like a slow-moving wall. But there was always a gap. A single narrow gap.

[MUSIC: PRECISE TENSION]

Randy spotted it and called the formation. One by one, they turned sideways and filed through — arrows inches from their faces, blue light flickering on all sides. Nobody breathed until they were clear.

Then, from somewhere off to their left, a scream cut through the forest.

[SFX: AGONIZED CRY]

Zane looked. A warrior from another team, one of the Star Field Divine Kingdom's geniuses, had taken an arrow in the shoulder. The blue light spread across his skin instantly. His flesh began to corrugate and dissolve, boiling from the inside out. In moments, there was nothing left.

[MUSIC: HORROR CRESCENDO]

Camilla had gone pale. They'd been warned. But being warned and watching it happen were two different things.

The dead warrior's team didn't linger. They'd all accepted the risk when they entered the Sacred Feather Sea. They moved on.

“Don't look,” Zane said to Camilla. “Keep moving.”

[AMBIENT: FOREST WITH ARROWS WHISTLING]

They were just about to break free from the treeline when several figures suddenly appeared, blocking their path.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STOPPING ABRUPTLY]

“Fredrick!” Randy frowned, recognizing the crown prince of the York Divine Kingdom. They shouldn’t be here. By his calculations, Fredrick’s group was way ahead of them and should have already cleared the Slow Arrow Field. Had they turned back?

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

“There’s no way through,” Fredrick declared, his voice cold and flat.

“What are you talking about?” Randy had been to this island before; he knew this was the right path.

“See for yourself,” Fredrick said, gesturing helplessly behind him.
