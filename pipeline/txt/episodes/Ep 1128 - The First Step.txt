Ep 1128 - The First Step

Word count: 1115

[MUSIC: MAJESTIC POWERFUL]
The figure standing beside Zane was one every Demon Night warrior knew on sight — and the shock of seeing her here, in the flesh, stopped them cold.
Every member of the Demon Night knew what Lavender looked and smelled like. In a way, they knew her better than their own parents.
[SFX: RITUAL CHANTING]
From birth, they were all baptized before the statues of the three queens. The Queen of Life strengthened their bodies, the Queen of Punishment taught them obedience, and the Queen of Slaughter—their Lavender—steeled their courage.
[SFX: SPEAR HUMMING WITH ENERGY]
Her soul had recovered sixty percent of its power, and the Nightshard Spear in her hand hummed with a dangerous, majestic energy. 
[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASPS OF AWE]
When the Demon Night warriors of the Oaken Pavilion saw her, a tremor of pure shock and awe ran through them. They knew, instantly, that their queen had truly come for them.
The Dark World was a backwater region, a crossroads where countless races mingled. The entire realm was little more than a massive trading hub. And while humans, ogres, and the Demon Night had established strongholds here, they were still considered minor factions in the grand scheme of things.
A personal visit from any queen was an unimaginable honor. To the Demon Night, a queen was more important than any Supreme Lord.
[SFX: ARMOR RUSTLING]
[SFX: WARRIORS KNEELING IN UNISON]
A wave of movement rippled through the hall as every last member dropped to their knees, their heads bowed in deference to their queen.
Floating above them, Lavender’s gaze swept over her subjects before finally landing on the woman in pink. 
[SFX: COMMANDING ETHEREAL VOICE]
“What’s your name?”
“You can call me Nyla, My Queen,” the woman answered, her voice thick with reverence.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Just a few months ago, word had spread like wildfire: the Queen of Slaughter had returned to the Upper World and immediately clashed with the Queen of Punishment. Lavender had used one of Zane’s Roaring Tokens to broadcast their entire argument for all to hear. 
It wasn’t just a private matter for the Demon Night anymore; everyone knew. The other races, the ones who hated the Demon Night, were practically licking their lips, waiting for the inevitable civil war. Lavender’s return was a match dropped in a powder keg.
Lavender gave a cold, sharp nod. 
[SFX: COMMANDING VOICE]
“Nyla, I want all of you to swear an oath of allegiance to me. Right here. In front of that statue.”
[AMBIENT: UNEASY MURMURS]
At her command, Nyla and her subordinates lifted their heads, their unease written on every face. What Lavender was asking of them was an impossible choice. They had three queens, not one. Swearing allegiance to her alone was a line they’d never thought they’d be asked to cross.
And the risks were real. Lavender’s former followers had scattered — most were dead or in hiding. The Queen of Punishment had consolidated her power in the time Lavender was gone. Anyone who sided with Lavender now was a target.
Zane sat on a railing at the edge of the hall, his face giving nothing away. But his jaw was tight. He had promised to help Lavender reclaim her throne, and this moment — getting her first followers to swear — was where that promise would either hold or break. If Nyla refused, Lavender’s whereabouts would reach the Queen of Punishment before nightfall. With his current strength, he couldn’t stop what would come next.
This was a risk they had no choice but to take.
The silence stretched.
Lavender let it. Her soul pressure settled over the room — not violent, but absolute, the weight of a sovereign reminding her people what she was. When she finally spoke, her voice was quiet and precise.
“Is it so difficult? Or do you no longer trust your Queen of Slaughter?”
The words landed like a blade. Nyla’s face went pale. Around her, the other warriors flinched.
It was true that facing the Queen of Punishment meant facing the cruelest of the three queens — one who showed no mercy even to her own. Every one of them knew what being implicated would mean. 
But it was equally true that every one of them had grown up praying to Lavender’s statue. The Queen of Slaughter had always been the queen closest to their hearts — the one who woke the courage in them, who made them feel alive in battle.
The conflict between the two queens had already reached the point of no return. There was no staying neutral forever. The only question was which side you chose, and when.
A resolute look crossed Nyla’s face. She raised her head.
“Glory is our Queen, and glory is our life,” she said, her voice steady. “With eternal slaughter, we will protect our clan’s territory.”
[SFX: VOICES JOINING IN UNISON]
One by one, then all at once, the other warriors took up the words. The oath filled the hall.
[SFX: SOFT STONE GLOW]
The surviving statue — Lavender’s — pulsed with a faint red light.
Lavender’s eyes brightened. She said nothing, but something in her expression shifted — the barest softening, there and gone.
After they had all taken the vow, Lavender spoke again, her tone softening almost imperceptibly. “You may stand.”
Nyla nodded and led the others to their feet. 
[MUSIC: REVERENT REFLECTIVE]
By swearing that oath, she had just pledged herself to a single queen, forsaking the other two. It was a painful, terrifying decision. But it was also a supreme honor. They prayed to the queens’ statues every single day, but most of them would live and die without ever seeing one in person. Now that Lavender herself had come to them, how could they possibly refuse?
[SFX: DETERMINED VOICE]
“My Queen,” Nyla said, her voice steady now, “what do you command?” She knew Lavender wouldn’t have come all this way unless she needed something important.
[AMBIENT: QUIET CHAMBER]
“I need you to go to the Eternal Night World,” Lavender said. “Find someone, and give this runestone to her.”
[SFX: RUNESTONE PRODUCED]
As she spoke, Zane, who had been standing silently at her side, produced a runestone etched with critical information.
“The Eternal Night World,” Nyla repeated, her eyebrows shooting up. She knew the name well. 
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING]
It was the realm where the Queen of Slaughter once ruled. Since Lavender’s disappearance, it had been completely taken over by the Queen of Punishment.
But Nyla had just sworn an oath. Lavender’s orders were absolute. It didn’t matter if she was being asked to walk into a deathtrap. 
She had no reason—and no right—to refuse.
