Ep 825 - A Different Kind of Monster

Word count: 1098

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

Picking a fistfight with Zane was a huge mistake.

Sure, the Little Monster had a special body—his power was practically unmatched across the entire Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms, and any brawl that came down to pure muscle was supposed to be a guaranteed win for him.

Too bad he ran into Zane.

[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCH IMPACT]

Even the strongest fighter in the union couldn't stand up to Zane's power; a single punch from him could drop a mighty Soul Sea Realm warrior. 

On top of that, Zane's body had recently transformed into a living Relic-Class weapon. A standard sword or saber couldn't hope to match the sheer density and power of a human body forged into a weapon of that caliber, which meant Zane’s physique was naturally stronger.

In a close-quarters brawl, the Little Monster didn't stand a chance. He'd sealed his own fate.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

Watching the fight unfold, War King York was seething. Zane’s last punch had to have hurt the Little Monster badly, but York kept his mouth shut. He knew how tough the kid was; he wasn't going to die that easily. Both he and the Holy Maiden were counting on the monster to kill Zane, and stepping in now would be the same as admitting defeat. He definitely didn't want that.

Still, deciding to fight Zane with brute strength alone, without using any Vitality, was just plain stupid. The Little Monster had brought this on himself, and it made York furious. The Holy Maiden had warned him that Zane was an incredibly skilled fighter, claiming he’d killed a fourth-level Life-and-Death Realm warrior, and even Master Abel and Lord Wallace.

York wasn't buying all of it—he refused to believe Zane could have taken down Wallace and Abel himself—but he had a strong suspicion the young man was somehow connected to their deaths.

[SFX: SERIES OF THUDS]

A series of sickening thuds echoed as Zane’s fists hammered into the Little Monster, and with every blow that landed, York's expression grew darker.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT DRUMS]

The other warriors watching, however, felt the complete opposite. They were thrilled.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]

The Little Monster had a terrible reputation. He was a well-known troublemaker who’d done all kinds of awful things, and if he'd been born a crown prince, he would've been kicked off the throne ages ago. Everyone hated him, but nobody dared to cross him because of his strength. The only way to deal with him was to stay far, far away.

The warriors from the Nine Cauldron Martial House were especially ecstatic to see him getting pummeled. For them, it was like a long-overdue payback, and Zane was delivering it personally.

Up on the steps, Fiona watched with a quiet smile. She had her own history with the Little Monster. 

War King Wind had once brought her to the York Divine Kingdom specifically to spar with him — his talent was supposed to help her break through faster. It had worked. But during the session, the Little Monster had harassed her relentlessly, spewing filth and nearly injuring her. 

War King Wind's face had gone green with rage. He'd come within a hair's breadth of killing the creature on the spot, and only War King York's intervention had saved the Little Monster's life. Fiona had broken through quickly enough, but she'd spent the next six months waking up from nightmares.

She had feared him ever since. Hated him too.

Her gaze drifted to Zane. Since his fight with her, she'd found herself turning him over in her mind. Who had taught him? How had someone from such a small, remote place grown to this level? She kept the questions to herself — a princess didn't wear her curiosity on her face — but she couldn't stop them from forming.

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

This wasn't how it was supposed to go. The Little Monster had always set his standards high, training to fight opponents a whole realm above him. Even now, in the late stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, he could take on a fifth or even sixth-level Life-and-Death Realm warrior. 

His grand plan was to hold back, to break through the Spirit-Tribulation Realm and jump straight into the Life-and-Death Realm. He figured he’d land at the third or fourth level, more than enough to face off against most Soul Sea Realm masters. He wouldn't be able to defeat War King York—not yet—but it would be enough to escape the Divine Kingdom Continent. Only after he got strong enough would he return for revenge.

What no one knew — not War King York, not the Nine Cauldron Martial House — was that the Little Monster had been suppressing his own breakthrough for years. He knew exactly what would happen the moment he entered the Life-and-Death Realm: the Soul Sea masters would use his talent to push their own cultivation. That wasn't something he was willing to give them. So he waited, suppressing it, biding his time until he was strong enough to break through and run before anyone could stop him.

But his entire plan was crumbling before this other monster. Zane's power level was lower, but he was every bit as strong.

He had seriously underestimated his opponent. 

Just as Zane was winding up for another swing, he felt a sharp pain shoot through his hand. His grip broke. The Little Monster's arms snapped back to their normal length and retracted instantly.

The Little Monster floated upward, Vitality finally coursing through him.

Zane rose to meet him, expression flat. “I thought you really wouldn't use it.”

“I underestimated you,” the Little Monster said. For once, there was no sneer in it. Just a plain admission.

Zane looked at him for a moment. Even after everything he'd thrown at the creature, it was only mildly worse for wear. He had to give it that. In terms of physical resilience, the Little Monster was the toughest opponent he'd ever faced at this level — second only to himself.

Now, the Little Monster knew it was time to get serious.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

He finally unleashed his Vitality, letting it surge through his body and pool in his hands.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

A fleck of golden light flickered to life in his palm.

Zane couldn't help but frown. There was something strange about that energy, something he couldn't quite place, as if the golden Vitality held some kind of complex, fundamental law of nature within it.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

“No wonder you can't kill him,” Lavender’s voice echoed in his mind. She hadn't been able to figure out the kid's origins before, but that golden glow confirmed it for her. “This guy isn't a monster, but he's not exactly human, either. He's a Son of the Plane.”
