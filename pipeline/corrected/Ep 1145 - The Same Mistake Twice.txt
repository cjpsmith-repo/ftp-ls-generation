Ep 1145 - The Same Mistake Twice

Word count: 1057

[MUSIC: DRIVING MOMENTUM]
The windy tunnels all twisted through the rock like tangled roots, but they all spat you out on the other side of the cavern.
Plenty of warriors were switching between the air currents—Lincoln and a few others had already jumped paths several times. But what Zane was doing made no sense. Everyone else was scrambling upward, all of them gunning for the Seed of the World Tree. Zane, however, was deliberately moving down.
“Now, switch again!” Lavender’s voice cut through the wind, sharp and commanding.
[SFX: FEET SLAMMING STONE]
[SFX: BODY LAUNCHING THROUGH AIR]
Zane slammed his feet against a towering stone pillar, breaking from the air current to launch himself onto a new path. It was a blur of his own raw physicality and her split-second directions.
[SFX: SHARP COMMAND SHOUT]
“Right! Now!”
“To the left.”
[SFX: BODY TWISTING IN WIND]
[SFX: RICOCHETING OFF SURFACES]
At her command, Zane twisted in midair, using the gusts to ricochet from wall to pillar. His movements were a fluid, dazzling spectacle, but he was always staying near the cavern floor.
“What is this guy doing?” one of the warriors muttered, staring dumbly. “Putting on a show?”
Zane hadn’t claimed a single treasure, but his skill earned a grudging respect from the others. They knew they’d be hard-pressed to pull off moves like that, especially with the storm-winds trying to throw them off balance.
After thirteen jumps between the wind tunnels, Zane reached the dead center of the cavern—and suddenly, everything stopped. 
[AMBIENT: WIND TUNNEL ECHOES]
[SFX: GENTLE WHOOSH]
He wasn’t moving forward or back. He was just… floating. A gentle updraft had caught him, wrapping around him like an invisible hand and lifting him slowly, steadily, toward the Seed of the World Tree at the very top.
As the current carried him upward, he called out to Lavender. “How did you even know this path existed?”
She was quiet for a moment—almost modest, which wasn’t like her. “The first time I came here, I mapped every tunnel with my scented mist. All of them. That’s when I realized the wind paths in this cave aren’t random—they follow an ancient formation. The Nine Tunes Ten Winds Formation.” She paused. “I recognized it, but I miscalculated the crossing. Ended up on the wrong side with nothing to show for it.”
“And then?”
“And then I went home and studied it until I had it cold.” A beat. “I wasn’t going to make the same mistake twice.”
[SFX: CROWD MUTTERS]
“What the—what’s happening?”
“He’s not flying, is he? You can’t fly in here!”
“It’s an updraft! It’s carrying him straight up!”
A wild, hopeful gasp escaped Freya. The Seed had seemed so impossible she hadn’t dared to even think about it, but now… it looked like nothing could stop him.
Even Lincoln, who was usually impossible to read, broke into a grin. He’d always admired pure strength, and he couldn’t help but respect someone who could pull off the impossible right in front of him.
The warriors from the Dark World, however, looked ready to kill. Their eyes burned with fury, all of them trying to gauge how much of a threat Zane really was. One of them, the same ogre who’d snatched Freya’s Ice Lotus earlier, glared at him with undisguised menace.
[MUSIC: TENSE DISCOVERY]
Outside the palace, the three Saint Lords had been watching through their surveillance arrays. The Ogre Saint Lord’s jaw tightened. He wanted to send a message in—order his warriors to seize the seed the moment that boy touched it. But his warriors were already in position, and the palace’s barrier held firm. He couldn’t reach them. And he already knew, watching the kid rise toward the treasure with that expression on his face, that the moment it was in his hand, it was going down his throat.
Zane had already marked the guy for death back then. Now, seeing that look, he could only offer a cold sneer in return.
The human Saint Lord watched in silence. There was no hiding the admiration in his eyes. Hundreds of wind paths, changes almost beyond counting—and this boy had threaded through all of them without a single misstep. That wasn’t luck.
 [MUSIC: MYSTICAL DISCOVERY]
[AMBIENT: SACRED CHAMBER REVERB]
He reached the top. The World Tree Seed hung beside him, dangling from a withered vine. 
[SFX: VINE SNAP]
He pinched it between two fingers, gave a gentle tug, and the vine snapped. It sat in his palm—smooth as a river stone, nothing remarkable to look at.
He turned it over, then looked at Lavender. “One thing. This thing grows by absorbing Vitality, right? My inner world runs on Chaos Energy. Will it even take root?”
Lavender’s expression shifted—just slightly. “Honestly? I don’t know. I’ve never seen this situation before.” She met his eyes. “But you’re not putting it back.”
Zane considered that for exactly one second.
With a smirk, Zane popped the seed into his mouth and swallowed it whole. He pulled a thread of Chaos Energy from his core, using it to guide the seed down into his inner world. Then he waited.
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL WHISPERS]
The Sea of Chaos within him remained perfectly calm, without a single ripple. He knew that whenever other warriors used their techniques, the Soul Sea inside them would churn like a stormy ocean. His was different. This sea, made of condensed Chaos Energy, was thicker, heavier than the Vitality most warriors controlled.
[SFX: SOFT MAGICAL POP]
The Seed of the World Tree dropped into his Sea of Chaos. 
[SFX: LIQUID RIPPLE]
A single, tiny ripple broke the surface before it sank and vanished without a trace.
“That’s it?” Zane asked Lavender, sensing the continued stillness.
Lavender shrugged. 
“What were you expecting, fireworks? It’s in your core now. You’re not getting it out, so just be patient. It’ll take some time for it to take root and sprout.”
Zane nodded, his eyes turning back to the ogre warrior.
[MUSIC: RISING CONFRONTATIONAL DRUMS]
[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD MURMURS]
When the ogre saw Zane swallow the seed so casually, his face contorted in a mask of pure fury. He wasn’t the only one, of course. None of the warriors who’d been fighting for it were happy. It was the oldest story in the book: if they couldn’t have it, they’d rather nobody did.
