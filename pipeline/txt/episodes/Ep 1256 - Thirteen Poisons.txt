Ep 1256 - Thirteen Poisons

Word count: 1270

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERIC]
[AMBIENT: EERIE VOID]
[SFX: DOUBLE PUNCH IMPACT]
Zane threw two quick punches, connecting squarely with the palm-sized skulls. He didn’t put much into it—just a fraction of his strength, which he figured would be more than enough to shatter them into dust.
But they didn’t break. They didn’t even crack.
[SFX: WHOOSHING PROJECTILES]
[SFX: SKULLS SHATTERING]
Instead, the impact sent them flying a few hundred yards, right through two of the giant skulls floating nearby. 
[SFX: SHARP WHOOSH RETURN]
Then, impossibly, they whipped back around, jaws open, and came right for him.
These things were seriously weird.
[SFX: LOW GRATING RUMBLE]
[SFX: CHORUS OF CURSING VOICES]
A low, grating sound filled the air, followed by what sounded like a chorus of curses from the giant skulls all around him. 
[SFX: VORTEXES OPENING]
[SFX: SKULLS POURING OUT]
Black vortexes spun open like smoky wounds in the space around Zane, spitting out more and more of the little skulls.
“So they really can’t be broken?” Zane murmured to himself.
Planting his feet, he shot his right hand out and snatched one of the small skulls clean out of the air.
This time, he wasn’t holding back. He poured the power of ten thousand Dragon Scales into his grip—enough force to twist a Divine Weapon into scrap metal. 
[SFX: POWER SURGE FLOWING]
[SFX: INTENSE CRUSHING STRAIN]
A surging power flowed down his arm and into his fingers. The skull in his hand didn’t even groan. It was just… solid. He couldn’t crush it.
*Twenty thousand Dragon Scales. Thirty thousand.*
It was just a tiny skull, but Zane was having to channel nearly all of his strength, pushing every ounce of power he had into his grip.
And still, not a single crack appeared on its surface.
[SFX: CHATTERING TEETH SWARM]
To make things worse, the other skulls were swarming him, chattering their teeth as they closed in for a bite.
[SFX: POWERFUL KICK]
[SFX: SKULLS SCATTERING]
Zane spun, lashing out with a kick that sent them scattering, and then shot forward. New plan: go for the source. He was going to kill the creatures hiding inside the enormous skulls.
[SFX: MASSIVE SKULL SHATTERING]
He smashed through the first one, then the second. Luckily, the ragged, black-cloaked things inside weren’t much of a fight. With Zane using his full strength, they stood no chance of escaping.
[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT THROUGH BRANCHES]
[SFX: REPEATED SKULL DESTRUCTION]
He moved like a blur, weaving through the canopy of the massive tree, destroying the giant skulls and the creatures inside them one by one. After a while, he noticed he was getting closer and closer to the top. It finally clicked: to get out of this maze, he had to destroy the whole thing.
But even as he figured out a path to the purple light at the summit, a new problem became obvious. What was he supposed to do about these little skulls? He’d assumed they would disappear once he killed the creatures that summoned them, but they were still on him, a relentless, chattering swarm.
By the time every last giant skull and creature on the treetop had been smashed to pieces, four or five hundred of the small skulls had gathered behind him, floating in a dense cluster, taking turns darting in to bite.
“Scram!”
[SFX: FORCEFUL BURST OUTWARD]
A wave of raw force tore out from his body and slapped the swarm away, scattering them in every direction.
[SFX: SKULLS REGROUPING]
But it didn’t take long before they drifted back together and resumed biting at him from behind.
The bites themselves weren’t strong — nothing that could actually hurt him — but the idea of dragging an endless, chattering swarm around for good was its own kind of problem.
Memory struck - a Heavenly Venerable in the middle heaven domain, trailed by an identical swarm of biting skulls. That warrior had killed the same shadow-creature and earned the same curse.
If a Heavenly Venerable couldn’t shake them off, neither could Zane - not now. That Venerable had eventually died somewhere in the wider universe. Zane, at least, faced this only within the Illusion Battlefield.
He let it go. Perhaps the swarm could serve as armor - tough enough to block a hit or two.
With that settled, Zane turned and made his way toward the purple light.
Breaking free of the maze had brought him within two thousand feet of it. Ahead of him, a staircase of stacked tree trunks rose into the sky, climbing all the way toward the crown of the World Tree.
The moment his foot touched the first step, one of the giant eyeballs overhead blinked slowly.
A strange fragrance filled the air. The gray fog shifted to pale yellow.
“Poisonous fog?”
The Sorcerer Race specialized in voodoo poison and curse techniques. The curse remained active - the little skulls grinding against his back proved that. But the poison itself?
He barely registered it.
This yellow mist was a long-lost poison so potent that even warriors with three transformations couldn’t resist it. When the Sorcerer Race had been at its height, countless warriors had died from it.
It rolled off Zane without a flicker of discomfort.
Ten feet higher, another eyeball blinked. The yellow fog gave way to blue.
This one made him frown - not from danger, but from the smell. Where the yellow fog had been almost pleasant, this blue one reeked. Zane pinched his nose and kept climbing.
Every ten feet, the fog changed color and scent. Most of them stank - rot, decay, one worse than the last. Aside from that first fragrant yellow cloud, nearly all left him wrinkling his nose.
These were the Sorcerer Race’s legendary Thirteen Strange Poisons - the pride of their civilization, each one uniquely lethal. Some could rot a warrior’s entire body on contact. Others lay dormant for years before erupting, leaving nothing whole behind.
If any of the Sorcerer Race’s long-dead masters could see their prized poisons reduced to an elaborate, smell-based staircase gauge for an uninvited guest, they’d probably choke on their own fury.
To ordinary warriors, a staircase like this would have been an impossible, deadly gauntlet. 
But for Zane, the poison was just an inconvenience. 
He passed through it all with ease and finally came to a stop right in front of the ball of purple light.
[AMBIENT: SOFT PULSING HUM]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
It floated in the air, pulsing softly. Zane stared at it, his face twisting in disappointment.
“So there are no world tree fruits and no Illusion Points… then what was this all for?”
That’s when he saw it: a rectangular iron ticket, floating right in the middle of the light. 
[SFX: METAL SCRAPING]
Curiosity getting the better of him, he reached into the glowing sphere and grabbed it. As his fingers closed around the cold metal, his eyes locked onto the three words etched into its surface: “Hall of Causality.”
[SFX: ETHEREAL VOICE ECHO]
In the same instant, a voice echoed directly in his mind.
*You may enter the Hall of Causality after paying five million Illusion Points. A total of ten warriors are required to open the hall. Three more are needed. Would you like to pay?*
Zane just stood there, stunned.
“I fought my way all the way to the top of this damn tree,” he said to the empty air, not sure if he wanted to laugh or cry. “I would’ve been fine with getting nothing. But you want me to *pay* you five million Illusion Points?”
How the hell was he supposed to pay that?
