Ep 28 - The Capital
Word Count: 1207
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR WILDNERNESS]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON VARIED TERRAIN]
Zane had traversed towering mountains, meandered across rolling hills, trekked over vast plains, and journeyed through countless cities until their names blurred together in his memory.
His boots, worn thin from the endless miles, carried the dust of a dozen provinces.
[MUSIC: MAJESTIC REVELATION]
Now, finally, he stood before The Capital—the gleaming heart that had existed in his imagination for so long. The journey had hardened his resolve, but nothing prepared him for the moment when the legendary city materialized before his eyes, its golden spires piercing the clouds like the fingers of gods reaching toward heaven.
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY]
[SFX: DISTANT CROWD MURMUR]
Zane stood before the city's towering golden gate, his mouth hanging open in pure awe. He'd always thought Castermere County was big, but this—a gleaming golden gate stretching hundreds of feet into the sky—it completely blew his mind.
Zane had devoured books about The Capital, memorizing descriptions of its grandeur and luxury. He'd thought he was prepared. But standing here now, he felt like a total country bumpkin gaping at the big city for the first time.
The books had been detailed, some even had illustrations, but nothing could capture this. He recognized it immediately: the Sky Arch. A staggering four hundred feet high and one hundred feet wide, just like the books said.
Thirty years ago, Princess Aria of Ogre had brought it with her as part of her dowry for the Prince of The Empire.
Zane stared up at the golden archway, glittering with embedded gemstones that fractured the sunlight into a thousand tiny rainbows. He could finally see why she'd brought it. This wasn't just a gate; it was a statement. A way for Ogre to make sure everyone in the Eastern Territory felt poor every time they walked under its ridiculously expensive shadow.
[AMBIENT: BUSY CITY STREETS]
[MUSIC: WONDER AND DISCOVERY]
After passing beneath the Sky Arch, Zane navigated Capitol Drive, attempting to absorb the magnificent sights without becoming overwhelmed by their grandeur. His eyes darted from one marvel to another, his country upbringing making each new wonder seem almost unreal.
Sunstone Hall commanded the skyline—a majestic tower exceeding a thousand feet in height, its crystalline spire piercing the clouds like a golden needle threading the heavens.
The enormous Dragon Shrine dominated an entire city block, its ancient stone dragons seemingly poised to take flight at any moment, their scales glittering with embedded gems that caught the afternoon light.
[SFX: FLYING VEHICLES OVERHEAD]
Zane's breath caught in his throat as armored trucks soared overhead, their engines humming with power that defied everything he understood about what was possible.
The air smelled of exotic spices and unfamiliar magic, a heady mixture that made his head spin.
Despite the captivating spectacle surrounding him, Zane steeled his resolve and maintained his focus.
The memory of Elara's face—her gentle smile now possibly twisted with fear—anchored him to his purpose.
He had journeyed too far and endured too much to allow himself the luxury of distraction.
The Skyreach Sect awaited, and with it, the first real chance to rescue his sister from whatever fate had befallen her.
[AMBIENT: BUSY CITY STREETS]
Still, having to stop and ask for directions at every other corner slowed him down. It took him the entire day, but he finally found it.
[AMBIENT: GRAND SECT GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: AWE-INSPIRING THEME]
The Skyreach Sect took up nearly a quarter of The Capital, making it several times larger than The Citadel itself. And that wasn't even counting the rolling Cloudcrest Range behind it.
Compared to the dazzling spectacle of the Sky Arch, the sect's gate was surprisingly plain.
Two large words were carved at the top, but other than that, there were no decorations. The entrance was flanked by massive green trees wrapped in thick vines, lending an ancient charm to the whole place.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
Two lines snaked out from the gate. The one on the left had maybe ten people, while the one on the right stretched for what looked like miles, disappearing around a corner.
Zane quickly surveyed the enormous queue, mentally calculating that well over a thousand hopefuls stood waiting their turn. The line stretched beyond his vision, disappearing around a distant corner.
Unaware of the distinction between the two queues, he opted for the shorter one, reasoning that efficiency trumped popularity. Within minutes, he found himself face-to-face with the enrollment official.
[SFX: PAPER SHUFFLING]
The administrator, clearly fatigued from processing countless applicants, barely lifted his gaze from his ledger. He extended an open palm toward Zane and stated monotonously, "Legacy Charter."
"Legacy Charter?" Zane echoed, his brow furrowing in confusion as his mind raced to decipher the unfamiliar term.
The receptionist exhaled deeply, his shoulders slumping with the weight of explaining this for perhaps the hundredth time today. He tapped a slender finger against a small, ornate placard on his desk that Zane had overlooked.
"This line," he clarified with practiced patience tinged with weariness, "serves Great House applicants exclusively." His eyes flicked up briefly to assess Zane's weathered appearance.
"Without a Charter, you'll need to join that queue instead." He tilted his head toward the massive line snaking away to the right, his expression betraying a hint of sympathy for the long wait that awaited yet another uninformed applicant.
"So that's why it's so short," Zane thought. It was exclusively for the children of Great Houses—a special class granted by the emperor himself.
Across the entire Empire, only a handful of powerful families held that title, and most were based right here in The Capital. The Rockwells of Cedar County were definitely not one of them, so of course, Zane had no charter to show.
[AMBIENT: CROWD LAUGHTER]
As Zane walked away, a ripple of laughter broke out from the long line. Waiting for hours was a drag, and gossiping was the only real distraction. Zane's mistake was a welcome bit of entertainment.
"Where'd that hick crawl out from?"
"Seriously. Doesn't even know what a Legacy Charter is and thinks he can just walk into the Skyreach Sect? Hilarious."
Ignoring the taunts, Zane just nodded to the receptionist and made his way to the back of the line on the right. He could feel their sneering glares on his back, but he didn't give them a second thought.
After enduring two years of servitude and narrowly escaping death's cold grasp, these petty barbs from strangers barely registered in Zane's consciousness. His spirit, tempered in the forge of genuine suffering, remained impervious to such trivial mockery.
The calluses on his soul had grown thicker than those on his hands.
[AMBIENT: BUSY OUTDOOR CROWD]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON PAVEMENT]
The queue inched forward with excruciating slowness, each step a small victory in what promised to be a day-long battle. Sunlight beat down on the waiting applicants, and the air grew heavy with anticipation and perspiration.
Since Zane knew no one in this vast metropolis, he simply closed his eyes, allowing his mind to drift into a meditative state. The distant murmur of conversation washed over him like waves on a shore, meaningless yet somehow soothing.
"First time here for the Entrance Exam?" a soft voice suddenly inquired from directly ahead.
