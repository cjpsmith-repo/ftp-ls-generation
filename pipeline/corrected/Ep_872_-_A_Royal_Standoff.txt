Ep 872 - A Royal Standoff

Word count: 1059

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

War King York didn’t dignify Zane’s insolent question with a response.

Arguing with a Spirit-Tribulation warrior was beneath him — a monarch trading words with someone three full realms below his station. He knew it, and everyone watching knew it. So he didn’t argue.

He also knew he couldn’t simply kill Zane, not after the Little Monster’s standoff. Zane had absorbed the ability to redirect force into whatever he chose — any attack York unleashed could be transferred straight back, or into the ground, or into someone standing nearby. 

Inside a Soul Sea domain, that would change. York’s domain would sever the connection, reduce Zane to exactly what he was: a Spirit-Tribulation warrior standing in front of a god.

[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]

York took a single step forward, and the air around him tightened. He wasn’t going to argue. He was going to end it.

[MUSIC: URGENT STRINGS]

But before he could close the distance, a figure stepped directly into his path.

Fiona.

She planted herself in front of Zane, her expression composed and utterly serious. The red jade bracelet on her wrist trembled with a faint, continuous vibration. A heartbeat later, Callan moved to stand beside her.

York stopped. His gaze moved from Fiona to Callan and back again. Crown Prince and Princess of the Wind Divine Kingdom. He could have obliterated any other saint-level genius on this island without a second thought. These two were a different calculation.

“Step aside,” he said. His voice was almost courteous.

Fiona’s voice didn’t waver.  “Zane injured Fredrick because Fredrick deserved it. Your son ran a scam, War King York. He extorted nine thousand supreme Aether crystals from my brother. When it fell apart, he refused to let it go. Zane stepped in. If your son was injured, he brought that on himself.”

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

York’s expression darkened. He raised one hand and released a casual, almost gentle wave of invisible force — enough to sweep two Spirit-Tribulation warriors off their feet without a second thought.

[SFX: FIRE SHIELD IGNITING]

The force hit a brown fire shield that hadn’t been there a moment before. War King Wind stood beside his children, one hand raised, his expression dark. The shield held. The invisible force dissipated into heat and scattered.

“York.” War King Wind’s voice was low and even. “If there’s a dispute between the younger generation, we settle it by talking it through. That’s how it’s always been done.”

Scott appeared at War King Wind’s shoulder, arms folded, his usual boisterous energy gone. He wasn’t smiling. “He’s right,” he said. “Let’s hear the full story before anyone does anything they can’t take back.”

[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]

York looked at the three of them. His jaw clenched. Once before, he’d been stopped by the others interceding. Now it was happening again.

He held his position. He would wait.

War King Wind turned to his daughter. “Fiona. Tell me everything.”

Fiona nodded and laid it out plainly — Fredrick’s scheme, the nine thousand crystals, Callan’s good faith, the scam collapsing, and Fredrick’s refusal to walk away. She didn’t embellish, and she didn’t spare anyone. 

When she was finished, the silence that followed was pointed.

[MUSIC: LOW, DARK UNDERSCORE]

War King Wind’s face had gone very still. He turned back to York. “It sounds like your son has only himself to blame. Wouldn’t you agree, York?”

Scott shook his head slowly. “Your son ran a con, got caught, still couldn’t let it go, and came out worse for it. That’s not Zane’s fault. That’s your son’s.”

York’s composure fractured at the edges. “My son was injured. The Heart Concentrating Necklace. The Heavenly Thunder Halberd — both gone. Someone pays for that.”

War King Wind’s eyes went cold. “Pays for it? York, your son bullied mine. I haven’t asked for a single crystal in compensation. You want to talk about what’s owed — are you sure you want to open that ledger?”

York opened his mouth. Closed it. The War King’s tone had been mild, but the implication underneath it was not. The four divine kingdoms had maintained their balance for a long time. That balance didn’t mean York could collect a debt from a Wind royal without Wind collecting one in return.

[AMBIENT: LONG PAUSE]

York turned away without another word.

[MUSIC: TENSION RELEASING]

War King Wind watched him go, then turned back to Zane with a broad, uncomplicated smile, as if the last two minutes hadn’t happened at all. “Jones. You did right by my kids. As long as I’m standing, nobody touches you. Anyone who tries is asking to die.”

The directness of it — a Soul Sea Realm monarch speaking to a Spirit-Tribulation warrior like an equal — sent a ripple of murmuring through the crowd of geniuses watching from a distance.

Zane dipped his head. “Thank you, War King Wind.”

“Ha! Thank Fiona. She’s the one who jumped in front of a War King for you.” War King Wind gave his daughter a look of exasperated pride, then turned his attention back to Zane with a more appraising eye. The longer he looked, the more his expression settled into something thoughtful.

Zane didn’t linger. “I appreciate it. But I still have mining to do.” He gave a short nod to Scott and War King Wind both. “If you’ll excuse me.”

He turned and started back toward the pyramid, the Black Star Pickax across his shoulder.

[SFX: CROWD MURMURING]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC SWELL]

He’d only taken a few steps when the murmuring behind him changed pitch entirely.

Every Soul Sea Realm master on the island had their eyes fixed on the tool in his hand. It was black, rough-hewn, unremarkable to look at from a distance — like a dark hammer. But the closer they looked, the more familiar it seemed. All of them had researched it. All of them had tried, at one point or another, to pull it free.

None of them had managed it. So none of them had believed, even subconsciously, that anyone could.

York was the first to see the three divine textures on the head. Ten-star. His eyes went very still.

The Black Star Pickax.

In Zane’s hand.

[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]

The same question moved through every Soul Sea master present, unspoken and identical:

What the hell is going on?
