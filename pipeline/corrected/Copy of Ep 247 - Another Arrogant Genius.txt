Most disciples in the Skyreach Sect agreed: Zane's payback was justified. Mack had clearly gone too far and gotten what was coming to him.
Still, Zane's brutal methods left them impressed---and a little spooked.
He was vicious for a kid his age.
While the more powerful disciples kept their cool, the weaker ones could only stare at Zane in awe. They couldn't help but imagine that if they were the ones facing him in the battle ring, they'd freeze up and lose their nerve in a heartbeat.
After all, what a second-level Adept had just done to a highly-regarded disciple was insane.
Everyone knew the disciples in today's All Peaks Competition were at least at the seventh or eighth level of the Adept Realm. Half of them were already at the peak of the Adept Realm, while a handful were masters of the Soul Realm.
The Soul warriors hadn't seen Zane's true strength, since he was playing his cards close to his chest. They only saw his brutality and didn't think much of him. At the end of the day, the power gap between an Adept and a Soul master was just too massive.
Aside from a rare talent like Finn, it was impossible for any other disciple to challenge a Soul warrior. Finn was already at the peak of the Adept Realm, miles ahead of Zane. Zane's second-level Adept status wasn't even worth a second glance from any of the real masters.
It was common knowledge that top inner disciples rarely bothered with The Demon Purge. And even though Zane had an impressive run, it was nothing compared to what a legend like Dalton had accomplished. Dalton was known for his incredible skill, yet even he hadn't qualified to represent Skytop Peak in the competition.
So, to the legendary figures from each peak, Zane's performance barely registered.

The whole reason the Skyreach Sect was split into so many Peaks was simple: to breed competition.

If Zane hadn't avenged Eliot, it would've crushed Misty Peak's morale. The disciples would have been ashamed and discouraged. The strongest Peaks might easily get their top thirty disciples onto the list, but weaker ones like Sea Peak and Misty Peak were lucky to even get their top ten qualified.
Getting into the Top 400, however, was a whole different challenge. Most of them were at the peak of the Adept Realm, and the Top 100 were basically all masters of the Soul Realm.

In the battle ring now were two such masters: Jeff, ranked ninety-ninth, and Nicholas, ranked seventy-second.
So far in the group competitions, the Soul disciples had either wiped the floor with their opponents or their opponents had forfeited the second they stepped into the ring. It was obvious that a peak Adept stood no chance against a Soul master, so most didn't even try. The common disciples just didn't have the guts---or the power.
Unless you were a freak of nature like Finn, you wouldn't risk the humiliation of being crushed by a superior opponent.
Mack, the magic array prodigy Zane had just taken down, actually had what it took to challenge a Soul disciple. His arrays were powerful enough to trap even a master, making it tough for them to escape.
His big mistake was running into Zane. Zane's unique constitution allowed him to completely absorb Mack's fire, leaving the prodigy defenseless. The result was a brutal beatdown. Even with pills from Blackrock Peak, it was doubtful he'd recover in time, meaning he was probably out of the All Peaks Competition for good.
Both Jeff and Nicholas had only recently broken into the Soul Realm.
There was a huge power gap between the Soul Realm and the peak of the Adept Realm. But when that gap narrowed, disciples could learn the technique of Burning Vitality, causing their fighting power to spike.
Because of this, their match was getting a lot of serious attention.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC RIVALRY]
Jeff represented Skytop Peak, the reigning number one among The Thirty-Three Peaks, whose influence had been growing over the last few years. Nicholas, on the other hand, hailed from Alabaster Peak, an old and respected peak that had once held the top spot for generations. Even though its power had been declining lately, no one was foolish enough to underestimate Alabaster Peak.
It had, after all, received a ton of resources and perks from the Sect over the years. It had more secret training sanctums than any of the other Thirty-Three Peaks. And while those six sanctums weren't as good as the ones that charged points, they were at least free for Alabaster disciples. Many of them practiced there day and night, hoping one of them would be the one to restore their peak to its former glory.
Nicholas was ranked second among all the disciples of Alabaster Peak.
Despite his rank, he had been training for years, so no one knew the true extent of his power. Soul Realm disciples rarely fought in the ring, and the Top 100 rankings only changed every three years.
As for Jeff, he had only broken through to the Soul Realm a year ago. He was already at the top of his class as a peak Adept; now, his strength was on a whole other level.
