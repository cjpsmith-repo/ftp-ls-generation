Ep 287 - Defeat is Not an Option
Word Count: 1050
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Over a hundred Dragon Scales flared to life at the same time, surging power through Zane’s body like a flash flood roaring through a canyon.
The primal energy transformed him, causing his shoulders to swell and veins to writhe beneath his skin. His eyes gleamed with predatory focus as he calculated his opponent’s weaknesses, drawing on instincts that felt ancient and unfamiliar.
Just before their bodies collided, Jacob threw back his head with arrogant confidence. "I’m gonna smash you to pieces!" he bellowed, his voice echoing across the arena as he lunged forward, certain of his superiority.
The crowd held its collective breath, expecting Zane’s destruction. But Zane pivoted at the last possible instant, evading the lethal horns of the White Bone Armor.
Time seemed to slow as he channeled every ounce of draconic energy into his shoulder and drove it directly into Jacob’s armored frame.
[SFX: BONE-CRUSHING IMPACT]
[SFX: SHOCKWAVE BLAST]
The impact was a deafening shockwave that ripped through the arena.
To everyone’s horror, Jacob—encased in his heavy White Bone Armor—was launched across the fighting ring. He flew from one end to the other and slammed into the enchanted barrier.
Fighters hit the glowing curtain all the time. It was designed to be soft, to cushion blows and keep the fighters from tumbling into the crowd. It wasn't built for this. Zane had channeled the explosive power of every last Dragon Scale into that one hit.
If Jacob and his armor were a war machine, Zane was a force of nature.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER SHATTERING]
That pure, raw power sent Jacob flying backward so hard the barrier couldn’t take it. The crowd watched in terror as the curtain of light flickered violently. Spiderweb cracks shot across its surface like a shattering mirror, and then it exploded into a shower of glittering fragments.
Jacob didn't stop at the barrier. He blew straight through it and into the spectator area. In the stands where the Sect's Chosen were gathered, Finn and a still-recovering Wynn moved fast — catching Jacob before he could crash into anyone behind him.
A collective gasp went through the crowd. This up-and-comer had already stunned the Skyreach Sect all through the competition, but this... this was something else entirely.
[SFX: IMPACT CRASH]
While the audience reeled, Jacob, the man at the center of it all, was a complete mess. He staggered to his feet with the help of his friends, his mind racing. A second ago, he'd been picturing this cocky kid getting crushed. Then came the impact, and his thrill had curdled into confusion. No one knew better than him just how much power Zane had unleashed. Being thrown clean out of the ring shoved him past confusion and straight into ice-cold fear. Now, all that was left on his face was a look of utter helplessness.
He knew Zane was tough. But Jacob had always been the brute, the one with enough raw strength to overwhelm anyone. To his shock, Zane was even more savage, even more relentless. Jacob had been so sure his White Bone Armor would let him crush Zane to a pulp. But Zane hadn’t even tried to dodge. He’d met the charge head-on with overwhelming force and simply won.
Technically, Jacob was still in the match. A match was only over when someone surrendered or couldn’t fight anymore. He could argue that the barrier’s failure was a technical malfunction, not a legitimate outcome. Unless he admitted defeat, he had get back into the arena.
His mind was a wreck, his once-orderly thoughts now scattered like broken glass. It felt like a chess grandmaster getting outmaneuvered by a novice who’d only ever played checkers.
Being beaten at his own specialty—brute force—cut deeper than any previous defeat, an unbearable humiliation. His reputation, carefully cultivated over years of dominance, now hung by a thread as whispers spread through the crowd.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
But no matter how he felt, he had to get back in the ring. The arrogant sneer was gone, replaced by a deadly seriousness as he stepped back onto the field. He locked eyes with Zane, knowing he had to go all out.
Even so, a sliver of doubt crept in. He knew how strong his White Bone Armor was. For Zane to hit it with that much force... his own bones should have shattered right along with the barrier.
But when Jacob looked up, Zane was standing there calmly, without a single scratch. How? Jacob wondered, the confusion returning full force.
Unlike the storm of emotions raging inside Jacob, Zane was completely relaxed. As Jacob stepped back into the ring, Zane just rolled his shoulders, rubbed his hands together, and shot him a taunting grin.
[SFX: CONFIDENT TAUNT]
"Round two? I’m ready when you are."
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
The words hit Jacob like a punch to the gut. A bitter taste rose in his throat. The tiny sliver of doubt he'd felt before erupted into full-blown disbelief.
"This spear is carved from the spine of a Drake," Jacob said, his voice low and his eyes glinting with menace. "You’re lucky. You’ll be the first in the All Peaks Competition to feel its power."
A Drake.
Zane knew what that meant. A type of dragon, sure, but a far cry from a True Dragon. It wasn’t a Greater Legendary Beast, not even close. It was nothing compared to Tracy's Blue Phoenix. But Tracy, for all her bloodline, wasn’t strong enough yet to command the Phoenix’s full power.
What Jacob held was a piece of a dragon's spine. And no one knew what a Drake Bone Spear could really do.
[SFX: LOW HUM]
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
But the moment the bone spear let out a low, humming roar, something shifted in Zane’s mind. He felt the Cobalt Dragon inside The Soulforge stir, letting out a faint, silent roar of its own.
The Dragon Language was strange and complex, full of sounds too quiet or on a frequency humans couldn’t even hear. Zane had never understood it.
So he was stunned when, in that one instant, he knew exactly what the dragon was saying.
What? he thought, the dragon’s intent washing over him. You want to... eat it?
A strange, predatory smile crept onto Zane’s face.
