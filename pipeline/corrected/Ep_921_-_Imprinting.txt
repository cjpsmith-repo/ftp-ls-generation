Ep 921 - Imprinting

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

It’s a universal truth: every thinking creature wants to be stronger. 

It’s the engine that drives entire civilizations, pushing every race in the cosmos to grow, evolve, and reach for more power. And the fastest way to ensure that growth was to pour everything into the next generation. Each race developed its own brutal benchmarks, its own trials designed to forge its most promising young talents into legends.

Of course, every culture had its own methods, but a few were held in higher regard than others. Besides the esoteric lotus flower observation, three tests were seen as the most accurate of all: the Heavenly Path of the humans, the Ogre Abyss, and the three magical towers of the Demi-Immortal Race.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Earning a title from those towers was a life-changing event. Even the lowest rank, bronze, granted the full protection of the Demi-Immortal Race, marking the title-holder as one of their own. But it was a crowded field at the bottom. Of the fewer than one hundred thousand living members who’d ever earned a title, a full eighty thousand of them held bronze. Only eighteen thousand ever reached silver, and a mere thousand managed to claim gold. Beyond that, the vaunted Warlord and Reaper titles were held by a legendary sixty.

As for the celestial rank, only six people in history were known to have achieved it. Six. And every single one of them had become an absolute pillar of the Demi-Immortal Race, their names etched into legend.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]

So, when Zane made his claim, Dorothea’s world simply stopped. The idea that this man standing before her possessed that kind of impossible talent felt like a hallucination.

She had ranked first on the Talent Tablet herself. It was something she was quietly proud of — though whenever she met a titled Demi-Immortal, that pride had a way of shrinking. A faint inferiority she couldn't quite shake. She understood, rationally, that talent was only one part of the equation. Hard work and fortune could close almost any gap. She'd seen it happen.

But standing in front of Zane now, she didn't feel that familiar competitive sting. There was nothing to compete with. The distance was simply too vast to measure.

“You're really not lying to me?” Dorothea's voice came out unsteady, barely controlled.

Zane met her eyes. “No. I'm not.” He paused. “But—“

“But what?” She leaned forward slightly, as if bracing for him to take it back.

“The egg,” he said.

Just moments ago, she had been ecstatic about discovering the Tower of Sin. Their mission was to capture the Ancestral and Evil Dragons, and finding the Tower was an unexpected, career-making bonus. Bringing it back to her people would have been a greater achievement than securing both dragons combined.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

But now, all of that felt ridiculously small. A potential golden celestial rank trumped everything.

If this man truly carried a gold celestial title, then the Ancestral Dragon choosing him wasn't a setback. It was almost fitting. The word that came to her, unbidden, was destiny.

Dorothea’s first instinct was to call off the entire dragon hunt. Her mind was already racing, plotting. She had to get him to the Tower of Sin immediately, verify this insane claim, and then—

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Her thoughts screeched to a halt as her eyes landed on the Ancestral Dragon Egg.

A soft crack split the silence.

[SFX: SHELL CRACKING]

The eggshell, still wrapped in its layers of chains, split open. A small nose pushed through first, prying the shell apart from the inside. Then the rest of it came — a furry body, slender-headed, with a pair of oversized eyes that glowed faintly green.

It didn't look like a dragon. It looked like a tiny seahorse.

The little creature took a moment to survey its surroundings. Then, methodically, it began to eat its own eggshell — swallowing it piece by piece until nothing remained.

[SFX: SOFT CRUNCHING]

[MUSIC: SOFT ETHEREAL SWELL]

Inside the spire, Brianna's voice came urgent and low in Zane's mind. Keep her talking. Let it notice you first. The moment its eyes land on you and hold, the imprint sets.

Zane understood. Unlike creatures born helpless — humans, who couldn't even lift their heads at birth — egg-born creatures came into the world with full mobility and the beginnings of independent thought. That was why imprinting worked on them. The first thing they truly saw became mother.

He kept his eyes on Dorothea and kept talking.

She and her team had a plan to prevent this very thing from happening. They were supposed to use the Time Law to hatch the Ancestral Dragon, seal it before it could even open its eyes, and ship it back to their superiors. A clean, simple job. 

But the dragon had hatched ahead of schedule, right as she was chasing Zane into the spire. Following the instructions of the girl in the pillar, he had stumbled his way into earning the newborn dragon’s complete trust.

The little dragon finished the last of its shell. Its green eyes swept the room.

They found Zane.

[SFX: BABY BIRD CAW, SOFT THEN LOUDER]

[SFX: SMALL WINGS FLUTTERING]

It floated free of the chains — straight out of the Refraction space as though the seal had never existed — and launched itself at him.

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

Cradling the small creature, Zane braced himself, expecting Dorothea to finally explode with rage. He was already mapping out his escape routes.

But she didn’t move. She just stood there, staring at him with a blank, distant expression, completely ignoring the priceless baby dragon in his arms.

“What’s with her?” Zane muttered, asking the spirit of the girl inside the pillar.

She sounded just as baffled. “I have no idea. Maybe she’s in shock from those ridiculous stories you just fed her.”

“I wasn’t making it up,” Zane shot back under his breath.

“Right,” she sneered. “I’ve seen your gift for bluffing, remember? I don’t believe a word of it.”

The little dragon buried its furry head against his chest and let out another soft cry. Its tail curled around his forearm.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC SHIFT]

“You’re coming with me,” Dorothea suddenly blurted out, her voice sharp and absolute.

Zane was completely lost. “Go with you? Why?”

Her eyes narrowed, her focus locking onto him with unnerving intensity. “I’m dropping the mission for the Ancestral Dragon. But first, you will do exactly as I say.”
