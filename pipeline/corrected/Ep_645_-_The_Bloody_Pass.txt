Ep 645 - The Bloody Pass
Word count: 1076
[AMBIENT: EERIE CAVE ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS DRONE]
The Dark Nether Cave glowed with eerie Death Light. Only the toughest creatures survived its brutal conditions.
Even the grass proved deadly—resilient blades sharp enough to slice through Mythic weapons. A careless step could leave fatal gashes. Weaker creatures bled out in the gloom. Top-level War Lords, however, remained unscathed; the grass couldn't penetrate their protective Vitality.
Esther led the way with a fluid grace, Zane and the others following close behind her. A few members of the Twilight Group moved like ghosts in their wake, their breathing completely suppressed.
"The Bloody Pass is just ahead," Esther said softly. "Everyone, suppress your Vitality and any other energy signatures. And don't make a sound."
"The Bloody Pass?" Zane looked ahead and could just make out two small mountains forming a narrow path between them. That had to be it. But being told to drop their defenses felt all kinds of wrong.
"There are two powerful monster lairs on either side of the pass," Fabian explained in a low voice. "One belongs to the Frost Wolf, the other to the Shadow-maw—that strange bird we saw earlier. We have to be completely silent going through here. Even the sound of us breathing could get us killed." As he finished, he pulled a heavy cloak from his Storage Ring and threw it over his shoulders.
The others didn't seem to fully grasp the why, but seeing one of the veterans do it was enough. They quickly pulled out their own cloaks and followed suit. Unlike Zane, they couldn't resist the strange effects of the red light. The Dark Nether Death Light had weakened in this part of the cave, but it was still strong enough to cause extreme discomfort, like a thousand needles pricking your skin. The cloaks were essential for blocking its glow.
[SFX: DISTANT HOWLS]
From somewhere beyond the pass, a chorus of wolf howls drifted toward them — low, distant, rhythmic. Zane had never seen a Frost Wolf, but if it held territory alongside the Shadow-maw, it wasn't something any of them could afford to wake up.
Once everyone was ready, they held their breath and started moving.
[AMBIENT: EERIE MOUNTAIN WIND]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
An eerie silence fell over the group. The four top-level War Lords from the Twilight Group moved like the wind, not making a single sound. Esther and the other warriors moved more slowly, but just as carefully.
[SFX: DISTANT CREATURE CRY]
The pass was narrow but not long. Step by step, they closed the distance. Less than three hundred feet from safety, Fabian let out the quietest exhale of relief. "We're going to make it," he mouthed.
Everyone's shoulders dropped half an inch.
And that's when they saw it — a single, glowing plant growing precariously on the cliffside.
The Moon Shadow Begonia. Palm-sized, three leaves spreading in different directions, and at the top of its stem, a furry stamen pulsing with faint orange light.
Everyone who recognized it went rigid. The four Twilight Group War Lords' eyes lit up with something close to hunger. They began drifting toward it before they'd even made a conscious decision to move.
Esther's hand shot out in a hard gesture — stop. She couldn't speak. She couldn't even push a thread of Vitality to transmit her voice. This close to both lairs, the smallest fluctuation would be enough. She fixed the Twilight Group with a stare cold enough to freeze stone.
They didn't listen.
Fabian and Jarrod were sweating through their cloaks.
Zane glanced sideways at Lavender.
She was his sword spirit. She didn't need Vitality to move — her body produced no energy signature at all. If the Twilight Group was already creeping toward it, the monsters were going to be disturbed one way or another. Better to get there first.
Lavender read it in his eyes. She was already moving.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]
She crossed the distance in silence, a ghost with a spear. Before the Twilight Group War Lords had covered half the ground, she was already there.
[SFX: SWIFT BLADE CUT]
The tip of her spear found the stem of the Moon Shadow Begonia with surgical precision — one clean flick, and the plant lay balanced at the tip, its orange glow pulsing softly.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
The War Lords stopped dead. The look on their faces moved through surprise, then fury.
A cold, victorious smile spread across her face. Turning her back on the others, she flew over to Zane.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
The four War Lords from the Twilight Group were furious. One of them hissed, his voice a low growl, "Give it back!"
"And what makes you think you have any right to it?" Lavender shot back, her own voice a dangerous whisper.
"If you don't hand it over, we'll summon the Frost Wolves!" one of the War Lords threatened through gritted teeth.
[MUSIC: DARK POWERFUL THEME]
As the Demon Night Queen, Lavender was not the type to be intimidated. "Go ahead," she dared him. "Do it." With her power, she had no reason to back down.
[AMBIENT: TENSE MOUNTAIN PASS]
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Meanwhile, Esther and Fabian were getting seriously annoyed. The Twilight Group shouldn't have been there in the first place, and after being so disciplined, they were now acting like a pack of starving animals fighting over a scrap of meat.
What none of them said aloud was what the Moon Shadow Begonia actually was. Only three had ever appeared in the Dark Nether Cave. Every warrior who'd taken one had broken through to the Soul Sea Realm. Amber herself had claimed one of them — and look where she stood now. The Twilight Group knew exactly what they'd lost.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
One of the War Lords, his eyes fixed on the glowing Moon Shadow Begonia at the tip of Lavender’s spear, forgot all about the dangers of the pass. He threw caution to the wind and charged straight at her.
[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSH]
[SFX: SHARP STAB]
"Catch," Lavender said, flicking her spear and sending the plant spinning through the air toward Zane. In the same fluid motion, she spun the weapon in her hands and, without even looking, stabbed backward, right into the man charging up behind her.
Zane knew he wouldn't have stood a chance against that War Lord in a fair fight. But Lavender had just taken him out without so much as a glance. A perfect strike.
The Twilight Group warrior trembled in disbelief as he realized what had happened. How did I not even dodge that?
[SFX: PIERCING SCREAM]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
As he fell, he let out a piercing cry—partly from the pain, and partly to spitefully draw the attention of every beast in the pass.
[AMBIENT: DEADLY SILENCE]
At the sound, everyone froze in sheer terror.
