Ep 575 - Among the Stars
Word count: 1039
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
Zane never would've believed a creature that massive could exist in this universe. Not in a million years.
At this point, he was barely a wisp of consciousness. No breath, no thought. Just pure instinct reacting to the impossible scene unfolding before him.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]
Even with nine dragons living in his mind, their forms were presented in a way that shielded him from their true scale. But he had a gut feeling that even those nine dragons were nothing compared to this colossal turtle-beast.
How could something that huge even survive? Wouldn't it crush countless worlds just by shifting its weight? What could it possibly eat? And how in the hell did it get so gigantic?
The questions swirled, but no answers came.
Since his consciousness was floating free, he couldn't ask the Cobalt Dragon any of it. Looked like he'd have to wait until he was back in his own body to get some answers.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
A cold dread washed over him.
[SFX: DISTANT TREMOR]
The colossal turtle-beast was getting closer to his world. He had no idea what its destination was, but if it strayed even slightly from its path, it would just… step on his world. And crush it into dust.
A helpless smile touched Zane's lips at the thought.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
In this endless universe, he was just a tiny speck, smaller than a grain of sand.
Even on his own world, he was just one soul among a trillion.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]
As he drifted higher, he saw a massive, grayish-brown tree growing out of the densely packed scales. The roots of the gargantuan tree pierced straight through dozens of the scales, meaning a single root could be tunneling through dozens of worlds.
The sheer height of the tree was even more terrifying. It had to be at least a hundred times taller than its root system was wide.
If that tree ever fell, it would smash through thousands of worlds in a single second. And considering the width of its trunk, that number could easily be ten times higher.
All Zane could do was sigh.
[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]
The speed of his consciousness kept increasing, accelerating as he pulled away from the tightly packed scales. As he flew, he spotted other things—an immeasurably huge mountain and even a structure like Stonehenge, all built right on the beast’s back.
[SFX: OMINOUS VIBRATION]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRONE]
Suddenly, Zane felt a strange vibration ripple through the universe ahead of him.
He looked up and saw something pitch-black, spinning relentlessly, not far to his left.
[AMBIENT: VOID SILENCE]
[MUSIC: DARK ATMOSPHERIC]
Black.
That was the only word for it. In reality, it probably didn't even have a color. It was just an absence of everything, a void that devoured light itself.
Fortunately, his consciousness wasn't bound by physical forces. Whatever that thing was, it couldn't pull him in.
[MUSIC: COSMIC WONDER]
His consciousness pressed on — and then, ahead of him, the first star.
It was ice blue, rotating slowly in the dark. Beautiful, from a distance.
The moment Zane's consciousness drew close, he felt it: a powerful will pushing back against him. A bone-deep cold flooded through him, like being submerged in a world of ice.
"It has its own will," he murmured, staring at the spinning sphere. "And it's rejecting me."
He circled it once, then moved on.
The next star was milky white, its surface marked by a vast, slow-turning whirlpool.
Same result. The moment he approached, he felt the rejection.
The star after that burned deep crimson, its surface rolling with enormous flames that would stretch thousands of miles if they erupted. Its will was hot and agitated, snapping at him like a bad-tempered animal.
"Not great with visitors," Zane muttered.
Star after star after star. Over forty of them. Each one with its own personality — some cold, some restless, some simply indifferent — and every single one turned him away.
Then he stopped.
Below him hung a light purple star, soft and quietly radiant.
[MUSIC: GENTLE ETHEREAL THEME]
[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]
As he drew close, something was different. The will he felt from this one wasn't hostile. It was… gentle. Welcoming, even. If he had to put it into words, it felt like being received rather than refused.
"Is this it?" he thought.
A vast, invisible presence wrapped around his consciousness — not warm exactly, but safe. Like being held.
He had done it. On his first attempt, he had connected with a star.
The purple star's will continued to pulse and shift around him, as if it were trying to tell him something. Like a voice speaking just below the threshold of understanding — present, expressive, but not yet decipherable.
Zane stayed with it for a long moment.
Then, not wanting to waste the opportunity, he pushed onward — searching for a second connection. He flew past a hundred more stars. Every one of them rejected him.
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
I guess I'm being too greedy, Zane thought, pulling his focus back.
In the end, he gave up on searching for a new star. He’d made a connection on his first try, and for now, that was good enough.
Traveling through the void as pure consciousness should've been impossible. But the secret technique of the Transcendent Divine Might had actually worked.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DEEP TONES]
On his way back, his attention drifted to the vast field of scales once more. He was incredibly far from them now, so far he couldn't see where they ended. The far edge was shrouded in total darkness, a place so distant that not even light could reach it.
[AMBIENT: COSMIC EXPANSE]
"The universe is infinite, and it's all connected," he mused. "If there are millions of great worlds out there, what lies at the very end of them all?"
Zane shook his head. His mind was reeling from the sheer scale of it all, and he couldn't even begin to wrap his head around the question.
The Stellar Body must have used some kind of secret technique, borrowing gravity from the stars to slingshot his consciousness out here. That was probably how he could travel so fast, hopping between the stars and their gravitational pulls.
But he couldn't wander the Great Void on his own power. At his current level of strength, there was no way he could explore deep space or reach the edge of the universe.
How powerful would he have to become to travel the Great Void in his own body?
