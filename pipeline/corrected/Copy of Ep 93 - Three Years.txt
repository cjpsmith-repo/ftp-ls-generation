[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ENERGY]
The world is teeming with Life Energies. Some could make you strong enough to wrestle tigers or tear mountains down to rubble. Others could make you so fast you could cross a million miles in the blink of an eye.
Every person's Crafting Formula is different, so their Life Energy manifests in a unique color, shape, and ability. But the Corrupt Vitality inside Zane wasn't just consuming his own energy—it was now surging across his skin, ravenously trying to devour Adrian's Vitality too.
Once a person reached the Nature Level, their Life Energy evolved into a shapable Vitality. Adrian, having reached the Soul Realm through the Scrolls of Serenity, could mold his into Ghost Orchids—as deadly as they were beautiful. But in a shocking display, the Corrupt Vitality pouring from Zane's body began swallowing the orchids whole.
[SFX: CRACKING]
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
The moment one of the pristine flowers pulsed with Void Energy, the dark essence pounced like a starved predator, devouring it whole. A hairline crack appeared on the bloom's immaculate white surface, splintering across its delicate structure until the entire flower fractured and dissolved into nothingness, leaving not even dust in its wake.
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD TENSION]
[SFX: GASPS]
Within heartbeats, the entire layer of Ghost Orchids vanished, revealing the horrifying wounds beneath that made even hardened warriors gasp. The fighters on Infernal Peak exchanged uneasy glances, their faces contorted with shock and disbelief at a sight none had witnessed before. Zane's skin resembled ancient pottery left too long in fire—covered in a web of deep, jagged cracks that glowed with an eerie darkness, as if he were a porcelain statue on the verge of shattering into a thousand pieces.
The brutal truth hung in the air like a physical presence: the crushing pressure from Adrian's Ghost Orchids possessed enough force to reduce an ordinary cultivator to nothing but bloody mist.
It was only Zane's extraordinary body—transformed through suffering and ancient mysteries—that had prevented his complete obliteration, leaving him damaged but stubbornly intact. Each crack in his flesh told a story of survival against impossible odds, a testament to his unyielding spirit that refused to bow even as his physical form threatened to collapse.
Zane ignored the pain. He clenched his right hand into a fist, his left hand steadying his arm.
[SFX: ENERGY SUMMONING]
"Earthshatter! The first move of the Titan Strike!"
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
He'd never been able to execute the move before, no matter how many times he practiced. He lacked the Corrupt Vitality. But he had studied the rare Titan Strike manual so often he could recite it from memory.
Now, as he channeled the Corrupt Vitality into the punch, it all clicked. In that single instant, Zane understood every nuance of the technique as if he'd been born knowing it.
Instead of hesitating, he threw the punch right at Adrian.
[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCH]
[AMBIENT: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC IMPERIAL THEME]
"Adrian, do you want your whole family to die?" Isla Novak shouted, her voice laced with an icy threat.
His family, dead? Adrian was a prodigy who'd reached the Soul Realm at a young age; his future was bright.
But House Warrick stood beneath the Seven Great Houses in the imperial hierarchy. Should Isla, wielding the Imperial Signet with all its authority, resolve to eliminate them, she possessed the power to accomplish this devastation without assistance.
This threat emanating from the princess carried genuine weight—not merely the empty posturing of a privileged royal accustomed to getting her way. Though House Warrick maintained its prestigious status as a Great House, commanding vast wealth and widespread respect throughout the empire, their influence paled against imperial decree. Adrian recognized the grim calculus: their generations of accumulated power and prestige could vanish in an instant over what might be deemed a trivial dispute.
[SFX: ABRUPT HALT]
Adrian's clenched fist halted mere inches from Zane's face, his knuckles white with tension before gradually relaxing. The murderous intent drained from his features, replaced by a carefully composed mask of nonchalance. He pivoted toward Isla with practiced elegance, offering a dismissive shrug that belied the cold sweat forming at his temples.
"Your Highness," he addressed her with a forced smile that didn't reach his eyes, "I was merely entertaining our guest with a friendly match. A simple test of strength between cultivators." His voice softened as uncertainty crept in. "Surely the imperial family wouldn't exterminate an entire bloodline over such a trivial matter of honor?"
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Isla's voice cut through the air like winter frost. "I think you'd better stop. For your family's sake," she warned, her eyes narrowing with imperial authority that brooked no argument.
Adrian's lips curled into a contemptuous sneer as he regarded Zane, his gaze traveling dismissively over the younger cultivator's battered form. "You've got guts, I'll give you that," he admitted with reluctant respect. "But right now, you're not worthy of even looking at Elara, let alone fighting me for her."
Zane stood his ground despite his exhaustion, blood trickling from the corner of his mouth. He raised his hand deliberately, holding up three fingers in a gesture both defiant and solemn. "Three years," he declared, his voice steady despite the pain wracking his body.
"What's that supposed to mean?" Adrian asked with a frown, genuine confusion momentarily replacing his arrogance.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]
Drawing himself up to his full height, Zane met Adrian's gaze unflinchingly. The determination in his eyes burned brighter than any flame. "In three years," he proclaimed, each word carrying the weight of an oath, "I'll come back to Infernal Peak, defeat you, and take Elara." The promise hung between them, charged with all the power of his unwavering resolve.
Adrian erupted with thunderous laughter, as if Zane had delivered the most hilarious jest imaginable. "Three years!" he howled, wiping a tear from his eye. "I adore such audacity! Very well—three years you shall have. But mark my words," he leaned forward, his voice dropping to a deadly whisper, "should you fail to defeat me by then, Elara becomes forever beyond your reach, and I'll personally extinguish your miserable existence." He jabbed his finger at Zane's chest, his eyes glittering with malevolent promise.
[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]
With that declaration hanging in the air like a death sentence, Adrian shot skyward, his silhouette cutting through the clouds.
[SFX: MAGICAL DISINTEGRATION]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS]
Below him, the Ghost Orchids disintegrated into shimmering particles of Vitality, swirling briefly before vanishing completely—leaving only the weight of an impossible challenge and the ticking clock of Zane's mortality behind.
