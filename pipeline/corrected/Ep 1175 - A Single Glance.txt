Ep 1175 - A Single Glance

Word count: 1056

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL WONDER]
When you hear the word “heavens,” you think of something massive—grace, power, the force that holds an entire era together. And usually, the current Cosmic Age is pretty stable. If something were to shake it up, you could bet the consequences would be huge.
[SFX: DEEP RESONANT BOOM]
Typically, a disturbance like that meant one thing: a Supreme Lord has fallen, and a Crown of Destiny is back in play.
A Crown of Destiny was the greatest prize in the universe — and the most dangerous. When one appeared in the hands of a weak race, the newly crowned Supreme Lord rarely survived long enough to matter. There had once been a single Crown that passed through seven hands in rapid succession, each new Supreme Lord killed almost immediately, before a small-race lord finally surrendered it rather than die holding it.
There are only one hundred and twenty-eight Supreme Lords in the entire universe, and they all live for an obscenely long time. So while a death is rare, it’s also a massive opportunity for every race out there. Even a major power like the Demon Night has only four Supreme Lords. Adding another one to their ranks would be a game-changer.
Not that all four of them were on the same page. The Queen of Punishment had Supreme Lord Gunter as one of her staunchest allies, but Supreme Lord Leroy had always been a shadow, consistently backing Lavender. Of the other two, one was loyal to the Queen of Life, and the last remained neutral, though he occasionally took orders from the Board of Elders.
If the queen could secure another Crown of Destiny, the Demon Night would gain a fifth Supreme Lord—one whose loyalty would be to her and her alone. It would cement her power completely.
[SFX: HEAVENLY TREMOR]
[AMBIENT: THRONE ROOM]
“The heavens just trembled. Did a Supreme Lord die?” the Queen of Punishment asked, her eyes lighting up with a brilliant blue sparkle.
Gunter dipped his head. “No, my queen. All the Supreme Lords are still alive.”
“No?” She raised an eyebrow. “Are you sure? Then what was that?”
Gunter looked uncertain, hesitating for a moment. 
“While no Supreme Lord has fallen,” he said, “I can feel… an extra Heavenly Destiny.”
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
“An *extra* Heavenly Destiny?” the queen repeated, the words catching in her throat. “How is that possible? I’ve never heard of such a thing.”
It was common knowledge across the universe, an unbreakable law: a Cosmic Age had exactly one hundred and twenty-eight Heavenly Destinies for the Supreme Lords to claim. What Gunter was suggesting left her completely baffled.
“I don’t know why, either,” Gunter admitted, shaking his head. He had no idea what might happen if someone actually claimed this rogue destiny, but a Crown was a Crown—they had to go for it. “Regardless, I’ve already located it.”
“Where?” she asked, leaning forward.
“The Dark World,” Gunter replied. “But it’s been moving constantly since yesterday. It looks like it’s already flown into deep space, heading for the Northern Sky Region.”
A silence fell over the throne room.
Then the Queen of Punishment laughed — soft at first, then brighter.
[SFX: QUIET LAUGH — BUILDING]
Gunter stared at her, uncertain. “My queen?”
“Lavender,” she said, still smiling. “She fled the Dark World under pursuit and headed for the Northern Sky Region.” She let the implication settle. “The Crown isn’t moving on its own, Gunter. She’s carrying it.”
He understood immediately. The Demon Night’s people were already in pursuit of Lavender. Which meant the Demon Night had seized the initiative — before any other race had even begun to move.
A strange light crossed the Queen’s eyes. She rose from her throne and walked toward the palace entrance. The doors swung open before she reached them.
[SFX: HEAVY PALACE DOORS OPENING]
Outside, the elders of the Board had been waiting for hours. When they saw her emerge, a ripple of surprise passed through the crowd. Kaelan, stationed at the threshold, turned sharply.
[AMBIENT: AGITATED CROWD]
The elders surged forward the moment they saw her. Two queens killing each other violated the laws of their ancestors. Lavender was no traitor. The three-queen system had to be preserved.
The Queen of Punishment let them finish. Then she waved a hand. “I don’t have time to listen to your nonsense,” she said flatly.
“Nonsense?! Our suggestions are nonsense? Then what use is the Board of Elders at all?”
A spark of curiosity flashed in the queen’s eyes. 
[SFX: MAGICAL PULSE]
Then, a faint blue light pulsed from within her, and the perfect lines of her face hardened. 
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]
“Indeed,” she said coolly. “The Board of Elders has outlived its usefulness. I think I’ll just get rid of it.”
[SFX: SHOCKED GASPS]
“Get rid of it?!”
“How dare you!”
The queen’s words shattered the room’s tense silence, sending waves of outrage through the assembled elders.
[SFX: CHAIR SCRAPING]
The head of the Board, an old man who had lived for over eight million years, shot to his feet. He jabbed a trembling finger at the queen. “We never should have helped you rise to power! How could you do this to us?”
Kaelan frowned, instinctively moving to stand in front of the queen. Even if the old man was the head of the Board, insulting the queen so brazenly was a death sentence. Still, things could get messy if he tried to pull rank here.
The queen, however, was a statue of calm. 
[MUSIC: ICY TENSION]
“Gunter,” she said, her voice dropping to absolute zero.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
Gunter stepped forward from behind her, his gaze locking with the head elder’s.
The old man froze. A sudden realization seemed to strike him, and his eyes widened in horror. 
“Gunter!” he screamed, his voice cracking with rage and disbelief. “How dare you! You can’t!”
Before Gunter became a Supreme Lord, he’d been a member of the Board of Elders himself. The last time the Demon Night had acquired a Crown of Destiny, they’d decided to choose one of their own to claim it. It was the head of the Board who had recommended Gunter for the honor.
He never imagined that millions of years later, he would die by that very same man’s hand.
[SFX: BODY COLLAPSING HEAVY THUD]
There was no grand display of power, no brutal struggle, not even a flicker of Vitality.
The head of the Board of Elders—a decorated Demon Night warrior, a Grand World Lord—simply collapsed to the ground. 
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
A single glance from a Supreme Lord had been enough to end his life.
