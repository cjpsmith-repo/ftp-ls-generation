Ep 5 - The Human Punching Bag

The Training Hall loomed before Zane like an executioner's block. Darren was conspicuously absent this morning—no doubt nursing both his wounds and his pride after last night's confrontation—but his cronies had shown up in his place, roughly shoving Zane into his battered leather armor.
"Move faster, trash," one of them hissed, giving Zane a hard shove between the shoulder blades. "Someone special's been asking for you today."
[SFX: STONE CRACKING]
As they approached the hall, the sound of stone cracking under powerful fists echoed across the courtyard. Through the morning mist, Zane could make out the silhouettes of Lowell children demolishing Stone Golems with brutal efficiency—and beyond them, the hollow-eyed slaves waiting their turn as living targets.
Beaten day and night, most of them spent their nights writhing in pain, sleep a luxury they could rarely afford. But they were property, bought and paid for by House Lowell, with no choice but to endure it.
[AMBIENT: TRAIING GROUNDS]
"All slaves, get ready!" bellowed Master Corrin, the trainer.
The shout snapped the slaves from their daze. Corrin was a sadist. If he caught a slave flinching or making the slightest mistake, he would fly into a rage and bring out his whip—a nasty piece of work that was said to peel skin from bone with a single strike.
The Lowell kids knew the drill. They walked over to the line of slaves and began to pick their targets. A tough-looking teenager named Melvin Lowell chose Zane.
Sympathetic glances followed Zane. Melvin's talent was mediocre—he was still stuck at the Skin Refining Level—but he was born with an immense innate power that gave him the strength of a Muscle Refining Stage warrior.
He was savage and cruel, having already killed or crippled several slaves.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Being picked by Melvin was basically a death sentence.
[SFX: KNUCKLES CRACKING]
Melvin cracked his knuckles, a predatory glint in his eye. It was getting harder to find death-row inmates these days, as other families were buying them up, too.
Since House Lowell barely fed their "human punching bags," they died off quickly, making each one a rare commodity.
[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDUP]
For the kids, beating a slave wasn't just good practice; it was a way to blow off steam.
But for Melvin, this was personal. His punching bag today was the former young master of House Lowell. The idea of making Zane—a boy who had once far outshone him—beg for mercy under his fists was a thrill he couldn't wait to feel.
Zane's jaw tightened. He knew Melvin's reputation for raw power all too well. The last time, Melvin's beating had left him with internal injuries that took half a month to heal. But two years as a human punching bag had taught him how to stay calm under pressure. He took a few quick, deep breaths, settled the leather armor on his shoulders, and faced his opponent.
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]
Melvin charged, pouring everything he had into a brutal technique called the Bull Rush Strike. He'd been itching to try on a live target. He came at Zane like a raging bull, his fist slamming into Zane's chest.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
Everyone watched, eyes wide, as the impact sent a shockwave through the air.
Bracing himself, Zane took a deep breath, hoping the leather armor would absorb some of the blow.
Even through the thick hide, pain exploded through his body. It felt like he'd been hit in the chest with a sledgehammer.
[SFX: EXHALE]
At the moment of impact, he instinctively blew all the air out of his lungs, hollowing his chest. It was a survival trick he'd perfected—a way to "unload power" by creating a buffer to absorb some of the force.
But Melvin's punch was just too powerful. Even after his technique, the pain was excruciating. A pained grin touched Zane's lips as his whole body shuddered. He flew backward like a puppet with its strings cut.
[SFX: BODY HITTING GROUND]
Lying on the ground, dizziness washing over him, he thought this was it. The punch must have caused serious internal damage. But a moment later, he was stunned to find he felt no pain at all. In fact, he felt nothing.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TRANSFORMATION]
Then, a strange warmth began to spread from his chest. As the warmth flowed through him, his body seemed to awaken, like a starving wolf greedily devouring a meal. It spread through his limbs, reaching every single cell, and wrapped him in a surprisingly comfortable embrace.
[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]
"What is going on?" Zane wondered, feeling the warmth seep deep into his muscles.
He may be a slave now, but he was once a young master of House Lowell. He had learned enough to know this was no ordinary reaction. Rejuvenating pills were incredibly rare, as were the legendary Aether Crystals that House Lowell possessed. The ingredients were nearly impossible to find, which was why it had taken a family as wealthy as House Lowell hundreds of years just to acquire two of them. Their restorative effects were the stuff of myths.
Lying on the ground, Zane's mind raced. He connected the feeling to the strange experience from the night before.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
"Use the flesh as a weapon. Use your body as the vessel, temper it, purify it, and awaken its vitality," he recalled a fragment from the gold foil.
Was this what it meant to "temper" his body? Did he need to get beaten?
Just as the text had said, his body had become some kind of mysterious weapon. Maybe this was the forging process. By taking that hit, his body had generated the very warmth needed to purify itself. It seemed that getting beaten was like taking an Aether Crystal.
[SFX: HEART POUNDING]
The thought sent a jolt of adrenaline through him, his heart pounding at the possibilities. To test his theory, he pushed himself to his feet. Though he wasn't hurt—in fact, Melvin's punch had actually helped him—he put on a show, groaning and pretending to struggle, his body trembling so no one would question why he wasn't injured.
When Melvin saw Zane get up, his face twisted with surprise. He'd been sure that one punch would either kill or permanently cripple him. The fact that Zane was still standing was a stinging blow to his pride.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
"Well, well, no wonder you're at the peak of the Strength Level. You can really take a hit!" Melvin sneered. "That's great. It just means I get to hit you again!" He charged forward, throwing another punch.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
[SFX: BODY HITTING GROUND]
Boom! Zane was hit hard again. He went flying, tumbling across the ground like a training dummy.
[MUSIC: ENERGETIC TRANSFORMATION]
But secretly, he was thrilled. Instead of pain, he felt that rush of warmth again. It flooded his body, mending his organs. It felt like tiny serpents of energy slithering through his veins, strengthening his body and refreshing him from the inside out.
