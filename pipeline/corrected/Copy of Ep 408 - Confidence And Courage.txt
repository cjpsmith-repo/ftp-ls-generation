Ep 408 - Confidence And Courage

Word count: 1117

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL MURMUR]

When the steward handed Zane a purple Cloud Hall Token, the students from the Tier-Two sects had no choice but to swallow their pride. The students from Cloud Hall itself, however, weren't having it.

[SFX: ANGRY CROWD VOICES]

"Zane? Who the hell is he?" someone shouted from the back.

"Isn't that the kid from the Skyreach Sect? The one who took first place?" another voice added, dripping with disbelief.

A third student crossed his arms, his face twisted with resentment. "One of our own always wins. How come none of them ever got a purple token before?"

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The grumbling from the crowd swelled like a rising tide, each voice feeding off the others' outrage. These were disciples who had trained their entire lives within Cloud Hall's walls, who had bled and sacrificed for every scrap of recognition. Watching an outsider—a nobody from some backwater sect—receive such unprecedented honor felt like a slap across the face.

A Cloud Hall steward stepped forward, his patience clearly exhausted. His eyes swept across the crowd with barely contained fury. "Quiet down! Anyone who makes another peep gets thrown out of here, no questions asked."

[AMBIENT: WIND HOWLING AT HEIGHT]

Cloud Hall floated miles up in the sky, but that wasn't the real threat. Most of the students were at the Soul Realm and could fly, so a fall wouldn't kill them. They understood the steward’s real meaning perfectly: he wasn't talking about tossing them off the edge, but about kicking them out of Cloud Hall for good.

[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]

That shut everyone up fast. They had all trained since they were kids for a single shot at getting into Cloud Hall, and most were carrying the hopes of their entire families on their shoulders. As much as they envied Zane, nobody was about to get expelled over it.

Truthfully, the two stewards were jealous, too. As masters in the Inner-Core Realm, even they didn't possess a purple Cloud Hall Token, and they couldn’t fathom why Zane was the exception.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WHISPERS]

"You think he has some kind of connection to the leader?" one of them murmured.

[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]

"She's notoriously private," the other replied. "I've never heard a whisper about her being involved with anyone."

The world of martial arts was rarely fair, a fact the stewards had long since accepted. With no better explanation, they simply concluded Zane must have a powerful family backing him and decided it was best not to get on his bad side.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

With that, the two stewards approached him, their smiles polite but stiff. One of them even gave a slight bow as he held the purple Cloud Hall Token out to Zane with both hands.

[MUSIC: INFORMATIVE TONE]

Afterward, the stewards launched into the official orientation. For the next hour, they went over the endless list of Cloud Hall's rules—how to use the Attunement Sanctums and other facilities, which areas were strictly off-limits, and everything in between.

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR COURTYARD]

[SFX: DOOR OPENING]

When they were finally dismissed from the Elite House, Zane walked out with Finn and Patrick, ready to grab their things and find their new rooms.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STOPPING]

The first thing they saw upon stepping outside was a massive map mounted on the wall.

"That's the map of The Heartlands," Finn said.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Zane had always thought the Eastern Territories were huge. But now, looking at the map spread before him, he finally understood why everyone considered his home a backwater. A small inset in the corner showed the territories for comparison; if the Heartlands were an elephant, the Eastern Territories were nothing more than a field mouse. In terms of sheer scale, they weren't even in the same league.

Lewis, who’d been shadowing Zane since they met on the Cloud Road, ambled over as the three of them stared at the map. It offered only a rough layout of the major powers, which made sense. Cloud Hall wasn't about to post its strategic intelligence on a public wall.
But scattered across the map were individual names — warriors powerful enough to stand alone, unaffiliated with any sect. About ten of them in total. Zane's eyes moved across them slowly.
Then stopped.
There — in the eastern quarter of the Heartlands. A single name: Maddox.
He stared at it for a long moment without speaking.

[SFX: QUICK FOOTSTEPS]

Zane turned and walked away, his steps quick and sharp.
Lewis jogged to keep up. "So that's Maddox? I've heard things about him. Apparently he wants to build his own nation — proclaim himself emperor, the whole thing. Oh, and something about an array requiring three top-quality—"
"Lewis." Finn's voice was flat. "Shut up."
Patrick didn't even look at him. "Idiot."
Lewis blinked. "Who did I offend?"

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]

"Zane! Wait up!" Finn and Patrick called, jogging to catch him.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

"What's up?" Zane asked, his voice hollow. He didn't even turn around. The map had knocked the wind out of him, leaving a cold, sinking feeling in his gut.

He’d poured everything he had into passing the final test, into proving himself, only to be crushed by the sudden, sickening realization that he was still nowhere close to his real goal. All that work, all that struggle… and for what? The man he had to defeat was an untouchable force in a world so vast it made him feel insignificant. His ambition felt like a cruel joke.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL PIANO]

"Maddox is on a whole other level," Finn said, his voice low. "He's the strongest man in The Heartlands. I bet if he really wanted to, he could destroy Cloud Hall by himself. But," he added, a conviction in his tone that made Zane pause, "I've got a feeling that one day, you're going to be the one to take him down."

Patrick nodded firmly. "I believe it, too."

Their words cut through the despair. A faint smile touched Zane’s lips. He finally stopped and turned to face them, a genuine grin spreading across his face. "Do you guys really think I can do it?"

"I'm not just saying that to make you feel better," Finn said, his expression dead serious. "Anyone else would think we're crazy, but I honestly believe you can. No question."

Patrick's gaze was just as certain.

[MUSIC: INSPIRING ORCHESTRAL]

Their unwavering faith was like a spark in the darkness, reigniting the fire in his soul. The hollowness in Zane's eyes was replaced by a sharp, focused glint.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

"Alright," Zane declared, his jaw tight with renewed purpose. "If you guys believe in me, then I'll believe in myself." He met their eyes, his voice dropping to a low, chilling promise. "One day, I will kill Maddox."

Zane didn’t know how far his journey would take him, but he knew one thing for sure: sooner or later, he would have to face that impossible enemy. And thanks to his friends, he now dared to believe he could win.
