Ep 298 - A Mountain of Swords
Word count: 1105
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WILDERNESS]
[MUSIC: AWE-INSPIRING ORCHESTRAL]
The mountain looming before Zane looked like some primordial beast.
From its base to its peak, hundreds of thousands of swords were embedded in its slopes, one thrust on top of another.
"These swords..." Zane breathed, completely stunned.
He couldn’t help but gasp, his eyes tracing the countless long swords jutting from the rock face.
The blades near the mountain’s foot weren’t high-grade, but every single one was a Mythic weapon—low, middle, or top-grade. The mountain bristled with a huge variety of them: wide-blades, short-handle swords, serpentine blades, jade hilts, and more.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
The Mythic weapons increased in quality from the mountain’s base upward, creating a gradient of power and craftsmanship. As Zane scrutinized the gleaming blades with widening eyes, a startling revelation struck him.
Not all of them were Mythic weapons—interspersed among them shimmered the unmistakable aura of Spirit Weapons!
His heart raced at the discovery, fingers trembling slightly as he traced the air before them, not daring to touch such treasures. Each weapon seemed to pulse with its own consciousness, as though acknowledging his presence on this sacred ground. The mountain had revealed its first secret, but Zane sensed there were many more layers to uncover in this realm of blades.
[SFX: ETHEREAL RINGING]
The Spirit Weapons covered just the lowest portion of the mountain. Looking higher up, Zane spotted Mythic weapons halfway up the slopes!
To his astonishment, he could see low-grade, middle-grade, and even top-grade Mythic weapons.
Even a low-grade Mythic weapon was valuable enough to drive the warriors of the Eastern Territory into a frenzy. Disciples from declining noble houses like Ethan's, and even family heads like James, didn’t own a single one!
The top Seven Great Houses might have a few low-grade Mythic weapons, but on average, each family would only have a handful at most.
Middle-grade Mythic weapons were even rarer. Only five of the Seven Great Houses owned one. House Sutton and House Walker were the two without.
Each of the Seven Great Houses was fabulously wealthy with a prestigious history. The heritage of House Sutton and House Walker was no less impressive than the other five, which made this all the more shocking to Zane.
He’d long suspected the Cobalt Dragon in his mind had a consciousness of its own. It had never tried to communicate with him until the battlefield competition against Jacob. The dragon's desire for the Drake bone was what finally made it roar to life and speak to him.
It was with the Cobalt Dragon's help that Zane had defeated Jacob and swallowed the Drake bone.
Technically, he hadn’t defeated Jacob on his own; he’d had the Cobalt Dragon’s help.
But it didn’t feel right to give the dragon all the credit, either. After all, Jacob had his own advantage with that Drake Bone Spear.
The birth of a genius always came down to a few things: strength, talent, opportunity, and luck. You couldn’t succeed if you were missing even one.
[SFX: ETHEREAL DRAGON VOICE]
"Yes, you are correct. I am the Cobalt Dragon in your mind," the dragon’s voice confirmed in his head.
It explained that it had awakened when Zane first ignited one of his Dragon Scales.
But after waking, it hadn’t had enough energy to communicate. It had been sealed within The Soulforge for so long that it had grown incredibly weak.
While the Celestial Essence had helped the dragon restore a fraction of its power, there was just so little of it. The essence had only been enough to activate a little over a hundred of the countless scales on its massive body.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL DRAGON THEME]
When the Cobalt Dragon sensed the Drake bone on the battlefield, it finally decided to act. Normally, a Cobalt Dragon wouldn’t give a mere Drake’s spine a second thought. As a True Dragon, it saw Drakes as nothing more than distant, lesser beasts with very little True Dragon blood in them.
But no matter how diluted that blood was, it would still help a dragon as weakened as this one. Even a small supplement could help it slowly recover its strength.
After swallowing the Drake’s spine, the Cobalt Dragon had finally regained enough power to properly communicate with Zane.
"You called this the Killing Sword Mountain?" Zane asked, his thoughts racing. "Is this all in my head? And why can’t I pull any of these swords out?" Since the dragon knew the mountain’s name, it had to know its secrets.
"Ha! The Killing Sword Mountain couldn’t possibly exist only in your mind," the Cobalt Dragon explained with a gentle laugh. "This is a sacred ground for swordsmanship. It can’t be owned by any single person. What’s happening here is like a dream come true. There was a resonance between your soul and the Killing Sword Mountain the moment you achieved the Consummation of sword intent. You’ve been chosen to practice here, Zane. This is an incredible opportunity! As for your opponent, that boy Finn... he must have been taken somewhere else to practice. But I doubt it’s anywhere near as remarkable as this."
The idea that someone else had reached the Consummation of sword intent at the Marrow Refining Level hit Zane like a physical blow. A genius like that shouldn’t exist in the Eastern Territory, or even in the Heartlands. It was a rarity anywhere in the world!
It was truly unbelievable.
But as unsettling as it was, Zane didn’t let it get him down.
There’s always some unfairness in the world, he thought.
He was a perfect example. He’d been thrown in prison at fifteen, while disciples from noble houses got special pills, practiced tier-four cultivation methods, and used Spirit Weapons that even masters from ordinary families could only dream of.
And yet, he had surpassed them all by leaps and bounds.
The path of martial arts was a long one. Maybe it was inconceivable for someone at his level to grasp the Consummation of sword intent, but there was no denying his own power. He had outstripped descendants of great families with supreme bloodlines—people born with gifts others couldn’t even imagine, whose daily expenses could cover everything Zane had ever gained.
But as Zane thought about all the unfairness in the world, he found he didn’t care.
He wasn’t born with a silver spoon, so he’d just have to take everything he wanted for himself, piece by piece.
He took two steps toward the mountain. The nearest swords began to tremble — then sword intent burst from them, aimed directly at him. Marrow Refining level. Someone had reached the consummation of sword intent at the Marrow Refining Level. 
[SFX: SWORD INTENT BURST]
He tightened his grip on the Streamer.
"Break!" Zane roared.
