Ep 443 - We Meet Again

Word count: 1320

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS SPIRITUAL PRESSURE]

[MUSIC: DARK ATMOSPHERIC TENSION]

  The spiritual pressure from the Owl Beast’s ghost radiated from the black sphere, pressing down on the souls of every practitioner inside. But the pressure wasn’t spread evenly.

It was weakest near the edge of the sphere and grew more intense the closer you got to the center.

The sphere was divided into six concentric rings. Each one unleashed a different level of soul pressure, growing exponentially stronger the deeper you went. In other words, the innermost rings were absolute hell. 

Zane, like most of the others, was currently in the outermost ring. But after half a day of training there, he could already feel his soul strengthening. He was ready for the second ring. 

[MUSIC: DETERMINED RISING STRINGS]

The moment his soul felt recovered, he headed straight for it.

The practitioners resting around the Warding Lanterns were stunned. Some of them had been in the black sphere for months and still didn't have the guts to move deeper. But Zane, after just half a day, was already striding toward the second ring.

Zane’s quick progress wasn't just luck; his soul was already tougher than the average person’s before he’d even set foot in the sphere. He also didn’t waste a single second. He could have taken longer breaks in the protected area, but the instant he felt recovered, he was right back in the ring. He spent far more time strengthening himself than he did resting.

In contrast, most practitioners spent more time huddled around the little Warding Lanterns than they did struggling in the outermost ring. The psychic pain was just too much for them to handle for long. A single run might take them two or three hours; in that same amount of time, Zane could knock out dozens.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Just as Zane was about to step into the second ring, a group of four—two men and two women—walked up to the sphere’s edge. Zane recognized them. It was the same group he’d met back at the obelisks. One of them was Christopher.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TONE]

Christopher was considered one of the top talents to ever ascend from the Lower World. His raw skill had been his ticket to the upper world, having conquered the obelisks for both the Spatial and Fire Laws. Because of that, Elder Howard saw Christopher as a top-tier disciple who commanded respect.

But that all went up in smoke the moment Zane showed up. He had completely stolen the spotlight, grasping all the Original Laws—everything except the Time Law—in just three days after his Baptism by Laws. It was the fastest anyone had ever done it in recorded history.

Suddenly, Christopher’s own accomplishments felt like a firefly buzzing next to the sun. From that moment on, a deep-seated grudge against Zane took root.

Christopher had never endured such humiliation. Throughout his entire life, he'd been fed a constant stream of praise for his genius. That unrelenting adulation had shaped him into someone insufferably arrogant, convinced of his own superiority. The problem was, Zane had just collided with that exact brand of mindless, unreasonable pride—and Christopher wasn't the type to let such an affront slide.

His jaw clenched as he watched Zane from across the hall. Every compliment directed at this newcomer felt like a personal attack, each word of admiration a knife twisting in his gut. Christopher's hands trembled slightly at his sides, not from fear, but from barely contained rage.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

"Christopher's going after that guy again," someone whispered nearby.

"Of course he is. You know Christopher—he's never swallowed an insult in his life. Anyone who dares to upstage him pays for it."

A third voice chimed in with obvious satisfaction. "Serves the newcomer right. He completely stole Christopher's thunder. Now he's going to learn what happens when you embarrass someone like Christopher."'

The murmurs rippled through the gathered disciples like wildfire. Some watched with anticipation, eager for the confrontation. Others exchanged knowing glances, already predicting how this would end. Christopher's reputation preceded him—brilliant, talented, and utterly ruthless when his pride was wounded.

[AMBIENT: DISTANT WHISPERS]

The three practitioners who came with Christopher stood by a Warding Lantern, whispering among themselves. Without Christopher’s help at the Dragon Soar Arena, they never would have made it to the upper world. So, of course, they’d take his side.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The other people in the sphere watched the two square off. For a moment, they completely forgot their own training, their eyes glued to the confrontation.

Just training under the intense soul pressure was agony; most practitioners were just grateful to make it back to the Warding Lanterns with their minds intact. The pressure drained all their energy, forcing them to retreat to the lanterns to recover. So to see two people with enough energy left to actually fight in this place? It was insane. Still, as Christopher started chasing Zane down, a sense of worry spread through the crowd.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

Fighting here was incredibly dangerous. If Christopher managed to kill Zane, the intense pressure could shatter his mind and leave him an empty shell.

The black sphere had already turned more than a few great practitioners into mindless husks. Their pride got the better of them; they pushed themselves too hard, trying to withstand the pressure. Eventually, their souls were so damaged that they didn't have the strength to make it back to the Warding Lanterns. The pressure would then finish the job, crushing their souls completely and leaving them catatonic.

[AMBIENT: WORRIED CROWD CHATTER]

"That guy's luck is clearly running out!" one disciple muttered, shaking his head.

Another cultivator crossed his arms, his expression a mixture of concern and disbelief. "If I were him, I'd pull my soul out of there and lay low for a while. No treasure is worth becoming a vegetable."

"Exactly right," a third voice chimed in, tinged with nervous energy. "He'd be fine if he just withdrew from the sphere. Why push himself to the breaking point like this?"

The gathered onlookers exchanged worried glances, though none made any move to intervene. They used the unfolding confrontation as justification to linger near the protective glow of the Warding Lanterns, their own souls still recovering from the sphere's relentless pressure. Despite their words of caution, a morbid curiosity kept them rooted in place.

They settled in to watch the drama unfold, each silently grateful they weren't the ones fighting for their life in that crushing darkness.

A cold blue light radiated from Christopher's soul as he stepped into the second ring — a secret soul technique from his faction, one that blocked the pressure entirely. His three companions followed, their own weaker versions flickering and cracking under the doubled force until they fell back to the nearest Warding Lanterns. Christopher barely noticed. He'd spotted Zane.

[SFX: SOUL IMPACT]

[MUSIC: DANGEROUS CONFRONTATION]

He moved fast. Out here, deep in the sphere, there were no upper-world elders to enforce clan rules — only lower-world ascenders, none of whom would stop him. He'd been waiting for exactly this.

Without a word, he launched a soul strike directly at Zane.

Zane sensed it a half-second too late. He twisted aside, took a glancing hit, and scrambled to the nearest Warding Lantern, letting the yellow light knit his soul back together. He turned to face his attacker.

"I have no quarrel with you," Zane said, his voice flat. "Why?"

Christopher shrugged. "I don't need a reason. I just don't like you."

Zane's eyes narrowed. "A lot of people have said something like that to me. They all regretted it." He let the silence sit for a moment. "Sure you want to keep going?"

Christopher's answer was to raise his hands — and transform them. His soul-fingers stretched and flattened into twin blades.

[SFX: SOUL BLADE FORMING]

Zane recognized it instantly. The same principle as his Psyche Spike — soul shaped into a weapon. Except Christopher's version was sharper, more refined. He'd been training this for years.

Christopher slashed. Zane didn't fight back — he ran, tracing the ring to the next Warding Lantern, buying himself time to think.
