Ep 647 - The Tide of Chaos
Word count: 1015
[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
For Esther, this outbreak of the Tide of Chaos was a massive opportunity. Her planned route only skimmed the outer circle of the Dark Nether Cave, but she’d been hoping for a chance to push deeper. The legends spoke of incredible potential in the unexplored depths—places normally sealed off, but now, the Tide had torn a new path open for them.
Fabian, on the other hand, was thinking the exact opposite.
Venturing into the inner circle was a suicide mission even on a good day, with full preparations. The odds of making it out alive were slim to none. And now, with the Tide of Chaos raging? The danger was off the charts. No one in their right mind would even consider it.
[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]
Zane blinked, his expression dead serious. “Then let’s go into the inner circle.”
[SFX: ANGRY SHOUT]
“Are you insane?” Fabian shot back. “Going in there now is just asking to die!”
Zane glanced over his shoulder at the churning swarm of monsters, then nodded toward the two Purgatory Fire Snakes coiled nearby. He just shrugged. “Staying out here is asking to die, too.”
[AMBIENT: MONSTER GROWLS]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The truth in his words sent a ripple of tension through the group. The only ones who seemed unaffected were the two silent women, Lavender and Yolande, who stood as still as statues. He had a point. Behind them, a terrifying horde of Shadow-maws and Frost Wolves was closing in, more than enough to tear them all to shreds. And while the two Purgatory Fire Snakes ahead were a lesser threat, Esther and Fabian knew getting past them wouldn’t be simple.
They were completely out of options.
After a tense moment, Esther’s voice cut through the fear, firm and decisive. “We’re going into the inner circle!”
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
These were top-level War Lords, every one of them forged in the crucible of battle; they knew how to make a hard call when it counted. And this wasn't even a hard call. The only path that didn’t end in certain death led deeper inside.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
[AMBIENT: BEAST GROWLS]
The moment Esther issued the command, they bolted, sprinting toward their only escape route while Shadow-maws and Frost Wolves closed in with terrifying speed.
"Faster!" someone yelled, their voice raw with desperation.
They pushed harder, legs burning, lungs screaming, racing toward the dark maw of the inner circle with everything they possessed. Zane's heart hammered against his ribs. Around him, he could hear the ragged breathing of his companions—warriors who had faced death countless times, now running for their lives.
Ahead, Zane spotted a distinct dividing line—a sharp border where reality itself seemed to fracture. On this side, the cave retained its familiar texture, its stone walls rough and tangible. Beyond that threshold, everything dissolved into uniform grey, as if the world had been drained of substance.
The boundary grew closer with each desperate stride. Zane's muscles protested, but he drove himself forward. Behind them, the creatures' snarls grew louder, closer. He could feel their presence like a physical weight pressing against his back.
"They're here!" A warrior's scream pierced the air, his voice cracking with raw terror.
[SFX: SWOOPING WINGS]
[MUSIC: HORROR STRINGS]
Thousands of Shadow-maws blotted out the cavern ceiling, circling once before they started to dive.
[SFX: CREATURE ATTACK]
[SFX: DEATH SCREAM]
A War Lord from the Demon Night clan was the first to go. He was engulfed by a living wave of monsters, his scream cut off in a sickening instant. The sight sent a fresh spike of adrenaline and fear through the rest of them. They ran harder, faster, pushing past their limits.
"We're not gonna make it!" Jarrod screamed over the chaos.
Zane didn't slow down. The black fire between his brows was already moving. He spun mid-sprint and released it — a wisp of black flame that shot straight up into the mass of diving Shadow-maws, tearing through dozens in seconds. Fifty birds burned to ash before it pulled back. The ones that hadn't touched it recoiled. It bought them three seconds.
It was just enough.
[SFX: MASSIVE CAVE COLLAPSE]
[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]
Everyone crossed the line. The Shadow-maws hit the boundary like a wall — pulling up hard, wings beating frantically, not one crossing over. The Frost Wolves reached the line a moment later and stopped dead, pacing and whimpering, eyes fixed on the group with frustrated hunger. Neither species took a single step into the grey.
For a moment, there was only the sound of ragged, desperate breathing in the sudden, oppressive silence.
"We wait," Jarrod said between breaths. "They'll lose patience eventually. Then we go back."
Fabian and Esther nodded. Nobody wanted to go deeper.
[AMBIENT: DEEP CAVE]
[SFX: CRYSTAL PULSING]
Catching his breath, Zane felt a familiar thrum against his chest. The Crystal Butterfly. He’d carried it for ages with no reaction, but the moment he’d stepped into this cave, it had started to pulse with a faint light. Vesper’s inheritance… it had to be here. In the inner circle.
Originally, his plan had aligned with Fabian’s: wait out the Tide, then retreat the way they came. He had no more of a death wish than any of them. A warrior’s life was their most valuable asset; everything else was meaningless if you were dead. But the butterfly changed everything. Now, he had to go deeper.
He’d considered just coming back next year. In a year, he’d be a top-level War Lord himself. His Stellar Body would be mature, and with the Transcendent Divine Might, he might even be able to take on a War Sage. He’d be so much more prepared.
But that was the problem. He didn't have a year. He was running out of time.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
Zane looked over at Esther and Fabian. “You guys wait here,” he said, his voice steady despite the adrenaline still coursing through him. “I’m going to explore the inner circle.”
Without a word, Lavender gave her spear a practiced flick and fell in line behind him. Yolande was right beside her.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Fabian and Esther scrambled to block his path, their faces a mask of alarm. “Zane, absolutely not,” Fabian said, his voice firm. “I’m not letting you charge in there to die.”
Esther, desperate, dropped to one knee before Lavender. “My Queen,” she pleaded, “please, reconsider!”
