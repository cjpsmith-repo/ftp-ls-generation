Ep 841 - Nowhere to Hide

Word count: 1067

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

By now, everyone was convinced Callan had some kind of master plan. That giant lodestone he was hauling around was otherwise useless, except for one thing: it was ridiculously tough. Not even a warrior from the Soul Sea Realm could put a dent in it.

The other princes were floored. Why on earth would anyone drag something so pointlessly heavy all this way?

Callan just smirked at their stunned faces. “Believe me now?”

His sister, Fiona, offered a kind smile. “If you want to get through the Slow-Arrow Field, you'll follow us,” she said.

Fredrick just shook his head, a wry grin on his face. “Alright, you win. Frankly, I'm impressed you can even lift that thing.”

[SFX: HEAVY STONE SCRAPING]

[MUSIC: EPIC DRUMS]

Pleased, Callan nodded. He took a deep breath, channeled his Vitality, and hoisted the lodestone. The damn thing was as tall as two men stacked on top of each other, and so heavy that even Callan’s powerful frame strained under the weight. 

Seeing her brother’s face flush a deep red, Fiona held out a hand, unleashing a surge of energy from the red jade bracelet on her wrist. That small boost was all Callan needed to steady himself and start moving.

With their combined strength, the siblings carried the lodestone forward, leading the way. The other warriors stuck close behind them. This might be their only shot at getting into the Feather Emperor's Secret Land, and no one was about to miss it.

Randy nudged Zane with a grin. “Come on! Let's go!”

Zane just rolled his eyes but gave a reluctant nod.

[AMBIENT: WHISTLING ARROWS]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL ORCHESTRA]

A long, snaking line of people formed behind the lodestone, which now served as a massive shield against the thick wall of arrows hurtling toward them. Even knowing the stone was practically unbreakable, a few stomachs dropped at the sight. If this didn't work, they were all dead.

[SFX: CLANK]

They couldn't see past the stone shield, but they heard the sharp, metallic ring of the first arrow bouncing off it.

That single sound was followed by a deafening storm.

[SFX: SERIES OF CLANKS]

The barrage was relentless. The sound was a continuous, roaring cacophony, and soon, both Callan and Fiona’s faces were beet red from the effort. It was obvious they couldn't hold on much longer.

“These arrows are too strong! Help us!” Callan yelled, his voice strained.

The arrows weren't moving at their normal speed; the strange Time Law of the area had slowed them to a crawl. But that didn't change the force of their impact. Each one slammed into the lodestone with its original, terrifying power. Even with the aid of her Semi-Divine Weapon, the red jade bracelet, Fiona was buckling under the assault.

[MUSIC: HEROIC TENSION]

As Callan cried out, the massive stone began to dip. 

But before it could fall, a figure pushed through the crowd. Zane slammed his hands against the lodestone, his muscles straining as he took the brunt of the weight. The moment his power surged through the stone, both Callan and Fiona felt the pressure ease almost entirely off their shoulders. Zane was carrying ninety percent of it on his own.

Fiona glanced over at him. “Thank you.”

Zane's expression stayed flat. “Everyone should be thanking you and your brother. Without the lodestone, I don't think many of us would be making it through.”

Technically, that wasn't entirely true — he could travel through space, slip past the arrows on his own without touching a single one. But there weren't many here who could do the same, and he wasn't about to leave the group behind.

The other warriors, who had been scrambling for cover, could only stare. The thought was unanimous: This guy's strength is unbelievable—no wonder he could wound a Soul Sea Realm warrior.

[SFX: CRACKING STONE]

Someone screamed from the side. “They're coming! The arrows are breaking through!”

[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]

[SFX: ARROWS WHISTLING]

“Hurry up!”

“There are too many of us! We can't all fit!”

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

Despite Zane’s monstrous strength, he was still just one man. The line of people was too wide, and arrows began to slip past the edges of the stone. Chaos erupted. Warriors who couldn't find shelter behind the stone could only stand there and die, or move. People scrambled over each other, a desperate, flailing mess of leaps, rolls, and dives.

Zane's jaw tightened. He crossed his palms and drove them into the face of the lodestone.

[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACT]

With a deep, percussive boom, the stone launched forward — fifty, sixty feet in an instant. The Dragon Scale blazed with power behind the strike. He caught up to it in two strides and hit it again. Another sixty feet. Another twenty from the momentum alone.

“Move! Now!” he roared.

The crowd didn't need to be told twice. First-tier geniuses or not, every last one of them sprinted.

A prince from the Star Field Divine Kingdom shoved his way to the front — broad-shouldered, with the kind of raw physical power that made other warriors give him a wide berth in sparring. Innate divine strength, the sort of gift that made close combat with him a losing proposition for almost anyone his age. He planted himself beside Zane and threw himself into the lodestone.

[SFX: DULL THUD]

The stone moved less than a foot.

A beat of silence passed over the group — or as much silence as a hail of poisoned arrows allowed.

The Star Field prince stared at his own hands. The warriors around him stared too. More than a few of them had been quietly calculating how much they could contribute if they stepped up to help. Those calculations evaporated on the spot.

Zane didn't acknowledge it. He hit the stone again.

[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACT]

Sixty feet.

But the arrows were relentless.

[SFX: ARROW IMPACT]

[SFX: FLESH TEARING]

“Ahhh!”

Screams ripped through the air as arrows found their mark. Flesh blackened and rotted on contact, a horrifyingly fast decay that spread from the wounds.

“We're almost there! Just hold on!” Fiona shouted, her face flushed and slick with sweat. This was her first expedition to the Sacred Feather Sea, and it was far more than she'd bargained for. At first, she’d been thrilled when her father had finally let her come, tired of his overprotective nature stifling her thirst for adventure.

But now, watching the carnage unfold, she finally understood why he’d been so hesitant. The Sacred Feather Sea was every bit as dangerous as he had warned.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Just as the thought crossed her mind, four arrows shot toward her from both sides, boxing her in. 

There was nowhere left to dodge.
