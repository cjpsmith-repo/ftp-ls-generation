Ep 806 - Blast From the Past

Word count: 1108

[AMBIENT: SACRED TEMPLE]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHOIR]

The Holy Maiden of the York Divine Kingdom was a figure of near-mythical reverence. Her family, House White, had been practically unknown until the day she was born. Once a secret order chose her as their Holy Maiden, her family’s name was suddenly on everyone's lips.

It had all started with one of the kingdom’s princes—a man whose talent was ranked ninth in the entire kingdom. Some time ago, a commoner had given him an enchanted lamp, one of only nine in the world, said to lead to the legendary celestial tomb. The prophecy claimed that once all nine were lit, their holders would be granted entry.

For years, the prince and the Holy Maiden lit the lamp together on the third day of the third month, hoping to open the way. But as time passed without a sign, their hope began to fade.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

So, when a shimmering passageway finally did appear, she couldn't believe it. Caught completely off guard, they scrambled to assemble a small retinue, grabbing a few guards from the Life-and-Death Realm before plunging into the tomb.

[MUSIC: DARK DRAMATIC STRINGS]

No one could have predicted the prince would die on that journey. His death sent shockwaves through the York Divine Kingdom. A prince was dead. And while War King York had many children, this son had been special—the ninth most talented person in the kingdom and one of its most promising leaders. To make matters worse, some nobody from the Heartlands had dared to defy them.

It was the Holy Maiden herself who had made the arrangements that followed. She was the one who reached out across the Chaotic Ocean of Stars to contact Maddox and Simon, enlisting two Life-and-Death Realm warriors to support the Divine Kingdom's interests and hunt Zane down.

The kingdom immediately dispatched two mighty warriors from the Soul Sea Realm, Lord Wallace and Master Abel, to investigate. 

Before Wallace departed for the Heartlands, he had told the Holy Maiden something that gave her pause. Zane had not only destroyed his soul projection — he had killed Maddox, a fourth-level Life-and-Death Realm warrior. She had been stunned. Two years ago, Zane had been nothing. Now he had done something that shouldn't have been possible. Whatever Wallace had glimpsed in Zane's mind when his soul was devoured, it had been worth going personally to investigate.

But not a single word was sent back before their life tokens shattered, signaling their deaths. The sudden loss of two masters sent the kingdom’s leadership into a quiet panic, but they held back from acting rashly. After all, if something in the Heartlands was powerful enough to kill two masters, sending two more wouldn't solve the problem.

They kept the deaths a secret and decided to turn their attention to the Sacred Feather Sea for now. The storms over the Chaotic Ocean of Stars would eventually calm, and when they did, they could send a proper team of Soul Sea warriors to the Heartlands. If some new power had truly emerged there, they would simply wipe the entire region from the map.

[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]

Though the Holy Maiden was burning to kill the man responsible—a warrior from the Heartlands named Zane—the deaths of two Soul Sea masters took precedence. She couldn't recklessly send more people after him. All she could do was wait for her chance.

And now, hearing Randy call out the name “Zane Jones,” her eyes snapped to him. Two years had passed, and his face had matured, but a person’s spirit, their very aura, never changed.

Zane Jones? she thought. So close to… Zane Lowell.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

A cold smile spread across her face. She walked to the edge of the platform and floated gracefully into the air, her eyes locking onto Zane with pure, unadulterated hatred.

Seeing the Holy Maiden suddenly take flight, a flicker of confusion crossed War King York’s face. “Holy Maiden,” he asked, “what are you doing?”

She turned to him slowly, her smile never wavering. “Your Majesty, I’ve just found something very interesting.”

“Something interesting?” War King York’s brow furrowed, his expression clouding over.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

“Mhm.” She just nodded before turning her cold gaze back to Zane. “Zane Jones? Humph. Isn’t your real name Zane Lowell?”

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Since Zane had chosen a path that put him in the spotlight, he had expected this. He was ready for the Holy Maiden to recognize him and had already worked out a few ways to handle it. He’d been planning to fly into the enchanted barrier to challenge Logan, who was waiting for him with a contemptuous smirk. Logan was clearly thinking the same thing as everyone else: the Harlen Divine Kingdom must be truly desperate if they were sending a mid-stage Spirit-Tribulation fighter against him.

After the Holy Maiden spoke, Zane looked up, mirroring her strange look right back at her. Then he smiled. “My name is Zane Jones. I don’t know this ‘Lowell’ you’re speaking of. Why do you ask?”

Randy's figure flashed. He planted himself squarely between the Holy Maiden and Zane, his voice dropping to something cold and flat. “Zane Jones is a warrior of the Harlen Divine Kingdom. He fights out of the Crystal Martial House. Holy Maiden, if York is having second thoughts about the bout, Logan is welcome to concede.”

The Emperor of Harlen said nothing. He simply looked at the Holy Maiden with calm, level eyes. The silence carried its own weight.

“Whether he’s the one or not, I’ll still kill him,” Logan declared with a confident grin.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

A flash of hesitation crossed the Holy Maiden’s face as she tilted her head. Then, her eyes lit up. She turned back to the emperor. “Your Majesty, I am certain this man is Zane Lowell from the Heartlands. He’s the one who killed the prince! If you don’t believe me, you can ask Elder Roland!”

When the prince had entered the tomb, he’d taken five bodyguards from the Life-and-Death Realm with him. After his son's death, the enraged War King had executed four of them on the spot. Only Elder Roland was spared, thanks to a lifetime of heroic service. Now, he was the only one who could identify the killer.

“Holy Maiden, are you certain?” War King York’s already grim expression darkened further. “Is this the man who killed my son?”

Honestly, the Holy Maiden’s outburst was rash, even rude. But the emperor knew that while she could be eccentric, she wasn’t one to make things up in a moment like this.
