Ep 127 - Apex Dragon

[AMBIENT: BUSY STREET]
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
Josef bolted, Zane hot on his heels. They burst from the barracks and out onto the open street.
Josef was fast, but with Zane dogging his every step, he started pulling out every trick in the book. When his throwing knives had no effect, he whipped out a brown parchment scroll. "Armored Soldier!" he yelled, throwing it into the air.
[SFX: MAGICAL FLAMES]
The scroll burst into brilliant flames, summoning a fully armored soldier that materialized from the inferno. With mechanical precision, the conjured warrior raised its gleaming sword and charged toward Zane, its blade whistling through the air as it slashed downward.
"Fall apart!" Zane commanded, his voice resonating with absolute certainty. He met the attack with perfect timing, his fist connecting with the armored figure's chest.
[SFX: SHARP CRACK]
A single, sharp crack echoed through the chaotic street, the sound crisp and definitive. The soldier—Josef's magical defense—shattered into countless fragments of light before vanishing as swiftly as it had appeared, leaving only wisps of dissipating energy in its wake.
Without missing a beat, Zane flexed his hand, shaking it out casually as he closed the distance again. Trapped, Josef gritted his teeth. In a final, desperate move, he crushed the pendant hanging from his neck.
[SFX: MAGICAL PENDANT CRUSHING]
[AMBIENT: EERIE GLOW]
It was a Sterling family heirloom, given to him when he became an advisor to the Imperial Army—a last resort, promised to save his life in a moment of mortal danger. The instant he crushed it, a pale blue shield of light flared to life, enveloping him in an eerie glow.
"Break!" Zane yelled, slamming his fist into the shield.
[SFX: SHIELD IMPACT]
The shield flickered for a second but immediately pulsed back to full strength.
Zane frowned. "That strong?" The shield had withstood the full force of his punch. It had to be a Sterling family heirloom, imbued with the power of his ancestors.
Zane hammered the shield a dozen more times in rapid succession. It shuddered violently, but he couldn't even scratch its surface.


"Now you die!" A gleaming dagger materialized in Josef's hand. He lunged, aiming for Zane's neck. Josef knew he wasn't the strongest fighter, so he always carried hidden tricks like this. In a tight spot, they could be lifesavers.
[SFX: PSYCHE SPIKE SCREAM]
But just as the dagger was about to strike home, a searing pain exploded in Josef's mind. He cried out, clutching his head as he collapsed, writhing on the ground.
"Psyche Spike," Zane said, his voice flat. He looked down at the groaning man, his eyes merciless. As the paralyzing attack wore off, Zane moved his now-numb body and picked up the dagger Josef had dropped. In a flash, he was standing over him.
"Halt! Strike down an Imperial Army advisor, and you'll earn the enmity of the entire Empire!" The warning echoed through the alley, sharp and desperate.
[AMBIENT: DARK ALLEY]
[SFX: MENACING GROWL]
A dark figure materialized from the shadows, his throat emanating a menacing growl that reverberated in the confined space. Zane registered the threat but remained undeterred, his grip tightening on the dagger as memories of Josef's cruelty toward Ingrid flashed through his mind.
[SFX: BLADE STABBING FLESH]
"Another Imperial lackey?" Zane's brow furrowed, his eyes hardening with resolve as he plunged the blade into Josef's chest. The steel slid between ribs with sickening ease. "Your reign of terror ends here," he whispered, watching the light fade from Josef's eyes. With a swift motion, he extracted the blade, sending crimson droplets spattering across the dead autumn leaves like macabre raindrops.
The metallic scent of blood filled Zane's nostrils, turning his stomach. Taking lives never sat well with him—each death etched another scar on his conscience. Yet he understood the harsh reality: without the Psyche Spike technique, their positions would be reversed, with his corpse cooling on the ground instead. The weight of his decision pressed upon him, but he couldn't afford the luxury of mercy for a man who had moments ago attempted to murder him in cold blood and threatened unspeakable horrors against Ingrid.
[SFX: ROARING BATTLE CRY]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
"You're dead!" the general roared, charging forward. His Life Force erupted, taking the form of a massive Apex Dragon wreathed in roaring flames. A wave of heat washed over Zane, crackling with terrifying power.
"A Soul Realm master," Zane sneered. This was only the second one he'd ever faced. The first had been Adrian, and against that kind of power, Zane knew he didn't stand a chance.
A Soul Realm master could burn their Life Force to multiply their power tenfold, crushing Nature Spirits as easily as a man steps on an ant. Zane knew exactly what he was up against. His only option was to run.
But the dragon, a beast of pure Life Force, seemed to anticipate his retreat. It lunged, its jaws wide, ready to swallow him whole.
Just then, Ingrid yelled, "Zane, get down!"
Without a second thought, Zane dove flat onto the ground.
[SFX: LOUD BLAST]
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[SFX: DRAGON HOWL]
A split-second later, a loud bang echoed as a Concussive Blast shot over his head and slammed right into the Apex Dragon.
Ingrid's Concussive Blast packed the punch of a Soul Realm master's attack, but her control over it was shaky. The pocket pistol was powerful, just not always accurate.
Power gaps were like walls. Sometimes you could climb them with the right weapon, a unique technique, or just raw talent. But the chasm between him and the general was too wide to cross.
Facing the terrifying Apex Dragon, Zane felt his own Life Energy surge from his core. It crashed forward, gathering in his fists as he prepared to unleash his Titan Strike: the Earthshatter.
This very punch had once deflected a Soul Realm cultivator's full-force strike, and since then, both his technique and strength had flourished considerably. Facing this general stirred far less dread within him than his confrontation with Adrian had—this opponent didn't approach that formidable threshold.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
With resolute determination, Zane unleashed his attack. Nether Essence erupted from his clenched fist, materializing into a colossal purple-black phantom hand that thundered forward, colliding with devastating impact against the immense dragon—the shockwave of their collision reverberating through the very air itself.
