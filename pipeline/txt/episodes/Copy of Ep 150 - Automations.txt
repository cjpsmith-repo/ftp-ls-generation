[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
[MUSIC: TENSE UNDERCURRENT]
 To House Pauling, Walsh was an acceptable loss. Zane had only managed to kill Walsh, who everyone knew was the weakest fighter among the Great Houses. The laughter rippled through the onlookers. *The nerve of that kid, threatening to kill anyone who crossed him. What a joke.* The mockery continued until a long-haired woman finally cut it short.
"Leon, kill the boy. And don't disappoint me," she said, her voice sharp. "He may have only killed Walsh, but there's still something... off about him. Be careful."
Walsh was only a rank six in the Nature Level. Leon was a rank eight—the power difference was massive. Killing Walsh, who wasn't all that strong to begin with, only made the members of the Great Houses look down on Zane even more. Their caution faded. They figured he'd just gotten lucky.
But Leon was on another level. Zane was barely half a step into the Nature Level. Luck wouldn't be enough to close a gap that wide. As far as the high-and-mighty houses were concerned, the kid was already dead. Still, a few of them shot curious glances his way, wondering how the masked kid had actually managed to pull it off.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BUILDUP]
 At the woman's command, Leon started toward Zane, his approach deceptively casual. Leon was nothing like Walsh. Walsh was an incompetent assassin who couldn't keep his mouth shut. Leon, on the other hand, was quiet, all business. He never underestimated an opponent. The masked kid might only be half a step into the Nature Level, but Leon would treat him as a genuine threat. He wouldn't give the boy a single opening.
Zane let out an exasperated sigh. "Fine."
No one was listening. They mocked him, blew him off—it didn't matter what he did. Threaten them, beg for his life, it was all the same. The only way to make them take him seriously was to kill them. Only then would they finally understand he wasn't someone to be trifled with, not some stray they could just kick around.
[SFX: ENERGY CRYSTALLIZING]
 Leon approached with eerie deliberation, his penetrating gaze never wavering from Zane's face. Life Force gathered around his hands, swirling like emerald mist before crystallizing into twin blades of vibrant green, their edges humming with lethal intent.
When he closed to about twelve feet away, Leon's movements shifted into something unnervingly unnatural. The spectators murmured in confusion as his body appeared to bend the rules of space, somehow circling *behind* Zane while his gleaming swords remained poised to impale the young man through the chest. The visual contradiction made several onlookers blink and shake their heads.
[SFX: BATTLE SOUNDS]
Zane's muscles tensed as he pivoted to face the threat, but Leon had anticipated this reaction. With serpentine grace, the experienced fighter vaulted over him, his form briefly eclipsing the sun. The green swords swept through the air with calculated precision, creating an inescapable cage of deadly light. Panic flickered across Zane's face as he realized every potential escape route had been methodically sealed.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
 "Killzone!" Leon announced with quiet confidence, a rare smile touching his otherwise impassive features. The name of his technique hung in the air like a death sentence.
The blur of Leon's green swords looked chaotic, but they had methodically sealed every possible escape route. Leon never took chances, and he was certain Zane would die today. No one had ever survived his Killzone. But as Leon swooped in for the kill, swords flashing, he met his opponent's eyes and felt a sudden, icy chill. He'd only ever seen that look…
A sharp spike of soul energy shot from between Zane's brows — invisible, instant. Leon's concentration shattered. The green swords dissolved mid-air as agony tore through his soul.
[SFX: GROUND TREMBLING]
 Suddenly, the earth beneath them trembled with ominous intensity.
Zane's gaze darted toward the Starlight Arch, his senses instantly heightening as adrenaline coursed through his veins. The imminent threat of Leon's attack momentarily forgotten, he braced himself against the vibrations rippling through the square.
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC BATTLEFIELD]
[SFX: PANTHER ROAR]
 A magnificent Storm Panther burst through the arch, its electric-blue fur crackling with energy as it bounded forward in desperate flight. Mere heartbeats later, two colossal figures emerged in pursuit, their massive War Hammers clutched in iron grips. The giants moved with an unsettling mechanical precision, their joints clicking and whirring like enormous clockwork contraptions. Despite their terrifying power, they possessed no Life Force signature—hollow vessels animated by some unknown means.
[SFX: MECHANICAL WHIRRING]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS CRESCENDO]
 "Impossible," Zane whispered, his throat constricting with a mixture of awe and dread. While the Eastern Territory boasted renowned craftsmen capable of constructing formidable puppets, no artisan had ever fashioned anything with such uncanny verisimilitude. These Automatons transcended all known boundaries of craftsmanship, looming hundreds of meters tall like mountains given terrible purpose. Their metal skin gleamed in the sunlight, ancient runes etched across their surfaces pulsing with arcane energy that made Zane's own Spirit Runes tingle in response.
The ground vibrated violently with each of their thunderous steps. The shockwaves threw Zane and the others off their feet, tossing them into the air again and again. Fortunately, everyone present, Zane included, was a skilled fighter. The tremors alone would have killed an ordinary person.
Next to the colossal Automatons, the sprinting Storm Panther looked like a tiny ant.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACT]
One of the giants heaved its War Hammer, sending it hurtling toward the Storm Panther. The panther was impossibly fast. It shot forward like a bolt of lightning, dodging the cataclysmic blow just in time.
The hammers wielded by the giants loomed impossibly vast, each one rivaling a small mountain in size. Zane's mind reeled, unable to comprehend the devastation such weapons would inflict upon human flesh—would anything even remain to identify the victim?
[SFX: MASSIVE HAMMER IMPACT]
[AMBIENT: DESTRUCTIVE SHOCKWAVE]
 When one of the colossal hammers finally struck the ground, it unleashed a visible ripple of concentrated force that radiated outward from the impact point. The energy wave tore across the square with unstoppable momentum, carrying the destructive power of a tsunami about to engulf everything in its path.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE CRESCENDO]
 "Gods above, my Storm Panther!" shrieked a man nearby, his voice cracking with raw terror. His face contorted in horror as he watched the deadly shockwave race toward his prized creature. "If that blast connects, it's obliterated! We'll never breach the Inner Arch in time!" His final words hung in the air as the wave of destruction surged forward, seconds away from deciding all their fates.
