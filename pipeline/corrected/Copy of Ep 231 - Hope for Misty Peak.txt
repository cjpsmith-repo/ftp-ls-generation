Ep 231 - Hope for Misty Peak
Word Count: 1204
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
Thanks to his unbelievable performance at the trial, Zane was the only thing people could talk about.
The word was that no one taking the trial could break fifty-five thousand points. Even the legendary Eliot, who had reached Consummation, had never managed to hit that mark.
So, what did that mean, if it was something even Eliot couldn't do? It meant Zane's combat level had surpassed Consummation. Hell, he was probably on par with a master in the Soul Realm.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN TOP COMPOUND]
[SFX: EXCITED MURMURS]
Awe and hope spread through the disciples like wildfire.
Rankings among the Thirty-Three Peaks were a huge deal; they decided a peak's standing, its resources, and its funding. The monthly allowance for disciples at top-tier places like Skytop Peak and Blackrock Peak was enough to make the disciples from Misty Peak sick with envy.
On top of that, the Skyreach Sect sent masters to construct large-scale magic arrays, creating secret cultivation sanctums. These were private training grounds where the inner disciples from those peaks could hone their techniques in total seclusion.
These secret sanctums probably couldn't compare to the ones that cost a fortune, but that hardly mattered. They were still a world away from anything Misty Peak had to offer.
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
The Skyreach Sect operated on a simple principle: those with power received the finest of everything.
This stark reality explained why cultivating genuine strength at Misty Peak proved nearly impossible. Throughout its storied history, only Eliot had ascended to remarkable heights, yet even his journey stalled out at Consummation, greater power remaining forever beyond his grasp.
Meanwhile, Skytop Peak and other top peaks lured in gifted disciples with their lavish resources and unparalleled opportunities. Over time, the gap between them grew wider; the powerful only got more powerful, while the weak fell even further behind. Disciples at Misty often gazed toward the privileged peaks with a mixture of envy and resignation, their dreams constrained by circumstance.
Lady Isla's arrival had brought modest improvements, her determined leadership breathing fresh life into their neglected halls. But her considerable talents alone couldn't reverse generations of institutional disadvantage. Even she couldn't convince promising new recruits to choose Misty over the seductive advantages offered by Skytop or Blackrock. After all, why would anyone willingly choose to study at the weakest peak in the Sect?
But now, Misty Peak finally had a glimmer of genuine hope in Zane. His extraordinary abilities made him the caliber of talent that might challenge the dominance of the other thirty-two peaks and elevate Misty's standing. His presence sent ripples of excitement through the once-despondent disciples.
No one indulged in fantasies about claiming the top spot---such dreams remained too distant to contemplate. But if Zane could achieve just a modest advancement to the top fifty, or even the top thirty, Misty Peak might finally shed its humiliating reputation as the Sect's perpetual underachiever. Senior disciples spoke in hushed, reverent tones about possibilities that had seemed unthinkable mere months ago.
Many disciples, their eyes bright with newfound optimism, speculated that Zane might replicate Elara's remarkable achievement from several years past.
[MUSIC: HEROIC THEME]
Since Elara had only been imprisoned on Infernal Peak for the last two years, plenty of disciples still remembered her incredible record. It was unheard of for a woman to rank so high, but she'd steamrolled so many opponents on her way to the finals. Through her efforts alone, she'd secured the number two spot for Maiden's Peak.
The more gossip-prone disciples noticed that Elara and Zane shared a last name: Lowell. Whispers started spreading that the two might be siblings.
But for all their digging, the gossips couldn't find a shred of proof of their relationship. All they could do was speculate.
Besides, it was pure conjecture. Lowell was one of the most common last names in the world---how could anyone possibly connect them based on that alone?
[AMBIENT: QUIET CONTEMPLATION]
Steward Rogers was getting used to Zane's rapid progress, but something was still bugging him. A few days ago, he'd reported Zane's training to the leader, figuring a rare talent like him shouldn't go unnoticed by the Sect.
Steward Rogers had expected some kind of reward for his diligence, but he never thought the leader's response would be a flat-out denial.
The two words---"Not approved"---spoke volumes. Talented outer disciples were groomed to become inner disciples, who in turn could be promoted to be personal students of the masters. Above all that, every disciple was a potential candidate for the prestigious Cloud Road.
The Cloud Road would never overlook a talent like Zane, and neither would the Sect itself. That's why Steward Rogers had reported it, but it had been useless. The terse reply suggested something was wrong. Maybe Zane had offended someone important, or maybe he was caught up in something far more complicated.
Unlike Steward Carter, who kept reporting things and ended up in hot water for it, Steward Rogers knew when to back off.
A few days later, Isla found him with unusual urgency in her step. "I have to leave for a while," she said. "Two months, maybe less. I'll be back before the competition." She didn't explain further, and Zane didn't ask.
But after she left, he asked around quietly. None of the other mentors knew anything either.
He thought of her sitting alone in the pavilion that evening, staring at nothing. She was a princess in the middle of a succession crisis — the kind of storm that had swallowed stronger people than her. Zane couldn't do much about that. Not yet. But he filed it away.
There were two people he intended to keep alive through whatever came next: Elara, and now Isla.
[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION ROOM]
Zane devoted every waking moment to his cultivation, while nights were spent in intense study of the Titan Strike. Free time had become a distant memory, replaced by relentless determination.
The fifth-tier refinement method offered far more than mere Corrupt Vitality; it presented a pathway to unprecedented power. Titan Strike, the first technique he'd mastered, delivered a devastating single-target strike that drained his Vitality reserves considerably.
Initially, he'd dismissed Ogre Horde as a simple follow-up technique. However, as he immersed himself in meditation, peeling back the layers of ancient wisdom embedded within the method, he discovered its profound complexity. The technique's true nature revealed itself gradually---a manifestation ability allowing him to shape his Vitality into shadowy, demonic phantoms.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL SANCTUM]
Sitting cross-legged in the secret cultivation room, Zane felt a familiar tingle of anticipation course through his veins. The chamber's mystical energy pulsed around him as he laced his fingers together, twisting his body into the intricate stance required for the technique. Beads of sweat formed on his brow as he concentrated, his muscles taut with effort.
"Ogre Horde!" he called, his voice reverberating throughout the chamber with otherworldly resonance.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY WHIRRING]
A low, ominous whirring filled the air as dark purple Vitality began to unfurl around his form like tendrils of midnight smoke. The energy responded to his will, coalescing and shifting with each subtle movement of his hands, promising terrible power to those who could master its secrets.
