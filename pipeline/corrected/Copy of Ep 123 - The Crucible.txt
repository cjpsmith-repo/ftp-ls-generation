Ep 123 - The Crucible
Word Count: 1359
[AMBIENT: MEDICAL ROOM]
 Payne shuffled over and peered down at Zane, his brow furrowing at the sight of the wounds covering the young man’s body. He turned to Lennie.
"This guy's been run through with so many Bone Spurs he looks like a damn sieve. It's brutal."
"If he weren't this messed up, I wouldn't have bothered you," Lennie shot back.
Lennie had a whole stash of Energy Capsules, but Zane was on death's door. This was beyond a simple fix; bringing him to Payne was his only move.
Payne extended a thick, stubby finger, carefully pressing against Zane's neck and working around the Healing Needles already in place. His hand traveled down Zane's torso, probing gently until it rested on his stomach.
[SFX: OMINOUS PULSE]
 Payne’s frown deepened as he felt something… unusual.
"This guy's body… It's strange," Payne murmured, a look of surprise on his face. "I've worked on thousands of people, and I've never felt anything like this."
"Ha! The kid took down several Demon Commanders solo, and he's just a High-level candidate. I'd be more shocked if he wasn't special!"
Lennie wasn't surprised. The powerful always had an ace up their sleeve—a special Meditation Technique, a unique magic weapon, or some crazy innate ability. The world was huge, filled with people who could pull off what looked like miracles. After all they'd seen, Lennie and Payne weren't easily fazed.
Lennie added, "Alright, Payne, cut the crap. You're just trying to drive up the price. Don't even try it."
Payne cracked a sheepish grin. "I'm serious. Feel his Mana Channels. They're hard as steel. It’s like he’s not made of flesh and blood. You find me one other person like this in the whole damn world?"
[SFX: COINS COUNTED]
Payne held up three fingers.
"Thirty Crystal Cubes?" Lennie asked.
Payne's smile sharpened. "Three thousand."
Lennie's face went scarlet. "Three thousand — you might as well just kill me!"
Payne shrugged. "Take him elsewhere then. You won't find anyone else in Silverspire who can do what I can."
"I'll pay," Ingrid said quietly.
"You will not." Lennie's hand went up. He exhaled through his teeth. "Fine."
Payne waved a hand. "Touching. Now get out — both of you — while I work."
[AMBIENT: MEDICAL CHAMBER]
Six hours later, Payne emerged from the room, his forehead damp with sweat.
[AMBIENT: HOSPITAL HALLWAY]
 When Payne stepped out of the room, Ingrid rushed up to him. "Doctor Hale, How is he?" she asked, her voice tight with worry.
Payne chuckled. "Relax. You'd think he caught some incurable plague. He was just banged up. He's fine—he'll be on his feet in a day or two."
Zane had been critically wounded. If he'd gotten a high-grade Energy Capsule like the Crimson Haze Pill right after the fight, he might have recovered on his own. But too much time had passed, his body had grown too weak, and Payne's intervention had become a necessity.
[AMBIENT: QUIET RECOVERY ROOM]
 Zane had been lost in a dreamless void from the moment Lennie placed the Healing Needles to the moment he woke. It took him a few minutes for the world—and his memory—to come rushing back.
Zane blinked, finding himself on a soft bed. He reached a hand up, gently touching his chest. His eyes widened in shock. The wounds… they were gone.
"I'm… okay? The wounds are all healed?"
Realizing he was completely whole, Zane sat up, then swung his legs over the side of the bed. He hopped down, stretched his limbs, and paced the room a few times, testing his body while trying to channel his Life Energy. 
He felt perfectly fine.
[MUSIC: TENSE FLASHBACK]
 Even through the deep, dreamless sleep, the memories before he passed out were crystal clear. He'd faced The Infernal Six in a desperate fight for his life. He remembered unleashing his Corrupt Vitality, killing every last one of them.
But even for a Living Weapon like him, recovering from the Demon Commander's brutal Bone Spurs was no small feat. And just before the darkness took him, he remembered it: a rain of countless tiny silver swords, piercing the demon general.
The memory sharpened: he’d been trying to save a Core Apprentice. Ingrid.
[SFX: DOOR CREAKING OPEN]
 Just then, the door creaked open. A young woman in a light purple dress stood in the doorway, a slender gold barrette shimmering in her hair. She was so beautiful it knocked the air right out of him.
"Why are you staring at me like that?" she asked with a gentle smile.
Zane blinked. The voice was familiar. "Ingrid?" he finally managed to ask.
He almost hadn't recognized her. The first time he'd seen her, she was bundled up in a Shadow Cloak. Now, in a simple dress, she looked completely different.
"Please, call me Ingrid," she said softly, a faint blush creeping up her cheeks. She'd never asked a man to use her first name before.
"Ingrid," Zane repeated with a nod, not thinking much of it.
 "Where are we?" Zane asked, glancing out the window at the tall, white buildings that filled the view. He didn't recognize the place at all.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS WALKING]
 Ingrid laughed. "Come on, I'll show you." She turned and led the way out of the room, Zane following with a small smile.
As they walked, Ingrid announced, "Welcome to Silverspire!"
"Silverspire?" Zane's head snapped around, taking everything in. "The one guarded by the imperial army?" He remembered reading about it—a city carved from white dolostone, the main line of defense for The Underworld.
Ingrid led him further — up through the city's interior and onto the walls themselves. Silverspire was built into a mountainside; standing on the ramparts was like standing on a peak.
[AMBIENT: OPEN AIR — WIND, DISTANT RUMBLE]
Zane looked out and went still.
In the far distance, a massive black rift split the sky from earth to horizon — jagged, crackling with lightning, a wound in the world that had never healed. Below it, darkness moved. Not shadow — armies. Demon Soldiers and Commanders, massed in numbers that made the forest ambush look like nothing.
"The Crucible," Ingrid said quietly beside him.
He could see where the two towers had stood. The gaps where they weren't anymore.
"My uncle said the demon race sent four Demon Kings this time," Ingrid said. "That's never happened before. They may be trying to take Silverspire in a single push."
Zane looked at the massing darkness below. If Silverspire fell, there was nothing between the demon army and the imperial capital.
He said nothing for a long moment. Then: "Let's go find the others."
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR CROWD]
[SFX: DOOR OPENING]
 The moment they stepped outside, a crowd of disciples noticed him. A murmur went through the group, their worried expressions turning to relief.
Zane just offered a small shrug and a reassuring smile. "See? I'm fine."
Dalton and the others from Eagle Peak pushed forward, quickly surrounding Zane, their relief obvious. In seconds, all the disciples were gathered around him, chattering excitedly.
Zane's smile faded slightly as he took in the crowd. He knew the Skyreach Sect had suffered heavy losses on this mission.
Nearly every peak had lost someone. Blackrock Peak was hit the hardest—all ten of their members had died protecting the disciples from Maiden's Peak.
On top of that, the Demon Commander had completely wiped out three other peaks.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
 As everyone was talking, a group from Stormcrown Peak swaggered into the camp. The moment the disciples from Eagle, Misty, and Maiden's Peak saw them, the mood soured.
A tense confrontation with Stormcrown Peak had erupted immediately after Zane's pursuit of the demon general. They had retreated at the time, undoubtedly recognizing Eagle Peak's superior strength.
Now, however, within the protective walls of Silverspire, their leader Preston had reverted to his characteristic arrogance. Whispers circulated that his elder brother wielded considerable influence as a high-ranking officer in the imperial forces stationed here.
[MUSIC: THREATENING UNDERTONES]
 At that precise moment, Preston's eyes locked onto Zane across the crowded space. A calculating smile crept across his face—predatory and full of malice—as he cut through the throng with purposeful strides, never breaking his gaze from his target.
