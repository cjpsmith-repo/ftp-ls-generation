Ep 833 - Still Standing

Word count: 1059

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

It was Fiona who had spoken — and the word hung in the air like a blade as every eye turned toward her.

Her interruption shattered what little patience War King York had left. His face went dark as he spun on War King Wind, his voice a low hiss. “Get your daughter in line.”

But even War King Wind had no idea what his daughter was thinking.

[SFX: MAGICAL HUM]

Fiona drifted toward the enchanted barrier, landing gracefully at its very edge. She held out her left arm, and with a soft hum, the red jade bracelet on her wrist slid off. Holding it by a single finger, she offered it to Zane.

“I’ll lend this to you,” she said.

[AMBIENT: CROWD GASP]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

A collective gasp rippled through the crowd. Zane himself looked completely stunned. The bracelet wasn't just some trinket; it was a Semi-Divine Weapon. And she was offering it to him as casually as if it were a sparring glove.

“It’ll make you much stronger,” she insisted, urging him to take it.

A slow smile spread across Zane’s face as he saw how serious she was. “I appreciate the thought, but I don’t think I’ll need it.”

Fiona blinked, her expression one of pure innocence. “What? Why not?”

Their quiet exchange only stoked the War King’s rage. “Little girl,” he snarled, his eyes fixed on Fiona, “this barrier wasn’t built to withstand a punch from me. I suggest you get far away from here before you get hurt.”

[SFX: WHOOSH]

That was all War King Wind needed to hear. He vaulted from the stands, rushing to the edge of the arena and pulling his daughter away from the chaos about to erupt. 

War King York was a master of the Soul Sea Realm, after all, and nobody knew for sure if the barrier could take a direct hit from him.

[AMBIENT: PANICKED CROWD]

[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]

Panic flared among those standing closest. If that barrier broke, they were dead meat. In an instant, the saint-level prodigies scrambled back, putting a good ten thousand feet of distance between themselves and the square.

The warriors of the Life-and-Death and Soul Sea Realms, however, stood their ground. They were strong enough to weather a blow from York.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]

The War King turned his murderous gaze back to Zane. “You brought this on yourself, you brat. You have only yourself to blame for angering the York Divine Kingdom. Now go to hell!” A violet light flared around his fist as he threw a punch aimed straight at Zane’s head.

[SFX: RUSHING AIR]

The punch seemed to swallow the air around it. Even Zane's breath felt blocked for an instant.

In that same instant, Zane reached out with his will — and connected with the West Dragon River.

The blow landed.

[SFX: THUNDEROUS BOOM]

Smoke. Dust. A roar like the world splitting open.

The saint-level warriors who had retreated to the far edge of the square squinted through the haze, waiting for the worst. When the dust began to settle, one of them grabbed another's arm.

“He's still standing.”

Zane hadn't moved a single step.

[SFX: DISTANT RUMBLING]

Then the sound came — not from the barrier, but from beyond it. From the mountain range flanking the West Dragon River. A deep, grinding crack, then another, and then a cascade of collapses as more than twenty mountains gave way at once, sending walls of smoke and stone thundering into the river below.

[AMBIENT: DISTANT CRASHING, ROLLING THUNDER]

The billowing dust swept outward like a curtain, swallowing the riverbank whole.

Even Zane was quietly stunned. Eighty percent of a Soul Sea master's power — and it had taken twenty mountains to absorb it.

The Soul Sea masters on the platform stared.

“He transferred it,” one said, barely above a whisper. “Just like the Little Monster.”

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]

The realization moved through them like a current. If Zane had truly inherited the Little Monster's ability — if he could transfer force the same way — then their hope wasn't dead after all. Maybe Zane was now the key to their own breakthrough.

It was that thought that kept them silent. None of them stepped forward to stop what came next.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]

In York’s mind, Zane was already dead. The sheer power of a Soul Sea Realm master was no joke, and he was holding nothing back. He’d once controlled the Little Monster and benefited immensely from its power. But now that Zane had seemingly inherited that power, the other masters would be the ones to capitalize on it, leaving him with nothing. The thought was unbearable. 

He had to kill Zane, right here and now, whether the kid truly had the creature’s ability or not. All York wanted was to see him broken at his feet.

York's eyes blazed. He drew back his fist again — this time, he would hold nothing back.

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL CRESCENDO]

“War King York, stop this right now!” the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom shouted.

“York, let’s investigate first!” War King Wind added, his voice strained. “What if Zane —”

[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]

But it was too late. The attack was too fast. York’s fist was already inches from Zane’s face.

Zane’s expression hardened. “You really think one hundred percent of your power is enough to take me down?” he growled, his voice low and dangerous. “Going back on your word… Shameless.”

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

With a surge of will, he unleashed every Dragon Scale coiled within his body.

With the scales active, Zane’s strength suddenly rivaled the War King’s. On top of that, he still had the ability to redirect any attack thrown at him, just as he had before. 

York was about to be hit with the combined might of Zane’s Dragon Scales, his explosive energy, and his own raw power, all at once.

The sudden, terrifying power now radiating from Zane stunned the War King.

When Zane’s fist connected with York’s, it felt like being struck by a falling mountain.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

[SFX: BODY HITTING GROUND]

The punch sent the War King flying backward, and he hit the ground with a sickening thud, coughing up a spray of blood.
