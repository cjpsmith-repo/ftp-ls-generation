Ep 1035 - Different Types Of Power

Word count: 1086

[AMBIENT: ILLUSIONARY ARENA]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

Fenton was probably the first real talent Zane had fought since coming to the Upper World.

Winning or losing didn’t matter. Dying in the Illusionary Arena was no big deal, after all. But this fight? This was a chance for Zane to see what he was really made of.

So, when Fenton launched his third attack, Zane braced himself.

[SFX: TORNADO FORMING]

[SFX: GOLDEN SHIMMER]

Out of nowhere, fierce gales swirled to life in the endless arena, circling Zane.

Soon, the formless winds began to compress, twisting into a powerful tornado right in front of him, shimmering with a golden light.

“Metal Law,” Zane’s eyebrows twitched. “A perfect fusion of two Laws?”

[SFX: MATERIALIZATION]

Fenton’s form materialized beside the coalescing winds. There was no need to hide anymore.

He was surprised that Zane had deflected his second attack so easily.

Still, Zane’s power was about what he’d expected.

Fenton wasn’t treating Zane like some random warrior from the Lower World. And as much as he couldn’t stand the smug foresight of the Thirteen Palaces, he had to admit they knew how to pick ‘em. Zane definitely had a few tricks up his sleeve.

But that’s all they were: tricks. An opponent like this didn’t even require his full strength. He was sure Zane wouldn’t be able to block this third strike.

[SFX: WIND WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

As the wind and golden light merged, Fenton plunged his Verdant Edge into the heart of the tornado.

[SFX: SWORD SHATTERING]

His Verdant Edge was a Low Grade-Three Divine-Class weapon, but it was exceptionally soft. The moment it pierced the winds, it instantly disintegrated into dust.

But as the sword shattered, Fenton kept his grip on the hilt and smirked at Zane. “I think it’s time we continued this conversation outside the Arena.”

Once Zane’s physical form in the Illusionary Arena was destroyed, he’d be gone—effectively ‘killed’ by this one strike.

[SFX: WIND SWIRLING]

[SFX: BLADE FORMING]

Without waiting for a reply, Fenton casually lifted the hilt. The swirling vortex of wind instantly reshaped itself, forging a new blade for his Verdant Edge from the spinning currents. With his other hand, Fenton swept two fingers along the new ‘blade,’ then swung the sword with all his might.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

A wind blade, flickering with golden light, shot straight toward Zane.

[SFX: DRAMATIC IMPACT]

A golden wind blade! A jolt of shock went through Zane.

He’d seen this before. In the heart of the Chaotic Ocean of Stars, there had been swarms of them, golden wind blades that seemed to have minds of their own—far more powerful than the black or two-colored ones.

But he caught himself.

This golden wind blade was similar in appearance only. Fenton’s version couldn’t possibly match what lived in the heart of the Chaotic Ocean of Stars — where Soul Sea Realm warriors had struggled to handle even the black wind blades, let alone the two-colored ones, let alone gold. For Fenton to have reached that level defied all common sense.

The pressure in Zane’s chest dissolved. He was overreacting. These were different things entirely.

This was why he hadn’t bothered with other cultivation handbooks. The Chaos Energy circulating in his body was a power source none of them could even comprehend.

He’d completed multiple Origin Source Laws inside the Elysian Keep. His affinity with every Law was essentially at its ceiling. Wind Law was as natural to him as breathing. He raised his sword and let it flow.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

Most warriors absorbed Vitality from the world around them, transforming it into energy. They followed established rules to harness nature’s power and unleash incredible force. But Chaos was different. It was purer—the very source of everything from a blade of grass to a mountain range. The Laws that erupted from Chaos were on a completely different level than those born from Vitality.

The trials Zane had endured to master the Primordial Chaos Tactic were something most people couldn’t even imagine. But the power he would one day wield was just as far beyond their comprehension.

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY SURGE]

Fenton’s attack was so powerful that it felt like it could shatter the continent. If he’d unleashed it anywhere in the Myriad Spirit House besides the Illusionary Arena, the entire complex would have been obliterated.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

By contrast, Zane’s strike looked pitifully weak. As he swung his sword, a single green wind blade condensed from the light of his blade.

In the depths of Fenton’s eyes, a flicker of disdain.

Wind Law was his primary specialty — the Law he had spent his life mastering. To see this ascender respond with a third-level Wind blade was almost an insult. Whatever tricks Zane had shown earlier, this confirmed it.

I overestimated you, Fenton thought.

The blade, formed by a third-layer Wind Law, looked nowhere near strong enough to block Fenton’s attack.

[AMBIENT: CROWDED HALL]

By now, the hall had swollen to six or seven thousand — three deep on the inside, three more layers pressing in from outside. Warriors who hadn’t made it back in time were the only ones missing.

Smirks spread across the faces of the students from The Crucible watching the fight. They were sure the green blade would be swallowed whole by Fenton’s attack, with Zane’s body torn to shreds right along with it. They couldn’t wait to see the look on his face when he got kicked out of the Arena.

[AMBIENT: QUIET OBSERVATION CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS UNDERTONE]

What none of them knew — not the crowd, not Fenton — was that the Saint Lord and a group of founding elders were watching in silence from elsewhere, apart from the rest.

The Saint Lord’s eyes were fixed on Zane’s wind blade. “What do you make of it?” he said quietly to the man next to him.

The man, one of the Soulbound Sect’s founding elders, took a moment before answering. “Strange. The Law level is only third, far below Fenton’s. But that wind blade feels completely different from any ordinary one. I can’t explain why.”

The Saint Lord nodded slowly. “I thought the same thing. He used no special technique. Just pure Wind Law channeled through his sword. And yet...” He trailed off.

They could see the exquisite precision in Zane’s swordsmanship.

What they couldn’t see — what they had no framework to even imagine — was that he hadn’t used Vitality at all.
