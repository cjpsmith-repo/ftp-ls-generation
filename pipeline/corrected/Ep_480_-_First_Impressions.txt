Ep 480 - First Impressions

Word count: 1011

[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT GROUNDS]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Zane couldn't believe it. After everything he'd endured, was this petty steward really going to bar him from the tournament?

"I'm telling the truth," Zane insisted, keeping his voice steady despite the frustration building in his chest. He reached into his robes with deliberate calm. "I'm here for the Martial Tournament. If you don't believe me, I've got my signaling token right here to prove it."

The steward didn't even glance at the token. Instead, he sneered, his lips curling with practiced disdain.

"In all my years as a steward, I've seen my share of punks try to sneak in," he declared, crossing his arms over his chest. "But I've never seen someone from the Soul Realm with the nerve to try and pull one over on me. You're a first, boy."

Zane's brow furrowed. The accusation stung—not because it wounded his pride, but because it wasted precious time he couldn't afford to lose. He stood there silently, his mind racing through possible solutions. How could he make this stubborn fool see reason without causing a scene that might draw unwanted attention?

The steward watched Zane's silence with growing satisfaction. Seeing that the young cultivator wasn't backing down, a sly grin spread across his face. He turned toward the arena, cupping his hands around his mouth.

"Monkey!" he shouted, his voice carrying across the grounds. "Got an idiot over here looking for trouble. How about you teach him a lesson and get him out of my sight?"

Zane's jaw tightened. This was about to get complicated.

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

A mountain of a man shot out from the arena. He was an intimidating eight feet tall, built like an iron wall, and the ground seemed to tremble with every step he took.

"Who's got the guts to cause trouble for the World Merchants' Guild?" the man called Monkey boomed as he charged out, his eyes immediately locking onto Zane. "You're just asking for a world of hurt." With a mocking laugh, he reached out to grab him.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Zane had already flooded his body with Primordial Energy, transforming himself into a blur of motion. His muscles responded with impossible agility, each fiber singing with power. Without even tapping into his Vitality reserves, he slipped past the giant's grasping hand as though it moved through water.

"Huh?" Monkey grunted, his brutish features contorting with genuine surprise. His target had vanished like smoke. Zane moved with the fluidity of an eel, leaving the mountain of a man swiping at empty air.

The fighters assembled nearby represented the tournament's elite—survivors of brutal pre-qualifying matches that had crushed the dreams of hundreds. Each one had clawed their way through blood and sweat to stand here.

Zane felt their eyes on him now, measuring, calculating. Some showed curiosity; others barely concealed hostility. He'd made an entrance, certainly—though not the one he'd intended. His heart hammered against his ribs, but he kept his expression neutral, refusing to show weakness before this gathering of predators.

[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT WAITING AREA]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

As Zane approached, the other fighters turned to stare, their faces etched with shock.

Most of the competitors who’d made it to the main tournament were at the Inner-Core Realm. Only two were at the ultimate level of the Soul Realm—one was Owen, who had been in Zane's pre-qualifying group, and the other was a stranger.

It was rare enough for a fighter at the ultimate level of the Soul Realm to make it this far. The power gap between them and the Inner-Core Realm was massive, and just passing the pre-qualifiers was considered a monumental challenge. They were already blown away that two had managed to qualify.

So who in their right mind would have guessed that a kid at the fourth grade of the Soul Realm would show up in the waiting area with them? They thought they had to be seeing things.

[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]

Whispers immediately started to ripple through the crowd.

"How the hell did a guy at the fourth grade of the Soul Realm get into the main tournament? The second he steps into the ring, he’s going to be torn to shreds."

"I don't get how he even qualified. With his power level, he shouldn't have made it past the pre-qualifiers, but here he is. Something's not right."

"So what if he qualified? With his level of strength, stepping into that arena is a death sentence."

Amid the murmurs, a loud laugh cut through the crowd.

[SFX: LOUD MOCKING LAUGH]

It came from the disciple of the Quarryheart Sect. "Oh, I know exactly how this punk got in," he boomed.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

That got everyone's attention. "How'd he do it?" one of the fighters asked.

"I was in his pre-qualifying group," the disciple exclaimed. "During the magic array stage, you're not gonna believe this, but he had a twelve-level defensive tower! A tower like that has the power of twelve masters from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. With that kind of firepower, he could've passed the qualifiers in his sleep!"

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

"You bastard," the fighter snarled, his disdainful glare fixed on Zane. He'd forgotten all about the cheap tricks in the magic array. All he could focus on was the sheer nerve of this low-level kid from the Soul Realm daring to look down on him—a master at the middle level of the Inner-Core Realm and the most talented disciple the Platanus Order had seen in centuries. His fists clenched so tightly his knuckles cracked, ready to tear Zane apart right then and there.

Unfortunately for him, Zane didn't even give him the time of day.

Watching the whole scene unfold from a distance, a slow smile spread across Owen’s face. "This Martial Tournament might actually get interesting after all," he murmured to himself.

[SFX: DRAMATIC ANNOUNCEMENT BELL]

Just as the brief interruption ended, an elder from the World Merchants' Guild suddenly shouted, "The Mysterious Luna Hall is arriving!"

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

At his words, everyone looked up to see a massive coffin shooting through the sky like a cannonball, headed straight for the arena.

Zane’s eyes widened slightly, a faint smile touching his lips. The real heavy hitters were finally making their entrance.
