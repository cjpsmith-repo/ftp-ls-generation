Ep 213 - Zane's Dark Flame
Word count: 1113

[MUSIC: QUIET TENSION]
The woman watched intently as Zane produced his flame. The stakes couldn't have been higher, but he was confident in his ability to impress her and gain the sword.
As long as his fire spirit cooperated.
The black flame hovered in the air, pulsing with an otherworldly rhythm. Zane stood motionless in the Weapon Pavilion, aware of the woman's intense scrutiny as she observed his mysterious companion. The flame---if that's what it truly was---drifted lazily above them, casting no heat yet commanding the full attention of everyone present. Even the golden bird-like spirit belonging to the woman retreated slightly, its luminous feathers quivering with what appeared to be instinctive caution.
"Call it back to you," the woman challenged, her voice barely masking her fascination. "Show me you have control over this... phenomenon."
Zane nodded, his expression betraying nothing as he raised his hand toward the dancing black flame. He focused his mind, establishing the silent connection that had formed between them since their first encounter.
The little creature was smart. It knew a good deal when it saw one---food and shelter, courtesy of Zane. So after circling once more and eyeing the bird greedily, it dove at his command and vanished right between his eyebrows.
With the test over, Zane turned to the woman. She was still just staring, her mouth hanging slightly open.
"I can't say for sure if it's a fire spirit," he said, his tone even. "I'm not certain myself. But you saw what it can do. I hope you'll stick to our agreement."
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
But the woman stayed silent, her eyes distant. She was replaying the image of that majestic black fire, her mind racing to make sense of what she'd just witnessed.
She'd seen countless fires and fire spirits in her life, but nothing like this. And from this young man of all people? That was the real shock. She knew from experience that a fire's true power wasn't in its raw heat, but in its control. The cooler it seemed, the stronger it actually was.
A fire spirit that could drift over dry wood without scorching it was considered powerful.
This black fire, though... it gave off no heat at all. It was as if it had sealed its energy away so completely that it wasn't even fire anymore, but a living creature sculpted from shadow and flame.
If it could contain all of its heat, just how powerful was it, really?
No one knew for sure---not even its owner, Zane.
All he knew was that so far, nothing had stood in its way. It could tear through high-grade Mythic weapons, melting them down to pure Celestial Essence, which it would then greedily devour. To the black fire, everything was as soft as wax, just another meal waiting to be consumed.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
The silence in the room lingered heavily between them. The woman studied Zane with piercing eyes that revealed nothing of her thoughts. After what seemed an eternity, she finally broke the silence.
"You've proven your fire is special, whatever its nature might be," she said, her voice carrying both respect and doubt. "What puzzles me is why someone with such a gift hasn't pursued the path of weapon refinement. What are you waiting for?"
"I haven't become a weapon refiner yet," Zane admitted. "But I will be, and soon. Deep down I know that I can transform this sword into a Mythic Weapon worthy of its creator's vision. All I can ask is that you trust me. I know I have what it takes." His words resonated with unwavering certainty, no trace of hesitation or doubt.
[SFX: BLACK FIRE FLARING]
The woman considered what she'd just seen with the young man in front of her. It was true Zane hadn't mastered the skills of a weapon refiner. But he'd gathered more Celestial Essence than anyone, and everyone knew that was the key to forging powerful weapons.
Still, the whole idea was absurd: some kid with no credentials or experience claiming he could turn Chase's final masterpiece into a Mythic Weapon. If she hadn't seen that black fire with her own eyes, she would've laughed him right out the door. She'd come here ready to expose a fraud, and now she wasn't so sure.
Parting with the sword had never been an option, but she couldn't deny the truth: this boy was more than he seemed. Far more.
She remembered her mentor's words, but still... giving such a precious sword to someone so young felt reckless. Foolish, even.
The Shopkeeper could no longer hold his tongue. "Madame, please tell me you aren't seriously considering this. For goodness' sake, the kid is just an Adept," the Shopkeeper protested, his face a mess of confusion and complaint as he struggled for words.
"Just an Adept?" the woman countered. "My judgment of people has never failed me. He might be an Adept for now, but he won't be with the Skyreach Sect forever. He's destined for greater things, and when he gets there, this sword will become a legendary weapon in his hands."
She turned, clapped once, and nodded toward the sword. "Give it to him."
The shopkeeper's face fell, but he crossed the room and held the sword out to Zane.
Zane took it with both hands and bowed his head. "Thank you."
The woman waved him off. "Just don't waste it."
As she spoke, a golden light seemed to shimmer in her eyes. She carried herself with the regal bearing of a magnificent peacock surveying lesser birds from her lofty perch, her natural confidence drawing others into her orbit.
"What makes you so certain of him?" the Shopkeeper asked, apprehension still lingering on his face.
The woman's lips curved into a smile as she gestured toward the ancient furnace. "You missed the test, which explains your skepticism. But take a look at the fire spirit in the furnace. I believe you'll discover your answer there."
[SFX: FIRE CRACKLING]
The crackling flames cast shifting shadows across the workshop's walls, their dance suddenly erratic and disturbed.
Confused, the Shopkeeper followed her gaze, his eyes widening at the sight before him. The normally proud fire spirit was trembling violently, pressing itself against the furthest corner of the furnace. It cowered like a terrified animal that had glimpsed a predator beyond comprehension.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
The mysterious tension in the room thickened, almost palpable in the suddenly heavy air.
All color drained from the Shopkeeper's face, leaving him ashen and shaken. In his decades of service, through countless tests and trials, he had never seen the spirit show such profound terror.
"Why?" he whispered, his voice barely audible above the disturbed crackling of the flames. "What could possibly have terrified it so much?"
