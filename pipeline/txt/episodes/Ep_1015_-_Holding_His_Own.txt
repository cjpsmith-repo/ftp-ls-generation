Ep 1015 - Holding His Own

Word count: 1073

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Ernest had just made Zane an offer — hand over every Sovereign Stone, deliver more every day, or die — and Zane had smiled and said no.

The seven warriors who’d reached the Soul Sea Realm couldn’t believe their ears.

[SFX: COLD WIND GUST]

At the same time, a sudden chill cut through the air, one that hadn’t been there a moment ago.

Back in the Lower World, they’d been rulers of divine kingdoms, leaders of sacred orders. And while they didn’t run into Life-and-Death Realm practitioners every day, they’d seen enough to know their place. They’d always looked down on them.

And now, here was some kid from the Life-and-Death Realm, looking for a fight.

Where did he get the nerve? How could he possibly think he could take on seven Soul Sea Realm warriors all by himself?

Was he out of his mind? He was biting off way more than he could chew.

[SFX: MOCKING LAUGHTER]

The sheer absurdity of it hit them, and the seven warriors burst into mocking laughter.

Freya, standing beside Zane, was just as stunned. She had no idea where he was getting this confidence, or what he was thinking—other than getting himself killed. Did he have a backer? No way. If he had friends in high places in the Upper World, why would he be stuck here mining? He’d have been scooped up long ago.

But even as her mind raced, Freya kept her expression perfectly calm. Zane had already pulled off the impossible twice. Maybe he had some trick up his sleeve for this, too.

[MUSIC: MENACING DRUMS]

Still chuckling, the warriors stared at Zane with open scorn. Their leader, Ernest, was laughing so hard he was practically doubled over.

When he finally caught his breath, his laughter curdled into a vicious sneer. “I’ll give you one chance to live. Hand over every Sovereign Stone you have, right now. From now on, you bring me enough stones every single day, and I’ll let you be. But if you come up short, even once… your life is over.”

“Thanks for the offer, but I’ll pass,” Zane interrupted with a lazy smile.

[SFX: ENERGY BURST]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

In the next instant, the Chaos Energy inside him erupted, and he closed the distance to Ernest in a flash.

Ernest’s smile died. Something was wrong — he felt it in his gut. He’d learned that lesson the hard way once before, back in the Lower World, when he’d underestimated an opponent and nearly paid for it with his life. He’d sworn he’d never make that mistake again.

Almost on instinct, he spread his arms wide.

“Ten Thousand Ghosts!”

[SFX: GHOSTLY WAIL]

Ghost shadows erupted around him. Dark energy coiled through the air in thick strands.

“Too late,” Zane said.

He was already inside it. At point-blank range, the domain couldn’t take hold. Before the ghost shadows could even take shape, Zane’s palm landed.

Four thousand Dragon Scales. The Vital Spirit in his arm surged, carrying the full power of the Law of Wind. His hand exceeded the speed of naked-eye perception — there was only a flash of blue light, and then a sound like a thunderclap.

[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACT]

Ernest’s body launched backward. He hit the cave wall and kept going — straight through it. The rock swallowed him whole. From deep in the mine came a long, rolling rumble, the sound of collapse after collapse, fading slowly as he plunged thousands of feet down.

Then silence.

The remaining six warriors stood frozen at the edge of the shaft, staring down into the dark. Not one of them spoke. Ernest had been the strongest among them — that was why they’d followed him. Watching him get swatted through solid rock like he was nothing drained every last drop of fight from their faces.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE STRINGS]

Zane’s final months in the Lower World hadn’t just been training; they had been a complete transformation.

On top of practicing the Primordial Chaos Tactic and the Stellar Body, he’d discovered his Master’s private collection: massive tomes containing the Original Laws. Most practitioners had to settle for worn-out stone tablets, but these books allowed for flawless, total mastery. Combined with Zane’s raw talent and accelerated learning, he’d absorbed them at a terrifying rate.

By the time he ascended, he’d refined his cultivation to the Fifth Level of the Life-and-Death Realm. And Chaos Energy didn’t just fuel his power — it amplified the Laws exponentially. Even at his breakthrough, he’d been able to hold his own against ordinary Soul Sea Realm warriors. Now he was well beyond that.

This crew of seven was a pretty small-time operation. They’d banded together to shake down newcomers for Aether crystals, but other, bigger gangs of dozens monopolized entire mines, forcing anyone who entered their territory to pay up. That’s why these guys resorted to robbery.

When they’d first spotted Zane, they’d been confused. A lone practitioner from the Life-and-Death Realm? How did a guy like him even get here? It made no sense.

But they hadn’t given it a second thought. They weren’t about to waste their energy on a nobody. They hadn’t even taken Freya seriously, let alone Zane.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL HIT]

Now, they understood. Zane’s power was on a completely different level. He was far stronger than someone who had just broken into the Soul Sea Realm. Ernest hadn’t even had time to mount a defense.

Freya’s jaw went slack as she replayed the split-second assault in her mind, her face a mask of disbelief. In her wildest dreams, she could never have imagined this.

She’d wondered if Zane had a backer, but it never occurred to her that he was his own backup.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

Zane glanced down at his palm with a faint smile, as if the violent scene had never happened. “So,” he asked, his voice chillingly casual, “I’m curious. Does the Soulbound Sect really not care if people get killed over their possessions? Because if not, that works for me.”

At his words, the color drained from the other warriors’ faces.

[SFX: PANICKED FOOTSTEPS]

“We have to go! Now!”

They knew a threat when they heard one. If they didn’t play this smart, this kid would kill them without a second thought. No one dared answer his question.

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

In a blur of motion, they were gone.
