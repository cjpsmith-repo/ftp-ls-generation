Ep 448 - Choosing Sides

[AMBIENT: DARK VOID]

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Madame Vermillion, the leader of the Dragonfall Palace, shot into the black sphere, swallowed by darkness in an instant.

[SFX: CRACKLING THUNDER]

Above her, the Soul Shackle crackled with immense destructive power, yet she passed through it completely unharmed.

The moment she breached the darkness, her eyes widened. "What is this?"

She had more than enough power to crush the strongest Owl Beast. A weak specimen like the one Zane had faced should have been trivial. But the Owl Beast wasn't what stopped her in her tracks—it was the sight of Zane's soul, suspended alone in the vast emptiness.

The Owl Beast had vanished. Not defeated or fleeing—simply gone, as if it had never existed. Only Zane's soul remained, floating in the void like a solitary star in an endless night.

What happened here? The question echoed through her mind as confusion replaced her usual confidence. How is any of this possible?

Madame Vermillion had witnessed countless soul battles and guided hundreds of disciples through these trials. Never in her decades of experience had she seen an Owl Beast simply disappear. Where in the world did it go?

She couldn't begin to wrap her head around it.

[SFX: ELECTRICAL CRACKLING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Meanwhile, arcs of lightning from the Soul Shackle continued to surge into the darkness. Zane's soul distorted under the assault, shimmering with an iridescent sheen like oil on water.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

"Whoa… Unbelievable. His soul is actually breaking through to the Fighting Soul level!" The realization hit her, and a flicker of intense curiosity replaced her shock. Elder Howard had said Zane underwent a Baptism by Laws—that’s why she’d come to help. She never imagined he’d be attempting something like this.

Isn't he just in the Adept Realm? How did he even get to this point?

She didn't deliberate. She reached to her wrist, produced a small red pill — strange-shaped, almost alive, pulsing as it moved — and tossed it gently into Zane's soul.
Zane sensed it immediately. His focus nearly broke. But the Cobalt Dragon's voice cut through: "Let it in. It's good for you."
He relaxed. The pill dissolved through his soul like red ink through water, spreading to every corner, stabilizing what had been chaotic. Then — a burst of red light.


[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TRAINING GROUND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

It was one thing for a powerful warrior to turn falling leaves into deadly weapons; that was just a matter of skill.

But reaching the Fighting Soul level? That was a feat beyond most cultivators.

Only a handful of warriors in the Inner-Core Realm ever managed to train their souls to that level of strength. Most in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm couldn’t achieve it in their entire lifetime.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT REVELATION]

Yet here stood Zane—a warrior from the Adept Realm—attaining the Fighting Soul level. It was absolutely incredible.

The other warriors who had trained alongside Zane were less surprised. They'd seen his explosive progress firsthand, watching him climb from the outer ring to the inner, advancing relentlessly until he reached the Owl Beast's doorstep. 

But from the outside, the city lord, Elder Howard, Alan Nolan, and the others couldn't see what transpired inside the black sphere. They possessed only the bare facts: someone from the Adept Realm had achieved the Fighting Soul level. The very notion should have been impossible. Most cultivators spent decades—sometimes entire lifetimes—chasing this milestone, only to fall short. Yet this teenager had grasped it in mere hours.

Inside the sphere, Zane's soul snapped back into his body. His eyes opened. They were clear and steady — bright in a way that hadn't been there before, like two stars just cresting the horizon. 

The warriors nearby who'd trained alongside him took one look and understood instantly: he'd done it.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The city lord leaned forward, his face creased with concentration. "Hold on," he murmured, eyes fixed on the black sphere's surface. "Does his Fighting Soul possess some kind of... unusual attribute?"

A knowing smile touched Elder Howard's lips, the expression of someone who'd anticipated this exact question. "If I'm not mistaken, our leader decided to give him a Glimmerwood Soul Pill from the Palace."

The revelation hung in the air like thunder before a storm. Such generosity spoke volumes about how highly the Palace valued this young cultivator.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC NEGOTIATION]

Alan forced a tight smile. "Your Dragonfall Palace is certainly generous. A Glimmerwood Soul Pill is ridiculously expensive, even a low-grade one." He then strode over to the edge of the darkness and called out to Zane, "Zane! Join the Dragonblaze Hall. I can promise you a future beyond your wildest dreams. Siding with the best in the realm is the smartest move you could ever make!"

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

Zane frowned, peering toward the source of Alan's voice. Though he could sense the incredible power radiating from everyone present, something about the man's oily tone set his teeth on edge. "Who are you?" he called back. "And why should I join your Dragonblaze Hall?"

Alan's laugh rang with practiced confidence. "Ha! My identity hardly matters. What you should know is that billions of warriors would sacrifice everything for the chance to join the Dragonblaze Hall, yet only a select few ever qualify! Join us, and you're destined for greatness. I'll even sweeten the deal—fifty drops of true dragon's blood, offered on behalf of the Hall itself!"

Alan's eyes gleamed with triumph. In his mind, no rational cultivator could possibly refuse such a generous offer.

Madame Vermillion giggled, clearly amused. "He already dislikes you intensely. What other reason would he need to turn you down?"

Alan froze, the woman's casual dismissal striking him like a physical blow. 

He was merely a vice leader—a fact that burned in his chest whenever he stood near true power. Among the Dragon-blooded, the gulf between his status and someone like Madame Vermillion stretched as wide as an ocean. He couldn't simply eliminate this upstart, not with her watching. Not when she'd clearly taken an interest in the boy.

[MUSIC: RESPECTFUL STRINGS]

"I cannot thank you enough, Madame," Zane said, his voice steady as he bowed toward where he sensed her presence. Gratitude warmed his words. "Thank you for protecting me while I broke through to the Fighting Soul level."

Madame Vermillion's laughter sparkled with genuine amusement. “The honor was all mine, young man.”

Zane hesitated, then added with a slight laugh, "You know, your voice sounds like you're sixteen at most. Doesn't calling you 'Madame' seem rather excessive?"

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Elder Howard's breath caught in his throat. The absolute nerve of this boy! he thought, his mind reeling. Does he possess a death wish? Speaking so casually about our leader's age—has he lost all sense of propriety?

He held his breath and looked to see how Madame Vermillion would handle this flagrant breach of etiquette.

Alan Nolan fought to suppress a smile. Maybe he wouldn't have to worry about killing this kid.

Maybe she would do it for him.
