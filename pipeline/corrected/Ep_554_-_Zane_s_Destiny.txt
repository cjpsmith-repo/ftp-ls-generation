Ep 554 - Zane's Destiny
Word count: 1172
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Zane stared at the crystal ball. It was riddled with cracks, so fragile-looking that a single touch might shatter it into a million pieces.
[SFX: ECHOING LAUGHTER]
Vesper's wild laughter echoed from within, but it was laced with a profound sorrow—the sound of a powerful master in his final years, remembering a lifetime of both great joy and terrible pain.
"Sir?" Zane murmured, his lips tightening.
The laughter from the crystal ball rolled on, completely ignoring him. It went on for what felt like minutes, finally sputtering into a series of wheezes before Vesper calmed down.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
[SFX: BOOMING VOICE]
"The destiny to lead the world of chaos! To lead the world of chaos!" his voice boomed from the crystal. "It's the destiny to lead the world of chaos!"
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL MINDSCAPE]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE HARP]
Zane was completely lost. While Vesper was raving about his fortune, the Cobalt Dragon was already conferring with his brother inside Zane's mind.
"This immortal just confirmed it with his Starlight Astrology. Zane really is destined to lead the world of chaos!"
The Cobalt Dragon's seventh brother agreed with a nod. "He's right. With a destiny like that, and the way the world is now… he's bound to be the hero of this age!"
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
The Cobalt Dragon hummed thoughtfully. "If that's true, there might still be a chance for the nine of us. We couldn't have picked a better person to be stuck inside." A chance for a comeback… The thought filled him with a rush of hope.
The dragon's seventh brother let out a long, weary sigh. "Perhaps."
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
While the two dragons spoke, Zane just stood there, stunned. What kind of destiny is that? he thought. The destiny to lead the world of chaos? He'd never heard of anything so insane, not even from the Cobalt Dragon.
Zane’s brow furrowed. "Sir, what are you talking about? What does 'leading the world of chaos' even mean?"
Vesper barreled right over his question. "Ha! For two thousand years, I've waited for someone with a destiny this powerful. And to think it would be you! Such a grand destiny… a truly epic fate!"
"Sir," Zane pressed, his patience wearing thin as Vesper just kept muttering to himself, refusing to give him a straight answer.
Vesper's muttering cut off abruptly. "Wait! Something's not right. If your destiny is so powerful, where's your guide?"
Zane flinched. "Guide?" he repeated, throwing his hands up in exasperation. "I don't have a clue what you're talking about."
The crystal ball drifted back and forth in front of Zane as if it were pacing. "Let me see, let me see…" Vesper mumbled, lost in thought. The ball suddenly stopped dead in the air. "You!" he declared.
"You have a younger sister!"
Zane rolled his eyes. The old man was right, but what did that have to do with anything?
"Your sister," Vesper said quietly, "is your guide."
[MUSIC: TENSE REVELATION]
The words struck Zane like a blow. This was Vesper's tomb. The Wheel of Fortune was Vesper's creation. And Maddox—the man who had taken his sister—remained somewhere inside it.
Zane fought to maintain his composure as he met the eyeless gaze directly. "Sir. My sister's name is Elara. A man named Maddox took her. He's still in the Wheel of Fortune. Can you kill him for me?"
Silence settled over the chamber.
Zane had never feared a fight, not even against someone as formidable as Maddox. But Maddox hadn't fought fairly—he'd taken Elara. Against such a man, Zane had no interest in playing by the rules.
He'd already tried ending it at Cloud Hall, laying a trap within the sect's protective formation. It hadn't worked. Maddox had slipped away.
The clock was ticking. With Maddox's luck, it was only a matter of time before he found someone with the Violet-Core Physique or broke through. Every day that passed widened the gap.
If Vesper could end it here, right now—that was the cleanest solution.
Vesper considered for a long moment. "I'm sorry. I can't do that. The Wheel of Fortune was built two thousand years ago to find the warrior with the greatest karmic luck—not to kill anyone. The soul you're speaking to is barely a wisp. I no longer have the power to intervene, even against someone as insignificant as a Life-and-Death Realm warrior."
Zane's face darkened. "I understand."
What Vesper didn't say was that he had lied. He could have altered the Wheel to destroy Maddox. But his mastery of Starlight Astrology had shown him something Zane couldn't yet see: Elara wasn't just a captive. She was the force driving Zane forward. The urgency of saving her fueled his growth. To remove that—to hand Zane an easy victory—would sever the thread pulling him toward his destiny.
So Vesper said nothing and let the lie stand.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATIVE]
Zane knew leaving the sect's protection was suicide, anyway. The moment he stepped outside Cloud Hall's protective array, Maddox would be on him. He'd be dead before he even reached the Heartlands, which meant he was stuck. But he couldn't get any stronger just sitting around cooped up in Cloud Hall, either.
He'd thought about crossing the Chaotic Ocean of Stars to the divine kingdoms, but that was a bust now. Not after killing a prince and pissing off the Holy Maiden. Besides, that journey was a nightmare in itself.
Which brought him back to Vesper's suggestion. It wasn't a bad idea—it might even be his best shot. But…
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane narrowed his eyes. "What's your angle? Why do you really want me to go there?" The immortal had gone to too much trouble—building this celestial tomb, the Wheel of Fortune, preserving the last shred of his soul—just to find some prophesied hero. He had to have his own agenda.
To his credit, Vesper didn't beat around the bush. "There's a great secret hidden beneath the Tower of Sin," he said plainly. "I need you to go and claim that inheritance."
Zane raised an eyebrow. "And why didn't you go claim it when you were alive?" he asked, his voice dripping with sarcasm.
A sigh echoed from the crystal. "I can't go to that place," Vesper's voice was heavy with old pain. "Don't ask why. You'll understand when you get there."
[SFX: HEAVY STONE GRINDING]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING TOMB]
Just then, the massive coffin behind them began to shake, its lid slowly creaking open.
"These are the rewards I prepared for those who passed the Wheel of Fortune," Vesper announced. "Since you came in first, you get first pick. The rest will go to the runners-up. I'll leave it to you to hand out their prizes."
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT BRASS]
Zane’s eyes lit up as they swept over the treasures spilling from the coffin. "You're letting me decide?"
He had a feeling Vesper's generosity was going to cost him far more than anything in that coffin was worth.
