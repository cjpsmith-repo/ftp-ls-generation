Ep 1090 - Great Hair

Word count: 1029

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Landry’s laughter died away, and he and Damian were left alone again with the blank, silent rock.

After all that time and energy, the two warriors had come up with nothing. The disappointment was so sharp it twisted their faces into masks of pure rage as they collapsed, utterly defeated.

[AMBIENT: MYSTERIOUS ATMOSPHERE]

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

Just then, a thin, white mist began to seep from the surface of the massive rock. It was so faint at first that neither of them noticed it, too lost in their own despair. But slowly, the wisps of vapor grew thicker, coalescing into a cloud that was impossible to ignore.

Landry was sprawled on the ground, staring at the sky with a bitter, hollow laugh. Damian, however, was staring at the rock. The crazed smile on his face froze, his eyes going wide with shock.

Noticing his friend’s strange expression, Landry pushed himself up. “What is it?”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]

Damian pointed a trembling finger at the swirling mist. “What the hell is that?”

Landry’s head snapped around. He stared at the rock, and as he processed what he was seeing, his eyes lit up with a wild, ecstatic joy.

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

“That’s it,” he choked out, his voice cracking with emotion. “That’s the Sword Cloud left by the Supreme Lord Sword!”

Damian was just as thrilled, gaping at the sight as wisps of the cloud continued to drift out from the giant stone, leaving it slick with moisture. The mist condensed slowly, taking its time to form even a thin layer. Landry knew what it was, but he couldn’t yet make out its true shape. All they could do was wait.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ARENA]

[MUSIC: TENSION BUILDING]

Meanwhile, Zane had no idea what was happening back at the rock. The trials were almost over; he and Layla were the only ones left. The man in simple cotton robes made the call, and hearing their names, the two of them stepped into the crucible.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL MIST]

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

A moment later, Zane found himself in the Illusionary Arena. The place was shrouded in a thick, dreamlike fog, feeling less like a battlefield and more like some otherworldly paradise. Floating in the mist were two hundred circular stone platforms, one for each warrior taking the trial.

[AMBIENT: HUSHED ANTICIPATION]

Outside the mist, the Lord of the Sunset World, the Palace Masters, the Vice Palace Masters, and every warrior who’d already finished the trial were watching now — only the two of them left.

The old man had arranged it this way on purpose — whether on his own judgment or at the Lord of the Sunset World’s quiet suggestion, no one could say. He’d noticed Layla hadn’t gone all-out in the tower, and he wasn’t about to let her hold back again.

Layla reached up and pulled the last of her hair ornaments free, one by one, until there was nothing left. Her hair tumbled loose, impossibly long, trailing out behind her into the mist.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

She raised her sword and called across to him, her voice carrying easily through the fog. “This time, I’m not holding back.”

She’d told herself twenty-eight thousand miles was untouchable — that no one alive could match it, so there was no need to push harder. Zane had proven her wrong. Now it was just the two of them, with no one else’s trial left to interrupt. She wasn’t giving him another opening.

[SFX: STONE GRINDING]

Beneath their feet, a series of round plates were set into the platform, each etched with a number. The first was marked “One.”

“You can begin,” the man in cotton clothes called from somewhere beyond the mist. “Step on the plate to start.”

Zane stepped onto the first plate without hesitation. Across from him, Layla did the same, the motion almost lazy.

As she turned, her hair swept through the air behind her in a single long arc, like a brushstroke.

[SFX: BATTLE SOUNDS]

A ring of heavily armored soldiers rose up around Zane’s platform. He didn’t bother with anything fancy — one horizontal slash and the first wave came apart. Another ring formed, then a third, and he cut through all of them in the same breath.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

By the time he finished — ten breaths, start to end — he glanced over and found Layla already done, her hair spread out across the stone, watching him with her head tilted.

[SFX: RAPID SWORD SLASHES]

[MUSIC: FAST-PACED COMBAT]

Without missing a beat, Zane picked up the pace for the second round. Thundergust became a blur, cutting down the white-robed warriors that swarmed him. These new opponents were a step up from the soldiers in the first round, but he still dispatched them with ease, clearing the round in thirty seconds flat.

He felt pretty good about his time until he glanced over at Layla. She was just standing there on her platform, her long hair fanned out around her, a smug little smile playing on her lips.

Zane was floored. He thought thirty seconds was fast, but she’d beaten him again.

“You’re slow,” she teased.

He just shrugged, turning to step onto the third platform. This time, as a group of bronze-armored warriors materialized around him, he didn’t rush to fight. He held back, wanting to see just how she was moving so incredibly fast.

Layla had already started. The second the warriors appeared, she gave him a sweet smile and barely lifted her sword.

[SFX: SWORD VIBRATION HUM]

A low hum filled the air as the blade began to vibrate.

[SFX: ARMOR SHATTERING]

The warriors around her suddenly started to shake violently, and then, in a single, silent flash, their armor shattered, and they disintegrated into nothing.

“Holy shit,” Zane breathed, completely stunned. He had no idea what he’d just seen, but one thing was crystal clear: that was how she was clearing these trials so damn fast.

He was so mesmerized that he barely noticed the bronze-armored warriors on his own platform closing in.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

Snapping back to reality, Zane gripped his sword and braced for the fight.
