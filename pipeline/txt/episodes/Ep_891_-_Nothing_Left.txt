Ep 891 - Nothing Left

Word count: 1194

[AMBIENT: EERIE NECROPOLIS]

[MUSIC: DARK MYSTICAL TENSION]

With everyone’s souls laid bare, their strength was gutted—everyone except for Zane.

His soul was simply on another level. It hadn’t quite reached the Nightjade Fighting Soul stage, but once a soul had merged with the World’s Will, not even a Soul Sea Realm master could compete with it.

[SFX: OMINOUS LAMP GLOW]

The lamp hanging before the necropolis entrance was what the warriors called a warding lamp. It was designed to repel ghosts, but in their current soul-state—not quite human, not quite spirit—it was just as dangerous to them. 

That was exactly why the Soul Sea Realm master had stopped Fiona and the others from rushing ahead, terrified they’d be obliterated by the very thing meant to protect them.

[SFX: DRAGON ENERGY SURGE]

Before he moved, Zane reached inward toward the Cobalt Dragon. A wave of calm came back almost instantly — the shared True Dragon Lineage would work in his favor. It wouldn’t be a problem.

“All of you, wait here,” the Soul Sea Realm master ordered, his voice laced with a stern warning. “You can go in once this lamp goes out. Don’t even think about it before then.”

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[SFX: EXPLOSION]

A moment later, a black shadow shot past them, blurring into the necropolis at an impossible speed. The instant it crossed the threshold, the lamp exploded with a blinding light that lashed out at the intruder.

Another kind of soul attack, Zane thought, an eyebrow twitching in appreciation. The dragon-blooded really built some incredible things.

But the black shadow was the soul of a Soul Sea Realm master, and it was more than prepared. As the light surged forward, a portion of the shadow instantly morphed into a shield, deflecting the attack.

A faint smile touched Zane’s lips as he watched. He’s actually reshaping his soul into a weapon. The warrior might be at the Soul Sea Realm, but his soul was only at the Fighting Soul Realm, meaning he couldn’t just tank the lamp’s assault—he had to actively block it. 

Zane felt a quiet confidence. Given the current strength of his own soul, he could probably walk right through the light without taking much damage at all.

Shortly after the master’s soul vanished inside, the lamp flickered and slowly dimmed. That was their cue. The remaining masters entered one after another, with Zane and the younger warriors following close behind.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

The scale of what lay beyond was difficult to take in all at once. Tombstones stretched in long rows on either side of the path, countless in number, their surfaces worn smooth by time. The ground underfoot was blanketed in thick dust — undisturbed for years, perhaps centuries. No living person had set foot here. Even the four Divine Kingdoms’ warriors had only ever entered in soul form.

“Aren’t we being disrespectful?” Fiona’s voice came through consciousness rather than sound — souls couldn’t speak, only project thought. “Taking over someone else’s body?”

Randy answered before Zane could. “The corpses we’re here for aren’t under these stones. The ones buried along this path were ordinary people — no preservation method was ever applied to them. After all these years, there’s nothing left. Not even bone.”

Zane nodded. He knew it was true. Without a specialized technique, no body survived more than a few centuries intact. Whatever had once lain beneath these markers had long since returned to dust.

The Soul Sea masters led the way in silence, their dark forms drifting ahead. Behind them, the dozen or so younger warriors followed in a loose cluster.

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

The cavern opened before them—a vast chamber of tiered platforms ascending into darkness. A central staircase climbed through the space, flanked by thirteen levels of platforms. The lowest tier held nearly a thousand platforms, each bearing three man-sized huts. Higher tiers held fewer platforms and fewer huts, until the uppermost levels vanished into shadow with only a handful visible.

“Listen carefully,” one of the Soul Sea masters said, turning to address the group. His consciousness reached them all at once. “The bodies on these platforms are arranged by strength. Higher means stronger. We brought you here to find vessels — choose carefully. A weak body won’t move an inch inside the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land.” 

He paused. “There is one danger you need to understand. The Soul Purifying Light sweeps the stairs at intervals. If it catches you on the steps, your soul will be destroyed instantly. You won’t survive it. When you see it coming, get to a platform immediately. Do you understand?”

The younger warriors nodded, though the unease on their faces said plainly that understanding and feeling ready were two different things.

The Soul Sea masters exchanged a glance among themselves. In truth, the reason they had brought the young generation in at all was simple: the surge of Soul Cores recovered from the top floors had left them feeling, for the first time in years, as though they could afford to be generous. Training the princes was an afterthought born of abundance. Whether it produced results or disasters was secondary.

[SFX: ENERGY BEAM]

Just as they took it all in, a brilliant beam of light erupted from the top of the central staircase and began cascading downward like a tidal wave.

“Quick! To the platforms!” War King Wind yelled.

In a mad dash, everyone scrambled for the raised platforms flanking the stairs, barely making it to safety. From his perch on the left, War King Wind’s eyes were locked on Fiona and Callan. Of all his children, he feared most for them, his heart pounding with the terror that they’d be consumed by the Soul Purifying Light. But by the time the wave of energy washed over the floor below, everyone had made it. They were safe.

They were now on the second tier, where more platforms materialized out of the gloom.

[AMBIENT: EERIE TOMB SILENCE]

[SFX: MYSTICAL HUM]

Zane’s eyes were drawn to one of two small huts on his platform, where a single coffin rested. Is the corpse still intact? he wondered. He focused his perception, trying to probe the coffin’s contents, but his power was immediately deflected. The coffin seemed to be made of the same material as the soul conduit, completely immune to any outside sense.

Seeing the flicker of frustration on his face, Randy offered a casual smile. “The corpses on this layer are too weak, only around the Core-Forged Realm. They’re worthless to us. We need to aim for the ones on the upper floors.”

Zane nodded, a slight smile returning to his own face. 

He knew Randy was right. To even survive in the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, you needed a powerful vessel. A weak corpse wouldn’t be able to move a single inch there. The risk of climbing higher wasn’t just a choice—it was a necessity.

His gaze drifted upward, into the towering darkness. He couldn’t begin to imagine how powerful the corpses on the top floor might be.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Were they the remains of legendary warriors from the Soul Sea Realm itself?
