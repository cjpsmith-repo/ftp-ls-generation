Ep 67 - Razorwing Queen

[SFX: SHARP MANDIBLE CLICKS]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Confronting six Razorwing Alphas simultaneously would make even the most hardened Nature Level warrior quake with dread. These Alphas possessed not only superior strength and agility compared to their ordinary counterparts, but wielded truly nightmarish venomous thorns that struck fear into the hearts of experienced fighters.
The world teemed with lethal creatures, each region boasting its own deadly predators. In the scorching deserts, scorpions lurked with stings capable of ending a life in mere moments. Throughout dense forests, Whisperwing Drones unleashed toxic powder that devastated vast stretches of land, leaving nothing but decay in their wake. Yet Nature Level warriors typically dismissed such threats with casual confidence. Their Life Energy created an impenetrable barrier, rendering them virtually immune to conventional poisons.
The Alphas' venom, however, defied all expectations. Though not immediately fatal, it inflicted a fate many considered worse than death.
This insidious toxin bypassed Vitality Shields completely, striking directly at the nervous system and inducing total paralysis. Even the mightiest Nature Level warrior would find themselves helplessly immobilized, their powerful muscles betraying them as they lay conscious but utterly defenseless.
And in the merciless world of Razorwings, paralysis carried a singular, terrifying meaning—you had become nothing more than their next meal, forced to experience every excruciating moment of being devoured alive.
[MUSIC: HEROIC THEME]
Most fighters feared that poison, but Zane wasn't most fighters.
His body was some kind of weapon, rendering him immune.
So, while charging six Alphas alone looked reckless, he knew exactly what he was doing.
[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]
Soaring through the air, he slipped a ring onto his finger, his other hand gripping the Shattered Dagger. Six Alphas in a single strike. That was six points right there—a much faster way to rack them up than hunting down the common grunts.
[SFX: CREATURE SCREECHES]
But unlike the common drones, the Alphas were smart. They didn't fight with mindless aggression. Seeing Zane take down six of their kin in one go, the remaining Alphas scattered, screeching commands for the lesser Razorwings to swarm him instead.
Zane kept his arm swinging, the force sending the Shattered Dagger whipping violently around him.
[SFX: BLADE SLICING AIR]
"Whirl, whoosh!"
[SFX: INSECT MANDIBLES CLICKING]
[SFX: BLADE SLICING THROUGH AIR]
A dozen Razorwings charged, their mandibles clicking with savage hunger, but Zane merely adjusted his stance with fluid precision. The dagger whistled through the air, slicing through chitinous bodies as if they were parchment. Their bisected corpses descended in a macabre rainfall, spattering the forest floor with viscous fluids.
To Zane, these creatures—the same ones that sent seasoned warriors fleeing in terror—registered as nothing more significant than common insects beneath his boot.
[SFX: INSECTS HITTING THE GROUND]
Before long, a mound of Razorwing remains had accumulated at his feet, the distinctive helmets of several Blackout Division Captains jutting out from the grisly pile. The sight might have turned the stomach of an ordinary person, but Zane felt only cold satisfaction at each addition to his tally.
[AMBIENT: INSECT SWARM CHITTERING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
The swarm's numbers vastly exceeded those of previous attacks, their chittering bodies darkening the sky like a living storm cloud. Yet thanks to Zane's relentless assault, as he transformed into a whirlwind of lethal precision, the creatures met their end even more swiftly than before. His movements blurred with deadly grace, each swing of his blade ending multiple lives, his expression betraying neither fatigue nor mercy as he continued his grim harvest.
[SFX: INSECT RETREAT SOUNDS]
Once the last of the dozen Alphas was slaughtered, the rest of the swarm began to pull back. Zane let them go. There was a mountain of corpses on the ground, and he had spoils to collect.
Shane observed the swarm's unexpected retreat, his brow furrowed in confusion. Then, a horrifying realization dawned on him, draining the color from his face as his expression transformed into one of grim certainty.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
"No... impossible," he whispered, his voice barely audible before it rose to a desperate shout. "Retreat! Everyone, fall back immediately!"
Zane glanced up from his task, fingers still bloody from prying a crystal core out of an Alpha's mutilated chest. "What? Why?" he inquired, perplexed by the sudden shift from victory to panic. The triumph he'd felt moments ago evaporated like morning mist.
"When the common drones retreat like this, it means..." Shane's words faltered, his face ashen as though he'd glimpsed his own death. Fear constricted his throat, choking the explanation he desperately needed to convey.
"What is it? There's a what?" Zane pressed, his own heartbeat quickening as he registered the sheer terror etched into Shane's normally composed features. Whatever caused a seasoned warrior to tremble must be truly formidable.
[SFX: DISTANT MONSTROUS ROAR]
"A Razorwing Queen," Shane finally managed, the words escaping as a strangled whisper that carried more dread than a scream ever could. His eyes darted frantically toward the forest's edge, searching for signs of the approaching nightmare.
He'd been stationed at Dragon's Keep for years and knew the swarm's hierarchy inside and out. The Alphas and common drones all answered to a single Queen. While Alphas could think for themselves, the Queen was the swarm's true commander.
The common drones, on the other hand, were mindless berserkers. They didn't know how to retreat; they only knew how to attack, no matter how powerful the enemy. They were born to fight and die. So if they were falling back now, it could only mean one thing: the Queen had ordered it.
[SFX: URGENT SHOUT]
"Run!"
The word had barely left Shane's lips when the Queen moved. Years of imperial army training were all that kept him moving in this life-or-death moment.
[SFX: MASSIVE CREATURE MOVEMENT]
[SFX: SWORD SLASHING]
It was massive, yet it moved with terrifying speed, instantly blocking their escape. Its two greatswords scythed through the crowd.
Zane vaulted into the air, scrambling over the sweeping blade just in time. Others weren't so lucky. Eight more soldiers were cut down.
From his position behind the other soldiers, Rex glimpsed the massive greatsword arcing toward him with unstoppable momentum. Escape was impossible.
Grim resolve hardened his features as he summoned every ounce of his power. His body erupted in brilliant golden light, the hallmark of a High-tier candidate preparing to unleash his ultimate technique.
[SFX: DEAFENING CRACK]
A deafening crack shattered the air.
Rex channeled his golden energy into a single, concentrated point and hurled it at the approaching blade with everything he possessed. The desperate blast connected, momentarily slowing the greatsword's lethal trajectory—but only for a heartbeat.
[SFX: SWORD CUTTING FLESH]
[MUSIC: TRAGIC DEFEAT]
Then the blade continued its merciless path, cleaving through flesh and bone as it sliced Rex perfectly in two, his golden light extinguishing in a spray of crimson.
