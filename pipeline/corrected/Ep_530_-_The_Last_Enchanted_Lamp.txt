Really, how many people in The Heartlands actually had the guts to stand up to Maddox?
Even Zane wasn't about to go head-to-head with him. It would be suicide to pick a fight with a warrior from the Life-and-Death Realm without a damn good reason. But Zane didn't have a choice. Ever since Maddox had taken Elara, a line had been crossed. There was no going back.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]
So when he finally came face-to-face with Maddox, Zane didn't hold back. Begging was off the table. He knew he’d have to be the one to save Elara, and if that meant a fight to the death, so be it.
Perhaps this was all just part of Zane's path to power.
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CROWD]
[SFX: DISTANT MURMURS]
After Maddox left, word of the Cloud Hall’s Order-protecting array spread through The Heartlands like wildfire. An array that had not only blocked a full-force attack from Maddox but had nearly killed him in the process? It was almost impossible to believe.
What did this mean? It meant that in all of The Heartlands, there wasn't a single faction or warrior that could truly threaten the Cloud Hall anymore. If a war broke out between the orders, the Cloud Hall would barely be scratched. At that point, they might as well be considered a fifth-grade sect.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]
A single runic master had rewritten the destiny of an entire Fourth-Grade Order. It looked like Zane's name was about to be etched into the history of The Heartlands. At the very least, one thing was for sure: his skill with runes blew Arnold’s out of the water.
In the days that followed, Zane and the Cloud Hall's array were all anyone could talk about.
"Zane's the talk of every order in The Heartlands now!"
"Ha! The Runic Tower was always so high and mighty. Now that everyone knows how skilled Zane is, do you think he'll be even more arrogant?"
"His runic skills might be something else, but Maddox is not someone you mess with. The moment Zane sets foot outside the Cloud Hall without that array to protect him, he’s a dead man!"
[AMBIENT: BUSY RESTAURANT]
[SFX: CROWD CHATTER]
Talk like this was happening everywhere. This particular conversation was taking place in a packed restaurant.
[SFX: TABLE SLAM]
Suddenly, a tall warrior slammed his hand on the table and roared with laughter. "I wouldn't be so sure! You're underestimating him. He's only in the Soul Realm, but he already beat a warrior in the middle stage of the Inner-Core Realm. And did you hear what he said to Maddox? 'I'll spare your life!' Ha! The balls on that kid!"
"He's just talking big from inside his little turtle shell," another, more sober voice countered. "Without that array, Maddox would've crushed him with a flick of his finger."
"So what?"
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Across the Heartlands, rumors swirled. Within Cloud Hall, perception had shifted—no one viewed Zane as a mere disciple anymore. He'd brought the crisis and driven it away. Who else could claim that?
Whispers circulated behind closed doors: Zane and Tia shared something beyond alliance. The Hall Master's fondness exceeded gratitude. No one dared speak it openly.
[MUSIC: FESTIVE STRINGS]
A few days later, the Lantern Festival arrived.
[AMBIENT: FESTIVAL ATMOSPHERE]
[SFX: CHILDREN LAUGHING]
Night transformed Cloudhaven. Red lanterns blazed along every street. Children carried paper lanterns through crowds. Adults released sky lanterns upward—tokens of fortune for the coming year.
Hundreds floated toward Cloud Hall, some bursting against the protective array in brilliant cascades. From below, the barrier seemed aflame.
[AMBIENT: FESTIVE CITY SOUNDS]
Families slaughtered livestock for feast tables spilling into the streets. The city pulsed with warmth and noise.
For warriors, this night held a different meaning.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: DISTANT SWAMP SOUNDS]
Deep in the Heartlands lay Cloud Dream Swamp—a fog-choked wilderness harboring Cloud-Swallowing Beasts whose cores yielded life-extending elixirs. Warriors flooded in annually to hunt them.
Some emerged with treasures. Others never returned.
Soul Realm warriors had no business entering. Inner-Core masters could venture three hundred miles. Spirit-Tribulation cultivators managed eight hundred before facing genuine lethality. Beyond a thousand miles, even the strongest might perish.
Past fifteen hundred miles—no records existed.
[AMBIENT: DEAD SWAMP SILENCE]
[MUSIC: EERIE STRINGS]
At the edge of a vast black quagmire stood a straw hut. A short, unremarkable man emerged—a Life-and-Death Realm warrior whose name appeared in no records.
He looked up at the sky and murmured to himself. "The Lantern Festival again. Seventy-seven times I've lit this lamp." He paused. "I wonder if my wish will finally come true this year."
[SFX: FAINT FLAME]
He drew a plain oil lamp and touched its wick. Deep red flame bloomed at his fingertip, igniting a faint yellow glow.
Nine tiny markings circled the lamp's surface. Six glowed softly. Three remained dark.
He studied them, smiling faintly. Last year, eight had burned. Would the ninth appear tonight?
[AMBIENT: QUIET GUILD HALL]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Meanwhile, inside the headquarters of the World Merchants' Guild, Hobbes looked stunned. "The ninth Enchanted Lamp? You mean...?"
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
The ancient tycoon nodded. "That's right. I've run the calculations. I'm certain of it—the ninth Enchanted Lamp has surfaced. The problem is, I don't know who has it. That's why I had you send out the invitations. To force their hand, to get them to light it!"
"Then what are we waiting for?" Hobbes, never one for patience, exclaimed. He stood and gave the Enchanted Lamp a light pat.
[SFX: MAGICAL FLAME IGNITION]
A flame shot out, and as the lamp ignited, a seventh mark began to glow on its surface.
The quiet Burns nodded. "Things are moving fast this year. Last year, the eighth mark didn't appear until midnight. I was starting to think its owner had given up!"
"Hopefully, we can still make it. I'll go inform the elders," Kieran said with a smile, his eyes fixed on the glowing marks.
[AMBIENT: OCEAN WAVES]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
On a small island in the vast South Sea, Maddox took out his own Enchanted Lamp. His preparations for the journey to the celestial tomb of mist were complete.
He was perched atop a massive tree, and at its base, six cloaked figures stood guard. These were powerful warriors from the middle and late stages of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, all sworn to die for his dream of building a divine kingdom. He would need them for the journey into the celestial tomb.
Still, Maddox had his doubts about whether the final Enchanted Lamp would ever appear.
[SFX: MAGICAL FLAME IGNITION]
Focusing his intent, he lit his own lamp, and the eighth mark flared to life.
Maddox stared at the faint yellow symbol on the small lamp and muttered to himself, "I wonder if the last one will light up tonight."
