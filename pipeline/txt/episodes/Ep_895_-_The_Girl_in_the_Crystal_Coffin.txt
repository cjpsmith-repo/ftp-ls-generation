Ep 895 - The Girl in the Crystal Coffin

Word count: 1010

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TOMB]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Alexander Shaw’s body had barely climbed out of the coffin before it was clear that Scott’s victory was going to be short-lived.

The difference in skill between Scott and Fiona was staggering. When Fiona had tried to control the corpse, it moved like a marionette with tangled strings, barely able to put one clumsy foot in front of the other. But the moment Scott slipped inside, the body moved with a startling fluidity, as if it were his own flesh and blood. After climbing out of the coffin, he leaped into the air and landed with the solid, grounded confidence of someone who had done this a thousand times.

And he had. His experience in the Emperor’s Secret Land was what set him apart, giving him a mastery over the dead that Fiona couldn’t hope to match.

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

He stumbled away from the coffin, pressing a hand to his forehead as his face twisted in agony. It wasn’t Alexander’s pain—that man had been a pile of bones for years. This was all Scott, fresh and searing.

[SFX: DEEP STONE GRINDING]

At that exact moment, a deep grinding sound echoed from the center of the platform as a massive stone pillar began to rise from the floor. The pillar was easily twenty feet wide, and though Zane knew it had to have been there all along, seeing it emerge from the seamless floor sent a jolt of shock through him. The other Soul Sea masters, however, watched it with a placid familiarity that only deepened Zane’s confusion.

As the pillar ascended, the details became clearer. Four rivets, each the size of a dining table, were embedded in its surface. Four pitch-black iron chains snaked out from them, converging on a coffin held fast in the very center of the stone.

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CHOIR]

[SFX: CHAIN RATTLING]

It was the most unique coffin Zane had ever seen: a transparent, sparkling blue crystal case. Because it was suspended upright by the chains, the person inside wasn’t lying down. She was standing.

It was a young girl in a thin, white dress. Her bare legs were shackled by the same black chains that held the Crystal Coffin in place.

“Gods, she’s incredible,” the Crown Prince of the Wind Divine Kingdom muttered, his eyes wide. “Who is she?” Even for a man accustomed to the most beautiful women in the kingdoms, her appearance was breathtaking.

Zane just stared, his mind racing to make sense of the impossible tableau.

The Soul Sea masters showed no surprise. Their eyes went straight to Scott.

While everyone was still reeling, the girl’s eyes snapped open.

[SFX: EYES SNAPPING OPEN]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Her gaze, a piercing gold, swept over the men standing below.

The moment her eyes opened, Scott’s hand shot to his head. He wasn’t just pressing his forehead anymore — he was clutching it, stumbling sideways across the platform.

A pressure radiated from the girl’s body, vast and focused, aimed entirely at him.

“Brave warrior of my Dragon-blooded,” she said, her voice light and unhurried. “Since you have awakened, do not forget your mission. Do not become a walking corpse.” She paused. “Answer my question. Tell me your name.”

Zane’s eyes flicked to the Emperor. Names. That was what he’d meant.

“Alexander Shaw!” several Soul Sea masters called out at once, feeding Scott the answer. “It’s Alexander Shaw — say it!”

Scott’s knees buckled. The corpse crashed down hard, its head slamming into the stone floor with a crack that split the platform. The body was strong enough that it wasn’t the corpse that was damaged.

“My name is—” Scott’s voice came out strangled. “My name is—” He was fighting it. The masters kept shouting the name at him. It made no difference. “My name is Scott.”

A ripple of disappointment moved through the Soul Sea masters.

“You have already bewitched yourself to death,” the girl said quietly.

[SFX: SOUL EJECTION PULSE]

Scott’s soul shot out of the corpse. The body of Alexander Shaw rose slowly to its feet, walked back across the platform under its own power, and lay down in its coffin.

The girl closed her eyes. In an instant, she was still again — no breath, no warmth, no sign of life.

And just like that, the entire puzzle clicked into place for Zane.

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TOMB ECHO]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHOIR]

The Dragon-blooded had a failsafe. 

They had preserved the bodies of their greatest masters for other tasks, but this created the obvious risk of them reanimating as mindless zombies. To prevent that, they had built a warden into the necropolis itself. If a corpse turned, the girl would test its consciousness. If it failed, she would instantly purge the soul animating it.

What the Dragon-blooded had never accounted for was human souls using Soul Cores to hijack the bodies for their own purposes.

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TOMB CHAMBER]

“Don’t let it get to you,” War King Wind said, clapping Scott on the shoulder. “Want to try again?”

Scott shook his head, the pain still etched on his face. “No. I’m going to the twelfth floor to find a new body. Wind, why don’t you, the Harlen Emperor, and York give this one a shot?”

[SFX: MAGICAL LIGHT FADING]

[SFX: WHOOSH]

As he spoke, the Soul Purifying Light on the nearby stairs faded. Without another word, Scott shot up toward the twelfth floor.

After he was gone, War King Wind exchanged a look with the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

“Alright,” the War King declared, his voice booming with renewed purpose. “My turn.”

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

He strode across the platform, but bypassed Alexander’s corpse, stopping instead before a plain black coffin. As the War King’s soul began to hover over it, one of the masters behind him spoke up.

“That’s the leader of the Dragon-blooded from seventy thousand years ago. His name is Hank Shaw. It’s an easy name to remember.”

Easy to say, but would it work?
