Ep 1134 - A Light in the Dark

Word count: 938

[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND PALACE]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
The Supreme Lord’s origins were a total mystery, a story lost to time. People had explored this Underground Palace before, but not one of them had ever figured out who he really was.
That alone was strange. Since the dawn of the new age, the history of every Supreme Lord had been meticulously recorded. Forgetting something so fundamental was almost unheard of. A Supreme Lord was a constant in the universe, a position held until death—an event that would shake the heavens. But for this one? There wasn’t a single clue in the entire Dark World.
The only hint was the massive Hydra statue at the palace entrance, which suggested he might have shared its bloodline. 
[SFX: ECHOING STONE RUMBLE]
The statue had nine heads, each one a potential key. Breaking one of the stone heads would shatter an enchanted barrier and grant a single chance to enter. After that, the palace would go dormant, waiting for its next opportunity.
Lavender had gotten in over two million years ago through the sixth head. Just a hundred thousand years later, Lincoln had earned his chance with the seventh. 
The sacred guardians of the Dark World watched the palace constantly, always knowing when a new chance to enter was granted. If Lincoln could get the seventh entrance open, they were ready to send their best young champions right in after him.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
As Lincoln stepped into the mouth of the seventh hydra head, everyone held their breath. The silence was absolute, a heavy quiet that came from being buried miles underground. 
Then it came.
[SFX: DRIPS, SLOW THEN FAST]
A single sound, small and wet, falling somewhere in the darkness beside the statue. Then another. Then another, faster, the rhythm building until it filled the vast space like a quiet countdown.
The brothers looked at each other. Even Empyrean Realm warriors felt the unease of it — no one knew what lurked in a place like this.
Zane’s eyes narrowed. A faint green light flickered in his pupils as he activated his Magic Vision, allowing him to see in the lightless space. What he saw made his brow furrow.
“Brother Lincoln,” he said, his voice calm and level. “How long until the entrance opens?”
Lincoln’s reply drifted out from inside the seventh head. “A little while yet.”
“Please hurry. There’s trouble here.”
Freya glanced at him sharply. She had never heard Zane call something trouble in that tone. She leaned close. “What is it?”
“The Lantern Pearl,” Zane said quietly, using Vitality to carry his voice to her alone. “Look how dim it is — it should be brighter than that. Something is suppressing it. A trace of Dark Law, if I had to guess.” He paused. “Cody is in for it.”
Freya looked. He was right. The orb in Cody’s hand cast barely a few feet of light in every direction — far less than it should have for its grade.
Adrian took a quiet step back.
Abner overheard none of it. He caught the look on Zane’s face and his lip curled. “No guts, no glory,” he said flatly. “You want a share of the palace treasure, you’d better learn to hold your nerve.” He turned to the third of his brothers — a broad-shouldered man who’d been the loudest complainer on the two-day walk in. “Cody. Go take a look.”
Cody flashed a grin at Zane as he passed, all swagger. He walked into the dark, holding his Lantern Pearl out ahead of him. Even at full brightness, the orb barely reached the ground at his feet — something was swallowing the light.
“Cody — anything?” Abner called.
“Not even a strand of hair,” Cody called back, laughing. “Some people spook easily.”
The brothers burst out laughing with him. The tension broke, and for a moment, the darkness felt like nothing at all.
Then the laughter died.
One by one, the smiles froze on their faces. They stared at Cody — who was still grinning — with eyes gone wide and blank with horror.
[SFX: PEARL SHAKING]
[SFX: BRIGHT LIGHT BURST]
In response, Cody fumbled for his Lantern Pearl, shaking it until it cast a bright light over himself.
Freya gasped, her hand instinctively shooting out to grab the sleeve of Zane’s shirt.
[SFX: MULTIPLE FOOTSTEPS SCRAMBLING BACK]
At the same time, Cody’s brothers scrambled back as one, their faces pale.
[AMBIENT: DARK CONSUMING HISS]
[SFX: FLESH DISSOLVING]
The beam of light revealed a sight from a nightmare. The darkness wasn’t just around Cody; it was *on* him, consuming him, eating away at his flesh and blood like acid. The most disturbing part was that he didn’t seem to feel a thing, his expression blank, as if he had no idea what was happening to his own body.
Then, as he saw the terror in their eyes, the realization finally hit him.
[SFX: AGONIZED SCREAM]
[SFX: STUMBLING FOOTSTEPS]
“Aaaahhhhh!” he screamed, stumbling back toward the group for help.
[SFX: PANICKED SHOUT]
“No! Don’t you dare come over here!”
[SFX: PANICKED SHOUTING]
“Just stay there and die!” his brothers shouted back at him, their earlier confidence gone. Their strength was for fighting beasts, not for facing a creeping, unknowable horror like this.
“What do we do?” Freya asked, her voice shaking with panic as she looked to Zane.
He didn’t answer. He just grabbed her by the waist and yelled a single command to the others.
“Retreat!”
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS SCRAMBLING]
They were gone in a flash, the six brothers scrambling to follow Zane’s lead without a moment’s hesitation.
Cody, still trying to run toward them, stood no chance against the corrosive shadow. 
[SFX: BODY DISSOLVING HISS]
[SFX: BONES CLATTERING ON STONE]
His body dissolved in seconds, and all that was left was a clean skeleton that clattered to the stone floor. 
[SFX: PEARL ROLLING ON STONE]
[SFX: MOTH WINGS FLUTTERING]
The Lantern Pearl, his only beacon, rolled away from the bones, its soft light catching the fluttering wings of cannibal moths that danced in the oppressive dark.
