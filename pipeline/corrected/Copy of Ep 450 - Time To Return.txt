Ep 450 - Time To Return

Word count: 1220

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Zane couldn't come up with any other explanation for how the Owl Beast's spirit had just… vanished. But it was a fact. One second it was there, the next it was gone, and he'd been standing right there.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

Honestly, the city lord was being generous by even asking. If it weren't for Josh and Madame Vermillion, he would’ve had Zane in chains already.

When the city lord saw the Palace leader offer Zane a drop of True Dragon's Heartblood, he knew he couldn't touch the kid. Besides, Zane was somehow tangled up in whatever was going on with Josh and the whole nine True Dragons mystery. He was more than happy to wash his hands of the whole mess.

Still, catching an Owl Beast spirit was a real pain. That meant Zane had to compensate him for the loss. The city lord knew it was pointless trying to get repayment from a warrior who’d just reached the peak of the adept realm, so he was really just testing the waters with Madame Vermillion.

[AMBIENT: PALACE HALL]

Just as he’d figured, Zane kept his mouth shut. It was Madame Vermillion who spoke first. "It's just one Owl Beast spirit. Dragonfall Palace can cover the cost. But are you seriously asking me to go catch one for you?"

Catching an Owl Beast spirit was no easy feat. You needed a hell of a lot of luck just to find one, and even then, cornering it was another story entirely.

Since Madame Vermillion had already offered to compensate him, the city lord knew better than to push his luck. If he pissed her off, she might just take Zane by force, and there wouldn’t be a damn thing he could do about it.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL FADE]

So he decided to drop it. For now.

"Let's go." 

 [SFX: FOOTSTEPS DEPARTING]

Alan waved a dismissive hand and left with Elder Jovan. Since Madame Vermillion had offered a drop of True Dragon's Heartblood to Zane, Alan knew he had lost. There was no point in staying. He, the leader of the Dragonblaze Hall, had come in person and still couldn't tempt the kid. Offering True Dragon's Heartblood to a boy at the peak of the adept realm? What an absolute waste. The woman had to be out of her mind.
Before she left, Madame Vermillion pressed a small amulet into Zane's hand. "This will save your life once, when nothing else will. I can't do more than that — the rest of the path is yours to walk." Zane closed his fingers around it and nodded.
Elder Howard pulled him aside before he could follow. He handed Zane a bundle — a sword and a set of armor. "Standard issue for Dragonfall Palace disciples. Nothing special."
Zane unrolled the bundle and went still. Both pieces were Sacred Weapons. The kind that warlords in the Heartlands started wars over. Standard issue.
He didn't say anything. There was nothing to say.


[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STRINGS]

Seeing Alan leave, Vermillion smiled. She turned to Zane. "You have a solid foundation, but your power level is still low. This city is a gateway to the Lower World, and there are plenty of Cultivation Methods here that can help you improve. I suggest you refine the True Dragon’s Heartblood right here. After that, you can use the teleportation array to come to Dragonfall Palace."

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]

It was a good suggestion, but Zane shook his head. "I appreciate the offer, but I can't accept. Not right now. I only came to the Upper World to train, and I have unfinished business back in the Lower World. So, I can't go to Dragonfall Palace just yet."

Zane knew he’d be passing up a huge opportunity.

Back in his world, warriors who sought to grasp Law Power spent decades deciphering faded inscriptions on crumbling obelisks, often dying before achieving even a fragment of understanding. Here in the Upper World, Complete Law-Stones existed—crystallized wisdom that could be studied directly—and Cultivation Methods beyond his wildest dreams lay within reach.
He stayed two months.
Two months of relentless training in the Dragon-blooded grounds — Laws, soul tempering, cultivation — and when he finally stood at the city gate and looked out at the hundreds of thousands of transmission points stretching to the horizon, he was no longer the peak Adept who had arrived. He had broken through the bottleneck entirely, pushed into the Soul Realm, and kept going until he reached its third stage.
He burned his Vitality and rose into the air. The transmission array dissolved him into light.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY]

The opportunity tantalized him, yet Zane knew the reality. Simply remaining in a place like this presented challenges he hadn't anticipated. The upcoming contests had transformed the entire city. Every inn and tavern overflowed with cultivators from distant regions, their excitement palpable in the crowded streets. Prices had skyrocketed accordingly. A modest room that ordinarily cost a few low-grade Crystal Cubes per night now demanded tens of them—an extortion that would drain his resources within days.

[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]

Cries of frustration erupted from warriors checking lodging prices, their indignation echoing through the marketplace. Arguments broke out between innkeepers and desperate travelers. Some cultivators, their pride wounded by the astronomical costs, threatened to sleep in the streets rather than submit to such exploitation. Others grudgingly counted out their crystals, faces tight with resentment.

Yet for all the complaints, nothing changed. The innkeepers remained unmoved, their expressions carefully neutral as they turned away those who couldn't pay. This was the harsh arithmetic of opportunity: if warriors wanted to witness the contests, to potentially gain insights from watching masters compete, they had to accept the price.

[AMBIENT: ARENA EXTERIOR]

[MUSIC: EPIC DRUMS]

Meanwhile, the Dragon Soar Arena was finally ready. Fences and armed guards surrounded the massive structure, with members of Cloud Hall, Bloodwood Cliff, the Black Mountain Order, and the Ethereal Spirit Sect all standing watch.

In reality, the elders of the sects had gathered days ago, and the World Merchants' Guild had also sent people to take command. No one dared lay a hand on the Dragon Soar Arena; it would be a death sentence. The sects posted guards mostly as a show of force—a way to declare their stake in the game and intimidate rivals.

[AMBIENT: LIGHT RAIN]

[MUSIC: ANTICIPATORY STRINGS]

The weather was perfect. After a long, hot spell, a light summer rain had finally cooled the air.

[AMBIENT: LARGE CROWD MURMURING]

A huge crowd of warriors stood around the Dragon Soar Arena, buzzing with discussion. None of these warriors was qualified to compete, but the rumor that the Dragon Soar Arena itself was the grand prize was enough to draw them in for a look.

"They say anyone who enters the Dragon Soar Arena can fly straight to the Upper World! They'll become an immortal or a god. Can you imagine a treasure like that? Everyone must want it!"

"No wonder all the big sects are fighting over it. Man, I'd do anything to join whichever one wins!"

"Ha! The sects in this contest are Fourth-Tier sects or higher. Forget the Dragon Soar Arena, you really think you have what it takes to get into one of them?"

[SFX: METEOR WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

As the crowd buzzed, a bright light suddenly flared in the sky. 

It looked like a meteor, streaking down from above and heading straight for the top of the Dragon Soar Arena.

They shielded their eyes, mouths dropped in surprise. What could it be? 

Or who?
