Ep 1265 - She Lost

Word count: 1188

[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]
[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL REVERB]
The walls of the Hall of Causality were inscribed with Celestial Laws, the same kind that made the ogre cities practically indestructible. So when the small skull connected with the wall, it didn’t break through—it shot off with terrifying force. It ricocheted so fast that dodging was out of the question. The whole crowd just froze, too stunned to even breathe.
[SFX: SKULL RICOCHETING OFF WALL]
[SFX: METAL IMPACT ON ARMOR]
The skull zipped toward an ogre warrior and smashed right into a spike on his shoulder armor. Now, this wasn’t just any armor; it was a Grade-One Divine Weapon that constantly absorbed the warrior’s own life force, fusing with his body to become incredibly strong.
But the skull didn’t just break the spike. It hit an inch from the main plate and vaporized it, turning solid metal to dust. 
[SFX: ARMOR VAPORIZING]
[SFX: HEAVY BODY CRASHING]
The force of the impact alone was enough to send the massive ogre crashing to the ground.
[SFX: RAPID RICOCHETS]
The skull pinballed across the hall, hit the far wall, and shot back out the way it came, with the deflected ray of white light right on its tail. Through the whole chaotic blur, nobody had moved a muscle. Standing still was the only smart move. 
The second the two projectiles vanished from the hall, the entire room let out a collective sigh of relief.
But the ogre warrior was already scrambling back to his feet, glaring daggers at Zane. 
“Are you trying to kill me?” he snarled. “The second we’re out of this hall, you and I are dueling!”
The impact had sent a nasty shockwave through the skull, and even though Zane had let go instantly, his fingers were still numb. He flexed them slowly, a faint, sharp smile touching his lips. 
“If you want a fight,” he said, his eyes glinting, “I accept.”
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
The calm intensity in Zane’s stare gave the ogre pause. He might have seemed reckless, but he was actually a pretty cautious guy, and he’d been watching Zane practice the whole time. He hadn’t understood what Zane was doing, exactly, but he’d seen the woman’s reaction and knew Zane had to be powerful. The challenge had been pure, hot-headed anger. Now, he wasn’t so sure. He only had five million Illusion Points left after paying to get into the hall. 
Getting his ass kicked and thrown out now would be a total waste.
A lot of the other warriors were just as annoyed, but this wasn’t the time or place for a brawl. Besides, now that they understood the rules of this first hall, it was actually pretty straightforward. It was all about controlling your speed and timing.
With the pattern figured out, the rest of the hall took only a few breaths to clear, one warrior at a time.
But just as the last of them stepped past the threshold, a small black shape came hurtling back through the entrance behind them — the same skull, still carrying momentum from its earlier ricochet, now colliding head-on with another oncoming beam of light.
[SFX: SECOND IMPACT COLLISION]
Nobody waited to see what happened next. The whole group bolted, Zane included, sprinting clear of the hall entirely.
[SFX: GOLDEN LIGHT SPARKLING]
On the platform beyond, a scatter of soft golden light spots drifted toward them.
“The reward,” someone said.
“One hundred points? That’s it?” another warrior snapped. “That’s a beggar’s payout.”
“You told us the first hall paid well,” the ogre warrior said, turning on the woman in white.
She threw up her hands. “Don’t look at me — I didn’t design this place. The first hall’s never been difficult, so the reward’s never been much. I only pulled fifty points out of it last time.”
Grumbling, the group filed back onto the platform. There was nothing to do but keep climbing — turning back now would mean the five million points were gone for nothing.
The woman in white shot Zane a look. “You’re the one who made it harder than it needed to be.”
“Agreed — you’re being dangerous,” the short human warrior added, though his tone was more even than heated. “I get wanting to deal with those skulls, but think about the rest of us next time.” He offered a small smile after saying it.
Zane inclined his head. “Fair. I didn’t expect that light to hit that hard.”
“No harm done. Just be careful,” the warrior said. Then, glancing at the skulls still clinging to Zane’s back, he couldn’t help himself. “Out of curiosity — are you actually all right? Those things have been gnawing on you the whole way up.”
“I’m fine,” Zane said simply. “Let’s keep moving.”
The warrior studied him a moment longer, clearly unconvinced but unwilling to push, then turned and started up the stairs himself.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVERENT]
Many of them belonged to one of the Four Great Clans. And while they were called “clans,” their influence was far greater than any tenth-tier sacred place; a single one of them was more powerful than some entire races in the universe. Their real power came from the fact that each of the Four Great Clans was backed by Supreme Lords.
The woman in white was especially impressive, considered second only to the very top talents of those four clans.
The group continued up the stairs, the woman in white no longer leading the way but simply climbing alongside the rest of them.
“I still don’t know your name,” she said, glancing back at Zane.
“Zane Jones,” he answered.
She smiled and kept climbing. “You’re lying.”
“How’d you know that?” Zane asked, not bothering to deny it.
“It’s a talent of mine. Everyone’s heart beats a little faster when they lie.”
“Noted.” Zane inclined his head. “And you? What should I call you?”
“You can call me Janet.”
“That’s a good name,” Zane said with a smile.
She returned it. 
“The attunement method you were just using… it looked familiar,” she said. “I’ve seen a friend of mine use it before, and I was wondering if there was a connection.” She’d been dying to ask about it, but she knew she couldn’t just come out and grill a stranger about his techniques.
“Your friend? Who?” Zane asked.
She hesitated for a second before answering. “Sloane Kincaid.”
A real smile spread across Zane’s face. 
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
“Funny you should mention her. I learned this secret skill from her.”
“What? You’ve met Sloane?” 
The woman was floored. She couldn’t imagine why Sloane would ever teach an outsider a House Kincaid secret—it was a crime that not even a top talent like her would be forgiven for.
“I’ve met her,” Zane confirmed. “And I’ve fought her.”
“Fought her?” the woman blurted out, her mind still reeling from the implications. “Did she… defeat you?” Even as powerful as Zane seemed, she couldn’t imagine him beating Sloane.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
“She lost.”
The woman stopped dead in her tracks. Zane, already a few steps ahead on the stairs, turned back. 
“What’s wrong?”
