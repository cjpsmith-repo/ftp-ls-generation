Ep 1320 - I’m Here To Find My Husband

Word count: 1141

[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING FOOTSTEPS IN GRAND HALL]
Tia trailed behind Priscilla, her heart pounding with anxiety. She was smart enough to know just how deep her trouble ran. Having just arrived from the Lower World, she had no connections, no foothold—what chance did she really have against such overwhelming odds?
Her only hope was Zane.
But from what she could gather, Zane could barely handle his own problems. Sure, he was famous across the universe, but he hadn’t gotten there by kissing up to some big-shot organization. In fact, it sounded like he’d managed to piss off a lot of powerful people along the way.
Even though Priscilla was being incredibly polite, Tia had no idea if that was a good thing or a very, very bad one. Her biggest fear was falling into the hands of the Jadeheart Sanctuary. If they used her as a pawn against Zane, it would be her worst nightmare realized. But she was utterly powerless to stop it.
[AMBIENT: LARGE GATHERING MURMURS]
They rounded the hall and stepped into a massive square, where it seemed all the core members of the Jadeheart Sanctuary were gathered. Thirty-two palace leaders stood among various hall leaders, stewards, and elders.
[SFX: ENERGY PRESSURE WAVE]
The sheer energy rolling off these warriors was intense, a physical pressure in the air. The weakest among them was at the Spirit Transformation Realm, and a few had power that was completely off the charts. Tia, who was only at the Soul Sea Realm, couldn’t even begin to guess the strength of the most powerful ones. They were probably on the verge of becoming World Lords.
All eyes turned to her, staring with open curiosity, sizing up her talent and potential.
“Is that woman really Zane Lowell’s wife?”
“He’s from the Lower World, so it’s not impossible. But it’s way more likely she’s faking it.”
“Faking it? We’ll see. The Saint Lord will find out the truth. She’ll pay dearly if she’s lying.”
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]
A low murmur spread through the crowd as Priscilla led her forward. With the competition in the Illusion Battlefield heating up, everyone was glued to the leaderboards. Their own star talent, Josiah Sloan, had just broken into the top seventy thousand, and his rank was still climbing fast. Honestly, they would have been thrilled if he’d just made it into the top hundred thousand, so this was a huge surprise.
After all, they were just a tenth-grade sacred place, one of hundreds of thousands like them across the universe.
Of course, Josiah’s success was nothing compared to Zane’s. The way he was climbing the ranks had the entire Alliance watching.
Priscilla brought Tia forward to an old man seated in an ancient chair, his gaze fixed on the pyramid overhead. She bowed deeply before him. “Master, I have brought the person.”
This was the Saint Lord, master of the entire tenth-grade Sanctuary. Whatever cultivation method he practiced had left him looking impossibly old, his movements slow and deliberate—but his eyes, sunk deep in a face full of wrinkles, were sharp and alert.
He turned that gaze on Tia. “Are you really Zane’s wife?”
“I’m here to find my husband,” Tia answered.
The Saint Lord nodded slowly. “Look into my eyes.”
[SFX: SOFT ETHEREAL HUM]
The moment their eyes met, Tia felt herself pulled in, like her whole body was sinking into his gaze, but strangely, she felt no discomfort, just the pull.
After a long moment, the Saint Lord closed his eyes, and Tia felt a small tremor run through her before it released her. She let out a breath.
“Zane ascended to the Soul Sea Realm,” the Saint Lord said at last, “and was sent to cultivate at the Thirteen Palaces. He’s one of ours, in a sense. Since you’re his wife, and you’ve found your way to the Jadeheart Sanctuary, I intend to look after you properly. Zane himself was sent directly to the Mist Palace. If you’re willing, I could send you there as well.”
“The Mist Palace?” Tia asked.
“The cultivation ground of the Human Alliance itself,” the Saint Lord said. “Only the most outstanding warriors are permitted to set foot there.”
Tia shook her head. “I’m not going. I’d rather stay here and wait until Zane arrives.”
The Saint Lord’s expression softened into something like a smile. “You may stay. Once the fighting in the Illusion Battlefield ends, I can send out a summons to call him here myself. Though you should understand his situation—the great powers surrounding the Elysian Keep have no intention of letting him go easily. It may be some time before he’s free to come.”
“I understand,” Tia said. “Thank you, Master.”
The Saint Lord turned to Priscilla. 
“Take good care of her,” he commanded.
Priscilla’s eyes lit up. Taking Tia on as a student was an opportunity anyone would have jumped at. Her own talent aside, everyone knew Zane had a bright future ahead of him. Helping his wife was a smart move, one that could earn Zane’s gratitude—maybe even his friendship. 
Besides, the Saint Lord had already used his Soul Theurgy to confirm it: this woman was definitely his wife. A warrior might have flings on the road, but a wife was different. She clearly meant the world to him.
“But I don’t want to train right now,” Tia said, her voice firm, her eyes fixed on the towering ranking pyramid in the center of the square. “I want to stay here and watch the leaderboards.”
“That’s no problem at all,” Priscilla said with a warm smile, immediately leading her to a prime viewing spot. “I’ll stay and watch with you.”
[MUSIC: UPLIFTING TRIUMPHANT]
[AMBIENT: SACRED TEMPLE INTERIOR]
And just like that, Tia became an official disciple of the Jadeheart Sanctuary. She was even granted access to the very heart of the place, the Jadeheart Sanctum itself.
When she’d first heard about the Sanctum, she’d dreamed of seeing it, wondering if her husband would ever be worthy enough to step inside. Now, the Saint Lord was letting her in simply because of her connection to Zane. 
The world felt light, unreal, like she was walking through a dream.
*Just what did you do to earn this much respect in the Upper World?* she wondered.
She had no idea about Zane’s incredible performance in Passing the Torch, or that the most powerful Supreme Lords in the Alliance were watching his every move.
Her eyes found his name on the board. Seventy-seventh.
“Incredible,” she breathed, a proud smile spreading across her face. “I had no idea he’d come this far.”
[MUSIC: WARM NOSTALGIC STRINGS]
Tia remembered when she first met him, back when he was only at the Adept Realm. A wave of emotion washed over her, rich and deep as the years between then and now.
