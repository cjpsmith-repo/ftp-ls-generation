Ep 455 - Maybe You've Heard of Me

Word count: 1118

[SFX: SCREAMING]

[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND]

The wind howled, sharp and desperate.

[SFX: SWORD MOVEMENT]

At that sound, the Septuplets of the Ethereal Spirit Sect froze mid-strike. They turned their swords sideways, watching the ethereal glow from their blades slowly fade like dying embers.

[SFX: SWORD SHEATHING]

Tia sheathed her icy-blue halberd in one swift motion, the weapon singing as it slid home. She tucked her legs beneath her and settled onto its shaft with ease, the phoenix feathers of her cloak cascading around her like a silken curtain. Despite the interruption, her expression remained utterly composed—almost bored.

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

Three portly men stumbled into view, gasping for breath as though they'd run miles rather than mere yards. Sweat beaded on their foreheads. Their robes, fine but rumpled, marked them as merchants rather than warriors. They appeared less threatening than the average street vendor, yet every powerful figure gathered here straightened at their arrival.

The friendly-looking one was Kieran. The fierce one, whose gaze could freeze blood, was Hobbes. And the one with the perpetually red face was Burns.

These three men, who huffed and puffed like common peddlers, commanded respect that even sect elders couldn't ignore. They were the three managers of the World Merchants' Guild—and their harmless appearance was the most dangerous deception in the Heartlands.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

The Guild answered to no faction. It was founded by a coalition of businessmen who now controlled over seventy percent of the vitality mines and eighty percent of all commerce in the Heartlands. With that kind of wealth and influence, it was an open secret that the Guild was one of the strongest forces in the land—on par with a sixth-grade sect. That level of power earned them the status of a “sacred place,” a destination most warriors could only dream of reaching.

Everyone accepted the World Merchants' Guild's power, but the most astonishing thing about them was how quietly they’d amassed it, far from the public eye. Take these three: all were masters of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, yet they looked like simple country merchants you could easily push around. They gave off no aura of power whatsoever.

And yet, no one dared to touch them. Not Cloud Hall. Not the Ethereal Spirit Sect.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Since Tia’s arrival, the area around the Dragon Soar Arena had become the new focal point of Oracle City. The elders from the Ethereal Spirit Sect, the leader of Cloud Hall, and countless other warriors—both from sects and independents—had their eyes fixed on this spot. Oracle City was massive, but distance meant little to masters of their caliber; they could arrive in a flash, and each had their own reason for being here.

Kieran stepped forward placatingly. "Everyone, please, you are all honored guests in Oracle City. A fight like this isn't right. Why don't we talk this through peacefully?"

Tia glanced at him, her expression unreadable, then shook her head. "Alliance Master Kieran, Zane is my Cloud Hall disciple. I'm taking him."

"So what if he's a Cloud Hall disciple?" Lady Darkmere interjected sharply. "If that boy refuses to reveal the Dragon Soar Arena's secret, there's no point holding this Martial Arts Tournament in Oracle City!"

The Septuplets' leader nodded. "Lady Darkmere speaks truth. Zane is the only person who has successfully ascended the arena. He holds its secret."

Kieran smiled diplomatically. "Many sects wish to compete for the Dragon Soar Arena as a pathway to the Upper Realm. If we can't solve its mystery, winning it becomes meaningless." He turned to Zane. "If Zane reveals the secret, everyone benefits."

All eyes shifted to Zane.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Tia flashed forward, positioning herself between Zane and the others. "Zane, don't tell them!"

"But..." Zane understood the precarious situation. Despite Kieran's pleasant words, they were forcing his hand. Cloud Hall was powerful, but the Septuplets' sect alone could match them. Combined with the World Merchants' Guild, refusal could bring serious consequences.

Besides, the truth was so simple that revealing it wouldn't matter.

Zane tugged at her sleeve. She glanced back and caught a faint smile on his face—a hint of slyness that lasted only an instant. What is this boy planning? Tia wondered.

Ignoring her emotions, Zane addressed the crowd. "Sect Masters, the truth is... the Dragon Soar Arena has returned to normal."

[SFX: CROWD CHATTER]

"What? Can it be ascended again?" Lady Darkmere glanced at the arena.

"Explain yourself clearly—why has it returned to normal?" Raven demanded.

Even Tia stared at Zane with wide eyes. She had expended considerable effort to shield him, yet now he was simply telling them the truth. Based on her understanding of Zane, he would never do this without reason.

Kieran's expression grew serious. "Zane, can you explain clearly? Why has it returned to normal now?"

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Zane laid it out. "Look, in a moment of desperation, I tweaked the runes on the sixth floor of the Dragon Soar Arena. That’s why you couldn’t fly. But I just set them back to normal, so it should work fine now."

"Changed the runes?" Raven scoffed. "Hmph. Don't be ridiculous. Not even the best runic masters in the Heartlands can alter the arena's runes—they're far too complex. How could a mere disciple possibly manage it?" He was right to be skeptical. Several of the leading sects had already pinpointed the problem on the sixth floor and sent their top runic experts to investigate. Every single one had failed.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]

The runes were simply too sophisticated for anyone to decipher.

Zane just shrugged and turned his gaze to Kieran. "I guess the World Merchants' Guild in Cloudhaven never passed along the message to you," he said calmly. "If they had, you'd already know about my... particular skill with runes."

Zane’s tone was casual, but his words made every head in the clearing turn toward Kieran.

[SFX: REALIZATION GASP]

Kieran blinked, a blank look on his face. He shook his head slowly, then his eyes went wide as he slapped a hand to his forehead. "Zane… Zane Lowell! Of course! How could I forget!"

A couple of months back, Michael Nash—the Guild's branch leader in Cloudhaven—had sent a priority message. A new runic expert with "perfect brushwork" had emerged. From that day on, Zane Lowell was to be considered a person of interest. Kieran just hadn't connected that name with the young man standing right in front of him.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

How could he have forgotten such a report? Squinting, Kieran gave a firm nod to the assembled masters. "He's right," he declared, his voice carrying across the clearing. "When it comes to the art of runes, there isn't a soul in the Heartlands who can touch him… except, perhaps, for Arnold himself."

[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]

The words hung in the air, followed by a thick, stunned silence that fell over the entire crowd.
