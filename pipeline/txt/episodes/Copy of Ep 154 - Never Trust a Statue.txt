Ep 154 - Never Trust a Statue
Word Count: 1092

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TEMPLE]
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING]
 A deep, grinding rumble echoed from the runic statue, and every eye in the room was glued to it.
The first statue slid slowly across the floor, the ground humming with a slight vibration as it came to a stop on the “Heaven” glyph.
As he watched, Zane remembered something from Chase's book on ciphers. Chase had written the number "three" next to the entry for "Heaven." 
“That must be it,” Zane thought. “Three statues go on that glyph.”
After the long-haired woman, Sandra, positioned the first statue, she bowed her head, calculating again. A moment later, she guided a second statue onto the “Heaven” glyph. After a final, quick calculation, she slid the third statue into place to join the others.
Zane gave a small nod. She'd been way too modest saying she wasn't any good with the Runic Cipher. Her first move matched Chase's notes exactly. He just hoped her luck—and her skill—held out for the rest.
[SFX: MAGICAL ACTIVATION]
 As the third statue settled onto the “Heaven” glyph, the carving flared with a golden light. She’d gotten it right.
A wave of confidence washed over the group.
“Sandra’s grasp of these formations is incredible!”
“She’s not just good—she’s a master!”
The other Soul Realm masters showered her with praise.
Sandra just shook her head, sweeping her long hair back. “Don’t celebrate just yet,” she warned. “The first step is always the easiest. It’s only going to get harder from here. The more complex the calculations, the higher the chance of failure.”
With that, she lowered her head and returned to her calculations.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL POWER]
[SFX: ENERGY PULSING]
 Now, Seymour and several other Soul Realm masters began to activate the Vitality within them. Their bodies pulsed with brilliant flashes of light, radiating a terrifying power.
The pressure in the room was immense. Zane knew better than to say a word.
After a moment's hesitation, Sandra bit her lip, committing to her own judgment.
[SFX: RUMBLE]
Once again, a low rumble echoed from one of the statues.
[SFX: STONE SLIDING]
 A fifth statue rumbled to life, sliding toward the “Valley” glyph. 
[SFX: CLICK]
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS HUM]
The moment it clicked into place, the glyph illuminated—not with the expected golden radiance, but with a nauseating, blood-red light that pulsed like a wounded heart.
[MUSIC: APPROACHING HORROR]
Horror swept across the chamber as pallor claimed every face. Zane's warning echoed in their minds, his foresight now painfully validated. They had dismissed his caution, and realization dawned far too late, leaving a bitter taste of regret.
"No," Sandra whispered, her fingers trembling as she stepped back from the cipher. "What have I done?"
But remorse offered no sanctuary now. The die was cast; their fate sealed by a single, irreversible decision.
[SFX: TREMORS]
[SFX: MAGICAL SPINNING]
The ground beneath them convulsed violently, sending several stumbling to their knees. Ancient mechanisms groaned to life deep within the structure, a sound like the awakening of something that should have remained dormant for eternity. The entire Runic Cipher began to rotate, its symbols blurring into concentric rings of arcane power. 
[SFX: MIST]
When it finally shuddered to a halt with a bone-jarring finality, tendrils of black and white mist rose from its surface, intertwining like lovers before coalescing above them. The vapor shaped itself into two ominous words that hung in the air, their meaning unmistakable and chilling:
[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]
 The Desperate Melee.
“What does that mean?”
“This is bad! We’ve fallen into a trap!”
The Soul Realm masters cried out, their faces ashen with horror. The Elysian Keep was a dangerous place, and now, they were doomed.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY WHIP]
[SFX: ENERGY STRIKES]
 The words vanished. A split second later, a tether of pure Vitality shot out from the Cipher, striking Sandra. It coiled around her waist in an instant. Before anyone could react, the tether lashed out, snaring the master beside her, then Wilson Hawthorne.
When the tendril of Vitality whipped toward Zane, he leaped into the air, trying to dodge. It was no use. The tether was as fast as light, sensing his movement and changing course to intercept him. It wrapped tight around his waist, yanking him from the air.
When the chaos subsided, every person in the central spire was bound by the same, single tether of Vitality.
“Why is it tying us together?” one of them choked out in a panic.
Zane stared down in disbelief at the glowing cord wrapped around his waist.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
 Suddenly, it all clicked. The Desperate Melee…
The meaning crashed down on him. Everyone connected by the tether had to fight until only one was left standing.
A wave of horrified realization rippled through the group. They trembled, their eyes wide with shock.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSE]
 They were trapped. A fight to the death.
The Seven Great Houses might hold deep grudges and constantly vie for power, but they were supposed to be allies on this expedition. Killing each other after they'd just been working as a team was a hard pill to swallow. But while they might hesitate to turn on one another, they'd have no problem ganging up to eliminate an outsider first.
A grim reality set in as Zane stared at the glowing tether connecting him to them all. No matter how this played out, he was the odd one out. Eventually, he'd have to face a Soul Realm master.
He’d almost taken down Hugh with his Five-Clawed Golden Dragon, but that had been a fluke—pure luck. In a straight-up fight, he knew he was no match for any of these masters.
“What am I going to do?” he muttered to himself. The masters were already starting to turn on each other, their alliances fracturing in an instant. For now, no one was paying him any mind. That gave him a precious few seconds to think.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[SFX: HAWK SHRIEK]
 Just then, one of the masters summoned a giant hawk of pure energy. With a piercing shriek, the massive bird dove straight for Seymour.
[SFX: SWORD ENERGY ACTIVATION]
 In response, a flash of green light erupted around Seymour as his Emerald Sword flared to life. Sidestepping the hawk’s attack, he swung his blade not at the bird, but at the master who’d summoned it.
Caught off guard by Seymour's direct assault, the other master scrambled to defend himself, his concentration broken. The giant hawk, a construct of pure Vitality, lost its direction and simply hovered aimlessly right above Zane’s head.
