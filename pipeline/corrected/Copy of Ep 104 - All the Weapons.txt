Ep 104 - All the Weapons
Word count: 1036

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]
 The Armory dominated the marketplace with its impressive facade, though Zane harbored no illusions about finding exceptional treasures or rare Spirit Weapons in such a commercial establishment. The truly valuable artifacts never gathered dust on public shelves.
That suited his purposes perfectly. His objective wasn't to discover some legendary relic; he sought mid-grade weapons in quantity, and a sprawling emporium like this would surely maintain substantial inventory.
[SFX: DOOR OPENING]
 Upon entering, Zane's senses were overwhelmed by a dazzling display of weaponry adorning every wall—golden axes gleaming under the shop's crystal lights, emerald spears catching his eye with their lustrous shafts, crimson blades hanging in elegant rows. Despite their visual appeal, his trained eye immediately recognized them as merely basic-grade implements, beautiful but unremarkable to a serious cultivator.
[AMBIENT: BUSY SHOP INTERIOR]
[SFX: HAGGLING VOICES AND CLINKING METAL]
 The shop bustled with activity. Customers streamed in and out continuously, creating a symphony of haggling voices and clinking metal. Yellow-coated clerks darted between patrons like industrious bees, their practiced sales pitches floating through the air. Zane wandered through the aisles, occasionally raising his hand or making eye contact, a familiar knot of frustration tightening in his chest as clerk after clerk breezed past him without acknowledgment.
It wasn't hard to guess why. The clerks probably took one look at Zane's white robe, the standard uniform for a Skyreach Sect pledge, and wrote him off. Everyone working in the mart knew pledges had just joined the sect. Whatever bonuses they earned were usually spent on Crystal Cubes for Advancement. Figuring he couldn't afford much, they paid him no mind.
After a while, Zane finally spotted a clerk who wasn't busy. The man reluctantly made his way over.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
 "What can I help you find today? We've got an extensive selection of weaponry. Simply state your preference, and I'll locate the perfect piece for you," the clerk offered, his sales pitch flowing with the polished cadence of someone who had delivered it countless times before.
Zane nodded thoughtfully, his eyes still scanning the impressive but ultimately ordinary displays. "I've noticed you only showcase basic-grade weapons in this area. Could you direct me to where you keep your Legendary Weapons?"
"Legendary Weapons?" the clerk echoed, his eyebrows rising slightly as he assessed Zane more carefully, gaze lingering on the white pledge robe that typically signaled limited funds.
A flicker of calculation crossed the clerk's face. Despite his initial dismissal, experience had taught him caution. Wealthy heirs from the Great Houses occasionally amused themselves by dressing modestly, testing how merchants treated those of apparent lesser means. The memory of losing a substantial commission last season to such a disguised patron still stung. He straightened his posture, unwilling to risk another such misjudgment based solely on appearances.
Putting on a warmer smile, he said, "Of course. Our Legendary Weapons aren't displayed on the main floor. If you'd like to see them, I can take you to a private room. Right this way!"
 Zane nodded and followed the clerk toward the back. The Armory was huge, and as they walked deeper, he passed rows of tactical-grade weapons, which soon gave way to even higher-quality pieces.
[SFX: DOOR OPENING]
[AMBIENT: QUIET SHOWROOM]
 "Sir, this is our collection of Legendary Weapons. Every single one is of the highest quality!" the clerk announced proudly, leading Zane into a large, well-lit room.
Zane's eyes scanned the display cases. At first glance, he couldn't tell if these weapons were anything special. There could be a huge difference in quality between any two Legendary Weapons. The ones he'd taken from Evil Jace, for instance, had only produced five or six drops of Celestial Essence when melted down.
"Still, I'll take them all. Please pack them up," Zane said with a quick smile.
[SFX: SURPRISED GASP]
 The shopkeeper, a middle-aged man who had come to greet him, just froze. Zane's request was so out of left field that the man was rendered speechless. He'd been running The Armory for years and had seen every kind of customer, but he’d never met a kid who threw money around like it was nothing.
 "You're not serious, are you?" the shopkeeper asked, his voice cautious. If he hadn't spotted the gold medallion from the Sacred Vault on Zane, he would have had him thrown out. But the medallion meant Zane could actually back up his words.
"I'm dead serious," Zane replied. "And since I'm buying in bulk, can I get a discount?"
"A discount? Absolutely! Of course, you'll get a discount!" the shopkeeper said, his voice buzzing with excitement. He was now convinced. This kid really was going to buy all two hundred and twenty Legendary Weapons, even if he couldn't imagine why.
 "Everyone, gather here immediately!" the shopkeeper called out, his voice echoing through the spacious store with newfound enthusiasm. Once eight clerks had assembled before him, their curious expressions reflecting the unusual commotion, he gestured dramatically toward Zane. "I want every single Legendary Weapon in our inventory wrapped and prepared for our distinguished guest! No delays!"
[SFX: BUSTLING ACTIVITY]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
 The clerks sprang into action, their movements swift and purposeful as they retrieved the precious weapons from their glass displays and velvet-lined cases. Fingers that normally handled such treasures with reverent slowness now moved with efficient urgency. The clerk who had initially dismissed Zane stood frozen in place, his complexion draining to a ghostly pallor. His jaw slackened in disbelief, eyes widening as the magnitude of the transaction registered in his mind. He watched his colleagues bustle about, each handling weapons worth small fortunes, while the bitter taste of missed opportunity filled his mouth.
In that instant, a cold, sickening wave of regret washed over him. He’d thought Zane was just some punk trying to mess with him. He never imagined the kid was actually serious. A huge chunk of his salary came from commissions on these weapons; the payout from just one would have equaled several months of his base pay.
Had he merely shown respect from the beginning, a lifetime of financial security would have been his. The clerk's mind replayed his contemptuous expression, his apathetic demeanor—mistakes that now haunted him as irredeemable folly.
Yet as he wallowed in regret, a far more unsettling question eclipsed his self-pity: what could this young stranger possibly intend to do with such an arsenal of Legendary Weapons?
