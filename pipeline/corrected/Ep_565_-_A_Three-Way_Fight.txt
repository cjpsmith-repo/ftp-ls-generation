Ep 565 - A Three-Way Fight
Word count: 1021
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL FOREST]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORCHESTRAL]
On this chaotic continent, the Demon Night were few and far between. In fact, they were the smallest of all the races.
But the Demon Night were an incredibly powerful race, no matter what corner of the universe you looked in. From the moment of their birth, they possessed the strength of Adept-level creatures. They were a force to be reckoned with, even in the upper worlds.
Despite their small numbers, fate had been more than fair to them. The Demon Night was practically unmatched. Whether it was strength, talent, or even looks, every last one of them was close to perfect.
They had one fatal flaw, though: they couldn't reproduce. Growing their population was a near-impossible challenge. If it weren't for that single weakness, the Demon Night would have taken over this untamed continent long ago.
The Demon Night were a proud people, but not especially bloodthirsty. When Zane had first led a group of humans into their territory, they'd simply warned him to leave. That same pride made them reluctant to work with other races, which was exactly why the three of them were so stunned to see Zane's sword spirit.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
"Let me get a better look," the young woman with the bone bow said. A pale blue light flashed in her eyes, her pupils turning the color of the sky. The moment her gaze fell on Zane's sword spirit, the color drained from her face.
[SFX: DRAMATIC GASP]
[MUSIC: EPIC REVELATION]
"Queen!"
[SFX: SWIFT MOVEMENT]
[SFX: SOFT LANDING]
The word was a breath escaping her lips. In the next instant, she leaped from the treetop and dropped to one knee.
[AMBIENT: DENSE FOREST]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
"Queen?" the other two repeated, full of doubt. But when their eyes shifted to Lily, their expressions mirrored her shock. They knelt, eyes shining with reverence.
After paying their respects, the three slowly rose. "Phoebe," one said quietly. "Why is our Queen traveling with a human?"
Phoebe shook her head. "I don't know. There's more to this human than we thought. We never should have stopped him."
"He's no match for the Ogre War Lord. Go. Save him. I'll inform Elder May."
[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]
[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]
Phoebe nodded, then shot forward. She bounded from branch to branch like an agile leopard, already nocking an arrow mid-flight.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC BATTLE THEME]
Facing the Ogre War Lord, Zane fell back, letting his sword spirit, Lily, take the lead.
[SFX: MASSIVE SWORD SLASH]
Lily swung the Blood Sword in a vast arc toward the War Lord's head.
[SFX: CLAPPING HANDS]
The War Lord didn't dodge. He slapped both enormous crimson hands together and caught the blade between his palms. Then he twisted, trying to wrench it from Lily's grip and send her flying.
But Lily's sword dance was unlike anything the War Lord had encountered. Even as she was thrown back, she hooked her hand at an impossible angle, wrenched the Blood Sword back, and came at him again from a completely different direction.
[SFX: CLASH OF METAL]
It wasn't enough. The War Lord's fist crashed into the blade a second time, shattering the sword aura and leaving Lily completely exposed.
Zane's jaw tightened. One more hit and she'd be done.
He was about to call her back when—
[SFX: ARROW WHISTLING]
A bone arrow screamed through the air, trailing a long ribbon of milky-white light. Runes flickered across its surface. It hit like a thunderbolt aimed straight at the War Lord.
This was no warning shot.
[SFX: ARROW CRUSHED]
The War Lord laughed and snatched it from the air. His fingers closed around it.
[SFX: BONE CRUMBLING]
It crumbled to dust in his fist.
"Hah!" he sneered. "Since when do the proud Demon Night make alliances with cowardly humans?"
Zane's brow furrowed. Lily was from the Demon Night. When he'd discovered her on Killing Sword Mountain, the Cobalt Dragon had told him they were a formidable race. But here? On this continent? He hadn't expected that.
[SFX: SOFT FOOTSTEPS]
Two figures emerged from the treeline. Phoebe walked at the front, her steps light and rhythmic, carrying a quiet authority.
"This human is special," she said simply. "He has earned the protection of the Demon Night."
The War Lord looked at her and laughed. "Two Demon Night War Generals think they can stop me? Keep dreaming."
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
[MUSIC: HEROIC BRASS]
Then more than twenty figures burst from the nearby forest, weapons drawn, shooting into the sky toward Zane.
"Ha!" a big, booming voice rang out. "These are fellow humans! This has nothing to do with your Demon Night or your Ogres, does it?"
It was the bearded general.
The other humans fanned out around Zane, weapons raised.
Zane's eyes lit up. These people were different. The hundreds he'd been leading for days were ordinary — farmers, herders, people who'd never held a weapon. But these were cultivators. Real ones.
If there were human cultivators on this continent, there was a human faction. And if there was a human faction, there was infrastructure, knowledge, a foothold.
That was the best news he'd had since arriving.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BASS]
Still, the numbers weren't reassuring. Twenty-odd human War Generals. Two Demon Night War Generals. One Ogre War Lord. Zane ran the math quickly and didn't love the answer.
The Ogre War Lord barely gave the twenty-odd human War Generals a second glance. "Hiding like turtles this entire time? You finally dare to show your faces?" he said, eyes sliding toward the two Demon Night.
"Well, we're here now, aren't we?" the silver-haired man replied coolly.
[AMBIENT: TENSE FOREST]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The clearing had become a three-way standoff, but the Ogres clearly held the most power. While the Demon Night had no interest in expanding their territory, even the Ogres didn't dare provoke them. That left the humans as the weakest link, forced to hide inside their fort.
Zane, caught in the middle of the tense standoff, turned to the civilians. "All of you, go with them. Now."
The refugees didn't understand what was happening, but they obeyed. Once he gave the order, the crowd of hundreds began shuffling slowly toward the human warriors.
God, they're so slow, Zane thought, frustrated. If they hadn't held me up for days, I'd be a thousand miles from here by now.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BASS]
The Ogre War Lord’s eyes were locked on Zane. To him, the weak, ordinary people were nothing more than a herd of cattle.
All that mattered was keeping Zane right where he was.
