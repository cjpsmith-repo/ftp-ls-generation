Ep 636 - A Dangerous Place
Word count: 1092
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD]
"You know what's in that cave, don't you?"
Lavender's question hung in the air like a blade. Every War Lord in the hall had gone still, eyes moving between the two women, waiting.
Yolande blinked, all wide-eyed innocence. She wasn't about to lie. "Yeah, I know," she said, her voice small but certain.
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]
The War Lords erupted. They swarmed her with questions, their composure totally gone.
"What? You know about the Dark Nether Cave?"
"What’s in the core region?"
"How was it even formed?"
Yolande just turned her head away, stubborn. "I'm not telling."
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Her defiance hung in the air, leaving the high-ranking War Lords speechless. You could never guess what that girl was going to do next. She’d been a fixture on the tenth floor of the Tower of Sin since back when they were just generals. It was like she’d just appeared there one day, and she’d never left. No one knew where she came from, and because of that, no one dared to cross her.
"Lavender, what is going on?" Zane finally asked, his voice tight with frustration. "Why won't you let her come with me?"
Lavender’s eyes flickered. "I don't know how the Dark Nether Cave was created," she sent the thought directly into his mind, "but my guess is that it’s a battlefield left behind by warring world lords."
Zane flinched. World lords? "What did you just say? Who are they?"
Lavender explained. "The most powerful warriors from different worlds. Countless masters died here. If I'm right, this entire Cursed Land is a domain created by the Cursed World Lord, a being who once roamed the universe."
The Cobalt Dragon, who had been silent until now, finally spoke, his deep voice rumbling. She's right. The Cursed Land is almost certainly the Cursed Domain.
Zane’s brow furrowed. "And you didn't think to mention this earlier?" It was clear now that both of them had known all along.
"You didn't ask," they said in perfect, deadpan unison.
The response left Zane completely speechless.
The Cobalt Dragon continued in that same flat tone. The Cursed World Lord was a legend, even in the upper worlds. His Great Curse Technique could tear hundreds of great worlds apart and brand them with his power.
"Then why is his Cursed Domain useless against anyone below a War Sage?" Zane couldn't help but ask.
"Because you're too weak," Lavender replied coldly. "You don't even register as part of the Cursed World. That's why this place only affects warriors at the level of the top War Lords and above."
Her analysis was brutal, but it clicked. It was like two armies building fortifications to keep out enemy soldiers, not ants. To beings like world lords, anyone below the rank of War Sage was an insect, not even worth noticing.
"But what about Yolande?" Zane pressed. "If she knows the cave, having her with us improves our odds. Why are you so against it?"
Lavender's gaze stayed fixed on Yolande. "This woman has a million clones," she said privately. "I don't know what she's after, but my guess is she wants to ride your luck into the cave and take a share of whatever you find."
Before Zane could respond, the Cobalt Dragon spoke again. "Lavender is not wrong to be cautious. But you should know what you're dealing with. Yolande is the Sector Lord of the Infinite Realm — your human race's own world lord. She used the Clones Technique to split her soul into a million parts and sent them into different realms to be reborn. That is how she seeks to break through to a higher level."
"She's the Sector Lord of the human race?" Zane repeated slowly.
"Don't let that reassure you," Lavender cut back in. "I've encountered her real body before. She is deeply calculating. Whatever she wants from this cave, she has been waiting a long time for it. She intends to use you."
Zane was quiet for a moment. Then he smiled. "Even so — we can use each other."
"I agree," the Cobalt Dragon said. "The Dark Nether Cave is too dangerous for you to face alone. Refusing her help would be foolish."
Lavender said nothing more. She pressed her lips together, expression tight, and went silent.
Across the hall, Yolande had been waiting with the patience of someone who had all the time in the world. She watched Zane with calm, unreadable eyes — clearly aware that a private exchange had been happening, equally clearly unbothered by it.
"Well?" she asked simply. "Can I come?"
"Of course," Zane said.
Yolande gave a small nod and said nothing else.
[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE ECHOES]
[MUSIC: TENSE GATHERING]
The entry order was already settled. The Demon Night held the most seats and went first — a rule originally set by the Ogres, back when they had dominated the Tower. The hall thinned as the Demon Night cultivators climbed the spiral staircase at the far end and filed through the small door at the top. When most of them had passed through, it was the humans' turn.
Fabian and Jarrod led the way. Zane and Yolande followed behind.
[AMBIENT: PULSING RED LIGHT HUM]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
The cave was massive, but not completely dark. An eerie, deep red light pulsed through the space.
Zane instinctively shielded his arm, and the prickling sensation on his skin stopped. The second he moved his hand away, it returned, this time sharpening into a distinct, stabbing pain. "Is that the Dark Nether Death Light?"
"It is," Fabian confirmed. "Right now, the light is weak, so it only feels like needles. But when it flares, the pain is unbearable. No one can withstand it."
"We need to get down there, and fast," Jarrod cut in, his voice urgent. "Flying around in the Dark Nether Cave is just asking to get eaten by some demon."
The Tower of Sin was like a colossal blade plunged into the earth. They had just stepped off the top of it, and now they were heading straight down into the maw of the cave below.
Jarrod had a point. This was no place for a casual chat.
[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSE]
There was a story about a group of Ogre War Lords who stepped out of the Tower only to be ambushed by a massive fiend that had been lying in wait. It swallowed ten of them in a single gulp. That thing was known for guarding the area right around the Tower. The real threat here wasn't the Death Light; it was the monsters hiding in the dark.
[SFX: DISTANT CREATURE GROWL]
Even here, on the very edge of the outer layer, it was already ridiculously dangerous.
