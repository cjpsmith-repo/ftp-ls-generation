Ep 403 - Big Day for Skyreach Sect

Word count: 1026

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

[MUSIC: FAST-PACED ADVENTURE]

While Zane had taken his time claiming the Blood Fiend Emperor's treasures, the other group had barreled toward the final stretch of the Cloud Road.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS RUNNING]

Frank and his group of four burst through the fifth stage. Before them stretched a straight, clear shot to the finish line. The realization struck them all at once—the Death Road had been a massive shortcut, and now first place lay within their grasp.

For Frank, however, the sting of missing out on the blood pool remained fresh. He could still picture Zane disappearing into that crimson pool, absorbing power that should have been his. The thought gnawed at him.

Though he was usually easygoing, Lewis had a sharp mind. He was the first to react, exploding into a dead sprint toward the finish line. A split second later, the others caught on and gave chase.

Just like that, it became a raw footrace—a test of pure speed and endurance.

Lewis was a powerhouse. His explosive burst of speed left even Frank trailing in the dust. But his stamina was a known weakness, a fact everyone in the group understood. They still had a long way to go, and for the first half of the sprint, he easily maintained his lead. His powerful legs devoured the distance, each stride carrying him farther ahead.

But by the halfway point, Frank's superior endurance began to pay off. He closed the distance steadily, his breathing controlled, his pace unwavering. Victory was so close he could taste it.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

[SFX: AGGRESSIVE MOVEMENT]

Then, the unexpected happened. As Frank pulled alongside him, Lewis lashed out with a sharp elbow, his friendly demeanor vanishing as if he'd become a different person entirely.

Rage flashed in Frank's eyes. The memory of his last failure—the humiliation of losing to Zane—soured in his gut like spoiled wine. He was not losing first place again, especially not to someone he'd considered beneath his notice. The audacity of Lewis trying to stop him ignited something primal in his chest. You think I'm some declawed kitten? Frank's thoughts burned with indignation. I'm a sleeping tiger, and you've just woken me up.

Fueled by a fresh surge of adrenaline that set his blood singing, he fought back with vicious determination.

[SFX: FIST FIGHT]

[SFX: SPLASHING WATER]

It wasn't a true fight—more of a desperate, vicious scrap meant to trip the other up, to gain any advantage possible. Fists flew in wild arcs. Feet tangled. Water sprayed in all directions as they grappled and shoved, neither willing to yield an inch. But while they were locked in their bitter squabble, consumed by their mutual determination to win, the other three runners caught up with steady, measured strides.

The distraction had cost them both dearly.

[SFX: WATER WHIRLPOOL]

Seizing the opening, Finn was the first to dive into the whirlpool at the finish line. The water swallowed him whole, pulling him down into its churning depths. He was certain he'd won.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

But when he emerged on the other side, gasping and triumphant, he saw Zane already sitting calmly beside Toby and the other masters, as composed as if he'd been waiting there for hours.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

Finn's jaw went slack. Zane was just... there. Sitting calmly beside the masters as if he'd been waiting for hours, not plummeting into an abyss moments ago. The impossibility of it made Finn's head spin.

No way, Finn thought, his chest still heaving from exertion. His wet robes clung to his skin as water dripped from his hair. There's no explanation for this! I saw him fall into that abyss with my own eyes—watched that disciple drag him down into the darkness. 

He was certain Zane was dead. The Drake, the endless void below... no one could survive that. Yet here sat Zane, completely dry, without a scratch on him. Not even winded.

Even if he somehow survived the fall... Finn's thoughts trailed off. There was simply no logical path from that abyss to this finish line. Not in the time it had taken Finn to complete the final stage.
Frank burst through the vortex a moment later. "Second," he said automatically. Finn shook his head. "Third." Frank followed his gaze — and saw Zane.

[SFX: WATER SPLASHES]

Patrick emerged from the whirlpool next, water cascading off his monastic robes. His disciplined training favored precision over raw speed, making him only a hair faster than Lois, who surfaced moments later, sputtering and gasping.

Both their faces transformed the instant they spotted Zane—shock giving way to the same stunned disbelief that still gripped Finn. Patrick's usual serene composure cracked completely. His eyes widened, and his mouth opened as if to speak, but no words came.

Lois simply froze, staring at Zane like he'd witnessed something that defied the very laws of cultivation itself.

[SFX: LIGHT CHUCKLE]

Toby chuckled. "Why are you all staring at him like that? I know it's impressive that he beat all of you, but you don't have to look like you've seen a ghost!"

When Zane saw Finn and Patrick, he stood up, walked over, and clapped them both on the shoulder with a friendly smile.

The three disciples from the Skyreach Sect had officially passed the Cloud Road, securing first, second, and fourth place. Sir Kenneth was practically vibrating with excitement. As a Second-Tier Sect, their sect had only received three spots in the competition, and all three had placed in the top five. To him, this was better than digging up a mountain of treasure.

[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Suddenly, Frank spun toward Zane. His voice was sharp and loud, deliberately carrying across the clearing. "Zane, I saw you fall into the abyss. How did that Drake not eat you?"

He didn't wait for an answer, pressing on. "Don't Drakes eat people? Did you ride it to the finish line? And where's that mysterious girl from Cloud Hall? Is she with you?"

The accusations, clearly meant for everyone to hear, were his real goal. He knew Zane would never answer him in private, so he put him on the spot in front of Toby and the other masters.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

Just as he'd intended, every master turned to look at Zane.

Zane’s expression tightened. This guy, he thought, is just determined to make my life difficult.
