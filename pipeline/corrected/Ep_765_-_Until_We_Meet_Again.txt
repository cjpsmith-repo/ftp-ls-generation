Ep 765 - Until We Meet Again

Word count: 1077

[AMBIENT: FOREST BIRDS]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

 Zane never held back when it came to the people he loved, and that included embracing the chaotic destiny he shared with Elara. 

To have any real control over it, though, he had to be powerful enough to seize it, and he was nowhere near strong enough yet. So, he could only watch helplessly as Elara was taken from him, completely unable to change their fate.

He explained everything to her, his voice low as she listened in silence. 

When he got to the parts about the dangers he’d faced, her eyes went wide, and her hands flew to her mouth, her expression a mask of pure worry. It was hard for her to stomach the idea that her brother had walked through fire for the last few years, all for her sake.

When Zane finished, Elara let out a long, shuddering sigh. It was starting to click into place—their shared destiny, this difficult path they were meant to walk together. 

But she was luckier than him. All she had to do was be patient and wait. Zane, on the other hand, was out there risking his life, fighting fate head-on. A single misstep could get him killed.

“I’m sorry,” she said, her eyes red-rimmed. “I get it now.” She knew she never should have blamed him.

Zane just managed a small smile. “I’m the one who should be sorry. I feel useless. If I were stronger, I wouldn’t have let anyone take you away. Not for anything. And definitely not him.”

At that, Elara’s own smile returned. “I believe in you. You’ll get stronger. I know it.” 

The look in her eyes was all the answer he needed—pure, unwavering trust.

Their reunion was short, but they cherished every second, taking a quiet walk through the forest before reluctantly heading back toward the Ethereal Spirit Hall.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL HALL]

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

 As they entered, Zane’s gaze locked onto the young man with the feathered crown. “I’ll let you take Elara,” he said, his voice cold.

A faint smirk touched the young man’s lips. “It’s not like you have a choice.”

A dangerous light sparked in Zane’s eyes. He stared the man down. “You’re right. I’m not strong enough to stop you now. But one day, I will be. And if anything happens to Elara before then, you will pay.” 

Zane held back the full force of his threat. He didn’t sense true malice from the man, just a cold purpose, so he chose a warning over a fight he couldn’t win.

The young man's smile didn't waver. "I look forward to the day you can actually back that up."

Zane held his gaze for a moment, then looked away. There was nothing more to say — the gap between them was too vast to measure. But there was one more thing he needed to try. "I have one more request." 

The young man waited.

Zane told him about Tia — the Life and Death Wind, the Law of Causality, her fading life force.

The young man listened, then smiled. "I could resolve that level of karma easily." He paused. "But I won't. Because that is also your fate. I've already broken enough rules today. You'll have to find your own way. Now — I'm taking Elara."

Zane lowered his head. 

That is also your fate. 

He turned the words over, still processing them, when he felt a pair of small, cold hands wrap around his neck. Elara kissed him on the cheek.

Understanding their shared destiny seemed to have settled something in her. She didn't want to leave him with a burden, so she gave him her brightest smile instead. "Brother. You can do it."

Zane saw straight through it. She was carrying this just as he was, choosing to be strong for his sake. He smiled and nodded. If that was what she needed from him, he would give it to her.

Just then, Owen stepped forward. "Sir," he said, addressing the young man directly. "Faye's six senses are still sealed. Could you release her as well?"

The young man glanced at Faye, then waved a hand. Unlike Zane's fate, this didn't concern him. The seal dissolved instantly.

Faye's eyes opened. Owen crossed the hall in three strides, his face flooded with relief. He had nearly died at the Martial Tournament. He had waited months for this moment.

"Work hard, little guy," the young man said, turning back to Zane one last time. "Don't let me down."

[SFX: SOFT WHOOSH]

Then he took Elara's hand, and they were gone. No spatial tunnel. No tearing of space. Not even a ripple of the Law of Space. One moment, they were there; the next, the hall was empty where they had stood.

Zane remained where he was for a long time.

Not far away, Owen was speaking quietly with Faye, his expression brighter than Zane had ever seen it. He had god-grade talent and a future ahead of him in the Guild. Now that the Corrupted Divine Kingdom's threat was finished, nothing was standing in his way.

The journey back was quiet. Zane said little. Losing Elara again, even knowing it was inevitable, sat heavily on him.

[AMBIENT: GUILD INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE THEME]

 Back at the World Merchants’ Guild, Kieran wasted no time pulling Zane aside. Judging by Zane’s confident tone earlier, Kieran figured he must have some kind of plan, but he couldn’t imagine how Zane intended to deal with Soul Sea Realm warriors from the York Divine Kingdom.

 Sensing the anxiety coming from Kieran and the other leaders, Zane offered a reassuring smile. “I have a way to handle this. The World Merchants’ Guild has connections all over The Heartlands, right? I need you to help me find someone extraordinary—someone who can cure Tia.”

 The calm certainty on Zane's face baffled the three guild leaders. His battle with Maddox had pushed him to his limit—how could he face far stronger enemies with such confidence?

Had he concealed his true strength? No. If he possessed such power, he would never have allowed Maddox to harm Tia with the Life and Death Wind.

But what else could explain his absolute confidence? No matter how they turned it over in their minds, they couldn’t find an answer. Still, Zane had never let them down before. They had to wonder if, against all odds, he really did have a solution.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED ORCHESTRAL]

 Finally, Kieran nodded. He promised the World Merchants’ Guild would use every connection it had to find the person Zane was looking for.
