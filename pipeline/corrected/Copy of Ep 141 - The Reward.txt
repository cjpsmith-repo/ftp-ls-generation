[AMBIENT: TENSE MEETING CHAMBER]
 Lennie wasn’t too worried about the military office coming after Zane. He was pretty sure nothing would stick. Still, he wasn't about to stand by and let Yolande drag the kid there herself.
"Zane might be a hero to everyone else, but we can't just ignore what he did," Yolande Shaw said, her voice sharp and unyielding. "He went after Hugh. He killed a military counselor. He has to stand trial."
"Yolande, just let it go," Axel urged, stepping up beside Lennie.
"Yes, he killed a counselor, but I looked into it," Axel continued. "That guy from House Sterling drew first blood—he tried to kill Zane, and Zane just got the upper hand. On top of that, he fought with us against Aldrich. So let me be clear: even if Zane had killed an Imperial officer without cause, I'd still pardon him!"
[MUSIC: AUTHORITATIVE THEME]
 Axel's words resonated through the chamber, weighted with unmistakable authority. His stance and tone conveyed what everyone already knew—as Supreme Commander of the Imperial Army, his judgment would prevail in this matter.
"I stand with Axel," Hugh interjected, stepping forward and positioning himself beside Yolande. His eyes, previously hard with suspicion, now reflected understanding. "And I've no intention of pursuing charges against the boy. The truth has become painfully clear to me—Josef's death falls squarely on my shoulders. I failed to recognize what he had become."
Yolande remained silent, her slender fingers curling into tight fists at her sides. The muscles in her jaw worked visibly as she wrestled with her strict adherence to military protocol. Her piercing gaze swept over Zane, then Axel, before finally settling on Hugh. 
 After what seemed an eternity of tense silence, she exhaled sharply and offered a reluctant nod. "Very well," she conceded, her voice crisp but resigned. "The matter is closed."
 A collective sigh of relief went through Axel, Lennie, and Hugh. Even though they'd been ready to fight her on it, they knew how stubborn Yolande could be. They didn't call her the 'Killer Maiden' for nothing—she was known for her ruthless single-mindedness. Once she set her sights on something, almost nothing could change her mind.
[SFX: MAGICAL DISSOLVING SOUND]
 Then, Yolande raised her right hand. With a delicate snap of her fingers, the transparent shell encasing Zane began to shimmer and soften, dissolving back into pure Vitality before vanishing completely.
"Kid, you did good," Lennie said, stepping forward to clap Zane on the shoulder. "Damn good. You should be proud—you saved all our asses." In the back of his mind, he couldn't help but picture Zane and Ingrid together. They'd make a hell of a couple.
Zane shifted uncomfortably under the weight of their admiration, his gaze dropping to the floor. "It was merely a fluke," he mumbled, raising a hand to dismiss their praise. "Truthfully, had Yolande not brought me into the confrontation with Aldrich, I would never have had such an opportunity."
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
 An unspoken question lingered in the chamber, reflected in every pair of eyes fixed upon him: how had he accomplished the impossible by absorbing the Hellfire?
[MUSIC: OMINOUS MYSTICAL]
 Throughout the Empire, Hellfire was renowned as a mystical, devastatingly destructive force—a perilous power that strengthened demons while proving fatal to humans. Its flickering crimson flames consumed flesh and bone with merciless efficiency, leaving nothing but ash in their wake.
Any ordinary mortal thrust into Hellfire's embrace would vanish in an instant, their existence erased in a flash of agony. Even masters who had ascended to the Soul Realm, formidable warriors like Yolande herself, could merely endure its touch, not harness it. During the battle, even her extraordinary senses and vision had been severely diminished by mere proximity to the infernal flames. Yet Zane stood before them unscathed—an inexplicable anomaly that defied everything they understood about the natural order.
"Let me tell you an old story," Axel said, his tone earnest, clearly wanting Zane to accept the praise. "It's about a respected man from the old days named Dawson. Back when he was poor, he saw a beggar pull a drowning person from a river. Dawson had no money for a reward, so he simply knelt and bowed to the man. Today, you helped the Imperial Army win a war. You saved thousands of lives. You've more than earned our respect."
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT CEREMONIAL]
 Axel paused, then raised his voice for all to hear. "Since you're not an enlisted soldier, we can't give you an official military honor. But, on behalf of the entire Imperial Army, I am awarding you The Trinity Stones. You will choose them yourself from our Vault!"
"The Trinity Stones!"
"He gets to choose them himself!"
[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD]
A buzz of shock and excitement went through the crowd, their eyes wide with disbelief. The Imperial Army's Vault housed a collection of treasures built up over generations, with only the most priceless artifacts kept in the treasury at Silverspire. Earning a reward from it was a near-impossible feat, but to be allowed to *choose* your own… it was unheard of. The other officers couldn't hide the envy on their faces.
[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND STONE PASSAGE]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DESCENT]
 After the crowd dispersed, Axel personally escorted Zane to the Empire's treasury, buried deep beneath the city. They descended through winding stone staircases and traversed a long, narrow corridor that seemed to stretch endlessly into darkness. At last, they reached an imposing door carved from ancient granite, adorned with runes that pulsed with faint blue energy.
[SFX: ANCIENT STONE DOOR OPENING]
 Axel pressed his palm against the center sigil. The massive stone barrier groaned open, revealing absolute darkness beyond. With deliberate ceremony, he lit a single tallow candle.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL TREASURY]
[MUSIC: AWESTRUCK WONDER]
 The flame's light danced across the chamber, igniting a breathtaking spectacle. The cavernous vault erupted in blinding golden radiance, as if they stood at the heart of a captured sun. Mountains of gleaming treasures stretched into shadowy recesses—weapons forged by forgotten masters, armor that had turned aside legendary blades, jewels the size of fists that pulsed with inner fire. Ancient scrolls and artifacts of impossible design lay scattered among heaps of precious metals.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY SHIFT]
 But as Zane's eyes adjusted to the overwhelming brilliance, he noticed something peculiar in the chamber's furthest corner—a pedestal bathed in shadow despite the surrounding light, bearing three distinct objects that seemed to absorb the very radiance around them.
