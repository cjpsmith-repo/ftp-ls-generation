Ep 856 - Purple Stone-Eater Bugs

Word count: 1048

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

From the forty-fifth floor up, the leaves of the mining tree were unbelievably hard. Not even a master of the Soul Sea Realm could make a dent in them, and it was said that even stronger warriors would fail.

After conquering the Sacred Feather Sea, the union of seven divine kingdoms had uncovered a trove of ancient knowledge from the secret lands. A few of the tomes revealed that the leaves on the highest floors could only be broken by the Black Star Pickax, a divine-class weapon hidden inside a special golden leaf. 

The real challenge, however, was actually getting the pickax out of the leaf—a feat no one had ever managed. Without the weapon, breaking the upper leaves and getting to the Soul Cores inside was impossible.

[SFX: INSECT CHITTERING]

But then they found the bugs, and their hope was renewed. The creatures didn’t have a name at first, but after the divine kingdoms observed them eating ores to survive, they began calling them Stone-Eater Bugs. The same leaves that had stopped the strongest masters were no match for them. So, the warriors gave up on the Black Star Pickax and began studying the bugs instead. Their work eventually paid off, and with the Soul Cores they gathered, they were able to enter the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land.

The rumor that the pickax was impossible to retrieve spread like wildfire, and soon, everyone in the union of seven divine kingdoms had given up trying.

Zane, of course, had no idea about any of this. He’d never even heard of the Black Star Pickax, let alone how notoriously difficult it was to claim. He’d actually thought he could just pull it right out.

“Well, that explains why I couldn’t get it,” Zane sighed, feeling a little foolish for trying so hard to do something everyone else knew was impossible.

He decided to forget about it and move on.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

[MUSIC: DETERMINED DRUMS]

Getting to his feet, he headed back through the narrow doorway of the pyramid to search for the Ancient Heartstones. His own pickax was broken, but his fists were as tough as a Relic-Class weapon. He figured he just had to put a little more muscle into it to break the leaves.

He tried for two hours straight and got nowhere.

To find the Ancient Heartstones, Zane stopped trying to mine the other ores. The ones on the thirty-fifth floor and up were valuable, but he didn’t have the time or energy to waste. He kept ducking in and out of the pyramid, scouring the tree for any sign of his prize. 

From the thirty-fifth floor all the way up to the fortieth, the leaves began to thin out. Zane felt like he went back and forth a thousand times, checking every single one, but he couldn’t find a single Ancient Heartstone.

[SFX: INSECT BUZZING]

To make things worse, the green Stone-Eater Bugs had gotten smarter. Every time Zane landed on a leaf, they snapped their wings open and flew off before he could get anywhere near them. He had no chance of catching one.

His expression darkened. At this rate, even reaching five hundred Ancient Heartstones was going to be a problem.

He made a decision. He climbed straight to the fortieth floor.

Randy was waiting near the entrance. The moment he saw where Zane was headed, he stepped forward. “Zane — above the fortieth floor, only Soul Sea Realm masters go in. If you enter, I’m afraid—”

Zane turned to Randy. “I’m just going in to look around. I’ll be careful.”

Even if he ran into purple Stone-Eater Bugs — ranked as level-eleven beasts, equivalent to Soul Sea Realm warriors — they couldn’t use domain power to cut him off from the world’s force. As long as that connection held, his force transfer ability held too. They couldn’t kill him.

He’d run into a few green Stone-Eater Bugs during his search, but now, stepping through the narrow door onto the fortieth floor, he found something else entirely.

The purple Stone-Eater Bugs were twice the size of the green ones, with two extra pairs of compound eyes on their heads. A cold, gloomy energy radiated from their bodies.

Six of them were on the adjacent leaf, heads down, feeding. Their huge purple abdomens were raised high as they worked their mouthparts through the ore.

Zane landed quietly and swept his eyes across his own leaf. The ores embedded here were as large as a human head, faintly pulsing with traces of various laws. He didn’t know their names or their exact value, but he knew they were extraordinary.

There were no Ancient Heartstones.

He’d expected that. But he’d had to check.

One of the purple bugs raised its head. Four black compound eyes turned slowly toward him. Its feelers swayed.

Then it shrieked.

[SFX: INSECT SHRIEK]

[MUSIC: DANGER RISING]

Zane hadn’t sensed any domain power coming from them, which was why their sudden attack caught him completely off guard. Without a second to think, he spun on his heel and jumped out of the leaf.

[SFX: HIGH-PITCHED INSECT SHRIEKS]

The moment Zane leaped clear, the lead bug—barely three feet away—shot its spindly legs out from under its belly, trying to snatch him from the air. If that thing caught him, the other five would swarm him in an instant. He’d be in serious trouble. He had to act fast.

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

[SFX: WHOOSH]

“You think you can catch me? Dream on!” Zane yelled, twisting his body in midair.

A cold light flashed in his hand as he drew his longsword and slashed at the purple bug.

[SFX: CLANG]

To his shock, his prized Relic-Class weapon did nothing. The purple bug didn’t even have a scratch on it—not a single mark. Its leg, no thicker than a man’s arm, had met his blade with a sharp metallic sound and a shower of sparks, completely unharmed.

How is their armor so tough? he thought, stunned. The recoil from his own strike sent him flying backward.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Pushed by the force of his attack and pulled by gravity, he was suddenly falling, and fast. As he fell, the purple bugs dove right after him in hot pursuit.

Seeing them coming for him, he desperately tried to fall faster.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

He frantically channeled his Vitality, unleashing the Forceful Energy in his body. Combined with the pull of gravity, it made him plummet at an incredible speed.
