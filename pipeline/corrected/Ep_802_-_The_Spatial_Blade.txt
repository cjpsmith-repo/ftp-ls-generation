Ep 802 - The Spatial Blade

Word count: 1153

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

Warriors live to fight—other warriors, fate, even the heavens. For Zane, competition was the crucible where martial arts were perfected.

He longed to test himself against the divine kingdoms' talents, but Tia waited back home. His mission: collect Ancient Heartstones without drawing attention. Catching the eye of a Holy Maiden or Soul Sea Realm master would invite disaster. He had to keep a low profile.

But it was looking like Lord Randy was about to ruin all that. It seemed like everywhere Zane went, someone was dragging him into the spotlight, and there was never a damn thing he could do about it. He just knew Randy was going to ask him to fight in his place.

It was no secret that Lord Randy and his brother, the Crown Prince, were locked in a bitter rivalry to prove themselves to their father, the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom. Their personal strength was well-known, but their real power was measured by their influence and the fighters they could attract. The Crown Prince had a proven heavy hitter in Edwin on his side. Lord Randy only had Zane. That was why Randy, despite his high status, always treated Zane as an equal and spoke to him plainly.

[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

In the arena, Edwin was making his mark — literally. Each slash of his saber left a faint score across Logan's spatial armor. The armor held, but the marks were accumulating, deepening with every pass. If Edwin kept this up long enough, the whole structure would eventually collapse.

“This saber is called Dragon's Tooth,” Edwin called out, his figure flickering like wind as he closed the distance again. “Forged from the tooth of a true dragon. That shell of yours will crack sooner or later. A warrior who does nothing but hide will always lose in the end.”

[SFX: RESONANT CLANG]

He slashed again. Another mark. The crowd leaned in.

Logan watched him with cold eyes. Then, without a word, he let the spatial armor dissolve.

[SFX: ENERGY HUM]

The air began to thrum, a low vibration that grew into a steady buzz. Light gathered and coalesced in Logan’s hand, forming a hazy, shimmering longsword. It wasn’t a physical blade but space itself, somehow compressed and sharpened into a weapon. The man had forged a sword from thin air, a testament to his mastery of the Spatial Law, which he could apparently use for both attack and defense.

[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD MURMUR]

“That’s Logan’s Spatial Blade! Oh man, this is gonna be good!” someone in the crowd yelled.

“It’s his ultimate move,” another added. “For Edwin to force it out of him… he’s really backed Logan into a corner.”

“Edwin did a hell of a job, but he’d better watch himself. That sword isn’t for show—it’s made to slice off limbs.”

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA CROWD]

The murmurs reached Edwin, and the grim reality of the Spatial Blade settled in. His eyes hardened with determination. He had come from nothing, fighting his entire life to become the top young warrior at the Crystal Martial House. He would keep fighting until his strength earned him the respect he deserved. Besides, the Crown Prince himself was counting on him. He had to win. One ultimate move wasn't about to break his resolve.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

“Did you hear them?” Logan asked, his voice cool and detached. “My Spatial Blade was made to take limbs. If you want, you can leave a hand behind. I’ll let you walk away.”

[SFX: WOOD SHATTERING]

A sharp crack echoed from the stairs. The Crown Prince had slammed his hand down on the Ironwood armrest of his wheelchair, shattering it into dust. He didn’t fall, but simply floated in the air where he sat. An attendant instantly reacted, sliding a new wheelchair beneath him.

The Crown Prince settled back into the seat with an annoyed click of his tongue. “Logan is getting far too arrogant,” he muttered.

The other rulers from the surrounding divine kingdoms simply smiled at the outburst. This was a matter for the younger generation, and there was no reason for them to get involved.

[AMBIENT: ROYAL PLATFORM MURMURS]

Seated among the other dignitaries on the high platform, the Holy Maiden chuckled. She turned to the fuming prince of the Harlen Divine Kingdom and teased, “Getting a little angry, are we?”

The Crown Prince just snorted, refusing to take the bait. He turned his gaze back to the battle raging in the sky.

Lord Randy, on the other hand, felt a wave of relief. Edwin was technically representing the Harlen Divine Kingdom, but he wasn’t one of Randy’s men. The warrior had been invited by the Crown Prince, which made this his brother’s problem.

[SFX: BLADE STRIKING ]

[MUSIC: SHARP PERCUSSIVE HIT]

Logan moved. Three slashes, almost too fast to follow. The Spatial Blade was nearly transparent — you could only track it by the way the light bent around it.

The first slash cracked Dragon's Tooth straight down the blade.

The second shattered it entirely.

The third came to rest against Edwin's shoulder — deliberate, controlled, close enough to sever the strap of his guard and draw a thin line of blood. One more inch and the arm would have come off at the joint. Weaponless, with that impossible blade settled at his shoulder, Edwin had no move left to make.

[SFX: CROWD GASP]

Zane's eyes narrowed. Three moves. Clean, efficient, devastating. Logan wasn't just strong — he was precise.

“Too powerful—”

“The Spatial Blade cuts through mid-grade Relics like they're nothing—”

“Who's going to challenge that?”

Edwin gritted his teeth and descended, still clutching the shattered halves of Dragon's Tooth. The saber had carried him through every battle of his career, and Logan had carved it apart in two strokes. He landed without a word. He had nothing to complain about. His skills simply weren't enough.

Logan turned to face the square, unhurried. “Who else?”

“Challenge!” the Crown Prince suddenly yelled, his anger making him forget the rules.

“Don’t be so impatient, Son, or people will laugh at you,” the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom said calmly from his throne.

War King York chuckled in agreement. “The emperor is right. Some things just can’t be rushed.”

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom shot a cold look at his rival. The union of the seven divine kingdoms was a delicate balance of alliance and competition, but there was no denying the York Divine Kingdom had grown arrogant over the years, and its emperor had become a first-rate show-off. While the wins and losses of a few junior warriors were ultimately meaningless, they provided the perfect excuse for them to take subtle digs at one another.

Scott, the ruler of the Star Field Divine Kingdom, turned to his son. “Horace, do you have anyone you’d recommend?” Their best fighter had already been defeated by Logan, leaving Scott with no other options. He could only hope his son had a plan.

Horace, the crown prince of the Star Field Divine Kingdom, nodded, his eyes scanning the crowd before landing on one person. 

“Willard! You’re up! Fight for me!”
