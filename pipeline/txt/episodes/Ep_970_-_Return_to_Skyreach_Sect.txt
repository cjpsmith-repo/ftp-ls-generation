Ep 970 - Return to Skyreach Sect
Word count: 1120
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
As Zane gazed south, an image flashed through his mind, sharp enough to make him flinch.
Isla Novak. The eldest princess of the Empire, the one who’d always looked out for him. He could still picture her, always dressed in green.
After leaving the Eastern Territories, he’d thrown himself completely into saving Elara. All this time, Isla had been waiting for him back home, but this was the first real chance he’d had to return.
He remembered them running wild through the palace halls, her carefree smile lighting up her face as they stirred up trouble. She’d never been demanding, acting more like a big sister than a princess, gently teaching him about the world. She always tried to do the right thing and never asked for anything in return.
[MUSIC: SOMBER CELLO]
The memory brought a sharp pang of guilt.
Zane’s name had become legendary in Cloud Hall. He was a force to be reckoned with in the Heartlands, and stories of his power had surely reached the Eastern Territories. That alone should have been enough to keep Isla safe.
Sir Kenneth, the leader of the Skyreach Sect, was no fool. He wouldn’t dare let a single hair on her head be harmed.
Zane wasn’t worried about her safety. He was worried about his promise. He’d sworn to return, but four years had passed. To most, four years wasn’t a long time. They just saw a hero returning home after a short, glorious campaign. But Zane knew better. He’d had chances to go back, but he’d chosen to pour everything into his training. For Elara.
It felt like he’d just… left Isla behind. Not a single word in all that time.
[AMBIENT: WIND OVER OPEN PLAINS]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
There were two ways to get back to the Eastern Territories. One was the teleportation array that led into the ruins of a Sixth-Tier Sect. The other was the long road straight across the Heartlands.
In the past, Zane probably would have taken the shortcut.
[MUSIC: PEACEFUL JOURNEY THEME]
But he wasn’t in a hurry this time. For the first time in a long while, it felt like he could finally breathe. He traveled east with Tia and Cora, taking the scenic route across the Heartlands before they’d cross into the Eastern Territories.
[AMBIENT: FOOTSTEPS ON DIRT ROAD]
“I’ve never been to the Eastern Territories,” Tia said, a genuine smile on her face.
Both women seemed happy to be heading that way. Zane wasn’t sure if their little feud was really over, but at least they weren’t at each other’s throats anymore.
Cora’s eyes lit up. “Me neither! I can’t wait to see where you grew up, Zane!”
[AMBIENT: CITY GATES — CROWDS]
[MUSIC: BUSTLING THEME]
A few days later, the three of them stood before the gates of the Empire’s capital. The moment they stepped into view, heads turned. Whispers rippled through the crowd — two women who looked like they’d stepped out of a painting, and a young man whose cultivation no one could read. People nudged each other and murmured, but no one dared approach.
Tia took one look at the city and said nothing. She oversaw Cloudhaven, a city home to powerful families and major sects — this capital didn’t come close. As for Cora, she’d grown up in Ethereal City, famous across the Heartlands for its resources. She glanced around once and lost interest just as quickly.
Zane’s feelings were different. He said nothing either, but as he walked the familiar streets, a quiet ache settled in his chest. Four years. Everything looked the same, and nothing felt the same.
The Eastern Territories were just an extension of the Heartlands.
Still, someone had tried to report Zane’s incredible performance to the top leaders, but a few of the Guardians had blocked him.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
During the All Peaks Competition, Sir Kenneth had punished those Guardians—even killed one of them. After that, the remaining Guardians started coming after Steward Carter to get back at him for the humiliation. It wasn’t Carter’s fault that Sir Kenneth had been angry, but they found an excuse to demote him to a patrolling steward anyway.
[AMBIENT: SKYREACH SECT ENTRANCE]
[MUSIC: FAMILIAR STRINGS]
At the entrance to the Skyreach Sect, a steward on a Flying Chariot spotted the three approaching figures and descended to meet them. He looked them over — couldn’t read the tall man’s cultivation at all, nor the dark-haired woman beside him. The third, at least, was clearly at the Inner-Core Realm.
He cupped his fist. “Excuse me — what brings you to the Skyreach Sect?”
The man smiled. “Steward Carter. Don’t you remember me?”
Carter blinked. There was something familiar about this face — the line of the jaw, the set of the eyes. He’d seen this man before, somewhere. But four years had passed, and the boy he was trying to place had been a teenager. The person in front of him was someone else entirely — taller, harder, carrying a presence that made the air feel different.
Then it clicked.
His eyes went wide.
Zane gave a small, quiet nod and pressed a finger to his lips.
Carter swallowed hard and kept his voice even. “Of course. Welcome back, Zane.”
“Why were you demoted?” Zane asked, his curiosity piqued.
Steward Carter’s face lit up. Zane must be in a powerful position now to even ask. He couldn’t expect a handout, but since Zane himself was asking, maybe… just maybe… he could put in a good word for him. Moving up was nearly impossible for someone like Carter, but for a man like Zane, it might be as simple as saying a word.
He quickly laid out everything that had happened.
Zane nodded, but before he could say anything, Tia frowned. “I’ve never set foot in the Skyreach Sect, but for a Tier-Two Sect, this is just pathetic. Petty politics like this? I have to wonder how Kenneth runs this place at all. When I see him, I’m going to have to take him to task over this mess.”
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Steward Carter’s jaw dropped. Kenneth? She was talking about the leader of the Skyreach Sect like he was some underling. And she was going to take him to task? Who in the hell was this woman? He didn’t dare ask, but his mind was racing.
Seeing the look on Carter’s face, Zane gave a small smile. “Alright, then. Since the boss is on the case, I’d say your problem is as good as solved.”
“The… boss?” Carter finally found his voice, gesturing tentatively toward Tia. “This lady… she is…?”
Zane gave a casual shrug. “She’s the leader of Cloud Hall.”
