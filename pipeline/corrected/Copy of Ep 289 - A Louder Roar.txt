Ep 289 - A Louder Roar
Word Count: 1056
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
Jacob stood frozen in disbelief. After such a devastating blow, Zane was rising to his feet, trying to form words despite his injuries. The sight defied all logic.
Even with his own powerful skills, Jacob never imagined Zane could withstand a direct assault on his soul. This resilience suggested the young fighter was even stronger than he realized—strong enough to resist the overwhelming presence of a Drake’s ancient power. A chill ran down Jacob’s spine as he reassessed his opponent.
The Drake Bone Spear clutched in his hand was far more than just a weapon. Meticulously carved from the third vertebra of a Drake’s spinal column—the precise section that housed its most concentrated essence and lifeblood. The spear embodied primal might.
Jacob had spared no expense, commissioning the legendary weaponsmith Brady to inscribe it with thirteen Runic Glyphs. Six of these intricate markings awakened the dragon bone’s latent power, while the other seven amplified the spear’s destructive force.
Each painstakingly crafted glyph had cost him a fortune—one hundred thousand crystals apiece. The total investment reached a staggering one-point-three million, a sum that had depleted even his considerable resources.
That’s precisely why, as he watched Zane struggling to stand, Jacob felt more astounded than even the gasping spectators surrounding the arena. He alone understood the true, terrifying magnitude of the force he had just unleashed—power that should have completely obliterated his opponent.
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR ECHO]
"Well, this is unexpected," Jacob said, a flicker of annoyance crossing his face. He’d poured everything into that attack, fully expecting to end the fight in one blow. He'd clearly underestimated Zane. "You took my hit, but your soul is shattered. You were tough enough for three dragon roars, I’ll give you that. But my next attack will either kill you or leave you a drooling, empty shell. Admit defeat now, and I'll let you walk out of here."
Jacob wasn’t the merciful type, but seeing Zane withstand the roars had earned the guy a sliver of respect—just enough to offer him the chance to throw in the towel.
[SFX: BLOOD WIPE]
Zane wiped a smear of blood from his lips with the back of his hand. "Am I supposed to be grateful for your generosity? For letting me walk away a loser?"
Jacob just shook his head, his expression a mixture of pity and impatience. "Precisely. You’ve exhausted all your reserves. There's no way you can keep this up. Continuing this charade is simply delaying the inevitable."
A faint smile flickered across Zane’s pale face, his eyes gleaming with defiance despite his battered state. "I’ll give you this much—you have a right to be proud. The vast majority of people in the Skyreach Sect will never touch your level of power," he said, his voice growing stronger with each word. "But no matter how strong you become, there’s always someone stronger. And I don’t believe that’s you." He straightened his posture, ignoring the searing pain coursing through his body. "You’re in no position to tell me to surrender."
Jacob’s eyebrow arched sharply, genuine confusion replacing his previous confidence. What game was this guy playing? Was he truly delusional, or did he possess some hidden technique not yet revealed, an ace up his sleeve?
The calculated look in Zane’s eyes suggested intelligence, not desperation. Jacob felt a flicker of unease ripple through his certainty. What powerful secret could this mysterious fighter still be concealing?
[SFX: SWIRLING ENERGY]
While Jacob tried to tell if Zane was bluffing, the Corrupt Vitality swirling behind his opponent began to transform, twisting and coalescing into demonic phantoms that writhed with ancient evil.
Surrender was not an option for Zane. To win the All Peaks Competition, to ascend as the top disciple of the Skyreach Sect—these were necessities etched into his very soul. A single setback, no matter how devastating, could never shatter the foundation of his indomitable will. The taste of copper filled his mouth as he smiled through bloodied lips, drawing upon reserves of strength that transcended mere physical limitations.
[MUSIC: SOMBER TONE]
[AMBIENT: CROWD CHATTER]
In the stands, disciples struggled to comprehend the spectacle unfolding before them.
"I guess that’s it for him," murmured one, shaking his head solemnly.
"It’s worse than that," added another, leaning forward with morbid fascination. "He might be braindead."
"What a damn shame," lamented a third, his voice tinged with genuine disappointment. "Misty Peak finally discovers a prodigy for the All Peaks, and this is how he meets his fate!"
As these grim assessments filtered through the crowd, Master Warren’s face drained of color, his features contorted with mounting dread.
Eliot, standing beside him with his own wound now substantially healed, noticed his master’s distress. "Master Warren, don’t listen to their premature conclusions," he urged, trying to inject confidence into his voice despite his own doubts. "Zane has been in tighter spots than this. He always discovers a path to victory, even when all seems lost. I’m sure he’ll be fine."
"I lack your optimism this time," Master Warren replied, his shoulders slumping as despair weighed heavily in his tone. "You saw what those three roars did to him. Now Jacob has channeled that immense power into his spear…"
He paused, swallowing hard. "I cannot see how Zane walks away from this encounter." The unspoken truth hung between them—all his hopes for Misty Peak’s future rested on Zane’s shoulders.
Eliot sighed deeply. In truth, he didn't see a way out for Zane either. Jacob was just too powerful, and that bone spear gave him an insurmountable advantage. All Eliot could offer were hollow platitudes. "Maybe he’s got a plan."
Even he didn't believe it.
[SFX: DEAFENING MONSTER ROAR]
[MUSIC: NEW DANGER]
Just then, a deafening roar tore through the arena, erupting from the violet-black mist of Corrupt Vitality.
But this was different. This roar was deeper, louder—on a whole other level than the ones before.
[SFX: DEEP ANCIENT ROAR]
The difference was night and day. If the previous roars were the screams of a child, this was the bellow of an ancient god.
It was a sound that carried the weight of ages, filled with a desolate, primal dignity.
Even Master Elwood, who was shielding the crowd from the shockwaves, felt a jolt of alarm. He could feel the raw, lethal threat in this one.
