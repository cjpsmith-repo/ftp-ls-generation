Ep 269 - Out of the Abyss
Word Count: 1172
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO] Once you spiral into madness, it’s nearly impossible to stay calm through future challenges. Every minor setback feels like unbearable torture, while persistent anxiety gradually erodes both mind and spirit.
When submerged in such depths of despair, the responsibility for salvation rests solely with yourself—no one else can pull you out of the abyss.
[AMBIENT: COMPETITION ARENA]
Master Oliver stood back from the competition arena, his face darkening like a thundercloud. He had just watched his greatest pride, his brilliant protégé Adrian, utterly shattered by Zane—a complete mental collapse before countless spectators.
Oliver’s fingers clenched involuntarily at his sides, his jaw tightening with suppressed emotion. But despite his desire to intervene, Oliver recognized the futility of immediate action. Attempting to reach someone in such a fragmented state would merely propel them deeper into their personal void.
The situation was similar to trying to console someone consumed by fury. They might barely maintain their composure, teetering on the edge of control, but a single misplaced phrase, even one offered with genuine compassion, could shatter what’s left of their control and trigger a complete meltdown. Oliver knew that Adrian now walked a precarious path that he must navigate alone.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
[AMBIENT: HUSHED ARENA]
Nevertheless, Zane approached the white lotus with measured steps, his expression unreadable. The once-magnificent flower, now drained of its Vitality, withered visibly before his eyes. Adrian, lost in his internal collapse, neither noticed the lotus’s decay nor registered Zane’s advancing presence.
With deliberate patience, Zane crossed the arena floor, each footfall echoing in the tense silence that had enveloped the crowd. Spectators held their breath, uncertain whether they were witnessing the prelude to mercy or further humiliation. When he finally reached the lotus, Zane towered over his defeated opponent, his shadow falling across Adrian’s huddled form.
He studied the broken prodigy with a complex mixture of satisfaction and something almost resembling pity in his eyes.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
"I’ll give you one shot," Zane said, his voice cutting through the silence. "Three years from now, you can challenge me again. But even then, you won’t win. Of course, if you don’t have the guts to even try, you might as well crawl away in shame and give up for good. You’ve got incredible talent, but you have zero grit for the warrior’s path. Just look at you. One little setback and you completely fall apart. You’re a wreck. Even if I hadn’t beaten you today, someone else would have, and soon.
This world is huge. The Eastern Territory alone has billions of people. And from what I hear, the Heartlands has ten times that. Out there, you have geniuses who were mastering skills way above tier four when they were still kids. They use every miracle cure you can imagine to get stronger. Their talent is in their bloodline, and they have powerful masters guiding their every move. Any one of those prodigies could crush you—even a nine or ten-year-old. Hell, they could probably crush me right now. Your pride is a joke! It’s not pride, it’s just pure arrogance.
Only when you realize that pride of yours is a lie can you move past it and start training for real. Maybe then you’ll have a shot at catching up to me. But if you can’t do that, you’ll just be a loser for the rest of your life. No, actually—it'll just prove you were a loser all along!"
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
Every word from Zane was another twist of the knife in Adrian’s heart.
[SFX: ANGRY SHOUT]
The more Master Oliver listened, the darker his expression grew. Finally, he couldn’t take it anymore. "Zane!" he roared. "You’ve won! Don’t push it!"
It was true, Zane had already crossed a line. This wasn’t criticism; it was a brutal, personal takedown. Adrian was on the verge of completely breaking, and Zane showed no sign of letting up. Before the match, most of the crowd had been rooting for Adrian. But after watching Zane’s victory, opinions had shifted. Now, a lot of them were on Zane’s side.
[MUSIC: ENLIGHTENMENT REVELATION]
[SFX: LOTUS BLOOMING — SLOW, MASSIVE]
A lotus shadow flickered beneath Adrian's feet. The despair on his face drained away, replaced by something distant and unreadable — as if he were seeing everyone and no one. He stayed that way for several long breaths, then closed his eyes.
An enormous white lotus erupted from the ground beneath him, wrapping around him entirely. The competition paused. No one spoke.
[AMBIENT: SILENCE — HELD]
After what felt like an eternity, cracks spread across the surface of the lotus and branched outward until the whole flower shattered. Adrian opened his eyes. The fierce, domineering edge that had always defined him was gone — replaced by something quieter, more tranquil. Even his Vitality felt changed.
Instead of shattering him, Zane’s harsh words actually broadened Adrian’s mind, giving him a sudden insight into the very spirit of the Lotus Scriptures.
Master Oliver had once told Adrian that while the Lotus Scriptures was a Tier Four Cultivation Method, its true power was in a class of its own. Since it came from a powerful clan in the Heartlands, Adrian needed to study it deeply to unlock its full potential. Though Adrian had a basic grasp of its spirit, he still had a long way to go.
[MUSIC: HUMBLE REALIZATION]
"Thank you," Adrian said, his voice laced with a new humility. He bowed to Zane before walking off the competition arena. He went straight to Master Oliver, bowed deeply again, and said, "Master, I’ve had a breakthrough. I need to go into seclusion to train. I won’t be able to continue in the All Peaks Competition."
"What?" Master Oliver was floored. He took the All Peaks Competition very seriously. If Adrian had placed in the top three, it would have made him an incredibly proud master. Not to mention, the top three finishers would earn a chance to go to the Cloud Road—a huge opportunity for Adrian.
But the look on Adrian’s face stopped him. Maybe Adrian really had understood something profound. Going into seclusion to master what he’d learned might be the best thing for him. It wasn’t the end of the world. The Cloud Road would still be there in three years. An epiphany, on the other hand, was a rare gift most people never received.
So, Master Oliver nodded. "Alright, Adrian. I’ll grant you permission to go into seclusion."
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REFLECTION]
The fight between Zane and Adrian had been a total nail-biter. Who would’ve thought that Adrian, already having the upper hand, would reveal an ancient Sacred Weapon, only for Zane to somehow turn the tables? Even now, no one quite understood how Zane had defeated Adrian’s sword with just his vibration skill.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
Adrian’s breakdown after the fight, teetering on the edge of madness, had kept everyone glued to their seats. Then, in a riveting climax, Zane’s verbal assault had somehow provoked a sudden epiphany, turning the entire situation on its head.
