Ep 624 - Negotiations
Word count: 1101
[AMBIENT: TENSE HALL ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
The hall on the tenth floor of the Tower of Sin, usually a lively hub for warriors, had been cleared out. Some left with their heads bowed in disappointment, others seething, but they all left. A group of top-level War Lords had taken over. Normally, the area was open to anyone, but in a moment this critical, the rules had been tossed right out the window.
The lesser War Generals filed out reluctantly, their opinions kept behind gritted teeth. A massive conflict was brewing, and the only honorable choice was to obey the top-level War Lords.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
Inside the now-empty hall, Maurice sat in a corner with a scornful look plastered on his face. Ogres were known for their staggering height and bulk, but Maurice was a giant even among his own kind, towering over everyone else even while seated. Three expressionless War Lords stood behind him, radiating an intimidating energy. Despite their differences, they were all focused on the same problem. The death of two of their War Sages was a massive blow, a loss the Ogres couldn't just stomach. It had cost their people dearly, and Zane’s very presence here only made a bad situation worse.
Zane sat flanked by Fabian and Jarrod. Even though he could probably handle himself—he controlled the entire Tower of Sin, after all—they weren't about to let Maurice try anything. Bringing three top-level War Lords from the Demon Night had been a smart move by Esther Sterling. If a fight with the Ogres broke out, they were more than ready.
“You really are a young hero,” Maurice rumbled, a laugh shaking his massive chest. “For thousands of years, no one has ever managed to take complete control of the Tower like you have. I don’t believe in fate, but with you… it almost feels like this was all meant to be.”
“I don’t believe in fate either,” Zane replied, a faint smile on his lips. “It’s all on us. We make our own choices, create our own destiny.”
[AMBIENT: TENSE MEETING ROOM]
“A human once said that even the strongest have their limits,” Maurice chuckled, clearly amused with himself. “You have to know when to pick your battles, or you’ll be the one who pays the price.”
The message was clear: even if Zane had the upper hand here—even if he allied with the Demon Night or kicked the Ogres out of the Tower entirely—he couldn’t ignore the power of the Flaming Place and the Dread Place.
Zane just chuckled in response.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
“But I’m curious,” Maurice pressed, leaning forward slightly. “How much of the Tower can you actually control? The legends say it’s a weapon that belongs to some master from the upper world. Is any of that true?”
Zane didn't answer with words. He raised one hand and gave a slow, casual wave.
[SFX: LOW RUMBLE]
The walls of the hall began to disappear, layer by layer, peeling back like smoke. Beyond them, the secret cultivation rooms that lined the outer edge of the living area were suddenly exposed — and the War Generals inside them, who had been sitting in quiet meditation, found themselves staring out into open air. They looked around in a panic, spotted the ring of top-level War Lords watching them, and scrambled to their feet with terrified expressions.
[MUSIC: DARK RESONANT SWELL]
The Ogre War Lords behind Maurice went rigid. Their grave eyes moved from the dissolved walls back to Zane.
Maurice was quiet for a moment. Then he brought his enormous hands together in a slow, deliberate clap. "Unimaginable," he said. "You haven't refined the Tower, and yet—"
"Yet I can control everything in it," Zane finished, his eyes sharp. "Which brings me to something I'd like to settle. Your people have been trying to kill me. How exactly do we square that?"
Maurice chuckled, spreading his hands wide. "You're a straightforward man. I respect that. Which is why I'll be straightforward in return — what I want from this negotiation is simple. Peaceful coexistence."
[MUSIC: LOW STRINGS]
Esther Sterling's expression didn't change, but behind her eyes something shifted. Peaceful coexistence. From Maurice. The same Maurice who had never once restrained himself in a dispute with any race — not the humans, not the Beast Race, not even the Demon Night.
Maurice didn't do peaceful coexistence. He did dominance.
And yet here he was, saying the words.
[SFX: ANGRY MURMURS]
The War Lords behind Maurice looked furious. The real topic of this meeting — giving seats to the humans — was a humiliation. It meant Ogres would lose their hard-won spots.
"As a show of sincerity," Maurice said, folding his hands on the table, "our people are prepared to give up five seats to the Human race."
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
The War Lords behind Maurice looked like they'd swallowed something bitter. Five seats — handed to humans. To them, it was a humiliation that went bone-deep.
Fabian and Jarrod, on the other hand, were practically vibrating with excitement.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
It was an unbelievably generous offer. Five more seats meant twenty-five more passes for humans into the Dark Nether Cave. Add that to the four seats they already had, and their total would jump to nine. It might not be enough to rank third in the Tower, but it would absolutely lock them into fourth place.
Right now, their eyes were shining with a desperate hope. They were practically itching to send the good news back to the Violet Heart Sanctuary. When Zane had first gotten their attention, the Sanctuary had only hoped he might secure them a single extra seat. But to get five, and to do it before he’d even reached the top of the Tower? It was mind-blowing. They’d never dreamed something like this was possible.
Take the deal, Fabian silently pleaded, his fists clenched so tight his knuckles were white. Jarrod was right there with her, staring at Zane with wide, eager eyes. The Ogres had dominated everyone for so long. For them to just hand over five seats was a gift of epic proportions. What if Maurice suddenly changed his mind?
Esther couldn't help but sigh inwardly at the pair of them. The humans had been weak for too long — she understood why five seats felt like a miracle. But this was a negotiation, and Zane held every card. If she were in his position, she wouldn't open her mouth for anything less than ten.
She kept her face still and waited to see what he would do.
