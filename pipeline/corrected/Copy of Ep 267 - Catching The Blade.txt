Ep 267 - Catching The Blade
Word count: 995
[MUSIC: NOBLE FAMILY THEME]
Adrian came from House Warrick, a family that was prominent, but still stood several rungs below the Capital’s elite circles. Unlike The Seven Great Houses with their vast fortunes and political connections, the Warrick family’s coffers lacked the astronomical wealth required to purchase a Sacred Weapon for their talented son. Such treasures remained the exclusive privilege of those born to true power.
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TOMB]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ADVENTURE]
The Sovereign’s Blade had come to Adrian through a much more perilous path. In reality, Adrian had found the weapon himself, stumbling upon it in a dusty, forgotten imperial mausoleum. The ancient burial chamber, thick with dust undisturbed for centuries, concealed a labyrinth of deadly mechanisms—poison darts that hissed through stale air, floor tiles that collapsed into bottomless pits, and walls that suddenly crushed inward without warning.
Most terrifying of all were the skeletal guardians that awoke at his intrusion, their hollow eye sockets gleaming with malevolent intelligence as they pursued him through narrow corridors. These were ancient warriors whose power rivaled masters of the Soul Realm.
Adrian had escaped that tomb by mere heartbeats, barely making it out alive. Naturally, the blade was his most cherished possession, and not something he used for just any occasion.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL LOTUSES]
"This is The Sovereign’s Blade," Adrian announced, his voice smooth with pride as he presented the weapon. A shower of ethereal lotuses descended around him, their translucent petals catching the light. "It belonged to Emperor Amos of the ancient Draconian Dynasty. Your body may be tough as iron, Zane. But let’s see if it can stand up to this."
The weapon gleamed with an otherworldly luster. Adrian’s eyes reflected its glow, revealing a mixture of reverence and arrogance as his fingers tightened around the hilt.
Zane’s eyebrow shot up as he studied the blade, a flicker of genuine surprise crossing his usually composed features. He never would have pegged Adrian for someone with a Sacred Weapon. The revelation shifted something in Zane’s assessment of his opponent; Adrian was either extremely lucky or far more resourceful than his privileged demeanor suggested.
[MUSIC: ANCIENT LEGENDS]
Legend had it that in the old days, weaponsmiths could perceive the Celestial Law far more easily than they could now. Because of this, becoming a master of the craft was common, and forging a Sacred Weapon was a much more achievable feat.
But then, the world changed. The Celestial Law grew distant and obscure, making it nearly impossible to forge new Sacred Weapons. As for what caused this shift, or why the Law became so hard to grasp, no one knew.
That's why ancient weapons were so much more powerful than modern ones. For example, even though The Sovereign's Blade in Adrian’s hand only had a single Sacred Sigil—making it a low-grade Sacred Weapon—its power was equivalent to a medium-grade weapon forged in the current era.
Zane had no doubt a blade like that could cut him. His body was tough, but it wasn’t tougher than a medium-grade Spirit Weapon.
"A Sacred Weapon? Must've cost a fortune," Zane shot back. "But can you even hit me with it?"
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
The words were barely out of Zane’s mouth before he lunged forward. Fueled by the full power of the Phoenix Crystal, he became a blur of motion, a gust of wind. At the same instant, six Shadow Phantoms flickered into existence around him, darting toward Adrian from every direction.
[SFX: MAGICAL BLOOMING]
Faced with the six rushing phantoms, Adrian merely flicked his wrist. The large white lotus at his feet bloomed, its six petals breaking away and shooting out to intercept the attackers, blasting each phantom into the air. The lotus, it turned out, was a weapon in its own right.
[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]
With the lotus petals engaging his phantoms, Adrian leaped gracefully into the air, charging Zane with The Sovereign's Blade held high. "I don't know how you do it," he called out, his voice cutting through the air. "You're only at the second level of the Adept Realm, yet you move that fast. You've even mastered the Sword Step without a shred of sword intent. You really are a freak of nature. But like I said—it's useless. None of it will save you."
A murmur rippled through the crowd. "Wait, he's unarmed! Is he going to try to block that thing with his bare hands? He's nuts!"
"Catch Adrian's sword? Impossible! Zane's about to lose both his hands!"
Seeing Zane preparing to meet the blade empty-handed, many in the crowd shook their heads in disbelief. Catching a blade wasn't some legendary technique; plenty of warriors could do it. But it required one crucial thing: being overwhelmingly stronger than your opponent, strong enough to halt the blade’s momentum.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]
But Adrian was stronger than Zane, and he was wielding a Sacred Weapon. Zane's move wasn’t just bold, it was suicidal. Onlookers cringed, already picturing the gruesome spray of blood as his hands were severed.
But that's not what happened.
Because Zane had no intention of catching the blade at all. He wasn't stupid enough to face down a Sacred Weapon like that. He wasn’t about to lose his hands.
[SFX: FIST IMPACT ON METAL]
Instead, he balled his right hand into a fist, spun, and slammed it into the flat of The Sovereign's Blade.
Can't block the edge, he thought, but I can hit the flat!
A sneer twisted Adrian's lips as he saw Zane’s counter. Pathetic, he thought. Weaklings are always weak. Even when cornered, all they can manage are cheap tricks. It’ll be easy enough to counter this. I’ll just let the blade follow the force of his punch and redirect it. I was aiming for his left shoulder, now I’ll take his right. It makes no difference.
But the instant Zane's fist connected with the steel, the smug look on Adrian's face vanished.
[SFX: INTENSE VIBRATION]
An intense vibration shot out from Zane's fist, racing up the length of the sword. The shock jolted Adrian's entire arm, his grip faltered, and it looked like The Sovereign's Blade might fly out of his hand.
