Ep 901 - Trapped Inside

Word count: 1186

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL MINDSCAPE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Zane's soul was somewhere it had no business being — trapped inside the inner world of the woman who had been sealed inside the Crystal Coffin. 

And she was offering to make him immortal.

Zane rolled his eyes. Great. I’m trapped with a lunatic.

He was still trying to wrap his head around the situation when a familiar psychic presence washed through his thoughts.

“Zane,” the Cobalt Dragon’s voice echoed in his mind, “you seem to have run into some trouble.”

The Cobalt Dragon and the Soulforge were housed back in his physical body, but Zane had already figured out he could still connect with the dragon even when his soul was separated. How the dragon always knew what was going on, though, was a total mystery.

“Yeah, you could say that. Small problem,” Zane muttered back. “I think I’ve stumbled into a crazy woman’s head.”

“A crazy woman?” the Cobalt Dragon asked, his mental voice tinged with an odd curiosity.

“Yeah.” Zane quickly filled him in on the whole necropolis nightmare.

The second he mentioned that the woman’s pupils were golden, the Cobalt Dragon cut him off. “Hold on. Are you telling me this woman has golden eyes and was sealed inside a Crystal Coffin?”

“Yes,” Zane confirmed.

[SFX: DEEP DRAGON LAUGH REVERB]

A deep, rumbling laugh echoed in his mind. “Ha! That explains everything.”

“What’s so funny?” Zane shot back, his patience wearing thin. “You’re some all-powerful dragon. Isn’t there anything you can do to get me out of here?”

Just then, the woman’s gaze sharpened on his soul. “Who are you talking to?”

This was her world, after all. Even a telepathic conversation wasn’t going to slip by her unnoticed.

Zane pressed his lips together, ignoring her. The Cobalt Dragon’s voice came through again. “Zane, ask her if her name is Brianna Shaw.”

“You know her?” Zane asked, surprised.

“Maybe,” the dragon replied, his tone suddenly distant, as if lost in a memory.

Zane nodded to himself, then turned to the woman with a slight, almost challenging smile. “Someone wants to know if your name is Brianna Shaw.”

[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]

[AMBIENT: EARTHQUAKE RUMBLE]

The name had barely left his lips when her entire body went rigid. A violent tremor shot through the ground, and the sky above them cracked with a deafening peal of thunder. The whole inner world was coming apart at the seams. 

[SFX: MASSIVE WAVES CRASHING]

The island beneath his feet vibrated intensely as the Sea of Vitality outside roared to life, hurling waves the size of small mountains against the shore.

[AMBIENT: PANICKED CROWD SHOUTING]

On the island, the indigenous people she’d created scrambled in a panic, shouting in a language Zane didn’t recognize—probably another one of her inventions.

“Who told you that name?” she demanded, her golden eyes blazing as she fixed her stare on him. “Who?”

Before Zane could answer, the Cobalt Dragon's voice cut in again. “Tell her it's her seventh grandpa.”

Zane blinked. Seventh grandpa? No way. “Hold on — she's your granddaughter?”

The Cobalt Dragon said nothing. His silence was answer enough.

Zane turned back to the woman, barely suppressing a grin. “He says to tell you — it's your seventh grandpa.”

[SFX: THUNDER CRACK — SECOND STRIKE]

[AMBIENT: LIGHTNING SPLITTING SEA]

Another massive bolt split the sky. Where it struck the Sea of Vitality, columns of silver light erupted — each one as wide as a mountain. The island shuddered again, harder this time.

And then she stopped.

Her face crumpled — not in rage, but in something far older and heavier than that. And then she began to cry.

Her soul had no tears to shed. The sound came through, raw and broken, but her face stayed dry. It was somehow worse for it.

[MUSIC: SORROWFUL STRINGS]

Zane stood there completely at a loss. He couldn't comfort her — he barely knew her. But he couldn't just do nothing either. Whatever she looked like, this woman was almost certainly older than anyone he'd ever met.

“Cobalt Dragon,” he said quietly. “What do I do?”

”Tell her to stop crying,” the dragon replied, his voice flat.

Zane stared. “That's your advice?”

”Tell her.”

Zane exhaled and turned back to her. “Your seventh grandpa says — stop crying.”

It worked. She went still almost immediately, blinking at him with red-rimmed golden eyes. Then, in a small voice, she asked, “Why is seventh grandpa with you? What about first grandpa… and the others?”

Zane hesitated. He'd never told anyone about the nine True Dragons — not really. Even the black flame in the Soulforge was something he kept close to his chest. The only person who'd ever known the full truth was Lavender.

“Can I tell her?” he asked the Cobalt Dragon.

”She's not an outsider. Tell her everything.”

[MUSIC: QUIET REVELATION]

So Zane told her. About the Soulforge. About the nine True Dragons living in the space behind his mind. About how the Cobalt Dragon had been with him since before he understood what any of it meant.

“So it sounds like they'd be your first grandpa, second grandpa… all the way down,” he finished.

She absorbed this slowly. She nodded once. “I understand.”

It all clicked into place for Zane. She must have been using some kind of technique to delay the Five Aging Processes — and from her reaction, she hadn't cast it on herself. Someone else had done this to her.

Zane let out a long breath. “Alright. Now that that's settled — can you let me out?”

She blinked at him. “Why would I do that?”

“Why?” He gestured around at the island, the churning sea, the bewildered indigenous tribe still huddled behind their huts. “You want me to stay here and help you build your little civilization?”

She didn't answer that. Instead, she said simply, “I've been sealed with the Soul Race's Soul Sealing Technique. My soul is imprisoned in my own inner world.”

It was a known, if extreme, method in the Upper World for powerful beings to extend their lives. But the downside was brutal. Until the spell was broken, you were a prisoner in your own body. Your soul was trapped, completely cut off from the outside, with nothing to do but create an inner world to keep from going insane. Even an average cultivator at the Spirit Supreme Realm could create a world like this with just a fragment of their soul; they didn’t need to be completely locked inside.

This girl hadn’t just been sealed; she’d been deliberately trapped in the most helpless way imaginable.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

“I don’t have control over my body,” she said, her voice cutting through the pandemonium. A wide, almost manic smile spread across her face. “But since your soul has fused with my Soul Core, I can help you control it!”

Seriously? It could work like that?

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]

For a second, Zane was just speechless. Then, a wild, incredulous surge of hope washed over him. Controlling her body had been his goal from the start, but he never would have guessed it would happen like this.

He was just damn lucky. Lucky that the girl in the pillar and the Cobalt Dragon had some kind of history. Without that connection, he would have been toast. 

A lone soul facing off against a master from the Spirit Supreme Realm? He wouldn't have stood a chance.
