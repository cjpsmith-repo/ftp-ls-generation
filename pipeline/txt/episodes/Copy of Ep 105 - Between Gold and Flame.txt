Ep 105 - Between Gold and Flame
Word Count: 1293

[AMBIENT: QUIET ARMORY]
[SFX: CALCULATOR CLICKING]
 Over two hundred mysterious weapons were lined up in neat rows. The only sound in The Armory was the sharp click-clack of a gold calculator. The middle-aged shopkeeper held it in one hand, his other finger flying across the keys as he tallied everything up. Aside from the clicking, the room was dead silent.
When he finally finished, he looked up. "Okay, that's two hundred and twenty weapons, all with different prices. The grand total comes out to twenty-two thousand Crystal Cubes."
After giving him the total, the shopkeeper offered Zane a business-like smile. "Since you're buying in bulk, The Armory is happy to give you a ten percent discount. We'll just round it down and call it an even twenty thousand Crystal Cubes. Sound good?"
It was a good deal. Zane figured ten percent was more than fair, so he gave a simple nod.
[AMBIENT: MARKETPLACE]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
 A few minutes later, Zane and the shopkeeper walked out of The Armory together. They found a Sacred Vault on a corner of the marketplace. When Zane first walked in, the tellers barely glanced up. But the moment he produced his gold medallion, their postures straightened, their expressions shifting from indifference to attentive respect.
The staff at the Sacred Vault knew that anyone with a gold medallion was either incredibly wealthy or dangerously influential. Either way, they deserved top-notch service.
A teller quickly issued a banknote for twenty thousand Crystal Cubes and handed it to Zane. He passed the note to the shopkeeper, whose eyes widened almost imperceptibly before he smoothed his expression. The man was clearly thrilled with the massive sale but was trying his best to look professional. With that, their deal was done.
[AMBIENT: SECLUDED AREA]
 Before he could even think about auctioning the Celestial Essence, Zane needed to melt down the Legendary Weapons in his Storage Ring. He found a secluded spot and immediately rummaged through his ring, pulling out a pair of short, blue steel swords.
[SFX: MAGICAL SUMMONING]
 Zane laid the swords out and focused, calling on the huge Furnace in his mind. He hadn't summoned the Black Fire in a while, and it shot from his consciousness like an eager bird, immediately engulfing the blades.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
[SFX: FLAMES]
 In the blink of an eye, the swords vanished, leaving seven shimmering drops of Celestial Essence floating in their place. Zane marveled at them for a second. Just as the Black Fire was about to hungrily swallow the essence, a small bottle appeared in Zane's hand, and the drops slid neatly inside before the flame could touch them.
The Black Fire zipped around him like a restless spirit, clearly protesting that he’d snatched away its prize.
Seeing this, Zane flicked his Storage Ring open and tossed out a large, golden battle axe. The Black Fire rushed toward it with the same excitement as before.
Of all the legendary weapons Zane owned, the massive axe was the most impressive-looking. If the short swords yielded seven drops, he figured this had to produce even more.
He was surprised when the axe melted down into only three drops of Celestial Essence. Zane was beginning to realize just how much the quality of these legendary weapons could vary; some were clearly better than others. It was a good thing he had so many to burn through.
From what he could tell, a legendary weapon might produce anywhere from two to nine sacred drops, depending on its quality. On average, he could expect about five drops per weapon.
He spent the whole night melting them down. One by one, Zane fed the weapons to the Black Fire. After about a hundred of them, he’d collected over five hundred drops of Celestial Essence.
[MUSIC: TIRED MELANCHOLY]
 But the Black Fire had been working non-stop, and it was clearly running out of steam. Its once-cheerful flicker had become a weak, sluggish pulse. It just hovered listlessly in front of Zane.
It seemed even the Black Fire could get tired. Seeing it looking so pathetic, Zane felt a pang of pity and decided to stop. He took two hundred drops of Celestial Essence from his bottle and flicked them toward the flame. Although consuming the celestial essence restored the Black Fire's strength, it wasn't enough to also awaken any new Dragon Scales.
Although consuming the Celestial Essence restored the Black Fire's strength, it did more than that. Two hundred drops poured into the Cobalt Dragon's mouth — and forty dragon scales lit up at once, glowing a clear, bright green. Including the seven already active, that brought the total to forty-seven.
Zane observed the glowing patch in his mind. Forty-seven scales. If he channeled all of them at once — he could probably shatter Finn's Tectonic Rings now. A freshly-minted Nature Level cultivator might not survive a single punch.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
 Like a starving pup catching the scent of meat, the Black Fire flared back to life. As soon as it devoured the essence Zane offered, some of its old spark returned. Then, it retreated back into his mind to rest.
Zane took a deep breath and channeled the remaining three hundred drops into his Titan Strike technique, cultivating the Life Energy his Nether Essence needed to grow stronger. Practicing the Titan Strike was incredibly difficult, but he was slowly mastering it.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRAINING]
He could now perform the first move, Earthshatter, with ease and had also gotten the hang of the second, Ogre Horde. It wasn't as powerful as the first move, but it allowed him to attack multiple targets at once. He figured it would be perfect for crowd control, if he ever had to fight off a mob.
Over the following days he returned to the remaining weapons, feeding them to the Black Fire in batches as it recovered. He set aside more than six hundred drops of Celestial Essence to sell — but not all at once. Fifty drops had caused pandemonium at the auction. Six hundred released together could attract the wrong kind of attention. He would sell in batches, carefully.
[SFX: BELLS]
 Deep in meditation, Zane's concentration broke when Misty Peak's resonant bells began to toll across the mountainside. The unexpected sound echoed through the secluded training area he had chosen specifically for its isolation, leaving him with no fellow disciples nearby to explain the sudden summons.
"The bell only rings for a full assembly," he mused, a flicker of concern crossing his face. "What in the world is going on that requires everyone's presence?"
Standing at the cliff's edge, Zane gazed down at the winding paths below, observing the steady stream of disciples making their way toward the summit plaza. Their hurried movements suggested something significant was underway.
[SFX: WIND RUSHING]
 Curiosity overcame his reluctance to abandon his training session. With a decisive exhale, he closed his eyes momentarily to center himself, then leaped gracefully from the precipice. The wind rushed past his ears as he descended, his body moving with practiced precision through the air before landing with barely a sound.
Adjusting his robes, Zane quickened his pace toward the gathering, his mind racing with possibilities about what could have prompted such an unusual assembly.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
 When Zane finally arrived, the plaza teemed with disciples, packed shoulder to shoulder in anticipation.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY HUM]
At the heart of this sea of robes stood Isla Novak, adorned in an elegant yet understated light blue dress that accentuated her regal bearing. Her presence commanded attention, but all eyes inevitably drifted to the colossal object beside her—a mysterious form completely shrouded beneath a yellow cloth. Ancient runes and arcane incantations shimmered across its surface, pulsing with an energy that seemed to bend the very air around it, leaving everyone to wonder what power lay concealed beneath.
