Ep 1314 - Give It Back

Word count: 1141

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: EERIE WIND]
Slade had offered himself to the Saint-Killing Spear, and it had turned him into a mindless killing machine.
As Zane backed away, the thing that was once Slade came right for him.
But the city was protected by its Celestial Laws.
[SFX: IMPACT ON BARRIER]
At first, Slade was stopped cold at the walls by an invisible barrier, just like the skulls had been. The difference was, the skulls couldn’t get through. Slade, on the other hand, was just a vessel for the spear’s power now—and the spear could.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: BARRIER SHATTERING]
With a deafening boom, a blast of blood-red light from the spear tore a hole in the barrier. He’d forced his way in.
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
But the moment he stepped inside, the city’s Celestial Laws clamped down on him. The spear’s power was enough to break the rules, but not enough to ignore them completely.
Zane was already scrambling back the second Slade breached the walls.
[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSHING THROUGH AIR]
The spear spun wildly, tearing through the air as it fought against the city’s laws to get to him. Zane couldn’t fight back—the rules forbade it. Not that he had any intention of fighting here. With both flying and fighting prohibited, his only option was to run.
[MUSIC: TENSE PULSATING]
Luckily, the city’s laws were slowing Slade down. It took an incredible amount of power just to move, let alone attack, and every step was a visible struggle. Even fueled by the spear, Slade was barely any faster than Zane.
The other warriors could only watch, helpless.
Up on the city walls, Zane kept backing away as Slade trudged forward, closing the distance inch by inch. The spinning tip of the spear was less than a foot from his face.
One hundred feet. Two hundred. Three hundred.
[SFX: SHARP METALLIC SCRAPE]
By the time Zane had retreated a thousand feet along the wall, the spear’s point was less than two inches from his nose.
And then, Slade just… stopped. He froze mid-stride.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
[AMBIENT: HUSHED CROWD MURMURS]
A hush fell over the crowd; no one had ever seen a real fight break out in the city. A soft, whirring sound filled the air, and as a light breeze drifted past, flakes of what looked like ash started peeling from Slade’s body. In seconds, his whole body was crumbling, disintegrating into dust that scattered on the wind.
[SFX: METAL SPEAR CLANGING ON STONE]
[SFX: SOFT WHIRRING]
[SFX: BODY DISINTEGRATING]
The self-offering wasn’t unlimited—it came with a hard clock built into it, tied to Slade’s own cultivation level. At his stage of the Soul Sea Realm, the sacrifice could hold for less than fifty breaths. Once the spear had drained him completely, the city’s own Celestial Law bore down on his defenseless body with crushing weight, and that alone was enough to reduce him to dust where he stood.
The spear was the only thing that had been holding him together.
[SFX: SOFT CHIME]
A faint red light drifted up from the dust and vanished—Slade’s soul, already on its way back to the main city to be revived.
[AMBIENT: HUSHED PUZZLED MURMURS]
Zane frowned. “No Illusion Points?” It didn’t add up. Slade had broken out of the Halls of Causality and fought his way through this main city for a long time—he should have been carrying an enormous fortune in points. But nothing had appeared.
Janet, Jeffrey, and the others climbed down from the wall and gathered around him, just as puzzled. Ranked fourth in this world and eighty-eighth across the entire Illusion Battlefield, Slade’s point total should have been staggering. Murmurs spread through the crowd. “Why didn’t any points drop?” “Is that even legal?” “Maybe he had some kind of trick up his sleeve.”
Hailey let out a quiet breath of relief. However people wanted to describe what Zane had just done—retreating, running—he’d still won in the end.
One of Slade’s three surviving followers sauntered over, a sly grin creeping across his face. “Heh. If the boss of the Underworld was willing to sacrifice himself, you’d better believe he already had an out planned.” His eyes drifted toward the spear, still lying untouched on the ground—unusual, since a warrior’s gear was supposed to vanish and reappear with them on death.
Before he could reach it, Zane hooked the spear up with the toe of his boot and tucked it into his Storage Ring, smiling faintly. “I really am curious. Care to explain?”
The warrior’s face stiffened. “Slade holds three Exemption Medallions,” he said coldly. “They mean he loses no Illusion Points on death. Hand back his weapon, or he won’t let this go.”
A ripple of envy passed through the crowd at the mention of three tokens.
Zane just smiled. He had a rare token of his own tucked away—it wasn’t as if this kind of thing didn’t exist. “Won’t let it go?” he said. “Without this spear, what exactly does he have to back that up?”
[SFX: BODY MATERIALIZING]
Slade’s form flickered, then solidified, no longer a mindless puppet but himself again—and he was furious.
“He took your weapon!” one of the warriors shouted from the sidelines.
Slade’s eyes blazed.
“Give it back!” he roared, lunging forward with an outstretched hand.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]
Zane just smiled, weighing the spear in his hand. “It was never yours to begin with. Why would I give it back?”
With the Saint-Killing Spear in his possession, Zane’s mind was already racing. He could spend five million Illusion Points to take a trip through the hall… If he had the time, he could finally give Lavender that surprise.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
[AMBIENT: CITY SQUARE CROWD]
Slade glared at him, a sneer twisting his lips.
“So this is the great Zane Lowell, famous across the universe. Nothing but a coward who runs to the city with his tail between his legs. The laws here saved you, but I still almost had you.”
He was just trying to get a rise out of him; they both knew they couldn’t fight in the city.
“We can fight after we leave,” Zane said calmly.
“Then give me the spear back, and we’ll fight,” Slade demanded.
Zane just smirked. “Not happening.”
“Fine,” Slade said, his voice cold. “You can’t touch me in here anyway.” He forced himself to calm down. It wasn’t a total loss. Sure, Zane had the spear, but Slade’s fourth-place ranking in this world was more than enough to get him to the next stage. Besides, the spear was just a projection. Losing it wasn’t the end of the world—it just meant he’d have to use his own weapon from here on out.
His eyes scanned the crowd of onlookers, finally locking onto Hailey.
“You, witch!” he snarled. “Get over here.”
