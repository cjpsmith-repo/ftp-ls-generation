Ep 167 - You Can’t Fix Stupid
Word Count: 1042
[SFX: BLADE AGAINST THROAT]
“Oh, really?” Zane taunted the attendants. “So how are you planning on doing that? Where do you want to start? Here?” He jabbed a thumb at his own chest. “Or here?” He tightened his grip, yanking the Young Master back and pressing the sharp edge of his broken blade against the kid’s throat.
“Are you insane? Let the Young Master go!” the two servants roared, but their fury was tempered with caution. They began to inch forward.
Just then, the Young Master of the Price Family finally broke out of his shock. “You bastard!” he shrieked. “Let me go right now, and maybe if you kill yourself with that blade, I’ll let it slide! Otherwise, I’ll have your entire family wiped out!”
[SFX: SLAP]
The words were barely out of his mouth before Zane slapped him, hard. The crack of the blow silenced him.
“My family?” Zane's voice dropped to a low, dangerous whisper. He pressed the blade a little deeper, just enough to draw a thin line of blood on the kid's neck. “You have no idea who you’re talking to. Tell me, how exactly do you plan on killing me?”
“You hit me?” the Young Master sputtered, blood trickling from the corner of his mouth. “My own parents have never laid a hand on me! You’re dead! I’m going to---”
[SFX: HARD SLAP]
He never got to finish. Zane slapped him again, even harder this time.
[SFX: SLAP]
The Young Master's eyes blazed, ready to scream another threat, but another sharp slap cut him off before he could start.
[SFX: COMBAT KICKS]
Seeing their master being slapped around, the two servants finally snapped. Gripping their weapons, they charged. Zane dispatched them with two simple kicks, sending them sprawling.
With the servants out of the way, Zane’s attention returned to the sniveling kid in his grasp. This is not what I wanted, he thought. He wasn’t looking for a fight; he’d just wanted to get stronger, not get tangled up in some rich family’s drama.
[AMBIENT: TENSE STANDOFF]
[MUSIC: THREATENING ORCHESTRA]
But he felt no fear. “You’re just an Adept,” the man---Price---said, his voice cold. “You think you can beat up my son in front of my own house and get away with it? You think no one in the entire Province of Galeria can touch you?” With a sharp gesture, Price sent his bodyguards forward.
They were elite masters, even for the Province of Galeria. One was a seventh-grade Adept, the other an eighth-grade.
Zane was genuinely surprised. Seventh and eighth-grade Adepts? Back in his hometown, Castermere County, a fifth-grade Adept could run the whole place. What were two masters of this level doing playing bodyguard? It didn’t add up.
He pushed the thought aside. If he were still just a half-step Adept, this might have been tricky. But now that he had broken through completely, taking them down would be a piece of cake.
Following their boss’s orders, the bodyguards closed in, but they didn’t attack immediately. Sensing Zane was also an Adept, they figured they could intimidate him into surrendering.
They were shocked when Zane moved first.
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]
This time, Zane didn’t even bother drawing on the Vitality in his body. He channeled the Phoenix Crystal on his right hand, pushing his speed to its absolute limit, and unleashed the deeper power coiled inside him. His speed was unbelievable; the two men had no time to even raise their arms.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACTS]
[SFX: BODIES HITTING GROUND]
He slammed a fist into each of their chests. The impact caved their armor in, and they were launched backward, hitting the ground with a sickening thud.
Zane had held back. If he hadn’t, they would have ended up just like that Adept from the noble clan back in Dragon Valley---disintegrated into dust by the power of his stars.
“Any more questions?” Zane asked, his voice flat.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATION]
Price's face twitched with annoyance at how pathetic his bodyguards looked. He alone knew the fortune he'd paid to hire these masters.
Some masters couldn’t be bought for any price, so he’d considered himself lucky to have two Adepts on his payroll. With them at his side, no one in the Province of Galeria had ever dared to challenge him.
He was floored. His high-level Adepts couldn’t even take a single hit. This kid, also an Adept, had flattened them without breaking a sweat. Looking at their crumpled forms, Price knew they weren’t getting up.
Price had clawed his way to the top of the business world; he was a survivor, a shark. But he’d gotten complacent, thinking his money and his hired muscle made him invincible.
In one swift moment, Zane had shattered that illusion. The arrogance on Price’s face dissolved, replaced by something else entirely. Zane saw the shift instantly.
“My dear friend,” Price began, his tone suddenly smooth as silk, “I am so glad you came\...”
Zane decided not to push it. He could tell Price wasn’t a petty man, just a father who spoiled his son.
After Zane was gone, the Young Master whined, “Dad, why did you sell him the Lightning Horse! You know it’s my favorite---”
[SFX: SLAP]
Before he could finish, Price slapped him clean across the face.
It was the first time he’d ever struck his son.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]
“I’ve told you to stay out of trouble!” Price seethed. “If you piss off some nobody, I can clean it up. But you cross someone with real power? You’ll be dead before you hit the ground!” Even as he yelled, Price blamed himself. He’d spoiled the boy, sheltered him from the real world.
The Young Master clutched his cheek, whimpering. “I was wrong. But Dad, you paid thirty Crystal Cubes for that Lightning Horse. Why did you tell him it was only two? He thought he was being generous giving us four!”
Price stared at him and sighed. If the boy wasn’t the spitting image of him, he’d swear he wasn’t his. He had none of his father’s brains, none of his ambition. They say a tiger doesn’t raise a dog, but looking at his son, Price could only think one thing: "you just can’t fix stupid."
