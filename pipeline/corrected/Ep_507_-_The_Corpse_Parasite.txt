Ep 507 - The Corpse Parasite
Word count: 1220
[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
As a warrior, Owen was famous for his incredible speed. A practitioner of the Wind Discipline, he'd even mastered the first layer of Wind Law—an understanding that already put him on par with Zane. How he'd managed such a feat was a mystery to everyone.
But this was a world of fighters, where the exceptionally gifted often had luck on their side. For someone with Owen's saint-level talent, a rare stroke of fortune wasn't so surprising.
Marilyn, though, was something else entirely. Even with his mastery of Wind Law, Owen couldn't touch her. She moved through his attacks like water flowing around stone, her expression never shifting from that calm, almost bored composure. Each failed strike seemed to chip away at Owen's confidence.
[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]
After failing to land a single blow, Owen finally resorted to the secret art of Beastly Transformation.  This was his trump card—the technique he'd sworn never to reveal unless absolutely necessary.
[SFX: TRANSFORMATION ENERGY SURGE]
[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD]
"What's that? Owen's body is... changing!" someone shouted from the crowd.
Another voice called out, tinged with both excitement and fear."He's moving too fast! I can't get a clear look. What did he become?"
[SFX: BEAST ROAR]
"A leopard of some kind… Look at the markings! That's a Legendary Beast—the Cloud Leopard! He must have Cloud Leopard blood, and the concentration must be off the charts for him to transform like that!"
Among the crowd, there were clearly a few who knew their stuff.
The Cloud Leopard was a Legendary Beast. While not as powerful as a Greater Legendary Beast, it was in the same class as the Blue Phoenix, the Roc, and the Celestial Lynx.
But just having Legendary Beast blood wasn't enough to trigger a transformation. Zane, for example, had the blood of a True Dragon but couldn't become one because his bloodline wasn't pure enough. A fighter’s blood had to reach a certain level of concentration to unlock that kind of power. For Owen to do this meant the Cloud Leopard blood in his veins was incredibly potent.
[SFX: TRANSFORMATION GROWL]
[AMBIENT: EERIE TENSION]
He was now a terrifying hybrid of man and beast. His fingernails had become razor-sharp claws, and his round pupils had narrowed into the thin, vertical slits of a leopard, glowing a startling yellow. Vicious fangs glinted at the corners of his mouth as his entire body became shrouded in a pale, bluish-green light.
[SFX: BEAST ROAR]
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
Owen let out a guttural roar. In his beast form, his speed had multiplied. Channeling the ferocious aura of a Legendary Beast, he lunged at Marilyn.
The sudden burst of speed caught Marilyn completely off guard. She couldn't dodge in time.
[SFX: CLAW SLASH]
His claws raked across her shoulder.
[SFX: FABRIC TEARING]
The razor-sharp talons shredded her clothes. In an instant, her shirt was in ribbons, revealing the bindings wrapped around her chest.
The crowd fell into a stunned silence. Then, all at once, it shattered.
[SFX: CROWD JEERS]
Whistles and shouts erupted from the stands—crude, ugly sounds that made several of the more composed fighters look away in disgust.
Marilyn didn't flinch or reach for the torn fabric. She simply looked down at the wound on her shoulder, then back up at Owen, her expression unchanged.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TENSION]
But Marilyn had already begun to move.
Her fingers shifted almost imperceptibly, and from her body—from her sleeves, her hair, the folds of what remained of her clothing—small dark flowers began to fall. One by one, drifting down like ash. They looked almost gentle, almost decorative. Most of the crowd didn't even notice them.
Owen noticed nothing at all.
[SFX: BEAST GROWL]
Still locked in his berserk state, he lunged again. Marilyn retreated with that same fluid, unhurried grace, drawing him forward, letting him press her back step by step toward the edge of the arena. To the crowd, it looked like desperation. To Owe,n it felt like victory closing in.
He pounced. Then he stopped dead.
[SFX: SHARP CRACK — THORNS SPLITTING FLESH]
Fine black thorns erupted from his own body—bursting outward from beneath his skin, threading through muscle and pulling tight. They grew with horrifying speed, winding around his arms and legs, anchoring him in place as if the ground itself had reached up to claim him.
[SFX: BEAST HOWL]
His roar became a howl of pain and confusion. Blood ran freely where the thorns tore through him, and from that blood, fed by it, small crimson flowers began to bloom along the lengths of the black branches.
[AMBIENT: HORRIFIED CROWD MURMURS]
The sight was grotesque and strangely beautiful. Owen—half man, half leopard, now half consumed by flowering thorns—stood immobilized in the center of the arena.
Marilyn walked toward him slowly. She reached out and plucked one of the red blooms from where it had flowered against his arm. She held it to her nose for a moment, her eyes meeting his.
"I told you," she said quietly, "that you couldn't touch me."
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC SHIFT]
Owen's transformed eyes—those glowing yellow slits—stared back at her. The ferocity had left them entirely. What remained was something rawer. Despair.
[SFX: INSECT WINGS BUZZING]
But then, a bug appeared in his other hand. It was no bigger than a thumb, its back a swirl of iridescent colors. The moment it materialized, it spread its wings and shot straight for Owen’s head.
Before it got halfway there, Marilyn thrust the flower in her hand toward it.
[SFX: SHARP PIERCE]
[SFX: IMPACT THUD]
The stem lanced through the bug, pinning it to the ground.
Even skewered, the tiny creature was vicious, and it thrashed wildly.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
A small, cold smile touched her lips as she glanced down at the struggling insect. "A Corpse Parasite," she said, her voice flat. "Owen, you were really going to go through with it. If this thing enters your body, you’d become more powerful, sure. But you’d also be a walking corpse, your soul devoured by the parasite. Worse, you’d be denied reincarnation. Your soul would just… scatter."
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
The Corpse Parasite was a truly vile thing. Anyone possessed by it was doomed to a horrific end, their mind and soul eaten from the inside out. In the process, however, the host would become an unstoppable killing machine, every last ounce of their potential unlocked in one final, desperate surge. To even consider using it was the ultimate act of desperation.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]
The light in Owen’s eyes faded. All hope had left him.
While most in the arena had never heard of flesh-eating flowers, everyone knew the legend of the Corpse Parasite.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
Seeing his gamble laid bare, many of the fighters in the crowd sighed, a wave of pity washing over them.
"He was going to use the Corpse Parasite on himself… He's that determined."
"To pay that price for the woman he loves… What a waste."
"I've never heard of anyone doing that to themselves! The desperation…"
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane’s fists clenched at his sides. His eyes, fixed on the arena, were perfectly still, his gaze as steady and calm as the surface of a deep lake.
But inside him, a storm was brewing.
