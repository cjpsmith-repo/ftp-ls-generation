[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: ENERGETIC CULTIVATION THEME]
"Dustin!" Zane called out across the grounds, his enthusiastic voice shattering his friend's hard-earned concentration.
Dustin spun around, surprise etched across his sweat-drenched face. "Zane? What are you doing here?" He wiped his forearm across his brow. The sight of his powerful friend at Falcon's Perch simply didn't align with what he knew about the training hierarchy.
In the few months since joining Misty Peak, Zane had accomplished what many disciples couldn't achieve in years—ascending from the Stage of Marrow Refining to the prestigious Adept Realm. His meteoric rise had turned heads throughout the sect, inspiring both admiration and jealousy among his peers. His raw power now eclipsed that of every other outer disciple, placing him firmly on an accelerated path toward inner disciple status, a coveted position that most could only dream of attaining.
For someone of Zane's extraordinary caliber, training at the modest facilities of Falcon's Perch was basically a waste of time—like a master swordsman practicing with wooden sticks. The training ground, while adequate for most disciples, offered nothing that could possibly challenge Zane's burgeoning abilities.
[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]
 The moment Dustin said his name, every outer disciple from Misty Peak stopped what they were doing and turned to look. Zane's meteoric rise had made him a legend, and his name alone was enough to command attention.
[SFX: DRAMATIC TENSION]
At the sound of Zane's name, an outer disciple named Flynn snapped his head around, a competitive fire instantly igniting in his eyes. His hands clenched involuntarily, betraying the ambition that constantly simmered beneath his composed exterior.
Flynn possessed remarkable talent and unwavering determination. Coming from a common family without connections or prestige, he'd scratched and clawed his way through the ranks of the Skyreach Sect. The contemptuous glances and whispered mockeries about his humble origins had only fueled his burning desire to silence his critics by defeating the very best. 
He dismissed the notion of challenging someone like Dustin as meaningless. Only one person occupied his thoughts, dominated his ambitions—Zane. In Flynn's mind, defeating this prodigy would be the ultimate validation, the only victory that would ever truly matter. At night, he often visualized their confrontation, rehearsing every possible scenario until exhaustion claimed him.
But right now, defeating Zane was a pipe dream. Flynn had only recently broken through to the Initiate Adept level. It wasn't nothing, but it paled in comparison to Zane's full-fledged Adept status. 
Falcon's Perch, however, had been Flynn's training ground for countless seasons. He reasoned that challenging Zane to a climbing competition on this terrain—his domain—might finally give him the advantage he desperately needed.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
 While Zane was still talking to Dustin, Flynn strode right up to them, his thousand-pound weighted suit straining with every step. He shot Zane a challenging glare, then spun on his heel and bolted toward the cliff face.
[SFX: ROCK CLIMBING SOUNDS]
The familiar sensation of rough stone beneath his fingertips triggered an almost meditative focus. Flynn attacked the vertical surface with practiced efficiency, his movements fluid and precise. Each placement of hand and foot reflected countless hours of repetition as he scrambled upward with preternatural speed. Within moments, he surpassed the thirty-three-foot mark that challenged most beginners, driving himself relentlessly higher.
His muscles burned as he pushed beyond previous limitations, ascending to an astonishing height of one hundred and fifty-five feet. Sweat trickled down his brow, but his concentration never wavered. 
Without sparing a single downward glance that might betray uncertainty, Flynn maintained his iron grip on the stone face. One hundred and fifty-five feet—it was a new personal record. The triumph coursing through his veins felt almost worth the countless hours of solitary training.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ACHIEVEMENT]
[SFX: SLIDING DOWN ROCK]
 After hitting his peak, he carefully slid back down, his eyes locked on Zane in open defiance. The second his feet hit the dirt, he marched right up to him, with a sneer on his face. "Think you can top that?" He jabbed a finger toward the cliff. "See that handprint way up there? They say the strongest inner disciple from Misty Peak ever left that mark."
Flynn unstrapped his weighted vest and held it out. "There are options — a hundred pounds, five hundred, or eight hundred. Take your pick."
He didn't need to say which one he'd been wearing.
"Take the lightest one," Dustin said quietly.
Zane reached past the lighter options and took the five-hundred-pound vest. He strapped it on, then bounced lightly on his heels twice. It was heavy. Not heavy enough to matter.
Flynn knew Zane had only just advanced from Initiate Adept to Adept. He also knew that leap in power was all about Raw Vitality, not brute physical strength. So, he was betting everything that Zane couldn't touch the handprint's height—or even match his own.
Climbing the cliff wasn't just about raw strength; it demanded flexibility and perfect timing, especially on the initial run-up.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
 Zane knew he couldn't just unleash his full power. If he did, the other disciples wouldn't even be able to track his movements, but brute force wasn't the answer here. This was a test of speed and control. If he went all out, he’d just smash into the cliff face. He had to perfectly calibrate his speed and momentum to launch himself skyward.
Since this was his first time attempting the climb, he decided to go with his gut.
[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]
 As he neared the base of the cliff, Zane dug his feet into the earth, bent his knees, and exploded upward.
[SFX: SUPERHUMAN SPEED]
He shot upward at a blistering pace. He was still flying, propelled by that one initial leap, his feet not having touched the cliff face once.
Dustin's jaw dropped in shock. He stared, completely speechless, captivated by the sight. *He's not even touching the cliff!* he thought in disbelief. *How is he still rising from just one jump?*
Normally, disciples had to constantly push off the cliff with their hands and feet to gain more height. But to everyone's absolute astonishment, Zane cleared sixty-five feet with nothing but the raw power of his initial jump.
"That's insane! How did he fly up that high with just one jump?"
"I... I'm speechless. It's not even fair. Seeing the gap between him and me... it just makes me want to give up."
Flynn watched from the crowd, his face a cold mask. "You can't climb a cliff by jumping," he scoffed. "Sure, he got a good sixty-five feet out of that first burst, but he's gonna fall the second his momentum gives out. See? He's slowing down!"
[SFX: MOMENTUM SLOWING]
 With that one leap, Zane hit a height of ninety feet. Just as his upward momentum began to fade, he reached out, ready to start climbing the old-fashioned way.
[SFX: HAND TOUCHING SMOOTH STONE]
 But the moment his hands made contact with the stone, Zane was startled. The cliff face was impossibly smooth. There wasn't a single handhold to grab onto.
There was no way he'd make it.
