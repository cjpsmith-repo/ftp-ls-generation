Ep 855 - Black Star

Word count: 1089

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CAVE ECHOES]

Camilla’s answer was exactly what he’d expected — and exactly what he didn’t want to hear. “I couldn’t find many Ancient Heartstones, but I did get a ton of supreme Aether crystals. I’ve got over thirty thousand of them now.”

She knew Zane wasn’t interested in the crystals; he was only after Ancient Heartstones. The problem was, every last one that the other warriors had managed to dig up was already gone. She’d bought them all herself at a ridiculously high price, and now the well was dry. She’d even tried baiting other miners with rare ores, but no one was biting. Every available Heartstone was already in her possession, and there was simply no one left to get more from.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

“I see,” Zane said, already turning back toward the pyramid.

He’d cleared the thirty-fourth floor and was heading for the next. The odds of finding Ancient Heartstones might get worse the higher he climbed, but he had to try.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS TEMPLE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

When he reached the thirty-fifth floor, he found a special door. All the others had been simple, framed by two gray pillars with just enough space for one person to squeeze through. This one, however, was different. The pillars were carved into two massive True Dragons, their mouths gaping as they stared down at him. They looked so real that it was unsettling.

This has to be something special, he thought, a flicker of curiosity rising in him.

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

Most warriors stuck to mining below the thirtieth floor. Even the saint-level prodigies wouldn’t venture up to the thirty-fifth. It was just a waste of time. The ore in the leaves at this level was impossible for them to dig out, and running into Stone-Eater Bugs was a death sentence. A single green one could take them down.

Without a second thought, Zane stepped through the dragon-mouthed doorway.

[SFX: WHOOSH FALLING]

Just like on the previous thirty-four floors, the world gave way beneath him, and he found himself falling.

[SFX: SOFT LANDING]

He landed softly on a giant leaf, but this one was nothing like the others. The leaves below had been like glass, glowing with a cool blue light that grew darker the higher he went. This one, however, pulsed with a warm, golden light.

“This is… different,” Zane murmured, scanning the golden surface. As he was looking it over, he suddenly froze.

[MUSIC: DISCOVERY CHIMES]

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

Embedded deep within the leaf, like an insect in amber, was a pickax.

“Whoa,” he breathed. “What is that?” It was clearly a pickax, but it looked unlike any tool he’d ever seen.

It was pitch black. And around its head, divine runes swirled — three of them, each one a ten-star.

Zane’s breath caught. 

He knew divine runes. The Cobalt Dragon had taught him the Dragon-blooded technique directly. Ten-star divine runes were the highest grade that existed — even at his peak, the Cobalt Dragon had needed enormous effort and rare materials just to inscribe a single one. 

Zane had used three five-star divine runes to hold off Life-and-Death warriors. The gap between each star was like heaven and earth. So what exactly was a weapon carrying three ten-star runes?

He didn’t have an answer. But he knew it wasn’t anything ordinary.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

His first instinct was to dig it out. His second thought stopped him. This door wasn’t hidden. Every Life-and-Death master and Soul Sea master who had ever entered the thirty-fifth floor had seen this leaf. If any of them could have taken it, it would already be gone.

He took out Randy’s pickax anyway.

He walked to the center of the leaf and tapped it twice, lightly. The golden surface gave two dull, muffled sounds.

He raised the pickax and swung with everything he had.

[SFX: SHARP CRACK]

[SFX: PIECES SKITTERING]

The pickax head shattered on impact. The broken pieces skidded across the leaf and tumbled off the edge, falling straight down into the dark below. The golden leaf didn’t have a mark on it.

Zane stared at the stub of the handle in his hand.

The leaf’s hardness was equivalent to a divine-class weapon in itself — he was sure of it now. He reached into his Storage Ring and drew his high-grade Relic sword. Swords were sharper than pickaxes, but they weren’t built for this kind of force. He held it over the leaf for a long moment.

Then he put it away. He wasn’t going to snap his sword for nothing.

He picked up the broken pickax handle, tucked it under his arm, and jumped down.

Randy spotted the broken handle the moment Zane reappeared beside the pyramid. “You broke the pickax!”

Zane held it up with a helpless smile. It was Randy’s tool, after all.

“There’s no way a low-grade Relic-Class weapon should break that cleanly,” Randy said, turning the shattered tool over in his hands. He sounded more confused than angry. “This thing was perfect for mining—thick head, wide blade. It should have been almost impossible to break.” 

He wasn’t blaming Zane; a friend was worth more than any tool, even a rare one. Still, he was baffled.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

“I found something… weird on the thirty-fifth floor,” Zane admitted.

“What?” Randy looked up, and then a flash of understanding crossed his face. “Wait. You mean the pickax in the golden leaf? You didn’t actually try to dig it out, did you?”

It was obvious Randy knew about the golden leaf; it wasn’t exactly hidden. Zane just nodded, feeling a little stupid for breaking his friend’s gear.

Randy just laughed. “Well, that explains it! Man, that’s my bad. I should’ve warned you,” he said, shaking his head.

[MUSIC: REVELATION THEME]

“That golden leaf is unbreakable. And the pickax inside it is called ‘Black Star.’ The Dragon-blooded records mention it. It’s a divine-class weapon!”

[SFX: REALIZATION SOUND]

“A divine-class weapon?” Zane was stunned. He’d always assumed they were graded like Relic-Class items—low, mid, and high—but apparently not. The truth was, he’d never even seen one. With both the Cobalt and Red Dragons asleep and Lavender preoccupied with the Soulcradle Wood, there was no one he could even ask about it.

Randy just smiled. “I can’t tell you exactly how powerful a divine-class weapon is, but that’s what the records say. Its main purpose isn’t for fighting. It’s for mining. It was made to break the leaves on the very top floors of this place.”
