Ep 927 - A Strange Bargain

Word count: 1156

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The moment the White-Haired Priest learned that the seven strangers were from the Demi-Immortal Race, his entire demeanor shifted. You could see a whole war of emotions play out across his face.

The Demi-Immortal Race didn't exactly have a sterling reputation in the upper world. They were a mixed-blood people with a small population, but almost every single one of them was an elite warrior. The bigger issue, though, was that they were all completely, certifiably insane.

Just a minute ago, the priest had been hoping to scare them off by name-dropping his backers, The Legion of Thunder Punishment. Sure, it was the weakest of the Three Great Human Powers, but it still controlled two thousand supreme worlds. That’s a force to be reckoned with. 

If he’d known he was dealing with the Demi-Immortal Race, he wouldn't have even bothered trying to talk. The problem was, he absolutely had to get his hands on that Ancestral Dragon.

The masters from the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms just stood off to the side, silent. They knew the Ancestral Dragon wasn't falling into their hands either way. If the priest was powerful enough to take it, he was welcome to try.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

The White-Haired Priest’s eyes darted between them. “So, you’re from the Demi-Immortal Race,” he began, trying to sound reasonable. “That’s fine, but I’ve been working to get that Ancestral Dragon for—”

Vorn cut him off, his voice dripping with impatience. “I don’t care how much time you wasted. Thinking you could use these locals to waltz into the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land and grab the dragon’s egg was a pipe dream from the start. We’re the ones who hatched it. We’re the ones who brought it out.”

[SFX: WEAPONS DRAWN]

[MUSIC: AGGRESSIVE PERCUSSION]

Beside him, the young man in black and the burly man started fidgeting with their weapons, flashing nasty grins at the priest. They were clearly itching for a fight.

The priest tried again. “Look, even if you’re Demi-Immortals, you can’t be this unreasonable—”

He didn’t even get to finish the sentence. 

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

The young man and the burly man lunged, launching a surprise attack. Warriors at the Spirit Supreme Realm moved with terrifying speed, and the two of them shot toward the priest like cannonballs while the crowd watched, wide-eyed.

But the priest was just as fast. As they charged, he rocketed backward, putting thousands of feet between them in an instant.

[SFX: ENERGY SHIELD ACTIVATION]

“Hold on! We can talk this out!” he yelled. A small shield in his hand flared, transforming into a shimmering ray of multi-colored light that deflected their incoming attacks.

The two attackers cursed under their breath and retreated to Vorn’s side.

The priest steadied himself. He knew the math — seven of them, one of him, and he had no chance. His only remaining option was to request help through the Reverse Spirit Channel. He'd have to buy time.

His eyes moved to Zane and the dragon. Something nagged at him. If the Demi-Immortals had hatched it, why was it following this young man?

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

From the sidelines, the masters representing the seven divine kingdoms just watched, their faces giving nothing away.

Vorn walked toward Zane, his tone noticeably softer than before. The Mark confirmed it — Zane had been inside the Tower of Crystal. That made him one of their own, at least provisionally. “Since you have the Mark,” Vorn said, “the next step is to find the Tower of Crystal. I trust you'll take us there.”

“Of course,” Zane said.

His eyes moved to Brianna's body, sitting motionless where he'd left it. He turned inward. “What happens to her now?”

The Cobalt Dragon's voice was quieter than usual. “Her soul is sealed in her inner world by the Soul Sealing Spell — a secret method of the Soul Race. Her body is alive but effectively inert. Only someone proficient in that technique can undo it, and there are very few such beings.” A pause. “The nine of us have a long history with her. I would like to see her freed.”

Zane looked at Brianna's still face. Then he looked at Dorothea.

Meanwhile, a few feet away, the girl found herself feeling a flicker of hope, even though she despised Dorothea.

Dorothea had already drifted toward Brianna's body, her earlier disappointment about Zane apparently forgotten. 

She crouched down and studied Brianna's face with open curiosity — then reached out and touched her cheek.

From somewhere deep inside her inner world, Brianna's consciousness flared. “That's my face.”

Dorothea's eyes went wide. “There's another soul in there?”

Zane stepped over. “Her soul is sealed in her inner world,” he said. “Soul Sealing Spell.”

“Soul Sealing Spell.” Dorothea said it slowly, turning it over. Then her expression sharpened with recognition. “I can undo that.”

Zane felt something loosen in his chest. “Can you?”

“I can,” Dorothea said. “But there's a condition.”

Inside her inner world, Brianna went very still. One hundred and twenty thousand years. That was how long she had been alone in there — building her island, nurturing her tribe, watching generations live and die while she remained sealed and unchanging. She hated Dorothea on instinct. But the thought of being free—

[MUSIC: PLAYFUL ROMANTIC THEME]

Dorothea giggled, seeming to forget all about Zane for a moment as her usual cheerful demeanor returned. She smiled at the girl. “The condition is simple: you have to marry me. What do you say?”

Zane coughed, loud and pointed.

The other Demi-Immortal warriors just sighed and shook their heads. This was Dorothea, through and through. They knew her personality—and her preferences—all too well.

The girl fell silent, unsure how to respond. She wasn't into women, but the Soul Sealing Spell was a fate far worse than marrying Dorothea.

Sensing her hesitation, Dorothea blinked innocently. “You know, breaking a Soul Sealing Spell is a huge pain. I’ll have to call in a favor, and it’s going to cost me a lot. It’s not that big of an ask, is it?”

Zane just gave a slight shake of his head and kept his mouth shut. Whatever was going on between those two was none of his business.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL ORCHESTRAL]

He was finally out of the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. He had the Ancestral Dragon, and more importantly, what he needed to save Tia. First, he’d go back to the Heartlands and wake her up. Then, he had to get this crew to the main continent to find the Tower of Sin.

He wasn’t sure what Brianna and Dorothea were hashing out, but it must have ended well, because Dorothea flashed a deeply satisfied smile. It looked like they’d struck a deal.

Vorn turned to Zane, his expression all business. “We shouldn’t stick around. You know where the Tower is. Take us there now.”
