Ep 536 - The Chasm
Word count: 1189
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL GARDEN]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
The Cobalt Dragon had a sharp eye. So when he said the herbs weren't worth picking, Zane didn't think twice and headed straight for the center of the garden.
A few of the elders were frantically scooping up herbs, and neither Elder Imogen nor Tia made a move to stop them.
Sure, Cloud Hall had its own herb garden, but the quality of this stuff was on another level. The elders had risked their lives coming to the Celestial Tomb of Vesper, desperate for a piece of the fortune. It was only fair Tia let them have at it.
The Cloud Hall disciples, meanwhile, looked like they’d hit the jackpot.
The herbs they were gathering were considered top-tier even back home, worth a small fortune. Even Spirit-Tribulation Realm elders would kill for them, so it was no wonder the Inner-Core disciples were ecstatic.
They didn't dare venture into the center of the garden with the elders, but they were more than happy to stick to the edges.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON SOIL]
Zane walked to the garden's center. The elders glanced at him but paid little attention.
At his feet lay a palm-sized patch of brown earth among the black soil.
"Is this the 'Swelling Earth' you mentioned?" Zane asked.
"Yes. Dig it out," the Cobalt Dragon said.
Zane drew his sword and cut four quick lines, then pried the clump free. It was surprisingly heavy. As it emerged, the earth condensed into a small, dense ball with a metallic sheen—neither metal nor soil.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
"What's that?" Emanuel's eyes sharpened. He crossed the garden and reached for it.
The other elders looked up, uncertain but intrigued.
Tia remained silent. Whatever was found belonged to the finder.
Emanuel's hand shot toward the ball in Zane's palm.
Zane flipped his hand back and stepped away. The Swelling Earth vanished into his Storage Ring.
"What are you doing?" he asked.
Emanuel smiled. "Nothing. Just curious."
Zane said nothing. Emanuel had gambled that Tia wouldn't intervene over something trivial and that Zane wouldn't react quickly enough.
He'd miscalculated.
Zane scanned for other treasures while behind him, the group stripped the garden bare—every herb, down to the roots, until only black soil remained.
When nothing was left, they moved on.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
As they walked, Zane found his thoughts drifting back to the word carved at the garden entrance. Rites. It had seemed strange at the time — what did a herb garden have to do with rites? But now, connecting it to everything else: the nine lamps, the passage, the corpses that weren't quite dangerous enough, the garden that seemed almost generous — it started to feel like a pattern.
It felt like Vesper himself was baiting people to come and take his stuff. Zane couldn't shake the creepy feeling of it all; he had no clue what Vesper's game was. Still, he was already here. There was nothing to do but press on.
[AMBIENT: ECHOING CHAMBER]
[SFX: HEAVY DOOR CREAK]
A few moments after leaving the garden, they came to a massive door. It was a strange sight: embedded in its surface were dozens of smaller doors, all laid out in rows. Zane took a closer look and counted around forty-five of them.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]
"Why are there so many little doors?" Tia asked, her brow furrowed as she stared at the wall.
"We should go through the same one," Saint Morphen suggested.
In a place like this, splitting up was a death wish. He was right—sticking together was the only smart move.
[SFX: DOOR SLAM]
Tia hesitated for only a second before pulling open one of the small doors. The instant she stepped through, it slammed shut.
"What the hell was that?" Zane and Elder Imogen frowned, caught completely off guard.
"Maybe it's one person per door?" Zane guessed. He reached out and pushed, but it wouldn't budge.
Elder Imogen jabbed her walking stick at the door, trying to force it open.
There was a dull thud, but the door didn't even have a scratch on it.
They all exchanged horrified glances. Who knew what Tia was facing on the other side?
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane’s expression darkened. He reached for the handle on the door next to Tia's and yanked it open. The moment he stepped inside, a powerful force ripped him forward. He fought against it, but the pull was too strong—there was no turning back.
[SFX: HEAVY DOOR SLAM]
The door slammed shut behind him.
So that's why Tia's door shut, he realized. Once you went through, there was no going back.
[AMBIENT: DARK TUNNEL]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
Zane found himself in a pitch-black tunnel. The only thing he could see was a single point of light shining up ahead.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
"Might as well see this through," Zane muttered. He walked toward the light, his eyes fixed on the end of the passage. Soon, he emerged from the tunnel onto a stone platform, about ten feet long and five feet wide.
"Zane!" Tia’s voice called out from his left.
Relief washed over him. She was safe, standing on a platform right next to his.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER SHIMMER]
Only a couple of feet separated them—an easy step. But when Zane moved forward, an emerald barrier shimmered into existence, blocking him.
Tia extended her fingers across the platform's edge. The barrier rose to meet them. "There's a barrier around each platform," she said.
Zane stepped back, taking in the full picture.
[AMBIENT: VAST ABYSS]
They stood on the edge of a massive ring-shaped abyss—so deep he couldn't see the bottom, just descending layers of darkness. Around the rim, nine large platforms jutted out, each lined with forty or fifty smaller platforms. Emerald barriers enclosed every single one.
Nine platforms. Nine lamps. No coincidence.
People were still emerging from dark tunnels across the abyss. One group had already filled thirty-four platforms.
"The Ethereal Spirit Sect," someone murmured behind him.
On the far side, the Sect's leader—a lean man with a calm demeanor—caught sight of Elder Imogen and smiled.
"Elder Imogen. I didn't expect Cloud Hall to possess one of the lamps."
Elder Imogen smiled back. "I could say the same about the Ethereal Spirit Sect."
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
In the Heartlands, people had speculated for centuries about who held the nine lamps. Now, at last, they were all in the same room.
[AMBIENT: ECHOING CHASM]
"Zane!" a playful voice called out from where the Ethereal Spirit Sect members were.
Zane turned toward the sound and managed a weak smile. It was Brent, the so-called saint-level genius from their Sect.
"You still owe me a fight!" His voice was young, but his tone was dead serious.
Zane just shrugged. "We'll see if we get the chance."
When Zane dropped out of the Martial Tournament at the last minute, Brent had been the most bummed out. He was constantly complaining that he never got his shot at Zane, even after they’d returned to the Ethereal Spirit Sect.
"I'm holding you to that!" Brent shot back, making a face.
"Mhm," Zane mumbled, not having the energy to deal with the kid right now.
Back at the Martial Tournament, Maddox had mentioned holding an Enchanted Lamp. If that was true, he should be showing up any second now.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
[SFX: DEEP VOICE ECHO]
And right on cue, a powerful voice echoed through the chasm. "It seems we meet again, boy."
