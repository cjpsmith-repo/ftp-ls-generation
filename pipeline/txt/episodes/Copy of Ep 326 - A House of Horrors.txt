Ep 326 - A House of Horrors

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]
Elara glanced at Grey’s body sprawled on the ground, her expression cold and empty of remorse. Without hesitation, she followed her brother Zane. Though she had abandoned House Lowell years ago and had no connection to the dying man, her faith in her brother’s judgment was absolute. In her mind, if Zane killed him, he must have deserved it.
Tristan lingered for a moment, his face contorted with years of pent-up hatred. He spat on Grey’s face, the gesture carrying the weight of countless humiliations, before hurrying after Zane with newfound loyalty shining in his eyes.
[SFX: LAST BREATH]
Grey’s body twitched violently one last time before falling eerily still, his vacant eyes staring up at the sky.
[SFX: ETHEREAL VAPOR]
A sinister, dark vapor seeped from the corpse, writhing like a living shadow as it drifted toward Zane. This was Malice—the cursed essence born from a soul’s desperate, furious refusal to embrace death.
The potency of Malice was a direct reflection on the strength of its source. A mediocre person like Grey produced merely a wisp of the dark energy, barely visible in the morning light, yet Zane tracked its movement with hungry eyes.
Zane wouldn’t even let this meager offering go to waste. Every fragment of Malice he absorbed made him stronger. Today marked the beginning of his vengeance—he would purge House Lowell of his enemies through merciless slaughter, leaving no one who opposed him standing.
His father, Milo, had extended olive branches where he should have wielded swords. That fatal compassion toward his brothers had cost him everything. In the end, Bryson and Ken repaid his kindness with betrayal, orchestrating his brutal murder with calculated precision.
Milo had foolishly attempted to answer evil with kindness, and his reward had been a painful, premature death that haunted Zane’s dreams.
Standing now at the threshold of his ancestral home, Zane’s eyes hardened with resolve. The vow he had sworn in the darkness of his imprisonment burned brighter than ever—he would never make his father’s fatal mistake.
[AMBIENT: LARGE MANOR HOUSE]
[MUSIC: TENSE BUILDUP]
As they stepped into Lowell Manor, the wide muster station spread out before them.
The last time Zane stood on this muster station, he’d been forced to fight his cousin Andrew just for a chance to leave. Even after he won, his uncle Ken broke his promise and refused to let him go.
A group of men blocked their path, swords drawn. They hadn't heard the exchange at the gate — only that someone had killed the housekeeper.
Zane glanced at Tristan. "Who are they?"
"Grey's hired muscle," Tristan said. "Brought in to keep the family in line."
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
Zane moved through them without breaking stride. Strength Level, Bone Refining — none of them were a match. They dropped one by one. The Lowell disciples watching from a distance went pale, convinced they were witnessing a massacre.
"Don't panic!" Tristan called out. "This is Zane Lowell — our rightful Patriarch!"
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
The commotion at the gate had already drawn a crowd.
"He’s the rightful leader of our house! He’s the son of our former leader, Milo! The eldest son of the main branch! With Milo gone, leadership passes to Zane. He has to lead us now!"
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT RISE]
The members of House Lowell stared, first in shock, then in a dawning, thrilling realization. The terror that had gripped them moments ago melted away, replaced by a surge of pure joy.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT RETURN]
Zane was back, and he was powerful. House Lowell wouldn’t be destroyed.
Zane would save them.
Under the current leader, Bryson, House Lowell had been slowly rotting from the inside, overrun by thugs he let run wild. But they were all born of this house; none of them wanted to watch their family crumble.
Their roots were here, even if those roots were rotten. They had no choice but to swallow their pain and watch House Lowell decay, knowing it was doomed.
But now, they understood.
Zane's return wasn't just a return—it was salvation. He was the light they had been desperate for.
[AMBIENT: EMOTIONAL GATHERING]
One by one, they rushed toward Zane, gathering around him. Tears welled in their eyes, their gratitude too deep for words. Hope, real and overwhelming, had finally returned to their home.
[SFX: SURPRISED MURMURS]
"Hey! What about me? You all rush to my brother and forget I’m even here?" Elara asked from behind them. She pouted, blinking innocently.
"Brother?" one of them repeated, turning to get a better look at her.
His eyes went wide. "My God... it's Elara!"
"Elara is back, too!" someone shouted.
"What? I thought Perrin said she was locked away in Infernal Peak."
[AMBIENT: DARK OMINOUS]
No one knew the full details of Infernal Peak, only that it was a place of pure misery—a prison where disciples of the Skyreach Sect were sent to suffer endless, inhuman torture.
Elara had once been the pride of House Lowell, the most talented of her generation. Her acceptance into the Skyreach Sect had been a moment of glory for the entire family.
Later, they'd heard she was punished and imprisoned. No one had heard a word about her since, or what horrors she had endured.
And now, impossibly, she was back.
Her strength felt profound, an aura so powerful they couldn’t begin to measure it. It seemed a million times stronger than Bryson’s.
Because Zane, now a Level Three Adept, was concealing his true aura, the family members mistakenly thought his strength was merely on par with Bryson's—maybe slightly stronger, but not by much.
[AMBIENT: DUNGEON]
[SFX: PUNCH TO GUT]
[SFX: GRUNT OF PAIN]
Beneath the house, Miles, a son from the Second Branch of House Lowell, drove his fist into Melvin's gut. Melvin had made too many enemies.
Framed by rivals, he'd been punished, thrown in the dungeon, and reduced to a living training dummy for the family’s fighters.
With Perrin stripped of his title as young leader, the position had fallen to his younger brother, Miles.
A young man in a yellow robe stood nearby, smirking at the spectacle.
"Having fun, Miles?" he drawled. "Looks interesting. Mind if I take a turn?"
"Be my guest, Darcy," Miles said with a grin, stepping back.
"I’m just worried I might kill him with one punch. Then what?" Darcy asked, feigning concern.
[SFX: CRUEL LAUGHTER]
"Don’t worry about that," Miles laughed, shooting a contemptuous look at Melvin. "They’re just training tools. No one cares if one of them dies. Punch as hard as you want. No one will give you any trouble, I promise. Ha!"
Melvin, bruised and bloodied, glared up from the floor. "Miles!" he spat. "I may be a training tool, but I'm here for House Lowell members.
He's an outsider. What right does he have to lay a hand on me?"
Miles and Darcy looked at the bleeding boy with amused surprise.
Talking back to them like that - did this kid have a death wish?
If so, they’d be happy to oblige.
