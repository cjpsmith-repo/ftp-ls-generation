Ep 272 - Girlfight
Word Count: 1262
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA CROWD]
When Tracy and Jill stood in the battle ring, lots of guys watching—not merely Bastian—ran their mouths voicing their crude opinions. It was a sad but true stereotype - most male disciples from the Thirty-Three Peaks perpetuated this behavior, their disparaging remarks reflecting a deep-seated belief in female inferiority.
[MUSIC: DEVIOUS PLOTTING]
But Tracy’s attack on Bastian went far beyond his gross remarks. She deliberately targeted him, knowing Zane would intervene. His chivalrous nature was as predictable as it was admirable.
With each match, Zane’s performance in the arena grew more impressive, and so did Tracy’s fascination with him. The memory of their first encounter, when she had merely punctured his defenses twice, now seemed painfully inadequate as a means of connection. This realization prompted her to devise a more elaborate plan—one that might bring them into closer proximity.
"If I actually manage to defeat Zane," Tracy wondered as she strode purposefully across the grounds, her lips curving into a slight smile, "how should I punish him?" The thought sent an unexpected thrill through her, one that mingled competitive ambition with something more personal than she cared to admit.
Technically, the rules said that if a fighter left the ring for no reason, the refs were supposed to declare them the loser. But the Skyreach Sect always had a soft spot for its elite disciples, especially one like Tracy. Besides, her family was powerful. The referees weren’t about to risk humiliating her. Getting on Tracy’s bad side was just asking for trouble. So, they pretended they didn’t see a thing.
Jill had been waiting in the ring the whole time. She was a sweet girl who rarely lost her temper and was usually kind to everyone. She’d been offended when the guys were running their mouths earlier, but it just wasn’t in her nature to come up with some cruel revenge plot like Tracy.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
Tracy walked right past Jill, shooting her opponent a bored look. She shook her head and said confidently, "You’re talented, and you’re strong, but you’re not in my league."
"Why?" Jill lifted her chin, a spark of defiance in her eyes.
"I can tell just by looking at you. You’re not ruthless enough—especially when it comes to men," Tracy said, a faint smile playing on her lips. "And if you can’t be ruthless, you’re no match for me."
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
Jill raised her eyebrows. "You think I’m no match for you? I might not be ruthless with men, but that doesn’t mean I can’t be ruthless with women."
"You can be ruthless with women?" Tracy repeated, her smile widening. "I don’t believe you."
"Stick around and find out," Jill shot back. As she spoke, she casually flipped the delicate Emerald Sword in her hand. Jill’s fighting style was all about agility and speed. Her sword wasn’t meant to be lethal, but she could use it to break through an opponent’s defense in a heartbeat. She’d won almost every match by pressing her Emerald Sword against her opponent’s neck.
But now, facing a powerful elite like Tracy, Jill knew she couldn’t hold back. She had to give it everything she had.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Without warning, Jill sprang forward, unleashing a ferocious assault with her Emerald Sword. The delicate weapon flashed green as it cut through the air with deadly precision. Her eyes narrowed with determination, her normally pleasant features hardening into a mask of competitive intensity.
The sudden shift in her demeanor caught several observers by surprise—the playful banter of moments ago had vanished, replaced by the calculated movements of a seasoned fighter who understood exactly what was at stake in this match. Tracy’s eyes widened slightly, her body tensing as she recognized the genuine threat approaching with alarming speed.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
"Sword of the Petal Maiden!" Jill cried out.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SWIRLING]
She lunged forward, brandishing her sword. Instantly, streaks of Vitality condensed into a flurry of flowers that swirled around her blade. Taking a deep breath, Jill moved like a blur, heading straight for Tracy.
[SFX: RAPID SWORD STRIKES]
In a brilliant flash, a hundred strikes from Jill’s sword hurtled toward Tracy, each blade trailing ethereal flower petals that shimmered in the arena light.
Jill, known throughout Misty Peak as a serious and methodical fighter, had poured countless solitary hours into perfecting this technique. The crowd gasped collectively, their voices rising in astonishment. Several senior disciples exchanged meaningful glances—to think she had mastered such an impressive level of swordplay at her age!
The audience’s excited murmurs swelled into a crescendo of speculation about the match’s outcome, with passionate debates breaking out among supporters of both combatants. Even some of the elites paused their preparations to observe the technique.
[SFX: RAPID STRIKES]
In a single flash, a hundred strikes from Jill’s sword flew toward Tracy.
Jill was a serious girl—not just talented, but incredibly dedicated. The crowd gasped. To think she could reach such an impressive level!
[SFX: WEAPONS CLASHING — RAPID]
Tracy's Stiletto Hairpins met the Emerald Sword in a blur. The two women traded dozens of blows each pass — speed against speed — too fast for most of the crowd to follow. The male disciples who'd been running their mouths earlier watched in silence.
The fight stretched on. Then a sharp crack — the Emerald Sword hit the ground. Tracy's hairpin rested against Jill's forehead.
[SFX: SWORD HITTING GROUND]
"Not convinced?" Tracy smiled. She drew one finger across Jill's cheek. "I'll give you another chance. But if you lose again, I will decide the punishment."
Jill turned to the referee. "I forfeit."
Tracy withdrew her hairpin, looking faintly disappointed. "Was that really necessary?"
[AMBIENT: CROWD CHEERS]
[MUSIC: TRANSITION]
Once the excitement over Tracy’s victory died down, most disciples turned their attention to the next two matches.
First up was Finn versus an elite disciple named Jaden. The second match pitted two more elites against each other: Theo and Barnaby.
In his last match, Finn hadn’t even bothered to draw his sword. Everyone was shocked by his arrogance, and it had totally infuriated Jaden. As an elite disciple, he’d never been insulted like that before.
But in the end, Jaden lost. The result just proved Finn had been right not to draw his sword in the first place.
The second match between the elites, Theo and Barnaby, was just as wild. They nearly destroyed half the battle ring. In the end, Barnaby managed to get a slight edge on Theo and took the win.
These two fights were basically the highest level of competition you could see among the Sect’s disciples. Jaden, Theo, and Barnaby were all skilled enough to make the top ten on the Skyreach Sect List.
The skill gap between disciples in the top hundred and the top fifty wasn’t that big. But the gap between the top thirty and the top fifteen? That was huge. And for the disciples who actually cracked the top ten, it was almost impossible to climb any higher. Their opponents were rare geniuses, each with their own insane set of skills.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ANNOUNCEMENT]
"For the next match," the referee’s voice boomed, "Zane from Misty Peak will fight the personal disciple Johanson!"
[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD MURMURS]
One of the disciples shouted excitedly. "Oh my god, Johanson! They say he’s good enough to crack the top five on the Sect’s list. He’s an elite disciple under Saint Augustus himself!"
His friend agreed. "I don’t know how Zane’s gonna handle him. This match is going to be insane! Wow, this is getting good. I gotta say, this year’s All Peaks Competition is next level!"
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSE]
Zane’s eyes narrowed when he overheard the disciples. Saint Augustus’ personal disciple? His expression hardened, turning wary.
This might be harder than he thought.
