Ep 1279 - The Stupid One

Word count: 1008

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT STONE HALL]
The hook had its own rules, and Zane knew he couldn’t break them. It was just like the boulders back in the Second Hall—they were nothing but ordinary stone, but because they were bound by one of the Celestial Laws, he hadn’t been able to budge them an inch, even with all his strength. 
In this universe, only the Supreme Lord was powerful enough to defy those laws, and Zane was nowhere near that level yet.
He trailed off, his gaze landing on Hailey. 
[SFX: BOW STRING TIGHTENING]
She stood quietly, a longbow in one hand and a hook in the other, her eyes fixed on the enemy with unwavering caution. 
Zane recognized the bow instantly. The Moonburst Flamebow. A slow grin spread across his face. 
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
“Maybe,” he murmured, an idea taking shape, “it’s my turn to be the stupid one.”
Coleman and Janet shot him doubtful looks. Even Ansel, carefree as ever, seemed to snap out of his daze.
“You want to let them hook you?” he grunted, confused.
Everyone remembered what had happened to Damon. It was a brutal lesson. If Zane got hooked, he’d end up just like him—yanked around like a rag doll and slaughtered.
“Maybe!” Zane said with a shrug, sending his voice across the three-thousand-foot gap with a pulse of Chaos Energy. 
[MUSIC: ANXIOUS STRINGS]
The standoff dragged on, and the warriors trapped in the hall were getting anxious. Everyone here was good enough to rank in the top three hundred of the Upper World, but the Illusion Battlefield was a brutal competition. While they were stuck, other top-ranked fighters were out there racking up Illusion Points like crazy. Every minute they wasted here meant their own rankings were slipping. You could almost feel the collective dread each time someone checked their status and saw they’d dropped another spot or two.
“Larkin, are we just going to wait forever?” someone on the other side finally cried out.
Larkin, who hadn’t moved from his spot, just smiled. “And why not?” he asked, his gaze fixed on the swirling mist that separated them. “They’re probably more antsy than we are. Sooner or later, they’ll lose their nerve.”
But truth be told, he wasn’t so sure. He wasn’t worried about his own skill, but he couldn’t shake the feeling that this was all a performance. Damon had tried to taunt Zane with words; now, Zane was baiting *him* with actions.
Before anyone on the other side could react, Zane stepped clear out of the fog entirely—no cover, no hesitation—and stood in plain sight of all nine remaining warriors.
[SFX: STARTLED MURMURS]
Chaos broke out in the darkness across the ravine.
“He’s out of the fog—is he trying to pull a Damon on us?”
“Is he looking to die? He can’t be as strong as Damon was!”
“So what if he is? Hook him over, and we’ll play with him just the same!”
“Let me do it!”
Someone lunged for their hook, eager to throw.
“Wait,” Larkin said quietly, and the warrior stopped cold.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Larkin studied Zane through the gloom, frowning. This man had already killed Damon—he clearly understood exactly how the hooks worked. Someone that reckless was either a fool, or he had a plan.
“You’re the one who spoke earlier,” Larkin called out. “Damon—was that your kill?”
“It was,” Zane answered evenly. “Something wrong with that?”
“You’re awfully willing to show yourself. Not afraid of dying?”
“I’m not planning on it,” Zane said. “Why would I be?”
Larkin’s expression flickered with something like hesitation. If the man was willing to stand out here in the open, he had to be working an angle—but what?
Not taking the bait, Zane thought, a flicker of frustration under his calm smile.
[MUSIC: RISING AGGRESSIVE PERCUSSION]
“Since you won’t come to me,” he called out, “I’ll come to you.”
[SFX: CHAIN WHIPPING]
The hook shot forward, aimed straight at Larkin’s blurred outline in the dark.
Larkin’s reflexes weren’t slow. He shifted only two feet—just enough—and let it pass. It was almost lazy, a display of confidence more than urgency.
But Zane wasn’t actually trying to land it. This was the exact same play Damon had run on him earlier—just wordless. Where Damon had needled him with taunts, Zane was doing it with steel.
Zane didn’t even pause when his first hook missed, his eyes already locked on the blurry figure in the mist. 
[SFX: CHAIN WHIPPING]
The hook in his hand shot out again.
[SFX: HOOK CUTTING AIR]
As it flew toward Larkin, the figure sidestepped, letting it sail past.
Once.
Twice.
A third time.
[SFX: REPEATED CHAIN WHIPS]
Again and again, Zane cast his hook, and again and again, it was useless. But a faint, patient smile never left his face.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS LOW DRONE]
Hidden in the darkness, Larkin’s expression finally soured.
[SFX: BLADE SLICE]
[SFX: CHAIN WHIPPING THROUGH AIR]
As he dodged another one of Zane’s hooks, he threw his own, aiming it straight for Zane’s chest.
*Finally!*
Zane’s eyes flashed. This was the moment he’d been waiting for. 
[SFX: CHAIN STOPPING ABRUPTLY]
[SFX: CHAIN YANKED BACK]
He smiled as the enemy hook rushed toward him, only for it to stop dead in the air just a few feet away. Larkin grabbed the end of the chain and yanked it back.
[SFX: CHAIN RETRACTING]
As the hook retracted into the mist, Zane’s smile turned sly. “What’s wrong? Why’d you pull it back?”
Silence. 
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Then, a sharp presence emerged from the gloom. It was Larkin.
“Larkin, why didn’t you do it?” one of his warriors demanded.
“He would’ve been dead if you’d hooked him. Why did you hesitate?” Another sounded completely baffled.
Larkin slowly stood up and looked Zane up and down. 
A gut-wrenching feeling had washed over him the moment he’d raised his hook, and he’d pulled it back on pure instinct. 
The truth was, Damon hadn’t been weak—second only to Larkin himself among all ten of them. Yet he’d been toyed with and killed without landing a single blow. That alone was enough to make Larkin’s instincts scream that something here was very wrong.
But the warriors around him were done waiting. 
[SFX: HOOK WHIPPING TOWARD TARGET]
Before Larkin could stop him, one of them let out a frustrated yell and whipped their own hook straight at Zane.
