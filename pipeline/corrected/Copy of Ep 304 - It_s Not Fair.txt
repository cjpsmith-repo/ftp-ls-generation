Ep 304 - It’s Not Fair
Word count: 1181
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS INTRODUCTION]
Lily was anything but ordinary. She was downright stunning.
Her hair was impossibly shiny, with cropped, angled bangs that framed perfectly symmetrical features. Right now, her expression was neutral, but there was no doubt that her smile would be captivating.
Her outfit was nothing like the usual fashion in the Eastern Territories. Compared to the modest styles women wore in those regions, her clothes were far more revealing and bold, showcasing her sleek and strong figure. Even though she and Zane were about the same height, the way she carried herself made her seem taller.
There was no denying it: Lily was a perfect copy of the woman from the Demon Night. Her sudden arrival had left nearly everyone in the crowd stunned. Instead of trying to guess who this beautiful woman was, they were all just wondering where in the world she had come from.
[AMBIENT: CROWDED HALL]
[MUSIC: SCENE TRANSITION]
Plenty of spectators were watching the tournament from outside the arena, too. On Maiden’s Peak, for example, a large Scrying Slate had been placed in the middle of the main hall.
Typically, the female disciples of the Skyreach Sect were more particular about their surroundings than the men. A perfect example was on Misty Peak, where the male disciples had just plopped their Scrying Slate down on the dusty muster grounds, unconcerned by the dirt as they all gathered around to watch the live broadcast.
The female disciples on Maiden’s Peak, on the other hand, had cleaned their hall spotless before setting up their Scrying Slate.
[SFX: MURMURING CROWD]
"Who is she? And why is she standing next to Zane?" Helen was bursting with questions, but it was obvious no one around her had any answers.
On Misty Peak, Ingrid had been watching Zane's every move throughout the competition. She'd teased him, doubted him, and quietly cheered for him — but this was something else entirely. The gap she'd sensed growing between them was one thing. Seeing a stunning woman materialize beside him out of thin air was another.
"Who is that woman?" she murmured. "How did she just appear next to him like that?"
No one around her had an answer.
And she wasn’t the only one. In fact, almost no one could figure out what was going on. The question on everyone’s mind was, "Who in the world is that girl? How did she just appear out of thin air, without a single sound or warning?"
Even some of the Guardians present couldn’t make sense of it.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
"A sword spirit! A sword spirit with a physical body!" Sir Kenneth muttered, his eyes glued to the woman in the arena. "Master Elwood, don’t tell me Zane just manifested that sword spirit! And please don’t tell me it was just formed!"
Hearing Kenneth, Elwood was left speechless and dejected. He had no idea what to say, so he didn’t answer directly. In his head, though, he was fuming. *What am I supposed to tell you? Can’t you see for yourself?
You know a swordsman has to fully grasp sword intent before they can even form a sword spirit. And it usually takes years for that spirit to take on a human form! Only the most incredible talents manage that, and even then, only after a long time. Some of the greatest swordsmen of the past mastered their sword intent completely but never managed to give their sword spirits a physical form.* On top of all that, destiny played a huge part.
And Zane’s luck was just too damn good. Even Master Elwood felt a sting of jealousy. Normally, as a high-level master known for his unshakable calm, he could take any surprise in stride. People joked he wouldn’t even blink if a mountain collapsed in front of him. But this time, Elwood couldn’t stay calm. Talent and luck this extraordinary would make anyone jealous.
[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]
Even someone as composed as Patrick was floored by Zane’s progress. His eyes widened, and the moment he saw the sword spirit, he shot up from his meditation. A cornerstone of the Monastic Path was maintaining a calm and level head. Patrick, who was renowned for his emotional control and considered a remarkable practitioner, couldn’t hold on to his composure now.
He felt like a monk who had spent his entire life chanting scriptures and living a disciplined life—waking to the morning bell, ending his day with the evening drum, and ignoring all worldly things like wealth, relationships, and love. His path was everything. He was devout, praying to one day earn his inheritance. But then one day, a young new monk arrives at the temple and, in no time at all, finds enlightenment and receives the ultimate blessing.
Faced with a situation like this, all Patrick could do was laugh bitterly and curse to himself, Damn it. This is so unfair.
[AMBIENT: EXCITED MURMURS]
Meanwhile, Tracy was bombarding Patrick with a non-stop barrage of questions. "Patrick, what in the world is that? I have a feeling she’s not a real person! How did she just show up out of nowhere?"
Thanks to her Blue Phoenix’s Blood, Tracy’s instincts were screaming that Lily wasn’t human—that she was something else entirely. She could sense Lily wasn’t a living person, but she couldn’t figure out what she was.
"That’s a sword spirit!" Patrick snapped, annoyed by her endless questions.
"Oh, really?" Tracy pouted, sensing his irritation. She didn’t get why he was so upset. What was the big deal? It wasn’t like they’d never heard of a sword spirit before. Why would that make Patrick so short-tempered?
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAINTOP]
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
Finn could have gained so much more if he’d been able to practice his sword intent there on the mountain. But he never had a choice. It was all up to fate. The sacred sites always chose the person, not the other way around. And he just hadn’t qualified.
"You’re just on another level, Zane," Finn murmured, a resigned look on his face. "I used to think I wasn’t as strong as Patrick, but I always believed I could catch up to him someday. But you... you’re the first person I’m not sure I can ever reach."
"Still," he continued, "the world is full of more talented people than I can even imagine. Maybe I’m considered a genius in the Eastern Territories, but I’m nothing in the Heartlands! I still want to catch up to you, though. Even if I can’t keep pace, just chasing after you will make me stronger!"
If he couldn’t match a genius, he could at least follow in their footsteps.
It was easy to see that Finn had a good head on his shoulders. At least he wasn’t letting jealousy drive him crazy or forge an inner demon that would block his path to improvement.

"So, even though I know I can’t win, I’m still going to finish this fight! I’m going to stick to my path and never give up!" After saying that, he raised his Blood-Corroding Sword. He summoned his Vitality, and as he unleashed the Apex of Sword Intent, thunder and lightning crackled around the blade.
And with that, he lunged at Zane.
