After a few more violent rolls, they lurched to a stop. A manic spark lit Evil Jace's eyes as he leaned in close, breathing in Zane's scent like it was a rare perfume. "I've never encountered a body as... special as yours," he murmured, his voice a low hum of excitement. "It's unbelievable. Your scent... it's almost identical to my mysterious weapon."
"Are you serious?" Zane shot back, fighting to keep his voice steady. "Look at me! I've got arms and legs. I'm flesh and blood, just like you. How am I anything but human? I'm useless for forging weapons, so you might as well let me go." It was unnerving how sharp Jace's nose was, that he could pick up on something so subtle. Still, Zane forced his face to remain a calm mask.
"Hahahaha! Let you go?" Evil Jace's wild cackle echoed in the metal drum. "Not a chance! Finding someone with a body like yours is a once-in-a-lifetime discovery! Just imagine what kind of powerful artifact I'll create when I toss you into my forge... I can't wait to find out!"

With that, Evil Jace shackled Zane's hands to the forge's interior, the heavy chains biting into his wrists. The madman's fingers worked with practiced efficiency, his eyes gleaming with anticipation before he scrambled out, leaving Zane alone with his mounting dread.
[SFX: FORGE BEING HOISTED]
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
Inside the metal beast, Zane lurched violently as Jace hoisted the forge onto his back and launched into a dead run. The pace exceeded their previous journey, the world outside becoming a disorienting blur. A cold sweat broke across Zane's forehead as the reality of his situation sank deeper into his bones.
At least now he was spared the suffocating stench of Jace's unwashed body and the worst of the bruising impacts. Stretching his neck to its limit, Zane managed to peer through a small opening in the Bronze Forge, desperate to track their path.
[SFX: CHAINS RATTLING]
He yanked at the chains binding him, muscles straining against the unyielding metal. Not even the slightest give. These weren't ordinary restraints—too strong to be simple gold or iron, likely forged with some cultivation technique that made them nearly unbreakable.
Zane's mind raced through potential escape scenarios, each more implausible than the last. The terrifying truth settled over him like a shroud: this deranged weaponsmith hadn't shown a flicker of fear when confronting his Uncle. If someone with Bryson's formidable power couldn't intimidate Jace, what hope could Zane possibly have? Drawing a deep breath to calm his thundering heart, he decided that panicking would only drain the energy he'd need later. Better to conserve his strength and wait vigilantly for any opportunity, however slim.

The air grew noticeably cooler against Zane's skin, and daylight dimmed to a murky twilight, signaling their descent into the heart of these ancient woods.
[AMBIENT: OPEN CLEARING]

The world suddenly brightened as they burst through the dense tree line. Craning his neck until the muscles screamed in protest, Zane glimpsed a towering cliff face looming before them, growing alarmingly closer with each passing second. His heart leaped into his throat as he realized Jace showed no signs of slowing his relentless pace.
As they reached the precipice, the madman merely tightened his grip on the Bronze Forge, muscles coiling like springs beneath his tattered clothes.
[SFX: PRIMAL GRUNT]
With a primal grunt that sent chills down Zane's spine, Evil Jace hurled himself over the edge, launching them both into the terrifying emptiness beyond.
[SFX: FALLING WIND]
"Holy crap! He just jumped off a cliff! We're going to die!" Terror seized him, and he gripped the chains tighter.
[SFX: SUDDEN STOP]
Then, with a sudden, violent jerk, they stopped falling.
Clinging to the forge with one hand, Evil Jace used the other to scale down the mountain, grabbing onto a series of sharp, thorn-like rocks that jutted from the cliff face.
Halfway down, he landed on the sturdy, twisted trunk of a massive tree.
[SFX: BRANCHES MOVING]
With his free hand, he pushed aside a curtain of thick branches, revealing the dark maw of a cave.
[SFX: SCRAPING METAL]
The entrance was so narrow the Bronze Forge scraped against the rock as they squeezed through, but the passage quickly opened up into a much larger space. "No wonder the families from Castermere County could never catch him," Zane thought. "This place is impenetrable."

Ethereal flames licked upward from the earthen floor, transforming the cavern into a macabre natural smithy. Dominating the center stood a sinister, four-legged Weapons Forge, its dark metal surface reflecting the dancing firelight.
[SFX: DRIPPING BLOOD]
Along the distant wall, eight cruel spikes protruded outward, each one impaling a human victim whose lifeblood trickled steadily into collection troughs beneath. Zane's stomach twisted violently at the grotesque display of these emaciated, skeletal figures.
Though their appearance suggested death, the almost imperceptible rise and fall of their hollow chests revealed an impossible truth—they clung to life in this perpetual agony.
Scattered near the forge stood a dozen cast-iron cages, each barely three feet wide, confining a single prisoner. These unfortunate souls curled into themselves, trembling with fear or cold, their vacant eyes staring into nothingness as if their spirits had already departed their bodies.
The whispered rumors about Evil Jace had grossly understated the horror; this nightmarish reality exceeded even the darkest tales a thousandfold. Zane remained transfixed, his mind struggling to process such calculated brutality.
[SFX: HEAVY METAL CRASH]
The Bronze Forge crashed to the ground with a resonant thud that echoed throughout the cavern. Within moments, Evil Jace slithered back inside, his face uncomfortably close as he inhaled deeply above Zane's skin, nostrils flaring like a predator savoring its prey's scent.
"I've developed a fascinating theory," Evil Jace whispered, an unsettling grin stretching across his gaunt face. "When I forge weapons with human flesh and blood, I can permanently seal their grudges within the metal. All that concentrated hatred and suffering transforms the blade into something transcendent!"
His eyes gleamed with maniacal excitement as he leaned even closer, his fetid breath washing over Zane's face.
