Zane stood unwavering at the center of the Seven-Star Platform, his posture relaxed yet vigilant. The sword in his hand---Streamer---seemed almost an extension of his will rather than a physical object.
As each new wave of Arcane Aurora materialized before him, he dispatched it with fluid precision, his movements economical and deliberate. The air around him crackled with dissipating energy as severed waves dissolved into nothingness.
Unlike Zachary, whose exertion had left him collapsed and panting, Zane showed no signs of fatigue. His breathing remained steady, his focus unbroken, as if the increasingly powerful challenges posed merely an interesting puzzle rather than a grueling test of skill and endurance.
Instead of bailing after going out, which is what he'd usually do, Zachary lingered today, compelled by curiosity to witness the extent of Zane's capabilities. His pride stung from his own performance, but a grudging admiration kept him rooted to the spot. He had to see how far this guy could go.
Before them, waves of Arcane Aurora continued to materialize with increasing intensity, each formation more imposing than the one before it. But Zane cut each one down with unbelievable composure. His eyes betrayed no anxiety, only calculated focus.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[SFX: CRYSTAL SHATTERING]
The fiftieth wave manifested---a wall of energy that seemed almost solid in its density---yet Zane met it with the same unwavering precision.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY BLASTS]
The nonstop barrage of Arcane Aurora blasts was overwhelming to Zachary; his muscles tensed up as his mind calculated the impossibility of survival.
Zane, however, moved with fluid grace, his blade dancing through the air as he methodically dismantled each threat. It wasn't just skill on display - it seemed like the boundaries that limited ordinary cultivators simply didn't apply to him.
[MUSIC: TENSE DETERMINATION]
Yet even extraordinary talents face limitations. By the sixtieth wave, Zane was finally starting to look worn out. His movements were getting sluggish, his breaths coming in measured gasps.
"The Arcane Aurora only reacts to sword intent," Zane murmured, his brow furrowed. "Even with the sword radiance from the Streamer, I'm just not fast enough." This realization stung---it meant he hadn't truly captured the essence of sword intent, that elusive quality separating masters from mere practitioners.
This sword was the king of all weapons, its sharp blade capable of cutting through any obstacle, physical or mystical.
Yet true swordsmanship was more than mere physical prowess. The blade was a conduit for intent---that ineffable force which, when properly channeled, unleashed devastating power that defied ordinary limitations. Many cultivators spent decades pursuing this understanding, often falling short.
"How might I sharpen my intent?" Zane wondered, feeling sweat trickle down his temple as another wave approached.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
As the pressure intensified around him, Zane's focus narrowed to a singular point of perfect clarity. His consciousness distilled into one word, a single command that reverberated through every fiber of his being: "Break!"
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
With newfound conviction behind his swing, Zane's blade carved through three Arcane Aurora blasts simultaneously. Yet his momentary triumph offered little reprieve as more luminous projectiles already hurtled toward him from the distance, their brilliance promising even greater challenge.
[SFX: CRYSTAL SHATTERING]
With a powerful swing, he cut through three blasts of Arcane Aurora in an instant, but more were already hurtling toward him from a distance.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC SURGE]
Zachary watched, sure that Zane couldn't take much more. There were just too many of them. Still, to survive more than sixty waves on a first try... especially for someone only at the second level of the Adept Realm? That was unheard of. Zachary was sure no one in the entire history of the Skyreach Sect had ever pulled off something like this.
Zachary came from a noble family and had seen his fair share of prodigies, but he was certain about this: not even the most legendary geniuses could touch Zane's record.
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
After a flurry of desperate yet precise swings, Zane deflected the sixtieth wave, though one errant blast veered dangerously close to the crystal ball, missing it by mere inches.
Zachary leaned forward, a smirk playing across his lips as he calculated the inevitable failure. At this punishing intensity, he estimated Zane wouldn't survive beyond the sixty-third wave---no one ever had at his cultivation level.
"Just a matter of time now," Zachary thought, settling back with arms crossed, savoring the anticipated downfall of this upstart who had so thoroughly outshined him.
Yet with each passing moment, Zachary's satisfaction curdled into disbelief. Wave after wave, Zane not only endured but seemed to find a rhythm, his movements becoming more fluid, more purposeful. The sword in his hand appeared to dance between the energy blasts, guided by an intuition that defied explanation.
Against all conceivable odds, against everything Zachary understood about cultivation and natural talent, against the very limits of what should have been possible---Zane reached the one-hundredth wave, his face a mask of serene concentration that belied the extraordinary feat he was accomplishing.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[SFX: CRYSTAL SHATTERING]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PAUSE]
After the one-hundredth wave of Arcane Aurora, everything on the platform went still.
"Huh? Is one hundred the limit?" Zane asked, surprised.
Zachary felt like he should be numb to the shock by now, but this... this was something else. He nodded, his voice trembling slightly. "The limit for Level Eight is one hundred waves. But... practically no one ever reaches it."
Every swordsman strives to perfect their sword intent. That's why Zachary always trained on Level Eight---it was the perfect challenge for him. Fending off forty, maybe fifty blasts was a good day's work. After that, he'd feel like he'd earned the right to move on. And this guy just cleared a hundred. On his first try.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS ENTRANCE]
[SFX: CHIME]
Meanwhile, at the entrance to the training grounds, the steward jumped as a loud chime echoed from the Magic Array Plate behind him.
"That chime... no way," the steward muttered. "Don't tell me someone actually held off all one hundred waves."
Intrigued, the steward considered who it could be. It was rare for anyone to be that persistent. The true masters could clear lower levels easily enough, but why would they? They wouldn't waste their energy like that; they'd just move on to a higher difficulty.
When the steward picked up the Array Plate to check, he saw that someone had cleared all one hundred waves on Level Eight difficulty. He remembered that only two people had chosen Level Eight today: Zachary and a junior member named Zane.
Zachary practiced on The Seven-Star Platform constantly, and the steward was very familiar with his abilities. He knew Zachary usually tapped out around thirty blasts.
The steward's eyes widened in disbelief.
Could it have been that junior member... Zane?
