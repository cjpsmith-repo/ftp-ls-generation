Ep 741 - As You Wish
Word count: 1151
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
Beneath Wavecrest City, tens of thousands of martial artists from the Corrupted Divine Kingdom sat in disciplined rows, their uniformed bodies lost in meditation. The air itself seemed to hum as they drew on their Vitality, occasionally crushing low-grade Aether Crystals to absorb the raw energy before channeling it outward.
Above them, hundreds of Inner-Core Realm warriors streaked through the air. They moved in precise formations, weaving their combined Vitality into thick, pulsing beams of light that shot straight into the sky.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
Higher still, six senior warriors in green robes, each at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, manipulated those beams. They were painstakingly constructing a massive, shimmering rune in the heavens, and trapped at its very center was a single figure: Tia.
Under normal circumstances, no one in Wavecrest City could have touched her. An army would have been nothing; Tia was strong enough to have single-handedly slaughtered every last warrior they threw at her. But these weren't normal circumstances. The Corrupted Divine Kingdom had devised a strange and devastatingly effective new tactic, gathering the power of tens of thousands to fuel a celestial prison, which was then focused and controlled by their six strongest masters. And right now, Tia was their prey.
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
[SFX: CRACKLING POWER]
Even for a warrior of the Life-and-Death Realm, the shimmering sphere was unbreakable. She hammered against the rune again and again, but the faintest hint of exhaustion was already beginning to shadow her face. Each time she gathered her power, a terrifyingly cold energy erupted from her, causing the glowing cage to dim, but it never shattered. Still, not a single one of her captors dared to underestimate her.
[AMBIENT: WIND SWEEPING]
While this siege unfolded in the sky, Zane was making his way toward Wavecrest City. He was only five or six miles out when he stopped.
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
A moment later, Finn, Patrick, and Lewis pulled up alongside him. One look at the sky and their expressions collapsed.
"Master Tia —" Lewis started.
"The Palace Lord is trapped up there," Patrick said, his voice tight. "We need to get back to Oracle City and call for reinforcements."
"It's too late for that," Finn said, his jaw set.
Zane's eyes were already locked on the formation. He'd seen something like this before — the same principle behind the Trinity Killing Array he'd built for Cloud Hall. Small runes feeding a large one, labor divided, efficiency multiplied. Tens of thousands of warriors contributing Vitality they never had to spend on offense. It was elegant, and it was brutal. He turned to the others. "Don't engage. Not yet. If you rush in there, you're dead."
Patrick and Lewis exchanged a look but didn't argue. Finn closed his eyes.
[SFX: MAGICAL COMMUNICATION CHIME]
Zane slowly raised his message token. “Tia,” he said, his voice low. “I see you.”
His eyes were locked on her distant form, still trapped within the rune. He watched her narrowly dodge a gout of fire that erupted from the barrier, and saw her head snap in his direction as his voice reached her. Pain was etched across her features, but she said nothing, turning back to resume her assault on the cage.
“I know you heard me,” Zane said.
She had, of course. She just had no intention of answering him.
“You’re not going to talk to me? Fine,” he said, a hint of a joke in his tone. “I’m leaving, then.”
[AMBIENT: TENSE MAGICAL BATTLE]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]
Still no reply. He watched as she gathered her energy for another attack, but the rune held firm, not even flickering. Then, her strained voice finally came through the token. “Just leave. You aren’t a disciple of Cloud Hall anymore. Whether I live or die has nothing to do with you.”
Zane couldn’t help but sigh. So stubborn, he thought. A flicker of anger rose in him, but he forced it down, taking a slow breath. “I’m sorry, Tia.”
“Sorry for what?” she shot back, her voice utterly flat.
“For how I spoke to you,” he replied softly.
[SFX: CRACKLING FLAMES]
[SFX: ICE SHIELD FORMING]
Tia’s lips pressed into a thin line. The Vitality in her hands swirled into a blue curtain of frost and ice just as a raging inferno blasted out from the rune. The flames crashed against her shield, but the wall of ice held strong.
“There’s nothing to be sorry for,” she said, her voice as cold as the power she wielded. “Don’t men always do what’s right?”
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]
A wry smile touched Zane’s lips. She still couldn’t let go of what had happened with him and Cora. Even now, at a moment like this, she was dredging up old grudges. He was at a loss for words.
“I admit it, I was wrong,” Zane said, his voice laced with apology. “I am so, so sorry.”
“It’s too late for sorry, Zane,” she replied coldly.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL CRESCENDO]
“Then what will it take for you to forgive me?” he asked, his voice pleading.
“I’ll forgive you if you die,” she answered without a trace of emotion. The wall of frost around her began to melt, and she had to channel another stream of Vitality to reinforce it.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
“As you wish,” Zane replied with a sudden, unnerving smile. Without another word, he launched himself toward Wavecrest City.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
“What? Wait! Zane, no!”
Tia’s cry was sharp with panic as she watched him charge toward the city like a madman. Her eyes went wide. Are you insane? she screamed in her mind.
What Zane didn't know was that she still had options. The formation, though powerful, wasn't flawless. Tens of thousands of warriors contributing Vitality simultaneously created inevitable inconsistencies that even six Spirit-Tribulation masters couldn't entirely smooth. Given time, she could find a crack and slip through.
His reckless charge didn't help—it made everything worse. She could defend herself and eventually escape, but breaking through would take time.
[AMBIENT: HIGH ALTITUDE]
High above, Darius watched the newcomer with cold amusement. "Another fool flying straight into the cage," he murmured, a thin smile crossing his pale face.
He didn't know who Zane was. If he had, he wouldn't have acted so carelessly.
With precise gestures, he redirected half the formation. Three Spirit-Tribulation masters peeled away from Tia and turned toward Zane, thousands of warriors' Vitality streams shifting with them. The pressure on Tia dropped sharply—her prison walls suddenly felt thinner.
[SFX: CRACKLING LIGHTNING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]
With a deafening sizzle, the combined Vitality of tens of thousands of warriors transformed into a massive, crackling net of blue lightning. It descended from the sky and wrapped around Zane, shrinking violently, unleashing a force far greater than any single Spirit-Tribulation warrior could muster.
[AMBIENT: DISTANT BATTLE]
Miles away, Finn’s eyes were shut tight as he frantically gathered his strength. He knew Zane was in mortal danger. The power of the Sword Eye was terrifying, but it took an agonizingly long time to activate. Patrick and Lewis stood guard at his sides, wanting to help but knowing they were hopelessly outmatched.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: CLIMACTIC ORCHESTRA]
The electric net tightened to its absolute limit and then exploded, consuming Zane in a ball of white-hot light.
