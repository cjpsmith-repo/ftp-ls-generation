Ep 650 - Don't Look Back
Word count: 1116
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The weeping that had begun as a distant echo was now close enough to feel.
That voice—a sound so terrifying it felt like ice water down their spines—made every warrior want to spin around. It was a primal, gut-level instinct, the one that screamed at you never to leave your back exposed to an enemy.
Just as the urge was about to overwhelm them, Yolande stared dead ahead and snapped, her voice like cracking ice, “Don’t turn around. If you do, you’re dead.”
Her warning hit like a slap. Zane, who had already started to glance over his shoulder, whipped his head forward again. Esther, Fabian, and Jarrod did the same, their eyes locked on the path ahead. But it was too late for one of the top-level fighters from the Demon Night. He’d already spun around to see what was behind them.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT]
Even without looking themselves, the others saw the man’s expression freeze. His eyes went wide, locking in place as if he’d just witnessed the impossible. Pure, unadulterated shock. He opened his mouth to scream, but no sound came out—only a faint, wet gurgle from the back of his throat.
These guys were seasoned veterans, warriors who had stared death in the face a thousand times. They’d waded through battlefields littered with corpses and killed countless enemies to earn their rank. What hadn’t they seen?
But in that one instant, this hardened fighter looked absolutely, utterly terrified.
A low whine escaped his lips before a strange, unnerving smile spread across his face. His eyes glazed over, the light draining out of them completely. Then came a sharp crack, like pottery shattering.
[SFX: SHARP CRACKS]
[SFX: BODY COLLAPSE]
A thin fracture line appeared on the warrior’s forehead. It spiderwebbed down his face, and within seconds, his entire body was covered in a web of cracks. With a sickening crunch, he simply crumbled, falling to the ground in a heap of broken pieces. His clothes, however, were left behind in a perfectly neat, empty pile.
Watching him fall apart, Fabian was shaking. “What the hell is back there?” he choked out.
Zane’s brow furrowed, his eyes narrowing. His own mind was a fortress, but even he could feel the immense, crushing pressure radiating from behind them.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION RISING]
From the corner of his eye, he caught it — a faint light, distant at first, but growing. Closing in.
"That thing is getting closer," Jarrod said, his voice barely holding together. He was a seven-foot wall of muscle and scar tissue. But right now, every inch of him was rigid.
Yolande said nothing. She was somewhere else — deep in a strange, focused stillness, her pale eyes locked forward. She was not afraid. Whatever was behind them, she was already turning it over in her mind, looking for the seam.
The light kept growing. The crying kept rising. And then — tentacles.
[SFX: SINISTER ENERGY]
They came slowly, drifting in from behind like smoke given form. Pale yellow, almost weightless, condensed from light rather than flesh. They touched faces, necks, arms — not with pain, but with something worse. Like cold gauze drawn across skin that shouldn't be touched.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane called out, his voice low. “Yolande, what’s the plan?”
“Do not look back,” she whispered, her voice a fierce, desperate warning. “No matter what happens, you cannot turn around.” She seemed a million miles away, almost in a trance.
Nobody would have dared to turn around now, not after that. The Demon Night warrior was no pushover; he was a damn sight stronger than Fabian.
But Fabian was losing the fight with himself.
His eyes had gone bloodshot. His hands were balled into fists hard enough to crack bone. The tentacles kept brushing at him, and under that pressure — that grinding, suffocating pressure — something in him was starting to break. He was about to spin around and fight. It was written all over him.
Zane saw it happening. There was no time. He had to do something to keep Fabian from turning around.
[SFX: SHARP CRACK]
A needle of pure soul force shot from between Zane's brows and drove straight into Fabian's mind.
[SFX: BODY THUD]
[SFX: AGONIZED SCREAM]
Fabian collapsed. He hit the ground, clutching his head, screaming.
Esther's head snapped toward Zane. She'd seen soul attacks before — simple ones, blunt instruments. But what she'd just witnessed wasn't blunt. And the soul behind it was wrong. Not wrong in a dangerous sense. Wrong in a way that made no sense at all.
Zane's soul carried a faint jade light. That meant it was already brushing against the War Soul Realm. Among peak War Lords, not a single one had crossed that threshold. Even most War Sages hadn't. And yet here was a ninth-level War General with a soul that was nearly there — nearly blue.
She filed it away. There was no time to be stunned.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
The sound seemed to snap Yolande out of her trance. Biting her lip so hard it could have drawn blood, she said, “It’s moving. It’s coming around to the front.”
“What do we do?” Jarrod asked frantically.
[MUSIC: URGENT DRUMS]
“Run,” Yolande said, her tone flat.
“Run where?” Esther cried.
Yolande’s lips were a thin, hard line. “Straight ahead. Just remember the rule.”
[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Zane glanced at the shadowy tentacles still swirling around his own body. “When?”
“Now,” Yolande yelled. “Go!”
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
Before the word was even fully out of her mouth, she was already sprinting, her feet barely touching the ground. Jarrod, Esther, and the other warriors took off right behind her. As Zane broke into a run, he veered toward Fabian, grabbing him by the collar and hauling him along. He’d been the one to pierce Fabian’s soul earlier, leaving the man lost in a world of agony, and he’d be damned if he just left him there to die. Thankfully, Zane was strong enough to carry him.
[SFX: HIGH-PITCHED WAIL]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CHASE]
As they fled, the sound behind them grew sharper, more shrill—a high-pitched wail that echoed through the desolate landscape. It was chasing them. And it was fast.
The rule seemed simple enough: don’t look back, and you won’t shatter into a thousand pieces. But just running blindly wasn’t a plan. How were they supposed to escape?
“Yolande, what now?” Zane shouted over the wind.
She was ten feet in the air, her focus absolute. “Just wait! I’ll handle it!” she yelled back.
“How long do we have to wait?” Esther screamed.
Yolande’s only reply was, “I don’t know.”
