Ep 679 - Fall Back

Word count: 1048

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Maurice let out a long sigh that seemed to age him decades in an instant. He wasn't even that old—by the continent's calendar, he was only sixty, practically a kid for a martial artist. But in that one moment, he looked ancient.

Zane stared him down, his voice like ice. "You still haven't answered me. Or is your memory that bad?"

[SFX: NERVOUS MURMURS]

A ripple of fear passed through the Ogre War Lords. Zane had sworn he’d kill every Ogre he found in the Tower of Sin. They exchanged panicked glances. Was he actually going to do it?

After a long, tense silence, Maurice finally spoke. "We'll leave now."

"Leave?" Zane gestured to the bodies littering the ground, a humorless smirk twisting his lips. "You think you can just slaughter our people and walk away?"

Maurice snorted. "What more could you want? Even with control of the Tower, you can't afford to make enemies of the Flaming Place and the Dread Place."

Zane let out a harsh, barking laugh, but his eyes were dead serious, burning with a promise of violence. "The Flaming Place and the Dread Place? You think I'm afraid of them?"

Just as Zane coiled to strike, Esther put a hand on his arm. "Zane, maybe we should let them go."

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

These weren't just any Ogres; they were the future of their people, warriors destined to become War Sages, maybe even masters. Killing them all would be a devastating blow to the Ogre's sacred home, an act that would guarantee all-out war.

Esther was thinking about the bigger picture. If Zane pushed the Flaming Place and the Dread Place too far, they would almost certainly retaliate and wipe the Violet Heart Sanctuary off the map.

[MUSIC: DARK THREATENING STRINGS]

"I know what you're thinking," Zane told her, his voice low and final. "But today, every last Ogre dies."

[SFX: WHOOSH]

With that, he launched himself off the stairs, blurring toward Maurice.

[SFX: BATTLE CRY]

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

"You dare?" one of the Ogre War Lords roared, unable to hold back any longer. He charged to intercept, a wave of raw power erupting from him.

[SFX: FLESH TEARING]

[SFX: BLOOD SPLATTER]

Zane barely glanced at him. He flicked a finger, and an invisible force wrapped around the charging Ogre. There was a sickening, wet rip, and the warrior screamed—a sound that was cut short as he was torn to shreds. Dark blood splattered across the stone.

Seeing that, Maurice didn't hesitate. "Fall back!" he bellowed at his War Lords. "Everyone — fourteenth floor, now!"

The Ogres stampeded for the stairs. Dozens of them, each the size of a small hill, thundering downward.

[SFX: STONE CRACK]

Zane was faster. He punched a crack through the ceiling, shot upward, and dropped back down directly in front of the stairway entrance to the fourteenth floor — blocking it before the first Ogre reached him.

[MUSIC: SLAUGHTER THEME]

They tried to push through anyway. The first one to reach him was torn apart before he could raise his hands. The second. The third. Corpses piled up on the steps, dark blood running down the stone. Zane walked forward slowly, stepping through the carnage, and the ones still standing turned and ran.

[AMBIENT: SCREAMING CHAOS]

He gave them nowhere to go. Using the Tower's power, he cut them down as they fled — screams echoing through the stairwell, remains scattered across every step. On the fourteenth floor, creatures who had once envied the Ogres stood watching, their faces white. For the first time, they were glad they hadn't been born into that race.

[MUSIC: EERIE QUIET]

Zane stepped back onto the top floor. Maurice was gone.

He scanned the room. "He went into one of the self-contained worlds?"

Yolande shook her head. "He went outside. Into the cave."

Zane's eyes widened slightly. "Into the Death Light?"

But he didn't stop moving. Maurice wasn't the type to throw himself into certain death without a plan. Whatever he had up his sleeve, Zane needed to find him.

He poured Vitality into the pendant. The orange barrier flared around him. Then he walked out into the Dark Nether Cave.

[AMBIENT: CAVE EXTERIOR]

[MUSIC: TENSE PURSUIT]

Yolande, Lavender, Esther, Fabian, and Jarrod followed close behind.

Outside, the Death Light fell in sheets. Zane circled the tower, eyes sharp. No sign of Maurice. He couldn't have gone far — no one could survive the Death Light for long without protection.

Zane stopped. He closed his eyes.

[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]

[MUSIC: SINISTER CHOIR]

"I'll offer your body as a sacrifice to the Tower!" Zane’s face was a mask of cold fury. He closed his eyes, his senses stretching out across the grounds. A moment later, a sneer touched his lips. He drew a Relic-Class sword from his Storage Ring and began walking slowly, deliberately, toward the back of the Tower.

[AMBIENT: EERIE QUIET]

Fabian, Esther, and the others exchanged a look. Had he really found something?

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

Suddenly, a burly figure stumbled out from behind the Tower of Sin. It was Maurice, who had been hiding there all along.

"Zane," he bellowed, his voice raw with desperation. "If you want me dead, you're coming with me!"

[SFX: MAGICAL FLARE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

A blinding light flared to life above his head. He was burning his spiritual altars.

While a mage might detonate their core to take an enemy down with them, a body refiner could sacrifice their spiritual altars for one last, terrifying surge of power. It was a one-way trip—once burned, they were gone forever. As a body refiner, Maurice had three, and the power now pulsing from him was immense. Zane remained unfazed, but the faces of Esther and the others went pale with horror.

[SFX: BATTLE CRY]

"Get him!" Esther yelled.

She, Fabian, Jarrod, Yolande, and Lavender all attacked at once. Normally, Esther could hold her own against Maurice, but his power had multiplied. He was strong enough to fend off all of their attacks by himself.

[SFX: RAPID PUNCHES]

[AMBIENT: BATTLE CHAOS]

"You're wasting your time! You can't stop me!" Maurice roared, unleashing a blinding volley of punches.

He batted their attacks aside like they were nothing, a whirlwind of unstoppable force.
