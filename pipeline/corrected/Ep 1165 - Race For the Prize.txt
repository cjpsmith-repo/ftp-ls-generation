Ep 1165 - Race For the Prize

Word count: 1076

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL CELESTIAL STRINGS]
The laws of the heavens were absolute. No one could defy them.
Upon attaining Supreme Lord status and bearing the Heavenly Destiny, one could forge divine power through the Law of Causality and gain millennia of extended life. The problem: only one hundred and twenty-eight positions existed across the entire universe - one hundred thousand supreme worlds, each with its own World Lord, and dozens of grand World Lords ruling entire clusters.
Whenever a Supreme Lord fell, World Lords fought viciously for the vacant position.
This time, however, something unprecedented occurred. An extra Supreme Lord position had appeared from nowhere.
Its existence warped the heavens themselves, throwing the celestial order into chaos.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DEEP RESONANCE]
A grim look settled on the face of every last one of the Supreme Lords. They had no idea what was coming; nothing like this had ever happened before. What did an extra position even mean? How did it get there? And how was anyone supposed to claim it?
Because it shattered the fundamental rules of the universe, the new position had the attention of every powerful being in existence.
Back in the palace, Lavender had already moved to Zane’s side. She stared at the trembling Crown with a focused expression.
[SFX: LOW RESONANT HUM]
“This is the Crown of Destiny,” she said quietly. “It only appears when a Supreme Lord dies.”
Zane looked at her. “You’ve seen one before?”
“Once. A beast-race Supreme Lord reached the end of his life and couldn’t turn it around. When he fell, the Crown appeared. I gathered every Demon Night tribe I could, and we fought for it — against every other race that wanted it.” A pause. “We won.”
“And who wore it?”
Lavender’s expression shifted slightly. “Leroy Flynn. He inherited the Heavenly Destiny and became a Supreme Lord. That’s why he’s followed me ever since.”
“Then if I put it on now,” Zane said, “I become a Supreme Lord?”
“No,” Lavender said, shaking her head. “You can’t bear the Heavenly Destiny at your current level. You must complete the third transformation of the world overlord realm first. The Soul Sea opens the divine sea. The Empyrean Realm brings life into your inner world. The third transformation changes the nature of your soul itself. Only then can a warrior bear the Heavenly Destiny. Try to wear it before that, and you simply cannot carry it.”
Zane turned the Crown over in his hands. “So it’s useless to me right now.”
A faint smile touched Lavender’s lips. “Useless now,” she said. “But once you complete the three transformations, it will matter enormously. What I really want to know is what happens when a one hundred and twenty-ninth Supreme Lord actually shows up.”
[MUSIC: FOCUSED DETERMINATION]
[AMBIENT: DIVINE RUNE HUM]
Livingston led Zane to the center of the palace, where three thousand six hundred divine runes were carved into the floor in concentric rings. “Sit,” he said.
Zane sat.
“To control the Wheel of Reincarnation, you need to fully activate all three thousand six hundred runes,” Livingston said. “These are from the previous cosmic era — they’ll be unfamiliar to you. How long it takes depends on how fast you learn.”
Zane said nothing. He closed his eyes and began.
[SFX: RUNES ACTIVATING]
The runes were unlike anything he’d encountered. The logic behind them was alien — built on laws that no longer existed in the current age. He had to feel his way through each one, learning the pattern before he could light it.
Two hours in, one hundred runes glowed.
[SFX: RUNES BRIGHTENING]
Four hours in, nearly three hundred.
Six hours in, over a thousand — and accelerating. The more he learned, the faster the patterns came.
[MUSIC: BUILDING ANTICIPATION]
Eight hours after he’d begun, the last rune lit.
Zane’s eyes snapped open.
“Done?” Lavender asked.
He nodded. He spread his hands.
[MUSIC: URGENT COSMIC TENSION]
What none of them knew, however, was that all one hundred and twenty-eight Supreme Lords in the universe were frantically trying to pinpoint the exact location of that Crown.
[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND PALACE]
Meanwhile, outside the Underground Palace, the other warriors who’d gone in started trickling out. The expedition had been a huge success for them; while the second cave was a bust, they’d all found valuable treasures in the third and fourth. The success came at a steep price, though. The casualties had been staggering. The Demon Night, the humans, and the Ogres had all lost more than half their people.
The Saint Lord of the Violetwood Sanctuary was waiting for Zane and Lavender, just as the Queen of Punishment had instructed. He never imagined this trip would spiral into such a massive ordeal.
Still, choosing between the two queens wasn’t a hard choice. The Queen of Punishment had already sent Cassian, Taryn, Maren, and Paige to the Dark World. The Saint Lord had his doubts about the orders, but he was responsible for his entire sanctuary. More importantly, he knew exactly what happened to those who defied the Queen of Punishment. It would be stupid to cross the true leader of the Demon Night race.
He could crush Lavender and Zane easily with his current strength — but if Lavender resisted, the crime of killing a queen was not something he could afford to bear. All he needed to do was wait. Let the Queen of Punishment’s people handle the rest.
Whatever the queens decided, the Saint Lord had his own score to settle with Zane. That wasn’t going to change.
The human Saint Lord, standing apart from the others, was less certain. He knew Zane was of the Mist Palace. But the situation had moved well beyond anything he’d anticipated, and he didn’t know how to intervene — or whether he should.
[MUSIC: CATASTROPHIC RUMBLING]
[SFX: EARTHQUAKE TREMORS]
Suddenly, the entire Underground Palace began to shake violently.
The warriors near the entrance stumbled, losing their footing.
[SFX: CRACKING STONE]
[SFX: COLLAPSING STRUCTURE]
They had no idea what was happening inside, but they watched in shock as the very space around the palace began to crack and collapse.
“The palace — it’s moving!”
“How is that possible? The Endless Abyss itself is shifting!”
“God knows what the hell is causing this!”
Even the three Saint Lords stared, completely baffled. They were mesmerized by the impossible spectacle unfolding before their eyes.
The Wheel of Reincarnation was a massive spatial artifact, and with it now under his control, Zane was raising it from tens of thousands of miles beneath the earth.
[SFX: MASSIVE GROUND RUPTURE]
[SFX: BUILDINGS COLLAPSING]
[SFX: DUST CLOUD RISING]
A moment later, a huge crack tore through the center of Auspice City. 
[SFX: MASSIVE GOLDEN PLATE RISING]
[MUSIC: AWE-INSPIRING REVELATION]
Through the haze, everyone could only watch in disbelief as an enormous golden plate emerged from the chasm and floated into the sky.
