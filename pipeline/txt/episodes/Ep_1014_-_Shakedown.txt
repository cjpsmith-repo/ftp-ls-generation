Ep 1014 - Shakedown

Word count: 1016

[AMBIENT: TENSE CAVE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane and Freya didn’t say a word to these newcomers; they simply exchanged a look with each other.

As seasoned warriors, they’d seen enough to know trouble when it walked up to them, and this crew was practically screaming it.

Freya’s brow furrowed. “What’s this about? What do you want?”

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

The bald warrior in the lead let his gaze crawl over Freya, a greasy smirk twisting his lips. “What do we want? Just a little chat. That’s all.” He paused, jutting his chin toward the cave behind her. “You know who made this cave?”

“No idea. Enlighten me,” Freya said, her voice dripping with sarcasm.

[SFX: CHEST THUMP]

“I did!” the bald warrior boomed, thumping his chest. “Dug it with my own two hands!”

Zane’s expression stayed cool as he idly tossed a Sovereign Stone in his hand. He hadn’t said a thing.

Freya, on the other hand, just stared, completely baffled. “You dug it? So what?” The whole mountain was shot through with thousands of mine shafts. You had to dig to get Aether crystals; it was hardly something to brag about.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The bald warrior chuckled. “You don’t get it, do you? You’re a little slow.” A predatory glint appeared in his eyes. “Don’t worry, I like that in a woman. See, since I dug this cave, anything you pulled out of it belongs to me!”

“That’s right! That’s the rule!”

“Ha! Ernest’s got you there!”

The other Soul Sea Realm warriors behind him grumbled their support.

Freya knew they were trouble, but the sheer shamelessness of it all was stunning.

There was no way they dug this massive cave. After all, countless miners had come and gone, leaving hundreds of thousands of holes in this mountain range. And even if they had dug this one, they had no right to shake her and Zane down.

Her expression hardened. “Why don’t you just drop the act? You’re here to rob us.”

The bald warrior threw his head back and laughed. “Ha! Took you long enough!”

[AMBIENT: TENSE MINE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

“You can’t just rob people in broad daylight!” Freya’s voice was sharp. “What about the Soulbound Sect? You think they’re just going to let this slide?”

The bald warrior sneered. “The Soulbound Sect? They don’t care about ascenders. As long as someone can pay enough, they won’t lift a finger — not for killing, not for robbing.”

Zane filed that away and said nothing.

A knot of worry was tightening in Freya’s stomach. All seven of them were at the Soul Sea Realm. While Freya could hold her own, she glanced at Zane, who was only at the Life-and-Death Realm. He’d just pulled off something incredible, sure, but she wasn’t confident he could fight them. The power gap between the realms was just too massive. Worse, Zane hadn’t even opened his Inner World, which meant he couldn’t summon his own domain. He’d be completely defenseless.

She made a decision. She reached into her Storage Ring and produced a handful of supreme Aether crystals. “I dug these up in the mine. Take them and let us go.”

The bald warrior glanced at them and sneered. “That’s it? I don’t believe that’s all you’ve got.” He tilted his head. “Let me check your Storage Ring.”

Freya’s jaw tightened. She extended her hand.

He grabbed it — and then, under the pretense of examining her Storage Ring, his hand moved to her body.

“Get your hands off me!”

Vitality exploded from her, condensing into a ring of snow that blasted outward in all directions and forced him back.

The smile didn’t leave his face. “Tell you what — keep me and my brothers company for a while, and I’ll forget all about the crystals.”

[SFX: SHARP GASP]

Her thoughts were cut short by a sudden gasp from one of the other warriors. He was pointing a trembling finger at Zane. “Those… where did you get all those Sovereign Stones? That’s gotta be worth hundreds of thousands of supreme Aether crystals!”

“Ernest, we hit the jackpot!” another one shouted.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Their usual shakedowns in the mines might net them a few hundred supreme Aether crystals a month—a few thousand, if they got lucky. But with seven of them needing to scrounge up seven million supreme Aether crystals just to leave this place, that was a drop in the bucket. It would take them forever. And as more warriors got wise to their game, the pickings got slimmer. So, seeing a fortune in Sovereign Stones just sitting there… their eyes practically bulged out of their heads.

The bald warrior, Ernest, licked his lips, his gaze locking onto Zane with a mix of glee and genuine confusion. “I can’t tell if you’re an idiot or just naive. Who in their right mind flashes that many Sovereign Stones around? What were you thinking, showing those to us? Even the dumbest rock-hauler in this mountain knows better than that.” He seemed genuinely curious.

Zane held his hand out, displaying the gems. “Actually,” he began, his voice calm, “before I came into the mines, I thought about robbing people myself. It’s a lot faster than digging, that’s for sure. But I figured it was a low way to operate. Besides, I wasn’t sure if the Soulbound Sect would step in. So, I decided to do things the hard way.”

He reached into his Storage Ring and produced the rest — all thirty-eight — and held them out.

“Zane!” Freya’s voice cracked. She stared at him like he’d lost his mind.

Zane slowly curled his fingers, pulling the Sovereign Stones back into his Storage Ring as he lifted his head. A chilling hint of killing intent bled into his expression. He spoke softly, but the tone sent a shiver down the warriors’ spines.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS THEME]

[AMBIENT: ECHOING SILENCE]

“But now… I have to thank you. You just cleared something up for me. The Soulbound Sect won’t get involved if I kill someone in here. Or rob them.”
