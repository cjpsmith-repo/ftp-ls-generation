Ep 383 - The Sky-Reaver

Word count: 1113

[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]

[AMBIENT: NIGHT FOREST]

Hellish flames roared to life, lighting up nearly half the night sky.

[SFX: BREAKING WOOD]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC QUEST]

Zane and the other two charged toward the Karmafire, smashing through branches and twigs as they went. The Apex of Sword Intent flowing from them was so strong that it splintered the trunks of ancient trees in their path.

Watching them go, the corners of Lewis’s mouth twitched. “Those two are just unbelievable. Both have the Apex of Sword Intent? Are they really from some second-rate sect?”

Lewis could be a motormouth, but he was still confident in his own strength. But even after making it this far, he couldn’t shake the feeling that he was no match for Zane or Finn. Still, he had an ace up his sleeve. If he unleashed everything he had, he figured he might just have a shot against them.

When Zane and the others burst out of the woods, the scene that met them was staggering.

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FIRE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC BATTLE]

A massive crater, a thousand feet across, had been carved out of the forest floor. It was clearly the aftermath of a brutal battle. In the center, Patrick was engulfed in a blazing Black Lotus Karmafire, locked in combat with a monstrous beast.

The creature had wings sprouting from its back, a horn jutting from its forehead, and the face of a bear. It stood on its hind legs, bathed in an eerie silver light.

Patrick’s Karmafire was legendary in the Skyreach Sect. The last time they’d fought, Zane had only survived it because his body was like a Spirit Weapon. Even then, he hadn't taken the Black Lotus Karmafire head-on; his body had simply neutralized its power.

And yet, this beast was somehow resisting the scorching flames.

[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSHES]

Frank fought nearby with masterful spear techniques, systematically shredding the beast's hands despite its size and agility. Several other disciples watched from the sidelines, keeping a safe distance from the battle.

Watching Frank fight so effortlessly made Zane think. When an opponent was out of your league, you could either work to catch up or join their side. Finn had done the same thing—realizing he couldn't surpass Zane, he chose to follow his lead, knowing Zane's victories would benefit them both. It might have felt like a surrender, but it was the smart play. Some people were just born with more talent, more strength. There was always someone better out there.

So the question wasn't if Zane would challenge Frank, but when.

But now wasn't the time to worry about that. With unknown creatures crawling all over the Death Road, his priority was helping the people he was with.

[SFX: SWORD SWOOSH]

[SFX: SPEAR WHOOSH]

[SFX: FLESH IMPACT]

A white spear suddenly shot through the air, piercing straight through the beast’s arm.

[SFX: MONSTROUS ROAR]

A ferocious look twisted its face as it let out a deafening howl that made everyone's ears ring. 

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]

The beast lunged at Frank, its massive body hurtling through the air.

[SFX: WING FLAPS]

[SFX: SWOOSH]

Despite standing ten feet tall with a bulky frame, the creature moved with surprising agility. Its wings beat powerfully as it swooped down again and again, each attack more vicious than the last.

Even under the relentless assault, Frank remained completely calm. He met every strike with his spear, his movements precise and economical, wasting not a single motion.

[SFX: POWERFUL THRUST]

Frank's voice rang out with absolute confidence. "Sky-Breaking Thrust!"

"Recurring Thrust!"

"Circling Thrust!"

[SFX: MULTIPLE IMPACTS]

[SFX: BLOOD SPLATTER]

Each time his spear shot forward, the blade connected with one of the beast's palms. The creature tried to adapt, tried to change its attack pattern, but Frank anticipated every shift. After several strikes, the monster's hands had become a bloody mess, shredded flesh hanging from bone. Yet still it fought on, driven by instinct and fury, refusing to acknowledge the inevitable outcome of this battle.

[AMBIENT: CROWD CHEERING]

"Frank, get him!" a woman's voice rang out from the sidelines, where she stood with a small group of disciples.

Since Zane and his friends hadn't encountered any women from Cloud Hall during the trial, he reasoned she must belong to the Luminous Sect. The way she cheered for Frank with such familiarity suggested a close relationship.

Lewis piped up, his tone carrying the satisfaction of someone sharing valuable intelligence. "That's Lois from the Luminous Sect. Word is she's engaged to Frank. Their betrothal was arranged by their respective masters three years ago."

Zane shook his head with a small smile, glancing at his companion. Lewis was a classic know-it-all, the type who collected gossip and trivia with the same dedication others devoted to cultivation. Still, Zane had to admit the information often proved useful. 

[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Tall, slender, and beautiful, Lois looked like a perfect match for Frank. The two of them seemed made for each other.

“Let’s go join them,” Zane suggested. “Looks like Frank has this beast handled, but they might need help with others.”

With that, the three of them started toward the group.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON GRAVEL]

Just then, Lois and her companions noticed them approaching. Their expressions were a mix of shock and disbelief—they clearly hadn't expected to see anyone else on the Death Road.

As they got closer, Frank snapped without ever taking his eyes off the beast.

[SFX: BEAST GROWL]

“Leave! You’ll just get in the way!”

He clearly didn’t think their arrival was any help. He saw them as a liability.

After all, the three of them were pathetically low-ranked. One was only at the Third Level of the Adept Realm, another had just reached the Adept Realm, and the third was barely at the First Level of the Soul Realm. They should’ve known better than to set foot on the Death Road. Now they were just extra baggage.

From his point of view, only Lois and Patrick were qualified to fight by his side. As for Randy from Duskfall Sect, the guy would have died a thousand times over if not for Frank’s protection.

Frank was supremely confident in his own strength, but he was also incredibly arrogant. Since he possessed what he felt was absolute power, he didn’t mind protecting a few people.

But if others wanted to jump into his fight, they’d better ask themselves if they were on his level.

And it was obvious that Zane and his friends weren't.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

That’s why Frank had chosen to face the transfigured Sky-Reaver all by himself.

And anyone who got in his way would pay the ultimate price.
