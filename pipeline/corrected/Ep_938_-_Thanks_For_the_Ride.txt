Ep 938 - Thanks For the Ride

Word count: 1044

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

One of Simon's men stammered, his voice trembling.”It's him! Z-Z-Zane!” 

But Simon didn't need the warning. He’d already seen him.

Zane was the one person the remaining members of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom truly feared. The image of him killing Maddox was burned into their minds, a nightmare that would haunt them for the rest of their lives.

Simon’s heart hammered against his ribs, but his mind was racing. He quickly sized Zane up and was horrified to find he had broken through to the first level of the Life-and-Death Realm.

Even so, maybe there was still a chance. Since Zane was back in The Heartlands, Simon figured he could flee the region, get overseas, and just disappear.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

But then Simon’s gaze drifted to the people with Zane, and the hope drained from his face, replaced by pure despair.

He couldn't sense their power levels—not at all. And that could only mean one thing: the gap between them was far greater than a single realm. A bitter taste filled his mouth, worse than any poison he'd ever brewed.

[SFX: BLADE UNSHEATHING]

“So, that the guy you were after?” the young man in black asked Zane, raising his saber. “Want a hand? One swing and this is all over.” He glanced at Simon like he was something you’d scrape off your boot.

Meanwhile, Delroy and the others kept their eyes closed, deep in their cultivation. They couldn't be bothered with some Life-and-Death Realm warrior from the Lower World. A fighter at that level was nothing more than an ant to them, and who spares a thought for an ant?

Zane knew the offer wasn't really about helping—the young man was just getting impatient. He just shook his head. “No. This one's mine.”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Suddenly, Simon whipped out two black poison beads. He was desperate, but he wasn’t ready to die. Seeing Zane preparing to move, Simon’s own Vitality flared.

[SFX: GLASS BREAKING]

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]

He crushed the beads in his hands, unleashing clouds of black, poisonous mist. His surging Vitality instantly fanned the toxic fog, blanketing the area.

[SFX: CHOKING GASPS]

“Oh god! Augh!”

“How could you?!”

“I… I can't—“

[SFX: MULTIPLE BODIES FALLING]

Simon's own men never saw it coming. The poison was incredibly potent, and the faces of the few Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors closest to him turned black as they collapsed, choking on their leader’s betrayal.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: SWIRLING FOG]

Under the cover of the fog, Simon turned into a streak of light and shot backward. He hadn't given up. Zane hadn't given chase — maybe there was still a sliver of hope.

Dorothea glanced back. “He's running.”

Zane nodded. “I know.”

[SFX: METAL SCRAPING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BUILDUP]

He reached into his Storage Ring and drew out a dark golden longbow. With his other hand, he pulled a single black arrow from a quiver.

The young man in black's eyes sharpened. “A semi-divine bow,” he said, in a tone somewhere between mockery and envy. “And a divine arrow.”

Delroy cracked one eye open, looked at the weapons, and closed it again. Divine weapons were rare even in the Upper Realm — but a semi-divine bow and a consumable arrow weren't worth his full attention.

This was Zane's first time using the bow. He had been waiting for a worthy target. The moment he nocked the arrow, something unexpected happened — a faint telepathic thread connected him to it. The arrow seemed to understand his intentions. He could feel it.

He raised the bow and drew. The bowstring let out a low hum. Drawing it had cost him nearly two hundred Dragon Scales.

The fog still blanketed everything before him; he couldn't see Simon with his eyes. He closed them.

Through the world's memory, he found him instantly.

“There you are,” he murmured.

He released.

[SFX: ARROW LOOSED — SHARP WHISTLE]

The black arrow pierced through the poisonous fog. Its initial speed was unremarkable — roughly that of a Soul Sea Realm warrior at full sprint. But Zane could still feel the connection. The arrow held three divine runes. With a thought, he activated the first.

[SFX: DISTANT CRACK]

The arrow's speed doubled.

He activated the second.

[SFX: DISTANT CRACK]

It doubled again.

He activated the third.

[SFX: DISTANT CRACK]

At this speed, even a Soul Sea Realm master could not dodge it.

[AMBIENT: WINDSWEPT WILDERNESS]

Miles away, Simon was fleeing at full tilt. He extended his perception — no pursuit. He almost allowed himself to breathe.

Then he felt the arrow.

He had just enough time to think: an arrow won't catch me.

[SFX: ARROW IMPACT]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

It already had. It passed through the back of his skull and exited between his eyebrows, leaving a single small bloody hole. His body hung suspended in the air for one breath, Vitality still cycling out of habit. Then it dispersed.

[SFX: HEAVY BODY IMPACT]

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

Zane casually lowered a longbow and tucked it back into his Storage Ring. He smiled. “Thanks, Max. I've taken care of who I came for.”

A few of Simon’s men were still alive, but the remaining Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors were beneath his notice—not worth the time or energy to hunt down.

The entire trip—from leaving Oracle City to arriving at the Southern Sky Mountains and killing Simon—had taken less than fifteen minutes.

Max gave a slight nod. Now that Zane had settled his business, they could head back. Their next stop was the Tower of Sin.

[SFX: MAGICAL TELEPORTATION]

[AMBIENT: ORACLE CITY]

Moments later, Wade activated The Compass Wheel, and they reappeared in Oracle City. The whole group was gathered in front of the Heavenly Stellar Palace. When the three leaders of the World Merchants' Guild and Tia saw the Compass Wheel materialize, they just looked confused. It had only been a few minutes. How were they back already?

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Their confusion turned to disbelief when Zane told them he'd already killed Simon at the Southern Sky Mountains.

The speed was unbelievable. 

An assassination like that should have taken days of preparation, but Zane had eliminated a target thousands of miles away in just a few minutes.
