Ep 1030 - Find Fenton

Word count: 1061

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

Zane had been granted access to the most exclusive cultivation ground in the Soulbound Sect — a fact that had already set the Holy City buzzing.

He had no intention of staying long at the Soulbound Sect, and he couldn’t care less about the Myriad Spirit House. But since the others meant well, he wasn’t about to turn them down flat.

Once Zane was gone, the Saint Lord of the Soulbound Sect clapped his hands once.

[SFX: SHARP CLAP]

Two old men immediately walked over from the side of the hall.

“Your orders, Saint Lord?” one of them asked.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The Saint Lord said, “Find Fenton. I want him to challenge this young man and test his mettle.”

A strange look passed between the two old men. One asked, his curiosity piqued, “Saint Lord, you want Fenton to challenge Zane Lowell?”

“I don’t think Zane Lowell is even in Fenton’s league,” the other said with a dismissive scoff.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]

The Saint Lord simply nodded. “Fenton has already passed through nine Small Heavenly Tribulations, but whether he’ll face the tenth is anyone’s guess. Zane, on the other hand, has only passed through five. His cultivation is far lower than Fenton’s, but The Alliance must have chosen him for a reason. If Fenton can defeat him, it’s my chance to get him into The Alliance.”

The Alliance — a body that controlled thousands of worlds — had sent word that they were coming for Zane. The Saint Lord had no idea why.

The two old men stared, dumbfounded. “Saint Lord, are you saying The Alliance chose this guy?”

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORCHESTRAL]

The Saint Lord nodded, his own expression puzzled. “Zane is special, yes, but for The Alliance to take this much interest in him is… unusual. I don’t get it either. But they’re sending people to pick him up. Before that happens, I want Fenton to challenge him. It’s the perfect opportunity to introduce Fenton to them. If Fenton joins The Alliance, it will be the chance of a lifetime for him.”

The two old men nodded, their faces full of admiration. “A brilliant strategy, Saint Lord!” they said in unison.

What neither old man said aloud — and what the Saint Lord wouldn’t admit to anyone — was that there was a private sting to all of this. Fenton, the greatest genius the Soulbound Sect had produced in generations, had not been chosen by the Alliance. Zane, a fresh ascender who had spent weeks mining in a pit, had. The Saint Lord couldn’t make sense of it. What he could do was use Zane’s connection to get Fenton through the door.

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

Fenton was the crown jewel of the Soulbound Sect’s younger generation—the future of the entire sect.

Though he hadn’t yet reached the Soul Sea Realm, he’d already passed the Ninth Small Heavenly Tribulation.

For most warriors in the Life-and-Death Realm, breaking through to the Soul Sea Realm happened after their fifth or sixth tribulation. The truly exceptional might face seven, while the most talented faced eight. But the number of warriors who could withstand nine tribulations was vanishingly small.

It was a simple truth: the more tribulations a warrior overcame, the greater their potential.

Fenton had already passed nine and still hadn’t broken through. It was entirely possible he was on the path to face the legendary Tenth Small Heavenly Tribulation.

What made him even more remarkable was that his lotus flower had already bloomed when he faced his very first tribulation. By the time he survived his ninth, it had opened seven leaves. That was the true reason the Saint Lord placed such extraordinary hope in him.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: CURIOUS STRINGS]

Meanwhile, Master Hill and Master Sloan, who had escorted Zane to the Myriad Spirit House, could only watch in disbelief. He found a corner, sat down cross-legged, closed his eyes, and just started meditating. They had spent the entire trip hyping up the benefits of the place, but he didn’t seem interested in the slightest. What a strange kid.

“Zane, the Attunement Sanctums in here are incredibly beneficial for warriors in the Life-and-Death Realm,” Master Hill couldn’t resist pointing out.

But Zane just smiled. “I appreciate the thought, Master Hill. But I prefer to follow my own path.”

Realizing they weren’t going to change his mind, the two masters figured their job was done. Whatever happened next was none of their concern. With a few quick goodbyes, they left.

Zane sat in his corner, cultivating by himself, completely ignoring the glares being shot his way from across the hall. To them, he was an eyesore.

[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]

And they had every reason to be bitter. The Myriad Spirit House held exactly nine thousand warriors — no more, no less. For every new entry, someone had to leave. Noble families of the sixth and seventh tier had spent millions of Sovereign Stones trying to buy their way in, and failed. The Saint Lord had just added an ascender who’d been mining dirt a week ago, making it nine thousand and one.

Word had spread across the Holy City within hours.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

“He’s just putting on a show! You really think he’s going to walk away empty-handed after getting into a place like this? I don’t buy it. Give him a day. He’ll be begging to use the Attunement Sanctums.”

“Exactly. The Saint Lord makes a huge exception for some nobody from the Lower World, and he has the nerve to act so high and mighty!”

“Someone ought to knock him down a peg.”

Zane, deep in his cultivation, had no idea he’d made so many enemies just by sitting still.

The truth was, the Myriad Spirit House’s benefits were simply lost on him. After cultivating the Primordial Chaos Tactic in the Elysian Keep, he had abandoned the conventional Vitality system entirely. The energy in his Vitality core was no longer standard Vitality — it was Chaos Energy. Ordinary cultivation methods, no matter how refined, did almost nothing for him. He wasn’t putting on a show. He genuinely had no use for any of it.

No one bothered him as he trained. There weren’t many warriors in the Myriad Spirit House to begin with, and most just shot him a curious glance before hurrying on their way.

[MUSIC: TIME PASSAGE]

[SFX: WIND CHIMES]

Three days passed.

Those who had been so sure Zane was putting on an act were finally forced to admit they were wrong. He truly had no need for the Myriad Spirit House at all.
