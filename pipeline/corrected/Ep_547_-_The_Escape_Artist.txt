Ep 547 - The Escape Artist
Word count: 1117
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL FOREST]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
Zane had no idea how to use his new Magic Vision. All he knew was that his eyes had felt completely rejuvenated the moment the sap touched them.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
When the process ended, he opened his eyes. The change was nearly imperceptible.
"Is that it? My reward is just... cleaner eyes?" Zane asked.
"Ha! You might not realize it now, but that's one hell of a reward," the Cobalt Dragon said. "Your eyesight is so keen you'll see through most illusions. It's a damn sight better than another five hundred years of life, at least."
'He really thinks this beats five hundred more years?' Zane thought, dumbfounded.
Five hundred years meant everything to a Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior. But to a being like the Cobalt Dragon, it was barely a blink. From that perspective, the Magic Vision made sense.
[SFX: SOFT MAGICAL PULSE]
Zane blinked, and the mysterious green light in his pupils faded, retracting deep within them.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]
With his turn over, Zane now held the lead with sixty-six steps. Tia was right behind him in second with sixty-three. The Holy Maiden sat in third—with her curse finally lifted, she was poised to close the gap fast.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
When the Divine Kingdom man saw Zane leading, his face darkened with murderous intent. Inside the Celestial Tomb of Vesper, where Brent's power was suppressed, Zane would prove a formidable opponent.
The beam swung to the Divine Kingdom Prince. He snorted, punched the die—it landed on one.
His expression soured. One step.
But when the disc appeared glowing red, everything changed.
He lunged for it.
[SFX: DARK MAGICAL RESONANCE]
"Free Challenge."
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT DARK STRINGS]
The Divine Kingdom Prince stared at Zane and laughed—loud, wild, unrestrained.
The Holy Maiden watched him with cool eyes. "Stop laughing," she said flatly. "Just kill him."
"Of course, Little Sister," the Prince said, his grin sharpening into something vicious. He raised a finger and pointed straight at Zane. "I want to challenge that… ant."
Not far away, Brent pursed his lips. He'd wanted that Free Challenge. Back at the tournament, he'd never gotten his shot at Zane—he'd won the Rising Dragon Platform, but that felt hollow. What good was it if he couldn't ascend to the upper realm? He'd have traded it in a heartbeat for a proper fight with Zane.
His luck in the Wheel of Destiny had been decent—smooth sailing, six points nearly every roll. Close to thirty steps. But no red disc.
"I wanted that challenge," he muttered darkly.
What Brent didn't know: even with his rare physique and divine-grade talent, he was no match for Zane here. Inside this tomb, his cultivation was suppressed by a full realm. Zane's wasn't. At the seventh level of the Soul Realm, Zane could take on a late-stage Inner-Core warrior. A suppressed Brent would be lucky to survive a single hit.
The moment Zane caught the man’s intent, a flicker of green light—his new Magic Vision—flashed in his eyes. A quiet confidence settled over him, but otherwise, his expression was perfectly calm.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]
[SFX: PLATFORM MATERIALIZING]
High above the abyss, a massive, disk-shaped platform materialized. Zane and the man from the Divine Kingdom stepped onto it from opposite sides.
"[MUSIC: OMINOUS CONFRONTATION]
You've always been an annoying little pest, and now you've managed to stumble into the lead," the Divine Kingdom Prince said with a menacing chuckle. "Too bad for you, this is the end of the line. This is where you die."
"Oh yeah? What makes you so sure?" Zane asked, his eyes narrowing slightly.
[MUSIC: DARK THREATENING STRINGS]
"Because I'm going to kill you," the prince laughed, his whole demeanor shifting into something vicious.
Zane just rolled his eyes. What a moron, he thought. Still, messing with idiots like this could be fun.
[SFX: METALLIC RING]
With a tap on his Storage Ring, he summoned his sword—a low-grade Relic-Class weapon.
"A Relic-Class weapon? Interesting," the man sneered. "I can't believe they're so common in The Heartlands that even some Soul Realm nobody can get his hands on one. Unbelievable."
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
The man from the Divine Kingdom then drew his own blade—a Serpentine Sword. Its blade curved like a snake's body, a golden design etched down its center. It was also a low-grade Relic-Class treasured sword.
The man had planned to show off his own abilities before the union of the seven divine kingdoms. But this… Among all of them, only a select few had ever grasped even the first level of the Spatial Law, and they were all ancient masters who had lived for thousands, even tens of thousands of years.
But this kid in front of him? He looked like a teenager! How could he have gotten so lucky, so fast? The Sky Northern Region was supposed to be a desolate wasteland! How the hell did he find an opportunity like that there?
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[SFX: BATTLE CRY]
"Die!" the Prince roared. His figure flashed forward, Serpentine Sword twisting through the air.
"Spirit Snake Paradise!"
The blade moved like a living thing—hissing, coiling, lunging straight for Zane. Zane raised his sword to block.
[SFX: CLASH OF BLADES]
And then the Prince's sword changed direction.
Like a snake dodging a strike, it curved around Zane's guard and drove straight into his chest.
[SFX: IMPACT]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
"What a sword technique," Zane said through gritted teeth, staggering back. The wound wasn't deep—but the hit had landed.
The crowd stirred.
"Did you see that flexibility? That sword intent?"
The Prince pressed forward, sword dancing, snake shadows filling the air around Zane. Every shadow was a potential strike, every shift a feint. There was no angle to block from—the blade never came from the same direction twice.
"The platform isn't big," the Prince sneered, advancing. "Let's see where you run to."
Zane took one step forward.
[SFX: SPATIAL RIPPLE]
And disappeared.
He reappeared on the far side of the platform, completely unhurried, a faint smile on his face.
"Are you sure I can't escape?" he asked.
The man's shock hardened into resolve. A parlor trick. Against a true master, the Spatial Law meant nothing—the cultivation gap between them was insurmountable.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS CONFRONTATION]
"Space Law," the Prince said slowly, the words landing like a stone. His expression cycled through shock, disbelief—and then settled back into cold resolve.
In the four Divine Kingdoms, only ancient masters had ever comprehended the first level of the Space Law. Men who had lived for thousands of years. Giants who had survived for tens of thousands. And this kid—this teenager from a backwater region—had it too?
The Prince's jaw tightened. It didn't matter. The gap in cultivation between them was a chasm. No law, no trick, no talent could bridge it.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[SFX: BATTLE CRY]
"Go to hell!" the man roared, his voice dripping with contempt. "I'll pulverize you, you little ant!"
He charged, raising his Serpentine Sword for a direct strike.
