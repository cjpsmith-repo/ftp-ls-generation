Ep 678 - Knock Knock

Word count: 1050

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS TOWER INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The air in the Tower of Sin was so thick you could taste it. Everyone waited, their breath held tight in their chests, nerves etched onto every face. The moment the Dark Nether Death Light flooded the cave again, any warrior still inside would be vaporized. It was an ironclad rule of the Tower—no exceptions. Not even the most powerful War Lords could survive it.

But a small ember of hope still glowed in the dark, and it had a name: Zane.

Before Zane, nobody had ever hit the Celestial Rank. Nobody had ever managed to control the Tower of Sin, let alone bend its power to their will. From the day he’d first set foot inside, Zane had been one miracle after another. Maybe, just maybe, he had one more left in him.

Even the veteran War Lords of Demon Night shared that fragile hope. Just yesterday, a message had arrived from the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary: Lavender’s statue was still glowing, however faintly. It was dim, but it hadn't gone out. She was alive. And if she was alive, it meant there was a very real chance Zane had made it back. How he could have gotten through the Dark Nether Cave and its lethal light was a mystery to Elder May, but the possibility hung in the air.

[SFX: LOUD METALLIC BANG]

A sharp sound echoed from the tower’s entrance.

Maurice’s face was stone. "Nobody touch that door," he snorted. "It's probably just some monster that wandered too close."

He wasn't just making things up. Years ago, an unbelievably powerful beast had slammed into the Tower, shaking the entire structure so violently that the War Lords on the top floor had been terrified, to say nothing of the warriors below. The impact caused a mass panic, with fighters on the lower levels fleeing the Tower and not returning for months. In the end, the monster failed to break through and, after a few more futile rams, had slunk away in defeat.

"He's right," a War Lord from the Twilight Group agreed. "No mortal warrior could make it back through that. It has to be a monster."

[AMBIENT: TENSE CAVE INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

"Not necessarily," Elder May muttered, just loud enough to be heard.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

Maurice rounded on her, his voice dropping to a low threat. "Don't even think about opening that door."

Elder May met his anger with a calm shrug. "I'm not going to," he said lightly. "If it's Zane, he'll get in on his own. A door can't stop him." After all, he was the one who controlled the Tower; back on the first floor, he’d opened cracks in the very walls.

[SFX: KNOCKING]

The knocking continued — steady, rhythmic, deliberate.

"A monster wouldn't knock like that," one of the human War Lords said quietly. "That's a person."

No one answered, but no one disagreed either.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS DRONE]

But as time stretched on, the cave sealed itself shut. Zane didn't appear. Maurice’s connection to his agent, Malcolm, went dead. He knew then that he’d likely sent both Malcolm and Timothy to their deaths, but he’d seen it as a necessary sacrifice. A worthy price to pay to finally be rid of Zane.

[SFX: DRAMATIC STING]

[MUSIC: SHOCKING REVELATION]

[SFX: STONE CRACKING]

A one-meter crack split the wall beside the entrance. It widened like a mouth opening, and a figure stepped through.

Then another. And another.

[AMBIENT: EXCITED MURMURS]

"It's Zane! He's back!"

"Fabian — and Jarrod!"

"Esther Sterling — she's alive!"

The human War Lords couldn't help themselves. Some of them had tears running down their faces. They'd been minutes from dying. And now this.

Zane stepped into the hall. His eyes went immediately to the floor.

Two bodies. Both human War Lords. One of them he recognized — the man who'd thrown up a wall of flame to slow the Shadow-maws at the cave entrance, what felt like a lifetime ago. The blood had already been absorbed into the stone. A third War Lord lay slumped against the wall, barely breathing.

Zane's expression went flat. His eyes moved to Maurice.

"Didn't I say it before I left?" His voice was quiet, almost conversational. "Any Ogre I find in this Tower gets killed without mercy." He tilted his head slightly. "Maurice — do you not have a brain? Or is your memory just that bad?"

In truth, Maurice had never considered what he'd done a retreat. When he'd walked out of the Tower the first time, he'd called it a strategic withdrawal — a temporary concession to buy time. Malcolm and Timothy in the passage, the cave closing around Zane, the Death Light rising to its peak — it had all been going exactly to plan. When the cave sealed and Zane didn't return, Maurice had been certain. The transaction was complete. A worthy price.

And yet here was Zane, standing on the stairs, looking down at him.

"How did you get back?" Maurice asked, his voice a raw whisper.

[MUSIC: COLD MENACING THEME]

Zane gazed down at him, his expression unreadable. "And why would I tell you?" he replied, his voice chillingly calm.

"Where's Malcolm? Where is Timothy?" Maurice demanded, his own voice rising with desperation.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ROOM ATMOSPHERE]

Esther Sterling, standing beside Zane, let out a low, mocking sneer. "Aw, were those two your last hope? Sending a couple of War Sages after us was a bold move, I'll give you that. Unfortunately for you…" She let the sentence hang in the air.

"What? Where are they?" Maurice pressed.

Yolande answered flatly, her face a perfect blank. "We killed them."

A stunned silence fell over the room. No one but the Ogres had known what was happening. How had Malcolm and Timothy—two top-level War Lords—even gotten into the Dark Nether Cave? They went after Zane? And when the hell did they become War Sages? The questions flew silently through the minds of the human and Demon Night warriors, their eyes wide with disbelief.

"The Ogres have been hiding things from us," one of the War Lords finally breathed. "Colin and Neil were War Sages this whole time, and they somehow stayed in the Tower!"

"That's not the point!" another shot back. "The point is that Zane survived an attack from two War Sages. He didn't just survive—he won. They're the ones who ended up dead!"

[MUSIC: RISING DREAD]

A third voice cut through the rising panic, heavy with awe and a new kind of fear. "Is there anything left that can actually stop him?"
