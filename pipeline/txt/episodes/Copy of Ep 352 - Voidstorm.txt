Ep 352 - Voidstorm
Word count: 1048

With every new experience, Zane could feel his mind sharpening, growing calmer. The impulsive, quick-tempered kid had gradually transformed into someone more measured, more deliberate. His thoughts flowed with newfound clarity, each decision stemming from reason rather than reaction.

Elara's absence created a hollow space inside him; it was deeply unfair that he'd fought so long to find his sister, only to have her ripped away again. But if he was going to keep her safe, he couldn't give in to the sadness and anger.  Instead, he locked those feelings away inside, focusing instead on the challenges that lay ahead. For now, it was the only way to keep his sister safe.

[AMBIENT: CLOUD PALACE]

[MUSIC: ANTICIPATORY]

A few days later, Zane, Finn, and Patrick gathered, their faces reflecting the gravity of the moment. It was the day of the Cloud Road Trial; they couldn't believe the day had finally arrived.

The atmosphere pulsed with their divergent emotions, each young man approaching this milestone from a unique perspective. Finn could barely contain himself; he was practically vibrating with excitement. He'd been dying for this opportunity to get to The Heartlands and measure his abilities against the wider world. 

Patrick stood in quiet contemplation. As the reincarnation of The Immovable Guardian, he saw this trial as more than just advancement—it offered the promise of claiming an even greater inheritance. 

For Zane, however, the trial evoked a curious detachment. He watched his companions' excitement with mild interest, yet felt none of it himself. This challenge, significant as it might be to others, registered in his mind as just another stepping stone—as natural and inevitable as a child learning to walk. 

Until they could be reunited, a part of his mind would be with  Elara, to the promises he had made, and to the power he needed to see them through.

 Sir Kenneth's voice thundered across the courtyard as he emerged from the Skyreach Sect, his imposing figure silhouetted against the pale sky. "You three, with me!" 

With a dramatic flourish of his billowing sleeves, he created a current of air that swept Zane and his companions into his slipstream, launching them into the sky

[SFX: WIND RUSHING]

[AMBIENT: SKY FLYING]

The trio assumed that their journey to the Cloud Road would be just like Elara's experience—a long ride to The Heartlands. They'd mentally prepared for the two-week journey across the vast mountain ranges separating The Eastern Territories from their destination, a journey that would give them time to steel their nerves and contemplate the challenges ahead.

But as the landscape unfurled beneath them, confusion settled over the group. Sir Kenneth was flying them in the complete opposite direction. Zane exchanged puzzled glances with his companions, a sense of unease creeping into his thoughts as they rocketed toward an unknown destination.

[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WILDERNESS]

Before long, a mountain rose before them, tucked away on the grounds of the Skyreach Sect. It wasn't a famous peak, and it seemed the Sect had left it completely untouched—no buildings, no signs of construction. It looked utterly ordinary.

[SFX: DIVING WHOOSH]

[SFX: LANDING THUD]

But as they got closer, Sir Kenneth suddenly dove, setting them down at its base.

The three of them exchanged bewildered looks. What was Sir Kenneth up to? Could the legendary Cloud Road really be hidden inside this mountain? The place felt secretive, ancient. What was it hiding?

"Follow me," Sir Kenneth said, offering no other explanation as he started walking.

[SFX: SHADOWS MELTING]

They trailed behind him to a flat rock face on the mountainside. Sir Kenneth waved a hand, and the very shadows in front of them seemed to melt away, revealing the wide, dark mouth of a cave.

[AMBIENT: CAVE INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]

The four of them stepped inside. A long tunnel opened into a massive, circular chamber. In the center of the room stood a platform, about ten feet wide and two feet high. Its surface was carved with all sorts of runes that none of them recognized, and six brilliant Aether crystals were embedded around its edge.
"What kind of Aether crystals are these?" Finn breathed, his voice electric. "They're glowing with so much pure Vitality… I've never seen anything like it."

Even Patrick was stunned by the pure, raw Vitality radiating from the crystals. A flicker of awe crossed his usually stoic face.

[SFX: TELEPORTATION SOUND]

[AMBIENT: VOID DIMENSION]

Suddenly, after a few disorienting minutes in what felt like a teleportation channel, a bright blue light flared to life up ahead.

"What's that?" Finn asked, leaning forward.

[SFX: ROARING VOIDSTORM]

[MUSIC: DANGER THEME]

Sir Kenneth's gaze snapped toward it, his face instantly grim. "Stay behind me!" he roared. "That's a voidstorm!"

The three of them had no idea what a voidstorm was, but one look was enough to know its power was far beyond them.

[AMBIENT: VOID CHANNEL]

Even Sir Kenneth never wanted to run into a voidstorm. They weren't a guaranteed part of the trip. This time, their luck had just run out.

Facing the storm, Sir Kenneth planted his feet, his broad frame seeming to expand as motes of yellow Vitality erupted on his skin, spreading like wildfire.

[SFX: VITALITY EXPLOSION]

"Ha!" he bellowed. The yellow Vitality exploded outward, swelling to ten times its size and forming a massive shield around them. 

[SFX: VOIDSTORM CRASH]

In the same instant, the swirling voidstorm crashed down on them.

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING STATIC]

A sound like crackling static filled the channel as the voidstorm tore relentlessly at Sir Kenneth's Vitality shield.

Sir Kenneth kept pouring his power into the shield, straining to hold back the chaotic energy.

Patrick and Finn could only watch, their breath caught in their throats. One mistake, one slip in Sir Kenneth's focus, and the storm would swallow them whole, scouring their bodies to ash.

Zane's eyes, however, never left the voidstorm, his expression completely unreadable.

"Zane, what are you looking at?" Finn managed to whisper.

Patrick shot Zane a confused look. The voidstorm was an incredible sight, sure, but staring at it for too long would burn out anyone's eyes.

[SFX: ENERGY BOLT]

As Zane stared into the swirling chaos, a bolt of bright-blue energy lanced through Sir Kenneth's Vitality shield like a stray lightning strike, heading straight for his eyes.
