Ep 822 - Fighting the Monster

Word count: 1031

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

After Zane’s victory, the saint-level geniuses from the union of seven divine kingdoms could only stare in stunned silence. The defeat settled over them, a heavy, nervous energy filling the arena.

Any thought of challenging Zane had evaporated. After all, he’d just walked out of those terrifying flames without a single scratch. The only casualty was his clothes, which had been burned clean off his body.

[SFX: CROWD GASPS AND WHISPERS]

Whispers started rippling through the crowd: Was he even human? Maybe his body was forged from some unknown, indestructible material. It was the only explanation that made sense; surviving that kind of power unharmed was simply impossible.

Zane, of course, couldn't have cared less what they were whispering.

[MUSIC: POWERFUL EPIC THEME]

As the golden runes faded from his skin, he could feel it—his body had been forged into something new, as hard and resilient as a Relic-Class weapon. Better yet, he still had a reserve of Primordial Energy he could use to temper his already invincible body even further. The process was excruciating, but for a warrior like him, the reward was more than worth the pain.

Closing his eyes, Zane stretched, feeling the new power settle into his limbs. A faint smile played on his lips. His eyes snapped open, finding Randy and the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom on the platform. The emperor gave him a beaming thumbs-up. Zane gave a slight nod in return, then swept his gaze across the silent arena.

His voice cut through the quiet. “Who else wants a shot?”

The Wind Divine Kingdom had tried and failed. Logically, the Star Field or York Divine Kingdoms were next. 

The pressure was now on the Star Field Divine Kingdom, which was feeling the heat more than anyone, having not secured a single victory. The Wind Divine Kingdom had at least made a good showing. Their king had only sent one warrior, Fiona, and even in defeat, her strength was undeniable. Few among the younger generation would dare claim they could match her.

Now, after Zane had taken down both Logan and Fiona, none of the First Tier warriors from any martial house dared to move. They knew that at their level, Zane would crush them in an instant. 

In truth, the only ones who might stand a chance were the crown princes of the divine kingdoms. The only question was whether any of them had the nerve to try.

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

But just as the royals from the Star Field Divine Kingdom were debating their next move, a figure stepped forward. It was the “Little Monster” from the Nine Cauldron Martial House.

In the York Divine Kingdom's corner, the Holy Maiden watched with a complicated expression. It was she who had gone to the trouble of personally inviting him out — specifically for this moment, specifically for Zane. Now, watching him cross the arena floor, she wasn't sure if it had been the right call.

[SFX: WHOOSH AND THUD]

His hunched back suddenly uncoiled. He leaped, somersaulted in midair, and landed inside the barrier in a single fluid motion — his black eyes already locked on Zane, burning with intense interest.

[MUSIC: UNSETTLING DISSONANT TONES]

A strange smile touched Zane's face. The moment he laid eyes on the Little Monster, something stirred in his gut — a deep, wordless revulsion he couldn't explain, as though his body itself was rejecting the creature in front of him.

“How about I challenge you?” the Little Monster said, smacking his lips.

“You're already standing here,” Zane replied flatly. “That answers your own question.”

The Little Monster shrank his long neck and kept grinning. “If I lose — can I kill you anyway?”

“The rules don't allow it.”

“That's a pity.” The black eyes gleamed. “I don't really know how to fight without killing. I don't have a good sense of propriety. What should I do?”

Zane's expression didn't change. “There are no guarantees in a fight between cultivators. Let's just start.”

“Okay!” The Little Monster's mouth split open in a sharp smile — his teeth were nothing like a human's.

[SFX: INHUMAN HISSING]

They came to points, row after row, like the teeth of something that lived in the dark.

[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS ON BARRIER WALL]

[MUSIC: UNSETTLING PERCUSSIVE THEME]

He moved like something that had never learned to walk upright. Scrambling along the surface of the barrier wall — sideways, upward, defying gravity — he closed the distance in seconds and launched himself at Zane with one outstretched hand.

[SFX: SPATIAL DISPLACEMENT WHOOSH]

Zane sidestepped through space and reappeared three feet away. He watched the Little Monster land and tilt his head.

No weapon. No Vitality. Just his body.

“You're not a body refiner,” Zane observed, “and I don't sense any Forceful Energy on you.”

“You're right,” the Little Monster shot back. “I don't need it to deal with you!”

The Little Monster clicked his tongue. “Your strength and talent are decent. But you're timid.”

Zane said nothing. He rose.

“Okay.”

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

With a ghost of a grin, Zane launched himself into the air, soaring straight up until he was hovering near the shimmering peak of the enchanted barrier. This fighting space, set up by a Soul Sea Realm master, was massive, but it always felt too small for warriors at the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.

[AMBIENT: LARGE ARENA]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

He looked down at the tiny figure of the Little Monster far below. “So,” he called down, his voice laced with amusement, “how exactly do you plan on getting up here to fight me?”

“You!” The Little Monster’s expression froze.

His physical strength was explosive, even without using Vitality, but Zane had created an impossible distance between them. He had no way to reach him.

Zane would have happily taken on any other warrior in a contest of pure physical strength—whether it involved Forceful Energy, Vitality, or raw power, he feared nothing. But he couldn't stand the Little Monster’s swagger.

Plenty of people in the crowd were happy to see him put in his place. Though many had improved their skills by sparring with him, the Little Monster wasn’t exactly popular. It was hard to like a guy who not only looked like a monster but was also cruel and unpredictable.

[SFX: CROWD LAUGHTER]

[MUSIC: HUMOROUS STRINGS]

When the Little Monster could only stand there, stumped and speechless, ripples of laughter broke out across the arena. The sound washed over him, and his face flushed a furious, humiliated red.
