Ep 1200 - Cora’s Journey

Word count: 1017

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL TENSION]
[AMBIENT: COSMIC RUMBLING]
The universe was on the brink of chaos.
The heavens themselves seemed to know it, blessing the most brilliant talents of the age with incredible luck. These chosen few were destined to lead the world through the coming storm, to become the legends of their time. 
But destiny is a fragile thing. Even the one meant to lead could be killed, and as chaos loomed, these destined warriors were fated to clash, each one striving to become the champion who would unite the races against the great danger threatening the universe.
[SFX: MYSTICAL PORTAL OPENING]
The Illusion Battlefield was where they would compete. It was a crucible designed to forge that champion.
[MUSIC: GENTLE ETHEREAL MELODY]
[AMBIENT: SOFT WIND CHIMES]
For the past five years, when Zane wasn’t training, he was using the Roaring Token to tell stories to a mysterious singer in a distant, ethereal world. She rarely had time for him, spending most of her days in a deep sleep, and would only wake once every year or two. The first thing she always did was demand a story.
And every time, Zane would ask for news about Elara. The young woman would tell him she’d had her people search, but that she’d heard nothing back.
So, despite his disappointment, Zane kept telling her stories.
He couldn’t be sure of anything, not really. The upper world she inhabited felt distant and unreal, a place that even the one hundred and twenty-eight Supreme Lords knew nothing about. But this mysterious singer might be his only hope of ever finding Elara.
[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]
[SFX: CELESTIAL BELLS]
As the heavens themselves shook, signaling the opening of the Illusion Battlefield, she woke up again. This time, however, she didn’t ask for a story.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ANNOUNCEMENT]
In a strange, cold voice, she announced, “The Illusion Battlefield has opened. This time, it will accept all Soul Sea Realm warriors in the universe.”
[SFX: STARS SPINNING RAPIDLY]
As her words faded, the stars began to spin wildly, and Zane opened his eyes. 
[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATIVE]
[AMBIENT: COURTYARD WITH DISTANT WIND]
He was sitting in the courtyard of The Elysian Keep.
“The Illusion Battlefield is your chance to shine,” Lavender said flatly, glancing at the swirling sky. “Get a high enough rank, and the World Lords posted outside will have no choice but to leave.”
“I tricked and killed the Supreme Lords of their races,” Zane shot back. “You really think they’ll just pack up and leave without getting revenge? And what about the Queen of Punishment? You think she’s just going to let us off the hook?”
Lavender smiled faintly. “The Queen of Punishment, of course...”
[SFX: SWIRLING SKY RUMBLE]
Above, across the spinning sky, beams of light began raining down across the universe — one for every Soul Sea Realm warrior in the hundred thousand supreme worlds. A circular spot appeared directly in front of Zane. He stepped into it without hesitation and sat down.
A moment later, another spot appeared in front of Freya. She stopped, looking at it uncertainly.
Lavender glanced at her. “It’s a vast illusion space,” she said. “No danger to your life. But for a Soul Sea Realm warrior, it’s a cultivation opportunity you don’t want to miss.”
Freya looked at the spot where Zane had just been. Then she stepped in.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CULTIVATION]
Far away, in the Violet Core Realm, Cora was alone at the top of a tall tower.
[SFX: SWORD ARRAY HUMMING]
[SFX: BLADES SHIFTING IN FORMATION]
Hundreds of thousands of giant swords circled around her, filling the air in every direction. She raised one hand slightly — and they rolled together at once, folding into a tight sword array that wrapped around her like armor. Then she turned her palm over, and the entire array shifted. The killing intent that poured off it was staggering.
But her face was still. So many years had passed since she’d last seen Zane. She’d spent every one of them here, training, waiting for the Illusion Battlefield to open. Her master had promised her: when it did, Zane would be in it.
She made a quiet gesture. All the swords flattened at once, spreading into a wide surface above the tower, hovering motionless. She sat down among them, knees drawn up, and let herself think about him.
[SFX: SOFT FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
Elder Apeiron appeared behind her.
“Master,” Cora said, without turning around.
“Are you thinking of Zane again?” Elder Apeiron asked.
“No,” Cora said.
Elder Apeiron smiled. “Of course not.” She moved to stand beside her. “He should have entered the Illusion Battlefield by now.”
Cora’s head came up. “It’s open?”
“It’s open.”
[MUSIC: UPBEAT ANTICIPATION]
[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL ECHOES]
Cora was so excited she was practically bouncing. She had been waiting for this day for so long.
“All Soul Sea Realm warriors can enter the Illusion Battlefield,” Elder Apeiron said with a nod. “You’re currently in the Violet Core Realm, which the beams of light can’t penetrate. Once I take you outside, you’ll be able to enter as well.”
“Are you sure Zane can get into the Illusion Battlefield too?” Cora asked, unable to hide her uncertainty.
Elder Apeiron gave her a small smile. “I’m not certain he can enter right this second, but I am certain he will get in eventually. If you want to see him, though, you’ll have to be strong enough to find him yourself.”
Hearing that, Cora’s confidence surged. “Okay, Master, I get it! I’ll give it my all.”
Seeing her disciple’s determination, Elder Apeiron nodded in satisfaction. “I’m confident you’ll do well. You’re my most outstanding student yet, and with your Violet-Core Physique, I have high hopes for you. Now, let’s go. It’s time to leave the Violet Core Realm.”
[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE CONTEMPLATIVE]
Over the past five years, Zane had used the Roaring Token many times across the universe. Elder Apeiron, who often traveled outside the Violet Core Realm, was well aware of his activities. She even knew that he had been ambushed by the World Lords of several races in the Northern Sky Region.
She’d decided not to tell Cora, however. Her disciple needed to focus on her training. If Cora was strong enough, she would naturally meet Zane in the Illusion Battlefield.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL JOURNEY BEGINS]
[AMBIENT: VAST VIOLET LANDSCAPE]
Cora strode forward, ready to leave with her master. She took one last look at the world bathed in violet light, her heart swelling. She knew her real journey was about to begin: the search for Zane.
