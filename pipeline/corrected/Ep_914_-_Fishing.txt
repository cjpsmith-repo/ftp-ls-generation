Ep 914 - Fishing

Word count: 1068

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC DESTRUCTION]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Zane was frozen in place, and the sheer chaos erupting in front of him wasn’t exactly helping him stay calm.

What the hell are these guys doing? he asked the girl in his mind, the shock plain in his voice.

Brianna snorted. Who knows? The Demi-Immortals are a bunch of lunatics. Seriously. They once tried to wipe out every star in the universe. I think they got through more than twenty thousand before the heavens finally slapped them down. Hard. A lot of them died, so they had to scrap that little project.

Her words left him reeling. He remembered being recognized by one of them back in the Tower of Sin. Did that mean he was a member of this bizarre race?

He really, really hoped not.

[SFX: RUMBLING DEBRIS]

Zane’s body was pulled forward against his will, drifting through piles of rubble until he came upon a massive circular crater. Six figures stood at its edge, among them the burly man and the black-clad youth he’d seen earlier.

[SFX: EARTH CRACKING]

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS RUMBLING]

From the center of the crater, drakes began crawling out of the earth, one after another.

[SFX: MULTIPLE DRAKE ROARS]

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

[SFX: SONIC WAVES]

The monsters immediately swarmed the people surrounding them, and all six were instantly bombarded by invisible waves of sonic force. The attacks never landed, though. The young man in black simply brandished his saber, and with each swing, black light streamed from his hands, cleaving the drakes into sprays of bloody mist.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CRATER ATMOSPHERE]

“Damn thing’s patient,” the burly man grumbled. “It just refuses to come out!”

Dorothea strolled over, the small army of Soul Sea masters she controlled trailing behind her like puppets. “Then maybe we need a different approach.” She turned to a man in a silver mask. “Your foresight is uncanny, Max. The Heavenly Stellar Secret Land was supposed to be empty, but it looks like we found some bait after all.”

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE FROM CRATER]

The seven Demi-Immortals had gathered here to capture whatever creature lived in that crater.

Max, draped in a blood-red cloak that contrasted with his silver mask, just nodded. “Let’s try it. Wade, you’re up.”

“Sure thing, Max,” one of them answered.

He had a tattered straw hat pulled low over his face and an easy, good-natured smile. From beneath the brim, he produced a golden fishing rod.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

He gave it a lazy flick. A thin green line sailed out toward the crowd of frozen warriors and snagged one of the Soul Sea masters, lifting him into the air. The man's soul was locked inside the corpse — Dorothea's confinement had seen to that — so he hung there, limp and helpless, unable to resist.

Wade lowered the line slowly toward the crater.

Halfway down, dozens of drakes exploded out of the pit.

[SFX: MULTIPLE DRAKE ROARS]

[SFX: SONIC BLAST BARRAGE]

The roars hit the dangling corpse from every direction at once. There was nothing left. Body and soul — gone, reduced to fine dust in an instant.

Max was unmoved. “Hmph. Backbone. It didn't eat. Put more in.”

[MUSIC: DARK DREAD]

The remaining masters watched in silent horror. Their souls were screaming inside their skulls. They'd clawed their way to the Soul Sea Realm. They'd just learned the ascension passage could be repaired. And now they were bait.

Wade's line flicked out again — and this time it caught six at once. Among them were two masters from the Profound Jade Martial House, counted among the most powerful in all four kingdoms. The line didn't dangle them. Wade released it and let them drop straight into the pit.

[SFX: BODIES FALLING]

At the same moment, the flower in Dorothea's eye turned slightly.

[SFX: SCREAMS FROM THE PIT]

Screams erupted from below — raw, desperate, unmistakably human.

The young man in black looked up from his work. “Why are they screaming?”

Dorothea's smile didn't waver. “I released part of the confinement. Let them add a little flavor for whatever's down there.” She tilted her head. “You're welcome.”

“Little Big Sister, you're terrible,” he said, shaking his head with a grin.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS DRONE]

The screams lasted two, maybe three seconds. Then silence.

[SFX: BONES CRACKING]

From deep inside the crater came the slow, rhythmic sound of something working through what had been thrown to it.

“Should be done,” Dorothea said pleasantly. “Again.”

Wade just laughed, clearly used to her threats. He flicked the rod again. The green line trembled and arced outward — and this time it was heading straight for Zane.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Zane’s attention snapped back to Brianna's voice in his head.

Don’t rush, she told him, her tone unnervingly calm. The guy with the fishing rod is only at the Spirit Supreme Realm. Out of all of them, only that one named Max is stronger than me. The real question is, how much of my strength can you actually handle?

Her Vitality was completely alien to his own; he couldn’t control this body well enough to tap into its full power. He could try to run, but he wasn’t sure he’d make it. 

He had only one shot at this. He had to be smart.

But help came from the last person he expected. 

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Just as a green thread snaked out toward him, Dorothea cut in. “Wade, leave this one to me!”

“She’s alive?” Wade exclaimed, genuinely surprised. He’d just now realized the girl wasn’t a corpse.

Dorothea nodded.

“Oh, no wonder,” Wade chuckled. “For a second there, I thought you had a thing for corpses.”

Dorothea’s face twisted. “Shut your mouth, or I’ll kill you.”

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Wade just laughed, clearly used to her threats. The green thread trembled for a moment before changing course, heading instead for War King York. With only seventy Soul Sea masters left, the odds of one of the four Emperors being chosen were high. It looked like York had drawn the short straw.

As the line shot toward him, the flower in Dorothea’s right eye spun almost imperceptibly, and the force restraining him eased just enough.

War King York suddenly roared, his voice filled with terror. “I am the Emperor of the York Divine Kingdom! I’ll give you anything you want! Just spare my life!”
