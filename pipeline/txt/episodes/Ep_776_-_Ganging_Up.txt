Ep 776 - Ganging Up

Word count: 1064

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

 For a split second, panic seized the masters trapped inside the Bewitching Domain. Their hearts hammered against their ribs, but the fear vanished as quickly as it came.

Fights between Soul Sea masters weren't like ordinary brawls. They were wars that began with the clashing of personal domains—unique pockets of reality—before descending into contests of raw Vitality and physical might. A warrior’s domain was everything. Some were aggressive and overwhelming, while others acted more like support. Lord Wallace’s domain was designed to play mind games, to disrupt an opponent’s focus and leave them too irritable to channel their power effectively. In a one-on-one duel, it would have been devastating.

But stretched thin across nine different masters? The effect was so diluted it was barely a nuisance.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

 Inside the hazy domain, Dorian casually waved his hands. Spiraling ribbons of color erupted from his palms, twisting around him like vibrant tendrils before detonating in a blinding flash. He became a miniature sun, an orb of pure light too brilliant to look at directly.

“A mind-altering domain?” Dorian’s voice boomed from the heart of the light, laced with contempt. “Cute. But far too weak to matter.”

The Violet Heart Sanctuary might be one of the lower-ranked sacred places on the main continent, but that didn't mean its champions were weak. If they were, the sanctuary—and the entire human race along with it—would have been wiped out long ago. Their survival was owed in large part to their three strongest masters, and Dorian was one of them. While he might not have been on the same level as Amber or the leaders of the Flaming and Dread Places, he was still easily one of the ten most powerful warriors on the continent.

“Soul Division Domain!” he roared.

This was the true power of a Soul Sea master: the ability to impose your will on reality, to carve out your own little slice of the world and rewrite its rules. Of course, the influence was limited. If you could change the rules of the entire universe at will, you’d be a god.

Within the range of Dorian's light, tens of thousands of copies of him appeared — clones, each carrying a thousandth of his true strength. At a glance, it looked like an army. They closed in on Wallace from every direction.

Wallace's eyes went wide. He slapped his own forehead, and an eye condensed above his head — ancient, unsettling, the kind of thing you felt before you saw. The Heartfire Evil Eye. He wouldn't normally waste something like this on a fight like this, but normal had stopped applying the moment nine Soul Sea masters surrounded him.

"Heartfire Evil Eye!"

[SFX: CRACKLING FLAMES]

Tongues of colorful flame swept outward. They were powerful — genuinely powerful — and for a moment the sea of fire rolled through Dorian's clones, burning them clean.

"Not bad," Dorian said from somewhere inside the light. His voice was almost admiring. "Unfortunately, not nearly enough."

The clones began to reappear. A steady stream of them, pouring back in from every direction. Each one wore the same faint smile. Each one raised a hand.

"Hand of Calamity!"

[SFX: MASSIVE ENERGY RELEASE]

The attack came from ten thousand directions at once — the combined force of ten thousand clones, each carrying a thousandth of Dorian's strength, all striking simultaneously. It was like being hit by ten Dorians at the same time.

On the city walls below, Zane watched with burning eyes. A battle between Soul Sea masters — the kind of thing most warriors never saw in a lifetime. He wasn't going to waste a second of it. He studied every exchange, every clash of domains, turning it over in his mind. What would his own domain look like when the time came? Would it hold its own against something like this?

Around him on the walls, the effect on the city's warriors was visible. Some had gone pale and quiet — men who had spent their whole lives pushing toward greatness, suddenly face to face with how far they still had to go. A few had the look of men who'd already given up. But others were different. Their eyes were bright and hungry, fixed on the sky as if they were memorizing every move. For them, this was fuel.

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

[SFX: LABORED BREATHING]

 Huddled inside his defensive turtle-shell artifact, Lord Wallace had barely survived Dorian’s initial assault. He was in terrible shape, his face a sickening dark red that spoke of deep internal injuries.

Dorian was actually a little surprised the man was still alive. The Heartlands might be weaker than the main continent, but the divine inheritances they guarded were clearly superior. Without that turtle-shell artifact, Wallace would have been atomized.

“I’ll drag you to hell with me!” Wallace snarled through gritted teeth.

A cruel smirk spread across Dorian’s face. “Fight me?” he asked, letting the question hang in the air. “Or us?”

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

 Wallace had assumed it would be a one-on-one duel. And Dorian was confident he could win, though killing another Soul Sea master in a fair fight was notoriously difficult. Besides, he had no intention of letting Wallace escape with some cheap trick. As if on cue, the domains of the other eight masters began to converge, their distinct energies pressing in on Wallace from all sides.

[MUSIC: DARK STRINGS]

 The moment he felt their power closing in, Lord Wallace’s heart plummeted into his stomach. He was a dead man.

He’d left the York Divine Kingdom burning with ambition, crossing the vast Chaotic Ocean of Stars with dreams of conquest. He never, ever imagined the Heartlands would be his grave.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

[AMBIENT: THUNDER AND WIND]

Then, all nine masters attacked at once. A storm of twisted, overlapping domains collapsed on Lord Wallace and Master Abel, trapping them in a vortex of raw power. The sky above Oracle City rocked with thunderous explosions, sending a shiver of dread through everyone watching from below.

[MUSIC: SOMBER SILENCE]

 And just like that, it was over.

The two mighty warriors from the York Divine Kingdom, men who might have one day become Emperors, were annihilated. 

Watching from the city walls, Kieran and the others could only shake their heads. What a waste.
