Ep 727 - Jealous Hearts
Word count: 1143
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Tia looked Cora straight in the eyes, and in that unguarded moment, she saw a flicker of the truth. It was a truth that jabbed at a familiar insecurity, a sore subject she rarely let surface.
She’d been so buried in her duties at the Cloud Hall that she hadn’t found a single moment for a real conversation with Zane. Two years he’d been gone, and she still had no idea what had truly happened—why he’d returned now, or what had transpired in the Celestial Tomb of Vesper. And now this: the young lady from House Kensington, appearing on their doorstep looking for him. The questions piled up, each one more unsettling than the last. She made a silent promise to herself: once her work was finished, she and Zane were going to talk. A serious one.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Tia’s mind raced, trying to place the girl. Cora Kensington. She’d heard the name before. In fact, Tia herself had suggested the Ethereal Spirit Sect arrange a marriage for her, and quickly. Cora possessed the rare Violet-Core Physique, a dangerous gift that would make her a prime target for a monster like Maddox. If he ever got his hands on her, the fallout would be catastrophic, and could even drag Elara into the mess.
So how did a sheltered girl like Cora even know Zane? Had she followed him all the way here?
At Tia’s pointed question, a wave of humiliation had washed over Cora’s face. For all her stubbornness and short temper, she was still a young lady from a noble house, and discussing such personal matters in front of strangers was mortifying.
That flicker of shame only deepened Tia’s confusion. With her mind already racing through a dozen different scenarios, she kept her voice even. “Drop the enchanted barrier and let her in.”
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER DISSOLVING]
At her command, the shimmering energy field around the Cloud Hall dissolved. The moment Cora stepped inside, the barrier reformed with a faint shimmer, sealing them all in once more.
Zane, however, just stood there, completely stunned and unsure how to react.
[MUSIC: TENSE EMOTIONAL STRINGS]
His mind went back to the underground palace — Maddox closing in, Simon closing in, and the cold certainty that if Cora fell into Maddox's hands, the ritual would be complete and nothing could stop him.
What he'd done, he'd done to protect her. Their sleeping together had awakened her Violet Core Physique in a way no forced ritual ever could. He'd told himself it was the right call. Maybe it was. Hell, it had been her idea. But that didn't make it fair to her, and he knew it.
Whatever else was true, she was the first woman in his life — and she was standing right here, as though there was nowhere else she'd rather be. He felt the energy between them, in spite of himself.
Ignoring the stares of everyone in the hall, Cora drifted the last few feet and leaned against Zane’s side.
Seeing the intimate gesture play out right in front of her, Tia’s expression hardened.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION BUILDING]
A hot, sharp surge of anger flared through her chest. But she was the leader of the Cloud Hall. She couldn’t afford to show a single crack in her composure. A master of controlling her emotions, Tia ruthlessly shoved the anger down, burying it deep beneath a mask of serene authority.
"Miss Cora," she said pleasantly. "You still haven't told me. Why did you follow Zane to Cloud Hall?"
The elders standing behind her took one look at that smile and began finding reasons to be somewhere else. Within moments, Saint Morphen and the others had quietly melted back toward the hall entrance. This was not their problem.
"I have nowhere else to go," Cora said. "The Ethereal Spirit Sect is under attack. I can't go home."
"And why Zane specifically?" Tia pressed.
Cora blinked. Something in Tia's tone put her on guard. She looked at her steadily. "I'm willing to follow him. Does the Hall Master have a say in that?"
Tia opened her mouth. Then closed it.
The girl was right. Zane was a Cloud Hall member, not her personal property. Even the strictest sect in the Heartlands wouldn't presume to dictate something like this. She had overstepped, and everyone still present knew it.
A flash of something crossed her face — not anger. Loneliness, maybe. Then it was gone.
"You're right," she said quietly. "I overstepped." She glanced at Zane, just for a moment. "She has the Violet Core Physique. She can't fall into Maddox's hands. Let her stay here for now."
Then she turned and walked away.
Zane watched her go. A crawling discomfort settled over him. Tia didn't yet know the full story of what had happened in the underground palace. He was not looking forward to the moment she found out.
A faint red light slipped out from him — Lavender, stepping back into the world for the first time in two days. She watched the direction Tia had gone, and she was smiling.
"She already reacts like that," Lavender said, mostly to herself. "I wonder what she'll do when she finds out the rest."
Cora's face went scarlet. She said nothing.
Zane's expression was one of pure suffering.
"Come on," he said flatly. "I'll find you a room."
[AMBIENT: QUIET NIGHT]
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
The room Zane arranged for Cora was, by the Cloud Hall’s standards, quite comfortable—certainly a step above the quarters of the other female disciples. But Cora couldn’t get used to it.
She sat straight up on the bed, hugging her knees as she stared out at the dark sky. A small, determined smile touched her lips. This life was nothing like the one she knew, but for Zane, she would endure it. It seemed the picky, stubborn young woman had finally found a reason noble enough to withstand a little hardship. Anyone from her old life would have been shocked to see her now.
But Cora wasn't the only one lying awake that night.
[AMBIENT: ICE PALACE]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL MEDITATION]
In the quiet cold of the ice palace, Tia tried to focus on her cultivation. Having just broken through to the Life-and-Death Realm, she needed to stabilize her power and learn to manage her Vitality. This new realm allowed her to liquefy her internal energy, the first step toward the Soul Sea Realm, where she could forge that liquid into a spiritual sea—and eventually, create an entire world within herself.
But even in the meditative silence, her mind was a storm.
Cora’s face, her shy but possessive gesture toward Zane, kept flashing in her thoughts, shattering her concentration.
Tia wondered, with a sharp pang of uncertainty, if she even had the right to ask him about any of it.
