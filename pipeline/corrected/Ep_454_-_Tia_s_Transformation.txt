Ep 454 - Tia's Transformation

Word count: 979

[AMBIENT: MARTIAL TOURNAMENT GROUNDS]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Dealing with Lady Darkmere from Bloodwood Cliff and Raven from the Black Mountain Order was the easy part. They were skilled, sure, but they weren't the kind of fighters who could intimidate a seasoned warrior. Bloodwood Cliff and the Black Mountain Order were both fourth-grade sects, but they were a joke compared to Cloud Hall. Cloud Hall was known as the best of the fourth-grade, home to some of the most promising young warriors in the land.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

But the fifth-grade Ethereal Spirit Sect was a different story entirely. Outside of the World Merchants' Guild, they were the most powerful and influential force in the Heartlands.

[SFX: DRAMATIC WHOOSH]

When the Septuplets of the Ethereal Spirit Sect appeared, malicious glee flickered across Lady Darkmere's and Raven's faces.

The Martial arts tournament crackled with tension. Multiple factions vied for the Dragon Soar Arena, but the real prize was its gateway to the upper world—which only Zane could activate. The investigation had revealed altered runes. Without him, the arena was worthless.

No one expected a Cloud Hall disciple to hold the key. They couldn't let him go.

[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]

[MUSIC: EPIC RISING]

The crowd gasped. Phoenix feathers erupted from Tia's back, spreading like a blazing fan

[SFX: ICE CRYSTALLIZATION]

Ice crystals clung to her eyebrows. She examined the changes to her body as an icy blue halberd materialized in her grip.

[SFX: WEAPON MATERIALIZATION]

The frozen blade stretched nine feet nine inches—nearly twice her height.

Zane's eyebrows shot up. He'd never seen her wield such a weapon on the Cloud Road. How could someone so petite even lift it?

So this was the real Tia.

Disappointment crossed Lady Darkmere's and Raven's faces. They'd hoped Tia would surrender Zane, but she clearly intended to protect him—even against the Ethereal Spirit Sect's overwhelming power.

The character on Tia's forehead faded. Her eyes became frozen oceans, endless snowflakes swirling in their depths.

"Zane is a disciple of Cloud Hall," she said, her voice cold and distant. "As my student, I am obligated to protect him. Whether you represent Bloodwood Cliff, the Black Mountain Order, or the Ethereal Spirit Sect, if you believe superior strength will intimidate me into submission, you are profoundly mistaken."

Frost covered her beautiful face, revealing calm defiance.

[SFX: PHOENIX CRY]

A phoenix cry rang out. The ice phoenix had grown to over ten feet, its crystalline body radiating dreamlike luminescence.

The creature dove toward Tia.

She tilted her head back as icy light enveloped her. Fragments of ice crystals swirled, condensing piece by piece into crystalline armor that accentuated her figure. The remaining light spread along her back, forming long phoenix feathers.

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLE ATMOSPHERE]

[SFX: CRACKLING ICE ENERGY]

The air crackled around the icy halberd in Tia's hand. Her eyes flashed with a dangerous light, promising destruction to anyone who crossed her.

[SFX: SWORDS DRAWN IN UNISON]

The Septuplets of the Ethereal Spirit Sect remained stone-faced as they advanced, moving into a battle formation and drawing their swords in perfect, chilling unison.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

"Tia. Are you actually declaring war on the Ethereal Spirit Sect?" the leader of the Septuplets asked, his voice laced with disbelief.

"I want nothing from you but to be left alone," Tia replied, her voice as cold as the weapon in her hand. "But Zane is a disciple of Cloud Hall. I will protect him."

Zane's chest tightened. He was moved, but he couldn't drag Cloud Hall into a war for his sake. He hadn't meant for any of this to happen, and he wasn't about to let someone else clean up his mess. This was his problem, and he would be the one to solve it. As grateful as he was for her loyalty, he couldn't let anyone get hurt defending him. An idea began to form, a way to settle this himself.

"Cloud Hall has grown fast," the leader of the Septuplets sneered. "Impressive, for your level. In another ten years, you might even stand with us." His voice dropped, laced with menace. "But you're getting ahead of yourself, Tia. If I were you, I'd learn my place. Picking a fight with us will cost Cloud Hall everything!"

[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

"Don't fool yourself," Tia shot back. "I know you look down on us, but don't think we'll be an easy fight. You'll take Zane over my dead body. And while you're busy bleeding out against us, just remember all the other sects in The Heartlands, watching and waiting."

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR ARENA]

Suddenly, three large men appeared, looking like the overfed sons of a rich landlord. Their bodies jiggled with each heavy step.

Though they were all heavyset, it was easy to tell them apart.

The first had surprisingly gentle features, with skin as smooth as silk and impossibly long eyelashes.

The second was sun-darkened and fierce, with a scowl that could curdle milk. He was the kind of person who terrified you on sight.

As for the third, he had a healthy, rosy complexion and a permanently cheerful expression, like a jolly friar ready to help any stranger he met.

They were all gasping for breath, clearly in terrible shape.

[SFX: EXHAUSTED BREATHING]

They finally stumbled to a halt before the Dragon Soar Arena, utterly spent. Their massive frames—helpful in combat, perhaps—had become burdens during the desperate sprint. Each stride had demanded enormous effort, and now sweat poured from their bodies in rivulets, darkening their robes and dripping onto the cobblestones.

The gentle-faced man planted both hands on his hips and doubled over slightly, his chest heaving. Despite his kind features, frustration flickered across his expression. "Okay, stop, stop!" The words came out between ragged gasps, barely coherent.

His companions fared no better. The sun-darkened one with the boar-like scowl wheezed loudly, his fierce demeanor undermined by the way he braced himself against his knees. Even the cheerful third brother had lost his jovial air, his rosy complexion now flushed an alarming crimson as he struggled to catch his breath.

Zane looked at them, confused. What was so urgent to make them run with this much desperation?
