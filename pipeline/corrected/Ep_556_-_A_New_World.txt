Ep 556 - A New World
Word count: 1110
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL TOMB SILENCE]
After everyone left, a deep quiet settled over the Celestial Tomb of Vesper, its chaotic energy finally gone.
[SFX: GRINDING STONE]
[AMBIENT: DUSTY CHAMBER]
A section of one of the huge walls ground slowly aside, kicking up clouds of dust that hung in the still air. As it settled, it revealed a giant teleportation array, easily ten times bigger than any he'd ever seen.
[SFX: TELEPORTATION ARRAY HUM]
A low hum began to build from the array. Zane didn't hesitate, stepping straight onto the platform.
Right now, he had two more tokens in his Storage Ring. The one inscribed with the word "thunder" was the key to the Elysian Keep's true path, but he couldn't use it yet. He'd have to get back to the Eastern Territories for that.
On top of that, he’d also scooped up the remaining treasures from the massive coffin.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
But the most important prize was the Crystal Butterfly from Vesper. The old man had been maddeningly vague, telling him only that the legacy beneath the Tower of Sin was crucial—tied to the fate of all humanity—but refusing to say what it actually was.
All Vesper would explain was that the Crystal Butterfly would give him a chance to unlock the inheritance. He’d also warned that the path, and the destination itself, were incredibly dangerous. It was why he’d chosen someone with Zane’s kind of luck for the job.
People blessed with great luck were notoriously hard to kill. It was almost impossible to take them down, and even their chances of dying from natural causes were slim.
Zane’s own life was solid proof; he’d faced one impossible danger after another and was still standing. He had the kind of luck a journey like this demanded.
"That’s all I can tell you," Vesper had said, his voice grave. "You may be destined to preside over an age of chaos, but that doesn’t make you immortal. Your luck makes you difficult to kill, nothing more. Warriors with destinies just as great have fallen before. Be careful. The rest is in the hands of fate."
Zane had nodded, the weight of it all settling on him.
He had to stay alive, not just for himself. Elara's fate—the fate of the entire Cloud Hall—was tied to his. Maddox couldn't break their enchanted barrier for now, but that wouldn't last forever. If he found another warrior with a Violet-Core Physique, the balance of power in the Heartlands would shatter.
"Are you ready?" Vesper asked.
"Yes," Zane said.
[SFX: BLAZING LIGHT SURGE]
[MUSIC: EPIC SURGE]
Blazing white light poured down like liquid, flowing through the channels of the array and tracing a vast, luminous pattern across the floor. When the light filled every line of the array, a blinding radiance erupted.
A force like a giant's hand seized Zane and hurled him upward into the array. Blue light swallowed him whole. He was moving at a speed far beyond anything he'd experienced before, hurtling through the channel.
Then the blue spatial storm hit.
Long-distance arrays always had unstable zones — weak points where space itself fractured into violent turbulence. The first time Zane had encountered one, he'd sheltered behind a stronger warrior and caught a glimpse of something extraordinary: the first layer of the Law of Space.
He wasn't afraid of spatial storms anymore. He moved through the blue currents like a fish through water, reading the patterns, feeling for the Law within.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE UNDERSCORE]
But after a few passes, his excitement faded. The blue storm was made entirely of first-layer Space Law — nothing he hadn't already mastered. He could teleport short distances, ten feet at most, but breaking through to the second layer required something this storm couldn't offer.
Disappointed, he turned to exit.
[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]
[MUSIC: SUDDEN DANGER]
And walked straight into a wall of purple.
This storm was nothing like the blue one. It was threaded through with deep, complex patterns — veins of Space Law so far beyond the first layer that Zane couldn't even begin to read them. Before he could react, it pulled him in.
The purple storm tore at him. He could feel it trying to rip him apart at the seams.
[SFX: IMPACT — BODY HIT]
He fought the current with everything he had, but the storm held him like he was nothing.
Without his tempered body, he'd have been shredded. Even so, damage mounted with every second—internal injuries compounding. His vision blurred.
Zane lost consciousness, carried helplessly by the purple storm.
[AMBIENT: WILDERNESS — OPEN SKY]
[MUSIC: DISORIENTED STRINGS]
On a distant, nameless continent, a massive vortex split the sky. Deep thunder rolled. Birds burst from the canopy; animals scattered.
Zane shot from the vortex and fell.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT — CRATER FORMING]
The ground shook. Dust erupted. When it settled, Zane lay at the bottom of a crater, staring upward.
Clouds drifted lazily across the blue sky.
He had no idea where he was. This is bad.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON DIRT]
A couple of hours later, a small head poked over the edge of the crater.
It was a boy, maybe eight or nine years old, sturdy-looking with bright, curious eyes.
Zane tried to speak, but couldn't even manage a whisper. He couldn't feel his Vitality at all; trying to circulate it was out of the question.
[SFX: CHILD SHOUTING]
"Lori, come look!" the boy yelled, his eyes wide but showing no trace of fear as he called for someone to come see.
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
A moment later, a girl's face appeared next to his. She was about the same age, her own big eyes blinking in terror. "He's alive!" she gasped. "I'm telling Dad."
[AMBIENT: DESOLATE CRATER]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
"Shh!" the boy hissed, his gaze fixed on Zane. "Don't tell Dad. I got you a present!"
The girl asked, her curiosity overriding her fear, "What is it?"
[SFX: SLIDING ROCKS]
The boy grinned and slid nimbly down the side of the crater. He landed softly next to Zane, then reached out and plucked the ring from his chest.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS DRONE]
A cold fury flared in Zane's eyes.
That was Tia's Storage Ring. It held nearly everything he owned, but more than that, the ring itself… it was irreplaceable.
The problem was that Zane couldn't move. He couldn't even twitch a finger to stop the kid from taking it. All he could do was watch, helpless, as the boy scrambled back up the crater wall.
"Here, it's for you," the boy said, grinning as he held it out.
"No! You stole it! I'm telling Dad!" The girl recoiled, her voice sharp with anger.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS RUNNING]
"Ugh, why do you have to be like that? Fine, forget it!" the boy snapped. He turned and vanished over the crater's edge.
The girl gave Zane one last, pitying look before running after him.
Lying in the crater, Zane heard her voice call out from a distance, "If you don't give it back, I'm telling!"
Zane looked up at the sky. ‘This is really bad.’
