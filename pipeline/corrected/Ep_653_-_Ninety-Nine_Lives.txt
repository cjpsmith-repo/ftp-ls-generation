Ep 653 - Ninety-Nine Lives

Word count: 1230

[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD]

[SFX: DISTANT FLAMES CRACKLING]

The battle had raged for a full day and night. By the time it was over, the streets of the imperial city were choked with bodies, the stone slick with blood beneath the orange glow of a thousand fires. The war had cost the people everything.

Zane, for his part, had single-handedly cut down ten masters of the Inner-Core Realm. Only one had escaped his wrath: the princess, Isla. Now, after leading his troops to storm the palace, he had finally taken the throne he’d fought so relentlessly to claim. He stood at the highest point of the palace wall, gazing out at his new world.

[MUSIC: EPIC TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

A million soldiers in gleaming gold armor knelt before him, a sea of devotion that was as spectacular as it was terrifying. This was it. The absolute peak of his life. And there, dragged before him, was Isla, the last descendant of House Novak, now his captive.

“I should be the one to kill the last princess of House Novak,” he declared, his voice ringing with authority.

[SFX: SWORD SWOOSH]

With a roar, he leaped from the wall, a golden sword materializing in his hand as he descended upon Isla.

[SFX: METAL CLANG]

But just as the blade was about to kiss her throat, a flash of sword light shot out of nowhere, deflecting his attack. Zane’s sword was knocked harmlessly aside.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

“Finn?” Zane stared. There he was, dressed in a simple white robe, his expression completely unreadable. A million soldiers surrounded him, yet not a flicker of fear crossed his face. “Who are you?” Zane couldn’t help but ask.

Finn offered a faint, almost condescending smile. “Who am I? I’m a swordsman.”

“Then why are you here?” Zane demanded.

“The Empire is an ally of the Skyreach Sect,” Finn said casually, as if explaining something to a child. “And the Skyreach Sect is under the authority of Cloud Hall. I come from Cloud Hall. If you want this throne, you have to get our approval first.”

“Go to hell!” Zane bellowed, launching himself at Finn.

[SFX: INTENSE SWORD CLASH]

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

Steel screamed against steel as they clashed, their movements a blur. They exchanged a thousand strikes in the blink of an eye, the air crackling with the force of their blows. Finally, Finn retreated a hundred feet and raised his longsword. In that instant, the world seemed to bend to his will.

[AMBIENT: THUNDER RUMBLE]

[SFX: PRESSURE WAVE]

Dark clouds roiled overhead, and a suffocating pressure descended, crushing the morale of Zane’s million-strong army.

“With your level of strength,” Finn said, his voice cutting through the unnatural silence, “you’re nowhere near qualified to rule this country.”

With that, he lunged. The strike was so blindingly fast, so overwhelmingly powerful, that Zane couldn’t even raise his blade to block it.

[SFX: SWIFT BLADE STRIKE]

Finn’s sword sliced clean through his neck.

The moment he died, a tidal wave of resentment, rage, and crushing helplessness washed over him.

[AMBIENT: VOID EMPTINESS]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL TRANSITION]

The scene dissolved, and Zane found himself adrift in darkness once more.

He was beginning to understand. The Gate didn't just show you lives — it made you live them. Every emotion in that body, every shred of unwillingness and hunger and grief, flooded into you at the moment of death. You absorbed it. And through that absorption came something harder to name: a deepening. A weight added to the spirit that no amount of training could replicate.

So this was how a warrior's spirit was forged in the Upper World.

Before he could finish the thought, the golden spiral appeared again.

[SFX: DEEP RESONANT HUM]

[MUSIC: ANCIENT ETHEREAL]

In his second life, Zane was not a warrior at all. He was a cook — rough hands, smoke-stained clothes, days measured by fire and the smell of meat. Unremarkable in every way, until the day he found a jade pendant buried in a random carcass. That pendant changed everything. He left the kitchen, walked onto the path of a martial artist, and never looked back.

No great sect took him in. He built his own, stone by stone, until it stood as tall as any institution in the Eastern Territories.

The third life: a scholar. A demon invasion. A city holding its walls through sheer force of literature and will.

The fourth. The fifth. The sixth.

[MUSIC: MONTAGE — SWEEPING STRINGS]

Life after life spun past him — each one vivid, each one complete, each one ending in death and releasing a fresh torrent of emotion that crashed through him and settled somewhere deep. The specific textures of a hundred different fates, each leaving its mark.

Zane moved through them all without losing himself once.

[AMBIENT: VOID EMPTINESS]

[MUSIC: QUIET RESOLUTION]

When the ninety-ninth life ended, and the darkness returned for the last time, there was no new spiral. Just stillness.

Zane stepped out of the Gate.

He was slightly dazed. The weight of ninety-nine lives pressed on him from every direction, more than he could sort through in a single moment. He stood still for a beat, just breathing.

"How many cycles did you go through?" Esther asked.

Everyone was looking at him. He hadn't realized until now how long he'd been inside.

"What?" he said. "Didn't everyone go through the same number?"

Fabian let out a breath. "You were in there for two hours. We thought you'd died in there."

"The number of cycles is set by the strength of your warrior's spirit," Yolande said. "The stronger the spirit, the more lives the Gate will give you. But if your spirit wavers — if you start to lose yourself and can't tell the reincarnation from reality — the Gate releases you immediately."

"I went through nine," Fabian said.

"Six," Jarrod said, with no particular embarrassment.

Esther said, "Thirteen." She looked at Zane. "How many for you?"

Zane thought for a moment. "Ninety-nine, I think."

"Ninety-nine!" Fabian's eyes went wide. "And you never lost yourself? Not once?"

Zane just looked at him, genuinely puzzled. "Lost myself? Why would I?"

Fabian was speechless.

"Ninety-nine is the Gate's maximum," Yolande said quietly. "Built from the Six Paths of Reincarnation, it breaks most people. That Zane reached the limit and returned intact…" She trailed off.

Fabian eyed the shimmering portal. "If only I could bring it to the Violet Heart Sanctuary."

Lavender sneered. "And keep it how?"

"What makes you think we couldn't?" Fabian bristled.

"Because outside the Cursed Land, its aura would be exposed," Lavender said flatly. "Supreme masters from the upper world would burn this continent to ash for it."

Fabian paled. Some artifacts in the Dark Nether Cave were too powerful for any lower world faction to protect.

Zane stared at the Gate, the phantom emotions still clinging to him. Finally, he shook them off. “If that’s the case, let’s keep exploring.” The Gate of Many Lives was incredible, but it wasn’t the inheritance Vesper had spoken of.

“I’m afraid you won’t get the chance!”

A deep voice boomed through the cave, so loud it was physically painful, making their ears ring. Zane froze. He knew that voice. Whipping his head toward the sound, he saw a burly, familiar figure emerge from the shadows in the distance.

[MUSIC: DARK DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

It was Malcolm.
