Ep 720 - Made For Each Other
Word count: 1177
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]
[MUSIC: HAUNTING STRINGS]
Tia’s head throbbed, her vision swimming in and out of focus. This was what happened when a soul was injured. It was like being blackout drunk, the kind that made you see things that weren't there. And right now, she thought she could see Zane.
He was so special to her, the one person she missed more than anything. But she knew it was just a hallucination brought on by weakness, a sign that she was about to die.
A bitter wave of disappointment washed over her. She had been so overconfident, so sure she could survive this Life-and-Death Tribulation. How could I have been so stupid? she thought, kicking herself. All she wanted was to see Zane one last time. The illusion of him made her feel warm, giving her a phantom flicker of strength.
[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]
[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL BUILD]
Her battered mind projected nightmares into reality. To others, the swirling chaos resembled petals on the wind, but to her, monstrous beasts clawed at her soul. Zane's figure flickered into existence, moving toward her. With her last strength, Tia reached out and pulled the illusion into a desperate embrace.
As Cloud Hall's master, her status had always exceeded Zane's. Yet since meeting him on the Cloud Road, she'd felt an inexplicable pull that defied rank. None of it mattered now. This wasn't real—just a ghost conjured by her dying mind.
Two years of training had made him tall, lean, and powerful. He stood a full head taller than her now. She wanted only to hold him one last time. As her arms closed around empty air, a broken smile touched her lips. An illusion of Zane was better than nothing at all.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
[SFX: RUSHING AIR]
From above, the real Zane saw her start to collapse, and a primal fear—the fear of losing her—seized him. He dove toward her, the Celestial Fragment clutched in his hand. The moment he was within reach, Tia’s arms wrapped around him, her hug so tight it left him stunned.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Holding her close, Zane activated the Celestial Fragment just as Lavender had taught him. The fragment was special, she’d explained, capable of absorbing an immense amount of Vitality. Don’t cause a surge, she had warned. Just enough to get it working.
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY ABSORPTION]
As Zane pushed a sliver of his energy into the artifact, it came alive, sucking in the deadly petals. Lavender had told him the Celestial Tribulation was a force of nature—one that the fragment could easily absorb.
They were opposing forces, but strangely, they were made for each other.
[SFX: WHIRLWIND DISSIPATING]
The effect was almost instantaneous. The petals swirled inward like a whirlwind and vanished. The sky above the ice palace went still. The tribulation was over.
Tia's eyes stayed shut. She leaned against his chest, her expression somewhere between peaceful and wrecked. Every so often, her head gave a small, involuntary shake, the last echoes of the nightmares working through her.
[AMBIENT: ICE PALACE INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: TENDER PIANO]
Zane landed on the floor of the ice palace and sat cross-legged, settling her into his lap. He reached into his Storage Ring and pulled out an emerald green pill — a soul-healing medicine from the main continent, stronger than anything the Heartlands could produce.
When he pressed it to her lips, she frowned and turned away. He eased it back, waited, and tried again until she swallowed it. Then he placed his hand flat against her stomach and fed a thin thread of Vitality into her to dissolve the medicine, since she was too weak to do it herself. She didn't resist. In her state, she couldn't have if she'd tried.
After a few minutes, her breathing steadied. She tilted her head against his chest and slept.
[SFX: HEAVY DOORS OPENING]
The doors to the ice palace opened. Saint Morphen stepped in, followed by two Cloud Hall elders, drawn by the silence where the tribulation had been. They stopped when they saw Tia asleep in Zane's arms. A strange look passed between them — not entirely surprised, given the rumors that had circulated since Zane first joined the hall. But it wasn't their place. Saint Morphen began to speak. Zane raised a finger to his lips. The three exchanged a quiet nod and withdrew.
Whatever doubts they might have had about Tia's new strength, they didn't voice them. She had only survived one wave of the tribulation — but her technique was unlike anyone else's. Even freshly entered into the Life-and-Death Realm, her strength would not be far behind those who had held the title for years.
[AMBIENT: MORNING BIRDS]
Twelve hours passed. Then fourteen. The sky outside the hole in the roof had gone dark and come light again. Morning had broken by the time Tia's lashes began to flutter.
Tia felt the rough texture of clothes clutched in her fists and a solid warmth against her cheek. Am I still alive? she wondered, completely disoriented.
She took a shaky breath, just to prove she still could.
Her eyes snapped open and met Zane’s handsome face, inches from her own. A jolt shot through her, making her tremble from head to toe.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL STRINGS]
Zane, who had been meditating beside her, opened his eyes. Relief washed over him when he saw her staring back.
He broke into a wide smile. "You're awake!"
Tia pressed her lips together, her mind refusing to believe what her eyes were seeing. This had to be another illusion, a trick her mind was playing to keep her happy.
She ached to reach out and touch his face, to feel if he was real, but she couldn't bear the thought of him dissolving like smoke. Illusions were fleeting, and she didn't want to risk making this one disappear.
"Say something," Zane urged, his smile faltering into a worried frown. He wondered if the tribulation had damaged her soul, if her mind was fractured.
Tia’s gaze drifted past him, up to the massive hole torn in the sky. Clouds drifted lazily across the opening, and bright rays of morning light streamed down, illuminating air thick with the wild, chaotic energy of Heaven and Earth.
It wasn't an illusion.
It was Zane. He had really come back for her.
[MUSIC: TENDER PIANO]
As the realization sank in, the corners of her mouth turned up in a slow, radiant smile. Her heart swelled with a joy so powerful she thought she might burst. She wanted to laugh, to shout his name to the heavens, but instead, her eyes suddenly turned red, and hot tears began streaming down her face.
Zane just stared, completely baffled. Why was she crying? She was alive. The Life-and-Death Tribulation was over. She was more powerful than ever. So what was with the tears? He started to seriously worry that her mind had been damaged. She was laughing and crying at the same time. What in the world was wrong with her?
