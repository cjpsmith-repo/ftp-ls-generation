Ep 618 - A Low Profile
Word count: 1062
[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUND]
Lavender admired the new silver spear, a satisfied smile playing on her lips as she spun the weapon, its weight feeling just right in her hands. She caught Zane’s surprised stare and leveled a calm gaze at him.
“It’s a shame you’re so set on the sword,” she said, her voice smooth. “Otherwise, I could teach you some of the Demon Night’s spear arts.”
Zane didn’t say anything, just blinked. Her spear arts were incredible—one of the signature disciplines of the Demon Night. But he’d chosen the sword from all the weapons in the world, and he couldn't abandon it now. It was a rookie mistake for any martial artist to get greedy and try to master multiple weapons at once. If he picked up a spear, he’d have to start all over again, training himself from scratch to understand spear intent. He wasn’t about to do that.
But when Amber heard Lavender’s offer, she saw something else entirely.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
It was unheard of for one of the three queens of the Demon Night to offer their secret techniques to anyone. Even the other two queens couldn’t master Lavender’s unique style. Yet here Lavender was, seemingly willing to teach her signature discipline to a total outsider.
Something wasn’t right. That, combined with the broken statue she’d seen back at the palace, made Amber suspect that something major had happened in the upper world.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Unfortunately, she had no way of knowing what. Amber might be the leader of a seventh-grade sacred place, but a Soul Sea Realm warrior like her would be a nobody in the upper world’s Demon Night. At best, she’d be a squad leader. A battle between the queens was so far above her pay grade that it was laughable. She’d have no place in a conflict like that unless she could somehow become a master in the upper world herself.
She’d already tested her aptitude on the Talent Tablet and made it to the second row. That was a massive achievement on the main continent, but it meant nothing in the upper world. When she’d first taken the test, there had been five names ranked above her. Now, there were two more: Zane and Yolande. Even though Amber wasn’t in the Tower of Sin, most sacred places had spies moving between the two, reporting back on every major event.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
So, of course, she already knew Zane had kicked two Ogre War Sages out of the Tower in the blink of an eye. This human kid was a real force to be reckoned with. If he could really eject any living creature from the Tower of Sin, he basically controlled the entire place.
[SFX: SPEAR HANDED OVER]
"I'm done choosing," Lavender said, handing the spear to Zane. "Store it for me."
As his sword spirit, she had no way to carry anything herself. The spear could only be drawn when she needed to fight. Zane took it and slipped it into his ring without comment.
"Go ahead and look around," she added. "Take what you need."
[MUSIC: THOUGHTFUL CONTEMPLATION]
Zane turned back to the treasury. He already had the top-grade Relic-Class sword Amber had given him, and glancing across the vault, nothing else called to him. He shook his head—until a different idea surfaced.
"Do you have any high-grade crystals?" he asked.
Amber blinked. "Crystals? For inlaying weapons?"
"You could say that," Zane said.
She assumed he was a weapon refiner, or knew one. Either way, she didn't press it. At a gesture from Amber, one of the Demon Night War Sages crossed to a corner of the vault and returned with an embroidered box. Inside, nestled in rows, was a collection of crystals in every shape and color—Fire Condensing Crystals pulled from the depths of active volcanoes, Purple Cloud Crystals dredged from deep-sea trenches, and a dozen others, each polished to a uniform size.
"How many do you need?" Amber asked.
"Two."
She gave him a curious look but said nothing further. A War Sage stepped forward and walked Zane through each crystal's properties one by one.
Back when Zane first inlaid crystals into his arms in White Emperor City, he'd chosen the Phoenix Soaring Crystals for speed—his greatest weakness at the time. But he'd since comprehended the Law of Space. Speed was no longer a problem. The Blood Jade Crystals had served him well, too, their energy capable of shattering barriers—but the barriers he faced now were built for War Generals and War Lords. A single crystal wasn't enough anymore.
He needed something new.
After listening carefully, Zane selected two crystals that suited where he was now, not where he'd been. He didn't explain his choices to anyone.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR WIND]
[MUSIC: DETERMINED MARCH]
Two days later, Zane, Esther Sterling, and Cheryl left the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary and headed back to the Tower of Sin. During their time there, Amber had made it clear that if Zane intended to get rid of the ogres in the Tower, the Demon Night would do whatever it took to help him.
The ogres were everything the Demon Night was not: violent, chaotic, natural-born killers who stood in direct opposition to their values of peace and order. On the main continent and in the Tower, the ogres had always been a thorn in their side. It was only after Amber brought the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary to power that they’d finally started to back off.
If a chance to wipe them out for good presented itself, Amber wasn’t going to miss it. As a master of the Soul Sea Realm and the leader of the Demon Night, she had no intention of showing them any mercy. That was just how the world worked. Being kind to your enemies was the same as being cruel to yourself.
[SFX: MAGICAL TELEPORTATION WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: TOWER INTERIOR]
After passing through the teleportation array, Zane arrived back in the Tower of Sin. The two massive gates that the Ogre War Sages had smashed were already fully repaired.
When Zane left, only the warriors on the tenth floor knew what had really happened. But in the few days he’d been gone, the story of his victory had spread to every corner of the Tower. Someone—no one knew who, or why—had made sure every single person heard how he’d taken down the Ogre War Sages.
And ever since, the ogres inside the Tower had been keeping a very, very low profile.
It wouldn't last.
