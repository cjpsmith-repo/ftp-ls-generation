Ep 476 - Primordial Energy

Word count: 1065

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL STRINGS]

Back in the Upper World, the Cobalt Dragon had managed to swallow the Owl Beast's malevolent spirit.

The spirit had fractured into three remarkably useful fragments. The first possessed restorative properties capable of healing the Cobalt Dragon; the second held enough power to awaken one of his slumbering brothers from their long hibernation; the third could help Zane forge a soul seed—a foundation for unprecedented spiritual growth.

After extensive contemplation, weighing each option against centuries of wisdom, the Cobalt Dragon selected awakening his brother. Zane accepted this decision without complaint, understanding that some bonds were more important than even his own urgent needs.

[MUSIC: MEDITATIVE DRONE]

[SFX: DEEP BREATH]

Settling into a cross-legged position, Zane released the tension coiled in his shoulders and spine. He closed his eyes, drew a measured breath that filled his lungs, then exhaled slowly. His thoughts scattered like leaves on water as he sank into a quiet stillness, allowing his soul to drift through layers of consciousness into the profound depths within.

[AMBIENT: ECHOING VOID]

[SFX: DRAGON SCALES SHIFTING]

In that interior realm, Zane discovered the Cobalt Dragon wrapped protectively around the colossal Soulforge. The dragon was a fearsome sight, but that intimidating expression transformed the instant recognition flickered across those ancient features. The harsh lines softened, and something almost paternal warmed the dragon's gaze as he regarded Zane.

[SFX: HEAVY MOVEMENT]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS LOW STRINGS]

Uncoiling from the Soulforge with deliberate grace, the dragon slithered toward Zane. His massive jaw opened, revealing something extraordinary resting on his tongue—a huge, black bead that pulsed with concentrated power.

Zane's breath caught. "Is that it? The bead from the Owl Beast's spirit?"

The Cobalt Dragon nodded his head in confirmation. "It is," he said, his voice carrying both satisfaction and hope. "Absorbing that spirit made me considerably stronger. The energy it contained was... remarkable." He paused, his ancient eyes growing distant for a moment. "Hopefully, this will be enough to wake my brother. He's been dormant for far too long."

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

Without further ceremony, the Cobalt Dragon spat out the bead. Zane watched as it arced over their heads, propelled by an invisible force. The sphere shot straight toward the dragon-shaped sculptures etched into the Soulforge's surface, disappearing into the open mouth of one.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

[SFX: LIQUID TRANSFORMATION]

The instant the bead made contact with the sculpture, transformation began. It melted, dissolving into a thick, viscous black fluid that reminded Zane of the Soul Essence refined by Nether Phantoms, but infinitely purer. The sculpture appeared to drink the dark matter, drawing it inward with an almost sentient hunger.

The very fabric of the space responded. Reality itself seemed to ripple and shift around them.

[SFX: ETHEREAL VOICE ECHO]

A new voice, laced with dry amusement, resonated through Zane's mind. "Zane?"

"Yes," Zane answered, unable to suppress the eagerness and pride coloring his tone. He had no idea what the dragon found so funny.

"Across this universe, trillions upon trillions of creatures draw breath," the seventh brother began, his words measured and deliberate. "But you possess something utterly unique—a body unlike any other in existence." He paused, letting the weight of that statement settle before continuing. "And what did you do with the Primordial Energy refined through its transformation? You used it to... clean out your insides?" Disbelief saturated every syllable. "Tell me, what exactly was going through your mind?"

Zane's brow furrowed. "Primordial Energy?" The words felt foreign on his tongue; he was completely lost.

The seventh brother released a sound somewhere between a scoff and a laugh—bitter and incredulous. "My brother never explained this to you?" Frustration sharpened his tone. "Your body doesn't merely contain Primordial Energy, boy. Your body is a vessel of Primordial Energy itself!"

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

His words hung in the air. "It's a damn shame you have no idea how to use it," the dragon began, his voice dropping low and cold. "A total waste. The moment you reached the Adept Realm, you switched to the Vitality cultivation system and just... flushed the Primordial Energy. You used it to cleanse your body because you didn't know what else to do with it! I bet you have no idea how precious that energy really is."

"What?!"

The revelation hit Zane like a physical blow. He didn't know. If he'd had any idea, he never would have been so careless.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]

The dragons were the supreme beings of this world—practically gods. If they weren't in their current state, either of them could shatter the universe and leave The Heartlands in ruin with a single swipe of a claw. That was the kind of power they wielded. The energy that these mighty beings valued so highly, he had used like cosmic drain cleaner. His jaw hung open, utterly speechless. He felt like a total idiot.

"However," the seventh brother said, his voice flat, "my brother told me you risked your life for me. I owe you a debt. To repay it, I can teach you the Body Refining arts." He paused. "Let me be clear. I've never been a fan of teaching these skills to humans. But your body… it's a special case. It would be a crime not to refine it. The only question is whether you can handle the strain." The offer was a rare opportunity, and Zane intended to seize it.

"I'm warning you again—the refinement will be intensely painful. Prepare yourself," the dragon cautioned.

In the space of his mind, Zane's soul simply nodded. He was ready.

[AMBIENT: QUIET COURTYARD]

[MUSIC: CALM MEDITATION]

When Zane's consciousness returned to his body, he blinked, shaking his head to clear the mental fog. He took another deep breath to center himself before stepping out into the courtyard. With the Martial Tournament happening nearby, renting a private space like this was outrageously expensive. But with two hundred supreme %%vitality_crystal%%s to his name, the cost was nothing. He'd rented the whole place for the day and had it all to himself.

Standing in the middle of the open space, he heard the seventh brother's voice in his mind. "I will now begin refining your body with its own Primordial Energy."

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC]

The moment the dragon finished speaking, Zane's entire body seized up, going completely rigid.

What was happening? He couldn't move!
