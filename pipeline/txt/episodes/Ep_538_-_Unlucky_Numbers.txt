Ep 538 - Unlucky Numbers
Word count: 1050
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL TOMB]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
A stunned silence fell over the group. A single, six-sided die floated before them. Here, in a celestial tomb shrouded in mist? It made no sense.
It had materialized out of thin air, spinning lazily before its rotation bled away to nothing. Now it just hung there, suspended in the space between platforms.
[SFX: MAGICAL BEAM]
While everyone was still trying to wrap their heads around it, a single beam of light shot from the die’s core. It swept across the crowd like a searchlight, passing over the factions from Bloodwood Cliff and the Ethereal Spirit Sect, over Zane and Tia, over every single person gathered there.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The beam’s rotation accelerated, becoming a blur, then gradually began to decelerate, the suspense building with every passing second until it finally settled on an elder from the Black Mountain Order.
The elder on the platform found it hard to breathe. A tremor ran through his hands. "What... what is this thing?" he stammered.
No one could answer. A tense, ignorant silence was the only reply. This was something new, something terrifyingly unknown.
[SFX: OMINOUS FLICKERING]
A moment later, the light fixed on him began to flicker. Its brilliant yellow sputtered, flashing to a blood-red, then back again, the two colors warring for dominance like ancient enemies locked in eternal combat. The oscillation grew faster, more violent, casting the elder's horrified face in alternating shades of gold and crimson.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
Real panic set in. The elder's chest heaved as his breathing turned ragged. His eyes darted wildly to his sect leader, Raven, searching desperately for salvation. "Sir, save me!"
Raven stood on a nearby platform, close enough to see every detail of his subordinate's fear-twisted features. Yet the shimmering green barrier separating them might as well have been a mile-thick wall of steel. It was unbreakable. His fists clenched at his sides, knuckles white beneath his dark robes. Even if he wanted to intervene—and the rigid set of his shoulders suggested he did—he was utterly powerless.
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
[SFX: VAPORIZATION]
The flickering stopped. The beam locked in, a solid, blinding crimson that bathed the entire chamber in hellish light. A terrifying wave of energy erupted from the focal point, building in intensity until the air itself seemed to scream. In a silent, searing flash that left afterimages burned into every witness's vision, the elder was simply… gone. Vaporized. Not even ash remained to mark where he had stood moments before.
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD GASPS]
A collective gasp rippled through the crowd. Jaws hung open, eyes wide with disbelief. Raven’s face was hidden behind his mask, but you could tell from his rigid posture that he was just as floored as the rest of them.
The power in that beam was absolute. Unstoppable. The man it had just erased was a master of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, yet he’d been annihilated in an instant. Not even a warrior from the Life-and-Death Realm could have withstood that blast.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]
[SFX: PULSING ENERGY]
What the hell was this thing? A game of chance where the loser was executed? The thought sent a chill down every spine. Before anyone could process it, the die pulsed, and the crimson beam began to spin once more.
A silent, desperate prayer echoed in everyone's mind: Not me. Please, not me.
[AMBIENT: COLLECTIVE RELIEF SIGH]
As the beam slowed again, a sudden realization swept through the crowd. It wasn’t an executioner. It was a selector. You were chosen, you attacked the die, and the number you rolled determined how many steps you could take. A collective sigh of relief washed over them, thick enough to feel. The fear didn't vanish, but it was replaced by a tense understanding.
[SFX: ENERGY BEAM ACTIVATION]
After Zane took his two steps, the beam reignited. It whirled around the platforms before coming to rest on Brent, a disciple from the Ethereal Spirit Sect.
[SFX: DICE ROLL]
[SFX: PLATFORMS MATERIALIZING]
Brent grinned and threw a casual punch. The die tumbled through the air and settled on a six. Instantly, six glowing discs materialized before him. As he confidently stepped across them, he shot Zane a smug look over his shoulder. "Looks like my luck runs a little hotter than yours," he teased.
Zane just chuckled, completely unfazed.
The beam spun for a fourth time, this time landing on an elder from the Mysterious Luna Hall. The man smirked, channeling his power. As a Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior, he put about eighty percent of his strength into the blow, sending the die rocketing into the air. It came crashing back down and landed on three.
"Three. Not bad," the elder grunted, stepping forward without a hint of hesitation.
[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]
The moment his boot touched the third disc, the platform's translucent white surface flashed to a burning, fiery red.
"Blazing Thunder Prison," a disembodied voice whispered through the tomb, each syllable dripping with ancient malice.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: AGONIZED SCREAMS]
An inferno erupted from beneath the elder's feet, engulfing him in a cage of fire that rose twenty feet high. His confident smirk vanished, replaced by wide-eyed terror as he realized his Spirit-Tribulation Realm cultivation offered no protection.
At the same instant, veins of crackling purple lightning tore through the air above, striking down again and again like rabid serpents hunting prey. The elder's screams—raw, primal, filled with the kind of agony that strips away all dignity—were lost in the roar of the flames and the crack of thunder as his flesh blackened and peeled away.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
Zane watched, his jaw clenched tight. Even he felt a chill run down his spine. This wasn't a test of strength or skill—it was pure, merciless execution.
After a few agonizing seconds that stretched like an eternity, the fire and lightning died out. The disc's glow faded back to translucent white. All that remained where the elder had stood was a pile of smoldering ash, still smoking, with fragments of bone visible among the char.
A new, more profound horror settled over the survivors like a suffocating blanket. Several cultivators took involuntary steps backward.
The die wasn't the only threat. The path itself could kill you—and it didn't care how powerful you were.
