Ep 125 - Attack of the Demons
Word Count: 1306
[AMBIENT: CRUMBLING STONE]
[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
 Rubble and dust cascaded down the lower floors as heavy footsteps thundered up the ancient stone staircase. Zane, his heart pounding against his ribs, led the frightened disciples from the Skyreach Sect through a narrow side stairwell toward the barracks rooftop. His muscles burned with exertion, but fear drove him forward. 
[AMBIENT: OPEN AIR]
 When they finally burst onto the open rooftop, the horrific panorama before them stole the breath from their lungs and froze their blood.
[AMBIENT: DISTANT BATTLE]
 The once-pristine sky had transformed into a churning nightmare of dark, ominous clouds and acrid smoke that stung their eyes. Zane's fingers instinctively tightened around his weapon as he surveyed the grim scene. In the distance, like a relentless tide of darkness, countless demon figures swarmed the city walls—Demon Commanders with their grotesque features and mindless Grunts, scaling every vulnerable side of Silverspire with unnatural speed and coordination. 
The disciples exchanged terrified glances, their faces pale with the realization of what they faced.
[SFX: WINGS FLAPPING]
[AMBIENT: AERIAL THREAT]
 As if this vision weren't sufficiently terrifying, the remaining patches of sky disappeared beneath the wings of black, humanoid Harpies. Their massive, scaly appendages cast moving shadows across the city like death's own fingers. The creatures' eyes gleamed with malevolent intelligence as they circled overhead, their muscular bodies adorned with primitive armor, each clutching a longbow that promised swift death from above. 
Zane swallowed hard, tasting fear like metal on his tongue as he watched the invasion unfold before his disbelieving eyes.
They circled high above, each armed with a longbow. Hundreds more clenched Demon Commanders and Grunts in their massive talons, and as they soared over the edge of the city, they simply opened their claws, dropping the demons straight into the streets below.
This sky-borne horde wasn't even the main force of the enemy's army. They were just the vanguard, the opening act sent to soften the city for the true horrors to come.
For years, the Twin Spires had kept war from Silverspire’s gates. But with the towers now fallen, the demons had wasted no time, mounting a full-scale assault with shocking speed.
[SFX: ARROWS FLYING]
[MUSIC: IMMINENT DANGER]
 The leathery beat of Harpy wings cut through the air. Spotting the disciples, the creatures banked, aiming their longbows. A volley of black arrows rained down. 
Luckily, the arrowheads were crude, and the disciples’ training allowed them to deflect the barrage. Zane was their main shield, batting arrows from the air.
Down on the walls, it was a different story. The rank-and-file soldiers, most only at the Marrow Refining Level, were struggling. They could barely hold their own against the Grunts; a Demon Commander was a death sentence.
But military orders were absolute: no retreat. There was a saying in the imperial army: "As long as you're breathing, the catapult keeps firing." Abandoning your post meant a fate worse than death, and besides, their pride as imperial soldiers wouldn't let them run.
[SFX: CATAPULT CRANKING]
A Demon Commander advanced toward the catapult position. While two soldiers frantically cranked the siege weapon's mechanism, their companion drew his blade and positioned himself in the creature's path, hoping to purchase his comrades a precious few moments.
The gesture appeared futile, yet it embodied the spirit of Silverspire's imperial army. Having battled demonic forces throughout the year, surrender had vanished from their vocabulary. Death before retreat had become their unspoken creed.
Though merely at Marrow Refining Level, the soldier fought against the paralyzing terror threatening to seize his limbs. He inhaled deeply, steadying his quivering hands as he raised his sword. Sweat beaded on his brow, but determination hardened his gaze—if death awaited, he would face it standing.
[SFX: BONE CRUSHING IMPACT]
 The Demon Commander barely acknowledged his existence. It continued its relentless climb, dismissing the human with a casual backhand. The devastating blow shattered the soldier's ribcage and launched him from the wall, his scream fading as he plummeted to his death below. Nothing now stood between the demon and its target.
[SFX: SCREAMING FALLING MAN]
[SFX: CATAPULT LAUNCH]
 The remaining soldiers launched one final volley skyward, their fingers moving with practiced precision as they nocked and loosed arrows with grim resolve. 
[SFX: EXPLOSION]
 A demon bird and its rider erupted into brilliant flames, momentarily illuminating the soldiers' faces. Their expressions remained eerily calm despite the certainty of their impending doom. In their eyes flickered not fear but acceptance—they would soon join their fallen comrade.
The Demon Commander reached the catapult. As it raised a clawed hand to smash them, a flicker of danger made the hairs on its neck stand up. It whipped around—nothing. A fluke. It turned back to the soldiers.
[SFX: WHISTLING BLADE]
 At that precise moment, a Shattered Dagger hurtled through the air toward the demon's face!
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
 The creature's crimson eyes widened in sudden recognition of danger. Too late to evade the incoming threat. Primal instinct seized control of its movements, and the demon thrust its right arm upward, confident that its thick, battle-hardened hide would deflect the insignificant human weapon.
This confidence proved a fatal miscalculation. The blade was anything but ordinary.
[SFX: BLADE SLICING THROUGH FLESH]
 The dagger carved through the demon's forearm with terrifying ease, slicing through layers of leathery skin, dense muscle, and calcified bone as though passing through morning mist. Without losing momentum, the weapon continued its deadly trajectory with a sickening, wet sound of tearing flesh until it embedded itself deep within the demon's skull. 
[SFX: MEATY IMPACT]
 The impact split the creature's head cleanly in two, spraying dark ichor across the stone battlements. Both halves of its ruined head collapsed onto the cold stone with a hollow, meaty thud. Even as death claimed it, the demon's eyes remained fixed in an expression of profound shock and disbelief, as if unable to comprehend how a mere human weapon had ended its centuries of existence in a single, perfect strike.
After retrieving the Shattered Dagger, Zane nodded once at the two surviving soldiers. They stared back at him, speechless.
[AMBIENT: DYING BATTLE SOUNDS]
Then, as suddenly as it had begun, the demon army pulled back. The harpies banked away from the walls, the Grunts retreated, and an eerie quiet settled over Silverspire. They'd left nothing behind but corpses.
Back in the barracks, no one celebrated. Everyone understood what the withdrawal meant.
"That was a probe," Dalton said. "They were testing the defenses. The real attack is coming."
[AMBIENT: NERVOUS MURMURING]
[MUSIC: TENSE DISCUSSION]
 "If no one's coming for us, why don't we just try to get back on our own?" one of the disciples suggested. "We just have to avoid the demons, right?"
The idea caught on like wildfire, a spark of hope in their growing panic. They weren't playing a major role in this war. The imperial army certainly wasn't going to waste resources protecting them once the full-scale invasion began.
It wasn't as if the Sect hadn't tried. Word had filtered through that two rescue Skycruisers had already been sent — both shot down by demon birds before they got close. The Sect wasn't coming until the skies cleared.
Dalton and Zane could probably handle a Demon Commander or two, but what if a whole legion descended on them? Or worse, a Netherlord? They'd all be wiped out in an instant. The truth was, the disciples were completely out of their depth.
[SFX: ARMORED FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: DOOR SLAMMING OPEN]
 Just as their argument started to get heated, the heavy clang of armored footsteps sounded from the other side of the barracks door. The door flew open, revealing a general in the imperial army's full dress uniform.
 "All disciples of the Skyreach Sect are dismissed," the general boomed, his voice carrying over the din. "Except for Zane!"
