Ep 132 - Familiar Darkness
Word count: 1442
[AMBIENT: TENSE CONFERENCE ROOM]
 Doubtful murmurs rippled through the room. Coming from anyone else, it would've been dismissed as a joke. Hugh had been a Soul Realm warrior for some time now. Even if he wasn't the strongest among them, he was still leagues beyond any High Smith.
Everyone knew the power gap between the Soul Realm and an Advanced Initiate was like a tiger to an ant. So, when did a Soul Realm warrior get so weak he could be beaten by a High Smith? It was why Yolande Shaw's claim left them so baffled.
But this was Yolande Shaw. She wasn't one for lies; if anything, she was brutally honest. If she said this boy—a mere High Smith—had nearly killed Hugh, then it had to be true. They wanted to believe her, but they just couldn't wrap their heads around how it was even possible.
"Are you serious, Yolande?" Axel asked, his surprise echoing the sentiment of the entire room.
"I am. If I hadn't stepped in, Hugh would be dead," Yolande replied, her voice as calm as ever.
[SFX: ROOM FALLING SILENT]
 The room fell silent once more. As all eyes turned back to Zane, they held a new, strange light. To defeat a Soul Realm warrior while still only an Advanced Initiate… it was unbelievable. What kind of monster was this kid?
While the others were filled with curiosity, Hugh was burning with shame and fury. After all, he was the one they were talking about. His face flushed a deep crimson, so dark it was almost purple, and he looked like he might actually combust.
 "No, no, no! She's exaggerating! I wouldn't have died! Maybe I'd have been badly hurt, but that's it!" Hugh sputtered, jumping to his feet to defend his honor.
The words were barely out of his mouth before he realized how stupid and impulsive he sounded. If the room hadn't been packed with so many important figures, he might have burst into tears. He replayed the entire disaster in his mind, wishing the ground would open up and swallow him whole.
"Don't worry about the military office…" Zane heard someone whisper. It was Lennie, who had leaned in close and gave him a knowing wink.
The military office was a dangerous place for most soldiers, but Lennie seemed confident he could handle it. Zane had, after all, killed an imperial army counselor named Josef. But for Lennie, getting Zane clear of the military office seemed like a simple matter.
Zane nodded to show he understood.
Axel leaned back. "Yolande. The Military Law Office is reopened — effective now. Release him to the deacons and they'll hold him there until this is resolved."
Yolande blinked. Considered it. Nodded. "That's acceptable."
She waved a hand and the barriers dissolved. Zane straightened up slowly, working the stiffness out of his limbs.
[SFX: DOOR CLOSING]
 Soon enough, a few officers from the military office arrived. Following Axel's orders, two of them escorted Zane from the room, shutting the door quietly behind them.
[AMBIENT: MILITARY COMMAND ROOM]
 After they were gone, Axel began to lay out the imperial army's battle plan. The others gathered around the table as the discussion started.
"The demons must have been planning this attack for a long time," Axel began, his tone analytical. "The fact that they're bold enough to attack the city directly means we can't underestimate them. We need to be extremely careful."
The demons never attacked unless they were thoroughly prepared and certain of victory. For ages, the Sky and Stone Spires had stood untouched, never directly assaulted. But last time, the demons came prepared and caught the imperial army completely off guard, capturing both towers in a single, devastating battle.
And now, they were coming for Silverspire. Who knew what they had in store, or what kind of power they were banking on this time?
Determined not to repeat the mistakes made at the Sky and Stone Spires, everyone in Silverspire was on high alert. They couldn't afford to let their guard down; they were taking the threat of this attack very seriously.
"The Four Dreadlords attacking Silverspire this time are incredibly powerful, especially Aldrich and Orion," Axel said, his voice grim. "The true extent of their power is a complete mystery. When the fighting starts, I propose that a few of us focus our efforts on containing Aldrich."
Axel went on, naming specific officers for the task. Everyone, especially those he called out, leaned in, listening intently.
[SFX: HOLOGRAPHIC DISPLAY ACTIVATING]
 As he spoke, Axel pressed his palm flat on the tabletop. Holographic images shimmered into existence in the middle of the table, which, it turned out, was a massive Engraved Slab. Displayed on it were the names of the Four Dreadlords, along with their known strengths and weaknesses. Using the intel, they began to hammer out a strategy.
 Suddenly, Axel's expression shifted, and a wry smile twisted his lips.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE PREPARATION]
 "Looks like this meeting is being cut short," he announced. "I can feel several Colossal Wraiths heading straight for Silverspire!"
He didn't need to explain. Everyone knew the Wraiths had to be the vanguard for the Four Dreadlords Axel had just been talking about.
"In that case, let's go knock them out of the sky!" an old man declared. He had been standing silently nearby until that very moment.
[SFX: CHAIRS SCRAPING]
[AMBIENT: SOLDIERS MOBILIZING]
 Axel nodded in agreement. "Let's get ready to fight!"
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: CRASHING THROUGH ROOF]
 At his command, everyone rose to their feet. They rushed out of the room, some bursting right through the roof of the governor's house in streaks of brilliant light.
Yolande, however, remained perfectly still for a moment. Then, she tilted her head as if recalling something and shot into the air. But instead of blasting through the roof, she zipped out the front door, hovering just above the rocky ground.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR ATMOSPHERE]
 A short distance away, Zane was walking with the military officers.
"You'll need to wait things out in the military office while the city's under attack," one of the officers told him. "Don't worry, it's clear the governor has no intention of making trouble for you."
Hearing that, Zane just nodded. Anywhere was better than being near Yolande Shaw. The sheer power of her aura was physically agonizing to him.
He'd barely nodded when he heard footsteps behind him — fast ones. He turned.
Yolande's fist drove into his stomach before he could react. His body buckled under it, doubling him over.
[SFX: IMPACT — SHARP AND HEAVY]
Transparent barriers snapped into place around him again.
"Master Yolande," one of the deacons said carefully, "the Commander himself ordered his release—"
"I'm worried he'll escape," Yolande said. "I'm taking him with me." She didn't wait for a response.
[SFX: SUDDEN WHOOSH — VERTICAL]
The ground dropped away beneath him. In seconds, Silverspire had shrunk to a map below — walls, streets, the Governor's Mansion all miniaturized. Zane pressed against the side of the box, dizzy, thousands of feet up and rising.
He turned to shout at her. Thought better of it.
He was a fish on a chopping board. She could do whatever she wanted.
[SFX: ARROWS WHISTLING]
A flock of demon birds banked toward him, loosing arrows. They pinged off the barrier without leaving a mark and slid away into the air below.
At least the box was good for something.
[SFX: BILLOWING FABRIC]
Meanwhile, Yolande hovered above them, her snow-white dress billowing around her like a cloud. Beneath the fluttering hem, her long, straight legs were visible, her simple leather sandals looking as if they might slip off at any moment, yet they clung stubbornly to her feet.
[MUSIC: SUMMONING DARK MAGIC]
[SFX: SWOOPING WINGS]
[SFX: IMPACT AGAINST BARRIER]
 Harpies swooped in to attack her, but their strikes slammed against an invisible barrier, deflecting uselessly.
Suddenly, a faint light gleamed in the whites of her eyes, and she squinted.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIELD SHATTERING]
 No one saw exactly what she did, but the magic shield around her shattered into a thousand invisible pieces. The fragments—jagged triangles, perfect circles, and other impossible shapes—shot outward. An unseen force propelled them toward the Harpies like a shower of crystalline rain.
[SFX: SLICING SOUNDS]
[SFX: DEMONIC SCREAMS]
 The invisible fragments tore through the Harpies instantly, slicing the demons to pieces.
[SFX: BODIES FALLING]
 A few of the demons let out shrill, metallic squawks—the lucky ones. Most were instantly shredded, their scaly limbs showering the battlefield below.
As dismembered Harpies cascaded from the sky, Zane shook his head, a sinister delight coursing through his veins while he observed Yolande's merciless carnage. 
"Absolute fools," he mused, savoring each falling corpse. "Of all the opponents to challenge, you chose Yolande." The corners of his mouth curled into an unsettling smile as the demons' remains littered the ground. Their doomed assault stirred something primal within him—a dark, inexplicable pleasure that both thrilled and disturbed him. What troubled him more was not the satisfaction he felt watching their slaughter, but how familiar that feeling had become.
