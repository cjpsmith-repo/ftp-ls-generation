Ep 313 - A Human Weapon
Word count: 1082
[AMBIENT: FIGHTING ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Zane had somehow managed to do the impossible: he’d set off Patrick’s temper. The guy was always so calm and collected; nobody thought he even knew how to swear, but he now muttered expletives under his breath, his legendary composure cracking like thin ice.
Even stranger, the battle unfolding in the arena didn’t look quite right. Something about it seemed bizarrely distorted to the confused spectators.
To the casual observer, the match was an utterly one-sided beatdown.
Zane absorbed blow after punishing blow, occasionally managing to land a single counterattack only after enduring a barrage of Patrick’s strikes. His body twisted and jerked with each impact, yet never crumpled.
[SFX: BODY HITS]
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
But that was the strange part—with each successful hit Patrick delivered, Zane’s vitality visibly intensified. His eyes blazed with an almost supernatural glow, as though he were gaining energy rather than losing it. A slight smile played at the corners of his mouth, as though each punishing blow merely fed some hidden reservoir within him.
Meanwhile, Patrick, despite dominating the exchange, was showing increasing signs of distress. His brow furrowed deeply, sweat beading across his forehead in frustration. His expression cycled between confusion, anger, and a dawning, terrible realization that perhaps he had gravely misunderstood the nature of this confrontation from the beginning.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
What the crowd couldn’t see was the warm current of energy flooding Zane's body with every blow. There was no pain. In fact, it felt... good. How could he not be stoked when getting pummeled felt this incredible?
The crowd was loving the raw, hand-to-hand brawl. But for Patrick, it was just a tedious, pointless slugfest. Still, he had no choice but to keep fighting, to keep landing punches on Zane.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER CRACKING]
After a while, the golden light shimmering around Patrick began to flicker and dim, especially on his chest and arms where Zane had landed his counterattacks. The light in those spots faded completely, revealing the skin beneath.
The whole point of the Form of the Adamant Soul was to make a person's body indestructible, but its power came from that golden light. After taking too many hits, the Vitality that formed the barrier would just... shatter. And once that happened, the Form of the Adamant Soul was basically worthless.
[SFX: CROWD ROARING]
"Holy crap, he broke it! Zane broke through the golden light with his bare hands! I knew it! That guy’s a monster!" someone screamed from the stands.
"He’s not a monster, he’s a weapon! A living, breathing magical weapon in human form! It’s like nothing can even touch him!"
The guy who shouted it could barely believe his own words, not even realizing he was basically calling Zane a human weapon. But he was dead right. More than that, Zane was like a magical weapon being forged in the heat of battle, tempered and strengthened by every blow.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER SHATTERING]
With one last lunge, Zane shattered the last of the golden light shimmering around Patrick. A pained groan escaped Patrick’s lips as he stumbled back a few steps, clearly injured internally.
But Patrick barely registered the pain. What really ate at him was losing to Zane in a straight-up fistfight. It was a humiliating failure.
And what really got under his skin was that stupid, happy grin still plastered on Zane's face, like he hadn’t been beaten enough.
At this point, Patrick started to entertain the insane thought that Zane was smiling just to get him to hit him more. It was a crazy idea, one he could never say out loud. Who in their right mind would ask for a beating? He had to believe Zane wasn’t some masochist who enjoyed pain.
Still, a small part of him couldn’t shake the crazy thought that it just might be true.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Meanwhile, Zane felt like he still hadn’t taken enough of a beating.
But now, he was in a whole new world of trouble.
[SFX: WHOOSHING MOVEMENT]
The shadow of The Immovable Guardian was terrifyingly fast. Its massive palms mimicked Patrick's every move in perfect, instantaneous sync.
What made it a nightmare was that Patrick’s hands were normal-sized, but the Guardian’s were seven or eight times bigger. When Patrick's hands became a blur of motion, so did two giant palms of death.
It was all happening too fast. Zane could barely dodge the attacks, let alone get anywhere near Patrick.
He knew if he couldn’t close the distance, this fight was over. Even with his legendary stamina, the drawn-out battle was starting to wear him down. Meanwhile, Patrick could just stand there and wave his hands, attacking from a distance. He could probably keep it up all day, waiting for Zane to collapse from exhaustion, and he’d do it without breaking a sweat.
As Zane scrambled for a solution, the huge palms swung at him again.
Patrick was relentless, not giving him a single second to breathe, let alone think.
[SFX: SWORD TOSSED IN AIR]
But this time, as he dodged, he did something completely unexpected.
Zane tossed his sword, Streamer, straight up into the air.
[SFX: SPINNING SWORD]
Streamer spun end over end before gravity took hold, and it started to fall. The move left the entire crowd baffled.
[SFX: MAGICAL ERUPTION]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]
A blood-red shadow erupted from Zane’s body, materializing into the elegant form of Lily, his sword spirit. Her blood-red outline shimmered with deadly grace as she took shape, her eyes flashing with determination and battle-hunger.
[SFX: WHOOSHING MOVEMENT]
With supernatural speed, Lily shot upward toward the falling Streamer, her movements fluid as water yet precise as a striking viper. She captured the blade mid-fall, her fingers wrapping around the hilt.
Without hesitation, she surged forward, the weapon gleaming as she directed its point straight at Patrick.
Far from just an extension of Zane’s will, Lily was a sword master in her own right, more than capable of wielding the blade. The true challenge, however, lay not in her capability but in Zane’s divided attention.
[SFX: BATTLE SOUNDS]
His consciousness strained as he simultaneously evaded the Guardian’s massive, crushing palms—each near-miss sending gusts of wind that tore at his clothes—while mentally orchestrating Lily’s relentless assault against Patrick.
Sweat beaded on his forehead as the mental strain tested the limits of his concentration, his breath coming in controlled gasps as he balanced on the knife-edge between offense and survival.
