Ep 517 - Worth the Trouble

[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT ARENA]
[MUSIC: GRAND ORCHESTRAL]
In the end, Maddox had withdrawn, giving up his claim on the Dragon Soar Arena. The decision surprised no one, not after witnessing the devastating power Zane had unleashed against both his disciples.
Still, warriors from other sects crowded the perimeter, their eyes gleaming with ambition. Independent fighters, too, pressed forward through the throng, determined not to let this rare opportunity slip through their fingers. For many, this tournament represented their only chance to prove themselves worthy of recognition in the Heartlands.
Kieran surveyed the eager faces before allowing himself a satisfied smile. "Both of Maddox's disciples are out," he announced, his voice carrying across the arena. "The remaining matches will proceed as planned, but as you can see, the arena's a wreck." He gestured toward the shattered stone and scorched earth where Zane's techniques had torn through the platform's defenses.
As he spoke, Kieran gave Hobbes a curt nod—barely perceptible, yet unmistakable in its authority.
Hobbes immediately departed from the tournament grounds, his massive frame disappearing into the crowd without a word.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
A moment later, the crowd heard the heavy thump of footsteps drawing closer.
Everyone turned toward the sound. A few onlookers gasped, their eyes widening as Hobbes approached, hauling the massive combat platform on his bulky shoulders.
This battle stage was nearly two hundred feet wide, constructed with an intricate design to withstand the power of Inner-Core Realm masters. Some of its parts were heavier than gold and steel, making the platform's total weight staggering. Despite all that, Hobbes had just casually hoisted it onto his shoulders, carrying it with nothing but raw strength.
[SFX: EARTH-SHATTERING THUMP]
With a single, earth-shattering thump, Hobbes set the platform down.
The various sect leaders on the high platform didn't so much as blink. For most masters of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, a feat like that was nothing special.
[AMBIENT: CROWD GASPS AND SHOUTS]
But for every warrior in the stands, it was a stunning display, and the crowd erupted in shouts and gasps.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
The sect leaders returned to their seats, each one lost in thought.
At that moment, Tia honestly didn't care if protecting Zane was worth all the trouble it was bringing down on Cloud Hall.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]
Her expression darkened, a wave of guilt washing over her. "Forgive me, Master," she said. "Maybe I'm just not worthy of leading Cloud Hall. Maybe I'm not cut out for this."
Seeing the guilt on Tia's face, Elder Imogen, the leader of Cloud Hall, sighed heavily. "Ah, I know you can be… impulsive, but your talent is undeniable. Once you master the Goddess Path, you'll have the power of a Life-and-Death Realm warrior even while you're still in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. And after you break through? I believe you can lead Cloud Hall to become a fifth-grade sect. Maybe even guide it to becoming a sacred place."
A Sixth-Grade sect that didn’t seek political power could eventually become a sacred place. If it chose to compete for authority, it would build its own kingdom of warriors—a divine kingdom. Cloud Hall had no desire for dominance; its ultimate goal was to become a sacred place, the highest honor a sect could achieve.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]
Just then, Zane cut in, his tone serious. "Elder Imogen, I think I've found a way to deal with Maddox."
[SFX: SHARP BREATH]
Elder Imogen sneered, giving him a look of utter contempt. "Do you really think you're untouchable after what you did? You killed Marilyn right in front of him. If it weren't for the World Merchants' Guild, you'd be a pile of ashes right now! A warrior who relies on luck instead of their own strength is a fool, and I promise you, that luck will run out."
"I consider luck one of my strengths," Zane replied politely. Please, may I finish?"
[AMBIENT: TENSE HALL ATMOSPHERE]
"Fine, continue," she responded coolly. It was clear she didn't care for Zane. His sister had been taken, and yet he'd had the nerve to lay the blame on Tia. He was just a young warrior in the Soul Realm, yet he dared to blame her prized apprentice. How was she supposed to stomach that?
Besides, the Elder Imogen could see as clear as day that Tia had feelings for Zane. She was always partial to him, fond of his every move. Because of that, the leader felt she had to tread lightly around him. On top of that, everyone suspected Zane was blessed with good fortune. As someone who had straddled the line between life and death and understood its very nature, how could she fail to see Zane's immense Destiny?
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Among warriors, luck was elusive. Those blessed with great fortune could absorb the luck of others, emerging unscathed while companions perished.
[SFX: SLOW, MEASURED BREATH]
Elder Imogen feared Zane's luck might devour Cloud Hall's fortune, leaving the sect exposed to ruin.
Unaware of her thoughts, Zane said, "I'd like to withdraw from the tournament.
Elder Imogen fixed him with a piercing stare."Why?"
"I want to build Cloud Hall's Sect Protecting Array," Zane said quietly.
The Dragon Soar Arena mattered to Zane—but he alone knew the truth. Anyone entering that arena faced imprisonment for a thousand years. For most warriors, that equaled death.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Elder Imogen's expression shifted. "Are you certain you can build an array strong enough to hold against Maddox?"
Zane gave a confident nod. “Eighty percent certain.”
In truth, he was completely certain. After consuming the Owl Beast's evil spirit, his Soul Power had finally been restored. If Zane ran into any trouble, he could still call on the Cobalt Dragon for help.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TENSION]
The Cobalt Dragon was capable of creating a Grand Marvellous Array—a formation powerful enough to destroy Heaven and Earth. Setting up an array for a fourth-grade sect, one capable of protecting it from masters of the Life-and-Death Realm, should be child's play for it.
Elder Imogen was vastly experienced. It didn't take a genius for her to see the value in Zane's proposal.
[SFX: DECISIVE GESTURE]
She nodded slightly. "If what you say is true, then there's no reason to hesitate. Once you've built this sect-protecting magic array, Cloud Hall will cover all material costs and fees! You have my word!"
Hearing her words, Zane thought to himself, 'What a weird thing to say. Of course, you're going to pay for the materials. I'm building YOUR magic array.' He mentally rolled his eyes. 'Who else did she think was going to foot the bill? Weird old lady.'
Of course, he'd never dare say any of that out loud.
[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]
Still, as the weight of the agreement settled over them, Zane's thoughts drifted back to Maddox—and the quiet, dangerous certainty that the man was already making plans of his own.
