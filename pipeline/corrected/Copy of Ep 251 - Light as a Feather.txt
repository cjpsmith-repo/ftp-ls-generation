Ep 251 - Light as a Feather
Word count: 1129
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL SHOWDOWN]
[SFX: HEAVY WINDS]
Zane stood solid in the middle of the swirling chaos. Cecil’s massive wind blade descended upon him, but instead of dodging, he faced it directly. The crowd braced themselves, certain they were about to witness a gruesome defeat. The Battle Referee lurched forward, nearly intervening before catching himself—this was, after all, a sanctioned combat.
Zane smiled, showing no sense of the danger that everyone else in the room felt. He knew he had something special up his sleeve.
The Phoenix Crystal embedded in Zane’s right arm was no ordinary gem. Legend claimed it was once a feather, dropped by a phoenix as it soared across an ancient sky.
As the feather drifted down to earth, it landed in the sticky resin of a pine tree. Over thousands of years, the essence hidden within it slowly seeped into the resin, eventually hardening into the crystal we see today.
Or at least, that's how the story went. No one really knew how the Phoenix Crystal was formed.
What they did know was that a weaponsmith from the Eastern Reaches had uncovered its unique properties. He tried embedding it into a weapon and discovered it made the weapon impossibly light.
Take a thousand-pound ghostly machete, for example. Once a Phoenix Crystal was set into it, its weight dropped to less than two hundred pounds.
While the crystal was rare, plenty of weapons had been fitted with one. But no one had ever been crazy enough to embed it into human flesh.
A human body, in its natural state, hardly qualifies as a weapon. Zane, however, had transcended this limitation through his strange Weaponsmithing technique, essentially turning his own body into a lethal instrument. It was the only way he could have merged the Phoenix Crystal with his arm.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
As Cecil’s Twins Chaos Cyclone bore down upon him, Zane activated the crystal with a mere thought. A slight, knowing smile touched his lips while the ancient power surged through his veins, illuminating the runes etched across his skin with a subtle golden glow.
As the crystal’s energy flooded through every fiber of his being, Zane felt himself become incredibly light, his body now responsive to the slightest impulse. The battlefield’s constraints seemed to fall away, and for a breathtaking moment, he understood what it might feel like to be truly unbound.
[SFX: WILD WIND]
[MUSIC: MAGICAL DISCOVERY]
A wild wind ripped through the arena, and Zane began to move.
He was surprised he didn’t even need his own strength; he felt as light as a feather. 
The key was speed. Moving with the wind meant he and the wind blades were travelling at the same velocity — and a blade that can't overtake you can't cut you.
Usually, disciples at the peak of the Adept Realm didn’t go all out. Even in the fight between Finn and Nicholas, they had pulled their punches at the last second. That battle had been incredible, but it was nothing compared to this.
[SFX: VIOLENT WIND CYCLONE]
Watching Cecil unleash such terrifying power, several disciples couldn’t help but wonder what they'd do if they were caught in that cyclone. "I'd be torn to shreds in a second," one thought.
Even Cecil looked tense, frozen in the center of the arena.
He didn’t have total control over the cyclone once it was summoned—that’s where the "Chaos" in its name came from. The power of the Twins Chaos Cyclone was so immense that anything within a hundred-foot radius would be ripped apart by its wind blades.
In the eye of the storm, however, the wind wasn’t nearly as destructive.
[SFX: TWIN CYCLONES COLLISION]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ESCALATION]
But that all changed the moment the two cyclones collided. As the twin vortexes—equal in power but spinning in opposite directions—crashed into each other, the airflow in the arena became dangerously unstable. Even the eye of the storm was no longer safe.
It was a deliberate choice. Cecil had fanned in the opposite direction — summoning a second cyclone to spin against the first. He'd never tried it before and couldn't predict the result, but Zane riding the wind had left him no other option.
Cecil could feel the shift in the air currents. He knew he had to stay within the eye to survive. One wrong step, and he'd be shredded by his own attack.
[SFX: WIND RUSHING]
Light as a bird, Zane was lifted into the sky. He let the turbulent winds carry him, drifting effortlessly through the chaos.
And while Cecil's wind blades were sharp, they were nothing compared to James's sword energy.
James had focused a terrifying amount of deadly sword intent into his attacks. If Zane had been hit by that, he'd have been cut in two, no question. The only way to survive an attack like that would be to upgrade his body to the level of a Mythic Weapon.
Besides, Cecil’s cultivation method wasn’t really designed for one-on-one duels. On a battlefield, his Twins Chaos Cyclone would be devastating, wiping out entire armies caught in its massive range.
Truthfully, no one from House Sterling was known for being a great duelist. The cultivation methods passed down through their family focused on military strategy and large-scale battle formations. It’s why they so often served as advisors in the Imperial Army. They were born leaders, not lone fighters.
So, even when the wind blades sliced through Zane’s clothes, they couldn’t pierce his flesh.
A blade hit him square on — and he felt nothing. No pain. Just a warm current spreading through his body where the impact landed, the force dissolving harmlessly into his skin. It didn't even leave a mark.
He'd been curling up to minimize his profile. Now he spread his arms wide.
No more dodging.
[SFX: BODY IMPACT — THUD]
[SFX: VIOLENT WIND GUSTS]
Violent gusts tossed him through the air. He was a butterfly in a hurricane, fluttering between wind and rain.
"An impact like that has to hurt! The blade probably tore right through him!" someone yelled.
"With the power of that cyclone, he’s lucky he hasn’t been ripped to pieces already. That was just the first hit. A few more stray shots like that, and he’s done for."
"Why hasn’t he surrendered? If I were him, I’d have given up ages ago. So what if he loses this year? He can try again in three years. I don’t care how strong this guy is—he’s a lunatic!"
[SFX: POWERFUL WIND WHOOSH]
As the disciples muttered among themselves, a massive wind blade suddenly shot straight for Zane.
[SFX: FEMALE SCREAMS]
Imagining the gruesome damage it would do, many of the female disciples screamed.
The Battle Referee's face went pale. According to the rules, he could only step in to save Zane if Zane himself gave up. With the blade about to hit, he waited anxiously for Zane to yell the words, "I give up!"
But to the Referee's astonishment, instead of surrendering, Zane met the wind blade head-on.
