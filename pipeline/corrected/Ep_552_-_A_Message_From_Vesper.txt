Ep 552 - A Message From Vesper
Word count: 1002
[AMBIENT: MYSTERIOUS ANCIENT CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL ORCHESTRAL]
The first thing Zane noticed when he stepped into The Tomb was that everything was impossibly huge.
Are all immortals giants? he thought to himself.
It just didn't add up.
Humans were everywhere. Once they got powerful enough, they could cross the void into the upper world. But their bodies weren't supposed to change that much, even after becoming immortal. It was a puzzle Zane was still trying to piece together.
[AMBIENT: COLD WIND]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
A cold draft cut through the darkness as Zane cautiously approached the enormous coffin. Its surface was covered in intricate, complex carvings.
Zane tried to lift the lid, but no matter how hard he pushed, it wouldn't budge.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[SFX: POWERFUL BLAST]
He drew on the power of the Dragon Scales, channeling the raw energy that surged within him. Still, the lid didn't move an inch.
"This thing is built like a tank," Zane breathed in amazement.
A blast like that could have leveled a mountain, but it hadn't even scratched the coffin.
Frustrated, Zane gave up and started looking for another way in.
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TOMB CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
He found a giant astrological chart etched into the ground, its surface covered in palm-sized golden symbols. Zane’s eyes widened as he scanned the engravings.
"Six White, Seven Red, One White… this is…" Just as recognition dawned, a faint voice echoed through the chamber.
[SFX: ETHEREAL WHISPER ECHO]
"Starlight Astrology. It reveals a person's fortune. Young man, since you've made it this far, go and stand on it."
The voice made Zane freeze, every muscle tensing. "Who's there?"
"Me? I'm the owner of this tomb," the voice replied.
[SFX: STONE GRINDING]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRONE]
A scraping sound, like stone grinding against stone, drew Zane's gaze upward. A triangular stone panel in the ceiling was slowly sliding away, revealing a transparent crystal ball that rolled out and floated in mid-air.
[SFX: MYSTICAL SWIRL]
Inside the ball, a black shadow swirled into a human-like figure. It had no eyes, just two empty, black sockets that stared right at him.
A knot of dread tightened in Zane's stomach. He gave a slight bow, his voice cautious. "Are you Vesper?"
"No need to be so tense. A dead man can't really be called an immortal. To be precise, I'm more of a final message from Vesper." The crystal ball hovered silently for a moment. "It took an incredible amount of time and a host of powerful techniques just to preserve this sliver of my soul. If I'd been forced to wait another thousand years, even the time sand would have run out."
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
The voice grew quieter, more distant. "In order to wait for the right person, I mixed what was left of my soul with the time sand — that was how I held onto thousands of years of memories after death. But splitting my soul came at a price." A long pause. "I gave up my place in the cycle of reincarnation."
Zane went still. He understood what that meant. Every living thing — human, beast, immortal — eventually passed through the six paths and was reborn. To surrender that was to erase yourself. Not just death, but the end of everything.
"Why?" Zane asked.
"I was waiting for someone," said Vesper quietly.
"Waiting for who?"
"You."
Zane wasn't entirely surprised. The Cobalt Dragon had already explained that the Wheel of Fortune wasn't just a game — it was a mechanism for finding the warrior with the greatest karmic luck. Zane had been the first to pass through it. That was the whole point.
[MUSIC: TENSE UNDERSCORE]
"Though I should tell you," Vesper added, with what almost sounded like mild disappointment, "your karmic luck is still somewhat… insufficient."
Zane blinked. "Insufficient? I just beat a one-in-a-million wheel."
"Closer to one in seventeen billion," said Vesper matter-of-factly. "You used six throws to pass through the wheel. The odds of doing that are roughly one in sixteen point nine billion."
Zane opened his mouth. Closed it again.
Vesper continued. "My original design was for the chosen one to pass the wheel in a single attempt. One throw. Six steps. Done."
"...One throw?" Zane repeated slowly.
"The odds of that would be approximately one in fifty-six trillion."
[AMBIENT: DEAD SILENCE]
Zane stared at the crystal ball for a long moment. Fifty-six trillion. The entire population of the Central Domain — every city, every clan, every warrior — wouldn't come close to that number even if you counted every soul ten times over.
The shadow inside the ball seemed to shrug. "Perhaps my requirements were a little high."
"Just a little," Zane agreed.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
"Even so," said Vesper, "you should still stand on the chart. Let me calculate your karmic luck."
Zane's eyes narrowed with curiosity. He turned the question inward. Does the human race have a method for calculating karmic luck?
The Cobalt Dragon's voice drifted back, cool and unhurried. "Of course. I'd suggest you let him try."
What Zane didn't know was that the Cobalt Dragon had his own reasons for encouraging this. He and his seventh brother had long harbored a suspicion that Zane carried the Fate of the War of the Worlds. But it had always been just that: a suspicion. Neither of them possessed the ability to calculate fate and karmic luck. Vesper, for all his limitations, apparently did.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY HUMMING]
Zane's attention snapped back to the chart as the palm-sized symbols on the ground began to glow, circulating a golden light that twisted into thin, slithering lines.
A few rays of light touched Zane's body before bouncing off toward a large symbol on the ground.
Standing in the middle of it all, Zane didn't feel a thing. The crystal ball floated before the center of the astrological chart, the darkness inside it swirling furiously, as if it were calculating his fate.
[SFX: CRYSTAL CRACKING]
After a long moment, the crystal ball suddenly trembled and fell, hitting the stone floor with a heavy thud. A crack shot across its surface.
Zane was shocked. He stared at the fractured sphere. "Vesper, are you okay?"
The black smoke inside the crystal ball churned, finally coalescing into the faint shape of a head.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
A weak but utterly astonished voice cried out, "You… you have a token, too!"
