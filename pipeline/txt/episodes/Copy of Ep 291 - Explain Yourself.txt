Ep 291 - Explain Yourself
Word Count: 1137
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
It felt like the whole world was holding its breath, every soul present desperate to find out what would happen. No one was more on edge than Master Warren, for whom decades of hope and disappointment compressed into this singular moment.
Throughout the arena, aside from the handful of disciples who had passed out, all eyes were transfixed on the battlefield below.
[AMBIENT: FADING MAGICAL ENERGY]
As the swirling tendrils of Corrupt Vitality dissolved into nothingness, they revealed a solitary figure standing in the clearing mist. The young man was slender in frame, but he emanated an unmistakable aura of raw, primal power that radiated off him in waves. Despite his modest height, he commanded the space with such presence that he seemed to loom over everyone present.
"Zane!" Master Warren cried out, his normally composed voice cracking with a joy so intense it bordered on pain. The elderly instructor’s eyes glistened with tears that threatened to spill over.
[AMBIENT: CROWD COMMOTION]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT REVELATION]
The disciples exploded into a frantic buzz.
"No way! That’s impossible!" one of them yelled, his voice laced with disbelief. "Someone tell me what the hell just happened! Why is Zane standing there, and Jacob is on the ground?"
"Don’t ask me! I couldn’t follow any of it!" another shouted back.
"It was obvious Jacob was on the attack! I mean, his spear sounded like a damn dragon!" a third exclaimed, completely floored. "So why is Zane on his feet and Jacob looks like he got hit by a truck? It makes no sense!"
The outcome was the last thing anyone expected. No one could wrap their head around what they had just seen.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Zane shook his head, the Streamer trembling in his hand as he took a deep, ragged breath. His eyes seemed to burn brighter than before, a telltale sign his soul had been tempered and strengthened once again.
When Jacob unleashed his Drake Spear Volley, Zane had managed to parry it with his Streamer. But he'd barely been able to withstand the raw power of the Drake channeled into the attack. Even giving it his all, Zane wasn't sure he could win—the Drake’s power was just too immense.
As Zane countered with his Streamer, the Cobalt Dragon coiled within his consciousness woke up from its slumber. The ancient entity unfurled its wings and unleashed a primal roar that reverberated through Zane’s entire being.
This wasn’t merely a challenge to the Drake’s dominance—it was an absolute declaration of supremacy that extinguished the Drake completely.
The sound, though inaudible to the arena’s spectators, resonated on a spiritual plane that Jacob couldn’t ignore. The confident combatant who had entered the arena with swagger now stood paralyzed, his face drained of color, his limbs suddenly leaden with dread at the presence of something far beyond his comprehension.
[SFX: MAGICAL FIRE WHOOSH]
That single moment of hesitation was all Zane needed. He summoned the black fire from deep within his body, and in the blink of an eye, it swarmed over and devoured Jacob's Drake Bone Spear.
But Zane was stunned by what happened next. Normally, the black fire would just melt a weapon down to its Celestial Essence. This time, as the Drake Bone Spear dissolved, a few drops of blood were pulled from the weapon. This wasn’t human blood, though. Normal blood would have splattered on the ground, a liquid mess. These drops bounced off the stone floor like tiny, crimson rubber balls.
The black fire then carried the strange droplets into Zane's mind, presenting them to the Cobalt Dragon. Naturally, the dragon swallowed them in a single gulp.
[SFX: BEAMS OF LIGHT]
At the same instant, two beams of light shot out from The Soulforge. One plunged into Zane’s head, the other into Jacob’s.
This light held a strange power, one that could rapidly mend an injured soul. Even if a soul was on the verge of shattering, the light could restore it to perfect health. Zane had experienced it himself the first time he'd used The Soulforge. The refinement process had repeatedly fractured his soul, but this same light had healed it, every single time.
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL RESOLUTION]
Nearby, the referee stirred. He fumbled for a soul-nourishing pill, got it down, and hauled himself upright. His voice came out thin and unsteady, but he got the words out. "Misty Peak disciple… Zane. Wins."
The elders rushed to the battlefield to question Zane, eager to get to the bottom of this highly unusual situation.
Zane wasn’t ready to let them in on his discovery, so he quickly made up a story that involved seeing a seven-colored light.
"Seven-colored light?" Master Jasper asked, his brow furrowed in thought. He racked his brain but came up empty. What was even more baffling was its ability to instantly heal a grievously wounded soul. He knew of pills that could aid in a soul’s recovery, but nothing worked that fast. And if the damage was too severe, the pills were useless. A person's soul was impossibly fragile, and just as difficult to mend.
"Seven-colored light?" Sir Kenneth repeated, his mind racing. ’Could it be the Seven-Star Herb? No, that's impossible. There shouldn’t be any in the Eastern Territory,’ Kenneth thought. ’The Seven-Star Herb is just a myth. They say if you get close to one, it emits a seven-colored light that nourishes the soul. But if a Seven-Star Herb really existed, it would change the color of the sky and the land for miles. Everyone would see it. You couldn’t hide it. How could something like that just happen to pop up for Zane and Jacob today?’
Zane felt a wave of relief, seeing he’d managed to throw Kenneth off the scent. He kept his face a mask of ignorance. "I really don’t know what happened. How is Jacob?"
Just then, an attendant hurried over to Jacob’s side and checked him over. "Sirs, he seems to be alright. He’s just unconscious. He should be waking up soon," the attendant reported.
Kenneth nodded but said nothing, his eyes narrowed as he weighed his options. He frowned, glancing back at Zane, still deeply suspicious. Zane's story didn’t really explain anything.
"Let me wake him. We’ll just ask him what happened," Master Jasper said. As he spoke, his hand began to glow with a soft, green Vitality. He walked over to Jacob and channeled the light into his body in a steady stream.
A moment later, Jacob's eyelids fluttered. "Where… where am I?" he mumbled.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Ice shot through Zane’s veins. After all, Jacob had been right there. He'd had a front-row seat when the black fire consumed his Drake Bone Spear.
If Jacob saw what really happened and told them, Zane was completely screwed.
