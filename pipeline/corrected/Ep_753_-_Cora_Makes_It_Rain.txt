Ep 753 - Cora Makes It Rain

Word count: 1029

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

 Maddox didn’t fully understand how the Life and Death Wind worked, but he sure as hell knew how to use it to kill people. 

Tapping into that power came with permanent, nasty side effects, putting him at huge risk every single time. It was a last resort, but since he had to win this fight, he had no choice but to go all in.

The fragment shimmering in front of him flashed with countless colors, and one look was enough to know it was no ordinary object. Maddox wondered if this was the “big secret” Lord Wallace had mentioned. As far as he was concerned, Zane was just a kid with way too many secrets, and getting his hands on them would be a massive score. 

That meant Zane had to die, and soon.

[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]

 He took a step toward the young man, ready to claim the fragment, but a blindingly sharp streak of sword light shot toward him from outside the city wall.

The blade screamed through the air, humming with a terrifying aura.

There was no time to react. Even Maddox couldn't dodge it. The strike was too swift, too certain, like a judgment delivered from the heavens to punish him for his sins.

[SFX: SLICE]

The blade sliced clean across his wrist, severing one of his hands.

[AMBIENT: CITY WALL]

[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]

 Meanwhile, high on the city walls, a young man collapsed onto the green bricks. Two thick streams of pitch-black blood pooled beside him, the price of the immense power he’d just unleashed. 

Finn lay there on the bricks, clutching his sword. That single strike had taken everything he had, nearly costing him his life.

A damn shame, he thought with a flicker of regret, just before he blacked out completely. It missed his vitals.

Still, just cutting off Maddox’s hand was a staggering accomplishment. After all, Finn was only an Inner-Core Realm warrior, but the power behind his Celestial Blade was already on par with the Transcendent Divine Might. His strike had channeled the very laws of the heavens, gathering the universe's aura and moving with its will. If he kept on this path, Finn’s future accomplishments could actually rival Zane’s.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]

 A murmur went through the onlookers. "That attack… it came from an Inner-Core Realm warrior."

"Yeah! That was Finn from Cloud Hall. I heard the Celestial Blade he mastered has enough power to kill a Spirit-Tribulation Realm master. Looks like the rumors were true!"

[MUSIC: TENSE QUIET]

Maddox looked down at the stump where his hand had been, then up at the wall where Finn had collapsed. Killing intent crossed his face — and then, deliberately, he let it go. At his level, a severed limb could be regrown. But the regenerated hand would need to be recultivated from scratch, or it would become a permanent flaw. He didn't have time for that now.

What mattered was the fragment.

He raised his remaining hand and pressed it against the wound, sealing the blood. Then he reached toward Zane.

[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: URGENT STRINGS]

A figure in white came flying in from the edge of the battlefield — moving like a leopard, fast and low. Cora reached Zane before Maddox's hand got there. She dropped to her knees beside him and pressed her palm to his chest.

Cold. His skin was cold. She searched for his life force and found nothing.

She didn't know what the fragment on his chest was for. She didn't know whether he was alive or dead. She only knew that the man in front of her had done this to him.

Cora slowly raised her head and looked at Maddox.

"There are things between us that need to be settled," she said. Her voice was quiet. "My family. My great-grandfather. The millions who died when the Ethereal Spirit Sect fell."

Maddox glanced at her with mild curiosity. "And what exactly are you planning to do about it?"

"Kill you."

Maddox almost smiled. He looked her over once — her cultivation, her bearing — and dismissed her entirely. Her Pristine Essence was gone, which made her worthless to him for any ritual purpose. And her cultivation was Inner-Core at best. Whatever she'd done to awaken her Violet-Core Physique, she was still just a girl.

He wasn't afraid of her.

[SFX: LOW METALLIC HUM — BUILDING]

Then the sky began to fill.

One sword. Ten. A hundred. A thousand. Ten thousand. A million.

[SFX: METALLIC RINGING]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

 Above Oracle City, the sky itself had turned into a field of gigantic swords. 

The outside of the city went dark under their shadow. Every warrior on the field looked up, and not one of them could speak.

A few dozen blades had been enough to stop Maddox in his tracks during Zane's earlier fight. This was something else entirely. The entire sky was blades.

How many disciples from the Corrupted Divine Kingdom could possibly survive once that storm fell?

Only then did the full scope of his mistake hit Maddox. "You!" he roared, pointing his remaining hand at Cora. "H-how did you do that?"

 Cora just sneered. 

[SFX: MASSIVE SWORD RAIN]

 With a flick of her finger, more swords rained down. 

She had the ultimate physique in the Violet Power Realm: a top-tier Violet-Core Physique. The number of swords she could summon was naturally far beyond what anyone with an ordinary physique could ever dream of. The several million blades she’d summoned weren’t even her limit. If she was willing to pay the price, Cora could summon all ninety-nine billion swords and wipe the entire Heartlands off the map.

But of course, that would be overkill.

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC BATTLEFIELD]

[SFX: PANICKED SCREAMS]

 "Retreat!"

"Quickly, run!"

"We can't escape! There are swords everywhere!"

[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACTS]

[SFX: EARTH SHATTERING]

 A deafening barrage of crashes echoed across the field. Massive swords plunged into the earth again and again, tearing through the warriors below and leaving nothing but a gory mess. In a matter of seconds, over a hundred thousand soldiers from the Corrupted Divine Kingdom were wiped out.

Only Simon and a handful of other Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors managed to survive the onslaught.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]

 The battlefield was a scene straight out of Hell. When the swords finally vanished, all that remained was a churned-up mire of blood, flesh, and mud. Maddox could only stare in disbelief at the carnage, the grim look on his face promising a storm was about to break.
