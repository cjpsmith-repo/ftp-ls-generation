Ep 163 - A New Kind of Foe
Word Count: 1120

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL BATTLEFIELD]
[SFX: GHOSTLY SHRIEK]
 Saul watched in silence as Zane wiped out the last of the evil ghosts. After a moment, he spoke, his voice slow and deliberate. "Interesting. It looks like you've got a dragon lying dormant inside you."
A dragon? Zane didn't bother denying it. The truth was, there wasn't just one dragon inside him—there were nine. 
So far, only the Cobalt Dragon had woken up, its power the very thing that had just destroyed the evil spirits. The only problem was, he had no idea when the other eight would awaken. He had a hunch the next one might not stir until every one of the Cobalt Dragon's scales was lit.
"Hah! Not bad at all!" Saul chuckled. "You've more than earned your shot at the fifth round. You've passed four tests, so I won't try to talk you out of this anymore. If you can pass this final test, you'll get the Tapestry of Sun, Moon, and Stars—the most powerful Contemplation Picture in all of The Elysian Keep. But your only shot at winning is to go all out. Those ghosts' attacks did a number on your soul, so I can help you recover."
As a creature of vast knowledge from his time in The Elysian Keep, Saul had seen it all. But he'd consistently underestimated Zane, who just kept overcoming every obstacle and passing the tests one by one. Saul finally realized that trying to intimidate the kid was pointless; he might as well just let him move on.
[SFX: ENERGY WAVE]
 A luminous halo descended from above the moment Saul fell silent, gently sinking into Zane's head with ethereal grace. 
An incredible wave of relief washed over Zane.   His tense muscles relaxed as the healing energy coursed through him, washing away the lingering pain from the ghost attacks. 
For a being who claimed to be merely a puppet, Saul had effortlessly demonstrated healing abilities that would make even accomplished Soul Menders jealous. Zane wondered what other powers this ancient guardian might possess, hidden beneath his unassuming appearance.
As the halo dissolved into nothingness, vitality flooded through Zane's veins, restoring his depleted strength.
[MUSIC: APPROACHING DANGER]
Across the chamber, a towering figure emerged from the shadows—an Armored Warrior stepped forward, its gleaming plates reflecting the  light. Zane immediately recognized the distinctive shimmer emanating from the imposing figure as Raw Vitality, though unlike any he had encountered before.
But this Raw Vitality was strange. It wasn't a single color, but a swirling mix of several, constantly shifting hues.
“What kind of training gives him Raw Vitality like that?” Zane wondered. *Whatever it is, I need to strike first and seize the advantage.*
[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
 Zane exploded forward, a blur of motion as he charged the Armored Warrior. In the same instant, he flung the broken throwing knife straight at his opponent's head, the blade letting out a sharp whir as it spun through the air.
[SFX: KNIFE WHIZZING THROUGH AIR]
He had a plan. He needed the Warrior to dodge, creating an opening for him to use the Psyche Spike. Then, he'd recall the knife for the finishing blow.
But the Armored Warrior foiled his plan with a single, simple move that was brutally effective.
He barely moved, just tilting his head to the side. The dodge was impossibly fast, clean, and precise. The knife zipped past his cheek, missing by less than half an inch.
“He's so confident. It's like he knew the knife's exact path!” Zane thought, his breath catching in his throat.
He flicked his thumb, recalling the knife along its original path. With a second flick, he sent it hurtling toward the back of the Warrior's head.
Just like before, his opponent tilted his head just enough. It was like he had eyes in the back of his head; he dodged the attack effortlessly.
"Uh…" Zane could only stare, dumbfounded.
Both attacks had failed. The Warrior had dodged them so easily it was shocking. Zane had used his best opening gambit and was already out of ideas.
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
 That's when the Armored Warrior charged, closing the distance with incredible speed.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Zane's eyes widened as the Warrior blurred toward him. "Is this truly the caliber of an opponent barely advanced into the Adept Realm?" he thought, his mind racing alongside his quickening pulse. The display of raw speed left him momentarily breathless, defying everything he'd previously understood about cultivation tiers.
Then recognition dawned  in his mind. He'd witnessed this phenomenon before—the startled expressions of Adepts and even Soul Realm masters when confronting him. The Armored Warrior transcended ordinary classification. He wasn't just a half-step into the Adept Realm; he was also fused with a soul remnant from The Immortal. Such a fusion elevated his capabilities beyond normal standards. 
[SFX: ELECTRICAL  CURRENTS]
As he rushed Zane, the Armored Warrior stretched out his long, wiry arms, shaping the Raw Vitality into two colorful arcs of light. The moment they formed, they shot forward with blinding speed.
The Warrior had already put him on the ground twice — a full-body slam, then two Vitality fist-shadows to the chest that sent him rolling.
Spitting out a mouthful of dirt, Zane glared at the Armored Warrior.
The Warrior was moving too fast. If he kept up that speed, Zane wouldn't even be able to track his movements, let alone fight back. It would be a guaranteed loss.
*I have to get in close, fight him hand-to-hand,* Zane decided.
His own physical strength was his biggest asset; he never shied away from a close-quarters brawl.
[MUSIC: CLOSE BRAWL]
Without hesitating, he hurled the broken knife again. This time, he wasn't aiming for the Warrior himself, but for the space beside him, trying to cut off his escape.
In the same motion, he lunged forward, closing the distance and reaching out to grab him.
But the moment Zane’s hand shot out, the Warrior simply deflected it, batting his arm aside.
"You think you can push me away? Not happening!" Zane roared.
When the Warrior deflected his left hand, Zane immediately countered with his right, his muscles tensing with determined precision. The movement flowed naturally from his training—a reflexive response honed through countless hours of practice.
[SFX: INHUMAN SPEED]
But nothing could have prepared him for the Warrior's supernatural dexterity. The armored figure's limbs shot through the air like Spirit Serpents, graceful and lethal. 
In a flash, one of those whip-like limbs coiled around Zane's right arm, locking it in a vice grip.
Panic flashed across Zane's face as he realized the terrible disadvantage of being caught in such a hold, his heartbeat quickening as he struggled against the immovable force.
