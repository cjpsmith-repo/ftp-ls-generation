Ep 128 - The Shadow Dragon
Word Count: 1164
[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA CROWD]
[SFX: DRAGON GROWLS]
The crowd held its breath, every eye fixed on the fight as the Apex Dragon slithered around Zane, its five massive claws scraping the ground like a colossal snake. Zane knew his Titan Strike was a flicker against the dragon’s inferno. The long battle had drained him, leaving him running on fumes.
After all, he was still just a High-level candidate, capable of summoning and wielding only Nether Essence. While powerful in its own right, Nether Essence was a shadow compared to the raw strength of Life Force. To make matters worse, the Apex Dragon was actively absorbing Life Force from the air, growing stronger with every passing second.
The fight was completely one-sided. A baby bird against a full-grown eagle. And Zane was the helpless chick.
[SFX: SHARP CRACK]
A sharp crack echoed through the arena as a shadowy projection of Zane’s fist slammed into the Apex Dragon. The shadow rippled and then sank into the dragon's body, vanishing from sight.
From his vantage point, the general of the imperial army smirked, then let out a derisive sneer. The kid was cocky, completely overestimating his own power. The general had always been proud of his Dragon Fist, and the thought of Zane’s pathetic attack doing any real damage was laughable. In a flash, he channeled his own Life Force, intending to neutralize the blow instantly.
The spectators, Dalton and Cliff among them, shook their heads in collective disappointment. This was exactly what they’d expected. No one here could stand up to a master who had reached the Soul Realm. Against that kind of power, you couldn’t even put up a fight.
In the crowd, Ingrid’s face was a mask of worry and frustration. She’d brought three Trinket Boxes on this trip, each loaded with a Concussive Blast, but she’d already fired her last one at the Apex Dragon when it first appeared. She was out. Still, even with her Concussive Blasts gone, she had an arsenal of other hidden weapons ready, just in case.
[SFX: DRAGON SHUDDER]
[MUSIC: DARK TRANSFORMATION]
Suddenly, the golden dragon shuddered. Its radiant scales began to darken, the gold bleeding into a deep, blackish-purple as Zane's Nether Essence corrupted it from within.
Zane’s head snapped up. “Okay, so it’s a Shadow Dragon now,” he thought, his mind racing. “But how do I control it?”
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
The answer hit him like a lightning strike. Without a second thought, he pushed his consciousness from his body, his very soul reaching out to command the corrupted beast.
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
[MUSIC: EPIC FANTASY THEME]
A deafening roar ripped through the air as if the dragon was waking from a thousand-year slumber. The monstrous creature began to rise, its shadowy body coiling protectively around Zane.
His eyes went wide. A thrill shot through him, pure, electric shock.
"No way," he breathed, a grin spreading across his face. "It actually worked! I can control it!"
The Shadow Dragon twisted through the air before lowering its massive head to its new master. Zane reached out and gave its forehead a gentle pat, then locked eyes with the general, staring smugly at the rage boiling in the Soul Realm master’s face.
But he could already feel it — his Vitality draining fast, the Shadow Dragon burning through his reserves at a rate he couldn't sustain. He couldn't keep it circling forever.
So he didn't.
He gave it a light push forward.
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR — CHARGING]
The Shadow Dragon raised its head, roared, and launched itself at the general. The general's eye twitched — there was something deeply unpleasant about having your own technique turned against you.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
The entire audience was stunned into silence. They couldn't wrap their heads around what they were seeing. How had a High-level candidate managed to steal the general's own dragon right out from under him?
“What just happened?” a thousand minds wondered at once. A moment ago, the golden dragon was about to tear Zane apart. Now, in the blink of an eye, it had turned blackish-purple and was acting like his loyal pet. It had completely stopped attacking him, awaiting his command.
Could things possibly get any weirder?
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]
Ingrid's eyes lit up with a spark of hope, the same question echoing in her thoughts. The hand she had wrapped tightly around her Thunderstone trembled, but a sense of relief washed over her. Maybe she'd been overthinking things. Zane was just a High-level candidate, sure, but he was clearly a once-in-a-generation talent. Of course, he'd find a way to take control of the dragon. Even against an opponent from the Soul Realm, he'd found a way to fight back. She was just glad he could hold his own.
Meanwhile, Dalton stood with a black sword in one hand and a white one in the other, frozen in disbelief. He was still debating whether he should even jump in to help.
Dalton was arrogant, no doubt about it. He might not be in the top ten on Eagle Peak, but his talent was undeniable, and he had climbed the ranks faster than almost anyone. Even if he wasn't there now, he knew he’d make the list someday. A man that proud hates owing anyone a favor. It just wasn't in his nature.
Zane had saved his life back in the sky forest, and that was the only reason he was even here, ready to step in. He just wanted to repay the debt.
Masters of the Soul Realm were powerful, yes. But Dalton was training a mystical skill of his own. Once he mastered it, his strength would skyrocket. He was confident he could cut the general’s power in half, but whether Zane could land the winning blow was entirely on him.
Like everyone else, Dalton had no idea what kind of bizarre trick Zane had just pulled. He was floored. The kid hadn't even flinched when facing a master of the Soul Realm, and now he’d somehow hijacked the man’s own attack and turned it into his personal weapon.
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Just then, the general’s *real* Apex Dragon materialized in a flash of golden light. Without hesitation, it lunged at the Shadow Dragon, and the two beasts became a whirlwind of claws and fury, their bodies twisting violently in the sky.
[SFX: VIOLENT CLASH]
The battle was over almost as soon as it began. The new golden dragon was infinitely more vicious. In a matter of seconds, it tore the Shadow Dragon to pieces, scattering its dark essence into nothing.
A smug smile returned to the general's face.
"That thing you controlled was just an illusion!" he gloated. "It crumbled the second it faced a real Apex Dragon!"
He spoke truth. The two dragons existed in entirely different realms of power. The Apex Dragon, crafted from pure Life Force, towered in might over the Shadow Dragon—a mere facsimile fashioned from Life Energy. This fundamental distinction rendered the golden beast a thousandfold more formidable than its dark counterpart.
The spectacle had whiplashed the onlookers through a tempest of emotions. First, the general commanded the battlefield, then Zane executed a breathtaking reversal, only for the general to reclaim his supremacy with devastating finality.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TURN]
Yet as the general basked in his triumph, his victorious expression suddenly froze, then crumbled away like sand in a rising wind.
