Ep 74 - No Easy Choices

[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
Randall was stewing. He knew Shane wasn't just gonna let this go. If Shane hadn't picked a fight in the first place, Randall never would've locked him up. He just never figured Shane had a guy like Leo from House Brandt in his corner.
Now, Randall was stuck between a rock and a hard place.
He'd pissed off a noble from House Brandt just to suck up to Felix of House Sterling... and for what? He got nothing out of it. And now it was way too late for regrets.
"Shane, let's just cool it for now. We can regroup and figure this out back at base," Zane said, giving him a quick wink. Now that Shane was out, they needed a real plan before their next move. Starting another fight with Randall right now would just land them in another tough spot.
As stubborn as he was, even Shane could see the writing on the wall. He shot Randall one last venomous glare but clamped his mouth shut.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS LEAVING]
Seeing that things had settled down, Jon led the group out of the yard. Randall stayed right where he was, fuming, and glanced at Liam Shaw, who had also hung back.
Randall rounded on him, his voice a low growl. "You told me Zane was a nobody. No connections. So why the hell is a guy from House Brandt sticking his neck out for him?"
If he'd known this was coming, Randall would have stayed far away from the whole mess.
His fuse was short, and every part of him just wanted to beat Liam Shaw to a pulp. But Liam was here on behalf of Felix Sterling, so Randall couldn't afford to push it. He'd already angered Leo from House Brandt; if he couldn't lock down an alliance with House Sterling, he'd be in an even deeper hole.
"It's, uh, it's because..." Liam stammered, at a complete loss for words.
Graves leaned in. "It doesn't matter how. What matters is — Zane walks out of Dragon's Keep, we finish it. No more waiting around."
Randall's expression hardened. He'd already made an enemy of House Brandt. If he didn't deliver Zane's head to Felix Sterling, he'd have nothing to show for any of it. "When he leaves the Keep," he said quietly, "we end it."
[AMBIENT: MAIN CAMP EXTERIOR]
Outside the main camp, Jon pulled Zane aside before they parted ways. His voice was low. "You've made a real enemy of Randall. He's not the type to forget. My advice — collect your points and get back to the Skyreach Sect as fast as you can. Don't give him an opening."
Zane was a hell of a fighter, but even he wasn't strong enough to take on a Razorwing Queen. Hearing Jon's amazed praise about the kill, Zane just offered a tight smile, deciding it was best to keep his mouth shut.
There was no way he could tell them the truth. He couldn't just say, "I didn't kill the Queen, the Scorpiomane did." The Queen's Core was one thing, but the Scorpiomane's Inferno Core was on another level entirely. It was prime material for alchemy and weapon forging. The seven Great Houses would kill to get their hands on a Scorpiomane's core.
If word got out about a Scorpiomane, every House would send hunters swarming the area. They'd find both bodies, put two and two together, and realize Zane had both cores. It would be a fatal mistake, and Zane wasn't dumb enough to make it.
So, Zane changed the subject. "Leo, how many Points can I get for the Queen's Core?"
[SFX: LOUD COUGHING]
At such a basic question, Max, who'd been quiet until now, broke into a loud coughing fit. He had to pound his own chest to get it under control.
He turned to Zane, his voice full of disbelief. "Whoa, hold on, Zane. You're telling me you seriously don't know?"
Zane just shrugged. He really had no clue. He'd never imagined he'd actually get his hands on a Queen's core, so he'd never bothered to look up the payout.
"A Razorwing Queen's crystal core will get you a thousand Points," Leo said with a broad smile. "So, you're about to come into a small fortune."
[SFX: GASPS OF SURPRISE]
"A small fortune?" Leo's casual words left Zane and Max reeling. A thousand Points was worth two thousand Crystal Cubes.
Across the entire Empire, maybe fifty families—mostly from the Great Houses—had that kind of wealth. And to Leo, it was just pocket change. Zane and Max were floored. They figured all the kids from the Great Houses must be loaded, but this was something else entirely.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
"A thousand points is a hell of a start, but it's not enough. I'm staying here to earn more," Zane said, looking straight at Leo.
Honestly, Zane had only been aiming for a hundred points—enough for a pass to visit his sister at Infernal Peak. But now... now he had a new plan. He was going to rack up every point he could, killing as many Razorwings as possible.
Saving Elara Lowell from Infernal Peak would cost a million points. So far, he had just a fraction of what he needed.
Hunting the Razorwing swarms, especially the Alphas, was the perfect opportunity. He figured if he could just get that million points by the time the swarms died down, he could finally rescue Elara. It was a naive thought. Nothing was ever that easy.
Still, if Zane could actually pull together a million points and had the strength to back it up, House Brandt might just be willing to throw their weight behind him. But it all came down to Zane himself. House Brandt would only get involved if he proved he was a winning bet. That was Leo's assessment, anyway. He didn't even notice how invested he'd become in Zane's success.
"If you're dead set on staying here to hunt the swarms," Leo said, "you need to watch your back. Especially around the others."
Zane nodded. "I will. And Leo... thanks for the assist back there."
Leo patted Zane on the shoulder, a faint smile playing on his lips. "Don't mention it," he said. "I hope you collect those million points swiftly and liberate her from Infernal Peak."
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL REVELATION]
Zane's blood turned to ice. He'd referenced the million points during their conversation, certainly, but never once had he uttered a word about Elara Lowell or her imprisonment. With nothing more than that single financial detail, Leo had unraveled his entire scheme.
As Leo turned to leave, the realization struck Zane like a physical blow—if this brilliant strategist of House Brandt could deduce so much from so little, who else might already know his plans?
