Karmafire earned its name from the ancient belief that evil karma could scorch one from the inside out—a flame of pure malevolence that mirrored the infernal fires said to rage in hell, eternally punishing the wicked. Cultivators whispered of its vengeful nature, how it sought out the darkness in men’s hearts and fed upon their transgressions, leaving nothing but ash and regret.
The ancient scrolls taught that two flames stood supreme in the cosmos, matched only in their devastating power yet opposite in nature.
Voidfire, wielded by the Ascended, represented divine judgment, while Karmafire embodied retribution for mortal sins. Both could reduce mountains to dust and oceans to vapor with equal efficiency.
Though countless tales celebrated the Ascended’s miraculous interventions and righteous deeds throughout the ages, the workings of karma remained shrouded in mystery. Scholars and sages alike had spent lifetimes attempting to decipher its patterns, only to die with more questions than answers. Some claimed karma was sentient, a cosmic force that remembered every slight and virtue across generations, patiently waiting to balance the scales when least expected.
Patrick smiled at the Black Lotus Karmafire in his hand, while Zane stood, awestruck by what he saw.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
"The black lotus blooms in a world of corruption," Patrick said, his voice low, "but it remains pure. Its only desire is to burn it all down, to turn this world into a living hell."
[SFX: MAGICAL FIRE IGNITION]
[SFX: FIREBALL SLAMMING TO GROUND]
The Black Lotus Karmafire in Patrick’s hand flared brilliantly, then split in two, one large, one small. The smaller flame, containing hundreds of delicate lotus blossoms within its malevolent glow, darted toward Lily with predatory precision, while the large one crashed down upon the arena with devastating force.
The power of the Black Lotus Karmafire was beyond comprehension.
[SFX: WHOOSHING FLAME]
As the smaller flame lunged at her, Lily—usually fierce and aggressive in battle—suddenly froze, the fighting spirit quickly draining out of her.
Despite being surrounded by her own Malice, she recognized the futility of resistance against such ancient, corrupting energy. The flame seemed to whisper dark promises directly to her essence. Noticing her momentary vulnerability, Zane swiftly signaled retreat with a fluid motion of his Streamer, his heart clenching at seeing his companion so uncharacteristically shaken.
[SFX: MAGICAL TELEPORTATION]
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
Lily dissolved into brilliant flashes of light, retreating to the sanctuary of Zane’s body. He returned the Streamer to his Storage Ring, his fingers trembling slightly from the residual energy in the air.
Through calculated advances, Zane had methodically pushed Patrick beyond his limits, forcing him to unleash the full devastation of his legendary flames. A tactical victory, though at tremendous risk.
The ancient wisdom rang true: when a common man is enraged, blood is spilled. When an emperor is furious, millions die.
But when a Guardian is seething, the whole world burns.
[SFX: PANICKED SCREAMS]
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC STAMPEDE]
The Black Lotus Karmafire spread with suffocating force, making it hard for the hundred thousand disciples on the field to even breathe. A wave of panic hit the crowd. Screams ripped through the air as disciples scrambled for their lives, trampling one another in a desperate stampede to escape.
A few of the disciples were smarter, though.
They held back, watching the chaos like it was some kind of show, even though they knew that if the Black Lotus Karmafire kept spreading, everything would turn to ash. This kind of thing had happened before—spectators had been injured in past competitions, like when the Dragon's roar wounded a ton of disciples during Zane's match against Jacob at the All Peaks Competition.
But the fighters in that competition were the best of the best. The hundred thousand disciples watching today were far weaker. If they hadn't run, they would have been burned to a crisp by now.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
It seemed, however, that the Guardian could control the Karmafire and wasn’t about to let it run wild.
The spectators watched in stunned silence from a safe distance, their expressions a mixture of terror and awe. While Patrick’s Guardian contained the deadly Karmafire within the arena, preventing a catastrophic spread, another drama unfolded at the edge of the battlefield.
A slender figure with flowing hair pushed through the retreating crowd, her violet aura flaring visibly as she approached one of the tournament officials.
"She’s just a disciple, but she’s going right up against an elder!
And her aura is just as strong as his!"
"Who is she? Looks like she’s trying to save him. Seriously, why are all the girls around Zane so powerful? It’s not fair!"
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

[SFX: CROWD GASPING]
Just as Elara and the elder were about to clash, someone in the crowd suddenly yelled, "What?! Hey, look! Over there! I think... I think I see Zane!"
"No way. He wasn’t burned to a crisp?" another onlooker gasped, his eyes wide. "Whoa... check it out! His whole body is glowing gold. Did he... did he just achieve the Form of the Adamant Soul?"

[SFX: FIRE CRACKLING]
Elara spottedthe person who’d shouted, then followed his gaze to her brother. Just as he'd said, Zane was bathed in a golden light. He was engulfed in the Black Lotus Karmafire but was completely unharmed. Tiny golden whirlpools swirled across his skin, actually absorbing the fire wherever it touched him.
The power of the Karmafire was terrifying. How could he just... absorb the flames? How could his body possibly withstand the Karmafire’s burn from the inside?
Elara was completely baffled that Zane was unhurt, but a wave of relief washed over her. She gave the elder an apologetic look.
Looking down at the girl’s small frame, the elder's anger faded. He just shook his head, figuring she was just scared for Zane.

Standing in the middle of a hell of his own making, Patrick felt a chill, like he’d been thrown into a house of ice. He couldn’t feel the heat of his own flames—only a bone-deep cold as he stared blankly at Zane.
This is insane, Patrick thought, his mind reeling. He’s not afraid of Karmafire... he’s absorbing it!
