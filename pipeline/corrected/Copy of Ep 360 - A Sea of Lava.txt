The taunts she had so confidently hurled earlier had spectacularly backfired, leaving her reputation tarnished. Embarrassment colored her cheeks a delicate pink, a telling sign of her wounded pride.

[SFX: MAGICAL SCRYING DEVICE HUMMING]

Toby Dillon's eyes were glued to Zane's image in the Scrying Slate, his brow furrowing with analytical intensity. "His movements are... odd. He appears to have picked up something about the Spatial Laws, but only a little."

"Only a little?" The Leader of the Providence Sect scoffed, shaking his head. "Master Dillon, you underestimate what we're witnessing. If my decades of study serve me correctly, that's Saint Morphen's Mystical Pace. One can't even attempt something like that without mastering the first level of the Spatial Laws!" His voice carried the weight of reverence mixed with astonishment as he leaned forward to scrutinize the slate more closely.

The white-robed Leader of the Providence Sect, a man older than Sir Kenneth, possessed knowledge few others could claim. He'd once been granted the rare opportunity to enter the Spatial Sanctum of Cloud Hall. There he'd studied the Spatial Laws meticulously carved into living stone by masters long departed. Although he would never consider himself an authority on such profound mysteries, his understanding far surpassed that of most cultivators in the realm.

"What we're witnessing," he announced, "appears identical to Saint Morphen's Mystical Pace—a legendary technique where the practitioner glides forward along an invisible line without shifting their stance." His eyes narrowed as he observed Zane's movements with scholarly precision. "A true master of this art can cross leagues in mere heartbeats, slipping through the fabric of reality itself."

The sage paused, gesturing toward the scrying device. "But our young friend clearly stands at the beginning of this knowledge. See how he lurches forward awkwardly, only managing to go about a foot at a time? His talent is undeniable, but his grasp of Spatial Laws remains rudimentary at best."

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL COUNCIL CHAMBER]

Toby nodded. "You're right, that is the Mystical Pace. But the kid's a complete beginner—he has almost no grasp of the Spatial Laws. That's the only reason he can even manage it in a broken space like this. Don't you see how he's staggering, almost like he's feeling his way through?"

The leaders stared at the Scrying Slate. They could see Zane wasn't walking in a straight line on the path of trials at all. Instead, he moved in a zig-zag pattern, taking a few steps left, then a few steps right.

"I'd bet he's using the rifts in this broken world to practice," Toby mused. "The path of trials exists in a fragmented, unstable reality. It's not just the sky that has Fractured Seams; space itself is collapsing in other areas. Even if the path is stable, there are enough existing seams for him to exploit."

Toby's confidence in his assessment grew as he watched Zane's erratic movements. An ordinary person could never decipher the intricate patterns of Spatial Laws so easily, regardless of how rudimentary the application might be.

His eyes narrowed, a mixture of admiration and skepticism on his face. "There's no question he's picked up a little something about the Spatial Laws. But back in The Heartlands, it'll be an entirely different story. The boy won't find such convenient rifts to exploit there."

This kid's natural affinity both intrigued and unsettled him.

And as it turned out, Zane was right. The initial challenges scattered along the trial path were relatively simple. Each disciple encountered unique attacks tailored to their specific route, creating personalized tests of skill and resolve. 

The variations seemed deliberately designed to assess different strengths, yet remained conquerable by those with proper training. 

Despite the intimidating reputation of the Cloud Road trials, almost all of the elite disciples from second-tier sects made it through these obstacles. Even the ones who struggled for a moment managed to recover and press forward, hungry with determination. They refused to falter at the first hurdle of what promised to be a far more grueling journey ahead.

[SFX: PLANT ATTACK]

Along his path, Zane encountered several bizarre plants. One specimen, a great toothed fan-leaf with serrated edges gleaming like daggers, lunged from its camouflaged position and tried to swallow him whole. The plant's maw snapped shut with alarming force, but Zane, drawing upon his hard-earned combat instincts, shattered it to fragments before it could digest him.

Further along, an even nastier threat awaited—an Ensnaring Leaf, its massive foliage spread flat across the ground like an innocent carpet of vegetation. The moment Zane's foot pressed against one of its leaves, the others sprang to life with surprising speed, shooting upward and wrapping themselves tightly around him.

[AMBIENT: BUBBLING LAVA]

Cresting the hill, Zane was met with the sight of an endless sea of lava stretching out below.

"How am I supposed to cross this?" he muttered, a feeling of helplessness washing over him. Even though his body was as durable as a Top-Grade Spirit Weapon and could withstand the heat, trying to swim across an entire sea of molten rock was out of the question.

He wondered how the others had made it. They didn't have his unique resilience. This can't be how the trial was designed, he thought. Something is wrong.

Just then, Zane noticed a few other disciples standing near the edge of the lava. They were all just… waiting.

But waiting for what?
