Ep 458 - Stubborn Zane

Word count: 1073

[AMBIENT: QUIET TEMPLE HALL]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

The old woman may have looked frail, but a startling power glinted in her cloudy eyes. Her shaky steps seemed to belie a serious, hidden skill.

It wasn't until Zane heard Tia call her "Master" that it clicked. This was the woman pulling the strings behind Cloud Hall, the true power behind the throne.

Tia might be a prodigy—talented enough to have nearly mastered the final stage of the Goddess Path, one of their most advanced techniques—but she was still too green to be leading Cloud Hall.

[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

The old woman ignored Tia's greeting, her gaze fixed solely on Zane. "You're just a disciple from the Elite House. Who do you think you are, questioning the Head of Cloud Hall to her face?"

She then rounded on Tia. "You've made a fool of yourself. How can you just stand there and let some student speak to you that way? Where's your authority? You, the head of the entire Cloud Hall, actually apologized to him!"

Tia opened her mouth, wanting to explain that Zane was different, but the words died on her lips. She was afraid her master would ask why.

Why was he so different? She couldn't answer that. She just never felt the need to play the part of the powerful leader around him. That's what set him apart. But saying that out loud would only give her master more ammunition for another lecture.

"Maddox plans to perform the Soul Harvesting Ritual to break the bonds of death," the old woman said to Zane, her voice a dry rasp. "For it, he needs three warriors at the Core-Forged Realm. Each must possess the rare Violet-Core Physique."

[SFX: HEARTBEAT]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

The name ‘Maddox’ was like a shard of ice in Zane's gut. Instantly, Marilyn’s face flashed in his mind—the memory of her smile just before she'd left the Skyreach Sect. A smile he had sworn he would wipe from existence.

"Are you saying Marilyn has the Violet-Core Physique?" he asked, his voice tight.

[MUSIC: SINISTER WARNING]

A faint, knowing smile touched the old woman's wrinkled face. "You're a smart boy. Lucky, too. If you'd already killed her, Maddox would hunt you to the ends of the earth."

Zane nodded slowly. "The Martial Tournament is coming up. Will she be there?"

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

"Yes," she answered. "In the Martial Tournament, there are no rules to hold you back. The winner claims the Dragon Soar Arena. The World Merchants' Guild hosts it every decade to find the world's greatest warriors. Killing isn't just allowed—it's encouraged. If you're not strong enough, you'll be cut down without a second thought."

It was, for all intents and purposes, a gladiator arena. With the Dragon Soar Arena as the ultimate prize, every order paid close attention, each one desperate to claim first place. Naturally, the competition was brutal.

It was often said that the Guild's decennial tournament was a proxy war between the orders—a chance to test each other's strength during a time of relative peace.

"Are there requirements to enter?" Zane asked.

Before her master could respond, Tia cut in, her brow furrowed with concern. "You're not actually thinking of entering, are you?"

[MUSIC: DETERMINED STRINGS]

"Of course I am," Zane said, his face set like stone.

He had always planned on competing in the Martial Tournament. As a dragon-blood, he belonged to Dragonfall Palace, and to ascend to the upper world, he needed the Dragon Soar Arena. It was non-negotiable.

"Do you have any idea what level the other competitors are?" Tia challenged him.

Zane paused for a moment before shaking his head. "No, not really."

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

A bitter smile touched Tia's lips. "You're going into the Martial Tournament blind? Zane, the warriors in that contest are the best of the best from level four and five orders, some reaching the late stage of the Core-Forged Realm! You're only at the third level of the Soul Realm. You don't stand a chance. Besides, the competitors have already been chosen. You'd have to enter as an independent and qualify on your own. If you want to go get yourself killed, be my guest," she finished, her words a clear, frustrated warning.

Zane stood motionless, his jaw tight as he processed her warning. The silence stretched between them, heavy with unspoken fears.

[MUSIC: DARK DETERMINED THEME]

”I have to enter," he finally said, his gaze hardening to steel. "I will claim the Dragon Soar Arena, and Marilyn must die."

The words hung in the air like a death sentence—perhaps his own.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS WALKING AWAY]

He turned sharply and strode toward the door, each step deliberate, final. There would be no negotiation, no compromise.

"You—!" Tia's voice cracked as she called after him, but he had already disappeared through the doorway.

She stood frozen, her hands clenched at her sides. She had tried to make him understand the magnitude of what he faced, to show him this wasn't courage but suicide. Yet his stark declaration had shut her down completely. The certainty in his voice, that absolute conviction—it terrified her more than any enemy she'd ever faced.

Because she recognized it. The same unyielding determination that had driven him to challenge House Lowell, to defy princes, to stand alone against impossible odds. It was the quality that made him extraordinary.

It was also the quality that would get him killed.

[AMBIENT: QUIET HALL]

[MUSIC: GENTLE CONTEMPLATIVE MELODY]

Her master, however, just smiled with a flicker of appreciation. She had lived a long time and had seen more than most. How could she not see what was developing between Tia and Zane?

The way Tia spoke to him was completely different from how she treated anyone else. Her authority as the Head of Cloud Hall simply melted away. Her master knew something had happened between them on the Cloud Road, but the details didn't matter. She could see the bond Tia was trying to hide.

As long as Zane kept getting stronger, as long as he could become a man of true power, she wouldn't object to a match between him and Tia.

In her eyes, Zane was a rare genius, his potential as vast as Tia's. He would be worth investing in, worth backing with all of Cloud Hall's resources.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS FORESHADOWING]

It was a shame he was still so weak. Potential was a fragile thing, and not every genius lives to see their prime. In planning to kill Marilyn, Zane was effectively declaring war on Maddox.

Surviving the Martial Tournament would be hard enough; surviving what came after might be impossible.
