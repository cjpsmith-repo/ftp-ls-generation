Ep 86 - My Student ID, Please

[AMBIENT: SHOWER RUNNING]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE MORNING]
The next morning, Zane hauled his aching body from beneath the tangled sheets and stumbled into the shower. The cascading water soothed his battered muscles, each droplet a tiny relief against his skin.
Even with his exceptionally resilient physique, Boyd's relentless barrage had left its mark. Purple bruises bloomed across his torso like strange flowers, yet the wounds remained merely superficial—nothing that would slow him down for long. He traced a finger along a particularly vivid contusion, wincing slightly at the tender flesh.
The fight, however, had yielded far greater rewards than the physical toll it had exacted. A faint smile played across Zane's lips as he reflected on this exchange.
He'd not only mastered defensive techniques against a formidable opponent but had also mentally cataloged numerous strategies for future confrontations. Each blow he'd endured had taught him something valuable—a weakness to exploit, a pattern to recognize. During quiet moments beneath the shower's steady rhythm, he visualized these tactics, refining them in his mind's eye.
Perhaps most significantly, his body had absorbed the tremendous energy from Boyd's Ruin Ring, the powerful essence permeating his marrow and purifying it further. The battle had acted as a crucible, burning away impurities that might have taken weeks of meditation to eliminate. Steam swirled around him as he closed his eyes, sensing the subtle changes within—his Life Force flowing more smoothly, more potently through his meridians. At this accelerated pace, reaching the pinnacle of the Marrow Refining Level seemed not just possible but imminent.
But Zane wasn't celebrating. He couldn't afford to get too impressed with himself. Back in some small-town county, his skills would have made him a local legend. But this was the Skyreach Sect.
Here, achieving the pinnacle of Marrow Refining Level merely established one's baseline competence, while Zane constantly found himself pitted against an endless tide of privileged students from the Great Houses, each bearing generations of cultivation advantages.
[SFX: DOOR CLOSING]
Zane contemplated these harsh realities as he dressed methodically, his fingers tracing the fading bruises on his skin. A persistent knot of anxiety twisted in his stomach—not debilitating, but a constant reminder of the stakes. Nevertheless, he squared his shoulders, exhaled slowly, and departed his quarters, making his way toward the ridge with determined strides.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR MORNING AMBIENCE]
[MUSIC: UPLIFTING THEME]
[SFX: CHEERING CROWD]
The moment he emerged into the morning light, a chorus of enthusiastic greetings washed over him. Pledges who had previously regarded him with suspicion now called his name with newfound respect in their voices. He was no longer merely the mysterious newcomer; overnight, he had transformed into someone of consequence. Their admiring glances followed him as he passed, their whispered conversations falling silent in his presence. They had witnessed his victory over Trista, ranked thirtieth among Misty Peak pledges—a feat impressive enough on its own—but the manner of his triumph had left them awestruck.
"Morning, Zane!" called a young pledge with bright eyes. "That fight yesterday was incredible!"
What truly astonished his peers was the meteoric pace of Zane's ascension. His rise defied conventional wisdom about cultivation progression, shattering expectations with each passing day. No one had anticipated such a dramatic upset from an unranked outsider.
Though no official ranking bore his name and no formal documentation validated his prowess, the collective consciousness of Misty Peak had already rendered its verdict. In the minds of students and instructors alike, Zane had carved out an undisputed position as the premier pledge of their peak. His reputation now preceded him—a shadow longer and more formidable than his physical presence—built not through politics or family connections, but forged in the crucible of combat for all to witness.
[AMBIENT: ACADEMY GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT TRANSITION]
He left them behind and went to find Isla Novak, who was waiting for him.
Zane had returned from the Sunbelt completely unscathed, and Isla figured he must have come back loaded with spoils from his trip. She had watched his fight with Boyd and realized she had seriously underestimated him.
Zane had been so damn insistent on going to the Sunbelt. At the time, Isla had thought he was just being reckless—that hunting the Razor Swarm was a suicide mission. Now, thinking back on it, she realized it wasn't impatience at all.
Zane set his Storage Ring on the table and started pulling out crystal cores.
One after another, they piled up — ordinary Razorwing cores, hundreds of them, until they formed a small mountain on the floor.
"Four thousand six hundred and twenty-six," he said. "Regular cores."
Isla's expression didn't change. "That's barely two hundred Points. Is that all?"
He reached back into the ring. The Razorwing Alpha cores came next — smaller, darker, two hundred and sixty-six of them.
Her lips curved slightly. "Adding it up, that's nearly five hundred Points. Impressive for one trip, but not exactly—"
Then Zane placed the last core on the table.
Fist-sized. Purple. Unmistakable.
Isla stopped talking.
She knew what a Razorwing Queen's core looked like. She'd killed two Queens herself, and it hadn't been easy either time. She stared at it for a long moment.
"Who gave this to you?" she asked, her voice careful.
"I picked it up," Zane said.
She didn't believe him. He smiled and said nothing else.
[SFX: DIGITAL INTERFACE BEEPS]
Isla Novak's delicate fingertips glided across his Mission Slate, marking it with practiced precision. Her slender white hands then moved to his Student ID, where Zane observed a subtle shimmer of light pulse across its surface. With a graceful motion, she extended the card toward him.
"All set," Isla announced, her voice carrying a hint of admiration. "I've transferred one thousand five hundred Points to your account."
[SFX: CARD ACTIVATION SHIMMER]
Zane's expression brightened instantly, his eyes gleaming with barely contained excitement as he reached forward. His fingertips hovered mere millimeters from the ID when Isla deftly pulled her hand back, a mischievous smile playing across her lips.
"You can have this," she teased, her eyes twinkling with playful authority, "but on one condition."
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
The sudden stipulation froze Zane mid-motion, his momentary elation evaporating like morning dew. His brow furrowed as he withdrew his hand, shoulders tensing slightly.
"What is it?" he inquired, his voice flat and guarded, the earlier enthusiasm draining from his face.
"Tell me what you're going to do with all these Points," Isla pressed. It wasn't the first time she'd asked. In fact, she'd asked him the same thing when he first took on the hunting mission, and he had dodged the question then, too.
He still wasn't about to give her an answer. Refusing to play along, he just shook his head and said, "I have something important I need to do."
Her curiosity piqued, Isla persisted. "What mysterious venture consumes your resources? Perhaps I could offer assistance."
She twirled the ID tantalizingly between her slender fingers, her expression a mixture of expectation and intrigue as she awaited his response.
"This burden rests solely on my shoulders," Zane replied, his voice firm yet distant. "With all due respect, the matter lies beyond your concern. It bears no connection to your life, and I prefer to shield you from its complications."
Every action, every Point he accumulated served a single purpose—finding Elara. The thought of his imprisoned sister strengthened his resolve to maintain this solitary path.
"You're impossible, infuriating—!" Isla finally erupted, her composure shattering. Her cheeks flushed crimson as her eyebrows arched dramatically in undisguised exasperation.
Zane remained unmoved by her outburst. Extending his hand once more, he demanded in a voice devoid of emotion, "My student ID. Now."
Isla's expression shifted. Then her lips curved slightly and she handed it back.
What Zane didn't know: before returning it, she'd tampered with it — a small modification that let her track him through the letter tablet in the pavilion on the ridge. She watched his image move northwest across the sect grounds. The thirty-three peaks were south and east. There was nothing northwest except Infernal Peak.
So that was what the points were for.
