Ep 747 - Fiend
Word count: 1102
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane held back the killing blow, but there was no mercy in him. He’d already slipped into that focused, fight-or-die headspace, his face a calm, emotionless mask.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
Without warning, he threw a punch. One moment, he was still; the next, his fist was a blur of motion, erupting with raw energy and the power of the Dragon Scales. Compared to Maddox’s Supreme Emperor Fist, Zane’s attack didn’t look as flashy, but it carried a far more brutal, lethal edge.
[SFX: MIST]
Just an instant before impact, a black mist bled from Maddox’s body, wrapping around him like a shroud.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[SFX: MUFFLED THUD]
Zane’s fist met not flesh and bone, but something soft and yielding, like punching a mountain of cotton. All that unstoppable force just…vanished, a stone dropped into a bottomless ocean.
Zane’s brow tightened. What the hell is this?
He’d seen that same bizarre mist before. Back in Ethereal City, the Septuplets of the Ethereal Spirit Sect had thrown their strongest attack at Maddox, and it had done absolutely nothing. Now, Zane finally understood the shock on their faces. Still, he kept his expression perfectly blank. With a guy like Maddox, you had to expect the unexpected. The only thing to do was stay cool and bring everything he had.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
The mist might have saved Maddox from the blow, but Zane wasn't finished. A second wave of attacks followed.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
He lunged forward, grabbing hold of Maddox—still shrouded in that damn mist—and unleashed another surge of energy, driving him straight down.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
[SFX: DRAGGING THROUGH DIRT]
[AMBIENT: DUST CLOUD]
Zane slammed him into the massive crater, but he didn't stop there. He just kept running, dragging Maddox’s body through the dirt, gouging a deep trench in the crater floor. He carved one circle into the earth, then another, and another, kicking up a colossal cloud of dust that swallowed them both completely. The hundreds of thousands of warriors on both sides could only watch the spiraling plume of smoke.
[SFX: RAPID EXPLOSIONS]
Finally, Zane skidded to a halt, a glint of true killing intent now hardening his eyes. He raised his fist and brought it down on Maddox again, and again, and again. A chain of violent explosions ripped through the air, rapid-fire like a string of firecrackers on New Year’s Eve. A mushroom cloud of smoke and dust bloomed in the corner of the crater, completely obscuring the fight from view.
Then he stopped. His chest heaved. He stared coldly into the black mist, breathing hard. He had poured almost everything into that sequence — enough combined force to flatten ten mountains. And yet the mist sat there, undisturbed.
[AMBIENT: CROWD ROAR]
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL RISING THEME]
On the city walls, the warriors of the World Merchants' Guild and the people from Cloud Hall were on their feet, a roar building in their throats.
For decades, Maddox had been a nightmare hanging over every sect in the Heartlands. No one had ever pushed him. No one had ever tested his true ceiling. His deterrence alone had rivaled the combined weight of Cloud Hall and the Ethereal Spirit Sect — the Trinity Killing Array had given Cloud Hall an unbreakable shield, but offensively, everyone had always assumed Cloud Hall was outgunned. Zane had just torn that assumption apart. A Cloud Hall disciple had driven the number one warrior in the Heartlands into the dirt.
"He's not fighting back at all," someone said, voice cracking with disbelief. "Two years ago he was in the Soul Realm. Two years."
"That's beyond genius. There's no word for what that is."
On the other side, the independent warriors of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom, who had looked so defeated moments before, now watched with a fierce, renewed hope. Their Lord couldn't be this weak.
Among them, Simon stood with his arms folded, face unreadable. His feelings were complicated. Maddox being too weak was a problem. Maddox being too strong was also a problem. He watched the mist and said nothing.
[AMBIENT: DARK MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]
[SFX: SWIRLING MIST]
Then, from deep inside the black smoke, came laughter. Low at first, almost gentle, then building — faster, louder, until it was rolling out of the crater like thunder.
On the wall, Tia's expression went still. She shook her head slowly. "He's not injured."
Saint Morphen frowned beside her. "Under all of that — not a scratch. Maddox is truly unfathomable."
The ancient tycoon and the three Guild leaders said nothing, but the unease on their faces said enough. They had known Zane's barrage wouldn't kill Maddox. Knowing it and seeing it were different things. Even their estimates hadn't accounted for this.
Across the field, the Corrupted Divine Kingdom warriors who had begun to waver let out a slow breath. Their king was still standing.
Slowly, the black mist thinned and dissolved, revealing Maddox's tall, powerful figure. He was changed. His body was now covered in a layer of glistening black scales, and a thin, sharp horn protruded from his forehead.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
“You’ve truly surprised me,” Maddox’s voice echoed as he floated slowly into the air. “To think you could become a threat in just two years… I never expected to find talent like yours in The Heartlands.” A vicious light glinted in his eyes. “But it's not enough to defeat me.”
Zane’s gaze was as steady as still water. “Not enough? You think I’m weaker than you?”
“You’re not even close!” Maddox roared.
[SFX: BONE CRACKING]
A sickening crackle echoed from his body as his bones began to shift and rearrange. The change was shocking. His lower legs bowed forward, reshaping into the animalistic limbs of a predator. The muscles across his chest and arms swelled and bulged until he had doubled in size. He no longer looked human at all, now towering like one of the Ogres from the main continent.
Zane stared, startled by the transformation. He has some kind of special bloodline. Is he an Ogre? A Dragon? What is he?
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[SFX: WHISPER ECHO]
A faint voice echoed in his mind, answering the unspoken question. It was the Cobalt Dragon. You’re on the right track. Maddox is from another race. But he’s not a descendant of any of the hundred races from the upper world. He’s a fiend. From the Netherworld.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
A fiend. Zane’s heart hammered against his ribs, but he kept his face a calm mask, his eyes locked on the monstrous figure standing not far from him.
