Ep 569 - The Food Chain
Word count: 1084
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS WIND]
[MUSIC: DARK MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Doubts about the Tower of Sin churned in Zane’s mind.
He finally understood why Vesper the Immortal had warned him to stay away. Vesper’s power was far beyond any War Lord on this continent, which meant that by all rights, he should have been struck down by the curse of this land.
The fact that the Cursed Land’s lethal power had scared off someone as strong as Vesper was hard to wrap his head around.
So, what was the link between the Cursed Land and the Tower of Sin? Why were they both here? The questions just kept piling up.
[MUSIC: CURIOUS WOODWINDS]
Phoebe smiled, seeing his confusion. "That's just the way the Tower is," she explained. "It’s been upside down since the day it showed up. Nothing weird about it. In fact, only a tiny piece of the tower is above ground. Most of it’s buried."
How could anyone even build something like that? The sheer scale of it was mind-boggling.
[AMBIENT: DISTANT CREATURE SOUNDS]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON GRAVEL]
The closer they got, the more creatures they saw milling about.
[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL TOWN ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: GENTLE HOPEFUL MELODY]
The Tower of Sin was surrounded by a small town. In the heart of a continent defined by constant bloodshed, the town felt unnervingly peaceful. It was a neutral zone, a place where the curse prevented any fighting, allowing all sorts of races to live together.
The town had markets, auction houses, and all manner of trade. Zane barely glanced at any of it.
[AMBIENT: BUSY TOWN STREET]
Phoebe led Zane through the town, and they soon arrived at a building designated for humans.
"Every race has its own registration center," Phoebe told him. "You have to get a token before you can enter the Tower. This one's for us."
[AMBIENT: REGISTRATION HALL]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Seeing a few other human fighters coming and going, Zane walked right in.
In the middle of the lobby, a man sat behind a huge table. Back in the Heartlands, his power level would have put this man in the Inner-Core Realm, but here on the main continent, he was a War General.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
When the War General saw Zane, he sneered. "Don't tell me a seventh-level War Fighter thinks he can try his luck in the Tower of Sin?"
Zane frowned. "Is there a problem?"
"I admire the courage," the War General replied, his voice calm and condescending. "But trust me, you're just asking to get yourself killed. You could be a top prodigy from the Violet Heart Sanctuary, and you’d still end up as a corpse in there."
Zane shrugged matter-of-factly. "I'm going in."
The War General studied him for a moment, then sighed. He reached under the table and slid a steel token across to Zane. "Then… good luck."
He didn't try to explain further. People who didn't know the immensity of heaven and earth rarely survived their first night. He'd seen it too many times.
[AMBIENT: QUIET STREET]
Zane pocketed the token and stepped outside. Phoebe was waiting.
"Why did he try so hard to talk me out of it?" Zane asked. "Most of the fighters in there looked like War Fighters, not War Generals."
"Because you're human," Phoebe said with a small laugh. "Human War Fighters and Generals are generally weak here. When I entered the Tower as a War Fighter, nobody batted an eye."
Zane's eyes flickered. Back in the Heartlands, humans were the undisputed powerhouses — abundant resources, endless talent, and other races barely scraping by. But this continent ran on different rules. Resources were scarce. Numbers meant nothing when there wasn't enough to go around. What mattered here was raw individual strength, and in that contest, humans didn't have the edge.
Were humans really that far down the food chain in this godforsaken place?
[AMBIENT: TOWER BASE]
[MUSIC: EPIC BUILD]
They made their way through the rest of the town and arrived at the base of the Tower. Up close, it was overwhelming — a colossus pressing down on the senses, its inverted bulk disappearing into the earth. Someone had built a staircase up the side.
Zane stood there for a moment, just staring. Then they began to climb it.
At the top, two giants blocked the entrance. They were more than twice the size of any Ogre Zane had seen.
"Nameplates," one of them said, its voice like a boulder shifting.
Zane and Phoebe held theirs out. Zane noticed that Phoebe's was completely different from his — hers was plated in purple-gold.
"Why is yours different?" he asked.
"Every floor has its own nameplate," she said. "What you're holding gets you into the first floor. If you make it to the next, you'll need a new one." She smiled. "You'll understand once you're inside."
The giants stepped aside. The great door opened.
[AMBIENT: TOWER INTERIOR CORRIDOR]
Zane stepped into the Tower of Sin. A long, narrow corridor stretched ahead of him, and then opened into something vast.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
Just then, the battle in the nearby arena was reaching its climax.
It was a brawl between an Ogre and a short, incredibly stocky Orc.
Both had honed their bodies into living weapons. When they unleashed their energy, they just slammed into each other head-on. With every brutal impact, the crowd roared. The two fighters’ eyes were bloodshot, burning with killing intent as they locked into a savage, hand-to-hand slugfest.
"You can fight in here?" Zane asked.
Phoebe nodded. "You can — or rather, you have to. The Tower isn't really a cultivation ground. It's a fighting ground. Every resource inside is taken by force. The powerhouses who come out of here are the real deal — they earned it the hard way. Think of it as a miniature version of every race war happening on this continent, all compressed into one place."
[SFX: BRUTAL IMPACT]
[SFX: BONE CRACK]
In the end, the Ogre War Fighter gained the upper hand. A vicious elbow strike knocked the Orc out cold. The Ogre then grabbed his opponent's limp body and, with a roar of pure, brute strength, ripped his head clean off.
[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]
Blood sprayed across the arena floor like a fountain.
[AMBIENT: FRENZIED CROWD ROAR]
The crowd, far from being sickened, erupted in a frenzy. "Borgan! Borgan! Borgan!"
Borgan—that must have been the Ogre’s name. He casually waved the Orc's severed head in his hand and bellowed, "Who else?! Who wants to challenge the Ogre race? You're all trash from the lesser races!"
[AMBIENT: MIXED CHEERS AND CURSES]
His words ignited the audience. Cheers exploded from the Ogres, while furious curses rained down from the other races.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Zane barely registered the taunts. His attention was fixed on something else, something bizarre. The crimson stains on the arena floor… they were already vanishing.
What is going on? he thought, his eyes locked on the clean stone, completely bewildered.
