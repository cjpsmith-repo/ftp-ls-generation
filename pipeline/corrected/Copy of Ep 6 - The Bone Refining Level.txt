Ep 6 - The Bone Refining Level

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
[SFX: FIST IMPACT]
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
"I feel invincible," Zane thought, as Melvin's fist connected with his jaw. The impact should have shattered bone—instead, it sent a surge of power coursing through him like liquid fire. Every blow that was meant to break him was only forging him stronger.
[SFX: BONE CRACKING]
"Go to hell!" Melvin roared, his knuckles splitting against Zane's face. "I'll beat you to death!" Another punch landed with a sickening crack.
But with each impact, Zane had to fight to hide his smile. If Melvin only knew—each brutal strike was transforming Zane from a slave into something his tormentors would soon fear.
[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]
[MUSIC: TENSION EASING]
After what felt like an eternity, Melvin finally stopped, his shoulders slumped and his chest heaving as he gasped for air.
He glared down at Zane, who was sprawled on the ground but looked almost completely unharmed. Melvin was just baffled. He'd heard Zane could take a beating, but he never expected this.
The kid had taken punch after punch, yet he kept getting back on his feet, even if he trembled and struggled to do it.
Of course, Melvin had no idea Zane was faking the struggle. He was actually enjoying it.
The other captives standing nearby just shook their heads. They couldn't figure out why Zane kept defying Melvin, forcing himself to stand after every blow. If he were smart, like them, he'd just stay on the ground and play dead. The guards would haul him back to the cellar, and he could rest and avoid getting hurt worse.
But Zane just got up again and again, facing every single punch Melvin threw.
Maybe the guy liked being a punching bag. Did he actually enjoy getting beaten up?
[SFX: BODY FALLING]
[SFX: ENERGY PULSES]
[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]
After Melvin's last punch, Zane didn't get up. He just lay there, savoring the warm current pulsing through his entire body. He felt calm, invincible.
The other reason he stayed down was to avoid suspicion. Nobody could take a beating like that and not be seriously injured. If he kept popping back up without a scratch, people would start asking questions.
So, to keep from raising any red flags, Zane decided to stay on the ground and enjoy the feeling. Besides, he knew he'd just get beaten again tomorrow.
Seeing that Zane wasn't getting up anymore, Melvin let out a quiet sigh of relief. If Zane had stood up one more time, it would have been a total embarrassment.
[AMBIENT: CELLAR DOOR SLAM]
[MUSIC: ENERGETIC TENSION]
That night, Zane limped his way back to the cellar. The moment the heavy door slammed shut behind him, he dropped the act. Instantly, he felt alive, practically buzzing with energy.
[SFX: PAPER BAG RUSTLING]
Darren didn't show his face that evening, either. Instead, he had his goons bring three Vita Shards to Zane. Zane peeked into the paper bag and smiled. Looked like Darren had learned his lesson and wasn't going to try stealing his stuff again.
'Did that little stunt yesterday really scare him that much?' Zane wondered. 'Is he actually going to behave himself now?'
He shook his head. He didn't believe for a second that Darren would change that easily. He had a gut feeling a lowlife like that was already plotting something new. He wouldn't give up that easily. He was probably planning something worse, like trying to poison him.
"Who cares?"
Zane knew he needed to be more careful, but he also knew guys like Darren were cowards at heart. He decided not to waste his energy worrying about him.
'I'll just deal with whatever he throws at me when it happens.' He tossed the Vita Shards aside. He didn't need them anymore.
[SFX: WATER SPLASHING]
As his mind settled, Zane focused. A full day of fighting meant he felt filthy. He walked over to the water tank and quickly stripped. Scooping cold water into his palms, he poured it over his head.
[SFX: WATER FLOWING, DRIPPING]
The clear, cold water streamed down his body, pooling into a dark, muddy mess at his feet. But when Zane looked down, he was surprised to see that the dirty water was mixed with some kind of light, whitish substance.
Just as he'd suspected—the beating had forced the impurities out of his body.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL MEDITATION]
He thought back to the principles of refining.
The Strength Level was where impurities were expelled from the body.
The Bone Refining Level was where they were removed from the bones.
The Core Strength Level meant removing them from the organs.
These levels were a crucial process of purifying the entire body, starting from the outside and working in: from the skin to the flesh, then the bones and organs. It was a step-by-step refinement that only stopped once the impurities were cleared from the spinal marrow.
By completing all the levels, a person could shatter their physical limits and ascend to a new level of existence.
The biggest difference between Zane and everyone else was that they had to rely on their training regimens to slowly push impurities out. It was a tedious process that could take years, decades, or even a lifetime.
But after what he'd experienced, Zane now knew his path was different. He needed to get beaten up. That rush of warmth that flowed through him—it had the same effect as an Aether Crystal—let him discharge impurities at an incredible rate.
Compared to other people who, through sheer hard work, only shed a tiny amount each year, Zane's refinement was a thousand times faster.
Zane had been stuck at the peak of the Strength Level for a while. The last two years as a punching bag left him no time to practice, so he'd plateaued.
But after today's beating, more impurities had been flushed from his system.
He felt like his body had gone through a fundamental change. His flesh felt incredibly pure, and the pale white impurities washing off him could only mean one thing.
Impurities from the flesh were usually just black dirt.
These pale white ones were said to come from the bones. This was the proof he needed. He was sure of it: he had entered the Bone Refining Level.
[SFX: EXCITED TRAINING]
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
Energized and buzzing with excitement, Zane moved his iron cot out of the way, clearing a space in the cellar. He began to practice the Purple Flash Fist. As he concentrated, his whole body became bathed in a faint purple light, the same kind of glow that had surrounded Perrin Lowell earlier that day.
[SFX: MAGICAL MOVEMENT]
[SFX: LOUD CRACK]
Suddenly, Zane unleashed all his energy in a single punch. A sharp, ringing crack shattered the cellar's silence.
[SFX: DEEP WHOOSH]
[SFX: PAPERS SCATTERING]
A powerful rush of air blasted through the room with a deep whoosh, sending loose papers scattering across the floor and making the candle flames dance wildly.
Unfortunately, the cellar was cramped, leaving him little room to move. There was no kettlebell or Stone Golem to test his newfound strength on. Besides, Zane didn't want to make a lot of noise and tip off the guards. Still, he took a moment, letting himself enjoy the deep satisfaction of reaching the Bone Refining Level.
