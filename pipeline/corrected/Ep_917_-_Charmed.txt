Ep 917 - Charmed

Word count: 1072

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Even with the entire Demi-Immortal race's attention focused on the Evil Dragon, they couldn’t help but notice when Zane abruptly vanished.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

“Looks like a little mouse is trying to sneak away,” the young man in black taunted with a smirk. “Hey, Dorothea, isn’t that the one you have a crush on?”

The man in the silver mask cut in, his voice sharp with warning. “Don’t let him get away. Go after him. It’ll be a disaster if he interferes with the Ancestral Dragon Egg’s hatching.”

The flower pattern in Dorothea’s eye spun slightly, a flicker of surprise on her face. “Delroy put a magical barrier around it. It won’t break, will it? Still… I guess I should probably go grab him.” She let out a light chuckle and took off after Zane, her laughter echoing behind her like bells.

[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]

[MUSIC: URGENT CHASE THEME]

Zane was already shooting through the air like a missile, desperately channeling Vitality into Brianna's body while pushing the Wind Law to its absolute limit.

This guy… the girl thought, completely floored.

Just grasping thirty percent of the Cloud-Chaser Skill in such a short time was an incredible feat. She’d told him it was possible, sure, but she hadn’t actually expected him to pull it off. She’d only taught it to him as a last-ditch effort, a Hail Mary when she had no other options. She never imagined he’d master almost all of it so quickly.

Just how much natural talent did he have?

She couldn't even begin to catalog all his abilities. She already knew about the nine dragons living in his mind, and the fact that her own grandfather had chosen him meant Zane was far from ordinary. Still, seeing his power firsthand was staggering. Even in the upper world, a place overflowing with gifted warriors, she had never seen anyone with so many skills.

In just five minutes, Zane had somehow mastered seventy percent of the Cloud-Chaser Skill. Actually, judging by his speed, it was probably closer to eighty. The faint spatial fractures she could see trailing behind them were proof—they only appeared when the Cloud-Chaser Lion, the Legendary Beast the technique was based on, ran at its absolute top speed, compressing the very air around it. She herself could use the skill to its full potential, and she knew exactly what those distortions meant.

In seconds, Zane reached the arched exit of the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land and shot straight through it.

He stopped. There was no teleportation channel. 

He'd entered through the arch using the two dragon-shaped keys — they'd activated a channel inside it. But now the channel was gone. The arch was just an arch.

[SFX: EERIE SILENCE]

“The teleportation channel's been closed,” he said.

[MUSIC: GROWING DANGER]

Dorothea's laughter drifted toward him before she appeared. “Frightened? Good. Now be a good boy and come back with me. I won't kill you.”

“I have no quarrel with you,” Zane said. “Why chase me like this?”

“Who said anything about a quarrel?” she said, tilting her head. “I like you. So you're coming with me. When we're finished here, I can take you somewhere worth going. The ascension passage in this world is destroyed — you don't want to be trapped here forever, do you?”

Zane glanced inward. “She seems to have taken a fancy to you,” he said to Brianna.

From inside her inner world came an immediate scream. “Let her die!”

“Don't move,” Dorothea said, her voice dropping to something soft and warm. The flower in her right eye began to rotate slowly as she stepped closer.

[SFX: ENCHANTMENT HUM]

[MUSIC: HYPNOTIC STRINGS]

Something shifted. Zane's eyes went unfocused. The urgency drained out of him. Dorothea seemed to glow — radiant, beautiful, impossible to look away from. He stood there, perfectly still.

“You've been charmed! Run!”

Brianna's scream cut through it like a blade. Pain followed — sharp, sudden, jolting him back.

[SFX: SHARP IMPACT]

Dorothea was right in front of him, hand reaching for his face. Zane drove his fist out and threw every last drop of Vitality into the Wind Law at the same moment.

He was gone. A hundred feet of distance opened between them in a single heartbeat.

Dorothea didn't hurry. She followed at a stroll, watching him dart through the ruins ahead. In her estimation, the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land was barely the size of her palm. There was nowhere for him to go.

[MUSIC: TENSE CHASE]

Zane changed direction, scanning everything around him as he moved. There had to be a spatial crack somewhere — some tear in the fabric of the Secret Land he could slip through. He covered the ground fast, eyes sharp.

Nothing.

[SFX: DISTANT EXPLOSION]

Across the Secret Land, a pillar of fire roared into the sky. The battle between the Demi-Immortals and the Evil Dragon had reached its peak. Even from this distance, the heat was visible.

Zane tried to ignore the battle raging elsewhere, but that brief lapse in focus cost him. 

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

As he raced onward, a white flower suddenly bloomed out of thin air just ahead. It opened at an impossible speed, and Dorothea stepped out from its center. A smile played on her lips.

“You’re not getting away, little thing,” she purred.

Zane didn’t even slow down, just zipped right past her. 

[AMBIENT: CELESTIAL WIND]

Dorothea followed at a relaxed pace, content to pursue him through the celestial landscape. After a moment, Zane spotted a transparent, shimmering barrier surrounding a spire in the distance.

His eyes glinted. “What is that?”

Something dramatic was happening inside. The base of the spire was teeming with life, covered in all kinds of strange flowers and herbs. Vines wrapped around the spire’s outer walls, growing so fast he could actually watch them move. But it wasn’t just growth; it was death, too. 

[SFX: RAPID PLANT GROWTH AND DECAY]

The vines writhed, twisted, grew, withered, and died in a rapid, endless cycle.

“Time,” Zane breathed, his mind racing. “The flow of time inside that barrier is incredibly fast.” He turned to the girl. “What exactly is in there?”

She shook her head. “I’m not sure. The barrier is blocking almost all of the aura from inside. I can’t feel a thing.”

“We’re going in.” Zane didn’t waste another second thinking about it. It wasn’t like he had much of a choice. 

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

The moment he got close, his Vitality surged, and he slammed his fist into the barrier with all his might.

[SFX: DEEP RESONANT THUD]

The impact sent a deep, resonant thud through the air, but even with the full power of a Spirit Supreme, his punch didn’t leave so much as a scratch.
