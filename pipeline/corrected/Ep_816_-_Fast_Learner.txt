Ep 816 - Fast Learner

Word count: 1091

[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUND]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

From the sidelines, War King Wind let out a long, heavy sigh, his gaze locked on the battle. 

This, he thought, is the real test for Fiona.

It wasn’t that Fiona was a stranger to combat. She’d just never had a real fight for her life. As soon as she could defeat a warrior at her own level, he’d bring in someone stronger, and she never once disappointed him. 

Before long, she could consistently defeat warriors an entire realm above her. Now, in the late stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, she could easily handle someone in the second grade of the Life-and-Death Realm. Honestly, it wouldn't be a shock if she could outmaneuver someone in the third grade, either.

But those were just training bouts. The combatants always pulled their punches at the last second. She was the princess of the Wind Divine Kingdom, after all. Who would dare to actually harm her, even if the War King himself ordered them to go all out? That kind of sparring had never pushed her to the brink.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC STRINGS]

Facing Zane was a true trial by fire, and War King Wind could only hope his daughter made the right call and didn't embarrass them all.

When Zane heard Fiona begging him to give up, he wasn’t sure whether to laugh or cry. But for a warrior, quitting was never an option. 

After a short chuckle, he shot back, “I’m not giving up, no matter how much you beg.”

Fiona was at a loss. Her eyes darted around, searching for advice.

When her gaze met her father’s, he simply closed his eyes, refusing to give her a single hint. He wanted her to find her own way.

His silence, however, left a vacuum that others were all too happy to fill.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

Seeing the princess’s hesitation, the warriors of the Wind Divine Kingdom began to roar.

[SFX: ANGRY CROWD ROAR]

“Your Highness, just kill him! The punk doesn’t know what’s good for him. You showed him mercy, and he spit in your face! Finish him!”

Kill him? Fiona bit her lip, shoving the thought away. She’d never killed so much as an animal in her entire life.

After reaching the Adept Realm, she could sense all living things — when she walked on grass, she was careful not to damage it, and on the road, she stepped around ants and insects rather than crush them. 

Kill a person? Even if her life depended on it, she wasn't sure she could.

In her anxiety, her eyes went red. Crystal tears welled up and trembled at the edge of her lashes.

The Wind Divine Kingdom geniuses watching from below lost their minds. Every one of them had spent years admiring her from a distance. Seeing her on the verge of tears broke something in them.

[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD]

“Zane! You're no match for the princess, and you know it! Stop being shameless and admit defeat!”

“What kind of man makes a woman cry? Have you no dignity?”

“Admit defeat right now, or I swear I will kill you myself!”

The shouting grew louder. And then, cutting through all of it, came a roar from the ground.

“Would you all SHUT UP! You're disturbing my sleep!”

The Little Monster had rolled over. He squinted up at the Wind Divine Kingdom warriors with his black, white-less eyes, visibly irritated. Then his gaze drifted to Fiona.

“Hey, princess! If you can't bring yourself to kill him, just knock him out. It's not that complicated.”

Fiona blinked. Her tears stopped. Her eyes lit up.

She turned back to Zane with a small, sweet smile. “Since you won't admit defeat no matter what — I'm sorry, but I'm going to have to knock you out.”

[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE THEME]

Her white and blue dress fluttered as she leaned forward and floated toward him, light as a fairy. Time Law spread outward from her wrist.

Zane stood there in a daze, watching her approach at impossible speed while his own body moved like it was submerged in deep water. He looked completely helpless.

Fiona circled behind him. Her hand formed a blade and came down toward the back of his neck — a precise, calibrated strike. Enough to knock him out. Not enough to injure him seriously.

And then Zane moved.

[SFX: SUDDEN SPEED BURST]

Not at full speed, but tenfold what he'd been moving at a moment before. His arm came up and caught the blow.

[SFX: HARD IMPACT]

The force sent him flying forward like a rubber ball. But when he landed and turned around, he was smiling. He shook out his aching arm. No serious injury.

Fiona stared at him. “How — your speed just now — how were you that fast?”

She had mastered the first layer of Time Law completely. Within a hundred feet, she could slow time to a ratio of one hundred to one — for every breath that passed outside, one hundredth of a breath passed inside her field. At that compression, it shouldn't have mattered how fast Zane was. A hundredfold slowdown reduced even the strongest warrior to a crawl.

But he had just moved ten times faster than the compression allowed. Which meant he had pushed back against her Time Law with Time Law of his own.

“Did you just comprehend Time Law?” she asked. “Just now? During the fight?”

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

And in that instant, the Emperor understood. This young man hadn't just been taking a beating. He’d been learning. He was absorbing Fiona’s Time Law in the middle of the fight.

What was even more terrifying was that he seemed to be getting the hang of it.

War King Wind took a sharp breath. “We all know how incredibly difficult the Time Law is to grasp,” he said, his voice low and laced with disbelief. “What I want to know is, did this kid really learn it just now, or did he already know it?”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]

Scott, War Emperor York, the Harlen Emperor, and all the other masters at the Soul Sea Realm were lost in the same deep, unsettling thought. The Time Law was exponentially harder to master than even the Spatial Law. 

Not a single person in the crowd believed it was possible to learn it just from the ambient power Fiona was throwing around, not in such a short time. 

And yet, they had all just seen the impossible happen.
