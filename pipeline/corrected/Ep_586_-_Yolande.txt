Ep 586 - Yolande

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
The jump from a simple War Fighter to a War General was a monumental one, like breaking through the Soul Realm to reach the Inner-Core Realm.
But it wasn't as simple as it sounded. To make that leap, a warrior had to gather an insane amount of Lucky Light.
[SFX: BATTLE CLASH]
Inside the Tower of Sin, warriors could level up at an incredible speed. But even the most talented, saint-level geniuses had to constantly fight and prove themselves to advance.
Luckily for Zane, he was being challenged left and right, which slowed his climb to the next floor but sent the amount of Lucky Light he was collecting through the roof. Instead of burning through it, Zane was smart. He was saving it all up for one big push to break through to War General.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
The Tower's hierarchy was straightforward: the seventh floor housed third to sixth-level War Generals, the eighth held fifth to eighth levels, and the ninth contained seventh to tenth levels.
With Zane's strength, clearing the first nine floors posed no challenge.
The problem was constant challenges. War Generals granted default victories only to War Lords, whom they respected as superior. Viewing Zane as merely a War Fighter—regardless of his obvious power—they forced him to fight repeatedly, draining his energy.
A month passed. Floor by floor, match by match, Zane ground his way through all nine. And then, finally, he set foot on the tenth.
[AMBIENT: SANCTUARY HALL]
Back at the Violet Heart Sanctuary, the mysterious human who had suddenly appeared in the Tower of Sin was all anyone could talk about.
[MUSIC: HEROIC FANFARE]
Looking to boost morale, the human leadership began recording Zane's battles and broadcasting them across the entire sanctuary using a Scrying Slate. So, even though Zane had never set foot in the place, he’d already become a hero to countless human warriors.
The hype around Zane just wouldn't die down; in fact, it grew stronger by the day. Rumors started flying that he might be the legendary holy son of the human race. Slowly but surely, he was becoming a legend in their eyes.
What made it all the more staggering was that no human War Fighter had ever climbed this high before. It was a record that had seemed untouchable — and yet Zane had done it without once showing his true strength. From the first-rank War Generals on the sixth floor to the tenth-rank War Generals on the ninth, not a single opponent had pushed him to his limit. No one had even come close.
[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
Before Zane showed up, everyone saw Dylan and Olivia as the perfect couple — the two most talented members of the younger generation at the Violet Heart Sanctuary. But everyone secretly agreed that Olivia's talent was a notch above Dylan's.
But now, with everyone marveling at Zane's strength, Dylan and Olivia were completely overshadowed.
Olivia didn't seem to mind being out of the spotlight. She couldn't stop praising Zane whenever his name came up. This, of course, drove Dylan insane. Trying to talk to Olivia lately was frustrating; no matter the topic, she'd always find a way to steer the conversation back to the newcomer.
What made it worse was that he felt powerless to change things. He'd been training like a madman, fighting in the arena four times in the last month alone and winning every single match. An achievement like that should have had the entire Violet Heart Sanctuary singing his praises and showering him with rewards.
Unfortunately for him, all eyes were on the mighty Zane now. It was as if Dylan's own saint-level genius and incredible strength meant nothing anymore.
[MUSIC: DARK BROODING]
Dylan's face hardened into a stoic mask when he finally spotted Zane, a bitter reminder of how quickly everyone had moved on.
[AMBIENT: TENTH FLOOR ENTRANCE]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ARRIVAL]
A group of humans was waiting at the bottom of the stairs when Zane stepped onto the tenth floor. Olivia was at the front, smiling.
"One month," she said. "You're here earlier than I expected."
Zane chuckled. "Later than I expected, actually."
Olivia's eyes flashed with something between amusement and admiration. She fell into step beside him, close enough that a faint fragrance drifted from her. "Come on. The opponents up here are a different breed."
Zane nodded and followed.
Dylan hung back, jaw tight, eyes fixed somewhere to the side. He wasn't convinced — but even he couldn't deny that Zane had earned his place here.
[AMBIENT: LARGE ARENA]
[SFX: ECHOING FOOTSTEPS]
The layout of each floor in the Tower of Sin was more or less the same. After following Olivia down a flight of stairs and around a corner, they found themselves looking out over the large arena. The sight sent an involuntary shiver down Zane's spine.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
In the arena, a small figure was darting through the air, and Zane couldn't take his eyes off her.
He tilted his head. She was human. The woman fighting wore a colorful long skirt that danced around her as she gracefully dodged the heavy swings of an Orc's axe. With pinpoint precision, she flicked a fair, almost glowing hand through the air. Strangely, she wasn't using Vitality or an enchanted barrier for her attacks. It was more like some kind of Point Strike.
[SFX: ENERGY BURST]
A powerful burst of energy shot from the woman's finger, slamming into the Orc's massive axe.
[SFX: METAL CRUNCH]
The Orc was already struggling just to hold up the huge weapon, but after taking the hit, the top-grade Mythic axe was suddenly riddled with dents, its sharp edge completely ruined.
The axe slammed into the arena floor with a thunderous crack — and the floor swallowed it whole, the stone writhing and closing over the damage like a healing wound until the surface was smooth again.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS PIANO]
"Is her name really Yolande?" Zane asked slowly, his voice laced with an unease he couldn't hide.
Olivia nodded. "Yes." She gave him a confused look. Why is he asking that? If he already knows her name, why does he need me to confirm it? The question echoed in her mind as she tried to figure him out.
The truth was, Zane wasn't sure himself.
The Yolande before him had the same name and face as the Yolande from his memories of Alba Imperia. But something about her felt different, distant. He took a sharp breath, biting the inside of his cheek as he asked himself one more time.
Was it really her?
