Ep 331 - The Power of Blood
Word count: 1148

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMURS]
[MUSIC: GROWING TENSION]
The coppery scent of blood wafting off Bryson sent waves of shock rippling through the assembled members of House Lowell. Several gasped, stepping backward instinctively as the metallic odor filled the hall.
"That reek of blood... it’s overwhelming!" exclaimed an elder at the back, his face contorting with disgust. "That’s not a Lowell cultivation method—nothing in our lineage carries such a foul signature!"
A young woman trembled as she clutched her robe tighter as she whispered. "Wasn’t he just using the Purple Flash Fist technique? Why the sudden switch to something so... unnatural?"
A gray-haired master cultivator narrowed his eyes suspiciously. His voice carried the weight of recognition and dread. "Is that... the Blood Evil Force we’re witnessing?"
Another man called out, panic evident in his strained tone."Isn’t that the Blood Prisoner’s signature technique? The forbidden art that drains life essence? Why would Bryson resort to something so dark and corrupted?"
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]
Hodge Lowell and the others went pale, their faces draining of color as recognition dawned.
Not long ago, Castermere County officials had circulated urgent notices about the Blood Prisoner, a notorious criminal whose dark attunement methods had terrorized the region. The warrant laid out all the details: his distinguishing characteristics, physical appearance, and the signature technique that had become his grisly calling card.
[SFX: DARK MAGIC ENERGY]
The bloody aura rolling off Bryson matched perfectly with the Blood Evil Force described in those official documents. Hodge felt his stomach tighten. Despite the damning evidence before his eyes, he hesitated to voice his suspicions aloud. Such an accusation carried weight that could shatter what was left of House Lowell’s fragile reputation.
"Could it truly be?" he thought, his mind racing through possibilities. None among them had encountered the Blood Prisoner face-to-face, and the warrant’s descriptions, though detailed, lacked the specificity needed for absolute certainty.
Hodge glanced at the horrified expressions of his family members. Their wide eyes and trembling hands mirrored his own internal turmoil. Even if they couldn’t definitively connect Bryson to the infamous fugitive, one thing was certain: the cultivation method Bryson now wielded was absolutely forbidden, a violation of every sacred principle of their cultivation world.
Such forbidden arts carried a price that extended beyond just physical corruption—they tainted the very soul.
[SFX: SWORD SLASHING]
The sound of blades cutting through the air echoed across the arena.
With a flick of his fingers, twelve swords shot up behind Bryson, streaking into the sky and leaving trails of light in their wake.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
After dodging a storm of attacks, Zane sprinted along the length of the wall before leaping toward a small platform. Just before he landed, Bryson sneered.
"Zane, did you really think you could outrun my swords? You’re so damn predictable. I knew you’d jump for this platform. Let’s see you squirm your way out of this!"
[SFX: SWORDS FLYING THROUGH AIR]
As his voice faded, the twelve swords screamed through the air, converging on the platform. Zane was still airborne, with no way to dodge.
[SFX: ANGRY SHOUT]
"Rot in hell!" Bryson shouted, his lips curling into a mocking smile as he watched Zane’s apparent struggle. 'This kid’s still too green,' he thought. 'Another decade training with the Skyreach Sect, and he might’ve actually surpassed me. I wouldn’t have stood a chance.’ Bryson flexed his fingers, feeling the corrupted power coursing through his veins. ’But now? I’ve hit a whole new level. The Blood Evil Force, given to me directly by the Blood Prisoner, transforms everything. This isn’t just about brute strength—my skills, my power, my very essence, they’re on another plane entirely!’
[SFX: BLADES THROUGH AIR]
He clenched his fist, watching his blood-red swords converge on their target. The metallic singing of the blades cutting through air sounded like sweet music to his ears. A shiver of anticipation ran down his spine as he thought, ’Your little tournament victories mean nothing here, Zane. Your pathetic revenge fantasy ends today!’
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
But then, to the horror of the crowd, Zane made a move no one expected.
"No, not there!" a member of House Lowell shrieked. "Bryson laid a trap on that platform from the beginning! It was a setup! All those other attacks were just to funnel Zane right into it!"
[AMBIENT: CROWD GASPS AND MURMURS]
The members of House Lowell could only imagine the horrific outcome. It looked like the tables had turned completely, giving Bryson the upper hand. Some even squeezed their eyes shut, unable to bear the sight of twelve swords turning Zane into a pincushion.
Even the Lowell elders, who’d been so impressed just moments before, were speechless. They sighed in disappointment. "I can’t believe Zane walked right into Bryson’s trap."
At this, Melvin and Tristan clenched their fists, their knuckles white, praying for a miracle even as Zane’s chances dwindled to nothing.
Meanwhile, standing at the back of the crowd, Elara just shook her head and let out a soft sigh. 'With Zane’s body being practically indestructible, those swords could pierce his flesh and it wouldn’t even slow him down.I can’t wait to see the look on Bryson’s face when he realizes Zane’s about to beat him to a pulp.' A small smirk played on her lips.
Bryson was ecstatic, adrenaline coursing through him. Wanting to make Zane’s death even more dramatic, he roared, "The gap between us is wider than you can possibly imagine!"
[SFX: SWORD PINGS]
As the swords converged on Zane, they didn't tear through flesh.
Instead, a series of sharp pings rang out as the blades bounced harmlessly off his body as if it were forged steel.
At the back of the crowd, Zane's great-grandfather watched through blurred eyes. When the swords bounced away, two tears ran slowly down his weathered face.
"Heaven has eyes," he said, his voice barely above a whisper. "Your father can rest in peace, Zane. He has a successor."
Zane landed on the small platform, completely unscathed, and launched himself toward Bryson.
The sight shattered Bryson’s confidence. His strongest attack had failed spectacularly; Zane's power was clearly out of his league.
A cold dread washed over him. The arrogance vanished, replaced by a single, primal thought: run.
[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]
But there was no outrunning the indestructible Zane. He closed the distance between them in a heartbeat.
In a desperate panic, Bryson could only scream, "Help me!"
But Zane was already there, materializing behind him. He muttered icily, "Not even the Emperor could save you now."
[SFX: VIOLENT CRASH]
Zane slammed his fist into Bryson’s back. The force of the blow sent Bryson flying, crashing straight through the wall of a nearby house.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS THREAT]
Suddenly, a chilling voice cut through the stunned silence from nearby.
"Who dares harm my disciple? I will devour you whole!"
