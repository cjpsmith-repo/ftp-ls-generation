Ep 228 - Bet on the Underdog
Word Count: 1098
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL INTRODUCTION]
Throughout her years as a master cultivator, Isla had evaluated countless prodigies who crossed her path. She'd personally recruited numerous talented cultivators from the Eastern Territory to join Misty Peak, each showing remarkable potential in their own right.
But despite her diligent search and keen eye for exceptional ability, dissatisfaction lingered inside her. The truly extraordinary talent---that rare individual who could transcend ordinary boundaries of cultivation---continued to elude her grasp. Each promising candidate had ultimately fallen short of the extraordinary standard she secretly harbored in her heart, leaving her with the harsh realization that she still hadn't found the one she was truly hoping for.
[AMBIENT: QUIET INDOOR SETTING]
"Lady Novak?" Zane called out. He'd come straight to her room after getting the message, but she wasn't there. After a quick search, he finally found her in the central spire.
The troubled look on her face softened into a smile the moment she saw him. "There you are."
"You looked worried just now, Lady Novak," Zane observed. His gaze was sharp, and he could see the smile hadn't quite chased the tension from her eyes.
Isla neatly sidestepped the comment. "What have you been practicing?" she asked, her eyes narrowing slightly as she studied him. "Your focus... it seems much sharper than before."
[SFX: SUBTLE ENERGY PULSE]
Having reached the Consummation of the Adept Realm herself, she could feel a strange energy radiating from him---something sharp and cutting. It was so potent that it felt like it was scraping against her own protective Vitality. "He must have mastered some form of intent to be projecting an aura like that," she thought to herself.
It was the same kind of power cultivators in the Soul Realm used to overwhelm people with just a look.
"Could he have learned a soul-attacking skill?" she wondered. No, this was different. A soul attack was a direct strike, a weaponized piece of one's own spirit. Intent was more of a constant state, a specialized energy a cultivator radiated that pressed down on everyone around them.
He could have reined in his sword intent, but clearly, he wasn't. Maybe his instincts told him not to, especially since he'd only just mastered it. In hindsight, her decision to recruit him had seemed impulsive, almost naive. But she didn't regret it. Isla was the kind of person who stood by her choices.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
"To develop sword intent this quickly after starting your training... that's more than just talent. It's incredible luck." 
Isla wasn't quite sure what to say to him. She'd originally summoned him to discuss the Inner Disciple Trial. If he passed and became an inner disciple, he'd be eligible to compete in the All Peaks Competition.
The competition was held every three years, and Isla wanted Zane to give it a shot. Not that she expected him to place high---he was only seventeen, a kid with a mountain of potential but not nearly enough experience. He might be a top contender at the next competition, but certainly not this one.
There was just so much young talent out there these days. Zane was lucky, sure, but so were plenty of other top prospects. That's why Isla was keeping her expectations for him in check.
[SFX: DETERMINED FOOTSTEPS]
Shaking off her thoughts, she finally got to the point. "Now that you've reached Adept, you qualify to become an inner disciple. But you still have to pass the trial. It's tomorrow, and all thirty of the top outer disciples will be there."
Zane simply nodded. A trial like that shouldn't be much of a challenge for him.
Seeing that quiet confidence on his face, Isla pressed on. "Do you have any idea why I'm pushing for you to become an inner disciple right now?"
"Is this about the All Peaks Competition?" Zane asked.
Isla nodded. "I want you to participate in the All Peaks Competition. There's no pressure---I'm not asking you to win. Just treat it as a learning experience to get a feel for it." She leaned forward slightly. "But you should understand one thing: each peak's representation is limited based on overall strength. Misty Peak only receives seven spots."
"The competition brings together the finest inner disciples from all Thirty-Three Peaks," she continued, her voice measured. "However, the disparity in power between peaks is substantial. Skytop Peak consistently ranks as the strongest---most of their inner disciples have already achieved Consummation of the Adept Realm, with several having advanced to the Soul Realm. Compared to their prowess, Misty Peak appears insignificant. Our most accomplished inner disciple has only recently reached Consummation."
She continued. "It would be entirely unjust if every peak received equal representation. At Skytop, even a cultivator at Consummation barely qualifies, while here at Misty, some positions go to Fifth or Sixth-Level Adepts. That's why the allocation is tiered---Skytop, being superior, sends the most competitors."
Isla's eyes softened as she studied Zane's face. "Despite these challenges, I've decided to put my faith in you. Your progress has been extraordinary, and your sword intent\..." She paused, a rare smile crossing her features. "Perhaps Misty Peak might surprise everyone this year."
But even as she said it, the thought that had been nagging at her all evening returned. Less than a year. That's how long before Farrell would have to take the throne — and the moment he did, the Seventh Prince and the Third Prince would move. Zane was extraordinary, but extraordinary wasn't enough if the timing was wrong. She'd found the right person. She just wasn't sure she'd found him in time.
[AMBIENT: GOSSIPING CROWD]
The trial hadn't even started, but the gossip was already tearing through the crowd.
"Zane's already an Adept. He could be promoted directly to inner disciple. Why is he even here for the trial?"
"You don't get it. Making inner disciple is the easy part. The rumor is he's trying to crack the top seven so he can get a spot in the All Peaks Competition. This trial, this magic array---it's his chance to prove he deserves it."
"No way. I know Misty Peak isn't the strongest, but come on. The seventh-ranked inner disciple is a Seventh-Level Adept. Zane's good, but he's not \*that\* good. He can't beat someone that many levels above him."
"Ha! Have you ever seen Zane actually hit his limit? Besides, why else would the black-robed disciples be here watching? Look over there. The one on the far side, that's Ben. He's ranked seventh. He's supposed to have one of Misty's spots in the competition, but he looks like a total wreck. If Zane puts on a show today, he could knock Ben right out of the running."
Everyone thought they knew who Zane was, but they had no idea what he could really do.
