Ep 1059 - We’ll Give It To You

Word count: 1061

[AMBIENT: GRAND PALACE HALL]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORIENTAL]

In a hall on the highest floor of the Mist Palace, an old man in a long black robe sat with a young woman in a green dress.

The girl was young, only twenty—two years younger than Zane.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

“I was just passing through this world and thought I’d visit. And while I’m here, I need you to do me a favor,” the old man said slowly.

Yolande, sitting beside them, was the picture of respect. She smiled at the old man. “Elder Dunn, please, just tell me what you need. I’ll do whatever I can to help.” As she spoke, she sized up the girl beside him. “Are you hoping to have this young lady train here at the Mist Palace?”

Yolande’s deference was well-placed. The old man was a former leader of the Mist Palace and the original owner of Thundergust. He was a legendary figure. Rumor had it he was on the verge of becoming a Supreme Lord, which would make him one of the most revered figures in the entire Alliance.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

The old man shook his head. “I led the Mist Palace for thirteen thousand years and taught countless disciples, but I never took on a personal student. Until now. She is my first, my only, and my last.”

The young woman stood a little taller, a flicker of arrogance on her face.

Yolande smiled at her expression. If the old man really did become a Supreme Lord, this young woman would be considered his heir. At her age, with the chance to inherit the resources and attunement handbooks of a Supreme Lord, she had every right to be arrogant.

“Congratulations, Elder Dunn, on finding such a remarkable disciple. If I’m not mistaken, she seems destined for great things in the coming chaos,” Yolande remarked.

The old man looked pleased. But after a moment of quiet laughter, his expression turned somber. He sighed.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

“The age of chaos is already upon us. Even Supreme Lords are fated to be mere spectators. We can only watch and see how the chosen warriors of this era prove themselves on that stage.”

His disciple immediately declared, “Master, I won’t let you down!”

The old man nodded. “I know you won’t, Layla.” He then turned back to Yolande. “If I remember correctly, the Alliance is holding the ‘Passing the Torch’ event soon. I want Layla to participate on behalf of the Mist Palace. Would you be willing to arrange that?”

Yolande nodded without hesitation. “Consider it done.”

She understood now why he had come. Passing the Torch was the Human Race’s foremost method of testing talent — a universal measure of potential, open only to human cultivators. Its authority in the Alliance rivaled that of the Demi-Immortal Race’s three testing towers, and a strong result there could open doors that years of cultivation alone could not. Having a disciple of Elder Dunn’s line compete under the Mist Palace’s banner was no small thing.

“If Layla performs well, the honor is shared,” she added. “We would be grateful for the opportunity.”

[MUSIC: QUIET PRIDE]

Elder Dunn smiled. “You needn’t worry about her result. Layla has already opened nine lotus leaves.”

He said it lightly, but the weight of it was not lost on Yolande. Nine lotus leaves. How many cultivators across the entire Alliance could claim that?

Layla kept her expression composed, but inwardly her goal was already fixed. A good result was not enough. She intended to place first.

After a moment, Elder Dunn said, “If I remember correctly, one of my weapons is still in your Hall of Arms. I was hoping to bring Layla there today and let her retrieve it herself.”

He did not ask for it outright. The implication was plain: he was confident Layla could claim it, and he wanted Yolande to witness it.

A small, helpless smile crossed Yolande’s face.

“Is something the matter?” Elder Dunn asked.

“Not exactly,” she said. “It’s only that … that sword was taken a few days ago.”

[MUSIC: SUBTLE SURPRISE]

Elder Dunn raised an eyebrow. He knew better than most what it took to claim a weapon from the Hall of Arms — especially one of his. “Oh? By whom?”

“An ascender. His name is Zane. He’s at the Fifth Level of the Life-and-Death Realm and has passed five Small Heavenly Tribulations.”

“Fifth Level of Life-and-Death,” Elder Dunn repeated. For a moment, he was still. Then, slowly, he smiled. “I didn’t expect the Mist Palace to be hiding someone like that. I’d very much like to meet him.”

Beside him, Layla said nothing. But her jaw had tightened. Thundergust was supposed to have been hers.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]

Zane was surprised by the urgent summons. He got ready and hurried over, following her directions.

Two stewards led him straight into the grand hall.

When the old man saw Zane, his eyes widened for a split second, a bewildered look he quickly masked with a smile. “I hear you’re the one who claimed my Thundergust.”

Layla studied him with open assessment. This was the man who had taken what should have been hers. She had expected someone exceptional. What she saw was an ascender who didn’t look like much.

[AMBIENT: QUIET HALL]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Zane gave a respectful nod. “Yes. I’m the one.”

“Good!” the old man commented. “I’m sure you’ll make good use of it.”

“Does that mean you don’t want it back?” Zane asked.

It was Layla who spoke up. “Are you kidding? My Master has no use for a Grade-One Divine Weapon. Why would he want it back? Consider it a gift.”

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Zane’s jaw tightened. “I earned this sword with my own strength, and I paid for it with Sovereign Stones. What do you mean, you’re ‘giving’ it to me?”

If the old man had said it, Zane could have let it go. But hearing it from this girl, dripping with condescension, felt like an insult. He wasn’t some beggar taking handouts, and she certainly hadn’t given him anything.

“You—” Layla started to snap back, but she fell silent when her master shook his head.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]

He said, “He’s right. The rules of the Hall of Arms were set by my own master long ago. Whoever has the ability to claim a weapon becomes its rightful owner. Young man, since you obtained the Thundergust, I’ll also give you its corresponding mind method. Without it, you’ll never be able to wield the sword to its full potential.”
