Ep 1159 - Nowhere to Go But Down

Word count: 1127

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]
With all his advancements, Zane knew he still wasn’t strong enough yet—not nearly strong enough to protect Lavender. That was the whole reason her hiding inside his body had to stay a secret.
If the Queen of Punishment found out, she’d hunt Lavender down relentlessly. And Lavender had been away from her throne for far too long; her followers weren’t nearly powerful enough to stand against the queen. This was absolutely not the time for that secret to get out.
[SFX: ENERGY FLARE BURST]
But now, as the blood-red Faith Energy flared and reflected off his body, that secret was about to blow wide open.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACTS ON BARRIER]
[AMBIENT: DEEP CHAMBER RUMBLING]
A deep rumbling echoed through the chamber as Lincoln, still a puppet of the Malice, threw himself against the golden-haired woman’s shimmering barrier like a wild animal. But she was too distracted to even fight back. Her eyes were locked on Zane, her brow furrowed in confusion.
[SFX: TREMBLING VOICE]
“My Queen?” she mumbled, her voice trembling slightly. “Why is the light reflecting onto a man? What’s going on?”
Outside the Underground Palace, the Saint Lord of the Violetwood Sanctuary watched the scene unfold, his expression growing more grim by the second.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
“Our queen has been missing for so long,” he breathed. “It seems she’s been hiding inside this human warrior!”
The Saint Lords of the Ogres and Humans looked even more stunned. Just moments ago, the two Demon Night queens had been slinging curses at each other with Roaring Tokens. And now, it seemed the long-lost Queen of Slaughter had been hiding in this very palace the whole time.
[SFX: SWIFT WHOOSH]
[SFX: ENERGY BARRIER FADING]
The blood-red light vanished as Lavender, from wherever she was, cut the connection to the Faith Energy. The crimson glow faded from the golden-haired woman’s barrier, though it remained strong enough to hold back Lincoln’s onslaught.
But it was too late. The damage was done.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DARK STRINGS]
[SFX: ECHOING LAUGHTER]
A moment later, the Queen of Punishment’s manic laughter echoed from nowhere and everywhere at once.
[SFX: MANIACAL VOICE]
“Hahaha! I thought our little game of cat and mouse would last a bit longer, but I didn’t expect to catch you so soon! Oh, Lavender, you disappoint me. You’re even dumber than I thought!”
[SFX: ROARING TOKEN ACTIVATION]
[AMBIENT: COSMIC BROADCAST HUM]
The queen had used a Roaring Token, broadcasting her voice across the entire universe. Her words landed perfectly in Zane and Lavender’s ears, and in the ears of everyone else.
Now the whole universe knew: the Queen of Slaughter of the Demon Night had been found.
“My Queen, where are you?” a man’s urgent voice called out almost immediately. Supreme Lord Leroy, one of Lavender’s most powerful loyalists, had been searching for any sign of her, and the queen’s taunt had given him a clear signal.
[AMBIENT: PALACE INTERIOR]
Watching from The Mist Palace, Yolande’s expression flickered. She lowered her head and muttered, “This is bad.” 
 It wasn’t simply that this was an internal Demon Night affair — any move she made risked pulling in Supreme Lords from both the Alliance and the Demon Night at the worst possible moment. Across the universe, figures of immense power were lying low, waiting for the new Mandate of Heaven to descend. To drag them into a quarrel between two queens now would be deeply unwise.
She shook her head slowly. “Too careless,” she murmured. “I hope you can turn this around.”
[SFX: TELEPATHIC EXCHANGE]
Inside the abyss, Zane’s jaw was tight. This had been a pure accident — even Lavender hadn’t known the woman would invoke the Power of the Three Queens. Neither of them had seen it coming.
“Now what?” he asked quietly.
Lavender’s reply came after a beat — measured, but with an edge of steel underneath. “We go deeper. Get through the abyss first. Everything else waits.”
It was the only move that made sense. The Violetwood Sanctuary’s Saint Lord was already at the palace entrance, blocking the way out. Walking into him now would be walking into a trap. And they couldn’t stay in the abyss forever — Saint Lord-level fighters could force their way in if they chose to, and that patience wouldn’t last indefinitely.
So there was only one direction: down.
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL SHIMMER]
[SFX: GLITTERING LIGHT PARTICLES]
Back in the abyss, bright specks of light began to swirl from the walls, a pure Power of Purification. The motes glittered like snowflakes, dancing through the chamber before sinking into Zane and Freya.
It didn’t do much for Zane physically—he’d already shunted the Malice he absorbed into Killing Sword Mountain—but a wave of calm washed through him, easing the knot of anxiety in his chest. The power didn’t just cleanse the body; it cleared the mind.
As the light entered Freya, however, the change was dramatic.
[SFX: MALICE EVAPORATING]
[SFX: ETHEREAL CLEANSING PULSE]
The Malice inside her evaporated, and the vicious red glow in her eyes faded like mist in the morning sun. 
Soon, she was completely free, her expression returning to normal.
“Z-Zane…” Freya murmured, her voice weak. Just moments ago, she’d been lost in a frenzy. Her mind, controlled by the Malice, was a complete blank where the rage had been.
[SFX: STERN COMMANDING VOICE]
“Leave The Endless Abyss,” Zane said bluntly, his voice leaving no room for argument.
Freya froze. His tone wasn’t friendly or teasing like it usually was; it was dead serious. She looked down, biting her lip. “Okay,” she said quietly. She knew she was just a burden. Following him was only holding him back.
Zane saw the hurt in her eyes, the slight tremble of her lip that her steady voice couldn’t hide. He offered a small, reassuring smile. “Hey. It’s not what you think,” he said gently. “I just have something I need to do.”
“Something else?” Freya asked, her voice filled with concern.
“You’ll find out soon enough.” 
Behind them, still inside the barrier, the golden-haired woman watched Zane’s retreating figure in silence. She had grown up in the Violetwood Sanctuary, protected and valued by the Sacred Master. Her bloodline had always been her secret — she’d never expected to use it like this, never expected it to matter.
The roar of the Queen of Punishment’s voice still rang in her ears, and a sick feeling had settled in her chest. This dispute between queens was nothing to do with her. And yet somehow she had been the one to light the fuse.
She didn’t know if that could ever be undone.
[MUSIC: RESOLUTE DEPARTURE]
[AMBIENT: DEEP ABYSS ECHOES]
Zane turned back toward the wall. He reached it in three strides, turned his body horizontal, and planted both feet flat against the rock. With a single explosive push, he launched himself diagonally downward — and felt his descent speed spike sharply. He hit the opposite wall, adjusted, and kicked off again.
[SFX: RAPID IMPACTS, ACCELERATING]
Each push added force. Each ricochet drove him faster. Within moments, he had left the frenzy far above him, the sounds of battle fading into the dark.
Freya watched as he disappeared into the darkness. She shook her head, then activated her escape rune.
