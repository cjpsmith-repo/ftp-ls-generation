Ep 1151 - Too Good to be True

Word count: 1179

The true dragon and the demon queen didn’t really have much of a history.
[SFX: DEEP DRAGON RUMBLE]
Of course, the Cobalt Dragon living inside Zane had known Lavender for a while, so he had a pretty good read on her. Even if she was a hundred thousand years old, to him, she was still just a kid. He could practically see her emotions scroll across her face. And right now, she was clearly pissed that the Snow Demon was using Elara’s face to mess with Zane.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]
Since taking the throne, Lavender had become the Demon Night’s symbol of death, a name that struck fear into the hearts of outsiders. Her counterpart, the Queen of Punishment, handled internal affairs, representing the law and bringing judgment upon any of their own who committed heinous crimes. A queen’s ascension was always a momentous event, turning her into a living symbol for her entire race. It was even said that a queen of the Demon Night possessed a sliver of divinity.
After all these years, Lavender was more than just a queen but not quite a True God. Still, her people saw her as a goddess—all-powerful, absolute. 
[MUSIC: SOFT CONTEMPLATIVE]
But for someone who was practically divine, Lavender was showing a very human emotion: she was into Zane.
[SFX: CONCERNED SIGH]
The Cobalt Dragon had been genuinely surprised when he first picked up on it. But it wasn’t his business, so he’d decided to just let it be. For now, the Snow Demon had Zane’s allies trapped and had revealed his deepest vulnerabilities. The dragon could only hope Zane would figure a way out of this, and fast.
The illusion wasn’t entirely the Snow Demon’s work. The creature just laid the groundwork, pulling a person’s deepest desires to the surface. The victim’s own mind did the rest, building a fantasy so perfect it was nearly impossible to escape.
[MUSIC: GENTLE MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: SOFT WIND]
For Zane, that desire was a world of warmth and quiet peace.A sunset bathing Lowell Manor in a soft, orange glow. He’d be on the mansion roof, looking out at the serene landscape of Castermere County. No fights, no noise—just him, his family, and the profound silence of a life without struggle. 
He couldn’t even remember the last time he’d felt something like that. The past few years had been a blur of constant striving, of clawing his way up for more power. It was an endless, uphill battle, and the summit was nowhere in sight.
Elara was there too, sitting quietly beside him. She’d found a stack of old calligraphy and paintings in a corner of the ancestral house and dragged them up to the roof, brushing the dust off each one and organizing them by name with the kind of careful attention she’d always given to small things. 
Every so often, she’d unfold one and hold it up for him to see. “Brother, look at this one,” she said, her voice light. “It was left behind by the only top scholar in our family’s history. It’s too good to be left in a corner.”
He looked. He evaluated. Time moved the way it only did when nothing was wrong.
The sun dipped below the horizon. Night fell. Zane lay back and watched the stars appear, Elara’s voice a soft murmur at his side. The old ease crept in — the kind he hadn’t felt since childhood. His eyes grew heavy.
He let them close.
[MUSIC: GROWING DANGER]
Beside him, something shifted. The Snow Demon — still wearing Elara’s face — leaned slowly toward him, her mouth opening to reveal white, sharp teeth.
Zane’s eyes snapped open.
He stared up at her. His gaze was cold, but there was something reluctant in it too — a trace of a feeling he hadn’t expected to find still living in him. Then he smiled, faintly.
“This was really something. I liked it.” A beat. “I have to kill you now. Thank you.”
His hand shot out like lightning and drove straight into the Snow Demon’s chest.
The creature burst into a cold laugh, its hands twisting into claws, but it was already too late. Its mental attack had never been its strength. Once broken free of, it had nothing left. Zane twisted, and the Snow Demon came apart.
The illusion dissolved. The rooftop, the stars, Elara’s voice — all of it spiraled away like a curtain being torn down. Zane blinked, and the Endless Abyss came back.
[MUSIC: QUIET AFTERMATH]
He stood there for a moment, watching the last fragments of the Snow Demon scatter and disappear. He didn’t feel triumphant. He just felt the absence of something.
Luckily, Zane hadn’t been fully ensnared. He stood beside Freya and Lavender, watching the others who were still lost in their dreams. 
But the Snow Demon’s influence was a subtle poison. It didn’t just show you what you wanted; it showed you what *others* wanted. In the periphery of the illusion, Zane caught a glimpse of how Freya saw him—a version of himself that was taller, stronger, and undeniably more handsome.
They say you idealize the people you care about, that they always look a little better in your head than they do in real life. If you hate someone, the opposite is true. Still, the thought made a faint heat rise in Zane’s cheeks.
“You’re out?”
Lavender’s voice was as cold as always. But there was something else underneath it — quiet, half-buried. Zane didn’t catch it.
“Yeah,” he said, and almost smiled.
Trying to push the feeling aside, he turned to Lavender.“So, what’s the plan? Do we try to wake them up, or just wait for them to break out on their own?”
The Snow Demon hadn’t physically hurt anyone yet, but that didn’t mean it wasn’t dangerous. 
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
If someone gave in, letting the creature fully capture their heart, their soul would be devoured. All that would be left was an empty shell for the monster to pilot.
“We wait,” Lavender advised, her voice low and steady. “The illusions are powerful, but I think they have a good chance of breaking free.”
Not far away, the golden-haired woman from the Demon Night stood perfectly still. Her Snow Demon circled her slowly, shifting and reforming — but it couldn’t find a shape to wear. Whatever treasure she’d used to steady her heart, it was working. The creature couldn’t get in.
Just as she spoke, one of the warriors’ eyes snapped open. 
[SFX: UNNATURAL HIGH LAUGH]
[SFX: SWORD MATERIALIZING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT DRUMS]
“He’s been taken!” Lavender’s command was sharp as ice. “That’s not him anymore. Kill it.”
But Zane was already moving.
He yanked Freya behind him as a flash of light erupted from his Storage Ring. 
[SFX: Storage Ring FLASH]
[SFX: SWORD DRAWN]
[SFX: NETHER SHADOWS WHOOSHING]
His own sword was in his hand, and with a single, fluid motion, he unleashed a volley of three Nether God Shadows that shot toward the possessed warrior like bolts of darkness.
