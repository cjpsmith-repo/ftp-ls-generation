Ep 1141 - Windy Tunnels

Word count: 969

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]
It all went down in three seconds.
The moment Argus hit the blades, his rune flared to life. 
[SFX: DIVINE ENERGY PULSE]
[SFX: METAL BLADES WHIRRING]
A pulse of purple light engulfed him, freezing him in place just long enough for it to yank him back, pulling him clear of the shredding steel. A split-second slower, and he would have been ribbons.
[SFX: BODY IMPACT]
[SFX: BLOOD DRIPPING]
Even so, he was a wreck, covered in deep cuts and bleeding from a dozen gashes. He’d burned his one-shot escape ability, which meant there was no getting out of this a second time. As one of the top prodigies from the ogres’ sacred lands, Argus was seething. The purple light was still dragging him backward when he roared down into the abyss at Zane.
[SFX: ENRAGED ROAR ECHOING]
“You bastard! Even if you survive this, I swear you won’t leave the Dark World alive!”
The other ogre warrior wasn’t so lucky. 
[SFX: BLADES SLICING FLESH]
He never saw Zane coming and was caught completely off guard, giving him no time to react before a pair of intersecting blades sliced him to pieces. No one blamed Zane, though. Everyone knew it was Argus’s fault.
Still, the grisly fate of the two ogres made everyone watch Zane with a new kind of wariness. He’d been ambushed, yet he’d walked away unscathed while taking out two of his attackers. And the most unbelievable part? The kid was only in the early stages of the Soul Sea Realm. He was clearly not someone to mess with.
After that, most of the others stopped fighting for position. Watching what happened when Argus tried to wrestle his way through a gap in the blades was enough to convince them that staying put was the safer option.
Of all the groups in the abyss, the Demon Night warriors had fared best through the blade section. While the other factions had taken injuries and casualties, the Demon Night had come through without a single wound. It was a quiet display of supremacy that didn’t go unnoticed.
An hour passed before the blades finally vanished. Just as everyone was bracing for the next trap, someone shouted from below.
“Look! There’s a platform down there!”
They’d been falling through the open abyss ever since entering the underground palace, with nothing but air to grab onto. A solid-looking platform was enough to draw every eye.
“Let’s go!” a woman with striking golden hair commanded. 
“We go too,” Zane said. Lincoln, Freya, and Abner adjusted their positions and followed.
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
[AMBIENT: DEEP CAVERN ECHOES]
The golden-haired woman reached the platform first. She landed without a sound — weight distributed perfectly between feet, legs, and waist, the impact absorbed so completely it was almost elegant. Her people followed in the same manner, each landing as controlled as the last.
The Demon Night warriors landed like meteorites. One after another, they smashed down, and each impact shook the platform underfoot. A few of the human warriors shot nervous glances at the rock beneath them, wondering how much it could take.
Freya came in differently. As she fell, cold air gathered beneath her feet. She shaped it fast — stacked cubes of ice, one on top of another — and when her feet hit them on impact, the ice shattered in sequence, each layer absorbing momentum until she touched down cleanly.
Zane landed beside her and said nothing, but his eyes noted it.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WONDER]
Across a deep chasm, another opening glowed, revealing a cave packed with rare treasures and resources—a haul that would be legendary even for a tenth-tier sacred place.
“This is the first reward of the Endless Abyss,” Lavender said quietly. “Whether you can obtain any of it is up to you.”
Zane’s gaze moved across the treasure cave, then swept back to the group. He found the narrow passage cut into the rock and led his people in.
The cave was tight. Humans and the golden-haired woman’s people passed through without difficulty. The Demon Night warriors were another matter entirely. Their frames were too broad for the passage, and they had to squeeze through chest-to-back, each one cursing under their breath as the stone walls pressed in from both sides.
“So how do we get over there?” someone asked. “Can we fly?”
A few warriors tried, but it was no use. Now that the blade trap was gone, they’d hoped the restriction on flight was lifted, but they were still grounded.
[SFX: FRUSTRATED VOICE]
“Well, how are we supposed to get our hands on all that if we can’t even fly?” another warrior complained.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
The golden-haired woman’s eyes scanned the chasm wall below them. “There,” she said, pointing.
Everyone followed her gaze.
[MUSIC: AWE AND DISCOVERY]
[SFX: POWERFUL WIND GUSTS]
Lining their side of the cavern was a series of over a hundred natural stone platforms, each one jutting out from the rock face. At the base of every platform was a circular tunnel, and it was from these that the strange music they’d been hearing was coming. Powerful gusts of wind blasted out of the tunnels, whistling as they rushed past the stone pillars. The differently sized platforms created different pitches, turning the entire cavern into a colossal, natural flute.
The wind was fierce, but it moved in patterns, following rules no one had set consciously. There were no wind blades. The gusts weren’t colliding or being shaped by any living hand. They simply flowed.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON PLATFORM]
[SFX: WIND RUSHING]
The woman hopped onto the nearest platform and leaned over, peering into the wind tunnel below. After a moment, she straightened up. “It looks like each tunnel leads to a different path.”
A murmur of understanding went through the crowd.
“She’s right!” one of the warriors shouted, the realization dawning on him. “If we take different tunnels, we’ll end up in different spots on the other side. We can get different treasures!”
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
[SFX: URGENT WARNING SHOUT]
But just as the group began to surge forward, a human warrior pointed up toward the cavern’s ceiling and yelled, “Wait, look! What is that?”
