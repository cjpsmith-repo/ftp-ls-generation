Ep 608 - Lily's Real Name
Word count: 1042
[AMBIENT: MYSTERIOUS WIND]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
Lily’s origins had always been a blur to Zane. He’d had his suspicions, of course—his own little theories about who, or what, she really was.
Most sword spirits were just puppets, blindly following their master's orders. They could only handle simple, robotic commands.
But Lily… Lily had a sword dance that Zane couldn't begin to comprehend. How could a sword spirit know a martial art her own master had never even seen? The fact that even the all-knowing Cobalt Dragon was stumped only deepened the mystery.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL REVELATION]
As the pieces clicked into place, a wild theory formed in Zane’s mind: his sword spirit might actually be possessed by the soul of the Demon Night queen. That would mean she had her own thoughts, her own will.
Putting a soul into a sword spirit wasn't impossible. He’d learned that much back on Killing Sword Mountain when he ran into a guy named Vale. Vale had merged a piece of his own soul with his sword spirit and acted like it was no big deal.
But for this Demon Night queen to have somehow slipped her entire soul into his sword spirit without him sensing a thing? That was on another level. If he couldn't detect it, what chance did the Cobalt Dragon have?
Staring at his sword spirit, Zane struggled to find his voice. He had to know who she was. The curiosity was eating him alive, a question that had stolen countless nights of sleep.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]
[SFX: ETHEREAL CHIME]
"My name isn't Lily," the sword spirit said, her gaze coolly indifferent as it swept over Zane. "It's Lavender. That is my true name."
"Lavender?" Zane repeated, his brow furrowed. "Like the flower?"
He suddenly recalled two other names from the top of the Talent Tablet: Fire and Cliff. He sighed, trying to force another piece into the puzzle.
Humans weren’t usually named after things in nature. That meant Fire and Cliff... they could also be from the Demon Night. Just like Lily—no, Lavender.
Then again, the upper world was home to countless races, so there was no guarantee. While it was rare, other cultures might have similar naming customs.
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
"Can you tell me how you ended up as my sword spirit?" Zane asked, managing to rein in his emotions. At least she didn't seem hostile, which was a small relief.
"I can't explain that now. I hope you can understand," Lavender said, her tone leaving no room for argument.
[MUSIC: REVERENT ORCHESTRAL]
"For now, I need you to go to the sacred place of the Demon Night." She then turned her attention to Elder May and Madeline. "What is our sanctuary called? And its grade?"
Elder May bowed his head. "Your Majesty. Our sanctuary on the Main Continent is called the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary. It is a seventh-grade sanctuary."
Lavender was quiet for a moment. "To develop a seventh-grade sanctuary in a place this barren — very good. Take me there now."
There was no request in her voice. Only certainty.
"We would be honored to serve you," Elder May and Madeline said together.
To the Demon Night, Lavender was absolute. If she had asked them to lay down their lives on the spot, neither would have hesitated.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
"Wait."
Malcolm's voice cut across the arena like a blade.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
He stepped forward, his massive frame blocking the path. The scene had left him completely turned around. He'd allowed Cheryl to fight, thinking it would buy time — that he'd still get to kill Zane when it was done. That was how this was supposed to go. Instead, Cheryl had knelt in the middle of the fight, a queen had materialized out of nowhere, and now Zane was about to simply walk away.
That was not happening.
Lavender looked at him. Her expression didn't change. "What is it?"
"I don't care what you are to the Demon Night," Malcolm said, his voice hard. "Whatever you do, wherever you go — that's your business. But the boy stays."
He didn't fully understand what Lavender was. Her aura was unlike anything he'd encountered, and some part of him felt small standing in front of her. But in a world built on strength, aura alone wasn't enough to make him step aside. Zane had to die. That hadn't changed.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
The moment Malcolm finished speaking, two figures materialized at his sides.
Elder May and Madeline. No warning. No words.
[SFX: BLADE CUTTING AIR]
[SFX: MAGICAL PETALS SWIRLING]
Elder May's black dagger drove straight at Malcolm's ribs. Madeline's fingers bloomed with white flower petals, each one carrying a terrifying compressed force, sweeping toward his head.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[SFX: BLADE PIERCING FLESH]
Malcolm threw both fists at once — one to scatter Madeline's attack, one to push Elder May back. He managed it, barely. But Elder May's dagger found his shoulder anyway, and a thick stream of brown blood burst from the wound.
Malcolm clamped a hand over the gash, trying to staunch the bleeding.
[MUSIC: RISING DANGER]
But he didn't know that Elder May's dagger was enchanted to inhibit healing. The Ogre's regenerative abilities were failing him.
"Is this a declaration of war?" Malcolm snarled, his face twisting in disbelief. "Has the Demon Night finally decided to move against the Ogres?" He couldn't wrap his head around it. These two were insane, attacking him without a thought for the consequences. And who the hell was this Lavender they were protecting?
"Anyone who humiliates our queen forfeits their life," Madeline said, her voice dangerously low.
"We would burn two of your sanctuaries to the ground to appease our queen," Elder May added with chilling confidence. "What makes you think we'd hesitate to declare war on your entire race?"
They're insane, Malcolm thought, panic rising in his chest. The Demon Night are all stark raving mad!
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
He couldn't fathom their recklessness—the sheer scale of the fallout from this one act.
If he’d known it would come to this, he never would have let Cheryl challenge that human. Now, with this injury, his chances of beating Zane were shrinking by the second.
Whether Malcolm had agreed to Cheryl’s challenge or not, this confrontation was inevitable. Nothing would have changed.
[AMBIENT: DISTANT ARENA CROWD]
But if the fight in the arena could just be dragged out a little longer, Elder May still had a chance to keep Malcolm from killing Zane.
Whether Zane himself had any say in that was another question entirely.
