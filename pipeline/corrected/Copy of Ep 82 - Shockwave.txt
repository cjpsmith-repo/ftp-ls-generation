The Misty Peak pledges might not have been the strongest, but they weren't stupid. They knew a pledge with that kind of power at the Marrow Refining Level was destined for great things. That's why this was so tough to watch.
But Zane was sticking to his plan. One punch. That's all it would take.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
An incredible power erupted from the Dragon Scale Shards, flowing into Zane's own energy as he threw the punch.
[SFX: HEAVY PUNCH]
The blow landed square on Keith's shoulder—his strongest point, and the very one he'd planned to ram into Zane with. Seeing Zane raise a fist to counter didn't faze him. Confident in his raw power, Keith didn't even try to dodge. He just slammed his body right into it.
Keith figured the moment Zane's fist met his shoulder, the sheer force of the impact would snap his arm in two.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
But the second his shoulder crashed into Zane's fist, Keith's expression twisted. He felt a colossal force—far greater than his own—explode from the point of impact.
If Keith's power was a charging beast—all raw momentum and explosive force—then Zane's was an immovable mountain. The collision sent Keith flying, launching him clean out of the arena.
The Misty Peak pledges went absolutely wild. Words failed them. All they could do was roar, letting the cheers pour out of them, the only release for their insane excitement.
[AMBIENT: DISTANT CHEERING]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS OBSERVATION]
Hundreds of feet away, perched atop a small building, two women had been watching every single fight. Both carried themselves with an elegant poise, their beauty striking.
"Isla Novak, you played me!" the speaker, Chloe, one of Eagle Peak's masters, exclaimed. "You said we should set up a competition to motivate your division's pledges, and I jumped right on board. I brought my most talented kids from Eagle Peak, and all the while you were hiding a prodigy like this?"
The truth was, the two masters had orchestrated the entire challenge themselves. They'd try just about anything to motivate the pledges in their respective divisions and light a fire under them. A Divisional Championship was one of the most effective tools they had.
A loss for Misty Peak might have stung their pride for a while, but it would have also been a hell of a wake-up call, proof that they needed to work harder to keep up. It was a harsh lesson, but a necessary one: only the weak get pushed around. That's why Isla had accepted the challenge in the first place.
[MUSIC: TENSE REVELATION]
Isla frowned, her eyes fixed on Zane. "That Outer Disciple took a contract to handle a Razor swarm down in the Sunbelt. He left Misty Peak not even half a month ago. I never expected him to be back so soon... let alone this strong. His progress is... startling."
"What?" Chloe's eyes widened. "A Marrow Refining Level pledge took on a Razor swarm? Am I hearing you right? Then again... I guess this kid actually has the muscle for it." She shot a helpless glance toward the battle ring. "He's ruthless. Taking out every opponent with a single punch. It's like he's making a statement."
Hearing Chloe's lament, a smug smile touched Isla's lips. "Or maybe," she said, her voice dripping with mock sympathy, "the pledges from your peak are just too weak!"
"Oh, you're one to talk," Chloe shot back, though there was no real heat in her voice. "I'm out here helping you motivate your division, and you're gloating! Zane is powerful, I'll give you that, but Eagle Peak isn't out of talent yet. Our last one is Boyd. He's our number one for a reason—he's already a nature creature. As long as Boyd gets in that ring, Zane doesn't stand a chance."
"A nature creature? Then why is he still just an Outer Disciple?" Isla asked, her surprise genuine.
Dozens of pledges get promoted to Varsity members every year, and most of them are only High-tier candidates. For a nature creature to still be an Outer Disciple was almost unheard of.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD ROARING]
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
As the masters bantered and the crowd roared, the two fighters in the ring remained oblivious, their world shrinking to the space between them.
[SFX: PULSING ENERGY]
The Aegis Band in Boyd's hand pulsed with dormant energy, its surface rippling like liquid metal. "You're remarkably powerful," he observed with an appreciative yet casual demeanor. "Defeating three High-tier candidates consecutively? Such a feat has never crossed my path before."
"I value your acknowledgment," Zane responded, offering a measured nod while studying his opponent's relaxed posture.
"Yet you've also tarnished Eagle Peak's reputation," Boyd continued, his eyes reflecting a calculated intelligence behind their serene exterior. "Some might perceive my involvement as excessive, given my nature creature status, but defeat isn't an option I can entertain." He paused, brushing a strand of hair from his face with deliberate grace. "This confrontation transcends a simple match. Should I falter, Eagle Peak's standing crumbles, leaving us without credibility throughout the Skyreach Sect." His voice maintained its tranquil quality, devoid of aggression or excitement—as though discussing something as mundane as changing weather patterns rather than an impending battle.
Zane mirrored his opponent's composure with a placid smile, though his muscles tensed imperceptibly beneath his robes. "In that case, I hope you'll show restraint."
"Rest assured," Boyd replied, the corners of his mouth lifting slightly as his fingers caressed the glowing band. "I'll merely render you incapable of continuing. No lasting harm will befall you."
[SFX: ENERGY SWIRLING]
With those words hanging in the air, he casually flicked his wrist, sending the Aegis Band—a swirling manifestation of his own Vitality, shimmering with intricate patterns of energy—floating toward Zane like a predator approaching its prey.
