Ep 1051 - The Crowd Goes Wild

Word count: 1076

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane hung in the air, biding his time.

From this height, he had a clear view of the blue dot of light’s erratic path.

Once he had its trajectory figured out, a determined glint flashed in his eyes, and he shot forward.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Even with his energy nearly spent, he didn’t slow his rush.

He figured his chances were good—eighty percent, at least.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]

Down below, a buzz went through the other warriors.

“He’s so close! How is he still moving that fast? The guy’s a beast!”

“Any one of those Divine Weapons is priceless. He’s about to snag one right after joining the Mist Palace. I’m so jealous.”

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

[SFX: WIND RUSH]

Zane leaped toward the blue dot, closing the distance between them in a heartbeat.

The point of light drifted aimlessly, but Zane had already calculated its likely path for the next few seconds and positioned himself for the intercept.

He was one step ahead, waiting for the light to come to him. As it moved, it drifted closer and closer.

[AMBIENT: TENSE HALL ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

“So close!” A small smile touched Zane’s lips as he reached out to grab the point of light.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

When it was less than a foot from his hand, the light suddenly seemed to sense the danger and stopped dead in mid-air.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

The smile on Zane’s face froze. He gritted his teeth and lunged for it in a flash.

But as he moved, the point of light darted backward.

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMURS]

Zane was stunned. Down below, so were the other warriors in the Hall of Arms.

[SFX: GASPS OF DISBELIEF]

“Are you kidding me? I’ve never seen anything like it! The light just… it dodged him!”

It was just like when Ryder had tried for the red light earlier. He’d almost had it, but it slipped away at the last second.

The warriors knew the orbs were supposed to float randomly. With Ryder, they’d just chalked it up to bad luck, figuring he would’ve caught it if he’d been a little faster.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

But what that blue dot just did was insane.

The second it was about to reach Zane, it stopped cold. Then, when he reached for it, the thing looked like it decided to dodge him.

It was unbelievable.

Zane refused to let it get to him. Instead, he focused on regaining his altitude. The lunge had cost him four feet, and now, his expression grim, he slowly pushed himself back up. Fighting the powerful gravitational pull just to stay afloat was a feat in itself.

[SFX: RAPID WHOOSH, REPEATED]

Once he’d steadied himself, he went after it again. He poured on speed — and the blue dot poured on speed right back.

It was like an eagle chasing a hummingbird. Except the hummingbird knew exactly what it was doing.

The two of them circled the dome ceiling — once, twice, again — Zane cutting angles, the orb cutting sharper ones. The gravity clawed at him the whole time, bleeding off every burst of speed before he could close the distance. After the third pass, he was forced to stop, hovering in place, chest heaving, eyes locked on the light.

It floated just out of reach and stayed there.

[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD MURMURING]

Meanwhile, the mood below soured. The warriors grew louder, their voices sharp with tension. A few of them stepped toward the white-eyebrowed elder. “Elder, what’s the deal? Are you messing with us?”

The white-eyebrowed elder cleared his throat. “There is a remnant of spiritual intelligence left in the former Palace Master’s weapon,” he said, keeping his voice steady. “Catching a sword like that naturally requires… considerably more effort.”

[SFX: SKEPTICAL MURMURS]

The crowd went quiet for exactly one second. Then the noise came back louder than before. Nobody believed him. The explanation was the kind of thing you’d say to a child and hope they didn’t ask follow-up questions.

Among those glaring at the elder was Ryder. He wasn’t there to defend Zane — he couldn’t care less about Zane. But the elder’s cover story had put a thought in his head that he couldn’t shake: had the same thing happened to him with the red orb? He’d been so close. At the last second it had slipped away. At the time he’d blamed his own luck.

He still told himself that now. But he kept the glare on the elder anyway.

“Yeah! If you don’t want to give up the weapons, just say so! Stop playing games!”

“Hey, man, get down from there! You can’t get it! The elder’s just making a fool out of you!”

“Three hundred Sovereign Stones for one attempt and we leave with nothing,” someone called out. “Let’s spread the word. Tell every warrior in the Mist Palace — don’t waste your stones here!”

“That’s right! This whole hall is a con!”

As the crowd got louder, the white-eyebrowed elder couldn’t hide his dismay. His face was beet red with embarrassment. If word of this got out, no warrior would ever come to the Hall of Arms again. That was the last thing he wanted.

He had to admit, even though Zane couldn’t seem to catch the light, the warrior’s physical strength was off the charts. Zane was somehow holding steady at two hundred feet. Every time he dropped, he immediately pushed himself back up.

The white-eyebrowed elder had no idea what Zane’s limits were. He could increase the gravity from the divine texture, but if Zane couldn’t handle it… Besides, messing with the divine texture was a huge hassle.

While the elder stood there in a daze, getting an earful from the crowd, Zane saw his opening.

He’d seen the commotion below and knew exactly what was happening. Wasting no time, he took advantage of the distraction and went for the blue light again.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT DRUMS]

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Pushing himself to the absolute limit, he surged forward with a dramatic burst of speed.

[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]

His fingers closed around the blue light, and he immediately dropped like a stone. He landed steady on his feet, a shockingly long and sharp sword now in his hand.

[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC CROWD SHOUTING]

Even with Zane holding the sword, the other warriors didn’t stop yelling at the white-eyebrowed elder.

The whole scene was spiraling out of control.
