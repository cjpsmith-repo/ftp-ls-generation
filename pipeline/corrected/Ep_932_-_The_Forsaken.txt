Ep 932 - The Forsaken

Word count: 1168

[MUSIC: DARK ORCHESTRAL]

The seven warriors were all masters of the Spirit Supreme Realm, and from the looks of it, Max was the strongest of the bunch.

But if they were that powerful, what were they running from? What was really lurking in the darkness on the other side of this world?

Even Zane was curious.

Before Max could say anything, Delroy spoke up. “They are the Forsaken.”

“The Forsaken?” Zane echoed, a flicker of interest in his eyes.

Delroy nodded. “A race despised by the gods. The Shadow Creatures are made up of all sorts of different peoples, but the most powerful among them are the Forsaken.”

Max nodded. “Delroy’s right. The universe has a flip side. But compared to the chaotic fabric of this world, the space in the upper world is much more stable—harder to tear through. On that flip side, there's a vast, empty world. Shadow Creatures are rare here, but over there? There’s an insane number of them. And the Forsaken hold absolute rule over them all. They can’t leave that side, but they still have a massive influence across the entire universe.”

“A race despised by the gods… But why do the gods despise them?” Zane asked.

Max let out a short laugh and shook his head. “I have no idea. Maybe the World Lords and Supreme Lords know the answer to that.”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Zane nodded, but a deeply unsettling feeling crept over him, like something unseen was trying to worm its way into his body. Dread coiled in his gut as he asked, “So why would the Forsaken come to this world of chaos?”

Zane knew that feeling of crisis all too well. Ever since he’d fused with the world’s will, he had slowly started to become one with the entire world of chaos. Now, knowing that such a terrifying force existed on its flip side, he couldn’t help but feel its dread seeping into him.

“I don’t know, but I’ll report this to the Demi-Immortal Race. Whether they decide to investigate or not is out of my hands,” Max said, his gaze fixed on the path ahead.

The Forsaken were a mystery, and they were powerful. Even masters of the Spirit Supreme Realm couldn't get in their way. Max’s only mission right now was to find the Tower of Sin.

Zane nodded. Whatever these Shadow Creatures were planning, he wasn't strong enough to interfere. It was better to focus on what he came here to do.

Four hours. That was all it took. When Zane had crossed the Chaotic Ocean alone, it had taken him several days. Wade's blood had changed everything.

When the storm finally broke, a dark shape was rising slowly on the horizon. Zane stared at it.

The Heartlands. The eastern edge of the continent, just coming into view. After nearly half a year away, he was finally back.

[AMBIENT: SECT GATE — DISTANT VOICES]

Far below, at the easternmost sect of the Heartlands, disciples were training outside the gate when the Compass Wheel streaked overhead. A young warrior squinted at the sky.

“What — what was that?”

“An illusion,” his elder snapped. “Get back to your forms.”

“But I saw it too—“

“That speed — not even Zane could—“

“We all imagined the same thing at once, apparently.”

They shook their heads and went back to their training.

[MUSIC: BUILDING STRINGS]

Since Zane had killed Maddox, his name had spread across the Heartlands like wildfire. He had replaced Maddox as the number one warrior of the continent, and stories about him traveled from sect to sect, growing with each telling.

But the Heartlands hadn't been still while he was gone. The Ethereal Spirit Sect, devastated in the Corrupted Divine Kingdom's rampage, was being rebuilt — key figures had survived, and the foundations were intact enough to move quickly. 

Even so, it would take centuries for them to fully recover their standing. And Simon was stirring again. He fled when Maddox fell, but word had reached him that Zane was away — and now it seemed he was gathering the old remnants of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom, plotting something.

Zane could have known all of this in an instant. Since absorbing the world's will, any person, any place, was a single thought away. But he used the ability sparingly. Mostly, he used it to check on Tia.

[SFX: WIND RUSHING]

Oracle City appeared below them barely long enough after entering the Heartlands to burn an incense stick.

Max looked down from the Compass Wheel, expression unreadable. “Is it here?”

Zane nodded.

“Go quickly,” the young man in black said. “We'll wait.”

Zane cupped his hands. “Since you've come all this way to the Heartlands, why not—“

“No need,” Delroy said, not even bothering to open his eyes fully. “We're not interested in this kind of place.”

Max said nothing. The others settled into cross-legged silence, half-asleep.

Zane rolled his eyes. He'd just been being polite.

He turned to step off the wheel.

[SFX: SUDDEN FOOTSTEPS]

Suddenly, a sharp voice cut in. “And where do you think you’re going?”

“None of your business,” Dorothea snapped back, then grabbed Zane and jumped. 

[SFX: WHOOSH]

She’d clearly had enough of Delroy and the young man in black’s superior attitudes. She said to Zane, “Don’t pay any attention to them. They’re from the upper world—born with a chip on their shoulder.”

Zane just smiled. It didn't get to him.

He’d run into plenty of arrogant warriors since he’d started practicing martial arts. He’d seen them everywhere—in Castermere County, the Imperial City, the Skyreach Sect, and Cloud Hall. He was used to it. These six on The Compass Wheel might have been masters of the Spirit Supreme Realm, but their pride was just as common as any other fighter's.

[SFX: WHOOSH OF WIND]

[AMBIENT: OPEN SKY]

The two of them shot down from the sky, heading straight for the Heavenly Stellar Palace.

Drawing on the memories of the world itself, Zane knew Tia had been at the Heavenly Stellar Palace for the last six months. He was in such a rush to see her that he didn’t even bother to let the three leaders of the World Merchants' Guild know he was coming.

[SFX: MAGICAL RUNES ACTIVATING]

[AMBIENT: HEAVENLY STELLAR PALACE]

The second Zane’s feet touched down in the Heavenly Stellar Palace, hundreds of runes blazed to life all around them.

[SFX: ENERGY BEAMS FIRING]

Zane and Dorothea were nearly blinded as every rune fired off a brilliant beam of light, streaking right for them.

“Are you trying to get yourselves killed?!” a voice bellowed from the palace.

[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]

[SFX: MAGICAL POWER BUILDING]

Dorothea had no idea what was going on, but she was already getting pissed. The flower in her right eye began to spin.

The light beams themselves were nothing to her—she could handle that kind of power. 

But attacking her? That was a huge mistake—a mistake that could get a person hurt, or maybe dead.
