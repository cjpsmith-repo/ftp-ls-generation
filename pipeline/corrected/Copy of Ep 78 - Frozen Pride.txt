The sheer force of Zane's Shattered Dagger caught Randall completely off guard, slicing through the air with a whisper that belied its devastating power and giving Zane a critical advantage in their deadly dance. The blade's edge gleamed with an almost hungry light as it cut toward its target, leaving a trail of displaced air in its wake.
Randall's overconfidence had been his undoing; his prized icy shield, which could normally stop a Nature Spirit in its tracks and had earned him countless victories in past confrontations, shattered like brittle glass against Zane's attack, leaving him exposed and vulnerable.
[SFX: GLASS SHATTERING]
The sound of his defense crumbling echoed in his ears, a haunting reminder of his miscalculation.
Rage boiled in Randall's chest at the thought of losing so badly, his face flushing crimson as his fingers trembled with barely contained fury.
The humiliation burned hotter than any physical wound, scorching his pride and threatening to consume his rational thought. He had underestimated this upstart from Misty Peak, a mistake he vowed would not happen again.

[SFX: ICE FORMING]
[AMBIENT: FROZEN BATTLEFIELD]
Randall threw out an arm, channeling his Vitality until a sheet of gleaming ice coated the entire battlefield. The moment it solidified, he was on his feet, skating full-tilt toward Zane.
He wouldn't make the same mistake twice; he knew now to watch out for that bizarre weapon.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
Quick-witted as ever, Randall devised a counter. With a wave of his hands, he summoned five sharp Ice Spikes. They glittered in the air, trailing streaks of light as he poured his Vitality into them.
After several deep breaths, Randall glided past Zane with predatory grace, his eyes narrowed with calculated determination as he prepared to re-engage his opponent.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
As Randall closed the distance, his body coiled like a spring ready to unleash, Zane pivoted sharply on his heel and aimed with deadly precision. Smirking with newfound confidence, Randall summoned his power and unleashed five gleaming Ice Spikes that whistled through the air.
[SFX: VIOLENT IMPACT]
The crystalline projectiles collided violently with the Shattered Dagger, the impact reverberating through the frozen battlefield as they hammered the weapon off its intended path and drove it deep into the unyielding frozen ground.
"I've figured out your little trick, Zane!" Randall bellowed triumphantly, his voice echoing across the icy terrain as he surged forward to eliminate the remaining distance between them, savoring what he believed would be his moment of victory.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
A cold dread washed over Zane, seeping into his bones as he frantically wrestled with his embedded dagger, his fingers burning against the ice as the weapon remained stubbornly lodged in the frozen surface. His heart pounded in his chest as he sensed rather than saw Randall already upon him, an icy spear materializing in the man's hand, its lethal tip aimed directly for his exposed back.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT INTENSIFIES]
In that moment of desperation, clarity struck Zane like lightning, and a single thought crystallized in his mind: "The Psyche Spike!"
[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]
Zane was ready. He focused his soul strength, forming a gray spike of pure mental energy aimed at his enemy. In the same instant, the dagger ripped free from the ice, whipping around to fly straight at Randall.
[SFX: MENTAL ATTACK IMPACT]
Tunnel-visioned on the kill, Randall was blindsided by a stabbing pain that erupted in his skull, so intense it nearly sent him to his knees.
[SFX: BODY ROLLING ON ICE]
But years of battle had honed his instincts. Even as his mind screamed, they screamed danger. He twisted, bracing against the mental assault, and threw himself into a roll across the ice just as the Shattered Dagger sliced through the air where he'd been standing.
Blood still seeped from his injured shoulder.
"Ugh!" Randall gritted his teeth as a fresh wave of pain shot through him. Frustration was boiling over into pure rage. Zane was supposed to be an easy kill, and the fact that he was still standing was infuriating.
What good were decades of combat experience if they meant nothing against this kid?
[SFX: ROARING BATTLE CRY]
[AMBIENT: ICE CRACKING]
Randall let out a roar as his Vitality erupted, creating a storm of countless Ice Spikes that blasted out in every direction. The air cracked with the sound of splintering ice as another force intervened.
"Damn it, Jon! Stay out of this!"
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
"Stay out of it? I'm not just interfering, Randall, I'm enforcing the law! You broke it. You altered the Emerald Task Force's marching route. You led your own soldiers to their deaths at the hands of the Razorwing Queen. And on top of all that, you tortured Shane for information! If we hadn't gotten there when we did, he'd be dead! Is that what a soldier of the Imperial Army does, Randall? Is it?" Jon's voice was sharp, cutting through Randall's rage.
The Imperial Army had a strict code, and by any measure, Randall's crimes were treason—a capital offense.
But in the grand scheme of things, a few dead soldiers from the Emerald Task Force were just a rounding error to the brass. And Shane, the real victim, had no connections, no power to make a formal complaint stick.
That's why Randall thought he could get away with it—just lock Shane up and make the whole mess disappear.
"Everything you're saying might be true, Jon," Randall said, his voice suddenly, chillingly calm. "But you and I both know you can't beat me."
"You've got the skill to back up that talk, I'll give you that," Jon admitted.
A malicious smile contorted Randall's lips. "Then stand aside and allow me to conclude this matter. Friendship notwithstanding, I'll show no mercy."
"You might possess the muscle, Randall, but you've perpetually lacked intellectual fortitude," Jon retorted, a casual grin spreading across his features as though they were merely exchanging playful insults in their quarters.
[SFX: ICE CRACKING]
At that precise moment, an intricate network of fissures began to spread across Zane's icy prison. The crystalline walls trembled, hairline fractures multiplying with alarming speed.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE ICE SHATTERING]
The magnificent edifice of Frostbite Valley suddenly erupted with a deafening crack. What had been an impenetrable fortress of ice now disintegrated into countless glittering particles, swirling through the air like diamond dust.
As the frozen barrier dissolved, it revealed Zane standing tall and unscathed, his eyes blazing with cold fury. Not a single mark marred his body where the crushing ice should have left him broken.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
[AMBIENT: FRIGID ATMOSPHERE]
The temperature in the clearing seemed to drop further as Zane took one deliberate step forward, the ground beneath his foot crystallizing with each movement. His voice, when it finally came, carried across the battlefield with unnatural clarity: "My turn."
