Ep 183 - Bob the Bully
Word count: 1022

[SFX: WATER SPLASHING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]
 Once Zane figured out how to use vibrations to boost his speed, the rest of the Gillwretches were easy pickings.
Zipping back and forth a few more times, he took out the last five of them.
[SFX: BLADE SLIDING]
 The flying blade slid back into his grasp. 
[SFX: WATER SPLASH]
 Zane floated in the quiet water, his gaze fixed on the depths. Then, without a second thought, he plunged back down.
It was as if he’d completely forgotten his time was running out.
[AMBIENT: LAKESIDE ATMOSPHERE]
 "He killed all six of them... the yellow Gillwretches," the steward mumbled, his face pale with shock. He was sitting on a stone bench by the shore of Emberfin Lake.
Leo, on the other hand, was completely unfazed.
He’d been training nonstop since returning from the south. Feeling stronger and more skilled, he’d come to Emberfin Lake to push his own limits.
Even at his best, he could only dive about seventy meters. He knew the lakebed was crawling with Gillwretches—far too many for him to handle at once.
But Zane, on his very first try, had gone deeper than Leo ever had. And he’d killed six yellow Gillwretches. Leo didn't know what to make of it. The only word that came to mind was "extraordinary".
He wasn’t frustrated at all, even though Zane was clearly rattling some of the other disciples. They were good friends, and Zane still owed him a favor from their time in the south.
Seeing how fast Zane was improving, Leo was sure his friend was on the path to becoming incredibly powerful. He remembered the promise Zane had made, and figured he’d be there to help the Brandt family when the time came.
The better Zane performed, the higher Leo’s expectations grew.
[AMBIENT: UNDERWATER PRESSURE]
Just then, a group of people walked up. Leo glanced at the Magic Array Plate and saw that Zane was still underwater. He’d encountered a few more Gillwretches before diving past ninety meters. Did this kid even have a limit? The pressure at that depth was insane.
If Zane weren't so special, the intense pressure would have crushed him into pulp.
Even a seasoned Adept would struggle with the crushing force ninety meters down, but Zane looked like he was trying to go even deeper.
Leo was dying to see just how far he could go. "Maybe he's just testing his limits!" he exclaimed.
"But his time is up, and there are others waiting," Steward Carter replied nervously.
As a steward for the Skyreach Sect, Carter had a sharp eye for talent. He knew Zane had serious potential. His performance at Emberfin Lake was staggering—the kind of thing you rarely saw, even among the Sect’s top prospects. With more time, Zane would only get better. He was a rising star, someone to watch. Carter knew he'd probably have to kiss up to him sooner or later.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Steward Carter hesitated, acutely aware of which disciple demanded his attention. The young man named Bob wasn't someone to be trifled with—disciples from Great Houses rarely acknowledged those they considered to be inferior, especially ones with differing opinions.
Emberfin Lake was no stranger to Bob's volatile outbursts. His previous disputes had often spiraled into heated confrontations, giving him quite a reputation. His rotten temper flared up at the slightest provocation.
The fact that he was this annoyed after waiting only fifteen minutes said everything you needed to know about his character, a testament to his entitled upbringing and fragile ego.
The atmosphere thickened with tension as Bob's expression darkened, his lips twisting into a nasty sneer. He was used to other people waiting on him, not the other way around. Indignation radiated from his rigid posture as he looked around the room with disgust.
[AMBIENT: TENSE WAITING ROOM]
"Is that disciple out yet? Carter, you said he’d be out by four, didn't you? It's four now," Bob said, his voice sharp with irritation.
Steward Carter straightened his spine, forcing his features into a placating smile. "Please, just another minute," he implored, his voice betraying his nerves. "He should be out any second now."
[SFX: CHAIR SCRAPING]
The harsh scrape of chair legs against stone echoed through the chamber as Bob shot to his feet, looming over the steward with undisguised contempt. "I'm done waiting. His time is up. Force him out with the Magic Array Plate. Now."
"I, uh…" Steward Carter stammered, his mouth hanging open. He didn't know what to say. Bob had put him in a terrible spot. If he used the array to force Zane out, he’d make an enemy of a future powerhouse. But if he refused Bob, he'd have an immediate problem.
Angering Zane, who was just an outer disciple for now, wouldn't have immediate consequences for Carter's career. But pissing off Bob? If Bob complained to his family elders, he could make Carter’s life at the Skyreach Sect a living hell.
After a moment's calculation, Steward Carter made his choice. But just as he moved to act, Leo cut in.
"My two hours are up, but I have enough points to pay for twenty more. Just keep deducting from my total until Zane comes out," Leo said, his expression hardening.
He was usually polite and easygoing, but when pushed, he had a surprisingly firm backbone. Clearly Leo wasn't a fan of Bob's bullish behavior.
Steward Carter was stuck between a rock and a hard place, completely at a loss. He wanted to activate the array, but one look at Leo’s face told him that would be a mistake. At the same time, there was no reasoning with Bob; he knew all about his explosive temper.
[SFX: ENERGY PULSING]
 "They say the Brandt family is good at reading a room, but you don't seem to have a clue," Bob sneered, glaring at Leo with pure hatred. A powerful energy began to radiate from him as he spoke. He'd already reached the peak of the Adept Realm, just one step shy of the Soul Realm.
[MUSIC: CONFRONTATION THEME]
The second he heard that, Leo stood up, his own eyes turning dark and dangerous. "Are you really trying to start a fight with me on Sect's grounds? Do you have any idea what the penalty is for that?"
