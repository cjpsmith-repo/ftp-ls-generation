Ep 257 - Breaking the Laws of Nature
Word Count: 1123
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
Initially, Richard had assumed he was in a completely different league than Zane. His confidence, however, was shattered when Zane suddenly erupted with a powerful surge of Vitality, forcing him to take this fight seriously.
Though Zane’s physical strength had caught him off guard, Richard had dismissively categorized his opponent as nothing more than a mindless brute—all brawn and no finesse. This led him to believe that Zane posed no legitimate threat.
Even when faced with Zane’s tough-as-nails defense, Richard had maintained his composure, certain of his strategy. A smug smile had played across his lips at the thought—as long as Zane failed to breach his meticulously crafted Hymn of Warding, victory was pretty much guaranteed.
[SFX: SURGING ENERGY]
Now, staring at the tendrils of raw Vitality coiling around Zane, Richard felt a knot of dread tighten in his stomach. The palpable energy pulsing before him shattered his earlier confidence. This wasn’t going to be easy, not by a long shot."Why now?" he pondered, his mind racing through Zane’s previous matches. The calculated restraint seemed deliberate, almost cunning. Why hadn’t Zane unleashed this kind of power before this moment?
There were only two possibilities. First, Zane’s Vitality manifestation was nothing more than an elaborate bluff—a desperate attempt to intimidate when his only real asset was that freakish, superhuman strength of his.
The second possibility was much scarier: Zane was far more dangerous than anyone imagined, and he’d deliberately concealed his true capabilities throughout earlier contests, strategically holding back his most devastating abilities until absolutely necessary.
The implications chilled Richard to his core. If the latter proved true, he stood before an opponent who not only possessed overwhelming strength but also the cunning to orchestrate his rise through the ranks with calculated precision. His fingers tightened around his Runic Brush as beads of cold sweat formed along his hairline.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
Based on what he was seeing now, the first option was a joke. A body and physical power like Zane’s couldn’t possibly be backed by weak Vitality. Besides, the two swirling vortexes of dark purple Vitality were undeniable proof of his power.
This power is insane! Richard's mind reeled.
[SFX: RAPID BRUSH STROKES]
Even as the thought hit him, Richard was already retreating, his brush pen a blur in his hand. A stream of ’Kill’ sigils shot from the tip. This was going to be a long fight.
In that moment, Richard completely forgot Zane was only a Level Two Adept. He was fighting him as if he were the most formidable opponent he’d ever faced in the ring.
The ’Kill’ sigils kept coming, one after another.
They flowed from his brush, weaving themselves into a terrifying formation as they hurtled toward Zane.
It was the Array of Thirteen Kills.
The Array of Thirteen Kills was Richard’s ultimate move, the most powerful attack in his arsenal. He'd never planned on using it against Zane. He was saving it for the personal disciples of the high-ranking elders.
As the top student of Blackrock Peak, the second-ranked peak in the Skyreach Sect, Richard had his sights set on first place in the All Peaks Competition. The elders’ personal disciples were a cut above the other students from the Thirty-Three Peaks, and Richard had earmarked this move for them.
But now, he realized he had no choice but to unleash it on Zane. Otherwise, he wouldn’t stand a chance.
Richard could have just admitted defeat. With his undefeated record, he could afford to lose a round and still advance. But Richard was no coward. He had too much pride for that. Besides, his opponent was only a Level Two Adept. If he was truly outmatched, he’d rather lose with honor than forfeit the match. Quitting wasn’t in his nature.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
When the Array of Thirteen Kills materialized, an overwhelming wave of murderous pressure flooded the arena, transforming the atmosphere into something palpable and sinister. Zane felt as though he’d been violently thrust into the deepest circles of hell, each breath becoming a struggle against the crushing weight of Richard’s technique.
The Battle Referee, a veteran who had witnessed countless deadly confrontations, went deathly pale and scrambled out of the ring with undisguised terror etched across his face. Throughout the stands, spectators leaned forward with bated breath, their eyes widening in a mixture of horror and fascination. Veterans among them recognized the legendary array, while novices simply felt its oppressive aura bearing down upon them like an invisible mountain.
[SFX: SURGING ENERGY]
Zane stood his ground, watching as six Shadow Phantoms — dark silhouettes of himself, each crackling with purple-black Vitality — surged toward the array.
[SFX: ENERGY TRANSFORMATION]
When the phantom made contact with the first sigil, it instantly disintegrated into particles of purple-black mist. Yet to everyone’s astonishment, these fragments didn’t dissipate but instead coalesced, reforming the Shadow Phantom completely unharmed. The duplicate continued its relentless march forward, destroying and regenerating itself with each barrier it encountered, defying what every cultivator present knew to be impossible.
"This is insane!" The words escaped Richard’s lips before he could stop them, his composure shattering.
He took stock. One phantom down — but it had cracked three of his Kill glyphs with it. Ten remained. He started writing, trying to replenish his glyphs before the next phantom hit.
Then the shattered phantom pulled itself back together.
Richard's brush stopped mid-stroke.
His Vitality reserves were deeper than Zane's — they had to be, Soul Realm against Adept. He could outlast him. That had been the plan. But how do you outlast something that doesn't stay dead?
For any normal warrior, manifesting Vitality was a one-way street. Once his sigils were shattered, for instance, their energy was meant to break down and revert to Primordial Vitality, dissipating back into the environment.
That was the fundamental law of Vitality.
During training, warriors absorbed Primordial Vitality, converted it into their own, and then released it upon use. It was a cycle—a borrowing of power from nature itself.
And nature always reclaimed its own. If it didn’t, the world's reserves of Primordial Vitality would have been exhausted centuries ago, given how many people draw upon it.
No one could stop that cycle. It was an absolute.
But what had he just witnessed?
When his Array of Thirteen Kills had destroyed Zane’s Shadow Phantom, the phantom’s energy should have broken down and vanished. That was the law.
[SFX: ENERGY REFORMING]
But, to Richard’s utter astonishment, the dissipating energy had just... pulled itself back together, reforming the Shadow Phantom right before his eyes.
It defied the very laws of nature, spitting in the face of everything he’d ever learned.
And Richard wasn’t the only one who saw it.
Sir Kenneth, who knew a thing or two about the Titan Strike, wasn’t nearly as surprised. Though he couldn’t practice it himself, he knew how incredible the cultivation was and had seen it in action before. Master Young, however, was a different story. He was utterly speechless, his jaw hanging slack. He'd only heard legends of the Titan Strike; he’d never imagined it could be this powerful.
[SFX: CROWD ROARING]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT]
Meanwhile, the students who knew next to nothing about the finer points of Vitality were just losing their minds. The stands erupted in a roar of cheers and frantic chatter.
