Ep 666 - The Roar

Word count: 1180

[AMBIENT: DEEP CAVE RUMBLE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

A knot of ice formed in everyone’s stomach. The thought was immediate and absolute: they didn't stand a chance.

Yolande yelled, her voice cutting through the stunned silence as she whipped her own head away from the colossal creature. “Turn around! Don’t look at it! Cover your ears!”

The others scrambled to obey, spinning around and clamping their hands tight over their ears. There was no point in even thinking about fighting back.

[SFX: MONSTROUS ROAR]

[AMBIENT: VIOLENT CAVE TREMOR]

[SFX: ROCKS CRASHING]

[SFX: ENERGY BEAM BLAST]

The Celestial Root threw its head back and let out a roar that was less a sound and more a physical force. The entire Dark Nether Cave shuddered violently, and jagged spears of rock broke free from the ceiling, raining down around them. One, a monster over two hundred feet long, came hurtling straight for the group. Yolande’s eyes narrowed. On pure instinct, she threw up a hand, and a beam of light shot from her fingertip, blasting the falling rock into a shower of dust and gravel.

The cave continued to shake as the roar echoed, an assault that lasted five long minutes before silence crashed back in.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Yolande turned to the others, her voice a tense whisper. “The Mad Scorpion is awake. Get ready.”

Together, they risked a look at the Mad Scorpion. Sure enough, its eyes were open.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[AMBIENT: PULSATING HUM]

Waking from a sleep that had likely spanned ages, the scorpion’s gaze was still unfocused. It raised a massive pincer and absently tapped the top of its head. As it did, the Dark Nether Death Light pulsating above it flared, growing several times stronger. A blinding scarlet light flooded the cavern, shining with the intensity of a sun.

“The fog won’t stop a light that strong,” Esther murmured, her head lowered. “If there were any other warriors left down here… they’re probably dead now.”

Most had already retreated to the Tower of Sin ahead of the Tide of Chaos. Only a few desperate souls had stayed behind, hoping to scavenge for treasure in the ensuing mayhem. They had gambled with their lives, and it seemed they had just lost.

[SFX: CREATURE MOVEMENT]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

The Mad Scorpion’s enormous head swiveled slowly, its eyes finally focusing on the Celestial Root. It opened its cavernous mouth and answered with a roar of its own.

[SFX: MONSTROUS ROAR]

[MUSIC: URGENT PERCUSSION]

Fear flashed across Yolande’s face. “Zane, quick!” she screamed at him. “Make a deep pit! It sounds like they’re going to tear this whole place apart!”

[SFX: RUSHING WIND]

She'd barely finished speaking when a hurricane took shape — visible to the naked eye, a wall of churning air born from the scorpion's roar. The abyss was vast enough to buy them a few seconds before it hit. They had to use every one of them.

[AMBIENT: DEEP RUMBLING]

[SFX: FRANTIC DIGGING]

Zane threw everything he had into the ground — Dragon Scale power surging through his fists as he hammered the earth. Malcolm drove in alongside him, hitting just as hard, just as fast. He had no intention of being noble about it. One step outside the dome meant the Death Light would kill him. That was all the motivation he needed.

In seconds, they had blasted a crater wide enough for everyone. The group dropped in together.

Then the wind hit.

[SFX: WIND BLAST]

The roar above them was deafening even from below. The crater's edges began shaving off layer by layer, the walls collapsing inward as the hurricane stripped them away. Timothy was a half-second slow raising his protective energy — the wind found him, and it cut. He came up bloody.

"Deeper!" Malcolm shouted and punched downward again.

Three times they dug. Three times, the wind caught up. Each round drove them further down, the debris from above half-burying them as they went.

[SFX: ROCKS CRUMBLING]

Finally, two thousand feet down, the wind above them thinned to something survivable. The roaring continued — the scorpion and the Celestial Root trading blows that shook the entire cave — but down here, it was just barely endurable.

[AMBIENT: TOWER OF SIN — TENTH FLOOR]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLY STRINGS]

Above ground, the Tower of Sin was shaking.

On the tenth floor, a young Demon Night warrior named Phoebe had just stepped away from the Talent Tablet. Her result — third-floor ranking — was the best the Demon Night had achieved in recent memory. It should have felt like a victory.

It didn't.

She'd come to the Tower to challenge Zane. That had been the plan. But the moment she'd seen his name on the Talent Tablet — golden, towering above everything else — the plan had collapsed. She didn't have the qualifications to consider him a rival. Not yet.

She clenched her weapon and turned back toward the arena. She would work ten times harder. One day.

[SFX: RUMBLE]

The floor shook beneath her feet.

[SFX: ROAR]

Then the roar came — from underground, from somewhere impossibly deep. Every race in the Tower froze.

"What is that?"

"Something's roaring under the Cursed Land—"

"Zane went into the Dark Nether Cave. So did Esther Sterling. Neither of them came back—"

"They're dead. Nobody survives something that sounds like that."

It wasn’t just the Tower. People all over the Cursed Land heard the apocalyptic roar. Two hours later, the sound had spread across the entire main continent.

[AMBIENT: SACRED FOREST]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

In the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, a woman named Amber stood clad in purple at the very top of a colossal holy tree, her sharp ears already on alert.

[SFX: EARTH RUMBLING]

[SFX: TREE CREAKING]

As the violent sound rumbled up from deep within the earth, the holy tree of the Demon Night began to tremble beneath her feet. The ancient tree had a soul, and it was now projecting its raw, primal fear directly into her mind. In that moment, every living being in the sacred grove was gripped by a terror that felt like the end of the world.

“Don’t be afraid. It’s okay,” Amber said softly, gently stroking the bark to soothe the tree’s quaking heart.

And it wasn't just the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary. From the Violet Heart Sanctuary of the humans to the Flaming and Dread Places of the Ogres, from the Sanctum of Eternal Night of the Twilight Group to the farthest corners of the land, all living creatures were seized by the same profound, instinctual dread.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[SFX: WIND DYING DOWN]

After what felt like an eternity, the unholy roar finally began to fade. The raging wind above them slowly died down.

Once they were sure the coast was clear, they started pushing aside the rocks and loose soil, slowly climbing their way out of the darkness and back to the surface.

Even two thousand feet down, with rock and soil packed above them on every side, the roar still found them. Some sounds couldn't be buried.
