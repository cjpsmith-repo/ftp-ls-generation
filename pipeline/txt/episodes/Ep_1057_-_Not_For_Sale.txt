Ep 1057 - Not For Sale

Word count: 1009

[AMBIENT: AUCTION HALL]

[SFX: CROWD GASP]

The crowd gasped at the price the short warrior was offering. A single Sovereign Stone was worth ten thousand times more than an Aether crystal.

At that rate, three hundred thousand Sovereign Stones were the equivalent of three billion supreme Aether crystals. Just thinking about that kind of fortune was enough to make your head spin.

The Thundergust was a genuine Grade-One Divine Weapon, sure, but three hundred thousand Sovereign Stones was still a staggering offer.

Still, the warriors knew the bidder was good for it.

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

“Caleb’s family controls eighty percent of the mines across dozens of supreme worlds. He can definitely afford it!”

“But if that sword was mine, I wouldn’t sell it for any price. You can always earn more Stones, but finding a Grade-One Divine Weapon is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity! Besides, a weapon like that could save your ass in a real fight!”

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Caleb Garrett ignored the chatter, his eyes fixed on Zane. “So, what do you say? It’s a fair price, isn’t it?”

But Zane just shook his head. “Sorry. It’s not about the money. The sword isn’t for sale.”

Caleb was stuck. He couldn’t force the issue, not when he’d seen Zane take down Jared—a warrior far more skilled than himself.

[AMBIENT: TENSE COURTYARD]

Done wasting his time with the warriors from Groundstone Hall, Zane turned to shut the door.

[SFX: STONE ROLLING ON FLOOR]

Just as he was pulling it closed, a single Sovereign Stone was tossed through the gap, rolling to a stop near his feet.

[MUSIC: THREATENING TENSION]

A new voice called out from the crowd.

A messenger stepped forward. “Brock of Cloudswallow Hall offers one Sovereign Stone for the sword. Give it to him, and he’ll guarantee your safety in The Mist Palace. If you refuse… well, your life here might get complicated.”

Zane walked over and stepped on it.

[SFX: SHARP CRACK]

The Sovereign Stone shattered underfoot, a burst of compressed energy exploding outward.

The messenger’s face went cold. He didn’t dare force the door. But he burned a Message Rune on the spot.

[AMBIENT: QUIET MANOR INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]

Zane closed the door and leaned against it for a moment, shaking his head. The Mist Palace was chaos. Back at the Soulbound Sect, people at least didn’t openly rob each other in the settlement. Here, nobody seemed to care. He’d have to ask Yolande about it when he had the chance.

He sat cross-legged on the floor, waiting.

Lavender slipped out of him and settled beside him, hugging her knees.

“Why’d you come out?” Zane asked.

“I want to see how much your strength has grown,” she said with a smile.

“They might not even come,” Zane said.

“They’ll definitely come,” Lavender said.

The Thundergust rested in his hands, and Lavender watched as his gaze grew sharper with every passing second.

[AMBIENT: TENSE COURTYARD]

Most of the watching warriors outside had started to drift away. They settled back in when they heard the name — someone from Cloudswallow Hall was on the way. This was going to be worth watching.

[MUSIC: RISING ANGER]

The sheer audacity made Zane’s blood boil.

What was wrong with these people? He’d gotten this sword from The Hall of Arms. It was his, fair and square. But these greedy bastards were too lazy to get one for themselves. Now that he had it, they were all crawling out of the woodwork to try and take it.

He’d sent Jared flying with a single punch, hoping it would scare these guys off. He never figured they’d get even more aggressive about the sword.

And now, warriors from Cloudswallow Hall were getting in on it. He knew there was a huge power gap between the two factions—Groundstone Hall was nothing compared to Cloudswallow Hall—but Zane didn’t care.

Before long, Brock himself arrived. Delicate skin, hair combed meticulously back, something almost graceful in the way he carried himself. He stepped forward and smiled. “I’ll give you one more chance. One Sovereign Stone. Sell me the sword, and I’ll guarantee your safety.”

He took another step forward. “I am a collector of swords. I treat them as friends. A weapon like the Thundergust deserves a proper home — someone whose strength matches it. With your cultivation level, this sword will never reach its potential. It will be wasted with you. Sell it to me and give it a good home.”

[AMBIENT: TENSE COURTYARD]

Zane could have argued, but he knew it would be a waste of breath. It seemed the only way to earn respect in The Mist Palace was to fight. Looked like he’d have to handle this the same way he did back in the Lower World.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Taking Zane’s silence as a “no,” Brock spoke through the door. “Don’t be a fool. If I have to take this from you, you’ll be lucky to walk away with all your limbs.”

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

With that, Brock started toward the manor’s entrance, clearly planning to just let himself in.

Everyone knew Zane wasn’t going to hand the weapon over willingly. Brock, on the other hand, was a powerful warrior from Cloudswallow Hall. A collective sigh went through the crowd. They all knew Zane was probably about to get torn apart.

Just as Brock stepped within fifty feet of the manor’s entrance, Zane’s arm twitched.

[SFX: SWORD HUM]

[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]

The sword in his hands hummed, and the power of wind and thunder merged with his sword intent, sending a faint, invisible wave pulsing from the blade.

He hadn’t tested the sword’s power yet. If Brock was going to push this, he might as well be useful.

He held back, not wanting to kill anyone. He was new here and didn’t know the rules of The Mist Palace yet. All he wanted was to teach Brock a lesson.

[SFX: ENERGY WAVE WHOOSH]

But even Zane was startled by the raw power of the invisible wave he’d just unleashed.
