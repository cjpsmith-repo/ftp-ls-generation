Power surged through Zane's veins. He could feel the legendary beast's blood mixing with his own, a sudden rush of strength flushing his skin. It felt like it was stretched taut, struggling to contain the energy about to burst from inside him.

It was a long time before the deep flush faded from his skin.

"Whew," Zane let out a slow breath. A deep warmth radiated from his core, and it felt like every pore on his skin had opened.

With a drop of true dragon's blood now in his veins, Zane was one of the Dragon-blooded.

[AMBIENT: TRAINING CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STRINGS]

When he walked out of the training chamber, the old man was waiting for him. The elder’s wise, ancient eyes seemed to look right into Zane's soul. After a long moment, a calm smile spread across his face. "You look much better," he said. He took a step back. "You are now one of the Dragon-blooded. Come with me."

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR MONASTERY]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE FLUTE]

Zane followed him past the building, his eyes drawn to a series of obelisks in the distance. Countless practitioners sat before the massive stone pillars, their faces tight with concentration, as if trying to solve an impossible puzzle.

[SFX: MYSTICAL HUM]

Even from this distance, Zane could feel a mysterious energy radiating from the obelisks.

"Those obelisks—" Zane began, but the old man cut him off, seeming to know exactly what he was about to ask.

"Those are the Obelisks of Souls," he said. "They hold the power of the various Original Laws. By meditating on them, you can understand those Laws on a much deeper level."

[MUSIC: AWE-INSPIRING CHOIR]

Zane’s breath caught as he stared up, craning his neck to take in the sheer size of the obelisks towering over them.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: AWE-INSPIRING ORCHESTRAL]

On Cloud Road, he’d found a broken stone carving no bigger than his palm. But here, dozens of obelisks stood before him, each a thousand times larger than that tiny fragment, and every one of them was perfectly intact.

"To understand the Old Laws, you must place your soul upon one of the Obelisks. It will grant you a deeper connection with your spiritual energy.But a word of warning." H paused, his eyes locking onto Zane’s with sudden intensity. "Do not be too ambitious. If you push too far, you could permanently damage your soul. No short-term gain is worth that price."

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL HUMMING]

Zane nodded, the weight of the warning settling in. He walked over to the field of obelisks and began to circle them, examining each one. The huge pillars soared hundreds of feet into the air, each one shrouded in a mysterious gray haze.

Zane marveled at them. No two were alike. 

[SFX: CRACKLING FLAMES]

One radiated a scorching heat, and as he got closer, he recognized the symbols carved into its surface: the script of the Fire Law. 

[SFX: ICY WIND]

As he moved on to the next one, the heat gave way to a chilling mist that drifted from the stone. This must be the Ice Law, Zane thought.

His heart pounded as he continued to circle them. 

[SFX: SPATIAL DISTORTION]

He stopped before one that radiated a subtle, warping energy—the Spatial Law—and watched the faint, shimmering forms of other souls clinging to its surface. Some were motionless, their focus locked on the inscriptions.

I get it, Zane realized with a flash of insight. He decided then that he wouldn't rush his climb. He would take his time, studying the obelisk's intricate lines until he understood them.

[MUSIC: MEDITATIVE AMBIENT]

Zane settled before the obelisk, crossing his legs and letting his breathing slow. He cleared his mind of all distractions. In that moment, nothing existed but his soul and the intricate lines etched into the pillar's surface.

The patterns seemed to pulse with ancient wisdom, each curve and angle whispering secrets of space itself. Zane felt a strange peace settle over him.

[SFX: ETHEREAL VOICE ECHO]

The Cobalt Dragon's voice echoed through his consciousness. This boy... His capacity for understanding is truly remarkable.

The ancient dragon rarely gave out such praise. In all his millennia of existence, he had watched countless cultivators rush headlong toward power, their impatience blinding them to deeper truths. Yet here sat Zane—young, driven by vengeance, burdened by impossible odds—choosing patience over haste.

Most would have grabbed what they could and fled, the dragon mused, a note of approval warming his ancient voice. But he knows that true strength cannot be rushed. Perhaps there is hope for him yet.

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]

The principles of the Spatial Law flooded Zane's mind as he focused on the mysterious lines, deciphering the endless stream of words and symbols.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Five minutes passed. Then ten. Then fifteen.

Zane’s soul remained motionless on the obelisk, absorbing the law woven into the stone. After what felt like an eternity, he sensed the repulsive force pressing against him begin to fade.

[SFX: MAGICAL RESONANCE]

Excitement surged through him, electric and validating. I was right! The better you understand the Law, the higher your soul can climb. His instinct had proven correct—this wasn't merely a test of raw power, but of comprehension.

Zane continued his meditation, letting the ancient knowledge seep into his consciousness like water into parched earth. By the time he had absorbed nearly all the foundational principles of Spatial Law, the obelisk's repulsive force had diminished to almost nothing. What had felt like an invisible hand shoving him away now barely registered as a whisper of resistance.

He opened his awareness fully, testing the change. The difference was staggering.

His mind was consumed with only one thought: Now!

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL RISE]

Along the way, he shot past the souls of countless other practitioners clinging to the obelisk's face.

One of them, a master at the Inner-Core Realm, was struggling against the repulsive force with his own considerable understanding of the Spatial Law. 

[SFX: SURPRISED GASP]

[MUSIC: FAST-PACED ADVENTURE]
