Ep 792 - There Is Only Ever One

Word count: 1039

The crowd was still buzzing as Zane walked away from the red circle, the cracks in the floor behind him the only evidence of what had just happened.

Zane couldn't have cared less about the internal politics of the divine kingdom. He just wanted the Harlen Token.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING COURTYARD]

 After the test, all the successful recruits were herded back to the courtyard where they’d first registered. Now that he’d passed, Zane was officially a member of the Crystal Martial House, for all the good it would do him.

The woman at the registration desk, an Inner-Core warrior herself, gave Zane a long, searching look. She clearly couldn't figure out how someone at his level—the middle stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm—had managed to pass the trial.

She took his wooden entry token and swapped it for a purple one, which still wasn’t the Harlen Token he was after. “With this,” she explained, “you can enter the Crystal Martial House to study and train. You’ll also have access to some of our resources.”

Zane knew most freelance warriors would have been ecstatic. Going it alone was a brutal grind, and joining a martial house was a golden opportunity to get stronger. But he had no interest in their training or their resources. He didn't need their techniques or their pills; he’d gotten this far on his own, and the house had nothing he truly wanted. Nothing except the Harlen Token.

[AMBIENT: QUIET STREET]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

 Leaving the Crystal Martial House behind, Zane found Francis waiting for him outside, right where he’d been told to stay. Francis wouldn’t have dared to disobey.

As they walked, Zane idly flipped the purple token in his hand. “How long will it take to get the Harlen Token and get into the Sacred Feather Sea?” he asked after a moment.

[AMBIENT: QUIET CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

 Francis offered a tentative smile. “One of the three secret lands opens each year, but there’s no telling which one is on the schedule for this one.”

The Sacred Feather Sea was home to several hidden realms: the Feather Emperor’s Secret Realm, the Divine Emperor’s Secret Realm, the Mysterious Dream Secret Realm, and the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. The only one Zane cared about was the Feather Emperor’s. He was banking on it opening this year; any other outcome would be a massive setback.

“Is there any way to find out which one is opening?” Zane pressed, his brow tightening.

“I suppose there might be a way,” Francis replied carefully, “but it’s the rulers of the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms who decide which land opens. It’s entirely their call. I have no idea if you can just ask them, but I imagine that kind of information isn’t easy to come by.”

[MUSIC: TENSE RISING TENSION]

 Zane’s jaw clenched. If the Feather Emperor’s Secret Realm didn’t open this year, he’d have to wait another full year. He didn’t have another year to waste. He was running out of time, and he was nowhere near powerful enough to influence the rulers of the entire Union. What was he supposed to do?

“I see,” Zane said, his voice flat. “You can go now.”

He watched Francis depart, his mind racing. The Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms had once been considered a Sixth-Grade sect, but now they were an Eighth-Grade power—a staggering leap of two entire grades. Even the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary on the main continent, led by Amber, was only a seventh-grade sect. He was completely outmatched.

He sat cross-legged and let his breathing settle, spreading his Vitality slowly outward as he prepared to cultivate. The Cobalt Dragon rarely spoke unprompted. In all the time since the main continent, it had almost never given specific advice or asked him for anything directly. When it did speak, it mattered.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]

Frustrated, Zane turned his focus inward. So, what exactly is in the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land? he asked, his curiosity finally getting the better of him. He wondered what the Cobalt Dragon was after and why it was being so cryptic about it.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ECHO]

[MUSIC: ANCIENT DRAGON THEME]

 The Cobalt Dragon's voice echoed in his mind, quiet and measured.

The Sacred Feather Sea was never theirs.

Zane opened his eyes slightly.

The Cobalt Dragon continued. It was Dragon-blooded territory. The heart of their civilization. For tens of thousands of years, seven human kingdoms threw everything they had at them and couldn't break them. In the end, the Dragon-blooded did something desperate — a blood sacrifice to tear open a passage between worlds. And through that passage, they called for help.

The image came to Zane unbidden: The Soulforge, the nine dragons etched into its surface. Different sizes, different powers. And beneath them all, the tenth — the largest, the most intimidating, still dormant.

Our eldest brother answered that call.

Zane went still.

With a single breath, he destroyed three kingdoms. But the sacrifice had cost the Dragon-blooded everything. The four kingdoms that survived had backing from the Upper Realm. They regrouped. And when it was over, the Dragon-blooded were gone.

The Red Dragon's voice joined in then, dry and unhurried. Before the last of them fell, they used the lives of their entire race to seal the Sacred Feather Sea. Every wall you saw. Every restriction. That's what those are. A civilization's dying act, locking their inheritance away so the kingdoms could never fully claim it.

They've been picking at the seal for thousands of years, the Cobalt Dragon continued, and something in its tone sharpened. Those seven kingdoms were sixth-rank sects once. Now four remain, and they are eighth-rank powers. Two full grades. All of it fed by an inheritance that was never theirs.

"And the Ancestral Dragon Egg," Zane said slowly. "That's what your brother came for."

It's still in there, the Cobalt Dragon said. In the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land. Waiting.

"What is it exactly?"

The Red Dragon answered. There is only one in existence. When the Ancestral Dragon dies, the egg appears somewhere in the cosmos. It hatches. A new Ancestral Dragon is born. Another pause. There is only ever one.

Zane stared into the darkness behind his closed eyes, letting that settle.
