Ep 310 - Return Home For Revenge
Word count: 1140
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
Years of imprisonment on Infernal Peak had severed Elara from her family, from almost the entire outside world. She never imagined her reunion with her brother, Zane, would begin with the news of their father’s death.
Grief hit her like a physical blow, but a burning need for vengeance quickly followed, settling deep in her bones.
But her rage didn’t erupt in a scream. It was a quiet, chilling thing.
Tears welled in her eyes, rimming them red, but they couldn’t diminish her fierce grace. To Zane, she looked like a cornered leopard—small, wounded, and coiled to strike.
[SFX: VITALITY CRACKLING]
The Vitality surrounding Elara crackled visibly, twisting and condensing into needle-sharp spines that hovered in the air like crystallized fury.
Unlike conventional cultivation techniques, Elara’s cultivation method possessed a rare intimacy with her consciousness—her emotions and thoughts directly shaped the Vitality, transforming invisible energy into physical manifestations of her inner turmoil.
Her head whipped toward Master Elwood, eyes narrowed with cold determination. In that heartbeat, every gleaming spike—dozens of them—pivoted in perfect unison, their lethal points aimed directly at the elder’s heart. The air between them hummed with tension, the spines vibrating slightly as if they were eager to be unleashed.
[MUSIC: HIGH TENSION]
The sudden move startled Master Elwood, though not for his own safety.
His power was far beyond hers. What worried him was the grief threatening to shatter her. After all, she was still so young, even younger than Zane.
Elara had already pieced together why she was really on Infernal Peak. She'd made her decision long ago: if that lord ever actually gathered what he needed, she'd end her own life before letting him take anything from her.
"Master Elwood, I need to leave Infernal Peak for a few days," Elara said, her voice eerily calm.

Master Elwood hesitated. Letting her leave was against protocol. The Skyreach Sect had her imprisoned here under the pretense of "training," and if anything happened to her on the outside, the Sect’s reputation would take the hit.
"The Sect Leader already gave her permission, didn’t he?" Zane reminded him. "He said my sister could leave Infernal Peak for a little while."
Master Elwood looked at Elara. "And what do you intend to do out there?"
She didn't hesitate. "Go home and kill people."
He nodded slowly. He could hardly argue with that.
Elara trusted Zane, but it was still hard to wrap her head around. Her brother, only at Second Level of the Adept Realm, had won the All Peaks Competition? It didn’t seem possible.
She had to see for herself.
[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION ROOM]
For the next three days, Zane secluded himself in a secret attunement room, absorbing the lessons from the All Peaks Competition.
Talent and a solid foundation: those were the two pillars of a true warrior.
Some disciples, like those from the Seven Great Houses, might have plenty of talent, but they were paper tigers in a real fight. They didn’t put in the work, and worse, they relied on potent pills to rush their advancement. It gave them a high power level on the surface, but their foundation was dangerously unstable.
It wasn’t that pills were useless—they could be a powerful tool, but only for someone who already had a solid foundation.
Without one, a warrior’s growth was capped. They’d hit a plateau they could never break through.
That’s why some of the most talented warriors deliberately suppressed their advancement, holding back their power until it overflowed, forcing a natural breakthrough.
Zane’s own progress had been slow, but that was by design—he’d never touched a single enhancement pill. As a Level Two Adept, his power level wasn’t flashy, but his foundation was rock-solid, far sturdier than his peers’.
[SFX: ENERGY BUILDING UP]
But since the All Peaks Competition had begun, something had shifted inside of him. He could feel a breakthrough simmering just beneath the surface, the pressure building until he knew he couldn’t contain it much longer.
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY SURGE]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT BREAKTHROUGH]
When Zane finally emerged from the secret attunement room three days later, his power had surged. He had broken through to the Third Level of the Adept Realm.
He’d been so immersed in his training that the three days had felt like mere moments. His breakthrough wasn’t the only fruit of his labor, though. He'd also gained a profound new understanding of Malice.
Ever since connecting with Killing Sword Mountain, Zane had begun to investigate the strange energy.
There was one problem, though. Lily's Malice wasn't inexhaustible — every bit he drew from her was one less. And since he wasn't the type to go on a killing spree, replenishing it would be a challenge he'd have to solve eventually.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
Zane had initially assumed Malice was purely destructive—after all, it was born from killing. But after channeling the Malice from his sword spirit, Lily, he realized he was wrong. As he meditated on the energy, a new truth revealed itself.
Malice wasn’t a force of destruction, but a twisted form of life force.
When a creature is killed, its final moments are a storm of unwillingness, rage, and sorrow. It dies cursing its killer, wishing them anything but a peaceful death, its soul saturated with resentment.
That resentment, that final, desperate curse left behind by a dying being—that was Malice.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: TOURNAMENT FANFARE]
The finals of the All Peaks tournament drew spectators from every corner of the realm, an unmissable spectacle for cultivators and commoners alike. Those unable to attend physically connected through Scrying Slates, which transmitted the battles in real time. While the Skyreach Sect’s Slates offered decent picture clarity, they could never capture the electric atmosphere that permeated the arena in person.
The venue swelled with over a hundred thousand attendees, their bodies forming vast, concentric circles around the combat ground. The air buzzed with anticipation, charged with the collective energy of countless disciples eager to witness history unfold.
Tucked away in a corner, Elara watched the spectacle through the narrow slits of her purple mask, her astonishment growing with each passing moment. Though she had witnessed the All Peaks Competition before, never had she experienced it on this kind of scale - the sheer size and energy of the crowd was overwhelming.
Has the Skyreach Sect really grown this much in just a few years? she wondered. *And how has Zane become the name on everyone’s lips?
When did my brother become so famous?* The irony wasn’t lost on her. Even during her peak as a rising talent, when masters had praised her rare abilities, Elara had never commanded such fervent attention.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
No easy answers came, and after a moment, she pushed the questions aside. She took a deep breath, her long lashes fluttering. Then, her eyes curved into crescents as she spotted Zane walking into the arena.
He waved right at her.
She couldn’t wait to see what he would do next.
