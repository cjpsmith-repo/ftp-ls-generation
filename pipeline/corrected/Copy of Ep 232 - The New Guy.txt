Ep 232 - The New Guy
Word count: 1172
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TRAINING ROOM]
Through relentless practice, Zane's Corrupt Vitality grew darker, the purple undertones gradually vanishing from its essence. The flecks embedded within this mysterious energy now radiated with an intense brilliance, like distant celestial bodies against the void of space.
Six elegant streams of Vitality emanated from Zane's tensed body. With a mere thought, he commanded them to transform, watching with quiet satisfaction as they shaped themselves into miniature silhouettes mirroring his form---demonic phantoms born from his will.
[MUSIC: MAGICAL INCANTATION]
Zane narrowed his eyes, studying his creations. He pointed a finger toward them and whispered, "Rise."
The phantoms responded immediately, launching themselves into flight around the chamber. Their Vitality composition made them weightless, giving them extraordinary speed and agility that delighted Zane. He smiled faintly, imagining how these six sentinels circling him would create an almost impenetrable defense against incoming threats.
At his current level of mastery, Zane could maintain only six phantoms simultaneously. The ancient text of the Titan Strike, however, described how a true master with enough psychic power might summon hundreds. His fingers twitched involuntarily as he contemplated the potential.
Each phantom had the ability to absorb an enemy's Vitality to strengthen itself---a unique property bestowed by the Corrupt Vitality from which they were formed. Zane shuddered slightly at the implications. Once these phantoms reached full maturity, their destructive capacity would be nothing short of terrifying, perhaps beyond even his control.
[SFX: PHANTOMS FLYING]
[SFX: WALL IMPACT]
Something shot across the room and slammed into the wall in the blink of an eye.
[SFX: WALLS CRUMBLING]
One of Zane's phantoms hurtled across the chamber, escaping his still-weak grasp before slamming violently into the practice room wall. It tore through the wall as though it were made of parchment, then crashed through two additional walls before finally breaching the house's exterior. Ancient stonework crumbled away, sending dust and debris cascading to the ground below.
Zane winced, both from the destruction and the mental strain of trying to rein in these increasingly powerful manifestations. Each phantom seemed to possess a will of its own---a troubling development he hadn't anticipated when first mastering the Titan Strike.
Every inner disciple's residence had a secret practice room---sacred spaces where they could refine their unique abilities away from prying eyes. These sanctuaries allowed disciples to experiment with techniques too dangerous or too secret to reveal publicly, their walls typically reinforced with protective enchantments. Zane's room, however, clearly required additional fortification given his rapidly expanding capabilities.
[AMBIENT: DIFFERENT LOCATION]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE]
"When did he even learn to use a sword?" Cliff thought. "And how could he possibly have grasped sword intent if he's never used one?"
Cliff had been practicing swordplay for decades and still hadn't managed it. Sword intent wasn't something you could just learn; it had to emerge naturally. Only those with a profound level of understanding could ever hope to find it.
"And Zane grasped it in such a short time?"
As hard as it was for Cliff to believe, the faint but powerful spiritual aura radiating from Zane was undeniably sword intent.
In front of so many people, Cliff couldn't bring himself to ask Zane about it directly. He'd find out soon enough. During the All Peaks Competition, Zane would have to face opponents strong enough to force his hand, and everyone would see his sword intent---if he really had it. But deep down, a bitter part of Cliff couldn't stand the thought that it might be true. After all, sword intent wasn't supposed to be easy. He knew that firsthand, after years of fruitless practice.
"The All Peaks Competition? Alright," Zane said with a nod. It was probably a good idea to get a sense of the other disciples' strength before the tournament anyway.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN PAVILION]
[MUSIC: TENSE DISCUSSION]
Meanwhile, in a well-appointed pavilion at the summit of Misty Peak, six disciples in black robes were gathered.
They were the inner disciples ranked second through seventh on the peak.
The senior disciple, Eliot, had been number one until Zane's performance in the trial knocked him down a peg. He still hadn't challenged Zane to win his rank back.
"Eliot, you could totally take him. You're the best disciple we have!" one of them said with a huff.
"I agree," another added. "You can't let some new kid outrank you. His Adept Realm is only second-tier. Yours is top-tier!"
"And what's worse, now we have to invite that kid to discuss the All Peaks Competition?" another disciple burst out angrily. "He was just promoted! What right does he have to sit with us?"
Clearly, none of them were impressed with Zane holding the top spot. He was just too new.
Even though he'd gotten the highest score in the trial, it hadn't earned him any respect. To them, Zane didn't deserve to be number one.
Eliot set down his cup. "In the Skyreach Sect, strength is the only qualification that matters. Zane earned that score — which means he earned the seat." He paused, letting that land. "Besides, we're thinking about this the wrong way. If he can push Misty Peak's ranking up even a few places, we all benefit. Better rankings mean better resources — magic arrays, sanctums, monthly allowances. Everything we've been watching the top peaks enjoy for years." He looked around the table. "So let's not waste time resenting him. Let's use him."
The room went quiet.
According to the rules, the trial's magic array determined the initial rankings based on points. If an inner disciple wasn't satisfied, their only option was to challenge someone ranked above them. The winner in the battle ring would then take the higher rank.
In the last trial, Zane had scored fifty-five thousand points, blowing everyone else out of the water and replacing Eliot as the top-ranked disciple. If Eliot wasn't happy about it, he'd have to prove it. And since only the top seven would represent their peak in the competition, they needed to be a united front.
[SFX: DOOR KNOCK]
A few moments later, a knock came at the door. "Come in!" one of the disciples called out.
It was Cliff. Hearing the summons, he pushed the door open and whispered to Zane, "Eliot and the others are already inside. You can go in and join their discussion."
Although Cliff was stronger than most disciples on Misty Peak, he hadn't cracked the top seven. That meant he wasn't eligible to attend meetings about the All Peaks Competition.
[SFX: DOOR OPENING]
[AMBIENT: MEETING ROOM]
Zane thanked him and stepped inside.
Eliot looked up at Zane, then stood with a smile. "You must be Zane. Good to finally put a face to the name. I'm Eliot."
"It's an honor to meet you, Eliot. I've heard a lot about you," Zane said sincerely, offering a respectful bow.
"Please, take a seat," Eliot said heartily, gesturing to the seat at the head of the table. It had been his seat, but now he was offering it to Zane.
Unaware of the seating politics of the inner circle, Zane simply did as he was told. He walked to the head of the table and took the seat Eliot had offered him.
Would he come to regret trusting a fellow competitor?
