Ep 662 - The Amulet

Word count: 1150

[AMBIENT: OPPRESSIVE WIND]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Malcolm and Timothy were in a real bind, and they were both smart enough to know it. The situation was spiraling out of control with every passing second.

"Stop!" Malcolm shouted, his expression hardening.

Zane glanced back at him. "What's wrong?"

"We're stuck," Malcolm said, his voice strained. "This is as far as we can go."

The crushing pressure was just too much. And they weren't the only ones feeling it.

Fabian and Esther, who focused on cultivating their energy rather than pure physical strength, could normally just use an Aether crystal to restore their Vitality. But now, even they were burning through their personal reserves and their crystals at a terrifying rate.

"He's right, Zane," Fabian said, panting. "If we keep pushing like this, we'll have nothing left when we face the Dark Nether Death Light."

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]

Suddenly, Lavender let out a cold sneer. "Are you all really that dense? It doesn't matter if you move or not. This fog is dragging you toward that red dot anyway."

Her words hit them like a physical blow. The closer they got to that ominous red dot, the stronger the Dark Nether Death Light would become. There was no escape. They’d made a huge mistake coming to the core; the fog wasn't dampening the light’s power anymore. Facing it head-on... the thought alone was paralyzing.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHIME]

Just then, Yolande's voice cut through the panic. "Channel your Vitality into the amulet around your neck," she said to Zane.

[AMBIENT: TENSE MURMURING CROWD]

Yolande had slipped the Crabstone Amulet over Zane's head just moments earlier. Everyone had been curious then, and now they all stared at the strange gem resting on his chest.

Zane didn't hesitate.He lifted a hand and poured a surge of Vitality into the amulet.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]

[AMBIENT: PROTECTIVE DOME HUMMING]

A low hum vibrated through the air as an orange glimmer burst from the stone. The light expanded in a rush, creating a protective dome nearly two hundred feet wide around them. As the orange glow washed over the group, the suffocating pressure of the Dark Nether Death Light finally weakened, receding like a tide.

[AMBIENT: RELIEVED CROWD CHATTER]

"It's working! It's actually blocking it!"

"See? Yolande had a plan all along!"

"But why did she give the amulet to Zane?"

The relief lasted about three seconds. Then Malcolm's eyes locked onto Zane, and something shifted in his expression.

He took a step forward. Esther, Fabian, and Jarrod moved instantly, planting themselves between him and Zane.

"What are you doing?" Esther said flatly.

Malcolm frowned. "I want to stay close to Zane."

"Why?" Lavender's voice cut in, cold and sharp. She already knew. They all did, really. The closer they got to the red dot, the stronger the Dark Nether Death Light.

If Zane chose to abandon Malcolm and Timothy and the Twilight Group survivors, the dome moved with him — and they died. Malcolm wasn't going to let himself end up in that position.

Garrick and the remaining Twilight Group War Lords nodded along. So did Timothy.

Malcolm stiffened up. "Agree, or we settle it right now." The black wings on his back rose, a dark aura flickering to life around him.

After only a few hours of uneasy peace, both sides were back on the edge.

Zane stared at him. "Fighting here isn't a smart move."

"I know," Malcolm said. "But if I don't act now, I may not even have the strength to fight later. So. What's it going to be?"

A beat of silence. Then Yolande stepped forward.

"Zane walks with me," she said, her voice leaving no room for argument. She turned and pointed at Esther, Fabian, and the others. "The rest of you — stay with them."

Zane and Yolande were at the front, everyone else grouped together behind. If Zane ran, the dome ran with him — and both sides would be left to die equally. It wasn't a perfect arrangement, but it was the only one that gave everyone a fair chance.

Malcolm considered it. He looked like he wanted to push further. Then Yolande said, simply: "If you don't accept this, you don't leave."

That was enough. Malcolm nodded.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS FOREST]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The plan seemed to be working, but when they glanced over at Yolande, who was usually the picture of calm, they were startled to see how pale her face had become. This place was still trying to kill them. Realizing there was no time to waste, they broke into a run, staying huddled together inside the protective barrier of light.

[SFX: RUSTLING VEGETATION]

Garrick spotted a strange plant and broke away from the group, rushing toward it before anyone could stop him. The two emerald leaves shimmered with a deep green light — clearly no ordinary growth. Malcolm saw it; Esther and Fabian's expressions shifted as well. Even they could tell it was something unusual.

But Yolande's face changed. The calm, puppet-like stillness she always wore cracked — just for a second — into something urgent.

"Go," she said, grabbing Zane's arm. "Now. Run."

She didn't wait for a question. She pulled him forward and broke into a sprint, and the dome moved with them.

Everyone else bolted after them without a word. You didn't need an explanation when Yolande looked like that.

Garrick had already reached the plant. He stretched out his hand to take it —

[SFX: MONSTROUS ROAR]

— and the plant shot upward. A small bud burst from between the two leaves and bloomed in an instant. Not into a flower.

Into a face.

[MUSIC: HORROR CRESCENDO]

[SFX: FLESH DISSOLVING]

The rest could only look back in horror as the protective shimmer of Garrick’s Vitality vanished like smoke. In the next horrifying second, his skin dissolved, revealing the raw, red muscle beneath. Then the flesh itself sloughed away, exposing a clean, white skeleton. Before anyone could even scream, the bones turned to dust, leaving nothing but a few scraps of his shirt fluttering to the ground.

"What in God's name was that?" Malcolm choked out, his face ashen.

Fabian just shook his head grimly. "Who the hell knows? In a place like this, you have to be ready for anything."

"But it doesn't have hands or feet," Jarrod stammered, his eyes wide with terror. "It can't chase us, right?"

Yolande didn't answer. She just grabbed Zane's arm and kept running, pulling him along as she led the way.

[SFX: GROUND ERUPTING]

[MUSIC: PANIC RISING]

Just as Jarrod finished speaking, a flash of green erupted from the ground ahead of them. It was the same twin leaves, bursting from the dirt right in their path.

And in that instant, they all understood why Yolande had been so desperate to run. The plant didn't need legs. It could travel underground.
