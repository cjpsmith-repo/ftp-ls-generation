Ep 330 - Crime and Punishment
Word count: 1238
[MUSIC: EPIC FANTASY THEME]
Despite Zane’s insolent words ringing in his ear, Bryson couldn’t tear his gaze away from Zane’s sword. Its otherworldly gleam captivated him.
There was a time when even a low-level Spirit Weapon commanded awe in Castermere County. The mere sight of such a blade would draw whispers and admiring glances from passersby. Back then, with high-level Spirit Weapon to his name, Bryson had strutted through the county like royalty, his status unimpeachable. The memory of that power still burned bright in his mind.
But that golden age had vanished with the mining boom. After several massive mines were discovered and exploited, wealth flooded the region, elevating its fighters to unprecedented prosperity. Now, Spirit Weapons were a common sight in the streets.
The Lord of House Hawthorne had benefited most of all from this newfound abundance. He’d become so ridiculously wealthy that he’d acquired a middle-level Spirit Weapon—an acquisition that once would have seemed impossible to local warriors.
Having spent his entire life in Castermere County, Bryson’s understanding of power had never extended beyond Spirit Weapons.
Sacred blades existed only in tavern tales and bedtime stories told to wide-eyed children—until now, as one gleamed before him, shattering his perception of reality.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
As Bryson stared, transfixed by the impossible weapon, Zane’s voice sliced through the tension, each word sharp and cold as ice. "Do you see what’s happening here, Bryson? The younger generation of this house has no respect for you. And they shouldn’t; a leader, you have been an absolute failure."
A humorless smile twisted Bryson's lips. The twelve longswords that floated in the air behind him began to tremble. "You dare call me a failure? They're just a bunch of kids stirring up trouble. You think some half-baked story is enough to topple me? Do you have a single elder on your side?"
[SFX: DOORS OPENING]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
As the words left his mouth, three middle-aged men marched purposefully into the Hall, their faces set with grim determination. A dozen younger members of the house followed eagerly behind.
The man in the lead spoke for them, his voice resonating through the hall with unwavering conviction. "We stand with Zane."
"You’ve pushed boundaries too far these past years, Bryson," added another man. "Your leadership has brought nothing but shame to our house."
The third man nodded solemnly in agreement. These three men—Zane’s uncles—embodied the quiet strength that had sustained House Lowell through generations.
[AMBIENT: TENSE GATHERING HALL]
After hearing Zane’s damning accusations echo through the estate, they’d immediately rallied their loyal followers. Without hesitation, they had abandoned their daily duties and rushed to the hall to position themselves beside their nephew, a united front against tyranny.
Bryson's face darkened, and his expression grew stormy. "Cletus, Hodge, and Chet! Are you trying to rebel against House Lowell?"
He knew that with the elders involved, this was no longer just a challenge—it was a coup. Zane’s uncles were the heads of the other branches of House Lowell. If they chose to rebel, their branches would follow.
"We're rebelling against you, Bryson, not House Lowell!" Cletus Lowell declared.
They had been fed up with Bryson and Ken's tyranny for years. But they'd never acted, partly because they feared no one else was qualified to lead, and more importantly, because no one had dared to make the first move. None of the branches felt strong enough to lead a revolt alone, knowing Bryson would slaughter whoever tried.
All they had needed was an opening.
With Zane's return, the elders finally felt they had a candidate they could trust. So, they decided to back him, all at once.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Hearing Cletus, Bryson let out a bitter sigh, his lips curling into a sneer. "Fine. So that's how it is. When a wall shows cracks, everyone wants to help push it over. But I'm telling you, every single one of you will regret this."
[SFX: TAP-TAP-TAP OF WALKING STICK]
Just then, the sharp tap-tap-tap of a walking stick echoed from the gate of the Martial Arts Hall, growing steadily closer.
An old man appeared at the entrance — white-haired, barely able to walk, supported on either side by servants. He made his way slowly across the hall. The room went quiet.
When he finally stopped, he caught his breath and said in a hoarse voice, "We will not regret it."
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
It was Zane's great-grandfather — the oldest living member of House Lowell, rarely seen in public. No one dared ignore him.
Bryson stared, speechless.
Zane moved through the crowd to the old man's side and bowed. A trembling hand reached out and took his arm.
"This family has wronged you," the old man said quietly. "Wronged your father. You are the only one who can save us now."
"That's why I came back," Zane said.
If a warrior's will wavers, their opponent will seize the opening to strike them down.
Seeing Zane and all those who stood with him, Bryson's conviction in his right to rule House Lowell began to falter. A crack formed in the warrior's heart that he had spent a lifetime forging.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Then, Zane called him out, listing every one of his sins and every unjust deed with surgical precision. Each word struck like a hammer blow, resonating through the hall and piercing Bryson’s carefully constructed façade.
"You poisoned my father," Zane continued, his eyes never leaving Bryson’s face. "You turned family against family, robbed the innocent, and bathed our honorable name in shame."
The accumulated weight of truth crashed down upon Bryson’s conscience.
His spirit, already fractured by doubt, now shattered completely beneath Zane’s relentless indictment.
A violent storm of conflicting emotions—rage, fear, shame—ravaged Bryson’s body. The internal damage was so severe that blood welled up in his throat.
Zane looked at him with pity. "You’re spitting blood. That means there’s still a shred of humanity left in you. A flicker of guilt buried beneath all that corruption." He shook his head slowly, fingers tightening around Streamer’s hilt. "But it’s too late. You’ve already dragged House Lowell into a pit of disgrace. I will clean up your mess, uncle, and the price for that is your life."
[SFX: MANIACAL LAUGHTER]
A hysterical, unhinged laugh tore from Bryson's throat. The roar echoed through the hall before it finally died.
"Yes, you're right! I'm guilty, just as you said!" he snarled, his face twisting into a mask of pure malice. "But so what? You want to kill me?
What makes you think you can? If you die here, who in House Lowell will dare stand against me? In this world, strength is everything!"
Bryson flew into a rage. "I'll kill Zane, then I'll kill all of you! You old bastard, Bridger, Cletus Lowell, Hodge Lowell! Who else dares to defy me? Who else dares to speak a word against me?"
[SFX: COUGHING BLOOD]
[AMBIENT: DARK ENERGY MANIFESTATION]
As he finished his tirade, he coughed, spraying a fine mist of blood.
From it, streams of pure Malice began to seep into the air. The deep purple Vitality clinging to his swords bled into a visceral, bloody red.
[SFX: DEMONIC SWORD TRANSFORMATION]
A stench like a slaughterhouse rose from each blade, as if they had just tasted the lives of thousands.
"Come on, Zane!" Bryson bellowed. "You joined the Skyreach Sect, didn't you? You must have gotten stronger. Let's see if you're anything more than talk!"
