[AMBIENT: COMBAT ARENA]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FANTASY]
Lily darted through the air with supernatural grace, her hair streaming behind her like a battle flag. She caught Streamer mid-flight, the blade humming with anticipation in her delicate hands.
The sword’s edge gleamed with a hungry light as she pivoted toward Patrick’s Shadow of the Immovable Guardian. In one fluid motion, she executed a complex maneuver that no human swordsman could match.
She was part of the Demon Night, a creature of long, slender legs and a dangerous aura of Malice. She had become Zane’s sword spirit after his journey to Killing Sword Mountain. Her eyes revealed an ancient intelligence that belied her youthful appearance. Each graceful movement carried the deadly precision of countless battles fought across centuries before Zane had ever drawn breath.
And she was about to cause Patrick some serious damage.
[SFX: CRASHES]
"Lily, the sword! Attack!" Zane shouted, his voice cracking with urgency as he flung himself sideways. His heart hammered against his ribs while sweat stung his eyes. He twisted his body in mid-air, narrowly avoiding two massive, descending palms that crashed into the ground where he had stood mere moments before. The impact sent tremors through the arena floor. He couldn’t afford a single stray thought. The slightest distraction would be fatal; the giant hands would crush him into paste.
[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]
But just as the Immovable Guardian’s palms were about to slam down on him, a flash of movement in his peripheral vision made him freeze.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
[SFX: FURIOUS SPINNING]
Lily began to spin, her aura of Malice coiling into a tight spiral like a storm gathering strength. The dark energy condensed around her slender form before she launched herself toward Patrick with impossible speed, leaving only a blur in her wake.
She moved with hypnotic grace, transforming combat into artistry as the sword became an extension of her ethereal presence. Her movements flowed with deadly precision—a mesmerizing dance of death where each step, each turn promised oblivion to her opponent. The blade sang through the air, trailing wisps of that sinister Malice.
"What kind of sword technique is this?" Zane whispered, utterly astounded by the display before him. His mind raced to categorize what he witnessed, but no known style matched her movements.
Despite their connection, Zane realized how little he truly knew about Lily. Since binding her to his blade, he had summoned her presence but never explored her capabilities or essence. Their relationship had been purely functional—a wielder and his weapon—with no genuine understanding between them.
Beyond her striking ethereal beauty and the strange melancholy that sometimes flickered across her features, Lily had appeared no different from the other sword spirits Zane had encountered during his trials on Killing Sword Mountain. But as he watched her, he felt both awe and a twinge of guilt for his ignorance of her true nature.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL MOUNTAIN]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE]
Zane had seen them all on the mountain. He'd started with the basic, formless spirits that were little more than motes of light, and encountered more advanced ones the higher he climbed. Many of them took on distinct shapes, most often humanoid.
But even they were practically mindless puppets, unable to speak, their faces utterly blank.
The only exception was Val, who Zane had encountered on his climb up Killing Sword Mountain. But Val controlled his sword spirit by pouring a piece of his own soul into it.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Lily had no soul inside her. That's what made it so baffling that she could unleash an attack this complex the instant he summoned her.
As Lily's spin accelerated, the spirals of Malice multiplied around her.
At first, Patrick hadn’t paid Zane’s sword spirit much mind. He just had to concentrate on controlling the Immovable Guardian’s body. He figured that even with the Streamer in its hand, he could simply dodge whatever the spirit threw at him.
He never, ever expected it to wield a sword with such terrifying skill.
[SFX: SWORD SLASHING AIR]
 What is this? It’s a dance! But she wasn’t just dancing; she was weaving a devastating sword style into every step, every turn. And she was using a real blade to do it.
Realizing he might not be able to evade her bizarre sword dance, Patrick was forced to pull back one of the giant palms descending on Zane and redirect it toward her.
[SFX: MASSIVE PALM DESCENDING]
The Guardian's palm came down on Lily like a hammer. She didn't dodge. The Malice spiraling off her blade erupted outward in a burst of spinning thorns — and the palm came apart. Fingers severed. The whole thing reduced to nothing.
Patrick stared.
With one hand gone, the pressure on Zane vanished. Patrick couldn’t handle them both. Lily was a true combatant, using her unique dance to channel Malice with deadly purpose—a strategy Zane had never even dreamed of.
In fact, she looked even stronger than he was.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS RETREATING]
Under their combined attack, Patrick was finally driven to the very edge of the arena.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL BARRIER HUMMING]
Behind him, the enchanted barrier shimmered like a curtain of light. He had two choices: admit defeat, or face Lily's sword dance head-on.
He was out of room to run.
As Lily closed in for the kill, Patrick pressed his palms together once more. A golden Seal of the Guardian materialized in his eye.
"Physical suffering strengthens the spirit. Do not be moved by the external world. Let no distraction enter the heart."
Patrick began to chant. As Lily's sword dance grew closer, he appeared to give up, all resistance draining from his body.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHATTERING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
But then, the Seal of the Guardian in his eye shattered, its power flooding his pupil. His right eye began to glow with a brilliant, blindingly golden light.
A murmur went through the crowd. Everyone could feel it: Patrick was about to do something big.
But he was already using the Immovable Guardian's virtual shadow. What in the world could be stronger than that?
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
As golden light blazed from his right eye, the right eye of the massive shadow behind him ignited in perfect sync. Bathed in its glow, the Guardian's shattered palm began to reform.
[SFX: MAGICAL IMPACT]
The moment the hand was whole, Patrick slammed his palms together again.
When he pulled them apart, a flickering, black-and-green flame danced in the space between them.
The shadow mimicked his every move. A much larger black-and-green flame roared to life between its own colossal hands.
[SFX: ROARING FLAME]
"Black Lotus Karmafire!"
