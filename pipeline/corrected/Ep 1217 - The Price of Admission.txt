Ep 1217 - The Price of Admission

Word count: 1095

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERIC]
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
Murmurs of doubt were spreading, and it was all because of one name: Zane. As a warrior from the renowned Mist Palace, the expectations for him were sky-high. For anyone else, ranking in the top five million would have been a massive achievement, something to be proud of. But this was Zane. The guy’s sword strike still held the number one spot in the Hall of Blades, a feat that left pretty much every warrior who saw it speechless.
So when people saw him ranked at nearly four million, they weren’t just surprised—they were disappointed. Honestly, it would’ve been better if his name hadn’t shown up on the lists at all.
The Illusionary Arenas were scattered across every sacred place in the universe, and Zane’s reputation preceded him. Stories of him outsmarting and killing Supreme Lords, plus the occasional updates from his Roaring Token, meant a lot of people were already searching for his name. 
[AMBIENT: CROWD CHATTER]
Breaking into the top five million just put a bigger spotlight on him, and not in a good way.
“So, this Zane guy isn’t all he’s cracked up to be,” someone scoffed online. “The battlefield’s been open this long, and he’s barely in the top four million?”
“See? I told you,” another voice chimed in. “He just got lucky with that weird Elysian Keep. His own power is nothing special. How’s he supposed to compete with the real prodigies from the thousands of supreme worlds?”
“If he’s this weak, he shouldn’t have even bothered showing up. He’s just embarrassing himself.”
“I heard he’s basically a dead man walking, trapped in the Elysian Keep for killing those four Supreme Lords. Maybe he’s just desperate, hoping for some miracle breakthrough in here to deal with that mess.”
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
While countless beings across the universe debated his strength, Zane himself was oblivious, just floating outside the city of ogres. He’d made his point by killing Lance, and now the rest of the warriors in the city were giving him a wide berth. Everyone there knew how strong Lance was; most of them wouldn’t have survived a single hit from the guy. Yet Zane, this human warrior, had killed him—three times—as if it were nothing.
After a display of strength like that, nobody was about to volunteer their hard-earned Illusion Points. The city had gone quiet. With no one willing to fight him, Zane had to find another way to climb the ranks.
[AMBIENT: CIRCLING WIND]
Left with nothing else to try, Zane circled the main city — twice. But the fight with Lance had drawn too much attention. Everywhere he flew, a mass of warriors trailed him along the top of the city wall, unwilling to miss a chance to watch a fight like that unfold. And any warrior still fighting outside the city took one look at the crowd gathering above and beat a hasty retreat back inside rather than get caught in the open near him.
Two full circuits, and Zane hadn’t earned a single point. He allowed himself a bitter, involuntary smile. Those were just the rules of the Illusion Battlefield — no use complaining about them, and complaining had never been his style anyway. Whatever the city had to offer him was gone. It was time to try the wilderness instead.
[AMBIENT: HUSHED CROWD]
On top of the wall, six Ogre warriors watched him go, their eyes fixed on the direction he’d disappeared. Each of them carried real strength — not far behind Lance himself — and none of them wanted to let a target carrying that many Illusion Points simply walk away. If the six of them worked together, they reasoned, they might actually stand a chance. It was tempting.
But as Zane’s shape shrank into the distance, one of them shook his head. “Forget it. That man hasn’t even shown half of what he’s got.” The others reluctantly agreed. Whatever Zane’s true limits were, none of them wanted to find out the hard way. They let him go.
What none of them realized was that Zane had slowed down on purpose as he left — hoping exactly this kind of greed would draw someone out to chase him. When no one took the bait, he allowed himself a small, rueful thought: he really had been getting greedy, killing Lance three times in a row like that. Then again, he reasoned, even one kill wouldn’t have changed much — there just weren’t many easy targets left to catch. With no pursuers behind him, he picked up speed and struck out for the open wilderness.
[AMBIENT: CAVE ENTRANCE WITH DISTANT SKITTERING]
He glanced over at the Celestial Mice skittering near a cave. *The reward for killing one isn’t bad,* he thought. *A thousand points for the kill, and I get another three thousand each time. A strong enough person could just farm them over and over.* It was a safe way to get points, but most Soul Sea Realm warriors still struggled with them, figuring it was easier to just hunt each other outside the city. It felt inefficient to Zane, but then again, his own progress wasn’t exactly lightning-fast at the moment.
“Might as well check out that cave,” he decided. “The battlefield must have put it here for a reason.”
Without hesitating, he shot toward the three Celestial Mice. He’d done this before, and it was even easier the second time around.
[SFX: CRACKLING LIGHTNING SURGE]
[SFX: THUNDEROUS EXPLOSION]
He unleashed his Eight Smoky Melodies and Thunderbolt Annihilation, luring the creatures into the crackling lightning before finishing them all with a final Heavenly Tribulation.
Another three thousand points flowed into him, and he headed straight for the cave. 
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER ACTIVATION]
Just as he neared the entrance, a shimmering beam of light flared to life, blocking his path. A voice echoed directly in his mind.
*’Entering the Evil Wind Cave requires three hundred thousand Illusion Points.’*
Zane’s eyes were fixed on the inky blackness of the cave beyond the barrier. He just nodded. “Alright.”
[SFX: VACUUM FORCE SURGE]
[SFX: ENERGY POINTS STREAMING]
The moment he agreed, a strange, powerful force erupted from the cave. It felt like a vacuum, violently yanking the shimmering yellow points of light from his body. He watched as all of his Illusion Points streamed into the barrier and vanished.
Just like that, three hundred thousand points were gone. Even though he’d been the one to make the call, it still stung watching all that work get erased in a second.
