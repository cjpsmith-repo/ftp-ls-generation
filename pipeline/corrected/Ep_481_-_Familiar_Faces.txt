Ep 481 - Familiar Faces

Word count: 1055

[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT GROUNDS]

[MUSIC: LIVELY GATHERING]

Only fourth-grade sects or higher received automatic entry into the World Merchants' Guild's Martial Tournament. Their disciples got to skip the pre-qualifiers entirely—a privilege that spoke volumes about the hierarchy of power in the Heartlands.

The Mysterious Luna Hall stood among the oldest sects in this region, its history stretching back centuries before most sects had even been founded. They fought with a vicious, cruel style that made even hardened cultivators uncomfortable to watch. Concepts like "good" or "evil" held no meaning for them—they did whatever they wanted without pretense or apology. Such ruthlessness didn't earn them much respect among the cultivation world's more traditional powers, but no one was stupid enough to cross a fourth-grade sect. Especially not one led by a legend like Elder Luna, a man who had walked the earth for over nine hundred years and accumulated secrets most cultivators couldn't fathom.

[AMBIENT: LARGE OUTDOOR ARENA]

High platforms lined the far side of the field, reserved for the tournament's most distinguished participants. A moment later, a massive coffin descended from the sky, its shadow falling across the crowd like a dark omen. It landed heavily on one of the platforms with a thud that sent tremors through the stone.

[SFX: HEAVY COFFIN IMPACT]

[SFX: CREAKING COFFIN LID]

[MUSIC: DARK MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

The lid slid open on its own the second it touched down, and an old man in a black cloak crawled out. His face was a roadmap of deep wrinkles, so ancient and frail he looked like he could fall asleep and just… never wake up. But despite the age etched into his skin, his hair was jet-black, a stark, unsettling contrast.

It could only be the man of legend himself: Elder Luna, head of the Mysterious Luna Hall.

In the martial arts world, anyone powerful enough to possess a supreme Aether crystal was a big deal. They were all part of an exclusive club, especially the titans from The Heartlands, so everyone knew everyone.

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING AND GASPS]

A ripple of recognition surged through the crowd. Murmurs erupted and spread like wildfire among the hundreds of thousands of fighters, their voices rising in a mixture of awe and trepidation.

"That's Elder Luna…" one cultivator breathed, his face pale. "They say he's almost a thousand years old! And he's still alive!"

His companion leaned closer, lowering his voice to a reverent whisper. "Not quite. I heard he's nine hundred seventy-six. Still got another twenty-something years to go before he reaches the millennium mark."

"You think he'll even make it?" The question hung in the air, heavy with doubt. "The man looks like a corpse already."

"Shh! Are you crazy?" The other fighter grabbed his arm, eyes wide with genuine fear. "Keep your voice down. One of his disciples hears you, and you're a dead man. They don't tolerate disrespect—not even when it's whispered."

Around them, similar conversations rippled outward in waves. The contradiction was unsettling—that something so seemingly fragile could command such absolute fear.

[AMBIENT: LARGE TOURNAMENT CROWD]

[MUSIC: ANTICIPATORY STRINGS]

Dozens of elders and disciples from the Mysterious Luna Hall appeared alongside him, but with only four tournament spots, no one knew which of them would actually get to compete.

[SFX: ANNOUNCEMENT GONG]

"Bloodwood Cliff," the World Merchants' Guild elder announced.

[SFX: CARRIAGE LANDING]

[MUSIC: SEDUCTIVE STRINGS]

A carriage soared through the air, coming to a stop on a neighboring platform. A delicate, fair-skinned foot emerged first, followed by a woman in a gown of red satin. Lady Darkmere.

Against the red fabric, her skin seemed to glow, her presence immediately captivating every man in the crowd.

"It's Lady Darkmere… God, she's gorgeous!"

"Yeah, so? She's ancient. Who knows how many guys she's been through?"

"Hey! You can't judge a martial artist by their age. She could be two hundred, and she still looks twenty! Looks as fragile as a sixteen-year-old, even."

Lost in the massive crowd, the fighters felt bold enough to gossip openly about Lady Darkmere. Zane, however, was focused on his own problems. He wasn't afraid of Maddox. This tournament was just a chance to get stronger before their inevitable clash.

[SFX: POWERFUL FLIGHT WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

Suddenly, a powerful figure flew toward their section of the field, a small entourage trailing behind him.

Zane recognized the man's large, imposing face instantly—he'd seen it at the Skyreach Sect. It was Maddox. The strongest practitioner in the Heartlands.

Zane's greatest enemy.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

Zane stared, burning the man's face into his memory. But then his gaze shifted to the person flying right behind Maddox, and his breath caught in his throat.

Is that Marilyn? he wondered.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The woman was huge, with rolls of fat under her chin that completely swallowed her neck. But that wasn't the strange part. The strange part was that Zane couldn't get a read on her power level at all.

What the hell?* he thought. *She's supposed to be middle-stage Inner-Core Realm. Why can't I feel a thing?

Zane could almost always size someone up within seconds; it was a gift. His perception only ever failed him when someone was overwhelmingly more powerful than he was—and Marilyn shouldn't have been anywhere near that level.

As he tried to make sense of it, his eyes fell on another woman just behind the first. This one fit the description: young, beautiful, dressed in a tight black outfit. And he could clearly sense her power—middle-stage Inner-Core Realm. This had to be Marilyn — a warrior who wielded the rare Violet-Core Physique. Maddox had taken her under his wing, and it was to Maddox that Cloud Hall had surrendered Zane's sister Elara. Marilyn was the woman Zane had sworn to kill.

That's the real Marilyn, he decided. It was a guess, but he felt it in his gut.

There were three others with them. One was a sharply dressed young man with a handsome, almost soft face. Maddox's son, probably, Zane thought. If the kid was competing, he'd be another rival to watch out for.

And behind the young man were two teenage girls.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]

Zane’s gaze swept over them, and then snapped back to one. His world stopped. He blinked, shaking his head, refusing to believe his own eyes.

[SFX: HEARTBEAT PULSE]

It was Elara.

But she looked… wrong. Completely different. Her eyes were shut, and she floated along behind the group like a piece of driftwood caught in a current, lifeless and hollow.

[MUSIC: DARK INTENSE ORCHESTRA]

A white-hot rage flared in Zane's chest. Elara, he thought, his hands clenching into fists at his sides. What did they do to you?
