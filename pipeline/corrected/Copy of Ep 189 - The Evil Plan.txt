[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL EASTERN]
Silvermere Spire was a marvel of determination that stood against nature’s will. The treacherous spire, devoid of any established path, challenged even the most seasoned climbers, its jagged slopes repelling all but the most determined souls. Reaching the summit remained virtually impossible without specialized equipment---a deliberate barrier that preserved the sanctuary’s exclusivity. Those blessed with Soul Realm cultivation simply soared upward through the mists, while lesser disciples relied on precious flying chariots to bridge the impossible gap between earth and sky.
Every stone, every timber had been hauled skyward through sheer force of will, each piece representing countless hours of backbreaking labor. A serene, shimmering lake encircled the spire’s base, its mirror-like surface capturing perfect reflections of the towering monument above. The waters glinted with mysterious depth, completing the ethereal landscape that seemed plucked from ancient legends rather than the mortal realm.
From the summit, the world unfolded in panoramic splendor---endless mountain ranges diminished to mere wrinkles on the earth’s skin, clouds drifting below like slow-moving rivers of mist. Such vistas cleansed troubled minds and elevated contemplation, offering perspective that few places in the world could match.
It was upon this isolated pinnacle, far removed from worldly concerns, that many of the Skyreach Sect’s most revered saints established their sanctuaries. Here they meditated, cultivated, and shaped the destiny of the Sect, their decisions rippling outward from this lofty perch to influence the world below.
[SFX: WIND WHISTLING]
At one corner of the spire, a long, narrow pavilion jutted out from the cliff face. Half of it rested securely on solid rock, while the other half hung suspended over the dizzying drop below. Just standing on the edge would be enough to make anyone with a fear of heights tremble.
[AMBIENT: GENTLE BREEZE]
[SFX: TEA POURING]
The fragrant aroma of tea drifted from the pavilion. There, an old man in a purple hat raised a gold-rimmed teacup to his lips and took a slow sip. This was Saint Augustus, one of the ten highest-ranking saints in the Skyreach Sect.
[SFX: WHOOSHING ARRIVAL]
As Augustus savored his tea, a streak of light blazed across the sky, closing in fast. A moment later, an old man materialized at the pavilion’s edge. It was Saint Viana, ranked tenth among the Sect’s saints.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Saint Viana wasted no time on pleasantries, his voice tight with urgency. “If he succeeds, he’ll dig into what we did to Elara. And when he finds out the truth, he won’t let it go. We have to eliminate him before he grows any stronger!”
Unlike Augustus, Viana had faced Zane in combat.
Viana could never forget the day he’d tried to crush Zane with the Deathly Palm. Zane, who was only in the Adept Realm at the time, had actually stopped the attack cold. The shock of it still rattled Viana.
His subsequent investigation revealed something even more alarming: Zane had not only taken the forbidden text, Titan Strike, from the Celestial Library, but had somehow managed to master it.
Zane was still just an Adept, but his capacity for learning was terrifying. If he reached the Soul Realm... could Viana actually defeat him?
And if he conquered the Cloud Road and earned the favor of Cloud Hall, he would have the power to do whatever he pleased to Viana.
Viana’s fears were not unfounded.
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
The person Zane cared about most in the world was Elara. He would let no harm come to her. Viana remembered the fury in Zane’s eyes on Mount Judgment, the moment he learned that Viana, Saint Aldric, and the others had imprisoned Elara in Infernal Peak. Zane had wanted to kill them all right then and there.
Only his lack of power had forced him to stay his hand.
[SFX: MAGICAL MOVEMENT]
Saint Augustus listened to Viana’s words, quietly finishing the last of his tea. With a flick of his sleeve, the entire tea set vanished from the table.
Saint Augustus’ tone was carefully level. “Your concerns are valid, but the world is full of talent. Only a rare few ever ascend to true power. The Sect has hundreds of thousands of disciples, ten thousand of whom could be called gifted. Yet how many actually become masters?”
“Is that so?” Viana shot back. “Have you ever known an Adept to withstand my Deathly Palm?”
“Well\... no,” Augustus admitted, trailing off. He was at a loss for words.
[MUSIC: TENSE REVELATION]
When Viana first told him how Zane had blocked the attack, Augustus had dismissed it. It seemed impossible. But Viana’s conviction had been absolute, leaving Augustus no choice but to accept the incredible story.
And if Zane truly was that gifted, his deep-seated hatred for the Sect made him a ticking time bomb. For the good of the Sect, Augustus knew he had to devise a plan to deal with Zane.
Viana continued to press him. “During his first training session at Emberfin Lake, Zane dove over three hundred feet. Have you ever heard of an outer disciple doing such a thing? He’s more than qualified for the All Peaks Competition.”
"The steward guarding the pond submitted two reports recommending Zane for recognition and resources," Viana added. "I buried both of them."
Augustus raised an eyebrow. "And if the steward submits a third?"
"He won't," Viana said flatly. "He's worked out what's happening. He's not going to push it."
Augustus mulled that over. “But what if he has no desire to become an inner disciple?” What if he has no interest in the competition at all?“
“Then our plan is useless,” Viana stated flatly.
Augustus smiled confidently. “He’ll want to. Isla Novak has invested too much in his training, and she’s counting on him to place well. Besides, Misty Peak is the laughingstock of the Thirty-Three Peaks. Zane will want to change that. I’m sure of it.”
"Why not simply have him killed outright?" Augustus asked.
Viana's expression darkened. "Isla Novak holds the Emperor's Token. Any direct move against Zane leaves traces. If she follows them back to us, we're finished. But a death in the All Peaks Competition?" He spread his hands. "Accidents happen. Every competition, someone doesn't walk out. Even Isla couldn't make a case against us for that."
Augustus was quiet for a moment. Then he nodded.
[MUSIC: DARK PLOTTING]
Viana nodded. “You have a point. Now... who do we send to kill him? I’ll need to choose carefully.”
[SFX: BITTER LAUGH]
A sharp, bitter laugh escaped his lips. The thought of being defied by a boy like Zane was infuriating. He wouldn’t stand for it.
Then again, Zane’s skill was undeniable. Finding someone who could actually kill him during the competition would be a challenge. But the Skyreach Sect was teeming with masters. Viana was certain he could find the perfect instrument for the job.
