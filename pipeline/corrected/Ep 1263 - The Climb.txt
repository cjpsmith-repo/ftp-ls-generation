Ep 1263 - The Climb

Word count: 1112

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]
The four walls collapsed and vanished in an instant. 
[SFX: WALLS COLLAPSING]
[SFX: CEILING DISAPPEARING]
A moment later, the ceiling blinked out of existence.
In their place stood a colossal mountain, its peak shrouded in thick white clouds, looking like something torn from a myth. 
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
The ten warriors, Zane included, found themselves on a stone platform at its base.
“The iron ticket just said ‘the Hall,’” Zane said, breaking the silence. “So what’s with the mountain?”
No one had an answer. For most of them, it was their first time here, and they were just as lost as he was.
“We go up the mountain, we find the Hall,” the Woman in White said with a knowing smile. “And just so you know, all restrictions are lifted the second we step off this platform. But I wouldn’t recommend you start fighting.”
A sinister voice cut through the air. “And why should we listen to you?”
It was one of the other warriors. 
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
A few of them were already sizing each other up, seeing potential targets. After all, if someone could drop five million Illusion Points just to get in, they had to be loaded.
The Woman in White’s smile never faltered. “Because the points you can earn in there are in the tens of millions. If you’re lucky, you might even snag a Divine Weapon. So, by all means,” she gestured around them, “waste your time and energy fighting over pocket change down here. Go right ahead.”
The tension broke instantly. The logic was cold and simple. The warriors who’d been spoiling for a fight suddenly looked hesitant.
The Woman in White didn’t give them another thought. She just nodded at Zane. “Let’s go.”
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
With that, she was the first to step off the platform and onto the winding stone stairs that led up the mountain.
Zane’s eyes lit up. If she was right, he could make a fortune in here. Maybe far more than just ten million points. It suddenly clicked why his rank had been dropping. The warriors ahead of him weren’t just better—they’d probably already been here.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
He thought of the old legends—how the ancient Sorcerer Race had supposedly pushed the limits of the heavens until the heavens themselves struck them down, wiping them from the universe in a single night. A billion years had passed since then, and the story was more myth than history, but standing here, it felt a little more real.
“Oh, and I forgot to mention,” the Woman in White called back without turning, “flying is forbidden. We climb.” She continued up the winding staircase without breaking her stride.
Zane followed right behind her.
[SFX: COUGHING RATTLE]
A dry, rattling cough sounded from behind him. Zane’s stomach dropped — he knew that sound.
The skulls had found him again.
[MUSIC: SUDDEN DANGER]
A chorus of startled gasps rippled through the group as the little swarm surged into view, and even the Woman in White froze mid-step, glancing back with something close to alarm. “What — what are these things?”
“I provoked them in a mystic realm,” Zane said, keeping his voice level. He was more or less used to them by now.
“These can’t be gotten rid of?” The Woman in White walked over and plucked one of the small skulls from his back, turning it over in her fingers.
[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACKLE]
A thin bolt of lightning bloomed from her fingertips and wrapped around the skull. Small as it was, the strike carried enough force that even Zane felt a flicker of unease watching it land.
When the light faded, the skull hadn’t so much as scuffed.
Zane wasn’t surprised. These things were built to survive worse than a lightning bolt — he’d once watched an entire swarm just like it clinging to the back of a Supreme Lord, and if even a being at that level couldn’t shake them off, what chance did anyone here have?
“Hehe — let me have a go,” said the ogre, the same one who’d been picking fights back in the sealed chamber. He reached over and pulled off a skull of his own.
“Let me try too,” said another.
“These things are Divine Weapons — wonder if they can even be broken.”
“Give me one—”
“There’s a whole line of them on his back. Go pull your own—”
Zane just stood there while chaos broke out around him, completely at a loss. Somehow every scrap of attention had ended up on the skulls stuck to his back instead.
The Woman in White caught his expression and laughed behind her hand. With a flick of her wrist, the skull in her grip flew back and reattached itself to Zane’s back with a bite. “If I had to guess,” she said, “these came from a curse.”
“You’d guess right,” Zane said with a shrug.
“There’s an old legend that the ancient tribes’ witchcraft lined up with Celestial Law itself — that they used cursework to steal a piece of Heavenly Destiny. That’s supposedly why their curses are so hard to break.” She paused. “There are only one hundred twenty-eight Heavenly Destinies in the entire universe. Not one more. When a Supreme Lord falls, theirs dissolves, and a new one is born elsewhere. Reaching for one through methods like that — Celestial Law doesn’t tolerate it.”
“Steal Heavenly Destiny?” Zane repeated, caught off guard.
“I don’t fully understand it myself,” she admitted. “But most think that’s exactly why the Sorcerer Race vanished overnight. Of course, it’s been over a billion years — there’s no telling how much of the story’s true anymore.”
The warriors behind them, meanwhile, were each trying their own methods to break the skulls loose.
“Damn it!” one of them, a hulking ogre, roared in frustration. He held up a black saber, his face twisting in disbelief. 
“It didn’t even scratch the skull, but it cracked my Dark Demon Saber!”
His blade, a Grade-One Divine Weapon, now had a half-moon-shaped crack marring its edge. The tiny skull was perfectly fine. 
Thankfully, weapons used on the Illusion Battlefield were magically preserved back in the real world; otherwise, the ogre might have actually lost his mind. Still, with his main weapon damaged and plenty of fighting ahead, he was in for a rough time.
The other warriors just stared at him like he was an idiot. They’d been curious enough to grab a few skulls off Zane’s back, sure, but was it really necessary to try to shatter one?
After a few more failed attempts, they gave up. Defeated, they let the skulls go. 
[SFX: SKULLS ZIPPING THROUGH AIR]
[SFX: TEETH LATCHING ONTO ARMOR]
The tiny things zipped back to Zane, latching onto his back once more with their sharp little teeth, and the eight warriors fell in line behind him, starting their long climb up the stairs.
