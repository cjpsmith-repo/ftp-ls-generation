Ep 396 - Mastering the Puppet
Word count: 1176

[AMBIENT: EERIE CAVERN]

[MUSIC: TENSE JOURNEY]

On their way back, Zane and Tia moved through the Drake's body in a heavy silence. Whether it was from embarrassment or just plain anger, neither of them said a word.

When they finally reached the head, Zane stared at the Drake's tightly shut mouth. Runes were carved deep into its massive teeth, which interlocked like a perfect, impenetrable seal. The Blood Fiend Emperor must have gone to great lengths to modify the Drake's teeth when he created this colossal puppet.

But with its mouth sealed, how were they supposed to get out?

Even if they did, the floating stones were probably gone by now. Flying was forbidden during the trial, and climbing the sheer walls of the abyss was next to impossible.

Just as Zane was weighing their options, Tia shot him an icy glare and pulled out the crystal key. Without a word, she pressed it firmly against a section of the wall beside her—what looked like the back of the Drake's mouth. That part had also been reinforced, its surface covered in intricate runes.

Tia began to trace the runic patterns with the crystal key.

[SFX: RUMBLING CHAINS]

[SFX: STONE GRINDING]

As she did, Zane felt the floor tremble beneath him. He heard the grinding scrape of chains against stone from outside. The Drake was rising! Tia was actually controlling the puppet!

Zane gawked at her, completely stunned.

"This Drake puppet was the holy beast meant to protect the Blood Fiend Emperor's Sect," Tia said icily, her voice dripping with contempt. "It shouldn't be a surprise that the crystal key can control it."

As the Drake ascended, the sound of chains scraping against the abyss walls grew louder. 

[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]

[SFX: METAL SNAPPING]

Zane felt a violent lurch as the Drake burst through the gateway at the top, followed by the sharp crack of snapping metal.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]

It dawned on Zane that the sect leaders and Toby knew almost nothing about this trial. The whole thing was taking place within the ruins of a Sixth-Tier Sect, which meant it had forbidden areas and a holy beast to act as a guardian.

If a disciple had wandered into a forbidden area and released the beast, every single person in the trial could have been killed.

Worse, the holy beast could have escaped the trial grounds and wreaked havoc on the outside world, endangering everyone.

"Looks like it's gone," one of the sect leaders said, his voice shaky with relief. "We're safe now."

He and the others breathed a collective sigh of relief as the Drake's silhouette vanished into the distance.

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

[SFX: MECHANICAL HUMMING]

Inside, Tia steered the massive puppet, periodically changing its direction. She pulled her Scrying Slate from her Storage Bracelet, using it to get a view of the world outside. They were now soaring high above the Sea of Raging Fire.

"What are you looking for?" Zane finally asked.

Tia’s only response was a glare filled with pure hatred. She said nothing.

In her mind, this was all Zane’s fault. She had never been so humiliated in her entire life. She needed payback, but for now, there was nothing she could do to him.

Instead, she refocused on piloting the Drake, her eyes glued to the Scrying Slate as she scanned the fiery sea below.

[MUSIC: DISCOVERY THEME]

"There it is!" Tia suddenly shouted, a flicker of excitement breaking through her cold fury.

On the slate, an island materialized in the middle of the Sea of Raging Fire. It was completely deserted, with no visible path leading to it.

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL BARRIER HUMMING]

A massive, blood-red enchanted barrier shimmered around the entire island.

[SFX: BARRIER OPENING]

[SFX: DRAGON DESCENDING]

"We're going down," Tia announced, steering the Drake toward it. As they neared the barrier, a huge hole rippled open, and the puppet slipped through the opening.

The island was dotted with ancient-looking buildings. A huge square stretched out before the main gate, with a massive black column rising from its center.

[SFX: HEAVY METAL SCRAPING]

[SFX: DRAGON SETTLING]

Noticing Zane's confused stare, Tia simply guided the massive Drake to coil its body around the column. After circling it several times, the Drake's head came to rest on the ground, its mouth creaking open.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

Zane cautiously stepped out and looked up at the towering pillar.

'If the Drake just landed on the ground, it would take up a ridiculous amount of space,' he thought. 'Wrapping it around the column is brilliant. It saves room and keeps everything accessible. The Blood Fiend Emperor was a genius.'

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT STONE HALL]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]

Behind the large square stood a grand main hall. A tablet above the entrance was inscribed with three words: Blood Fiend Hall. This had to be the Blood Fiend Emperor's original home.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

A moment later, Tia emerged from the Drake's mouth, crystal key in hand. 
Without a backward glance, she stalked toward the hall.
Tia didn't head for the hall. Instead she crossed the square to a low stone platform near its center. Set into the platform was a large crystal, roughly two feet across — and in its middle, a groove, shaped exactly like the key.
"Get closer," she said, not looking at him.
Zane moved to the platform's edge without asking why.
[SFX: KEY LOCKING IN]
Tia pressed the crystal key into the groove.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING — GROUND SHAKING]
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT POWER AWAKENING]
The entire square trembled. Then the ground lit up — hundreds of runes, some the size of flower petals, others like spinning gears, all glowing and rotating. They spun faster and faster until beams of light shot outward from their centers, arcing across the sky in every direction, connecting to distant points across the Cloud Road.
One of those beams was already on its way.

Meanwhile, elsewhere on the Cloud Road, Frank and Finn's group had already cleared the Death Road and pushed through to the next stage. They'd found a hidden reward there.
Finn had done well. Better than Frank, in fact. His prize was a high-grade Sacred Weapon — the kind of reward that made even Frank raise an eyebrow.

[AMBIENT: DESOLATE PLATFORM]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Lennon was utterly exhausted after clearing the trial on the second platform. His body was a wreck of severe burns, but the thought of the blue treasure box just ahead had driven him onward.

If he could just get the Lifeblood of the Nine-Headed Dragon, he would gain immense power, and his body would heal in no time.

But he was so badly injured that he could barely move. He had to drag himself forward, inch by agonizing inch. Even though the blue treasure box was only ten feet away, it might as well have been on the other side of the world.

Still, Lennon forced himself onward. It doesn't matter! he thought, gritting his teeth against the pain. Getting the Lifeblood will be worth all of this!

Through a haze of pain, with one final, desperate burst of effort, he almost reached it.

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

Just as he stretched out his trembling hand, a line of light shot out and wrapped around the blue treasure box.

[SFX: SWOOSHING WIND]

Then, the box was yanked into the air and zipped away, following the line of light into the distance.

His mind reeled in disbelief. What? It's... flying away? 

[MUSIC: TRAGIC VIOLIN]

Stunned speechless, all he could do was stare as the blue treasure box vanished from sight.
