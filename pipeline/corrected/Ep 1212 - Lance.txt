[AMBIENT: MASSIVE ARENA CROWD MURMUR]
Inside the Illusion Battlefield, the weaker fighters were being knocked out one after another. 
Names that had been fixed at the top of the leaderboards were suddenly plummeting to the bottom, while underdogs were clawing their way into the spotlight.
In every Illusionary Arena across the universe, crowds watched the ranking pyramid with bated breath. 
They tracked the top talents from every race, but many were paying special attention to one name in particular: Zane Lowell.
By now, there wasn’t a corner of the universe that didn’t know his name. The fame of other great talents—even the sons of Supreme Lords and the heirs of World Lords—paled in comparison. The reason was simple: he had the Roaring Token. 
Trillions had heard his name chanted over and over by that mysterious singer, broadcast for all to hear. 
The news that he’d been surrounded by dozens of Supreme Lords, and had even managed to kill some, had spread like wildfire years ago.
No one in history, not even the Supreme Lords themselves, had ever commanded that kind of fame. So it wasn’t just Yolande watching Zane’s ranking; the members of the Demi-Immortal Race and just about everyone else were glued to it, too. Even the Supreme Lords were curious to see how the most famous man in the universe would fare in the Illusion Battlefield.
But when they looked for his name, they came up empty.
The pyramid of names displayed trillions of competitors, but the rankings at the very bottom were crammed so tightly together they were impossible to read. Only the first few million fighters were even visible, and they’d already racked up a ton of Illusion Points, creating a massive gap between them and the rest. People scanned the pyramid from the top down, through tens of millions of names, but Zane’s was nowhere to be found.
“Doesn’t Zane have that incredible Elysian Keep? Why isn’t he even in the top ten million?”
“It’s simple. That Elysian Keep of his is just a lucky gimmick. He probably isn’t talented enough to crack the top ten million on his own. He seems weaker than the prospects from a core sanctum.”
“You’re right. The top ten million is a pretty low bar. Looks like a disaster is about to unfold for him.”
“Disaster? What are you talking about?” someone asked, confused.
“That immortal mansion of his,” one voice cut in, lower, more uneasy. “Indestructible thing like that, impossible to breach—you really think word hasn’t spread? How long before every Heavenly Venerable in the universe starts circling, looking for a way to pry it loose from him?”
“Nobody’s going to just let a prize like that sit with one warrior,” another added grimly.
“Ogre Flame City,” another person explained. “It’s on the Star of Golden Sand. The Ogres just conquered it, and all the fighters who were hiding inside got wiped out. That’s probably where Zane was.”
“But with that indestructible Elysian Keep, he should have been fine, right? How could he have been defeated?”
“Who knows? Maybe his luck finally ran out.”
This was the hot topic of conversation in countless supreme worlds.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: DESOLATE WASTELAND WIND]
Right then, on the Star of Golden Sand, Zane was floating just outside the Ogre-held city, his robes stark against the wreckage. He’d just returned, only to find the place in ruins.
Zane had planned to do the same, but he was too late. The Ogre warriors had already swept through, earned their points, and vanished behind the city walls. The weaklings were long gone, and on his way back, Zane had only managed to find two stragglers worth hunting.
Seventy thousand points. Rank nineteen million. He’d need three hundred thousand just to crack open that cave back in the wilderness—and at this rate, that was a long way off.
As he circled the city walls, his bitter smile only deepened. Something about this didn’t sit right.
It wasn’t that he lacked the will to hunt. He simply couldn’t find anyone worth hunting. The warriors who’d actually accumulated decent points had gotten smart fast—they’d ducked back inside the city, where fighting was banned outright, and dug in. He couldn’t touch them in there.
Meanwhile, the ones who did come out to meet him weren’t worth the time it took to kill them. A handful of “suicide squad” types kept rushing him with nothing to lose and nothing to gain—killing them paid him zero points, and some of them even chased him down afterward, forcing him to waste effort just shaking them loose.
“Something’s broken about how this place is set up,” he muttered to himself, circling again.
Still, he figured it wouldn’t last. The Illusion Battlefield had only just opened. If every point-rich warrior just hid behind city walls forever, nobody’s strength ever really got tested, and the whole exercise lost its purpose. Sooner or later, the rules would have to change.
[SFX: RUMBLING CRATER]
[SFX: CRACKLING PURPLE FLAMES]
A moment later, a skinny figure rose slowly from a nearby crater. It was Lance, the very Ogre everyone was talking about. A streak of Purple Demonfire coiled into a giant hand beneath his feet, lifting him into the air.
He’d clearly already had a two-hundred-thousand-point head start, and he was burning through targets faster than Zane was. That explained the empty battlefield—Lance, and whoever else had gotten here first, had already cleared the place out before Zane even arrived.
Although Lance was an Ogre, he wasn’t built like one. He was still much taller and stronger than a human, but among the hulking warriors of his clan, he was considered a runt.
After he emerged from the pit, his eyes swept over his surroundings. 
[SFX: DERISIVE SNORT]
He saw the fighters hiding behind the city walls, and a mocking sneer crossed his face. Even if those cowards decided to leave the city, he wouldn’t waste his time on them.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
But as his gaze moved along the edge of the city wall, it landed on a lone figure floating in a corner at the very end.
It was Zane.
A flicker of doubt crossed Lance’s face. Why wasn’t this one hiding behind the walls like the other humans?
As the successor to the Ogre Flame World Lord, conquering this city was a minor task for someone like Lance. He was here to fight the true powerhouses of the Illusion Battlefield. From the start, he’d looked down on this city and everyone in it.
So when he saw Zane just floating there, not even trying to hide, Lance was genuinely puzzled. Had the human been scared stupid?
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CALM]
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL WIND]
While he was still trying to figure it out, he saw Zane flying slowly toward him, a warm smile on his face.
