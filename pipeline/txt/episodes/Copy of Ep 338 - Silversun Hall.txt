Ep 338 - Silversun Hall

Word count: 1061

[AMBIENT: DISTANT BELL TOLLING]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS MEDIEVAL]
The evening bell tolled across the darkening sky, its mournful sound echoing in the air. But the gates of the Palace were still wide open.
Normally, the palace gates were sealed shut at precisely sunset, creating an impenetrable barrier until dawn broke the following day. But for nearly a week now, they had remained accessible until well after nine, the guards standing at attention with expressions that betrayed their discomfort at this breach of custom.
Word on the street was that the Crown Prince was furious. He'd even issued an imperial order demanding the gates be closed at dusk, just as they always had been.
But the order was ignored. The Crown Prince, it seemed, had gone from a respected ruler to a toothless tiger, his commands falling on deliberately deaf ears. The palace gates stood as a physical manifestation of his weakening influence, a silent rebellion against his leadership. The open gates became a revolving door for high society—heads of noble houses, military generals—who came and went as if they owned the place.
Even the common folk, watching from a distance, understood the significance. The writing on the wall was obvious—the Crown Prince’s reign was in very real trouble, his power eroding with each visitor who passed through those defiantly open gates. It was only a matter of time before he was overthrown.
The Third and Seventh Princes were circling like sharks, each ready to make their move. The only thing keeping the Crown Prince on his throne was their mutual distrust; neither brother dared to act while the other was watching. But as soon as one gained the upper hand, the Crown Prince’s downfall was a certainty.
To that end, the Third Prince had been throwing lavish parties in his Silversun Hall, while the Seventh Prince hosted his own at the Crimson Moon Mansion. Both were courting allies, trying to tip the scales in their favor. That was the real reason the palace gates stayed open so late.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
Zane had done a little digging on the situation, and while a lot of it was still murky, he honestly couldn’t care less who ended up running the Empire. All that mattered right now was seeing Isla.
[AMBIENT: IMPERIAL PALACE EXTERIOR]
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
He finally reached the palace gate, but before he could cross the threshold, a formation of imperial guards swiftly moved to block his path.
"Hold it," commanded one of them, his voice deliberately flat and detached. "This is the imperial palace. State your name and who you’re here to see, then present your visitor’s pass."
Under normal circumstances, only a representative of some major power would dare to approach these gates directly. So, for a lone warrior at only the Third Level of the Adept Realm, the guard's tone was surprisingly civil.
Zane offered a respectful bow, his face betraying nothing of his inner impatience. "I’m here to see the Sky Princess. I don’t have a visitor’s pass, but I have my acolyte’s sigil from the Skyreach Sect." His fingers traced the intricate patterns on the metal emblem as he presented it, the polished surface catching the fading light of dusk.
The guards exchanged subtle glances, clearly uncertain how to proceed with someone of his unusual status.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
"Zane Lowell?" The imperial guard examined the sigil. The Skyreach Sect commanded tremendous respect throughout the empire, but a disciple just showing up at the palace gates was highly unusual. Before he could voice his concerns, Ray, the Third Prince, materialized from the shadows of the courtyard, his elegant robes rustling softly as he approached. A look of genuine surprise flickered across his aristocratic features.
The Third Prince, Zane thought, his shoulders tensing.
Any commoner would have immediately dropped to their knees before royalty, touching their forehead to the ground in reverence. Martial artists, however, played by different rules. In their world, strength—not bloodline—was the only thing that earned true respect.
Even the Emperor, for all his political might, wasn’t immune; if he was weak, a powerful warrior could take his head as easily as anyone else’s.
Zane recalled how the Sixth Prince, Nile, had discovered this truth painfully when he was unceremoniously eliminated from the All Peaks Competition, his royal status offering no shield against defeat.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
Zane planted his feet firmly on the stone pathway, meeting the prince’s gaze directly. He could feel the calculated warmth rolling off Ray—a practiced charm that had likely ensnared countless ambitious courtiers.
Behind those eyes lurked the unmistakable assessment of a predator evaluating potential prey or ally.
Zane sensed a recruitment attempt forming on the prince’s lips and felt a surge of irritation. He had zero interest in getting tangled in the palace’s political drama.
He cut straight to the point. "Your Highness, I didn’t come to the Palace for you. I’m here for the Sky Princess."
A flicker of displeasure darkened Ray’s features before vanishing beneath his practiced smile. His entourage, however, wasn’t nearly as controlled.
[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD]
"It’s an honor that the Third Prince is even talking to someone like you!" one attendant snarled, his face flushing crimson with outrage.
"Nobody cares who you’re here for!"
"I’ve never seen such an arrogant brat," another courtier chimed in, his voice trembling with barely contained fury. "His Highness graces you with his attention, and you just stand there? Who the hell do you think you are?"
[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]
Ray lifted his hand with elegant authority, and the insults died instantly, as though he had turned off an invisible tap. To Zane’s astonishment, the prince’s smile broadened, revealing perfect teeth that gleamed in the palace light. "Her Highness is resting in the Radiant Hall. If you want to see her, I can have her brought to you."
Zane’s gaze swept coldly over the men surrounding Ray, assessing each one. Most had the power of Adepts, although three had recently broken into the Soul Realm—formidable, but not threatening to someone of his caliber.
He turned his blank stare back to the prince. "I’d appreciate that.
Thank you, Your Highness."
[AMBIENT: GRAND PALACE HALL]
[MUSIC: ROYAL PROCESSION]
With that, Zane followed the Third Prince through the ornate doorway into the magnificent Silversun Hall to find the girl he’d come all this way to see.
