Ep 868 - Breaking Down

Word count: 955

[AMBIENT: GENTLE WIND]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Zane sat on the golden leaf, letting the world fall away. With each passing moment, the storm in his mind settled, leaving a calm, clear focus in its wake. 

He could feel the problem in his own bones: his body was the bottleneck, a physical limit keeping him from unleashing his true power. Shattering that ceiling was the only thing that mattered.

His mind sifted through the possibilities. 

Since absorbing the will of the world from the Little Monster, his perception had fundamentally changed. He could now connect with the very essence of the world of chaos—a flower, a log, a mountain, a sea. It was as if the world’s memories had been poured into his own mind, making him its voice. He could even sense the distant source of that power, a mystery he’d have to unravel later. Right now, he had a more immediate problem.

His Power Transfer technique was his usual ace in the hole. He could channel devastating force away from himself, letting something else absorb the blow. He’d done it before, using the mountains at West Dragon River to weather a punch from War King York, and again when he used the entire Sacred Feather Sea to block Fredrick’s Heavenly Thunder Halberd. 

With Power Transfer, he could probably withstand the force of ten thousand Dragon Scales—hell, maybe even a hundred thousand. The energy that should have shattered his bones would simply be redirected elsewhere.

But that was the issue. He didn’t need to withstand force; he needed to apply it. Every ounce of his power had to be focused directly into this golden leaf. If he used Power Transfer, the energy from the Dragon Scales would just harmlessly bypass his target.

Transfer…

[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

The word echoed in his mind. He tapped his forehead, and then it hit him—a flash of insight so simple it was brilliant. He’d been thinking about it all wrong, viewing Power Transfer as a purely defensive tool.

The key wasn’t to transfer the force away from himself. It was to transfer it into the leaf.

His body could handle four thousand Dragon Scales — that was his ceiling. But if he channeled the remaining force directly into the golden leaf through Power Transfer, his body never had to bear it. Four thousand scales held in his body. Ten thousand transferred straight into the leaf. All fourteen thousand applied at once — just through two different paths.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL PIANO]

Randy’s eyes lit up at the sudden shift in Zane’s posture, the spark of discovery in his gaze. He had to have figured something out. A small, knowing smile touched Fiona’s lips. She knew better than anyone how clever Zane could be; if anyone could find a way out of this, it was him.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSIONS]

Without another moment of hesitation, Zane shot to his feet. He planted himself firmly on the leaf and took a deep, steadying breath.

[SFX: MAGICAL POWER SURGE]

“The power of Dragon Scales!” he roared, and the spectral scales of the Cobalt Dragon flared to life around him.

[SFX: ENERGY BUILDING]

One thousand. Two thousand. Three thousand. Four thousand — his body expanded, bones straining at the joints, just as before. But this time he didn’t stop.

Five thousand. Six thousand. Seven. Eight. Nine. Ten.

The excess force poured downward through the connection he’d established with the leaf itself, the golden surface bowing under the transferred weight. He was just standing on it, but the leaf was sinking.

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMURS]

“Look — the leaf! It’s going down!”

“He’s not even moving. How is he doing that?”

“What in the world is happening?”

Eleven thousand. Twelve. Thirteen. Fourteen.

Every single Dragon Scale was lit. Four thousand in his body. Ten thousand transferred directly into the leaf. The golden surface was pressed nearly to its limit, trembling under the invisible weight.

I’ll hit it again, Zane thought. 

If this still wasn’t enough, he had a fallback — ask Fiona to purify him again and go back to the armory for more Celestial Essence. But he didn’t think it would come to that.

[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]

He leaned down, slamming both fists onto the golden leaf.

[SFX: CRACKING METAL]

A faint crack split across the pale surface.

A trace of a smile crossed Zane’s face.

[AMBIENT: ERUPTING CROWD]

“He did it!” someone shouted. “The leaf that hasn’t broken in ten thousand years — he actually cracked it!”

“How is that possible? How is he this strong?”

“It’s just a pickax, but it’s still a divine-class weapon — do you understand what that means?”

“This is going to be the talk of all four kingdoms.”

The saint-level geniuses stared in silence. Almost all of them — even those who had been cheering Zane on moments before — felt it at the same time: a cold, involuntary knot of envy. They couldn’t help it. The ones who had wanted him to succeed suddenly found themselves hoping the leaf would hold.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

A second crack splintered across the surface, branching off from the first one. The initial break had been the hardest part; now, that fracture was a glaring weakness. Zane didn’t let up, hammering that same spot again and again, determined to shatter it completely.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACTS]

His fists rained down, each powerful strike sending a fresh web of fractures across the leaf.

Seeing the cracks spreading like lightning, Zane narrowed his eyes. A final surge of Vitality flooded his trembling fists, erupting into a whirlwind of starlight that engulfed his entire body. He gritted his teeth, pulling back for one last strike.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

“Star Shakes the Earth! Smash to pieces!”

[SFX: BREAKING]

The sound that followed was sharp, final, and unmistakable—the sound of something breaking for good. The golden leaf beneath Zane’s feet split cleanly in two, its stems snapping apart.

[AMBIENT: QUIET AFTERMATH]

[SFX: TINKLING FRAGMENTS]

As the thousands of glittering fragments rained down, the black star pickax was finally revealed, lying silently in the aftermath.
