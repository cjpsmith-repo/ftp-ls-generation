Ep 985 - Falling Books
Word count: 1049
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT LIBRARY]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STRINGS]
Zane scowled as the Eternal Scribe laughed at his question.
“What’s so funny?” Zane asked, confused.
Saul, standing beside him, grunted impatiently. “Don’t mind him. The old bookworm’s just read too many stories. He loves the drama.”
The Eternal Scribe just snorted at Saul, not even dignifying that with a response. Instead, he explained to Zane, “There’s no such thing as a ‘good’ or ‘bad’ attunement handbook. The kind of Transcendent Divine Might you’re describing would have to be tied to the Law of Causality. Handbooks like that are extremely rare, but yes... we have a few.”
Zane took that in, then turned his thoughts inward. “Is all Transcendent Divine Might related to the Law of Causality?” he asked the Cobalt Dragon.
Zane had only begun to grasp the true power of the Law of Causality after he’d acquired the Black Star. He was still practicing the Stellar Body back then—and hadn’t even finished the first half. He had no clue if the Stellar Body was connected to the Law of Causality at all.
Inside the Elysian Keep, the Cobalt Dragon had been stunned into silence by the sheer scale of the place. But Zane’s question snapped him out of it. “Yeah, you could say that,” the dragon replied. “The Law of Causality is woven into most forms of Transcendent Divine Might. But I only have the first half of the Stellar Body. If the Law of Causality is part of it, it must be hidden in the second half.”
“I see,” Zane murmured.
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
The Eternal Scribe shot Saul an annoyed glance before turning back to Zane with a warm smile. “I know you want a handbook that’s right for you, but there are simply too many to choose from. The founder of The Elysian Keep built this archive to guide future generations, and he entrusted me with the method to find the perfect handbook for any warrior.”
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT LIBRARY]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL STRINGS]
With that, he led them down another aisle of towering bookshelves. Glancing back at Tia and Isla, he said, “The handbooks in this row are attuned to female warriors. You two, come over here.”
Tia and Isla both looked to Zane, who gave them a quick nod. Only then did they follow the Scribe.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
The Eternal Scribe pointed to the floor, where a circle of light materialized. A thin beam shot out from its edge, pulsing softly. “Who wants to go first?”
Tia’s eyes lit up, and she immediately stepped into the glowing circle.
[SFX: RUMBLING EARTHQUAKE]
The moment she was inside, the entire bookshelf began to shake violently, trembling as if it were caught in an earthquake.
[SFX: LOUD BANG]
A book descended from high on the shelf, drifting slowly down until it settled in front of Tia.
The Eternal Scribe looked it over and nodded with quiet approval. “Your luck is good,” he said. “That handbook contains the Law of Causality at its later stages — a genuine Transcendent Divine Might.” He paused. “The Goddess Path you’ve been cultivating is incomplete, but it wasn’t wasted. It laid exactly the right foundation for this.”
Tia said nothing. She just looked at the book.
“Go on,” the Scribe said. “Read it.”
She poured her perception in.
[MUSIC: SOFT AWE]
When she came back, she stood still for a moment. Then she grabbed Zane’s hand.
She tried to speak. Couldn’t. Tried again.
As Cloud Hall master, she had faced wars, betrayals, near-death, and loss without flinching. But right now she couldn’t find the words.
Zane watched her with growing curiosity. What was in that book?
After a long moment, Tia finally found her voice. “Husband,” she said, and there was something new in her expression — fierce and bright at the same time. “If I cultivate this method… I might surpass you.”
Zane laughed. “Then I’ll have to work harder.”
[MUSIC: GENTLE TRANSITION]
While Tia was still finding her footing, Isla stepped into the circle.
The bookshelf rumbled again. A book descended beside her — a wind-attribute cultivation method, its pages faintly stirring as if touched by a breeze.
Isla’s expression was calm. But her eyes were steady in a way that meant she had already made up her mind. She knew she couldn’t match the resources or the history of the Heartlands. She just intended to close the gap by other means.
She picked up the book without a word.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED STRINGS]
Once both women had their handbooks, the Eternal Scribe turned and led the group further into The Archives. “The women’s row won’t suit you,” he said to Zane without looking back. “Come.”
He stopped at a different row of shelves and drew another circle of light on the floor.
Zane stepped in. The bookshelf shuddered.
“Not bad,” the Eternal Scribe said, his focus already shifting to Zane as a different book slid forward from the shelves. “It looks like the Heaven and Earth Sword Skill has chosen you.”
[SFX: HEAVY SLIDING STONE]
He was cut off as another large book slowly began to move, pushing its way out from the shelf right next to the first one.
“Huh?”
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Both Saul and the Eternal Scribe looked stunned.
There weren’t any rules against choosing more than one book from the archives; Zane was free to take a few more if he wanted. But the Scribe’s whole purpose was to find the one most suitable handbook for a warrior. Normally, only one would present itself. Now, it seemed two handbooks were equally right for Zane.
“It’s rare for two handbooks to appear at once, but I’ve seen it happen,” the Eternal Scribe said, his surprise melting into a faint, intrigued smile.
[SFX: BOOK SLIDING]
But just as he spoke, a third book on the shelf started to slowly inch its way out, looking like it was about to join the others.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
“A third one!” The Scribe’s face was now a mask of pure shock.
Saul frowned. “Is there something wrong with your little trick?” he demanded. “Why are three of them showing up at the same time?”
The Scribe’s method was supposed to pinpoint a warrior’s single best match. Two was a rare anomaly. But three at once? Saul figured the old man’s magic must be on the fritz.
[SFX: MULTIPLE BOOKS SLIDING]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS CRESCENDO]
As if in response to his doubt, more books began to slide out. A fourth. A fifth. Then a sixth.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
Seeing what was happening, a flicker of genuine fear crossed the Eternal Scribe’s face. “Back up!”
