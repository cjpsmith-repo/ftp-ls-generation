Ep 254 - A Reasonable Foe
Word count: 1087
[MUSIC: INTENSE TOURNAMENT THEME]
The tournament raged on, weeding out the weaker students and leaving only those with real power to advance.
Zane was already halfway through, but he had yet to face a truly challenging opponent.
Along the way, two students had made the mistake of underestimating him—one from Sea Peak and the other from Blackrock Peak. Both were at the Peak of the Adept Realm, skilled fighters handpicked for the prestigious All Peaks Competition.
They figured Zane's victory over Cecil was a fluke and that they could take him down easily.
[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCHES]
But the fight didn’t go anything like they’d imagined. Zane's fists were a relentless storm, giving them no room to breathe, let alone counter. By the end, they were both sprawled on the arena floor, utterly spent.
[SFX: CROWD GASPS]
A stunned silence fell over the crowd after each match. No one knew what to make of Zane's strength. They had no idea what realm he was in, but they were sure the only person who seemed to be in his league was Finn.
From then on, the tournament became a cakewalk for Zane. Most of his opponents would step into the arena, trade a few blows, and then simply give up. A few didn't even bother showing up, forfeiting from the start. They all recognized the chasm between their strength and his, and none of them wanted to get seriously hurt. For Zane, the competition was starting to feel more like a formality.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
As the tournament progressed, the true power of the prodigies from the Thirty-Three Peaks was slowly being put on display.
So far, Zane was sitting on an incredible eleven-win streak with zero losses. A few of his matches—especially against Mack and Cecil—had the whole crowd on their feet, roaring with excitement.
But despite Zane’s impressive run, almost nobody believed he stood a chance against Richard.
That sentiment was cemented when the final payout odds were announced. A bet on Zane to beat Richard was a one-to-nine payout. A bet on Richard to win was one-to-one. The crowd booed at the lopsided odds; the outcome felt like a foregone conclusion. Almost every bet was placed on Richard.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD NOISE]
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
After the referee's announcement, Zane prepared to step into the arena.
Master Warren, sitting beside him, looked tense as the match drew near. Just as he was about to launch into another warning, Zane cut him off with an awkward smile. "Master Warren, please, you’re smothering me."
Master Warren was a natural worrier, constantly fussing over Zane like a mother hen with the same old speech. Zane could recite it by heart: "You have to protect yourself. It doesn’t matter if you lose. If you think you can’t beat him, just forfeit. You’ve already made it to the next round anyway."
Master Warren managed a faint smile before his expression turned serious again. "Don’t forget you hit that student from Blackrock Peak, Mack, in the last round. They’re definitely holding a grudge. I’m afraid Richard is planning to get revenge. Please, be careful," he urged.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
But the warning didn’t faze him one bit. Zane's hands tightened into fists. "Don’t worry about me, Master Warren. No matter what, I’m giving this everything I’ve got. I’d rather lose fighting my hardest than just quit."
Seeing Zane's resolve, Master Warren didn’t press the issue. He just nodded.
Even though Zane had improved by leaps and bounds, Master Warren couldn’t help but fear that his stubbornness would do more harm than good. Richard was, after all, the strongest opponent Zane had ever faced. He didn’t want Zane to burn himself out on this one fight and lose his motivation for future training. As far as Master Warren was concerned, Zane’s journey was just beginning.
Still, he couldn’t bring himself to talk Zane into forfeiting. He had to admit, there was something unbelievable about this tournament, about Zane beating one elite student after another.
He might not be the strongest, but he’s absolutely the most persistent, Master Warren thought. Having seen his share of tournaments, he knew better than anyone that the ultimate victor wasn’t always decided by raw power, but by grit and willpower. A fighter with both strength and an unbreakable spirit was truly unbeatable.
"I’m sorry for worrying so much," Master Warren said, his voice softer now. "You’re right. You have to give it your best, no matter what happens. And I believe you won’t let yourself down."
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
Zane nodded with a small smile. Then he turned toward the arena, his eyes blazing with focus, and walked toward the stage.
[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT ARENA]
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Among the small number of people who gathered at the edge of the arena, whispers circulated about Zane’s chances against Richard. Most spectators had already dismissed Misty Peak’s rising star, expecting his winning streak to end dramatically today.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
Richard approached Zane before the match began, his expression serious but not hostile. "You should forfeit.”
Zane raised an eyebrow. "Why would I do that?"
"Listen, I need to tell you something," Richard said, keeping his voice low. "Master Young has instructed me to take you down within the first minute and burn you."
Zane turned his gaze back to Richard, a smirk playing on his lips. "Yeah, I was pretty much expecting it. But why the heads-up? Wouldn’t it be more fun to just beat me up without the warning?" he asked, a mocking glint in his eye.
Richard sighed. "That’s not it. I’m telling you because I don’t think what happened was your fault, or Eliot’s. Mack was out of line. He hit Eliot first. You hit him back. As far as I’m concerned, that’s a fair trade."
Richard’s words threw Zane for a loop. He’d had no idea his opponent was so reasonable. After a moment, Zane asked, "But you’re still going to get even for your friend Mack, right? Is that what this fight is about?"
"Honestly? Yes. But it’s more Master Young’s idea than mine," Richard admitted, a helpless look crossing his face. "And between you and me, I’m no fan of Mack’s either."
It was true. Mack was a cruel, underhanded guy, and someone as humble and straightforward as Richard would never choose him as a friend.
Richard was the type to live a simple life, learning all he could from books and experience, and treating others with respect. He would never bully someone weaker than him. Getting revenge for Mack was clearly an insult to his character.
But he was under orders to take Zane down, and he had no choice but to follow through.
