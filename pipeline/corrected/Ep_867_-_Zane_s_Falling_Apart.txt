Ep 867 - Zane’s Falling Apart

Word count: 1066

[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The crowd murmured in confusion, their voices a ripple of concern across the floating leaves.

“How did Zane get hurt? I didn’t see anyone attack him!”

“Something’s wrong. What’s going on with him?”

“I’ve seen this before,” one of the older warriors said, his voice grim. “It happens when you absorb more power than your body can handle. But Zane is so strong… what kind of power could do this to him?”

Zane gasped for air, his face a stony mask, giving nothing away even as his body threatened to collapse. 

If he kept pushing the power of the Dragon Scales any further, he knew the intense pressure might just tear him apart. Had five thousand Dragon Scales really pushed him to his absolute limit? He struggled to take a single step forward, and even in the painless Serene Void, the sight was horrifying.

For a split second, he actually wondered if he could activate six thousand. The doubt flickered across his face before he immediately shut the thought down.

[SFX: BLOOD SPLATTER]

Just taking that one extra step had caused blood to lance from his skin in three or four different places.

Okay, five thousand it is, he thought. He was at his limit, and he wasn’t stupid enough to try for six. If his body gave out here, no one could save him.

Zane planted his feet, digging his toes into the golden leaf and tightening his core until he was rooted to the spot directly over the trapped pickax. Slowly, deliberately, he clenched his fists.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

The power thrumming inside him surged into his arms. Veins bulged along his forearms like coiled snakes as more streams of blood erupted from his skin, shredding the sleeves of his shirt in an instant. Still, he gritted his teeth and began to raise his fist. 

He didn’t dare use his Vitality or any other source of energy; his body was already stretched far beyond its breaking point. One tiny bit of additional force could be enough to make him completely unravel, maybe even explode.

Time to let it all go.

[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]

[AMBIENT: RUMBLING EARTH]

His fist slammed down toward the pale golden leaf with incredible force. The punch wasn’t fast—with this much raw power behind it, he didn’t need speed. As his knuckles neared the surface, the immense energy contained in his body began to pour into the leaf, and his swollen physique started to shrink, deflating back to its normal size.

[SFX: DEEP RESONANT CLANG]

The leaf didn’t shatter. It sank — a full ten feet straight down, driven by the force of the blow. Then, like a bowstring pulled to its limit and released, it snapped back.

Zane was launched into the air.

[SFX: BODY TUMBLING]

[AMBIENT: SHAKING TREE]

The shockwave tore through the entire mining tree. Warriors perched on the edges of nearby leaves lost their footing as the vibration rolled through the wood beneath them. One by one, they slipped, tumbling off the leaves and disappearing into the portals below.

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD GASPS]

“He failed,” someone said. “But that power — I’ve never seen anything like it. I don’t think even a Soul Sea master hits that hard.”

“What a shame.” Even a few of Fredrick’s people shook their heads. Whatever their feelings about Zane, no one could watch that and not feel a twinge of disappointment.

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

Zane landed back on the golden leaf and crouched there, panting. He’d dropped out of the Serene Void. The pain came flooding back — a full-body roar of damage from every muscle and joint at once.

He’d failed. Five thousand Dragon Scales, and the leaf was still intact.

[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE STRINGS]

He sat, breathing hard. The leaf hadn’t shattered because the root absorbed the impact—its elasticity bent under force, then redirected everything back. Even ten thousand Dragon Scales might fail against that mechanism. He had fourteen thousand lit, but his body couldn’t channel them.

He was stuck.

This was what it took. To see Elara again, he had to train tens of thousands of times harder than anyone else. That was his goal. He knew that the slow and steady path would take a lifetime. He had plenty of time to grow, but he couldn’t afford to waste a single second of it. He had to get stronger, faster, tackling every impossible challenge until he could finally stand before that man again.

And that was why he couldn’t give up on the Black Star pickax.

“Give up, Zane.” Randy’s voice drifted up from two floors below. He’d been watching from the thirty-third leaf and had seen everything clearly. “I’ll help you get the Ancient Heartstones. There are still some scattered across the four kingdoms — we’ll track them down, I promise.”

Before Zane could respond, Fiona’s voice came from above — she was on the thirty-sixth leaf, legs folded neatly, watching him with quiet concern. She channeled her Vitality to carry her words across the distance. “My father has some Ancient Heartstones. If we pool what we have, I think we can reach your goal. How many do you still need?”

“Zane.” Camilla’s voice was the last to reach him. She was sitting on the thirty-second leaf, looking up. “Eight more. I just found eight more.”

Zane straightened slowly. The warmth he felt at their words was genuine.

“Thank you,” Zane said quietly, channeling the last of his Vitality into his voice. It was for Randy, Fiona, and Camilla, for their kindness in offering their help.

[MUSIC: MAGICAL CONTEMPLATION]

He then sat down cross-legged and closed his eyes, his mind racing to figure out a new way to break the leaf. His body couldn’t handle any more power. It felt like an impossible situation.

Time ticked by. On the other leaves, the warriors watched him. The scene was static, almost boring—just a man sitting silently—but every fighter there seemed to possess an endless well of patience. It felt like the long, quiet pause before a story’s grand finale; the crowd held its breath, waiting to see what he would do.

“There’s no way,” someone finally whispered. “He can’t do it.”

“For over ten thousand years, masters from the Soul Sea Realm have tried to solve this. They’ve tried every method imaginable, and not one of them ever succeeded.”

“Zane’s a rare talent, it’s true,” another added. “But every warrior who reaches the Soul Sea Realm is at the top of their game.”

[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]

Zane’s eyes were still closed. Whatever answer he was looking for, he hadn’t found it yet. But he hadn’t moved, either.
