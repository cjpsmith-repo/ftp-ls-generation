Ep 311 - Finals

[AMBIENT: CROWDED ARENA]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC FANFARE]
Zane stood dead center in the arena and, in front of hundreds of thousands of disciples from the Skyreach Sect, he waved at Elara. The gesture captured every spectator’s attention throughout the massive stadium.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
"Who’s that? Who’s he waving at?" a voice called out from the stands.
A ripple of curiosity swept across the audience like wildfire, heads turning at the same time as they all turned to follow his gaze.
Their collective gaze settled upon a veiled young woman standing in the crowd. She was a striking presence—tall and willowy, carrying herself with the effortless poise and delicate grace of a lotus blossom on still water.
Though her features were hidden behind the purple fabric, her bright, captivating eyes were enough to draw everyone in.
"Whoa, who is that girl? She’s absolutely stunning!" exclaimed one awestruck disciple, leaning forward for a better view.
"Never seen her before today," replied his companion, unable to look away. "Those eyes alone could hypnotize anybody. I’m sure that behind that veil she’s even more gorgeous."
A third disciple snorted dismissively. "Dream on, brother. A woman like that is probably just as powerful as the other top contenders, and just hiding her strength. She’s light years out of your league."
"Zane is so lucky," sighed another young man, shoulders slumping in defeat. "I wish I could meet a girl like that."
[SFX: CROWD MURMURING]
Whispers thick with envy and jealousy spread through the stands.
Feeling the weight of thousands of eyes on her, Elara felt a flutter of nerves. It had been a long time since she'd been in front of a crowd this massive.
From her spot on the sidelines, Ingrid glared at the beautiful, veiled woman.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
It had only cost one point to watch the match from the arena's edge—a price she considered well worth it. One point was nothing to her. But when she'd arrived, the first thing she saw was Zane waving at some woman.
Ingrid had never seen Zane with a woman. But ever since the All Peaks Competition started, he seemed to have a whole crowd of them around him.
She pouted, her annoyance impossible to hide.
She had no idea the veiled woman was Zane’s sister, Elara. Her jealousy was completely off the mark.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ENTRANCE]
While the disciples were still gossiping about the mysterious woman, Patrick entered the arena.
Patrick's calm eyes settled on Zane. "I appreciate the three days. Without the Immovable Guardian, I'm not sure I could beat you."
Zane drew the Streamer. "It wasn't generosity. My martial heart wouldn't let me fight you at half strength."
Patrick studied him for a moment. "I haven't met an ambition like yours in a long time."
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
In his last fight, he had summoned The Immovable Guardian to block Finn’s Sword of Celestial Judgment, a blade infused with Extreme Thunder Sword Intent.
The sheer power he'd displayed had left everyone stunned.
It was only then that they realized just how deep Patrick had been hiding his true strength. It was no exaggeration to say the Skyreach Sect was packed with hidden talent. Patrick was still so young, yet he commanded so much power as the reincarnation of The Immovable Guardian.
People were already saying his future could rival that of the Sect’s own leader, Kenneth.
As for Zane, the disciples had a bad feeling about his chances.
Even though Zane had also beaten Finn and possessed a sword spirit, he seemed completely outmatched. How could he possibly compete with Patrick, the reincarnation of The Immovable Guardian? The Philosophy of Inner Peace was a path of deep and complex secrets, and Patrick had used it to build an unshakable foundation. Zane was impressive, sure, but he just couldn’t compare, in either skill or raw power.
[SFX: SWORD DRAWN]
Zane attacked first. He knew Patrick's Buddhist techniques would grind him down if he let the fight come to him — Finn had learned that the hard way.
Patrick fired back with a finger strike that caught Streamer and knocked it wide. He expected Zane to recover the blade. Instead, Zane dropped it entirely and threw a punch.
[SFX: BONE-DEEP IMPACT]
The force sent him crashing into the barrier wall.
"I don’t know... I have a feeling Zane is going to win," one disciple muttered.
Another one nodded. "I’ve got the same feeling."
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Everyone knew the fight with Patrick would be intense, and a huge crowd had gathered to watch. The organizers had reinforced the light curtain ahead of time just to be safe. That’s why, even after the violent impact, the barrier only wobbled instead of shattering.
"Zane just sent him flying with a single punch! That’s a miracle!" a disciple yelled, his face a mask of shock.
"You didn’t bet on Patrick, did you? What can I say?" said the disciple who had predicted Zane’s win. "And this isn’t the first time. I bet Patrick’s about to get serious now, and I bet Zane will be ready for it."
"Zane is nothing but brute force. It’s way too early to call it.
Patrick hasn’t even used his real skills yet."
"But do we even know what Zane’s limits are? He hasn’t even used his Vitality."
[SFX: CROWD ARGUING]
More and more people jumped into the discussion, which quickly turned into a heated argument—a verbal battle raging in the stands.
Amid the bickering disciples, only one person remained completely calm: Zachary. With his hands clasped behind his back and a small smile on his face, he muttered, "Let them argue all they want. I’ve already made enough money."
For the final round, Zachary hadn’t bet on either of them. He had put his money on Zane in every single match leading up to this, and he’d won every time. His winnings were now massive—so massive, in fact, that the bookie he’d been betting with had gone completely bankrupt.
After that, nobody else would dare take a bet from Zachary if he put his money on Zane. No one knew if Zane’s winning streak would hold. If Zane lost, they’d be relieved. But if he won again, they’d go bankrupt too, thanks to the mountain of cash Zachary was willing to wager.
So, Zachary didn’t really care who won this match. He’d already made a fortune. He was probably the only person in the entire Skyreach Sect who’d gotten rich off the All Peaks Competition. He owed it all to Zane, so, in his heart, he genuinely hoped Zane would pull off another miracle.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
As Patrick pushed himself up from the arena floor, a dark, serious look hardened his features. The sting of humiliation was sharper than any blade. He had never been so embarrassed in his entire life.
