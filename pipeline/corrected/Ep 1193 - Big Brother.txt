Ep 1193 - Big Brother

Word count: 1049

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]
Zane was a blur of motion, his sword a spinning wheel of light as he tore through the air. 
[SFX: SWORD SLICING THROUGH AIR]
[SFX: RAPID COMBAT STRIKES]
The elite warriors of the Sacred Race were supposed to be formidable, each with their own unique fighting style, but Zane cut them down without even breaking a sweat. In just five minutes, he’d already cleared nearly half the true path of the Elysian Keep.
[AMBIENT: QUIET ENTRANCE HALL]
At the entrance, Freya, Livingston, Lavender, and Saul watched in stunned silence.
“His training is… coming along,” Saul said, his voice laced with understatement. “Two years ago, he was barely at the first level of the Life-and-Death Realm.” And even then, Saul remembered, Zane hadn’t cleared the second stage on his own strength.
Freya shot him a sharp look. In two years, Zane had jumped an entire realm. All Saul could say was his training was “coming along”? The kid’s progress was beyond unbelievable. She knew firsthand what it took to break through that level—it had cost her a fortune that could have funded a small nation.
Lavender nodded in agreement. “He’s fast, no doubt about it. But the Illusion Battlefield is about to open, isn’t it? He’ll need to get even stronger before he goes in.”
Livingston, however, was just frozen, his eyes glued to Zane’s figure carving a path through the enemy. He couldn’t have cared less about how much Zane had improved, or how much further he had to go. All he saw was the Armory in the Elysian Keep getting closer and closer.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL ENERGY SWIRLING]
In a matter of moments, Zane had dispatched the last of the warriors. But then, something strange happened. 
[SFX: ENERGY DISSIPATING]
The swirling Dark and Light Energies that made up the path didn’t reform into new enemies.
For the first time, Zane stood alone.
*This is too easy,* he thought, a flicker of doubt crossing his mind. *Why is the third stage so simple?*
Still, he wasn’t about to waste the opportunity. 
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
With no one left to fight, he broke into a run, putting as much distance behind him as he could.
The peace didn’t last. 
[AMBIENT: SWIRLING ENERGIES]
After a few hundred feet, the energies began to swirl again, this time converging on a single point. 
[SFX: MAGICAL COALESCENCE]
Slowly, the wisps of dark and light coalesced into the shape of a person.
The shape finished forming — and then it ran.
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS CLOSING FAST]
Zane’s eyes narrowed. Whoever this was, they were moving fast — faster than he was. He kept his grip on Thundergust and held his ground. At thirty feet out, he made his move.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[SFX: RAPID PROJECTILES CUTTING AIR]
Seven Nether God Shadows erupted from him and shot toward the oncoming figure.
A laugh rang out — easy, unbothered.
[SFX: SHARP BODY MOVEMENT]
The figure twisted slightly in mid-stride. Zane blinked — for just an instant, his vision blurred — and all seven shadows passed harmlessly through empty air.
“What kind of movement is that?” Zane’s eyes flashed. He raised Thundergust and brought it down toward the figure’s head.
[SFX: SWORD HISS]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS CONFRONTATION]
“Wait, don’t rush,” the young man said, raising a hand — and with the lightest flick of his fingers, he tapped Thundergust’s flat.
[SFX: METALLIC DING]
[SFX: SWORD VIBRATION]
[SFX: WEAPON CLATTER]
A violent tremor ripped up the blade and tore it straight from Zane’s grasp. Zane let it go without chasing it and stepped back, sword tip down, watching.
“Who are you?” he asked.
Saul’s voice drifted in from the entrance of the path, calm as ever.
“His name is Gus. Same master as you — the Master of the Keep took him on first.”
Zane looked at the young man with new eyes. In the world of cultivation, that made Gus a sort of big brother — a fellow disciple, not a blood one. And the Master of the Elysian Keep, as far as Zane knew, had never taken on anyone else.
Gus’s smile deepened. He looked Zane over with something between amusement and disbelief. “Master actually took another disciple. I didn’t think that was possible.” Then a thought crossed his face — and his expression sharpened. “Wait. Master swore he would only take a new disciple if they could cultivate the Primordial Chaos Tactic.” He studied Zane. “Don’t tell me you’ve actually cultivated it.”
“There’s no need to tell you,” Zane said.
Gus laughed. He put his hands together and began walking forward, slow and easy. “Interesting. Little bro is very arrogant.” He stopped a few feet away. “I may just be a wisp of soul energy made of Dark and Light, and I may only be at the Soul Sea Realm — but I don’t think you’ll be getting past me. So. Salute your big brother, answer my question, and I’ll let you go without a scratch.”
“That sounds like a great deal,” Zane said, a humorless smile touching his lips. “But I’m not interested.”
The words were barely out of his mouth before his sword, Thundergust, flashed forward — and Gus sent it spinning from his grip with the same lazy flick as before. Zane didn’t chase it.
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
[SFX: FIST WHOOSH]
Instead, he channeled the power of ten thousand Dragon Scales into his fists and threw two lightning-fast punches straight at Gus’s chest.
“Hey now, Zane. Get this straight,” Gus chuckled, raising a casual hand to block the attack. “I’m from the Sacred Race. The power gap between us is—”
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[SFX: BODY CRASH]
[SFX: GROUND SLAM]
He wasn’t taking the attack seriously. It wasn’t that Gus looked down on Zane, exactly. It was a simple fact: the Sacred Race was a perfect lineage, the origin of all primates. Humans were just an offshoot, inheriting only a fraction of their strength.
But Gus never could have anticipated the raw, explosive power packed into Zane’s fists—not from a mere Soul Sea Realm warrior. Before he could even finish his sentence, the two impacts slammed into him and sent him flying. 
He shot backward for a thousand feet before crashing hard against the ground.
