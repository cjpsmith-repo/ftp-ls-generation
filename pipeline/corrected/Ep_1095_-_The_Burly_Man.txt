Ep 1095 - The Burly Man

Word count: 999

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

The warriors facing Layla never stood a chance. Her sword was a relentless blur, tearing through them until they were ejected from the Illusionary Arena, vanishing one by one.

As a surge of energy coursed through her, a warm, white mist rose from her skin, instantly drying her damp clothes. She ran a hand through her impossibly long hair, and the mist followed, clinging to the shimmering locks all the way to the ends.

But Layla didn’t look happy. Even in the heat of battle, she’d noticed Zane. He had cleared the twelfth round long before she had. Now, he was just standing there, watching her with a knowing look, Thundergust cradled in his arms.

She shot him a glare. “Don’t get cocky! I’ll crush you in the next round!”

Zane just gave a small, noncommittal smile and silently started the thirteenth round.

[AMBIENT: CROWDED SQUARE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Adrian had already sent three groups of fighters to deal with them, but right now, he had more important things to do. While Zane and Layla fought, he moved through the crowd in the enormous square, heading toward a set of large cages.

[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]

There were seven of them, all set on a high platform. Beating the final challenge of the Passing the Torch event meant opening these cages and freeing the prisoners inside.

The cages were huge, and the prisoners within were radiating a brutal energy you could feel from a mile away. Most of the warriors in the training wouldn’t dare get near them. One of them, a burly, powerfully built man, seemed to be the strongest of the group. He hadn’t opened his eyes once.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Adrian flew onto the platform, making a beeline for the cage in the center.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

The burly man was sitting inside, eyes still closed. “Who are you?” he asked, sensing Adrian’s approach.

“My name is Adrian Rowe,” Adrian answered with a smile.

“What do you want?” the man’s voice was gruff.

Adrian leaned in close to the bars and whispered, “I’m looking for something. It’s about the secret treasure of the Dark World.”

[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]

Before Adrian could even finish his sentence, the man’s eyes snapped open, and he shot to his feet.

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY BLAST]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC IMPACT]

The moment they opened, a piercing force slammed into Adrian, rattling his very soul. He trembled and collapsed, his face turning deathly pale as he coughed up a mouthful of blood.

“You’re a nobody!” the tall man growled. “How could you possibly know about the secret treasure of the Dark World?”

Even as he wiped the blood from his lips, a faint smile played on Adrian’s face. “I’m a nobody, sure,” he said. “But you’re not exactly somebody either — not anymore.”

The burly man’s eyes narrowed.

“Once, maybe,” Adrian went on. “Gossamer Pavilion doesn’t take just anyone. But now you’re a death-row prisoner of the Villain World. Eight hundred years left, and that’s it.”

“Say what you came to say,” the man growled.

“Every prisoner here knows the deal,” Adrian said. “Ambush one of the Thirteen Palaces geniuses, win, and you walk free. Problem is, you rarely get the chance — not against anyone who’d actually test you.”

“And?”

“There are only two contestants left in the whole trial,” Adrian said. “And I happen to be the one deciding which group goes up next. I can put you on that platform. And I can tell you exactly how to beat whoever you’re up against.”

The man went quiet for a long moment. “...So that’s why you came to me.”

“Mm-hm.” Adrian’s smile widened. “In return — when you find what you’re looking for in the Dark World, I want thirty percent.”

“Thirty percent?” The man let out a short, disbelieving laugh. “Do you have any idea what that treasure actually is?”

“I do,” Adrian said simply. “That’s why thirty’s enough.”

The man closed his eyes, and for a moment, something like exhaustion crossed his face. A tiger that’s fallen on hard times gets pushed around by dogs — and here he was, his fate in the hands of some Life-and-Death Realm nobody.

“Everyone’s useful for something,” Adrian said quietly, reading the look. “Strength matters most out here, sure. But it’s not the only thing that does. With me, getting what you want just got a lot easier.”

The man opened his eyes again, studied him for a long moment, and finally exhaled. “...Fine. Agreed.”

[AMBIENT: TRAINING ARENA]

[SFX: CROWD CHATTER]

He found himself surrounded by warriors, all buzzing about the fight. “Adrian,” one of them yelled, “we can’t touch that woman! How are we supposed to counter her moves?”

“The third group is about to get wiped out!” another shouted. “She’s too strong! You have to give us a plan!”

A faint smile touched Adrian’s lips. “It’s fine,” he said, his tone serious. “The next group will…”

Truthfully, he had no interest in attacking Layla and Zane right now. But to keep these eager fighters motivated, he had to give them something.

[SFX: CROWD ROAR]

After laying out a detailed strategy, he was met with a roar of approval. He picked warriors for the fourth group and sent them into the Illusionary Arena.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD ROAR]

The fourteenth round.

The fifteenth round.

The sixteenth round.

[MUSIC: AWE-INSPIRING ORCHESTRAL SWELL]

Inside the crucible, the warriors from the Thirteen Palaces watched, stunned. The power Zane and Layla were showing was beyond anything they had imagined. Even warriors who had previously cleared nineteen rounds probably wouldn’t last this long in the current fights.

But Zane and Layla weren’t ordinary. Though each round grew harder, they continued to dominate their opponents, clearing every test on their own.

[MUSIC: SUBTLE TENSION STRINGS]

Still, a small gap was opening between them.

Zane was still just using his Thundergust, showing no sign of needing another weapon.

[SFX: STRATEGIC THINKING PULSE]

Layla, on the other hand, had already been forced to play several of her trump cards. The problem was that every time she unveiled a new skill, her opponents would analyze and counter it, making the move useless in the next round. Her options were dwindling.

But she still had one advantage: an arsenal of fighting styles so vast it was almost unimaginable.
