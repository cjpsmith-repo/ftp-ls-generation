Ep 504 - Heartstone

[AMBIENT: QUIET WORKSHOP]
[MUSIC: FOCUSED CONCENTRATION]
The tip of the runic quill danced across the polished surface. Even for a simple three-star rune, Zane's execution was breathtaking—each stroke flowing into the next without hesitation, as though his hand knew the pattern before his mind could command it.
It was a known fact among cultivators: a runic master's true strength didn't lie in their power level, but in their soul force, which far outstripped that of any warrior at the same cultivation stage. The soul was the wellspring from which all rune-crafting flowed, and a weak soul could never sustain the intricate work it required.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
Kieran and the attendants watched in stunned silence as Zane completed the first rune in a single, fluid motion. The young man's face showed no strain, no tremor of exhaustion—only calm focus. For someone barely eighteen, such composure bordered on unnatural.
This kid's soul force is off the charts, Kieran thought, his merchant's instincts already calculating the potential value of this discovery. He shot a subtle wink at an attendant from the World Merchants' Guild standing beside him, a silent command passing between them.
[SFX: JADE BOX OPENING]
The attendant caught the signal and immediately opened a small jade box, revealing a lustrous black pill nestled within. He extended it toward Zane with both hands in a gesture of respect.
"What's this?" Zane asked, his gaze shifting from the rune he'd just completed to the offered box.
"A top-grade Soul-Nurturing Elixir," the attendant replied, his tone carefully polite. "It should help restore your energy. Drawing a rune of that caliber must have taxed your reserves considerably."
Zane grinned. "No thanks. Barely broke a sweat."
The attendant nodded and withdrew, the jade box disappearing into his robes.
The truth was far more complex than Zane's casual dismissal suggested. Ever since his soul had crossed the threshold into the Fighting Soul Realm, he'd felt it expanding, strengthening with each passing day. The sensation was subtle but unmistakable—like a muscle growing more resilient through constant use. He estimated he could draw five hundred three-star runes in a row without needing a break. A Soul-Nurturing Elixir, no matter how refined, would be wasted on him.
Beyond that, there was a more practical concern. Even the finest elixirs left behind microscopic impurities that accumulated over time. Most cultivators accepted this as the price of rapid recovery, but Zane had grown cautious. The process of purifying his system afterward would consume hours that were better spent on actual cultivation or rune practice.
So he'd declined with a smile, keeping his reasoning to himself. No need to explain the intricacies of his cultivation to strangers, no matter how well-connected they might be.
[AMBIENT: WORKSHOP TENSION]
Kieran, meanwhile, shot a glare at the three so-called runic experts, cursing them under his breath. Those guys made a whole production out of drawing a single half-finished rune—bathing, washing their hands, burning incense, the whole nine yards—before downing a Soul-Nurturing Elixir, terrified of straining their precious souls in the slightest.
All that arrogance, and for what? The runes they produced were mediocre at best. But you wouldn't know it from their prices. They charged an outrageous, fixed rate—no discounts for the World Merchants' Guild or anyone else. The Runic Tower was making an absolute killing.
And even though the Runic Tower kicked back a portion of their profits to the Guild, Kieran couldn't stand the whole organization. Their entire way of doing business just rubbed him the wrong way.
Which was exactly why watching a real talent like Zane was so damn satisfying.
Finally, Kieran sneered to himself, someone to put these three old frauds in their place.
And he got his wish. The three runic experts, Nate included, couldn't even meet his gaze, their faces burning with shame. This teenager was drawing a three-star rune with an ease they could only dream of.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL WONDER]
[SFX: MAGICAL GLOW]
Zane worked with Cloud Powder—a fine dust of ancient bone and other rare ingredients—and as he drew, the patterns on the stone ignited with a golden light, revealing a divine, mysterious texture from within the material itself.
When Zane finished the four-star rune, Kieran let out a slow, satisfied breath.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT VICTORY]
"Nate," he called out, a wide, taunting smile on his face. "Looks like it's time to hang your name upside down on the wall!"
[AMBIENT: QUIET WORKSHOP TENSION]
Nate's eyes were fixed on the completed four-star rune. After a long, heavy silence, he finally choked out, "I'll do it the moment I get back."
Every expert at the Runic Tower had their name displayed in runes on their door; to hang it upside down was the ultimate public humiliation.
That ought to shut them up, Kieran scoffed to himself. About time they learned some humility.
[SFX: RUNE DRAWING]
Zane completed the second four-star rune quickly, then moved straight on to the third. His brush danced across the surface in fluid strokes, each movement deliberate and controlled. Attendants hovered nearby, their breath held as they watched the final lines take shape. When he finished, they stepped forward reverently, collecting all three runes as though handling ancient relics that might crumble at the slightest misstep.
Kieran approached, warmth radiating from his expression—a stark contrast to the cold formality he'd shown the other experts. "Mr. Lowell, you've drawn over a hundred runes now." Genuine concern colored his voice. "Are you certain you don't need to recharge your soul force? I know the elixirs available here carry their impurities, and prolonged use can damage your cultivation foundation." He paused, then reached into his robes. "As a bonus for your incredible work, I have something far superior: a Soul-Nurturing Heartstone."
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]
"A Soul-Nurturing Heartstone!"
The three experts gasped in unison. Nate's composure shattered completely, his mouth falling open. Raw, undisguised envy blazed in their eyes as they stared at the luminous stone now resting in Kieran's outstretched palm. The heartstone pulsed with a soft, ethereal glow that seemed to call to their very souls.
[MUSIC: BITTER ENVY]
They all knew the Guild possessed such a treasure—a legendary stone capable of strengthening and purifying a cultivator's soul. Nate and the others had practically prostrated themselves begging for access over the years, offering favors, promising exclusive rune designs, even pledging portions of their future earnings. Every single time, they'd been refused without exception. The heartstone was simply too precious, too rare to waste on anyone who hadn't proven themselves indispensable.
And now, to their utter disbelief and mounting resentment, Kieran was casually offering it to this seventeen-year-old kid.
Something was very wrong here.
