Ep 334 - Head Of The House
Word count: 1278

[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSE]
A terrified glint flashed in Ken’s eyes as he stared at the young woman before him. Though she appeared as delicate as a spring flower, her presence radiated danger that chilled him to the bone. He recognized that deep down, she was vicious as a demon.
Ken begged, his voice cracking with desperation as the reality of his trapped situation sank in. "Please, Elara, show mercy!" Sweat beaded on his forehead while his hands trembled slightly at his sides. "I’m your uncle, for heaven’s sake! Doesn’t family mean anything to you?"
Elara tilted her head slightly, her eyes cold and distant. "Are you under the impression that our blood connection gives you some special consideration? You think I’m some sweet little girl who’ll just roll over for you?" Her voice remained soft yet carried an edge sharp enough to slice through steel.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
Her mind flashed back a few years to when she lost her father. Even though she was young, the blow had been devastating. Glaring at her uncle, she coldly dismissed his pleas.
"Bryson was the one behind your father’s death! I had nothing to do with it!" Ken stammered.
Elara nodded. "That’s true. You couldn’t have killed my father. He was much stronger than you. But I need to know what really happened to him. So, no, I’m not letting you go."
The color drained from Ken’s face, his expression hardening as a dark, cunning glint entered his eyes.
"So that’s how it is," he whispered, his voice gaining strength with each word. "Fine. If you won’t let me leave, then you can just go to hell!"
Ken had earned his reputation as the most handsome man in House Lowell through years of careful cultivation. His fair skin, smooth as polished stone, made him appear much younger than his forty years. Women throughout the county had fallen for his charming smile and elegant demeanor.
But now, that handsome face had transformed into a twisted mask of deep lines, giving him a harsh, almost unrecognizable appearance. Desperation had stripped away his carefully maintained facade, revealing the true man beneath.
[SFX: METALLIC OBJECTS APPEARING]
With a flick of his wrist, three small, metallic boxes materialized in his palm.
[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]
[MUSIC: BATTLE THEME]
"Stormkill Halberd!" Ken roared, his voice trembling with both fear and determination.
When Ken’s oldest son, Andrew, had fought Zane, he’d used a weaker version of this very weapon—the Stormbreaker Halberd—in his failed attempt to kill him. Now here was Andrew’s father, facing off against Zane’s sister with the real deal.
About a century ago, an ancestor of House Lowell had gotten his hands the Stormkill Halberd, a rare, mechanical hidden weapon of extraordinary power. It contained only three charges—three individual halberds in total—each capable of devastating destruction. The weapon had remained untouched in the family vault for decades, its power so terrifying that no one in House Lowell had ever dared to use it. Ken wouldn’t have revealed such a precious heirloom unless he was truly desperate.
The Stormbreaker Halberd had just been a copy, but its incredible effectiveness only hinted at the true power of the original.
[SFX: METALLIC CLINKING]
[SFX: LIGHTNING EXPLOSION]
[SFX: STABBING SOUNDS]
A sharp metallic clang rang through the air as lightning erupted from the containers, twisting and coiling like a dragon before materializing into three gleaming halberds. They streaked toward Elara with deadly precision, piercing through her slender frame as though she were made of parchment.
Ken’s lip curled into a triumphant sneer, his eyes gleaming with malicious satisfaction. That’s it! he thought, savoring what he believed was his moment of victory. *Those halberds are inside you now!
They’ll tear you apart from the inside out! No one survives the Stormkill—you’re finished!* The acrid, bitter stench of what seemed to be burning flesh permeated the chamber, stinging Ken’s nostrils. He fixed his gaze on Elara, imagining the lightning raging through her body, scorching her veins and ripping through her internal organs. His heart raced with anticipation as he waited for her to crumple to the ground, convinced she would be obliterated at any moment.
But as he stood there, muscles tense and breath shallow, the seconds stretched into an uncomfortably long silence. Elara remained standing, the halberds still protruding from her body, her unsettling smile never wavering.
He kept waiting, but nothing happened. What’s the deal with these damn kids? Why are they like this?
[MUSIC: SCENE TRANSITION]
[AMBIENT: CROWD GATHERING]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT]
Two servants helped Zane's great-grandfather up onto the platform. The old man steadied himself, took two slow breaths, and looked out at the assembled family.
"Zane Lowell is the son of Milo Lowell," he said, his hoarse voice carrying across the hall. "Eldest son of the eldest branch. The position of head of this house is his by right. Bryson and Ken took what was not theirs — and they paid for it in blood." He paused. "I ask the family: is there any objection?"
[AMBIENT: CROWD AFFIRMATION]
There was none. The voices that rose were not objections but endorsements, one after another, until the whole hall was in agreement.
Zane stepped up onto the platform.
Later, Zane stood before the members of House Lowell. With Bryson defeated, a new era for the House was dawning. He made a promise to his people.
"If any of our property was damaged in the fighting today, House Lowell will cover it. And every family in this county that helped themselves to what was ours while we were weak — I will make them pay it back. Double."
[SFX: CROWD APPLAUSE]
The crowd erupted in thunderous applause. That one simple promise was enough to electrify everyone.
For years, they had been under Bryson’s thumb, bullied and pushed around by other clans. They’d been forced to be patient, to swallow their pride just to keep the peace, and the House had weakened for it.
But now, not only was the abuse over, they could finally get some payback.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
As the applause faded, Zane turned to Bryson, disgust etched on his face. "Now, Uncle Bryson," he demanded, "you’re going to tell me how my father died."
Bryson let out a weak, wheezing laugh. His shoulders shook as he lay on the floor, a sly, hateful sneer on his face. He spat on the ground.
"Why even ask? You’ve already made up your mind. So fine! I killed him!"
Zane shook his head firmly. "No. My father was in the Adept Realm. You were never strong enough to take him on alone. There was someone else."
"And you think I’m just going to tell you who it was?" Bryson taunted, a crooked grin spreading across his face. His eyes held a strange glint. "Promise me—right here, in front of everyone—that you won’t kill me. Then maybe, just maybe, I’ll talk."
Zane’s lips tightened into a thin line as he slowly shook his head.
"If I’m a dead man either way, why the hell would I tell you anything?" Bryson jeered.
Since Bryson wouldn’t talk, Zane’s gaze shifted to Ken, hoping he had the answer. But before Ken could say a word, Bryson shot him a warning glare. "Ken, if you tell him, he’ll kill you on the spot. So you keep your mouth shut."
At Bryson’s threat, Ken’s jaw clamped shut. He clearly wasn’t going to say a word.
[MUSIC: DARK THREATENING]
A cold smile touched Zane’s lips. "Do you really think I can’t make you talk?"
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY FORMING]
As he spoke, a translucent Psyche Spike materialized between his eyebrows and shot straight for Bryson.
