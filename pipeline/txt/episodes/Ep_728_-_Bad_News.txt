Ep 728 - Bad News
Word count: 1122
[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
For most warriors in the Life-and-Death Realm, condensing Vitality until it became liquid was a smooth, almost meditative process. But tonight, for Tia, it just wasn’t happening. Her mind was a complete mess, a storm of distractions that made it impossible to focus, and every attempt ended in failure.
She knew it was dangerous to keep pushing. With martial arts, one wrong move in this state could lead to a crippling injury, and most people would have the sense to stop until their head was clear.
But Tia was nothing if not persistent.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
One lapse in concentration was all it took. A sharp, searing pain jolted through her lower abdomen as a single strand of Vitality slipped from her control. The rogue wisp of energy shot off course, triggering a chain reaction that sent the rest of the Vitality swirling in her gut into a chaotic frenzy.
Tia forced herself to breathe, slow and steady, trying to rein it all in, but it was already too late. The energy surged upward uncontrollably, ripping right through her core.
[SFX: LIQUID SPLATTER]
The coppery taste of blood filled her mouth, and she spat a dark spray onto the pristine floor.
A wisp of Vitality had just torn through her core. That meant her abdomen was seriously damaged. The core was a warrior’s most vital and fragile center; even partial damage could be ruinous.
She swallowed a few pills, grimacing as she fought to regain control over the storm of energy inside her. Slowly, the pills began to work, and the violent churning started to subside, but her face remained ashen.
[AMBIENT: NIGHT WIND]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
Realizing she couldn’t focus, Tia gave up on cultivating for the night. She drifted over to the window and stared out at the bright moon hanging in a sky with only a few scattered stars. As she watched, one of them caught her eye—it was pulsing with a strange, orange glow.
“What kind of star is that?” she murmured, frowning. She could feel its light, a focused beam cutting across an impossible distance, aimed straight at Cloud Hall. It felt like someone here had somehow tapped into the star’s power, a feat she would have thought impossible. Astonished and intrigued, she decided to get a closer look.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
A flicker of movement, and she vanished from the ice palace.
[AMBIENT: MEDITATION CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Miles away, Zane wasn’t overthinking anything. His focus was singular: casting his consciousness out into the void, trying to communicate with the stars. He’d tried and failed more times than he could count since that very first, fluke success. The string of failures had started to wear on him, and he was beginning to understand that the Transcendent Divine Might technique was far more demanding than he’d ever imagined.
But tonight was different.
[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]
His consciousness finally broke through, connecting with a beautiful orange star that bathed his mind in a gentle, calming glow. Most importantly, it offered no resistance. With every new star he activated, he could feel the power of his Stellar Body grow stronger. He was still a long way from closing the gap between himself and Maddox, but this was a start.
The purple light that already faintly surrounded him when he cultivated now mingled with a second layer of orange, the two colors weaving together across his skin in shifting, iridescent patterns.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL STRINGS]
On the roof's edge, Tia watched from a distance. She'd suspected it was him the moment she saw the light moving. Now she was certain.
She looked at him for a long moment. Two years — and he'd crossed from War General to Spirit-Tribulation Realm. Whatever he'd been through out there had cost him. The thought moved her more than she wanted it to.
She wanted to go to him. She had so much to ask.
Then she thought of Cora, and the impulse died. She let out a quiet breath, turned, and disappeared back into the night.
[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]
[MUSIC: DARK DRAMATIC]
There were seven influential third-grade houses in Ethereal City: Langley, Hawthorne, Chambers, Sutton, Shaw, Carter, and Kensington.
The elders gathered in the meeting hall were already talking in low, troubled voices when Zane walked in. They went quiet the moment they saw Cora behind him.
Saint Morphen's eyes moved to her, then to Zane.
"Speak," Zane said.
Saint Morphen took a breath. "The Ethereal Spirit Sect has fallen. The Corrupted Divine Kingdom took it. The Septuplets were wiped out — Maddox and Simon together were more than Elder Xander could hold alone. The teleportation array was destroyed in the attack, which is why the news only reached us now."
Among all of them, House Kensington was preeminent. Cora couldn’t bring herself to ask about them directly. The thought of the noblest house in the land suffering the same fate as the others was too painful to even voice.
But Saint Morphen understood the unspoken question in her eyes and got straight to the point. “House Kensington was attacked as well,” he said, his voice grim. “From what I’ve heard, they were the main target. The Corrupted Divine Kingdom was determined to leave no one alive. Every last member of House Kensington has been slaughtered, and the estate has been burned to the ground.”
[MUSIC: TRAGIC STRINGS]
The words hit Cora like a physical blow. Her legs gave out, and she collapsed to her knees, the world dissolving around her. She stared into the distance with glassy eyes as tears began to stream down her face. Gone. It was all gone.
She could never have imagined that a powerhouse like the Ethereal Spirit Sect could be wiped out so completely. In her eyes, her great-grandfather, Xander, was invincible. The sect had stood for tens of thousands of years, known as the greatest Sect in the world, and now… it was just gone.
The elders of Cloud Hall could only watch, their faces etched with pity at the sight of the girl’s miserable, helpless expression. Just then, Tia swept into the hall.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
“I just received word,” she announced, her tone cold and sharp. “A group of survivors from House Kensington is on its way to Cloud Hall right now.”
“House Kensington?” Cora gasped, a flicker of hope instantly igniting in her empty eyes. “You mean… there are survivors?”
“Yes,” Tia confirmed. “They will arrive today. And I’ll require your cooperation.”
“Cooperation?” Cora asked, confused. “What do you mean?”
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS UNDERTONE]
Tia knew the truth would be cruel. It was better to let Cora see it for herself. “You’ll find out when they get here.”
