The Double Stars Brilliance sat in Zane's hands like a lit fuse, boiling and unstable, ready to tear itself apart. Forty feet away, the two yellow streams of Life and Death Wind drifted toward him — patient, inevitable, unbothered by anything he had done.

The Stellar Body was a technique of the Transcendent Divine Might, and it was a whole other level of difficulty compared to other combat arts.

The very concept—reaching out with your mind across the vastness of space—was enough to shut down most warriors before they even started. But if you could master it, you could channel starlight into a fight, weaving it with your own energy to unleash a power far beyond what any ordinary warrior could dream of.

Legend had it that the Supreme Lord Star himself was once unbeatable in the upper world, all thanks to this very technique.

[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BUILDUP]

 As Zane separated his hands, swirls of purple and orange starlight churned together. The two distinct stellar energies roiled like boiling water, forming a small ball of light between his palms. A faint, colorful mist seeped from the sphere, which looked like it could erupt at any moment.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING ENERGY]

 Back when he was training, he had only managed to mix a tiny, rice-sized speck of the two energies. Even then, the resulting explosion was almost impossible to control, and he’d nearly blown himself to pieces more than once. Now, he was holding a fist-sized ball of that same unstable power. If it blew up in his hands, he’d be nothing but ash.

All things considered, the success rate for the move—Double Stars Brilliance—was only about forty percent. Zane hadn't fully mastered it, which meant this was a massive gamble.

He carefully stabilized the volatile energy in his hands, his eyes locked on the two bright yellow streams of the Life and Death Wind closing in. A flash of determination crossed his face.

Then he threw it.

[SFX: ENERGY RELEASE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

The fist-sized ball of churning starlight streaked across the sky toward Maddox. For a single breath, time seemed to stop. The light dimmed. Every battle on the field went still. Every eye in Oracle City turned upward.

Zane and Maddox moved at the same instant — both their faces going pale.

Zane watched the Life and Death Wind keep coming, indifferent to the explosion he had just launched. It didn't slow. It didn't bend. It cut through the air like a pair of death gods walking toward him with all the patience in the world.

And Maddox — even transformed, even with a demon's body that could shrug off almost anything — saw what was in that ball. He felt the power inside it. And he ran.

[SFX: RAPID RETREAT — WIND RUSH]

He was already shooting backward before the thought had fully formed. That ball would kill him. He was certain of it.

[SFX: CATASTROPHIC DETONATION]

[MUSIC: SILENCE, THEN MASSIVE ORCHESTRAL HIT]

Three breaths later, the ball split open.

Two types of starlight — orange and purple — detonated against each other. A massive fireball erupted in the pitch-black sky, swallowing the space where Maddox had been standing. When the light cleared, a section of Oracle City's wall was simply gone — as if a giant hand had reached out of another dimension and erased it.

The shockwave hit a fraction of a second later. A dull, colossal sound rolled across the city. Every warrior on the field — friend and enemy alike — felt blood pour from their ears.

In the silence that followed, no one moved. They just stared.

Years later, warriors across The Heartlands would call this the Battle of Oracle City. Any cultivator who had been there would eventually come to this moment — the moment Zane threw a fist of starlight at the strongest man in The Heartlands and made him flinch. That moment became a legend. Time itself didn't seem to forget it.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DESCENT]

But the Life and Death Wind hadn't stopped.

It never stopped.

[SFX: SOFT, TERRIBLE IMPACT — TWO FAINT SOUNDS]

The two yellow streams reached Zane and Tia. They passed through everything — through his guard, through her defenses, through the air between them — as if none of it existed. There was no crack, no flash. Just a soft, quiet intrusion, barely a sound at all.

Tia's composure broke. The Head of Cloud Hall — the woman who had faced down an army without flinching — looked suddenly lost. Her face was full of panic, like a child who had wandered too far from home and didn't know the way back. She staggered forward and collapsed into Zane's arms.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

[SFX: HEARTBEAT SLOWING]

 Then, suddenly, the strength drained out of him. His Vitality vanished. A wave of exhaustion washed over Zane, so profound it stole the air from his lungs. He was just so tired. So sleepy. All he wanted was to curl up and let go.

He remembered his father, who had always wished for him an endless journey. Is this it? he thought. The end of the road?

[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL STRINGS]

 Zane struggled to keep his eyes open. He saw Tia lying in his arms, her face peaceful, as if she were asleep and lost in a beautiful dream. A strange sense of gratitude washed over him. Maybe this was a good way to go. A peaceful end, without any more pain.

Elara.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL RISING THEME]

 The name was a jolt of lightning to his system. A faint, weak rhythm fluttered in his chest, but it was a rhythm nonetheless. He was back from the brink. He was awake. He had to save Elara. He couldn't give up now.

 A flicker of Vitality surfaced from deep within him and surged through his energy conduits, pouring directly into his Storage Ring. The seal that had once locked it was broken, leaving it without an owner.

Lavender immediately pulled a small fragment from within the ring.
