Ep 322 - First Place
Word count: 1315
[SFX: SWORD CLASH]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC BATTLE]
Zane’s blade collided with Evan’s, unleashing a brilliant shower of sparks that illuminated their faces. The metallic ring echoed across the arena, sending a shiver through the spectators.
When Evan raised his sword for another strike, his eyes narrowing with frustrated intensity, Zane parried the attack with a flawless block.
Although it was a textbook move from basic swordsmanship, Zane’s execution had a precision that transformed the simple movement into something approaching artistry.
Evan’s jaw clenched in growing aggravation as he put more force behind his blade. The weight of his reputation as the Skyreach Sect’s premier disciple hung heavily on each increasingly desperate attack.
He swung again.
[SFX: SWORD CLASH]
And again.
[SFX: SWORD CLASH]
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD]
With each ringing clash of steel, the tension in the arena grew thicker.
The spectators collectively held their breath, eyes fixed on the duel where Evan, despite his superior cultivation, could not penetrate Zane’s defense.
Eventually, Evan groaned in frustration. "Come on, just stop blocking!
Please?" The once-proud arrogance had vanished from his face, replaced by a pathetic desperation that made several onlookers wince in secondhand embarrassment.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED CROWD]
A wave of disbelief rippled through the crowd, stunning them into silence. Many exchanged glances, appalled by the shameless plea from someone of Evan’s standing.
Zane remained stone-faced, his expression betraying nothing of his inner thoughts. He maintained his stance, holding his Streamer with a deceptively casual grip. Meeting Evan’s desperate gaze, he offered only a slow, deliberate shake of his head.
In that moment, the contrast between the two fighters couldn’t have been more stark—one unraveling, the other unshakable.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
Evan snarled, his entire demeanor shifting in a heartbeat. "Fine! If that’s how you want to play." The pleading was gone, replaced by a cold ruthlessness. "No more games. I’ll fight you for real!"
At the same time, his sword style changed completely.
[SFX: POWERFUL AURA SURGE]
The Sword of a Thousand Blossoms was a light and nimble style, but Evan abandoned it. A powerful Aura erupted from him as he planted his feet on the arena floor, suddenly looking as immovable as a mountain god.
Zane instinctively took a step back, his own stance mirroring the change. He knew a simple block wouldn’t be enough this time.
His opponent was strong, which meant he had to be stronger. He had to overcome whatever trick Evan had up his sleeve.
[SFX: BATTLE CRY]
"Earthshatter Sword!" Evan roared.
He brought his blade down in a crushing, destructive arc that tore through the air. It wasn’t a typical sword attack; heavy blades were for power, while swords were for speed. It was rare to see such raw, devastating force from a sword, a strike that looked like it could split mountains.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
As the attack hurtled toward him, Zane's eyelids fluttered. His wrist dipped, letting the tip of his sword drop for a split second.
A flick of the sword. It was the most potent move in all of basic swordsmanship—a simple technique refined to its absolute peak, focused into a single, devastating point. Nothing could escape it without a price.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT]
[SFX: THUNDEROUS CRACK]
The collision was deafening. A flash of light was followed by a thunderous crack that shook the arena. A white blade went spinning through the air like a pinwheel before clattering onto the stone floor.
[MUSIC: VICTORIOUS THEME]
When the light faded, the crowd gasped. Evan's Cloudrender lay on the ground. Zane stood perfectly still, the tip of his Streamer aimed directly at Evan’s chest.
Evan was frozen, his face a mask of pure disbelief as he stared at the sword point. He gasped for air, his chest heaving. This was not how it was supposed to end.
"You lose," Zane said.
"I refuse to accept this!" Evan snapped, his voice cracking. "You're no match for me!"
Elder Gates stepped into the arena. "Evan. A loss is a loss."
"If I could use my Vitality, I'd have you in three moves!" Evan shot back.
Zane began wiping down the Streamer. He didn't look up. "You're right. Right now, I can't match you. But it won't be long before you can't match me."
[SFX: STUNNED SILENCE]
The words hit Evan like a slap. He opened his mouth — and found nothing to say.
Zane sheathed the Streamer and stepped off the arena without another glance. Evan was still standing there when Elder Gates finally had to usher him out.
[MUSIC: CEREMONIAL ANNOUNCEMENT]
Elder Gates returned to the center of the arena and called for silence. Then he began to read the official standings aloud — first place through one hundredth — his voice carrying across the full arena without effort.
First place: Zane Lowell.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]
Second place went to Patrick. Third to Finn. The rankings continued down from there, each name landing with the weight of years of work. As for Evan — he had fallen from first to second, and then, with his defeat, was removed from the standings entirely.
For any disciple of the Skyreach Sect, appearing in the top thousand was a mark of distinction that many spent their entire careers chasing. The top hundred was something most could only dream of. The top three — almost unimaginable.
But the students watching didn’t seem surprised.
Zane hadn’t lost a single match since the All Peaks Competition began.
During their last match, Evan had Zane on the ropes, but this time, the tables had completely turned. It was clear to everyone that Zane’s victory felt like destiny. He had this incredible ability to get stronger when faced with tougher opponents, and that was his most impressive trait.
Not even Evan could stop him.
What an incredible talent. Even more amazing, Zane’s performance had won over every single person watching.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN AIR]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL FANTASY]
[AMBIENT: HIGH ALTITUDE WIND]
The Cloud Palace was aptly named. Its eaves curled upward like wisps of clouds, and perched high on the mountain, it looked less like a home and more like something from a fairy tale.
[SFX: SKYCRUISER LANDING]
Finally, the Skycruiser came to a smooth stop on a platform before the palace gates. The attendant who had guided them led them inside.
In the center of the vast palace stood Sir Kenneth, as still as a statue, his breath pluming in the cool air.
Zane, Patrick, and Finn walked in and stopped before him, bowing respectfully.
The leader studied the three of them, a satisfied nod answering their greeting. Their performance at the All Peaks Competition had truly impressed him. Both Patrick and Finn had far surpassed the competitors from previous years.
He couldn’t help but remember his disappointment from the last competition, when that brat Evan had taken first. Even then, Sir Kenneth hadn’t been happy with him. And for the last three years, he’d had to endure the taunts of his peers at Cloud Hall after Evan’s embarrassing failure on the Cloud Road. It had been a long, frustrating three years.
This time, he was finally going to shut them up.
He was grateful the results hadn’t disappointed him. He was impressed by the talented Finn and by Patrick's solid performance.
But most importantly, Zane had taken first place.
[MUSIC: CEREMONIAL ANNOUNCEMENT]
After looking over the other two, his gaze finally settled on Zane.
"Zane Lowell," he said, his eyes twinkling. "For your first-place victory, you will receive a Draught of Clarity. You will also be granted a Sacred Weapon. The choice of which one... is yours."
[SFX: HEARTBEAT QUICKENING]
"A Sacred Weapon?" Zane’s heart leaped.
He thought of Lily — her sword dance, the killing intent that had rolled off her in waves during the competition, surpassing anything he himself could produce. He needed a weapon equal to what was coming.
 "I can really choose anyone I want?"
