[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Master Jasper reeled under the implication of Sir Kenneth’s blind support of Zane. Praise that high was unprecedented, leaving the elder momentarily speechless.
Sir Kenneth, a distinguished servant of the Cloud Hall, possessed a vision that transcended the limited scope of the Eastern Territory. He’d witnessed the wonders and terrors that lurked beyond the horizon, in realms most cultivators could scarcely imagine.
Though billions of souls called the Eastern Territory their homeland, and the Heartlands dwarfed this region tenfold in size, these five combined territories hardly dominated the world, as most inhabitants presumed. They merely occupied a modest corner of the vast continent, a fragment of the whole.
The world was overflowing with souls, more numerous than grains of sand on a shore—far too many to ever count. In a population that vast, geniuses were bound to exist. Zane had talent, sure, and even better luck, but there had to be people out there even more powerful than him.
In truth, Zane’s achievements had profoundly unsettled Master Jasper. Sure, the natural order dictated that the younger generation will eventually surpass their elders, but did Zane really warrant being called a reincarnated immortal? If that were true, what would it mean for the world?
Noting the profound disbelief on Master Jasper’s face, Sir Kenneth’s lips curved into an amused smile. "I’m just kidding, Jasper. Don’t look so serious."
Jasper, over a century old himself, found himself frozen in bewilderment. To hear his superior so casually joke about matters of such importance left him adrift, with no idea how to reply.
"Nevertheless, Zane is truly special," Sir Kenneth continued, his voice taking on a more contemplative tone. "He lacks the advantage of a prestigious lineage. His background and bloodline might be ordinary, but this generation has already produced two extraordinary figures: Elara, blessed with her unique constitution, and now Zane, with his monstrous strength. Two exceptional talents, one right after the other. It’s pretty remarkable, wouldn’t you agree?"

"My turn!"
Zane began to move his own feet, matching the rhythm of the Sword Step.
Though Zane was copying Finn’s technique, there was a stark difference between their styles.
[AMBIENT: TRAINING FIELD]
[MUSIC: TENSE MARTIAL ARTS]
Finn’s Sword Step was a study in fluid precision. If you looked closely, every step was perfectly measured to match the three-foot length of his Blood-Corroding Sword. His movements seemed gentle, but they were a clever mask for his killing intent—an intent that would erupt the moment his blade was drawn.
Zane’s footwork, in contrast, was unpredictable yet perfectly natural. His steps weren’t as fluid as Finn’s, but they seemed to hold far more explosive power.
The two circled each other on the field, their steps falling into a tense rhythm as they searched for the slightest opening in the other’s defense.
But at this level of mastery, neither would give an inch. An unforced error was unthinkable.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
Their Sword Steps wove together in a contest of skill that looked like a breathtaking dance. The disciples of the Skyreach Sect watched, completely mesmerized, their voices rising in a murmur of awe.
"I can’t look away!"
"This is incredible to watch. If I could master the Sword Step like that, I’d be unstoppable in the Adept Realm!"
"Yeah, right. To learn the Sword Step, you have to grasp sword intent first. If you could do that, you’d already be invincible in the Adept Realm. You really think you’ve got what it takes?"
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
As the crowd buzzed, Finn was the first to break the stalemate.
[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]
He shot forward as if gliding on ice. When he stopped, thirteen cyclones of sword intent screamed toward Zane.
Six surged from the front, three from the left, three from the right, and the last one descended from above, boxing Zane in from all sides.
"Nice." Facing the thirteen cyclones rushing at him, Zane answered with a deft sidestep, conjuring four cyclones of his own. Though he had fewer, each one was larger and crackled with greater power.
[SFX: ENERGY COLLISION]
The opposing vortexes of energy smashed into each other, shattering on impact. A perfect stalemate.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
Just then, Finn unsheathed his sword.
This was Finn's way. He might hold back for an entire fight, but once he committed to an attack, it was a relentless storm. He gave his opponents no room to breathe, no chance to recover.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
"Blade of Megrez!"
This was the first time Finn had used his sword in the competition.
The motion was upward — but the force wasn't. The moment Finn's blade moved, a crushing weight bore down on Zane from above, as if the sky itself were pressing him into the ground. Zane's breath tightened.
He recognized it instantly. Finn wasn't just using his own power. He was borrowing from something vast — a thread of the Celestial Law, woven into the strike. Just a trace. Just a concept. But even that was nearly enough to buckle his knees.
As the blade left its sheath, his entire presence shifted. It was in moments like this that fighters often found a breakthrough.
[SFX: RAPID SWORD CLASHING]
Zane didn't retreat. Borrowed power, no matter how immense, couldn't be fully controlled. But his own moves — even the most basic ones — were entirely his. That was the gap Finn couldn't close.
The ring of steel became a constant hum as they clashed fifty times, then a hundred, then a hundred and fifty in the span of heartbeats.
With a heavy sigh, Finn broke away, retreating in silence.
He realized he couldn’t beat Zane in a pure contest of swordsmanship. Continuing to trade blows was pointless.
A raw, simple power was hidden in Zane's most basic forms. But simplicity, when taken to its absolute extreme, becomes a form of profound complexity. It wasn’t a contradiction—it was mastery.
And Finn couldn’t find a single flaw in it.
[SFX: MAGICAL SWORD HUMMING]
As soon as he steadied himself at a distance, he smoothly drew a circle in the air with the tip of his sword. Vitality billowed out from him, mingling with his sword intent and causing the Blood-Corroding Sword to thrum with power.
"The Light and Dark Swordsmanship! I am not a cruel man, Zane... but I must demonstrate the power of the heavens!"

A powerful aura radiated from Finn’s body, and his whole demeanor transformed. It was as if he'd become a conduit for the heavens, a messenger walking the mortal world. He looked like a child of the divine.
