Ep 898 - A Living Corpse

Word count: 1092

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CAVERN]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Of the four secret lands, the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land was by far the most important to the dragon-blooded—and the most inaccessible. None of the Soul Sea Realm warriors had ever managed to claim the Ancestral Dragon Egg, which meant Zane had to find another path to power. His eyes landed on the body of the girl from the Spirit Supreme Realm. That was his path.

When he’d shown the Black Star pickax to the other warriors, especially War Emperor York, their excitement had been palpable. 

It wasn’t that they didn’t want the girl’s powerful body for themselves; they just couldn’t get to it. No matter how hard they’d tried, none of them had ever managed to so much as scratch her Crystal Coffin, let alone break the chains that bound her.

Zane’s Black Star had given them a surge of hope. To enter the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, you needed the strongest vessel possible. If Zane could finally free the girl, it meant they might all get a chance to claim her body. But for now, they gave him a wide berth. 

Getting the Ancestral Dragon Egg would be the ultimate prize, but no one was stupid enough to get in Zane’s way and make an enemy of him. Even War Emperor York, who had a bitter history with Zane, knew better than to challenge him now.

[SFX: METAL SCRAPING]

Zane pulled a heavy pickax from his Storage Ring and approached the stone pillar where the girl was encased.

The Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms’ raid on The Sacred Feather Sea had yielded a trove of information, identifying nearly every preserved body in the necropolis. Except one. The young woman in the pillar was a total enigma.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

A knot of unease tightened in his gut. What if she wasn’t really dead? If he let her out, what kind of trouble would he be unleashing on them all?

His soul drifted closer to the pillar. The Crystal Coffin was perfectly clear, giving him an unobstructed view of the young woman standing within. She was dressed in little more than rags, her skin a tapestry of ugly scars.

[SFX: CHAINS RATTLING]

Heavy iron chains were wrapped tightly around her hands and feet.

Who is she? he wondered, the question echoing in his mind. And why is she chained up like this?

Without overthinking it, Zane raised the Black Star and swung.

[SFX: SHARP CRACK]

[SFX: CRACKING GLASS]

There was no resistance. The pickax’s head tapped the crystal surface, and a web of cracks shot outward from the point of contact. He didn’t need to strike again. The cracks kept spreading on their own until the entire coffin crumbled into fine powder and drifted to the floor.

The girl was still standing. The iron chains held her fast to the pillar.

A faint fragrance drifted out from where the coffin had been.

Zane paused. He leaned closer. Then, carefully, he reached out and pressed his hand to her chest.

[SFX: HEARTBEAT PULSE]

A strong, steady heartbeat pushed back against his palm. Powerful. Unmistakably alive.

His eyes widened. This wasn’t a corpse. This was a living person.

The Dragon-blooded had sacrificed everything in the blood collapse — so why was there a living body here? He could only guess that her soul had been destroyed and her body preserved by some secret method. If that was the case, possessing her would be entirely different from using a Soul Core on a corpse. A living body needed no Soul Core. It would respond at full power.

He made up his mind. He raised the Black Star and broke the iron chains binding her hands. Then he set the pickax back in his Storage Ring, reached out, and lightly pinched her cheeks.

“Sorry,” he said quietly. “I’ll only borrow it for a while.”

His soul entered her mouth.

[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]

It went smoothly. Too smoothly. No rejection. No repulsive force. The Soul Core should have activated the moment he entered — but there was nothing. Just silence and darkness.

Too easy, Zane thought, and his instincts screamed at him. Retreat.

He moved immediately — one breath, two — but the moment he tried to pull his soul back out, he found he couldn’t.

He’d seen this happen to War King Wind and Scott. They’d been helpless against the commands of the young girl in the pillar, forced to walk toward her against their will. But that wasn’t what really had him on edge. The real problem was the very real possibility that he wouldn’t be able to state the corpse’s name when asked.

[SFX: TELEPATHIC WHOOSH]

He decided to try something else first. “Who are you?” Zane projected the question into her mind.

[SFX: SHARP MENTAL ECHO]

A voice, tinged with sharp annoyance, shot back. “Me? Haven’t you been staring long enough? You mean you still don’t know?”

“You’re not dead,” he sent. “So why are you sealed in a necropolis?”

“Who said I’m not dead?” her soul replied, its tone flat and weary. “I died. I just never expected someone to show up after all these years, trying to steal my body. Maybe you’d like to stay and keep me company?”

“You’re dead,” Zane repeated to himself, the words making no sense. She was a body with its own soul, perfectly preserved. But how could she be dead when her heartbeat felt stronger than his own?

“I can’t really explain it to you,” the voice said, a hard edge creeping into it. “But since you’re here, you might as well stay. Don’t even think about escaping.”

Zane quickly threw his hands up, a gesture of surrender. “Miss, I am so sorry. I stumbled in here by accident. It was a mistake. Please, just let me go.”

The young woman cut him off with a mental scoff. “What did you just say? You ‘accidentally’ barged into my mind? Do you think I’m an idiot? I wasn’t born yesterday. You came here to take my body. You think I’m just going to let you walk away? Not a chance.”

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

[SFX: PSYCHIC STRUGGLE]

Zane’s calm shattered. If she wasn’t going to let him leave, he’d have to force his way out. He tried to yank his soul back, to tear it free from her consciousness.

It was useless. The woman’s soul was impossibly strong.

[SFX: DARK ENERGY PULSE]

She just snorted, and suddenly, crimson ripples spread through the edges of her dark mind, pulling him deeper inside.
