Ep 196 - Matters of the Heart
Word count: 1046

[MUSIC: ROMANTIC THEME]
 Yates had met Ingrid a few times, but "acquaintance" didn't quite cover it. 
The truth was, he'd fallen hard the moment he first laid eyes on her. But the odds were stacked against him – her last name was Solomon. 
House Solomon was one of the seven Great Houses of the empire. Yates, on the other hand, was just the young master of a small, fading noble house. Word on the street was that Ingrid held a special place in House Solomon —so special that even though she had no particular strength in the martial arts,  she outranked even the heir to the main family line. To say her family doted on her was an understatement.
The social gap between them, and between their houses, was epically wide. Yates knew the usual tricks he might pull out to try and win a girl's heart wouldn't cut it with someone this special. When it came to marriage, the Great Houses only ever considered families of equal standing.
 No matter his strength, talent, or wealth, marrying Ingrid felt like a pipe dream. If House Moon ever dared to send a matchmaker to formally propose, it wouldn't just be his own humiliation; the entire house would become a laughingstock.
When Yates heard Ingrid was planning to join The Demon Purge, he'd become desperate to pass the Eagle Peak exam himself. But the competition was brutal. He'd cleared the initial test but was ultimately knocked out by another disciple.
So Yates played the only card he had: he asked his cousin, Brenda, to bring Ingrid to their estate. Brenda was Ingrid’s friend from their time at Maiden's Peak at the Skyreach Sect, and Yates hoped he could win Ingrid over through her.
[AMBIENT: ELEGANT DINING HALL]
 Servants led the young women from Maiden's Peak to their table, pulling out chairs and inviting them to sit with practiced respect.
[SFX: MOMENT OF RECOGNITION]
As Ingrid settled in, she felt a pair of eyes on her. She glanced around, and her gaze swept over a nearby table before freezing. Her eyes locked with Zane's.
"Zane?" Ingrid breathed, a jolt of surprise and excitement shooting through her.
This was the man who had risked everything to save her, who had pushed himself to the brink to slay the demon generals. The memory of him was burned into her mind.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REFLECTION]
[AMBIENT: AFTERMATH OF BATTLE]
After that brutal battle between the Imperial Army and the Demons in Alba Imperia, all the disciples from the Sect had been sent home—all except for Zane.
Yolande had detained him over his fight with Hugh.
[AMBIENT: QUIET CONTEMPLATION]
 Ingrid had been sick with worry. Before leaving the city, she’d pleaded with her uncle, Lennie, to protect Zane no matter what.
And even though her uncle had readily agreed, the anxiety had lingered.
Later, letters from her uncle brought news: Zane hadn't just survived the war; he'd made incredible contributions.
Even as a mere Initiate Adept, he'd accomplished the impossible in a war dominated by Soul Realm masters. With every report, Zane grew more legendary in her eyes.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
 But once he left Alba Imperia, the trail went cold. Ingrid heard nothing.
She'd even considered going to find him herself.
But the thought of a young woman traveling thousands of miles just to chase after a man felt improper. Even in the relatively open-minded Empire, a girl from a top Great House like hers was raised to be more conservative than that.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL MELODY]
Then she'd heard he was back at the Skyreach Sect, spending most of his time training at Emberfin Lake.
The news left her feeling both relieved and disappointed.
She was relieved that he was back, safe and sound. But she couldn't help but feel disappointed that he seemed to have forgotten her entirely, caring only for his relentless training.
"Maybe I should just go see him," she'd thought more than once. But in the end, her reserved nature always won out, and she pushed the thought away.
She hadn't even wanted to come to today's party, but her close friend Brenda had been so persistent that she'd finally given in.
Never in a million years did she expect to run into Zane here.
Ingrid had missed him so much, but it seemed he hadn't given her a second thought.
A cold knot of disappointment tightened in her chest. *It was one thing when I thought he was just obsessed with training. But he has time for a party at House Moon, and no time to see me? Did he really forget me?*
[MUSIC: ROMANTIC TENSION]
But then he looked up, and his clear, dark eyes met hers. He smiled. And just like that, the cloud of her hurt and frustration vanished.
Completely ignoring an attentive Yates, she rose from her seat and walked straight toward Zane’s table.
[AMBIENT: ELEGANT PARTY MURMURS]
Dustin watched the whole scene unfold with keen interest. He knew a fair bit about Ingrid Solomon; a few years back, his own Blackwood House had tried to arrange a marriage between her and their heir.
But the social standing of Blackwood House was a far cry from House Solomon's. It was a fool's errand from the start, and after sizing up the situation, they'd dropped the idea.
In truth, the real deal-breaker was the attitude of House Solomon's elders. Marriages were typically arranged by parents and elders, especially among the Great Houses where a union was more about two families than two people. But House Solomon was adamant about not restricting Ingrid’s choice. That alone showed just how much they adored her.
Thanks to that failed matchmaking attempt, Dustin knew more about Ingrid than most. Which made him wonder how on earth Zane knew her. He'd never heard of any connection between them.
“Ingrid's staring at him like she's in love, but he seems almost indifferent to her,” Dustin mused.
“Zane needs to wake up. He must know, this isn't just some noble girl. Her position in House Solomon makes her more influential than some disfavored princess. How many men are desperate for a moment of her time? How many dream of marrying her? And yet, here she is, chasing after him. What a fool he is.” 
Thinking it over, Dustin couldn't help but let out a low whistle. His friend Zane was smart in many ways, but in matters of the heart, he seemed like a damn fool.
