Ep 1144 - Hand It Over

Word count: 1169

[MUSIC: GENTLE WONDER]
For someone who’d made it all the way to the Upper World, Freya wasn’t what you’d call ambitious. She couldn’t even identify half the rare, priceless treasures lining the giant cave. It was only by listening to the others murmur that she’d figured out the tiny Seed of the World Tree at the very peak was the grand prize.
But the seed wasn’t for her. 
[SFX: ICY SHIMMER]
Her eyes were locked on a frosty glow emanating from the side of the cave—an Ice Lotus. Mastering the ice law was her only real goal, and a flower like that would be an incredible boon. Still, there was no guarantee she’d get it—plenty of fighters who braved the wind tunnels left with nothing but scrapes and bruises.
Seeing the doubt on her face, Zane offered a warm smile. “Go for it. You got this.” It was a simple encouragement, but it was all he could really offer from the sidelines.
A little of his confidence seemed to rub off on her. Freya nodded, a new determination in her eyes. “Thanks. I’ll give it everything I have.”
She stretched out her arms, and a plume of frigid air coiled around her. 
[MUSIC: TENSE RISING STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: SWIRLING WIND TUNNELS]
As she launched herself off the platform, the wind caught her like a kite, pulling her into one of the swirling tunnels. 
[SFX: BODY SPINNING IN WIND]
Her small frame spun end over end in the current, and it took her a few dizzying moments to find her balance. Nerves, maybe. She steadied herself, her gaze fixed on her target. 
*Just another hundred feet.*
As she drew closer, the crosswinds from the other tunnels began to shove her back and forth. She was almost there, just inches away. Freya reached out, her fingers straining. 
[SFX: VIOLENT GUST]
But just as she was about to close her hand around the stem, a violent gust slammed into her, yanking her away. Her fingers grazed the petals, missing by a fraction of an inch.
Gritting her teeth, she refused to give up. 
[SFX: ICE WHIP CRACK]
An icy blue ribbon materialized in her hand and shot out like a whip, wrapping securely around the Ice Lotus. 
[SFX: ICE SHATTERING]
With a sharp tug, the flower broke free in a shower of freezing energy. A real, triumphant smile broke across her face as she pulled her prize toward her. She had it.
[SFX: CONTROLLED AIR BURSTS]
[SFX: STONE LANDING]
A few controlled bursts of air later, she landed gracefully on a stone platform in a more isolated part of the cave.
She turned the lotus over in her hands, studying it. Seven seeds nestled at its center, each one a perfect crystallization of the ice law. Just one of them would bring her a significant step closer to where she needed to be.
She looked up, found Zane across the cave, and smiled.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
A hulking figure stepped onto her platform—one of the Ogre cultivators, looking down at her like she was an inconvenience. He held out a hand, palm up. Ten Sovereign Stones dropped from his fingers and clattered onto the stone floor at her feet.
“Sell me the Ice Lotus.” He didn’t bother making it sound like a request.
Freya’s expression tightened. “I don’t want any trouble,” she said carefully. “But I won’t be selling it.”
The Ogre’s eyes narrowed. He raised his hand.
Lincoln was already there.
He stepped in front of her without a word, his broad frame blocking the Ogre’s reach. The pale green light at the center of his forehead pulsed once, faint but unmistakable.
[MUSIC: LOW THREATENING PULSE]
It didn’t help. More Ogres materialized from the shadows, spreading out to flank them—a wall of cultivators at the Spirit Supreme Realm, forming a wall between Lincoln and any exit. On the far side of the cave, the human cultivators—Adrian, Abner, and the others—watched the scene unfold and did absolutely nothing. Neither did the Magnolia Sanctum cultivators nearby. Freya and Lincoln weren’t part of their group. It wasn’t their problem.
“Kid.” The Ogre leader’s smile didn’t reach his eyes. “Step aside. I want the lotus, not you. Don’t throw your life away over a flower.”
Lincoln didn’t move. His eyes were calm. He was already mapping the fight.
Freya’s face had gone pale. She glanced at the lotus in her hand, then back at the ring of Ogres.
Then a voice reached her, quiet and close, as if Zane were standing right beside her.
Her expression shifted. She looked at the Ogre leader and gave a small, graceful shrug. “You’re right,” she said. “It’s a beautiful flower, but it’s not worth dying over.”
She held the lotus out. The Ogre caught it.
“Human women are smarter than they look,” he said, and laughed.
Freya smiled at him. It was a perfectly pleasant smile—meant entirely for someone else.
Lincoln stepped back. He glanced across the cave toward Zane’s platform, read the calm there, and let it go.
On the far side of the cave, Zane’s eyes were still and level. 
*Give them the lotus,* he had told her. *I’ll deal with it later.*
Zane had been watching her from his own platform, the one below the most peculiar wind tunnel in the cavern. No other fighter had picked it, for a simple reason: when the woman from the Demon Night had tested the currents with her scented mist, the wind in this particular tunnel had blown straight down. Since the whole point was to go up, everyone had written it off as useless.
But Lavender had told him to pick this one, and that was that.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ANTICIPATION]
Only a few fighters remained, and now, all eyes were on him. 
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
The murmurs started up almost immediately.
“Looks like he picked the worst possible route.”
“It’s pointless. Some people try to be clever, but that wind just goes straight to the bottom. He doesn’t stand a chance.”
“Yeah, what a waste of time.”
[MUSIC: INTENSE RUSHING]
[AMBIENT: ROARING WIND TUNNEL]
Zane took a breath, ignored them, and stepped off the edge. The fierce wind roaring from the tunnel swallowed him whole. 
[SFX: BODY PLUMMETING THROUGH AIR]
He shot straight down, plunging toward the cave floor at a ninety-degree angle as the onlookers shook their heads.
But after dropping more than two hundred feet, he did something impossible. 
[SFX: WIND SHIFT]
He shifted his body, changing his angle against the current, and began to rise. The same powerful wind that had pulled him down now carried him upward. In less than three seconds, he was hovering near the right wall of the cave.
[SFX: SHARP VOICE CUTTING THROUGH WIND]
“To the left!” Lavender’s voice cut through the howling wind, sharp and clear.
Zane didn’t hesitate. 
[SFX: KICK OFF STALAGMITE]
[SFX: BODY BANKING THROUGH AIR]
He kicked off a nearby stalagmite, launching himself out of the downward draft and banking perfectly into another wind tunnel.
