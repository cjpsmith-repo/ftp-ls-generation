The Little Monster flexed his brand-new arm like nothing had happened.

Even the mightiest Soul Sea Realm warrior would be crippled for life if they didn’t set a broken arm. No normal person could just heal from an injury like that. But this creature… it wasn’t normal. And that was exactly why the Nine Cauldron Martial House became obsessed with the Little Monster.

[AMBIENT: SECRET LABORATORY]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Back at their headquarters, the house fighters studied the creature in secret, and one day, they uncovered its unbelievable talent: an innate ability to boost the training of other fighters.

Every warrior is born with a certain amount of natural aptitude. Sure, some rare alchemical herbs could enhance it, but those were impossible to find on the Divine Kingdom Continent. To help their warriors grow stronger and reach higher realms, the union of seven divine kingdoms had poured resources into martial houses, secret training grounds, and all kinds of tournaments. It was all designed to push fighters to their limits through life-or-death battles against fierce beasts, unlocking their full potential.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT REVELATION]

So, when the Nine Cauldron Martial House discovered what the Little Monster could do, they were ecstatic.

Take one of their Adepts, for example. The guy had been stuck at a plateau for years, with no hope of ever reaching the Soul Realm. Then he fought the Little Monster. Just one fight, and he had a breakthrough, advancing almost overnight.

When they’d first captured it, the monster was barely at the Marrow Refining stage—not even a true Adept. The house quickly learned that sparring with it could help others shatter their bottlenecks, but only if the power gap wasn’t too wide. An Inner-Core Realm master would gain almost nothing, but for an Adept, a single match was a godsend.

And as the Little Monster grew stronger, so did his value. Once he broke through to the Inner-Core Realm, he could help Spirit-Tribulation warriors crack their bottlenecks. The Nine Cauldron Martial House nurtured him carefully, watching his potential climb. If he ever reached the Life-and-Death Realm — if he could help Soul Sea masters break through — the implications were staggering.

[SFX: HEAVY CHAINS]

And so, the Nine Cauldron Martial House built a stone tower and chained the Little Monster inside. Every day, they sent in a different Adept to face it. Many of them died, torn apart by the savage creature.

[MUSIC: DARK DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

But the ones who survived all found themselves rapidly advancing to the next level.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL]

The thing was, the Little Monster wasn't stupid. Even a beast could figure out it was being used.

It simply refused to fight. No matter how they tortured it—even cutting off its hands and feet—the monster wouldn’t lift a finger. The standoff lasted for months before the Nine Cauldron Martial House finally caved. Wasting its unique talent was unthinkable.

The Soul Sea masters of the divine kingdoms had their own reasons to care. War King York, War King Wind, and the Emperor of Harlen — all of them had been stuck at the Soul Sea Realm for countless years. The resources of the lower world were too scarce to push them further, and with the ascension path destroyed, the Little Monster was the only hope any of them had left. If he reached the Life-and-Death Realm, he might finally give them what centuries of cultivation could not.

But for ten years now, the Little Monster had been stuck. Late Spirit-Tribulation, and no further. No one could explain it. The Soul Sea masters waited. They had time. What they couldn't afford was losing him.

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL]

In the York Divine Kingdom's corner, the Holy Maiden watched the barrier with a complicated expression. After what she'd seen Zane do to Fiona, her certainty had started to crack. Zane's talent was something she hadn't accounted for.

What is this thing made of? Zane thought, his patience wearing thin. It should have been torn to pieces by now, but it looks perfectly fine!

[SFX: WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]

First things first, he had to get it off his back. Zane spun, wrenching the monster’s other arm, then launched himself into the air to bring a powerful stomp down on its chest.

The Little Monster saw the move coming. “Trying to get rid of me?” it jeered. “Dream on!”

But the Little Monster wasn't finished. Its neck suddenly elongated, head rearing back — then it drove its skull straight into Zane's face.

Zane's left hand came up. He punched the head clean, and the force sent it rocketing two hundred feet sideways into the barrier wall. It hit with a crack that shook the whole structure.

Then it bounced back.

Faster. Harder.

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

But in that same instant, a wave of force erupted from Zane’s body. As he kicked out, the power of the Dragon Scales surged through him. He shot like a meteor toward the edge of the enchanted barrier, the monster’s hands still clamped tight around his torso.

Zane was ready for it. The moment they hit the barrier’s edge, he yanked with all his strength.

[SFX: HARD IMPACT]

The monster’s soft body slammed hard into the shimmering, invisible wall.

“Dream on?” Zane yelled back. “Even your body has its limits!” He got a better grip on the creature and slammed it into the barrier again. And again. And again.

A series of sickening thuds echoed through the arena as Zane used the monster’s body like a club, swinging it back and forth and smashing it against the barrier with brutal force. The entire barrier shuddered with every impact, and you could only imagine the punishment the creature’s body was taking.
