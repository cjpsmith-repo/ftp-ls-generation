Ep 293 - I Believe in Zane
Word Count: 1254
[MUSIC: EPIC FANTASY THEME]
Zane’s sister Elara was an undeniable prodigy in the Skyreach Sect. You could see it in the way she'd already surpassed Zane in both skill and speed. By the time the All Peaks Competition rolled around, she had reached the Soul Realm—and she was only fifteen.
Zane, on the other hand, was seventeen and still just at the Second Level of the Adept Realm. Compared to her, his progress seemed glacial. But that didn’t mean he was outmatched.
When it came to actual combat, he left Elara in the dust.
And it wasn’t just her. Zane outclassed almost all of the Sect’s Chosen. In the last All Peaks Competition, the disciples representing the various peaks had gotten wiped out. The top three—hell, the top five spots—were all swept by the Sect’s Chosen, who had easily dominated the peak disciples.
But this year’s tournament was a whole different story. Two of the top three contenders, Zane and Finn, were both fighting for the peaks. The craziest part? Neither of them had even reached the Soul Realm. It was completely unprecedented, something no one could have ever imagined.
Zane and Finn weren’t the only underdogs to shatter expectations. The best inner disciples from other peaks—Wynn, Shayna, Jason, and Jill—had also taken down members of the Sect’s Chosen with little effort.
So this time around, the Peak Disciples had completely turned the tables on the Sect’s Chosen in the All Peaks Competition.
Among the Sect’s Chosen, only Patrick had managed to secure a spot in the top five. Everything was riding on him now.
Considered the rock of the Sect’s Chosen, he'd mastered the enigmatic Monastic Path, and his true power was a mystery to just about everyone. The honor of the Chosen now rested squarely on his shoulders. If he lost too, this year would go down as a complete and utter humiliation for them.
The deacon stepped forward and read from the draw list. The referee announced the pairing.
"Misty Peak — Zane. Eagle Peak — Finn."
The crowd erupted. 
[SFX: CROWD ROAR]
When the two stood face to face, Zane spoke first. "Everyone's been waiting to see your sword. Are you really going to go the whole competition without drawing it?"
Finn shook his head. "I don't draw it because I don't need to. I'm not being mysterious."
"And now?"
"Now I need to."
[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT CROWD MURMURS]
[SFX: SWORD SLOWLY UNSHEATHING]
On the stage, Finn slowly drew his sword, inch by inch.
[AMBIENT: CROWD CHEERING INTENSIFIES]
With every flash of steel, the crowd’s roar grew louder.
"He’s finally drawing his sword! This is it!"
"Looks like he’s actually taking Zane seriously!"
"What kind of sword is it? I can’t wait to get a look at it!"
Every disciple held their breath, their eyes glued to Finn’s hand, desperate to see the weapon.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
When Finn finally pulled the sword free of its sheath, the entire arena fell silent, stunned.
"Is that... a rusty sword? Wait, do swords even rust?"
"Of course not, you idiot."
"So what is it, then? A plain iron sword? Oh my god! The sword Finn's been lugging around this whole time is just a piece of scrap metal!"
"Ha! Only an idiot would think that. You really think a guy with Finn’s skill would carry a piece of trash on his back?"
"Alright, Mr. Smart Guy, then tell us what kind of special sword rusts."
The sword Finn had drawn was, in fact, covered with red spots.
Weaponsmithing in the Eastern Territory was so advanced that any weapon, even Mythic-grade ones, could be preserved for decades. Properly cared for, Spirit Weapons were supposed to last for centuries without a hint of rust or a dulling of their edge. As for Mythic weapons, they could last for millennia.
A rust-free sword wasn’t automatically the best, but a rusty one was almost certainly garbage.
Yet here was the Skyreach Sect's top young swordsman, holding a rusty blade. The other disciples just couldn't wrap their heads around it. They'd been expecting something legendary.
[SFX: SWORD BEING DRAWN]
Finn was oblivious to the crowd's whispers. He slowly lifted the sword and pointed its tip to the ground. "This sword," he said slowly, "is called the Blood-Corroding Sword. The red spots on the blade... they aren’t rust. They’re droplets of blood."
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
Zane stared, stunned. The disciples who'd been mocking the rusty sword fell silent, their jaws hanging open.
Whose blood could stay so fresh after thousands of years?
Normally, blood from an ordinary person would dry instantly, leaving a stain. But even that would fade over time, vanishing completely within a few years. No normal bloodstain could last more than three.
Yet the stains on Finn’s Blood-Corroding Sword were supposedly thousands of years old. Could it be the blood of some Legendary Beast?
"How could blood stay fresh for that long?" Zane wondered aloud, his curiosity piqued. He could feel a strange power radiating from it.
Finn shook his head. "I don’t know. But this Blood-Corroding Sword has helped me understand the path of the blade. It has a soul that connects with my own. I tell you this out of respect, swordsman to swordsman."
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
A smile touched Zane’s lips. Interesting guy. Polite, too. He reached into his Storage Ring and drew his own blade. With a soft chime, the Streamer slid from its sheath.
"My sword is named Streamer," he told his opponent. "It’s a low-grade Mythic weapon. The blade itself was forged by Chase."
"Forged by Chase?" Finn repeated, his interest piqued.
Finn studied the blade — he'd seen this one before. Or something very close to it. On the second floor of the Weapon Pavilion, Chase had left behind an unfinished sword. He'd always assumed it was still there, incomplete.
"If I remember correctly," Finn said carefully, "Chase left that sword unfinished. Without his hand to complete it, it shouldn't have been possible to bring it to full power. Who finished the refinement? I don't know anyone in the Eastern Territory who could do it."
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
Up in the stands, Master Elwood leaned toward Sir Kenneth. "That’s not something one can just lie about. Anyone can verify it. There are tons of disciples at Quenching Peak. If it was a lie, he’d be called out immediately. Zane has no reason to tell such an obvious lie."
Hearing this, Sir Kenneth let out a long breath and glanced at Master Elwood.
Master Elwood caught his meaning and nodded. "I’ll send someone to Quenching Peak to confirm it."
Master weaponsmiths were vital to the Skyreach Sect, so it was no surprise Kenneth was taking this so seriously.
"Do it now," Kenneth ordered. "And get word back to me as fast as possible. Though," he added with a sigh, shaking his head, "I don’t think the result really matters."
Elwood raised an eyebrow. "You believe what Zane said, then?"
"What do you think?" Kenneth returned.
"I believe him," Elwood replied with a faint smile.
[MUSIC: INTRIGUING REVELATION]
Sir Kenneth pointed a finger at Master Elwood, a complex look on his face as he tried to process all the talent Zane had shown. "I believe Zane, too," he finally said. "He’s already given me so many surprises. Forget this claim about forging a Mythic weapon. At this point, if he claimed to be the reincarnation of an immortal, I’d probably believe him."
The other elders didn’t try to conceal their shock.
No one in the entire Skyreach Sect could earn that kind of faith from Sir Kenneth.
How special could this kid really be?
