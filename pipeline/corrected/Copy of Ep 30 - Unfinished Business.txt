Ep 30 - Unfinished Business
Word Count: 1158
[AMBIENT: COURTYARD]
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Zane knew he'd run into Perrin at the Skyreach Sect sooner or later, but he never thought it would be before he even had a chance to take the Entrance Exam.
From the way his cousin was talking, it was clear he knew what happened on Family Training Day.
That meant Perrin knew Zane had crippled Andrew—crippled him so badly he could never cultivate again.
"The Skyreach Sect welcomes all who qualify. My presence here is as legitimate as yours," Zane remarked with deliberate calmness, his eyes never leaving his cousin's face.
Perrin threw his head back and released a hollow laugh that echoed across the courtyard. "A traitor to House Lowell dares speak of legitimacy? After crippling Andrew and betraying our family's trust, every soul back home thirsts for your blood. Did you honestly believe you could simply stroll into the prestigious Skyreach Sect and claim membership?" His voice dripped with venom as he leaned forward. "Wake up from your delusions. I'll destroy you here and now, cousin."
[SFX: ENERGY PULSING]
A pulsating purple aura began to emanate from Perrin's body, bathing his features in an eerie light that accentuated the hatred in his eyes. He advanced toward Zane with measured steps, his face contorted into a sneer of pure contempt that revealed how deeply his resentment ran.
Zane exhaled softly and shook his head. This aggressive posturing was quintessential Perrin—all threat and bluster. With fluid precision born from countless hours of practice, he shifted his weight and dropped into a defensive stance, his muscles coiling like springs ready to release devastating force.
His cousin's training at the Skyreach Sect had evidently yielded impressive results, judging by the intensity of his aura. Yet Perrin remained blissfully ignorant of the transformation Zane had undergone during their time apart, the crucible of suffering that had forged him into something far more dangerous than the boy Perrin once knew.
"I already defeated Jace, a master weaponsmith halfway to the Nature Level," Zane reflected silently, a flicker of confidence warming his chest. "What possible chance does Perrin have against me now?" The thought brought not arrogance but calm certainty, like a swordsman who knows the battle before the first strike falls.
[SFX: ROBE RUSTLING]
Just then, a disciple in a black robe grabbed Perrin's arm. "Hey, easy there, Perrin," he warned. "This is the Skyreach Sect. You know the rules. Start a fight here, and the punishment is severe."
Perrin's scowl deepened. He knew the disciple was right; the penalties for breaking the Sect's bylaws were no joke. He hesitated.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
A young man named Caleb, who carried himself with an unmistakable air of authority, stepped out from the group of black-robed disciples. It was obvious he was their leader. "Perrin," he said, his voice even. "You have a history with this guy?"
Perrin inclined his head with exaggerated deference toward Caleb, his posture betraying the practiced subservience of someone well-versed in court politics. "Indeed. This individual betrayed House Lowell's honor and trust. As heir and young master of our illustrious House, the burden of administering justice falls squarely upon my shoulders."
"Then eliminate him," Caleb responded with chilling indifference, his voice as cold as winter steel. "While the Sect maintains stringent regulations, this applicant has merely registered for examination, not attained disciple status. Dispatching him now circumvents any technical violations." He gestured dismissively toward Zane, his casual manner suggesting he viewed human lives as mere commodities to be bartered. "I'll personally manage any consequences that might arise."
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSE]
Though Perrin had arrived primed for confrontation, Caleb's unexpected offer cast a shadow of hesitation across his features. His eyes narrowed slightly as he considered the implications. The political calculus unfolding behind his furrowed brow revealed his internal struggle.
In the cutthroat world of cultivation politics, favors represented dangerous currency, and Perrin harbored no illusions about Caleb's generosity. A debt to someone of Caleb Warrick's standing could demand repayment at the most inopportune moment, extracting a price far exceeding its apparent value. Especially when incurred over someone as supposedly insignificant as his disgraced cousin Zane.
Perrin turned back to Caleb. "I'm in no hurry. I want to see if this loser can even pass the entrance exams. If he fails, I'll crush him like an ant. If he makes it in, I'll have plenty of chances to deal with him later."
Then he fixed his gaze on Zane. "I'll let you live a few more days. Don't get comfortable. I'll be coming for you."
Zane just burst out laughing.
He pointed a finger at his cousin. "Are you serious? Perrin, did no one from House Lowell tell you the whole story? I didn't just beat Andrew. I beat Owen Lowell, too. How exactly are you planning on killing me?"
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
As he spoke, Zane let his full power surge outward. His body, tempered for twenty hours in the flames of Jace's forge, had gained the resilience of a Mid-Tier Weapon.
Though he was still at the Bio-Integration Level, the power that rolled off him was staggering. To Perrin, it felt like a tidal wave crashing down, threatening to crush the very air from his lungs.
Perrin instinctively took a step back. A carrier pigeon from House Lowell had reached him two days ago with a brief note. It only said that Zane had crippled Andrew, leaving him unable to cultivate. There was no mention of Owen Lowell. The last he'd heard, Zane was barely at the Strength Level. Now, his power was on another plane entirely.
Originally, Perrin had been confident he could crush Zane. He'd taken an Aether Crystal, which had massively boosted his cultivation speed. On top of that, by getting in good with Caleb, he'd already been accepted as a Core Apprentice.
In the past, Perrin had been so much stronger that he'd never even considered Zane a threat.
A letter from home had even suggested Zane was dead—captured by a Soul Forger named Jace—so Perrin had dismissed him entirely.
He had practically forgotten the guy existed.
[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY AURA]
But he never expected to see Zane standing here, alive and radiating a power so immense that Perrin was suddenly afraid.
He was afraid his cousin might not be weaker than him anymore.
Seeing Zane's arrogance, the Core Apprentices with Perrin scowled. This guy wasn't even a member of the Sect. Even if he passed the trials, he'd be a lowly Outer Disciple. Who the hell did he think he was, acting so cocky in front of them? They all wanted to put him in his place.
Their eyes shifted to Caleb, waiting for a signal. Caleb just offered a cool smile and turned to Perrin.
[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL TENSION]
"Perrin, it looks like this loudmouth from your House is getting a little out of line. What are you waiting for? Go shut him up."
