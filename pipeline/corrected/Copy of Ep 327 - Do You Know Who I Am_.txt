Ep 327 - Do You Know Who I Am?
Word count: 1081

[AMBIENT: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Gazing up at his tormentors, Melvin’s thoughts mirrored those Zane had harbored during his own captivity. I broke the rules of House Lowell, so they knocked me down to a slave. Now I'm nothing but a living punching bag. This is my fate.
[SFX: PUNCH]
[SFX: GASP OF PAIN]
But there’s one person here who can’t even lay a finger on me! Melvin reflected bitterly, a flicker of defiance burning in his otherwise hollow eyes. The momentary satisfaction of this thought vanished as Miles’s fist slammed into his ribs, driving the air from his lungs.
Miles bellowed as his face flushed with rage and veins protruded from his clenched fist. "Melvin, you should feel honored that someone like Darcy is stooping so low as to hit you!" His voice cracked with barely contained fury as he leaned closer, spittle flying from his lips. "Say one more goddamn word, and I swear I’ll kill you right here!"
[SFX: PUNCH IMPACT]
[SFX: COUGHING]
The punch sent Melvin doubling over, coughing.
But Melvin was a hothead. Gritting his teeth, he spat back, "Miles Lowell! You’re a disgrace to your own house! You're even worse than your brother, Perrin. At least Perrin ran with the big shots from the noble houses in the Imperial City. Who’s this Darcy clown you’re fawning over? Some nobody from House Hawthorne! House Hawthorne is a joke. They aren’t even worthy of being in the same room with us. And here you are, kissing his ass? Pathetic!"
A smirk twisted Melvin’s bruised face. That finally got Darcy’s attention.
"So, House Hawthorne isn’t good enough to talk to House Lowell?"
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
This time, Darcy’s easy smile vanished, his expression turning to ice.
Seeing that look, Miles scrambled to smooth things over, his voice dripping with false sincerity. "Darcy, don’t listen to him. He’s just running his mouth. I’ll punish him for this later, I promise!" Miles was walking on eggshells.
"No," Darcy said, stepping forward. "Let me. I’ll show him what ’unqualified’ really means."
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
As he spoke, Darcy began to focus his Raw Vitality. A palpable pressure filled the air as his power surged.
[MUSIC: POWERFUL DYNASTY THEME]
Darcy Hawthorne was the young heir of House Hawthorne, a family that had shot to power over the last few years. No one knew how the Lord of House Hawthorne had found the resources, but they had allowed Darcy to jump three power levels in a row, landing him at the fifth level of the Adept Realm.
The Lord of House Hawthorne was now arguably the strongest fighter in all of Castermere County.
Because of his strength, House Hawthorne was thriving. Other families had no choice but to bow down and accept their place, letting House Hawthorne take the lion’s share of the county’s wealth.
Most of that wealth used to belong to House Lowell. In other words, House Hawthorne had swallowed up House Lowell’s fortune, along with the fortunes of the other families in Castermere County.
And still, House Lowell didn’t dare say a word. That was why Miles had to suck up to Darcy. If he pissed Darcy off, House Hawthorne would retaliate, and things would get even worse for his family. Miles's skill at buttering Darcy up was the only reason House Hawthorne hadn’t completely devoured their assets.
But it was only delaying the inevitable. House Lowell was headed for ruin.
From Darcy's point of view, House Lowell was just a bunch of pathetic losers, Miles included. But since Miles was always boosting his ego, Darcy didn’t mind keeping him around as a lackey.
And now, some human punching bag had the nerve to look down on him. A man like Melvin, who still admired the legendary courage of the former young master of his house, couldn’t stand to see such bravery forgotten. It would be a damn shame if a man like that ended up dead.
[SFX: DOOR OPENING]
Just when Miles thought he was a dead man, the door swung open with a decisive crack, silencing the room. Zane stepped through the threshold, his presence commanding immediate attention. Streamer gleamed at his side, catching the light as he surveyed the scene with cold calculation.
His gaze fell on Melvin—bruised, bloodied, yet defiant even in his humiliation.
Without hesitation, Zane moved forward, his footsteps echoing against the stone floor. The others backed away instinctively, sensing something dangerous in his measured stride and unflinching eyes. Whatever arrogance had filled the chamber moments before evaporated.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL REUNION]
Melvin looked up, disbelief washing over his battered features as recognition dawned. "Zane... I can’t believe you’re back." Suddenly, tears welled in the big man’s eyes, and he choked back a sob.
As the others watched in shock, Zane walked slowly toward Melvin. "Long time no see, old friend. How’d you end up as a human punching bag?" He looked at the older man, waiting.
"It’s a long story," Melvin sighed.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
"You can tell me later. Let’s deal with this first," Zane said, his gaze flicking over to Darcy. "I heard someone wanted to beat you to death. Is that him?"
Zane's confidence was absolute. Darcy, meanwhile, was fuming. Is this guy looking for a fight? The nerve! Doesn’t he know who I am? He's got a death wish! Darcy thought bitterly.
"I’m the one who wants to beat him to death," he sneered. "You got a problem with that?" He paused, then asked, "Do you have any idea who I am?"
He raised an eyebrow, expecting fear, or at least recognition.
Zane just shook his head. "Nope. Can’t say I do."
He twitched his fingers, and the sword at his hip gave a low hum.
"I am—" Darcy was cut off.
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
Before he could finish his own name, Zane’s sword, Streamer, was out.
It moved in a silver blur, a flash of steel that sliced cleanly across Darcy’s neck.
[SFX: CHOKING GURGLE]
Darcy gurgled, his eyes wide with disbelief. He couldn’t speak, couldn’t breathe. In the last seconds of his life, his mind screamed with confusion and regret.
Why didn’t you let me finish? If you knew who I was, you never would have dared! What a waste... I’m dying! My father will avenge me! That was Darcy’s last thought.
Zane looked into Darcy’s wide, lifeless eyes and calmly shook his head.
"I don’t give a damn who you are."
