Ep 539 - Try Again
Word count: 1020
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS VOID]
[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSE]
Thinking they could just roll the dice and stroll across the abyss was a huge mistake. Everyone had completely underestimated the danger, but what happened to the elder from the Mysterious Luna Hall was a brutal wake-up call. This was no game.
The discs were deadly traps, popping up at any second to kill them. That Blazing Thunder Prison from before was so powerful that even a master from the Life-and-Death Realm wouldn't bet on surviving it.
It was a game of pure chance.
[SFX: DICE ROLLING]
As the beam of light spun, a couple more people risked it, stepping deeper into the abyss. Luckily, none of them triggered another Blazing Thunder Prison. Someone rolled a one, then another rolled a five. Before long, the beam of light landed on Tia.
[SFX: DICE CLATTER]
Tia slapped her palm against the dice, sending it skittering across the floor. It landed on a four.
[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION]
[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]
Instantly, four discs materialized in front of her. Three were transparent, but the fourth glowed a deep, emerald green.
Seeing that emerald green disc gave her pause. Just moments ago, the elder from the Mysterious Luna Hall had stepped on a fiery red one and unleashed that Blazing Thunder Prison. Who knew what horrors this emerald one held?
Still, opportunity and risk always went hand-in-hand. Bracing herself, Tia took the step. There was no going back now.
[SFX: FOOTSTEP ON DISC]
The second she stepped onto the emerald disc, a voice boomed through the void.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY BURST]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]
"Light of Longevity. You are rewarded with five hundred years of life," the voice announced, as a brilliant green light erupted from the disc, enveloping her.
"A reward?" Zane murmured, his voice laced with disbelief.
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMUR]
The realization crashed over the crowd, and a wave of envy washed through them.
After the brutal death sentence of the Blazing Thunder Prison, no one had dared to imagine one of these discs could hold something so incredible.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVELATION]
Five hundred years of life.
Sure, there were ways to extend your life in the Heartlands—things like Life Extension Pills or Long Life Pills. But it was never that simple. Those pills were priceless, and their effects were limited. A Life Extension Pill, for example, only worked once, adding a measly three years to your life. The Long Life Pill offered a more tempting twenty years. Still, it came with a devastating side effect: an incurable poison that permanently stunted a warrior’s growth, stopping them from ever getting stronger.
Only a warrior on death's door would risk the Long Life Pill,  just to cling to this world for a few more years. Gaining time always came at a price.
Tia had just gained five centuries for stepping on a glowing tile.
Whispers erupted into debate.
"Cloud Hall's luck is unbelievable. First Zane, now this."
"With their array and Tia at Spirit-Tribulation level, who in the Heartlands can challenge them?"
"They're quasi fifth-grade at minimum. Maybe just fifth-grade."
The green light faded. Tia's expression brightened.
The Guild tycoon nodded across the abyss. "Congratulations, Hall Master."
Tia smiled. "Thank you."
She glanced at Zane, expecting praise.
He leaned closer. "Five hundred years. How old are you, exactly?"
Her glare could have scorched stone. "Older than you. That's all you need to know."
Zane's mouth twitched.
The crowd's mood shifted. If punishment and reward both existed here, every step was a gamble worth taking.
[SFX: PULSING ENERGY]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]
The beam spun again, landing on Maddox.
Zane's chest tightened. If Maddox died here, Elara would be free. Cloud Hall's influence would protect her.
Maddox tapped the die. It rolled to five.
Five discs appeared. Four white. One pitch-black.
Zane's pulse jumped.
Without hesitation, Maddox stepped onto the black disc.
[SFX: OMINOUS RUMBLE]
"Extreme Darkness Illusion."
Black light erupted, swallowing Maddox whole. Its surface churned with writhing beast phantoms.
"Can he survive that?"
"Better if he doesn't. The Heartlands would finally know peace."
The Ethereal Spirit Sect and World Merchants' Guild watched with desperate hope.
Then came the roar.
[SFX: ROAR]
Maddox's voice tore from the sphere—raw, furious, overwhelming. Its force pressed against every warrior like a physical weight.
"I didn't expect the Heartlands to produce someone like this," the handsome man from the Divine Kingdom remarked.
The woman in black didn't glance up. "He's still an ant."
[SFX: VIOLENT BATTLE SOUNDS]
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS MIST]
A storm of violent sounds erupted from the black mist, as if Maddox was battling something immense.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL RESOLUTION]
Slowly, the phantoms flickered out of existence, and the Black Illusion dissolved with them. When the mist finally cleared, Maddox was still standing on the disc, his expression unreadable.
[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]
[AMBIENT: AMAZED CROWD]
A collective gasp went through the crowd. "Maddox survived!"
"He's unbelievably powerful! That Black Illusion had to be some kind of lethal infernal array, and he just tore right through it."
"He really is the top master in the Eastern Territories. No doubt about it."
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL VOID]
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
Everyone was stunned by his power, but only Maddox knew how close he'd come to death. Inside the Black Illusion, an entire menagerie of legendary beasts had materialized to attack him all at once. They weren't as strong as their real-world counterparts, thank God, but his own power had been drastically suppressed—knocked down from the Life-and-Death Realm to the level of a late-stage Spirit-Tribulation warrior.
He’d been forced to burn through a whole arsenal of cultivation methods just to take them down one by one. The battle had pushed him to his absolute limit, draining nearly everything he had before he finally broke free.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]
After his harrowing trial, the beam of light moved on, finally settling on the woman in black from the Divine Kingdom.
No one knew what cultivation methods she commanded. She simply gave the dice a cool, indifferent glance, and it began to spin, as if pushed by her gaze alone.
A moment later, it settled on a six.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
The woman didn’t even flinch. As six discs appeared before her, she calmly walked forward. This time, the sixth one was a bright, canary yellow.
[SFX: ETHEREAL VOICE ECHO]
The moment she stepped onto it, the disembodied voice spoke again. "Throw the dice again."
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]
A faint, knowing smile touched the woman's lips, but a ripple of shock went through the crowd. No punishment? No reward? They all wondered why she was the exception.
