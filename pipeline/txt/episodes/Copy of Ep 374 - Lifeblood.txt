Ep 374 - Lifeblood

[AMBIENT: CLIFF BASE WITH CROWD MURMUR]

At the base of the cliff, the Cloud Hall disciples stood frozen, their minds struggling to process what they had just witnessed. They had spent countless hours running through all sorts of strategies for getting past the flames. But none of their elaborate plans had prepared them for this: someone simply walking through the inferno as though it were nothing more than morning mist.

[SFX: GASPS OF AMAZEMENT]

"Holy crap, the fire can't even touch him!" one disciple burst out, his voice cracking with disbelief. He gripped his companion's arm, needing the physical contact to confirm he wasn't hallucinating. "I didn't know that was even possible!"

Another disciple shook his head slowly, his face pale. "We've been cultivating for years, studying every technique Cloud Hall has to offer, and this outsider just..." His voice trailed off, unable to finish the thought.

The gap between them felt suddenly insurmountable—not just a difference in power, but in fundamental understanding of cultivation itself.

[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATIVE]

Lennon, standing on a nearby rock, went quiet as he stared up at Zane. His face was a blank mask, as if the shock was too much to even register. As a disciple of Cloud Hall, he knew just how hard it was to reach those treasure boxes. The stories were legendary—the farthest anyone had ever gotten was the Head of Cloud Hall himself, who had managed to reach a silver treasure box.

And now, Zane was about to open a blue one. After that, he’d probably go for the silver next.

But he's only Level Three Adept Realm, Lennon thought. Does that mean he has more potential than me?

Lennon knew he was no match for Frank, but he couldn't stomach being weaker than some kid from a second-rate sect. 

Resentment and disbelief churned in his gut. It was a bitter pill to swallow.

[AMBIENT: ROARING FLAMES]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL]

The flames spewing from the arcane mechanism on the cliff couldn't touch Zane as long as his body was covered in those golden runes. His only real worry now was the second attack waiting in the cave—the huge fireball.

That same fireball had sent the cloaked man running after he’d made it through the flames, and Zane had no intention of making the same mistake.

[AMBIENT: CAVE INTERIOR]

[SFX: FLAMES RECEDING]

Letting out a slow breath, Zane watched the flames ahead begin to recede. His body went taut; he knew the second attack was coming.

Unlike the cloaked guy who’d just rushed in, Zane was calm and collected as he stared into the cave’s darkness. People who relied on luck were usually the ones who lacked confidence, and that’s why they failed. If that guy had just thrown everything he had at the fireball, he might have actually snagged the treasure.

Zane stopped just shy of the treasure box. He drew his sword, Streamer, and summoned six Shadow Phantoms to circle him. His Shadow Phantom ability wasn't his most powerful move, but it was versatile and quick to deploy.

[SFX: RUMBLING STONE]

The moment the last flame winked out, a red glow ignited deep inside the cave. A low rumble followed, the sound of grinding rock.

[SFX: MASSIVE FIREBALL EXPLOSION]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

A deafening roar shattered the silence as a massive fireball erupted from the darkness, hurtling toward Zane with unstoppable force.

His heart hammered against his ribs. The platform beneath his feet was barely wide enough for him to stand on, and the rolling fireball towered as tall as he was—a wall of flame with no mercy. There was nowhere to run. Nowhere to hide.

Time seemed to slow. Zane's mind raced through his options, each one more desperate than the last. His Dragon Scales could protect him, but for how long against such concentrated heat? His Shadow Phantoms stood ready, but would they be enough?

He tightened his grip on Streamer, feeling the familiar weight of the blade in his hand. Whatever happened next, he would face it head-on.

[SFX: PHANTOM WAR CRY]
All six Shadow Phantoms lined up and charged. They hit the fireball one by one, each shattering in sequence, bleeding momentum from the boulder with every impact. By the time the last one broke, the fireball had slowed just enough.

[SFX: DRAGON SCALES SURGE / SWORD STRIKE / CRACKING ROCK]

Zane flooded his body with Dragon Scale power, raised Streamer, and drove it forward. Cracks spiderwebbed across the burning boulder — then it exploded apart, flaming chunks raining down on the disciples below.

He'd been pushed back three feet. No more.

[SFX: SLOW FOOTSTEPS]

He sheathed Streamer and walked to the blue treasure box. Slowly. Every Cloud Hall disciple was watching, and he wanted them to feel every step.

[SFX: CHEST LATCH CLICKING]

The box shook violently the moment he touched it. A red shadow burst from inside — he twisted away but it circled back and drove straight into his chest.

His heart stopped.

[SFX: SILENCE — THIRTY BEATS]

Then a single thunderous heartbeat rang out — so loud it carried all the way down the cliff. The disciples below froze, staring up at him.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL GATEWAY]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

The treasure in the blue box was known to bolster a cultivator's vitality. They'd seen for themselves how Zane's own spirit had grown stronger after merging with the blood shadow, the transformation visible even through the Scrying Slate.

Toby shook his head in awe as he watched the magical display. "My God, it's the Lifeblood of the Nine-Headed Dragon! And from a mature one, no less!" 

He glanced at the others, searching their faces for recognition. "Ring a bell?"

[MUSIC: TENSE FLASHBACK]

Sir Kenneth's eyes went wide with recognition. "You mean like with Elder Osmond?" As Osmond's best friend, he remembered it all too well. "When that enemy cut off both his hands in that fight? He managed to get away, and when he came back three days later, they'd grown back. They were still weaker than his old ones, but it was enough of a surprise for him to finish the guy."

"We all know Osmond has incredible vitality," the Leader of Providence Sect said with a sigh, "but few know why. It's because of the treasure in that very box. The Lifeblood of the Nine-Headed Dragon supercharges a cultivator's own life force. Zane is one lucky kid." The disappointment was clear in his voice: he wished it had been his own disciple, Lewis.

Toby just smiled. "Well, maybe this is just the warm-up. You really think Zane's going to be satisfied with just the Lifeblood? His next stop is that silver box. The Head of Cloud Hall is the only person who's ever opened one. I wonder if Zane can do it, too."

"Are you serious?" Lady Sigmund cut in with a cold sneer. "He stumbled his way to the blue one on dumb luck. You actually think he's going for a silver one? In his dreams!"

[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL DRAMATIC]

Kenneth finally snapped, his face flushing with anger."What the hell is that supposed to mean, Lady Sigmund?" He'd been biting his tongue through all her little digs, but this was the last straw. "Zane hasn't done a single thing to you! He's not even competing with Frank, your precious disciple's fiancé! So why do you keep tearing him down? You think I'm deaf? I'm the head of the Skyreach Sect, and I've heard every last word you've said!"
