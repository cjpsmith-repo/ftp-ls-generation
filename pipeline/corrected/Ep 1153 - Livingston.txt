Ep 1153 - Livingston

Word count: 1054

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS EXPLORATION]
[AMBIENT: QUIET CAVE WITH DRIPPING WATER]
This cave was a definite downgrade from the last one—so small you could take it all in with a single glance, and almost painfully uninteresting. While Freya carefully sifted through the remaining ancient treasures, Zane decided to stick around for a few minutes and take one last lap, just in case they’d missed something.
After a quick stroll, he was sure of it. There were no more secrets here.
When Zane turned back, his eyes went wide. The mountain of treasures, which had been piled high just moments before, was now half its original size. The other warriors had clearly been busy stuffing everything they could grab into their Storage Rings, looting the place clean. Zane just shook his head. Once the Celestial Essence was gone, these things were basically worthless junk.
[SFX: METALLIC GLINT]
A faint smile touched his lips as he started to leave, but then something caught his eye: a sword, stuck upright in the ground nearby.
*That thing can’t possibly be an ancient treasure,* he thought, his brow furrowing in confusion.
Everything else in the pile, though worn down by time and drained of its power, had once been a top-tier item. This sword, however, was a much lower grade. The blade was completely dull. It barely even looked like a sword.
Even though the cave had been a bust, Zane couldn’t help but feel a flicker of interest. The sword was just so bizarrely out of place, so obviously worse than everything else around it. 
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
[SFX: HAND GRIPPING METAL]
He walked over and wrapped his hand around the exposed part of the blade.
[SFX: STRAIN EFFORT]
It didn’t budge. The sword was stuck fast, as if the earth itself had a death grip on it.
“Huh.”
His curiosity deepened. He’d only used a fraction of his strength, but pulling it out should have been effortless. Maybe it was just buried deeper than he thought.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS PLANTING]
[SFX: METAL SCRAPING STONE]
Zane planted his feet, got a solid two-handed grip on the sword—one hand resting on the flat of the dull blade—and gave a real tug. The metal didn’t even scratch him, not that it would have mattered. With his current strength, a sharp blade wasn’t much of a threat.
Then, he put his back into it, pulling with all his might.
[SFX: METAL SCRAPING STONE]
[SFX: SWORD BREAKING FREE]
The blade shot upward, breaking free from the ground with a huge surge of force.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
[SFX: CAVE RUMBLING]
The entire cave trembled.
The other warriors froze mid-loot. 
Heads came up across the cave. Eyes locked onto Zane.
“What’s he pulling?”
“Looks like a sword. Can’t be ordinary if it’s buried that deep.”
“Should we—”
“You want to try taking something out of his hands? After what happened to that ogre warrior?”
That settled it. No one moved.
Zane, meanwhile, had stopped mid-pull.
What he’d grabbed wasn’t a sword.
Below the hilt was a head — metal, smooth, oval. Below the head, a neck. Below the neck, a body. He kept pulling, and the rest of it came up out of the ground: four limbs, a torso, the whole strange shape of it emerging from the cracks in the cave floor like something that had been planted there and forgotten.
He took a step back.
“A… puppet.”
[SFX: DIRT AND STONE FALLING]
It was unlike anything he’d seen — bald, with an oddly shaped oval head and a sword-like ornament jutting from the top of its skull. That ornament was what had been sticking out of the ground. The thing he’d mistaken for a sword was just the puppet’s hat.
The other warriors stared. A few of them felt the familiar pull of greed — whatever Zane had just found, it had come out of the same abyss as the World Tree seed. It couldn’t just be nothing. But then they thought about the ogre warrior, about what Zane’s blade had looked like coming down, and the greed quietly went back where it came from.
[SFX: FAINT PUPPET WHISPER]
Throughout the whole process, the puppet had been making noise — a faint, dry, barely-audible whispering. Zane couldn’t make out a single word. Whatever it was trying to say, it didn’t have the strength to say it properly. But it was saying something. Which meant it was still alive in there.
Zane grabbed it by the head and dragged it fully clear of the ground, then dropped it to one side.
[SFX: HEAVY THUD]
It hit the cave floor hard. The material held without so much as a dent.
He circled it once, looking it over. On its back, he found a small groove.
Lavender had wandered through this cave the last time the Endless Abyss opened. She hadn’t found it. No one had, across all six openings. Whatever this thing was, it had been down here a long time waiting for someone to pull hard enough.
Zane immediately pulled a supreme Aether crystal from his Storage Ring and slotted it into a small groove on the back of the puppet.
[SFX: MECHANICAL CLICK]
With a loud click, the puppet suddenly cried out, its voice thin and crackly, “I… I can actually…”
But just as quickly, the voice faded into silence.
Zane glanced at the groove in confusion. The supreme Aether crystal was already completely drained.
“That was fast,” he muttered, stunned. The puppet had sucked it dry in an instant, and it still wasn’t enough.
[SFX: CRYSTAL INSERTION]
Without a second thought, Zane took out a Sovereign Stone from his Storage Ring and pushed it into the groove.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT COMEDIC FANFARE]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING CAVE]
“I… I’m actually alive again! 
[SFX: EXCITED SHOUTING VOICE]
I can’t believe it!” the puppet shouted, its voice now booming with excitement.
Zane stared at it. “Who are you?”
[SFX: WOODEN PUPPET SHUFFLING]
The puppet looked Zane up and down before slowly pulling its limbs together and shuffling toward him. It paused, then puffed out its chest and announced with a proud grin.
“I… am Livingston! Haha!”
It clearly expected this announcement to shock the entire cave. 
[MUSIC: AWKWARD SILENCE UNDERSCORE]
But instead, the other warriors just stared back, completely clueless.
“Livingston?”
“Never heard of him.”
“Is that name supposed to mean something?”
Zane had the same question. He did a quick mental check—not only had his sword spirit, Lavender, never heard the name, but even the ancient Cobalt Dragon drew a blank. Clearly, whatever fame Livingston once had, it hadn’t survived the centuries he’d spent buried.
[SFX: INDIGNANT SCREECHING]
“Huh? You’ve never heard of Livingston?” the puppet screeched, its voice dripping with wounded pride. “I am the single greatest weaponsmith in the entire universe! Are you people just ignorant, or completely clueless?”
