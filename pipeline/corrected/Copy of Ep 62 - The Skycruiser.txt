Ep 62 - The Skycruiser
Word Count: 1264
[SFX: HOVERING SKYCRAFT]
Skycruisers were a rare sight in the Empire, and for good reason—they cost as much as a legendary weapon. But while a weapon could actually boost a warrior's power, a Skycruiser was just a way to get around.
That's why no house back in Castermere County, where House Lowell was based, would ever drop that kind of cash on one. You only really saw them in wealthy hubs like the Capital.
[SFX: HOVERING ENGINE SOUND]
[MUSIC: AWESTRUCK DISCOVERY]
As the massive Skycruiser hovered, slowly descending, Zane just stood there, completely awestruck.
He'd never seen one this big before. The other disciples, used to the sight, got to their feet as the behemoth approached. It was time to go.
[SFX: METALLIC GANGWAY EXTENDING]
The Skycruiser gracefully docked alongside the Bluesky Parking Ground, its sleek hull gleaming in the sunlight as a metallic gangway extended from its side to the edge of the lot. Several passengers had already disembarked, gathering near the ramp while Skyreach Sect disciples presented their tokens to the stern-faced attendants. Upon verification, they ascended the gangway with varying degrees of excitement and trepidation visible on their faces.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON GANGWAY]
Zane joined the steady flow of people and offered his token when his turn arrived. The attendants exchanged startled glances when they registered his Marrow Refining Level, their eyebrows rising in undisguised surprise. Such a vessel typically transported only High-tier cultivators or higher, yet they remained silent, perhaps sensing something unusual about the young man before them.
After a brief inspection that left Zane's heart pounding with anxiety, they motioned him through with reluctant nods. He crossed the gangway with measured steps, feeling the slight sway beneath his feet as he boarded the magnificent vessel.
[AMBIENT: LUXURIOUS VESSEL INTERIOR]
The interior of the Skycruiser revealed itself as unexpectedly spacious, its polished wooden panels and ornate fixtures speaking of luxury beyond anything Zane had experienced in Castermere County. Rows of plush seats lined the curved walls, each designed for comfort during long journeys through the skies.
[SFX: ENGINES RUMBLING TO LIFE]
A tremor ran through the floor as the Skycruiser lifted off. Zane turned to the window.
The Skyreach Sect spread out below him — an unbroken mountain range stretching to the horizon, vast enough to swallow the Capital whole. But what caught his eye wasn't the grandeur of it. It was a single mountain, standing apart from the rest. Where the others were green and lush, this one was pure black, cloaked in slow-rolling gray smoke that reeked of sulfur even from this height. It looked exactly as Rhys had described it.
Infernal Peak.
Elara was in there.
Zane pressed his eyes to the glass, willing himself to see something — anything. But he caught himself and looked away with a bitter smile. They were thousands of feet up. He couldn't make out a single figure in those mountains even if he tried. Still, he kept his gaze locked on the black peak until the Skycruiser banked and it slid out of view.
Only then did he look away.
[SFX: CREATURE WINGS FLUTTERING]
"Was that a Razorwing?" Zane whispered, his throat suddenly dry as he caught a clear glimpse of the creature through a nearby portal window. A chill ran down his spine as he studied its features.
The beast displayed an unsettling emerald carapace that seemed to absorb and reflect light in equal measure.
Its massive compound eyes dominated its angular head, gleaming with an alien intelligence that made Zane's skin crawl. Roughly the size of a full-grown man, the creature's translucent wings fluttered with deadly precision while its six appendages moved with predatory grace.
Most terrifying were the uppermost limbs—evolved into long, flat blades that appeared sharp enough to slice through steel. The nightmarish vision burned itself into Zane's memory, a harbinger of the dangers that awaited them all.
[SFX: GLASS SHATTERING]
[MUSIC: BATTLE CHAOS]
Zane had barely left his seat when the window beside him exploded inward. A Razorwing burst through. A pair of green blades shot through the jagged opening, slashing at his waist from both sides with terrifying speed.
It was the first time Zane had seen one of the vicious beasts up close, and a jolt of adrenaline shot through him. But instead of flinching, he reacted, throwing a powerful punch right at the beast's stomach.
But the exoskeleton on its midsection was thick. Zane had thought his punch would be enough to crack it, but the shell was just too tough.
"Damn it!"
His missed attack gave the giant insect the perfect opening to counter with its two blade-like forelimbs.
[SFX: SWORD SLICING AIR]
But before they could connect, a sword sliced through the air from behind Zane. A black-red light shimmered from the blade as it split the Razorwing's head clean open. Even with its head split, the beast thrashed, its blades swinging in a frenzied, blind attack. But with its speed and strength gone, Zane had no trouble ducking to safety.
"Thanks for the save," Zane said to the black-robed disciple, backing into a safer corner.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY]
The disciple looked young, with long hair pulled back. The black-red energy glowing on his sword wasn't just Life Energy—it was pure Life Force. "That means he's at the Nature Level," Zane realized.
"Don't worry about it. We're both disciples of the Skyreach Sect," the young man said casually. He then added, "Their abdomens are tough as rock. Go for the head. And don't let them corner you unless you're made of steel."
Zane nodded, taking it in.
The guy might have seemed cold, but he'd not only saved his life but also given him crucial intel on the creatures' weak spots. It was rare to find someone that decent in the Skyreach Sect.
[AMBIENT: AERIAL BATTLE]
[SFX: CREATURE SCREECHES]
The Razorwings kept hurling themselves at the Skycruiser. The battle was on. No one had expected the swarm to dive-bomb them while they were still airborne.
In the chaos, an alarmed voice shouted, "They're tearing through the bottom of the cruiser!"
[SFX: SMASH]
The Skycruiser's power source was at its base; if that got destroyed, they were all in deep trouble. Zane dodged a set of incoming blades, focused his strength into one arm, and smashed a creature's head with a single punch.
[SFX: AIRBORNE IMPACTS]
He seized the twitching carcass and flung it backward, knocking aside two advancing Razorwings that had attempted to flank him.
The creatures vastly outnumbered the refiners, with encirclement promising certain death. Heeding the disciple's warning, Zane maneuvered carefully to avoid entrapment. Of the four that had swarmed him, he'd eliminated one with a single punch, used its remains as a barrier against two others, leaving just one opponent.
Child's play.
Before the creature could strike, Zane sprang forward, grasping the vulnerable joints where its blades connected to its torso.
[SFX: VIOLENT RIPPING SOUND]
[SFX: CREATURE SHRIEK]
With one savage wrench, he ripped both appendages completely off.
A single Razorwing posed little challenge, Zane reflected. Any High-tier candidate could dispatch one easily, as could someone at his Marrow Refining Level. Even a Core Strength disciple might prevail with sufficient effort.
[SFX: DEAFENING SCREECH]
But as the dismembered Razorwing collapsed at his feet, a deafening screech pierced the air. Zane's blood ran cold as he turned to face the source—only to find himself looking up and even more of them.
As the massive swarm blotted out the sun, the first of their razor-sharp blades began to tear into the Skycruiser's hull with a deafening screech of rending metal.
