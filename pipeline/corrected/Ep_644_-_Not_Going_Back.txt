Ep 644 - Not Going Back
Word count: 1088
[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE ATMOSPHERE]
A pang of envy hit Fabian and the others as Esther Sterling presented the Black Nether Beads to Lavender. The feeling vanished as quickly as it came, though—Lavender was the Demon Night Queen, after all. Such treasures were her right.
As Zane tucked the two beads into his Storage Ring, Esther dropped to one knee before Lavender.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
“My Queen,” she pleaded, “the Tide of Chaos will soon make the Dark Nether Cave treacherous. I beg you, return to the Tower of Sin!”
But even as she spoke, Esther knew it was pointless. Fabian must have already given Zane the same warning, and the fact that he was still standing here told her everything she needed to know. Both he and Lavender were determined to stay.
This was the second time someone had tried to send her back to the tower. Even if it was for her and Zane’s own safety, Lavender’s expression hardened at the words. She knew Zane wasn’t about to pass up a golden opportunity to explore this place. He was the one who’d helped her reclaim the throne, and she hated hearing Esther say something that might undermine his confidence.
“I’m not going back,” Lavender said, her tone laced with ice.
The queen’s coldness sent a shiver down Esther’s spine. Realizing her advice had only made things worse, she quickly rose to her feet. “In that case, I beg you to let us stay and explore the Dark Nether Cave with you.”
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL MELODY]
“That’s a great idea!” Fabian exclaimed, his relief palpable. Esther was the most powerful War Lord in the Tower of Sin, a woman whose name had once climbed to the fourth row of the Talent Tablet. With her current strength, she could probably hold her own against an average War Sage. Having her on their team made things feel a hell of a lot safer.
Just a few days ago, Esther had been worried sick about Zane’s plan to follow the human War Lords into the cave. She’d even invited him to travel with the Demon Night instead, a calculated move to keep him safe. After all, Lavender was still a sword spirit, intrinsically linked to Zane; if he died, she would perish with him. Everything she did was for her queen.
Unfortunately, Zane had flatly refused her offer.
Now, with a real threat looming, she decided the only option left was to stay by their side and protect them herself.
[AMBIENT: DARK FOREST]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Esther and Fabian immediately pulled out their maps, putting their heads together to find a relatively safe route. Yolande had been standing off to the side, looking completely relaxed, but she suddenly went rigid. She spun around, her finger aimed like a dart into the suffocating darkness.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
[SFX: IMPACT]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
Four figures emerged from the forest.
Esther's eyes moved to Yolande for just a moment — sharp, involuntary. The four had concealed themselves well enough that even she hadn't detected them. And yet a tenth-level War General had picked them out without blinking.
"Twilight Group," Esther said, turning back to the four. Her body began to flicker with light. "Are you here to kill Zane?"
It wasn't an unreasonable question. The Twilight Group were assassins — former vassals of the Ogres, cultivated over years to eliminate problems in the Tower. With the Ogres expelled, their protection was gone, and their standing had collapsed. Zane had encountered their kind before.
The lead figure — lean, sharp-eyed, carefully composed — raised both hands. This was Garrick, the strongest seat controller in the Twilight Group.
"Not at all," he said quickly. "The Tide of Chaos has made the cave too dangerous to navigate alone. We only want to travel with your group."
Esther's light didn't fade. "You think you're qualified?"
The words landed like a slap. Garrick's face tightened, but he didn't dare push back. Facing Esther directly, they had no chance.
"Let them come," Fabian said. "The Twilight Group are scouts. They can sense danger before it reaches us. In a place like this, that's worth more than an extra sword."
Esther weighed it. Even if Garrick had other intentions, he'd have to go through her first. She let the light die. "Fine."
Garrick smiled carefully. "Brother Fabian is right. Our strength isn't much, but we do have our uses."
The group set off.
[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND PASSAGE]
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
For an Ogre, breaking through to become a War Sage was no easy feat. That secret passageway they’d used had made Malcolm’s ascension a living hell. It was just big enough for a couple of humans or Demon Night members, but for his kind, it was a suffocating crawlspace. The surge of power, trapped in that claustrophobic nightmare, had nearly killed him.
But in the end, he’d clawed his way through. Timothy hadn’t broken through yet, but Malcolm’s newfound strength would be more than enough to finally kill Zane.
[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE]
[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]
“We've gotten word,” Timothy reported, following close behind him. “They’ve found Zane.”
Malcolm gave a sharp, satisfied nod. “Good.” He used to rank below Timothy, but his recent breakthrough had completely flipped their dynamic.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING CAVE]
“I’ve been stuck in this shithole for months. It feels good to finally get out,” Malcolm growled, emerging from the other side and stepping into the Dark Nether Cave.
Timothy followed, stretching his body. “I have no idea how Colin and Neil squeezed through that thing,” he grumbled. “It’s suffocating!”
Malcolm just grunted.
[SFX: KNUCKLES CRACKING]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]
“Let’s go. The humans had their fun playing around in our tower. It’s time for the Ogres to take back what’s ours.” He cracked his knuckles. “Starting with Zane.”
As the new controller of the Tower of Sin, Zane had been untouchable. The Ogres couldn’t confront him directly, but they weren’t the type to just sit back and do nothing. They had been waiting for their chance. Now that he’d ventured out into the Dark Nether Cave, he was vulnerable, and Malcolm’s promotion to War Sage was the ace up their sleeve.
[MUSIC: DARK TRIUMPHANT THEME]
As long as they killed Zane, the Ogres could march right back into the tower and reclaim their glory. Every seat that the humans and the Demon Night had stolen would be theirs again.
After all, no other race could produce a War Sage in the Tower of Sin. Only the Ogres could.
