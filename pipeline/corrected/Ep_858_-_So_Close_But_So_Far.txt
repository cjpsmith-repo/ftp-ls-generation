Ep 858 - So Close But So Far

Word count: 1100

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT RUINS]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORCHESTRAL]

Randy’s voice faded as the scale of it sank in — millions of weapons, an entire civilization’s worth of forging, all cursed into uselessness.

The Feather Emperor’s Secret Land had once served as the dragon-blooded empire’s private armory.

When their empire collapsed, the dragon-blooded cursed every weapon they couldn’t destroy, rendering millions of arms permanently useless. Saint-level geniuses exploring the island now dismissed them as junk—low-grade mystic arms to Relic-Class weapons, all rusted and worthless.

But a rare few had escaped the curses. An untouched Relic-Class weapon would be the score of a lifetime, though finding one proved nearly impossible. The geniuses called it “picking through trash.”

Some had abandoned the pyramid mines to try their luck at the arsenal. Most continued digging.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

At least, they had until Zane led those purple Stone-Eater Bugs down to the lower floors. Now, nobody wanted to set foot back inside. The potential rewards of mining just weren’t worth dying for.

“Got it,” Zane said with a nod.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

He had zero interest in picking through trash, so he decided to head back into the pyramid anyway.

Callan and Fiona were skipping the arsenal for now, and so were Fredrick, the Crown Prince, and the other royals. For people of their status, even a top-grade Relic-Class weapon was hardly worth a second glance. They were only interested in Semi-Divine and Divine weapons, so they were content to wait outside the pyramid and avoid the danger.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL BUILDUP]

As Zane prepared to leave, all the warriors who stayed behind just stared at him.

Zane couldn’t care less what they thought. Still, since he was the one who’d led the bugs down there and spooked Callan and Fiona out of the mine, he offered, “If those things start heading back to the upper floors, I’ll come out and let you know.”

Fiona hadn’t been that into mining anyway. She’d only gone inside the pyramid three times, spending most of her time sitting in a corner, watching Zane go in and out. She wanted to tell him not to go, but she held her tongue. Just like Randy, she knew there was no changing Zane’s mind once it was made up.

Fredrick and a few of the other royals, however, seemed almost pleased to hear Zane’s plan.

This time, Zane bypassed the fortieth floor entirely and went straight to the forty-first.

[MUSIC: TENSE EXPLORATION]

The floors above the fortieth were sparse. The forty-first had around two hundred leaves. The forty-second had a hundred and fifty. The forty-third, a hundred. The forty-fourth, sixty. Above that — ten leaves on the forty-fifth, six on the forty-sixth, three on the forty-seventh. The tree narrowed to almost nothing at its peak.

From the forty-first floor, Zane scanned the floors below. He found the five purple Stone-Eater Bugs quickly — they had returned to the fortieth floor, back to their usual range. He dropped down, sent word to the group outside, then climbed straight back up.

Floor by floor, he went. Forty-first, forty-second, forty-third, forty-fourth. Each one was empty of Ancient Heartstones. He hadn’t expected otherwise.

Below, Fiona sat in her corner of the pyramid and watched him go in and out, in and out. She found herself wondering — who was he collecting all these stones for? The thought brought a faint curve to her lips.

Then she saw him enter the forty-fifth floor.

She blinked. Even she knew about the top three floors by now. Soul Sea masters from all four kingdoms had tried everything for years — the forty-fifth, forty-sixth, and forty-seventh floors had never yielded a single thing. The leaves couldn’t be broken. There was no point going in.

Zane went in anyway.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: WONDER AND DISCOVERY]

The moment he stepped through the narrow door and landed on the forty-fifth floor, a faint fragrance reached him.

He paused. There had been nothing like it on the forty-fourth floor. He looked up.

Through the gaps between the leaves, he could see the forty-sixth floor’s six leaves above, and above those, the three leaves of the forty-seventh floor — the very top of the tree. And there, in the center of the three highest leaves, something was blooming.

A flower. But not like any flower he had ever seen.

Its petals, its stamen, its stem — every part of it was made of ore. Mineral upon mineral, fused into the shape of a blossom, angular and geometric where a real flower would be soft. The symmetry of it was uncanny. It looked sharp enough to cut, and yet the overall shape was unmistakably beautiful.

Zane stared at it for a long moment. Then he forced himself to look away. He had more important things to do.

He looked down.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DISCOVERY]

[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]

Deep inside, Zane came across something strange. Ancient Heartstones always appeared in clusters of even numbers—ten, twelve, fourteen—arranged in a circle. The ores in front of him now followed that pattern — fourteen of them, each roughly the size of an egg, their surfaces almost translucent.

Peering through the thick, energy-infused leaf that blocked his path, Zane could see right through their shimmering outer shells. Floating inside each one were Aether crystals.

They have Raw Vitality, too! he realized, his focus sharpening. And it’s so potent, so strong. 

[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BUILDUP]

The closer he looked, the more he could feel the sheer power radiating from them, a force so immense that not even the thick leaf could dampen it.

Normally, crushing an Ancient Heartstone released only a tiny spark of Raw Vitality; they just didn’t contain very much. But these were different. He guessed a single one of them held a thousand times more Raw Vitality than a normal heartstone. That meant one would be more than enough to completely restore Tia.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]

Looking at the small, glowing stones, Zane was hit with a sense of awe at the incredible things the universe could create. At the same time, a powerful thrill shot through him.

Just one, he thought, his eyes locked on the prize. I just need to get one of them out of here. That’s all I ask.

But there was a problem. No one had ever managed to break through the ten leaves on the forty-fifth floor, the six on the forty-sixth, or the three on the forty-seventh. Not even the most powerful masters at the Soul Sea Realm in the four divine kingdoms could move them. 

The kingdoms had probably even used the Reverse Spirit Channel to ask for help from the humans in the upper world, and they still couldn’t do a thing about them.

[MUSIC: SOMBER REALIZATION]

The reality of the situation was crushing. As much as he wanted just one, getting it was damn near impossible.
