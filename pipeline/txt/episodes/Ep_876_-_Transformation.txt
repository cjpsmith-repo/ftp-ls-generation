Ep 876 - Transformation

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

The great worlds of the Upper Realms were overflowing with resources—powerful training methods, ancient legacies, and just sheer, sprawling space. When it came to training, warriors from up there had access to a level of support that fighters in the Lower Realms could only dream of. 

The Upper Realms definitely had their perks, but life in the Lower Realms had its own advantages, too. For one, the competition wasn’t nearly as cutthroat.

This difference in attitude was perfectly captured by the Tribulation Flower. Warriors from the Lower Realms avoided it like the plague, since it made their ascension trials exponentially harder. Fighters struggling to break through to the Soul Sea Realm just wanted to survive; the last thing they needed was a greater challenge. 

For warriors of the Upper Realms, however, the flower was a priceless treasure, more valuable than even a Divine-Class weapon.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED STRINGS]

Zane had no intention of being trapped in the Lower Realms. To prepare for his own leap from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm to the Life-and-Death Realm, he needed to build a rock-solid foundation.

The problem was that the stronger a warrior’s foundation, the harder their Celestial Tribulation. 

Tia, for instance, practiced the Goddess Path, making her far stronger than any other warrior at her level. When she made her breakthrough, the Lesser Celestial Tribulation she faced was so powerful that it even included a rare Nightmare Tribulation. 

Zane was only at the middle stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, but he could already go toe-to-toe with masters from the Soul Sea Realm. Unless they trapped him in a domain, Zane was practically unstoppable.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

This meant the tribulation he would have to face would be unimaginable. Adding the effects of the Tribulation Flower to that? It was a potential death sentence.

Zane stared at the beautiful flower.

[SFX: WEAPON SWING]

Without a second of hesitation, he walked over, gripped his black star pickax, and swung.

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY BURST]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC IMPACT]

An invisible force exploded from the tool. Zane knew it was impossibly powerful; he wondered if anything in the Upper Realms could stand against it, let alone here. The Law of Causality woven into the pickax was simply too absolute.

[SFX: GLASS SHATTERING]

The flower shattered. The polygonal petals broke apart like glass, revealing the stamen at its center — a column as thick as a wrist, crystalline, filled with a light green liquid. The moment Zane held it, an intoxicating fragrance hit him.

Around him, the Soul Sea masters who had stayed to watch went very still. Every one of them had spent years wondering what that flower did. Every one of them had failed to reach it. The envy on their faces was plain.

Pick the stamen and drink the nectar, the Cobalt Dragon said.

Zane brought the stamen to his lips and poured the liquid in.

[SFX: BURNING ENERGY]

[MUSIC: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]

The burning started in his mouth and didn’t stop. It spread down his throat, into his chest, and then his stomach — as if someone had poured fire directly into him. Then the real pain arrived. His organs felt like they were alight. His Vitality surged almost out of control. He reached for Serene Void — and couldn’t get there. The pain was too great. He dropped to the leaf and rolled.

“Ah—”

He rolled off the edge.

[SFX: FALLING]

[SFX: HARD IMPACT]

He dropped through the pyramid’s light circle and reappeared at the base, hitting the ground and twisting. The stone beneath him cratered with each movement. Fiona, Randy, and Callan pushed through the crowd and gathered around him.

Randy frowned. “Could he be poisoned?”

Fiona shook her head. “Dragon Arsenic didn’t affect him. There’s nothing in this realm that could poison Zane.”

Scott, descending from the tree, said quietly, “He swallowed the stamen of the flower at the top. I don’t know why the reaction is this strong.”

York’s lip curled. “That stamen is probably one of the most poisonous things in existence. He ate it without knowing what it was. He deserves it.”

War King Wind turned on him, voice cold. “Even if Zane dies, no one else can control the Black Star Pickax. No one in the seven divine kingdoms can mine those floors. What exactly is your plan then?”

York said nothing for a moment. “We have enough Soul Cores this year,” he said finally. “Once the kingdoms control the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land, we won’t need more.”

War King Wind didn’t bother responding.

As the other powerful masters of the Soul Sea Realm continued their frantic debate, Zane suddenly stopped writhing on the floor. A powerful aura began to radiate from his body.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

“What’s happening?” one of the warriors exclaimed. “His aura is getting stronger!”

“He pulled through,” another said, his eyes wide with disbelief. “He’s advancing to the late stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm!”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

“He actually leveled up? At a time like this? It’s unbelievable!”

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]

The pain vanished. Zane felt his Vitality gathering in his core, condensing in a way he’d never experienced before. His Vitality, once like a gas, was being squeezed down until it started turning into liquid. Only warriors at the Life-and-Death Realm were supposed to be able to condense their Vitality. He was still in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, but his body was already preparing for the next stage of his journey.

The Vitality within his core continued to compress, its flow slowing as it thickened. For most warriors, condensing Vitality was an extremely slow process, and the end result flowed like water. But as Zane’s Vitality condensed, it grew heavier and heavier, turning into a thick, viscous paste.

Sitting on the ground, Zane frowned. “It’s getting harder to make my Vitality circulate. I think it’s starting to freeze.”

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

The Cobalt Dragon’s voice rang in his mind, laced with shock. Solidifying? That’s… bizarre. I can’t believe it’s already hardening when you haven’t even officially broken through to the Life-and-Death Realm!

Zane was just confused. “Vitality can solidify? What do you mean?”

What exactly was happening to him?
