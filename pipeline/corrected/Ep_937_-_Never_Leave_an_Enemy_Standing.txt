Ep 937 - Never Leave an Enemy Standing

Word count: 1081

[AMBIENT: TENSE ROOM ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]

A sneer tightened the lips of the young man in black. Vorn, however, just asked, “I'm not in a hurry. But Zane, why do you need to borrow The Compass Wheel?”

Zane smiled. “I have an enemy in the Heartlands,” he explained. “I need to kill him before I go. This has gone on long enough.”

[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]

A murmur of shock rippled through those who knew the name Simon. Zane wanted to kill his enemy now? And not just anyone, but Simon?

As they all knew, Simon wasn't reckless and arrogant like Maddox. The man was cautious to a fault. Even the World Merchants' Guild, with all their eyes and ears in The Heartlands, had trouble tracking him.

It didn't help that anyone powerful enough to have surpassed the Soul Sea Realm—like the man sneering at him now—was notoriously short-tempered, and their group already seemed to have it out for Zane.

Kieran stepped forward. “Zane, a man like Simon has a dozen boltholes. It could take us forever to find him. If he gets wind you're back in the Heartlands, he'll just go to ground. You can't rush this.”

Zane waved him off. He couldn't just let Simon continue to be a threat. If Simon would just crawl into a hole and stay there for good, Zane might have let it go. He didn't enjoy killing, but he never left an enemy standing. If someone hadn't crossed an unforgivable line, he’d let them be.

[MUSIC: RESOLUTE DETERMINATION]

But Simon was a fool who didn't know when to quit. Trying to resurrect the Corrupted Divine Kingdom was bad enough, but doing it while Zane was away? That just sealed his fate.

“Oh, that's not a problem for us. Won't take long at all,” Vorn said with a nod. “Alright, then. Just point us in the right direction, and I'll have Wade pilot The Compass Wheel to take you there.”

The young man in black's jaw clenched. Who did this guy think he was? Some nobody from the Life-and-Death Realm, ordering them around like he was somebody. Zane might be a talented fighter, but that didn't give him the right.

Zane didn't even glance at him. “Thanks, Vorn. I appreciate it.”

He then turned to Tia and Cora. “You guys wait here. I'll be right back!”

“But Zane, Simon…” Kieran started, still worried.

A reassuring smile touched Zane's lips. “Kieran, don't worry. I've got this.”

With a soft whistle, the Ancestral Dragon leaped from Cora's arms into Zane's. Zane then stepped onto The Compass Wheel.

[SFX: ACTIVE ENGINE]

The moment he was on board, he reached into the world's memory. Every corner of the Heartlands spread out before him — every mountain, every valley, every living creature. He found Simon in an instant.

“Got him,” Zane said quietly, and gave Wade the location.

[SFX: RAPID ACCELERATION]

The Compass Wheel rose slowly, then shot forward like a bolt loosed from a bow. In the space of a single breath, it had become a thin line on the horizon and vanished.

Kieran stared at the empty sky. “That speed…” he murmured.

Hobbes shook his head slowly. “Zane's world is no longer one we can follow.”

Only three years ago, Zane had been a rising name in the Heartlands — remarkable, but still within the scope of what they could understand. Now he stood somewhere entirely beyond them, and the distance was still growing.

Tia watched the sky where the Compass Wheel had disappeared. A small smile touched her lips. “But he's still Zane, isn't he?”

The three Guild leaders looked at each other. Then, one by one, they nodded.

[MUSIC: DARK BROODING STRINGS]

Simon was a cautious man. He always had been — careful to a fault, some would say cowardly. He had lived in hiding for hundreds of years and had grown thoroughly tired of it. When he had finally thrown his lot in with Maddox and helped build the Corrupted Divine Kingdom, it had felt like a chance at something real.

Then Zane had destroyed all of it. Maddox dead. The Corrupted Divine Kingdom finished. And Simon had run.

Now, in the months since, regret had been eating at him like a worm in wood. When Tia had been struck by the Life and Death Wind and lay dying, when the Guild's old leader had already fallen — he had been right there. He could have killed Zane then. He could have ruled the Heartlands. The first Divine Kingdom in a thousand years, and it could have been his.

He had missed it. And there was no medicine for that kind of regret.

But then word reached him that Zane had left for the Divine Kingdom Continent. And Simon had allowed himself to believe in a second chance. Experts were as common as clouds in the Divine Kingdoms. The likelihood of Zane not returning was real. And the three Guild leaders, formidable as they were together, couldn't touch him.

He had begun gathering the old remnants of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom. He had sent assassins to the Heavenly Stellar Palace — carefully, probing for a crack in the defenses. So far, none had found one.

Today, he had called his people together to decide their next move.

[SFX: WHOOSHING SOUND]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Before their meeting could even begin, he spotted a thin line slicing across the sky. It looked like a shooting star, but it blazed across the heavens with an unnatural speed, tearing a seam in the air as it came.

“What is that?” Simon's eye twitched. An uneasy feeling coiled in his gut, sharp and sudden.

[SFX: MURMURING CROWD]

Hearing him, all the subordinates of the Corrupted Divine Kingdom looked up.

“A celestial omen!”

“Look at the speed!”

“Could it be a treasure falling from the heavens?”

“Let's go get a closer look!”

No one could tell what it was, and their curiosity was piqued. Only Simon felt that cold knot of dread tightening.

Then the streak, which had been flying parallel to the horizon, banked sharply, arcing directly toward him and his men.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

[SFX: INTENSE WHOOSHING]

Simon's eyes flew wide, raw panic surging up from his gut. He still couldn't make out what it was, but he knew—he knew—it was coming for him. The sheer momentum it carried was terrifying. If that thing hit him…

[SFX: SCREECHING HALT]

A mere six seconds after it first appeared, the object screeched to a dead halt directly above them and began to descend.

When the descending object resolved into the shape of a man, Simon’s blood ran cold. The breath hitched in his throat, his whole body locking up.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC IMPACT]

It was Zane.
