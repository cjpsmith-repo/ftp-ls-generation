Ep 235 - The Competition Begins
Word count: 1124
[AMBIENT: BUSY ARENA]
[MUSIC: CEREMONIAL EXCITEMENT]
Across the vast competition grounds, hundreds of disciples gathered in anticipation of the All Peaks Competition. The air hummed with tension as participants from the Thirty-Three Peaks sized up their opponents, their cultivation auras subtly radiating power.
Spectators filled the viewing platforms, eager to witness the clash of talents that would determine peak rankings for the coming year. Among them, whispers spread about the unusual participant from Misty Peak---a mere Level Two Adept daring to stand among the elite.
Usually, even the weakest students in the All Peaks Competition were pushing the Consummation level. Last time, the least powerful student from Misty Peak to even compete was a Level Seven Adept.
While five of the thirteen students from Sea Peak had hit the Peak level, the others were all at the eighth or ninth Level of the Adept Realm.
So, seeing a student at just Level Two of the Adept Realm in the competition was practically unheard of.
Unusual, yes, but not impossible for a once-in-a-generation talent.
For instance, students personally taught by the saints and elders of the Skyreach Sect were a different breed entirely. They mastered incredible cultivation methods that gave them power far beyond their rank. It allowed them to enter the All Peaks Competition at Levels Two and Three and still do surprisingly well.
Three years ago, a student named Jacob Wilson shocked everyone. Mentored by a grand master in the Sect, Jacob cracked the top one hundred despite only being a Level Two Adept at the time.
Anyone who wanted to break into the top one hundred had to be strong enough to challenge students at the Consummation level. It was insane to think about the kind of inner power a student must have to go toe-to-toe with peers so much more advanced.
Rumor had it that Jacob had improved his skills a considerable amount over the past three years, making him the hands-down favorite to win the championship this year.
Jacob was a personal disciple, meaning he had a special mentor. The support, facilities, and training methods he had access to were on a completely different level from anything at Misty Peak.
It just went to show how the All Peaks Competition was the ultimate test of strength for every prodigy in the Sect.
[MUSIC: CEREMONIAL FANFARE]
Once all the students from the Thirty-Three Peaks and the personal disciples had gathered, Sir Kenneth rose from his seat and announced the start of the competition.
[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]
Right on cue, an elder soared over the field. He clutched dozens of long lists, dropping one with the master of each Peak as he passed.
The list detailed the contestants' names, their opponents, and their assigned fighting rounds.
Skytop Peak had over forty students listed, while Misty Peak had only seven. In total, there were nearly seven hundred names on the list---more than six hundred from the Thirty-Three Peaks and over fifty personal disciples.
In the beginning, the group trials would mercilessly weed out most of those seven hundred hopefuls.
[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
After Master Warren of Misty Peak scanned the list, his brow furrowed, and he let out a deep sigh.
Eliot moved closer. "Master Warren, who are we up against?"
With a bitter smile, Master Warren handed the list to him. "Everyone, take a look," he said. "You should all know who you're facing."
Naturally, all seven contestants from Misty Peak crowded around, anxious to see their first opponents. One by one, their faces fell as they read the names.
[SFX: PAPER RUSTLING]
Misty Peak's first-round opponents were all from top-ten Peaks, including Blackrock Peak, Sunspire Peak, and Azure Peak.
Since all those opponents were high-ranking members of their own Peaks, they were bound to be much stronger. And it looked like they were, in fact, way more powerful than the hopefuls from Misty Peak.
"I guess being from Misty Peak means we're just stepping stones for the other students," Master Warren said, sighing heavily. But when his gaze landed on Zane and he saw how calm the boy was, he paused.
Zane wasn't flashy, but every time he showed up for a trial or a competition, he defied the odds and pulled out a win for Misty Peak. He just had this quiet confidence about him.
[MUSIC: CALM DETERMINATION]
Zane was calm now. At first, he'd been serious, feeling the weight of the competition. But as the day drew closer, he'd found a balanced sense of inner peace.
But just because Zane wasn't stressed didn't mean he was taking this lightly. He was actually looking forward to it, his mind completely focused on the tournament ahead.
Glancing at the paper, Zane saw that his first opponent was a student from Azure Peak named Langdon, who had reached Level Nine of the Adept Realm.
"Zane, I know Langdon," Eliot warned. "He's a natural powerhouse, with skills that put him on par with a Consummation-level fighter. He's ranked nine hundred and thirty-sixth in the Skyreach Sect Rankings. You need to be careful."
Zane nodded thoughtfully. "What about you, Eliot?" he asked. "Feeling confident about your match?"
Eliot just managed a wry smile. "Nope. My rival is Mack from Blackrock Peak." The name carried weight. Mack was an inner disciple known for his mastery of magic arrays and battle formations---a dangerous combination that could dramatically amplify his power once set up. Facing him meant you had to strike fast, before he could deploy his array.
[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT ARENA]
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
In one of the adjacent arenas, another first-round match was already underway. Benjamin, a big, imposing cultivator who specialized in poison-based techniques, squared off against Jill, a slight but deceptively quick swordswoman wielding an Emerald Sword.
Orbs of toxic gas billowed across the arena, expanding by the second. It meant Benjamin could defeat Jill without even landing a direct hit. With seventeen or more circles of poisonous Vitality spreading unevenly across the field, it didn't matter how skillfully Jill dodged. She was about to run out of room to escape the toxic clouds.
Finally forcing Jill into a small corner, Benjamin smirked. "Just give up, little girl," he sneered. "If you get infected by my Creeping Blight Venom, I'll have to cure you later. It's a huge hassle, and trust me, the process isn't pleasant."
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
As Benjamin was running his mouth with his creepy taunts, a soft sunglow suddenly enveloped Jill. She seemed to shrink for a split second before vaulting out of her corner and rocketing straight at him.
Watching Jill's fight, Zane nodded. "That big guy is going down," he commented.
Eliot shot Zane a surprised look but said nothing. He was sure Jill was going to lose. She couldn't escape the poisonous Vitality, which left her no way to attack Benjamin and gave him what looked like an unbeatable advantage.
But what happened next proved Zane right.
