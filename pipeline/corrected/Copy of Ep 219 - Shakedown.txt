Ep 219 - Shakedown
Word count: 1169
[AMBIENT: PEACEFUL COURTYARD]
The Masters' Courtyard fell silent as Zane followed the two seventh-grade cultivators. He'd let them lead him away from the forge and the growing crowd, but he could tell that their motives were far from selfless.
Nevertheless, the Masters' Courtyard was a quiet haven, a peaceful escape from the usual crowds and noise.
[SFX: PULSING ENERGY]
The first thing Jerry noticed upon entering was the sheathed sword Zane was clutching. Normally, a sheathed sword wouldn't give off any kind of aura. But the light from this one was so brilliant it practically pulsed through the scabbard.
It was a simple rule among weaponsmiths: you use the best materials for the blade and handle, but you never waste Celestial Law on the hilt. It was just inefficient.
Yet Jerry and Mark could both see the hilt of Zane's sword thrumming with an insane amount of energy, even through the leather.
They were stunned. Had this kid really wasted his understanding of Celestial Law on the handle? And how could he even comprehend Celestial Law in the first place? The craftsmanship was staggering. The sheer skill it must have taken to forge such a fine weapon sent a thrill of excitement through them both.
"I can't believe you forged a Mythic Weapon at your age. That's insane," Jerry said, his eyes glued to the sword. "So, have you given your new baby a name?"
A smith always named their creation upon its completion. The sword hadn't been named before because Chase had left it unfinished, a legacy for someone else to complete. Now, that honor fell to Zane.
"Streamer," Zane replied softly, his fingers caressing the hilt with unmistakable pride.
"Streamer? That's a beautiful name! I like it!" Jerry nodded enthusiastically, his eyes gleaming with genuine appreciation. "Yeah, that's really a fitting choice! May I examine it more closely?"
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Zane stepped back. "No."
Mark tried a different angle. "Instructor Jerry knows his craft. He could tell you if there are any flaws in the blade. We're all Skyreach Sect, no need to be cautious."
"I appreciate it," Zane said. "Now — you mentioned a record. If that's all this is, I'm happy to cooperate. If there's nothing else, I'll be on my way."
Jerry's smile didn't waver. "The record can wait. What we really need to know is how you forged that weapon."
"I already told you," Zane said. "I can't say."
The air between them thickened as Zane's expression hardened into a mask of cold determination. The courtyard fell silent, save for the distant whisper of wind through the training grounds.
Just as he'd thought. It was an ambush.
"Since you've decided to threaten me," Zane said, his voice dropping to a dangerous whisper that carried more weight than any shout could, "I believe you've earned the right to witness Streamer's power firsthand."
[SFX: MAGIC ENERGY PULSE]
As he spoke, Zane slowly drew the blade from its scabbard. He held it out, the tip of Streamer pointed right at the two masters. A look of absolute calm was on his face, as if he wasn't the least bit concerned about facing off against them.
Zane's brazen move shocked Mark and Jerry. They couldn't believe it. What was this kid talking about? They were seventh-grade Adepts, and he was just a first-grade. How dare he look down on them?
[SFX: ENERGY FLAME]
"Ha! Misty Peak might be the bottom of the barrel in the Skyreach Sect, but they sure know how to raise an arrogant disciple!" Jerry sneered. He held out his hand, and a purple flame flickered to life in his palm. "You're barely a first-grade Adept, and you've got the nerve to look down on us? That's rich."
Every weaponsmith had their own unique flame.
A pink flame, as delicate as a peach blossom, danced on Mark's palm. It looked weak, but it held an incredibly hot and destructive power.
A tiny thread of dark purple flame flickered on Jerry's palm. While it wasn't as powerful as Mark's, it had a unique quality: it couldn't be extinguished. It could burn on almost any surface, even water, making it seem indestructible. Of course, it wasn't a true immortal flame; a smith of his level couldn't possibly wield one.
Mark clearly wasn't letting this go. "You'd be smart to tell us the secret to forging that weapon and hand over the sword," he threatened. "Otherwise, my flame will turn you to ash."
[MUSIC: DARK POWER RISING]
Dark tendrils of Corrupt Vitality began to seep from deep within Zane's skin, coiling around his hands. The energy grew, spreading across his skin and engulfing Streamer. As the Corrupt Vitality, charged by the Mythic Weapon's power, coursed through him, his own strength surged to an incredible level.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
"Have you forgotten you're masters in the Skyreach Sect?" Zane scoffed. "Shame on you for acting like common thugs. Did you really think I'd come to Quenching Peak and forge a Mythic Weapon if I was afraid of you? Did that never cross your minds? Look at yourselves, so consumed by greed. Fine. If this is how you want to play it, you'll pay the price."
"Mark, just grab him!" Jerry yelled, trying to hide the unease Zane's terrifying aura sparked in him. After all, there was no way this kid could be stronger than a seventh-grade Adept. "I'm sure he'll tell us everything we want to know once we have him!"
[SFX: MAGICAL FIRE BLAST]
Mark didn't hesitate. With a wave of his hand, a blast of pink flame shot from his palm. Zane stood his ground, unflinching.
[SFX: ENERGY ABSORPTION]
As the flames raced toward him, Zane's Corrupt Vitality surged out to meet them. The pink fire shifted — first purple, then deep purple-black — as his energy consumed and transformed it completely. By the time it reached him, it was his.
He raised his free hand, letting the two dark orbs of converted flame hover lazily in his palm. He looked at Mark with a faint smile. "Were you planning to burn me with this?"
[SFX: FLAMES CRACKLING]
Mark's face went pale.
[MUSIC: BATTLE THEME]
He could have ended the fight right there with his power alone, but he'd just gotten Streamer and was dying to test it out. So, instead of letting his power also consume Jerry's purple flame, he rushed forward, raising Streamer to strike.
His first move was a basic swordsmanship chop.
It was the most common of the seventeen basic sword forms---a simple downward cut. But the moment he finished the move, his blade was already rising for another.
[SFX: SWORD SLICING THROUGH MAGIC]
The two strikes looked ordinary, but at Zane's speed, they became a blur. He sliced through the threads of fire, shattering them into tiny, flickering fragments. Of course, he couldn't truly "kill" the flames. They weren't physical; they would simply reform.
But Zane held a Mythic Weapon. Even if he couldn't unlock its full potential, each swing snuffed out more of the flames.
[SFX: MAGICAL FLAMES EXTINGUISHING]
Under his relentless assault, Jerry's threads of fire were mowed down by Streamer like stalks of bamboo, until they vanished completely.
He and Mark looked at each other. Had they just made the worst mistake of their lives?
