Ep 513 - The Golden Fist

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Heavy footsteps echoed across the dusty stone floor as Zane made his way toward Marilyn.
His last punch had been strong enough to shatter nearly every bone in her body. An ordinary woman would have died instantly, but Marilyn was a cultivator at the Inner-Core Realm. Her chest still rose and fell in shallow, ragged breaths. With the right elixirs and enough time, she'd eventually heal.
Zane had no intention of allowing that.
Each step he took felt deliberate, measured. His face remained expressionless, but inside, a cold fury burned—the same fury that had sustained him through two years of humiliation, through watching his father's murderer walk free, through every insult and dismissal he'd endured. Marilyn had chosen to stand in his path. She had underestimated him, mocked him, tried to crush him.
Now she would pay the price.
[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD MURMURS]
"Zane won! He actually did it! Thank God I bet on him!"
"But look… he's going in for the kill. He's not actually going to kill her, is he?"
"No way. Maddox is right there!"
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Whispers rippled through the crowd like wind through tall grass. Spectators leaned forward in their seats, torn between horror and fascination. If Zane went through with this, the entire situation was about to explode into something far more dangerous than a simple arena match.
The air itself seemed to thicken with tension, each second stretching into eternity as Zane closed the distance to Marilyn's broken form.
[SFX: MAGICAL PULSING]
[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL BUILD]
Nearby, Tia's fingers constricted around the crystal in her palm, her Vitality flooding into it until the artifact pulsed with brilliant light. Inside, the magical eye of the Angel of Death spun wildly, its iris contracting and dilating like a living thing. Her heart hammered against her ribs in an erratic rhythm. She knew, with absolute certainty, what Zane was about to do—and the knowledge filled her with equal parts dread and anticipation.
[SFX: AETHER ENERGY CRACKLING]
Maddox's calm voice boomed from the high platform, cutting through the tension like a blade. "Leave the arena now, Zane, and I'll let you live." He strode to the platform's edge, each footfall deliberate and measured. His fists clenched at his sides, already glowing with a yellow Vitality that cast harsh shadows across his face. "Take one more step, and you're a dead man."
[SFX: STONE CRACKING]
The platform, built by the World Merchants' Guild from solid basalt, suddenly cracked under his feet. A spiderweb of fractures shot out from where he stood, spreading in all directions. He had shattered solid basalt with a single, light step, the casual display of power more terrifying than any shouted threat.
[AMBIENT: DEAD SILENCE]
[SFX: SPLINTERING ROCK]
The crowd fell into dead silence. Only the sharp crackle of splintering rock echoed across the arena.
Leaders of the major sects stood frozen, their faces masks of disbelief. Even Brent—normally oblivious to subtlety and unable to stop talking—sensed the crushing weight of tension and wisely held his tongue.
Zane paused, lifting his gaze to meet Maddox's eyes. A flicker of regret crossed his features. He understood what Marilyn meant to the man—perhaps better than anyone else in this arena could comprehend. The bond between master and disciple ran deeper than blood. But Elara's face haunted him. For any chance of saving her, Marilyn had to die.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[SFX: SWORD SWOOSH]
He exploded into motion, closing the distance in a blur. His longsword sang through the air, the blade aimed with surgical precision at Marilyn's exposed neck. No hesitation. No mercy.
Maddox's roar shook the arena, raw anguish and fury bleeding through every syllable. "You're asking to die!"
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE PUNCH]
[SFX: AIR WARPING]
He unleashed a torrent of fury, throwing a punch down at the arena below. Maddox hadn't fought in years, but he was still the number one warrior in the Heartlands, and the power behind that punch was terrifying. The air seemed to warp around his fist, a bright, golden projectile that rocketed toward Zane at an impossible speed.
Maddox was certain—he would kill Zane long before his sword ever touched Marilyn.
[AMBIENT: CROWD GASPING]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
The crowd gasped, holding their breath as the golden fist descended. The number one warrior in The Heartlands had made his move.
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER ACTIVATION]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
But just as Maddox’s attack was about to hit, the runes carved into the arena floor blazed to life. A complex web of light erupted from the stone, forming a shimmering, translucent barrier over Zane just in time to intercept the golden fist.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
The impact was silent but immense. The light flared violently, but it held. Maddox's attack, an attack that could level a building, had been stopped cold.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
In that moment, in the eyes of the sect leaders, Zane became a priceless treasure.
Their minds reeled. A defense of that level… from the arena's own runes? There were only a few hundred runes laid beneath the stones, a minor array at best. And Zane had only had time to redraw a little over a hundred of them.
A protective magic array was the ultimate defense, something every sect craved. They all remembered the story of the Black Mountain Order, a Fourth-Grade sect that had been harassed for years by a single, weaker cultivator from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. He was too fast to catch, striking whenever their leader was away, and the great sect was eventually forced to sue for peace. An array like this would have changed everything.
And now, here was Zane, stopping the most powerful warrior in the Heartlands with just over a hundred redrawn runes.
What could he do with thousands? Could he build an array strong enough to hold off a warrior from the Life-and-Death Realm? Or even kill one?
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]
The thought was staggering. You could feel the shift in the air as the leaders looked on, their shock turning to something else. Even Lady Darkmere, who despised Zane with a passion, was now staring at him with a raw, predatory hunger.
He was no longer just a reckless boy; he was a prize.
