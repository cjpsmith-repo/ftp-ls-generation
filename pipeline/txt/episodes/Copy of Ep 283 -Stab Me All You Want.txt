Tracy stood poised in the center of the arena, radiating an ethereal glow. Behind her, the phantom image of a Blue Phoenix hovered—faint and almost translucent, yet giving off an overwhelming power that seemed to bend the air around her. She was transformed, no longer just a talented disciple but something transcendent—a goddess incarnate whose radiance made spectators squint and shield their eyes. Her lips curved into a confident smile, knowing the effect her revelation was having on everyone present.
The Blue Phoenix was a branch of the Phoenix bloodline — it hadn't inherited the Phoenix's divine fire, but it had inherited something else entirely: mastery over wind. With her bloodline awakened, Tracy didn't channel Vitality to move air. She simply was its master.
[SFX: CROWD WHISPERS]
The crowd fell into stunned silence, their collective breath caught as the arena’s atmosphere shifted dramatically. Whispers gradually rose as realization dawned on them. Some leaned forward in their seats, eyes widening in disbelief, while others exchanged nervous glances.
"Impossible," someone muttered, voice carrying in the hushed arena.
It defied all expectations. A disciple from the Skyreach Sect wielding the legendary power of one of the Mythic Beasts—something many had only heard of in ancient tales.
Everyone present reached the same conclusion: the match was basically over. There was no conceivable way for Zane, talented though he might be, to beat Tracy now that she’d unleashed the Blue Phoenix’s might. Spectators nodded knowingly to one another, certain he would make the prudent choice and concede the match before suffering unnecessary humiliation. Even Misty Peak’s supporters looked resigned, their earlier enthusiasm dampened by the overwhelming display of power before them.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
[MUSIC: HEIGHTENING TENSION]
But to their absolute shock, Zane pulled out his sword.
"A sword? Since when does Zane use a sword? I thought he was a hand-to-hand fighter. What is happening?"
"Yeah, seriously. I never pegged him as a swordsman. And look at that thing—is that a Mythic weapon?"
"Holy crap. You think he’s been holding back this whole time? His flying blade, that soul attack... none of that was his real ace? I gotta see what he does with this!"
The crowd was still trying to process Tracy's Blue Phoenix, and now Zane had thrown another wrench in the works.
Zane, only at the Second Level of the Adept Realm, had already floored everyone by beating opponents at the peak of the Adept Realm, the Soul Realm, and even some of the Sect’s own Chosen. And he still had more tricks up his sleeve. In an instant, Zane had become a complete enigma.
His mysterious reputation came from a seemingly endless arsenal of fighting techniques. After seeing him use so many different skills, the audience had no idea what his real limits were—or if he even had any.
When they saw Tracy's Blue Phoenix, they were sure Zane was done for. But now that he'd drawn his sword, the fight was once again anyone's guess.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMURS]
They're both on another level, was the thought that echoed through the minds of the other disciples. Most knew they could never hope to reach the power of the two fighters in the arena.
In the stands, Finn watched the match unfold, his gaze sharp and fixed on the sword in Zane’s hand. His eyes burned with the desire for a fight.
He and Zane had that in common.
They were both dark horses in the All Peaks Competition, both punching way above their weight class—though Zane's feat of defeating a Soul practitioner was far more impressive. Finn, by contrast, hadn't drawn his sword against a single opponent yet — which made him just as much of an unknown.
But that didn’t necessarily mean Zane was stronger. Finn was a swordsman too, but he hadn’t drawn his blade against a single opponent yet, making him just as much of a mystery.
Finn had been fascinated with Zane ever since seeing his Sword Step. He was itching for a chance to fight him, but it was a shame they hadn’t been matched up this round.
[SFX: SWORD ENERGY ACTIVATION]
"Sword intent: shield!"
As Tracy suddenly lunged, Zane threw up a protective barrier of pure sword intent.
[SFX: METALLIC PINGS]
A series of sharp, metallic pings sang through the air as Tracy’s weapons met the barrier.
[SFX: ENERGY BARRIER BREAKING]
Zane's sword intent was strong, but it wasn’t enough to push her back. A blue light flared in Tracy's eyes as she surged with power. Her Stiletto Hairpins tore clean through the shield and shot straight for him.
[SFX: WEAPONS PIERCING FLESH]
Realizing his defense wouldn’t hold, Zane tapped into the power of the Phoenix Crystal in his arm, trying to dodge. But it was no use. The hairpins sank into his shoulders, and blood immediately began to well.
"Damn, she’s fast," Zane muttered, hissing as he swiped at the blood. His gaze on Tracy turned icy.
"I was going to give you just two wounds," Tracy announced, her voice chillingly empty of her usual warmth. "But now? Now I’ll keep going until I’m satisfied." Her personality may have shifted with the Blue Phoenix's power, but her stubborn streak was clearly still there.
"Oh, really?" Zane said, a sarcastic smirk pulling at his lips.
"That smirk just earned you another. I’ll add a new stab for every time you dare to look at me like that," she warned in that same cold tone. "We’re up to eleven now."
"Hey, you can’t just jack up the price like that," Zane shot back, his complaint laced with an infuriating casualness. He wasn’t threatened. He was prodding her, trying to see just how far this new power went.
"That makes twelve," Tracy spat, gritting her teeth.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
"Stab me all you want," Zane challenged, his voice shedding its playful veneer to reveal a steely edge beneath. "That is, if you think you’re strong enough."
He lifted Streamer with deliberate precision, settling into an attack stance. The earlier probing dance between them was over; his eyes now reflected unwavering resolve. This time, he was serious.
[AMBIENT: SUDDEN SILENCE]
The instant Zane adopted that position, the entire arena fell into a hush so profound that even Sir Kenneth, perched at his observation point, remained motionless, his gaze fixed on the unexpected development below.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
Then, like a wave gathering momentum, whispers rippled through the stunned audience, gradually swelling into astonished murmurs.
"That’s… that’s basic swordsmanship," someone uttered in disbelief.
To the collective amazement of everyone present, Zane had assumed the first, most fundamental stance taught to novice swordsmen—a position so elementary that advanced practitioners typically abandoned it years into their training.
Yet something about his execution transformed the simple posture into something profoundly unsettling.
Where was he going with this?
