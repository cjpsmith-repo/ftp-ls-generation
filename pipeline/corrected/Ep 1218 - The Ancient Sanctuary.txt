Ep 1218 - The Ancient Sanctuary

Word count: 1140

[MUSIC: TENSE ELECTRONIC PULSE]
[AMBIENT: DIGITAL INTERFACE HUM]
The moment the Illusion Points left Zane’s account, his ranking tanked.
The pyramid churned with trillions of warriors battling for points. Rankings shifted every second. Nobody watched the middle or bottom - all eyes tracked the top contenders.
When Nigel, the former number one, plummeted to fortieth-thousandth place, the entire system trembled. He’d been killed, losing half his points instantly. Two facts stunned everyone: someone had killed the top-ranked warrior, and even after losing half his points, Nigel still ranked in the top forty thousand.
[SFX: CASCADING DATA CRASH]
[SFX: DRAMATIC SYSTEM ALERT]
The question on everyone’s mind was simple: Who could have possibly done it? Who had the skill to take down a Godly Genius from the Thoughtless Minds?
No one had a clue.
Zane’s ranking wasn’t nearly that high, but he was starting to make a name for himself. A lot of warriors were keeping a close eye on him, seeing his potential to rocket up the ranks. 
So, naturally, when his name didn’t just drop but vanished from the boards entirely, the people watching were more than a little confused.
Yolande, for one, was stunned.
Her first thought was that Zane had been killed. If he’d lost half his points, she figured she could probably guess where his new rank would land.
But when she checked, he wasn’t there. 
[SFX: DIGITAL SCROLL WHOOSH]
Yolande kept scrolling down, past the twelve millionth spot, and still nothing. Zane’s name was just… gone.
*How is that possible?* she wondered. Even if he died once, there was no way he’d drop out of the top ten million. Unless… did he die twice in a row?
That seemed impossible. It took a long time to bring someone back, so dying twice in quick succession was almost unheard of.
“He couldn’t have… could he?” she muttered, a flicker of disbelief crossing her face.
She wasn’t worried, just shocked. This was Zane, after all. During the Passing the Torch event, he’d started at the bottom and clawed his way to the top. The guy was a master of bouncing back. The only thing that bothered her was that his rank had completely disappeared.
Meanwhile, the man himself was hoping his trip would be worth the three hundred thousand Illusion Points it had just cost him. 
[AMBIENT: SETTLING SILENCE]
He didn’t need anyone else to tell him what had happened. He already had slightly more than ten thousand Illusion Points left to his name. When he checked his ranking, the number confirmed it — he’d fallen below forty millionth in this world, and below two billionth across the entire Illusion Battlefield.
“Forget it,” he muttered, shaking his head. The three hundred thousand points were already gone. All he could do now was hope the cave gave him something worth the price.
[SFX: LIGHT CONDENSING HUM]
As the swirling barrier finished absorbing his points, the light coalesced into something new — a small, six-winged creature made entirely of yellow light, hovering quietly at his side. The moment it appeared, the resistance that had been blocking his path vanished completely.
[SFX: SOFT CHIME]
Zane studied it for a moment, then nodded to himself and stepped forward into the cave.
[AMBIENT: NARROW STONE PASSAGE]
The passage ahead was barely ten feet wide, sloping steadily downward into the dark. Every ten feet or so, a white, whirling pool of light blocked the way forward.
The first time Zane approached one, his stomach dropped — if each one cost him another three hundred thousand points, he’d never make it through. But before the thought could fully form, the six-winged creature drifted ahead of him and absorbed the light whole. The resistance disappeared just as quickly as it had the first time.
“I’m going to need you to get through this place,” Zane murmured, glancing at the creature now trailing faithfully beside him.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE — DISTANT ECHO]
One hundred feet. Then another hundred. The creature cleared each rotating light in turn, and Zane pressed on, deeper and deeper into the dark, until at last the passage opened into a vast underground chamber.
[AMBIENT: CAVERNOUS ECHO]
An ancient temple stood at the bottom of the cave — weathered, half-crumbling, and yet radiating a quiet, unmistakable power.
Something about the place set him on edge the moment he laid eyes on the statue in its entryway. Its carved eyes seemed to track him no matter where he stood, and though he knew full well it was just stone, he couldn’t shake the feeling of being watched.
Still, turning back wasn’t really an option — not after what he’d already paid to get this far. Worst case, dying here just meant waking up back in the main city. He’d already spent three hundred thousand points on this detour. He wasn’t about to walk away without something to show for it.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT MONASTERY HALL]
With a final, decisive thought, Zane walked past the statue of a jolly-looking friar and stepped into the inner sanctum.
He moved so carelessly that he didn’t notice the statue’s expression shift the moment he passed. 
[SFX: STONE GRINDING CREAK]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DARK STRINGS]
The Grinning Deceiver’s smile had vanished, its carved eyes and mouth flipping upside down into a mask of pure, nightmarish malice.
[SFX: HEAVY DOORS SLAM]
[SFX: ECHOING BOOM]
The two heavy doors slammed shut behind him, the sound echoing through the chamber. Zane jumped, spinning around. He’d just stepped foot in the inner sanctum, and now he was trapped.
The doors were made of wood that looked ancient and rotten, and for a second, he thought he could probably just shatter them. But he knew better. Looks could be deceiving. If this place was strong enough to trap someone like him, he doubted a pair of rickety doors would be the weak point.
Realizing he had nowhere to go, Zane turned and quickly took in his surroundings. Four other stone statues stood in the quiet space, their monastic robes carved in fine detail. Each one held a long staff pointed toward the center of the room—right where he was standing.
“Stone sentinels?” Zane muttered, sizing them up.
[SFX: STONE CRACKING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE PERCUSSION]
Just as the words left his lips, a sharp cracking sound split the silence.
He whipped his head around and saw the source immediately. The four stone sentinels were cracking, but not from damage. The fissures were spreading as their limbs began to move.
In a matter of seconds, the statues, which had been lifeless stone moments ago, were fully mobile. They leaped from their lotus-shaped pedestals, charging straight for him. You’d think being made of rock would slow them down, but they didn’t lumber. 
They moved with a terrifying, human-like speed, closing the distance in a blur.
