Ep 1220 - A True Dragon

Word count: 985

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL TENSION]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL SANCTUM ECHOES]
The Gilded Warden seemed to be lost in thought for a moment, and for good reason.
In truth, Zane hadn’t used the Gods-Intimidating Strike just now. He’d mastered the sword skill years ago, a process that had stripped away many of his most intense emotions. But he could never completely sever that part of himself, which meant his execution of the legendary strike was far from perfect. Still, even after all this time, a hint of its ruthlessness lingered in every attack, and that was what the Warden had noticed—the ghost of a power Zane had long since sealed away.
Zane ignored the Warden’s scrutiny and focused on the chaos around him, forcefully tearing strands of raw energy from the Chaos Sea itself. The energy twisted in his grasp, exploding into a storm of lightning and razor-sharp wind that ripped through the sanctum. For a split second, the spectral shadow of a Nether God even flickered among the elements.
[SFX: LIGHTNING STORM]
[SFX: RAZOR WIND SLASHING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
He gave the four stone sentinels fifteen seconds to deal with his assault before his eyes glinted and he charged.
[SFX: HEAVY STONE IMPACTS]
The sentinels were powerful enough to swat away the lightning and wind with heavy palm strikes, but the relentless barrage was wearing them down. Their movements, while impressive, were growing clumsy, leaving openings you could see with the naked eye.
And Zane’s eyes were sharp enough to spot every single one.
[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT WHOOSH]
He shot forward like a blur, closing the distance in an instant. Before the nearest Tiger-Taming Sentinel could even register his approach, Zane’s palm was flat against its chest.
“Break,” he said, the single word hanging in the air.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT]
A loud bang echoed through the chamber. A perfect handprint appeared on the sentinel’s solid chest, and from it, cracks spiderwebbed out, covering the statue’s entire body in seconds. He’d attacked after his opponents, but his one decisive strike had ended the fight for one of them instantly.
[SFX: STONE CRACKING AND SHATTERING]
The shockwave from his attack shattered the stone, and the wind blades scattered the rubble into dust.
Zane didn’t pause.
[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]
He spun, the longsword at his side flashing upward in a dazzling arc of light as he lunged for the next Tiger-Taming Sentinel.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT]
The blazing light of his sword completely engulfed the statue. The sentinel, which had been winding up a powerful palm strike of its own, froze mid-motion.
[SFX: STONE CRACKING AND CRUMBLING]
The moment Zane turned to dash toward the next target, a network of fine cracks spread across its body, and it crumbled into smooth, polished pieces.
[SFX: RUBBLE SCATTERING]
The last two Tiger-Taming Sentinels never got the chance to regroup. Without their full formation, they were nothing — Zane closed the distance and finished them both without breaking stride, the same way he’d finished the first two. Seventeen courtyards down, and he hadn’t taken a single real hit yet.
It had been a long, grinding path to get here, but Zane felt a flicker of real anticipation now. Whatever this place was hiding, he had to be close. One courtyard left. The eighteenth, and the last.
[SFX: DISTANT DRAGON ROAR]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS SWELL]
A roar rumbled through the sanctum before he’d even stepped through the final doorway. When he did, he found four more statues waiting in the corners of the chamber — each one with a True Dragon carved coiling around its shoulders and chest.
*Dragon-Taming Sentinels,* he guessed, sizing them up. Seventeen courtyards of steady progress didn’t mean the eighteenth would be any easier — if anything, it was probably the hardest test in the place. 
Zane wasn’t about to wait around and find out the hard way. This time, he moved first.
[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT WHOOSH]
He closed in on the nearest statue and drove a finger straight toward its head, meaning to end the fight before it even started.
[SFX: STONE HORN CRACKING]
But the carved dragon on its shoulder reared up first, snapping its horn into the path of his strike. The horn shattered on impact — but it held, just long enough to stop him.
[SFX: STONE AWAKENING RUMBLE]
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
That was all the opening the statue needed. It came fully alive, a dragon’s roar tearing out of it as its two palms clapped together in a single, thunderous strike — and the dragon carving along its shoulder lit up scale by scale with a cold jade-green glow.
The statue’s stone fists aimed for Zane’s chest. His assault had been fast, but not fast enough.
He had no time to defend himself before the attack slammed into him.
[SFX: BODY CRASHING INTO STONE WALL]
His feet dug a jagged trench into the stone floor before he smashed hard into the far wall of the inner sanctum.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
[SFX: VIOLENT IMPACT SHAKING FOUNDATIONS]
The collision was violent enough to shake the very foundations of the world. Back in the Lower World, a hit like that would’ve not only collapsed a mountain but triggered a domino effect that leveled the entire range behind it. The walls of this inner sanctum, however, looked ancient and crumbling, as if a strong gust of wind could topple them. And yet, when Zane slammed into them with enough force to shatter a mountain, they didn’t even budge.
Then again, this was the Illusion Battlefield. The city walls here looked ordinary, too, but not even the most powerful warriors could dent them. There had to be a reason something built by the Heavens was so indestructible.
But Zane wasn’t in the mood to ponder architecture.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DREAD]
[AMBIENT: EERIE STONE CHAMBER]
His focus was locked on the source of that terrifying power: the True Dragon carved onto the sentinel’s shoulder.
Part of Zane’s own strength came from a dragon, but he’d never imagined a stone sentinel could channel the same kind of power.
[SFX: SCALES GLOWING WITH ENERGY HUM]
As the statue had thrown its punch, the scales on the True Dragon sculpture had begun to light up, one by one, with a chilling, jade-green glow.
It was just a stone carving. Terrifying, yes, but something he could still fight.
But if that had been a real True Dragon, and all its scales had lit up… Zane knew he would have been blasted into ash before he even hit the ground.
