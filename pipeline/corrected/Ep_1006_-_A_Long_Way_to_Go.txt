Ep 1006 - A Long Way to Go

Word count: 1112

[AMBIENT: PRISON CHAMBER]

[SFX: METAL CHAINS CLINKING]

Zane glanced down at the shackles clasped around his wrists and couldn’t help but shake his head.

So much for the Upper World’s impressive resources. These things were just a mid-grade Relic-Class weapon. Trying to bind him with them was a joke. Even with the faint divine patterns etched into the metal, Zane was sure he could shatter them with his own power.

Still, just as the armored warriors said, there was no reason to make things difficult for them—or for himself. He was facing down several Soul Sea Realm warriors; with his current power level, a clean getaway was out of the question.

He’d known he had to play it smart when he got to the Upper World. The reckless, impatient kid he’d once been was long gone.

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

With Zane shackled, one of the armored warriors led him toward a nearby spire.

[SFX: MUFFLED SQUAWK FROM BAG]

Just as the warrior was about to leave, Zane felt a stir in his bestiary bag, followed by an indignant squawk.

The sound made the armored warrior shoot Zane a curious look. “You actually brought a spirit beast with you to the Upper World? A bonded beast?”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane’s heart skipped a beat. This was bad.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The Demi-Immortal Race was hunting the Ancestral Dragon, which meant it was priceless. If anyone found out he was hiding it, he’d be in an impossible position. He forced a calm smile. “Yeah, it’s a bonded beast I captured back in the Lower World.”

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS FADING]

The armored warrior just nodded and turned to leave. To warriors from the Upper World, anything from the Lower World was basically worthless. Luckily for Zane, this particular guard couldn’t be bothered to check.

[AMBIENT: QUIET CHAMBER]

“That was too close,” Zane muttered to himself once the warrior was gone. “Hey, keep it down in there,” he whispered, patting the bestiary bag. “If you get caught, I can’t save you.”

After just a few days of growth, the Ancestral Dragon already had the intelligence of an eight or nine-year-old kid and understood him perfectly.

Before Zane ascended, Cora had cooked a mountain of food and packed it in the bestiary bag. The Ancestral Dragon had probably scarfed it all down by now.

As a creature with a body like a divine-class weapon, the Ancestral Dragon didn’t actually need to eat; it could grow just by absorbing the vitality of Heaven and Earth. But Cora had spoiled it with delicious food, and now it was a picky eater. It was probably just throwing a fit about being cooped up in the bag after stuffing its face.

But Zane’s threat worked. The Ancestral Dragon saw him as its “mother,” after all, and it quieted down immediately.

[MUSIC: QUIET TENSION]

Then came the wait.

For the first seven days, not a single other ascender left their Ascending Well. Zane sat alone in the spire, watching the wells from a distance. On the tenth day, the first one finally emerged — Freya, the plainly dressed woman. She walked in with shackles on her wrists and found a seat beside him without ceremony.

She looked at him for a moment. Then: “How did you do it?”

Zane smiled faintly. “Do what?”

“You know what.” She studied him. “There’s only one explanation for adapting to Vitality Pressure that fast — you’ve been to the Upper World before. You came back down, and now you’ve returned through the ascension passage.” She paused. “Am I right?”

Zane’s expression shifted — just slightly. Then he shook his head. “I’m not from the Upper World.”

Freya pursed her lips. She didn’t believe him. But she let it go.

After a moment she said, more lightly, “Wherever you’re from, you’ll go far here. My uncle is the city lord of one of the more important cities in the Soulbound Sect. He ascended from the Lower World himself. When our service is done, I’m going to him. You’re welcome to come.”

Zane looked at her evenly. He remembered how she’d pretended not to know whose sacred order this was, back in the well — playing ignorant while the others panicked. She’d known all along.

“I’ll think about it,” he said — which was his way of saying no.

His plan hadn’t changed. Serve out whatever obligation the Soulbound Sect required. Then leave. He had no interest in belonging to anyone’s organization up here.

[AMBIENT: BUSY SPIRE]

[SFX: CHAINS RATTLING]

Soon, more and more ascended warriors were brought to the spire, all of them fitted with the same standard shackles.

[AMBIENT: TENSE PRISON ATMOSPHERE]

These were all powerful Soul Sea Realm warriors, each with a healthy dose of arrogance. Why would they ever agree to be shackled and controlled? Naturally, they clashed with the armored guards, who showed them zero sympathy. Instead, the new arrivals got a harsh lecture: no matter how powerful they were in the Lower World, ascending meant starting from scratch.

One of the armored warriors yelled at the fuming Soul Sea Realm warriors. “Back in the Lower World, you might have held the power of life and death over thousands with a single word! You might have been kings! But up here, you are nobody!”

[SFX: HEAVY CHAINS CLANKING]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

The insult got under the skin of two of them, and they started throwing punches. The outcome was predictable. The two Soul Sea Realm warriors paid the price for their pride. They ended up in a special set of handcuffs, twice the size of the regular ones. These were true divine-class weapons, each attached to two massive black balls that looked heavy enough to be permanent.

No one knew what the black balls were made of, but even those powerful warriors could barely drag them along the floor. Flying was definitely out of the question with those things chained to them.

[AMBIENT: ECHOING SPIRE HALL]

Three days later, all the Soul Sea Realm warriors had been moved from the Ascending Wells and were waiting with Zane in the spire.

After the armored warriors did a headcount, they registered everyone’s name before leading them to a teleportation array.

[SFX: TELEPORTATION ARRAY HUMMING]

The Upper World was so massive that even powerful Soul Sea Realm warriors had to use teleportation arrays to get around. Otherwise, traveling the hundred million miles between two major cities would take a warrior the better part of a year.

[MUSIC: VAST WORLD THEME]

But even that understated it. The Soulbound Sect alone was so vast that at his current flying speed, crossing it from end to end would take three years. And the Soulbound Sect was not even the dominant power in this region — it was among the weaker of the tenth-tier sects ruling here. Below them stretched an endless hierarchy of smaller powers, too numerous to count.

Zane had come from a world where the four Divine Kingdoms together barely qualified as an eighth-tier power.

He had a long way to go.
