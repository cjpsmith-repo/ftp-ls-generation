Ep 1056 - Three Breaths

Word count: 1079

[AMBIENT: TENSE ROOM ATMOSPHERE]

This shouting match between the Demon Night queens had been raging for somewhere around fifteen hours.

Lavender finally paused, catching the stunned look on Zane’s face.

“Is something wrong?” she asked.

He smiled. “No, not at all! Curse away.”

[MUSIC: SOFT EMOTIONAL PIANO]

A blush crept up Lavender’s neck. She hadn’t realized just how much she’d been swearing in front of him. For some reason, the thought of him thinking less of her for it stung, and she cut her rant short with a shake of her head.

But Zane totally got it. She needed to vent. At least with the Roaring Token, she could chew out the Queen of Punishment and get under her skin whenever, wherever.

She handed the token back, watching as he tucked it away. “Where did you even get this thing?” she asked.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

Lavender had used Roaring Tokens tens of thousands of times, but she’d never dreamed one like this—one with unlimited uses—could even exist.

“I told you about the Elysian Keep, right?” Zane said.

Lavender nodded. “Yeah.”

She’d heard him mention it, but it never really registered. To her, some place called the Elysian Keep in the Lower World hadn’t seemed like a big deal.

“That’s where I got it,” he told her. “I might get the chance to go back someday. You’ll get it then.”

So the Elysian Keep held treasures like this. She’d completely underestimated it. The place was far more extraordinary than she’d ever imagined.

[SFX: DOOR BANG]

Just then, the door to Zane’s manor was thrown open with a deafening bang.

[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD]

“Kid! I’ve been waiting around the clock for you! Why are you still holed up in there?” the intruder roared as he stormed in.

Zane just shook his head and shot Lavender a quick wink. She nodded, understanding, and slipped back inside him.

With a sigh, Zane braced himself and went to face the crowd gathering outside his home.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

A mob of warriors was already gathered at his entrance. While Zane was used to this kind of attention, he couldn’t figure out why they were here. He’d just arrived at the Mist Palace and hadn’t had time to make any enemies yet. What was all the commotion about?

Word had spread fast through the Mist Palace — Zane had walked out of the Hall of Arms with the previous Palace Master’s sword. Warriors had been scheming ever since. Some wanted to buy it. Some figured a newcomer at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm could be intimidated into handing it over. A few were thinking about simply taking it.

The man who’d stormed in was broad-shouldered and heavy-bearded, his expression set to permanent menace. “I’m Jared Bishop,” he announced. “Ranked fourth in Groundstone Hall. You know what that means.”

“Not really,” Zane said.

[SFX: SWORD DRAW]

He drew Thundergust — not quickly, not aggressively. He ran one finger slowly along the blade, then suddenly sped up. The sword sang.

“Yes,” Zane said mildly. “That’s the sword.”

Bishop’s eyes locked onto it. “I’m lacking a good sword. Hand it over. Save us both some trouble.”

“I took this sword myself,” Zane said. “If you want one, the Hall of Arms has plenty. Three hundred Sovereign Stones and it’s yours.” He tilted his head. “I’ll give you three breaths to leave.”

Bishop laughed. “Three breaths? I haven’t heard that kind of arrogance in a long time.”

The crowd had quietly let Bishop take the lead. He was overbearing enough that no one dared move in front of him — and besides, they’d all figured the same thing: this newcomer was going to get beaten badly, and he’d brought it on himself.

What they hadn’t counted on was Zane being even more arrogant than Bishop.

[SFX: SUDDEN BLUR OF MOVEMENT]

Zane’s figure flashed and vanished. The next instant, Bishop left the ground like a rubber ball struck dead center, his chest caving inward as he flew backward and crashed into the small palace behind the crowd.

Zane stood where Bishop had been, sword in one hand, the other fist still clenched. His eyes were flat. “Three breaths go by fast,” he said.

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED WHISPERS]

A stunned silence fell over the crowd before erupting into hushed, frantic whispers.

“He’s so fast!”

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMURS]

[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]

“He’s insane! Bishop went flying from one punch!”

“Bishop is strong, but man, he’s arrogant.”

They all knew you had to be strong to even hold the Thundergust. Still, Zane was only at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm. There was no way he should be able to touch a master from the Soul Sea Realm. And yet…

That single, lightning-fast punch made every single one of them question what they thought they knew.

The more clear-headed ones had quietly reasoned it out from the start: anyone who could walk out of the Hall of Arms with the previous Palace Master’s sword wasn’t ordinary. The Thundergust was a Grade-One Divine Weapon — and not just any Grade-One. It was the finest kind, the sort that could potentially earn a place among the top Divine Weapons in the entire universe. A weapon like that didn’t let just anyone pick it up.

Most of the crowd had ignored that logic. Zane was fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm, they’d figured. How dangerous could he be?

Now they knew.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

As he started back toward his door, Zane shook his head in regret. “Should have tested the sword’s power,” he muttered.

Those close enough to hear him swallowed nervously. If a single punch could do that to Jared Bishop, what kind of damage could he do with the sword? The unspoken question hung in the air like a death sentence. They knew, in that moment, that this kid wouldn’t hesitate to kill anyone who crossed him.

[SFX: HURRIED FOOTSTEPS]

Zane reached his door and was about to shut it when a voice called out from the crowd. “Wait!” A short warrior scrambled through the throng of onlookers.

“What is it?” Zane asked.

“The sword! I’ll buy it!” the short warrior blurted out. “I’ll make sure it’s a good price! A fair price!”

But Zane just shook his head. “It’s not for sale.”

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

“Three hundred thousand Sovereign Stones!” the warrior yelled, the number tumbling out in a single, desperate breath.

The crowd couldn’t believe their ears.
