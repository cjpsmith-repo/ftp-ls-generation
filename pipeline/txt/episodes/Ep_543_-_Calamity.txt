Ep 543 - Calamity

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The Holy Maiden's turn arrived.
She blinked once, slowly, and the die tumbled through the air.
[SFX: DICE ROLL]
The chasm fell silent. Every spectator held their breath as the die bounced across the invisible surface, its faces blurring until it settled with six dots facing the heavens. The Holy Maiden of the Divine Kingdom allowed herself a knowing smile—controlled, practiced, the expression of someone accustomed to victory. She stepped forward with deliberate grace.
"Six again," she announced, her voice carrying effortlessly across the void.
[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]
Six discs materialized before the Holy Maiden, the last glowing with faint yellow light.
"Roll again!" she commanded.
She turned toward Tia, her eyes narrowing with contempt—disdain for this Heartlands upstart, certainty in her own superiority.
Tia met the scornful gaze without flinching, her expression unreadable.
[SFX: DIE ROLLING]
Soon, the die at the center of the chasm spun again, and a hush fell over the crowd. It slowed, wobbled, and finally stopped on five.
[MUSIC: GROWING DREAD]
The Holy Maiden’s face darkened. She refused to believe her luck was worse than that of some woman from the Heartlands. She just wouldn't accept it.
One by one, transparent discs appeared before her.
[SFX: OMINOUS HUM]
When the fifth disc materialized, it began to glow with a murky brown light.
She didn't like the color. It felt wrong, like it was hiding a punishment.
If she had a choice, she wouldn't step on it. But she didn't. Reluctantly, she planted her foot on the disc.
[SFX: DARK MAGICAL RESONANCE]
“Calamity,” a disembodied voice announced to the chamber. “You may unleash a calamity upon another player, reducing their luck for a short time.”
The Holy Maiden’s eyes narrowed as she stared at Tia, a cruel, determined smile spreading across her face. “Oh? So I can ruin someone’s luck?”
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
Everyone in the Divine Kingdom understood this was pure chance—strength meant nothing here. Only luck mattered, and success promised extraordinary rewards.
Vesper's motives were irrelevant. After enduring the Five Aging Processes—Filthy Clothes, Falling Hair, Sweaty Pits, Stinking Body, and Restless Mind—no recovery was possible. The Law of Life was absolute. Even immortals couldn't reverse it.
Zane wasn't alone in recognizing this celestial tomb as a trap. Everyone knew. Yet they entered anyway, lured by potential rewards despite the dangers.
The Holy Maiden and her companions understood this, perhaps better than anyone.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
She smiled coldly and pointed a finger at Tia. “Let’s see what she can do now. Time to ruin her luck!”
[SFX: ANGRY SHOUT]
Zane’s blood went hot. “Don’t you dare!” he yelled.
The Holy Maiden just giggled. “Oh, but I will. You really think you can stop me?”
[SFX: LIGHTNING CRACKLES]
Above Tia, a small black cloud materialized, crackling silently with dark lightning.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
Tia pressed her lips together, shooting Zane a helpless glance—cursed for advancing too quickly.
"An immortal designed this game," Zane murmured. "There must be a countermeasure."
"Seventh brother—can someone's destiny be thrown off this easily?" he asked inwardly.
A quiet laugh echoed. "True destiny can't be altered. Tia's fortune runs deep—no curse can touch that. But this Wheel isn't reality. It's a game with bendable rules."
"How?"
"The curse poisons her luck. Her next six steps lead to punishment. Though her destiny is strong enough to gravitate toward the lightest available."
Zane blinked. "Her destiny is that powerful?"
"Slightly weaker than yours."
He'd only moved three steps. If that was strong destiny...
The Cobalt Dragon's seventh brother smiled but said nothing more.
[MUSIC: SHIFTING TONE — NEW ROUND BEGINS]
The next round swept through the chamber. Warriors with strong destinies stumbled into punishments. Those who had suffered finally broke through into rewards. Most fell somewhere in the middle.
Maddox and the other Heartlands powerhouses swung between brilliance and punishment, their sheer strength absorbing what others couldn't.
[SFX: DICE ROLL]
Zane threw his die. It tumbled end over end—and landed on five.
A brown disc shimmered into existence before him. His heart jumped.
He recognized that color.
He stepped onto it.
[SFX: DARK MAGICAL RESONANCE]
"Calamity," the disembodied voice announced. "You may unleash a calamity upon another player, reducing their luck for a short time."
Zane smiled faintly. "Lucky me."
Across the chamber, Maddox's eyes narrowed as Zane's gaze swept toward him.
"Your destiny is tough," Zane said coldly. "Even cursed, I think you'd find a way through."
Maddox sneered. "Glad you understand that."
Zane's eyes moved on—and settled on the Holy Maiden.
Sensing his intentions, her chest rose and fell rapidly. "If you dare curse me," she said, her voice sharp and clear, "I will flatten the Heartlands. Every last corner of it."
Zane rolled his eyes. "If you could flatten the Heartlands, you would have done it already. Who are you trying to fool with a threat like that?"
The handsome man beside her stepped forward, his tone shifting to something smoother, more persuasive. "Friend, don't curse my little sister. I'm offering you something far greater in return—an opportunity that dwarfs anything you'll find in this tomb. I can guarantee it."
“Do you really think I’m that stupid?” Zane shot back, his voice dripping with sarcasm as he gestured to the man beside her. “Or maybe you’re the stupid one for thinking I’d fall for any of this.”
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
He laughed, a sharp, humorless sound. “You tell me there’s something more valuable than what’s in this tomb, so why are you still here?”
The man from the Divine Kingdom’s face went grim. “Friend,” he said, his voice low and threatening, “you don’t seem to grasp who you’re talking to. What I’m offering is a dream come true. You’d better make the right choice, or you’ll live to regret it.”
The other warriors from the Divine Kingdom, all at the Life-and-Death Realm, fixed Zane with icy stares.
“So she’s the Holy Maiden, and you must be a prince or something,” Zane scoffed, waving a dismissive hand from one to the other. “Means nothing to me. Go on, use the Calamity on her!”
[SFX: CRACK]
A small black cloud materialized above the Holy Maiden's head, crackling with dark lightning that cast sinister shadows across her face.
She was getting a taste of her own medicine—karma for the trap she'd orchestrated against Tia.
The Holy Maiden stared up at the ominous cloud, her expression transforming from shock to rage. "Elders! Kill him!" she shrieked, her voice shrill with fury as she thrust a trembling finger toward Zane.
[SFX: WEAPONS DRAWN]
[MUSIC: BATTLE TENSION]
The five Life-and-Death Realm elders materialized behind her, weapons drawn, their killing intent saturating the air.
Roland stepped forward reluctantly. "Miss, we can't break Vesper's barrier. Now isn't the time."
"I don't care!" Her scream shattered the chamber's silence.
She had spent her entire life surrounded by reverence. No one had ever shown her such disrespect, and the humiliation seared through her chest like acid.
First, this nobody had dared use a Calamity against her. Now her own elder questioned her command before witnesses. Her hands clenched until her nails drew blood.
The man from the Divine Kingdom placed a hand on her shoulder. "Sister, don't be reckless. If I get a Free Challenge, I'll handle him."
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]
But it was too late. Her bloodlust for this young brat would not go away.
