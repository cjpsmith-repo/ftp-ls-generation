Ep 1093 - Sticking Together

Word count: 1094

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

Zane had already cleared the eleventh round. He stood on his platform, watching Layla’s battle and shaking his head.

[SFX: INTENSE SWORD CRY]

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

She was using some kind of intense Sword Cry Technique, and the arena echoed with the blood-curdling screams of her opponents as they were torn apart by the sound. It was brutal. Even the spectators flinched at the sheer violence of it. Finally, a robed official stepped forward to call a halt to the match.

[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]

A fine mist of blood exploded across the platform as the warriors were ejected from the Illusionary Arena.

The fight was over, but the image of their agony was seared into their minds, a paralyzing memory that made them feel like they were still trapped in that living nightmare. Their faces were deathly pale, and more than a few simply collapsed, their bodies giving out as they fainted on the spot.

[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD MURMUR]

On the other side of the plaza, the warriors from Willow Island watched with growing rage. The violence had been completely unnecessary. Everyone knew Layla was a top talent from the Thirteen Palaces, but there was no reason to torture her opponents like that. They were just training partners. Why was she using them to show off?

“That girl is a psycho!” one of them snarled.

“I’ll take her down in the next round,” another vowed. “Teach her a lesson.”

“We can only send out the second group for the next round, though,” someone pointed out. “With her strength, I’m not sure they can stop her.”

“Even if they can’t, they have to put her in her place!”

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD CHATTER]

The plaza was buzzing with angry muttering. The thousands of people gathered there were divided into dozens of groups, ranked by strength. The second group of warriors, who would be facing the twelfth round, were a bit stronger than the first, but it was a slim margin.

[AMBIENT: CROWDED PLAZA]

Just then, a young man in a white tunic stepped out from the crowd. “Everyone, quiet down!” he called out, his voice cutting through the noise.

[SFX: CROWD SILENCE]

The plaza fell silent.

The young man wasn’t particularly powerful—his cultivation was only at the seventh level of the Life-and-Death Realm, placing him in the first group. Since they had sent their best warriors against Zane and Layla, the trials the two faced were much harder, and weaker fighters like him never got a chance to enter the arena.

His name was Adrian Rowe. What he lacked in strength, he made up for in cunning. He was quick-witted and resourceful, and during the earlier group stages, he’d offered strategies and advice that had led to several key victories over the Thirteen Palaces.

“Adrian, what’s on your mind?” a warrior asked.

Adrian’s eyes glinted with certainty. “It’s going to be impossible to beat those two head-on in the twelfth round,” he said. “But I have an idea. If you all work with me, we might just have a shot.”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

He let that sink in before continuing. “These two aren’t going to stop at twelve. Probably not at thirteen or fourteen, either. Whatever they’re cultivating, whatever’s backing them — it’s not anything you or I can match. Right now, they can fight opponents several levels above them and barely notice. Trying to simply outlast them is a waste of time.”

“So what’s the point, then?” someone called out.

“The point,” Adrian said, “is to make them show their hands. Force out their trump cards now, while it costs us nothing — so that when our actual strongest reach round nineteen or twenty, they’ll already know what they’re up against.”

He turned to the warriors with the sharpest eyes in the crowd. “That woman — all she’s shown us is the Sword Cry Technique. From what I can tell, it’s built on vibration, sound waves at a specific frequency. I think I know how to break it.”

Then he nodded toward another cluster. “As for him — all we’ve seen is how sharp his sword is, and whatever those shadow-things are. We don’t know his real limits. So don’t fight him. Just don’t let him stand still. Anyone confident in their footwork, you’re with me.”

That settled it. Whatever doubts lingered, nobody had a better idea.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Releasing this many Nether God Shadows at once should have drained him fast — but ever since his soul reached the Nightjade Fighting Soul, the cost barely registered. He’d planned to lean on them hard this round.

When Zane released his Nether God Shadow, his new opponents immediately scattered, using different kinds of Law Power to evade his attacks. Some used the Spatial Law to create illusions, others used the Earth Law to tunnel underground, and a few used the Wind Law to take to the air.

It was strange. They were all using different methods, but none of them were fighting him directly.

Was this some new obstacle the Old Man had designed? Or had someone on the outside come up with a plan to counter him?

Zane considered it for a moment before a faint smile touched his lips. It didn’t matter what their goal was. He just had to stick to his own plan.

[SFX: VINE WHIPPING]

Still, their evasion techniques were impressive; he couldn’t just stand here and wait. But as he started to move, something grabbed him from below, pulling him back. He glanced down to see a thick brown vine wrapped tightly around his ankle, anchoring him to the ground.

It’ll take more than that to hold me, Zane thought.

Ignoring the vine, he took a heavy step forward, expecting to tear it apart with sheer force. To his surprise, the vine didn’t snap. It stretched, incredibly tough and resilient, holding him fast.

[SFX: SWORD SLASH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

[SFX: MULTIPLE FOOTSTEPS CHARGING]

A quick slash of his sword severed the vine, but two more shot up from the ground in its place, grabbing both his legs and holding him tight. In that same instant, the warriors who had been dodging his attacks all changed direction, charging him from every side.

The coordinated attack left the warriors of the Thirteen Palaces stunned. They never expected this level of teamwork.

Even the Lord of the Sunset World looked surprised. One of the palace masters murmured, “Someone on Willow Island must have given them that idea. I hate to admit it, but that’s a clever plan.”
