[AMBIENT: MEETING ROOM]
Ethan nodded at his friends; it was clear they weren’t going to let him get away without offering up some information about himself. “Yeah, I’m from House Moon, in the northern part of the city.”
House Moon wasn’t one of the big names, but they did have a legend in their family tree: Jack Moon. Ages ago, he’d joined the Imperial Army and led them to one victory after another. His achievements were so incredible that House Moon was granted the title of a Great House.
But Jack was the last big name to come out of the house. Ever since, they'd been stuck at the bottom of the Great Houses' pecking order.
“So if you’re from a Great House, why’d you have to take the Skyreach Sect’s entrance exam?” Zane asked.
If Ethan really was from a Great House, he shouldn’t have had to take the exam at all. Zane distinctly remembered seeing him sign up right beside him.
Ethan offered a wry smile. “My house didn’t give me a writ of nobility, so I had to sign up for the exam like everybody else.”
Dustin jumped in. “Sounds like you’re on the outs with your family, then. Not happy with how things are going at home?”
Ethan nodded. “Yeah, that pretty much sums it up. We haven’t had a big name in generations, but everyone’s still scrambling to improve our house’s standing. I heard my cousins are bringing friends they made in the Sect to the next family gathering. And me? I’ve got nothing to show for myself.”
But Ethan was holding back.
Boosting a house’s standing wasn’t just about having powerful members; it was about having powerful connections. Zane and Dustin exchanged a look, then turned back to Ethan with knowing smiles, waiting for him to get to the point.
[MUSIC: SERIOUS DETERMINATION]
After a moment of hesitation, Ethan’s expression shifted, his voice taking on an earnest quality. “Zane, Dustin, I’d be honored if you’d accompany me to my house’s gathering next month. Your presence would mean a great deal.”
Zane and Dustin exchanged glances before nodding with warm smiles, understanding the weight behind the simple invitation.
“Hey, what about me? Did my invitation vanish into thin air?” Rhys interjected, feigning offense as he delivered a hearty clap to Ethan’s shoulder that nearly sent him toppling forward.
“You too, of course!” Ethan exclaimed, recovering his balance with a genuine laugh. “How could I possibly overlook the infamous Rhys?”
[MUSIC: FUN FRIENDSHIP]
The evening unfolded into something special as the four companions shared stories beneath the amber glow of tavern lanterns. Their laughter echoed through the establishment while tankards clinked and empty plates accumulated.
Dustin regaled them with tales from his homeland, while Rhys’s outrageous anecdotes left them gasping for breath between fits of laughter. Zane noticed how Ethan’s shoulders gradually relaxed, the burden of family expectations momentarily lifted in the company of friends. Hours melted away until they finally surrendered to the late hour and parted ways.
[AMBIENT: QUIET APARTMENT]
[MUSIC: THOUGHTFUL MELODY]
When Zane got back to his place, he started to take stock of the haul from his latest adventures.
His trip to Alba Imperia had been profitable. Besides the two crystals now embedded in his arms, he’d collected a ton of other valuables.
The real prizes were the Spirit Weapons he'd taken from the Soul Realm masters he defeated in The Elysian Keep. When the masters fell, their weapons didn’t vanish with them. They just dropped to the floor, and Zane had scooped up every last one.
He’d even scored four Storage Rings, one of which had way more space than his own.
The quarters the Skyreach Sect provided were decent-sized, but they were no match for the mountain of new loot. He'd have to figure out what to do with it all in the morning if he wanted any room to move.
[AMBIENT: MEDITATION SPACE]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CULTIVATION]
Over the next few days, when he wasn’t cultivating his strength, Zane spent most of his time studying the Tapestry of Sun, Moon, and Stars.
For martial arts cultivators, the tapestry was a potential game-changer.
Back in The Elysian Keep, he'd managed to absorb some of the Stars’ power the very first time he studied it. Now, that same power was a spark within his Corrupt Vitality.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
When he channeled the Star power, Zane was blown away by the energy it unleashed.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS POWER]
"I’ve only absorbed a fraction of that Star power,’“Zane thought, his pulse quickening. ”What would happen if I could harness all of it---the Stars, the Sun, and the Moon? Would my Corrupt Vitality become stronger than anything I could imagine?"
But studying the tapestry now, that incredible sensation from the first time was gone. He also found it much harder to “see through” it.
[AMBIENT: QUIET CONTEMPLATION]
Beyond studying the tapestry, Zane also had to figure out a practical strategy for his cultivation.
The Skyreach Sect was a mecca for cultivators, sure---it had tons of Handbooks---but its real draw was the top-notch Attunement Sanctums.
As a new junior disciple, Zane didn’t know much about the sanctums.
He’d been so focused on earning points that he'd even gotten into it with Lady Novak over how to get them faster. At least his clash with Isla had clarified how to earn the points he needed for training.
He could grind out easy tasks for a couple of points here and there, but it would take an eternity to save up enough for the sanctums that way.
A hard task would earn him more points, but those could take anywhere from ten days to half a month, and the reward still wouldn’t cover even an hour in one of the sanctums.
You could also just buy points with a boatload of crystals, but that was a privilege reserved for members of the Great Houses. Even if a regular disciple somehow scraped together enough crystals, they weren’t allowed to make the exchange anyway.
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING ORGANIZATION]
The Skyreach Sect was run like a massive corporation. Every month, it shelled out a fortune on salaries for its staff and officers, not to mention the monthly allowances and elixirs for hundreds of thousands of disciples.
To cover those massive expenses, the Sect had to charge steep fees for its facilities.
On top of that, just maintaining the training sites themselves devoured a staggering amount of crystal energy.
It wouldn’t be easy. But Zane loved a challenge.
