Ep 161 - Pretty Poison
Word Count: 1040

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]
Saul's words hung in the air, generating excitement and confusion.  "The temporary owner of the Elysian Keep?" Zane repeated, intrigued by the title.
Saul chuckled. "Yep. It's a rule from the Former Host of the Elysian Keep."
"So, what does being the 'temporary owner' get me? Can I use whatever's in here?" Zane asked.
Saul shook his head. "Nope. All it means is you can take the final test and earn a Contemplation Picture."
"A Contemplation Picture? What's that?" Zane was completely lost.
Saul gave him a curious look. "What kind of world did you say you were from? You're a warrior, and you've never heard of a Contemplation Picture?"
Zane wracked his brain. He'd read countless books, but the term meant nothing to him. He just shook his head. "Honestly, I have no idea."
[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]
 "Okay, even if you're just a half-step into the Adept Stage... don't warriors in your world use Vitality?" Saul's brow furrowed. "Can't any of them create a Vitality Avatar? I saw a bunch of people fighting in the central spire who had them. You need a Contemplation Picture to create a proper Vitality Avatar," Saul explained.
The explanation only confused Zane more. "In the Eastern Territory, warriors create Vitality Avatars through meditation, based on their cultivation methods," he replied. "No one uses a Contemplation Picture."
"I see," Saul said with a nod. "That explains a few things. From what I've observed, even the powerful warriors from the Eastern Territory—the ones at the Soul Realm—are a lot weaker than others I've seen at the same level. It must be because they don't use Contemplation Pictures. It makes their Vitality Avatars... flimsy."
"Hold on, can you break that down for me?" Zane asked, leaning in slightly. "So, if I want a powerful Vitality Avatar, I need one of these pictures. Is that right?" 
The way Saul stressed the name told Zane everything. He didn't fully get the 'why' yet, but he had a gut feeling this was a game-changer.
[MUSIC: REVELATION THEME]
 A smile spread across Saul's face. "Sure, the fighters in the Eastern Territory can create a Vitality Avatar without one. But the kind they make through meditation alone lacks a solid foundation. They can't be passed down, and their power is a pale shadow of what it could be. But if you refine Raw Vitality into Vitality and forge an Avatar using a Contemplation Picture... it could be ten, maybe even a *hundred* times stronger."
It finally clicked for Zane.
Saul and the Former Host of the Elysian Keep... they weren't from his world. Their methods of cultivation were completely different from anything in the Eastern Territory. To create a true Vitality Avatar, you needed this thing called a Contemplation Picture.
"Okay, then. How do I get one?" Saul's explanation had Zane hooked.
Saul laughed. "Weren't you listening? I just told you. You've passed the first two tests. Now you take the third. Your reward is based on how well you do."
[SFX: MAGICAL LIGHT FLASHES]
 With that, Saul pointed to a nearby wall. A few points of light flashed, and five images materialized out of thin air.
Zane stared at the five pictures, but they were blurry, the details impossible to make out.
"These are your potential rewards," Saul said. "Five Contemplation Pictures, each with a different power. The first one, the Heavenly Sword Picture, is the weakest. The last one…"
Saul continued his description, but the moment Zane heard the words "Virulent Toxin," a faint smile touched his lips.
Almost any other attack would have given him pause. But Zane had the body of a Spiritual Weapon. He wasn't afraid of a little poison.
What could Hydra venom possibly do to him?
[SFX: MONSTROUS GROWLS]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Twelve infant hydras towered before him, each one rising a hundred feet into the air. Their heads displayed a bizarre kaleidoscope of colors—sickly yellows that reminded Zane of festering wounds, earthy browns resembling rotting vegetation, and crimson reds that mirrored freshly spilled blood. 
Several heads boasted shockingly vibrant hues that seemed almost unnatural in their intensity, pulsing with an otherworldly glow that sent a chill down Zane's spine despite his confidence.
The ancient wisdom echoed in his mind as he assessed the creatures: the brighter the head, the deadlier the venom. Nature's warning system, displayed in all its terrifying glory before him. Zane's fingers tightened around his weapon as he calculated his approach, his heart beating steadily despite facing creatures that would send most cultivators fleeing in terror.
[SFX: SHARP CRACK]
 A sharp crack split the air.
 The Hydra at the front lunged first.
[SFX: VENOMOUS SPRAY]
[SFX: SIZZLING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
 All nine of its heads sprayed a volley of venomous saliva straight at Zane. He sidestepped the attack. A sizzling sound echoed through the illusion as the venom hit the grass and flowers, which withered instantly, turning the ground beneath them pitch black.
Zane was taken aback by the venom's potency. A single drop would kill a normal person on the spot. Even Adepts would have to be incredibly careful fighting a Hydra; one wrong move, and it was over.
But Zane was feeling reckless. After dodging the venom, he hovered an inch off the ground, then shot behind the Hydra. With a flick of his wrist, he sent the shattered dagger in his hand screaming toward the creature’s body like a bolt of lightning.
[SFX: KNIFE WHOOSHING]
 The knife whooshed through the air.
[SFX: NECKS WHIPPING]
 The hydra's necks were impossibly flexible. The moment Zane got behind it, all nine heads whipped around and unleashed another spray of venom right at him.
Zane weaved around the Hydra, but he couldn't escape the relentless spray. Realizing he was cornered, he gathered his strength, bent his body into an arc, and—instead of dodging away—charged straight up from underneath it.
"What, is he trying to get himself killed?" Saul exclaimed, completely baffled. He couldn't fathom why Zane would do something so insane.
[SFX: SICKENING SIZZLE]
 A sickening sizzle filled the air.
Venomous saliva splattered all over Zane, eating away at his skin. But his expression remained calm as he rushed toward the hydra's heads in one fluid, unstoppable motion.
