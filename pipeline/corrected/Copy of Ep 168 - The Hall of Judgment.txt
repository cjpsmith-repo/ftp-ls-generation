Ep 168 - The Hall of Judgment
Word count: 1002

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ADVENTURE]
 The Lightning Horse was no ordinary mount. It was a Spirit Beast, a special kind of creature tamed only by the most powerful and experienced individuals.
[SFX: GALLOPING ACCELERATION]
 At first, its pace was just average, but as the Lightning Horse built momentum, it accelerated into a blur of motion. With Zane on its back, it streaked across the landscape like a bolt of lightning.
Over the next several days, Zane pushed on, stopping only for brief rests to let the Lightning Horse catch its breath before they were back on the road.
Even at that blistering pace, it took him several days to travel from the Province of Galeria to the capital of the Empire.
[AMBIENT: STABLE SOUNDS]
[MUSIC: ARRIVAL THEME]
 When he finally reached the Skyreach Sect, Zane stabled his Lightning Horse with the other Spirit Beasts and headed straight for Misty Peak.
He’d been gone for The Demon Purge for a while now, and the other acolytes who’d taken part in the trial had returned much earlier. Zane figured he had to be the last one back, so he needed to report in.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN ATMOSPHERE]
 Just as Zane reached the base of Misty Peak and was about to start his climb, he spotted two figures on the mountain path. They wore the distinctive armor of the Skyreach Sect, marked with violet insignias—Inspectors.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
 When the two inspectors saw Zane, they strode over to him. One of them pointed at Zane’s waist and commanded, "Show us your Acolyte's Sigil before you go any further!"
The Inspectors of the Skyreach Sect held immense authority. While the peak masters handled discipline on their own turf, the inspectors were responsible for maintaining order everywhere else.
Hearing their demand, Zane didn't argue. He simply unclipped his Acolyte's Sigil and handed it over without a word.
One of the inspectors snatched it. After a quick look, he shot his partner a meaningful glance. Zane had heard stories about the Hall of Judgment torturing acolytes who broke the rules, and he couldn't help but wonder what they planned to do to him in a place like that.
[AMBIENT: FORMAL HALL]
[MUSIC: SERIOUS JUDGMENT]
 Inside the hall, a middle-aged man sat behind a large desk. He wore a purple and gold robe embroidered with a flying fish pattern, his square face set in a grim, stern expression. “This must be the Chief Judge,” Zane thought.
[SFX: GAVEL SLAM]
 The two inspectors shoved Zane forward, then stepped back in perfect unison. The movement drew the man's attention. He slammed a gavel down on his desk with a loud crack.
"Who is this?" the man demanded. "Kneel!"
"Kneel?" Zane repeated. "I kneel for the heavens, the earth, and my parents. For no one else."
"How dare you!" the middle-aged man roared, his face darkening. "You should know the rules here. People who don't know their place tend to die… painfully."
"The rules?" Zane shot back, his tone laced with defiance. "Which rule says I have to kneel in this hall? Was it written by the leader of the Skyreach Sect? Or was it written by you?"
Zane had already done his research. In his efforts to understand Infernal Peak, he'd looked into both Mount Judgment and its Hall of Judgment. He knew exactly where he stood.
The records were clear: Mount Judgment hadn't always held so much power. As the Sect grew, its interrogation methods were expanded. To better question rule-breaking acolytes, the Hall of Judgment had copied practices from the imperial judgment halls of the Empire.
However, none of those practices were official laws of the Skyreach Sect. As an acolyte, Zane followed the Sect's official rules. But these unofficial house rules? They meant nothing to him.
The middle-aged man's name was Murry Cook, and he was the vice-director of the Hall of Judgment. In his time, he had presided over the trials of more than eight hundred acolytes.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
 Murry was on the verge of exploding at Zane's open defiance, ready to teach the boy a lesson, but then he remembered his instructions and reined in his anger. "All right, have it your way. Stand," he said. "Do you know why you've been brought here?"
"No idea," Zane answered, shaking his head.
He honestly couldn't think of any rule he could have possibly broken. He’d been traveling outside the Sect's territory ever since The Demon Purge ended. The inspectors had intercepted him before he'd even set foot on Misty Peak. Racking his brain, Zane couldn't come up with a single transgression.
A sly smile crept across Murry's face. "Heh. Are you sure about that?"
"I have no reason to lie," Zane said flatly. He'd broken no rules. He had nothing to fear.
Murry felt a flicker of guilt. According to the laws of the Sect, the Hall of Judgment had no authority to execute anyone. Only the high council could sentence an acolyte to death. The worst Cook could do on his own was send them to Infernal Peak.
[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]
 But he'd been given a task, and he had to see it through, no matter what. Their goal was to end Zane’s life.
If he was going to kill an acolyte, he had to be clever to avoid repercussions. *I can't kill him outright,* Murry thought, *but I can have him beaten to death.*
If Zane fought back, it would be the perfect excuse to kill him on the spot. If he didn't, the Sky-Shatter Cudgels would do the job anyway.
Zane steeled himself, ready for a fight. He decided then and there: anyone who tried to take his life would pay for it. No exceptions.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
 But as four men entered carrying four heavy cudgels, an unexpected wave of joy washed over him—the exact opposite of what he should have been feeling.
The last thing he was afraid of was getting hit.
Now that he'd reached the Adept Realm, his real worry was that no one would be strong enough to truly test him.
So, Zane decided not to resist. He was curious to see what would happen when the blows landed. Would it make him even stronger?
