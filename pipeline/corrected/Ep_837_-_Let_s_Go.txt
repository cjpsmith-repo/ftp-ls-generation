Ep 837 - Let's Go

Word count: 1039

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ORIENTAL THEME]

Surviving a Celestial Tribulation was brutal and near-impossible, but survivors emerged stronger than ever.

Yet there was a catch: the world eventually exiled those too powerful—a process called “ascending to the upper world.”

The tragedy? This world's ascension path was broken. Even when the world tried expelling a warrior for excessive power, it simply couldn't.

[SFX: ETHEREAL DRAGON VOICE]

The Cobalt Dragon’s voice was a calm rumble in Zane’s mind. “Yes. With the resources available in the Lower World, it should be impossible for a warrior stronger than a Soul Sea Realm master to even exist here.”

“But this guy… his strength is way beyond the Soul Sea Realm,” Zane said, a hint of hesitation in his voice. “So how is he here?”

“The answer is simple, Zane,” the dragon explained. “He’s not from this world. He must have come down from the upper world.”

“Oh,” Zane breathed out, his face lighting up as the pieces clicked into place. “That explains everything!”

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

His heart hammered against his ribs as he immediately dove into the Memory of the World's Will, searching for any information on the White-Haired Priest’s origins.

A moment later, his face twisted in frustration. “What?” he exclaimed. “There’s nothing on this guy!” The Memory of the World’s Will was a complete blank; there wasn't a single trace of the powerful priest.

“That’s perfectly normal,” the Cobalt Dragon soothed. “The World’s Will you possess lets you know everything about this world. But this priest came here through the Reverse Spirit Channel. He wasn’t born here. I’m sure he paid a heavy price for that journey.”

That settled the question of the priest's nature, but something still wasn't sitting right with Zane. “But I found the Soulcradle Wood just fine. Why can’t I find any information on him?”

“The Soulcradle Wood is an inanimate object,” the dragon replied patiently. “If you don’t believe me, try searching for the Dark Nether Cave, or Vesper’s celestial tomb, or the Elysian Keep. You’ll find it’s impossible to get information on living things that don’t belong to this world.”

Still skeptical, Zane did as the dragon suggested. He sifted through the World’s Will and found the dragon was right. He couldn’t find a single mention of the Dark Nether Cave, the Tower of Sin, or the Elysian Keep. He did, however, find the celestial tomb.

Even without the dragon’s help, Zane figured out why. Vesper might have been a warrior from the upper world, but he was born here. That’s why a record of him existed in the world’s memory.

He tried one more search — the young man with the feathered crown, the one who had taken Elara.

Nothing.

He stared at the blank for a moment, then let it go.

[AMBIENT: SHUFFLING CROWD]

[MUSIC: CEREMONIAL STRINGS]

Under the priest's direction, Life-and-Death warriors moved through the crowd, handing out small vials to the saint-level geniuses. The curse had been partially dispersed, but what remained was still strong enough to trap and kill a Spirit-Tribulation warrior who entered unprotected. Every warrior entering the Sacred Feather Sea needed a drop of True Dragon's blood to resist it.

When a vial reached Zane, he took it without expression. Inside, he was smiling. He already carried more True Dragon's blood than anyone here — including blood essence, which couldn't regenerate and was worth far more than anything in that vial. But he swallowed it alongside everyone else without comment.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

Looking up from his thoughts, Zane saw the way his teammates were watching him. They had been so sure of his limits before, but now… now they knew they were wrong. They had no idea how strong he really was. Not only had he crushed the top talents from the other divine kingdoms, but he’d also wounded War King York—a master who was considered an elite even among his peers. If they hadn’t seen it with their own eyes, they never would have believed it.

[AMBIENT: QUIET REVERENCE]

The way Camilla and the others looked at him had completely changed. In Camilla’s eyes, especially, there was nothing but pure, undisguised awe. They knew how lucky they were to have him on their team. With Zane around, this trip was going to be a massive success.

[SFX: APPROACHING FOOTSTEPS]

As the crowd began forming into teams, a voice reached him.

“Zane Jones — would you consider joining my team for the exploration?”

He turned. It was the crown prince of the Wind Divine Kingdom, standing with a small retinue, his expression carefully neutral. To be invited by a divine kingdom's crown prince was no small thing. Zane understood the logic. After what he'd just done, every team in the square would want him.

“I appreciate it,” Zane said. “But I've already accepted Lord Randy's invitation.”

Randy was already walking over. He clapped Zane on the shoulder and grinned at the Wind crown prince. “That's right. You're a step too late.”

The Wind crown prince offered a faint, genuine smile and walked away. He'd known it was a long shot.

Zane gave them a firm nod. “Alright. When do we leave?”

“We can head out now,” Randy said with a grin. “According to the rules, the winners get to enter the Sacred Feather Sea first. The Crown Prince of the Harlen Divine Kingdom is already leading his people up.”

He pointed, and sure enough, several figures were already soaring high into the sky, following the West Dragon River upstream toward the sea.

[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS ORCHESTRAL]

“Then let’s go,” Zane said, a surge of excitement running through him. 

This was it. The entire reason he’d joined the Crystal Martial House and fought for the kingdom was to get into the Sacred Feather Sea.

Without another word, Zane, Randy, Camilla, and the others shot into the sky, chasing the group ahead of them.

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY PULSE]

[AMBIENT: CRASHING WAVES]

As they approached, waves crashed against the shore of the sea, and a faint golden light pulsed with what Zane recognized as the Power of Blood Sacrifice. The moment he flew over the water, he felt an overwhelming force wrap around him, thick and heavy.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Zane’s eyes flashed. So this is the Power of Blood Sacrifice.

[SFX: HEAVY PRESSURE WHOOSH]

Under its influence, his speed dropped dramatically. 

He glanced over and saw that Randy and Camilla were struggling even more, an invisible hand seemingly pulling them down toward the churning water below.
