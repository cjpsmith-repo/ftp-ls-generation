Ep 1054 - The Last Supporter

Word count: 1063

[AMBIENT: DARK THRONE ROOM]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The Queen of Punishment stared at Lavender’s statue, and it felt like the damn thing was staring right back. A cold dread washed over her. If she didn’t deal with Lavender—and soon—her throne was as good as gone.

The statue itself had revealed that a sliver of Lavender’s soul was still out there. As long as the stone hadn’t gone completely black, it meant Lavender was clinging to life somewhere in the universe.

But at the time, the Queen had dismissed it. A soul fragment was like a candle in a hurricane, she’d figured, ready to be snuffed out at any moment. She hadn’t given it a second thought.

After all, what could a tiny piece of a soul do? Its new little champion, Zane, was just some warrior in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. A mosquito, nothing more.

The next time Zane popped up on her radar, he’d already broken through to the Life-and-Death Realm. It had only taken him a year or two—an incredible rate of growth. Still, she hadn’t considered him a real threat. In her eyes, warriors from those lower realms were nothing but insects.

But she had decided to be cautious back then.

That was why she’d seized the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary—to wipe out Lavender’s soul once and for all. But that nuisance, Zane, had seen through her plan and stopped her.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

And now… Lavender’s soul was impossibly stronger.

[SFX: SUPERNATURAL PULSE]

She could feel it pulsing from the statue, a killing intent so potent it was almost a physical force.

A powerful Master must have helped Lavender repair her soul. The Queen’s face became a mask of stone. Whoever it was, it seemed they had already ascended to the Upper World.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Even repaired, the soul fragment wasn’t a direct threat. Not yet. But the Queen realized with a sickening lurch that the spark she’d ignored now had the potential to become an inferno.

That was the source of the dread coiling in her gut. If Lavender’s soul could be restored this much, she could eventually reform a physical body. She could regain her power. She could fight back.

The thought made bile rise in her throat. Even the statue seemed to mock her with its cold, stone eyes.

“How dare you mock me?” she hissed, teeth clenched. With a flick of her finger, an invisible wave of power shot out.

[SFX: STONE SHATTERING]

Cracks spiderwebbed across the statue before it exploded into dust.

But the head hadn’t shattered cleanly. It lay among the debris, eyes still open, still staring up at her.

[SFX: STONE CRACK]

She flicked her finger again. This time, it shattered completely.

Even then, her mood didn’t improve. She paced the hall, the silence pressing in around her, her irritation sharpening with every step.

[SFX: ARMORED FOOTSTEPS]

Two female guards in leather armor entered the hall and dropped to one knee.

“My queen,” the first reported. “We’ve found Amber. She ascended to the Dark Light World three days ago.”

A cold smile crossed the Queen’s face. “Is that so? I thought she’d never have the nerve. Bring her to me.”

Amber had walked a careful line throughout the conflict between the two queens — never openly opposing the Queen of Punishment, never fully betraying Lavender either. In her own quiet way, she’d done what she could to help. The Queen of Punishment had never forgotten it. Neutrality, in her eyes, was just betrayal wearing a polite face. And anyone who crossed her — however carefully — would answer for it eventually.

The Queen of Punishment wasn’t the only one who had noticed. Throughout the Demon Night clan’s sacred places, warriors paid their respects to the king statues every day. When Lavender’s statue blazed back to life, the whole clan felt it. Her former subordinates — those who had stayed loyal through everything — stirred for the first time in years. Among them was at least one Supreme Lord.

Suddenly, a voice boomed through the hall. “You witch! You have the gall to call me a traitor?”

Lavender’s head snapped up. “It’s him,” she said, something bright cutting through her usual stillness. “Leroy Flynn.”

“Who is he?” Zane asked.

“One of my supporters,” Lavender said. “The last Supreme Lord who’s still loyal to me. He was attacked by several of the Queen’s allies and badly injured. He’s been hiding somewhere outside the Demon Night clan ever since.” A shadow crossed her face. “He shouldn’t still be fighting for me. But he never stopped.”

Leroy continued. “Lavender’s soul has recovered—some powerful Master must have helped her! You’ll be torn from that throne someday, I swear it! I may be entering the Five Aging Processes, but I’ll live long enough to see you cast out!”

Leroy’s words sent a jolt through Lavender. She had always been so calm, so cold, no matter the circumstances. But Zane had never seen this fire in her eyes before.

Leroy must have been watching her statue all along. When it blazed back to life, he’d put the pieces together — a powerful master, the Upper World, Lavender’s soul restored. He’d reached for his Roaring Token the moment he understood.

[MUSIC: SINISTER LAUGH]

“A powerful Master? Ha!” the Queen of Punishment sneered. “Don’t be a fool, Leroy. Only a fragment of her soul is repaired. She’s not whole. She can’t even respond to you—I bet she doesn’t even have a Roaring Token!”

[AMBIENT: COSMIC VOID]

[SFX: ETHEREAL ECHOES]

Just then, other voices echoed from across the universe.

“Leroy, you need to find a replacement. The Queen of Punishment is right. Why are you so damn stubborn? Just accept reality!”

“It’s impossible for Lavender to return to the Demon Night. That last wisp of her soul won’t last.”

“Are you deaf, Leroy? Who in the entire universe could possibly dethrone the Queen? You? Don’t make me laugh!”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]

The voices, all using Roaring Tokens, belonged to the Supreme Lords of the Demon Night—all of them loyal to the Queen.

Hearing them, Lavender bit her lip, her shoulders slumping. “It’s a shame I don’t have a Roaring Token,” she murmured.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

As her face fell, Zane just smiled.

Lavender caught his expression and frowned. “What’s so funny?”

[SFX: DRAMATIC REVEAL]

“Because I do,” Zane said with a sly grin.
