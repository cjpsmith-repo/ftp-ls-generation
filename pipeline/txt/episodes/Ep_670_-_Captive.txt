Ep 670 - Captive

Word count: 1103

[SFX: VIOLENT TREMOR]

[SFX: RATS SCATTERING]

Even though his usual reserves of power were useless here, Zane could still feel the Dragon Scales humming beneath his skin. He drew on the power of just over a hundred of them, and a violent tremor ripped through his body, instantly blasting every last one of the chittering rats off him.

For Esther, Fabian, and the others, the swarm of so-called Murderous Rats was a desperate struggle for survival. For Zane, they were a nuisance. An annoyance. They couldn't even scratch him. While they fought for their lives, carving a bloody path through the tide of creatures, he was basically just swatting away flies.

[AMBIENT: DEEP WILDERNESS]

[SFX: DISTANT DRUMS]

Then, from somewhere deep in the wilderness, came the sound of drums.

[SFX: SLOW HYPNOTIC DRUMBEAT]

[SFX: HIGH-PITCHED SHRIEK]

[SFX: RATS BURROWING]

The rhythm was slow, hypnotic. As it rolled over them, a high-pitched squeal sliced through the air. The sound was so sharp it felt like a needle in the ear. In that same instant, every single rat attacking them broke off, scattering in a frenzy. They clawed at the dirt, burrowing into the ground until, in a matter of seconds, they were gone.

"That shriek," Zane said, his eyes tracing the dark, bloody trail of corpses they'd left behind. "Must have been their king."

[SFX: SWOOSHING WINGS]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

A few moments later, dozens of black shadows swooped down from the sky, hurtling straight for them. From this distance, the others couldn't make them out, but a faint green light flared in Zane's eyes as he activated his Magic Vision.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[SFX: SPEARS RATTLING]

They weren't Human or Ogre, and they were nothing like the demons from the Night. Nobody had ever seen anything like them. Their bodies were covered in slick, green scales, and they clutched long spears in their hands. Before anyone could react, they had the group completely surrounded.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

The one in the lead scanned the group, his gaze finally landing on Zane. He raised his spear and barked something in a bizarre, guttural language nobody could understand. They all exchanged tense, confused glances, nobody daring to make a move.

"What are they?" Fabian asked, his hand tight on his sword hilt.

Esther shook her head. "Natives, maybe? The original inhabitants of this place."

"So, what's the plan? They look like they're itching for a fight," Malcolm mumbled, wincing. He was still nursing wounds from the rats. Ever since they'd entered the Mad Scorpion's body, it felt like he'd done nothing but get beaten up.

"What's there to be afraid of?" Timothy shot back, glaring up at the sky. "If they want a fight, we'll give them one to the death!"

Everyone looked at Timothy as if he'd lost his mind.

Then the leader moved. He raised one protruding ear, listening for something beneath the ground. Then he swung his spear down in a sharp arc.

[SFX: GROUND SPLITTING]

An invisible wave burst from the tip and tore through the earth in a long, jagged crack. The ground split apart, and at the far end, the crack detonated with a violent explosion.

[SFX: EXPLOSION]

Soil blasted upward. A yellow rat shot out of the crack, squealing in midair. The leader snatched it out of the sky without breaking stride.

The rat king thrashed and writhed in his grip. It couldn't break free.

"That's the rat king," Zane said quietly. "The one that sent them after us."

Nobody replied. They were all staring at what the leader had just done. Without True Essence, without Vitality — with nothing but a spear and raw technique — he'd split the ground, flushed out a burrowing creature, and caught it mid-flight. None of them could have done that. Not Esther. Not Fabian. Not anyone.

Timothy said nothing. His earlier bravado had gone very quiet.

The green-skins conferred among themselves. Then they turned back to look at Zane's group — and the killing intent on their faces was unmistakable.

Zane's hand moved to his ring. If it came to a fight, he'd draw his Shattered Dagger and take at least one or two of them down before it was over.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Then Yolande spoke, arguing something in their language that nobody else could follow.

After a long moment, one of them answered.

They went back and forth. Then the green-skins reached behind their backs and pulled out a large net.

"Don't dodge," Yolande said.

"What?" Esther's expression darkened.

"I've convinced them not to kill us. They'll take us captive instead."

"And then what?" Fabian demanded.

The net was already coming down.

[SFX: NET SWOOSH]

It blanketed the entire group in an instant. But while the rest were instantly tangled, it couldn't hold Lavender. She slipped right through the strands.

[MUSIC: AGGRESSIVE PERCUSSION]

One of the green-skinned creatures pointed at her and shouted a command. With her famously short fuse, Lavender didn't hesitate, raising her spear to strike.

"Lavender, stop," Yolande called out.

"Why should I?" Lavender snapped, never one to hide her dislike for Yolande. "I don't take orders from you."

"If you want Zane to get out of here alive," Yolande said, her voice cold and firm, "you'll do as I say."

Lavender froze. She glanced at Zane, trapped in the net, and her defiance faltered. With a frustrated sigh, she tossed her spear into his Storage Ring.

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

Then she dissolved into a speck of red light and shot directly into his body.

[AMBIENT: CREATURE GROWLS]

[SFX: ROPE CREAKING]

The display caught the attention of every single creature above them. Their eyes locked onto the Storage Ring, glinting with greedy light, but for some reason, they didn't try to take it. Instead, they each pulled on a thick rope attached to the net, hoisting everyone into the air. The golden fibers were impossibly strong; their swords couldn't even scratch them. Tangled up like this, they were completely helpless.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TONE]

"They're taking us back to their lair!" Jarrod yelled, his voice thick with anger and fear. "We're at their mercy! They can slaughter us whenever they want!"

"Yolande, you said you had a way out of the fog!" Esther cried, her voice cracking. "What is this? We're doomed!"

Malcolm stayed silent. After Yolande had broken his arm, he clearly didn't dare say another word against her.

Yolande just sneered at their panic. "You were trapped by the fog's enchantment, seconds from being dragged into an abyss. Without me, you'd all be dead already."

Hearing that, no one dared to say another word.
