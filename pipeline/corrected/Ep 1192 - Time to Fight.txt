Ep 1192 - Time to Fight

Word count: 1008

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
Across the universe, people were still mulling over the story Zane had just told. But, of course, not everyone was a fan.
You see, a lot of martial artists have to train in complete isolation, where the slightest noise can throw them off. When you’re at a critical point in your training, one single interruption can make your powers backlash catastrophically. 
[SFX: DEEP RESONANT ROAR ECHOING]
The sound of a Roaring Token is unstoppable, echoing through every corner of existence. It doesn’t matter if you’re buried deep underground or floating in the void—it pierces through everything. There’s no way to block it.
The Supreme Lords only have a handful of these tokens, and they use them sparingly. Usually it’s one a day, at most, and sometimes they’ll go two weeks without using one at all. Even then, when that sound rings out, it’s a huge risk for any martial artist attempting a breakthrough. It can completely derail their progress.
And now, Zane had used a Roaring Token just to tell a story—a story that had been going on for hours.
For those martial artists, it was pure torture. Some of them had been forced to abandon their training altogether, their frustration simmering into a quiet resentment for Zane. Not that it mattered. None of them could afford a Roaring Token in a thousand lifetimes. Even if they screamed their fury into the void, Zane would never hear it.
[SFX: METAL CLANGING]
[SFX: RAPID FOOTSTEPS]
Just as he was wrapping things up, a series of clangs echoed from behind him. He turned to see Livingston scrambling toward him. In a flash, Livingston was there, grabbing Zane’s arms, his voice shaking with excitement.
“Z-Zane…”
“What is it?” Zane asked, a little taken aback by his intensity.
“I… I…” Livingston waved his hands, too worked up to get the words out. Finally, he managed to spit it out. 
“The Archives. I need to tell you about the Archives.” He let go of Zane’s arms and tried to collect himself. “When I first went in, I thought — the owner of this place is a wasteful fool. Using materials like that for floors and walls. Like handing a masterwork blade to someone who uses it to cut vegetables.”
Zane waited.
“But then I started reading.” Livingston’s voice had gone quieter. “The weapon-refining texts. Every bold idea I ever had — things I proposed and couldn’t realize, things I thought were impossible — they were real. Not just real. Completed. Perfected beyond anything I’d imagined.” He paused. “These books didn’t come from this universe. They came from above the Celestial Law. And I—” He stopped again. “I came out of that room, and I couldn’t stand straight.”
“I need to get into that Armory,” he said. “Whatever’s in there — if the books are like that, the workshop will be—” He couldn’t finish the sentence.
Zane gave a slight, apologetic smile. “Now that I’m back, I’m definitely going to challenge the path again. But I can’t promise I’ll be able to open The Armory.”
The problem was how the token system worked. Zane already held four — the token to enter the Keep, the token to run the true path, the token to carry the Keep, and the token to access the Archives. The four he still needed corresponded to the Armory, the Alchemist’s Laboratory, the training tower, and one more. But each run of the true path yielded a token at random. There was even a chance of pulling a duplicate. He couldn’t control which door opened next.
[SFX: METALLIC CLANG]
[SFX: BODY COLLAPSING]
At his words, Livingston’s shoulders slumped, and he just sort of collapsed to the ground in a heap of dejection. It made Zane realize he was getting ahead of himself. His power was still too low. He just needed to focus on training.
After some preparation, Saul led the way. Zane, Freya, and Lavender followed, with Livingston clanging along behind them. They moved through the Keep’s main hall and headed straight for the true path.
The last time Zane had run this path, he was still in the lower realm. Back then, he’d used Power Transfer — a technique that let him channel strength from across the entire lower world. That was how he’d cleared the first two stages. Now, having ascended, that connection was severed. He had only himself.
But he wasn’t the same person he’d been then. He stepped into the vast fog without hesitation.
[MUSIC: DARK BATTLE THEME]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL COMBAT ARENA]
*Time to fight.*
[SFX: SWORD SWEEP]
[SFX: SHADOWS STREAMING]
He raised his sword, and with a slow sweep, dozens of Nether God Shadows streamed from the blade, fanning out to engage the Sacred martial artists filling the path. 
[SFX: COMBAT STRIKES]
[SFX: DISSOLVING ENERGY]
The shadowy figures tore through their opponents with silent ease, dissolving them into little more than wisps of dark and light energy. Zane knew this first part of the third stage wouldn’t be much of a challenge. The shadows alone were enough to shred these warriors. He kept a steady pace and moved forward.
But after about five minutes, something changed. 
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
The warriors formed from the Dark and Light Energies seemed stronger, more solid. Now, almost every opponent he faced was a late-stage Soul Sea Realm master.
“Elites from the Sacred Race?” Zane’s gaze sharpened.
These weren’t like the others. 
[SFX: ARMOR CLANKING]
[SFX: WEAPONS DRAWN]
They were decked out in ornate golden armor, intricately carved, and they wielded an arsenal of Divine-Class weapons. The Nether God Shadows, which had been so effective moments before, barely made a dent. The Elites cleaved through the shadows with contemptuous ease and began to circle him.
The Sacred Race. The same golden bloodline as the Demi-Immortal Race — though what exactly connected the two, Zane still didn’t know.
[SFX: SWORD SWING]
Zane met the first one that rushed him, swinging his Thundergust.
[SFX: METAL CLASH]
The impact sent a sharp vibration up his arm. Zane’s eyes widened slightly in surprise. The Elite had blocked him, catching his blade on a golden halberd.
Zane grunted, twisting his sword in a fluid motion. 
[SFX: BLADE WHOOSH]
[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]
The blade became a blur of light, wrapping around the Elite’s neck. A spray of golden blood erupted, and by the time it hit the ground, Zane was already a thousand feet down the path.
