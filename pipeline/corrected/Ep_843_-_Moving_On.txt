Ep 843 - Moving On

Word count: 1093

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Randy and Camilla were just as stunned as everyone else. They had absolute confidence in Zane—the guy could handle anything—but they had no idea how he was shrugging off the Dragon Arsenic.

Sure, masters from the Soul Sea Realm could probably block arrows moving that slowly, but even they wouldn't want to get hit. Dragon Arsenic was a secret poison brewed by the dragon-blooded, and it was lethally effective. To this day, no one in the entire Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms had ever found an antidote.

How was he doing it?

[SFX: ARROW WHISTLES]

Just then, a sharp scream ripped through the air, sounding dangerously close. They watched in shock as more and more arrows began flying in from both sides, boxing them in and leaving them with nowhere to run.

Seeing the rising panic, Zane knew he didn't have time for a lengthy explanation. “Get out of the Slow Arrow Field!” he roared. “Get out of the way!”

[AMBIENT: PANICKED CROWD]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

His body was already coated in a sickening sheen of Dragon Arsenic, but he was still sprinting forward, a sight that freaked everyone out. The poison was visibly spreading across his skin, turning him into something like a grotesque blue statue. Who in their right mind would dare touch him?

At his command, the people in front of him scrambled to clear a path, letting him charge through the chaos.

[AMBIENT: TENSE MURMURS]

“Look at it spreading on him! You think the poison's already in his head?”

“Damn. He's lost his mind. The guy's completely nuts.”

“Well, at least he's going all out, pushing that stone and sacrificing himself for the rest of us.”

The other high-level geniuses watched Zane, whispering among themselves. The Dragon Arsenic was fatal, so how was he still moving? They couldn't make sense of it, guessing he must have some secret technique to buy himself a little more time. But once that ran out, he'd be dead for sure.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

[SFX: STONE CRACKING]

With a series of heavy thuds, Zane slammed his body into the lodestone again and again. The warriors who had been helping him push now kept a wide berth, dodging him cautiously. With Zane's full strength behind it, the massive stone surged forward.

Then he saw it — a row of black walls standing at the edge of the Slow Arrow Field, dotted with perfectly arranged round holes. That was where the arrows were coming from.

[SFX: POWERFUL KICK]

[SFX: STONE SLAMMING INTO WALL]

Zane planted his foot and drove it into the lodestone with everything he had. The massive stone launched through the air and crashed into the nearest wall with a thunderous boom, its bulk sealing off a row of the round holes and cutting the arrow flow by half.

“Up! Over the wall!” he shouted.

He jumped, landed on top of the lodestone, and vaulted over the wall in a single motion. The others didn't hesitate. One by one, they scrambled up — using the lodestone as a step, hauling themselves over — though every last one of them gave Zane a wide berth. The blue Dragon Arsenic coating his skin was not something anyone wanted to brush against.

[AMBIENT: CALM ATMOSPHERE]

When the last of them dropped down on the other side, the roar of the Slow Arrow Field faded behind them. They were out.

Callan walked up to Zane. “You saved my sister. Thank you.”

Zane gave a faint shrug. “It was nothing.”

Fiona was still staring at the blue coating on his skin, her brow creased with worry. “Jones — are you alright?”

Zane looked down at his hands. The Dragon Arsenic had spread across his entire surface, but no deeper. It was strange — the poison seemed to travel along the skin rather than into the body. He could feel it, but it hadn't touched anything vital.

“It's an odd poison,” he said. “Spreads on its own. But I don't think it can kill me.”

Randy and the others visibly exhaled.

Fredrick barely glanced over. “Let's go,” he said flatly, and walked on without another word. Not a trace of gratitude.

Zane sat down on the wall and closed his eyes. He drew his Vitality up slowly from his core, extending it along his arms, and pushed it outward to meet the Dragon Arsenic coating his skin.

[SFX: HISSING SMOKE]

The moment the two made contact, the poison reacted violently. Billowing blue smoke poured off his body in thick curls.

“Back up — now!” Zane snapped. “The smoke is poisonous!”

Fiona, Callan, Randy, and the others scrambled back instantly, putting fifty or sixty feet between themselves and Zane. Renzo kept going — a hundred feet, then a little more, until Camilla started laughing.

“You don't have to run that far!” she called after him.

Renzo's face went red. He slowed, turned, and walked back with as much dignity as he could manage.

[SFX: POISON SMOKE HISSING]

The blue smoke kept coming. The Dragon Arsenic was corroding his Vitality as fast as he pushed it out, but the corrosion was working both ways — each exchange neutralized a portion of the poison. Zane leaned into it, drawing out more, letting the smoke roll off him in waves until the last of it had burned away.

After a little more than ten breaths, the blue was gone. His skin was his own color again.

[MUSIC: GENTLE MELODY]

He hadn't even stood up when a jade-like hand extended toward him, offering a small carved stone. Zane blinked. It was a supreme Aether crystal — but shaped, with real skill, into a perfect peach.

Fiona watched his curious expression as he accepted it. She smiled. “I carved it myself when I was bored. Not bad, right?”

[SFX: CRYSTAL CRACK]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

With a soft crack, Zane gently crushed the crystal in his hand and began absorbing the pure Vitality sealed inside. He nodded. “Not bad. Not bad at all.”

Randy finally spoke up. “Alright, let's keep moving.” He saw that Callan, Fiona, and their group were sticking with them and realized they were naturally forming a new team, with Zane as their unspoken leader. Fiona might act naive, but Randy suspected she was even stronger than he was. 

A new, stronger team was a good thing.

[MUSIC: NEW ADVENTURE]

“After we get past the Slow Arrow Field, we'll be in The Spectral Plains,” Randy explained as they walked. “But because of the blood sacrifice, all the spirit beasts there have been wiped out.”

The dragon-blooded blood sacrifice involved offering the lives of their entire clan. In theory, that meant nothing was left alive in any of the four great secret lands.

“So, does that mean there's no more danger?” one of the geniuses following Callan asked.

Randy just gave him a small, knowing smile. “What do you think?”
