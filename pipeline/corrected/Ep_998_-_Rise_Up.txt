Ep 998 - Rise Up
Word count: 1134
[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND]
[SFX: STONE SCRAPING]
All three thousand black wind blades had only managed to scratch the surface of Sacred Mount Lodestone.
But a single, double-colored blade carved a gash ten feet deep into the stone.
The difference in power was staggering.
If one golden wind blade was worth ten thousand of the double-colored ones, it could probably cleave Sacred Mount Lodestone clean in two.
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]
[SFX: WHOOSHING BLADES]
All Zane had to do was bait a single golden blade. Pulling that off would save him an incredible amount of time and energy. It was a perfect plan, with just one tiny flaw: more than a hundred golden blades were currently screaming after him.
Where am I supposed to redirect all this power? Zane thought, his frustration mounting.
Toward the Divine Kingdom Continent? The Heartlands?
The terrifying force would rip the entire continent apart.
[MUSIC: TENSE CHASE]
With no good options, Zane just kept running.
Luckily, he was quick on his feet—and in his head. The instant he sensed the golden blades locking onto him, he shot off, flying as fast and as far as his energy would take him. He put some distance between them, but the thought of a hundred golden blades hurtling after him was still enough to send a chill down his spine.
[SFX: WHOOSHING BLADES]
He knew exactly what they could do. With that much destructive power on his tail, he wasn’t safe at all. They could close the distance in a split second, he thought, sweat beading on his forehead.
He gave up trying to find a solution. He couldn’t bring himself to sacrifice the Divine Kingdom Continent or the Heartlands, and he refused to let the main continent take the hit. If the blades caught up, he’d probably have to let them tear apart the savage lands once he’d moved the mountain.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL REVELATION]
Then, an idea sparked. “I have the token! The one with ‘mountain’ on it! It should get me into The Elysian Keep!”
[SFX: BLADES STOPPING]
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
But just as he was about to make his move, the golden wind blades stalled, circling in the air. A second later, they whipped around and flew away from him.
[MUSIC: RELIEF]
Confused, Zane stopped mid-flight and watched them go. A wry smile touched his lips. “Huh. They’re actually smart,” he muttered, letting out a long sigh of relief as the blades vanished over the horizon. That was way too close.
He watched the golden blades disappear and felt the pull of curiosity. Whatever was at the center of this storm hadn’t come from this world. The lower realm couldn’t produce something like that. Even if he was willing to sacrifice everything to get there, he doubted he’d survive the approach. He turned away from the storm’s heart and flew toward the mountain.
That had been the easy part. What followed was half a month of work.
Back and forth across the storm, absorbing one double-colored blade after another, transferring each impact to the mountain’s base. By the time it was done, he’d taken nearly thirty thousand wind blades in total.
His body had paid for every one of them. But when he checked the mountain through the World’s Will, the base was almost completely free — only a few thin connections at the edges still held it to the ocean floor.
Zane had already used the World’s Will to pinpoint the mountain’s location west of the Chaotic Ocean of Stars. Now he flew directly toward it.
[AMBIENT: OCEAN WIND]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS WONDER]
He’d observed the mountain countless times through the World’s Will, but this was the first time he’d actually come to it in person. The scale of it stopped him. Only one-sixth of Sacred Mount Lodestone rose above the water — just the tip of a mountain that plunged deep into the ocean floor. What sat above the surface was already enormous. What lay beneath was almost beyond imagining.
Zane landed on the summit and looked down at the peak beneath his feet. Something clicked. The lodestone fragment Fredrick had produced in the Divine Emperor’s Secret Land — person-tall, green-mottled, radiating the five elemental laws — it would fit right here. He could see it clearly. He had no idea how it had broken off or when. But the shape of the absence was obvious now that he was standing in it.
He filed the thought away and got to work.
He started at the summit, pressing the first divine texture into the stone, then leapt off and dove into the water.
[SFX: UNDERWATER PRESSURE]
[MUSIC: TENSE UNDERCURRENT]
Underwater, the mountain stretched away in every direction — vast, green-mottled, ancient. Zane worked his way around its circumference, imprinting divine textures one by one into the stone. He was halfway through when he felt it: a disturbance in the water behind him.
He turned.
Dozens of creatures were sweeping toward him from the deep. Strange fish with bodies like nothing he’d seen on the surface. A massive octopus, its skin covered entirely in black teeth. Other things he couldn’t name. Tenth-Level Beasts — the equivalent of Life-and-Death Realm warriors — and a few Ninth-Level ones mixed in.
They’d lived around this mountain for who knew how many thousands of years. He was about to rip their home out of the ground. He couldn’t really blame them.
“You’re courting death.”
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
[MUSIC: VICTORIOUS DRUMS]
Chaos energy surged from his Vitality core. A longsword formed in his hand. He swung once.
The blade of Chaos-infused sword intent carved through the water in a dusky cyclone, trailing a string of tiny bubbles, and the first wave of beasts was simply gone. Cut into pieces before they registered the strike. The sharpness of it surprised even Zane. Replacing his Vitality with Chaos energy had changed something fundamental. He’d known it in theory. Now he felt it.
His sword strike had taken down dozens of the Tenth- and Ninth-Level Beasts. The remaining creatures finally seemed to realize how outmatched they were, scrambling over each other to flee.
Good for them, because Zane had no intention of giving chase. He had just destroyed the land they’d lived on for tens of thousands of years. If they hadn’t been so reckless, he never would’ve had the heart to kill them.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD-UP]
With the beasts scattered, Zane quickly laid out the divine textures across the mountain. Once everything was in place, he activated them one by one, following the method from the Myriad Manifestations of Nature. Finally, he returned to the summit of Sacred Mount Lodestone and activated the last texture.
[SFX: RUMBLING EARTH]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
He slammed his palm down on the mountain’s peak and roared, “Rise up!”
