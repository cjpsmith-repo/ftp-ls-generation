Ep 791 - Drawing Zane In

Word count: 1095

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

Randy lay crumpled on the ground outside the red circle, his legs having given out beneath him — beaten not by a technique or a weapon, but by the sheer, crushing weight of Zane's power.

 The air grew thick, choked by a heavy silence. Hundreds of warriors from the Spirit-Tribulation Realm could only stare, their minds struggling to process the scene unfolding before them.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]

[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMUR]

 From their vantage point, the eyes of three powerful masters from the Soul Sea Realm glinted with an otherworldly light. These men were the pillars of the Crystal Martial House—one head and two deputies, figures seen as the bedrock of the entire Harlen Divine Kingdom. And they, more than anyone, knew just how strong Randy was supposed to be.

The prince was a beast. He didn’t just train his body and Vitality; his unique physical makeup, combined with the imperial family’s secret Cultivation Method, allowed him to fuse raw, Forceful Energy with his life force. That fusion granted him power far beyond anyone else at his level.

And Randy hadn't even played his trump card. All he’d used was that fusion technique. Then again, Zane hadn’t used any special moves either—just a flicker of Forceful Energy and maybe half the power of his Dragon Scales.

 And just like that, Randy had lost.

The three leaders watched Zane, a look of stunned pleasure dawning on their faces. Could this young man’s talent be First Tier? they all wondered. Is it possible he’s on the same level as Lord Randy and the man from the Divine Kingdom?

 Whether they wanted to believe it or not, the truth was right in front of them. The victory was undeniable, and they all had the distinct feeling that Zane was still holding back. 

“We misjudged him,” one of the leaders finally said, his voice low. “This man is a monster. I have no idea where he came from.”

“Ha! Who cares where he came from?” another shot back with a grin. “His presence is a massive win for the Crystal Martial House. Let’s stay out of the drama between Lord Randy and his rival. Since this guy is here for our assessment, he’s one of ours now.”

“We’re in luck,” the third leader added. “Until now, Edwin was the only First-Tier talent our house ever produced — and the Crown Prince claimed him long ago. Now we have this new guy. I bet he’s just as good, if not better.”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]

 Down below, Edwin, the top warrior of the Crystal Martial House in the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, stared at Zane, his heart hammering against his ribs. A faint trace of excitement crossed his face.

If this were any other time or place, he would have already been walking over to challenge him to a duel. But this was the Crystal Martial House assessment, and with the three leaders watching, he wouldn’t dare break the rules.

Randy, on the other hand, answered to no one.

A manic grin spread across his face as he looked at Zane. "Okay, tough guy. You've passed the assessment. Now — I'm challenging you."

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]

 His aura erupted. It rolled outward like a physical force, washing over the Spirit-Tribulation warriors around him and sending visible shock through the crowd. This wasn't a Spirit-Tribulation aura. It was something closer to a Life-and-Death Realm warrior's pressure.

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMURS]

 "He's been hiding his true strength this whole time—"

"That aura — even a first-stage Life-and-Death warrior might not match that—"

"That mystery kid is in serious trouble."

Zane frowned. He was here for a token, not a duel. He shook his head. "I refuse."

Randy blinked. "Refuse?" He laughed, as if the word hadn't fully registered. "Here's my offer — fight me, win or lose, and I'll hand you a Harlen Token personally. On top of that, you can pick whatever you want from my treasury. Rare materials, elixirs, name it."

A wave of envy rippled through the watching crowd. Randy's personal wealth alone was said to rival the Crystal Martial House's entire accumulated stores. Even losing to him wouldn't be shameful — and walking away with a token plus treasures of your choosing? Everyone in the square was thinking the same thing: that kid Zane Jones is unbelievably lucky.

Zane shook his head again. "I only need the token. I'll earn it myself."

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

Randy's face went dark red. The Harlen Divine Kingdom had many princes, but Randy carried himself as if he were in a league of his own. He knew the leaders were right, but reason was the last thing on his mind.

[SFX: WHISPER EFFECT]

 “Lord Randy, listen to me,” Master Hill said, a knowing smile playing on his lips. His mouth barely moved, his words carried on a thread of Vitality that only the prince could hear.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS FADE]

 After a long, tense silence, Randy finally gave a stiff nod. The suffocating pressure radiating from him vanished, and his face relaxed into a placid mask. He motioned to Zane.

“I admit defeat. You’re a powerful warrior,” he announced. “I’d like to be friends, Zane. I’ll invite you to my home for a feast another day!”

Zane was completely baffled. The sudden shift in attitude was so jarring that it set off alarm bells in his head. Who knew what this guy was really planning? He was, of course, on edge. The divine kingdoms were hornet nests of political conflict, and by revealing his true strength, he might have just kicked one wide open.

But whatever Randy was plotting had nothing to do with him. For now, all he could do was play along. He was a guest in the Harlen Divine Kingdom; embarrassing a prince in his own territory was a bad move. So, Zane just nodded and offered a polite gesture in return.

[AMBIENT: CROWD EXHALES]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS FADING]

 Only after Randy turned and walked away did the crowd seem to remember how to breathe.

[MUSIC: DARK CONSPIRACY THEME]

 Amid the quiet, Edwin watched Randy’s retreating back before his eyes slid slowly to Zane, a flicker of suspicion in his gaze.

Though a member of the Crystal Martial House, Edwin secretly worked for Randy’s greatest rival. Their conflict was ancient and bitter. Now, this unknown had appeared, demonstrating incredible power. Whether they could win Zane over to their side was secondary; the most important thing was making sure a man like him didn't end up allied with Randy.

He had to get word to his master. Immediately.
