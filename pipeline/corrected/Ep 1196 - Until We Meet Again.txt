Ep 1196 - Until We Meet Again

Word count: 1020

[MUSIC: TENSE AFTERMATH]
Zane’s only goal was to get through the third section of the Elysian Keep’s true path. 
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ECHOING]
This was the exact spot where Gus had ambushed him.
Even though he knew Gus was their master’s second disciple, Zane hadn’t held back. He couldn’t. Gus was just too strong, and giving him even an inch could have cost Zane the fight.
But now, with Gus beaten and unable to move, it was the perfect time for that talk he’d wanted.
Seeing the sly grin spread across Zane’s face, Gus rolled his eyes. “Seriously? You’re gonna do me like this?” he demanded, his voice full of indignation.
He’d been bullied by their master’s first apprentice for years. He’d figured when he finally met the third, he could finally be the one to throw his weight around. Instead, Zane had fought him like a demon from the moment they met. It pissed him off to no end.
Zane’s grin widened. “Is *this* what you wanted to talk about?” 
[SFX: SWORD GRIP TIGHTENING]
[SFX: BLADE SCRAPING]
He tightened his grip on his sword, Thundergust, holding it like he was ready to lop Gus’s head off at a moment’s notice.
“Of course not,” Gus grumbled.
Zane waited a beat. “Then what, exactly, did you want to say?”
“Look, I was doing this for your own good! We have the same master, after all,” Gus said, his voice laced with desperation. “If our big brother finds out about this, he’ll probably decide to punish me again.”
Zane just stared, his expression completely blank.
“You’re a disciple of the Elysian Keep, but that’s not all you’ve trained in, is it?” Gus pressed. “The primordial chaos tactic is one thing, but your other techniques… they aren’t from the archives, are they?”
Zane nodded. Though uncertain how Gus knew, it was true - he’d been selective with the archives’ countless martial arts, studying only the primordial chaos tactic and books on the Original Laws.
“What a waste,” Gus sighed, curling his lip. “You have access to so many treasures, and you don’t even use them.”
“Before I go,” Gus said, his voice dropping to something more serious, “there are three cultivation methods in the archives you should find. Not Transcendent Divine Mights — something beyond that. I can’t say exactly what, but I relied on all three to cross the heavens myself. Combined with the Primordial Chaos Tactic, they’ll change everything.” He paused. “You’re lacking in a lot of ways. But you shouldn’t be too far off.”
“Hey,” Zane cut in, gesturing with his chin. “Your body is fading away. You might want to get to the point.”
[SFX: DISSOLVING PARTICLES]
Gus glanced down. His neck was already beginning to dissolve, wisps of Dark and Light drifting upward from the edges. “I know,” he said.
“Then stop wasting what’s left of it,” Zane said.
Gus glared at him.
Gus’s form had been constructed from Dark and Light Energies. 
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY SHIMMER]
He didn’t seem to care. “This little fragment of my soul can’t tell your fortune,” he continued, “but as our master’s disciple, it’ll be a joke if you can’t even ascend to the heavens.”
His head was nearly gone.
“To be honest, if you rely only on the Divine Path of Sealed Emotion, crossing the heavens will be harder than you think. So since master isn’t here — your big brother is giving you this guidance in his place.”
With that, the last of him dissolved. The Dark and Light energies swirled for a moment — and then a single point of light emerged from the dissipating wisps.
[SFX: SOFT CHIME]
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL LIGHT HUM]
It circled Zane once, slowly, and then a voice entered his mind directly — warm, unhurried, unmistakably Gus.
“Work hard. Climb up. Your big brother will be waiting for you at the top — and when you get there, I’ll finally get a good look at you. Hahaha…”
[SFX: ASCENDING TONE FADING INTO SILENCE]
The point of light shot upward, rising higher and higher through the Keep until it vanished through the barrier at the top. Then it was gone.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
Above the heavens, somewhere in the upper world, a jade palace rose from dense fog.
Atop one of its rooftops, a boy sat astride an ox, a flute pressed to his lips, playing softly.
[SFX: DISTANT FLUTE MELODY]
A figure came leaping up through the palace rooftops in the distance, moving fast, waving both arms.
“Big brother! Big brother!”
[SFX: HEAVY LANDING]
[SFX: ROOF TILES CRACKING]
The figure landed hard. Roof tiles shattered. The ox startled.
The boy lowered his flute and fixed the newcomer with a cold stare. “Why are you so flustered?”
“I can read the Destiny of anyone under heaven—except for those who have none. That’s the only explanation.” The boy frowned, staring down at his own fingers for a moment before shooting a glare at Gus. 
“You’ve trained in the vision technique. Even a tiny fragment of your soul should be able to read a person’s fortune. You met him in the Keep. Why didn’t you read his?”
“Well,” Gus said, an innocent look plastered on his face, “I *did* recommend some homework to him.”
“That’s not enough! You’re useless!”
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CREATURE BREATHING]
[SFX: POWERFUL HOOFBEATS]
[SFX: WHOOSH OF FLIGHT]
With that, the ox lifted its four hooves and soared into the sky.
Just then, Gus seemed to remember something. 
He smacked his head. “Wait, one more thing! From what I could tell, our little brother is practicing The Divine Path of Sealed Emotion.”
“The Divine Path of Sealed Emotion?”
The boy paused, turning back with a thoughtful look. “But isn’t he only at the Soul Sea Realm?”
Gus shrugged. “I thought it was strange, too. But he’s already mastered a good deal of it. My soul fragment took the form of a Soul Sea Realm warrior, and his sword intent killed it instantly!”
“You really are useless.” 
[SFX: RAPID FLIGHT DEPARTURE]
With that final, dismissive comment, the boy and his ox were gone.
Gus watched them disappear into the clouds above the jade rooftops. He sat down heavily on the cracked tiles, his expression darkening.
For countless years it had been like this. He was used to it.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL MISCHIEVOUS]
A moment later, though, a faint smile crept onto his face. If Zane really did manage to ascend to the heavens one day, then he’d finally have a little brother of his own to push around.
