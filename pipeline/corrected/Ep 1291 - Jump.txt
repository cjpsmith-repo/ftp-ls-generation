Ep 1291 - Jump

Word count: 1146

[SFX: MASSIVE FIST WHOOSHING THROUGH AIR]
[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: DEEP CAVERN RUMBLE]
The Lava Giant’s fist was the size of a small building, and as it swung through the air, Coleman looked like a gnat buzzing next to it, even from ten feet away. The force behind the punch was overwhelming; it was clear that even a glancing blow would shatter anyone into a million pieces, and not even a spiritual weapon could withstand it
Such creatures could challenge Spirit Transformation Realm warriors, even World Lords. Placing one in the Fourth Hall signaled the stage’s brutal difficulty - and the promise of an extraordinary reward.
Their only real hope was that the giant wasn’t very smart. If it had the intelligence of a person, getting through the hall would be impossible, even with seven hundred warriors on their side. What they were doing was undeniably insane, but since you couldn’t really die in the Illusion Battlefield, everyone was willing to take the risk.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACT]
[SFX: GROUND RIPPLING]
The fist slammed down, and the impact sent a shockwave through the solid ground, making it ripple like water. 
[SFX: DEEP RESONANT HUM]
It wasn’t just the floor shaking; the entire cavern vibrated with a deep, resonant hum, from the hall’s entrance to the core of the mountain itself.
The raw power left Zane stunned. He knew his Dragon Scales, in their current state, were nothing compared to this. The confidence he’d drawn from the Cobalt Dragon wavered; he hadn’t even come close to unlocking the scales’ full potential. 
This thing could go toe-to-toe with a World Lord, and its brute strength was probably greater. Under that kind of pressure, the only thing keeping the cavern from collapsing was the ancient magic woven into its very design.
Just in time, Janet whipped back her Lofty Wood and clapped a hand over the Frosty Cage, feeling the little flame kitten trembling inside. 
[SFX: WHOOSHING MOVEMENT]
[SFX: GRASS SPROUTING]
Without a second thought, she shot backward, her movements fluid and serpentine. Her toes glowed with a faint green light, and as she moved, blades of grass sprouted from the stone floor in her wake, leaving a bright green trail. It was her clan’s special technique: Deer Steps.
But retreating wasn’t the plan—not really. They hadn’t spent all that effort luring the giant just to run from it now. The whole point was to use its body as their bridge.
“Go! You get one shot at this—jump up along its arm!” Zane didn’t wait to see if anyone followed. He roared and leapt first, his body arcing through the air before landing hard on the giant’s forearm.
[SFX: FEET SIZZLING ON HOT STONE]
The burn hit his feet instantly—but the giant’s fire lived deep in its body, not on the surface, so it did no real harm.
Hailey, Jeffrey, and Janet came right behind him, shielding themselves with Vitality as they scrambled up onto the fist and pushed higher along the arm.
“Hey—wait for me!” Coleman had been standing frozen, staring at the fist, and only snapped out of it when he realized the others had already left him behind. He detonated a burst of Vitality and surged forward, closing the distance fast—his size hadn’t gotten in the way of five million Illusion Points, after all, even if he still lagged a step behind the others.
The seven of them now clung to the giant’s massive body like ants.
“Janet—give me the cage!” Zane flashed to her side, reaching for the Frosty Cage. If the giant only cared about the kitten, better that he be the one holding the bait.
But Janet pulled it back before he could take it. “Let me handle this one. Only I can control the Lofty Wood.” She flashed him a sideways smile. “Don’t worry. I’ve got it.”
“Then—be careful!” 
[SFX: DEEP GIANT GROWL]
The words had barely left his mouth when the giant let out another low growl. Its other arm rose, and its huge palm swung toward the shoulder they were climbing.
The seven of them broke into a full sprint.
What had started as a gentle climb up the arm was steepening fast—as the giant rose to its feet, the “hillside” was quickly becoming a sheer, near-vertical drop, with nothing below but the lake of unforgiving fire. Only the countless jagged protrusions along its rocky hide gave them anywhere to plant a foot.
The giant’s palm swept toward them, wide enough to catch all seven at once.
“Jump!” Janet lifted the cage and leapt first, clearing a hundred feet straight onto the giant’s shoulder.
Zane went next, the rest following in a scramble.
[SFX: VITALITY BURST EXPLOSION]
“Jump!” Coleman bellowed last, twin bursts of dark Vitality exploding from his feet, launching his heavy frame into the air. He couldn’t fly—nothing in the Hall allowed that—but the force was enough to carry him up and over.
[SFX: HAIR SIZZLING]
Coleman came down headfirst, straight into a crack in the giant’s shoulder. Flame licked up out of the crack the instant his head sank into it.
He cried out as he scrambled for cover, yanking his head back with a grunt. The heat had already scorched half of his long, brown hair to a crisp.
At that moment, the Lava Giant brought its left fist crashing down onto its own right arm. 
[SFX: MASSIVE STONE IMPACT]
[SFX: ROCK FRAGMENTS WHISTLING]
[SFX: LAVA HISSING]
The blow sent stone fragments flying like shrapnel. The immense pressure turned them into deadly projectiles that whistled through the air before raining down into the molten lava below with a series of sharp hisses.
“Go! Keep moving!” Zane yelled.
No one stopped. Their only way across the sea of fire was to use the giant’s body as a bridge.
The Lava Giant hadn’t managed to hit Janet. 
[SFX: MASSIVE CREATURE MOVEMENT]
It twisted its enormous head, opened its mouth, and lunged not at the group on its shoulder, but directly at the Frosty Cage in her hands.
[SFX: DEAFENING ROAR]
A deafening roar ripped through the cavern. The giant’s mouth was a thousand-foot-wide, bottomless chasm. Peering inside, they could see the fiery glow of its throat, and they knew its stomach must be a churning lake of molten rock.
“Everyone, up! Don’t let it swallow you!” Zane shouted.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTFALLS]
[SFX: LEAP THROUGH AIR]
As the massive jaw started to close, Zane led the charge, leaping from the giant’s shoulder straight onto the top of its head.
[SFX: WOOD CRACKING INTO STONE]
[SFX: RAPID PLANT GROWTH]
[SFX: WHOOSH OF VAULT]
Janet jammed the tip of her Lofty Wood into a crack in the giant’s rocky hide and let it grow. 
Gripping the other end of the staff and holding the cage tight, she used its rapid extension to vault herself upward, launching clear of the snapping maw.
