Ep 38 - No More Running

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR PATH]
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT UPLIFTING]
Walking back to the square along the same path, Zane felt incredible. Despite the familiar pressure still weighing on him, he felt as light as a feather, like he could fly with a single jump.
Now at the peak of the Core Strength Level, he could hold his breath way longer than before, and he felt a massive jump in his strength. The moment the square came into view and the pressure began to fade, Zane broke into a run.
The recruits who'd failed the Entrance Exam were already gone.
Only those who succeeded had gathered in the square, waiting for the next round. Zane scanned the crowd, guessing there were about a thousand of them left.
Compared to the tens of thousands who had started, it was a brutal ninety-percent elimination rate.
[SFX: CROWD MURMUR]
Just as he stepped into the square, Zane saw a guy waving excitedly in his direction. He headed over, a smile spreading across his face when he recognized Ethan.
"Hey, Zane! Over here! You passed! Congrats, man!" Ethan cheered, his eyes fixed on Zane's Crystal Talisman, which was now glowing a bright red.
Zane nodded. "So, I trade this for a Student ID, right? Where do I do that?"
"Yeah, you gotta get your ID. I know where to go, I just got mine. Follow me!" Ethan said, grabbing Zane's sleeve excitedly.
[AMBIENT: BUSY SQUARE]
Seeing how pumped Ethan was, Zane just smiled and followed him toward a corner of the square where a long table had been set up.
"Here we are," Ethan said as they got closer. "Once we trade the talisman for a Student ID, we're officially temporary students of the Skyreach Sect."
With Ethan's help, Zane unfastened the Crystal Talisman and got ready to hand it over. Behind the table, two members of the Skyreach Sect were handling the exchanges.
[SFX: CRYSTAL TALISMAN GLOWING]
One of them glanced at Zane and took the talisman, examining it for a moment, his eyes scanning the information recorded inside.
[SFX: WOOD CARVING]
Once he was done, the man pulled out a small, silver-wrapped wooden board and a tiny graver, then began carving into the wood.
The chisel moved with surprising grace — less like a tool, more like a brush in a steady hand. In moments, "Zane Lowell" was cut clean into the surface. The man set it down. "Keep this safe and wait over there." Zane turned the finished card over in his fingers, then pocketed it and fell in beside Ethan.
Zane's eyebrows shot up. "Crimson Powder", he realized.
He'd heard of it—an essential material for Illusion Crafting. Crafters mixed it into enchanted weapons to give them disorienting effects, and it was a key component in Runic Circles and Illusion Papers.
Ethan nodded at him. "Yeah, I heard the higher-ups used Crimson Powder to turn all of Bloody Mountain into one giant Runic Circle for trials. That's why the instructors brought us here—for The Gauntlet."
Zane had heard whispers about The Gauntlet, but the details were murky. Since Crimson Powder was used for Illusion Crafting, he figured the test must have something to do with illusions.
Though all the recruits were buzzing with curiosity about the test and the mountain, the Skyreach Sect mentors offered no more information. The students were simply led to the foot of the mountain and settled into rows of houses there.
That evening, everyone turned in early, bracing themselves for The Gauntlet the next day.
[AMBIENT: MORNING CROWD]
[SFX: DISTANT CHATTER]
[MUSIC: DETERMINED MORNING]
Early the next morning, the students gathered at the foot of the mountain. But hours crawled by with no order to head up. Finally, they figured out the holdup: they were waiting for other people to join them.
Another group was joining the thousand who'd passed the Entrance Exam for The Gauntlet: students from the Great Houses.
Because of their status, the kids from the Great Houses got to skip the Entrance Exam and head straight to The Gauntlet. Compared to the regular recruits, they played by a whole different set of rules.
[SFX: SKYCRUISERS LANDING]
[AMBIENT: ENGINES HUMMING]
Hundreds of Skycruisers descended, blotting out the sun before landing neatly in front of the mountain. Each recruit from a Legacy House arrived with an entourage of three or four people, including powerful guardians who radiated intimidating auras.
When the new arrivals saw the thousand regular recruits waiting on the ground, the disdain on their faces was obvious. They stepped out of their Skycruisers and formed their own exclusive cliques, keeping a clear distance from the ordinary recruits. Their entire upbringing screamed that they were better than everyone else.
By now, the regular recruits were getting seriously ticked off. They normally wouldn't mind waiting a few hours, but being forced to cool their heels for a bunch of entitled brats felt like a slap in the face.
Still, even as they muttered curses under their breath, no one dared to speak up. Without powerful connections, they knew better than to challenge the kids from the Great Houses. They couldn't afford to piss them off.
What really got under their skin was the blatant unfairness of it all. These kids had received better training since birth and had access to all kinds of ways to enhance their bodies and skills.
Their abilities were on a whole different level, yet they were all about to compete in the same event.
Zane stood with eyes closed, consciousness turned inward — a habit since he'd discovered he could sink his awareness deep into his own mind. He had nothing to gain from watching entitled strangers climb out of Skycruisers.
Then the atmosphere shifted. A pocket of silence moved through the crowd as recruits parted around something. He opened his eyes — and found himself the only one still standing in the path of an approaching group of Legacy House disciples.
Two stepped to the front: Caleb Warrick and Perrin Lowell.
Zane's eyes went cold. "Perrin. Keep it between us — you don't need backup."
Perrin's mouth curled. He was about to speak when Caleb touched his shoulder. "Let me." He stepped forward, lazily turning a small deep-blue butterfly pendant in his fingers. "I heard you told Perrin to handle his own problems," he said pleasantly. "This is a family matter. House Warrick has no stake in it."
"What if I decide it does?" Caleb smiled. "I'm not known for being reasonable. But do one thing, and I'll walk away."
"What?" Zane asked.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]


[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
"Go jump off the Blackwater Bridge. You do that, and I'll stay out of your house's business," Caleb said, his tone so casual he could have been talking about the weather.
The words hit Zane, and his face flushed hot with a rage that exploded inside him. But a moment later, he just laughed.
"Hahaha! That's a good one!"
Zane had sworn revenge for his father's death long ago. But even when Caleb sent four guys to rough him up during the Entrance Exam, Zane hadn't really fought back. He didn't want to provoke Caleb; House Warrick was a Legacy House, and Zane was all alone.
But after all his efforts to lay low, Caleb was still hunting him, still messing with his life. To Caleb, Zane was just some bug to be squashed at his convenience. He wasn't taking Zane seriously at all. He clearly saw Zane as a dead man walking, someone with nothing to bargain with.
You couldn't reason with guys like that. The only way to get Caleb to back off wasn't with words, but with fists.
[MUSIC: DEFIANT CHALLENGE]
"Caleb, you think I'm scared of you? You want me to quit The Gauntlet? You want me to kill myself? That's the most ridiculous thing I've ever heard," Zane shot back. "I'm standing right here. If you think you can take my life, then go ahead and try. Let's see if you've got the guts to do it!"
As he spoke, a powerful energy surged from his body. His face was a mask of calm, but inside, Zane's heart was hammering with pure rage.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[SFX: GROUND CRACKING]
Power erupted from Zane's body—not just energy, but visible waves of force that rippled the air around him. The ground beneath his feet cracked in a spiderweb pattern. Several Legacy House disciples stepped back instinctively, their eyes widening.
Caleb's face contorted with rage and disbelief. His hand moved to the weapon at his side as whispers erupted through the crowd.
"He's dead," someone murmured. "No one challenges like that and lives."
