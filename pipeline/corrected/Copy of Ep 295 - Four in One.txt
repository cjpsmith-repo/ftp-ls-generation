Ep 295 - Four in One
Word count: 1081
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]
There are countless ways to wield a sword, and even more to infuse one with spiritual power. Among these countless styles, Finn had mastered The Celestial Blade—a cultivation method so extraordinarily powerful and sophisticated that it defied any conventional ranking. It existed in a league of its own, beyond comparison with ordinary sword arts.
The true brilliance of this cultivation method lay in its elegant simplicity: a single, perfect image known as The Celestial Blade Illustration. Unlike other techniques requiring extensive scrolls or complex manuals, this singular visualization contained within it all the profound mysteries of an ancient, terrible power.
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: TENSE HISTORICAL]
When the legendary technique first appeared some eight hundred and seventy-five years ago, it threw the Eastern Territory into chaos. Noble houses, the Palace, the Skyreach Sect—every major power and independent warrior hunted for it relentlessly. The pursuit was savage, spilling rivers of blood for a single illustration. The rumor was that it held the key to divine power. Master it, and you could unlock the secrets of the heavens and claim immortality.
Hundreds, maybe thousands—no one knows exactly how many great fighters died trying to claim it.
In the end, the Skyreach Sect triumphed over their rivals and secured The Celestial Blade Illustration, later sending it to the Palace for safekeeping.
The news left countless warriors grinding their teeth in frustration. They desperately coveted the illustration, but no one dared risk the wrath of both the Skyreach Sect and the Palace to steal it from the heavily guarded palace. It seemed the secret was locked away forever… until everything changed.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
Just one year later, copies of The Celestial Blade Illustration were somehow leaked, spreading far and wide. And now, eight hundred and seventy-five years later, Finn had mastered the fourth part of the technique.
[SFX: GASPS OF AMAZEMENT]
"The fourth part! He’s mastered the fourth part! That’s the Blade of Phecda, the fourth illustration of The Celestial Blade!" gasped a spectator, eyes widening in disbelief. "By the heavens, he’s the first person to accomplish this feat in eight hundred years!"
[SFX: POWERFUL SWORD ENERGY]
"Finn is going to become a legend," another voice trembled with awe, "but look—he’s not merely utilizing the fourth part. He’s unleashing all four sword forms at the same time, weaving them into a single attack! Zane is a dead man!"
"It’s finished for him," muttered a veteran cultivator, his face grim with certainty. "I’ve heard that each part of The Celestial Blade draws power from the heavens themselves. They say anyone who masters this technique is blessed by the cosmos, marking them as a true child of destiny. With all four forms combined, there’s no way Zane can possibly win!"
"I can’t believe this," whispered a young disciple, her voice cracking with disappointment. "I genuinely thought Zane was gonna take it, but against a technique like that… he doesn’t stand a chance."
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
[AMBIENT: RISING WIND]
A tiny, shadowy seed cracked open in the air before Finn. From it, a thread of pure energy shot from his crown straight into the sky. In an instant, a massive whirlwind materialized above, then came crashing down with incredible force.
[SFX: WHIRLWIND CRASH]
The moment it hit the ground, the whirlwind scattered like liquid mercury. This was Primal Vitality—the raw, untamed energy between heaven and earth.
In its raw form, the energy was violent and dangerous, not yet refined by a warrior into usable Vitality. But in that untamed state, it held a trace of the fundamental laws of nature.
It was said that a warrior at the highest levels of cultivation could theoretically harness this Primal Vitality at will—essentially commanding the world itself.
Finn even at the Consummation of the Adept Realm, couldn’t command it directly. But that didn’t matter. The Celestial Blade technique didn’t need to control the Primal Vitality; it simply drew upon it automatically, a process beyond Finn’s conscious control.
And even just borrowing that power made him unimaginably strong.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
"Four-in-One!" Finn commanded.
[SFX: SWORD ENERGY FLARE]
A shimmering shadow of sword intent flared around his Blood-Corroding Sword. He used it to seize that trace of nature’s law, channeling its immense power into a single, overwhelming thrust aimed directly at Zane.
This was no longer about elegant swordplay; it was a strike of absolute power.
Inside the arena, the effect was something else entirely. To Zane, it felt as if the world had dissolved. He stood alone on a boundless ocean, nothing around him but water and sky. It wasn't a soul attack. It was just power. Pure, borrowed, overwhelming power creating an illusion his mind could barely process.
Any other disciple would have broken right there. A single crack in the heart, and it was over.
Zane's heart didn't crack. 
[SFX: WIND ROAR]
As the sword surged forward, the very sky seemed to turn upside down. The air vibrated and twisted, and even the sun appeared to waver in the heavens.
Finn’s Four-in-One sword radiated terrifying power, but it was a purely physical assault—nothing like the soul attack Jacob had used.
Fueled by the power of the stars, Zane’s own sword hummed with a fierce, terrible energy in response.
Corrupt Vitality climbed his sword arm. Above it, two specks of starlight ignited — drawn from his work with the Tapestry of Sun, Moon, and Stars. When they collided with each other, the annihilation energy didn't add — it multiplied. Tenfold. The Streamer blazed. 
[SFX: STARLIGHT COLLISION]
He only borrowed a sliver of Primal Vitality, Zane scoffed internally. That aura is nothing. Let the heavens themselves come crashing down; he would shatter them all. His resolve hardened into steel.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
Finn’s Four-in-One sword crashed against Zane’s blade. In that instant, their gazes locked, each recognizing in the other an unyielding determination to win, no matter what.
[SFX: POWERFUL SWORD CLASH]
[SFX: EXPLOSION]
A deafening clang filled the arena as a ball of blazing light erupted from their weapons’ point of contact. The light intensified to a blinding glare, while a scorching wave of heat swept across the spectators, temporarily robbing them of sight.
When visibility finally returned moments later, the audience saw an astonishing tableau. Zane remained perfectly positioned, still as a mountain, his expression betraying neither strain nor concern. Finn, the celebrated prodigy, had staggered backward five steps, his composure shattered. A trickle of blood leaked from the corner of his mouth, his eyes wide with disbelief at this unprecedented defeat.
"Holy crap!" exclaimed a student, his voice breaking with excitement. "Zane’s single strike broke right through that monstrous attack!"
Around him, fellow disciples exchanged stunned glances, their previous certainties about combat prowess and peak hierarchies crumbling before their eyes.
