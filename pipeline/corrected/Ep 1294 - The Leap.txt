Ep 1294 - The Leap

Word count: 1057

[MUSIC: INTENSE ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: LAVA FLOWS AND RUMBLING]
They were so close. 
Just as the far shore was within sight, the Lava Giant they’d knocked down scrambled back to its feet and resumed the chase.
The creature was massive, but it was far from slow. 
[SFX: HEAVY GIANT FOOTSTEPS]
The heavy *thump, thump, thump* of its footsteps hammered in their ears, each impact sending a fresh wave of panic through them. If that thing caught up, it would send the giant they were riding plunging into the molten rock below.
[SFX: SWORD RAISED]
Coleman raised the Blade of Hallowed Censure, squinting as he tried to gauge the distance. He lowered the sword with a grim, tight smile.
“Six thousand feet to the shore,” he announced. “Close, but still way too far to jump.”
High-level warriors could normally fly, but that ability was forbidden here in the hall—a restriction that was making their lives a thousand times harder right now. Leaping, however, was still on the table. A Soul Sea Realm warrior could clear over two thousand feet in a single bound, and the height of the giant’s head gave them an extra advantage.
But six thousand feet? That was a death sentence. They had one shot, and a fall meant instant elimination.
“Not even you, Coleman?” Ansel asked, his voice tight. Of all of them, the monk had the surest footing and the longest leap. If he couldn’t make it, nobody could.
Coleman stared at the distant shore, then shook his head.
“No way. I can’t make it.”
[SFX: HEAVY STONE DROPPING]
His words landed like a stone, and a wave of despair washed over the group. To get this close only to fail? They refused to accept it.
“Can all of you jump three thousand feet?” Zane suddenly asked.
Coleman’s eyes lit up with understanding. “That’s about our limit, yeah.”
“I can do that,” Hailey said.
Janet nodded. “If I push it, I think so. Why?”
[SFX: RUMBLING FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
Zane glanced over his shoulder at the rapidly closing Lava Giant. There was no time to explain. He just looked back at his team and said, 
“When I give the signal, jump to the left!”
As the words left his mouth, Zane was already moving. 
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
He walked to the edge of the giant’s head, crouched low, and began drawing on the power of his Dragon Scales, channeling the energy through his entire body.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE LAUNCH]
With a single, violent burst, Zane shot forward, leaving two deep footprints scorched into the Lava Giant’s head behind him. His body cut through the air like an arrow loosed from a bow.
“He’s going for it—all six thousand feet, alone!”
“Is he insane? That’s suicide!”
“Don’t forget what he’s capable of,” Janet said quietly, cutting through the murmurs.
She wasn’t wrong. Back in the second Hall of Causality, Zane had walked away with ten million Illusion Points on raw strength alone—more than enough to make a leap like this almost easy for him. But that didn’t stop the sting of watching him go. They’d crossed this entire lake together, and now, at the very end, only Zane was going to make it across?
[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]
In the blink of an eye, Zane crossed the full six thousand feet and landed cleanly on the far shore, breathing hard.
Behind him, the enemy Lava Giant was closing fast, its massive frame barreling toward the group with a speed that promised to end them in a single collision.
Zane raised one hand.
[SFX: BUBBLING LAVA]
Confusion rippled through the group at the strange gesture—until the surface of the lava lake began to churn violently, thick bubbles rising and bursting as something beneath it started to grow.
A peak broke the surface, small at first, then rapidly swelling in size.
“That’s—that’s the little mountain he was carrying earlier!” Janet said, her face lighting with sudden hope. “How is it getting so big?”
It was, in fact, Zane’s Sacred Mount Lodestone—thrown into the lava moments before his jump, now being willed back to its full size, a solid stepping-stone rising up out of the molten rock itself.
“Hurry and jump!” Zane shouted from the shore.
As Zane’s plan unfolded, Coleman was the first to move. He took two calm steps back from the edge of the giant’s head, then launched himself, soaring through the air with a practiced grace—gliding the three thousand feet in a single smooth arc, like a great bird spreading its wings—before dropping lightly onto the mountain’s slope.
Hailey jumped alongside him, using what little momentum a leap alone could offer. Soon, Janet was the only one left. Seeing her teammates land safely on the distant mountain, she finally put away the Lofty Wood she’d been carrying.
[SFX: ICE MELTING]
[SFX: WOOD CREAKING AND SHRINKING]
The Frosty Cage encasing it melted, and the Lofty Wood’s branches shrank until they disappeared completely, the entire artifact retracting into her palm until it was no bigger than her forearm.
Freed from its icy prison, the little flame kitten could finally move.
[SFX: KITTEN MEOW]
It looked terrified, but instead of running, it just stood there, shivering.
“Go on,” Janet said softly, looking down at it. “Jump into the lava. It’s not safe here anymore.”
The kitten just stared back, its wide eyes pleading.
With a frown, Janet reached for it. As a Law Creature born of fire, the kitten would normally scorch anyone who touched it. But after being trapped in the Frosty Cage for so long, its inner fire had dimmed. 
[SFX: PROTECTIVE ENERGY WRAPPING]
Janet quickly wrapped her hand in a protective layer of her own Vitality, and when she picked it up, it felt little more than scalding hot.
[SFX: KITTEN TOSSED INTO LAVA]
“Be free,” she whispered, tossing the kitten out and away from the giant. For them, the molten sea was death. For the kitten, it was home.
“Janet! Jump!”
“Hurry up, Janet!”
Her friends’ voices cut through the air. Janet nodded, gathering a stream of Vitality. 
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT SOARING]
[SFX: POWERFUL LEAP WITH ENERGY SURGE]
Then, with a final, desperate push, she launched herself into the air, soaring toward Sacred Mount Lodestone.
