Ep 475 - Getting Ready

[AMBIENT: AGITATED CROWD]

[MUSIC: TENSE NEGOTIATIONS]

Zane watched the spectacle unfold before him as the few remaining disciples scrambled over one another. Each tried to outbid the others, frantically purchasing a place in the finals alongside him—or more accurately, purchasing the right to avoid facing him in combat.

The Quarryheart Sect was also a third-grade order, but geography had blessed it with something far more valuable than prestige. Its location, nestled beside a mine brimming with supreme Aether crystals, granted it a massive advantage over rival factions of equal rank. Where other sects scraped together resources, Quarryheart disciples could afford to throw wealth at problems.

The two intimidating fighters representing Quarryheart stood ready to drop fifty supreme Aether crystals without hesitation. Their strategy was transparent: secure the slots immediately and strangle any bidding war before it could begin. Their expressions remained impassive, though Zane detected a flicker of calculation in their eyes—they were weighing whether he was worth such an extravagant price.

After Zane's devastating onslaught, only seven combatants remained standing, himself included. The magic array, which had teemed with ambitious disciples mere minutes ago, now felt cavernous and hollow.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

A hush descended over the nearly empty space, broken only by the shallow breathing of exhausted fighters. Then, movement—a figure rose deliberately from one of the small towers, his silhouette commanding attention. When he spoke, his voice sliced through the stillness like a blade through silk.

"Eighty supreme Aether crystals."

The deep voice belonged to Owen—the only other fighter in the pre-qualifying matches, besides Zane, who had reached the Soul Realm. His tone carried barely suppressed irritation, each word edged with frustration. He'd assumed his cultivation would guarantee him an easy spot in the main tournament, that his strength alone would pave his path forward. Never had he counted on someone like Zane appearing at the eleventh hour to shatter those comfortable assumptions

[SFX: WIND WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]

Owen figured his chance of beating Zane was thirty percent at best, and even that felt generous. When Zane had launched his attack, every instinct had screamed at Owen to retaliate. Whirlwinds of razor-sharp energy had already begun coalescing around his hands, hungry for release.

But he'd held back.

The odds were stacked against him. Zane was much stronger than his apparent third-grade cultivation—Owen had seen enough fighters in his years to recognize when someone was operating leagues beyond their visible level. But perhaps this confrontation presented a different kind of opportunity. Money could accomplish what fists couldn't. And if Owen had one resource in abundance, it was supreme Aether crystals.

[AMBIENT: CAVE ECHO]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

His wealth traced back to a lucky accident during training exercises in the Fleecy Downs. He'd lost his footing and stumbled into a cave. Inside lay a massive hoard—treasures accumulated by an extinct order, forgotten by time, waiting for someone bold enough to claim them.

That discovery had transformed Owen's circumstances entirely. Even as an independent fighter without sect backing, he now commanded resources that dwarfed what the two Quarryheart disciples could muster.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Their faces fell when they heard Owen's bid. Once the bidding started, things always got complicated.

"A hundred supreme Aether crystals," one of the Quarryheart fighters shot back through clenched teeth.

A disciple’s pay wasn't much. The order always took a hefty cut of any quest rewards, so a hundred supreme Aether crystals was about all they could scrape together.

"A hundred and fifty!" Owen countered, his voice firm despite his pounding heart.

The two Quarryheart disciples exchanged a look, then began a silent, tense conversation using their life force.

With the bidding war taking care of the other slots, Zane breathed a quiet sigh of relief. His own qualification was all but guaranteed.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY]

[MUSIC: CALM MEDITATION]

Time remained before the main tournament began, yet Zane refused to squander a single moment. Rather than returning to Cloud Hall, he headed into Oracle City's bustling markets to procure essential supplies.

Afterwards, he rented a modest room at a quiet inn on the city's outskirts and settled on the floor to meditate.

[AMBIENT: QUIET INN]

[MUSIC: DETERMINED ORCHESTRAL]

Every waking hour became devoted to either rigorous training or painstaking work on divine textures. Each brushstroke, each circulation of energy through his meridians—all of it focused on a single name: Marilyn.

They had never met face to face, yet her reputation preceded her like a storm cloud. She commanded power at the mid-level of the Inner-Core Realm, placing her far beyond any opponent Zane had faced thus far. The gap between them wasn't merely a difference in cultivation—it represented a chasm that had swallowed countless talented fighters who'd dared to bridge it.

She would be his toughest challenge yet. But he'd overcome impossible odds before. This would be no different.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

On top of that, she wielded the Violet-Core Physique—a gift so rare that entire generations passed without producing a single bearer. Maddox himself, the most powerful cultivator in the Heartlands, had taken her under his wing. Whether she was officially his disciple remained unclear, but one truth stood beyond question: she had absorbed vast knowledge from a master whose very name made lesser cultivators tremble.

Zane confronted this reality without flinching. Every fiber of his being understood what lay ahead—this battle would demand everything he possessed and more.

It took him a full day to complete two divine textures. His hands moved with surgical precision, following the Cobalt Dragon's exacting specifications down to the smallest detail. When at last he completed both patterns, he inscribed them onto his forearms, watching as they sank beneath his skin and faded to barely visible lines of dormant power.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY PULSE]

Yet he knew their true nature. Once activated, these markings would blaze across his flesh like living tattoos, pulsing with energy that could shift the tide of battle.

The moment he finished the textures, Zane plunged back into training with renewed ferocity. He pushed harder, driven by the image of Marilyn's overwhelming strength.

Each meditation session stretched longer. Each technique drill grew more intense. He refused rest, refused comfort, refused anything that might slow his ascent. The memory of Frank's shattered arm served as a reminder—power meant survival, and hesitation meant death.
[SFX: POWER SURGE]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT CRESCENDO]

Just two days later, his singular focus on getting stronger paid off: he broke through to level four of the Soul Realm.

[SFX: MYSTICAL ECHO]

Before he could even process the transformation, the Cobalt Dragon's voice resonated through his consciousness. "Zane, I'm ready to awaken my seventh brother."
