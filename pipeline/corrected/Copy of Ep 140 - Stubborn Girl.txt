Ep 140 - Stubborn Girl
Word count: 1247
[AMBIENT: ROCKY TERRAIN]
[MUSIC: TENSE CHASE]
[SFX: BOOTS ON ROCKS]
 The frantic slap of boots on jagged rock echoed through the air. The hairs on the back of Zane’s neck prickled—danger was closing in fast. He kept running, but it was useless. He knew exactly what was chasing him, and there was no shaking it.
Flight was futile. The moment for retreat had passed, replaced by the cold necessity of confrontation.
[SFX: LEAP AND AERIAL TURN]
 Zane propelled himself skyward with explosive force, his body gracefully rotating in mid-air as he pivoted to face his relentless adversary. The maneuver, born of desperation and calculated risk, allowed him to confirm what his instincts had already whispered—Aldrich loomed mere feet behind, his grotesque form bristling with protruding Bone Spikes that seemed to pulse with malevolent intent. These osseous weapons extended from the demon's corrupted flesh like macabre antennae, each one tracking Zane's movement with unnerving precision. The nearest spike, its tip gleaming with an unholy sheen, hovered just inches from Zane's face, close enough that he could feel the cold emanating from the calcified weapon.
A chill ran down Zane's spine as he locked eyes with Aldrich, whose face contorted into a triumphant snarl. The demon's confidence was palpable—he believed his prey was cornered. Perhaps he was right, Zane thought, his heart hammering against his ribs as time seemed to slow in this deadly aerial dance.
[SFX: DEAFENING BEAST ROAR]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
 Suddenly, a massive, slender Armored Wyvern materialized overhead, letting out a deafening roar as it dove from the sky. Aldrich twisted aside, dodging the initial attack, but the creature didn’t vanish. It was Zane’s now, under his command, and he directed it to fly parallel to him.
His own abilities were useless here; for now, the Wyvern was his only defense. It was a risky play. If Aldrich went all-in for the kill, he might still get through. Zane could end up dead even with the Wyvern attacking. But if Aldrich backed off, just for a second, Zane might actually get out of this alive.
But Aldrich wasn't about to back down. His target was within reach—he could end this in an instant. The only problem was the damned Armored Wyvern already diving for another pass.
[SFX: WING FLAPPING]
 “Heh, you think you can get away?” Aldrich sneered, flapping his large, scaly wings as he feinted to the side. He was baiting the Wyvern, waiting for it to pull away so he could strike Zane again.
Unfortunately for Aldrich, that wasn't the play.
Across from him, a sly grin touched Zane’s lips as he gave a slight flick of his finger. The Armored Wyvern dove, but instead of attacking Aldrich, it changed direction and flew right at Zane. 
[SFX: BEAST SWALLOWING]
 As it closed in, its massive jaws opened, and it swallowed him whole.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE SOUNDS]
 The Wyvern was formed from his own Corrupt Vitality, so being inside it didn't harm him at all. In fact, it was the perfect shield.
Aldrich clearly never saw a move like that coming. One second, he was dead set on the kill, and the next, Zane was completely hidden deep inside the Wyvern's body. There was nothing he could do.
[SFX: INHUMAN SCREECH]
 Aldrich let out an inhuman screech. 
[SFX: BONE SPIKES ERUPTING]
 Dozens of Bone Spikes erupted from his back, blasting into the Armored Wyvern. Their sharp tips tore through its flesh, but Aldrich had no idea where Zane was. He could only fire blindly.
Meanwhile, Zane lay perfectly still inside the Wyvern as the spikes thudded into the creature's hide just above his head. He looked calm, but his heart was pounding. All he could do was hope he got lucky.
And for once, luck was on his side. Not a single spike found him.
Outside, Aldrich had made his decision. He was seriously injured — staying meant death. He spread his wings to flee.
[SFX: BARRIER SNAP — RAPID SUCCESSION]
Three barriers appeared behind him before he could clear them. The first — blood-red — he smashed through. The second caught him. The first reformed. He was caged between them, thrashing.
"Zane." Yolande's voice came through calm and flat. "Send the Wyvern through. I'll open gaps in the barriers."
He had no particular reason to take orders from her. But a caged Dreadlord wasn't an opportunity that came twice.
His fingers moved. Three gaps opened in the barriers one by one, and the Wyvern drilled through each.
Aldrich watched the Wyvern coming and understood. There was no escape.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING — BUILDING]
"Humans," he snarled, "you will pay for this—"
He detonated his Demon Core.
[SFX: CATASTROPHIC EXPLOSION]
The blast tore through Yolande's barriers like paper. The shockwave hit Zane like a wall and sent him tumbling through open air — the barrier floor he'd been standing on was gone.
[SFX: FALLING BODY]
He couldn't fly. He was dropping fast.
Yolande's figure flashed. Six transparent barriers snapped into place around him mid-fall, slowing his descent until they touched down steadily on the ground.
Zane looked at the familiar walls surrounding him and didn't know whether to laugh or cry.
In the distance, the remaining two Dreadlords had seen enough. They turned and flew — black shapes shrinking against the sky. Below, the demon forces retreated in unison. The battle for Silverspire was over.
[AMBIENT: ECHOING SPACE]
 “Hey! I saved your life, you know!” Zane yelled, his voice echoing inside the enchanted box.
Yolande Shaw just blinked at him from the other side. “If you hadn't, you would've died,” she replied, her tone flat. “You didn't save my life. You saved your own.”
Zane was speechless. He couldn't really argue with that logic. “Okay, fine!” he shot back. “But you don't have to keep me locked in here!”
“I can let you go once I've handed you over to the military office,” Yolande replied coolly.
Hearing that, Zane actually felt a wave of relief. The military office wasn't so bad, all things considered. Yeah, he’d killed a military advisor from the Sterling family, but he’d also just helped the army take down a Netherlord. That had to count for something. He figured that even Hugh wouldn't dare give him any trouble after this.
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD AFTERMATH]
[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
 One by one, the Soul Realm warriors descended from the heavens, their silhouettes cutting through the smoke-filled sky. Lennie and Axel, faces etched with concern, made a beeline for Yolande the moment their feet touched the ground. Axel's sharp eyes narrowed dangerously when he spotted Zane still imprisoned in the shimmering enchanted box, his jaw clenching with barely contained anger.
[SFX: ANGRY FOOTSTEPS]
 "Yolande," he growled, his voice carrying the weight of battlefield authority, "release him immediately. What possible justification do you have for keeping him caged like this?"
The woman met his fierce gaze without flinching, her slender frame somehow radiating unyielding resolve. She brushed a strand of hair from her face and shook her head firmly.
"I'll free him after I've delivered him to the military office," she replied, her melodic voice belying the steel in her words. "Protocol exists for a reason, even in times of chaos."
Hearing this, the others—Lennie and Hugh included—sighed in frustration. That girl was unbelievably stubborn. Even after everything he'd done, she was still dead set on turning him in.
 Lennie's patience finally snapped. “Yolande, you can't take him to the military office!” he insisted.
Her steely gaze fixed on him. “Why not?” she asked simply.
“Because of what he just did!” Lennie shot back. “He's a hero! He saved Silverspire!”
Normally, the Soul Realm warriors wouldn't pick a fight with Yolande. They had defended Silverspire with her long enough to know how stubborn she could be and that it was a losing battle.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]
 But today was different. This time, Lennie wasn't backing down.
