Ethan gave Zane's sleeve a discreet tug. "Let's find another table," he whispered.
This guy was clearly looking for a fight, and Ethan figured it was best to just walk away. Zane, however, paid the disciple no mind, continuing to eat his meal in silence.

"I told you to get out of my seat. You deaf or something?" the young man shouted. He slammed his hand on the solid rock maple table to make his point. When he lifted it, a perfect handprint was branded into the wood. The guy's strength was insane.
But Zane remained completely unfazed by the show of force. He just kept chewing his food, acting like he didn't have a care in the world.
[SFX: SPITTING SOUND]
Seeing he still wasn't getting a reaction, the man leaned in and spat directly onto Zane's plate. "Ha! Enjoy your meal," he sneered.
[SFX: CHAIR SCRAPING FLOOR]
Before the sneer had fully formed on the man's face, Zane's chair scraped against the floor with a piercing screech.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
He sprang to his feet with lightning speed, his fingers clenching the man's collar in a vise-like grip. Drawing back his fist, knuckles whitening with tension, Zane prepared to deliver a punishing blow. His eyes narrowed dangerously, reflecting years of pent-up frustration and a lifetime of fighting for respect.
Just as his arm tensed to swing, Ethan lunged forward and seized his wrist. "Zane, man, calm down!" he pleaded, his voice tight with concern. "You can't fight here! You know the Skyreach Sect rules—any members who fight get a Point penalty. If you run out of Points, you're either kicked out or thrown in Infernal Peak!"
Ethan's desperate words penetrated the red haze clouding Zane's judgment. He blinked twice, awareness gradually returning to his features as his grip on the man's collar slackened. A muscle twitched in his jaw as he fought to master his temper.
Ever since joining Misty Peak, the Sect's rules had been hammered into his consciousness like nails into wood.
The rigid structure of penalties and rewards governed every aspect of their lives here. Zane knew Ethan spoke the truth—his precarious position afforded him no luxuries. With no prestigious lineage to shield him, he needed to follow the regulations meticulously. Both Rhys and Isla had warned him repeatedly about this vulnerability, their cautionary words now echoing in his mind.
[MUSIC: TENSE REVELATION]
The realization dawned on him like cold water. This confrontation wasn't random—it was calculated. The man had deliberately provoked him, hoping to trigger exactly this response. Zane's eyes flickered with newfound understanding as he recognized the trap laid before him. This disciple was merely a pawn, likely sent to goad him into violating Sect rules and facing expulsion.
Once he saw the trap for what it was, Zane swallowed his anger. He let go of the man's collar and held his gaze for a long moment. "I don't know who put you up to this, but you're not smart enough to pull it off. You really thought a cheap trick like this would work on me? But since you're so desperate for a fight, I'll give you one. How about a formal challenge?"
The young man roared with laughter. "Ha! That's what I was waiting to hear. Let's take this to the battle ring. Don't you dare chicken out."
Private fights between members were strictly forbidden in the Skyreach Sect, with severe penalties for anyone caught.
But the Sect was a training ground for Close Quarters Combat, and its millions of aggressive members were bound to have conflicts. The battle rings were the solution. Every peak had a few.
"Hey, guys, just calm down! There's no need for the battle ring!" Rhys said nervously, stepping forward, but it was too late.
Rhys grabbed Zane's arm before he could follow the man out. "Do you know who that is? Tristan is ranked thirtieth among every outer disciple on Misty Peak — and he's half a step from the Nature Level. There's an entire level between you."
Around them, the dining hall had gone quiet. Zane could feel the stares — other disciples watching with something that looked a lot like pity. In the battle ring, fists and feet had no eyes. If a fight went wrong, no one was held responsible. The crowd knew it. So did Rhys.
Zane was already thinking about the Furnace inside him. When it came to pure strength, he knew he was far stronger than any cultivator at the Marrow Refining Level. Taking on a challenge from a fighter already halfway to the Nature Level seemed insane, but Zane knew he had a shot. He might even be able to win.
"I'm not backing down," Zane said quietly. Rhys started to argue, but Zane cut him off. "This person came looking for me. I can't avoid that even if I wanted to. And if I retreat from this today, I'll find a reason to retreat tomorrow. That's not a path I'm willing to start down."

After Zane and Ethan Grant left the dining hall, Rhys Bolton was still on edge about the duel. Zane was only at the Core Strength Level and had barely been at Misty Peak for half a month.
What was he thinking, accepting a challenge in the battle ring from Tristan Hayes? Was he an idiot?

After wrestling with his conscience for hours, Rhys finally decided he had no choice but to report the entire incident to Master Isla Novak. The responsibility weighed heavily on his shoulders as he made his way to her quarters, his footsteps echoing in the empty corridor.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATION]
Only days earlier, Isla had drawn him aside during evening meditation, her voice dropping to a whisper as she instructed him to monitor Zane closely. "Report anything unusual about Lowell," she had urged, her eyes intense. "Particularly any remarkable progress or confrontations. I need to know everything."
To Rhys's astonishment, when he delivered the news of the impending duel, Isla's face remained impassive—not a flicker of concern crossed her features. She simply nodded, her fingers lightly tapping the wooden desk before her. "I shall attend the match in three days' time," she remarked with unsettling tranquility, as though discussing nothing more consequential than the weather.
Rhys opened his mouth, then closed it again, his throat tight with unspoken protest. He longed to demand why she wouldn't intervene, why she would allow a Core Strength novice to face certain humiliation—or worse—against a seasoned Marrow Refining fighter like Tristan. The words burned in his chest, but something in her cool gaze prevented him from voicing them.
[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]
But of course, Isla Novak had no intention of interrupting the duel. Her ultimate goal was to forge a champion for Misty Peak, a warrior who would one day become the most powerful fighter in the entire Skyreach Sect.
In truth, Zane was just one of several candidates. Misty Peak had a few other Core Apprentices with incredible potential, and in terms of raw power, they were all currently stronger than Zane.
Deep within her, Isla harbored an inexplicable certainty that Zane's power would soon explode beyond all expectations, leaving even her—a master cultivator—stunned by its magnitude.
Intervention? No. She had always maintained that only when facing annihilation would an apprentice's hidden talents truly emerge. The crucible of mortal danger forged the strongest warriors.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS ANTICIPATION]
In fact, had Tristan not initiated this confrontation, Isla would have orchestrated another challenge herself. She watched the preparations for the upcoming duel with calculating eyes, a slight smile playing at the corner of her mouth. What would emerge from this deadly test? A broken apprentice... or something the Skyreach Sect had never witnessed before?
