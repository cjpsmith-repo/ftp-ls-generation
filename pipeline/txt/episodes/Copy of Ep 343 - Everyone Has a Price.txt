Ep 343 - Everyone Has a Price

Word count: 1046
[AMBIENT: EXOTIC MARKETPLACE]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS]
Zane stared at the scene before him with barely concealed disgust at Ray’s obvious attempts at manipulation.
The prince smiled at Zane, gesturing toward the three beautiful women with an air of proprietary pride. "They’re Vigo women, from the Western Territory. Very powerful. Surely you’ve heard that a kiss from a Vigo woman contains incredibly potent Blood Energy. Should you choose to accept their affections, you could harness that power entirely for yourself."
The proposition hung in the air between them, laden with unspoken implications. Strong Blood Energy was an extraordinary advantage for any serious cultivator—it was basically a concentrated dose of supercharged life force. Someone with enough of it could bounce back from nearly any injury in a heartbeat. For a cultivator, this translated to wounds healing at an almost magical speed, flesh knitting together before one’s eyes.
The traditional path to building that kind of power was through decades of punishing, relentless training—a commitment every bit as tough as strengthening one’s soul. Alternative methods existed, of course; specialized pills could provide shortcuts to similar results, but these rare concoctions commanded prices so astronomical that even wealthy cultivators blanched at their cost.
Yet here stood three Vigo Women, offering that same priceless energy through something as simple as a kiss.
The opportunity to dramatically amp up his power so easily - and pleasurably - a temptation few men could resist. That kind of proposition was so enticing that their breathtaking physical beauty, capable of stopping travelers in their tracks, became almost an afterthought when compared to the power they harbored within.
[SFX: BOX OPENING]
Zane averted his gaze from the enticing women, his jaw tightening with resolve as he prepared to refuse the offer. Before he could speak, however, Ray sauntered toward an ornate brocade one of the other women held in her arms. With theatrical nonchalance, he casually flipped open the lid, revealing a sight that made Zane’s breath catch involuntarily.
[SFX: CRYSTAL PULSING]
Inside the velvet-lined container sat three light-red crystals, each pulsing with hypnotic rhythm. They emanated a dense, powerful Vitality that seemed to call to something primal within Zane’s core. Unlike the energies he had encountered before, this Vitality possessed a remarkable purity—refined enough to be absorbed directly into one’s cultivation reserves without the usual painstaking conversion process.
Zane’s fingers twitched at his side as he fought the instinctive urge to reach for such potent power, his cultivator’s senses recognizing their extraordinary value even as his mind remained wary of Ray’s intentions "Supreme Vitality crystals," Ray explained, "the currency of the Heartlands. They can restore a cultivator’s Vitality in an instant. Right now, a single one of these is worth a million crystal cubes back in the Eastern Territories." His smirk promised that this was just a taste, hinting at even greater treasures in the other boxes.
Crystal cubes were the standard currency in the Eastern Territories. You could use them to buy things like Skycruisers and Scrying Slates, but cultivators couldn’t absorb their energy directly. They had to build up their Vitality the long way, through training. Running low in a critical moment could be a death sentence.
These supreme Vitality crystals changed the entire game. They contained pure, unbonded Vitality that required no purification process whatsoever and could be immediately absorbed into one’s cultivation base. Such rare treasures would tempt even the most disciplined cultivator.
As for who would be the next emperor of the Empire—whether the Crown Prince ascended, or the Third Prince seized power, or even if the Seventh Prince somehow claimed the throne—Zane couldn’t have cared less. Political machinations of the royal family held no sway over his ambitions.
He met Ray’s gaze and shrugged his shoulders. "No thanks; I think I’ll pass."
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
At Zane’s frigid rejection, Ray’s confident smile froze into a rigid mask. The prince stood motionless for several heartbeats, his carefully crafted composure thoroughly shattered by such unprecedented defiance.
Never before had someone dismissed his generosity with such casual disdain.
[AMBIENT: MURMURING CROWD]
Seeing the shock on the prince's face, some of the guests couldn’t hold back their outrage and started yelling at Zane.
To them, Prince Ray was offering the world, and Zane was just spitting in his face. Each box held a priceless gift, with more still unopened—the kind of generosity even the Seven Great Houses rarely saw from the royal family.
"Zane, are you really that stubborn? Can’t you see His Highness’s generosity? The prince is trying to honor you, and you just keep shutting him down. Who the hell do you think you are?"
"So what if you’re talented? You can barely handle a low-level Soul Realm cultivator. Any high-level master from that realm could crush you without breaking a sweat."
"His Highness has masters far more powerful than you in his service.
Compared to them, you’re nothing."
"What are you trying to pull, kid? Playing hard to get? Let me give you some free advice: don’t get greedy. It never ends well for guys like you."
The guests kept piling on, one after another.
Ray raised his hand, silencing his entourage. The look on his face was a tangled mess of frustration and disbelief.
He finally got it—Zane’s mind was made up. Ray had completely misjudged the man before him. Throughout his life, Ray had operated on the principle that everyone had their price, that any loyalty could be purchased with the appropriate incentive. Now he felt like an idiot, suddenly aware of how little he understood what actually drove people.
Despite this rejection, Ray couldn’t help but admire Zane’s exceptional talent. His determination to win the young man’s favor hadn’t gone away. He wracked his brain, desperately trying to figure out what Zane did care about.
[MUSIC: REVELATION MOMENT]
Suddenly, his eyes lit up. A slow smile spread across his face.
[SFX: DRAMATIC REALIZATION]
He had been so consumed with offering Zane material wealth and beautiful women that he had completely forgotten the one thing Zane was looking for: Ray’s sister, Isla. The realization flooded him with renewed confidence.
Ray nodded to himself, convinced he had finally discovered the perfect leverage. Everyone had their price after all—Zane’s just happened to be more personal than most.
