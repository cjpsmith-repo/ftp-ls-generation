Ep 979 - Dark And Light Energies

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane could feel the girls’ worry radiating off them. He met their anxious gazes with a confident grin. “Trust me,” he said. “I’ll be fine.”
They were all worried, of course, but Zane had made up his mind. There was no stopping him now.
Besides, this was just part of their world. In the life of a martial artist, danger was always on the menu. Except for Cora, who’d grown up sheltered, all of them had faced their share of perilous fights. Isla had survived a few near-death encounters herself, and it wasn’t long ago that Zane had pulled Tia back from the very brink.
As Zane stepped onto the true path of the Elysian Keep, Tia’s voice was firm. “Zane is meant for great things. I know he’ll make it through.”
She hadn’t been with him on his journeys overseas, so she couldn’t possibly know the kinds of horrors he had faced. But she had absolute faith in his strength—and his luck.
[SFX: MYSTICAL WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL MIST]
The moment Zane’s foot touched the path, mist began to curl up from the chasms on either side of the ridge. Two different mists, two different colors. The one on his left was an inky black, the one on his right, a stark white.
“That’s… strange,” Zane muttered to himself. A bizarre sensation washed over him as he watched the mists swirl and writhe, feeling as if they held the potential to become anything in the universe.
[SFX: ECHOING VOICE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRONE]
Just as a sliver of doubt crept into his mind, Saul’s voice echoed in his head. “That is Dark and Light Energy, harnessed by the master of the Elysian Keep. Be careful.”
[MUSIC: ANCIENT MYSTICAL THEME]
[AMBIENT: COSMIC RESONANCE]
Ancient legends told of a time when Heaven and Earth were nothing but primordial chaos. Out of that infinity, a supreme force emerged, creating the two opposing energies. These forces then gave birth to the four images—east, south, west, and north—and from them, the Runic Cipher was born. The Cipher then multiplied into sixty-four runes, and from there, creation expanded, on and on, without end.
If the Dark and Light Energies were left to evolve, they could theoretically recreate the birth of the world itself. The mysteries they held were far beyond Zane’s comprehension.
But he didn’t need to understand them right now. All he had to do was move forward.
[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]
With that single thought, Zane’s focus narrowed, and he launched himself down the path like an arrow shot from a bow.
[SFX: ENERGY MORPHING]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY FIELD]
The Dark and Light Energies instantly morphed, twisting into the shapes of strange flowers and thorny vines that lashed out at him.
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
Zane’s expression was a mask of calm. A longsword materialized in his hand. He slashed to the left, unleashing a wave of razor-sharp sword intent, then spun the blade to his right, gripping it tight as the energy wrapped around him like a protective shield.
Flashes of steel danced through the air. In a heartbeat, the energy-forged flora were shredded to ribbons.
The mist reformed instantly — this time into insects. Hundreds of them, then thousands, buzzing and skittering across the ridge in waves. Zane’s sword cut through them without slowing. They grew larger as he ran, some reaching the size of his fist, their carapaces ringing against the blade. Then the insects gave way to reptiles — lizards and snakes pouring from both sides of the path. After them came the beasts: lions, tigers, leopards, all charging hard and dying faster. Zane didn’t break stride.
Then the primates came. Smaller than the beasts, but they threw rocks. They used fire. Something behind their eyes had changed.
As the last of them dissolved back into mist, Zane glanced over his shoulder. The women were gone. The black and white fog had swallowed everything behind him. There was nothing to see but the ridge stretching ahead and the churning mists on either side. He was completely alone.
A strange hollowness settled over him for a moment — then he pushed it down and kept moving.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL MIST]
Something clicked. The flowers and vines. Then insects — he’d barely registered them as threats, cutting through them almost without thinking. Then reptiles. Then beasts. And now these — something almost human. Each wave had been more complex than the last, each one a step further along some vast, unspoken progression.
The true path wasn’t random. It was arranged according to the evolutionary arc of living creatures — life emerging from nothing, growing in complexity, climbing toward intelligence. He was walking through the history of existence itself.
The realization sent a quiet chill through him that had nothing to do with Tia’s ice.
An obscure piece of lore surfaced in his mind about creatures of pure energy—they were known to be notoriously weak at reproducing, their populations always small despite their individual talents. The number of true masters among them was a fraction of what humanity could produce. Every race had its strengths and weaknesses.
But these perfect beings standing before him seemed to defy that. They looked like a flawless combination of the greatest strengths of many different races. So why had Zane never even heard of them?
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT DRUMS]
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
They took advantage of his momentary distraction. In a blur of motion, the energy-formed humanoids charged. They moved with an eerie understanding of combat, their strikes aimed directly at his vital points.
A sharp grunt escaped Zane’s lips as he snapped back to the present, driving a fist toward the lead creature. He held back the power of the Dragon Scales; he was still on the early stretch of the path and didn’t think he needed to go all out. He was confident that one clean punch would be more than enough.
[SFX: BONE CRACKING]
[AMBIENT: EERIE WHISPERS]
But just as his fist was about to connect, the creature in front of him suddenly folded in on itself, curling into a ball that absorbed most of the blow. Its bones contorted in an impossible, sickening way no human ever could. It collapsed, writhing on the ground in what looked like agony. Then, a moment later, its mangled form began to knit itself back together.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Zane froze, his eyes wide with disbelief.
These things had the aura of perfection, yet he could tell they’d never cultivated any martial arts. Their bodies were pure, but their power level was only that of an Adept.
The punch he’d thrown was devastating. It would have vaporized a warrior at the Inner-Core Realm. An Adept should have been obliterated without a trace.
And yet, this thing had not only tanked more than half the force by twisting its body, but it was already getting back on its feet without a scratch.
[AMBIENT: OMINOUS SILENCE]
How did it do that?
