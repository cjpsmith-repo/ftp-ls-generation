Ep 871 - A Useful Tool

Word count: 1237

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The drop of blood landed on the Black Star Pickax, not soaking in, but not rolling off either. It just sat there for a second before it started to creep across the metal like a tiny, crimson insect.

“Is this going to work?” Zane whispered, his gaze locked on the pickax, every nerve in his body pulled taut.

He watched the droplet slide slowly from the head of the pickax down the handle. With every inch it traveled, the hope in his chest shriveled. It wasn’t binding. It was just… moving.

[SFX: MAGICAL ABSORPTION]

Just as he was about to write the whole thing off as a failure, the blood drop shuddered and then burst, spreading across the surface in a flash. In the blink of an eye, it was gone, absorbed completely into the metal.

A moment later, he felt it—a new connection humming in his mind, a link to the Black Star Pickax that felt as deep and undeniable as a blood bond. 

He’d felt something similar before, when he’d forged his sword spirit, Lavender. But that was a connection between two souls. The pickax was different. It had no mind, only a simple, primal awareness that now recognized him as its master.

[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

A low hum started to vibrate in the air around him. The three divine textures etched into the pickax erupted in a blinding flash of light, blasting straight through Zane’s own spiritual energy and rocketing into the sky.

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR CROWD MURMURS]

“What the hell is going on in that pit?” one of the onlookers muttered from a safe distance.

“Who knows?” another shot back. “And who’s dumb enough to go check? He set this whole thing up to keep us away. Walking over there is just asking to get killed.”

“Yeah, no thanks. I go over there, he’ll put me in the ground for sure.”

The mangled bodies of the geniuses who’d already tried to steal the pickax from Zane served as a grim testament to the fate of anyone who got too close.

[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]

[AMBIENT: ROCKY PIT]

After the light finally faded, Zane climbed out of the pit, the Black Star Pickax slung over his shoulder, and a triumphant grin on his face. He sent a thought to the Cobalt Dragon in his mind. “So, does this thing have any special powers?”

The dragon’s voice echoed in his head, dripping with condescension. It’s a pickax. It digs. It can dig through anything.

Zane froze. That’s it?

Isn’t that enough? Stop thinking of it as a weapon. It’s a tool. A ridiculously powerful one.

Zane’s brief moment of triumph evaporated, the disappointment hitting him like a physical blow.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE UNDERSCORE]

Don’t underestimate it, the Cobalt Dragon added, its tone shifting from dismissive to something more measured. A divine-class weapon contains a unique law of causality. The cause is the pickax itself. The effect is that it digs through everything. No defense can stop it.

Zane turned this over in his mind for a moment. Then he rolled his eyes. So in the end… It’s still just digging.

The Cobalt Dragon didn’t answer immediately. When it spoke again, there was something almost like reluctant admiration in its voice. The craftsman who forged it had one goal: to create the most powerful pickax in the universe. He never intended it as a weapon. But think about what that means. Every defense — every barrier, every wall, every law — is useless against it. If it were a sword, warriors across the continent would be trembling. As a pickax… The dragon trailed off.

As a pickax, nobody knows what to do with it, Zane finished.

Exactly.

Zane let out a long breath and looked up at the pyramid. 

He hadn’t worked this hard for the pickax itself — he’d worked this hard for what the pickax could do. The forty-fifth floor. The fourteen egg-sized stones, each one carrying enough Raw Vitality to fully restore Tia. No leaf in this mine could stop a tool that dug through everything.

[SFX: RUSHING WIND — MULTIPLE SOURCES]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TRANSITION]

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR CROWD MURMURS]

He was about to start climbing when dozens of streaks of light tore across the horizon, moving at a speed that made the saint-level geniuses below go rigid. 

Flying above the Sacred Feather Sea wasn’t easy — the blood sacrifice power that saturated the air made it nearly impossible for anyone below the Soul Sea Realm to sustain flight. Which meant whoever was coming...

Zane looked up. Soul Sea Realm. All of them.

War King York. The Emperor of Harlen. War King Wind. Scott of the Star Field Divine Kingdom. More than twenty Soul Sea masters descending in formation, their combined pressure washing over the pyramid like a wave.

He understood immediately why they were here. Not for the ores — Soul Sea masters had no use for what the lower floors produced, and the leaves above the fortieth floor were beyond even their ability to break. They’d come for the Stone-Eater Bugs. Specifically, for the Soul Cores inside them — the only key to the Divine Emperor’s Secret Land, and the only path to the Dragon-blooded corpses within.

[SFX: HEAVY LANDING — MULTIPLE IMPACTS]

War King York was the first to touch down, his arrival sending a visible tremor through the stone underfoot. Several warriors immediately rushed toward him, carrying Fredrick between them. The crown prince’s eyes were closed, his aura thin and unsteady.

[AMBIENT: TENSE THRONE ROOM]

[MUSIC: DARK IMPERIAL THEME]

For a regular saint-level genius, a soul injury would be a crippling, potentially life-altering event. But for the crown prince of a divine kingdom, it was merely an inconvenience. The resources of the York Divine Kingdom were beyond what any ordinary sect or noble house could imagine. War King York wasn’t worried about his son’s recovery; he was furious that someone had dared to touch him in the first place.

Essentially, the entire continent was the backyard of the seven divine kingdoms. And the crown prince of the House of York had just been knocked unconscious in his own territory. Who on this continent would have the audacity to challenge the authority of the royal family?

Even as War King York considered the question, he knew the answer. No one would dare.

Except, perhaps, for Zane.

They’d fought once before, a brutal affair where the Emperor had only spared Zane’s life because the other emperors had pleaded on his behalf. He couldn’t imagine Zane would be stupid enough to cross him again. Doing so would be a death sentence.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

A saint-level genius from the York Divine Kingdom trembled under the War King’s suffocating rage. “Your Majesty,” he stammered, “it—it was Zane. His soul pressure did this to the crown prince!”

War King York strode to his son’s side and reached into Fredrick’s robes, pulling out a delicate amulet. The gem set in its center was completely shattered, its light extinguished. 

His jaw clenched, and his gaze shot toward Zane, who was now standing at the edge of the pyramid.

[SFX: THUNDEROUS ROAR]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

“Zane!” the Emperor roared, his voice shaking the very air. “Kneel and await your death!”

Zane simply blinked at the enraged emperor. “If I wanted to die,” he said calmly, “why would I kneel for you?”
