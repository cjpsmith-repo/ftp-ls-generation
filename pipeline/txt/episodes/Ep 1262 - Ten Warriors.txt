Ep 1262 - Ten Warriors

Word count: 1029

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN TRAINING GROUND]
Okay, Zane hadn’t exactly nailed it, but he was getting there.
During his fight with Sloane, she’d lashed out with a palm strike that unleashed a magnetic power thousands of times stronger than anything he could muster. 
Now, having just managed to suppress the Laws of the Five Elements in his own body, he was trying to forcefully extract that same magnetic power from the small mountain in front of him. 
[SFX: MAGNETIC ENERGY EXTRACTION]
After just three tries, he managed to pull out a single, shimmering strand of it.
If Sloane had seen that, she would have been floored. It was just too fast.
The head of her family, the Kincaid patriarch, had sent her to Sacred Mount Lodestone to train for three years. It took her until the end of her second year to draw out a tiny sliver of magnetic power. Zane had been at it for an hour.
The woman in white, watching him from the corner, knew exactly how insane that was. She was a core disciple from another powerful clan, second only to prodigies like Sloane. She’d grown up hearing about how intimidating those top-tier talents were, and she’d seen it firsthand. 
Among warriors her age, Sloane was like a phoenix, soaring above everyone else, constantly praised and doted on by the elders. The strength she displayed made her peers feel like children.
Of course, the woman in white had never been content with second place. The gap between them in the clan rankings was a constant source of irritation. She’d secretly trained twice as hard, pouring everything she had into closing that chasm, determined to surpass Sloane, come hell or high water. But she wasn’t delusional. She knew some people were just built different. 
She was starting to accept that even if she worked herself to the bone, the gap between her and Sloane might only get wider.
Sloane, a prodigy, had spent six years mastering this secret skill. It had taken Zane a few minutes to even begin to grasp it. What kind of monster was he?
The woman was dying to see just how much of the clan’s secret technique he could absorb in one sitting, but just then, Zane put Sacred Mount Lodestone away.
It wasn’t that he didn’t want to keep training. The problem was that fighting was forbidden in this room. Drawing out even a single stream of that magnetic power here would be a complete waste.
After storing the mountain, Zane didn’t stop. He immediately went back to manipulating the five types of Law Power in his palm with Chaos Energy, searching for the perfect equilibrium. Only when they were in perfect balance could he wield the magnetic power with any real control.
[SFX: GLOWING DOOR MATERIALIZING]
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
[AMBIENT: SEALED CHAMBER]
Suddenly, a thin line of light appeared on one of the walls. It started in a corner and shot out, stretching for about seven feet before taking a sharp right turn, outlining a perfect door. 
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ENTERING]
A low hum filled the air as the entire shape began to glow, and a moment later, a warrior in a round hat strode into the room.
The man scanned the room, his expression gloomy and arrogant, then let out a cold snort. “What’s there to see? Is there a flower growing on my face?”
The ogre warrior — already in the room — wasn’t about to let that slide. “Hehe, can’t I look?”
“Why don’t you look again?” the round-hat warrior shot back, staring him down.
“I’ll look again!”
“If you look again, I’ll—”
[SFX: METAL RINGING]
A short sword floated into a third warrior’s outstretched hand, cutting the argument short. Its blade was plain at a glance, but a faint golden light traced along it, etched with fragments of ancient script. “If you two keep this up, I’ll kill you both the second we’re out of this room.”
The round-hat warrior seemed to reconsider his tone. He pulled his hat down and retreated to a corner of the room without another word. The ogre warrior’s eyes stayed sharp with provocation, but he didn’t push it further.
[SFX: GLOWING DOOR MATERIALIZING]
Just as the man in the round hat found a spot, another glowing line sliced through a different wall, and a second door opened.
The seventh warrior had arrived.
She was a woman from the Demon Night clan. She entered without a sound, her eyes sweeping over everyone in the room before she found a spot with her back to the wall, standing ramrod straight.
[SFX: ARROW DRAWN FROM QUIVER]
She pulled a long arrow from her quiver and began tracing the gleaming arrowhead with her fingertips.
Plenty of warriors in this world had gotten their hands on the iron tickets needed to get here, and they were all keeping an eye on who else had them. Most had hesitated before using them, though. After all, it cost five million Illusion Points to enter the hall—a price that meant giving up something precious. But as more and more warriors took the plunge, the ones waiting outside finally made up their minds.
[AMBIENT: MULTIPLE WARRIORS GATHERING]
The room started to fill up fast.
[SFX: HEAVY DOOR OPENING]
Less than three minutes after the Demon Night woman arrived, another door swung open. The eighth warrior. An ogre.
[SFX: DOOR OPENING]
Seconds later, another door. The ninth.
[SFX: DOOR OPENING]
Then the tenth.
Zane had been waiting in this room for nearly half a day, and now, finally, ten people were gathered.
“Looks like we’re all here,” the woman in white said with a smile. She stood and turned to Zane. “Alright, we can head out.” She didn’t seem hostile in the slightest; in fact, she was almost polite.
He smiled back. “But there’s no door. Where are we supposed to go?”
“You don’t need to move,” she replied. “Just stand where you are.”
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
[SFX: WALLS SHATTERING]
[AMBIENT: VAST OUTDOOR SPACE]
The moment the words left her mouth, the walls around them didn’t open—they shattered, collapsing outward with a deafening bang and revealing the world outside.
