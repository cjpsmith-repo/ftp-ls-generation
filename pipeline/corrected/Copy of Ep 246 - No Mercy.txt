Ep 246 - No Mercy
Word count: 1159
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
While everyone's attention was locked on Zane and Mack's battle, Master Young and Master Robert, a respected master skilled in array design who had created the Array of Corporeal Brilliance used by Blackrock Peak's outer disciples, were chatting casually at the other end of the battle ring, looking completely relaxed.
"Mack had a real talent for arrays from the moment he joined Blackrock Peak. You remember setting up that secret attunement sanctum for our outer disciples, right? The Array of Corporeal Brilliance," Young said with a smile.
Robert nodded. "I remember. Back then, only a few peaks like Blackrock, Skytop, and Azure had their own sanctums for outer disciples. I designed the Array of Corporeal Brilliance to help your disciples temper their bodies."
Most of the Skyreach Sect's outer disciples hadn't reached the Adept Realm yet. A lot of them were still at the Stage of Marrow Tempering, the fifth level of the Foundational Realm. So, Master Robert had created the magic array to help them cleanse their bodies of impurities.
"One day, the array just stopped working. I had no idea what was wrong with it---maybe the heavy rain and lightning from the day before messed it up. I knew you were swamped, so I didn't want to bother you, but I couldn't find anyone who knew how to fix it. That's when Mack offered to take a look. I didn't think he could do it, being just a new outer disciple, and that array is so complex. But I let him give it a shot, and what do you know? He actually fixed it! It's been running perfectly ever since! Ha ha!" Master Young said, beaming.
"Oh, is that so? He fixed the array?" Robert rubbed his beard. "Well, then. I'll have to keep a closer eye on his next fight. If he can make it into the Top One Hundred, I'll consider taking him on as my personal disciple."
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]
The guy was far more ruthless than he'd expected. Mack realized his only chance was to find an opening to forfeit.
But just as he opened his mouth, another fist connected. Zane wasn't giving him a single opening.
[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCH IMPACT]
[SFX: BLOOD SPLATTER AND TEETH CRACKING]
Zane's punch landed square on his face. Blood sprayed from his nose and mouth, and a few of his teeth cracked and tumbled to the ground. Even though pills could heal these kinds of injuries and regrow his teeth, Mack knew a beating like this would take a long, long time to fully recover from.
[SFX: BRUTAL PUNCHES]
Zane gave him no chance to surrender. He carefully controlled the force behind each punch, just enough to keep Mack conscious while making sure he felt every last bit of pain.
The referee watched the whole thing with cold indifference. As far as he was concerned, he was just there to enforce the rules. Whether these disciples lived or died meant nothing to him.
Seeing this, the disciples of the Skyreach Sect began to whisper among themselves.
"Zane is being brutal! He won't even let the guy forfeit! He's taking it too far!"
"You didn't see the last match, did you? If you ask me, Mack is getting exactly what he deserves."
"What do you mean?"
"Earlier today, Mack beat Eliot, the top inner disciple from Misty Peak. He showed him zero mercy, just kept wailing on him until Eliot passed out. Never gave him a chance to quit, either. Now he's getting the same treatment from Zane, who's also from Misty Peak. Mack had it coming."
"Oh, I get it. Payback's a bitch! But Zane is unbelievably strong. He's only at the second level of the Adept Realm and he's already getting revenge for Eliot. It's insane! He's gotta be stronger than Jacob was all those years ago. Jacob was at the same level when he broke into the Top One Hundred three years ago. The way Zane fights, he's a shoo-in for the Top One Hundred. I bet he gets chosen as a personal disciple!"
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURING]
The crowd was buzzing, and everyone seemed to have an opinion on the fight.
[SFX: EXCITED SHOUT]
"Yes! That's it!" Master Warren leaped to his feet, his face flushed with excitement. You would've thought he was the one in the arena landing those punches.
The disciples from Misty Peak stood a little taller, swelling with pride. But no one was more thrilled than Eliot. He was still sitting cross-legged on the ground, but he'd completely forgotten about absorbing the pill. His fists were clenched so tight his nails dug into his palms, drawing blood.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL RESOLUTION]
"Zane... thank you," Eliot whispered. The humiliation from Mack's attack---the shame of it all---finally started to wash away.
[MUSIC: BACK INTO THE BATTLE]
"Still not giving up?" Zane sneered, landing another punch on Mack. "Fine. I'll be waiting. I'll wait and see just how Blackrock Peak plans on paying me back."
At the edge of the arena, Master Young's face had gone dark. He stepped forward. "Zane. Mack has lost. Stand down."
Zane didn't look up. "When Mack was doing this to Eliot, I don't recall hearing you say that."
Master Young's jaw tightened. "Let him go, and I'll make sure our disciples go easy on you in the next rounds. Push this further, and I promise you'll get back everything you're giving him — tenfold."
Zane punched Mack again. "Sounds good," he said. "I'll be waiting."
Standing just behind Master Young was a young man in a purple-crowned robe, a jade pendant at his waist. He had the bearing of someone used to being the most dangerous person in the room. "Should I go in?" he asked quietly.
Master Young shook his head. "Against the rules. Stay back."
The young man —the Sixth prince, and the Third Prince's younger brother — said nothing more. He watched Zane with calm, measuring eyes.
Zane wasn't fazed. He kept up his rhythm, hitting Mack again and again. Mack was too battered to fight back. Zane kept his blows just light enough to keep him conscious but never gave him an opening to forfeit. It was pure, sadistic torture.
Funny how Mack seemed to have forgotten he enjoyed dishing out this exact same kind of humiliation.
[SFX: POWERFUL KICK]
Finally, Zane launched Mack into the air with a kick.
[SFX: BODY SLAMMING INTO BARRIER]
He slammed into the shimmering barrier of light and was knocked unconscious.
[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD REACTION]
[MUSIC: VICTORY THEME]
The referee announced, "Zane from Misty Peak wins. Mack from Blackrock Peak loses."
Mack's defeat definitely turned some heads.
A few people knew Mack wasn't as weak as he'd looked in there and that his magic inferno array was incredibly powerful. What they hadn't counted on was Zane's sheer strength.
Most people didn't even understand how Zane had won. The fight had happened inside the magic inferno array, and the curtain of light it created had blocked their view.
Sir Kenneth was one of the exceptions. He glared at Zane, his brow furrowed. No one could tell what was going through his mind.
But he was already forming a plan to take care of this brash young upstart.
