Ep 1045 - Reach

Word count: 1124

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

Zane hung back at the entrance to the Hall of Arms for a bit, watching as two guys approached.

He recognized them from the stairs of the Mist Palace earlier. They each had a small crystal pendant, and after a quick flash of them to the two Spirit Supreme Realm guards, they were let inside.

As Zane watched them go in, it suddenly clicked.

He felt like an idiot. Of course the crystal pendant Yolande gave him was for something.

So, he strolled up to the guards, flashed his own pendant, and walked into the Hall of Arms right behind the other two.

[SFX: CROWD COMMOTION]

The warriors still outside erupted into a commotion, trying to squeeze in after him.

The two guards scowled, immediately blocking their path.

“Hey, we just want a look! We don’t have pendants, so it’s not like we can pick any weapons.”

“What’s the harm? C’mon, just let us in.”

Zane glanced back, dumbfounded. He was just picking out a weapon. What was there to even see? Shaking his head, he figured there was no shortage of looky-loos anywhere, not even in the Upper World.

To his surprise, their begging actually worked. One of the guards relented. “Alright, but don’t cause any trouble in there,” he warned. “Anyone who breaks the rules gets thrown out.”

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ECHOING]

Once they had the guards’ okay, the warriors happily streamed into the Hall of Arms behind Zane.

[AMBIENT: LARGE ECHOING HALL]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]

“Hey, hey, you’re the newcomer, right?” a warrior—eighth-level Life-and-Death Realm—reached out to clap Zane on the shoulder from behind.

But before his hand could land, Zane twisted just enough for the pat to miss. “What is it?” he asked coolly.

The warrior’s eyes widened in surprise for a second. “So,” he asked, “what rank of weapon are you hoping to snag?”

Zane replied simply, “The one that suits me best.”

“Ha! A good answer,” a white-eyebrowed elder called out from a corner of the hall. “All these young pups just go starry-eyed over the most powerful weapons. They have no clue what actually suits them!”

Zane gave the old man a small, appreciative smile. “If you’re ready to choose a weapon,” the elder said, “you can give me your pendant now.”

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL WONDER]

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL HUM]

After handing over his pendant, Zane took a proper look around the Hall of Arms.

[SFX: ETHEREAL SHIMMER]

A wide dome dominated the center of the hall. Beneath it, countless orbs of light drifted through the air.

As Zane studied the orbs, he quickly realized they each held a weapon of a different shape and size.

So many Divine-Class weapons…

No wonder Yolande had looked at him with that mix of amusement and disbelief when he asked if they had Divine-Class weapons. She must have thought he was completely underestimating the Mist Palace.

[MUSIC: AWE-INSPIRING REVELATION]

Every single weapon inside these orbs of light was a Divine-Class weapon.

The white-eyebrowed elder spoke up again. “The weapons at the bottom of the dome are Grade-Three Divine Weapons — the lowest grade. The higher you go, the better the weapon. But the higher you go, the harder it gets. What you can reach depends entirely on your own ability.”

Zane nodded. The rules were simple enough.

[AMBIENT: HALL INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]

Just then, the two disciples from the stairs reappeared on the far side of the hall — and they’d brought company. Walking beside them was an older warrior, already at the Soul Sea Realm. He wasn’t old exactly, but he carried himself with the settled authority of someone who’d long since stopped proving himself.

This was Ryder Golding — Matthew’s elder brother. He was a member of Groundstone Hall, ranked high enough that the next advancement batch to Cloudswallow Hall was a real possibility. He’d come personally to escort Matthew for his weapon pick.

The watching warriors shifted when they noticed him. A Soul Sea Realm warrior in the Mist Palace wasn’t someone to ignore.

[MUSIC: CONFRONTATION]

Ryder’s eyes landed on Zane. He walked over. “A Life-and-Death Realm newcomer. An ascender.” He looked Zane up and down. “Being this arrogant is rare.”

The warriors nearby murmured to each other. How had this newcomer already managed to offend someone like Ryder Golding?

Zane didn’t bother responding. He turned to the white-eyebrowed elder instead. “Is there only one chance to pick a weapon?”

The elder nodded. “One chance. That’s the rule.”

Ryder looked between Zane and his brother. “Matthew came first. He picks first.”

The elder glanced at Zane. Zane shrugged — he had no objection.

“It doesn’t matter who came first,” the elder said. “Both of you can enter at the same time.”

Ryder put a hand on Matthew’s shoulder. “Do your best,” he said. “Go for a Grade-One Divine Weapon.”

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

[MUSIC: ANTICIPATION STRINGS]

Matthew stepped to the center of the dome and launched himself into the air.

But by the time he reached about twenty feet, his momentum was already giving out. It was obvious he was struggling.

[AMBIENT: DISAPPOINTED CROWD WHISPERS]

The warriors below, their eyes glued to the pathetic display, started murmuring to each other.

“I doubt he’ll even make it to the Grade-One weapons. He might not even reach a Grade-Two. Tsk, tsk. Pathetic.”

“What a waste. I’d trade my Grade-Two for his pendant and give it a shot. I bet I could snatch a way better one.”

Zane hadn’t understood the deal with the orbs at first, but now he got it. Matthew was also a ninth-grade Life-and-Death warrior, putting him on par with Fenton. Even so, it clearly wasn’t as simple as just grabbing a good weapon.

[SFX: HEAVY THUD]

Matthew’s face went beet red as he strained, struggling with everything he had until he finally snatched an orb of light just before plummeting back to the floor.

He’d only managed to snag a Grade-Three Divine-Class weapon—an axe. It was worth maybe twenty to thirty Sovereign Stones. Valuable or not, the humiliation was absolute.

[SFX: METAL CLANG]

“I don’t want this!” Matthew yelled, scrambling to his feet. He tossed the golden axe aside, steadied himself, and was about to launch himself toward the dome again.

[SFX: CRUSHING FORCE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS BASS]

But the white-eyebrowed elder just raised a hand and made a pinching motion in the air. An invisible force slammed Matthew down just as his feet left the floor. He hit the ground hard, a sharp jolt of pain shooting through him like he’d been struck by a hammer.

[AMBIENT: DEAD SILENCE]

“The first one’s on the house,” the elder stated coldly. “You want to try again, it’ll cost you three hundred Sovereign Stones. Upfront.”
