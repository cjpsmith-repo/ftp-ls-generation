[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS]

Standing at the arched gateway, Toby laughed as he watched the scene unfold. “That’s incredible! The kid’s got some serious guts. He’s a hell of a recruit, Kenneth. Looks like all that time you spent training him is paying off!”

A wave of relief washed over Sir Kenneth as he watched Zane’s performance. Zane wasn't just powerful; his mindset and composure were miles ahead of Evan's.

To be fair, the arrogance of the Cloud  Hall disciples was a direct result of the organization’s power and influence. That’s what gave them the nerve to bully disciples from the Tier-Two Sects. You couldn’t really blame them; anyone who made it into Cloud Hall would probably act the same. But Zane’s will was just built differently.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED STRINGS]

Zane understood exactly who his opponent was: a Cloud Hall disciple, with all the prestige that entailed. But he also had unshakable confidence in his own abilities. His strength would speak for itself, loud enough to catch Cloud Hall's attention on its own merits.

He figured, Sure, the guy's already a Cloud Hall disciple, but I'm about to be one too—and a much better one at that.

In his mind, they were equals. He'd fought too hard, sacrificed too much, and overcome too many obstacles to let anyone intimidate him with mere affiliation.

[MUSIC: AWKWARD TENSION]

When Sir Kenneth heard Toby’s comment about how much work he must have put into Zane, he couldn't help but feel his face flush.

As the leader of the Skyreach Sect, Kenneth had poured all his attention into Finn, Patrick, Jacob, and Tracy. He’d never even heard Zane's name before, much less known that he was Elara's brother.

Zane had just come out of nowhere. If he hadn't absolutely dominated the All Peaks Competition and taken first place, Kenneth would have never given the kid a second thought.

[MUSIC: IRONIC REVELATION]

The irony was that Kenneth hadn’t put a single ounce of effort into training Zane. He hadn't offered him so much as a word of guidance.

So, hearing Toby’s praise, Kenneth could only force a smile and try to laugh it off.

Still, Kenneth was feeling pretty good about the Cloud Road Trial. Zane was already earning praise from Toby, and on top of that, Finn and Patrick were making a name for themselves. Through the Scrying Slate, Kenneth saw that both of them had already cleared the second phase of the Cloud Road.

Meanwhile, the leaders of the other four Sects could only watch with envy. Eddy was silent. Finn, who was only ranked third in the Skyreach Sect, had completely wiped the floor with every single one of Eddy’s disciples from the Crimson Sect. And then there were Patrick and Zane, whose power was just off the charts. Eddy had already given up any hope of his disciples beating Kenneth’s.

"No matter how powerful Zane is, if he runs into Frank and Lennon from Cloud Hall, I’m just afraid he’s in for a rough time," Toby said, shaking his head with a small smile. "I just hope Zane doesn't push their buttons too hard when he finally meets them."

Lady Sigmund's expression shifted at the mention of Frank. "Elder Dillon, you're speaking of Emanuel Drew's son, I take it?"

Toby nodded. "Emanuel wanted to attend in person, but was called away on other matters."

A rare smile crossed Lady Sigmund's face. "Frank is a real dragon among men. Don't compare him to a nobody like Zane."

Kenneth's jaw tightened. "Lady Sigmund. My disciple just earned praise from Elder Dillon himself. I'd appreciate it if you kept your opinions to yourself."

"Hmph. The boy is too sharp for his own good. Mark my words — he won't go far." She crossed her arms. "Frank and Zane will meet on this road. Just wait and see."

The other sect leaders hid their smiles. Nobody was going to stop those two from going at it.

[AMBIENT: DENSE FOREST]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane pushed out with his heightened senses, scanning the area around him. 

His awareness swept through the forest like an invisible net, probing every shadow, every gap between the ancient trees. Still, he saw nothing but gnarled trunks and tangled undergrowth.

He released a slow breath and started walking again.

[SFX: RUSTLING LEAVES]

After just a few more steps, the rustling sound returned—closer this time, more deliberate.

Zane frowned, freezing in place. What is that? He scanned his surroundings again, turning in a slow circle, but the forest appeared empty. No movement. No threat. Nothing.

Yet despite the evidence of his eyes, a primal instinct in his gut screamed that he wasn't alone. Something was tracking him, watching him. Whatever stalked him through these woods was an expert at staying hidden—so good that even his heightened senses couldn’t get a lock on it.

A bead of sweat traced down his temple. His muscles coiled with tension.

He took another cautious step forward. The rustling resumed immediately, matching his rhythm like a sinister echo. Zane's patience snapped. He whipped around with explosive speed, his eyes blazing with determination.

This time, he saw them.

[SFX: CREEPY CRAWLING]

[MUSIC: HORROR TENSION]

Spiders swarmed across the trees—each one the size of a man's fist. Zane's breath caught in his throat. Their sheer number made his skin crawl, but their size wasn't what disturbed him most. On each one's back, a pattern emerged that looked like a human face twisted in eternal agony.

His jaw dropped in horror. These weren't ordinary forest creatures—they were predators, and he had stumbled directly into their territory.

[SFX: SUDDEN REALIZATION GASP]

"Ghost-Faced Spiders? And they're huge!"

Ghost-Faced Spiders were rare, but the creatures were infamous throughout the Eastern Territories. They had a ghastly face on their back and were extremely poisonous. The spiders were valuable ingredients for medicine, pills, and potent poisons.

But the Ghost-Faced Spiders people usually ran into were tiny. Zane had never even heard of one as big as his fist. On top of that, these things seemed to be able to camouflage themselves. They could mask their presence so perfectly that when they hid among the leaves, Zane couldn't sense them at all.

[AMBIENT: EERIE FOREST]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Dozens of Ghost-Faced Spiders hung from the trees, staring down at him greedily, their legs twitching as if they were about to swarm him at any second.

[SFX: SWORD HUMMING WITH ENERGY]

Keeping his eyes locked on the spiders, Zane slowly backed away. "Get back!" The Apex of Sword Intent flowed from the Streamer he held, making the blade hum with energy.

[SFX: SPIDER HISSING RUSTLE]

With a unified, hissing rustle, the spiders lunged.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

[SFX: SPIDER SWARM ATTACK]

The instant Zane shifted his weight, they attacked. The Ghost-Faced Spiders swarmed, pouring from the treetops in a chittering wave designed to overwhelm him through sheer numbers alone. The deadly creatures descended like a living avalanche.

His pulse quickened, but his mind remained clear—two years of surviving assassination attempts had taught him that panic meant death. 
He would not retreat. Not from spiders, not from disciples, not from anyone.
