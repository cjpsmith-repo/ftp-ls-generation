Ep 1040 - You Were Shouting So Loud

Word count: 978

[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Zane had been dying of curiosity about this so-called “big shot.” With so little to go on, he couldn’t help but be wary.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL UNDERTONE]

It felt like someone was watching him from the shadows.

But how did they even know he existed? And that he was with the Soulbound Sect?

So, the moment he heard the elder’s announcement, Zane was on his feet.

[AMBIENT: ECHOING FOOTSTEPS IN HALL]

[SFX: DOOR OPENING]

He followed the elder back to a familiar hall.

[AMBIENT: FORMAL CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]

Inside, all the elders of the Soulbound Sect were already in their seats. But the person in the host’s seat wasn’t the Saint Lord. It was a woman.

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL WONDER]

[SFX: SOFT RUSTLING FEATHERS]

She wore a flowing, nine-colored skirt woven from the feathers of nine legendary beasts. Light blue tail feathers from an ice phoenix radiated a palpable chill, while dark red ones from a fire phoenix seemed to smolder. The plumes, the softest down from a Celestial Mouse’s back, shifted with her every move.

That skirt was priceless.

To think so many precious materials were wasted just for decoration… The skirt’s creator had to have been unbelievably extravagant.

[AMBIENT: TENSE HALL ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Her small, pale feet, clad in wooden clogs, swung playfully back and forth. But there was nothing playful about her gaze as it swept across the elders. When her eyes met theirs, their hearts seized.

Her power alone wasn’t what was so terrifying. She was on the cusp of becoming a World Lord, but so was Elder Roland; their strength was comparable. In this hall, even the Saint Lord’s power was equivalent to a World Lord’s. Of course, there could only be one true lord of a supreme world, and the lord of The Psychean Realm was the Saint Lord of the Psychean Sanctum, not the Soulbound Sect.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]

No, it wasn’t her strength that had them all holding their breath. It was her identity.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

She was one of the leaders of the Thirteen Palaces. More than that, her father was the Supreme Lord. She was a Supreme Lord descendant, and her position in The Alliance was untouchable.

[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION]

[AMBIENT: POWER SURGE]

Suddenly, several warriors materialized in the hall. The aura coming off one of them was several times stronger than that of the Soulbound Sect’s Saint Lord.

[MUSIC: REVERENT THEME]

The moment he entered, he turned to the woman and gave a slight bow. “Welcome to our Psychean Realm,” he said. “I am the Lord of the Psychean Realm.”

He had received word that a powerful ascender had appeared at the Soulbound Sect — chosen by the Thirteen Palaces. That alone hadn’t surprised him. Powerful ascenders surfaced occasionally. What had surprised him was that the Palace Lord had come personally. He wanted to see what kind of person was worth that.

This was the true lord of the supreme world, master of the strongest tenth-grade sacred place.

[SFX: CEREMONIAL SILENCE]

The World Lord took his seat. The hall settled into formal silence.

The woman glanced toward the entrance. “Is he not here yet?” she muttered.

Her voice was soft, but in that hall nothing was unheard. The Saint Lord shifted. “He should be here shortly, Palace Lord. Please allow just a moment longer.”

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ECHOING]

Then Zane walked in.

He moved through the hall without ceremony, eyes sweeping the room — and stopped when they landed on the nine-colored skirt. He knew that skirt. He knew the small feet swinging beneath it.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

A smile broke across his face. “So it’s you, Yolande.”

[SFX: SHOCKED GASPS]

The hall went very still. The elders stared. Even the World Lord’s expression shifted slightly. This ascender had just walked into one of the most powerful rooms in the Psychean Realm and addressed the Palace Lord of the Thirteen Palaces by her given name.

Yolande smiled faintly at Zane. “I told you I couldn’t decide where you’d end up,” she said. “And yet here you are — in my territory. I suppose I have no choice but to take you.” She paused. “Besides, I’m not afraid the Demi-Immortal Clan will come looking for trouble over you.”

[SFX: NERVOUS WHISPERS]

At that, the breath in the room changed. Several elders exchanged glances.

Zane couldn’t help it. He had met Yolande’s clones twice before, in the Lower World — had spent real time alongside them, talked with them, fought beside them. Over that time, without thinking about it, he had come to treat her as someone of his own generation. Knowing her true station now didn’t change what had already become habit.

The woman in front of him, though, was different from those clones. He noticed it immediately. The clones had always been strange — not cold, exactly, but incomplete. Missing something. Joy, anger, sorrow — they had felt hollow where those things should have been. The real Yolande sitting in front of him felt like a full person. Complete.

Zane finally smiled. “And how did you know where to find me?”

Yolande just rolled her eyes playfully. “You were shouting so loud,” she teased, “I’d have to be deaf not to hear you.”

When Zane had used his Roaring Token to look for Elara, Yolande had heard it all. Figuring out his identity was simple after that. She had a list of every person who had ascended to the three thousand supreme worlds in the past year, and it took her less than five minutes to find Zane’s name. There had been two, actually—it wasn’t unheard of for ascenders from the Lower World to share a name. But a quick check of their appearances and other details confirmed he was the one who had joined the Soulbound Sect.

The only problem was that she’d been busy gathering her clones at the time and couldn’t just drop everything to come find him. As soon as she was done, she’d hurried over from The Mist Palace.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

Across the hall, the faces of the Lord of the Psychean Realm and the Saint Lord of the Soulbound Sect had gone rigid.

“Shouting so loudly?” one of them muttered, his voice tight. “What is she talking about?”
