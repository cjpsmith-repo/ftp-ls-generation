Ep 705 - Suspicious Help
Word count: 1045
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
The Spirit-Tribulation warrior was barely ten feet away, but the instant his senses registered Zane's presence, it was already too late. A surge of power exploded from Zane, and in the space of a single breath, he closed the distance, materializing right in the man's face.
"You're—"
The warrior's mouth opened, the name "Zane" forming on his lips, but the world was moving too fast for him to process. Before he could even finish the question, Zane cut him off.
[SFX: BONE CRACK]
A sickening crack echoed as Zane's fist slammed into the man's chest. The warrior felt a brief, blinding flash of pain as his ribs shattered, and then nothing. He crumpled to the grass, dead before he hit the ground. The kill was brutally efficient — one clean, devastating blow.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
Cora stared, utterly speechless. She was a skilled fighter herself and had seen countless duels between the great families of Ethereal City. From her reserved seat in the stands, she'd earned a reputation for her sharp eye, correctly calling the winner eighty percent of the time. But this was different. Compared to the raw, lethal power Zane had just unleashed, those arena fights suddenly seemed like clumsy brawls between children.
She never would have believed that someone who had just broken into the Spirit-Tribulation Realm could so effortlessly destroy a warrior in its middle stage.
Zane, however, wasn't paying any attention to Cora's awe.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Bits of light flickered around him as Lavender materialized, her sword spirit form coalescing out of the air at his shoulder. She took one look at Cora and her expression hardened.
"I told you," she said. "This woman is a nuisance. If she hadn't screamed, none of this would have happened. Killing her is still the right call."
He dropped down and grabbed Cora's arm. "Let's go!"
This time, he didn't bother hiding his Vitality, letting it flare and mingle with his raw power.
[SFX: ENERGY WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]
The energy exploded from him, launching them across The Dreaming Marshes in a blur of light.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
As they drove deeper into the marsh, the landscape began to change. The emerald greens of the outer swamp gave way to dark brown — the plants, the water, the ground itself all taking on a lifeless, rotting cast. The air grew heavier with every mile. Whatever lived out here, it wasn't anything Zane wanted to meet.
He pushed the thought aside and focused on the pursuit. Multiple Vitality signatures were closing behind him — several weaker ones fanning out through the fog, and behind those, two that were in a different class entirely. One was unmistakably Maddox. The other was nearly as powerful, its signature unfamiliar but massive.
Zane's brow furrowed. One Life-and-Death Realm master was already a disaster. Two was something else. And the gap was closing — forty miles at first, then thirty, now barely ten. The weight of carrying Cora was bleeding his output, and his Vitality couldn't sustain this pace indefinitely.
Then Maddox's voice rolled through the fog behind him, unhurried and almost amused.
"Zane. I already told you — you can't escape. All you're doing is burning through your strength."
Zane said nothing and kept moving. But he knew Maddox wasn't wrong. If nothing changed, this ended badly.
Then a different voice cut through the fog directly into his mind.
[SFX: TELEPATHIC ECHO]
"To the right!"
[MUSIC: URGENT PERCUSSION]
Zane banked hard on instinct, then caught himself. He didn't recognize the voice. "Who are you?" he sent back.
A low, evasive laugh. No answer.
Zane's mind worked fast. The transmission had come from behind — from one of the two Life-and-Death masters on his tail. Someone back there was steering him. But why?
"Why are you helping me?" he pressed.
"I don't want that woman falling into Maddox's hands," the voice replied. "Same as you."
Zane's eyes narrowed. He turned it over. There was no tactical reason for either pursuer to tip him off — unless one of them stood to lose more from Maddox's breakthrough than from Zane's escape. That logic pointed in one direction.
Then it clicked. He'd heard this voice before. Two years ago, at the Wheel of Fortune. There had been a moment — a curse that could have been turned against Zane, and instead the Poison King had handed the dispel to someone else entirely. An unpredictable move that had cost him nothing and revealed everything about how he operated.
"If I follow your directions," Zane sent back, "I'm an easy target. You know that. So what's your real play — Poison King?"
A pause. Then Simon let out a quiet laugh. "Sharp. You're right — I don't need Maddox getting stronger. The simplest solution is still to kill the girl."
"And leave myself exposed to you," Zane said flatly. "Not a chance."
Silence. Then: "Fair enough. Ten miles ahead — underground palace. I can't promise you'll lose Maddox in there, but I can buy you time."
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Zane weighed the offer. Simon had no logical reason to tip him off unless he genuinely needed Maddox slowed—unless the Poison King stood to gain more from their mutual enemy's delay than from Zane's death. The Wheel of Fortune had already shown him one truth: Simon played his own game, beholden to no one, bound by no loyalty except his own survival. In a twisted way, that made him more predictable than an ally ever could be.
It would have to be enough.
Zane banked right, his body cutting through the air like a blade. He didn't look back. Behind him, he could feel Maddox's presence—a suffocating weight that pressed against his senses even at this distance. But ahead lay uncertainty, and uncertainty was better than certain death.
[MUSIC: DARK SINISTER THEME]
The underground palace Simon mentioned was a place only he knew existed—a vast subterranean labyrinth hidden beneath The Dreaming Marshes, where the earth itself seemed to breathe with malevolent intent. He'd explored it twice over the years, driven by greed and curiosity. No treasures waited in those depths. Just death, patient and hungry, waiting in the dark.
A cold smile touched Simon's lips as he watched from his vantage point, invisible to both hunter and prey. The palace will take both of their lives, he thought, satisfaction warming his chest. His best move had always been clear: lead Zane straight into that tomb and let the darkness do the rest.
