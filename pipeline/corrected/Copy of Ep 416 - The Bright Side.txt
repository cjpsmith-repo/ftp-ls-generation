Ep 416 - The Bright Side

[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]

[MUSIC: TENSE VICTORY]

If Zane had drawn his sword to defeat Johnny, the other disciples might have accepted the outcome with grudging respect.

Johnny was undeniably powerful—even stronger than Frank, who had just recently ascended to the Soul Realm. Since Zane had already taken down Frank's offense, most disciples had privately assumed that Johnny would likely fall as well. The mathematics of combat seemed straightforward enough.

But Zane had shattered those expectations in the most humiliating way possible: he'd beaten Johnny with nothing but his bare fists.

This wasn't just a victory. This was a statement—brutal, calculated, and utterly devastating.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

Johnny’s hand-to-hand combat was the stuff of legend in Elite House. For anyone who knew what he was capable of, hearing he’d lost a fistfight was flooring. Most martial artists relied on weapons to boost their attack power, with very few choosing to fight empty-handed. While many learned some basic techniques to cover their bases, Johnny had made his fists his primary weapon. He had trained so relentlessly that his confidence was absolute, and his reputation was built on it. No one in their right mind would ever underestimate the power of his fists.

And Zane was a swordsman. His true strength lay in the elegant precision of his blade. The fact that his fists alone could deliver such devastating power left everyone stunned and uncertain.

It was like watching a master archer arrive at a duel and defeat a champion swordsman at his own game—wielding a sword instead of a bow. A loss like that didn't just sting; it shattered an opponent's very foundation, leaving them questioning everything they'd built their reputation upon. And Zane, it seemed, was becoming dangerously good at delivering exactly that kind of psychological blow.

The disciples exchanged uneasy glances, their earlier confidence crumbling. If Zane could dominate with his secondary skills alone, what horrors awaited anyone foolish enough to face him when he actually drew his weapon? The question hung in the air, unspoken but palpable, seeding doubt in even the most arrogant hearts.

[AMBIENT: QUIET HALL]

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]

Leaning against a pillar in a corner, his sword resting on his back, Finn allowed himself a small smile. He knew exactly why the so-called elites of Cloud Hall were so stunned. Zane was a jack-of-all-trades with no obvious weaknesses. His raw strength, physical toughness, and swordsmanship were all top-tier, honed by brutally straightforward principles.

Having even one of those advantages could make a man a formidable opponent.

Dominic's eyebrows shot up in surprise.

[AMBIENT: TRAINING ARENA]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

As one of the most powerful fighters in the Soul Realm, his perspective went deeper than the others’. It was standard for a martial artist like Johnny to use Vitality to reinforce his body and enhance his techniques. But Zane… he hadn't suffused his whole body with Vitality. He’d channeled it exclusively into his fists, a technique seen only in dedicated body refiners.

Most warriors focused on body refining before reaching the Adept Realm. Afterward, they channeled energy through their core using Vitality-based cultivation, which was leagues more powerful. While a small few stuck to the path of pure physical refinement, it was a difficult road. Humans simply lacked the natural advantages of races like Demons or Ogres, who were born strong.

What truly puzzled Dominic was the absence of the distinct, powerful aura that body refiners always gave off—an energy similar to Vitality but impossible to shape through meditation. If Zane was a body refiner, it should have been obvious…
The silence broke fast. A dozen voices surged forward at once, each demanding to be next.
Zane scanned the room and pointed — twice more, both times landing on someone toward the lower end of the hall's talent pool. The crowd jeered both choices. Neither fight lasted long.
After the third bout, Zane rolled his shoulders. "Same time tomorrow." He turned to leave.
The hall erupted. Disciples surged to block the exit. Then Saint Morphen's strings pulled taut — Zane's body twisted through the crowd like smoke, threading gaps that shouldn't have existed, and launched into the air before anyone could grab him.

[SFX: POWERFUL LEAP]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

But as Zane turned and leaped from the arena, soaring into the air with impossible speed, Dominic’s train of thought shattered. A wave of shouts erupted from the stunned disciples below.

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMURS]

"What the hell!"

"I've never seen an Adept-level fighter fly like that, let alone that fast!"

"It doesn't matter! He'll be back. We need a plan to deal with him!"

[MUSIC: OMINOUS UNDERTONE]

Zane had declared he would challenge everyone in Elite House, picking his fights three at a time. But there was nothing to stop him from targeting the weaker disciples, grinding them down one by one while avoiding the truly dangerous opponents.

[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL MURMURS]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

After Zane departed, a junior disciple voiced what everyone was thinking: if the weaker members simply didn't appear for the next challenge, Zane would have no choice but to face someone stronger. The suggestion spread through the hall like wildfire. Disciples huddled in tight clusters, their voices urgent and overlapping as they debated strategies. All of them burned with the same desire: to knock Zane down and teach him the humility he so desperately lacked.

Zane knew he was making enemies of the entire Elite House, but he didn't have a choice. This was what Tia wanted. She'd orchestrated this nightmare, and he was just the puppet dancing on her strings.

Yet as he soared through the air toward his quarters, a strange clarity settled over him. Every fighter understood that real growth came from battle, and many died in the process. Combat separated the mediocre from the exceptional. Yet here he was, able to challenge anyone he pleased, test himself against genuine opponents, and then simply leave the ring at a critical moment—a luxury few cultivators could ever imagine. Most fighters risked their lives with every serious bout. Zane risked only his pride.

Tia had meant to torture him, but she'd inadvertently given him the perfect training ground.

[AMBIENT: QUIET CHAMBER]

[SFX: TREASURE BOX OPENING]

That evening, after his usual training, Zane opened the red and blue treasure boxes one by one. The Soul Essence from the red box would fortify his soul, while the Lifeblood from the blue one would amplify his vitality. Both were priceless resources. He knew the Elite House disciples were plotting against him, and his only option was to get stronger, fast.

[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL]

After consuming all the Soul Essences, Zane felt a noticeable surge; his soul felt a good thirty percent stronger. He closed his eyes, savoring the constant, comforting warmth that now radiated from his core. A moment later, he opened them, his thoughts directed inward.

"You promised to teach me how to make the rune," he projected to the Cobalt Dragon. "Is my soul strong enough now?"

[MUSIC: ANCIENT MYSTICAL]

A short pause followed before the Cobalt Dragon's lazy voice echoed from the depths of his mind, carrying with it an air of ancient amusement.

"Zane, you have no idea what you're talking about," the Dragon said, each word dripping with the kind of patient condescension reserved for a master instructing a particularly naive student.

Zane felt a flicker of irritation rise in his chest, though he kept his expression neutral. He waited, knowing from experience that the ancient being would explain when it suited him—and not a moment sooner.
