Ep 186 - Blinded by the Light
Word count: 1130
[AMBIENT: BATTLE CROWD]
[MUSIC: TENSE COMBAT]
Each drop of water flew through the air glinting with deadly promise. Bob, the peak Adept from House Pauling, had summoned thousands of the Chillwater Drops after Zane had astonishingly vaporized his first attack.
The crowd fell silent as onlookers held their breath, watching the impossible confrontation unfold. Despite being merely a first-level Adept facing someone at Consummation, Zane stood unflinching beneath the lethal barrage.
His Phoenix Crystal pulsed with energy as he prepared to demonstrate once more why cultivation levels alone could not predict the outcome of this battle.
Each drop of water radiated a shockingly potent, numbing chill.
Zane didn't move.
The droplets pierced his Vitality field like it wasn't there, closing in from every direction. The temperature around him plummeted. Frost crept across his eyelashes. Ice crystals formed in his hair.
He waited.
His feet stayed planted. His expression didn't change. He was waiting for the moment when enough of them had gathered — close enough, dense enough — that one detonation would take them all.
[SFX: FREEZING WIND]
The cold was almost unbearable. The droplets were less than a meter away.
Just by condensing the moisture in the air, Bob could form a barrage of lethal ice. A single touch meant at best, serious injury, at worst, death.
After his time in seclusion, Leo's own skills had improved dramatically. All that dedication had paid off, getting him to the fifth level of the Adept Realm.
And yet, Zane had just tossed him over a hundred feet like it was nothing.
Leo scrambled to his feet, ready to offer Zane a hand, but stopped. Zane was standing as solid as a mountain, and a strange sense of awe washed over Leo.
"Is he really that confident he can beat Bob?" he wondered.
Zane wasn’t a reckless person, not really. But to Leo, he'd seemed completely irrational when he lost his temper and picked fights at Dragon Valley and the army camp. It was like he didn’t even notice his opponents were leagues stronger than him.
Then again, the results always spoke for themselves---Zane always entered a fight with a confidence that seemed unearned, then emerged triumphant, proving his doubters wrong every time.
"But this is Bob," Leo thought, a wave of skepticism washing over him. "Does he really think he’s more powerful?"
Leo knew firsthand the massive gap between the first and final levels of the Adept Realm. Steward Carter, beside him, knew it too. And Bob, of all people, knew it best. It was common sense for anyone who’d trained their way up from the bottom.
Plus, most importantly, Bob was mean. He might have been from an honorable family, but there was nothing honorable about the way he conducted himself. There was nothing he wouldn’t do to crush Zane like a bug, and Zane knew that.
So where was all this confidence coming from?
[SFX: ENERGY EXPLOSION]
They could only watch, transfixed with astonishment, as the sparkling particles of Vitality ignited in a spectacular chain reaction.
Zane had already turned his head, squeezing his eyelids tightly shut as the overwhelming surge of energy erupted around him.
[SFX: BLINDING FLASH]
When he finally dared to look again, the explosion had completely vaporized the Chillwater Drops, leaving nothing but dissipating wisps of vapor.
[SFX: CRIES OF AGONY]
But unlike Zane, who had anticipated the flash, Bob was temporarily incapacitated, his vision swimming with painful afterimages from the blinding light. A grimace of frustration twisted his aristocratic features as he blinked desperately, trying to clear his sight.
[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
A cold smile played across Zane’s lips. He wasn’t about to let an opportunity like that slip away. With his heart pounding and adrenaline coursing through his veins, he prepared to strike.
[SFX: RUSHING ATTACK]
Before Bob's sight could return, Zane shot toward him like a predator closing in for the kill.
Though blinded, Bob was still on high alert, poised to counter. All Adepts had heightened senses, and Bob’s were exceptionally keen. Even with his eyes shut, he could feel Zane coming.
Seeing what Zane was capable of, Bob’s composure finally cracked.
He'd expected Zane to form a Vitality shield to block the attack---a suicidal move, since the impact of the Chillwater Drops would have obliterated him.
But to Bob’s shock, Zane had chosen a far more direct, more violent method.
He’d detonated his Vitality. Bob never imagined the resulting energy would be so terrifyingly powerful, enough to vaporize every last one of his Chillwater Drops.
Panic flared in his chest. He couldn’t comprehend how Zane’s Vitality was so strong. The sheer power contained in those tiny, sparkling motes made his heart hammer against his ribs.
But Bob was a master at the Consummation of the Adept Realm. His mind had been forged by countless battles on the long road from novice to his current level.
He knew staying calm wasn’t enough; he had to counterattack, and fast.
[SFX: SWORD SWING]
Sensing Zane's position, Bob swung his sword. He put so much force into the swing that the blade, as thin as a dragonfly’s wing, nearly bent to its breaking point. Tens of thousands of new Chillwater Drops shot out.
"This should at least be enough to slow him down," Bob thought.
[SFX: DRAMATIC IMPACT]
But he had gravely underestimated Zane’s speed.
Zane didn’t even try to dodge the droplets. It wasn’t that he couldn’t---he just didn’t have to.
The tables had turned, and he was going all-out to overwhelm Bob.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
The Phoenix Crystal in his right arm flashed. Energy surged through his veins, flooding his entire body.
[SFX: WHOOSHING SOUND]
In an instant, Zane became a blur. Like a living gale, he tore through the cloud of Chillwater Drops, scattering them harmlessly.
Just as their vision cleared, Leo, Carter, and Bob saw Zane dissolve into a blur. They were so shocked they thought their eyes were playing tricks on them.
Some people were just born with incredible strength; even back at the Stage of Marrow Refining, Zane could hold his own against Initiate Adepts.
But even natural strength had its limits.
How could a physical body reach such an insane level of power?
There was supposed to be a world of difference between a first-level Adept and one at the top. But Zane was forcing Bob to throw all that out the window.
No one had seen this coming. Bob himself could never have imagined admitting it, but in that moment, he knew: Zane was stronger.
[SFX: VITALITY ACTIVATION]
Snapping out of his shock, Bob instantly activated his Vitality.
He wasn’t about to just throw in the towel. Losing to some kid at the first level of the Adept Realm was utterly unacceptable.
[SFX: CRYSTALLIZING ICE]
Vitality flowed from his arm and up his sword, solidifying into crystalline white snowflakes along the blade.
The snowflakes then coalesced, forming a long, thin line that converged at the very tip of his sword.
Bob smiled. The kid had no idea what he had coming.
