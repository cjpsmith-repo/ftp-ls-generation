Ep 853 - The Soul Core

Word count: 1099

[AMBIENT: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane frowned at the sound of that voice. He knew it belonged to Fredrick, the crown prince of the York Divine Kingdom.

Honestly, the threat was laughable. Not a single one of the so-called saint-level geniuses here had the strength to challenge him, and that included Fredrick. Zane was operating on a whole other level, a bitter pill that most of them, especially the princes, had a hard time swallowing.

The fact that Fredrick was getting this worked up meant the Stone-Eater Bug had to be more than just some weird pest. There was a secret here.

A faint smile touched Zane’s lips as his gaze locked onto the prince. “I’ll be dead? How exactly is that going to happen, Fredrick? Care to explain?”

Fredrick had been staring at the Stone-Eater Bug, his breath catching in his throat when he realized Zane had actually caught it bare-handed. A green one, he thought, his anger flaring. After a few tense seconds, he forced a smile. “Those bugs are caught by Soul Sea Realm masters every year! Since when is it your turn to catch one? You don’t have the right!”

Zane glanced over at Randy, who gave a slight nod and leaned in to explain. “He’s not totally wrong. There aren’t many Stone-Eater Bugs—only thirty or forty show up each year. Even Life-and-Death Realm masters have a hell of a time catching them. Usually, only the Soul Sea Realm masters from the union of the seven kingdoms can pull it off.”

“Is this thing really that important?” Zane asked, his curiosity piqued.

To him, the bug was just… peculiar. It ate all kinds of ore, sure, but it was also absurdly agile. Its reaction time was off the charts. Zane had literally teleported to try and grab it, and the creature still managed to dodge him in the blink of an eye. Still, he couldn’t imagine why a Soul Sea Realm master would bother with them. The ore in their stomachs might be valuable, but not valuable enough to warrant that level of attention.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

“Yes,” Randy said with a knowing smile. “These bugs have a special ability. They can cut any leaf on the giant tree.”

“Okay? And?” Zane asked, more confused than ever.

Randy seemed to expect the question. “Only Life-and-Death Realm warriors can enter the thirtieth floor and above, mainly because they might run into these bugs. They’re a huge threat to anyone weaker, so Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors are forbidden. As for the fortieth floor and higher, that’s Soul Sea Realm territory, mostly because you might find purple Stone-Eater Bugs—the mature ones.”

He paused, lowering his voice. “But here’s the thing. The top three floors — forty-five through forty-seven — contain the most extraordinary ores in the entire mine. Leaves so hard that even a Soul Sea master can’t break them with a pickax. No one can dig them. No one except a Stone-Eater Bug.”

Zane’s eyes sharpened. He’d seen War Emperor York’s punch transfer enough force to flatten a mountain range. And these leaves could stop that?

Randy nodded, reading his expression. “Now you understand. The Soul Sea masters can’t get to what’s up there themselves. So every time the Secret Realm opens, they hunt the bugs instead — kill them and check their stomachs. If a bug happened to fly up to the top floors just before the Realm opened and swallowed a Soul Core before it could digest it, that’s their one chance. There’s a small chance of finding a Soul Core inside a purple bug, but the odds of finding one in a green one are almost zero.” 

The real trick was timing. A bug might have consumed a Soul Core from the top floors a year ago—long since digested. The ultimate prize? Catching a bug that had just swallowed a Soul Core before the Secret Land opened, then capturing it before digestion began. That master would claim the honor of delivering a Soul Core to their kingdom.

“In reality, though,” Randy admitted, “over the last few years, even the strongest warriors have slaughtered hundreds of these things and haven’t found a single Soul Core.”

Zane let that sit for a moment. Then he looked back at Fredrick. “So the Soul Sea masters hunt these bugs every year. But they haven’t forbidden anyone else from catching them — have they?”

Fredrick’s jaw tightened.

Randy shook his head. “No. There’s no rule against it.”

“Then—” Zane’s gaze settled on Fredrick, calm and cold — “what exactly gives you the right to tell me what I can and can’t touch?”

Fredrick held the stare for one second, two — then looked away. But his voice carried a sharp edge. “Fine. Catch all the green ones you like. But if you find a Soul Core in there — I’d think very carefully before you decide to keep it.”

Zane said nothing. He drew his sword.

[SFX: BLADE SINGING]

The bug’s scales were dense and tough. It took two clean strokes to get through them — the first broke the outer layer, the second opened the abdomen.

[SFX: ORES SPILLING]

The Stone-Eater Bug’s appetite had been extraordinary. Hundreds of ores tumbled out with its fluids — easily two to three hundred pieces, in every color and shape imaginable.

The crowd of saint-level geniuses stared in silence. The ores were valuable, many of them, but that wasn’t what anyone was looking for. Every eye was searching for one thing. The rest — the hundreds of ordinary ores tumbling out alongside them — nobody cared about at all.

 A look of disappointment crossed Randy’s face, but Zane just watched with a calm smile. “What’s wrong, Randy? No Soul Core?”

Randy shook his head. “Nope. No Soul Core.”

Zane couldn’t have cared less. For one, he still wasn’t entirely sure what a Soul Core even did, and for another, he was only there for Ancient Heartstones. And while this particular bug didn’t have a Soul Core, the huge pile of Ancient Heartstones it had just coughed up was more than enough to make him grin.

After a moment, he turned to Camilla, who was standing beside him. He put on a hopeful look and smiled. “Could you help me sell these?”

Her eyes went wide as she stared at the gory mess. She wrinkled her nose and pouted. “Ew, no way! That’s disgusting!”

Of course, Zane wasn’t actually going to make her do it. Chuckling, he knelt and started picking the Ancient Heartstones out of the bug’s guts himself, one by one.
