Ep 829 - You Can't Die

Word count: 1141

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The world's will hit Zane's soul like a tide — and the cracks had already begun.

Zane went still, a cold certainty washing over him. 

This was probably the most dangerous moment of his life.

Retreat? The thought was a useless flicker. He couldn't just let his soul be ripped from his body, leaving his physical form behind like an empty shell. And if he couldn't run, there was only one thing left to do.

He had to fight.

[SFX: PSYCHIC COLLISION]

Another silent, psychic collision rocked him. This time, Zane unleashed his Psyche Spike, a concentrated shard of his own will. But the moment it met the kaleidoscopic energy of the other being, it didn't just fail—it shattered. The will of the world was not something his soul, at its current level, could even begin to resist. 

The Little Monster's voice cackled inside his mind. “Despair. Tremble. You're still just way too weak.” Then, almost as an afterthought: “Beg for mercy.”

Zane said nothing.

“Go on. Beg. I won't spare you anyway — but at least it would suit the situation.” A pause. Then, colder: “That expression of yours is making me very unhappy.”

Zane's face didn't change.

The Little Monster decided to finish it.

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

But just as the killing blow gathered, a roar echoed from the depths of Zane’s consciousness. A deep, guttural sound that he recognized instantly. The Cobalt Dragon.

[SFX: DUAL DRAGON ROARS]

Another roar answered the first. The Red Dragon had woken up, too, its own cry splitting the psychic space. They were still trapped within the soulforge, but that didn't stop them from launching a simultaneous attack, throwing what little power they had against the overwhelming, colorful will.

A profound sadness washed over Zane as he watched them. Neither dragon had recovered much since they’d absorbed that Owl Beast. At full strength, maybe—just maybe—they could have stood a chance. But not like this. Not now.

[SFX: POWERFUL ENERGY BLAST]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Their two soul attacks slammed into the will of the world in a pair of soundless impacts. On the soulforge, the vibrant colors of the Cobalt and Red Dragons dimmed instantly, fading to a pale imitation of what they’d been. The will of the world, however, only flickered once before erupting in an even more brilliant, blinding light.

The Little Monster’s voice piped up again, full of mocking curiosity. Interesting! You’ve actually got a forge in your head. And what’s with these dragons? Aren’t they supposed to be legendary beasts? Why are they so pathetic?

[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACT]

Before he even finished speaking, the will of the world struck Zane again.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]

[SFX: CRACKING GLASS]

The impact sent him reeling. He could feel his very soul trembling, the cracks spiderwebbing across its surface growing deeper. It felt fragile, like it could break apart at any second.

Zane closed his eyes. One more hit and it was over.

Then something slammed into the world's will from the side.

[SFX: SOUL COLLISION]

[MUSIC: DESPERATE STRINGS]

It hit with a force equal to the world's will itself — and knocked it away.

Zane opened his eyes. Lavender's soul hung in the space between them, cracks already forming across its surface. She had taken the blow meant for him. Her soul had been nourished by the Holy Tree in the Sacred Feather Sea — still just a fragment, but grown stronger for it. Enough, barely, for one intervention.

Her expression was dim. But her voice was steady. “You can't die.”

“Lavender—” Zane started.

She had already turned to the soulforge. “Cobalt Dragon.” Her voice was flat, not a request. “Open it. Use the black flames.”

A long silence from the Cobalt Dragon. “Last time I refined his soul, they noticed,” it said finally. “If I use it again—”

Lavender's tone didn't waver. “The most important thing right now is survival. Trust me. He is the right candidate.”

The Cobalt Dragon and Lavender came from different worlds, but both had placed their remnant souls in Zane. Both had staked everything on him.

“You're right,” the Cobalt Dragon said quietly. “We've come this far. I should trust him.”

The soulforge began to turn.

Its surface split open. The black flames inside roared to life.

[SFX: SOULFORGE OPENING]

[SFX: BLACK FLAMES IGNITING]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS POWER SURGE]

The Little Monster saw it. And for the first time, its voice cracked with something it had never shown before.

“What — what is that?”

It had grown up in the forest among beasts. Its instincts were sharper than any human's. The moment it saw the black flames, every one of those instincts screamed at once. 

It didn't understand what it was looking at. It only knew, with absolute certainty, that those flames could burn everything — the heavens, the earth, everything.

Zane's soul was right there. One more collision and it was done. But the Little Monster pulled back.

The Cobalt Dragon's voice echoed in his mind, laced with a weary sigh. Oh, you want to escape? It's too late for that.

[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY BEAM]

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL CHOIR]

Just then, a beam of seven-colored light shot out from the soulforge. It swept gently across the battlefield of his mind and, in a soft flash, sucked the will of the world inside itself.

Lavender’s soul, though incredibly weak, managed a wide smile. Once the soulforge refines it, you can absorb it. If it works, you’ll inherit the will of the world. You’ll become the Son of the Plane.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]

[MUSIC: TENDER EMOTIONAL STRINGS]

As she spoke, Zane’s cracked soul drifted toward her. He wrapped his arms around her, holding her tight. In the quiet space of his mind, two broken souls embraced.

Lavender stiffened at the sudden contact, her fiery red eyes flashing with surprise. Then, she closed her eyes and leaned into him.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL HUM]

[SFX: MECHANICAL SPINNING]

The soulforge continued to spin slowly. It was, after all, processing the will of an entire world. Even for a relic of its power, this wasn't going to be easy.

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]

Down in the square, everyone was staring blankly at the sky. Zane and the Little Monster hung motionless, locked in a battle no one could see.

“Zane’s soul is already as strong as a Soul Sea warrior’s,” one person whispered. “But that Little Monster… It’s so confident. It actually tried to overpower him with its own soul.”

“But did you feel that aura it gave off?” another replied, their voice tight with fear. “That thing isn’t human. It felt like it was channeling the entire world of chaos. What kind of power even is that?”

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: DEAD SILENCE]

A hush fell over the crowd as they watched, waiting in terrified silence to see who would fall.
