Ep 375 - The Automaton

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL GATE]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Sir Kenneth glared at the Scrying Slate, his knuckles whitening as he fought the urge to grab it and smash it on the stone floor. He had put up with Lady Sigmund's sour attitude for far too long, and his patience had worn dangerously thin. The woman's bias had become insufferable, poisoning an otherwise exciting Cloud Road Trial.

Every time Frank or Lady Sigmund's prized disciple, Lois, appeared on the slate, her entire demeanor transformed. She became all smiles, lavishing them with praise so effusive it bordered on embarrassing. Her voice would soften, taking on an almost maternal warmth that she showed to no one else. The change was so dramatic that it felt like she became a different person altogether.

Lady Sigmund treated Frank like he was her own son, yet she acted like every other disciple braving the treacherous Cloud Road was completely worthless, beneath her notice except as targets for her scorn. In her eyes, Lois was the only one worthy of standing by Frank's side. The other disciples might as well have been training dummies for all the respect she afforded them.

As an elder of Cloud Hall who had served for decades, Toby was familiar with most of the disciples and their capabilities. He already knew about Frank and Lennon's impressive backgrounds and formidable abilities—their achievements came as no surprise to him. 

His attention was naturally drawn instead to the surprising outliers, those unexpected talents who were rewriting expectations with each passing moment. Disciples like Zane, Lewis, Finn, and Patrick were performing far beyond what their records had suggested, and Toby found himself leaning forward with genuine interest each time they appeared on the slate.

[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]

The funny thing was, every time Zane’s face appeared on the Scrying Slate, Lady Sigmund couldn’t resist saying something nasty.

If she’d only insulted him once or twice, it could have been ignored. She was a powerful woman, after all, with a notoriously short temper. But Lady Sigmund seemed to hold a deep, personal grudge against Zane, belittling him every single time he showed up. How could Kenneth just stand there and let her irrational behavior go on?

So when Kenneth finally spoke up, the other leaders were thrilled, silently cheering him on.

Even though Lady Sigmund’s sharpest comments were aimed at Zane, she was rude to the other disciples, too. It had clearly gotten on the other leaders’ nerves, but until now, they’d all bitten their tongues, just like Kenneth.

[SFX: CANE TAP]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

Lady Sigmund sneered, tapping her cane on the ground. “Hmph. What future does some kid from a backwater hole in the East have? The Eastern Territories? You call that a territory? It’s a complete joke! What are you all looking at? Yes, I’m talking down on the Eastern Territories. Zane has already made an enemy of Lennon, which is the same as picking a fight with the combined House Warrick and House Anderson. What’s he going to accomplish now? The boy is ignorant and stupid! I’d say Lewis is smarter than Zane. At least he had the sense to cover his face when he was robbing that treasure box.”

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Kenneth was a blunt, impulsive man who admired Zane’s guts for taking on Lennon so openly. He decided to challenge Lady Sigmund, consequences be damned. He let out a short, sharp laugh. "Lady Sigmund, don’t you forget that our Cloud Hall is a Fourth-Tier Power. You really think some member of their little alliance can push around one of Cloud Hall’s elite? You’re giving them far too much credit! You’re building them up while tearing us down! Lady Sigmund, where do your loyalties even lie?"

Sir Kenneth’s plan was simple: use Cloud Hall's authority to put Lady Sigmund in her place. If Zane passed the Cloud Road, he'd become an elite disciple of the Hall. Then he’d be protected from this kind of political nonsense.

[AMBIENT: WINDY PLATFORM]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TRIAL]

[SFX: GRINDING NOISE]
Meanwhile, Zane stood alone on his platform, his attention drawn to the dark opening in the cliff face where an ominous grinding sound echoed from within.

The noise grew louder, and a moment later, a strange figure emerged from the mouth of the cave.

"What is that... an automaton?" Zane mumbled to himself.

It was hard to tell what he was looking at. It was some kind of construct, built from pillars of metal. Zane couldn't think of anything to compare it to. Since it moved on its own and didn't seem to breathe like a living creature, he could only assume it was an automaton.

But this one was different. Its arms and legs were made of gleaming metal columns. In place of hands and feet, it had sharp blades that shone in the dim light, clearly forged from high-quality steel. Its head, shoulders, and other joints were also studded with blades.

This thing was built for one purpose: to kill.

The second the automaton stepped out of the cave, it charged straight for Zane.

[SFX: METAL SCRAPING STONE]

With every step, the blades on its feet gouged deep into the stone platform.

[SFX: MECHANICAL WHIRRING]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE MUSIC]

As it closed the distance, its thin, cylindrical torso suddenly began to spin. Its arms spun with it, rotating at a terrifying speed until they became a deadly windmill of sharpened steel aimed right for him.

"How do you even fight something like this?" Zane whispered, his mind racing.

Automatons didn't have the same weak points as living beings. He figured it had to have a power core somewhere. If he could just shatter that, he could shut it down.

The problem was that he had no time to look for it. He was standing on the very edge of the platform. If he couldn't block this first onslaught, he’d be knocked off the cliff, and his test would be over.

A flicker of an idea—a dangerous one—lit up his eyes. 

The automaton's waist was nothing more than a thin iron pillar. If he used the right move, Zane was certain he could slice right through it.

Zane dropped into a low crouch, coiling like a tiger ready to pounce. It was a stance called the Pouncing Tiger. 

[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH]

As the automaton lunged, Zane exploded off the ground, launching himself forward like a striking predator. 

At the same instant, he thrust his sword, the Streamer, out horizontally.

This has to work.
