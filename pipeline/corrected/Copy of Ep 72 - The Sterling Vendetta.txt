Ep 72 - The Sterling Vendetta

[MUSIC: TENSE DRAMATIC]
What Zane had pulled off was so far beyond Leo's understanding of the Marrow Refining Level that he couldn't even wrap his head around it. Chasing after the Razorwing Queen like a madman... not only surviving, but actually taking her down and coming back with her crystal core. A sour twist pulled at Leo's lips.
"You talk a big game," he said, a sudden laugh bursting out of him. He shook his head so hard his ponytail whipped back and forth. "An insane one, actually. And it sounds like total bullshit!"
"But," he said, his face turning dead serious in a heartbeat, "I like it. You've got guts, Zane. So, I'm in. I'll help you sort this mess out."
A wave of relief washed over Zane. Getting someone from one of the Seven Great Houses involved changed everything. No one would dare cross them—well, except for another one of the elite. With Leo backing him, this just got a whole lot easier.
"One thing, though, Zane," Leo said, his expression shifting. "What did you mean about that lunatic, Felix, trying to kill you multiple times?"
"It's a long story," Zane said with a sigh. "My beef with the Sterlings started back during the Gauntlet at the Skyreach Sect." After a beat, he laid out the whole ugly story for Leo: how he'd gotten Connor thrown out of the Gauntlet, and the beatdown he gave Felix's steward, Miles, at Misty Peak.
With Leo committing to help, Max scrapped any plans he had to bail. He knew he wouldn't be much help on his own, but everyone had their motives. "If Leo's willing to go to bat for Zane," Max thought, "it can't hurt to be on his good side."
[AMBIENT: MORNING ATMOSPHERE]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
Soon after, they called it a night. First thing the next morning, the three of them strode toward the center of Dragon's Keep, a sprawling complex that housed thousands of barracks for the various squads. Leo’s retainer, Jon—a wiry, anxious man who’d served the Brandt family since boyhood—had been waiting for them at the barracks gate. He fell in step beside Leo, and within seconds, started in on the same argument he’d been making all night. Leo cut him off before he could finish. But Jon pressed on anyway.
Jon cut him off. Even before the words were out, Leo knew what was coming.
"But—the Sterling family," Jon sputtered, as if he wasn't sure Leo truly grasped the situation.
"I know, Jon!" Leo snapped, his face hardening. "I already told you!"
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ECHOING]
Jon's head bobbed in a quick, nervous nod. "Alright then. If you insist, Young Master, I'll drop it. Follow me." He turned and led them deeper into the main barracks.
[AMBIENT: COURTYARD CHATTER]
[MUSIC: CONSPIRATORIAL]
Deep within the main grounds, a courtyard opened up. Four men were gathered around a blue stone table piled high with fruit, roasted meat, and jugs of wine. Drinking was technically forbidden in the barracks—an offense that came with disciplinary action—but these four were lounging and pouring freely, clearly not giving a damn about the rules.
"We should be toasting you, Randall!" Liam Shaw laughed.Randall was a Nature Level fighter from Dragon’s Keep—cold, calculating, and well-connected. He’d been put in charge of the operation to eliminate Zane. "None of this would've gone so smoothly without you!" He raised his glass and downed it in one go. Elias Pierce was the Sterling family’s enforcer inside Dragon’s Keep—the man who pulled the strings when the Sterlings needed dirty work done far from home.
In response, Randall tilted his head back, letting the wine pour into his crooked smile. "Don't mention it," he said with a shrug. "Elias Pierce gave me the job. It was a piece of cake."
"Easy?" Roman Stryker grumbled, snatching the wine jug and drinking straight from it. "We've been rotting in this godforsaken place for three days, with a few more to go!"
"Tell me about it," Graves agreed. "I should've just beaten the kid to death. Then we wouldn't be stuck here waiting."
"It's not that simple, you two," Randall said patiently. "A Skyreach Sect disciple dying under mysterious circumstances on their turf? Dragon's Keep wouldn't just let that slide."
"Zane's body hasn't been found, but the Razorwing Queen was on his tail. He has to be dead by now!"
Liam Shaw nodded in agreement. "Zane's tough, I'll give him that, but no one survives the Queen. We just have to wait a few more days, then we can report back to Elias Pierce that the job is done."
He raised his glass with a grin. "Cheers!"
[SFX: GLASSES CLINKING]
Completely oblivious to the figures approaching their little celebration, the four of them clinked their cups together, basking in their victory.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
As they neared the table, Jon called out, his voice laced with false cheer, "Well, hello Randall. Looks like you boys are having a good time." He stopped before the table, taking a theatrical sniff of the air. "Smells delicious!"
"What do you want, Jon?" Randall demanded, his smile vanishing.
Then Liam Shaw glanced up from his cup, and the color drained from his face. His jaw went slack.
"How..." he stammered, pointing a trembling finger. "Zane! You—you're not dead?"
Liam's cry snapped the others to attention. Randall, Graves, and Roman Stryker, none of whom had ever seen Zane's face, all whipped around to stare. The sight of him standing there, very much alive, left them just as stunned as Liam.
"We're not here to quarrel," Leo cut in, his voice level. He looked at Randall. "You have a man called Shane in custody. Let him go."
Randall shook his head slowly. "Can't do that. If I let him go, he'll cause more trouble. I'm not making problems for myself."
"Don't let him go," Leo said quietly, "and your problems get considerably larger."
Randall's expression turned mocking. "Larger? Do you know who I'm working for?"
Without a word, Randall, known for his ability to refine his intent, unleashed a wave of pure, chilling malice. It was an attack that could freeze a man's soul in his chest. But the only thing it froze on Zane was the scornful smile plastered across his face.
He didn't so much as flinch, letting the frigid pressure wash over him like a cool breeze. After withstanding the raw, vengeful intent of the beast in the Celestial Library, Randall's pathetic chill felt like little more than a parlor trick.
Randall's confidence was shattered, his face a mask of disbelief as he saw his attack do absolutely nothing.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
Zane didn't waste the opening. He let out a cold snort as a translucent gray spike erupted from his forehead.
"Psyche spike!"
[SFX: WHOOSHING IMPACT]
The translucent spike hurtled across the courtyard with lightning speed, piercing Randall's forehead before anyone could even register the movement.
[SFX: BODY FALLING]
[SFX: AGONIZED SCREAMS]
The others stood frozen, their minds still processing Zane's dismissive snort as Randall collapsed to the ground. His body convulsed violently, fingers desperately clawing at his skull while unholy screams of torment tore from his throat.
Zane gazed down at his writhing opponent, hands clasped calmly behind his back, his expression betraying neither mercy nor concern. "You wanted to teach me a lesson?" he taunted, voice dripping with cold disdain. "Instead, you've merely demonstrated your own incompetence."
