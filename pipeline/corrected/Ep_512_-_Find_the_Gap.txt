Zane had much bigger problems than the twenty-one giant swords descending on him.
Where the hell was he supposed to find a spatial anchor?
When he posed the question, both dragons hesitated before admitting their uncertainty.
"Her spatial anchor is definitely somewhere in the arena," the Cobalt Dragon said after a pause, his ancient voice carrying a note of frustration. "It's hidden—cleverly so. But if you keep jumping around with the Spatial Law, you're bound to stumble on it eventually."
Zane felt a flicker of annoyance. He'd hoped for more concrete guidance, but the dragons' knowledge had its limits. Still, their insight gave him something to work with—a strategy, however imprecise. He would have to trust his instincts and let the Spatial Law guide him through the chaos of battle.
[SFX: SPATIAL WARP]
"So that's it," Zane murmured as he sighed with grim determination.
His body flickered and dissolved into nothing. In a heartbeat, he reappeared several yards away, having stepped through the fabric of space itself. The giant swords that had been slashing toward his previous position met only empty air.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
[AMBIENT: ARENA DESTRUCTION]
The blades plunged into the ground with devastating force, splitting the arena floor like brittle parchment. Stone exploded upward in jagged chunks. The entire structure groaned like a dying ship caught in a storm. One end heaved violently into the sky while the other sank into the ravaged earth, creating a horrifying slope that sent debris cascading into the widening cracks.
Zane's heart hammered against his ribs as he watched the destruction unfold. Each sword strike wasn't just an attack—it was an attempt to reshape the battlefield itself, to eliminate any space where he might find refuge. Marilyn was forcing him to keep moving, to burn through his Vitality reserves with constant spatial jumps.
He couldn't afford to make a single mistake.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Zane weaved between the descending blades—up, down, left, right—the Vitality in his body burning as he pushed the Spatial Law to its limit.
More swords crashed down, then faded from existence. Zane’s form seemed to blur with each warp, leaving countless afterimages hanging like ghosts in the air.
[AMBIENT: CONFUSED CROWD MURMURS]
The sight left the warriors in the crowd dumbfounded.
"What the hell is he doing?"
"Sure, he's dodging, but for how long? More and more swords keep appearing. He's just delaying the inevitable."
"He's stalling for time. Pathetic. Looks like Marilyn's taking the whole tournament. The Dragon Soar Arena is as good as hers."
Even though none of them were qualified for the tournament, they had a good read on the fights. In their minds, the only real contenders for the top three spots were Brent, Marilyn, and Zane.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Marilyn wouldn't let Zane escape this arena alive—everyone could see that. That meant the final would inevitably pit Marilyn against Brent, and from what they'd witnessed, Brent stood no chance against her Violet Power Domain.
The spectators exchanged knowing glances, some shaking their heads with a mixture of awe and dread. Her Violet Power Domain defied comprehension—an absolute fortress of energy that rendered her untouchable. Once she activated that shimmering barrier, opponents found themselves fighting a ghost.
Inside that violet sphere, Marilyn reigned supreme, and no cultivator at their level possessed the raw power necessary to breach it.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]
[SFX: CRYSTAL CHIME]
A transparent crystal floated above Tia's palm. Inside, a walnut-sized eyeball swiveled frantically, tracking every detail of the fight.
Tia gripped the crystal, her gaze locked on Zane.
The artifact held the third eye of a young Angel of Death—retrieved from the Arctic Icecap four thousand years ago by Cloud Hall's thirteenth leader. The creature was the natural mortal enemy of the True Dragon.
The eye had been refined for three uses. In Cloud Hall's history, it had been called upon twice during existential crises. Both times, it had killed False Tribulation Realm experts outright.
Tia knew the cost of using it here would be severe. But she also knew what it could do—it couldn't touch Maddox, but it could find Marilyn hiding in her spatial fold.
Her fingers tightened around the crystal. She waited.
[SFX: MASSIVE SWORD IMPACTS]
Forty blades now hung suspended above the arena. No safe ground remained. All forty fell at once.
Zane flickered through the chaos, warping from point to point as swords cratered the stone around him.
Then, mid-jump, he felt it—a subtle resistance in space. A distortion.
Marilyn's hiding place.
He gave nothing away, continuing his desperate pattern while orienting himself. When he had the angle, he pulled a sword from his Storage Ring and drove it into the empty air.
[SFX: SWORD PIERCE]
Crimson bloomed in midair. Marilyn staggered out of the fold, one hand pressed to her chest, her composure shattered.
Zane kept his sword leveled. "Who said I couldn't break your Violet Power Domain?"
Marilyn straightened slowly, a thin smile crossing her lips despite the wound. "I genuinely didn't expect it. But don't mistake finding me for beating me."
She lowered her hand. The blood didn't spread—instead, droplets moved with insect-like precision, arranging themselves into an intricate rune across the stone.
Vitality flowed from Marilyn's body into the pattern. The rune pulsed.
A second lance of violet light shot into the spatial node behind her.
[SFX: OTHERWORLDLY LOW RESONANCE]
The Cobalt Dragon's voice struck with unprecedented urgency. "She is summoning the True Spirit of the Violet Domain. Even the weakest True Spirit will end you. Kill her. Right now."
Zane didn't hesitate.
[SFX: SWORD SLASH]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

Marilyn twisted through the air, dodging again, but not cleanly.
[SFX: BLADE CUTTING AIR]
The blade sheared off a lock of her long hair.
"Again!" The killing intent in Zane’s eyes burned hotter. He was on her in an instant, giving her no room to breathe.
Marilyn knew this was a critical moment. Her body flickered, trying to warp away.
But an attack infused with the Spatial Law was nearly impossible to dodge.
[SFX: SWORD PIERCE]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

"A barrier? Useless!"
