Ep 558 - Resist
Word count: 1042
[AMBIENT: RURAL FARM]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
Zane fought back his horror as he took it all in. On a human farm, it was normal to see cattle or sheep being raised for slaughter. They’d be fattened up, then cooked for a meal.
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
But here, in an Ogre's pasture, humans were the livestock. Raised like cattle and slaughtered once they came of age.
Vesper had once warned him that this continent was a chaotic place, one where humans weren't the dominant species. Only now did Zane truly understand just how powerless they were.
A sharp cough escaped him.
[SFX: COUGH]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
The two Ogre envoys had sharp hearing. They whipped their heads around, their attention fixed on the nearby tent.
"There's one missing," one of them said, a note of surprise in his voice. "Didn't I say to bring everyone?"
The middle-aged man hurried to explain, "Sir, he’s an outsider."
"An outsider? From another pasture?" one of the Ogres grunted, striding toward Zane's tent.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL CRESCENDO]
Zane lay helpless on the mattress, his eyes locked on the approaching Ogre. A cold, murderous intent began to harden his gaze.
"Hmm, this one looks badly hurt. Count him in," the Ogre said.
[SFX: TENT FABRIC TEARING]
He tore open the tent flap, pulled out a noose, and went to slip it over Zane's head.
[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC STRINGS]
Just as the noose left the Ogre's hand, a spike of pure energy shot from Zane's mind and slammed into the envoy's. It tore through the creature's soul, shattering its consciousness in an instant.
Everyone saw the Ogre’s arm swing forward with the noose, but then his body convulsed violently. He froze, stock-still.
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]
[SFX: BLOOD DRIPPING]
His back was to the crowd, so no one saw the thick, brown blood that began to trickle from his eyes. He was already dead.
A gentle breeze rustled through the camp.
[SFX: HEAVY BODY FALL]
[AMBIENT: WIND RUSTLING]
The Ogre toppled backward, his massive body hitting the ground with a sickening thud.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]
The second Ogre envoy stared, his face a mask of shock. "What happened?" he cried out, his voice edged with fear.
The captive humans exchanged bewildered glances, completely baffled as to how the envoy had just dropped dead without a sound.
[SFX: SPEAR DRAWN]
[MUSIC: THREATENING DRUMS]
The other Ogre drew a ten-foot spear and approached Zane cautiously. Realizing his companion was dead, he leveled the spearpoint at Zane's chest. "Human! What did you do?"
[MUSIC: DARK TRIUMPHANT THEME]
A faint smirk touched Zane's lips. "I killed him," he said calmly.
The Ogre's face was a mask of disbelief. "You… killed him?"
"Whether you believe me or not doesn't matter," Zane said. "You can die too."
His eyes flashed. A second pulse of soul energy left his body and found its mark.
The spear dropped. The second Ogre hit the ground.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
Zane looked at the two bodies lying in the dirt. Seven or eight hundred people stared back at him, trembling.
The leader — the same middle-aged man who had pulled him from the crater — finally found his voice. He walked forward on unsteady legs, looked at the two corpses, and turned to Zane with an expression that was more accusation than gratitude. "I saved your life," he said. "Why did you kill them?"
Zane's frown deepened. "They were going to eat you. They were going to eat me. They deserved to die."
"Deserved to die?" The man shook his head, his voice earnest and completely serious. "The Ogres gave us this pasture. We owe them in return. That's just how it is."
Zane stared at him.
[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]
[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]
Other voices rose from the crowd, nodding along.
For generations, these people had accepted their fate as Ogre food. Rebellions had been crushed long ago—the humans were too weak. Paralyzed by ancestral fear, they'd stopped thinking of themselves as people at all.
Zane closed his eyes. Livestock didn't have minds. Apparently, neither did these humans.
He refocused on clearing his meridians.
A new voice cut through the noise.
"Father. I think he's right."
Zane looked up.
A teenager stepped forward, gripping a sharpened wooden stick. "Why should we give them people? Just because this pasture belongs to them?"
"What do you know?" his father snapped. "Our ancestors have lived here for generations. We give a few people each year, and everyone else survives. That's the arrangement."
"It works for them," the boy shot back. "Didn't any of our ancestors think of fighting back?"
"Fight the Ogres? That's just dying faster."
"Either way we die. Why should we wait like animals?"
Something loosened in Zane's chest. Not everyone had given up.
"What's your name?"
The teenager turned. "Craig." He looked at Zane with reverence—the man who had just killed two Ogres without moving a finger."
"I'll teach you how to fight back," Zane said.
Craig's eyes lit up. "That technique you used — that was some kind of power, wasn't it?"
"It's not a technique. It's strength. There's a difference." Zane nodded toward the space beside him. "The Ogres will come back. Stay close and watch."
The rest of the tribe went back and forth, arguing in low voices, arriving nowhere. Eventually, they drifted away, leaving Zane alone. All except Craig, who planted himself beside Zane's mattress, wooden stick in hand, and didn't move.
All the while, Zane's concentration was turned inward, focused on purging the residual Spatial Laws from his body.
[SFX: GROUND RUMBLING]
[AMBIENT: THUNDERING HOOVES]
Hours later, a deep trembling in the ground caught Zane's attention. He looked up. A column of Ogres on massive coursers was thundering toward them. The beasts were giants, standing nearly twenty feet tall with bloodshot eyes—far from any ordinary steed.
[SFX: HEAVY ARMOR CLANKING]
"What's going on here? Where are the envoys?" The leader, a hulking brute in a heavy helmet and spiked shoulder armor, was an intimidating sight.
"Save your breath. I killed them," Zane said flatly.
The Ogre leader dismounted, his gaze dropping to the two corpses on the ground. He looked back at Zane, his ugly face twisting in disbelief. "You?"
Craig stood protectively over Zane, holding his sharpened stick at the ready.
"Yes," Zane said with a nod—the only part of his body he could move.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
The Ogre leader let out a gravelly chuckle. "Humans with the nerve to fight back? Now this is interesting. Seize him! We're taking him with us!" he ordered with a wave of his hand.
[SFX: MULTIPLE FOOTSTEPS]
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
At his command, the other Ogres dismounted and closed in, forming a tight circle around Zane.
