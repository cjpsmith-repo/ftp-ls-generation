[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUNDS MURMUR]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Xander, who held authority over the Ethereal Spirit Sect disciples present, snapped at Brent, putting him in his place.

"Brat?" Xander's voice cut through the tension. "Brent, that's rude. Zane's older than you—show some respect."

The Ethereal Spirit Sect prided itself on discipline and decorum. Every member, regardless of rank, learned proper etiquette from their first day of training.

Brent's face flushed crimson at the rebuke. He lowered his gaze, clearly embarrassed by his lapse in judgment. "You're right," he admitted quietly. "I'm sorry."

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Xander studied Zane with renewed interest, his analytical mind already working through the implications. "He's a body refiner," he observed, his tone shifting from reproach to assessment. "That means he can channel raw physical power through the Apex of Sword Intent in ways we can't replicate. His approach will be fundamentally different from ours."

He paused, letting the weight of his words settle over the group. "We'd be fools to underestimate him just because his methods seem unconventional."

One of the septuplets from the Ethereal Spirit Sect added, “Martial artists from the Heartlands mainly train their Vitality, so we've never seen a body refiner this strong. Cloud Hall isn't known for powerful body refining skills either. It means we have no idea what that kid is capable of.”

Xander's eyebrow shot up at the casual phrase 'that kid.' A frown creased his features, deepening the shadows across his face.

The septuplets might be core members, but even they weren't exempt from the Sect's fundamental rules. Disrespect toward fellow disciples, regardless of rank or origin, violated the very principles the Ethereal Spirit Sect stood upon.

The septuplet caught Xander's darkening expression immediately. His shoulders tensed, and he quickly bowed his head. "My apologies," he said, genuine remorse coloring his voice.

He straightened, his earlier dismissiveness replaced by something closer to wonder. "So that's how he passed the pre-qualifiers, even with his low cultivation level. He's a body refiner!" The puzzle pieces were finally clicking into place.

Xander nodded slowly, though his mind was already racing ahead. Body cultivators were rare enough in the Heartlands, but one capable of challenging Soul Realm masters? That defied everything he'd learned about their limitations.

[SFX: SURPRISED GASP]

"I've never heard of a body refiner possessing such strength!" The septuplet's voice carried a mixture of awe and disbelief. "I've encountered plenty over the years, and even the finest among them couldn't challenge an ordinary Soul Realm warrior. What makes this Zane so powerful?"

Another disciple leaned forward, his curiosity piqued. "How many Nexus Points has he refined? Dozens, perhaps?"

Refining Nexus Points formed the cornerstone of Body Refining—a truth every cultivator knew.

The practice had fallen out of favor in the Heartlands, where most considered it an inefficient way to gain power. This meant that no one had ever managed to refine all one hundred and eight points. Yet Zane had done exactly that, in a matter of days.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

"He refined his body to that extent in just a few days?" Tia's eyes widened, her composure cracking for the first time. Her fingers tightened around the railing before her.

She had secretly seen him refining his body only days earlier, watching from the shadows with clinical interest. She never imagined he could ascend to a level of strength she hadn't even known existed—and certainly not in such an impossibly short span.

The revelation sent her thoughts spiraling. What other secrets was he hiding?

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

Tia didn't know the whole truth: Zane's body had undergone special refinement using advanced techniques and Primordial Energy. Beyond that, the purest Law Power had honed his Nexus Points—an advantage even the mightiest forces of the Upper World couldn't access. These hidden elements formed the foundation of his explosive growth, a secret that would remain buried for now.

[SFX: CROWD ROAR]

[MUSIC: VICTORIOUS DRUMS]

"Zane, of the Cloud Hall, wins!"

The old man's voice echoed through the arena. A moment of stunned silence followed before the crowd erupted in disbelief. Members of the World Merchants' Guild rushed onto the stage, their faces grim as they assessed Alex's broken form. The young master lay motionless, his breathing shallow and labored, blood pooling beneath him in dark rivulets.

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

Zane stepped back from the carnage, his chest heaving. The adrenaline that had sustained him through the fight began to ebb, leaving behind a hollow exhaustion.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TONES]

From the stands, Alex's family glared daggers at Zane, looking like they were ready to jump down and strangle him themselves.

Elder Toby appeared and put an arm around Zane. "Great work, my boy! According to the rules, you may draw a card," Elder Toby announced.

A servant presented a wooden box. Zane withdrew a card marked "Exempt."

"This Exemption Card allows you to forfeit one match without penalty," Elder Toby explained.

Zane nodded, pocketing it. A Challenge Card would have let him face Tia directly, but confronting her now might alert Maddox.

[AMBIENT: ARENA MURMURS]

He turned toward the independent fighters' section.

Tia's voice cut across the arena. "Zane, you are a Cloud Hall disciple. You don't need to stand there."

Cloud Hall maintained its own platform. Standing elsewhere would suggest internal conflict, damaging the Order's reputation.

Zane had no beef with Cloud Hall itself, only with Tia, who had opposed his participation. He faced her. "I entered through the outer trials. I'll stand with them."

He walked away without waiting for her response.

Embarrassment flickered across Tia's features.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

In the stands, fighters from other Orders discussed the incident. Beyond cursing Zane's audacity, outsiders could say little about Cloud Hall's internal affairs.

Elder Toby grew increasingly puzzled. During Zane's Peripheral Test, Tia had attended personally, then instructed them never to mention her presence. What drama was unfolding here?

He couldn't decipher it. Shaking his head, he activated the jade platform. "Independent fighter Quincy versus Black Mountain Order's Raven!"

The third bout proved evenly matched. Both early Soul Realm warriors fought for nearly half an hour before Quincy secured victory by the narrowest margin.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: TENSE DRUMS]

Then came the fourth, fifth, and sixth fights. After each match, the winner drew a card, but something was off. Almost all of them pulled the same thing: an exemption card. Not a single challenge card had been drawn.

Everyone had assumed the odds were fifty-fifty, but now it seemed the chances of drawing a challenge card were incredibly slim.

[SFX: MURMUR OF SURPRISE]

The old man clarified that drawing a challenge card had only a one-in-five chance.

Beyond Zane's clash with Alex, the matches proved lackluster. With no rules against killing, fighters exercised extreme caution. They conserved strength for later rounds—depleting their Vitality or losing limbs early would destroy any chance of reaching the top ten.

The Guild offered every participant a gift. Though generous, it paled beside the top-ten rewards. Most fighters harbored no illusions about winning Dragon Soar Arena; even victorious independent warriors lacked the resources to utilize it. Their true objective was simply breaking into the top ten.
