Ep 1213 - Punching Bag

Word count: 1067

[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]
[AMBIENT: CROWDED CITY WALLS]
That damn smile on Zane’s face was really starting to get under Lance’s skin.
In this ogre city, humans served as mere cannon fodder - outnumbered, trapped, expendable. Lance’s presence alone kept tens of thousands frozen within the walls. So why did this human parade around with such calm confidence? Was he an idiot or suicidal?
What Lance didn’t realize: while he saw himself as a predator, Zane saw him as prey. Zane’s only concern was scaring Lance back into the city before claiming his two hundred thousand Illusion Points. Even half would net Zane a hundred thousand - more than doubling his current stash.
The spectators were just as baffled.
[SFX: MURMURING CROWD]
“Where did this human come from? He’s going right after Lance?”
“Does he have a death wish?”
“Wait, I know that guy,” a voice cut through the chatter, hushed and urgent. “He’s the one who killed Thane in the southwest. A real monster. Shattered Thane’s axes with his bare hands—and those were a Low Grade-One Divine Weapon!”
To Zane, a weapon like that was practically a toy. But for most warriors in the Soul Sea Realm, it was a treasure you might only dream of finding.
“Is he really that strong?” someone asked, and the ogre warriors in the crowd began looking at Zane with a sliver of respect.
“Even so, he’s no match for Lance,” another scoffed. “They’re not in the same league. Lance has already mastered parts of the Fire Law. This kid is just asking for it, trying to provoke him like this. Is he trying to throw away his Illusion Points?”
While the ogres debated, the few human warriors in the crowd felt a spark of hope. Forced into this city and hunted by ogres simply for being different, they saw in Zane a champion fighting for them all - a release for their pent-up frustration.
Still, a knot of worry tightened in their stomachs. They’d seen Lance’s last fight. They knew the kind of terrifying power he could unleash.
[AMBIENT: BATTLEGROUND]
A hundred feet apart now, the two of them locked eyes, each privately bracing for the other to lose his nerve and bolt back behind the city walls. But as the distance kept closing, that worry melted away on both sides—what each of them saw in the other wasn’t bravado. It was the kind of bone-deep confidence that only came from surviving fight after fight after fight.
“Can’t say I understand where you get that confidence from,” Lance said when they were a hundred feet apart, a faint trace of mockery in his eyes. “But fight me, and I’ll show you exactly what despair feels like. Be grateful this is the Illusion Battlefield. At least you won’t actually die here.”
Zane’s expression stayed calm, a small smile playing at his lips. “Call it overestimating myself, if you want.”
Zane had no intention of winning traditionally. The Illusion Battlefield’s rules worked against him - a clean victory would only earn half of Lance’s points, and the ogre would simply retreat to the city afterward, never risking his points again. Zane needed Lance to believe he was too stubborn to quit.
[SFX: FLAME IGNITING]
A ball of purple flame bloomed in Lance’s open palm. He squeezed it shut, snuffing it out—and in the same instant, a hundred-foot ring of identical purple flames bloomed to life around Zane.
[SFX: WINGS OF FIRE UNFURLING]
Fire roared up Lance’s back and split into two great wings of purple flame. 
[SFX: RUSHING FLAME]
Zane’s body lurched, looking for all the world like a man scrambling out of the way too late, his timing and angle both just slightly wrong.
[SFX: FIST IMPACT]
[SFX: FLAME EXPLOSION]
The punch connected dead-on. A column of purple fire several feet wide erupted outward, and a second later, Zane shot free of the blast like an arrow, slamming hard into the earth.
[SFX: GROUND CRATER IMPACT]
The impact tore a crater into the ground beneath him.
[SFX: CROWD JEERING]
Lance didn’t chase him down. He hovered there instead, looking down at the smoking crater with a smirk—and a small, nagging itch of doubt underneath it. The sharpest fighters tended to have good instincts for this kind of thing, a read on an opponent’s real strength built off nothing but bearing and presence and the look in their eyes. It wasn’t foolproof. But it was rarely far off, either.
And something about the calm on Zane’s face, even now, didn’t sit right.
Zane pulled himself out of the crater a moment later, expression serious, something almost like reluctance flickering behind his eyes.
“Heh. Don’t look at me like that,” Lance said, flame-wings spreading again behind him. “You’ve banked yourself a decent pile of points, I’ll give you that. But where I’m standing? You’re just a sheep. And I’m the wolf.”
He surged forward again, a trail of fire marking the path behind him.
A deafening *boom* ripped through the air as Zane’s body jolted. He put on a good show of trying to dodge, but the ball of purple flame caught him square in the chest. 
[SFX: BODY IMPACT AGAINST WALL]
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
The wall itself, a product of the Illusion Battlefield’s rules, was indestructible; the impact didn’t even leave a scratch. Zane, however, was not. 
[SFX: BODY SLIDING DOWN STONE]
He slid down the unyielding surface like a broken decoration.
Even with a body as tough as a Divine Weapon, the unprotected impact sent a brutal shock through his system. Zane gritted his teeth, pushing the pain down. It was all part of the plan.
“Still not dead?” Lance grunted, a flicker of annoyance in his eyes. “Must have some kind of trick.” He’d honestly expected the impact to finish the kid off. Zane’s body was unbelievably tough, a rare trait, but that’s all it was. A strong body just made for a better punching bag.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS CHARGING]
Done thinking, Lance charged again.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
Again and again, Zane was smashed against the wall. 
The crowd of warriors watched in stunned silence. This wasn’t a fight; it was a beatdown. The human was just a living target.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING]
[AMBIENT: HUSHED ANTICIPATION]
But as Lance lunged for what looked like the final blow, a faint, almost invisible smile touched Zane’s lips.
*It’s about time,* he thought.
