Ep 1026 - Jump the Line

Word count: 968

[AMBIENT: MINE INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Zane had agreed to go to the Holy City with Master Sloan and Master Hill — not that he’d had much choice in the matter.

But why him? Out of all the warriors, why was he the one chosen?

He wasn’t the only one with questions. The armored guards, the other warriors, and even the two hall leaders were just as curious.

Sure, Zane was talented and had adapted to the Vitality Pressure in record time, but that wasn’t exactly a golden ticket. The Upper World was massive, and the Soulbound Sect was crawling with talent. Why would he, of all people, get a trip to The Holy City just for showing a little potential? It just didn’t add up.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Since Zane wasn’t resisting anymore, the two hall leaders finally allowed themselves to relax. They didn’t have the full story, only that getting Zane to The Holy City was a mission of the highest importance. The order had come from someone you just don’t cross, so they were playing along, taking care not to get on Zane’s bad side.

Normally, leaving the mine involved tracking down the guards and drowning in transfer paperwork. But this was a special case, so they cut right through the red tape.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

The lead guard even seized the opportunity, stepping forward to announce gravely, “Zane is leaving the mine under special circumstances. He shouldn’t have had to pay the Sovereign Stones, so I’m returning them.”

Talk about a creative excuse. The guard was sharp, alright—clearly trying to score some points with Zane right in front of the two hall leaders.

[SFX: COINS JINGLING]

For a warrior at Zane’s level, one hundred Sovereign Stones was a small fortune, so he was more than happy to take them back.

A hundred stones was pocket change to the hall leaders. They knew the guard was just trying to curry favor. With faint smiles, they simply led Zane away.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The moment Zane was gone, the mine was thrown into a major shake-up, and a warrior named Rylan was the first to get hit. His luck had officially run out. Every Sovereign Stone he’d saved over the years was confiscated, and to top it off, he was forced to pay another hundred just to be released from the mine.

The other warriors were sick with envy when they heard Zane had just been whisked away, but there wasn’t a damn thing they could do about it.

[MUSIC: LONG JOURNEY]

The journey was longer than Zane expected. Along the way, the two hall leaders tried to chat Zane up, their tone almost friendly. They were dying of curiosity, trying to figure out who had ordered them to bring him to The Holy City. They were fishing, hoping to learn what big shot was backing him.

But Zane was just as clueless. How was he supposed to answer that? He didn’t know a single person of importance in the entire Soulbound Sect.

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

As they neared the Holy City, Zane found himself standing on a teleportation platform ten thousand feet off the ground. He looked up — and went still.

[AMBIENT: VAST CITY ATMOSPHERE]

The city stretched in every direction without end. Buildings the size of mountains rose around him, all carved from some material that glowed with a pale blue light, as though the entire city had been cut from crystal. He couldn’t see its borders.

“Is this the Holy City?” he asked.

Master Hill’s expression carried a faint pride. “Nearly one hundred billion people live here. The city alone covers one-tenth the area of an entire mortal world — three times the size of the Heartlands.”

Zane stared. He finally understood why the journey had taken as long as it had.

Then he noticed the lines.

[MUSIC: WONDER AND DISCOVERY]

Thousands of thin lines ran through the air at different heights, crisscrossing the entire city. Warriors flew along them at speed — red lines for those heading in, blue for those heading out, the varying heights keeping streams of traffic from colliding.

“Too many flyers,” Master Hill said simply. “Without these, the sky would be impassable.”

In the mortal world, flight required Soul Sea Realm cultivation — rare enough that no one needed to manage it. Here, the number of Soul Sea warriors and above was beyond counting. The lines were the only thing keeping the sky from chaos.

[SFX: RAPID FLIGHT ACCELERATION]

The hall leaders guided him onto one of the shimmering lines, and they shot forward.

Just as he’d seen from the platform, all the warriors on their line were flying in the same direction. People moved at different speeds, but the whole system was surprisingly orderly.

[SFX: ROWDY SHOUTING]

[MUSIC: TENSION RISING]

He’d only been on the line for a few moments when a loud, unruly group of warriors came barreling through.

They weren’t even pretending to follow the rules, yelling and chasing each other while flying way faster than the suggested speed. They looked like they were in their early twenties, but their power levels were shockingly high. Most had reached the Soul Sea Realm, and a few were even at the Life-and-Death Realm.

As the rowdy group approached, the other flyers near Zane quickly scattered. Even though the newcomers were young, they radiated the confidence of powerful, wealthy families, and nobody wanted to risk picking a fight.

Seeing this, the two hall leaders frowned, their lips tightening.

As hall leaders, they held a high rank, but they clearly didn’t want any trouble in The Holy City. The city was a melting pot of powerful people from all walks of life. While leaders of the Thirty-six Halls were major figures in the Soulbound Sect, there were plenty of elders and other higher-ups above them. It was best to avoid making waves.

[MUSIC: SUDDEN DANGER]

[SFX: SHARP WHOOSH]

Seeing his escorts back down, Zane moved aside as well. The last thing he expected was for two of the youths to suddenly break from the pack and veer straight toward him.
