Ep 701 - Another Way
Word count: 1178
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
For the Ethereal Spirit Sect, this was a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity. They couldn’t afford to screw this up.
Even though the Dragon-Snare was one of their most powerful weapons, pinning down Maddox, the strongest warrior in The Heartlands, had them on edge. To make matters worse, the black mist pouring from Maddox was actively eroding the magical trap. If he broke free, the Sect’s entire plan would crumble, and everything they’d sacrificed would be for nothing. That just wasn’t an option.
After forming the Seven-in-One Array, the combined strength of the Septuplets skyrocketed. Their leader alone was now channeling power equivalent to the late stage of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm. They were confident that with the power of all seven warriors merged into one, a single sword strike would be enough to end Maddox for good.
[SFX: SWORD SWOOSH]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
The leader's sword flared to life, a brilliant streak of light plunging into the churning black mist.
Everyone held their breath, their eyes locked on the spot where the sword had vanished. With Maddox trapped by the Dragon-Snare and his Vitality suppressed, there was no way he could block an attack like that.
But as Zane stared into the darkness, a sick feeling twisted in his gut. He couldn’t believe Maddox would be taken down this easily.
Unlike Zane, the others were brimming with confidence. Robin, the head of House Kensington, was already imagining the glory. By helping the Ethereal Spirit Sect eliminate Maddox, his family’s name would be known not just in The Heartlands but across the entire world.
[SFX: METAL SHATTERING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRAMATIC ORCHESTRA]
Just as the Septuplets poured all their combined power into the finishing blow, the sharp sound of shattering metal cut through the air. A split second later, several beams of light shot out from the black mist.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACTS]
Their sword was broken into pieces. The fragments, crackling with immense power, slammed into the chests of four of the Septuplets. They tore straight through flesh and bone, blowing horrifying, gaping holes out of their backs.
[SFX: BODIES FALLING]
A shower of blood erupted in the sky as the four warriors went limp and plummeted toward the ground.
It took a moment for the horrific reality to sink in. Their attack hadn't just failed—Maddox had shattered their sacred sword and turned it into a weapon against them, instantly killing more than half their number.
[MUSIC: TRAGIC STRINGS]
The three survivors cried out in grief. None of them had ever fallen. Now four were gone in the span of a heartbeat.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS SWELL]
The black mist began to thin. Layer by layer, the Dragon-Snare fractured and came apart, its light dissolving into nothing. As it fell away, Maddox emerged — his aura violent and uncontained, dark scales rippling across his skin.
"Not bad," he said, his voice low with something almost like pleasure. "You actually pushed me this far. Consider that your reward — I'll crush the Ethereal Spirit Sect personally."
He laughed then, and the sound of it rolled across the sky like thunder. The wind shifted. Clouds darkened. The whole of Ethereal City seemed to shrink beneath the weight of his presence.
[MUSIC: URGENT ACTION DRUMS]
Zane didn't hesitate.
"Retreat!"
He shot toward Cora instantly, grabbed her, and poured every ounce of Vitality he had into his movement. His body became a streak of black against the sky.
His instinct had been right from the start. A man who had the confidence to build his own kingdom and march openly on the Ethereal Spirit Sect didn't run out of tricks after one.
Brent and Robin split off in opposite directions without a word. Neither of them tried to stop Zane from taking Cora. They understood — right now, she was safer with him than with anyone else.
The three remaining Septuplets held their ground. They were no match for Maddox in his current state, but they were still Spirit-Tribulation warriors. They bought what time they could.
[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSE]
Zane's expression was grim as Ethereal City shrank behind them. He'd braced for Maddox to be dangerous. He hadn't braced for this. Before, he could have matched him on speed, maybe even slipped free. Now, he wasn't sure he could hold his own in a direct clash. The scales, the aura, the sheer weight of his presence — something had changed.
Sure enough, after barely enough time to catch his breath, Zane looked back. A black shape was growing in the distance.
Maddox. Already.
As long as Zane kept his Vitality burning at full output, the gap held. But he couldn't sustain that forever — no cultivator could. The moment he eased up, that black shape would close in. He'd been in this position before, back on the main continent, hounded by War Sages until he nearly ran dry. He had no intention of reliving it.
[MUSIC: EMOTIONAL PIANO]
If he’d been protecting anyone else, Zane would have already been long gone, trusting that Maddox wouldn’t bother with them. But Cora wasn’t just anyone. She possessed the Violet-Core Physique, a prize Maddox would hunt to the ends of the earth for. The thought of the impossible choice he had to make was crushing him.
Throughout all of it, Cora hadn't made a sound. She lay expressionless against his shoulder.
Then, without warning, she spoke. "Zane… Just kill me."
Lavender, flying alongside him, let out a cold, humorless laugh. “What’s this? You’re not afraid of dying anymore?”
Cora’s expression hardened, her fear replaced by a raw, painful determination.
“I used to be terrified of death,” she said, her voice steady. “But not anymore.”
Today had been a tragedy for her, a day that made her feel like a complete fool.
The members of House Kensington had treated her well enough, but in the end, not a single one of them had trusted her—not even Brent, the boy she’d grown up with. The heartbreak was a sharp, physical ache, worse than anything she had ever felt.
[SFX: SPEAR MATERIALIZING]
[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]
The moment the words left her mouth, Lavender’s spear materialized in her hand, its tip pointed directly at Cora’s forehead. In Lavender’s mind, it was simple: killing Cora was the only way for Zane to escape Maddox at full speed. Honestly, she’d been wanting to get rid of the dead weight for a while.
“Stop,” Zane ordered. His voice was flat, devoid of emotion.
“Zane, she’s slowing us down!” Lavender argued, her spear unwavering. “We’ll have a better chance if she’s gone! Just think about it!”
“I know,” Zane replied, his eyes fixed on the horizon ahead.
“Then why won’t you let me do it?” she demanded, unable to understand why he would risk everything for a burden like Cora, especially now.
[SFX: ENERGY BURST]
[MUSIC: DETERMINED ORCHESTRAL]
Zane took a deep breath, and another surge of Forceful Energy burst from him, propelling them forward even faster. “Because,” he said, his voice cutting through the rushing wind, “I know another way.”
