Ep 752 - Maddox On Top

Word count: 1027

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Of all the Law powers, the Law of Causality was the purest, the most direct. Every cause and its effect was built into the very foundation of the heavens, set in stone the moment a Cosmic Age was born. This made the Law of Causality the ultimate authority, a power no one could fully comprehend unless they became the heavens themselves.

Lavender watched Zane with a knot of dread in her stomach. The Celestial Fragment in his possession held several threads of cause and effect, but it was just that—a fragment. Neither she nor the Cobalt Dragon knew much about its true power. If Zane died, his sword spirit would fade, and her own soul, just a fragile strand clinging to him for support, would dissipate into the air. The Cobalt Dragon would fare no better, sealed away once more inside The Soulforge.

The force of Zane’s first strike had been beyond anything they could have imagined. He’d unleashed the power of a middle-stage Spirit-Tribulation Realm warrior, a level of strength that dwarfed even those from the Life-and-Death Realm.

How could Maddox have possibly survived an attack like that?

His fate was the only thing that mattered. If he died, Simon would be the last master left standing in the Corrupted Divine Kingdom. And while Simon was a formidable Life-and-Death Realm warrior, he couldn’t take on everyone at once. 

Without Maddox, the Corrupted Divine Kingdom was vulnerable, and the World Merchants’ Guild and the Cloud Hall might gain the upper hand. Both sides knew it. Their own skirmishes were just a sideshow.

[SFX: DEBRIS SETTLING]

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

 The intertwining rays of starlight finally faded, the wind carrying them away along with the dust, the shattered rocks, and the rubble that was once the city wall. As the haze settled, a figure came into view at the bottom of a massive crater.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS DRONE]

 The body was charred black, like a log pulled from a fire, its burnt skin stretched tight over a motionless frame.

[SFX: CROWD MURMURS]

 “It’s Maddox!”

“Is he dead?”

“He’s not moving. He has to be. There’s no way he could have survived that.”

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

The warriors on Oracle City's wall held their breath. If Maddox was dead, the battle was over. The war was over. Every Cloud Hall fighter and Guild member on the field felt it — that fragile, desperate flicker of hope.

Then the body moved.

A charred arm shifted. Then the other. Then Maddox pushed himself upright from the crater floor, his burned frame rising slowly, deliberately, until he was standing.

The Cloud Hall side went silent. The Corrupted Divine Kingdom erupted.

"Our King is mighty! Our King is mighty!"

[SFX: CROWD ROAR]

The roar shook the heavens. Independent warriors who had thrown their lot in with Maddox screamed until their voices cracked. He had taken the full force of Double Stars Brilliance and walked away from it.

[MUSIC: DESPERATE CHARGE]

Saint Morphen didn't wait. He understood immediately — Maddox had taken a devastating blow, and this was the only window they would get. He broke from the wall and charged straight for the crater, moving at full speed.

[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]

He was almost there. Close enough to see the cracks running through the charred skin, the ragged edges of the damage Zane had done. Close enough to believe it might actually work.

Maddox's eyes opened.

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

One punch. That was all it took. The fist came up from the crater's edge without any windup, without any warning — a single strike that connected before Saint Morphen could change course. He was launched out of the crater and hit the ground hard. No one could tell if he was breathing.

Maddox's charred face split open into a grin. Two rows of white teeth showed through the blackened skin. It was one of the ugliest things anyone on that field had ever seen.

"It's over."

The words passed through the crowd like a cold wind. Everyone understood what it meant. The sect war — whatever hope had remained — had just ended.

[MUSIC: COLLECTIVE DREAD]

Only then did anyone think to look for Zane and Tia.

They were already on the ground, arms around each other, motionless. The Life and Death Wind — those two bright yellow streams everyone had watched drift toward them — had never been stopped. A single brush of that wind had killed the ancient tycoon of the World Merchants' Guild. No one seriously believed Zane and Tia had fared any better.

Maddox walked out of the crater. With every step, a cloud of fine dust rose around his feet. His gaze moved past the crowd, past the wreckage, until it found them.

[SFX: SLOW FOOTSTEPS]

Lavender was already standing over Zane's body, spear raised.

[MUSIC: STANDOFF STRINGS]

"Stop," she said.

Maddox looked at her with something like curiosity. "I've always known there was something extraordinary about you," he said, his voice still raspy from the blast. "But Zane is already dead. Why not come with me?"

Lavender's expression didn't change. A cold, contemptuous smile crossed her face.

"What a joke," she said. "A small region. A small lower realm. An existence no better than dust — and you speak like a king." She leveled the spear at him. "Back when I had my full strength, I could have killed you with a millionth of it. Without even blinking. And you're asking me to follow you?"

The warriors who heard it went still. A millionth of her strength. If she wasn't lying — and nothing about her said she was — then whatever Lavender had once been was something none of them had a word for.

Maddox's grin faded. He filed the information away and said nothing.

His gaze landed on a piece of debris resting on Zane's chest, and his eyes lit up with a peculiar glint.

"This fragment…"

He didn't know what it was or how powerful it might be, but he could see it was the source of the Vitality still clinging to Zane. He knew, instantly, that it was no ordinary object.

Though Maddox could wield the Life and Death Wind, he didn't truly control it. He understood nothing of the Law of Causality.

[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]

All he knew was that the wind was magical, terrifyingly powerful, and absolute. Anything it touched died. Simple as that. But what if it wasn't so simple?
