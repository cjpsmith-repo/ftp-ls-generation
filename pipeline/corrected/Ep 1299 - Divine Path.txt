Ep 1299 - Divine Path

Word count: 1125

Zane barely had time to register the flame’s warning before the pressure wave hit.
[SFX: DEEP EXPLOSION]
It came from the flame, which erupted in a brilliant flash right in front of him. But it wasn’t a fire that burned the body—it was a flame of the void, something that seared the soul itself, and the power rolling off it was unbelievable.
[MUSIC: TENSE OTHERWORLDLY]
[AMBIENT: HOWLING ICY WIND]
The long corridor ahead of Zane dissolved, and in its place, a world of bitter ice and snow slammed into existence. A frigid wind swirled around him, thick with the raw power of ice.
*My soul… I’ve been pulled into this world of ice and snow,* he thought, looking down at his hands. A jade-green glow clung to his body. He was in his pure soul form.
[SFX: CLEAR BELL CHIME]
Then, the clear, crisp jingle of a small bell cut through the air.
Zane looked up to see a small bell hanging in the air right in front of him.
*What is this?*
[MUSIC: HAUNTING ETHEREAL]
As the bell appeared, a figure began to emerge from the snowstorm—a woman, her graceful form wrapped only in a swirling ribbon of green and blue. She drifted toward Zane, her bare feet passing through the endless cold as if it were nothing. Her eyes were fixed on him, her expression unreadable.
The cold hadn’t bothered him before; his soul was strong enough to withstand any normal chill. But under this woman’s indifferent gaze, it was a different story. A cold so deep it felt ancient seeped into him, as if he’d been plunged into a ten-thousand-year-old glacier. It wasn’t just cold—it felt like his very soul was being sealed in ice. 
[SFX: DEEP FREEZING CRACKLE]
A thin layer of frost began to form over its surface, the chill so intense it was becoming hard to even think.
*Incredible control over the power of ice,* he thought.
But then his focus sharpened. *No — this is powerful, but it’s still just comprehension released from the soul. It’s an illusion, nothing more.*
His eyes flashed, and the jade-green light wrapped around him blazed brighter.
[SFX: ICE CRACKING]
Countless hairline cracks split across the frost sealing his soul. A heartbeat later, the fragments broke away entirely and fell, scattering into the cold.
[SFX: DELICATE BELL CHIME]
The woman lifted the bell and gave it a light shake. A fresh curtain of snowflakes drifted down between them.
“Again?” Zane’s eyes went cold. He surged forward toward her.
[AMBIENT: SWIRLING SNOWFALL]
But she only swayed, light as a single snowflake caught on the wind, and floated just out of reach — retreating exactly as fast as he advanced. No matter how hard he pressed, the distance between them refused to close.
*This movement technique… I know this,* he thought, brow furrowing. Eight Smoky Melodies. *Only someone with real command over it could hold a fixed gap like this — Sloane broke through mine once, using this exact same principle.*
Understanding dawned, and a thin white mist began to curl off his soul. He let his own weight fall away, drifting as light and aimless as smoke — and this time, when he pushed off toward her, there was almost no force behind the motion for her to borrow. 
Without his momentum to push against, she could no longer glide away from him. The gap between them began, finally, to shrink.
As Zane drifted slowly toward her, a flicker of confusion crossed the woman’s ethereal features.
“You… you know the Floating Feather Technique, too?” she asked. It was the first time she had spoken since appearing.
“Floating Feather Technique? No, this is something different.”
He drew closer, reaching a hand out toward the small bell.
[SFX: DELICATE BELL CHIME]
The woman twisted at the waist, a delicate movement that made the bell ring again as she slipped just out of his reach. At the same time, the snowfall intensified. Flakes the size of dinner plates spiraled down, then condensed, hardening into massive hailstones that rained down like boulders. 
[AMBIENT: MASSIVE HAILSTORM]
[SFX: ICE CRACKING AND SHATTERING]
The air filled with the sharp crack and shatter of ice.
Zane and the woman weaved through the downpour, their movements fluid and effortless, but the storm was a wall between them, forcing them apart. He couldn’t get close.
“It’s so strange,” the woman said, her brow furrowed slightly. “You’re clearly using the Floating Feather Technique. Who taught you?”
Zane smiled. “I’ll tell you. But first, you have to tell me who you are.”
“This is the Corridor of the Emperor,” she replied, her voice indifferent.
“An Emperor?” Zane cut in, his focus sharpening.
“No,” the woman said, shaking her head. “I am the mark left behind by a True God.”
“A True God?” The words hit Zane like a physical blow. A mark… he knew what that meant.
The woman saw the understanding dawn on his face and let out a soft sigh. 
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING TENSION]
“This is your test. Though I am just a mark, I will still test your soul.”
As she spoke, she waved her hands. 
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING ICE FORMATION]
[SFX: MASSIVE GLACIER DESCENDING]
A glacier materialized in the sky and began to thunder down toward Zane.
[SFX: TEMPERATURE DROP WHOOSH]
[SFX: AIR FREEZING CRACKLE]
The glacier was a hundred feet wide, and the temperature plummeted in its path. It descended with murderous speed, freezing the very air as it fell, a mountain of ice aimed right at him.
“So this is it,” Zane murmured. “Testing my soul with the imprint of a True God.”
His expression hardened into grim focus as he watched the glacier fall. 
[SFX: POWERFUL FIST SWING WHOOSH]
Then, he swung his fist.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT CRACK]
[SFX: GLACIER SHATTERING EXPLOSION]
[SFX: ICE SHARDS RAINING DOWN]
His fist met the glacier. A single crack appeared at the point of impact, then shot outward, branching through the thick ice like a spider’s web. A heartbeat later, the entire glacier shattered, exploding in a deafening roar. 
Shards of ice rained down, blanketing the ground for hundreds of yards in every direction.
[SFX: MASSIVE GLACIER DESCENDING]
A second glacier tore loose from the sky above and came hurtling down after the first.
[SFX: POWERFUL FIST SWING WHOOSH]
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT CRACK]
[SFX: GLACIER SHATTERING EXPLOSION]
Zane’s fist met it just as squarely. It broke apart the same way, collapsing into ice and thunder.
The woman floated above the wreckage, bell still in hand, her expression calm. “Before you, one thousand four hundred warriors have passed through this corridor,” she said. “Not one of them was compatible with my Divine Path.”
She flicked her fingers, and pale blue light sparked across the sky.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING ICE FORMATION]
Dozens of ice-rivers poured down at once, filling the air with thunder.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT CRACK]
[SFX: GLACIER SHATTERING EXPLOSION]
Zane wove through the barrage, shattering each one as it came, until the last of them broke apart around him.
“Your Divine Path,” Zane said, catching his breath. “What is it, exactly?”
The woman’s eyes flickered. “I made ice my face and spent one hundred thousand years sealed inside a river of ice. In the end, I comprehended the Godly Sigil and obtained the Godly Way of Snow. My name is Hayden.”
Zane nodded. It reminded him, distantly, of Sloane — the same rare, terrifying category of talent.
