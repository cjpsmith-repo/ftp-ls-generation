Ep 155 - Corrupt Vitality
Word Count: 1103

An idea struck Zane. He gathered the Vitality in his body, swung his fist, and roared.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
The wave of Corrupt Vitality cascaded over the giant hawk, transforming its mottled gray and brown plumage into lustrous, midnight-black feathers. The darkness seemed to seep into every quill, creating a creature of shadow and menace.
[SFX: HAWK FLYING]
[MUSIC: MAGICAL EMPOWERMENT]
A slow, satisfied smile crept across Zane's face as he observed the transformation. He extended his arm and beckoned with subtle fingers. Responding to his unspoken command, the magnificent bird soared gracefully through the air before settling on his shoulder, its talons gripping firmly yet carefully.
"Hey, kid!" bellowed the hawk's former master, a Soul Realm cultivator. His face contorted with rage as he jabbed an accusatory finger toward Zane, disbelief evident in his widened eyes. "That's my spirit companion!"
"Ha! You should be watching your own back instead of your pet!" Seymour interjected with savage delight. He streaked past the distracted man in relentless pursuit, his Jade Saber gleaming with deadly purpose in the ambient light, leaving a trail of ethereal green in its wake.
The winner of this battle would claim everything inside The Elysian Keep. No one was willing to share the prize; they were all driven to kill their opponents. Greed and selfishness, after all, are just human nature.
With the newly corrupted hawk on his shoulder, Zane paced the edge of the spire, choosing to stay out of the fight for now.
If anyone’s Vitality strayed too close, he simply had the black hawk on his shoulder devour it.
[SFX: CREATURE GROWTH]
Within seconds, the black hawk began to expand dramatically, its corrupted form swelling until it loomed over thirty feet tall against the sky. The monstrous creature's wings stretched wide, blotting out the sun as dark feathers rippled with sinister energy.
No longer able to rest upon Zane's shoulder, the transformed beast cast an imposing shadow across the spire. Zane gazed upward at his creation, a mixture of awe and satisfaction crossing his face. He raised his hand with deliberate authority, commanding the hawk to hover nearby, its massive talons flexing as it awaited his next instruction. 
The air around them seemed to pulse with the creature's corrupted power, and Zane felt a strange connection to the beast—a dark extension of his own will. For one, the dark power suddenly turned deadly.
[SFX: SWORD STAB]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC BETRAYAL]
 With a sudden grunt, a man felt a blade pierce his chest. He looked down at the broadsword and saw it belonged to his own younger brother. He turned, his face a mask of disbelief. “Why? Why would you do this?”
His brother just shook his head. “I’m sorry. Only one of us can make it out of here, and I couldn’t risk you being my last opponent. I’ll take care of Mom and Dad.”
Zane could only sigh as he watched the betrayal unfold.
If Sandra had just trusted him, these Soul Realm masters wouldn’t be slaughtering one another right now. But they were trapped in a fight to the death with no way out.
Watching them, Zane realized there was no trust left here. Not even between brothers.
He then noticed that the blue orb of Sandra's Life Essence still hadn't faded. 
[SFX: HAWK SHRIEK]
He gave the hawk a silent command through their mental link. The massive bird shrieked—a piercing cry that echoed across the battlefield—and dove toward the glowing blue light with talons extended. Its wings cast a dark shadow as it descended upon Sandra's lingering Life Essence.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
After absorbing the luminous essence and converting it into more Corrupt Vitality, the hawk transformed before everyone's eyes. Its wingspan stretched wider, feathers darkened to obsidian, and eyes blazed with newfound power. The creature had become a terrifying extension of Zane's will.
Zane still wasn't in a hurry to join the fray. He stood his ground with calculated patience, his face betraying nothing while his mind raced through possible scenarios. He watched, breathing steadily, fingers lightly tracing the outline of his weapon as he bided his time for the perfect moment to strike.
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD CHAOS]
 When the Desperate Melee began, everyone had been connected by a thin tether of Vitality. At the start, with so many fighters, the tethers were a tangled mess.
But as the battle raged and the Soul Realm masters fell, their tethers snapped, one by one.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
 Now, only three streams of Vitality remained, linking Zane, Seymour, and an old man clutching a Dragon-Headed Staff.
Zane watched Seymour and the old man fight, their battle fierce and desperate.
Having made it this far, both of them were clearly skilled fighters. They knew only one could survive to claim the treasures of The Elysian Keep. To them, Zane—a kid who had barely reached a half-step into the Adept Realm—was no threat at all.
Now, just one step from victory, the fight between the two masters intensified. Their raging Vitality erupted, blasting waves of heat across every corner of the spire. Even from a distance, Zane felt the searing energy and ducked behind the black hawk for cover.
[SFX: SERIES OF EXPLOSIONS]
After a series of thunderous explosions, Zane stepped out from behind the hawk. As the searing energy faded, he saw Seymour standing alone, Jade Saber in hand.
He looked like he'd been dragged through hell, his clothes torn and his hair singed. Seymour cursed as he limped, glaring at Zane. "Damn it! You really think you can get that kind of power so fast? Even *I* can't do that. But it's not enough. You think you can beat me with that overgrown bird? Dream on!"
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
 With a glint in his eye, Seymour launched himself into the air, moving with incredible speed as he swung the Jade Saber right at Zane.
[SFX: FAST MOTION]
A Soul Realm master could move unbelievably fast. In the blink of an eye, Seymour closed the distance, appearing less than three feet from Zane.
[SFX: MAGICAL ACTIVATION]
[SFX: BURST OF SPEED]
*Damn, he's fast,* Zane thought, his eyes widening. He instantly activated the Phoenix Crystal in his right arm, and his speed surged tenfold. With an explosive burst of power from his legs, Zane shot back more than a hundred feet, narrowly dodging Seymour’s sword.
Zane was only at a half-step into the Adept Realm; he couldn't burn his Vitality or fly. Seymour, on the other hand, could do both. 
Seeing his target slip away, Seymour gave chase without hesitation. Since Zane couldn't fly, he was at a serious disadvantage.
