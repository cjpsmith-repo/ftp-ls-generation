Ep 1072 - Desperation

Word count: 1137

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Zane wasn’t arrogant enough to think he could solve the ancient mystery left by Supreme Lord Sword—that would be giving himself way too much credit. Still, he couldn’t deny that the strange white sword shadows were making his own sword spirit, Lavender, stronger.

[SFX: SHARP WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]

But there was a catch: excruciating pain. Each time a shadow passed through him, a sharp, stabbing sensation shot straight through his heart. After thousands of hits, he was honestly surprised it hadn’t just burst in his chest.

Even so, he’d been through worse. This was nothing compared to the agony of swapping his Vitality for Chaos Energy. He wasn’t about to back down over a little discomfort.

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: DARK STRINGS]

When Zane stumbled back toward the huge rock, he was met with a startling sight. The two Spirit Supreme Realm warriors were glaring at him, their usually placid expressions contorted with a rage so intense it looked like murder.

“You… what did you do?” Landry’s voice trembled.

Damian was more direct, his voice a low growl. “How? How did you make the presence vanish?”

Their eyes blazed like cornered animals. These two had spent tens of thousands of years meditating here, dedicating their entire existence to the words carved on that stone. Now this newcomer had triggered some bizarre change. How could they stay calm?

Because they’d devoted their lives to meditation, their physical strength had atrophied, leaving them far weaker than typical Spirit Supreme Realm warriors. They’d trained their minds for peace, shunning violence. But this sudden shift brought their buried emotions roaring to the surface.

Supreme Lord Sword was said to be powerful enough to fight True Gods. The swordsmanship he’d left behind could fell a deity - if only someone could decipher it. They’d invested lifetimes trying to unlock that power. Its sudden disappearance felt like theft.

“I didn’t do anything!” Zane insisted, shaking his head.

“Impossible! Don’t play dumb with us!” Damian snarled. “We’ve been here for millennia. You show up, and suddenly the essence of the stone is just… gone. Explain yourself!”

“Who are you trying to fool?” Landry added, his voice cracking with desperation.

Zane looked at them for a moment, then shrugged. He had no reason to hide it — he’d already told Yolande and Layla. “I saw some faint sword shadows coming off the words,” he said, sketching a rough shape in the air with his hand. “I shattered one of them. After that, it became like this.”

The two men stared.

“Sword shadows,” Landry repeated slowly.

“What did they look like?” Damian pressed. “Describe them.”

Zane opened his mouth to answer — and then stopped. He could feel it again. The heart-piercing sensation, faint but unmistakable, threaded back through his chest. He smiled slightly. “They’re back.”

The two men felt it too. The sensation they’d lived with for twenty thousand and sixty thousand years — the one that had vanished without warning — had returned. They looked at each other, then back at the rock, then at Zane.

“So that feeling… all these years… that was the sword shadows?” Damian murmured.

“Why can’t I see them?” Landry’s voice had gone very quiet. “Why can’t I see them…”

The two of them trailed off, staring at the rock with hollow expressions. Zane felt a flicker of pity. Then it passed. Even if they could see the shadows, there were a dozen ways to sharpen sword intent — these two had just chosen the hardest possible path and stayed on it for too long to find another. He stopped engaging and turned back to the rock.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

Their desperation was a fever in their blood, urging them to grab Zane and shake the secrets out of him. But they held back. Attacking a warrior from the Thirteen Palaces—especially now, with Willow Island swarming with them for the Passing the Torch—was suicide.

A new idea sparked through their panic: what if they could work with him? If Zane really had unlocked the sword art, maybe they could get a piece of the reward. Their minds raced, fighting the primal urge to resort to violence.

But Zane was barely listening. He was still reeling from the last barrage of shadows, but something new was cutting through the haze of pain. He could feel it—Lavender was stronger. Strong enough, he realized, that he might actually be able to block the shadows this time. The thought sent a surge of adrenaline through him, overriding the agony.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

[SFX: SWORD SLASH]

This time, Zane didn’t run.

He planted his feet in the desert sand, held Thundergust straight out in front of him, and pressed two fingers of his free hand lightly against the flat of the blade. His eyes fixed on the rock.

No Law power. No tricks. Just sword intent, pure and concentrated, wrapped tight around the steel.

[SFX: RAPID SWORD STRIKES]

He triggered the shadows with seven slashes — and then the torrent came. He didn’t move his feet. He just swung.

The speed of his blade quickly exceeded what the eye could follow. Anyone watching would have seen only flickering sleeves and arcs of blue light, the sword itself invisible. Shadow after shadow rushed in, met the blade, deflected, circled, returned — and after the seventh deflection, each one shattered, releasing its sparks into his body.

He held on. One breath. Two. Three.

Then the torrent thickened beyond what he could manage, and it swallowed him whole.

[SFX: BODY COLLAPSE]

Zane’s eyes rolled back and he went down.

[SFX: RUSHING MOVEMENT]

Lavender stepped out of his body the moment he fell, caught him before he hit the ground, and set him down. She straightened and looked at Landry and Damian with cold, flat eyes. Her Argent Fighting Soul was more than enough to handle two Spirit Supreme Realm warriors even as a disembodied soul — and both men knew it. She didn’t speak. She just stood there, between them and Zane, and waited.

Landry and Damian stared at Zane’s unconscious body. Whatever he’d found in that rock, they were certain of it now. And they weren’t going to touch him — not with the Thirteen Palaces all over this island, and not while that sword spirit was watching.

They could still hope for a share of whatever came next.

After a while, Zane woke up again.

[MUSIC: DARK REPETITIVE RHYTHM]

He fell into a brutal cycle: fight, get overwhelmed by the shadows, pass out from the pain, and wake up only to do it all over again.

[MUSIC: RISING TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]

With each attempt, his sword spirit, Lavender, grew stronger, and Zane could feel his own sword intent sharpening right along with it.

They were just getting started.
