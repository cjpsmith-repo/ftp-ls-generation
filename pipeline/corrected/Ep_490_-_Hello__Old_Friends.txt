Ep 490 - Hello, Old Friends

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

A loud voice boomed across the arena, silencing the crowd. "This is the first fight for a disciple of Bloodwood Cliff! Crush him, Brody! Show 'em what you've got!"

[SFX: CROWD ROAR]

The Bloodwood Cliff disciples roared their support.

[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]

Meanwhile, Lady Darkmere, seated in the shadows at the back of the stands, stared coldly at Zane.

This kid, Zane Lowell, had humiliated her once before. Her, the leader of Bloodwood Cliff. She wasn’t about to let that story get out, of course. She was the respected leader of a fourth-level sect; there was no way she was letting her reputation get dragged through the mud by some punk. But that didn't mean she was going to let him walk away from this.

"Brody, come here," Lady Darkmere said, her voice low.

"Yes, my Lady!" Brody Booth lumbered over to her side.

[SFX: BLOOD DROP]

Lady Darkmere raised a delicate finger to her lips and bit down. A single drop of blood welled on the soft tip.

Brody’s eyes twitched. "My Lady," he said, his voice trembling.

Bloodwood Cliff wasn't exactly known for its morals; they were infamous for dabbling in dark magic and forbidden arts. Brody knew exactly what she was planning.

[MUSIC: SINISTER STRINGS]

"Don't worry," Lady Darkmere said with a vicious little laugh. "This soul-killing poison blood will unlock your full potential for a short time. I'll help you recover the lifespan you'll lose. But you have to go down there and kill that brat!"

Brody stared at her, his skepticism warring with his fear.

"My Lady, I don't need a boost to kill that kid!" he said, shaking his head frantically.

But Lady Darkmere just giggled, the cruel twist of her lips somehow making her even more beautiful. "I know, but... just in case!" She drew a blood talisman with crimson energy from her fingertip and pressed it into Brody's chest. The talisman flashed brilliant red before sinking into his flesh.

Brody transformed instantly. His eyes blazed scarlet, and a feral grin twisted his features. "My Lady, he's going to die!"

Brody leapt toward the arena while Zane strode forward from the opposite side, his expression unreadable.

[SFX: CROWD CHATTER]

The spectators erupted into conversation.

"It's that fourth-level Adept Realm kid's turn again!"

"He beat Alex—we can't just dismiss him as ordinary."

"Pure luck, nothing more. He doesn't stand a chance against a true Soul Realm cultivator!"

Though Zane hailed from a fourth-level sect, the notion of defeating a Soul Realm master with Adept cultivation struck many as absurd. Only elite disciples from fourth-level sects could reliably fight above their station—such prodigies were extraordinarily rare, appearing perhaps once in a generation.

Alex belonged to a prestigious family and possessed heaven-grade talent, the kind of natural gifts that made elders salivate with envy. Yet Zane had crossed an entire major realm and beaten him unconscious with a single strike. The memory still burned in the minds of those who had witnessed it.

The gathered cultivators desperately wanted Zane to lose—and lose badly. His continued success threatened their understanding of cultivation itself. If someone from the Adept Realm could humiliate Soul Realm masters, what did that say about their own achievements? The thought gnawed at them.

[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT CROWD]

[MUSIC: ANTICIPATORY DRUMS]

Several more disciples entered the stands from the rear entrance, wearing white robes with clouds embroidered across their chests—Cloud Hall disciples. Finn and Patrick led the group, with Lewis close behind.

Their last mission had wrapped up in a county near Oracle City. With the Martial Tournament about to kick off, there was no way they were going to miss it, so they'd headed straight there the second they were done.

"Looks like a Bloodwood Cliff disciple is up there. Wonder who he's fighting," Patrick said casually, glancing at the arena.

Finn’s sharp eyes scanned the stands, then drifted to the arena floor. Someone was walking slowly toward the center… someone who looked familiar.

[SFX: DRAMATIC STING]

His eyes widened. "It’s Zane!"

"What? Zane? He's here for the tournament, too?" Lewis asked.

Patrick followed Finn's gaze, and his easygoing expression vanished. "It's really him," he said with a wry smile. "And he's not here to watch. He's in the arena."

"What!" Lewis shot to his feet, craning his neck over the crowd.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

His jaw dropped when he got a clear look. Sure enough, there was Zane. "It really is him! What is he doing up there? Is he trying to get himself killed?"

Patrick, Finn, and Lewis were disciples in the Elite House of Cloud Hall now, getting the best training out there. They had all made incredible progress over the last few months. Finn, for instance, had managed to grasp two Laws while studying the Extreme Thunder Sword Intent: Metal and Thunder. He hadn't built up much Law Power yet, but just understanding a Law was something maybe one in a thousand cultivators could pull off. For someone still at the Soul Realm, it was proof of some serious talent.

Patrick's own strength had skyrocketed, too. He could summon The Immovable Guardian multiple times without resting, and it had even sprouted a second pair of arms. The Elder Teachings said the strongest Guardian had six, so he still had a ways to go, but it was a hell of a start.

Lewis, on the other hand, hadn't progressed quite as fast as them, but his movement techniques had become ridiculously smooth. He could sneak up on an opponent without even casting a shadow and vanish without a trace.

Their rapid progress had them feeling pretty good about their standing in the Elite House. They were confident it wouldn't be long before they were ranked among the best of the best.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

But seeing Zane in the arena made every achievement they were so proud of feel like a joke.

They’d spent the whole trip to Oracle City talking about the tournament, sizing up the strongest disciples from each sect. Lewis, whose speed had given him an edge on their last mission, had been feeling especially confident after raking in a ton of Aether crystals.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING ARENA CROWD]

"Don't get so worked up," Finn said, putting a hand on Lewis's shoulder and nodding toward a glowing sign. "The odds are out. And they're crazy high against Zane. I'm putting money on him!"

Finn wasn't much of a gambler, but he had a hunch about Zane—luck always seemed to find him. He was about to test that theory.

"No way!" Lewis yelled, clutching his Storage Ring protectively.

"Give 'em to me," Finn said coldly.

"No!" The two started wrestling over the ring.

[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]

A faint breeze whispered past, and suddenly the tip of Finn's sword was pressed against Lewis's neck. Again.

Lewis let out a long-suffering sigh. He was never going to win an argument with Finn, not as long as a blade to the throat was considered fair game. With a defeated groan, he had no choice but to hand over his winnings.

[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER DISSOLVING]

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

The moment Finn placed his bet, the glowing barrier in the center of the arena vanished.

The battle between Zane Lowell and Brody Booth began.
