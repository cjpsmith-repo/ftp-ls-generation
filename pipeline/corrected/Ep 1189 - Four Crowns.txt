Ep 1189 - Four Crowns

[MUSIC: TENSE AFTERMATH]
Of the five Supreme Lords, only Magnus was left standing. 
[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]
His energy was shot, his face a ghostly white. Even for a mid-rank Supreme Lord—a little stronger than the other four, sure—strength meant nothing in a free-for-all like that. He knew he was only alive because of a sliver of dumb luck.
Magnus stared at the single grain of sand on the ground, a cold knot of fear tightening in his gut. He had to hand it to Zane; with a weapon like the Elysian Keep, the kid had every right to be cocky.
The other Supreme Lords kept their distance from that tiny speck, terrified of being caught again in its strange, life-draining tether.
Supreme Lord Ranell, however, was furious. He’d jumped in earlier to try and save Addison, a move that had failed spectacularly and left him badly wounded. 
But instead of backing off like the others, Ranell stalked right up to the grain of sand. 
A cold sneer twisted his face. “The Desperate Melee is incredible, I’ll give you that. But since I’m the only one here, you can’t use it on me, can you?”
He was testing the waters. Ranell knew how these kinds of spells worked—it was just a magical cage match, forcing everyone inside to fight to the death. They weren’t unheard of in the upper world. The Ogres used their Hundred-in-One Array to forge elite warriors, throwing in a hundred soldiers and waiting for the one survivor to emerge, having absorbed the life force of the other ninety-nine. The Blood Spirit Race had their Slaughter Feast Array, which did the same thing.
But those spells were never supposed to work on beings as powerful as World Lords, let alone Supreme Lords. And yet, this one’s energy tether was inescapable. It was utterly unbelievable.
From inside his sanctuary, Zane just smiled at the arrogant lord.
“You’re right, the Desperate Melee doesn’t work on just one person,” Zane said, his voice echoing from the sand. “So what’s the plan? You just going to stand there and guard a speck of dust for the rest of your life?”
Ranell snorted. “A nobody from the Soul Sea Realm like you isn’t worth my time. I’ll just have a few World Lords stand guard in shifts. You’ll be trapped in that little keep of yours forever.”
Hearing this, the other Supreme Lords nodded. Tricking them into killing four of their own was no small crime. Zane had to be contained.
But even as they agreed, their minds were already elsewhere.
[MUSIC: TENSE UNDERCURRENT]
Four Supreme Lords were dead. And when a Supreme Lord died, their Crown of Destiny didn’t die with them — it detached, drifted free, and scattered to some random corner of the universe, waiting for a new bearer. Four Crowns were now loose in the universe. Four positions, suddenly open.
That was what mattered. That was why, one by one, the assembled Lords were already making their excuses and preparing to leave. Posting a guard here was a small matter. Finding those Crowns before anyone else did — that was everything.
Even within their own factions, the competition would be savage. In the Demon Night alone, thousands of World Lords would be jostling for any chance at a Crown. The same was true for every other race represented here. No Supreme Lord could afford to stay and babysit a grain of sand while that race was happening.
Within a few hours, the assembled Supreme Lords were gone. Each had pledged to send a World Lord to stand watch. Then they left — without another word, without looking back.
The Queen of Punishment was the last to go. She said nothing. She simply turned and disappeared into the void.
Maverick, meanwhile, made sure to stay a few hundred miles away from Tanya. If the light from that spell flared up again, it would lock onto them both, and only one would be walking away.
Maverick and Tanya were World Lords — and World Lords had ambitions. With four Crowns of Destiny now adrift in the universe, every World Lord of sufficient strength had a chance, however small, to claim one. Being posted here, standing guard over a grain of sand while that window was open, was not something either of them had signed up for. But the Queen had given the order. They couldn’t refuse.
They kept their distance from each other and said nothing about it.
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL ASCENDING]
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY HUMMING]
Just then, a soft glow bloomed on the surface of the grain of sand. 
[SFX: SPINNING WHOOSH ACCELERATING]
It began to spin, faster and faster, expanding until it was a palm-sized model of The Elysian Keep. 
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING EXPANSION]
The miniature fortress just kept growing, and Maverick stared, not daring to blink, as the tiny object swelled back to its original, massive size.
He and Tanya exchanged a long look across the distance—a silent, shared sigh of awe and frustration. To think a treasure like that actually existed in the universe.
[SFX: MAGICAL EXPANSION WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: EPIC CHASE]
The moment it reached its full size, the Elysian Keep shot off, blazing a trail north.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS RUNNING]
Maverick and Tanya glanced at each other one last time before giving chase. Their mission was never to destroy the keep, just to make sure Zane stayed locked inside it. Wherever it went, they would follow.
[MUSIC: MEDITATIVE CALM]
[AMBIENT: INTERIOR FORTRESS QUIET]
Inside, Zane had already slipped into a meditative state, beginning a new round of cultivation. The Elysian Keep wasn’t powered by his own strength, but by his master’s. He couldn’t hide in here forever.
Ranell’s threat had put him in a real bind. If the Supreme Lords really posted World Lords to stand guard outside, he’d be cut off from the world, trapped for good. He saw no way out. If he stepped outside, the two Demon Night World Lords would kill him instantly. All he could do was keep the fortress moving toward the Northern Sky Region and hope he could figure something out.
But just as he settled into his meditation, another voice suddenly echoed through the keep.
