Ep 369 - The Cliff

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

From his hidden vantage point, Zane observed the Cloud Road disciples with careful attention. He'd already pinpointed the critical weakness in their formation—now patience would be his greatest weapon.

The young disciple in the white robe was strong, Zane had to admit. Under different circumstances, he might have challenged him directly. But the other twelve disciples had already sprung their trap, activating the Formation of the Twelve Signs the moment he'd entered the valley. He'd been their first target—caught before he could even assess the terrain.

A bitter smile crossed his face. Talk about rotten luck.

Still, formations like theirs took enormous physical and spiritual energy. No group, however skilled, could maintain it indefinitely. The knowledge settled his racing heart. If he remained smart, kept his distance, and waited for the right moment, he'd survive this. More than survive—he'd find his opening.

He just needed to be patient. Let them exhaust themselves. Then he'd make his move.

[SFX: BATTLE SOUNDS]

[AMBIENT: CREATURE SWARM]

Meanwhile, Lennon Anderson directed the twelve disciples to continue their relentless assault on the Ghost-Faced Spiders. The creatures swarmed across the small island like a living tide, covering almost every inch of stone and earth. Yet each one that ventured too close met the same fate—struck down the instant it touched the Formation's radiant barrier. Their corpses piled high at the perimeter, a grotesque testament to the Formation's devastating power.

After about fifteen minutes of sustained combat, the wave of attackers began to thin out. Fewer spiders emerged from the shadowed woods with each passing moment. Even creatures driven by instinct could recognize a losing battle, and the survivors finally retreated into the forest's depths.

[SFX: MAGICAL DISPERSION]

Lennon watched the last of them disappear before raising his hand, satisfaction flickering across his sharp features. "Disperse!"

[SFX: SHATTERED BARRIER]

[AMBIENT: QUIET FOREST]

[MUSIC: CALM RELIEF]

The glowing circle of Vitality beneath their feet shattered like glass and vanished into wisps of fading light. The twelve disciples rolled their wrists and stretched their ankles, their movements stiff and weary. Several leaned against nearby rocks, chests heaving as they finally caught their breath.

The Formation of the Twelve Signs was devastatingly powerful, but maintaining it took a serious toll on those who wielded it.

"So, Lennon, how do we pass this Hidden Test?" one of the disciples asked, wiping sweat from his brow. His voice carried a note of exhaustion mixed with curiosity. "Or are we just here to kill a bunch of spiders?"

Lennon cracked a small smile, his eyes gleaming with the confidence of someone who held all the answers. "Of course not. The spiders were just a warm-up—a test of our coordination." He turned his gaze upward. "The real challenge awaits us up there."

[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

The other Cloud Hall disciples followed his line of sight, their eyes drawn to the towering cliff that loomed above them like a silent sentinel. Some squinted against the wind, trying to discern what made it so special.

With a flick of his wrist, Lennon sent a swallow-shaped dart flying toward the cliff face.

[SFX: WHOOSH DART THROW]

Given Lennon's considerable strength and reputation, the disciples naturally expected the dart to sink deep into the rock with a satisfying thud.

[SFX: METALLIC DING RICOCHET]

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

Instead, the moment it touched the cliff, the dart ricocheted off a shimmering, invisible barrier with a sharp metallic ring. The projectile spun harmlessly through the air before clattering to the ground at Lennon's feet. Several disciples exchanged surprised glances, their fatigue momentarily forgotten in the face of this new mystery.

"An enchanted barrier. What’s that about?" one of the disciples asked.

This time, Lennon didn't answer. Instead, he walked over to the cliff face and began running his hand along its surface.

His fingers found something — a hidden mechanism. He pressed it.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING]
The valley shook. Caves cracked open across the cliff face, and stone platforms thrust outward from the rock in tiers.
On each platform sat a treasure chest. Red ones on the lowest tier, then blue, silver, gold — and at the very top, a single black chest. The higher the chest, the fewer there were. Several of the red and blue ones had already been opened by previous Cloud Hall disciples, their lids hanging loose.
Lennon turned to face the group. "The spiders were the warm-up. This is the real trial. Everyone picks a target and climbs. One attempt only — fall, and you're done." His eyes moved up the cliff. "The higher you go, the better the reward."
A beat of silence. Then one of the disciples stepped forward, eyeing the lowest red chest. "Isn't it just opening a box?"
Lennon said nothing.

Curious, the other disciples gathered around to see what he was up to. 

Zane watched from a distance, safe in his hiding spot.

[SFX: POWERFUL WIND GUST]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC DANGER]

Suddenly, a powerful gust of wind suddenly blasted out of the cave, hitting one of the disciples and throwing him off balance. He reacted fast, trying to use a technique to anchor himself, but it was no use. The wind just kept shoving him toward the edge.

[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Realizing the technique was useless, he dropped flat and clung to a rock jutting out from the platform. It helped ease the pressure, but the wind was still too strong for him to even lift his head. All he could do was grip the rock platform with both hands, his knuckles turning white from the strain.

This was a rare chance to be part of the test on the Cloud Road, and no one wanted to let it slip away. The disciple was determined to get that treasure chest. He gritted his teeth, fighting against the gale.

Zane just shook his head. Yeah, he’s not gonna make it.

Lennon shook his head too, though he didn't look surprised. He'd expected the first one to fail.

After struggling for a good thirty seconds, the disciple's grip gave out, and the wind sent him tumbling off the rock platform.

[SFX: LOUD FALL]

His fellow Cloud Hall disciples rushed to his side, helping him to his feet with steadying hands on his shoulders and back.

Despite the violent tumble, the disciple appeared more frustrated than injured. His cultivation at the Soul Realm level had fortified his body well beyond ordinary human limits—a fall that would cripple a mortal merely bruised his pride.

He shook off their concerned grips, his jaw set with stubborn determination. "I'm going again!" His voice carried an edge of desperation that made the others exchange uneasy glances. "I refuse to lose like that!"

The conviction in his tone suggested he'd already rewritten the narrative in his mind—the wind had simply caught him unprepared, that was all. Next time would be different. 

His pride demanded nothing less.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED DRUMS]

Before anyone could voice their doubts or offer cautionary words, he was already sprinting back toward the precipice.

Zane braced himself. This wasn't going to end well.
