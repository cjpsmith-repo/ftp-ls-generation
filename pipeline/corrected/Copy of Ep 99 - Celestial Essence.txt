Ep 99 - Celestial Essence
Word count: 1244
[AMBIENT: AUCTION HALL MURMURS]
Zane didn't have enough Crystal Cubes on him, but he remembered what Dustin had told him: he could still bid on anything he wanted. As long as the final price didn't top what he'd make from the Phoenix Gem, he was covered.
"Twenty thousand, one hundred Crystal Cubes!" Zane called out, his voice cutting through the stunned silence of the auction hall as he re-entered the bidding against Felix when the Principles of Forging appeared.
Prior to his confrontation on Infernal Peak, Zane would have hesitated at the thought of investing such a vast fortune in a single book. Every Crystal Cube represented a step closer to freeing Elara; he would have meticulously hoarded each one, calculating their exact value when exchanged for Points at the Skyreach Sect. The old Zane would have considered such an extravagant purchase foolish, perhaps even selfish.
But the battle on Infernal Peak had shown him just how wide the gap was between his skills and Adrian's. If he ever wanted to close it, he had to get better in every way imaginable—not just stronger.
If he could learn Weapon Forging—hell, if he could become a Master Weaponsmith—they might not refuse his request to see Elara, or even save her. Of course, there was no guarantee this one book would make him a master. But what other choice did he have? He had to stick to the plan, and if the "Principles of Forging" could give him an edge, he was damn well going to get it.
"Don't worry, you two," Zane said with a reassuring smile. "I've got this covered."
Ethan and Rhys exchanged a worried frown, wondering where Zane had suddenly come up with tens of thousands of Crystal Cubes. But then they remembered he was never reckless, and that helped them relax a little.
[SFX: CROWD WHISPERS]
Heads turned in their private room's direction as Zane's bid echoed through the hall. The murmurs grew when they noticed the beautiful Nessa standing with them. Zane, however, simply turned his head and asked her calmly, "What do you want?"
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
"My master sent me to tell you to drop out of the bidding," Nessa said, her eyes locked on his with unwavering intensity, her posture rigid with practiced authority.
A small smile played on Zane's lips as he measured her demand. "And why would I do that?" he asked, leaning slightly forward, genuinely curious about the audacity behind her request.
"Because my master wants you to," she replied, a malicious glint sparking in her eyes. Her voice carried the confidence of someone accustomed to others yielding without question, her fingers tapping impatiently against the railing as she awaited his compliance.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
The arrogance. It radiated from her like heat from a forge, suffocating in its presumption. Zane felt a familiar tightness in his chest—the same sensation he experienced whenever someone attempted to dictate his choices through nothing but inherited privilege.
Zane's brow furrowed. Ever since joining the Skyreach Sect, he'd run into more than his fair share of people like this. He scoffed. "Your master? And who's that?"
"My master is of House Sterling," Nessa replied, puffing up with pride. The name was famous throughout the Empire, and she clearly expected it to be enough to make Zane back down.
"Sterling?" Zane murmured, his expression turning grim. From Bloody Mountain to the Sunbelt, it seemed he couldn't shake them. Members of House Sterling were like leeches, always finding a way to latch on.
"That's right. My master is Felix Sterling. I'm sure you've heard the name," Nessa said, a smirk tugging at her lips. "So I'd advise you not to bid again. It would be a shame if you ran into some... trouble."
"Ah, Felix Sterling!" Zane nodded, a look of mock recognition on his face. "Isn't he the one they call 'Madman' Felix?"
Nessa's smirk widened. "So you "have" heard of him."
"Tell him to drop dead," Zane shot back, his voice dripping with contempt.
[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]
Nessa froze, the grin plastered on her face. She'd clearly assumed he would back down the second she mentioned her master. She never imagined he'd refuse so bluntly, or with such disrespect. This boy... he wasn't afraid of Felix at all.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
A flicker of Vitality radiated from her. Nessa's straight hair began to writhe and surge like dark ocean waves, twisting itself into five sharp, spiraling points. An instant later, all five points shot toward Zane like spinning drill bits.
"Everyone who crosses my master ends up dead," she hissed. "Do you want to be next?"
Zane didn't even flinch. He just smiled and gestured toward the main auction hall. "You really want to try that in here? Take a look around. Remember where you are."
[AMBIENT: AUCTION HALL MURMURS]
[SFX: APPROACHING FOOTSTEPS]
The commotion in the private room had already drawn attention from the main hall. Noticing the tense standoff, a few House Blackwood guards started heading for their door. The Gilded Gavel had a strict no-fighting policy, and they took it very seriously.
[SFX: ANGRY FOOTSTEPS]
Nessa shot a look at the approaching guards, and her hair fell limp, the Vitality receding. With a disgusted snort, she turned and stormed out of the room.
As she walked away, Zane smirked and shouted after her, "Twenty-four thousand and one hundred!"
Hearing Zane's defiant bid and seeing the furious look on Nessa's face as she returned, Felix knew the boy hadn't taken his threat seriously at all.
"So," Felix asked, a thin smile on his face. "What did our friend have to say?"
Nessa's expression was tight. "He told you to go die."
Felix was quiet for a moment. Then he smiled — not with anger, but with something closer to amusement. He leaned back and waved a lazy hand. "Then I'm done bidding."
Nessa stared at him. "You're withdrawing? After all this?"
"If I keep pushing, those old families in the hall will climb back in and drive the price up past what it's worth. Better to let the boy have it." He glanced toward the side room, his eyes cool and calculating. "He's wearing Skyreach Sect white robes. Finding him afterward will be easy enough."
With Felix out, no one else moved to counter. The Principles of Forging fell to Zane.
"And now," Alec announced, producing a small jade bottle from the table, "something no one here was expecting today."
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL WONDER]
A single drop could upgrade a Superior Magic Weapon to the highest level. But Celestial Essence was impossibly rare; no one knew how to acquire it in any real quantity. Any time even a trace was found, its new owner would immediately bond it with their weapon. It was said to be the fundamental component of all legendary arms—from Mysterious Weapons to Soul Armaments, and even Mythic Weapons.
The secret to their tremendous power was that Master Weaponsmiths infused them with a spark of the Celestial Law during forging. Celestial Essence was that Celestial Law, pure and in staggeringly high concentrations. Rumor had it that even a Basic Enchanted Weapon might hold a trace of Celestial Essence, but to this day, no one had ever figured out how to extract it.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT]
[SFX: SHIMMERING PARTICLES]
Zane stared, stunned, at the Celestial Essence. As the droplets danced in the air, shimmering in the light, he could feel his heart begin to pound against his ribs. He leaned forward, eyes fixed on the floating silver particles.
Weren't these the exact same particles as the Celestial Essence his Black Fire produced whenever it melted down a mysterious weapon?
