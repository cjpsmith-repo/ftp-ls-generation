Ep 638 - The Enemy Overhead
Word count: 1086
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Lavender’s spear pulsed with invisible waves of energy, crackling with power. She aimed, and the waves shot straight for the strange bird.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE IMPACT]
The spear’s special ability was a single, devastatingly powerful strike. Lavender poured everything she had into its tip, making its piercing power absolutely terrifying. As the energy hit the Shadow-maw, it punched clean through the creature’s tough, seemingly unbreakable hide.
“It’s useless,” one of the senior War Lords muttered, shaking his head as he ran for his life.
Next to the Shadow-maw’s massive body, the spear was just a needle. He figured a pinprick like that wouldn’t kill it—it would just piss the thing off.
[SFX: VITALITY ERUPTION]
At the same time, Fabian and Jarrod flanked Zane, one on his left and one on his right, black and white Vitality erupting from their bodies. When they fought together, their power multiplied, and right now, their only job was to protect Zane, even if it meant throwing themselves in harm’s way.
They knew Lavender was special, but they weren’t about to bet Zane’s life on it. She was his sword spirit, after all. Since their fates were tied, their power levels couldn’t be that far apart. How could she possibly stop that bird’s attack on her own?
After thrusting her spear, Lavender gave her wrist a gentle twist.
[SFX: BONES CRACKING]
A sickening, explosive crack echoed in everyone’s ears, loud as a thousand bones shattering at once.
[SFX: MASSIVE CREATURE COLLAPSING]
Everyone looked up just in time to see the skin of the hundred-foot-long Shadow-maw ripple and collapse inward. Its massive body looked like it was being wrung out by a giant, invisible pair of hands, crumpling into a grotesque, twisted heap.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[SFX: CRASHING THROUGH BUSHES]
The bird dropped from the sky, crashing to the ground and tumbling into the nearby bushes.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
Even the War Lords, veterans of countless battles, stopped dead in their tracks, their jaws slack with disbelief.
Zane had been giving Lavender plenty of chances to practice in the arena lately, and every time she won, he absorbed their opponent’s Lucky Light. All the top-tier War Lords here had seen glimpses of her mysterious spear arts before. A few from the fourteenth floor of the Tower of Sin had even challenged her just to get a closer look at her famous techniques. They all knew she was a powerful fighter, but they never dreamed she was this incredible.
When Fabian and Jarrod teamed up, they could hold their own against a rookie War Sage. With enough power, they probably could have killed the huge bird, too—but not with the clean, effortless grace Lavender had just displayed.
“Nice move!” Zane praised, not breaking his stride.
Lavender gave a single nod, expression unchanged, and fell in close behind him.
[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE]
[SFX: MONSTER SQUAWKS]
[MUSIC: RISING DREAD]
The situation wasn't improving. Every commotion drew more of them. The Shadow-maws kept coming, circling higher before diving again, and the group's numbers were already thinning.
Fabian's scalp prickled as he counted the shapes above. He felt sick.
He'd known this was a bad idea. He should have refused Zane entry — should have risked the argument, risked the insult, done whatever it took to keep him out of this cave. It was too late for that now. And the worst part was, he understood the math clearly: it didn't matter if every last one of them died, himself included. As long as Zane walked out alive, the humans kept their position in the Tower. But in this chaos, could he guarantee even that?
Fabian didn't have an answer.
[SFX: MONSTROUS SQUAWK]
[MUSIC: SUDDEN ATTACK SURGE]
From the side, more than ten Shadow-maws peeled off and dove at once. The sweep of their wings covered Zane and the entire group.
"This is bad!" Fabian cried out. He called to Jarrod and spun around, ready to throw himself into it.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
Zane turned too, his eyes calm. Faint starlight began to gather around his body as the Vitality in his core started to spin. Above his head, energy accumulated in a slow, building pulse.
Lavender's spear swung upward. "We can't run," she said quietly. "Deal with them here."
Zane's fist filled with force. He judged the distance, waited — then swung.
"Titan Strike!"
[SFX: MASSIVE WING SNAP]
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[SFX: CRACKLING ENERGY]
[AMBIENT: WIND RUSH]
The air tore apart around his fist with a sharp scream, his sleeve burning from the friction. The surging energy converged into a massive spiral that shot upward — and caught the nearest Shadow-maw dead in its spin. The bird's enormous wings began to curl and crack under the rotation, folding inward as it was dragged down.
By the time it reached him, its wings were mangled and wrapped tightly around its body. The creature fell like a giant stick, its beak, dozens of feet long, plunging into the ground right behind him.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PAUSE]
Even though it was a terrible time to be distracted, many of the War Lords were stunned. Wasn’t that punch a little too powerful? What kind of fighting style was that?
Zane’s victory was a huge morale boost. If a ninth-level War General like him could take down a Shadow-maw like it was nothing, then they, as high-ranking War Lords, sure as hell couldn’t do any worse.
[SFX: WOODEN CLOGS CLATTERING]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
Yolande, who had kept a steady ten feet from Zane the entire time, now stepped forward, her wooden clogs clattering on the ground as she calmly faced another Shadow-maw. With a slight twitch of her fingers, two thin beams of mysterious light shot from her fingertips, striking the creature squarely in its huge eyes.
[SFX: CREATURE CRASH]
[AMBIENT: DUST AND DEBRIS]
Blinded, the bird instantly lost its bearings. It flapped its wings wildly before crashing headfirst into the dirt, thrashing around and kicking up clouds of dust and gravel. Its powerful wings carved deep trenches in the earth.
But no one had time to worry about that one. A single blinded Shadow-maw on the ground wasn’t the threat; the flock still circling overhead was.
[SFX: MULTIPLE EXPLOSIONS]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
The War Lords attacked at once. Explosions of colorful energy and flashes of Vitality lit up the air.
Working together, they managed to kill a dozen of the fierce creatures, but not before two of their own were snatched away into the sky.
