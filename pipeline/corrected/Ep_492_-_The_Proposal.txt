Ep 492 - The Proposal

Word count: 1149

[SFX: ICE SHARDS]

[MUSIC: MOODY MYSTERY]

Eden casually brushed away the shards of mysterious ice. He lifted the shimmering crown, a slight smile playing on his lips as he admired it. "This is a gift for the head of Cloud Hall!"

[AMBIENT: HUSHED CROWD MURMUR]

He turned to smile at Tia, then looked down proudly at the sparkling circlet in his hands. Three stunning crystals were embedded in the delicate frame. Instead of reflecting light like normal gems, these brilliant white crystals seemed to drink it in, glowing with a soft, silvery light. Three feathers were attached to the side.

A crown of ordinary gold and gems would be worthless to a warrior. After all, a few Aether crystals could buy a mountain of gold and gemstones. Even a tiny shard of the mysterious ice was worth ten times what an ornate crown like this would normally cost.

But the fact that Eden sealed it in mysterious ice meant its value had to be equal to, if not greater than, the ice itself.

Most of the warriors in the crowd had no idea what it was and just stared in confusion.

[SFX: CROWD GASP]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]

But with so many people there, someone was bound to recognize it. Sure enough, a voice cried out from the crowd, "Whoa! That's the Heavenly Feather Crown from the Serenity Palace!"

"The Serenity Palace? You mean the one from the Arctic Lands? Didn't that place get destroyed ages ago?"

"Oh, right! I remember now. I've seen a picture of the Heavenly Feather Crown. It's a Relic-Class weapon!"

"Before her defeat, the Holy Maiden of the Serenity Palace had plenty of Relic-Class weapons," someone explained. "When the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms crushed her army, they divided her arsenal among themselves—but the Heavenly Feather Crown vanished. They tore the Palace apart searching for it and found nothing. Incredible that Eden possesses it now!"

[AMBIENT: GRAND ARENA]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

Mythic weapons were common enough throughout the Heartlands. Every leader of a sect had a high-grade Mythic weapon, but Relic-Class weapons were incredibly rare.

At this point, there were only three known Relic-Class weapons in the Heartlands. One was the Soul Flag, held by the Ethereal Spirit Sect. The World Merchants' Guild had the Time Copper Coin, and Maddox himself possessed the Nameless Saber.

These were the only confirmed Relic-Class weapons. Who knew if there were others hidden away in the region, or who might be holding them?

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

A single Relic-Class weapon held enough power to determine whether an entire sect would rise to glory or crumble into dust.

Warriors believed these legendary artifacts ushered in eras of great fortune, as though each carried a divine blessing woven into its very essence. Even if never drawn in battle, merely enshrining such a weapon within a sect's halls was said to attract prosperity and good luck for generations.

[AMBIENT: ANGRY CROWD MURMURS]

The warriors packed into the stands understood exactly what Eden was attempting, and fury rippled through their ranks. His calculated proposal wasn't just arrogant—it was an insult to everything Cloud Hall represented.

Even though these warriors weren't the strongest, they could see what was happening, and they were all on the same page: Eden was being shameless. Soon, the air was filled with their insults.

"Just because your father's Maddox doesn't mean every woman in the world is yours for the taking! Shameless!"

"He's dreaming if he thinks Tia will say yes just for a Relic-Class weapon!" another voice rang out, thick with contempt.

"It's a sly move, though," someone else admitted grudgingly. "He offers up a Relic-Class weapon, and in return, he gets Tia and control of Cloud Hall. Not a bad trade for him!"

The crowd's anger intensified. Eden wasn't proposing an alliance—he was attempting to purchase Cloud Hall's future, treating their most talented disciple like a commodity to be bartered. The sheer audacity of it made their blood boil. Several voices hurled vicious insults.

Eden's head snapped toward the crowd, his eyes blazing with cold fury. "What's that filth spewing from your mouths? Do you have a death wish?"

"We're talking about you!" someone shouted back, emboldened by the surrounding warriors. "What will you do about it? Shameless bastard!"

Another voice cut through the tension. "You really believe you're the cleverest man here, don't you? That the rest of us are fools dancing to your tune?"

Eden's jaw tightened. The casual arrogance that had colored his proposal moments ago evaporated, replaced by something darker and more dangerous.

"Your father earned his power through blood and strength," a grizzled veteran called out. "He built his reputation with his own hands. Without him standing behind you, who the hell are you? Just another spoiled princeling playing at greatness."

The words struck deeper than any blade. Eden's face flushed crimson, a vein pulsing at his temple. His entire body radiated barely contained rage—the fury of a man whose carefully constructed image had been stripped away in front of hundreds of witnesses.

[AMBIENT: TOURNAMENT CROWD NOISE]

Even with Maddox right there, the warriors were betting that Eden wouldn't dare start a fight. This was the Martial Tournament, after all. They couldn't risk starting a massacre in front of hundreds of thousands of people. The World Merchants' Guild would never let it slide. So the crowd kept yelling, completely unafraid.

Eden snuck a look at Tia to see what she thought of his offer.

[MUSIC: COLD TENSION]

A faint smile touched Tia's lips as she glanced toward Maddox. Then her gaze fell on Eden, her expression turning icy. "Did your father put you up to this? Wasn't taking Elara from us enough? What do you take Cloud Hall for? A marketplace?" She paused, letting the silence stretch. "And do you really think you're worthy of me?"

The memory surfaced unbidden—Maddox's demand that they send Elara to him. Cloud Hall's leadership had deliberated for days, weighing honor against survival. In the end, not wanting to make an enemy of the most powerful man in the world, they had reluctantly agreed.

The decision had ignited a massive fight between Tia and Zane. She could still see the betrayal in his eyes; he'd trusted her to protect Elara, and she had failed him. The guilt gnawed at her constantly, a wound that wouldn't heal. She'd replayed that conversation a thousand times, searching for words she might have used to change the outcome.

She just never imagined that Maddox's own son would have the audacity to show up and propose to her. The sheer arrogance of it—as if the Maddox family could simply take whatever they wanted from Cloud Hall, first Elara, now her. As if she were some prize to be claimed, another trophy for their collection.

It was the biggest joke she'd ever heard, though the humor was bitter as poison on her tongue.
