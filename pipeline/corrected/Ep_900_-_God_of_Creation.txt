Ep 900 - God of Creation

Word count: 1203

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane’s soul slipped into the girl’s mind, and the world outside went silent. The Soul Core didn’t trigger any alarms, and he wasn’t immediately ejected. So far, so good.

Outside, the other masters held their breath. The union had scoured the necropolis, yet the woman in the coffin remained a complete mystery. Whether living or dead, her voice revealed nothing. Taking her body was a massive risk, but Zane had committed the moment he shattered that Crystal Coffin.

Still, she’d been locked away for an impossibly long time. Even a master of the Spirit Supreme Realm should have had her lifespan run out ages ago, unable to escape death despite the Five Aging Processes. After all, not even the greatest masters in the universe could outrun their own mortality; some just lasted longer than others.

That was why the risk was worth it. War King Wind, the Emperor of Harlen, and War King York had all been ready to make the same gamble, but Zane had been faster, seizing the body before anyone else could.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

As Zane’s soul vanished into the girl, all movement around the coffin ceased. The masters grew anxious, waiting to see what would happen next. A few of them, piloting their own commandeered corpses, crept closer for a better look.

The nearest one was only a few feet away when he leaned in.

[SFX: DRAMATIC WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: INTENSE PERCUSSION]

In that instant, the girl’s eyes snapped open.

The masters were all possessing the bodies of powerful warriors, their reflexes honed and their strength partially restored by Soul Cores. But it wasn’t enough. The one who’d gotten too close had no time to escape.

The girl’s eyes were sharp, her pupils a piercing gold.

[SFX: ENERGY BEAM]

A beam of golden light shot from them, enveloping the master, who immediately cried out in agony. Cracks began to spiderweb across his corpse’s skin, spreading like fissures in a dry riverbed during an earthquake. The body, preserved for ten thousand years, was bone-dry; when it finally crumbled, no blood flowed out.

Instead, the master’s soul was violently ejected from the disintegrating corpse.

[SFX: SOUL SCREAM]

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]

The powerful Fighting Soul howled in the open air, trapped in the girl’s gaze. But under that golden light, it simply melted away like ice on a hot summer day.

[SFX: DISSOLVING ECHO]

[MUSIC: DARK FINALE]

In the blink of an eye, it was gone.

[SFX: GASPING CROWD]

“My god… she’s alive!”

“Fall back! Everyone, get back now!”

[SFX: PANICKED FOOTSTEPS]

Dozens of masters scrambled backward, Callan, Randy, and the others among them, their faces etched with terror. Several voiced what everyone was thinking — they shouldn’t have let Zane go in there; if he died here, everything was lost.

The girl slowly closed her golden eyes. The tension in the room dropped by a fraction.

The Emperor turned to War King Wind. “Wait,” he said.

War King Wind looked at him.

“Zane opened the Greater Lotus Flower,” the Emperor said quietly. “Someone with that destiny doesn’t fall in the lower realm. It doesn’t follow. We wait.”

War King Wind nodded slowly. The other masters held their positions.

There were still a few days before the blood sacrifice power in the Sacred Feather Sea recovered fully. They could afford to wait.

[AMBIENT: VOID SILENCE]

[MUSIC: ETHEREAL DRONE]

Meanwhile, Zane was completely unaware of the chaos outside. Once his soul was pulled inside her mind, he found himself floating in a pitch-black void. The darkness was absolute, a perfect nothingness without a single point of light.

Where am I? he thought, trying to get his bearings.

[SFX: MAGICAL EXPANSION]

Then, a single point of light appeared in the darkness, expanding with impossible speed until it formed a vast, empty continent below him.

“An island?” Zane wondered, observing the raw, freshly formed land. “It looks like it was just created.”

[SFX: ETHEREAL ECHO]

“It was,” the girl’s voice echoed out of the darkness. “I just made it. Isn’t it neat?”

“You created it?” Zane was stunned. “But I just watched it happen… it appeared in an instant.”

She giggled. “Of course. This is my Inner World. I can do anything I want in here.”

The truth hit Zane like a physical blow. 

He was trapped inside her Inner World.

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL WONDER]

“You created a world?” he asked, his voice full of awe.

“Yes,” she said. “But this is just a barren continent. To make it a real world, you need the right ingredients. Bursts of vitality have to strike the land, creating a Sea of Vitality where the first, low-level creatures can appear.” 

She went on, her tone turning professorial. “From there, life slowly evolves. It can take hundreds, thousands, even tens of thousands of years for the world to really come alive.”

She finished with a soft sigh. “It took me one hundred and twenty thousand years to nurture this continent and all the creatures on it.”

“A hundred and twenty thousand years… for that?” Zane was floored. He’d expected the process to be much faster.

The girl seemed to shake her head with a smile. “You don’t understand. The creation speed of an Inner World is incredibly fast. The birth of the very first, most insignificant creature might take a hundred thousand years, but after that, it only takes a few thousand for civilization to grow and prosper.”

As she spoke, a bolt of lightning struck the sea in the distance. The entire inner world shuddered. Zane flinched.

“Don’t be afraid,” the girl said. “It’s not strong — it just feels that way in here. Lightning is how I feed this world. The Crystal Coffin couldn’t draw it in directly. I had to rely on the chains during thunderstorms. It was very slow.”

She led him toward the center of the island. A primitive tribe came into view. The moment the girl appeared, an old man in a skull-draped robe crawled forward waving a bone staff, and the entire tribe dropped to their knees.

“They call me the God of Creation,” the girl said, with a small, satisfied smile. “I think that’s about right.”

“Oh, I see,” Zane said, a little lost. He didn’t sense any real malice from her, so he carefully ventured, “But why did you lock me up in here?”

[MUSIC: PLAYFUL MYSTERIOUS]

To his complete surprise, she just smiled. “Creating a world is so boring when you’re all alone,” she said. “You should stay and do it with me. Won’t that be fun?”

Zane was speechless.

He wanted to argue that it sounded like the opposite of fun, but he held his tongue. “My soul is separated from my body,” he said after a moment. “It’s still out there. I can be away for a few days, but any longer than that… I’m not sure what will happen.”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]

“That’s not a problem,” she pouted. “My soul is disconnected from my body all the time. See? My body is fine, and so am I. I have a way to make your soul immortal—a secret I inherited from the Soul Race. Want me to teach you?”
