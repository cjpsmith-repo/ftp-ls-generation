Ep 923 - Sealed Shut

Word count: 1046

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The warriors from the Seven Divine Kingdoms knew they were lambs to the slaughter. 

Frozen in place by the Demi-Immortals, they were completely powerless, able to do nothing but watch. Once the Evil Dragon was dealt with, they’d be next. Why would these powerful beings bother letting them go? The old saying had never felt so true: the weak are prey for the strong. To these Demi-Immortals, they were nothing more than ants.

Trapped and helpless, each warrior faced their impending doom differently.

Many, like War King York, simply couldn’t accept it. He’d already drawn the short straw once, used as live bait to lure out the Evil Dragon. By some miracle, the beast hadn’t swallowed him whole, and he’d scrambled back to safety. Now, with a second lease on life he didn’t intend to waste, York desperately searched for an escape, any escape at all.

Others, like War King Wind, had already made peace with their own deaths. His only regret was a crushing guilt for dragging Fiona and the crown prince into this mess. No parent should have to watch their children die. Fiona, however, couldn’t have cared less about her own fate. Her only thought was for Zane—had he gotten away?

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

A horrifying roar ripped through the air as the Evil Dragon twisted its long, serpentine body, trying to claw its way back into the massive crater it had emerged from.

“Don’t let it get in there! Stop it!” someone yelled.

[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]

Vorn, the man in the silver mask, moved first. He raised a gnarled walking stick and shot forward, planting himself at the lip of the crater to block the dragon’s path.

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

[SFX: FIRE BLAST]

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIELD]

The Evil Dragon’s caution shifted to aggression as it unleashed a torrent of fire directly at him. Anyone else would have panicked, but behind the silver mask, Vorn’s expression was unreadable. He simply held out his walking stick, and an oddly shaped crystal shield materialized out of thin air to absorb the fiery blast. 

Seeing its attack had failed, the dragon opened its maw, ready to swallow him whole. Though this one was just an infant, a fully grown dragon was said to be large enough to encircle a continent.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]

Undeterred, Vorn drew a slow circle in the air with the tip of his walking stick, tracing a faint line that hung there like a thread. His voice dropped to a murmur. “The sky is spinning, and the earth is spinning. Go down.”

[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]

[SFX: GRAVITY SHIFT]

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

The moment the Evil Dragon's body made contact with that line, it flipped. Not from force — from something more fundamental than force. One instant, it was lunging; the next, an invisible hand had seized its enormous frame and hurled it sideways.

The dragon hit the ground like a falling mountain.

[SFX: EARTHQUAKE RUMBLE]

[SFX: ROCKS FALLING]

[SFX: BODIES HITTING GROUND]

The impact sent shockwaves rippling outward. The ground heaved in waves. Every Soul Sea master in the area lost their footing — knocked flat, pelted by rocks thrown up from the churning earth. The corpses they occupied absorbed the worst of it, shattering stones on impact, protecting the souls within.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

“Even with my own luck boosted,” Vorn sighed, a bitter edge to his voice, “it seems someone out there has me beat.”

As a core member of the Demi-Immortal Race and a silver-titled veteran of the Tower of Illusion, Vorn had lived long enough to become a firm believer in destiny. So when he'd seen Brianna's body escape with the newly hatched Ancestral Dragon, his first reaction wasn't anger, but a weary, bitter resignation.

The Evil Dragon, unhurt despite the fall, realized it couldn't reach its lair. It curled in on itself. Dust began to drift down around its body — slowly at first, then thicker.

“Stop it!” Vorn's voice sharpened. He rushed forward.

The burly man was already there. He spun his scythe and drove it straight into the wall of falling dust.

[SFX: SCYTHE SCRAPING]

The blade skipped off without cutting through. He swung again. Another spark. The dust held like steel.

The others arrived and fared no better. Vorn floated up and touched the edge of the cloud with his walking stick. He was quiet for a moment.

“It's sealed itself,” he said. “This is an instinct — something super divine beasts are born with. We can't break it from the outside.”

“How long?” one of the others asked.

“Not forever. But that's not the problem.” Vorn's voice was flat. “While it's sealed, it undergoes its fourth transformation. When it comes out, it won't be something the seven of us can handle.”

A silence fell over the group.

Vorn glanced toward the horizon. Three figures were approaching. 

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TRANSITION]

“Leave the dragon for now,” he said quietly. “Dorothea's situation looks more troublesome.”

[SFX: HEAVY LANDING]

“Dorothea, what happened?” the burly man yelled as she landed nearby, his face a thundercloud of frustration. His eyes moved past her to Zane — and his bell-like eyes went wide. 

Beside him, Delroy's brow furrowed as he looked at Brianna, not knowing that Zane was trapped inside. “What's wrong with her? She was supposed to chase the girl down — why did she let her keep the Ancestral Dragon?”

[MUSIC: FRUSTRATED DEFEAT]

It was one loss after another. The Evil Dragon had resealed itself, making their entire effort a waste. And now the Ancestral Dragon egg was gone, snatched and hatched by some girl who’d slipped right through their fingers.

Dorothea’s eyes flashed, but she didn’t deign to answer him.

“Dorothea, what’s going on?” Vorn asked, his tone more level. “Did the Ancestral Dragon hatch because she rushed into the spire?”

She finally nodded. “Something like that.”

“But how did she get through my enchanted barrier?” the scholarly man demanded, unable to process it. “I constructed it myself.”

A small, knowing smile touched Dorothea’s lips. “She has the Black Star—a divine-class weapon. No matter how strong your barrier was, Delroy, it never stood a chance. The Law of Causality within that weapon is on a whole other level than yours.”
