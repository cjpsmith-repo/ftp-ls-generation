Ep 446 - Rise and Grind

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CAVE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

The Cobalt Dragon laughed. "The Owl Beast's spirit is trapped by the Soul Shackle. It's made the thing incredibly weak—almost as weak as I am. All you have to do is break the lock. I'll handle the rest."

Zane listened, but his expression soured. "And how are you gonna do that? If you just absorb the Owl Beast's spirit, what am I supposed to tell everyone else?"

This spirit had a Nightjade Fighting Soul; it wasn't some minor ghost you could just make disappear. If Zane absorbed it in front of all these warriors, he'd never be able to explain himself.

[MUSIC: DARK SCHEMING]

"Don't worry about them," the dragon chuckled. "Just cause a scene. Go a little crazy. Do whatever you want, as long as it gives me the opening to absorb the spirit. Besides, you'll get something pretty great out of this, too."

A knot formed in Zane's gut. The dragon knew so much, and he spoke about the spirit with such reverence. Zane had no reason to doubt him. This thing had to be the real deal.

"What's in it for me? You have to tell me that first," Zane demanded, his voice tight with a mix of greed and fear.

[MUSIC: REVELATION]

"I plan to use part of the spirit's energy to heal myself and another part to wake up my seventh brother," the Cobalt Dragon replied, his voice brimming with confidence. "Whatever's left, we can use to forge a soul seed for you."

"Wake your seventh brother?" Zane repeated, his mind reeling. He was shocked the dragon even had a brother, let alone seven of them.

"Your body has already been refined to the level of a Spirit Weapon," the dragon continued. "Push it further and it becomes a Sacred Weapon. My seventh brother specializes in body refinement — he can make proper use of what you've built. That warm current your training produces? Right now it's just cleansing your core. It's being wasted."

Zane didn't ask what a Soul Seed was. He didn't need to. He was already moving.


[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: SOUL REALM]

He thought back to when he first met the Cobalt Dragon. Within his soul sea, nine dragons had resided—all of them slumbering in the beginning, their ancient forms coiled in silent stillness. The revelation that one might awaken stirred both excitement and apprehension inside of him.

[SFX: PSYCHIC ENERGY PULSE]

Though Zane had zero formal soul combat training, his Psyche Spike proved to be brutally effective against the lesser ghosts. He'd developed the technique through instinct and desperation rather than instruction, yet it cut through spiritual flesh with satisfying precision. The Owl Beast ghosts swarmed in abundance, but their strength disappointed him—far weaker than he'd braced himself for.

He established a rhythm: dart forward, strike down one ghost after another with focused mental blades, then retreat to the faint protective glow of the Warding Lantern before the horde could overwhelm him. The pattern became almost meditative. With each cycle, he felt his soul expanding, growing stronger and more resilient. 

From the outer circles, the other warriors watched Zane, their jealousy curdling into bitter frustration. Compared to him, their own progress was so glacial they couldn't imagine how long it would take to even reach the inner circle, let alone achieve the Fighting Soul level they all craved.

They could only watch as Zane tore his way from one circle to the next. 

[SFX: ENERGY BARRIER BREAKING]

Before long, he blew past the third circle and stepped into the fourth. The onlookers had no choice but to accept it: Zane was simply on another level. Some of them couldn't even compare.

[MUSIC: EXHAUSTED DETERMINATION]

Once he entered the fourth circle, his soul's growth slowed to a crawl. It was a grind. He fought for two straight days before his spiritual form finally flickered back out of the black sphere. After a full day of rest, he plunged back in.

[SFX: TRIUMPHANT ENERGY SURGE]

It took him three grueling days, but he finally crossed the fourth circle and entered the fifth circle. He just had to get to the sixth. Then the dragon's plan could begin.

Zane knew this wasn't the cakewalk the dragon made it out to be. The Cobalt Dragon was weak, too, no matter how much he postured. Still, the potential reward was more than worth the risk.

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL TELEPORTATION CHAMBER]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ANTICIPATION]

While Zane trained day and night, Elder Julian finally made his move. He strode to the massive teleportation array in the heart of the city and placed a message crystal inside.

[SFX: CRYSTAL ACTIVATION HUM]

The Upper World wasn't just another realm; it was a universe unto itself, its scale staggering the imagination. The Dragon-Blooded Territory was just a single speck in its ten-thousand-part expanse. The message crystal would have to travel trillions of miles to reach the Dragonblaze Hall, Julian's home. The cost was astronomical; he never would have spent it on anyone but Zane.

Lately, Elder Julian had been devouring every book he could find, trying to understand what was happening. He’d discovered the last Baptism by Laws had occurred eight thousand and four hundred years ago. The recipient was a man named Brandt, who was now the head of the Green Dragon Palace.

And now, the same miracle had happened to a young man from the Lower World, of all people.

Of course, the baptism wasn't a guarantee of greatness. A warrior's path to power was forged from a volatile mix of luck, brutal training, hard-won breakthroughs, and a mountain of resources.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

"I'm ready," the dragon's voice echoed in Zane's mind, firm and earnest. "Be careful. When I break the Soul Shackle, the soul pressure is going to spike like crazy."

As a disembodied soul, Zane couldn't take a deep breath to steady his nerves. He hunched his spiritual form, eyes locked on the colossal, slumbering spirit of the Owl Beast. 

[SFX: WHOOSH]

He counted to three in his head, then shot forward into the sixth circle.

To the onlookers, it just looked like another desperate push toward the Fighting Soul level. They had no idea he had just declared war on the Owl Beast's core spirit itself.

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY HUM]

[SFX: TELEPORTATION FLASH]

At that exact moment, the huge teleportation array in the city center blazed to life. A man in a heavy fur coat materialized in the shimmering light, flames dancing in his pupils. He stepped off the platform, his expression calm as he surveyed his surroundings. It was none other than the second leader of Dragonblaze Hall.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL ORCHESTRAL]

A jolt went through Elder Julian, who had been waiting on pins and needles. Relief washed over him as he scrambled toward the man. "Mister Nolan! It's so good to see you! I've been waiting for what feels like an eternity!"
