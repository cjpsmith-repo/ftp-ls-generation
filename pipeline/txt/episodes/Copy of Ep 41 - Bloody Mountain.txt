Ep 41 - Bloody Mountain
Word Count: 1212
[SFX: MARCHING FOOTSTEPS]
Once the disciples from the Great Houses were on their way up Bloody Mountain, it was time for the thousand ordinary disciples. They lined up and filed onto the mountain, one by one. A dozen paths snaked up the southern face, so everyone just picked one and started the trek.
Zane hung back at the end of the line, lost in thought. A subtle unease had settled over him, and his mind was racing with questions.
It wasn't that he was afraid of Caleb and House Warrick, but he couldn't wrap his head around why Caleb was going to such lengths for Perrin. Sure, Perrin had House Lowell behind him, but compared to the Great Houses in the Capital, they were just some small-time house from Castermere County. Perrin had nothing to offer Caleb that would be worth the effort.
"What the hell are they planning?" he muttered to himself. Caleb's motives just didn't add up.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
His thoughts were cut short by a shimmering band of colorful light up ahead. It danced and rippled across the path like the entrance to some fairytale.
"That's the outermost Magic Circle," another disciple said. "Once we pass through that ribbon of light, we're in the real Gauntlet." After saying his piece, he walked right into it. The light wrapped around his body for a moment, and then, in the blink of an eye, he was gone.
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL TRANSITION]
Without a second thought, Zane stepped into the light.
The world around him changed in an instant.
"So this is the Magic Circle!"
When he'd first arrived, Zane had been struck by Bloody Mountain's strange, monochrome red, right down to the plants. But inside the circle, the world exploded with color, from the towering trees to the dense patches of undergrowth.
He knew the landscape was an illusion created by the circle, but the danger was real.
He pushed deeper into the mountain, past Zone One, past the boundary where the crowds thinned and the air felt heavier. Zone Two.
SFX: PROJECTILE NEEDLES]
Suddenly, something burst from a nearby tree—a volley of steel-like needles shot straight for him.
[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCH]
Zane reacted in a flash, lunging forward and punching the tree trunk. He used the force of the blow to launch himself two feet into the air, reaching for the creature—a Lion-mane Ape.
[SFX: APE GRUNTS]
The ape was unbelievably agile. It dodged his grab, swung around the trunk, and leaped to another tree.
[MUSIC: INTENSE CHASE]
Seeing the Lion-mane Ape about to escape, Zane gave chase.
Letting a Level-two Phantom Beast get away would be humiliating. He took off after it, a blur through the woods as the ape vaulted from branch to branch.
The ape didn't have much of an offense beyond its hard, steel-like mane. But since Zane's body was as tough as any weapon, he could ignore its attacks completely.
The ape was so nimble that it was leading him on a wild goose chase through the forest, making it incredibly difficult for Zane to get a hold of it. More than once, he was inches from grabbing it, only for the creature to roll and slip out of his grasp at the last second.
[SFX: CHARGING THROUGH FOREST]
After a long chase, Zane switched tactics. He scanned the trees, saw an opening, and charged. Bracing himself, he slammed into a thick tree directly in the ape's path with all his strength.
"Timber!"
[SFX: TREE CRACKING AND FALLING]
The massive tree cracked and splintered before crashing to the ground.
A second later, the ape sailed through the air. It had been aiming for that very tree, but with its target now gone, it flew straight toward Zane.
Zane grinned. His trap had worked. The ape had nowhere to run.
[SFX: PIERCING SHRIEK]
But as it flew toward him, the Lion-mane Ape let out a piercing shriek. Its eyes glowed a deep crimson, and it let out a sharp squeal. In the next instant, its entire body flushed red as its mane bristled.
Zane immediately registered the creature's strange behavior. A sudden wave of danger washed over him. He instinctively threw his arms over his head and stumbled back in horror.
[SFX: DEAFENING EXPLOSION]
He barely took two steps before the ape's body swelled to twice its size and suddenly exploded. The sound was deafening.
When the dust settled, Zane saw a deep crater marking the spot of the explosion. The force of the blast had embedded the ape's steel-like quills all over the area—and deep into Zane's body.
"Damn," Zane muttered in surprise, pulling the needles out one by one. "Didn't expect a Level 2 Phantom to pack that kind of punch." If he hadn't jumped back, he could have been seriously injured.
The classification of Phantom Beasts was similar to humans, but the key difference was that many of them could unleash terrifying innate power when their life was on the line.
[AMBIENT: FOREST AFTERMATH]
After yanking the last needle free, Zane walked over to the crater and found a Sunstone Shard glowing in the center. He only needed thirty of these to pass—way less than the green crystals. Of course, the monsters he'd have to face for them were a lot more ferocious.
Just as he was about to pocket the shard, a voice boomed from the trees.
"Put the crystal down and get lost!"
[MUSIC: CONFRONTATION THEME]
Zane frowned at the voice. Turning, he saw three men standing behind him. From their clothes, he could tell they were disciples from the Great Houses. He realized this test wasn't just about fighting beasts—it was about fighting each other.
The Magic Circle was just there to make sure no one actually died.
[SFX: QUICK COMBAT SOUNDS]
His two companions were on the ground before they knew what hit them. The third man, the one who'd shouted, stumbled backward, his face a mask of shock and fear. All his earlier arrogance was gone.
"My house! My house—" he stammered.
Zane cut him off with a low growl. "If you don't want to end up like them, hand over every crystal you have. I don't give a damn who your house is. They can't save you in here."
[SFX: MENACING FOOTSTEP]
With that, Zane took a menacing step forward.
[SFX: POUCH TOSSED]
[SFX: SCRAMBLING FOOTSTEPS]
The disciple from House Fane didn't need to be told twice. He tossed a pouch of crystals at Zane's feet, turned, and scrambled away into the woods as if his life depended on it.
Zane just shook his head. He wasn't a thief, but lately, it felt like everyone was trying to rob him.
He gathered the crystals. There were twenty green fragments and seven Sunstone Shards. Robbing people was definitely a faster way to pass the test, he thought wryly. Compared to grinding it out hunting beasts one by one, this was far more efficient.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE DETERMINATION]
But he pushed the thought away. The whole point of the Gauntlet was to push his own limits, not take shortcuts. Still, if anyone else tried to rob him, Zane decided, he wouldn't hesitate to rob them right back.
And there were plenty of people ready to rob him.
