Ep 462 - An Interesting Array

Word count: 1101

[AMBIENT: PALACE HALL]

[SFX: ECHOING FOOTSTEPS]

Zane's soft footsteps echoed through the palace corridor, each sound swallowed by the vast emptiness around him. The polished marble beneath his feet gleamed in the lamplight, reflecting distorted shadows that danced along the walls. A moment later, a pale-faced warrior materialized before him, deliberately blocking his path with calculated precision.

[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

The man crossed his arms, his posture radiating arrogance as he sized Zane up from head to toe with an insolent gaze that lingered far too long. His eyes held the particular contempt reserved for those he deemed beneath him.

"Wow, third rank of the Soul Realm," the warrior sneered, his voice dripping with mockery. "I'm genuinely curious how someone like you even managed to sign up for this trial." He flashed a toothy grin that never reached his cold eyes, clearly expecting a reaction.

Zane barely glanced at him, his expression remaining impassive. He had encountered too many bullies in his life to be rattled by another one now. From the fluctuations of the man's Vitality—unstable and barely controlled—Zane could tell he had only recently broken into the Core-Forged Realm. The warrior's energy signature betrayed his inexperience, wavering like a candle flame in the wind.

"Get out of my way," Zane said, his voice cold and measured.

He had come here solely for the pre-qualifying test, not to engage in petty confrontations. Besides, this was World Merchants' Guild territory, and they maintained strict order within their walls. They would never permit a fight to erupt on their premises, especially not during an official trial.

"Tsk, tsk." The pale-faced warrior clicked his tongue and shook his head slowly, as if disappointed by a child's poor behavior. His smirk widened, revealing teeth that seemed too white, too perfect. "All that pride, yet so little talent to back it up. So tell me—I'm dying to know—how exactly did you manage to pass the lodestone test?"

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane frowned, a weary look in his eyes. It seemed like everywhere he went, someone was trying to give him a hard time. He thought for a second, then smirked. “You really want to know?”

“I said I’m curious, didn’t I? Are you deaf?” the warrior demanded, cocking an eyebrow impatiently.

“Curious or not, it’s none of your business,” Zane answered, his voice calm and even. “How I passed is no concern of yours.”

[SFX: CROWD LAUGHTER]

A ripple of laughter went through the other warriors watching nearby. The sight of a Core-Forged warrior getting shut down by a mere Soul Realm practitioner was apparently hilarious.

Of course, the pale-faced warrior didn’t find it amusing at all. The laughter brought an ugly flush to his sickly pale cheeks.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

“You…” he snarled through gritted teeth, settling into an aggressive stance, ready to pounce.

But Zane just walked past without flinching, as still and patient as a predator waiting for its moment to strike.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

Back in the upper world, he hadn't just mastered the nine Original Laws—his soul had even entered the Fighting Soul Realm. On top of that, during his last two months in the Sanctum of the Dragonblood, he’d absorbed a drop of True Dragon’s Heartblood and ten more of its blood, sending his power level skyrocketing and fortifying his body.

He’d now reached the third level of the Malevolent Cycle of Life and Death, a Tier Five Cultivation Method. He’d already seen its power back when he was just an Adept—at the first level of the skill, he’d easily defeated Frank. Now, at the third rank of the Soul Realm, his strength was on a whole other level, something he'd already proven at the Dragon Soar Arena.

[AMBIENT: TENSE HALL]

Zane stood before the entrance to the testing chamber with the other hopefuls, his pulse steady despite the weight of expectation pressing down on him. The Guild's investment in him wasn't charity—they expected results.

A stern-faced examiner emerged from the shadows, his robes marked with intricate silver threading that identified him as a senior Guild official. He studied Zane with calculating eyes, measuring him the way a merchant appraises rare goods.

"You've been granted an unusual privilege," the examiner said, his tone neutral but firm. "The Guild rarely opens this chamber to anyone below the Soul Realm. Your rune work has earned you this opportunity, but talent alone won't carry you through what lies ahead."

Zane met his gaze without flinching. "I understand."

The examiner gestured toward the massive bronze doors behind him. "Beyond this threshold waits a trial designed to push your limits. It will measure not just your cultivation, but your adaptability, your resolve, and your capacity to grow under pressure."

As they approached the doors, Zane felt the air thicken with concentrated spiritual energy. Whatever awaited him inside had been prepared with meticulous care and formidable power.

The test itself was an enormous magic array set up by the World Merchants' Guild, far more advanced than any other Zane had ever seen. The one back at the Skyreach Sect was like a kid’s toy in comparison.

The examiner spent a few moments explaining the rules, then stepped aside.

[SFX: HEAVY CURTAIN SWOOSH]

With a wave of his hand, he pulled back a heavy curtain hanging on the palace wall.

[AMBIENT: LARGE CROWD MURMUR]

“It’s time to enter the magic array!” the examiner announced. He added with a smile, “Good luck, everyone!”

[AMBIENT: MAGICAL PORTAL HUM]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Behind the curtain was a large, swirling shadow—the entrance to the massive array. The participants lined up and filed into the darkness one by one.

As the last to arrive, Zane was at the back of the line. The examiner caught sight of him just before he went in, his eyebrows raising in surprise at the warrior's low power level. Still, he offered Zane a small smile. “Good luck to you.”

“Thank you,” Zane said calmly, then stepped inside.

The examiner rubbed the bridge of his nose. Unbelievable. The test had been running for days, and even warriors at the peak of the Soul Realm were few and far between. Yet here was someone at the measly third rank. And still, the examiner could sense the kid’s confidence. Did he really think he could get into the main tournament?

[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]

[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL VOID]

Inside the magic array, Zane found himself suspended in a vast, empty white space. Slowly, the outlines of his surroundings began to take shape.

[SFX: REALITY MATERIALIZING]

[AMBIENT: GENTLE WIND IN GRASS]

A lush grassland materialized around him. On it stood a stone tower, about as tall as three men standing on each other’s shoulders. The name “Zane Lowell” was carved into its side. On top, an archer with a longbow stood waiting.

[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS THEME]

A slow smile spread across Zane's face.“Now this is an interesting magic array.”
