Ep 818 - It's Alive

Word count: 1005

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

Up in the stands, War King Wind and the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom—along with every other powerhouse from the Soul Sea Realm—watched the spectacle unfold.

Without Time Law slowing him down, the speed difference between Zane and Fiona was massive. But for all her lightning-fast strikes, Fiona couldn't actually damage him. His body was just too tough; he was like a walking fortress. As long as he could keep enduring her attacks, his own understanding of the Time Law would deepen with every passing moment. The longer this went on, the more the fight tipped in his favor. Eventually, she would be the one to lose.

“Oh no,” Fiona murmured, a flicker of genuine worry crossing her face. She could feel it—a subtle shift in Zane’s speed. He was getting faster. He was still about four times slower than her, but the gap was closing, and it was closing fast.

Losing to Zane was not an option. After landing one last palm strike that did nothing, she broke off her attack.

[SFX: WHOOSH]

Instead of pressing forward, she floated gracefully to a corner of the enchanted barrier.

“Zane, give up,” she called out.

“Why?” Zane had no clue what was going through her head, but he was starting to get used to her unpredictability.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

“I'm going to use fire,” Fiona said, her tone dead serious. “And I'm afraid it might kill you.” She paused. “I can't knock you out anymore — you're too fast now. And your Time Law is advancing too quickly. This is my only option left. And if I don't use it now, it won't be long before you surpass me entirely. At that point, I won't be your opponent anymore. So please — give up.”

She had just laid out her entire tactical situation to him. Openly. Without hesitation.

“Fire?” Zane’s eyebrows shot up.

Ever since the last time he’d tempered his body, his Primordial Energy had been building up, coiling inside him. It was about time to do it again and push himself to the next level, but he hadn't been able to find the right kind of fire source. He wasn’t sure exactly what kind of flame he needed, only that the stronger he got, the more powerful the fire had to be. He did have access to the black fire inside the soulforge, but that was pure, terrifying intensity. He didn’t dare risk using something so volatile.

But this could be it. Fiona wasn’t just skilled in the Time Law, but the Fire Law, too. If he could use her flames to forge his body into a Relic-Class weapon, it would be a perfect solution.

Zane said nothing.

Fiona's expression shifted. “Give up,” she said again. “I beg you. I don't want to use this.”

It was the second time she had begged him.

Zane shook his head slowly. “I'm sorry. If it were anything else, I'd consider it. But give up — that I can't do.”

Fiona puffed her cheeks.

The Wind Divine Kingdom geniuses watching below looked ready to commit violence on Zane's behalf.

She sniffed. “There's no other way then.” She raised her right hand high.

[SFX: BRACELET SLIDING]

Her arm was slender, and when she lifted it, the red jade bracelet slid from her wrist all the way up to her elbow, settling against her upper arm. Then, instead of the bracelet vibrating as it usually did, her entire body began to tremble — a fine, rapid shaking, so fast it blurred her outline. The bracelet blazed.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS BUILD]

The Wind Divine Kingdom's Soul Sea masters turned to War King Wind. One of them stepped forward. “Your Majesty — should we stop her?”

War King Wind's expression flickered. He didn't want her to use this. But he had made his decision. He had sheltered her too much, for too long. Even a lion pushes its cubs off a cliff. This was her trial. As long as her life wasn't in danger, he would let her see it through.

“Leave her,” he said.

[SFX: PHOENIX CRY]

[MUSIC: EPIC MYTHIC THEME]

A mark split open between Fiona's brows. Fire poured out of it.

[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]

“True Phoenix Fire — come out.”

[AMBIENT: INTENSE HEAT WAVE]

[SFX: PHOENIX MATERIALIZATION]

A phoenix materialized behind her. The heat hit the square like a wall. Spirit-Tribulation warriors scrambled to reinforce themselves with Vitality just to keep standing. Even through the barrier, the temperature was almost unbearable.

[AMBIENT: SHOCKED CROWD MURMURS]

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL RISING TENSION]

“Is that… the True Phoenix bloodline? Is she a descendant of the Phoenix Race?” someone shouted.

“Her bloodline is insanely pure. She has to be a direct descendant!”

Zane watched, his eyes wide, as flames began to gather around her. He’d once absorbed the blood and Life-Essence of True Dragons, which had been perfect for tempering his body. The problem was, he’d only managed to get a few drops. It wasn't enough to make a real difference, and certainly not enough to master their unique powers.

[SFX: ETHEREAL PHOENIX CRY]

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FLAMES]

Fiona, on the other hand… she seemed to be a true descendant of a Greater Legendary Beast. The purity of her bloodline was off the charts, and the phantom of a True Phoenix taking shape around her was already looking incredibly solid.

He remembered learning back at the Skyreach Sect that Tracy had the Blue Phoenix bloodline. But a Blue Phoenix was in a completely different league, far weaker than the True Dragons and True Phoenixes of legend. Fiona was the real deal. It seemed like she possessed a complete, undiluted bloodline.

[SFX: INTENSE FIRE WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL CRESCENDO]

From a single point between her eyebrows, streams of fire surged out, condensing and slowly fusing together. The shape it took was unmistakable: a phoenix. It was completely different from the Ice Phoenix Tia had once created, which had been nothing more than an illusion. 

This thing… it looked alive.
