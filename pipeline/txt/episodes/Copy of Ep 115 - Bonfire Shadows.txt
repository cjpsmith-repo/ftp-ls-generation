Ep 115 - Bonfire Shadows

[SFX: CRACKLING BONFIRE]
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
 In the flickering light of the bonfire, Zane scanned his surroundings. He spotted about ten female apprentices crouched on a wide spruce branch overhead. The one in front held a longsword, her knuckles white around the hilt.
[SFX: FEMALE VOICE SHOUTING]
 She yelled down, "Stop right there! What peak are you from?"
Zane looked up. "We're apprentices from Misty Peak!"
Of all the Sentinel Peaks in the Skyreach Sect, only Maiden's Peak exclusively housed female apprentices. Since the group on the branch was all girls, it was a safe bet that’s where they were from.
Since Elara's arrival, Maiden's Peak had transformed into a formidable powerhouse within the Sect. Though she remained imprisoned now, her influence lingered, allowing the peak to maintain its prestigious standing—a stark contrast to Misty Peak, which languished at the bottom of the rankings despite its potential. The disparity between the two peaks had only grown more pronounced over time.
The unique distinction of housing exclusively female cultivators made Maiden's Peak extraordinarily sought-after among male apprentices throughout the Sect. Young men frequently invented flimsy pretexts to visit, their transparent motivations earning knowing smirks from the women who trained there. After all, where better to seek a companion who understood the cultivation path than among the skilled fighters of Maiden's Peak? Many lasting partnerships had formed through these encounters, creating a tradition that persisted despite the occasional disapproval from stricter instructors.
The girl on the branch was named Ingrid, from the Blackwood Clan, a family famous for their deadly Throwing Needles. Her strength and Advancement level marked her as a Core Apprentice, which was why she was leading her group during The Reckoning.
 "Misty Peak? Stay put!" Ingrid warned again, aiming a small, ornate box at Zane's group.
[SFX: MAGICAL DEVICE ACTIVATION]
 Ingrid was surprised her Needle Storm hadn't worked on Zane, but as a member of the Blackwood Clan, she had plenty of other tricks up her sleeve. She held up the box. "Even when a demon takes on a victim's form, it still gives off a faint, evil aura. When this Detection Needle senses that presence, it turns from green to red."
Zane nodded. It figured. Each of the Seven Great Houses had its own specialties. The Blackwood Clan were masters of Hidden Weapons and all sorts of clever devices like this one.
"Alright, girls, you can descend now. The area is clear of demonic presence," Ingrid called to her companions, tucking the Detection Needle back into her sleeve with practiced precision.
[AMBIENT: FOREST NIGHT]
[SFX: CRACKLING BONFIRE]
 At her command, the apprentices from Maiden's Peak gracefully dropped from their perches among the branches, landing with the silent agility that marked their training. They gathered around the crackling bonfire, its amber light illuminating their wary expressions. After a moment's hesitation, the Misty Peak apprentices joined them, forming an uneasy circle as flames cast dancing shadows across their faces.
"Your level of vigilance suggests experience," Zane observed, studying the tightness around their eyes and the way they positioned themselves defensively even in repose. "You've already encountered demons on your journey, haven't you?"
[MUSIC: SOMBER TENSION]
 The question hung heavy in the night air. Immediately, a palpable darkness seemed to descend over the Maiden's Peak girls. Their shoulders tensed, and several exchanged glances laden with unspoken grief. The haunted look in their eyes told a story of recent trauma that words could scarcely capture.
 Ingrid lowered her gaze, her fingers absently tracing the emblem of the Blackwood Clan on her sleeve. When she looked up, unshed tears glistened in her eyes, catching the firelight like trapped stars. "We believed this year's Reckoning would be manageable," she confessed, her voice barely above a whisper. "Several Varsity members from Blackrock Peak swore blood oaths they would stand as our shields." Her hand clenched into a fist. "We were naive, unprepared for the nightmare awaiting us in these woods."
The Skyreach Sect organised the demon-slaying mission every year. Humans and demons had been at war for generations, but since the combined protection of the Sunstone Citadel and the Skyreach Sect had kept demons out of the Empire for years, most apprentices didn't grasp how dangerous they truly were. The whole point of the training was to teach them how to use their skills in a real fight, without being overwhelmed by a demon's malevolent presence.
Gaining that experience meant facing a real demon in the sky forest. It was a life-or-death trial, and the very idea of it was terrifying.
But in truth, it was rare for any disciples to actually die during The Reckoning. That's because the demons sent to scout the sky forest were usually just Demonic Grunts, who weren't very powerful. Any Varsity member who passed the test to enter the sky forest was more than strong enough to take one on.
This time, the girls from Maiden's Peak had volunteered for the mission because they’d teamed up with the Varsity members from Blackrock Peak, who had promised to protect them.
Blackrock Peak was ranked second among the Sentinel Peaks. Even if their members in The Reckoning weren't the absolute strongest, they were more than capable. Some of them weren’t highly ranked in the sect yet, but they were already known for their skill.
But this time, while they were exploring the sky forest, a demon *general* had infiltrated their group.
[MUSIC: SOMBER MELODY]
 Exhausted from the day’s ordeal, Ingrid and the other girls leaned against each other and drifted off. The Misty Peak disciples took turns standing guard.
[AMBIENT: DARK FOREST NIGHT]
 The night dragged on, thick with anxiety for those on watch. Even the ones who managed to sleep found no real rest, jolted awake by nightmares. The suffocating darkness only fed their growing sense of hopelessness.
Somehow, the rest of the night passed without incident.
[MUSIC: MEDITATION THEME]
 Zane remained awake throughout the night. His soul existed in a realm incomprehensible to ordinary beings, allowing him to function flawlessly for days without slumber yet maintain perfect vitality. Cross-legged on the forest floor, he immersed himself in mastering the 'Ogre Horde'—the second, more devastating movement of his Titan Strike technique.
As dawn broke, he maintained his meditative posture, unmoved since dusk. 
[SFX: ENERGY WAVE]
 Suddenly, Zane's eyes snapped open, releasing a powerful wave of Nether Essence that rippled through the surrounding area. His night-long communion with the technique had yielded far more than mere understanding—it had transformed his grasp of the Titan Strike into something altogether more terrifying.
