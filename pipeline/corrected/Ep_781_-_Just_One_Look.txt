Ep 781 - Just One Look

Word count: 1062

[AMBIENT: VAST WINDSWEPT PLAINS]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

The continent belonging to the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms sprawled out before him, a landmass so vast it seemed to defy an endpoint. Even Zane couldn't begin to guess its true size, but he was damn sure it dwarfed both the main continent and the Heartlands combined.

“So,” Zane started, “which kingdom’s territory is this?”

A small, knowing smile touched Francis’s lips. “We came all the way from the Heartlands and crossed the Chaotic Ocean of Stars. The Fire Divine Kingdom borders one side of the ocean, the York Divine Kingdom the other. Right now, I’m pretty sure we’re in Fire Kingdom territory. Where are you headed?”

“Fire Kingdom it is, then.”

 Zane had no intention of settling down here. His goal was brutally simple: he needed Ancient Heartstones to save Tia. Since the Sacred Feather Sea was off-limits, that meant joining a martial house. He didn’t know a soul in the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms, and more importantly, no one knew him. 

Still, he had to be smart. In the Celestial Tomb of Vesper, he’d killed a man from one of the kingdoms, and its Holy Maiden had sworn revenge. Walking right into the York Divine Kingdom would be idiotic. While these kingdoms were packed with people, truly powerful fighters were a rarity anywhere. Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors were few and far between, but Zane wasn't about to test his luck against a pissed-off Holy Maiden on her home turf.

Francis didn't question the decision, just fell in step behind him, his senses on high alert. He had managed to survive the Heartlands, after all. And though he was Zane’s slave for now, he knew this arrangement wasn’t permanent. The moment Zane died—or the day Francis was no longer useful—would be the day he was finally free.

[AMBIENT: WIND RUSHING]

[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS STRINGS]

 Just as Francis had predicted, the coastline was practically deserted. They flew at top speed for what felt like ages, the landscape a blur beneath them, without spotting a single city of any real size. 

The place felt even emptier than the Heartlands. But as they pressed deeper inland, towns began to appear, slowly at first, and then with more frequency. Zane glanced down, noting that most of the inhabitants were just regular folks. The few martial artists he sensed were barely at the Adept Realm. He wasn’t about to waste his time here, so he pushed onward.

They passed third-rank and fourth-rank martial houses along the way, but Zane didn't stop. Francis had been clear: only fifth rank and above earned quotas into the Sacred Feather Sea.

[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE PIANO]

 After passing a few medium-sized cities, massive ones began to dot the horizon. Zane was starting to realize that the layout of this divine kingdom was completely different from anything he’d known. He had been thinking of the martial houses as being like sects, but he’d had it all wrong. The divine kingdoms were like the sects. The martial houses were more like the Elite House within the Cloud Hall—subdivisions, not independent powers. Their resources, their authority, everything flowed directly from the kingdom.

It finally clicked. To the martial houses, there was no such thing as a freelance warrior. 

After a few more days of travel, a massive city rose before them. At its front gate stood a stone statue nearly the height of a small mountain.

"The founding War Emperor of this kingdom," Francis said, following Zane's gaze. "He built everything here. Lived thirteen thousand years." A pause. "And still died."

Zane looked at him.

"That's the curse hanging over every warrior in the Divine Kingdoms," Francis said quietly. "In the Heartlands, almost no one reaches Soul Sea Realm, so it doesn't matter. But here, there are Soul Sea masters everywhere. And none of them can ascend to the Upper Realm. No one knows why the path is closed. So they cultivate, they grow powerful, and eventually their lifespan runs out. They call it Heavenly Decay. Even the strongest men who ever lived in these kingdoms all ended the same way."

Zane said nothing. He stared at the statue for a moment longer, then looked away.

This was the second largest city in the kingdom, according to Francis. Flying was forbidden inside, so they went on foot.

[AMBIENT: BUSY CITY STREET]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]

The moment Zane stepped through the gate, he felt the difference. In this place, the sheer density of powerful cultivators was unlike anything he'd seen in the Heartlands.

"The Crystal Martial House is here," Francis said. "Sixth rank. One of the most respected houses in the four kingdoms. With your strength, you should have no trouble getting in."

Zane had one more question. "Don't martial houses usually recruit beginners? Someone at my level walking through the door — won't that raise suspicion?"

Francis shook his head. "It's not like the Heartlands. Here, everyone inside the kingdom's borders is already considered one of its own. The martial houses don't recruit disciples to cultivate from scratch — they'll take you at any level."

 With a new understanding of the game, Zane asked for directions to the Crystal Martial House and set off, Francis trailing close behind. He soon found himself on a street flanked by a high, red wall. 

Over the top, he could hear the faint, rhythmic crash of waves and smell the sharp, briny scent of salt in the air.

That smells like the sea, he thought. Could it be?

[AMBIENT: DISTANT OCEAN WAVES]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

 They had traveled deep into the kingdom, far from the Chaotic Ocean of Stars. If there was a sea on the other side of that wall, it could only be one thing.

Francis nodded, confirming his unspoken question. “You’re right. The Sacred Feather Sea is just on the other side of this wall.”

Zane stared at the impassable barrier, his confusion growing. What was so damn mysterious about this sea that he wasn’t even allowed to look at it?

Francis wasn't surprised by his bewilderment. He chuckled. “The Sacred Feather Sea is the source of the Union’s power. This whole inland sea is their most sacred, forbidden territory. Ordinary martial artists aren’t even permitted to lay eyes on it.”

“And if I do?” Zane asked.

Francis laughed again, a dry, humorless sound. “One look could get you killed.”
