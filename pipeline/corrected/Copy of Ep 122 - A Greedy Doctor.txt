After so many years in the army, the Skyreach Sect felt… foreign to him. Lennie vaguely remembered the Sect having pledges and Varsity members, but he couldn't recall if Nature Spirits were ever among the pledges.
Judging by the feel of Zane's Vitality, Lennie figured the impressive young man had to be a High-level candidate. What threw him off was that the kid's Vitality felt strange. No one, not even a master like Lennie, would believe a mere High-level candidate could perform so incredibly well.
Despite all his injuries, Zane stood perfectly still, like a statue carved from sheer will.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
Curious, Lennie walked over and gently pressed his fingers to a vein on Zane's neck.
[SFX: HEARTBEAT FAINT]
“His heart, liver, spleen, lungs, and kidneys are all damaged. His veins have ruptured… He shouldn't even be on his feet with injuries this bad. This kid’s got some serious willpower,” Lennie said, stunned. He turned to his niece. “Ingrid, who is this kid?”
“Uncle, it doesn’t matter who he is! He’s hurt badly, and we have to help him! Please, save him. Please,” Ingrid begged, her voice trembling. “Can you save him?”
Lennie managed a small smile. “This kid is tough enough to be still breathing after all that, so yeah, saving him isn't impossible. But there are a dozen ways to heal him, and every single one costs a fortune. That's why I have to ask who he is.”
Lennie had faced countless life-or-death situations. He’d pay any price for Ingrid. But for a complete stranger? He wasn't about to go broke. He needed to know who this kid was.
“He… he saved me. He’s like this because he was protecting me,” Ingrid said, looking at Zane with a complicated storm of emotions. She added, "
[SFX: VITALITY PULSE — SHARP AND PRECISE]
A spark of Vitality appeared in Lennie's hand and instantly shaped itself into a long golden needle. He moved without hesitation — pressing it into an acupoint at Zane's center, then continuing in rapid succession, sealing acupoint after acupoint across his chest and torso. Fourteen in total, struck in one unbroken sequence.
The wounds that had been bleeding freely went still.
Then Lennie began pulling out the bone spurs.
[SFX: BONE SPURS EXTRACTED — WET, HEAVY]
Each spur that came free was long, slick with blood, and had driven deep into vital areas. Any one of them should have been fatal. He pulled out the last one and stood back, staring at the boy.
How was he still alive?
He shook his head slowly, then shaped a strand of Vitality into a small silver sword and positioned it beneath Zane, letting it support his weight. He turned to Ingrid and gave her a quick once-over — checking for injuries she might not have mentioned.
"You're all right," he said, satisfied. "Let's move."
[SFX: SWORD CUTTING AIR]
[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]
They rose into the sky, Lennie's sword carrying all three of them northwest at speed. Midway through the flight, Ingrid's face fell.
"Uncle — my companions are still in Sky Forest."
"They'll be fine," Lennie said. "Silverspire already sent people in to find the Skyreach disciples. They knew something went wrong."
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
The Skyreach Sect has been sending people to hunt demons in the Sky Forest for years. In all that time, they've only ever run into a single demon general. But on this mission, we saw so many Demon Commanders. Something has to be wrong, right?”
Coming from a Great House, Ingrid was sharp. Seeing so many Demon Commanders at once told her something was very, very wrong in Silverspire. Everyone knew Silverspire was located in The Crucible, and for years, it had been the strongest and most important military outpost in the Empire. Aside from a few worthless Demonic Grunts, it was never easy for the far more powerful Demon Commanders to get past Silverspire. But this time was different.
Lennie nodded grimly. “Yeah, something’s wrong in Silverspire. The demons in The Crucible are getting bolder. About two weeks ago, the imperial army got routed. The demons took both towers, and our forces had to fall back to Silverspire.”
Ingrid was stunned. For years, the people of The Crucible had fought tooth and nail to push back the demons. Bit by bit, they had built two massive towers—the Sky Spire and the Stone Spire—to hold them back. With the demons having captured both, the situation was worse than it had ever been.
[SFX: SWORD CUTTING AIR]
[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]
The sword sliced through the air, carrying them northwest at a speed that made running seem laughably inadequate. Wind lashed against their faces and tore at their clothes as they soared above the landscape, Lennie's grim expression never wavering despite the breakneck pace. Within minutes, their destination emerged on the horizon, its silhouette gradually sharpening against the pale sky.
Ingrid drew a sharp breath, her eyes widening as she beheld the magnificent white metropolis sprawling before them. Her fingers tightened instinctively around Zane's limp hand.
"It's Silverspire!" she exclaimed, momentarily forgetting their dire circumstances in the face of such grandeur.
[MUSIC: MAJESTIC THEME]
The city gleamed like a beacon in the distance, its massive structures and formidable walls crafted entirely from dolostone—a resilient white stone that reflected sunlight with an almost ethereal quality. This distinctive material had given the city its evocative name centuries ago, and even now, with war threatening at its doorstep, Silverspire stood as a defiant monument to human resilience. As they approached, Ingrid could make out the intricate spires that crowned the tallest buildings, their needle-like points reaching skyward as if to pierce the very heavens.
[AMBIENT: CITY GATE ACTIVITY]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]
Lennie and Ingrid arrived at the gates of Silverspire, carrying an unconscious Zane.
Several soldiers at the gate spotted Lennie and approached. "Sir," one said respectfully, "to enter the city, you'll need to present your Wax-sealed Token and submit to a security screening for identification."
[AMBIENT: MILITARY CHECKPOINT]
Lennie knew the drill. He untied the token from his waist and handed it over. After carefully inspecting it, the soldier handed it back. Then, he signaled to the soldiers on the wall above, who were operating a large Mirror of Truth. The mirror functioned just like a Revelation Pin from House Solomon—it could spot a demon in an instant.
Once cleared, the gates opened. Inside, Lennie dismissed the silver sword and let Zane's weight shift onto his shoulder. No flying within the city walls — that was the rule here. He walked, Ingrid at his side, through the white stone streets until they reached a low white building near the city's interior.
"Ingrid," Lennie said, stopping outside the door. "Zane's injuries are too severe for field treatment. There's a doctor here. Best in Silverspire. His price will be outrageous."
Ingrid didn't hesitate. "Pay it. Whatever he asks."
Lennie's jaw tightened. "It's not that simple. I swore I'd never come back to this man."
"Then I'll sell something," Ingrid said brightly.
"Absolutely not." Lennie pressed a hand to his temple, looking as though he might be ill. His grandfather had given Ingrid entirely too many family heirlooms. "I'll pay. Don't sell anything. I'll handle it."
The two of them had bad blood. Payne was a brilliant doctor, but he was greedy, always demanding outrageous sums for his services.
Lennie remembered the incident Payne had mentioned all too well. One of his comrades had been bleeding out in The Crucible, and when Lennie pleaded for help, Payne had quoted him an astronomical price.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANGER]
The memory still made his blood boil. It was then that Lennie had called him a brilliant but money-grubbing bastard and sworn he’d rather die in The Crucible than ever ask Payne for help again.
"I'll pay whatever you demand," Lennie muttered through clenched teeth, his voice constricted with bitter resentment.
Had it not been for Ingrid, he would never have stooped to seeking help from this man again.
"Splendid," Payne replied, satisfaction slicing through his tone. "And since I cherish wealth far more than wounded pride, I shall graciously overlook your previous slights and render my assistance." He punctuated his agreement with a smug chuckle.
[AMBIENT: MEDICAL CHAMBER]
[SFX: CRYSTAL BED ACTIVATION]
With a flourish, Payne beckoned the three to enter his chambers. Lennie approached the Crystal Bed gleaming ominously in the corner and carefully lowered Zane's limp form onto its luminescent surface, which immediately began to pulse with an eerie, blood-red glow.
