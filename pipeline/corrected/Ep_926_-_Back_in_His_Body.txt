Ep 926 - Back in His Body

Word count: 1140

[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER IMPACT]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]

The blue arrows were supposed to be unstoppable, but they shattered harmlessly against Delroy’s enchanted barrier. 

Shielded from the constant onslaught, the group passed through the Path of Heroes without a scratch and stepped into the Divine Emperor Secret Land.

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT NECROPOLIS]

“So that’s how this works,” Vorn said, taking it all in. “The Heavenly Stellar Secret Land is connected to a massive necropolis. It all makes sense now. You possessed these corpses to get through the blood sacrifice. They came from in there, right?”

Even though they were from the Demi-Immortal Race and usually looked down on warriors from the Lower World, the seven of them had to admit that it was an impressive workaround.

“That’s right,” Zane confirmed with a slight nod.

“So where are your real bodies?” Vorn asked, his curiosity getting the better of him.

“On the other side of The Emperor’s Isle,” Zane replied.

Normally, the warriors from the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms would return the corpses to their coffins on their way back through the necropolis. It was standard procedure. After all, well-preserved bodies were a rare find, especially those of Soul Sea Realm warriors. Each trip caused a little more wear and tear, and if they just tossed the bodies aside, they’d soon run out.

This time, however, they didn’t stop. 

[MUSIC: OMINOUS MARCH]

The masters of the Soul Sea Realm kept control of the corpses, marching right through the necropolis and heading straight for the coast of The Emperor’s Isle.

[AMBIENT: OCEAN WAVES]

A number of them were already guarding the shore. With so many of their own inside the secret land, it would be a catastrophe if their physical bodies were destroyed while their spirits were away. They’d be trapped, souls without a vessel to return to.

The White-Haired Priest, the Holy Maiden, and the others who had stayed behind were all waiting there. Rows of soul conduits were lined up on the sand, and the real bodies of the Soul Sea Realm masters sat meditating on the ground, lifeless but preserved.

“They’re back! They’re back!” yelled a warrior on watch, his voice echoing across the beach.

The Holy Maiden and the White-Haired Priest’s eyes lit up. But as the group drew closer, their excitement turned to confusion. They were still inhabiting the corpses.

“What’s going on?” the Holy Maiden asked, stunned. “Why didn’t they leave the bodies in the necropolis?”

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

The White-Haired Priest's gaze was fixed on the young woman near the front of the approaching group. In her arms was a small furry creature — strange-looking, nothing like any dragon he knew. But the aura leaking from it was unmistakable. His face broke into a wide smile. The Ancestral Dragon. They'd actually gotten it out.

He started forward.

Then he felt it. Seven presences — each one at the Spirit Supreme Realm. He stopped dead.

In all his years in this Lower World, nothing had ever given him that kind of pressure. Not the War Kings, not even Zane, whose cultivation was only Life-and-Death Realm for all his extraordinary power. Seven Spirit Supreme warriors was something else entirely.

[SFX: MULTIPLE IMPACTS]

War King Wind and the others had barely touched the shore before their souls were already leaving. They'd spent long enough inside those corpses. The bodies crumpled to the sand one by one as the Soul Sea masters rushed back to their real forms.

Zane didn't do the same. Brianna wasn't dead — abandoning her body carelessly wasn't an option. He found a quiet space away from the others, sat down, and carefully guided her soul back before letting his own go.

[MUSIC: SOFT ETHEREAL SWELL]

He opened his eyes in his own body.

The little dragon was already running toward him.

Zane blinked. The imprint had formed when he was in Brianna's body — by that logic, the dragon should have gone to her. Instead, it was charging straight at him, tail spinning.

The Cobalt Dragon's voice came quietly. “Imprinting isn't by appearance. It's by soul. The dragon knows you.” A pause. “Thank you, Zane. You did it.”

[SFX: SOFT CAW]

The little dragon leapt into his arms and began licking his face.

Not far away, Dorothea had watched the soul leave Brianna's body and settle into Zane's. She stared for a moment. Then: “He's a man.”

The disappointment on her face was plain.

Vorn looked at Zane steadily. Then he gave a small nod. “He has the Mark. He was genuinely inside the Tower of Crystal.”

“The Mark only requires the second row,” Delroy said immediately. “It doesn't confirm a title.”

“It doesn't matter,” Vorn said. “We verify by finding the tower. That was always the plan.”

Dorothea wasn't listening. She was still staring at Zane. “He's a man,” she said again, quieter this time.

But the burly man's approach cut off the White-Haired Priest before he could reach Zane. “Who do you think you are?” the man growled.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The priest's face darkened. “I work for the Supreme Lord of Thunder Punishment!” He knew he was probably no match for these seven; their combined aura was overwhelming. When facing experts of his own level, dropping his patron's name was the only card he had left to play.

The White-Haired Priest steadied himself and turned back toward Zane. “Zane,” he said, offering a slight bow. “Thank you for bringing the Ancestral Dragon out of the Heavenly Stellar Secret Land.”

Zane glanced at him. “Why are you thanking me?”

The priest frowned. “We've spent a long time searching for that egg. You're the one who got it out. It seems only right.”

The burly man just laughed. “Seriously? Are you trying to scare us by name-dropping a Supreme Lord? That trick might work on the locals in this Lower World, but it won’t work on us. We’re from the upper world.”

“Deceive them?” the white-haired priest muttered, his eyes narrowing.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

“The Supreme Lord of Thunder Punishment is the weakest of the three,” Vorn cut in, his voice ringing with authority from behind his silver mask. “But I’ll admit, a Supreme Lord isn’t someone we can just dismiss.”

“Good. As long as you know that,” the priest replied, regaining a sliver of his pride. It was obvious that the man in the silver mask was the strongest of the group. While he might have been able to take on the other six one-on-one, he didn’t like his chances against this one. He chose his words carefully.

“But even if you do work for him,” Vorn continued, his tone turning to ice, “the Ancestral Dragon belongs to our Demi-Immortal Race. No one else will ever get their hands on it.”

“Demi-Immortal Race?” The priest’s face twitched, the color draining from his cheeks.
