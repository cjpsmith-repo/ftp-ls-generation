Ep 365 - Solidarity
Word count: 1083

[AMBIENT: VOLCANIC WASTELAND]

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

As the inferno ray swam away in defeat, Zane fought to steady his breathing. He might have been strong, but slicing through the inferno ray's tail with his Apex of Sword Intent had drained him to his core. His arms trembled from the exertion, muscles burning with the memory of that single, desperate strike.

He stared at the creature's severed tail, its surface still radiating heat that distorted the air above it. What the hell was this thing even made of? The material had resisted his blade with a density unlike anything he'd encountered before.

Zane knelt and picked up the tail, turning it over in his hands. A tail from a beast of this caliber represented a rare find indeed. His mind already raced through possibilities: the material could be used for Weaponsmithing, maybe even for upgrading Streamer itself. And if the forges couldn't handle it? Well, collectors would pay a fortune for such an exotic specimen.

[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]

He slipped the tail into his Storage Ring, feeling a small surge of satisfaction despite his exhaustion. In this brutal trial, every advantage mattered.

[MUSIC: GRATEFUL STRINGS]

The disciple pressed his palms together in a show of deep gratitude. "Man, thank you. Seriously. You saved my life.” He obviously knew the inferno ray's tail was incredibly valuable, but he also knew better than to pick a fight with the guy who’d just saved his life over it.

This disciple was from another Tier-Two Sect, and on paper, his power level was much higher than Zane's. Zane was only at the Third Level of the Adept Realm, so it was no surprise the other fighters had written him off as some kid who was lagging behind. 

But now, the man's opinion had done a complete 180; he was looking at Zane with something like awe. He'd tried to take down the inferno ray himself, throwing his Skilled Blade Intent and every ounce of strength he had into the attack. His blade hadn't even left a scratch. And then Zane came along and sliced the tail clean off like it was nothing. The disciple had a sinking feeling Zane hadn't even been going all out.

As far as he was concerned, picking a fight with someone that powerful would be a very, very bad idea.

"Don't worry about it," Zane said. "We're all on the same path, so it's only right we watch each other's backs."

[SFX: GLASS CLINKING]

Another disciple clapped a hand to his pocket, producing a vial with ten healing pills. With a quick flick of his wrist, he sent them flying to the other fighters, one for each of them. “Take these,” he ordered. “They’ll restore some of your strength and Vitality.”

Vitality was something disciples normally had to build up through meditation, so pills that could instantly replenish it were priceless. For this guy to hand out ten at once was a huge gesture.

It was a smart move. He knew he couldn't take on these creatures alone; he needed everyone at their best. Restoring their Vitality and physical strength was their best shot at getting out of this hellhole alive.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

And it worked—for a while. They didn't run into any more vicious creatures, but their luck was about to run out. Another disaster was brewing right under their feet.

[SFX: RUMBLING LAVA]

"The lava's rising!" a disciple shouted.

"I see it! But look—the next platform! It's not far!" another disciple yelled back, pointing ahead.

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FIRE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHOIR]

The second high platform in the Sea of Raging Fire was much larger than the first, like a castle suspended in midair on dozens of massive pillars. A stone road stretched out from it, but what really caught their eye was the small island floating right beside it.

An island, somehow surviving in the middle of a sea of fire.

[MUSIC: URGENT DRUMS]

"Hurry! To the platform! We'll be safe there!"

As shocked as they were by the sight of the island, the immediate threat of the rising lava kept their focus sharp. The platform was their only hope. 

[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

Without another word, every fighter broke into a dead sprint. Reaching the towering structure, they let out a collective sigh of relief. It stood a good thirty feet high, and one by one, they leaped, landing safely on its surface.

[SFX: HEAVY LANDING]

[AMBIENT: VOLCANIC ISLAND]

Zane landed among them, his boots striking the stone platform with a solid thud. His legs absorbed the impact, feet planted wide for balance. For a moment, he simply stood there, chest heaving as he drew in deep breaths of scorching air. The heat radiating from the lava below made every inhalation feel like swallowing fire, but he was alive—they all were. He glanced back at the churning sea of molten rock they'd just escaped, watching the surface bubble and spit. 

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

One disciple looked around, wiping sweat from his brow."We're on the middle platform of the Sea of Raging Fire. That means we're about halfway there." 

"I heard there’s a Hidden Test on this platform," another chimed in, his eyes fixed on the mysterious island. "Supposedly, the rewards for passing are incredible."

One of the Cloud Hall disciples sneered. "Sorry, this Hidden Test is only for Cloud Hall disciples. You're not authorized to enter."

[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL DRUMS]

Zane let out a short, incredulous laugh. "What do you mean? We're all here for the Cloud Road test. Why can't we go in?" It seemed like no matter where he went, it was always the same story: someone thinking they were better than him just because of where they came from.

First, it was the disciples from the noble houses back at the Skyreach Sect. Now it was these guys from Cloud Hall, acting like they owned the place. Did they really think the Cloud Road was built just for them?

And he was right.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS BASS]

That's exactly what they thought. As far as they were concerned, they were the official representatives of Cloud Hall. Anyone from the subordinate Tier-Two Sect was, by definition, inferior—not qualified to even be in the same league. Some of the Cloud Hall disciples had already entered the passage to the Hidden Test, so the guards at the entrance simply assumed anyone left was "unauthorized" and had no right to be there.

Clearly, they didn't know about Zane.
