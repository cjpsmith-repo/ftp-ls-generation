Ep 675 - The Return

Word count: 1074

[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The Demon Night were on edge. Lavender was with Zane, and that put their queen deep inside the Dark Nether Cave, a place no one was supposed to survive. As an extra precaution, Elder May had sent a contingent back to the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary just to check on the queen’s statue.

Word came back that the statue still held a faint red glow—a sliver of hope—but Elder May wasn’t convinced. The Dark Nether Death Light had returned, and no one ever made it back from the cave after that.

The humans had their own reasons to dread the news. If Zane was dead, Lavender was dead with him — and if Lavender was gone, the Violet Heart Sanctuary had lost its protection. Without the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary standing behind them, the Ogres' Sacred Land would tear them apart. The entire human race in the tower, every last War Lord and War General, would be swept away. There would be nothing left.

Some of the human War Lords had even started to resent Zane for it. Before he arrived, they'd been struggling just to survive. He'd swept in, handed them a moment of glory, given the Violet Heart Sanctuary something that looked like a future — and now, less than a year later, it might all come crashing down. How were they supposed to accept that?

Because of this, everyone just assumed Esther Sterling was gone, too. Losing her would be a catastrophic blow to the Demon Night. She was the strongest warrior among their seat-holders, the only one who really stood a chance against Maurice and his Ogres.

If Maurice decided to make his move now, the Demon Night would be backed into a corner.

Meanwhile, the Twilight Group had been doing what they always did — feeding information straight to the Ogres' Sacred Land. Every update on the Death Light's recovery, every hour that passed without Zane emerging, went straight to Maurice.

[SFX: HEAVY DOOR SLAM]

[MUSIC: TOLLING BELL]

On Elder May's command, the passage to the top of the tower was sealed. The deadline had come and gone.

[AMBIENT: PANICKED CROWD]

“It’s over,” a human War Lord wailed, his face a mask of despair. “It’s all over! We’re dead! Someone, anyone, help us!”

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

Panic rippled through the ranks of the human War Lords. They were terrified and utterly defeated. Among them, only Fabian and Jarrod had any real leadership experience. The rest of the so-called seat-holders were a joke—most of them had been nothing more than glorified guards on the fourteenth floor before all this.

How were they supposed to stand against the Ogres? What was going to become of them now?

[AMBIENT: CURSED LAND]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

Meanwhile, deep in the Cursed Land, an intricate teleportation array pulsed with dormant energy. Towering sculptures of fearsome birds and beasts stood sentinel around its perimeter, a clear display of Ogre power.

[SFX: MAGICAL FLASH]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC HORNS]

A flash of white light erupted from the array, and a figure the size of a small mountain materialized. It was Maurice, the Ogres’ supreme War Lord.

After being driven from the Tower of Sin, most of the top Ogre War Lords had used their accumulated Lucky Light to ascend to the War Sage level. But not Maurice. He could have easily made the leap himself, but he had other plans. The moment he’d been forced out, he’d begun plotting his return. He’d sent Malcolm and Timothy with one mission: kill Zane in the Dark Nether Cave.

That was why Maurice had held back, preserving his current power level while in the Ogres’ two sacred lands. He refused to ascend, all in preparation for this very day—the day he would reclaim his throne in the Tower of Sin.

[SFX: PORTAL OPENING]

[MUSIC: MENACING STRINGS]

One by one, more Ogre War Lords emerged from the portal behind him, their faces twisted in cruel smiles. They had stewed in the humiliation of their exile for long enough. Now, with revenge finally within their grasp, the feeling was intoxicating. The shame of being cast out was a stain on their race, and today, they would wash it away with blood.

[SFX: MARCHING ARMY]

The Ogre horde marched on the Tower of Sin. The Twilight Group, ever the Ogres’ puppets, were the first to fall in line beside Maurice. Even with the Ogres gone from the Tower, their loyalty had never wavered—after all, their own Sanctum of Eternal Night was protected by the Ogre sacred lands.

The Giant Clan guards at the tower entrance took one look at the approaching column and scrambled to pull the doors open. Maurice didn't even glance at them.

[SFX: BOOTS ON STONE]

Floor by floor, the Ogre War Lords filed upward. On every level they passed through, the fighting stopped. Creatures stared. Whispers spread ahead of them like fire.

[AMBIENT: CROWDED FLOOR]

"Why are the Ogres back? Didn't Zane throw them out?"

"Haven't you heard? He died in the Dark Nether Cave."

"Then the humans are finished."

The human warriors scattered across the lower floors heard every word. Their legs felt like stone. They wanted to leave, but couldn't move.

[AMBIENT: GRAND HALL]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]

Maurice's iron boots hit the floor of the top hall. He drew a long breath, turned to face the sealed doors of the private chambers, and let his voice fill the room.

"All seat controllers. Out. Now."

The Twilight Group seat controllers were on their feet before the echo died, crossing to Maurice's side without a word. The humans and Demon Night took their time.

[SFX: BOOMING LAUGHTER]

Maurice’s gaze swept over the human commanders, and he let out a booming laugh. “You call yourselves War Lords? Weren’t you the pathetic worms scuttling around the fourteenth floor? What gives any of you the right to hold a seat? Ha ha ha!”

“Ha ha ha!”

[SFX: CROWD LAUGHTER]

The Ogre War Lords roared in amusement, and even the members of the Twilight Group chuckled along.

Rage burned on the humans’ faces, but they were powerless. In the Tower of Sin, strength was the only currency, and without Zane, they were bankrupt.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The Demon Night, however, remained composed, drawing strength from the knowledge that the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary stood behind them.

Elder May’s voice cut through the noise, cold and sharp. “What do you want?”
