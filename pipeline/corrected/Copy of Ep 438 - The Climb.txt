Ep 438 - The Climb

Word count: 1107

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL TEMPLE]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Elder Howard, the old man who'd brought Zane to this place, stared upward in disbelief, his face slack with astonishment.

For decades, his job had been to scout talented individuals from the Lower World—those rare souls who possessed a natural affinity for learning Law Power. He'd seen countless hopefuls attempt these obelisks, watched their struggles, and witnessed their failures.

After all, disciples from the Lower World faced nearly insurmountable odds when trying to grasp the advanced levels recorded on these ancient monuments. The obelisk of Fire Law, for instance, contained only the first level of its power—a deliberate limitation. Similarly, the structure Zane was currently on held only the first level of Spatial Law, yet even this fundamental tier had humbled warriors far more experienced than this young man.

The old man's hands trembled slightly as the realization struck him. He's already a third of the way up. And in no time at all.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL TENSION]

That meant the kid had already grasped a piece of the first level of Spatial Law. How was he climbing so fast?

Learning from an obelisk was a rare opportunity, which was why some warriors stayed for months on end. Some of the Dragon-blooded had been here for half a year, a full year, even longer.

It was supposed to be easier to learn Law Power directly from the obelisk, but the old man had never seen anyone scale the one for Spatial Law this quickly.

The old man searched for a logical explanation. The only thing that made sense was that the boy had studied Spatial Law before and already had a decent foundation. That had to be why he was so fast now. Once he hit the limits of his prior knowledge, he’d probably stop being able to climb any higher.

It was the only explanation that fit.

Then it hit him. He nearly slapped his head. Zane was his. The city lord's special approval, the wasted drop of True Dragon's blood — he'd been so focused on the oddity of it that he'd written the boy off entirely. If Zane kept climbing like this, Elder Howard hadn't come back empty-handed after all.

[MUSIC: IRONIC TWIST]

Of course, the old man couldn't have known the truth—Zane's previous encounter with Spatial Law had been nothing more than a lucky accident. During that chaotic moment on the Cloud Road trial, when blue lightning struck him without warning, he'd absorbed fragments of understanding he could barely understand, let alone control. 

Now, as he scaled the obelisk, those scattered fragments were finally coming together into something coherent. Each step upward felt less like learning and more like remembering—as though the lightning had planted seeds in his mind that were only now beginning to sprout. 

[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

As Zane climbed another hundred feet, the spatial repulsion intensified dramatically. A sharp, lancing pain shot through his chest and limbs, as though invisible hands were trying to pry him from the obelisk's surface. His muscles trembled under the strain.

The force grew stronger with each passing moment, pressing against him like a physical weight. Zane's jaw clenched as he fought to maintain his grip. He knew his limits—pushing further would risk serious injury, and worse, might damage the fragile understanding he'd only just begun to grasp. Pride wasn't worth permanent harm.

Reluctantly, he paused his climb and shifted his focus to studying the Law Power etched into the obelisk's surface. His fingers traced the intricate patterns carved into the stone, feeling the faint hum of energy beneath them. He could afford to stop; he'd already surpassed most of the other climbers clinging desperately to the surrounding obelisks. Only a handful remained higher than him, their forms barely visible in the distance above.

Below, Elder Howard kept his gaze locked on Zane. He waited, patient and still, to see whether his theory about this peculiar young cultivator would prove correct.

[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR GATHERING]

[MUSIC: CONFRONTATIONAL DRUMS]

Just then, a middle-aged man approached, followed by several young martial artists. He shot the old man a smug look and asked, "Elder Howard, how many elite disciples did you manage to pick this time?"

Elder Howard frowned, his voice cold. "That remains to be seen. Did you find anyone promising?"

The middle-aged man gestured to the young people behind him. One of them was clearly a key player in the rivalry between the two elders.

[SFX: CLIMBING EFFORT]

[MUSIC: COMPETITIVE TENSION]

Meanwhile, Christopher, a big shot from the Lower World, kept climbing, not stopping until he had a solid lead in their unspoken race. He looked down at Zane and taunted, "Ha! Crawling like a turtle down there, kid!"

Zane frowned. Where did this idiot come from? He decided to ignore the taunt and focus on the Law Power.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

To his surprise, Christopher actually climbed back down to his level. "Hey, I'm talking to you. Are you deaf?"

As a big shot in the Lower World, Christopher was used to being the center of attention.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Being ignored was a slap in the face, and he was furious.

Zane finally snapped, his patience shot. "Would you stop climbing up and down like a monkey?" 

"Hey, this takes skill!" Christopher sneered. "Go on, I dare you. Try and get past me."

Zane didn't say another word. He just turned his full attention back to the knowledge on the obelisk. He wasn't getting drawn into some stupid challenge. He was here to learn Spatial Law, not to compete with some loudmouth.

[SFX: CLIMBING SOUNDS]

When Zane refused to take the bait, Christopher's enthusiasm deflated. The fun had gone out of taunting someone who wouldn't react. With a dismissive grunt, he resumed his climb.

Twenty feet higher, the spatial patterns shifted dramatically. Christopher's fingers dug into the stone as he forced himself to stop, his breath coming harder now. Fresh knowledge flooded his mind—he had no choice but to absorb it. The obelisk's defense mechanism was unforgiving; anyone who tried to climb without comprehending each layer would be hurled away by invisible forces powerful enough to shatter bones.

Christopher had always been quick with Spatial Law—quicker than most disciples twice his age. He would master the first level soon enough. He had to.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]

But just as that thought crossed his mind, movement below caught his attention. Zane had started climbing again, his movements fluid and purposeful. Something had changed in his expression—a subtle shift that spoke of breakthrough rather than struggle. His eyes held that particular gleam Christopher had seen in masters who'd just grasped a principle that had eluded them for months.

A cold knot formed in Christopher's stomach. That look shouldn't be there. Not yet. Not on someone who'd just started.

Who the hell was this kid?
