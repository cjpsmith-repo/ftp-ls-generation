Ep 1286 - The Fourth Hall

Word count: 1137

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
[AMBIENT: QUIET SANCTUARY HALL]
The woman in red frowned at Tia’s confession. Was she really supposed to believe that the infamous Zane Lowell was her husband?
 “It could just be a coincidence,” she said, though the words sounded hollow even to her.
She and Tia had ascended together, and in that short time, they’d grown close. When she’d found out Tia was searching for her husband, she’d felt a pang of sympathy. The universe was just too vast. She had even gently advised Tia to give up, that the search was hopeless. 
So, hearing Tia now, so certain… it was hard to believe. There had to be countless warriors named Zane. It wasn’t exactly an uncommon name, after all. But the woman didn’t know that Tia had already confirmed it was him.
Tia turned to the other warrior who’d been listening in. “You know the story ‘Killing the Snake Spirit,’ right? It’s famous all over the Heartlands. About a man who falls in love with a snake spirit on some thorny mountain, but she can’t control her wild side and starts hurting people. In the end, the poor guy has to kill her. That’s the original story, isn’t it?”
“Right!” The warrior’s eyes were locked on Tia, his nod sharp. He was hooked.
That was the exact story Zane had broadcast across the universe with his Roaring Token three or four years ago. The warrior remembered it clearly. And this woman, who’d just ascended, knew it verbatim. There was only one explanation: she and Zane had to be from the same Lower World. Which meant she was probably his wife.
Tia hadn’t really been trying to prove anything to them. Just knowing her husband was alive, knowing where he was, and that he was doing well—that was everything.
[MUSIC: HOPEFUL STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: VAST ARENA SPACE]
*He’s still full of surprises,* she thought, her gaze drifting up to the massive pyramid where countless names flickered and shifted, rising and falling as warriors won and lost their battles.
[MUSIC: SCHEMING TENSION]
The warrior, however, saw something else entirely: an opportunity. He hadn’t ascended on his own and had no connections in the Jadeheart Sanctuary. To earn even the most basic rewards, he’d been stuck on grunt duty, supplying Vitality to the Illusionary Arena. If this woman was lying, he lost nothing. But if she was telling the truth—and it felt like a damn good chance she was—then helping the wife of a man like Zane could be the break a common-born warrior like him only dreamed of.
Tia was oblivious to the calculations spinning in his head. After a moment, she asked, “But why would Zane use a Roaring Token to tell stories to the whole universe?”
The warrior chuckled. “He did it for the mysterious singer.” Everyone knew about the public exchange between Zane and the singer; he was no exception.
“The mysterious singer?” Tia’s brow furrowed.
The warrior shrugged. “No idea where she came from. Nobody in this whole universe knows her real identity. But she made Zane a promise—if he told her stories, she’d help him track down his missing sister.”
“His sister?” Tia’s breath caught. “Elara?”
“That’s the one!” The warrior’s eyes lit up, more certain than ever.
Tia lowered her head slightly. So they still hadn’t found her.
Her gaze drifted back to the pyramid, scanning the endless shifting names. Not far below Zane’s, one caught her off guard.
Cora. One hundred sixty-fourth.
Tia stared, stunned. Back in the Central Domain, Cora had been nothing more than a middling Divine Core warrior, entirely reliant on her bloodline to get anywhere. How had she climbed high enough to stand almost shoulder to shoulder with Zane? Tia was, by any measure, a genius herself—and even she couldn’t help the pang of quiet disbelief.
[AMBIENT: STONE STAIRWAY WIND]
The third Hall of Causality’s reward—three million Illusion Points, split evenly among the group and padded further by the points forfeited by Larkin—had pushed Zane’s ranking up by two more places. With the hooks tied to the Law of Causality fading from their hands the moment they stepped outside, there was nothing left to do but climb. 
Zane led the way up the mountain’s stairs, with the others close behind him—toward the fourth Hall of Causality, the second-to-last trial standing between them and the Sacred Feather Sea.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS RISING]
[AMBIENT: INTENSE HEAT WAVES]
Inside the hall, tendrils of shimmering heat writhed in the air. 
[SFX: HEAT SHIMMER DISTORTION]
Even from the entrance, the oppressive warmth poured out over them in waves.
Janet had been here before. On her first climb through these halls, this was where she’d fallen—the fourth Hall of Causality had turned back nearly every cultivator who’d ever faced it, ninety-nine among every hundred. Whatever waited inside, it would not be forgiving.
“It’s so hot!” Ansel yelled, his voice tight with a surprising note of panic.
“The reward for this hall is substantial, but it won’t be easy,” Janet warned, her voice cutting through the heat. 
“Everyone, stay sharp.”
“No matter how hard it gets, we can’t turn back now,” Zane said, his eyes already fixed on the path ahead.
His ranking was climbing fast, and he wasn’t about to slow down. 
Without a moment’s hesitation, Zane stepped into the hall first. The others exchanged a quick look before following close behind.
[SFX: CRACKLING LAVA BENEATH FLOOR]
The floor was a terrifying web of cracks, each no wider than a finger. But deep within them, red-hot molten lava pulsed like blood vessels filled with fire. Tiptoeing across the fractured ground felt like being slowly roasted from below. 
[SFX: ENERGY SHIELDS ACTIVATING]
The heat was so intense it was unbearable, and even though it couldn’t truly harm warriors like Janet or Hailey, they still summoned shields of protective Vitality one by one, just to cope.
Zane, of course, didn’t bother. Ordinary flames couldn’t touch him.
The deeper they ventured, the more the structured hall gave way to a raw, natural cavern. The walls became jagged, irregular rock, and the air shimmered violently. 
[MUSIC: EPIC DRAMATIC STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: ROARING FIRE SEA]
Through the heat haze, they saw it: a sea of raging fire stretched out before them.
Everyone froze. 
[SFX: LAVA BUBBLES POPPING]
The fiery sea was deceptively calm, the only movement the soft pop of an occasional bubble bursting on its glowing surface. A new kind of caution settled over the group.
And then they saw the hall’s guardian. 
[MUSIC: MENACING BOSS THEME]
[SFX: MASSIVE LAVA CHURNING]
Standing at the edge of the fiery sea was a giant made of pure lava, its body the size of a small mountain. It turned its enormous head slowly, its molten form churning as it looked down on Zane and the others with a terrible, burning intensity.
