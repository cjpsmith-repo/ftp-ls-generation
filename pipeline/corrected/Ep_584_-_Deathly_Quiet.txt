Ep 584 - Deathly Quiet
Word count: 971
A sliver of doubt crossed Zane’s face.
[AMBIENT: EERIE TOWER INTERIOR]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS DRONE]
The Tower of Sin was a bizarre place. Rumor had it that the structure itself had an insatiable appetite, slowly consuming any object left within its walls until nothing remained. It didn't discriminate, devouring everything from Spirit Weapon blades to Mythic armaments and even Relic-Class artifacts.
It wasn't just a tower; it was a monster that fed on lifeless things.
The practice rooms, the arenas—none of it had been built by anyone on the main continent. They were simply… here. And despite the ages that had passed, everything inside still looked brand new.
Zane picked a practice room at random.
[SFX: DOOR CREAK]
[AMBIENT: EMPTY ROOM ECHO]
It looked empty, but the moment he crossed the threshold, a prickle of awareness ran up his spine. He wasn't alone. Frowning, he shut the door behind him and settled onto the floor.
The feeling lingered, and Zane remained on high alert.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
He could feel a strange energy signature in the room, but even with his Fighting Soul, he couldn’t lock onto its source. Whatever was in here with him was a ghost; its stealth was flawless.
The Tower of Sin might have had strict rules against fighting in the safe zones, but as Phoebe had warned him, assassinations were always a possibility. And with the Ogre holding a grudge, Zane knew he had a massive target on his back.
The thought had barely finished forming when his gaze snapped to the corner.
His eyes swept the room, his senses straining to pinpoint the other presence. He was sure of it now—someone else, living and breathing, was in here with him. Hiding. Waiting for the perfect moment to strike.
"Doesn't matter," he muttered, every muscle in his body coiled tight. "You have to make a move eventually."
To an outside observer, Zane looked perfectly relaxed, but he was a spring wound tight.
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]
Zane wasn't the only one feeling the pressure. The assassin from the Twilight Group was sweating.
How had this kid sensed him? He was an eighth-rank War General. He'd once killed a War Lord outside the Cursed Land. His concealment technique could bend the refraction of light itself — no target had ever spotted him. And yet this brat, a mere eighth-rank War Fighter, had somehow felt his presence.
Still, the assassin steadied himself. Sensing a presence was one thing. Knowing exactly where he was — that was another. The kid's gaze had swept the room and moved on. He hadn't pinpointed him. Not yet.
[AMBIENT: TENSE STILLNESS]
The assassin began to move, sliding silently along the wall, bone blade in hand. A pale phosphorescence shimmered across his skin, shifting color like oil on water — the technique that made him invisible, that had never once failed him. With each step, the distance between them shrank.
One step away. The assassin's grip tightened around the hilt, his pulse steady despite the thrill of the kill.
At this distance, he had never missed.
[SFX: BLADE STRIKE]
"Sun Piercer!"
The bone blade erupted forward with lethal precision, aimed straight at Zane's heart. Years of training condensed into a single perfect thrust.
[SFX: IMPACT STOP]
And then it stopped.
The blade had buried itself in Zane's back—and refused to advance another inch. The assassin's face went slack, his confidence evaporating like morning mist. Through the blade's connection to his mind, he felt the tip pierce skin, then encounter something impossibly dense. Like striking solid iron. He pushed harder, channeling his War General cultivation into the thrust. The blade didn't budge.
Panic flickered through his chest. What was this kid made of?
[SFX: HEAVY PUNCH]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]
Zane turned his head slowly, a faint smile on his face. "Your concealment technique is something else. I couldn't find you, no matter how hard I looked." He paused. "Unfortunately for you—"
Before he could finish, the assassin threw himself backward. The phosphorescence flared, his body seeming to blur and fragment at the edges.
"You can't escape!"
Zane's hand shot out. He'd been waiting for exactly this — waiting for the assassin to reveal himself with that first strike.
He'd known from the start that no assassin willing to operate inside the Tower of Sin could be a War Lord. A War Lord would never throw their life away like this. Which meant whatever this man could hit him with, it wasn't going to be enough. So Zane had simply sat still and let him come.
His body had become something beyond flesh. An immortal-grade weapon in human form. Ordinary blades didn't cut it. Ordinary men didn't break it.
[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]
[AMBIENT: TENSE ROOM]
The force of the punch sent the assassin stumbling back against the far wall. [SFX: BLADE UNSHEATHING]
 [MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
He scrambled to his feet, pulling a wicked-looking bone blade and spitting a mouthful of blood onto it. The blade pulsed with an eerie red light as the assassin’s eyes bled to crimson, his body hunched like a scorpion ready to strike.
He was cornered, but he wasn't about to just lie down and die. The element of surprise was gone. His only chance now was to go all in.
"Did the Ogres send you?" Zane asked, his voice casual.
The assassin’s eyes narrowed. “You really think I’d answer that?”
Zane snorted. “Fair enough. Doesn’t matter to me either way.” The words were barely out of his mouth before he lunged, his fist raised.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT]
[SFX: STONE CRACKING]
The assassin scrambled aside just as Zane's punch slammed into the wall with two deafening BANGS. The deep dents in the stone immediately smoothed over, vanishing without a trace.
That was the Tower’s magic—instant regeneration. It wasn’t so much a building as a living organism. A punch like that would have brought a normal structure crumbling down.
[MUSIC: INTENSE CHASE THEME]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING FOOTSTEPS]
A grim chase began, the two of them circling the small room.
The assassin desperately tried to keep his distance, but Zane just kept coming, a faint, predatory smile playing on his lips.
