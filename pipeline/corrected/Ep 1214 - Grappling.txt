Ep 1214 - Grappling

Word count: 1166

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[AMBIENT: OPEN BATTLEFIELD WITH DISTANT CROWD]
[SFX: LARGE WINGS SPREADING]
Lance spread his wings, ready to launch the same attack at Zane. He was going to destroy him, and honestly, using the guy as a punching bag to test his own brute strength was a bit of a guilty pleasure.
This time, though, Zane didn’t seem interested in dodging. He just stood there, waiting to face him head-on. He twisted his left hand slightly as he brought his right up to his side.
[SFX: CROWD MURMURING]
“Grappling?”The martial artists watching from the city wall murmured among themselves, their faces a mix of confusion and concern. They exchanged curious glances before their eyes snapped back to the titanic battle raging outside.
Ogre martial artists were all about Body Refining. Grappling was the most basic of the martial arts, practically a beginner’s technique. How could a move like that possibly stand up to Lance’s power?
Was Zane actually trying to take down Lance with a grappling move? And would Lance even fall for something so simple? The consensus was that something had to be wrong with Zane. The heat must have finally gotten to his head.
[SFX: POWERFUL WING FLAPS]
Lance let out a cold laugh, flapping his wings lightly. His flight turned almost graceful as he built up speed, then shot straight for Zane.
Even though the strength Zane had shown so far was pathetic, Lance didn’t let his guard down for a second. He refused to believe Zane had shown his true power yet. But it didn’t matter. Whatever tricks the guy was trying to pull, Lance was confident he had him completely under control. Tricks were useless against absolute strength.

[SFX: FLAMES WHOOSHING]
As he rocketed toward Zane, the purple flame in his hand swelled like a toxic blossom. “It’s over!”
But just as he closed in, Zane’s right hand shot out like lightning. 
[SFX: FLAME EXTINGUISH]
With a single, sharp squeeze, he snuffed out the purple flame in an instant.
Then, his left hand snaked out, grabbing Lance’s arm. The sheer force of it caught Lance completely off guard. An unbelievable power surged through him, and he could only stare, dumbfounded.
[SFX: BONE CRACK]
The sharp snap of bone echoed loud enough for everyone to hear.
Zane had just broken Lance’s arm with one hand.
[SFX: FIREBALL WHOOSH]
A flash of disbelief crossed Lance’s face as another ball of fire appeared in his free hand. He tried to slam it right into Zane’s head.
[SFX: FLAME EXTINGUISH]
But Zane extinguished the flames again just as fast, grabbing Lance’s other arm in the same motion. In his grip, Lance felt like he was caught in a pair of steel pliers. He couldn’t move at all.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: CITY WALL WITH MURMURING CROWD]
Watching this from the top of the city wall, the martial artists grew even more confused.
Once warriors reached the Soul Sea Realm, their fights were usually about Vitality cultivation or high-level martial arts. Zane’s sudden use of grappling was already strange enough, but why was Lance also ‘playing’ along? The two were just locked in a stalemate. Were they screwing around? This was supposed to be a fight to the death.
Lance was just as baffled. He’d known Zane probably had a trump card—he just never guessed it would be raw physical strength.
[SFX: GRINDING PRESSURE]
Before he could plan a counter, a force ten times stronger tore through his arm. There was no resisting it, and no time to try.
[SFX: AGONIZED CRY]
[SFX: BODY TEARING APART]
[SFX: RED LIGHT STREAKING AWAY]
Pain hit a half-second before his body gave out. 
A streak of red light shot back to the city. A field of yellow Illusion Points burst free, rushing toward Zane.
[SFX: POINTS RUSHING, ACCELERATING]
Ten thousand. Fifty thousand. One hundred thousand. One hundred forty thousand.
Zane had figured Lance was sitting on roughly two hundred thousand—a kill should’ve netted him half that. Instead Lance had banked closer to three hundred thousand. Combined with what he already had, Zane’s total jumped to two hundred ten thousand.
[SFX: STUNNED MURMURING]
Nobody on the wall envied him. Mostly they just looked lost. Zane had taken hit after hit without fighting back, then somehow Lance ended up torn apart. It didn’t track.
“That wasn’t fair,” one Ogre said. “Lance never even got to show what he could do.”
“He’s going to be furious.”
“Once he revives, he’s coming straight back for this human.”
The faint smile returned to Zane’s face. Exactly the reaction he wanted.
Killing Lance once would have been wasteful. Zane had beaten him deliberately - a wounded ego held grudges longer than clean losses. Lance would return for revenge, providing another seventy-thousand-point payout.
Still, strength alone didn’t make Zane untouchable. Layla proved that. He could probably defeat her eventually, though not easily. And if she existed, stronger warriors certainly did too.
This fight was barely the opening note of what the Illusion Battlefield actually had in store.
“You’d better get back to the city!” a human warrior on the wall shouted down, giving Zane a friendly warning.
Zane had just earned a ton of Illusion Points. It would be a shame to lose them all, and then some, to Lance.
Hearing the advice, Zane just smiled and nodded at the warrior. But he didn’t move. He stood his ground outside the city, his expression calm as he looked toward the main gate, quietly waiting.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
[AMBIENT: QUIET CITY INTERIOR]
Inside the ogre city, a dot of red light appeared, floating slowly in the air.
As it drifted, the red dot began to expand. 
[SFX: MAGICAL EXPANSION WHOOSH]
[SFX: PULSING ENERGY HUM]
The pinpoint of light grew to the size of a fist, then exploded into a ten-foot orb, pulsing with the powerful essence of life itself.
Reviving on the Illusion Battlefield was different from reviving in the Illusionary Arena. If you died in the Arena, you were just kicked out; the next time you entered, a new projection of you was created.
But on the Illusion Battlefield, a dead warrior had to be completely reformed by the power of the world itself. So, when some warriors discovered that these orbs were filled with the raw laws of life, they started staying inside the city. They specifically sought out the balls of light to study them.
With deaths outside the city being endless, resurrections were endless, too. The city was filled with floating red lights, one after another. For many warriors who never got a chance to study high-level Original Laws, this was a golden opportunity to improve their skills.
[SFX: MATERIALIZATION SHIMMER]
Before long, a figure slowly began to form inside the ball of light.
Once the figure fully materialized, he shuddered, then stepped out of the light.
He shot a furious glare at the warriors who had been watching him, then blasted off, flying back out of the city.
