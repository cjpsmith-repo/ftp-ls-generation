Ep 896 - New Old Body

Word count: 1126

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT TOMB]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

The warriors from the union of seven divine kingdoms knew exactly what—or who—was inside the coffins. They’d spent years studying the ancient corpses, desperate to learn the secret of controlling them.

The problem wasn’t the process itself, but the names. While the names were simple enough, the corpses instinctively rejected any soul that tried to merge with them. Worse, the moment a soul entered a new body, the girl sealed in the central stone pillar would begin her interrogation, a psychic assault that scrambled their memories and made recalling the crucial name nearly impossible.

War King Wind had actually succeeded once before, blurting out the correct name in a moment of pure luck. But he could never remember how he’d done it, and his fluke remained the union’s only success in all their years of trying.

[SFX: HEAVY STONE SCRAPING]

As the masters from the Soul Sea Realm heaved open the coffin lid, they revealed the thin, withered old man lying within. This was Hank Mercer, the vessel they’d been discussing.

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRONE]

War King Wind’s spirit flowed forward, a stream of ethereal energy that poured into the corpse’s parted lips.

[SFX: CREAKING BONES]

His soul began to merge with Hank’s body, and with a creak of long-unused joints, the corpse slowly sat up and climbed out of its coffin.

But the body was already fighting him.

[SFX: STUMBLING FOOTSTEPS]

[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]

As Wind tried to walk, the corpse staggered like a drunk, lurching uncontrollably toward the stone pillar. The next few moments were critical. He had to speak the name to break the girl’s hold, but seeing as Scott had already failed, no one held out much hope for Wind.

[SFX: VIOLENT SHAKING]

Just as they feared, a grimace of agony twisted the corpse’s face as the girl’s telepathic questioning began. The body shook violently, its jaw working as it struggled, and failed, to form the words “Hank Mercer.” His fate was the same as Scott’s.

[SFX: SOUL EJECTION WHOOSH]

War King Wind looked utterly defeated. Unlike Scott, he didn’t head for the twelfth floor. Instead, he sank into deep thought, his expression grim but determined. He would try again.

War Emperor York and the Emperor of the Harlen Divine Kingdom paced around the other coffins, devising a better strategy. For years, the union couldn’t even enter the Feather Emperor’s, Divine Emperor’s, or Dream God’s Secret Realm—let alone this one. They had finally pioneered entry into the first three. If they could claim these ancient warriors’ bodies, the power gained would be immeasurable. These warriors were so legendary that even now, no beast in the surrounding wilderness dared approach their resting place.

Zane had been frowning through all of it. The name problem. That was what it came down to. Both Scott and War King Wind had drilled the name into their heads beforehand. People had been shouting it at them from the sidelines. And still, the moment the girl’s will hit them, it was gone. The chaos didn’t erase the memory — it just made it unreachable.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE]

He walked toward one of the coffins.

“Zane — are you going to try?” the Emperor asked.

The Soul Sea masters turned to watch. Whatever Zane did, it couldn’t be measured by ordinary standards. One of them stepped forward. “The warrior in this coffin is called Victor Vayne. The most powerful of the Dragon-blooded. Born with divine strength. A domineering aura even in death.”

“Victor Vayne,” Zane repeated quietly. Then he turned and walked back to the stone pillar.

“What is he doing?”

“Looks like he’s going to carve something — they’ve tried that before, it doesn’t work—”

[SFX: STONE CARVING]

[MUSIC: FOCUSED TENSION]

Zane raised one finger. The tip sharpened. He began to carve into the surface of the pillar — not the name itself, but the name broken down stroke by stroke. Every line, every hook, every dot that made up the characters was carved in sequence onto the stone.

The Soul Sea masters watched in silence. It was strange. But if it worked, every one of them would copy it.

When he finished, Zane stepped back and looked at the pillar. Then he turned to the coffin. “Open it.”

[SFX: HEAVY COFFIN LID SCRAPING]

[AMBIENT: ECHOING TOMB]

The masters broke the protective rune and heaved the lid aside. The coffin was longer than the others. Inside lay a powerfully built man, broad across the shoulders, with two golden axes resting beside him.

[SFX: METAL RING PLACED ON STONE]

Zane set his Storage Ring down next to the coffin. It was too important to risk losing inside a borrowed body.

[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]

[MUSIC: BUILDING TENSION]

As Zane’s soul entered a new body, he felt a powerful, repulsive force trying to shove him back out. An ordinary Fighting Soul, even one from a warrior in the Soul Sea Realm, would have been instantly expelled. That was why most of the union’s warriors settled for the corpses on the eleventh and twelfth floors; they knew the bodies on the thirteenth floor were simply too difficult to command.

[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]

[MUSIC: EPIC DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

But Zane’s soul was far stronger than that of a normal warrior, or even that of the emperors. His spirit had fused with the will of the world of chaos itself. He met the repulsive force head-on, his will slamming against it as his soul ignored the resistance and seized direct control of the corpse’s brain.

[AMBIENT: DEEP RESONANCE]

[MUSIC: SLOW MYSTICAL DRONE]

Even so, the fight wasn’t over. His soul was anchored, but he still couldn’t control the body. 

He had to wait, letting the power of his Soul Core spread like a network of roots, fusing completely with his new vessel.

Streams of gray light pulsed through the man’s limbs. After a few seconds, Zane started to regain his senses, the feeling of arms and legs returning to him. Soon, the body felt like his own.

Get up, he commanded.

Zane tried to stand, but the movement was far harder than he’d anticipated. He wobbled on unsteady feet before tumbling right back into the coffin with a heavy thud.

The clumsy display might have been comical under other circumstances, but no one dared to laugh. Considering Zane’s age and power, the fact that he’d managed to claim the corpse at all was a staggering achievement.

A few moments later, Zane tried again. This time, he rose in a single, smooth motion. He planted his feet, and the powerful new body settled into a stance as firm and immovable as a mountain. 

He opened his eyes, and a gaze that felt centuries old swept across the room.
