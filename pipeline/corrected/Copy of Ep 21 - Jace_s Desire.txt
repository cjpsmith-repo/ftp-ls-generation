Ep 21 - Jace's Desire

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS]
As a Level 1 Weaponsmith, Jace practiced his dark craft with sinister Crafting Formulas—arcane principles he had meticulously applied to create three mysterious weapons of exceptional caliber. Each piece bore the unmistakable mark of his twisted genius.
He regarded all his creations with malevolent pride, but the Hellfire Blade stood apart as his crowning achievement—a weapon that radiated menace even at rest, ranking among the three finest implements of death he had ever brought into existence.
Yet satisfaction eluded him. Jace hungered for greater accomplishments, his ambition burning as hot as his forge.
What consumed his thoughts day and night was the possibility of crafting something truly transcendent—an unholy weapon that would cement his legacy.
[SFX: FORGE FIRE INTENSIFIES]
His fevered mind fixated on the legendary Spirit Weapons, artifacts of such power they bordered on the mythical.
In this realm, even standard Graded Weapons commanded staggering fortunes, coveted treasures that reflected humanity's endless thirst for power. Spirit Weapons, however, existed in a rarefied category beyond ordinary comprehension—items of such extraordinary value that wars had been fought for mere rumors of their existence.
[MUSIC: NOBLE HOUSE THEME]
Even a family as formidable and affluent as House Lowell, the undisputed rulers of Castermere County, possessed only a single Legendary Weapon: the fabled Greenwind Blade, which had once belonged to Zane's father. The sword's elegant deadliness had become almost synonymous with the man who wielded it, before his untimely demise.
It was a peerless sword with no equal in the county, and he rarely used it. Most of the time, it was hidden away in a top-secret location within House Lowell, a fact that only deepened its legend. It was a treasure everyone coveted.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
Ever since killing Zane's father, Bryson had been tearing the county apart looking for that sword, but so far, he'd found no trace of it.
For all its power, however, the blade was still just a top-tier mysterious weapon—not a Spirit Weapon.
Every great house vied for a Spirit Weapon. But if someone from an ordinary family got their hands on one, it was a death sentence, not just for them, but for their entire family. They'd have to hand the weapon over to a house like the Lowells immediately or face annihilation.
But the rewards were as wondrous as the risks were devastating. The special qualities imbued in Spirit Weapons varied; some could accelerate the owner's mastery, while others could exponentially increase their combat skills. It was these incredible abilities that drove people to greed, to risk the ruin of their entire family line just for a chance to possess one.
Listening to Jace, Zane gave a bitter smile. His voice dripped with cynicism. "The foundation of any top-tier forging is simple: the best materials and the hottest fire. You know that better than I do. So how in the hell do you expect to forge a Spirit Weapon with just my flesh and blood? You can't be serious. That's insane."
Zane had read his share of books on Alchemy and Armament Design, but he was no expert. His obsession had always been mastering Combat; he'd never paid much attention to anything else.
But now, trapped and desperate, he clung to whatever knowledge he had. If there was even the slightest flicker of hope that he could reason with Jace, he had to take it.
"So, you do know a thing or two about forging," Jace spat, his voice full of contempt. "Doesn't matter. It won't help you."
As he spoke, Jace was already unlatching the chains from inside the forge.
[SFX: CHAINS CLANKING]
With a loud clank, they came free, and he gave them a savage yank. Zane stumbled out of the Copper Forge, dragged along by the madman.
[SFX: BODY THUD]
With another heave, Jace hurled him into a nearby cage. Zane landed hard, the impact echoing through the cavern with a deafening thud.
From the floor of his new metal prison, Zane looked up.
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS THEME]
A slow, unnerving smile was spreading across Jace's face. "The rare materials I need are too far away, and I'm not strong enough to get them on my own. The fire I have is second-rate. So, to create a Spirit Weapon, I have to get... creative. Use a little dark magic. It's the only chance I've got. Using you in the forge is just the next step in the process. So... let's hope for the best! After all, your flesh and blood are something special. And who knows? Maybe my luck will hold."
"Let's hope for the best." This phrase echoed through forges across the world, a sacred mantra every Master Weaponsmith lived by. Everyone in the craft acknowledged that forging was ninety percent skill and ten percent luck. That modest ten percent might seem insignificant, but in reality, it meant everything. It distinguished those Artificers who stumbled upon legendary treasures from those who meticulously followed every rule their entire lives only to produce nothing but worthless scrap.
That elusive ten percent represented fortune—the wild card, the unpredictable element that ultimately determined success or failure in the most critical part of the entire process.
When an Artificer uttered, "Let's hope for the best," they weren't merely using a casual expression. They were offering a solemn prayer, their equivalent of "God bless us," a desperate plea to the fickle forces governing their craft.
[AMBIENT: COLD CAVE ECHO]
[SFX: METAL CAGE CREAK]
Zane stared at the ceiling of his cage, the cold metal pressing against his back. His entire existence, all his hopes and dreams, had been reduced to a madman's desperate gamble. The irony of it struck him like a physical blow.
[SFX: HOLLOW BITTER LAUGH]
A bitter laugh escaped his lips, a hollow sound that reverberated through the cave—pure disbelief crystallized into sound. The walls of the workshop seemed to close in around him as he refused to accept this nightmarish reality.
"There's nothing I can do to change this," he thought, his hands clenching into fists so tight his knuckles turned white. His father's teachings, his years of training, his newfound power—all rendered meaningless in this metal prison.
[MUSIC: SOMBER DEFEAT]
All he could do was sit there in the dim light, self-recrimination burning in his chest, blaming himself for being too weak to escape his fate. The taste of failure was acrid in his mouth.
He'd made remarkable progress in the last month, advancing from Bone Refining all the way to Core Strength Level—a journey that took others years to complete. But facing the insanity of Evil Jace, it wasn't enough. It wasn't nearly enough to save him from becoming fodder for a madman's forge.
