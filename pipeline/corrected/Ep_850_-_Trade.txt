Ep 850 - Trade

Word count: 1021

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CROWD CHATTER]

When Zane stepped out of the dragon's mine, he was already planning his route to the thirtieth floor. But he found a large crowd milling around the entrance, their voices a low hum of chatter. He frowned. Why weren't they taking the chance to explore the mine themselves? What were they all doing out here?

It didn't take him long to figure it out. They were all gathered to trade for ores from the upper floors. While no one knew the giant tree's secret for creating them, the ores found within its mines were unique, and by trading, warriors could get the specific materials they needed.

Just as Zane was about to head up, an idea clicked.

[SFX: SUDDEN SILENCE]

He walked over to the group, and a sudden quiet fell over them. The stunt he’d pulled last time had made an impression, and these young, saint-level geniuses hadn't forgotten the power he'd shown.

[SFX: HEAVY METAL CLINKING]

Ignoring their wary looks, Zane pulled a handful of ores from his Storage Ring and piled them on the ground. “Anyone with Ancient Heartstones,” he announced, “can trade for my ores.”

Many of these prodigies had explored the floors above the twentieth, but the highest any of them had ever reached was the twenty-sixth. None of them were strong enough to break through the tough, crystalline leaves higher up, so they never came back with the kind of ores Zane now had on display.

He’d gathered these on the twenty-ninth floor. Along with the Ancient Heartstones, he’d found a few other ores with interesting shapes and beautiful colors, stashing what he could. With a Mythic-grade pickax and the power of his Dragon Scales, digging them out hadn't been too difficult. The higher he went, the better the ores became, but most were useless to him. A few, however, were exceptionally rare, their value easily matching—if not exceeding—that of the Heartstones.

It was no surprise, then, that their eyes lit up the moment Zane dumped his haul. Some of the ores were exactly what they'd been desperately searching for.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING MARKETPLACE]

[MUSIC: TENSE NEGOTIATION]

The pile was undeniably tempting. “I don't have any Ancient Heartstones,” one of the warriors said, pointing to a shimmering emerald green ore. “But I've got plenty of supreme Aether crystals. Name your price.”

These saint-level geniuses were all from noble houses and were fantastically rich. While Ancient Heartstones were rare, they had Aether crystals to burn.

Zane just shook his head. “Not for sale. If you want something, it'll cost you Ancient Heartstones.”

The warrior’s face hardened with disbelief. 

A second later, he shouted, “Two hundred supreme Aether crystals!”

Zane didn't even blink. He wasn't going to repeat himself.

“Three hundred! Three hundred supreme Aether crystals!” the man pressed, his voice getting louder.

“Four hundred!” the man's voice cracked with frustration. Zane still didn't move.

Meanwhile, another warrior stepped forward and pointed to a green ore near Zane's feet — its surface laced with wisps of color, transparent but hazy at its center. “I have ten Ancient Heartstones. I'll trade them for the Emerald Green Ore.”

Zane looked at it. “Deal.” He flicked the ore toward the man.

The warrior handed over ten Heartstones with a pleasantly surprised expression. He'd clearly gotten the better end of it. A few onlookers muttered that Zane had no idea what he was doing. Zane didn't care. Ten Heartstones for one ore he couldn't use was an easy trade regardless of the market rate. Ancient Heartstones were worth more to him than anything else in this mine. Even six would have been worth taking.

“Anyone else with Ancient Heartstones can trade,” Zane said.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVEAL]

Just then, a sharp, clear voice cut through the noise. “How about this: three thousand five hundred supreme Aether crystals plus the twelve Ancient Heartstones I have right here,” a woman declared, holding them up, “for that Golden Warm Stone. And that’s the only price.”

[SFX: ANGRY CROWD MURMUR]

“You!” another man in the crowd spun around, fuming at the woman. He’d been hoping to get that same stone for just six Heartstones, and now she had just blown his plan to pieces. He was about to use his other six to trade for it, but now he was completely outmatched. He glared at Camilla, but there wasn't a thing he could do.

[MUSIC: TENSE NEGOTIATION]

Zane looked at the woman—it was Camilla. He remembered her. He took in her offer: a staggering number of Aether crystals plus a dozen Heartstones. He nodded. “Deal.”

After they sealed the trade, Zane took the twelve Ancient Heartstones but pushed the bag of Aether crystals back toward her. “Thanks,” he said. “This is your reward, but I don't need these.”

[SFX: BAG THUD]

“Don't be an idiot!” Camilla snapped, shoving the crystals right back into his hands. “You're not just trading ores for Heartstones. You can use these to buy more Heartstones from other people. They're usually found in clusters, eight or ten at a time. The fact that the guy admitted to having six means he was obviously holding out on you. He was hiding the rest and hoping you wouldn't know the difference.”

Zane was stunned. Camilla was way sharper than he’d given her credit for.

It wasn't his fault, though. He had no idea what these ores from the upper floors were actually worth. They came from a place called The Sacred Feather Sea, and he hadn't found a single mention of them in the memories from the will of the world of chaos. But Camilla, a martial artist from the Harlen Divine Kingdom, had probably been around these materials her whole life and knew their value instantly.

[MUSIC: CLEVER SCHEME]

“You saved me before,” Camilla said, a shrewd smile playing on her lips. “So I'll help you trade for the rest of your Ancient Heartstones right here. But,” she added, her smile widening, “I get a commission on every deal.”

Zane looked at her for a moment, then smiled. He had a feeling Camilla was going to be very good at this.
