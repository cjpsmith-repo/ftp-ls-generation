Ep 694 - A Brush With Death

Word count: 1151

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS STRINGS]

To Zane, it was simple. His sister, Elara, was the only person in his life who mattered, and anyone who could possibly threaten her had to be dealt with. He’d killed a woman named Marilyn for that very reason.

This girl, Cora, was innocent, sure, but that didn’t change the fact that her mere existence posed a potential risk. And Zane didn’t tolerate risks.

So, he gave her a choice, plain and simple: come back with him to Cloud Hall, or die right here.

[MUSIC: SOFT MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

Cora was, for what felt like the first time in her life, completely stunned. She'd always been the smartest girl in the room, the beloved youngest daughter of her generation, second only to Brent.

Even her great-grandfather Elder Xander, the imposing head of the Ethereal Spirit Sect, had a soft spot for her. He so rarely visited the Kensington estate that even her own father, Robin, struggled to get a meeting with him. But whenever Elder Xander did return, the first thing he did was ask for Cora. He doted on her so much that he’d allowed Brent to accompany her on this trip, knowing how inseparable they were.

It was this lifelong pampering that made the Kensington family endlessly patient with her, giving her all the time in the world to choose a husband. If she’d been from a lesser branch of the family, they would’ve married her off years ago. After a lifetime of being cherished, the idea that this stranger would actually threaten to kill her if she didn't obey was just… unthinkable.

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

Robin and Brent were just as frozen as she was, their faces darkening at Zane's audacity. How dare an outsider come to their home and threaten Cora like this? The other Kensington elders looked on, their expressions grim. Though they were all stressed about her marriage prospects, not one of them would have ever dreamed of forcing her hand.

"What if I don't choose?" Cora said. Her face had gone pale, but her voice held. There were elders all around her. Surely someone would act.

"Then I'll help you understand," Zane said.

"You dare!" one of the elders barked, stepping forward.

Robin silenced him with a single look.

Brent, who had grown up beside her and loved her more than a blood brother, felt a surge of fury.

[SFX: FOOTSTEP]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

He took a step forward, ready to shove Zane away from her, but Robin's hand shot out, stopping him in his tracks.

Brent shot Robin a confused look. This was his daughter — wasn't he worried at all? But he held his ground, trusting the older man's judgment even as his stomach churned.

Robin's reasoning was his own. He suspected Zane wouldn't actually do it — but he also thought: who else in this city has the standing to teach her this lesson? No one inside these walls dared, out of deference to Elder Xander. The young man in front of him was different. He'd faced down Maddox himself. Elder Xander meant nothing to him. Robin wanted to see if Zane could do what none of them had managed.

Cora looked at her father. He didn't move. She looked at the elders. They stood still. The realization settled over her like cold water. "If I die, then I die!" she cried out, tears finally breaking through.

[SFX: DEEP RESONANT PULSE]

[MUSIC: OVERWHELMING DREAD]

Zane didn't move toward her. Instead, his eyes locked onto hers, and he simply let the killing intent find her — a concentrated spike directed like a blade at her mind.

To everyone else in the room, it was invisible. To Cora, it felt like a hand closing around her heart, like standing at the edge of a drop with nothing below. Her body locked. Her breath stopped. This wasn't pain — it was the absolute, physical certainty that she was going to die.

It had been building for years — accumulated in the Tower of Sin, in the Dark Nether Cave, in every life taken and every death survived. When he released it, it didn't stay in the hall. It poured through the estate walls and into the streets of Ethereal City like a wave of pressure, and every living thing it touched went cold.

Outside, cultivators stopped in their tracks.

"What is that? Has Maddox attacked?"

"No — Maddox doesn't walk the path of slaughter. He doesn't carry killing intent like this."

"Then who? Who in the Kensington estate—"

No one had an answer.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

For Cora, however, that inaction was a cold slap of reality. The man holding her by the throat might as well have been death itself, and her own father wasn't moving. No one from House Kensington was doing a thing to save her. Tears welled in her eyes as panic finally clawed its way up her throat. Her survival instinct kicked in, and she began to struggle.

[SFX: DESPERATE SCREAM]

"Dad! Save me!" she shrieked, her voice thin with terror.

But Zane’s grip on her neck didn't loosen one bit. And though it clearly pained him, Robin didn't move a muscle, his face a mask of stone, as if he couldn't even hear her pleas.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS DRONE]

The chilling aura coming off Zane intensified, and he layered it with a sliver of his soul pressure—a force so immense that even a fraction of it was enough to make her world tilt. He held back, not wanting to knock her unconscious, but the message was clear: the danger was real, and it was growing with every breath she didn't take. Staring down at the beautiful, delicate woman he was choking, Zane hated feeling like the villain. Most men would want to protect her on sight; a part of him did, too. But his sister came first.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION]

[AMBIENT: OMINOUS SILENCE]

"Are you sure you want to die?" he asked, his voice a low, menacing growl.

Cora’s lips were turning pale. The moment his grip loosened just enough for her to speak, she gasped, “I… I’ll go with you.”

[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]

[MUSIC: RELIEF STRINGS]

The instant she surrendered, the suffocating pressure vanished. The malice that had been pouring off him receded as if it had never been there, flowing back into the Killing Sword Mountain. Zane let her go, and she collapsed, gasping for air in ragged, desperate breaths.

Seeing her like that, a wave of helpless anguish washed over Robin, Brent, and the other members of House Kensington. They could only watch, their hearts aching for her.

[MUSIC: DARK EMOTIONAL PIANO]

Cora, trembling like a cornered animal, stared up at Zane with pure, unadulterated fear.

She had chosen to live, but in that same moment, a cold, hard seed of hatred took root in her heart.
