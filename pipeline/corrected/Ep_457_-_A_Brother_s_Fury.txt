Ep 457 - A Brother's Fury

Word count: 1221

[AMBIENT: ANCIENT LIBRARY]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

In all his years navigating this wild world, Zane had heard a few stories about the True Dragon.

The Dragon-blooded had descendants scattered across the globe. Back where Zane was from, they’d held power for a while before they were eventually overthrown. They kept beasts like Drakes, Winged Dragons, and Horned Dragons as familiars, and when their rule came to an end, these creatures were simply left to roam free.

The legends of the True Dragon went back one hundred and sixty thousand years, to a massive war between the Union of Seven Divine Kingdoms and the Dragon-blooded on the other side of the Chaotic Ocean of Stars.

Even with the power they inherited from the True Dragon, the Dragon-blooded were ultimately defeated by the alliance. The Seven Kingdoms’ goal was to wipe out every last one of them. Even after their victory, they hunted down and slaughtered any survivors who tried to escape.

But there’s nothing more dangerous than a cornered animal.

[MUSIC: DARK CHOIR]

[SFX: RITUAL CHANTING]

With no way out, the Dragon-blooded offered millions of their own as a sacrifice to their ancestors. With the power of all those souls, they ripped open a portal and summoned a True Dragon.

[SFX: DEEP RUMBLE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

According to the old tales, the Dragon that appeared was so immense that no one could even tell where it began or ended. All anyone saw was a glimpse of its head, its eyes burning like coals in the darkness.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXHALE]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]

Then, it exhaled. A single breath. The portal collapsed, leaving no trace that the Dragon had ever been there.

The world’s ambient energy was simply too weak to sustain such a legendary beast. Even with millions of souls as fuel, it had only been enough to bring it into their world for a single, fleeting moment. In just one breath, the Dragon was gone.

But the Dragon-blooded’s sacrifice wasn't in vain.

[SFX: DISTANT EXPLOSION]

[MUSIC: TRAGIC STRINGS]

That one breath was so powerful it completely wiped out three of the Seven Kingdoms. The four kingdoms that exist today are the only ones that survived.

The Dragon-blooded were all but extinct, but no one ever forgot the Dragon. It inspired a terror and awe that burned itself into the memory of every survivor. They passed the story down, and the legend is still told to this day.

[AMBIENT: WIND THROUGH RUINS]

[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE PIANO]

Over hundreds of thousands of years, the world changed beyond recognition—mountains crumbled, seas ran dry. But the image of the True Dragon remained, as clear as if it had happened yesterday.

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]

The True Dragon’s power was beyond anything imaginable.

Which was why Tia was so floored.

The day Zane had secretly left Cloud Hall, Tia had found him. She’d seen what he could do back on the Cloud Road, and she knew he was capable of a lot. The guy had a knack for landing on his feet; he wasn't going down easy. So it was no surprise he’d ended up in Cloud Hall with the other top recruits.

Tia's assumption barely scratched the surface. Zane's treasures exceeded her wildest estimations.

A drop of True Dragon's blood—offered as a gift! What fortune had he stumbled upon?

Tia could produce something equivalent, but the significance of such a treasure was immeasurable. She could never simply give it away. Was this man extraordinarily generous? Or had he lost his mind?

"I just overheard them discussing the Martial Arts Competition. Is this about the Dragon Soar Arena?" Zane asked.

Tia nodded. "After the Dragon Soar Arena emerged three months ago, several major sects disputed its ownership. The World Merchants' Guild intervened and moved the Martial Arts Competition forward to this year, adding the Dragon Soar Arena as the grand prize. But without the True Dragon's Blood, claiming it proves useless."

"Only Bloodwood Cliff, the Black Mountain Order, and the Ethereal Spirit Sect are participating?" Zane pressed.

Tia regarded him strangely. "Certainly not. Fourth-grade sects like the Mysterious Yin Manor are competing. Even independent cultivators and disciples from third-grade sects are participating, such as the Eastern Evil King Maddox with their disciples," Tia explained.

At the mention of "Maddox," killing intent flashed through Zane's eyes—subtle, but Tia caught it.

Suddenly, Tia's eyes widened as she pieced it together. Tia had dispatched someone to deliver Elara Lowell to Maddox. Elara hailed from the Eastern Territories, as did Zane. Elara was Zane's sister.

Her heart went out to Zane, but confronting Maddox over Elara would be profoundly unwise—irresponsible to the entire Cloud Hall.

Anger kindled in Zane's chest. He had buried Elara's fate deep within his heart. Apart from Sir Kenneth and Patrick, no one else knew. Sir Kenneth had explained why Cloud Hall surrendered Elara to Maddox—he was too formidable. Yet resentment lingered in Zane's soul, and finally, it erupted.

"Don't you think you owe me an apology?" Zane's voice was tight with controlled fury.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

"How could you just leave her there?" Zane had yelled, his voice raw with rage. "Just abandon her like that?"

Tia might be the leader of Cloud Hall, but she was still young. She carried a crushing weight on her shoulders, but beneath it all, her heart was still soft. A wave of guilt washed over her, so sharp it made her eyes sting.

She had fought right beside Zane on the Cloud Road.

That time with him was the freest she’d ever felt. She’d forgotten her title, her duties… for once, she was just a normal girl her age, allowed to make mistakes.

Anyone else would have been speechless to see a master of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm—the leader of Cloud Hall, no less—getting chewed out by a mere disciple.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

Her face crumpled with guilt, like a kid who’d just been scolded and was about to burst into tears. She hung her head and murmured, so softly Zane almost missed it, "I'm sorry."

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

He wanted to scream at her, to tell her that her "sorry" meant nothing. But then he saw her face, and the rage just… deflated.

He clenched his fists. It was useless to blame her.

The world of warriors was cruel. Maddox didn't belong to any sect. He was an independent, but he was also the most powerful man in the Heartlands. Even the Ethereal Spirit Sect wouldn't want to get on his bad side, let alone the much weaker Cloud Hall.

As the leader of Cloud Hall, Tia had just made the only call she could.

"Right," Zane grunted.

Looking at him, Tia opened her mouth to speak, but the words wouldn't come.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

Suddenly, an old woman's sharp voice cut through the tension. "Young man, it was Maddox who took your sister. If you’ve got the guts, go find him and save her yourself. Don't blame Cloud Hall. This is your fight to win."

Zane was stunned. An old woman with silver hair was walking toward them, her back hunched with age.

[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER SHIMMER]

She walked straight through the shimmering barrier Tia had raised as if it were nothing but air.

Tia rushed to her side, taking her arm. "Master."
