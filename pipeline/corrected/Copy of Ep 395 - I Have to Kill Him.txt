Ep 395 - I Have to Kill Him

Word count: 1252

[AMBIENT: CAVE INTERIOR]

[MUSIC: HEAVY FEELINGS]

The aphrodisiac coursed through Zane's veins like liquid fire, clouding his thoughts and making every breath feel heavy with desire. Yet even through his own haze, he could see that Tia had succumbed far worse—her usual sharp composure had shattered completely.

Her eyes captivated him, their depths filled with hypnotic swirls that seemed to pull at something deep within his chest. On any normal day, meeting that gaze directly would have sent his heart hammering against his ribs like a war drum. Part of him wanted nothing more than to lose himself in those mesmerizing depths, to forget everything else and simply drown in them.

[SFX: DISTANT DRAGON ROAR]

But the distant roar of the Fiery Dragon Spirit echoed through the cavern, a harsh reminder of their dire situation. The creature would breach their sanctuary any moment now.

This was absolutely not the time.

"Hey, wake up!" Zane gripped her shoulders and shook her, desperation creeping into his voice. They needed her focused, needed her power. Without Tia's Ice Phoenix, they stood no chance against what was coming.

[SFX: MAGICAL HUMMING]

But Tia had vanished into herself, utterly lost in a trance. Her left hand barely channeled enough Vitality to sustain the phoenix feather embedded in the ice, while her right drifted upward with dreamlike slowness to cup his cheek.

[SFX: SWELLING ROMANCE]

The cool touch of her skin jolted through him—a silent testament to the raw, primal need simmering beneath her vacant expression. Her eyes, normally sharp and calculating, had glazed over with something far more dangerous than any technique or weapon.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL HEARTBEAT]

Zane swallowed hard, his pulse hammering in his ears. How had their desperate alliance spiraled into this? He'd faced down cultivators who wanted him dead, survived impossible trials, even defied princes—but this? This was territory he had no map for.

He opened his mouth to snap her out of it, to say something—anything—that might break through the haze that consumed her. But before he could form the words, she slipped her fingers past his lips.

The cool slide of her fingers against his tongue, the faint sweetness of her skin, the intimacy of the gesture—it was enough to short-circuit his brain completely. His breath caught. His mind went blank.

Zane was young and hopelessly inexperienced in such matters; he couldn't summon the will to resist. Some instinct he didn't recognize took over, and he found himself kissing her slender fingers, his eyes closing despite the danger surrounding them.

For one suspended heartbeat, the cavern, the trial, the approaching death—all of it ceased to exist.

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

[AMBIENT: CAVE ECHO]

Just then, a furious roar echoed from the mouth of the cave as the Fiery Dragon Spirit charged back in, its flames blazing with renewed fury.

The sound jolted Zane back to reality. His heart hammered against his ribs as the danger crashed over him like a wave. In pure, unthinking panic, he bit down.

[SFX: ENERGY BLAST]

"Ah!" Tia cried out, the sharp pain in her fingers snapping her out of the trance. She yanked her hand back, her eyes widening in shock and indignation. A reflexive surge of Vitality blasted from her other palm, the force so powerful it shoved the Fiery Dragon out of the cave again, its roar echoing into the distance.

But her clarity was short-lived. The dragon dealt with, she whipped her head around and fixed Zane with a dangerous stare. Her cheeks flushed crimson—not from embarrassment, but from pure rage. Her breathing came quick and shallow, her chest rising and falling as she struggled to process what had just happened.

[MUSIC: INTENSE ACTION]

That was it. Zane was done. Heat flooded his own face as embarrassment warred with survival instinct. "Forget it," he gritted out, backing away from her murderous expression. "I'll handle the damn Dragon Spirit myself!"

[SFX: HEARTBEAT — THEN SILENCE]

He turned to face it. The Fiery Dragon Spirit filled the cave mouth, enormous, furious, its flames raging hotter than before.

Zane exhaled. Then something stirred between his brows — a flicker of black, barely visible.

[SFX: BLACK FIRE SHRIEK]

His Black Fire shot out like a bolt, small and fast as a striking bird. The moment it hit the Fiery Dragon Spirit, the creature's flames began to shrink. The fire peeled away — first the head, then the body, then the tail — each piece swallowed into the black flame until nothing remained. The Black Fire circled once in the empty air, then returned to him.

[SFX: SILENCE]

The cave was still.

[SFX: IMPACT STRIKE]

He dodged her reaching hand and brought his own down in a sharp chop to the back of her neck. Her physical power was so immense that he had to put more than half his strength into the blow just to knock her out cold.

[MUSIC: TENDER MOMENT]

Tia crumpled into his arms, her body soft and limp against his. He caught another faint whiff of her scent.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

As consciousness slipped away, the last few moments played back in her head. Nothing had really happened, but for a girl who was always in absolute control, the memory was mortifying.

A single thought echoed through the fog: I have to kill him.

With a temper like hers, Zane had just earned a spot at the top of her hit list. The only problem? She’d sworn a warrior's vow. She couldn't kill him.

Tia's face scrunched up in a pained, confused frown.

If Toby could see her now, he’d be speechless. The fierce girl who’d shaken the Heartlands to its core for years was actually… hesitating. He would never have believed it.

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

A sharp crack of wind was the only warning.
[SFX: IMPACT — BODY HITS WALL]
Zane slammed into the cave wall, Tia's palm print still burning in his chest. She stood over him, phoenix feathers scattered across the floor, silver teeth gritted. "What did you do to me?"
Zane picked himself up, rubbing his chest. For someone who'd just been knocked unconscious, she hit hard. He kept his expression carefully neutral. "What did I do? Maybe you should be asking what you did to me."
Tia's face went crimson.

After a long moment, still unable to sort through the conflicting emotions churning inside her, she gritted her teeth. Her voice emerged cold as winter steel. "You are going to forget that any of this happened. Give me the crystal key. We're getting the treasure."

Zane handed over the crystal key, though the corner of his mouth twitched into a smirk he couldn't quite suppress. Part of him knew he was playing with fire, but another part couldn't resist. "Sure, I can forget," he mumbled, just loud enough for her to hear. A pause. "But I have a feeling someone else is going to be thinking about it for a long, long time."

[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]

Her head snapped toward him, eyes blazing with an intensity that could have melted the Soulforge itself. "What did you say?"

The temperature seemed to drop several degrees. Zane felt the hairs on the back of his neck stand up as he registered the dangerous edge in her tone. 

This was a woman who had likely ended lives for far lesser provocations. Deciding it was probably unwise to further antagonize someone who looked like a cornered lioness, Zane shook his head. He arranged his features into an expression of perfect innocence. "Nothing. Didn't say a thing."
