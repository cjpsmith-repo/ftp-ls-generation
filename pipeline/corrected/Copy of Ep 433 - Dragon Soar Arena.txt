Ep 433 - Dragon Soar Arena

Word count: 1298

[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN VALLEY]

[SFX: CROW CAWS]

Crows streaked across the blue sky, their wings beating so rapidly they appeared as little more than dark blurs. Within moments, the entire flock descended upon the mountain creek, their massive shadow darkening the water below.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Mocking laughter echoed through the valley, bouncing off the stone walls. "Hahaha! Well, well, Lady Darkmere. Looks like someone beat you to it." 

Hovering above the flock of crows was a man clad entirely in black, his face concealed behind a ghost mask carved from obsidian. His age remained impossible to guess—the mask revealed nothing, but he seemed to be ancient. His robes rippled in the mountain wind, and there was something predatory in the way he held himself, perfectly still despite floating dozens of feet above the ground.

Lady Darkmere's voice cut through the air like a blade of ice, each word sharp with barely contained fury. "So what? I arrived first. The Dragon Soar Arena is mine."

Raven's grin remained hidden behind his mask, but it lived in his voice, dripping with condescension. "Lady Darkmere, you're one of the top fighters in the Heartlands. Surely someone of your stature does not need to be so unreasonable."

"Oh, yeah? And what if I am?" She didn't raise her voice, but the flatness of her tone carried more menace than any shout. "You really think I'm scared of you?"

The tension between the two masters thickened the air until it felt suffocating. Below them, the three Inner-Core Realm cultivators from House Kendrick exchanged weary glances, their faces drawn with resignation. 

Their hopes for House Kendrick securing even a modest share of the Dragon Soar Arena's treasures were dwindling with each passing moment. All they could do now was pray their young master possessed enough luck—or skill—to survive whatever waited inside.

[SFX: POWERFUL ROAR]

Just as their argument was heating up, a loud, clear roar ripped through the air.

The sheer force of the sound rattled everyone present, vibrating through bone and sinew. Even the Soul Realm masters felt the pressure wave wash over them like a physical blow.

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND][MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]

A massive Drake appeared in the sky, gliding toward them with an eerie, fluid grace, as if swimming through invisible currents. The creature's wingspan stretched wide enough to cast a shadow over half the assembled crowd.

"A Drake!" Lady Darkmere's face darkened, her earlier confidence evaporating. Drakes were legendary beasts—even a young one could slaughter Soul Realm cultivators with ease.

Raven tilted his head, that expressionless mask somehow conveying intense scrutiny."No... I can't sense a life force. It's a puppet!"

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

Hearing the Drake's roar, the winged dragon guarding the Dragon Soar Arena roared back, the sound cracking like thunder. It seemed to be asserting its own dominance. Too many powerful beings were gathering today, and even a creature as mighty as the winged dragon was getting nervous, feeling threatened.

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL]

As the Drake drew closer, its mouth opened to reveal three people standing inside. Right in the middle was Tia, the current leader of the Cloud Hall.

A month of training in seclusion had completely transformed her. It was obvious she’d broken through from the Soul Realm to the Inner-Core Realm. Tia had reset her power nine times, and each time, she’d rebuilt it without hitting a single roadblock. Her ascent was unbelievably fast—reaching the Inner-Core Realm in just a month.

[SFX: SHOCKED GASP]

"The Cloud Hall! Tia! Where did she get a Drake puppet?" Raven demanded.

Lady Darkmere, standing beside him, stared at Tia with a flicker of admiration. As leaders of fourth-grade sects, they knew exactly how valuable a Drake puppet was. In a clash between sects, a weapon like that could turn the tide of battle.

Tia just chuckled. "Oh, you want to know? Maybe I'll tell you, maybe I won't."

Before anyone could respond, a massive ship dropped from the horizon, jumping through space in strange lurching increments. Seven men in identical green robes descended from it — same face, same bearing, same expression.
"The Ethereal Spirit Sect," Raven muttered.
The Seven Disciples bowed politely to each faction in turn and said nothing. They didn't need to.
[SFX: DISTANT RUMBLE]
They weren't the last to arrive. Representatives of the Mysterious Luna Hall appeared shortly after, drawn by the same rumors.
Tia surveyed the assembled factions and smiled. "It seems like we should all sit down and talk."

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

Inside the Dragon Soar Arena, Zane remained completely unaware of the political maneuvering unfolding beyond those walls. At this moment, pure joy flooded through him.

[SFX: SWORD BATTLES]

He fought through level after level, his sword singing through the air as he claimed each victory. With every fallen opponent, streams of Lucky Light and Malice flowed into him, strengthening his cultivation in ways that made his heart race with anticipation.

Throughout his journey on the martial path, Zane had built his foundation with painstaking care. Where other cultivators rushed forward on shaky ground, he had spent years reinforcing every aspect of his cultivation. This meticulous approach came at a price—his advancement crawled forward at a glacial pace compared to his peers. But the Cobalt Dragon's teachings had proven true: once he finally broke through a barrier, his cultivation speed exploded, surpassing ordinary practitioners by magnitudes.

A fierce grin spread across Zane's face as he felt the energy coursing through his body. The exhilaration made him feel invincible.

By the time he cleared the fourth floor, the Lucky Light had pushed him to the ninth level of the Adept Realm. One step from peak.

But reality tempered his enthusiasm as he ascended higher in the Dragon Soar Arena. Each successive level presented challenges that grew far more difficult. The opponents moved faster, struck harder, coordinated better. Sweat began to bead on his forehead as he realized this trial would demand everything he had.

[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]

[SFX: ARMOR CLANKING]

Zane cut through his opponents with Blood Drinker, but when he reached the fifth layer, he was stunned by what he saw: a fully armored cavalry unit. The entire floor was packed with them—thousands, it seemed.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

[SFX: SWORD SLASHING]

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SUMMONING]

Facing the cavalry head-on, Zane knew Blood Drinker alone wouldn't be enough. He summoned Lily. With her massive sword in hand, Lily was a natural-born killer. Each swing of her giant blade carved a path through the armored ranks, chopping them to pieces.

[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY FLOW]

In that moment, streams of Malice and flecks of Lucky Light poured into Zane's body, settling deep within him.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT ORCHESTRAL]

And just like that, the fifth layer was clear.

[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]

Without a second thought, Zane rushed to the sixth layer.

[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE]

When he arrived, he found it strangely empty. The divine runes on the floor hadn't even been activated yet.

Is this it? The top floor? Zane wondered. He hadn't expected it to end so soon; he still hadn't absorbed enough Lucky Light to level up. He was at the peak of the Adept Realm, and with just a little more, he could finally break into the Soul Realm.

[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]

The Cobalt Dragon, who had been silent until now, finally spoke."Haha! We finally made it to the top!" 

Zane’s eyes swept his surroundings, and he soon spotted a circular Glyph hanging from the sloped ceiling. He turned to the Cobalt Dragon. "You still haven't told me what this is all for. What happens when we conquer this place?"

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

At that moment, the young master below him—already at the peak of the Inner-Core Realm—was rapidly closing in. If Zane couldn't secure the top floor now, he would be in grave danger.

"Just do as I say. Now, approach the circular Glyph," the Cobalt Dragon commanded.

Zane nodded and walked toward the sloping wall, stopping directly beneath the oval Glyph.

The instant he stood still, a tiny flicker of his soul brushed against the Glyph. It began to spin slowly. 

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY BLAST]

Suddenly, a flash of light shot out, aimed straight for him.
