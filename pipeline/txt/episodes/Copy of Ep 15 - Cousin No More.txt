Ep 15 - Cousin No More
Word Count: 1223
[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA]
Cousin.
Zane hadn't heard anyone call him that in a long, long time. He could still picture little Andrew chasing him all over the Lowell House, yelling, "Cousin Zane!" Whenever another kid picked on him, Andrew would run straight to Zane for protection.
Hearing that word again took him right back to his childhood, to those sweet, simple memories. God, those were the good days. He never dreamed that growing up meant family politics would rip House Lowell in two, driving a wedge between his father and his uncles. The last two years had been a living nightmare.
Zane nodded, returning the address. "Cousin Andrew. Let's begin."
"So, the rumors are true," Andrew sneered. "You still haven't learned your place. I found that hard to believe, but hearing you call me 'cousin'... I guess you really are that stupid. I just said it to see what you'd do. You really think that makes us family again? That we're still equals? Ha! That's hilarious. I'm putting an end to this fantasy today. I'm going to kill you myself."
With that, Andrew's face twisted into a hostile mask.
[MUSIC: COLD DETERMINATION]
At his words, the warmth of Zane's nostalgia turned to ice. His expression went cold and flat. Whatever shred of family feeling he had left for Andrew died in that instant.
[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]
Purple light exploded around Andrew's body. He took a single, explosive step forward and charged.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
"Bang!"
The blast of energy slammed into Zane's chest, the force of it knocking him back several steps just to stay on his feet. An Aether Crystal boost, no doubt. It was the only way Andrew could have reached the Bone Refining Level.
Lately, Zane had been getting frustrated. His body had become so resilient that the daily beatings from the other Lowell kids barely registered anymore. They couldn't generate the deep, radiating warmth he needed to keep strengthening his bones. But Andrew's hit... this was different. A familiar, potent warmth rushed through him.
A small smile touched Zane's lips. He welcomed the chance to purge more of the toxins from his system. The feeling of that warmth surging through him, flushing out the impurities and healing him from the inside out, was incredible.
[SFX: PUNCHES AND KICKS]
While Zane secretly savored the feeling, Andrew, fueled by pure rage, attacked with everything he had, giving him no room to breathe. He threw a quick jab at Zane's chest, followed immediately by a brutal cross. Before Zane could even mount a defense, Andrew was on him, unleashing a whirlwind of strikes—an elbow to the ribs, a sharp side kick, a front snap kick to the gut. He hammered Zane relentlessly with every savage move he knew.
[SFX: RUSHING ENERGY]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BUILDUP]
A powerful rush of warmth surged through Zane, feeling like a dam had just burst inside him. He could feel the Toxins in his bones being squeezed out, washed away bit by bit by that intense heat.
[AMBIENT: CROWD CHEERING]
"Yeah, that's it! Finish him, Andrew!" Grey Hawn screamed from the sidelines, pumping his fist as if he were landing the blows himself.
A few other Lowell kids cheered for Andrew, but many more stood silent, their eyes fixed on Zane. Most of them were from the outer branches of the family, and deep down, they missed the days when Zane's father had led House Lowell with fairness and honor. Back then, your talent mattered more than your name.
But everything changed when the second and third branches seized control. They hoarded all the resources and privileges, leaving the rest to fight for scraps.
Zane, the former young master, was the last of his line. Since his uncles had spared his life, he represented their only hope. They knew that if he died, any chance of a just leader returning to House Lowell would die with him.
Meanwhile, Zane just kept taking Andrew's hits.
[SFX: MULTIPLE BLOWS]
[MUSIC: TENSE OBSERVATION]
Owen Lowell, Andrew's father, frowned as he watched. He knew his son's capabilities inside and out. After taking the Aether Crystal, Andrew's strength had skyrocketed. Each punch landed with the force of a battering ram; he was almost as strong as a war beast. Owen was certain his son was the second most powerful fighter of the younger generation, outmatched only by Perrin.
He watched Andrew land dozens of punishing blows on Zane. The attacks were ferocious, and yet, while Zane was clearly losing ground, he wasn't seriously hurt. Why wasn't he going down?
Owen knew the old saying: the best defense is a good offense. It was true because absorbing an attack was infinitely harder than dishing one out. Even a seasoned warrior like himself would likely crumble under such a relentless assault from Andrew. That's why legendary body-strengthening techniques like the King Kong Cover and the Radiant Body were so priceless.
Zane had taken so many brutal hits, but he was still on his feet. Did that mean he'd learned some special body-refining technique? Or had Owen's own older brother discovered a method to strengthen the body, kept it secret from the family, and taught it to Zane before he died?
Considering Zane had been taking beatings in the Training Hall for two years and was still alive and kicking, it was obvious he knew something they didn't.
A greedy glint appeared in Owen's eyes. Of course. It had to be a secret technique.
[AMBIENT: COMBAT ARENA]
On the arena floor, Andrew was growing impatient. His constant attacks weren't working. His punches got harder, his strikes more vicious as he hammered away at Zane's body.
"Shadow Fist!"
[SFX: DEVASTATING IMPACT]
A devastating force, equivalent to six simultaneous punches, crashed into Zane's chest with bone-rattling intensity. The impact reverberated through the arena, drawing gasps from onlookers.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSE]
Andrew had staked everything on the destructive power of the Shadow Fist, certain it would bring his cousin to his knees. Yet Zane merely grunted, his stance unwavering as though rooted to the earth itself. Not only was he still standing, but his eyes—to Andrew's mounting horror—seemed to emanate an eerie, unsettling glow that betrayed something beyond mere endurance.
"Impossible," Andrew whispered under his breath, his confidence crumbling like sand.
A knot of dread coiled in Andrew's stomach, tightening with each passing second. This opponent defied everything he understood about combat. The familiar certainty that had accompanied him into the arena evaporated, replaced by a creeping, unfamiliar fear. How was Zane absorbing such punishment? What dark secret allowed him to withstand attacks that would cripple others?
Then, most disturbing of all, Andrew caught sight of it—a small, knowing smile playing across Zane's lips, a subtle curve that spoke volumes. That single expression shattered what remained of Andrew's self-assurance. The smile seemed to mock him, to laugh at his futile efforts, to strip away the façade of superiority he had carefully constructed.
"You're enjoying this," Andrew realized with a start, his voice barely audible.
[MUSIC: INTENSE RAGE]
The flicker of panic that had ignited within him transformed, evolving into pure, unadulterated rage that consumed his rational thought. Andrew's eyes blazed with fury, his vision clouding with a crimson haze that narrowed his world to a single point—the smiling face of his former cousin, the living embodiment of his failure.
