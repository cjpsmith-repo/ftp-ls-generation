Zane took a deep breath, letting the flame and lightning course through him.

[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]

The opposing forces converged at the center of his skull with startling intensity, heat, and electricity intertwined. As the elements refined his body from within, the twentieth Nexus Point of his Crown Conduit began to pulse with golden light—faint at first, then growing steadier with each passing moment.

[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME][MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT STRINGS]

An hour later, the shimmering glow faded to nothing. Zane opened his eyes, feeling fundamentally changed. He had just refined his first Nexus Point—a milestone that should have taken years.

Body cultivators were a rare breed in the Heartlands, though not unheard of. A few smaller local sects maintained disciples who specialized in the arduous practice, dedicating their entire cultivation paths to refining flesh and bone.

Some spent decades in meditation chambers, channeling energy day after day with little progress. Many gave up entirely, their bodies unable to withstand the strain.

Zane had done it in an hour.

His incredible speed would have humbled any of those struggling body refiners. More than that, it would have shattered their understanding of what was possible. Zane felt a flicker of satisfaction, quickly tempered by the knowledge that this was merely the beginning. Nineteen more Nexus Points awaited him in the Crown Conduit alone.

Several factors contributed to his remarkable speed. Zane's body had basically already been transformed into a Spirit Weapon. The initial absorption of Primordial Energy had fundamentally altered his physical structure, laying the groundwork that others could never replicate.

For Zane, absorbing Primordial Energy followed a brutal but straightforward path: endure punishment until his body broke down and rebuilt itself stronger. The seventh brother had explained that advanced masters faced far greater difficulty getting their hands on even a fraction of such power.

The Primordial Energy coursing through his veins had transformed his body at the cellular level. This gave him an efficiency in refining Nexus Points that defied conventional understanding—what took others decades of painstaking meditation, he accomplished through sheer physical superiority.

Zane flexed his fingers, feeling the energy pulse beneath his skin. The power hummed with a clarity that felt almost alive, responsive to his slightest intention. He'd never fully appreciated how profoundly different his cultivation journey had become until this moment, standing at the threshold of a realm most body refiners never reached.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL]

The flame and lightning he used were Law Power, the purest form of energy in existence.

Body cultivators in The Heartlands had to jump through hoops just to find flame and lightning power suitable for their cultivation, and even then, what they found was nowhere near as pure as Zane’s. The energy drawn from the earth realm was typically more volatile and harder to control. They had to be far more careful, inching forward with caution. If they tried to channel it directly into their Nexus Points like Zane, they’d be risking their lives.

Without pausing, Zane moved on to the next Nexus Point.

An hour later, he had refined the points in his temples.

Next, he focused on the Nexus Point in his forehead.

Then came the points beside his ears and eyebrows.

[AMBIENT: EVENING CITY SOUNDS]

[MUSIC: TRANSITIONAL STRINGS]

Time flew by as he lit up one Nexus Point after another. Outside, the Oracle City skyline burned orange as the sun sank low, finally surrendering the sky to darkness.

[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY CROWD]

[MUSIC: ANTICIPATORY DRUMS]

The buzz in Oracle City centered on a single topic: the Martial Tournament.

The World Merchants' Guild had set up a massive competition arena that dominated the city's heart. Sprawling stands stretched twenty thousand feet in length and rose twelve rows high, engineered to accommodate a hundred thousand spectators. The scale of it made Zane's breath catch—he'd never seen construction of such magnitude, not even in the Heartlands.

As he made his way toward the headquarters, the sheer number of cultivators flooding the streets left him staggered. They came from every direction, their robes bearing insignias of sects he'd never seen, speaking in dialects that twisted his ear. Some moved with the casual confidence of Soul Realm masters; others radiated an intensity that made the air around them shimmer.

Zane's pulse quickened. Somewhere among this sea of cultivators were fighters who could crush him without breaking stride. The thought should have terrified him, yet instead, a familiar fire kindled in his chest, a determination to succeed no matter what.

He touched the gold and crystal nameplate hidden beneath his robe, drawing strength from its weight. The Guild believed in him. The Cobalt Dragon trained him in divine texture. He wasn't the helpless boy anymore.

[AMBIENT: MASSIVE CROWD ROAR]

[SFX: TOURNAMENT PREPARATIONS]

Even though it was still early, the area was already a packed, roaring mass of cultivators.

[AMBIENT: MASSIVE CROWD MURMUR]

[MUSIC: GRAND ORCHESTRAL]

A huge number of them were from The Heartlands. Countless sects, powerful families, and influential groups—like the Cloud Hall and the Ethereal Spirit Sect—had all made the journey.

Millions of cultivators had flooded the city to watch, and even that was just a fraction of the total crowd expected.

[SFX: VENDOR SHOUTING]

"Tickets to the Martial Tournament! Just one supreme Aether crystal each!" a man shouted as he wove through the throng.

Zane was speechless. One supreme Aether crystal for a single ticket? The price was insane.

Still, even at that crazy price, tens of thousands of cultivators were lining up to pay. The World Merchants' Guild really knew how to turn a profit. Either that, or these cultivators were just obscenely rich.

[MUSIC: DETERMINED STRINGS]

Zane picked up his pace and headed straight for the competition arena.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STOPPING]

A steward from the World Merchants' Guild stepped in his path. "Hold it! If you're here to watch, you need to go that way."

Glancing at Zane and seeing he was only at grade four of the Soul Realm, the steward didn't give a second thought to the idea that he might be there to compete.

Zane offered a polite smile. "I think there's been a misunderstanding, sir. I'm a contestant."

[SFX: DISMISSIVE LAUGH]

"A contestant?" the steward asked, a short, humorless laugh escaping his lips. He sized Zane up with a dismissive look. "Kid, do you even know where you are? This isn't the place for jokes. Now, beat it."
