Ep 255 - Breaking Down Barriers
Word count: 1269
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
In the tense moments before their battle, Zane and Richard carefully assessed one another, each searching for weaknesses in their opponent’s demeanor.
Richard had no desire to avenge Mack. As far as he was concerned, Mack was a genius with magical arrays—a true savant in the arcane arts. The troubling reality, however, was that this genius came packaged with a sadistic streak; Mack relished bloodshed and had transformed killing into a macabre hobby, his eyes lighting up at others’ suffering.
Within the Skyreach Sect, blind loyalty and raw power reigned supreme, while ethical considerations remained conspicuously absent from their doctrine. This moral vacuum deeply disturbed Richard, whose conscience frequently troubled him during sleepless nights. But he felt powerless against the Sect’s hierarchy. Rather than fighting an entrenched system, he had carved out a private sanctuary of principles, adhering to personal ethics while navigating the Sect’s ruthless corridors.
Richard’s thoughts drifted to Master Young’s countless hours of patient instruction at Blackrock Peak. The master had believed in him when others dismissed his potential, investing time and knowledge that transformed Richard’s abilities. Rejecting Young’s directive now was a betrayal he couldn’t stomach.
A compromise materialized in his mind: perhaps he could approach Zane diplomatically and persuade him to surrender without bloodshed. Unlike Mack, who gloried in inflicting pain, Richard found no satisfaction in needless brutality, preferring victory without cruelty whenever possible.
Zane's eyes widened as Richard made his proposal. He never would have pegged Richard as such an honorable guy. With a small nod and a slight smile, he said, "The Skyreach Sect is a place where strength is all that matters, not your character. A lot of our fellow disciples are talented, sure, but they're as cruel and vicious as wild animals. Compared to them, you're a real diamond in the rough. But I’m not surrendering."
"If you don’t, I might have to get rough to win," Richard pressed. He couldn’t refuse Master Young, and he’d promised to teach Zane a lesson unless he tapped out first.
"Oh, don’t worry about that," Zane said, choosing his words carefully. "We won’t know who’s winning until the fight actually starts. Besides, even if I’m on the ropes, you can just pull your punches and give me a chance to forfeit. I doubt your master would hold that against you."
A faint smile touched Richard’s lips, his eyes reflecting a mixture of relief and respect. "Yeah, that works for me." He rolled his shoulders, the tension visibly easing from his posture. "Alright then, let’s give the audience the show they came for without losing our honor."
[SFX: BRUSH DRAWING]
With that, he drew a Runic Brush from his sleeve.
In the world of martial arts, you saw every weapon imaginable. But only a handful of fighters ever specialized in using a pen. Most of those who did wielded a Pen of Judgment—a metal stylus shaped like a brush, basically a stiletto used for stabbing.
Richard's weapon, however, had a wooden handle and a tip made of wolf hair. It was a true Runic Brush.
As Richard brandished his weapon, black Vitality began to bleed from his palms, swirling into the air around him. The haze thickened, slowly condensing into a dark, viscous liquid before forming a mysterious, hovering black orb.
When Richard dipped the tip of his brush into the substance, Zane realized what it was: ink.
His opponent had just spun his Vitality into magical ink.
Zane was stunned. Using a writing tool as a weapon was a rare sight.
He didn't pause to think about it. The moment Richard raised the brush, Zane was already moving — closing the distance in a breath, both fists driving in from opposite sides.
Richard didn't flinch. With a single fluid stroke, he wrote a character in the air beside Zane's left hand — and just like that, Zane's entire left arm went dead. The Seal glyph had locked it out completely.
Zane kept going with his right.
Still, he didn’t dare let his guard down. Richard was a top disciple from Blackrock Peak—the second strongest of the Thirty-Three Peaks—and he’d already torn through several opponents in a row. As a formidable fighter himself, Zane knew that underestimating your opponent was a good way to get yourself killed.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIELD IMPACT]
[SFX: MAGICAL BATTLE SOUNDS]
As the battle intensified, Richard emerged as a formidable adversary far beyond Zane’s expectations. A constellation of defensive spells hovered around him like luminous sentinels, requiring Zane to deliver multiple strikes merely to fracture a single shield. The moment one barrier began to crumble, Richard’s brush danced through the air with elegant precision, crafting a replacement while Zane still struggled against the remaining defenses.
The Hymn of Warding transformed Richard into an almost impenetrable fortress, his face betraying no strain as he maintained the complex magical architecture surrounding him. Sweat beaded on Zane’s brow as frustration mounted within him.
Between exchanges, Richard spoke as calmly as if they were in a training hall. "Each Hymn of Warding shield holds for five strikes. The Seal glyph on your left arm locks power and Vitality both — normally it won't break for an hour."
Zane stared at him. "You're telling me how to beat you?"
Richard dipped his brush and kept writing. "I prefer a fair fight."
Zane swung his left arm — dead weight. He focused inward and pushed the Phoenix Crystal's energy into it. A blood-red crack split the Seal glyph down the middle and it shattered.
[SFX: SEAL BREAKING]
A flicker of surprise crossed Richard's face. "Impressive. But breaking one glyph won't be enough." He laid down seven or eight Hymn of Warding shields in rapid succession. Then he began writing again, slower and more deliberate.
"These next three — be careful," he said.
Three Deathly Canticle glyphs formed a triangle and shot toward Zane.
While deflecting the lethal symbols of Richard’s Deathly Canticle, Zane’s thoughts whirled frantically. His eyes narrowed in concentration, seeking any vulnerability in his opponent’s seemingly perfect defense. The spectators’ voices faded to a distant murmur as he focused entirely on finding a solution.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
[SFX: MAGICAL EXPLOSION]
The instant his fist connected with the approaching “Slay” glyph, Zane felt an overwhelming counterforce emanate from the solidified character. The massive word erupted with blinding intensity, releasing a thunderous detonation that hurled him backward through the air. His stomach lurched with the sudden acceleration as his body became weightless, his vision blurring from the concussive force.
[SFX: BODY SLAMMING AGAINST BARRIER]
Zane lost his balance, thrown off his feet by the immense power as he smashed hard against the enchanted barrier surrounding the arena.
The barrier was part of the Skyreach Sect’s Great Array, and was designed to take an incredible amount of punishment. But when Zane slammed into it, the entire shield visibly trembled under the force. Every enchanted barrier had a breaking point.
[SFX: CRACKING BARRIER]
The combined force of Zane’s punch and Richard’s spell had pushed this one to its limit. A spiderweb of cracks spread from one corner.
The Referee stared, jaw-dropped at the damage. The barriers were there to separate the arena from the auditorium and protect the crowd. No one could have imagined one would crack from a single exchange. The combined strength of these two was beyond anything anyone had anticipated.
[SFX: MAGICAL REPAIR]
Seeing the unprecedented event, the Referee glanced toward an old man sitting in the auditorium—an Elder of the Skyreach Sect. Understanding the silent plea, the elder simply waved his hand. Vitality flowed from his palm and into the barrier, sealing the crack and making it whole again.
After crashing into the barrier, Zane slid to the ground. He sat there for a moment, dazed, the world spinning from the force of the blow.
