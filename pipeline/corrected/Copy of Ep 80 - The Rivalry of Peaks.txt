Ep 80 - The Rivalry of Peaks
Word count: 1220
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN SECT GROUNDS]
Zane hadn't told them he'd accepted the mission to hunt the Razor swarm when he left the Skyreach Sect, so they had no clue what he'd been up to.
"It's a long story. I'll fill you in later," Zane said, glancing around at the crowd. "So, what's with the assembly? Why's everyone hanging out here?"
Typically, new pledges at Skyreach Sect obsessed over training, rarely leaving the grounds. The unusual gathering on Misty Peak caught Zane's attention.
"While you were away, Misty Peak landed in serious trouble," Ethan explained. "Not our concern though."
"What kind of trouble?" Zane asked, bewildered that anyone would challenge a Skyreach Sect peak, even the lowest-ranked one.
Seeing the confused look on Zane's face, Rhys said, "A few days ago, some pledges from Misty Peak got into it with a few from Eagle Peak. Things kept escalating until the heads of both peaks had to step in. But when they couldn't sort it out, the head of Eagle Peak issued a challenge to Misty Peak."
"A challenge? I thought only pledges could do that, not entire peaks," Zane said, confused.
Rhys smiled and explained, "One peak can challenge another. The Skyreach Sect is full of competitions and rankings—the big rivalry between peaks every three years, the yearly trials for Core Apprentices, the competition for the Shadow Cloak... you name it."
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
They stood in the crowd, their eyes glued to the battle ring. The Core Apprentices couldn't participate in this kind of challenge. It wouldn't really affect them if the pledges lost, but it would be a huge blow to Misty Peak's reputation, so everyone was on edge.
This wasn't a spontaneous rivalry. Vulture Peak had engineered the whole thing — picked a fight with Misty Peak's disciples deliberately, escalated it until the masters had no choice but to accept the challenge. 
And the reason was simple: Misty Peak was last. The weakest peak was the easiest target. Win the challenge, and Vulture Peak walked away with Misty Peak's entire monthly share — elixirs, salary, all of it.
Zane found a good vantage point and looked up to see Dustin Blackwood standing in the center of the ring.
"Why is Dustin in the arena?" Zane asked.
Rhys said, "Eagle Peak has already won three straight matches. They've beaten three of Misty Peak's pledges: Otto, who's ranked sixteenth; Samson Voss, ranked thirteenth; and Truman, ranked ninth. So now, it's Dustin's turn."
Hearing that, Zane finally understood. Things were not looking good for Misty Peak.
The money and Energy Capsules meant little to Zane, but as a Misty Peak pledge, this was his fight too. He'd step up for their honor if needed.
[SFX: BATTLE GONG]
"It's starting," Rhys said. "Seth from Eagle Peak has already defeated three of our pledges. I hope Dustin beats him."
"Dustin's strong," someone replied, "but Seth outclasses us all, and he's only ranked sixteenth at Eagle Peak. If Dustin wins, they'll just send someone stronger."
The Misty Peak pledges muttered among themselves, their anxiety palpable. It was obvious they didn't like their chances.
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA CROWD]
Just then, a man leaped into the battle ring. He was tall—at least six feet—and rail-thin. That had to be Seth.
As soon as Seth landed, he scanned the crowd, a flicker of arrogance in his eyes. He casually sized up the pledges from Misty Peak before his gaze locked onto Dustin.
He sneered, his lip curling. "Is this the best Misty Peak has to offer? Why'd you send a butterball up here to fight me?"
"Whoa..."
Issuing challenges on another peak's territory demanded discretion, regardless of one's confidence. Antagonizing local disciples risked mob violence that even Skyreach Sect's rules couldn't effectively punish.
Seth, however, flaunted his arrogance after winning three consecutive matches, his insult instantly igniting fury among the Misty Peak pledges.
He just laughed. "What, you don't like my commentary?" he taunted. "Fine. Let's get this over with. I'm going to give you a beating so bad you won't be able to stand."
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
[SFX: SLAM]
Then Seth raised his hands, and a surge of Earthen Energy erupted from him. With a sudden, loud shout, he slammed his hands down, channeling the energy into the floor of the battle ring.
"Sacred Lunge!"
[SFX: GROUND CRACKING]
The ground cracked and groaned.
When Zane saw this, his eyebrows shot up in surprise. Seth's Life Energy seemed to have a strong affinity for the Metal Element. He was somehow able to channel his Life Energy into the battle ring, which was made of solid gold and iron.
[SFX: WOODEN STAKES ERUPTING]
A glowing brown light flashed beneath Dustin's feet as two wooden stakes erupted from the ring floor. Seth's High-level Life Energy created only semi-solid sacred wood, not the fully physical objects a Nature Level fighter would produce. Still, the stakes could inflict grave injuries if they connected. Zane watched anxiously. Despite his size, Dustin moved with unexpected agility.
For a man his size, he was impossibly fast and light on his feet. He effortlessly weaved around Seth's signature move, the Sacred Lunge, making every deadly strike miss by inches. Seth was throwing everything he had at him, and it wasn't landing.
Seeing an opening, Dustin closed the distance, a smirk spreading across his face. "Guess you're not all you're cracked up to be," he sneered.
At the taunt, Rhys Bolton leaned over and murmured to Zane, "That Sacred Lunge is Seth's ace in the hole. It's how he smoked Misty Peak three times in a row. But it looks like Dustin figured him out."
Zane nodded. Seth's power was on par with Randall's, no doubt, but Randall was the smarter fighter by a mile. Seth's attacks weren't completely useless, but relying on one signature move is a huge gamble. Once your opponent sees it coming, you're a sitting duck. The same move that had won him his last three matches was about to cost him this one.
[SFX: ENERGY GLOW]
That's when Zane saw it: a faint yellow glow under Dustin's feet. The counter. If it were him, Zane would've just overpowered the Sacred Lunge, shattering it with brute force.
But Dustin chose a smarter defense. He was channeling his Life Energy into the soles of his feet, sensing his opponent's next move before it even happened.
Seth's frustration was boiling over. With every failed lunge, his confidence withered and his aggression soured into desperation. Finally, pushed to the edge, Seth unleashed everything he had left.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGES]
An instant later, a dozen swirling brown vortexes of Life Energy erupted into being, creating multiple Sacred Lunges aimed straight for Dustin.
[SFX: SHARP WHIZZING SOUND]
A sharp whizzing sound cut through the air.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN TRAINING GROUNDS]
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
Meanwhile, at Misty Peak, a short, cocky pledge was goading the others.
"Who's your last fighter? Or are you all out? Face me!" he called, voice laden with arrogance.
Misty Peak had lost four contestants, with only one remaining.
"Casey's our best option. He's fourth-ranked."
"Terry's stronger!"
"Henry could defeat him!"
"Henry's retreated to master combat. Our rivals are exploiting his absence—the masters should never have agreed to this challenge!"
Henry, widely regarded as Misty Peak's finest pledge, had isolated himself for his imminent breakthrough to Nature Level.
"Misty Peak breeds only cowards," sneered the short man, relishing his apparent victory. "Your defeat is inevitable!"
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
[AMBIENT: HUSHED CROWD]
Zane stood, his face a mask of glacial fury and lethal focus. The room fell silent as all eyes turned to him.
