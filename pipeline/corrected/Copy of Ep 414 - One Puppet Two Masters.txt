Ep 414 - One Puppet, Two Masters

Word count: 1113

[AMBIENT: TRAINING GROUND CROWD]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Marcus and the other top disciples could only stare, their jaws slack with disbelief.

It didn't matter how talented Zane was. It didn't matter that he'd shattered Frank's arm or scored impossibly high on Lady Isla's trial. At his core, he remained an Adept—and Adepts couldn't fly. 

This was a fundamental law of Vitality cultivation, as unbreakable as gravity itself. Only those who had reached the Soul Realm possessed the power to burn their Vitality and suspend themselves in the air. This rule had stood unbroken throughout all of recorded history, carved into every cultivation manual, whispered by every master to their disciples.

Yet there Zane hovered, floating as steadily as a stone suspended in clear water, his robes barely stirring in the breeze. His breathing was calm and controlled. Not even a tremor in his limbs betrayed the effort such a feat should require.

Marcus's blood-red spear trembled in his grip, his knuckles white with tension. His eyes burned with a mixture of fury and something else—something that looked dangerously close to fear

[SFX: MAGICAL CHIME]

While the crowd below erupted in chaos, Zane floated calmly in the air, his expression betraying none of the turmoil churning inside him. A faint smirk played across his lips as he reached for his signaling token, sending a quiet message to Tia. "Didn't expect you to be so nice."

Her reply arrived instantly, and the words extinguished his amusement like water on a flame.

[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS TONE]

"If you die that easily, how am I supposed to exact my revenge? I'd much rather torture you. Slowly." Even through the token, he could sense the cruel satisfaction in her tone. "Starting tomorrow, you're going to come here and challenge the elite disciples of Cloud Hall. Every. Single. Day. Much more interesting than a quick death, don't you think?"

Zane could only manage a grim smile. Sure, having a powerful backer at Cloud Hall had its advantages, but he really wished her training methods weren't so… homicidal.

The weight of what she was demanding settled over him like iron chains. Daily challenges against Soul Realm masters who would gladly crush him. Daily humiliation. Daily brushes with death. And all of it orchestrated by someone who found his suffering entertaining.

But it wasn't like he had a choice in the matter.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

Marcus wasn't about to give him a moment's peace. The shock of witnessing an Adept fly flickered across his face—genuine surprise that momentarily broke his composure. But that astonishment wouldn't translate into mercy. Flight was standard for any Soul Realm cultivator, after all, particularly for a ninth-level master of his caliber.

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

Marcus forced the shock from his mind. His pride wouldn't allow him to be made a fool by someone who shouldn't even be capable of what he was witnessing. He leveled his weapon at Zane once more, determination hardening his features.

Zane moved with unbelievable agility, his body flowing through the air as though he'd been flying for years. Marcus gave chase, his weapon cutting through the air in a furious barrage—dozens of strikes, each one precise and deadly. Yet he couldn't even graze his target.

Frustration began to creep into Marcus's movements. His breathing grew heavier. How was this possible? An Adept shouldn't possess this kind of aerial mastery. The ease with which Zane evaded him defied everything Marcus understood about cultivation realms and their limitations.

[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]

Marcus couldn't comprehend Zane's speed. He couldn't see the invisible thread of light connecting Zane’s body to Saint Morphen's hands miles away, a connection that allowed the master puppeteer to control Zane's every move.

It was a hopeless fight. Marcus wasn't just up against Zane; he was up against Saint Morphen, whose legendary Fusion Realm puppetry skills allowed him to channel his own power directly into his marionette. Marcus wasn't just fighting an Adept—he was fighting two masters at once.

After watching the one-sided chase for a moment, Tia let out a lazy stretch and a yawn.
Then Saint Morphen's fingers moved again.
Zane's mouth opened. "Ha! Cloud Hall's elites are all trash! Come find me tomorrow — I'll be back every single day!"
The building erupted in curses. Zane's body turned, kicked the door open, and floated away through the abuse.
[SFX: DOOR CRASH]
[SFX: CROWD SHOUTING]
A short distance away, control released. Zane dropped, hit the ground, and immediately grabbed his signaling token.
"Tia, I'm begging you. Don't play with me like this."
Tia's reply was cheerful. "Rest up. We go again tomorrow morning."
Zane stared at the token for a long moment, then put it away.

[AMBIENT: STONE HALL WITH CROWD MURMUR]

[MUSIC: ANTICIPATION]

Meanwhile, inside the grey stone Elite House, the disciples had already gathered. They were all itching to see the arrogant newcomer for themselves.

One disciple grumbled."Dominic, you should be the one to put that kid in his place. He's from some second-rate sect in the Eastern Territories, acting like he owns the world. He's got no respect for any of us!"

But Dominic just shook his head. "A little confidence isn't a crime. Besides, there's a line of people waiting to challenge him. He'll learn his lesson. He's just an Adept—beating him wouldn't be anything to brag about."

"Dominic's right," another disciple chimed in. "He's only an Adept. He doesn't even deserve to be in the same ring as Dominic!"

[AMBIENT: TENSE HALL]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

"There he is! Look!"

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

Zane appeared in the doorway. Against his will, his body moved on its own, carrying him inside with smooth, confident strides he hadn't commanded.

The disciples of the Elite House stared daggers at him. For most of them, this was their first glimpse of the upstart who'd shattered Frank's arm and reputation in a single clash.

Until now, many had dismissed the rumors as exaggeration. But seeing Zane in the flesh, they realized the impossible was true. What shocked them even more was his cultivation level. He hadn't even reached the peak of the Adept Realm. He was only at the fourth level.

[MUSIC: DARK TENSION RISING]

The disciples glared with undisguised hostility, their collective killing intent so thick it seemed to press against Zane's skin like a physical force. 

At the same time, several noticed something peculiar. Despite the rumors of his arrogance, Zane's face bore an innocent, almost honest expression—open and guileless in a way that didn't match his reputation at all. The disconnect made what came out of his mouth all the more jarring.

[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]

"Well, well," his voice drawled with a condescension he didn't feel, each word dripping with mockery. "Look at all the 'elites.' Been waiting long?"

The words had barely left his mouth when Zane's own expression soured, his jaw tightening with frustration.

Inside, he cursed Tia a million times over, each mental insult more creative than the last.
