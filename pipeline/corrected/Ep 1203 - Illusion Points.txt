Ep 1203 - Illusion Points

Word count: 1166

[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
[AMBIENT: BUSY CITY STREET]
Zane and the cloaked warrior stood their ground in the heart of the Ogre city, Thane’s silver-armored bulk looming over them, his threat of “mercy”—being killed only twice—still hanging in the air.
Zane just smirked at the suggestion.
“Who cares what they say?” the cloaked warrior said, his voice low and conspiratorial. “Guys like that are just talking trash, anyway.”
“My bad,” Zane replied, then fell silent.
Seeing their casual attitudes, Thane’s face twisted in anger. 
He took two heavy steps forward, a cruel smile spreading across his lips. “You’ll regret this.”
The cloaked warrior immediately pinched the bridge of his nose. “Actually,” he said, “I’m already starting to.”
Thane looked confused for a second.
“Your breath stinks,” the warrior added flatly.
Thane drew himself up to his full height and roared at his followers, “You two, tail them! The second they step out of the city, kill them on sight!”
[SFX: WARRIORS SHOUTING]
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS SURROUNDING]
“Yes, sir!” his warriors bellowed in unison, immediately moving to surround Zane and the cloaked man.
Two hours later, the wait was over.
[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE THEME]
[AMBIENT: MASSIVE BATTLEFIELD]
All at once, the curtain of light that had been protecting every city in the Illusion Battlefield vanished. 
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: BATTLE CRIES]
The moment it disappeared, warriors poured out of the city gates, and an endless slaughter began across the plains.
“Time’s up. Let’s head out,” the cloaked warrior suggested.
Zane nodded, and the two of them began to stroll toward the city entrance, completely unhurried. 
[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]
As they walked, Zane noticed faint red streaks of light flying back toward the city from the battlefield outside. He knew exactly what they were: the souls of warriors who had already been killed.
According to the rules of the Illusion Battlefield, when a warrior died, their body would dissolve into a stream of light and return to the nearest city for resurrection. For most, this place was less a competition and more the world’s most intense training ground. The only real penalty for death was the loss of Illusion Points.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: ARMOR CLANKING]
As Zane and his temporary ally wandered out of the city, the two squads of Ogre warriors fell into step right behind them. They were sticking to Thane’s orders, ready to kill their targets the moment they were clear of the gates. What they didn’t know was just who, exactly, they were dealing with.
“I don’t want to fight you just yet,” the cloaked warrior told Zane as they neared the exit.
“That’s fine,” Zane replied casually. “I can wait.”
“Good,” the warrior said, a grin evident in his voice.
[MUSIC: TENSE SUSPENSEFUL]
[AMBIENT: CITY GATE MURMURS]
Normally, warriors either charged straight for a target or sprinted out of the city, on high alert. No one ever lingered by the gates. But after Zane and the cloaked warrior stepped outside, they just kept walking at a slow, leisurely pace, effectively blocking the main exit.
“Thane’s orders,” the Ogre squad leader called out. “Cut them down every time they try to leave the city—keep doing it until every last Illusion Point is stripped from them!” The moment the words left his mouth, the entire group surged forward.
Zane and the cloaked warrior turned to face them at the same moment, matching smiles spreading across their faces—the look of wolves who’d just spotted a flock of sheep.
Zane didn’t draw a weapon. He simply condensed Chaos Energy at his fingertip into a slender wind blade, spinning it lazily in place.
[SFX: WHISTLING BLADE]
[SFX: BODIES FALLING]
Zane moved through them like they were grass beneath a scythe, six of them gone before they’d even registered him as a threat.
The cloaked warrior fought just as efficiently—tucking his pipe behind his ear, he never drew a weapon either, just laid his palms gently against heads and chests in a string of strange seals.
[SFX: BONES CRACKING]
Each touch ended in a sickening crunch, bodies bursting apart like overripe fruit. Five Ogres fell to his hands alone.
[AMBIENT: LIGHT MOTES DRIFTING]
As each Ogre died, their body dissolved—releasing a cluster of pale motes alongside a single red one. 
The red mote always wrapped itself around the fallen warrior’s soul and drifted back toward the city, waiting to be reborn. The pale motes, though, drifted straight toward whoever had landed the kill. These were Illusion Points—the Battlefield’s true currency.
Every warrior started the Illusion Battlefield with one hundred points. Each death halved whatever points remained—fifty after a first death, twenty-five after a second, and so on—with the difference flowing straight to the killer.
The cloaked warrior’s five kills had been each warrior’s first death, netting him two hundred fifty points total. Zane’s six kills did the same, earning him three hundred—bringing his total, with his own starting hundred, to four hundred even.
A thought surfaced in Zane’s mind, unbidden—a ranking. “Overall, eight point eight billion,” he murmured. “Within this world, one point nine billion.” A faint smile crossed his face. The Battlefield had barely opened, and already warriors were fighting like their lives depended on it.
The cloaked warrior wore a helpless smile of his own—five kills put his rank lower than Zane’s. Neither of them much cared. These first kills were grass, not sheep, and grass alone wouldn’t fatten anyone up.
[SFX: CONFUSED CROWD MUTTERING]
This bizarre, casual attitude confused the crowd gathered at the gate. They stopped, unsure of what to do, which created a bottleneck. 
The warriors behind them, unable to get out, started shouting and cursing.
But no matter how much the crowd complained, the Ogres at the front refused to move. 
[SFX: DISPERSING FOOTSTEPS]
Eventually, fed up with the gridlock, most of the other warriors gave up and headed for the other city gates.
Watching them disperse, Zane knew his plan had failed. The Ogres weren’t stupid. They knew he and the cloaked warrior were guarding the entrance, and they weren’t about to rush out just to get slaughtered. Even though death here wasn’t permanent, it would still cost them points.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
The two of them exchanged a look of mutual, helpless understanding. They turned away from the gates and quickly headed off in separate directions. Both of them knew they would have to fight each other eventually, but now wasn’t the time. The fight wouldn’t be worth it until their opponent had racked up enough Illusion Points to make for a decent prize.
The moment he was clear of the gate, Zane settled back into a relaxed walk, his mind already calculating the best way to collect points. Just standing at the city gates and picking people off was unrealistic. Killing a warrior only earned fifty points, and killing the same one again only got you twenty-five. It was a terrible way to farm.
As he mulled it over, his eyes landed on a nearby mountain peak. 
[SFX: POWERFUL LEAP]
[SFX: LANDING ON STONE]
After a moment of thought, he leaped to the top of it, finding a good vantage point.
[AMBIENT: DISTANT BATTLEFIELD CHAOS]
“I was in too much of a rush,” he murmured to himself, watching the chaos unfold below. “The sheep haven’t fattened up yet, and neither have the wolves. I can wait a bit longer, until they’re all nice and plump.”
