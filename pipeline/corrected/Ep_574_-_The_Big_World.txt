Ep 574 - The Big World
Word count: 1234
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL WIND]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL WONDER]
The secrets of the Transcendent Divine Might were vaster than Zane could have ever dreamed.
[AMBIENT: NIGHT SKY ATMOSPHERE]
Countless stars twinkled trillions of miles beyond reach—unreachable even after a million years of travel.
Yet the Transcendent Divine Might guide offered hope: a method to communicate with those distant stars and channel their formidable power.
The more stars one connected with, the stronger their Stellar Body would become.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS REVELATION]
Among the mysteries of the Transcendent Divine Might lay a secret method: using one's soul to connect with distant stars. Zane had found what he sought, yet it seemed almost too impossible to believe.
Humans understood their immediate world—from the Tower of Sin looming before them to insects fluttering in stale air. They perceived trees, crowds, and starlight glimmering in the vast atmosphere.
Yet their perception of celestial bodies remained frustratingly vague. Beyond observing faint glows, no one could extract real information from any particular star. How to harness their power, how to activate it—these questions had stumped generations.
No answers had ever emerged.
[MUSIC: FOCUSED CONCENTRATION]
Blindly puzzling over it wouldn't solve anything. So instead, Zane began to experiment.
Following the secret technique, he let his perception stretch outward — slowly, carefully.
A hundred feet. Two hundred. Five hundred. A thousand.
At ten thousand feet, it stopped. Any further and it began to fray, blurring into nothing.
He frowned. "This is my limit. How am I supposed to reach stars that are millions of miles away? That's like trying to touch the moon with a stick."
The Red Dragon chuckled. "Exactly. And if you keep trying that way, it won't matter how strong you are — even someone ten thousand times more powerful than you couldn't bridge that gap by brute force. Now close your eyes."
Zane closed his eyes.
"The secret technique has a name for what comes next," Red Dragon said. "A single thought becomes divine — and your consciousness roams the void."
Following the Red Dragon's guidance, Zane shifted the way he was operating his perception. Instead of pushing outward, he let it rise — condensing at the top, pulling upward, thinning into a single point.
Then everything disappeared.
He couldn't feel his body. His consciousness had merged entirely with his perception, and something above was pulling him — fast, impossibly fast — up and away from the ground.
[SFX: WHOOSH ASCENSION]
[AMBIENT: HIGH ALTITUDE WIND]
He didn't have to wait long before the main continent itself began to shrink, growing smaller and smaller below him. Captivated by the view, the young man thought it looked like a tiny boat adrift on an endless ocean.
[MUSIC: AWE-INSPIRING ORCHESTRAL]
“So… huge,” Zane mumbled, awestruck by what his formless consciousness was revealing to him. A profound sense of wonder washed over him. The world was so much bigger than he'd ever imagined.
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL WIND]
As Zane's consciousness continued to expand, he saw seven or eight continents appear across the limitless ocean. The higher he rose, the more he could see. At this staggering height, he could finally distinguish the continents from one another, all separated by immense distances.
[SFX: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
Narrowing his spiritual eyes, his gaze locked onto one continent in particular, and he gasped. “That’s the Heartlands!”
His heart pounded. Among the continents, the Heartlands was the smallest. Beyond the Chaotic Ocean of Stars lay the union of seven divine kingdoms—ten times larger than the Heartlands.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Dazed, he turned and gasped. "Another continent?!"
This unknown landmass dwarfed everything—a hundred times larger than the union of seven divine kingdoms. The Heartlands, the union, and even the main continent appeared as insignificant specks beside this giant.
"What is that place?" he whispered, mesmerized.
His curiosity about the upper world surged. Despite its hundred thousand great worlds and tens of thousands of races, a sinking realization struck him: he knew almost nothing about the world he called home.
[AMBIENT: RUSHING WIND]
As he flew farther and farther away, the continents shrank until they were just vague shapes in the distance. Then, his consciousness suddenly accelerated, rising infinitely higher, with no telling where it was taking him.
[MUSIC: NOSTALGIC STRINGS]
A memory surfaced, unbidden.
As a small child, Zane had asked his father what lay beyond the Eastern Reaches, then the Heartlands, then the sea. His father had given him a book of myths instead—stories claiming the sea ended at a great cliff where waters poured endlessly over the edge.
The young Zane had asked what lay at the bottom. No one could tell him.
Now, rising higher than any living being should, Zane saw the answer.
The sea ended not at a cliff, but at a wall of white mist—vast, impenetrable, cutting across the ocean. The entire world was enclosed by it, forming a diamond shape.
Beyond the mist lay another sea. Another world, pressed against his own.
"The worlds are connected," he murmured.
Then suddenly, a massive vortex of wind materialized around him.
[SFX: THUNDEROUS BOOM]
[AMBIENT: VIOLENT WINDSTORM]
His features tightened with anxiety. The howling gusts could shred anything—wind masses colliding with piercing booms, spawning gales like a tornado magnified tenfold. The windstorms grew exponentially in endless cycles of creation and destruction.
No one below had known such gales existed. They'd likely been here since the universe's dawn, yet no living being had ever breached this impenetrable layer.
Even his physical body—a top-grade Mythic Weapon—would be ground to dust.
Then he glanced down and remembered that he wasn't in his physical body. Relief washed over him.
Safe in spirit form, Zane looked back one last time at the world below.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]
He bit his lip. It was instantly clear that there were multiple worlds, all linked together. Each one was separated by four thick, wall-like mists, just like the ones he'd seen around his own world. From this distance, the interconnected worlds looked like menacing scales on the hide of some vicious beast.
“What is that?” he whispered, his eyes wide with a strange mix of horror and fascination.
[AMBIENT: DEEP COSMIC RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL CRESCENDO]
It took him a moment to process what he was seeing. Those weren't just scales floating in space. They were attached to something. A colossal turtle-beast.
[SFX: DEEP RESONANT HUM]
The creature was coated in absolute black, as if born from the void of night itself. It looked like a giant turtle crawling at an impossibly slow pace, seeming to absorb any light that dared to touch it.
Despite the primal fear the creature stirred in him, Zane couldn't help but marvel at it, especially at its remarkable scales.
Dumbfounded, he finally understood: every diamond-shaped scale was a world. The colossal turtle-beast was crawling across more than a dozen of them, which meant it was carrying over a dozen distinct worlds on its back. The fact that it could even move with such a burden was astounding.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION BUILD]
As Zane stared, dazed, at the colossal beast, the realization hit him like a stone in his gut. With a body larger than a dozen worlds combined, it could probably crush a world with a single stomp of its colossal feet.
The breath caught in his throat. He was speechless. As his astonishment peaked, Zane could only blink at the beast, a wave of shock washing over his entire being.
