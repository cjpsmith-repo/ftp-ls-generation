Ep 300 - The Grand Scheme of Things
Word count: 1244
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL DRAGON]
The Cobalt Dragon chuckled at Zane, amused by his naivete. "This world stretches beyond the boundaries of your imagination, teeming with marvels that would eclipse your wildest dreams. If you actually manage to reach the pinnacle, you'll get to see some of those wonders for yourself. But the journey there remains nearly insurmountable."
The Cobalt Dragon sighed, shaking its ancient head. "Even the Demon Night, creatures blessed with extraordinary power from birth, represent merely a drop in the vast ocean of races that inhabit the cosmos."
Zane’s mind reeled at the implication. A race that strong counts for nothing in the grand scheme of things? The revelation shattered his perception of power and possibility.
Noting the stunned expression on Zane’s face, the Cobalt Dragon softened its tone. "Don’t lose heart, Zane. Humans, for all their frailties, possess a remarkable potential for greatness." Its voice resonated with unexpected warmth. "Given your exceptional talent, the undeniable luck that seems to follow you, and…" The creature hesitated, nearly mentioning The Soulforge before reconsidering. Its luminous eyes studied Zane thoughtfully before concluding, "You might—and I stress might—have a sliver of a chance to reach the top."
[SFX: GLIMMERING SOUND]
At that moment, the Cobalt Dragon saw the glint in Zane’s eyes. They lit up like a star-filled sky, burning with excitement for the challenges ahead.
This kid didn’t hear a single word I said, the Cobalt Dragon mused.
Zane’s unexpected reaction stirred something long dormant within the ancient being. Melancholy washed over the immortal creature. The Cobalt Dragon hadn’t experienced such deflated emotions in countless centuries, perhaps millennia. Where most would crumble under the weight of cosmic insignificance, this human boy somehow drew strength from it.
The massive entity observed Zane’s determined expression, noting how his shoulders squared rather than slumped, how his breathing steadied rather than faltered. In the face of overwhelming odds, the boy appeared invigorated rather than defeated.
"Maybe I’ve underestimated this one after all," the Cobalt Dragon conceded silently, its massive head shaking in reluctant appreciation. Something about Zane’s stubborn spirit reminded the ancient being of heroes long turned to dust—mortals who had defied the very boundaries of possibility.
[MUSIC: DETERMINED HEROIC]
Just then, Zane's gaze landed on the strange weapon left behind by the Demon Night. "Cobalt Dragon," he said, "I know where I come from. And even though I've made a name for myself in the Skyreach Sect, I'm not about to get cocky or rest on my laurels. I'm not gonna stop pushing just to have an easy life! We humans have the will to keep going, to fight on, even when we're staring down a thousand men."
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
"Whatever—or whoever—gets in my way, I'll tear right through them!"
Just one thousand men?
The Cobalt Dragon almost shot back by instinct.
But Zane wasn't finished. "Of course, I don't mean that literally. I know I'll have to face countless powerhouses in this world—the best of the best among thousands, millions, even trillions of creatures!"
Since the dawn of time, those who reached for the heavens always possessed incredible talent and an unstoppable drive.
[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE STRINGS]
As Zane grew, he'd met one genius after another, and his understanding of the world had expanded with each one. He'd faced people like Jacob, Finn, Patrick, and so many others.
The stronger his opponents, the more ferocious his own will to reach the summit of martial arts became.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL PRESENCE]
In the Cobalt Dragon's eyes, Zane was weaker than an ant. But there was something about the kid's sudden fire, his raw will to conquer everything, that actually moved the ancient dragon.
This kid is... interesting, the dragon thought. A shame I can't wake my third brother to talk this over with him. Well, I'll just have to wait and see.
In truth, the nine dragons all belonged to the same clan.
Somehow, they had all been turned into stone sculptures, bound to The Soulforge. Now that The Soulforge resided in Zane's mind, the nine dragons had come along for the ride.
The Cobalt Dragon's first impression of Zane had been... underwhelming. By the dragon's standards, the kid was painfully average in every way. But he was proving to be full of surprises.
As Zane drew close, the plain sword erupted — not with a spirit, but with raw killing intent. Pure, concentrated, murderous. No figure emerged. Just a wave of death aimed directly at his head.
"No sword spirit?" Zane noted.
"A low-grade Mysterious weapon can't cultivate one. But even without it — feel that intent." 
[SFX: KILLING INTENT BLAST]
He did. Ten strikes to shatter it. More than anything else on the mountain.

When the sword finally stilled, a single spirit escaped — blood-red, the size of a grain of rice. Tiny compared to everything he'd collected before.
The moment it entered his body, something changed. The killing aura inside it spread through every spirit he'd absorbed, and the colors began to shift. Black, yellow, blue — all bleeding toward red. They were fusing. 
[SFX: SPIRITS MERGING]
The Cobalt Dragon went still. Sword spirit fusion took years — absorb, fuse, absorb, fuse, over and over until maturity. He had seen countless swordsmen walk that path. None of them had done it like this.
Zane had barely reached the mountainside.
Inside his body, the fusing spirits were taking shape — vague at first, then clearer. Atorso. Arms. A head. A face. 
[SFX: SPIRIT COALESCING]
[SFX: SUDDEN REALIZATION]
What on earth is going on?
I’ve never seen this woman in my life. So why the hell did my sword spirits take her shape?
[MUSIC: CONFUSED TENSION]
Zane was completely lost.
Staring at the beautiful, human-shaped sword spirit, he just couldn't wrap his head around it.
If he'd had any say in the matter, his sword spirits would not have formed into a woman. It was just... too damn weird. The thing was, shaping sword spirits was nothing like shaping Vitality.
Molding Vitality was a simple matter of willpower. If a practitioner was halfway through and didn’t like the shape, they could just dissolve it and start over in their mind. No big deal. Plenty of practitioners could do it.
But the shape of sword spirits was out of the practitioner’s hands. It was determined by the collective will of every single sword spirit inside the user at the very moment they coalesced.
The practitioner had absolutely no control over the outcome.
Seeing the utterly baffled look on Zane’s face, the Cobalt Dragon let out a low snicker.
"Cobalt Dragon, what the hell is going on?" Zane demanded, his head swimming.
"If I had to guess, the woman your sword spirits formed is the original owner of that sword," the dragon replied, its voice flat.
"What? No way." At the dragon's words, the color drained from Zane's face. "The Demon Night aren't human, are they?"
"So why does she look like... this? She's more beautiful than any human I've ever seen!"
By any human standard, the woman who had materialized from his sword spirits was flawless from every conceivable angle.
Zane's travels had brought him face-to-face with Demons, ogres, and other foul creatures. The ogres were passable, he guessed. But the Demons he'd fought back in Alba Imperia were a different story entirely. Their appearance was the stuff of nightmares.
[MUSIC: EXCITING DISCOVERY]
When the Cobalt Dragon first mentioned the Demon Night, Zane had pictured something like the Demons he was used to—all extra mouths and misplaced eyes.
But to his utter shock, this woman of the Demon Night was breathtaking.
In a way, she was even more perfect than any human could ever be.
