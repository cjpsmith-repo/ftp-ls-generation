Ep 305 - Thunderstruck
Word count: 1200
[MUSIC: EPIC BATTLE]
Zane admired a lot of things about Finn Donovan, but his humility was right at the top of the list.
Finn had started with nothing, so staying grounded after getting so damn good couldn’t have been easy.
Some of the other students from similar backgrounds got a massive ego after just a few wins. They'd start acting like they were God’s gift to the world, thinking they were untouchable. Then they'd start slacking off in their training. Even the most talented among them lost their edge to arrogance, and over time, they just faded into the background.
But that wasn’t Finn. He never got arrogant or reckless. He kept his head on straight, even when Zane’s performance left him feeling completely outclassed. It didn’t seem to faze him one bit. He'd even told Zane he would do his best to keep up, even knowing he could probably never beat him.
Plenty of people may have surpassed Finn in natural talent, but not many had a heart as noble as his. His genuine character shone through his every action, a rare quality among the ambitious youth of the Peaks.
Still, despite his humble demeanor, Finn’s next sword move betrayed his true power—a display of breathtaking mastery that commanded respect.
Zane felt his muscles tense instinctively as he observed his opponent’s stance shift. "This won’t be like the others," he thought, his mind racing to anticipate what would come. Finn’s eyes had changed, reflecting a determination that transcended mere competition, and Zane knew better than to underestimate what was coming.
[SFX: ENERGY BLADE SWING]
Finn’s Celestial Blade technique could channel the terrifying power of the heavens with unprecedented mastery. Now that he’d mastered the Apex of Sword Intent, his technique had evolved into something far more devastating than anyone had witnessed before—a perfect fusion of skill and cosmic energy that rippled through the air with palpable force.
More importantly, during his time at The Sword Tempering Pool, Finn had unlocked the ancient secret of absorbing the power of thunder.
Now he wielded not one elemental force but two—the majestic power of thunder intertwined with the boundless might of the heavens. When these forces combined within his blade, each strike manifested as divine judgment incarnate.
[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]
Zane's breath caught as the sword came down, looking for all the world like a bolt of divine punishment.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
At Zane’s command, Lily dissolved into countless shimmering fragments of sword intent, seamlessly integrating with the Streamer clutched in his hand.
In truth, Zane hadn’t yet mastered the art of controlling his sword spirit. A more accomplished practitioner wouldn’t need this two-step process—summoning Lily first, then directing her to merge with the blade. The ideal technique would channel her directly through his body into the Streamer in one fluid motion.
But this limitation was just a matter of technique. Zane felt confident that with practice and familiarity, his connection with the sword spirit would strengthen.
Lily's Malice was unbelievably strong. It was impossible to imagine the countless creatures she must have killed to cultivate such a powerful aura. Just standing beside Zane, she contained this terrifying essence within herself, but once she merged with the Streamer, that raw, primal power erupted unrestrained.
The phenomenon resembled the subtle aura that clings to a seasoned butcher after years of slaughter. They develop an almost imperceptible presence—not visibly menacing, yet detectable by the most sensitive individuals. The average person might walk past without noticing anything unusual.
But dogs, with their acute sensitivity to such energies, will always bark wildly when a butcher passes them on the street. Their instincts recognize the lingering shadow of death, triggering profound terror in these animals.
This Malice, in its purest form, posed a direct threat to all living beings—a primordial force that whispered of extinction to anything that drew breath.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
[SFX: MAGIC BATTLE SOUNDS]
In one fluid motion, Zane unleashed the full potential of his merged sword spirit. Lily’s Malice, now channeled through Streamer, created a golden arc that sliced through Finn’s thunder-infused defenses.
[SFX: BARRIER SHATTERING]
The audience fell silent as Finn’s Celestial Blade technique—previously thought invincible—shattered against Zane’s enhanced swordplay. Finn stumbled backward, his weapon clattering to the ground as he acknowledged defeat with a respectful nod.
The referee's voice rang out across the arena. "Misty Peak's disciple, Zane Lowell, wins. Skytop Peak's disciple, Finn Donovan, loses!"
On the Misty Peak sideline, Master Warren pressed his eyes shut for a moment. From last place among the thirty-three peaks to first. He hadn't let himself believe it until just now.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
The last opponent Zane would have to face was Patrick.
So far, no one in the tournament had even come close to forcing Patrick to go all out. As the top student in the Skyreach Sect—second only to the freakishly talented Evan—it was impossible to gauge his true strength.
[SFX: SWORD UNSHEATHING]
Confronting Patrick in their upcoming match was going to be an extraordinary challenge for Zane. The prospect of facing the Sect’s second-ranked student loomed like a mountain on the horizon.
Before mastering the Apex of Sword Intent, Zane had harbored serious doubts about his chances against such a powerful opponent. But now, with that remarkable achievement under his belt and Lily’s magnificent presence at his side, his confidence had been boosted. The sword spirit’s ethereal beauty and devastating power offered him a distinct advantage few competitors could claim.
Still, trying to guess his odds felt impossible. Patrick’s true capabilities remained shrouded in mystery. Each time he stepped into the arena, he dispatched his opponents with such casual efficiency that his actual limits remained unknown.
Zane’s stomach tightened at the thought. He fervently hoped that fate might intervene, pitting Patrick against a worthy challenger before their inevitable confrontation. Such a match might force the enigmatic prodigy to reveal more of his arsenal, giving Zane precious insight into what waited for him in the finals.
Unfortunately, the list of students who could actually challenge him was pretty short. Wynn was a powerhouse, but Patrick had beaten him inside three moves. Jacob was strong too, but since Zane had absorbed his Drake Bone Spear, he couldn’t fight at full strength. As for Tracy, Zane figured she wouldn’t even bother. He bet she'd just surrender, even though she had gone all out and activated the Body of the Blue Phoenix when she'd fought him.
Then, to Zane's surprise, Tracy's name was drawn to face Patrick in the arena.
And just like Zane figured, Tracy cheerfully forfeited the second they stepped onto the platform.
Guess she only saves that killer instinct for me, Zane shook his head.
Luckily, Sir Kenneth had already expressed an interest in watching Zane fight, so after Tracy surrendered, the referee drew names again. And once again, Patrick’s name was picked.
This time, his opponent was Finn.
[AMBIENT: NEW MATCH]
A slow smile spread across Zane’s face. There weren’t many people who could force Patrick to show his hand, but Finn was the perfect guy for the job.
Still, Zane couldn’t help but wonder. How long would Finn last? And could he actually win?
Either way, things were definitely looking up.
