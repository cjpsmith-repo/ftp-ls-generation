Ep 1113 - Ten Times Stronger

Word count: 1089

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
[AMBIENT: HALL OF BLADES ECHO]
The screen in the Hall of Blades glowed, displaying the rankings of the warriors. 
If Gorman remembered correctly, the top record—set by Jasper—was a power output thirty-two times that of a standard Soul Realm warrior. By that metric alone, Jasper was easily ten times stronger than Gorman. 
Of course, a real fight had more variables than just raw power. Some attunement techniques were incredibly potent but didn’t register as pure force. Still, while it wasn’t a guarantee that Jasper was *exactly* ten times stronger, it was a sure bet he was in a completely different league.
But when Gorman’s eyes landed on the new number one, he froze. “Three hundred and seventy-nine times? That’s impossible.”
The words caught in his throat. In terms of sheer power, the person at the top of that list was over a hundred times stronger than him. They were more than ten times stronger than Jasper, the top disciple of Cloudswallow Hall.
[SFX: DISBELIEVING MUTTER]
“No way,” he mumbled, shaking his head.
A warrior capable of unleashing that kind of power couldn’t be at the Soul Sea Realm. They had to be a Spirit Supreme, at the very least. It was the only explanation that made any sense. But the strongest disciples in any of the Thirteen Palaces were only at the Soul Sea Realm; the Mist Palace didn’t accept disciples who had already reached the Spirit Supreme level.
He ran through the possibilities. It had to be a steward from the Mist Palace, right? Or maybe an executive, or the palace leader himself? But that felt wrong. The Hall of Blades was just a training area for swordsmanship. Gorman had never seen an executive practice in here before. Besides, if someone that powerful cut loose, the record wouldn’t just be a few hundred times higher. It would be tens of thousands, and their attacks would have overwhelmed the Illusionary Arena entirely.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ECHOING]
[SFX: MURMURED CONVERSATIONS]
As the sky brightened, more warriors trickled into the Hall of Blades. Most of them glanced at the ranking screen out of habit before heading inside—for the disciples of the Mist Palace, getting on that list was a massive accomplishment.
[SFX: SURPRISED EXCLAMATION]
“Three hundred and seventy-nine? Who the hell set that record?”
“Am I seeing that right?”
“You think it was Zane?”
[MUSIC: CURIOUS WONDER]
[AMBIENT: GOSSIP AND WHISPERS]
Most of the warriors weren’t qualified for the Passing the Torch competition, but even they had heard the rumors. News of the incredible displays of skill had spread like wildfire throughout the Mist Palace. Some of them guessed it had to be the newcomer who’d shaken everything up.
“Those two are so damn lucky,” one of them muttered. “I’m jealous.”
But there was nothing to be done about it. If they had the talent of Zane and Layla, they too would be showered with attention from the Silverwood and Gossamer Pavilions. 
The next morning, Layla walked into the Hall of Blades under the eyes of half the palace. Word had spread fast — everyone wanted to see what she would do with that three-seventy-nine staring down at her from the top of the board.
She glanced at it once, then stepped into the Illusionary Arena without a word.
[SFX: SPATIAL SHIFT HUM]
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]
Half an hour later, she walked back out. The screen updated. Eighty-two times. Impressive enough to knock Jasper’s thirty-two down to third — but the three-seventy-nine didn’t move.
Layla’s expression tightened. She turned and went back in.
[SFX: CROWD REACTION — LOW MURMUR]
Eighty-four. Then eighty-one. Eighty-six. Eighty-five. Eighty. Each run, she came back out, checked the screen, and went straight back in. The rankings shifted with every attempt — but only below first place. Every slot except the top was hers now, her scores stacked one after another, crowding every other warrior off the board.
The onlookers watched in silence. For most of them, a single score above eighty would have been the achievement of a lifetime. Layla was logging them like practice runs. No one said anything. There wasn’t much to say.
[MUSIC: STRAINED EFFORT]
On her final attempt, she pushed to ninety-six. When she came out, her face was pale, the color of someone who’d burned through more than just stamina. She stared at the screen for a long moment.
The three-seventy-nine held.
Layla said nothing. She turned and left.
[AMBIENT: HUSHED MURMUR]
“So it’s not her,” someone said.
“Then it has to be Zane.”
“I still can’t believe it. Maybe it’s someone else.”
But no one pushed that last argument very hard.
[MUSIC: PRESTIGIOUS REVERENCE]
Everyone knew what joining the Gossamer Pavilion meant. Each new member was given a Gossamer Token, a pass that allowed them to travel freely through any of The Alliance’s supreme worlds. No one could stop them. And if they ever ran into trouble, all they had to do was show the token to get immediate help.
For most warriors, just crossing a few supreme worlds was a massive undertaking, with tolls and fees that could bankrupt them. A token that wiped all that away was a dream.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: DISTANT WIND]
Meanwhile, Zane kept to his routine. He trained. He watched the slow churn of chaotic energy in his inner world, still thickening, still not liquefying. Occasionally, the faint echo of a Roaring Token reached him — Supreme Lords somewhere out in the universe trading barbs over some lost treasure. He listened without interest and went back to his cultivation.
Sometime later, Lincoln—the warrior Zane had faced in Passing the Torch—finally arrived at the Mist Palace, having rushed there the moment he was freed. The journey had taken him nearly two months; the distance between supreme worlds was just that insane.
[SFX: URGENT FOOTSTEPS]
After finding his sister, Layla, the first thing he asked was, “Is Zane still here?”
Layla didn’t understand why her brother was so fixated on him. In her mind, Zane was just some guy her brother had already beaten.
But then Lincoln told her the truth: the only reason he’d won his freedom was because Zane had let him.
Layla was stunned.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REALIZATION]
She suddenly remembered Zane’s cool, detached smile from the arena, and a sickening wave of understanding washed over her. It all clicked into place. In that moment, she was utterly convinced that he was out of her league in every conceivable way, from his talent to his strength, and even his character.
