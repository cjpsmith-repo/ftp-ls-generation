Ep 368 - Cut and Run

Word count: 1050

[AMBIENT: DARK CAVE]

[SFX: SKITTERING LEGS]

The first eight Ghost-Faced Spiders leaped into the air and rushed Zane. He could see their venomous fangs, which glistened with a black, oily sheen. They were clearly dripping with a virulent toxin; if they bit him, he'd be paralyzed or, even worse, killed on the spot.

Zane wasn't afraid of their venom. His body was as tough as a top-grade Spirit Weapon, immune to all poisons. Still, the thought of getting covered in that nasty gunk was disgusting.

"Break!"

[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

With a silent command, countless cyclones of sword intent lashed out at the Ghost-Faced Spiders.

[SFX: METALLIC CLANG]

To Zane's surprise, his attack met their shells with a resounding metallic clang that echoed through the valley.

"Seriously? They're that tough?" He stared at the creatures in disbelief.

His mastery of the Apex of Sword Intent was incredibly sharp—sharp enough to slice through steel like parchment. But against these creatures, it couldn't even pierce their armor. The cyclones of intent only managed to send them tumbling backward, their shells deflecting his strikes as if they were nothing more than a nuisance.

Frustration flickered through Zane's chest as he watched the spiders he'd hit roll several times across the ground. They scrambled right back to their feet, completely unharmed, their multiple eyes glinting with predatory hunger. Not a single crack marred their glossy black shells.

He clenched his fist around Streamer's hilt. This was going to be more difficult than he'd thought.

[SFX: ANGRY HISSING]

The spiders opened their mouths, releasing a buzzing hiss that sent vibrations through the air, and charged at Zane again.

He assessed his options. He knew he could kill these creatures if he went all out—their defense was certainly weaker than the inferno ray's had been. But the thought of picking them off one by one made his chest tighten with impatience. He didn't have time for this. Every moment spent fighting these mindless beasts was a moment wasted, a moment the Cloud Hall disciples gained on him.

His mind raced through the calculations. How many spiders surrounded him? Even with his speed and skill, eliminating them individually would drain precious energy he might need later.

No. There had to be a smarter way.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[SFX: SCATTERED SPIDERS]

Zane adjusted his grip on Streamer, feeling the familiar weight of the blade in his palm. Instead of striking to kill, he channeled his sword intent differently—using it as a battering force rather than a cutting edge. The cyclones of energy erupted from his blade, not to slice but to shove, sending the spiders tumbling aside like leaves before a storm.

He pushed forward along the path the Cloud Hall disciples had already cleared. The spiders scrambled after him, their chittering growing more frantic, but Zane kept moving, deflecting rather than engaging, conserving his strength for whatever lay ahead.

[AMBIENT: JUNGLE VALLEY]

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]

Once he broke past the swarm, the jungle finally opened up. He found himself in a wide valley, and that’s where he spotted them: the disciples from Cloud Hall.
The white-robed youth at the center spotted Zane the moment he emerged. His face darkened. "This is a Cloud Hall breakthrough. What are you doing here?"
"Did your family build this road?" Zane said. "Last I checked, it's open to everyone."
The youth's eyes went cold. He didn't say another word.

He turned back to the formation without another glance at Zane.

It looked like they had run into the spiders, too. The valley floor was littered with huge piles of their dead. Not far from him, twelve of the Cloud Hall disciples had arranged themselves into a special formation.

It was a common technique for warriors—channeling their Vitality through special Cultivation Methods to create powerful Magic Circles.

The twelve of them were combining their techniques, creating a formation unique to Cloud Hall that was unbelievably powerful. 

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

As a massive swarm of spiders closed in around them, the young man in a white robe at the center of the formation gave the order, "Stab of the Iron Ram!"

The six disciples on the left simultaneously thrust out their left hands, each pressing a pressure point on their right arm. The Vitality from all six of them surged together, and a massive golden spear erupted from the ground. 

[SFX: MASSIVE SPEAR IMPACT]

In the blink of an eye, it shot forward, skewering a whole line of spiders.

[SFX: FIRE WHOOSH]

The young man in white barked another order, "Burn of the Wyrm!"

Zane decided against heading off on his own. These Cloud Hall guys clearly knew more about the Cloud Road than he did; there had to be a reason they were clearing out every last spider in this valley. Zane ducked behind a rock outcropping to watch, unseen.

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL CHAMBER]

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

Meanwhile, back at the arched gateway, Zane’s image flickered to life on the Scrying Slate.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

The sheer power of the Scythe of the River-Winds had been so terrifying that Kenneth could barely stand to watch. If Zane had gotten caught in that, he’d have been a dead man, no question. Luckily, the kid was fast.

"Lennon is no pushover," Toby said, shaking his head. "Zane's just asking for trouble, and he's going to pay for it."

[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]

"Hmph. And Frank is even stronger than Lennon," Lady Sigmund chimed in, a smirk playing on her lips as she watched the image of Zane being chased by the Scythe. "Zane tucked tail and ran the second he saw Lennon. If he runs into Frank, it’ll be even worse for him. Kenneth, you really should teach your Skyreach Sect disciples their place. They only get one life, after all."

But Toby shook his head. "It's not over yet. Those Cloud Hall disciples on the Cloud Road are the cream of the crop. That Scythe of the River-Winds is powered by the Formation of the Twelve Signs, a technique that requires all thirteen of them, including Lennon. Even a strong warrior in the early Inner-Core Realm would struggle against that, let alone Zane. He was smart to run."

Kenneth seized the chance to fire back at Lady Sigmund. "Master Dillon's right. That's thirteen against one. I don't think he's the coward you're making him out to be. Besides, they can't maintain that Formation of the Twelve Signs forever. Zane might just find an opening and turn the tables!"

He looked back at the Slate. Come on kid, don't mess this up.
