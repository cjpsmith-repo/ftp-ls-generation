Ep 931 - The Strange Storm

Word count: 1177

[AMBIENT: OCEAN WAVES]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

The seven members of the Demi-Immortal Race couldn't fathom how Zane had just shattered the black wind blades.

[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSIONS]

Fifteen hundred miles away, a mountain jutted out of the sea, its peak hundreds of feet above the waves. Zane had channeled the force toward it, and in the distance, eight explosions rocked the air, one after another.

The mountain shimmered with a kaleidoscope of colors. 

It had withstood the violent storms of the Chaotic Ocean of Stars for millions of years, the black wind blades battering it relentlessly, yet it remained completely unscathed.

[MUSIC: EPIC REVELATION]

This was Sacred Mount Lodestone, a place long thought lost to the Chaotic Ocean of Stars.

Most of the mountain lay submerged, with only its peak piercing the surface. Ferocious waves crashed against its sides, and the wind blades sliced at it without end, but the mountain stood firm, unmoved.

When Zane faced the terrifying power of the Celestial Tribulation, he’d redirected its full force into the five million mountains of the world of chaos. The resulting devastation had leveled every single peak, save for those in the Eastern Reaches.

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC REFLECTION]

Zane had never meant to damage the world of chaos; it was the last thing he’d ever wanted. A deep guilt gnawed at him—he had sacrificed a world for his own survival. Sacred Mount Lodestone was indestructible; nothing could harm it. He should have directed the force here instead. But it was too late for regrets. Everything had happened so fast, there hadn't been time to think it through.

He'd learned his lesson. He wouldn't make the same mistake twice.

[AMBIENT: MECHANICAL WHIR]

[SFX: WIND HOWLING]

A low whir filled the air as the Compass Wheel sailed forward.

[SFX: VIOLENT WIND BLASTS]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]

The seven members of the Demi-Immortal Race fought back against the storm of black wind blades with everything they had.

[SFX: CRUTCH TAP]

Vorn leaned heavily on his crutch, his voice tight with anxiety. “Delroy, we can't keep this up. You have to do something!”

“I've already used the Plate of Seven Wards five times! I'm not about to waste another charge,” Delroy shot back.

Delroy's Plate of Seven Wards was a precious, divine-class treasure with a catch: it only had seven uses. He owed his life to it several times over, making each charge feel like an extra life. The thought of wasting one of those precious chances here was infuriating.

Vorn's expression didn't soften. “We don't know how vast this ocean is or how long before we cross it. Do you think we can hold on without it?”

Delroy's jaw tightened. He glared back at Vorn. “And what about you? Can't you use the Sacred Blood Secret Technique?”

The question landed like a slap. 

Vorn's face darkened instantly. He said nothing — just stared at Delroy until the silence became its own answer.

Delroy muttered something under his breath. Then, with visible reluctance, he reached into his storage space and drew out a milky white plate.

[SFX: STARS APPEARING]

The moment he activated it, something impossible happened. Seven stars appeared in the sky above them — blazing clearly despite the storm and the broad daylight, as if the clouds and wind simply didn't apply to them.

Watching from the side, Zane's eyes narrowed with interest. The principle behind the Plate of Seven Wards felt strangely familiar — close to something the Red Dragon had taught him, the way the Stellar Body drew on stars for power. 

But where Zane's connection was built into his own body, Delroy's ran through the plate as an intermediary. And unlike Zane's, it had a limit.

[SFX: BARRIER EXPANDING]

A milky white barrier bloomed outward from the plate, soft at first, then spreading fast in every direction until it had swallowed the entire group. 

Wind blades hammered against it immediately — denting the surface, pressing it inward — but never breaking through. The barrier gave like cloth, then pushed back. There was a hidden toughness in it that the blades couldn't find an edge to cut.

“It'll hold,” Delroy said flatly, not bothering to hide his irritation at having to use it.

Around him, the tension drained from the group. Even the young man in black let out a slow breath.

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]

The exchange snapped everyone's attention back to their current predicament, and then, to the one person who didn't seem fazed by it. All eyes turned to Zane.

“You shattered those wind blades with your bare hands. How are you not even scratched?” Dorothea asked, stepping up to Zane and taking his hands to inspect them herself.

Zane wasn't sure how to explain, so he just let out a small laugh. “Compared to what you all are capable of, my little trick is nothing.”

Dorothea raised an eyebrow. “So you're not going to tell us?”

“It's… complicated,” Zane said with a deflective smile.

They could tell Zane was being modest—and hiding something. But they couldn't exactly force the truth out of him. Everyone had their secrets. Besides, he clearly wasn't an enemy and showed no ill will. If he had truly earned a title, any title at all, he was one of them.

[AMBIENT: HOWLING WIND STORM]

[SFX: WIND BLADES CUTTING]

Silence fell over the group. They watched the storm of wind blades rage outside their enchanted barrier, a swirling vortex of destruction. Even for masters of the Spirit Supreme Realm, the display was a humbling reminder of nature's raw power.

“This storm isn't right,” Vorn said quietly. “No matter how you look at it — it's abnormal.”

Delroy crossed his arms. “This whole world is abnormal.” He paused. “Before we came, we looked into it. The ascension passage is destroyed — we knew that, dismissed it. Millions of worlds have broken passages. But shadow creatures in the void, and now this?” He shook his head. “A storm like this has no business existing in a small world.”

He said it with the casual certainty of someone who had seen countless small worlds and found them all beneath him.

Zane said nothing. But he quietly agreed. 

The Chaotic Ocean had always been there — always raging, always isolating the Heartlands from the Divine Kingdoms. The two landmasses weren't impossibly far apart. And yet no one crossed. No one ever really asked why.

Maybe no one knew.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

“Vorn,” Zane said, breaking the silence. “That creature from the void—the one chasing us. The Forsaken. What is it?”

The storm was intense, but Zane could see the masters of the Spirit Supreme Realm were powerful enough to handle it. Blocking the wind blades was a pain, sure, but they were managing. And judging by the exchange between Delroy and Vorn, they still had other tricks up their sleeves.

[MUSIC: DARK SUSPENSEFUL]

But when the Forsaken had been hunting them through the void, Vorn had been bone-white with terror.

What in the entire Lower World, Zane wondered, could scare a man like that so badly?
