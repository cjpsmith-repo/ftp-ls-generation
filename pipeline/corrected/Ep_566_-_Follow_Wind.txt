[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The tension was so thick you could taste it, sharp and bitter.
The ordinary humans shuffled single file in the direction Zane had pointed, clinging to desperate hope that his orders meant safety.
Zane scanned the standoff. The Ogre War Lord was clearly the strongest thing on the field. But there were over twenty human War Generals, plus two from the Demon Night—and those two were nothing to scoff at. If they all actually teamed up, Zane figured the Ogre War Lord might not stand a chance.
But they weren't teaming up. Everyone was just playing the waiting game, waiting for backup. They all had their own agendas, and Zane was no different.
He'd already evaluated these warriors. The fighters on this chaotic continent weren't top-tier. He wouldn't stake his life on them.
The Demon Night had offered protection, but despite his sword spirit being one of them, he couldn't trust another race completely.
As for the Ogres? They would never let him walk away alive.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS DRUMS]
While he was weighing his options, two more enormous figures crested the horizon, coming from the same direction as the Ogre War Lord. A wave of raw power rolled off them, a palpable threat that grew with every step they took.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE ENERGY BURST]
The second the new Ogre War Lords appeared, Zane didn't hesitate. His forceful energy detonated, and he launched himself forward in a blinding burst of speed. His soul had sensed the shift in power an instant before anyone else could even process it.
He was already a blur on the horizon before most of the others even flinched.
The first Ogre War Lord’s face split into a wicked grin as he sensed his allies' arrival. But before a triumphant roar could leave his throat, Zane was gone.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS]
[AMBIENT: RUMBLING GROUND]
The Ogre’s expression soured, and with a heavy shift of his massive body, he gave chase.
Running was a no-brainer. Twenty human War Generals were no match for one Ogre War Lord, let alone three. With only two Demon Night warriors on his side, staying put would have been suicide.
Behind him, the twenty-odd human War Generals stood frozen. They'd been ordered to protect Zane. But three Ogre War Lords? If those three turned around at any point, not one of the humans would survive. All they could do was watch.
The Demon Night, however, moved. Phoebe turned and slipped back into the forest, circling wide through the trees. She slung her bone bow across her back, looked up, and whispered two words.
"Follow Wind."
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
[SFX: WINGS UNFURLING]
A gust swept past her, and a pair of pale green wings formed at her back. She launched herself into the sky and streaked after the War Lords.
[MUSIC: FAST-PACED ACTION THEME]
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
As Zane tore across the landscape, the advantage of having trained in both his body and his Vitality became brutally clear.
That initial explosion of forceful energy had turned him into a streak of light, devouring over ten miles in a matter of seconds. This was the dual-engine system he'd built. The forceful energy gave him explosive bursts, enhanced by the raw speed of the Cloud-Chaser Lion's talent. But for the long haul, he needed Vitality to kick in and maintain the blistering pace.
His training as a body refiner was critical here. Vitality could be drawn from the world around him, a near-limitless fuel source. But forceful energy was different—it was generated purely from within. You couldn't just pull more from the air.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
The War Lords were fast. Dangerously fast. And the gap between them was shrinking.
A thousand feet. Eight hundred. Five hundred.
Every time they closed in, Zane detonated another burst of forceful energy and tore the distance open again. Then he'd switch back to Vitality and hold pace until the next surge was ready.
Miles burned away beneath him. A hundred. Two hundred. Three hundred.
The War Lords had the same problem he did. They were body refiners too — built for explosive short bursts, not sustained long-distance flight. Zane was betting everything on outlasting them.
[SFX: CRYSTAL SHATTERING]
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
Mid-flight, he reached into his Storage Ring and crushed a top-grade Spirit Stone. Pure Vitality flooded into him, refilling what the chase had burned through. Forceful energy was another matter — no stone could replenish that. It could only be generated slowly from within.
And that was the problem. The higher his cultivation, the faster he'd recover it. But Zane wasn't there yet.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
His reserves of forceful energy were already running dangerously low.
He needed those explosive bursts, but a simple flare of energy wasn't enough. To get that insane speed, he had to channel it through his Confluence Platform, tapping directly into the Cloud-Chaser Lion’s talent.
[SFX: HEAVY BREATHING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DRUMS]
Zane felt his forceful energy sputter. His speed began to drop. Behind him, the thundering footsteps of the Ogre War Lords didn't slow one bit.
The closest one was now barely eighty feet back and closing fast.
[SFX: THUNDERING FOOTSTEPS]
"Not bad, little human," the Ogre War Lord bellowed, his voice a gravelly taunt that carried easily over the distance. "Leading us on a thousand-mile chase at that speed… I'm almost impressed. But it ends now. I'm going to drag you back to our sacred place and rip every last secret from your bones. Now, stop running!"
He cocked back a fist the size of a small boulder and swung.
[SFX: SOUL RELEASE]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL TENSION]
In that split second, Zane unleashed his soul. It shot from his body like a phantom missile, twisting into a sharp Spike aimed directly at the pursuing Ogre’s head. An attack like this could obliterate a War General instantly. Against a War Lord? Zane had no idea if it would even leave a scratch. It was a desperate gamble.
[SFX: WHOOSH GRAZE]
The Ogre, clearly not expecting a soul attack, flinched hard. His stride broke for just a second as he jerked his massive head aside, the Spike grazing past his skull.
[SFX: ROAR]
"A Fighting Soul Realm?!" The Ogre's face contorted with a mixture of shock and disgust. "This human kid is a goddamn freak of nature!"
