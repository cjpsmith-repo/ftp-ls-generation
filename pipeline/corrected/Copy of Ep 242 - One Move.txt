Ep 242 - One Move
Word count: 1119
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
Mad-Monster Hacking was the strongest sword skill in Tavish's arsenal of techniques.
His fighting style was like the wind itself---pure speed, raw force, and merciless violence converging into a deadly art form.
As he raised his machete to strike Zane, small whirlpools of air spun to life around the blue-black antique blade, emanating an icy chill from their swirling currents.
Mastering an intent---whether for blade, sword, or spear---was a feat few warriors ever achieved in their lifetimes.
Even Tavish's rudimentary grasp of blade intent transformed him into a formidable adversary, multiplying the power behind each strike he delivered. His years of dedicated training had culminated in this moment, his confidence soaring with each passing second.
His sole limitation was that Tavish hadn't yet developed the ability to coat his entire machete with blade intent. He could only channel a fragment of this power into the swirling energy surrounding the weapon before launching his attack against Zane.
Still, Tavish felt sure that this mere glimpse of his power was more than enough to crush---perhaps even kill---his opponent. His heart raced with anticipation.
"There's no way you can stop this," Tavish muttered, his eyes narrowing to slits as bloodlust and anticipation mingled in his expression. His knuckles whitened around the machete's hilt, muscles tensing for the decisive strike that would cement his reputation among the Peaks.
[SFX: WIND RUSHING]
A cold smirk spread across his face as he charged, unleashing his Mad-Monster Hacking.
[SFX: BLADE CUTTING AIR]
As Tavish lunged forward, Zane narrowed his eyes, focusing intently on the gleaming machete that sliced through the air toward him. The weapon's approach seemed almost sluggish to his heightened senses.
He detected the faint, wavering pulse of blade intent emanating from the metal---a pitiful attempt at infusing the weapon with spiritual power.
Such meager intent posed no danger. It wasn't enough to hurt him. It wasn't even enough to be a threat.
Though Zane had only recently awakened his own sword intent, he had mastered it with remarkable speed, wielding it effortlessly at will. His protective field of intent now extended over thirty square feet around him, an invisible dome of lethal potential. Anyone foolish enough to venture within that circle risked being torn apart with nothing more than a casual gesture from his wrist---a thought that gave him no pleasure, merely certainty.
The blade intent Tavish had boasted about, had trained countless hours to perfect, was nothing but a hollow pretension, a joke.
Tavish stared at his blade for a moment, then raised it again. "That was nothing. Let me show you what I'm really capable of."
Zane tilted his head. "Funny. I thought you said three moves." He smiled. "Wasn't that your third?"
Tavish's face froze.
[SFX: SWORD CLASH]
Before Tavish's attack could penetrate his defenses, Zane's eyes flashed with cold brilliance.
[SFX: ENERGY BURST]
[SFX: ENERGY SHATTERING]
His sword intent flared to life, a torrent of invisible force that collided with Tavish's feeble attempt and obliterated it instantly. The sensation reminded Zane of crushing an eggshell between his fingers---effortless and almost disappointing in its simplicity.
The "Mad-Monster" aspect of Tavish's technique came entirely from his blade intent. Without it, his fearsome reputation crumbled, leaving just an ordinary fighter swinging an ordinary weapon with increasingly evident desperation.
"What the hell?" Tavish gasped, his voice cracking with disbelief.
Zane's effortless counter left his opponent suspended in motion, arm still extended mid-swing, face transformed by shock. The color drained from Tavish's cheeks as comprehension dawned.
The blade intent he had dedicated years to perfecting, the technique that had defined his identity as a warrior and earned him accolades throughout Sea Peak, had vanished like morning mist---gone without ceremony or acknowledgment.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
Impossible!
Blade intent was formless, an extension of his own will. It should have been indestructible... unless Zane had mastered an intent of his own, whether it was for a sword, a spear, or something else entirely.
"Did he figure out some kind of intent on his own?" Doubt began to cloud his mind.
Zane's counterattack had been so fast, and he'd pulled it back even faster, that Tavish had no idea what had just happened.
[SFX: BLADE SWOOSH]
As his mind reeled, Tavish's once-precise Mad-Monster Hacking technique deteriorated into clumsy, disjointed movements. A seasoned warrior requires an unwavering spirit---the moment uncertainty infiltrates one's thoughts, the battle has already slipped away. Like a foundation crumbling beneath a fortress, Tavish's confidence collapsed under Zane's inexplicable mastery.
Zane gracefully sidestepped another of Tavish's increasingly desperate attacks, noting how the once-proud Sea Peak disciple's footwork had grown erratic, his breathing labored. A smirk played across Zane's lips as he decided to twist the knife.
"Is this really the best Sea Peak has to offer?" he called out, his voice carrying across the arena. "I expected at least a challenge before lunch."
"Shut up!" Tavish bellowed, his face flushing crimson with humiliation as sweat beaded on his brow. The crowd's murmurs only heightened his mounting frustration.
[MUSIC: INTENSE COMBAT]
Unable to take it anymore, Tavish focused all his energy on trying to land a single hit.
[AMBIENT: COMBAT ARENA]
"That's four moves... five... six..."
As he deflected Tavish's relentless attacks, Zane counted every single one.
Tavish couldn't believe how fast Zane was, how he reacted to every attack. Exhaustion was setting in, but he was still giving it everything he had. And still, Zane was completely untouchable, proving he was the faster, smarter fighter. To make it worse, with every failed attack, Zane would effortlessly counter and mock him.
"That's eighty moves. And now one hundred. You're pathetic, Tavish. You've made one hundred moves and still haven't even touched me."
Zane's mocking laughter was relentless.
[SFX: ANGRY SHOUT]
"Go to hell!" Rage burned through Tavish's veins as he unleashed the Mad-Monster Hacking technique again.
[SFX: POWERFUL PUNCH]
"You've attacked me a hundred times and failed every single time. Let me tell you the truth, Tavish. I can beat you with just one move." The smile vanished from Zane's face. In a single, explosive blur, he leaned forward and slammed his fist square into Tavish's chest before the machete even had a chance to connect.
It turned out Zane had been holding back his true power. His move was so fast the entire audience was frozen in shock, stunned by his incredible speed and explosive force.
[SFX: IMPACT THUD]
[SFX: BODY SLAMMING INTO BARRIER]
The powerful strike sent Tavish flying into the air until he slammed into the enchanted barrier high above the arena.
These enchanted barriers divided the competition field into nine separate sections, keeping the fighters from interfering with each other's matches.
Lucky for Tavish, the barrier was soft, absorbing most of the impact. Otherwise, every bone in his body would have been shattered.
Still, the blow was devastating. He felt several of his ribs crack, and all he could do was lie there against the shimmering wall, silent and paralyzed by shock and terror.
