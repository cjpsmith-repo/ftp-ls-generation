Ep 724 - Gifts
Word count: 999
[AMBIENT: BUSY HALL]
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
The house lords had no choice but to accept Zane's terms. They would pay in installments, whether they liked it or not.
Cloud Hall’s business was training elite warriors, and its primary income came from the missions it assigned. A slice of the rewards went to the disciples who pulled off the job, but the lion’s share went straight back to the Hall. It was a steady stream of cash, but compared to the old Houses, it was a drop in the bucket.
The Houses had their fingers in everything. They owned mines and banks, ran security firms, and even had a stake in the city’s brothels. More importantly, they controlled the teleportation arrays in Cloudhaven, charging a fortune for every use. On top of that, Cloudhaven was home to tens of millions of ordinary people who, while nothing compared to the warriors, traded in gold and silver. All that coin, gathered up and converted first to low-grade Aether Crystals and then to mid-grade, eventually found its way into the Houses’ coffers.
So no, they couldn’t just pull twenty thousand supreme Aether Crystals out of a hat. Even Cloud Hall would have a hard time with that kind of sum. But Zane had a simple solution. The Houses would sign a payment contract, and they could even pay in installments.
“Don’t forget to pay the interest,” Zane added with a thin smile.
A wave of discomfort rippled through the House leaders. They shifted on their feet, mouths twitching. Tia had been ruthless enough, but this kid, Zane… this new generation was something else. They had come into their power at a terrifying pace, their talent and strength almost unbelievable. And as if that wasn’t enough, they were both cold-blooded.
The House leaders knew this was the price of failure. They had gambled on a rebellion against Cloud Hall and lost. Now, it was time to pay up.
[AMBIENT: QUIET ESTATE]
[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE MELODY]
After a few hushed, tense discussions, the leaders hurried back to their estates to scrape together the crystals. Zane, however, headed straight for Cloud Hall. He’d been so worried about Tia that he hadn’t had a chance to see Finn and the guys. Now that things had settled down, he couldn’t wait to catch up.
Tia wanted to tell him to stay, but the words caught in her throat, and she said nothing.
The life seemed to drain from her face, a shift that didn’t escape Saint Morphen’s notice. He watched from a distance as her eyes went vacant and her shoulders slumped. He kept his silence. This wasn’t his business.
[AMBIENT: FESTIVE HALL]
[MUSIC: CHEERFUL CELEBRATION]
Laughter echoed from a small spire in Cloud Hall, where Zane, Finn, Patrick, and Lewis were celebrating. The table was piled high with food, and the wine was flowing. With their level of cultivation, getting drunk wasn’t a concern; a hundred barrels wouldn’t even give them a buzz.
Finn drained his cup and set it down with a rueful smile. "I thought that after my last breakthrough, I might actually be able to fight you," he said. "Clearly I was kidding myself."
It was pure Finn. Ever since their days at the Skyreach Sect, he'd never once admitted defeat. He'd watched the gap between himself and Zane widen at the martial arts competition in Oracle City and had refused to accept it, pushing himself harder than ever. And still, it wasn't enough.
"Don't sell yourself short," Zane said. "Given enough time—"
"I know my own limits," Finn cut in. "I've got one good shot. If my opponent survives it, I'm done." He looked at Zane evenly. "You'd survive it."
Zane didn't insult him by pretending otherwise. He nodded.
"I'd survive it too!" Lewis announced from across the table.
Finn turned a flat look on him. "Yes. And then I'd still beat you."
Lewis opened his mouth, thought about it, and closed it again. Patrick hid a smile behind his cup.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
“Zane, you’re not seriously giving this sword to Finn, are you?” Lewis blurted out. “That’s a complete waste!”
“What did you just say?” Finn shot back, his eyes narrowing until they glinted like the edge of a blade.
[SFX: WHOOSH]
In a flash, Lewis vanished and reappeared behind Patrick’s chair. “I’m just telling it like it is! A Relic-Class weapon is wasted on you! Why not give it to me instead, Zane?”
“Is this one of the gifts you were talking about?” Patrick asked, his voice low and cautious. He couldn’t imagine where Zane had found something so valuable.
“This sword is for Finn,” Zane said simply. “A great swordsman deserves a great sword.” As a swordsman himself, he knew the truth in those words.
[MUSIC: TENSE EMOTIONAL STRINGS]
Finn closed his eyes, taking a long, slow breath. When he opened them again, they burned with a raw passion as he stared at the sword. Then, with visible effort, he shook his head and tore his gaze away. “I can’t accept this, Zane,” he said, his voice tight with the effort of turning it down. “A Relic-Class weapon is too precious.”
He knew for a fact that even Cloud Hall and the World Merchants’ Guild only owned one or two Relic-Class weapons. The idea that Zane was just giving one away made his heart hammer against his ribs, but he couldn’t take it. It was too much.
[MUSIC: WARM HOPEFUL MELODY]
Seeing the conflict warring in Finn’s eyes, Zane just smiled. “Don’t say no. A Relic-Class weapon might be priceless to you guys, but it’s not to me. I want you to have it, Finn.”
“Not to you?” Lewis’s jaw practically hit the floor. “Zane, where in the hell have you been for the last two years?”
Patrick just stared, completely speechless.
[SFX: METALLIC UNSHEATHING]
Zane then produced a pair of shortswords. It was a set of four blades in total—twin blades nested inside two larger sheath-blades.
“This set is also a low-grade Relic-Class weapon,” Zane said, then flicked his eyes toward Lewis.
[MUSIC: RISING ANTICIPATION]
“No way,” Lewis whispered, his entire body starting to tremble as he stared at the blades. “Is that… for me?”
