Ep 139 - Escaping Death
Word count: 1057
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
 The two Netherlords were just barely holding back the Soul Realm masters, struggling to stop them from unleashing their Vitality. 
But they couldn’t wrap their heads around why the kid had suddenly summoned an Apex Dragon. What was he planning?
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
 The Apex Dragon circled high above, descending toward Zane at a steady pace. Once it was in range, Zane balled his hand into a fist and shot a punch right at it.
[SFX: ENERGY CHARGING]
 “Earthshatter!”
[SFX: DEAFENING BOOM]
 A shadowy, purple-black fist of energy shot straight toward the Apex Dragon. It slammed into the dragon with a deafening boom, leaving nothing but a small black spot on its hide. 
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
 At first, it looked like it had done nothing else. But then, in the blink of an eye, that little black dot spread like wildfire, consuming the dragon until the entire Apex Dragon had morphed into an Armored Wyvern!
Zane directed the Armored Wyvern, making it circle and drift through the air under his command.
[SFX: SWORDS MATERIALIZING]
 Meanwhile, Lennie, watching from a distance, suddenly lit up with an idea. He waved his hand, and several silver swords materialized out of thin air. The moment they appeared, he launched them straight at Zane.
Following Lennie’s lead, Hugh summoned another Apex Dragon from his hand and sent it soaring toward Zane.
They weren't the only ones. Every Soul Realm master on the field — everyone except the Commander, whose Vitality was locked inside his avatar — drew from their reserves and sent it toward Zane. 
Dragon after dragon, sword after sword, the Wyvern kept eating.
[SFX: MONSTER GROWING]
 Apex Dragon or gleaming swords—it didn't matter. Zane took them all on without flinching. He had the Armored Wyvern swallow them whole, and with each attack it devoured, the beast swelled to more than double its original size.
Normally, an enemy could just dodge the dragon, wait for it to disappear, and then come back to finish Zane off. But this situation? This was the perfect stage for the Armored Wyvern to shine.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
 Aldrich was locked in a battle of Vitality with Yolande Shaw. He couldn't afford the slightest distraction, let alone move an inch. But when he saw the Armored Wyvern, his face darkened. He couldn't believe that Zane—just some human kid—had created such a fearsome beast.
The Netherlord was completely baffled.
“Was this a trap from the very beginning, or just some freak coincidence?” he thought. “If it's a trap, it's an insane gamble. Did they really bet everything on a human kid who's nothing more than a High-level candidate? No, that's impossible.”
“But if this is a coincidence... is it a sign from the heavens that I'm meant to die today?” The Netherlord refused to accept such a cruel fate. Beating these humans from the Soul Realm was supposed to be child's play. How had it all gone so wrong? Not only had that punk Zane stolen his Hellfire, but now he'd summoned a massive Armored Wyvern to finish the job.
“I won't give up!”
While Aldrich was raging internally, Zane had already sent the Armored Wyvern crashing through the Light Barrier, straight for him. Yolande Shaw went all in, too, pouring the last of her Vitality into the attack as the dragon charged. She was determined to end Aldrich in one final blow.
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
 “Grr!” The Armored Wyvern tilted its head and let out a guttural growl, lunging at Aldrich. Its roar was so powerful it shook the very ground. With massive jaws gaping, it charged relentlessly forward.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CLIMAX]
 Aldrich was cornered. Trapped.
“Do I dodge?" he thought. But Yolande Shaw would never let him escape. He knew that human girl would hunt him down relentlessly. *So, do I just stand here and take the full force of the Armored Wyvern's attack? Can I even survive that?*
Even for someone as powerful as him, survival wasn't guaranteed. And even if he *did* manage to block the Armored Wyvern and live, the human girl was right there, waiting to strike the killing blow.
[SFX: MONSTROUS ROAR]
 "Grrr! You insolent human punk!" Aldrich roared, his voice trembling with rage that masked a growing desperation. "I'll extract payment for this humiliation with every drop of your miserable life!"
Backed into a corner with options dwindling, the Netherlord's crimson eyes narrowed as he reached a grim conclusion. There remained only one desperate gambit—he would have to sacrifice everything.
[SFX: ENERGY WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD ENERGY]
 In a shocking move that sent ripples of energy across the battlefield, Aldrich recalled all his Phantoms at once. The spectral entities streaked back toward him like shooting stars, each one carrying a fragment of his power. In the unforgiving calculus of Vitality combat, such a withdrawal was tantamount to self-destruction—a desperate measure reserved only for the most dire circumstances.
Blood trickled from the corner of Aldrich's mouth as the price of his decision manifested physically. His once-imposing frame seemed to diminish slightly, the brilliant aura surrounding him flickering like a candle in a storm. He stood wounded and diminished, left with merely a shadow of his formidable strength. 
Yet even in this weakened state, Zane could feel the overwhelming power radiating from the Netherlord—an ancient, terrible force that still dwarfed anything he had ever faced.
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
With a flick of his wrist, Aldrich effortlessly swatted the Shattered Dagger aside. The blade, which had never failed Zane before, was completely useless against him now.
The attempt proved futile, yet afforded Zane a precious moment to retreat. He managed to scramble several yards away during the distraction, but the Netherlord's velocity defied comprehension—far exceeding anything Zane could have anticipated. 
[SFX: POWERFUL WING BEAT]
 With a single powerful beat of his massive wings, Aldrich's enormous form sliced through the air, descending upon Zane with the ferocity of a primordial predator.
"Human brat, journey to the Underworld!" Aldrich snarled, his eyes gleaming with murderous intent. "I shall feast upon your flesh and drain every last crimson drop from your veins!"
[SFX: BONE SPURS ERUPTING]
[MUSIC: INTENSE DANGER]
 In that instant, four gleaming bone spurs erupted from Aldrich's body, each hurtling toward Zane from different angles in a perfect killing formation. Time seemed to slow as the deadly projectiles converged. Zane knew with terrifying certainty—if even a single spur penetrated his defenses, his fate would be irrevocably sealed. 
Before his eyes flashed the horrific vision: his body, impaled and withering as the enraged Netherlord methodically extracted his Vitality, reducing him to nothing but a desiccated husk, discarded and forgotten.
