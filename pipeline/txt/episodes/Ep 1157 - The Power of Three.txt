Ep 1157 - The Power of Three

Word count: 1019

[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
Freya’s hands were pinned, trapped as if in a vise. There was no way for her to break free. But as grim as her situation was, Zane’s worry went deeper than just her physical restraints. The real problem was the Malice corrupting her from the inside. How was he supposed to get that out of her?
Normally, Malice was something that built up over centuries. You had to kill a hundred thousand living things for it to become a serious threat—a feat usually reserved for the most depraved warriors, the kind who’d wipe out entire cities and earn the hatred of everyone. But down here, warriors were accumulating it at an insane rate. How was anyone supposed to purge that much? Even if Zane managed to get Freya out of this underground palace, the Malice would still be a death sentence. The Endless Abyss would just be their final resting place.
Zane was the only one holding it together. Thanks to his time on Killing Sword Mountain, he could channel its power to resist the crushing pressure. But the other warriors were already losing their minds, turning on each other as a storm of violent energy swirled above their heads. If this kept up, none of them were getting out of here alive.
His thoughts were spiraling when a voice cut through the noise, calm and clear in his mind. 
[SFX: TELEPATHIC WHISPER]
It was Lavender. *“Don’t worry about her,”* she sent. *“Freya will be fine once we get her out of this place.”*
The certainty in her voice steadied him. He didn’t press for an explanation — Lavender rarely offered reassurance without reason. If she said Freya would be fine, he believed her. He loosened his grip slightly, just enough to let Freya breathe, and turned his attention back to the chaos unfolding around them.
Across the chasm, the golden-haired Demon Night woman was in trouble. She’d been working the edges of the frenzy since it started — nimble, controlled, not fully lost to the Malice the way the others were. But Lincoln had locked onto her, and he was relentless. He’d been driving her steadily backward, cutting off every exit, and now she had nowhere left to go.
[MUSIC: RISING DANGEROUS STRINGS]
Trapped, her expression hardened. 
She lifted the lantern she was holding, her lips moving in a low, indecipherable chant.
Zane couldn’t make out the words, but he saw Lavender’s composure suddenly shatter.
*“Oh, no,”* her voice echoed in his head, tight with alarm. *“This is bad. Something is about to happen.”*
“What do you mean?” Zane asked telepathically. He was hanging back from the main fight. The brawls were intense, but so far, they hadn’t pulled him in. Based on what Lavender had told him, his only job was to endure this part of the trial. So he watched the chaos unfold, his main focus on keeping Freya from throwing herself into the fray.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY CRACKLING]
*“That woman—she has the ancient Demon Night bloodline!”* Lavender’s thought was a panicked rush. *“She’s calling on the Power of the Three Queens!”*
“The Power of the Three Queens?” Zane was floored. That was the last thing he expected.
*“Yes,”* Lavender confirmed. *“Only a handful of Demon Night warriors still carry the ancient bloodline. It lets them tap into the Faith Energy stored in the clan’s three Pools of Faith. But what is she doing in the Violetwood Sanctuary?”*
[AMBIENT: DISTANT DIVINE POWER RESONATING]
The three Demon Night queens were worshipped by trillions, and their Faith Energy was always growing. It was an incredibly potent source of power. All of it was collected and channeled into three sacred pools. Besides the queens themselves, who had unlimited access, only those with the ancient bloodline were qualified to borrow from them. They could only draw a tiny fraction, but even that sliver granted them terrifying strength.
But warriors with that bloodline were incredibly rare. As soon as they were identified, they were typically brought into the highest echelons of the Demon Night clan. So why was this woman here, of all places, in the Dark World’s Violetwood Sanctuary? Lavender couldn’t make sense of it.
As the blonde woman finished her chant, the power she’d called for arrived. 
[SFX: ENERGY BEAM DESCENDING]
A stream of energy crossed the endless void and shot down into the abyss. But then, something impossible happened. 
[SFX: IMPACT RICOCHET]
The red light didn’t go into her. Instead, it shot sideways, slamming into Zane with the force of a physical blow before ricocheting off him and finally surging toward its intended target.
“Ah!” the woman cried out, stunned.
Luckily for her, the power still reached its destination. 
[SFX: MAGICAL BARRIER FORMING]
The Faith Energies gathered around her, forming a shimmering, three-colored barrier. 
[SFX: FIERCE STRIKES ON BARRIER]
Lincoln’s attacks grew even more ferocious, striking the glowing shield and making it shudder, but he couldn’t put a single scratch on it.
The woman didn’t fight back. Her gaze was locked on Zane, her face a mask of utter confusion.
She wasn’t the only one confused. Zane had felt the impact — a hard, percussive strike against his body, like something had tried to pass through him and been stopped cold. He knew what that meant before Lavender even said a word.
The Faith Energy hadn’t been drawn to him. It had been drawn to what was inside him. To her. The beam had reached out across the void, found its queen, and announced her presence to anyone with eyes to see. There was nothing either of them could have done to stop it.
“Lavender—”
“I know,” she said quietly.
And she wasn’t the only one. 
[MUSIC: DREAD SUSPENSE]
[AMBIENT: UNDERGROUND PALACE EXTERIOR]
Far away, outside the underground palace, the Saint Lord of the Violetwood Sanctuary stared into the void, his expression one of absolute dread. The Faith Energy didn’t lie. It had found its queen — and its queen was inside a human warrior who had no business being anywhere near this place. Whatever came next, nothing about it was going to be simple.
