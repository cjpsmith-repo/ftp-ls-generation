Ep 944 - The New Hope

Word count: 1176

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

Lamar was a Soul Sea Realm master—that’s why his reaction was lightning-fast.

[SFX: ARROWS WHOOSHING]

[SFX: HEAVY IMPACT]

But the moment the black arrows slammed into his ghost staff, his massive body wrenched violently to the side.

[SFX: FLESH TEARING]

[SFX: BODY IMPACT]

One after another, the three arrows tore into his huge frame. Because he’d twisted, two of them sank into his right shoulder and thigh. The third punched straight through his chest, barely grazing his heart.

Six small, gruesome holes opened up on his body, pulsing blood. But Lamar was a tough Ogre; the injuries weren't life-threatening.

That didn’t mean he was out of the woods, though.

Zane had aimed those three arrows straight for Lamar’s head and heart. If the Ogre hadn’t contorted his body at the last possible second, he’d be a corpse right now.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Lamar knew how close he’d come. He frantically scanned the area, trying to pinpoint where the attack had come from. When his eyes landed on Zane, his fear boiled over into pure shock and rage.

“A human? An early-stage War Sage?” Lamar muttered, his voice a low growl. “How the hell could he fire arrows that powerful? He’s damn sure not from the Violet Heart Sanctuary. Where did this guy even come from?”

[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]

Standing beside Zane, Esther Sterling’s hand flew to her mouth, her eyes wide with disbelief. She’d been with Zane in the Tower of Sin for a long time and knew he was strong. But she never imagined three simple arrows could do that kind of damage to a Soul Sea Realm master—especially not to a monster like Lamar.

She could see it clear as day: those arrows had almost killed him.

“You’re just a human, but you actually managed to hurt me,” Lamar said, his voice dangerously flat. “I’ll give you that much. You get one more shot. Then, I’m going to make you wish you’d never been born.”

[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]

Consumed by rage, Lamar completely forgot about Lacey. He gathered the roaring flames around his body and shot toward Zane with terrifying speed.

[AMBIENT: HIGH ALTITUDE WIND]

High above the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, the battle between the Ogre and Demon Night War Sages ground to a halt. The black arrows Zane had unleashed had shocked them all. Now, the Ogres sneered as they watched Lamar charge.

Zane’s attack was a surprise, sure, but they figured it had to be a fluke. After all, he was just an early-stage War Sage. They were certain he was in for a world of hurt for picking a fight with Lamar.

Lamar’s body was like a small mountain hurtling straight for Zane, his shadow swallowing the ground below.

Zane didn't move.

Esther grabbed his arm. “Zane —“

“Back off,” he said quietly.

She let go.

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: POWER BUILDING]

In the space behind his eyes, the Dragon Scales surged. One thousand. Two thousand. Three thousand. Four thousand. Five thousand. The limit.

Lamar hadn't used his domain. Zane had clocked it the moment the Ogre started his charge — too angry, too certain of his own superiority to bother. As long as that domain stayed closed, Zane could hold onto his connection to the World's Will. He was effectively invincible. He had one window. He couldn't waste it.

“A puny first-level War Sage, and you're not even going to dodge?” Lamar sneered, his bronze arm swelling as he drew back his fist. The arm grew to monstrous proportions, his fist alone half the size of Zane's entire body. “The price for that kind of courage is getting crushed to powder!”

[SFX: MASSIVE PUNCH INCOMING]

[MUSIC: EPIC COLLISION]

Zane threw his own punch. There was a strange smile on his face.

Lamar caught it — that smile — and for just a fraction of a second, hesitated. This guy didn't look like a lunatic. Why was he smiling?

But it was already too late to pull back.

[SFX: SHATTERING BONE]

The two fists connected. And the crowd watched as the flesh on Lamar's massive fist began to crack. The bones beneath fractured — layer by layer — then disintegrated entirely. The veins in his elephant-thick arm ruptured. His muscles peeled away from the bone. And the bones themselves, already shattered, kept breaking — smaller and smaller — until they were fine powder.

With one punch, Lamar's entire right arm ceased to exist.

[SFX: BODY LAUNCHED THROUGH AIR]

The recoil sent him hurtling backwards across the sky.

[MUSIC: STUNNED SILENCE]

The entire sky above the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary went dead quiet.

[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]

Esther stood frozen. She'd known Zane was strong. But this — a Soul Sea Realm Ogre, the pride of the Flaming Place, his arm reduced to powder by a first-level War Sage's bare fist — her mind couldn't fully process it. Without thinking, it pulled her back to the Tower of Sin. To the Talent Tablet. To the golden title above all the others.

A Celestial Rank. Gold tier. She'd wondered then what it really meant. Standing here now, she thought she was beginning to understand.

[SFX: SHOCKED GASP]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]

“Zane! That warrior is Zane!” an Ogre War Sage suddenly roared, his voice thick with dawning recognition.

Most of the Ogre War Sages there had never laid eyes on Zane before. But a handful of them had been in the Tower of Sin and knew that face. They had left that cursed place to become War Sages only after breaking through to the next level.

Just moments ago, they hadn’t been sure it was him. The figure looked familiar, but according to reports from the two Ogre sanctuaries, Zane had left the main continent a long time ago.

But now, there was no mistaking it.

[SFX: SHOCKED MURMURS]

“Zane! He’s that human from the Tower of Sin!”

“The sworn enemy of our sacred places!”

“A year ago, he was just an early-stage War Lord. How is he a War Sage now?”

“But even as a War Sage, how could he possibly stand up to Lamar?”

The Ogre War Sages were a mess of shock and fury. Back then, Zane had driven them out of the Tower of Sin, but that was only because their own masters couldn't enter the Cursed Land. 

This was different. Zane wasn't hiding anymore. He was standing right here in the Heavenly Feather Sanctuary, openly taking on one of their best. In fact, he’d already destroyed one of Lamar's arms with a single, bare-handed punch. The sight of it all was too much for many of the Ogre War Sages to believe.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL RISING THEME]

For the War Sages of the Demon Night, however, it was a different story. They had been drowning in hopelessness, but the moment they recognized Zane, a spark of hope finally ignited. Even though he was only a War Sage, the way he was fighting Lamar proved just how powerful he was.

He was their new hope.
