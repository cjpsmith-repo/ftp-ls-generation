Ep 296 - Aim Higher
Word Count: 1193
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
You'd think the fight between Zane and Finn would be less intense than his last one with Jacob. At least, that’s how it looked at first.
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
The roar of the Cobalt Dragon during that match had been sheer, destructive terror. It was a sound Zane would never forget.
But even with the raw power Zane had unleashed against Jacob, this fight with Finn was way more captivating. Why? Because most of that last battle had been completely hidden from view.
[AMBIENT: MUFFLED BATTLE SOUNDS]
Zane had flooded the fighting pit with his Corrupt Vitality, cloaking everything in a thick mist that blocked everyone's sight. The audience heard the horrifying roars of a dragon, but they had no damn clue what was happening inside.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
This fight, though? This was a whole other level.
[SFX: SWORD CLASHING]
These were two master swordsmen, each with a profound understanding of sword intent. They were easily two of the best in the Skyreach Sect, and the duel between them was so perfectly balanced it felt like it could end in a draw at any moment.
One was a once-in-a-generation genius who had already glimpsed the supernatural mysteries of The Celestial Blade Illustration, allowing him to wield heavenly power. The other was a grounded, noble warrior, whose sword style was both fundamental and deeply mysterious. He didn’t back down an inch, even when facing that celestial power. He just held his sword with unshakable confidence, countering every single move.
[AMBIENT: EXCITED CROWD MURMURS]
Seeing it all play out, the disciples of the Skyreach Sect were practically buzzing with excitement. After Finn had completely botched his demonstration of The Celestial Blade, they were hungry to see something truly spectacular.
The senior disciples had seen the tournament enough times to usually predict the outcome. But none of the past tournaments held a candle to this one.
"Finn’s skill with a sword is just unreal," one disciple muttered to another. "I mean, he’s the first person in eight hundred years to hit the fourth level of The Celestial Blade. And Zane... well, he's something else entirely.”  He's actually managed to stand against the star force..."
They spoke of legends about how the star force worked, but those were just stories. And none of those legends had ever come from a place like The Eastern Territory.
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
Finn had no idea that Zane wasn’t meditating on the stars in the sky. All he needed was the Tapestry of Sun, Moon, and Stars—an artifact that didn’t belong to this world at all. The power of the beings who had created it was beyond anything this world could imagine.
"You know, I used to think no one at my level could touch me," Finn said slowly, his voice carrying across the silent arena. "Even warriors who've broken through to the Soul-Illuminated Realm would most likely lose to me. The only one who's a real rival is Patrick. If we fought right now, I’d say it’s a coin toss. But once I reach the Soul Realm with the strength I have now? At the Consummation of the Adept Realm? It would be impossible for me to lose!"
Patrick, sitting cross-legged with his eyes shut, didn’t move a muscle, his face a perfect mask of indifference.
[MUSIC: AMBITIOUS RISING]
Then Finn added, "I have to aim higher, find a bigger world. The Eastern Territory is too small for me; it's holding me back. The whole world is out there for the taking. I need to challenge the thousands of talents this world has to offer, not just settle for being the best in this small Sect. After the All Peaks Competition, I don’t care if they let me walk the Cloud Road or not. I’m going to The Heartlands. I'm going to find a bigger stage."
[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]
A collective gasp went through the disciples. No one had ever realized just how ambitious Finn really was.
Everyone had dreams, sure. Warriors from nothing hoped to reach a high enough level to support their families and make a name for themselves. The business-minded ones wanted to build fortunes.
But warriors from the noble houses dreamed bigger. They fought their own siblings for control of their family, then spent years trying to make their house stand out among the others. But none of them had ever dared to dream of competing with families from other regions.
The Heartlands was a different league entirely from The Eastern Territory. The Seven Great Houses could throw their weight around here, but they’d be laughed out of The Heartlands if they tried that same crap there.
Which meant Finn was either incredibly brave or just plain reckless. He actually thought he could take on the families from The Heartlands and the rest of the world.
The world was vast, and most of it was still a mystery. He sounded like a beggar from some backwater town, with no idea how big the country even was, suddenly announcing he was going to be king.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
It was absurd. Then again, even the Emperor who founded the Empire had started as a beggar before he became a ruler.
Anyone with an ambition that huge was already a step above nearly every disciple in the Skyreach Sect.
"Meeting you before I leave The Eastern Territory is quite a surprise, Zane. You know that?" Finn said, his focus returning to his opponent. "You're only two steps into the Adept Realm, using basic sword techniques to... decide the outcome of this battle. My practice of The Celestial Blade creates a thread of connection to the heavens. Because of that, I feel things others don't. And what I feel about you, Zane — you're either a gift from the heavens or a test. Either way, I have to overcome you.”
He reached up and touched the Blood-Corroding Sword. "I was saving this next move for Wynn. You've forced my hand."
A seed appeared between Finn's brows and shattered. His eyes flooded with brilliant blue light. 
[SFX: PRIMAL VITALITY SURGE]
Primal Vitality flooded the arena again — greater than before, but this time condensed entirely into the Blood-Corroding Sword. 
Outside the ring, Wynn opened his eyes. 
[AMBIENT: RISING WIND]
The final impact was... ordinary. It released only a tiny flicker of power.
Compared to the last round, this display of swordsmanship was far from terrifying or stunning. Honestly, a regular training session between disciples looked more impressive.
Two blurs of steel met for a split second, then parted without a sound.
[AMBIENT: CROWD MURMURS]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
"What the hell? Is that it? I thought the world was going to shake again! What a letdown!"
"Is it over? Who won? Is the Referee gonna say something?"
"Is he pulling a prank? This is a joke, right? What a massive disappointment! I waited all day for this, and I can’t believe the match ended so... quietly."
The abrupt, anticlimactic end to the fight sent a wave of confused chatter through the crowd. No one had a clue what had just happened.
Meanwhile, the Guardians of the Sect, and even Patrick, stared down at the fighting pit, their eyes wide with disbelief.
Tracy, hiding in a corner, slammed a hand over her mouth to stifle a scream. Her face was a mask of horror. She had figured it out.
