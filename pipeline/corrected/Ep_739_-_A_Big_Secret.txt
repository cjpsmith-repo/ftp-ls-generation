Ep 739 - A Big Secret
Word count: 1099
[AMBIENT: EERIE WIND]
[MUSIC: DARK OMINOUS DRONE]
Zane had no idea how the boy was doing it—how he’d managed to project his soul across an entire city of a few hundred thousand people. Thinking about the strange events and the “Spirit Container” the boy had mentioned, Zane could only guess he’d somehow seized control of every last soul.
It was a monstrously cruel act.
Wars between the sects in the Heartlands were nothing new. When they broke out, millions of warriors would clash, and the losses would be staggering. But ordinary people were rarely involved. For one thing, killing mortals was just too easy; they had no way to fight back against a warrior. For another, slaughtering them for no reason generated far too much Malice. It was a risk that offered no reward.
Besides, mortals were the foundation of The Heartlands, the wellspring from which countless talented individuals were born. They were the future. The main continent had its Tower of Sin for cultivation, but the Heartlands had no such place, which was why warriors generally left the common folk alone.
If it weren't for the Corrupted Divine Kingdom, the Heartlands might have seen a new sacred place or two rise within a hundred years. A thousand, and maybe even a seventh-grade sect would have emerged, along with warriors powerful enough to reach the legendary Soul Sea Realm.
[MUSIC: INTENSE RISING TENSION]
But this time, the Corrupted Divine Kingdom had crossed a line. They clearly didn't give a damn about the consequences.
[SFX: ETHEREAL WHOOSH]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
As the boy’s soul bored into his mind, Zane didn’t fight it. He simply opened the gates and let it crawl inside.
[SFX: SINISTER WHISPER ECHO]
"Given up already?" the boy’s voice sneered in his head. "The warriors of the Heartlands are pathetic. It’s not just that you’re physically weak—your souls are fragile, too!"
The other warriors he’d consumed had at least tried to struggle. Zane’s complete lack of resistance only convinced the boy that his soul was the weakest of them all.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ETHEREAL SPACE]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CONTEMPLATIVE]
But deep within Zane’s mind, his own soul floated, glowing with a steady, emerald-green light. It was on the verge of becoming a nightjade fighting soul, but the Cobalt Dragon had advised him to halt his training for now. Once the soul seed was finally ready, his soul would merge with it, shattering the barrier to the next realm. Creating that seed was proving to be an impossibly complex task, and even with the Cobalt Dragon’s expertise, the progress felt agonizingly slow.
Then the boy saw it.
The sneer on his face froze. His voice came out as a stammer. "Blue — Blue Soul Realm?"
"My soul is indeed weak," Zane said pleasantly. "But yours is even weaker. Like an ant."
The boy bolted. Soul battles happened in an instant — the moment he understood what he was facing, he was already trying to flee.
"Since you're here," Zane said, "don't think you can leave."
From the depths of his mind came a roar that shook the air itself.
[SFX: DRAGON ROAR]
The boy's face went white. In the depths of Zane's consciousness, something vast was stirring — the suggestion of a dragon so large it filled the space, its mouth opening to release a beam of light that rolled outward like a wave. It hit the boy's soul and pinned it in place. He couldn't move. He couldn't even struggle.
"My opponent isn't me," Zane said. "It's him."
The beam of light engulfed the boy entirely. Then it withdrew, taking him with it. The Cobalt Dragon had him.
A moment later, the Cobalt Dragon spoke. "His soul is composite. A fragment of his own, woven together with wisps extracted from the people of this city. Hundreds of thousands of them, each giving up a sliver. That's how he projected himself here."
Zane opened his eyes and descended.
Finn was on his feet. "I saw something enter your mind," he said, the worry on his face unguarded. "Are you alright?"
"It's handled," Zane said. "I killed it."
Around them, the mortals were coming back to themselves. Most were clutching their heads, faces contorted in pain. Some had collapsed. The extraction had damaged them — not fatally, but enough. If Zane hadn't arrived when he did, the process would have continued until there was nothing left of them worth saving.
[MUSIC: TENSE OMINOUS]
[SFX: CRACKING GLASS]
Thousands of miles away, in the hall the Corrupted Divine Kingdom had claimed as its temporary headquarters, a mirror cracked.
In the mirror, the boy’s face flickered into view.
"Lord Wallace! What is it? What’s wrong?"
"Lord Wallace, hold on! I'll go get Maddox!"
[SFX: HURRIED FOOTSTEPS]
[AMBIENT: ECHOING HALL]
The Spirit-Tribulation Realm warriors in the hall practically scrambled over each other to leave. Lord Wallace was acting strangely, and even though he was just a projection in a mirror, a chilling aura radiated from him. Using Maddox as an excuse, they got the hell out of there.
[SFX: WHOOSH ARRIVAL]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC TENSION]
A moment later, Maddox appeared in the hall.
[AMBIENT: DARK CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
He eyed the crack in the mirror’s surface with a peculiar expression before bowing his head respectfully. "Lord Wallace," he asked the rippling reflection, "what's happened?"
[SFX: DISTORTED VOICE ECHO]
"Hmph! The piece of my soul I projected into Melody City was just devoured," Lord Wallace snapped, his voice sharp with fury.
Maddox’s head shot up in shock. "But that projection had the strength of the Fighting Soul Realm," he said immediately. "Who in all of The Heartlands could possibly devour your soul?"
[MUSIC: SINISTER REVELATION]
A cruel sneer twisted the boy's face. "This person's soul is unbelievably powerful. And thanks to him, I've stumbled upon a massive secret."
"A secret?" Maddox asked, leaning forward with sudden eagerness. "What did you find?"
[SFX: ETHEREAL FADE]
"You don't need to know," Lord Wallace said, a wicked smile spreading across his lips as his face began to fade from the mirror. "When the Star Storm ends, I will come to The Heartlands myself. Leave that young man to me."
[AMBIENT: SILENT HALL]
[MUSIC: FOREBODING ATMOSPHERE]
Lord Wallace’s message left Maddox standing in the silent hall, a grim feeling settling in his gut. He had a pretty good idea who the boy was talking about. In the younger generation of The Heartlands, there was only one person known for having such a powerful soul: Zane.
But as for the secret Lord Wallace had discovered, he couldn’t even begin to guess.
