Ep 1242 - A Familiar Face

Word count: 1032

[MUSIC: TENSE CONTEMPLATIVE]
[AMBIENT: QUIET MOUNTAIN WIND]
Once she handed over the runestone, Sloane would have no way to hold Zane to their deal. 
This wasn’t just any secret; it was a magnetic power technique that wasn’t even passed down within her own clan. The patriarch himself had taught it to her in private.
Normally, Sloane trained inside Sacred Mount Lodestone, cultivating her power with the Five Elements to draw on the magnetic forces in her surroundings. 
But the mountain itself was incredibly heavy. Without knowing how to refine it, she could never hope to carry it around the way Zane did. If he’d been a total stranger, she never would have considered giving up such a powerful secret. But the refining method he possessed was just too valuable to let go. 
She couldn’t let him walk away with something she so desperately needed, so Sloane had no choice but to swallow her pride and make the trade.
[MUSIC: CALCULATING STRINGS]
Zane, however, was no fool. He saw the hesitation warring on her face and knew exactly what she was thinking. He just waited, letting her make the call. If she didn’t trust him, he could just leave. Now that he knew the potential of the mountain’s magnetic power, he could always dig up information on it in The Archives. It might take a while, but he’d find the method eventually. Still, that would mean waiting until he was out of the Illusion Battlefield. Getting the information from Sloane right now was just so much more convenient.
Sloane chewed on her lip, her shoulders finally slumping in defeat. 
“You can’t share this with anyone,” she said, her voice low. “If you do, House Kincaid will hunt you to the darkest corners of the universe.”
Zane had to fight the urge to roll his eyes. After killing four Supreme Lords, he wasn’t exactly shaking in his boots over a threat from House Kincaid. They could get in line with the long list of enemies already waiting for him back at his Elysian Keep.
Still, he gave her a nod of understanding and took the runestone.
With the stone in his hands, he focused his senses, his mind absorbing the technique in a flash. Just as she’d said, this alone would be enough to extract the magnetic power from Sacred Mount Lodestone. 
[SFX: MYSTICAL ENERGY PULSE]
The moment the last bit of knowledge settled into place, he felt a familiar shift—an epiphany.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Sloane felt a knot of dread tighten in her stomach as Zane’s expression went blank. She wasn’t normally so jumpy, but she’d just handed an outsider a technique gifted to her personally by the patriarch of House Kincaid. The consequences would be severe if he ever found out.
But there was still the other half of their bargain to settle. Zane hadn’t come away from this empty-handed for her, either — a deal was a deal. He didn’t bother inscribing anything onto a runestone. Instead, he reached out with a thread of True Essence and spoke directly into her mind, walking her through the method step by step: the blood sacrifice.
[SFX: SOFT PULSE OF ENERGY]
As he explained it word for word, Sloane’s eyes grew brighter, taking on a clear, watery shine. It wasn’t just useful — it was elegant, built on an understanding of the world’s laws deeper than anything she’d encountered. 
“I wonder who came up with something like this,” she murmured, almost to herself. Whoever it was, their insight had to rival the House Kincaid patriarch’s own.
When he finished, Zane asked, “Have you memorized it well?”
Sloane nodded, still turning the method over in her mind. Then his voice cut back in, flat and businesslike.
“Now you need to hold up your end — the other condition.”
“Of course,” she said.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BUILD]
[AMBIENT: OUTSIDE CITY WALLS]
A short while later, two of Sloane’s own warriors stood before Zane outside the main city, fury written plainly across their faces. Whatever arrangement their Young Master had struck with this outsider, it meant handing over Illusion Points they’d spent painstakingly collecting — points they’d expected to eventually hand off to Sloane herself, once she killed them in turn.
Now Sloane had ordered them to stand still and let this stranger do it instead. 
[SFX: TWO QUICK IMPACTS]
Zane’s fists moved twice. Both warriors dropped, and two glowing spheres of Illusion Points burst free — roughly two hundred thousand each, more than four hundred thousand in total, absorbed into him in an instant.
His ranking surged again. Without lingering, Zane turned and drifted away from the city.
In the Illusion Battlefield, everyone started with a hundred points. As time passed, those points concentrated in the hands of the powerful few - a food chain where the weak fed the strong, who in turn became prey for the stronger. This was the warrior’s way.
Zane now held roughly five million Illusion Points, likely placing him in the top ten thousand. But comfort was a luxury he couldn’t afford. Here, warriors either climbed or fell - standing still meant falling behind. His five million would mean nothing in hours as others surged ahead. The only way to maintain position was to keep hunting.
Behind him, Sloane watched Zane’s silhouette shrink into the distance, her expression more complicated than she wanted to admit. 
There would be another chance to face him before this was over. The Illusion Battlefield’s first stage was really just a numbers game — collect enough Illusion Points to matter — but the world was split into fifty great worlds, and the strongest of every one of them were bound to collide in a final stage. Sloane was confident she’d be standing there when it happened. 
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS EXPLORATION]
[AMBIENT: FOREST WIND WITH RUSTLING LEAVES]
[SFX: HEAVY LANDING]
Zane scanned the map before swooping down toward the main city he’d chosen, landing hard on the ground. A sprawling, emerald-green field stretched out before him, dotted with towering trees that reached into the clouds, each one supporting a massive treehouse. The city walls were formed by rows of ancient, intertwined tree roots that pulsed with a dense, living energy.
His eyes flashed with curiosity. So, this was the main city of the Demon Night race. It was an interesting sight, but he quickly reminded himself why he was here: to get more points.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
But he never expected to run into a familiar face in a place like this.
