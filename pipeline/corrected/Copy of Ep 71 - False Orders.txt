Ep 71 - False Orders

[AMBIENT: NIGHT CAMP PERIMETER]
"It's Zane Lowell," he called out as he neared the door. "I need to speak with Shane."
[SFX: TORCH FLAMES]
As the torchlight fell across Zane's face, the two soldiers' expressions went from suspicion to slack-jawed shock.
"Hey, man! I don't believe it," one of them gasped, his voice choked with relief. "You're alive! Thank God!"
Both men were from the Emerald Task Force—survivors, really.
Captain Shane's battle strategies had earned a reputation for near perfection. Meticulous and prudent, he never risked his soldiers' lives unnecessarily, which explained why casualties under his leadership remained exceptionally rare. His men trusted him implicitly, knowing that each tactical decision prioritized their survival alongside mission objectives.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REMEMBRANCE]
Yet the recent engagement had shattered this pattern of success. Dozens of soldiers perished in a single afternoon—a catastrophic loss that sent shockwaves through the entire task force. Veterans who had served multiple campaigns stood hollow-eyed at roll call, the empty spaces beside them a stark reminder of comrades who would never return. The devastating toll weighed heavily on everyone's spirits, casting a pall of grief and confusion over Dragon's Keep.
The survivors knew the truth: if Zane hadn't drawn the Razorwing Queen away, none of them would have made it out. Had Shane been the only one to act as bait, the queen would have killed him and still caught up to the rest.
It was Zane's sacrifice that had saved them, and it was a debt they could never repay.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
Until this moment, they had all believed him to be dead. Seeing him standing there, completely unharmed, was unthinkable.
They knew Zane was an Outer Disciple from the Skyreach Sect.
And while he was only at the Marrow Refining Level, his strength far outstripped others at his rank. Still, the gap between him and the Razorwing Queen was immense.
His escape remained an utter enigma to everyone present.
"Zane, the captain... he isn't in the camp," one of the soldiers informed him, his voice cracking with evident distress.
Zane's brow furrowed as a knot of concern tightened in his stomach. "What do you mean? He didn't make it back?" His fingers instinctively curled into fists, preparing for bad news.
"Oh no, he made it back, but..." the soldier trailed off, glancing away at the muddy ground, clearly wrestling with whether to continue. Shadows from the nearby torchlight danced across his troubled face.
Seeing the worry etched deeply on the man's weathered features, Zane stepped closer and pressed, "But what? What happened to him?" The urgency in his voice betrayed his growing unease.
The soldier lowered his head, shoulders slumping as his voice dropped to barely above a whisper. "The captain was locked up, Zane." The words hung heavy in the air between them.
[MUSIC: RISING ANGER]
Zane's expression hardened into granite, his eyes flashing with disbelief and rising anger. "Locked up? Why?" He demanded, the muscles in his jaw tightening as he struggled to comprehend this unexpected turn of events.
"Someone changed our marching route yesterday, sent us down a more dangerous path on purpose," the soldier explained, his voice tight with anger. "Captain Shane went to the higher-ups to protest. He ended up pissing off his superior, and they arrested him. We're afraid he's going to be charged under military law for insubordination."
The two soldiers turned to Zane, eyes glistening. "Brother Zane — do you know anyone who could speak up for him? The captain didn't do anything wrong."
Zane gritted his teeth. "I'll get him an explanation. I promise."
He left the barracks and went straight to his own quarters. When he pushed the door open, Leo and Max looked up from their packed bags.
Max's mouth fell open. "Brother Zane... you're not a ghost, are you?"
Even Leo, who rarely showed surprise, looked shaken. He studied Zane for a long moment. "Did some powerful general from the Keep find you? That's the only way this makes sense."
Zane ignored the question. He looked at the packed bags. "You're leaving?"
"Withdrawing from the mission," Max said.
Leo shrugged. "I wasn't here for the points."
"What about Shane?" Zane said. "He risked his life leading the Queen away to save all of us. Do you know what's happened to him?"
Max shifted uncomfortably. "Zane, I understand, but the waters in Dragon's Keep run deep. I'm just an outer disciple. This isn't something I can wade into."
Leo's expression darkened — not at Max, but at the implication. "I'll say the same. And I'll tell you why: the person behind this isn't someone you want to cross. It's House Sterling."
Zane's mind reeled. Someone had deliberately rerouted them, putting the entire Emerald Task Force in mortal danger, and nearly getting them all slaughtered.
The disciples from the Skyreach Sect—Leo, Max, and himself—had barely escaped. Poor Rex hadn't been so lucky, cut down by a single strike from the Razorwing Queen.
Then something clicked. His anger went cold and sharp.
The route hadn't been changed to harm the Emerald Task Force. The soldiers were collateral. House Sterling had been gunning for him — and they'd used Shane's unit to do it.
"House Sterling again," he said softly, and laughed — a hard, humourless sound. "I understand now. I finally understand."
"What do you understand?" Leo asked.
Zane didn't answer directly. Instead: "Leo — are you afraid of House Sterling?"
Leo's jaw tightened. "Afraid? My family and theirs stand on equal ground. But I won't pick a fight with them over this."
The more Zane thought about it, the angrier he got. He'd only known Shane for a short time, but he respected the man's leadership and his cool head under pressure. Now, he was paying the price for doing the right thing.
Zane knew asking Leo for help was a long shot. Picking a fight with someone from House Sterling to help a captain he barely knew? It was a naive move, and Zane knew Leo would see right through his desperation.
He let out a weak, humourless chuckle and pleaded anyway. "Leo, please. Just this once. I'll owe you. House Brandt will have my loyalty."
Leo just shook his head. "Zane, I'll give you this—you've got talent. But that's not enough for me to stick my neck out for you."
Honestly, Leo wanted to be on good terms with Zane. The kid was a rare genius, even by the Skyreach Sect's standards, and he might become someone important someday. But "someday" was a long way off. After a quick calculation of the risks and rewards, Leo had his answer. Pissing off Felix Sterling for Zane just wasn't worth it.
If Zane were already an unbeatable master, sure, Leo would've helped in a heartbeat. But he wasn't. For all his incredible talent, Zane could still be crushed for ticking off one of The Seven Great Houses.
[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]
Just then, Zane's expression hardened with resolve. He reached into his Storage Ring and pulled out the crystal core he'd taken from the Razorwing Queen, holding it up for Leo and Max to see.
"Nobody saved me yesterday, Leo," Zane said, his voice ringing with defiance. "The Razorwing Queen is dead, and I'm the reason. That psycho Felix has tried to kill me more than once, but I'm still standing. I am going to be someone in the future. And one day, I'll be in a position to help House Brandt!"
[AMBIENT: MAGICAL ENERGY HUM]
[MUSIC: DETERMINED THEME]
The fist-sized crystal core radiated a brilliant light, casting an ethereal glow across Zane's face, illuminating the fierce determination etched into his youthful features. His declaration resonated with an unshakable conviction that transcended mere bravado.
[SFX: ENERGY PULSES]
Leo's gaze locked onto the pulsing core, his expression transforming from dismissal to astonishment. The significance struck him like a physical blow—not for the core's monetary worth, but for the impossible feat it represented: the dreaded Razorwing Queen had fallen.
The details of her demise paled in comparison to the shocking reality before him. Zane stood there, alive and victorious, clutching irrefutable evidence of an accomplishment that seasoned warriors only whispered about in taverns. The impossible was real, forcing Leo to reconsider everything he thought he knew about Zane.
