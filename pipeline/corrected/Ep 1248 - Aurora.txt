Ep 1248 - Aurora

Word count: 989

[MUSIC: TENSE ORCHESTRAL]
[AMBIENT: BATTLEFIELD WITH DISTANT ROARS]
The Ogres’ eyes burned with rage. They’d teamed up the moment they entered the Illusion Battlefield and had already plundered three entire cities, earning two of their members a rank in the top ten thousand of this world. They’d stumbled upon The Deadwood Gate by pure chance and figured it for an easy score.
They never expected to face resistance this fierce.
After wasting hours trying to break the Falling Leaves Array, they were down two men, and their patience was gone.
[SFX: SHIELD MATERIALIZING]
The lead Ogre roared, a giant shield materializing on his arm. “I’ll take the lead! You guys, follow close and get it together! I don’t think they can hold out much longer!”
“Let’s go!” one of them yelled back.
“Damn it, this is a total waste of time!” another shouted. “What kind of treasure is even on this cursed tree?”
[SFX: WAR CRIES]
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
The others joined in, their complaints turning into war cries as they finally unleashed their full power, ready to end this for good.
On the front line, a tall man from the Demon Night stood beside Amber. His long, blue hair was so vibrant it looked spun from the sky itself. 
[SFX: MAGICAL SWIRLING LEAVES]
Right now, countless leaf fragments swirled around his hand, forming a shimmering bracelet.
“Amber, I can’t hold it much longer,” Aurora said, his voice tight with exhaustion.
“It’s okay,” Amber replied, offering a faint, reassuring smile. “We’ll get another chance.”
Aurora just frowned, lost in thought. 
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
With a flick of his fingers, the edges of the swirling leaves began to glow with a golden light, making them look sharper, deadlier. “I admire your optimism,” he said. “If you ever get your freedom, you’ll have a bright future, even in the Demon Night of the Upper World.”
Amber’s smile didn’t falter. She was used to Aurora’s bleak pronouncements, but what she was hoping for was a long shot. The rules for the warriors of the Demon Night weren’t overly strict, but an order from their queen was absolute. Amber had personally offended the Queen of Punishment—an ordinary warrior like her wasn’t supposed to survive that.
“Those brutes are coming!”
“Then let’s do this.”
Resolve settled over both their faces as they braced for the charge. 
[MUSIC: SUDDEN SHIFT]
“Looks like we’re not late!” a voice called out from behind the Ogre line.
Every head turned. A human warrior — here, at the Deadwood Gate? The Ogres stared in open disbelief. Behind them, Aurora and his Demon Night warriors were just as thrown.
“Rowan! She brought reinforcements — but a human?” one of the Demon Night cultivators demanded, bewildered. There’d always been an understanding in the main city: outsiders didn’t set foot inside the Deadwood Gate. And yet here Rowan was, having apparently brought one herself.
Zane walked forward at an easy pace, a faint smile on his face, seemingly indifferent to the wall of Ogres in front of him. His eyes never landed on them at all — they went straight past, and found Amber’s.
Amber’s breath caught. The sword in her hand trembled, its faint glow flickering. She stared at him, disbelief written across her face.
She’d heard his voice before — carried across the universe, echoing even into the depths of her imprisonment. Back when they’d first crossed paths, he’d been nothing but a scrawny Warrior. It hardly seemed possible that the same person now stood here, having caused this much of a stir in only a few short years.
There’d been a small, secret hope she’d let herself hold onto once — that if Zane rose far enough, he might someday be the one to free her. But she’d long since talked herself out of it. Even if Zane climbed to the very top, why would his attention ever fall on some forgotten prisoner tucked away in a corner of the universe? It wasn’t possible. 
And yet it had happened. 
She could hardly speak past the emotion of it.
“Human cultivator, get lost. You don’t belong here,” one of the Ogres snapped.
Zane kept walking, that same faint smile in place, his gaze still fixed past them on Amber.
A flicker of unease crossed the lead Ogre’s face — something about this human didn’t sit right with him. “Kill him,” he ordered flatly.
[SFX: MASSIVE MACE SWING]
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]
The Ogre with the giant shield surged forward first, spiked mace raised high, his strength detonating outward like something closer to a falling mountain than a swing of a weapon.
But Zane didn’t even try to dodge.
Standing just inside the formation, Aurora stared, stunned. He had no idea what he was planning.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
Zane simply reached out, his hand glowing with energy as he slashed upward in a slow, diagonal arc. The motion wasn’t fast, but it seemed to radiate an impossible power. 
[SFX: THUNDEROUS IMPACT]
The deafening crack of his palm meeting the mace sent a shockwave through the air.
In that instant, everyone froze.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
[SFX: STEEL SHATTERING]
The thick steel mace shattered against his open hand as if it were glass. The top half flew backward, dissolving into a cloud of black dust that rained down on the great tree. 
[SFX: FLESH TEARING]
[SFX: BLOOD SPRAY]
At the same time, the Ogre’s hands, still clutching the handle, burst open in a spray of thick, brown blood.
The damage didn’t stop there. 
[SFX: BONES CRACKING]
[SFX: MUSCLE RIPPING]
The force of the impact shot up his arms, splintering bone and tearing muscle all the way to his shoulders, ripping them from their sockets.
All from a single, simple strike.
Standing behind Zane, Rowan’s eyes were wide with awe. This man’s strength was beyond anything she could have possibly imagined.
Even Aurora, who came from a noble family and had witnessed incredible displays of power, could only stare in shock. 
[AMBIENT: EERIE SILENCE WITH WIND]
A profound sense of danger radiated from him, eclipsing everything else. 
A moment ago, he’d been ready to fight the Ogres to his last breath. But watching what Zane had just done, a chilling helplessness washed over him. 
The thought of fighting the Ogres suddenly seemed trivial. Against a man like this, he wouldn’t stand a chance.
