Ep 625 - The Hard Way
Word count: 1016
[AMBIENT: TENSE COUNCIL CHAMBER]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
In the end, all eyes were glued to Zane.
He just stood there with a deadpan look, his mind seemingly a million miles away, while the most powerful beings in the room waited for him to speak.
Ogres are born with fiery tempers, and it didn't take long for one of them to crack. "Kid, are you going to make up your mind or not?" a high-ranking War Lord blurted out. "Five seats are more than enough respect for you humans!"
The Ogre who’d spoken was Bradley, one of the top ten War Lords and ranked fourth among all one hundred twenty-eight seat owners. In terms of power and status among his people, he was second only to Maurice.
Fabian's jaw tightened. He couldn't speak — not here, not in the middle of a negotiation — but he pushed his voice through a Vitality transmission directly into Zane's ear. "Zane. Take the deal. Five seats — we're more than satisfied."
Zane gave no sign he'd heard a word of it.
He finally shifted his gaze to Bradley, a faint smirk playing on his lips. "We can do this the easy way, or we can do this the hard way. And you're really making me think you'd prefer the hard way. Keep pushing, and you can forget about those five seats. We'll have to think long and hard about giving you even one."
[MUSIC: LOW BUILDING TENSION]
Zane turned his eyes back to Maurice. “Peaceful coexistence… When I entered the first floor, you didn't want peaceful coexistence. Second floor, third, fourth, all the way up to the tenth, and even after I walked out of the Talent Tablet room, you were still sending people to kill me. You brought out two War Sages. I used the Cursed Land to finish them.” He tilted his head slightly. "And now, you want to talk about peaceful coexistence?"
Fabian and Jarrod went pale. This wasn't bargaining. Zane had something else entirely in mind, and neither of them could begin to guess what it was.
Esther Sterling's eyes flickered. Her expression stayed composed, but something shifted behind it. His vision isn't fixed on this Tower at all, she realized. It never was.
The Tower of Sin, the main continent — they were too small a stage for someone like him. Whatever he was reaching for, it was something far larger. She let out a quiet breath and settled back to watch.
Maurice's face had gone from carefully neutral to dark. "Since you don't intend to coexist peacefully," the Ogre leader rumbled, "then what do you want?"
The corner of Zane’s mouth twitched up into a full-blown grin. "The Ogres will give up all their seats and get the hell out of the Tower of Sin."
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The air went still. Fabian and the other humans held their breath, their eyes wide. Esther Sterling instantly dropped into a defensive stance, bracing for the fight she was sure was coming. The other Demon Night War Lords tensed up, their hearts pounding.
Zane’s demand was so outrageous that it was almost unthinkable. The Ogres had come here in good faith, ready to cooperate, and this kid had just told them to pack up and leave their entire territory.
Through it all, Maurice remained motionless on his massive seat. His face was grim, but he showed no trace of anger.
His men, however, weren't nearly as composed. The War Lords behind him bristled at the humiliation. Bradley was practically vibrating with rage, a crushing aura flooding the room as he readied himself to attack.
The other Ogres were breathing heavily, their eyes locked on Zane like predators ready to tear him apart.
Just as they were about to charge, Maurice raised a hand, stopping them in their tracks. He turned back to Zane, his voice low and heavy. "You want us to leave the Tower. That is a demand we could never agree to. It's unreasonable. Ask for something else."
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
"Unreasonable?" Zane actually chuckled. "You tried to have me killed. Was that not unreasonable? If I hadn't been strong enough to defend myself, I'd have been dead a hundred times over by now. I'm not asking for your heads—I'm just telling you to get out of my tower. I’d say that’s pretty damn reasonable."
Maurice's eyes grew complicated. Either Zane had no idea what he was up against, or he was absolutely certain he had every Ogre in the room at his mercy. The latter, Maurice was beginning to realize, was frighteningly likely.
"The Ogres have never feared another race," Maurice said, and for the first time, a note of steel entered his voice. "Countless War Sages have walked out of this Tower over the years, and we have never once lost our hold on it. I will not agree to this. Change your condition."
Bradley had heard enough. "Stop wasting breath on this brat," he snarled. "Kill him and be done with it."
[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]
He was on his feet in an instant, his full War Lord power erupting like a wave crashing over the room, sweeping toward Zane.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY]
Esther moved at the same moment, a fine net of energy unfurling from her body and snapping into place between Bradley and Zane. The other Demon Night War Lords surged to their feet.
Fabian and Jarrod didn't know whether to fight or flee.
Only Maurice and Zane remained seated, eyes locked on each other, perfectly still.
[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]
Bradley froze mid-charge. Not by choice. An invisible force had closed around him like a fist, stopping him dead before he'd even reached Esther's net. He thrashed against it, roaring.
"Human brat — when I get free, I'll tear you apart!"
He struggled with everything he had. It made no difference. The harder he fought, the tighter the force drew around him, like a web pulling taut. A peak War Lord, helpless as an insect.
Zane didn't even look at him.
"Kill him," he said.
[SFX: SICKENING IMPACT]
The invisible force obeyed. Bradley's body came apart.
[AMBIENT: SHOCKED SILENCE]
[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
A collective, sharp intake of breath swept through the chamber.
Zane’s control over the Tower of Sin was far greater than any of them had ever imagined.
