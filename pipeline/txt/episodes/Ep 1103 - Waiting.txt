Ep 1103 - Waiting

Word count: 1169

[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]
No one was really paying attention to Zane, and you couldn’t blame them. He was only at the fifth level of the Life-and-Death Realm, waiting on a Sixth Small Heavenly Tribulation that was basically nothing compared to the Tenth that Layla was about to face. So, naturally, all eyes were on her.
As Jasper kicked off his own Tenth Small Heavenly Tribulation, a few others followed suit. 
[SFX: THUNDER RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]
The sky lit up with all kinds of power, the clouds rumbling like the start of a massive storm.
Two hours crawled by before Jasper’s tribulation finally ended. The lotus flower floating above him spun gently, its shadow fading away as a flawless Soul Sea formed inside him. 
He was now the textbook definition of a Soul Sea Realm warrior. As his power grew, that perfect Soul Sea would unlock even greater strength and endless possibilities—things only achievable with a foundation as solid as his. He closed his eyes, sinking into the feeling of his newfound power and solidifying it.
Meanwhile, Layla and Zane were still waiting. Their tribulations hadn’t even started.
The Tenth Tribulation Introducing Pill didn’t guarantee an immediate trial, and as more time passed, Layla was getting anxious. Zane, on the other hand, couldn’t care less. It didn’t really matter to him if his Sixth Small Heavenly Tribulation came now or later; he was getting stronger regardless, and it was bound to happen eventually.
[MUSIC: ANXIOUS PIANO]
But for Layla, this was everything. She couldn’t afford to miss this chance.
Another hour went by. By now, every other warrior who’d taken the pill had gone through their trial. The ones who had reached the ninth level of the Life-and-Death Realm were all exceptional talents, and while the tribulations they faced were immense, they had come prepared. No one was in any real danger. A couple of them did pass out right after finishing, but they’d only failed because they got cocky, underestimating the trial’s power and pushing themselves too hard.
“It still hasn’t come,” Layla murmured, her voice tight with stress. She glanced over at Zane, who looked completely calm, as if the long wait wasn’t bothering him in the slightest.
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: ETHEREAL STRINGS]
Just then, a faint shadow appeared over her, growing thicker until a lotus flower took shape in the air.
“It’s here!” she breathed, a jolt of surprise and relief in her voice.
[SFX: ENERGY CRACKLE]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ESCALATION]
At the same moment, a small black point appeared in the sky above her—and began to grow. Within seconds, it had widened into a yawning black hole several feet across, with violet lightning coiling around its edges. Strange, glowing runes drifted in the lightning, patterns no one had drawn—they seemed to have formed on their own.
One of the palace masters watched in awe, his eyes widening. “Of course,” he said to himself. “A cultivation base like hers—the heavens wouldn’t dare hold back.”
Around the platform, more than a few of the watching cultivators went pale. If something that powerful came down on her at full strength, would there be anything left of her at all?
[SFX: THUNDER CRACK]
The black hole let loose. Layla met it head-on—and beneath it came more. Wave after wave, each one named, each one worse than the last: searing fire that left no shadow, a creeping nightmare-force that clawed at the mind. Each of these alone would have been the worst tribulation most warriors would ever face. Layla had to take them one after another, every wave hitting harder than the one before.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
She threw everything she had into it, her whole body in motion against the relentless onslaught, refusing to give an inch.
[MUSIC: DECRESCENDO TO CALM]
At last, it was over. The tribulations broke apart and faded into nothing.
Layla dropped back to the ground, settling cross-legged, exhaustion written across her face. But underneath the exhaustion was a smile—she’d done it. This was the hardest tribulation she’d ever faced, and she’d come through it.
The lotus above her spun, and then a second, even larger one materialized above the first, this one with far more petals. The Lesser Lotus Flower had nine petals, but the Greater Lotus Flower had far more than that.
Normally, once a tribulation passed, the lotus flower above a warrior would fade and dissolve completely. But Layla’s didn’t fade. If anything, it was growing more solid by the second.
The Lord of the Sunset World’s expression sharpened. “This is—”
Yolande’s voice was calm. “She might be opening the Greater Lotus Flower.”
“That has to be it,” one of the palace lords said, sounding stunned. “She’s barely broken through to the Soul Sea Realm, and she’s already opening the Greater Lotus Flower? That’s—”
Even the most seasoned of the palace masters found it hard to keep their composure.
Opening the Greater Lotus Flower was already vanishingly rare. For someone to do it this early—fresh off breaking through to the Soul Sea Realm—meant something far beyond ordinary talent. 
Every cultivator gathered here was an elite of the Thirteen Palaces—the best the Alliance had to offer, every one of them having opened at least the Lesser Lotus Flower in full. 
But the Greater Lotus Flower was something none of them had ever dared imagine for themselves. And here it was, blooming over Layla in front of everyone.
[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT CRESCENDO]
As the Greater Lotus appeared, its first petal slowly unfolded. Then a second, a third, a fourth, and a fifth. Layla stared up at the blooming flower, a faint, tired smile finally lighting up her face. Five petals from the Greater Lotus, plus the nine from the Lesser, made fourteen in total.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
[AMBIENT: PALACE HALL]
After the fifth petal opened, the Greater Lotus began to fade, disappearing a few moments later.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
The palace leaders watched with a mix of awe and envy; the power Layla was displaying was enough to make even them jealous.
[MUSIC: CONFIDENT THEME]
Once her lotus was gone, Layla shot a meaningful smile at Zane.
She was thinking back to the Enchanted Barrier Tower. Sure, he’d gotten the better of her then, but only because she’d had to bail early. And yes, she’d beaten him in the Illusionary Arena, but that was all thanks to her brother’s strength. 
Deep down, she knew Zane wasn’t that far behind her in pure skill, and that fact had always bothered her. She wanted to create some real distance between them, to prove she was undeniably the better warrior.
A satisfied smile spread across her face. With a result like this, how could he possibly catch up to her now?
Zane wasn’t stupid. He saw the look and knew exactly what she was thinking. But it didn’t bother him. Right now, he was more focused on his own tribulation. 
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL DRUMS]
He just kept waiting, wondering why the hell it still hadn’t shown up.
