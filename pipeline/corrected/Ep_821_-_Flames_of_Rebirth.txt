Ep 821 - Flames of Rebirth

Word count: 1041

[AMBIENT: TENSE MAGICAL ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Fiery Shine's Sun Flame Attack hit Zane dead center — and he didn't even flinch.

War King Wind, a master of fire-based cultivation, understood the Fire Law far better than Fiona. He knew precisely what was coming.

Despite her third-layer Fire Law comprehension and Semi-Divine red jade bracelet, Fiona couldn't withstand every flame—especially true Phoenix fire.

The War King had only been testing her. He'd never intended serious harm, which was why he'd pulled her from the barrier when danger struck.

The Emperor had felt the same protective urge toward Zane. Yet he couldn't read the young warrior at all. Zane had already shrugged off the Phoenix's Sun Flame Attack, seemingly eager for more. 

Why was he deliberately provoking the creature? Did he actually want it to unleash its full power? The thought left the Emperor feeling utterly helpless.

[SFX: WHOOSHING FLAMES]

Just as War King Wind pulled Fiona to safety, the Phoenix inside the barrier coiled in on itself, its massive form shrinking as the ring of fire surrounding it tightened into a suffocating vortex.

Fiona stared, wide-eyed, at her bonded beast. “He’s provoked it,” she said, her voice tight with dread. “It’s going to use the Flames of Rebirth to burn him to nothing.”

The Phoenix was a symbol of immortality. While they were a step below True Dragons in raw power, their life force was in a league of its own. A Phoenix could be reborn an infinite number of times. As long as fire existed in the world, so could they.

[MUSIC: EPIC ORCHESTRAL BUILD]

That was the secret behind the power Zane had sensed earlier. When Fiona first summoned Fiery Shine, he’d felt a power that surpassed the third layer of the Fire Law, because the creature had just been reborn. It had already died once before this fight even began. 

Death wasn’t a threat to a True Phoenix; it was a tool.

[SFX: PHOENIX REBIRTH FLAMES]

And in that moment of rebirth, they could unleash the most devastating power in their arsenal: the Flames of Rebirth. It was the manifestation of the Fire Law’s fourth layer.

The Phoenix kept shrinking, smaller and smaller, until it curled into a perfect sphere — a phoenix egg, glowing with a deep orange-red luster, smooth as a gem. Fourth-layer Fire Law poured from its surface in waves, igniting the air itself into a golden flame storm that swept outward and engulfed everything inside the barrier.

[SFX: ROARING FIRESTORM]

[MUSIC: THUNDERING ORCHESTRAL SURGE]

The Soul Sea master maintaining the barrier set his jaw. The gap between each layer of the Law was immense, and the fourth layer was nothing to take lightly — but he held firm, pouring everything he had into keeping the barrier sealed. Beyond the boundary, the Soul Sea warriors watching had gone pale. None retreated. None wanted to be the first to show fear.

Inside, the barrier had become a fiery hell. There was nothing but flame.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

And then — a golden rune flickered to life on Zane's hand.

Then another. His feet. His shoulders. His face. One after another they surfaced, spinning across his skin, and as they spun, they began to drink.

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT BRASS]

The runes absorbed the storm. The heat that should have been agony became something else entirely — warmth, deep and pervasive, settling into his bones. 

Zane looked down at his hands. Golden. He looked like he'd been cast in bronze.

He was unharmed. More than unharmed — he felt good.

Zane smiled and walked forward through the Flames of Rebirth.

[MUSIC: AWE-STRUCK STRINGS]

[AMBIENT: CRACKLING FIRE BARRIER]

He crossed the storm slowly, unhurried, and came to stand before the egg floating at the center of the barrier. Fourth-layer Fire Law crackled across its surface. He reached out and laid his fingers against it.

“A little hot,” he murmured. “And something's moving inside.”

He thought of the Ancestral Dragon Egg waiting somewhere in the Sacred Feather Sea, and wondered if it would feel the same.

[SFX: CRACKING SHELL]

Under his touch, fractures raced across the egg's surface. Then a small, blazing shape burst free — a miniature Fiery Shine, no larger than a hawk, trailing embers as it spiraled upward and began to grow.

[AMBIENT: FADING FLAMES]

The flame storm slowed. Died. Fiery Shine hung in the air above Zane, full-sized again, and looked down at him with a complicated expression. Its fire had done nothing. It turned this over, trying to understand, and could not.

It was young — young enough to have bonded with Fiona, young enough that the fourth layer was only accessible through Nirvana. It had never met a human whose flames it couldn't touch. Its instincts, honed across countless generations, gave it the only answer that made sense: reverence.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

Zane looked up at it. “Thank you,” he said.

[SFX: PHOENIX CRY]

[MUSIC: TRIUMPHANT HORNS]

[SFX: WINGS FLAPPING]

With a long, mournful cry that echoed with a newfound respect, the Phoenix unfurled and flew out of the enchanted barrier.

[AMBIENT: ARENA CROWD MURMUR]

“Lower the barrier,” Fiona commanded the warrior managing the arena. “Let Fiery Shine out.”

The man complied instantly, and the Phoenix soared directly to its master. Fiona whispered a few quiet words, and the creature’s agitated energy seemed to settle. Then, she walked slowly to the edge of the barrier and faced Zane. 

“I’ve lost,” she said, her voice clear and steady. “You’re stronger than me. There’s no denying it.”

[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE STRINGS]

The moment War King Wind had intervened, the match was over. But saying the words out loud solidified it in her mind. She’d never really stood a chance. With the weight of the fight lifted, a strange sense of calm washed over her. She was surprisingly satisfied with how it all ended.

Zane just nodded, a small smile touching his lips. “You’re not so bad yourself.”

“Yeah, I know,” Fiona shot back, a hint of her usual pride in her tone. But he knew she was serious. She was a force to be reckoned with and was well aware of her own strength—and so was he.

After a quick glance to make sure no one was close enough to overhear, she leaned in and lowered her voice to a conspiratorial whisper. “Listen, that Little Monster… he’s seriously powerful. You have to be careful, or he’ll kill you.”

Fiona had a hunch that Zane’s victory had something to do with a special talent, an immunity to flame that was unique to him.

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: DARK FOREBODING]

But the other one, the ‘Little Monster,’ was a different kind of danger altogether.
