Ep 1171 - I Won’t Back Down

Word count: 1137

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]
Lavender stared at Zane, her face completely blank. Lost in a sea of grief, her mind had simply shut down, leaving her looking like a hollowed-out doll.
A wave of pity washed over Zane. 
[SFX: GENTLE WHOOSH]
Seeing her frozen there, he reached out with his mind, gently taking control of her light sword spirit and drawing her into his arms. She hung limply against him, her hands slack at her sides, not moving at all.
*You didn’t kill them,* he thought, the words a fierce, silent promise aimed directly at her. *This is exactly what the Queen of Punishment wanted. It was them or you. This isn’t your fault. I’ll help you kill her. I swear, I’ll help you.*
“Thank you,” Lavender whispered.
Zane’s own thoughts stuttered. “What?”
A flicker of life returned to her dazed eyes. Their minds were connected, and she’d felt the raw determination behind his silent vow.
“I won’t back down,” she said, her voice soft but firm. “I promise you. I’ll kill her.”
She was still heartbroken over the deaths of Cassian, Maren, Taryn, and Paige—the people who had been so loyal to her. She had started this journey as an outcast and had clawed her way to this point, pouring everything she had into becoming strong. Now that her mind was set, she wouldn’t be easily swayed.
A small smile touched Zane’s lips when he saw a flicker of her old self return. But he pushed, needing more. “Who are you talking about? Who is it?”
[MUSIC: RISING DETERMINATION]
“The Queen of Punishment,” she said, her voice growing louder, a dangerous glint filling her eyes. “I’ll kill her with my bare hands!”
“Louder,” Zane encouraged her.
“I will kill you, Queen of Punishment!” Lavender screamed. Her voice, usually so musical, tore from her throat in a high-pitched shriek—the furious, desperate sound of a girl pushed too far.
The rage in her eyes burned brighter, pure and murderous.
Zane pointed up at the star-filled sky. “They can’t hear you up there. You have to be louder!”
She was about to scream again when he raised a finger to her lips, a sly smile playing on his face as he pulled out a Roaring Token he’d already activated. “Now they can hear you.”
[SFX: DEEP SHUDDERING BREATH]
Lavender’s whole body went rigid. She took a deep, shuddering breath and unleashed a roar into the token.
[MUSIC: EPIC COSMIC DECLARATION]
[SFX: UNIVERSE-WIDE PIERCING SCREAM]
In an instant, a piercing scream filled the entire universe, a declaration of war that echoed across the vast emptiness of space.
“Queen of Punishment! I will kill you! You will pay for this!”
[AMBIENT: CHAOTIC BEAST FRENZY]
The sudden, intense shriek startled every living creature in existence. Primal beasts, driven by instinct, flew into a frenzy, attacking anything and everything for no reason.
A moment later, a different voice echoed back, dripping with amusement. “Ha ha! You want to kill me? Lavender, do you really think you’re safe? By screaming like that, you’ve just announced your location to everyone.”
“Those four sacrificed themselves for you — and it changes nothing,” the Queen of Punishment continued. “I was willing to send Cassian himself after you. You can’t predict what I’ll do next. You can’t escape me.”
Lavender’s eyes went cold. She reached for the Roaring Token — but Zane was faster, plucking it from her hand with a faint smile.
“Hey,” he said into the token. “Do you remember me? In the Lower Realm, you tried to kill me. Several times.” A beat. “Do you still want to?”
The Queen’s reply came without hesitation. “You’re nothing. You only ascended because you stumbled across her remnant soul. She’ll die. And so will you.”
“What if I don’t die?” Zane asked.
Before she could answer, he activated the token again. “Actually, I just figured something out. You punish everyone around you, and you rule the Demon Night. Put them together, and you’re not a queen at all — you’re just a bully with a crown.”
A laugh escaped Lavender — sudden and unguarded.
Inside the Wheel of Reincarnation, Freya heard it too and couldn’t help herself. She covered her mouth, shoulders shaking.
[SFX: QUIET AMUSED MURMUR — CROWD OF VOICES]
Across the universe, countless warriors had been listening. Many of them laughed too — not just at the insult, but at the sheer spectacle of a junior openly mocking the ruler of the Demon Night and living to tell about it.
Within the Demon Night itself, reactions were more complicated. The three-queen structure had stood since the clan’s founding. The Queen of Punishment had been slowly consolidating power ever since the Queen of Life went silent — and the Board of Elders had been watching in private fury, too cautious to speak. Now, a human boy had said what they couldn’t. They weren’t angry. They were delighted.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS FOREBODING]
And just as Freya had predicted, the other Supreme Lords were taking notice.
Over a year ago, a mysterious young man had appeared out of nowhere, using a handful of Roaring Tokens to first curse Supreme Lord Armond, then to chat with some unknown woman. No one ever figured out who the woman was, and for the last year, both she and the young man seemed to have vanished from the cosmos.
Then, suddenly, Lavender’s voice had ripped across the universe. And right after, the voice of that same mysterious young man was heard once again.
Anyone with half a brain could connect the dots: the mysterious man was Zane, which meant Lavender must have gotten the Roaring Tokens from him.
“Hey, kid,” a gruff voice boomed through another Roaring Token, proving the point. “You’ve got a lot of those things. Where’d you get them?”
Zane wasn’t scared in the slightest. He’d already made an enemy of the Queen of Punishment; what was one more annoyed Supreme Lord? 
“None of your business!” he yelled back.
The Supreme Lord was clearly furious. “Brat, do you have any idea who you’re talking to?”
“You’re a Supreme Lord, right?” Zane shot back. “So what?”
“Since you know who I am, aren’t you afraid I’ll kill you?” the Supreme Lord sneered. “I can find you through the Demon Night, you know.”
Hearing this, Zane activated his own Roaring Token and let out a loud, arrogant laugh. 
“I’m already dodging the Queen of Punishment. You think one more Supreme Lord hunting me down is going to make a difference? Why would I be scared of you?”
His words dripped with unbelievable arrogance. 
[MUSIC: TENDER MELANCHOLIC]
With a small, tired smile, Lavender reached out and gently covered the Roaring Token, shaking her head. “Okay,” she murmured. “I’m okay now. You can put it away.”
She knew he had done all of this, caused all this chaos, just for her.
