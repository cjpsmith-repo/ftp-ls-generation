Ep 546 - No One is Safe

[AMBIENT: MISTY MARSHLAND]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS FLUTE]
For years, martial artists had hounded Simon, forcing him to hide in the Dreaming Marshes. There, he'd tamed two Spirit-Tribulation-level beasts and learned to trust them over people.
Elder Roland hesitated. Though he was a Divine Kingdom warrior equal to the Holy Maiden, he recognized the danger. Unlike his reckless compatriot, Roland knew when to bend rather than break.
[SFX: TENSE BREATH]
"Fine," Elder Roland ground out through gritted teeth. "I swear on my warrior's spirit."
With the deal struck, Simon chuckled and pointed at the Holy Maiden.
[SFX: ENERGY BEAM]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHIMES]
A white beam of light shot from his finger.
The light washed over her, seeping into her clothes and flowing up over her head.
[SFX: MAGICAL DISPERSION]
In an instant, the dark cloud above her—and the black lightning crackling within it—vanished.
The Holy Maiden let out a shaky breath, her relief quickly hardening into a furious glare aimed straight at Zane.
Zane, however, didn't give her a second glance. He just sat on his disc, watching the next person roll the dice.
A white-haired elder from the Mysterious Luna Hall rolled a three.
[AMBIENT: TENSE CROWD MURMUR]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
When a black disc appeared, a hush fell over the crowd. The elder might have reached the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, but not even he could survive a punishment like that.
The old man hesitated. Steeling himself, he moved to step onto the black disc.
[SFX: FOOTSTEP HESITATION]
But this one was different; a thin white line ran across its surface.
Just as the elder’s foot hovered over the edge, a voice boomed, "You can kill anyone here."
The atmosphere shifted, the air crackling with a new kind of tension.
They all knew the immortal's power. If the elder followed the rules and chose someone, no one could stop what happened next.
[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]
Maddox, Simon, and the other Life-and-Death Realm warriors went very still. After everything it had taken to claw their way to that realm, the thought of having their life and death handed to someone else was deeply unsettling.
Zane and Tia, on the other hand, were less worried. They had no quarrel with this elder—he had no reason to come after them.
But something else caught Zane's attention. He noticed that the leader of the Mysterious Luna Hall had gone deathly pale—even though it was his own sect's elder who had drawn the kill power.
Zane turned it over in his mind. Could it be…
[SFX: RELIEVED SIGH]
A long sigh of relief escaped the white-haired elder’s lips, his face splitting into an excited grin. "Ha-ha! I never thought I'd get the chance to kill someone! Now, who should I pick?" he mused, scratching his chin.
[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]
The elder turned and fixed his gaze on Elder Luna, the Mysterious Luna Hall's own leader.
"I want to kill him," he said simply.
[SFX: SHARP INTAKE OF BREATH]
Elder Luna's face changed. Dense blue energy erupted around him.
It didn't matter. A gray beam shot from the die and pierced straight through. His eyes widened. His body swelled, then vanished—head, torso, limbs—erased piece by piece.
In moments, a man who had cultivated for nearly nine hundred years was gone.
[SFX: EMOTIONAL OUTBURST]
The white-haired elder burst into tears. "I finally did it! I finally killed that old monster! Ha-ha-ha!"
The other elders of the Mysterious Luna Hall broke into congratulations, clapping him on the back.
The warriors from the other sects could only shake their heads. Whatever Elder Luna had done to earn that kind of hatred from his own people, it was clearly well deserved. The internal grudges of another sect were none of their business.
But the moment had shaken everyone. That kill couldn't be resisted. It didn't matter how strong you were.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
Then the beam of light swept around and landed on Zane.
He smiled faintly and flicked his hand. His fist connected lazily with the die.
[SFX: DICE ROLL]
Six.
The last disc glowed yellow.
"Again," Zane said.
Six.
“Again.”
Six.
The chamber fell silent. Maddox's expression darkened with every throw. This wasn't random fortune—it was destiny. From a forgotten outer disciple to the top of two great sects, Zane hadn't stopped rising.
Roll after roll, he caught up to Tia—then passed her. When the light faded, his final disc glowed green with two eye-shaped markings at its center.
He stepped onto it without hesitation.
"Clear Eyes."
[SFX: MAGICAL RESONANCE]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL WONDER]
Above him, the shadow of a massive tree materialized. From its branches, drops of emerald green sap began to fall—and instead of dropping into the abyss, they curved through the air and flowed directly into Zane's eyes.
The warriors from the Heartlands exchanged confused looks.
"He's… washing his eyes with tree sap?"
"What does that even do? It can't be worth more than five hundred years of life, surely."
"That's the legendary divine tree from the Wood Divine Kingdom! My god… how can he be using its sap to wash his eyes? What a waste!"
The holy maiden and the man from the divine kingdom stared, jaws slack, at the scene unfolding before them. The man turned to Elder Roland. "Are you sure that's the divine tree?"
"I'm positive," Roland confirmed, his voice tight. "The divine tree only appears once every five hundred years. Each time, only a single drop of sap falls from its branches. The Wood Divine Kingdom has been collecting it for over ten thousand years, and they only have about a dozen drops left."
[AMBIENT: TENSE SILENCE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]
A ripple of shock went through the other warriors.
The Wood Divine Kingdom had barely a dozen drops of the divine sap. But Zane had just used dozens to simply wash his eyes.
[SFX: LIQUID FLOWING]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHIMES]
The emerald green liquid flowed into Zane's eyes, bringing a cool, soothing sensation.
As the sap seeped in, he could feel a strange new energy building behind his eyes. The excess liquid trickled from the corners, dripping down into the endless abyss below.
A muscle in Elder Roland's jaw jumped. It was such an obscene, unbelievable waste.
The Wood Divine Kingdom treasured the sap, using it to craft healing pills or swallowing it directly. They would never waste a single drop. And here was Zane, letting the runoff just spill into nothingness.
If Zane wasn't his enemy, Roland would have lunged at him for squandering something so precious.
[SFX: MAGICAL RESONANCE]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
When the last of the sap had drained away, Zane slowly opened his eyes. Deep within his pupils, a faint green light now glowed.
