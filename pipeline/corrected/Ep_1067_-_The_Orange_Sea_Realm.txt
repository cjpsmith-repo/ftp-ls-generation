Ep 1067 - The Orange Sea Realm

Word count: 1067

[AMBIENT: TRAINING HALL]

[MUSIC: TENSE STANDOFF]

Cora crossed her arms and refused to move.

Another elder nodded reluctantly and said, “Fair enough, you’re not wrong. But it’s not uncommon for a man to have a few partners. And now that he’s in the Upper World, he might have found someone new already. How do you expect to compete with some new flame if you aren’t strong enough to stand by his side?”

“Whatever, I don’t care!” Cora shot back.

They’d tried this trick on her too many times. It worked at first, but the endless, boring routine of training had worn the tactic thin.

Now, Cora just sulked in the corner, refusing to even pick up her sword.

She was throwing a full-blown tantrum, and the elders were out of ideas. They were all at their wits’ end with the girl’s stubbornness.

What made it so frustrating was that Cora’s raw talent was every bit as great as her brother’s. But she was headstrong and had been spoiled by the clan her whole life, so no one could ever make her train.

It seemed like Zane was the only one who could ever get through to her.

Here were some of the most famous figures in the Violet Core Realm, and not a single one of them could convince her to train.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

Just then, a figure walked in quietly, stopping a short distance away.

When Cora saw who it was, she whined, “Master!”

The others noticed the newcomer too and turned to greet her respectfully. “Elder Apeiron!”

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Elder Apeiron strolled toward them. Seeing the pout on Cora’s face, she smiled gently. “I heard the Illusion Battlefield will be opening in a few years.”

Cora looked up, her curiosity piqued. “Master, what’s the Illusion Battlefield?”

Elder Apeiron’s smile widened. “You’ve trained in the Illusionary Arena before, haven’t you?”

Creating an Illusionary Arena was a common practice in the Upper World; many of the great sacred places had one for their warriors to spar in.

Cora nodded. She had.

“Well,” Elder Apeiron continued, “when the Illusion Battlefield opens, young warriors from every race across the universe will compete against each other. And I’m quite sure Zane will be among them.”

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL THEME]

“Reach the Soul Sea Realm,” Elder Apeiron said, “and I’ll let you enter. Fight your way to a place on your own strength, and finding Zane won’t be difficult at all.”

Cora stared at her. For a moment, she didn’t say anything.

She’d been avoiding the question she knew she couldn’t answer — how long would it actually be before she saw him again? Ten years? A hundred? A thousand? Just thinking about it made her stomach turn. But this was different. This was a timeline. A path with an end she could actually see.

“That said,” Elder Apeiron continued, her smile easy, “every race in the universe will send its geniuses to the Illusion Battlefield. The competition won’t be gentle. Even reaching Zane once you’re inside won’t be simple.”

Cora’s expression shifted slowly. The determination that settled into her eyes was quiet and absolute — everything she had, for one reason.

“I understand, Master.”

[MUSIC: DETERMINED ORCHESTRAL]

It was a different story for Zane. Unlike Cora, who had Elder Apeiron coaxing her every step of the way and the entire tower treating her like royalty, he had no one handing him anything. Everything he had, he’d fought for himself. The rewards waiting in Passing the Torch were no exception — he wasn’t about to let them go.

[AMBIENT: VAST OCEAN WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]

Before they reached the island, the ship had broken free of the passage into a world unlike anything Zane had seen. Beneath them stretched an endless orange sea — vast, strange, and utterly still. He couldn’t place the feeling it gave him.

“A Broken World,” Layla said, following his gaze. “Like the Violet Core Realm. That’s why the Alliance chose it for Passing the Torch.”

She called it the Orange Sea Realm — named for the microscopic creatures packed so densely into the water that they’d turned the entire sea that color.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS STRINGS]

Millions of years ago, it had been a complete realm. Then several human Empyreans quarreled and chose to settle it here. The realm couldn’t survive the weight of that much power. It collapsed. What remained was barely a tenth of what it had been.

Their destination was an island in the middle of it — Willow Island. Layla called it an island, but by lower-world standards, it was ten times the size of the Heartlands. Just another reminder of the scale of everything up here.

Zane had come from the lower realm. There was a lot he didn’t know about Passing the Torch, and he wasn’t about to keep asking Yolande every time a question came up. So he listened to Layla instead.

[AMBIENT: FOOTSTEPS ON GROUND]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC BUILDUP]

An hour’s walk inland brought them before a huge rock.

The rock was immense, easily ten thousand feet high and a hundred thousand feet wide, with a poem carved into its face.

[SFX: MAGICAL RESONANCE]

[MUSIC: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

The words were etched deep into the stone, and they seemed to pulse with a strange, magical power. Just looking at them made many of the warriors deeply uncomfortable, as if the letters themselves were sharp swords aimed right at their hearts.

But most of them tried to hide it, especially when they saw others looking perfectly calm. As warriors, none of them could stand the thought of looking weaker than their peers. If others could handle the pressure, then so would they. It was a matter of pride.

Layla suddenly turned to Zane, a smirk on her face. “How are you holding up?”

He kept his eyes on the rock. “A little uncomfortable,” he admitted quietly.

A grin spread across her lips. “At least you’re honest. Look at those guys. They’re all in agony but are trying to act like nothing’s wrong. So desperate to protect their pride.”

She didn’t bother to lower her voice, and several warriors nearby overheard. They shot her furious, embarrassed glares for calling them out. For many of them, the writing on the rock really was torture.

[MUSIC: REVERENT TONE]

“This poem was carved by a Supreme Lord with his own sword,” Layla explained. “The sword intent sealed in the words strikes right at your heart. His was said to be perfect—flawless.”

She could feel the angry stares burning into her back, but she didn’t seem to care one bit, casually explaining everything to Zane.

He had a feeling the rock was just the beginning.
