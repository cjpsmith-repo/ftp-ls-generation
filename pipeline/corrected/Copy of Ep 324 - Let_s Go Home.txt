Ep 324 - Let’s Go Home
Word count: 1326
[AMBIENT: HUSHED CHAMBERS]
[MUSIC: REVERENT DISCOVERY]
When Sir Kenneth saw the serious expressions on his disciples’ faces, he just smiled. "Don’t worry. The Cloud Road in the Eastern Territories isn’t as daunting as you’re imagining. Besides, you’re all stronger than most people your age. I have no doubt you’ll do great in the trial."
Finn, however, didn’t look convinced. "Sir Kenneth, is the Cloud Road... an actual road?"
"It is," Kenneth said slowly. "But to be fair, the name 'Cloud Road' is a bit misleading. That's just what we call it here in the Skyreach Sect. Its original name was the Trial Road for Cultivators."
The three disciples nodded, letting the weight of his words sink in.
To the warriors of the Eastern Territories, powerful figures like Kenneth—and even the Emperor —were all seen as members of Cloud Hall. Passing the test on the Trial Road for Cultivators was the only way to join them, to gain a status equal to the leader of the Skyreach Sect or the Emperor himself.
That’s why warriors in the Eastern Territories started calling it the Cloud Road—a direct path into Cloud Hall. Some legends even claimed that anyone who passed the trial would become immortal.
Of course, those were just exaggerations, but they showed just how desperate everyone was to take on the Cloud Road.
Unfortunately, history has shown that only a select few ever get the chance to even set foot on the Cloud Road. For most, it remains nothing more than a dream.
Kenneth leaned forward, his voice dropping to a confidential tone. "You should know, you three aren’t the only ones on the trial road. There’s still a month to go, and the competition remains fierce throughout the realm. While you three have earned your places, others still vie for the remaining spots. As the final month approaches, disciples across the Eastern Territories intensify their preparations, each hoping to secure this rare opportunity."
He paused, studying their expressions carefully before continuing.
"Remember, the true challenge awaits beyond selection. Many have dreamed of walking the Cloud Road, but few have ever completed its trials."
[AMBIENT: MANOR EXTERIOR]
[SFX: BODY HITTING GROUND]
Meanwhile, at Lowell Manor, a bloodied young man crashed onto the dirt path, violently thrown through the front door.
His face contorted with indignation. "You’re nothing but a servant, Grey!" he cried out, spitting blood as he struggled to rise.
Heavy footsteps resonated against stone as the confrontation escalated.
The young man pushed himself to his knees, his voice cracking with desperation and ancestral pride.
"I am a descendant of House Lowell!" he shrieked, trembling fingers clutching at the earth beneath him. "How dare you throw me out of my own home?"
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
Grey appeared in the doorway, backlit by the manor’s interior glow, a self-satisfied grin stretching across his face. He surveyed the fallen youth with cold amusement, savoring his newfound authority.
He sneered down at the young man, his voice dripping with contempt.
"How dare I? You question my authority now? Go ahead, then. I dare you to place one foot back inside these walls."
Several burly men flanked Grey, their smug faces mirroring their leader as they gazed down at the battered Lowell heir sprawled before them.
The kid was stubborn. Despite his injuries, he scrambled to his feet and lunged toward the manor gates, but before he could cross the threshold, two of the guards grabbed his arms.
The two goons were at the Bone Refining Level; the Lowell kid was only at the Strength Level. He was completely outmatched; he was stuck between them, unable to move even an inch.
Helpless rage consumed him as he screamed, "Grey, you heartless monster! I’ll kill you!"
[SFX: CLUB SWINGING]
A cruel smile twisted Grey's lips. He raised a thick club and brought it down hard against the boy's legs.
[SFX: BONE BREAKING]
[SFX: AGONIZED SCREAMS]
The sickening sound of breaking bones echoed with each heavy blow. The boy’s furious shouts turned to shrieks of pure agony as Grey shattered his legs.
[SFX: BODY TUMBLING DOWN STAIRS]
The two thugs tossed him aside like a sack of trash. His body tumbled down the stairs, and he lay crumpled in a heap of pain on the ground.
But the boy was impossibly stubborn. Even with broken legs, he tried to crawl back toward the gates of Lowell Manor, dragging himself up the steps and leaving a smear of blood in his wake.
[AMBIENT: QUIET MANOR GROUNDS]
A servant sweeping the grounds nearby watched and sighed. The blood was staining the steps. He'd just have to clean them again. It wasn’t that he didn’t feel bad for the boy—he’d worked for House Lowell for decades and cared for the family. But the Lowell family of today wasn’t the one he used to serve.
This sort of thing happened a few times a month. He was used to it by now. It was obvious the Lowell family was falling apart, but what could a simple servant do?
[SFX: VIOLENT KICK]
Grey continued to taunt the boy. "Come on! Get up here!" He strode down the steps and kicked the boy squarely in the chest. The kid had made it halfway up, but the kick sent him tumbling back to the bottom.
"Slow as a damn turtle!" one of the thugs jeered.
The other one laughed.
The servant just shook his head.
As the thugs mocked the boy’s struggle, two figures approached from the mist-shrouded path. A young man with a confident stride led the way, his posture betraying years of disciplined training. Beside him walked a graceful young woman, her violet eyes scanning the cruel scene before them. Their footsteps fell in perfect rhythm against the cobblestones, drawing the attention of Grey and his men.
"What’s happening here?" the young man asked, his voice calm yet commanding.
Grey turned, annoyance flashing across his face at the interruption. He squinted at the newcomers, a flicker of uncertainty crossing his features. Something about them seemed vaguely familiar, yet he couldn’t place them among Lowell Manor’s regular visitors or residents.
"Move along," Grey barked. "House business doesn’t concern outsiders."
The young woman’s lips curved into a subtle smile as she exchanged a knowing glance with her companion.
"Who the hell do you think you are, sticking your nose in our business?" Grey demanded. He could tell these two weren’t ordinary, but he didn’t recognize either of them.
It made sense that he wouldn’t recognize Elara; she'd been just a little girl when she left Lowell Manor. Now, she had blossomed into a beautiful young woman.
As for Zane, he had changed even more. He was a young man now, his body hardened by training. He carried himself with a quiet, impressive confidence and had grown strikingly handsome.
That's why Grey and the others didn’t recognize them.
Zane crossed to where Barnaby lay and crouched down beside him. "Barnaby," he said. "What are you doing?"
Barnaby froze. The pain cut out for a moment. How did this stranger know his name?
Zane reached into his Storage Ring and pressed an elixir into Barnaby's hand. "Take it."
Barnaby didn't hesitate. He swallowed it.
[SFX: HEALING ENERGY PULSE]
A warmth spread from his core outward. The searing pain in his legs cooled, then vanished entirely. He watched the wounds close at visible speed — and then he was on his feet, standing straight, staring at his own hands in disbelief.
Zane and Elara ignored Grey completely. Zane's eyes were only on Barnaby. "Barnaby," he said, his voice firm but gentle. "Let's go home."
Barnaby stared back, his expression clouded with pain and confusion.
Go home? The words stunned him. Did he just say... home? Only a descendant of House Lowell could call this place home.
And then, it hit him. Barnaby Lowell's eyes shot wide, a spark of disbelief and then overwhelming joy breaking through the pain.
Just as he was about to gasp Zane’s name, Zane gave him a small, firm nod. He put a hand on Barnaby’s shoulder, then glanced at Elara, who nodded back.
"Come on," Zane said again, his voice solid as stone. "Let’s go home."
