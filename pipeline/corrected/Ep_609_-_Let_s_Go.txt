Ep 609 - Let's Go
Word count: 1179
[AMBIENT: TENSE ARENA SILENCE]
An eerie silence fell over the arena. No one dared to breathe. Everyone figured Zane was a dead man walking the second he stepped into the ring.
The humans watched in despair, devastated that they couldn’t protect a talent like him. They would have done anything, but they couldn’t risk dragging the entire Violet Heart Sanctuary into the mess. In the end, their own weakness forced them to sacrifice their prized talent to save the sanctuary.
Malcolm, on the other hand, was already plotting Zane’s murder for the second the battle ended.
The ogres’ way of doing things was simple: after killing Zane, they’d just toss the humans ten Relic-Class weapons as “compensation.” It didn’t matter if the humans agreed—the weapons were just a technicality, a way to sidestep punishment for breaking the rules.
Malcolm's original plan had been to make his move after Zane’s fight with Boris. But then Cheryl, a saint-level genius from the Demon Night and every bit as strong as Boris, threw a wrench in his plans by suddenly stepping up to challenge Zane herself.
The challenge was a total shock and messed everything up, but Malcolm figured Zane would die one way or another. It wasn't just the ogres who thought so; pretty much every creature in the Tower of Sin, including the humans, felt the same way.
[SFX: MAGICAL ERUPTION]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
But then, in a flash, everything changed. A sword spirit erupted from Zane's body, revealing itself to be the queen of the Demon Night.
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
[SFX: COLLECTIVE GASP]
The queen's appearance completely flipped the script for her entire race. Their plan to stay neutral went right out the window.
Fabian and Jarrod could only stare, completely blindsided. What the hell was happening?
Before, the Demon Night had been neutral. But now, they were forced to pick a side—and they chose the humans. It was simple, really: whether in the Tower of Sin or out in the Cursed Land, the Demon Night would never accept a loss to the ogres.
Realizing this, they both shared a smirk.
Olivia's face flushed with excitement. She’d noticed Zane’s incredible confidence, how he acted like he had everything under control. She couldn't make sense of it before, but now she understood. Who would’ve guessed Zane had the queen of the Demon Night on his side?
Still, even with the answer right in front of her, something felt… off.
Just a moment ago, Zane had looked just as stunned as everyone else when the sword spirit appeared beside him.
Which meant he hadn't known. This wasn't his plan at all. So where had that confidence actually come from?
The question sat uneasily in Olivia's mind. She couldn't shake it.
Of course, what she didn't know — what none of them knew — was that Zane's confidence had nothing to do with the Demon Night. It came from something else entirely. Something he wasn't about to advertise.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
The atmosphere grew heavier by the second. Some of the more timid creatures were already edging toward the exits. If the Ogres and the Demon Night came to blows here, it would pull in War Lords from the upper floors. The tenth floor would become a battlefield.
Malcolm stretched his arm slowly, the brown muscles of his wounded shoulder rippling. The gash from Elder May's dagger compressed and went still — he'd forced the bleeding to stop through sheer will.
[SFX: FOOTSTEPS]
Then footsteps. Heavy, deliberate, and many.
A group of Ogre War Lords came down from the passage leading to the eleventh floor. Then more. Then more still — from the twelfth, the thirteenth. By the time they stopped coming, there were over sixty of them.
The creatures on the tenth floor went rigid. No one ran. Under the crushing aura of that many War Lords, their legs simply wouldn't move.
[SFX: CROWD CHATTER]
"There are more than sixty Ogre War Lords…"
"The ones at the front — those are peak War Lords. The ten seat holders…"
"The one leading them is Maurice. Second seat out of all one hundred and twenty-eight. The strongest fighter in the Tower."
[SFX: CRACKLING FLAMES]
[AMBIENT: INTENSE HEAT]
Maurice wore a set of fiery red armor traced with glowing lines, as though lava ran through the joints. Before he'd even crossed the floor, the heat rolling off him made it feel like standing at the mouth of a volcano. When he stopped, the silence that followed was absolute.
"Malcolm," he said flatly. "What's the situation?"
Malcolm looked at Zane. "I wanted to kill the human. Elder May and Madeline interfered. They wounded me."
Maurice's cold gaze moved to Elder May and Madeline. "It seems the Demon Night has no interest in coexisting peacefully with us."
Elder May and Madeline's expressions flickered — just for a moment. Maurice outranked them. Both were peak War Lords and seat holders, but there were differences within those ranks, and everyone knew it. Maurice was stronger than either of them alone.
But the flicker passed. Lavender was behind them.
"The safety of our queen is not negotiable," Elder May said, and the sharpness in his voice rose until it pushed back against Maurice's aura. "There is nothing to discuss."
"If that kid dies, we walk away from this," Maurice said, his voice a low growl.
Elder May shot back firmly. "Our queen is bound to his body. He can't die!"
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
A vicious glint flashed in Maurice’s eyes. "Is that so? Well, if that's the hill you want to die on, then let's fight!"
[SFX: WHOOSH]
He shot forward like a cannonball, dodging both Elder May and Madeline to go straight for Zane.
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC PERCUSSION]
"Stop him!" Madeline and Elder May cried out, their hearts seizing as they scrambled to intercept the Ogre War Lord. They never imagined he'd be so reckless, especially with the Demon Night still being a major power. They hadn't expected him to be this decisive. Who exactly was backing him?
The same question raced through both their minds as they rushed to block his path.
Did the ogres have some kind of secret weapon? Or were they getting support from one of the Tower's mysterious masters?
[SFX: WHOOSH]
Two massive hands lunged for him, but Zane felt no fear. He shot backward in a flash, dodging Maurice’s attack. Zane was brave, not stupid; he knew which fights he could win. Even burning all the Lucky Light stored in his chest, the best he could hope to take on was a level-three or maybe a level-four War Lord. Maurice was a peak-level War Lord—the strongest of the strong. Zane knew he didn't stand a chance.
The moment Maurice charged, Zane was already leaping back off the stage.
Maurice sneered at the retreating figure. "You're just a War Fighter, kid. You really think you can run?"
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC ORCHESTRAL SWELL]
Just then, Madeline and Elder May flanked Maurice, cutting him off. At the same instant, Lavender, the Demon Night queen, raised her black greatsword high.
[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]
"Stellar Body!"
Instantly, Zane's body began to glow as he activated his Stellar Body. He threw a punch even as he retreated!
[SFX: POWERFUL IMPACT]
"Star Shakes the Earth!"
The tenth floor of the Tower of Sin shook. And everyone on it knew — this was only the beginning.
