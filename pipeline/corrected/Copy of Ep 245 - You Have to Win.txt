Ep 245 - You Have to Win
Word Count: 1082
[AMBIENT: BATTLE ARENA]
[SFX: CRACKLING FLAMES]
The explosion had ripped through the arena with a deafening roar, engulfing everything in a blinding flash of orange and crimson. Spectators shielded their eyes as debris scattered across the battlefield, and a collective gasp rippled through the crowd.
When the smoke finally began to clear, all eyes searched desperately for any sign of Zane. Surely no one, not even Misty's prodigy, could withstand such a devastating blast. But there he stood, unmoved, his silhouette emerging from the dissipating flames like a phoenix born of destruction itself.
[MUSIC: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
"The fire... What happened to the fire?" Mack stammered, his face twisting in disbelief.
The flames engulfing his stone men were anything but ordinary.
Even within the Celestial Inferno Array, the strength of celestial fire could vary wildly, and its true power depended entirely on the user's own level.
Mack had always been confident in his fire. Once, while out training, a meteorite had crashed to earth, carving out a massive crater. The otherworldly flame it left behind had burned in that pit for three days and three nights, and Mack had spent every moment of that time studying it, meditating on its very essence.
He knew how lucky he'd been. Not every warrior got the chance to observe a Meteor Flame up close. From that day on, it had become his ultimate weapon.
But Mack couldn't just summon the Meteor Flame at will. Only after setting up the inferno array could he convert his Vitality into this powerful fire and channel it through his illusions.
The stone men were illusions, yes, but the Meteor Flame was very, very real.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE]
And now, Zane stood right in the middle of it, completely unharmed and radiating an aura of immense power.
Mack's mind raced. "How is he safe from the Meteor Flame? Is he immune to fire? No, that's impossible. Even the legendary Son of Fire isn't completely immune. He could only hope to offset damage from something like a Meteor Flame once his own mastery reached an insane level."
[SFX: ROARING FLAMES]
"I don't believe you're not burning! Stone men, get him! Go!" Mack roared, gritting his teeth as the flaming figures surged toward Zane from all sides.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY HUM]
Usually, when attacked by fire, Zane's body would generate warm currents that absorbed the energy, lulling him into a peaceful, meditative state. But he noticed this Meteor Flame was barely having an effect. He glanced down at the dense golden runes coating his skin and just shook his head.
His body had been tempered by the Flame of the Evil Jace, then the Fire of the Earth's Core, and even the Demon's Holy Fire.
The Meteor Flame was powerful, sure, but its purity was nothing compared to the Earth's Core or the Demon's Holy Fire. Besides, his body had already reached the level of a Mid-Grade Spirit Weapon. This flame just wasn't strong enough to refine it any further, which was why his body wasn't absorbing it.
His body was rejecting the Meteor Flame because it was simply too weak. Instead of forming a golden whirlpool to draw the fire in, his skin had erupted with a shield of golden runes to ward off the heat.
Covered in tens of thousands of shimmering runes, Zane looked like a living golden statue. He faced the charging stone men without a hint of fear.
A sneer spread across his face as he watched them close in.
"Break!" Zane shouted.
Then he started punching.
[SFX: EXPLOSIVE PUNCHES]
"Boom! Boom! Boom!"
In the blink of an eye, Zane had shattered several more of the flaming constructs.
[SFX: CREATURES SWARMING]
The remaining stone men converged on Zane in a desperate wave of flaming fury. Mack's face contorted with rage and fear---his prized technique failing before his eyes. With sweat beading on his brow and his voice cracking with desperation, he unleashed his final gambit.
"You won't make a fool of me!" he shrieked, his fingers forming a complex array pattern. With a frantic, hate-filled roar that echoed throughout the arena, Mack triggered all his remaining constructs to detonate at the same time, sacrificing his carefully crafted weapons in one last attempt to salvage his pride.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
"Explode! Let's see you survive this!"
[AMBIENT: QUIET MEDITATION SPACE]
[MUSIC: CALM MYSTICAL]
Eliot sat perfectly still, regulating his breathing. After swallowing the Crimson Cloud Pill, he had to circulate it through his body with Vitality, guiding it through every one of his Channels to activate its effects.
[MUSIC: TENSE ANTICIPATION]
But even as he focused, he couldn't take his eyes off the battle ring.
Eliot had always been a calm person, no matter the situation. As the son of his father's secondary wife, he'd been mistreated since childhood, and that had forged a steely resolve in him. Through sheer effort and extraordinary talent, he had clawed his way up to become the top disciple of Misty Peak. And even though Misty Peak ranked last among the Thirty-Three Peaks, his personal power surpassed that of most disciples from all the other peaks combined.
But Eliot was never satisfied. He had seen what truly high-level warriors could do, and he knew his own achievements were nothing to brag about.
He had always dedicated every fiber of his being to his training, relentlessly climbing toward the peak of his practice. But today, Mack had handed him a crushing defeat. It wasn't just him; all the disciples from Misty Peak looked grim and dejected.
Except for Zane.
Everyone knew Eliot, the top disciple of Misty Peak. Most of them didn't even know who Zane was.
[MUSIC: SOMBER REFLECTION]
Shame washed over Eliot. He, the most powerful disciple of their peak, had been defeated so easily by Mack. He hadn't even been able to put up a real fight. The shame was a specter haunting him.
It felt like a heavy stone pressing down on his chest, making it hard to breathe.
If Eliot couldn't get past this, he'd create a mental demon for himself, a block that would keep him stuck at his current level forever.
He had to overcome it himself... or he had to rely on Zane to do it for him. If Zane could teach Mack a lesson on behalf of Misty Peak, Eliot knew the stone on his chest would finally lift.
With a tightness in his chest, he let out a whisper. "Zane, you have to win."
