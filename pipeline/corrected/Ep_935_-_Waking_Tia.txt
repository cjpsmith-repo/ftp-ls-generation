Ep 935 - Waking Tia

Word count: 957

[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]

[MUSIC: SOMBER STRINGS]

Even with her Raw Vitality drained, Tia’s appearance hadn’t changed. But anyone near her could feel it—the life force in her body was just a flicker, barely enough to keep her asleep.

[MUSIC: HOPEFUL PIANO]

As Raw Vitality from the life crystal seeped into her, Tia’s worn, pale face began to transform. The deathly pallor faded, replaced by a warm flush in her cheeks. It was as if she was regaining her strength right before their eyes.

Cora and Dorothea felt it, too—the Raw Vitality in Tia’s body surging. It was like watching a withered tree sprout new buds, its roots digging deep, filling the room with a sense of renewed life and hope.

[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]

Tia had absorbed every last drop of Raw Vitality from the life crystal. Zane could only stare, his stomach twisting with anxiety.

“Why isn’t she waking up?” he murmured, his voice tight.

His expression darkened. Saving Tia was the whole reason he’d gone to the Divine Kingdom Continent. The life crystal had been his only hope. She'd absorbed all its energy and looked a hundred times better, so why was she still unconscious?

“Zane…” Cora’s heart ached for him, and she wanted to offer some comfort, but Zane was frozen, like a statue carved from stone.

A flicker of surprise crossed Dorothea’s face, her lips parting slightly. She knew that after the Life and Death Wind had drained Tia, her body had triggered a self-preservation response, forcing her into a deep sleep. It was her body’s only way of conserving what little life force she had left.

[SFX: MAGICAL PULSE]

The flower in Dorothea's right eye spun gently, sending an invisible pulse through the room. A slow smile spread across her face. Her unique talent stemmed from that flower, making her far more sensitive to souls than other cultivators of her level. In that instant, she realized Tia was already awake.

Zane sure knows how to pick 'em, Dorothea thought to herself. 

[SFX: TELEPATHIC WHISPER]

Then, using her Vitality, she sent a thought directly into Zane’s mind. “She's awake.”

“What?” Zane's heart hammered against his ribs. 

When he looked closer and saw Tia, eyes still shut, pretending to be asleep, a mix of relief and annoyance washed over him.

He turned away from her and waved a hand. “She's still not awake. Let's give her some space — we'll wait outside.”

Cora blinked at him, confused by the sudden shift. Dorothea gave Cora a light pat on the shoulder.

Cora flinched as if she'd been stung. She shot Dorothea a fierce glare, stepped sharply away, and kept her distance. Dorothea just smiled.

The three of them filed out of the courtyard.

[AMBIENT: GARDEN]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

A moment after they left, Tia's fingers stirred. She rose slowly, her ice-blue dress settling around her, the ribbon trailing as she moved. She smiled to herself and walked carefully toward the palace.

She never extended her perception. She had no idea Zane was waiting behind a pillar just ahead.

[SFX: SUDDEN GRAB]

[MUSIC: ROMANTIC SURGE]

He stepped out and caught her — arms locked around her like iron — and she let out a startled cry. Before she could react, he had already kissed her.

Tia was a head shorter than him. He lifted her slightly; her feet barely grazed the ground. She stretched up on her toes, the tips of her feet just touching the cold stone, and stayed there.

[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC STRINGS]

Inside the palace, Cora and Dorothea watched in silence.

Cora had told herself, over and over, that she'd made peace with it. That Tia was like an elder sister. That she understood her place. She had stayed in this palace for six months, caring for a woman she had no obligation to care for, and she had convinced herself that her heart was settled.

Watching now, she realized how thin that convincing had always been.

Dorothea glanced at her. Through the flower in her right eye, Cora's soul was an open book — every ripple of it visible. She smiled gently. “Men,” she said. “Not a single one of them is any good, is he?”

Cora looked at her. Despite everything, despite the strangeness of this woman and the way she looked at people, Cora heard herself answer: “Not a single one. Zane is the exception.”

Dorothea laughed softly. She looked at Cora again with that same odd, appraising expression. “Well then. If that's how you feel, why not come with me instead? I could use a wife.”

Cora's eyes went wide. Then she snapped, “Crazy!”

[MUSIC: ROMANTIC PIANO]

[AMBIENT: QUIET ROOM]

Zane and Tia held each other, lost in a long, deep kiss before finally pulling apart. She gazed up at him, just inches away. 

A sweet smile touched her lips, only to vanish a moment later. “Maddox…” she murmured.

After being hit by the Life and Death Wind, Tia had fallen into a deep slumber. She had no idea how long she’d been out, or anything about the battle with the Corrupted Divine Kingdom. Even with Zane standing right in front of her, safe and sound, she couldn't stop the worry from creeping in.

[MUSIC: DARK REVELATION]

 “He's dead. I killed him,” Zane said immediately.

Tia nodded. With Zane standing here, Maddox’s chances of survival had been slim. Still, she couldn't wrap her head around how Zane had actually managed to pull it off.

Zane gave her the short version of what happened after she fell unconscious. He mostly glossed over his journey to the Divine Kingdom Continent for the Ancient Heartstones, though.

[MUSIC: GENTLE EMOTIONAL PIANO]

[AMBIENT: SOFT ROOM TONE]

Even though Zane was downplaying how hard the trip was, Tia knew it must have been brutal. She leaned her head against his chest and asked him to tell her everything. He had no choice but to tell her the truth. 

As he spoke, she traced idle circles on his chest with her fingertips, her mind drifting along with his story.
