Ep 1228 - The Woman In The Snow

Word count: 1077

[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR COMBAT ARENA]
The blue-robed warrior’s jaw went slack, his eyes wide at the scene unfolding before him.
[SFX: METALLIC WHOOSH]
[SFX: THREE SWORDS MATERIALIZING]
He felt the explosive power building behind him and instinctively threw up a hand in a protective gesture as three swords shot out of his Storage Ring. One was gold, one was silver, and the last was bronze, their blades etched with elegant lines that made them shimmer in the light.
The black sword, a gift from the City Lord—the young heir of House Kincaid—was the one Zane had already twisted and broken. These three metallic swords, however, were this warrior’s true weapons. He’d carried them for years, and they were bonded to his very soul.
[SFX: WIND GUST]
[SFX: SWORDS SPINNING RAPIDLY]
A sharp gust of wind swirled as the three swords flew, humming in resonance with one another before their blades began to spin at a dizzying speed. As they whipped around the blue-robed warrior, they merged into a single, tri-colored tornado of steel.
“Tri-Sword Tempest!”
[SFX: TORNADO ROARING]
The tornado howled, sounding like some great beast as it tore through the air, aimed straight at Zane.
[SFX: HEAVY FOOTSTEPS CHARGING]
But Zane didn’t hesitate. He just charged forward, his body a living spear.
[SFX: METAL STRIKING METAL REPEATEDLY]
A deafening, high-pitched ringing of metal on metal echoed as the three swords, hidden within the blur of the tornado, hammered against his body.
The attack looked terrifying, but Zane didn’t need any armor. His own body was as tough as a low-grade Divine Weapon, so even sharp swords couldn’t do more than glance off him. 
The only thing that really annoyed him was his clothes. All the fighting had shredded his tunic, and it wasn’t the first time. It was getting expensive, constantly having to buy new clothes.
[SFX: WHOOSHING WIND]
[SFX: RAPID MOVEMENT]
He powered through the tri-colored tornado and shot straight toward the blue-robed warrior, his hands lunging out like lightning.
[SFX: FABRIC FLAPPING]
[SFX: ORB CRUSHING]
Seeing there was no escape, the warrior flipped backward like a kite caught in an updraft. He flicked a golden orb into his hand and, without a second thought, crushed it.
“You think you can have my Illusion Points? In your dreams!” he screamed, his voice cracking. “I’ve been collecting these for the City Lord! Hahaha!”
Zane just felt a flash of confusion. All he had to do was defeat this guy and take half his points. Why was he acting like that was impossible?
[SFX: ENERGY PULSE EXPANDING]
His thoughts were cut short as the crushed golden orb unleashed a violent pulse of energy, spreading out in every direction.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
Zane knew, with a sinking feeling, that this was bad news. 
[SFX: SUPERSONIC RETREAT]
He immediately shot backward, moving so fast that he put a hundred feet between them in a single instant.
[SFX: ORB SHATTERING]
[SFX: CRACKLING RED LIGHTNING]
If Zane had been caught in that hundred-foot radius, he’d have lost half his own points. Even a self-inflicted death cost half your points under the Illusion Battlefield’s rules — that was why the warrior had chosen to end himself rather than let Zane land the kill.
[SFX: LIGHT BURSTING]
A massive burst of pale light bloomed where the warrior died, at least two hundred thousand points. But since Zane hadn’t struck the killing blow, the light didn’t come to him.
[SFX: LIGHT STREAMING AWAY]
It streamed straight toward the main city instead.
Zane stared after it, stunned. Could the City Lord actually bend the rules of the Illusion Battlefield itself?
Up on the wall, the guards just watched him with mocking smiles. The warriors at the gate — all City Lord loyalists — offered nothing.
If one subordinate could farm points like this, there had to be more. Zane shot off along the city wall, hunting for his next target.
[SFX: WHOOSHING DEPARTURE]
The trail of pale light was easy enough to follow. Zane tracked it deep into the city, until it curled into a courtyard tucked into the southwest corner.
[AMBIENT: SNOW FALLING IN SILENCE]
The change hit him before he even landed. Everywhere else in the city, warriors fought and bled for points under a punishing sun. Here, snow drifted down in fat, weightless flakes, blanketing the courtyard in white. It wasn’t natural — the cold radiating off the ground was sharp enough that other warriors gave the place a wide berth without knowing why.
In the corner of the courtyard, a woman in a purple veil sat alone, undisturbed by the chaos beyond the walls. A small silk-bound book rested in her hands — no illustrations, just dense rows of text. A novel.
Zane stared. Whatever was happening with his missing points, it led here — to her.
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
[AMBIENT: QUIET MONASTERY COURTYARD]
Things like that—suicidal last-ditch efforts—weren’t popular among the warriors. Novels, on the other hand, were. And for some reason, it was all Zane’s fault.
[SFX: ROARING TOKEN ACTIVATION]
[MUSIC: GENTLE STORYTELLING MELODY]
It had started when he’d used his Roaring Token to tell stories to the mysterious woman he’d heard singing. Though plenty of warriors were annoyed by the interruption, some couldn’t help but listen in, and they got hooked.
Zane still had to focus on his training, so he didn’t have much time for storytelling. Besides, he only ever did it for her. Other warriors would gather to listen whenever she emerged from her seclusion, but the moment she was gone again, Zane fell silent.
The universe was full of warriors, but it was even fuller of regular people, and they tended to appreciate culture a little more than the fighters did. 
[SFX: AETHER CRYSTAL CHIME]
Soon, the warriors discovered that a single Aether crystal could buy you countless novels, or even hire hundreds of storytellers to narrate them for you, day in and day out. Over the past few years, it had become the trendiest thing you could do.
Because of this, the status of certain regular people rose dramatically. 
[AMBIENT: BUSTLING CITY MARKETPLACE]
In a small city governed by a sacred sect of the Thoughtless Minds, for example, one storyteller became a local celebrity.
[SFX: CROWD APPLAUSE]
All the stories he told were his own creations, and he was such a master that he could hold an audience captive no matter his mood. A warrior from the sect took a liking to him and introduced him to influential people, and his status just kept climbing. Rumor had it that two sects even ended up fighting over him.
Eventually, a warrior gave the storyteller a pill that extended his life by a few hundred years—an unheard-of privilege for a regular person, something not even the kings of some small countries ever received.
But in the end, the rising status of storytellers and scholars all came back to one person: Zane. 
If he hadn’t used his Roaring Token to tell stories to a mysterious singer, catching the attention of other warriors, the whole trend never would have started.
