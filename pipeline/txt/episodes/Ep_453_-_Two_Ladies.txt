Ep 453 - Two Ladies

Word count: 1114

[MUSIC: DARK SEDUCTION]

Zane looked up, stunned by what he saw.

A young woman materialized before him, floating through the air with an otherworldly grace. She reclined in the emptiness as though sprawled across an invisible chaise. Her emerald eyes blazed with an intensity that commanded attention—Zane found himself utterly transfixed, unable to tear his gaze away. When those eyes locked onto his, they narrowed with unmistakable invitation, a silent dare wrapped in seduction.

Heat flooded Zane's face. His pulse quickened as an overwhelming compulsion seized him—a desperate need to please her, to obey whatever command might fall from her lips. The sensation terrified him even as it enthralled him.

"Wow, her glamour is scary powerful," he muttered through clenched teeth, fighting against the invisible chains wrapping around his will. His brow furrowed in concentration as he felt something tremble in the depths of his soul—a warning, perhaps, or a defense mechanism awakening. Then, as suddenly as it had come, the compulsion shattered.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

The fog suffocating his thoughts cleared like morning mist under harsh sunlight. The world snapped back into focus. Zane drew a shuddering breath, his hands trembling slightly as awareness returned.

The woman was Lady Darkmere, the leader of Bloodwood Cliff. A master of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm, her glamour was her specialty — a seductive technique honed over decades that could ensnare the will of even seasoned warriors.

"What?" Lady Darkmere's expression contorted, confusion replacing her confident smirk. She leaned forward slightly, studying him with newfound wariness. Her glamour had never failed her—especially not with young men barely out of boyhood. The technique was her specialty, honed over decades. She remained utterly baffled by what had just occurred.

He looked seventeen, maybe eighteen at most—that awkward age where boys were just turning into men, totally inexperienced. They were usually the easiest to influence, and her glamour should have been irresistible. But he’d only been caught for a few seconds before breaking the spell. How was that possible?

Of course, she had no way of knowing Zane’s soul was at the Fighting Soul level. Fooling a soul that powerful with mere glamour was next to impossible. She gave him a condescending glare. “What exactly do you think you're doing here?”

“What I’m doing is none of your business!” Zane snapped, his face set in a grimace.

“None of my business?” she spat, her voice laced with venom. “The man you just injured was a disciple of Bloodwood Cliff! Do you really think you can just walk out of here in one piece? Are you out of your mind?”

[MUSIC: RISING TENSION]

A wicked gleam lit her eyes, and her voice dropped to a sultry purr. “If you like dreaming so much, why not come dream with me? When I'm done with you, all you’ll feel is total, blissful relaxation.”

[SFX: SEDUCTIVE LAUGHTER]

Lady Darkmere’s suggestive laughter echoed around them.

Zane saw right through it. Her glamour was just a trap to drain a man’s Vitality, and he wasn’t falling for it. He knew a “dream” with her would only leave a corpse behind.

[MUSIC: HYPNOTIC STRINGS]

Her glamour was intoxicating, and she could even weave it into her laughter. As her sweet, alluring voice filled the air, the other men nearby, Dominic included, felt their blood heat. The effect was even stronger on the lower-level fighters within earshot. Their faces flushed and their eyes glazed over, mouths hanging slack as they stared, completely dazed—like zombies mesmerized by her beauty.

[SFX: ANGRY SHOUT]

Zane met Lady Darkmere's gaze and smiled. "They say one smile can topple a city, another can topple a kingdom. You're beautiful, I'll grant you that, but I'm not interested in old women."

[SFX: ENERGY SURGE]

[MUSIC: GROWING DANGER]

Her seductive expression froze. Waves of killing intent erupted from her body as she transformed from butterfly to scorpion.

"A junior in the Adept Realm dares reject me?" she hissed. "You're begging for death!"

Dominic's expression changed drastically. How could they face a Spirit-Tribulation Realm master?

Zane had secretly activated his talisman—Tia should be rushing over. If necessary, he'd retreat to the Dragon Soar Arena and lure Lady Darkmere into the True Dragon Territory, where she'd face imprisonment for a thousand years.

An old man in a black mask appeared in midair — Raven, the leader of the Black Mountain Order. His sect, like Bloodwood Cliff, was a fourth-grade power in the Heartlands.

"Lady Darkmere, you can kill this boy after I get answers!" Raven demanded.

Her face darkened. "Raven, you want to interfere again?"

"He belongs to whoever catches him." He turned to Zane. "Boy, come with me and reveal the Dragon Soar Arena's secret. I'll grant you a painless death. Otherwise, you'll experience agony beyond imagination! You'll regret it for the rest of your miserable life!"

Dominic's expression grew uglier. Before he could retrieve his message talisman, Zane said, "I've already sent the message."

A clear, cold voice rang out. "Regret? Do you think it's so easy to make a Cloud Hall disciple regret?"

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

Translucent snowflakes fell from the sky. Tia descended beside the Dragon Soar Arena, her eyes filled with killing intent as she stared at Raven and Lady Darkmere.

During these three months, Tia's cultivation had recovered. She'd broken through from the  Soul Realm to the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.

"Tia, do you really think you can intimidate me?" Raven shouted. "You're still no match for me."

Tia sneered.

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

“True, I haven’t mastered the Goddess Path yet, so my power hasn’t fully returned. You might not be afraid of me, but…” she paused, letting the silence hang, “…are you afraid of my master?”

At the mention of her master, the color drained from Raven's and Lady Darkmere's faces.

Lady Darkmere, Raven, and Tia were all leaders of fourth-grade sects. But while the Black Mountain Order and Bloodwood Cliff were ordinary fourth-grade sects, the Cloud Hall was in a league of its own—an elite, top-tier powerhouse.

The gap between an elite sect and an ordinary one was massive. The Cloud Hall’s power didn't come from Tia, but from her master. Though her master had gone into seclusion and stepped down from her leadership role, leaving the sect’s daily affairs to Tia, her shadow loomed large.

Once Tia mastered the Goddess Path, she would elevate the Cloud Hall to an even higher level, making it the second fifth-grade sect in all the Heartlands.

So while Lady Darkmere and Raven weren't afraid of Tia, they wouldn’t dare cross her master.

[SFX: MARCHING FOOTSTEPS]

“Even if your master were here, you can’t take Zane,” a new voice cut in. A group was approaching them, their boots caked with dust. It was clear they’d traveled a long way on foot. “He’s part of a major event involving the Dragon Soar Arena. He has no choice but to share its secrets with us!”

The men were from the Ethereal Spirit Order.

As Tia watched them approach, her brow furrowed in annoyance. “Oh great, it's you.”
