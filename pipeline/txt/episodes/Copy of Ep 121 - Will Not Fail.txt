Ep 121 - Will Not Fail
Word Count: 1335
Demons, humans, ogres—fundamentally, they shared more similarities than differences. Each possessed sentience and independent thought, experiencing the same spectrum of emotions: love, fear, sadness. These universal feelings transcended species and origin.
[AMBIENT: TENSE BATTLEFIELD]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC COMBAT]
 Blood-soaked and battered, Zane maintained his position with unwavering determination. His ragged breathing punctuated the tense silence, yet his eyes burned with resolute defiance.
The Demon Commanders froze, visibly shaken by the human's extraordinary willpower. For a breathtaking moment, not one of these fearsome beings dared advance, their hesitation betraying a flicker of something almost resembling respect.
 "Zane... Zane, forget about me," Ingrid whispered, her voice breaking as tears welled in her emerald eyes. She clutched her trembling hands together, the reality of their situation crushing down upon her. "If you can get away, just go!"
Though not imposing in stature, Zane's silhouette against the dim light transformed him into something larger than life. Ingrid watched, heart pounding, as his bloodied figure stood between her and certain death. In that profound moment, his image as a hero etched itself indelibly into her consciousness—a memory she knew would remain with her until her final breath.
Hearing her plea, Zane glanced back at her and managed a faint smile. In a life-or-death moment like this, it was somehow incredibly comforting. He tightened his grip on the Shattered Dagger, his clothes soaked in blood, and faced the Demon Commanders without so much as a flinch.
[SFX: DEMONIC LANGUAGE]
 Then, a few words spoken in the harsh Infernal tongue cut through the air. It was the demon general holding Ingrid hostage. 
The second the words left its mouth, the Demon Commanders surrounding Zane braced themselves, ready to charge as one.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY CRACKLING]
 A streak of purple-black Nether Essence coiled up Zane's arm. He was about to unleash every last drop he had. He held the dagger to guard his chest, his other hand balled into a fist that crackled with swirling energy.
Though outnumbered and outmatched, Zane stood defiant. His stance conveyed a silent challenge to his adversaries: approach if you dare face mortality. A cold determination settled in his eyes, masking the pain that already wracked his battered body.
[SFX: COMBAT MOVEMENT]
 When the first demon general lunged forward, Zane executed a calculated step backward, drawing his opponent into his deadly game. Simultaneously, another fiend rushed from the flank, its gleaming bone spur aimed directly at his chest—a strike meant to impale and kill instantly.
[SFX: BONE PIERCING FLESH]
[SFX: GRUNTS OF PAIN]
 Rather than evade the attack, Zane made a decision that defied all survival instincts. He thrust himself forward, deliberately allowing the jagged bone to pierce his flesh and sink deep into his torso. A searing agony exploded through his body, yet his face betrayed nothing. With inconceivable fortitude that shocked even the battle-hardened demons, he forced his bleeding form further along the weapon, sliding down the length of the spur with grim resolve. Each excruciating inch brought him closer to his target, his life's essence spilling freely, but his purpose unwavering.
As the blade tore through him, he shoved himself forward along its length, closing the distance to the demon general. The part of the bone spur sticking out of Zane’s back had gone from dull bronze to a slick, sickening scarlet, dripping his blood onto the forest floor.
Seeing Zane not only take the hit but drive himself deeper onto the weapon, panic flared in the demon general's eyes. It tried to snap the bone spur, to get away.
But it was too late.
[SFX: WET TEARING SOUND]
 A wet, tearing sound echoed in the clearing.
[SFX: MAGIC ENERGY SURGE]
[SFX: DEMONIC DEATH WAIL]
 Zane slammed his fist into the demon general's chest, injecting wisps of Nether Essence into its body. Without a second glance, he turned to face the next one. He knew that once the Nether Essence was inside, it was a death sentence.
The first one died.
[SFX: BONE SPUR IMPACT]
[SFX: HOWL OF PAIN]
 Zane had barely pivoted when another bone spur tore through his flank, sending crimson rivulets cascading down his already blood-soaked garments. Rather than retreat from the excruciating pain, he embraced it, transforming agony into resolve. With a primal growl escaping his clenched teeth, he executed the same lethal maneuver—lunging forward despite his gushing wound, allowing momentum to drive him deeper onto the demonic appendage. The world narrowed to a tunnel of determination as he slammed his fist into the second demon general's chest cavity, feeling the creature's alien anatomy yield beneath his knuckles.
"Die," he whispered as Nether Essence flowed from his veins into the monster's core.
[SFX: MAGICAL TRANSFORMATION]
[SFX: THUNDEROUS BODY COLLAPSE]
 The second demon's eyes widened in disbelief before the light within them extinguished forever, its massive frame collapsing with a thunderous impact that shook the forest floor.
[SFX: STAGGERING FOOTSTEPS]
[SFX: LETHAL STRIKE]
Without pausing to savor the victory, Zane staggered toward the third general, his body a tapestry of wounds, each step leaving crimson footprints in his wake. The third demon hesitated, witnessing the inexplicable determination of this blood-drenched apprentice who refused to fall. That moment of hesitation proved fatal as Zane closed the distance and delivered the same devastating strike to the third commander.
The fourth.
The fifth.
He couldn't hear anything anymore. One belief kept him upright: he would not quit. Not until every drop of Nether Essence was gone, every scrap of strength spent, every drop of blood dried up.
[SFX: BONE SPUR IMPACTS — RAPID, OVERLAPPING]
By the fifth kill, bone spurs jutted from him in every direction—arms, sides, torso, every vital area. He looked like a hedgehog. He stood anyway.

"Zane, Zane," Ingrid sobbed, tears blurring her vision as she watched him tear himself apart for her.
The last General held Ingrid with one hand, bone spur extended from the other, muscles slowly contracting. When it spoke, the words came in human tongue.
"I admire your willpower. But we are not the same kind. Today… you die."
[SFX: VITALITY CRACKLE]
Ingrid fired a volley of red Vitality needles at it—everything she had. The General blocked with one arm and shoved her into a spruce trunk. 
More Demon Generals stepped out of the forest, Demon Soldiers fanning out behind them.
"I'm sorry," she whispered to Zane. "I can't help you."
The General raised its spur to fire—and silver flashed in the sky.
[SFX: METALLIC SPINNING — BLADES FINDING TRUE NORTH]
[SFX: SWORD RAIN — SILVER BLADES WHISTLING DOWN]
Dozens of small silver swords materialized overhead, each spinning once to find its mark, then dropping like rain across the entire clearing and tree line. The demons ran. There was nowhere to go. When the last blade struck earth, it dissolved into wisps of Vitality and vanished. Sky Forest went silent.
Every demon was dead.
[SFX: SUDDEN APPEARANCE]
 Suddenly, a man appeared beside her. He was an expert at Forging Soul Blades, but he was a total mess, dressed so sloppily that he’d earned the nickname, the Messy Sword Genius.
"Ingrid! Are you okay?" he asked, his voice laced with concern. "I got your message and came as fast as I could!"
 Lennie stood on a small Steel Longsword, descending slowly toward her. He breathed a sigh of relief when he saw she was unharmed. The moment the demon had grabbed Ingrid, she'd activated her Dispatch Rider—a device House Solomon designed for emergencies. Once triggered, it instantly alerted the nearest family members.
But the system had a major flaw: help, no matter how fast, almost never arrived in time.
Lennie was supposed to be fighting demons in The Crucible near Silverspire for House Solomon, but a major event had pulled him from the front lines. It was sheer luck that he happened to be near Sky Forest when Ingrid's signal went out. It seemed fate was on her side today.
"I'm okay, Uncle Lennie! I'm fine! Save him! Please, save Zane!" Ingrid cried, a surge of hope in her voice.
[SFX: GASP OF SHOCK]
 Lennie turned, and his jaw dropped. He saw Zane, drenched in blood, standing over the bodies of several Demon Commanders.
"He's merely a youth," Lennie thought, astonished. "Undoubtedly a Skyreach Sect apprentice participating in the trials alongside Ingrid. Yet how could this mere boy vanquish an entire contingent of Demon Commanders single-handedly? His white robe—if memory serves—marks him as nothing more than a Skyreach pledge!"
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC REVELATION]
 Lennie inhaled sharply, his astonishment transforming into utter disbelief. His eyes widened to their limits as he detected the faintest trace of Vitality emanating from the bloodied figure. The revelation struck him like a physical blow. Zane hadn't even achieved Nature creature status. The boy who had slaughtered multiple demon commanders was merely a High-level candidate—a fact that defied everything Lennie understood about power and cultivation.
