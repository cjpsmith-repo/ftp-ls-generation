[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
Tucked behind their rock, Cora and Tia had a clear view of the reunion — Zane’s arms around Isla, the blush creeping up her neck, the way she didn’t actually pull free.
Tia had seen enough. “He’s got so many women! He is not a good guy,” she seethed.
For some reason, the sight of the normally composed girl pouting made Cora smile. Maybe she thought it was cute. Whatever the reason, Tia’s jealousy seemed to take the edge off her own bitterness. “But Zane knew her first,” she teased gently.
“I don’t care! He’s a player, end of story!” Tia huffed, her face flushing.
Cora clicked her tongue. “If Zane were really a bad guy, he would’ve just bailed on us. But he’s a man of his word. He kept his promise.”
Tia stubbornly shook her head, tears welling in her eyes. “No! He’s just not a good person!” she cried.
Cora sighed, giving in, and reached out a sleeved arm toward her.
Tia flinched. “What are you doing?”
Ignoring the question, Cora used her sleeve to gently wipe the tears from Tia’s cheeks. “He might not be a ‘good guy,’ but he’s still our husband,” she reminded her softly.
[MUSIC: REFLECTIVE STRINGS]
The words stopped Tia’s crying cold. Cora was right. Whatever she thought of Zane, at the end of the day, he was her husband, and she had to stick with him. She knew she could get possessive, but she’d always known someone like Zane could never belong to just one person. It was a hard pill to swallow, but it was time she accepted it.
Deep down, Cora felt the bitterness too. But she carried something Tia didn’t — a quiet certainty that no matter how many women came and went, she would be the one still standing beside him in the end. That belief made it easier to breathe.
[AMBIENT: GENTLE WATER SOUNDS]
[MUSIC: CONTEMPLATIVE STRINGS]
Meanwhile, floating a little ways off, Sir Kenneth couldn’t help but feel a pang of jealousy as he looked at Tia.
Zane is a legend, he thought.
Tia was the leader of the biggest sect in The Heartlands. And while Kenneth didn’t know the woman beside her, he could tell from her bearing that she came from a powerful family. Zane’s wives were true women of status.
He just had that huge wedding with Tia not too long ago, and now he’s already with Isla, Kenneth thought, admiring the man. Still, he stole another glance at Tia. I hope she’s not taking it too hard.
[MUSIC: BITTERSWEET STRINGS]
On the other side of the peak, Cora watched Tia’s mood settle and took her hand. Tia yanked it back. “What are you doing?”
“Come on. Time to say hello,” Cora said.
“No.”
“You’ll have to meet her eventually. You can’t avoid it forever.”
Tia said nothing. After a long pause, she let out a breath and fell into step behind Cora.
They flew down toward the mountainside together. Zane spotted them coming, and his expression shifted — somewhere between braced and helpless. He had no idea how to handle this.
[SFX: SOFT FOOTSTEPS — LANDING]
Cora greeted Isla first, graceful and easy, as if there were nothing unusual about the situation at all. Tia hung back at her side, saying nothing. The three women looked at one another. Whatever passed between them in that moment was impossible to name — but the mountainside didn’t catch fire, and Zane quietly counted that as a win.
[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]
Zane took his time comforting Isla. Eventually, the conversation turned to her father.
Kane Novak had once been a steward of Cloud Hall before disappearing on a mission. There had been no word of him since. Until now.
Zane’s mind moved quietly. The Memory of the World’s Will held a record of every living being in the world, past and present. He reached into it, searching for Kane Novak.
He found the answer almost instantly. His expression went still.
“Your father,” he said carefully, “died in a relic somewhere in the Heartlands.”
Isla’s eyes flickered. She had held onto a thread of hope — but after so many years of silence, some part of her had already known. She didn’t ask how Zane knew. She just believed him.
“I’ll have Cloud Hall send someone to bring his remains home,” Zane said.
Isla nodded. She didn’t speak. After a moment, she quietly put some distance between herself and Zane — more composed than she wanted to feel, and more alone than she let on.
[AMBIENT: OUTDOOR WIND]
[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS THEME]
A few days later, Zane left House Lowell and headed west.
The women were confused. With his business done, they had expected to rush back to The Heartlands. Why were they going west?
Of course, Zane refused to say, preferring to be mysterious about it. He just told them he was taking them to a secret place.
[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS ETHEREAL]
This “secret place” turned out to be Dragon Valley in the Cloudy Mountains, the location of The Elysian Keep—a place Zane had entered once before.
The place was bizarre. Besides Zane, the only other person who had ever set foot there was Vesper.
But even with all his power at the Spirit Supreme Realm, Vesper couldn’t control The Elysian Keep. He’d only managed to snag one more token than Zane. His token, marked with the character for “thunder,” he had given to Zane, who already had the “wind” token.
According to Vesper, the Elysian Keep had no name because no one in the entire hundred thousand supreme worlds of the universe was worthy of giving it one.
Zane figured Vesper was probably exaggerating, but he knew he absolutely had to explore the Keep before ascending to the upper world. He didn’t know if he could unlock its secrets even after reaching the Life-and-Death Realm, but he had to try.
“So this is where you wanted to take us?” Tia asked, staring out at the empty valley.
“Follow me,” he said, walking toward a random-looking corner of the valley.
[SFX: MAGICAL WHOOSH]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUM]
He stepped between two bamboo stalks, and with a flash of green light, he simply vanished.
