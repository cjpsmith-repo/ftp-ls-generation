Ep 119 - The Infernal Six
Word Count: 1208
[AMBIENT: FOREST AMBIENCE]
[SFX: HIGH SPEED RUNNING]
[MUSIC: TENSE ACTION]
A shadowy figure tore through the Sky Forest.
Zane had pushed his speed to the absolute max, channeling the power of the dragon scales into his legs and launching himself forward like a shot.
Each footfall pounded the forest floor with such tremendous force that it tore through the soft earth, leaving a trail of deep craters resembling the aftermath of a devastating bombing raid. The raw power behind his movements scarred the landscape in his wake.
[SFX: RUSHING WIND]
He hurtled through the forest at breathtaking velocity, his feet barely skimming the ground as he propelled himself forward. The air around him shrieked in protest, creating a high-pitched wail as he expertly navigated between the ancient trees. Zane's face contorted with determination, his jaw clenched tight against the stinging wind that threatened to blind him.
[SFX: WOOD SPLINTERING]
At such blistering speed, complete avoidance proved impossible. His shoulder occasionally slammed into massive tree trunks, splintering wood and snapping thick branches that would have stopped an ordinary man cold.
The impacts sent jolts of pain through his body, but Zane refused to slow down. His sister's face flashed in his mind each time he considered pausing, even for a heartbeat. Desperation fueled him as much as his extraordinary abilities.
The chase stretched into a maddening exercise in futility. With every ounce of strength Zane poured into accelerating, the demon general somehow matched his pace with infuriating precision. The distance between them remained cruelly constant, as though some invisible force maintained their separation.
[MUSIC: DESPERATE PURSUIT]
Frustration burned in Zane's chest, threatening to overwhelm his focus as he pushed his already extraordinary limits. His lungs burned and muscles screamed, yet the demon remained tantalizingly out of reach, like a nightmare where the pursued always stays just beyond grasp.
When this started, he could at least make out Ingrid in the demon's arms. Now, all he could see was a fleeting silhouette against the trees.
[SFX: DAGGER WHISTLING]
Zane threw the Shattered Dagger. The General fired a bone spur to block it — the dagger cut it in half and kept going. The General rolled to dodge, losing speed. Zane closed the gap and drove both palms into the earth with Corrupt Vitality. A hill buckled up, then collapsed into a sinkhole. The General scrambled clear and ran.
He recalled the dagger and kept pursuing. The General started knocking spruce trunks into the dagger's path each time he threw it, throwing off his aim. The deadlock held.
Both of them possessed endurance beyond any mortal limit; at a normal pace, they could run for months without rest. But this was no casual jog. This was an all-out sprint at a speed no ordinary person could even comprehend.
After what felt like hours, the demon general finally started to flag. Even a being known for its stamina couldn't maintain that inhuman pace forever.
[SFX: ENERGY PULSE]
Zane, on the other hand, felt a fresh surge of energy. His true strength came from the Cobalt Dragon Scale embedded in his mind. Each time his stamina threatened to give out, the scale would pulse with a green light, completely washing away his fatigue. But he knew it wasn't infinite. The light was growing dimmer with each use; eventually, the Dragon Scale's power would run out.
*He’s slowing down,* Zane realized, a grim sense of hope rising in him. *He can’t keep this up.* Instead of easing up, he pushed even harder, determined to close the gap and save Ingrid.
Meanwhile, the demon general was wrestling with a growing sense of dread as the human closed in.
Demons were naturally stronger, tougher, and had more stamina than any human. The general had underestimated his pursuer, assuming the mortal would tire and fall behind. At first, he had. The demon had nearly lost him. But then, out of nowhere, the human had found a second wind, closing the distance at an impossible speed. The general shouldn't have been surprised, though. This was the same human who had killed one of his fellow generals—a feat no ordinary person could ever hope to accomplish. Clearly, there was more to this man than met the eye.
[SFX: SUDDEN STOP]
[AMBIENT: FOREST CLEARING]
The chase ended abruptly as Zane burst into a clearing and skidded to a halt. He was facing The Infernal Six. His expression turned grim. This was no joke. He could take on one demon general, maybe even two if he pushed it. But three or more? His only option would be to run for his life. And now, surrounded by all six, there was nowhere to go.
[SFX: DEMONIC LAUGHTER]
The demon general holding Ingrid let out a low, grating laugh, its mangled face twisting into a look of pure, triumphant glee.
The other Demon Commanders joined in, their laughter a grotesque chorus that echoed through the trees. Zane was completely surrounded. He’d been in enough life-or-death situations to know that panic was a death sentence. The only way out was to stay calm and think. He had to save Ingrid, no matter the cost. If he died here, trying to save her, he could live—or rather, die—with that.
[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE THEME]
With a sharp, deliberate motion, Zane hurled his Shattered Dagger toward the ground.
[SFX: BLADE THUDDING INTO SOIL]
The weapon plunged into the soil, burying itself deep with a dull thud that seemed to resonate through the clearing. Slowly raising his gaze to meet the lead demon general's eyes, Zane allowed a sly, calculating smile to spread across his face—a predator's expression rather than prey's.
The demon general's triumphant laughter withered in its throat, replaced by an unsettling silence. Confusion flickered across its grotesque features as it studied the human's unexpected confidence.
"What is he doing?" the creature growled, its previous certainty evaporating like morning mist.
Bewilderment rippled through the demonic ranks. This human stood surrounded, apparently disarmed, and mere heartbeats from annihilation—yet he smiled as though holding all advantages. The general's clawed hands tightened around Ingrid's limp form as doubt crept into its mind. Had this mortal just voluntarily discarded his only weapon? The creature's red eyes narrowed, searching for the trap, the hidden strategy behind such an incomprehensible action.
"The boy has lost his mind with fear," whispered one general, though uncertainty tinged its rasping voice.
"Heh. Pull whatever tricks you want, human. It won't change your fate," the demon sneered, its tongue flicking out to lick its upper lip. "Your flesh looks so… fresh."
[SFX: DEMONIC GROWLS]
The general flicked his wrist, signaling the other commanders to advance for the kill, their monstrous forms converging like shadows around prey.
Zane dismissed the demon's hollow provocations, finding them unworthy of acknowledgment. Rather than waste breath on creatures beyond reason, he extended his arms outward and set his body into motion, rotating with deliberate precision.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
[SFX: BLADE VIBRATING]
As his form whirled faster, the broken blade embedded in the earth responded—first with a subtle tremor, then with escalating vibration, until it too began to spin violently.
[SFX: SOIL ERUPTING]
The soil around it churned and erupted, particles rising in an expanding spiral that matched Zane's increasingly frenetic movements.
