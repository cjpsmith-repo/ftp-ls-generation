Ep 981 - The Token of the Mountain
Word count: 1170
[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
Tia’s fists clenched at Saul’s dismissive tone.
[SFX: CREATURE ROARS]
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE THEME]
A massive horde of creatures from the Life-and-Death Realm swarmed Zane, attacking from all sides. To make matters worse, each one wielded a different Law Power with deadly skill. This might be the single greatest challenge he had ever faced.
[AMBIENT: QUIET TENSION]
[MUSIC: SOFT WORRIED PIANO]
Isla pressed her palms flat against her chest, her eyes squeezed shut. There was nothing she could do to change what was happening. All she could do was pray.
Cora, on the other hand, had never cared much for rules. She stalked forward and raised her hand.
“And what do you think you’re doing?” Saul asked coolly.
“I’m tearing down this whole ‘true path’ myself!” Cora had no patience for this. She wasn’t methodical like Tia; all she cared about was getting Zane out of there.
But before she could act, Saul spoke again. “Your Violet-Core Physique is powerful, I’ll grant you that. But you can’t activate the spatial nodes from in here.”
Cora ignored him. What a complete ass. With a subtle flick of her finger, she tried to connect with the massive swords in the Violet Core Realm.
[SFX: FAILED MAGIC ATTEMPT]
[MUSIC: FRUSTRATED TENSION]
She swept her hands in a series of circles, but nothing happened. Not a ripple in the air, no response at all. It was just as Saul had said; she couldn’t reach the Violet Core Realm. The entire area felt sealed off, insulated from her power.
A wave of helplessness washed over her. Watching Zane struggle against that onslaught of Law Powers made her sick to her stomach.
She couldn’t stand it another second.
[SFX: SUDDEN MOVEMENT]
[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CRESCENDO]
She lunged for the true path of the Elysian Keep herself.
[SFX: SUDDEN GRAB]
“What do you think you’re doing?!” Tia yelled, grabbing Cora’s arm in an iron grip.
If Cora had jumped onto the path before Zane, the challenge would have scaled to her level. But now? The warriors were conjured for the Life-and-Death Realm. How could she possibly fight them?
In some ways, Cora was sharper than Tia—she understood Zane’s heart, had a better read on people. But when it came to a fight, she was out of her depth. Tia knew, with cold certainty, that jumping onto that path now would be suicide.
Luckily for her, Cora was nowhere near as strong as Tia. No matter how much she thrashed, she couldn’t break free.
[MUSIC: CALM PIANO]
That’s when Isla’s calm voice cut through the panic. “Don’t worry.”
Cora shot her a venomous look. “Easy for you to say,” she scoffed. “You don’t seem worried about him at all!”
“That’s not it,” Isla said, shaking her head, her voice soft but firm. “I just… I have faith in him. I know he’s strong enough to win this.”
Tia nodded in agreement. “Zane’s a lot stronger than he lets on. Don’t listen to this jackass,” she said, jerking her thumb at Saul.
[SFX: VIOLENT IMPACT]
A burst of light exploded from somewhere deep in the mist. All three women flinched.
When the light cleared, Zane was still standing. His clothes were in tatters — Isla immediately looked away, her face burning — but he was unhurt.
Saul’s expression shifted. For the first time, something like genuine surprise crossed his face. “Interesting,” he said quietly. “He seems to have transferred his power to some kind of spirit item.”
Tia stared at the mist. “How is that possible?”
“I can’t say for certain,” Saul replied. “It seems like he’s fused with some kind of will — and he’s routing the force directly into that object rather than letting it hit him.” He paused. “Whatever that object is, it’s absorbing attacks that would kill a Soul Sea Realm warrior. That’s no small thing.”
Cora and Isla just exhaled. They didn’t need to understand how it worked. He was alive. That was enough.
Tia, on the other hand, couldn’t stop turning it over in her mind.
[MUSIC: INTENSE BATTLE DRUMS]
[AMBIENT: ENERGY CRACKLING]
Not long after, the mist at the far end of the path began to thin. Then Zane burst through it, landing lightly on the ground in front of them.
“Did I pass?” he asked, grinning.
“First stage,” Saul confirmed.
The ridge behind Zane dissolved into black and white mist, which swirled upward and condensed slowly into a single token. It drifted down and settled into Zane’s hand.
Tia leaned in. “It’s the Water token.”
Saul nodded. “With that, you can enter The Archives — the library of this Keep. Every text the original master ever collected is in there.” He paused to let that land.
It landed. Zane’s eyes lit up immediately. Before ascending to the upper world, there was no better resource he could imagine.
“Your power transfer technique is what kept you alive,” Saul continued. “The Sacred Mount Lodestone absorbed force that would have destroyed most warriors. What you’re routing that power into — effectively the entire world itself — has a carrying capacity that’s almost impossible to exhaust. As long as it holds, you can go further.” He looked at Zane steadily. “The second stage is harder. But if your spirit item can bear it — it’s worth attempting.”
Zane didn’t hesitate. “I’m going.”
Cora opened her mouth. Tia’s hand landed on her arm. They both knew it was pointless.
The fog below parted again. The true path of the Elysian Keep opened before him, and Zane stepped back in without looking back.
The second stage of the test lasted for an hour, and the sacred warriors only grew stronger. Now, the warriors forged from Dark and Light Energies were at the Soul Sea Realm, despite using no attunement handbooks. The Law Powers they wielded were more advanced, too, striking with the force of the third or fourth level.
Thankfully, the ethereal warriors couldn’t create domains. Zane kept to his strategy, redirecting every attack into Sacred Mount Lodestone, letting it absorb the onslaught.
But he was so focused on pushing forward, on surviving the path, that he had no time to check the condition of the Lodestone.
Even if it was nearly indestructible, just how much of a beating could it take? The combined power of dozens of Soul Sea Realm warriors hammering it again and again… could it really hold?
[SFX: OMINOUS CRACK]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]
The truth was, most of Sacred Mount Lodestone was submerged deep in the sea below. And down there, unseen by Zane, a small crack had formed. With every new impact, it spiderwebbed wider.
“Done!” A breath of relief escaped Zane as he passed the threshold, completing the second stage. It was only then that he noticed Isla blushing and looking away. A glance down confirmed his clothes were in tatters. With a wry smile, he pulled a fresh set of clothes from his Storage Ring and quickly changed.
[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ATMOSPHERE]
As a thick fog swirled around him, another token materialized in the air. This one was shaped like a mountain.
“What’s this one for?” Zane asked, eyeing the new token.
Seeing the token in Zane’s hand, the barest hint of a smile touched Saul’s lips. “With that,” he said, his voice holding a new note of respect, “you can carry The Elysian Keep with you… wherever you go.”
