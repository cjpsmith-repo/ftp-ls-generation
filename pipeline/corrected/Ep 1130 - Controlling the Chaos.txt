Ep 1130 - Controlling the Chaos

Word count: 993

[MUSIC: TENSE MYSTERY]
[SFX: CRYSTAL EXPLOSION]
 Without warning, the supreme Aether crystal exploded.
It shattered into a million tiny pieces, but the Vitality it once held didn’t so much as spark. 
[SFX: Chaos Energy TENDRILS SLITHERING]
Instead, tendrils of Chaos Energy slithered out, replacing it. Zane had only fed a tiny wisp of that energy into the crystal’s shell, but now it had grown into something immense. 
[SFX: WALLS AND FLOOR POCKMARKING]
Currents of chaotic power ripped through the room, pockmarking the walls and floor with countless tiny dents.
[SFX: LOW HISSING]
[SFX: CORROSIVE ENERGY CRACKLING]
[SFX: CRYSTAL SHATTERING]
Zane could only stare, completely baffled. In just a few seconds, the number of dents had multiplied, the slithering strands of energy eating away at his surroundings and spawning even more of their kind.
“Zane, if you don’t get this under control right now, the whole house is coming down!” The Cobalt Dragon’s warning barely cut through the haze in Zane’s mind, but it was enough.
That snapped him out of it. 
[SFX: ENERGY STRANDS RETRACTING]
[SFX: Chaos Energy GATHERING]
He lifted a finger, and the wild strands obeyed, shrinking back. One by one, the little gray snakes crawled out from every corner of the room, gathering in his open palm. 
A moment later, he was holding a mass of Chaos Energy a thousand times larger than what he’d started with. The wisp he’d poured into the crystal had been no thicker than a thread; this was the size of his fist.
“It seems the Chaos Energy absorbed way more than just the Vitality in the crystal,” Zane said in a daze, staring at the roiling mass in his hand. “If I hadn’t stopped it, it would have swallowed the whole house.”
“It’s capable of more than that,” the Cobalt Dragon’s voice echoed in his mind, a rare hint of shock in his tone. “Chaos Energy can devour *everything*.”
Even the dragon seemed unnerved. He was the one who had suggested Zane’s experiment in the first place, and based on what just happened, the chance of failure seemed far higher than success. 
In his hundreds of thousands of years, the Cobalt Dragon had seen countless attunement handbooks. When Zane was first choosing his path in the Skyreach Sect, the dragon had kept the Titan Strike technique in mind—a method said to manipulate Vitality with enough power to ravage and devour anything in its path. Its origins were a mystery, and no one was even sure if it truly came from the Ogres.
For a moment, Zane just stared at the fist of churning energy in his palm. Then his expression shifted — a slow, spreading grin.
He had solved it. And he suspected his master had never even seen this step coming. The Primordial Chaos Tactic only speculated that Chaos Energy would eventually liquefy the way normal Vitality did. That speculation had been wrong — and now Zane had found the actual answer himself.
[SFX: ENERGY HISS]
He pointed at the square table in the corner of the room. The fist-sized cloud of Chaos Energy drifted toward it, and then sank in.
[SFX: TABLE DISINTEGRATING]
In a few breaths, the table was gone. Not broken, not scattered — simply gone, consumed entirely. The chaos cloud had grown again.
Zane retrieved it and turned it over in his palm, thinking. The devouring was real, and it was controllable. But it wasn’t uniform.
[MUSIC: METHODICAL PERCUSSION]
He spent the next stretch of time running experiments — feeding the chaos cloud different materials, watching how fast it worked through each one. Furniture and ordinary objects went almost instantly. Soil, wood, brick, and tile took longer but still fell quickly. Metal was slower. Weapons were slower still — a Mythic Weapon resisted for a time, a Spirit Weapon longer, a Sacred Weapon for close to half a day before the Chaos Energy finally consumed it entirely. A Divine Weapon, he suspected, would take far longer.
The limits were real, but so was the potential. As long as he kept feeding materials in, the Chaos Energy grew. And as it grew, the pressure inside his Vitality core began to change.
[SFX: INTERNAL RUMBLING]
He could feel his Vitality core swelling — a sensation he hadn’t experienced since the earliest days of cultivating Chaos Energy. The Inner World was narrowing as the energy condensed, pressing inward, building toward something.
Just then, the door swung open.
[SFX: DOOR OPENING]
Freya came in carrying a pot of tea. She took one look at the walls — covered in tiny dents from floor to ceiling — and her expression went carefully neutral. She set the tea down quietly, turned around, and walked back out without a word.
She was good at that. There were things she wasn’t meant to know, and she never pushed.
As Zane channeled the Chaos Energy into himself, he never felt full. 
[SFX: ENERGY COMPRESSION RUMBLE]
[MUSIC: OMINOUS BUILDING TENSION]
Once the concentration reached its absolute limit, it would have to liquefy. Zane kept feeding more Chaos Energy into himself, paying close attention to every subtle change in his body.
He wasn’t disappointed.
Soon, a vision of his Inner World bloomed in his mind’s eye. Masses of chaotic power were gathering, coalescing into a thick, brown liquid that sank into the depths. 
[SFX: LIQUID DRIPPING]
[SFX: RUMBLING UPHEAVAL]
[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL ENERGY HUMMING]
The liquefaction continued for six days. On the seventh day, the brown sea inside Zane began to ripple. He had technically entered the Soul Sea Realm after passing ten minor Heavenly Tribulations, but it was only today that he had truly created his Soul Sea. And it was nothing like the ones other warriors had. 
[SFX: WAVES CHURNING]
The constantly churning waves of his Soul Sea were so potent that they could completely swallow even a Divine Weapon.
[SFX: MESSAGE RUNE BURNING]
Not long after his Soul Sea finally settled, a familiar voice rang in his ears. It was Lincoln, burning the Message Rune Zane had given him. He must have finally arrived in Auspice City.
Zane had been waiting to hear from him. He’d come to the Dark World on Lincoln’s invitation, and with the secrets Lavender had shared, things were finally starting to work in his favor. He had every intention of getting his hands on the resources hidden here.
Zane responded to Lincoln’s message, then found Freya. 
[MUSIC: DETERMINED RESOLVE]
“Pack your things,” he told her. “We’re leaving.”
