Ep 771 - The Power Gap

Word count: 1019

[AMBIENT: TENSE ATMOSPHERE]

[MUSIC: OMINOUS ORCHESTRAL]

The York Divine Kingdom's forces had arrived over Oracle City — nine Soul Sea Realm masters and ninety Life-and-Death warriors — and Kieran had just said what everyone already knew: it didn't matter whether Zane's reinforcements showed up or not.

Not a soul in the Heartlands could hold a candle to masters this powerful.

On the main continent, a master of the Life-and-Death Realm was essentially a War Sage. By that measure, Tia, having just entered the realm, was like a first-level war sage. But the truth was, every master and every War Sage had to face a series of life-or-death trials—the same Small Heavenly Tribulations Lavender had mentioned. These weren’t just about survival; they were a crucible that forged a warrior's strength.

After reaching the Life-and-Death Realm, a warrior could only ascend to the Soul Sea Realm by passing a number of these tribulations. The exact number varied. Most needed to conquer at least five or six. It was a double-edged sword: the more talented the warrior, the more trials they faced. But surviving them meant entering the Soul Sea Realm with immense power. The number of tribulations you endured literally defined your future potential.

There were plenty of War Sages on the main continent, but the men standing here were the absolute elite, most of them sixth or seventh-level. They were just one step away from the Soul Sea Realm, having already survived six, or even seven, of those life-or-death crises. Any one of them was leagues stronger than Maddox, who had only passed through four. In fact, a single one of these men could have probably destroyed the entire Heartlands on his own.

[SFX: RUMBLING POWER]

 And today, more than a hundred of them were gathered in one place. The sheer force radiating from them was suffocating.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The warriors of the World Merchants' Guild were stunned into silence. A primal fear seized Kieran, Hobbes, and Burns, the kind that made the hair on their arms prickle and their blood run cold. The last time they’d faced an attack from the Corrupted Divine Kingdom, they’d at least had the guts to fight back. But looking at this assembly of demigods, they didn't even dare to think about resistance.

The power gap was just too vast. Any struggle would be utterly pointless.

Despite this, Kieran squared his shoulders and flew forward. Bowing low, he addressed the group, his voice tight with forced composure. "I am the leader of the World Merchants' Guild. Welcome, masters from the divine kingdom."

[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]

 Dorian, standing at the forefront, eyed the heavy-set man with a blank, unimpressed expression. "Where is Zane?"

Kieran was strong — but only by the standards of the Heartlands. To Dorian, he was a peak War Lord, and a middling one at that — probably inferior to Fabian and Jarrod, who were themselves below the Demon Night's finest.

The question hung in the air. Kieran said nothing.

He knew what he should do. As Guild leader, drawing a clear line between the Guild and Zane was the rational move — the survival move. These warriors had no quarrel with the World Merchants' Guild. Give them what they wanted and walk away intact. It was just business.

But Zane had saved them. More than once.

Hobbes's voice came low and urgent from behind him. "Brother." 

Kieran opened his mouth.

He never got the words out.

[SFX: RUSH OF WIND]

A beam of light shot out from Oracle City. Zane.

Below, in the streets and on the walls, countless Guild deacons and warriors had been watching the sky in secret since the alarm bells rang. Every face had gone the same color — ash-white. Even a blind man could have sensed what was bearing down on Oracle City. They watched Zane fly out to meet it alone, and not one of them made a sound.

Zane flew straight toward the assembled force, his bearing completely unhurried — as if the hundred-odd warriors hanging in the sky above him were simply not worth adjusting his pace for. The Guild leaders stared. Was he genuinely unafraid, or had he simply lost his mind?

Zane stopped in front of Dorian and smiled. "When we parted at the Heavenly Eagle Prairie, I never imagined I'd be calling on you so soon. I'm ashamed to trouble you with something so small."

Dorian returned the smile. "Young Master Lowell says too much. What you did for our people in the Tower of Sin — the human race will feel that for generations. This is nothing."

[MUSIC: MYSTICAL REVERENT]

[SFX: MAGICAL MATERIALIZATION]

 Specks of red light scattered from Zane's body, intertwining and condensing until Lavender stood beside him — his sword spirit, fully materialized.

[SFX: ARMOR CLANKING]

[AMBIENT: REVERENT SILENCE]

 The moment she appeared, every Demon Night warrior present dropped to one knee.

"Greetings, My Queen."

The words came in unison, from six Soul Sea Realm masters and dozens of Life-and-Death Realm warriors, all of them bowing their heads to a single slender figure.

Kieran was speechless. 

No one could have ever guessed the truth: that Lavender, the sword spirit living inside Zane, was the queen of the Demon Night, the spiritual guide who ruled their entire race. In the upper world, beings far more powerful than she had to bow in her presence. For these men, kneeling was simply the natural order of things.

Lavender glanced at the masters from the Soul Sea Realm. "You may rise," she said softly.

Only then did the Demon Night masters get to their feet, moving respectfully to the side.

[MUSIC: UNCERTAIN STRINGS]

 Kieran floated hesitantly toward Zane, his voice trembling. "Zane… what is happening?"

He’d heard Dorian call Zane "Master Lowell," and a sudden wave of anxiety washed over him. Was it even okay for him to use Zane's first name anymore? He was so much weaker than everyone else here.

But Zane was only twenty! Should he really be calling him "Master Lowell"? The thought was just too weird. He didn't even show that kind of deference to Maddox.

Kieran was completely torn.
