Ep 1167 - You’ll Regret This

Word count: 1002

[MUSIC: EPIC TENSION]
Even the terrifying force of the ogre Saint Lord, Yosef, couldn’t stop the Wheel of Reincarnation. 
Freya hung unconscious in Yosef’s grip, grey chains still binding her.
His voice boomed across the landscape. “Stop this now, or the human girl dies!”
Zane just scoffed. 
“You touch her,” he said, his voice ice, “and I’ll flatten your entire sacred place.”
To prove he wasn’t bluffing, he sent the Wheel of Reincarnation surging forward. 
[SFX: MASSIVE ARTIFACT ACCELERATION]
[SFX: MONSTROUS TORNADO]
The massive artifact accelerated, kicking up a monstrous tornado that tore across the land in its wake. Zane himself was stunned. He never imagined the Wheel of Reincarnation could move this fast.
Traveling through a supreme world was supposed to be a long, arduous journey—that’s why most warriors used the designated channels. The distance to the ogres’ sacred place was immense; a round trip would normally take a Soul Sea Realm warrior an eternity. But with Yosef chasing him, Zane had covered the distance in less than two hours.
[MUSIC: DESTRUCTIVE RAMPAGE]
[AMBIENT: OGRE CITY]
Strange, black buildings began to dot the horizon—he was already at the edge of ogre territory, closing in on one of their biggest cities. 
[SFX: EAR-SPLITTING ROAR]
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT CRASH]
[SFX: GROUND TEARING]
With an ear-splitting roar, Zane slammed the Wheel of Reincarnation down into the ground. It carved a trench hundreds of miles long and several miles wide, a hideous scar slashed through the heart of the city.
There was no telling how many ogres had been caught in its path.
[SFX: MASSIVE EXPLOSION]
Fury blazed in Yosef’s eyes as he watched the destruction. The kid wasn’t bluffing. It was only a border city, and Yosef wouldn’t have mourned its loss for long. But if the kid kept this up—if he reached the heart of the sacred place—he could wipe out everything the Saint Lord had spent years building.
[SFX: DEEP RUMBLING]
[SFX: BUILDINGS COLLAPSING]
A deep rumble echoed as the Wheel continued its rampage, leaving a trail of pure devastation. 
[SFX: WINGS BEATING]
[SFX: WARRIOR SHOUTS]
Ogre warriors flew up from the city below, swarming into the air, staring in shock and rage at the colossal artifact. But the burly fighters were like gnats before it, completely helpless and with no idea why this disaster had suddenly fallen upon them. A few brave—or foolish—warriors tried to fight back, but what could they do? If their own Saint Lord couldn’t stop the Wheel of Reincarnation, they stood no chance at all.
[SFX: HEAVY METAL SEALING]
The moment Zane took control, he had sealed off every entrance. He could sense the pathways in the artifact’s design—there were several, including one on the very bottom—and he had locked them all down.
The Wheel slowly lifted back into the air. Zane’s cold voice rang out from inside it.
“I’ll give you a chance to regret this.”
Then it lurched forward again, driving deeper into ogre territory.
[SFX: SPACE TEARING]
[SFX: RUSHING FOOTSTEPS IN AIR]
Yosef moved. He crossed the space in an instant, teleporting directly into the Wheel’s path — and held Freya out in front of him like a shield.
The Wheel of Reincarnation bore down on them and then ground to a halt, stopping just dozens of feet from where Freya hung limp in his grip.
“Have you thought it through?” Zane’s voice came from inside, flat and cold. “Let her go. We’ll pretend this never happened.”
Yosef’s face twisted. His city lay half in ruins behind him. And this junior was telling him to pretend it never happened.
But there was no room left to negotiate. He knew it. After a long moment, the fury drained from his face and left something worse behind — helplessness. He loosened his grip. 
[SFX: CHAINS DISSOLVING]
The grey chains binding Freya dissolved.
With his other hand, he touched two fingers lightly to her forehead. 
[SFX: DARK ENERGY FLASH]
A black flash of light shot out — and Freya’s eyes opened.
She blinked. She was in the grip of an enormous ogre she didn’t recognize, suspended in mid-air, with a colossal golden disc hovering in front of her. The last thing she remembered was the Endless Abyss.
Zane’s voice came from inside the Wheel. “Freya.”
Her eyes went wide. “Zane?” 
[SFX: BODY BREAKING FREE]
She shook her shoulders free of Yosef’s loosened grip and kicked off into the air, drifting toward the Wheel.
“Come to the bottom,” Zane said. “There’s an entrance.”
Yosef watched from a distance, silent and fuming.
Zane’s voice cut through the chaos, sharp and mocking. “You! Get out of here.”
Yosef’s face twisted with rage. He was a Saint Lord, one of the most respected beings in the world, and this… junior… was ordering him around? 
“You little brat,” Yosef snarled through gritted teeth.
“I don’t have time for your tough-guy act,” Zane’s voice shot back, leaving no room for argument. “If you don’t want your precious sacred place reduced to rubble, then get out of my way.”
Yosef let out a long, ragged breath, the sound of pure frustration. He had no choice but to back away. He’d had a half-formed plan: wait for the girl to try and board the Wheel, forcing Zane to open an entrance, and then teleport inside during the chaos. But the odds were slim. And this kid… he didn’t seem like the forgiving type. If the plan failed, his sacred place really would be destroyed. After weighing his options, he retreated.
What Yosef didn’t—couldn’t—know was what lay hidden inside the Wheel of Reincarnation: a Crown of Destiny.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS REVELATION]
[AMBIENT: ETHEREAL HEAVENLY VIBRATION]
Its activation sent a tremor through the very fabric of the Heavens, a vibration felt only by the one hundred and twenty-eight Supreme Lords. A Crown of Destiny was the kind of artifact a World Lord would go mad for. If Yosef had known, he would have risked everything—his life, his sacred place, all of it—for even a chance to claim it.
