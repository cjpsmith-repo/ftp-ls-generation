Ep 357 - Our Turn
Word count: 1009

[AMBIENT: TENSE CONFRONTATION]

[MUSIC: DRAMATIC CONTEMPLATION]

The moment Sir Kenneth issued his challenge, every member of the Crimson Sect pivoted toward Zane, their gazes sharp and assessing. Several disciples leaned forward with barely concealed hostility, their hands unconsciously drifting toward their weapons as predatory smiles crossed their faces.

Zane was still just a Third Grade Adept; nothing could change this fact. The conventional wisdom was that determination and skill could overcome a gap in strength, but the gulf between Adepts and Soul Realm cultivators seemed insurmountable.

In their eyes, defeating this upstart youth represented more than a simple victory—it offered redemption, a chance to reclaim their wounded dignity and salvage their collective pride after Alistair's humiliating defeat.

Eddy, the Crimson Sect's leader, was running his own calculations. Alistair is our top disciple, and he got taken down so easily. It was humiliating. If we can't get a single win against their guys, our reputation is shot. Hoffman is our number two, and he's almost on Alistair's level. The last time they sparred, Alistair barely scraped by. Maybe if I send Hoffman out against this kid now…

A prickling sensation on his neck made Eddy pause his scheming. He glanced over and caught the glint in Sir Kenneth's eye, the shadow of a sly smile playing on his lips. His gut screamed at him to drop the idea.

Then it hit him. Wait a second. Kenneth knows Zane is just a Third Grade Adept, but he's practically daring my disciples to fight him. What's his angle? Kenneth's confidence... it has to mean Zane is way stronger than Finn let on. I'd be an idiot not to trust my read on Kenneth. If Zane is that good, he might actually have some grasp of the Spatial Laws…

Even after connecting the dots himself, Eddy had a hard time swallowing it. But the logic was sound. In the end, he decided to trust his gut.

[SFX: FOOTSTEP FORWARD]

Hoffman declared, stepping forward with shoulders squared and jaw set in determination."Sir, I want to challenge Zane." 

Eddy shook his head, his voice firm. "No. You need to save your strength for the Cloud Road trial."

"But—!" Hoffman sputtered, his face flushing red with outrage. The young disciple struggled to comprehend his master's unexpected caution. He couldn't understand why Eddy exercised such caution now. So what if he lost? It would only cast a temporary shadow over the Crimson Sect's reputation. Surely Eddy couldn't be that concerned about taking two hits in a single day.

Eddy snapped, his face darkening. "I said no, and that's final!" 

Hoffman's stomach dropped at the look on Eddy's face.  A cold sweat broke across his brow as he recalled the harsh lessons learned by those who had previously defied Eddy's commands. As head of the Crimson Sect, Eddy's word went beyond mere authority—it was more binding than an imperial decree from the Emperor himself.

Rebellion still simmered beneath his skin, but Hoffman realized he had no choice. He gave Eddy a stiff, respectful nod and backed away with measured steps, his pride wounded but intact. As he withdrew, he cast a final venomous glare toward Zane, silently promising that this matter remained far from settled.

Finn and Patrick frowned, a flicker of disappointment in their eyes. They'd been hoping to see another Crimson Sect disciple get put in their place. After the beatdown they'd each taken from Zane, in the All Peaks Competition, watching someone else get a taste of it would have been sweet.

[AMBIENT: FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING]

At that moment, a new group approached with confident strides, their robes billowing slightly in the gentle breeze. The distinctive insignia on their attire caught Patrick's eye immediately.

He nudged Zane and whispered with barely contained excitement, "Look, it's the disciples of Cloud Hall…." His words trailed off as his gaze followed the newcomers, a mixture of awe and wariness flickering across his face.

[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY CHARGING]

Their attention was drawn to an arched gateway dotted with small holes, where Toby Dillon stood. With a fluid wave of his arms, Aether crystals shot out from his Storage Ring one after another.

[SFX: STORAGE RING ACTIVATION]

[SFX: CRYSTALS FLYING AND CLICKING INTO PLACE]

A dense stream of Aether crystals flew out, each one slotting perfectly into a hole on the gateway.

Zane watched breathlessly. "Whoa... those are all Supreme Aether crystals. There must be over two hundred holes there! That means they're using more than two hundred Supreme Aether crystals!" The Cloud Hall was on a whole other level. He'd never expected a fourth-tier Sect to be this loaded.

Zane had always thought of himself as pretty well-off. By melting down Mythic and Spirit Weapons, he could rake in a ton of crystal cubes. But it took a million crystal cubes just to equal one Supreme Aether crystal. Two hundred of them... that was four hundred million crystal cubes.

It hit Zane that with that kind of cash, the Cloud Hall could probably buy out all Seven Great Houses.

Of course, Zane wasn't the only one in shock. Aside from the coolly detached disciples from the Cloud Hall, just about everyone else was staring at the gateway, their jaws on the floor.

[SFX: GATEWAY TREMBLING]

Once all the Supreme Aether crystals were in place, the gateway began to tremble. 

[AMBIENT: SWIRLING VORTEX]

A gray whirlpool materialized in the center, swirling like it wanted to suck them all straight down to Hell.

A lot of the disciples were itching to jump in first, hoping for a head start in the secret area. 

[SFX: BOOMING VOICE]

But then Toby's voice boomed out, "The gate to the Cloud Road is now open! Disciples of the Cloud Hall will enter first!"

The Cloud Hall disciples, all dressed in white, walked confidently toward the whirlpool, shooting slight smirks at the other Sects as they passed.

The other disciples could only watch, gritting their teeth at the smug looks. They wanted nothing more than to wipe those smirks clean off, but they knew how powerful the Cloud Hall disciples were. So they just stood there and took it.

Patrick leaned in to whisper to Zane. “They're in, Zane. Now it's our turn.”

[AMBIENT: MYSTICAL PORTAL]

[MUSIC: ADVENTUROUS THEME]

Zane nodded and headed for the whirlpool.
