Ep 268 - Broken Spirit

[MUSIC: TENSE BATTLE]
Adrian tightened his grasp on the Sovereign Blade, visibly shaken that Zane had nearly dislodged the legendary weapon from his possession. His fingers trembled slightly against the ornate hilt, betraying the anxiety coursing through his veins.
The prospect of a Soul Realm master like himself falling to a mere second-tier Adept represented more than defeat—it would shatter his reputation and standing among his peers. Adrian’s jaw clenched as beads of sweat formed along his brow, each drop a testament to the mounting pressure he felt. The whispers that would follow such a humiliation would haunt him through the halls of every Peak, transforming him from respected master to cautionary tale overnight.
[SFX: POWER SURGE]
Even though Zane felt a surge of power, he knew brute force wasn’t enough to knock the sword from Adrian’s hand.
Warriors at the Adept Realm and above didn’t just grip their weapons with raw muscle—they channeled Vitality. For a swordsman, a solid grip was a fundamental, critical skill. There were generally two ways to do it: the three-point grip and the six-point grip.
The three-point grip was standard for Adepts. It meant securing the hilt by channeling Vitality from three focus points in the hand: one at the thumb and two at the pinky.
But the six-point grip was a technique for masters in the Soul Realm. It expanded on the three-point method by adding three more focus points: one on the index finger, one on the middle, and one on the ring. This created a tight, enveloping sheath of Vitality around the hilt. Since masters had far denser and more powerful Vitality than Adepts, they could pull off this advanced technique with ease.
The pinnacle of swordsmanship was the legendary union of warrior and weapon, something only possible through a deep bond between a swordsman’s Vitality and his blade. That’s why, for some masters, the sword looked like a natural extension of their arm.
[SFX: SWORD CLASH]
Adrian was a master of the Soul Realm, using the six-point grip. His sword, The Sovereign’s Blade, was so light, so fluid, it seemed to redirect force rather than meet it head-on. That made it nearly impossible for Zane to disarm him, no matter how much strength he put behind the blow.
But somehow, Zane had almost pulled it off.
[SFX: CRYSTAL ACTIVATION]
Zane’s last punch hadn’t even been his most powerful. Though he’d activated the Phoenix Crystal, the dormant power of the Dragon Scales remained untapped, a reservoir of strength held in reserve.
The punch instead unleashed a shocking vibrational energy that rippled through the air. When his fist connected with the sword’s hilt, the legendary blade began to shake violently, its perfect balance suddenly compromised.  These vibrations, though small, resonated at a frequency that was off the charts. Zane’s attack instantly disrupted the six streams of Vitality binding Adrian to his cherished weapon. The Soul Realm master’s fingers loosened involuntarily, his perfect six-point grip faltering as The Sovereign’s Blade—an artifact that had never left its master’s hand in battle—nearly slipped from his grasp.
Zane didn't pause. The moment Adrian's grip faltered, he closed the distance and started throwing punches.
[SFX: RAPID FIST IMPACTS]
Adrian retreated, trying to use the Sovereign's Blade to push Zane back — but Zane was fighting chest-to-chest, too close for the sword to be useful. Every time Adrian tried to condense a lotus, another punch came in and broke his concentration.
[SFX: BODY IMPACTS — REPEATED]
Most of the blows Adrian managed to absorb with his Vitality — but each one carried that same shuddering vibration, rattling through his defenses and into his bones. After the third punch landed clean, something warm hit the back of his throat.
[SFX: VIOLENT VIBRATION]
Excruciating vibrations surged up through the hilt and coursed into Adrian’s arm, penetrating deep into his muscles and bones. His blood churned violently beneath his skin, vessels constricting as the jarring frequency disrupted his entire system. A crushing wave of nausea overwhelmed him, twisting his stomach and blurring his vision.
Adrian’s face paled to an ashen gray, his confident expression crumbling into one of disbelief and mounting horror as he realized the extent of damage from what had appeared to be a simple punch.
[AMBIENT: STUNNED SILENCE]
The audience could only stare, stunned into silence once again.
Not everyone in the crowd was a swordsman, but most were warriors with their own weapons, and the principles of a proper grip were similar for nearly all of them. Wielding a spear, for example, also required channeling Vitality from both hands, just from different focus points than a swordsman used.
If The Sovereign's Blade had been a heavier, broad-bladed weapon, Zane might have actually had an easier time.
The pain and shame left Adrian speechless. He knew that in his current state, there was no way he could summon the Lotus War Body.
[MUSIC: TENSE CONFRONTATION]
"Give up," Zane said. "You’re no match for me like this. And even if you could still summon your Lotus War Body, what makes you think I don’t have something else up my sleeve?"
Just six months ago, beating Adrian would have been an impossible dream. But today, at the All Peaks Competition, he'd taken him down with his bare hands in front of the entire Skyreach Sect.
[MUSIC: SOMBER DEFEAT]
Adrian slowly raised his head, his eyes dull and lifeless as they met Zane's.
He’s right, Adrian thought. He didn’t even use his throwing blade. If he’d thrown it when that flash of light blinded me, I’d be dead right now.
And his soul attack... he didn’t use it once. I brought a special crystal pendant to block it, but I doubt it would have done any good. He’s gotten so much stronger since the last time...
A master of the Soul Realm... and I can’t even touch a second-tier Adept. Was I ever really a genius? Or have I just been arrogant this whole time?
[SFX: HEARTBEAT SLOWING]
Adrian stood rooted to the spot, his mind reeling.
Adrian had always been defined by his arrogance. But now, frustration and despair were plain on his face. Zane saw it in his eyes: Adrian's warrior's spirit was completely shattered.
Zane stared at Adrian, feeling an unexpected pang of pity. Some people had an unbreakable warrior’s spirit; they could lose a thousand times and still get right back up. But Adrian came from a noble family. He was proud, arrogant, and had never known a day of real hardship. Now, as a master of the Soul Realm, any setback felt like a world-ending blow.
He couldn’t handle failure. His spirit crumbled in an instant, a collapse that would have dangerous side effects on his training.
Once a warrior's spirit was broken, it was nearly impossible for them to ever improve.
[MUSIC: OMINOUS TENSION]
And if the backlash was severe enough, it could drive him mad.
