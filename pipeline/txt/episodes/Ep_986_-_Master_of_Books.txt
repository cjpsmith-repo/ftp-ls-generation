Ep 986 - Master of Books
Word count: 1071
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL WONDER]
[AMBIENT: VAST LIBRARY ECHO]
The shuddering hadn’t stopped — it had spread. One by one, then all at once, the books on Zane’s row began to move.
Each book was a thirty-foot-tall behemoth, lined up on a bookshelf that was a mountain in itself, reaching at least ten thousand feet high. A single shelf held about three hundred rows, meaning tens of thousands of books were stacked on it alone.
[SFX: RUMBLING EARTHQUAKE]
Suddenly, the massive books began to topple from the shelf.
[SFX: MASSIVE IMPACT CRASH]
Each one weighed as much as a mountain range, and they crashed down with a terrifying, earth-shattering roar.
The Eternal Scribe threw out a hand, catching a few of the falling tomes, but it was useless—he couldn’t stop the avalanche.
[SFX: WHOOSHING AIR]
[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS]
Thinking fast, Zane grabbed Isla and pulled her back. Tia, the Scribe, and Saul scrambled to follow.
[SFX: FINAL THUNDEROUS CRASH]
By the time they reached a safe distance, the shelf was completely bare. The books now lay in a colossal heap on the floor, forming a mountain of their own.
“You idiot! I told you something was wrong with your method,” Saul snapped, shaking his head in frustration.
[AMBIENT: ANCIENT LIBRARY]
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS TENSION]
For a long moment, the Eternal Scribe just stared at the chaos in stunned silence. Finally, he spoke, his voice low. “No… that’s not it.”
“Then what is it?” Saul shot back, rolling his eyes. As the one in charge of the archives—which hadn’t been opened in years—Saul was already convinced the old man was incompetent. Now, with Zane having finally gained them access, he figured the Scribe had just blown their one big chance.
[SFX: ETHEREAL WHISPERS]
[MUSIC: MYSTICAL CHOIR]
“I can hear them,” the Eternal Scribe muttered, his eyes distant. “The books… they’re calling to Zane. All of them.”
[SFX: DRAMATIC IMPACT]
Saul’s jaw dropped. “Impossible!”
Every warrior had their strengths and weaknesses; that was just a fact. No exceptions. Sure, when Zane first grasped Law Power, he’d shown a knack for the Spatial Law, and he’d grown even stronger with the others after the Baptism by Laws. But this? Attracting every single book on the shelf? That should have been impossible.
Some of the handbooks weren’t even based on attributes or an understanding of the Laws; they responded only to the strength of one’s soul.
[MUSIC: EPIC REVELATION]
There was only one explanation for what happened: Zane himself.
He wasn’t just compatible with all of them—in a way, he was their master.
Both men fell silent, equally bewildered. Even that explanation didn’t fully account for it. There being no shortcomings was one thing, but to attract every last handbook, with nothing to distinguish one over another? Neither of them had words for it.
[SFX: LIGHT FOOTSTEPS]
Zane walked toward the mountain of books with a helpless smile. “So… which one do I actually cultivate?”
Tia fell into step beside him. “Easy,” she said, her tone perfectly dry. “Cultivate all of them.”
Zane gave her a flat look. She kept her eyes forward, the picture of innocence.
[MUSIC: SUBTLE WONDER]
Then something caught his eye. High on the shelf, somewhere around the twentieth row, one book remained. While every other tome had hurled itself to the floor, this one sat alone in the corner, perfectly still.
[SFX: SOFT WHOOSH]
Zane leapt. He shot upward, landing cleanly on the massive shelf at the twentieth row and walking toward the lone book. Tia and Isla rose slowly behind him. The Eternal Scribe and Saul flew up after them, too impatient to wait.
[AMBIENT: QUIET LIBRARY — HIGH UP]
Standing before it, Zane studied the book carefully. Every other tome in The Archives had a faint spiral rune on its cover — the same rune the Eternal Scribe had shown him how to use: press your perception into it, and the text would open to you. This book had nothing. No rune. No marking of any kind. Just a plain, unbroken cover.
He reached out and grabbed a corner of it.
[SFX: STRAINING EFFORT]
It didn’t move.
[SFX: MAGICAL ENERGY SURGE]
Zane frowned. He pushed harder — Dragon Scales igniting, two thousand of them blazing to life across his body.
The book didn’t budge.
Three thousand.
Nothing.
Four thousand. Five thousand.
[SFX: DEEP RESONANT HUM]
The air around him shook with the force of it. The shelf groaned. The book sat perfectly still, as though it weren’t a book at all, but a wall.
[SFX: HEAVY THUD — TWO IMPACTS]
“I’ll do it.”
“I’ll do it.”
Saul and the Eternal Scribe spoke at the same moment. Neither was going to let the other go first. They exchanged a look, then — wordlessly — both grabbed the cover together.
Combined, they dwarfed Zane’s strength by a wide margin. The shelf shuddered under the force of their grip.
The book did not move an inch.
[MUSIC: TENSE STRINGS]
“What gives, old man?” Saul asked, his frustration boiling over. He was dying of curiosity but had no clue how to even begin to open the damn thing.
The Eternal Scribe shook his head, looking defeated. “I’ve searched our Master’s memoirs for an answer, but there’s nothing. I… I don’t know what to do.”
The memoirs had been the Scribe’s first resort. He knew every book in the archives intimately, having pored over them thousands of times; he could recite any one of them from memory. That was the easy part. But in all his studies, his Master had never once mentioned a book without a rune. Could it have been so important that it needed to be hidden this well?
[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS CHOIR]
“It has to be some kind of trick,” the Eternal Scribe finally reasoned. “Our Master probably didn’t want this book to be found. If that’s the case, there must be a powerful seal on it. A seal I doubt we’re strong enough to break on our own.”
[AMBIENT: QUIET LIBRARY]
[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL PIANO]
Zane stared at the immovable book, his mind racing with ways to open it. He knew the tomes scattered across the floor were more than he’d ever need for his training, but it didn’t matter. No one could deny the pull of this strange, silent book; it had them all hooked.
Whatever kind of handbook was locked inside, Zane was determined to be the one to find out.
