Ep 686 - The Sea Wyrm Sect

Word count: 1156

[MUSIC: DARK INTENSE STRINGS]

Tessa had never killed anyone, but watching her friend get hurt flipped a switch. All mercy vanished.

[SFX: SWORD WHOOSH AND IMPACT]

She sent the flying sword spinning straight through the warrior’s chest, the blade chewing a brutal, gaping hole in its wake.

“Wray!”

The roar tore from Bernard’s throat. He and his men had fought in countless life-or-death battles over the years, their caution and strategy always seeing them through. To lose one of them now—to lose Wray—while hunting down three girls? Unthinkable.

[MUSIC: AGGRESSIVE BATTLE DRUMS]

A fresh wave of fury surged through him, and he lunged for Tessa, intent on making her pay for it in blood.

Seeing him charge, Tessa went still. All the fight drained out of her, replaced by a grim resolve. She knew she couldn’t win, but if she was going to die, she’d face it head-on. But just as Bernard was about to reach her, he froze, his body locked in place.

[SFX: ETHEREAL WHISPER ECHO]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS SUSPENSEFUL TONE]

A cool, calm voice echoed in her head. “Use your flying sword. Stab him again. Let’s see if you can kill him.”

Tessa’s head snapped up, her eyes scanning the empty clearing. There was no one there.

[SFX: MAGICAL FREEZE SOUND]

In front of her, Bernard stood like a statue, his eyes wide and vacant. A spell. This was her chance. She didn't waste a single second.

[SFX: SWORD RIPPING FREE]

With a flick of her will, the sword ripped free from Wray’s body and shot back to her, its bloody tip now aimed squarely at Bernard's face.

Though his body was paralyzed, his voice wasn’t. “You witch! How dare you!” he snarled, but the rage was thin, stretched over a thick layer of terror. The look on Tessa’s face confirmed it: she was going to kill him. Panic clawed at his throat. “Who’s there? Show yourself, you coward!” he bellowed into the air.

[SFX: SHADOW MATERIALIZATION]

In response, a figure materialized from the shadows behind Tessa. It was Zane. He regarded Bernard with a lazy, indifferent gaze—the kind of look you give a man who’s already dead.

Rage and disbelief crashed over Bernard. Zane? He was supposed to be critically injured. He was just an early-stage Spirit-Tribulation warrior. How could he possibly stand against Elder Holt and his men? Were they… dead? That just couldn't be.

[SFX: SWORD PIERCE]

His frantic thoughts were cut short as Tessa’s sword shot forward, punching straight through his skull. The blade hung in the air for a second, trailing blood, before losing its light and tumbling into the sea below.

[AMBIENT: OCEAN WAVES]

[MUSIC: MELANCHOLIC PIANO]

With the threat gone, Tessa turned, her gaze falling on the man standing behind her. It was him—the stranger they had pulled onto the reef. “Please,” she gasped, the adrenaline that had kept her going finally giving way. “My friends… you have to save them.” The tension of the fight, the horror of what she'd done—it all crashed down on her at once. Her vision swam, the edges turning dark, and she collapsed.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS HARP]

She came to what felt like a long time later, propping herself up on her elbows. Her two friends were lying beside her, safe.

Across the reef, Zane sat with his eyes closed. She flew over and landed softly beside him. He opened his eyes.

"You're awake," he said.

Tessa nodded. "Who are you? What's your name? You're our savior."

"I'm not your benefactor," Zane said. "If it weren't for me, you wouldn't have been in danger in the first place. Your own courage saved you."

"My father always said people should know how to repay kindness," Tessa said quietly. "That's all I know."

Zane looked at her for a moment. What kind of father raised a daughter like this and then let her walk into the world of warriors? He shook his head.

"My name is Zane. Zane Lowell."

Tessa's eyes went wide. "Zane Lowell — from the Cloud Hall? But — weren't you in the Soul Realm? How are you already…" She stared at him.

Zane didn't answer that. Instead: "What's the situation in the Heartlands right now?"

Tessa filled him in. "All the sects have united. They're hunting independent cultivators, forcing them to join or be eliminated. That's why our sect is full of all kinds of people now."

Zane nodded slowly. That explained the five pirates. "What about the Cloud Hall? The World Merchants' Guild? The Ethereal Spirit Sect?"

Tessa stared at him blankly. He looked at the other two women, now stirring awake. "Do they know?"

"No," Tessa said without hesitation.

Zane exhaled. "The Sect isn't far from here?"

"I can show you the way," Tessa said, grateful to finally be useful.

The two other women woke, and once they learned who Zane was, all three of them talked without stopping the entire way — questions layered on questions, their near-death experience apparently already forgotten. Zane's head began to ache.

[SFX: MAGICAL SHIMMER]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS HARP]

Then Lavender shimmered into existence beside him, releasing a sharp pulse of presence toward the three girls.

"You three. Quiet."

The women fell silent instantly — then immediately swarmed her.

"Whoa, your skin is so pale!"

"And what is this robe made of? It looks like leaves, but it feels so strong!"

"Huh? Wait, why are your ears so… pointy?"

Lavender rolled her eyes and shot a look at Zane. "Nope. I'm not dealing with this," she said, before dissolving into motes of red light that flowed back into his body.

[AMBIENT: MOUNTAIN WIND]

[MUSIC: MYSTERIOUS STRINGS]

Fortunately, the Sea Wyrm Sect was built on a mountain overlooking the water. After a short flight over the coast, Zane spotted the sprawling complex. For a third-grade sect, it was surprisingly large, with enough disciples to fill a good third of the Violet Heart Sanctuary. It was a stark reminder of how sparsely populated the main continent was; back in The Heartlands, any decent sect boasted millions of disciples, with behemoths like the Cloud Hall and the Ethereal Spirit Sect numbering in the tens of millions.

[SFX: ENERGY HUM]

[AMBIENT: PROTECTIVE ARRAY]

As they approached the entrance, the Sect's protective array hummed to life, sending hundreds of streaks of light shimmering across its surface. A moment later, two beams of light shot out from the gate, resolving into a pair of warriors who landed in front of them. Both were in the middle stages of the Spirit-Tribulation Realm.

[MUSIC: TENSE PERCUSSION]

One of them stepped forward, his hand on his sword. “State your purpose.”

Tessa quickly smiled. “Master Croy! It’s us. This is Zane Lowell, from the Cloud Hall.”

“Zane Lowell?” The two masters exchanged a confused look.

[MUSIC: SUSPENSEFUL STRINGS]

The first one, Young, narrowed his eyes at Zane. “Who are you, really? The Zane Lowell from the Cloud Hall is dead.”
